# Xendit Appoints Nazim Ali as Chief Revenue Officer to Scale Client Adoption and Global Go-To-Market


**ami**
Nov 5, 2025 •


2


min read


- [5 November 2025](https://www.xendit.co/en/blog/2025/11/05/)


Xendit, Southeast Asia’s leading financial infrastructure company, today announced the appointment of Nazim Ali as Chief Revenue Officer (CRO), underscoring its commitment to expanding global and regional partnerships and becoming the preferred payments platform for global, regional, and high-growth companies operating across Southeast Asia.


Based in Singapore, Nazim will lead Xendit’s global go-to-market organization, overseeing commercial strategy, sales, account management, marketing, and partnerships as the company strengthens its role in powering Southeast Asia’s digital economy.The appointment marks a key milestone in Xendit’s evolution from a regional scale-up to an enterprise-grade financial infrastructure partner for the region’s largest businesses.


In 2024, Xendit processed over US$32.5 billion in total payment volume, supporting more than 10,000 active merchants and 100,000+ sub-accounts—demonstrating the company’s ability to serve complex organizational structures and diverse business needs across Indonesia, the Philippines, Thailand, Malaysia, Vietnam, and beyond.


“Southeast Asia’s digital economy is entering a new phase of scale, and businesses need trusted partners for that growth,” said **Moses Lo, Co-Founder and Group CEO of Xendit** . “Nazim’s global experience in payments and enterprise sales will help us accelerate our next stage of expansion and reinforce Xendit’s leadership in the region.”With two decades of experience scaling high-growth technology and financial services businesses globally, Nazim has led commercial and platform growth at some of the world’s most recognized financial institutions and fintech companies.


Before joining Xendit, he served as Global Co-Head of Sales for eCommerce, Technology, Media, and Telecommunications clients at J.P. Morgan Payments, expanding the bank’s engagement with Asia’s leading technology companies and entrepreneurs. He previously held senior leadership roles as CEO Singapore and SVP, Head of Platform Sales & Strategy (APAC) at Airwallex, and at Adyen, where he helped scale the company’s Asia Pacific business. Nazim began his career at Citi in New York and Singapore, working across Risk Management, Corporate Banking, and Markets.


“Southeast Asia is entering a defining decade for digital commerce,” said **Nazim Ali, Chief Revenue Officer at Xendit** . “As businesses scale across borders, they need infrastructure that matches their ambition—reliable, compliant, and deeply localized. Xendit’s combination of regional depth and world-class capability creates a powerful platform for growth, and I’m honored to help expand it to become the preferred choice for global and regional enterprises operating in the region.”


Nazim’s appointment adds global depth to Xendit’s leadership as it advances its 2026 growth roadmap—focused on enterprise expansion, cross-border payments, and ecosystem partnerships—cementing its position as Southeast Asia’s leading financial infrastructure provider for global and regional businesses.
