Most roadside assistance programs operate like it’s still 2005. Lengthy verification calls. Manual dispatch. No visibility into service provider locations. The result? Frustrated customers and damaged brand loyalty.

### **AI Changes Everything**


Smart dispatch algorithms don’t just find the nearest provider — they factor in traffic, provider expertise, and real-time availability. The result is a 5-minute dispatch time that transforms customer anxiety into confidence.


But technology alone isn’t enough. The real breakthrough comes from removing friction at every touchpoint:


**Automatic verification** replaces complex VIN searches with simple mobile number authentication.


**SMS location sharing** eliminates the “where are you exactly?” phone tag.


**Digital VCRF** captures real-time vehicle condition photos, preventing disputes before they start.


**Live tracking** keeps customers informed with precise ETAs through WhatsApp and SMS.


### **The Dealer Revenue Multiplier**


Here’s what forward-thinking OEMs understand: roadside assistance isn’t a cost center — it’s a revenue driver. When vehicles get towed to authorized dealers instead of random shops, service revenues increase. Customer retention improves. Brand loyalty strengthens.


Smart dealer portals let service advisors sell RSA plans during routine maintenance, creating new revenue streams while building stronger customer relationships.
