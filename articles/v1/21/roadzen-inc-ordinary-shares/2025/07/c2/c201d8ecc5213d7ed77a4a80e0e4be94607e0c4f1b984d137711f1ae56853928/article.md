**Smarter Underwriting for Insurers**
Underwriting is all about accuracy, and AI makes this easier than ever. With AI-driven vehicle inspections, insurers can assess a car’s condition in minutes. This ensures policies are issued only after identifying any pre-existing damage. The result? Policies that are fair, accurate, and reduce risks for both insurers and customers.


**Enhanced Operations for OEMs and Dealerships**
Ever notice how service advisors take photos when you drop your car off for maintenance? Now, imagine combining that with AI to pinpoint damages down to the smallest scratch. Not only does this improve transparency with customers, but it also surfaces additional repair opportunities. For dealerships, this means an increase in body shop revenue from paint or dent repairs that would otherwise go unnoticed.


**Precision in Fleet and Rental Management**
For rental companies and commercial fleets, AI simplifies the intricate process of tracking vehicle condition. Whether it’s before-and-after photos for rentals or daily vehicle check-ins across large fleets, AI ensures transparency and accountability. Issues like dents or scratches are recognized instantly, alerting both customer and business in real time.


**The Bigger Picture**
From preventing losses to optimizing operations, data-driven AI inspection is setting new industry standards. For businesses ready to adopt these advancements, now is the time to act.
