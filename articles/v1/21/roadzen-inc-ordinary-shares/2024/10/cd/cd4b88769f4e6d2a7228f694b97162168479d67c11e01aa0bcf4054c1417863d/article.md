Fleet operators understand that minimizing downtime is crucial for maintaining productivity and profitability. Whether in logistics, transport services, or any other vehicle-dependent industry, downtime significantly impacts productivity and costs. With predictive maintenance and AI-powered solutions, unexpected breakdowns can be avoided, timely support can be provided, and improved driving behavior can be incentivized.


**Predictive Maintenance**


AI-powered predictive maintenance is vital in keeping fleets operational. By analyzing data and real-time sensor inputs, AI can predict part failures or maintenance needs, extending vehicle lifespans and optimizing resource use, thereby enhancing efficiency.


**Safety and Compliance**


Advanced technologies enhance safety and regulatory compliance by monitoring driver behavior. Telematics can track driving patterns, speeding, and harsh braking to provide accurate insights. Computer vision-enabled platforms can alert against potential accidents and coach better driving behavior in real-time.


**StrandD Fleet: Roadside Assistance**


StrandD Fleet is Roadzen’s on-demand roadside assistance platform, streamlining service management to maximize fleet uptime. By combining scheduled maintenance with real-time support, it reduces unexpected disruptions, keeping fleets moving efficiently.
