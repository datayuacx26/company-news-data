## WSFS Financial Corporation Announces First Quarter 2026 Earnings Release Date and Conference Call


04/09/2026


Download (opens in new window)


WILMINGTON, Del.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


WSFS Financial Corporation (Nasdaq: WSFS), the parent company of WSFS Bank, expects to report its first quarter earnings at the end of business on Thursday, April 23, 2026. Management will conduct a conference call to review this information at 1:00 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) on Friday, April 24, 2026.


Interested parties can register in advance[here](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fattendee%2F644124236&esheet=54482315&newsitemid=20260409390553&lan=en-US&anchor=here&index=1&md5=57d3be99657127e961e22e42e7ee565a) or access the conference call live at[investors.wsfsbank.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.wsfsbank.com%2Fnews-and-events%2Fevents-and-presentations%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54482315&newsitemid=20260409390553&lan=en-US&anchor=investors.wsfsbank.com&index=2&md5=704b3d278380ba3214b45df3a3375045) . Earnings release and supplemental materials will be available prior to the start of the event via the[Investor Relations](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.wsfsbank.com%2Foverview%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54482315&newsitemid=20260409390553&lan=en-US&anchor=Investor+Relations&index=3&md5=10a9602c94c789bc2e42cac59f3239ef) section of the Company’s website, and participants are advised to log on at least 15 minutes prior to the broadcast.


For those who cannot access the live conference call, a replay will be accessible shortly after the event concludes through the links above.


**About WSFS Financial Corporation**


WSFS Financial Corporation is a multibillion-dollar financial services company. Its primary subsidiary, WSFS Bank, is the oldest and largest locally headquartered bank and wealth management franchise in the Greater Philadelphia and Delaware region. As of December 31, 2025, WSFS Financial Corporation had $21.3 billion in assets on its balance sheet and $97.4 billion in assets under management and administration. WSFS operates from 113 offices, 87 of which are banking offices, located in Pennsylvania (58), Delaware (37), New Jersey (14), Florida (2), Nevada (1) and Virginia (1) and provides comprehensive financial services including commercial banking, consumer banking, treasury management, and trust and wealth management. Other subsidiaries or divisions include Arrow Land Transfer, Bryn Mawr Trust Advisors, LLC, Bryn Mawr Trust®, The Bryn Mawr Trust Company of Delaware, Cash Connect®, NewLane Finance®, WSFS Wealth® Management, LLC, WSFS Institutional Services®, and WSFS Mortgage®. Serving the Greater Delaware Valley since 1832, WSFS Bank is one of the ten oldest banks in the United States continuously operating under the same name. For more information, please visit[www.wsfsbank.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.wsfsbank.com&esheet=54482315&newsitemid=20260409390553&lan=en-US&anchor=www.wsfsbank.com&index=4&md5=b3de1d65530fe1ad4ce62243f24629d7) .
