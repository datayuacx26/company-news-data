# INAUGURAL SCRIPPS SPORTS WOMEN’S BASKETBALL SHOWCASE HEADED TO MORTGAGE MATCHUP CENTER ON DECEMBER 16

# INAUGURAL SCRIPPS SPORTS WOMEN’S BASKETBALL SHOWCASE HEADED TO MORTGAGE MATCHUP CENTER ON DECEMBER 16


Aug. 14, 2026


By jim.weiss


*-Defending Champion UCLA Bruins and USC Star JuJu Watkins to Headline Elite Women’s College Doubleheader –*


**PHOENIX (August 14, 2026)** – Position Sports and The E.W. Scripps Company (NASDAQ: SSP) are partnering to present the inaugural Scripps Sports Women’s Basketball Showcase, a new premier women’s college basketball event debuting on **Wednesday, December 16** , at **Mortgage** **Matchup Center** in Phoenix.


The inaugural showcase will bring two marquee matchups to the Valley, with defending national champion **UCLA** facing **Duke** and **USC** taking on **Miami** in a nationally televised doubleheader on ION, Scripps’ national sports and entertainment network available over-the-air, across pay television, connected TV platforms and streaming services, reaching more than 128 million households nationally.


Tickets to the doubleheader will go on sale, **Thursday** , **August 20** , at 10:00 AM AZ (1:00 PM ET) at **ticketmaster.com** . One ticket provides admission to both games.


“We are thrilled and proud to partner with Position Sports to create the Scripps Sports Women’s Basketball Showcase,” said **Brian Lawlor, President of Scripps Sports** . “This groundbreaking doubleheader represents everything Scripps Sports and ION stands for – creating meaningful connections through sports while elevating women’s athletics on a national stage. UCLA, Duke, USC, and Miami represent some of the finest programs in women’s college basketball, and we’re honored to provide a national television platform for these incredible student-athletes in what promises to be two captivating and inspiring basketball contests.”


**UCLA vs. Duke | 5:00 PM AZ (7:00 PM ET)** The Bruins enter as the defending national champion and an expected preseason Top 15 program, bolstered by the addition of Addy Brown, a transfer from Iowa State who averaged more than 13 points, 8 rebounds and 5 assists per game over her three-year career with the Cyclones. Duke, a projected preseason Top 5 team, welcomes back three of its five starters from last year’s ACC Championship squad, while adding transfer Aaliyah Crump from Texas. The No. 5 recruit in the 2025 class, Crump will play alongside Emilee Skinner, the No. 4 recruit in that same class.


**USC vs. Miami | 7:30 PM AZ (9:30 PM ET)** The Trojans return JuJu Watkins, the 2024-25 consensus National Player of the Year, who missed all of last season recovering from a knee injury, and bring in the nation’s No. 1 recruiting class, headlined by five-star signee Saniyah Hall. Hall marks the third time in four years USC has landed the No. 1 overall recruit, following Watkins in 2023 and Jazzy Davidson in 2025. Miami counters with four veteran transfers added to a team that won 18 games a season ago, including Jess-Mine Zodia, who arrives in Coral Gables with a decorated international resume after representing France in multiple FIBA competitions throughout her career.


“Partnering with Scripps Sports allows us to put four of the nation’s top programs on a national stage for Valley fans,” said **Melissa Meacham-Grossman, President of Position Sports** . “This doubleheader builds on Mortgage Matchup Center’s momentum as a destination for premier women’s college basketball, following the venue’s recent hosting of the NCAA Women’s Final Four.”


Media credential information will be shared at a later date.


The Scripps Sports Women’s Basketball Showcase adds to Scripps’ growing portfolio of women’s sports rights, including the Women’s National Basketball Association (WNBA), NWSL (National Women’s Soccer League), PWHL (Professional Women’s Hockey League), the 2027 FIVB Women’s Volleyball World Cup and Premier Women’s Rodeo (PWR).


For more Scripps Sports Women’s Basketball Showcase information and updates, please visit[mortgagematchupcenter.com](http://mortgagematchupcenter.com/) or follow **@iongameday** and **@scripps_sports** on all social platforms.


**UCLA vs. Duke Series History**


The Bruins hold a 3-2 edge in the series that dates back to the inaugural matchup in 1997. UCLA won the most recent meeting at the NCAA Elite Eight in Sacramento in March, 70-58.
