# W-8BEN for Indian Freelancers: Upwork Guide to 0% Withholding

## Key takeaways


- W-8BEN certifies you are a non US individual, so US payers can apply the right withholding rate, usually 0% for services performed from India.
- Use your PAN on Line 6, leave Line 5 blank if you do not have a US TIN or ITIN.
- If you work with US clients or platforms, including[Upwork](https://www.karboncard.com/blog/platform-tax-statements-upwork-india) , you will be asked for W-8BEN before payouts.
- Work performed entirely in India is generally foreign source income, so the 30% default withholding does not apply once a valid W-8BEN is on file.
- Do not submit W-9 if you are an Indian resident, and do not use W-8BEN-E unless you operate as an entity.
- Renew W-8BEN every three years, or sooner if your address or status changes.
- Keep invoices, payment proofs, and e-FIRA or FIRC for FEMA and Indian tax compliance, platforms like[Karbon Business](https://karboncard.com/) can automate this.
- If you physically perform services in the US, different rules and forms, like W-8ECI and possibly an ITIN, may apply.


## W-8BEN Meaning: What the Form Actually Does


**Form W-8BEN** is the IRS certificate that proves you are the beneficial owner of income, and that you are a non US individual. In simple words, it tells a US payer, or a platform, that you are an Indian resident individual, not a US person.


US payers and platforms request W-8BEN to follow IRS rules, avoid default 30% withholding where it does not apply, and report correctly.


> *W-8BEN is not an Indian tax form, not a US tax return, and not a registration in the US tax system. It is a status certification, typically valid for three years.*


### Understanding the Different W Forms


- **W-8BEN** : For individuals, like freelancers. One page, straightforward.
- **W-8BEN-E** : For entities, like companies, LLPs, partnerships.
- **W-9** : For US persons only, never submit this as an Indian resident.
- **W-8ECI** : For income effectively connected with a US trade or business, for example, work done while physically in the US.


If you are an individual freelancer working from India, *W-8BEN is your form* .


## W-8BEN for Indian Freelancers: When Needed


**You generally need W-8BEN when** :


- You are paid by US clients, companies, or platforms that act as withholding agents, they must keep a valid W-8 on file.
- Your income may be within US Chapter 3 withholding rules unless you certify foreign status.


**You often do not need it when** :


- Non US clients pay you from outside the US, for example a UK company via SEPA or FPS, or a European client sending EUR, where US withholding rules do not apply.


Some global platforms still request W-8BEN as policy, to keep records uniform.


### What About Virtual USD Accounts?


Having a USD virtual account for receiving ACH does not make you a US person, nor does it change your tax residency. The source of income usually follows where services are performed. If you work from India, it is typically foreign source income, even if funds arrive in a USD virtual account.


## US Withholding: What It Is, When It Applies, and Why the W-8BEN Matters


Let us talk about[US withholding](https://www.karboncard.com/blog/platform-withholding-us-india-freelancers) , this is where the 30% fear starts.


US withholding is tax deducted by US payers on certain US source income. If you do not give a valid W-8, they may withhold 30%, or apply 24% backup withholding in some cases.


**The good news** : services done entirely in India are usually *foreign source income* , not subject to US withholding. W-8BEN certifies your foreign status, which commonly results in 0% withholding.


*Example* : A Delhi designer serving a New York startup, all work done from India. This is foreign source income, so with a valid W-8BEN, payers usually do not withhold.


### When Withholding Might Apply


If you travel to the US and work physically there, that portion may be US source income. In that case, W-8ECI and possibly an ITIN could be required. Seek advice if this applies.


### The India US Tax Treaty


The India US DTAA exists. For services performed outside the US, your foreign status alone often suffices for 0% withholding. Some freelancers claim treaty benefits in Part II for other income types, like royalties or dividends, citing articles, for example Article 15 for independent personal services.


For straightforward service income done in India, many leave Part II blank, since Part I certification is enough. When in doubt, consult a professional.


## W-8BEN for Upwork: What to Expect on the Platform


[Upwork](https://www.karboncard.com/blog/platform-tax-statements-upwork-india) is widely used by Indian freelancers, and it requires all non US freelancers to submit W-8BEN, or W-8BEN-E for entities, to keep payouts smooth.


- **No ITIN required** : You can submit W-8BEN without a US TIN or ITIN if services are performed outside the US. Use your PAN on Line 6.
- **Where to submit** : In your account, go to Settings, then Taxes or Get Paid, follow the tax information prompt, upload the W-8BEN PDF.
- **If you do not submit** : Upwork may hold earnings, restrict withdrawals, or start 30% withholding until a valid form is on file.
- **Renewal** : Valid for three years, or until your details change, Upwork will prompt you to renew.


## How to Fill W-8BEN: Step by Step Field by Field


Download the latest W-8BEN from IRS.gov, fill digitally or print with black ink, avoid corrections. Here is a line by line guide for Indian freelancers.


### Part I: Identification of Beneficial Owner


**Line 1, Name** : Enter your full legal name as per PAN, for example, Rajesh Kumar Sharma.


**Line 2, Country of citizenship** : India.


**Line 3, Permanent residence address** : Your Indian physical address, not a P.O. box.


123 MG Road
Bangalore, Karnataka 560001
India


**Line 4, Mailing address** : Leave blank if same as Line 3.


**Line 5, US TIN** : Leave blank if you do not have SSN or ITIN.


**Line 6, Foreign TIN** : Enter your PAN, for example ABCDE1234F, strongly recommended.


**Line 7, Reference number** : Optional, use only if the payer asks.


**Line 8, Date of birth** : Use MM DD YYYY, for example 08 15 1990.


### Part II: Claim of Tax Treaty Benefits


For services done fully in India, many freelancers leave Part II blank, since foreign status typically yields 0% withholding. If you claim treaty rates for other income types, complete Lines 9 and 10 with the relevant article, seek professional advice if unsure.


### Part III: Certification


Read carefully, then sign, print your name, and date in MM DD YYYY. You certify you are the beneficial owner, you are not a US person, and details are correct.


*Pro tips* : Use your Indian residential address, match your PAN name, save a PDF copy, set a reminder to renew in three years or sooner if details change.


## Common Mistakes to Avoid


- **Using W-8BEN-E by mistake** : This is for entities, individuals should use W-8BEN.
- **Listing a US address** : Always use your Indian address to reflect residency.
- **Leaving PAN blank** : Enter PAN on Line 6 for smoother validation.
- **Incorrect treaty claims** : For services done in India, you usually do not need to claim treaty benefits, errors can delay processing.
- **Not updating after changes** : New address, name, or citizenship means a new W-8BEN.
- **Submitting W-9** : Do not, W-9 is only for US persons.

## FAQ


### Is W-8BEN mandatory for Indian freelancers working with US clients on Upwork?


Yes, if you earn via a US payer or a US based platform, Upwork requires W-8BEN, or W-8BEN-E for entities, before withdrawals. Submit the form with PAN on Line 6 to avoid 30% default withholding when your services are performed in India.


### I do not have SSN or ITIN, can I still fill W-8BEN correctly?


Yes. Leave Line 5 blank, enter your Indian PAN on Line 6, that is sufficient for services performed outside the US. Sign and date the form in MM DD YYYY format.


### Will 30% US tax be cut from my Upwork earnings if I am in India and I submit W-8BEN?


If your services are performed entirely in India and a valid W-8BEN is on file, payers typically do not withhold US tax. The 30% applies when no valid W-8 is provided, or when specific US source income rules trigger withholding.


### What should I write in Line 6 of W-8BEN, PAN or Aadhaar?


Use your PAN on Line 6 as the foreign tax identifying number. Aadhaar is not needed on this form. PAN makes validation easier for platforms and clients.


### Do I need to claim India US DTAA benefits in Part II to get 0% withholding on my service income?


For services done fully in India, many freelancers do not claim treaty benefits and leave Part II blank, foreign status in Part I often suffices for 0% withholding. If you receive other income types like royalties or dividends, consult a professional to cite the correct treaty article.


### My US client wants W-9, but I am an Indian freelancer, what to do?


Politely decline W-9, it is for US persons. Share a completed W-8BEN instead. If they need your foreign TIN, provide your PAN on Line 6, and confirm that services are performed from India.

### Can I receive ACH or wire in a USD virtual account and still remain non US for taxes?

### What happens if I travel to the US for a short project, do I still use W-8BEN?


The portion of income from work physically done in the US may be US source. In such cases, W-8ECI and possibly an ITIN could apply. Engage a cross border tax professional before invoicing.


### How often should I renew W-8BEN, and what changes require a new form?


Renew every three years. File a new form sooner if your legal name, residential address, or citizenship changes. Keeping an updated form prevents payout holds and unwanted withholding.
