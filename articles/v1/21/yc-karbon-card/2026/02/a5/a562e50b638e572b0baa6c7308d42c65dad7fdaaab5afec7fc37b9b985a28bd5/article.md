## Compliance Deep Dive


[e-FIRA](https://www.karboncard.com/blog/e-fira) is your electronic Foreign Inward Remittance Advice, the RBI proof of legitimate export earnings. It lists sender, currency, purpose code, conversion rate, INR amount, and credit date. Tax filings, GST refunds, audits, and bank credit lines all depend on consistent e-FIRAs.


- **Automatic e-FIRA** saves hours each month and avoids last mile mistakes.
- **Purpose codes** like P0802 or P0806 must be correct for RBI reporting.
- **Audit trail** should include invoice, service proof, payment confirmation, e-FIRA, conversion, and settlement details.


## Common Pitfalls to Avoid


- **Zero fee traps** , the real cost hides in a worse FX rate, always compare to the mid market rate you see on Google or Xe.
- **Skipping e-FIRA** , global wallets are convenient, but you will chase documents at tax time.
- **Defaulting to SWIFT** , when local rails are faster, cheaper, and more predictable.
- **Mismatched currencies** , always invoice and receive in the same currency for clean reconciliation.
- **Ignoring holding windows** , a simple 15 to 30 day hold policy can add 1 percent to 3 percent to annual receipts when trends favor you.
