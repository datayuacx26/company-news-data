# TRC & Form 10F: Stop Double Tax on Freelance Payments

## Key takeaways


- A[Tax Residency Certificate](https://www.karboncard.com/blog/trc-certificate-guide) proves you are a tax resident of India for a specific period, letting you claim DTAA benefits and reduce foreign withholding on freelance payments.
- Apply using Form 10FA, receive your certificate on Form 10FB, and share it with foreign clients along with forms like[IRS Form W-8BEN](https://www.karboncard.com/blog/platform-withholding-us-india-freelancers) for US payers.
- Typical processing time is 15 to 30 working days, there is no government fee, and you should renew annually for the next period.
- Maintain strong documentation, bank statements, invoices, and e-FIRAs, platforms like Karbon Business make this easier by providing clean records for TRC applications and ITR filing.
- Keep copies of your TRC, invoices, and any foreign withholding certificates, use them to claim Foreign Tax Credit in India if tax was deducted abroad.


## What is a Tax Residency Certificate and why it matters for freelancers


A[Tax Residency Certificate](https://www.karboncard.com/blog/trc-certificate-guide) , or TRC, is issued by India’s Income Tax Department on Form 10FB after you apply using Form 10FA. It certifies that you are a resident taxpayer of India for a stated period, usually one financial year, April 1 to March 31.


Without a TRC, foreign clients often withhold 20 to 30 percent tax by default. With a TRC, you can invoke India’s Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements so your client withholds little or nothing, and you pay tax in India instead.


> *Real world impact: a US client may default to 30 percent withholding, but with a TRC and a properly completed*[IRS Form W-8BEN](https://www.karboncard.com/blog/platform-withholding-us-india-freelancers) *, the rate often drops to zero to 15 percent, saving you thousands over a year.*


For more context, see this practical[overview of TRC in India](https://www.commenda.io/blog/tax-residency-certificate-in-india) .


## When do you actually need a TRC as a freelancer


- **Your client or platform asks for it,** many US, UK, EU, Canada, and Australia payers will not apply DTAA rates without a TRC.
- **You want to avoid double taxation,** proactively share a TRC to reduce or eliminate foreign withholding.
- **You plan to claim Foreign Tax Credit in India,** a TRC plus proof of foreign tax paid supports your ITR.
- **You are scaling up,** beyond a few lakhs of foreign income, the savings justify the small effort.


If a client operates in a treaty country, a TRC is essential. Review typical requirements here:[TRC requirements in India](https://www.aspora.com/blog/tax-residency-certificate-requirements-india) .


## Am I eligible, understanding tax residency in India


You are a resident if you stay in India for 182 days or more in the financial year, or for 60 days or more in the financial year and 365 days or more in the preceding four years, subject to exceptions for certain Indian citizens abroad. Most freelancers living and working from India meet the 182 day test easily.


The Assessing Officer verifies residency using your proofs, passport stamps, address documents, returns, and bank statements. You may request a financial year or calendar year period, as long as your proofs support it. For a helpful explainer on freelance taxes, see[freelance and taxes in India](https://help.mellow.io/en/articles/9134949-freelance-and-taxes-india) .


## How to get TRC India, the complete application process


### Step 1: Confirm eligibility and decide the period


Count your days in India and choose the period to cover, most freelancers pick the full financial year for simplicity. If a client follows a calendar year, you can request that period if you remained resident.


### Step 2: Fill out Form 10FA


Provide PAN, address, contact details, period requested, basis of residency, and purpose, for DTAA benefits. A concise reference:[TRC meaning, benefits, and application process](https://poonawallafincorp.com/blogs/tax/residency-certificate-trc-meaning-benefits-and-application-process) .


### Step 3: Gather supporting documents


- PAN, Aadhaar, passport with visa and stamp pages
- Address proof, rent agreement, utility bills, bank statements
- Latest ITR acknowledgment, computation if available
- Freelance evidence, invoices, contracts, platform profiles
- Self declaration of days stayed in India


Include a brief cover letter if helpful. More on what officers look for:[TRC document expectations](https://www.commenda.io/blog/tax-residency-certificate-in-india) .


### Step 4: Find your Assessing Officer and submit


Locate your jurisdictional AO via the e filing portal, and submit online if available, otherwise file physically and keep the acknowledgment. Quick reference:[Tax Residency Certificate essentials](https://www.taxaj.com/tax-residency-certificate) .


### Step 5: Track and respond to queries


Processing typically takes one to four weeks. If the AO requests additional proof, reply promptly with clear documents to avoid delays.


### Step 6: Receive your TRC on Form 10FB


Once approved, you get a one page certificate stating your name, PAN, address, and the certified period. Keep digital and printed copies. For a recap of the flow, see[this guide to TRC](https://poonawallafincorp.com/blogs/tax/residency-certificate-trc-meaning-benefits-and-application-process) .

## TRC validity and renewal


Your TRC is valid only for the period stated on Form 10FB. There is no automatic renewal, apply afresh each year, preferably at the start of the financial year.


- **Multiple years,** usually not issued in one shot, request year wise.
- **Past periods,** possible if your proofs support it, apply as soon as you realize you need it.
- **One TRC, many clients,** yes, you can use the same TRC across countries for the same period.


For nuances and officer practices, see this[TRC insights article](https://www.commenda.io/blog/tax-residency-certificate-in-india) .


## Using your TRC for DTAA, sharing with foreign clients


- **US clients and platforms,** complete[IRS Form W-8BEN](https://www.karboncard.com/blog/platform-withholding-us-india-freelancers) , claim India US DTAA rate for your service category, attach your TRC.
- **EU and UK clients,** share your TRC with a short declaration that you are an Indian resident and the income is taxable in India per the treaty.
- **Other DTAA countries,** reference the treaty and provide your TRC, have the client’s tax advisor confirm rates.
- **No DTAA,** withholding may not reduce, but you can usually claim Foreign Tax Credit in India with your TRC and proof of tax paid.


Country specific documentation often mirrors the pattern described here, see[TRC requirements](https://www.aspora.com/blog/tax-residency-certificate-requirements-india) for more examples.


## Common problems and how to fix them


- **AO asks for more proof,** provide additional bank statements, rent receipts, mobile bills, and a day by day travel log with passport stamps.
- **Borderline 182 days,** reconstruct your calendar with tickets and entries, explain clearly in a cover note.
- **Apostille requested,** though rare, get apostille via the Ministry of External Affairs if a client insists.
- **No ITR filed yet,** you may still be approved with strong alternative proofs, but file your ITR soon after.
- **Delay over 30 days,** follow up politely, ask if any document is pending, and submit immediately.


Additional troubleshooting pointers are compiled in this[TRC application guide](https://poonawallafincorp.com/blogs/tax/residency-certificate-trc-meaning-benefits-and-application-process) .

## TRC vs other tax forms, avoiding confusion


- **Form 10FB,** your TRC issued by the Indian Income Tax Department.
- **Form 10FA,** the application you submit to obtain Form 10FB.
- **Form 10F,** used by non residents to claim DTAA benefits in India, not for Indian freelancers receiving foreign income.
- **W 8BEN or W 8BEN E,** US IRS forms to claim treaty benefits, attach your Indian TRC to support your claim.
- **Form 15CA and 15CB,** for outward remittances from India, usually not relevant when you are receiving money into India.

## FAQ


### How to get TRC India if I am a freelancer working with US or UK clients and they are cutting high TDS?


Apply via Form 10FA with PAN, address, passport, ITR, bank statements, and freelance proofs, receive Form 10FB as your TRC in one to four weeks, then share it with your clients. For US, attach it to a W 8BEN so they apply India US DTAA rates. Using Karbon Business for invoicing and e FIRA gives you neat records to support the application.


### DTAA ka benefit kaise milega agar client pehle se 30 percent withhold kar raha hai?


Future payments ke liye apna TRC bhejein aur W 8BEN ya client ke local forms file karein so reduced treaty rate apply ho. Jo tax pehle kat chuka hai, uska refund aap client ke country me return file karke le sakte hain, ya India me Foreign Tax Credit claim kar sakte hain, iske liye TRC, invoices, bank credits, aur withholding certificates rakhein. Karbon Business ke e FIRAs aur statements se matching asaan ho jata hai.


### TRC ke liye kaun se documents sabse zaroori hote hain for Indian freelancers?


PAN, Aadhaar, passport with travel stamps, Indian address proof, latest ITR acknowledgment, Indian bank statements showing freelance credits, invoices and contracts, plus a self declaration of days in India. Karbon Business se milne wale invoices aur e FIRA strong supporting proof ke taur par kaam aate hain.


### Kya TRC har saal renew karna padega, ya ek hi certificate lifetime chalega?


TRC sirf certificate me likhe period ke liye valid hota hai, usually ek financial year. Agle saal ke liye naya TRC apply karna hota hai, process repeat hota hai, lekin documents ready hone par approval jaldi mil jata hai.


### Online TRC application possible hai kya, ya AO office jana mandatory hai?


Kuch jurisdictions me e filing portal par Form 10FA upload option hota hai, wahan online submission fastest hai. Agar online option na ho to physical submission AO office me karna hota hai. Pehle portal par AO details check karke call ya email se submission mode confirm kar lein.


### Client ne apostille manga TRC ka, kya ye required hota hai generally?


Aksar countries plain TRC accept karte hain, apostille rare cases me chahiye hota hai. Agar client insist kare to Ministry of External Affairs se apostille possible hai. Pehle client ke tax advisor se double check kar lein, unnecessary steps avoid ho sakte hain.


### 182 din rule borderline hai, international travel zyada tha, to residency prove kaise karu?


Passport entry exit stamps, boarding passes, hotel bills, bank and phone records ke basis par day wise calendar bana kar submit karein. Clear computation aur self declaration attach karein, AO ko residency period samajhne me asaani hogi.


### Kya bina ITR file kiye TRC mil sakta hai, main naya freelancer hoon?

### US client ke liye W 8BEN me kya fill karu jab main Indian sole proprietor freelancer hoon?
