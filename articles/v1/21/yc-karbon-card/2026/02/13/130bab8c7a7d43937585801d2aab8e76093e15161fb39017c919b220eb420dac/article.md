## How Western Union Business India works, and why freelancers look elsewhere


For years, many in India have relied on Western Union’s business flows that route through partner banks and end as INR credits. While the brand offers familiarity, users face opaque FX spreads, intermediary bank deductions, manual e-FIRA handling, and settlement times that often stretch beyond expectations. The result is a higher total cost, slower cash flow, and extra admin work when you need to focus on billable projects.


- **Unclear total costs** , platform fees, FX spreads, intermediary deductions, and GST can add up quickly.
- **Slower settlement** , funds typically arrive in 2 to 5 business days, and you rarely have precise visibility.
- **Manual documentation** , you often chase e-FIRA proofs and export documentation with your bank.
- **Client friction** , non local details on invoices increase effort and, sometimes, client fees.


> *Bottom line* : Evaluate alternatives using a clear checklist, total cost, time to INR credit, compliance automation, client experience with local rails, and onboarding plus support quality.


## Understanding the full cost, fees that add up quickly


Your real cost is a stack, platform fee, FX markup, intermediary bank charges, withdrawal costs, and GST on the platform fee. Traditional SWIFT based methods often combine a variable transfer fee with a 1 to 3 percent FX spread, then add $15 to $40 in correspondent deductions. On a $2,000 invoice, that can mean losing 3 to 5 percent, leaving around $1,900 to $1,960.


Global wallets are convenient for clients, but effective costs tend to be higher, especially when you include FX spreads and withdrawal charges. India first platforms flip the model by using mid market rates, charging a flat platform fee, and removing intermediary surprises, so you keep more of every invoice.


## Measuring speed, how long before you see your money


[SWIFT](https://www.karboncard.com/blog/ach-vs-swift-fees-india) based networks, including Western Union business flows and direct bank wires, generally take 2 to 5 business days, sometimes longer with correspondent checks. Global wallets offer better visibility but are still typically 2 to 5 days end to end.


India first platforms use local rails, clients pay via ACH, SEPA, FPS, or EFT into virtual accounts in your name, you then claim the payment and receive INR in 24 to 48 hours. Real time alerts via dashboards or WhatsApp keep you in the loop, which means you can plan payouts, vendor bills, and investments with more confidence.


## Compliance and documentation, the e-FIRA question


Every service export needs proper documentation.[e-FIRA](https://www.karboncard.com/blog/efira-firc-documents-guide) is your official proof that foreign currency entered India correctly, and it is essential for taxes and audits. Traditional setups make you request it from your bank, sometimes waiting days. India first platforms automate this, generating e-FIRA for each settlement, typically within 24 hours, ready to download whenever you need it.


Always confirm RBI and FEMA compliance, AML controls, and security standards such as ISO 27001. Automation here saves hours of back and forth, especially during tax season.

### How is GST applied on international payment fees for freelancers in India?


GST at 18 percent applies on the platform fee only, not on the full invoice or the FX amount. If the platform fee is $20 on a $2,000 payment, GST is $3.60, bringing the total fee to $23.60. ### What exactly is FX markup, and how does zero FX markup help my take home?


FX markup is the spread a provider adds over the mid market rate, for example adding 2 to 3 percent over the true rate. Zero FX markup means you convert at the mid market rate, so you avoid losing that extra percentage. With a flat 1 percent fee and no FX spread, platforms like Karbon Business typically let you keep about $1,980 on a $2,000 invoice.
