## Understanding US withholding on YouTube earnings


Google applies US withholding on income from US viewers, this includes ads, YouTube Premium, Super Chat, memberships, and similar features. This is separate from Indian taxes, and separate from AdSense platform fees. If you do not submit tax information, Google can apply backup withholding of up to 24% on your total global earnings, not just the portion from US viewers. With a valid W-8BEN and the India treaty rate, withholding falls to 15% and applies only to the US sourced slice.


For background and policy context, see this[TaxGuru explainer on YouTube creator withholding](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/withholding-tax-payment-youtube-creators-google.html) .


> **Bottom line:** File your tax form, claim the treaty, and the US takes a smaller percentage, only from US viewers, letting you keep more of your hard earned income.


## The India US tax treaty and why it matters


The India US Double Tax Avoidance Agreement prevents the same income from being taxed twice. Under Article 12, royalties, which include YouTube ad revenue, Indian residents can claim a 15% treaty rate on the US sourced portion. If you skip the treaty claim, the default US rate can be 30% on your US earnings, and if you provide no tax info at all, backup withholding of up to 24% can hit your entire global payout.


The treaty only applies to US audience revenue, income from viewers in India or any non US country is not subject to US withholding. This is why filing the right YouTube tax forms in AdSense is so valuable. For additional industry coverage, see[Economic Times on YouTube tax rules for Indian creators](https://economictimes.com/tech/technology/indian-creators-neednt-panic-about-new-youtube-tax-rules/articleshow/81451725.cms) .


## Step by step, how to file YouTube tax forms W 8BEN in AdSense


### Step 1: Navigate to tax settings


Log in to AdSense. Go to Payments, then Manage settings, find United States tax info, and click Add tax info.


### Step 2: Select the correct form


Choose W-8BEN if you are an individual creator. If you operate as a registered entity, company or LLP, you will use W-8BEN-E instead, see this quick guide to[W-8BEN and W-8BEN-E](https://www.karboncard.com/blog/guide-to-w-8ben-and-w-8ben-e) . Most solo creators should select W-8BEN.


### Step 3: Part I, personal information


Enter your legal name as per PAN, select India as your country of citizenship and tax residency, and provide your Indian residential address. Avoid using any US address unless you are actually a US tax resident.


### Step 4: Part II, tax identification and treaty


Enter your PAN as the Foreign TIN. Tick Claim tax treaty benefits, select India, choose Article 12, Royalties, and enter 15% as the treaty rate.


### Step 5: Part III, certifications and signature


Read and sign to certify that you are an Indian tax resident, that details are accurate, and you are entitled to the treaty rate. Sign electronically and submit.


### Step 6: Submit, confirm, and monitor


After submission, Google confirms by email, typically within hours. The new rate applies immediately or from the next cycle. W-8BEN is usually valid for about three years, update early if your situation changes. Verify withholding under Payments → Transactions in AdSense, if numbers look off, review your filing or raise a ticket. For further reference, see the[TaxGuru walkthrough](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/withholding-tax-payment-youtube-creators-google.html) .

## Common mistakes that increase YouTube earnings tax withholding


- **Not filing any form:** Triggers backup withholding, up to 24% on total worldwide earnings.
- **Choosing the wrong form:** Individuals must use W-8BEN, entities use W-8BEN-E, wrong selections cause delays or higher rates.
- **Missing or incorrect PAN:** PAN is your Foreign TIN, typos or blanks can invalidate benefits.
- **Using a US address:** May push you toward W-9 and US person treatment, avoid unless true.
- **Letting W-8BEN expire:** After expiry, Google reverts to backup withholding, renew on time.
- **Not checking reports:** Always confirm the rate under Payments → Transactions.


## Understanding Form 1042 S and AdSense reports


Google issues Form 1042 S annually, usually by March, if any US tax was withheld. It shows your US sourced income, the tax withheld, and the rate applied. Download it from Payments → Tax documents, store it safely, and use it while filing your Indian return to claim Foreign Tax Credit via Form 67.


- **Box 2:** Income code, usually 16 for royalties.
- **Box 7a:** Gross income paid, US sourced.
- **Box 7b:** Tax withheld.
- **Box 12a:** Tax rate, 15% if treaty applied.


## Reporting YouTube income and claiming tax credits in India


YouTube income is business income in India. Report it in your ITR, pay tax per slab, or consider presumptive taxation if eligible. The US withholding does not auto reduce Indian tax, you must claim Foreign Tax Credit by filing Form 67 before filing ITR.


Keep these documents ready, AdSense statements, Form 1042 S, bank statements, and e FIRA or FIRC for each inward remittance. Your CA will compute eligible FTC to prevent double taxation.
