### OneMain Holdings Announces Date of First Quarter 2026 Earnings Release and Conference Call


April 15, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s203.q4cdn.com/410697831/files/doc_news/OneMain-Holdings-Announces-Date-of-First-Quarter-2026-Earnings-Release-and-Conference-Call-2026.pdf)


NEW YORK


, April 15, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- OneMain Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: OMF), the leader in offering nonprime consumers responsible access to credit, plans to report its first quarter 2026 results before the market opens on Friday, May 1, 2026. The earnings release will be available on OneMain's investor relations website at[http://investor.onemainfinancial.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4665512-1&h=1323228807&u=http%3A%2F%2Finvestor.onemainfinancial.com%2F&a=http%3A%2F%2Finvestor.onemainfinancial.com) .


A conference call to discuss the company's results, outlook and related matters will be held that morning at 9:00 a.m. Eastern. The general public is invited to listen to the call by dialing 800-420-1271 (U.S. domestic) or 785-424-1634 (international), and using conference ID 31259, or via a live audio webcast through our investor relations website. For those unable to listen to the live broadcast, a replay will be available on our website after the event.


**About** **OneMain Holdings, Inc.**


OneMain Financial (NYSE: OMF) is the leader in offering nonprime consumers responsible access to credit and is dedicated to improving the financial well-being of hardworking Americans. We empower our customers to solve today's problems and reach a better financial future through personalized solutions across 48 states, available online and in more than 1,300 locations.
