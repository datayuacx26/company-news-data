# Polymath Robotics Blog | Meet Kai: The AI-Powered Humanoid Robot for Heavy Industry

Polymath Robotics is proud to introduce Kai, a cutting-edge humanoid robot poised to transform heavy industry. Named for its "Knowledge AI" capabilities, Kai offers unmatched versatility and efficiency.


Our CEO Stefan Seltz-Axmacher remarked, when we reached him on the bridge of his boat, "Once we realized how good humanoids are at locomotion and fine motor skills, we shifted focus immediately from automating vehicles to developing Kai. After all, why automate the vehicle when you can automate the driver?"


‍


Concept art for KAI, the modern industrial humanoid


Kai has a wide range of industrial applications including agriculture, construction, and modern dance.


CTO Ilia Baranov adds "I've always said: 'Why legs?' But when I saw what Kai could do, I said, 'Yes, legs. Definitely legs.'"


What separates Kai from other impressive humanoid projects is its sophistication.


"A superhuman amount of engineering has gone into Kai," Stefan notes, emphasizing that Kai is "definitely NOT our intern in a robot suit."


Preorders from major construction, mining, and traveling dance troupes highlight Kai's industry-wide appeal.
