# Polymath Robotics Blog | Alex Yuen talks radar perception at ROSCon 2024!

We are excited to announce that Alex Yuen, Senior Engineer at Polymath Robotics, will be presenting at **ROSCon 2024** in Odense, Denmark! His talk, titled *Enhancing Path Planning with Real-Time Obstacle Detection* , will take place on **October 23rd, from 4:20 PM to 4:40 PM CEST** .


Alex will share Polymath Robotics' innovative approach to improving navigation in ROS 2 by utilizing neural networks and radar systems to detect the size, position, and velocity of dynamic objects. This enables smarter path planning for mobile robots in complex environments.


Don't miss it! Join Alex on **Day 2 of ROSCon** to learn more about this cutting-edge technology.
