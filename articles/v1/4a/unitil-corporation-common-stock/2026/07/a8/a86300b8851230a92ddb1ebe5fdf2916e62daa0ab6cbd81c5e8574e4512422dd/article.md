# Get the Most out of your Ceiling Fans


Ceiling fans can help improve comfort and efficiency year-round.

If you use air conditioning to cool your home, a ceiling fan will allow you to raise the thermostat setting about 4°F with no reduction in comfort. Follow these simple tips to get the most out of your ceiling fans!


## Choosing a Fan:


Choose a fan based on the size of your room:


- For rooms up to 225 square feet, use a 36-inch or 44-inch diameter fan.


- For larger rooms, choose a fan with a diameter of 52-inches or more.


- For longer rooms, consider multiple fans for optimal comfort.


Be sure to choose a model with an Energy Star label for maximum efficiency!


## Direction Matters:


Ceiling fan direction should be adjusted seasonally to optimize comfort:


- In the summer, fans should operate in a counterclockwise direction to create a cool
breeze.


- In the fall, switch fans to turn clockwise at a low speed to circulate warm air. This
improves both comfort and efficiency.


Look for the switch on the base of the fan to change its direction.


## Other Tips:


Turn off ceiling fans when leaving the room to save energy.


## Key Takeaways:


- If you use air conditioning, a ceiling fan will allow you to raise the thermostat setting
about 4°F with no reduction in comfort.


- Ceiling fan direction should be adjusted seasonally for optimal comfort,
counterclockwise in the summer and clockwise in the fall and winter.


- Choose a fan that has the Energy Star label and is an appropriate size for the room.
