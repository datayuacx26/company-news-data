# 60 Travel Tips to Celebrate CLEAR’s 60th Airport Launch

In celebration of CLEAR+ now being available in 60 airports across the United States, we are providing 60 quick tips to help you get the most out of your travels. From a pre-departure checklist to destination guidelines, check out CLEAR’s 60 travel tips for the best possible experience:


‍


## **Planning & Prep**


1. Make a packing checklist a few days before leaving.


2. If traveling solo, share your itinerary with a friend or family member.


3. Set[flight price alerts](https://www.clearme.com/partner-detail-pages/going) to track deals.


4. Book accommodations with free cancellation when possible.


5. Research local events happening during your trip.


6. [Get a CLEAR+ Membership](https://enroll.clearme.com/enroll/) for a fast airport experience.


7. Confirm check-in and check-out times before booking.


8. Download travel apps for maps, tickets, and reservations.


9. Plan downtime into your schedule so you’re not rushed.


10. Double-check operating hours of attractions before you go.


‍


## **Packing**


1. Roll clothes to maximize space.


2. Use packing cubes to stay organized.


3. Pack versatile clothing you can mix and match.


4. Bring a light rain jacket—even if the weather doesn’t call for rain, it can always change.


5. Keep toiletries in a clear, waterproof pouch.


6. Always pack one “just in case” outfit.


7. Slip an extra tote or foldable backpack into your luggage.


8. Carry a reusable water bottle.


9. Label your bags with both phone and email.


10. Bring light snacks for the trip—you can[bring food through airport security](https://www.clearme.com/blog/can-you-bring-food-through-tsa) .


‍


## **Flights & Airports**


1. Check in to your flight 24 hours before departure.


2. Use an[airport concierge service](https://www.clearme.com/clear-concierge) to get help with your luggage.


3. Keep essentials in your carry-on in case luggage is delayed.


4. Download your boarding pass in advance.


5. Layer clothing—planes can be hot or cold.


6. Order food available after security ahead of time.


7. Bring noise cancelling headphones or earplugs.


8. Carry disinfectant wipes.


9. Walk and stretch during long layovers.


10. Check baggage weight limits to avoid surprise fees.


‍


## **Money & Safety**


1. Split cash and cards into separate locations.


2. Keep a backup credit or debit card in a different bag.


3. Take photos of your IDs, tickets, and receipts.


4. Use a small lock for your luggage.


5. Use a crossbody bag or backpack you can zip shut.


6. Keep emergency contacts in your phone.


7. Lock hotel doors and use the room safe when available.


8. Back up photos and documents to the cloud.


9. Avoid carrying all valuables at once.


10. Carry a small first-aid kit.


‍


## **Staying Connected**


1. Download offline maps of your destination.


2. Use airplane mode and Wi-Fi to save battery/data.


3. Keep a portable power bank handy.


4. Save hotel or Airbnb info in your phone and on paper.


5. Download shows, books, or podcasts before leaving.


6. Share your location with a trusted contact if[traveling solo](https://www.clearme.com/blog/solo-travel-tips) .


7. Carry a universal charging cable.


8. Learn how to enable Wi-Fi calling if needed.


9. Store travel documents in a notes app or digital wallet.


10. Take a small notebook for quick notes or sketches.


‍


## **At Your Destination**


1. Wake up early to enjoy attractions before crowds.


2. Ask locals or hotel staff for hidden gem recommendations.


3. Try local food spots instead of only well-known chains.


4. Take free walking tours to learn the area.


5. Stay hydrated, especially if walking all day.


6. Take plenty of photos, but also soak in the moment.


7. Use public transportation when it’s safe and convenient.


8. Carry a reusable bag for groceries or souvenirs.


9. Don’t overpack your schedule—allow for spontaneity.


10. Trust your instincts—if something feels off, skip it.


‍


Whether you fly out of John Wayne Airport regularly or are heading on an international family vacation, use these travel tips to win the day of travel. And join CLEAR+ for a faster airport experience, helping you get from home to gate, and back again.
