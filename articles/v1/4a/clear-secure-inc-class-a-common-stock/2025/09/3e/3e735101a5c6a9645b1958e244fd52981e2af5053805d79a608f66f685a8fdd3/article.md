# Is TSA PreCheck® Worth It? Yes, and Here’s Why

Following the DHS’[announcement on July 8, 2025](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2025/07/08/dhs-end-shoes-travel-policy) , travelers in standard security lanes no longer need to remove their shoes—many travelers are asking the question, “ *Is TSA PreCheck® still worth it?”* This policy update has caused some to reconsider the value of the TSA PreCheck® enrollment fee, but our take is that the membership still offers considerable advantages.


Let’s explore the benefits of TSA PreCheck®, what’s changed, and how pairing it with CLEAR+ and CLEAR Concierge can create an even easier travel experience.


‍


## **What Is TSA PreCheck®?**


[TSA PreCheck®](https://www.clearme.com/tsa-precheck) is a trusted traveler government program administered by the Transportation Security Administration. It provides expedited screening at participating U.S. airports for low-risk travelers. Once approved, you can use dedicated TSA PreCheck® security lanes, which tend to have much shorter waits than standard ones.


When you enroll in TSA PreCheck® through CLEAR, the fee is $79.95 for five years. After pre-enrolling online with a simple application process, head to any CLEAR TSA PreCheck® enrollment location with a valid U.S. passport or[other accepted forms of ID](https://www.tsa.gov/precheck/required-Identification) , where you’ll be fingerprinted, have your photo taken, and submit payment. The process takes less than ten minutes, and if approved, you will receive your Known Traveler Number (KTN) in 3-5 days, though some applications can take up to 60 days. You can then add your KTN when booking flights to get the TSA PreCheck® indicator on your boarding pass.


## What's Changed: New TSA Policy on Shoes


The July 8, 2025 policy update now allows travelers in standard lanes to keep their shoes on. While this may seem like a reason to forgo getting TSA PreCheck®, it's only one of the many benefits that come with the membership.


‍


## Benefits of TSA PreCheck®


Even with TSA now allowing travelers to keep their shoes on in standard lanes, TSA PreCheck® still offers other benefits that streamline airport security screening:


- No need to remove your laptops, 3-1-1 liquids, belts, or light jackets
- Shorter security wait times
- Available with 90+ airlines at 200+ airports nationwide


About 99% of TSA PreCheck® travelers wait less than 10 minutes at security checkpoints. For more information, read the[TSA Travel Tips](https://www.tsa.gov/travel/travel-tips/2025) .
