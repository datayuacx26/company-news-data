## **Services Offered by an Airport Concierge**


While services vary by provider and location, most airport concierge offerings include some or all of the following:


- **Meet-and-greet service** : A friendly professional meets you at the curb, entrance, or arrival hall.
- **Luggage assistance** : Help with checking bags, navigating luggage carts, and handling carry-ons.
- **Expedited security access:** Skip long lines using an[airport fast pass](https://www.clearme.com/clear-plus) service.
- **Gate escort** : Get help locating your gate, navigating terminals, or rebooking during disruptions.
- **Lounge access** : Assistance finding eligible lounges.
- **Connections support** : Help with making a tight connection, especially one in a different terminal.
- **Personalized assistance** : From dietary needs to wheelchair support, concierge teams handle all types of special requests.
