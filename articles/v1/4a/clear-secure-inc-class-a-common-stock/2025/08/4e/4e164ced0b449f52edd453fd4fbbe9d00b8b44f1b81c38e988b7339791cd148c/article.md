## **How It Works**


Once you enroll in CLEAR+, simply head to the CLEAR+ Lane at airport security where we’ll verify You are YOU with our face-first technology. Then, our friendly Ambassador will bring you straight to security screening, making it one of the[fastest ways to get through airport security](https://www.clearme.com/blog/how-to-get-through-airport-security-faster) .


CLEAR+ is available in the U.S. at 150+ Lanes across 62 airports.

## **What About Global Entry?**


While **Global Entry** is great for reentry into the U.S., CLEAR+ Members enjoy a faster, easier security experience when flying out of the U.S.—no interviews, no appointments, and now, open to global travelers.
