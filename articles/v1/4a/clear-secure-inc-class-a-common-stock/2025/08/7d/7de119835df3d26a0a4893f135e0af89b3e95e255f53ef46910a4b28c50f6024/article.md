## How to Renew Your Passport Online


If you want to know[how to renew your passport online](https://www.clearme.com/blog/renewing-your-passport-just-got-easier) , this process has become significantly easier, thanks to recent updates from the U.S. State Department. Many United States citizens can now complete the entire renewal process online, eliminating the need for physical paperwork and in-person visits.


### Who's Eligible for Online Renewal?


You may qualify for online passport renewal if you meet all of the following criteria:


- You're at least 25 years old.
- Your current passport is or was valid for 10 years.
- Your passport was issued within the last 15 years.
- You have your passport in your possession.
- You're located in the U.S. or a U.S. territory.
- You're not requesting any changes to your name, gender, date of birth, or place of birth.
