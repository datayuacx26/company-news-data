# Introducing eGates—A Reimagined Airport Experience

## A first for U.S. airports. A game-changer for travelers everywhere.


At CLEAR, we believe the future of travel should be predictable, trusted, and easy. That’s why we’re proud to launch **eGates** —cutting-edge innovation that enhances the passenger experience, coming to U.S. airports *for the first time ever* .


American travelers deserve the best possible journey through the airport. With[eGates](https://www.clearme.com/egate) , CLEAR+ Members can now verify instantly and go directly to bag screening, unlocking a truly frictionless path from curb to gate. CLEAR eGates represent the latest advancement in our mission to transform the way people move through airports and beyond.


Travel is hard and getting harder—[by 2030 there will be another million passengers coming through U.S. airports](https://www.clearme.com/blog/travel-is-on-the-rise-and-so-is-innovation) . CLEAR is obsessed with the Member experience and helping you win the day of travel—because the journey should be as enjoyable as the destination.


‍


## A New Era of Frictionless Travel


This isn’t just an upgrade—it’s a complete reinvention of the airport experience. Members can now verify instantly and go directly to bag screening with our privacy centric, opt-in eGates.

## World-Class Security Meets U.S. Innovation


Already in airports around the world, the launch of our eGates in the U.S. will enhance security across CLEAR’s nationwide network. With the U.S. preparing to welcome millions of visitors for the upcoming World Cup and Olympic Games, this innovation is happening at the perfect time, enabling U.S. airports to meet the moment with trust and predictability.

> *“We are obsessed with the Member experience, which is why we’re bringing eGates to U.S. airports for the first time ever—an innovative, automated technology that strengthens security and enhances the customer journey \[...\] Because with CLEAR, you should feel unstoppable.”*

## How It Works


To use eGates, you can simply:


1. **Scan your boarding pass** when you enter the[CLEAR+](https://www.clearme.com/clear-plus) Lane.


2. **Walk straight up to the eGate** and verify with your face—instantly and without breaking your stride.


3. **Go directly to bag screening** .
