## U.S. Winter Wonderlands

A few U.S. destinations stand out as the ultimate December escapes.


### Aspen, Colorado – Skiing and Luxury Living


Few destinations capture the magic of the winter months like Aspen. Known for its world-class ski resorts, Aspen combines high-energy outdoor activities with luxurious dining and après-ski culture. Whether you’re hitting the slopes, enjoying a day trip to the nearby hot springs, or strolling through a winter wonderland downtown, Aspen always delivers.

New York in December is iconic. From ice skating at Rockefeller Center to exploring Christmas markets in Bryant Park and Union Square, it’s a holiday dream. The city also offers plenty of family-friendly activities, like Broadway shows or a visit to one of its many museums.

### Yellowstone National Park – Outdoor Adventures in the Snow


If you’re craving rugged beauty, Yellowstone in December is unforgettable. Snow blankets the park, wildlife is more active, and outdoor activities like cross-country skiing or guided snow coach tours make it a bucket list winter getaway.

## Warm-Weather Escapes


Not a fan of the cold? Choose one of these warm-weather escapes instead.


### Maui, Hawaii – Beaches and Outdoor Activities


If winter isn’t your season, Hawaii offers a sunshine-filled alternative. Maui in December boasts warm beaches, scenic drives along the Pacific Coast, and family-friendly snorkeling trips. It’s a popular month for humpback whale watching too—a truly magical experience.

### Miami, Florida – Sunshine and Culture


Miami blends beach weather with vibrant culture, making it one of the best places to visit in December if you want to stay close to home. The art scene, nightlife, and Cuban cuisine make it a dynamic getaway, well beyond the beach. Throw in outdoor adventures, like kayaking in Biscayne Bay, and you’ve got the perfect winter escape.


### Los Cabos, Mexico – Relaxation and Adventure


Just a short flight from many U.S. cities, Los Cabos is a prime December destination. Think luxury resorts, hot springs, and outdoor activities ranging from whale watching to ziplining. It’s also an excellent choice for family-friendly vacations with kid-approved resorts and tranquil beaches.

## European Winter Charm


Europe provides some of the most magical places to explore in December. Travelers looking for history, tradition, and holiday charm can't go wrong with the destinations below.


### Vienna, Austria – Iconic Christmas Markets


If your dream December trip involves twinkling lights and holiday festivities, Vienna definitely delivers. The city is famous for its Christmas markets, mulled wine, and charming architecture. The festive atmosphere makes it one of the most magical international trips you can take during this time of year.


### Lapland, Finland – Chasing the Northern Lights


Lapland is the ultimate winter wonderland where you can visit Santa’s Village, go dog sledding, or take in the northern lights. For any traveler in search of true holiday spirit, this is one of the most memorable destinations in the world.


### Prague, Czech Republic – Culture and Winter Magic


Prague in December feels like a city straight out of a storybook. Between gothic architecture, lively Christmas markets, and cozy cafes, it’s a perfect mix of history and holiday cheer.

## Family-Friendly Picks for December


If you’re traveling with kids, these destinations combine festive fun with family-friendly activities for everyone to enjoy.


### Gaylord Opryland Resort in Nashville, Tennessee


If you’re looking for a festive destination perfect for families, the Gaylord Opryland Resort is a must. The hotel transforms into a sprawling winter wonderland with dazzling lights, holiday shows, and plenty of kid-friendly activities. Families can enjoy twinkling indoor gardens, themed riverboat rides, ice skating, and even snow tubing without ever leaving the resort. It’s an easy choice if you’re after holiday cheer and convenient indoor fun, all in one place.


### Orlando, Florida – Theme Park Magic


For families with young kids, Orlando is always a top contender. December adds holiday decorations, themed parades, and fireworks to the already impressive lineup of theme parks.

### Quebec City, Canada – Old-World Holiday Charm

Quebec City is home to winter festivals and family-friendly outdoor adventures, such as sledding and snowtubing.
