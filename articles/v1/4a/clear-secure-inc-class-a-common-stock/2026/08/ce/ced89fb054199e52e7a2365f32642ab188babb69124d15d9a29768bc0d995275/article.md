# CLEAR Airport Guide: Boston Logan International Airport

Boston Logan International Airport (BOS), commonly known as Boston Logan, is New England's largest airport and the primary hub serving Boston and the surrounding region.


From local dining and New England-inspired shopping to exhibits celebrating the region's history, here's everything you need to turn your travel day into a great day at Boston Logan.


‍


## Why Fly Through Boston Logan?


Boston connects travelers to destinations across North America, Europe, Latin America, and beyond, while offering one of the country's most convenient airport locations. Just a few miles from downtown Boston, it's easy to reach whether you're visiting the city or connecting to other parts of the world.


The airport's four terminals serve a mix of domestic and international airlines, offering many nonstop destinations while remaining easy to navigate compared to many major U.S. airports.
‍


> **Did You Know?** Much of Boston Logan sits on land reclaimed from Boston Harbor. Since opening in 1923, the airport has expanded well beyond its original footprint to become New England's busiest airport.


‍


## Experience the Best of New England at Boston Logan


Boston Logan is more than a place to catch a flight—it's an introduction to New England. From fresh seafood and local breweries to exhibits celebrating the region's history and culture, the airport offers a glimpse of what makes New England unique.


### Where to Eat at Boston Logan


Boston's food scene is built on fresh seafood, neighborhood institutions, and local breweries. You can experience all three and more before your flight. Our CLEAR Ambassadors share their favorite local spots below.


You'll find Legal Sea Foods in Terminals A, B, C, and E, making it easy to enjoy New England seafood no matter where you're flying from. Order a bowl of their award-winning clam chowder or a classic lobster roll before you take off.


In Terminal A, pair a local craft beer at Harpoon Tap Room with a classic lobster roll from Roger Fish Co. If you're departing from Terminal B, stop by Cisco Brewers or Kelly's Roast Beef, one of the Boston area's most iconic sandwich shops.


Travelers in Terminal C can choose between Davio's Northern Italian Steakhouse for an upscale meal or Santarpio's Pizza, one of East Boston's best-known pizzerias. Before an international flight from Terminal E, enjoy locally inspired cocktails at Boston Harbor Distillery or visit Monica's Mercato, an outpost of the beloved North End specialty market.


No trip to Boston feels complete without Dunkin'. With locations before and after airport security in every terminal, it's an easy stop for coffee, breakfast, or a donut before your flight.


### Where to Shop at Boston Logan


Looking for a last-minute gift or souvenir?


Browse New England Collections in Terminal A for regional keepsakes, shop Vineyard Vines in Terminal B for classic New England style, or stop by The Black Dog in Terminal C for apparel and gifts inspired by Martha's Vineyard.


‍


## Make the Most of Your Time at Boston Logan


Whether you have extra time before boarding or several hours between flights, Boston Logan offers plenty to explore.


### Before You Board


Boston Logan features stores and exhibits that celebrate New England's history, culture, sports, and aviation.


Sports fans can explore exhibits that showcase Boston's legendary teams and athletes in Terminal B, while the Tuskegee Airmen exhibit in Terminal C honors the first Black military aviators in the U.S. Army Air Corps, whose service helped shape aviation history. In Terminal E, discover the Hall of Flags, where more than 50 flags represent countries with nonstop service to and from Boston, and Logan 100, an exhibit celebrating the airport's first century and its role in connecting New England to the world.


If you have extra time before boarding, Terminal E offers sweeping views of Boston Harbor, the downtown skyline, and arriving aircraft.
‍


> **Did You Know?** American flags fly outside Gate B32 in Terminal B and Gate C19 in Terminal C, marking the departure gates of American Airlines Flight 11 and United Airlines Flight 175 on September 11, 2001. The flags serve as a lasting tribute to the passengers and crews who lost their lives that day.


### Extra Time at Boston Logan


If you have an hour or more before boarding, consider exploring another terminal to discover additional New England restaurants, regional retailers, and airport exhibits. The free Massport shuttle makes it easy to travel between terminals before airport security, while Terminals A and B are connected by pedestrian walkways.


### What to Do During a Long Layover


Boston Logan is just a few miles from downtown Boston, making it one of the easiest major airports for exploring the city during a layover.


If you have several hours to spare, the MBTA Silver Line SL1 provides free service from the airport to South Station, where you can connect to many of Boston's most popular neighborhoods and attractions. Just be sure to allow enough time to return, get through airport security, and reach your gate.


### Tips for Tight Connection


If you're connecting to another flight, check your departure gate after landing.


Your next flight may depart from a different terminal. While some terminals are connected by pedestrian walkways, others require the free Massport shuttle.

### Accessibility at Boston Logan


Boston Logan offers wheelchair assistance, family restrooms, nursing rooms, service animal relief areas, and additional accessibility services throughout the airport. Visit the airport’s[official accessibility page](https://www.massport.com/logan-airport/at-the-airport/ada-compliance-and-accessibility) for the latest information, and contact your airline before you travel if you need accessibility assistance.

#### Hidden Disabilities Sunflower


Boston Logan is a proud member of the Hidden Disabilities Sunflower program. Travelers with non-visible disabilities, including autism, dementia, anxiety, or chronic pain, can request a Sunflower lanyard as a discreet way to let airport staff know they may benefit from additional support, assistance, or simply a little more time during their journey.


For more information or to request a lanyard, visit an airport information booth to connect with Customer Service through the Virtual Assistant or call 1-800-23-LOGAN.


‍


#### Sensory and Accessibility Support


Travelers with sensory sensitivities can use the airport's Sensory/Calming Room in Terminal E, located post-security near Gate E15. The space includes calming, tactile features and an airplane simulation room to help passengers prepare for the travel experience.


Boston Logan also partners with[Aira](https://aira.io/) , a free visual interpreting service that connects travelers who are blind or have low vision with trained live agents through a mobile app or assistive smart glasses. Agents can provide step-by-step guidance from the curb through check-in, airport security, and all the way to your gate.


Travelers who are deaf, hard of hearing, or have difficulty speaking can access relay services through the Voice Massachusetts Relay System, and TTY assistance is also available through the Boston Logan Call Center.

#### BOS PAWS


Need a moment to relax before your flight?[BOS PAWS](https://www.massport.com/logan-airport/at-the-airport/bos-paws) , Boston Logan's therapy dog program in partnership with Alliance of Therapy Dogs, brings certified therapy dogs into the terminals to help reduce travel stress. You might meet Belle the Great Pyrenees, Campbell the Labrador Retriever, Remi the Giant Schnauzer, Waffles the French Bulldog, or one of the other friendly members of the BOS PAWS crew.

## Navigating Boston Logan


### Know Your Terminal


Boston Logan has four terminals: A, B, C, and E. Terminal A primarily serves Delta Air Lines, Terminal B serves American Airlines, United Airlines, Southwest Airlines, and Air Canada, Terminal C is home to JetBlue and several regional carriers, while Terminal E handles most international arrivals and departures.


Gate assignments occasionally change, so check Boston Logan's[official airline directory](https://www.massport.com/logan-airport/flights/airlines) before your trip to confirm your departure terminal.


### Moving Between Terminals


The free[Massport shuttle](https://www.massport.com/logan-airport/getting-to-logan/on-airport-shuttle) connects all four terminals, the Rental Car Center, Airport Station on the MBTA Blue Line, and the airport's water transportation dock.


Terminals A and B are connected by pedestrian walkways before airport security. Other terminal transfers require the Massport shuttle. If you leave the secure area to change terminals, you'll need to clear airport security again before heading to your gate.


### Airport Security


Each terminal has its own airport security checkpoint, and wait times vary throughout the day.
