# CLEAR Travel Tips: Planning Travel for Your Destination Wedding

Planning a destination wedding means coordinating more than the ceremony itself. Guests may be flying in from different cities, arriving at different times, and staying at different hotels. Organizing travel ahead of time for your guests helps reduce last-minute questions and keeps the focus on celebrating this magical moment.


‍


## Book Flights and Hotels Early


Giving guests plenty of notice about wedding details makes it easier for them to compare flights, book accommodations, and plan their travel days.


As soon as details are confirmed, share:


- Wedding date and time
- Rehearsal dinner schedule
- Welcome party or group events
- Share recommendations for hotels or room blocks
- Hotel booking deadlines
- Convenient airports


If you've reserved a hotel room block, include the reservation deadline and booking instructions. If you're recommending multiple hotels, consider offering options at different price points so guests can choose accommodations that fit their budget.


‍


## Coordinate Arrival Plans


Guests don't need to all book the same flights—focus on helping everyone arrive at a time that works for them while making sure they can attend wedding events.


Share recommended arrival dates, the closest airport, and transportation options from the airport to the hotel or venue. This gives guests flexibility while helping the weekend stay on schedule.


‍


## Keep Travel Information in One Place


As guests begin booking travel, here are some common questions:


- Which hotel should I book?
- Which airport should I fly into?
- How do I get to the venue?
- When should I arrive?


Instead of answering each question individually, create one central hub where guests can find all relevant information.


A wedding website or shared planning document can include:


- Hotel information
- Airport transportation options
- Event schedule
- Dress codes
- Maps and directions
- Emergency contact information

## Keep Your Wedding Party Connected


Plans can change quickly—a dedicated group chat can serve as one place for your wedding party to share real-time updates.


Use the group chat to communicate:


- Flight delays or schedule changes
- Airport arrival times
- Transportation updates
- Meeting locations
- Last-minute schedule changes


Keeping everyone in one conversation reduces confusion and prevents important updates from getting lost across multiple text threads.


‍


## Pack Important Items Across Multiple Bags


If several members of the wedding party are traveling together, avoid putting every essential item into one checked bag.


Whenever possible, divide important items across multiple carry-ons, including:


- Wedding attire
- Rings
- Marriage license
- Vows
- Shoes and accessories
- Small décor items


If one checked bag is delayed, you'll still have access to many of the items you need for the weekend.

## Plan Airport and Ground Transportation


Help everyone start the weekend with confidence by sharing the following details:


- Hotel shuttle availability
- Rental car recommendations
- Rideshare pickup locations
- Group transportation between events


If your destination has more than one airport, let guests know which airport is closest to the hotel or venue. If transportation will be provided for wedding events, include pickup times and locations in your travel information.


‍


## Help Your Guests Prepare for Their Travel Days


A few reminders before departure can help guests avoid common travel day surprises.


Encourage everyone to:


- Have their boarding passes ready
- Turn on flight notifications
- Review baggage policies before packing
