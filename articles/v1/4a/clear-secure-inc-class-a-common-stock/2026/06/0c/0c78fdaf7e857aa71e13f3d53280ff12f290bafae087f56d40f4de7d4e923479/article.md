# CLEAR eGates Are Strengthening Security

## CLEAR is the Standard for Trusted Identity


The celebration of America’s 250th birthday will bring record-breaking travel volume over the holiday weekend, and as more than 11 million travelers have experienced CLEAR’s secure eGates, we are proud to be strengthening security and enhancing the travel experience across America.


CLEAR eGates fortify security, unlock massive operational efficiency, and delight travelers by seamlessly integrating high-assurance identity verification, real-time authentication and physical access management.


Air travel is at record highs with TSA screening roughly 3 million travelers every day with the expectation of that figure increasing to 4 million travelers a day over the next few years. As the threat environment continues to evolve, the need to strengthen security while simultaneously driving throughput is paramount.


CLEAR is a trusted U.S. secure identity company with decades of experience in secure biometric identity and access solutions. Operating in **172** TSA checkpoints across **65** major U.S. airports and serving more than **41** **million** Members nationwide, CLEAR has a strong public private partnership with DHS and is a SAFETY Act Certified Qualified Anti-Terrorism Technology.


Opt-in is core to this privacy protected identity platform enabling members to use their biometrics to instantly and securely authenticate who they are: unlocking faster, safer access in high-stakes environments where trust and safety matters most.


##
eGates Make Experiences Safer and Easier


CLEAR eGates have verified more than **11 million** traveler verifications across **48** airports with zero security issues since eGates were first deployed in August 2025. Checkpoint capacity in airports with eGates has increased by ~50k travelers per day, TSA labor efficiency has increased up to 25%, and traveler experience scores have increased by 14 points.


This technology scales rapidly: single, double, and triple eGates yield 250–750 travelers per hour as biometric verification takes **less than 5 seconds** . eGates serve as a major security force multiplier—allowing TSA officers to focus on other mission critical screening tasks. To date, eGate deployment has saved more than **59,000 TSO hours** , alleviating frontline fatigue and helping the TSA maximize the utility of its existing checkpoint footprint.


Travelers are thrilled as they are benefitting from a faster, more seamless experience, contributing to customer satisfaction scores at eGate-enabled checkpoints that consistently exceed a **95 Lane Experience Score** . Travel is a major contributor to the US economy with 1 in 10 jobs connected to travel—making travel safer and easier is a major win for the US economy and our competitive positioning in the world.


CLEAR has deployed eGates at zero cost to the American taxpayer, demonstrating the value of public-private partnerships to drive much needed modernization in an era of government funding shortfalls and shutdowns.


##
eGates Make Travel More Secure


CLEAR’s eGates strengthen airport security by integrating high-assurance identity verification into TSA’s system, combining multi-layered biometric authentication with automated physical barriers to deliver the highest level of checkpoint security.


- CLEAR’s identity proofing and authentication processes meet the highest industry and government standards set in the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Digital Identity Guidelines.
- Identities are corroborated against the issuing source to meet or exceed Identity Assurance Level 2 (IAL2) verification standards for strong identity validation and credential-to-user binding. CLEAR is the only company in aviation today that source-corroborates digital identities against U.S. Passports and state-issued IDs.
- These controls reduce the risk of impersonation, synthetic identities, credential misuse, and unauthorized checkpoint access.


CLEAR eGates enforce a strict defense-in-depth security model that combines:


- Source corroboration of government-issued ID
- Biometric identity verification
- Certified liveness and anti-spoofing protections
- Encrypted communications and secure U.S. hosted infrastructure
- Real-time operational monitoring
- Human oversight and escalation controls
- Robust operational and technical assessments by third parties


Physical credentials are no longer enough. As artificial intelligence makes it easier to create deepfakes, synthetic identities, and sophisticated impersonation attacks, trusted identity has become foundational to national security. The ability to verify that a person is truly who they claim to be—in real time, with high assurance—is essential to protecting critical infrastructure, transportation systems, and the traveling public. Identity is security.


CLEAR eGates are a great public private partnership and are raising the standard for aviation security: combining biometric authentication, source-corroborated identity verification, and layered security controls to help TSA stay ahead of emerging threats and delivering a faster and safer experience at scale to all travelers.
