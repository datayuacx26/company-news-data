## Key Takeaways: The Difference Between Manufactured and Modular Homes


-


Manufactured and modular homes are both prefabricated homes built in an indoor factory setting.


-


Both types of homes are built in modules in the factory, delivered to the final home site, and then installed on the property.


-


The main difference between manufactured and modular homes is the codes they’re built to.[Manufactured homes](https://www.championhomes.com/blog/can-i-customize-a-manufactured-home) are built to the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) Code, and modular homes are built to state codes, such as the International Residential Code, or IRC.

## Manufactured Homes


[Manufactured homes](https://www.championhomes.com/blog/top-4-manufactured-homes-with-small-floorplans) are built in an indoor factory setting protected from the elements, reducing the impact of weather-related delays so you can move in faster.


You may have heard[manufactured homes](https://www.championhomes.com/blog/figuring-out-where-to-put-a-manufactured-home) referred to as “prefabricated homes.” They’re built in sections and then delivered to the home site, where they’re installed and ready for move-in.

### The Difference Between Manufactured Homes and Mobile Homes


You’ve likely heard[manufactured homes](https://www.championhomes.com/blog/what-should-i-research-before-buying-a-manufactured-home) referred to as mobile homes in your day-to-day conversations. Before 1976 when the United States Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) code was developed, they were referred to as mobile homes.


After the HUD code was developed, they were and still are referred to as manufactured homes.

## Modular Homes


Just like manufactured homes,[modular homes](https://www.championhomes.com/blog/how-does-the-modular-home-installation-process-work) are built in sections in an indoor factory setting, then delivered to the final property for installation.


[Modular homes](https://www.championhomes.com/blog/how-do-i-find-land-for-a-modular-home) are another type of prefabricated home. The difference is that modular homes are built to state codes, such as the International Residential Code, or IRC.


In some ways,[modular homes](https://www.championhomes.com/blog/what-do-i-expect-when-i-visit-a-modular-home-retailer) are more comparable to site-built homes because of the code they’re built to, and[modular homes](https://www.championhomes.com/blog/how-do-i-finance-a-modular-home) are typically available for traditional mortgage financing.

## Similarities & Differences Between Manufactured and Modular Homes


The main similarity you’ll find between manufactured and[modular homes](https://www.championhomes.com/blog/what-should-i-research-before-buying-a-modular-home) is that they’re both built in an indoor factory environment by skilled craftspeople.


The key difference you’ll find between manufactured and[modular homes](https://www.championhomes.com/blog/can-i-customize-a-modular-home) is the codes they’re built to. The HUD code can differ from the code of a specific state, town, or county, and this all helps to determine what kind of house can be built and where.
