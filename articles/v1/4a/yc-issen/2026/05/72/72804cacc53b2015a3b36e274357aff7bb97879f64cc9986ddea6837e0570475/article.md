## What Are AI Voice Tutors


An AI voice tutor is a speaking-first language tool that listens to you, talks back in real time, and runs an actual conversation in your target language.[Speech recognition technology](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/speech-recognition) picks up what you say, voice generation responds, and the whole exchange happens out loud instead of through typing or multiple-choice taps.


That distinction matters because most apps train the wrong muscle. You build reading comprehension, collect vocabulary, finish a lesson tree, and then a colleague asks you a question in Spanish and your mouth stops working.


Voice tutors target that gap. You practice retrieving words while a clock is ticking and someone is waiting for your reply.

### Can I practice speaking while commuting or walking?

### Which voice tutor works best for advanced learners who plateau on gamified apps?
