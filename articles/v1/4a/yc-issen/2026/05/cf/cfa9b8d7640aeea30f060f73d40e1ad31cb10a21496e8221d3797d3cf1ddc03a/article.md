## What are AI language tutors for conversation practice


An AI language tutor is a voice-driven program that holds spoken conversations with you in your target language, in real time. You talk, it listens, it responds, fast enough to feel like a phone call.[Research on AI language learning](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/29984475.2026.2647961) shows these tools fill a gap traditional apps leave open.


Traditional apps were built around tapping vocabulary cards and grammar drills. Useful early on. Limited once you can read at an intermediate level but freeze the moment someone asks you a question out loud.[Research shows AI-based instruction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10652775/) can improve speaking skills where traditional methods fall short.


That freeze is the gap most learners hit. Conversation practice only builds through speaking, and most apps quietly skip it. AI tutors fill that hole with a patient partner available whenever you want.

## FAQ


### Which AI language tutor is best for practicing while commuting or doing chores?


ISSEN is the only tutor in this list with true Background Mode, which means you can practice hands-free while walking, driving, or cooking. The others require you to hold your phone and look at the screen.


### How do I choose between a tutor with more languages and one with better conversation features?


If your target language is covered by multiple tutors, pick based on features you'll use daily: real-time voice quality, whether the tutor remembers context across sessions, and whether you can practice hands-free. Coverage matters less than the conversation experience in the language you're actually learning.


### Can AI tutors handle when I accidentally switch back to my native language mid-sentence?


ISSEN's tutors are multilingual and can understand when you code-switch, then gently steer you back to your target language. Most other tutors treat mixed-language input as an error or ignore it entirely.


### Which tutor works best if I can read at an intermediate level but freeze when speaking?


ISSEN and Talkpal both support open conversation practice at intermediate levels. ISSEN pulls ahead with faster response times and the ability to drill pronunciation separately in Shadowing mode, while Talkpal offers structured roleplay scenarios at a lower price point.


### How much does an AI tutor cost compared to a human tutor?


AI tutors run $8 to $29 USD per month for unlimited practice. A human tutor on italki or Preply costs $15 to $30 per hour, which means two sessions per month already match the cost of most AI subscriptions.
