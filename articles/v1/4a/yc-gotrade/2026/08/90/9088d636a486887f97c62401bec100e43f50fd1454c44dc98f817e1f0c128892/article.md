# Oil Sinks ~5%, Dow Jumps 600+ as Trump Halts Iran Strike

Gotrade News - After President Donald Trump called off a planned strike on Iran and moved to resume negotiations, oil prices plummeted by about 5% and the Dow Jones Industrial Average surged by more than 600 points, relieving weeks of geopolitical risk priced into crude. Trump stated that any agreement would include the "Immediate, Complete, and Total OPENING OF THE HORMUZ STRAIT," with negotiations to resume later in the day, according to[Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/commodities-news/oil-drops-to-3wk-low-as-trump-calls-off-iran-strike-announces-fresh-talks-4829733) . As investors repriced the likelihood of a wider Middle East conflict, the reversal drove the war premium out of energy markets and caused stocks to soar.


## The Hormuz Premium and Oil's ~5% Slide


Crude saw a significant decline on both benchmarks. According to[Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/commodities-news/oil-drops-to-3wk-low-as-trump-calls-off-iran-strike-announces-fresh-talks-4829733) , WTI crude fell 5.8% to $79.75 per barrel, a three-week low, while Brent October futures fell 4.9% to $83.66 per barrel. According to the same report, prior U.S. strikes had targeted shipping lanes in the Red Sea and the Strait of Hormuz, maintaining a supply-disruption premium in the market until Trump announced a shift to diplomacy.


Much of a summer rally based on fears of conflict was eliminated by the action. WTI was last quoted at $79.77 (down 5.88%) and Brent at $83.47 (down 5.07%), both benchmarks having risen more than 20% in July due to U.S.-Iran hostilities and a Houthi port blockade, according to[OilPrice.com](https://oilprice.com/Latest-Energy-News/World-News/Oil-Prices-Plunge-5-as-Trump-Halts-Iran-Strike-Plans.html) . Because of that run-up, crude was open to the exact type of de-escalation headline that came in, and traders quickly unwound their positions.
