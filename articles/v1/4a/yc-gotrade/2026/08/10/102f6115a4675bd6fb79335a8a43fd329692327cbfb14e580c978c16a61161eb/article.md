## Tonight's Catalysts 🧨


### AMD's Q2 print and the data-center engine


**AMD ([AMD](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/amd/) )** puts its growth engine in the data-center segment, from EPYC server processors to Instinct AI accelerators. Investors will hunt for data-center revenue and gross margin, with the roughly 56% full-year gross-margin target under pressure from the Helios ramp. MI350 shipments and evidence of Helios rack orders will show whether the AI momentum is real.


### Read-through to Nvidia and the chip supply chain


As a bellwether, AMD's result often carries into **Nvidia ([NVDA](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/nvda/) )** , **Broadcom ([AVGO](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/avgo/) )** , and memory suppliers like **Micron ([MU](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/mu/) )** . A strong demand signal can lift the entire AI-infrastructure complex, while any caution on pricing or supply can pressure the sector quickly.
