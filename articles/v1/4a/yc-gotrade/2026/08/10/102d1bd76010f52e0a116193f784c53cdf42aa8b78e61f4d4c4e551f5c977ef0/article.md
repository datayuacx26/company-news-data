# Gotrade Daily: July CPI Lands Tonight, Cisco Earnings Follow

### Cisco, the session's opening earnings act


Cisco reports fourth-quarter fiscal 2026 results after the close, roughly 3:00am WIB. As one of the largest networking infrastructure providers, its numbers offer an early read on whether enterprise tech spending is still leaning into AI workloads. Reaction to this print often spills into other large-cap tech names once US markets reopen.


### Middle East tension, an overlay worth tracking


Beyond the two scheduled catalysts, geopolitical risk around the Strait of Hormuz continues to weigh on oil prices and safe-haven assets like gold. It isn't a dated catalyst like CPI or an earnings report, but it remains worth watching given how crowded tonight's session already is.


## Pre-Market Pulse 📊


Ahead of the open, major US index futures are trading in a tight range as the market waits out the CPI release an hour before the bell.


Large-cap tech and rate-sensitive names are the most closely watched heading into the print, while any meaningful move in Cisco or Nebius will only show up once both reports land after hours.


## Macro Note 📝


Tonight combines two catalyst types that rarely land in the same session: a scheduled macro release (CPI) and large-cap tech earnings (Cisco, Nebius). The CPI print typically sets the session's initial direction, while the two earnings reports tend to shape how the following session plays out.


## Takeaway


Tonight comes down to two questions rather than one: does inflation still leave the Fed room to cut rates, and does AI demand on the networking and cloud side hold up to what the market expects. Both answers only become clear once CPI lands tonight and Cisco's numbers follow in the early hours.


Ahead of a data point this heavy, managing risk matters more than guessing direction before the numbers are out. Watch how the market actually reacts once CPI and earnings land, rather than positioning on speculation beforehand.
