## Tonight's Catalysts 🧨


### Cloudflare is tonight's volatility test


**Cloudflare ([NET](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/net/) )** could swing about 11% after its report, one of the most volatile events tonight. The company has beaten estimates four quarters running. Investors want to see revenue acceleration toward mid-30% growth and proof that demand for its security and AI products stays strong.


### Datadog reads cloud and AI spending


**Datadog ([DDOG](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/ddog/) )** reports earlier, with consensus revenue of $1.08B, up roughly 31% year over year. Because its tools monitor cloud infrastructure, the results are a signal for whether corporate spending on cloud and AI projects is still aggressive or starting to slow.


### The Trade Desk and Atlassian, two sides of software


Not all software is growing at AI-infra speed. **The Trade Desk ([TTD](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/ttd/) )** is seen growing only about 8%, pressured by softer consumer ad spending. **Atlassian ([TEAM](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/team/) )** closes its fiscal year, with focus on forward guidance and AI-feature adoption. The contrast against Cloudflare and Datadog's hypergrowth is tonight's real story.
