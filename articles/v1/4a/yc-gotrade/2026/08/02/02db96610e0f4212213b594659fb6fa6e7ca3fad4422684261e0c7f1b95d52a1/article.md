## Tonight's Catalysts


### The memory and chip rally goes global


The semiconductor rally is now genuinely cross-continental. YMTC overtook Kioxia in NAND shipments for the first time, while Korea's Kospi entered a bull market led by SK Hynix. For US investors, that is a read-through catalyst for **Micron** ([MU](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/mu/) ) and **Western Digital** ([WDC](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/wdc/) ), two memory names that already rallied hard yesterday.


### Cisco's FY27 guide gets tested


Cisco closed up 2.86% yesterday heading into its earnings, with a $7.5 billion AI-infrastructure target and FY27 revenue guidance of $72.2 to $73.4 billion. The report landed after the close, and the early reaction has leaned negative despite the strong-sounding guide, a sign that expectations were already high. Watch whether this level finds buyers once the regular session opens.
