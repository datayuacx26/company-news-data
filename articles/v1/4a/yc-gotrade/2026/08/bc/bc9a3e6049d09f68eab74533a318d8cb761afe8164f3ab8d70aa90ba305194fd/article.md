## Tonight's Catalysts


### Cloudflare and Twilio, the winning face of software


**Cloudflare ([NET](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/net/) )** leapt about 17% to a record after solid full-year and current-quarter guidance, signaling demand for its security and AI products stays strong. **Twilio ([TWLO](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/twlo/) )** followed up roughly 16%, lifted by a revenue beat and voice-AI momentum. Both prove the software theme still has clear winners.


### The Trade Desk craters, ad-tech wobbles


On the other side, **The Trade Desk ([TTD](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/ttd/) )** sank 22% to 26% after hours. Next-quarter revenue guidance came in around $650M, well below the ~$751M hoped for, alongside an EPS miss and a simultaneous CFO and CMO reshuffle. A sign that digital-ad spending and ad-tech growth may be cooling.


### Jobs report sets the tape tonight


The July US nonfarm payrolls report lands before the US market open, with consensus near 83,000 jobs added and a 4.2% unemployment rate. It matters because the Fed held rates last month with three officials who actually wanted a hike. A hot print could revive hike talk, a weak one sharpens the hold-or-cut debate.
