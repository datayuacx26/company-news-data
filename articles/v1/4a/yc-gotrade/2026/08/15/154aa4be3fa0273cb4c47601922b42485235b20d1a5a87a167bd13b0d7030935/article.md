## Tonight's Catalysts


### CoreWeave's profitability test


CoreWeave (CRWV) reports Q2 2026 after the close. Wall Street expects revenue near $2.55 billion and a loss of about $1.23 per share. The focus is the order backlog, which reached $99.4 billion, and whether surging GPU demand keeps converting into revenue growth and a clearer path to profit. The stock carries a Moderate Buy consensus.


### Super Micro and the margin rebound


Super Micro (SMCI) reports Q4 fiscal 2026. The company guided adjusted gross margin to a range of 15% to 17%, a sharp improvement from the 8.2% to 8.4% projected back in May. Its backlog hit a record with more than $60 billion in new orders during the quarter.[DELL](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/dell/) is the closest read-through as a fellow server vendor.


### Chips coiled ahead of CPI


AI hardware names advanced on Monday, with Dell up about 3% and the AI-server complex broadly higher into earnings. The move is fragile, though, with inflation data landing tomorrow.
