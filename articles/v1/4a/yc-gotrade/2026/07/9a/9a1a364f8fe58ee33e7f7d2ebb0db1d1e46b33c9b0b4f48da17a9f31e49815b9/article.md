## Tonight's Catalysts **🧨**


### AI-spending fears: Tesla and Alphabet


**Tesla ([TSLA](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/tsla/) )** led the drop after Q2 free cash flow swung negative to -$1.1B, its first negative reading in over two years. Thin operating margins pushed several analysts to cut price targets. **Alphabet ([GOOGL](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/googl/) )** posted strong revenue, but a jump in AI capital spending left investors worried about future cash flow.


### Oil tops $100 and energy stocks


Oil surged on Middle East escalation, including Houthi attacks on tankers in the Red Sea. Brent held above $100 and WTI traded near $92. **Chevron ([CVX](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/cvx/) )** and **Occidental ([OXY](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/oxy/) )** each rose more than 2% pre-market as direct beneficiaries.


### Schlumberger earnings today


**Schlumberger ([SLB](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/slb/) )** reports Q2 before the US open. Analysts expect EPS of $0.52, down about 30% year over year, on revenue near $8.67B. Management had flagged that Middle East disruptions would weigh on the quarter. As an oilfield services provider, SLB is a barometer for how the oil spike feeds through.
