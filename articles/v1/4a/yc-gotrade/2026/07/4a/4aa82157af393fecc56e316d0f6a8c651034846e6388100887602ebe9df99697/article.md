# Apple Overtakes Nvidia as World's Most Valuable Company, Closing In on $5 Trillion

> "Once criticized for not spending more on AI, they have been able to avoid some of those capex pitfalls," said Jay Woods, Chief Market Strategist at Freedom Capital Markets.


## Rotation From AI Compute to Consumer AI


The change at the top also says something about how investors now value AI. According to[Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/apple-overtakes-nvidia-worlds-most-175312095.html) , money is shifting away from the chips and hardware that drove Nvidia's rise and toward companies putting AI directly in front of consumers. Apple fits that second group. Its devices already reach billions of people, so it can roll out AI features without building expensive new data centers. Nvidia's 7% gain this year next to Apple's 22% is the plainest sign of that move. The compute story is not finished, but for now investors are paying less of a premium for growth that leans on heavy spending.


## Apple Approaches $5 Trillion Before Thursday Earnings


On July 27, 2026, Apple climbed as high as roughly $4.944 trillion during the trading day, still short of the $5 trillion mark, according to[9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/27/apple-reclaims-most-valuable-company-title-from-nvidia-as-it-barrels-toward-5t/) . Nvidia had already passed $5 trillion before that, the first company ever to do it. So the two are now sitting right next to each other at the very top of the market. The next big test comes Thursday, when Apple reports earnings. It will be Tim Cook's last earnings call before he steps down as CEO on September 1, 2026, according to[Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/apple-overtakes-nvidia-as-most-valuable-company-as-iphone-maker-avoids-capex-pitfalls-150316032.html) . A strong set of numbers could push Apple past $5 trillion. A weak one could just as easily hand the lead back to Nvidia.
