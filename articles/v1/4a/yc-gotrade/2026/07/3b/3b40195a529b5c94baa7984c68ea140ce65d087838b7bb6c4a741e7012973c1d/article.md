# Stocks Open Higher as Chips Rebound, Megacap Earnings Loom

## Key Takeaways


-


S&P 500, Nasdaq, and Dow all opened in the green Monday, with semis doing most of the work.


- Nvidia, AMD, Intel, and Micron all clawed back after last week's rout.


- Tesla and Alphabet headline a packed Q2 earnings slate that kicks off Wednesday.


## Chipmakers Lead the Rebound: NVDA, AMD and MU Climb at the Open


Semiconductors did the heavy lifting.[Nvidia (NVDA)](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/nvda/) picked up 1.74% to $206.33.[AMD](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/amd/) ran harder, up 3.49% to $513.07 as the market kept chewing on its new Helios rack partnership with Microsoft Azure (per[Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/wall-st-opens-higher-as-chips-recover-megacap-earnings-loom-4801154) ). Memory was where the real action lived. Micron popped 4.28% to $885.33, Intel tacked on 3.68% to $98.54, and SanDisk led everyone with a 5.57% jump to $1,430.32.

## Tesla and Alphabet Headline a Packed Q2 Earnings Week


The bounce sets up a make-or-break stretch for the AI trade.[Barchart's](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3361032/option-volatility-and-earnings-report-for-july-20-24) options data has[Tesla (TSLA)](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/tsla/) and Alphabet both due Wednesday, July 22. The options market is bracing for a swing of about 7% on Tesla and 6.6% on Alphabet. Same day: IBM (implied move around 6.8%) and ServiceNow, where the tape is pricing in a hefty 12.5% jolt. Then Intel closes out the week on Thursday carrying the biggest implied vol of the bunch 14.9%.
