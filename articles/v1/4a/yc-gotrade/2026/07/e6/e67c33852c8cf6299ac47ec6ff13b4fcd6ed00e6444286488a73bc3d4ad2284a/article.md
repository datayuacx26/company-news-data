## Tonight's Catalysts **🧨**


### Tesla and Alphabet open Magnificent Seven earnings


**Tesla ([TSLA](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/tsla/) )** reports tonight after the close. Consensus is around $26.4 billion in revenue and $0.53 EPS, off a record 480,126 Q2 deliveries. The focus now shifts to automotive gross margin and the pace of energy-storage deployment. **Alphabet ([GOOGL](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/googl/) )** follows the same evening, expected to post roughly $116.8 billion in revenue and $2.89 EPS. Investors are watching Google Cloud growth and the scale of AI infrastructure spending.


### A memory-chip rally lifts the market


Memory names led Tuesday's gains. **Micron ([MU](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/mu/) )** surged 12% and Sandisk rose 14%, while **AMD ([AMD](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/amd/) )** added 8% after its AI deal with Microsoft. Super Micro jumped 15%. Morgan Stanley expects memory prices could climb 25% on AI demand, a positive signal for **Nvidia ([NVDA](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/nvda/) )** and the broader chip supply chain.
