### Memory Stocks Rebound


After coming under pressure last week, memory stocks are turning higher. **Micron ([MU](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/mu/) )** rose pre-market as supply-glut fears eased and demand for high-bandwidth memory (HBM) stayed strong. **Nvidia ([NVDA](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/nvda/) )** traded roughly flat and remains the AI bellwether, especially ahead of Microsoft and Meta on Wednesday.
