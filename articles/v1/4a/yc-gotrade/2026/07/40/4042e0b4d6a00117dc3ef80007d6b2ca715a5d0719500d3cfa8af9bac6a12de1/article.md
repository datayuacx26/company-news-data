# Gotrade Daily: AWS Roars Back, Microsoft's Record Day

Amazon added fuel. AWS grew 37% year-on-year, its fastest in 18 quarters, and sales topped $200 billion. Tonight the market digests those reactions while awaiting Exxon and Chevron earnings.

## Tonight's Catalysts 🧨


### AI cloud roars back: Microsoft's record, Amazon's AWS blowout


**Microsoft ([MSFT](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/msft/) )** surged about 16% and added roughly $450 billion in value, driven by Azure growth. **Amazon ([AMZN](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/amzn/) )** followed with AWS up 37%, its fastest in 18 quarters, and sales past $200 billion. Together they reignited the case that the AI trade is far from over.


### Apple lags despite record iPhone sales


**Apple ([AAPL](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/aapl/) )** moved the other way. iPhone sales rose 22% and results set a record, yet the stock fell more than 4% after hours on a weak forward forecast and supply-chain worries. At least one Wall Street firm cut its rating. It is the counterweight to the cloud optimism.
