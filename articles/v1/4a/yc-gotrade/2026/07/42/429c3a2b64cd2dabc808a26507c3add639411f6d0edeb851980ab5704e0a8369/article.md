## Tonight's Catalysts 🧨


### Apple and Amazon report tonight


**Apple ([AAPL](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/aapl/) )** reports after the close. The market is watching iPhone demand, Services growth, and China exposure. **Amazon ([AMZN](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/amzn/) )** follows, with focus on AWS growth, capex for AI and cloud, and retail-business margins. Their results will set the tone for Friday's trading.


### The Fed's hawkish surprise


The Fed held rates but three members voted to hike, and Chair Kevin Warsh signaled a possible September increase. Wall Street reacted negatively, with the Dow falling about 1,155 points. The 10-year Treasury yield rose to around 4.64% and became the main pressure on equities.


### A megacap split and AI memory


**Microsoft ([MSFT](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/msft/) )** surged about 9% after revenue of $90.01B and 43% Azure growth, while **Meta ([META](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/us-stock/meta/) )** fell about 9% on an earnings miss and soft guidance. Separately, Samsung's chip profit soared on AI-memory demand, a positive read-through for Nvidia and Micron.
