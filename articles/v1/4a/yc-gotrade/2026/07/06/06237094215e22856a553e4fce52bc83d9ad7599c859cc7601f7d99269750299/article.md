# Jakarta Composite Sinks 1.88% as Foreigners Sell

## CUAN, ARCI and TINS Lead the Top Losers


According to Katadata, commodity and mining names bore the brunt of the decline, with Petrindo Jaya Kreasi (CUAN), Archi Indonesia (ARCI) and tin miner Timah (TINS) all sliding around 7%.

Banking stocks added to the weight on the index. Bank Mandiri (BMRI) fell 5.71% to Rp4,130 and saw the most trading value of any stock, at Rp1.68 trillion. BBNI and BBRI dropped too, by 2.22% and 1.00%, and only BBCA got through the day unchanged at Rp6,275. Gains were rare. The few that rose, rose sharply. SINI was up 9.93%, MORA 3.94%, and AKR Corporindo (AKRA) 2.48%.


## LQ45 and Islamic Index Also Slide


Other benchmarks fell along with the main index. The LQ45, which tracks the larger blue chips, lost 1.97% to end at 614. The Jakarta Islamic Index dropped a bit more, down 2.41% at 371, and the IDX30 was off 1.86% at 352.
