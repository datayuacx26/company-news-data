# Viatris to Report Second-Quarter 2026 Financial Results on August 6, 2026

PITTSBURGH


, July 1, 2026


/[PRNewswire](http://www.prnewswire.com/) / --[Viatris Inc.](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4723031-1&h=1152704314&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.viatris.com%2Fen&a=Viatris+Inc.) (Nasdaq: VTRS), a global healthcare company, today announced it will report second-quarter 2026 financial results on Thursday, August 6, 2026. Company executives will host a conference call and live webcast at 8:30 a.m. ET on the same date to discuss the results.


Investors and the general public are invited to listen to a live webcast of the call at[investor.viatris.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4723031-1&h=2666050807&u=https%3A%2F%2Fedge.prnewswire.com%2Fc%2Flink%2F%3Ft%3D0%26l%3Den%26o%3D4523504-1%26h%3D2958493950%26u%3Dhttps%253A%252F%252Finvestor.viatris.com%252F%26a%3Dinvestor.viatris.com&a=investor.viatris.com) or by calling 844.308.3344 or 412.317.1896 for international callers. A replay of the webcast also will be available on the website.
