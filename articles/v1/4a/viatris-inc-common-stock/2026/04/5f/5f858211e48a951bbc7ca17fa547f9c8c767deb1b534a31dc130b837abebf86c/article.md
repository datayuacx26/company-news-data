# Viatris to Report First Quarter 2026 Financial Results on May 7, 2026

PITTSBURGH


, April 13, 2026


/[PRNewswire](http://www.prnewswire.com/) / --[Viatris Inc.](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4661739-1&h=1182664824&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.viatris.com%2Fen&a=Viatris+Inc.) (Nasdaq: VTRS), a global healthcare company, today announced it will report first quarter 2026 financial results on Thursday, May 7, 2026. Company executives will host a webcast at 8:30 a.m. ET on the same date to discuss the results.


Investors and the general public are invited to listen to a live webcast of the call at[investor.viatris.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4661739-1&h=2619499445&u=https%3A%2F%2Fedge.prnewswire.com%2Fc%2Flink%2F%3Ft%3D0%26l%3Den%26o%3D4523504-1%26h%3D2958493950%26u%3Dhttps%253A%252F%252Finvestor.viatris.com%252F%26a%3Dinvestor.viatris.com&a=investor.viatris.com) or by calling 844.308.3344 or 412.317.1896 for international callers. A replay of the webcast also will be available on the website.
