# TD announces $250,000 donation in response to tragic events in Tumbler Ridge

Four Canadian organizations dedicated to local crisis and trauma response, including mental health initiatives, to receive funding


TORONTO


, Feb. 13, 2026


/CNW/ - Following the devastating mass shooting in Tumbler Ridge, British Columbia


earlier this week, TD Bank Group (TD) is announcing a donation of CAD$250,000


to support community organizations providing localized assistance. Funding is directed to areas of crisis support services, trauma response, and mental health initiatives.


"Our deepest condolences go out to the families and loved ones of those who lost their lives, to the survivors and entire Peace River


community," said Julie Armour, Senior Vice President and Pacific Region Head, TD Bank Group. "In times of tragedy, access to the right support is critical. Through today's donation, we aim to help organizations provide the care and resources the community needs to begin healing."


TD is making contributions to the following organizations:


- [Canadian Red Cross:](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4619762-1&h=4117490482&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.redcross.ca%2F&a=Canadian+Red+Cross%3A%C2%A0) Tumbler Ridge Tragedy Appeal- a national organization that, through this appeal, supports immediate needs locally by addressing impacts on individuals and families in the community.
- [Foundry B.C.](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4619762-1&h=4062301829&u=https%3A%2F%2Ffoundrybc.ca%2F&a=Foundry+B.C.) - a provincial organization that offers free and confidential mental health and wellness services in British Columbia


for youth ages 12-24 and their families.
- [South Peace Community Resources Society](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4619762-1&h=269397779&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.spcrs.ca%2F&a=South+Peace+Community+Resources+Society) - a local organization serving the region and Tumbler Ridge


, that provides victim services, crisis intervention, and trauma-informed support for families, while also strengthening local service capacity.
- [Kids Help Phone](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4619762-1&h=2580771781&u=https%3A%2F%2Fkidshelpphone.ca%2F&a=Kids+Help+Phone%C2%A0) - a national 24/7 mental health and crisis support service for youth that offers confidential accessible resources during periods of trauma and uncertainty.


In addition to these direct contributions, TD will be accepting donations through our branches, EasyLine and EasyWeb. Within the Bank, colleagues can make direct donations to Kids Help Phone through the Bank's colleague donation portal.
