# Robinhood Markets, Inc. to Present at the Bernstein Strategic Decisions Conference on May 27, 2026

MENLO PARK, Calif., May 20, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Robinhood Markets, Inc. (“Robinhood”) (NASDAQ: HOOD) today announced that it will be participating in the upcoming Bernstein Strategic Decisions Conference on Wednesday, May 27, 2026.


Robinhood’s Chief Financial Officer Shiv Verma is scheduled to present on Wednesday, May 27, 2026, at 4:30 PM ET / 1:30 PM PT. Interested parties may access a live audio webcast of the presentation by visiting[investors.robinhood.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=67kbzReDX-IqNeH5ImX638b5LBsGt6DgVfZhp-JmBZXnOBM6uEu8wFha6UDGpCpJjzoD6-2clwL5gYCQQkOiZlj3FWROTW30vYggaXvr-1E=) . Following the presentation, a recording will be available for replay for at least 90 days on the same website.
