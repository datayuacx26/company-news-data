# PRESS RELEASE: Omoi to Release Legendary Shojo Manga Delinquent Detective: Sukeban Deka in English for the First Time, Adds Dozens of Kodansha Series

**Experience the iconic crime-action series by Shinji Wada, a timeless classic that continues to inspire generations. Coming this fall exclusively on Omoi’s digital platform.**


WASHINGTON, DC—At their industry panel at Otakon 2026, manga platform and publisher Omoi announced that they have licensed *Delinquent Detective: Sukeban Deka* by Shinji Wada for exclusive digital distribution on their platform. Omoi will also add 54 titles from Kodansha, with volumes purchasable through the Omoi platform.


*Delinquent Detective: Sukeban Deka* is a highly influential classic *shojo* manga. Omoi licensed the series from Akita Shoten in Japan via Digital Catapult, and it originally ran in *Hakusensha’s* magazine *Hana to Yume* from 1975 to 1982. Omoi will begin releasing English chapters in Fall 2026, with chapters available exclusively through the Omoi Premium digital subscription. The synopsis is as follows:


The legendary shojo crime thriller Sukeban Deka will be available in English for the very first time, coming this fall exclusively to Omoi!


Saki Asamiya is a fearsome high school delinquent imprisoned in an inescapable juvenile detention center. Her chance at freedom comes when she accepts a deal from the police: infiltrate Japan’s elite schools to investigate crimes the cops can’t touch, and her mother will be spared from execution. Undercover as a regular student, Saki sets off to unmask criminal conspiracies, wielding a custom steel yo-yo as she faces off against corrupt teachers and scheming students. Experience the classic shojo manga that inspired the beloved live action *Sukeban Deka* series and influenced generations of anime and manga, including *Kill la Kill* !


In addition, Omoi announced 54 Kodansha titles arriving on the platform in the coming weeks. Early volumes from six titles — *After the Rain* , *Blood on the Tracks* , *Dororo* , *nichijou* , *Paradise Kiss* , and *Welcome to Demon School! Iruma-kun* — are available today, and the full catalog will be available in the coming weeks. All of these series will be available as volume purchases, with Premium members getting 20% off all volume purchases.
