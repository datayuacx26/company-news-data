# Azuki Launches Individual Chapter Sales with the Addition of 213 Series from KADOKAWA and Square Enix

***The Villainess and Villain Try to Avoid Another Unhappy Ending*** **,** ***The Flower Princess of Sylph*** **, and hundreds more now available via Azuki website and iOS/Android apps**


Digital manga service Azuki has added 213 new manga series from manga publisher KADOKAWA and Square Enix’s Manga UP! app, nearly doubling the number of series available on Azuki’s platform. Azuki launched as a subscription service in 2021, and previously expanded its library with the addition of new volumes available via in-app ebook sales. With these new series, Azuki is also launching individual chapter purchases in its iOS, Android, and Web apps, allowing fans to purchase chapters and permanently add them to their digital library. These series are available for purchase only (not included in Azuki’s Premium membership), and most series have their first chapter available for free reading.
