# PRESS RELEASE: Omoi Kicks Off Black Friday with Hundreds of New Kodansha Manga Series Available via Subscription and Direct Purchase, Launches 50% Off Deal

**Just $4.99/month for nearly 350 Kodansha manga series including *Shikimori’s Not Just a Cutie* , *Ran the Peerless Beauty* , *The Untouchable Midori-kun* , plus thousands of new purchasable volumes.**


SAN FRANCISCO, CA—For the first time since launching under the name Azuki in 2021, digital manga service Omoi is adding hundreds of series from publisher Kodansha to its Premium subscription library, more than doubling the number of Kodansha series included with a Premium membership.


211 manga series, including hit romance series like[Shikimori’s Not Just a Cutie](https://www.omoi.com/series/shikimoris-not-just-a-cutie) ;[Ran the Peerless Beauty](https://www.omoi.com/series/ran-the-peerless-beauty) ; *[The Untouchable Midori-kun](https://www.omoi.com/series/the-untouchable-midori-kun)* ; and *[Don’t Toy With Me, Miss Nagatoro](https://www.omoi.com/series/dont-toy-with-me-miss-nagatoro)* , are available starting today for Premium members. Some Kodansha series will also be removed from the subscription library on January 1, 2026, due to licensing restrictions. As of January 1, the total number of Kodansha series included in an Omoi Premium membership will be 347 (a 109% increase), and the total number of Premium series across all publishers will be 580.


Kodansha series on Omoi have roughly half of their volumes available as part of the Omoi Premium subscription, while the remainder are available for purchase as digital volumes. New series and volumes will be added periodically as Kodansha releases new manga. In addition to this significant expansion of the subscription library, today Omoi is making the full Kodansha catalog available for purchase, including hundreds of non-subscription titles. Going forward, all new digital volumes from Kodansha will be available for purchase on Omoi. Plus, Premium members get 20% off volume purchases as part of their subscription, making Omoi one of the most affordable ways on the market to read a wide variety of manga.


Finally, to celebrate all this new manga to read, Omoi is bringing back their popular Black Friday deal, offering 50% off annual Premium memberships from now through Cyber Monday. New subscribers can get an entire year of unlimited access to hundreds of series for just $29.99 ($2.50/month). This deal is available on the Omoi website at[omoi.com](https://omoi.com/) and in their iOS and Android apps.


“For over four years, fans have been exploring Kodansha’s impressive library of manga across nearly every genre on Omoi, but we’ve gotten one question, again and again: when are we getting more?” said Omoi Co-Founder and Licensing and Marketing Director Evan Minto. “The answer is ‘today,’ and you can expect more where that came from in the coming months. We can’t wait to hear about Omoi readers’ favorite manga as they dive into these exciting new series from Kodansha!”

**About Omoi and KiraKira Media Inc.**


Omoi is a digital comics publisher and subscription app run by a small team of manga fans and tech experts at KiraKira Media Inc. in San Francisco, CA. Omoi was originally launched under the name Azuki in 2021 before changing their name in November 2025.


Omoi is available on the web at omoi.com and in the iOS App Store and Google Play Store. The app features over 900 manga series, with new chapters updated daily. Manga fans can start reading for free with advertisements today, or upgrade to Premium to unlock thousands of chapters for $4.99 a month. A 14-day free trial and discounted annual plan are also available, and additional volumes and chapters outside the subscription are available for purchase. Most titles are available worldwide (except Japan).
