# PRESS RELEASE: Azuki Adds 13 More Manga from J-Novel Club to Subscription Library

*****Campfire Cooking in Another World with My Absurd Skill: Sui’s Great Adventure* , *I’ll Never Set Foot in That House Again!* , *The Conqueror from a Dying Kingdom* , and 10 more now available via Azuki website and iOS/Android apps****


SAN FRANCISCO, CA—Digital manga service Azuki is expanding their partnership with light novel and manga publisher J-Novel Club (JNC), distributing 13 new manga series from J-Novel Club on Azuki’s apps. Full series are available now through Azuki’s $4.99-per-month subscription, and select chapters are available for free with ads to non-subscribers.


The full list of all 13 series is included below:


*Black Summoner*
By Doufu Mayoi, Kurogin (DIGS), DaiXt


*Campfire Cooking in Another World with My Absurd Skill*
By Ren Eguchi, Akagishi K


*Campfire Cooking in Another World with My Absurd Skill: Sui’s Great Adventure*
By Ren Eguchi, Momo Futaba


*The Conqueror from a Dying Kingdom*
By Fudeorca, Muramasa Sabiku


*The Faraway Paladin*
By Kanata Yanagino, Mutsumi Okubashi


*Full Clearing Another World under a Goddess with Zero Believers*
By Isle Osaki, Hakuto Shiroi


*Fushi no Kami: Rebuilding Civilization Starts with a Village*
By Mizuumi Amakawa, Yoruno Kuroki


*How a Realist Hero Rebuilt the Kingdom*
By Dojyomaru, Satoshi Ueda


*I’ll Never Set Foot in That House Again!*
By milli-gram, Jirou Yuuki


*The Magic in this Other World is Too Far Behind!*
By Gamei Hitsuji, COMTA


*Mapping: The Trash-Tier Skill That Got Me into a Top-Tier Party*
By Udon Kamono, SAVAN


*The Unwanted Undead Adventurer*
By Yu Okano, Haiji Nakasone


*A Very Fairy Apartment*
By Amakara Surume
