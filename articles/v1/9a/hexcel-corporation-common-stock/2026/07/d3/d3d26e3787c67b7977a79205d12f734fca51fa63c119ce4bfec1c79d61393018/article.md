### Hexcel Schedules Second Quarter 2026 Earnings Release and Conference Call


July 1, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s21.q4cdn.com/180688177/files/doc_news/2026/06/HXL-Q2-2026-earnings-call-date.pdf)


STAMFORD, Conn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Hexcel Corporation (NYSE: HXL) announced today that it will report financial results for its second quarter of 2026 on Wednesday, July 29 after the market close.


The company will host a webcast and conference call to discuss highlights of its financial results on Thursday, July 30 at 9:30 a.m. ET. The call will be hosted by Chairman, CEO and President Tom Gentile and Chief Financial Officer Jamie Coogan.


The event will be webcast via the[Investor Relations webpage](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fattendee%2F221265810&esheet=54562313&newsitemid=20260701179427&lan=en-US&anchor=Investor+Relations+webpage&index=1&md5=5e893ec7f83bae684f0c3a7084a191ce) at[www.Hexcel.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.Hexcel.com&esheet=54562313&newsitemid=20260701179427&lan=en-US&anchor=www.Hexcel.com&index=2&md5=16367949b46d0cc1001dbb4a6656a523) . The event can also be accessed by dialing +1 (646) 307-1963. The conference ID is 2360739. A replay of the call will be available on the investor relations page of the Hexcel website approximately two hours after the conclusion of the call.
