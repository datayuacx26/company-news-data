STAMFORD, Conn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Hexcel Corporation (NYSE: HXL) announced today that the Company’s Board of Directors has declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of $0.18 per share on the common stock of Hexcel, payable to stockholders of record as of May 4, 2026, with a payment date of May 11, 2026.
