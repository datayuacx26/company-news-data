# Hexcel Schedules First Quarter 2026 Earnings Release and Conference Call

### Hexcel Schedules First Quarter 2026 Earnings Release and Conference Call


April 1, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s21.q4cdn.com/180688177/files/doc_news/Hexcel-Schedules-First-Quarter-2026-Earnings-Release-and-Conference-Call-2026.pdf)


STAMFORD, Conn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Hexcel Corporation (NYSE: HXL) announced today that it will report financial results for its first quarter of 2026 on Wednesday, April 22 after the market close.


The company will host a webcast and conference call to discuss highlights of its financial results on Thursday, April 23 at 9:30 a.m. ET. The call will be hosted by Chairman, CEO and President Tom Gentile and Interim Chief Financial Officer Mike Lenz.


The event will be webcast via the[Investor Relations webpage](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fattendee%2F224695473&esheet=54472798&newsitemid=20260401074204&lan=en-US&anchor=Investor+Relations+webpage&index=1&md5=9d14999e8016d93193a6348295e0cfb4) at[www.Hexcel.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.Hexcel.com&esheet=54472798&newsitemid=20260401074204&lan=en-US&anchor=www.Hexcel.com&index=2&md5=457ff90a1869c0b76f48cc4d700a8f33) . The event can also be accessed by dialing +1 (646) 307-1963. The conference ID is 2360739. A replay of the call will be available on the investor relations page of the Hexcel website approximately two hours after the conclusion of the call.
