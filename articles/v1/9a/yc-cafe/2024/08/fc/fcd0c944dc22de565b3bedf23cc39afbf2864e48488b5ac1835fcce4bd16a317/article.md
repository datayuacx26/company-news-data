# Top Strategies for Enhancing Employee Engagement and Retention in 2024

‍


Primarily due to the strict and severe RTO policies implemented by giant tech companies over the last two years, and despite increasing workplace engagement programs, many organizations still find it challenging to keep their employees engaged and retained. As we go through the remainder of 2024, it's essential to understand the current landscape of employee engagement and push companies to implement effective strategies to cultivate a motivated, committed, and satisfied workforce.


## **Current State of Employee Engagement**


Employee engagement has experienced notable fluctuations in recent years. There are years like 2023 where engagement increased by[three percentage points from 2022](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/608675/new-workplace-employee-engagement-stagnates.aspx) , yet it began to decline as the year progressed. Overall, the growth areas in 2023 still need to catch up to their peak levels in 2020.


This continues to add to the poor employee engagement rates, where employees feel more detached and less satisfied with their organizations compared to 2020. This detachment is particularly pronounced among younger workers and those whose jobs can be done remotely. Workplace engagement among workers under 35 has risen slightly.


Still, it has not reached the benchmark many corporations expect of their employees, considering the ongoing struggles in clarity of expectations and feeling valued at work. Workers aged 35 and older have seen minimal change but must catch up to their 2020 engagement levels in several key areas.


## **How to improve Employee Engagement**


As previously highlighted, employee detachment and dissatisfaction are directly correlated to employees' feeling valued. Enhancing clarity of expectations is a crucial first step in improving workplace engagement. Simple ways this can be done include regular one-on-one meetings between managers and employees to ensure that roles and responsibilities are well understood. Providing clear, written job descriptions and updating them as needed can help maintain this clarity. Additionally, goal-setting frameworks like OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) can align individual objectives with broader company goals.


Additionally, fostering meaningful feedback is another essential strategy. Implementing a structured feedback system where employees receive regular, constructive feedback can significantly improve engagement. Training managers to give effective feedback that motivates and guides employees is also critical. Encouraging peer-to-peer feedback helps build a collaborative culture, enhancing overall engagement and transparency throughout a corporation, where employees feel that their opinions matter.


Further, strengthening the connection to the company’s mission and purpose is vital. Communicating the company’s mission and values consistently ensures employees understand the bigger picture. Highlighting how individual contributions align with organizational goals can reinforce this connection. Celebrating successes that reflect the company’s mission and impact can further strengthen an employee’s sense of purpose and appreciation within an organization. Hence, those who excel have a growing passion for continuing to do their best work—providing a win-win situation.


Promoting employee well-being is also essential for sustained workplace engagement. Comprehensive well-being programs that include mental health support, flexible working arrangements, and wellness resources can significantly enhance employee satisfaction. Encouraging work-life balance by promoting flexible work schedules and remote work options helps employees manage their personal and professional lives effectively. Providing opportunities for employees to participate in wellness activities and programs can further promote their overall well-being.


Lastly, developing growth opportunities is critical to maintaining workplace engagement. Creating clear career paths and development plans for employees can help them see a future within the organization. Offering training and development programs tailored to their needs and aspirations can facilitate their professional growth. Encouraging mentorship and coaching can provide additional support and guidance, fostering a culture of continuous learning and development.


## **Strategies for boosting employee Retention**


As corporations implement workplace engagement strategies, flexible work arrangements have also proven to keep employees engaged and raise retention rates. Allowing employees to choose their work location, whether remote, on-site, or hybrid, meets their expectations for work-life balance and strengthens the work culture and commitment to the organization. The Conference Board’s survey highlights that only 15% of HR leaders at organizations allowing employees to choose their work location reported retention difficulties, compared to 45% at organizations mandating on-site work. This data underscores the importance of flexibility in modern workplaces.


Another essential strategy for corporations is creating a feeling of belonging within the work culture. Fostering a culture where diversity is valued, and employees feel they can enhance retention. Training all employees on diversity, equity, and inclusion ensures that everyone understands and supports these values. Encouraging employee resource groups and supporting their initiatives can further promote inclusivity and a feeling of belonging.


In addition to curating a feeling of belongin, recognition and rewards are powerful tools for retaining employees. Implementing a robust recognition program to acknowledge employees’ contributions can boost morale and loyalty regularly. Offering competitive compensation and benefits packages is also critical for retention. Providing non-monetary rewards, such as additional time off, professional development opportunities, and team-building activities, can further enhance employee satisfaction and commitment.


For those who are early in their careers, career development opportunities are crucial for retaining top talent. Investing in employees' professional growth through continuous learning opportunities helps them stay engaged and motivated. Encouraging internal mobility by promoting from within and offering lateral moves provides employees with new challenges and opportunities. Supporting employees in gaining new skills and competencies relevant to their roles ensures they continue to grow and thrive within the organization.


Finally, promoting employee well-being and support is essential for retention. Prioritizing mental health support through Employee Assistance Programs (EAPs) and counseling services can help employees manage stress and maintain their mental health. Promoting physical health with wellness programs, fitness memberships, and healthy workplace initiatives can enhance overall well-being. Ensuring a supportive work culture where employees can voice concerns and seek help without stigma is crucial for maintaining their commitment to the organization.


## **Checklist for 2024 Employee Engagement and Retention**


Here are a few ways that employers and corporations can ensure they are taking initial steps toward increasing workplace engagement and retention:


- **Clarity of expectations:** organizations should conduct regular one-on-one meetings, provide updated job descriptions, and use goal-setting frameworks to align individual and company goals.


- **Fostering meaningful feedback** : training managers to give effective feedback, and encouraging peer-to-peer feedback to build a collaborative culture.


- **Well-being programs:** encouraging work-life balance, and providing opportunities for participation in wellness activities and programs.


- **Growth opportunities** : offering tailored training and development programs, and encouraging mentorship and coaching.


- **Flexible work arrangements:** allow employees to choose their work location and support flexible working hours.

Implementing these strategies and utilizing this comprehensive checklist can enhance employee engagement and retention, fostering a thriving workplace in 2024 and beyond. As the workplace evolves, staying ahead of these trends and focusing on these critical areas will be essential for organizational success.
