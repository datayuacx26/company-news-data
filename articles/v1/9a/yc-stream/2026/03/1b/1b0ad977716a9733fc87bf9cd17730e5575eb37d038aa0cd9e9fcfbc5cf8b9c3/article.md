# Stream Joins Guidewire Insurtech Vanguards Program

**San Francisco, CA, March 9, 2026** – Stream, the AI Platform for Modern Claims Teams, announced that the company has joined the[Guidewire Insurtech Vanguards](https://www.guidewire.com/partners/programs/insurtech-vanguards) program, an initiative led by property and casualty (P&C) cloud platform provider, Guidewire (NYSE: GWRE), to help insurers learn about the newest insurtechs and how to best leverage them.


Stream was built by claims professionals to transform claims data into real-time insights and automated workflows. “The future of claims adjudication is here, bringing AI and human expertise together to achieve superior outcomes for claimants and insurers alike,” said **Eric Yen, former workers’ compensation adjuster and Co-Founder & CEO of Stream** . “Stream embeds directly into everyday workflows via Guidewire ClaimCenter, helping teams improve decision velocity, increase adjuster throughput, and surface risks earlier across disability, medical, litigation, and fraud workflows. On average, Stream delivers a 5% cost reduction in workers' comp and bodily injury claims above $50,000 in exposure.”


**Insurtech Vanguards** is a community of select startups and technology providers that are bringing novel solutions to the P&C industry. As part of the program, Guidewire provides strategic guidance to and advocates for the participating insurtechs, while connecting them with the Guidewire P&C customer community.


“We’re excited to welcome Stream to the Insurtech Vanguards program,” said **Laura Drabik, Chief Evangelist of Guidewire** . “Stream is tackling one of the hardest problems in claims, turning unstructured information into actionable intelligence inside core workflows. Their claims-native approach and vision for integrating with Guidewire supports insurers in accelerating resolution while improving consistency and transparency.”


Stream’s AI Platform for Modern Claims Teams can be deployed immediately as a standalone solution or integrated into core systems like ClaimCenter to power decisions inside existing workflows. Stream serves P&C claims organizations and service providers handling high-complexity claims.
