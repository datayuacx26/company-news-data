# Your Schema is Your IP. Stop Leaking It.

In an era where data drives innovation, your database schema is much more than just technical documentation. It's your intellectual property (IP). It defines your business logic, relationships, and processes in a way that competitors could never easily replicate. Yet many organizations unknowingly put this valuable asset at risk every time they send their schema to third-party AI services for processing.


### The Hidden Danger of Schema Exposure


Sending your database schema to external AI services is akin to handing over the blueprint of your most prized invention to a stranger. While AI-powered tools promise amazing insights, the cost of potential data leaks or misuse of your IP far outweighs the benefits.


Security incidents originating from cloud-based AI services are increasingly common, and database schemas often contain sensitive structural information, business rules, and even hints at confidential data flows. Each data transfer outside your secure perimeter exposes you to risks of interception, unauthorized access, or compliance violations.
