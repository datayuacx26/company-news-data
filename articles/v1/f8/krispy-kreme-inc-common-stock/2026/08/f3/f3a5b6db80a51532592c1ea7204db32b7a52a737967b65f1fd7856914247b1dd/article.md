## I Choose You! KRISPY KREME® Launches All-New Pokémon Doughnut Collection Featuring Five Beloved Pokémon


08/13/2026


Download (opens in new window)


*Fans can sweeten celebration of 30 years of Pokémon with special-edition cups showcasing iconic Pokémon*


CHARLOTTE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Your next Pokémon adventure just got a whole lot sweeter. Krispy Kreme® is teaming up with The Pokémon Company International to celebrate 30 years of Pokémon with a new Pokémon Doughnut Collection, featuring five beloved Pokémon transformed into Krispy Kreme creations fans can catch and share.


Fans can sweeten celebration of 30 years of Pokémon with special-edition cups showcasing iconic Pokémon


Beginning Tuesday, Aug. 18, for a limited time, Trainers and doughnut lovers can discover Krispy Kreme's new **Pokémon Doughnut Collection** at participating shops nationwide. Catch five iconic Pokémon-inspired doughnuts with fun flavors or stock up on Poké Ball doughnuts before setting off on your next adventure:


- **Bulbasaur Doughnut** – an Original Glazed® doughnut dipped in vanilla flavored icing and topped with frosting and a Bulbasaur piece.
- **Charmander Doughnut** – an unglazed shell doughnut with chocolate and toasted marshmallow flavored filling, dipped in orange colored caramel icing and sparks of red and gold sugar, topped with a Charmander piece.
- **Squirtle Doughnut** – an Original Glazed® doughnut piped with cotton candy flavored buttercreme, dipped in sanding sugar and nonpareils, and topped with a surfing Squirtle piece.
- **Pikachu Doughnut** – an unglazed shell filled with lemon flavored Kreme™ filling dipped in vanilla icing, decorated with electrifying drizzle and nonpareils, and topped with a Pikachu piece.
- **Jigglypuff Doughnut** – an Original Glazed® doughnut piped with strawberry flavored buttercreme, sprinkled with strawberry flavored cookie, and topped with a Jigglypuff piece.
- **Poké Ball Doughnut** – an Original Glazed® doughnut dipped in vanilla flavored icing and red sprinkles, piped with chocolate icing.


The celebration doesn't stop with the doughnut collection! Krispy Kreme is marking 30 years of Pokémon with more can't-miss surprises:


- **Special-edition Pokémon cup** available in five exclusive Pokémon designs, filled with **10 Original Glazed® Gold Dots** for fans to catch and celebrate.
- A Pokémon Dozen served in **special edition packaging** featuring five iconic Pokémon, making every purchase a fun way to celebrate Pokémon.
- Fans who wear Pokémon attire to participating shops on Saturday, Aug. 22, can receive a **FREE Original Glazed® Doughnut** . Limit one per guest, in-shop and drive-thru only.


“We had so much fun bringing the world of Pokémon to life through doughnuts in a way only Krispy Kreme can,” said Alison Holder, Chief Brand and Product Officer at Krispy Kreme. “From Pokémon-inspired creations and playful flavors to themed cups, special packaging and fan surprises, this collection is made to be caught and shared as we celebrate 30 incredible years of Pokémon.”


Krispy Kreme’s Pokémon Doughnut Collection will be available in-shop and for pickup or delivery via Krispy Kreme's app and website, individually and by the dozen.
