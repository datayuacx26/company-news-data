## KRISPY KREME® Introduces $10 Original Glazed® Clean Sheet Dozen Featuring ‘Red, White and Goooaaal’ Doughnut for America’s Match Wednesday


06/30/2026


Download (opens in new window)


*If America delivers a “clean sheet” Wednesday, Krispy Kreme will celebrate Thursday with free Original Glazed® doughnuts nationwide*


CHARLOTTE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Krispy Kreme® is sweetening fandom and watch parties for America’s big soccer match with a one day only $10 Original Glazed® Clean Sheet Dozen, available beginning at 5 p.m. ET / 2 p.m. PT Wednesday, featuring the all-new “Red, White and Goooaaal” Original Glazed doughnut.


If America delivers a “clean sheet” Wednesday, Krispy Kreme will celebrate Thursday with free Original Glazed® doughnuts nationwide


As a proud supporter of clean sheet soccer, Krispy Kreme is offering this one-day dozen with $0 delivery on Wednesday. And to boot, if America keeps its opponent off the scoreboard with a clean sheet (no goals allowed) on Wednesday, Krispy Kreme will celebrate on Thursday by offering FREE Original Glazed doughnuts all day long, limit one per guest, no purchase necessary.


Zero goals against = zero-dollar Original Glazed doughnuts. The sweetest kind of shutout!


The Original Glazed Clean Sheet Dozen includes three Red, White and Goooaaal doughnuts: an Original Glazed® doughnut dipped in white icing and drizzled with blue and red icing, along with nine iconic Original Glazed® doughnuts.


With patriotic pride running high this week, Krispy Kreme will also “Bring Fourth the Fun,” beginning Thursday by offering guests an Original Glazed dozen for just $2.50 with the purchase of any dozen or 16-count Minis at regular price. Available through Sunday, in-shop and drive-thru BOGO orders are limited to two per guest, while online pickup or delivery orders using promo code "USA" on the Krispy Kreme app or website are limited to one per order.


As part of the celebration, Krispy Kreme is also inviting fans to show their patriotic spirit on Saturday, July 4, by wearing red, white, and blue to receive a FREE Original Glazed doughnut, no purchase necessary, limit one per guest.
