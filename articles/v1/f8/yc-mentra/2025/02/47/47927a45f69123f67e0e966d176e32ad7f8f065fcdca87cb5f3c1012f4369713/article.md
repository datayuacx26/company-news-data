# Why We're Building MentraOS: The Smart Glasses Operating System

We’re building the open operating system for smart glasses.


We believe that building an open-source, community-driven OS and ecosystem for smart glasses is the best path to ensure the next personal computing device upgrades humanity for the better. We’re building the open, self-empowered, augmented future.


## Why Build MentraOS


The modern mobile OS ecosystem isn’t ready for smart glasses. But MentraOS is.


MentraOS enables any app to:


1.


Instantly run on any pair of smart glasses.


2.


Continuously access smart glasses I/O.


3.


Run persistently — no iOS or Android shutting it down.


4.


Get discovered — reach every smart glasses user.


For users, this means you can run many proactive apps simultaneously without restarts or disconnections. MentraOS is a **semantic OS** you control what you see and when you see it.


Today’s platforms decide for us based on engagement metrics and ad revenue. MentraOS flips that model — you decide the filters on your media and your reality.


## Core Beliefs


**Proactive AI** - We’re shifting from explicit tools to proactive AI agents that augment human thought. True hybrid thinking requires continuous context capture and a high-bandwidth interface to the mind. Smart glasses are perfect for this: they see what we see, hear what we hear, and can modulate what we perceive.


**Underspec** - To stay under 40 grams and last 8+ hours, all-day smart glasses must remain low-power. They can’t run heavy applications natively - MentraOS bridges that gap.


**Memes Matter** - Smart glasses will redefine how ideas spread. Current algorithms amplify outrage and ads. MentraOS gives each individual control over what ideas they transmit and absorb.


**Stay Out of the Way** - The OS should do only what an OS must. MentraOS provides a minimalist UI, a fully-permissive SDK, and a decentralized app store. It empowers developers and users — not corporations.


## Vision


Our goal is an open-source, individually controlled, decentralized future for smart glasses - one that augments human intelligence instead of controlling it.


We see smart glasses as humanity’s next great step - an *exocortex* that multiplies our intelligence a millionfold.


Let’s win the next general-purpose computing platform for society.


## Spatial Augmented Reality


HUD (heads-up display) smart glasses will be as big as smartphones, and spatial augmented reality (AR) will be as big as the internet. Spatial AR is the next phase of the internet - merging the digital and physical worlds. HUD glasses enable **semantic AR** , modifying thought and conversation through an overlay interface.


**Spatial AR** will come later - when glasses understand their physical surroundings. For now, lightweight HUD devices already unlock enormous potential. MentraOS evolves with this timeline. It solves today’s HUD problems while advancing toward full spatial AR and eventually brain-computer interfaces (BCIs).


Today: optimized for lightweight HUD devices.
Tomorrow: the foundation for full spatial and cognitive computing.
