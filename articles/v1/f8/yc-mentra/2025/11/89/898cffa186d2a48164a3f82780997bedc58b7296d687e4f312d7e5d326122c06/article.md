# Even Realities G2 Support Coming on MentraOS

Even Realities has launched the Even Realities G2 smart glasses. Compared to the G1, they’re lighter, slimmer, have better battery, have a much bigger screen, and have improved microphones, while still coming in at $599.


## Even G1


We previously worked with Even Realities and the community to offer full MentraOS support on Even G1, and we’re continuing to work hard to support our thousands of users using G1 on MentraOS.


## Even G2


We’re excited to announce that we will fully support the Even Realities G2. Full MentraOS support for Even Realities G2 is expected in January 2026. Stay tuned to this blog and[our mailing list](https://manage.kmail-lists.com/subscriptions/subscribe?a=YAvT3k&g=RZz9s5&utm_source=blog&utm_campaign=even_g2_support_announce_blog_post) for more information soon!
