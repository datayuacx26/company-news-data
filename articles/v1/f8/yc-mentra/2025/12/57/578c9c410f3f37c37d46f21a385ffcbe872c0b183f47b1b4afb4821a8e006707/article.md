# Mentra Live Shipping Update

We’re delaying Mentra Live shipping by six weeks, and it will now ship to all US batch 1 customers on


February 15


. We’re sorry for the delay.


The Mentra Live hardware is complete and working end-to-end. This extra time is about finishing some critical pieces so the experience feels magical and seamless from day one.

## What we’re finishing (and why we’re delaying)


The hardware is solid. The remaining work is about final software changes and making the day‑one experience feel magical.


- Charging case update


: we found that if you put the case upside down and shook it, the glasses would stop charging. We had to fix this for users who keep Mentra Live in their backpack. It’s been fixed and updated cases are now being built


- Final integration of a custom microphone solution


so the Mentra Live microphone can run continuously and reliably


- Camera stabilization


: finishing software image stabilization so footage is smooth while walking and moving


- Mentra Notes


: automatic AI note‑taking with Mentra Live


-


POV video calls


: share your glasses camera feed on a call
