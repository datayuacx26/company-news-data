# MemCards: the First Third-Party App to Launch on Mentra Market

Software development studio Tomtau has released **MemCards** on the Mentra Market! That means anyone with MentraOS-compatible smart glasses can download and use MemCards right now. MemCards is a flashcard app that helps people learn new languages or memorize any information.


Mentra is excited to accept MemCards by Tomtau as the first official third-party app on the Mentra Market. This marks the shift toward a competitive community marketplace for smart-glasses apps.


## Why It Matters


We understand that every great personal-computing shift has been driven by third-party developers. VisiCalc, a third-party app, transformed the Apple II from a hobbyist machine into the first indispensable personal computer. Social apps did the same for mobile. Killer apps rarely come from the platform creator, they emerge from the community. We’ll keep building the core, table-stakes open-source apps, but the real breakthroughs will come from developers who invent things none of us can predict. MemCards is the first step toward that future.
