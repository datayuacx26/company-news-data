# Intelligent Connectivity: The Next Evolution of AI Infrastructure

## The AI Scaling Challenge: When Bandwidth Alone Isn’t Enough


The AI era is driving an unprecedented transformation in data center infrastructure. Modern AI clusters are rapidly scaling from thousands to tens of thousands of GPUs connected through increasingly complex PCIe, Ethernet, and optical fabrics. As these systems grow, connectivity is becoming just as critical as compute.


Historically, connectivity solutions were designed to move data from one point to another. But at AI scale, bandwidth alone is no longer enough. Infrastructure teams need visibility into the health, performance, and behavior of the fabric itself. They need the ability to rapidly identify issues, accelerate root-cause analysis, and maximize utilization of increasingly valuable AI resources.


## The Hidden Cost of a Single Degraded Link


Consider a 10,000-GPU AI cluster. A single degraded link may not cause an immediate failure, but it can introduce retransmissions, increase latency, reduce throughput, or impact workload efficiency. Identifying the source of the problem can require hours or even days of investigation across multiple devices, protocols, and system layers. As AI infrastructure continues to scale, these challenges become increasingly difficult to manage.

At Credo, we believe the industry is entering the era of Intelligent Connectivity. Intelligent Connectivity combines high-performance interconnect technologies with real-time observability, diagnostics, analytics, and actionable insights. This vision is enabled by PILOT, Credo’s platform for connectivity observability and intelligence.
