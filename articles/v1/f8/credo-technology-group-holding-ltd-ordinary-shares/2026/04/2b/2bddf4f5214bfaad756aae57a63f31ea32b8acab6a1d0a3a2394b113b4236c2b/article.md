# Credo AECs Named “Data Center Solution of the Year” in Data Breakthrough Awards Program

Credo’s active electrical cables (AECs) have been named “Data Center Solution of the Year” in the 7th annual Data Breakthrough Awards program conducted by Data Breakthrough, an independent market intelligence organization that recognizes the top companies, technologies and products in the global data technology market today.


Recognized for their distinctive purple cables, Credo’s[ZeroFlap AECs](https://credosemi.com/products/zeroflapaec/) are known for delivering highly reliable interconnect for AI backend networks. AECs are the de facto standard for inter-rack connectivity and are now displacing optical rack-to-rack connections up to 7 meters.


AECs are narrow-gauge copper cables with industry-standard connectors at each end. Credo has integrated its low-power, high-performance retimers, gearboxes and FEC circuitry in the connectors to ensure signal integrity at 56G and 112G per lane. These SerDes devices not only provide signal integrity retiming, and speed shifting, but they enable throughput with cables that are lighter weight, less volume, and more reliable than other solutions.


“Credo’s innovation in Active Electrical Cables is reshaping how modern data centers handle high-speed connectivity. As AI infrastructure scales and network demands intensify, traditional approaches are hitting limits in power, cost, and performance,” said Steve Johansson, Managing Director, Data Breakthrough. “Credo’s AEC technology delivers a compelling alternative—combining reliability, signal integrity, and energy efficiency to enable the next generation of AI-driven data centers. We’re proud to name them ‘Data Center Solution of the Year.’”


“We’re honored to have our AECs recognized as Data Center Solution of the Year by Data Breakthrough,” said Don Barnetson, SVP of Product at Credo. “AECs provide the power efficiency and reliability for connecting dense GPU clusters and server racks used in large AI training deployments. This award validates our innovative system-level approach and unique position in the industry of owning and delivering the entire solution stack.”


The global Data Breakthrough Awards program provides a forum for public recognition around the achievements of data companies and solutions in categories including data analytics, DataOps, data management, infrastructure and hardware, storage, Business Intelligence and more. For more information visit[DataBreakthroughAwards.com](https://databreakthroughawards.com/) .
