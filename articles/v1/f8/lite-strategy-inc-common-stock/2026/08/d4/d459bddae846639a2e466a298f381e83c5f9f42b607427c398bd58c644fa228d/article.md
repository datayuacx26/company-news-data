# Why Lite Strategy Is Going All In on Litecoin While Everyone Else Chases Bitcoin | Jay File

Host Erin Gambrel (The Blonde Broker) sits down with Jay File, CEO of Lite Strategy, exactly one year after the company closed its deal to make Litecoin its primary treasury asset — and he makes a compelling case for why that bet is just getting started. Jay breaks down what he calls DAT 3.0, the idea that just buying and holding crypto is table stakes now, and the real opportunity is in actively monetizing your treasury through covered calls, share buybacks funded without debt, and strategic investments into ecosystem infrastructure. He walks through Lite Strategy’s $1 million lead investment into LitVM and what the market keeps getting wrong about Litecoin — it was never meant to replace Bitcoin, it was built to complement it.
