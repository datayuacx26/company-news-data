# Lite Strategy (NASDAQ: LITS): CEO Justin (Jay) File on Litecoin Treasury Upside & CLARITY Act

Jane King interviews Justin (Jay) File, CEO, CFO & Board Member of Lite Strategy (NASDAQ: LITS), about the company’s treasury strategy focused on Litecoin exposure and opportunities in the evolving digital asset market.


Jay discusses why Litecoin may offer significant upside optionality, how investors can gain access through a publicly traded company, and why transparency matters in crypto treasury management.


The conversation also explores the broader outlook for the crypto industry in 2026, institutional adoption, and how the CLARITY Act could impact growth and regulatory certainty.
