# Lite Strategy – $LITS – Ep:061 – Nano Cap Podcast

In this episode, Jay File, CEO of Lite Strategy, joins the Nano Cap Podcast to discuss how the company is approaching Litecoin as a treasury reserve asset and why it believes Litecoin has a unique role to play in the broader digital asset market.


The conversation covers Lite Strategy’s investment thesis, its strategy for building exposure to Litecoin, and how the company thinks about risk, liquidity, volatility, and long-term value creation. Jay also shares his perspective on Litecoin’s utility as a fast, low-cost, globally accessible payment network, and why it may continue to gain relevance as digital assets become more integrated into financial markets.
