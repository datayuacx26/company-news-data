# Expanding Opportunities for Veterans With Disabilities: Collaboration with Montana, Knife River, Teleo, RDO Equipment, and John Deere

The remote work is possible because of Teleo’s technology, which allows operators to circumvent the physical limitations of job sites and instead operate from the safety and comfort of an accessible workstation. This remote workstation makes the job more accessible for veterans living with disabilities.


During a recent onsite demonstration, the governor used our technology to remotely operate a John Deere loader to move gravel while seated at a command center near the work site.


“The latest developments in construction technology are changing the way we work from so many different angles,” said David K. Zinke, president of Knife River’s Mountain Region. “Most encouragingly, technology provided by Teleo is opening new opportunities for workforce inclusion in the construction industry.”
