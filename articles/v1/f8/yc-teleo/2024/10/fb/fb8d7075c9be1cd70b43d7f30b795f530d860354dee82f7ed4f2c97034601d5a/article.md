# Our Strategic Expansion to Bring Autonomous Operations to Industries Beyond Construction

Construction and mining company


[Black Cat Civil](https://www.blackcatcivil.com.au/) has confirmed a significant contract for 10 machines. This marks our expansion into Australia supported and deployed by our Australian distributor


[Aptella](https://www.aptella.com/) (formerly Position Partners). Black Cat will integrate Teleo Supervised Autonomy into 10 of its fleet of


[Caterpillar 740EJ](https://www.cat.com/en_US/products/new/equipment/articulated-trucks/three-axle-articulated-trucks/1000034268.html) articulated dump trucks (ADTs). This implementation will demonstrate the performance of the technology to prove suitability in Australian conditions.


Black Cat is integrating Teleo’s system to remove the danger of machines working within hazardous areas and improving productivity challenges, as well as improving working conditions for its workforce with autonomous and remote operations. Currently, the company deploys operators to a wide range of worksites across Australia. Teleo Supervised Autonomy will also provide flexibility for operators, reduced


planning for costly polluted air travel, accommodation and other related logistics. Once fitted with the Teleo system in Q1 of 2025, Black Cat will determine the sites where the machines will be deployed.


[MS Park Construction](https://pnk.group/ms-park-construction/) , a construction company and wholly-owned subsidiary of PNK Group located in New York, has placed an order for 10 machines. The ten machines will be immediately equipped with Teleo’s technology on eight John Deere 410P ADTs and two 850L SmartGrade bulldozers by the end of this year to build a large logistics warehouse site located in the Atlanta, Georgia area. The articulated dump trucks will perform large scale earthmoving, and will autonomously move dirt across the construction site. The dozer will perform precision grading. The company plans to bring their vision of a fully remote and autonomous jobsite to fruition.


MS Park Construction is leveraging Teleo’s technology to improve the safety and efficiency of its operations. This includes reducing idle machine time and truck bunching, which is when faster trucks are slowed by the presence of slower trucks, and results in production losses and increasing fuel consumptions and idle time. Additionally, the company is working closely with Teleo and


[Dobbs Positioning Solutions](https://dobbspositioningsolutions.com/) , a division of


[Dobbs Equipment](https://www.dobbsequipment.com/) ,


which is also facilitating the deployment with MS Park Construction, to create an operator training plan. The program will leverage Dobbs Positioning Solutions’ remote command center located at its Atlanta store and Teleo’s testing site to train MS Park Construction’s operators to ensure they are well equipped to supervise the autonomous machines upon deployment.
