# Usul Launches Across All 32 NATO Nations

# A Broken System Built in Isolation


NATO was designed for allied nations to work together. The procurement infrastructure that supports it was not.


Every member nation built their own contracting system in isolation. Their own portals, their own languages, their own processes. The result is a patchwork of 50+ disconnected databases that companies are somehow expected to monitor simultaneously, in real time, across dozens of languages and borders.


A UK company building torpedo systems can't win Greek defense contracts because those contracts are posted on a portal they've never heard of. In Greek. With a deadline they missed by three days because they were busy searching somewhere else. The same is true for an IT firm in Germany trying to win healthcare contracts in Portugal, or a cybersecurity company in Canada competing for infrastructure programs across Eastern Europe.


This isn't a niche inefficiency. It's a structural failure that prevents the best companies from reaching the governments that need them most.


### One Platform for the Entire Alliance


Usul is the first platform that unifies government contracts across all of NATO. We ingest 50+ government portals across 30 nations, translate everything into a single interface, and surface the right opportunities to the right companies regardless of language, industry, or border.


Today, Usul is connected to 70+ government contracting sources spanning the United States, Western and Central Europe, the Nordics, Africa, the Middle East, Central Asia, and APAC. The platform doesn't just aggregate. It matches. Based on your capabilities, your relationships, and your strategic priorities, Usul surfaces contracts actively looking to buy what you build, before you would have found them on your own.


No more manual searches across a dozen databases. No more missed deadlines. No more leaving contracts on the table because the opportunity was posted in a language you don't speak.


### Governments Buy More Than You Think


When people hear government contracting, they picture fighter jets and defense programs. That's one slice of a much larger market. Allied governments buy cloud infrastructure, hospital equipment, energy systems, construction materials, and cybersecurity tools at scale every year. That spending is growing, it's predictable, and most of it goes to companies with existing relationships simply because newer competitors never knew the opportunity existed.


Usul fixes the discovery problem across every category, for every company, across every allied nation.
