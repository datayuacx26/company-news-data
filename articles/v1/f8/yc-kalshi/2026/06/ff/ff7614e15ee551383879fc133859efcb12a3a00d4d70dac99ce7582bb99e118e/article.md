# Kalshi Partners with The Men in Blazers Media Network Ahead of Global Soccer Tournament

The drama doesn't stop when the final whistle blows.


Kalshi and Men in Blazers Media Network have teamed up to bring fans a new way to experience the 2026 summer of soccer.


Men in Blazers is launching “Night Cup” – a nightly show breaking down the biggest moments, surprises, and storylines from every day of the tournament. Hosted by Roger Bennett and a rotating cast of celebrity guests, it's where fans come each night to make sense of everything they've just witnessed and to discuss how both the smallest and wildest moments can impact a team’s chances in the tournament.


## **“Why Everyone Suddenly Believes”**


As part of the partnership,


[Kalshi](https://kalshi.com/soccer-cup)


will power a custom segment within “Night Cup” called, "Why Everyone Suddenly Believes" – built around the moment collective expectation shifts.


A team wins, but their chances of lifting the trophy quietly fall. A star scores twice and a nation's title hopes surge. England wins by three and the market still won't believe. Argentina draws and the crowd doubles down. An injury, a tactical gamble, an unexpected result, and suddenly the tournament looks entirely different to everyone watching.


Using real-time prediction market data from Kalshi, the segment pinpoints exactly when and why those moments happen, going beyond the scoreline to capture how the tournament is actually evolving – and what fans believe comes next.


## **Understanding the Tournament Through the Crowd**


Every four years, the world tunes into the tournament, driven by belief. Every match reshapes expectations, creates new favorites, and rewrites narratives in real time.


Kalshi's prediction markets offer a unique window into those shifts – a live read of how millions of people are processing the tournament as it unfolds. Predictions on Kalshi are hard to ignore since it’s people putting real money behind what they believe will happen, not what they want to happen. Paired with Men in Blazers' analyses and deep passion for the game, “Night Cup” gives fans a new way to experience the biggest sporting event in the world.


Tune in each night throughout the tournament for “Night Cup” – and find out why everyone suddenly believes.
