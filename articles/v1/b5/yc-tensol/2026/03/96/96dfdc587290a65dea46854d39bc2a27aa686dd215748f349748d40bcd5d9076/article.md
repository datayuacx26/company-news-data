# How to Deploy OpenClaw for Your Business in 5 Minutes

## Why Deploy OpenClaw on Tensol?


OpenClaw is the open-source AI assistant platform with 220,000+ GitHub stars. It runs 24/7, connects to 13+ messaging channels, and supports 100+ integrations via OAuth.


But self-hosting OpenClaw means managing servers, updates, security patches, and VM isolation. Tensol handles all of that — each customer gets their own dedicated VM running the full OpenClaw stack.


## Step 1: Sign Up


Head to[app.tensol.ai](https://app.tensol.ai/) and create your account. No credit card required.


## Step 2: Connect Your Tools


Click through OAuth flows for the tools your team uses:


- **Slack** — monitor channels, post alerts, answer questions
- **GitHub** — track PRs, monitor deploys, review code
- **Sentry** — watch error rates, correlate with deploys
- **HubSpot** — update CRM, track deals, log calls
- **Linear** — create tickets, track issues, manage sprints


One click per integration. Your AI employee gets the same access as a real team member.


## Step 3: Configure What to Watch


Tell your agent what matters:


- P0 errors in production → alert the CTO immediately
- Stale deals over $50K → ping the sales manager
- Customer complaints in Slack → escalate to support lead
- New signups → qualify and route to the right rep


## Step 4: Deploy


Hit deploy. Your OpenClaw agent starts running on its own dedicated VM with AES-256 encryption, full audit trail, and 99.9% uptime SLA.


The entire process takes under 5 minutes.


## What Happens Next


Your AI employee works 24/7 from day one:


- **Monitors** every connected tool continuously
- **Detects** patterns across systems (errors + deploys + customer complaints)
- **Acts** autonomously — creates tickets, drafts emails, alerts the right people
- **Remembers** every interaction with persistent memory across months
- **Learns** from your overrides and feedback over time


## Enterprise Security


Every Tensol deployment includes:


- Isolated VM per customer — no shared resources
- AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit
- API keys never exposed to the AI agent
- Full audit trail of every action
- GDPR and CCPA compliant
- 99.9% uptime SLA
