## Reimagining Bidding as Usual


With rapid expansion, heightened volatility, new products, and a steady flow of emerging regulations, the energy trading market is always in flux. How asset owners and operators bid and trade, however, hasn’t necessarily kept pace.


For example, many still rely on outdated strategies, like chasing spikes that used to occur in real time that don’t exist in the same way. While less sophisticated strategies may still produce feasible outcomes, they don't maximize revenue potential. They also may not account for change: An approach that works one day won’t necessarily work every day.


Fluence designs Mosaic to consider change a constant feature of today’s energy markets. We stay focused on the evolving market and market structures, watching trends like narrowing spreads and shifting midday pricing dynamics, as well as variables beyond single price forecasts, such as probability distributions for energy, ancillary services, deployment likelihoods, and system conditions.


Ongoing enhancements to our Mosaic software—including those that led to summer 2025’s standout statistics—demonstrated this strategy in action.


## DART: **More Informed, Adaptive, and Profitable Trading**


The standard CAISO bidding process is sequential. Operators bid for full positions in day-ahead markets and defend those positions in fifteen-minute market (FMM) trading and real-time dispatch (RTD).


With our patent pending DART enhancements we brought a new approach to Mosaic: ingesting more forward-looking information and explicitly modeling the interactions between the day-ahead and real-time markets, rather than treating them independently. Such an approach enables more informed, adaptive, and profitable bidding strategies. Knowing the real-time potential is critical to avoid being shut out of opportunities by over-participating in the day ahead.
