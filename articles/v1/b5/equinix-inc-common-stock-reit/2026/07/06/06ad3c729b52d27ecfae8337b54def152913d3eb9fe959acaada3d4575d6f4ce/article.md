# Equinix Declares Quarterly Dividend on Its Common Stock

REDWOOD CITY, Calif.


, July 29, 2026


/PRNewswire/ --[Equinix, Inc.](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4741307-1&h=286073754&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.equinix.com%2F%3Fls%3DPublic%2520Relations%26lsd%3D21q1__no-program%2B__pr-equinix_pr-newswire_press-release__us-en_AMER_2020-Q4-earnings_awareness%26utm_campaign%3Dus-en__press-release_2020-Q4-earnings_pr-equinix_awareness%26utm_source%3D%26utm_medium%3Dpress-release%26utm_content%3Dno-program%2B_&a=Equinix%2C+Inc.) (Nasdaq: EQIX), the world's digital infrastructure company®, today announced that its Board of Directors has declared a quarterly cash dividend of $5.16 per share on its common stock. The quarterly common stock dividend will be paid on September 16, 2026, to shareholders of record on August 19, 2026.
