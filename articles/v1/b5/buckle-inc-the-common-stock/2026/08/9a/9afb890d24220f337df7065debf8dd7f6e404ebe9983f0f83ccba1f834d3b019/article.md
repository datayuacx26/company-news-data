## The Buckle, Inc. Press Release Narrative for 8/6/2026


08/06/2026


Download (opens in new window)


Our August 6, 2026 press release reported that comparable store sales, for stores open at least one full year, for the four-week period ended August 1, 2026, increased 1.6 percent in comparison to the prior year four-week period ended August 2, 2025. Total net sales for the four-week fiscal month increased 4.1 percent to $115.4 million compared to net sales of $110.8 million in the prior year four-week fiscal month ended August 2, 2025.


On the men’s side of the business, total sales for the four-week fiscal period ended August 1, 2026 decreased 1.5 percent in comparison to the prior year four-week period ended August 2, 2025. The men’s business represented approximately 47.0 percent of total sales for the month, versus approximately 49.5 percent for the same period in the prior year. For the fiscal month, overall price points on the men’s side of the business were up about 2.0 percent.


On the women’s side of the business, total sales for the four-week fiscal period ended August 1, 2026 increased 9.0 percent in comparison to the prior year four-week period ended August 2, 2025. The women’s business represented approximately 53.0 percent of total sales for the month, versus approximately 50.5 percent in the same period a year ago. For the fiscal month, overall price points on the women’s side of the business increased about 5.0 percent.


Within the men’s and women’s categories combined, accessory sales for the fiscal month increased approximately 3.0 percent in comparison to the prior year four-week period ended August 2, 2025, and footwear increased about 6.0%. These two categories accounted for approximately 10.0 percent and 4.0 percent, respectively, of both the current and prior year fiscal July net sales. Average accessory price points for the month were up about 5.0 percent, and average footwear price points increased approximately 10.5 percent.


For the month, UPT’s decreased approximately 0.5 percent while the average transaction value increased about 3.0 percent compared to the prior year four-week period ended August 2, 2025.


Buckle currently operates 447 retail stores in 42 states, compared to 440 stores in 42 states as of August 6, 2025.
