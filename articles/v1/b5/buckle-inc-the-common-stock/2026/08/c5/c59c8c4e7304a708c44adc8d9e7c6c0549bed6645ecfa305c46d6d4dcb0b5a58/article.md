## The Buckle, Inc. Reports July 2026 Net Sales


08/06/2026


Download (opens in new window)


KEARNEY, Neb.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


The Buckle, Inc. (NYSE: BKE) announced today that comparable store net sales, for stores open at least one year, for the 4-week period ended August 1, 2026 increased 1.6 percent from comparable store net sales for the 4-week period ended August 2, 2025. Net sales for the 4-week fiscal month ended August 1, 2026 increased 4.1 percent to $115.4 million from net sales of $110.8 million for the prior year 4-week fiscal month ended August 2, 2025.


Comparable store net sales for the 13-week second quarter ended August 1, 2026 increased 2.1 percent from comparable store net sales for the 13-week period ended August 2, 2025. Net sales for the 13-week fiscal second quarter ended August 1, 2026 increased 4.6 percent to $319.8 million compared to net sales of $305.7 million for the prior year 13-week fiscal second quarter ended August 2, 2025.


Comparable store net sales year-to-date for the 26-week period ended August 1, 2026 increased 3.5 percent from comparable store net sales for the 26-week period ended August 2, 2025. Net sales for the 26-week fiscal period ended August 1, 2026 increased 5.3 percent to $608.6 million compared to net sales of $577.9 million for the prior year 26-week fiscal period ended August 2, 2025.

**About Buckle**


Buckle is a specialty retailer focused on delivering exceptional service and style through unforgettable experiences. Offering a curated mix of high-quality, on-trend apparel, accessories, and footwear, Buckle is for those living the styled life. Known as a denim destination, each store carries a wide selection of fits, styles, and finishes from leading denim brands, including the Company’s exclusive brand, BKE. Headquartered in Kearney, Nebraska, Buckle currently operates 447 retail stores in 42 states, which includes the opening of one new store located in Dawsonville, Georgia earlier this week (after the fiscal month ended). The Company operated 440 stores in 42 states as of August 6, 2025. To listen to the Company’s recorded monthly sales commentary, please call (308) 238-2500.
