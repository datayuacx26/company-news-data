## The Buckle, Inc. Press Release Narrative for 6/4/2026


06/04/2026


Download (opens in new window)


Our June 4, 2026 press release reported that comparable store sales, for stores open at least one full year, for the four-week period ended May 30, 2026, increased 2.2 percent in comparison to the prior year four-week period ended May 31, 2025. Total net sales for the four-week fiscal month increased 4.5 percent to $92.4 million compared to net sales of $88.4 million in the prior year four-week fiscal month ended May 31, 2025.


On the men’s side of the business, total sales for the four-week fiscal period ended May 30, 2026 increased slightly in comparison to the prior year four-week period ended May 31, 2025. The men’s business represented approximately 50.0 percent of total sales for the month, versus approximately 52.0 percent for the same period in the prior year. For the fiscal month, overall price points on the men’s side of the business were up about 3.0 percent.


On the women’s side of the business, total sales for the four-week fiscal period ended May 30, 2026 increased 9.0 percent in comparison to the prior year four-week period ended May 31, 2025. The women’s business represented approximately 50.0 percent of total sales for the month, versus approximately 48.0 percent in the same period a year ago. For the fiscal month, overall price points on the women’s side of the business increased about 6.5 percent.


Within the men’s and women’s categories combined, accessory sales for the fiscal month increased approximately 1.5 percent in comparison to the prior year four-week period ended May 31, 2025, while footwear were flat. These two categories accounted for approximately 11.0 percent and 5.5 percent, respectively, of the current fiscal May net sales. This compares with approximately 11.5 percent and 6.0 percent for each in the prior year. Average accessory price points for the month were up about 6.0 percent, and average footwear price points increased approximately 9.0 percent.


For the month, UPT’s decreased approximately 1.0 percent while the average transaction value increased about 4.0 percent compared to the prior year four-week period ended May 31, 2025.


Buckle currently operates 445 retail stores in 42 states, compared to 438 stores in 42 states as of June 4, 2025.
