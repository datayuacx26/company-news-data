## The Buckle, Inc. Press Release Narrative for 5/7/2026


05/07/2026


Download (opens in new window)


Our May 7, 2026 press release reported that comparable store sales, for stores open at least one full year, for the four-week period ended May 2, 2026, increased 0.2 percent in comparison to the prior year four-week period ended May 3, 2025. Total net sales for the four-week fiscal month increased 0.9 percent to $86.3 million compared to net sales of $85.5 million in the prior year four-week fiscal month ended May 3, 2025.


On the men’s side of the business, total sales for the four-week fiscal period ended May 2, 2026 decreased 5.0 percent in comparison to the prior year four-week period ended May 3, 2025. The men’s business represented approximately 48.0 percent of total sales for the month, versus approximately 51.5 percent for the same period in the prior year. For the fiscal month, overall price points on the men’s side of the business were up about 3.5 percent.


On the women’s side of the business, total sales for the four-week fiscal period ended May 2, 2026 increased 9.5 percent in comparison to the prior year four-week period ended May 3, 2025. The women’s business represented approximately 52.0 percent of total sales for the month, versus approximately 48.5 percent in the same period a year ago. For the fiscal month, overall price points on the women’s side of the business increased about 7.0 percent.


Within the men’s and women’s categories combined, accessory sales for the fiscal month decreased approximately 1.0 percent in comparison to the prior year four-week period ended May 3, 2025, and footwear sales decreased about 5.0 percent. These two categories accounted for approximately 11.0 percent and 5.5 percent, respectively, of the current fiscal April net sales. This compares with approximately 11.0 percent and 6.0 percent for each in the prior year. Average accessory price points for the month were up about 6.0 percent, and average footwear price points increased approximately 11.0 percent.


For the month, UPT’s decreased approximately 1.0 percent while the average transaction value increased about 4.0 percent compared to the prior year four-week period ended May 3, 2025.


Buckle currently operates 443 retail stores in 42 states, compared to 439 stores in 42 states as of May 7, 2025.
