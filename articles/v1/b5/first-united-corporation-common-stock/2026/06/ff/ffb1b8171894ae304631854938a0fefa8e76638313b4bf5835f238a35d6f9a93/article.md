# First United Welcomes Maggie Robey as Vice President and Commercial Relationship Manager

Josh Bosley, Vice President and Managing Director of Commercial Services at[First United Bank & Trust](http://mybank.com/) , is pleased to welcome Maggie Robey as Vice President and Commercial Relationship Manager, based at the Oakland, MD, Community Office.


In her role, Robey will oversee both the sales and daily servicing of business clients, including lending, deposits, and treasury management. She’ll help grow the bank’s commercial loan and deposit portfolio, while supporting local businesses with customized financial solutions.


Robey brings 30 years of banking experience to the Team with a strong background in commercial banking sales, relationship development and management, having previously served as a commercial lending officer and market leader. Robey has a proven track record of improving both client satisfaction and business performance given her high level of customer service.


“We are excited to have Maggie joining our Commercial Services Team. Her experience, leadership, and dedication to serving business clients make her an excellent fit for this important role in our organization,” Bosley said. “She brings both strong technical expertise and a genuine commitment to relationships that will benefit our customers and our communities.”


A graduate of Southern Garrett High School in Oakland, MD, Robey is also certified as an Emergency Medical Responder. Her commitment to service extends beyond her professional role, as she actively contributes to several community organizations. She serves as President of the Bloomington Volunteer Fire Company Ladies Auxiliary and is a member of the Bloomington Volunteer Fire Company as an EMR. Additionally, she serves on the Mountain Laurel Medical Center Compliance Committee, the Board of Directors for the Garrett County Arts Council, and as a Loan Committee Member for the Tri County Council.


Robey will be located at the First United Bank & Trust Oakland Community Office, and can be reached at301-533-2320 ormarobey@MyBank.com .


First United Corporation operates one full-service commercial bank, First United Bank & Trust. The Bank has a network of community offices in Garrett, Allegany, Washington, and Frederick counties in Maryland, as well as Mineral, Berkeley, and Monongalia counties in West Virginia. First United’s website can be located at MyBank.com. As of 3/31/2026, the corporation posted assets of $2 billion.
