# First United Bank & Trust’s Erica Riley earns Certified Associate in Project Management designation

First United Bank & Trust is proud to announce that Erica Riley, Project Specialist, has successfully earned her Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM®) designation from the Project Management Institute, an internationally recognized credential demonstrating excellence in foundational project management skills.


The CAPM certification validates Riley’s ability to support and manage a wide range of projects using today’s leading methodologies, including predictive project management, agile principles, and essential business analysis techniques.


Riley has been part of the First United team for more than five years, serving in multiple customer‑focused and digital service roles, including Digital Banking Specialist, Customer Service Advisor, and Relationship Advisor. Her diverse background and strong understanding of internal systems uniquely position her to support and lead projects that drive efficiency and enhance customer experience.


“Erica’s dedication to advancing her skills is so inspiring,” said Stacey Evans, Director of Project Management. “The CAPM certification requires discipline, critical thinking, and a strong desire to grow, which are qualities Erica brings to our team every day. This achievement will further empower her to lead with confidence, deliver strong project outcomes, and continue contributing to the success of First United.”


The CAPM emphasizes a growth mindset and builds a solid foundation for leading successful projects in a rapidly changing business landscape. With this certification, Riley is equipped to apply best‑practice methodologies that will elevate the projects she supports across the organization.


First United Corporation operates one full-service commercial bank,[First United Bank & Trus](http://mybank.com/) t. The Bank has a network of community offices in Garrett, Allegany, Washington, and Frederick counties in Maryland, as well as Mineral, Berkeley, and Monongalia counties in West Virginia. First United’s website can be located at MyBank.com. The Corporation posted assets of $2.1 billion as of December 31, 2025.
