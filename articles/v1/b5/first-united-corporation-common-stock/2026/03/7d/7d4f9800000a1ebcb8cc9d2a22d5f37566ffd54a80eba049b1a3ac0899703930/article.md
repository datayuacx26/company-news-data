# First United welcomes Tin Ly as Mortgage Relationship Manager

Scott Hostetler, Managing Director of Mortgage Solutions at[First United Bank & Trust](http://mybank.com/) , is pleased to announce the addition of Tin Ly as a Mortgage Relationship Manager in the Frederick, MD, area.


In this role, Ly will guide prospective homebuyers through the mortgage process with care and expertise, supporting individuals and families as they make one of life’s most significant financial decisions.


Ly brings strong production skills, proven relationship‑building abilities, and a deep commitment to customer service. While he most recently served as a Branch Manager at an area bank, Ly is no stranger to First United. He previously worked in the bank’s Mortgage department, where he honed his ability to listen to customers, understand their stories, and match them with solutions tailored to their goals. He looks forward to applying this customer‑centered approach to help clients find the mortgage options that fit them best.


“We are excited to welcome Tin back to the Mortgage team,” said Hostetler. “He is highly engaged, passionate about helping people, and dedicated to creating positive outcomes for his clients. Tin brings a level of energy and sincerity that will be a tremendous benefit to both our customers and our partners across the community.”


Ly holds a Bachelor of Arts in Sports and Exercise Science from Messiah College. He is eager to deepen referral relationships, support local homebuyers, and contribute to First United’s continued growth in the region.


Ly will be located at the First United Bank & Trust Baughmans Lane Office in Frederick, MD, and can be reached at301-696-8169 ortly@MyBank.com


First United Corporation operates one full-service commercial bank, First United Bank & Trust. The Bank has a network of community offices in Garrett, Allegany, Washington, and Frederick counties in Maryland, as well as Mineral, Berkeley, and Monongalia counties in West Virginia. First United’s website can be located at MyBank.com. As of 12/31/2025, the corporation posted assets of $2.1 billion.
