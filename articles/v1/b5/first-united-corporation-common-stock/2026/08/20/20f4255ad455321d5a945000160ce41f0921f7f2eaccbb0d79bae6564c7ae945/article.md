# First United Bank & Trust to Host Hot Dogs for Hospice Fundraiser August 14

First United Bank & Trust invites the community to support a great cause during its annual Hot Dogs for Hospice fundraiser from 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. on Friday, August 14, 2026, at the Bank’s Oakland Community Office.


All proceeds from the event will benefit Hospice of Garrett County, helping provide compassionate care and support to individuals and families throughout the community.


Community members can enjoy a variety of lunch favorites, including beef hot dogs served plain or topped with chili and cheese. Additional menu items will be available for purchase.


Hot Dogs for Hospice will be held at:


[Oakland Community Office](https://mybank.com/location/oakland/)


19 S. Second Street


Oakland, MD 21550


First United Corporation operates one full-service commercial bank, First United Bank & Trust. The Bank has a network of community offices in Garrett, Allegany, Washington, and Frederick counties in Maryland, as well as Mineral, Berkeley, and Monongalia counties in West Virginia. First United’s website can be located at MyBank.com. As of 6/30/2026, the corporation posted assets of $2.1 billion.
