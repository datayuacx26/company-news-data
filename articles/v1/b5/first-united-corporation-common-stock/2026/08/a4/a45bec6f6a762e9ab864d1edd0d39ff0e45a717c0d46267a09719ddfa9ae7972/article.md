# First United Bank & Trust to Host Blood Drive Wednesday, August 19

OAKLAND, Md. — First United Bank & Trust is proud to host a blood drive from 9:30 a.m. – 3:00 p.m. Wednesday, August 19, at the First United Operations Center, 12892 Garrett Highway, Oakland.


Every day, blood donors help patients of all ages: accident and burn victims, heart surgery and organ transplant patients, and those battling cancer. In fact, every two seconds someone in the U.S. needs blood.


Please consider donating blood during this event.


To schedule an appointment, visit[redcrossblood.org](https://www.redcrossblood.org/) and enter zip code 21550 in the “Find a Blood Drive” search box. Click on the First United Bank & Trust link to sign up, or call 1-800-RED CROSS (1-800-733-2767 ).


First United Corporation operates one full-service commercial bank, First United Bank & Trust. The Bank has a network of community offices in Garrett, Allegany, Washington, and Frederick counties in Maryland, as well as Mineral, Berkeley, and Monongalia counties in West Virginia. First United’s website can be located at MyBank.com. As of 6/30/2026, the corporation posted assets of $2.1 billion.
