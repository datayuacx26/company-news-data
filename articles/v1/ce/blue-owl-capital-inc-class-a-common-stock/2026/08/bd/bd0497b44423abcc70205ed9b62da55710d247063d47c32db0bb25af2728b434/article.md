# Blue Owl Capital Hires Takeo Ikemori to Lead Institutional Capital in Japan

*Takeo brings over three decades of experience in financial services serving institutional clients and delivering customized fund solutions*


**NEW YORK and TOKYO, August 3, 2026** -- Blue Owl Capital Inc. (“Blue Owl”) (NYSE: OWL), a leading alternative asset manager, announced today the hiring of Takeo Ikemori as Managing Director, Representative in Japan and Head of Japan Institutional Capital.


Alongside Yoichi Nakamura, Managing Director, Representative in Japan and Head of Japan Private Wealth, Takeo will co-lead the Tokyo office as Blue Owl continues striving to strengthen its presence in Japan.


Takeo will be based in Blue Owl’s Tokyo office, reporting to Michael Dale, Managing Director and Head of Asia Institutional Capital. The appointment of Takeo adds a veteran senior leader with extensive relationships among Japanese financial institutions and a track-record of fundraising and the creation of customized portfolio solutions.


Previously, Takeo served as Managing Director, Head of FIG Coverage, Japan at Natixis Japan Securities, managing the growth of its relationships with clients. In his role, he collaborated across product teams to bring the full range of the firm’s capabilities to clients, including Japan’s leading banks, insurers, and asset managers.


Prior to Natixis, Takeo spent 16 years at Barclays, where he held a series of senior leadership roles including Managing Director, Head of Rates Sales Japan and Co-Head of Cross-Asset Solution Sales Japan. He built and led coverage for some of Japan’s largest financial institutions with responsibility for credit, equity, and fund product sales.


Michael Dale, Managing Director and Head of Institutional Capital, Asia at Blue Owl, said: “Japan's institutional market is one of the most significant in the world and is built on trust and the ability to deliver tailored solutions. With Takeo’s nearly three decades of experience building relationships across Japan's institutions, combined with the breadth of Blue Owl's $319 billion platform, we look forward to what Takeo and the team can accomplish on behalf of our clients in Japan.”


Takeo Ikemori, Representative in Japan and Head of Japan Institutional Capital at Blue Owl, said: “I am delighted to be joining such a dynamic and innovative firm. Over my career, I have had the privilege of working closely with Japan's leading financial institutions, and I have watched their interest in private markets grow considerably over that time. Blue Owl's strategies focused on income and capital preservation are well suited to what clients are looking for. I look forward to working with Michael and the broader team to continue to grow our institutional capital business in Japan.”
