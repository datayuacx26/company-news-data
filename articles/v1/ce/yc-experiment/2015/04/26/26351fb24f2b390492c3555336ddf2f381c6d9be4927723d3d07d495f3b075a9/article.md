# RFP: NASA Open Data


NASA has just announced their new portal for their open datasets and APIs ([http://open.nasa.gov/blog/2015/04/07/welcome-to-nasas-data-portal/](http://open.nasa.gov/blog/2015/04/07/welcome-to-nasas-data-portal/) ). We’d love to see new experiments done with this exciting opportunity. This is a request for proposals that will conduct new experiments or analyze the newly available data in an open and transparent way.


As this is a computational and data-heavy area, costs should be fairly low and should mainly cover computational time and man hours. The scope of proposals should be in the range of $500 to $5,000.


If we receive enough proposals, we’ll post them to the blog and make them publicly viewable. If there’s interest, we could decide democratically which projects could move forward. I am personally guaranteeing that the selected projects will get the necessary funding to get off the ground, provided they fit within the scope of cost and the goals for openness.


Anyone interested is encouraged to[create a new project at Experiment](http://experiment.com/start) , or[contact us with any questions](http://experiment.com/feedbacks/new) .


For anyone interested in helping to fund these micro experiments, watch this space!
