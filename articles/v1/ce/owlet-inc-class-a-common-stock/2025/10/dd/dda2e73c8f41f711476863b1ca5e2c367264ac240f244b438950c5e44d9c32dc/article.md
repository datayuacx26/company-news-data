With Dream Sock, you're not just getting a monitor—you’re getting a clinically-proven tool that helps you better understand your baby’s well-being and feel more confident in your care.


# FDA Clearance vs. FDA Approval: What’s the Difference?


Both FDA clearance and FDA approval involve review by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration, but they apply to different types of products:


- **FDA approval** is a more extensive process that is typically reserved for drugs and high-risk medical devices


- **FDA clearance** —which applies to Dream Sock—means that a product has been rigorously tested and reviewed for safety and effectiveness for its intended use.


So while the pathways are different, the commitment to safety and accuracy is the same. FDA clearance means the safety and performance of Dream Sock for its intended use are supported by trusted science and FDA review, which is what matters most.


# A New Warning from the FDA


On September 16, 2025, the FDA issued a public safety communication urging families to be cautious about unauthorized infant monitoring devices that claim to track vital signs, but have not been reviewed for safety or effectiveness.
