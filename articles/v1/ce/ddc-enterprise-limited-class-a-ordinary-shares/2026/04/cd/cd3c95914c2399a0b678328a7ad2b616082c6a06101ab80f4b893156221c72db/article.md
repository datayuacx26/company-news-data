# DDC Announces Fiscal Year 2025 Earnings Conference Call for April 21, 2026


April 15, 2026 9:00 AM EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_840b70dccbe560e0b046537d6b4fa238/daydaycook/news/2026-04-15_DDC_Announces_Fiscal_Year_2025_Earnings_84.pdf)


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- DDC Enterprise Limited (NYSEAMERICAN: DDC) (“DDC” or the “ **Company** ”), a global Asian food platform and digital asset treasury company, today announced that it will report its financial results for fiscal year 2025 on Tuesday, April 21, 2026 at 8:30 AM EDT. DDC will cover the financial results for the twelve months ended December 31, 2025, as well as provide a business update.


DDC will release its earnings before the call at approximately 7:30 AM EDT on April 21, 2026. A copy of the earnings release will be available on the Company’s Investor Relations website at[https://ir.ddc.xyz/](https://ir.ddc.xyz/) .


**Conference Call Information:**


- **Date** : April 21, 2026
- **Time** : 8:30 AM EDT
