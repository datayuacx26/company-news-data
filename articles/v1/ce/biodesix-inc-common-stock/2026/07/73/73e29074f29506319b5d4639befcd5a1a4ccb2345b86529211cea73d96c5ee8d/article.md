# Biodesix to Report Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results on August 5, 2026

LOUISVILLE, Colo., July 22, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Biodesix, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=pAtC_-pLNWuyFQX5_jqydwLPnfiHTZs_9Elrbi5TXzHejVoxUa3KPqtX_nLWUcWnloUqERv9uA-tELK9frD91tCYRNXmA1ZI30zHJM671kE=) (Nasdaq: BDSX), a leading diagnostic solutions company, today announced that it will release financial results for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026 after the close of trading on Wednesday, August 5. Biodesix management will host a conference call and webcast to discuss its financial results and provide a general business update at 4:30 p.m. Eastern Time on the same day.


Listeners can register for the webcast via this[link](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=ZgRE3W2BLkUZS7JLbRwKWXsEFpZbE1EbcyiAtdqifUR4c6sf8G8tfcCt67MmCQAcj39IQdV9U0wCE-3-UzIY120jmdVbg93ixKY2IsGHmQs=) . Analysts who wish to participate in the question and answer session should use this[link](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=ZgRE3W2BLkUZS7JLbRwKWdbArS7r_sIEaEvkLl21fcW6hScOSX3MIE5Ujtb3lDAbPXBT1uwdJk476CCf9-RIWWVCuLcGX9nyTCZP_SW3k952j2cdhTcjxAJwfB_1PJNluV4gVx2o0be61KrMjp33P7TX1d9I0C9ZD1m3d6vkPtA=) . A replay of the webcast will be available via the company’s investor website approximately two hours after the call’s conclusion. Participants are advised to join 15 minutes prior to the start time.


**About Biodesix:**


Biodesix is a leading diagnostic solutions company, driven to improve clinical care and outcomes for patients. **Biodesix Diagnostic Tests** , including Nodify Lung® Nodule Risk Assessment and IQLung® Cancer Treatment Guidance, support clinical decisions to expedite personalized care and improve outcomes for patients with lung disease. **Biodesix Development Services** enable the world’s leading biopharmaceutical, life sciences, and research institutions with scientific, technological, and operational capabilities that fuel the development of diagnostic tests, tools, and therapeutics. For more information, visit[biodesix.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=BXn96eUOMVTs9fpyIqPJA-DgHNk-fdKoJbIpdVc_yzQzri4rxco7YgrjFmKIZPP3HWvF9vpGRuYoCvMbAR9SPA==) .
