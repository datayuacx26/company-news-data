**About Company**


U.S. Gold Corp. is a publicly traded, U.S. focused gold and copper exploration and development company. U.S. Gold Corp. has a portfolio of exploration properties. The Company's CK Gold Project is located in Southeast Wyoming and has a Feasibility Study technical report, which was completed by Halyard-Micon International, Inc. The Company's Keystone exploration property is on the Cortez Trend in Nevada. The Company's Challis Gold Project is located in Idaho. For more information about U.S. Gold Corp., please visit[https://www.usgoldcorp.com/](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4689591-1&h=1126433287&u=https%3A%2F%2Fedge.prnewswire.com%2Fc%2Flink%2F%3Ft%3D0%26l%3Den%26o%3D4660289-1%26h%3D967527427%26u%3Dhttps%253A%252F%252Fwww.usgoldcorp.com%252F%26a%3Dhttps%253A%252F%252Fwww.usgoldcorp.com%252F&a=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.usgoldcorp.com%2F) .
