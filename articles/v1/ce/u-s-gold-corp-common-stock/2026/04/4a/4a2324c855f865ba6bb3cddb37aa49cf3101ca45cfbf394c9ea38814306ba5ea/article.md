# U.S. GOLD CORP. TO PARTICIPATE IN THE INAUGURAL SWISS MINING INSTITUTE CONFERENCE IN PANAMA CITY APRIL 15-16 :: U.S. Gold Corp. (USAU)

CHEYENNE, Wyo.


, April 9, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- U.S. Gold Corp. (NASDAQ: USAU) (the "Company" or "U.S. Gold") today announces its participation in the inaugural Swiss Mining Institute Conference (the "Conference"), taking place in Panama City, Panama on April 15-16, 2026.


U.S. Gold Corp. CEO George Bee will deliver a presentation at the Conference on April 15 at 2:00 p.m. local time. Mr. Bee will provide an overview of the Company's strategic direction, key projects, and initiatives aimed at enhancing long-term shareholder value.


The Swiss Mining Institute Conference brings together industry leaders, investors, and mining professionals to discuss key developments, investment opportunities, and the future of the global mining industry.


For more information about the Conference and to register, please visit:[https://swissmininginstitute.ch/](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4660289-1&h=707166869&u=https%3A%2F%2Fswissmininginstitute.ch%2F&a=https%3A%2F%2Fswissmininginstitute.ch%2F)


**About U.S. Gold Corp.**


U.S. Gold Corp. is a publicly traded, U.S. focused gold and copper exploration and development company. U.S. Gold Corp. has a portfolio of exploration properties. The Company's CK Gold Project is located in Southeast Wyoming and has a Feasibility Study technical report, which was completed by Halyard-Micon International, Inc. The Company's Keystone exploration property is on the Cortez Trend in Nevada. The Company's Challis Gold Project is located in Idaho. For more information about U.S. Gold Corp., please visit[https://www.usgoldcorp.com/](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4660289-1&h=967527427&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.usgoldcorp.com%2F&a=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.usgoldcorp.com%2F) .
