## How to Choose the Right Staffing Alternative


### Can the platform fill shifts quickly?


If demand changes daily, speed matters first. Look for platforms that can fill shifts in hours, not days. Slow fill times can hurt output, service, and labor planning.


### Are the workers reliable and vetted?


Worker quality affects productivity from the first shift. Review vetting methods, attendance performance, and post-shift ratings. Reliable workers reduce rework and manager time.


### Do you need temporary or direct hiring?


Some tools are built for shift staffing. Others are built for job applications. Choose based on whether you need labor now, long-term temporary support, or full-time hires.
