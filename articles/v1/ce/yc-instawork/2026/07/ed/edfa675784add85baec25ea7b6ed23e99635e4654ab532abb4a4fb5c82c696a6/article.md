### PeopleReady


Trustpilot Rating:[2.1](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/peopleready.com)


PeopleReady is one of the most direct alternatives because it serves many of the same temporary labor needs. It can work well for on-site, local, and short-term staffing. Businesses comparing it with Instawork may focus on how much visibility they want into worker selection, scheduling, and time tracking.


### Randstad


Trustpilot Rating:[2.0](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/randstadusa.com)


Randstad is a large staffing agency with broad coverage across industries and role types. It may fit businesses hiring at scale or across multiple departments. Compared with platform-based options, it is often better suited for companies that prefer agency support over self-serve speed.


### Adecco


Adecco is another large staffing agency option for temporary and permanent hiring. It may appeal to businesses that want a traditional recruiter-led process. Companies that need shifts filled faster may compare it against Instawork’s dispatch model and app-based workflow.


### Manpower


Manpower supports temporary, contract, and permanent hiring in many markets. It is often considered by businesses that need broad labor support. If your priority is shift-by-shift flexibility and fast fulfillment, platform alternatives may be easier to manage day to day.


### Kelly


Trustpilot Rating:[2.3](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/kellyservices.com)


Kelly is a long-standing staffing provider with experience in office, education, science, and industrial hiring. It may be a fit for businesses with specialized hiring needs. For employers focused on local hourly staffing, comparing speed and attendance controls matters.


### Upwork


Trustpilot Rating:[3.9](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/upwork.com)


Upwork is different from TrueBlue because it focuses on freelance talent rather than local shift labor. It can help with remote project work, admin support, and specialized tasks. It is less suited for businesses that need in-person workers for scheduled shifts.


### Indeed


Trustpilot Rating:[2.5](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.indeed.com)


Indeed is a job board, not a staffing agency or on-demand labor platform. It can drive applicant volume for permanent and hourly roles. Businesses choosing between Indeed and Instawork should decide whether they want applicants to screen themselves or ready-to-work labor matched quickly.


### ZipRecruiter


Trustpilot Rating:[4.5](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/ziprecruiter.com)


ZipRecruiter is another job board option for employers building a candidate pipeline. It may fit businesses with more time to review, contact, and interview applicants. It is less aligned with urgent shift coverage than a staffing marketplace.


### Snagajob


Trustpilot Rating:[1.8](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.snagajob.com)


Snagajob focuses on hourly hiring and can help businesses attract frontline applicants. It may be useful for ongoing recruiting. Teams that need workers to claim open shifts quickly may prefer a marketplace with dispatch, vetting, and attendance tracking built in.


### Craigslist


Trustpilot Rating:[1.3](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.craigslist.org)


Craigslist is a low-cost job posting option for local hiring. It can generate responses, but screening and coordination stay manual. Businesses often compare it with managed or marketplace options when reliability and time savings matter more than posting cost alone.

## How To Choose The Right Staffing Alternative


### Can The Platform Fill Shifts Quickly?


Speed affects revenue, service levels, and overtime costs. If you need coverage in hours, a staffing marketplace may fit better than a recruiter-led process or job board.


### Are The Workers Reliable And Vetted?


Worker quality affects output and manager time. Look for vetting, ratings, attendance history, and repeat worker performance before choosing a provider.


### Do You Need More Hiring Flexibility?


Some businesses need one shift covered. Others need long-term temporary or full-time hiring. Choose a partner that supports the labor mix you actually need.
