## How To Choose The Right Staffing Alternative


### Can The Platform Fill Shifts Quickly?


If open shifts hurt revenue, speed matters first. Look for a provider that can fill roles within hours, not days. Platforms built for shift dispatch usually move faster than job boards or branch-led agencies.


### Are The Workers Reliable And Vetted?


A filled shift only helps if the worker shows up and can do the job. Ask how workers are screened, rated, and matched. Reliable attendance and repeat worker quality reduce daily operational risk.


### Do You Need Temporary Or Full-Time Hiring?


Some providers focus on same-day labor. Others support long-term temporary work or direct hire. Choose a partner that matches your actual workforce mix so you do not need multiple vendors.


### Is Pricing Clear Before You Commit?


Hidden fees make labor planning harder. Ask how markups, conversion fees, and minimums work. Clear pricing helps you compare total labor cost, not just hourly pay.
