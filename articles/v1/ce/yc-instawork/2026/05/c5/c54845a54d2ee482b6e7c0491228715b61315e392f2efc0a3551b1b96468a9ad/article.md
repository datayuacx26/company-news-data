## How To Choose The Right Staffing Alternative


### Can The Platform Fill Shifts Quickly?


Start with speed. If open shifts hurt output or service, slow hiring creates real cost. Ask how quickly workers can be placed and whether the provider supports same-day or next-day needs. Instawork is built for speed, with shifts filled from two hours to less than 24 hours.


### Are The Workers Reliable And Vetted?


Fill speed only matters if workers show up ready to work. Ask how workers are screened, rated, and matched. Instawork vets workers, conducts skill assessment calls at scale, and gives businesses worker ratings after shifts. That helps improve quality over time.


### Do You Need Agency Support Or Self-Serve Control?


Some businesses want recruiter support. Others want to post shifts and manage labor directly. Agencies can reduce internal work, but platforms often give more speed and visibility. Choose based on your team capacity and how often your staffing needs change.


### Is Pricing Clear Before You Commit?


Labor costs need to be predictable. Ask how markups, conversion fees, overtime, and minimums work. A clear pricing model makes comparison easier and reduces surprises. Transparency matters when you are managing margins across many shifts.
