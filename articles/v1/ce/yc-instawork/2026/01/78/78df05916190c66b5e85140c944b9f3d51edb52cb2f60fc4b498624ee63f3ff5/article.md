# Employee Spotlight: Haig, Staff Software Engineer

Our mission at Instawork is to create economic opportunities for local businesses and professionals, globally. In order to achieve that mission, we have built a team composed of talented and hard-working individuals who are passionate about connecting skilled hourly professionals with local businesses who need their services.


Our *Employee Spotlight* series takes the opportunity to highlight members of our team and to give a glimpse into our culture and what it’s like to work at Instawork. To highlight the diversity of our team, we’ve asked ***Haig, Staff Software Engineer,*** to share more about her experience working at Instawork.


‍


### **What do you do at Instawork and how long have you been with the company?**


I’ve been building software and leading larger technical projects with Instawork for close to a year.


‍


### **What initially attracted you to Instawork?**


Purpose powering what I’m doing, while working with extremely talented and thoughtful people, and being empowered to try novel, tech-focused approaches to solve hard and challenging problems.


‍


### **What's your favorite part of your job?**


Making an impact on Pros by flexing their talents and creating income. There's endless potential to grow opportunities and make it easier and more intuitive for Pros and partners to gain value from our platform.


‍


‍


### **What's the most important thing you've learned since starting at Instawork?**


When you collaborate and deeply understand an issue, problems and dead ends become opportunities to learn and try a novel approach by embracing the latest technology.


‍


‍


### **In 10 words or less, how would you describe the culture at Instawork?**


Owning tough problems and solving collaboratively.
