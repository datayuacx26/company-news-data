# Best Weekend Jobs to Make Extra Cash

Looking for a way to make extra money on the weekend? Weekend jobs are a great option if you want flexible hours, quick cash, or a way to build new skills without committing to a full-time role. Many businesses are busiest on Saturdays and Sundays, which means more shifts, higher demand, and often better pay. Here are some of the best weekend jobs and why they’re worth considering.


## What's a good weekend job?


A good weekend job offers flexible hours, quick onboarding, and steady pay without long-term commitments. Popular options include warehouse shifts, event staffing, hospitality roles, delivery driving, and gig-based work. These jobs are ideal for students, part-time workers, or anyone looking to earn extra income on Saturdays and Sundays while keeping their weekday schedule open.

## How to Get a Weekend Job


The fastest way to land a weekend-only job is to go in person. Bring a printed resume and explain clearly that you’re looking for weekend-only work.


Build rapport with employees when you visit. Let them know you’re open to weekends and ask if they can pass your resume to a manager or HR. Full-time staff often prefer weekdays, so managers are usually happy to hire people who specifically want weekend shifts.


## Highest Paying Weekend Jobs


When people think of weekend work, they often imagine lower-paid roles like retail or entry-level hospitality. But many weekend gigs pay **well above average** , especially once you factor in tips or demand spikes on Saturdays and Sundays.


### Delivery & Rideshare Driving


Driving for food delivery services (like DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub) or rideshare platforms can be lucrative on weekend nights and busy afternoons — many workers report significantly higher earnings due to peak demand and tips, often **$15–$30+ per hour** before expenses.


### Bartending & Event Service Work


Bartenders at busy weekend venues often earn a modest base wage but make the bulk of their income from **tips** , which can push effective earnings well above typical hourly rates — especially at private events or high-traffic bars.


### Pet Sitting & Dog Walking


Weekend schedules are ideal for pet care services. Platforms like Rover and Wag match sitters with owners, and rates typically range from **$15–$50+ per hour** depending on service type and location.


### Tutoring & Specialized Freelance Work


If you have expertise in a subject area, tutoring can pay **$20–$50+ per hour** — especially for test prep or STEM topics. Similarly, freelance gigs like writing, design, or coding can command premium rates for weekend projects.


### House Cleaning & Brand Promotion


Weekend house cleaners often earn **$20–$50/hour** , particularly for deep cleans or turnover jobs (e.g., Airbnbs). Brand ambassadors and promotional staff can also earn **$18–$30+ per hour** working weekend events or retail pop-ups.

## Where to Find Weekend Jobs


Finding quality weekend jobs (especially well-paying ones) requires knowing where employers and gig platforms list them. Here are the best places to start your search:


### Job Boards & Aggregators


General job sites like **Indeed** , **Snagajob** , and **Talent.com** list weekend-focused positions across industries — from hospitality to healthcare to logistics. You can filter specifically for **part-time or weekend schedules** .


### Gig & Temporary Work Apps


Apps like **Instawork** and **Indeed Flex** let you book weekend shifts on demand. These platforms match you with short-term gigs — such as event work, restaurant shifts, and warehouse jobs — often with flexible scheduling and fast pay.


### Company Career Pages


Many businesses that rely on weekend staff (retail stores, supermarkets, hospitals, and logistics firms) list openings directly on their careers pages. If you know local employers in your area, check those pages weekly because weekend roles can fill fast.


### Local Community Boards & Social Platforms


City-specific Facebook groups, Reddit job threads, community bulletin boards, and apps like **Nextdoor** or **Craigslist** often have *local gigs* that don’t get posted on big job boards — these can include farmers markets, food trucks, event help, or weekend service gigs.


### Networking & Direct Outreach


Especially for seasonal, hospitality, or service-industry weekend work, dropping off a resume in person or asking managers directly about weekend availability can be very effective. Many small businesses prefer to interview walk-ins, especially when they’re short-staffed.

## FAQ


### How to make money on weekends from home?


There are several ways to earn money from home on weekends depending on your skills and availability. Common options include freelance work such as writing, graphic design, or virtual assistance, as well as remote customer support or online tutoring. You can also sell products online, manage social media for small businesses, or complete microtasks on gig platforms. Choosing flexible work that allows you to log in for a few hours on Saturday or Sunday makes it easier to generate extra income without interfering with your weekday schedule.


### What warehouse jobs are best for students or people looking for weekend work?


Some of the best warehouse jobs for weekend workers include picking and packing orders, sorting packages, loading and unloading trucks, and inventory counting. These roles typically offer flexible shifts and do not always require prior experience, making them ideal for students or people looking to earn extra cash on weekends. Many warehouses also offer short shifts during busy periods, which allows workers to choose hours that fit around school or other commitments.


### Where should I look for weekend job opportunities?


You can find weekend job opportunities on job boards, gig work platforms, and staffing apps that specialize in flexible shifts. Local businesses such as restaurants, retail stores, warehouses, and event venues often need extra help on weekends and regularly post openings online. Checking company career pages, local community groups, and job marketplaces focused on hourly work can also help you find roles that specifically offer Saturday or Sunday shifts.


### How to find Saturday jobs?


To find Saturday jobs, start by searching job boards using filters such as “weekend,” “Saturday,” or “part-time.” Many gig platforms and staffing apps allow you to browse available shifts and choose ones that fit your schedule. Retail stores, restaurants, warehouses, and event companies often need extra staff on Saturdays, so checking listings from these industries can increase your chances of finding consistent weekend work.


### Where can I find weekend jobs quickly?


If you need a weekend job quickly, look for platforms that offer on-demand or shift-based work. These services allow workers to apply for available shifts and start working once approved. Businesses in hospitality, retail, events, and warehousing frequently need last-minute coverage for busy weekends, which means opportunities can open up with little notice and allow you to start earning right away.


### How to make extra income on weekends?


Making extra income on weekends usually comes down to choosing flexible work that fits your availability. Many people pick up short shifts in industries like hospitality, retail, or warehouses, while others take on freelance projects or gig work. You can also combine multiple small opportunities, such as a Saturday shift plus a few hours of online work, to increase your total earnings without committing to a full second job.


### What are the top sites for weekend part-time jobs?


Several online platforms specialize in part-time and flexible work opportunities. Job boards, gig apps, and staffing platforms often list weekend shifts in industries such as hospitality, retail, delivery, and warehouse operations. Using platforms that focus on hourly or shift-based work can make it easier to find weekend jobs because they allow you to browse available shifts and apply quickly for roles that match your schedule.
