### Wonolo


Trustpilot Rating:[2.4](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wonolo.com)


Wonolo is another on-demand labor platform for hourly shifts. It can work well for businesses that want flexible labor access. Compared with Instawork, buyers should review worker quality controls, speed to fill, and repeat worker consistency for their market.


### Bluecrew


Bluecrew focuses on hourly staffing and flexible labor. It may fit employers that prefer a different worker classification model. Compared with Instawork, it is best to evaluate local worker supply, fill speed, and platform ease of use before deciding.


### Indeed


Trustpilot Rating:[2](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.indeed.com)


Indeed is a job board, not an on-demand staffing marketplace. It can help if you want applicant volume across many job types. Compared with Instawork, it usually requires more hands-on screening, scheduling, and follow-up, which can slow hiring for urgent shifts.


### PeopleReady


Trustpilot Rating:[2](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/peopleready.com)


PeopleReady is a traditional staffing agency with a focus on temporary labor. It can suit businesses that want branch-based support. Compared with Instawork, agency models may offer less real-time control inside a self-serve platform and can be slower for very fast shift coverage.


### Snagajob


Trustpilot Rating:[1.8](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.snagajob.com)


Snagajob is a hiring marketplace for hourly roles. It may help employers build an applicant pipeline. Compared with Instawork, it is often a better fit for planned hiring than last-minute shift fill, since employers still manage much of the recruiting process.


### Veryable


Trustpilot Rating:[3.1](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/veryableops.com)


Veryable serves operations roles, especially in manufacturing and warehousing. It may fit businesses in those sectors. Compared with Instawork, buyers should assess role coverage, local worker depth, and whether they need broader support across more industries.


### Staffmark


Trustpilot Rating:[2.9](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/staffmark.com)


Staffmark is a staffing agency for light industrial, clerical, and professional hiring. It can work for employers that want a traditional recruiting partner. Compared with Instawork, agency workflows may involve more manual coordination and less direct visibility into shift-level performance.


### Qwick


Trustpilot Rating:[2.6](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/qwick.com)


Qwick focuses on hospitality staffing. It may be a fit for restaurants, events, and catering teams. Compared with Instawork, businesses should compare coverage by market, role depth, and flexibility if they hire beyond hospitality.


### ShiftSmart


Trustpilot Rating:[1.3](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.shiftsmart.com)


ShiftSmart supports labor needs in field operations, retail, and support work. It can be useful for distributed teams. Compared with Instawork, businesses should review worker vetting, fill reliability, and how well the platform supports repeat high performers.


### Express Employment Professionals


Trustpilot Rating:[2](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/expresspros.com)


Express Employment Professionals is a staffing agency for temporary and direct-hire roles. It may suit employers that want local recruiter support. Compared with Instawork, businesses should consider whether they need branch-based service or a faster marketplace model for ongoing shift demand.


## How To Choose The Right Staffing Alternative


### Can The Platform Fill Shifts Quickly?


Urgent demand needs fast coverage. Compare how quickly each option can deliver confirmed workers. Platforms built for shift-based staffing often move faster than job boards or traditional agencies.


### Are The Workers Reliable And Vetted?


Fill speed means little if workers do not show up. Look for vetting, post-shift ratings, and evidence of repeat worker quality. Reliable attendance reduces manager workload and service risk.


### Do You Need Platform Control Or Recruiter Support?


Some businesses want self-serve speed. Others want more recruiter involvement. Choose based on your team capacity, hiring volume, and need for visibility into billing, attendance, and worker performance.
