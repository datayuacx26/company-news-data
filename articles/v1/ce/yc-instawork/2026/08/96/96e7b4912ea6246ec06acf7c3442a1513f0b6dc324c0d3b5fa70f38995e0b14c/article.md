## Job brief


We are seeking a skilled **Retail Merchandiser** to enhance our product visibility and drive sales in stores. In this role, you will be responsible for managing **point-of-purchase displays** and **shelf management services** to ensure our products are effectively showcased. You will determine your call schedule by prioritizing tasks in collaboration with your supervisor, focusing on **product promotions** , **new product launches** , and **price adjustments** .


Your responsibilities will include maintaining strong relationships with **store managers** , **department heads** , and staff, addressing inquiries, and fulfilling special requests while highlighting **product features** . You will ensure that store shelves are well-maintained by monitoring **product displays** , removing damaged items, and optimizing the presentation of our products.


**Inventory management** is a key aspect of this role; you will restock shelves, monitor inventory levels, and coordinate with store management for reorders when necessary. Additionally, you will assist in the return and credit process for damaged products.


You will complete **call reports** by analyzing competitor displays and pricing, and support field sales representatives with special promotions by setting up displays, monitoring customer reactions, and providing feedback to management. Your insights on the growth, expansion, or closure of retail locations within your territory will be invaluable.


To succeed in this position, you should have a keen eye for detail, strong analytical skills, and a passion for retail. Your commitment to maintaining high-quality standards and enhancing our merchandizing reputation will contribute significantly to our overall success.
