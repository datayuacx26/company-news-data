## What Is a Warehouse Inventory Management System and What Does It Do?


A Warehouse Inventory Management System (WIMS) is software designed to track inventory levels, locations, and movement within a warehouse.


It focuses primarily on what inventory you have, where it is stored, and when it moves, rather than managing every warehouse operation.


Core functions include:


- Real-time inventory tracking
- Stock level alerts and reorder points
- Location-based inventory (bins, shelves, zones)
- Receiving and stock updates
- Barcode scanning and SKU management
- Basic reporting and inventory analytics


Warehouse inventory management systems are often:


- Easier to use than full WMS platforms
- More affordable
- Ideal for small to mid-sized warehouses


## How to Choose the Right Warehouse Inventory Management System for Your Business


Choosing the right system depends on warehouse size, order volume, and operational complexity.


### Key factors to consider:


**1. Warehouse size & complexity**


- Small warehouse → simple inventory software
- Multi-location warehouse → advanced inventory tracking


**2. Inventory volume**


- Hundreds of SKUs → basic system
- Thousands of SKUs → scalable, automated system


**3. Barcode scanning**


- Look for mobile or handheld scanner support
- Barcode scanning significantly reduces picking errors


**4. Integrations**


- Ecommerce platforms (Shopify, Amazon, WooCommerce)
- ERP or accounting tools (QuickBooks, NetSuite)


**5. Cloud vs on-premise**


- Cloud-based = faster setup, lower upfront cost
- On-premise = more control, higher IT effort


**6. Budget**


- Free or low-cost tools for small teams
- Enterprise systems for high-volume operations

## Warehouse Management System vs Inventory Management System: What’s the Difference?


These two are often confused, but they serve different purposes.


Feature Inventory Management System Warehouse Management System


Tracks inventory levels ✅ Yes ✅ Yes


Controls warehouse workflows ❌ No ✅ Yes


Manages picking & packing Limited Advanced


Labor & task management ❌ No ✅ Yes


Best for Small to mid-sized warehouses Complex, high-volume operations


**Simple rule:**


- Inventory system = tracks stock
- WMS = runs the warehouse


## How Inventory Control Works Inside a Warehouse Management System


In a full **Warehouse Management System (WMS)** , inventory control is just one component.


A WMS adds:


- Directed putaway
- Optimized picking routes
- Real-time inventory movement tracking
- Cycle counting automation
- Inventory accuracy by worker and zone


This level of control is ideal for:


- High-volume fulfillment centers
- 3PLs
- Large distribution warehouses

## FAQ


### What’s the best system for managing warehouse stock levels?


The best systems provide real-time visibility, automated reorder alerts, and support for multiple SKUs. Look for WMS platforms like NetSuite, Fishbowl, or Zoho Inventory that fit your warehouse size and workflow.


### What is the best tool for real-time warehouse inventory tracking?


Real-time tracking tools use barcodes, RFID, or mobile scanning to update stock levels instantly. Systems like Oracle WMS, Manhattan, and NetSuite offer reliable real-time inventory monitoring.


### What are the best warehouse scanning systems?


Handheld scanners, mobile devices, and RFID readers improve picking accuracy and speed. Top options include Zebra, Honeywell, and Datalogic scanning systems.


### Should we implement a barcoding system to improve picking accuracy?


Yes. Barcoding reduces errors, speeds up order fulfillment, and ensures accurate inventory tracking. It’s especially effective in medium to large warehouses with high SKU volumes.


### What are the best warehouse inventory management systems?


Leading systems include NetSuite, Fishbowl, and Zoho Inventory, which combine stock tracking, order management, and reporting. Choose one that matches your warehouse size and operational needs.


### How does warehouse inventory management work?


Warehouse inventory management tracks stock from receipt to shipping, monitors quantities, and ensures products are in the right location. Modern systems automate counting, reporting, and reorder alerts.


### Which warehouse management system should I choose?


Select a WMS based on warehouse size, SKU volume, and integration needs. Popular choices for small and mid-sized warehouses include NetSuite, Fishbowl, and Zoho Inventory.


### What are the best warehouse inventory control systems?


Inventory control systems like NetSuite WMS, Manhattan, and Fishbowl provide accurate stock counts, automated alerts, and support multi-location warehouses.


### What’s the best inventory management platform for high SKU volumes?


Platforms with advanced tracking, reporting, and reorder automation like Oracle WMS, Manhattan, and NetSuite are ideal for warehouses with thousands of SKUs.


### What are the best warehouse operations management tools?


Top tools combine inventory tracking, labor management, and order fulfillment, such as NetSuite, Fishbowl, and Manhattan WMS. These systems help streamline warehouse workflows and reduce errors.
