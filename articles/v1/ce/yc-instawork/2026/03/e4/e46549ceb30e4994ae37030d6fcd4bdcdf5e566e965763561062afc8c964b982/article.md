## Differences between event staffing software, event staffing applications, and event staffing apps


### What are event staffing apps


Instead of relying on agencies or manual hiring, event staff apps streamline the process of hiring staff for various events.


**These digital platforms typically allow you to:**


- Post shift details and role descriptions
- Filter or receive candidate matches
- Review detailed profiles (background checks, experience, ratings)
- Book and manage staff via an app or desktop


**They also include features like:**


- Scheduling
- Messaging
- Worker time tracking (clock in and clock out)
- Payments
- Worker roster


### What is an event staffing software


Event staffing software is a full-scale platform that manages all aspects of event staffing, from scheduling and payroll to compliance and reporting. It’s ideal for large venues or agencies that need detailed control over complex staffing operations.


### What are event staffing applications (application software)


Event staffing applications are accessible tools that offer core staffing features like shift management, worker communication, and basic reporting. They’re suited for smaller events or mid-sized teams and often integrate with other systems to streamline workflows.
