## FAQs


### Can a hospitality staffing app replace a hospitality staffing agency?


In many cases, hospitality staffing apps *can replace traditional agencies for filling shift-based or urgent roles* because they offer real-time matching, mobile booking, and flexible scheduling without long recruitment cycles. However, agencies may still be valuable for executive placements, complex payroll handling, or compliance support in specific jurisdictions.

[Instawork](https://www.instawork.com/) is a mobile app that lets hospitality workers find shifts and let businesses post open staffing needs directly from their phones.


### How do hospitality staffing apps work?


Most hospitality staffing apps let employers post shifts or jobs, workers apply or claim available shifts, and both sides manage scheduling, communications, and sometimes payments via mobile interfaces.


### Are hospitality staffing apps good for small businesses?


Yes — many apps (like[Instawork](https://www.instawork.com/) ) are scalable and let small restaurants or venues post a few shifts at a time, helping them handle last-minute shortages without agency overhead.


### Do hospitality staffing apps handle payroll?


Some apps provide built-in payroll or pay-processing features, while others simply handle shift booking and workforce communication; additional payroll tools or integrations may still be needed for full compliance.


### Do hospitality staffing apps screen workers?


Many platforms include vetting such as background checks, skill verification, or ratings based on performance to help businesses find reliable staff.
