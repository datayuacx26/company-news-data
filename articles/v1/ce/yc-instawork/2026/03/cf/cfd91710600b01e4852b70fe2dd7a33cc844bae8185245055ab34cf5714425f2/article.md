# What Is a Staffing Agency?

A staffing agency is a company that connects employers with workers.


They help businesses find temporary, permanent, or contract employees and handle parts of the hiring process like posting job openings, screening candidates, and sometimes managing payroll or benefits.


Workers use staffing agencies to find jobs quickly, gain access to multiple employers, and sometimes get specialized placement in industries like healthcare, warehouse work, IT, or hospitality.


Essentially, staffing agencies act as a bridge between companies needing staff and people looking for work. They are also called recruitment firms, consulting companies, or employment agencies.


Staffing agencies serve a wide range of industries, including:


- healthcare
- technology
- logistics
- finance
- engineering
- hospitality
- administrative services

## Types of Placement


### Temporary (Temp) Staff


Short-term assignments help cover seasonal demand, special projects, or unexpected staff absences.


### Temp-to-Hire


These positions start as temporary roles with the possibility of converting to permanent employment if the fit works for both parties.


### Direct-Hire or Permanent Candidates


Agencies help businesses find full-time employees for ongoing roles, streamlining the search for permanent talent.


## Types of Staffing Agencies


### General Staffing Agency


These agencies handle a variety of roles across different industries, making them a flexible solution for both employers and job seekers.


### Specialized Staffing Agency


These agencies focus on specific industries or types of roles, such as IT, healthcare, or industrial jobs, providing targeted recruitment for specialized positions.


### Temporary Staffing Agency


These agencies provide workers for short-term assignments, helping businesses manage seasonal demands or unexpected absences.


### Remote Staffing Agency


These agencies specialize in connecting employers with remote employees, often for roles in customer service, IT, marketing, or administrative support.


### Administrative Staffing Agency


Focuses on office and clerical roles, including receptionists, executive assistants, data entry clerks, and office managers.


### Legal Staffing Agency


Specializes in placing paralegals, legal assistants, contract attorneys, and other legal professionals.


### Technology Staffing Agency


Provides IT professionals such as developers, system administrators, cybersecurity experts, and data analysts.


### Warehouse Staffing Agency


Focuses on logistics and warehouse roles like forklift operators, pickers/packers, and inventory specialists.


### Accounting Staffing Agency


Places finance and accounting professionals, including bookkeepers, accountants, and auditors.


### Engineering Staffing Agency


Connects companies with engineers in fields such as civil, mechanical, electrical, and software engineering.
