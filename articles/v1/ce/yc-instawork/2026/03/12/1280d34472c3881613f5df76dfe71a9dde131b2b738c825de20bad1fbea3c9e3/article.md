## What Is Restaurant Staffing Software?


Restaurant staffing software is technology designed to help restaurant owners and managers schedule, hire, and manage employees more effectively. These platforms reduce the chaos of last-minute shift changes, high turnover, and manual spreadsheets by providing real-time visibility into your workforce.


With restaurant staffing software, managers can:


- Create optimized schedules for front- and back-of-house staff
- Track hours, attendance, and labor costs
- Match employees to shifts based on skills, certifications, or availability
- Forecast staffing needs for peak periods like weekends or holidays
- Access temporary or on-demand workers when needed


[Instawork](https://www.instawork.com/) , for example, allows restaurants to instantly book vetted, pre-screened workers for shifts, making it easy to handle last-minute staffing needs or temporary labor surges.

### Point-of-Sale (POS) Integrated Labor Management
