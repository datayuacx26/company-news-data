## Expert advice


[George Wojciechowski](https://www.linkedin.com/in/george-wojciechowski-870b814/) , co-founder of ShipBob and Manifest.co, shares three key tips that have proven successful for his team at Manifest:


1. **Offer Flexibility** : Recognize that employees value control over their schedules. Provide flexible shifts and strive for predictable hours to attract and retain talent.


2. **Invest in Growth** : Create opportunities for upskilling, certifications, and clear career paths. This approach keeps employees motivated and engaged for the long term.


3. **Emphasize Purpose & Inclusion** : Foster a culture-driven environment focused on making a collaborative difference. Embrace sustainability and diversity to attract talent aligned with your mission.


These strategies highlight the importance of adapting to workers' needs and creating a workplace that offers more than just a paycheck. By implementing these approaches, warehouse operators can position themselves as employers of choice in a competitive labor market.
