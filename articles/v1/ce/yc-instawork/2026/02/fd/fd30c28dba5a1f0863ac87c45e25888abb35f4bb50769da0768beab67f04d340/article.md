# Custodian Interview Questions (With Sample Answers)

Custodians keep facilities safe, clean, and welcoming, protecting health and extending the life of floors, fixtures, and equipment. Interviews can be challenging because they must uncover reliability, safety habits, and hands-on skill across different facility types and shifts. This guide provides practical Custodian interview questions, sample answers, and evaluation tips for candidates and employers.

## General and Motivation Questions


### What interests you about working as a custodian at our facility?


**Sample Answer:** I enjoy keeping spaces safe and presentable, and your facility’s high traffic makes consistent standards essential. The role matches my strengths in reliability, detail, and teamwork, and I’m excited about contributing to a clean, welcoming environment for everyone who uses the space.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Clear motivation tied to the facility type, schedule, and standards
- Examples of reliability and pride in work quality
- Awareness of how cleanliness impacts health, safety, and user experience


### How do you handle stress or repetitive tasks during long shifts?


**Sample Answer:** I follow a checklist and break work into manageable blocks, which keeps me focused and consistent. Short stretch breaks and rotating tasks help prevent fatigue while maintaining quality and pace.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Concrete habits: checklists, pacing, rotation, or timed intervals
- Commitment to quality despite repetition or time pressure
- Healthy coping strategies that remain professional and safe

## Experience and Tools Questions


### Describe your experience with floor care equipment and different floor types.


**Sample Answer:** I’ve used auto-scrubbers, burnishers, and buffers on VCT, terrazzo, rubber, and sealed concrete, adjusting pads, speed, and chemicals to manufacturer specs. I schedule strip-and-wax, scrub-and-recoat, and daily maintenance based on traffic and finish condition.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Specific machines, pads, chemicals, and floor types
- Maintenance cycles and inspection routines
- Safety steps like signage, cord management, and PPE


### What cleaning products and tools are you most familiar with?


**Sample Answer:** I’m comfortable with microfiber systems, HEPA vacuums, carpet extractors, and EPA-registered disinfectants, following label directions and dwell times. I organize carts by task zone and color-code cloths and mop heads to prevent cross-use.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Tool names with appropriate uses and limitations
- Correct chemical use, dilution, and dwell-time awareness
- Organization methods that support efficiency and hygiene


## Safety, Compliance, and Hazard Response Questions


### How do you handle and store cleaning chemicals safely?


**Sample Answer:** I review Safety Data Sheets, wear required PPE, and use proper dilution systems while never mixing incompatible chemicals. All containers are labeled, stored ventilated, and secured, with SDS access available to the team at all times.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Reference to SDS, PPE, labeling, and secure storage
- Explicit mention of avoiding dangerous mixes (e.g., bleach with ammonia)
- Procedures for spills, exposure, and emergency reporting


### What steps do you take to prevent cross-contamination between areas?


**Sample Answer:** I use color-coded cloths and mop heads, separate restrooms from food or office areas, and follow correct dwell times for disinfectants. Tools are cleaned and stored between zones, and I work from clean to dirty areas to avoid recontaminating surfaces.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Color-coding and zone separation
- Clean-to-dirty workflow and correct chemical dwell times
- Tool decontamination and storage practices


### Tell me about a time you handled a challenging spill or hazard. What did you do?


**Sample Answer:** A large spill near an outlet required blocking off the area, cutting power, and using absorbent materials designed for that liquid. After safe cleanup and disposal, I documented the incident and followed up to prevent recurrence.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Immediate safety actions: signage, isolation, PPE, power considerations
- Use of appropriate materials and correct disposal
- Documentation and preventive follow-up


## Prioritization and Time Management Questions


### How do you prioritize tasks when multiple requests come in at once?


**Sample Answer:** I triage by safety and health impact first—spills and restrooms, then high-traffic touchpoints, followed by routine tasks. I communicate expected timelines and adjust the schedule, documenting changes so nothing is missed.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Clear triage logic: safety/health, impact, then routine
- Communication with stakeholders about timing
- Documentation or checklists to track deferrals


### How do you balance speed and quality when schedules are tight?


**Sample Answer:** I focus on critical areas first and use efficient tools like microfiber and autoscrubbers, while maintaining standards like dwell times. If timing conflicts with quality, I escalate early to reset expectations rather than cut corners.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Prioritization of critical tasks and areas
- Efficiency methods that don’t compromise safety
- Proactive communication when scope or time must change


## Working Around People and Customer Service Questions


### How do you minimize disruption when cleaning around staff, students, or the public?


**Sample Answer:** I schedule noisy work for off-peak times, use signage, and give courteous heads-up before closing restrooms or entries. I keep pathways open and adjust my route to avoid interrupting meetings, classes, or customer lines.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Planning around peak times and events
- Clear signage and polite communication
- Flexible routing that maintains accessibility


### How would you handle a complaint about cleanliness or an odor?


**Sample Answer:** I listen without interrupting, thank them, and resolve the issue promptly—then investigate root cause, like ventilation or product choice. I follow up to confirm the issue is resolved and note any preventive changes needed.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Professional, empathetic communication
- Swift resolution plus root-cause analysis
- Follow-up and process improvements


## Security, Access, and Integrity Questions


### How do you manage keys and access to restricted areas?


**Sample Answer:** I follow key control policies, sign keys in/out, and never share them. Doors are re-secured after tasks, and I report lost keys or access issues immediately per protocol.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Knowledge of key logs, badge policies, and chain of custody
- Re-securing spaces and confidentiality awareness
- Immediate reporting of exceptions or losses


### What would you do if asked to do something outside your job scope?


**Sample Answer:** I help when it’s safe and appropriate, and if specialized skills are needed, I escalate to the right team. I document the request and communicate timelines or limitations to keep expectations clear.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Safety-first decision-making
- Willingness to assist while respecting policy and skill limits
- Clear escalation and documentation


## School Custodian Questions


### What makes you a strong school custodian?


**Sample Answer:** I’m attentive to student safety, quick to address spills or hazards, and keep restrooms and classrooms consistently sanitary. I communicate with teachers and follow school protocols, including radio use and emergency procedures.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Student safety and hazard vigilance
- Consistent sanitation in high-use spaces
- Team communication through school systems and protocols


### How do you interact with students and staff while staying on task?


**Sample Answer:** I’m friendly and respectful but keep a professional pace, offering quick help when needed without falling behind. If a request affects priorities, I coordinate with the office to align on timing.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Professional, approachable communication
- Boundary setting to maintain schedule
- Coordination with administrators on priorities


## Startup/Office Scenario Questions


### If you joined us, what would your first 30 days look like to make an immediate impact?


**Sample Answer:** I’d audit routes, supply par levels, and traffic patterns; fix quick wins like signage and restocking gaps; and set up a simple task-tracking system. By week four, I’d present a plan for route optimization, preventative floor care, and supply budgeting.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Discovery: audits, data collection, and stakeholder input
- Quick wins with measurable outcomes
- A short plan for ongoing improvements and documentation


### What system do you prefer for documenting tasks, inspections, and reporting issues?


**Sample Answer:** I’m comfortable with CMMS or shared task apps for checklists, photos, and timestamps. Standardizing notes and categories speeds handoffs and helps spot trends for preventive work.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Familiarity with digital or paper systems and consistent use
- Evidence value: visibility, accountability, and trend analysis
- Photo/document attachments for clarity


## Inventory, Waste, and Events Questions


### How do you track supplies and avoid running out of essentials?


**Sample Answer:** I maintain par levels by area, review weekly usage, and reorder with lead times in mind. I keep substitutes on hand and communicate early if deliveries slip to adjust routes or product choices.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Par levels, reorder points, and lead-time awareness
- Substitution plans and product standardization
- Communication when shortages may affect service


### What’s your experience with recycling, composting, and waste programs?


**Sample Answer:** I set up labeled stations, train staff on what belongs where, and schedule pickups to prevent overflow. I track contamination issues and adjust signage or bin placement to improve compliance.


**What a Strong Answer Includes**


- Knowledge of local guidelines and stream separation
- Signage, placement, and education tactics
- Monitoring and continuous improvement


## What Great Answers Sound Like


**For both candidates and employers:** strong responses are specific, safe, and results-focused.


- Specifics: names of equipment and chemicals, correct methods (dilution, dwell time), and examples with measurable outcomes
- Safety-first: PPE, SDS access, signage, cord control, biohazard protocols, and never mixing incompatible chemicals
- Professionalism: clear communication, documentation habits, integrity with keys/access, and respectful conduct around people


## Practical Assessment Ideas for Employers


- Floor care demo: identify floor type, choose pads/chemicals, set signage, and explain steps before a short machine pass
- Chemical safety quick check: interpret two SDS excerpts and select proper PPE and storage
- Route and triage exercise: prioritize a list with a restroom issue, spill, and routine tasks; explain timing and communication

## FAQs


### What qualities matter most in custodians?


Reliability, safety awareness, attention to detail, communication, and respect for confidentiality. Technical familiarity with your facility’s equipment and surfaces is essential.


### How can we assess real skill quickly?


Use a brief hands-on demo (floor care or spill response) plus a chemical safety check. Review a sample checklist the candidate uses to manage routes and quality.


### How should we tailor interviews to our facility?


Match questions to floor types, traffic patterns, and shift needs (nights/weekends). Add scenarios for biohazards in schools or confidentiality in offices/healthcare.
