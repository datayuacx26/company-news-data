# A10 Thunder 3360S ADC: Performance Built for Enterprise and AI

Enterprise application traffic has changed. Nearly all of it is encrypted. TLS 1.3 adoption is now the norm, and AI-powered services are pushing real-time, latency-sensitive workloads onto infrastructure that was never designed for them. Mid-size enterprises are caught in the middle: They need to manage dynamic traffic patterns, scale without ripping and replacing hardware, and be ready for what’s coming next, including the shift to post-quantum cryptography.


## A Modern Mid-range ADC, Without the Compromises


The Thunder 3360S ADC is a 6th-generation mid-range application delivery controller that pairs high throughput with the security and efficiency of modern apps’ demand. It delivers:


- **High performance** across L4 and L7, with headroom for growth
- **TLS 1.3** and hardware-accelerated SSL for encrypted traffic at scale
- **Modular, scale-as-you-grow licensing** to buy the capacity you need today and unlock more without a hardware refresh
- An **AI-ready operating system** and advanced ADC capabilities engineered for lower latency


It’s a single platform that spans Thunder ADC and CGN, in addition to[SSL Insight](https://www.a10networks.com/resources/thunder-ssli-resources/) and secure web gateway use cases, consolidating functions that once required separate boxes.


## Built as a Foundation for the Quantum Computing Future


Beyond the immediate performance refresh, the Thunder ADC 3360S is designed to provide a strong foundation for enterprises preparing for the quantum computing future. Thunder ADC 3360S gives customers a practical path to begin adopting[next-generation cryptographic protection](https://www.a10networks.com/blog/post-quantum-cryptography-comes-to-a10-ssl-tls-data-plane/) as standards mature. This positions the solution as a scalable hardware platform for future ADC, SSL/TLS/PQC, AI-driven traffic, and security use cases.


## Performance that Scales with You


The Thunder 3360S ADC is offered in three modular license tiers — 50, 100, and 150 Gbps — on a compact 1U appliance powered by an Intel Xeon 24-core processor, 64 GB of ECC memory, and dedicated hardware/security acceleration.


Metric Thunder 3360S (up to)


Application throughput (L4 / L7) 150 Gbps / 130 Gbps


Layer 4 concurrent sessions 256 million


Layer 4 connections per second 3 million


SSL bulk throughput 50 Gbps


SSL CPS RSA: 120K, ECDSA: 60K


That’s enterprise-grade capacity in a power-efficient footprint, a typical draw of just 343W, with 80 PLUS platinum dual redundant power supplies.


## Clear Leadership over the Competition


We benchmarked the Thunder 3360S against comparable mid-range platforms from the competition. The gap is generational. The Thunder 3360S handles far more sessions, more connections per second, and more encrypted traffic per box than competing mid-range appliances, which means fewer appliances, less rack space, less power consumption, and lower operational cost to hit the same targets.


## Why it Matters for Your Enterprise Business


- **Cost efficiency** : Higher performance density shrinks your hardware footprint and operating costs
- **Scalability** : 256M concurrent sessions absorb enterprise growth without an infrastructure overhaul
- **Security ready** : Superior[SSL/TLS](https://www.a10networks.com/solutions/security/ssl-tls-inspection/) throughput keeps encrypted traffic fast at scale, with PQC readiness for the road ahead
- **Future-proof** : Real headroom for emerging AI workloads and rising traffic demands


## The Right Time to Modernize Your Enterprise Infrastructure


For teams running earlier-generation mid-range appliances, the Thunder 3360S is a natural refresh path: roughly 3x the performance of the platforms it replaces, with modular licensing that lets capacity follow demand instead of forcing a forklift upgrade. As older ADC generations reach end of sale and end of support, moving now means long-term software support, a modern OS, and a platform that’s ready for post-quantum security.
