## One Capacity Pool.
Greater Flexibility.


Unlike traditional licensing models tied to fixed deployments, FlexPool consumption-based licensing enables organizations to dynamically allocate and redistribute software capacity across virtual, bare metal, private cloud, and public cloud environments.


## Key Challenges Customers are Facing


#### Rigid Licensing Models


Traditional licensing models are tied to fixed deployments, making it difficult to scale resources efficiently across changing application and infrastructure demands.


#### Capacity Management Complexity


Managing software capacity across physical appliances, virtual environments, bare metal, and cloud deployments can increase operational complexity and reduce infrastructure flexibility.


#### Infrastructure Overprovisioning


Organizations often provision infrastructure for peak demand, leading to underutilized resources, higher operational costs, and inefficient capacity planning.


## Simplify How You Scale Capacity


### Flexible Capacity Allocation


Dynamically allocate and redistribute capacity. Support flexible instance sizing across environments. Scale services based on changing demands. Improve utilization across shared capacity pools.


### Operational Efficiency


Support capacity portability across hybrid and[multi-cloud environments](https://www.a10networks.com/resources/videos/video-what-is-a-multi-cloud-environment/) . Reduce unnecessary overprovisioning. Scale pool capacity as infrastructure grows. Maximize infrastructure and licensing investments.


### Cost Control


Align capacity consumption with business needs. Reduce unused or stranded licensing capacity. Simplify budgeting across distributed environments. Support more predictable infrastructure spending.
