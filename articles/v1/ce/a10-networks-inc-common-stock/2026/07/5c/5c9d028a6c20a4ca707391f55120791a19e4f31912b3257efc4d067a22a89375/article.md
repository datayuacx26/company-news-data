# Why Choose A10 Networks

**A10 protects infrastructure investments.**


Enterprise and service providers need solutions that extend the value of what they’ve already built while supporting what comes next.


**A10 supports seamless migration.**


Most customers live in hybrid environments, and they need consistency as they modernize. A10 helps customers transition to the cloud and cloud-native architectures.


**A10 helps customers secure and deploy new AI applications.**


As AI becomes embedded into production workflows, performance and security must operate together, in-line, at scale.


**A10 defends networks and applications from cyberattacks.**


Availability is the first promise customers make to their users, and security is about keeping systems online.


**A10 helps simplify operations.**


With intelligence and automation, customers can operate more efficiently as environments grow more complex. Customers trust A10 when failure isn’t an option. That trust allows us to operate alongside some of the biggest brands in the world.
