# Krea partners with Cinder to safely launch the world's most aesthetic open-source image model

Krea has been shipping AI image products since 2022, and increasingly to enterprise customers. In May of 2026, architecture firm Henning Larsen announced a Krea partnership to embed AI image generation into its design workflow, negotiating data security, copyright, and EU AI compliance terms directly into the deal.


The stakes of identifying safety vulnerabilities are high. When guardrails are missing, the first users find the gaps, and the product gets defined by its worst use cases instead of its best ones. For a model heading into enterprise procurement, that risk is the difference between winning or losing a deal.


With Cinder Red Teaming, the model was ready for enterprise customers who have a very high standard. They want and need evidence that a model is safe. The kind of third-party verification they recognize: something they can point to in a procurement conversation and say, definitively, this model has been evaluated by experts.


Krea had run its own internal benchmarks, but those results had not been validated. Enterprise customers wanted external validation.
