# Viant Integrates with Publica to Expand Premium Connected TV Access

# Viant Integrates with Publica to Expand Premium Connected TV Access


July 8, 2026 in[Blog](https://www.viantinc.com/insights/blog/)


*Advertisers gain a more direct path via Viant’s Direct Access to high-quality streaming inventory from Publica publishers*


**NEW YORK, July 8, 2026** — Publica by IAS, a leading ad serving and monetization platform for Connected TV (CTV), today announced a new integration with Viant Technology Inc.’s[Direct Access](https://www.viantinc.com/solutions/publisher/) supply path product that enables Viant advertisers to buy premium CTV inventory from Publica publishers. The integration creates a more seamless path for advertisers to activate campaigns across leading streaming environments with greater efficiency, transparency, and signal quality.


“This integration with Viant’s Direct Access is another step forward in making premium CTV easier to access and activate at scale,” said Cameron Miille, CRO of Publica by IAS. “Publica’s goal is to help publishers unlock more demand while making it easier for buyers to connect with high-quality streaming inventory. Viant is an important partner in that effort.”


The partnership is designed to simplify CTV buying by bringing advertiser demand closer to premium streaming supply. For publishers, the integration helps maximize demand from performance-minded advertisers seeking quality inventory and stronger outcomes across CTV.


“We are excited to expand Direct Access to Publica’s CTV publisher footprint,” said Richie Hyden, SVP of Publisher Solutions at Viant. “Through this new integration, our advertisers gain a direct path to premium CTV inventory that improves signal quality and working media to deliver stronger campaign performance. Publishers also gain increased monetization and yield, making this a win-win for both sides.”


As CTV continues to mature, reducing complexity across the supply chain is becoming increasingly important. Publica’s strong publisher footprint and Viant’s extensive advertiser relationships together create a direct line between premium supply and performance-driven demand.


“Publica’s integration with Viant creates an efficient path for advertisers to access our premium CTV inventory while helping us connect with demand that values high-quality streaming environments,” said Aulden Kaye Yi, Head of Advertising Partnerships at Philo. “We’re excited to partner with both Publica and Viant via this new integration that supports strong monetization for publishers and drives outcomes for brands.”


This integration reflects Publica’s continued focus on building the infrastructure that helps premium publishers grow monetization while giving advertisers better access to the inventory that matters most. Advertisers activating CTV campaigns across Publica publisher inventory can also leverage[IAS Total TV](https://integralads.com/news/ias-launches-ias-total-tv-solutions-giving-marketers-linear-like-transparency-for-connected-tv/) , IAS’s suite of CTV solutions that delivers show, genre, and rating-level transparency, bringing linear-like visibility and brand suitability controls to CTV campaigns.

## Frequently Asked Questions


What is a cookieless DSP?Expand


A cookieless DSP is a programmatic advertising platform that identifies and reaches audiences using durable, privacy-first identifiers rather than third-party cookies.


Instead of relying on fragile browser tracking, these platforms leverage advanced solutions like Household ID™ to maintain scale and precision. This approach ensures that digital campaigns remain effective and measurable regardless of evolving browser restrictions or privacy regulations.


How does programmatic advertising work without cookies?Expand


Programmatic advertising functions without cookies by activating first-party data through identity resolution frameworks like Unified ID 2.0 and the Household ID. Marketers also utilize contextual targeting to align ads with real-time content and prioritize natively cookieless channels such as CTV and DOOH. These methods allow for high-impact reach and frequency management without the need for invasive user-level tracking.


What is the difference between a DSP and a cookieless DSP?Expand


Traditional DSPs rely on syncing third-party cookies across exchanges to build audience profiles, a process that is increasingly fragmented and unreliable. A cookieless DSP uses people-based identity, first-party data, and privacy-safe signals to reach consumers across all devices.


Viant DSP was purpose-built to be cookieless from the ground up, ensuring it never relied on the legacy infrastructure of third-party cookies.


What targeting options are available in a cookieless environment?Expand


Marketers in a cookieless environment can utilize a robust suite of options including contextual targeting, first-party data onboarding, and household-level identity resolution.


Furthermore, interoperability with alternative ID frameworks like Unified ID 2.0 allows brands to maintain significant scale across the open web. These diverse tactics provide a durable, privacy-forward alternative to outdated cookie-based audience segments.


How is campaign performance measured in a cookieless world?Expand


In a cookieless world, campaign measurement is achieved through modern attribution models such as incrementality testing, Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM), and secure data clean rooms.


The Household ID serves as a critical cross-device attribution layer, linking high-impact exposures on CTV to conversions on other digital devices within the home. This transition allows advertisers to prove ROI and optimize spend without relying on fragmented, cookie-based signals.
