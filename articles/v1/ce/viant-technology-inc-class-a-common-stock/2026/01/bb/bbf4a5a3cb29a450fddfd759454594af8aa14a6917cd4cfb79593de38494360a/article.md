# Building What Comes Next in Advertising: Recognized by the BIG Innovation Awards

# Building What Comes Next in Advertising: Recognized by the BIG Innovation Awards


January 16, 2026 in[Blog](https://www.viantinc.com/insights/blog/)


Innovation in advertising is rarely a single moment. More often, it’s the result of years spent building infrastructure, testing assumptions, and making deliberate bets on where the industry is headed—often well before the market is ready.


It requires steady execution, technical conviction, and the willingness to build new systems when the industry’s ambitions move faster than its tools.


This year, that mindset earned industry recognition for Viant—[the Business Intelligence Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/business-intelligence-group-big-awards-/) named CEO and Co-Founder Tim Vanderhook an Innovative Individual in[the 2026 BIG Innovation Awards](https://www.bintelligence.com/posts/2026-big-innovation-awards-159-trailblazers-prove-where-innovation-is-really-happening) , AND recognized[ViantAI](https://www.viantinc.com/ai/) as a winning product in the Internet and Technology category.


These two awards tell a single story: leadership that builds, and a product that proves it out in the market.


## Leadership Grounded in Building


[Tim Vanderhook](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tim-vanderhook-01b362/) has spent more than 25 years building businesses at the intersection of media and technology, with a career defined by identifying structural shifts in how advertising operates and building platforms to support them **.** He co-founded Viant alongside his brother,[Chris Vanderhook](https://www.linkedin.com/in/chris-vanderhook-b1033/) , with a focus on building advertising infrastructure that could adapt as the industry changed. Today, Viant operates as a leading[independent](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jon-schulz-4780ba4_adtech-openinternet-dspneutrality-activity-7397417853823512576-j30p?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAACC4j1AB7GN0XTTY97p_lDdwJRZ6_6TTxUo)[buy-side platform](https://www.viantinc.com/solutions/dsp/) , helping advertisers reach audiences across channels with a clear focus on performance, accountability, and transparency across the open internet.


Over the course of his career, Tim has recognized moments of industry inflection early, and built the platforms that carried the market forward. He helped lay early programmatic foundations and later co-founded XUMO, alongside Chris before[streaming and CTV](https://www.viantinc.com/solutions/omnichannel/connected-tv/) began to reshape television, contributing to one of the earliest large-scale free, ad-supported platforms before FAST became the familiar acronym it is today. A product-driven CEO, he has also remained close to the technical side of the business; Tim holds multiple patents tied to[household-based identification](https://www.viantinc.com/solutions/planning-and-buying/household-id/) and programmatic marketplace design that continue to influence how addressability and measurement function today.


That mix of long-range thinking and hands-on execution shows up clearly in the roadmap of ViantAI. Rather than stepping back as the product took shape, Tim personally designed and coded the initial alpha, recognizing AI’s potential to reduce friction and drive more efficient campaign execution at scale.


This award from[the Business Intelligence Group](https://www.bintelligence.com/) underscores what sets Tim apart as a leader and visionary in AdTech: the instinct to see what’s coming, the technical fluency to build for it, and the discipline to turn ideas into systems marketers can rely on.


## ViantAI in Market


Alongside Tim’s recognition,[ViantAI](https://www.viantinc.com/ai/) was also named an Innovative Product in the Internet and Technology category by[the Business Intelligence Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/business-intelligence-group-big-awards-/) . Viant launched this AI suite to address a familiar challenge in digital advertising, where teams spend hours on manual work across planning, execution, and reporting while those functions live in separate, fragmented systems. The result is disconnected tools, constant data reconciliation, and insights that often arrive too late to meaningfully influence performance.


ViantAI transforms this process by bridging those workflows in a single, intuitive platform. With just four inputs, marketers can generate omnichannel media plans, activate them directly within the Demand-Side Platform and monitor performance through conversational measurement prompts. Optimization continues throughout the campaign, using Viant’s proprietary data and bid-stream intelligence to maximize efficiency.


Since launch, adoption has grown steadily, with ViantAI Bidding automating approximately 85% of all ad spend on the Viant platform. Customers report up to 50% lower media costs, 42% lower CPMs, transforming campaign planning, setup, trafficking, and optimization from days to hours.


ViantAI’s recognition by the Business Intelligence Group, alongside recent honors from[Adweek](https://www.viantinc.com/company/news/press-releases/viantai-named-best-strategic-ai-platform-in-adweeks-tech-stack-awards/) and[MarTech Breakthrough](https://www.viantinc.com/company/news/press-releases/viantai-named-best-ai-powered-advertising-solution/) , reflects a platform that has already crossed an important threshold—AI is no longer an add-on, but embedded infrastructure shaping how campaigns are planned and optimized.


## Introducing Outcomes


At[CES 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7417328184192294912) , Viant introduced the next phase of ViantAI with the launch of[Outcomes](https://www.viantinc.com/company/news/press-releases/viant-launches-outcomes-the-first-fully-autonomous-open-internet-ad-product/) , the industry’s first fully autonomous advertising product built for the open internet. Where ViantAI’s planning, optimization, and measurement tools act as a media co-pilot, Outcomes shifts these capabilities into autopilot, while the platform drives relentlessly toward a defined performance goal for advertisers.


Outcomes is powered by Viant’s newly developed[AI Lattice Brain](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5wGvLm5T3fo) , a decisioning architecture designed to run campaigns autonomously while remaining transparent. The system evaluates multiple proprietary signals in parallel, including Viant Household ID, contextual intelligence through IRIS_ID, supply quality, historical performance, and campaign delivery data to guide execution in real time.


With Outcomes, advertisers simply define the business result they want to achieve, such as customer acquisition or return on ad spend. ViantAI then manages campaign decisioning and optimization toward that outcome, while still giving teams visibility into where ads run and how performance is delivered—a level of transparency that walled simply cannot offer. That combination of autonomy and transparency is foundational to how Viant approaches AI-driven advertising on the open internet.


Outcomes is the natural next chapter for ViantAI. As the platform takes on more decisioning and optimization, its launch marks a clear shift in how advertising is managed, with more responsibility moving into the system and less friction left for marketers to navigate.

## Frequently Asked Questions


What is a cookieless DSP?Expand


A cookieless DSP is a programmatic advertising platform that identifies and reaches audiences using durable, privacy-first identifiers rather than third-party cookies.


Instead of relying on fragile browser tracking, these platforms leverage advanced solutions like Household ID™ to maintain scale and precision. This approach ensures that digital campaigns remain effective and measurable regardless of evolving browser restrictions or privacy regulations.


How does programmatic advertising work without cookies?Expand


Programmatic advertising functions without cookies by activating first-party data through identity resolution frameworks like Unified ID 2.0 and the Household ID. Marketers also utilize contextual targeting to align ads with real-time content and prioritize natively cookieless channels such as CTV and DOOH. These methods allow for high-impact reach and frequency management without the need for invasive user-level tracking.


What is the difference between a DSP and a cookieless DSP?Expand


Traditional DSPs rely on syncing third-party cookies across exchanges to build audience profiles, a process that is increasingly fragmented and unreliable. A cookieless DSP uses people-based identity, first-party data, and privacy-safe signals to reach consumers across all devices.


Viant DSP was purpose-built to be cookieless from the ground up, ensuring it never relied on the legacy infrastructure of third-party cookies.


What targeting options are available in a cookieless environment?Expand


Marketers in a cookieless environment can utilize a robust suite of options including contextual targeting, first-party data onboarding, and household-level identity resolution.


Furthermore, interoperability with alternative ID frameworks like Unified ID 2.0 allows brands to maintain significant scale across the open web. These diverse tactics provide a durable, privacy-forward alternative to outdated cookie-based audience segments.


How is campaign performance measured in a cookieless world?Expand


In a cookieless world, campaign measurement is achieved through modern attribution models such as incrementality testing, Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM), and secure data clean rooms.


The Household ID serves as a critical cross-device attribution layer, linking high-impact exposures on CTV to conversions on other digital devices within the home. This transition allows advertisers to prove ROI and optimize spend without relying on fragmented, cookie-based signals.
