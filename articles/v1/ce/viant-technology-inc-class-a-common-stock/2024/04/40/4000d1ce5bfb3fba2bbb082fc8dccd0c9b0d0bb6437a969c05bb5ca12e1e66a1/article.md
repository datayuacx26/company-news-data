# Measuring the Impact of Your CTV Campaigns

One thing most if not all marketers can agree on: CTV is fast becoming their favorite advertising channel. And numbers support this enthusiasm, from a growing audience base (thanks to cord-cutting away from linear) to the increased availability of premium inventory.


But despite this popularity, the channel has, until now, presented a major hangup: measurement.


In our latest cheat sheet, **Measuring the Impact of Your CTV Campaigns,** we discuss the historic issues with CTV measurement and how marketers can use the latest ad technology to overcome them.
