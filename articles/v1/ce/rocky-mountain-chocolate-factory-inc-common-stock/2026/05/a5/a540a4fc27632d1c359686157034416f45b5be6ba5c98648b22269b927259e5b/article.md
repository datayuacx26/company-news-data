**About Rocky Mountain Chocolate Factory, Inc.**


[Rocky Mountain Chocolate Factory, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=I16O3IuFYXPzduZEFaIDJq5O-hQpNyiOK8ojSs-3qm7fPeSKBc4XUdJ0mQwl5IyhitIn2fWbwfqe3mm-NPcgSI09nkWRr0Hq77er9muZcjxFXkfTOJv5xqWICZkXum-7) is a leading franchisor of premium chocolate and confectionary retail store concept.
