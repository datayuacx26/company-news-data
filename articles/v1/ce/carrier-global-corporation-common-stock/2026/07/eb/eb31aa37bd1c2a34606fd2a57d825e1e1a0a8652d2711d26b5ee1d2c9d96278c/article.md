# Carrier Named 2025 Organization of the Year | Carrier Abound

**PALM BEACH GARDENS, Fla., May 6, 2025** —[Carrier Global Corporation](https://www.corporate.carrier.com/) (NYSE: CARR), global leader in intelligent climate and energy solutions, has been named an Organization of the Year in the 2025 Excellence in Customer Service Awards by the Business Intelligence Group. The annual awards recognize companies transforming customer service through innovation, responsiveness and tangible impact.


This recognition highlights the impact of Carrier's Carrier Customer Command Centers, which leverage AI-driven insights and advanced connectivity through Carrier’s Abound™ Suite and Building Automation Solutions. By continuously monitoring and managing building operations, the Command Centers help reduce equipment downtime, lower energy and maintenance costs and enhance occupant comfort.


“We are honored to be recognized with this award by the Business Intelligence Group,” said Anuj Bhargava, Vice President of Aftermarket and Services, Carrier. “Our Carrier Customer Command Centers are redefining what world-class service looks like — delivering unmatched operational efficiencies, cost savings and peace of mind for our customers. This recognition is a testament to the power of predictive intelligence, our investment in digital innovation and the expertise of our global service teams. It reflects our unwavering commitment to helping customers unlock the full potential of their buildings — making them smarter, more resilient and more sustainable.”


Carrier Customer Command Centers offer tailored service options — from advisory support to fully managed services — to meet each customer’s goals. Each month, the centers handle more than 10,000 proactive service requests identified through advanced system analytics — often before customers detect an issue. They also support more than 12,000 service technician calls monthly, accelerating repair and maintenance efforts. This proactive approach enables customers to swiftly address challenges and achieve their business objectives.


In 2024 alone, Carrier Customer Command Centers helped customers save an estimated more than $19 million* in operational costs and over 600 million kilowatt-hours of energy.


For more information about Carrier and its award-winning customer service initiatives, visit[www.corporate.carrier.com](https://www.corporate.carrier.com/) .
