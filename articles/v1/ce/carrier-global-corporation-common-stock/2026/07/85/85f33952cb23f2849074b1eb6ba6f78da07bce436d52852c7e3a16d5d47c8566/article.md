# Carrier Unveils AI-Powered Abound Platform Upgrade for Smarter Building Management

**KENNESAW, Ga., Sept 25, 2025** — Carrier today introduced a major upgrade to its award-winning Abound Insights platform, delivering advanced AI-powered capabilities that empower building operators to efficiently manage operations, optimize resources, and simplify maintenance across their portfolios. Carrier Abound is part of[Carrier Global Corporation](https://www.corporate.carrier.com/) (NYSE: CARR), global leader in intelligent climate and energy solutions.


The enhanced Abound platform drives smarter decisions and proactive remote support, helping customers improve energy efficiency, occupant comfort, and reduce operational costs.


The Abound Insights Assistant app now provides:


- **Interactive maps with AI-enhanced markers,** displaying connected sites with weather and air quality overlays, and offering actionable operations and maintenance recommendations.
- **Updated site list view,** which now offers advanced filters and export capabilities, allowing users to download site lists, equipment details and work order reports for streamlined planning and tracking.


“With these enhancements, we’re giving building operators the power to see and manage their entire portfolio like never before,” said Yasir Qureshi, General Manager, Carrier Abound. “By delivering a truly enterprise-wide view, we’re helping our customers unlock smarter decisions, drive measurable energy savings, elevate occupant comfort, and streamline operations across their portfolio—all through the intelligence of the Abound platform.”


Over the past 12 months, the Abound platform has enabled us to save customers more than 650 million kWh of energy and avoid over 40,000 technician dispatches, preventing an estimated 437,900 metric tonnes of CO₂ equivalent emissions1.


Carrier continues to deliver intelligent, sustainable building solutions using the power of AI. Learn more about how Carrier Abound optimizes building operations by visiting[www.abound.carrier.com](https://www.abound.carrier.com/) .


*1 These results are based on outcomes Carrier has delivered in various engagements with its customers.
