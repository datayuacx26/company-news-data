# Carrier Wins the 2024 IoT Breakthrough of the Year Award for its Abound Edge System

**PALM BEACH GARDENS, Fla., July 2, 2025** —[Carrier Global Corporation](https://www.corporate.carrier.com/) (NYSE: CARR), is proud to announce that[Carrier Abound](https://abound.carrier.com/) has won IoThinkTank’s 2024[IoT Breakthrough of the Year Award](https://abound.carrier.com/en/worldwide/media-resources/archive/carriers-ecoenergy-insights-earns-gold-for-iot-enabled-services-at-2022-iot-innovator-awards) for its Abound Edge system. This recognition highlights Carrier’s commitment to transforming the Internet of Things (IoT) landscape, particularly in optimizing building operations through connectivity, centralized management and artificial intelligence (AI)


IoThinkTank, a leading platform dedicated to celebrating excellence in the IoT industry, recognizes companies, products, and individuals making significant strides in innovation and technological advancement. Their Best of 2024 Awards recognize groundbreaking achievements, visionary leadership and transformative solutions shaping the future of the Internet of Things. The Abound Edge System stood out for its ability to address a common challenge faced by business owners: the lack of visibility into how their buildings are performing due to insufficient automation and centralized intelligence.


Carrier Abound tackles this challenge with a suite of digital solutions and services specifically designed for customers from a variety of industries. Using a combination of edge hardware, AI-powered software, and expert consulting services, the Abound Edge System transforms buildings into a fully connected ecosystem where AI can be applied and autonomous actions can be initiated, delivering enhanced occupant comfort, improved energy efficiency, and reduced operational costs across a building portfolio.


The system aggregates, secures, transforms and optimizes data from connected edge devices, allowing for seamless communication using IoT technologies. It also supports automatic updates for the system itself and to connected devices. Features such as cloud-deployable program profile templates, centralized schedule and set-point management, and control logic configuration enable customers to effectively manage their buildings.


Customers from diverse industries are already realizing significant savings with Carrier Abound. With management extending to over 33,000 sites, Carrier Abound has enabled multi-site clients to save over 6.5 billion kWh in energy use to date. In one instance, Carrier Abound helped a multi-site customer save over 10% in energy costs over a 3-year period.1


“We are thrilled to receive the IoThinkTank’s 2024 IoT Breakthrough of the Year Award”, said Yasir Qureshi, General Manager, Carrier Abound. “This achievement underscores our commitment to developing innovative solutions that deliver significant financial benefits for customers. As a leader in AI technology for buildings, we will continue our innovation journey and look forward to achieving even more impactful customer outcomes."


For more information about Carrier Abound and how it can transform your building operations, please visit[www.abound.carrier.com](https://abound.carrier.com/) .
