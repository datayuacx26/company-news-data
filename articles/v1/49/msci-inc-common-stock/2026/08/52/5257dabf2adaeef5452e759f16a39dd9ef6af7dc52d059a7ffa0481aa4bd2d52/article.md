# MSCI Equity Indexes August 2026 Index Review

LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 12, 2026-- MSCI Inc. (NYSE: MSCI), a leading provider of critical decision support tools and services for the global investment community, announced the results of the August 2026 Index Review for the MSCI Equity Indexes. All changes will be implemented as of the close of August 31, 2026.


Highlights include:


**MSCI Global Standard Indexes:** Fifty-five securities will be added to and 92 securities will be deleted from the MSCI ACWI® Index. The three largest additions to the MSCI World Index measured by full company market capitalization will be SanDisk (USA), Carpenter Technology (USA) and ATI (USA). The three largest additions to the MSCI Emerging Markets Index measured by full company market capitalization will be Z.AI Co H (China), Nanya Technology (Taiwan) and Guangdong Dtech Technology A (HK-C) (China).


**MSCI Global Small Cap Indexes:** There will be 203 additions to and 261 deletions from the MSCI ACWI® Small Cap Index.


**MSCI GIMI™:** There will be 184 additions to and 279 deletions from the MSCI ACWI IMI™.


**MSCI Global All Cap Indexes:** There will be 137 additions to and 73 deletions from the MSCI World All Cap Index.


**MSCI Frontier Markets Indexes:** There will be six additions to and five deletions from the MSCI Frontier Markets Index. The three largest additions to the MSCI Frontier Markets Index measured by full company market capitalization will be Asia Commercial Joint Stock Bank (Vietnam), Oman India Fertiliser Company (Oman) and Southeast Asia Commercial Joint Stock Bank (Vietnam). There will be 26 additions to and 17 deletions from the MSCI Frontier Markets Small Cap Index.


MSCI will continue to not implement changes as part of this Index Review for any securities classified in Bangladesh for the MSCI Bangladesh Indexes or impacted composite indexes. MSCI will resume the implementation of index review changes as well as corporate events for the MSCI Bangladesh Indexes starting from the November 2026 Index Review.


These changes, along with other changes across MSCI Equity Indexes including the MSCI US Equity Indexes, MSCI US REIT Index, MSCI China A Onshore Indexes and China All Shares Indexes are available on MSCI's "Index Review" web page:[www.msci.com/index-review](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.msci.com%2Findex-review&esheet=54588659&newsitemid=20260812321802&lan=en-US&anchor=www.msci.com%2Findex-review&index=1&md5=e1088f76a64dc06c3435dd2e57b6f5ee) .
