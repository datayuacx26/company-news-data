# MSCI Completes Acquisition of First Street

NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 3, 2026-- MSCI Inc. (NYSE: MSCI) has completed its acquisition of First Street, a leading provider of physics-based climate risk data and analytics across more than 2.4 billion structures worldwide.


The acquisition was initially[announced](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.msci.com%2Fdiscover-msci%2Fmedia-room%2Fmsci-acquires-first-street-to-enhance-physical-climate-risk-capabilities-for-financial-decision-making&esheet=54582342&newsitemid=20260803530906&lan=en-US&anchor=announced&index=1&md5=2ff715a40b94297d11cdbdadaff234ee) on June 24, 2026.


**Richard Mattison, Head of Sustainability and Climate at MSCI, said:** “We are pleased to complete the acquisition of First Street. By integrating First Street’s data into our existing climate solutions, we are strengthening our physical climate risk capabilities to enable clients to better understand how their risk exposures are evolving and incorporate that directly into financial decision making.”
