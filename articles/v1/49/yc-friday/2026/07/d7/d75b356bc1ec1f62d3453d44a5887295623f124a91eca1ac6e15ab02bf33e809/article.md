# Friday is now Freestyle

Today, Friday becomes Freestyle.


When we started in 2024, we picked a name that captured a feeling: the freedom of a Friday afternoon, when the week's work is behind you. We wanted email to feel the same way, like something that's already handled.


But as the product grew, the name started to fit less well. Friday was a nod to a day of the week; what we're building is a way of moving through your inbox. Fast, unstructured, effortless. Freestyle.


## What's changing


- **The name.** Friday, fridaymail, Friday AI, they're all now Freestyle.
- **The website.** Our home is[freestyle.ai](https://freestyle.ai/) . Old links redirect here.
- **The look.** New logo, new branding, and our social handles are moving to the Freestyle name.


## What's not changing


Everything that matters:


- **The product.** It's the same Chrome extension for Gmail, with AI drafts in your voice, meeting scheduling, bulk unsubscribe, and natural-language search.
- **Your account.** Your login, rules, automations, and billing carry over. There's nothing you need to do, the extension updates automatically.
- **The team.** Same people, same office in San Francisco, still backed by Y Combinator.
- **Our commitments.** Your emails are never stored and never used to train AI models. Nothing about our[permissions or privacy practices](https://freestyle.ai/privacy) changes with the name.


If you're wondering about the details, we've written up the full story at[freestyle.ai/friday](https://freestyle.ai/friday) .


Here's to email at the speed of thought.
