# Seed

[← Back to blog](https://freestyle.ai/blog)


Email is about to change forever.


For thirty years, we've watched email evolve from a revolutionary communication tool into a daily burden. Our inboxes have become endless streams of consciousness, demanding constant attention and draining our creative energy. We check our emails first thing in the morning, last thing at night, and countless times in between, not because we want to, but because we have to.


But what if we didn't have to?


At Freestyle, we're fundamentally rethinking how humans and machines can work together to communicate. We're building an AI that truly understands your email, one that can distinguish signal from noise, intention from information, and urgency from importance.


We've spent the last few months crafting an experience that feels less like email management and more like having a super smart personal assistant who knows exactly what matters to you, one that drafts responses in your voice, identifies action items before you ask, and ensures you never miss what's important.


Today, we're excited to announce our $2M seed round with participation from Y Combinator, SV Angel, A.Capital, Palm Drive Capital, Pioneer Fund, Metsu Capital, and others.
