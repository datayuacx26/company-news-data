# Manifesto

> "Email was not designed to be used the way we use it now. Email is not a messaging protocol. It's a todo list...and a disastrously bad todo list."
>
>
> —[Paul Graham](https://arc.net/l/quote/fssvqsto)


Email has become a burden, despite being an essential part of our daily lives. It isn't going away anytime soon, but in 100 years, it's hard to imagine we'll still be buried in our inboxes.


How do we make email suck less? Not just less, but 10x less? Solutions that offer a faster or more pleasant email client are missing the point. We need something that *eliminates* the need for going through email altogether.


Freestyle is an AI virtual assistant that does just that. It prioritizes action items, summarizes emails, and handles routine responses faster than any human could. Freestyle delivers the same level of service as a personal assistant, 10x cheaper, for everyone, from students to CEOs, so you never need to check your email again.
