# TWFG Safe 2 Save Feeding the Frontline


**THE WOODLANDS, Texas, April 16, 2020**


TWFG Insurance is once again partnering with Safe 2 Save to help support *Feeding the Frontline* , a grassroots community effort in Montgomery County during the Coronavirus Pandemic.


Safe 2 Save is a mobile app loyalty program that rewards you with points for undistracted driving that you can then redeem with local partners for free and discounted food or services.


Now people using the app can donate their points to *Feeding the Frontline* , a mission initiative of *Reflective Life Ministries* , who are partnering with local restaurants to feed our Healthcare Heroes— those working tirelessly to save lives in our community during these uncertain times. For every 1000 points redeemed, TWFG Insurance will contribute $2 to *Feeding the Frontline* , up to $2,500.


“We wanted to find a way to let people contribute or donate without leaving their homes, to a group of folks who are making a tremendous impact in our community. Carla McDougal, (founder of *Reflective Life Ministries* ) and her team are a true example of giving back” says Gordy Bunch, Founder and CEO of TWFG Insurance.


The Safe 2 Save app can be downloaded from the Apple and Google Play Stores, and users can search for TWFG to contribute to the cause.
