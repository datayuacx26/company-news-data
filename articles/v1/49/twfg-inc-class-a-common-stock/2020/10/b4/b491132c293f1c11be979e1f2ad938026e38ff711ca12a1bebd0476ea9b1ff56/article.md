# TWFG Insurance Continues to Expand with a New Branch Opening in Humble, Texas


*Branch owner Coye Roane to open a new branch of TWFG Insurance.*


**Humble, TX \[October 9th, 2020\]** TWFG Insurance is excited to announce the opening of TWFG Coye Roane Insurance at 907 E Main Street in Humble, Texas. TWFG Coye Roane Insurance will provide Humble area residents with trusted insurance advisors who can shop hundreds of carriers to help their customers find the coverage they need.


Branch owner Coye Roane is proud to have the opportunity to open her own business, even amid an unprecedented pandemic. “Over the last couple of years, I’ve thought it is time to open my own agency, be my own boss. Since the pandemic, I had been working from home and realized that I could do this,” said Coye when talking about why she decided to open a branch amid such uncertainty. “I feel proud to work for a company where I feel valued, motivated, and capable of reaching my full potential. My goal is to be a trusted advisor to my clients and build my business through long-lasting relationships,” continued Roane.


The Woodlands Financial Group, or TWFG, is a nationally recognized insurance company based in The Woodlands, TX. Since its founding in 2001, TWFG has rapidly grown into the number one privately owned insurance agency in Texas and Louisiana, and number eight in the nation, for personal lines, according to Insurance Journal. With nearly 400 TWFG branches and over 3,000 independent agents, TWFG Insurance continues to provide its clients across the country with trusted access to insurance with a high standard of customer care.


To learn more about TWFG Insurance, visit www.twfg.com.
