# TWFG Insurance to Open New Branch in Southwest Houston


*Lincoln Kariuki becomes TWFG Insurance’s newest Houston area branch owner*


**Houston, TX \[October 13th, 2020\]** TWFG Insurance is excited to announce the opening of their newest branch at 6464 Savoy Dr., Suite 730, in Houston, TX. TWFG Lincoln Kariuki Insurance is excited to provide customers in the Houston area with comprehensive insurance coverage at a price they can afford.


Branch owner Lincoln Kariuki joins nearly 400 TWFG Insurance branches nationwide in bringing quality personal and commercial insurance coverage to their clients. As a part of TWFG’s unique business model, Kariuki now has access to hundreds of carriers, allowing him to find the best rates possible for his clients. “I have the tools now to face the competition… Also, I like working with my customers, and having a way to source whatever policy fits their needs,” said Kariuki when explaining what his new branch of TWFG Insurance will mean for his customers in the Houston area.


The Woodlands Financial Group, or TWFG, is a nationally recognized insurance company based in The Woodlands, TX. Since its founding in 2001, TWFG has rapidly grown into the number one privately owned insurance agency in Texas and Louisiana, and number eight in the nation, for personal lines, according to Insurance Journal. With nearly 400 TWFG branches and over 3,000 independent agents, TWFG Insurance continues to provide its clients across the country with trusted access to insurance with a high standard of customer care.


To learn more about TWFG Insurance, visit www.twfg.com.
