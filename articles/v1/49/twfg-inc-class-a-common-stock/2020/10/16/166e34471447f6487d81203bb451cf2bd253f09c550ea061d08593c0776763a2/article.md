# TWFG Insurance Continues to See Growth Amid Unprecedented Pandemic


*New Branch, TWFG Casey Cato Insurance, Opens in Houston, Texas.*


**Houston, TX \[October 6th, 2020\]** TWFG Insurance is announcing its newest branch in Houston, Texas. TWFG Casey Cato Insurance is now open and serving clients in and around the Houston area. Branch owner Casey Cato is looking forward to providing customers with reliable insurance coverage at a price they can afford.


Cato, an experienced insurance agent, brings her talent and years of experience to TWFG, where she was excited to take control of her career and become an independent business owner. Even amid an unprecedented pandemic full of uncertainty, Cato was able to gain the support needed to start a new business through TWFG Insurance’s unique business model. “As a TWFG Branch Owner, I have the opportunity to own my own revenue stream and make all the decisions about my agency while also having the unparalleled support from training, to systems marketing to service after my sales that TWFG Insurance provides,” said Cato.


The Woodlands Financial Group, or TWFG, is a nationally recognized insurance company based in The Woodlands, TX. Since its founding in 2001, TWFG has rapidly grown into the number one privately owned insurance agency in Texas and Louisiana, and number eight in the nation, for personal lines, according to Insurance Journal. With nearly 400 TWFG branches and over 3,000 independent agents, TWFG Insurance continues to provide its clients across the country with trusted access to insurance with a high standard of customer care.


To learn more about TWFG Insurance, visit www.twfg.com.
