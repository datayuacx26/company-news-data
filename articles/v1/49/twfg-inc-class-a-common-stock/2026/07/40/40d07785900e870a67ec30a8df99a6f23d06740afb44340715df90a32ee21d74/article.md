# TWFG, Inc. Announces Earlier Call Time for Upcoming Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results on Thursday, August 6, 2026

The Company will host a conference call to discuss its financial results at a new time of 11:00 a.m. Central Time (12:00 p.m. Eastern Time) on Thursday, August 6, 2026. (Call was previously scheduled and announced for 4:00 p.m. Central / 5:00 p.m. Eastern)
