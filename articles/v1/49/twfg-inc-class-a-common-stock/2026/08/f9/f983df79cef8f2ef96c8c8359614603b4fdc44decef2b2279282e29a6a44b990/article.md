# The Woodlands Financial Group and the United States Coast Guard Ring the Closing Bell to Celebrate Coast Guard Day

**About This Event**


The Woodlands Financial Group (Nasdaq: TWFG), founded by CEO Richard “Gordy” Bunch III, proud U.S. Coast Guard Veteran, visits the Nasdaq MarketSite in Times Square. This Bell Ringing Event celebrates Coast Guard Day and announces the opening of the National Coast Guard Museum in 2027.


In honor of the occasion **, Richard “Gordy” Bunch III, CEO, and Vice Commandant of the United States Coast Guard, Admiral Thomas G. Allen** jointly ring the Closing Bell.
