# How we are building our next 40 satellites

Kicking off our next-generation BlueBird campaign with launches every one to two months on average during 2025 and 2026. Capacity to build 6 BlueBirds per month by the end of 2025, and on track to produce 40 satellites equivalent of microns by early 2026. At 2,400 sq ft, our next-gen BlueBirds will feature the largest commercial communications arrays ever in low Earth orbit—delivering space-based cellular broadband to everyone, everywhere, for good.
