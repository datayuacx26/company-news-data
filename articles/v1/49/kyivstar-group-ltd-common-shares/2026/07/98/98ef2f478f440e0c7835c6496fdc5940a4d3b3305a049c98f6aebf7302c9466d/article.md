# Kyivstar Rings Nasdaq Closing Bell to Celebrate One Year as a Listed Company

## Kyivstar Rings Nasdaq Closing Bell to Celebrate One Year as a Listed Company


27.07.2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.kyivstar.ua/node/7931/pdf)


**KYIV, Ukraine, DUBAI, United Arab Emirates and NEW YORK, July 27, 2026** - Kyivstar Group Ltd. (Nasdaq: KYIV; KYIVW), the parent company of JSC Kyivstar (“Kyivstar”), Ukraine's leading digital operator and part of VEON Group (Nasdaq: VEON), today commemorated the first anniversary of Kyivstar’s historic Nasdaq listing by ringing the Closing Bell at the Nasdaq stock exchange in New York City. Led by Kyivstar President Oleksandr Komarov, the ceremony honored Kyivstar’s notable distinction as the first Ukrainian company to list on the Nasdaq, connecting one of Ukraine's leading businesses directly to American and global capital markets.


“One year ago, Kyivstar became the first Ukrainian company to ring in a new era on the Nasdaq, a milestone that demonstrated the ingenuity, ambition, and global potential of the Ukrainian business community,” **said Oleksandr Komarov, President of Kyivstar Group.** “Today, we return to the Nasdaq having shown that a Ukrainian company can continue to grow, innovate and create value even under the most difficult circumstances. We remain committed to strengthening our digital services, creating long-term value for our shareholders, and contributing to a more connected and prosperous Ukraine.”


The anniversary caps a year of disciplined execution as a public company, during which Kyivstar has continued to deliver long-term value for shareholders while advancing its digital transformation. Over that period, the company has expanded its digital ecosystem across connectivity, digital healthcare, mobility and entertainment: it strengthened its healthcare offering through the acquisition of Tabletki.ua, scaled Uklon's mobility platform, and rolled out new connectivity solutions, including Starlink Direct to Cell capabilities


Nasdaq livestreamed the ceremony, and a recording is available here:[https://www.nasdaq.com/marketsite/bell-ringing-ceremony](https://www.nasdaq.com/marketsite/bell-ringing-ceremony)


**About Kyivstar Group Ltd.**


Kyivstar Group Ltd. (“Kyivstar Group”) is a Nasdaq-listed holding company that operates JSC Kyivstar, Ukraine's leading digital operator and the first Ukrainian company to list on a U.S. stock exchange. Kyivstar Group companies provide a broad range of connectivity and digital services, including mobile and fixed-line voice and data, ride-hailing, e-health, digital TV, and enterprise solutions such as Big Data, cloud, and cybersecurity.


For more information, please visit[https://investors.kyivstar.ua.](https://investors.kyivstar.ua./)


**About JSC Kyivstar**


JSC Kyivstar is Ukraine’s leading digital operator, serving more than 22.0 million mobile customers and over 1.2 million home internet fixed line customers as of March 31, 2026. The company provides services using a wide range of mobile and fixed technologies, including 4G, Big Data, cloud solutions, cybersecurity services, digital TV, and more.


JSC Kyivstar is wholly owned by Kyivstar Group Ltd. (Nasdaq: KYIV; KYIVW), whose shares are traded on the U.S. stock exchange Nasdaq.
