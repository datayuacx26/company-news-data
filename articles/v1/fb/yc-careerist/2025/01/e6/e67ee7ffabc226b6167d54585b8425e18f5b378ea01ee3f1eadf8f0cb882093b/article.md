## What Is a QA Game Tester?


QA game testers are the folks who make sure video games are bug-free before they hit the shelves. Their job is to playtest games under various scenarios, report glitches, and ensure the game runs smoothly for end-users. Essentially, they’re the guardians of gaming quality, making sure players enjoy seamless experiences.


But don’t be fooled—it’s not just about playing all day. QA game testers need a sharp eye for detail, strong communication skills, and the patience to replay the same levels repeatedly to catch even the smallest errors.


## How Much Does a QA Game Tester Earn?


Let’s talk money. The game tester salary varies based on experience, location, and the company you work for. On average:


- Entry-Level QA Game Tester Salary: $30,000 to $50,000 annually
- Experienced QA Game Tester Salary: $60,000 to $80,000 annually
- Specialized QA Game Tester Salary (e.g., VR or mobile games): $80,000+ annually


Freelance testers can earn per project, ranging from $10 to $25 per hour. While it’s not six figures right out of the gate, experienced QA testers can grow their salaries significantly, especially if they specialize in automation or work with major studios.
