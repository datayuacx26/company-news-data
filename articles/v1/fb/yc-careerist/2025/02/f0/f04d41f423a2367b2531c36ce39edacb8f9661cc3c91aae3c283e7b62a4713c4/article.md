# Is Bootcamp Better Than University? The Truth About Breaking Into Tech


So, you want a career in tech but don’t know if you should spend four years in college or take a shortcut with a bootcamp? You’re not alone! The debate over whether a QA bootcamp or a traditional university degree is the better path to success has been heating up for years. And honestly? It all depends on what you’re looking for.


If your goal is to land a well-paying job as fast as possible, then bootcamps—especially those with a bootcamp job guarantee—might just be the smarter choice. But let’s break it all down and compare these two career-launching options.


## The Time Factor: Speed vs. Stability


### University Path (4+ Years)


A Computer Science degree typically takes four years (or more if you go part-time).It provides a broad foundation in tech, but you may not graduate with hands-on experience.You’ll spend time learning advanced math, theory, and electives that might not directly help you land a job.


### Bootcamp Path (3-6 Months)


A QA bootcamp like Careerist takes just a few months to complete.You focus only on the skills you need to get a job, skipping unnecessary courses.Many bootcamps, including Careerist, offer real project experience to build your resume.


Verdict? If you’re eager to start working in tech ASAP, a bootcamp wins on speed.


## The Cost Factor: Affordable vs. Expensive


### University Costs


A four-year degree can cost anywhere from $40,000 to $200,000 (not counting living expenses and lost wages while studying).Student loan debt? Yep, you’ll probably have it.


### Bootcamp Costs


A QA bootcamp usually costs between $5,000 and $15,000.Some offer deferred tuition or job guarantee programs (like Careerist!), so you don’t pay until you land a job.


Verdict? If affordability matters, a bootcamp is the budget-friendly winner.


## Job Prospects: Who Gets Hired Faster?


### University Grads


Employers do value degrees, but graduates often struggle with lack of hands-on experience.Some companies require degrees for senior roles, but entry-level jobs often don’t.


### Bootcamp Grads


Many bootcamps partner with tech companies to connect students with hiring managers.Careerist offers a bootcamp job guarantee, helping grads land positions quickly.Because of their hands-on training, bootcamp grads often have a portfolio to showcase.


Verdict? Bootcamps help you land a job faster, while a degree might give you more flexibility in the long run.
