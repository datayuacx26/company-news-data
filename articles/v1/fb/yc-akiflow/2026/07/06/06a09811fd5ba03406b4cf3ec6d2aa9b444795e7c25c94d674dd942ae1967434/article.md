### When is Akiflow the right choice?


Akiflow is the right choice when people trying to apply GTD across email, Slack, meetings, projects, and calendar commitments need tasks, calendar commitments, and review habits in the same planning surface. It is not the right choice for someone who only wants a basic grocery-list style app or a passive calendar with no task execution layer.


### What should a one week trial include?


A useful GTD setup should process email, meetings, delegated work, and project next actions without creating five separate inboxes. A single polished onboarding session is not enough. ### What setup mistake should readers avoid?


The common mistake is treating GTD as a folder structure instead of a decision loop. Start with fewer imported tasks, clearer names, and a weekly review. A smaller system that exposes ownership and timing is more valuable than a giant imported backlog that looks complete but still does not tell anyone what to do next.


### How do you know the system is working?


The system is working when fewer commitments live in memory, important work has visible time, overdue items are reviewed instead of ignored, and the next action is clear without rebuilding the plan from scratch. It should also make tradeoffs easier to discuss because the calendar shows capacity rather than pretending every task can happen today.
