# Jack Henry Leaders to Present at Upcoming Investor Conferences

MONETT, Mo.


, May 29, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Jack Henry® (Nasdaq: JKHY) announced today that it will be participating in several upcoming conferences.


**President & CEO Greg Adelson** will present at the following conferences:


- The 2026 RBC Capital Markets Financial Technology Conference on June 9. The presentation takes place at 12:15 p.m. ET.
