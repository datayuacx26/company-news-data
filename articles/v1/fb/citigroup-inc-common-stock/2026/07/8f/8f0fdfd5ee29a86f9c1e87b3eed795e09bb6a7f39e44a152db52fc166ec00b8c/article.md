# Frequently Asked Questions


Find answers to common questions about Citi Institute.


## What is Citi Institute?


**


Citi Institute is Citi’s public-facing thought leadership platform. We deliver timely, data-driven insights to help our clients and stakeholders navigate the business and financial landscape. Our work focuses on emerging trends in finance and the global economy—and their impact on society.


## Why was Citi Institute created?


**


To better position Citi as a trusted advisor and strategic partner for our clients. The Citi Institute builds on the firm’s long history of delivering insight-led content for institutional audiences. By connecting insights from across the firm with perspectives from leading external voices, Citi Institute enhances our ability to influence key conversations and deepen client engagement. Institute content does not contain investment advice and is publicly available.


## Who is the target audience?


**


• Institutional investors and private wealth clients
• Corporate and commercial banking clients
• Government officials, policymakers, and regulators
• Journalists and thought leaders in finance and economics


## What type of content does it feature?


**


• **Citi GPS Reports** - Flagship thought leadership, original insights and data
• **Expert Interviews** - Timely Q&As with Citi SMEs on topics salient to clients
• **Citi Institute Podcast** - Deep dives with Citi leaders and outside experts
• **Curated Intelligence** - Select insights from across Citi’s global network

## How can I use Citi Institute with clients?


**


• Share reports, articles or podcast episodes that align with your client’s priorities
• Use the material to initiate strategic conversations on key themes
• Reference Citi Institute in client presentations, memos, or events as a source of thought leadership and the firm’s expertise and authority
• CI is a client-facing unit, our authors are subject matter experts who frequently join client events and 1x1 meetings
