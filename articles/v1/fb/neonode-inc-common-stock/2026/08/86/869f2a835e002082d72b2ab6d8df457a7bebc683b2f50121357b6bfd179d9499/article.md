#### Helping Commercial Vehicles Meet Tomorrow's Safety Requirements


As commercial vehicles continue to adopt higher levels of driver assistance, fleet operators and manufacturers face increasing expectations for driver awareness, occupant safety and regulatory compliance. Neonode enables OEMs to address these challenges using a flexible, camera-based platform that delivers multiple safety applications from a single sensor. Whether supporting Driver Monitoring Systems, monitoring seatbelt usage or enhancing digital mirrors with natural head-tracked views, our software is designed to improve safety while simplifying system integration.
