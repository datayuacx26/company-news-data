# EuroShop 2026, Düsseldorf, Germany


Multisensing


Retail


Self-checkout


Neonode is thrilled to exhibit at EuroShop 2026, the world’s leading retail trade fair, taking place in Düsseldorf February 22–26. Visit us at Booth C20 in Hall 7 to discover our computer vision solution for self-checkout – preventing theft and delivering real-time insights with detailed analytics.


Neonode is proud to be unveiling its advanced[computer vision solution for self-checkout systems](https://neonode.com/solutions/retail) at the triennial EuroShop exhibition in Düsseldorf, Germany. Based on Neonode's proprietary computer vision platform – MultiSensing® – the brand new solution offers a comprehensive feature set that addresses some of the most pressing challenges in the retail sector.


At Neonode's booth, **C20 in Hall 7** , visitors will get to experience a hands-on demonstration of Neonode's latest innovation for the retail sector. Enhancing **self-checkout systems** with computer vision-powered **theft prevention** , **real-time insights** and **analytics** , Neonode's MultiSensing platform delivers on the promise that artificial intelligence will revolutionize the retail sector with stronger margins for retailers and a superior shopping experience for customers.


Shown to the public for the first time, Neonode's EuroShop 2026 demo uses a **single camera** to achieve all functionality – significantly lowering the hardware threshold for introducing next-generation functionality into self-checkout systems.


We look forward to seeing you at EuroShop 2026 at booth C20 in Hall 7!
