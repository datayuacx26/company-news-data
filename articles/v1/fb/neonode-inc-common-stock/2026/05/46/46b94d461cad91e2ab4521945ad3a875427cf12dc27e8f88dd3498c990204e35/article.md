# NRF's Big Show APAC, Singapore


Retail AI Self-checkout Computer Vision


Neonode is excited to exhibit at NRF 2026, Retail’s Big Show APAC, taking place June 2–4 at Marina Bay Sands in Singapore.


Visit us at Booth 1420 to experience Neonode’s[AI-powered computer vision solutions for self-checkout systems](https://neonode.com/solutions/ai-self-checkout) , that help retailers reduce loss, improve operational efficiency and unlock real-time customer insights.


Neonode will showcase the latest advancements in edge-based computer vision for retail environments, powered by the proprietary[MultiSensing®](https://neonode.com/multisensing) software platform, which enables advanced loss prevention, age verification, customer interaction analytics and dynamic retail intelligence .


As retailers across the Asia-Pacific region accelerate investments in AI-driven retail technology, Neonode’s solutions provides a scalable path toward smarter, more secure self-service experiences. With real-time detection and analytics powered by a single camera setup, the platform helps retailers improve margins while delivering a faster and more seamless customer journey.
