# Join Us at InCabin USA 2026


Automotive


Neonode is heading to Detroit for the leading event dedicated to in-cabin technologies, driver monitoring and next-generation automotive user experiences. Join us to experience how intuitive camera-based sensing is enabling safer, smarter and more human-centric assisted driving systems.


## Experience Our Demo Vehicle Live


At InCabin USA, Neonode will be exhibiting our fully equipped demo vehicle, featuring a live showcase of our latest in-cabin sensing and Driver Monitoring technologies.


Visitors will be able to experience our camera-based solutions in action, including:


- Hands-on-wheel detection
- Driver distraction detection
- Occupancy sensing
- Seatbelt detection
- Digital Mirror Augmentation with head tracking


Our demo vehicle demonstrates how a single camera-based software platform, MultiSensing®, can support multiple safety and in-cabin experience applications while reducing system complexity for OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers.


## Speaker Session: Driver Readiness in the Era of Assisted Driving


We are proud to announce that Agnes Jernström, Technical Sales Lead,


will present a session exploring the evolving global landscape for assisted driving and how OEMs can meet emerging Driver Readiness requirements using a fully camera-based solution.


### Session Overview


As L2 and L2+ systems continue to dominate the automotive market, the role of Driver Monitoring Systems (DMS) is becoming increasingly critical. Regulations and safety organizations around the world are raising expectations for how vehicles evaluate driver engagement, visual attention, and readiness to take control when needed.


In this session, Agnes will examine:


- The outlook for assisted driving systems and market adoption
- Global regulations and testing procedures shaping Driver Readiness requirements
- UNECE DCAS regulations and the growing importance of continuous driver engagement monitoring
- The evolving safety expectations from organizations such as NHTSA and IIHS
- How Neonode’s camera-based Driver Readiness software simultaneously evaluates eyes-on-road and hands-on-wheel status using a single sensor solution


Unlike conventional systems that rely on separate steering wheel sensors for hands-on detection, Neonode’s software-based approach combines visual attention and hand position analysis into one integrated camera-based platform, helping OEMs reduce hardware complexity while enhancing safety and user experience.


We look forward to connecting with automotive OEMs, Tier 1 suppliers, and industry innovators shaping the future of mobility.
