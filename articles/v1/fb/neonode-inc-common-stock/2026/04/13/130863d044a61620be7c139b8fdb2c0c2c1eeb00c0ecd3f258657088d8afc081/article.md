# USA Roadshow 2026


##### Neonode is bringing our in-cabin experience to the USA. Meet us in April and May 2026 and get a personalized demonstration of our automotive technology at your address. Book your demo today.


We are excited to announce our upcoming roadshow in the United States, where we will be demonstrating our latest in-cabin and Driver Monitoring solutions. If you’re an OEM or industry stakeholder looking to integrate advanced in-cabin experiences into your next-generation vehicles, we would love to meet. Book a private demonstration where we will come to you (while availability lasts).


What you will see


Our smart demo car is fitted with Neonode's in-cabin technology. You will see, live in action;


-


Distraction Detection: The car will alert if you get distracted while in the driver's seat.


-


Occupancy: See occupancy in the vehicle using computer vision.


-


Hands-on-wheel Detection: The car will recognize if you have your hands on the wheel or not.


-


Augmented Mirrors: The image on the digital mirrors will update based on the driver's line-of-sight, so images move fluidly like a reflective mirror.


-


Seatbelt Detection: See if the seatbelt is being worn correctly.


Where to find us


Neonode will be available for bookings on the east and west coast. Contact us to book a demo at your address.


East Coast: 14-17 April, 2026
West Coast: 25-29 May, 2026
