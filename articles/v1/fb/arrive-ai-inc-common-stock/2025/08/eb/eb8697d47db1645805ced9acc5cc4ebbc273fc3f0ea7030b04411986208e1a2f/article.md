# From highways to the future: How one truck is driving Arrive AI’s mission across America

In the world of logistics, movement isn’t just about packages—it’s about ideas, innovation, and progress on the road. For Arrive AI, that progress is rolling across America one mile at a time, thanks to a partnership that perfectly embodies what it means to move the future forward.


Rodney Roberts, an investor in Arrive AI and Director of Tri R Logistics, offered one of his trailers for a full Arrive AI custom wrap, transforming it into a moving billboard showcasing our Nasdaq-listed mission to “discover the future of intelligent delivery.” It’s more than a striking visual on highways; it’s a daily reminder to thousands of drivers and travelers that autonomous, secure, and climate-assisted delivery is not a dream for tomorrow—it’s happening now.


The Arrive AI team gathered outside Launch Fishers on July 1, 2025 to welcome the traveling Arrive AI truck as it completed its first wave across the country


Over the past few months, this trailer has made its way across the South and Southwest, with stops in cities like Houston, Dallas, Omaha, Racine, and Kansas City, catching sunrises at truck stops and sunsets over highways as it delivers goods while sharing our vision. Recently, Rodney made a special detour, parking the trailer in our office parking lot at Launch Fishers in Indianapolis, giving our team the chance to stand beside the symbol of the movement we’re building.


But this isn’t just about Arrive AI. It’s about you, too. We’re inviting you to join us on this journey with our **#SpotArrive challenge** . If you see the Arrive AI trailer while on the road, snap a photo, tag us, and share where you spotted it. Every month, we’ll randomly select participants to receive exclusive Arrive AI swag, adding your story to the journey of bringing smart, secure delivery to every community.


This truck is a symbol of what happens when partners like Rodney believe in what’s possible and take action to share it with the world. As we continue to build out autonomous delivery networks that prioritize security, climate assistance, and last-inch delivery innovation, we are grateful to have champions driving alongside us, mile by mile.


The future of delivery is rolling forward. Keep your eyes on the road—and join us as we discover it together.
