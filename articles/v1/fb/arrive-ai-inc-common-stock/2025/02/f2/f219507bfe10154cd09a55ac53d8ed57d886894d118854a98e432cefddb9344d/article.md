# Arrive AI’s Core Patents: Pioneering Smart Drone Delivery


Arrive AI has developed and secured patents covering every essential aspect of last-inch delivery. Some of our most notable innovations include:


- Smart Drone Delivery Receptacle – A secure, high-tech mailbox that receives and protects drone-delivered packages, mounted on porches, rooftops, or underground.
- Temperature-Assisted Storage – Ensures food, medicine, and other sensitive items remain at the proper temperature until retrieval.
- Expandable/Accordion Floor – A receptacle that dynamically adjusts to accommodate multiple packages securely.
- Multi-User Smart Mailbox – A system enabling secure deliveries for multiple recipients in residential, commercial, and industrial settings.
- Drone Charging & Landing Stations – Smart docking stations where drones can land, recharge, or swap batteries, increasing delivery efficiency.
- Advanced Security & Sanitation – Integrates facial recognition, security cameras, and built-in UV sanitization to ensure package safety and hygiene.


Each of these patents reinforces our competitive advantage and enables us to license key technologies to industry players.


# The Game-Changer: The Winch (Tethering) Patent


One of the most strategic patents in our portfolio is the drone winch system, which allows precise package deliveries via a retractable tether. Many major drone delivery companies already use this system, but what they may not realize is that Arrive AI owns the core patent.


Why the Tethering System Matters


- Precision & Safety – The winch system ensures packages are lowered gently and precisely, avoiding damage and enabling deliveries in tight urban environments.
- Essential for Drone Logistics – Nearly every autonomous drone delivery company needs this technology to achieve reliable, real-world deliveries.
- Market Control – Owning this patent allows Arrive AI to license it to major logistics players or block competitors from using it without permission.


By controlling this patent, Arrive AI establishes itself as a gatekeeper of critical drone delivery technology, giving us strong negotiation power in the autonomous logistics industry.
