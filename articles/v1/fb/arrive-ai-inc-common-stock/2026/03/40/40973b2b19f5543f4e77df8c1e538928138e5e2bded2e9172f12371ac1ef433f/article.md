# How the Arrive Point™ Network Enhances Healthcare Logistics


Arrive AI delivers a comprehensive autonomous delivery platform anchored by our proprietary Arrive Point™. Arrive Points are smart, secure delivery stations that serve as hubs for item transfer, scheduling, and system orchestration within hospital facilities.


Think “Mailbox 2.0.”


By collaborating with[Ottonomy](https://ottonomy.io/) , a recognized leader in ground robotics whose solutions are already active in major hospital systems like Cedars-Sinai, Northwell Health, and Baylor Scott & White, we've integrated advanced robotic mobility with our unattended delivery network.


This synergy allows for seamless coordination between robots, staff, and couriers, ensuring every delivery is tracked, secure, and efficiently managed from start to finish. Our Arrive Points enable real-time updates, automated handoffs, and scalable logistics workflows that adapt to the unique needs of each healthcare environment, elevating operational reliability and freeing up valuable staff time for direct patient care.


# What is the HIMSS Global Health Conference?


Every year, thousands of healthcare professionals, innovators, executives, and technology leaders converge at HIMSS. It’s one of the most influential gatherings in health technology that attracts worldwide attention each year. Historically, the event has drawn more than 30,000 participants from across the global care ecosystem, making it a true catalyst for transformation in how health systems operate.


Brought together by the[Healthcare Information and Management Systems Society (HIMSS),](https://www.himss.org/who-we-are/) a nonprofit organization dedicated to advancing health transformation through information and technology, this annual summit serves as a hub for pushing boundaries and shaping the future of healthcare as it relates to artificial intelligence, business operations, cybersecurity, and other key areas.


HIMSS stands apart from other clinical conferences by emphasizing digital transformation, interoperability, cybersecurity, AI adoption, and health equity. These are all essential components for modern care, and attendees don’t just come to learn; they come to forge partnerships and strategies that will power tomorrow’s health systems.
