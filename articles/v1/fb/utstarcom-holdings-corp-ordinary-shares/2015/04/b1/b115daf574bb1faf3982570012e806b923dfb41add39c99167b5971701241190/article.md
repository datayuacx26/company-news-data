## UTStarcom discussed their plans and product line-up at ‘Nextgen Access & Transport India 2015’


UTStarcom, a global telecommunications infrastructure provider has announced their aggressive plans and their lineup product portfolio for transport, aggregation and access networking solutions for Indian market in ‘Nextgen Access & Transport India 2015’. In line up to the government initiatives like NOFN and Smart City, the company discussed about the future of Wi-Fi, Nextgen access and the transport in India.
