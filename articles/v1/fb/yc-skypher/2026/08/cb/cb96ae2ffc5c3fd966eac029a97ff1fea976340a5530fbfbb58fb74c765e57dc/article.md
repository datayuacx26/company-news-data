## What is Wolfia and how does it work?


Wolfia calls itself "your AI agent for security questionnaires, contract review, and your trust center".


‍


The product is six agents rather than one tool: including Skypher, do not addressnt.


‍


The Contract Review Agent stands out. It redlines security addenda against your standards. Most questionnaire tools, Skypher included, do not touch that work. If security contract review eats your legal team's time, that alone is a reason to look at Wolfia.


‍


Integrations as of August 2026: Slack, Google Drive, Notion, Salesforce, HubSpot, Confluence, Jira, OneDrive. They hold SOC 2 Type II, with per-tenant isolation, SSO, and an audit trail.

## Wolfia pricing in 2026


Wolfia does not publish pricing. Their pricing page shows no plan names and no figures.


‍


What it does say: billing tracks questionnaire and review volume rather than headcount. Unlimited users, no per-seat or per-viewer fees. All agents on one knowledge base. No caps on users, storage or features. Then: "Talk to us about pricing."


‍


Volume-based billing matches how this work actually scales, so the model is a good one. You will still need a sales call to get a number.


‍


One thing to note, because it comes up later. Wolfia criticises Skypher for not publishing pricing. Wolfia does not publish pricing either.

Enterprise deals closing 5x faster at Juicebox. Handshake at 90% automation with sub-two-day turnaround on Fortune 100 reviews. ## FAQ


### Is Wolfia a good security questionnaire tool?


Yes. Named customers include Amplitude, Miro, Instacart and CircleCI, with published results such as $1.5M+ in annual value at Amplitude and 90% automation with sub-two-day turnaround at Handshake. Its main differentiator is a source citation on every answer. The most cited limitation, per UpGuard, is the training period before automation rates climb.


### How much does Wolfia cost?


Wolfia does not publish pricing. As of August 2026 their pricing page describes usage-based billing, "priced around the work, not the seat", with unlimited users and no per-seat or per-viewer fees, then directs prospects to contact sales. You will need a conversation to get a figure.


### Does Wolfia have G2 reviews?


Not as of 2026. UpGuard's category comparison lists Wolfia as "Currently not rated" on G2, against Conveyor at 4.6 from 150 reviews and HyperComply at 4.3 from 12. If third-party reviews matter to your evaluation, factor that in.


### What are the best Wolfia alternatives?


Skypher, Conveyor, SafeBase (now part of Drata), and the RFP-first platforms Loopio and Responsive. Skypher fits teams needing answer style control, granular role-based permissions and a short path to a usable first draft. Conveyor publishes pricing from $9,600 a year. SafeBase fits trust-center-first teams.


### Wolfia vs Skypher: what is the actual difference?


Portal coverage and format handling are close to level. The real differences are that Wolfia has a Contract Review Agent for security addenda, which Skypher does not, and Skypher has answer style control and granular role-based permissions, which Wolfia does not publish. UpGuard also flags a training period before Wolfia's automation rates climb.
