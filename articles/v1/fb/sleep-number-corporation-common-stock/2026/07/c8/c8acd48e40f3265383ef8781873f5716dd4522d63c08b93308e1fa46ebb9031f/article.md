# New Sleep Number Research Shows Adjusting Bed Temperature Can Add Up to Nearly an Hour of Sleep Per Night

*Large-scale stud y presented at SLEEP 2026 explores how personalized temperature features can help people sleep longer*


**MINNEAPOLIS – (June 14** **, 2026)**


– New research from


[Sleep Number Corporation](http://www.sleepnumber.com/) ,


presented at SLEEP 2026


in Baltimore,


s


how


s


that adjusting the temperature of


one’s


microclimate


is


an


effective and actionable way to improve sleep. The findings also highlight how Sleep Number’s adjustable comfort and dynamic temperature capabilities help people sleep longer


.


Getting the right temperature at night is key to sleeping well.


The body relies on a natural cooling process to


initiate


and maintain sleep. As core temperature drops, the skin warms to help dissipate heat, creating the right conditions for rest


, but things like room temperature, bedding and physiology can disrupt that balance. Sleep Number’s temperature technology, like that found in its


[Climate ™ collection](https://www.sleepnumber.com/collections/mattresses-climate-collection) ,


is


designed to help solve this by adjusting


the bed’s


temperature throughout the night


, helping keep sleepers comfortable


.


For example,


the


[Climate360 ®](https://www.sleepnumber.com/collections/mattresses-climate-collection/Q1C360) and


[ClimateCool ®](https://www.sleepnumber.com/collections/mattresses-climate-collection/QT8)


beds cool up to 20x faster than a leading competitor* and


previous


analysis shows


Climate360


users get 52.5 minutes more restful sleep per


night.*


*


“Temperature has a powerful impact on your comfort and your sleep,”


said


Melissa Barra, Sleep Number’s EVP and Chief Product, Strategy and Technology Officer.


“As a leader in temperature innovation,


we designed


our beds


to


adjust with you through the night


—helping you sleep up to 15


degrees


cooler or warmer on each side—


so you stay comfortable and wake


up feeling more rested.


This research shows how mattresses are evolving


, and how personalized comfort that adapts to you can help


improve sleep and wellness over time.”


In one of the largest real-world sleep studies of its kind, Sleep Number researchers analyzed sleep data from


nearly


82,000


people


across more than one million


sleep sessions


gathered directly from


Climate360


sleepers in their home


.


The study looked at how


using the Climate360 bed


’s


different temperature settings and adjustments throughout the night influenced sleep patterns


The data showed that b


y


adjusting the temperature of the mattress,


sleep duration


increased


by


more than


52


additional


minutes per night compared with


those who did not adjust


temperature.


The most effective approach gently warms the bed early


in the night


to


promote heat loss from the body and support the natural drop in core body temperature


then


later


cools the


sleeper by actively drawing warm air away from their body


to


align with the


body


’s


natural


sleep


cycle


.


Together, these findings provide strong, population-scale evidence that dynamic temperature control is


an effective


, science-backed way to improve sleep.
