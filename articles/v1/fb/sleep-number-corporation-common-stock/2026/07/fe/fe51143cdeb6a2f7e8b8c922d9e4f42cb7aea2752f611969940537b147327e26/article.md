## Why Your Pillow Affects How Hot You Sleep


### How Heat Gets Trapped in Conventional Pillows


#### Traditional pillows often use dense materials that hold onto body heat. Combined with non-breathable covers, they can create a warm microclimate around your head and neck—leading to overheating and disrupted sleep.


### The Role of the Pillow in Your Overall Sleep Temperature System


#### Your pillow is just one part of your sleep environment. Sheets, mattresses, and even room temperature all work together to influence how cool (or warm) you feel at night. Learn more about optimizing your setup for better rest in this guide to[best temperature for sleep](https://www.sleepnumber.com/pages/best-temperature-sleep) .


### Signs Your Pillow May Be Making You Sleep Hotter


#### You might need a cooling pillow if you:


-


#### Wake up sweaty or overheated


-


#### Flip your pillow throughout the night


-


#### Have trouble falling back asleep due to heat
