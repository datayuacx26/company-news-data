# What the Sleep Number and Sleep Country Canada Partnership Means for You

At Sleep Number, our mission has always been to help people achieve better sleep through personalized comfort, innovation and exceptional customer care. As we begin an exciting new chapter alongside Sleep Country Canada, that commitment remains unchanged.


We know our customers may have questions about what this partnership means and what they can expect moving forward. The good news is that Sleep Number customers will continue to enjoy the experience they know and trust, including access to the Sleep Number® app, product deliveries, rewards, customer care and warranty support.


To share more about this exciting next step, Sleep Number CEO Linda Findley and Sleep Country Canada CEO Stewart Schaefer recently sat down for a conversation about the future of the Sleep Number brand, the shared values that brought the companies together and their commitment to continuing to serve customers for years to come.
