# The Ultimate Guide to Better Sleep

*[Sleep is one of the most essential pillars of health](https://www.sleepnumber.com/pages/health-well-being) , yet it’s often disrupted by stress, lifestyle habits, and even our relationships. Whether you sleep alone,[with a partner](https://www.sleepnumber.com/pages/mattress-couples) , or in a busy household, improving your sleep starts with understanding how your environment, emotions and routines affect your rest.*


## 1. Know Your Sleep Style


Everyone has a unique sleep personality. Some people fall asleep quickly and wake up early, while others are[night owls](https://www.sleepnumber.com/post/how-night-owls-and-early-birds-can-get-along) who need more time to wind down. Recognizing your natural rhythm helps you build a routine that supports your body’s needs.


**Try this:** Track your sleep for a week to identify patterns. Use the[Sleep Number® App](https://www.sleepnumber.com/pages/sleep-number-app) to get personalized sleep insights and understand your sleep.
