HR reporting shows you what happened through metrics and data summaries. HR analytics goes further by explaining why it happened and predicting what will happen next. inFeedo Report Studio combines both, giving you reports with AI-powered summaries that surface actionable recommendations alongside your workforce data.


### How much time can automated HR reporting save compared to manual slide decks?


Organizations using automated reporting tools typically reduce report creation time from hours to minutes. With inFeedo Report Studio, you can generate comprehensive EX reports with a single click using pre-built templates. The automated scheduling feature also eliminates recurring manual work by delivering fresh insights to stakeholders automatically.


### What types of HR reports can be automated?


Most employee experience reports can be automated, including pulse survey results, tenure analysis, exit interview summaries, and[eNPS tracking](https://www.infeedo.ai/enps-survey) . inFeedo's templates cover these common reporting needs while allowing customization for specific organizational requirements.

Purpose-built HR reporting tools like inFeedo Report Studio are designed for HR professionals, not data scientists.
