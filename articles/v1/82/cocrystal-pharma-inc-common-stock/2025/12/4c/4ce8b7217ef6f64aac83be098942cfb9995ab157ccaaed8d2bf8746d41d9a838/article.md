**BOTHELL, Wash., Dec. 01, 2025 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --**[Cocrystal Pharma, Inc](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=tGnt17nlgwnnfUsJEdJ2WTiOA9gDllYpZyWJx_myQWKK4BAO_mDQ1rV8jKYAHW3e5FvfWZ5vaPHjUs2CfepSW8lxFd8CSSi2vahyfslm0z4=) . (Nasdaq: COCP) (“Cocrystal” or the “Company”) announces that James Martin, CFO and co-CEO, will present a Company overview and clinical progress update at NobleCon 21 – Noble Capital Markets 21st Emerging Growth Equity Conference on Wednesday, December 3, 2025 at 10:00 am Eastern time. The conference is being held at the Executive Education Complex of Florida Atlantic University in Boca Raton, Florida.


Interested investors and guests of Cocrystal Pharma are welcome to attend the conference at a discounted rate. Please register[here](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=Hrd2sfZtZn84GhsAjZRhn1w0QjWLji1tUuvQn7Up2deFM8EY7_dv5xLAJ0JR5Jdsjhst7oKtf16IC6uIhGeDsVoz49ICdXWjWoOL4gqOELa4XFvaGZzbrUBg1kgHemF7sOVosqglspYHfVPcPWagC2LnB9HzYVPaUpwvHaEvV0U=) using the discount code COCPNOBLECON.


A video webcast of the Cocrystal presentation will be available on December 4, one day after the live event, on the Company's[website](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=kgxuIafcXYUCObVCQkAKB6tY6SfwoaYwx1lXMekZjHnBDq2bLWqYQwu3cwEqU79J7FrCi7FWqh_RBdsD-1YDeSdMceLHj2A21EVMgYrL2UU=) and archived for 90 days.
