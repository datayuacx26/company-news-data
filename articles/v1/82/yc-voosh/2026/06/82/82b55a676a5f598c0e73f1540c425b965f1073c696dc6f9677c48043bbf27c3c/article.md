# Personalized Delivery Promotions That Keep Diners Coming Back

## Personalized Delivery Promotions: A Powerful Tool to Keep Diners Coming Back


In today’s competitive food-delivery market, one-size-fits-all deals rarely work. Savvy restaurant operators are turning to personalized promotions


on apps like DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub to increase repeat business. A personalized promotion is a targeted offer (discount, free item or coupon) crafted from customer data - for example, offering a free dessert to a repeat diner or a discount on a customer’s favorite dish. These data-driven offers can dramatically boost loyalty and sales


: in fact, industry research finds nearly 1 in 3 customers


say tailored deals make them more loyal to a restaurant. By using order history and behavior to inform each deal, restaurants can create offers that feel relevant and valuable to individual diners.


## Why Personalized Promos Matter for Delivery Restaurants


Unlike random discounts, personalized promotions speak directly to each customer’s tastes. For example, if a customer frequently orders spicy curry, a targeted promotion might be “20% off your next curry order.” Restaurant Dive notes that leveraging large pools of customer data allows operators to “deliver personalized promotions to new and existing customers,” changing buying behavior and keeping diners coming back. This approach can protect your margins while still generating profit: according to one marketing report, customers exposed to targeted offers spend 35% more


on average, and 70%


say they’re more likely to visit a restaurant with personalized promotions. In short, these offers maximize revenue from each order


by increasing basket size and frequency.


## Types of Personalized Offers for Delivery Apps


- Loyalty Discounts:


Give regular customers a special deal, like “10% off after 3 orders.” This rewards repeat business and encourages continued ordering.


- Item-specific Coupons:


Tailor deals to past purchases (“Free appetizer with your next chicken biryani order”). These feel highly relevant to the diner.


- Time-Sensitive Deals:


Use urgency and segmentation (e.g. a lunch-hour special for nearby office workers). Target customers who order at certain times with flash promos.


- Tiered Offers:


Structure promotions based on spend or visit count (e.g. “$5 off after $50 spent”). This motivates upsells and higher AOV.


- Reactivation Offers:


Reach out to lapsed customers with a personalized discount to bring them back. Even a small targeted coupon can re-engage a dormant customer.


Each type should be selected based on clear goals (see next section).


## How to Create Targeted Delivery Promotions (Step-by-Step)


1. Segment Your Customers:


Start by analyzing your delivery platform data. Identify high-value segments (e.g. frequent vs. occasional customers, big spenders vs. budget diners). Voosh’s dashboard can help slice this data.


2. Set Clear Objectives:


Decide what each campaign should achieve – more orders on slow days, higher per-order spend, or re-engagement of inactive users. Your goal drives the promo type.


3. Craft the Offer:


Create a promotion that appeals to your target. For high-spenders, offer a tiered discount (e.g. “$10 off $100+ order”). For infrequent diners, try a welcome-back deal (e.g. “Free dessert on your next order”). Keep the value compelling but affordable.


4. Launch on Delivery Platforms:


Coordinate your offer through the apps’ marketing tools or through Voosh’s Ads & Promotions module. Ensure customers see it - for example, feature it prominently on your menu pages or send push notifications via the app.


5. Monitor and Adjust:


Track how each promotion performs. Key metrics include redemption rate, repeat order lift, and ROI. According to Voosh data, brands see anywhere from a 20%


to 50%


lift in repeat orders when promotions are well-targeted. Use these insights to refine future offers.


By iterating on these steps, you’ll create a cycle of increasingly effective promotions that drive loyalty and profits.


## Tracking Promotion Success (KPIs to Watch)


Effective promotions must be measured. Key performance indicators include:


- Redemption Rate:


Percentage of customers who used the offer. A low rate may mean the promo wasn’t appealing or visible.


- Repeat Order Lift:


Increase in orders from the targeted segment compared to a baseline. This shows loyalty impact.


- Average Order Value (AOV):


Did the promotion encourage larger purchases (e.g. upsells added to qualify for the deal)?


- Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):


If using paid campaigns on apps, compare additional revenue generated to your marketing spend.


- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):


For promotions to new customers, track how much you spend per new active user.


Voosh’s Ads & Promotions Analytics dashboard automates these reports across DoorDash, UberEats, etc., so you see exactly which promo is driving profit. If an offer underperforms (e.g. high cost per acquisition), reallocate budget to higher ROI tactics.


## Avoiding Common Pitfalls


- Over-Discounting:


Deep coupons too frequently can train customers to wait for deals. Keep promos valuable but sustainable.


- Ignoring Data Privacy:


Use only aggregated or opt-in data. Never misuse personal information.


- Lack of Differentiation:


Make sure your promotion stands out (e.g. unique items or combos) rather than repeating the same “10% off everything” generic deal.
