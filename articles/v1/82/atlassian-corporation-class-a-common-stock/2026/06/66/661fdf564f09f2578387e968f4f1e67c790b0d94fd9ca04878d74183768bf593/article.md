# Accelerating government modernization with AWS

Government technology teams face a growing mandate: modernize faster, strengthen security, and prepare for an AI-powered future all without disrupting mission-critical systems. To help agencies meet these demands, Atlassian and Amazon Web Services (AWS) have expanded our Strategic Collaboration Agreement.


Together, we’re delivering public sector teams cloud native capabilities to modernize at speed on trusted AWS infrastructure. With migration paths from Data Center to Atlassian Cloud or FedRAMP-Moderate-authorized Atlassian Government Cloud both of which are built on AWS organizations of all sizes can move to modern, secure infrastructure while maintaining the compliance standards their missions demand.


Agencies moving to Atlassian Cloud and Atlassian Government Cloud unlock benefits like:


- **Tighter collaboration and greater efficiency:** Tap into the speed, continuous innovation, and seamless integration of cloud products to drive better cross agency collaboration.
- **Stronger, unified data & insights:** Leverage the wealth of valuable data in your Atlassian portfolio to surface insights across your agency for greater visibility and better mission outcomes.
- **Improved productivity:** With AI capabilities (available now in Atlassian Cloud and coming soon to Atlassian Government Cloud), teams and individuals can make work move faster and focus on higher-value mission tasks.


Plus, agencies get enterprise-grade reliability and security out-of-the-box. Atlassian’s Cloud products run on the same high-availability AWS infrastructure already trusted across the federal landscape.


Atlassian Cloud also offers security capabilities and add-ons like customer-managed keys, Guard Premium, data residency, and more; while Atlassian Government Cloud is authorized at the FedRAMP Moderate level to help agencies address their compliance requirements.


> “Public sector agencies need to modernize quickly while maintaining the highest levels of security and compliance,” said **Amy Belcher, Director Global ISV Sales & GTM, AWS.** “That’s why our strategic collaboration agreement with Atlassian matters. By combining the secure, scalable environment powered by AWS with the FedRAMP Moderate authorized Atlassian Government Cloud, we’re helping government teams unlock their data, adopt AI responsibly, and collaborate more effectively so they can deliver on their missions at speed.”


Together, Atlassian and AWS are giving public sector teams a clear path to modern infrastructure, mission critical resilience, and AI-ready teamwork. Learn more on our[AWS partnership page](https://www.atlassian.com/partnerships/aws) .
