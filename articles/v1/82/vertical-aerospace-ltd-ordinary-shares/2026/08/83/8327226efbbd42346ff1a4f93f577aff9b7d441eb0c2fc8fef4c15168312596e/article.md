# Share your perspective on today’s helicopter workforce

As the helicopter industry continues to navigate workforce shortages, recruitment challenges, and employee retention, *Vertical Plus* has launched two global surveys to gather insights from employers and employees across all sectors of the industry.


The surveys are designed to capture perspectives from both employers and employees, helping tell the story of the industry’s workforce — from how organizations are navigating recruitment and retention to how professionals view their careers, opportunities, and workplace challenges.


Survey topics include hiring practices, recruitment and retention, compensation and benefits, career pathways, training, and the factors shaping both organizational and employee decision-making.


“As an industry publication, our role is to ask important questions and give a voice to the people doing the work,” said *Vertical Plus* publisher Mike Reyno. “These surveys will help us better understand today’s workforce and share those insights with the broader helicopter community.”


Findings from the surveys will be featured in the December/January issue of *Vertical Plus* and will help inform ongoing editorial coverage of workforce development, recruitment, retention, training, and other issues affecting the global helicopter industry.


Participation is confidential, and individual responses will not be identified unless respondents choose to be contacted for a follow-up interview.
