# Archer’s Midnight completes city-to-city flights in California

Archer Aviation has announced the completion of a piloted roundtrip journey with its all-electric Midnight aircraft on July 30, departing from Salinas Municipal Airport and landing at Monterey Regional Airport before making the return flight.


Archer worked closely with the U.S. Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) to execute this piloted, round trip inter city flight, a major step toward launching Midnight operations under the White House’s eIPP program later this year.


Midnight covered each leg of the journey in roughly nine minutes, a trip that can take approximately 35-plus minutes by car, demonstrating the kind of short, time-saving hops the aircraft was built to fly.


“This roundtrip journey is exactly the kind of route we plan to fly with Midnight under the eIPP and in LA during the Olympic Games,” said Adam Goldstein, founder and CEO of Archer.


“This mission provides a template for us to replicate with our partners across Texas, Florida and New York as we get ready for eIPP operations later this year.”


As part of its broader eIPP efforts, Archer is working closely with the FAA, federal, state and local partners to establish the procedures, infrastructure and local operational capabilities needed to support future commercial air taxi operations. That work also supports preparations in Los Angeles, where Archer will serve as the Official Air Taxi Provider for the LA28 Olympic Games.


Archer’s[Midnight](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Farcher.com%2Faircraft&esheet=54582098&newsitemid=20260803279745&lan=en-US&anchor=Midnight&index=2&md5=40405e38eb6c3ba63d9aad89184757d2) air taxi is designed to enable passengers to replace 60-[90](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/rotor+x/90) minute trips on the ground with quiet, all-electric flights, dramatically reducing travel times compared to traditional ground transportation and helping avoid growing levels of congestion.
