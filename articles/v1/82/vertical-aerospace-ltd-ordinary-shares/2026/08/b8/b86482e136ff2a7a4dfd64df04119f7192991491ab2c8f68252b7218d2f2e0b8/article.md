# Skyfly signs letter of intent to establish U.S. operations hub in Florida at SunTrax

Skyfly Technologies Ltd. announced it has signed a letter of intent (LOI) with the Florida Department of Transportation to operate at SunTrax in Auburndale, Florida.


Skyfly is developing a unique and groundbreaking two seat fixed-wing aircraft that is built to conform to the FAA’s new MOSAIC rules for Light-Sport Category aircraft.


The Skyfly-FDOT agreement marks a significant milestone in Skyfly’s U.S. expansion strategy, establishing the company’s first dedicated presence in the United States and reinforcing its commitment to the U.S. as a core market for its advanced air mobility technology.


Skyfly’s plans were publicly showcased on Aug. 10, 2026, when Florida Governor Ron DeSantis visited the SunTrax facility in Auburndale to highlight the site’s expanding role in advanced air mobility and autonomous vehicle testing.


Florida Department of Transportation Secretary Jared W. Perdue, P.E. also took part in the event, underlining the state’s support for Skyfly’s move into the Florida market.


“Access to SunTrax gives us a world-class testing environment and puts us at the heart of one of the most important advanced air mobility ecosystems in the country,” said Michael Thompson, CEO of Skyfly Technologies. “This is an important step in bringing Skyfly’s technology to the U.S. market.”


“FDOT’s partnership with Skyfly brings vital testing and workforce opportunities to Florida, further advancing this emerging mode of transportation,” said Jared W. Perdue, P.E., Florida Department of Transportation Secretary. “Skyfly establishing operations at SunTrax brings Florida one step closer to taking highways to the sky and making flying cars a reality.”


Under the terms of the LOI, Skyfly intends to commence occupancy of the leased space in the fourth quarter of 2026. SunTrax will also support Skyfly’s growth in Florida, including assistance with local workforce development, state and federal grant guidance, and regulatory introductions, including with the FAA.


The parties intend to finalize a formal lease agreement in the coming months.
