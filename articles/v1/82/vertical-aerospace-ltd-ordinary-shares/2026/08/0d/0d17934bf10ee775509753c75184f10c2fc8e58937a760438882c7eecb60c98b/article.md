# BOOST Systems receives EASA approval for H125/AS350 cargo basket and Starlink mount

BOOST Systems has received European Aviation Safety Agency (EASA) approval for its[H125](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h125) /AS350 Cargo Basket, Boarding Steps and Starlink Mount, expanding the availability of the company’s certified mission equipment for helicopter operators throughout Europe.


The EASA approved stainless steel cargo basket product line is available in multiple configurations, with options providing up to 500 pounds of cargo capacity and up to 22 cubic feet of cargo volume.


“We are building several different stainless-steel Cargo Baskets with a variety of features based on the operator’s mission,” said Jeff Yarnold, VP of operations at BOOST Systems. “Our goal is to give operators practical, certified solutions that improve the capabilities of their aircraft.”


“Our most recent value-added product for the cargo baskets is our Starlink Mount,” said Yarnold. “This solution enhances what we can offer operators in the field. With this capability, crews can integrate approved Starlink connectivity into their aircraft, providing enhanced real-time communication, data access, and overall mission effectiveness.”


The EASA approval represents another milestone in BOOST Systems’ expanding portfolio of certified helicopter mission equipment and strengthens the company’s ability to support operators worldwide.


BOOST Systems is a global leader in fully certified Human External Cargo (HEC) Systems and continues to develop a growing range of next-generation mission equipment for helicopter operators.
