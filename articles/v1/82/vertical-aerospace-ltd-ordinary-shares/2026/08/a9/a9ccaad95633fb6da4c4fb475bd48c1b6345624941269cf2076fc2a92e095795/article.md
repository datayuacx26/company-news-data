# Vertical Plus announces results of 2026 Helicopter & Engine Manufacturers Survey

Nearly 1,300 helicopter industry professionals contributed to *Vertical’s* 12th annual Helicopter and Engine Manufacturers Survey, reinforcing its position as the industry’s leading benchmark for OEM customer support.


The full results are available in the August/September issue of *Vertical Plus* ,[now available online](https://issues.verticalmag.com/554/766/1745/V25I4/index.html) .


Among the engine manufacturers, Safran Helicopter Engines claimed the top ranking, while Bell once again led the airframe category. Robinson Helicopter Company and Airbus Helicopters also posted notable improvements over last year’s results.


“Our survey has become an important resource for both operators and manufacturers because it provides direct, measurable feedback from the people using these aircraft and engines every day,” said *Vertical* publisher Mike Reyno. “Year after year, it gives OEMs valuable insight into what’s working, where improvements are needed, and how customer support is evolving across the industry.”


While participation in this year’s survey increased notably over last year, respondents were unevenly distributed across OEMs. Hélicoptères Guimbal and MD Helicopters did not receive enough responses to qualify for ranking this year, but both companies shared updates on their customer support initiatives, which are included alongside the survey results.


This year’s responses indicate the helicopter industry is continuing to adapt to a “new normal,” shaped by ongoing supply chain constraints. Parts availability remains a major concern across the board, and respondents made it clear that the manufacturers’ ability to navigate these challenges plays a significant role in customer satisfaction.


The survey also highlights the industry’s persistent workforce shortage, with 61 percent of respondents reporting difficulty finding experienced mechanics and engineers, while 37 percent continue to struggle to recruit experienced pilots.


“We’re grateful to everyone who took the time to share their experiences, and to the manufacturers who are willing to listen and respond,” Reyno added. “That’s what makes this survey so valuable. Ultimately, those conversations help strengthen the industry for everyone.”
