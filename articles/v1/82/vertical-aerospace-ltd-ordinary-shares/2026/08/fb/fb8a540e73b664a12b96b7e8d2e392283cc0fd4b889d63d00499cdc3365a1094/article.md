# Pegasus Aero Group and SkyConnect reach long-term, multi-aircraft UH-60 Black Hawk lease agreement

Pegasus Air Group Photo


Pegasus Aero Group and SkyConnect Leasing have entered into a strategic agreement and signed a long-term multi-aircraft lease agreement to add UH-60L Black Hawk helicopters for wildfire suppression, civil protection and emergency-response operations.


The agreement combines Pegasus’s operational expertise with SkyConnect’s dedicated Black Hawk leasing platform, enabling Pegasus to develop new capabilities for the European and Latin American markets.


The transaction is a milestone in Pegasus’s growth plan and strengthens the company’s position in the heavy-lift helicopter segment, a category that has historically played an essential role in demanding aerial missions.


By adding a significant number of[UH-60](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/sikorsky/uh-60) Black Hawk helicopters, Pegasus—one of Europe’s leading aerial emergency operators—underscores the importance of high-capacity, high-performance and highly versatile aircraft in addressing the challenges posed by increasingly intense and prolonged fire seasons in Europe and Latin America.


The first helicopters will be delivered from March 2027 under a long-term lease signed by the two companies. The programme also provides for future fleet growth and close collaboration between the two companies on developing operational capabilities associated with the Black Hawk platform.


The decision to commit to the[UH-60](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/sikorsky/uh-60) Black Hawk aligns with an increasing demand in Europe, where high-capacity aircraft are required for water-dropping and other emergency services. The selected model combines high payload capacity, reliability, availability and flexibility for complex missions.


Through this strategic agreement, Pegasus strengthens its heavy-lift helicopter operations, a segment in which it has accumulated more than 12,000 flight hours over its 60-year history. The addition of these aircraft will take Pegasus’s fleet beyond 140 aeroplanes and helicopters for firefighting, rescue and medical-emergency missions.


SkyConnect Leasing is a specialized aviation lessor focused on mission-critical aircraft for emergency response operations. The company has developed a dedicated Blackhawk leasing platform for firefighting, combining flexible financing, aircraft availability and specialized support for experienced operators that need to deploy capabilities quickly and efficiently.


Miguel Angel Tamarit Almagro, Vice President and Head of Business Development, said: “The addition of the[UH-60](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/sikorsky/uh-60) Black Hawk represents far more than the arrival of a new aircraft. It strengthens Pegasus’s position in the heavy-lift helicopter segment and our ability to meet our customers’ future needs. We are investing in a platform with an outstanding operational record that is becoming an international benchmark for firefighting and emergency missions. SkyConnect shares our long-term vision and brings deep knowledge of the international Black Hawk market. This agreement gives us access to distinctive capabilities and enables us to accelerate our growth plan with a sound and sustainable financial structure.”


Patrick Moulay CEO of SkyConnect Leasing, said: “Pegasus is an exceptional operator with decades of experience in aerial firefighting and emergency-response missions. This long-term agreement validates SkyConnect specialist leasing model and demonstrates the role that we can play in delivering proven, high-capacity aircraft into service where they are needed most. We are proud to support Pegasus’s growth and to establish a partnership that strengthens wildfire-response capability in Spain and across Europe.”
