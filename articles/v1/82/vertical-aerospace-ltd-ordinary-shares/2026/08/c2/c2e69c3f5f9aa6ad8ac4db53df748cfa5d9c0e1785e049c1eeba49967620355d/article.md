# Robinson Helicopter Company to deliver 5,000th R44 Raven II

[Robinson Helicopter Company](https://www.robinsonheli.com/) (RHC) has reached a new milestone, selling its 5,000th[R44 Raven II](https://www.robinsonheli.com/helicopters/r44-raven-ii-clipper-ii) helicopter to its dealership ARICA in Ecuador.


This achievement marks a defining moment in the company’s history and reflects steady, global demand for the[R44](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/robinson/r44) globally.


The[R44](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/robinson/r44) is known for its simple design, low operating cost and reliable performance. Pilots and small businesses fly it for training, tours, farm work, news coverage and personal travel. It has become one of the most common helicopters in the sky today.


“Delivering the 5,000th[r44](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/robinson/%3Ca%20href=) +raven+ii" target="_blank">[R44](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/robinson/r44) Raven II reflects the enduring trust operators around the world place in Robinson,” said David Smith, president and CEO of Robinson Helicopter Company.


“The[R44](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/robinson/r44) remains the most widely sold and flown light civil helicopter on the planet because it delivers unmatched reliability, low direct operating costs, and multi-mission versatility. Whether it is powering flight schools, supporting agricultural operations, or serving private and utility operators, the Raven II is the benchmark for modern light rotorcraft.”


Robinson certified the[R44](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/robinson/r44) in 1990 and delivered the first[R44](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/robinson/r44) in 1993. Since then, the model has grown into one of the best-selling helicopters ever made with owners and operators in more than 100 countries flying it today.


The[R44](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/robinson/r44) comes in multiple variants, including the Raven I, Clipper I, the Cadet for training and the Raven II and its counterpart the Clipper II. Robinson recently unveiled a new Utility Interior available on all R44s, with easy to clean seat fabrics and replacing the carpet and headliner with a more ruggedized materials for tough missions.
