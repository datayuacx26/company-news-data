# Life Link III names Shane Farmer as president and CEO

Shane Farmer. Life Link III Photo


Life Link III has announced Shane Farmer, DHA, MHA, FACHE, CPHQ, as its next president and CEO, effective July 31, 2026.


Farmer brings over 25 years of experience in patient care, healthcare operations, and executive leadership.


He began his career as a flight paramedic serving children’s hospitals, an experience that inspired his lifelong commitment to patient access, healthcare quality, safety, and equity.


Throughout his career, Farmer has held executive leadership roles with PHI Air Medical, Air Methods Corporation, Intuitive Health, and CSI Aviation, leading complex healthcare operations while driving improvements in financial performance, clinical growth, quality, patient safety, and workforce engagement.


As president and CEO, Farmer will work closely with Life Link III’s board of directors and its nine-member health systems to strengthen regional access to care, advance strategic priorities, and support the organization’s long-term success.


“I am honored to join Life Link III, an organization with a remarkable legacy of excellence, innovation, and service,” said Farmer.


“I look forward to partnering with our exceptional team and member health systems to build on that legacy, honor our longstanding commitment to safety, and continue setting the standard for excellence.”


Farmer is a fellow of the American College of Healthcare Executives and a certified professional in healthcare quality. He earned both his doctor of healthcare administration and master of healthcare administration from Oklahoma State University and is also a published healthcare textbook author and editor.
