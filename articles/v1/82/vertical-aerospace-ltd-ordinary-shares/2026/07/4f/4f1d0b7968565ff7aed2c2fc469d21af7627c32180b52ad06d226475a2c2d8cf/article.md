# Bell celebrates the first IFR-configured Bell 407GXi order in the United Kingdom

Bell Textron Inc. announced during[Farnborough International Airshow 2026](https://news.bellflight.com/en-US/268110-mv-75-cheyenne-takes-center-stage-at-farnborough-international-airshow/) that it received a signed purchase agreement for an instrument flight rules (IFR)-configured[Bell 407GXi](https://www.bellflight.com/products/bell-407) , marking the first time that the platform with an IFR configuration kit will be operated by a customer in the United Kingdom.


“Our team is honored to celebrate this signing and expand our Bell[407GXi](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/bell/407gxi) offerings to customers in the United Kingdom,” said Robin Wendling, managing director, Europe, Bell.


“The Bell[407GXi](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/bell/407gxi) has earned its name as a multi-mission workhorse, and we continue to see steady interest in the Bell[407GXi](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/bell/407gxi) platform across the region.”


In addition to the aircraft order, Bell recently completed a demonstration tour with the platform in the United Kingdom, making stops at Lydd Airport, Goodwood, and High Wycombe.


“Combining its IFR capability with the platform’s latest Designer Series interior offering, the Bell[407GXi](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/bell/407gxi) provides customers with the ultimate VIP experience to suit their business needs,” added Wendling.


Making its[debut](https://news.bellflight.com/en-US/247699-bell-launches-new-designer-series-interior-for-the-bell-407gxi/) in 2025, Bell introduced its new luxury interior options for the Bell[407GXi](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/bell/407gxi) as part of its Designer Series – a series of offerings that set new standards for Bell interiors.


The[Bell 407GXi](https://www.bellflight.com/products/bell-407) allows operators to seamlessly perform in demanding environments while relying on its Garmin G1000H NXi Flight Deck, an all-glass avionic suite, as it relays critical safety information.


To find out more about how Bell aircraft advances missions worldwide, please visit the[Bell product page](https://www.bellflight.com/products) or visit the Bell pavilion (#A031) this week during[Farnborough International Airshow 2026](https://news.bellflight.com/en-US/268110-mv-75-cheyenne-takes-center-stage-at-farnborough-international-airshow/) .
