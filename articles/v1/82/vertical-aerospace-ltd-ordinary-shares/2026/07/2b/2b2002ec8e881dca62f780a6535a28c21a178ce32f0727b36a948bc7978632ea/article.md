# AEM grows sales force in Middle East and North Africa

Canadian special-mission avionics manufacturer, AEM, is growing its sales team into the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) by welcoming experienced aviation professional Sachin Suramwar as sales and business development representative.


Suramwar will represent AEM in the region to better support its customers and help grow adoption of its mission critical solutions.


“We are really impressed by Sachin, and his track record of supporting operators and public service agencies in the region speaks for itself,” said Tony Weller, AEM’s sales and marketing director.


“His experience representing complimentary product lines and his background position him well to effectively support the unique requirements and needs of our customers.”


Based in Dubai, UAE, Suramwar brings more than 20 years of airline maintenance, repair and overhaul and rotary-wing experience, supporting operators, original equipment manufacturers, and government agencies across search-and-rescue, helicopter emergency medical services, and defense sectors.


“I’m looking forward to educating the market on AEM’s special mission communication products and facilitating the installation of their suite of products with our regional partners throughout MENA,” said Suramwar.


“I see tremendous and immediate opportunity to improve operators’ fleets with AEM’s AS350 consoles, their digital audio system and loudspeaker systems.”
