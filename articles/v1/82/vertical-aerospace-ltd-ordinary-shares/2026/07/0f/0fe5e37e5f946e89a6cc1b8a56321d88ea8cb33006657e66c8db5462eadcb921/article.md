# Loft Dynamics launches Airbus H145 multi-pilot VR simulator, Babcock commits

[Loft Dynamics](http://www.loftdynamics.com/) has announced the release of what it said is the world’s first Airbus[H145](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h145) Multi-Pilot VR Simulator.


Now commercially available to operators and training organizations worldwide, the simulator allows[H145](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h145) pilots and crews to train together in VR for the first time, expanding access to advanced multi-crew training on one of the world’s most widely used helicopter platforms.


[Babcock France,](https://www.babcockinternational.com/) the country’s largest helicopter emergency medical services (HEMS) operator, is one of the first operators to commit to the newly released platform. The company signed a letter of intent at the Farnborough International Airshow for the planned deployment of France’s first[H145](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h145) VR simulator, which would give[H145](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h145) -qualified Babcock pilots greater access to frequent, mission-focused training.


“The aviation industry faces a growing mismatch: demand for highly trained crews is rising, but access to advanced helicopter simulation remains limited,” said Sebastien Borel, CEO of Loft Dynamics.


“The[H145](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h145) supports a large global community flying some of aviation’s most demanding missions, yet operators have never had a way to train entire crews together in virtual reality. Our[H145](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h145) Multi-Pilot VR Simulator changes that by making realistic, aircraft-specific multi-crew training accessible enough to become a regular part of how pilots and crews build proficiency. Babcock France’s early commitment carries real weight. It shows that immersive VR is moving beyond an emerging training option and becoming infrastructure that major operators can build into their long-term[H145](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h145) training strategies.”


“The[H145](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h145) multi-pilot VR simulator is a game-changer for operational safety,” said Romain Trapp, executive vice president, Customer Support and Services, at Airbus Helicopters.


“By bringing advanced, accessible training directly to the doorstep of major operators like Babcock, it allows crews to train together frequently and with total precision. We are thrilled to see our partners adopting this immersive technology to enhance crew coordination where it matters most.”


Developed in close collaboration with Airbus Helicopters, the simulator enables pilots and crew members to train together in a shared virtual environment for normal, abnormal and emergency procedures.


It is built using flight dynamics, systems data and helicopter software provided directly by Airbus Helicopters, and was rigorously tested and validated throughout development by Airbus pilots. The simulator replicates the aircraft’s aerodynamics and performance characteristics, allowing operators to train using the same procedures they follow during flight operations.


The system combines a six-degrees-of-freedom full-motion platform, a virtual, full-scale cockpit replica and a 360-degree panoramic field of view to create a highly realistic training experience.


It also supports repeated training on complex scenarios that would be difficult, costly or impractical to conduct in an aircraft, including license-related training such as initial type ratings, multi-crew cooperation and instrument training, as well as recurrent training and checking for[H145](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h145) operators.


Babcock France plans to use the simulator across its[H145](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h145) training program, with a strong focus on mission rehearsal and specialized HEMS and search-and-rescue operations. The company also intends to make simulator capacity available to operators and pilots across France and neighboring countries, helping address the growing shortage of[H145](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h145) simulator access in the region.


“Our mission is to ensure our crews are fully prepared for the complex and critical operations they perform every day,” said Pierre Basquin, chief executive, Aviation, and CEO of Babcock France.


“Loft Dynamics’[H145](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h145) Multi-Pilot VR Simulator will significantly enhance our training capabilities by enabling more frequent, realistic and collaborative training for pilots and technical crew members. It will strengthen operational readiness, support the highest standards of safety and performance, and expand access to advanced[H145](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h145) training for operators across France and the region.”


Babcock France operates Airbus[H145](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h145) helicopters and has more than 60 pilots qualified on the type. From 21 HEMS bases across France, its ambulance-configured helicopter fleet provides essential air medical support to hospitals and emergency response networks, completing more than 11,000 missions annually and ensuring patients receive critical care as quickly as possible.
