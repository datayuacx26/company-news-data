# Aerolite receives FAA STC for H145 air medical interior

Aerolite has received a Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) supplemental type certificate (STC) for its lightweight air medical interior for the Airbus[H145](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h145) D3 helicopter.


The interior builds on Aerolite’s European Aviation Safety Agency (EASA)- and Transport Canada Civil Aviation (TCCA)-certified[H145](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h145) designs and features modular layouts for a variety of medical transport missions.


It includes flexible medical equipment placement, enhanced patient safety features, improved crew access, a no-lift roll-in stretcher, and a certified pack rack that allows devices to remain attached during takeoff and landing.


Developed with input from U.S. operators, the updated interior incorporates the latest medical equipment and safety considerations. Aerolite said the FAA STC allows it to offer the new interior configuration for the latest generation of[H145](https://helitrader.com/for-sale/helicopters/airbus/h145) helicopters.
