# How to Refine Textures on AI-Generated 3D Models from Meshy, Rodin, Tripo, Trellis, and Hunyuan

Text-to-3D and image-to-3D technologies have made remarkable advancements with tools like Meshy, Rodin, Tripo, Trellis, and Hunyuan. These platforms have revolutionized how quickly we can generate 3D models from simple prompts or reference images. However, despite these innovations, there remains a significant gap: the ability to refine and edit textures with precise creative control.
