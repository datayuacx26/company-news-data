# May '26 product updates

## Doppler On-prem is here


Organizations can now run Doppler entirely within customer-managed infrastructure while keeping the developer experience teams already love.


Built for regulated industries and security-sensitive environments, Doppler On-prem gives enterprises full control over how and where secrets are managed, without sacrificing usability or speed.


With Doppler On-prem, teams can:
• Keep secrets and backups inside their environment
• Meet strict compliance and data residency requirements
• Support isolated or restricted networks
• Simplify secrets management across services and environments


This launch brings modern secrets management to organizations where external SaaS tools aren’t an option.
