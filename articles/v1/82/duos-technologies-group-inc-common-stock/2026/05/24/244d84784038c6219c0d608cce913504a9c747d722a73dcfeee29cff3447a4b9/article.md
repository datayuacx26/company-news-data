# Duos Technologies to Participate at the 23rd Annual Craig-Hallum Institutional Investor Conference


May 21, 2026 8:30am EDT[Download as PDF](https://ir.duostechnologies.com/_assets/_d327e787bebc204750fdf30f7dbd8b57/duostechnologies/news/2026-05-21_Duos_Technologies_to_Participate_at_the_23rd_841.pdf)


JACKSONVILLE, Fla., May 21, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Duos Technologies Group, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=djD1cpZ-_MhMmW3TnEWMeBRY1ltRfzlLlvCgDNDqFZddLULjDjrNGjtE0SDGOH38_0NiYjdkjG1nM-b0-nccAorSVf7zukXhdclttUUx5N_vlpX8mxkkzaZaDFliMzNx) (“Duos” or the “Company”) ([Nasdaq: DUOT](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=NMPOj6y2NTEOKpTku2SwUbqBY-9MkwmwAaNLUnCrm4TbdcjW3uubMU13Jo9hA4wDQreZDFJO0mKcxNpg1COcoYg9v9tdF4mniNRlLvLgXSk=) ), a provider of modular, colocation Edge and AI data centers and technology infrastructure solutions, today announced that Company management will be participating at the upcoming 23rd Annual Craig-Hallum Institutional Investor Conference being held on Thursday, May 28, 2026 at the Depot Renaissance Hotel in Minneapolis, MN.


Duos management will be available for one-on-one and small group meetings with institutional investors throughout the conference. Those interested in scheduling a meeting with Duos management should contact their Craig-Hallum representative or the investor relations contact listed below.


For more information, please visit the Craig-Hallum[conference website](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=DWwtfNts1zHKK04wwiGRk31I300uVI0tQJh0PTC2LSEHu6kFrHs6n6Ue4b6q0A4KvYphHYgt-i6fvj8zGDEugjglMMZNCBnxnn5rjG9TsnyhCWmHKpru71H4b0KeG26Z5eCP64Hfm59HVykGTKrG6w==) .


**About Duos Technologies Group, Inc.** Duos Technologies Group, Inc. ([Nasdaq: DUOT](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=NMPOj6y2NTEOKpTku2SwUelpnOFSXdFYFVWJCUsCvN4oktc_BlaLbFSi3ucBS34ft5nkI8bTePTzauhdbbYCTJbs-BdQA5QxTSo2FZdtEZA=) ), based in Jacksonville, Florida, is focused on providing and managing modular data center colocation facilities and infrastructure solutions. Duos is strategically focused on scaling its edge data center platforms in conjunction with its data center infrastructure solutions business.
