# Duos Technologies Group Closes $55M Registered Direct Offering


June 17, 2026 8:45am EDT[Download as PDF](https://ir.duostechnologies.com/_assets/_d327e787bebc204750fdf30f7dbd8b57/duostechnologies/news/2026-06-17_Duos_Technologies_Group_Closes_55M_Registered_845.pdf)


JACKSONVILLE, Fla., June 17, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Duos Technologies Group, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=8aZotEI2IPO39jhCfCezm3TS5Rn9TkLLHJIDbakhe2qB_u7WXXvh9KxL_s7qEDBN-vo8_Pa4hIUt9CjMNyh5_YX7oqP0DqjsVK8Ba3xv1j0I-6uQcUbwY4EnJLaYqRTyzKFjRwJ3B-Fg9wCJ4jnzFwjB59M3lLoMHTawLgCJk4YKwLTbFAv1y1m33ZKZsPzypHQ0KuCUgAs-GpNnJg8fkhZtssHic3p-qeL8U60-0buR80IPs7jt-rLriH0uMvaaVcbTMsnQXPWKZrSGvoVy5hzjzJNZzri9N1DI8S_Hwj_JYkW-5UT9ZkSNqeMZsE6g) (“Duos” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: DUOT), a leading provider of adaptive, modular, and scalable Edge Data Center (“EDC”) solutions, today announced an underwritten registered direct offering of 2,000,000 shares of common stock and 3,800,000 pre-funded warrants at a price of $9.50 per share or warrant for total gross proceeds of approximately $55 million, before deducting underwriting discounts, commissions, and offering expenses.


The registered direct offering was completed by a single large institutional investor, to support the Company's continued growth plans. The net proceeds from the offering will be used to expand, accelerate, and further commercialize the Company’s Edge Data Center business and for working capital and general corporate purposes.


TD Cowen acted as lead bookrunner on the transaction. Cantor acted as joint bookrunner on the transaction.


The public offering was made pursuant to a shelf registration statement on Form S-3 (File No. 333-293372) filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”) on February 11, 2026, and declared effective by the SEC on February 12, 2026. The public offering was made only by means of a final prospectus supplement and the accompanying base prospectus that form a part of the registration statement.
