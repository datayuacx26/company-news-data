# MillerKnoll Earns Great Place to Work® Certification, Highlighting Positive Employee Experience

ZEELAND, Mich., August 13, 2026 -- MillerKnoll (NASDAQ: MLKN), a global design company, announces it has been Great Place to Work® Certified™ in the United States for 2026-2027, marking its third consecutive year earning the certification.


The honor establishes MillerKnoll as a company committed to fostering a strong employee experience and a workplace culture grounded in collaboration, innovation, and trust.


Since first earning certification, MillerKnoll has increased its Great Place to Work® score across all categories. This underscores the company’s growth within five essential dimensions of employee experience: credibility, respect, fairness, pride, and camaraderie.


Across its global network of brands, MillerKnoll focuses on programs that strengthen culture, build connections, and develop leadership skills. Employees can cultivate relationships and gain experience through a variety of programs such as:


- Global Day of Purpose: a day off granted annually for employees to give back to their communities
- MillerKnoll Foundation's Global Ambassador program: a network of employees who help lead volunteer initiatives and drive local impact
- Business Resource Groups: employee-led committees centered on shared interests and experiences to help promote a positive culture
- Bridge and Propel programs: supports manufacturing employees building skills for future leadership roles
- Accelerate program: an initiative designed to foster well-rounded leaders through shared learning


MillerKnoll’s inspiring network of showrooms, stores, offices, and manufacturing facilities further support connection among employees, giving them thoughtfully designed spaces to collaborate and innovate. The company has also demonstrated its strength in cultivating a workplace where people choose to build long-terms careers. Through its Watercarrier program, MillerKnoll honors employees with 20 or more years of service with more than 2,000 Watercarriers recognized to date.


The certification follows recent recognitions for MillerKnoll’s workplace culture from organizations including Forbes, Fortune, Newsweek, and TIME. More on recent awards the company has won can be found on[MillerKnoll Awards](https://www.millerknoll.com/about/awards) .


Great Place to Work® is the global authority on workplace culture and employee experience. Certification is based on validated employee feedback collected through surveys administered by the Great Place to Work Institute to a representative sample of U.S.-based employees.


A summary of the company’s results can be found on[MillerKnoll’s Great Place to Work profile](https://www.greatplacetowork.com/certified-company/7053712) .


**About MillerKnoll**
MillerKnoll is a global collective of design brands built on the foundation of two icons of modernism: Herman Miller and Knoll. The portfolio also includes furniture and accessories for commercial and residential spaces from Colebrook Bosson Saunders, DWR (Design Within Reach), Edelman, Geiger, HAY, HOLLY HUNT, Knoll Textiles, Maharam, Muuto, NaughtOne, and Spinneybeck | FilzFelt. Guided by a shared purpose—design for the good of humankind—MillerKnoll generates insights, pioneers innovations, and champions ideas to better align spaces with how people live, work, and gather. In fiscal year 2026, the company generated net sales of $3.8 billion. For more information, visit millerknoll.com.
