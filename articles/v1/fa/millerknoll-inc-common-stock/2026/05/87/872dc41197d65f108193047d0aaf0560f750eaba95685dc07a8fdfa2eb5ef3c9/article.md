# Colebrook Bosson Saunders is Back at Clerkenwell Design Week — and the Ice Cream Van is Coming Too

***London, May 2026*** *--* Great news for design lovers (and ice cream lovers):[Colebrook Bosson Saunders](https://www.colebrookbossonsaunders.com/?srsltid=AfmBOooPUdpkVtxdEpPnDVxyCyY1e7S1eQwkawA7v7zq2uarF6KBwxkp) (CBS) is heading back to Clerkenwell Design Week 2026 (19–21 May), and we're bringing the fun with us.


For the second year in a row, our iconic ice cream van will be parked up in St John's Square for the full three days of CDW, because what better way to enjoy the best week in the design calendar than with a scoop in hand? Come say hello, grab a treat, and soak up the Clerkenwell atmosphere.


And when you're ready to get serious, head over to The Sans, where Colebrook Bosson Saunders will be showcasing our full product portfolio across the MillerKnoll spaces.


**Come find us:**


Ice cream van — St John's Square, daily 19–21 May


Product showcase — MillerKnoll at The Sans, CDW 2026


**About Colebrook Bosson Saunders**


Established in 1990 and part of the MillerKnoll collective since 2022, Colebrook Bosson Saunders focuses on seamlessly connecting people with technology, driven by its core values: to innovate, connect, and create.
