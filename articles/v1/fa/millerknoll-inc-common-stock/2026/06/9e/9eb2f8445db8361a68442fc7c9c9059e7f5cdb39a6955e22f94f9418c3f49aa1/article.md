# Spinneybeck Introduces Søuld Fragments: A New Acoustic Wall Tile Crafted from Danish Eelgrass

June 25, 2026—Expanding its collection of proudcts made from eelgrass, Spinneybeck is thrilled to introduce Søuld Fragments. Søuld Fragments is a modular wall tile crafted from eelgrass, a marine plant grown along the shores of Denmark. Eelgrass loses its leaves as part of its annual life cycle and is collected after it washes ashore and, through a fully circular manufacturing process, is pressed into durable sheets to be used as building materials.


True to its name, Søuld Fragments is produced from offcuts generated from the production of eelgrass sheets, giving a second life to an already environmentally sustainable resource. Smaller pieces and cuttings are reassembled into a refined design that celebrates the inherent beauty, variation, and texture of the natural material.


Beyond its visual impact, the natural texture and dimensional surface of these tiles deliver exceptional acoustic performance, achieving a Noise Reduction Coefficient rating of 0.70. Søuld Fragments helps soften sound and creates a more comfortable and welcoming environment across a broad range of spaces, from hospitality and workplace interiors to residential settings.


Each tile features soft rounded edges complemented by precise chamfer details, creating subtle dimension, shadow, and visual rhythm across installations.


“At Søuld, we believe that the end-use must be considered to honor the story of eelgrass fully. That is why we are making products that can have a long life, be recycled, and be part of systems designed for repair, disassembly, and reuse,” shared Tobias Øhrstrøm, founder of Søuld.


Learn more about Søuld Fragments at[spinneybeck.com/sould-fragments.](https://www.spinneybeck.com/news/view/a-sustainable-balance-of-form-and-function)


**About Søuld**


Founded by architects Tobias Øhrstrøm and Pi Fabrin, sustainable design engineers Gunnar Agerskov and Kirsten Lynge, and eelgrass thatcher Henning Johansen, Søuld is a Danish manufacturer that provides designers, architects, and contractors with sustainable and recyclable products made from eelgrass. Søuld was founded in 2010 with a mission to offer natural and eco-friendly alternatives to traditional building materials and carry on a centuries old practice deeply rooted in Danish culture.
