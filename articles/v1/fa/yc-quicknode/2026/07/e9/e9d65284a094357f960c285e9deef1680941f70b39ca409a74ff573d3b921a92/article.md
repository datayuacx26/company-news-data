Introducing Quicknode Earn: Morpho yield, on autopilot


Your USDC should never earn less than it could. Quicknode Earn moves it into the top-paying Morpho vaults across 7 chains and rebalances automatically. Non-custodial, audited, your keys always.

Your money should never sit still earning less than it could. But that's exactly what happens: you pick a good vault, rates drift, and months later you're earning yesterday's rate. Or your USDC sits idle because comparing chains and vaults never reaches the top of your list. Lending rates move constantly and don't line up across chains, and nobody refreshes that many dashboards around the clock. The gap isn't about discipline. It's structural. Taking that kind of complexity off people's plates is what Quicknode does, so we built Earn to close it.


## **What Quicknode Earn does**


Quicknode Earn is a non-custodial USDC yield optimizer. It constantly scores every eligible Morpho vault across 7 chains and moves your USDC vault deposits to the best rate that clears the rules you set. Moves across chains travel as native USDC through Circle's CCTP V2, with nothing wrapped or synthetic in between. Your vault shares mint straight to your wallet, and the rebalancer can only move funds between approved vaults or back to you.

### **Three things that set Earn apart.**


**Non-custodial by architecture.** Your vault shares live in your wallet from the moment you deposit. If Quicknode's services ever went dark, your vault shares remain yours, and you can close your position directly on Morpho. No permission from us, ever.


**A gas-cost fee, never a cut of your yield.** Earn charges a[transparent fee](https://earn.quicknode.com/docs/how-it-works/fees-and-gas) tied to the network cost of each rebalance, never a percentage of what you earn. No deposit fee, no fee to hold your position, no fee to leave.


**Audited by OpenZeppelin.**[OpenZeppelin](https://www.openzeppelin.com/about-us) , a leading smart-contract security firm, audited the contracts behind Earn. You can[read the full audit report](https://github.com/quiknode-labs/earn-smart-contracts/blob/main/audits/OpenZeppelin_Audit.pdf) before you deposit.
