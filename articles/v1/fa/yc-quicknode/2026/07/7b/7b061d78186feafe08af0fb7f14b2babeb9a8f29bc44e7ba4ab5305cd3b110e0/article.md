## What Is Robinhood Chain?


Robinhood Chain is a permissionless, EVM-compatible Ethereum Layer 2 built on the Arbitrum Orbit stack. It settles to Ethereum mainnet and uses Ethereum blobs (EIP-4844) for data availability, inheriting Ethereum's security model while delivering the throughput and speed financial applications need.


Unlike general-purpose L2s, Robinhood Chain is explicitly designed for regulated financial use cases. Robinhood offers Stock Tokens like NVDA, GOOG, APPL and MSFT that you can[read directly onchain](https://www.quicknode.com/guides/robinhood/read-stock-tokens-data-onchain) . The architecture supports compliance-first development through a partnership with TRM Labs for transaction monitoring.
