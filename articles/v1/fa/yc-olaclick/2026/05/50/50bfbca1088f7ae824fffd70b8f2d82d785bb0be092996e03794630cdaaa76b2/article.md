If your restaurant uses GloriaFood to receive online orders, you need to act now. **GloriaFood is shutting down on April 30, 2027.** Oracle, which acquired GloriaFood in 2021, has decided to permanently close the platform — including all customer accounts and the partner program.


This isn’t a migration or an upgrade. GloriaFood is closing for good.


## Why is GloriaFood shutting down?


Oracle acquired GloriaFood in 2021 as part of its restaurant technology portfolio. After several years of operation under Oracle’s corporate structure, the company decided to discontinue the platform entirely. The decision affects thousands of restaurants across more than 50 countries — including the US, UK, Australia, Canada, and all of Latin America — that relied on GloriaFood as their commission-free online ordering solution.


## What does GloriaFood closing mean for your restaurant?


When GloriaFood shuts down on April 30, 2027, you will lose:


- **Your entire menu setup** — all products, photos, prices and categories
- **Your order history** — sales data, frequent customers, best-sellers
- **Your menu link** — the URL you shared with customers for months or years
- **Any website integrations** — if you have the GloriaFood widget embedded on your site
- **Your online ordering capability** — customers can no longer place orders through the platform


## The 3 things you need to do right now


### 1. Export everything from GloriaFood today


Download all your product photos in high resolution. Save your menu items, prices, descriptions and categories in a spreadsheet. Export your delivery zones, fees and schedule settings. Do this before you do anything else — this data is yours and you need it to set up your new platform.


### 2. Choose your GloriaFood replacement


Not all alternatives are equal. When evaluating your options, look for:


- **No commissions per order** — GloriaFood charged nothing per order; your replacement should do the same
- **Easy setup** — you shouldn’t need a developer to configure your menu
- **WhatsApp integration** — especially important for LATAM markets
- **Financial stability** — you can’t afford to go through this again in two years
