# 600 Team Members Volunteer For Annual Sprouts Day Of Service

Over 600 volunteers rolled up their sleeves to help with the 2025 annual Day of Service, completing more than
50 projects coast-to-coast. From revitalizing community gardens to packing food boxes, our Sprouts team
members came together to make a meaningful impact in the communities we call home.
