# From Farm to Table: Middle Country Public Library and the Foundation Bring Healthy Habits to Life

Inside Middle Country Public Library on New York’s Long Island is a mini, pretend Sprouts
Farmers Market where kids can get a feel for what “farm-to-table” actually means. The hands-on
play-learning exhibit allows kids to harvest produce, shop at the miniature market and then cook
what they bought, learning healthy habits that will serve them throughout their lifetimes.
