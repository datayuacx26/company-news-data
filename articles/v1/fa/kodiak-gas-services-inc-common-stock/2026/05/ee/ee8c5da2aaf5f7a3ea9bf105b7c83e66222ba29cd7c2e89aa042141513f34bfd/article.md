**About Kodiak**


Kodiak is a leading contract compression, distributed power, and energy infrastructure services provider in the United States. The Company serves as a critical link in the infrastructure chain that enables the safe, reliable, and efficient production of energy. Headquartered in The Woodlands, Texas, Kodiak provides contract compression, distributed power, and related services to oil and gas producers, midstream customers, and digital infrastructure operators.
