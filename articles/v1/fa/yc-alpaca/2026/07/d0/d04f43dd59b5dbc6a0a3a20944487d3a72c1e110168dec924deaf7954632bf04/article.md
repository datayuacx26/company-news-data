# Alpaca and Vacuumlabs Collaborate to Accelerate Time-to-Market for European Partners

[Vacuumlabs](https://vacuumlabs.com/?ref=alpaca.markets) , a fintech product development company specializing in wealth management and banking, today announced a strategic partnership with Alpaca, making Vacuumlabs an official integration partner in Europe.


Through this partnership, Alpaca powers the regulated brokerage infrastructure and embeddable APIs providing access to financial services, while Vacuumlabs leads the front-end integration and build, including onboarding, personalization, KYC, and any legacy modernization needed to reach a production-ready platform. This means our partners gain a coordinated approach from strategy through implementation, helping simplify infrastructure evaluation and integration.


Together, Alpaca and Vacuumlabs provide:


- An integrated approach from strategy through implementation
- End-to-end integration support across product, design, engineering, and AI
- Designed to help streamline implementation workflows
- Integration services designed for fintechs and broker partners across Europe


"Many wealth product launches stall when nobody owns the full path from infrastructure to something customers will enjoy using,” said[Tomas Masek, CEO of Vacuumlabs](https://www.linkedin.com/in/masektomas/?ref=alpaca.markets) . “Firms end up stitching together vendors, and the integration work between them is where we see months disappear. Becoming Alpaca's first official integration partner in the EU means we can close that gap. One team, one accountable path, from Alpaca's brokerage infrastructure to a production-ready product."


"Europe is a priority market for Alpaca, and we're scaling our regional presence to match that ambition,” said[Karan Shanmugarajah, CEO of Alpaca Europe](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kshanm/?ref=alpaca.markets) . “By collaborating with Vacuumlabs, we’re empowering partners to launch the next generation of wealth and investment products across Europe more quickly."
