# Alpaca Named Among Top 100 Most Promising Fintech Startups by CB Insights

New York, NY – October 23, 2025 –[CB Insights](http://www.cbinsights.com/?ref=alpaca.markets) today named[Alpaca](https://alpaca.markets/?ref=alpaca.markets) , a global leader in brokerage infrastructure APIs providing access to stocks, ETFs, options, fixed income, and crypto, to its Top 100 Most Promising Fintech Startups list in their eighth annual Fintech 100. The list recognizes startups at the forefront of financial innovation across embedded finance, blockchain, and wealth management.


Alpaca currently powers hundreds of partners across 40 countries, collectively serving over 7 million brokerage accounts with its developer-friendly[Broker API](https://alpaca.markets/broker?ref=alpaca.markets) . The company's inclusion highlights its role in building the global de facto standard for investing infrastructure.


Alpaca has recently unveiled multiple products and strategic initiatives, including their[Instant Tokenization Network](https://alpaca.markets/blog/us-stock-market-ready-for-instant-tokenization-with-alpacas-newly-launched-network/) ,[OmniSub technology](https://alpaca.markets/blog/introducing-omnisub-for-broker-api-alpacas-powerful-technology-for-sub-accounting/) *, support for[Shariah-compliant investing](https://alpaca.markets/blog/meeting-the-needs-of-the-gcc-how-zad-and-alpaca-are-collaborating-to-deliver-shariah-compliant-investment-solutions/) , and[Fixed Income Clearing Corporation (FICC), Options Clearing Corporation (OCC)](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-secures-occ-and-ficc-memberships-to-power-multi-asset-self-clearing-for-partners/) , and[Nasdaq memberships](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-secures-nasdaq-exchange-membership-advancing-full-stack-brokerage-infrastructure/) . These advancements lower barriers and empower entrepreneurs to build the next generation of financial products, furthering Alpaca’s mission to open financial services to everyone on the planet.


“We’re honored to be recognized by CB Insights as one of the 100 most promising fintech companies in the world,” says[Yoshi Yokokawa](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yoshiyokokawa/?ref=alpaca.markets) , CEO and Co-Founder at Alpaca. “By building comprehensive, full-stack investing infrastructure APIs, we empower hundreds of global fintechs to provide broader access to financial services. This recognition is a testament to our team’s dedication and the trust of our investors and partners.”
