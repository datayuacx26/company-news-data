# Alpaca Recognized as a 2025 Inc. Power Partner Award Winner

Alpaca, a global leader in brokerage infrastructure APIs providing access to stocks, ETFs, options, fixed income, and crypto, has been recognized as a[2025 Inc. Power Partner](https://www.inc.com/power-partner-awards?ref=alpaca.markets) . The prestigious list recognizes B2B organizations with proven track records of supporting entrepreneurs and helping startups grow.


Alpaca’s[Broker API](https://alpaca.markets/broker?ref=alpaca.markets) currently powers hundreds of partners across 40 countries, including ambitious early-stage startups and some of the world’s fastest growing fintechs, collectively serving over 7 million brokerage accounts.


The company’s recognition as a 2025 Inc Power Partner reflects its commitment to continuously delivering solutions that enable its global partners to innovate and scale rapidly. Recent product launches and strategic initiatives, including[Alpaca’s Instant Tokenization Network](https://alpaca.markets/blog/us-stock-market-ready-for-instant-tokenization-with-alpacas-newly-launched-network/) ,[OmniSub technology](https://alpaca.markets/blog/introducing-omnisub-for-broker-api-alpacas-powerful-technology-for-sub-accounting/) ,[support for Shariah-compliant investing](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-and-zad-partner-to-bring-shariah-compliant-investing-globally/) , and[Fixed Income Clearing Corporation (FICC), Options Clearing Corporation (OCC)](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-secures-occ-and-ficc-memberships-to-power-multi-asset-self-clearing-for-partners/) , and[Nasdaq memberships](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-secures-nasdaq-exchange-membership-advancing-full-stack-brokerage-infrastructure/) , demonstrate their continued support for partner growth.


“The entrepreneurial journey and community are core to Inc.’s mission, and it’s a true honor to celebrate this year’s Inc. Power Partners — the companies dedicated to helping small businesses and entrepreneurs,” says **** Bonny Ghosh, editorial director at Inc. “Whether they’re coordinating complex marketing campaigns or reliably supporting the day-to-day infrastructure of growing companies, these honorees aren’t simply B2B providers — they are true partners in helping businesses grow and succeed.”


“As entrepreneurs ourselves, we’re honored to be recognized by Inc. as a Power Partner,” says Yoshi Yokokawa, Co-Founder & CEO at Alpaca. “We’ve built Alpaca to empower other entrepreneurs around the world to launch and scale the next generation of financial products. This recognition reflects the trust we’ve built with our partners and our continued commitment to making financial services accessible to everyone on the planet.”


View the[complete list of 2025 Inc. Power Partner honorees](https://www.inc.com/power-partner-awards?ref=alpaca.markets) .
