# Alpaca Wins "FinTech of the Year" at the 2025 US FinTech Awards

Alpaca, a global leader in tokenization and brokerage infrastructure APIs providing access to crypto, stocks, ETFs, options, and fixed income, is proud to share that it has been recognized as the **FinTech of the Year** at the[2025 US FinTech Awards](https://usfintechawards.com/?ref=alpaca.markets) . The prestigious event, hosted by[FinTech Intel](https://fintech-intel.com/?ref=alpaca.markets) , celebrates the achievements of the country's best fintechs.


Alpaca has unveiled multiple products and strategic initiatives in 2025, including their[Instant Tokenization Network](https://alpaca.markets/blog/us-stock-market-ready-for-instant-tokenization-with-alpacas-newly-launched-network/) ,[OmniSub technology](https://alpaca.markets/blog/introducing-omnisub-for-broker-api-alpacas-powerful-technology-for-sub-accounting/) ,[support for Shariah-compliant investing](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-and-zad-partner-to-bring-shariah-compliant-investing-globally/) ,[24/5 Trading](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-launches-24-5-trading-for-broker-api-helping-deliver-global-access-to-us-markets/) , and[Fully Paid Securities Lending](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-launches-fully-paid-securities-lending-for-broker-api/) . Furthermore, Alpaca achieved key industry certifications, including[Fixed Income Clearing Corporation (FICC), Options Clearing Corporation (OCC)](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-secures-occ-and-ficc-memberships-to-power-multi-asset-self-clearing-for-partners/) , and[Nasdaq memberships](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-secures-nasdaq-exchange-membership-advancing-full-stack-brokerage-infrastructure/) . These advancements help lower barriers, empower entrepreneurs to build the next generation of financial products, and help make retail investing more accessible for global traders.


Members of the Alpaca team were in attendance to accept the "FinTech of the Year" Award


Alpaca also has several partnerships with fast-growing US-based financial institutions including[Kraken](https://alpaca.markets/blog/kraken-offers-us-securities-through-strategic-partnership-with-alpaca/) ,[Ondo Finance](https://alpaca.markets/blog/ondo-finance-and-alpaca-collaborate-to-tokenize-us-stocks-and-etfs/) ,[Composer](https://alpaca.markets/blog/how-composer-is-redefining-algorithmic-trading-with-their-no-code-platform/) , and more. Collectively, Alpaca powers hundreds of partners across 40 countries and serves over 7 million brokerage accounts between its developer-friendly Broker API and Trading API.


“We’re honored to be recognized as the FinTech of the Year at the US FinTech Awards 2025. The win highlights our efforts in building comprehensive, full-stack investing infrastructure APIs that empower hundreds of global fintechs and retail investors to access financial services. This award is a testament to our team’s dedication and the trust of our investors and partners as we continue to open financial services to everyone on the planet,” says[Yoshi Yokokawa](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yoshiyokokawa/?ref=alpaca.markets) , CEO of Alpaca.


Alpaca was also recently recognized as a[Top 100 Most Promising Fintech Startup by CB Insights](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-named-among-top-100-most-promising-fintech-startups-by-cb-insights/) and[Inc. Power Partner Award Winner](https://alpaca.markets/blog/alpaca-recognized-as-a-2025-inc-power-partner-award-winner/) .
