# Leading Wholesale and Tech-Driven Cannabis Platform, Nabis, Officially Launches in New York

**Pioneering Platform to Increase Access and Streamline Orders, Payments, Data, and Compliance Across the State’s Market; Projected to Hit $3.2B by 2030**


NEW YORK--([BUSINESS WIRE](https://www.businesswire.com/) )--[Nabis](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.nabis.com%2F&esheet=54154851&newsitemid=20241119037269&lan=en-US&anchor=Nabis&index=1&md5=b154f33f388110127c1a0f3e6ee70518) , the nation’s leading licensed cannabis wholesale platform, today announces its launch in the New York cannabis market, officially marking its status as a bicoastal operator. The launch brings Nabis’ best-in-class wholesaling technology that provides rapid order fulfillment, payment processing, customer service, and compliance to New York’s burgeoning market, with two storage facilities strategically located in the Bronx and Rochester to provide 2-day shipping turnaround times in nearly all regions across the state.


“Expanding to New York is about more than moving into a new market — by planting our roots on the East Coast, we’re laying the groundwork for our next chapter of building the national wholesaling infrastructure for brands and retailers across America,” said Vince C. Ning, CEO and Co-Founder of Nabis. “Our commitments to efficiency, compliance and customer satisfaction are unwavering, and we are eager and proud to foster strong relationships and support the growth of the country’s biggest up-and-coming cannabis market.”


New York’s cultural and global financial influence offers a unique opportunity for cannabis brands and operators to leverage a burgeoning market and diverse consumer audience—including 20 million New Yorkers and 60 million unique tourists each year—to continue destigmatizing cannabis on a global stage. Nabis’ innovative, one-stop-shop platform streamlines the significant and often overwhelming consumer demand emerging markets face, all while helping brands and retailers efficiently scale. With cutting-edge resources such as wholesale ordering, inventory management, payment processing, and sales metrics and analytics, Nabis’ proprietary technology centralizes the entire cannabis wholesaling experience for New York’s rapidly growing market.


“In the cannabis industry, trusted collaborations are essential for navigating complex markets and driving sustainable growth,” said Mack Hueber, President of Ayrloom. “We’re thrilled to work with Nabis, whose reliability and expertise in distribution, particularly in downstate regions, enable us to scale efficiently while maintaining exceptional product value.”


As the only platform in New York that has invested in dedicated cannabis distribution facilities, Nabis will serve nearly 10% of the state market by end of year, with capacity built for the market’s imminent and rapid scale. The structure in Rochester—a repurposed Kodak manufacturing facility—is upwards of 30,000 square feet, while the Bronx warehouse measures nearly 10,000 square feet. With the opening of these warehouses Nabis brings upwards of 100 new job opportunities to the state’s cannabis industry.


With the two warehouses strategically positioned on disparate ends of the state, Nabis is able to streamline and enhance New York’s supply chain efficiencies to create a more cost-effective and agnostic distribution network. This ensures timely and reliable deliveries of product to New York dispensaries and retailers across the state, increasing consumer access throughout the densely populated market.
