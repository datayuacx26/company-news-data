# Puff is the California Cannabis Brand Built for Repeat Customers

When[Puff](https://app.getnabis.com/marketplace/brand/lbs-distribution-llc) entered New York, a new market, reorder calls came in within days. Inventory sold through faster than anyone had forecast, not because of a hype campaign, but because the product performed.


Puff is a California-born cannabis brand founded in 2017 by[Ross Haley](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rosshaley/) and headquartered in West Sacramento. Operating across California and New York, the brand was built around one operating principle: deliver a consistent product at a price that earns the next purchase.


## **Built for the Gap Between Hype and Commodity**


When Haley founded Puff in 2017, the California cannabis market was polarized. Premium craft brands commanded attention on one end. Undifferentiated commodity products filled the other. According to Haley, consumers in the middle had few options they could reliably return to.


Haley built Puff in that gap – with process, disciplined operations, and price-point positioning that could drive volume without sacrificing quality. The bet was that consistency would generate consumer trust.

## **How Puff Operates**


According to Haley, “We run Puff like an operating company, not a hobby brand. We’re KPI-driven and customer-obsessed. We solve problems fast, communicate clearly with partners, and back decisions with data and P&L reality.”


That internal discipline is what makes the external promise — same product, every time — possible to keep.


Manufacturing is process-driven by design. Potency, flavor, and experience are held to tight standards across every SKU so that retailers can set expectations and consumers can count on them. Haley describes the brand's values as accountability, speed, clear ownership, continuous improvement, and being easy to do business with.


In a market where supply chain friction and inconsistent fulfillment are common, operational reliability is a competitive differentiator.


## **The Product Line**


Puff's portfolio covers four categories: pre-rolls, infused pre-rolls, vapes, and flower.


Puff pre-rolls are designed for everyday consumers – accessible and consistent. Infused pre-rolls add potency for consumers seeking an elevated experience without leaving the brand's value range. Vapes are built for convenience, with hardware engineered not to fail mid-session and inputs clean enough to back that up. Flower anchors the portfolio as a core category.


‍


‍


Every product is designed to be scalable versus situational. Puff doesn't build limited releases around hype cycles. The goal is a shelf presence that moves steadily and comes back on the next order.


The sell-through story was one signal. Earn trust through performance, then scale.


Haley put it plainly: "Puff was built for repeat customers. If we deliver consistency and value, trust follows — and the brand grows."


## **What's Next for Puff**


Puff's near-term focus is on distribution depth rather than geographic breadth. The team is working to expand retail coverage in its current markets, further tighten manufacturing processes, and bring new products to shelf that align with the brand's value framework. Collaborations are in development with partners who share the same standards on quality and execution.


‍


‍


It seems Puff’s goal is to build a brand that outlasts trends — one reorder at a time.
