# Time Machine is a Playful Greenhouse Cannabis Brand for Adventurous Spirits

Some cannabis brands sell a lifestyle.[Time Machine](https://app.getnabis.com/marketplace/brand/pacific-stone) sells the trip.


Founded in 2022 and greenhouse-grown in Carpinteria by the Pacific Stone team, Time Machine is inspired by how cannabis connects people to history and playful experiences—offering a unique mental and physical journey.


## **Time Machine at a Glance**


**Founded:** 2022


**Location:** Carpinteria, California


**Markets:** California


**Products:** Greenhouse-grown flower pouches, 28-pack and single Fuel-Rod Pre-Rolls, infused smalls pouch (available Summer 2026)


## **Built on History, Designed for Now**


The brand name Time Machine was a sticky first idea (or “highdea").


"We had this idea of history and legacy, so it was Time Machine from day one," said Colin Graham, VP of Marketing.


Cannabis's long history, and how, for some, its consumption carries them to another state of consciousness, gave the brand something to build its personality around. Time Machine leans into that fact without taking itself too seriously.


"Our lightheartedness with the branding is all about that feel-good moment," Graham said. "Time Machine's goal is to offer a quality experience that's accessible to everyone."


Time Machine 7-count pre-roll tins of the Cheetah Piss strain


## **Greenhouse-Grown in Carpinteria**


Carpinteria is not an incidental location for Time Machine.


Tucked between the Santa Ynez Mountains and the Pacific coast in Santa Barbara County, the area offers one of the most stable growing climates in California. Temperatures are mild, humidity is low, and the light is consistent, making it one of the most naturally suited regions in the state for greenhouse cannabis cultivation.


Pair Carpinteria’s climate with Time Machine’s disciplined greenhouse operations, and you get a brand built on smooth smoke and bold flavor as a consistent promise to its customers.


"Our team's commitment to both the flower and the customer is unmatched," Graham said. "We always consider the customer and the value that we can give them with a top-quality product."


Time Machine 28-pack pre-roll jar of the wedding cake strain


## **The 28pk Fuel-Rod: A Format the Market Didn't Have**


Time Machine identified a market gap with its launch of the 28pk Fuel-Rod Pre-Roll jar, emphasizing an innovative format that defined its brand story in California.


The team loved the idea of abundance, and the market responded. The 28pk became one of the brand's strongest retail performers and a product customers recognized and returned to.


Other brands noticed. Brands across the California market adopted the format in the years that followed.


"It didn't take long for the market to see our success with this product, so naturally other brands followed suit," Graham said. "Imitation is the finest form of flattery, so we weren't bothered by it. Our commitment is to our customers and always innovating on their behalf."


The moment that still resonates for the team comes at the point of purchase: a customer opening a 28pk jar for the first time and seeing 28 pre-rolls lined up and ready. Their reaction is immediate. "Seeing their eyes light up always brings a smile to everyone's face," Graham said.


## **A Consumer Base as Diverse as the Lineup**


Time Machine's customers aren't a single type of person.


Graham describes a broad consumer base drawn to the brand's strain variety, multiple pack sizes, and consistent value. The flower pouch and pre-roll formats offer different entry points for different consumers, and the pricing helps customers feel confident about what they are spending.


"They are getting a quality of product that is frankly unmatched at its price," Graham said. "Time Machine's goal is to offer a quality experience that's accessible to everyone."


The flower pouches have continued to grow in consumer rankings across California, giving dispensary buyers a reliable SKU that performs consistently across customer segments and contributes to stronger basket sizes at retail.

## **What's Next: The Infused Line Returns**


This summer, Time Machine is relaunching its infused product line — and the format is different from what came before.


The original infused offering was a 7-pack of pre-rolls. The relaunch is a 14-gram pouch of cannabis smalls infused with THCa, designed to deliver extra potency and flavor in a format that fits alongside the brand's existing flower pouch lineup. The team has been developing it in line with current consumer expectations, and the result reflects both what Time Machine has learned about its customers and where the infused-product category is heading.


The cannabis community remains the brand's primary compass. Customer feedback and market trends shape what gets built next—and summer 2026 is the next stop on the timeline.

Time Machine is a California cannabis brand founded in 2022 and greenhouse-grown in Carpinteria. The brand produces greenhouse-grown flower and pre-rolls, including the 28pk Fuel-Rod Pre-Roll, and distributes across California through Nabis.
