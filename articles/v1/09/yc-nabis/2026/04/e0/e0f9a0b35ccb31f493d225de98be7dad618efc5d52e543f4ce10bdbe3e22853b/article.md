# Inside Pacific Stone: Decade of California Cannabis from a Family-Owned Carpinteria Greenhouse

Pacific Stone's greenhouse


[Pacific Stone](https://app.getnabis.com/marketplace/brand/pacific-stone) is a family-owned and legacy grower-operated cannabis brand founded in 2015 by Mike Palmer in Carpinteria, California, based on the belief that high-quality cannabis should be accessible and affordable for all consumers.


That belief has produced one of the most recognized and recommended brands in the California market.


Operating exclusively in California, Pacific Stone has become one of the state's most consistently budtender-recommended brands at dispensaries. Its catalog spans pre-rolls, blunts, and large-format flower, all designed to deliver quality at accessible price points.

## **Built by Growers, for the Market**


Pacific Stone was established to make high-quality cannabis affordable to everyone in California. By leveraging decades of legacy cultivation experience and disciplined greenhouse operations, the founders closed the gap between premium quality and accessible pricing, defining the brand around value, consistency, and quality for everyday consumers.


Pacific Stone GMO


Carpinteria is not an incidental location for Pacific Stone. Tucked between the Santa Ynez Mountains and the Pacific coast in Santa Barbara County, the area offers one of the most stable growing climates in California, with mild temperatures, low humidity, and consistent light, making it among the most naturally suited regions in the state for greenhouse cannabis cultivation.


"We maximize efficiency in every corner of our operation to ensure that our customers are met with an extremely high-quality product at an attractive price point," said Colin Graham, Pacific Stone vice president of marketing.


Colin Graham, Pacific Stone vice president of marketing


For Pacific Stone, efficiency means growing its cannabis in-house using advanced greenhouse techniques, then putting every batch through expert curing, trimming, and hand-finishing. The greenhouse model gives the team control over the growing environment while keeping costs, such as lighting, below those of pure indoor cultivation—a trade-off that is reflected directly in the consumer’s cost.


That control gets tested by something no production system can eliminate: the seasons. Growing greenhouse cannabis powered by natural light means Pacific Stone operates at the mercy of California's climate.


Summer and winter produce meaningfully different growing environments, and the flower responds accordingly. "Attention to detail and understanding the variances that weather provides means that we need to be actively reacting to the plants' needs," said Graham. "Ultimately, to maintain consistency, we need to be very flexible with how we treat the plant and make sure that we always put the plants' needs first."


## **Why Pacific Stone Is One of California's Most-Recommended Affordable Cannabis Brands**


Pacific Stone's portfolio is broad, but a few formats have defined its presence in the California market.


Strain selection has played an equally important role. Wedding Cake, Blue Dream, 805 Glue, Cereal Milk, and Purple Tangie have each built followings among California consumers. 805 Glue, in particular, carries the kind of regional specificity that signals genuine California roots.


### **Pacific Stone 14-Pack 7g Pre-Roll Tin**


The 14-pack 7g pre-roll tin is the brand's most popular product, and its development reflects Pacific Stone's operating philosophy. The format originally launched in a plastic case, which was functional but occasionally frustrating to open. As the product grew in popularity, the team redesigned the case, landing on the metal tin now on shelves. The change wasn't driven by aesthetics, "Our priority is giving customers the most value for their dollar, and it doesn't stop at just our flower," said Graham.


### **Pacific Stone Blunts**


Each blunt contains 1.75g of the same flower as Pacific Stone's pre-rolls, wrapped in hemp with a glass tip. The result is a product with more flower per unit, offering a shareable format and a more deliberate experience than a standard half-gram pre-roll. "Using quality materials such as a hemp wrap along with a glass tip, we elevate the experience to make it feel more special and unique," said Graham. As Graham put it, the two products serve the same customer in different moods.


### **Pacific Stone Big Bags**


Big Bags, available in 14g and 28g sizes, offer substantial quantities for daily users and deliver preferred strains at highly competitive pricing for California consumers.


Pacific Stone Purple Tangie


## **Word of Mouth as a Business Model**


According to Pacific Stone, it is among the first brands California dispensary staff recommend to first-time cannabis consumers. The pattern has recurred across the state long enough to become nearly institutionalized. When someone doesn't know where to start, they're pointed to Pacific Stone.


"We find out in the field and speaking with cannabis consumers that almost everyone has a Pacific Stone story," said Colin Graham, VP Marketing. "Oftentimes, Pacific Stone is the first brand recommended to someone when they first walk into a dispensary or when they first share an experience with friends and family."


For a brand without celebrity partnerships, a large marketing budget, or a single-origin cultivation story, this type of organic reach comes from consistent performance. Quality must remain high for every batch, format, and price point, since the next recommendation depends on it.


For retailers, that dynamic has a practical implication. Pacific Stone is one of the few brands in the California market that reliably converts first-time buyers into repeat customers, with minimal lift required from floor staff. That makes it a low-friction SKU and a reliable baseline in any dispensary's core assortment.


## **What's Coming in 2026**


Pacific Stone is not resting on its catalog.


The brand is launching Diamond and Hash Infused 1g Pre-Roll 2-packs, a move into the infused pre-roll segment that brings its same value-forward approach to a higher-potency format. Infused pre-rolls have been among the[fastest-growing segments in California cannabis](https://mjbizdaily.com/news/infused-pre-rolls-ignite-californias-marijuana-market/404813/) , and Pacific Stone's entry brings that same value-forward approach to a higher-potency format.


Pacific Stone Blue Z


The blunt line is growing as well, with the addition of a 2g Hash Infused Blunt and a 2g Diamond Infused Blunt arriving in Spring 2026. Both extend the format that has earned Pacific Stone some of its most loyal repeat customers.


"We are inspired every day by our consumers, striving to give them a better experience every time they enjoy our products," said Colin Graham, VP Marketing.

**Is Pacific Stone cannabis good quality?**


Pacific Stone grows cannabis in-house at its Carpinteria greenhouse facility, with every batch hand-trimmed and expertly cured before packaging. The brand is consistently recommended by dispensary staff to first-time cannabis consumers in California and has maintained that reputation since 2015.

Pacific Stone was built by legacy growers specifically to close the gap between quality and affordability in the California market.
