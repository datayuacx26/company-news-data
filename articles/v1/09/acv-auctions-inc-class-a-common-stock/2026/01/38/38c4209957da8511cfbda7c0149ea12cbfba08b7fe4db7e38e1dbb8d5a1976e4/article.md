BUFFALO, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- ACV (NYSE: ACVA), the leading digital automotive marketplace and data services partner for dealers and commercial partners, announced today that the Company will report fourth quarter 2025 financial results after market close and host a conference call at 5:00 p.m. ET on Monday, February 23, 2026.


Investors and analysts interested in participating in the call are invited to dial 877-704-4453 (international callers please dial 1-201-389-0920) approximately 10 minutes prior to the start of the call.
