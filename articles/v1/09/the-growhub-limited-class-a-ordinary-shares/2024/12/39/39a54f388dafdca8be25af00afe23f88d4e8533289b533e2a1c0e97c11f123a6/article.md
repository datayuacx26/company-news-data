# Lester Chan and Daiki Nakaoka Visit to Strengthen Entrepreneurship Partnership

On November 29 (Friday), Lester Chan, CEO of The GrowHub Innovations Company, and Daiki Nakaoka, Representative Director of GrowHub Japan and an alumnus of this university, paid a courtesy visit to former President Kurozaka and current President Zaima. The GrowHub Innovations Company has signed a comprehensive partnership agreement with this university since last academic year, aimed at developing a global environment to cultivate students' entrepreneurship. During this courtesy visit, discussions were held to further strengthen collaboration, particularly in relation to the planned establishment of the "Entrepreneurship Program" set to open in April 2026, with a focus on advancing partnerships in areas related to "global business."
