# Sustainable Saskatchewan Reception hosted by the Honorable Jeremy Harrison, Minister of Trade and Export Development, the Government of Saskatchewan

# Sustainable Saskatchewan Reception hosted by the Honorable Jeremy Harrison, Minister of Trade and Export Development, the Government of Saskatchewan


Feb 8


Written By[Tech Admin GrowHub](https://thegrowhub.co/news?author=62dfa0f218b0af5ca6325224)


The Growhub Innovations Company attended the Sustainable Saskatchewan Reception yesterday evening held at Paradox Singapore Merchant Court. It is a privilege to be included in this “by invite only” event hosted by the Honorable Jeremy Harrison, Minister of Trade and Export Development, the Government of Saskatchewan, in cooperation with the Canadian Chamber of Commerce in Singapore. The event is aimed to feature insights from Minister Harrison about Saskatchewan's vision and its endeavors to strengthen ties with Southeast Asia. We were able to interact and connect with representatives from the Saskatchewan Singapore Trade and Investment Office, the Canadian Chamber of Commerce in Singapore, and leaders from government, academia, and business sectors in Singapore and the region.
