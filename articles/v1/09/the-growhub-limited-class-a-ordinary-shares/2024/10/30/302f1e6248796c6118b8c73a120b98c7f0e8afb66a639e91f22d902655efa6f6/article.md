# The GrowHub Joins Project We Care Seminar


Oct 7


Written By[Guest User](https://thegrowhub.co/news?author=696085f024d98b24c865f480)


The GrowHub started participating in Project We Care program by People's Association last year to give back to the society in the little ways that we could. It is the first time that the company is invited to the biennial Project We Care Seminar. It is heartening to engage representatives of like-minded companies of all sizes and to discuss how we can better serve the needy in our community.
