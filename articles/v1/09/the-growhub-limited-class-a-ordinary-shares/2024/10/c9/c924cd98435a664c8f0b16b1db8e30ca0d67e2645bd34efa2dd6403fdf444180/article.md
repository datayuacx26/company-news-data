# Mono.sg Hosts CSR Event on Sustainability and Regenerative Farming

# Mono.sg Hosts CSR Event on Sustainability and Regenerative Farming


Oct 11


Written By[Guest User](https://thegrowhub.co/news?author=696085f024d98b24c865f480)


As part of the company's CSR efforts, our Chief Administrative Officer,[Alec Ngo](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alec-ngo-567a4450/) shared about issues concerning sustainability and introduced the concept of regenerative farming to a group of students from[Hwa Chong Institution](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hwa-chong-institution/) visiting[Mono.sg](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mono-sg/) 's premises located at Tekka Place this morning.


Together with[Mono.sg](http://mono.sg/) , we hope to raise awareness of the food waste problem in Singapore and encourage wise consumption in our community.
