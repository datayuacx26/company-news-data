# SOLAI Limited Files 2025 Annual Report on Form 20-F

AKRON, Ohio


, April 20, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- SOLAI Limited (NYSE:[SLAI](https://solai.com/news-detail?id=201594#financial-modal) ) (previously traded under "BTCM") ("SOLAI" or the "Company"), a technology-driven personal AI and digital infrastructure provider, today announced that it filed its annual report on Form 20-F for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission ("SEC") on April 20, 2026. The annual report can be accessed on the SEC's website at[http://www.sec.gov](http://www.sec.gov/) , as well as on the Company's investor relations website at[https://ir.solai.com/](https://ir.solai.com/) .


The Company will provide a hard copy of its annual report containing the audited consolidated financial statements, free of charge, to its shareholders and ADS holders upon request. Requests should be directed to the Company's IR Department at[\[email protected\]](https://www.prnewswire.com/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#3c554e7c4f53505d55125f5351) .


**About SOLAI Limited**


SOLAI Limited (previously known as "BIT Mining Limited") (NYSE:[SLAI](https://ir.solai.com/news/pressDetail/196182#financial-modal) ) (previously traded under "BTCM"), is a technology-driven personal AI and digital infrastructure provider. Building upon its historical legacy in digital asset


mining and blockchain network operations, the Company is leveraging extensive experience in large-scale hardware deployment, data center operations, and high-performance computing to build the foundational infrastructure for personal and digital assets globally.
