# SOLAI Limited to Report Third Quarter 2025 Financial Results on November 21, 2025

AKRON, Ohio


, Nov. 19, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- SOLAI Limited (NYSE:SLAI ) ("SOLAI" or the "Company") (previously known as "BIT Mining Limited"), a leading technology-driven cryptocurrency


infrastructure company, today announced that it plans to release its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2025, after the U.S. market close on Friday, November 21, 2025.


**About SOLAI Limited**


SOLAI Limited (previously known as "BIT Mining Limited") (NYSE:SLAI ) (previously traded under "BTCM"), is a technology-driven cryptocurrency


infrastructure company expanding from its foundation in crypto


mining to build a blockchain-based ecosystem spanning AI, stablecoins and payment infrastructure, and Solana


treasury and staking operations — supporting use cases across institutional settlement, commerce, consumer payments, and AI-native agent transactions. By leveraging its blockchain and data infrastructure expertise, SOLAI aims to enhance on-chain efficiency and expand participation across Solana


and other blockchain ecosystems.
