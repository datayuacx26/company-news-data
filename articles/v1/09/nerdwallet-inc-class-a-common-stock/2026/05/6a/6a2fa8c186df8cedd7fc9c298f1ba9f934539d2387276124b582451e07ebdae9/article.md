# NerdWallet Announces Winners of Its 2026 Best-Of Awards for Mortgage Lenders

*NerdWallet Recognizes Top Mortgage Lenders to Help Borrowers Choose With Confidence*


SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--May 4, 2026--[NerdWallet, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.nerdwallet.com%2F%3Futm_campaign%3Dbr_mktg_paid_BOA2025%26utm_source%3Dpr%26utm_medium%3Dpressrelease&esheet=54526560&newsitemid=20260504582030&lan=en-US&anchor=NerdWallet%2C+Inc.&index=1&md5=c83d052890a2f9b78dbe080ebff76d9b) (Nasdaq: NRDS), which provides trustworthy financial guidance to consumers and small and mid-sized businesses (SMBs), today announced the winners of its 2026 Best-Of Awards for Mortgage Lenders.


This award recognizes the top mortgage lenders meeting the needs of home buyers and homeowners. To determine this year’s winners, seven NerdWallet mortgage experts with more than 30 years of combined experience evaluated 48 lenders across 12 categories. Three distinctive sources shaped every decision:


- The newly released federal dataset from the 2025 Home Mortgage Disclosure Act (published March 31, 2026)
- A detailed survey of lenders on loan products and customer service
- First-hand research by NerdWallet's editorial team into the borrower experience


New this year, NerdWallet introduced two new categories: Best Mortgage Lender for Manufactured Home Loans and Best Mortgage Lender for Second Home Loans.


See the full list of winners, methodology and to meet the NerdWallet editorial team behind the research, visit:[https://www.nerdwallet.com/l/awards-mortgages-2026](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.nerdwallet.com%2Fl%2Fawards-mortgages-2026&esheet=54526560&newsitemid=20260504582030&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.nerdwallet.com%2Fl%2Fawards-mortgages-2026&index=2&md5=9845a005261424a23515c981198e55c2)
