# Does Your Power Supply Measure Up?

### **SL Power Electronics Launches ‘Does Your Power Supply Measure Up’**


SL Power Electronics is pleased to announce the launch of ‘Does Your Power Supply Measure Up’. This program highlights the unparalleled capabilities of the SL Power Application & Support team and serves as a unique service for our clients.


OEM’s can now log on and fill out an application to initiate comparison testing between SL Power products and those of their competitors.


This service is free of charge and is intended to help OEM’s chose the right power solution for their end applications. After the SL Power Application team conducts the necessary testing, the user will receive a detail report highlighting the differences of key performance variables including: EMI, EMC, Holdup Time, Life, Thermal Performance, and more.


At SL Power, we pride ourselves in providing value added services to our clients that help them make more informed decisions.
