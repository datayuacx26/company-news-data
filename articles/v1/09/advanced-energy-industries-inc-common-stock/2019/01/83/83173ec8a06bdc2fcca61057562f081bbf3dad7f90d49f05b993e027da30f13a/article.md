# TEGAM, Inc. Awarded Five-Year Digital Thermometer Contract with US Navy

#### ***US Navy Contracts with TEGAM, Inc.***


GENEVA, Ohio—In 2017 the US Navy requested a proposal for a Temperature Meter to fulfill the technical requirements of their SCAT 4213. While application details were not disclosed, the Navy specifications were complete and demanded a great deal in **precision, accuracy, durability and price** . TEGAM responded to this solicitation with the new[912B Dual Channel Handheld Digital Thermometer](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/sense-and-measurement/thermal-sensing/thermocouple-thermometers/digital-thermometers/912b-thermocouple-thermometer/) .


*“With 0.04% basic accuracy, 2000 hours of battery life, and MIL-spec construction, the 912B gives our Navy technicians a level of confidence not available in the past,” explains Kevin Kaufman, Vice President of U.S. Government Sales. “TEGAM works hard to not only meet but to **exceed** these requirements, because we understand what is on the line.”*


TEGAM was **awarded a 5-year contract** to supply these digital thermometers to the United States Navy. The solution, a TEGAM Model 912B Thermocouple Thermometer outfitted with a vital tilt stand/magnetic hanger and a foam-filled, hard-shell carry case, met every specification sought by the Navy. TEGAM is currently processing the initial order.


The TEGAM reputation for quality and its commitment to **delivering world-class measurement instrumentation** are integral parts of the company’s core values and what the TEGAM brand is built upon. The contract awarded with the US Navy further solidifies a pledge to provide[digital thermometry](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/sense-and-measurement/thermal-sensing/thermocouple-thermometers/digital-thermometers/) and other instrumentation products that meet the most stringent standards in the world. TEGAM delivers mission-critical measurements in demanding environments.
