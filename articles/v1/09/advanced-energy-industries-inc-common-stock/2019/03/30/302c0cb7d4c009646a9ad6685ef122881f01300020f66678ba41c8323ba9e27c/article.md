# Excelsys Technologies, Inc. products are now Advanced Energy Low Voltage Solutions

Since Excelsys joined the Advanced Energy family in July 2017, they have further strengthened our applied technologies portfolio - offering new power solutions to Advanced Energy’s worldwide customer base. With a complementary platform of electronic power supplies for mission-critical applications, Excelsys joined our applied technologies organization, thereby enhancing our presence in new areas within medical equipment, metrology, clinical test & measurement, lasers and general industrial applications.


Over the past several months, we’ve worked to incorporate the Excelsys portfolio into our broader applied technologies product offerings and, today, are pleased to announce that the Excelsys line of power supplies is now part of Advanced Energy’s Low Voltage Solutions. From today, you can visit[https://www.advancedenergy.com](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/) to access the full portfolio of low voltage power supplies we offer, including our latest innovation in fanless technology for medical and industrial applications, the CoolX1000.
