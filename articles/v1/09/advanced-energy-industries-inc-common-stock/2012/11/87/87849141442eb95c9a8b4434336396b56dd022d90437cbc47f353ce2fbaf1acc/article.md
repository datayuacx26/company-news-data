# Voltmeter Case Sports Safety Features


### Posted


November 06, 2012 by


[Advanced Energy Editor](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/about/news/authors/advanced-energy-editor/)


### Contains no metal, making it safer around energized conductors


Advanced Energy offers a new Cordura Nylon Carrying Case to improve worker safety. The model 1204A case will fit any of the three Safety Volt Meters, models 110A, 122, and 125 (note, all SVM models are now discontinued). The sturdy case, has several features that help users make safer measurements:


- No metal parts, making it safe for use around energized conductors.
- Buckled shoulder strap for carrying and clipping over a pipe or pole, allowing a user to use both hands for making a measurement.
- Magnet in the cover flap to allow a user to attach it to any steel surface.
