# Advanced Energy Previews Industry’s First Titanium Efficiency M-CRPS Power Supply at OCP Global Summit


### Posted


October 16, 2023


**Proof-of-concept is first 1800 W PSU in the 60 mm M-CRPS form factor**


**DENVER, Colo., October, 16, 2023** — Advanced Energy Industries, Inc. (Nasdaq: AEIS)


– a global leader in highly engineered, precision power conversion, measurement and control solutions – has unveiled the industry’s first Modular Hardware System Common Redundant Power Supply (M-CRPS) capable of delivering 1800 W in the industry’s new, ultra compact 60mm x 185mm form factor.


Showcased at this year’s Open Compute Project (OCP) Global Summit in San Jose, the ultra-efficient proof-of-concept technology is also the first power supply in this form factor to be certified to the


80PLUS


®


Titanium efficiency standard


. Operating with efficiencies of up to 96%, the high-power new technology has been developed to address the high-performance, high-power-density demands of next-generation computing applications including Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML).


“With growing demand to deliver space-efficient solutions capable of powering the high-performance computing processors behind generative AI and other high-performance cloud computing applications means that data center operators need 80PLUS Titanium efficiency and ultra-high-power densities,” says


Richard Caubang, Advanced Energy’s


senior director – enterprise & networking product marketing. “As the first and only company to achieve Titanium certification in the new 1U, 185 mm by 60 mm M-CRPS form factor, Advanced Energy is again demonstrating its technology leadership in the area of high-performance, high-power density OCP-compliant supplies for demanding high performance compute applications.”


Visitors to the 2023 OCP Global Summit can see the 1800 W M-CRPS proof of concept, 80PLUS® Titanium PSU at the Advanced Energy booth (A7) and the OCP Summit Experience Center,


where engineers showcase best-in-class technologies.
