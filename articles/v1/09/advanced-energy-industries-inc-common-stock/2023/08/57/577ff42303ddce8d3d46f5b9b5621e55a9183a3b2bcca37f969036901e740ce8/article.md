# Engineered to Power Innovation: New Website for Power, Sensing and Control Integrates Full Artesyn Portfolio

Advanced Energy is putting design solutions and products at engineers’ fingertips with the unveiling of its[new website](http://www.advancedenergy.com/) on August 29, 2023. The new, fully re-designed site now integrates comprehensive information on the Artesyn family of products and ensures rapid access to the key information needed to accelerate the development of systems built around advanced power, sensing and control solutions.


The new site features intuitive navigation that quickly guides visitors to everything from technical product specifications and application notes to development tools and stocking information. Built from the ground up to deliver the best possible user experience, the site combines ease of use with robust and intuitive parametric and smart keyword search capabilities and a host of rich, design-oriented content.


Visitors to[www.advancedenergy.com](http://www.advancedenergy.com/) can search Advanced Energy’s full range of standard and modifiable products including low-, medium- and high-voltage AC/DC power supplies, DC-DC converters, plasma power systems, amplifiers and sense and measurement technologies. An application section allows engineers to find technical solutions, compliance expertise and other resources important to meeting design objectives, while the pages dedicated to design resources provide a wealth of software, tools and technical content to help engineers quickly and easily find and work with AE’s products.


“AE is a leader in innovating complex, precision power, measuring, sensing and control solutions. We want the new site to be the first port of call for engineers developing next-generation technologies that rely on these products,” says Rajiv Kommineni, Vice President of digital experience and commerce at Advanced Energy, “High volumes of relevant content, easy-to-find application and product solutions and robust and intuitive search capabilities will ensure the site becomes an important, bookmarked resource for engineering teams in applications ranging from a wide semiconductor manufacturing and medical systems to computing, telecom and robotics and transportation, and the full spectrum of advanced industrial use cases.”


With the launch of the new website, AE also made it easier to access support through live chat and phone contact that allows customers to connect with qualified engineers at any time and from any location. To enable orders, an integrated stock check facility speeds product location and ordering and ensures fast fulfilment of samples.


Whether it’s our product portfolio, our first-rate customer service or our website, our focus is always on innovating to meet the needs of engineers.
