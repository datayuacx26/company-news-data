# Energy Efficiency and Advanced Node Performance Key Topics at SEMICON Korea


### Posted


March 04, 2025 by


[Juergen Braun](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/about/news/authors/juergen-braun/)


Blog Summary


1. Focus on Energy Efficiency and Performance:


At SEMICON Korea 2025, Advanced Energy (AE) emphasized energy efficiency and high performance in their products, showcasing solutions that reduce energy consumption while maintaining top-tier performance.
2. Launch of Omni™ RF Diagnostic Cart:


AE unveiled their new Omni™ RF diagnostic cart, which enables RF calibration and diagnostics in customer facilities. The live demo received positive feedback for its ability to measure multiple frequencies with a single cart.
3. Diverse Product Portfolio:


AE presented a range of products designed to improve energy efficiency in the semiconductor industry, including the eVerest®, NavX™, eVoS®, MAXstream®, iHP, NeoPower™, FCM, LCM, and TF series, all aimed at supporting next-generation semiconductor processes and reducing operational costs.


---


At this year’s SEMICON Korea, February 19 - 21 in Seoul, South Korea, the topics that stood out were energy efficiency and the performance to enable advanced technology nodes. Advanced Energy (AE) was an exhibitor and featured a new product launch and energy-efficient products for the semiconductor industry.


This event was an opportunity for AE to connect with industry leaders, potential partners and customers who share our passion for innovation and performance. As industries continue to prioritize sustainability and cost-effectiveness, visitors to AE’s booth (A554) were able to see how our solutions reduce energy consumption while maintaining top-tier performance.


Advanced Energy unveiled new RF diagnostic cart


Advanced Energy announced our new Omni™ RF diagnostic cart. With a live demo, visitors to the AE booth experienced firsthand how our Omni cart enables RF calibration and diagnostics in customer facilities. Many visitors were excited about the ability to measure multiple frequencies with a single cart. The positive feedback reinforced our belief that this uniquely capable and mobile calibration and diagnostics capability will be a game changer.


“I was proud to represent the Global Services team in launching the Omni RF diagnostic cart,” said Sarah Williams, Advanced Energy’s lead applications engineer for Global Services, Plasma Power Products. “It was gratifying to be able to demonstrate this new product and receive interest from many attendees. We are excited for this latest addition to our portfolio and look forward to hearing customer success stories in the near future.”


[Read more about the Omni RF diagnostic cart.](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/plasma-power-products/integrated-systems/omni-rf-diagnostic-cart/)


Included alongside the new Omni™ RF diagnostic cart, Advanced Energy showcased leading semiconductor plasma power, power conversion, and critical temperature measurement products that help our customers make leaps forward in innovation.


Enhancing energy efficiency with AE's diverse product lines


Energy efficiency is at the core of our products for the semiconductor industry. We understand that our customers are looking for solutions that both deliver high performance and help reduce energy consumption and operational costs.


Here’s a closer look at some of our products that are designed to improve energy efficiency and were featured at SEMICON Korea:


- [eVerest® Series:](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/plasma-power-products/rf-plasma-generators/everest/) The eVerest RF power delivery system optimizes energy consumption while supporting next-generation semiconductor etch and deposition processes with configurable multi-level pulsing and high-speed controls.
- [NavX™ Series:](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/plasma-power-products/rf-match-networks/navx/) The NavX matching network redefines sophistication, and RF synchronization for exacting plasma control across the most complex pulsing profiles.
- [eVoS® Series:](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/plasma-power-products/power-delivery-systems/evos%E2%84%A2-me-asymmetric-bias-waveform-generator/) The eVoS bias solution enables precise command of etch and deposition geometries at very small dimensions by maximizing the ability to tailor ion energy.
- [MAXstream® Series:](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/plasma-power-products/remote-plasma-sources/maxstream/) The MAXstream remote plasma source delivers best-in-class plasma ignition reliability and ensures long chamber life and low particle generation.
- [iHP Series:](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/ac-dc-power-supply-units/configurable-modular-psus/ihp/?bz-page=1&bz-results-per-page=10) The iHP configurable high-power systems offer precise accuracy, resolution, leading efficiency, and stability for a wide range of applications.
- [NeoPower™ Series:](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/ac-dc-power-supply-units/configurable-modular-psus/neopower-configurable-series/) The NeoPower configurable AC-DC Power supplies offer ultra-high power density and include multiple options to create a custom power solution.
- [FCM Series:](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/ac-dc-power-supply-units/enclosed-psus/fcm/?bz-page=1&bz-results-per-page=10) The FCM AC-DC enclosed power supplies have > 95% efficiency with 0.98 power factor correction to reduce utility costs and minimize heat.
- [LCM Series:](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/ac-dc-power-supply-units/enclosed-psus/lcm/?bz-page=1&bz-results-per-page=10) The LCM AC-DC enclosed power supplies have a reputation for outstanding quality and high efficiency at a competitive cost, with safety approvals for industrial and medical equipment.
- [TF Series:](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/ac-dc-power-supply-units/enclosed-psus/tf/?bz-page=1&bz-results-per-page=10#/) The TF AC-DC enclosed power supplies are flexible, compact, and reliable to cater to a wide range of platforms and applications.


Summary


As a leading supplier of precision power conversion, measurement, control and testing systems for the semiconductor industry, Advanced Energy is at the forefront in providing next-generation solutions that support the fabrication and testing of cutting-edge semiconductors. At SEMICON Korea 2025, AE was on hand to display and discuss our comprehensive product portfolio that addresses the needs of the semiconductor market, including energy efficiency and process development for chip architectures measured on the angstrom scale.


For more information on AE’s presence at SEMICON Korea, visit:[SEMICON Korea 2025 | Advanced Energy.](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/about/news/events/semicon-korea-2025/)

Juergen Braun joined Advanced Energy in July 2007 and now serves as executive vice president of plasma power products. In his current role, Braun leads the plasma power and service business.
