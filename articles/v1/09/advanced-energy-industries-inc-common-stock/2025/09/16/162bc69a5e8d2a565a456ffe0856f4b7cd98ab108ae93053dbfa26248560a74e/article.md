# 33 kW Power Shelf Sets New Efficiency Standard for Semiconductor Test


### Posted


September 30, 2025


**Advanced Energy’s Evergreen™ Vento™ FCM33 delivers over 95% efficiency with modular scalability to MW and high reliability**


**DENVER, Colo., September 30, 2025** — Advanced Energy Industries, Inc. (Nasdaq: AEIS), a global leader in highly engineered, precision power conversion, measurement and control solutions, today expanded its Evergreen Vento portfolio of high-power modular AC-DC conversion platform with the FCM 33 kW 1U shelf, a high-density 48 VDC power supply engineered for semiconductor IC test, burn-in applications, and high-power industrial DC applications.


As power demands surge in semiconductor testing and high-power DC environments, the Evergreen Vento FCM33 meets the challenge with a modular, hot-swappable design that scales from kilowatts to megawatts. Multi-shelf paralleling enables flexible system configurations, while digital system-level control streamlines advanced command and monitoring.


“The Evergreen Vento FCM33K delivers high-density, efficient power conversion for IC and component testing, saving space and energy while offering flexible configuration options for bulk DC power,” said Joe Voyles, vice president, Industrial Power Conversion, System Power.


The FCM33 operates reliably across a wide input range (208–230 VAC nominal, no derating down to 200 VAC), ensuring stable performance even in regions with lower line voltages. Its 1U form factor delivers 33 kW total output—six independent 5.5 kW, 49 VDC channels—at over 95% peak efficiency, minimizing energy use and heat.


Engineered for uptime, the FCM33 features high mean time between failures (MTBF), low mean time to repair (MTTR), and supports dual input redundancy. Hot-swappable modules simplify maintenance and maximize system availability.
