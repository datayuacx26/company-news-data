# Advanced Energy Launches Compact Fiber Optic Thermometry Converter for Advanced Etch and Deposition

#### Advanced Energy Launches Compact Fiber Optic Thermometry Converter for Advanced Etch and Deposition at SEMICON® West 2025


**Oct 06, 2025 11:00 AM Eastern Standard Time**


**Enhanced accuracy and broader temperature measurement range enable higher quality and yield**


DENVER--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Advanced Energy Industries, Inc](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.advancedenergy.com%2Fen-us%2F&esheet=54333602&newsitemid=20251006623310&lan=en-US&anchor=Advanced+Energy+Industries%2C+Inc&index=1&md5=46977eb60c2975bf239e6f820f1701d4) . (Nasdaq: AEIS), a global leader in highly engineered, precision power conversion, measurement and control solutions, will preview its next-generation fiber optic thermometry (FOT) converter, the M-1200, at SEMICON® West 2025.


This press release features multimedia. View the full release here:[https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251006623310/en/](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251006623310/en/)


“For semiconductor OEMs, space and reliability are critical. The M-1200’s compact design and ability to withstand harsh environments provide repeatable performance that supports consistent manufacturing quality and yield,” said Dhaval Dhayatkar, Advanced Energy’s vice president, Critical Sensing and Control, System Power.


Smaller, more accurate, and with a broader temperature measurement range than most contact temperature sensing devices, the M-1200 opens process development possibilities for Angstrom-Era innovation. With ultra-fast EtherCAT communication, flexible probe configurations, and a compact form factor, it streamlines integration into complex semiconductor tools. Its -200 to 450°C measurement range widens application coverage, while proprietary phosphor formulations optimize probes for specific temperature ranges.


The new converter will be on display at SEMICON West at the Phoenix Convention Center, Ariz. from October 7-9 at[booth #5945](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.advancedenergy.com%2Fen-us%2Fabout%2Fnews%2Fevents%2Fsemicon-west-2025%2F&esheet=54333602&newsitemid=20251006623310&lan=en-US&anchor=booth+%235945&index=2&md5=9ccdf8e2d678bc784a0aeafba3c5b6c5) , where Advanced Energy’s experts will discuss how AE is helping to advance the Angstrom Era with AE power and unmatched control.


For more information on the M-1200 please visit[here](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.advancedenergy.com%2Fen-us%2Fproducts%2Fsense-and-measurement%2Fthermal-sensing%2Ffiber-optic-sensors%2Fluxtron-m-1200-fiber-optic-temperature-converter%2F&esheet=54333602&newsitemid=20251006623310&lan=en-US&anchor=here&index=3&md5=234f95bb3dbd0ff228df89170e90b6cc) .
