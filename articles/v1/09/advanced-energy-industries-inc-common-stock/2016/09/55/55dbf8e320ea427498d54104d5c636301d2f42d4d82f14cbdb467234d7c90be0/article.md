# New TEGAM Bluetooth® Hand-Held Datalogging Thermometers Aid Food Manufacturers in Preventing Foodborne Illness


### Posted


September 14, 2016 by


[Advanced Energy Editor](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/about/news/authors/advanced-energy-editor/)


## New 900 Datalogging Thermometer Series Sets New Performance Standards for Accuracy, Convenience and Wireless Ease of Use


September 13, 2016 12:14 PM Eastern Daylight Time
GENEVA, Ohio–([BUSINESS WIRE](https://www.businesswire.com/portal/site/home/) )–Advanced Energy's TEGAM, Inc., a leading supplier of digital hand held thermometers for 30 years, is introducing a new series of industrial thermocouple thermometers to aid food manufacturers in preventing foodborne illness.


> **“In addition, we incorporated the TEGAM Cloud™ to make it easy for food manufacturers to store critical temperature data and easily achieve compliance.”**


TEGAM’s new[900 Series datalogging thermometers](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/sense-and-measurement/thermal-sensing/thermocouple-thermometers/datalogging-thermometers/) are the only *Bluetooth®* low-energy, technology-enabled, universal thermocouple handheld thermometers made in the U.S.A. They provide **a complete data logging solution** with cloud storage and wireless transmission for no additional investment. These highly capable units provide 0.05% accuracy, 1000 hour battery life, a three-year warranty and easily cleanable cases.


These new digital thermometers are the most accurate thermometers ever offered by TEGAM to meet the increasing demands of the regulatory requirements where process temperature measurement is critical. They are available in a variety of configurations:


- 911A single and 912A dual input simple, durable and accurate[thermocouple thermometers](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/sense-and-measurement/thermal-sensing/thermocouple-thermometers/)
- 931A single and 932A dual input[datalogging thermocouple thermometers with Bluetooth](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/sense-and-measurement/thermal-sensing/thermocouple-thermometers/datalogging-thermometers/)


**Superior instrument accuracy, combined with the capabilities of the “Probe Offset” feature to zero out any probe errors, makes the 900 Series’ “system” accuracy unmatched in the industry.** The units are able to provide a long battery life using commonly available AA batteries to provide convenient and dependable portable measurement capability without the need for expensive batteries, AC adapters or chargers. The creative ergonomic design makes these instruments easy to hold, easy to read and easy to clean. Designed for one-handed operation, these tools are comfortable to carry and use for extended periods of time. The heavy-duty case is specifically designed for durability and resistance to contaminants such as dust and chemicals. The smooth, sleek, seamless case means that cleaning is quick and certain.


Real-time statistical data increases user productivity, process efficiency and product quality. Displayed on-demand MIN, MAX, AVG, RNG and STDEV (standard deviation) provide critical process information and diagnostics that allow the user to analyze manufacturing processes and make immediate adjustments for maximum efficiency and quality.


The on-board data-logging feature allows users to accurately record and store up to 1000 time-stamped measurements. The user may review them on the device and transfer them using Bluetooth wireless technology via a phone, tablet or PC to be stored locally or on the freely accessible cloud server. This seamless connectivity allows the user to collect, transmit and store accurate temperature data for analysis or compliance reporting without any additional or third party software.


To learn more about the TEGAM’s complete line of thermocouple thermometers,[visit this website section](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/sense-and-measurement/thermal-sensing/thermocouple-thermometers/) .


Like all TEGAM products, their thermometers are designed, assembled and tested in the United States. TEGAM backs this durable instrument with **a standard three-year warranty and a two-year calibration guarantee.**
