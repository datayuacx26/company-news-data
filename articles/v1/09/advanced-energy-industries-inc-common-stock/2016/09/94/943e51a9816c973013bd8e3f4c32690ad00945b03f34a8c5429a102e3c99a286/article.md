# U.S. Navy Has Selected TEGAM Model R1L-E2A for Aircraft and Ship Maintenance


### Posted


September 28, 2016 by


[Advanced Energy Editor](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/about/news/authors/advanced-energy-editor/)


GENEVA, Ohio–([BUSINESS WIRE](https://www.businesswire.com/portal/site/home/) )–Advanced Energy's TEGAM, Inc., a leading supplier of test and measurement equipment, is proud to announce that the U.S. Navy has selected the[TEGAM Model R1L-E2A Intrinsically Safe Portable Micro-ohmmeter and Bond Meter](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/sense-and-measurement/resistance-measurement/bond-meters-milli-ohmmeters/portable-bond-meters/r1l-e2a-portable-bond-meter/) to fulfill aircraft and ship maintenance requirement of the U.S. Navy and U.S. Marine Corps. Defense-rated orders totaling over $900,000 have been awarded and will be delivered in full before the end of CY16.


### “We are proud that the U.S. Navy and U.S. Marine Corps. have chosen TEGAM Inc.’s bond meter to ensure the safety of our soldiers while working in potentially explosive work areas.”


The R1L-E2A, which is **also specified for support of the Lockheed F-35 Joint Strike Fighter program** , is accurate, reliable, and safe for use in Class 1 Zone 0, Group IIA and Class 1 Division 1, Group D hazardous environments. TEGAM is proud to support the U.S. defense efforts with the **UL/ATEX certified RIL-E2A** , which is also commercially available from TEGAM for the most rugged bond testing applications. This makes the TEGAM R1L-E2A useful in applications not possible with other ground bond meters and micro-ohmmeters.


“We are proud that the U.S. Navy and U.S. Marine Corps. have chosen TEGAM Inc.’s bond meter to ensure the safety of our soldiers while working in potentially explosive work areas,” said Adam Fleder, President of TEGAM, Inc. “We appreciate protecting our soldiers with the R1L-E2A while they are protecting America all over the world.”


To learn more about the TEGAM’s complete line of[bond meters](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/sense-and-measurement/resistance-measurement/bond-meters-milli-ohmmeters/) ,[micro-ohmmeters](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/sense-and-measurement/resistance-measurement/micro-ohmmeters/) and[intrinsically safe bonding meters](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/sense-and-measurement/resistance-measurement/bond-meters-milli-ohmmeters/) , please follow these links.


**Like all TEGAM products, our intrinsically safe bond meters & micro-ohmmeters are designed, assembled and tested in the United States. TEGAM backs this durable instrument with a standard three-year warranty.**


For more information,[contact us](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/contact-us-form/) .
