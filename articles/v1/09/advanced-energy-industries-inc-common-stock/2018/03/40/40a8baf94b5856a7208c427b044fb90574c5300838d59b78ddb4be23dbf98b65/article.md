# TEGAM, Inc Announces New Datalogging Thermometers

TEGAM, Inc Announces the 931B/932B Datalogging Thermometers


***NEW PRODUCT ANNOUNCEMENT***


GENEVA, Ohio–([FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE](http://www.businesswire.com/) )–TEGAM, Inc., a world-wide leader in precision digital thermometers, announces the next generation in Handheld Thermometry: the 931B/932B Data Logging, Industrial, Digital Thermometers.


For over 30 years, TEGAM’s team of engineers at the Northeast Ohio factory have consistently worked to improve the accuracy, durability and usability of digital thermometers while incorporating the latest technology.


“TEGAM’s advanced line of industrial thermometers enables accurate and repeatable temperature measurements in challenging environments. Our innovative features, accuracy and durability are unequaled in a hand-held thermometer.”


Dan Jackson, Thermometry Product Manager discusses, “I am thrilled with the advances our team has made to the 931/932 datalogging thermometers. This new ‘B’ line has once again positioned TEGAM as the gold-standard in the manufacture of thermometry products that offer precision temperature monitoring, collection and data-logging.”


Some Features available on the[931](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/sense-and-measurement/thermal-sensing/thermocouple-thermometers/datalogging-thermometers/931b/) B/[932](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/products/sense-and-measurement/thermal-sensing/thermocouple-thermometers/datalogging-thermometers/932b-datalogging-thermometer/) B dataloggers include:


- Certificate of Calibration
- 1000-hour battery life on 3 AAA Batteries
- 04% of reading +(0.3ᵒC) Accuracy
- J, K, T, E, B, R, N, S thermocouples
- 4-Digit Auto-ranging 0. 1/1 ᵒC/ᵒ F/K Resolution
- Washable Case
- One Hand Operation
- Probe offset adjustment
- 3-year Warranty
- 2-year Calibration Guarantee
- 1000 data points
- Bluetooth® Connectivity
- Free TEGAMLink T app (iOS and Android)
