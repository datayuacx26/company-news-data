# New Connector Options Collateral

SL Power Electronics is pleased to release the newly redesigned version of the Connector Options collateral, which is a one-page representation of all standard output connectors available for external power supplies. The new version is aesthetically enhanced, easier to use and consistent with the look and feel of other SL Power marketing collateral.


The Connector Options are available in both print and online. Printed copies are available upon request from your[Regional Representative](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/about/#location-map) or direct from[SL Power’s Corporate Headquarters](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/support/) .


Each standard connector features an image, model number, and technical description. SL Power also has the capability of incorporating other types of output connectors to their external power supplies.


Choosing the right output connector is dependent upon a number of variables and our team is readily available to provide professional support and guidance[Contact Us](https://www.advancedenergy.com/en-us/contact-us-form/) .
