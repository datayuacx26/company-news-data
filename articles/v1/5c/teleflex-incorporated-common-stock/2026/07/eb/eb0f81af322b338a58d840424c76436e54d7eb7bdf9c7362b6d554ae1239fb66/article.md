# Jaffrey Facility Extrusion Expansion ‹ Teleflex Medical

**Teleflex Medical OEM Holds Groundbreaking Ceremony at Jaffrey, NH Manufacturing Facility**


**** Teleflex Incorporated (NYSE: TFX), a leading global provider of medical technologies, held a groundbreaking ceremony to launch the expansion project for its Teleflex Medical OEM facility in Jaffrey, New Hampshire. The project includes an over 8,200-square-foot addition to boost extrusion capacity.


The expansion is driven by accelerated global demand for Teleflex Medical OEM’s precision extrusion and access devices used in cardiology, neurology, radiology, and other areas.


“Expanding our facility will enable Teleflex Medical OEM to continue to support our customers’ needs,” said Jake Wagner, Sr. Engineering Manager of Teleflex Medical OEM. “Our customers provide life-saving products, and speed-to-market is crucial, and having a partner that can continue to grow with them is essential. “


Teleflex Medical OEM anticipates the expansion project will offer additional job opportunities to the area. The expansion project is expected to be completed by the end of 2023.
