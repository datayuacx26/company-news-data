# From Passion to Profession

Hannah Kirby’s Inspiring Journey at Teleflex Medical OEM


Hannah Kirby began her journey with Teleflex Medical OEM in 2018, working in both administrative and Human Resources support roles at our Limerick, Ireland manufacturing facility. She quickly found her passion for the HR side of the business. In 2020, with a growing family and the encouragement of her managers and peers, Hannah made the decision to pursue a degree in Human Resource Management at the University of Limerick.


Thanks to her hard work and determination, Hannah was promoted to Office Manager and Human Resources Representative in 2022. As she neared the completion of her degree, she earned another promotion to HR Generalist in 2023. In August 2024, Hannah graduated with a Bachelor of Arts in Human Resource Management & The Future of Work.


Teleflex Medical OEM congratulates Hannah on her remarkable achievements, and we look forward to working together in the years to come.
