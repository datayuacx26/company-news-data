# Live Oak Ventures Participates in Financing of Cascading AI, Inc. - Aug 15, 2026 #SBA

Live Oak Ventures joined Canapi Ventures and other investors in Casca’s $29M Series A. The investment underscores Live Oak’s long-term commitment to technology that empowers banks and small businesses. By supporting Casca’s AI-native loan origination system, Live Oak is helping create faster, fairer lending that expands access to capital for entrepreneurs nationwide.
