# Why Canapi is Leading Casca's Series A - Aug 15, 2026 #SBA

Casca has raised a $29M Series A led by Canapi Ventures, alongside Live Oak Ventures, Huntington, and Alloy Labs. The funding accelerates Casca’s mission to modernize small business lending by building the first AI-native loan origination system, helping banks move faster, lower costs, and expand access to capital for entrepreneurs.
