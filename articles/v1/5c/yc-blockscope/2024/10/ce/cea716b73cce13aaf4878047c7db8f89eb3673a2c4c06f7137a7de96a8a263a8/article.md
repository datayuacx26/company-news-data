# Demystifying DeFi: Tornado Cash

#### Written by: Tushar Tiwari, Analyst at[Blockscope](https://medium.com/u/e3c579ea4506)


Source: Tornado Cash


### The Veil of Privacy: A Journey Through Mixers


In the intricate world of cryptocurrencies, where transparency is often hailed as a virtue, privacy still exists in a small corner — facilitated by *mixers, often referred to as tumblers.* These cryptographic tools are designed to obscure transaction details, shielding users from unwanted scrutiny.[Tornado Cash](https://tornado.ws/) , launched in 2019, is one such modern privacy solution that has taken anonymity to a new level through **zero-knowledge proofs (ZKPs)** , allowing users to maintain discretion over their financial movements.


The concept of mixing cryptocurrencies emerged in the early days of Bitcoin. Initially, the early mixers combined funds to blur the line between sender and receiver, but they were far from perfect. Tornado Cash, with its use of ZKPs, introduced a robust and efficient method of disconnecting deposit and withdrawal addresses, setting itself apart from earlier, flawed systems. However, this enhanced privacy has also brought it into a regulatory gray area.


[In August 2022, the U.S. Treasury’s Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) sanctioned the popular mixer for its role in money laundering, including $455 million stolen by the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea’s (DPRK) Lazarus Group, a state-sponsored hacking organization, in the largest known crypto heist.](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/jy0916)


Tornado Cash’s journey from a privacy tool to a target of international sanctions underscores the delicate balance between privacy and regulation.


### The Rise of Tornado Cash


**Tornado Cash** became the go-to for users seeking anonymity on the Ethereum blockchain. It operates by pooling user deposits into what are known as “ **Anonymity Pools** ,” allowing withdrawals at unrelated addresses, thereby making tracing transactions virtually impossible.


*Tornado Cash distinguishes itself from traditional mixers by asserting that assets are never commingled.* The sophistication of its cryptographic methods and the level of anonymity it provides stem from its use of **zero-knowledge technology** — a feature widely praised within the crypto community for its effectiveness in countering financial surveillance. Additionally, the protocol deploys the use of **Core Deposit Circuits** , Proxy Contracts ( **Tornado Proxy** ), and Chunked Merkle Trees ( **Tornado Trees** ) to ensure privacy and security.


How Tornado Cash works


However, Tornado Cash isn’t just another privacy tool; it represents a pioneering step in decentralized privacy solutions, addressing the growing demand for privacy in an increasingly transparent financial landscape.


### Governance


At the heart of Tornado Cash is its ungovernable and immutable core. Once deployed, its core functions cannot be altered, even by the developers.


Instead, the protocol is governed by its community through the **TORN** token, a native asset that allows holders to influence platform decisions. Governance proposals, ranging from security updates to protocol changes, are voted on by TORN holders, ensuring decentralized decision-making.


### The Tornado Cash Dilemma


Tornado Cash finds itself at the center of a larger dilemma: how to balance privacy with legal compliance. Its use has grown among privacy-conscious users, but at the same time, it has been exploited for criminal purposes. Law enforcement agencies have focused on its involvement in hacks, scams, and ransomware attacks, creating a complex legal landscape for its developers and community.


> The association of Tornado Cash with criminal activities, including money laundering and terrorism financing, has raised serious concerns.[The U.S. Department of the Treasury sanctioned Tornado Cash in August 2022, citing its role in laundering over $7 billion, including funds linked to North Korea’s Lazarus Group.](https://time.com/6205143/tornado-cash-us-crypto-ban/)


> Supporting Tornado Cash co-founder **Roman Storm** , Et[hereum’s co-founder Vitalik Buterin donated 100 ETH (around $240,000) to Storm’s legal defense fund.](https://news.bitcoin.com/vitalik-buterin-shows-support-for-tornado-cash-developer-with-additional-100-eth-donation/) This is Buterin’s third contribution, bringing the total raised to over 327 ETH, underscoring his commitment to privacy and decentralization within the crypto space.


Tweet from Roman Storm


While developers argue they are creating tools for public benefit, authorities increasingly view them as complicit in facilitating illicit activity. This friction highlights the challenge for decentralized platforms to protect privacy while staying on the right side of the law.


### **Privacy vs. Regulation: The Way Forward**


Despite these regulatory hurdles, Tornado Cash has experienced a resurgence in 2024, *with $1.9 billion in new deposits* , demonstrating a persistent demand for privacy tools. The platform’s future hinges on its ability to strike a delicate balance between privacy and compliance. This may involve enhanced governance, legal safeguards, or further decentralization. Adapting to these demands is crucial for Tornado Cash’s sustainability and relevance amidst increasing scrutiny.
