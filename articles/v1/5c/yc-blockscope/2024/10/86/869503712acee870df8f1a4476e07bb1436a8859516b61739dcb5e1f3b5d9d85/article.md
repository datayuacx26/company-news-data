Imagine you have a treasure chest filled with gold coins and you know that if you lock this chest away for a year, it will magically double in value. However, during that year, you can’t use any of the coins inside. Staking on Proof-of-Stake (PoS) blockchains, like Ethereum, works similarly. There are rewards, but staking locks up funds, making them inaccessible for months or even years, depending on the network’s withdrawal schedule.


*For Ethereum, the minimum requirement to participate in staking is a hefty 32 ETH* — a sum out of reach for many. This creates a dilemma for users who want to stake to earn rewards while maintaining liquidity.


This is where **Lido** comes into play with its approach of liquid staking, allowing users to stake any amount of ETH (or tokens on other PoS chains) and, in return, receive **stTokens** that represent their staked assets while allowing them to remain liquid.


Before we dive deeper into Lido, let’s first review how staking works.


## ***Understanding Staking: The Basics***


Staking allows cryptocurrency holders to participate in network security and earn rewards by locking up their assets on PoS chains.


To stake, users transfer their coins into a wallet that supports staking functionalities. They then lock (stake) these coins, typically by selecting a validator node to delegate their stake to. Alternatively, users can run a full node and stake directly, though this method is more resource-intensive.


Staking typically offers returns between **3% to 5% APY** (Annual Percentage Yield) for cryptocurrencies like Ethereum, while other networks, like Polkadot, can offer up to 15%.


However, traditional staking requires users to lock up their funds for a longer period, making them illiquid. Additionally, custodial solutions offered by exchanges or third parties pose trust risks. Non-custodial options exist too, allowing users to earn yields without these risks, but with its own cons.


## Unlocking Liquidity: The Lido Revolution


Lido is a decentralized liquid staking protocol, *founded in December 2020 by Konstantin Lomashuk, Vasiliy Shapovalov, and Jordan Fish* . It allows users to stake their tokens on various PoS blockchains, including Ethereum, Solana, Polygon, and others. Lido does this by providing *tokenized staking derivatives* ****** like **stETH** (staked Ether) and **wstETH** (wrapped staked Ether).


Unlike traditional staking, Lido lets users stake their assets without losing liquidity or dealing with technical complexities. As of today, Lido has the highest amount of Ether staked on it, which amounts to more than 28% of the market share.

## *stTokens Explained*


Users who stake their ETH (or any other PoS token) through Lido receive stETH, which represents their staked ETH and accrues staking rewards over time. These stTokens are fully liquid and represent the staked Ether plus any rewards earned, allowing users to earn staking rewards while being tradable, held, or used in various DeFi applications.


stETH and ETH trade at similar prices, but stETH’s price is usually slightly higher because it includes the accrued rewards.


Lido also offers wstETH (wrapped stETH), which maintains a stable, predictable 1:1 exchange rate, making it easier to use in DeFi applications.


**How to Stake with Lido:**


1. Set up a wallet (e.g., MetaMask or Ledger).
2. Connect your wallet to the Lido platform.
3. Choose how much ETH you want to stake and confirm the transaction.
4. Instantly receive **** stETH tokens, which represent your staked ETH and grow with rewards over time.

## **Governance and LDO Tokens**


Lido is governed by its community through the **Lido DAO** (a decentralized autonomous organization). Decisions regarding protocol upgrades, validator selection, and fee structures are made by **LDO token** holders.


LDO tokens function as both governance tokens, providing holders with voting rights, and utility tokens, aligning the community’s interests with the protocol’s long-term success. The governance process is designed to be transparent and inclusive, ensuring that all stakeholders have a voice.


## Security First: Protecting Your Staked Assets


Security is a top priority for Lido, the largest liquid staking pool in the market. Lido employs industry-standard encryption to protect user data and funds and undergoes regular security audits. Lido has a strong bug bounty program with Immunefi, offering up to $2,000,000 for reporting vulnerabilities.


## **Lido’s Expanding Ecosystem**


Lido has revolutionized the staking landscape by becoming a one-stop solution for staking on all PoS chains. Regardless of the chain or tokens you hold, Lido optimizes the staking process for you, whether it involves swapping, bridging, or staking on different PoS chains using tools like *Lido’s Adapter* .


Along with that, Lido continues to innovate by expanding its services to multiple blockchains and integrating with leading DeFi protocols. Recently, Lido approved a canonical bridge to BNB Chain via Wormhole and Axelar, unlocking significant cross-chain liquidity.
