# Introducing OneCheckout

It's time to buy. From our inception, OneText has focussed on one goal: helping merchants sell more through frictionless buying. We are proud to be the leaders in text-to-buy, AI upsells and 1:1 shopping. Today, we're excited to take another step forward with[OneCheckout.ai](https://onecheckout.ai/) - the best way for agents to buy online.


‍


## Explore OneCheckout.ai


‍


[OneCheckout.ai](https://onecheckout.ai/) allows developers, agents and platforms to purchase from any site. It's built on OneText, and our newly-released agentic purchase feature. We see this as the natural evolution and complement of our core business.


‍


We think our e-commerce customers will find exciting uses for our MCP. We know many of our brands are active Claude Code and Codex users. OneCheckout is the perfect add-on for any AI development environment. It makes integrating new software and services much easier. It also lets you programmatically control any paid API service, and that includes OneText for RCS, SMS and MMS.


‍


## Be Featured


We are not a passive transaction layer. We think brands deserve AI tools that help them sell, upsell, and tell their stories. And OneCheckout faciliates this. OneCheckout will actively steer shoppers and developers to purchase directly from first-class members of the OneText Network. Look for opportunities to feature and promote your products to OneCheckout buyers.


‍


## Brands First


Brands and merchants come first at OneText. We're dedicated to continue to build on the best texting and payment solution for ecommerce. OneCheckout is now a chapterj of that story. Thank you for continuing to be part of our journey. We're excited for what's next.


‍
