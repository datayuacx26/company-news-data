We’ve improved the **edges between nodes** to prevent overlapping, making flow diagrams **clearer and easier to follow** . This solves previous issues where connections sometimes created confusing layouts.

Merchants and support teams can now **send multiple images in a single text thread** - making it easier to share product photos, order confirmations, and visual responses. No more sending images one by one!‍👉 Try it out in the Texts tab today!


## **🚀 One-Click Popup Preview and Publishing**


Merchants can now **preview and publish their OneText popups instantly** to their website. No complicated setup - just one click and your popup goes live!

## **🤖 AI Just Got Smarter: Global Intent Handler is Live!**


Our AI assistant now listens **globally for cart update intents and FAQ questions** , making responses faster and more accurate. Expect improved **real-time assistance** for shoppers.


## **❌ One-Click Subscription Cancellations**


Merchants can now **cancel text refills directly in a text thread** - simplifying the process and improving the customer experience.


## **📊 UTM Tracking Enabled by Default**


No setup required! UTM tracking is now **automatically enabled for all merchants** , giving them better insights into their SMS campaign performance.


## **📈 Usage Bars for Enterprise Billing**


Enterprise merchants with **usage-based billing** can now see **real-time usage bars** in their billing dashboard, providing better visibility into monthly spend.
