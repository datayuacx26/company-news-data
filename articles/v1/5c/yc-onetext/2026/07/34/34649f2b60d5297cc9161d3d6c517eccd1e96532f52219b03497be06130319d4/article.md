# Getting More Opt-Ins for Your SMS/Email Strategy: 10 Proven Tactics to Grow Your List

##### Your SMS and email list is one of your most valuable marketing assets. It gives you direct access to your audience — without relying on algorithms or rented platforms. But building that list? That’s where many businesses hit a wall.


‍


In this post, we’ll break down ten proven strategies for increasing opt-ins to your SMS and


email programs—without annoying your audience or offering shallow gimmicks.


‍


### **1. Create Irresistible Incentives**


People won’t give up their contact info for nothing. Offer them something valuable in


return—whether that’s a discount, freebie, or exclusive access. Examples:


● 15% off their first purchase


● Early access to sales


● A free guide, ebook, or webinar


● Free shipping on their next order


💡 Tip: Tailor your incentive to your audience’s real desires, not just a generic discount.


‍


### **2. Use On-Brand Pop-Ups Wisely**


Pop-ups work—when done right. Use entry-intent, scroll-triggered, or exit-intent pop-ups that


match your brand voice and offer a clear benefit.


Best practices:


● Use a bold headline


● Keep copy short and benefit-driven


● Use a single, compelling call-to-action (CTA)


‍


### **3. Embed Signup Forms Across Your Site**


Don’t hide your opt-in form in the footer. Instead:


● Add inline forms to your homepage, blog posts, and product pages


● Create engaging pop-ins (like you’d find with OneText) that allow for fun surveys and


games to help entice visitors to sign up


● Create a dedicated signup page you can link to from ads, socials, and email footers


‍


### **4. Leverage Checkout & Account Creation**


Your checkout page is prime real estate. Add an opt-in checkbox (pre-checked only if compliant


with local laws) to collect consent during:


● Checkout


● Account registration


● Booking or scheduling


This is especially effective because users are already in “action mode.”


‍


### **5. Offer Exclusive SMS-Only or Email-Only Content**


Make your SMS/email program feel like a VIP club. Promote it as the place for:


●Back-in-stock alerts


● Flash sales


● Insider content


● Limited-time drops


People want to feel like they’re getting something special.


‍


### **6. Run Contests or Giveaways**


These are goldmines for opt-ins—if structured right. Example:


“Enter to win a $100 gift card—join our text list to enter!”


Make it easy to join, follow up with value-based content, and comply with promotional rules in


your region.


‍


### **7. Use Social Proof & Social Media**


Show that others are signing up:


● Add testimonials or quotes about your newsletter/text program


● Highlight the number of subscribers (“Join 10,000+ people who get our deals first!”)


Promote your opt-in across Instagram, TikTok, and other social platforms with swipe-up links or


link-in-bio tools.


‍


### **8. Tap Into Physical Locations (If Applicable)**


For brick-and-mortar businesses:


● Add QR codes at the register


● Include shortcodes on receipts or packaging


● Train staff to invite customers to join your list


Simple script:


“Want 10% off your next visit? Text JOIN to 12345!”


‍


### **9. Optimize for Mobile**


Most users are on their phones—especially for SMS programs. Ensure your forms are:


● Mobile-responsive


● Easy to complete in a few taps


● Clear and visually simple


Use autofill-friendly input fields and short forms.


‍


### **10. Test, Refine, Repeat**


Not every tactic will work equally well for every audience. Test different:


● Offers (discount vs. content)


● Formats (pop-up vs. inline)


● Language and CTA copy


● Placement and timing


‍


Use A/B testing to find your highest-converting combo—and keep optimizing.


‍


### **Wrapping Up**


Building your list takes intention, creativity, and a little bit of testing. But it’s worth it. A strong


SMS and email list gives you a direct channel to your best customers—one you truly own.


Start small. Pick 2–3 tactics from this list to implement this month. Measure results, iterate, and


keep growing.
