# Manufact is now a Claude connector

Manufact is now available as a connector on Claude. It's the same app we recently shipped to the ChatGPT App Store, for managing your MCP servers from a conversation. We run it on both surfaces from a single codebase with[mcp-use](https://github.com/mcp-use/mcp-use) , which maintains support for ChatGPT and Claude. For the full tour of what it does, read[the ChatGPT announcement](https://manufact.com/blog/manufact-chatgpt-app) .


[Use Manufact on Claude →](https://claude.ai/directory/connectors/manufact)


Once Anthropic publishes a connector, it becomes discoverable to every Claude user in the connector directory. In the days after ours went live, our signups jumped more than 10x.


Two days ago Anthropic released[observability for developers building connectors](https://claude.com/blog/observability-for-developers-building-connectors) : your connector listing now comes with analytics and a ranking. The same release also opened self-serve submission, so anyone with Admin or Owner access on a Team or Enterprise plan can now submit a cloud connector to the directory directly in Claude (currently in public beta).
