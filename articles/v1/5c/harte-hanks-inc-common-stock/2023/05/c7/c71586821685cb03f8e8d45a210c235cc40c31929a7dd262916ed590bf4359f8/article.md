# Harte Hanks’ 100-Year Anniversary Celebration Ringing the Closing Bell at Nasdaq

Centennial celebrations are a milestone few U.S. businesses reach. Harte Hanks has defied the odds and kicked off its 100th-anniversary celebration on May 9th by ringing the Nasdaq Stock Market closing bell.


Thank you to our clients and employees, past and present for “Making it Happen”.
