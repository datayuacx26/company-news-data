# Republic Services Again Recognized Among the World's Most Ethical Companies

## Republic Services Again Recognized Among the World's Most Ethical Companies


March 18, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investor.republicservices.com/node/30101/pdf)


***Company's eighth year on the list, highlighting its ongoing commitment to ethics, compliance and corporate governance***


PHOENIX


, March 18, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Republic Services, Inc. (NYSE: RSG), a leader in the environmental services industry, has been named to Ethisphere's list of the World's Most Ethical Companies® for the eighth time. The company is among 138 honorees representing 17 countries and 40 industries recognized by Ethisphere, a global leader in defining and promoting ethical business standards.


"Our company values guide how we operate, and working responsibly and with integrity is non-negotiable," said Jon Vander Ark, president and chief executive officer. "This recognition is a testament to our team's unwavering commitment to delivering results the right way."


The World's Most Ethical Companies assessment is grounded in Ethisphere's proprietary Ethics Quotient®, based on 240+ documented proof points on company practices that support robust ethics and compliance, including: corporate governance; program structure & resourcing; written standards; training, awareness, & communication; risk assessment & auditing; investigations, enforcement, discipline & incentives; measurement of ethical culture; third-party risk management, and environmental & social impact.


Ethisphere's 2026 list of the World's Most Ethical Companies is available at[worldsmostethicalcompanies.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4643578-1&h=2984916830&u=https%3A%2F%2Fworldsmostethicalcompanies.com%2Fhonorees%2F&a=worldsmostethicalcompanies.com) .


In addition to being named to the World's Most Ethical Companies list, Republic Services was recently named to the S&P Global[2026 Sustainability Yearbook](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4643578-1&h=2150368196&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.spglobal.com%2Fsustainable1%2Fen%2Fcsa%2Fyearbook&a=2026+Sustainability+Yearbook) , recognized for the fifth time as one of the[World's Most Admired Companies](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4643578-1&h=2049452689&u=https%3A%2F%2Ffortune.com%2Franking%2Fworlds-most-admired-companies%2F&a=World%27s+Most+Admired+Companies) and certified as a[Great Place to Work](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4643578-1&h=2207245879&u=https%3A%2F%2Fedge.prnewswire.com%2Fc%2Flink%2F%3Ft%3D0%26l%3Den%26o%3D4602804-1%26h%3D2865183599%26u%3Dhttps%253A%252F%252Finvestor.republicservices.com%252Fnews-releases%252Fnews-release-details%252Frepublic-services-certified-great-place-workr-ninth-consecutive%26a%3DGreat%2BPlace%2Bto%2BWork&a=Great+Place+to+Work) for the ninth consecutive year.
