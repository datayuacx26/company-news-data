### Movado Group, Inc. Announces Date of Conference Call and Webcast for Third Quarter Fiscal Year 2026 Results


November 18, 2025


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/374447251/files/doc_news/Movado-Group-Inc--Announces-Date-of-Conference-Call-and-Webcast-for-Third-Quarter-Fiscal-Year-2026-Results-2025.pdf)


PARAMUS, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Movado Group, Inc. (NYSE: MOV) invites investors to listen to a broadcast of the Company's conference call to discuss third quarter fiscal year 2026 earnings results on Tuesday, November 25, 2025, at 9:00 a.m. Eastern Time. A press release detailing the Company’s third quarter fiscal year 2026 results will be issued before the market opens and prior to the call. The conference call will be hosted by Efraim Grinberg, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, and Sallie DeMarsilis, Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer.


Investors and analysts interested in participating on the call are invited to dial (877) 407-0784 and reference conference ID number 13757189 approximately ten minutes prior to the start of the call. The conference call will also be webcast live at[www.movadogroup.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.movadogroup.com&esheet=54359802&newsitemid=20251118877897&lan=en-US&anchor=www.movadogroup.com&index=1&md5=a94429765dcfad9810498dc1e8ae3ada) . The webcast will be archived online within one hour of the completion of the conference call and remain available for 90 days. Additionally, a telephonic re-play of the call will be available at 1:00 p.m. ET on November 25, 2025, until 11:59 p.m. ET on December 9, 2025 and can be accessed by dialing (844) 512-2921 and entering replay pin number 13757189.


Movado Group, Inc. designs, sources, and globally distributes and sells MOVADO®, MVMT®, OLIVIA BURTON®, EBEL®, CONCORD®, CALVIN KLEIN®, COACH®, TOMMY HILFIGER®, HUGO BOSS® and LACOSTE® watches and, to a lesser extent, jewelry and other accessories, and operates Movado Company Stores in the United States and Canada.
