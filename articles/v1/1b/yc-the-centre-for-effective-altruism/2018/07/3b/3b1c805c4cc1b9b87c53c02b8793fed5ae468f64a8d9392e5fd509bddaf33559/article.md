### Articles


Peter Hurford and Marcus A. Davis have[created Priority Wiki](http://effective-altruism.com/ea/1q6/announcing_prioritywiki_a_cause_prioritization/) , a cause prioritization wiki anyone can edit which categorizes particular interventions within broader causes.


What are effective charities tackling climate change? John Halstead of Founders Pledge has just completed an extensive[cause report on climate change](http://effective-altruism.com/ea/1qs/new_research_on_effective_climate_charities/) , in which he recommends two charities in that area: the[Clean Air Task Force](http://catf.us/) and[Coalition for Rainforest Nations](http://www.rainforestcoalition.org/) .


Two years ago, researchers from Google, Stanford, UC Berkeley, and OpenAI released “[Concrete Problems in AI Safety](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1606.06565v1.pdf) ”. This seminal paper explains the safety problems in present and future AI systems for researchers. The Future of Life Institute has now[summarized](https://futureoflife.org/2018/06/26/a-summary-of-concrete-problems-in-ai-safety/?cn-reloaded=1) it for a more general audience.


[This 80,000 Hours podcast](https://80000hours.org/podcast/episodes/tara-mac-aulay-operations-mindset/) with Tara Mac Aulay, former CEO of the Centre for Effective Altruism, highlights the importance of good operations work and how the drive to improve organizations can change the world.


Which charities help slow population growth, either directly or indirectly? Brad Hurley addresses this question in[this blog post](https://www.thelifeyoucansave.org/blog/id/1449/tackling-population-growth) .

### Updates


**80,000 Hours** 80,000 Hours released 4 podcasts and grew subscribers ahead of targets to 9,500. The podcasts include[Tara Mac Aulay on operations](https://80000hours.org/podcast/episodes/tara-mac-aulay-operations-mindset/) ;[Rob on 80,000 Hours](https://www.stitcher.com/podcast/the-jolly-swagmen-podcast/e/54874814) ;[Aaron Hamlin on voting reform](https://80000hours.org/podcast/episodes/aaron-hamlin-voting-reform/) ; and[Anders Sandberg on nuclear war and solar flares.](https://80000hours.org/podcast/episodes/anders-sandberg-extending-life/) They also coached 31 people, attended 6 conferences and made their[targets](https://groups.google.com/forum/#!topic/80k_updates/q1FPop_N9Zk) .


**Animal Charity Evaluators** Animal Charity Evaluators published an[analysis](https://animalcharityevaluators.org/blog/sentience-institute-survey-analysis/) of Sentience Institute’s survey on attitudes toward animal farming and animal-free food. Also, their data scientist, Eric Herboso, published a[post](http://effective-altruism.com/ea/1q0/effective_advertising_and_animal_charity/) on the EA forums discussing ACE's advertising and outreach practices.


**Centre for the Study of Existential Risk** CSER researchers presented at a Governing Artificial Intelligence seminar co-hosted by the International Peace Institute and the United Nations University’s Centre for Policy Research ([videos of sessions](https://www.ipinst.org/2018/06/governing-artificial-intelligence) ), judged at the Global Challenges Foundation[New Shape Prize](https://globalchallenges.org/our-work/the-new-shape-prize/awardees) , presented at Effective Altruism Global[San Francisco 2018](https://www.facebook.com/media/set/?set=ms.c.eJxFkMkNBDEIBDNacTaQf2Irwxh~%3BS9CXMBnDAWUQgn8ygOOAEFpAA~_ICC1gZiV4gUimhuRpkDao~_YKjWQF6Qo5GrYdkv7h8AJay0IPeltDXM1oXPi9G9cOfRWCBjS9fWk1q0aINN23htJ3pudPeTlNk3ukz012VcbF0gs~_nr0hNCd8KidmH9A8K0Tk8~-.bps.a.2104164433160794.1073741832.1608831866027389&type=1) , and attended the Ninth Electric Infrastructure Security[Summit](https://www.eiscouncil.org/summit_Details.aspx?itemId=40) . An artist wrote a[blogpost](https://kathrynbrimblecombeart.blogspot.com/2018/06/existential-risk-research-art.html?spref=tw) about attending CSER’s 2018 Conference. A book chapter,[From Natural Hazard to Man-Made Disaster: The Protection of Disaster Victims in China and Japan](https://www.cser.ac.uk/resources/natural-hazard-man-made-disaster/) , was published.


**Future of Life Institute** For[FLI's monthly podcast](https://futureoflife.org/2018/06/28/podcast-mission-ai-giving-a-global-voice-to-the-ai-discussion-with-charlie-oliver-randi-williams/) , Ariel Conn spoke with Charlie Oliver and Randi Williams about how their new project,[Mission AI](https://tech2025.com/tech-2025-mission-ai-launches-to-bridge-the-gap-in-ai-research/) , aims to broaden the AI conversation and increase diverse voices. Lucas Perry spoke with Kaj Sotala on the[AI Alignment Podcast](https://futureoflife.org/2018/06/14/podcast-astronomical-future-suffering-and-superintelligence-with-kaj-sotala/) about suffering risks and superintelligence.


**GiveWell** GiveWell evaluated and[named](https://blog.givewell.org/2018/06/21/announcing-zusha-as-a-standout-charity/) the Georgetown University Initiative on Innovation, Development, and Evaluation (_gui__2__de_​)'s Zusha! Road Safety Campaign a[standout charity](https://www.givewell.org/charities/other-charities) . GiveWell also[published](https://blog.givewell.org/2018/06/29/givewells-money-moved-and-web-traffic-in-2017/) a report on the amount of money they directed to their recommended charities and their web traffic in 2017.


**Open Philanthropy Project** The Open Philanthropy Project announced grants of[£2,000,000](https://www.openphilanthropy.org/giving/grants/centre-effective-altruism-general-support-2018) to the Centre for Effective Altruism for general support,[$375,000](https://www.openphilanthropy.org/focus/us-policy/farm-animal-welfare/mercy-animals-us-broiler-chicken-welfare-corporate-campaigns) to Mercy For Animals and[£171,600](https://www.openphilanthropy.org/focus/us-policy/farm-animal-welfare/rspca-broiler-breed-study) to the RSPCA to improve broiler chicken welfare, and[$150,000](https://www.openphilanthropy.org/focus/global-catastrophic-risks/potential-risks-advanced-artificial-intelligence/machine-intelligence-research-institute-ai-safety-retraining-program) to MIRI and[$100,000](https://www.openphilanthropy.org/focus/global-catastrophic-risks/potential-risks-advanced-artificial-intelligence/ai-impacts-general-support-2018) to AI Impacts to reduce risks from advanced artificial intelligence.


**Sentience Institute** Sentience Institute published a[case study](https://www.sentienceinstitute.org/gm-foods) on what clean meat innovators can learn from the failures of GMOs, and a[post](https://www.sentienceinstitute.org/blog/three-big-changes) on three big changes their research suggests the farmed animal movement should make.


**The Life You Can Save** A recent TLYCS blog post by Nick Fitz,[To Grow a Healthy Movement, Go for Low-Hanging Fruit](https://www.thelifeyoucansave.org/blog/id/1433/to-grow-a-healthy-movement-go-for-low-hanging-fruit) , argues that many potentially highly-engaged effective altruists haven’t even heard of EA yet, and that if we used evidence to identify them, we could grow the movement more quickly and sustainably.
