# Introducing the Adaptyv Bio API

/


TL;DR


- -


We’re excited to release beta access for integrating our lab API with your protein design tools!


- -


Sign up for early access here!


Our API allows you to programmatically create experimental campaigns, get experiment updates and query lab results.


By abstracting away the complexity of the wet lab we’re enabling thousands of protein design teams to get easier access to experimental data to validate their protein designs.


From the smallest techbio to the largest pharma company - we’re making experimental data generation easier than ever before to allow you to engineer better proteins!


**Experiments-as-code**


- Submit experiments programmatically to our state-of-the-art lab facilities


- Receive updates about experiment statuses via webhooks


- Integrate wet lab results with your data analysis workflows


**AI-powered protein optimization campaigns**


- Schedule automated design campaigns to benchmark your AI models


- Use your AI models to optimize protein designs based on wet lab results


- Set a wet lab budget and run autonomous closed-loop optimization using AI agents
