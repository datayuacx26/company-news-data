# Submissions for the Nipah virus protein design competition are open!


### We’ve already received well over 100 protein designs in just the last couple days. Leaderboard coming soon 👀


[Adaptyv Bio](https://substack.com/@adaptyvbio)


Nov 01, 2025


Want to design a protein that blocks the deadly Nipah virus from binding to cell receptors? We’ll test your proteins in our lab for free!


**Go to [http://proteinbase.com](http://proteinbase.com/) to get started and submit your designs**
Submission Rules:


- Up to 10 proteins per submission.


- Each sequence must be max 250 AA.


- You can submit once every 24 hours


- New submission overwrites old submission


- Designs need to be at minimum 10 AA away from any published sequence -> We have a similarity check for that


[Submit your proteins](http://proteinbase.com/)
