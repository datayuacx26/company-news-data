# #TechnologyOnThe Move | Ft. Tim Simmons and Vinod Bidarkoppa

Technology on the Move (TOM) was conceptualized and launched in 2018 to highlight how Global Tech leveraged technology to further retail possibilities for Walmart. What followed, is a series of interviews featuring the Walmart leadership team as advocates of innovation, retail solutions, emerging technologies, culture, organization goals, sustainability, community giving, people-centric initiatives and thought leadership.


While it was paused during the pandemic, we’re happy to bring our popular series back in action. The first episode of this season of TOM features Vinod Bidarkoppa and Tim Simmons in conversation with Priya Subramani and Raghavan Balakrishnan.


Watch the video to get a sneak peek on how Sam’s Club is trailblazing the disruptive innovation for Walmart, and more!
