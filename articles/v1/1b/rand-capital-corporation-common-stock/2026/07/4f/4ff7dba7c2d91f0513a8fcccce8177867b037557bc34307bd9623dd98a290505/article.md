BUFFALO, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Rand Capital Corporation](http://www.randcapital.com/) (Nasdaq: RAND) (“Rand” or the “Company”), a business development company providing alternative financing for lower middle market companies, announced that it plans to release its second quarter fiscal year 2026 financial results before the opening of financial markets on Wednesday, August 5, 2026. The Company will also host a conference call and webcast to review its results that day.

**ABOUT RAND CAPITAL**


Rand Capital Corporation (Nasdaq: RAND) is an externally managed business development company (BDC). The Company’s investment objective is to maximize total return to its shareholders with current income and capital appreciation by focusing its debt and related equity investments in privately-held, lower middle market companies with committed and experienced managements in a broad variety of industries. Rand primarily invests in businesses that have sustainable, differentiated and market-proven products, revenue of more than $10 million and EBITDA in excess of $1.5 million. The Company’s investment activities are managed by its external investment adviser, Rand Capital Management, LLC. Additional information can be found at the Company’s website where it regularly posts information:[randcapital.com](https://www.randcapital.com/) .
