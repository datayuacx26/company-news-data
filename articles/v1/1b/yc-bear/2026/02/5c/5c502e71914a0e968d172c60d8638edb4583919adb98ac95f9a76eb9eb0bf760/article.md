# How to Track Your Brand’s Visibility on AI Search

## **What is AI Visibility?**


AI visibility is a metric that is steadily becoming more and more important to companies. As the paradigm shifts from Google Search to various AI engines powered by LLMs, companies are seeing drastic drops in their organic traffic. For many, this is a pipeline that inbound revenue relies on.


This fundamental change in how customers are finding information is defining a new key metric: AI visibility. AI visibility is a holistic metric that is meant to track the strength of a brand’s pipeline from ChatGPT. While other companies rely on superficial methods like “mention frequency” to track AI visibility, Bear AI takes a far more nuanced approach.

## **Why AI Visibility Matters**


Generative AI has fundamentally changed how people discover information.
Instead of scrolling through links, users are asking conversational engines for advice, recommendations, and analysis.


[According to a 2025 HubSpot AI Search Trends Report](https://www.hubspot.com/company-news/from-seo-to-lmo-hubspot-launches-the-first-free-tool-for-ai-discovery#:~:text=Customers%20and%20prospects%20are%20ditching,customers%20in%20the%20AI%20era.%E2%80%9D) , 37% of users now prefer AI-assisted answers over traditional search.


That means visibility inside AI responses is the new form of brand awareness.
When a model like ChatGPT lists your brand in its top recommendations, or cites your research in an answer, it signals trust and authority to millions of users.


If your content is missing from AI responses, you’re missing a growing share of attention, influence, and organic reach.


## **What AI Search Visibility Really Means**


AI visibility goes beyond rankings.
It’s about how your brand exists within the context of generative answers: what AI systems say about you, and whether they position you as a reliable voice in your space.


We track four forms of AI visibility:


1.


**Citations** : Direct links or attributions to your website in AI responses.


2.


**Mentions** : Natural references to your brand name in answers.


3.


**Context** : The surrounding language, tone, and sentiment.


4.


**Representation** : Whether the AI describes your product or values accurately.


Visibility is both quantitative (how often) and qualitative (how you’re framed).
Even one high-trust citation in a frequently surfaced query can have the impact of hundreds of keyword rankings.
