# How to Rank Higher in ChatGPT Search

Hundreds of millions of users around the world now rely on AI-powered search to discover brands, products, and services. They are making decisions based on the information provided by chatbots, especially the most widely used AI search model: ChatGPT.

## **How to Rank Higher in ChatGPT Search: The Basics**


The first step is to understand the basic concepts: how ChatGPT structures its information and how it differs from other search engines, such as Google. Even if your brand ranks incredibly well on Google, that doesn’t guarantee strong visibility on ChatGPT. Then, start applying best practices and identifying the most effective strategies for your brand.


### **What Is ChatGPT Search?**


If you’ve been following ChatGPT’s growth over the past few years or months, you probably know this: In October 2024,[OpenAI introduced ChatGPT search](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-search/) , enabling the chatbot to browse the web and deliver real-time information to users.


By February 2025, not too long ago, this feature had rolled out globally for all users. Now, ChatGPT includes live, up-to-date data in its responses for most types of queries.


Quickly, **ChatGPT became a search engine** , and it has been growing exponentially. Even if[Google remains 210 times bigger than ChatGPT](https://searchengineland.com/google-210x-bigger-chatgpt-search-462604) in search, many experts predict that this gap may start to close sooner than expected.


### **Understand the Main Technical Aspects**


ChatGPT has its own algorithm and ranking mechanisms for delivering answers and information to users. While the exact formula isn’t public, there are several well-known technical factors that influence how the model organizes and prioritizes content.


Anyone aiming to rank higher in ChatGPT Search should understand these key aspects:


-


**ChatGPT does not prioritize Google for web data:** In fact, ChatGPT prefers Bing over Google. Just remember that Microsoft is one of OpenAI’s major investors and the owner of Bing. When responding to a query, ChatGPT combines Bing search results with other indexed documents and data sources to generate its answers.


-


**It has its own reranking system:** A recent[study](https://metehan.ai/blog/chatgpt-5-search-configuration/?ref=ppc.land) revealed that ChatGPT doesn’t simply use the top-ranked pages from Bing. Instead, it creates a pool of relevant sources and chooses the most authoritative and relevant content. It also prioritizes newer over older content.


-


**It considers technical SEO elements:** Just as you would optimize for Google, your website should include essential technical elements such as clear headings, well-placed keywords, structured metadata, and optimized site architecture.
Another crucial factor is accessibility to ChatGPT’s web crawler, GPTBot. Make sure your site allows GPTBot to scrape and index your content by properly configuring or removing your robots.txt file.


-


**It focuses on users’ needs:** ChatGPT focuses on providing helpful and relevant information for users. It even considers conversational information like opinions and reviews, as well as content context for each query.

For ChatGPT to value your content and mention your brand, it needs to clearly understand what your business contributes, see evidence of your expertise and knowledge on the topic the user is asking about, and verify that your information meets basic quality standards. Include[Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) strategies](https://www.usebear.ai/blog/geo-explained-how-to-win-customers-with-ai-in-2025) , such as monitoring and analyzing the prompts your audience might use in AI searches.

### **Get mentions and references from other websites**


ChatGPT will consider your website’s content, but it also knows that you control that information. The AI model uses your data as a reference, but it will also include in its analysis what other websites, blogs, forums, and social media users say about your brand.


Platforms like Reddit have become valuable sources for AI models because they often include genuine discussions and real user experiences. Remember that AI systems prioritize authentic human perspectives. Getting mentions across multiple external sites is vital to ranking higher in ChatGPT.

### **Learn What ChatGPT Knows About Your Brand**


Have you ever asked ChatGPT what it knows about your brand? This is a crucial step in learning how to rank higher in ChatGPT Search: Understanding your current position.


Just bear in mind that asking it from your personal ChatGPT account might give you the most objective results. A better approach is to use a new ChatGPT account or rely on specialized Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) tools to analyze how your brand is perceived.

For now, ChatGPT does not allow brands to pay for ads to get featured on its platforms. Unlike Google, ranking among the top results in ChatGPT depends entirely on strong SEO and GEO strategies.


Once you have a clear SEO plan (remember to prioritize Bing SEO over Google SEO) and defined GEO approach, you must start testing. The time it will take for your brand to appear among the first ChatGPT results will depend on the factors we just mentioned.
