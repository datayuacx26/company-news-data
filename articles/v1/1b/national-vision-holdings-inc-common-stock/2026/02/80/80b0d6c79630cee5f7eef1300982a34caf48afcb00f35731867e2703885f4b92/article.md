# National Vision Recognized by Newsweek as One of America’s Most Responsible Companies for Fourth Consecutive Year


February 25, 2026 in[Company News](https://www.nationalvision.com/news/category/7)


National Vision is proud to announce our inclusion, for the fourth year in a row, in Newsweek’s 2026 list of **America’s Most Responsible Companies** , reinforcing our belief that long-term success is inseparable from doing what’s right for our people, our communities and our planet.


Newsweek, in partnership with Statista, developed this year’s ranking to spotlight companies demonstrating meaningful action across environmental, social and governance (ESG) priorities based on a broad review of public disclosures and third-party data. The assessment considers factors such as sustainability reporting, financial transparency, compliance history and a range of ESG metrics used to evaluate overall responsibility.


We continue to put our purpose into action through initiatives that reduce waste and improve efficiency, expand access to affordable vision care and support underserved communities through services and partnerships.


In 2024, National Vision’s philanthropic work supported more than 1.1 million lives—because it’s the right thing to do for our business, the planet and society.


This honor reinforces our commitment to operating responsibly while advancing our mission to help people see better to live better. Visit our[Corporate Responsibility](https://www.nationalvision.com/corporate-responsibility/) page to explore National Vision’s commitments and progress.


To learn more about Newsweek’s America’s Most Responsible Companies 2026 list, visit[https://rankings.newsweek.com/americas-most-responsible-companies-2026](https://rankings.newsweek.com/americas-most-responsible-companies-2026) .
