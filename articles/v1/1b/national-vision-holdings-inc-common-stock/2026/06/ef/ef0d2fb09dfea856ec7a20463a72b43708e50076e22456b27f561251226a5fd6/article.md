# National Vision Celebrates Growth, Leadership and Momentum at 2026 Area Doctor, District and General Manager Meeting

# National Vision Celebrates Growth, Leadership and Momentum at 2026 Area Doctor, District and General Manager Meeting


June 3, 2026 in[Company News](https://www.nationalvision.com/news/category/7)


National Vision recently welcomed over 1,200 field leaders from across the country to Dallas for its 2026 annual meeting with General Managers, District Managers, Area Doctors – a high-energy, multi-day event focused on professional growth, operational excellence and the company’s ongoing transformation journey.


Built around the theme “Transforming for our Customers and Patients,” the meeting brought together leaders from across the organization to celebrate progress, strengthen skills and discuss the future of eye care at National Vision. Throughout the week, attendees engaged in impactful educational sessions, exchanged ideas and strengthened relationships across the organization reinforcing a shared commitment to delivering exceptional experiences for National Vision’s customers and patients.


Beyond development opportunities, the meeting also served as a powerful celebration of the wins achieved across the organization and the energy driving what’s next. Leaders from across brands and regions came together to recognize wins, share best practices and focus on the behaviors that matter most: leading with purpose, listening to understand and creating experiences that truly elevate the experience for customers.


“Our transformation journey continues to create meaningful momentum across the organization. What makes me most excited about the future is the passion, talent and dedication of our teams. When we invest in our people and lead with purpose, we create better experiences for our customers and patients while continuing to position National Vision for long-term growth.” -Alex Wilkes, CEO


The energy throughout the week reflected what associates value most about working at National Vision: the opportunity to grow, make a meaningful impact and be part of a collaborative, purpose-driven team focused on helping people see their best to live their best.


As the meeting concluded, leaders left Dallas energized, connected and ready to build on the company’s momentum – continuing to lead with purpose while shaping the next phase of National Vision’s transformation journey.


**About National Vision Holdings, Inc.**


National Vision Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: EYE) is one of the largest optical retail companies in the United States with over 1,200 stores in 40 states and Puerto Rico. With a mission of helping people by making quality eye care and eyewear more affordable and accessible, the company operates four retail brands:[America’s Best](https://www.americasbest.com/) ,[Eyeglass World](https://www.eyeglassworld.com/) , and Vista Opticals inside[select Fred Meyer stores](https://www.findvistaoptical.com/) and on[select military bases](https://www.militaryoptical.com/) , and an e-commerce website[DiscountContacts.com](https://www.discountcontacts.com/) , offering a variety of products and services for customers’ eye care needs.
