# Finding Family, Flexibility and Focus Through National Vision Network

# Finding Family, Flexibility and Focus Through National Vision Network


December 15, 2025 in[Perspectives](https://www.nationalvision.com/news/category/40)[Clinical Culture](https://www.nationalvision.com/news/category/5)[Hear from Our Doctors](https://www.nationalvision.com/news/category/6)


From the time she filled out an “About Me” worksheet in third grade, Dr. Stephanie Thai-Fernandez knew she wanted to become an eye doctor. That early confidence guided her through healthcare-focused programs in high school and college, ultimately leading her to Salus University Pennsylvania College of Optometry, where she graduated in 2021.


While in optometry school, Dr. Thai-Fernandez recognized that commercial practice suited her growing professional goals. After graduating, she joined an America’s Best clinic in Hatfield, Pennsylvania where she now serves as the primary doctor with a supportive team by her side.


“Private practice just doesn’t fit my lifestyle, especially as a first-time mom. I’m fortunate to have the flexibility to do what I love while balancing life outside of work.” Dr. Stephanie Thai-Fernandez.


Beyond her work in the exam room, Dr. Thai-Fernandez also serves as a National Vision ambassador, creating content for both current and aspiring optometrists, including a[“Day in the Life of an OD”](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/national-vision-inc._dayinthelife-stephanie-thai-activity-7387142514220302336-bXhW/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAABH7y1oBMpsa0xH8T3Mkm1uLPaAQd0oEyTQ) video and various blog posts. This role allows her to promote National Vision’s practice model while sharing practical advice as a passionate OD about career choices, work‑life balance and patient care.
