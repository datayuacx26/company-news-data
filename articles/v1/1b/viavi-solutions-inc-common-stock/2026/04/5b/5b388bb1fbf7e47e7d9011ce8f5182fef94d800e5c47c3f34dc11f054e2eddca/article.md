# VIAVI Partners with Ground Control to Enable Assured Maritime Vessel Tracking and Navigation in GNSS-Denied Environments

**Chandler, Ariz., April 2, 2026 -**[Viavi Solutions Inc.](https://www.viavisolutions.com/en-us/node/113452) (VIAVI) (NASDAQ: VIAV) today announced its partnership with satellite communications and positioning solutions provider[Ground Control](https://www.groundcontrol.com/) to integrate its[Secure µPNT™ STL-1000](https://www.viavisolutions.com/en-us/products/secure-micropnt-stl-1000-leo-receiver-module) into the[RockFLEET Assured](https://www.groundcontrol.com/product/rockfleet-assured/) asset tracking and assured navigation solution.


In today’s contested global environment, resilient Position, Navigation and Timing (PNT) capabilities are crucial to safeguard critical infrastructure and assets. Reliance on GPS/GNSS alone leaves systems vulnerable to jamming, spoofing and disruption. VIAVI’s Secure µPNT STL-1000 is a compact software-defined receiver designed to operate with the VIAVI[SecureTimeSM altGNSS LEO services](https://www.viavisolutions.com/en-us/products/securetime-leo-services) . Delivering precise timing with holdover capability, it enables tracking, authentication and assured navigation in Denied, Degraded, and Disrupted Space Operational Environment (D3SOE).


“With jamming and spoofing now a core element of cyber warfare, resilient PNT solutions are no longer optional,” said Doug Russell, Senior Vice President and General Manager, Aerospace and Defense, VIAVI. “The Secure µPNT STL-1000 enables assured, uninterrupted operations, especially in contested environments. Its compact size and low power consumption makes it ideal for applications that require an extremely small, low-power, secure, resilient embedded PNT receiver.”


“As the frequency of jamming and spoofing continues to rise, reliance on GPS/GNSS signals alone increasingly exposes both commercial and military operations to risk,” said Alastair MacLeod, CEO of Ground Control. “Integrating VIAVI’s Secure µPNT STL-1000 into RockFLEET Assured delivers a trusted secondary position source, strengthening resilience for mission‑critical operations across defense, maritime and critical infrastructure environments.”


RockFLEET Assured is a marine-grade Assured Position, Navigation and Timing (A-PNT) solution designed to support maritime vessel navigation and oversight in GNSS-denied environments.
