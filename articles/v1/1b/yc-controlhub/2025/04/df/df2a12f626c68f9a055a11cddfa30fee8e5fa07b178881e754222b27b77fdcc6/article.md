# Understanding Purchase Requisitions vs. Purchase Orders

## What is a Purchase Requisition?


### **Defining Purchase Requisition and its Purpose**


A purchase requisition is an essential internal document in the entire procurement process, serving as a formal request from an employee to the purchasing department. This internal request kickstarts the procurement cycle, aiming to secure approval for the acquisition of goods or services before any financial commitment is made by the finance department.


The purchase requisition form acts as the preliminary step in the purchase order process, ensuring that the purchasing policies of the company are adhered to from the outset.


### **The Workflow of Purchase Requisition Processes**


The purchase requisition workflow is a critical component of efficient procurement processes. It begins with an internal request from an employee or department head, who identifies a need and fills out a purchase requisition form.


This form is a detailed document that outlines the specific requirements for the purchase request, including the delivery address and suggested vendors. Once this form is completed, it undergoes an approval process, where it is reviewed by the necessary department heads and the finance team to ensure compliance with company policies. After approval, the purchasing department moves forward with the purchase order creation, marking a transition from an internal document to an external document intended for the supplier.


### **Essential Information on a Purchase Requisition Form**


A purchase requisition form must include comprehensive details to support the purchasing request, facilitating a smooth approval process. This information typically encompasses the description of goods or services, the quantity required, the budget allocation, suggested vendors, and the desired delivery date.


This level of detail is crucial for the finance department and purchasing department to review the request adequately, ensuring that it aligns with business outcomes and purchasing policies.


### **Why Do You Need a Purchase Requisition?**


Implementing purchase requisitions within a business is vital for maintaining financial control and supporting efficient procurement processes. They act as a safeguard against unauthorized purchases, ensuring that every purchase request aligns with the business's strategic goals and budgetary constraints.


Additionally, the process of submitting and approving purchase requisitions creates an audit trail, which is invaluable for financial audits and maintaining transparency within the finance department. This paper trail also aids in tracking procurement KPIs, reflecting on the business's purchasing efficiency and effectiveness.


### **Sample Purchase Requisition Form**


Providing a sample purchase requisition form or a downloadable template can be a significant aid for businesses, especially for those transitioning from a manual purchase requisition process to an automated system, like purchase order software. Such templates serve as a guide for what information needs to be included, ensuring that all purchasing requests are comprehensive and in line with company policies.


This not only streamlines the approval process but also ensures that the purchasing department and payable team have all the necessary details to execute the purchase order process effectively, transforming the purchase requisition into a binding contract with the vendor.


## What is a Purchase Order?


### **Defining Purchase Orders and Its Significance**


A purchase order (PO) is a critical external document in the procurement process, created by the purchasing department and sent from a buyer to a seller. It outlines the buyer's intention to purchase specific types of goods or services, detailing quantities, and agreed prices, and functions as a legal document binding both parties to the transaction. The creation of a purchase order marks a formal request for goods or services and is pivotal in the entire procurement process, setting clear expectations for the delivery of products or services.


### **Key Information Incorporated in Purchase Orders**


A purchase order spells out what's being bought, how much of it, and at what price. It's a form that lists everything needed from a seller, including:


- **PO Number** : A unique identifier for the order.
- **Item Descriptions** : What the items are, clearly described.
- **Quantities** : How many of each item.
- **Prices** : How much each item costs.
- **Delivery Address** : Where to send the order.
- **Payment Terms** : How and when payment will be made.


These bits of info are key for the purchasing and finance departments to handle the order efficiently, ensuring everything runs smoothly from the moment the order is placed until the payment is sent out after delivery.


### **Why Do You Need a Purchase Order?**


The importance of purchase orders extends beyond the simple intention to buy. As a legal document, it serves as a binding contract between the buyer and seller, providing a clear audit trail that is crucial for financial audits and managing the company's paper trail. Purchase orders are instrumental in preventing unauthorized purchases by ensuring every transaction is recorded and approved through a formal approval process. This level of documentation is key for the finance department to verify transactions against deliveries, enhancing financial control and accountability within the company.


**The Purchase Order Process and Its Role in Business**


The purchase order process begins with the purchasing department converting an approved purchase requisition into a purchase order. This external document is then sent to the vendor as a confirmation of the purchase, specifying all necessary details to ensure the correct delivery of products or services. The process underscores the key differences between purchase requisitions (an internal request) and purchase orders (a formal request to a vendor), highlighting the transition from internal planning to external execution in the procurement cycle.


**Sample Purchase Order Form**


Providing businesses with a sample purchase order form or template can significantly streamline the purchasing process. Such templates ensure that the purchasing officer includes all necessary information, facilitating clear communication between the business and its suppliers. This not only speeds up the order creation but also minimizes errors and discrepancies, leading to more efficient procurement processes and better business outcomes.
