# Top 11 ERP Ecommerce Software Solutions for Businesses

## **What is an ERP System?**


An[ERP](https://www.controlhub.com/blog/top-erp-ecommerce-software) (Enterprise Resource Planning) system is software that helps businesses manage and connect all their main activities. In online businesses, it puts all key functions like accounting, stock control, sales, HR, and production under one system. This makes everything more streamlined and integrated.


### Why[ERP Systems](https://www.controlhub.com/blog/sap-vs-erp-differences) Matter for Online Businesses


ERP systems are vital for online businesses because they organize operations and make data easy to see and use across the whole company. They help managers make better decisions by providing complete, real-time data on important areas like stock levels, order processing, customer interactions, and[money matters](https://www.controlhub.com/blog/direct-indirect-procurement) . By bringing all this data into one place, ERP systems cut out inefficiencies that happen when different departments use different systems.


### How ERP Systems Boost Efficiency and Sales


[ERP](https://www.controlhub.com/blog/sap-vs-erp-differences) systems improve a business’s efficiency by automating regular tasks and simplifying complicated ones. This lets businesses focus more on growth and keeping customers happy. By managing everything from taking orders to shipping products in one system,[ERPs](https://www.controlhub.com/blog/sap-vs-erp-differences) help avoid mistakes and delays, enhance customer service, and drive sales and profits up. This is crucial for online businesses, where fast and accurate service is key to staying ahead of competitors.


## **Differences Between ERP and Other Systems like SAP**


### What is an ERP System?


An[ERP](https://www.controlhub.com/blog/is-sap-a-crm-or-erp-unraveling-the-mystery) (Enterprise Resource Planning) system is a software tool that helps companies manage and automate various functions across their business from a single platform. This includes departments like finance, human resources, manufacturing, and supply chain management.


‍


The primary purpose of an ERP system is to increase efficiency by integrating all these processes into one comprehensive system that shares a common database. This connectivity ensures that all departments have access to the same updated information, facilitating better decision-making and reducing the redundancy and errors that can occur when departments work in silos.


### What is SAP?


[SAP](https://www.controlhub.com/blog/sap-vs-erp-differences) , on the other hand, is a company that provides a range of software solutions, including ERP systems.[SAP's](https://www.controlhub.com/blog/is-sap-a-crm-or-erp-unraveling-the-mystery) offerings are not limited to ERP; they also include specialized business applications like customer relationship management (CRM), business warehousing (BW), and advanced ERP solutions like SAP S/4HANA. While SAP is a major player in the ERP market, it represents just one brand among many that offer ERP solutions.


### Key Differences Between ERP and[SAP](https://www.controlhub.com/blog/is-sap-a-crm-or-erp-unraveling-the-mystery)


‍


**Scope and Functionality:**


‍


- **ERP Systems:** Broad in scope, ERP systems are designed to integrate all primary business processes into a single unified system. They are typically modular, meaning they consist of different parts that can handle various business functions but are designed to work together seamlessly.
- **SAP:** Refers to the company itself and its suite of products, which includes ERP solutions among other software tools. SAP provides specific applications tailored to different business needs, which can be integrated but are developed as distinct products.


‍


**Integration vs. Specialization**


‍


- **ERP Systems:** Focus on integrating all business processes to eliminate data silos, improve communication across departments, and streamline operations across the organization.
- **SAP:** While offering integrated solutions, SAP also emphasizes specialized applications that address specific aspects of business operations, providing tools that are particularly tailored to complex business processes or industries.


‍


**Market Position**


‍


- **ERP Systems:** A generic term for a type of software used across various industries and by companies of all sizes. Many companies, from Oracle to Microsoft, provide ERP systems.
- **SAP:** A specific brand within the ERP market known for its comprehensive and powerful solutions that often lead the market in terms of innovation and scalability.
