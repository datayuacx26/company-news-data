# Requisition Numbers 101: The Key to Smarter Procurement

## Best Practices for Managing Requisition Numbers


‍


### Keep formats consistent


A standardized format is key. If one team uses “REQ2025-0031” while another uses “R-31,” your system quickly becomes messy and unreliable.


Choose a single format that fits your structure, something that shows the type of request, the year, and its sequence, and make sure everyone follows it.


This consistency keeps things searchable and makes it easier for teams to understand what each number represents without guessing.


### Avoid reusing numbers


This one is easy to overlook but incredibly important.


Once a requisition number is used, it should never appear again.


Even if the request gets canceled, keep that number archived as-is. Reusing it later can create confusion during audits or when generating reports.


Treat requisition numbers like purchase order numbers or invoice IDs; they’re unique identifiers that help you track the full history of every request accurately.


### Tie numbers to detailed request data


Here’s where you can really strengthen your process.


Every requisition number should connect to all the key details: who made the request, what was requested, when it was submitted, who approved it, and which supplier it involved.


Having all that information linked to one number makes tracing the full lifecycle of a purchase effortless. It also helps during audits or supplier reviews when you need context fast.


### Integrate into approval workflows


Ensure that requisition numbers are automatically displayed in your approval system.


When each comment, decision, or update links to the same number, everyone is on the same page. This transparency reduces backtracking, prevents miscommunication, and helps managers make faster decisions.


### Store securely for audits and reporting


Finally, keep all requisition records in a centralized, secure system.


Avoid spreadsheets or scattered folders; they can be a nightmare during audits. A digital, searchable log saves hours of frustration and gives you confidence that nothing gets lost in the shuffle.
