# Punchout Catalog: What you need to know

Wasting time is no fun and we can bet the feeling of wasting money is even worse.


Especially for businesses that have a lot to handle, deadlines to rush, and clients to satisfy.


That’s why we’ll be discussing all the key details you need to know about the PunchOut catalog.


Have you already heard about it? Feeling maybe confused about its differences with a regular e-commerce catalog?


Then you better keep reading on


## What is a PunchOut Catalog?


Never mind the seemingly complicated name. A PunchOut catalog is quite simple to understand.


It’s just a sort of digital catalog that gives you the opportunity to access a supplier’s list of products directly from your procurement system.


One of the biggest benefits of a[PunchOut catalog](https://www.controlhub.com/blog/punchout-catalogs-vs-hosted-catalogs) is that you can quickly go over the supplier’s website to view their latest products, pricing, and availability in real-time, make your choice and immediately return to your procurement system with your chosen items ready for approval and purchase.


‍


Quite helpful isn\`t?


## How does a PunchOut Catalog works?


The first thing is to have an integration implemented between your[procurement](https://www.controlhub.com/blog/5-rights-of-procurement) system and the supplier’s catalog, this is usually done relying on protocols like[cXML](https://www.controlhub.com/blog/cxml-oci-punchout-languages) (commerce eXtensible Markup Language).


‍


Once this is completed, there are some couple of steps left.


### Visit


If the integration has already been achieved, you can simply access to PunchOut catalog directly from your system and browse at leisure all the products the supplier has to offer. You can even apply filters and search for specific items, just like you would on a traditional e-commerce site.


### Selection


As you browse, you can add items to your cart, view detailed product descriptions, and check real-time pricing. When you are done shopping, it’s time to submit a requisition for approval.


### Approval


This is where you start to see the benefits, your requisition will be automatically filled with the details of your order, approved with an integrated workflow and obtain a[purchase order](https://www.controlhub.com/blog/purchase-order) without any additional effort. Your purchase is ready to go then.


### Receipt of goods


When your order is approved, the supplier will fulfill it just like any other order. The difference is that the entire process was automated and managed through your[procurement](https://www.controlhub.com/blog/what-is-procurement) system, making it a lot easier for you and your team.
