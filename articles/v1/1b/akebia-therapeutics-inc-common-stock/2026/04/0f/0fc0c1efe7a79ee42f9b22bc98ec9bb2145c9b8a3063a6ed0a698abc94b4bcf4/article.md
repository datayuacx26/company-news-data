# Akebia Therapeutics to Report First Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Discuss Recent Business Highlights


April 30, 2026 at 4:05 PM EDT


Akebia to Host Conference Call on May 7 at 8:00 a.m. EDT


CAMBRIDGE, Mass., April 30, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Akebia Therapeutics®, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=ogrFb6IbmHPLh1LwcPu1eqZSN-Ccn6vcaVzMR-aORDMRwSPoSVLrbexTl_nvph1bbpbyOFD5mwsYBnwuqMNbki8JSA_kx-S88TxLnYSAs6o=) (Nasdaq: AKBA) today announced plans to release its financial results for the first quarter ended March 31, 2026 on Thursday, May 7, 2026, prior to the open of financial markets.


Akebia will host a conference call on Thursday, May 7, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. EDT to discuss its financial results and recent business highlights.
