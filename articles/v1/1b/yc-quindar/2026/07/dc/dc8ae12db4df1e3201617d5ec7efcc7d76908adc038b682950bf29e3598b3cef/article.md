# Clearer Payload Health, Fleet Planning, and Runbook Workflows for Mission Operations

Blog


July 7, 2026


Amber Wesdorp


Mission teams work best when the operational picture is easy to read and the next step is easy to take.


With Quindar v0.36, we are continuing to refine the workflows teams use to monitor health, review work that needs attention, and move through day-to-day mission operations with less friction.


This release focuses on five practical improvements across the Asset Page, Fleet Planning, and Runbooks — updates designed to make important context easier to find and routine actions easier to complete.


**Clearer health visibility where operators already work**


AQUA system dashboard displaying payload health and operational status. Several payloads are marked as emergency while others remain nominal.


Quindar v0.36 adds a new Payload Health tab to the Asset Page Status Details drawer, giving teams a clearer, more scannable view of payload status alongside existing status history. Instead of bouncing between disconnected views to understand payload condition, operators can review that detail inside the workflow they already use to investigate asset status.


A real-time spacecraft fleet management dashboard displaying asset health, tasks, and ticket status. The interface provides mission-critical insights at a glance.


Fleet Planning also becomes easier to scan with a new Asset Health column in the table below the timeline. That helps teams spot which assets may need attention faster, especially when managing multiple spacecraft or reviewing plan changes across the fleet.


**A more readable operational picture on the timeline**


A mission planning timeline displaying scheduled contacts and tasks. The interface highlights communication windows and availability across several hours.


This release also improves how work is represented visually on the Gantt timeline. Tasks scheduled for specific start and stop times are restyled so they are easier to distinguish from contacts at a glance.


That kind of clarity matters during busy operational workflows. When operators can interpret the timeline faster, they spend less time parsing the interface and more time making decisions.


**Cleaner navigation for configuration and status details**


C2 Configuration panel displaying AQUA Adapter details and system status. The interface shows operational health and adapter metadata.


Quindar v0.36 also streamlines how teams access configuration details by converting the Connection String experience into a C2 Configuration drawer. Rather than sending users through a page-based workflow, the update keeps that information closer to the operational context where it is needed.


The result is a cleaner path to review configuration details without unnecessary navigation.


**Faster review workflows from signal to action**


A runbook management dashboard displaying an active anomaly response. Filters and execution details help teams monitor automation workflows.


Manual review work also becomes more actionable in v0.36. The Manual Reviews card now surfaces a count of items that need review, and clicking through can take users directly into Runbooks with the relevant review-status filter applied.


That reduces the friction between seeing that work exists and getting to the exact place where it can be handled. For mission teams balancing multiple priorities, that means less hunting and a faster path from awareness to action.


**Built for modern space operations**


Taken together, these updates make Quindar more readable, more direct, and more operationally useful in the moments that matter.


Quindar is the future of mission management. v0.36 strengthens visibility and control by making health context easier to access, review workflows easier to act on, and everyday operator workflows easier to navigate.


For mission teams working to stay mission-ready, that kind of clarity matters.
