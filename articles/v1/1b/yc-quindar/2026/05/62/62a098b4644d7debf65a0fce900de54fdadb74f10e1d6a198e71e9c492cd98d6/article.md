# Quindar Awarded U.S. Space Force Contract to Rapidly Design and Deliver Capabilities to Support Golden Dome

News


May 5, 2026


**\[Arvada, CO\] –** Quindar, the company purpose-built to revolutionize space enterprise systems, has been selected as one of the awardees to design and deliver capabilities for the U.S. Space Force’s Space-Based Interceptor Program in support of Golden Dome of America – one of the U.S. government’s most critical efforts to rapidly advance resilient, next-generation space capabilities.


“Golden Dome demands a fundamentally different approach,” said Nate Hamet, CEO of Quindar. “This is not about operating a single satellite constellation in a closed system – it’s about orchestrating a heterogenous constellation across distributed networks securely and in real time. We built Quindar from the ground up for that scale and complexity.”


While traditional providers have developed systems tightly coupled to their own spacecraft, Quindar delivers unified solutions designed to scale across heterogeneous constellations and mission environments.


“Our advantage is simple – we’re not building for a future concept. We’re operating real constellations today, and those missions get every capability first,” added Hamet. “That feedback loop is what allows us to deliver a system that actually works at scale.”


This award reinforces Quindar’s position as an enterprise leader in next-generation space capabilities – moving the world from siloed, hardware-bound systems to fully integrated, scalable, software-defined infrastructure.


## **About Quindar**


Quindar builds solutions to automate and scale satellite operations. Designed for hybrid, multi-domain missions, Quindar enables operators to manage space assets like modern cloud infrastructure – across any satellite, any network, and at constellation scale. The company supports both commercial and national security missions, with many active missions operating in space today.
