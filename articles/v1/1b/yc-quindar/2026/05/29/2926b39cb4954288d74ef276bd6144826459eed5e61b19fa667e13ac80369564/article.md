# A Stronger Operational Picture for Every Mission

Blog


May 13, 2026


Kathleen Mueller, Product manager


With new Mission Control capabilities that deliver a more unified operational picture, and an AskSage integration that brings secure, mission-aware AI assistance into the platform with IL5-certified and FedRAMP-authorized security, Quindar’s latest release is designed to improve both day-to-day execution and long-term operational resiliency.


**Mission Control: A Common Operating Picture**


Mission Control is designed to answer two essential questions: “What needs my attention right now?” and “Is my fleet, and the ground systems supporting it, healthy?” It brings the most important operational signals into a single view so teams can assess system health


faster, focus on the right issues sooner, and maintain a stronger common operating picture across the mission.


*A full view of Quindar Mission Control*


A key part of that experience is a 3D global visualization of all assets with a valid TLE, giving operators an immediate view of fleet position and propagation. This adds important context to the operational picture by helping teams quickly orient themselves and understand where assets are in relation to ongoing operations.


Mission Control also provides health summaries across assets, network, and infrastructure, using clear statuses like emergency, warning, and nominal to communicate where action may be needed. This provides not just visibility, but clarity: emergency states highlight active critical issues, while warnings surface conditions that may become more serious if not addressed proactively. Operators have a faster way to identify risk and respond before it escalates.


A close up view of the Asset, Network, and Infrastructure health statuses


The asset health summary helps teams quickly understand the condition of spacecraft across the fleet, making it easier to spot which vehicles need attention and where issues are developing. The network health summary, currently focused on ground stations, helps surface provider issues that may require contacts to be rescheduled or adjusted. The infrastructure summary extends visibility to the supporting services and architecture behind automation, helping teams identify issues that could affect ongoing workflows or lights-out operations.


*A close up view of the bottom portion of the screen, highlighting Tickets, Manual Reviews, System Events, and Space Weather*


Mission Control also highlights open tickets, requests, and runbooks, making it easier to see where manual action is needed to keep workflows moving. And by surfacing space weather directly in the operational view, teams can better incorporate environmental conditions into fleet planning without having to rely on another disconnected source of information.


Mission Control providers operators with clearer read on the current situation, with actionable paths into more detailed views for investigation and action. This helps operators move faster, manage risk more effectively, and maintain stronger resiliency across the mission.


**Secure, Domain-Aware AI Assistance with AskSage**


*An example question sent to Quindar Assistant*


This release expands Quindar’s LLM capabilities with secure, domain-aware AI assistance built for mission environments from AskSage. With IL5-certified capabilities and FedRAMP-aligned security, the integration helps organizations adopt AI support within the compliance frameworks required for government and defense-oriented operations. As always, the Quindar Assistant gives teams more context-aware support for analysis, troubleshooting, and operational decision-making, while staying aligned with the security and mission requirements these workflows demand.


**Turning Visibility Into Action**


Together, these enhancements make it easier for teams to see what matters, act on what needs attention, and do so within the security and operational constraints their missions require. The result is a more resilient, more informed operating environment—one that supports faster decisions, stronger workflows, and a clearer path toward scalable, lights-out operations.
