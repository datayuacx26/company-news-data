# Apollo Impact Mission: 2025 Annual Impact Report

Share


## Apollo Impact Mission (AIM)’s 2025 Impact Report highlights Apollo’s approach to driving scalable and measurable impact through the AIM platform.


Built on Apollo’s decades of[private equity](https://www.apollo.com/strategies/asset-management/equity/private-equity) experience, integrated investment capabilities and commitment to Sustainability,[AIM](https://www.apollo.com/strategies/asset-management/equity/impact-investing-platform) is designed to pursue attractive, risk-adjusted returns alongside positive, measurable social and environmental outcomes.[AIM](https://www.apollo.com/strategies/asset-management/equity/impact-investing-platform) applies a disciplined impact framework centered on two core themes: helping people and healing the planet.


In AIM’s fifth annual Impact Report, we share the progress that AIM’s portfolio companies have made and outline how Apollo continues to measure, manage and enhance impact across the platform’s investments.

### Impact Investing Platform


The Apollo Impact Mission strategy platform (AIM) pursues private equity opportunities that seek to generate positive, measurable social and/or environmental impact at scale while delivering attractive risk-adjusted returns. We adhere to a rigorous impact investment strategy that targets investments centered around two critical objectives – helping people and healing the planet.
