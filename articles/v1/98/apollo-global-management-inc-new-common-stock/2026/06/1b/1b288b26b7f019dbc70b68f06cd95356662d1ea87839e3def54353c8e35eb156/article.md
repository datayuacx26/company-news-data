## As traditional private equity strategies face longer holding periods, a record exit backlog and slower distributions, secondaries are becoming an increasingly relevant complement.


On this episode of The View From Apollo’s Allocation Insights series,[Alex Wright](https://www.apollo.com/aboutus/leadership-and-people/alexander-wright) and[Diana Sands](https://www.apollo.com/aboutus/leadership-and-people/diana-sands) are joined by Partner and Co-Head of Apollo's[Sponsor & Secondary Solutions (S3)](https://www.apollo.com/strategies/asset-management/equity/secondaries) business **[Steve Lessar](https://www.apollo.com/aboutus/leadership-and-people/steve-lessar)** to discuss how private market secondaries have evolved from a niche liquidity tool to a core allocation of modern private market portfolios, which can offer immediate exposure to mature assets and attractive risk-adjusted return potential.


The *View from Apollo* features conversations with thought leaders across Apollo and portfolio companies of funds managed by Apollo, each bringing their unique perspectives on current macroeconomic trends, the impact to various businesses and what it can mean for investors.
