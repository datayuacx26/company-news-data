# This Valentine’s Day, Protect What You Love

## Valentine’s Day is the perfect time to show your love and appreciation to those who matter most. Gifts like engagement rings, luxury jewelry, and high-end purchases are often exchanged, so it’s crucial to ensure that these valuable items are protected. Whether you’re newly engaged or simply making an important purchase this season, Donegal Insurance is here to make sure your most treasured possessions are safe.


**Protect Your Jewelry and High-End Purchases**


Donegal Insurance provides specialized jewelry coverage that can be added to your homeowners’ insurance. This additional coverage can protect your high-value items from risks like theft, loss, or damage. Don’t assume your standard homeowners’ policy will cover a diamond ring or other valuable gifts. Protecting your investment with personal property insurance ensures that if something unexpected happens, you’re financially covered.


**Remember to Keep Documentation**


When purchasing high-value items, it’s important to keep proof of purchase, including receipts and any appraisals. Having documentation helps establish proof of loss and confirms ownership of the item, making claims simpler and faster. Photos of valuable items along with receipts and a written inventory can be extremely helpful in case something goes wrong.


### **Newlyweds: Plan for the Future with the Right Insurance**


A recent engagement or marriage often brings new commitments and important financial considerations. As a newlywed, it’s the perfect time to reassess your insurance needs. Make sure your homeowners’ or renters’ insurance is updated to reflect your shared belongings. This is particularly important if you’ve made high-value purchases together, such as furniture, electronics, or other significant gifts.


**Protect Your Home and Property**


Your home is likely your most valuable asset, and Valentine’s Day celebrations can mean more valuables are entering or leaving your home. Whether it’s gifts or new purchases, ensure your home and property are protected with the right coverage. Regardless of if you own or rent, insurance can protect you from unexpected risks such as fire, theft, and storm damage.


If you’ve added new items like jewelry or expensive electronics, be sure they are covered under your current policy. Review your coverage regularly to ensure it aligns with your needs.


### **Auto Insurance: Coverage for Your Valentine’s Day Plans**


Valentine’s Day often means travel, whether you’re driving to a dinner date, shopping for last-minute gifts, or heading out of town for a romantic weekend. With Donegal’s auto insurance, you can drive with confidence knowing you’re covered.


If you’re a business owner or delivery service, commercial auto coverage is also key, especially when transporting high-value items like gifts or luxury products during busy shopping seasons.


### **Business Insurance: Safeguard Your Valentine’s Day Sales**


For small business owners, Valentine’s Day can bring a significant increase in foot traffic and sales, particularly if you’re selling jewelry or luxury gifts. Donegal’s commercial insurance can protect against risks like property damage, theft, or liability claims.


### **Review Your Coverage**


This Valentine’s Day, take a moment to review your insurance policies with your agent and ensure that the things you love are properly protected. Donegal Insurance offers comprehensive coverage options tailored to your needs, so you can enjoy peace of mind knowing that you’re fully covered.
