## How the Popl enrichment engine achieves 95%+ match rates


****


Popl's approach to list enrichment technology is fundamentally different from legacy providers. Here's how:


### 20+ data partners working in sequence


Rather than checking one database and stopping there, the Popl enrichment engine runs every lead through more than 20 hand-picked premium data partners. The process relies on[waterfall enrichment](https://popl.co/pages/waterfall-enrichment?) : if one provider doesn't have the email, the next one is queried. If the phone number isn't available from that source, another specialist fills it in.


Each partner in the waterfall specializes in specific data types, including work emails, mobile numbers, LinkedIn profiles, and company firmographics. This means the right source is queried for the right field, rather than relying on a generalist database to cover everything. The result is significantly higher data enrichment accuracy across all output fields.


All you need to provide is a first name and a company name. From there, the engine takes over.


### AI agents that close the gap


Even the best data providers have blind spots. When the waterfall doesn't return a match, Popl's AI agents step in. These agents are each purpose-built for a specific task, searching for particular data points in real time and applying intelligent email prediction to fill gaps that traditional databases miss.


This is what lifts Popl's[lead enrichment](https://popl.co/pages/lead-enrichment?srsltid=AfmBOoqPJK8ntGzMeoY9dImzjqPDF_GXHhI1vohvnueBp58N5UrIPFDa) match rate from the industry standard of 65% to 95%+. The AI layer searches, cross-references, and validates in real time before returning a result. Every badge scan and list enrichment upload to Popl's platform improves the engine, building on more than 1 million event badge scans processed across every major industry.


****


### Built-in data validation


A high match rate only matters if the data is accurate. That's why validation is built directly into the Popl enrichment engine.


Before any email is returned, it passes through a proprietary letter-matching algorithm and email verification system. If an email can't be verified as deliverable, it isn't returned. The same rigorous process applies to phone numbers and company data. If Popl enriches it, you can email it.


This commitment to event data quality means your team isn't wasting time sending emails into a void or calling disconnected numbers. Every contact returned is ready to act on.
