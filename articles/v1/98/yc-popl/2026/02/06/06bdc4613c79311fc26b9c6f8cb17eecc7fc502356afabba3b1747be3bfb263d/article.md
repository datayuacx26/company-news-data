## 7 AI tools reshaping event lead capture in 2026


The shift from traditional event lead capture methods to AI-powered solutions underscores sales teams’ growing focus on driving pipeline and measuring ROI in 2026.


Traditional solutions like paper business cards, paper forms, and rental badge scanners fall short in both areas because leads rarely make it into CRMs. Papers get lost, errors are common with manual data entry, and spreadsheets take hours to clean and enrich. As a result, follow-ups get delayed (if they happen at all), and event ROI is hard to track.
