### **Cvent Lead Capture**


**Best for:** Teams already using Cvent for event logistics and registration.


-


Strong badge scanning capabilities


-


Deep integration with the Cvent ecosystem


-


Best suited for **Cvent-managed events**


-


Requires additional configuration for CRM syncing


-


Less flexible for non-booth or non-Cvent scenarios


**TL;DR:** Cvent is powerful inside its own walls, but lacks the flexibility, enrichment, and day-of usability that modern field teams need across all event types.


### **Captello**


**Best for:** Organizations that prioritize **gamification and engagement activations** at their booth.


-


Offers customizable games and interactive experiences


-


Includes lead capture via badge or manual entry


-


CRM integration available (with setup)


-


Lead enrichment depends on integrations


-


More setup-heavy, with a steeper learning curve for teams


**TL;DR:** Captello is a fit for booth engagement, but not ideal for scalable, rep-driven lead capture across dinners, activations, and meetings.


### **Mobly**


**Best for:** Budget-conscious teams focused on basic badge or form capture.


-


Simple mobile lead capture tools


-


Limited enrichment or CRM sync features


-


Minimal setup, but also minimal customization


-


Better suited for smaller teams or local activations


**TL;DR:** Mobly is a starter option, but lacks the depth and automation that enterprise or scaling teams require to maximize event ROI.
