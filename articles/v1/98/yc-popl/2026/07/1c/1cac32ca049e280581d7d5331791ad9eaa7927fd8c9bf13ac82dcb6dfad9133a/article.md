# Popl and RocketReach Announce Landmark Data Partnership with First-Ever Category Exclusivity Option

[Popl,](https://popl.co/blogs/all/popl.co) the leading AI platform for event lead capture and in-person go-to-market, and RocketReach, a global leader in B2B data, today announced a strategic data partnership anchored by a category-exclusive option agreement.


RocketReach operates what the company describes as the world’s largest person-level dataset, spanning 700 million professional profiles.


Through the agreement, Popl and RocketReach have created a long-term strategic framework that gives Popl the option to secure exclusive access to RocketReach’s 700 million professional profiles across event lead capture and in-person GTM. The companies believe it is the first publicly announced agreement of its kind in the B2B data enrichment industry.


The partnership will strengthen Popl’s AI-native data engine today while creating opportunities to expand into prospecting, pre-event intelligence, signals, and new AI-powered experiences built around in-person interactions.


“We call RocketReach ‘the everything provider,’” said Jason Alco, founder and CEO of Popl. “Most data providers specialize in one data category, such as email addresses or phone numbers. RocketReach spans the entire data ecosystem, from contact and company intelligence to healthcare professional data, prospecting APIs, signals, and much more. The breadth and depth of their data are extraordinary.”


“We’ve only begun to tap into what RocketReach’s data can unlock,” Alco continued. “As AI becomes embedded in our in-person interactions, the possibilities for what Popl and RocketReach can build together are endless.”


"Popl is the leading provider for in-person events. Their product is unmatched, and this partnership sets the foundation for both companies to capture the tailwinds around the evolution of GTM. We're excited to work strategically with Popl's team, in supporting their product roadmap," said Scott Kim, CEO of RocketReach.


## Powering the In-Person AI Revolution


Popl’s AI-native platform lets teams scan event badges and capture face-to-face conversations, transforming those interactions into verified professional profiles complete with contact and company data, conversational intelligence, CRM context, and clear next steps.


RocketReach serves as a primary data source across every part of Popl’s enrichment engine, supplying contact, company, phone, healthcare, and prospecting data across more than 700 million professional profiles. The combination of scalable economics and millisecond API response times enables Popl to enrich millions of interactions in real time, making RocketReach uniquely suited for in-person use cases.


The companies also intend to expand their work beyond enrichment and into pre-event prospecting. Using RocketReach’s data, Popl will help teams identify and enrich exhibiting companies before an event, uncover the right decision-makers within those accounts, and build targeted outreach lists before anyone arrives on the show floor. This will allow revenue teams to prioritize who they want to meet, begin conversations in advance, and give AI agents the contact and company context needed to make each in-person interaction more valuable.


Through the option agreement, both companies gain the flexibility to deepen the partnership as the category grows, moving beyond a conventional vendor relationship toward long-term strategic alignment.


## About Popl


Popl is the leading AI platform for event lead capture and in-person go-to-market. Its universal badge scanner, proprietary data engine, event intelligence, AI recorder, CRM integrations, and AI agents help revenue teams convert face-to-face interactions into measurable pipeline.


## About RocketReach

Powered by proprietary technology, RocketReach provides access to over 700 million contacts and 60 million companies worldwide. Sales, recruiting, and marketing teams rely on RocketReach to connect directly with the right people and decision-makers.
