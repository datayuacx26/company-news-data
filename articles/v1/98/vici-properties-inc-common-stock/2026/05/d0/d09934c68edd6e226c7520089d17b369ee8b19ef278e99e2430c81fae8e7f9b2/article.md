# Greenberg Traurig Represents Blake L. Sartini In Acquisition of Golden Entertainment and Its Transition to Private, Family-Owned Operation

LAS VEGAS


, May 4, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Global law firm[Greenberg Traurig, LLP](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4680127-1&h=968838409&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.gtlaw.com%2F&a=Greenberg+Traurig%2C+LLP) represented Blake L. Sartini in the successful completion of Mr. Sartini's transition of Golden Entertainment to a private, family-owned operation following the close of a transaction involving Golden Entertainment and[VICI](https://www.stocktitan.net/overview/VICI/) Properties Inc. (NYSE:[VICI](https://www.stocktitan.net/overview/VICI/) ). The transaction closed April 30 with Golden Entertainment shareholders receiving a $2.75 cash dividend and 0.902 shares of[VICI](https://www.stocktitan.net/overview/VICI/) stock per share of Golden stock.


In connection with the transaction, VICI Properties acquired the real estate assets associated with seven of Golden Entertainment's casino


resorts and entered into a triple-net master lease with entities controlled by Mr. Sartini. Golden Entertainment will continue to operate its full casino


and tavern portfolio going forward.


"We were honored to represent Blake L. Sartini in returning Golden Entertainment to private family ownership. We congratulate Blake and the entire Golden Entertainment team!" said Greenberg Traurig[Las Vegas](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4680127-1&h=310244040&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.gtlaw.com%2Fen%2Flocations%2Flas-vegas&a=Las+Vegas) Co-Managing Shareholder[Michael J.
