# The Chefs’ Warehouse to Announce Second Quarter 2026 Results on July 29, 2026


RIDGEFIELD, Conn., July 15, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- The Chefs’ Warehouse, Inc. (NASDAQ: CHEF) today announced that the Company intends to release its financial results for the second quarter ended June 26, 2026 before the opening of the stock market on Wednesday, July 29, 2026 and host a conference call at 8:30 a.m. ET on Wednesday, July 29, 2026 to review those results.


The conference call will be webcast live from the Company’s investor relations website at[http://investors.chefswarehouse.com/](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=AB2QhYh_6oSXHcwEs_u05AEMjTAyt03u1-sbfvQWIOZIfuF7Yw289JySvuns9OF54t2yVzMlt7qIQouWe5ag1ihAh9-NxOmWjESQ7dvo2BtIKs8ZcvZyHELaC1C7_h1UW8qj_ZEjsg_dmFSjejdBQA==) . An online archive of the webcast will be available on the Company’s investor relations website for 30 days.
