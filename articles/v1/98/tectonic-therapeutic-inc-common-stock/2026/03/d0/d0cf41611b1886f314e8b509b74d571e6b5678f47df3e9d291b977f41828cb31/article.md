## Tectonic Therapeutic to Participate in March Investor Conferences


WATERTOWN, Mass., March 02, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Tectonic Therapeutic](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=USLknVc-Rj1NyzzPkYCS4saYNxB3qWTNWram5XZzVc8lv6ZK4bGP_Hr2QrCTl86fRgQtcL59xK-hp9t_ZuV-mBDa7MT6LIYvFt10qoqDCoY=) , Inc. (NASDAQ: TECX) (“Tectonic”), a clinical stage biotechnology company focused on the discovery and development of therapeutic proteins and antibodies that modulate the activity of G-protein coupled receptors (GPCRs), today announced that company management will participate in two upcoming investor conferences taking place in March 2026.


**TD Cowen 46th Annual Health Care Conference**


Date: March 4, 2026


Time: 11:10 AM EST


Location: Boston, MA


Format: Fireside Chat


Presenters: Alise Reicin, MD, President and Chief Executive Officer


Webcast:[Link](https://event.summitcast.com/view/9z5g2VrV6e6rbCqQgDRoHA/guest_book?session_id=34jWUcefXrux5HJjQPvQAz)


**Leerink Global Healthcare Conference**


**Date:** March 11, 2026


**Time:** 1:40 PM EST


**Location:** Miami, FL


**Format:** Fireside Chat


**Presenters:** Alise Reicin, MD, President and Chief Executive Officer


**Webcast:**[Link](https://event.summitcast.com/view/mT9poctHDNthc6b89WqVjf/guest_book?session_id=EkeAuaF8ac4cFf4ud8GUkR)


The live fireside chats can also be accessed under “[Events & Presentations](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=1484FKK5vJR_rY1ilTHZScIP4l53tFjqXYKUPJt20No4hSoLeJyljW0IPkO-07N5XcKzAuzH3dDxliBvulYGAq_uVWrwak--yZn8L5AuwuY3JeUBaUJAuTemVAW-oAZoYuB6j0k3UafoRZR6SdZaDgxIA8s00YOQzvT0nY2xrMY=) ” on the Investors section of the Tectonic website at[www.tectonictx.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=lA_jZ51InlY-Wl7zpFHfcq8dJ3tbY1TLf-jQkeb3L-G8uvVgit8D-vaSgom3vuOhIz2yzoWucA_dAGTpbofg79xiqIWdOWmLB515S8nDc-E=) . A replay of the webcasts will be available on the Company’s website for approximately 90 days.


The Tectonic management team will host one-on-one meetings during the two conferences. Interested investors should contact their TD Cowen and Leerink representatives to schedule meetings.


**About Tectonic**
[Tectonic Therapeutic](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=USLknVc-Rj1NyzzPkYCS4vLZBIf-VDKrDAk8S_mqpSupK4VbNRJIaymgRW7sld1KBFEShP9lreId7giPRoJstr_unKB6PkJBy1H_PCRD7Ps=) is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on the discovery and development of therapeutic proteins and antibodies that modulate the activity of GPCRs. Leveraging its proprietary technology platform called GEODe™ (GPCRs Engineered for Optimal Discovery), Tectonic is focused on developing biologic medicines that overcome the existing challenges of GPCR-targeted drug discovery and harness the human body to modify the course of disease. Tectonic focuses on areas of significant unmet medical need, often where therapeutic options are poor or nonexistent, as these are areas where new medicines have the potential to improve patient quality of life. Tectonic is headquartered in Watertown, Massachusetts.
