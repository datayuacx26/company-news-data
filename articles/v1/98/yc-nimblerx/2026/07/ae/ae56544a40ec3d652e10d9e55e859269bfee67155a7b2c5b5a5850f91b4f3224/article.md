# Nimble Connect

## Helping Your Pharmacy Talk to Patients the Easy Way


‍


Running a pharmacy is a lot of work. You have to fill prescriptions, answer questions, and help patients every day. **Nimble Connect** makes it easier by helping your pharmacy talk to patients automatically so your team can spend more time helping people in person.


‍


### **Patient Communication Isn't the Problem. Bandwidth Is.**


‍


Pharmacists already know patients need clear refill reminders and fast answers. The problem is time.


‍


Staff spend hours on manual calls and texts, and gaps still slip through. A missed reminder means a missed refill, a frustrated patient, or a call that could've been a two-line text.


‍


Nimble Connect closes that gap. It automates the outreach your team already knows patients need, so nothing falls through during a busy shift.


### ‍


### **How Nimble Connect Helps**


#### ‍


#### **1. Send Messages at Every Step**


‍


Nimble Connect can send messages to patients when:


- Their prescription is received


- Their prescription is ready to pick up


- It’s time to refill


This way, patients always know what’s happening, and your staff get fewer “just checking” calls.


#### ‍


#### **2. Smart, AI-powered Answers**


‍


Nimble Connect can answer common questions automatically, like:


- “What time are you open?”
- “Is my refill ready?”
- “Can I pick up or get delivery?”
- “Simple health questions”


Your staff can focus on helping patients with more complicated needs.


‍


#### **3. Easy Refills Online**


‍


Patients get reminders when it’s time to refill, and they can confirm online. They also get:


- A message when their prescription arrives
- A reminder to pick it up
- Follow-up messages so they stay on track


This keeps patients happy and on their medicine without extra work for your team.
