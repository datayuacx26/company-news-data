# What’s New with Nimble: Powering Your Pharmacy with New Customizations, Upgrades, and Integrations

At Nimble, we're constantly working behind the scenes to bring you the tools and features that make managing your pharmacy easier and more efficient. We're excited to share our latest product updates, designed to give you greater control, streamline your workflows, and enhance the patient experience.


‍


Let's dive in!


‍


## Tailored Pharmacy Hours Are Now a Breeze!


‍


We understand that your pharmacy's schedule isn't always a standard 9-to-5. That's why we've rolled out a brand-new feature that puts you in the driver's seat when it comes to setting and managing your pharmacy hours. Now, logging holiday closures and adjusting your regular hours is just a few clicks away!


‍


From this intuitive interface, you can:


‍


- **Effortlessly add one-time closures** for those unexpected days off or special events.
- **Schedule holiday closures in advance** , ensuring your patients are always informed.
- **View a clear list of your upcoming closure dates** for easy planning.
- **Adjust your pharmacy’s regular weekly hours and closures** to reflect your operational needs.


‍


## Take Control of Admin Fees with Custom Caps


‍


To help you maintain fair and reasonable pricing, especially for larger orders, we've introduced the ability to **set custom caps for your Admin Fees.** This new feature ensures that these fees remain within a specific dollar amount, providing cost predictability for both your pharmacy and your patients.


‍


## Refill Experiences, Optimized for Maximum Convenience


‍


We're committed to continuously refining the refill experience for both you and your patients. Our latest enhancements focus on intelligent delivery logic, ensuring that the most suitable option is automatically selected based on the lowest cost to you and your patients, and the fastest delivery timeline.
