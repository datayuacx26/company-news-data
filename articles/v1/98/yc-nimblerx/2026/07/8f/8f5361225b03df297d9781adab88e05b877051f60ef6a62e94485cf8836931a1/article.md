‍


Talking to patients is one of the most important parts of running a pharmacy — but it takes a lot of time. Constant phone calls, manual reminders, and answering the same questions over and over can pull staff away from real patient care.


[Nimble Connect](https://www.nimblerx.com/articles/nimble-connect) makes communication easier by sending the right messages at the right time.

### **How Nimble Connect Helps**


‍


- **Automated messages** when prescriptions are received, ready, or due for refill
- **AI-powered responses** to common questions (hours, refill status, and pickup options)
- **Online refill confirmations** and follow-up reminders
- **HIPAA-safe messaging** that protects patient information
