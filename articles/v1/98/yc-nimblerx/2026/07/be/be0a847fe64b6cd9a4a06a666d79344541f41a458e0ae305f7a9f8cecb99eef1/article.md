# How Dave’s Pharmacy Removes Friction at the Counter & Delivers a Better Patient Experience

‍


Dave Randolph opened[Dave's Pharmacy](https://daves-pharmacy.com/) in 2014 with a straightforward philosophy: where advanced care meets personal service. Located in Alliance, Nebraska, Dave's Pharmacy has grown well beyond the traditional dispensary model. The team offers point-of-care testing, immunizations, medication therapy management (MTM), long-term care at home, remote patient monitoring, care planning, wound care, and lab work, all under one roof.


‍


Dave's Pharmacy has built its reputation on genuine patient relationships, treating every person who walks through the door as a member of the community, not just a prescription to fill. That level of service sets the bar high, but it also creates pressure. Delivering a personalized, high-touch experience while managing prescription volume, patient flow, and administrative compliance is a tough balance, especially for an independent pharmacy without the infrastructure of a big chain.


‍


Dave needed tools that would support that model, not just add to his workload.


‍


## **The Challenge: Controlled Chaos and Compliance Risk**


‍


Pharmacy workflow has a predictable problem: it isn't predictable. There are quiet stretches followed by surges where the counter backs up, phones start ringing, and the entire team is stretched thin. Managing that cycle without friction requires getting as much work done during the slow periods as possible.


‍


Unclaimed prescriptions add cost and more operational drag. A patient who doesn't pick up a filled antibiotic with a $5 copay ties up dispensed inventory, pulls staff into follow-up calls, and blocks a fill slot that could have gone to someone else. Scale that across a week's worth of fills and the operational cost is hard to ignore.


‍


Compliance was another large concern. Dave's Pharmacy, like every independent, operates in an audit environment where documentation errors have real consequences.


‍


Before adopting a new platform, Dave needed to know it would hold up to scrutiny and not create new liability.


‍


## **The Solution: Nimble**


‍


Dave first heard about Nimble at a trade show a couple years ago, and his store manager was immediately interested.


‍


Nimble's patient app


[Nimble](https://www.nimblerx.com/pharmacies) is an end-to-end communication and checkout platform built specifically for independent and community pharmacies. It integrates directly with pharmacy management systems (PMS) to modernize the patient-facing side of the prescription fulfillment workflow. Nimble automates patient communications, including Rx-ready notifications, proactive refill reminders, and two-way HIPAA-compliant messaging. Nimble also enables patients to pay for and approve their orders digitally before arriving at the pharmacy, and it captures signatures electronically at the time of transaction.


‍


The platform syncs directly with the PMS Dave's team uses, Pioneer. Every signature captured through Nimble flows directly into the system, creating a complete, audit-ready record, including payment documentation.


‍


"We've been through audits with Nimble and we've had no problems whatsoever," Dave said. "It interfaces with Pioneer so we've got the signature right there and payment records."


‍


With his primary concern settled, every other benefit followed naturally.


‍


## **The Results: Predictable Flow, Fewer Abandoned Prescriptions, and a New Revenue Stream**


‍


### **Workflow Gets Predictable**


‍


The biggest operational shift was in how the team operates. When Nimble notifies patients that their prescription is ready and prompts them to complete checkout in advance, the pharmacy can process those orders during downtime rather than scrambling when volume spikes.


‍


"Pharmacy can be slow and all of a sudden all hell breaks loose and that's when you have your lines," Dave explained. "If during that slow time you can get things cleared out through Nimble, then when hell breaks loose, patients are in and out the door."


‍


That shift from reactive to proactive is the kind of workflow efficiency that compounds across every busy day.


‍


### **Fewer Prescriptions Go Unclaimed**


‍


Advance payment through Nimble directly reduces abandoned fills. When patients pay in advance, the pickup decision is already made. As Dave puts it: "They've already paid, their card's on file, and the prescription's waiting. There's no decision to make. They're coming in."


‍


That's a simple mechanism with meaningful impact. Fewer returned prescriptions means less wasted staff time, less reverse inventory management, and better adherence outcomes for patients.


‍


### **Patients Notice, and They Sign Up**


‍


Speed at pickup has become a visible differentiator for Dave's Pharmacy. When a patient walks in and walks out in under a minute, other patients waiting in line take notice. Dave describes a typical interaction:


‍


"We see Joe walk in the door, he grabs his prescription, and he’s out the door," Dave said. "Then we get people that ask us how he got in and out so fast. We tell them that Joe uses Nimble, it’s an app on your phone and you can start using it today."


‍


That word-of-mouth recruiting has helped grow the pharmacy's active Nimble user base organically, without a formal enrollment campaign.


‍


Dave has his own shorthand for the experience. He calls it being "Nimble-ized." As he describes it: "When a prescription is “Nimble-ized”, it means that the patient has already paid for and signed for it. So when they walk in the door, they get it right there and walk out the door."
