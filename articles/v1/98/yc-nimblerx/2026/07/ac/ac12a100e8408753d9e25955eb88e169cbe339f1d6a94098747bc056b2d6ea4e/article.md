# The Pharmacist’s New Best Friend: NimbleRx’s Mira AI

Pharmacists are overwhelmed with administrative work, leaving less time for what matters most—patients. NimbleRx’s AI technology is here to change that. By automating routine tasks, Mira AI empowers pharmacies to run faster, safer, and smarter.


‍


**Why Pharmacies Love AI:**


- **Faster Prescriptions:** AI can read and enter handwritten, faxed, or electronic prescriptions directly into pharmacy systems, cutting errors and saving precious time.


- **Safer Medications:** Automatically checking for interactions, contraindications, and dosing issues, gives pharmacists an extra layer of safety.


- **Coming Soon – Prior Authorizations:** AI will streamline submissions, saving hours and helping patients get their therapy faster.


- **Smarter Customer Support:** Nimble’s Mira AI handles common patient questions—like “Is my prescription ready?”—so staff can focus on what really matters.


**Empowering Pharmacists, Delight­ing Patients** Mira AI doesn’t replace pharmacists—it frees them to focus on patient care, counseling, and building trusted relationships. HIPAA-compliant and secure, Mira AI also keeps patients engaged and informed, improving adherence and satisfaction.


With Mira AI, pharmacies don’t just work—they thrive. Faster workflows, safer care, and happier patients. The future of pharmacy is here, and it’s powered by NimbleRx.
