# Nimble and DigitalRx Partnership

## Better Patient Care, Less Busywork; Nimble And DigitalRX Are


## Partnering To Give Independent Pharmacies Both


‍


Nimble and DigitalRx have officially partnered — and the result is something independent pharmacies have long needed: a connected platform that makes it easier to deliver great patient care without piling more work onto your staff.


‍


DigitalRx brings the PMS backbone that thousands of independent pharmacies rely on every day. Nimble brings the connected pharmacy layer — seamless patient experience, automated workflows, and the tools to grow a healthy, sustainable business. Together, the two platforms cover everything from the moment a prescription is written to long-term therapy continuity, without the manual overhead that usually comes with it.


‍


## A better experience for patients. More growth for pharmacies.


‍


**Patients get the support they need to stay on therapy.** Nimble's automated outreach and adherence tools keep patients informed and engaged — so they show up, pick up, and stay on track — without your team having to chase them down manually.


‍


**Less manual work for your staff.** Refill follow-ups, patient communications, and dispensing workflows run automatically through your existing DigitalRx setup — freeing your team to focus on the counter, not the phone.


‍


**A platform built to help you grow.** Nimble increases refills and reduces abandoned prescriptions which help strengthen your business. Fuel even more growth by easily promoting your OTC inventory and recouping your processing fees. Added bonus - Nimble pays you for every order completed through the platform! Last year, Nimble paid pharmacies a total of $3.4M.


‍


**Two systems that actually work together.** This partnership means Nimble and DigitalRx are aligned at the product level — built and maintained to work seamlessly together, not bolted together as an afterthought.
