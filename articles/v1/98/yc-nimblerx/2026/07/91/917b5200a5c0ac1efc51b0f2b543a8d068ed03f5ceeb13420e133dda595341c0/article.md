Pharmacy workflow automation has changed what's possible for independent pharmacies managing refills, but refill opportunities might still be slipping through the cracks.


‍


Knowing a patient has an upcoming refill is only half the battle. If the outreach is manual, inconsistent, or easy for patients to ignore, the prescription *still* doesn't get filled consistently.

## **How Nimble works to improve refills**


‍


Nimble keeps your refill queue full without disrupting how your team already works. Patients stay in control of their refills, and the process is built to reduce your staff’s manual workload.


‍


**Step One: The reminder**


‍


A few days before a patient runs out of medication based on their prescription, Nimble sends an automated reminder. Once the patient confirms, the refill request is automatically sent to your pharmacy team with no manual follow-up required.


‍


**Step Two: Real-time updates**


‍


Throughout the process, patients receive real-time status updates so they know exactly when their prescription is ready, ensuring they only show up when it's available for pickup. Your team can view the same estimated timing shown to the patient.


‍


The result is fewer inbound calls, better transparency for patients and staff, and a refill workflow that runs alongside your existing PMS setup without changing it.
