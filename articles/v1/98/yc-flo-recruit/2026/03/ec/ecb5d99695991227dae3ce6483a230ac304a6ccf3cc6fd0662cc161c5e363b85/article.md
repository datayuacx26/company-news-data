# Press Release: Flo Launches Legal Performance Management Solution

[From Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260324912187/en/Flo-Launches-Legal-Performance-Management-Platform-With-Eight-Law-Firm-Clients-Onboarded) : Flo Launches Legal Performance Management Solution With Eight Law Firm Partners Onboarded


AUSTIN, March 24, 2026: Flo today announced the launch of its[Performance Management solution](https://www.joinflo.com/products/performance-management) , with eight law firms already onboarded, marking a major expansion that allows clients to connect recruiting with talent development in a single platform.


Flo Performance Management powers law firm review cycles, partner promotion processes, new hire support, and more. The launch reflects growing market demand for agile software that supports, rather than slows, necessary innovation as law firms explore the use of AI and its impact on talent strategy.


> “I’ve watched Flo become so successful in the legal talent space,” said[Kelly Mixon Morgan](https://www.joinflo.com/resources/talent-leaders/kelly-mixon-morgan) , Chief Legal Performance Officer at Barnes & Thornburg. “There’s a huge need in performance management right now, and being part of developing it – having input into what it should look like – has been really meaningful. Flo listens to your customers and understands this industry.”


Talent leaders must find ways to drive higher participation in reviews to make data-driven decisions across coaching, promotions, compensation, and hiring. To achieve this, Flo Performance Management includes easy-to-use review writing for partners and managers, self-evaluations for associates and professionals, integrations with HR and matter/billing systems, and unified consensus reviews for leaders compiling insights.


Flo also announced upcoming AI capabilities, including writing assistance, summarization, and trend analysis across reviews, which are slated for release in summer 2026.


The launch coincides with the company’s rebrand from Flo Recruit to Flo, reflecting its expansion beyond recruiting software to a broader legal talent platform.


> “We’re committing to serve the legal industry in a bigger way, at a moment when AI is bringing waves of change to law firms, attorneys, and law schools,” said Katherine Allen, Co-Founder and CEO of Flo. “It’s never been more important to connect your full talent strategy, from recruiting to developing the right people.”


As the legal industry evolves amid rapid technological change, Flo continues to empower innovation in talent strategy for its clients.


About Flo: Flo builds legal talent software solutions that connect law firms, attorneys, law schools, and search firms, proudly serving 75% of AmLaw 100 firms and 80% of top 50 ranked U.S. law schools. Flo is headquartered in Austin, Texas.
