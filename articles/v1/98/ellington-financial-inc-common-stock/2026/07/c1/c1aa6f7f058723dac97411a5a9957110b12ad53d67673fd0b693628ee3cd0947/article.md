# Ellington Financial Announces Estimated Book Value Per Common Share as of June 30, 2026

[PDF Version](https://www.ellingtonfinancial.com/node/16901/pdf)


OLD GREENWICH, Conn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 23, 2026-- Ellington Financial Inc. (NYSE: EFC) ("we") today announced an estimated book value per share of common stock of $13.61 as of June 30, 2026. This estimate includes the effect of the previously announced monthly dividend of $0.13 per share of common stock, to be paid on July 31, 2026 to holders of record on June 30, 2026, with the same ex-dividend date.
