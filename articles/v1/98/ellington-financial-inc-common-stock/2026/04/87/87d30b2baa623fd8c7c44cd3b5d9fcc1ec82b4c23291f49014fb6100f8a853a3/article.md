# Ellington Financial Announces Release Date of First Quarter 2026 Earnings, Conference Call, and Investor Presentation

[PDF Version](https://www.ellingtonfinancial.com/node/16796/pdf)


OLD GREENWICH, Conn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Apr. 22, 2026-- Ellington Financial Inc. (NYSE: EFC) (the "Company") today announced that it will release financial results for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 after market close on Tuesday, May 5, 2026. The Company will host a conference call to discuss its financial results at 11:00 a.m. Eastern Time on Wednesday, May 6, 2026. To participate in the event by telephone, please dial (800) 343-4136 at least 10 minutes prior to the start time and reference the conference code EFCQ126. International callers should dial (203) 518-9843 and reference the same code. The conference call also will be webcast live and can be accessed via the "For Investors" section of the Company's website at[www.ellingtonfinancial.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.ellingtonfinancial.com%2F&esheet=54520661&newsitemid=20260422677559&lan=en-US&anchor=www.ellingtonfinancial.com&index=1&md5=e07d1df3c5068ab43740a97dbfef7947) . To listen to the live webcast, please visit[www.ellingtonfinancial.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.ellingtonfinancial.com%2F&esheet=54520661&newsitemid=20260422677559&lan=en-US&anchor=www.ellingtonfinancial.com&index=2&md5=ccbb186c99418c5d6dd1dc9811db0cfa) at least 15 minutes prior to the start of the call to register, download, and install necessary audio software.


A dial-in replay of the conference call will be available on Wednesday, May 6, 2026, at approximately 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time through Wednesday, May 13, 2026 at approximately 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time. To access this replay, please dial (800) 677-6124. International callers should dial (402) 220-0664. A replay of the conference call also will be archived on the Company's website at[www.ellingtonfinancial.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.ellingtonfinancial.com%2F&esheet=54520661&newsitemid=20260422677559&lan=en-US&anchor=www.ellingtonfinancial.com&index=3&md5=e091d263d7018640d0e20d86ab3a0cdf) .


In connection with the release of financial results, the Company will post an investor presentation to accompany the conference call on its website at[www.ellingtonfinancial.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.ellingtonfinancial.com%2F&esheet=54520661&newsitemid=20260422677559&lan=en-US&anchor=www.ellingtonfinancial.com&index=4&md5=c83171c9ee763265bc1e5c05e3abf25b) under "For Investors—Presentations" after market close on Tuesday, May 5, 2026.


**About Ellington Financial Inc.**


Ellington Financial invests in a diverse array of financial assets, including residential and commercial mortgage loans and mortgage-backed securities, reverse mortgage loans, mortgage servicing rights and related investments, consumer loans, asset-backed securities, collateralized loan obligations, non-mortgage and mortgage-related derivatives, debt and equity investments in loan origination companies, and other strategic investments. Ellington Financial is externally managed and advised by Ellington Financial Management LLC, an affiliate of Ellington Management Group, L.L.C.
