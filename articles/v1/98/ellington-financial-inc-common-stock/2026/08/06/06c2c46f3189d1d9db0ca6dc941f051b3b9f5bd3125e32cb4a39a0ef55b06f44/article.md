# Ellington Financial Declares Monthly Common Dividend

[PDF Version](https://www.ellingtonfinancial.com/node/16951/pdf)


OLD GREENWICH, Conn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 10, 2026-- Ellington Financial Inc. (NYSE: EFC) ("we") today announced that its Board of Directors has declared a monthly dividend of $0.13 per share of common stock, payable on September 30, 2026 to common stockholders of record as of August 31, 2026.
