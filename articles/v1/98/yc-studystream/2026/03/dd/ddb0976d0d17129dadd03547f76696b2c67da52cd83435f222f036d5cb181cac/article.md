## What Is the Study Stream Pomodoro Timer?


The StudyStream Pomodoro timer is a built-in productivity timer available inside our 24/7 focus rooms. It’s based on the Pomodoro Technique developed by Francesco Cirillo, which uses focused work intervals followed by short breaks to improve concentration and reduce fatigue.
