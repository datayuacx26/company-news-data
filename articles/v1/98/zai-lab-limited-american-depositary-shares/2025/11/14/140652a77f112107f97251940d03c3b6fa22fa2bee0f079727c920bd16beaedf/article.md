# Update on Amgen’s bemarituzumab Phase 1b/3 FORTITUDE-102 Study

November 6, 2025 at 2:49 pm


Amgen, Zai Lab’s partner and study sponsor, announced during its Q3 2025 earnings call on November 4, 2025, that the Phase 1b/3 FORTITUDE-102 trial evaluating bemarituzumab plus chemotherapy and nivolumab in patients with first-line gastric cancer was stopped for inadequate efficacy, at an ad hoc analysis requested by the data monitoring committee.We will continue to work closely with Amgen and provide further updates as appropriate.
