# Soap, Suds and Spectrum Business’ Seamless Connection in the Sunshine State

There’s more to a car wash than soap and water. Steve Schlossberg, owner of five Splash Express Car Wash locations in Volusia County, Florida, runs an operation that’s anything but old-fashioned. His high-tech business is packed with cameras, sensors and automated systems – all tied together by[Spectrum’s Fiber-Powered Internet](https://www.spectrum.com/cp/fiber-broadband-network) .


Every car that enters one of Splash’s car wash tunnels is scanned and measured, with equipment automatically adjusting to the vehicle’s size and the service selected. Safety is paramount – a network of 40 cameras, all monitored remotely and connected by Spectrum, keeps watch on everything. If a car veers out of its lane, the system halts instantly to prevent collisions.
