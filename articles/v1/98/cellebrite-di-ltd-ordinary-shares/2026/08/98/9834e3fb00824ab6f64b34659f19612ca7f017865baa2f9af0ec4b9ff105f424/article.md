*Cellebrite Genesis for Enterprise Brings it all Together for Faster, More Defensible Outcomes*


Picture a single case on your desk right now. It likely involves a mobile phone extraction, a stack of eMails and shared-drive documents, a chat thread, maybe some cloud data or call detail records and a video file or two. Now — picture five more cases just like it, all open at the same time, and all with someone waiting on an answer.


It was likely not a difficult picture to build in your mind, because if you’re part of an enterprise investigative team — this is your daily life. Investigations rarely come in one tidy format. A workplace complaint might involve text messages, badge records and a security camera clip. A compliance review might span years of financial documents and a handful of interviews. An eDiscovery request might touch dozens of custodians across email, chat and shared drives. Reviewing all of it by hand, one source at a time, takes time your team does not have.


[Cellebrite Genesis](https://cellebrite.com/en/products/cellebrite-genesis/) , now available for Enterprise, brings that evidence together in one place, using powerful AI to help your team make sense of it fast. Genesis also links every answer back to the original source so it can be checked and verified.


**Easily Connecting Multiple Evidence Sources: Compliance Investigations & eDiscovery**


The hardest part of investigating a case with multiple sources of evidence is connecting different data types. On their own, a name in a text thread, a timestamp in a video or a transaction in a spreadsheet are merely data points. Yet when they’re put together, they tell a story. Finding the threads that bring the story together manually means opening countless files and holding all the information in your head at once.


That pressure shows up clearly in the numbers. Fifty-four percent of enterprise professionals[rank eDiscovery as their most important use case](https://cellebrite.com/en/2026-industry-trends-private-sector/) and 57% say analyzing communications to find links between people is the highest-impact use of AI. Mobile is a growing part of that picture too. Mobile evidence is now involved in two-thirds of enterprise cases, up six points from 2024, with teams averaging 4 to 6 mobile device examinations a month on top of ongoing cloud and computer reviews. Most general tools still can’t read that evidence without converting it first.


This is precisely what Genesis was built to deliver, to give teams a faster, better-informed starting point. It analyzes more than 35 different evidence formats, multiple languages and quickly triages information to help teams narrow their focus.


**Tested on Real Cases, Not Just in a Demo**


Genesis’ journey was not straight from a lab to a launch announcement. Users in our Early Access program helped shape the Agentic AI product.


One of the Early Access users was Interpath, a financial advisory firm based in the United Kingdom. Their team used Genesis on a case that is related to a potential financial wrongdoing in an Initial Public Offering (IPO) that was ultimately scrapped “It took us months to piece together who was involved,” said Bruce Keeble of Interpath. “When we ran all the data through Genesis, it took minutes to surface the same information. The speed and accuracy amazed us and helped validate our original findings.”


Feedback gained through real-time discussions with customers like Interpath and others helped our developers better understand what would serve customers’ needs. When we set out to create Genesis, we had one goal in mind: a faster, more efficient way to help make investigators’ lives easier so they could close more matters with confidence. For our Enterprise customers, the goal remains the same, an easier workflow that’s faster and helps teams build defensible cases.


**What Sets Genesis Apart: Source-Traceable AI for Defensible Findings**


There is a lot of discussion right now about using AI in digital investigations, much of it centered around defensibility. A general AI model uses a wealth of publicly available information to summarize a document or answer a question about almost anything. While Genesis may reflect a similar design to a traditional LLM model, it is vastly different.


Its scope is far narrower and is built specifically to work with forensic and investigative data, including UFDRs, call detail records and case-specific evidence. It applies investigative practice built over 25 years of Cellebrite’s work in digital investigations.


That narrow focus is what makes validation possible. Validation is paramount to any investigation, digital or otherwise. As mentioned earlier, Genesis links every answer back to its source, so your team can check where a finding came from before acting on it, rather than working from an unattributed summary. In short, your investigation data stays in your environment. Cellebrite cannot access the data and none of it is used to train AI models.


For legal, compliance and eDiscovery teams, that difference is significant. Speed is only helpful if the result holds up. Genesis helps your team to answer fast and shows exactly how it got there, with complete audit trails that satisfy regulatory and legal requirements.


**The New Age in Enterprise Investigations**


The amount of digital evidence involved in enterprise investigations is going to continue growing and changing. Genesis makes sure your team’s speed does not depend on which shape it takes.
