For federal agencies, moving digital evidence to the cloud has never been a question of capability—it has been a question of trust. Investigative teams need modern collaboration, scalability, and speed, but without compromising the security, oversight, and control required for sensitive government data.


With FedRAMP® High authorization[now achieved](https://www.fedramp.gov/marketplace/products/FR2421035110/) for Cellebrite Government Cloud, that balance is no longer hypothetical. Federal agencies have access to a government‑approved cloud environment designed specifically to support secure digital evidence workflows at the highest federal security standard for sensitive unclassified information.


FedRAMP High authorization enables eligible federal customers to securely host, share, and review digital evidence in the cloud—while meeting rigorous requirements for confidentiality, integrity, availability, and accountability.


*View*[Inseyets](https://www.cellebritefsi.com/products/inseyets/) *and*[Guardian](https://www.cellebritefsi.com/products/guardian/guardian-collaborate/) *, now available in Cellebrite Government Cloud.*


## **Why FedRAMP High Is a Turning Point**


FedRAMP High represents the most stringent security designation in the[FedRAMP framework](https://www.fedramp.gov/archive/2017-11-16-understanding-baselines-and-impact-levels/) . It applies to systems handling sensitive but unclassified data where compromise could result in severe or catastrophic impact, including law enforcement and investigative information.


Achieving this authorization requires extensive security controls, continuous monitoring, and formal approval by a sponsoring federal agency. As a result, only a small number of cloud environments meet this bar.


Cellebrite Government Cloud was authorized at the FedRAMP High level with sponsorship and approval from the U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ), validating its readiness to support high‑impact federal investigative use cases in a trusted cloud environment.


## **Modernizing Inseyets Deployment Without Increasing Burden**


[Inseyets](https://www.cellebritefsi.com/products/inseyets/) has long been a foundational tool for digital evidence extraction, decoding, and analysis across federal agencies. Historically, leveraging its full capabilities required agencies to deploy and maintain on‑prem infrastructure—placing added demands on IT teams and slowing adoption.


FedRAMP High authorization changes that model.


By making Inseyets available within Cellebrite Government Cloud, federal agencies can access advanced forensic capabilities without the need to build or manage complex infrastructure. This approach supports:


- Reduced operational and maintenance overhead
- Faster deployment and scalability
- Security controls aligned with federal requirements
- Continuous access to the latest cloud‑enabled unlock and extraction capabilities


Existing forensic examination workflows using Inseyets Physical Analyzer remain familiar, while cloud‑hosted capabilities extend access and flexibility where appropriate.


## **Secure Cloud Evidence Sharing With Guardian**


Beyond analysis, collaboration is where cloud adoption delivers its greatest impact—and where compliance has historically been most challenging for federal environments.


With FedRAMP High authorization now in place,[Guardian](https://www.cellebritefsi.com/products/guardian/guardian-collaborate/) can support secure, cloud‑based digital evidence sharing and review for eligible federal customers. Agencies can collaborate across teams and authorized stakeholders while preserving chain of custody, access controls, and auditability within a government‑approved cloud environment.


This enables cloud‑based review and collaboration workflows that were previously constrained by authorization requirements—without compromising trust or oversight.


## **A Foundation for Expanding Cloud** ‑ **Based Investigations**


FedRAMP High authorization is not an endpoint. It establishes the security foundation required to responsibly expand investigative capabilities in the cloud under formal government oversight.


As additional approvals are completed, Cellebrite plans to make more Guardian capabilities available within the government cloud environment, including AI‑assisted review, enhanced forensic workflows, and centralized investigative collaboration—always aligned with FedRAMP High requirements and federal governance expectations.


This measured approach ensures innovation advances in lockstep with security, compliance, and accountability.
