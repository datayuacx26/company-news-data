HOUSTON, July 28, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- VAALCO Energy, Inc. (NYSE: EGY; LSE: EGY) (“Vaalco” or the “Company”) today announced the timing of its second quarter 2026 earnings release and conference call.


The Company will issue its second quarter 2026 earnings release on Thursday, August 6, 2026 after the close of trading on the New York Stock Exchange and host a conference call to discuss its financial and operational results on Friday morning, August 7, 2026 at 8:00 a.m. Central Time (9:00 a.m. Eastern Time and 2:00 p.m. London Time).


Interested parties in the United States may participate toll-free by dialing (833) 685-0907. Interested parties in the United Kingdom should dial +44 20 3769 4533. Other international parties may dial (412) 317-5741. Participants should ask to be joined to the “Vaalco Energy Earnings Conference Call.” This call will also be webcast on Vaalco’s website at[www.vaalco.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=YheJY48hqrw-BFGEAvz6E3JqNlLIIkJNn9Hv9cFRSYIIVwJGnH4y8DF18hZX_nmbdv4UJILc6ZE0nNDNlbeUXQ==) . An audio replay will be available on the Company’s website following the call.
