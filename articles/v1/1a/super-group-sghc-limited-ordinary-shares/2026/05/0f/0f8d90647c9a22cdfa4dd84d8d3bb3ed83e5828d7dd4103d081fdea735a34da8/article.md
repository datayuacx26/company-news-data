# DigiOutreach Supports Haven Night Shelter

Share this page:


-
-
-
-


**In April and May, DigiOutreach was proud to support[Haven Night Shelter](https://www.haven.org.za/) , an organization dedicated to providing safety and hope to individuals experiencing homelessness. Their mission is simple: to offer shelter, meals and pathways to reintegration for those living on the streets.**


DigiOutreach supported the charity by donating R15,000, funds that directly translates into beds and meals for the community. This provides approximately **200 people with five nights of accommodation and support each** , ensuring that vulnerable individuals have a safe place to sleep and the resources they need during challenging times.


**About Haven Night Shelter**
The Haven Night Shelter is a well-established non-profit organization serving communities across Cape Town and beyond. By offering temporary shelter, social work services and reintegration programs, Haven empowers individuals to move from vulnerability to independence.


Their work goes beyond providing a bed; it’s about restoring dignity, building confidence and creating opportunities for people to reconnect with family, employment and society.


**Pillow to Pavement campaign**
Alongside our donation, DigiOutreach proudly took part in the **Pillow to Pavement campaign** , a powerful initiative that raises awareness about homelessness by encouraging participants to experience a night without the comfort of their own beds. This campaign highlights the daily struggles faced by those living on the streets and inspires collective action to support shelters like Haven.


**Empowering communities together**
Our April and May donation aimed to strengthen Haven’s ability to provide:


- **Beds and meals:** Offering safe shelter and nourishment for hundreds of individuals
- **Social support:** Connecting residents with counseling, healthcare and reintegration services
- **Pathways to independence:** Helping people transition from homelessness to stability and self-sufficiency


We’re proud that our partnership with Haven Night Shelter reflects DigiOutreach’s ongoing commitment to supporting vulnerable communities and promoting inclusive community connections.
