# DigiOutreach supports Botha Foundation’s Project 1000

Share this page:


-
-
-
-


**In celebration of Food Appreciation Day, DigiOutreach proudly partnered with the Botha Foundation’s Project 1000 initiative to provide essential food to individuals and families facing hardship across Cape Town.**


[The Botha Foundation](https://bothafoundation.co.za/) is a registered non-profit organization committed to improving lives across South Africa through outreach, relief and development initiatives. By addressing immediate needs while investing in sustainable support programs, the foundation empowers individuals and strengthens the social fabric of the communities it serves.


**Supporting Project 1000**
As part of our Food Appreciation Day initiative, DigiOutreach was honored to contribute to Project 1000 and support the foundation’s efforts to reach those most in need.


**Through this initiative, the Botha Foundation is able to:**


- Deliver essential food to households experiencing hardship
- Encourage a culture of generosity and shared responsibility
- Provide practical relief that helps restore hope and stability
- Create opportunities for individuals and families to move forward with greater confidence


**Creating impact through collective action**
At DigiOutreach, we believe that no one should have to face hunger alone. Small acts of kindness can have a profound impact, and meaningful change is possible when people come together with a shared purpose.


Our support of Project 1000 reflects our commitment to making a positive difference where it is needed most. By working alongside organizations such as the Botha Foundation, we can help ensure that more people have access to the support, care and resources to build brighter futures.
