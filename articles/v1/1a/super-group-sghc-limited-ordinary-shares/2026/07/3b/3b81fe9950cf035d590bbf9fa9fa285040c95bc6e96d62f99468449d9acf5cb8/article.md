# Betway Africa marks Mandela Day with 9,000 meals packed

Share this page:


-
-
-
-


**More than 50 Betway Africa colleagues rolled up their sleeves this Mandela Day, joining forces with[Rise Against Hunger](https://africa.riseagainsthunger.org/) ﻿ to go All in and make a meaningful difference in just over an hour.** ﻿


Together, the team packed an incredible 9,072 nutritious meals, helping to support vulnerable communities and contribute to the fight against hunger.


**Fighting hunger together**
Rise Against Hunger is a global humanitarian organization working to end hunger by providing nutritious meals, supporting education, responding to crises and empowering communities through sustainable development projects.


Through the collective efforts of volunteers and partners worldwide, the organization helps build food security and create long-term opportunities for vulnerable communities to thrive. Partnering with Rise Against Hunger has become an annual tradition at Betway Africa, growing in popularity every year.


**67 minutes for a good cause**
Mandela Day reminds us that small actions can have a big impact, and the energy, teamwork and generosity shown by our colleagues proved exactly that. Every meal packed represents hope and shows how powerful we are when we come together for a shared purpose and go All in.


A huge thank you to everyone who joined us, and to Rise Against Hunger Africa for partnering with us to create a positive impact.
