# DigiOutreach supporting the Eric Miles Cheshire Home for Nelson Mandela Month

-
-

**In celebration of Nelson Mandela Month, DigiOutreach was proud to support[Eric Miles Cheshire Home](http://www.ericmiles.org/) (EMCH), a Cape Town-based organization dedicated to empowering people with disabilities and enhancing their quality of life.**


EMCH provides residential care and support services that promote dignity, independence and equal opportunities for people living with disabilities.


As part of our Mandela Month initiative, DigiOutreach donated a range of daily goods and toiletries to help meet the needs of residents and support the dedicated care provided by the EMCH team. This included:


- **Essential hygiene and personal care items** for residents
- **Daily support resources** that assist caregivers in delivering quality care


**Making every day count**
Nelson Mandela believed that each person has the power to make a difference in their community. Aligned to this ethos, DigiOutreach is proud to contribute to an organization that lives this spirit every day by empowering abilities, creating opportunities for greater independence and building a more inclusive and compassionate community.
