# Betway partners with Don Jazzy in Nigeria

Share this page:


-
-
-
-


Betway has announced a major new partnership in Nigeria, appointing award-winning music executive and cultural icon Don Jazzy as its official brand ambassador.


Through his work as founder of Mavin Records, Don Jazzy has built an enduring legacy and has firmly cemented himself as one of Africa’s most influential entertainment figures. His impact extends beyond music, with a powerful presence in popular culture and a strong connection to millions of fans across Nigeria and beyond.


Through this partnership, Don Jazzy will become a key representative of the Betway brand in Nigeria and his involvement is set to enhance Betway’s ability to connect with local audiences in a meaningful and authentic way.


The collaboration marks a significant moment for Betway as we continue to expand our footprint in Nigeria. By partnering with a figure who embodies ambition, creativity and confidence, we’re reinforcing our commitment to delivering an engaging and locally resonant experience.


**Neal Menashe, Chief Executive Officer of Super Group, the parent company of Betway Africa, said** : “Don Jazzy is a cultural force whose influence transcends entertainment. His commitment to excellence and community empowerment aligns with the core values Betway champions as we enter this exciting new chapter in Nigeria. This partnership puts confidence, authenticity and passion at the centre of our brand, and we’re proud to join forces with such a local star.”


**Don Jazzy added** , “Betway is a brand that understands ambition, creativity and confidence, which is why this partnership feels like such a natural fit. I’m excited to represent Betway in Nigeria and to be part of a brand that that values culture, connection and authenticity.”


As Betway continues to grow across global markets, this partnership will help us continue to build connections with the communities we serve.
