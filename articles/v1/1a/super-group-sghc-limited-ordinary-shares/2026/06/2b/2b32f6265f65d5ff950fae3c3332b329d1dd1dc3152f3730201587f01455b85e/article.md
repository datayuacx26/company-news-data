# Bringing warmth where it’s needed

Share this page:

**Betway Africa’s recent Winter Warmer blanket drive was a powerful reminder of what we can achieve when we come together with purpose.**


Over 70 colleagues showed up in full force, generously giving their time and energy to create care packages for those in need. The atmosphere was filled with a strong sense of teamwork and compassion, as colleagues worked side by side.


Each care package was thoughtfully prepared to provide comfort and everyday essentials. From basics like rice, toothpaste, face cloths, blankets and other necessities; each items will help to bring warmth and dignity to those experiencing homelessness in our communities.


What made the day truly special was not just the number of hands involved, but the superclass spirit behind the effort. Every item packed and every moment shared reflected our united commitment to going All-in on giving back and supporting those less fortunate.


Our colleagues were able to take a care package home, to be distributed to people in need, helping to provide a little relief during the colder months. While the items themselves are important, it’s the care and intention behind them that make the greatest difference.


Together, we’ve shown once again that when we come together for a common cause and embrace our Superhonesty, we can create real impact.
