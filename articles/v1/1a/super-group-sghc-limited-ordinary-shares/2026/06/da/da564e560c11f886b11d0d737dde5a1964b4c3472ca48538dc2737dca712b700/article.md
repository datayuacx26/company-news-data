# Vladimir Guerrero Jr. teams up with Betway to promote Responsible Gambling

-
-
-
-


Major League Baseball All-Star Vladimir Guerrero Jr. has teamed up with Betway to promote Responsible Gambling.


Recognized as one of Major League Baseball’s top performers, Vladimir Guerrero Jr. has established himself as a standout force in the sport. Guerrero Jr. is a five-time MLB All-Star who in 2021 led Major League Baseball in home runs, and was named the All-Star Game MVP. He has continued to build his own legacy with star performances, becoming one of the most recognizable and influential athletes in North American sport. His on-field achievements and strong connection with fans make for a perfect match.


This deal reflects Super Group’s ongoing commitment to placing player protection, education, and informed decision making at the centre of its customer experience across all of its brands and products.


As part of his role, Guerrero Jr. will support Betway in raising awareness of Responsible Gambling principles, helping promote tools and resources that empower individuals to enjoy betting and gambling safely, responsibly, and within their limits.


**Neal Menashe, Chief Executive Officer of Super Group, said:** *“We’re thrilled to team up with Vladimir to promote Responsible Gambling. His influence, integrity, and connection with fans make him an ideal figure to help amplify the importance of safe and responsible play. This collaboration supports our global commitment to ensuring that customer protection is embedded in everything we do.”*


**Vladimir Guerrero Jr. commented:** *“I’m pleased to team up with Super Group to support Responsible Gambling initiatives that matter. Helping people understand how to enjoy betting safely, with balance and the right tools, is something I’m proud to be a part of.”*
