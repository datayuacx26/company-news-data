# Super Athletes take on HYROX Johannesburg

**Our Super Athletes have done it again – taking on the intensity of HYROX Johannesburg and crossing the finish line with determination and grit.**


Competing in one of the toughest fitness challenges out there, our colleagues pushed through every kilometre, station and rep, showing their incredible resilience and team spirit from start to finish. HYROX is no small feat, but this group proved exactly what it means to go the extra mile.


But, the real impact goes far beyond race day.


Together, our athletes raised an incredible amount for their chosen charities – with Super Group proudly matching every donation. That means even more funding going to worthy causes, doubling the difference our people are making in communities around the world.


A big congratulations and well done goes to our athletes who raised donations for the following charities:


- Araceli Ortega, DigiOutsource – Cancer Research UK


- Ashante Barnes, Betway Africa – The Homeless Hub


- Jean-Pierre Lishman, DigiOutsource – NewDay United


- Junior Gaoganediwe, Betway Africa – The Rotarus home for Citizens


This is what Super Group Cares is all about: supporting our people to challenge themselves, champion meaningful causes and go


*All in* to drive real impact together.
