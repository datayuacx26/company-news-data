# Gulf Energy Awards 2026: Shortlisted in 7 categories for 8 technologies

Expro has been shortlisted for seven award categories for the second year in a row at the Gulf Energy Awards 2026, which will take place in Houston, Texas, on 15 October 2026.


With eight of our technologies recognized, this incredible achievement shows our continuous commitment to innovating with purpose.


See award categories and technologies chosen below:


1. **Best Completions Technology -** RCIS
2. **Best Controls, Instrumentation, Automation Technology – Upstream -** iTONG™
3. **Best Deepwater Technology -** ONEFIT™ (Caseless Insertable)
4. **Best Deepwater Technology -** VERSAFLO™ NXT
5. **Best Digital Transformation – Upstream -** Centri-FI™
6. **Best Health, Safety or Environmental Contribution – Upstream -** Spring ARK
7. **Best Pipeline Integrity Technology -** Velonix®
8. **Best Production Technology -** QPulse™


Well done to everyone involved! Read more about the awards[here](https://e2awards.com/finalists/) .
