# Expro CIO Scott Turner Named Winner at 2026 HoustonCIO ORBIE® Awards

Expro has been recognized for excellence in technology leadership as its Chief Information Officer, Scott Turner was named Enterprise Category Winner at the 2026 HoustonCIO ORBIE® Awards.


The HoustonCIO ORBIE *®* Awards, one of Houston’s premier technology leadership recognition programs, celebrate chief information officers who demonstrate exceptional leadership, innovation and business impact through technology.


Scott was selected as the winner in the Enterprise category, which recognizes CIOs leading technology strategy for large, complex organizations with significant operational scope and responsibility. Judges assess leadership effectiveness, the business value created through technology innovation, and engagement within the industry and wider community.


Under Scott’s leadership, Expro has delivered significant progress across key initiatives including cloud modernization, cybersecurity, data and AI, strengthening the organization’s ability to innovate and compete at scale.


Scott Turner said, “I am truly honored to be named an Enterprise ORBIE winner. This recognition reflects the strength of the team around me and Expro’s continued focus on using technology to support our people, our operations and our customers. It’s an honor to receive this award and I’m grateful to work alongside colleagues who are committed to continuous improvement and long-term value”


The 2026 HoustonCIO ORBIE *®* Awards ceremony took place on 24 April 2026 at the Marriott Marquis Houston.
