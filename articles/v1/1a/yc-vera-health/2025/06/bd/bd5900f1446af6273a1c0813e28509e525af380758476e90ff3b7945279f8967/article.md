# Vera Health welcomes Dr. Bernard P. Chang of Columbia University to its Scientific Advisory Board

## Introduction


San Francisco, CA — June 13, 2025 — Vera Health, the AI-powered Clinical Thought Partner, is delighted to announce the addition of Bernard P. Chang, MD, PhD, to its Scientific Advisory Board. Dr. Chang's cross-disciplinary expertise and passion for evidence-based medicine will help Vera accelerate its mission to deliver real-time, high-quality insights to clinicians.


## Leadership Perspective


> Dr. Chang has a rare knack for blending acute-care insights, behavioral science, and rigorous research. His perspective will make Vera smarter and indispensable across the healthcare landscape.


> What excites us most about Dr. Chang is his track record of turning complex data into simple, actionable guidance for physicians. That mindset aligns perfectly with our mission to provide fast and actionable evidence-based answers.


## Vera's Mission


Vera Health is redefining how clinicians access and apply medical knowledge. Powered by cutting-edge AI and a meticulously curated, daily-updated database of primary literature and clinical guidelines, Vera delivers real-time, evidence-based recommendations that span every specialty. Our goal is simple: give healthcare professionals the clarity they need — instantly — so they can focus on what matters most: exceptional patient care.


## Dr. Chang's Vision


> I'm thrilled to join Vera's Scientific Advisory Board and help shape a tool that empowers clinicians across the full spectrum of medicine. Timely, precise information is critical, and Vera's cross-disciplinary approach has the potential to elevate care in ways we've only imagined to help clinicians improve both scientific discovery and patient care.
