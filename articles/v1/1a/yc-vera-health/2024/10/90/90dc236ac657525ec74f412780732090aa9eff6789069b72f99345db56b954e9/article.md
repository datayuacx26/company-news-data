# Vera Health welcomes Dr. Christopher Baugh of Harvard Medical School to their Scientific Advisory Board

## Introduction


San Francisco, CA. September 25, 2024 — Vera Health, a best-in-class AI-powered Clinical Decision Support (CDS) resource for healthcare providers, is pleased to announce the addition of Christopher Baugh, MD, MBA, as a new member of its Emergency Medicine Scientific Advisory Board (SAB), as they gear up for their launch at ACEP24.


## Leadership Perspective


> We are thrilled to welcome Dr. Baugh to our SAB. His clinical expertise and leadership in emergency medicine will be invaluable as we continue to evolve and scale Vera's impact on clinical decision-making.


> Dr. Baugh's leadership in shaping clinical practice through innovative guidelines has been game-changing for us. His ability to translate complex clinical workflows into actionable insights has pushed our AI to a whole new level, making it smarter and more aligned with the real-world needs of physicians,


## Vera's Mission


Vera aims to transform how healthcare providers access and apply clinical knowledge through real-time, evidence-based recommendations. Vera's AI-powered tool is built to enhance decision-making processes and streamline clinical workflows by integrating seamlessly into electronic health record systems. Leveraging a curated database of primary literature and clinical guidelines that is updated daily, Vera ensures that providers have access to the most current medical knowledge. With pilots underway in major academic medical centers, Vera is at the forefront of shaping the future of clinical decision support.


## Dr. Baugh's Vision


> I am excited to join Vera's Scientific Advisory Board and support their mission to elevate clinical decision-making in the ED through innovative technology. As a physician, I've seen firsthand how critical timely and accurate clinical information is in delivering high-quality care, and I look forward to helping shape Vera's role in this space,
