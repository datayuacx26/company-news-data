# AutoRFP vs Arphie (2026): Which AI Response Platform Fits Your Team?

**Last reviewed:** February 2026. This comparison pulls from AutoRFP.ai and Arphie vendor pages, verified G2 and Capterra reviews, and independent third-party write-ups. Both tools are recent AI-native entrants, so their review counts are small and most pricing is quote-based. Features and prices shift quickly, so confirm the current details with each vendor before you commit budget.


## **TL;DR: AutoRFP vs Arphie**


AutoRFP fits teams that want fast AI first drafts built from their past RFP responses, with visible "Trust Scores" and source attribution on every generated answer. Arphie fits teams that want AI agents to assemble answers from a managed knowledge base and connected data sources, with a Slack "quick-ask" agent for ad-hoc questions.


Which one wins is not the right question, it depends on what your team needs: draft-speed off historical responses (AutoRFP) or agent-driven knowledge assembly (Arphie). If you are looking for this category broadly, it is worth reading a wider[RFP software comparison guide](https://www.inventive.ai/blog-posts/rfp-software-comparison-guide) before committing.

## **What Should You Consider When Choosing Between AutoRFP and Arphie?**


Answer-first: choose on how each tool sources answers, how it controls hallucinations, how it prices, and how well it fits the systems your team already uses. Run these criteria against your own edge cases in a demo.


• **AI drafting approach** — Does the tool draft from your past responses (AutoRFP) or from a managed knowledge base and connected sources (Arphie)?


• **Source of truth** — Can you feed it more than prior RFPs? AutoRFP users report being limited to past responses; confirm whether that fits your content spread.


• **Hallucination control** — Both attach citations and confidence signals. Test whether the tool flags "unknown" or invents an answer on edge cases.


• **Conflict detection** — Does the platform surface contradicting or outdated content, or leave that to manual review?


• **Content governance** — Who keeps the answer library accurate, and how much manual upkeep does it demand?


• **Narrative-style proposals** — Can it produce prose sections, not just Q&A pairs? Verify export formats.


• **Workflow automation** — How much of intake, tagging and assignment is automated versus manual?


• **Collaboration** — Assignments, reviewer workflows, SSO — check for your team size.


• **Integrations** — Match the list to your CRM, content stores and chat tools; see the integrations comparison below.


• **Security and compliance** — SSO, SOC 2 status, data handling. Ask for current attestations.


• **Analytics** — Progress tracking, reporting and export. Arphie users report reporting features still maturing.


• **Pricing model** — Per-project versus per-seat, and what a "project" means. Get quote-only figures in writing before you compare.


• **Ease of implementation** — Setup time and learning curve. AutoRFP reviewers note an initial curve.


• **Enterprise scalability** — Volume limits, term commitments and admin controls.

## **AutoRFP: Overview, Features and Limitations**


AutoRFP.ai is an AI-native platform for RFPs, DDQs and security questionnaires. Its positioning centers on speed: generating a first draft from your past responses, then showing a confidence "Trust Score" and source for each answer so reviewers know what to check. It pairs that with a collaborative workspace for assignment and review.


### **Best features**


• AutoRFP states it automates roughly 90% of the first-draft response from prior RFPs, cutting manual writing time, per AutoRFP.ai.


• Transparent "Trust Scores" and source attribution on each generated answer.


• Fast, intuitive initial setup; AI reportedly improves as it learns from prior responses.


• Collaboration features: assign answers for review, SSO via Okta, Microsoft Entra and Google Workspace.


• Broad connector list including Salesforce, SharePoint, Confluence, Notion, Seismic and Zendesk.


• Browser extensions for Chrome and Edge for in-context answering.


### **Limitations (sourced)**


• Web-only access with no mobile app, per[G2 reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/autorfp-ai/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons) .


• An initial learning curve reported by users during onboarding.


• Users report you cannot upload source documents beyond past RFP responses, if your content lives across many systems, weigh[AutoRFP alternatives that connect to more sources](https://www.inventive.ai/blog-posts/autorfp-alternatives-comparison) .


• As a newer company, some upload processes reportedly struggle with complex questions (reported as improving).


• Small public review base, so patterns are still forming — validate against your own use.


• Minimum 12-month term on Enterprise agreements (per the pricing page).


### **Pricing**


AutoRFP publishes tiers: **Scale at $899/month** (paid annually, 24 projects/year) and **Accelerate at $1,299/month** (paid annually, 50 projects/year), both with unlimited users and all features; **Enterprise** is quote-based on project volume with a 12-month minimum, per the AutoRFP pricing page. A "project" is one RFP, DDQ, tender or security questionnaire. For a fuller breakdown of tiers, features and user feedback, see this in-depth[AutoRFP AI pricing and reviews](https://www.inventive.ai/blog-posts/autorfp-ai-pricing-features-reviews) analysis.

## **Arphie: Overview, Features and Limitations**


Arphie is an AI-native platform for RFPs, DDQs and security questionnaires built around AI agents. Rather than drafting purely from prior responses, its agents assemble answers from a managed knowledge base and connected data sources, attaching exact source citations and confidence levels. A Slack "quick-ask" agent lets teams pull answers from the knowledge base without opening the app.


### **Best features**


• AI agents draft first answers from the knowledge base with exact source citations and confidence levels.


• Reviewers report a 60–80% reduction in time on large RFPs and questionnaires, per[Arphie G2 reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/arphie/reviews) .


• Slack "quick-ask" agent for ad-hoc questions against the knowledge base.


• Ease of use praised by reviewers, with responsiveness to training.


• Fast support response and resolution times, per reviews.

## **Is AutoRFP Better Than Arphie?**


Neither is universally better — it depends on what your team needs. Choose **AutoRFP** if you want fast first drafts built from your past responses with visible Trust Scores, published pricing, and Microsoft Teams plus multiple SSO options out of the box.


Choose **Arphie** if you want AI agents drafting from a managed knowledge base with website ingestion and a Slack quick-ask agent, and you are comfortable with a quote-only model. Both are early-stage with thin review histories, both are web-first, and both attach citations and confidence to answers. Test each on your hardest, most specific questions — that is where sourcing models diverge most.
