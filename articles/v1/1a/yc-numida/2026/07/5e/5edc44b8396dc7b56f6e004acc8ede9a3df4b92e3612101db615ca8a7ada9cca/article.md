### The 7 Silent Profit Killers in Your Business


#### 1. Energy Waste (Your Biggest Invisible Expense)


**Reality Check** : Ugandan SMEs spend up to 25% of revenue on power (UMEME Report)


**Quick Fix** : An Entebbe hotel switched to solar panels and cut electricity bills by 40%


#### 2. Inventory Mismanagement


**Problem** : Overstocking ties up cash; understocking loses sales


**Success Story** : FreshMart Groceries in Kampala reduced waste by 40% using Just-in-Time inventory


#### 3. Employee Inefficiency & Overtime


**Hidden Drain** : A Jinja factory discovered they were paying for 20+ unproductive hours weekly


**Smart Solution** : Implemented shift rotations and saved UGX 400,000 monthly


#### 4. Unoptimized Supplier Contracts


**Eye-Opening Fact** : Most Ugandan businesses never renegotiate terms


**Real Result** : A Mbarara print shop saved UGX 1.2M annually by simply asking for better paper prices


#### 5. Tax Leakages


**Warning Sign** : Missing eligible deductions and credits with URA


**Action Step** : A Gulu retailer recovered UGX 320,000 after proper record-keeping


#### 6. Fraud & Theft


**Painful Reality** : Employee theft costs Ugandan businesses 5% of revenue annually


**Prevention** : Dual approval systems and surprise inventory checks cut losses by 80%


#### 7. Outdated Technology


**Hidden Cost** : Manual processes eating staff time


**Upgrade Impact** : A Kampala salon automated bookings and freed up 15 hours weekly


### 5 Cost-Cutting Strategies That Won't Hurt Quality


#### 1. Conduct a 30-Day Cost Audit


Use this simple method to find money leaks:


-


Track ALL expenses (even small ones)


-


Categorize by necessity (essential vs. nice-to-have)


-


Flag anything surprising or increasing


#### 2. Automate Manual Processes


**Local Success** : Wandegeya Pharmacy switched to digital invoicing and saved 10 staff hours weekly


**Start Here** : Begin with your most repetitive tasks (invoicing, inventory, customer follow-ups)


#### 3. Outsource Non-Core Functions


Why pay full-time when you can pay part-time?


**Smart Move** : A Kampala restaurant outsourced payroll and cut accounting costs by 50%


#### 4. Master Bulk Purchasing


**Simple Script** : "I'm considering a larger order—what discount can you offer if I buy X amount?"


**Pro Tip** : Partner with other small businesses to reach minimum order quantities


#### 5. Prevent Fraud with Basic Systems

-

-
