# The Energy Audit Challenge

This article provides a self-guided checklist for facility managers to score their building’s energy efficiency.


****


**How to use this checklist**


Check every box that applies to your facility. Each item carries a point value based on its energy impact. Add up your points at the end and use the scoring guide to see where you stand — and what to do next.


**Section 1: Lighting & Controls**


□


All or most lighting uses LED bulbs


*5 pts*


□


Occupancy or motion sensors control lights in low-traffic areas


*5 pts*


□


Daylight harvesting (auto-dimming near windows) is in use


*4 pts*


□


Exterior lighting is on timers or photocells


*3 pts*


□


Exit signs are LED

□

□

*5 pts*

*4 pts*

*5 pts*


□


Building is on an HVAC preventive maintenance contract


*3 pts*


**Section 3: Building Envelope**


□


Roof insulation meets or exceeds current code


*4 pts*


□

*3 pts*


□


Walls have adequate insulation


*3 pts*


□


Loading dock seals or shelters are in good condition

**Section 4: Energy Management**


□


We track energy use monthly by reviewing utility bills


*3 pts*


□


We have an Energy Management System (EMS) or Building Automation System (BAS)


*6 pts*


□


Peak demand charges are actively managed


*5 pts*


□


We have compared energy suppliers in the past 2 years


*4 pts*


□


We are on a fixed-price or hedged energy contract


*3 pts*


**Section 5: Equipment & Operations**


□


Computers and monitors power down or sleep when idle


*2 pts*


□

*4 pts*


□


Kitchen/cafeteria equipment (if applicable) is ENERGY STAR rated


*3 pts*


□


Server rooms or data closets have dedicated cooling with efficiency controls


*4 pts*


□


An energy policy or sustainability goal exists for the facility


*3 pts*

*5 pts*
