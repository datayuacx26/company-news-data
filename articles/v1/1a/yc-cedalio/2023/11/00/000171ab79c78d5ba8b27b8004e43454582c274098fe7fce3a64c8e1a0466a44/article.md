Hello everyone,


We are are excited to unveil[Connect](http://www.cedalio.com/connect) . Our journey began with a simple observation: many of our customers in the traceability sector highlighted the challenges they faced in ensuring transparent ESG reporting and undeniable proof of product origins. Recognizing this need, we launched Connect.


With Connect, businesses can:


- **Effortlessly Gather Data** : Seamlessly pull data from various sources, even those legacy systems that might seem outdated. Store it in a way that aligns with sustainability or provenance objectives.
- **Share Transparently:** Decide on the data to certify and share it across the business ecosystem. This not only aids in scope 3 emissions accounting but also in creating digital product passports, ensuring data is auditable and trustworthy.
- **Support Claims with Authentic Data:** Stand by statements with certified, verifiable data. This ensures stakeholders can trust the information presented, confident in its authenticity.


The driving force behind Connect is our core technology,[Cedalio](http://www.cedalio.com/) . A blockchain-enabled database designed to guarantee data transparency, verification, and integrity. With Cedalio, data used in applications can be traced back to its origin, ensuring it’s verifiable and auditable by default.


We’re committed to fostering meaningful change, collaborating closely with leading companies in their sustainability and data transparency efforts. We invite you to explore Connect and see the difference it can make. If you have any thoughts, questions, or feedback, pleasereach out to us . Your insights are invaluable to us.


Warm regards,


The Cedalio Team
