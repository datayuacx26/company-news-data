# [New Feature] New Electricity Consumption Fields for In-Depth Analysis

Managing invoices can be a tedious process, especially when you need to extract and analyze detailed information. **But with Cedalio’s latest feature, we’ve made it easier than ever to transform your invoices into actionable insights.**


Cedalio’s invoice extraction feature automates the process of capturing essential data from your invoices. Imagine being able to instantly access details such as consumption type, meter number, consumption period, and total consumption all at your fingertips. Whether the invoice provides a summary or breaks down the usage into **peak, off-peak, mid-peak, reactive energy, power** , Cedalio intelligently aggregates this information for you. We also take care of the financial aspects, extracting the total and **subtotal amounts before taxes** , and identifying any **advance payments or overdue balances** .


But what makes this feature truly powerful is what you can do with the data. With Cedalio, you can:


- **Streamline ESG Reporting:** Collect and organize your energy, gas and water consumption data effortlessly, making it easier to generate accurate and comprehensive environmental, social, and governance (ESG) reports. This ensures you stay compliant while providing transparency to your stakeholders.
- **Understand Energy Consumption:** Leverage detailed consumption data to analyze patterns and identify opportunities for optimization. Whether your goal is to reduce costs, increase efficiency, or meet sustainability targets, Cedalio equips you with the insights you need to make data-driven decisions.


By transforming your invoices into structured data, Cedalio not only simplifies your workflow but also scales with your business needs. Say goodbye to manual data entry and hello to automation that empowers you to extract valuable insights effortlessly.
