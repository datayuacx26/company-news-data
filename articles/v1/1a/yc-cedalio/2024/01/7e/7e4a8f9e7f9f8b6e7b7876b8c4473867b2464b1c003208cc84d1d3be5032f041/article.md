## Introduction


Digital Product Passports (DPPs) are not just digital records; they are dynamic narratives of a product’s journey through its life cycle. From inception and manufacturing to usage and eventual recycling or disposal, DPPs provide a comprehensive view of a product’s environmental and ethical footprint. For companies like[Cedalio](http://cedalio.com/) , this means offering a platform where every phase of a product’s life is transparent and traceable.


## 1. Design and Manufacturing (Beginning of Life)


- **DPP Role** : DPPs provide detailed insights into the sourcing of raw materials, manufacturing processes, and production’s environmental impact. This includes adding value-adding information like certifications or best practices of suppliers.
- **Cedalio’s Contribution** : Cedalio’s blockchain technology not only ensures data accuracy regarding material origins and processing but also verifies the sources of this information, providing a high level of auditability. This foundational transparency supports responsible production and precise compliance reporting.


## 2. Distribution and Retail (Middle of Life)


- **DPP Role** : As products move through the supply chain, DPPs record their journey, ensuring that each stakeholder in the chain adheres to regulatory and environmental standards.
- **Cedalio’s Contribution** : Cedalio’s platform enables comprehensive supply chain traceability. This ensures that, regardless of the number of suppliers in the chain, the authenticity and accuracy of information are maintained, providing peace of mind and regulatory compliance.


## 3. Usage and Maintenance (Active Life)


- **DPP Role** : In this phase, DPPs offer essential details on product usage, maintenance history, and repair guides, which contribute to prolonged product life and eco-friendliness.
- **Cedalio’s Contribution** : Cedalio provides users with access to extensive product information, enhancing their capacity for effective maintenance. For products like automobiles, this means a complete history of service and repairs, adding significant value.


## 4. End-of-Life (Recycling and Disposal)


- **DPP Role** : DPPs provide crucial end-of-life data, including material breakdown, disassembly instructions, and recycling options.
- **Cedalio’s Contribution** : Cedalio aids companies in offering transparent end-of-life information, supporting responsible disposal and recycling processes. This is particularly valuable for companies with product recall cycles, as it ensures transparency and avoids issues like double-counting in sustainability metrics. Cedalio’s system enables companies to manage these recalls efficiently, aligning with sustainability goals and regulatory requirements.


## 5. Renewal (Circular Economy Integration)


- **DPP Role** : DPPs are instrumental in the circular economy, where products or their components are reused or repurposed. They provide traceability and history, ensuring that even in a new lifecycle, the product’s integrity and compliance with sustainability standards are maintained.
- **Cedalio’s Contribution** : Cedalio’s platform can track and verify the continued life of products or materials, supporting circular business models and sustainable product redevelopment.

Incorporating the concept of the product life cycle into Digital Product Passports, managed through Cedalio, offers a panoramic view of their comprehensive role.
