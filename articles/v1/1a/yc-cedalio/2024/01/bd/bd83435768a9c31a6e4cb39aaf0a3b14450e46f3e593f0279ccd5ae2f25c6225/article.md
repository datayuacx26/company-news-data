## **Our tree-Step Framework for Sustainable Supply Chain Management**


1. **Defining which is your role in the supply chain:** Our framework starts with a clear understanding of your position within the supply chain and determining the specific claims you wish to assert. There are two primary roles to consider: Business-to-Consumer (B2C) or Business-to-Business (B2B). Typically, B2C entities opt for Digital Product Passports to share information with consumers, while B2B organizations favor methods that minimize time investment for claim substantiation. This stage ends with a curated list of claims or commitments that the company intends to disclose and the requisite data needed to back up them.
2. **Digitalization of Products and Materials:** The journey continues with transforming physical products and materials into digital data, laying the groundwork for advanced analytics. To do so, we ***Gather*** data using different mechanisms that can be automated to make the process seamless.
3. **Crafting a Reliable Database:** We build a robust database that serves as the ***single source of truth*** for all product-related information, from manufacturing to distribution. Even more, we use blockchain technology to make sure that data is reliable and has not been tampered with.


## A **fter implementing our product you will be able to:**


1. **Build Data-Driven Trust:** use this information to validate sustainability claims, thus building trust with customers who increasingly value eco-friendly practices.
2. **Make Strategic Environmental Decisions:** implement targeted strategies to reduce your environmental footprint, making sustainability a core part of your business operations.
3. **Share your data with clients and regulators:** share the collected data with your clients for their scope 3 emissions calculations and regulatory authorities, providing transparency and complying with regulatory requirements. This further strengthens trust in your sustainable practices and demonstrates ongoing commitment to environmental responsibility.


## **Use Case: Adelphi — Scotland’s Greenest Distillery**


Adelphi, known as the greenest distillery in Scotland, has embarked on a sustainability journey to revolutionize the spirits industry. They partnered with Cedalio to track emissions, water usage, and validate their eco-friendly claims. Let’s see how they successfully applied our Five-Step Framework for Sustainable Supply Chain Management:


1. **Digitalization of Products and Materials:** Adelphi began by digitizing their entire production process, transforming the crafting of their renowned Scotch whisky into digital data. They utilized automated mechanisms to effortlessly gather data, including details on ingredients, distillation methods, and sourcing of materials.
2. **Crafting a Reliable Database:** Cedalio helped Adelphi construct a robust database that became the single source of truth for all product-related information. To ensure data reliability and prevent tampering, they use blockchain technology, safeguarding their commitment to transparency.
3. **Data-Driven Trust with Digital Product Passport:** Adelphi took transparency to the next level by introducing a QR code on their product labels. When scanned, this QR code leads to a digital product passport, allowing clients to access real-time data on the product’s sustainability metrics, including emissions and water usage. This innovative approach further solidified trust among consumers who increasingly seek out eco-friendly products.
4. **Strategic Environmental Management:** Adelphi used the insights gained from their data to implement targeted strategies for reducing their environmental footprint. They optimized their energy usage, improved water efficiency, and explored alternative sourcing methods to align sustainability with their core business operations.
5. **Share your data with clients and regulators:** Adelphy not only shared their collected data with clients but also utilized it as the primary source for their regulatory Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) reporting. By doing so, they ensured complete transparency and compliance with environmental regulations, reinforcing trust in their sustainable practices while meeting regulatory requirements.


*We track each and every finished bottle of whisky and see each bottle as a unique asset and, through the use of QR barcodes and other smartphone scannable technology, deliver individual data to our customers that interacts with our bottles. This allows the story of your bottle of whisky to be told directly to you, because we can prove every step along the manufacturing process (thanks to the blockchain!), allowing you to discover the provenance of your bottle of whisky right back to its origin, building trust in our process and strengthening your knowledge of your whisky.*
