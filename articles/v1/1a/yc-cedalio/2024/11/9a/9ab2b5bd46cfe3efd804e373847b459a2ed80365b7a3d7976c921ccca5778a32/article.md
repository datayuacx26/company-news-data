# 🚀 New Feature Alert: Fuel Receipts & Tickets Now Supported on Cedalio! 🚀

We’re thrilled to introduce a powerful new capability on the Cedalio platform! Expanding our mission to streamline ESG data collection, we’ve now added **fuel receipts and tickets** to our suite of automated data extraction tools, which already includes electricity, water, and gas bills. With this enhancement, you can achieve a new level of insight and precision for your Scope 2 emissions reporting.


Here’s how this feature can empower your operations:


- **Automated Fuel Data Extraction** : Instantly capture the exact volume of fuel purchased for every vehicle across your fleet. Ensure comprehensive, accurate fuel data collection for improved reporting and tracking.


- **Pricing Anomaly Detection** : Easily detect pricing anomalies by comparing per-liter fuel costs across different regions. Gain visibility into any unexpected cost variances by fuel type, enabling better fuel expense management.
- **Fuel Consumption Monitoring** : Track fuel consumption patterns over time, allowing you to identify inconsistencies or trends in usage that could impact your bottom line.


This new feature delivers greater transparency and control over your fleet’s fuel data, equipping your team to make more informed, data-driven decisions to optimize fuel use and manage costs effectively.
