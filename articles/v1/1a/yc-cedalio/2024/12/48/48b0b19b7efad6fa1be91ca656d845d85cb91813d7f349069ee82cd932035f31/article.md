In the fast-paced world of sustainability, businesses are increasingly tasked with not only tracking their energy consumption but also translating it into meaningful CO2 emissions data. At Cedalio, we understand these challenges—and we’re here to change the game.


### **Introducing Cedalio’s CO2 Emissions Tracking Feature**


Our latest feature empowers businesses to convert their utility data into precise CO2 emissions metrics effortlessly. By automating emissions calculations for electricity, gas, water, and fuel consumption, Cedalio eliminates the headaches of manual processes and delivers actionable insights at lightning speed.

### **Why This Feature Matters to Your Business**


**1. Goodbye, Spreadsheets—Hello, Efficiency**
Managing CO2 data with spreadsheets is time-consuming, prone to error, and difficult to scale. Cedalio eliminates the need for manual data entry and maintenance by automating emissions calculations directly from your utility bills. With Cedalio, your team spends less time wrestling with Excel and more time driving impactful sustainability initiatives.


**2. Instant CO2 Insights**
Time is money, and with Cedalio’s real-time calculations, you can immediately see CO2 metrics for every utility data point. This speed means faster iterations on sustainability strategies, such as evaluating renewable energy purchases or identifying energy efficiency opportunities across locations.


**3. Customizable for Your Needs**
Every business is unique, which is why Cedalio allows you to upload your own **custom emission factors** . Whether your operations span multiple countries or have industry-specific requirements, Cedalio ensures your CO2 calculations are tailored to your exact needs.


**4. Seamless Integration with Your Workflow**
From the **Explorer View** to detailed **data point displays** and **CSV exports** , CO2 metrics are fully integrated into Cedalio’s platform. You can even include emissions data in GRI Reports for Scope 1 and 2 tracking, making regulatory compliance smoother than ever.

### **How Cedalio Speeds Up CO2 Multiplier Iterations**


One of the most challenging aspects of emissions tracking is aligning data with ever-changing emission factors based on country, utility type, and even time period. Cedalio takes care of this complexity by:


- Matching each utility data point with the most accurate emission factor at the time of entry.
- Allowing users to upload updated emission factors yearly, automatically applying them to new data points.
- Simplifying updates to CO2 multipliers, so you can test different scenarios quickly and with confidence.
