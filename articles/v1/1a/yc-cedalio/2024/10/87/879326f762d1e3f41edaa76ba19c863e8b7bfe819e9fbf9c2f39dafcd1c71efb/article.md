# Food Delivery Giant Optimizes Carbon Reporting for 5000 Locations

## **The Challenge**


One of the world’s largest food delivery companies, headquartered in Germany, needed to gather energy consumption data from over 5000 mini-markets, warehouses, and offices around the globe. They were required to meet strict regulatory compliance and wanted to ensure accuracy in their carbon accounting system. With complex regulatory requirements, they needed a solution that could consolidate this data in real-time and feed it directly into their carbon accounting software, Watershed, for accurate regulatory reporting.


## **The Solution**


Cedalio provided a streamlined solution that captures and organizes energy consumption data from thousands of locations worldwide. Through a direct API integration with Watershed, Cedalio automated the data flow, ensuring that every kilowatt-hour is accurately accounted for in their carbon reports. Cedalio’s anomaly detection also helped flag unusual spikes in energy use, which led to targeted investigations and immediate fixes.


## **The Impact**


- **Regulatory Compliance** : Automated accurate reporting to meet European regulations.
- **Data Integration** : Seamless API connection with Watershed, removing manual data transfer.
- **Increased Visibility** : Real-time tracking of energy consumption trends across all global locations.
- **Cost Savings** : Anomaly detection helped uncover faulty equipment at 12 warehouses, reducing excess energy use by 15% and saving the company thousands in unnecessary expenses.
- **Time Savings** : Reduced data aggregation time from weeks to hours.
