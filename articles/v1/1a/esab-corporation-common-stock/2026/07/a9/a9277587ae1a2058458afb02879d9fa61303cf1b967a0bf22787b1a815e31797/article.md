# Ranjana Bhagwakar Joins ESAB Corporation as EVP of Enterprise Operations

*Ranjana (RJ) Bhagwakar*


ESAB Corporation announced the appointment of Ranjana (RJ) Bhagwakar as Executive Vice President, Enterprise Operations. Bhagwakar will lead ESAB’s enterprise operations, overseeing manufacturing, sourcing, products, quality, and integration to drive faster, more consistent execution across the


business to support long-term value creation.


Her appointment reflects ESAB’s continued evolution into a premier industrial compounder, with a differentiated portfolio spanning fabrication technology, gas control, and inspection and monitoring solutions that together enable customers across more of the industrial workflow.


“As ESAB continues to grow and evolve, consistent execution across our business becomes increasingly important,” said Shyam Kambeyanda, President and CEO of ESAB Corporation. “RJ is a proven enterprise leader with deep experience building scalable operating systems and integrating complex global businesses. Her leadership will help strengthen the capabilities that support our long-term strategy and continue creating value for our customers and shareholders.”


With more than 30 years of experience building high-performing, process-driven organizations at scale, Bhagwakar will help translate ESAB’s strategic vision into consistent operational execution across the enterprise.


“I’m genuinely excited to join ESAB at such a pivotal moment in the company’s evolution,” said Bhagwakar. “What attracted me most is the opportunity to bring together a collection of world-class businesses — each with deep expertise — and help them operate as one unified enterprise.”


Bhagwakar joins ESAB from GE HealthCare, where she served as chief lean and transformation officer, leading the global design and deployment of the Heartbeat Business System. Prior to that, she was vice president of High Growth Markets at Veralto, where she strengthened commercial execution and organizational capability in rapidly expanding markets. Bhagwakar spent nearly three decades at Danaher Corporation in senior finance and operational roles, helping build multi-billion-dollar business platforms through organic growth, disciplined acquisitions, and global operating systems. She is a certified public accountant and holds a Bachelor of Science degree from University of Mumbai.


“The foundation here is exceptional,” Bhagwakar said. “Our opportunity is to connect it — align our operating rhythms, share what works, and execute to the same standard across the business. By strengthening how we work across teams, we can create greater predictability, accelerate decision-making, and deliver stronger outcomes for our customers and stakeholders.”
