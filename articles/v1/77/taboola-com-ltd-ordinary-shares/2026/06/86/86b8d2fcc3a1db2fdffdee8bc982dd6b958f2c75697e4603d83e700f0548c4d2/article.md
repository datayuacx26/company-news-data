# Taboola Joins Cannes 2026

**New York, NY –**[Taboola](https://www.taboola.com/) (Nasdaq: TBLA), a global leader in delivering performance at scale for advertisers, today announced an upcoming panel at Cannes Lions 2026, which will explore how agentic AI is reshaping performance marketing and the open web.


**Who:** The panel will feature Krishan Bhatia, Chief Business Officer, Taboola alongside Steve Ellis, Senior Executive, Paramount+; Matt Groshong, Business Development Director, AdTech & MarTech, NVIDIA; and Jarrod Martin, Chief Transformation Officer, Omnicom Media. The panel will be co-moderated by Ryan Joe, Editor-in-Chief, ADWEEK and Imran Khan, Founder and Chief Investment Officer, Proem.


**What:** *Decoding The Agentic Advantage: Performance and Publishing in the LLM Era* will bring together senior leaders from across the advertising ecosystem to examine what it looks like when CTV, the open web, and commerce infrastructure stop operating as separate channels and start operating as a single, agent-ready ecosystem.


**When:** Thursday, June 25, 2026 at 1:00 PM


**Where:** The Adweek House, The Majestic Hotel, Cannes
