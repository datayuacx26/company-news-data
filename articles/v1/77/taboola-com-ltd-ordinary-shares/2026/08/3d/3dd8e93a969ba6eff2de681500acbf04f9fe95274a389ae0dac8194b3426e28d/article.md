# Advertising Awards Asia Pacific Names Taboola a Gold Winner for “Best Use of AI in Advertising”

Taboola (Nasdaq: TBLA), a global leader in delivering performance at scale for advertisers, today announced that it has been named a


**Gold Winner** in the


**“Best Use of AI in Advertising”** category at the


[Advertising Awards Asia Pacific 2026](https://marketech-apac.com/awards/advertising-asia-pacific-2026/) . The recognition is based on a successful campaign between


**Taboola APAC, Hilton Worldwide Holdings, and Dentsu Singapore** .


Results of the campaign demonstrated how


**Realize** , Taboola’s AI-powered performance platform, helped Hilton improve campaign performance by identifying high-intent audiences in real time beyond traditional travel affinity targeting. The campaign delivered an


**85% lower Cost Per Landing Page Engagement (CPLE)** against the campaign benchmark, achieved


**3.8x greater efficiency** than standard audience targeting, and drove significant improvements in brand metrics, including a


**+17-point uplift in Ad Recall** and


**+18-point uplift in Brand Image** .


**What:** The Advertising Awards Asia Pacific recognize excellence in advertising innovation across the region, celebrating campaigns that demonstrate outstanding creativity, technology, and measurable business impact.


**When:** The winners were announced on July 29 2026.


**Where:** The winners announcement can be found on the


[Advertising Awards Asia Pacific website.](https://marketech-apac.com/awards/advertising-asia-pacific-2026/)


According to the Advertising Awards Asia Pacific, the awards celebrate organizations and campaigns that are advancing the advertising industry through innovation, creativity, and technology-driven marketing excellence.
