a global leader in delivering performance at scale for advertisers,


today ann


ounced that it has been named a ‘Top Tech Provider’ on


[Digiday’s 2026 Top Workplaces](https://digiday.com/announcement/awards/announcing-the-2026-digiday-media-top-workplaces/) list.
