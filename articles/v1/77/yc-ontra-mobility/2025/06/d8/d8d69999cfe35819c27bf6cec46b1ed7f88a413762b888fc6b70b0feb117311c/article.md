# Introducing the Favorite Feature in the Ontra Mobility App

**NEW YORK, NY June 10, 2025** – Ontra Mobility today announced the release of its new favoriting functionality, a feature that enables users to save and quickly access their most frequently used transit stops and routes. This enhancement aims to streamline the user experience and make daily transit planning more efficient.


The favoriting feature allows users to mark specific stops as favorites, creating a personalized transit experience. Users can now easily access their saved locations with a single tap, eliminating the need to repeatedly search for the same destinations.


This new functionality is part of Ontra Mobility's ongoing commitment to improving the transit experience through user-friendly features. The favoriting system is designed to be intuitive and accessible, requiring minimal user interaction to save and manage favorite locations.


The feature is now available in the latest version of the Ontra Mobility app, which can be downloaded for free from the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.


## About Ontra Mobility


Ontra Mobility revolutionizes urban transit by providing a platform for cities and agencies to plan, integrate, and operate efficient, accessible, and sustainable bus, shuttle, and paratransit services. Their data-driven approach, developed by former Google engineers, optimizes routes and real-time dispatching to enhance ridership and reduce costs.
