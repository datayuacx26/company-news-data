# Unveiling the AI Platform for Transit Planning

**NEW YORK, NY October 14, 2025** – Meet Ontra Mobility's AI platform for transit planning: forecast demand, optimize fixed routes, integrate on-demand zones, and simulate what-ifs to balance cost, ridership, and convenience. Plan in weeks, not years.


Ontra's AI Planning Platform brings together demand forecasting, fixed-route optimization, and on-demand zone integration to design coherent networks that serve more riders with fewer resources. Agencies can rapidly test stop spacing, headways, route variants, and zone boundaries to understand the real tradeoffs between access, cost, and reliability.


Planners can compare scenarios side-by-side with clear metrics on access improvements, travel time changes, on-time performance, and operating cost impacts. Purpose-built workflows streamline stakeholder review and board-ready materials so teams can move from concept to decision in weeks, not years.


The platform integrates GTFS alongside agency operational and demographic datasets, ensuring analyses reflect real-world conditions. Results can be shared via interactive reports and exported for procurement, grant applications, and implementation.


## About Ontra Mobility


Ontra Mobility revolutionizes urban transit by providing a platform for cities and agencies to plan, integrate, and operate efficient, accessible, and sustainable bus, shuttle, and paratransit services. Their data-driven approach, developed by former Google engineers, optimizes routes and real-time dispatching to enhance ridership and reduce costs.
