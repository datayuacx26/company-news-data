# Meet the Ontra Mobility Journey Planning App

**NEW YORK, NY November 27, 2024** – Ontra Mobility today announced the launch of its new mobile application, now available for download on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. The Ontra Mobility app is designed to simplify public transportation, providing users with intuitive tools to navigate their cities efficiently.


Key features of the app include real-time vehicle tracking, personalized trip planning with multiple route options, service disruption alerts, and saved favorite routes and destinations for quick access.


The app connects to local GTFS data and provides accurate transit schedules and real-time vehicle locations to help users plan their journeys.


A key feature of the Ontra Mobility app is its design for seamless integration with on-demand transit services, alongside traditional fixed-route options. This provides a more comprehensive view of available transportation, helping riders choose the best way to get around.


The Ontra Mobility app is available for free download today.


## About Ontra Mobility


Ontra Mobility revolutionizes urban transit by providing a platform for cities and agencies to plan, integrate, and operate efficient, accessible, and sustainable bus, shuttle, and paratransit services. Their data-driven approach, developed by former Google engineers, optimizes routes and real-time dispatching to enhance ridership and reduce costs.
