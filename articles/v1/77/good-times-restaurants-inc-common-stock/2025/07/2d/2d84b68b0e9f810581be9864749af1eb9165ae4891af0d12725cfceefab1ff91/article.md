# Good Times Restaurants to Release Fiscal 2025 Third Quarter Financial Results on August 7, 2025

### Good Times Restaurants to Release Fiscal 2025 Third Quarter Financial Results on August 7, 2025


07/31/2025


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/967519051/files/doc_news/Good-Times-Restaurants-to-Release-Fiscal-2025-Third-Quarter-Financial-Results-on-August-7-2025-2025.pdf)


GOLDEN, Colo.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Good Times Restaurants Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.goodtimesburgers.com&esheet=54299108&newsitemid=20250731209724&lan=en-US&anchor=Good+Times+Restaurants+Inc.&index=1&md5=999534f8caca86de2a27f9cc4bbd686b) (Nasdaq: GTIM), operator of[Bad Daddy’s Burger Bar](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.baddaddysburgerbar.com&esheet=54299108&newsitemid=20250731209724&lan=en-US&anchor=Bad+Daddy%26%238217%3Bs+Burger+Bar&index=2&md5=1cc8db49dcaf6c679b4a87752f47afa2) and[Good Times Burgers & Frozen Custard](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.goodtimesburgers.com&esheet=54299108&newsitemid=20250731209724&lan=en-US&anchor=Good+Times+Burgers+%26amp%3B+Frozen+Custard&index=3&md5=b29e3c06720bd77b6c5c730941ed2410) , today announced that on August 7, 2025 it will release financial results for its third fiscal quarter ended July 1, 2025. The Company will host a conference call and webcast, following the release of its earnings announcement, to discuss its financial results.


A live audio webcast of the call will be available on the Investors homepage of the Company’s website[www.goodtimesburgers.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fattendee%2F678508173&esheet=54299108&newsitemid=20250731209724&lan=en-US&anchor=www.goodtimesburgers.com&index=4&md5=255f41a63b2d1dd1097629ce00276cb5) beginning at 5:00 p.m. ET on August 7th. The conference call can also be accessed by dialing (888) 210-2831, Conference ID 3024033. An archive of the webcast will be available at the same location after the call has been concluded.


**About Good Times Restaurants Inc.:** Good Times Restaurants Inc. owns, operates, and licenses 40 Bad Daddy’s Burger Bar restaurants through its wholly owned subsidiaries. Bad Daddy’s Burger Bar is a full-service “small box” restaurant concept featuring a chef-driven menu of gourmet signature burgers, chopped salads, appetizers and sandwiches with a full bar and a focus on a selection of craft beers in a high-energy atmosphere that appeals to a broad consumer base. Additionally, through its wholly owned subsidiaries, Good Times Restaurants Inc. owns, operates and franchises 30 Good Times Burgers & Frozen Custard restaurants primarily in Colorado. Good Times is a regional quick-service concept featuring 100% all-natural burgers and chicken sandwiches, signature wild fries, green chili breakfast burritos and fresh frozen custard desserts.
