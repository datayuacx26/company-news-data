### Good Times Restaurants to Release Fiscal 2025 Second Quarter Financial Results on May 8, 2025

- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/967519051/files/doc_news/Good-Times-Restaurants-to-Release-Fiscal-2025-Second-Quarter-Financial-Results-on-May-8-2025-2025.pdf)


GOLDEN, Colo.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Good Times Restaurants Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.goodtimesburgers.com&esheet=54245896&newsitemid=20250501177569&lan=en-US&anchor=Good+Times+Restaurants+Inc.&index=1&md5=0331df18a7d65cc452dff3fdeaff4dbf) (Nasdaq: GTIM), operator of[Bad Daddy’s Burger Bar](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.baddaddysburgerbar.com&esheet=54245896&newsitemid=20250501177569&lan=en-US&anchor=Bad+Daddy%26%238217%3Bs+Burger+Bar&index=2&md5=af21addaf9180d3eed539c18ffcf7c6e) and[Good Times Burgers & Frozen Custard](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.goodtimesburgers.com&esheet=54245896&newsitemid=20250501177569&lan=en-US&anchor=Good+Times+Burgers+%26amp%3B+Frozen+Custard&index=3&md5=3d6c94b39d711b614276df2ee741bd40) , today announced that it will host a conference call to discuss the fiscal 2025 second quarter ended April 1, 2025 financial results on May 8, 2025 **** at 5:00 p.m. ET. Hosting the call will be Ryan M. Zink, Chief Executive Officer and Keri August, Senior Vice President of Finance and Accounting. A press release with financial results will be issued prior to the call.


The conference call can be accessed live over the phone by dialing (888) 210-2831, Conference ID 3024033. The conference call will also be webcast live from the Company's corporate website[www.goodtimesburgers.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fattendee%2F776664090&esheet=54245896&newsitemid=20250501177569&lan=en-US&anchor=www.goodtimesburgers.com&index=4&md5=3b9e7f98aa4d21fa7a9b1f103f13fd25) . An archive of the webcast will be available at the same location on the corporate website shortly after the call has concluded.


**About Good Times Restaurants Inc.:** Good Times Restaurants Inc. owns, operates, and licenses 40 Bad Daddy’s Burger Bar restaurants through its wholly owned subsidiaries. Bad Daddy’s Burger Bar is a full-service “small box” restaurant concept featuring a chef-driven menu of gourmet signature burgers, chopped salads, appetizers and sandwiches with a full bar and a focus on a selection of craft beers in a high-energy atmosphere that appeals to a broad consumer base. Additionally, through its wholly owned subsidiaries, Good Times Restaurants Inc. owns, operates and franchises 30 Good Times Burgers & Frozen Custard restaurants primarily in Colorado. Good Times is a regional quick-service concept featuring 100% all-natural burgers and chicken sandwiches, signature wild fries, green chili breakfast burritos and fresh frozen custard desserts.
