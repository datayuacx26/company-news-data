GOLDEN, Colo.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Good Times Restaurants Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.goodtimesburgers.com&esheet=54580380&newsitemid=20260731240058&lan=en-US&anchor=Good+Times+Restaurants+Inc.&index=1&md5=4808216fa2af98c8fb3c23b33915df0a) (Nasdaq: GTIM), operator of[Bad Daddy’s Burger Bar](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.baddaddysburgerbar.com&esheet=54580380&newsitemid=20260731240058&lan=en-US&anchor=Bad+Daddy%26%238217%3Bs+Burger+Bar&index=2&md5=dd95f7c401788bde8f005afbe5dccb9f) and[Good Times Burgers & Frozen Custard](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.goodtimesburgers.com&esheet=54580380&newsitemid=20260731240058&lan=en-US&anchor=Good+Times+Burgers+%26amp%3B+Frozen+Custard&index=3&md5=cb341c224201f2979e2d9996de9ab2f6) , today announced that on August 6, 2026, it will release financial results for its third fiscal quarter ended June 30, 2026. The Company will host a conference call to discuss its financial results following the release of its earnings announcement and webcast.


The conference call can be accessed by registering online at[Q3 2026 GTIM Earnings Call](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fanalyst%2F477227420%3Fpwd%3Dh1PCo133&esheet=54580380&newsitemid=20260731240058&lan=en-US&anchor=Q3+2026+GTIM+Earnings+Call&index=4&md5=c6a5b7a1fe3268656f3f4bf80c553244) and you will be provided with dial in details. The live webcast will be accessible from the Company's investor relations website on the[Events page](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fattendee%2F477227420&esheet=54580380&newsitemid=20260731240058&lan=en-US&anchor=Events+page&index=5&md5=631ee6e5dfa7f31dc73f474e563e7bdf) . An archive of the webcast will be available at the same location on the corporate website shortly after the call has concluded.


**About Good Times Restaurants Inc.:** Good Times Restaurants Inc. currently owns, operates, and licenses 36 Bad Daddy’s Burger Bar restaurants through its wholly owned subsidiaries. Bad Daddy’s Burger Bar is a full-service “small box” restaurant concept featuring a chef-driven menu of gourmet signature burgers, chopped salads, appetizers and sandwiches with a full bar and a focus on a selection of craft beers in a high-energy atmosphere that appeals to a broad consumer base. Additionally, through its wholly owned subsidiaries, Good Times Restaurants Inc. currently owns, operates and franchises 28 Good Times Burgers & Frozen Custard restaurants primarily in Colorado. Good Times is a regional quick-service concept featuring 100% all-natural burgers and chicken sandwiches, signature wild fries, green chili breakfast burritos and fresh frozen custard desserts.
