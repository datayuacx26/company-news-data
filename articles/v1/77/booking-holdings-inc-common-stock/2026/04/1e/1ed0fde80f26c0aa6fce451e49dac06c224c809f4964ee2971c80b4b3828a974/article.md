### Booking Holdings to Make First Quarter 2026 Earnings Press Release Available on Company's Investor Relations Website on April 28


April 27, 2026


NORWALK, Conn.


, April 27, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- First quarter 2026 financial results for Booking Holdings (NASDAQ: BKNG) will be made available at approximately 4:00 p.m. ET on April 28 through a press release posted to the company's Investor Relations website:[http://ir.bookingholdings.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4668706-1&h=418000981&u=http%3A%2F%2Fir.bookingholdings.com%2F&a=http%3A%2F%2Fir.bookingholdings.com) .


Booking Holdings intends to hold a conference call on April 28 at 4:30 p.m. ET to discuss its first quarter 2026 financial results. The event will be webcasted at[http://ir.bookingholdings.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4668706-1&h=418000981&u=http%3A%2F%2Fir.bookingholdings.com%2F&a=http%3A%2F%2Fir.bookingholdings.com) and audio replays will be available on the website for seven days thereafter.
