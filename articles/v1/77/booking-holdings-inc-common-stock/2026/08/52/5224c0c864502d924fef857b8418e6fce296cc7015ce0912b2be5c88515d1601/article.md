The September equinox (this year falling on September 23) is more than a seasonal turning point. For centuries, cultures around the world have marked the equinox as a moment of balance, renewal and spiritual reflection, while astrologers view it as a significant shift in the cosmic calendar that signals the start of a new seasonal cycle. While the Northern Hemisphere welcomes the arrival of fall, the Southern Hemisphere steps into spring. Today, nearly four in ten (39%*) travelers consider spiritual influences when planning vacations, timing trips around the astrological calendar. From a mountain town renowned for its vibrant-colored fall landscapes to coastlines with captivating views of whales migrating at springtime, Booking.com explores six places to visit during the equinox where the wonder of the changing seasons brings a sense of renewal and reflection.


### **Northern Hemisphere** :


**Fall**


### [Vermont](https://www.booking.com/region/us/vermont.html) **,**[USA](https://www.booking.com/country/us.html)


September is one of the best times to experience Vermont, as the Green Mountains begin their spectacular transition into autumn.


[Best discovered by car](https://www.booking.com/cars/index.html) , the New England region of the Northeastern United States enjoys mild temperatures of 15–22°C (60–72°F) in September. These pleasant conditions make it the perfect time to explore scenic hiking trails, charming villages, and the region's renowned farm-to-table food scene. As the autumn equinox marks a season of change, balance and renewal, Vermont's iconic sugar maples start to glow with rich shades of gold, amber and crimson, creating a stunning backdrop for apple orchards, harvest festivals and countryside drives, before the peak foliage crowds arrive. For those looking to soak up the scenery, Stowe, located within north-central Vermont, is an idyllic base. Often considered Vermont's most picturesque mountain town, travelers can experience tours of the


[Mad River Valley and Montpelier](https://www.booking.com/attractions/us/prorgsjagsdx-small-group-tour-in-montpelier-mad-river-valley-vermont.html) , alongside endless opportunities to hike, browse independent boutiques, discover cozy cafés and experience the very best of New England in the fall.

### [The Dolomites](https://www.booking.com/region/it/dolomites.html) **,**[Italy](https://www.booking.com/country/it.html)


The UNESCO-listed Dolomites, a mountain region located within northeastern Italy, are at their most spectacular during the September equinox, when crisp mountain air, quieter trails and the first golden hues of autumn transform one of Italy's most iconic landscapes. As the seasons shift, visitors can witness


*transhumance* , the centuries-old tradition of herding livestock from high alpine pastures into the valleys, offering a fascinating glimpse into the region's rich cultural heritage. Adventurous travelers can immerse themselves in the scenery on a cycling tour through dramatic limestone peaks, alpine meadows and picture-perfect villages, while those seeking a slower pace can explore the historic city of Bolzano. Here,


[traditional food tours](https://www.booking.com/attractions/it/prlk87twojjo-bolzano-traditional-food-tour-do-eat-better-experience.html) celebrate the flavours of the Dolomites, with local cheeses, cured meats and mountain wines showcasing the region's renowned culinary heritage against a breathtaking alpine backdrop.

### [Kiso](https://www.booking.com/city/jp/kiso.html) **,**[Japan](https://www.booking.com/country/jp.html)


During the September equinox, Kiso, recognized as one of the most beautiful towns in the country, offers a peaceful glimpse into Japan's rich history as the surrounding forests begin to turn with the first colors of autumn. Once a vital stop along the historic Nakasendo Trail, the Kiso Valley is home to beautifully preserved Edo-period post towns, including Narai and Tsumago, where traditional wooden inns, family-run eateries and centuries-old customs remain remarkably unchanged. The equinox also coincides with


*Higan* , a Buddhist tradition, which falls twice a year during the fall and spring equinoxes, dedicated to reflection and honouring ancestors, adding an extra layer of cultural significance to a visit. Travelers can experience the region on the scenic


[Magome–Tsumago Trail](https://www.booking.com/attractions/jp/prqpby9nz7pb-explore-kiso-valley-magome-tsumago-mountain-trail-walk.html) , winding through mountain landscapes and tranquil countryside, or discover Kiso's renowned woodworking heritage in its artisan shops and workshops.

### **Southern Hemisphere: Spring**


### [San Carlos de Bariloche](https://www.booking.com/city/ar/san-carlos-de-bariloche.html) **,**[Argentina](https://www.booking.com/country/ar.html)


As spring arrives in the Southern Hemisphere, San Carlos de Bariloche comes to life with blooming wildflowers, snow-capped peaks and crystal-clear lakes, making the September equinox one of the most spectacular times to visit Northern Patagonia. With mild temperatures of around 10–15°C (50–59°F) and fewer visitors than the busy summer months, it's the perfect season to experience the region's breathtaking landscapes at a slower pace. Adventurous travelers can hike, cycle or take scenic drives through the UNESCO-recognized Nahuel Huapi National Park, discovering glacial lakes, ancient forests and panoramic mountain views along the way. Combining dramatic scenery with a sense of peace before peak season, Bariloche offers an unforgettable spring escape in the heart of Argentine Patagonia.

### [Tasmania](https://www.booking.com/region/au/tasmania.html) **,**[Australia](https://www.booking.com/country/au.html)


Tasmania, an island state located south of mainland Australia, is at its most enchanting in September, as spring brings new life, vibrant wildflowers and longer days to explore its ancient landscapes. Beyond its dramatic coastlines and old-growth forests, the island holds deep spiritual significance for Aboriginal communities, with many natural sites connected to thousands of years of cultural stories, traditions and ancestral knowledge. Travelers can experience this connection to the country through Tasmania’s wilderness, from the pink granite peaks and turquoise waters of Freycinet National Park to coastal trails rich with native wildlife.


[A half-day cruise](https://www.booking.com/attractions/au/prcmdrxzyl3t-wineglass-bay-cruise-from-coles-bay.html) from Wineglass Bay to Coles Bay offers the chance to discover the island’s iconic scenery, while spotting dolphins, seals, and sometimes even penguins, in addition to other native species along the way.

### [Garden Route](https://www.booking.com/accommodation/region/za/garden-route.html) **,**[South Africa](https://www.booking.com/country/za.html)


Garden Route, the 300-kilometer (186 mile) stretch along South Africa’s southern coast, is a spectacular coastal escape, where rugged cliffs, golden beaches, ancient forests and charming seaside towns come together along one of the country’s most scenic routes. Travelers can begin their self-drive adventure by


[hiring a car in Cape Town](https://www.booking.com/cars/index.html) and setting off along this iconic stretch of coastline. The September equinox marks the arrival of spring, bringing longer days, vibrant


*fynbos* , a unique, shrubland vegetation, and the start of one of the best times to explore the region’s natural beauty. It’s also peak whale-watching season, as Southern Right Whales migrate to the warmer waters of the Western and Eastern Cape, creating unforgettable wildlife encounters. Travelers can spot these majestic creatures from Knysna’s tranquil lagoon by embarking on a


[whale-watching excursion](https://www.booking.com/attractions/za/prvtmejgekzo-whale-watching-knysna-close-encounter-experience-ocean-odyssey.html) , hike through the lush forests of Wilderness or discover the pristine beaches and marine life of Plettenberg Bay. Combining adventure, wildlife and breathtaking scenery, the Garden Route is a perfect springtime journey through South Africa’s wild coastline.
