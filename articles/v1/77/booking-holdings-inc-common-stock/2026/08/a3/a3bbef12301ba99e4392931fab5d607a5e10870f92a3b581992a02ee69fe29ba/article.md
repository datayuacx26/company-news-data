# Hack Your Leave: Uncover Secret Staycations Right on Your Doorstep This September

Tucked away in the Antioquian highlands,


[Cabaña con Jacuzzi en Santa Elena](https://www.booking.com/hotel/co/hermosa-cabana-tipo-chalet-en-santa-elena-medellin.html) is a private, compact mountain cabin that offers a serene escape from the high-energy pulse of Medellín. Only 45 - 50 minute drive from central Medellín, it features a private jacuzzi terrace with panoramic views overlooking the dramatic ridgeline, the property blends rustic-modern interiors with an open fire and BBQ grill. September offers perfectly balanced weather, making it an ideal window for couples looking to explore nearby ecological treasures. Active weekenders can use this as a base to hike through the iconic Piedras Blancas Ecological Park or venture into the Arví Nature Reserve (Parque Arví) - a sprawling cloud forest sanctuary celebrated for its rich biodiversity, ancient trails, and native orchid species. Here, travelers can mountain bike through protected pine forests or visit traditional silletera flower farms that showcase the true cultural heritage of the region. After a day of outdoor exploration, the outdoor jacuzzi serves as the ultimate recovery ritual.
