# Traveller Review Awards 2026 Celebrate 1.81 Million Partners Worldwide and Reveal the Most Welcoming Destinations for the Year Ahead

### **Award-Winning Hospitality in 2026**


At a time when travelers are seeking authenticity more than ever, the Traveller Review Awards shine a light on the partners who consistently deliver memorable and trustworthy hospitality, and accommodations continue to take center stage.


[Apartments](https://www.booking.com/apartments/index.html) remain the most awarded property type for the ninth consecutive year with 901,481 awards, followed by


[holiday homes](https://www.booking.com/holiday-homes/index.html) (287,464), and


[hotels](https://www.booking.com/hotel/index.html) (197,658). Meanwhile,


[camping](https://www.booking.com/campings/index.html) and


[villas](https://www.booking.com/villas/index.html) saw the highest year-over-year increase in award recipients, each at 15%, followed by


[ryokans](https://www.booking.com/ryokans/index.html) at 13%. These patterns highlight travelers’ continued interest in unique, character-rich stays that offer a sense of place.


Rental car companies receiving an award increased to 1,977, a 49% increase from 2025, reflecting high levels of service across local and international fleets. Among them, the United States stands out with the largest number of recognized providers (203), with the United Kingdom climbing to second place (174) and Spain completing the top three (155). Airport transfer providers also made impressive gains with 137 awardees, representing an 11% year-over-year increase. These milestones underscore the expanding role of transport in ensuring seamless in-trip journeys and exceptional service at every stage of a traveler’s trip.


“From welcoming hosts who go above and beyond for guest stays to expert taxi drivers sharing ‘local-only’ tips, these personal touches can make a trip truly special for travelers,” said James Waters, Chief Business Officer at Booking.com. “The Traveller Review Awards continue to shine a well-deserved spotlight on our partners who help create lifelong memories for travelers. It’s one of our ways of saying ‘thank you’ from all of us at Booking.com and the hundreds of millions of travelers around the world."
