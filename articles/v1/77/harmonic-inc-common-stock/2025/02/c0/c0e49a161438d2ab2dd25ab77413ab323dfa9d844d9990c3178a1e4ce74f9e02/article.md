# Harmonic Leads Broadband Industry Transformation

The broadband industry is in a period of critical transformation driven by the rise of DOCSIS 4.0, deeper fiber deployments and the increasing demand for higher bandwidth. This has resulted in heightened economic and competitive pressures for broadband operators. Choosing the right technology partner with the necessary expertise, leadership and business stability will determine the success of their business evolution and longevity.


As some technology providers struggle with financial difficulties, layoffs and late technology rollouts, Harmonic is supporting operators with its unwavering commitment to innovation, focus on customer success and a collaborative approach to drive broadband transformation.
Today, Harmonic's cOS broadband platform powers over 33 million modems for nearly 130 operators


worldwide, including 14 Tier-1 service providers.


## Leading the Charge: Market Share Domination


As the industry shifts towards[Unified DOCSIS 4.0](https://www.harmonicinc.com/press-releases/comcast-and-harmonic-to-demonstrate-industrys-first-unified-docsis-4-0-and-fiber-solution-at-scte-techexpo24/) and increased fiber deployments, Harmonic has cemented its position as the clear leader:


- **98% market share in virtual CMTS:** This near-total dominance demonstrates the industry's trust in Harmonic's vCMTS solutions.
- **62% share of DAA node deployments globally:** We're at the forefront of distributed access architecture, enabling operators to deliver next-generation broadband experiences.
- **Strongest broadband innovation pipeline:** Our commitment to R&D ensures we continue to provide cutting-edge solutions that meet the evolving needs of the market.


## Empowering a Growing Number of Customers


Harmonic is setting the standard for next-generation broadband solutions, while others players still trudging through legacy limitations and slow transitions, placing it ahead of the rest of the vendor community as the true enabler of longevity and success.


## Driving Customer Success Through Innovation: The cOS Advantage


At the heart of Harmonic's success is the[cOS virtualized broadband platform](https://www.harmonicinc.com/broadband/cableos-core-software/) . This powerful solution provides operators with seamless DOCSIS and fiber integration as well as unparalleled flexibility and efficiency to deliver reliable, multigigabit broadband services. Harmonic customers leveraging the cOS platform enjoy several benefits, including:


- **Lower operating costs:** Operators maximize the value of the existing network, strategically utilize both fiber and DOCSIS, and ensure consistent reliability with a single solution, managed from a single pane of glass.
- **Faster network evolution:** Hybrid DOCSIS and fiber migration paths enable quicker and more efficient upgrades.
- **Future-proofed infrastructure:** With a scalable and adaptable platform, they are well prepared for tomorrow's demands.
