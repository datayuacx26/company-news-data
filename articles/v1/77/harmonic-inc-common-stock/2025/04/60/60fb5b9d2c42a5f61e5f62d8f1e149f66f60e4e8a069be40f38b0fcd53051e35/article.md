## DOCSIS 4.0 Fundamentals: FDX and FDD Explained


### DOCSIS 4.0 Basics


DOCSIS 4.0 is the next-generation cable broadband standard designed to deliver multi-gigabit symmetrical speeds and low latency. DOCSIS 4.0 has two implementations:


#### Full Duplex DOCSIS (FDX)


- Enables simultaneous upstream and downstream data transmission within the same 1.8 GHz frequency band.


- Minimizes latency and maximizes spectrum efficiency through advanced echo cancellation.


#### Frequency Division Duplex DOCSIS (FDD)


- Utilizes extended spectrum ranges, typically up to 1.8 GHz, to allocate separate frequency bands for upstream and downstream traffic.


- Offers enhanced upstream capacity for environments where high upload speeds or symmetrical services are essential.


### What is Unified DOCSIS 4.0?


Unified DOCSIS 4.0 combines both FDX and FDD capabilities. Unified DOCSIS 4.0 allows operators to deploy FDX and FDD, either independently with Remote-PHY devices (RPDs) installed in a same node, or with FDD and FDX capabilities combined into a same RPD.


Unified DOCSIS 4.0 maximizes value of existing infrastructures, while avoiding costly overhauling, and eliminating requirements for separate infrastructures. With the flexibility to switch between or to operate both FDX and FDD concurrently, operators quickly enhance service quality, ensure reliability and more easily adapt to evolving network demands with simplified network evolution.
