### Employee Spotlight


### Get to Know Senior Water Foreman, John Cave


John brings a unique set of skills to his role overseeing produced water operations in the Haynesville, where he and his team manage everything from facility operations to commercial agreements. A former PGA golf professional and logistics expert, he now channels that passion into leading the Expand Energy Charity Classic — the largest charity golf tournament in the Haynesville Basin.


*****How did you get into the energy industry, and what brought you to Expand Energy?*****
I was working for Lowe’s Home Improvement in their supply chain division, managing transportation for more than 100 stores across Pennsylvania, New Jersey and New York. I got a call from a recruiter in southwestern PA who covered our SW App operating area asking me to apply for a role out of the Tunkhannock, PA office. After learning more about the industry and meeting the team in Tunkhannock, I was really intrigued, so I gave it a shot. I stayed in the SW App area for about five years, managing logistics and produced water for our area locations. In 2022, I relocated from Pennsylvania to Louisiana to continue growing in my role and I’ve been in the area with my family ever since.


*********Walk me through a typical day — how would you describe your job to a neighbor****** ?***
No two days look quite the same. On any given one I might be dealing with saltwater disposal facility operations, produced water management, vendor relationships, commercial agreement negotiations, safety meetings, employee development, and a lot of internal collaboration. I’m fortunate to have a great team — we work together to anticipate and get ahead of the challenges in our part of the business. I’m fortunate to have a great team that works to stay ahead of the challenges we face, and thankful for a Haynesville leadership team that prioritizes safety in every decision.


*****You played an important role in executing the Expand Energy Charity Classic. How did you first get involved and why do you enjoy it** ?*** Golf is a passion of mine — earlier in my career I was a PGA golf professional at clubs in Florida and North Carolina. We convened a committee to plan the first Annual Expand Energy Charity Classic filling out all available slots, totaling 288 teams. For this year’s second annual Charity Classic were incredibly proud to raise nearly $375,000 to Oilfield Helping Hands, supporting oilfield workers and their families facing financial hardship, and The Community School in Shreveport, providing educational and therapeutic programs for children with autism and other developmental differences.


What makes the day special is the incredible sponsor support that makes us the largest charity golf tournament in the basin. Our business is fast-paced and high-pressure, so it’s wonderful to see all the fellowship and camaraderie the event creates. This year’s tournament was another huge success and wouldn’t have happened without the help of the committee that poured months of work into pulling it off.

### In the Community


During the past quarter, Expand Energy’s employees have been active members of their communities, supporting charitable initiatives in our operating areas. Highlights include:


- **‘On the Green’ for a Good Cause: The Expand Energy Charity Golf Classic**
On April 2, our Haynesville team hosted the second annual Expand Energy Charity Golf Classic. Thanks to a strong turnout of golfers, sponsors, vendors and suppliers, this year’s tournament raised nearly $375,000 for The Community School in Shreveport, which serves children with autism and other special needs, and Oilfield Helping Hands, which supports energy workers and their families in times of hardship.[Thank you to all the sponsors!](https://www.expandenergy.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/HV-Charity-Classic-Sponsors-2026.pdf)
- **Reeling in Smiles at Dream Hunt Fishing Day**
More than 25 members of our team spent the day volunteering with The Dream Hunt & Fishing Program, teaching young anglers how to fish and participating in team building activities throughout the day. The organization grants outdoor wishes to children living with critical illnesses, diseases or disabling physical handicaps, as well as children of fallen first responders — kids who love to hunt and fish and might not otherwise get the chance.
- **Pitching In for ‘Love the Boot Week’**
Our team rolled up their sleeves for Earth Day and the statewide Love the Boot — Louisiana’s largest anti-litter and beautification event. A group of our volunteers teamed up with the DeSoto Parish School Board to clear litter along the main road into DeSoto and we also provided tarps to help the DeSoto Parish sanitation department cover garbage truckloads. Other Expand Energy volunteers worked alongside the Bossier Parish Sheriff’s Office on a parish-wide cleanup.
