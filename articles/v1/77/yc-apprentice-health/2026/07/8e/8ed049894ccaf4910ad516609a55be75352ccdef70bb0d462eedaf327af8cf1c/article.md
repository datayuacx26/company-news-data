### Real-time operational intelligence reduces patient wait times


Improved real-time management of patient flow is needed to deliver an exceptional patient experience and reduce patient wait times. Even with the best staffing and scheduling software **,** the sheer quantity and diversity of patients, medical staff, and physical resources involved in care processes add a layer of complexity that slows down patient-flow *.*

**Requirements for high-performing patient flow**


- Real-time patient location
- Real-time patient status

**Real-time patient location**


Care teams need to be able to quickly locate patients to provide care or usher them to the next stage of their treatment. Care teams that rely on manual location entry are too often plagued by the wasted time tracking down patients and their status.

**Real-time patient status**

### Case Study: Improved OR Efficiency at Memorial Sloan Kettering (MSK) using RTLS


Memorial Sloan Kettering (MSK) Cancer Center opened a freestanding Ambulatory Surgery Center, Josie Robertson Surgery Center (JRSC) with RTLS systems for the purpose of facilitating perioperative efficiency and workflow. MSK’s Main Campus does not have RTLS technology, so the difference in the efficiency of anesthesiologists who have access to RTLS at JRSC versus anesthesiologists who do have access to RTLS at Main Campus was investigated. To that end, the study evaluated the duration from when the patient is admitted to the operating room (OR) and initiation of induction only for the first case of the day by attending anesthesiologist. The results show that the duration between admission to the OR and initiation of induction was significantly shorter in JRSC (with RTLS) than Main Campus ([1](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6034517/pdf/nihms969062.pdf) ).
