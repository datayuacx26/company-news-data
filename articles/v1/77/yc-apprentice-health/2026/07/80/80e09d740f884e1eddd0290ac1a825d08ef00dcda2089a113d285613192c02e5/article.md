# Managing patient wait time expectations makes a huge impact on their satisfaction

Resource Center


### Managing patient wait times expectations makes a huge impact on their satisfaction


**Prolonged patient wait time is a source of patient dissatisfaction**


There is a strong relationship between wait time and satisfaction ([Baalbaki et al., 2008](https://www.emeraldinsight.com/doi/abs/10.1108/17506120810865424) ;[Mehra, 2016](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/27477932) ;[Sorup and Jacobsen, 2013](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3844379/) ).[Barlow, 2002](https://www.emeraldinsight.com/doi/abs/10.1108/02686900210437507) and[Hensely and Sulek, 2007](https://www.emeraldinsight.com/doi/abs/10.1108/09604520710735173) found that as delays and wait time increase, dissatisfaction also increases.[Barlow, 2002](https://www.emeraldinsight.com/doi/abs/10.1108/02686900210437507) confirmed this finding in Emergency Rooms,[Bielen and Demoulin, 2007](https://www.emeraldinsight.com/doi/abs/10.1108/09604520710735182) across multiple services, and[Bleustein et al., 2014](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/25181568) in Primary Care.


[Bleustein et al., 2014](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/25181568) surveyed 44 ambulatory clinics within a large academic medical center and found that longer wait times are negatively associated with clinical provider scores of patient satisfaction, indicating that every aspect of patient experience – specifically confidence in the care provider and perceived quality of care – correlated negatively with longer wait times.


As waiting time to see a physician increases, so does patient anxiety, which relates to the patient experience and satisfaction with the provider ([Press Ganey, 2009](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/25181568) ).[Maister 1984](http://www.columbia.edu/~ww2040/4615S13/Psychology_of_Waiting_Lines.pdf) offers 8 basic principles that can be easily translated into the healthcare sector:


- Occupied time feels shorter than unoccupied time
- Time spent waiting to begin the service experience will feel longer than time actually spent in the experience itself
- Anxious waits feel longer than non-anxious ones
- Waits of uncertain length feel longer than certain ones
- Unexplained waits feel longer than explained waits
- Unfair waits feel longer than fair ones
- Solo waits feel longer than comfortable ones
- Interesting waits are shorter than uninteresting ones


RTLS-enabled wait time communication makes a huge impact


As we mentioned in our previous[post](https://www.apprenticehealth.com/resources/learn-how-rtls-driven-patient-scheduling-systems-are-the-key-to-reducing-patient-wait-times.html) , Dana Farber Cancer Institute clinically validated how RTLS data can be utilized to provide operational insights. They found that patients responded with **greater than 96% satisfaction with wait time communication** after an RTLS derived algorithm estimated and communicated wait times. Moreover, providers and leadership also reported increased satisfaction after the introduction of RTLS.
