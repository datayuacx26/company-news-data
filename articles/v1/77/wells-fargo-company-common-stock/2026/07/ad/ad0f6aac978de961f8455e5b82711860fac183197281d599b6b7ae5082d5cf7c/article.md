**SAN FRANCISCO** – Wells Fargo today announced the launch of AI Teammate, a new artificial intelligence (AI)–powered capability embedded within[Advisor Gateway](https://newsroom.wf.com/news-releases/news-details/2026/Wells-Fargo-launches-Advisor-Gateway-for-financial-advisors/default.aspx) that helps streamline how financial advisors, client associates, and support teams access information and complete everyday work.


AI Teammate marks a milestone in Wells Fargo’s broader effort to apply AI in practical, high-impact ways for the benefit of advisors. By connecting advisors to the information, tools, and workflows they use every day, AI Teammate helps reduce friction, accelerate decision-making, and enable more time for client engagement.


Over time, capabilities will expand to help advisors uncover new opportunities, prepare for client conversations with personalized insights, and focus on the actions that can strengthen client relationships and support practice growth. AI Teammate will be available to financial advisors across all Wealth & Investment Management (WIM) advisor channels, including independent advisors who are part of Wells Fargo Advisors Financial Network, “FiNet.”


“We are making significant investments in AI and technology to empower advisors, grow client relationships, and gain market share,” said Sol Gindi, head of the WIM Client Relationship Group and Wells Fargo Advisors. “AI Teammate builds on the foundation of Advisor Gateway and represents an important step in our broader vision for AI. We have a pipeline of additional AI tools in development that are all geared to helping our people focus on clients, deepen relationships, and deliver everything Wells Fargo has to offer more effectively.”


## Bringing information, tools, and workflows together


AI Teammate introduces a chat-based experience embedded within the firm’s central technology platform, Advisor Gateway. It enables users to ask questions in plain language, retrieve answers quickly, and take action.


In its initial phase, AI Teammate helps teams search and summarize information from WFA’s core resource center to:


- Find answers faster with plain-language search
- Quickly summarize product, process, and workflow information
- Move work forward with guided workflows and clear next steps
- Spend less time navigating multiple pages by bringing relevant information together in one place


This approach reflects a broader industry shift toward simplifying the advisor experience by applying AI to remove friction from high-frequency tasks and improve day-to-day productivity.


“By embedding AI into natural workflow entry points, we can help our teams work more efficiently and make better use of the insights available to them,” said Andre Mansour, head of WIM AI.


“AI Teammate advances the progress we’ve made in delivering a more seamless and intuitive advisor experience,” said Eve Varner, head of the WIM Chief Product Office. “It’s about making the tools advisors use every day more connected, more useful, and ultimately more effective in helping them serve clients.”
