**SAN FRANCISCO** – First Clearing today announced that the Retail Equity Sales and Trading Business arm of Brean Capital, LLC, has joined its platform for clearing and custody services, marking a return to First Clearing where the business previously operated under the FIG Partners name from July 2003 to May 2019.


Through the agreement, Brean Capital’s Retail Equity Sales and Trading business will have access to First Clearing’s clearing, custody, and technology capabilities to support its advisors and clients while maintaining its independent brand and client relationships.


This announcement reflects First Clearing’s commitment to investing in its platform to best serve a range of advisor practices, whether they are broker-dealers or registered investment advisors.


“Brean Capital has built a strong Retail Equity Sales and Trading business, and we’re pleased to support its next phase of growth,” said Leeann Markovitz, Head of First Clearing at Wells Fargo. “We are proud to provide a comprehensive platform, experienced service teams, and the clearing, custody, and technology capabilities that can help its advisors and clients operate with efficiency and confidence.”


Eric Lawless, Head of Equity Sales and Trading at Brean Capital, added, “We are excited to have the Retail Equity Sales and Trading division of Brean Capital join the First Clearing Platform. We had a great experience with this firm in previous years, and we are confident that our new relationship will help our business continue to grow,”


Established in 1983 as part of the Wealth & Investment Management division of Wells Fargo, First Clearing provides a fully integrated offering including clearing, custody, technology, and practice support, designed to help firms scale their businesses while navigating evolving market and regulatory complexities.
