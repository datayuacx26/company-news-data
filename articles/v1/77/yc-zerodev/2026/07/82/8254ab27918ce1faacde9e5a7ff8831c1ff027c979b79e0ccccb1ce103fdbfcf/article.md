# Solana Gas Sponsorship: ZeroDev Brings Seamless UX to the SVM

Web3 has a "first five minutes" problem. You build a world-class DApp on Solana, your marketing hits the mark, and a user finally lands on your site—only to be told they need to go to a centralized exchange, buy SOL, and transfer it back just to click a button. That isn't a user journey; it’s a brick wall.


At Offchain Labs, we believe your headlines and your products should be bold, authoritative, and focused on builder velocity. Today, we are officially expanding the ZeroDev stack beyond the EVM. ZeroDev’s Solana Gas Sponsorship is live.


The End of Infrastructure Juggling


For too long, the industry has asked users to act like network engineers—juggling chains, bridging assets, and managing gas balances. If we want blockchain to reach its full potential, we must challenge perception and make interfaces feel inescapably normal—transforming technical friction into accelerated business flow.


By introducing our industry-leading paymaster logic to the Solana Virtual Machine (SVM), we are eliminating technical friction for our audience. Developers can now sponsor transactions entirely, allowing users to interact with Solana-based apps without ever holding a single lamport of SOL.


Built for the Way You Actually Build


Generic tools often force you to choose between ease of use and technical flexibility. We don't believe in compromises. The ZeroDev Solana paymaster is engineered to be the most extensible foundation in the industry.


- Universal Compatibility: Whether your users are using a traditional wallet like Phantom or a complex multisig, you can pay their gas.
- PDA Support: We’ve optimized our infrastructure to work seamlessly with Program Derived Addresses (PDAs).
- Battle-Tested Logic: This isn't experimental; we’ve brought the same robust logic used by the world’s largest wallets to the SVM, specifically engineered for scale.

FAQ: Solana Gas Sponsorship & UX


How does ZeroDev's Solana Gas Sponsorship work?


ZeroDev’s Solana Gas Sponsorship allows Solana-based applications to sponsor transaction fees for users. It works across traditional wallets like Phantom, multisigs, and Program Derived Addresses (PDAs) to eliminate the need for users to hold SOL.


Does ZeroDev sponsor gas for Solana PDAs (Program Derived Addresses)?


Yes, ZeroDev handles the infrastructure for both wallet accounts and PDAs. You can abstract 100% of network complexity with chain abstraction that works across every account type.


Can I use a Phantom wallet with ZeroDev's Solana paymaster?


Absolutely. No matter whether your users are using a traditional wallet like Phantom or a multisig, you can pay gas for them using the ZeroDev Solana paymaster.
