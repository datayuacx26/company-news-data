# Introducing ZeroDev Wallet

ZeroDev Wallet is here.


It completes the[ZeroDev](https://zerodev.app/) platform by giving teams one place to build, launch, and scale embedded, programmable account experiences.


ZeroDev Wallet is an embedded wallet built on ZeroDev’s programmable account infrastructure. It gives teams one stack for wallet creation, signing, smart account execution, gas sponsorship, session keys, policies, recovery options, and transaction orchestration.


Until now, teams building with ZeroDev still needed to bring a separate signer or wallet provider. They could use ZeroDev for smart accounts, paymasters, bundlers, gas abstraction, session keys, policies, and transaction infrastructure, but the wallet layer lived somewhere else.


**ZeroDev Wallet changes that.**


Teams can now build the full onchain account experience through ZeroDev, from wallet creation and familiar sign-in to gasless transactions, asset management, recovery options, and automated onchain flows.


That matters because the next wave of onchain products will not feel like crypto products at all. Users will not think about wallets, gas, chains, approvals, signatures, or recovery flows. They will sign in, take action, and move on. But making that experience feel simple requires a lot of infrastructure behind the scenes.


**Why ZeroDev Wallet**


Onchain UX is still too fragmented for most users.


People should not need to manage native gas tokens, switch chains, approve multiple transactions, configure a wallet, or understand signing flows just to use an app.


And product teams should not need to stitch together an embedded wallet provider, signer, smart account implementation, bundler, paymaster, policy layer, session key system, and recovery flow just to ship a better experience.


For enterprise and fintech teams, that fragmentation creates more than UX friction. It creates integration complexity, vendor sprawl, maintenance overhead, and more places for the user experience to break.


ZeroDev Wallet brings the wallet layer into the ZeroDev platform so teams can build embedded wallet UX and programmable account infrastructure together.


The result is a cleaner stack for developers and a simpler experience for users.


**Built for enterprise and fintech onchain products**


Enterprise and fintech teams need account infrastructure that can support real product requirements.


They need onboarding that feels familiar. They need reliable signing. They need gasless transactions. They need recovery options. They need permissioned actions, policy controls, and transaction flows that match the product experience users already expect.


ZeroDev Wallet is designed for teams that want to bring financial, consumer, or enterprise workflows onchain without exposing users to the complexity underneath.


At launch, teams can build wallet experiences with email, oAuth, and passkey signers. They can integrate through wagmi, support EIP-1193 compliant transaction flows, and build on any chain currently supported by ZeroDev.


Because ZeroDev Wallet is built into the same platform as ZeroDev’s smart accounts, paymasters, bundlers, session keys, and policy infrastructure, teams can move from simple onboarding to more advanced account orchestration without rebuilding their stack.


Designed for production teams


ZeroDev Wallet was developed for teams evaluating production onchain deployments, including enterprise and fintech products where security, reliability, and operational confidence matter.


For teams reviewing ZeroDev Wallet for production use, the ZeroDev team can walk through architecture, implementation, and security considerations during[the demo process](https://zerodev.app/book-a-demo) .


**What ZeroDev Wallet enables**


ZeroDev Wallet is built for teams that want embedded wallet UX with smart account depth.


Teams can use ZeroDev Wallet to support:


- embedded wallet onboarding
- smart accounts from day one
- email, OAuth, and passkey signers
- gasless transactions
- sponsored transactions through paymasters
- session keys
- policies and permissions
- account recovery options
- transaction batching
- asset management
- automated onchain flows
- account orchestration across the user lifecycle


For users, this means fewer crypto-native steps between intent and action.


For developers, it means a more complete account platform for building product-native onchain experiences.


Instead of separately configuring a wallet provider, signer, smart account implementation, bundler, paymaster, recovery system, session key infrastructure, and policy layer, teams can build through ZeroDev.


**Programmable accounts from the start**


Most embedded wallet products begin with wallet creation and treat smart accounts as an additional layer.


**ZeroDev starts from programmable accounts. That difference matters.**


Programmable accounts give teams more control over how onchain actions happen inside their product. They make it possible to design flows around permissions, recovery, automation, gas abstraction, session keys, batching, and product-specific transaction logic.


With ZeroDev Wallet, teams no longer need to choose between embedded wallet UX and smart account flexibility.


They can create a wallet experience that feels native to the app while building on the programmable account infrastructure needed for more advanced product flows.


That creates a path from simple onboarding to deeper account orchestration as the product matures.


**From wallet creation to account orchestration**


A wallet is no longer just a place to hold assets. For modern onchain products, the account layer is becoming part of the product experience itself. It determines how users sign in, how they approve actions, how transactions are funded, how permissions are scoped, how automation works, and how users recover access when something goes wrong.


ZeroDev Wallet gives teams infrastructure for that full lifecycle:


- create wallets through familiar authentication methods
- connect wallet creation to smart account execution
- sponsor gas so users do not need native tokens
- define policies and permissions for safer transaction flows
- use session keys to enable automation and recurring actions
- support account recovery options as products mature
- orchestrate transactions in ways that feel native to the product


For fintech and enterprise products, this is especially important. Users expect software to feel seamless, reliable, and familiar. They should not have to understand the infrastructure underneath before they can use the product.


**A cleaner developer experience**


One of the biggest blockers to better onchain UX is integration complexity.


To build a modern onchain flow, teams often need to connect:


- a wallet or signer provider
- a smart account implementation
- a bundler
- a paymaster
- gas sponsorship logic
- session key infrastructure
- policies and permissions
- recovery
- transaction orchestration


Each layer adds work. Each integration adds maintenance. Each handoff creates another place for the experience to feel assembled instead of designed.


ZeroDev Wallet reduces that surface area.


Teams can use ZeroDev as the platform for wallet creation, smart account configuration, gasless execution, permissions, recovery options, session keys, and automation.


That means developers can spend less time stitching infrastructure together and more time building the product experience users actually touch.


**Use cases ZeroDev Wallet supports**


ZeroDev Wallet is designed for teams building onchain products where the wallet should feel invisible to the user and programmable for the developer.


A fintech app can let users complete onchain actions without holding native gas tokens.


A marketplace can batch approvals and purchases into fewer user steps.


A consumer app can create embedded wallets during onboarding and sponsor transactions behind the scenes.


A game or loyalty product can use session keys for limited, pre-approved actions.


An enterprise product can use policy controls to support more structured transaction flows.


An app with more advanced account needs can extend into recovery options, permissions, automation, and lifecycle orchestration without changing its account stack.


These are the kinds of product experiences programmable accounts make possible.


**ZeroDev Wallet vs. a standalone embedded wallet**


A standalone embedded wallet typically helps users create and access a wallet.


ZeroDev Wallet combines embedded wallet onboarding with ZeroDev’s programmable account infrastructure.


That means teams can create wallets, support familiar sign-in, sponsor transactions, configure smart accounts, use session keys, define policies, support recovery options, and orchestrate onchain actions through one platform.


For teams building enterprise, fintech, or consumer onchain products, that difference matters.


The goal is not just to create a wallet. The goal is to create an account experience that supports the full product lifecycle.


**The account layer is changing**


The shift is just beginning.


As more products move onchain, teams will need an account layer that is invisible to users, flexible for developers, and built for the complexity behind modern onchain apps.


That account layer will not be a simple wallet container.


It will be programmable.


It will support gasless transactions, safer onboarding, recovery options, scoped permissions, automation, and product-native transaction logic.


ZeroDev Wallet is built for that future.


**New brand. New wallet. Same focus: better onchain products, shipped faster.**
