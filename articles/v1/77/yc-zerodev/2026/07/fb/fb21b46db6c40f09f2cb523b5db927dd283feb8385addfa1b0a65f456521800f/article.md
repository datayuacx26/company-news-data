# ZeroDev Is Live on Robinhood Chain

ZeroDev is live on Robinhood Chain, giving developers account abstraction infrastructure for building better onchain apps from launch.


For teams building on Robinhood Chain, user experience matters from the first interaction. People should be able to open an app, understand what to do, and take action without getting stuck in wallet setup, network switching, transaction prompts, or confusing signing flows.


That is the gap account abstraction helps close.


ZeroDev gives builders the infrastructure to create smart account experiences that feel familiar to the user. Developers can use ZeroDev to support embedded wallets, flexible authentication, sponsored transactions, session keys, batched actions, and other flows that reduce friction for users.


For builders, this means more control over the product experience.


Instead of forcing every user through the same wallet-heavy path, teams can design onboarding and transaction flows around the use case they are building. A consumer app can make the first action easier. A fintech product can reduce repeated signing. A trading or investing experience can keep users focused on the product instead of the mechanics underneath.


Robinhood Chain gives builders a new environment for onchain products. ZeroDev helps teams build the account layer those products need.


With ZeroDev, teams building on Robinhood Chain can:


- Create smart accounts for users
- Add embedded wallet experiences
- Sponsor transactions
- Batch multiple actions into simpler flows
- Use session keys for smoother repeat interactions
- Design onboarding around the user, not the wallet


The result is a better foundation for apps that need to feel simple, fast, and reliable.


Onchain products are moving beyond early adopter UX. Infrastructure needs to support real product design: fewer dead ends, fewer confusing prompts, and fewer moments where users need to understand the underlying chain before they can do something useful.


That is what ZeroDev is built for.


We’re excited to support builders on Robinhood Chain from launch.
