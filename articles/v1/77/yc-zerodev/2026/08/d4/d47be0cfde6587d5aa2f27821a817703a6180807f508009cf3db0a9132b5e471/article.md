# July 2026 Newsletter

July marked another major step forward for ZeroDev.


More than $1 billion in outgoing volume moved through ZeroDev during the month. It is a significant milestone on its own and could signal the beginning of many more billion-dollar months as onchain products evolve from experiments into everyday financial infrastructure.


The network also executed 4.33 million UserOperations (UserOps), making July the third-largest month on record by UserOp volume.


These numbers represent real product activity: more accounts being funded, more transactions being completed, and more teams making blockchain infrastructure feel like an integrated part of the product rather than a separate task for users.


That same goal shaped what we shipped in July. ZeroDev expanded its account stack, went live in a new ecosystem, and helped partners turn programmable accounts into products people can use.


ZeroDev’s July Network Highlights


More Than $1B in Outgoing Volume


ZeroDev crossed the billion-dollar mark in monthly outgoing volume, reaching more than $1 billion during July.


4.33M UserOperations Executed


The ZeroDev network executed 4.33 million UserOps, making July its third-largest month on record by UserOperation volume.


A More Complete Programmable Account Stack


[ZeroDev Wallet](https://www.zerodev.app/embedded-wallet) launched in July, bringing embedded wallet onboarding into the same platform as smart accounts, paymasters, bundlers, session keys, policies, and transaction orchestration.


Product Spotlight: ZeroDev Wallet


A Programmable Embedded Wallet From the First Sign-In


Most embedded wallets solve the first step: helping users create and access an account.


ZeroDev Wallet is built for everything that comes next.


Launched on July 7, ZeroDev Wallet gives teams a smart-account-first path to embedded wallets within the ZeroDev platform. Users can sign in using familiar methods such as email, OAuth, and passkeys. Developers can then build account experiences that support:


- Sponsored transactions
- Transaction batching
- Scoped permissions
- Session keys
- Account recovery
- Automated transactions


At launch, ZeroDev Wallet supports wagmi and EIP-1193-compatible transaction flows. It also works alongside ZeroDev’s smart accounts, paymasters, bundlers, and policy infrastructure, eliminating the need to assemble the account lifecycle across separate systems.


The result is a cleaner product experience: familiar sign-in for users, programmable execution for developers, and one account stack that can grow with the application.

July Technical Changelog


ZeroDev Wallet Brings Embedded Onboarding Into the Platform


Embedded wallet onboarding now lives inside the ZeroDev platform. At launch, ZeroDev Wallet supports email, OAuth, and passkey signers, alongside a smart-account-first approach to building gasless, batched, permissioned, and automated transaction flows.


[ZeroDev Goes Live on Robinhood Chain](https://www.zerodev.app/blogs/zerodev-is-live-on-robinhood-chain)


Builders on[Robinhood Chain](https://robinhood.com/us/en/chain/) can now use ZeroDev’s account infrastructure to design onboarding and transaction experiences around their products.


The available capabilities include smart accounts, gas sponsorship, transaction batching, session keys, and programmable permissions.


Expanded Guidance for EIP-7702


ZeroDev’s July developer content explored how EIP-7702 can bring smart-account functionality to existing externally owned account (EOA) users.


The guidance covered capabilities such as transaction batching, gas sponsorship, and session keys, as well as upgrade paths that do not require users to begin with a forced wallet migration.


Partner Updates From the ZeroDev Ecosystem


Beezie Continues Growing on Base


[Beezie](https://beezie.com/) continued building momentum on Base with ZeroDev and expanded to Solana in July, a strong sign of the team’s growth across blockchain ecosystems.


World Launches USDC Paychecks Through Virtual Accounts


[World launched Virtual Accounts](https://x.com/useworldapp/status/2069167121842712588?s=20) , giving users a way to receive paychecks directly in USDC.


Employers do not need to understand blockchain infrastructure, while users receive a faster path from traditional payroll into an onchain account.


Vault777 Launches Its Founders Pass


[Vault777 launched its Founders Pass](https://x.com/UseVault777/status/2076678389612564697?s=20) , opening pre-registration and a waitlist ahead of launch.


Early participants can claim a pass, share it on X, and earn XP on the leaderboard. Vault777 is also offering up to $10,000 in launch bonuses.


What ZeroDev Is Building Next


The next set of ZeroDev updates will go deeper into the funding and execution layers behind modern onchain products.


The team is building a new foundation for moving funds across chains, with greater flexibility around how assets arrive and what happens after they reach their destination.


ZeroDev is also exploring a higher-level developer experience for translating supported financial outcomes into prepared routes and transaction data. Signing and broadcasting would remain inside the application’s existing wallet flow.


The goal is straightforward: let developers begin with what the user wants to accomplish, then make the underlying infrastructure easier to compose.


More updates are coming soon.
