# Best time to visit Rwanda: Weather, seasons and money tips

Rwanda is one of Africa's most rewarding travel destinations, known for its breathtaking mountain gorillas, rolling green hills and well-maintained national parks. If you're planning a once-in-a-lifetime wildlife adventure or simply want to explore Kigali and the country's natural beauty, when you visit can have a big impact on your experience.


The country's weather changes throughout the year, affecting everything from hiking conditions and safari experiences to accommodation prices and visitor numbers. Picking the right season can mean clearer forest trails, better wildlife sightings and a more comfortable trip overall.


For most travellers, the long dry season from June to September is the best time to visit Rwanda, especially for gorilla trekking. The short dry season between December and February is another excellent option. In this guide, you'll discover the best months to visit, what each season offers and practical tips to help you make the most of your budget.


## **Understanding Rwanda's weather and seasons**


Rwanda enjoys a mild highland climate throughout the year, so temperatures remain comfortable regardless of when you visit. Instead of dramatic changes between summer and winter, the country experiences two dry seasons and two rainy seasons, with each offering a different travel experience. The weather is influenced more by rainfall than temperature, making the timing of your trip especially important for outdoor activities.


The long dry season runs from June to September and is widely regarded as the best time for gorilla trekking, hiking and wildlife viewing. The short dry season, from December to February, also brings pleasant conditions with fewer muddy trails.


The long rainy season falls between March and May, while the short rainy season lasts from October to November. Rain usually comes in heavy showers rather than lasting all day, but trails can become slippery and some outdoor activities more challenging during these months.

## **When is the best time for gorilla trekking in Rwanda?**


If seeing Rwanda's famous mountain gorillas is at the top of your travel list, June to September is generally the best time to visit. This is the country's long dry season, when forest trails in Volcanoes National Park are firmer, less muddy and easier to navigate. While gorilla sightings are possible throughout the year, drier conditions make the trekking experience more comfortable, especially on steep mountain paths.


The popularity of this season also means demand is at its highest. Gorilla trekking permits are limited and often sell out months in advance, particularly during peak holiday periods. If you're planning to travel between June and September, book your permit as early as possible, along with your accommodation and transport. Planning ahead not only secures your preferred dates but also gives you a wider choice of lodges near the national park.


## **Where to go in Rwanda and the best time to visit**


The ideal time to visit Rwanda depends on what you want to experience. While the dry seasons are generally the most comfortable for travelling, each destination has its own highlights that make certain months especially rewarding.


#### **Lake Kivu**


If your idea of a holiday is slowing down and enjoying beautiful scenery, Lake Kivu is at its best between June and September. Sunny days and calm waters make it perfect for boat cruises, kayaking, lakeside cafés and beach resorts. The pleasant weather also makes exploring nearby towns much more enjoyable.


#### **Nyungwe National Park**


Nature lovers will appreciate Nyungwe National Park during the June to September and December to February dry seasons. With less rain, forest trails are easier to hike, making chimpanzee trekking, canopy walks and waterfall trails more accessible. Wildlife viewing is also more comfortable when paths are firm and visibility is better.


#### **Kigali**


Kigali is a destination you can enjoy throughout the year thanks to its mild climate and well-planned streets. Even so, the dry months are ideal for walking through neighbourhoods, visiting museums, shopping at local markets and enjoying the city's cafés without having to plan around frequent afternoon showers.

## **When is the cheapest time to visit Rwanda**


If your priority is saving money, March to May is usually the cheapest time to visit Rwanda. This is the long rainy season, when fewer tourists travel. As demand falls, many hotels, luxury lodges and tour operators offer lower prices, making it easier to enjoy Rwanda on a smaller budget.


The trade-off is that you'll encounter more rain, especially in the afternoons, and some hiking trails can become muddy. However, you'll also enjoy quieter national parks, fewer crowds and lush green landscapes that many photographers consider the country's most beautiful.


One important cost never changes: gorilla trekking permits. Rwanda keeps permit prices fixed at $1,500 per person throughout the year, so travelling in the rainy season won't reduce this expense. Your savings will come from accommodation, flights and selected tour packages rather than the trekking permit itself.

## **Frequently asked questions**


### **When is the best time for gorilla trekking?**


The best time for gorilla trekking is during Rwanda's dry seasons, from June to September and December to February. Forest trails are firmer, hiking is easier and photography conditions are better. Because permits are limited, book your trekking permit and accommodation several months before your planned travel dates.


### **How much is a gorilla permit?**


A gorilla trekking permit costs $1,500 per person for international visitors. This price remains the same throughout the year, regardless of the season. While hotels and flights may become cheaper during the rainy months, the permit itself is never discounted, so include it in your travel budget early.


### **What is the rainy season?**


Rwanda has two rainy seasons. The longest runs from March to May, bringing heavier rainfall, while a shorter rainy period occurs between October and November. Although rain can make hiking trails muddier, landscapes become greener, parks are quieter and accommodation prices are often lower during these months.


### **Is Rwanda expensive?**


Rwanda is known for premium eco-tourism, particularly gorilla trekking and luxury lodges. However, independent travellers can still enjoy affordable guesthouses, local restaurants and public transport. Your total cost depends on your travel style, making Rwanda suitable for both luxury holidays and carefully planned budget-friendly trips alike.


### **What currency does Rwanda use?**


Rwanda's official currency is the Rwandan franc (RWF). Many safari lodges, hotels and tour operators also accept US dollars, especially for larger payments. Carrying some local cash is useful for markets, taxis and smaller businesses, while cards are increasingly accepted in major towns and cities.
