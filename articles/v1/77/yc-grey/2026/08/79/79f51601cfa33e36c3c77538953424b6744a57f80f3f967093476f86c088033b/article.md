## **How work visa sponsorship works in Saudi Arabia**


[Getting a job in Saudi Arabia](http://my.gov.sa/) is often simpler than many people expect because your employer takes care of most of the immigration process. Once you're hired, the company applies for your work visa, obtains the necessary approvals and helps you receive your iqama, which is your official residency permit. Think of the iqama as your legal ID card in Saudi Arabia, it allows you to live, work, open a bank account, rent accommodation and access essential services.


Saudi Arabia operates under a sponsorship model known as the kafala system. In simple terms, your employer acts as your sponsor while you're employed. They are responsible for processing your work permit, maintaining your employment records and ensuring your residency status remains valid. Over the past few years, labour reforms have given many foreign workers greater flexibility to change jobs or leave the country under certain conditions.


The good news is that once your sponsorship is in place, settling into life in Saudi Arabia becomes much easier. Instead of navigating complex paperwork on your own, your employer guides you through each step, allowing you to focus on starting your new role and building your career in one of the Middle East's fastest-growing economies.

### **What is an iqama?**


An iqama is the official residency identity card issued to foreign workers living in Saudi Arabia. It confirms your legal status, allows access to essential services and is required for activities such as opening a bank account, renting accommodation and completing everyday transactions.


### **Do I need a job offer first?**


Yes, you usually need a confirmed job offer from a Saudi employer before starting the work visa process. The employer acts as your sponsor, handling the required approvals, paperwork and residency procedures needed for you to legally work in Saudi Arabia.


### **How long does sponsorship take?**


The timeline varies depending on the employer, documentation and approvals required. In many cases, the work visa sponsorship process can take several weeks, including document verification, medical checks, visa processing and preparation before travelling to Saudi Arabia.

Yes, eligible foreign professionals can sponsor residency visas for immediate family members, depending on requirements such as salary level, employment status and government regulations.
