# Best time to visit Kenya: Weather, seasons and money tips

Kenya is one of Africa’s best destinations. It offers some of the most notable wildlife safaris, beautiful beaches, and breathtaking mountains. Major cities are quite vibrant with a range of cultural experiences. However, the time you choose to visit largely determines how well you enjoy your stay.


The best time to visit Kenya is the dry season, from late June to October, when wildlife gathers around water, and the great wildebeest migration reaches the Masai Mara. January and February are also dry and good for safari. The long rains, from March to May, are quieter and cheaper.


So, whether you are coming for a Masai Mara safari, the beaches of Diani, or Lake Nakuru, timing your trip around the seasons makes the trip worth it. This guide covers the weather month by month, the best time for each type of trip, and practical money tips for getting the most out of your visit.


## Kenya's seasons at a glance


Kenya has two wet seasons and two dry seasons every year. Nairobi and the highlands are cooler than the coast, and the north is hotter and drier than the south.

Nairobi’s altitude is around 1,700 metres. The temperature is usually under 26°C. Coastal cities like Mombasa are usually warm, averaging 28 to 32°C year-round. The Masai Mara is warm by day and cool at night, particularly in July and August.


## Best time for a safari


The best time for a safari in Kenya is the dry season, from late June to October. As water sources dry up, animals concentrate around rivers and waterholes. This is why it is easier to see much of the wildlife during this period. The vegetation also reduces, so you can see further into the bush.


According to[Kenya's official tourism authority](https://magicalkenya.com/) , this period is particularly recommended for the Masai Mara, Amboseli, and Tsavo parks.


### **The wildebeest migration**


The great migration is one of the most dramatic wildlife events on earth. Around 1.5 million wildebeest, along with zebras and gazelles, move in a circular route between Tanzania's Serengeti and Kenya's Masai Mara. The dramatic Mara River crossings, where wildebeest charge through crocodile-filled water, typically occur between July and October. August and September are considered peak months for the crossings, though the exact timing varies each year.


### Best time with fewer crowds


January and February are dry, warm, and far less crowded than the main July-to-October season. Game viewing is excellent in Amboseli, where you can see elephants and Mount Kilimanjaro in the distance. Things are also relatively cheaper during this period than during the peak season.


### **Months to avoid for safari**


The long rainy season (March to May) is generally the least favourable time for safari. Heavy rain can make some park roads difficult to use, while thick vegetation makes wildlife harder to spot. However, many lodges offer significant discounts, making it an attractive option for budget travellers who don't mind unpredictable weather.


## Best time for the coast and beaches


Kenya’s coastline is bordered by the Indian Ocean, and this area has a slightly different climate from the non-coastal safari destinations. So, the best time to visit the coast and beaches is not the same as the best time to visit for safaris. Kenya’s coast includes Diani Beach, Mombasa, Malindi, and Watamu.


### **Best months for the coast**


January to March and July to September. The coast is warm year-round, but these months offer the driest conditions, light winds, and calm seas ideal for snorkelling and diving.


### **Long rains on the coast (April to June)**


Kenya’s coastal regions experience their heaviest rainfall between April and June. It is more humid, the seas can be rough, and some smaller hotels close temporarily. However, the region is still significantly less affected by rain than the inland areas. So, visitors who don’t mind the rain and want to enjoy the quieter atmosphere and lower prices opt for this period.


### **Short rains (November to December)**


This period, the rains are usually shorter and less predictable than the long rains. It typically doesn’t rain for long. This makes December dry and pleasant, making it a popular time to visit, especially for Christmas and New Year.


### **Sargassum seaweed**


Unlike parts of the Caribbean, Kenya does not experience widespread sargassum seaweed problems. However, small amounts of seaweed can occasionally wash ashore, so it's worth checking local conditions if you're planning a beach holiday.


### Cheapest time to visit Kenya


If you're travelling on a budget, March to May is usually the cheapest time to visit Kenya. Hotel rates, safari lodges, and international flights reduce significantly. So, if your priority is cost and you don’t mind the wildlife conditions, then April and May are your best bet.


Another good value period is November. The short rains are usually brief, visitor numbers are lower, and many lodges offer reduced rates compared with the peak safari season.


July to October is the most expensive time to visit. The Great Migration attracts visitors from around the world. Prices also go up during the Christmas and New Year holidays. Popular lodges in the Masai Mara book out months in advance for August and September. If you're travelling during these periods, book accommodation and safaris several months in advance.


### **Is Kenya worth visiting during the rainy season?**


Absolutely! Even during the rains, Kenya is still Kenya. Safaris still operate, the landscape is great, and the parks are far less crowded. While is might be more difficult to spot the animals, many travellers find the lower prices and peaceful atmosphere as good compensation.


## Money tips for Kenya


Kenya uses the Kenyan shilling (KES). Although card payments are becoming more common, carrying some cash is still essential, especially outside major cities.


### M-Pesa


M-Pesa is Kenya's most popular payment method. Many markets, taxis, cafés, and small businesses prefer mobile money over cash or cards. Most short-term visitors won't have access to M-Pesa, so carrying Kenyan shillings is useful for everyday purchases.


### ATMs


It is relatively easier to find ATMs in Nairobi, Mombasa, and other large towns. But once you move into the remote safari areas and coastal towns, it might be more difficult to find one. It is better to withdraw enough cash before travelling to national parks or smaller coastal towns. Most ATMs accept international Visa and Mastercard cards, although your bank and the local ATM operator may both charge fees.


### Paying by card


Hotels, supermarkets, shopping centres, and many restaurants accept card payments. However, local markets, roadside vendors, matatus (shared minibuses), and many smaller businesses are still cash-only.


### Exchanging money at the airport


Airport exchange counters often offer less favourable exchange rates than banks or ATMs in town. Exchange only enough money to cover transport and immediate expenses, then withdraw or exchange the rest after you arrive at your destination.

## Frequently asked questions


### **When is the best time for a safari in Kenya?**


If you want the best all-round experience, August and September are the top months to visit Kenya. This is when the dry season coincides with the peak of the wildebeest migration in the Masai Mara, making wildlife viewing exceptional. For fewer crowds and lower prices, January and February are also excellent months, especially for safaris and beach holidays.


### **When is the wildebeest migration in the Masai Mara?**


The wildebeest typically reach the Masai Mara between July and October, with the famous Mara River crossings most commonly occurring in August and September. The exact timing varies year to year and cannot be guaranteed in advance. The migration is a natural event that follows rainfall patterns rather than a fixed calendar.


### **What is the rainy season in Kenya?**


Kenya has two rainy seasons. The long rains run from March to May and are the wetter and more disruptive of the two. The short rains run from October to December, are more unpredictable, and are generally lighter. Safari parks can be muddy, and some roads become impassable during the long rains.


### **What should I pack for Kenya?**


Pack lightweight clothing for warm daytime temperatures, along with a light jacket for cool mornings and evenings on safari. Neutral-coloured clothing is recommended for game drives. Don't forget comfortable walking shoes, sunscreen, insect repellent, sunglasses, a reusable water bottle, and a universal travel adapter.


### **Is Kenya expensive to visit?**


Kenya sits in the middle of the range for African safari destinations. Budget travel is possible, particularly during the low season in April and May, when lodge prices drop significantly. Peak season safari camps in the Masai Mara are expensive by any measure, with some lodges charging over $500 per person per night. The coast offers more affordable options year-round.


### **What currency does Kenya use?**


Kenya uses the Kenyan shilling (KES). While some hotels, safari lodges, and tour operators may accept US dollars, you'll usually get better value by paying in Kenyan shillings for everyday expenses. Carry some cash for markets, local transport, and smaller businesses, as card payments are not accepted everywhere.


### **Can I pay by card in Kenya?**


In major hotels, upscale restaurants, and larger supermarkets in Nairobi and Mombasa, yes. In rural areas, national parks, local markets, and most mid-range and budget establishments require cash. Bring enough shillings for the portions of your trip away from tourist infrastructure, and withdraw from ATMs in cities before heading to remote destinations.
