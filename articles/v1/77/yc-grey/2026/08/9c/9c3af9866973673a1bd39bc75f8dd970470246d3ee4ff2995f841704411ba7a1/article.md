# Brazil visa requirements for US citizens: Full guide

Planning a trip to Brazil? Before you start looking for flights or building your Rio de Janeiro itinerary, it's worth checking the latest visa rules.


Brazil recently reintroduced visa requirements for travellers from the United States, meaning you can no longer arrive without prior authorisation. Fortunately, most visitors can now apply online through Brazil's electronic visa (e-visa) system, making the process much simpler than visiting a consulate.


As of 2026, US citizens need a visa to enter Brazil for tourism. Brazil reintroduced an electronic visa (e-visa) that you apply for online before travelling. You'll need a valid passport, a passport photo, proof of financial means, and the visa fee. Always confirm the latest requirements through the official Brazilian government website before booking your trip.


## Do US citizens need a visa for Brazil?


Yes. US citizens need a visa to visit Brazil for tourism, business, transit, sporting events, or artistic activities.


After several years of visa-free travel, Brazil reinstated the[visitor visa requirement for US](https://www.gov.br/mre/pt-br/consulado-miami/information-about-visas-in-english/electronic-visitor-visa-e-visa) , Canadian, and Australian passport holders on 10 April 2025. Eligible travellers can[apply online for a Brazil e-visa](https://brazil.vfsevisa.com/?country=United%20States) , removing the need to visit a Brazilian embassy or consulate in most cases.


The Brazil e-visa is intended for short visits and is designed to make the application process faster and more convenient. Once approved, you'll receive your visa electronically by email, which you should keep with your travel documents when flying to Brazil.


For most US tourists, the application requires:


- A valid US passport with at least two blank visa pages
- A completed online visa application
- A recent passport-style photograph
- Payment of the visa fee
- Proof of onward or return travel
- Proof of sufficient financial means, such as recent bank statements


Because visa policies can change, it's a good idea to check the official Brazilian government website before submitting your application or booking non-refundable travel.


## Brazil entry requirements for US citizens


Getting an approved e-visa is only one part of entering Brazil. Immigration officers may also ask you to meet several standard entry requirements when you arrive.


Before travelling, make sure you have:


### A valid passport


Your passport must be signed, remain valid for the duration of your stay, and have at least two blank visa pages available.


### A valid Brazil e-visa


US citizens travelling for tourism or short business visits must obtain an approved electronic visitor visa before departure. Airlines may check your visa before allowing you to board your flight.


### Proof of onward travel


Brazilian authorities may ask for confirmation that you intend to leave the country, such as a return ticket or an onward flight reservation. This is also part of the e-visa application process.


### Proof of financial means


Applicants currently need to show evidence they can support themselves during their stay. The official e-visa requirements include a recent bank statement showing transactions over the previous 30 days and a balance of at least $2,000.


### Health requirements


If you're travelling to areas where yellow fever is present, health authorities may recommend getting vaccinated before your trip. Depending on your travel history, additional health requirements may apply, so it's worth checking both the official Brazilian government guidance and the latest US travel advice before departure.


Meeting these requirements before you travel can help you avoid delays at the airport and make your arrival in Brazil much smoother.


## How to apply for a Brazil e-visa from the USA


Applying for a Brazil e-visa is entirely online and usually takes just a few steps.


### 1. Visit the official e-visa website


Start your application through Brazil's official electronic visa portal. Avoid unofficial websites that charge additional service fees or claim to speed up the process.


### 2. Complete the online application


Fill in your personal information exactly as it appears in your passport. Double-check spellings, passport numbers, and travel dates before submitting the form.


### 3. Upload your documents


You'll typically need to provide:


- Your passport information page
- A passport-style photograph with a white background
- Proof of your flight reservation
- Recent bank statements showing sufficient funds


Additional documents may be requested depending on your circumstances.


### 4. Pay the application fee


Once you've uploaded your documents, you'll pay the visa fee online using one of the accepted payment methods.


### 5. Wait for approval


If your application is approved, your e-visa will be sent to your email. Print a copy or keep a digital version available when travelling, as airlines or border officials may ask to see it.


## How much does a Brazil visa cost?


The cost of a Brazil visa for US citizens is US$80.90 for a standard electronic visitor visa (e-visa). This fee covers the application and processing of your visa and is paid online when you submit your application. The fee is generally non-refundable, even if your application is refused or you decide not to travel.


Before making your payment, double-check that:


- Your passport details are correct.
- All required documents have been uploaded.
- Your passport photo meets the official specifications.
- You're applying through the official e-visa portal.


If your application is incomplete or additional documents are requested, processing may be delayed, so it's worth taking a few extra minutes to review everything before submitting.


Keep in mind that visa fees can change. Always confirm the latest amount on the official Brazilian e-visa website before applying.


## How long does a Brazil visa take to process?


Most e-visa applications are processed within **up to five business days** , although processing times can vary depending on application volumes and whether additional documentation is required.


Because unexpected delays can happen, it's recommended that you apply at least two to four weeks before your departure date. This gives you enough time to respond if the authorities ask for extra documents or clarification.


To help your application move as quickly as possible:


- Complete every section accurately.
- Upload clear, high-quality copies of your documents.
- Ensure your passport photo meets the required standards.
- Regularly check your email for updates or requests for additional information.


Avoid leaving your application until the last minute, especially if you're travelling during peak holiday periods.


## How long can US citizens stay in Brazil?


With an approved visitor e-visa, US citizens can stay in Brazil for up to 90 days per visit.


The visa is generally valid for multiple entries over a period of up to 10 years, allowing you to visit Brazil more than once without applying for a new visa each time, provided each stay complies with the permitted duration.


If you wish to stay longer than your authorised period, you must apply for an extension through the Brazilian Federal Police before your current stay expires. Extensions are not guaranteed and are assessed on a case-by-case basis.


Overstaying your visa can lead to fines, administrative penalties, or difficulties entering Brazil in the future, so it's important to monitor your permitted length of stay carefully.


If you're planning to work, study, or live in Brazil, you'll usually need a different type of visa, as the visitor e-visa is intended for tourism, business meetings, transit, sporting events, and similar short-term activities.

## Frequently asked questions about Brazil visas for US citizens


### Is the Brazil e-visa mandatory for US citizens?


Yes. US citizens travelling to Brazil for tourism, business, transit, sporting events, or cultural activities must obtain an approved e-visa before travelling. Airlines may ask to see your visa before boarding, and you may be refused entry if you arrive without one.


### Can US citizens get a visa on arrival in Brazil?


No. Brazil does not offer a visa on arrival for US citizens. You must apply for and receive your e-visa before you travel.


### How long should my passport be valid to enter Brazil?


Your passport must be valid for the duration of your stay in Brazil and have at least two blank visa pages. Before travelling, always check the latest passport requirements on the official Brazilian government website, as entry rules may change.


### Do children need a visa to travel to Brazil?


Yes. Every US passport holder, including children and infants, must have their own valid visa to enter Brazil. Parents or legal guardians are responsible for completing the application on behalf of minors.


### Can I extend my tourist stay in Brazil?


In some cases, yes. If you wish to stay longer than your authorised period, you can request an extension from the Brazilian Federal Police before your current stay expires. Approval is not automatic and depends on your individual circumstances.


### Can I work in Brazil with a tourist e-visa?


No. The Brazil e-visa is intended for short visits such as tourism, business meetings, sporting events, or transit. If you plan to work, study, or live in Brazil, you'll need to apply for the appropriate visa before travelling.


### How early should I apply for my Brazil e-visa?


Although many applications are processed within a few business days, it's best to apply at least two to four weeks before your departure. This allows extra time in case additional documents or information are requested.
