# What is CHAPS? UK same-day payments

You might not come across CHAPS in everyday banking, but when you need to move a large amount of money quickly, it can become an important option. It’s particularly common when buying a property, making a large business payment, or completing another high-value transaction.


CHAPS is a UK payment system for sending large sums on the same working day with no upper limit. People often use it for high-value payments, such as buying a house. Banks usually charge a fee and have a same-day cut-off time. Unlike Faster Payments, CHAPS has no transaction cap.


If you have been told to make a "CHAPS payment" by your solicitor, bank, or estate agent, particularly when completing a property purchase, this guide explains exactly what CHAPS is, how it works, and when it makes sense to use it rather than the alternatives.


## What does CHAPS stand for?


CHAPS stands for the Clearing House Automated Payment System. It is a UK sterling payment system operated in partnership between the[Bank of England](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/) and the banking industry.


CHAPS is for large-volume, time-sensitive payments that must be completed on the same working day. Unlike some other UK payment systems, CHAPS transfers are settled individually and in real time, rather than in batches. This difference makes them faster and better suited to large, urgent transactions.


CHAPS has operated since 1984 and remains the standard payment method for the largest and most time-sensitive payments in the UK, including property purchases.


## How a CHAPS transfer works


A CHAPS transfer moves money from one UK bank account to another on the same working day, as long as you send it before your bank's cut-off time.


Here are some details on how a CHAPS transfer works.


- **Same-day settlement:** CHAPS payments are processed one by one in real time throughout the working day. This differs from other payment systems, where transactions are batched and processed in bulk. Once processed, CHAPS transfers are final and cannot be recalled. This is one reason CHAPS is trusted for high-value, legally significant transactions like property completions.
- **No upper limit:** Unlike Faster Payments, CHAPS has no maximum transaction value. This makes it suited for very large payments.
- **Cut-off times:** Each bank has its own cut-off time for same-day CHAPS processing, typically in the early to mid-afternoon (commonly around 2 pm to 3.30 pm, though this varies by bank). Payments submitted after the cut-off are processed the next working day.
- **Details needed:** To send a CHAPS payment, you need the recipient's account number and sort code, their full name, the amount, and typically a reference. Your bank processes the payment through the CHAPS system on your behalf; you do not interact with CHAPS directly.


### When to use CHAPS


CHAPS is best suited when the payment needs to arrive the same day, and this certainty is more important than the transaction cost. Here are some scenarios you should consider CHAPS:


- **Property purchases:** CHAPS is commonly used in the UK for property purchases because the funds need to arrive on the agreed completion date.
- **Large, time-critical business payments:** It can be useful for large invoices, business acquisitions, or other payments where a delay could lead to additional charges or contract issues.
- **Payments above the Faster Payments limit.** If the amount you need to send exceeds your bank's Faster Payments limit (commonly £250,000 to £1 million, depending on the bank), CHAPS is often the only same-day option available.
- **When completion matters:** Because CHAPS payments are settled individually and are generally irrevocable once processed, they provide greater certainty for high-value transactions.

## Costs and alternatives for sending abroad


Most UK banks charge £20 to £35 per outgoing CHAPS payment, although some banks with premium account tiers may offer free CHAPS transfer. Receiving a CHAPS payment is typically free to receive.


The higher fee compared to Faster Payments and BACS is due to the transfer speed, individual payment processing, and the certainty that the transaction will be completed on the same day.


CHAPS works only for domestic transactions within the UK. It is important to understand that CHAPS operates exclusively within the UK sterling payment system. It cannot be used to send money internationally. If you need to move money abroad, whether in GBP or another currency, you need a different payment system entirely. ## Frequently asked questions about CHAPS


### **What is a CHAPS payment?**


A CHAPS payment is a UK same-day sterling transfer processed through the Clearing House Automated Payment System, operated in partnership between the Bank of England and UK banks. It is designed for high-value, time-critical payments, most commonly used for residential property completions, with no upper limit on the amount that can be sent.


### **How long does CHAPS take?**


A CHAPS payment settles on the same working day, provided it is submitted before your bank's specific cut-off time, typically in the early to mid-afternoon. Payments submitted after the cut-off are processed on the next working day. Once settled, CHAPS payments are final and cannot be recalled.


### **Is there a limit?**


No, CHAPS has no maximum transaction value, which distinguishes it from Faster Payments (typically capped between £250,000 and £1 million depending on the bank). This makes CHAPS the standard choice for very large payments, including most property completions and significant business transactions.


### **How much does CHAPS cost?**


Most UK banks charge between £20 and £35 for an outgoing CHAPS payment, though some premium account tiers waive this fee. Receiving a CHAPS payment is typically free. The fee reflects the same-day, individually settled nature of the transaction compared to free alternatives like Faster Payments.


### **CHAPS vs Faster Payments: which should I use?**


For most everyday transfers, particularly below £250,000, Faster Payments is faster in practice (often settling within minutes), free, and available around the clock. CHAPS becomes the appropriate choice specifically when the amount exceeds your Faster Payments limit, or when an absolute guaranteed same-day settlement is a legal or contractual requirement, as is standard for UK property completions.


### **Can I use CHAPS to send money abroad?**


No. CHAPS is a UK domestic sterling payment system and cannot be used for international transfers.
