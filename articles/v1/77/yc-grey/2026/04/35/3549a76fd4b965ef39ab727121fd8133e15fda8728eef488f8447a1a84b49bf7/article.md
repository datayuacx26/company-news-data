# How to send Canadian dollars (CAD) to Canada with Grey

If you’re planning to[send Canadian dollars (CAD) to Canada](https://grey.co/send-money-to-canada) , you probably have a few questions about how the process works.


With Grey, you can send CAD directly to bank accounts in Canada via bank transfer or Interac .


In this guide, we’ll answer the most common questions and show you how to send Canadian dollars with Grey, including fees, transfer limits, timelines, and the details you’ll need before getting started.


## Ways to send Canadian dollars to Canada


Grey currently supports two methods for sending Canadian dollars to recipients in Canada: local bank transfers and Interac payments.


Both methods work with personal and business bank accounts across Canada, but they differ in speed and use cases.


## Bank transfers


A bank transfer sends money directly to the recipient’s bank account in Canada. It works with all major Canadian banks, which makes it a reliable option for payments like tuition, invoices, or supplier payments.


The cost is 2.50 CAD per transaction, and most transfers arrive within one business day.


There is a daily cutoff time of 18:00 Eastern Time. If you send your payment before this time, it usually begins processing the same day.


Because bank transfers move through the standard banking system, they’re often the best option for larger payments or planned transactions where instant delivery isn’t necessary.

## Interac transfers


Grey also supports Interac transfers, one of the most common ways people send money within Canada.


Interac is a domestic payment system that allows direct bank-to-bank transfers and is widely used across Canada for everyday payments.


Here’s how it works with Grey:


- **Transfer method:** In Canada, Interac transfers are typically sent using an email address or phone number. Grey uses bank account details.
- **What you’ll need:**


- Recipient’s full name
- Account number
- Institution number
- Transit number
- Address (city and postal code)


- **Fees:** 3.00 CAD per transaction.
- **Speed:** Payments are usually processed in real time, and in many cases, the recipient receives the money within minutes.


Because of this speed, Interac is often the preferred option for urgent transfers or smaller payments.


## Supported currencies for CAD transfers


CAD transfers can be made directly from your USD, EUR, GBP, or NGN balances, and Grey will automatically convert the amount during the transfer.


Before confirming the payment, you’ll see the exchange rate, the transfer fee, and the final amount the recipient will receive. This ensures there are no surprises once the payment is completed.


## Sending limits


The transfer limits depend on whether you are using a personal Grey account or a Grey Business account.


### Individual accounts


For individuals sending money:


- **Minimum:** 5 CAD per transaction
- **Maximum:** 10,000 CAD per transaction


These limits apply to both **bank transfers and Interac transfers** .


### Grey Business accounts


Businesses sending payments have higher limits to support larger transactions.


Grey Business accounts allow:


- **Minimum:** 5 CAD


Maximum per transaction:


- **Interac:** 25,000 CAD
- **Bank transfer:** 100,000 CAD


These limits make Grey suitable for:


- vendor payments
- contractor invoices
- supplier payments
- international payroll


## How to send Canadian dollars with Grey


Sending CAD to Canada with Grey is simple and can be completed in just a few steps.


First, log in to your Grey account and select the **Send** option. From there, choose your preferred payment method, either bank transfer or Interac.


Next, select Canada as the destination country and CAD as the payout currency.


You’ll then enter the recipient’s banking details and the amount you want to send.


Before confirming the payment, Grey will display a summary of the transaction, including the transfer fee, exchange rate, and the exact CAD amount the recipient will receive.


Once you confirm the payment, the transfer begins processing immediately. Interac transfers are usually delivered within minutes, while bank transfers typically arrive within one business day.

## Interac vs bank transfer: which should you choose?


Both transfer methods are reliable, but the right option depends on your needs.


### Use Interac when


- the payment is **urgent**
- you want **instant delivery**
- you’re sending **personal transfers**


### Use bank transfer when


- sending **large payments**
- paying **tuition or invoices**
- transferring **business payments**


## Common mistakes when sending money to Canada


Many payment delays happen because of small errors during the transfer process. The most common ones include:


- **Incorrect banking details:** Canadian transfers rely on the correct institution number, transit number, and account number, so it’s important to double-check these before confirming the payment.
- **Submitting transfers after the cutoff time:** Transfers initiated after 18:00 Eastern Time may only begin processing the following business day.
- **Overlooking currency conversion:** If your balance is not already in Canadian dollars, conversion will apply. Always review the exchange rate before confirming the transaction.
