# Citibank virtual card: How to get one and use it safely

Every time you shop online, your payment details pass through multiple systems before reaching the merchant. While most transactions are completed safely, data breaches, phishing attacks and card theft remain common concerns for online shoppers. That's why many banks have introduced virtual payment tools that help customers protect their real card numbers without changing the way they shop.


Citibank's solution is Citi Virtual Account Numbers (VAN), a feature that generates a temporary card number linked to your eligible Citi credit card. Instead of sharing your actual card details with every merchant, you can use the virtual number for online purchases, reducing the risk if a retailer's payment system is compromised.


This guide explains how Citi Virtual Account Numbers work, who can use them, how to generate one, the security benefits they offer and the situations where a virtual card is a smarter choice than using your physical card online.

## **What is a Citibank virtual card?**


Shopping online is convenient, but every purchase also means trusting another merchant with your card details. To reduce that risk, Citibank introduced Citi Virtual Account Numbers (VAN), a security feature that lets eligible cardholders generate a temporary card number linked to their existing Citi credit card. The virtual number can be used instead of your actual card number when making online purchases, helping keep your real payment details private if a merchant experiences a data breach or security incident.


Citi Virtual Account Numbers are available to eligible Citi credit cardholders, although availability depends on your card type and location. Citibank offers this feature to give customers greater protection against online fraud while maintaining the convenience of card payments. Because each virtual number is tied to your Citi account rather than replacing it, purchases, rewards and billing continue to work as they normally would, but with an added layer of security for online transactions.


## **How to find your Citibank virtual card number**


Eligible Citi cardholders can create a Virtual Account Number through their online account, customise its settings and start using it for secure online purchases within minutes.


- **Sign in:** Log in to your Citi Online account using your username and password, then access the credit card eligible for Citi Virtual Account Numbers.
- **Open card:** Select your eligible Citi credit card and navigate to the Virtual Account Numbers feature, where you can create and manage temporary card numbers.
- **Create number:** Generate a new Virtual Account Number and customise settings such as the expiration date and spending limit to suit your planned purchase.
- **View details:** Once generated, copy the virtual card number, expiration date and security code displayed on the screen for your online transaction.
- **Use online:** Enter the Virtual Account Number at checkout instead of your physical card details to complete eligible online purchases with an added layer of security.

## **Best ways to use your Citibank virtual card for safer online payments**


Citibank Virtual Account Numbers are most effective for online purchases where extra security matters, helping protect your real card details without changing how you pay.


- **Subscription services:** Use a Virtual Account Number when paying for streaming platforms, software licences or cloud storage. Setting an expiration date or spending limit can help you manage recurring payments and reduce the impact of unauthorised charges if your payment details are compromised.
- **One-time purchases:** When shopping from a retailer you don't expect to use again, generate a new Virtual Account Number instead of sharing your permanent card details. This keeps your actual Citi card number hidden while completing the purchase normally.
- **Unfamiliar websites:** Buying from a merchant you've never used before carries some risk. A Citibank Virtual Account Number adds another layer of protection because the temporary number can be limited or replaced without affecting your physical credit card.
- **Checkout process:** Enter the Virtual Account Number, expiration date and security code exactly as you would a regular Citi credit card. If the merchant accepts Mastercard or Visa, depending on your Citi card, the payment is processed through your linked account.


## **What are the limitations of a Citibank virtual card?**


Citi Virtual Account Numbers improve online payment security, but they are not available to every Citi customer. Not all Citi credit cards support the feature, and availability may vary by card type and country. Before looking for the option in your online account, confirm that your card is eligible for Citi Virtual Account Numbers.


There are also practical limitations to keep in mind. Virtual Account Numbers are designed primarily for online purchases and generally cannot be used for in-store transactions. Some merchants that require the original card at the point of collection, such as certain hotels or car rental companies, may reject them. In addition, some recurring subscriptions and automatic billing arrangements may not work if the virtual number expires or reaches its spending limit. Finally, not every online merchant accepts virtual card numbers, so your physical Citi card may still be needed in some situations.

## **Frequently asked questions**


### **Do all Citibank cards support virtual card numbers?**


No. Citi Virtual Account Numbers are only available on eligible Citi credit cards, and availability depends on your card product and country. If you can't find the feature in your online account, your card may not qualify. Check Citi's documentation or contact customer support to confirm eligibility.


### **Can I use a Citibank virtual card for subscriptions?**


Sometimes, but it depends on how you've configured the virtual number. If the card expires or has a spending limit that is reached, recurring payments may fail. For long-term subscriptions, it's important to choose settings that remain valid for the entire billing period.


### **How long does a Citibank virtual card number stay active?**


A Citi Virtual Account Number remains active until the expiration date you choose or until it is cancelled, depending on the available settings. If the number expires before a merchant processes another payment, future transactions using that virtual number will be declined.


### **Can I generate multiple Citibank virtual cards?**


Yes. Eligible Citi cardholders can generate multiple Virtual Account Numbers for different online purchases. Using separate numbers for different merchants helps reduce risk and makes it easier to replace a compromised virtual number without affecting payments made to other retailers.
