# Bank of America virtual card: How to set one up

Virtual cards are beginning to out-muscle physical cards in terms of functionality and ease. And for times when you might need a physical card, many virtual card providers now link with Google Pay, Apple Pay and Samsung Pay, allowing users to pay via contactless options where available.


A Bank of America virtual card is a digital version of your physical BoA card that you can use for online and in-app purchases. It is set up through the BoA mobile app: open the app, select your card, and tap "Set up digital wallet" or "Virtual card" depending on the card type. The virtual number can be used wherever the physical card is accepted.


This guide explains what the BoA virtual card is, how to set it up, where it works, and what to do if you need more than BoA offers.


## What is a Bank of America virtual card?


Bank of America doesn't offer standalone virtual card numbers for most consumer credit and debit cards, unlike some other banks. Instead, it supports digital wallets such as Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Samsung Pay, which use tokenisation to protect your real card details during payments.


When you add an eligible Bank of America card to a digital wallet, your actual card number is replaced with a unique digital token called a device account number. Merchants receive this token instead of your real card number, helping reduce the risk of card fraud if payment data is compromised.


Because the token is linked to your device rather than your physical card, your real card number remains hidden during contactless and supported online purchases.


**Which cards are eligible:**


- Bank of America personal debit cards
- Bank of America personal credit cards (selected products)
- Bank of America business debit cards
- Certain co-branded cards may also be eligible


## Bank of America virtual cards explained


Bank of America allows eligible customers to generate virtual debit and credit card numbers through the mobile app. While the process looks similar, the two options work differently behind the scenes and suit different situations.


### **Bank of America virtual debit card**


A virtual debit card is linked directly to your Bank of America checking account. In essence, it is an online form of your physical card. When you make a purchase, the funds are immediately withdrawn from your available balance. This transaction doesn’t affect your credit in any way, as it doesn’t involve the credit line.


Your BoA debit card can be added to Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Samsung Pay. When you add the card to a digital wallet, the wallet generates a device account number (a token) that is used for payments instead of your physical debit card number. This tokenised number is what merchants receive during a contactless or in-app purchase.


### Bank of America virtual credit card


On the other hand, a virtual credit card is linked to one of your Bank of America credit cards. Transactions are completed using your available credit limit instead of your checking balance. And just like physical credit cards, you still get the usual credit card benefits, including purchase protection, rewards points or cash back, and the ability to dispute charges more easily.


### Choosing between a virtual debit card and a virtual credit card


Whether you choose a virtual credit card or a virtual debit card, neither is exactly better than the other. However, each has its best use case


A virtual debit card is better when:


- You want to keep tight control over your checking balance and avoid any chance of going into debt
- You’re making everyday online purchases (subscriptions, small orders, digital services)
- You prefer not to use credit for certain merchants or categories
- You’re helping someone manage spending without giving them access to a credit card


A virtual credit card is better when:


- You want to earn rewards or cash back on the purchase
- You’re buying higher-value items and prefer the extra purchase protection and dispute rights that come with a credit card
- You want to keep a larger purchase off your checking account until the statement is due
- You’re shopping with new or less-familiar online stores and value the stronger fraud protections of a credit product


## How to get a Bank of America virtual card


Adding your BoA card to a digital wallet is straightforward and takes a few minutes.


**Adding to Apple Pay:**


1. Open the Wallet app on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Tap the plus icon to add a new card.
3. Follow the prompts to add a new debit or charge card. You can scan your physical card or type the details manually.
4. BoA will send a verification code to your registered phone number or email. Enter it to confirm.
5. Once verified, your card appears in Apple Wallet and is ready for contactless payments in stores, in-app purchases, and on websites with an Apple Pay checkout button.


**Adding to Google Pay:**


1. Open the Google Wallet app on your Android phone.
2. Tap "Add to Wallet" and select "Payment card."
3. Follow the prompts to add your BoA card. You can scan it or enter details manually.
4. BoA will verify the addition through a code sent to your contact details.
5. Your card is then available for contactless payments and Google Pay checkouts online.


**Adding to Samsung Pay:**


The process is similar: open the Samsung Wallet app, add your card, and complete BoA's verification step.


Once set up, tap your phone at any contactless terminal for in-store payments. For online purchases, use the Apple Pay or Google Pay button at checkout where available.


## How to find your BoA virtual card number


The virtual card number is located in the Bank of America mobile app, not on any physical card.


**Where to look:**


1. Open the BoA mobile app and navigate to the card you want to use.
2. Look for a virtual card section, sometimes labelled "Virtual card number," "Digital card," or "Shop online."
3. The screen shows the virtual card number, expiry date, and CVV for that card.
4. Tap to copy the number, or write it down to use at checkout.


**Why the virtual number matters for merchants:** Some online merchants require the full Primary Account Number (PAN) to process a payment. The virtual card number functions as this PAN for online purchases. Enter it exactly as displayed in the app.


**When the virtual number is not visible:** For cards that work exclusively through digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay), no separate virtual number is displayed. Tokenisation happens automatically when you use the card in the wallet. You see only the last four digits of the token in your wallet app.


## Bank of America virtual card limitations


The virtual card is useful, but it does not solve every problem.


- **No standalone virtual card numbers:** Unlike some banks, Bank of America doesn't let most consumer cardholders generate separate virtual card numbers for online shopping. Instead, it relies on digital wallets like Apple Pay and Google Pay to tokenise your card information.
- **Works only where digital wallets are accepted:** To use a tokenised card online, the merchant must support Apple Pay or Google Pay. If a website only accepts manually entered card details, you'll typically need to use your physical card number.
- **Limited in-store use without a digital wallet:** Tokenised payments work for contactless purchases only after you've added your card to a supported digital wallet. You can't tap to pay using your physical card details alone.
- **Device-specific protection:** The payment token is tied to the device where you added the card. If you get a new phone or reset your wallet, you'll need to add the card again.
- **Standard card fees still apply:** Using a tokenised card doesn't remove foreign transaction fees, interest charges, or any other fees associated with your Bank of America card.

## Frequently asked questions


### **Is the Bank of America virtual card free?**


Yes. Setting up a BoA virtual card through the mobile app or adding a card to a digital wallet does not cost anything beyond your standard account. There are no fees specific to the virtual card feature. Standard account fees, interest, and transaction charges that apply to your physical card continue to apply to purchases made with the virtual number.


### **Can I use the BoA virtual card abroad?**


Yes, for online purchases from international merchants. Enter the virtual number at checkout, just as you would for any other card-not-present purchase. Standard foreign transaction fees that apply to your physical BoA card also apply to international purchases made with the virtual number. These fees are typically 3% on personal cards.


### **Does the virtual card number change?**


For BoA cards that generate a static virtual card number, the number does not change with each purchase. It is a fixed alternative number tied to your account. For cards used through digital wallets, the tokenised device account number is static per device but different from your real card number.


### **Can I use a BoA virtual card in stores?**


No, you can't use a virtual card number in stores. For in-store use, you need to add your BoA card to Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Samsung Pay and use your phone for contactless payment. This is technically a form of tokenisation similar to a virtual card, but the in-store payment process differs from online checkout.
