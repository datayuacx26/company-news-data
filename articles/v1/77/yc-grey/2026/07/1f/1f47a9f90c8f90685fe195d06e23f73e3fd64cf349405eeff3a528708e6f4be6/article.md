# Portugal Transfer Limits from US Banks: Rules Explained | Grey

Sending money from the US to Portugal is common for many reasons. You might be supporting family, paying tuition or rent, receiving freelance income, or moving money between your own accounts. While sending money is generally straightforward, fees and transfer limits vary by provider. Understanding how these limits work can help you avoid delays and unexpected costs.


There is no official government limit on how much money Portugal can receive from US banks, but individual banks set their own daily and monthly transfer limits. Most US banks cap international wire transfers at between $25,000 and $250,000 per day. Portugal's banks do not impose a receiving limit, but transfers over €10,000 may require documentation under anti-money-laundering rules.


This article explores transfer rules in Portugal, the transfer limits, receiving rules, and what banks require. Learning these rules ensures you receive international payments in Portugal without trouble.


## Does Portugal have a receiving limit for international transfers?


Portugal does not have a legal limit on how much money you can receive from abroad. There is no government rule that caps the amount you can receive in an international bank transfer. However, large transfers may be reviewed under[anti-money laundering (AML) rules.](https://www.bportugal.pt/en/page/money-laundering-and-terrorist-financing)


If you receive €10,000 or more, your bank may ask you to verify your identity or provide documents showing where the money came from and what it is for. This is a reporting requirement, not exactly a restriction on receiving the funds.


These checks are part of EU financial regulations and are overseen by the Bank of Portugal. They are designed to prevent money laundering and terrorist financing, not to limit legitimate transfers.


For most people receiving money from the US, the biggest limit is usually set by the sender's US bank, not by Portugal itself.


## US to Portugal online bank transfer limits


While there are no government-imposed limits on transfers, the amount you can send from the US to Portugal still depends on your US bank, your account type, and how you make the transfer. Most US banks set daily limits for international wire transfers. These limits vary based on factors such as:


- Your bank and account type
- Whether you have a personal or business account
- Your verification level
- Whether you send the transfer online or at a branch


Business accounts and premium banking customers often have higher transfer limits. If you visit a bank branch, you can usually send larger amounts because the bank conducts additional identity checks before processing the transfer, compared with an online transfer.


US banks’ transfer limits are subject to change. You should cross-check these limits before making a decision.


### **How to request a higher limit from your US bank:**


Before you request a higher transfer limit from your US bank, you need to first confirm your daily limit. Most US banks will have this information somewhere on their app. So, if you need to send more than what your online limit allows, there are usually two options.


- **Visit a branch:** Banks often allow higher transfer amounts for in-person requests because they can carry out additional identity checks.
- **Request a higher transfer limit:** You can also contact your bank's wire transfer team to request a temporary or permanent increase in your transaction limit. The bank may ask for proof of identity, details about the source of the funds, and the reason for the transfer. For transfers above $100,000, additional documentation is often required to meet anti-money laundering (AML) regulations.


## How much can you send from the US to Portugal?


Most commonly, the biggest transfer limit comes from the US bank, not the receiving bank in Portugal. Many US banks set daily limits for online international transfers ranging from $10,000 to $250,000, depending on the bank and your account type. Portuguese banks usually don’t have a corresponding limit on incoming transfers. So, if you need to send more than your online limit, you have two options:


- **Split the transfer over several days:** Most banks reset their daily limits every 24 hours, so you can send the money in separate transfers. Some banks, however, have weekly or monthly transaction limits. So, even though the daily transaction limit resets after 24 hours, the total transaction volume must still be within the weekly or monthly limits for your account tier.
- **Visit a branch.** Banks usually allow much larger wire transfers in person after completing identity checks. Contact them ahead of time to confirm the maximum amount and the documents you'll need to avoid wasting time in the bank queue.


### For the recipient in Portugal


If the transfer is large, particularly over €10,000, the recipient's bank may request documents showing the source of the funds. This is a routine anti-money laundering (AML) check and does not usually prevent the transfer from being completed. You just might not access the money until the checks are complete.


Some documents required may include:


- Bank statements
- Employment or salary letters
- Property sale agreements
- Other documents showing the source of the funds


Once you initiate or expect an amount that may trigger AML checks, it is best to have these documents ready to avoid delays. This is the standard in global financial practice and should not be considered a problem, even if it may cause some inconvenience. In fact, you should be worried if the financial institution doesn’t comply with best global practices.

## Rules for sending money from the US to Portugal


There are several regulatory requirements for sending money from the US to Portugal.


### **FBAR (Foreign Bank Account Report)**


If you are a US citizen, resident, or certain business entities and the total balance across your foreign financial accounts exceeds $10,000 at any point during the year, you must file an FBAR with FinCEN annually by 15 April.


This also applies if you have a bank account in Portugal and your combined foreign account balances exceed the threshold. The FBAR is filed separately from your tax return.


### **FATCA (Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act)**


If you are a taxpayer in the US, and you have foreign financial assets above certain thresholds, you are required to report those assets on Form 8938 as part of your annual tax return.[Portuguese banks](https://getnifportugal.com/fatca-portugal/) are also required under FATCA to report accounts held by US persons to the IRS. These rules do not necessarily affect how you send or receive money, but they may create additional reporting requirements if you are transacting along this corridor.

## How long does a US-to-Portugal transfer take?


The transfer speed depends on the transfer method.


- **SWIFT wire from a US bank:** A traditional bank wire usually takes one to five business days, with most transfers arriving within two to three business days. Transfers can take longer if several correspondent banks are involved or if they're sent outside banking hours.
- **SEPA transfer (from a European account):** If the sender already has an account in the EU, SEPA Credit Transfers to Portuguese bank accounts are usually completed within one business day. Banks that support SEPA Instant transfers often complete transactions in seconds.
- **Fintech platforms using local rails:** Some fintech providers allow you to receive your USD through ACH in the US, convert it to EUR, and then send it through SEPA. This avoids the SWIFT network and correspondent banks, so transfers are often both faster and cheaper than traditional bank wires.

## Frequently asked questions


**Is there a maximum amount I can receive from the US in Portugal?**


No. Portugal does not set a legal limit on incoming international transfers. The practical limit usually comes from the US bank sending the money. Portuguese banks may ask for supporting documents on large transfers as part of AML checks.


**What documents does a Portuguese bank need for a large incoming transfer?**


For transfers above **€10,000** , the bank may ask for:


- Proof of identity
- Documents showing the source of the funds, such as bank statements or employment letters
- An explanation of why the money is being transferred


Requirements vary between banks.


**Do I need to declare money received from abroad in Portugal?**


If you are a Portuguese tax resident, foreign income may need to be reported on your annual tax return. Whether it is taxable depends on the type of income, such as employment, investments, or gifts. If you're covered by a special tax regime, different rules may apply. Consider speaking with a Portuguese tax adviser.


**How do I send a wire transfer to a Portuguese IBAN?**


Provide your US bank with the recipient's full name and address, the Portuguese IBAN (PT50 followed by 21 digits), and the bank's SWIFT or BIC code. Initiate the transfer through your bank's online platform or in branch. The bank applies its outgoing wire fee and USD to EUR conversion at its own rate. The transfer arrives at the Portuguese bank via SWIFT, typically within two to five business days.


**What is the SWIFT code for Portuguese banks?**


Each bank has its own SWIFT code. Examples include:


- Millennium BCP — BCOMPTPL
- Caixa Geral de Depósitos — CGDIPTPL
- Novo Banco — BESCPTPL
- Santander Portugal — BCPIPTPL
- BPI — BBPIPTPL


Always confirm the correct code with the recipient before sending the transfer.
