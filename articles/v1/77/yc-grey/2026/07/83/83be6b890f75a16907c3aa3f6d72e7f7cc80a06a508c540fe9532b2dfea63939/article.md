# Revolut business account: Fees, features and who it suits

Running a business across borders used to mean opening multiple bank accounts, waiting days for international payments and juggling expensive transfer fees. Today, business owners expect one platform that can receive payments, pay suppliers, manage team expenses and hold multiple currencies without unnecessary complexity. That's why digital business accounts have become increasingly popular with freelancers, startups and growing companies.


Among the biggest names in this space is Revolut Business. Built for modern businesses, it combines multi-currency accounts, international transfers, expense management and accounting software integrations into a single platform. For many companies, it has become an attractive alternative to traditional business banking.


But is Revolut Business the right fit for your company? The answer depends on how you get paid, where your customers are and which features you actually use. This guide explains Revolut Business fees, key features, limitations and the types of businesses that benefit most from the platform.


## **What is Revolut Business and who is it for?**


Businesses no longer need separate banking tools for receiving payments, paying suppliers and managing multiple currencies. As more companies sell internationally, the demand has shifted towards digital platforms that simplify cross-border finance. Revolut Business was created to meet that need, giving businesses one place to manage global payments and day-to-day financial operations.


Revolut Business is a digital financial platform designed for freelancers, small businesses, startups and larger companies. It allows businesses to hold, exchange and transfer money in more than 30 currencies, while sending payments to suppliers in over 150 countries. It is available in several markets, including the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area (EEA), the United States, Australia, Singapore and India, with availability depending on your business location.


In the United Kingdom, Revolut is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) for relevant financial services. Rather than replacing every traditional banking service, Revolut Business serves as a central platform for managing international transactions, multi-currency balances, corporate spending and cross-border business payments from one account.


## **Key features of a Revolut Business account**


Revolut Business combines banking, payments and expense management into one platform, helping businesses manage domestic and international finances more efficiently.


- **Multi-currency accounts:** Hold, receive and exchange more than 30 currencies from one account, making it easier to work with international clients, suppliers and business partners across different markets.
- **International transfers:** Send payments to businesses and suppliers in over 150 countries, with competitive exchange rates and faster cross-border transfers than many traditional business banking services.
- **Expense cards:** Issue physical and virtual expense cards to employees, set spending limits, monitor transactions in real time and maintain better control over company expenses.
- **Accounting integrations:** Connect directly with Xero and QuickBooks to automatically sync transactions, simplify bookkeeping and reduce the amount of manual financial administration required.
- **Payment links:** Create secure payment links that allow customers to pay online without requiring a website, making it easier for businesses to collect payments from anywhere.
- **Bulk payments:** Upload multiple payments at once instead of sending them individually, saving time when paying suppliers, contractors, employees or other business expenses.

## **Revolut Business reviews: What real users actually think**


Revolut Business receives strong ratings on independent review platforms, but user experiences are far more balanced once you look beyond marketing pages.


### **The positives**


- **Excellent user experience:** Many users describe the mobile and web apps as intuitive, modern and easy to navigate, making day-to-day banking straightforward.
- **Strong multi-currency features:** Businesses that work internationally value the ability to hold, exchange and spend multiple currencies from one account.
- **Fast onboarding:** Many reviewers report opening an account quickly and sending or receiving international payments without the lengthy setup associated with traditional banks.


### **The negatives**


- **Account freezes:** The most common criticism is automated compliance reviews that can temporarily freeze accounts or funds after unusually large or unexpected transactions.
- **Customer support delays:** Users on forums such as Reddit frequently mention difficulty reaching specialist compliance teams, especially when urgent account issues arise.
- **Extra charges:** Some businesses report unexpected costs after exceeding monthly allowances for transfers or foreign exchange, making it important to understand the pricing structure before choosing a plan

## **Frequently asked questions**


### **Does Revolut Business support all countries?**


No. Revolut Business is available only in selected markets and primarily supports businesses incorporated in countries such as the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area (EEA), the United States, Australia, Singapore and India. If your business is registered outside supported regions, you may need to consider an alternative provider.


### **What is the cheapest Revolut Business plan?**


The **Basic** plan is Revolut Business's lowest-priced option, costing **£10 per month** . It includes limited free local transfers, no free international transfers and a small monthly foreign exchange allowance. Businesses with higher transaction volumes may find better value in one of the higher-tier plans.


### **Can sole traders use Revolut Business?**


Yes. Revolut Business is available to eligible freelancers, sole traders and self-employed professionals, allowing them to separate business and personal finances. Users can receive international payments, hold multiple currencies, issue invoices and manage business spending from a dedicated account.


### **Does Revolut Business charge for incoming international transfers?**


Incoming transfers are not always free. Costs depend on the payment method, currency and your pricing plan. Some transfer types are included within monthly allowances, while others may attract additional charges after you exceed your plan's limits. Always review the latest pricing before receiving payments.


### **Is Revolut Business safe?**


Yes. Revolut Business operates under financial regulations in the markets where it is licensed and uses encryption, fraud monitoring and security controls to protect customer accounts. Like other regulated financial platforms, it also carries out compliance checks that may occasionally require additional account verification.
