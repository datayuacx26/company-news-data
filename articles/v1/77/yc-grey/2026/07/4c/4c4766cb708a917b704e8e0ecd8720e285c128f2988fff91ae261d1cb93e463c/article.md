## **Navy Federal routing number for wire transfers**


The Navy Federal Credit Union wire transfer routing number is 256074974. This is the routing number you should use when sending or receiving a domestic wire transfer through your Navy Federal account. Entering the correct routing number is essential, as it helps ensure your funds are routed to the right financial institution and prevents processing delays.


You may also see the wire transfer routing number referred to as an ABA routing number or ABA transit number. Assigned by the American Bankers Association (ABA), this unique nine-digit number identifies financial institutions in the United States for wire transfers and other banking transactions. Whenever you're asked to provide the ABA routing number for a Navy Federal wire transfer, use 256074974.

## **How to send a domestic wire transfer with Navy Federal**


Sending a domestic wire transfer with Navy Federal Credit Union is straightforward. You can complete it through online banking or by visiting a branch if you prefer in-person assistance. Before you begin, make sure you have the recipient's banking details ready to avoid delays or errors.


1. **Log in** to your Navy Federal online banking account.
2. **Navigate to "Transfers"** and select the " **Wire Transfer"** option.
3. **Enter the recipient's details** , including their full name, account number, bank routing number, and the receiving bank's name.
4. **Review all the information carefully** , as incorrect details can delay or misdirect your transfer.
5. **Confirm and submit** the wire transfer, then save or print the confirmation for your records.


Domestic wire transfers are typically processed on the same business day if submitted before Navy Federal's cut-off time. Transfers requested after the daily cut-off, on weekends, or on public holidays are usually processed on the next business day. Processing times may also vary depending on the receiving bank.


## **How to send an international wire from Navy Federal**


Sending an international wire transfer through Navy Federal requires a few more details than a domestic transfer because the payment must pass through the global banking network. Before starting your transfer, confirm the recipient's banking information to minimise delays or rejected payments.


To complete the transfer, provide the recipient's full name, account number, recipient bank name and address, the bank's SWIFT/BIC code, and the IBAN (International Bank Account Number) where applicable. Some countries may also require additional payment instructions or the details of an intermediary bank. Navy Federal recommends using the wiring instructions provided by the recipient's financial institution to ensure the funds are routed correctly.


International wire transfers typically take 1 to 5 business days, although some destinations or intermediary banks may extend the delivery time. Navy Federal requires that certain wire requests exceeding $5,000, such as third-party transfers, be submitted in writing via a secure message or at a branch. There is no published maximum limit for standard international wire transfers, but larger transactions may be subject to additional verification.

## **Navy Federal wire transfer limits**


Navy Federal Credit Union sets a daily outgoing limit of $100,000 for digital wire transfers initiated through online or mobile banking. This limit applies to outgoing domestic and international wires submitted electronically. If you need to transfer more than the online limit, the digital cap cannot be increased. Instead, you'll need to complete and sign a wire transfer request form and submit it through Navy Federal's secure eMessaging service or visit a branch to have the transfer processed. Larger transactions may also be subject to additional identity verification and security checks.


If your wire transfer exceeds the $100,000 daily limit, you have several options. You can split the transfer into multiple transactions over consecutive days, submit the wire directly through a Navy Federal representative by calling 1-888-842-6328, or visit a branch for assistance. Another alternative is to transfer your funds to an external bank account via ACH and initiate the larger wire transfer from that financial institution if it better suits your transfer needs.

## **Frequently asked questions**


### **How long does a Navy Federal wire transfer take?**


Domestic wire transfers are typically processed on the same business day if submitted before the daily cut-off time. International wire transfers generally take one to five business days, depending on the destination country, intermediary banks, time zones, and whether all recipient banking details were provided correctly.


### **Can I cancel a Navy Federal wire transfer?**


You may be able to cancel a wire transfer if it has not yet been processed or released by Navy Federal. Contact the credit union immediately if you notice an error or wish to stop the transfer. Once a wire has been completed, it is generally difficult or impossible to reverse.


### **What happens if I enter the wrong routing number?**


Entering an incorrect routing number can delay your transfer, cause it to be rejected, or send the funds to the wrong financial institution. Always verify the recipient's banking details before submitting a wire transfer. Contact Navy Federal immediately if you realise you've entered incorrect information.


### **Does Navy Federal charge for incoming wire transfers?**


No. Navy Federal Credit Union does not charge a fee to receive domestic or international wire transfers. However, if you're receiving an international wire, intermediary or correspondent banks involved in processing the payment may deduct their own fees before the funds reach your account.


### **Can I send a wire transfer from the Navy Federal app?**


Yes. Eligible members can initiate domestic and international wire transfers through the Navy Federal mobile app or online banking. However, certain transactions, particularly higher-value transfers, may require additional verification, a signed request submitted through secure messaging, or assistance from a branch representative.


### **Is there a minimum amount for a Navy Federal wire transfer?**


Navy Federal does not publish a minimum amount for sending a wire transfer. You can generally send any amount that meets your needs, provided it falls within the applicable daily transfer limits and you satisfy the credit union's verification and account eligibility requirements.
