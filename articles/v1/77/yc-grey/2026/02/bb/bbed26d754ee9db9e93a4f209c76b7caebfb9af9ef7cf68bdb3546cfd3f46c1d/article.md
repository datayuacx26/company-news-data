## **Why use Apple Pay or Google Pay with your Grey card?**


Adding your Grey card to your mobile wallet automates your payment and makes payments seamless


Once you add your card to Apple Pay or Google Pay, you can:


- Tap to pay in supported stores globally
- Check out faster online and in apps
- Use public transport where supported
- Keep your card secure inside your phone... and so much more


Behind the scenes, your Grey card stays automatically connected to your available balances. When you pay, it pulls from[your global bank balances](https://grey.co/cards) No need to manually top up your card or switch between wallets.


### **Before you start**


To add your Grey card to Apple Pay or Google Pay, make sure you have:


- An active Grey account
- Your[Grey virtual card](https://grey.co/cards) created in the app
- A compatible device (iPhone for Apple Pay, Android for Google Pay)
- The latest version of Apple Pay or Google Pay


Apple Pay and Google Pay availability may vary by region, so ensure your device and location are supported.
