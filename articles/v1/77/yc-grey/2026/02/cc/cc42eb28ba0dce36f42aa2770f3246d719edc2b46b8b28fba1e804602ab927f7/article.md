## **Why shopping abroad is often frustrating**


Shopping from US or European stores usually comes with these hurdles:


### **1. Many foreign merchants decline local cards**


Some US/UK online stores automatically reject certain African-issued cards, even if you have more than enough balance.


### **2. Stores bill strictly in USD, GBP, or EUR**


If your card requires manual currency conversion, it can cause payment delays that may result in declines.


### **3. Banks block “suspicious” international transactions**


This usually happens when the merchant is unfamiliar.


### **4. Conversion errors and hidden fees**


Some cards convert long after the purchase, leading to surprises on your statement.


These problems are common, but they’re also exactly what Grey card is built to avoid.


## **How your Grey card makes shopping internationally easy**

Your Grey card doesn’t require you to pick or switch wallets before paying. Instead, it automatically spends from your balances. Your USD account is debited first; if that’s empty, your USDC, and then the highest of your remaining balances.
