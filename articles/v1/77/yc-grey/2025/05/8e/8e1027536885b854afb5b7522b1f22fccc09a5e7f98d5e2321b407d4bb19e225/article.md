# Best countries to work in Asia

Asia is more than a destination. It's a gateway to bold career moves, vibrant cultures, and exciting new lifestyles. From fast-growing tech hubs to peaceful coastal towns perfect for remote work, the region has something for every kind of professional. Whether you’re aiming for a higher salary, better work-life balance, or a fresh challenge in a dynamic market, there’s an Asian country that fits your career goals.


This guide explores the best countries to work in Asia, with practical details on income, cost of living, work culture, and visa options.


## Things to consider before working in Asia


### Language considerations


English is widely used in countries like Singapore, Malaysia, and the Philippines, but in other places like Thailand, Vietnam, South Korea, and Indonesia, learning the local language can significantly improve your job prospects and integration.


### Healthcare systems


Most countries in Asia offer a mix of public and private healthcare. While public healthcare can be affordable, many expats opt for private health insurance for faster and higher-quality care. Providers like[Insured Nomads](https://grey.co/blog/travel-safer-with-grey-x-insured-nomads) offer international health plans suitable for expats in Asia.


### Minimum wage and salaries


Minimum wages vary widely across Asia, but salaries for skilled professionals are generally competitive, especially in industries like tech, finance, and manufacturing.


### Cost of living


Cost of living can differ greatly between countries — and even between cities. Singapore and Seoul are among the most expensive, while Vietnam and the Philippines offer more affordable lifestyles.


### Safety and stability


Most Asian countries in this guide are politically stable with low crime rates. However, it’s essential to stay informed about local regulations and customs.


### Accommodation options


Housing varies from luxury apartments in city centres to affordable suburban homes. Research rental options, average prices, and expat-friendly neighbourhoods before relocating.

### 1. Singapore: A hub for high earners and career growth


This sleek island nation is one of Southeast Asia’s powerhouses, where efficiency meets opportunity. With world-class infrastructure, top-tier healthcare, and sharp governance, Singapore offers not just a high standard of living but a high standard of *working* too.


If your life is colour-coded and your Google Calendar feels like a personal assistant, Singapore is your natural habitat. The city-state thrives on precision, professionalism, and innovation. Salaries in finance, tech, and logistics are among the highest in Asia.


It’s also a strategic hub, home to regional HQs of many global companies, giving you more room to grow.


**Living costs:**


Rent and dining out are expensive. A one-bedroom in the city can cost around $2,500/month. Public transport is affordable and reliable.


**Average monthly salary:**


$3,500–$7,000 (mid-level roles in tech, finance, marketing).


**Visa info:** Apply for the *Employment Pass* (EP) if you're a professional with a job offer. Learn more via[Ministry of Manpower](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass) .


### 2. South Korea: A playground for creatives and tech experts


If you're fuelled by art, aesthetics, and ambition, South Korea is your creative playground. It’s where centuries-old palaces share skylines with neon-lit cafés, and where tradition blends with modern trends.


From indie design studios in Seoul to film sets in Busan, Korea is rising as a hotspot for creatives in gaming, fashion, media, and entertainment. It’s also growing in software development and business strategy.


With a strong pop culture industry and thriving tech scene, South Korea is inspiring and full of opportunity.


**Living costs:**


Seoul is expensive, but other cities are more affordable. Expect $1,200–$2,500/month depending on location.


**Average monthly salary:**


$2,500–$5,000 (design, media, IT, digital roles).


**Visa info:** The *E-7 visa* covers foreign professionals in specialised fields. Check eligibility on[Hi Korea](https://www.hikorea.go.kr/) .


### 3. Thailand: Balancing work and lifestyle for freelancers and remote workers


You crave balance: a productive Zoom call in the morning, a mango smoothie by the beach in the afternoon. Thailand delivers both.


With a welcoming culture, low cost of living, and reliable internet, Thailand has become a top choice for freelancers, remote workers, and creative entrepreneurs.


Chiang Mai and Bangkok lead the way, with coworking spaces, expat communities, and lots of opportunities in tourism, e-commerce, education, and digital services.


Thailand has also launched[new visa options for remote workers](https://grey.co/blog/how-to-apply-for-the-destination-thailand-visa-thai-digital-nomad-visa) , making it easier to work legally while enjoying the lifestyle.


**Living costs:**


$800–$1,800/month, including rent, food, and transport.


**Average monthly salary:**


$1,500–$3,500 (remote roles or expat positions in tech, content, and similar roles).


### 4. India: A fast-paced environment for tech professionals and startup builders


If you thrive in organised chaos and want your work to mean something, India is where innovation meets impact.


India is cementing its position as a global tech and startup hub. Cities like Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Pune are full of opportunities in software, fintech, product management, and engineering.


Whether you're building the next big thing or solving major problems, working in India means being part of a fast-moving, purpose-driven ecosystem.


**Living costs:**


Even in major cities, costs are relatively on the lower side. $500–$1,200/month can easily cover most essentials.


**Average monthly salary:**


$1,000–$3,000 (tech, remote marketing, startup roles).


**Visa info:** The *Employment Visa* is available for skilled professionals with a job offer. Start at[Indian Visa Online](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/) .

### 5. Vietnam: Growing opportunities for early-career talent and entrepreneurs


Vietnam is the rising star many overlook, but it is quickly proving itself impossible to ignore. With a booming tech scene and a surge of new startups, the country offers exciting opportunities for developers, marketers, and early-career professionals eager to grow fast.


Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi are at the heart of this momentum, with vibrant communities of entrepreneurs, freelancers, and remote workers building something fresh. Add in a low cost of living, rich culture, and incredible street food, and you get a dynamic environment where creativity and ambition thrive.


If you are ready to build your future in a place that is going places, Vietnam is calling.


**Living costs:**


Very affordable: $600–$1,200/month including rent, food, and entertainment.


**Average monthly salary:**


$1,200–$2,800 (digital, creative, or tech roles).


**Visa info:** You’ll need a *Work Permit* and a *Temporary Residence Card (TRC)* .[More information on Vietnam Visa](https://vietnamvisa.govt.vn/) .


### 6. Malaysia: Affordable comfort with strong infrastructure


Kuala Lumpur offers the best of both worlds — skyscrapers, street food, and a relaxed pace of life. It’s an ideal base for professionals who want low-key luxury and steady career growth.


Malaysia is developing fast in digital services, engineering, and e-commerce. There’s also a[visa programme for digital nomads](https://grey.co/blog/how-to-apply-for-a-digital-nomad-visa-in-malaysia) , making it easy to settle in as a remote worker.


With English widely spoken and a diverse society, Malaysia is both welcoming and practical.


**Living costs:**


$800–$1,600/month for most lifestyles.


**Average monthly salary:**


$1,800–$3,500 (remote roles or local jobs in tech/business).


### 7. Indonesia: Creative lifestyle and flexible remote work options


Beyond Bali’s postcard-perfect beaches is a growing community of coworking spaces, startups, and remote professionals.


Key industries like tech, tourism, and sustainability are attracting professionals looking for flexibility and purpose. Bali and Jakarta are popular hubs with good infrastructure and strong expat networks.


Indonesia offers a balance of work and lifestyle that’s hard to beat, but be sure to research the infrastructure in your chosen location.


**Living costs:**


$600–$1,400/month, depending on the city and your lifestyle.


**Average monthly salary:**


$1,200–$2,500 (remote work or local startup jobs).


**Reality check:** Infrastructure varies by island — do your homework.

### **8. Philippines: A hotspot for remote work and outsourcing professionals**


The Philippines has become one of Asia’s most popular destinations for remote workers, digital professionals, and outsourcing experts. Known for its strong English proficiency, friendly communities, and growing digital economy, the country offers an ideal blend of work and lifestyle.


Metro Manila, Cebu, and Davao are the key hubs for freelancers, startups, and remote teams. The Philippines is particularly attractive to professionals in customer support, virtual assistance, marketing, content creation, and IT services, thanks to its robust outsourcing industry.


Beyond work, the country offers stunning beaches, vibrant cities, and a lower cost of living compared to many other Asian countries.


**Living costs:**


Affordable. You can live comfortably on $700–$1,500 per month, depending on the city or island.


**Average monthly salary:**


$1,200–$2,500 (remote roles, digital services, creative work, and outsourcing-related jobs).


**Visa info:**


For remote workers, the Philippines currently operates on long-stay tourist visas that can be extended for several months. Learn more about[how to apply for a digital nomad visa in the the Philippines](https://grey.co/blog/how-to-apply-for-a-digital-nomad-visa-in-the-philippines) .
