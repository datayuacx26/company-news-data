# Top sites to get international remote jobs in Nigeria

Nigeria’s vibrant young population makes it an emerging destination for recruiters and clients seeking various talents. With the rise of digital connectivity and global workforces, international remote jobs have become more accessible for professionals living in Nigeria.


Whether you are a developer, writer, marketer, designer, or virtual assistant, numerous platforms can connect you with clients and companies worldwide. If you don’t have a great remote job yet, you are probably looking in the wrong place.


This article explores the best websites for international remote jobs from Nigeria and how to receive your payments smoothly through[Grey](https://grey.co/?utm_source=article&utm_medium=blog&utm_campaign=global+landing+page) .


### Top sites to get international remote jobs in Nigeria


We have curated a list of reputable platforms for international remote jobs in Nigeria.


### 1. Upwork


Upwork is one of the most popular freelancing platforms in the world. It connects professionals with businesses looking for remote talent in IT, writing, customer service, design, virtual assistance, and data science. Remote workers can create a profile, bid for jobs, and grow their reputation through client reviews. To improve your chances of landing a gig on Upwork, it's crucial to focus on a niche and build a strong portfolio to stand out in a competitive marketplace.


### 2. Toptal


Toptal is a premium platform for top-tier freelancers in software development, design, and finance. The screening process is rigorous, but you gain access to high-paying remote gigs from major global brands once accepted. Due to its elite nature, applicants should have strong portfolios and professional experience.

### 3. We Work Remotely


We Work Remotely is a job board dedicated exclusively to remote roles. It features listings from companies around the world hiring for positions in programming, customer support, marketing, and more. Unlike freelance platforms, most roles here are long-term or full-time. You don’t need to create an account to view and apply for jobs.


### 4. Remote OK


Remote OK is another top job board for remote work. It features job listings from startups and established companies across tech, marketing, and design. Jobs are tagged by role type, salary range, and whether they’re open to applicants from anywhere.


### 5.[Freelancer.com](http://freelancer.com/)


[Freelancer.com](http://freelancer.com/) is similar to Upwork. It offers freelance jobs in a variety of categories. It’s ideal for remote workers just starting out, as you can land small gigs to build your portfolio and credibility. Note that fees on Freelancer can add up quickly, so factor them into your pricing.


### 6. FlexJobs


FlexJobs is a subscription-based job platform that curates legitimate remote and flexible jobs. While it requires a paid membership, it eliminates scams and low-quality listings, making it a safer choice for job seekers in Nigeria. You can also use their blog and job samples to scout companies that are hiring remotely.

### 7. Wellfound


Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent) is your go-to if you’re into startups. It features remote opportunities at tech startups across the globe. You can apply directly and view salary and equity offers upfront. Once you optimise your profile and pitch uniquely to each startup, you stand a chance to land a great remote job..


### 8. LinkedIn


LinkedIn has evolved into more than just a networking tool. With its job search feature, you can filter for remote and international roles. By optimising your profile and connecting with recruiters, you increase your chances of being found by global employers. Include filters like “remote” and “worldwide” or “outside Nigeria” to refine job listings.
