# Cyble Receives Global Rising Star Award; CEO Beenu Arora Named Global Young Leader (40 Under 40) by The Brand Story

# Cyble Receives Global Rising Star Award; CEO Beenu Arora Named Global Young Leader (40 Under 40) by The Brand Story


*Recognition highlights Cyble’s growing global presence and commitment to advancing AI-powered cybersecurity*


**London, UK — June 8, 2026** — Cyble, an AI-native cybersecurity company, has been recognized with the **Global Rising Star Award** by *The Brand Story* . In addition, Cyble Co-founder and Chief Executive Officer **Beenu Arora** has been named a recipient of the **Global Young Leader Award (40 Under 40)** , honoring business leaders who are driving innovation and creating meaningful industry impact.


The recognition was conferred at the **Global Brand & Leadership Conclave 2026** , held at the UK House of Lords in the British Parliament, bringing together distinguished business leaders and innovators from around the world. **Dipesh Ranjan, Senior Vice President – Sales & Global Partnerships** , represented Cyble at the ceremony and received both awards on behalf of the organization and its leadership. The occasion marks an important milestone in Cyble’s global growth journey and reinforces the company’s expanding presence across Europe and international markets.


The awards acknowledge Cyble’s rapid growth, continued innovation, and expanding role in helping organizations worldwide address evolving cyber threats. They also recognize the leadership that has shaped the company’s vision and growth since its founding.


### **Award Criteria and Significance**


*The Brand Story’s* Global Rising Star Award is presented to organizations demonstrating strong business growth, innovation, and market impact. The Global Young Leader Award (40 Under 40) honors individuals distinguished by leadership, entrepreneurship, and meaningful contributions to their industries.


For Cyble, this recognition reflects sustained investment in building technologies that enable security teams to make faster, more informed decisions in an increasingly complex threat landscape.


Building Intelligence That Goes Beyond Visibility


Since its inception, Cyble has focused on a simple belief: cybersecurity teams need more than data—they need intelligence that helps them understand what matters and act decisively.


This philosophy is embodied in **BlazeAI** , Cyble’s proprietary reasoning engine built on a temporal, multi-relational knowledge graph spanning billions of entities and trillions of relationships across the surface web, deep web, dark web, and other intelligence sources.


By connecting information across disparate datasets, BlazeAI enables security teams to uncover hidden relationships among threat actors, malicious infrastructure, compromised credentials, vulnerabilities, and emerging risks—delivering contextual, prioritized, and actionable intelligence rather than isolated alerts.


Today, Cyble supports enterprises, government agencies, and managed security service providers across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and the Middle East, helping strengthen cyber resilience and proactively manage risk.


### **Leadership Behind the Vision**


Under the leadership of **Beenu Arora** , Cyble has evolved from a startup with a bold vision into a globally recognized cybersecurity company trusted by organizations worldwide. This leadership emphasis on innovation, customer success, and practical applications of AI has positioned Cyble as a key player in the evolving cybersecurity landscape.


Commenting on the recognition, **Beenu Arora** , Co-founder and CEO of Cyble, said:


“These awards reflect the incredible work our team has delivered over the years and the trust our customers place in us every day. From the beginning, we believed cybersecurity could be more proactive, contextual, and intelligence-driven. This recognition reinforces that vision and motivates us to continue building solutions that help organizations stay ahead of increasingly sophisticated threats.”


### **Future Outlook**


As cyber threats continue to evolve, Cyble remains focused on delivering intelligence-led security solutions that enable organizations to identify risks earlier, respond faster, and make more confident security decisions.


Recognition from *The Brand Story* represents another milestone in Cyble’s journey and underscores the company’s continued commitment to innovation, growth, and shaping the future of cybersecurity.


### **About The Brand Story**


*The Brand Story* is a global platform recognizing organizations and leaders driving innovation, growth, and positive industry impact. Through its awards and business recognition initiatives, it celebrates excellence across industries and highlights individuals and companies shaping the future through leadership, innovation, and entrepreneurship.


### About Cyble


Cyble is an AI-native cybersecurity company delivering unified risk intelligence and decision support to enterprises and government organizations worldwide. The platform integrates threat intelligence, digital risk protection, attack surface management, vulnerability intelligence, and security operations to provide real-time visibility and actionable insights. By leveraging artificial intelligence, contextual intelligence, and automation, Cyble helps organizations proactively manage cyber risk and strengthen their security posture.
