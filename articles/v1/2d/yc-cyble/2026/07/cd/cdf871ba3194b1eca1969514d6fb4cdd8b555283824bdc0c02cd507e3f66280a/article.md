# Cyble Wins Best MSSP Product At CISO50 Awards

# Cyble Wins Best Product for MSSPs of the Year at the Prestigious CISO50 & Future Security Awards


**Dubai, UAE, September 20, 2024 – Cyble** , a global leader in threat intelligence and cybersecurity services, is proud to announce that it has been named the winner of this year’s CISO50 & Future Security Awards for Best Product for Managed Security Service Providers (MSSPs). The award ceremony, held at The Ritz-Carlton, Jumeirah Beach Residences, honored visionary leaders and enterprises making significant contributions to the cybersecurity industry across the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region.


This prestigious award underscores Cyble’s commitment to delivering cutting-edge cybersecurity solutions, furthering its mission to safeguard organizations worldwide. TahawulTech.com and Security Advisor ME editorial teams meticulously evaluated companies across the MENA region based on their technology innovation, relevance, and impact on the cybersecurity landscape.


Mandar Patil, VP of International Market Development at Cyble, who received the award, expressed his pride in the company’s regional achievements and growth. He stated, *“Building on strong business growth, Cyble has expanded its presence to 22 countries, serving over 500 clients globally. Our story in the Middle East is an exciting one. The region has been our fastest-growing market, with a robust presence in the UAE and Saudi Arabia. We have a network of over 53 partners in the region, including some of the largest managed security service providers (MSSPs). Our customers span diverse industries, including IT, Telecom, Retail, Banking, Insurance, and Government. Our contributions to the cybersecurity community have been widely recognized by major organizations such as Facebook, Cisco, and the U.S. Government.”*


With an increasing demand for cybersecurity solutions in the region, Cyble has prioritized its Middle East expansion, leveraging strong partnerships and investing heavily in regional growth. Mandar emphasized that Cyble anticipates a significant increase in demand for its services as it continues to bolster its presence.


Adding his vision to the celebration, Beenu Arora, CEO and Co-Founder of Cyble, commented, *“Cyble’s relentless pursuit of innovation and dedication to our clients’ needs have been key to our success. This recognition is a testament to our team’s hard work and commitment. We are honored to be acknowledged as a leader in the cybersecurity space, especially in the Middle East, a region that continues to be vital to our global strategy. Moving forward, we will focus on delivering world-class cybersecurity solutions, expanding our partner ecosystem, and helping organizations protect their digital assets in an increasingly complex threat landscape.”*


Cyble remains dedicated to creating a safer cyber ecosystem through proactive intelligence, community engagement, and a clear focus on helping organizations navigate today’s dynamic security challenges.
