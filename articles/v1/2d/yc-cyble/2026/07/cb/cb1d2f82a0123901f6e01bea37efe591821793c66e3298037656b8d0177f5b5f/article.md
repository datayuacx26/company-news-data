# Cyble Named a Sample Vendor for Digital Risk Protection Services in Gartner® Hype Cycle™ for Managed IT Services, 2024

# [Cyble Named a Sample Vendor for Digital Risk Protection Services in Gartner® Hype Cycle™ for Managed IT Services, 2024](https://cyble.com/cyble-named-sample-vendor-in-gartner-hype-cycle-for-managed-it-services/)


California


, GA – July 17, 2024 – Cyble, a leading global provider of digital risk protection services (DRPS) and threat exposure management, is honored to be recognized as a Sample Vendor in the Gartner® Hype Cycle™ for Managed IT Services, 2024, under the DRPS category. This acknowledgment, we feel, reflects Cyble’s unwavering dedication to delivering advanced cybersecurity solutions that address the dynamic needs of businesses worldwide.


Per Gartner, “driven by complexity and constant change, IT services remain highly focused on automation, acceleration and cloud adoption. Sourcing, procurement and vendor management leaders should use this Hype Cycle to assess the maturity of emerging managed IT services when planning their sourcing strategy.” “Digital risk protection services (DRPS) are a set of technology-led services which enable brand protection, third-party risk assessment and discovery of external-facing threats and offer technical response to identified risks. They provide visibility into the surface web, social media, dark and deep web sources to identify potential threats to critical assets and provide contextual information on threat actors, their tactics, techniques and procedures for conducting malicious activities.”


Cyble’s inclusion in the Hype Cycle, we feel, underscores its innovative approach to managing cybersecurity challenges. The company’s DRPS are designed to proactively detect and address external threats across various digital landscapes. Utilizing advanced threat intelligence and machine learning, Cyble offers valuable insights and contextual information on threat actors and their methods. Cyble’s next-generation platform, Cyble Vision, integrates Dark Web Monitoring (DWM), Threat Intelligence (CTI), External Attack Surface Management (EASM), Brand & Social Media Monitoring, Third Party & Supply Chain Risk Scoring, and Risk Management (TPRM) into a single, cohesive solution.


Beenu Arora, CEO of Cyble, commented, “Our commitment to innovation empowers organizations with the tools and insights they need to protect their digital assets and uphold their reputations in an increasingly complex threat environment. This recognition, for us, is a testament to our efforts, and we are delighted to be named in the Gartner Hype Cycle for Managed IT Services.”
