# Cyble Earns 36 Badges in G2 Summer 2026 Reports, Highlighting Leadership in AI-Powered Cybersecurity


**Cupertino, Calif. – May 26th** **, 2026** –


[Cyble](https://cyble.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) , a global leader in AI-powered cybersecurity solutions, today announced that it has earned 36 badges in the G2 Summer 2026 Reports across key cybersecurity categories, including Brand Intelligence, Dark Web Monitoring, Attack Surface Management, Threat Intelligence, Brand Protection, Web Security, and System Security.


The latest recognitions reinforce Cyble’s growing global presence and continued commitment to delivering intelligence-led cybersecurity solutions that help orga₹nizations proactively identify, investigate, and mitigate cyber threats. Cyble secured Regional Leader badges in Brand Intelligence, Dark Web Monitoring, Attack Surface Management, and Threat Intelligence, reflecting strong customer satisfaction and market leadership across regions.


The company also received multiple recognitions for usability, innovation, and customer impact. Cyble was recognized for Best Usability in Brand Intelligence, High Performer Enterprise in Attack Surface Management, Easiest to Use in Threat Intelligence, and Best Estimated ROI in Brand Protection. In addition, Cyble earned “Users Most Likely to Recommend” recognitions for Brand Protection and Enterprise Threat Intelligence, further demonstrating customer confidence in the platform’s ability to deliver actionable intelligence and measurable security outcomes.


Cyble also secured Momentum Leader badges across Brand Intelligence, Dark Web Monitoring, Attack Surface Management, and Threat Intelligence categories. Furthermore, the company was named a Leader across Brand Intelligence, Attack Surface Management, Brand Protection, Threat Intelligence, Dark Web Monitoring, Web Security, System Security, and E-Commerce Protection. Cyble additionally received the prestigious “Users Love Us” badge, awarded to organizations that consistently achieve high customer satisfaction ratings on G2.


The recognition also highlights the growing impact of Cyble Blaze AI, the company’s AI-powered cybersecurity capability designed to accelerate threat detection, contextual intelligence, and security operations. Integrated across Cyble’s cybersecurity ecosystem, Cyble Blaze AI enhances the speed and accuracy of threat analysis by enabling organizations to cut through alert fatigue, prioritize critical risks, and gain deeper visibility into evolving cyber threats. By combining AI-driven intelligence with human-led analysis, Cyble continues to help security teams make faster, more informed decisions in an increasingly complex threat landscape.


*“These recognitions from G2 reflect the trust customers place in Cyble’s innovation, intelligence capabilities, and customer-first approach,” said **Beenu Arora, CO-Founder and CEO at Cyble** . “As cyber threats become more sophisticated, organizations need AI-powered cybersecurity solutions that deliver real-time visibility, operational efficiency, and measurable impact. The recognition across multiple categories validates Cyble’s mission to help organizations stay ahead of emerging threats with proactive and intelligence-driven security.”*


G2 is one of the world’s leading software marketplaces, with rankings and badges based on verified customer reviews and satisfaction scores. The Summer 2026 Reports evaluate vendors based on usability, market presence, customer experience, and overall product performance.


Cyble continues to expand its cybersecurity capabilities through AI-native innovation, threat intelligence, digital risk protection, attack surface management, and dark web monitoring solutions designed to strengthen cyber resilience for enterprises and governments worldwide.
