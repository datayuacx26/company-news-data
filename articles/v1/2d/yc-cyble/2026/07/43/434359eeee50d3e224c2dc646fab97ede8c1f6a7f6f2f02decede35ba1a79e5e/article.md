# Cyble Recognized In Threat Intelligence Landscape Q1 2026

# Cyble Recognized in Forrester's External Threat Intelligence Service Providers Landscape, Q1 2026 report


**CUPERTINO, Calif. — April** **8** **, 2026** — Cyble, a global leader in AI-native cybersecurity solutions, today announced its inclusion in


*Forrester’s External Threat Intelligence Service Providers Landscape, Q1 2026* . The[report](https://www.forrester.com/report/the-external-threat-intelligence-service-providers-landscape-q1-2026/RES193019?ref_search=3187554_1774982016487) provides an overview of 34 vendors across the external threat intelligence ecosystem, highlighting key capabilities, market maturity, and top use cases shaping modern security and risk management strategies.


The Forrester Landscape highlights the rapid transformation of external[threat intelligence](https://cyble.com/solutions/cyber-threat-intelligence/) , with agentic AI emerging as a core driver of efficiency and effectiveness. As organizations face an increasingly complex threat environment, the ability to move beyond static intelligence toward real-time, automated decision-making is becoming essential. Cyble believes its inclusion reflects its commitment to operationalizing intelligence through agentic AI, enabling enterprises to transition from reactive defense models to continuous, intelligence-led security execution.


“For Cyble, being recognized in this Forrester Landscape reflects the shift the industry is undergoing—from fragmented tools to intelligent, autonomous security,” said Beenu Arora, CEO and Co-founder of Cyble. “Security teams today require more than visibility—they need systems that can interpret risk, prioritize actions, and respond in real time. Blaze AI was built to enable exactly that—transforming intelligence into immediate, actionable outcomes.”


At the core of Cyble’s platform is Blaze AI, an agentic, AI-native engine purpose-built to unify threat intelligence, digital risk protection, and security operations into a single, continuously learning ecosystem. By ingesting and correlating vast volumes of external and internal data—including[dark web](https://cyble.com/knowledge-hub/what-is-the-dark-web/) intelligence, attack surface exposures, and adversarial signals—Blaze AI provides contextualized insights that enable faster detection, precise prioritization, and automated response.


Unlike conventional solutions that rely heavily on manual workflows and fragmented datasets, Cyble delivers a proactive and adaptive security model. Its platform continuously analyzes emerging threats, identifies vulnerabilities, and translates intelligence into recommended or automated actions—helping organizations reduce response times, minimize risk exposure, and improve overall security posture without increasing operational burden.


With a strong focus on innovation, scalability, and measurable security outcomes, Cyble continues to support enterprises and government organizations in strengthening resilience, improving decision-making, and staying ahead of evolving cyber threats.
