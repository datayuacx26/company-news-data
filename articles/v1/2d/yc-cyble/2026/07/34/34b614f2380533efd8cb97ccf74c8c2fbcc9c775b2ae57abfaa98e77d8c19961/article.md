# Cyble Partners with Globe Business to Boost AI-Led Cybersecurity in the Philippines


Cyble, a global leader in AI-powered cyber threat intelligence and digital risk protection, announced its partnership with Globe Business, the enterprise arm of Globe Group, to introduce an AI-native threat intelligence platform designed to strengthen the cybersecurity posture of organizations across the Philippines.


As the country faces an unprecedented surge in cyberattacks, the collaboration brings Cyble’s advanced AI-driven threat intelligence capabilities to Philippine enterprises seeking stronger, proactive, and data-informed security strategies. In Q1 2025 alone, Cyble identified over 1.2 million compromised Filipino credentials on the dark web—highlighting the growing scale of identity theft, phishing operations, and malware-as-a-service campaigns targeting the region.


Through this partnership, organizations gain access to Cyble’s AI-native risk intelligence platform, powered by its proprietary BlazeAI engine, which continuously analyzes threat signals across the surface web,[deep web](https://cyble.com/knowledge-hub/what-is-the-deep-web/) , dark web, and cybercrime networks. With the ability to process more than 20 billion pages daily and monitor over 15,000 cybercrime sources, the platform provides real-time visibility into data leaks, brand impersonation, fraudulent domains, exposed credentials, and targeted attack activity.


### **Beenu Arora, Co-Founder and CEO of Cyble, said:**


*“The Philippines faces some of the world’s fastest-growing cyber risks. By combining the scale of AI with Globe Business’s local expertise, we aim to help enterprises stay one step ahead of attackers.”*


Serving as a trusted digital transformation partner, Globe Business supports organizations across key industries. The integration of Cyble’s intelligence ecosystem will empower multiple sectors, including:


- **Financial institutions** — detect compromised customer data early and reduce fraud risk
- **Retail and e-commerce** — identify brand impersonation, counterfeit activity, and spoofed domains
- **Government agencies** — gain visibility into early indicators of targeted breaches and APT reconnaissance


### **KD Dizon, Head of Globe Business, emphasized:**


*“The fight against cybercrime is now a race of intelligence and speed. Our partnership with Cyble is about democratizing that power, equipping Philippine enterprises with AI-driven foresight so they can move beyond reacting to breaches and achieve proactive, data-informed resilience.”*


This collaboration reinforces Cyble’s mission to make[threat intelligence](https://cyble.com/solutions/cyber-threat-intelligence/) accessible, actionable, and anticipatory—enabling organizations to shift from reactive security operations to intelligence-led cybersecurity in an era of rapidly evolving digital threats.
