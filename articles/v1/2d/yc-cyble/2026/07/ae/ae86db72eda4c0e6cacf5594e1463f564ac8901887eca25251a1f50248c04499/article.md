# Cyble Attains SOC 2 Type 1 Certification – Ushering in a new era of security assurance

Our journey to SOC 2 Type 1 certification underscores not only our dedication to building auditable, repeatable security programs but also highlights the seamless integration of patented Machine Learning (ML) algorithms and advanced Artificial Intelligence (AI) capabilities.


## What is System and Organization Controls (SOC 2) Type I?


SOC 2 Type I is an independent evaluation of a company\\’s internal controls concerning security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy. This evaluation provides stakeholders with assurance that the company has designed its controls to align with these service principles.


The SOC 2 framework, tailored for SaaS platforms, spans crucial areas such as security, governance, risk, and compliance. Over the past year, Cyble has been expanding the critical role that AI plays in all our offerings, leveraging patented ML algorithms to meet and exceed the stringent expectations set by SOC 2, thereby enhancing our operational security capabilities.


## How will this help Cyble customers?


Users of Cyble’s solutions stand to benefit immensely from SOC 2. Primarily, it serves as a robust demonstration of our commitment to data security, instilling trust and confidence among customers and stakeholders. Secondly, the process of preparing for a SOC 2 Type I audit enables Cyble to identify and rectify potential weaknesses in its internal controls, thereby enhancing our overall security posture. Additionally, the report ensures compliance with regulations like the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), further aligning Cyble with the latest data security standards.
