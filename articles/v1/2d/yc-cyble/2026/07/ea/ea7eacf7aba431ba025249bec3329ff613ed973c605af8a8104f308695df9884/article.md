# Cyble Named a Challenger in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Cyberthreat Intelligence Technologies

# Cyble Named a Challenger in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Cyberthreat Intelligence Technologies


**CUPERTINO, California – May 8, 2026 –**


[Cyble](https://cyble.com/) today announced it has been named a Challenger in the


[2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Cyberthreat Intelligence Technologies](https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/7798353?ref=shareSummary) . As adversaries grow increasingly sophisticated and enterprises demand intelligence that is not only comprehensive but immediately actionable, Cyble unifies external threat visibility, digital risk protection, and security operations into a single intelligence layer — enabling organizations to move from fragmented insights to faster, more decisive action.


*“Security teams are under constant pressure to respond faster with greater accuracy,” said **Beenu Arora, Co-Founder and CEO, Cyble** . “We believe, this recognition reflects our strong focus on delivering intelligence that drives action — cutting through noise, accelerating response, and enabling confident decision-making at scale.”*


### **From Fragmented Threat Data to Unified, Actionable Intelligence**


Cyble sets a new standard for AI-native threat intelligence, analyzing billions of signals across the surface, deep, and[dark web](https://cyble.com/knowledge-hub/what-is-the-dark-web/) to identify the most relevant threats based on real-world risk.


In our view, the Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Cyberthreat Intelligence Technologies confirms what the threat landscape has already made clear: threat intelligence can no longer be a passive reporting function — it must be an operational one. Cyble’s expanding product portfolio is engineered to operationalize intelligence across the entire security lifecycle, transforming raw threat data into decisive action.


**[Cyble Vision](https://cyble.com/products/cyble-vision/)** , Cyble’s flagship AI-native threat intelligence and digital risk protection platform, provides security teams with unparalleled visibility across the surface, deep, and dark web.


Powered by AI, Cyble Vision continuously monitors and correlates threat data — tracking[threat actors](https://cyble.com/knowledge-hub/cyber-threat-actor-and-types/) , campaigns, exposed credentials, compromised infrastructure, and emerging vulnerabilities — delivering the context security teams need to act with speed and confidence. By connecting external threat activity directly to an organization’s attack surface, Cyble Vision transforms raw intelligence into prioritized, operationalized action.


**[Cyble Blaze AI](https://cyble.com/platforms/blaze-ai)** further enhances automation, contextual analysis, and decision support — reasoning across adversary behavior and attack surface exposure in real time to help organizations stay ahead of rapidly evolving threats.


The Cyble platforms bring together AI-driven prioritization, digital risk protection, and SOC workflow integration in one unified intelligence layer.


**[Cyble TIP](https://cyble.com/products/cyble-tip/)** empowers organizations to centralize, operationalize, and scale threat intelligence — helping security teams detect faster, respond smarter, and stay one step ahead of adversaries.


[Cyble Titan](https://cyble.com/products/cyble-titan/) , a next-generation endpoint security platform, pairs native threat intelligence from Cyble Vision with real-time visibility, proactive detection, and automated incident response — all through a unified cloud-native console.


**[Cyble Saratoga](https://cyble.com/products/cyble-saratoga/)** rounds out the portfolio by quantifying cyber risk in business terms, enabling leaders to optimize security investments, strengthen resilience, and maximize value from every dollar spent.


Cyble’s threat intelligence capabilities span the full attack surface — from[ransomware](https://cyble.com/knowledge-hub/what-is-ransomware/) and exploited vulnerabilities to brand impersonation, executive exposure, and third-party risks — providing a consolidated view that connects external threat activity to internal exposure risk. Context-rich insights are seamlessly integrated into SIEM, SOAR, and case management systems, enabling faster triage and response without handoff or tool-switching.


Trusted by enterprises, government agencies, and MSSPs across more than 50 countries, Cyble delivers intelligence at the point of decision-making, at the speed of the threat.
