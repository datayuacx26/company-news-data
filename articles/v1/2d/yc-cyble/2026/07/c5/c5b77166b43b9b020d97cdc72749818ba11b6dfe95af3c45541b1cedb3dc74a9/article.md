# Cyble Earns SOC 2 Type II Certification For Data Security

# Cyble Achieves SOC 2 Type II Certification, Reinforcing Commitment to Data Security


**CALIFORNIA, US – September 6, 2024—Cyble** , a global leader in AI-powered Gen 3 Cyber Threat Intelligence and digital risk protection, is excited to announce that it has successfully completed a SOC 2 Type II audit and received our signed report. This significant milestone demonstrates our unwavering commitment to maintaining the highest standards of data security and privacy.


## What is SOC 2 Type II Certification?


SOC 2 Type II certification, developed by the American Institute of CPAs (AICPA), is a rigorous audit designed to evaluate a company’s ability to manage and protect customer data across five critical Trust Service Principles: Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy. While SOC 2 Type 1 focuses on a point-in-time assessment of these controls, SOC 2 Type II provides an ongoing evaluation over an extended period, ensuring the continued effectiveness of security protocols.


A SOC 2 Type II report is a comprehensive review of our systems and processes, offering assurance that Cyble has implemented strong security controls that protect the data of our customers, partners, and stakeholders.


## What This Certification Means for Cyble's Clients


For Cyble’s customers, this certification serves as independent verification of the company’s adherence to stringent security standards. It reassures clients that their data is protected against unauthorized access, system availability is prioritized, and confidentiality is maintained.


Achieving SOC 2 Type II certification required Cyble to undergo an exhaustive audit of its internal controls, reinforcing the company’s belief that data security is not just a technical requirement—it’s the foundation of trust with its customers. This certification highlights key benefits for Cyble’s stakeholders:


- Enhanced Trust: SOC 2 Type II certification reinforces our dedication to safeguarding sensitive information. It builds confidence among clients and partners while strengthening trust in our commitment to data protection.
- Improved Security: The in-depth audit helped us identify and address potential vulnerabilities, leading to stronger security measures and creating a more resilient environment for stakeholders.
- Competitive Advantage: SOC 2 Type II certification offers Cyble a significant competitive edge. It showcases our leadership in cybersecurity and demonstrates our commitment to industry-leading practices.


Beenu Arora, Co-Founder and CEO of Cyble, commented on this milestone: *“Successfully completing the SOC 2 Type II audit is a major achievement for Cyble. It reflects our relentless dedication to upholding the highest standards of data security and privacy.”* He further added, “This certification not only validates our rigorous security measures but also strengthens the trust our clients and partners place in us. We are committed to continuously enhancing our security practices and providing a secure environment for all our stakeholders.”
