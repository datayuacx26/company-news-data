# Cyble Named a Most Preferred Workplace 2026–27 by Marksmen Daily for Fifth Consecutive Year

# Cyble Named a Most Preferred Workplace 2026–27 by Marksmen Daily for Fifth Consecutive Year


*Recognition highlights Cyble’s enduring people-first culture as the AI-native cybersecurity leader continues its global expansion and innovation journey.*


**Atlanta, USA / Mumbai, India** – Cyble, an AI-native cybersecurity company, has been recognized as a **Most Preferred Workplace 2026–27** by **Marksmen Daily (Team Marksmen Network)** for the **fifth consecutive year** . The achievement reflects Cyble’s sustained commitment to building a high-performing, people-first workplace while rapidly expanding its global presence and advancing AI-powered cybersecurity innovation.


Receiving this recognition for five straight years places Cyble among a select group of organizations that have consistently demonstrated excellence in employee experience, leadership, workplace culture, diversity and inclusion, innovation, and organizational development—underscoring that its growth has been driven not only by technology, but also by its people.


### A Culture That Scales with Innovation


As cybersecurity becomes increasingly complex and talent competition intensifies, organizations are challenged to grow without compromising employee engagement and organizational values. Cyble’s continued recognition demonstrates its ability to scale globally while fostering a culture centered on collaboration, innovation, trust, and continuous learning.


> “Our people have always been the foundation of Cyble’s success,” said **Manish Chachada, COO and Co-founder of Cyble.** “Being recognized as a Most Preferred Workplace for the fifth consecutive year reflects the culture we’ve intentionally nurtured—one built on trust, collaboration, continuous learning, and a shared sense of purpose. This recognition belongs to every Cybler whose passion and commitment shape who we are today. As we continue to grow globally, preserving and strengthening that culture will remain one of our highest priorities.”


### Strengthening Leadership Through People


The recognition comes at a significant stage in Cyble’s growth as the company continues to expand its global operations, enhance its AI-native cybersecurity solutions, and support enterprises and government organizations in combating increasingly sophisticated cyber threats.


Cyble believes that sustained innovation is powered by an engaged workforce. By investing in employee development, leadership, well-being, and an inclusive workplace, the company continues to attract and retain top cybersecurity talent while accelerating product innovation and customer success.


This latest recognition further reinforces Cyble’s position not only as a trusted cybersecurity partner but also as an employer of choice for professionals seeking to build the future of cyber defense.


### About Cyble


Cyble is an AI-native cybersecurity company delivering unified risk intelligence and decision support to enterprises and government organizations worldwide. Its AI-powered solutions combine threat intelligence, digital risk protection, attack surface management, vulnerability intelligence, and security operations to provide real-time visibility into cyber risks. By combining artificial intelligence, contextual intelligence, and automation, Cyble enables organizations to proactively detect, prioritize, and respond to evolving cyber threats.
