# Cyble is now officially a partner of the Global Forum on Cyber Expertise (GFCE)


We’re proud to share that Cyble is now officially a partner of the[Global Forum on Cyber Expertise (GFCE)](https://www.linkedin.com/company/global-forum-on-cyber-expertise-gfce/) .


This partnership reinforces our commitment to strengthening global cyber resilience and collaborating with international stakeholders to advance cybersecurity capacity building worldwide.


Together, we aim to foster knowledge sharing, enable cross-border collaboration, and help build a safer and more secure digital ecosystem for governments, organizations, and communities globally.


We look forward to contributing to meaningful, impact-driven initiatives with GFCE and its partners.
