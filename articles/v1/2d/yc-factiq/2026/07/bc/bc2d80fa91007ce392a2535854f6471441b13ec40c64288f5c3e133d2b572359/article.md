# New data: European trade, EU & global health, and broader company financials

FactIQ just added several official data sources and expanded company-financials coverage. Everything below is live now and free - no plugin update is needed.


**Expanded company financials.** SEC coverage broadened from companies over $10B to companies over $5B - around 1,400 US-listed companies, extracted from 10-K, 10-Q, 20-F, and 40-F filings.


**EU trade - Eurostat Comext.** Monthly imports and exports for all 27 EU member states, by CN8 product and partner country, value and quantity. ~71 million series, 2002–present.


**EU macro, rates & climate.** Curated Eurostat EU/euro-area indicators, ECB policy rates and euro reference exchange rates, EEA greenhouse-gas outcomes, and EU ETS. ~5,700 series, 1980–present.


**Global health indicators.** WHO Global Health Observatory data on immunization, maternal and neonatal mortality, life expectancy, tobacco use, and the universal health coverage index. ~2,200 series, 1951–present.


**Global health financing, capacity & disease burden.** Health-spending shares by payer (out-of-pocket, government, private, external), physicians and hospital beds, universal health coverage, Eurostat hospital utilisation, and WHO burden-of-disease estimates. ~20,000 series, 1960–2024.


**US National Health Expenditures.** US national health spending by source of funds (Medicare, Medicaid, private insurance, out-of-pocket) and type of service, plus per-capita and % of GDP. 1960–2024


**US IRS Statistics of Income.** US individual income tax return aggregates - returns filed, adjusted gross income, taxable income, total tax liability, wages, and capital gains. 1913–2023.


With these additions, the catalog now spans 95M+ time series across 28 official sources. See the full[data catalog](https://www.factiq.com/catalog) .
