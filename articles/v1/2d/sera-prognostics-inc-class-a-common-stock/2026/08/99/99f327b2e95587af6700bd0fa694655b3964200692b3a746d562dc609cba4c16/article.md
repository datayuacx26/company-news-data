# SERA PROGNOSTICS REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS

> “During the second quarter, we continued to build momentum across the key drivers of long-term adoption, including payer engagement, policy advancement, clinical evidence generation, and organizational strength,” said Zhenya Lindgardt, President and Chief Executive Officer of Sera Prognostics. “The launch of our fourth partnership program, progress in payer engagement, the new Illinois Medicaid coverage law, the publication of compelling PRIME data in first-time mothers, and growing recognition from the maternal-fetal medicine community reinforce our belief that precision, biology-driven pregnancy care can improve outcomes for mothers and babies while creating meaningful value for healthcare systems. While we remain in the early stages of market development, we have been encouraged by the steady month-to-month increase in testing volume following publication of the PRIME study, which we believe reflects growing provider awareness and adoption.”
