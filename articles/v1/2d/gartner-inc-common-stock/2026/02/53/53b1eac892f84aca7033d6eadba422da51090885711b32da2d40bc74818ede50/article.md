# Gartner to Participate in the Bank of America 2026 Information & Business Services Conference

STAMFORD, Conn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Feb. 26, 2026-- Gartner, Inc. (NYSE: IT) today announced that David Cohen, SVP, Investor Relations will participate in a fireside chat at the Bank of America 2026 Information & Business Services Conference at 8:40 am ET on Thursday, March 12, 2026, in New York, NY.


A link to the live webcast of the fireside chat will be available via the Company's web site at[https://investor.gartner.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.gartner.com&esheet=54432576&newsitemid=20260226158172&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.gartner.com&index=1&md5=c5cad69660779b1af4efbeaa0fb5eb3a) . A replay of the webcast will be available for approximately 30 days following the presentations.
