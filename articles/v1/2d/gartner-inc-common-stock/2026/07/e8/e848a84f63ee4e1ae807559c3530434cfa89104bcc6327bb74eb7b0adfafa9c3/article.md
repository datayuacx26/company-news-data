# Gartner to Report Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results on August 4, 2026

STAMFORD, Conn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 21, 2026-- Gartner, Inc. (NYSE: IT) will report its financial results for second quarter 2026 before the market opens on Tuesday, August 4, 2026. The press release and earnings supplement, with accompanying financial information, will be posted on the Gartner investor website at[https://investor.gartner.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.gartner.com&esheet=54568096&newsitemid=20260721973372&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.gartner.com&index=1&md5=08bfd66a0798c89f51d27eaa65ea85f3) .
