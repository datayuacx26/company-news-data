# What Is Shift at Midnight? The Viral Skinwalker Gas Station Horror Game, Explained

If your feed is full of clips of gas station clerks staring at an ID, hesitating, and then either apologising or opening fire, you have met Shift at Midnight - the co-op horror game that turned a minimum-wage night shift into the most stressful job in gaming. Here is everything you need to know.


## The pitch: check IDs, spot the thing wearing a face


Shift at Midnight is an online cooperative detective horror game. You and up to two others work randomly generated shifts at a rural gas station, and your job is to investigate your customers - because some of them are only pretending to be human.


The tells are small and the pressure is constant. You examine an ID, cross-check the details on the store computer, and watch how the person in front of you behaves. Get it wrong and a creature walks into your store, at which point the game stops being paperwork and becomes survival: board the doors, set traps, find somewhere to hide.


## How the gameplay works


- Customers arrive one at a time. Serve them, verify them, or turn them away.
- Every shift is randomly generated, so there is no memorising the answers.
- Verification means comparing the ID, the computer records, and the person's behaviour for contradictions.
- Let the wrong one in and the game flips to a chase - barricading, traps, hiding, stamina management.
- Proximity chat means your teammates' panic is diegetic. It is the best and worst feature in the game.


## Who made it


Shift at Midnight is the work of a single developer, Bun Muen, exclusively licensed to publisher Kwalee. It is the kind of release that keeps proving a small, sharp idea beats a big budget.


## Release date, price and platforms


Shift at Midnight launched on 22 July 2026 on Steam for PC, and arrived on Xbox the same day, including day-one availability on Xbox Game Pass. It sits at roughly $9.99, and there is a free Shift at Midnight Multiplayer Demo on Steam if you want to try before buying. Reception has been strong - Very Positive on Steam, with 94% of thousands of reviews positive. A free Endless Mode update has been announced for later in 2026.


## Player count and co-op


The game is built for 1-3 players online. You can play it solo, and a post-launch update widened lobbies past three, but the developer has stressed the intended experience is a trio. Crossplay is partial: the Xbox console and PC Game Pass versions share a pool, while Steam players match with Steam players. For more on going it alone, read[our single-player breakdown](https://www.rosebud.ai/blog/shift-at-midnight-single-player) .


## Why it went viral


Shift at Midnight sits at the intersection of two proven formats: the identity-verification horror of That's Not My Neighbor, and the proximity-chat co-op chaos of Lethal Company and Phasmophobia. Both are extremely clippable, and TikTok did the rest - double faces, mutations mid-transaction, and clerks shooting the wrong customer have all done numbers. The skinwalker framing gave it a hook that needs no explanation to anyone who has spent five minutes on horror TikTok.
