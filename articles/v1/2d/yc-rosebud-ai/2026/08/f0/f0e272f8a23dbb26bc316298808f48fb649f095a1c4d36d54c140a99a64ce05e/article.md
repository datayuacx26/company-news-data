# How to Make Your Own Shift at Midnight-Style Skinwalker Game (No Code)

Shift at Midnight was made by one person. That is worth sitting with for a second: the biggest co-op horror hit of the summer came from a solo developer, Bun Muen, working on a premise simple enough to explain in a sentence - you work a night shift, and some customers are not human.


Simple premises are the ones worth building on. Six Midnight Shifts is a free skinwalker horror game inspired by Shift at Midnight, made with[Rosebud AI](https://www.rosebud.ai/) without hand-writing code. Here is how to make your own version.


## Step 1: Describe the fantasy, not the feature list


Rosebud turns a plain-English description into a working game. Lead with the feeling. Something like: 'A first-person night shift in a rural convenience store. Customers come to the counter one at a time. Some of them are not human. I can check their ID against a records computer, and I have a gun under the register.'


You will get something playable in minutes. That first version is a starting point, not the game - the iteration is where it becomes good.


## Step 2: Build the verification loop first


The heart of this genre is one decision made under pressure: serve, refuse, or shoot. Get that working before anything else. Ask for a records computer the player can look things up in, an ID the customer hands over, and a mismatch system - a face that does not match the photo, a birth date that contradicts the record, an answer that is one word wrong.


Test it obsessively. If the tells are too obvious the game is boring; if they are invisible it is unfair. That balance is the whole design.


## Step 3: Make the mundane work stressful


What makes Shift at Midnight work is that verifying identities is not your only job. Add the boring tasks - restocking, cleaning, running the register - and put impatient customers on a timer. Now the player has to split attention, and that is when they miss the detail that kills them.


## Step 4: Escalate across nights


Structure the game as a run of shifts rather than one endless session. Night one teaches the rules. By night four the anomalies are subtler, the queue is longer, and the thing at the counter has stopped bothering to hide. Six shifts is a good length - long enough to build dread, short enough to finish.


## Step 5: Punish the wrong kind of certainty


Make shooting a real customer a genuine failure, not a slap on the wrist. A gun that solves everything removes the tension you spent four steps building. The fear should be of being wrong in either direction.


## Step 6: Publish and share


Publishing from Rosebud gives your game its own page anyone can play in a browser, desktop or mobile, with no download and no store approval. That is how[Six Midnight Shifts](https://rosebud.ai/play/six-midnight-shifts-skinwalker-horror) shipped.


## Shortcut: remix instead of starting over


You do not have to begin from a blank prompt. Open Six Midnight Shifts on Rosebud and remix it - you get the full working game as your starting point, then change the setting or the monster. A hospital night shift, a border checkpoint, a lighthouse, a space station airlock: same loop, new nightmare.
