# Games Like Shift at Midnight: 7 Skinwalker and Anomaly Horror Games to Play Next

Shift at Midnight took a very old horror idea - something is pretending to be a person - and attached it to the most mundane job imaginable. You are behind a gas station counter at 3am, you have an ID in your hand, and you have to decide. It is short, it is cheap, and once you have cleared it you will want more of exactly that feeling.


Here are seven games like Shift at Midnight, starting with one you can play free in your browser in the next thirty seconds.


## 1. Six Midnight Shifts (free, in your browser)


The closest free match, and the only one on this list built specifically around the same loop: convenience store, night shift, customers who may not be human. You serve the queue, maintain the store, cross-check faces and answers against county records, and keep a sidearm under the counter for the moment you are certain. Six shifts, single-player, no download.


[Play Six Midnight Shifts free](https://rosebud.ai/play/six-midnight-shifts-skinwalker-horror) - browser-based, desktop and mobile. More on it in[our free-to-play guide](https://www.rosebud.ai/blog/shift-at-midnight-free) .


## 2. That's Not My Neighbor


The doppelganger game that walked so Shift at Midnight could run. You are a 1955 apartment doorman checking IDs against a bulletin of known impostors, and the tells are tiny: a wrong eye colour, a name spelled almost right. If the verification part of Shift at Midnight is what hooked you, start here.


## 3. Papers, Please


The origin point of the whole genre. No monsters, just documents, contradictions and a moral cost to every stamp. It is the game that proved a desk job could be the tensest thing on your hard drive.


## 4. Phasmophobia


Co-op investigation horror with proximity chat, where the fun is four people in a dark house disagreeing about evidence. There is even an official Shift at Midnight x Phasmophobia bundle on Steam, which tells you how closely the two audiences overlap.


## 5. Lethal Company


The modern co-op horror standard: quotas, scrap, proximity chat, and friends dying stupidly. Different job, identical group dynamic - the shared shift where somebody is definitely about to make a bad call.


## 6. I'm on Observation Duty


Pure anomaly spotting. You watch security cameras and report what changed: a chair moved, a painting turned, a figure that was not in that corner a minute ago. It trains exactly the muscle Shift at Midnight tests when something in your store is subtly wrong.


## 7. Night of the Consumers


Retail horror at its most literal. You are a supermarket employee, the customers are relentless and inhuman in a very different way, and the whole thing is a panic attack about service work. It is the comedy sibling to Shift at Midnight's dread.

## Frequently asked questions


### What game is most similar to Shift at Midnight?


That's Not My Neighbor is the closest paid game for the identity-verification loop. Six Midnight Shifts is the closest free one, and the only browser game built around the same convenience store night shift premise.


### Is there a free game like Shift at Midnight?


Yes - Six Midnight Shifts is free in the browser, and Shift at Midnight itself has a free multiplayer demo on Steam.


### Are these games co-op?


Phasmophobia and Lethal Company are co-op focused. That's Not My Neighbor, Papers Please, I'm on Observation Duty and Six Midnight Shifts are single-player.
