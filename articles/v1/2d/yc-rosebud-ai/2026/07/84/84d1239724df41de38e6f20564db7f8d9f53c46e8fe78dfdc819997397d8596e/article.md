# Dear Passengers Multiplayer: How Co-op Works + a Free Multiplayer Plane Game

Nobody is wishlisting Dear Passengers to play it alone. The entire pitch is co-op: you and your friends are the crew of the world's worst airline, and the game is what happens when everything goes wrong at once. Here's everything known about how Dear Passengers multiplayer works - and a free multiplayer plane crash game you can play in your browser right now.


## How Dear Passengers co-op works


Dear Passengers, from developer FLEXUS, splits the crew across roles:


- One player takes the cockpit and actually flies the plane through weather, turbulence, and air pockets.
- Everyone else works the cabin: serving meals, calming passengers, securing cargo, and fixing whatever breaks next.
- Before takeoff, the crew picks which passengers and cargo to accept - riskier choices pay more, and some crates contain things like live crocodiles.
- Everything is physics-driven, and the plane pitches under your feet, so even walking down the aisle is a challenge mid-crisis.


It's the Overcooked formula strapped to a failing aircraft: divided attention, constant small emergencies, and success depending entirely on how well your group communicates while screaming.


## The catch: you can't play it yet


Dear Passengers is targeting a 2026 release on Steam. There's no demo and no early access - just a wishlist button. Player count hasn't been officially confirmed either.

Everyone is on the same doomed plane, disasters keep hitting, and the goal is to survive longer than the other players - with sabotage very much allowed. Shove debris toward rivals, grab passengers, and be the last one standing when the wreck settles.
