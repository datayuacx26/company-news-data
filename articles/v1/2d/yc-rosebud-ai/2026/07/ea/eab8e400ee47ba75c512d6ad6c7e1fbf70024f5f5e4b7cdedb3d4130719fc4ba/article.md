In practical terms, a Rosebud user can ask the AI for a custom image (for example, ***“a logo of a rocket ship”*** ) or a 3D object, and the platform will create it and add it to the project’s asset library automatically.


**Rosebud even includes a background removal feature** for generated images, so you can produce PNG-style graphics with transparent backgrounds ready to use in your app’s UI.

For teams that need a lot of custom graphics or are building visually-rich experiences, **Rosebud’s approach offers a clear advantage in convenience and cost-efficiency.**

## Deployment and Hosting


Once an app is built, getting it live to users is the next big step.


**Rosebud AI makes deployment incredibly simple with one-click publishing** . As soon as you’re happy with your app or game, you can press a single **“Publish”** button and Rosebud will package your project and deploy it to a live URL automatically.


**There’s no need to configure servers** , set up hosting, or deal with deployment pipelines – all of that is handled in the background. Within seconds, you receive a sharable link (on Rosebud’s domain) where your live app is running.


Moreover, Rosebud supports easy iteration – **every time you make changes in the editor, your project is auto-saved in the cloud** , and you can deploy updates with another single click when ready.


The platform essentially serves as a combined development and hosting environment. It even keeps a version history of your sessions, so you can roll back or fork your project if needed without worry.

**Rosebud AI** is built with **ease-of-use as a top priority** .


To start, you don’t need to install anything or set up a complex environment – everything runs in your web browser. **The interface is organized into intuitive sections (for code, assets, previews, history, etc.) and you interact with the AI through a chat-like prompt panel.**


New users can simply type what they want and **watch Rosebud generate the app step by step.**
