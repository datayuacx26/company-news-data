# Games Like Dear Passengers: 7 Chaotic Co-op Games to Play While You Wait

Dear Passengers - the co-op game about crewing the world's worst airline - is targeting a 2026 Steam release, and there's no demo in the meantime. If the trailer left you wanting physics chaos, screaming friends, and systems failing in cascade, here are seven games that deliver the same energy today.


## 1. ATTENTION PASSENGERS (free, in your browser)


The closest match on this list, because it's directly inspired by Dear Passengers. ATTENTION PASSENGERS is a physics-driven plane crash survival game: disasters hit the plane mid-flight, and you dodge debris, grab other passengers, and try to outlast the wreck. There's a multiplayer mode where you can sabotage other players while you all fight to survive the longest.


[Play ATTENTION PASSENGERS free](https://rosebud.ai/play/attention-passengers-plane-crash-survival-game) - no download, works on desktop and mobile. We covered it in more depth in[our free-to-play guide](https://www.rosebud.ai/blog/dear-passengers-free) .


## 2. Lethal Company


The game that defined the modern co-op chaos genre. Scavenge derelict moons for scrap with your friends, stay under quota, and try not to laugh when the proximity chat cuts to static. If Dear Passengers becomes a hit, it will be walking the road Lethal Company paved.


## 3. R.E.P.O.


Physics-based co-op horror where you and your crew haul fragile valuables out of monster-infested buildings. The full-body physics comedy - objects swinging, teammates ragdolling - is exactly the flavor of slapstick Dear Passengers is promising at altitude.


## 4. Content Warning


Film yourselves doing dangerous things in the Old World and go viral on SpookTube. Less about planes, all about the friendslop formula: proximity chat, shared panic, and clips you'll rewatch for weeks.


## 5. PEAK


A co-op climbing game where one slip can wipe the whole expedition. It nails the same feeling Dear Passengers is chasing: a fragile shared vehicle for disaster, except here the vehicle is your own grip strength.


## 6. Party Animals


Floppy physics brawling with friends. No survival stakes, but the ragdoll sabotage energy - shoving your best friend off a ledge at the worst possible moment - is pure Dear Passengers cabin behavior.


## 7. Overcooked! 2


The classic co-op stress machine. Developers have compared Dear Passengers to Overcooked with a flight deck: divided roles, constant small emergencies, and a kitchen (or cabin) that never stops moving.
