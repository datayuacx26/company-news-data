## Deployment and Hosting: One-Click Instant Deploy with Rosebud


After building your app or game, Rosebud makes it incredibly simple to deploy and share it. The platform is entirely cloud-based and browser-run, meaning every project you create can be launched live with a single click – **no manual setup or external hosting required** .


In fact, Rosebud automatically provides a live preview as you build, and when you’re ready, the “Deploy” button instantly publishes your project to a production URL. **Your app or game goes live instantly, fully configured** with all necessary back-end and front-end settings taken care of.


This seamless deployment is part of Rosebud’s core offering: **even free users can share their projects immediately and host them on Rosebud’s platform at no cost** .


Furthermore, **Rosebud supports advanced needs like connecting a database or external services** (e.g., integrating Supabase for a backend) with minimal effort, as shown in their tutorials.


For those looking to scale or monetize, **Rosebud’s Pro plans allow selling your apps with no commission taken.**
