# SuperHarness: A Smarter Approach to Long-Row Solar Design and Performance

Every utility-scale solar project faces the same challenge: do more with less.


As demand from AI-driven data centers accelerates solar deployment, developers are under pressure to build faster, control costs, and deliver long-term performance. That’s not easy, especially across long, stacked tracker rows where wiring complexity can quickly add up.


That’s where **SuperHarness** comes in.[SuperHarness](https://shoals.com/en/products/homeruns-interconnection-and-extensions/superharness/) is the next evolution of Shoals’ patented interconnect string harness system. It is designed specifically for long-row solar sites, collecting multiple strings into a more streamlined architecture that supports both traditional combiner layouts and Shoals[BLA trunk bus](https://shoals.com/en/products/big-lead-assembly-bla-trunk-bus/big-lead-assembly-bla/) .


The result is fewer harnesses, fewer connections, and simpler wire management across the site. That means faster, cleaner installations and fewer opportunities for things to go wrong.


[SuperHarness](https://shoals.com/en/products/homeruns-interconnection-and-extensions/superharness/) also takes a smarter approach to materials. Traditional copper harnesses can become expensive when upsized to manage voltage drop over long distances. SuperHarness uses a strategic hybrid of aluminum and copper, delivering performance where it matters most, while keeping costs under control. And it shows up where it counts: simpler system design, shorter project timelines, and stronger long-term reliability.
