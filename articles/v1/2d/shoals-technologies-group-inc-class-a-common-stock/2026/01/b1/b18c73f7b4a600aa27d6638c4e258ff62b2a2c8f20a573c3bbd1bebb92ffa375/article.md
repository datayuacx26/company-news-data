# What Is EBOS? The Backbone of Modern Power Infrastructure

Every solar, energy storage, and data center project has a quiet hero working behind the scenes: **EBOS** .


Short for **Electrical Balance of Systems** , EBOS is what keeps electricity moving safely and smoothly, from solar panels and batteries to inverters, racks, and the grid. It’s the wiring, connectors, switches, and protection gear that make sure power shows up where it’s supposed to, when it’s supposed to.


Great EBOS stays out of the spotlight, until it doesn’t. Most[common safety and reliability issues](https://www.heliovolta.com/resources/pv-health-report-2025) in energy projects start at the connection level. That’s why EBOS plays such a big role in uptime, performance, and long-term success, especially in solar, energy storage, and data centers where power quality and reliability are everything.


That’s what our short video is all about. We break it down in plain language and show how smarter EBOS design can reduce risk and simplify installation. We illustrate how it can support the demands of modern energy and data center infrastructure.


At Shoals, we design EBOS to be simple, plug-and-play, and factory-tested, so there’s less guesswork in the field and fewer things that can go wrong later. The result is faster installs, fewer failures, and infrastructure built to keep power flowing.
