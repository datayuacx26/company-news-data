## Measurable AI Recognized for Excellence in Data Innovation at the DaLa Awards 2024


We are proud to share that Measurable AI has been honored with two prestigious accolades at the **DaLa Awards 2024** in Hong Kong: the[Best Data Monetization Award](https://pages.veplatform.com/10016500-3f59-4dff-b994-6b5e00200007/index.html?vid=0x676c2e7687d137de00173045019077058c77d7ae9c05b43ff786fd92dbba3c98&env=FORMAL) and the[Best Data Tech Startup Award](https://pages.veplatform.com/10016500-3f59-4dff-b994-6b5e00200007/index.html?vid=0x676c2e7687d137de001730464590a33540e066c99de172f6571470651d6a3bcc&env=FORMAL) . These achievements mark a significant milestone in our mission to deliver actionable consumer insights through anonymous e-receipt data, solidifying our position as a trusted leader in the data industry.


The DaLa Awards 2024, hosted by the Data Literacy Association and co-organized by the Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks Corporation (HKSTP), aim to spotlight leaders in the data industry who drive innovation, transparency, and the responsible use of data. The event gathered pioneers in data and technology, celebrating companies and individuals making data literacy accessible and valuable.


Winning these awards underscores Measurable AI’s commitment to developing a decentralized data economy, powered by blockchain, that empowers businesses with real-time, SKU-level insights from emerging markets. Our datasets are trusted by clients around the globe—from Wall Street financial institutions to Silicon Valley tech firms—enabling them to make data-driven decisions based on actual transactional data. Updated weekly, these insights reveal granular details about consumer behavior across sectors such as food delivery, e-commerce, and fintech, helping businesses stay ahead of market trends.


Measurable AI’s CEO, Bryan Tsui, expressed his gratitude to the community: “A heartfelt thank you to all our trusted clients. One of the most significant data monetization use cases at Measurable AI is generating consumer insights from actual transaction data points. The active participation of our users in the data ecosystem is what enables us to continuously enhance the value of our data and drive innovation.”


These recognitions affirm our team’s dedication to delivering high-quality, privacy-compliant data solutions that help businesses harness the power of data responsibly. As we celebrate this success, we are energized to keep advancing data and AI innovation, expanding our ecosystem, and delivering valuable insights to our global clients.


Thank you for being part of our journey. Here’s to more achievements and exciting innovations ahead!
