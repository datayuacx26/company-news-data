## Pokémon GO versus TCG Pocket: Pokémon’s top grossing game ranking


Trainers, ever wondered which Pokémon mobile game is raking in the most PokéCoins? While **Pokémon GO** is still the biggest hit in the franchise even after nearly a decade, other games like **Pokémon Unite** and **Pokémon TCG Pocket** have also found their own fans. **** Let’s check out the spending trends of Pokémon games and see which game is the true champion!

## **Pokémon GO Still Dominates in Transaction Volume**


Based on our e-receipt panel, **Pokémon GO** has consistently generated the highest number of in-app transactions each quarter since its launch in 2016. While newer entries like **Pokémon Unite** and **Pokémon TCG Pocket** saw bursts of activity after launch, their momentum quickly tapered off.


To make the comparison fair, we indexed each title’s receipt volume against **GO** ’s launch quarter (Q3 2016 = 100). This makes clear how far the others stand from **GO** ’s original scale.


*Measurable AI: *Order Volume Index in this chart are captured from Measurable AI’s unique consumer panel. Data range from 2016 Q3 to 2025 Q1.*


**Pokémon TCG Pocket** peaked at around ~20% of **Pokémon GO** ’s benchmark, while **Pokémon Unite** never crossed the 10% mark, highlighting the fact that **Pokémon GO** ’s early lead in user base and its location-based limited-time events gave it a competitive edge that’s hard to replicate.


## **Which Pokémon Mobile Game Delivers the Highest AOV?**


We also looked at **average order value (AOV)** on iOS App Store:


*Measurable AI: *iOS Average Order Value (AOV) in this chart is captured from Measurable AI’s unique consumer panel. Data of 2025 Q1.*


- **Pokémon Sleep** leads with the highest average order value (AOV) among all titles in Q1 2025, despite its relatively low transaction volume.
- **Pokémon Masters EX** shows fluctuation but maintains mid-tier AOV performance.
- **Pokémon TCG** **Pocket** stays solid with high AOVs, while **Pokémon GO** and **Pokémon Unite** remain consistent at the lower end.


## **Trainers’ Picks: The Most Popular Character Bundles**


Digging deeper into **item-level purchases** in **Pokémon TCG Pocket** , we analyzed six multilingual bundles and grouped them into three major character-themed offerings on Android side:


*Measurable AI: *Receipt Volume Index in this chart is captured from Measurable AI’s unique consumer panel. Data range from 2024 Q3 to 2025 Q1.*


The **Gardevoir Bundle** led the pack in popularity, followed closely by the Sinnoh League Champion — **Cynthia Bundle** . Despite Mewtwo’s legendary status, its themed bundle ranked lowest — possibly due to release timing or packaging format. This highlights how character loyalty can significantly influence in-game purchasing behavior.


*Fun Fact:* Not all legendaries are equal — especially when it comes to sales performance.
