Smart systems can merge data from different sources automatically, resolving conflicts and standardizing formats. They understand common business data patterns and apply the right transformations, like normalizing currency or fixing date formats across systems.


This automation extends to ongoing maintenance too. As new data flows in, platforms automatically apply established rules and flag anything that looks weird or different from historical patterns.
