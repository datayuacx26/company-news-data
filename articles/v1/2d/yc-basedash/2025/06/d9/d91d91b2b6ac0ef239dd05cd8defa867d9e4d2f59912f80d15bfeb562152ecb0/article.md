# Introducing Basedash: The AI-native BI platform built for today’s teams

We’re excited to announce that Basedash is officially open to everyone!


Earlier this year, we introduced our new AI-native platform in a limited Product Hunt launch, welcoming teams eager to explore the next generation of business intelligence. Today, we’re opening our doors to the public—anyone can now sign up and experience how effortless data insights can truly be.


### **Built from the ground up for the AI era**


Traditional BI tools like Metabase, Tableau, and Looker changed how businesses interact with data, but we’re taking things a step further. Basedash is a modern business intelligence platform built specifically for the AI age:


- **Unified Data:** Connect any of 750+ data sources, and we’ll unify everything seamlessly into a single data warehouse.
- **AI-Powered Queries:** No more SQL, no complicated setups. Simply describe a chart or ask a question like “Who are my top customers by revenue?”—and Basedash instantly visualizes and answers.
- **Instant Schema Understanding:** Our AI immediately understands your database schema, enabling you to query effortlessly within minutes of connecting.
- **Unmatched Speed and Usability:** Experience a lightning-fast, 60 FPS interface with complete keyboard shortcut support.
- **Secure by Design:** SOC 2 compliant with zero impact on your production database.


### **Effortless onboarding, instant insights**


When we first shared Basedash on Product Hunt, we offered white glove onboarding to ensure a perfect experience from the start. We’ve now opened up self-serve signups to everyone, but we’re still offering[onboarding calls to anyone interested](https://www.basedash.com/book/onboarding) .


Now, we’re making powerful AI-driven analytics accessible to everyone—no waitlists, just immediate data insights.


[Try Basedash today](https://charts.basedash.com/signup) and redefine how your team interacts with data.
