# The best June Analytics alternatives for B2B SaaS teams in 2026

June Analytics caught your attention with its promise of simple product analytics. But with the founding team joining Amplitude, you may be looking for alternatives.


This guide walks through the top June Analytics alternatives. We cover everything from open-source options to enterprise solutions. We’ll help you find a product analytics platform that fits your team’s needs and budget.


## **Why teams are moving beyond June Analytics**


June Analytics works well for what it does. It provides simple product analytics for early-stage B2B SaaS companies. The natural language queries and auto-generated reports make it easy for entire teams to use, not just data analysts. But success brings challenges that June struggles to handle.


### **June Analytics is shutting down permanently**


[In July 2025, June Analytics announced that it will officially wind down operations on August 8, 2025.](https://www.june.so/blog/a-new-chapter) The founding team is joining Amplitude. This shutdown creates immediate urgency for current users who need to migrate their data and find new analytics solutions.


Current June customers have 30 days to export their data or migrate to Amplitude. The company provides export tools and migration guides to ease the transition. While June offers refunds for unused subscription time and maintains full support during the transition period, the shutdown means teams must find new analytics platforms no matter how satisfied they are with June’s current capabilities.


This development makes the search for June Analytics alternatives not just about finding better features or pricing, but about finding a reliable long-term partner for your product analytics needs. The clock is ticking for current users to make this critical decision.


### **Limited customization options frustrate growing teams**


Those auto-generated reports that seemed so convenient? They start feeling restrictive when you need specific insights that don’t fit June’s pre-built metrics. While you can view and edit SQL queries, the overall flexibility stays limited compared to more robust analytics platforms.


Growing product teams often need custom dashboards, specific KPIs, and unique reporting formats. June’s simple approach works for basic needs but falls short when your analytics requirements become more advanced.


### **Integration gaps slow down team workflows**


June integrates well with Segment, HubSpot, and a few other platforms. But as your tech stack grows, you’ll likely need connections that June doesn’t support. This creates data silos that make complete analysis harder.


Most SaaS teams use dozens of tools across sales, marketing, and product development. When your analytics platform can’t connect to key tools, you end up with incomplete data and manual workarounds that waste time.


### **Analytics needs grow beyond basic metrics**


June focuses on core SaaS metrics like acquisition, activation, retention, and churn. These are essential, but mature product teams often need advanced features like cohort analysis, funnel optimization, or predictive analytics that June doesn’t provide.


As your product evolves, you need deeper insights into user behavior, feature adoption, and customer journey mapping. June’s basic metrics become insufficient for making complex product decisions.


## **Top 6 June Analytics alternatives for B2B SaaS teams**


We’ve evaluated dozens of analytics platforms to find the best June alternatives. Here are the top 6 options that offer better scalability, features, and value:


- Basedash - AI-native business intelligence platform
- PostHog - Complete open-source analytics solution
- Amplitude - Advanced cross-platform behavioral analytics
- Heap - Automatic event capture and analysis
- Matomo - Privacy-focused open-source analytics platform
- Mixpanel - Event-based product analytics platform


Let’s dive into what makes each platform special and which teams they serve best.


### **1. Basedash: AI-native business intelligence platform**


[Basedash](https://www.basedash.com/) takes a different approach by building AI capabilities into the platform from day one. Instead of adding AI features later, the entire experience is designed around making data analysis feel natural.


The setup process eliminates the complexity that often frustrates teams transitioning from June. You can ask questions in plain English and get meaningful answers through AI-powered natural language search. The AI-generated summaries and narratives make it easy to understand what your data is telling you.


Pricing transparency fixes one of the biggest pain points teams face when scaling beyond June’s free tier. Instead of usage-based surprises, you get predictable costs that scale with your business needs. For teams building customer-facing analytics, Basedash offers clean APIs and customization options that let you create seamless embedded experiences.


**Best for** : B2B SaaS teams who want AI-enhanced analytics without enterprise complexity.


### **2. PostHog: The complete open-source analytics solution**


PostHog combines product analytics, session replay, feature flags, A/B testing, and user surveys in a single platform. This all-in-one approach eliminates the tool sprawl that many teams experience when trying to get complete product insights.


The open-source foundation means you can self-host if data privacy is a concern, or use their cloud version for convenience. Unlike June’s limited feature set, PostHog provides deep functionality across multiple areas of product development.


Session replays with event timelines give you context that pure analytics can’t provide. You can see exactly what users did before they churned or converted, making it easier to identify optimization opportunities. The platform’s feature flagging and A/B testing capabilities let you experiment safely and measure results within the same tool you use for analytics.


**Best for** : Teams wanting complete product analytics with the flexibility of open-source software.


### **3. Amplitude: Advanced cross-platform behavioral analytics**


Amplitude specializes in user behavior analytics with advanced journey tracking and engagement analysis. Over 3,200 companies use it to understand how customers interact with their products across multiple touchpoints.


The platform excels at cohort analysis and retention studies that go far beyond June’s basic retention metrics. You can track user behavior over time, identify patterns that lead to expansion or churn, and measure the impact of product changes.


Real-time data updates and custom event tracking provide the flexibility that June’s pre-built metrics can’t match. The intuitive interface makes complex analysis accessible to non-technical team members. Amplitude’s extensive integrations broaden its utility across various platforms and ecosystems.


**Best for** : Teams needing advanced behavioral analytics and cross-platform user journey tracking.


### **4. Heap: Automatic event capture and analysis**


Heap automatically captures every user interaction, eliminating the manual event tagging that slows down many analytics implementations. This retroactive analysis capability means you can gain insights from historical data without prior setup.


The visual editor lets teams tag on-page events for analysis without engineering support. This democratizes analytics in a way that June attempts but doesn’t fully achieve due to its limited customization options.


Heap’s advanced funnel and retention analysis capabilities provide deeper insights than June’s basic metrics. You can analyze user journeys with granular detail and identify specific drop-off points in your conversion process. Integration with customer engagement tools and data warehouses enables complete analysis that combines product usage data with business metrics.


**Best for** : Teams wanting complete event tracking without manual setup requirements.


### **5. Matomo: Privacy-focused open-source analytics platform**


Matomo emphasizes user privacy and data ownership while providing complete analytics capabilities. You can self-host to maintain complete control over your data, which is increasingly important for companies serving privacy-conscious customers.


The open-source platform offers flexibility to customize and adapt analytics to specific needs. This addresses the customization limitations that frustrate teams using June’s more rigid structure.


Unlike platforms that store data on third-party servers, Matomo lets you keep everything on your own infrastructure. This ensures compliance with data protection regulations and gives you full control over data access and retention. The platform provides detailed analytics without compromising user privacy.


**Best for** : Teams prioritizing data privacy and ownership with the flexibility of open-source customization.


### **6. Mixpanel: Event-based product analytics platform**


Mixpanel focuses specifically on event-based tracking and user behavior analysis. The platform provides more granular control over event definitions and analysis than June’s simplified approach.


Advanced segmentation capabilities let you analyze user behavior across multiple dimensions. You can create cohorts based on behavior patterns, demographic data, or custom properties to understand different user groups.


The platform’s retention analysis goes deeper than June’s basic metrics, offering cohort tables and advanced visualization options. A/B testing integration helps you measure the impact of product changes directly within your analytics platform. Mixpanel’s API and integration capabilities make it easier to connect with other tools in your tech stack.


**Best for** : Teams needing detailed event-based analytics with advanced segmentation capabilities.


## **How to choose the right June Analytics alternative**


Selecting the best June Analytics alternative depends on your specific needs, team capabilities, and growth plans. Here are the 5 key factors that will determine which platform works best for your team:


- Technical resources and team capabilities
- Integration needs with existing tools
- Data privacy and compliance requirements
- Future scale and growth planning
- Budget and pricing predictability


Understanding these factors will help you avoid costly mistakes and find a platform that grows with your business.


### **Consider your team’s technical resources and capabilities**


Some platforms require more technical expertise but offer greater flexibility. PostHog and Matomo provide powerful capabilities if you have the technical resources to implement and maintain them. Basedash and Amplitude offer more managed experiences that require less technical overhead.


Think about your team’s current skills and available time for implementation. If you have limited technical resources, choose a platform with simple setup and ongoing maintenance requirements.


### **Evaluate integration needs with existing tools**


Consider how well each solution fits into your existing tech stack. June’s limited integrations might be holding you back, so prioritize platforms that connect with your current tools and can grow with your infrastructure.


Make a list of your essential tools and check which analytics platforms offer native integrations. The more seamlessly your analytics platform connects with your existing workflow, the more value you’ll get from your data.


### **Think about data privacy and compliance requirements**


If you’re serving customers in highly regulated industries or privacy-conscious markets, platforms like Matomo and Basedash that offer self-hosting options might be essential. Cloud-only solutions like Amplitude provide convenience but require trusting third-party data handling.


Consider your industry’s specific compliance requirements and your customers’ privacy expectations. Some businesses need complete control over their data, while others can work with trusted third-party providers.


### **Plan for future scale and growth**


June’s user limits and feature restrictions become problematic as companies grow. Choose a platform that can handle your projected growth without forcing another migration in six months.


Look at pricing tiers and feature limitations to ensure your chosen platform can accommodate your expected user growth, data volume, and feature needs over the next 12-24 months.


### **Budget for predictable costs and pricing**


June’s appeal includes its affordable pricing for small teams. Look for alternatives that offer transparent pricing models that won’t surprise you as your usage grows. Usage-based pricing can be cost-effective but makes budgeting harder.


Calculate potential costs at different usage levels to avoid bill shock as your business grows. Consider both current needs and projected growth when evaluating pricing options.


## **Making the transition from June Analytics smoothly**


Moving from June Analytics to a new platform requires careful planning to avoid losing historical data and insights. Here’s your step-by-step guide to making the transition smooth and successful.


### **Data migration and export considerations**


Most alternatives offer import capabilities, but the process varies significantly. PostHog and Mixpanel have specific migration tools, while others might require more manual work. Plan for this transition time in your implementation schedule.


Start by exporting your data from June using their provided tools. Then work with your new platform’s support team to ensure clean data import and validation. Allow extra time for testing to make sure your historical data transferred correctly.


### **Team training and user adoption strategies**


June’s simplicity is one of its main advantages. Ensure your new platform provides adequate training resources and support to help your team adapt. Platforms like Basedash and Amplitude prioritize user experience to ease this transition.


Schedule training sessions for your team and identify power users who can help others during the transition. Most platforms offer onboarding support and documentation to help your team get up to speed quickly.


### **Gradual rollout and testing approaches**


Consider running your new analytics platform alongside June for a period to ensure data accuracy and team comfort. This parallel approach reduces risk and provides a safety net during the transition.


Start with a small subset of your data or a specific team before rolling out company-wide. This allows you to identify and resolve issues before they affect your entire organization’s analytics workflow.


With June shutting down in August 2025, time is critical for making this transition. The sooner you start testing alternatives, the more time you’ll have to ensure a smooth migration before the deadline.


## **Time to upgrade your product analytics**


June Analytics serves its purpose well for early-stage B2B SaaS companies, but growing businesses need more advanced capabilities. Whether you need PostHog’s complete feature set, Amplitude’s advanced behavioral analytics, or Basedash’s AI-native approach, better options exist for most use cases.


The key is matching platform capabilities to your specific needs rather than trying to force a one-size-fits-all solution. Consider your team’s technical capabilities, integration requirements, privacy needs, and budget constraints when evaluating alternatives.


Most importantly, choose a platform that can grow with your business. The right analytics tool should enable your team’s success and provide insights that drive meaningful product improvements. Don’t let analytics limitations hold back your product development efforts.


Take advantage of free trials and demos to test alternatives with your actual data and use cases. This hands-on evaluation will reveal practical differences that specifications alone cannot capture, helping you make the best choice for your team’s long-term success.
