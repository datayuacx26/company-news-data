# Introducing Basedash for Excel

Today we’re launching **Basedash for Excel** — the fastest way to turn a spreadsheet into a live dashboard, and to get your dashboard data back into Excel when you need it.


Drop an` .xlsx` file into the Basedash agent and it reads your data, figures out what’s in it, and builds the charts and dashboards for you. When you want to keep working in spreadsheets, export any chart’s data straight back to a` .xlsx` file. It works both ways.


## Why we built it


Most teams still live in Excel. It’s where the numbers start, where the ad-hoc analysis happens, and where a lot of the real work gets done. But the moment you want a proper dashboard, a second opinion on the data, or to share something live with the rest of the team, you hit the edge of what a spreadsheet does well.


Basedash for Excel meets you where you already are. You don’t have to migrate anything or rebuild your data somewhere else — you hand the agent the file you already have, and it does the rest.


## Drop in a spreadsheet, get a dashboard


Attach an` .xlsx` file to the agent and ask for what you want — “turn this into a dashboard”, “what’s driving the Q2 jump?”, “break revenue down by month”.


The agent reads every row, infers your column types and totals, writes the queries, and builds the visualizations — no formulas, no pivot tables, no setup.


Because it’s the same[AI data analyst](https://www.basedash.com/features/ai-data-analyst) that powers the rest of Basedash, you can keep the conversation going: ask a follow-up, drill into a number, or pull in another file, and the dashboard updates with you.


## And export right back to Excel


The other direction matters just as much. Any chart in Basedash can export its underlying data to a` .xlsx` file with one click — so if your next step is a spreadsheet, you’re one click away from it.


Run the analysis in Basedash, hand the numbers back to whoever needs them in Excel, and nobody has to leave the tool they prefer.


## How it works


Under the hood it’s the same analyst that explores your schema, writes and verifies SQL, and uses your custom business context — now pointed at the spreadsheet you uploaded. Your Excel data becomes a real, queryable source the moment it lands.


## Excel and Basedash, both ways


This is what makes it click for teams who live in Excel: you get an AI data analyst, live dashboards, and real-time collaboration on top of the files you already trust — without giving up the spreadsheet workflow you know.


## Getting started


Basedash for Excel is available today for all Basedash users:


1. [Sign up for Basedash](https://charts.basedash.com/signup) (or log in)
2. Attach an` .xlsx` file to the agent and ask it to analyze or chart your data
3. Build out a dashboard, then export any chart’s data back to Excel whenever you need it
