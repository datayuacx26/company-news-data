# Introducing Basedash Subscriptions

Today we’re launching **Basedash Subscriptions** — subscribe to any dashboard or chart, and Basedash delivers a fresh snapshot to your inbox or a Slack channel on the schedule you pick.


Set it once: every Monday at 9:00 AM, to #metrics and your inbox. From then on, the report just shows up — up-to-date charts, with a link back to the live dashboard for anyone who wants to dig in.


## Why we built it


Every team has a reporting ritual. The Monday metrics review. The Friday pipeline check. The first-of-the-month board pack. And behind almost every one of them is a person who has to remember to open the dashboard, take a screenshot, and paste it somewhere before the meeting starts.


The dashboard was already built. The data was already fresh. The only thing standing between your team and the numbers was the errand of delivering them. Subscriptions remove the errand: the report comes to you, instead of the other way around.


## Subscribe to any dashboard or chart


Open the menu on any[dashboard](https://www.basedash.com/features/dashboards) or chart and hit **Subscribe** . Pick your schedule with a simple structured sentence — every day, weekdays, a weekly day, monthly or quarterly on a date, at whatever hour you want. It reads exactly like you’d say it: *every week on Monday at 9:00 AM* .


Then choose where it goes under **Deliver to** : email it to yourself or any set of teammates, send it to a Slack channel, or both. Click **Create subscription** and you’re done — there is no step three.


## Delivered where your team already looks


When the schedule fires, Basedash renders a fresh snapshot of every chart and sends it out:


- **In Slack** , the report posts to your channel with each chart embedded as an image, so the whole team sees the numbers in the thread — no clicking through, no logging in.
- **In email** , every recipient gets the charts inline with an **Open dashboard in Basedash** button, so the live version is always one click away.


Either way, the charts are rendered at delivery time from your live data — a subscription never sends a stale report.


## One dashboard, many rituals


Subscriptions are designed to stack. A single revenue dashboard might have a daily digest going to the exec team’s inboxes, a Monday morning post in #metrics, and a monthly snapshot for the board — three subscriptions, three schedules, three audiences, zero manual work.


Each subscription is managed right from the dashboard or chart it belongs to. Edit the schedule or recipients inline, and flip the **Active** switch to pause delivery without deleting anything — useful for reports that only matter during a launch or a quarter-end push.


If you want Basedash to *analyze* on a schedule — summarize the week, flag anomalies, write commentary — that’s what[Automations](https://www.basedash.com/features/automations) are for. Subscriptions are simpler on purpose: the dashboard you already trust, exactly as it looks, delivered on time.


## Getting started


Subscriptions are available today for all Basedash users:


1. [Sign up for Basedash](https://charts.basedash.com/signup) (or log in) and open any dashboard or chart
2. Open the menu and choose **Subscribe**
3. Pick your schedule and destinations, and create the subscription
4. Go do something else — the report will find you
