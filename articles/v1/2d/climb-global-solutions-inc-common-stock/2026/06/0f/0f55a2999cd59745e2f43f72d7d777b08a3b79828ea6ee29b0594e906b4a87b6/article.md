# Teacher Well-Being, Pay, and Intentions to Leave in 2026

**Key Takeaways:**


- Teachers reported lower well-being than similar working adults across every measured indicator in 2026.
- Female teachers have consistently reported experiencing higher job-related stress than male teachers and comparable working adults since 2021.
- Teachers reported an average base salary of $75,599 in 2026, compared with just over $105,000 for similar working adults.
- After accounting for inflation, only 40% of teachers received a pay increase in 2026.
