# Expanding the Pipeline to Support High School Educators

**Key Takeaways:**


- The National Applied AI Consortium is providing free AI professional development to high school teachers across the nation.
- This expansion beyond the NAAIC’s historical focus on community college AI pathways comes with a grant from the NSF.
- More than 600 high school teachers will receive free training focused on AI in education.
