# Where Innovation Meets Impact: Montana State University Leads the Way as an Adobe Creative Campus Innovator

**Key Takeaways:**


- MSU’s leadership noticed a gap in their students that is common across higher education: graduates that have strong academic skills but struggle to apply those skills to the real world.
- In response to this issue, MSU created a Digital Literacy Integration team to ensure students were learning how to apply their skills to potential careers.
- They proceeded to apply Adobe creative tools across disciplines and sponsor the creation of ePortfolios to support career readiness.
