# Future-Ready Security for MSPs: ThreatDown and ConnectSecure

**Bringing proactive risk visibility and endpoint protection together for the modern MSP security stack** .


**Eatontown, New Jersey (July 14, 2026)** – Climb Channel Solutions’ MSP Division is spotlighting ThreatDown and ConnectSecure as part of its monthly MSP line card series, designed to help managed service providers identify emerging, future-ready technologies that solve real business and security challenges across their customer base.


As MSPs continue to support increasingly complex client environments, the demand for practical, scalable security solutions has never been higher. Customers are asking for more than basic protection. They want visibility, accountability, cyber insurance readiness, compliance support, and confidence that their MSP is helping reduce risk before an incident occurs


ConnectSecure helps MSPs identify where risk exists across client environments through asset discovery, vulnerability scanning, attack surface visibility, risk prioritization, and compliance gap reporting. ThreatDown helps MSPs protect, detect, and respond at the endpoint with malware protection, ransomware defense, EDR/MDR capabilities, threat containment, and remediation.


Together, they give MSPs a more complete security motion: identify the exposure, prioritize what needs to be fixed, and protect the endpoint if a threat attempts to execute.


“MSPs are under pressure to deliver more strategic security outcomes without adding unnecessary complexity to their stack,” said Caitlyn Helsel, MSP Partner Manager at Climb. “ConnectSecure helps MSPs proactively identify and prioritize vulnerabilities, while ThreatDown delivers the endpoint protection needed to defend against today’s evolving threats. Both solutions reflect the kind of forward thinking, MSP focused technology we’re committed to bringing to our partners.”


### **Why This Matters for MSPs**


Many MSPs already have endpoint protection in place, but they may not have a formalized way to assess vulnerability exposure, prioritize remediation, document risk, or create executive-ready reporting for client conversations. Others may be building a managed security practice but are not ready to jump straight into a heavy SOC or XDR platform.


ConnectSecure gives MSPs the visibility to understand what assets exist, where vulnerabilities live, and which risks should be addressed first. ThreatDown provides the protection layer needed to stop malware, ransomware, zero-days, and active threats at the endpoint level.


**A simple way to explain the value: ConnectSecure shows where the doors and windows are unlocked. ThreatDown helps stop the intruder if someone tries to break in.**


For MSPs, this creates a stronger business conversation with clients. Instead of leading only with another security tool, MSPs can lead to risk reduction, client accountability, cyber insurance readiness, compliance support, and ongoing security improvement.


### **A Strong Fit for the Modern MSP Security Offering**


ThreatDown and ConnectSecure are especially relevant for MSPs that:


- Support regulated clients in healthcare, finance, legal, education, manufacturing, or municipalities
- Want to monetize security assessments, risk reviews, and QBR reporting
- Need better visibility into vulnerabilities, assets, and attack surface exposure
- Want to strengthen endpoint protection with EDR or MDR options
- Are helping clients prepare for cyber insurance, compliance, or board-level security conversations
- Need scalable, MSP-friendly tools that support multi-tenant service delivery


Whether the goal is improving security visibility, reducing risk, strengthening endpoint protection, or supporting compliance efforts, these solutions help MSPs deliver more strategic security services while demonstrating measurable value to clients.

### **About ThreatDown**


ThreatDown provides endpoint protection, detection, and response solutions designed to help organizations defend against malware, ransomware, zero-days, phishing-related payloads, and active threats. With EDR and MDR options, ThreatDown gives MSPs the ability to strengthen endpoint security while supporting scalable, service-driven delivery.


### **About ConnectSecure**
