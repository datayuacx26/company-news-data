# From Reflection to Creation: How SDSU Used Storytelling, Adobe Express, and AI to Reimagine the First-Year Experience

**Key Takeaways:**


- San Diego State University redesigned its First-Year Seminar to help new students build a stronger sense of identity, purpose, and belonging.
- Adobe Express and Adobe Firefly allow students, in their First-Year Seminar, to transform traditional written reflections into engaging multimedia projects using AI-powered creative tools.
- The program helps students build transferable skills in digital literacy, creative communication, and self-expression that support academic and career success.
