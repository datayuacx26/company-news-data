# Climb MSP Minute with Kaseya

**Meet Kaseya’s Josh Hardy,** **Senior Vice President of Product Marketing**


*Interview by Tim Popovich*


**Tim Popovich**


Chief Operating Officer


**Josh Hardy**


Senior Vice President of Product Marketing


**Welcome to the July 2026 MSP Minute!**


MSPs today are operating in an environment defined by constant change, where evolving technologies and rising client expectations are reshaping how services are delivered. As businesses demand more proactive support and measurable outcomes, MSPs are under pressure to optimize operations while continuing to drive growth and profitability.


In this edition of MSP Minute, Climb sat down with **Josh Hardy, Senior VP of Product Marketing at Kaseya** to explore how MSPs can simplify operations, strengthen security, and scale their businesses through a more unified approach to IT and security management.


##### **Meet Josh Hardy, Senior Vice President of Product Marketing**


Josh Hardy is Senior Vice President of Product Marketing at Kaseya, a leading provider of AI-powered IT management and cybersecurity solutions for MSPs and enterprise IT teams. He leads global product marketing across cybersecurity, backup and disaster recovery, IT operations, and platform solutions. With more than 30 years of experience in enterprise software and B2B technology, Josh has held executive leadership positions at Kaseya, Axway, IBM, and Sterling Commerce, and has advised Fortune 2000 and high-growth companies on go-to-market strategy, product positioning, sales enablement, and revenue growth throughout his career.


##### **********A Unified Platform Built for MSP Growth**********


Kaseya’s mission is centered on enabling MSPs to run smarter, leaner businesses without adding complexity. At the core of this approach is the **Kaseya Platform** , a unified, AI native ecosystem designed to connect every aspect of IT service delivery, security, and cyber resilience.


“Our mission is straightforward: give MSPs the tools, technology, and support they need to run leaner, more profitable businesses without adding complexity,” Hardy explained.


Powered by Kaseya Intelligence, the platform brings together data from across Kaseya and third-party tools to deliver context-aware insights, automation, and workflows.


“What sets Kaseya apart is a combination no other provider can match, an unrivaled depth of data paired with the breadth of an integrated platform,” Hardy noted.


##### ************************Addressing Today’s Expanding Threat Landscape************************


The cybersecurity challenges MSPs face continue to grow in both volume and sophistication. Ransomware remains a constant concern, while phishing, identity-based attacks, dark web exposure, and compliance requirements introduce additional layers of complexity.


“The core challenge is not just the volume of threats. It is that MSPs are defending multiple client environments simultaneously, often with limited security headcounts,” said Hardy.


Kaseya addresses this through a layered security approach built directly into its platform, enabling MSPs to deliver enterprise-grade protection at scale without significantly increasing resources.


##### ****************************************Reducing Complexity Through Integration and Automation****************************************


Kaseya’s platform is designed around deep integration, connecting RMM, PSA, documentation, security, and backup into unified workflows. Through the **Kaseya One** integrations hub, automations run natively across the product suite, reducing manual tasks by up to 40%, according to Kaseya benchmark reports.


For MSPs managing large client bases, this level of operational consistency is essential to scaling without continuously adding headcount.


##### **********************************************************Standing Out in a Crowded Security Market**********************************************************


In a competitive landscape filled with point solutions, Kaseya differentiates itself through both integration depth and economic accessibility.


“Most vendors sell point solutions that MSPs then have to integrate themselves, which creates gaps, adds overhead, and drives up cost,” Hardy noted.


Kaseya’s approach is built around a unified platform, where security tools are designed to work together natively. Solutions like Datto EDR integrate directly with RMM, enabling centralized deployment and real time visibility across all managed environments without requiring separate consoles or vendors.


On the economic side, **Kaseya 365** bundles advanced endpoint and user security into a single subscription, making it easier for MSPs to standardize security across their client base. This allows partners to position security as a core offering, simplifying customer conversations while creating a more consistent and scalable margin opportunity.


##### ********************************************************************************Scaling Operations Without Increasing Overhead********************************************************************************


Scalability is a critical priority for growing MSPs, and Kaseya approaches it from both an operational and financial perspective.


Operationally, the platform’s automation and integration capabilities ensure that adding new clients does not require proportional increases in technician time or tool configuration. Standardized workflows, templated documentation through IT Glue, and automated onboarding processes allow MSPs to bring new customers into their environment efficiently and consistently. This reduces variability in service delivery and helps maintain quality as businesses grow.


Financially, Kaseya provides flexibility through programs like **Kaseya 365** and **FLEXSpend** , enabling MSPs to scale licensing alongside their customer base while reallocating spends across the platform as needed to evolve. More flexible contract options, including month-to-month offerings for select solutions, further align costs with real business growth. Together, these capabilities ensure that expansion does not introduce unnecessary operational friction, allowing MSPs to scale with confidence while maintaining strong margins.


##### **********************************************************************************Driving Efficiency and Margin Through Automation**********************************************************************************


“Automation is arguably the single most direct driver of margin improvement in the Kaseya platform,” Hardy said.


By eliminating repetitive manual tasks such as patch management, backup monitoring, ticket creation, and alert response, MSPs can significantly increase technician productivity without adding headcount. Kaseya’s automation capabilities span the full platform, with prebuilt workflow templates that integrate across solutions like Datto RMM, Autotask, IT Glue, and Datto EDR, allowing partners to streamline operations without the need for custom development.


Centralized management further amplifies these gains. With a single pane of glass across RMM, PSA, documentation, and security, technicians can work more efficiently without switching between multiple consoles. Over time, these incremental time savings translate into faster ticket resolution, greater endpoint density per technician, and stronger overall margins, especially when compared to fragmented, multi-vendor environments.


##### ********************************************************************************************************Elevating MSPs to Strategic Security Partners********************************************************************************************************


[Kaseya’s Partner Program](https://www.kaseya.com/solutions/msp/partner/) enables partners to move beyond traditional IT support and position themselves as comprehensive security providers.


“Partners are using Kaseya to position themselves as true security partners, not just IT vendors with bolts on tools,” Hardy explained.


With capabilities spanning prevention, detection, response, and compliance, MSPs can deliver a fully integrated security practice that strengthens customer trust and drives long-term retention.


Partners are increasingly leveraging Kaseya’s platform to shift their role from service provider to true security advisor. By combining solutions such as BullPhish ID, Dark Web ID, INKY, and vPenTest, MSPs can proactively reduce risk across client environments. These capabilities are reinforced by **Kaseya SIEM** for centralized visibility, MDR for 24/7 monitoring and response, and SaaS Alerts to secure cloud applications. Together with Compliance Manager, partners can now offer customers an integrated security practice that covers their entire attack surface.


MSPs standardizing Kaseya can deliver enterprise-grade security at competitive prices, improving retention and standing out from fragmented competitors.


##### ******************************************************************************************************************************What’s Next for Kaseya Partners******************************************************************************************************************************


Looking ahead, Kaseya is focused on advancing its platform through AI-driven innovation.


“ **[Kaseya Intelligence](https://www.kaseya.com/platform/intelligence/)** is designed to turn operational data into autonomous action, surfacing insights, recommending next steps, and executing workflows without requiring manual intervention.” Hardy shared.


As the foundation of Kaseya’s agentic IT management approach, this represents a shift from reactive to proactive operations, with AI handling pattern recognition, anomaly detection, and routine tasks.


Kaseya also continues to invest in deeper integrations and automation across the platform, while increasing transparency into its roadmap. At the same time, its growing[community](https://www.kaseya.com/kaseya-community/) gives partners a direct role in shaping future innovation, ensuring the platform evolves alongside real MSP needs.
