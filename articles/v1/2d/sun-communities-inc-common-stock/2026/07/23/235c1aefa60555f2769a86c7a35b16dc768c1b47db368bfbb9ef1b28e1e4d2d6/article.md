# Sun Communities, Inc. Announces Date for Second Quarter 2026 Earnings Release and Conference Call

**Southfield, MI, July 20, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Sun Communities, Inc. (NYSE: SUI)** (the “Company”), a real estate investment trust ("REIT") that owns and operates, or has an interest in, manufactured housing (“MH”) and recreational vehicle (“RV”) communities (collectively, the "properties"), announces it will release second quarter 2026 operating results after the market closes on Monday, July 27, 2026. The Company will host a conference call to discuss these results on Tuesday, July 28, 2026, at 11:00 A.M. ET.
