# 2 Best Trigify Alternatives for 2026

Trigify is a great fit **if you already own enrichment and outreach tools and only need a feed of raw social signals.**


But you're probably here because you want something more all-encompassing (and for a reasonable price point for what you get.)


Below is a quick look at how Trigify stacks up against a few other intent tools out there. Use it as a shortcut to decide which tool would really move the needle for you.


## How Trigify Works


Trigify is a social signal monitoring platform built around professional network activity: profile interaction tracking, keyword-based alerts on posts and comments, and account-level monitoring for things like role changes and repeat interaction with competitor content.


It's a solid fit for teams that already have enrichment and outreach tooling in place and mainly want the raw signal layer underneath.


Trigify


Where it asks more of you: keyword matching surfaces any post containing the term rather than filtering for actual intent, and turning a signal into a booked meeting still requires you to build the qualification and outreach steps yourself.

## #2 Clay's "Professional Posts"


Clay’s new “Professional Posts” feature is another alternative (or complement) to Trigify.


It now lets you **ingest any social post’s likes and comments and push that into Clay.**


It does come with some limitations, though:


- You're limited to 5 keywords
- No CRM integration
- Can't operate automatically in the background
- Can't push to outbound systems easily (needs manual setup)
- Requires an expensive agency to set up
