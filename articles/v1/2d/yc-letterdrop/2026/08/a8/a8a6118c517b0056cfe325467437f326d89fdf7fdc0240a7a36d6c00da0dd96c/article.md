# G2 Intent Data Alternatives

G2 Buyer Intent captures first-party behavioral signals from inside the G2 marketplace; someone viewing your product profile, comparing you to a competitor, or checking your pricing page.


It rolls that activity into account-level Buying Stage and Activity Level scores that flow into CRM and ABM tools like Salesforce and HubSpot.


**Quick take:**


- **Strength:** high-fidelity, first-party signal - someone comparing you to a named competitor on G2 is a much stronger indicator than inferred third-party topic interest.
- **Limits:** it's account-level (no contact name), G2-only (nothing outside the marketplace), and a data feed rather than a full platform — no built-in enrichment or outreach.
- **Pricing:** not public; teams typically report $20K–$60K/year depending on category coverage.


That narrowness is why most GTM teams look at G2 alongside - or instead of - other options. Here's how the main alternatives compare.

### 2. Bombora


**Best feature:** the broadest topic coverage in the category, pulling intent from a co-op of over 17,000 B2B publishers - strong for early-funnel, category-wide visibility.


It's third-party and topic-level, so you're seeing who's *reading about* a topic, not who's comparing vendors. Company names arrive with no contacts attached, so enrichment is still a separate step.


###
3. 6sense


**Best feature:** enterprise-grade predictive scoring layered on third-party intent, website de-anonymization, and full ABM orchestration - advertising, sequencing, and deep Salesforce-native workflows in one platform.


Six-figure contracts are standard, and teams under a few hundred employees often find it more than they can operationalize - without the headcount to act on the intelligence, you've paid for a dashboard nobody works.

### 4. ZoomInfo Intent


**Best feature:** consolidation. It layers Bombora's topic data on top of ZoomInfo's own contact database and first-party "Scoops" (hiring, funding, and initiative triggers), so intent and contacts live in one login.


The topic intent underneath is Bombora's, not a unique source, and it's gated behind a ZoomInfo contract - not something you can buy standalone at a lower price point.


## Which one should you use?


- **Already active on G2 and investing in a listing?** G2 Buyer Intent is a reasonable, high-fidelity add-on.
- **Need broader third-party coverage?** Bombora or ZoomInfo fit better.
- **Running genuine enterprise ABM with budget and headcount to match?** 6sense is built for that.
- **Want fewer dashboards and more contact-level leads reps can act on same-day?** That's the gap Letterdrop closes - and the only option here that skips a separate enrichment step entirely.
