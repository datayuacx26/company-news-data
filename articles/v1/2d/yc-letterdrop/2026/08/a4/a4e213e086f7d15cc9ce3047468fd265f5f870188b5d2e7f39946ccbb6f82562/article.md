# Letterdrop vs G2 for Intent Data

Both tools promise to tell you who's in-market. But they're solving different problems, built from different data, and handed to your team in a different shape.


## The Core Difference: Account Signals vs. Contact-Level Leads


G2 Buyer Intent tracks behavior inside G2's marketplace: product profile views, competitor comparisons, pricing page visits, and rolls it up to the *account* level.


You learn that "Acme Corp" is researching your category, not who at Acme Corp or what to say to them. That's a separate research and enrichment step your team still has to run.


Letterdrop delivers named, contact-level leads.


When a signal fires, Letterdrop matches it against your ICP, enriches it with a specific person's name and title, and drafts a first outreach message.

## Where the Gap Is Widest: Competitor Signals


This is where the difference matters most in practice.


G2's version of a competitor signal is a comparison-page view - someone viewed a G2 page featuring your product against a competitor's, activity confined to G2's own marketplace, rolled up to the account level.


Letterdrop's is a dedicated product built around a sharper question: who is starting a sales cycle with a specific competitor, right now?


It pulls from **public buying signals from all over the web and reports roughly 30% accuracy for correlating a signal with a live competitor sales cycle** - enough, per the company, to function as the closest thing to visibility into a competitor's pipeline without access to their CRM.

## What Each Requires From Your Team


G2 hands you a data feed. Getting from "Acme Corp viewed our pricing page" to a booked meeting still requires:


1. Identifying the right contact at the account
2. Verifying an email or phone number
3. Writing outreach - typically stitched together with a separate tool like Clay or Apollo


For lean teams, this is often where signals get lost in the noise.


Letterdrop removes those steps. Because the lead already arrives as a named contact with a drafted message, reps report review-and-send workflows under 15 minutes per lead, with no separate research phase in between.


## Do You Have to Choose?


Not necessarily. Some teams use G2 Buyer Intent as one input alongside Letterdrop's broader signal set, treating G2's comparison-page views as a high-fidelity source among several.


If you already pay for G2 and see real comparison traffic, layering Letterdrop's Competitor Monitoring on top turns those account-level flags into named, actionable leads instead of a list your reps have to research manually.
