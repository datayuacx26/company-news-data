# WhiteLab Genomics Presents In Vivo Data for AI-Designed Genomic Medicine Platform, Demonstrating Targeted Brain Delivery After IV Injection

### AI-Guided Engineering of rAAV Capsids: Overcoming Native Tropism Constraints for Precision In Vivo Gene Delivery


May 13th 2026 - 10:15AM - Boston, MA - MCEC, Room 210 ABC (2nd Floor)


Our Global Director of Drug Design Dylan Serillon (PharmD, PhD) presents the latest in vivo results from WhiteLab's ALFRED platform. This work demonstrates what a fully rational, AI-guided rAAV engineering framework can achieve when built to simultaneously optimize receptor targeting, functional performance, and manufacturability rather than optimizing each in isolation.


The data reveals how structural and sequence-level multimodal modeling translates into measurable gains in tissue selectivity, transduction efficiency, and bioproduction scalability.
