# Exploring Quebec’s Highly Innovative Ecosystem: A Promising Business Mission

At the end of September, the « Mission entreprises » was jointly organized by the Île-de-France Region and Business France in Quebec. This initiative provided Whitelab with a valuable opportunity to explore the Quebec ecosystem, renowned for its cutting-edge innovations, particularly in the fields of artificial intelligence and quantum computing. This highly enriching expedition also allowed David, WhiteLab Genomics' CEO, to engage with other entrepreneurs on the challenges and development opportunities in North America.


The primary goal of this journey was to create business opportunities for leading innovators in the Île-de-France region and to enhance their international visibility, leveraging the partnership agreements established between the Île-de-France Region and Quebec.


We look forward to seeing how these new connections and partnerships will evolve in the future, thereby opening up exciting new prospects for our company on the international stage.
