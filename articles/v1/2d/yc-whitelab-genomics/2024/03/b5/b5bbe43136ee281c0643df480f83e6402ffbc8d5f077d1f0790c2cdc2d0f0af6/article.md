# WhiteLab Genomics is a laureate of the first French Tech Health20 class!

Our goal? To further develop solutions related to France’s main health, technological, and industrial sovereignty issues, particularly in the field of biotherapies et bioproduction. It will give us access to:


- Personalized support from the relevant State services – via the network of “French Tech Correspondents” present in more than 60 administrations and public services
- Specific support on regulatory issues and the legal framework
- An integration into certain economic diplomacy actions, opportunities, particularly in terms of visibility – in France and internationally
- Experience-sharing events on various issues: financing, recruitment, parity, inclusion and diversity, regulatory issues, international, ecological transition, external growth.


For this first promotion, the selection criteria focused on territorial anchoring, economic maturity and the alignment of our solutions with the major priorities of the France20230 plan on the “Health Innovation 2030″ component.
