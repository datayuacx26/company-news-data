# Here's how your business should be hosting videos

## Where should you host product and training video?


There's no single right answer, because the answer depends on who's watching and what you need to prove. Start with the embed target and the audience, then let the analytics requirement break the tie. Here are the five options most teams actually choose between.


**A dedicated video host** built for business, such as Wistia or Vimeo, gives you access controls, clean embeds, and engagement analytics down to the per-viewer level on higher tiers. This is the default for marketing and customer-education teams who care about drop-off and want video that doesn't drag their brand next to a competitor's ad. The cost climbs with bandwidth or seats, so it can get expensive at volume.


**YouTube** is free, handles unlimited bandwidth, and its analytics are better than people expect: you get an audience-retention curve, average view duration, and traffic sources. The trade-offs are real, though. You can't gate access properly, unlisted isn't the same as private, and every embed is one recommendation away from pulling your viewer somewhere else.


**An**[LMS](https://www.clueso.io/solutions/best-learning-management-systems) such as TalentLMS, Docebo, or your company's existing learning platform is the right home when training completion has to be tracked and attributed to a named learner. The video isn't the point here; the record of who completed it is — the input to any real[time-to-competency](https://www.clueso.io/blog/reduce-time-to-competency-with-video-training) measure. LMS-native video analytics are usually thin, but the platform ties viewing to a user, a course, and often a quiz score.


**Hosting inside the creation tool** you already use to make the video removes a step. You record, edit, publish, and embed from one place, and you skip the export-upload-reconfigure loop every time content changes. Clueso works this way. You[record a workflow](https://www.clueso.io/features/screen-recorder) , generate an[AI voiceover](https://www.clueso.io/features/ai-voiceovers) and a matching[step-by-step guide](https://www.clueso.io/features/help-articles) , then[host and embed the video](https://www.clueso.io/features/embed-videos-anywhere) with basic view and drop-off analytics, without moving files into a separate host. The analytics are lighter than a dedicated host's, so pair it with an LMS when you need per-learner completion records.


**A general help center or CMS** with a native video block (Zendesk, Intercom, or your docs platform) is fine for support content where the goal is deflection, not measurement — the[video knowledge base](https://www.clueso.io/blog/video-knowledge-base) pattern. You'll usually get play counts and little else, which is acceptable when the video sits beside a written guide that carries the detail.
