# How to turn release notes into product launch videos

## A repeatable workflow: release write-up to video


The point of a workflow is that it runs the same way every time, so the decision "does this ship get a video" stops being a debate. Here's the version that would hold up across a busy release calendar.


1.


**Start from the release note or Jira ticket.** Grab the summary, the customer-facing benefit, and any screenshots already attached. Don't rewrite from scratch — edit down what's there.


2.


**Cut a 45-to-75-second script.** One hook line, one or two benefit lines, one line on what changed visually, one close with the next step. Read it out loud; if it runs past 90 seconds spoken, cut it.


3.


**Choose two or three visuals.** For a launch video, a screenshot of the new UI with a text callout beats a full recording. Pick the frames that show the change, not the whole app.


4.


**Generate the video with narration.** Turn the script and screenshots into a motion-graphic video with an AI voiceover, so you're not booking a recording session for a 45-second clip.


5.


**Publish to the channels that already exist.** The release email, the social post, the in-app banner. Same asset, resized where needed — we break down[where to announce SaaS product updates](https://www.clueso.io/blog/announce-saas-product-updates) channel by channel.


Clueso can take a release write-up and a few screenshots and produce a motion-graphic launch video with an AI voiceover, and it can pull the source material from tools like Jira through its integrations, so the video draft starts from the ticket instead of a blank page. The pillar here is speed without a video team: a[product marketer](https://www.clueso.io/blog/videos-for-product-marketing-teams) can ship the launch video the same afternoon the feature goes live, without waiting on editing or a recording booth.


The workflow also compounds. Once the first three launch videos share a look — same intro frame, same caption style, same voiceover — the fourth one is mostly assembly.
