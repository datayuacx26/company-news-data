# How to scale product demo video production without a video team

## What does a workflow that scales look like?


A pipeline that keeps up has three stages, and only one of them needs you paying close attention.


First, record. Capture the screen while you talk through what you're doing, in one take, mistakes and all. Don't stop to fix anything. The goal is raw material, not a finished cut.


Second, enhance. This is the stage a tool should own, not you. Software transcribes what you said, strips the filler words and dead air, generates a clean AI voiceover from your script if you don't want to use your own audio, adds captions, and drops in your standard intro and outro. You walk back in to a draft that's roughly 95% done.


Third, publish. You read the draft, fix the one label that's wrong, tighten a sentence, and export to video plus a written step-by-step guide from the same recording. The last-mile edits are the only place your judgment is required, and that's the whole idea.


This is the workflow[Clueso](https://www.clueso.io/) is built around. You record a screen recording, it produces the enhanced draft with AI voiceover and captions, and you publish both a video and a matching step-by-step guide without opening a separate editor. The pitch isn't that it makes prettier videos. It's that it moves the tedious middle stage off your plate so a single person can run the pipeline.
