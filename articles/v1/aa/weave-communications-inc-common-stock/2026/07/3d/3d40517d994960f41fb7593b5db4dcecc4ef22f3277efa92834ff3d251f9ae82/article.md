# Weave Ranks No. 1 in G2 Summer 2026 Patient Relationship Management Grid Report

*Driven by verified reviews, Weave wins on secure communications and patient experience management*


**LEHI, Utah (July 8, 2026) —**[Weave (NYSE: WEAV)](https://www.getweave.com/) , a leading AI-powered patient engagement and payments platform purpose-built for healthcare practices, has been named the


[No. 1 Leader in G2’s Summer 2026 Grid® Report for Patient Relationship Management (PRM) software](https://www.g2.com/reports/95fc2206-fbd0-444c-b832-683df39389b0/preview) , earning the highest satisfaction score and largest market presence of any product in the category.


“Being named the No. 1 PRM platform in G2’s Summer report reflects the trust our customers place in us every day,” said Brett White, Chief Executive Officer of Weave. “Our vision is to deliver a better healthcare experience at every practice, for every patient, with every interaction. These results — driven entirely by verified customer feedback — affirm that we’re realizing that vision and making a real difference for the practices we serve.”


**Where Weave Wins According to Customers**


Weave’s top ranking is driven by a combination of satisfaction ratings and standout feature scores, including:


- Secure Communications (96%)


- Patient Experience Management (95%)


- Ease of Administration (94%)


- Ease of Use (94%)


- Team Management (92%)


Weave earned its Leader designation through G2’s dual-axis methodology, which scores products on customer satisfaction (derived from verified user reviews) and market presence (based on market share, seller size, and social impact). The Summer 2026 Report reflects reviews collected through April 28, 2026.


“Only about 10% of all vendors on G2 typically earn a spot in our quarterly Market Reports—making Weave’s recognition a true accomplishment,” said Eric Gilpin, President, GTM at G2. “This honor reflects the strong satisfaction of Weave’s customers and the trust buyers place in G2’s data-driven methodology.”


Beyond its No. 1 ranking in the overall PRM Grid Report, Weave holds Leader status across multiple adjacent G2 categories, including Patient Intake, Patient Engagement, Patient Scheduling, HIPAA Compliant Messaging, Optometry, Veterinary Practice Management, and AI Patient Engagement & Operations.
