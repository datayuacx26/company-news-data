# SLOs With Prometheus Done Wrong, Wrong, Wrong, Wrong Then Right


This talk was presented at Cloud Native Rejekts 2023 in Chicago, IL. It is a breakdown of the process we went through at Weave to implement automatic Service Level Objectives at Weave.


It details where our journey with SLOs began and the problems we came across. It also points out many of the mistakes we made and how others can avoid them.
