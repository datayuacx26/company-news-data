# Why Now Is the Time to Digitize HELOC and HELOAN Closings

Industry Trends at a Glance:


- Refinance applications are up more than 80% as rates hit their lowest point in 11 months.1
- Refi originations are projected to reach $502B in 2025 and nearly $700B in 2026.2
- Homeowners still hold over $35 trillion in tappable equity3, and while equity fell $374B4 YoY, lenders have an opportunity to capture more of this volume.
- MISMO released the[SMART Doc® V3 eHELOC Specification](https://www.mba.org/news-and-research/newsroom/news/2025/06/05/mismo-creates-standard-for-electronic-helocs) , signaling growing ecosystem readiness for digital home-equity lending.


### A Shifting Market Creates Both Opportunity, and Operational Pressure


U.S. homeowners hold almost $35 trillion in equity3—and they’re already tapping into it. While average available equity for homeowners with mortgages fell 2.2% in Q34 , the average homeowner still retains nearly $300K in available equity, presenting lenders with an opportunity to help borrowers access the cash they need through flexible, lower-interest home equity products.


With mortgage rates still above historic lows, most borrowers are reluctant to refinance their first mortgages. Instead, they’re turning to home equity lines of credit (HELOCs) and home equity loans (HELOANs) as cost-effective ways to borrow cash. The latest Household Debt and Credit Report from the Federal Reserve Bank of New York confirms the trend: home equity borrowing, especially via HELOCs, is on the rise.2


For lenders, this shift is a clear opportunity to grow loan volume while meeting evolving borrower needs. Historically, these second mortgages have been portfolio products, limited in part by secondary market transferability. But that’s beginning to change.


In response to growing demand, MISMO released the[SMART Doc® V3 eHELOC Specification](https://www.mba.org/news-and-research/newsroom/news/2025/06/05/mismo-creates-standard-for-electronic-helocs) for public comment. This standard enables verifiable data to travel with the PDF—improving document reliability, consistency, and investor trust of digital HELOC documents. As MBA’s Vice President of Industry Technology Richard Hill explained, this specification will help lenders and investors “gain efficiencies and trust while originating, buying, and servicing HELOCs.”


With borrower demand climbing, industry standards advancing, and investor interest increasing, the time is now to modernize home-equity operations, starting with the closing process.


### Understanding HELOCs vs. HELOANs


HELOCs and HELOANs are often referred to as second mortgages, but they serve different needs.


HELOCs


work like a credit card secured by home equity. Borrowers can draw from a line of credit as needed, typically over a 10-year period, and repay the balance over time. The available credit replenishes as the borrower pays down their balance, offering flexibility for ongoing or unpredictable expenses.


HELOANs


provide a one-time lump sum with a fixed interest rate and structured repayment schedule, making them ideal for planned, larger expenses like home renovations or debt consolidation.


Both products allow homeowners to borrow against their home without impacting their first mortgage, making them increasingly popular in a high-rate environment.
