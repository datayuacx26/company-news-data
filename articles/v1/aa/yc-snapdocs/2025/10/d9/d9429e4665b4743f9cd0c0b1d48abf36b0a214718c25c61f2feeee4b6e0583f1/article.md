##
CD Balancing


CD balancing is the last critical step before the closing appointment. Lenders and title companies must reconcile every fee down to the penny. Today, this often means over an hour of manual “stare and compare” per loan—pulling documents, re-entering fees, and chasing corrections over email. This manual process creates a high risk for errors that can lead to TRID violations, delayed closings, and higher labor costs from closer overtime as teams try to remediate errors while simultaneously maintaining loan throughput.


What Snapdocs delivers


Snapdocs CD Balancing transforms this step with AI that instantly compares disclosures, flags discrepancies, and syncs updates directly with the LOS. No more rekeying or endless back-and-forth.


AI at work


With Snapdocs’ AI-powered CD Balancing technology, teams can focus on the few anomalies that truly need attention—driving faster, more transparent closings, while lowering back-office costs and compliance risk.

##
Funding QC


Funding a loan can’t occur until every error in the package is resolved. Yet 20–25% of loan files have issues like missing signatures, stamps, or incomplete pages1. Manual reviews take time, cost money, and often miss mistakes—delaying funding and disappointing borrowers.


### What Snapdocs delivers


[Snapdocs Funding QC](https://www.snapdocs.com/quality-control#funding-qc) reviews critical-to-fund documents the moment they’re uploaded. The platform automatically checks for package completeness and verifies the presence of all required signatures, dates, initials, and stamps, eliminating time consuming manual reviews and accelerating the release of funds to borrowers.


AI at work


The system combines rules-based automation, pattern recognition AI, and generative AI to:


- Detect and locate visual elements on documents
- Interpret and classify text using Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Handle complex tasks with Large Language Models (LLMs), complementing rule-based and traditional ML approaches


This result is QC reports that are generated in under two minutes, with 99%+ accuracy in catching missing fields and pages, and a 99% reduction in documents staff must review. These efficiency gains save $25 per loan and recapture 10–15 minutes of staff time.

## Post-Close QC


The post-close review process is time-intensive, often requiring 30–60 minutes per loan1. Manual reviews still miss issues, leading to errors occurring on 85-90% of files. These errors delay delivery, drive up labor costs, and risk investor rejection.


### What Snapdocs delivers


[Snapdocs Post-Close QC](https://www.snapdocs.com/quality-control#post-close-qc) scans the entire loan file, indexes documents into eFolders, validates wet-signed pages, and checks critical forms like the Note, Mortgage, and CD. The system continuously improves by learning from over 2 million new pages processed each month.


AI at work
