# The fathers of NFE are positively shaping lives at home and at work

**“Always believe in yourself and never let fear stop you from chasing your goals. Be kind to people, work hard, and remember that your character and how you treat others will always matter more than anything else.”**


As an LNG Offshore Shift Supervisor supporting offshore LNG operations in Altamira, Mexico, Alex Verret brings more than 20 years of offshore experience to his role. From overseeing control room and field operations to supporting emergency response and production optimization, Alex understands the importance of leadership, accountability, and teamwork.


For Alex, Father’s Day is an opportunity to reflect on both the privilege and responsibility of being a father. “Working offshore means spending time away from my family, so moments with my wife and two kids are something I never take for granted,” he says.


Alex credits much of his approach to fatherhood to his own father, who taught him the value of hard work, integrity, and showing up for the people who matter most. “Watching the way he handled responsibilities, treated people, and stayed committed to our family helped shape the kind of husband, father, and leader I strive to be today.”
