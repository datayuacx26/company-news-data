# More than electricity: how energy access helps power communities with positive energy

Around the world, access to dependable energy supports schools, technology, and learning environments that allow students to reach their full potential. At New Fortress Energy, this commitment extends beyond energy infrastructure through partnerships and programs that help strengthen educational opportunities in the communities where we operate.


Across the communities where NFE operates, we partner with schools, nonprofits, and local organizations to expand access to educational opportunities and help empower the next generation. By providing students with access to books, computers, technology, and enrichment programs, we strive to make learning more engaging, accessible, and inclusive. From robotics and computer literacy programs to internships and STEM initiatives, these partnerships help equip young people with the knowledge, skills, and confidence they need to pursue their goals and succeed in an increasingly connected world.


NFE's commitment to education also includes creating pathways to long-term career opportunities. Through internship and workforce development programs, students gain hands-on experience and exposure to the energy industry, with many participants advancing into full-time roles across our corporate offices and terminal operations. By investing in local talent, we help build a skilled workforce while supporting the long-term success of the communities we serve.
