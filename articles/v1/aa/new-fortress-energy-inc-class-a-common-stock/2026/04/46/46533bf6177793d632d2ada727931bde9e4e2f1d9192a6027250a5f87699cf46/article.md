# Matthew LaVergne leads with purpose, and people first

Today, Matthew serves as an Offshore Installation Manager at NFE’s FLNG1 offshore facility in Altamira, Mexico. In this role, he is responsible for the safe and efficient operation of the facility, overseeing everything from process safety and asset performance to regulatory compliance. He leads emergency response efforts and ensures preparedness in high-risk environments. Beyond operations, he fosters a culture rooted in safety, accountability, and operational excellence.


What Matthew values most about New Fortress Energy is its commitment to empowering those on the front lines. “NFE’s’ empowerment not only strengthens operational results but also builds a more cohesive and high-performing team” Matthew shares. By trusting site-based teams to make decisions and contribute their expertise, the company creates a culture of ownership and continuous improvement. For Matthew, this approach strengthens both performance and team cohesion.


At the core of everything Matthew does is a simple but powerful source of energy: seeing others succeed. Whether it’s his team operating safely and confidently offshore or his family thriving at home, those moments fuel his drive and reinforce his purpose. Through discipline and a people-first mindset, Matthew LaVergne continues to lead with impact and positive energy both on and offshore.
