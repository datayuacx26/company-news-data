# Natural gas compared to other fuels

Diesel, traditionally, has been the dominant fuel for global transportation, backup power generation, and remote or off-grid applications, but natural gas is increasingly favored for stationary energy use. Natural gas results in fewer carbon dioxide emissions per unit of energy and significantly fewer air pollutants, making it a cleaner and lower-emission alternative.


From an operational standpoint, natural gas systems, particularly in power generation, offer greater efficiency and flexibility than diesel-based alternatives. Natural gas power plants can adjust quickly to respond to changes in electricity demand, while diesel generators are typically less efficient for continuous, large-scale operation. In regions with established pipeline infrastructure, switching from diesel to natural gas can reduce fuel costs, lower emissions, and improve overall energy efficiency, especially for industrial facilities and power generation. ****
