# María José Monteverde leads with curiosity and positive energy

Her greatest influence was her father, who shaped the foundation of how she approaches life. Each day, he would ask a simple but powerful question: “What did you learn today?” That daily challenge sparked in her a lifelong drive to dig deeper, to question, and to always seek understanding. His passing taught her another profound lesson, that time is finite, and “what remains at the end of our days is everything we did, embraced, loved, and shared,” she reflects. Today, that perspective fuels both her personal and professional journey.


Described by friends and family as responsible, proactive, and persistent, María José is powered by the desire to understand the why behind everything.


A trained biologist with a master’s degree in biological sciences, she considers herself fortunate to have built a career around something she is so passionate about. Her field allows her to continuously explore environmental law, regulatory compliance, ecosystems, sustainability, statistics, and beyond. For her, learning isn’t a task, it’s a way of life.
