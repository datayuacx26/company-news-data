# Puerto Rico approves 200 MW of additional gas conversions

Another 165 MW of conversions at Cambalache has been conditionally approved and is awaiting final authorization.


The next steps for all projects include securing U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) permits to complete the conversions and begin operations on natural gas.
