# IBS and gut-brain disorders in clinical practice

In this practical and informative webinar, Dr. Khokhar discusses:


- A simple bedside test for visceral hypersensitivity using a hot or cold drink
- Where pain perception can break down (gut, spinal cord, brain) and what that means for treatment
- How diet, the microbiome, and ultra-processed foods influence DGBI symptoms
- Practical advice on fiber, sleep hygiene, stress, medication review, and pelvic floor health
- When to consider SIBO and SIFO testing, and where the evidence stands on stool microbiome panels
- Why gut-directed hypnotherapy is increasingly a first-line option, especially for older patients.
