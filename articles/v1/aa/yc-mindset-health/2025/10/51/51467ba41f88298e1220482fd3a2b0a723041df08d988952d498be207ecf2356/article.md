# Nerva vs The Calm Gut App: What’s the Best IBS App?

## What is the best IBS hypnotherapy app? Nerva vs The Calm Gut app


When you’re looking for help managing IBS, you’ll find a range of options - from traditional treatments and dietary changes to a growing number of **apps claiming to calm your gut** . Two names that might come up are **Nerva** and **The Calm Gut app** .


Both mention gut-directed hypnotherapy (GDH), but that’s where the similarity ends.
**Nerva** is a **digital therapeutic** - a structured, clinically validated program proven to help manage IBS symptoms. **‍**


**The Calm Gut app** , on the other hand, is a **general wellness app** that offers relaxation audios inspired by hypnotherapy but without clinical testing or published evidence.


Here’s what makes that difference meaningful.


## Why evidence matters for IBS care


IBS isn’t just about stress or digestion - it’s a complex brain-gut condition. That means it responds best to interventions that retrain how the brain and gut communicate, not just ones that help you relax.


**Nerva** was built around this science. Its 6-week program combines **gut-directed hypnotherapy, CBT, and breathing exercises** to help users manage IBS symptoms and flare-ups at the root level.
In a **randomized controlled trial** published in the *American Journal of Gastroenterology1* , Nerva showed significant improvements in managing IBS symptom severity, abdominal pain, and anxiety compared to the control group.


By contrast, **The Calm Gut app** has not been evaluated in any peer-reviewed clinical trials. It offers a collection of audio tracks - helpful for general mindfulness and stress management, but not validated as an IBS program.


## How do the apps compare?


- **Clinically validation:**


- Nerva has a **randomized controlled trial** published in the *American Journal of Gastroenterology* showing significant improvements in IBS symptom severity, abdominal pain, and anxiety management.
- Beyond the RCT, Nerva has **five real-world studies** demonstrating real-world effectiveness across thousands of users.
- The Calm Gut app has **no published app-specific research** or clinical trials.


- **Evidence:**


- Nerva was developed with Dr. Simone Peters, a gut-directed psychophysiologist with her own randomized contrilled trial.
- The Calm Gut app is built around practitioner experience, not scientific validation. Lived experience is valuable, but it’s not a substitute for research.


- **Usage and reviews**


- Nerva has been used by **over 300,000 people** and recommended by **more than 15,000 healthcare providers** .
- The Calm Gut app is a great wellness app used by a few thousand people.


- **Structure:**


- Nerva follows a **6-week program** with daily sessions, psychoeducation, and breathing exercises - designed to retrain brain-gut signaling.
- The Calm Gut app is an **open audio library** with 200+ recordings, similar to content you can find on youtube for free. Users can listen freely, but there’s no defined therapeutic structure or progression.


- **Clinical integration**


- Nerva provides provider discounts, clinician dashboards, research summaries, referral workflows, and outcomes tracking.
- The Calm Gut app offers a practitioner discount but no integration tools or outcome data.


- **Access and trial period**


- Nerva is on iOS and Android while The Calm Gut App is also on web
- Both programs offer a 7-day free trial


## Why it matters


Whether you’re a **patient** looking for real relief or a **clinician** recommending care, the question is simple:
If you’re going to invest your time, effort, and money in a program, you want to know it works.


If you’re just looking for an app to help you relax, unwind, or explore mindfulness, **The Calm Gut app** can be a nice way to do that. It offers flexibility, calming audios, and a gentle introduction to mind-body concepts.


But if you’re looking for something that’s been clinically proven to help manage and live well with IBS symptoms - not just help you feel calm in the moment - that’s where Nerva stands apart.


Nerva has been evaluated in a randomized controlled trial, supported by five published real-world studies, and used by hundreds of thousands of people under the guidance of thousands of healthcare providers. It’s not just soothing - it’s structured, evidence-based, and built to work.


Because when it comes to IBS, calm isn’t enough.
It needs to be clinically effective.
