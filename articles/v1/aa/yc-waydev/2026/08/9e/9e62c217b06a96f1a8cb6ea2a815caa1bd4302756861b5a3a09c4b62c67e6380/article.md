## Why teams move off LinearB


LinearB is a capable product with strong marketing. The teams that leave are not leaving because it is bad. They are leaving because of a mismatch between what they bought and what they needed. These are the four patterns we hear most often from switchers.


### 1.


You bought analytics, you got automation


LinearB’s center of gravity is gitStream, its PR automation engine. Automation is useful, but it is not visibility. Teams that need commit-level work patterns, individual and team deep dives, and granular Git analytics find the analytical layer thinner than expected. Waydev is Git-first by design, backed by a USPTO patent in Git analytics.


### 2.


AI measurement became the board question


Your board is not asking about PR routing rules. It is asking what the AI spend is returning. Waydev was rebuilt around exactly this: AI adoption, impact, and ROI measured across your engineering org, connected to delivery outcomes, down to the spend. One platform, one answer, ready for the board deck.


### 3.


Dashboards when you needed answers


Configuring dashboards, tuning automations, and interpreting charts is work someone on your team owns forever. Waydev AI is conversational. Ask “which teams improved cycle time this quarter and why” and get a data-backed answer in seconds. No dashboard archaeology, no dedicated platform owner.


### 4.


Renewal pricing stopped matching value


If usage settled into a handful of reports while the invoice kept growing, you are paying platform prices for report value. Renewal is the one moment you have leverage. Use it to re-evaluate, even if you end up staying.

## Frequently asked questions


### Do we lose our historical metrics if we leave LinearB?


No. Your source data lives in your Git provider, not in LinearB. Waydev processes your full commit and PR history on connection, so cycle time trends, DORA baselines, and year-over-year comparisons rebuild from source.


### Will the numbers match what LinearB showed us?


Directionally yes, exactly no. Every platform defines metric boundaries slightly differently, for example where cycle time starts and ends. During onboarding we validate definitions against your LinearB baselines so leadership understands any deltas before the switch, not after.


### What happens to our gitStream automations?


They do not transfer. Audit which ones your team actually relies on. Alerting use cases move to Signals with no rule maintenance. Routing and approval policies usually belong in native GitHub or GitLab settings, where they survive any vendor change.


### Can we run both platforms during evaluation?


Yes, and you should. Both are read-only against your Git provider, so they coexist with no conflicts. A 30-day parallel run on real repos is the strongest evaluation you can do.


### What about security and compliance?


Waydev is SOC 2 certified and never stores your source code. Full security documentation is available for CISO review on request.


### We measure engineers in Europe. Can we deploy this?


Yes. Role-based access and anonymization controls are built in. Individual-level data can be restricted or anonymized while leadership keeps org-level visibility. Works councils and legal teams have approved this model at enterprise scale.
