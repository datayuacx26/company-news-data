# Announcing The WAY Framework

**Today we are announcing “THE WAY FRAMEWORK”**


Cortex published DRIVE. LinearB published APEX. Uplevel published WAVE. Each one comes with a guide, a maturity assessment, and a platform that happens to be the only one built to run it.


**Today we are announcing ours. Sort of.**


It is called[The WAY Framework](https://waydev.co/way-framework/) , and it is built on one decision that makes it different from everything else on that list: **it owns nothing.** No proprietary metrics. No composite index. No acronym-shaped pillars competing with the research. To explain why we built it this way, I have to tell you how we got here, because we watched the whole story happen.


Informed by data


## Twelve years of engineering measurement, in one timeline


2014 · DORA


The DevOps Research and Assessment program turns engineering measurement into a science. Four keys, tens of thousands of survey responses, and the landmark finding: **speed and stability are not a trade-off.** Elite teams get both from the same capabilities.


2017 · WAYDEV SHIPS


We launch one of the first Git analytics platforms in the world, built on the conviction that the truth about engineering lives in the work itself: commits, pull requests, reviews. We later patent the approach with the USPTO. Most of today’s vendors do not exist yet.


2021 · SPACE


The field’s course correction. The four keys were being abused as individual scorecards, so many of the same researchers publish SPACE: productivity is multidimensional, mix system data with perceptual data, and never let one metric stand alone, because **any measure that becomes a target stops being a good measure.**


2024 · DX CORE 4


DX compresses a decade of research into four dimensions with one headline metric each, developed with names from the DORA and SPACE lineage. It travels fast. Leaders adopt the vocabulary. There is one catch buried inside: the Effectiveness centerpiece, the DXI, is proprietary.


2025 · THE BILLION-DOLLAR SIGNAL


Atlassian acquires DX for one billion dollars. The market draws one conclusion: **the framework is the moat. Own the language leaders think in, and you own the shortlist.**


2026 · THE ACRONYM ARMS RACE


Within months, everyone has one. Cortex ships DRIVE with a book and a conference. LinearB ships APEX with an operating cadence. Uplevel ships WAVE with a diagnostic. Each is thoughtful. Each also maps, pillar by pillar, onto its author’s product.


2026 · THE WAY FRAMEWORK


We take the opposite position. Combine the best of the research under one roof, keep it current as the science evolves, and **never lock your organization’s vocabulary to our product.**


The pattern nobody says out loud


## Every vendor framework is a mirror of its vendor


Cortex sells a service catalog, and DRIVE is a governance framework whose metrics need a catalog. LinearB sells PR instrumentation, and APEX is a PR-centric flow framework. Uplevel sells calendar-and-survey insight, and WAVE requires exactly that instrumentation. DX sold a survey platform, and Core 4’s centerpiece is a proprietary survey index.


None of these frameworks is dishonest. Smart people built them, and each contains ideas worth stealing: DRIVE’s operational review ritual, APEX’s PR-level AI attribution, WAVE’s warning that AI inflates activity metrics while delivered value stays flat. Steal all of it. It is free.


What is not free is adopting a vendor’s framework as your company’s official definition of engineering performance. Your targets, your board narrative, and your managers’ vocabulary get expressed in terms only one platform natively speaks. **Data lock-in is a project. Vocabulary lock-in is a culture change.**


“A framework you cannot take with you when you leave the vendor was never a framework. It was an onboarding flow.”


What WAY actually is


## Three commitments: Work-first, Agnostic, Yours


WAY is a meta-framework: a set of commitments about how measurement should be built, distilled from nine years of doing this. **It is the only framework in this market you could, in principle, implement without us.** We simply intend to be the best place to run it.


W


### Work-first


Every framework in this market is a projection of the same underlying reality: commits, pull requests, reviews, deployments, and the spend around them. We measure at the source, continuously, at the commit and PR level. **Not calendars. Not self-reported time savings. Not catalog metadata.** Get the source right and every projection becomes available.


A


### Agnostic


WAY combines the best of the field instead of replacing it. Waydev implements **DORA and DORA Core with benchmarks, SPACE-consistent views, Core 4-style balanced scorecards,** and a full AI measurement layer: adoption, impact, and ROI per tool, per team, down to the spend. The ideas were never the lock-in. The instrumentation was.


Y


### Yours


Your metric definitions stay industry-standard and portable. Your history rebuilds directly from your Git provider, so even changing platforms costs no baselines. Your framework choice stays reversible. **Your organization’s language for engineering performance belongs to your organization.**


What this means for you
