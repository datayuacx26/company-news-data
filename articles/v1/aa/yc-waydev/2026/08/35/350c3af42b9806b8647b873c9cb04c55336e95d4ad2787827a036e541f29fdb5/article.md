## What to do before you evaluate any platform


Four decisions shape your evaluation more than any feature chart. Settle them before you take a single demo.


### 1.


Secure your historical data


Flow holds years of your engineering history: cycle time trends, review patterns, commit-level baselines. Export everything before your account closes. Then ask every vendor one question: can you ingest this, or do we start from zero?


### 2.


Map the processes built on top of Flow


The tool is not the problem. The workflows are. Weekly team reviews. Executive dashboards for the CTO. Automated stakeholder reports. List them all. You are replacing processes, not a login.


### 3.


Find your real deadline


Contract end is not your deadline. If leadership reviews engineering metrics monthly, your deadline is the next leadership meeting. If managers use the data weekly, it is sooner. Know what you are racing against.


### 4.


Know who signs off and what they care about


Your VP of Engineering wants proof at scale. Your CISO wants security documentation. Your CFO wants budget continuity. Your managers want zero disruption. Talk to them before you pick a winner, not after.

## Running a focused four-week evaluation


You do not have six months for an RFP. You do not need it. Four weeks gives you enough signal to decide with confidence.


WEEK 1


### Shortlist and requirements


Pick two or three platforms based on your mapped processes and stakeholder concerns. Decline the rest.


WEEK 2


### Data-first demos


Ask each vendor to connect to a real repository and show your data in their platform. One ask, more signal than an hour of slides.


WEEK 3


### Migration clarity


Get specifics. Onboarding timeline. Historical data handling. Who owns the migration and what their SLA is.


WEEK 4


### Decision and business case


Choose. Use the memo template below to fast-track sign-off with your VP and finance stakeholders.


### The five criteria that matter in a forced migration


- **Data continuity:** do you keep your history or start from zero?
- **Time to first value:** how fast does the team see useful data after connecting?
- **Process coverage:** does it replace what Flow covered and the workflows built on top?
- **Migration support:** structured onboarding or figure-it-out-yourself?
- **Net gain:** is this a lateral move or an upgrade? Make it an upgrade.
