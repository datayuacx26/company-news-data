# Every Chair is a Revenue Opportunity: Maximizing Revenue and Reducing Risk with Agilysys OnDemand Full Service

BLOGS


July 22, 2026


David Bain


Sales Engineer


[Connect with David in Linkedin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dmbain/)


Pool decks, beaches, cabanas, and outdoor venues often create unique operational challenges. Guests are often ready to order long before a server is available for their next visit. Every minute spent waiting is a potential missed revenue opportunity.
With **Agilysys OnDemand Full Service** , guests can place additional orders the moment they are ready—whether that’s another cocktail, appetizer, dessert, or round of drinks—without waiting for a server to return. Orders flow directly into the InfoGenesis POS ecosystem, helping properties capture incremental revenue that might otherwise be missed during busy service periods.


#### **Agilysys OnDemand Full Service helps minimize these risks through flexible pre-authorization capabilities that can be initiated by either the guest or a service team member.**


Guests can securely authorize a payment method before ordering, creating a frictionless experience that allows them to continue ordering throughout their stay without repeatedly presenting a credit card. Alternatively, staff can establish a pre-authorized tab as part of the service experience, allowing operators to maintain traditional high-touch hospitality while protecting revenue.


Menus, pricing, and service workflows remain centrally managed through InfoGenesis, helping operators maintain control while expanding service reach.


#### **Support Staff Without Increasing Labor Costs**


Many resorts, casinos, hotels, and clubs struggle to provide adequate service coverage across large pool decks, beaches, and outdoor venues.


OnDemand Full Service allows a smaller team to effectively serve more guests.


Rather than spending valuable time walking chair-to-chair taking orders, staff can focus on:


- Delivering food and beverages
- Building guest relationships
- Managing service quality
- Upselling premium offerings


This improves labor efficiency while maintaining high service standards during peak periods.


#### **Increase Guest Satisfaction and Loyalty**


Guests today value convenience as much as quality.


When someone relaxing in a cabana can order lunch with a few taps—or a guest by the pool can request another beverage without leaving their chair—it creates a frictionless experience that feels modern and personalized.


The easier it is for guests to order, the more likely they are to purchase, return, and recommend your property to others.


#### **The Bottom Line:**


**By combining self-service ordering with flexible pre-authorization, , properties can confidently expand food and beverage service beyond traditional restaurant settings— maximizing guest spending opportunities while maintaining the high-touch service experience guests expect.**


Whether serving guests poolside, in private cabanas, on the beach, or throughout the resort, OnDemand Full Service helps operators:


✅ Capture more revenue opportunities
✅ Improve order accuracy
✅ Enhance labor efficiency
✅ Reduce operational risk
✅ Deliver exceptional guest experiences everywhere


##### **Because the best seat in the house should always be a place where guests can order—and spend—with ease.**
