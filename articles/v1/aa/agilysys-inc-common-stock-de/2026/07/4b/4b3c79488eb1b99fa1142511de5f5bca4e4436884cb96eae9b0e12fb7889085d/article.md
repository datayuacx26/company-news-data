# The Inn At Little Washington Selects Agilysys Hospitality Software Ecosystem To Strengthen Its Technology Foundation

NEWS EVENTS


July 9, 2026


*Deployment of Agilysys hospitality technology innovations spanning PMS, POS, retail and booking solutions supports refined guest experiences at the iconic Virginia destination*


**ALPHARETTA, GA (July 9, 2026)** – Agilysys, Inc. (Nasdaq: AGYS), a global hospitality-focused technology and services provider, today announced that The Inn at Little Washington, the internationally acclaimed luxury five-star hotel in Virginia’s Shenandoah Valley, has selected a comprehensive suite of Agilysys hospitality solutions to support its dining, lodging and retail operations.


For more than four decades, The Inn at Little Washington has drawn guests from across the country and around the world for its Michelin-recognized cuisine, distinctive accommodation and highly orchestrated approach to hospitality. With 24 guest rooms and suites set within a collection of historic buildings, The Inn has built a reputation for delivering immersive, artful experiences where every detail is considered.


“Transitioning our core systems across dining and lodging was an important step for The Inn,” said Vincent Ward, Assistant General Manager, from The Inn at Little Washington. “We pride ourselves on delivering world class hospitality to all our guests and wanted our technology to support that mission. Agilysys provided a unified platform that supports our team in delivering a seamless and curated experience.”


The Inn selected Agilysys Versa PMS, Agilysys Book, Agilysys Digital Marketing, Agilysys InfoGenesis POS and related solutions to create an integrated environment spanning property management, amenities and food and beverage. Rather than implementing isolated systems, the property adopted a cohesive ecosystem designed to support its standards consistently across departments.


#### **A Unified Ecosystem Aligned to Service Standards**


To support its long-term vision, the leadership at The Inn at Little Washington implemented an integrated ecosystem that operates quietly in the background while connecting core functions across the property on a single platform.


With guest profiles, reservations and dining activity connected, The Inn can now maintain continuity across stays, dining, private events and retail experiences. Teams can quickly access the details that enable attentive, personalized service, while preserving the cadence and discretion that define the property.


In the dining room, Agilysys InfoGenesis POS supports curated coursing, tableside service and preference management, helping teams manage pacing and special requests with precision. Across lodging, Agilysys Versa PMS and Agilysys Book unify reservations and guest management for The Inn’s distinctive accommodations and event-driven business, creating a coordinated framework that supports refined service with consistent operations.


#### **Sustaining Standards for the Future**


The transition to a cloud-native, integrated technology ecosystem reflects a long-term investment in sustaining standards across dining, lodging and guest engagement. As expectations continue to evolve, The Inn selected a partner capable of aligning technology with a highly intentional approach to service.


“The Inn at Little Washington represents the highest standards of culinary artistry and hospitality, and we are proud to support that vision,” said Dan Bell, vice president of sales at Agilysys. “Our role is to provide a connected technology foundation that operates seamlessly and reliably, making it easier for teams to deliver the attentive, personalized experiences guests expect.”


#### **About Agilysys, Inc.**


Agilysys delivers state-of-the-art software and services built exclusively for hospitality, helping organizations go beyond traditional property management (PMS), point-of-sale (POS), and food and beverage inventory and procurement systems. Its best-in-class solutions operate independently or as a unified ecosystem that connects data and workflows across an operation, property or enterprise. Powered by intelligent guest profiles, data-driven capabilities, and embedded artificial intelligence (AI), Agilysys is architected on a unified data model with a single set of APIs that create shared operational memory across all systems, enabling guest-specific insights to be acted on in real time at any touchpoint. Agilysys delivers advantage through pace and integration, transforming hospitality technology from a system of record into a system of action that accelerates innovation and the rapid delivery of new capabilities.
