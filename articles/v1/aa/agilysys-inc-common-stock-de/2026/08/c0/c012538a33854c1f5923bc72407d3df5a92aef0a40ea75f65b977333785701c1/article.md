# The Future of Hospitality Isn’t More Systems. It’s Smarter Ones

BLOGS


August 5, 2026


John Michael Jenkins


Director, Product Management


If you have spent time in hotel operations or sales, you have probably heard some version of this comment: “We do not have a technology problem. We have too many technology solutions.” Over time, hotels have added systems to solve specific needs. Property management. Point of sale. Sales and catering. Spa, golf, activities, payments, guest engagement. Each system plays a role, but very few were designed to work together as part of a coordinated operating model.


Many of these platforms come from different vendors, built at different times, with different assumptions about how data should move and how work should be done. The result is not a single ecosystem, but a collection of tools that require constant effort to keep aligned. Instead of simplifying execution, technology often becomes another source of friction.


#### **Why Integration Alone Falls Short**


For years, the industry believed that integration would solve this problem. If systems could pass data back and forth, teams would stay aligned. In practice, integration usually moves information without creating shared understanding. Data flows, but context does not.


Teams still spend time checking whether updates made in one system are reflected in another. Information arrives late or incomplete. Reporting explains what already happened rather than helping teams decide what to do next. The technology works, but it does not always support how hotels actually operate.


#### **What Is Changing Now**


Hospitality is entering a different phase. Group business is growing again. Experiences matter more to planners and attendees. Properties are being asked to do more with leaner teams. In that environment, the question is no longer whether to invest in technology. The question is whether the technology helps teams operate with clarity and confidence.


Adding more tools does not solve this problem. What matters is whether systems help teams understand what is happening across the property and act on it in time to make a difference.


#### **Smarter Systems Support Real Decisions**


The next generation of hospitality platforms is designed to do more than store or move data. These systems are built to understand how different parts of the operation connect. They surface insights that reflect what is happening now, not just what happened last month. They help teams answer practical questions about performance, demand, and opportunity while there is still time to respond.


When systems truly work together, information flows across the guest journey. Room data informs event planning. Event changes influence outlet preparation. Spend patterns shape future sales strategy. The operation starts to behave as a single workflow rather than a set of disconnected tasks.


For group sales and catering leaders, this creates space for more strategic selling. For general managers, it provides a clearer view of how the property is performing as a whole. For ownership, it supports better decisions about where to invest and where value is being created.
