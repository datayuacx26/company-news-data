Is “Flushable” Just A Label? Here’s What It Actually Means.


“Flushable” refers to a wipe’s ability to break down safely in water without causing harm to plumbing or wastewater systems.[Honest Flushable Wipes](https://www.honest.com/flushable-wipes/flushable-wipes.html) are engineered to break down similarly to toilet paper**, passing both the[INDA](https://www.inda.org/) (Association of the Nonwoven Fabrics Industry) flushability guidelines and the[IWSFG](https://www.iwsfg.org/) (International Water Services Flushability Group) 2020 flushability assessment, both stringent standards in the industry.


Both associations have set forth guidelines that require products labeled as 'flushable' to go through specific testing and meet specific criteria. Both IWSFG and INDA have flushability assessment tests related to performance in toilet and drain line clearance, physical disintegration, settling of wipe (assessing buoyancy), and biodisintegration (breakdown of wipe by bacteria). However, the main difference between the two is the IWSFG requirement to 'pass' for physical disintegration is considered more stringent.


Both INDA and IWSFG standards ensure our wipes are designed to disintegrate quickly and thoroughly to help minimize the risk of home plumbing and municipal system damage.

However, it’s important to distinguish between generic wipes and those designed with modern flushability standards in mind.

Flush or Toss? How to Use Flushable Wipes Safely


Using flushable wipes responsibly ensures better plumbing health and peace of mind. These tips help you get the most out of yours—and how to use and dispose of them properly:


-


Flush one wipe at a time to avoid overloading your plumbing.


-


Only flush wipes that are labeled as both flushable and compliant with INDA GD4 and/or the stricter IWSFG 2020 flushability guidelines — never mix with baby wipes, paper towels, or other non-dispersible/non-flushable items.


-


Remember: Only flush in a well maintained sewage or septic system. If you’re unsure about your plumbing system or want an extra layer of precaution, simply toss used wipes into the trash instead of flushing.


Using your wipes as directed and disposing of them properly helps keep your home and community systems running smoothly.
