# Cypherpunk Accelerates Zcash Accumulation; Increases Treasury Holdings to 290,062.67 ZEC

CAMBRIDGE, Mass.


, Dec. 30, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- **Cypherpunk Technologies Inc.** (Nasdaq: CYPH) ("Cypherpunk"), the privacy-focused technology company, today announced a significant expansion of its corporate treasury. The company has acquired an additional **56,418.09 ZEC** for approximately **$29 million** at an average price of **$514.02** per ZEC.


This latest acquisition follows a series of strategic purchases over the preceding months. With this transaction, Cypherpunk's total Zcash holdings now stand at **290,062.67 ZEC** , representing an estimated **1.76%** of the total circulating network supply. The company's total ZEC position was acquired at a cumulative average price of **$334.41** per ZEC.


"We continue to execute on our goal of accumulating 5% of the Zcash network." said **Will McEvoy, CIO of Cypherpunk** . "As our Zcash position grows, we are well positioned for a market that is repricing the societal importance of privacy. We are excited to continue executing on our Zcash strategy while expanding our work across a broader set of privacy-preserving technologies and initiatives."
