# Why Small Business Automation is the Key to Growing a Startup

Small businesses often think that automation is for larger companies. But automated solutions are simple and affordable solutions for startups. In fact,[65%](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/the-imperatives-for-automation-success) of respondents at smaller firms report success with automation, compared with 55% at large organizations.


Growing a startup means doing more with fewer resources and a[tight budget](https://www.cococart.co/resources/start-home-business-budget-low-capital) . In this post, we’ll explore how small business automation can help.


## Key areas for small business automation


So, how do you automate a small business? Let’s look at some examples of automation for critical areas of your company.


### Sales and marketing


Automation in sales and marketing enables small teams to reach as many potential customers as possible. You can automate list segmentation, use predictive dialers for outreach, and streamline lead management.


Email marketing automation covers everything from abandoned cart emails to recommendations based on customer behaviors. Marketers can target the right people using data automatically pulled in from your customer relationship management system.


### Finance and accounting


Automation tools help you handle your finances, from accounting and invoicing to payroll. Instead of spending time on manual tasks, such as accounts payable and bank reconciliation, you can let the software do the math.


This means you can automatically generate and share invoices, purchase orders, and balance sheets. Automated tools will track what you’re owed, while integrated communications software will trigger a[thank-you message](https://www.cococart.co/resources/thank-customers-build-relationship-online-business) whenever someone pays.


### Operations and administration


Automation streamlines all kinds of business operations, from inventory management to HR and customer service. Businesses with limited resources can offer excellent customer support via auto-responses, ticket assignments, and call forwarding.


This results in less time spent on admin tasks like assigning projects and coordinating them. For example, automated business intelligence and[ERP software solutions](https://www.sage.com/en-us/erp/) can share information across the entire business, giving teams a wider overview and helping different departments collaborate in real time.


By gaining accurate insight into your daily operations, you can more effectively streamline various administrative tasks and processes. Along with historical data, this will help guide your resource planning efforts, such as calculating the number of employees needed for specific periods, boosting efficiency by improving the flow of information, and identifying areas for improvement as your business grows.


## Why automation is the key to growing your startup


Now, we’ll dive into why automation is not just nice-to-have but truly necessary for growing a startup.


### Automation can increase employee productivity


The productivity boost from small business automation cannot be overstated. You can program software to complete menial tasks, freeing your team to concentrate on higher-value activities. For example, a chatbot can handle basic queries.


Automated project management tools create and assign tasks based on employee skills, while automatic document creation is a huge time-saver. Marketing automation software also allows you to schedule[social media posts](https://www.cococart.co/resources/5-instagram-content-ideas-for-your-business) in advance and accelerate content creation.


You can easily evaluate performance and identify obstacles to productivity. Since automation empowers employees to achieve more in less time, they will find their work more rewarding.


### Automation can improve accuracy and reduce costly errors


By letting smart systems handle repetitive tasks, you significantly reduce human error. Instead of inputting customer data, the tools grab it from forms, social media platforms, and emails and add it to your CRM. Accounting mistakes such as double payments are also eliminated.


Another benefit is the ability to standardize processes since automated tools don’t skip over steps or repeat themselves unnecessarily. This helps you comply with laws and regulations, as well as providing brand consistency.


### Automation can reduce costs


As well as reducing errors, automation cuts costs by improving efficiency. When tasks take less time, there’s no need for overtime or unsociable working hours. For example, instead of hiring a receptionist to work 24/7, you can use an auto assistant.


You’ll be able to increase the workload without expanding your workforce and even employ fewer staff (although we’re not suggesting replacing humans altogether). A less stressful work environment increases staff retention, which also reduces costs.


By providing better service, you’ll also boost[customer retention](https://www.cococart.co/resources/best-customer-retention-software-small-business) . Meanwhile, automated tools show you ways to make savings, such as automatically choosing the most cost-effective supplier.


### Automation can enhance decision-making


Automation solutions provide enhanced visibility into business processes. They analyze data in real time, so you can easily track the status of finances, projects, and customer satisfaction. The tools automatically generate reports on any aspect of the business.


This helps you to make informed decisions and identify areas for improvement. For example, you can see how much stock to order at specific times. You can also plan marketing campaigns using data from customer interactions and measure their effectiveness.


### Automation can improve HR and payroll


HR tasks such as job posting, candidate screening, and benefits enrollment are streamlined with automation. This can benefit your employees as time-tracking software will enable you to spot employees who are struggling and offer extra training or support, and automated staff scheduling will reduce the likelihood of missed vacation requests or shortages.


An automated payroll system captures the data when employees report their activities and hours online. It’ll calculate and subtract taxes and benefits and automatically distribute paychecks.


If your business is particularly fast-paced, it’s also handy to have an[HR mobile app](https://www.sage.com/en-gb/sage-business-cloud/hr/app/) . Having an app can help you to stay up-to-date with your business as you can see data like staff attendance. All of which can be crucial, when it comes to monitoring the health of your business, service, and staff-well being.


### Automation helps SMEs compete with large companies


Automation enables small businesses to make the most of their data and focus on growth instead of getting bogged down by manual processes. This way, it levels the playing field to help you compete with large companies.


For example, you can scale your output without hiring more staff. It reduces costs by improving efficiency and tracking finances, meaning you’ll have more to invest. The ability to analyze customer feedback and provide fast responses also means you can nail customer service and foster loyalty.


### Automation can help adapt to changing market conditions


Automation software makes it easy to keep a close eye on changing market conditions. It finds patterns in data and identifies trends, which helps you to set fair prices, make accurate demand forecasts, and choose reliable suppliers.


You can also conduct a competitor analysis and use[AI-powered automation](https://www.cococart.co/resources/use-ai-small-business-practical-ways) to find out what your customers really want. For instance, social listening and sentiment analysis tools give you deep insights into audience needs and preferences.


## Final Thoughts


Your startup needs automation in order to grow and compete with the big players. Powerful tools are accessible to everyone, and they’ll drastically reduce your workload by handling tedious day-to-day tasks.


As well as saving valuable time and money, small business automation tools help you deliver a great customer experience. What’s not to love?
