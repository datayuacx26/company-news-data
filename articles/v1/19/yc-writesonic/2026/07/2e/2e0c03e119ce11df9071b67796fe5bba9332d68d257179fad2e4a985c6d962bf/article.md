## What GEO and AEO actually mean


**SEO** is how your pages rank in traditional search results. **GEO** and **AEO** are two names for the same job: making sure answer engines find, understand, cite, and recommend your brand inside generated answers. Teams need both, because AI answers still pull heavily from searchable web sources.


## What matters before you choose a tool


Third-party coverage shapes a large share of AI citations.[Muck Rack's May 2026 study](https://muckrack.com/blog/what-is-ai-reading-may-2026) analyzed more than 25 million cited links across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.


The study found that earned media accounts for 84% of all AI citations. As Muck Rack cofounder and CEO Greg Galant put it, "For communications teams, that is validation that the work they do to earn coverage in the right outlets has real consequences beyond traditional metrics."


Price also needs context. Entry plans often limit engines, prompts, refresh rates, shopping data, or execution. Profound and Semrush require annual billing for their advertised $99 entry prices. Check how many actions, engines, and prompts each plan actually includes.


Monitoring and execution now overlap. Several products recommend content or technical work. Fewer carry that work through approval, delivery, and later measurement.


## How I evaluated these AI visibility tools


I used six weighted criteria:


1. **Execution, 25%.** How far the product carries work from detection through prioritization, remediation, approval, delivery, and measurement.
2. **AI platform coverage, 20%.** Which answer engines are supported, including differences in geography, model, interface, and refresh cadence.
3. **SEO and AI visibility depth, 15%.** Technical SEO, content, links, demand, crawlability, and their connection to generated answers.
4. **Data scale and freshness, 15%.** Response coverage, historical depth, update frequency, locations, and modeled-demand limits.
5. **Customer evidence, 15%.** Named case studies and documented outcomes scored above testimonials or logo walls.
6. **Self-serve to enterprise fit, 10%.** Entry price, trials, seats, exports, APIs, SSO, security, and governance, so a small team and a large one can both tell whether the product suits them.


Each criterion receives a score from 1 to 5. A score of 1 means absent or very limited. A score of 3 means capable with meaningful constraints. A score of 5 means category-leading.


I calculated each total by multiplying every criterion score by its published weight. Features available on widely sold plans count for more than features that appear only in custom contracts.


Vendor scale claims aren't directly comparable. Prompts, conversations, responses, citations, and modeled searches measure different things. I treated each as evidence within its stated unit.

## How to compare your finalists


AI visibility scores are directional. Answers change across models, locations, interfaces, and repeated runs. Modeled demand adds another source of uncertainty.


Run the same five commercial prompts through each finalist. Ask every vendor to show:


- The source answer and citation history
- The engine, model, geography, and refresh schedule
- The plan limit attached to each feature
- The next recommended action and its concrete output
- How the product measures a completed action afterward


Keep the prompts and measurement window stable. This small test reveals more than another feature checklist.


## Frequently asked questions


### What is an AI visibility tool?


It tracks how answer engines mention, cite, describe, or recommend a brand. Most products monitor prompts, compare competitors, identify cited sources, and calculate share of voice. More advanced tools add shopping results, advertising appearances, technical diagnostics, content work, outreach support, or agent-facing delivery.


### How do AI visibility tools differ from SEO tools?


AI visibility tools analyze generated answers and the sources behind them. SEO tools focus mainly on search results, keywords, pages, links, and crawlability. The categories overlap because answer engines retrieve web pages and search results, but their reports, sampling methods, and recommended actions differ.


### Which AI engines should a visibility tool cover?


Start with the engines your customers actually use. For many teams, that includes ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini, and Perplexity. Claude and Copilot can matter more for B2B research. Confirm the exact model, interface, location, and refresh cadence before comparing coverage claims.


### How much do AI visibility tools cost?


Paid products in this comparison start at $29 per month and extend to custom enterprise contracts. Many self-serve tools begin between $95 and $199 monthly. Extra engines, prompts, domains, locations, shopping data, APIs, and execution features can raise the real monthly cost substantially.


### Are there free AI visibility tools?


Free access exists, but it is limited. AthenaHQ offers a 300-credit sampler across several engines. HubSpot AEO currently includes 25 free prompts across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity without requiring a card. Ahrefs and Semrush also provide limited checkers rather than complete ongoing monitoring plans.


### How often should I measure AI visibility?


Daily or weekly tracking fits high-value prompts, active launches, and fast-moving categories. Monthly analysis can be enough for broad discovery and executive reporting. Use the same prompts, engines, locations, and measurement window each time. Consistency matters more than buying the highest available refresh frequency.
