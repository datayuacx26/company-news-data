# BARK is Making Fetch Happen with Limited-Edition Mean Girls Toy Launch

*New York, NY - October 3, 2023* - BARK, creators of BarkBox, today announced a collaboration with cult-classic film Mean Girls and released a limited-edition Burn Book plush toy for dogs in collaboration with Paramount Consumer Products. In celebration of “Mean Girls Day'' on October 3, BARK wants to bring dogs in on the fun because we’re, like, in love with them or something.


Beginning today, BARK’s Burn Book dog toy is available as a free gift when customers sign up for a 6- or 12-month[BarkBox](https://www.barkbox.com/) or[Super Chewer](https://www.barkbox.com/super-chewer) subscription, or for $10 on[BARKShop.com](https://barkshop.com/) while supplies last. BARK’s in-house design team reimagined the iconic Mean Girls Burn Book from a dog’s perspective and filled it with squeakers, crinkles, and secrets such as the latest dog to chase coach’s car and which dog has a Pug-ly snout.


“We’re excited to work with Paramount Consumer Products and design the Burn Book toy in a clever way that brings Mean Girls into the dog world,” said Dave Stangle of BARK. “At BARK, we love finding ways to tap into cultural moments and people’s obsessions to reach even more dogs and dog parents. Almost twenty years since its release, Paramount Pictures’ Mean Girls is still loved and widely quoted by all, and we wanted to find a way to tell dogs they can always ‘sit with us.’”


BARK was founded in 2011 and has since served more than 6.5 million dogs around the world. To learn more about the Mean Girls dog toy and to sign up for a subscription, visit[BarkBox.com](https://www.barkbox.com/) or[SuperChewer.com](https://www.barkbox.com/super-chewer) .


Paramount Pictures’ Mean Girls is available to stream now on Paramount+.
