# BARK Launches Best-Selling and Limited-Edition Toy Collections at Chewy

*BARK toys are now available to over 20 million Chewy customers, making it easier*
*than ever for dog parents to spoil their dogs.*


**NEW YORK, N.Y., August 21, 2024** – BARK, Inc. (NYSE: BARK) (“BARK” or the “Company”), a leading global omnichannel dog brand with a mission to make all dogs happy, today announced that it has launched a collection of its best-selling toys at Chewy, a trusted destination for pet parents and partners everywhere. This marks the first time Chewy will carry BARK toys, furthering BARK’s mission to make all dogs happy by making its products conveniently available to dog parents wherever they shop.


“We’re excited to roll out BARK products at Chewy, bringing two of the most trusted names in the industry together for the first time. The initial customer feedback exceeded our expectations, and we look forward to expanding our offerings in the coming months,” said Matt Meeker, BARK CEO and Co-Founder. “This launch underscores BARK’s mission to make all dogs happy, and we’re thrilled to team up with Chewy to reach even more dogs and their humans.”


Dog parents shopping at Chewy have expressed enthusiasm for this anticipated launch, and BARK toys available at Chewy include best sellers such as[Grey Bear Hugger](https://www.chewy.com/bark-super-chewer-bear-hugger-2-in-1/dp/1281022) ,[Treats n’ Sweets Gumdrop Tough Treat Dispensing Toy](https://www.chewy.com/bark-super-chewer-treats-n-sweets/dp/1180822) ,[Steve Squeaky Plush Snake](https://www.chewy.com/bark-steve-squeaky-plush-snake-dog/dp/1180782) ,[Lucy’s Magic Bus Squeaky Plush](https://www.chewy.com/bark-lucys-magic-bus-squeaky-plush/dp/1181054) ,[Hare Bunny Rabbit Magician Hat Plush Toy](https://www.chewy.com/bark-hare-bunny-rabbit-magician-hat/dp/1180798) and more!


The launch of BARK’s best-selling toy collection is just the beginning. In the coming months, BARK will launch even more toys and consumables, such as toppers and dental line,[BARK Bright](https://bark.co/collections/bright-dental) , for Chewy customers.


Chewy and BARK are celebrating this moment by creating a limited-edition collection of giant versions of BARK plush toys, exclusively available at[Chewy](https://www.chewy.com/) . Starting today at 3 p.m. ET, dog parents can order oversized versions of BARK’s Prickly Pete, Slow Jams Dan, Santiago Seal and Sasquatch Josh toys for $15, while supplies last. To learn more about BARK please visit[bark.co](https://bark.co/) and to shop the BARK collection visit[https://www.chewy.com/bark](https://www.chewy.com/brands/bark-140366) .


**About BARK**
BARK is the world’s most dog-centric company, devoted to making dogs happy with the best products, services and content. BARK’s dog-obsessed team applies its unique, data-driven understanding of what makes each dog special to design playstyle-specific toys, wildly satisfying treats, great food for your dog, effective and easy to use dental care, and dog-first experiences that foster the health and happiness of dogs everywhere. Founded in 2011, BARK loyally serves dogs nationwide with themed toys and treats subscriptions, BarkBox and BARK Super Chewer; custom product collections through its retail partner network, including Target, Amazon and Chewy; its high-quality, nutritious meals with BARK Food; and products that meet dogs’ dental needs with BARK Bright®. At BARK, we want to make dogs as happy as they make us because dogs and humans are better together. Sniff around at BARK.co for more information.
