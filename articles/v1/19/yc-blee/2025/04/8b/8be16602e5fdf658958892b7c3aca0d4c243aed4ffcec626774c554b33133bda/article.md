Blee and Norm represent two distinct approaches to compliance technology: Blee focuses on providing scalable, enterprise-grade marketing compliance capabilities accessible to organisations of all sizes, while Norm offers a broad legal and compliance AI platform designed for large institutional clients managing complex legal workflows beyond marketing. For marketing teams in banking, insurance, fintech, and mortgage lending, understanding these differences is crucial for selecting the platform that matches your needs and future growth.


Here's what matters for your team.


## What is Norm?


Norm is a legal and compliance AI platform designed for large financial institutions. The platform claims to turn laws, regulations, and policies into AI agents. Solutions span regulated content review, contract review, DDQ/RFP automation, disclosures, and business workflows.


## What is Blee?


Blee is an enterprise-grade AI marketing compliance platform designed from day one to provide sophisticated capabilities accessible to organisations of all sizes. The platform has delivered measurable results across diverse organisations, across both scale ups and enterprise organisations.


Unlike generic compliance tools that flood teams with false positives, Blee deploys specialised AI agents per review that understand regulatory context and learn your company's specific risk tolerance, achieving 85-95% accuracy on flagging genuine risks. The platform embeds seamlessly into existing workflows without requiring system replacements: marketing teams continue working in Jira, Asana, Workfront, Salesforce, Figma, Google Docs, while files remain in Google Drive or Dropbox, and compliance teams review everything centrally in Blee.

## Deep Dive: Must-Win Dimensions


### Policy Coverage: Marketing-Specific vs Broad Legal Compliance


Blee maintains actively updated compliance rules for all 50 US states, plus UK, EU, Asia and MENA markets, automatically flagging jurisdiction-specific conflicts. The platform includes pre-built rules for FINRA, SEC, FTC, state insurance departments, and platform-specific requirements (Google, Meta). Legal engineers proactively monitor regulatory changes and update rule libraries in real-time.


Norm focuses on turning broad laws and regulations into AI agents. Without documented coverage of state-by-state marketing regulations, platform advertising policies, or marketing-specific regulatory libraries, marketing teams may find gaps in creative content compliance intelligence.


### Risk Detection: Intelligent Learning vs Institutional Model


Blee deploys specialised AI agents per review, each handling different aspects (transcription, video analysis, regulatory checking, rule orchestration) to deliver 85-95% accuracy on genuine risks. The system creates a custom LLM for each client that learns from your team's feedback, adapting to your organisation's specific risk appetite and policies over time.


Norm promotes AI agents supervised by legal engineers but does not document whether each client receives custom models, how the AI learns from marketing team feedback, or what accuracy rates are achieved specifically for marketing content review.


### Workflow Scale: Seamless Integration vs Document-Based


Blee embeds directly into existing workflows regardless of organisation size. Marketing teams continue working in Figma, compliance teams review in Blee, and everything synchronises automatically. The platform integrates with[Monday.com](http://monday.com/) , Asana, Workday, Wrike, Microsoft Word, and Google Docs, eliminating friction whether you're a 10-person team or 10,000-person enterprise. Implementation takes 6-10 weeks with dedicated support from former compliance officers.


Norm operates through Word and PowerPoint workflows. Without documented native integrations with design tools, project management platforms, or creative workflow systems where marketing teams work, this creates bottlenecks for creative teams managing visual assets and multi-channel campaigns.


### Records and Audit: Proactive Intelligence vs Institutional Documentation


Blee maintains complete audit trails with timestamped records of every review, approval, and content version, specifically designed for regulatory examinations. The system indexes all content (including within videos and images) making historical searches instant. When regulations change, Blee can retroactively check published content for new compliance issues.


Norm's audit capabilities for marketing-specific workflows are not documented. Without detailed information about creative asset version control or retroactive compliance checking for marketing content, teams may lack the proactive features that marketing-focused regulatory examinations require.
