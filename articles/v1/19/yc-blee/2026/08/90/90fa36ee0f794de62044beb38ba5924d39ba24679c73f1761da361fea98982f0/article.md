# AI Reasoning: See The Why, Not Just The What

## The flag, explained.


A flag tells you something's wrong. It doesn't always tell you why. So the reviewer does the digging—pulling the rule, hunting for how the team handled it last time, reconstructing a decision from scattered history before deciding to keep it or let it go.


Blee's AI Reasoning closes that gap. The why now sits next to the what, on the same flag.


Open the reasoning on any flag, and Blee shows its work: the rule it matched, precedents from your own past decisions, and a confidence level, all in one place. No searching. No second-guessing. No reconstructing a rationale from memory.


Because the reasoning cites your team's actual past calls, every reviewer is working from the same standard. The same flag gets the same decision, whether it's reviewed today or three months from now, by one reviewer or another.


For legal and compliance teams, it means AI review you can stand behind—grounded in your documented standard, not a black box. For reviewers, it means deciding in seconds instead of building the case by hand.


AI Reasoning on every flag. Always grounded in your decisions.
