# Blee for Figma: Compliance Review, Without Leaving the Canvas

## **Compliance, in your frame.**


Marketers don't think in compliance terms when they're working in Figma. They think in canvases, layers, and exports. So when a review process asks them to leave the file, upload to a portal, and wait, something gets lost in the handoff. Usually it's a logo, a claim buried in a callout, or a disclosure that should've been on the asset and wasn't.


Blee's Figma plugin closes that gap. Compliance review now happens inside the file, on the same canvas where the work is being made.


Right-click, open the plugin, and Blee reviews the canvas the same way it reviews everything else: flagging unapproved logos, off-brand elements, and missing disclosures, directly on the layers where they live. No exporting. No second tool. No waiting for someone to get to it.


‍


Every flag, comment, and version syncs one-to-one with the Blee dashboard. A marketer resolves an issue in Figma, and legal sees it resolved in Blee. A reviewer leaves a note in Blee, and it shows up where the work's already happening. Nothing gets lost between the two.


For legal and compliance teams, it means visual content is finally part of the same oversight as everything else. For marketers, it means review doesn't interrupt the work. It happens alongside it.


Available in Figma. And everywhere else you work, too.
