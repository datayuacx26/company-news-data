# Blee For Google Docs: Compliance Review, Without Leaving The Document

## Compliance, in your doc


Marketing doesn't happen in a review portal. It happens in a doc, mid-edit, with a draft that's still taking shape. So when compliance review asks teams to stop, export, and upload somewhere else, the work stalls right when it has momentum.


Blee's Chrome extension closes that gap. Compliance review now happens inside the doc, on the same page where the writing is happening.


Highlight a sentence, and Blee reviews it the way it reviews everything else: flagging overpromising language, missing disclosures, and unsubstantiated claims, directly inline. No exporting. No second tool. No waiting for someone to get to it.


Every flag, comment, and version syncs one-to-one with the Blee dashboard. A marketer resolves an issue in the doc, and legal sees it resolved in Blee. A reviewer leaves a note in Blee, and it shows up where the work's already happening. Nothing gets lost between the two.


For legal and compliance teams, it means oversight follows the work instead of waiting for it to arrive. For marketing, it means review doesn't interrupt the writing. It happens alongside it.


Available in Google Docs. And everywhere else you work, too.
