# Types of GIFT City Mutual Funds for NRIs

GIFT City mutual funds provide NRIs with a convenient way to invest internationally without the complications of currency conversion. These funds operate under the regulation of the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA) at Gujarat International Finance Tec-City (GIFT City) and are USD-denominated. This helps NRIs avoid risks associated with rupee fluctuations while building a diversified investment portfolio.


Asset Management Companies at GIFT City launch these offshore funds. Currently, GIFT City hosts over 200 Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs) and various mutual funds designed specifically for NRI investors. These funds benefit from exemptions from Tax Deducted at Source (TDS), making them attractive for tax-efficient global market investments.


‍


## **Categories of GIFT City Mutual Funds for NRIs**


- **Offshore Mutual Funds:** Invest in global markets using major currencies like USD, GBP, SGD, and AED, offering exposure to equities, bonds, and diversified asset classes.


- [Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs](https://www.goinri.com/blog/how-nris-can-invest-in-aifs) **):** Target sectors like Indian private equity, real estate, and infrastructure. They can be structured as Venture Capital Funds, Retail Funds, or Non-retail Funds depending on their approach.


- **Tata India Dynamic Equity Fund:** Designed for first-time international investors with a minimum investment of USD 500. It invests in Tata AMC’s Indian equity mutual funds and ETFs.


- **Family Investment Funds (FIFs):** Suitable for single families with a minimum corpus of USD 10 million over three years.


- [Portfolio Management Services (PMS)](https://www.goinri.com/blog/curated-for-nris-mutual-funds-vs-pms-portfolio-management-services) **:** A bespoke approach with a reduced minimum investment requirement of USD 75,000.


Each fund type caters to varying investment goals and capital availability, allowing NRIs flexibility in portfolio building.

## **Benefits of GIFT City Mutual Funds for NRIs**


- **Tax Efficiency:** These funds are exempt from Securities Transaction Tax (STT), Commodities Transaction Tax (CTT), and GST on offshore services, lowering transaction costs. Business income enjoys a tax holiday, and capital gains on certain securities have favorable tax treatment compared to domestic funds.


- **Currency Stability:** With investments and returns denominated in major international currencies, portfolios are protected from adverse rupee movements.


- **Repatriation Flexibility:** Investors enjoy 100% repatriation of capital and returns without restrictions.


- **Professional Fund Management:** Experienced managers operate under IFSCA’s oversight, ensuring compliance and expertise in navigating global markets.

## **How NRIs Invest in GIFT City Mutual Funds**


NRIs must confirm their status per FEMA (Foreign Exchange Management Act) guidelines before investing, as compliance with these regulations is essential for legal and seamless investment processes.


### **Investment routes available to NRIs include:**


- **Financial advisors offering personalized guidance:** NRIs can seek[expert advice](https://app.goinri.com/investments?section=gift-city) tailored to their financial goals and risk tolerance, helping select appropriate funds and investment strategies aligned with FEMA rules.


- **Direct investment through AMC digital platforms:** Many Asset Management Companies provide online portals designed for NRIs, enabling straightforward, independent transactions while ensuring adherence to regulatory requirements.


- **Banks facilitating compliant investments and transactions:** Authorized banks assist NRIs with opening necessary accounts, managing transfers, and ensuring investments comply with FEMA and IFSCA regulations.


### **Essential procedural steps include:**


- **Setting clear investment goals and amounts:** NRIs should clarify their risk appetite and financial objectives before proceeding.


- **Completing IFSCA-compliant KYC:** This involves submitting valid documents such as passports, PAN cards, and overseas residency proof to establish identity and eligibility in line with FEMA.


- **Opening appropriate accounts:** Depending on investment needs, NRIs must open NRE (Non-Resident External Rupee) or NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary Rupee) accounts for Indian currency transactions, or foreign currency accounts like FCNR through International Banking Units to handle multi-currency investments.


- **Fund transfer through authorized banking channels:** All monetary movements must comply with FEMA’s foreign exchange laws, ensuring transparent, traceable, and legal fund transfers.


Following these steps assures that NRIs invest within legal frameworks, avoid penalties, and efficiently manage their portfolios with full regulatory compliance. GIFT City investments further simplify this process under specialized FEMA provisions designed for international investors.

## **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)**


**Q1. What types of GIFT City mutual funds are available for NRIs?** Options include offshore mutual funds in major currencies, AIFs in Indian private equity and real estate, retail feeder funds, Family Investment Funds for affluent families, and Portfolio Management Services.


**Q2. What tax benefits are offered?** Exemptions from STT, CTT, and GST on offshore services, a tax holiday on business income, and lower capital gains taxes on specific securities make these funds tax efficient.


**Q3. How do these funds protect against currency risk?** Investments and returns are denominated in major currencies like USD, protecting against depreciation of the rupee.


**Q4. How do NRIs invest in GIFT City mutual funds?** By verifying NRI status, completing required KYC, opening suitable accounts, selecting an investment route, and transferring funds through approved channels.


**Q5. Are there minimum investment requirements?** Yes. For instance, retail feeder funds may require as little as USD 500, while Family Investment Funds necessitate a minimum corpus of USD 10 million over three years.
