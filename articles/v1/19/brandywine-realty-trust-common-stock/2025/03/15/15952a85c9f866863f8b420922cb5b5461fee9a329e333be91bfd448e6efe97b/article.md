# Smart Growth, Strategic Location: Veeva Expands at Radnor Financial Center

# Smart Growth, Strategic Location: Veeva Expands at Radnor Financial Center


Stronger Roots, Smarter Spaces


For more than a decade,[Veeva](https://www.veeva.com/?utm_source=homepage&utm_medium=nav&utm_term=local-website&utm_content=usa&utm_campaign=fy23_gen_all_glo_an_prospect_language_preference) has called Radnor Corporate Center home. Now, they’re expanding within Brandywine’s portfolio with a larger office footprint at 555 Lancaster Avenue. Their decision to grow in this location reflects more than just a need for more space—it’s about aligning with a workplace that fosters innovation, supports flexibility, and strengthens their connection to a thriving life sciences ecosystem.


*Veeva's new office at Radnor Financial Center*


**A Flexible Workplace Designed for Focus and Collaboration**


Veeva’s 10,152-square-foot expansion is designed to provide employees with a flexible, high-performance office environment that balances private offices, collaborative areas, and high-tech meeting rooms.


-


More private offices and workstations – With 23 private offices and 17 bench workstations, the office footprint has doubled to create an environment that supports heads-down work and seamless virtual meetings.


- Customer-ready meeting spaces – A large boardroom and dedicated training rooms make it easy to host in-person meetings and presentations with life sciences customers and partners.


- Flexible common areas – Thoughtfully designed social spaces encourage employees to connect, collaborate, and engage with colleagues when they choose to be in the office.


****


*Meeting space at Veeva's new office at Radnor Financial Center*


**Expanding Offices While Committed to Working Anywhere**


Veeva recognizes the ongoing value of a well-designed office while embracing a Work Anywhere approach which allows employees to work from the office or home on any given day. Their expansion at Radnor underscores the belief that while employees benefit from flexibility, having a high-quality, collaborative workplace is essential for team connection, client meetings, and fostering a strong company culture.


By investing in additional office space, Veeva is ensuring that employees in the region have access to a workspace that enhances productivity, fosters collaboration, and provides a welcoming environment when they come in.


**Why Radnor? A Smart Bet for Veeva’s Growth**


Radnor has been a key location for Veeva, due to the area’s deep-rooted life sciences and technology sectors. As a global leader in cloud-based software for the life sciences industry, Veeva’s success relies on proximity to top talent, key clients, and an innovation-driven ecosystem—with neighboring complexes like Radnor Life Sciences Center and Radnor Corporate Center.


Beyond its prime location, Radnor Financial Center delivers:


-


Unmatched accessibility – With quick access to major highways (I-476 and I-76) and regional transit, employees and clients can reach the office with ease.


- Premium workplace amenities – Conference rooms, coworking spaces, fitness centers, and high-end dining create a well-rounded office experience.


- Outdoor spaces for balance – Lush gardens, a cascading fountain, and outdoor seating allow employees to recharge throughout the day.


**A Workplace That Evolves with You**


At Brandywine, we believe that a great office isn’t just about square footage—it’s about creating an environment where companies and employees thrive. That’s why we work closely with tenants like Veeva to design spaces that support evolving work models, enhance employee well-being, and foster collaboration.


As Veeva expands its presence at Radnor Financial Center, we’re proud to support its vision with a workspace that not only meets today’s needs but continues to evolve for the future.
