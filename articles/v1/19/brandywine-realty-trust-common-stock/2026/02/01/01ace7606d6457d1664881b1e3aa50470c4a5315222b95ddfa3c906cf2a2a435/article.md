# The Brandywine, the Main Line’s Newest Boutique Hotel, to Welcome Guests Spring 2026


Joining Marriott Bonvoy’s Tribute Portfolio, The Brandywine is Set to Open in the Suburbs of Philadelphi a, Pennsylvania


**RADNOR, PA (February 12, 2026)** — Brandywine Realty Trust today announced *The Brandywine,* a new boutique hotel opening this spring at 165 King of Prussia Road in Radnor, Pennsylvania, ushering in a new era of hospitality, vibrant design, and bold culinary experiences on the Main Line. A Marriott Bonvoy Tribute Portfolio Hotel operated by Aimbridge Hospitality, the 121-room property will feature two signature dining concepts – Merrick’s Tavern


and Pomelo Rooftop Terrace


– that will serve locals, visitors, and the business community alike.


Owned and developed by Brandywine Realty Trust, the hotel is a key component of the firm’s 2.1 million-square-foot presence in Radnor, which includes amenity-rich office and lab space nestled in the area’s suburban landscape. The property’s captivating design, inviting guest rooms, and energetic social spaces will blend the warm familiarity of Bonvoy perks with the character and style of the Main Line.


“We are thrilled to introduce The Brandywine, a reflection of our continued commitment to our customers and the communities where we invest,” said Jerry Sweeney, President & CEO of Brandywine Realty Trust. “By partnering with Marriott and Aimbridge, we’re delivering an upscale hospitality experience that enhances our mixed‑use approach in Radnor, strengthens the amenity base for our office tenants, and brings something truly distinctive to suburban Philadelphia.”


The Brandywine’s guestrooms pair timeless elegance with modern conveniences, reflecting a relaxed sophistication designed for today’s leisure and business travelers – with soft hues and natural textures used to evoke nostalgic charm and spaces that feel calm, welcoming, and comfortable. Guest rooms also include suites with private outdoor patios overlooking lush landscapes for ultimate relaxation and solitude.


Merrick’s Tavern


, located on the property’s ground floor, will offer a welcoming setting where gatherings unfold easily. The menu will balance refinement with approachability, serving regional American classics, thoughtfully imagined cocktails, and a bar that leans into a deep affection for bourbon and rye, alongside local brews and an extensive wine list.


Perched above it all, Pomelo Rooftop Terrace


will host guests year-round with refined and effortless service. The rooftop blends moments of quiet with a lively social energy, offering creative cocktails rooted in nature’s apothecary and a locally sourced yet globally inspired menu – ultimately creating a standout destination for sunset gatherings, weekend celebrations, and special occasions.


Situated minutes from SEPTA’s regional rail station, the hotel will serve as an excellent amenity for Brandywine Realty Trust’s office tenant base, healthcare campuses and nearby universities. The Brandywine’s thoughtfully appointed guest rooms, flexible event spaces, and amenities will attract travelers seeking immersive weekend stays just minutes from center city Philadelphia.


The Brandywine also provides guests close proximity to cultural attractions, shopping districts, and outdoor recreation, and is located just minutes from several of the region’s top academic institutions, making it an ideal destination for travelers visiting Southeastern Pennsylvania for the many upcoming festivities hosted in the region including the FIFA World Cup 2026™, MLB’s All-Star Week 2026, Wawa Welcome America, the 2026 PGA Championship and many more.


More details about the grand opening, restaurant menus and reservation details, and additional guest offerings will be shared in the coming months. For meetings and events and group bookings, please contactAshley.Ridolfi@aimbridge.com .
