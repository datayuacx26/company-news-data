# What Is Coherent Routing?

Coherent routing, Routed Optical Networking, Converged Optical Routing Architecture (CORA), are all names for the same concept: an advanced network architecture which **integrates coherent optical transceivers directly into IP routers** . This convergence of layers creates a simplified and highly efficient IP-over-DWDM (IP over Dense Wavelength Division Multiplexing) network.


By embedding optical transmission capabilities within the routing platform, operators can strategically collapse redundant hardware, reducing network complexity and cost without sacrificing the flexibility of a symbiotic IP and optical architecture.


This approach marks a significant evolution from traditional network designs where IP routing and optical transport were managed as distinct, separate domains. Consequently, coherent routing enables a more streamlined, scalable, and automated infrastructure prepared for modern traffic demands.


## What Are Its Key Components?


The architecture relies on three fundamental components working in unison to deliver a converged IP and optical solution.


- **Coherent Pluggable Optic:** Compact, standards-based modules, such as 400GZR+, that contain the DSP and optical components. They plug directly into compatible router ports, enabling high-capacity optical transmission.
- **IP Routers with Integrated Optics Support:** Routing platforms designed to support coherent pluggables, including power, cooling, and software integration for monitoring and control. Ribbon’s NPT 2714 is an example.
- **Optional: Pluggable Amplifier:** A QSFP-DD module that boosts optical signals to extend transmission range.
- **Optional: Optical Line System (OLS):** Includes mux/demux and amplifiers to combine wavelengths and extend distance.

## What Are the Primary Benefits of Coherent Routing?


The primary benefits include **reduced network complexity, lower total cost of ownership,** and increased operational agility.


- **Reduced Total Cost of Ownership (TCO):** Eliminates transponder hardware, reducing CapEx, power consumption, and physical footprint.
- **Simplified Network Architecture:** Fewer elements make the network easier to plan, deploy, and manage.
- **Increased Scalability and Capacity:** Enables fast deployment of 400G services on existing infrastructure.
- **Enhanced Operational Automation:** A unified network simplifies automation using platforms like Ribbon Muse.


## Why Is IP and Optical Convergence Important?


Convergence is essential for creating a **service-aware, efficient, and automatable transport network** .


Historically, IP and optical layers operated as separate silos, leading to inefficiencies and underutilized bandwidth. Coherent routing eliminates these barriers by providing unified visibility and control.


This enables end-to-end automation, better resource utilization, and streamlined operations.


## What Role Do Pluggable Optics Play?


Pluggable optics, such as 400GZR+, are the key enablers of cost-effective coherent routing.


They allow operators to **add high-capacity optical interfaces directly to routers on demand** , avoiding large upfront investments.


This aligns with the concept of **Optical Freedom** —open, interoperable systems that prevent vendor lock-in and allow multi-vendor sourcing.


## What Are the Key Use Cases for Coherent Routing?


Coherent routing supports a variety of applications, including:


- Data center interconnect (DCI)
- 5G/mobile network aggregation
- High-speed broadband expansion


With a unified control plane, platforms like Ribbon Muse enable automated provisioning, traffic optimization, and proactive network management.
