# FMC Corporation Declares Quarterly Dividend

FMC Corporation (NYSE: FMC) announced today that its board of directors declared a regular quarterly dividend of 8 cents per share, payable on October 15, 2026, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on September 30, 2026.
