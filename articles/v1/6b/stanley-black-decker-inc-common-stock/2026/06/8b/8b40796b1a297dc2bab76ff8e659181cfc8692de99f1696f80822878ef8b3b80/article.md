# Stanley Black & Decker Announces Release Date for Second Quarter 2026 Earnings

NEW BRITAIN, Conn.


, June 23, 2026


/[PRNewswire](http://www.prnewswire.com/) / -- **Stanley Black & Decker** (NYSE: SWK) will release its second quarter 2026 earnings on Wednesday, July 29, 2026, before the market opens, followed by an earnings call at 8:00AM ET. The call will be available through a live teleconference and a listen-only webcast.


Direct links to register for the teleconference, access the webcast, and view the accompanying slide presentation will be available in the "[Events](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4717990-1&h=975979379&u=https%3A%2F%2Fir.stanleyblackanddecker.com%2Fnews-events%2Fevents%2Fdefault.aspx%3F_gl%3D1*1vg0ht1*_gcl_au*NDc0MjY0MDU5LjE3ODA4ODc5NDAuNDc0NTEzNTk3LjE3ODE3MTkzODIuMTc4MTcxOTM4Mg..&a=Events) " section of the Stanley Black & Decker Investors website at[www.stanleyblackanddecker.com/investors](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4717990-1&h=1242395100&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.stanleyblackanddecker.com%2Finvestors&a=www.stanleyblackanddecker.com%2Finvestors) . A replay will be available in the same location approximately two hours after the call.
