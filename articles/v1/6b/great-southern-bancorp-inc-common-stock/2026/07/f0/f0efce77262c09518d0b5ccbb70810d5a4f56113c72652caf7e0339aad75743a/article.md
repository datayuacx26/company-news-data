# A Greater Impact

**Springfield, MO** – Great Southern Bank is proud to share the significant impact of its community engagement efforts in 2025. Through corporate donations, sponsorships, volunteerism, and associate contributions, the Bank continued its 100-year tradition to making a difference in the communities it serves.


**Key Highlights for 2025:**


Corporate Donations & Sponsorships: $1,942,866.96 contributed to nonprofit and civic organizations.
Organizations Supported: Over 700 nonprofit and civic organizations received support.
Associate Giving: $56,297.68 donated through United Way campaigns and Casual Day contributions.
Volunteerism: Associates dedicated 6,290 volunteer hours to 340 organizations.


“Community isn't just something we support—it's who we are. Our associates don't just work in these communities; they live here, volunteer here, and care deeply about making them stronger. When you see that level of commitment, banking becomes more than transactions. It becomes a partnership. We're honored to stand alongside the incredible organizations doing this work every day,” said Great Southern Bank President and CEO, Joe Turner.


Each year, Great Southern Bank invests in community impact, over time infusing millions into local initiatives and donating thousands of volunteer hours. These efforts reflect the Bank’s mission to strengthen communities and improve lives through its Community Matters program. To discover the full Community Matters story, visit community.greatsouthernbank.com.
