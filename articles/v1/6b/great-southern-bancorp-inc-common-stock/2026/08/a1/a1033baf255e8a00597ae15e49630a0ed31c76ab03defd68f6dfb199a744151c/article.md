# Andrea Brady Honored as Nonprofit Board Member of the Year


Community Outreach and Development Manager recognized by Springfield Business Journal for years of board leadership across Springfield nonprofits


"I want Springfield to be a great place to live... I want to be involved in helping to make that happen." - Andrea Brady


Great Southern Bank


Andrea Brady, Community Outreach and Development Manager at Great Southern Bank, was recently named Board Member of the Year by the Springfield Business Journal as part of its Nonprofit Excellence Awards.


The recognition celebrates Andrea's long record of board service across the Springfield community, including her work with the Lost & Found Grief Center, the Springfield Business Development Corp., Springfield Innovation Inc., and the Greater Missouri Leadership Foundation, along with her involvement in Rotary.


[In an interview with SBJ](https://sbj.net/stories/andrea-brady-lost-found-grief-center,105182/p/stories/2026-nonprofit-excellence-honorees,105186) , Andrea shared that she rarely turns down a chance to help, saying that supporting nonprofits has taught her more about her community and given her a greater sense of purpose along the way. She pointed to Great Southern's own Community Matters program as an example of how the bank encourages associates to roll up their sleeves and get involved.


Congratulations to Andrea on this well-deserved honor!
