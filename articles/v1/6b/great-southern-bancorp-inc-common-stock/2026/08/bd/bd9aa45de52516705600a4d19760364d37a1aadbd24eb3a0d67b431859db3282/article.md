# Welcome Grace Bayer

**Springfield, MO** - Great Southern Bank is pleased to announce that Grace Bayer has joined the organization as a merchant services relationship manager, expanding the bank’s ability to support businesses across Missouri with secure, efficient, and relationship-driven payment solutions.


A graduate of Missouri State University, Grace brings a strong foundation in business knowledge, client service, and community engagement. In her role, she will partner with business owners in the St. Louis, Kansas City, and Columbia markets, helping them streamline their payment processing, adopt modern point of sale and digital payment technologies, and enhance their overall operational efficiency.


“Grace’s energy, professionalism, and commitment to building strong relationships make her an excellent addition to our Merchant Services team,” said Brad Heeren, managing director of VIP banking and business services.


Grace is passionate about supporting the growth and success of local businesses and looks forward to serving as a trusted advisor to merchants across the state.
