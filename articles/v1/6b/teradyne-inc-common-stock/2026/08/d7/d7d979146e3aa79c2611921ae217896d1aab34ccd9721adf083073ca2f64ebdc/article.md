# Perspectives on Test: Meeting the Demand of AI from Wafer to Data Centers

[← Back to Blog Index](https://www.teradyne.com/company/blog)


AI is redefining the infrastructure that powers it, and nowhere is that more true than at the compute layer. As server architectures and AI accelerators grow more complex, testing has to keep pace on both the hardware and software fronts. In this episode, Roy Chorev, Vice President and General Manager of the Compute Test Division, discusses the demands next-generation AI places on test, and how Teradyne’s platforms and software tools work together to deliver the speed, flexibility, and quality customers need.[Contact us](https://www.teradyne.com/company/contact-teradyne/) to learn more.
