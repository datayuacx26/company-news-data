***About Enhabit Home Health & Hospice***


Enhabit Home Health & Hospice (Enhabit, Inc.) is a leading national home health and hospice provider working to expand what's possible for patient care in the home. Enhabit’s team of clinicians supports patients and their families where they are most comfortable, with a nationwide footprint spanning 247 home health locations and 115 hospice locations across 34 states. Enhabit leverages advanced technology and compassionate teams to deliver extraordinary patient care. For more information, visit[enhabit.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.enhabit.com%2F&esheet=54423762&newsitemid=20260212765203&lan=en-US&anchor=enhabit.com&index=4&md5=e2fe98f0ab6999bee6d58c6b81cedf50) .
