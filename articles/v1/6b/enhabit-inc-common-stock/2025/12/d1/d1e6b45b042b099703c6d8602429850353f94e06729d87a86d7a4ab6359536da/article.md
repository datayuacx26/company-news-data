# Enhabit Announces Participation in Bank of America Home Care Conference 2025

### Enhabit Announces Participation in Bank of America Home Care Conference 2025


12/02/2025


DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Enhabit, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.enhabit.com%2F&esheet=54367303&newsitemid=20251202433385&lan=en-US&anchor=Enhabit%2C+Inc.&index=1&md5=2f9411b5d05cdac3a6798485b756fff3) (NYSE: EHAB), a leading national home health and hospice provider, today announced its participation in the Bank of America Home Care Conference 2025.


Enhabit’s President and CEO Barb Jacobsmeyer and Chief Financial Officer Ryan Solomon will participate in a fireside chat on Tuesday, Dec. 9, at 2:50 p.m. CT. The fireside chat will be webcast live at[https://bofa.veracast.com/webcasts/bofa/homecare2025/M4EufU.cfm](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fbofa.veracast.com%2Fwebcasts%2Fbofa%2Fhomecare2025%2FM4EufU.cfm&esheet=54367303&newsitemid=20251202433385&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fbofa.veracast.com%2Fwebcasts%2Fbofa%2Fhomecare2025%2FM4EufU.cfm&index=2&md5=431436cbc39ef31dd0517faceb38ee1e) , and a replay will be available at the same URL within 24 hours after the event’s conclusion. Both the live webcast and replay will also be accessible on[Enhabit's investor website](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.ehab.com%2F&esheet=54367303&newsitemid=20251202433385&lan=en-US&anchor=Enhabit%27s+investor+website&index=3&md5=3f0ded336fa30af62a6ced1950945339) under “Events & Presentations.”


**About Enhabit Home Health & Hospice**


Enhabit Home Health & Hospice (Enhabit, Inc.) is a leading national home health and hospice provider working to expand what’s possible for patient care in the home. Enhabit's team of clinicians supports patients and their families where they are most comfortable, with a nationwide footprint spanning 247 home health locations and 115 hospice locations across 34 states. Enhabit leverages advanced technology and compassionate teams to deliver extraordinary patient care. For more information, visit[enhabit.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.enhabit.com%2F&esheet=54367303&newsitemid=20251202433385&lan=en-US&anchor=enhabit.com&index=4&md5=0ff591699334acec7970ed0fb030503a) .
