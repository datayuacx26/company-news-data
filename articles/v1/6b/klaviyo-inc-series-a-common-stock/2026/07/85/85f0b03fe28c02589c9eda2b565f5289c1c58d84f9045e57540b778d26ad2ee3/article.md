# Klaviyo New Integrations: Figma, Yotpo, & Two More Worth Knowing - Klaviyo

---


A customer earns loyalty points, then gets the same generic email as everyone else. Another stalls at check-out and never hears from you again. A third fills out a post-purchase survey and tells you exactly how they feel, and the next message they get ignores every word of it.


Those missed opportunities aren’t happening because you don’t have enough data. They’re happening because there’s a gap between where your customer signals live and where your marketing actually runs.


4 new Q2 integrations in Klaviyo, the autonomous B2C CRM, close that gap across the customer lifecycle.


## Figma: Get branded assets into Klaviyo in one click


Export any frame, graphic, or component straight from[Figma](https://marketplace.klaviyo.com/en-us/apps/01KTYF9ED9WV1SB34CE1536H7Y) into your Klaviyo[image library](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/52060725969435) , with no downloading, renaming, or re-uploading files by hand.


Figma is where your brand visuals get made, and now it connects directly to your image library in Klaviyo. The creative your designers finalize is ready to drop into an automated flow the same day, not 3 email threads later.


Batch-export a set of assets in one job, and connect more than one Klaviyo account to switch between brands without exporting to the wrong one. For teams running multiple brands, that means building creative once from a single design source instead of rebuilding it in every account.


Picture the handoff: a designer finalizes a campaign banner, exports it in one click, and it's in a flow template that same afternoon. No file swap, no version confusion.


## Yotpo Loyalty: Make loyalty data trigger email and SMS flows


[Yotpo Loyalty](https://marketplace.klaviyo.com/en-us/apps/01K8WJQWZTZYZSE7AX4MKBWE52/) syncs points, tiers, rewards, and referral activity into your Klaviyo[segments](https://www.klaviyo.com/features/segmentation) , flows, Customer Hub, and campaigns, so all of them react the moment a loyalty event happens.


Instead of loyalty living in its own silo, it becomes a marketing trigger. Start automated flows based on loyalty events like points earned, tier changes, and reward expiration reminders. Build precise segments using more than 25 loyalty customer properties to send more relevant campaigns to the people who actually care about them.


Referral activity synced from Yotpo Loyalty can also trigger an automated follow-up when a customer refers a friend or when that referral converts, so the thank-you message and the next offer arrive right on time.


Let’s say a customer's reward is about to expire, and they get a text message nudge to use it. If they don't open it within a day, a follow-up email lands in their inbox with the same offer. When your repeat buyers feel seen and special, they’re more likely to keep coming back, spend more, and bring their friends with them.


## Contentsquare: Use behavioral data to trigger smarter campaigns


Sync behavioral cohorts from[Contentsquare](https://marketplace.klaviyo.com/en-us/apps/01jwxhsphgkhnjyc7rssqq4ga6/) into Klaviyo, and what happens on your site directly shapes what customers receive next.


Contentsquare connects your digital experience analytics to your marketing automation. Automatically sync behavioral cohorts to trigger win-back flows for people who stall in the funnel, or target your most active visitors based on high-frequency site activity.


With the Contentsquare x Klaviyo integration, cohorts update automatically as customer behavior shifts, so your segments stay current without manual list edits. You don't rebuild a list every week. The data does it for you.


It works both directions. Send Klaviyo marketing data back to Contentsquare to see how your owned channels drive long-term behavior and lifetime value, not just the next click.


## Fairing: Put post-purchase survey responses to work automatically


[Fairing](https://marketplace.klaviyo.com/en-us/apps/01ktm255qeg2z0cg0nbat2pxyh/) syncs every post-purchase survey response to Klaviyo in real time as events and custom profile properties, ready to power your flows and segments the moment a customer responds.


This is zero-party data at work: information customers volunteer directly, in their own words. Use attribution data to understand which channels drive your highest-value customers, deliver Net Promoter Score (NPS) surveys directly through Klaviyo[email](https://www.klaviyo.com/products/email-marketing) and[text messaging](https://www.klaviyo.com/products/sms-marketing) , and trigger personalized follow-up sequences based on exactly what customers tell you.


Here’s how this might work in practice: a low NPS score drops the customer into an apology and support flow that offers the customer various methods to resolve the issue. A high score triggers a review or referral request while enthusiasm is fresh. Same survey, two automatic follow-ups.
