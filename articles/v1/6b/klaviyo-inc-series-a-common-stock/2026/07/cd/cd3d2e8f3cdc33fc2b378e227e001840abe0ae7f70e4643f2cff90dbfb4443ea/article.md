Labor Day is a great opportunity to gather the zero- and first-party[customer data](https://www.klaviyo.com/glossary/what-is-customer-data) that makes personalization possible year-round.

## 1. Pair Labor Day seasonal clearance with free gifts


A Labor Day free gift offer is a good way to move summer inventory and make way for fall products. But it’s even better for learning who your discount-driven customers are.


What can look like a one-and-done sale is actually a source of customer data you can use to drive more revenue throughout the year. Customers who take advantage of a free gift are a distinct[audience segment](https://www.klaviyo.com/features/segmentation) : they’re deal-driven, and they’re motivated by different incentives than your high spenders or brand loyalists.


If you end up running this offer, make sure to create a segment for the people who buy from it. You’ll want to target them with future clearance offers, flash sales, and steep discounts.


Over time, these customers may spend more if they like your products. In the meantime, honor the way they spend now and target them with bigger discounts when you can offer them.


### Labor Day marketing idea: Laura Geller offers tiered free gifts alongside sitewide discounts


In this Labor Day marketing example, beauty brand[Laura Geller](https://www.laurageller.com/) runs a tiered free gift offer alongside a sitewide discount, giving customers a reason to increase their order value. Shoppers who spend $55 worth of products get a free mini primer, and those who spend $75 get a full-size mascara on top of that.


The tiered structure serves two purposes: it moves inventory, and it surfaces which customers respond to a good deal within certain product categories. This is spending and product preference data you can act on long after Labor Day is over.

## 2. Build your mobile messaging programs with Labor Day sale incentives


[SMS marketing](https://www.klaviyo.com/products/sms-marketing) revenue jumped 48% YoY during Labor Day 2025, and roughly one-third of that revenue came from first-time buyers, according to Klaviyo's 2025 Labor Day weekend sales data. Even with higher send volumes, revenue per send held strong, which means consumers are receptive to timely texts.


2024[research from Klaviyo and Recharge](https://www.klaviyo.com/marketing-resources/sms-consumer-report) also supports the idea that people who opt in to receive texts from your brand really want to hear from you: the survey found that 72% of people are willing to receive a text at least once a week.


All of that means the size of your text message marketing list going into Labor Day weekend has a direct impact on revenue for the rest of the year. Whether you’re promoting a summer clearance sale, an end-of-season discount, or early access to deals, Labor Day is a natural hook for text message sign-ups—and once someone’s on your list, you can reach them all year-round.


SMS has been a go-to channel for Labor Day campaigns for years, but[RCS marketing](https://www.klaviyo.com/products/sms-marketing/rcs) and[WhatsApp](https://www.klaviyo.com/products/whatsapp) are also worth adding to your mobile messaging mix. Here’s what that might look like:


- RCS turns traditional texts into interactive shopping experiences with carousels, images, and tappable buttons. For a Labor Day summer clearance sale, you could showcase end-of-season products in a swipeable carousel, complete with discounted prices and one-tap purchase links.
- A WhatsApp Labor Day campaign could start with a clearance sale image and a quick reply prompt: “Want early access?” Customers who tap yes get an automated follow-up with personalized product recommendations based on their past purchases. Rather than a one-way promotional message, the exchange captures customer preferences and guides each shopper to the most relevant products.


### Labor Day marketing idea: Use Labor Day to grow your text messaging subscriber list


Two weeks before Labor Day, add a pop-up to your site with a simple offer targeted at existing email subscribers: sign up for texts or WhatsApp, and get early access to the Labor Day sale before it goes public.


Around a week before Labor Day weekend, send the early-access text. Follow up on Saturday with a reminder highlighting your bestsellers, and again on Sunday with a last-chance message calling out anything that's running low on stock.


By Tuesday, you’ve hopefully made headway on clearing out your summer inventory. You’ve also created a list of high-intent subscribers who are warmed up to hear from you for BFCM.


## 3. Reserve something special for your VIPs on Labor Day


Your VIPs already know your brand and have shown you they'll keep coming back. They've earned something more than a sitewide discount code, and Labor Day is a good moment to reward them.


But it’s also important to remember that not all VIPs are the same. Ahead of Labor Day,[segment your most valuable customers](https://www.klaviyo.com/blog/how-to-create-a-vip-program) by purchase frequency, spend threshold, and loyalty tier to build an offer that fits each group.


Here are a few ways to target these VIP segments during Labor Day:


- Send frequent buyers an early-access code they can use 24 hours before the general sale goes live.
- Send high spenders personalized product recommendations, with a free gift on orders above their typical spend threshold.
- Give top loyalty tier members bonus points for every Labor Day purchase, moving them closer to their next reward.


### Labor Day marketing idea: Caitlyn Minimalist turns Labor Day into a VIP event


In this Labor Day marketing example, jewelry brand[Caitlyn Minimalist](https://caitlynminimalist.com/) invites subscribers to a Labor Day TikTok LIVE event featuring VIP gift boxes, flash sales, and grand prizes. The email leads with exclusivity, urgency, and a clear breakdown of what to expect: free jewelry, limited-time deals only available during the event, and a chance to win a box of 20+ pieces.

## 4. Use social proof to turn Labor Day browsers into buyers


According to Klaviyo's 2026 future of consumer marketing research, 25% of consumers say customer reviews and ratings are the top influence on their online shopping decisions, ranking above brand reputation, recommendations from friends, and social media.


Many brands default to discounts on Labor Day, but reviews can do just as much heavy lifting. They matter enough that some brands[offer discount codes](https://www.klaviyo.com/customers/case-studies/compass-coffee-reviews) in exchange for[product reviews](https://www.klaviyo.com/products/review-management) with photos.


And according to Klaviyo’s 2026[AI Consumer Trends Report](https://www.klaviyo.com/marketing-resources/ai-consumer-trends) , 41% of consumers have purchased an AI-recommended product within the past 6 months. Those recommendations are partially built on aggregated reviews that are detailed and contextual enough to answer the long-tail questions shoppers are actually asking.


If you didn’t do it last year, incentivize this year’s Labor Day customers to leave a detailed review about how your products made the weekend better for them. This is especially relevant if you sell products that are specific to the end of summer. That way, when next year rolls around, you’ll be more likely to show up in[AI search results](https://www.klaviyo.com/blog/how-to-adapt-your-online-presence-for-ai-shopping) when customers are searching for products that can help them celebrate.


When gathering reviews and social proof from happy customers, collect all of your[user-generated content](https://www.klaviyo.com/blog/user-generated-content-ugc) (UGC) in one place. This will make it easier to pull from your database next year when you want to showcase Labor Day-specific social proof in your marketing campaigns and flows.


### Labor Day marketing idea: Smile Brilliant pairs last-chance urgency with social proof


In this Labor Day marketing example, oral care brand[Smile Brilliant](https://www.smilebrilliant.com/) includes a discount, a product bundle with clear savings, and a social proof section featuring 5-star reviews from dentists, hygienists, and real customers.


In the live email, those reviews slide as a carousel. They add credibility to the offer and give shoppers detailed, contextual product information without leaving the email.

## 5. Turn saved products and browsing history into revenue


According to Klaviyo's 2026 future of consumer marketing research, the primary reason shoppers opt in to brand marketing updates is to receive exclusive discounts and promotional offers. People want to know when the products *they* browsed and like are on sale.


When customers log in to a[self-serve customer hub](https://www.klaviyo.com/products/customer-experience-hub) and save products to their wishlist, they're telling you exactly which products they’re interested in. And when that data flows back into a[unified customer profile](https://www.klaviyo.com/features/customer-profiles) in your B2C CRM, you can easily act on that information on Labor Day with personalized product recommendations.


A self-serve hub also gives shoppers a place to find answers before they commit to a Labor Day purchase, especially if it connects them to an AI customer agent that can instantly respond to simple product questions, make personalized recommendations, and escalate to a human if necessary (more on this next).


### Labor Day marketing idea: Ministry of Supply surfaces recently viewed products in self-serve hub and Labor Day sale email


Clothing brand[Ministry of Supply](https://www.ministryofsupply.com/) offers access to a self-serve hub where customers can see recently viewed items, order history with repurchase links, order tracking, and AI-powered product recommendations in a branded sidebar that sits alongside the shopping experience, without pulling shoppers away from the store.


Over one year, account holders completed over[12,000 self-serve support interactions](https://www.klaviyo.com/customers/case-studies/ministry-of-supply) , email tickets per customer started trending down, and the hub's product recommendations became a cross-selling tool that helps lift the brand’s revenue per session.


In this Labor Day marketing example, Ministry of Supply extends those principles of on-site personalization into email. The message opens with a private early-access offer for subscribers, and it closes with a "Recently viewed products" section that surfaces specific items each recipient browsed, available at 25% off with code LD25.

## 6. Answer Labor Day shopper questions instantly with an AI customer agent


Shoppers often do a lot of research before they buy, and AI is increasingly part of that process.


A[branded AI customer agent](https://www.klaviyo.com/solutions/ai/customer-agent) that's trained on your storefront and customer data can autonomously answer product FAQs and make personalized recommendations based on each shopper's browsing activity.


Over a long weekend when purchase intent is high and your team is stretched, that makes a difference. The same AI agent that can answer a sizing question can also proactively recommend products, apply promotions, and capture up-sell moments in the same conversation.


### Labor Day marketing idea: Naked Wardrobe's AI customer agent recommends personalized products in real time


In this Labor Day marketing example, apparel brand[Naked Wardrobe](https://nakedwardrobe.com/) 's AI customer agent helps a shopper find the right outfit for a Labor Day weekend wedding. It surfaces 3 product recommendations with prices and occasion-matched descriptions, then follows up with color and styling guidance and an offer to help with sizing.


Within a 90-day period, Naked Wardrobe’s AI customer agent[resolved 86% of customer queries](https://www.klaviyo.com/customers/case-studies/naked-wardrobe) and 94% of product recommendation queries autonomously. Whether a shopper is browsing for the first time or returning to the brand, the agent gives them the kind of attentive, personalized service that's hard to deliver at scale without AI.
