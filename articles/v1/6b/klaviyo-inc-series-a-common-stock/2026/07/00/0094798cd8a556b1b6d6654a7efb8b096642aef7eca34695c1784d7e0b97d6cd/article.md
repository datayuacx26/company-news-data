## Channel affinity: how K:AI chooses where to start


[Channel affinity](https://www.klaviyo.com/solutions/ai/what-is-channel-affinity) predicts the best channels for engaging each customer—email, text messaging, or mobile push—based on past behavior and preferences. That prediction is stored in each profile so you can dynamically route messages to the channels where customers are most likely to engage.


Use channel affinity in segmentation, flow filters, and conditional splits to start a message sequence in the right place and follow up across channels when you need to. Don’t use it as an excuse to shut off other channels. Consumers are[omnichannel](https://www.klaviyo.com/solutions/omnichannel) , and multiple, complementary touches often drive the best revenue.


“Channel affinity has really helped us reduce over-messaging and create opportunities to do more brand-building in extensive flows,” says Luke Styles, CRM manager at[Lorna Jane](https://www.lornajane.com/) . “I can send prominent communications in the most relevant way for every subscriber in our key flows, like post-purchase, abandoned browse, abandoned cart, and abandoned check-out.”

## Image Remix: creative at speed


[Image Remix](https://www.klaviyo.com/solutions/ai/remix) brings image editing into Klaviyo’s email editor, so you can turn a single image into many campaign-ready variations in seconds. Select any image in a template—product shot, lifestyle photo, or campaign banner—click “Remix,” and tell the AI what you want to create or change.


Remix uses Google Gemini’s latest image-editing API directly inside the editor, and supports chat-style prompting in any language. Here’s what it does:


- Generates tailored visuals in emails, like showcasing Fido when you reach out to dog lovers
- Tests and iterates without extra tools, teams, or time
- Leverages Gemini’s image enhancements directly in the editor, with chat-style prompts in any language


Common use cases include:


- Replacing or adding a product to an image
- Changing the color of a background or objects
- Changing an image’s environment
- Swapping people or models, or adjusting facial expressions
- Adding products from your catalog to an image (coming soon)


Remix was built to remove creative bottlenecks so marketers and teams with limited design bandwidth can go from idea to execution in minutes, not days.
