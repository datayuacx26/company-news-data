You don't own your Instagram followers. Meta does. Every like, comment, and DM lives on a platform you don't control, disconnected from everything else you know about that person as a customer.


At[Get Better](https://getbetter.co.uk/) , our team specializes in CRM strategy and Klaviyo-powered growth. Recently, we've been closing the gap between Instagram and our clients’ owned channels by pulling Instagram data into Klaviyo and putting it to work across[customer service](https://www.klaviyo.com/solutions/customer-service) , marketing, and[retention](https://www.klaviyo.com/blog/retention-marketing-strategies-brands) .


## Turn followers into subscribers


Instagram engagement doesn't have to stay on Instagram. With list growth tools built into Klaviyo, a comment, tag, or DM can become an actual opt-in for the different owned channels within Klaviyo, captured right where the interaction happens instead of routing someone to a separate sign-up form.


[Instagram tap-to-text](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/6294531159579) is the clearest version of this: someone messages your account and gets a fast path to opt into text message marketing, and from there they become a profile you can communicate with across channels.


[Klaviyo Social](https://www.klaviyo.com/products/social) auto-replies work the same way across email, text messaging, and WhatsApp, so the transition from "follower" to "subscriber" happens inside the conversation, not after it.


That's the same principle behind the follower count work. Once someone's opted in, you're not just capturing their consent. You're building the profile that makes everything downstream more personal.


## Give every Instagram follower a number that means something


Once someone's Instagram account is connected to their Klaviyo profile, follower count becomes a property you can act on, not just a vanity metric on someone's bio.


When customer service, review requests, and everything in between all runs through Klaviyo, you can start to talk to a customer with 200,000+ Instagram followers differently than one with 200.


That distinction pays off in two key places:


- **Customer service:** When a support ticket comes in from someone with a large following, your team sees it right away and responds accordingly. In practice, that usually means giving them the VIP treatment so they don’t air a bad experience on social media.
- **UGC and ambassador programs:** When you're sending review requests or building out[user-generated content](https://www.klaviyo.com/marketing-resources/user-generated-content-guide) (UGC) flows, a high follower count is a signal worth acting on. We recommend setting up a branch for anyone with a higher follower count to get extra incentives, or even trigger an alert so someone on your team can reach out directly and ask if they want to be a content collaborator.


All both of these moves require is that your follower data lives in the same place where you make your customer service and marketing decisions.


## Grow smarter with more personal, profitable paid ads


The same Instagram data that upgrades your customer service and UGC flows can also make your ad spend work harder. Once social engagement lives in Klaviyo alongside your purchase and email data, you can build segments around your actual highest-value customers and sync them to Meta, TikTok, Google, Pinterest, and more for[lookalike targeting](https://www.klaviyo.com/features/audience-sync) .


In addition to going after net-new customers who look like your best ones, you can also retarget the people who already know you: browsers, cart abandoners, and engaged followers. Either way, you're spending against signals you actually trust, not just platform-reported engagement.


## Put your Instagram UGC to work everywhere, not just on Instagram


The next opportunity is newer: the[Social Content Library](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/50223237602715) that syncs tagged Instagram posts and reels directly into Klaviyo, so you can use that content in email, SMS, RCS, and WhatsApp, not just in your feed.


It back-populates up to 200 previously tagged posts and Reels, then keeps syncing new ones on an ongoing basis. That way, timely, relevant content is always surfacing within the account. From there, you can drop that content into whatever channel you're already sending on.


Either way, the idea behind it holds up: the UGC your customers create on Instagram is worth more once it can travel to the channels where you're already trying to convert them.
