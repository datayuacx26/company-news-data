# How Koji Studio used Spline to deliver a 3D product showcase


Koji Studio used Spline to transform static product visuals into a scroll-based 3D product showcase, delivering a more engaging and memorable experience that highlights their client’s innovation and craft.


When Ori, a medical technology company, set out to launch a website for its new 3D dental scanner product, the challenge was clear: how do you communicate the precision and innovation of a physical device in a way that feels equally innovative online?


The company was looking to bring their new Ori Intraoral Scanner device to the market. A 3D dental scanning device designed to help dentists capture highly accurate, real-time digital models of patients’ teeth.


Looking to push beyond static images and videos to demonstrate this product’s technical advancement to their customers, they sought out KOJI Agency as the creative team for the product launch.


The KOJI team then decided to leverage the Spline integration within Webflow to craft an interactive 3D digital experience that made the scanner feel as tangible on the web as it does in a dentist’s hand.


Ori Scan's scroll-based interactive 3D website


## A full-stack design team embracing new tools


The KOJI Agency team behind Ori Scan’s web presence included Pavel, Slava, Nikita, and Liza each of whom are seasoned designers with many client projects under their belt.


With more than 20 years in branding, identity, and digital experiences, they’ve built everything from modular advertising systems to immersive narrative sites.


Their strength lies in being "full-stack designers" — blending product thinking, visual design, interaction, and code.


"We’re always researching, always trying to find new tools. That’s how we landed on Spline."


Slava Sheykin


—


Full-Stack Designer at KOJI


## Leveraging Spline to unlock interactivity


Ori Scan’s leadership didn’t just want another medical product website. They wanted an experience that would stand out to their audience of dentists and healthcare professionals.


The KOJI team saw interactive 3D as the best medium to highlight the scanner’s innovation, and the choice of Spline was equally intentional.


Website visitors rotate and explore the scanner as they move through the page


The team knew they needed the flexibility to iterate quickly, optimize performance, and present the scanner’s product designs interactively across devices.


"Spline was everywhere in the design community. This project was the perfect opportunity to finally use it and the seamless integration with Webflow made the decision even easier."


Pavel Dergachev


—


Founder & Creative Director at KOJI


## The design process: From CAD and polygon optimization to a polished web experience


The process began with Ori Scan’s technical CAD files, which the agency refined and optimized before bringing them into Spline.


The workflow was iterative — balancing polygon count, materials, and lighting to ensure a balance between a visually appealing result and smooth performance on the web.


Spline brought the technical and creative sides of the process together. Its performance tools gave the team clear insights into model optimization, ensuring a smooth integration across devices. The event system made it simple to set up interactive motion, while versatile material and lighting tools helped refine the product’s look so it felt polished and realistic on the web.


3D models and final mobile implementation


One of the standout details? The teeth 3D model. To show the scanner in action, the team modeled natural-looking teeth in Spline which created an essential touch that made the demo feel authentic.


This attention to detail elevated the demo beyond a technical showcase. By combining realism with interactivity, the team created a product story that felt authentic, engaging, and directly connected to how dentists would actually use the scanner.


"It was fun but also critical. Since the scanner’s purpose is to scan teeth, the 3D model had to look and feel natural."


Liza Grin


—


Digital Producer at KOJI


From top to bottom: Final implementation, original 3D models and initial draft


## Integrating with Webflow


The KOJI team leaned on Spline to bring the Ori Scan product to life and on Spline's native Webflow integration to implement the 3D assets into the website seamlessly.


This made it possible to pair scroll-based animations with interactive 3D allowing visitors to rotate and explore the scanner as they moved through the page.


"It’s very simple and fast. The learning curve is low, even junior designers can step in and integrate 3D into Webflow."


Pavel Dergachev

Founder & Creative Director at KOJI

By letting customers scroll through the product, rotate it, and explore its features in context, they created an experience that went far beyond what static photography or video could achieve.


"Interactivity changes everything. It makes the product feel real. And more importantly, it helps the client tell their story in a way that feels innovative."
