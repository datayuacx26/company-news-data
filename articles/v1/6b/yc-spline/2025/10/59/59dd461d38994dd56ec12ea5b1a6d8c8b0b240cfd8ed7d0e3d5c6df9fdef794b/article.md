# How Oscilar brands their AI risk platform with Spline


Oscilar uses Spline to turn complex AI decisioning concepts into interactive 3D visuals that make their technology clear, modern, and enterprise-ready.


Oscilar is an AI-native risk decisioning platform that helps fintechs and banks automate credit, fraud, and compliance workflows. Its agentic AI systems make decisioning more transparent, explainable, and accessible — a powerful yet complex product to visualize.


When Principal Brand Designer Andrew Pons joined, he was tasked with reimagining how Oscilar could communicate this intelligence visually and position the company as an innovative, enterprise-grade platform.


"Oscilar is a complex product within financial services — we deal with credit and risk decisioning. I joined with the goal of giving the brand a new style and Spline has been a big part of that."


Andrew Pons


—


Principal Brand Designer at Oscilar


Bringing Oscilar’s AI platform to life through interactive 3D design in Spline


## A modular language for intelligent systems


Andrew began by developing a modular, flexible 3D design system to reflect Oscilar’s scalable architecture.


Each element — from tile-based backdrops to layered modules to UI nodes — conveys the platform’s structured intelligence and adaptability across its four key verticals: onboarding (blue), credit (green), fraud (orange), and compliance (purple).


Using Spline, he conceptualized and designed these 3D elements. Consistent lighting, refined materials, and solid foundations gave Oscilar’s visuals a sense of precision — mirroring some of the characteristics of their AI risk decisioning platform.


3D visuals illustrating Oscilar’s scalable, modular platform


"It represents the modularity of the platform. It’s built with that meaning in mind — it gives the visuals a technical, almost computer‑like architecture vibe."


Andrew Pons


—


Principal Brand Designer at Oscilar


## Modernizing for enterprise audiences


The visual refresh was part of Oscilar’s larger strategy to position itself for enterprise adoption. The goal was to look more credible, trustworthy, and innovative in a market dominated by legacy systems and visuals.


By leveraging Spline, Andrew redefined how Oscilar’s brand communicates complexity — creating visuals that are not only engaging but also reflect the precision and innovation behind its AI risk decisioning platform.


The result is a cohesive, modern identity that helps the company stand out in a highly technical market.


3D visuals illustrating Oscilar’s platform across key financial sectors


"We wanted to appeal to an enterprise audience. That’s the part where Spline comes in. It helps the brand feel more cutting-edge than some of our competition."


Andrew Pons


—


Principal Brand Designer at Oscilar


## Visualizing the platform through 3D storytelling


Oscilar’s website uses Spline-powered interactive 3D visuals to tell the story of its intelligent risk system. A highlight is the scroll-based animation on the platform page, where each layer of their AI risk decisioning platform highlights as the user scrolls through the page.


Interactive scroll-based 3D visuals illustrating Oscilar’s layered risk intelligence platform


"A lot of people have commented that it helps them pitch the platform to prospects and explain what we do. It does make a big impact, and they feel we have a website that they can be proud of."


Andrew Pons


—


Principal Brand Designer at Oscilar


## From digital to real‑world impact


Spline’s flexibility extended beyond the web. When preparing for[Money20/20](https://www.money2020.com/about) , Andrew used it to visualize Oscilar’s event booth — designing spatial layouts and final graphics directly in 3D.


The ability to create and export production-ready, high‑resolution graphics meant that Oscilar’s 3D assets could move seamlessly across web, print, and physical installations, unifying its visual identity across every medium.


Spline 3D model of Oscilar’s booth used for visualizing layout and brand visuals


This use of Spline went beyond simple visualization — it became part of Oscilar’s collaborative design process. The 3D booth preview helped internal teams and stakeholders easily understand layout, scale, and brand visuals long before anything was physically built.


It allowed Andrew to communicate ideas quickly, align everyone around a shared vision, and speed up decision‑making on graphics and production details.


"I modeled our booth quickly in Spline. It gave everyone a sense of what it would look like and where the graphics would go. It’s definitely the coolest booth we’ve ever had."


Andrew Pons


—


Principal Brand Designer at Oscilar

Oscilar’s site is built in Framer, and Spline’s web-native nature and easy exporting options made embedding interactive scenes effortless.


"Our website is built on Framer, and being able to embed these easily on my own — there’s no other way I could create something this cool with any other tool."


Andrew Pons

Principal Brand Designer at Oscilar
