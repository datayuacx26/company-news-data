# Video Exports with Hana


Export your animated content in Hana as video


[Open Hana](http://app.spline.design/)


Since we launched Hana, video export has been one of the most requested features, and today we are shipping it. You can now export high-res videos directly from Hana with customizable settings.


## How to export videos from Spline Hana?


It's easy. Select a frame in your canvas and click "Export video" from the menu on the sidebar panel or the dropdown menu of the selected frame (represented by three dots).


The video will export any animation or transition action triggered by the Start event on your frame. You can learn more about the[export settings in our docs for Hana.](https://docs.spline.design/doc/exporting-in-hana/docqB6tzlb52)


Control the perspective, rotation, and z-position of the object in Hana


## Animating stroke trimming and dashes


We also shipped support for animated stroke trimming and dashes. This allows for more creative control when working with strokes. You can now build more complex interactions, combine them with effects, and create stunning interactive experiences that can be embedded into production or exported as video and used in your projects.
