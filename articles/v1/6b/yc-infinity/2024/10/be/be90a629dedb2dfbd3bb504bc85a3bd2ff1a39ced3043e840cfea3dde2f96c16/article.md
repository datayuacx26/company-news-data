## 4. SWIFT (Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication)


SWIFT is a global network used by financial institutions to securely exchange information and facilitate cross-border payments. It is a tried-and-tested method for Indian businesses that deal with large transactions or have international business partners.


Key Features:


Global Reach: SWIFT is accepted by banks worldwide, making it one of the most reliable methods for international transactions.


High Security: SWIFT’s stringent security protocols ensure that transactions are safe from fraud.


Large Transaction Capacity: Businesses dealing in large payments across borders often rely on SWIFT for its capacity to handle significant amounts.
