# How Startups Can Manage Liquidity During Economic Uncertainty?

If one has been following news in the Indian economy lately, he would know that it has just been a little on a rollercoaster ride. Businesses have to go through a mix of various challenges at this advent of evolving local policies and global shifts in markets. With greater stakes for startups, the resources are limited, and it's cash flow that keeps them running. Such is the turbulence that liquidity management is just not a financial task but a lifeline.


## What Liquidity Means for Startups


In simple terms, liquidity is the speed at which assets can be converted into cash to meet immediate financial requirements. To a startup, liquidity management means that even on bad days, the company can still pay its staff and suppliers. Good liquidity management enables a startup to remain agile and seize new opportunities without losing concentration on long-term objectives simply because of ups and downs in the economy.


## How Economic Uncertainty Affects Startup Liquidity


Economic uncertainty could worsen their liquidity issues. A decline in consumer spending would delay payments, credit lenders would become cautious in offering credit, and cash would dry up, eventually becoming insufficient for the timely payment of bills. For instance, many Indian startups barely managed to survive during the 2008 financial crisis because they put a tight rein on cash flow and cut all non-essential expenses.


## Key Strategies for Managing Liquidity During Economic Uncertainty


Cash Flow Management: Good liquidity means good cash flow management. This comprises effective invoicing and collections for a steady flow of cash. Follow this with trimming unnecessary operating expenses with minimal impact on core operations. Monitoring the inflows and outflows of cash will also enable the startup not to experience financial surprises.


Diversify Revenue Streams: Being dependent on one source of revenue during times when the situation is not that sure, is always a risk. Thereby, creation of new markets or products will help the startups generate other sources of income. For example, selling one's products or services online would attract newer customers from outside markets other than relying on the local markets. Diversification of the streams increases revenue and cushions the company from market volatility.


Maintain Access to Credit: Having access to credit when in need is important. A good rapport with banks and financial institutions offers a sort of financial buffer in times of need. Startups should also research any government programmes and funding opportunities that provide added support. This keeps open lines of credit, which can help the smooth operation of the business during lean cash flow months.
