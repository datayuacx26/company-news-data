## Delek US Holdings to Host First Quarter 2026 Conference Call on April 29th


04/10/2026


[Download (opens in new window) PDF 113 KB](https://s204.q4cdn.com/601669363/files/doc_news/Delek-US-Holdings-to-Host-First-Quarter-2026-Conference-Call-on-April-29th-2026.pdf)


BRENTWOOD, Tenn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Delek US Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: DK) (“Delek US”) today announced that the Company intends to issue a press release summarizing first quarter 2026 results before the U.S. stock market opens on Wednesday, April 29, 2026. A conference call to discuss these results is scheduled to begin at 9:00 a.m. CT (10:00 a.m. ET) on Wednesday, April 29, 2026.


The live broadcast of this conference call will be available online by going to[www.DelekUS.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.DelekUS.com&esheet=54492767&newsitemid=20260410266271&lan=en-US&anchor=www.DelekUS.com&index=1&md5=8e68931a461047e742bbd0855e5e99c1) and clicking on the investor relations section of the website. A presentation containing supplemental financial information will also be available online at ir.delekus.com prior to the conference call and webcast. The Company does not intend to furnish this presentation on a Current Report on Form 8-K. Investors are encouraged to review the presentation in conjunction with the Company’s earnings press release and webcast. The online replay will be available on the website for 90 days.


**About Delek US Holdings, Inc.**


Delek US Holdings, Inc. is a diversified downstream energy company with assets in petroleum refining, logistics, pipelines, and renewable fuels. The refining assets consist primarily of refineries operated in Tyler and Big Spring, Texas, El Dorado, Arkansas and Krotz Springs, Louisiana with a combined nameplate crude throughput capacity of 302,000 barrels per day.


The logistics operations include Delek Logistics Partners, LP (NYSE: DKL). Delek Logistics Partners, LP is a growth-oriented master limited partnership focused on owning and operating midstream energy infrastructure assets. Delek US Holdings, Inc. and its subsidiaries owned approximately 63.3% (including the general partner interest) of Delek Logistics Partners, LP as of March 31, 2026.


Information about Delek US Holdings, Inc. can be found on its website ([www.delekus.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.delekus.com&esheet=54492767&newsitemid=20260410266271&lan=en-US&anchor=www.delekus.com&index=2&md5=2869eb1741b5c6025cf475082cd2f307) ), investor relations webpage (ir.delekus.com), and news webpage ([www.delekus.com/news](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.delekus.com%2Fnews&esheet=54492767&newsitemid=20260410266271&lan=en-US&anchor=www.delekus.com%2Fnews&index=3&md5=9e5e625b6cbf57ec1dfa1d7bd9a4ba59) ).
