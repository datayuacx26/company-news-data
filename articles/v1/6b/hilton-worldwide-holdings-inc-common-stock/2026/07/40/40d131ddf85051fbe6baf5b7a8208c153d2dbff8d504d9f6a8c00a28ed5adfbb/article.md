# Travel + Leisure’s 2026 World’s Best Awards Spotlight Hilton’s Luxury and Lifestyle Brands and Properties

Listen to this article


0:00


Hilton strengthens its position in this award platform with more hotels, resorts, and brands featured than ever before


- **LXR Hotels & Resorts ranked No. 3 among the Best Hotel Brands in the World, with Waldorf Astoria Hotels & Resorts also earning recognition among the world’s top hotel brands.**
- **Twenty-two Hilton properties were recognized across Waldorf Astoria Hotels & Resorts, LXR Hotels & Resorts, Conrad Hotels & Resorts, Hilton Hotels & Resorts and Curio Collection by Hilton, including three No. 1 rankings.**


**MCLEAN, Va. –** The[Travel + Leisure](https://www.travelandleisure.com/worlds-best-awards-top-100-hotels-in-the-world-11952519) 2026 World’s Best Awards, one of the most prestigious awards in the travel industry and selected entirely by *Travel + Leisure* readers, once again recognized leading global hospitality brand Hilton (NYSE: HLT) with widespread accolades across numerous categories.


Twenty-two properties representing five of Hilton’s industry-leading brands –[Waldorf Astoria Hotels & Resorts](https://www.hilton.com/en/waldorf-astoria/) ,[LXR Hotels & Resorts](https://www.hilton.com/en/brands/lxr-hotels/) ,[Conrad Hotels & Resorts](https://www.hilton.com/en/conrad/) ,[Hilton Hotels & Resorts](https://www.hilton.com/en/brands/hilton-hotels/) and[Curio Collection by Hilton](https://www.hilton.com/en/brands/curio-collection/) – were recognized in the highly regarded annual list in 2026.


In a milestone achievement, two Hilton luxury brands ranked among the Top 25 Best Hotel Brands in the World:


- **LXR Hotels & Resorts, ranked No. 3** , consists of 40 trading and pipeline luxury properties in the world’s most alluring destinations. Each iconic property is set apart by its individual design, an unrivaled commitment to personalized attention, and authentic, immersive experiences that reflect the unique culture and character of each destination.
- **Waldorf Astoria Hotels & Resorts, ranked No. 22** , is a portfolio of 40 iconic properties that embodies its legacy through sincerely elegant service, one-of-a-kind experiences and a distinct sense of place.


From Waldorf Astoria Hotels & Resorts, 13 properties were recognized, including several standout achievements:


- **Waldorf Astoria Maldives Ithaafushi earned a Top 10 ranking among the Top 100 Hotels in the World** , securing the No. 9 spot in the prestigious category, along with receiving the No. 5 ranking in the Best Resorts in Asia.
- **Two recently opened Waldorf Astoria properties made their Travel + Leisure World’s Best Awards debut** , including Waldorf Astoria Costa Rica Punta Cacique, which ranked No. 5 among the 25 Best Resorts in Central America, and Waldorf Astoria New York, which ranked No. 6 among the Best Hotels in New York City.
- **Three Waldorf Astoria properties earned the No. 1 ranking in their respective categories** , including Waldorf Astoria Las Vegas, Waldorf Astoria Atlanta Buckhead and The Roosevelt New Orleans, A Waldorf Astoria Hotel.

- **Conrad Los Angeles earned the No. 6 ranking among the 10 Best Hotels in Greater Los Angeles** and **Conrad Tokyo received the No. 5 spot on the Best Hotels in Tokyo category** , highlighting the brand’s continued presence in major urban destinations.
- **Two Conrad resorts were recognized among the 25 Best Resorts in Mexico** , with **Conrad Tulum Riviera Maya ranking No. 13** and **Conrad Punta de Mita ranking No. 15** — showcasing the brand’s oceanfront retreats along Mexico’s Caribbean and Pacific coasts.

More than 207,000 *Travel + Leisure* readers completed the 2026 survey. A total of more than 661,000 votes were cast across over 10,088 properties (hotels, cities, cruise lines, etc.). Hotels were classified as either resort hotel, city hotel, inn, or safari lodge based on their location and amenities, and they were specifically rated on rooms and facilities, location, service, food, and value. For each characteristic, respondents could choose a rating of excellent, above average, average, below average, or poor. The final scores are averages of these responses. The *Travel + Leisure* World’s Best Awards will live in the August print issue of the magazine, and will remain on the publication’s website as a guide for readers making travel plans through the end of 2026 and beyond.


The 2026 World’s Best Awards list, as well as survey methodology, are currently featured[here](https://www.travelandleisure.com/worlds-best-awards-top-100-hotels-in-the-world-11952519) .
