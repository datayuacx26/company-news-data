# LivSmart Studios by Hilton Pensacola I-10 North Davis Highway Opens in Pensacola, Florida

**PENSACOLA, Fla.** –[LivSmart Studios by Hilton Pensacola I-10 North Davis Highway](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/pnsndey-livsmart-studios-pensacola-i-10-north-davis-hwy/) is now open, bringing Hilton’s newest long-stay brand to Florida’s Gulf Coast. Located at 7077 Plantation Road, the hotel is owned by 3H Group and managed by LBA Hospitality.


Designed for guests staying 10 nights or longer, LivSmart Studios by Hilton delivers a thoughtfully designed long-stay experience that combines comfort and functionality with the consistency, value and hospitality travelers expect when they stay with Hilton. As the fourth LivSmart Studios property to open, this hotel reflects the brand’s continued momentum and growing presence across the country, with many more locations slated to open this year and beyond.


LivSmart Studios by Hilton - Lobby Rendering


The Pensacola hotel is the second LivSmart Studios property opened by 3H Group and managed by LBA Hospitality, following the opening of LivSmart Studios Tullahoma in 2025 as the brand’s very first hotel. The companies are also preparing to open a LivSmart Studios property in Huntsville, Alabama, later this year.


“3H Group and LBA Hospitality have built valuable experience introducing new Hilton brands and operating hotels designed for today’s extended-stay traveler,” said Beau Benton, president, LBA Hospitality. “Pensacola’s year-round demand from military, higher education, healthcare, business and leisure travelers makes this an excellent market for LivSmart Studios, and we are proud to continue growing the brand alongside 3H Group and Hilton.”


LivSmart Studios by Hilton - King Guest Room - Rendering


Through thoughtful design, everyday convenience and consistent value, guests can seamlessly maintain their routines while immersing themselves in the local community. LivSmart Studios by Hilton Pensacola I-10 North Davis Highway features the brand’s signature apartment-style studios with fully equipped kitchens, including full-size refrigerators, stovetops and microwaves. Additional amenities include complimentary breakfast, free Wi-Fi and parking, a fitness center, guest laundry facilities, electric vehicle charging and pet-friendly accommodations.


Conveniently located near I-10 and I-110, the hotel offers easy access to Pensacola International Airport, the University of West Florida, Naval Air Station Pensacola, major healthcare providers, downtown Pensacola and Pensacola Beach.


LivSmart Studios by Hilton - Guest Laundry - Rendering


The hotel is led by General Manager Cody Beck, with Dual Sales Manager Shayla Fisher supporting sales efforts throughout the Pensacola and Mobile markets. “Our team is excited to welcome guests to LivSmart Studios and provide a comfortable, practical home base for anyone spending more time in Pensacola,” said Cody Beck, general manager, LivSmart Studios by Hilton Pensacola I-10 North Davis Highway. “This market welcomes visitors throughout the year, and our hotel is designed to make longer stays feel easier.”


The hotel is part of[Hilton Honors](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/) , the award-winning guest loyalty program for Hilton’s 28 distinct hotel brands. Hilton Honors members who book directly through preferred Hilton channels have access to instant benefits, including flexible Points and money payment options, exclusive member discounts and complimentary standard Wi-Fi. Members also enjoy popular digital tools available exclusively through the Hilton Honors mobile app, including digital check-in, room selection and Digital Key.
