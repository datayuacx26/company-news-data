# Hilton Continues Rapid Lifestyle Growth in South East Asia with Trio of Signings in Thailand and the Maldives

Listen to this article


0:00


New signings in Phuket, Chiang Mai, and the Maldives add to Hilton’s Asia‑Pacific luxury and lifestyle presence as the company progresses toward exceeding 250 properties in the segments in the region


- **Hilton signs three new hotels, marking its first Tapestry Collection by Hilton hotel in the Maldives and expanding its presence in Phuket and Chiang Mai with its award-winning lifestyle brands, Curio Collection by Hilton and Tapestry Collection by Hilton** .
- **Signings support Hilton’s ambition of surpassing 250 luxury and lifestyle hotels in Asia Pacific, reinforcing strong momentum in the segment** .
- **Expansion reflects continued confidence from owners and partners in Hilton’s brands and performance in the region** .


**SINGAPORE** –[Hilton](https://www.hilton.com/en/corporate/) (NYSE: HLT) today announced the signing of three lifestyle properties, including a[Curio Collection by Hilton](https://www.hilton.com/en/brands/curio-collection/) hotel in Phuket and two[Tapestry Collection by Hilton](https://www.hilton.com/en/brands/tapestry-collection/) properties in Chiang Mai and the Maldives, further expanding the company’s lifestyle brand portfolio in South East Asia. The hotels in Thailand will join a growing lifestyle portfolio that includes the recently opened[Kahavadi Chiang Rai, Curio Collection by Hilton](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/ceiqqqq-kahavadi-chiang-rai/) , as well as[Canopy Bangkok Sukhumvit](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/bkkthpy-canopy-bangkok-sukhumvit/) and[Nivata Koh Samui, Tapestry Collection by Hilton](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/usmupup-nivata-koh-samui/) , both of which are set to debut this year.


The signings underscore Hilton’s growth momentum as it continues to close in on its goal of surpassing 250 luxury and lifestyle properties across Asia Pacific. The new additions in Thailand and the Maldives will also expand Hilton’s footprint in South East Asia, where the company plans to more than double its luxury and lifestyle portfolio amid the growing demand for distinctive, experience-driven stays in sought-after destinations.


Alexandra Murray, vice president and regional head of South East Asia said, “Thailand and the Maldives have long been lifestyle powerhouses in South East Asia, and these signings provide new opportunities to showcase our independent-spirited brands. Tapestry Collection and Curio Collection are a natural fit for these destinations where guests seek not just accommodation, but experiences rooted in place. These signings reinforce our commitment to bringing the right brands to the right markets, as we continue to grow our lifestyle presence across key destinations.”


## Curio Collection by Hilton Arrives in Phuket


Andamanda Voyage, Curio Collection by Hilton - Rendering


Located within the Andamanda Waterpark complex in Kathu District, **Andamanda Voyage, Curio Collection by Hilton,** scheduled to debut at the end of 2026, will set a new benchmark for resort hospitality in one of Thailand's most popular beach destinations. The 246-key resort marks the Curio Collection brand's debut in Phuket, joining a global portfolio of over 200 Curio Collection properties, each designed for travelers seeking distinctive, high-end stays in sought-after destinations and locally inspired design that evokes each property’s unique character. It joins Hilton's growing presence alongside The Rawai Phuket, set to open in 2027 as Hilton's first[LXR Hotels & Resorts](https://stories.hilton.com/brands/lxr-hotels) -branded resort, a nature-immersive property on the southern tip of the island.


Guests will enjoy direct access to Andamanda's integrated retail and dining village, with Central Phuket's shopping complexes just moments away. The resort's central location also places Phuket's most sought-after experiences within easy reach, from the historic streets of Phuket Old Town and the white sands of Patong and Karon to world-famous attractions such as Phuket Fantasea and Carnival Magic.


## Tapestry Collection by Hilton Set to Debut in Chiang Mai


Chiang Mai, celebrated for its vibrant Lanna food culture, will welcome the city’s first Tapestry Collection by Hilton property. It is set to join more than 200 Tapestry Collection hotels worldwide, where each hotel’s story comes to life through elevated design and locally inspired food & beverage experiences.


Situated in the heart of the city’s storied Old Town – a UNESCO-recognized area encircled by ancient moats and home to over 300 ornate temples – the property will be set within a heritage building that reflects the artistry of Northern Thai architecture. With 65 carefully curated rooms, it embodies the brand’s vibrant, independent spirit and strong sense of place. Just minutes from key cultural landmarks, historic temples, weekend walking streets, and local markets, the property places guests at the center of the city’s rich experiences.


## Tapestry Collection by Hilton to Make Maldives Debut


Coco de Mer Maldives, Tapestry Collection by Hilton - Rendering


The Maldives, one of the world's most sought-after island destinations, is set to welcome its first Tapestry Collection property. **Coco de Mer Maldives, Tapestry Collection by Hilton,** scheduled to open in June 2027, will introduce the brand's vibrant, independent spirit through a 75-key resort, offering travelers an authentic Maldivian escape that combines premium experiences with greater accessibility.


Conveniently located just 25 minutes by speedboat from Malé, the property provides seamless access from Velana International Airport. Guests can look forward to thoughtfully curated dining concepts, a wellness center dedicated to relaxation and rejuvenation, and a range of recreational amenities, from a dedicated sea sports center to pickleball courts.


“We continue to see strong interest from owners in South East Asia for lifestyle brands, supported by solid market fundamentals and evolving traveler demand. These signings allow us to broaden our presence in high-demand destinations, where we see clear opportunities to scale our award-winning brands alongside trusted local partners and unlock new opportunities in both resort and cultural destinations,” said Maria Ariizumi, vice president, development, South East Asia, Hilton.


Curio Collection and Tapestry Collection hotels participate in[Hilton Honors](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/) , the award-winning guest loyalty program for Hilton’s 28 distinct hotel brands. Hilton Honors members who book directly through preferred Hilton channels have access to instant benefits, including flexible Points and money payment options, exclusive member discounts and complimentary standard Wi-Fi. Members also enjoy popular digital tools available exclusively through the Hilton Honors mobile app, including digital check-in, room selection and Digital Key.
