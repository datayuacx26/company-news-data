# 5 Must-See Experiences in Richmond, England: London’s Most Charming Village Escape

- Soak up local hotspots like The Prince's Head pub and stroll through charming streets like Paved Court, filled with cafés, shops and the classic red telephone box.

Take a short train ride from the hustle and bustle of London and discover an enchanting village filled with leafy lanes, riverside pubs and expansive green spaces. This is Richmond, a picture-perfect getaway that’s perfectly positioned between central London and Hampton Court Palace. Here, travelers can discover a Thames-front pocket of southwest London that offers royal heritage and small-town charm.


Richmond looks like it’s straight out of a jolly TV show, with its cozy neighborhood pub beckoning visitors to step inside and savor a pint with the locals. Saunter down quaint side streets, snap a pic with an unmistakable red telephone box and wander through cinematic scenes that bring an authentic feel to any English escape.


Looking for even more reasons to believe? Here are five can’t-miss experiences that capture the true spirit of Richmond.


### 1. Wander the Wild Beauty of Richmond Park


Richmond Park - Photo Credit: Marco Bongera/Shutterstock


Spanning more than 2,500 acres, Richmond Park is one of the area’s largest and most breathtaking green spaces. Stroll and take in a setting that feels worlds away from the city, with rolling hills, mature trees and wide-open meadows creating a peaceful landscape that encourages even the most frazzled jetsetter to breathe in the fresh air and relax.


Enjoy a slow morning walk or bike ride followed by a picnic under the trees. And keep an eye out for the park’s famous deer herds, which roam freely and add a magical, storybook quality to the setting. For the best views, head to King Henry’s Mound, where a protected sightline offers a perfectly framed glimpse of St Paul's Cathedral in the distance.


### 2. Sip and Stay Awhile at a Famous Local Pub


DoubleTree by Hilton London Kingston Upon Thames - Fish & Chips from Hawkers Bar and Brasserie


Richmond’s pub scene is as cozy as it is iconic, and there’s one particular spot that’s become a pilgrimage for anyone seeking famous filming locations. TV fans flock to The Prince's Head, tucked along the village green, which serves as the exterior for a fictional neighborhood pub that’s now instantly recognizable.


Inside, the pub is filled with warm wood and classic British fare abounds on the menu of comfort food favorites. For anyone hoping to soak in the buzz of Richmond in a welcoming atmosphere, this is the quintessential place to be to enjoy a pint and a relaxed meal.


### 3. Find the Street, the Shopfront and the Telephone Box


Red Telephone Box - Photo Credit: Claudio Divizia/Shutterstock


The Prince’s Head is a must-visit, but it’s not the only real-life Richmond location that’s become a star in its own right. Visitors are equally drawn to village hotspots like Paved Court, an Instagram-worthy cobbled lane lined with independent shops and cafés. And keep an eye out for that classic red telephone box, which is an unmistakable English icon.


Beyond Paved Court, the surrounding streets also provide a captivating setting for an afternoon amble. Come, explore and be sure to bring a camera! Every corner feels like a scene waiting to happen.


### 4. Stroll the Thames and Take in the View


River Thames


The River Thames is the heart of Richmond, and the riverside here is among the most picturesque in all of southwest London. Tree-lined paths, historic buildings and bobbing boats create an enticingly serene setting.


Walk from Richmond Bridge toward Petersham Meadows for a quieter stroll or settle in at one of the many waterside cafés and pubs to watch the world drift by for an unforgettable day along the banks.


### 5. Step Into Royal History at Richmond Green & Beyond


Hampton Court Palace


Richmond’s roots run deep, and Richmond Green is the perfect place to feel that history. Surrounded by elegant Georgian buildings, this open green space has long been a gathering place for locals and travelers alike.


From here, it’s easy to explore even more history. Browse boutiques filled with storied treasures, discover hidden gardens with flowers that have bloomed for ages or continue onwards towards nearby Hampton Court Palace for an even deeper dive into England’s royal past.
