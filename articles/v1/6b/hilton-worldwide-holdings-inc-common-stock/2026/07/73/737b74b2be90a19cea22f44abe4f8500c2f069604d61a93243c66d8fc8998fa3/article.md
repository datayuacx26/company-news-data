# From the Racetrack to the Red Carpet, How Hilton Honors Experiences Enhances The Stay

For more than a decade,[Hilton Honors Experiences](https://experiences.hiltonhonors.com/) has offered Hilton Honors members the opportunity to turn their Points into unforgettable moments. The program has grown to provide opportunities to access exclusive packages and once-in-a-lifetime events that span sports, music, entertainment, food and culture.


According to[Hilton’s 2026 Trends Report](https://stories.hilton.com/2026-trends) , nearly half (48%) of Hilton Honors members say they use Points to try activities outside of their comfort zone. Hilton Honors Experiences offers an opportunity to explore something new, whether that's falling in love with an unexpected hobby, cuisine or culture. From global sporting events to intimate celebrity meet-and-greets and cultural celebrations, Hilton Honors Experiences unlocks access to the passions, people and moments that make travel even more memorable.

### Unlocking Unique Stay Experiences


With packages that range in the number of Points needed, from attending a sold-out concert with an artist meet-and-greet to enjoying a uniquely curated dinner or premium spa experience during a hotel stay, members can choose rewards that reflect how they live and travel.


In addition to access to highly sought out experiences, Hilton Honors Experiences also spotlights and unlocks access to unique stay experiences, with many focused on dining or premium wellness offerings. Standout experiences include:


- [Cocktails Under the Sea at Conrad Maldives Rangali Island](https://experiences.hiltonhonors.com/cocktails-under-the-sea-at-conrad-maldives-rangali/isynmv1/aucd/3426220) , one of the most-popular on-property redemptions, available at any time for 25,000 Points, includes light canapes and champagne while sitting five meters below the surface of the Indian Ocean.
- [110-Minute Signature Moon Phase Massage at Waldorf Astoria Spa Los Cabos Pedregal](https://experiences.hiltonhonors.com/110-minute-signature-treatment-at-waldorf-astoria-spa/isynmv1/aucd/5692042) , available for 150,000 Points, includes a seaweed and eucalyptus salt scrub, seaweed wrap and deep tissue massage.

## About Hilton Honors


[Hilton Honors](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors-rewards-program/)


is the award-winning guest loyalty program for Hilton’s world-class brands comprising 9,400 properties in 144 countries and territories, with more than 260 million members. Hilton Honors members who book directly through preferred Hilton channels have access to instant benefits – from the Points & Money slider and exclusive member discounts to no blackout dates and the Fifth Night Free perk on reward stays booked with all Points. Members can earn and redeem Points for free nights, purchases on Amazon, exclusive experiences and charitable contributions, including through select[co-branded credit cards](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/credit-cards/) . Members also have access to contactless technology exclusively through the industry-leading[Hilton Honors app](https://www.hilton.com/en/p/hilton-honors-mobile-app/) , where they can check in, choose and access their room using Digital Key.
