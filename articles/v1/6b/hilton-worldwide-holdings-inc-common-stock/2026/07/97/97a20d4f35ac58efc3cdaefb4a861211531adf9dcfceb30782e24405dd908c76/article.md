# Hilton and ResortPass Expand Successful Partnership as Demand for Wellness and Day Experiences Continues to Grow

Listen to this article


0:00


Expanded collaboration builds on Hilton’s early leadership in the day‑access space and introduces “Off Hours,” a new multi‑city pool party series designed to champion wellness and play across Hilton properties


- **Hilton is expanding its partnership with ResortPass for three years, scaling day access to Hilton properties across the Americas and strengthening its leadership in wellness and day hospitality.**
- **The collaboration introduces “Off Hours,” a new multi-city poolside event series featuring DJs, local vendors, and elevated F&B programming, debuting in select Florida Hilton properties.**
- **The expansion reflects rising consumer demand for flexible, wellness-driven day experiences, including spa, fitness, and resort access without an overnight stay.**


**MCLEAN, Va. –**[Hilton](https://www.hilton.com/en/) **** (NYSE: HLT) today announced a three-year expansion of its longstanding partnership with[ResortPass](https://www.resortpass.com/) , the leading provider of day-access experiences, further strengthening Hilton’s position as a leader in the growing day hospitality and wellness space. Building on years of collaboration and continued guest demand, the expanded partnership will scale access to Hilton’s award-winning resort and wellness amenities across the Americas, offering locals and travelers alike day access to pools, spas, fitness centers, private cabanas and curated resort experiences designed to help guests relax, recharge and connect without an overnight stay.


As one of the earliest global hospitality companies to embrace day-access experiences at scale, Hilton has continued to evolve its offerings in response to shifting traveler and consumer preferences. What began as a convenience-driven offering has increasingly become part of consumers’ broader wellness and lifestyle routines, with growing interest in restorative experiences close to home. In particular, spa and wellness offerings have emerged as a key driver of demand, prompting several Hilton properties to expand their day-use wellness and experiential programming.


“Hilton has long been a leader in spa and wellness innovation,” said Jessica Shea, vice president, wellness, retail & leisure operations, Americas, Hilton. “The success of our ResortPass partnership has encouraged us to expand our offerings and accelerate how we approach wellness across our portfolio. We can now better connect our properties more meaningfully to the communities around them, offering flexible access to world-class wellness experiences.”


Hilton and ResortPass Off Hours Event Series


In celebration of the expanded partnership, Hilton and ResortPass introduced a new “Off Hours Event Series,” a poolside series of events, designed to transform underutilized pool hours into curated, high-energy ticketed experiences. Reflecting both companies’ shared commitment to helping people reclaim balance through accessible opportunities to rest, recharge, escape and play, the series reimagines hotel spaces as dynamic community gathering places. Featuring top DJs, curated local vendors and elevated food and beverage offerings, the series creates memorable moments for both hotel guests and locals alike. The series is making its debut with a collection of events across Florida, including activations at[Hilton Orlando](https://www.resortpass.com/hotels/hilton-orlando?date=any) ,[Hilton West Palm Beach](https://www.resortpass.com/hotels/hilton-west-palm-beach?date=any) ,[Beach House Fort Lauderdale, A Hilton Resort,](https://www.resortpass.com/hotels/beach-house-fort-lauderdale-a-hilton-resort?date=any)[Hilton Tampa Downtown](https://www.resortpass.com/hotels/hilton-tampa-downtown?date=any) and[Signia by Hilton Diplomat Beach Resort](https://www.resortpass.com/hotels/the-diplomat-beach-resort?date=any) .


While “Off Hours” brings a vibrant social element to the partnership, Hilton properties are also redefining how guests can make the most of a day away. Designed to transform a few free hours into something extraordinary, participating Hilton properties offer curated day experiences that spotlight their standout amenities, from sun-drenched resort pools and chef-driven dining to immersive spa therapies. Together, Hilton and ResortPass are redefining how a single day can deliver restorative wellness:


At[Waldorf Astoria Atlanta Buckhead](https://www.resortpass.com/hotels/waldorf-astoria-atlanta-buckhead) , day guests enjoy access to the indoor 60-foot saline lap pool and vitality whirlpool, dry saunas, steam rooms, along with seamless poolside food and beverage service. For those seeking a more indulgent escape, elevated experiences include HydraFacial treatments, immersive floating sound bath therapy, revitalizing yoga classes, and a curated selection of wellness-focused offerings.


At[Hilton Orlando](https://www.resortpass.com/hotels/hilton-orlando) , families can float the 892-foot lazy river, complete with waterfalls, fountains and water cannons, or enjoy the Splash Pad’s interactive spray features designed for younger guests. For a quieter retreat, the adults-only pool area features its own whirlpool along with private cabana rentals for a more secluded day in the sun. Beyond the pools, guests can unwind or play with experiences like the Topgolf Swing Suite, where entertainment and friendly competition come together in a lively social setting. Those seeking relaxation can retreat to the spa for treatments including HydraFacials, couples massages and more, creating a stay that balances energy, leisure and resort-style relaxation.


For a more active coastal escape,[Hilton Santa Barbara Beachfront Resort](https://www.resortpass.com/hotels/hilton-santa-barbara-beachfront-resort?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=23506635542&utm_content=193790142873&utm_term=resortpass&ad=794723836957&src=g&aw_dev=c&aw_target=kwd-333581262857&aw_placement=&gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=23506635542&gbraid=0AAAAAD1gvlR2NEeyMM_ChMJoyvv_dketC&gclid=CjwKCAiAwNDMBhBfEiwAd7ti1I9wgHKDw1bMz5atOVdBvvSjCWjRvA3B-qAUUUUH57F-a2eyZPIbZhoCy4QQAvD_BwE) offers day access to pickleball courts paired with pool amenities and Pacific Ocean views, blending recreation and relaxation in one seamless experience.


Those seeking a revitalizing escape can book day access at[La Prairie Spa at Waldorf Astoria Beverly Hills](https://www.resortpass.com/hotels/la-prairie-spa-at-waldorf-astoria-beverly-hills) , a Forbes Five-Star sanctuary that blends Swiss precision with the refined tranquility of Beverly Hills. Guests can unwind in serene lounges before or after treatments, extending the spa experience.


ResortPass also unlocks daytime access to tropical getaways, including[Embassy Suites by Hilton Dorado del Mar Beach Resort](https://www.resortpass.com/hotels/embassy-suites-dorado-del-mar-beach-resort)[.](https://www.resortpass.com/hotels/embassy-suites-dorado-del-mar-beach-resort) Located along Puerto Rico’s northern coast, this beachfront resort offers day guests access to a lagoon-style outdoor pool and hot tub overlooking the Atlantic Ocean, as well as a dedicated children’s pool. Guests can spend the day building sandcastles along the shore, book a deep tissue massage or enjoy signature pool party events.


For those seeking an active Wine Country escape,[DoubleTree by Hilton Hotel Sonoma Wine Country](https://www.resortpass.com/hotels/doubletree-by-hilton-sonoma-wine-country) **** offers day access to its pickleball courts through ResortPass. Guests can reserve a court for two hours and, if needed, rent equipment sets that include two paddles and two balls, making it easy to enjoy one of the region’s fastest-growing sports. To enhance the experience, guests can also take advantage of food and beverage packages, perfect for post-match refreshments or a leisurely afternoon by the pool. Paired with the hotel’s amenities and scenic Sonoma surroundings, the experience blends recreation and relaxation in one seamless day.


“ResortPass and Hilton are setting a new standard for how hospitality innovates on well-being beyond the traditional stay. The expansion of this partnership reflects a shared commitment to redefining how people rest and recharge,” said Michael Wolf, CEO, ResortPass.
