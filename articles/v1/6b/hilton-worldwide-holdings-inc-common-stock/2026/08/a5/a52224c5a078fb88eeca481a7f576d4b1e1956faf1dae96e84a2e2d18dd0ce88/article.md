## **Investing in Team Members**


As Hilton grows across Latin America and the Caribbean, our goal is to ensure team members have opportunities to grow too. Hilton is a place where people come for a job and stay for a career. Across Latin America, team members have access to meaningful development opportunities through initiatives such as Fun & Growth, cross-training experiences, Hilton University and leadership development programs that help them build skills, gain new experiences and advance their careers. According to internal Hilton survey data from team members across Latin America:


- **85% say that they can achieve their career goals at Hilton**


- **88% say they have opportunities to continually learn and grow**


- **93% say Hilton is a great place to work, while 94% say they are proud to work there**


At Hilton, nearly two-thirds of corporate and hotel leadership roles globally have been filled by internal candidates, with many coming to Hilton for a job, but staying for a career.


Regional programs like **Fun & Growth** bring Hilton's commitment to opportunity to life, giving team members the chance to visit another Hilton property while traveling and participate in cross-training experiences that foster professional development, create cultural exchange and networking opportunities across the region.


For Isac Thiago, a pastry team member from Hilton Barra Rio de Janeiro, growth started with an opportunity to try something new. Through Hilton's Fun & Growth program, Isac traveled to Hilton Bogotá in Colombia, giving him the chance to develop professionally while experiencing a new culture and expanding his perspective on hospitality.


During his time in Bogotá, Isac worked alongside experienced pastry professionals to deepen his expertise in laminated pastry techniques while learning from a new team and operating environment. But some of the most meaningful lessons happened outside the kitchen. He explored Colombian culture, experienced new places, and built friendships that continue today.


The experience also inspired him to continue investing in his future. After returning home, Isac enrolled in English classes, recognizing that language skills could open additional opportunities throughout Hilton's global network.
