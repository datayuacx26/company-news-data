# Hilton Ramps Up Growth Across Ireland with DoubleTree by Hilton Ashbourne Signing

Listen to this article


0:00


New signing brings DoubleTree by Hilton brand to Republic of Ireland and follows opening of Home2 Suites by Hilton Dublin City Centre


- **Hilton is accelerating growth across Ireland with a series of openings, signings and brand debuts.**
- **DoubleTree by Hilton will launch in the Republic of Ireland with the signing of DoubleTree by Hilton Ashbourne.**
- **Recent milestones include the European debut of Home2 Suites by Hilton in Dublin and Hilton's first hotel signing in Cork as part of the Tapestry Collection by Hilton brand.**
- **Hilton's expansion plans are expected to create more than 150 jobs across Ireland.**


**DUBLIN –**[Hilton](https://www.hilton.com/en/) (NYSE: HLT) is expanding its presence in Ireland with a range of signings, openings and brand debuts, marking a significant step in its growth and building on its existing portfolio of eight hotels across six brands. The upcoming openings will create more than 150 jobs and will bring the number of team members working at Hilton hotels in Ireland to nearly 1,000.


DoubleTree by Hilton Ashbourne


The growth is boosted by an agreement to bring DoubleTree by Hilton to Ireland with the conversion of a 156-room hotel in Ashbourne, near Dublin. Expected to open as DoubleTree by Hilton Ashbourne in early 2027 *,* the hotel will undergo a comprehensive refurbishment, including upgrades to guest rooms, public spaces, restaurants and bars. Located 25 minutes from Dublin Airport, the property is well placed for business and leisure demand, further strengthening Hilton’s presence in Greater Dublin.


Richard Mc Dermott, proprietor of DoubleTree by Hilton Ashbourne, said, “Partnering with Hilton marks an exciting next step for the hotel. Alongside a refreshed guest experience, it will help create employment opportunities and deliver long-term value for the local area. Backed by Hilton’s commercial strength and global loyalty programme, we’re confident the hotel will become a go-to destination for guests visiting Dublin and beyond.”


Home2 Suites by Hilton Dublin City Centre


The signing follows the opening of[Home2 Suites by Hilton Dublin City Centre](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/dubbsht-home2-suites-dublin-city-centre/) , marking the brand’s European debut. The 290-suite property introduces Hilton’s fast-growing extended-stay concept to the region, offering apartment-style accommodation designed for both short and longer stays. Located just moments from O’Connell Street, the hotel caters to the growing demand for flexible, home-like accommodation among both business and leisure travellers.


Paul Blackmore, vice president, development, UK & Ireland, Hilton, said, “Ireland’s enduring appeal makes it an attractive market for Hilton, with strong demand in cities and across the regions. The opening of Home2 Suites, alongside new signings including DoubleTree by Hilton Ashbourne, and continued expansion of brands such as Tapestry Collection by Hilton, underlines our confidence in the country. We remain focused on expanding our footprint and working with owners and partners to bring the right brands to the right locations while supporting tourism and long-term investment.”


Cork City Centre, Tapestry Collection by Hilton


Alongside these developments, Hilton recently signed[Cork City Centre, Tapestry Collection by Hilton](https://stories.hilton.com/emea/releases/hilton-strengthens-uk-lifestyle-portfolio-with-two-new-tapestry-collection-by-hilton-hotels) . Expected to open in 2027 in partnership with JMK Group, the 103-room hotel will be located on South Terrace in the heart of Cork and will feature a mix of standard guest rooms and extended-stay accommodation, alongside a restaurant, bar and fitness facilities. The signing marks Hilton’s debut in Cork and builds on the growth of its lifestyle portfolio across the UK and Ireland.


Hilton is also expanding its presence across Northern Ireland with a number of landmark projects spanning multiple brands. In Portrush, Hilton celebrated the one-year anniversary since the opening of[The Marcus Portrush, Tapestry Collection by Hilton](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/ldyldup-the-marcus-portrush/?_gl=1*t53dwy*_gcl_au*NTU1OTIxODQ5LjE3ODAzMDQzNTc.) , which marked the brand debut of Tapestry Collection in Northern Ireland. It also expanded its DoubleTree portfolio through the signings of[DoubleTree by Hilton Belfast City Ten Square](https://stories.hilton.com/releases/doubletree-by-hilton-to-debut-in-belfast-city-centre) and[DoubleTree by Hilton Derry‑Londonderry.](https://stories.hilton.com/emea/releases/doubletree-by-hilton-to-debut-in-derry-londonderry)


Looking ahead, Hilton is set to debut another brand in Northern Ireland –[Tempo by Hilton Belfast City Centre](https://stories.hilton.com/emea/releases/belfast-set-to-welcome-europes-first-tempo-by-hilton-hotel) . Expected to be the first Tempo by Hilton opening outside of the United States, the 144-room lifestyle hotel will bring Hilton’s wellness-focused brand to international markets for the first time, representing a significant milestone in the brand’s global growth strategy.


##### FAQ: Hilton Ramps Up Growth Across Ireland with DoubleTree by Hilton Ashbourne Signing


Ireland continues to be an attractive market for Hilton, with strong demand from both business and leisure travellers. The company sees significant opportunities to expand its portfolio across key cities and regional destinations while supporting tourism, employment and investment.


Hilton is highlighting a number of recent milestones in Ireland, including:


- Continued growth across Northern Ireland through multiple brand expansions.
- The signing of DoubleTree by Hilton Ashbourne.
- The opening of Home2 Suites by Hilton Dublin City Centre.
- The signing of Cork City Centre, Tapestry Collection by Hilton.


DoubleTree by Hilton Ashbourne will be a 156-room hotel located near Dublin. The property is expected to open in early 2027 following a refurbishment programme that will enhance guest rooms, public areas and food and beverage offerings. It will be the first DoubleTree by Hilton hotel in the Republic of Ireland.


The hotel is located in Ashbourne, County Meath, approximately 25 minutes from Dublin Airport, providing convenient access for both domestic and international travellers.


Home2 Suites by Hilton Dublin City Centre is a 290-suite hotel that marked the European debut of Hilton's extended-stay brand. Located close to O'Connell Street, it offers apartment-style accommodation designed for guests staying for both short and extended periods.


Cork City Centre, Tapestry Collection by Hilton is a 103-room hotel expected to open in 2027. Developed in partnership with JMK Group, the hotel will feature guest rooms and extended-stay accommodation, alongside a restaurant, bar and fitness facilities. The signing marks Hilton's first hotel project in Cork.


The planned openings and signings are expected to create more than 150 jobs across Ireland, supporting local economies and increasing Hilton's team member population in the country to nearly 1,000 people.


Hilton currently has eight hotels operating across six brands in Ireland, with additional hotels and brand debuts planned through its development pipeline.


Alongside expansion in the Republic of Ireland, Hilton continues to grow in Northern Ireland through The Marcus Portrush, Tapestry Collection by Hilton, the signings of DoubleTree by Hilton Belfast City Ten Square and DoubleTree by Hilton Derry-Londonderry, and the forthcoming opening of Tempo by Hilton Belfast City Centre, which is expected to be the brand's first hotel outside the United States.


The latest openings and signings demonstrate Hilton's commitment to growing its portfolio across Ireland through a diverse mix of brands, serving a wide range of traveller needs while strengthening its presence in key destinations throughout the island.
