# Hilton Hotels & Resorts Ranks No. 1 Most Valuable Hotel Brand for the 11th Consecutive Year According to Brand Finance

Listen to this article


0:00


Nine of Hilton’s brands are included in the annual Hotels 50 report, with Hilton Hotels & Resorts’ brand value increasing significantly in 2026


- **Hilton Hotels & Resorts ranked the world's most valuable hotel brand by Brand Finance for the 11th consecutive year, with a brand value of $19.2 billion.**
- **Hilton was one of only two hotel brands included in the Brand Finance Global 500 2026 ranking and climbed more than 30 positions from last year’s ranking.**
- **Nine Hilton brands were recognized in the 2026 Brand Finance Hotels 50 ranking, underscoring the strength of Hilton's growing brand portfolio.**


[Hilton Hotels & Resorts](https://www.hilton.com/en/brands/hilton-hotels/) is ranked No. 1 Hotel Brand for Brand Value for the 11th year in a row by Brand Finance, topping the[Hotels 50 ranking](https://brandfinance.com/press-releases/hilton-hotels-resorts-checks-in-as-the-worlds-most-valuable-hotel-brand-for-11-consecutive-years) with a brand value of $19.2 billion, representing a 28% increase from 2025. Eight additional Hilton brands are included, reinforcing the strength of Hilton’s portfolio.


- Hilton Hotels & Resorts
- Hampton by Hilton
- DoubleTree by Hilton
- Embassy Suites by Hilton
- Home2 Suites by Hilton
- Waldorf Astoria Hotels & Resorts
- Conrad Hotels & Resorts
- Homewood Suites by Hilton
- Curio Collection by Hilton


Each year, Brand Finance conducts the world's largest study on brand valuation for 5,000 of the biggest brands across all industries and publishes more than 100 reports, ranking brands across all sectors and countries. Hilton is also one of only two hotel brands included in the overall[Brand Finance Global 500 2026 report](https://brandirectory.com/reports/global) and climbed more than 30 positions from the previous year.


Hilton Key West Resort Marina


“As Hilton’s flagship brand, we are honored to once again be recognized by Brand Finance as the world’s most valuable hotel brand,” said Leonard Gooz, global brand leader, Hilton Hotels & Resorts. "For more than a century, Hilton Hotels & Resorts has evolved alongside travelers, continually re-defining the stay experience while staying true to the hospitality that defines our brand. From energized spaces and memorable culinary experiences to intuitive amenities and locally inspired design, we remain focused on creating stays that feel relevant to today's travelers while helping them connect more deeply with their destination and make the most of their stay.”


The recognition comes as Hilton Hotels & Resorts continues to evolve to meet changing traveler expectations. Brand Finance notes that travelers are increasingly prioritizing quality, personalized and experience-led travel, with leading hotel brands investing in innovation and customer experience to strengthen their competitive position. Across its global portfolio, Hilton Hotels & Resorts is focused on creating more meaningfully connected stays through refreshed public spaces, distinctive food and beverage offerings, thoughtful design and experiences that reflect the character of each destination.


Hilton Burau Bay Langkawi Resort


Brand Finance notes in their Global 500 2026 ranking that, in today's environment, global hotel brands are increasingly driving growth through sustained investment in innovation and customer experience. Hilton Hotels & Resorts' 11th consecutive year as the world's most valuable hotel brand reflects its ability to continually evolve the stay experience while maintaining the trust and hospitality that has defined the brand for more than a century.
