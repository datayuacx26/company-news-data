# Make a Witchy New England Escape That’s Full of Fall Magic

Listen to this article


0:00


Follow winding roads through bewitching landscapes, discover enchanting hidden bookshops and uncover captivating Autumn charm in New England


- **Plan a magical New England escape that blends coastal beauty, storybook villages, dark academia vibes and centuries-old history during the region's most hypnotizing season.**
- **From Boston to Providence and beyond, explore witchy autumn experiences ranging from ghost tours and ghoulish graveyards to cozy bookstores, misty harbors, covered bridges and colorful leaf-peeping drives.**
- **Stay in alluring Hilton properties throughout Massachusetts, Rhode Island, Connecticut and Maine, each steeped in their own stories, for a fall getaway filled with fantasy, folklore and seasonal charm.**


There’s something so magical about autumn in New England. The crisp air, colorful foliage and centuries-old homes give an enchanting feel to an already charming corner of the world. A trip here during spooky season is a vibe, offering cobblestone streets with eerily quiet corners, cozy bookshops filled with spellbinding tomes and winding roads through misty landscapes that make for the prime setting for ghost stories and witchy tales.


While Salem is a natural draw for those seeking a haunting hangout, some of New England’s most hypnotizing fall experiences can be found lurking elsewhere, in places where folklore, history, natural beauty and a touch of mystery create the perfect backdrop for a magical, mischievous getaway.


## Boston, Massachusetts is a Storied Setting Steeped in History


Beacon Hill Neighborhood in Boston, Massachusetts - Photo Credit: haveseen/Shutterstock


Every bewitching adventure needs a launching point, and Boston is the perfect place to begin. As one of America’s oldest cities, Boston offers centuries of history with an undeniable air of mystique. Here, colonial-era graveyards provide an unnerving backdrop for ghost tours and deep dives into local legends.

Explore Beacon Hill's gas-lit streets, browse independent bookstores or join an educational (and entertaining) walking tour before heading off in search of New England's most spellbinding towns.


## Providence, Rhode Island Offers Literary Magic and Gothic Charm


Providence, Rhode Island - Photo Credit: Sean Pavone/Shutterstock


When it comes to settings with an ethereal academia aesthetic, Providence provides everything from grand architecture to ivy-covered campuses and an artsy ambiance. And it’s especially mesmerizing during the fall months, when historic neighborhoods lined with Victorian homes offer a captivating setting in which to discover quirky antique shops and enjoy evenings filled with live music in a comfy cocktail bar.


Graduate by Hilton Providence


The city's literary legacy adds another layer of intrigue. Book lovers can spend hours exploring independent bookstores and historic landmarks connected to some of America's most influential writers before tucking into an evening at[Graduate by Hilton Providence](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/pvdgpgu-graduate-providence/) . Housed in one of the city's most recognizable historic buildings, the hotel embraces Providence's scholarly spirit with whimsical design touches and a moody elegance that’s perfect for fall.


## Connecticut's Quaint Villages are Storybook Settings Come to Life


Farm Stand in Connecticut - Photo Credit: ESK Imagery/Shutterstock


Connecticut is full of tempting towns that are ideal for enjoying laid-back autumn drives, wandering historic village centers and discovering places that feel like they’re plucked from the pages of a fairy tale. Start in the picturesque, and aptly named, village of Mystic, where historic ships, waterfront walkways and bespoke boutiques await.


Then, continue inland to discover the peaceful beauty of the Connecticut Quiet Corner to get lost amid scenic backroads, covered bridges, colorful orchards and fabled forests. Here, small towns like Woodstock and Putnam offer a softer side of the season filled with antique hunting, cozy cafés and farmers markets overflowing with pumpkins, apples and handmade goods. It's a practical magic-making setting for anyone looking for an autumn escape that’s more enchanting than eerie.


Graduate by Hilton Storrs


Situated amid it all,[Graduate by Hilton Storrs](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/bdlgsgu-graduate-storrs/) offers a comfortable retreat to savor between leaf-peeping adventures in Connecticut's most storybook-worthy towns.


## Portland, Maine is Home to Misty Mornings and Coastal Mysticism


Portland Head Light - Photo Credit: John A. Anderson/Shutterstock


Perched along the craggy coast of Maine sits Portland, a place filled with nautical history and irresistible culinary offerings. With fishing boats lining the hazy harbor, the city's Old Port district feels particularly hypnotic during the fall season. Venture beyond the city to explore nearby lighthouses, which stand watch over the dramatic coastal scenery and add a gothic air to this mystical region. Then, head back into town to wander through art galleries and dine at waterfront seafood shacks that create an inviting atmosphere in any season.


Canopy Portland Waterfront


A stay at[Canopy Portland Waterfront](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/pwmoppy-canopy-portland-waterfront/) places travelers in the heart of the action. Inspired by Portland's maritime heritage, the hotel offers an ideal home base for harbor strolls, scrumptious feasts and leaf-strewn drives along the coast (and beyond).
