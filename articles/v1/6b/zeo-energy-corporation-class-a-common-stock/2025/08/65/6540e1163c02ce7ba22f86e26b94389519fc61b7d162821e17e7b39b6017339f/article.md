# Zeo Energy Corp. to Present at the 2025 Gateway Conference on September 4th

NEW PORT RICHEY, Fla., Aug. 28, 2025 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- **Zeo Energy Corp. (Nasdaq: ZEO) ("Zeo," "Zeo Energy," or the "Company"),** a Florida-based provider of residential solar and commercial long-duration energy-storage solutions, is confirmed to present at the[2025 Annual Gateway Conference](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=6YHyk4KlrPZvFRe6bcnXAKnXg6h2o7Q4DNT3D57fDr3WspoSnSZuCFIlxp-8RXdt5FsMjOy6rIlZaBbgZ2AJe48KS3AD65ClSC0moLvTRuj0LeEaBBAzgJc21_igj_D_) , which is being held September 3-4 at the[Four Seasons Hotel](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=RcC26r6hXOiTf6whE5iSAnIwq17KUPzA5ybF-B_WumyPwiC8_xuDODtVEiM4myzvdrfAAChm0sc0tL8tYXPWVHPBIxpVmvB41BR9fPvML3zCshf2E5LdlbXzkCDUWkuQ) in San Francisco, CA.


Zeo's CEO, Tim Bridgewater and CFO, Cannon Holbrook are scheduled to present on Thursday, September 4th at 12:30 p.m. Pacific Time (PT). The presentation will be webcast live and available for replay[here](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=St8m33e4ZJXTwOPklnvmHFOLpqNksHMCj-ZSknaIGdbi-jEqcIf5g352ToPPyd7YREMwIDGabuq-VM3O0iWfR1y2RzBJ8IQLdR_aIFKhnzL1m1anqkES1f_5-y5JZd9BRH7z9qN8mvgF7lCNuFSL1Q==) . In addition to the presentation, Zeo executives will be available for one-on-one meetings throughout the conference.


For additional information, to request an invitation, or to schedule a one-on-one meeting, please email[conference@gateway-grp.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=UGQ68_C6hnKtcKCuS9ChWkr6Pk3zlXLSTi3c-Kijjmw2D4L9rtBJw6acIAq00KjhgEkOJ9lH9Ol_uc6b-shsNUgDSqXUB0m4xZcMNZjgBhBdjvezBUwDQm9RihA-p_oI) .

**About Zeo Energy Corp.**
Zeo Energy Corp. is a diversified clean energy company providing residential, commercial, industrial, and utility-scale solutions that cut costs and carbon emissions. Based in Florida, Zeo operates Sunergy, a residential solar, distributed energy, and efficiency solutions business, in high-growth markets with limited competitive saturation. It also operates Heliogen, Inc., a long-duration energy generation and storage business designed to deliver renewable power for high-demand applications such as AI, data centers, and other energy-intensive industries. With its vertically integrated approach, Zeo helps customers with a cost-effective transition to 24/7 clean energy.
