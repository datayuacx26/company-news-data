While

“Pillow support is crucial for maintaining proper alignment of the neck and spine,” says Palmer. “When lying down, the head and neck should be in a neutral position to reduce strain on the muscles and joints. A supportive


[pillow](https://purple.com/pillows) helps achieve this alignment.”


On the other hand, not using a pillow — or using the wrong one — may cause you to toss and turn throughout the night or wake up in pain due to discomfort and misalignment. By investing in a quality pillow, you’re improving your quality of sleep and day-to-day life.


## **5 Factors To Consider When Buying a Pillow**


The pillow you find most comfortable might not be the same one your partner prefers. Additionally, your own pillow preferences may change over time. For this reason, pillow quality is of the utmost importance.


“The quality of a pillow is defined by its design and materials,” says Palmer. “Ensure you choose a pillow made of high-quality materials that offer a smart design that provides you with excellent support, comfort, and features so you can sleep deeply.”


Here are the key factors to consider when shopping and


[replacing your pillow](https://purple.com/blog/how-often-should-you-replace-pillows) :


- **Feel:** Determine what feel you prefer. Generally speaking, there are five types of pillows and feels:


- [Down or down alternative](https://purple.com/blog/down-vs-down-alternative) for a soft, plush feel


- Foam or memory foam for a medium to firmer feel, conforming support


- Latex for a buoyant, responsive feel


- Adjustable or shapeable for customizing feel and/or height


- Ergonomically shaped for precise support


- **Features:** Size, shape, and other design elements can impact the sleeping experience. Many sleepers like to adjust the height, add extra support, or have enhanced cooling options that fit specific needs.


- **Sleeping position:** Your sleep position may influence the features and height you prefer. Typically, a medium or tall loft is


[best for side sleepers](https://purple.com/blog/best-pillow-for-side-sleepers) . Medium is also generally preferred by combination sleepers, while low pillows most often work well for back or stomach sleepers and those with smaller frames.


- **Number of pillows:** Depending on your personal preferences and sleep position, you may


[sleep with multiple pillows](https://purple.com/blog/how-many-pillows-should-you-sleep-with) or just one. If you're replacing one or more, consider opting for a pillow with a loft that better aligns with your needs.


- **Price:** Since you will be sleeping with this pillow every night for the next couple of years, be open to


[spending more money on a pillow](https://purple.com/blog/how-much-do-pillows-cost) to ensure you get something comfortable and durable.
