## **Purple’s Sleep Study Overview**


To find the truest impact of a Purple mattress, SleepScore Labs conducted three in-home studies; one on a Purple Essential Mattress, one on the Purple Restore Hybrid Mattress, and one on a Purple Rejuvenate Mattress. Each study followed a pre-post design where participants first tracked their sleep on their original mattress for four weeks, then switched to a Purple mattress (either from the Essential collection or Restore collection) for six weeks.


All Purple mattresses feature the


[GelFlex Grid](https://purple.com/purple-grid) ®—a patented, hyper-elastic material that draws heat away from the body, cradles pressure points, and


[supports spinal alignment](https://purple.com/blog/new-mattress-back-pain) in every position. This innovative technology is at the heart of the improvements seen in the SleepScore Labs study.


Sleep was measured using SleepScore Max, a validated non-contact monitor, along with daily surveys and the Chronic Pain Sleep Inventory **,** a self-administered sleep questionnaire that evaluates the role of pain in sleep interference. This comprehensive approach allowed researchers to capture both objective metrics like sleep efficiency and deep sleep, and subjective experiences like comfort, pain relief, and overall satisfaction—all without invasive procedures or medications.


The participants in this study reported difficulty with sleep but no sleep disorders such as insomnia. Therefore, this sample of healthy adults had less room for improvement than a clinical sample would, making the findings more impressive.

## **The Role of Cooling in Uninterrupted Sleep**


Temperature plays a critical role in staying comfortable through the night. When a mattress traps heat, your body has to work harder to regulate temperature, often leading to restlessness, position changes, and unnecessary wakeups. That’s why cooling isn’t just about how a mattress feels at first touch, but how it manages heat over hours of sleep.


In a 2026 SleepScore Labs study, participants sleeping on a Purple Rejuvenate Collection mattress reported significantly fewer issues with sleeping too hot compared to their original mattress. While 96% of participants reported sleeping too hot on their previous mattress, only 25% reported sleeping too hot after switching to Purple Rejuvenate Mattress.


Participants also rated Rejuvenate over 4.5x higher for cool-to-the-touch feel, reflecting the GelFlex Grid’s open-column design that allows heat to dissipate instead of building up beneath the body. By helping prevent temperature-related discomfort, the GelFlex Grid® removes one of the most common disruptors of sleep — so you can stay comfortably settled throughout the night


## **Why is Deep Sleep Important?**


[Deep sleep is the most restorative stage](https://purple.com/blog/how-to-get-more-deep-sleep) of the sleep cycle, and it plays a critical role in overall health. During deep sleep, your body repairs muscles and tissues, strengthens the immune system, and consolidates memories. It’s also when growth hormone is released, aiding recovery and physical performance.


Without enough deep sleep, you may feel fatigued, struggle with focus, and experience slower recovery from daily stress or exercise.
