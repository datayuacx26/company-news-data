## Mattress Support vs Feel


All Purple mattresses deliver dynamic support through the GelFlex® Grid, which flexes under pressure points while keeping your spine aligned, so you never have to choose between comfort and stability. While every Purple mattress offers this same level of support, each model feels different, ranging from softer, more plush options to firmer, more responsive designs to match your unique sleep preference.


Your ideal mattress feel often depends on your sleep position: back sleepers usually prefer a medium feel for balanced support, side sleepers tend to favor softer models for pressure relief, stomach sleepers benefit from a firmer surface to maintain alignment, and combination sleepers need an adaptable feel that works for multiple positions.

## **Purple Hybrid Mattresses vs. Non-Hybrid: Key Differences**


While all of Purple’s mattresses promote optimal airflow because of the GelFlex Grid® in every model, Purple’s


[Restore® Hybrid Collection](https://purple.com/mattresses#section-id-premium) mattresses have responsive edge-to-edge coil systems that enhance ventilation, improve heat dissipation and provide added comfort and support. These mattresses may offer cooling relief for


[hot sleepers](https://purple.com/blog/hot-sleeper) as well as support and adaptive comfort.


Purple carries both hybrid and non-hybrid mattresses. Purple’s hybrid mattresses are all the mattresses in the Restore® Hybrid and


[Rejuvenate® Luxe Collections](https://purple.com/mattresses/rejuvenate-collection) , namely: the Restore® Hybrid Mattress, the RestorePlus®, the RestorePremier®, the Rejuvenate®, the RejuvenatePlus®, the RejuvenatePremier®, and the RejuvenateUltimate™.


Purple’s non-hybrid mattresses make use of extra foam layers to either add support at the base or cushioning on top.


The mattresses in Purple’s Essential Collection are non-hybrid mattresses. These are the PurpleFlex® Mattress, the Purple Mattress®, and the Purple Plus® Mattress.
