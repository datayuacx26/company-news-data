# PL_Genesis: Frontiers of Collaboration Hackathon — The Next Chapter of Open Innovation

Introducing **[PL_Genesis: Frontiers of Collaboration (opens new window)](https://www.plgenesis.com/?utm_source=protocol.ai&utm_medium=blog&utm_campaign=hackathon&utm_content=pl_blog)** , the next phase of Protocol Labs' flagship hackathon and a call to teams working on the building blocks of frontier technology: Web3, Crypto, AI, Robotics, and Neurotechnology.


Last season, **PL_Genesis: Modular Worlds** challenged builders to assemble a new internet from composable, user-owned primitives. The goal was clear: move beyond monolithic platforms and toward interoperable systems where storage, compute, identity, and incentives are modular by design.


**Builders responded.**


- **1,900+** builders registered
- **350+** projects shipped
- **$250K+** in prizes awarded
- **20+** teams advanced into Founders Forge
- Multiple teams now in active investment conversations across the PL network


## # Now We're Expanding the Scope


**Frontiers of Collaboration** is grounded in a new reality: the most meaningful breakthroughs are happening across frontier domains, not within isolated verticals. Crypto coordinating autonomous agents. AI operating on decentralized infrastructure. Robotics interacting with programmable economies. Neurotechnology reshaping how humans interface with machines.


**This is about the collaboration layer between these systems.**


With **$150K+ in prizes** , targeted sponsor bounties across frontier technology domains, and a potential pathway into the **Founders Forge** early-stage accelerator, Frontiers of Collaboration is built for teams designing foundational systems.


Watch the[Founders Forge documentary (opens new window)](https://x.com/pl__genesis/status/2027054887889150062?s=46&t=TQsBsnJq4Dt8KJ-Dgf2KOg) , capturing the first cohort’s path from early prototype to venture-ready company, offering a transparent look at what the PL_Genesis pipeline can unlock for builders.


Whether you're launching something new or advancing an existing project, the invitation is open. Integrate at least one sponsor bounty and build something that pushes the frontier forward.


## # More Than Just Another Hackathon


- **$150K+ prize pool** across Fresh Code, Existing Code, and sponsor bounties, with continued additions throughout the season.
- **Founders Forge early-stage accelerator** → High-potential early teams are fast-tracked into our structured program with technical mentorship, venture guidance, and direct investor access.
- **Build Stations across key cities** → Closed-format, in-person collaboration nodes hosted with trusted developer hubs for high-signal teams who prefer IRL environments.

**PL Genesis is not a one-off event. It is the front door to a broader builder pipeline.**

## # Prize Structure


- **Fresh Code** → **$50,000** prize pool for new projects started within the hacking period (10 teams × $5,000)
- **Existing Code** → **$50,000** prize pool for continued development and meaningful integration (10 teams × $5,000)
- **Sponsor Bounties** → **$50,000+** in targeted awards tied to sponsor challenges


Every project must clearly integrate at least one sponsor bounty.


High-potential teams will be considered for **Founders Forge** regardless of final prize placement.
