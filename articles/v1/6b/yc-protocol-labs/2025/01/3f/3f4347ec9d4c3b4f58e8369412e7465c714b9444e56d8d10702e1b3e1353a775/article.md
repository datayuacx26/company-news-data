# Driving Breakthroughs: The Impact of Our Innovation Network

As we enter 2025, we’re taking a moment to reflect on the remarkable progress made across the Protocol Labs innovation network over the past 12 months.


From launching transformative projects to scaling innovative startups, our network drove meaningful change across industries. Together, we empowered over 600 teams, projects, and movements to tackle challenges in our three focus areas — establishing digital human rights, upgrading our economies and governance systems, developing safe new breakthrough tech — and beyond.


The momentum we’ve built is driving us into 2025 and PLv11 with ambitious goals and renewed energy. This year, we will expand into a fourth focus area: Neurotech, humanity’s next frontier. We’ll continue to foster cutting-edge research, invest in visionary founders, and build the tools needed to unlock the next wave of breakthroughs.


Take a quick look at the impact we’ve created together and what’s on the horizon for 2025.
