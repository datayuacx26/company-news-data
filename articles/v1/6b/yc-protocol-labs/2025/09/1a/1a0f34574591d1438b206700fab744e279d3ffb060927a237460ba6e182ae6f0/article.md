# LabWeek Web3 Returns — See You in Buenos Aires This November

**What** : LabWeek Web3
**Where** : Buenos Aires, Argentina
**When** : November 13–19, 2025
**Why** : A decentralized conference focused on digital human rights, real-world crypto applications, and next-gen infrastructure — by builders, for builders.
**Register now** :[25.labweek.io/25-w3 (opens new window)](http://25.labweek.io/25-w3)


[LabWeek Web3 (opens new window)](http://25.labweek.io/25-w3) returns this November — and we’re heading to Buenos Aires.


From November 13–19, 2025, builders from across Protocol Labs and the broader web3 ecosystem will gather for a decentralized week of workshops, research-focused seminars, summits, meetups, unconferences, and more! This year, LabWeek Web3 coincides with Devconnect, creating a powerful moment for connection, collaboration, and driving real progress on the technologies shaping the future of the internet.


### # What Is LabWeek Web3?


LabWeek Web3 confronts today’s most urgent questions at the crossroads of digital human rights and the decentralized web. We face mounting risks from centralized data control, censorship, insecure communication, and threats to privacy and freedom. LabWeek is about advancing technologies that restore agency — returning control of our data, our voices, our communications, and our digital futures to the people.


### # Why Buenos Aires?


Argentina’s history of economic instability has made it one of the world’s most active proving grounds for crypto adoption. In a place where traditional systems have broken trust, decentralized technologies offer a way to regain financial agency and protect digital rights. Hosting LabWeek Web3 in Buenos Aires reinforces what the movement is about: building systems that work under pressure, support autonomy, and have tangible real-world impact.


### # What’s New This Year


In 2024, we experimented with separate LabWeeks for each of our focus areas — Digital Human Rights, Public Goods, and Field Building. This year, we moved to smaller, more focused events and retreats for Public Goods and Neurotech throughout the year. But LabWeek Web3 is where the innovation network — and aligned ecosystems — converge IRL to focus on digital rights, decentralized infrastructure, and technologies with immediate impact.


### # Community-First: By Builders, for Builders


LabWeek Web3 is decentralized by design. Every event is independently organized by teams pushing their domain forward — from zero-knowledge proof to DePIN to decentralized AI and storage to edge computing and beyond! The goal is to bring together domain leaders to make real progress. If you’re building something, you can host something. Spots for hosting events fill fast — submit yours now at[25.labweek.io/25-w3 (opens new window)](http://25.labweek.io/25-w3) .


### # What to Expect in Buenos Aires


A week of high-impact gatherings, from headline summits to intimate dinners:


- **Opening Party** — Kick off LabWeek Web3 with the entire network
- **Founders Dinner** — Connect with high-agency founders
- **Cypherpunk Congress** — Join conversations at the edge of privacy, and safe and empowered tech
- **DePIN Dinner** — The go-to table for decentralized physical infrastructure innovators
- **ETHGlobal Buenos Aires** — Build our decentralized web3 future at the renowned hackathon series
- **Funding the Commons** — Advance public goods funding models for the internet age
- **FIL Dev Summit 7** — Dive deep with Filecoin developers and ecosystem partners
- **PL After Hours** — Unwind, swap ideas, and keep building after the day’s sessions wrap


Plus dozens more community-led gatherings, talks, and unconferences — all independently organized by builders pushing their domains forward. Because LabWeek Web3 is decentralized, organizers will continue to create events in the coming weeks, so check the schedule often.
