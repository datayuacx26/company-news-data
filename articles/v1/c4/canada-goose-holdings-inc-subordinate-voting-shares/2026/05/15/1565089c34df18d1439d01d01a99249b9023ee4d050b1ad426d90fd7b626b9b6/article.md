### Canada Goose to Announce Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year 2026 Financial Results on May 14, 2026


May 4, 2026


TORONTO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Canada Goose Holdings Inc. (NYSE, TSX: GOOS) plans to announce results for the fourth quarter and fiscal year 2026, which ended March 29, 2026, before markets open on Thursday, May 14, 2026.


The Company will host a conference call and webcast to discuss fourth quarter and fiscal 2026 results at 8:30am ET on Thursday, May 14, 2026.
