### Canada Goose to Announce Third Quarter Fiscal Year 2026 Financial Results on February 5, 2026


January 15, 2026


TORONTO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Canada Goose Holdings Inc. (NYSE, TSX: GOOS) plans to announce results for the third quarter of fiscal year 2026, which ended December 28, 2025, before markets open on Thursday, February 5, 2026.


The Company will host a conference call and webcast to discuss third quarter fiscal 2026 results at 8:30am ET on Thursday, February 5, 2026.
