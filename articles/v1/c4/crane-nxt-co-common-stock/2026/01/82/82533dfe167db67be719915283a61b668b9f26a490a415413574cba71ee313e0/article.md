### Crane NXT to Participate in the CJS Securities 26th Annual New Ideas for the New Year Conference


January 7, 2026


WALTHAM, Mass., Jan. 07, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Crane NXT, Co. (NYSE: CXT), a premier industrial technology company, announced that Aaron Saak, President and Chief Executive Officer, and Christina Cristiano, Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, will participate in a fireside chat at the CJS Securities 26th Annual New Ideas for the New Year Conference on Wednesday, January 14, 2026 at 8:45 a.m. ET.


The event is being held virtually and a live webcast of the fireside chat will be available on Crane NXT’s[investor relations website](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=8fLVu7G3ny_7GI5nZSMM276iwupv6agTXA0gK_XvnLZOpJe14W0St7P0r33Ar7mLw_LEETpv9Jcl9PvF9ozbucuc3iS1tXsIlK6frh7NhPFSZpiKRN_yPLGQqayQa5fxRMng4OxcLBaifS_nf66ujA==) . A replay of the webcast will be available for 1 year following the conference.
