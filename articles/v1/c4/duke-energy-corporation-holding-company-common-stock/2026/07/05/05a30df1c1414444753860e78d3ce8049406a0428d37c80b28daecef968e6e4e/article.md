# Duke Energy Florida’s Neighborhood Energy Saver Program saves Clearwater customers over $3.1M to date

-


### **More than 600 customers have received a free energy assessment to help lower their monthly bill**


-


### **The next phase of the program will begin in August**


ST PETERSBURG, Fla. – From the grocery store to the gas pump, household budgets are stretched thin. To help provide relief, Duke Energy Florida’s Neighborhood Energy Saver Program has been offered in the city of Clearwater since the start of the year. The program provides free energy assessments, including recommendations to reduce energy consumption and lower costs. Through May 2026, more than 600 local customers have participated, collectively saving over $3.1 million on their electric bills.


**Savings opportunities**


A free energy assessment can reveal savings opportunities for customers, including:


- **Verify your HVAC efficiency:** Have a qualified heating and air conditioning contractor verify your HVAC is operating efficiently.


- **Seal any leaks:** Caulking and weatherstripping your windows, doors and vents can help[reduce cooling costs by 10%-20%](https://www.energy.gov/energysaver/do-it-yourself-savings-project-using-caulk-seal-air-leaks) .


- **Replace filters monthly:** A dirty air filter makes a cooling system work harder, which uses more energy. Replacing a dirty filter with a clean one monthly can[lower your air conditioner's energy consumption by 5% to 15%](https://www.energy.gov/energysaver/maintaining-your-air-conditioner#:~:text=Replacing%20a%20dirty%2C%20clogged%20filter,by%205%25%20to%2015%25.#:~:text=Replacing%20a%20dirty%2C%20clogged%20filter,by%205%25%20to%2015%25.) .


- **Adjust the thermostat:** Save money by setting the thermostat to the highest comfortable setting in the summer.


**Our view**


- “For customers who qualify, the Neighborhood Energy Saver program is one of the most impactful resources available to help lower energy costs,” said Melissa Seixas, Duke Energy Florida state president. “An energy efficiency expert is able to walk through the home with the customer and make specific recommendations on how to reduce energy usage in practical ways. The savings that can result in these assessments can make a significant impact.”


**Community support**


- “This program meets families where they are,” said Fran Pheeny, Suncoast Housing Connections CEO. “A free energy assessment isn’t just about lowering a bill, it’s about giving people long-term financial stability by lowering monthly energy costs and one less thing to worry about at home.”


- “Energy affordability is a community issue, and we’re proud to help make sure the neighbors who need this program most know it exists and know how to access it,” said Glorida Campbell, Clearwater Urban Leadership Coalition executive director.


**What’s next**


With two phases complete, additional Neighborhood Energy Saver events are planned across Clearwater in the second half of 2026. Eligible customers will receive a direct invitation to attend a launch event, where they will receive up to 20 energy saving products and information on how to schedule a free energy assessment.


The program is available to residential customers, both homeowners and renters, living in communities identified by Duke Energy Florida using federal and state guidelines and census data.


To learn more about the Neighborhood Energy Saver Program and determine eligibility, please click[here](https://www.duke-energy.com/home/products/income-qualified/neighborhood-energy-saver) or call 855.227.2918.
