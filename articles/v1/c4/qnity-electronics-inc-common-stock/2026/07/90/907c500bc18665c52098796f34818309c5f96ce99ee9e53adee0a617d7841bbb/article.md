**Base Film Description**


**HN**


General purpose polyimide.


**HA**


Amorphous general purpose polyimide film exhibiting higher elongation


**FPC**


Higher dimensional stability, surface modified for improved adhesion to the film


**EN**


Lower CTE film to closer match copper


**B**


Black, homogeneous opaque film


**WS**


White film


**MT / MT+**


Higher Thermal Conductivity: up to 6 X HN (>0.7 W/mK)


**AE**


High modulus core, CTE matching copper, TPI outer layers


**FN**


Kapton ®


HN core with FEP fluoropolymer on one or both sides


**FCR / FCRC**


Composite film consisting of corona resistant Kapton® film with FEP fluoropolymer


** *Figure 2. Summary listing of Kapton® polyimide films.*


The versatility of Kapton® film extends to its coloration, with some products being available in black or white to absorb or reflect light, respectively, as needed by an application. The James Webb Space Telescope’s massive solar shield, for example, is made of five thin layers of Kapton® E film with aluminum and doped-silicon coatings to reflect the sun's heat back into space.
