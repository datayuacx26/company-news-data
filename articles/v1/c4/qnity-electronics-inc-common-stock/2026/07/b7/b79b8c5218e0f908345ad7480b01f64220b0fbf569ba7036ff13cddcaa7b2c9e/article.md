# Qnity Announces Third Quarter 2026 Dividend on Common Stock

**WILMINGTON, DEL., June 24, 2026** – **** Qnity Electronics, Inc. (“Qnity”) (NYSE: Q), a premier technology solutions leader across the semiconductor value chain, announced that its Board of Directors has declared a quarterly dividend of 0.08 cents ($0.08) per share for each share of issued and outstanding common stock of the Company (par value $0.01 per share). The dividend will be payable on September 15, 2026 to stockholders of record on August 31, 2026.


Learn about how Qnity is powering the next leap forward at[www.qnityelectronics.com](https://www.qnityelectronics.com/) .


**About Qnity**
Qnity is a premier technology provider across the semiconductor value chain, empowering AI, high performance computing, and advanced connectivity.
