# Now Live: Action Center

For most employees, finance tasks are not something they think about every day. They might need to upload a receipt, review a card transaction, respond to a request, or provide details on a purchase — but those actions usually happen in between everything else they’re doing.


If your team doesn't have a clear place to see what is waiting on them, the responsibility falls back on owners and finance teams to follow up manually.


We just rolled out the Action Center, a new place in Slash where team members can see the actions they need to take in their account. It gives employees a clearer way to stay on top of their responsibilities, while helping finance teams reduce follow-ups and keep things moving.


## How it works


Instead of checking multiple tabs or waiting for a reminder, employees can now open the Action Center to see the items assigned to them. Each action includes the context needed to understand what is being asked and what steps they need to take next.


Depending on how your team uses Slash, the Action Center can help employees stay on top of tasks like:


- Uploading missing receipts
- Reviewing or adding details to Slash Card transactions
- Responding to requests from admins or finance teams
- Completing required steps tied to their Slash account


Your team members no longer have to wonder whether something is waiting on them somewhere in Slash. They have one place to check.


## Built for team accountability


If an employee made a purchase, they are usually the best person to explain what it was for. If a transaction needs review, they often have the materials needed to resolve it. Action Center helps bring those tasks back to the right person, instead of making finance teams chase down information after the fact.


When employees can clearly see what they need to do, they are more likely to complete those actions on time. That can lead to:


1. Fewer manual reminders from admins and finance teams
2. Cleaner transaction records and supporting documentation
3. Faster resolution of outstanding account tasks
4. More accountability across the company
5. Less cleanup work later


### Action Center is available now in Slash


To try it out, log in to your Slash account and open the Action Center from the left sidebar. From there, you can review any pending actions assigned to you and complete the items that need your attention.
