# Now Live: Slash Global Cards

Slash Cards for Global USD account holders are finally here.³ Issued by our partner, Rain, Slash's Global Cards let international businesses spend anywhere Visa is accepted without needing a U.S. bank account or U.S.-incorporated entity. Now every Slash user can pay vendors, run ads, cover software subscriptions, and handle operating expenses in USD without extra steps in between.


## How Slash Global Cards work


When you make a purchase with your Slash Global card, the funds are drawn directly from your USDSL balance without currency conversions, intermediary banks, or delays. From your perspective, it works just like a regular card payment. Your USDSL is off-ramped at the point of sale, and settlement is batched each hour.


Here's what you get:


- **Virtual and physical cards** : Virtual cards are created instantly and ready to use for online purchases and vendor payments, while physical cards are shipped right to your door for in-person transactions.
- **Real-time authorization** : Purchases are authorized against your USDSL balance in real time.
- **Company branding** : Your business name can be displayed on the card.
- **Full card controls** : Set spending limits, freeze and unfreeze cards, and manage your team's spend from the Slash dashboard.


## The Slash Global USD Account


With the Global USD account, businesses in more than 130 countries get access to U.S. dollar infrastructure without setting up a U.S. entity. You get a U.S. bank account and routing number without needing a U.S.-registered LLC, corporation, or partnership. Your balance is held in USDSL, a stablecoin pegged 1:1 to the U.S. dollar.


The Global USD Account is built for international service businesses, ecommerce sellers, remote teams, and crypto-native companies that need reliable access to USD without the overhead of U.S. incorporation. Accounts can be funded through ACH, SWIFT, stablecoin deposits like USDC or USDT, or platform payouts from services like Stripe and Shopify.⁴
