# Slash Partners with Brycent to Put Startups in the Spotlight

Slash has partnered with Bryce Johnson (@Brycent), making him the company’s very first Go-to-Market Creator. Brycent is a digital content creator, angel investor, and the founder/host of Vesting TV,[a venture capital show](https://www.twitch.tv/brycent/videos) that spotlights early-stage startups and Y Combinator founders live on Twitch three times a week.


Brycent's network runs deep with startup founders and decision-makers, some looking for advice and some looking to invest in the same early-stage companies Slash was built to serve. Slash is a financial platform for entrepreneurs, founders, and business owners, built to handle the kind of growth Brycent spotlights every week on Vesting.


## How Slash & Vesting TV Can Widen Your Reach


If you sign up for Slash[through Brycent’s referral link](https://app.slash.com/i/brycent) , you can get Slash’s all-in-one financial stack alongside exclusive distribution across Brycent’s channels. Each business that joins Slash through Brycent’s referral automatically gets a Vesting post on Instagram about their company, displayed to an audience of over 300,000 followers that’s interested in partnering with and investing in budding startups.


Your reach doesn’t have to end there. Founders also have the option of personally joining Brycent’s own Twitch channel to put your company directly in front of his audience and talk about what sets your startup apart from the rest.


If you deposit $5 million into a Slash business account, Bryce will create a dedicated Instagram Reel about your company, including information that wouldn’t have otherwise been featured in the introductory post.¹ For more information on this offer, see the[Slash Partner Offer Terms and Agreement](https://www.slash.com/legal/partner-offer-terms-and-agreement) .


All you need to do to reach Brycent’s audience is click his link and join Slash’s business banking platform. If you’re interested in learning about cutting-edge startups and you want to hear advice about the world of venture capital from Brycent himself, follow him on his socials and tune in to Vesting on Twitch every Monday, Wednesday, and Thursday at 5:00 PM EST.
