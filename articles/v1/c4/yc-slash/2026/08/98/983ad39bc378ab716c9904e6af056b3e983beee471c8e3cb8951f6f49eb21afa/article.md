# Now Live: Metal Cards

Today, we’re introducing the Slash Metal Card so our users can carry a card that feels as premium as the financial platform behind it.¹


We spent months refining every detail, from its weight and finish to the way the design comes together in your hand. The result is a premium card with a clean, distinctive look built for founders who expect more from the tools you use every day.


## Designed to make an impression


The Slash Metal Card has a substantial feel and a refined finish, with a minimalist design that is instantly recognizable without being overstated. It’s made to feel at home whether you’re paying for software, meeting a partner over dinner, or covering the day-to-day expenses that keep your business moving.


It’s the Slash card you already know, just a little heavier.


## The same powerful card experience


Metal cards are connected to the same Slash controls and workflows that help businesses manage spend with confidence.


You can set card and group limits, apply merchant controls, monitor activity in real time, and keep transactions organized for accounting. That means you get a more premium experience without giving up the speed, visibility, and control of the Slash dashboard.


## Order yours today from your Slash dashboard


Eligible Slash customers can order a metal card directly from their dashboard:


1. Sign in to Slash and open **Cards** .
2. Choose to create or order a physical card.
3. Select **Metal** as the card design.
4. Confirm the cardholder and shipping details.
5. Review any card and shipping fees, then place the order.


If you don’t see the metal option yet, make sure you’re ordering for an eligible U.S. card account and check back as availability reaches your account.
