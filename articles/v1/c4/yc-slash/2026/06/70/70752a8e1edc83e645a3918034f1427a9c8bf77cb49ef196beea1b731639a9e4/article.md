# Now Live: Connect to NetSuite and DualEntry

Slash now connects directly with Oracle NetSuite and DualEntry, giving you more ways to sync transaction data from Slash into the accounting systems your team uses to close the books.


Depending on your setup, exported data can include transaction details and accounting dimensions such as:


- **GL/account codes** so spend posts to the correct account without manual coding
- **Departments** to attribute spend to the team or function that owns it
- **Classes** to tag spend by business line, product, or program
- **Locations** to attribute spend to a specific office, region, or site
- **Subsidiaries** to route spend to the right entity
- **Vendor or merchant details** so transactions match existing vendor records
- **Cardholder** and memo information for transaction context
- **Receipts** and supporting details where available


Instead of reclassifying transactions later, your finance team can map Slash fields to your accounting system once, so transactions flow to your ledger automatically.


## How to connect


If you're setting up your accounting dashboard for the first time, you'll be prompted to choose your ERP. Make sure you already have a NetSuite or DualEntry account before getting started. If you'd like to switch to a new integration, here's how:


### NetSuite


1. Open your Slash dashboard and go to Settings → Integrations.
2. Choose NetSuite from the list of available accounting integrations.
3. Follow the connection flow to authorize Slash.
4. Review your account mappings and sync preferences before turning on the integration.


### DualEntry


1. Head to Settings → Integrations.
2. Select DualEntry and start the connection process.
3. Authorize Slash to connect with your DualEntry account.
4. Confirm your mappings and sync settings to begin sending transaction data into DualEntry.


Slash doesn't replace your accounting software. It connects to it, so activity in Slash shows up where your finance team manages the books.
