#### The Prevailing Wage Problem (And Why It's Draining Your Profits)


Look, this doesn't need to be a therapy session but let's be honest about prevailing wage for a second.


It's not just about paying wage rates, it's wage determinations that vary by county and craft, fringe calculations that make you want to throw your computer in the trash, and certified payroll reports that age you in dog years. Oh and it's state rules stacked on top of federal rules, apprenticeship ratios, and documentation that has to be perfect because audits don't care if you got "close enough."


Here's what's really keeping contractors from going after more prevailing wage work:


**The admin nightmare.** One mistake and you're staring down penalties, back wages, or getting debarred.


**The payroll tax drain.** Most contractors pay the full prevailing wage (base plus fringe) straight on the paycheck. Cash fringe might seem easier but you're paying FICA, FUTA, SUTA, and workers' comp on everything, base AND fringe. Scale that across 50 people and you're burning thousands for literally no reason, when you should be taking credit for what you're already paying for.


**The compliance minefield.** Federal Davis-Bacon rules, state laws that differ county to county, and one audit could mean writing checks for tens of thousands if your paperwork has gaps.


That's why we built Hammr to handle payroll, HR, and compliance, and why we partnered with Beneco, because they've spent almost 40 years helping contractors structure fringe benefits without getting torched on taxes.


#### Why Beneco? (And How This Saves You $156,000/Year)


Beneco isn't a new company trying to figure construction out, they've been working with contractors for nearly four decades with hundreds of clients across the country. They know this game inside and out.


Here's the part nobody talks about: **how you actually pay fringe matters way more than you think.** Most contractors take what seems like the easy route, paying the full prevailing wage (base plus fringe) directly on the paycheck. But you're paying payroll taxes on all of it, every hour and every worker.


**There's a way better way to do this.**


Instead of paying fringe as taxable wages (cash fringe), you route those dollars into a bona fide trust like retirement plans, health coverage, HRAs, and vacation funds. When it's set up correctly, those contributions aren't subject to payroll taxes.


**Let's do some mathing:**


Say you've got a $40/hour base wage and $10/hour fringe requirement, with labor burden at 30%.


- **Fringe on the check:** $50/hour taxable × 30% = **$15/hour in payroll taxes**
- **Fringe in a trust:** $40/hour taxable × 30% = **$12/hour in payroll taxes**
- **Your savings: $3/hour per worker**


Now scale it: 50 employees × 1,040 prevailing wage hours/year × $3/hour = **$156,000 in annual savings.**


And that's JUST the payroll tax piece. You're also funding real benefits your crew wants like medical, 401(k), and paid time off, all while staying completely compliant with Davis-Bacon and state regulations.


**Beneco's track record:** $700M in contributions over the last decade and $125M in labor burden savings for contractors.These aren't theoretical numbers, this is real money staying in real businesses.
