When Michael joined ICC as Controller, he didn't walk into a smooth-running back-office. He walked into spreadsheets and disjointed systems. Payroll and accounting was a top priority for him to tackle.


*"They had what I'd call a bookkeeping setup. Not accounting. There was no process control and things were way too inefficient."*


ICC had experience with prevailing wage but with the team ramping up on public work, they were in no shape to handle the mountain of administrative work coming their way. Michael didn't panic. But he sure as hell wasn't going to keep wasting 20+ hours every pay period manually entering and reconciling data line by line.


So he started looking for a fix.


#### 🚨 Before Hammr: A Stack Holding Them Back


ICC's process before Hammr was tedious at best:


- Time tracked in ClockShark
- Hours re-entered into Excel
- Each week, a spreadsheet per employee per project
- Payroll entered line by line in accounting system
- Manual reconciliation every pay period
- Zero support for prevailing wage
- Zero automation for certified payroll reporting


*"Just payroll entry and reconciliation took a full day every pay period," Michael said. "Then I'd double-check that it matched the spreadsheets."*


And when prevailing wage came into play:


*"QuickBooks couldn't support prevailing wage and fringe components. I had to enter separate line items for every wage component - base rate, fringe, vacation, training - for every employee, every day. It was just draining our time."*
