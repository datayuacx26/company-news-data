## Before evaluating any AI tool, settle these three questions


### Q1: Does it resolve tickets, or just deflect them?


A deflection tool offers a help article. A resolution tool reads the order, issues the refund, updates the address, and closes the ticket. They are not the same.


Yuma bills only for a full-resolution ticket. This ticket is handled from start to finish with zero human involvement. So, however many steps the agent takes, it counts once. And the moment a human steps in, the ticket becomes free.


Since 68% of consumers confirmed that getting a complete resolution is the most important factor in any support interaction ([Glance report](https://www.glance.cx/2026-cx-trends-report-the-backlash-and-the-bounceback) , 2026), this makes Yuma a strong choice for fully automating customer support.


You can also ask each vendor to show you a ticket that the AI agent fully resolved from start to finish, without human involvement.


Learn why brands are[choosing Yuma AI agents to resolve tickets](https://yuma.ai/support-ai) .


### Q2: Does it connect to your helpdesk natively, or through middleware?


This question is important because you want a plug-and-play helpdesk integration, not a webhook that a developer must build and maintain indefinitely. The latter places heavy reliance on a developer to manage the operations, leaving your customer support agents powerless.


So ask: "What exact data does your AI read from my helpdesk at the moment a ticket is created?" A vague answer is a red flag.


See how[Yuma's AI support agent](https://yuma.ai/ai-support-agent) handles native integration.


### Q3: What does it actually cost at your ticket volume?


The last, but not least, question evaluates the pricing model for resolving tickets. You need the AI tool for ecommerce that is not just efficient, but also economically sustainable for your business.


Here are the three common pricing models:


- Per-seat: this scales with agent headcount, not ticket volume.
- Per-resolution: you pay only when the AI fully closes a ticket.
- Per-interaction: charges for every exchange, resolved or not.

## Frequently asked questions


### 1. What's the difference between an AI chatbot and an AI agent for ecommerce?


A chatbot answers customers' questions, while an AI agent takes action. The latter reads the order, processes the refund, updates the address, and closes the ticket.

No. Yuma layers on top of existing helpdesks. Migration only makes sense when the helpdesk itself is the problem, think weak Shopify integration, poor reporting, or a billing model that inflates costs as automation scales.


### 4. What should a vendor demo actually prove?


A meaningful demo should test how the AI handles your real customer tickets:


- Vague or messy inputs.
- Edge cases like billing disputes.
- Repeated questions asked in different ways.
- Queries pulling from multiple sources or languages.


If the system handles these consistently, it is likely reliable at scale.
