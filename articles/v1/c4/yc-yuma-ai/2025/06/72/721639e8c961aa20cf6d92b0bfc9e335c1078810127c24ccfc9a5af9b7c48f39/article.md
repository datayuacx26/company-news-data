# Top 5 benefits of AI according to customer support professionals

E-commerce leaders may have once been wary of implementing artificial intelligence (AI) tools, fearing impacts on job security and morale. However, today customer experience (CX) experts are eagerly embracing AI.[A recent HubSpot survey](https://blog.hubspot.com/service/ai-in-customer-service) reveals that four in five CX specialists consider automation solutions essential to their strategy, and three in five say AI helps them understand their customers better.


Customer service teams aren’t just adopting AI in CX because it promises better results, but because it makes their lives easier and their work more efficient. What are the benefits of AI from the perspective of CX professionals? And what are the common uses and applications of AI in their world?


## #1 24/7 availability


An overwhelming majority (90%) of customers[consider an immediate response to be important or very important](https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/live-chat-go-to-market-flaw) when they have a customer service issue, with “immediate” defined as 10 minutes or fewer. It may come as no surprise then that **36% of customer service professionals view 24/7 availability as one of the biggest benefits of AI in customer service.**


Leveraging natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning, AI is redefining e-commerce customer interactions, providing instant support when human agents are unavailable. AI tools can:


- Respond in real-time across time zones
- Support multiple languages to cater to a global customer base
- Cover for human staff during off-peak hours
- Assist human agents amid surges in demand


### Real-world impact


Consider Clove, a footwear apparel company that[partnered with Yuma AI to reduce its first response time](https://yuma.ai/case-studies/how-clove-achieved-3x-roi-40-ai-automation-and-25-cost-savings-in-just-3-months-with-yuma-ai) during peak hours from over one day to a mere three minutes. Clove successfully addressed this pivotal issue, enhancing customer satisfaction while fully automating over 40% of its CX tickets and achieving a threefold return on investment (ROI).


## #2 Time savings


According to HubSpot, **31% of customer service professionals rate time savings as a leading advantage of AI.** AI is changing the way CX teams operate, allowing them to[automate order status questions and FAQs](https://yuma.ai/blogs/customer-service-automation-software) and other repetitive work:


- Manage order status questions
- Respond to FAQs
- Troubleshoot
- Manage account updates
- Field billing, shipping, and return requests


This, in turn, streamlines efficiency and enhances productivity. These AI systems can understand and respond to mundane queries quickly and accurately, freeing human agents to tackle more pressing and complex matters.


### Real-world impact


Take Omnie, a customer service outsourcing company that joined forces with Yuma AI to revamp its CX. In addition to achieving 50% full automation rates for its top clients, they were able to[reduce their clients’ workload by nine full workdays in a month](https://yuma.ai/case-studies/omnie-and-yuma-ai-reshaping-e-commerce-customer-service-together) .


## #3 Faster responses


In the high-demand environment of e-commerce, CX teams often face a significant workload. Facing a nonstop influx of queries, human agents can become overwhelmed, leading to slower response times and difficulty keeping up with customer needs.


According to HubSpot, **30% of customer service specialists believe faster response times and less time spent on requests are essential perks of AI** . As we alluded to earlier, timely responses are crucial, making it all the more important that AI can quickly:


- Resolve routine FAQs and provide immediate answers using pre-programmed instructions
- Categorize customer queries and filter them to the appropriate destination
- Apply machine learning to predict behaviors and suggest appropriate outputs based on past interactions


### Real-world impact


These rapid responses lead to happier customers.[Yuma AI teamed up with MyVariations to automate 30% of its customer support tickets](https://yuma.ai/case-studies/myvariations) and immediately respond to customer queries using AI agents. As a result, the company maintained a 4.7/5 Trustpilot rating, underscoring its tremendous customer satisfaction.


## #4 Ability to address complex queries


By fully automating repetitive queries, many e-commerce platforms have been able to free up their human agents to focus on more nuanced issues. This is welcome news for customer service professionals, **28% of whom rank time available for complex queries as a key utility of AI in CX** .


CX professionals see the value in having more time to address challenging issues that require human empathy, contextual understanding, and critical thinking. AI solutions free them up by:


- Autonomously handling orders and payments
- Promptly addressing pre-sale questions
- Automatically cleaning support inboxes


### Real-world impact


Medical equipment company MFI Medical partnered with Yuma AI to fully automate 45% of its customer support tickets and slash first response times by nearly 90%. In the process, they[saved eight days per month](https://yuma.ai/case-studies/how-mfi-medical-cut-first-response-time-by-over-87-and-automated-45-of-customer-inquiries-with-yuma-ai) , a level of efficiency that allowed the team to reallocate resources to higher-value tasks.

Although AI isn’t perfect (it can sometimes hallucinate) it’s come a long way, particularly among top models such as GPT-4 Turbo, which is[97.5% accurate](https://github.com/vectara/hallucination-leaderboard) . - Learn from past interactions to continually improve response quality
- Integrate with knowledge bases to deliver precise information
