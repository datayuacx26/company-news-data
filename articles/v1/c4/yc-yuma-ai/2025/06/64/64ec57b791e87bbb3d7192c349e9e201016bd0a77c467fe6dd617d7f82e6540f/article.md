# Elyn Shuts Its Doors: What It Means for E-Commerce Returns — Plus 6 Alternatives to Consider

It’s a sad day in the e-commerce world: **Elyn AI** , the innovative French startup best known for its “try-before-you-buy” model and returns management system, has shut down. At Yuma AI, we specialize in AI-driven customer support, and we had integrated with Elyn to help merchants automate returns-related tasks. Though we’re disappointed to lose a partner that helped redefine the post-purchase experience, we also know e-commerce businesses still need robust returns solutions. In light of Elyn’s closure, here’s a look at how Elyn stood out—and six legitimate alternatives to consider.


Launched in 2022, Elyn attracted attention with its unique focus on converting refund requests into exchanges, store credits, or gift cards. By offering features like Instant Exchanges and a flexible voucher system, Elyn claimed to help merchants retain up to 30% of revenue otherwise lost through refunds. The Elyn company also emphasized data-driven strategies, offering analytics on return patterns to shape future policies.


## 6 Reliable Alternatives for E-Commerce Returns


While Elyn’s shutdown leaves a void, there are several established platforms that excel in managing returns and exchanges. Below are six real, reputable alternatives worth exploring:


### **1.**[Baback](https://baback.co/)


Baback provides a straightforward returns portal that emphasizes both merchant flexibility and customer satisfaction. Designed in to suit smaller boutiques as well as larger brands, Baback supports automated workflows, clear exchange processes, and customizable return policies. Beyond just handling refunds, Baback aligns with a customer-centric philosophy by offering merchants the tools to convert potential losses into future sales opportunities.


### **2.**[Rever](https://www.itsrever.com/en)


Rever focuses on creating a seamless post-purchase experience through efficient returns and exchange management. It offers a simple, user-friendly dashboard that lets merchants keep track of return reasons, process exchanges faster, and gain data-driven insights into customer preferences. Rever aims to balance the operational needs of merchants with a smooth, shopper-friendly interface, making it another strong option for businesses seeking a great alternative.


### **3.**[Loop Returns](https://www.loopreturns.com/)


Popular among direct-to-consumer (DTC) brands, **Loop Returns** specializes in turning refunds into exchanges. Much like Elyn’s approach, Loop encourages customers to swap sizes or colors before they opt for refunds. Its dashboard simplifies exchange workflows, and it integrates seamlessly with Shopify for a quick setup. Loop’s strong focus on user experience makes it ideal for smaller brands looking to scale.


### **4.**[yayloh](https://yayloh.com/)


Renowned in Europe for its fashion-focused returns platform, **yayloh** emphasizes sustainability and lowering return rates. It provides advanced analytics to understand why customers return products, which helps brands refine sizing and product descriptions. Yayloh also offers easy integrations with Shopify and Gorgias, making it a good plug-and-play option—especially for apparel and accessories sellers looking to reduce refunds.


### **5.**[Narvar](https://corp.narvar.com/)


With a strong foothold in post-purchase experiences, **Narvar** provides a holistic suite of tools for tracking, returns, and customer communications. One standout feature is its branded returns portal, which lets customers initiate returns or exchanges online with minimal friction. While Narvar caters to larger enterprise retailers, it also works well for midsize brands needing a flexible, data-rich returns solution.


### **6.**[Happy Returns](https://happyreturns.com/)


Owned by PayPal, **Happy Returns** focuses on simplifying the returns process both online and in-store. Their “Returns Bar” network allows customers to drop off unwanted items at physical kiosks, eliminating the need for return labels and packaging. This offline component offers a differentiator for brands wanting to provide quick, hassle-free exchanges to local customers. The system integrates with major e-commerce platforms, making setup relatively straightforward.


### Navigating the Road Ahead


Elyn’s shutdown highlights the importance of choosing returns platforms that adapt quickly to market challenges. Its success in boosting cash retention and streamlining exchanges pointed to a strong demand for flexible, user-centered solutions. As you look for alternatives, consider each platform’s feature set, integrations, and long-term viability. For many merchants, it’s not just about simplifying returns—it’s about improving brand loyalty and reducing operational overhead.
