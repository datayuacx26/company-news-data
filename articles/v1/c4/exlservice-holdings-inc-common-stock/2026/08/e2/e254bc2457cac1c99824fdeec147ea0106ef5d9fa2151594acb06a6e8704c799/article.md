## Engagement strategy


Our data-informed community engagement strategy is designed to be sustainable, measurable, and aligned with our long-term objectives.


We accomplish this strategy by:


- Creating shared value through programs that benefit the communities in which we work.
- Maintaining a strong commitment to ethical practices and transparency.
- Constantly innovating to leverage our expertise in data, analytics, and AI to impart relevant skills to adolescents and young people in our partnership programs.


## Our community engagement strategy is organized around four core pillars:


##### Education


We invest in education programs that expand access to quality learning for underserved youth and communities. Our STEM education initiatives in India, South Africa, and the Philippines equip students with critical science, technology, engineering, and mathematics skills, opening pathways to careers in the digital economy.


##### Digital skills


Leveraging our expertise as a data and AI company, we design and deliver digital skilling programs that build technology literacy and employability for young people and job seekers in the communities where we operate. These programs are structured as multi-year engagements to ensure lasting, measurable impact.


##### Employee engagement


Our employees are our most powerful community asset. Through structured volunteering opportunities, employee giving programs, and the EXL Social Impact Awards, we foster a culture of civic participation and social responsibility that connects EXLers to the causes they care about most.


##### Community impact


Beyond education and skills, EXL supports broader community wellbeing through initiatives spanning environmental stewardship, health, and economic empowerment. We measure outcomes rigorously, using a data-informed approach to continuously improve the depth and breadth of our community impact.


## Partnership strategy and highlights


EXL's community engagement strategy comes to life through long-term, outcomes-focused partnerships with educational institutions, non-profit organizations, and government bodies in the regions where we operate. Our partnerships are built on multi-year commitments and shared accountability for impact, with a focus on scalable, high-quality program delivery. Key partnerships span STEM education, digital employability training, and community wellbeing programs across India, the Philippines, South Africa, and the United States. We select and evaluate partners using a rigorous assessment framework that considers mission alignment, organizational capacity, and demonstrated community impact. Partnership highlights and outcomes data are updated annually in our Sustainability Report.
