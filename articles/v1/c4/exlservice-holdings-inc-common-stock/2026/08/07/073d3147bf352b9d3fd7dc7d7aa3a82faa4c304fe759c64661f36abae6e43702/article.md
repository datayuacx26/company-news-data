A path to sustainable outcomes


##


Our sustainability program reflects and advances our mission to be a company that continually improves, reinvents, and evolves.


[Sustainability report](https://info1.exlservice.com/hubfs/Asset-library/Corporate/EXL-Sustainability-Report-2024.pdf)


###


EXL aligns sustainability with business value by embedding it across our value chain. We integrate sustainability into our core business through deeper engagement across EXL functions; ongoing dialogue with our CEO, Board, and senior executives; and better measurement of the returns on investment of the sustainability initiatives we choose to undertake. And we continue to explore how our services and solutions help support the progress of our clients’ sustainability goals. This approach helps strengthen ROI, improve operational efficiency, support stakeholder confidence, and drive positive social and environmental outcomes. It is reflected in our governance, standards, priorities, and stakeholder engagement.


### Priority topics


We conduct regular materiality assessments to identify the sustainability topics most relevant to our business and stakeholders. To read more about our Materiality Assessment process and outcomes see \[link to Materiality page\].


### Standards and frameworks


EXL's sustainability reporting aligns with globally recognized standards and frameworks to ensure transparency and comparability. We report in accordance with the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Standards and align with the Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB) framework for the Technology & IT Services sector. We incorporate the recommendations of the Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) in our climate risk reporting, and we are a signatory to the United Nations Global Compact (UNGC), aligning our strategy with its ten principles on human rights, labor, environment, and anti-corruption. We submit annual climate data to CDP and align our science-based emissions reduction targets with the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) framework.


### Stakeholder engagement


Meaningful engagement with our stakeholders is central to how EXL shapes and advances its sustainability strategy. We identify and prioritize our key stakeholder groups — including clients, employees, investors, suppliers, communities, and regulators — and engage them through a variety of channels throughout the year. Client feedback informs our service delivery and sustainability integration. Employee engagement surveys, town halls, and listening sessions shape our People programs. Our annual investor outreach program promotes transparency on sustainability performance and governance. Supplier engagement through our onboarding questionnaire and Supplier Standards of Conduct advances sustainability across our value chain. Insights from these engagements inform our materiality assessment process and are reflected in our sustainability strategy.
