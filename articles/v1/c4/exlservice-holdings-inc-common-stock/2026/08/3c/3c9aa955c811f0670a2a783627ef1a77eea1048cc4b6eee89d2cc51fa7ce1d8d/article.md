###


At the heart of EXL lies a commitment to nurturing our most valuable asset: our people. We put our people, and their growth, at the center of everything we do, investing in their success and cultivating an inclusive culture driven by our core values.


EXL's approach to human capital management (HCM) is built on the conviction that our people are our greatest source of competitive advantage. Our HCM strategy is integrated with our business objectives, ensuring that talent decisions directly support our ability to deliver outcomes for clients. Overseen by our board's Compensation and Talent Management Committee, our people programs are designed to build a future-ready and highly engaged workforce that thrives in an AI-first environment. We measure our progress through regular employee engagement surveys, retention and development metrics, and workforce representation data, holding ourselves accountable to continuous improvement.


## Culture


Our culture is built around what we call the Better Way Code. These guidelines help our employees embed our core values into their day-to-day work including commitment, effort, inspiration, and learning.


#####


## Employee engagement


Whether working from home or in the office, EXL strives to engage and communicate with our people. That means ensuring our employees, leaders, and teams effectively work, collaborate, and feel connected to each other and our culture. To that end, we’re constantly using and improving digital communications, implementing collaboration platforms, and surveying our people to receive valuable insights into employee sentiment.


## Talent


Our talent development strategy emphasizes building a distributed workforce for our multifaceted talent needs. We’re focused on developing self-driven, democratized learning and growth experiences for our employees based on their personal goals and skills. This includes using AI, curated libraries, and intelligent web-sourced content. We use data, technology, and business insights to create a global talent network to build our future-ready workforce.


## Diversity, Inclusion and Belonging


Our diversity, inclusion and belonging philosophy is to create an inclusive work environment that harnesses the power of diversity to drive organizational success. We remain committed to acquiring and developing the best talent wherever they are, whoever they are, and building a leadership that represents the gender diversity of our world and clients. EXL encourages the formation of employee resource groups (ERG), such as our Women at EXL ERG supporting women at EXL, and EXL Pride ERG supporting LGBTQ+ people and allies at EXL.


[Learn more](https://www.exlservice.com/about/sustainability/people/diversity-inclusion)


## Health, safety, and wellbeing


Our people are our biggest asset, and we are committed to creating a safe and healthy workplace for our them – and we’ve got the awards to prove it.


- International Institute of Security and Safety Management Awards 2018-2022: Industrial Sector Safety Awards in IT/ITeS Category & Safety Excellence Award for Women Safety
- Winner of Golden Peacock Global award for ESG 2023 & 2024
- Sword of Honour Winner 2023 & 2024 : British Safety Council
- World Safety Forum: Global Safety Summit 2022: National Environment Award & National Safest Workplace Award
- 2023 Five Star Rating from the British Safety Council in Occupational Health & Safety for our India and UK Centers
