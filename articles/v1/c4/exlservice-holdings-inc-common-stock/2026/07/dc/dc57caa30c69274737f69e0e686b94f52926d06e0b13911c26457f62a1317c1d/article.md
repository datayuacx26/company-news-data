#### EXL Achieves Leader Designation in Everest Group PEAK Matrix®


Assessment 2021 for Property and Casualty Insurance BPS, and top-most spot for market impact


Key EXL strengths cited by Everest Group in the report include:


- **Go-to-market strategy** is shifting from a service-led approach to a solution-led approach, supported by investments in domain-infused AI-led platforms, advanced analytics and digital point solutions
- **Expansion of clientele and operations delivery scope** in the reinsurance and brokerage segment
- **Geographical diversification investments** , expanding beyond the US market and into Australia, Latin America and Asia Pacific
- **Exploration of outcome-based commercials** , pivoting away from an FTE-led operating model to more of a technology-led operating model
- EXL’s reference buyers expressed appreciation of operational delivery quality on SLAs, flexible approach and communication
