# Samit Gayen


VP and Head of Growth – Banking and Diversified Industries


## About our leaders


### Samit Gayen


Driving data-led transformation across Australia and New Zealand, Samit partners with enterprises to turn Data & AI into measurable business outcomes. At EXL, he works with leading organisations across banking, financial services, utilities, retail, transportation, travel, telco, and technology to deliver complex transformation programs—helping them harness data, redesign operating models, and unlock sustainable value. He is particularly focused on combining digital innovation with human-led problem solving to create practical, high-impact solutions that deliver real business results.


Prior to EXL, Samit worked with leading global system integrators and Australian IT services firms, shaping his approach to delivery and client engagement. He is known for his hands-on, relationship-first style—investing time to understand client goals and working collaboratively to deliver outcomes that matter.
