# Sezzle Provides Update on Antitrust Case Against Shopify

# Sezzle Secures $300 Million Credit Facility - Expanding Capacity and Lowering Cost of Capital


May 12, 2026


Minneapolis, MN, May 12, 2026 ([GLOBE NEWSWIRE](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/12/3293417/0/en/sezzle-provides-update-on-antitrust-case-against-shopify.html?_gl=1*8dp66q*_up*MQ..*_ga*MTM2NzMyNTIuMTc4NDI5NjA4Ng..*_ga_B6167QB2TF*czE3ODQyOTYwODUkbzEkZzAkdDE3ODQyOTYwODUkajYwJGwwJGg0NzY3MjM4MDk.*_ga_ERWPGTJ5X8*czE3ODQyOTYwODUkbzEkZzAkdDE3ODQyOTYwODUkajYwJGwwJGgw) ) -- **Sezzle Inc. (NASDAQ:**[SEZL](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=m9uyYC6v7kz0u2oGyrCD0JTDYZgv7IK4mx6m075CJfv7GE_-49ZjLBIMuwEbQ5uyp8bq0KnAiSkEr882VlogR1Ga7FqO6T8NiMUfU-0OUr3atIgd2pHulHgM6gUjRX1h) **)** ( **Sezzle** or **Company** ) // Purpose-driven digital payment platform,[Sezzle](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=ci3t70ndAQnWytTAsjNOwAEguuQiI0O44oTwYQVMoXOPMHggvcXBO6WSXgwTnGgmQnSPDuMqg3znE14RGihD2g==) , today announced that the United States District Court for the District of Minnesota has issued an Opinion and Order granting in part and denying in part Shopify, Inc.’s motion to dismiss Sezzle’s antitrust action.


The Court allowed Sezzle’s core claims to proceed, including claims for monopolization and attempted monopolization under Section 2 of the Sherman Act, unlawful restraint of trade under Section 1 of the Sherman Act, parallel claims under the Minnesota Antitrust Law of 1971, and a claim under the Minnesota Deceptive Trade Practices Act.


The Court dismissed without prejudice Sezzle’s unlawful tying claim under Section 1 of the Sherman Act and the corresponding portion of Sezzle’s state-law antitrust claim. The remaining claims will proceed.


The case, captioned *Sezzle, Inc. v. Shopify, Inc* ., File No. 25-cv-2395 (ECT/SGE), is pending in the United States District Court for the District of Minnesota. The Court’s ruling is procedural in nature and does not constitute a finding of liability against Shopify.


A copy of the Court’s Opinion and Order is available on Sezzle’s Investor Relations website at:[https://investors.sezzle.com/resource/sezzle-v-shopify/](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=qBmsBSn3cpwAL-vNcv40k8Lt3VvjKTyytMIohRUFTN0lu1pDrpP6U5yCHUxqzyPi90KDlAbWTFVx2tqWwQsTH52QUh9Fuz_HsHxXUex12z1J597HAX7jUlBMRY8EvslB7CkZK5Vc5D7vV9MrsQYWY8jBlZ6FQH44sPtIy1JJR9zk3SwDzDN79tuwMMxlAXaP) .
