# Sezzle to Join S&P SmallCap 600


December 9, 2025


Minneapolis, MN December 9, 2025 ([GLOBE NEWSWIRE](https://api.kscope.io/ks-doc-view?key=f7ffcb3f-03c2-11ee-acee-0ed29589fc89&content=benznews&docid=b46fc89624d972bb8bfb930111441b977c3e2529) ) -- **Sezzle Inc. (NASDAQ:**[SEZL](https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/stocks/sezl) **) (Sezzle or Company)** // Purpose-driven digital payment platform,[Sezzle](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=bPwHDRmTEFohVK9OX4V7BrM7jivp6YjRspFcrWxN71ZsOja-R97bt0f8xL38B7hJtYreay-xMeIu205kbIBfsw==) , today announced it will join the S&P SmallCap 600 index, effective after market close on Friday, December 12, 2025. The S&P SmallCap 600 seeks to measure the small-cap segment of the U.S. equity market.


"Our inclusion in the S&P SmallCap 600 highlights the progress Sezzle has made and sets the stage for our next phase of growth. It marks another step in strengthening the foundation we're building on," said Charlie Youakim, Sezzle Executive Chairman and CEO.
