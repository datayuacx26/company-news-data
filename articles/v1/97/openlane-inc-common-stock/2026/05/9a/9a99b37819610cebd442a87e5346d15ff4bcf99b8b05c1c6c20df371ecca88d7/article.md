Filling your dealership with the right inventory, at the right time and price can be a challenge for many dealers. Physical auctions have long been the go-to, but today’s fast-moving market demands a different approach.


That’s why more dealers are turning to online wholesale auto auctions.


Digital marketplaces like OPENLANE make it easier to source the vehicles your customers want quickly, efficiently and with confidence. Here are six reasons why making the switch can transform your sourcing strategy.


## 1. Be First to Local Inventory


Don’t wait for the transport truck to arrive at the physical auction lanes. Get an edge on your local competition by seeing inventory before it hits the physical auction. By sourcing vehicles within your region, you can keep your margins healthy and transport costs to a minimum while still getting first pick of the freshest local stock.


## 2. Expand Your Reach for the “Must-Haves”


While you’re looking for that “needle in a haystack” to fill a specific customer request, OPENLANE’s nationwide network has you covered. Our broad reach allows you to find the specific makes and models that might not be available in your immediate area. With new inventory added daily from across the country, you have a 24/7 tool for finding the vehicles you need to round up your lot.


## 3. Find the Right Vehicles Faster


Searching for the right car shouldn’t slow you down. Online tools make it easy to find what you need in minutes.


OPENLANE helps you move faster with:


- Advanced filters for features, fuel type, condition and more
- Saved searches for your most-used criteria
- Real-time alerts when matching vehicles are listed


Less time searching means more time selling.


## 4. Buy with Confidence using OPENLANE Intelligence


The days of “buying blind” are over. We give you a transparent, honest look at every vehicle so you can bid with the same confidence you’d feel on the physical auction floor.


- **Smarter Condition Reports** : 25+ interior and exterior photos, vehicle history and test drive notes based on in-depth inspections by experienced inspectors.
- **Damage Detection** : AI-powered imaging that works with an inspector to highlight dings and scratches you might have missed on a quick walk-around.
- **Engine Audio Analysis** : Engine audio recordings compared to thousands of “healthy recordings” assisted by AI to identify anomalies faster.
- **OBD2 Decoder** : OBD2 codes translated into plain language and prioritized by importance so you can understand the reconditioning work needed.
- **OPENLANE Predictive Pricing** : VIN-specific pricing guidance with forecasted valuations for today and the next 30, 60 and 90 days.


Every listing also includes a free AutoCheckⓇ report, so you can make informed decisions before you bid. To learn more about the OPENLANE inspection process , click[here](https://www.openlane.com/inspections/) .


## 5. Reduce Risk with Stronger Arbitration Coverage


When certain undisclosed items on a vehicle are not as described, arbitration matters. That’s where OPENLANE stands out. We move beyond the uncertainty of traditional “As-Is” sales, with 93% of vehicles on our platform eligible for our As-described Guarantee.


OPENLANE’s policies go beyond standard NAAA arbitration policies by covering more of what matters in today’s vehicles. That includes modern features like backup cameras, navigation systems and panoramic roofs, areas many competitors exclude.


You also benefit from:


- Coverage on vehicles priced under $6,000
- No restrictive single-item cost limits
- A 7-day As-described Guarantee, with the option to extend to 14 days


We take a more flexible, dealer-focused approach to arbitration, so you can buy with confidence.


[Learn more about arbitration](https://www.openlane.com/insights/how-openlanes-arbitration-policy-enhances-buyer-protection/)


## 6. Lower Costs and Improved Margins


Online sourcing doesn’t just save time, it can help you reduce your overall costs.


With OPENLANE:


- Lower fees help reduce acquisition costs
- No hidden fees that eat up your margin
- Transparent, integrated transportation price at time of bidding
- We handle the title transferring for you, limiting the time and burden of manual paperwork


Those savings add up quickly and can make a real impact on your margins.


## Get Support from a Team That Knows Your Business


Even in a digital marketplace, having the right support matters. You’re not doing this alone.


OPENLANE’s sales team is trained to understand dealership needs and market trends. Whether you need help with a purchase or guidance on strategy, you’ll be working with people who know your business.


The shift to online wholesale auto auctions isn’t a trend, it’s a smarter way to run your dealership. When you move your sourcing strategy online, you can expand your reach, save time, reduce risk and improve profitability, all at once.
