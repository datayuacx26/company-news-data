Independent dealers are heading to Denver for one of the year’s biggest industry events, and OPENLANE is ready to help make the most of it.


From June 21–24, visit OPENLANEⓇ at **Booth 809** during the NIADA Convention & Expo, where we’re teaming up with **AFC** and **autoniq** Ⓡ to showcase how our solutions work together to help dealers find the car, buy the car and finance the car—all from trusted brands designed to support dealer success.


Whether you’re looking to source more inventory, improve cash flow, gain deeper market insights or streamline your wholesale process, our teams will be ready to show you how we’re making wholesale easy, together.


## **Three Trusted Solutions. One Powerful Booth Experience.**


This year at NIADA, you’ll find OPENLANE, AFC and autoniq together at Booth 809, giving dealers access to solutions that support every stage of the wholesale journey.


### Find the car with autoniq


Successful sourcing starts with the right data.


autoniq helps dealers identify opportunities faster with lightning-fast VIN scanning, access to more than 400,000 vehicles from over 500 auction sources and a single view of the industry’s most trusted wholesale data. By bringing key market insights into one platform, autoniq helps dealers make smarter buying decisions with confidence.


### Buy the car with OPENLANE


OPENLANE is the most-preferred digital wholesale marketplace by dealers, helping buyers source inventory wherever and whenever they need it. From dealer trades and rental vehicles to exclusive off-lease inventory, OPENLANE offers access to the vehicles today’s customers are looking for.


Dealers can buy with confidence thanks to comprehensive condition reports featuring extensive photos, detailed inspections, complimentary AutoCheck® reports and innovative technologies such as AI-powered Damage Detection, Engine Audio Analysis and Predictive Pricing tools designed to help protect margins.


### Finance the car with AFC


AFC helps independent dealers keep inventory moving with customized floorplan financing solutions tailored to their business needs.


With competitive financing options, dedicated local support teams and 24/7 account management through the AFCDealer® platform, AFC gives dealers the flexibility and buying power they need to acquire inventory from OPENLANE and more than 1,400 additional sources.


## **See How It All Works Together**


One of the biggest advantages of stopping by Booth 809 is seeing how these solutions connect to simplify wholesale operations.


Dealers can access autoniq data directly within the OPENLANE marketplace through the autoniq Chrome® Extension and use AFC financing within the buying process, creating a more seamless experience from sourcing to purchase to financing.


It’s a connected approach designed to help dealers save time, reduce complexity and make more informed business decisions.
