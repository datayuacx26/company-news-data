## What is Absolute Sale?


When you mark a car as an Absolute Sale, you’re telling every buyer on the marketplace one simple thing: *The highest bidder wins.*


This transparency creates urgency and excitement among buyers because they know the vehicle is guaranteed to sell.


On OPENLANE, Absolute Sale ends every day at 3 p.m. CT, creating a high-energy “closing time” that mimics the excitement of a live auction block. Buyers often return during the final minutes to compete for vehicles they have been watching throughout the day.


## Why Dealers Use Absolute Sale


One of the biggest challenges in wholesaling is uncertainty. Dealers will often list a vehicle and wait, hoping the right bid comes through. By switching on Absolute Sale, you stop waiting and start driving the action.


**Higher Buyer Engagement**


Buyers prioritize these listings because they know there is no reserve standing in the way of a completed deal. That certainty gets buyers in the game, driving up both visibility and traction for your inventory.


**Ignite Bidding Wars**


Stripping away the reserve creates a high-energy, competitive environment where independent and franchise buyers actively contend for the winning slot. This surge in buyer action directly translates to a fast-paced, high-volume bidding frenzy on your vehicles.


In fact, 35% of cars get 10 or more additional offers once an Absolute Sale is launched, proving just how quickly these listings spark intense competition.


**Faster Inventory Turn**


Absolute Sale helps dealers move aging or unwanted inventory faster, reducing holding costs and freeing up capital for fresh inventory.


**No Back-and-Forth Negotiation**


Once the countdown ends at 3 p.m. CT, the sale is complete. No lengthy negotiations. No chasing approvals. Just a faster, more efficient wholesale process.


## How to Launch Absolute Sale: 3 Easy Options


You don’t have to be a tech expert to use Absolute Sale on OPENLANE. Dealers can activate the feature in three simple ways depending on their goals and inventory strategy.


1. **Launch in Absolute Sale**


If you receive a bid you’re comfortable accepting, you can manually toggle the vehicle to “Absolute” at any point during the listing.


Once activated, buyers will immediately see:


- An “Absolute Sale” badge
- A live countdown timer
- Clear messaging that the vehicle is guaranteed to sell to the highest bidder


This creates urgency and tells buyers: *“The reserve has effectively been met — if you want this car, bid now.”*


**Best For:**


- Dealers who want more control over timing
- Maximizing late-stage bidding activity


1. **Automated Absolute Sale**


For busy dealers, this is often the easiest and most efficient option.


When listing your vehicle, simply set the minimum price you’d be fully comfortable accepting. Once bidding reaches that number, the system automatically converts the listing into an Absolute Sale— no manual action required.


That means:


- No monitoring bids all day
- No manual updates
- No missed selling opportunities


The system handles everything automatically while buyers compete in real time.


**Best For:**


- High-volume dealers
- Busy operations teams
- Dealers focused on efficiency and speed


1. **Showcase in Absolute Sale at Floor**


Have a vehicle you’re looking to turn over quickly?


You can place the eligible vehicle directly into the Absolute Sale category before any bids are received. Your floor price becomes the opening bid, and once bidding begins, the vehicle becomes a guaranteed vehicle sale.


This option works especially well for:


- Units you want to maximize marketplace exposure
- Listings priced to sell without bids after day 1
- All units eligible according to our algorithm


**Best For:**


- Rapid inventory liquidation
- Units taking up valuable lot space
- Units priced to sell

To make sure your experience with Absolute Sale is successful, keep these four best practices in mind:


- **Let automation drive the competition:** Use Automated Absolute Sale to trigger bidding wars without babysitting your screen. Once your minimum price is met, the system automatically flips the listing to “Absolute,” signaling to buyers that the car will sell, creating instant urgency.


- **Price smart with Predictive Pricing** : Unsure where to set your floor price? Leverage[OPENLANE Predictive Pricing](https://www.openlane.com/insights/how-predictive-pricing-is-transforming-wholesale-auto-auctions/?_thumbnail_id=6019) before you launch. This data-driven tool analyzes real-time market trends, historical auction data, and current demand to help you pinpoint the sweet spot. Setting an accurate, competitive starting price ensures you trigger automated absolute status quickly and spark an immediate bidding war.


- **List your unit early** : While the heaviest bidding action happens in the final 30 minutes leading up to the 3 p.m. CT deadline, don’t wait until the last minute to launch. Toggling or listing your vehicle in Absolute Sale earlier in the day gives the OPENLANE marketplace maximum time to surface your vehicle. Buyers can add it to their watchlists, review the condition report, and prepare their budgets before the high-energy closing window begins.


- **Watch the clock** : Remember the magic happens at 3 p.m. CT. Most of the heavy bidding occurs in the final 30 minutes of the sale, so don’t panic if you don’t see a flurry of action at 10am.
