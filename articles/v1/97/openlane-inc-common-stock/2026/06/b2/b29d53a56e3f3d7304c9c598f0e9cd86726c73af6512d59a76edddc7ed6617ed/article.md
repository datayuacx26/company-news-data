# OPENLANE heads to Portugal for a two-week roadshow from 15 to 27 June


** 10 Jun 2026


**Tienen, 5 June 2026 – Following successful editions across Central Europe, OPENLANE is bringing its popular “OPENLANE On Tour” roadshow to Portugal this June. From 15 to 27 June 2026, the Portuguese OPENLANE team will travel across the Lisbon and Porto regions, meeting automotive professionals face-to-face and bringing the benefits of Europe’s trusted digital wholesale marketplace directly to customers.**


Driving OPENLANE-wrapped MINI COOPER® vehicles, the team will connect with car dealers and traders through a combination of customer visits and a dedicated event, offering personalised support, practical platform guidance, and expert advice on cross-border used car trading. Whether automotive professionals are new to OPENLANE or experienced users, the “OPENLANE On Tour” roadshow provides an opportunity to discover new ways to source stock, optimise operations, and grow their business.


## Meet the OPENLANE team in Lisbon


As part of the roadshow, OPENLANE will host a dedicated customer event in Alcochete, near Lisbon, on Thursday 18 June and Friday 19 June.


Designed as a flexible drop-in experience, automotive professionals are invited to visit the OPENLANE team at Casa das Oliveiras, Av. do Canto do Pinheiro 528, 2890-121 Alcochete, at a time that suits them. Throughout the two-day event, visitors can meet the local OPENLANE team, connect with fellow industry professionals, and enjoy informal conversations in a relaxed setting.


The event offers a valuable opportunity for dealers and traders to engage directly with OPENLANE account managers and platform experts, discover the latest developments and opportunities available on the marketplace, and exchange experiences with peers from across the automotive sector. Attendees are also encouraged to share their feedback and insights, helping shape future improvements so OPENLANE can continue to evolve in line with customer needs. Alongside networking and business discussions, guests can enjoy refreshments, interactive activities, and a range of giveaways throughout the event.


## Prefer a one-to-one meeting?


Customers in the Lisbon and Porto regions who are unable to attend the Alcochete event can request a one-to-one visit from the OPENLANE team at their own premises. These personalised meetings offer the opportunity to discuss business challenges, explore sourcing opportunities, and receive tailored advice on getting the most from the OPENLANE platform.


***“OPENLANE On Tour is all about bringing our people closer to our customers,” said Grainne van Berkum, President of OPENLANE Europe. “Digital marketplaces create efficiency, but nothing replaces a face-to-face conversation. We look forward to meeting our customers across Portugal, listening to their feedback, and helping them unlock new opportunities for growth.”***


Automotive professionals interested in attending the Alcochete event or booking a one-to-one meeting during the tour are advised to register in advance.[Pre-registration is required for event attendance](https://info.openlane.eu/en/openlane-on-tour-portugal/) .


## About OPENLANE Europe


OPENLANE Europe, a business unit of global vehicle remarketing and technology solutions provider OPENLANE (NYSE: OPLN), makes used car wholesale easy for its customers so they can be successful. The company seamlessly connects OEMS, Fleet owners, wholesalers, and dealers with buyers in more than 50 countries across Europe. Its robust online car marketplace reduces risk, improves transparency and streamlines transactions. Headquartered in Belgium, OPENLANE Europe has sales operations in Belgium, France, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Spain, and the United Kingdom.
