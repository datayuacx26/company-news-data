## DealerFest 2026 FAQs


### **What is DealerFest 2026?**


DealerFest 2026 is OPENLANE’s month-long summer sales event that rewards dealers for qualifying wholesale transactions completed throughout July. Dealers earn points that can be redeemed for exclusive prizes.


### **When does DealerFest 2026 end?**


DealerFest runs from **July 1 through July 31, 2026** . All qualifying transactions completed during the event period count toward your points total.


### **How do I earn DealerFest points?**


Dealers earn points by completing qualifying activities on OPENLANE, including buying vehicles, selling vehicles, choosing OPENLANE Transport and flooring eligible purchases with AFC. Qualifying commercial vehicle purchases earn an additional bonus point.


### **How do I redeem my DealerFest points?**


Dealers don’t need to do anything on their end to redeem points. Once certain point thresholds are reached, the dealer is automatically awarded the prize. Your sales rep will reach out to you with details on getting your prizes.


### **Do I need to register for DealerFest?**


Eligible dealers can participate by using the OPENLANE marketplace during the event. Visit the[DealerFest page](http://openlane.com/dealerfest) for complete eligibility requirements and official Terms & Conditions.


### **Can new OPENLANE dealers participate?**


Yes. New dealers can join OPENLANE during DealerFest and may also qualify for introductory buy or sell fee credits, depending on eligibility and dealership type.


### **Where can I learn more about DealerFest?**


Visit the **OPENLANE DealerFest** page to view complete program details, qualifying transactions, prizes, eligibility requirements and official Terms & Conditions.
