The used car business moves fast and your time matters. Every hour spent away from the dealership is an hour you’re not closing deals or planning your next inventory move.


That is why more independent and franchise automotive dealers are rethinking how they source wholesale inventory. Instead of spending a full day traveling to a physical auction, waiting for the right lane and hoping the right vehicles show up, many are building a stronger vehicle sourcing strategy by switching to digital auctions.


This shift is about more than convenience. Online vehicle sourcing can give dealers broader inventory access, better condition information and more control over when and how they buy. With OPENLANE, dealers can evaluate nationwide wholesale inventory, review detailed condition information and keep their sourcing process moving without putting the rest of the dealership on hold.


Here is how moving more of your wholesale buying online can help you protect your time, source with more confidence and make smarter inventory decisions.


## 1. Online auto auctions help you control your schedule


At an in-person auction, you have to work around someone else’s clock. You may spend hours waiting for a specific lane, only to get outbid or realize the vehicles available do not fit your needs.


When comparing online vs. in-person auctions, schedule control is one of the clearest advantages of digital sourcing.


## 2. Your sourcing network expands beyond your local market


When you rely on nearby physical auctions, your options are limited to the vehicles available in that place at that time. If local supply is tight or pricing does not support your margins, your choices can narrow quickly.


With digital wholesale marketplaces, you can source inventory both close to home and across the country, giving you the flexibility to buy wherever the right vehicle becomes available. You can also access inventory earlier in the remarketing cycle, helping you secure vehicles before they reach the auction lane and before your local competitors have the chance to bid on them.


Unlike traditional wholesale auctions, digital marketplaces let dealers compare inventory across multiple markets without leaving their dealership.


OPENLANE gives dealers access to a nationwide inventory network that includes off-lease and higher-mileage units from dealers, OEMs and financial institutions. That broader reach can help your online sourcing strategy uncover vehicles that better match local demand, whether you are looking for specific makes, trims, mileage or price points.

Some dealers still believe they need to be physically present to understand a vehicle’s condition. But a quick walkaround in a busy lane can make it easy to miss details that affect reconditioning costs, pricing and profitability.

A common reason dealers hesitate to buy online is simple: they want to know what happens if a vehicle is not what they were expecting.

## 5. You reduce the friction and overhead of physical buying


The true cost of buying in person is not only the final sale price. Travel time, fuel, lodging, meals, transportation coordination and the time away from your dealership all affect the economics of the purchase.


With OPENLANE, dealers can evaluate vehicles remotely, access integrated transportation options and use marketplace tools designed to make wholesale buying more efficient. That can help your team spend less time managing auction-day logistics and more time making disciplined inventory decisions.

## Digital Auction Frequently Asked Questions


### **Is online vehicle sourcing replacing physical auctions?**


Not completely. Many dealers still use several sourcing channels, including physical auctions, trade-ins and direct purchases. But digital wholesale vehicle sourcing gives dealers a faster, more flexible way to evaluate inventory without being limited by travel time, lane schedules or local market availability.


### **Are online wholesale auto auctions risky to buy from?**


Buying vehicles online can feel different than purchasing in person, but the right digital marketplace is designed to reduce uncertainty. Detailed Condition Reports, high-quality vehicle photos and buyer protections like the OPENLANE As Described Guarantee help dealers make informed purchasing decisions with greater confidence. By providing transparency into a vehicle’s condition before you bid, online wholesale auctions can help reduce risk while giving you access to more inventory.


### **What types of vehicles are available on OPENLANE?**


OPENLANE connects dealers to a nationwide inventory of wholesale vehicles from dealers, OEMs, fleet operators and financial institutions. Inventory includes off-lease vehicles, dealer consignments, higher-mileage units and a wide range of makes, models and price points. Whether you’re looking for late-model retail-ready inventory or value-priced vehicles that fit your market, digital sourcing gives you more opportunities to find the right vehicles close to home or across the country.


### **How can a dealership get started with digital wholesale vehicle sourcing?**


Start by identifying the inventory you need most, including makes, trims, mileage ranges, price points and turn-rate goals. Then use a digital wholesale marketplace like OPENLANE to build watchlists, review condition reports, compare opportunities and make sourcing part of your daily workflow.


### **Are online wholesale auto auctions risky to buy from?**


Buying vehicles online comes with different considerations than buying in person, but it doesn’t have to mean taking on more risk. Reputable digital wholesale marketplaces provide detailed Condition Reports, vehicle photos, title information and clear arbitration or buyer protection policies to help dealers make informed purchasing decisions before they bid.


The key is choosing a marketplace that offers transparency throughout the buying process. When dealers have access to comprehensive vehicle information and buyer protections for eligible purchases, they can source inventory online with confidence while benefiting from a broader selection and greater convenience.
