## **Frequently Asked Questions**


### **What are transactional notifications?**


Transactional notifications keep you informed about important marketplace activity, including auction updates, outbid alerts, purchases, post-sale milestones and other account activity that may require your attention.


### **What are marketing notifications?**


Marketing notifications keep you informed about OPENLANE promotions, marketplace events, new product features, educational resources and other opportunities designed to help you get more value from the marketplace.


### **Where can I receive OPENLANE notifications?**


OPENLANE notifications are available through desktop browser notifications, email and mobile push notifications, giving you flexibility to stay informed wherever you work.


### **Can I choose which notifications I receive?**


Yes. You can manage your notification preferences by logging in to OPENLANE on your desktop, then navigating to Settings -> Notifications. From there you can choose how and where you’d like to receive updates, allowing you to tailor the experience to your dealership’s workflow.
