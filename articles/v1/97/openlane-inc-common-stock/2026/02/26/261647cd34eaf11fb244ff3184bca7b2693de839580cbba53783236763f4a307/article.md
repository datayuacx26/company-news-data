# OPENLANE Europe extends its partnership with EUROCOC to keep cross-border sales moving faster


** 11 Feb 2026


**Cross-border vehicle transactions in Europe are full of opportunity— and with the right paperwork in place, everything moves smoothly and efficiently. One document in particular often determines how quickly a vehicle can be registered and put back on the road: the Certificate of Conformity or COC.**


That’s why we at OPENLANE Europe have officially extended our partnership with EUROCOC—continuing our joint mission to make COCs easier to access and cross-border vehicle sales more efficient for automotive professionals across Europe.


## Why the partnership matters


When a COC is missing, the vehicle registration process can become more of a hassle: delays, additional administration, and uncertainty about next steps can follow, especially when vehicles move between different EU markets.


By renewing this collaboration, **we continue to reduce friction in one of the most common bottlenecks in cross-border remarketing** .
