# OPENLANE travels across central Europe with roadshow

# **OPENLANE travels across central Europe** **with roadshow**


** 19 May 2025


**This June, OPENLANE is bringing its innovative marketplace** **directly to automotive professionals with the highly anticipated “OPENLANE On Tour”. Over the course of three exciting weeks, the fully branded OPENLANE bus will travel across 9 cities in Poland, Hungary, and Romania, offering a unique opportunity for dealers and car traders to connect, learn, and grow their businesses.**


From June 3rd to June 20th, the OPENLANE bus will hit the road, visiting no fewer than 9 key locations for an engaging and interactive experience. At each stop, attendees will receive hands-on support, personalized advice, and helpful guidance on how OPENLANE makes used car wholesale easy. Whether new to the platform or already experienced, dealers and car traders will gain actionable insights to enhance their strategies for success in the automotive industry.


## **Meet OPENLANE and industry peers**


Attendees will have the opportunity to meet with OPENLANE leadership and account managers, ask questions, and gain valuable insight on growing their businesses. With the chance to interact with industry peers, participants can strengthen their professional networks and forge relationships that could lead to future opportunities.


Furthermore, the tour offers participants the unique benefit of providing direct feedback to the OPENLANE team. Dealers will be encouraged to share their thoughts, voice concerns, and even vote on suggestions for platform improvements, ensuring that the community’s needs and ideas are reflected in future developments. Everyone who shows up gets the chance to score useful gear. No one leaves empty-handed!
