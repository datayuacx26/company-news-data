# OPENLANE expands with “Next-to-New”

# OPENLANE expands with “Next-to-New” vehicles: a new benchmark in used car wholesale


** 05 Mar 2025


**OPENLANE, Europe’s digital marketplace for used cars, is expanding its portfolio with “OPENLANE Next-to-New”, offering young, low-mileage vehicles to meet growing market demand.**


## Why Next-to-New?


Traditionally, OPENLANE has been the go-to marketplace for **4-5 year-old, end-of-lease vehicles** with an average of **160,000 km** . Now, it’s catering to demand for **under 2-year-old vehicles with a maximum of 50,000 km** **and no technical and/or body damage** , providing traders and dealers with high-quality inventory at competitive prices.


## Setting a New Standard


“By expanding our portfolio, we ensure buyers and sellers can capitalize on a high-demand market with ease and confidence.” says **Grainne Van Berkum, President at OPENLANE Europe** .


“Next-to-New” already makes up **20% of OPENLANE’s monthly offerings** , highlighting its rapid growth.

Finding “Next-to-New” vehicles is simple. Just log in to **openlane.eu** , go to **“Find cars”** , and use the **“Focus on” filter** . Access a new standard of high-quality used cars — just a click away!
