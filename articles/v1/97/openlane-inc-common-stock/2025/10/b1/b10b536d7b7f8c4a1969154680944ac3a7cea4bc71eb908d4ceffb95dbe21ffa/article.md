# OPENLANE Partner Referral Program – Earn 300€ for each referral


** 02 Oct 2025


**Bring great automotive businesses together — and everyone wins. Whether you’re introducing a trusted partner to OPENLANE or you’ve been referred to us, here’s how the program works, what you get, and what happens next.**


## Why participate?


- **Benefit for Referrers**


Get rewarded for your network: Starting today, you can earn 300€ in promo credit for every automotive professional you invite to OPENLANE who completes their first vehicle purchase.


- **Benefit for Referred Companies**


Welcome incentive: Get 300€ in promo credit awarded to your account when you join through a partner referral and complete your first purchase.


## How does the referral process work?


**I am referring a company:**


1. Share your unique referral link with other automotive professionals. Your unique link can be found under “My Account” > Referral Program.
2. The company you referred registers and buys a vehicle. You get rewarded with 300€ in promo credit, valid for 6 months
There’s no limit to how many rewards you can earn — the more dealers you refer, the more you gain.
3. Let your network benefit from OPENLANE. And let your business grow.


**I’ve been referred by a company:**


1. Register to openlane.eu through the unique link that was shared with you.
2. Place bids, win an auction, and complete the payment for your first car. You get rewarded with 300€ in promo credit, valid for 6 months. This will in turn trigger the promo rewarded to the company that referred you.
3. Benefit from your network and refer other automotive companies to register with openlane.eu to gain more promo’s.
