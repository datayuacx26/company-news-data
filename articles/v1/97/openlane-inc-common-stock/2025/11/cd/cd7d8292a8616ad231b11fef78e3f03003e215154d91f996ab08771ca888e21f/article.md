# The OPENLANE Auction Calendar: your go-to overview of weekly auctions


** 24 Nov 2025


**Finding the right car at the right time often comes down to one thing: being in the right auction when it happens. To make that easier than ever, we’re happy to introduce the OPENLANE Auction Calendar – now live and ready to use.**


This calendar gives you a clear overview of all our recurring auctions at fixed times, so you can plan ahead, stay organized, and never miss the auctions that matter most to you.


[View the Auction calendar](https://info.openlane.eu/en/auctions-calendar/)


## What is the auctions calendar?


The Auction Calendar gives you an at-a-glance overview of recurring auctions across markets.
Instead of searching manually or relying on memory, you can now:


- See when your preferred auctions take place every week
- Mark your calendar for upcoming auctions
- Plan your buying activity more strategically
- You get a simple, stable overview you can trust when planning your week.


**A snapshot of what’s live today**
The Auction Calendar already covers a wide range of recurring auctions across Europe.
This gives you a strong, structured overview of when vehicles are coming online in each market – perfect if you’re sourcing cross-border or focusing on local sales.


## Why the auction calendar matters for you


The goal is simple: help you **buy smarter, faster, and with more confidence** . Here’s how the Auction Calendar supports that.


1


##### Stay organized and informed


Instead of checking multiple sections or emails, you get **one central overview** of upcoming weekly auctions.


- Quickly see what’s happening today, tomorrow, and later in the week
- Focus on the auction formats and countries that fit your purchase needs
- Never miss a recurring auction that regularly delivers the stock you need


2


##### Plan your bidding strategy


If you participate in multiple auctions, timing is everything.
With the Auction Calendar, you can:


- **Mark your preferred auctions** in your own calendar
- Align your internal processes (e.g. budget approvals, sales planning, logistics)
- Decide in advance which auctions to prioritize, instead of reacting last-minute


This is especially valuable for professional buyers managing **high volumes or multi-country sourcing** .


3


##### More transparency, more trust


Knowing **exact dates and times** of recurring auctions removes guesswork.


- You see what’s coming well ahead of time
- You know when to log in, prepare bids, and organize your buying team
- The predictability of recurring auctions builds trust in the process and helps you make decisions with confidence


4


##### Better auction visibility


With everything in one place, it’s easier to **discover opportunities you might have missed** .


- Spot auctions in countries you don’t regularly check
- Notice patterns in the types of vehicles offered at specific times
- Expand your sourcing strategy by testing new recurring auctions


Over time, the auction calendar can become a key tool to **optimize your pipeline of stock** .


5


##### Simple and user-friendly


The Auction Calendar is designed to be **intuitive and easy to use** :


- Clean layout focused on times, countries, and auction types
- No clutter, no unnecessary complexity
- Ideal whether you’re quickly scanning for today’s auctions or planning the week ahead


Even if you’re new to OPENLANE, the calendar gives you a fast understanding of **how our weekly auction rhythm works** .


## How to use the auction calendar in your day-to-day job


Here are a few practical ways you can get the most value from the calendar:


- **Create a recurring routine**
Check the calendar at the start of the week and pick the auctions you don’t want to miss.
- **Sync with your personal or team calendar**
Add key auction times (e.g. every Monday 10:00 CET, every Wednesday 14:00 CET) to your Outlook, Google, or other calendar.
- **Match auctions to your purchase needs**
If you know certain auctions are strong in specific vehicle types, flag those as “must-attend”.
- **Coordinate with your teams**
Use the calendar as a shared reference point for volume planning and cashflow management.
