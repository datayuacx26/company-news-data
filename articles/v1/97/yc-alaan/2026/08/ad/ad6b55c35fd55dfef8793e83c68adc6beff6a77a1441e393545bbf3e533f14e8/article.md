The biggest hurdle to getting a business credit card isn't always your business. Sometimes it's the lack of a credit history.

## Business credit cards in the UAE: Credit checks and eligibility


When you're starting, it's tempting to use a personal credit card for business expenses, as it feels convenient.


However, hassle enters the scene when your company grows. Sooner or later, you, like most businesses, will have to switch to a business credit card or another business payment solution to gain better financial visibility and control.


The next question, of course, is whether your startup will qualify.


### Can you get a credit card in the UAE without a credit bureau check?


If you're specifically looking for a **business credit card without a credit check** , you must know that **most UAE banks carry out some form of credit assessment** before approving an application. For those who are unaware, the official credit bureau of the United Arab Emirates is the Al Etihad Credit Bureau (AECB), which provides credit information that banks use to evaluate lending decisions.


Getting a **credit card without an Etihad bureau check** is a bit of a challenge **.** However, a limited credit history doesn't automatically mean your application will be rejected.


When reviewing an application, lenders typically consider:


- **Credit history:** Information from the **Al Etihad Credit Bureau (AECB)** .
- **Business credentials:** Your trade license, company registration, and business activity.
- **Financial profile:** Revenue, bank statements, or expected business turnover, depending on the provider.
- **Banking relationship:** Existing accounts or previous banking history may also be taken into account.

## Individual corporate cards: A simpler alternative for startups


Not every business needs a traditional business credit card.


If you're a sole proprietor, freelancer, startup founder, or small business owner looking to separate business and personal spending, an individual corporate card can be a practical alternative.


Unlike traditional business credit cards, individual corporate cards are issued to employees or business owners, providing the company with complete visibility into spending. Depending on the provider, you can set spending limits, issue separate cards for team members, and track every transaction in real time.


They're a great fit for startups that haven't qualified for a business credit card yet, but want to move away from reimbursements, shared cards, and manual expense tracking.
