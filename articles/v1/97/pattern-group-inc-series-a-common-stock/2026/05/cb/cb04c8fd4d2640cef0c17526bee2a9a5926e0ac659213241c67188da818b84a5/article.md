# Pattern to Participate in Upcoming Investor Conferences

## Pattern to Participate in Upcoming Investor Conferences


May 4, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.pattern.com/node/7576/pdf)


LEHI, Utah--(BUSINESS WIRE)--May 4, 2026-- Pattern Group Inc. (Nasdaq: PTRN) (“Pattern”), a leader in accelerating brands on global ecommerce marketplaces leveraging proprietary technology and AI, announced today that senior management will host individual and small group meetings at the following investor conferences:


**Wolfe Research Badishkanian Small Group May Conference** Monday, May 11, 2026
Virtual Conference


**2026 J.P. Morgan Global Technology, Media and Communications Conference** Tuesday, May 19, 2026
Boston, MA


**2026 Jefferies Software, Internet, and AI Conference** Thursday, May 28, 2026
Newport, CA


**About Pattern**


Pattern accelerates brands on global ecommerce marketplaces leveraging proprietary technology and AI. Utilizing more than 66 trillion data points, sophisticated machine learning and AI models, Pattern optimizes and automates all levers of ecommerce growth for global brands, including advertising, content management, logistics and fulfillment, pricing, forecasting and customer service. Hundreds of global brands depend on Pattern’s ecommerce acceleration platform every day to drive profitable revenue growth across 70+ global marketplaces—including Amazon, Walmart.com,[Target.com](http://target.com/) , eBay, Tmall, TikTok Shop, JD, and Mercado Libre.
