# Pattern Recognized in U.S. News & World Report’s 2026-2027 Best Companies to Work For

*Pattern was named as a top company for fulfilling employee needs ‍*


**July 23, 2026** −[U.S. News & World Report](https://careers.usnews.com/companies/rankings) , the global authority in rankings and consumer advice, has named Pattern one of the 2026-2027[Best Companies to Work For.](https://careers.usnews.com/companies/pattern-148773624) Pattern was rated among the top companies for employees on factors most heavily considered by job seekers during their employment search.


“Being recognized by U.S. News & World Report reflects what we’ve believed from the beginning: extraordinary companies are built by extraordinary people,” said Pattern Chief People Officer Missy Johnson. “We work hard to hire exceptional talent, give them meaningful challenges to solve, and create an environment where they have the trust, ownership, and support to do the best work of their careers. This recognition is a reflection of the incredible team that chooses to build Pattern together every day.”


U.S. News’ Best Companies to Work For ratings examine employee sentiment using factors that include quality of pay and benefits, work-life balance, job and company stability, physical and psychological comfort, belongingness, career opportunities and professional development.


“The 2026-2027 job market is rapidly shifting the way employers, employees and job seekers think about work, and subsequently, workplace culture,” said U.S. News Vice President of Careers Carly Chase. “The list spotlights companies that scored high on multiple metrics that promote a positive work environment and everyday employee experience.”


For more information, view[Pattern’s profile on US News & World Report here.](https://careers.usnews.com/companies/pattern-148773624)
