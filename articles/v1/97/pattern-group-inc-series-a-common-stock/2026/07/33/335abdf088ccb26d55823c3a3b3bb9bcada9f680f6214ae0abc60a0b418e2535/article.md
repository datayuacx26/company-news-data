# Pattern Honored as an Inc. Power Partner for the Fourth Consecutive Year

**LEHI, UT — (November 18, 2025)** — Pattern Group Inc. (Nasdaq: PTRN) (“Pattern”), a leader in accelerating brands on global ecommerce marketplaces by leveraging proprietary technology and AI, today announced it has been named to the prestigious Inc. Power Partner list for the fourth consecutive year. The honor recognizes Pattern's unwavering commitment to empowering brands to achieve profitable growth and market leadership in an increasingly complex digital landscape.


The Inc. Power Partner Awards identifies top B2B companies worldwide. To compile the list, Inc. evaluates businesses on key attributes such as commitment, reliability, trust, and innovation, based on extensive client testimonials and data analysis. This year's recognition underscores Pattern's proven track record of helping its partners navigate challenges, accelerate growth, and achieve new levels of success.


This year, Pattern’s commitment to innovation has been demonstrated through the introduction of a powerful suite of AI-powered solutions. These include the GEO Scorecard, Chessboard, and TrendVision, which provide unprecedented visibility into how brands appear on AI assistants and why consumers convert. This is complemented by the opening of a state-of-the-art 115,000-square-foot warehouse in Las Vegas, strategically strengthening Pattern's technology and fulfillment backbone to ensure the reliability and speed that brands depend on to win in the global marketplace.


See the full list of honorees on[Inc.com.](https://www.inc.com/power-partner-awards)


## **About Pattern**


Pattern accelerates brands on global ecommerce marketplaces leveraging proprietary technology and AI. Utilizing more than 46 trillion data points, sophisticated machine learning and AI models, Pattern optimizes and automates all levers of ecommerce growth for global brands, including advertising, content management, logistics and fulfillment, pricing, forecasting and customer service. Hundreds of global brands depend on Pattern’s ecommerce acceleration platform every day to drive profitable revenue growth across 60+ global marketplaces—including Amazon, Walmart.com, Target.com, eBay, Tmall, TikTok Shop, JD, and Mercado Libre.
