# Pattern Recognized as One of America's 100 Most Loved Workplaces® of 2025

**LEHI, UT — (October 21, 2025)** — Pattern Group Inc. (Nasdaq: PTRN) (“Pattern”), a leader in accelerating brands on global ecommerce marketplaces by leveraging proprietary technology and AI, today announced it has been selected as one of America’s Top 100 Most Loved Workplaces® of 2025, a list presented by the Wall Street Journal in collaboration with the Best Practice Institute. The list honors companies where employees feel supported, inspired, and appreciated.


"We are incredibly proud to be recognized as a Top 100 Most Loved Workplace," said Missy Johnson, Chief People Officer at Pattern. "This award is a direct reflection of our team's passion and dedication. We believe that a great company culture is the foundation of our success, and we are committed to providing an environment where every employee can thrive, innovate, and contribute to our mission of accelerating growth for brands worldwide."


The award is a testament to Pattern's people-first culture and its commitment to fostering an environment of trust, collaboration, and respect. The designation is based on feedback from a survey of Pattern employees, which evaluated various elements that impact employee sentiment, including:


- How positive workers feel about their future at the company.
- The level of respect for employees at all levels.
- The alignment between employee values and company values.
- The degree of collaboration at the firm.

**About Pattern**


Pattern accelerates brands on global ecommerce marketplaces leveraging proprietary technology and AI. Utilizing more than 46 trillion data points, sophisticated machine learning and AI models, Pattern optimizes and automates all levers of ecommerce growth for global brands, including advertising, content management, logistics and fulfillment, pricing, forecasting and customer service. Hundreds of global brands depend on Pattern's ecommerce acceleration platform every day to drive profitable revenue growth across 60+ global marketplaces—including Amazon, Walmart.com, Target.com, eBay, Tmall, TikTok Shop, JD, and Mercado Libre. To learn more, visit pattern.com or email press@pattern.com.
