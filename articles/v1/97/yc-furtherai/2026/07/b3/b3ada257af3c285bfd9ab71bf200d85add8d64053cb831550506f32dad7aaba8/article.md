# What Is Agentic Insurance? The Third Era of Insurance

Insurance has moved through three distinct eras, and the difference comes down to who does the work.


In the first era, documentation was processed by hand. In the second, systems of record arrived: the data had somewhere to live, but people still entered it, moved it between systems, and made every decision on top of it.


We're now in the third era. Systems still store the data, but AI agents do the work, and humans apply judgment on top of that. That's what we mean by agentic insurance: workflows performed by AI and moderated by humans.


> *"We are in a 3.0 era where the system stores the data, the AI agents actually do the work, and then humans do the judgment on top of that. It's a lot about outsourcing work to agents, but the judgment remains with humans. That is what we are defining as agentic insurance, where workflows are performed by AI but moderated by humans." –* ***Aman Gour*** *, CEO and Co-founder of FurtherAI*


## What agentic insurance actually means


For decades, software in insurance has been a place to put information. An underwriter keyed in the details, clicked through the screens, and carried the decision, while the software waited.


Agentic workflows invert that. The agent reads the submission, pulls the data, runs the checks, and prepares the decision. The[underwriter](https://www.furtherai.com/persona/underwriting) or[claims handler](https://www.furtherai.com/persona/claims-professional) reviews the output, applies experience the model can't replicate, and signs off. People spend their time on judgment calls, not data entry.


This matters most where insurance work is repetitive but consequential. Submission intake, claims processing, and policy audits share one pattern: gather information, structure it, check it against rules, and route it to the right person. Agents handle that reliably, and they don't tire on the hundredth file of the day.


General-purpose tools like ChatGPT and Claude are good at ad hoc work, but agentic insurance means something more specific:


> *"When we think about agentic insurance, we think about end-to-end workflows which are purpose-built for a use case. These workflows involve processing documents. They involve reasoning on top of this data. It involves integration with internal systems to fetch additional context. And it involves the human in the loop at the right point in the process." –* ***Aman Gour*** *, CEO and Co-founder of FurtherAI*


That model is already running in production. FurtherAI works with three of the top 10 brokers, five of the top 20, along with carriers and other Fortune 500 insurers.
