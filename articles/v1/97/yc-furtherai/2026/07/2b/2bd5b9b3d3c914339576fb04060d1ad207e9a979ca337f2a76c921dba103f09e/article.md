# FurtherAI Launches Connectors for Insurance Systems

Every workflow in insurance starts with gathering data across a handful of systems: the CRM, policy admin, document repository, third-party enrichment sources. The actual work can begin only after all of that context is assembled, whether that's underwriting a risk, adjusting a claim, or standing up a new program.


That gathering work is where most AI deployments in insurance run out of room. An assistant that can only see what's directly inputted will always be a fraction as useful as one that can reach across the operational stack the way an insurance professional naturally does.


Today we're launching the FurtherAI Connectors: a library of native integrations to the CRMs, policy admin platforms, document repositories, agency management systems, and enrichment sources that already run modern insurance operations.


## **One connection replaces every custom integration project**


The Connectors library covers the categories that shape how a modern insurance team actually works.


**CRMs:** *Bring the customer relationship and account history into every workflow.*


- Salesforce
- Microsoft Dynamics


**Policy administration systems:** *Read and write policy records and exposure data directly.*


- Guidewire
- Duck Creek
- Majesco


**Agency management platforms:** *Connect the broker's book of business into the assistant.*


- Applied Epic
- AMS360
- Sagitta
- EZLynx


**Document systems:** *Pull loss runs and submission attachments into workflows automatically.*


- SharePoint
- ImageRight


**Enrichment sources:** *Layer third-party risk and compliance data on top of internal records.*


- OSHA
- OFAC
- HazardHub
- BuildZoom
- Trellis
- PitchBook
- Google


## **The base layer of the Agentic Workspace for insurance**


An Agentic Workspace only works when it can reach into the systems teams already use. The connectors are that layer, and every FurtherAI workflow reads from and writes to those systems directly.


Every Connector in the library goes through a security review before it ships. The scope of each integration (what data it reaches, what actions it can take, and how it authenticates with the underlying system) gets evaluated against the standards insurance teams need to trust the platform. Admins retain control over which connectors are active in their workspace and how each one is used.


More connectors are on the way. If you want to see FurtherAI across the systems your team already uses,[get in touch](https://www.furtherai.com/book-demo) .
