# FurtherAI Now Runs in Microsoft Outlook and SharePoint

Insurance work runs on documents, and most of those documents live in Microsoft 365. Submissions arrive in Outlook. Policy files, loss runs, and program documents live in SharePoint. Underwriters, adjusters, and operations teams spend a meaningful share of every workday reviewing and returning content across the Microsoft environment their organizations established.


FurtherAI extends its insurance-native AI workspace directly into Microsoft Outlook and SharePoint — expanding the power of FurtherAI to where insurance teams are already working, so intake, document workflows, and underwriting audits run in place. These Microsoft integrations reduce the switching cost and context loss between the assistant and the everyday tools where insurance work happens.


### **FurtherAI for Outlook: The Submission Starts Where It Lands**


The first touchpoint of nearly every underwriting workflow is Outlook. A broker submission arrives in a mailbox, and until now, someone had to move it out of the inbox before any automation could start. With FurtherAI's Outlook integration, that step goes away.


Insurance teams can use FurtherAI in Outlook across three core areas:


- **Submission triage.** FurtherAI reads incoming submissions the moment they land and routes them by line of business and complexity to the right underwriter or workflow.
- **Structured data extraction.** ACORD forms, SOVs, and loss runs get parsed and normalized inside the assistant, with the extracted data ready to hand off to the policy admin system or pricing engine.
- **Assistant access in the inbox.** Ask the FurtherAI assistant a question about a submission (coverage terms, prior loss history, comparable risks) and get a source-backed answer without opening a second tool.


### **FurtherAI for SharePoint: Read and Write to Your Existing Files**


For most insurance teams, SharePoint is the system of record for the documents that matter: policy files, loss runs, underwriting files, program materials, and audit trails. FurtherAI's SharePoint integration lets the assistant work against those files directly, without pulling them into a parallel document store.


- **In-place document workflows.** FurtherAI reads content from SharePoint, runs its analysis, and writes results back to the same locations the team is already using. No export and no re-import.
- **Folder-aware access.** The assistant respects the folder hierarchy your organization has built. Access controls stay exactly where they are, and audit trails stay intact.
- **Cross-document reasoning.** Ask FurtherAI to compare a submission against the last several renewals, and it can pull the source documents from the SharePoint locations they already live in.


### **Same Permissions, Same File Structures**


Everything FurtherAI does across Outlook and SharePoint runs under the permissions the user already has in Microsoft 365. An underwriter with access to specific mailboxes and folders sees exactly that scope inside the FurtherAI workspace, and no more. Admins retain the same control over access they've always had, and IT teams don't need to build new access models or migrate content anywhere.


The Microsoft environment stays the source of truth. FurtherAI adds an intelligence layer on top of it.


By extending FurtherAI's capabilities across Outlook and SharePoint, we're making it easier for insurance teams to use domain-specific AI directly inside the tools they spend their day in. FurtherAI runs as a connected partner alongside the Microsoft-native environments where underwriting, claims, and program management already live.
