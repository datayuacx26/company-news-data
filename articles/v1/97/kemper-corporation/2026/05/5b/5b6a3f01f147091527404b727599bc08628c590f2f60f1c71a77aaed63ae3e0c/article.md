### Kemper Announces Quarterly Dividend


May 6, 2026


CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Kemper Corporation](http://www.kemper.com/) (NYSE: KMPR) announced today that its Board of Directors has declared a quarterly dividend of $0.32 per share. The dividend is payable on June 2, 2026, to Kemper’s shareholders of record as of May 18, 2026.


**About Kemper**


The Kemper family of companies is one of the nation's leading specialized insurers. With approximately $12 billion in assets, Kemper is improving the world of insurance by providing affordable and easy-to-use personalized solutions to individuals, families and businesses through its Kemper Auto and Kemper Life brands. Kemper serves over 4.5 million policies, is represented by 24,100 agents and brokers, and has 7,400 associates dedicated to meeting the ever-changing needs of its customers.
