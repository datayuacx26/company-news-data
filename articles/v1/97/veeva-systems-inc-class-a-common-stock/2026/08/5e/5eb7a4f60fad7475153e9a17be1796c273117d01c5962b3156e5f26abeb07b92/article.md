### Veeva to Release Fiscal 2027 Second Quarter Results on August 26, 2026


August 5, 2026

/PRNewswire/ --[Veeva Systems](https://www.veeva.com/) (NYSE: VEEV) will announce financial results for its second quarter ending July 31, 2026 after market close on August 26, 2026.
