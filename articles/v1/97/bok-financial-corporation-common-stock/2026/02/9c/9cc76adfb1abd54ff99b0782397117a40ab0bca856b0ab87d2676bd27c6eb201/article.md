# Trlica named new head of BOK Financial energy services

Alex Trlica has been named the new executive director of Energy Financial Services at BOK Financial.


Since joining BOKF in 2017, he’s led key energy portfolios across upstream, midstream and syndicated markets, most recently serving as manager of Energy Financial Services for North Texas. His leadership has helped grow the business and build strong, effective teams across multiple regions.


“Alex brings deep credit expertise, market insight and a collaborative approach—making him well-prepared to succeed in this new role and continue driving results across BOKF’s energy business,” said Brad Vincent, EVP Specialized Industries Banking Executive. Trlica added, “The oil and gas business is inherently volatile and constantly evolving. What gives me confidence and excitement is knowing that one thing won’t change: this institution’s unwavering commitment to the industry. I get excited about the incredible team we have in place and their dedication to serving our customers as they navigate the path to success in a complex and ever-changing environment.”


Trlica earned his business degree from Texas Tech University. Active in his community, Alex is involved in Big Brothers Big Sisters, a committee member for the Dallas Petroleum Club and previously served as a board member for the Texas Energy Council.
