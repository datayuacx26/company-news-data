# Bank of Oklahoma gives $100,000 to Oklahoma City National Memorial & Museum’s Foreword campaign

OKLAHOMA CITY, OK – Bank of Oklahoma, through the BOKF Foundation, has made a $100,000 gift to support the Oklahoma City National Memorial & Museum’s Foreword campaign, a capital project to build new museum space for exhibits and education programs.


The campaign supports the Memorial’s ongoing work to share the story of the April 19, 1995, bombing and its lasting impact.


“Our partnership with the Oklahoma City National Memorial & Museum has spanned decades,” said Greg Wheeler, Bank of Oklahoma’s Oklahoma City market president. “Supporting the Foreword campaign is an opportunity to honor that history and help ensure these stories continue to educate and inspire future generations.”


BOK Financial and the BOKF Foundation contribute an average of $8 million to nearly 800 nonprofit organizations each year with the goal of enhancing the quality of life and economic wellbeing in the communities it serves.
