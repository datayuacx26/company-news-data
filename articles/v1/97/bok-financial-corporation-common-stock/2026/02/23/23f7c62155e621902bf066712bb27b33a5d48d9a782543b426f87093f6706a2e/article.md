# New exhibit chronicles 115 years of Tulsa’s story at 320 S. Boston

TULSA, OK - The history of the beloved Tulsa landmark, 320 S Boston, is being encapsulated in a new exhibit.


In the southeast corner of the lobby, bank clients and other building patrons can now find curated historical displays and installations detailing 115 years of history.


“This building is such an integral part of our history, and really Tulsa history,” said BOK Financial President and CEO Stacy Kymes. “This project showcases that intertwined history. From our founding through our move into the BOK/Williams tower it tells a story that is uniquely Tulsa.”


The exhibit highlights Tulsa’s oil boom, creation of the tunnels and vault, preservation of the iconic ceiling, the creation of motor banking and much more.


“We’re excited about our history, but even more excited about our future,” said Kymes.
