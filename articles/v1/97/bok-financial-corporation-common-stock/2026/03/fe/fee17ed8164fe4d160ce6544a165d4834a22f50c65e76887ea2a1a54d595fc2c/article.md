# BOKF Foundation surpasses $125 million in community giving

*TULSA, Okla.* — The BOKF Foundation has surpassed $125 million in charitable giving, marking a major milestone in its support of nonprofit organizations across the communities BOK Financial serves.


Through the foundation, BOK Financial supports initiatives focused on basic needs, education, and economic development, while also investing through United Way and encouraging employees to volunteer and give back in meaningful ways.


“At BOK Financial, giving back is more than a value. It’s a commitment that’s woven into the fabric of our culture,” said Leslie Paris, director of community and employee engagement and president of the BOKF Foundation.


In this video, employees across the company show how they bring that commitment to life through volunteerism and community partnerships.
