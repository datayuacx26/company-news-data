# Hey Cowboys: Bank of Oklahoma launches new OSU debit card

*Stillwater, OK, Aug. 17, 2026* —Bank of Oklahoma’s new Oklahoma State University Mastercard debit card launches today. Just in time for football season, the card, featuring Pistol Pete, also offers contactless technology, security features, and Mastercard’s Zero Liability protection and coincides with the opening of a new banking center in historic Campus Corner.


Our roots at Oklahoma State run deep, and we’re proud to continue investing in the university and Stillwater community,” said Tammy Alger, senior consumer market director for Bank of Oklahoma. “As the preferred banking partner of OSU Athletics, we’re excited to introduce this card as a fun way for fans, alumni and supporters to showcase their Cowboy spirit every day.


The cards are available at the new branch location at 229 S Knoblock St. Outside of Stillwater, clients can visit a banking center to request a card be ordered.
