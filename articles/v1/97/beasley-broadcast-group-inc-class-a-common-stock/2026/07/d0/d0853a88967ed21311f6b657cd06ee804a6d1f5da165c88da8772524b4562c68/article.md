# Beasley Media Group Promotes Jose Alberto Roldan to Head of Technology and Infrastructure

**NAPLES, Florida (July 15, 2026)** : Beasley Media Group is pleased to announce the promotion of Jose Alberto Roldan to Head of Technology and Infrastructure.


In this newly created position, Roldan will oversee the Company’s technology strategy, information technology operations, infrastructure, cybersecurity, and enterprise systems.


He will continue leading initiatives that strengthen Beasley Media Group’s technology foundation while supporting the Company’s ongoing business objectives and digital transformation efforts.


Roldan, who most recently served as Vice President of IT, joined Beasley Media Group in 2019 and has played an instrumental role in modernizing the Company’s technology environment, enhancing cybersecurity, improving operational efficiency, and advancing strategic technology initiatives across the organization.


“Jose has been an exceptional leader and trusted member of our technology team since joining Beasley Media Group,” said Caroline Beasley, Chief Executive Officer of Beasley Media Group. “His expertise, strategic vision, and unwavering commitment to innovation have helped strengthen our technology infrastructure and position the Company for continued growth. We are pleased to recognize his many contributions with this well-deserved promotion.”


With more than 20 years of experience in information technology and infrastructure management, Roldan is recognized for his collaborative leadership, technical expertise, and ability to deliver innovative, reliable technology solutions that support business growth and operational excellence.


“I am honored to take on this new role and grateful for the opportunity to continue serving Beasley Media Group,” said Roldan. “Technology plays a vital role in every aspect of our business, and I look forward to working alongside our talented teams to further strengthen our infrastructure, enhance cybersecurity, and help drive innovation across the Company.”


Roldan is based in Tampa, Florida.
