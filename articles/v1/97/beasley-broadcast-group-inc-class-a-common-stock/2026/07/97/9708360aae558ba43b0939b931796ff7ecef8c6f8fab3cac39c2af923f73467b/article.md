# Beasley Media Group Celebrates Two 2026 NAB Marconi Award Nominations for Outstanding Morning Shows

Beasley Media Group is proud to celebrate a pair of prestigious national honors as two of its flagship morning shows have been named finalists for the **2026 NAB Marconi Radio Awards** , recognizing excellence in radio broadcasting across the country.


The Bob & Sheri Show on WKQC-FM in Charlotte has earned a nomination for Network/Syndicated Personality of the Year, while The Andie Summers Show on WXTU-FM in Philadelphia has been recognized as a finalist for Major Market Personality of the Year. The nominations reflect the exceptional talent, creativity, and listener connection that define both programs and reinforce Beasley Media Group’s commitment to delivering compelling local and syndicated content.


Presented annually by the National Association of Broadcasters (NAB), the Marconi Radio Awards are among the broadcasting industry’s most respected honors. Established in 1989 and named after radio pioneer and Nobel Prize winner Guglielmo Marconi, the awards recognize outstanding radio stations and on-air personalities that demonstrate excellence in programming, innovation, and service to their audiences. Finalists are selected by an independent panel of broadcasters, making the recognition especially meaningful within the industry.


For decades, **The Bob & Sheri Show** has entertained audiences nationwide with its signature mix of humor, heartfelt storytelling, and relatable conversations. Its nomination for **Network/Syndicated Personality of the Year** underscores the show’s enduring appeal and its ability to connect with listeners across multiple markets.


In Philadelphia, **The Andie Summers Show** has become a beloved part of listeners’ morning routines. Known for its authentic personalities, engaging conversations, and deep ties to the local community, the show continues to deliver the energy and connection that make live radio a unique experience. Its **Major Market Personality of the Year** nomination recognizes the outstanding work of Andie Summers and her team in one of the nation’s most competitive radio markets.


The winners of the 2026 NAB Marconi Radio Awards will be announced during the annual Marconi Awards Dinner on Tuesday, October 20, 2026, at the iconic Edison Ballroom in New York City, held in conjunction with NAB Show New York.


These nominations are a tremendous achievement for **The Bob & Sheri Show** , **The Andie Summers Show** , and the entire Beasley Media Group family. They reflect the passion, creativity, and dedication of the on-air talent, producers, and behind-the-scenes teams who work every day to create memorable moments for listeners. Congratulations


to both shows on this well-deserved national recognition.
