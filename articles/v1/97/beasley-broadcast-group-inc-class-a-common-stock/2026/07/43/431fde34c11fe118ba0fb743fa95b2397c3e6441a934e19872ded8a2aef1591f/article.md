# Beasley Media Group’s 97.5 THE FANATIC Crowns a New Era & Becomes 97.5 LEBRON… HOME OF KING JAMES

***Philadelphia’s flagship radio home of the 76ers celebrates the arrival of LeBron James with a temporary station rebrand***


**Philadelphia, PA (July 24, 2026)** : Following today’s historic announcement that LeBron James is joining the Philadelphia 76ers, 97.5 The Fanatic is celebrating in true Philadelphia fashion. Effective immediately, the station will temporarily rebrand as “97.5 LeBron… Home of King James!” honoring one of the most significant moments in franchise history.


The temporary rebrand will be featured across the station’s on-air imaging, website, app, and social media platforms as Philadelphia welcomes one of the greatest players the game has ever seen. Throughout the day, listeners will hear new station IDs proclaiming “97.5 LeBron… Home of King James!” while The Fanatic’s hosts provide complete coverage of the blockbuster signing, including breaking news, fan reaction, exclusive interviews, press conference coverage, roster analysis, and what this landmark move means for the future of the 76ers.


“As the flagship station of the Philadelphia 76ers, we knew we had to celebrate this moment in a way that matched its magnitude,” said Paul Blake, Vice President and Market Manager of Beasley Media Group Philadelphia. “This is one of those rare days that changes the course of a franchise and energizes an entire city. Becoming ‘97.5 LeBron… Home of King James!’ is our way of celebrating alongside Sixers fans everywhere.”


“These are the moments sports radio is built for,” said Scott Masteller, Program Director of 97.5 The Fanatic. “Philadelphia has been waiting for this day, and every conversation is about LeBron and the Sixers. From the first show this morning through the final segment tonight, we’re bringing fans everything they need to know about one of the biggest stories in NBA history.”


As the official flagship station of the Philadelphia 76ers, 97.5 LeBron… Home of King James! will deliver comprehensive coverage throughout the day and beyond, featuring breaking news, exclusive interviews, fan interaction, expert analysis, and complete coverage of one of the most impactful acquisitions in NBA history.


As the official flagship station of the Philadelphia 76ers, 97.5 LeBron… Home of King James! will deliver comprehensive coverage of the teams and stories that matter most to Philadelphia sports fans.


For updates, visit 975TheFanatic.com and follow @975TheFanatic across all social media platforms.


***Note:** 97.5 LeBron… Home of King James is a temporary news promotion. No affiliation, sponsorship, or endorsement by LeBron James is implied.*
