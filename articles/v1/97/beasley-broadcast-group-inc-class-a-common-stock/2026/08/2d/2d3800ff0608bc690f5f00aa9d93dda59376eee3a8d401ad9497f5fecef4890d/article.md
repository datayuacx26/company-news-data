# Screamin’ Scott Scramble Smashes Records for The Rainbow Connection

**DETROIT, MI — August 6th, 2026** :


Golf, great music, and an even greater cause came together on **Sunday, August 2** , as the **9th Annual Screamin’ Scott Scramble** returned to **Fern Hill Golf Club** in Clinton Township for another unforgettable day of fun and fundraising.


Hosted by **94.7 WCSX’s Screamin’ Scott Randall** , the annual charity outing once again united listeners, local businesses, volunteers, and golfers of every skill level to support **The Rainbow Connection** , the Michigan nonprofit that grants wishes to children facing life-threatening medical conditions.


This year’s event proved to be the biggest yet, raising an incredible **$25,341** —a new fundraising record that will help make even more wishes come true for Michigan families. The outing also introduced attendees to this year’s **Wish Kid, Carson** , whose story served as a powerful reminder of why the event continues to grow year after year.


The fun started early with breakfast during check-in and a popular Bloody Mary bar before golfers hit the course. Along the way, players enjoyed lunch at the turn, dinner after 18 holes, exciting raffles, 50/50 drawings, and one of the outing’s signature attractions—the crowd-favorite **drone golf hole** . Every hole offered something memorable, creating an experience that was about much more than posting a great score.


Adding to the lively atmosphere, **Erik “E-Rock” Blundell** entertained golfers with live music right on the course, while local businesses, sponsors, and volunteers helped keep the energy high throughout the day. Their support transformed a summer golf outing into a true community celebration.


After nine successful years, the Screamin’ Scott Scramble continues to prove that when Metro Detroit comes together, amazing things happen. Between the record-breaking donation, the smiles shared with Carson, and another day packed with unforgettable moments, the 2026 outing was a resounding success—and another major win for **The Rainbow Connection** and the families it serves.
