# Nick Rivers Joins Beasley Media Group Charlotte as Assistant Program Director

**CHARLOTTE, N.C. — August 13** **th** **, 2026** — Beasley Media Group announced today that


**Nick Rivers** will join the company’s Charlotte radio cluster as Assistant Program Director for WSOC-FM/Country 103.7 and WKQC-FM/K104.7. In addition to his programming responsibilities, Rivers will host afternoon drive on Country 103.7, filling the opening created by Charlie & Debbie’s recent move to mornings.


Rivers brings nearly two decades of radio experience to the role, including the past 6 1/2 years with Hubbard Radio in West Palm Beach, Florida. Most recently, he served as assistant brand manager and music director for WIRK and WEAT while also hosting afternoon drive on WIRK


and


nights on WUBE in Cincinnati


.


Earlier in his career, he


held


programming


positions in Greensboro, North Carolina; Brunswick, Georgia; Savannah, Georgia; and Charlotte.


“We’re excited to have Nick Rivers coming on board,” said


**Mac Edwards, Vice President and Market Manager of Beasley Media Group’s Charlotte radio cluster** . “After an extensive search, Cameron, Jack and I agreed Nick’s programming expertise, on-air creativity, fresh ideas and energy made him the perfect fit for this key role on our Charlotte team.”


“We’re thrilled to welcome Nick to the Beasley Media Group team in Charlotte,” said


**Justin Chase, Chief Content Officer** . “His experience, creativity and passion for great radio will be tremendous assets to WSOC and WKQC. We look forward to his leadership as we continue creating meaningful connections with our listeners and serving the Charlotte community.”


The appointment marks a return to Charlotte for Rivers, whose radio career began in the market nearly 20 years ago. In his new role, he will work with the programming teams at Country 103.7 and K104.7 while taking on a prominent on-air role at Country 103.7.


“Nearly 20 years after my career began in Charlotte, I’m coming home,” Rivers said. “The opportunity to be closer to my daughter, Charleigh, while contributing to the legacy of WSOC and WKQC makes this homecoming all the more significant. My sincere gratitude to Mac Edwards, Cameron Moore, Jack Daniel and Justin Chase for entrusting me with this opportunity.”


Rivers also recognized the colleagues and leaders who supported his development during his tenure with Hubbard Radio.


“A heartfelt thank you to Grover Collins, Elizabeth Hamma, Greg Strassell and Bruce Logan for their confidence in me and investment in my growth at Hubbard,” Rivers said.


Rivers officially begins his new programming and on-air responsibilities with Beasley Media Group Charlotte on Aug. 24.
