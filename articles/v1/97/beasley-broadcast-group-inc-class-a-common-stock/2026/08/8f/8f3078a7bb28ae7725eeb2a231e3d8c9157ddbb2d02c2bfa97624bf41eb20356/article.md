# Tee Gentry Retires After 30-Year Career with Beasley Media Group

**AUGUSTA, Ga. – August 2nd, 2026** – Beasley Media Group announces the retirement of **Tee Gentry** , Operations Manager for the company’s Augusta radio cluster and Program Director and on-air personality for Kicks 99 (WKXC-FM). Gentry’s final day in his full-time role was July 31, concluding a distinguished 30-year career with Beasley Media Group that spanned multiple markets and leadership positions.


Throughout his three decades with the company, Gentry played an integral role in shaping programming, developing talent and serving listeners across the communities where he worked. In Augusta, he provided operational leadership for the market while guiding Kicks 99’s programming strategy and maintaining a strong on-air presence that connected with audiences throughout the region.


While retiring from his full-time position, Gentry will continue working with Beasley Media Group’s Augusta cluster in a consulting capacity. In this role, he will provide guidance on the market’s overall programming strategy, including Kicks Country, while continuing to support one of the region’s signature events, the annual Kicks 99 Guitar Pull. He also will remain involved in community relations initiatives to help ensure a smooth leadership transition and maintain the strong local connections he has cultivated throughout his career.


As part of the transition, **Chris O’Kelley** will serve as interim Program Director for Kicks 99 while continuing his role as the station’s midday host. In addition, **Cody** will expand her on-air responsibilities by taking over afternoon drive duties, strengthening the station’s weekday lineup while providing continuity for listeners.


“Tee Gentry’s commitment to Beasley Media Group, our stations and the communities he has served over the past 30 years has been nothing short of exceptional,” said **AJ Lurie, Vice President and Market Manager of Beasley Media Group Augusta** . “His leadership, passion for country radio and dedication to mentoring talent have left a lasting impact on our team and our listeners. We are incredibly grateful that, while Tee is retiring from his full-time role, he will continue to share his experience and insight as a consultant, helping guide our programming strategy, supporting Guitar Pull and strengthening our community relationships during this transition. We also look forward to Chris O’Kelley leading Kicks 99 on an interim basis while Cody steps into afternoons. Together, they bring tremendous experience, energy and familiarity that will ensure continued success for the station.”


Gentry’s retirement from full-time broadcasting marks the conclusion of an accomplished chapter in a career defined by local programming excellence, community engagement and strong leadership. His continued consulting role reflects Beasley Media Group’s commitment to preserving the station’s momentum while preparing for the future. The company will announce permanent programming leadership plans for Kicks 99 at a later date.
