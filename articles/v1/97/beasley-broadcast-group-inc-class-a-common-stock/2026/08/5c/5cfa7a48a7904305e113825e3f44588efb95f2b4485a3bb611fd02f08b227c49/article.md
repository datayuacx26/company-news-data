# Beasley Media Group Finalists Named for 2026 Radio Wayne Awards

**NAPLES, Florida, August 13, 2026** –


Beasley Media Group is proud to announce that four of its outstanding leaders have been named finalists for the[2026 Radio Wayne Awards](https://radioink.com/2026/08/13/radio-ink-names-2026-radio-wayne-award-finalists/) , presented by Radio Ink. These prestigious awards are the only honors in the industry dedicated exclusively to recognizing the streetfighters, digital sellers, managers, and support staff driving radio’s revenue forward.


This year’s finalists include:


**Digital Sales Representative**


- Jim McGowan


, Beasley Media Group, Boston, MA


- Ryan Dietrich, Beasley Media Group, Philadelphia, PA


**Digital Sales Manager**


- Daniel Mann, Beasley Media Group, New Jersey


**DOS/Sales Manager, Nielsen Market Rank 1-20**


- Brian Schneekloth, Beasley Media Group, Boston, MA


These team members now advance to the final round of competition, joining a distinguished group of professionals who have been honored with a Radio Wayne Award since the program’s inception. The winners will be revealed during at the[NAB Show New York](https://www.nabshow.com/new-york/) on October 21 at the Javits Center in Manhattan.
