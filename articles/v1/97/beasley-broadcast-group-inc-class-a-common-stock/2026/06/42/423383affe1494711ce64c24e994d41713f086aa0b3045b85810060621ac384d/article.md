# Beasley Media Group Featured at 2026 Florida Broadcasters Conference

Beasley Media Group was prominently featured at the 2026 Florida Association of Broadcasters Annual Conference, held June 17–18 at The Breakers Resort in Palm Beach, Florida.


On June 18, Beasley Media Group Chief Executive Officer Caroline Beasley participated in a one-on-one conversation with Congressman and Florida gubernatorial candidate Byron Donalds. Their discussion focused on Florida’s future, key community priorities, and the vital role broadcasters play in informing, connecting, and supporting local communities across the state.


Beasley Media Group’s Head of Digital Marketing, Dave Snyder, moderated a keynote panel on artificial intelligence in broadcast media, leading a discussion on newsroom workflows, audience growth, revenue opportunities, and maintaining audience trust as AI continues to reshape the industry.


The conference also marked a proud milestone for the company as Caroline Beasley was inducted into the Florida Broadcasters Hall of Fame in recognition of her leadership, innovation, and decades of contributions to Florida’s broadcasting industry. She was honored alongside fellow Hall of Fame inductee Jeannie Blaylock.


Congratulations


to Caroline on this well-deserved recognition and to the entire Beasley team for representing the company and the broadcasting industry with distinction!
