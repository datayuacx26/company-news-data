# 102.9 MGK Raises More Than $138,900 During 20th Annual Veterans Radiothon Benefiting Veterans Multi-Service Center in Philadelphia

**PHILADELPHIA, PA (June 24, 2026)** : Beasley Media Group’s Classic Rock 102.9 MGK proudly raised more than **$138,900** during its **20th Annual Veterans Radiothon** , held on Friday, June 12th, in support of the Veterans Multi-Service Center (VMC) in Philadelphia.


This year’s milestone event also marked the return of the Radiothon’s founder, recently retired WMGK-FM Morning personality and Radio Hall of Fame Inductee **John DeBella** , who launched the initiative in 2007. DeBella joined the station throughout the day to encourage listeners to support local veterans and their families through donations benefiting the VMC.


Broadcast live from Beasley Media Group Philadelphia’s Event Center, the day-long fundraiser featured MGK personalities **Paul Kelly, Tony Harris, and Kristen Herrmann** , along with volunteers staffing a dedicated phone bank to accept donations. Listeners also contributed through online and Venmo donation platforms.


The event generated more than $138,900, representing a significant increase over last year’s total. In addition to the overwhelming support from loyal MGK listeners, numerous Beasley Media Group clients and local business partners made generous contributions throughout the day.


“We are incredibly grateful to our listeners, clients, volunteers, and the entire MGK team for helping make this year’s Veterans Radiothon such a tremendous success,” said **Paul Blake, Vice President and Market Manager of Beasley Media Group Philadelphia** . “For 20 years, this event has demonstrated the extraordinary generosity of the Philadelphia community and its commitment to supporting the men and women who have served our country. We are especially honored to have welcomed John DeBella back for this milestone anniversary.”


Funds raised during the Radiothon will directly support the Veterans Multi-Service Center’s mission of providing housing, employment, wellness, and supportive services to veterans throughout the Greater Philadelphia region.


The Veterans Multi-Service Center has served veterans in the Philadelphia area for more than four decades, helping thousands of former service members and their families access critical resources and achieve greater stability and independence.

**About Veterans Multi-Service Center**


The Veterans Multi-Service Center (VMC) empowers veterans to navigate the transitions of life and supports them in achieving success. Through housing, employment, wellness, and supportive services, VMC assists veterans and their families throughout the Greater Philadelphia region.
