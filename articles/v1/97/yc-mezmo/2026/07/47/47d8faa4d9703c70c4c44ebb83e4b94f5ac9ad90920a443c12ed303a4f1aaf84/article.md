# NightVision Interview Series with Mezmo CEO, Tucker Callaway

Join Tucker Callaway, CEO of Mezmo, and Barbara Massa, Partner and COO of NightDragon, as they discuss the paradigm shift reshaping enterprise operations in the age of AI. The conversation will explore the challenge of making AI practical at scale, the rise of production AI as an emerging operating model, and why trust, context, and control are becoming foundational as AI moves into real-world systems. They’ll also touch on use cases like observability and agentic SRE, with perspective informed by Mezmo’s open-source work on AURA.
