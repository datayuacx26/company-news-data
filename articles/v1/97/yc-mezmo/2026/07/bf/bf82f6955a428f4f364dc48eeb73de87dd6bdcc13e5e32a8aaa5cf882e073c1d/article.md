# How AI-Driven Observability Is Transforming SRE: Insights from Mezmo CEO Tucker Callaway

Tucker Callaway, CEO of Mezmo, explores the evolution of observability and AI’s impact on Site Reliability Engineering (SRE). He highlights the significance of AI-driven observability, the necessity for human input in diagnosis, and the transformation in data analysis. The talk covers the evolving observability tools, AI integration, and the future of human-computer interaction, ending with an optimistic view on advancements in the field.
