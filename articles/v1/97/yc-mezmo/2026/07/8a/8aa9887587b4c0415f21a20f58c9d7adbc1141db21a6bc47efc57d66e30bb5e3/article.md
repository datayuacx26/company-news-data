# Top 4 Open Source Projects for AI-Driven Development

By[Carl Williams](https://www.techtimes.com/reporters/carl-williams)


Tech Times spotlights Mezmo's **AURA** , an open-source agent for production SRE that automates routine investigations and root-cause analysis, as one of four standout open-source projects reshaping AI-driven development. AURA appears alongside **SIA** (Hexo Labs' self-improving autonomous agent), **OpenHands** (a coding framework with 70,000+ GitHub stars), and **Hugging Face Transformers** (the widely used model-definition library). The piece frames Mezmo as making an ambitious bet on open-sourcing the AI SRE space.
