# Real-World Results: Atchley Steel Puts 3M™ Cubitron™ 3 Abrasives to the Test — MSC Industrial Supply Co.

What happens when experienced metalworkers put 3M's new abrasives to a real shop-floor test? At Atchley Steel in Alabama, grinding performance isn’t theory, it’s production, deadlines and pride in the craft. Hear the team share what changed when they switched to 3M™ Cubitron™ 3 grinding wheels, fiber discs and cloth belts.


From big welds and stair stringers to heavy cleaning and everyday steel fabrication, they talk about faster cut rates, longer life, fewer changeouts and noticeable gains in throughput. The feedback? Less downtime. Lower costs. Quality finishes. And a product the crew actually wants to use.


If you work in metal fabrication, welding or industrial grinding, this is a real-world look at what performance feels like on the floor — not just on paper.
