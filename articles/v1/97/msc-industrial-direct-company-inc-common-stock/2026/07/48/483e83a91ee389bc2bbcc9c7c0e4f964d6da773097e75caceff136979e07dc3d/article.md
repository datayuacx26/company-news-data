# OSG HY-PRO Sigma Next-Gen Taps — MSC Industrial Supply Co.

## Looking for full transcript of video?Read now


In this episode of Tooling Up, host Toni Neary sits down with Stephen Putek, Product Engineer at OSG USA, to explore how the new HY-PRO Sigma tap helps manufacturers improve thread quality, extend tool life, and simplify threading operations across a wide range of materials. As job shops and small-to-midsize manufacturers continue to juggle changing production demands, Stephen explains how HY-PRO Sigma was engineered to deliver consistent, reliable performance without requiring specialized tooling for every application.


The conversation explores the real-world challenges manufacturers face every day, including chip evacuation, broken taps, inconsistent thread quality, and frequent material changes. Stephen also shares how OSG combined decades of proven HY-PRO performance with new design enhancements to create a premium tap that offers greater versatility, durability, and value for today's manufacturing environment.


Watch the full episode to learn how OSG's HY-PRO Sigma tap is helping manufacturers reduce complexity, improve productivity, and machine confidently across carbon steels, stainless steels, cast irons, nonferrous materials, and more.
