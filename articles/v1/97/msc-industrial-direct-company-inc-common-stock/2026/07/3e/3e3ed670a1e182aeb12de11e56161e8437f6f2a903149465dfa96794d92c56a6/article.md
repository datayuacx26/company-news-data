# SHOWA ChemRest CC700: Chemical & Cut Resistant Safety Glove — MSC Industrial Supply Co.

## Looking for full transcript of video?Read now


CC700 is the ultimate industrial safety glove, combining high-level chemical protection with ANSI A5 cut resistance. Featuring a unique Orange Indicator Liner, this glove provides a "Visual Alert" to let you know exactly when it's time to change for maximum safety.


**Key Features:**


• **Visual Alert:** Orange liner reveals wear or damage instantly.


• **High Protection:** ANSI A5 / Level E Cut Resistance & EN ISO 374-1 Type A Chemical Protection.


• **Superior Grip:** Rough nitrile coating for excellent handling of liquids and powders.


• **Max. Dexterity:** Ultra-lightweight 18-gauge HPPE liner for precision work.
