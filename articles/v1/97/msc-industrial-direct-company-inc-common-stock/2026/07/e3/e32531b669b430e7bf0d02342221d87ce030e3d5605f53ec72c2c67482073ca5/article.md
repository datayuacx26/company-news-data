# Simplify Digital Data Collection — MSC Industrial Supply Co.

In this episode of Tooling Up, host Toni Neary sits down with Mitutoyo's Isaiah Babcock to explore how manufacturers can simplify measurement data collection and begin their digitization journey without overhauling their existing processes. From eliminating manual data entry and reducing transcription errors to connecting existing tools and building toward advanced quality systems, Isaiah shares practical strategies manufacturers can start using today.


Discover how digital measurement data can improve visibility, streamline quality processes, and help manufacturers make faster, more informed decisions. Whether you're just beginning to explore digitization or looking to expand your existing capabilities, this episode demonstrates how a step-by-step approach can deliver meaningful results.


Watch the full episode to learn how manufacturers can simplify data collection, reduce manual processes, and take the first step toward a more connected, data-driven quality program.
