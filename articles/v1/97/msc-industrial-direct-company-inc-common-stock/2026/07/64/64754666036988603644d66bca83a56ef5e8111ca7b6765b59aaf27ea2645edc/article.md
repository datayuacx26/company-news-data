# TOOLING UP: 3M Cubitron™ 3 Flap Disc 1169F | Grind Less, Weld More with Faster Cutting — MSC Industrial Supply Co.

## Looking for full transcript of video?Read now


In this episode of *Tooling Up* , host Toni Neary is joined by Mike Eggert, Senior Portfolio Manager at 3M Abrasives, to discuss how the new 3M Cubitron™ 3 Flap Disc 1169F is helping manufacturers reduce grinding time and improve productivity. As fabrication shops continue to navigate labor shortages and the growing skills gap, Mike explains how maximizing arc time can help shops get more from their skilled workforce.


The conversation explores how 3M's latest Precision-Shaped Grain technology delivers faster cutting, longer-lasting performance, and reduced operator fatigue while maintaining the versatility of a flap disc. Mike also shares how controlled disc wear, improved ergonomics, and integrated safety solutions help manufacturers improve efficiency from prep work through final weld cleanup.
