# Surprising Solutions: Norton Runs for the Roses — MSC Industrial Supply Co.

Often described as “The Most Exciting Two Minutes in Sports,” the Kentucky Derby is a feast for the senses. The colorful sight of elaborate fashions. The tingling taste of mint juleps. The hopeful feel of that betting marker carrying the wish of a win. The fragrant smell of the winner’s rose garland. The vibrating sound of thundering hooves. Every sense is stimulated. Let’s zero in on just one and consider those thundering hooves for a moment. Think about the critical role of the horses’ feet in racing to the finish line. And look, there’s Norton Abrasives back in the stable helping make success possible.


## A Shoe-in for Success


Horses don’t run on their natural hooves. Instead, a trained specialist known as a “farrier” combines blacksmith skills with equine lower-limb knowledge to support safe, healthy hoof function. A farrier will trim, balance, and shoe the horse on a regular basis (approximately every four to six weeks), often custom-shaping the horseshoes using traditional smithing techniques. Farriers require unique skills and specialized tools to perform their duties. That’s where[Farrier Products Distribution](https://www.farrierproducts.com/shop/horseshoes/c/1) (FPD) enters the picture. Established in 1990, FPD is now a full partner with the Holland-based Royal Kerckhaert Horseshoe Factory. FPD is a wholesale distributor that supplies a network of farrier supply shops across the US and Canada from their home base of operations in the heart of horse country: Shelbyville, Kentucky.


## Commitment to Quality


Even without selling direct to their target audience, FPD can still track the performance of the products they sell through individual farriers. Szymanski notes that for the last several years running, many of the horses in the Derby field have worn Kerckhaert race plates, even a few of the winners – including 2024 Derby winner Mystik Dan and 2025 Derby winner Sovereignty. With that track record and reputation at stake, they are very particular about the products that go into their inventory.[Norton RazorStar belts](https://www.mscdirect.com/browse/tn?navid=2100147&searchterm=razorstar) meet their tough criteria.


The primary use for the RazorStar belt is to do final modifications to the horseshoes before they're applied to the feet. Typically, the belts are used to bevel smoother edges on the shoes to protect the horse. Sometimes, the farrier will also use the belts to fit pads to shoes, trimming excess material off. The primary concern is the safety of the horse, taking away any material that doesn't need to be there to make sure the horse can't catch or cut itself or injure another horse because of excess steel sticking out where it shouldn't.
