# Find your Whistler feeling: The ultimate six-day itinerary

You know that feeling of pure joy and freedom? When the sun is shining, the atmosphere is electric and you’re fully absorbed in the present moment. That’s summer in Whistler.


Here, you can soar between mountaintops and paddle over serene water or savor a chef-prepared meal. Whistler delivers summer joy in all forms, whether you're craving an outdoor adventure, cocktails on a sunny patio or a cultural connection.

**Day 1 – Arrive & Settle into Mountain Life**


The journey begins as you spot the Coast Mountains and Pacific Ocean from the plane. Touch down at YVR International Airport in Vancouver, recognized as the Best Airport in North America by the Skytrax World Airport Awards. From here, hop on the Whistler Connection service to Whistler, winding north along the scenic Sea-to-Sky Highway.


Once you arrive in Whistler, check into the Adara Hotel, a stylish boutique hotel located in the heart of Whistler Village with a rooftop pool. Settle into the rhythms of mountain life with a stroll through the Village, then enjoy dinner at one of the European-inspired eateries owned by the Pare Restaurant Group. You'll be spoiled for choice with Mediterranean comfort food at Caramba!, authentic Italian at Quattro and contemporary French dining at Lorette Brasserie.


---


## **Day 2 – Adventures From Valley to Peak**


Spend your first full day in Whistler immersed in the mountains. The Whistler Blackcomb Summer Alpine Experience is a breathtaking gondola and chairlift journey that brings you from the Village to the alpine summit. Ride the record-breaking PEAK 2 PEAK Gondola between Whistler and Blackcomb Mountains, soaring 1,427 feet above Fitzsimmons Creek. Once in the alpine, you'll have access to 30+ miles of hiking trails that bring you to alpine lakes, jutting peaks, and panoramic viewpoints. Be sure to watch for the whistling marmots that gave Whistler Mountain its playful name.


After a day of hiking and sightseeing, download to the Village for a taste of local flavors at Alta Bistro. The intimate restaurant is built on farm-to-table dining and has received a host of accolades, including gold at the Vancouver Magazine Restaurant Awards. Indulge in a modern seasonal alpine menu with a curated wine pairing.


---


## **Day 3 – Lake Lounging & River Paddles**


In Whistler, the winding rivers and gemlike lakes add to the mystique of this idyllic setting. Cool off from the summer heat with a self-guided paddling tour along the famed River of Golden Dreams, with canoe rentals, life jackets and shuttles provided by family-run Backroads Whistler. The river snakes 3 miles, connecting Alta Lake to Green Lake and is a must-do summer activity for those looking to experience Whistler's natural beauty at a relaxing pace.


Wind down in Whistler Village or hang at a lakeside park with views of the mountains. As the sun sets, return to the Village for a one-of-a-kind meal at Teppan Village, where the chefs put on a whimsical show of culinary tricks before plating your Japanese-inspired meal.


---


## **Day 4 – Culture Meets Valley Bike Rides**


Spend an entire day taking in nature and culture in Whistler. Begin with a scenic ride on a guided E-Bike Valley Tour with Whistler Eco Tours. Hop in the saddle of a pedal-assisted townie electric bike to ride the Valley Trail with your guide, stopping to take in some of Whistler’s most beautiful lakes, parks, and scenic lookouts.


Whistler is pulsing with creativity and has a thriving arts and culture community. Visit the Audain Art Museum, for an exclusive private guided curatorial tour. The museum features a permanent collection that showcases the artistic heritage of British Columbia, bringing in a rotation of exhibits from Canada and around the world.


After a day of nature and artwork, spend the evening exploring the vibrant Village before unwinding in the peace of the mountains.


---


## **Day 5 – Explore the Rugged Backcountry**


Nestled in the vast Coast Mountain range, Whistler is at the edge of impressive backcountry terrain. Experience the towering peaks and forest valleys that have been carved by ancient volcanoes and glaciers by taking a Side-by-Side Tour with The Adventure Group. Also known as off-road buggies, these backcountry machines are built for rugged terrain, allowing you to splash through rivers and climb steep terrain.


After a thrilling tour, take a beat to explore more of Whistler, before ending your day in the renowned Rimrock Café. Unassuming from the outside, this cozy space in Creekside has a passionate following, serving up an upscale menu featuring seafood dishes, expertly prepared me meats and an award-winning wine list.


---


## **Day 6 – Whistler Village Strolls & Patio Hangs**


On your final full day in Whistler take time to soak in the Village vibes and celebrate your trip in style. Wander through independent shops, take in local attractions and street art, and explore more lakes and trails.


Be sure to stop in for a summer sipper at one of Whistler's many scenic patios, perfect for people watching and taking in the lively energy of Whistler Village. Visit restaurants, bars, and clubs owned by the Gibbons Hospitality Group, where you can use the Gibbons Après Pass for exclusive perks.


---


## **Day 7 – Departure Day**


After six days of soaking in the summer feeling of a Whistler stay, it’s time to head home. Take a shared Whistler Connection shuttle transfer to YVR for your return flight home.
