**What is a group purchasing organization (GPO)?** A GPO aggregates the purchasing volume of its members to negotiate contracts with suppliers. Members buy products and services at discounted group pricing with favorable terms. GPOs are used by over 90% of hospitals to improve operating margins — this partnership extends that model to independent psychiatry practices.
