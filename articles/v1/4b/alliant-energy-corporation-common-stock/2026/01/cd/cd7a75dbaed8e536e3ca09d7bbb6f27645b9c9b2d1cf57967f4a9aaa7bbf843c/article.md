## Alliant Energy Corporation Announces Fourth Quarter and Year-End 2025 Earnings Release and Conference Call


01/20/2026


Download (opens in new window)


MADISON, Wis.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Alliant Energy Corporation (NASDAQ: LNT) has scheduled its fourth quarter and year-end 2025 earnings release for Thursday, February 19th, after market close. A conference call to review the fourth quarter and year-end 2025 results is scheduled for Friday, February 20th at 9 a.m. CT.


Alliant Energy will webcast the event live at[www.alliantenergy.com/investors](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.alliantenergy.com%2Finvestors&esheet=54400118&newsitemid=20260120506633&lan=en-US&anchor=www.alliantenergy.com%2Finvestors&index=1&md5=d7decbc93bac26d73f6ac79e12ab4ec4) . The call is open to the public and will be hosted by Lisa Barton, President and CEO; and Robert Durian, Executive Vice President and CFO. Individuals who would like to participate in the conference call can do so by dialing (800) 549-8228 (Toll Free – North America) or (646) 564-2877 (International). The conference ID is 89157.


An archive of the webcast will be available on the company’s website at[www.alliantenergy.com/investors](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.alliantenergy.com%2Finvestors&esheet=54400118&newsitemid=20260120506633&lan=en-US&anchor=www.alliantenergy.com%2Finvestors&index=2&md5=21df3f47165a6a6fd7e3cac0eacb1df4) .


Alliant Energy Corporation (NASDAQ: LNT) provides regulated energy service to approximately 1 million electric and 430,000 natural gas customers across Iowa and Wisconsin. Alliant Energy's mission is to deliver energy solutions and exceptional service customers and communities count on – safely, efficiently and responsibly. Interstate Power and Light Company (IPL) and Wisconsin Power and Light Company (WPL) are Alliant Energy's two public energy companies. Alliant Energy is a component of Bloomberg’s Gender-Equality Index and the S&P 500.
