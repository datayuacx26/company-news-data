# Helping neighbors with Hometown Care


Our Hometown Care Energy Fund allocates financial support to our customers in need. Alliant Energy customers, employees, retirees and shareholders contribute money, and donations stay local. We partner with community organizations to distribute the funds in Iowa and Wisconsin. Last year, we provided more than $2 million to help our neighbors in need.


Please consider donating to this important cause. You can mail a check to:


Hometown Care Energy Fund
Alliant Energy ATTN Cash Processing PO Box 351 Cedar Rapids, IA 52406


Additionally, you can call us at 1-800-ALLIANT to


make a one-time contribution or add an amount to your regular bills


to devote to the fund.


[Learn more about Hometown Care](https://www.alliantenergy.com/account-and-billing/payment-assistance/hometown-care)
