# Gas generation upgrades in Sheboygan Falls and Neenah, Wisconsin


Energy needs are changing. Economic development and energy-intensive customers have growing energy needs in our service areas. One of the ways we’re adjusting to the growing need is with natural gas. It can help us respond quickly to fluctuations in energy demand and compliments renewable sources and battery storage.


To ensure the delivery of safe, reliable, cost-effective power, we’re upgrading and expanding two Wisconsin facilities. These upgrades to our Sheboygan Falls and Neenah facilities will further position our energy system for the future.
