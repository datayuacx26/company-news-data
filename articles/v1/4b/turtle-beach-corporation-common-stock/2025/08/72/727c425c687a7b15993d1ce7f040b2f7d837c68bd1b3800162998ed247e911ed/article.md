# Introducing The New Turtle Beach Atlas 200 Wired Gaming Headset

Leading gaming accessories maker


[Turtle Beach](https://corp.turtlebeach.com/) has announced the Atlas™ 200 Wired Gaming Headset, an officially licensed PlayStation® product that will offer users immersive gaming audio thanks to our 50mm Nanoclear™ Drivers.


The Turtle Beach® Atlas™ 200 Wired Gaming headset joins our pre-existing line of incredible gaming headsets, such as the


[Stealth™ 600](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/stealth-600-headset?utm_medium=pr&utm_campaign=TB_Stealth500AC_600_Purple_2025_ECM_NPIPO) and


[Stealth™ 700](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/stealth-700-gen-3-headset) .


## Turtle Beach® Atlas™ 200 Product Overview


The Turtle Beach® Atlas™ 200 is not only a great-looking headset, but it also provides an incredibly lightweight alternative to traditional headsets, as it weighs a mere 280g. The Turtle Beach® Atlas™ 200 also provides advantages thanks to our dedication to provide our consumers with the best experience possible, which coincides with some of the stand-out features of the Atlas™ 200.


- **50mm Nanoclear™ Drivers**


-


Unlock gaming audio detail like you’ve never heard before with precision-tuned 50mm Nanoclear™ drivers.


- **3D Audio Support**


-


Optimized for Tempest 3D AudioTech on PlayStation® 5 consoles, you’ll hear vibrant audio quality, as the game was intended to sound.


- **Lightweight Design**


-


Weighing in at approximately 280g, Atlas 200 is less fatiguing for long gaming sessions and competitive play.


- **Glasses-Friendly Comfort**


-


Patented ProSpecs™ dual-foam ear cushions alleviate pressure so gamers with glasses can play in comfort.


## Turtle Beach® Atlas™ 200 Other Key Features


- **Ultra-Clear, Flip-to-Mute Mic**


- Chat with precise voice accuracy thanks to the uni-directional flip-to-mute mic that conveniently flips to mute when you're not chatting.


- **Floating Fit**


- The Atlas™ 200 instantly conforms to the contours of your head, making it so comfortable that you'll forget it's even there with a floating headband and memory foam ear cushions.


- **Officially Licensed for PlayStation**


- Turtle Beach® Atlas™ 200 Wired Gaming Headset is officially licensed for PlayStation, PS5 and PS4 consoles.


## Turtle Beach® Atlas™ 200 Technical Specifications


- Connectivity


- 3.5mm Wired


- Driver Size


- 50mm Nanoclear™ Drivers


- Drive Frequency Response


- 20Hz-20kHz


- Microphone Type


- Unidirectional, Flip-to-Mute


- Ear Cushions


- Athletic Weave Fabric + Memory Foam


- Audio Cable


- 3.5mm 4-pole (Length: 12mm)

Eager customers will be able to begin pre-ordering the Turtle Beach® Atlas™ 200 today, with an anticipated shipping date of September 12th, 2025.


*If you pre-order, you'll be charged today and will receive an email once your order has shipped.*
