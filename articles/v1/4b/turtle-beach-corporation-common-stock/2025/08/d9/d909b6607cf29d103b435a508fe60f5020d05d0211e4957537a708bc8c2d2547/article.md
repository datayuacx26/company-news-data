# Battlefield 6 Twitch Drops: Cosmetics, Skins, Rewards and More

The Fall of 2025 is going to be marked with what fans are hoping is the triumphant return of Battlefield as the pinnacle of multiplayer FPS franchises.


With the beta in full swing, players are checking out the gameplay either by playing themselves, or watching streamers on Twitch.


Speaking of Twitch, we're going to run over all of the cosmetic Twitch drops for players to earn.


## Battlefield 6 Twitch Drops: Cosmetics, Skins, Rewards and More


Battlefield 6 beta has been soaring since it released, and it's easy to see why the game has gathered such incredible anticipation.


If you're simply watching on Twitch, you'll be able to earn some cosmetic items for when the game launches on October 10th.


These rewards will be:


- **Mimic Weapons Package**


– Watch for 1 Hour
- **Landslide Soldier Skin**


– Watch for 2 Hours
- **Shattered Vehicle Skin**


– Watch for 3 Hours
- **Imperial Soldier Skin**


– Watch for 4 Hour


In order to earn these rewards, you'll need to ensure your Twitch and EA are linked, which can be done[here](https://www.ea.com/twitchlinking) .
