## The Legacy Behind the Value: Turtle Beach's Rich Gaming Heritage


Turtle Beach's journey began in 1975. We're passionate about enhancing your gaming experience in 2005 with the first console gaming headset, the X51. This deep-rooted expertise in audio engineering forms the foundation of every product they create today.


The past decade has seen Turtle Beach become the world's best-selling gaming headset brand, having sold over 70 million headsets. In 2019, Turtle Beach expanded beyond gaming headsets and into the PC gaming peripherals space, and now has a full lineup of top-rated gaming keyboards and mice. In 2021, Turtle Beach further expanded its catalog to include game controllers for Xbox and PC, as well as into the flight simulation gaming accessories space.


In 2024, Turtle Beach added racing simulation gaming accessories to its catalog and acquired Performance Designed Products (PDP) - another leader in gaming accessories - along with its Victrix™ brand, which vastly expanded Turtle Beach's lineup of game controllers with more options for Xbox and PC, and added controllers for PlayStation, Nintendo Switch™, and other game systems.

## Innovation Meets Affordability: The Turtle Beach Advantage


### Cutting-Edge Audio Technology


Turtle Beach holds technology patents, ensuring that even their most affordable headsets benefit from innovations typically reserved for premium brands. Features like Superhuman Hearing and advanced spatial audio processing are available across multiple price tiers.


### Wireless Connectivity Leadership


Turtle Beach released the first headsets (Stealth 600 and 700) that could connect directly to Xbox One wirelessly, and the Stealth 600 became the best Xbox One gaming headset. This pioneering approach continues today with low-latency 2.4GHz connections and Bluetooth integration.


### Cross-Platform Compatibility


Unlike many gamers who require separate purchases for different consoles, Turtle Beach designs products with universal compatibility in mind, featuring switchable USB dongles that work seamlessly across Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch, and PC.
