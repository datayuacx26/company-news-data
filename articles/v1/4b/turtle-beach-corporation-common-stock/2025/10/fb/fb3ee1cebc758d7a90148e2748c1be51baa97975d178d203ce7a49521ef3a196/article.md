## **Innovative Leverless Design, The Future of Fighting Game Control**


The


leverless fighting game controller


revolution started with a simple question:


Why limit yourself to the travel time and potential inconsistency of traditional joysticks?


The Victrix Pro KO eliminates the traditional arcade stick in favor of four directional buttons, providing zero travel time between inputs and enabling faster, more precise execution with a lower degree of human error.


Traditional fight sticks require physical stick movement across multiple positions, introducing variables like wear patterns, dead zones, and inconsistent gate hitting.


Leverless controllers eliminate these issues


by using button presses for all directional inputs, allowing players to execute directional commands faster than rotating a joystick and providing greater precision for complex motions like 360-degree grapple moves and precise electric inputs.
