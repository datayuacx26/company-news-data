# Battlefield 6: All Achievements and Trophies

[Battlefield 6](https://www.turtlebeach.com/blog/category/guides?tag=Battlefield%206) is one of the most anticipated FPSs we've had in quite some time, and EA has revived the franchise back to the good old days with the latest iteration.


With players blasting through the game, it's a good time to run over all of the achievements and trophies that you'll be able to earn.


## Battlefield 6: All Achievements and Trophies


Battlefield 6 aims to reinvent the series, following a somewhat disappointing release for 2042, and so far, so good for EA and co.


While players have responded nicely to BF6, those looking to 100% the game, or are just curious into all of the achivements/trophies, then we've noted them all below.
