## **How to Maximize Wireless Controller Performance**


### **Placement and Receiver Tips**


Optimal receiver placement dramatically affects wireless performance. Position USB dongles away from interference sources and consider using USB extension cables for better positioning. USB 3.0 devices can interfere with 2.4GHz wireless signals, so separating these devices improves controller performance.


Xbox One controllers work within 19.2 feet of the console


, but objects between the controller and console can reduce this range significantly. Remove or relocate wireless devices, metal dividers, and dense furniture that might interfere with signal transmission.


### **Interference Avoidance**

- Use 5GHz WiFi networks when available

### **Controller Maintenance**


Regular maintenance ensures consistent wireless performance:


- Keep firmware updated through manufacturer apps


- Clean battery contacts to prevent power issues


- Calibrate analog sticks using built-in tools


- Store controllers in protective cases when not in use
