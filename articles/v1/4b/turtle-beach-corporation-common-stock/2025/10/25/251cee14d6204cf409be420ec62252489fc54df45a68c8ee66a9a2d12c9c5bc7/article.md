## **What Are 5-Stop Hall-Effect Clutch Triggers?**


### **The Science Behind Hall-Effect Sensor Technology**


Hall-Effect sensors detect magnetic field changes without physical contact, using electromagnetic induction discovered by physicist Edwin Hall in 1879. Unlike traditional analog triggers that rely on resistive contacts that wear down over time, Hall-Effect triggers have advantages that center on magnetic sensing that never degrades.


When you press a Hall-Effect trigger, a magnet attached to the trigger moves closer to the sensor, changing the magnetic field strength. This creates a voltage signal proportional to trigger position, providing an incredibly precise, linear response without any physical contact between moving parts. According to the Melexis Hall-effect principle documentation, this contactless sensing eliminates wear-related degradation.

## **Understanding Hair-Trigger Mode**


### **Defining Hair-Trigger Functionality**


Hair-trigger mode reduces trigger travel distance to the minimum actuation point, typically around half the total travel distance. This allows triggers to register input and reset faster, creating significant advantages in rapid-fire scenarios.


Hair-trigger mode benefits become immediately apparent in competitive scenarios. According to third-party modification data, professional gaming modifications can reduce trigger travel to as little as 1.5mm compared to standard 6-7mm travel distances, saving 4.5 times the distance and dramatically improving response times.
