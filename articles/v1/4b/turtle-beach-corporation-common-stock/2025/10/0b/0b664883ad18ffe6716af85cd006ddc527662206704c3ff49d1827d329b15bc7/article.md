## **FAQ**


#### What does flip-to-mute mean on a gaming headset?


Flip-to-mute means moving the microphone boom upward automatically silences the microphone through a built-in mechanical switch. When flipped up, the mic stops transmitting audio, and when lowered back to speaking position, it reactivates automatically.


#### Is flip-to-mute better than a mute button?


Flip-to-mute offers several advantages over buttons: it's generally faster to activate, provides intuitive tactile feedback, works without software dependencies, and eliminates the need to locate small buttons during intense gameplay. However, some users prefer buttons for stationary setups, and flip-to-mute mechanisms can occasionally experience reliability issues over time.


#### Do flip-to-mute microphones work on all consoles and PCs?


Yes, flip-to-mute functionality works universally across all gaming platforms because it uses mechanical switching rather than software controls. Whether connected to PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch, PC, or mobile devices, the physical flip action provides consistent muting.
