## How to Customize Your Victrix Controller for Comfort and Performance


### Trigger Customization


The Clutch Trigger system represents one of the most significant advantages of the Victrix Pro BFG. To adjust trigger stops:


1. Press and hold the Clutch Sliders on the controller's back


2. Depress the trigger to your desired stopping position


3. Release the Clutch Slider to set the stop


For maximum responsiveness, activate Hair Trigger mode by pressing and releasing the Clutch Sliders without touching the triggers, converting them to digital buttons.


### Module Swapping and Recalibration


Changing modules requires the included purple Module Swap Tool. After swapping the left module, recalibration is essential:


1.


Power off the controller by holding the PS button for 5 seconds


2.


Switch to wired mode using the purple mode switch


3.


Hold D-PAD DOWN while plugging in the USB-C cable


4.


Follow the LED indicators through the calibration process


### Controller Button Mapping


The Pro BFG features four programmable back buttons that can be mapped to any controller function, with three custom profiles for storing different configurations.
