## **What is Keystroke Life Cycle?**


Keystroke life cycle


refers to the total number of key presses a switch can handle before mechanical failure occurs. Most mechanical keyboard switches are rated between 50-100 million keystrokes, with manufacturers conducting exhaustive testing where each switch undergoes millions of simulated keystrokes to meet stringent durability standards.


How key switches are rated for durability


involves specialized testing machines that repeatedly press switches under controlled conditions. Premium mechanical switches can withstand extreme temperatures from -40°C to 180°C, as switches must endure various environmental conditions during shipping and storage.


The rating process isn't arbitrary; it's based on real-world usage patterns projected over years of intensive use. However, it's important to understand that these ratings represent laboratory conditions without the dust, moisture, and environmental factors present in real-world usage.
