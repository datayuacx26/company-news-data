## **PBT vs ABS Keycap Materials: Performance and Durability**


****


### **ABS Keycaps: Smooth Feel and RGB Optimization**


ABS (Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene) keycaps offer a smooth typing experience and superior light transmission for RGB lighting effects. For[the Vulcan II Max's stunning AIMO RGB lighting system](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/vulcan-ii-max-keyboard) , ABS keycaps provide enhanced illumination effects through better light transmission properties.


Best keycaps for RGB keyboards


often utilize ABS material because ABS is more receptive to light transmission and easier to manufacture with vibrant colors. ABS keycaps can be manufactured with double-shot injection molding, allowing multiple colors per cap and better legend illumination.


However, ABS keycaps can develop a shiny appearance over time due to finger oils and regular use, and they're generally less durable than PBT alternatives in long-term use scenarios.


### **PBT Keycaps: Superior Durability and Premium Feel**


PBT vs ABS keycaps for Vulcan II Max


comparison clearly favors PBT for long-term durability. PBT (Polybutylene Terephthalate) keycaps resist shining, offer superior wear resistance, and maintain their texture significantly longer than ABS keycaps.


PBT keycaps can last for several years to even a decade with heavy use, while ABS keycaps may show wear after several months to a few years of regular use. PBT provides a slightly textured, matte finish that many gamers prefer for improved grip during intense gaming sessions.


It's important to note that many keycap sets marketed as "PBT" are actually PBT/ABS blends, with pure PBT concentrations above 95% being relatively rare in the market. Despite this, PBT-blend keycaps still offer superior durability compared to standard ABS options.

## **DIY Customization Techniques and Maintenance**


### **DIY Keycap Replacement Tips and Installation**


Installing new keycaps on your Vulcan II Max requires careful technique to avoid damaging the TITAN II switches. Use a proper keycap puller to remove existing caps - never pull keycaps by hand, as this can damage the switch stems.


When installing new keycaps, align the cross-shaped mount carefully and press down until you hear a satisfying click. For larger keys like the spacebar, ensure the stabilizer wires are properly seated before pressing the keycap into place.
