# How To Turn Off The Narrator In Forza Horizon 5

Forza Horizon, has finally hit the streets and it's been soaring since it was released, as it's quickly gathering Game of The Year attraction.
&nbsp;


With over 500 cars to test drive, there's one feature within Horizon that most players opt to turn off, and this of course, is the narrators.


Here's how to turn them off within Forza Horizon 5


## How To Turn Off The Narrators In Horizon 5


For those who don't know, the narrators are basically the in-game GPS while playing Horizon.


They'll narrate most things you do within the game, including setting destinations or the objective you're approaching shortly.


So, if you just want to enjoy racing around Mexico, then it's best that you turn it off within the game.


In order to do this, you're going to want to head over to the in-game settings screen.


Then, head over to Accessibility, and scroll down until you see an option that says "Screen Reader." This is the one you're going to want to turn off, and once you save this change you should be good to go!
