# How To Get The DeLorean In Forza Horizon 5

#####


## How To Unlock The DeLorean In Horizon 5


With a record amount of cars within the game this time around, it can be tricky to get to grips with all the cars in the game.


This is why many of you aren't aware unlocking the DeLorean is rather easy compared to other rare vehicles.&nbsp;


Unlocking the DeLorean isn't the easiest, nor the hardest process, as it all revolves around one of the in-game festivals, and the DeLorean is based within the Summer one.


Over the course of the festival, you'll obtain points through winning races and completing other challenges.


These points can be used to unlock vehicles, including the DeLorean, which costs 26 of them. It really isn't a lot and shouldn't take you that long, but be sure to get this done before the seasons change.


As if you don't, you'll have to wait until the Summer festival rolls around yet again.
