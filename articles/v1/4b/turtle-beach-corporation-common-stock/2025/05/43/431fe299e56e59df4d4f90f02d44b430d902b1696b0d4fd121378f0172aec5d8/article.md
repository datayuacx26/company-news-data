# How To Unlock The Goliath Race in Forza Horizon 5

Forza Horizon, has typically been touted for having some of the best races within gaming, and Horizon 5 is living up to this claim.


With races centered around exploring active volcanoes, to other ones that are just long stretches' of drifting, there's always something for everyone.


With this said, if you've played Forza in the past, then you're well aware of the Goliath Race.


It's returned yet again in Horizon 5 and here's how to unlock it!


****


## How To Unlock The Goliath Race In Horizon 5


Basically, the Goliath Race is the longest track within any Horizon title each year, and it typically spans the entire length of the map.


Within Horizon 5, it'll take players roughly 10 minutes to complete, and it'll reward players with 40,000 XP, so well worth your time.


In order to unlock the Goliath Race, you're going to need to obtain enough accolades to proceed to the next story within Horizon.


Then, you can simply unlock the race via the main menu and you should be good to go!
