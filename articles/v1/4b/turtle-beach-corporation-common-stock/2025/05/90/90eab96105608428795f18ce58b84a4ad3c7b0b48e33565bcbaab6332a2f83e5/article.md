# How To Sell Cars In Forza Horizon 5

#####


## How To Sell Cars In Forza Horizon 5


Horizon 5 takes a lot of the same features that made Horizon 4 so popular and carries them over to their latest iteration, and the ability to sell cars is one of them.This feature is going to come in handy for most players, as more often than not, you're going to obtain car you don't particular want, or one that is surprisingly expensive.


So, it may be in your best interest to sell this car, as you can make an insane amount of money!&nbsp;


If you're looking to sell cars within Horizon 5, follow the below steps in order to achieve this.


- Head over to the Festival, which is marked on your map
- Load into the Auction House
- Pick the car you want to sell
- Set the price based on the currently available cars for sale
- Wait until the car is sold and the funds are in your account


A rather simple process, and one that's remained unchanged compared to previous titles!&nbsp;
