# Forza Horizon 5: Barn Finds Guide

In Forza Horizon 5, some of the game’s best rides are sitting around in barns just waiting to be discovered. Alas, it’s not as simple as providing you with a map, because Forza Horizon 5’s Barn Finds — as they’re known in Forza parlance — aren’t unlocked until you play through portions of the game. Another important thing to remember is that these vehicles take time to restore once you’ve claimed them — they’ve been sitting around in barns for a while, after all!


Want to know more about Forza Horizon 5 Barn Finds and the cars that await within? Our guide will spell out everything you need to complete a car enthusiast’s dream treasure hunt.


## How To Unlock Barn Finds in Forza Horizon 5


Before you can collect your classic cards, you’ll first need to unlock the Barn Finds.


Taking the in-game narrative around Barn Finds to its logical conclusion, Forza Horizon 5 presents barns and the vehicles waiting in them as the stuff of rumor. You have to hear the rumor of a specific barn before you can find and unlock it. Even if you know the location of each barn on the world map — which we do, as you can see below! — you aren’t able to just drive up to them and take the car inside.


A majority of the Barn Finds can be unlocked simply by continuing to play through the game, usually by driving nearby the location where the barn will eventually be revealed, while others appear by performing specific actions connected to Expeditions. We’ll lay out all of them for you below.


## Forza Horizon 5 Barn Finds Guide


Barn Finds are spread across Forza Horizon’s map, but unlocking them may take some time.


The numbers on the map above correspond to the following cars:


**1. 1968 Renault 4L Export Barn Find**


To reveal this barn, you’ll need to finish the Baja Expedition. Then you’ll want to buy the La Cabaña house, which will activate the rumor to unveil the Renault’s barn.


**2. 1973 BMW 2002 Turbo Barn Find**


This barn’s rumor should unlock at random during normal gameplay.


Several Barn Finds will be unlocked as you overhear rumors while driving around.


**3. 1968 Ford Mustang GT 2+2 Fastback Barn Find**


This barn’s rumor should unlock at random during normal gameplay, but note that some reviewers have reported that it unlocked late in their playthroughs.


**4. 1962 Ferrari 250 GTO Barn Find**


This barn’s rumor should unlock at random during normal gameplay, but note that this car takes an especially long time to restore compared to other Barn Finds.


**5. 1967 Ford Racing Escort MK1 Barn Find**


To hear the rumor for this barn, you’ll need to complete the ‘collect a sample from the hot spring lake’ Optional Accolade during the Baja Expedition. Happily, all you have to do is drive a bit through the lake to collect said sample.


**6. 1973 Porsche 911 Carrera 2.7 RS Barn Find**


This barn’s rumor should unlock at random during normal gameplay.


**7. 1956 Ford F-100 Barn Find**


This barn’s rumor should unlock at random during normal gameplay.


**8.** **1953 Chevrolet Corvette Barn Find**


This barn’s rumor should unlock at random during normal gameplay.


**9. 1993 Toyota T100 #1 Baja Truck Barn Find**


This barn’s rumor appears to unlock at random times, but may require you to be driving near the volcano while it is actively erupting.


Once you’ve received a Barn Find quest, check the map above to locate it easily.


**10. 1968 Dodge Dart Hemi Super Stock Barn Find**


This barn’s rumor should unlock at random during normal gameplay.


**11. 1991 Jaguar XJR-15 Sport Barn Find**


Discover this barn’s rumor by playing the Wilds Expedition and completing the ‘Discover the lost Barn Find notes’ Optional Accolade. You can pursue the vehicle right away during the Expedition or return to find it later.


**12.** **1970 GMC Jimmy Barn Find**


The rumor for this barn will unlock during the Apex Expedition, specifically by knocking out the ‘Reach the top of the central ruin’ Optional Accolade.


**13. 1999 Dodge Viper GTS ACR Barn Find**


This barn’s rumor should unlock at random during normal gameplay.


**14. 1989 Ferrari F40 Competizione Barn Find**


This barn’s rumor should unlock at random during normal gameplay.
