# League of Legends Testing WASD Movement: What We Know, Leaks and More

We’re now well under way into the new year of League of Legends, and with Riot taking a new seasonal approach this year, this has amped up the patch cadence.


Now, leaks are piling in regarding future updates, and it appears Riot is testing WASD movement to potentially be added into the game.


Here's what we know.


## League of Legends Testing WASD Movement: What We Know, Leaks and More


The next League of Legends patch will change quite a few champions and other elements of the game, as is the case with most LoL patches.


However, while all of these changes are much anticipated, SkinSpotlights on X, has noted that Riot is indeed testing WASD movement, as we could potentially see it be added sometime in the near future.


Using those keys for movement is something we haven't had the chance to do in LoL, and it could make moving around Summoner's Rift a lot easier, especially for newer players.
