# Black Ops 6 Season 4 Reloaded: Abomination Challenge Mode Explained

B lack Ops 6 and Warzone have been pushing along with frequent updates over the course of the season, and now with Season 3 Reloaded here, there's some more content for players to check out.


Some of the content includes a brand new game mode, and we're going to run over all we know about the Abomination Challenge.


## Black Ops 6 Season 4 Reloaded: Abomination Challenge Mode Explained


Black Ops 6 has seen a flurry of content since the game was released last year, and we're now nearing the return of a beloved map.


Season 4 Reloaded marks the first significant update of the season, and it's going to add a new event for the release of the Beavis and Butt-Head event.


The entire map will dawn the cell-shaded look, and it'll also include the following mechanics.


*Enter a cel-shaded version of Liberty Falls in this chaotic and seriously challenging Limited Time Mode! Your match begins with a baseline of classic RBZ:*


- *Available for crews of one to four players versus Zombies*
- *The match ends when all players are eliminated*
- *Mystery Boxes can contain Wonder Weapons*
- *Takes place on the entirety of the Liberty Falls map*


*However, there’s a twist. Or in this case, a vicious, drooling red-mouthed Abomination called Mo. This HVT is in a permanent state of fury, summoned from the Dark Aether when the Church is opened. Your main and overriding goal is:*


- *Defeat Mo, the HVT Abomination*
- *Exfil is disabled until the HVT is defeated*


#### *Additional Modifiers*


***Modified Features:** Expect a variety of Snacks and enemy-based modifiers as you desperately try to gain the upper hand against the undead onslaught:*


- *Rounds turn faster (expect Cranked or Rampage Inducer rates of enemy spawning)*
- *Additional enemies are added to Liberty Falls including:*


- *Amalgams*
- *Doppelghasts*
- *Elder Disciples*
- *Shock Mimics*
- *Toxic Zombies*


***Additional Features:** The Abomination Challenge LTM utilizes a modified version of the “Zombies Snacks” ruleset for Health—meaning you need to secure Snacks dropped as ground loot to increase your Health once you’ve taken damage—though players do not lose energy by moving.*


- *There is no Refrigerator Power-up*
- *Flow State is available, assuming you can slay enough of the undead without taking damage to activate it!*


***Disabled Features:** The following gameplay features are disabled in this Mode:*


- *Save and Quit*
- *Main Quest*
- *Easter Eggs*
- *Rampage Inducer*
- *Exit Strategy (GobbleGum)*
- *Idle Eyes (GobbleGum)*
