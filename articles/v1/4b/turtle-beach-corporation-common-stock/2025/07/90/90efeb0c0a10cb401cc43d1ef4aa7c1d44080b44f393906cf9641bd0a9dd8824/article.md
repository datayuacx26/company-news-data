# Destiny 2 Trials of Osiris Rewards and Map This Week (July 18-25)

**What is Trials of Osiris?**


In short, it’s Destiny’s elite top-tier PvP content and though Bungie has made steps to make it more inviting to new players in recent years, it’s still hard as nails under certain circumstances.


If you’ve played the game’s 3v3 Elimination PvPW mode, you’ll have a good idea of what to expect. The key difference with Bungie’s pinnacle PvP activity is that the stakes are so much higher.


Here’s the current rewards and map for Trials of Osiris for the beginning of January.


## Destiny 2 Trials of Osiris Map


```text
Update -   The Trials of Osiris Map is The Edge of Fate.
```


The Trials of Osiris PVP mode normally goes live on Fridays following the daily reset at 5 PM GMT/10 AM PT/1 PM ET.


## Destiny 2 Trials of Osiris Rewards This Week


```text
Update -   The Featured weapons this week is the Aisha’s Care and The Inquisitor.
```


Previously players would earn rewards when they reached 3, 5, and 7 wins, with another reward offered to those who could go flawless. However, moving forward Bungie has changed it so that players will earn Trials Engrams when they level up their reputation with Saint-14.


You can view your potential rewards below for each rank you earn, but do note that the flawless reward will change every week and offer an adept weapon.


- **Rank 4**


– Upgrade Module (x2)
- **Rank 7**


– Enhancement Prism (x3)
- **Rank 10**


– Trials weapon (changes depending on number of rank resets)
- **Rank 13**


– Upgrade Module (x2)
- **Rank 16**


– Trials weapon (changes depending on number of rank resets)
