## What Makes a Mechanical Keyboard Ideal For Gaming?


The heart of your keyboard is the mechanical switch. Pro gamers swear by switches that give tactile feedback or silky smooth linear presses, letting you feel every keystroke and hit your commands with precision. Whether you’re spamming abilities in an MMO or landing headshots in an FPS, the right switch type can shave precious milliseconds off your reaction time.


### Switch Performance


-


Linear switches give smooth keystrokes and fast reactions, ideal for fast-paced FPS titles.


-


Tactile switches have a bump you can feel, perfect for players who want subtle feedback.


-


Clicky switches offer a crisp sound with each press, great if you like to hear every move.
