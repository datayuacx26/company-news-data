## What is Tempest 3D Audio on PS5?


The Tempest Engine is Sony's dedicated audio processing technology that drives the PS5's 3D audio capabilities. Based on AMD graphics processing technology, the Tempest Engine is a re-engineered AMD GPU compute unit that processes huge amounts of sounds from different sources in game environments. Unlike traditional surround sound that relies on channel-based audio, Tempest 3D Audio creates fully immersive soundscapes where sounds appear to come from all around you.


The biggest advantage of having a dedicated audio engine built into the PS5 is eliminating reliance on TV or soundbar audio technologies. Sony researched various ear shapes and sizes using microphones and 22 individual speakers to develop Head Related Transfer Function (HRTF) profiles, ensuring personalized 3D audio experiences for different users.

## Setting Up Tempest 3D Audio with Atlas 200


Configuring your Atlas 200 for optimal Tempest 3D Audio involves specific PS5 settings adjustments:


1. Navigate to Settings >> Sound >> Audio Output >> Output Device


2. Select "Headset Connected To Controller" for Output Device


3. Set "Output To Headphones" to "All Audio"


4. Access Settings >> Sound >> Volume >> Headphones


5. Set "Volume Control (Headphones)" level to Maximum


6. Adjust microphone levels through Settings >> Sound >> Microphone >> Adjust Mic Level


Enable 3D Audio by accessing Settings > Sound and running the setup under 3D Audio for Headphones. The PS5 offers customizable audio profiles tailored to individual hearing characteristics for optimal immersive sound.
