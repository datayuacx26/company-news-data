## What is Superhuman Hearing®?


Superhuman Hearing® is Turtle Beach's exclusive, patented audio enhancement technology designed specifically for competitive gaming. Unlike standard equalizers that simply boost frequencies, Superhuman Hearing® intelligently amplifies critical in-game sounds that can mean the difference between life and death in competitive scenarios.


The technology identifies and enhances specific audio cues essential for tactical awareness in FPS games: enemy footsteps creeping up behind you, weapon reloads around corners, or distant vehicle approaches carrying reinforcements. Rather than indiscriminately boosting all frequencies, it specifically targets ranges where critical gaming audio occurs, ensuring you hear every detail without overwhelming background interference.


## How Superhuman Hearing® Works in FPS Games


Superhuman Hearing® leverages game developers' careful placement of tactical sounds in specific frequency ranges. By amplifying frequencies where footsteps, weapon handling, and environmental cues occur, it creates distinct audio advantages. Enemy footsteps on different surfaces produce unique frequency signatures - soft boots on dirt versus tactical gear on metal - and the technology enhances these differences for better enemy detection and positioning.


In fast-paced FPS environments, this enhanced audio awareness translates directly into tactical advantages. Players can detect enemies from multiple directions, identify weapons by reload sounds, and gauge distance based on audio clarity. The technology adapts to different scenarios, helping detect subtle environmental changes during quiet moments while ensuring crucial sounds remain audible during explosive action.
