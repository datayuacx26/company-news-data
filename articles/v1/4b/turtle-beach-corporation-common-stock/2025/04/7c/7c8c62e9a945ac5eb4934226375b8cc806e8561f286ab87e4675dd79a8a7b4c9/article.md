## Black Ops 6 Season 3 and Warzone: How To Unlock Kali Sticks


Black Ops 6 has seen a flurry of content since the game was released last year, and we're now nearing the return of a beloved map.


Kali Sticks are somewhat famous within Warzone, as the melee weapon wreaked havoc when they first released, and it appears they'll once again be a two-hit kill.


Players will be able to unlock them via an in-game event reward, that they've yet to shine further details on.


**Levels: 30**


**Mastery Badges: Yes**

**Customize Options:** Camos (MP, ZM, WZ).
