# Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 Editions: Deluxe Edition, Pre-Order and More

Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 is going to be the first major JRPG to release in 2025, and after a phenomenal year of JRPGs in 2024, players are amped.


While there is a ton of expectations pertaining to the game, it's going to be good to know what editions are launching with the game.


Here's what we know.


## Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 Editions: Deluxe Edition, Pre-Order and More


Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 was announced during Summer Games Fest 2024, and Sandfall Interactive has just premiered the brand new title.


The game itself looks incredible, with some JPRG elements that we all know and love, and graphically, the game looks stunning.


The game will feature some editions, that players will have the chance to pick and choose from, and these are noted below.


### Deluxe Edition


- Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 Base Game


- The “Flowers” Collection - Six outfits and hairstyles inspired by the Flowers of Lumière, along with six additional “Gommage” outfit variations. One for each playable character.


- “Clair” - A custom outfit for Maelle


- “Obscur” - A custom outfit for Gustave
