# Fortnite Chapter 6 Season 3 Battle Pass: All Skins, Cosmetics, Star Wars and More

Fortnite is now edging closer and closer to the release of a brand new season, as Chapter 6 Season 2 has been a blast.


However, there's always something to look ahead to in Fortnite, and the next season's battle pass is definitely one of them.


Here's all of the new skins coming for players to unlock.


## Fortnite Chapter 6 Season 3 Battle Pass: All Skins, Cosmetics, Star Wars and More


Waiting for the next Fortnite season is something no one truly enjoys but this time round it'll be well worth the wait.


While Chapter 6 Season 2 has been a blast thus far, there's a ton to look forward to, as Season 3 will be a shortened season. But, will bring the Star Wars theme back once again for some incredible content.


Some of this incredible content is going to be coming with the battle pass, which has the following skins:


- Emperor Palpatine
- Mace Windu
- Darth Jar Jar Binks
- Customizable Mandalorian
- Wookie Team Leader
- Captain Phasma
- Poe Dameron
- Jedi and Sith Evie
