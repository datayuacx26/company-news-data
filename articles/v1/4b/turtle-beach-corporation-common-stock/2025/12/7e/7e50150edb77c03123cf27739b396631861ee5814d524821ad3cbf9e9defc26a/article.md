## What is Active Noise Cancellation (ANC)?


Active Noise Cancellation uses microphones to detect external noise and generates opposing sound waves to cancel unwanted noise through destructive interference.


[Research in this field](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/1451965) has established the foundational principles of adaptive noise cancelling that modern gaming headsets utilize.


The technology operates on phase inversion to create "anti-noise." When two sound waves of equal amplitude but opposite phase meet, they cancel each other out, creating silence that allows focus solely on game audio.


## How Does ANC Work in Gaming Headsets?


Gaming headsets face unique challenges compared to traditional consumer audio devices. Between 20-40dB of noise reduction is quite common, which cuts the background noise level you hear to between one-quarter to one-sixteenth its original level. This substantial reduction creates an optimal environment for detecting crucial audio cues in competitive gaming scenarios, as demonstrated in


[recent ANC research applications](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/apsipa-transactions-on-signal-and-information-processing/article/recent-advances-on-active-noise-control-open-issues-and-innovative-applications/9E27156562A24026ECD6F6A49A54F53A) .


The process begins with strategically placed microphones that continuously monitor the ambient sound environment. These microphones capture external noise patterns and feed this information to a digital signal processor that analyzes the incoming sound waves. The ANC system continuously monitors the ambient noise and adjusts the anti-noise signals accordingly. This ensures that the cancellation remains effective even if the external noise changes in frequency or intensity.


Modern gaming ANC systems typically employ one of three approaches: feedforward, feedback, or hybrid systems. Feedforward ANC is, arguably, the simplest type of active noise cancellation. With feedforward technology, the noise-capturing microphones are placed on the outside of the headset, while feedback systems place microphones inside the ear cups. Hybrid systems combine both approaches for optimal performance across all frequency ranges, as detailed in


[IEEE research on ANC tutorial applications](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/763310) .

## Comparing ANC and Passive Noise Cancellation


Active Noise Cancellation advances beyond passive noise cancellation, which relies entirely on physical barriers. Passive isolation uses ear cup design and materials to block noise, while ANC actively generates counter-waves.


ANC works best for steady low-frequency sounds like air conditioning or traffic, while still reducing mid and high frequencies. This makes ANC effective against household background noise while preserving important gaming audio cues.


The Stealth Pro combines both approaches - leatherette memory foam provides passive isolation while ANC adds technological noise reduction. Other Turtle Beach models like the
