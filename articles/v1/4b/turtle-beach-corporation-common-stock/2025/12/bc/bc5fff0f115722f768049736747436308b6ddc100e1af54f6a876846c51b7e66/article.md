## Making Sense of DPI and Sensors


DPI controls how far the cursor moves per inch of hand motion. The higher the DPI, the faster the on-screen movement. However, stability matters more than raw speed. Turtle Beach’s Owl-Eye sensors (rated up to 30,000 DPI) track precisely, even at high speeds, offering accurate motion on both cloth and hard surfaces.


****


-


Use a lower DPI (400–1200) for first-person shooters to maintain control.


-


Use higher DPI (2000–4000) for RTS or creative applications.


-


Adjust DPI levels in Turtle Beach’s Swarm II software, which allows easy sensitivity switching mid-game.

#### How do I choose the right DPI for my gaming mouse?


DPI determines how far your cursor moves for every inch of physical mouse movement. A lower DPI (400–800) works well for precision-based games like Call of Duty or Valorant, while higher DPI levels (2000–4000) suit creative workflows or faster genres like RTS. Turtle Beach’s Swarm II software lets you create multiple DPI profiles so you can switch seamlessly between play styles. The key is to test sensitivity across different games until your movements feel smooth and instinctive.


****


#### Are wireless gaming mice as responsive as wired ones?


Yes, especially with Turtle Beach’s 2.4 GHz low-latency wireless technology. Models like the Burst II Air and Kone II Air deliver response times virtually identical to wired setups, giving you freedom without lag. Dual connectivity (Bluetooth and 2.4 GHz) ensures versatility whether you’re gaming on PC, console, or laptop. For tournaments or marathon sessions, you can always switch to wired mode for consistent power and uninterrupted play.


****


#### What customization can I expect from Turtle Beach gaming mice?


Turtle Beach gaming mice offer deep customization options through the Swarm II software suite. You can remap buttons, set up macros, and adjust lighting zones to suit each game profile. Even lighting effects can be synchronized with Turtle Beach headsets or keyboards for a unified setup that feels uniquely yours.
