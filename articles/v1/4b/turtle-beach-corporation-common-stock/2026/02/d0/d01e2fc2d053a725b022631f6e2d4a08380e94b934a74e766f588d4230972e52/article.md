World of Warcraft: Midnight is the game's next major expansion, and it's one that implements what might be its most significant changes yet.


Now, in the days leading up the launch of Midnight's early access portion, we're going to run over how players will be able to obtain this.


Here's what we know.


## World of Warcraft Midnight: How To Get Early Access


Midnight is bringing a whole array of changes to WoW, and these stretch from tweaks to all specializations to the full release of player housing.


There's always going to be a ton of new and returning players to the game, and the earliest that you'll be able to dive into the action will be on February 26th, as this is when the early access portion will begin.
