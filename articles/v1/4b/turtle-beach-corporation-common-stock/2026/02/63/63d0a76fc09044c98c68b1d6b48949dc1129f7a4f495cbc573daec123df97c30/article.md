# Marvel Rivals Season 6: Who Are The New Heroes?

Marvel Rivals made some waves during The Game Awards 2025, as we got our first look at both of the heroes coming in Season 6.

## Marvel Rivals Season 6: Who Are The New Heroes?


Marvel Rivals has certainly had one incredible first year of being released, as the game has released countless updates, and given players a ton of free rewards and cosmetics.


NetEase has remained committed to their two-month-long seasons, and this appears to once again be the case as both Deadpool and Elsa Bloodstone will be released in Season 6.


Deadpool's role has been changing every week since he was announced, so we won't be able to confirm his role for certain he until he arrives on January 16th. While Elsa Bloodstone's role is currently unknown, and we can assume we'll know more once Season 6.5 looms closer.
