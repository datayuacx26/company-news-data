# Black Ops 7 Season 2: All New Weapon Attachments

Black Ops 7 has been progressing smoothly since it was released, and while it does feel like other games have stolen the spotlight as of late, there is still anticipation around the new season.


Now, with the new season here, we're going to run over all we know about the new weapon attachments that have become unlockable.


Here's what we know.


## Black Ops 7 Season 2: All New Weapon Attachments


A new COD season means a ton of new content is about to be released for players, as we're once again in store for new weapons, maps and game modes.


Each season, Treyarch has consistently been adding new attachements each season for players; and the following ones are now new:


#### XM325 Titan Wield (In-Season)


*XM325 LMG, Weekly Challenge Reward*


*Attachment > Stock > XM325 Titan Wield*


*Use two devastating XM325s at once while managing belt-fed ammo and an overheat meter. Slows player movement and handling.*


#### FANG HoverPoint ELO (In-Season)


*All Weapons, Weekly Challenge Reward*


*Attachment > Optic >[FANG HoverPoint ELO](https://warfareengage.com/how-to-get-the-fang-hoverpoint-elo-fast-in-black-ops-7-bo7-zombies-warzone-and-endgame/)*


*Discreet with a thin frame, this reflex sight features a floating illuminated reticle and improves stability at the expense of handling.*


#### A.R.C. M1 Assault Frame (In-Season)


*A.R.C. M1 Launcher, Weekly Challenge Reward*


*Attachment > Underbarrel > A.R.C. M1 Assault Frame*


*Top mounted control system that shortens both the charge time and cooldown to support aggressive use against enemy personnel. Increases explosion radius and provides improvements to both mobility and handling.*


#### Razor 9mm Wildfire Conversion (In-Season)


*Razor 9mm SMG, Weekly Challenge Reward*


*Attachment > Fire Mods > Extreme Rapid Fire*


*Conversion kit offering a massive improvement to both fire rate and bullet velocity and a larger magazine. Adds spread to ADS and impacts movement speed when firing.*


#### Echo 12 Backlash Launcher Kit (Mid-Season)


*Echo 12 Shotgun, Event Reward*


*Attachment > Fire Mods > Grenade Launcher Conversion*


*Conversion to support the firing of fast-moving explosive projectiles that can bounce off surfaces. Explosive damage is increased if detonation occurs after bouncing.*


#### MXR-17 ANVL Conversion (Mid-Season)


*MXR-17, Weekly Challenge Reward*


*Attachment > Barrel > MXR-17 ANVL Conversion*


*Converts the MXR-17 into a harder hitting weapon with increased ammo. Recoil and fire rate decreases over time the longer the weapon is fired.*
