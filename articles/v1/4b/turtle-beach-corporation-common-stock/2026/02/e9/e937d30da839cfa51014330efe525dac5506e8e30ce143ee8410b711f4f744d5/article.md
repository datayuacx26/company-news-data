# Overwatch Hero Jetpack Cat: Abilities, Ultimate, and More

Overwatch has now been fully rebranded, and Overwatch 2 is no more, and Blizzard is essentially resetting the clock on the game's life cycle.


Now, they're adding five new heroes at the onset of Season 1, and we're going to run over all we know about Jetpack Cat.


Here's what we know.


## Overwatch Hero Jetpack Cat: Abilities, Ultimate, and More


Blizzard releasing five new heroes at the onset of Season 1 was something that no one expected, but here we are.


Now, some brand new heroes are coming, including Jetpack Cat, an adorable new Support for players to try out.


Jetpack Cat's abilites and more can be viewed below:


- **Biotic Pawjectiles (Primary Fire)**


- Mid‑range projectile spread that heals allies and damages enemies.
- **Lifeline** - Toggle into transport mode, allowing an ally to be towed. Increases movement speed and heals your ally.
- **Frenetic Flight**


- Accelerate in your movement direction. Fuel recovery is slower while carrying another player.
- **Purr**


- Pulsing area heal that increases in frequency over time. Knockback nearby enemies when activated.
- **Ultimate — Catnapper**


- Dive towards a ground location, knocking down enemies and tethering the nearest one to you.
- **Passive – Jetpack** - Permanent flight.
