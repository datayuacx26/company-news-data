# Beast of Reincarnation Platforms: PlayStation, PC, Xbox and More

Game Freak is a company that many of us know and adore, and while they're perhaps best known for Pokémon, they're seemingly branching out even further now.


Beast of Reincarnation has struck major similarities to other JRPG action titles, and we're going to run over all we know about the platforms the game will be released on.

## Beast of Reincarnation Platforms: PlayStation, PC, Xbox and More


Beast of Reincarnation was originally revealed in 2025, and now, during the most recent Xbox Developer_ Direct, they showcased some more gameplay.


With combat similar to Final Fantasy 7 Rebirth in many ways, there is certainly some anticipation around the new game.


Regarding the platforms the game will be released on, we know the following:


- PC
- PlayStation 5
