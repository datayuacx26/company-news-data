## Overwatch Season 1: Is 6v6 Back? Answered


Overwatch is hitting the refresh button on their game once again, as they're removing the '2' and going back to their roots in ways.


However, with the soft relaunch of the game; many were wondering if they're planning to scrap the 5v5 feature, which was a main selling point behind OW2.


At the time of writing, it does appear as that 6v6 will not return with the new Overwatch season, and 5v5 will once again be the primary game mode. But 6v6 will more than likely make its way into the Arcade section of OW for the time being.
