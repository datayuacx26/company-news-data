# PlayStation State of Play February 2026: Date, Time, Games and More

PlayStation's State of Play events have been some of the focal points throughout each year, as it's a fantastic way for developers to showcase their upcoming titles.


State of Play is back and we're going to run over all we know about the upcoming one for February 2026.


Here's what we know.


## PlayStation State of Play February 2026: Date, Time, Games and More


State of Plays are a community event, and the discussion and discourse around each event, whether happy or not; is always immense.


With titles such as Wolverine, Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet and Final Fantasy VII Remake Part 3 all having the chance to be shown, the excitement is building.


The February 2026 PlayStation State of Play is going to air at the following times on February 12th.


- 2:00 pm PST


- 5:00 pm EST


- 11:00 pm CEST


- 7:00 am JST (February 13th)


As well, they've noted the following into what players can expect.


*Tune in live this Thursday for 60+ minutes of news, gameplay updates, and announcements from game studios across the globe. February’s State of Play will spotlight eye-catching third-party and indie games headed to PS5, along with the latest from teams at PlayStation Studios.*


*State of Play broadcasts live February 12 at 2pm PT / 5pm ET / 11pm CEST | February 13 at 7am JST on YouTube and Twitch, and will be broadcast in English with Japanese subtitles also available. We’ll see you then!*
