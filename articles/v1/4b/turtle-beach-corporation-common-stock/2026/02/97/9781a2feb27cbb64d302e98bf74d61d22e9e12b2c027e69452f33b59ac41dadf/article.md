# Is Anran's Face Being Changed In Overwatch? All We Know and More

Overwatch has now been fully rebranded, and Overwatch 2 is no more, and Blizzard is essentially resetting the clock on the game's life cycle.


New in Season 1 is the DPS hero, Anran, the sister of Wuyang, and there has been some discussion about her design, and Blizzard has now responded to cries for her face to be redesigned.

## Is Anran's Face Being Changed In Overwatch? All We Know and More
