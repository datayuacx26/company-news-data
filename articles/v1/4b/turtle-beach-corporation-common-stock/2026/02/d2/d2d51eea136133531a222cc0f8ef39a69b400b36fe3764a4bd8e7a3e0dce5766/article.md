# Deadlock Hero Silver: Abilities, Ultimate and More

Deadlock has been Valve's worst-kept secret since the closed beta became public during the Summer of 2024.


Now, with a wide array of new heroes joining the game as of late, we're going to run over all we know about Silver.


Here's what we know.


## Deadlock Hero Silver: Abilities, Ultimate and More


Deadlock is the latest game from Valve, and much like their other titles, this one is diving into the category of a hero shooter, combined alongside a MOBA.


Now, Silver has joined the fray, and similarly to Gnar from League of Legends, you'll have two sets of abilities, which will depend on the current form that Silver is in.


Silver's abilites are as follows:


Human Form


- Slam Fire


- Instantly reload your gun. You gain bonus fire rate and deal bonus weapon damage, based on the target's health for a limited number of shots, but suffer from reduced accuracy.


- Boot Kick


- Dash forward and kick the first enemy hit, dealing melee damage while pushing yourself off and marking them. Shooting the marked target deals spirit damage and removed the mark.


- Entangling Bola


- Throw a bola, dealing spirit damage, applying slow and preventing movement abilities, stamina or jumping.


- Lycan Curse (Ultimate)


-


*****Passive:***** **** Dealing damage generates bloodlust, increased at low health. At max bloodlust, you automatically cast Lycan Curse.


-


*****Active***** ****:**** Instantly transform, gaining increased max health, move speed, and stacking fire rate on enemy heroes, and replacing your abilities and weapon with their ferocious versions.


Ferocious Form


- Go For The Throat


- Deal melee damage to all enemies in front of you, receiving a heal for a portion of the damage dealt. The damage is increased the lower health they have.


- Mauling Leap


- Leap forward, mauling the first enemy hit for melee damage. They suffer from reduced bullet resist and take spirit damage over time.


- Tail Wack


- Smash enemies around you, dealing spirit damage. They suffer from disarm and a brief slow.
