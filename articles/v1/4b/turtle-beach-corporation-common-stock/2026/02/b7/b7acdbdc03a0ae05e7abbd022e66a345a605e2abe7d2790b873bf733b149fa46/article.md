## Deadlock System Requirements: Minimum, Recommended, Steam Deck and More

But, after playing Deadlock ourselves, and testing the game throughout it's development thus far, we would at least recommend the following specs:


- Any CPU above an Intel i5 series or AMD Ryzen 5000 series
- GTX 960/R9 380 and higher is a safe bet
- 16GB Ram
- 35GB Storage
