Fable is a title that many of us have either played or at least heard of while growing up, as the three games by Lionhead Studios have many fond memories with gamers.

## When Is Fable Releasing? Answered


While Playground Games has built a reputation over the years as the Forza developers, they're branching out and taking on a mega-project in Fable.


The game has now been fully revealed to us, and the world of Albion has never looked more alive and rife with activities to do and people to talk to.


Regarding when Fable will be released, Playground Games has confirmed it'll be released sometime during Autumn 2026. With titles such as Wolverine and GTA 6 still on the slate for later this year, we could see an early Fall release date.
