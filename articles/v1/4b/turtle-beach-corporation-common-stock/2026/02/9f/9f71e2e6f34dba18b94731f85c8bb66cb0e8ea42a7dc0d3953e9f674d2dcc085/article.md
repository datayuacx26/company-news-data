# How Many Cars Are There In Forza Horizon 6? Answered

Forza Horizon 6 is perhaps the game that Xbox fans are most anticipating this year, and with Playground Games revealing more information lately, we're stoked.


While we're sure many of you cannot wait to drive around Japan, it's always good to know how many cars there will be in the game.


Here's all we know.


## How Many Cars Are There In Forza Horizon 6? Answered


Forza has been touted over the years as the premier racing title, especially with how lackluster some Need for Speed entries have been in recent memory.


Now, Playground Games is aiming to capitalize on this, as with the game taking place in Japan, this is one that many have been waiting for.


During the reveal broadcast for Horizon 6, Playground Games confirmed that there will be over 550 cars on launch for players to drive and collect. This is indeed the most ever for a Horizon title for its launch, and we suspect this number will grow over the years as DLC is released.
