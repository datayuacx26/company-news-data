## Black Ops 7 Season 2: All New Scorestreaks


A new COD season is always a good time to dive back into the game, as for numerous years now they've been intent on releasing a flury of content each go around.


Now, some brand new Scorestreaks are going to be making their way into the game, similarly to how they did in Season 1.


As of now, the following ones are confirmed to be added.


### **Scorestreak: Lockshot (Mid-Season)**


**Score:** 450


**Mastery Badges: Yes**


*A sleek smart pistol with 3 rounds that can lock onto multiple targets. A single trigger pull eliminates every locked target in quick succession. Unlock via Weekly Challenge.*


Multiple enemy Operators in sight? No problem. With this smart gun in hand, you can bundle them up, fire, and hit all marked targets in the blink of an eye.


**Overclock Abilities**


- **Multi-Lock:** Adds one round to the magazine, allowing you to lock onto one additional enemy.
- **Quick-Lock:** Reduces enemy lock-on time.
