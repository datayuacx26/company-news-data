# All Leaked and Rumored Heroes Coming To Marvel Rivals

[Marvel Rivals](https://www.turtlebeach.com/blog/category/guides?tag=Marvel%20Rivals) Season 4 has been released for over a month now, and there's been a ton of positive this season, from the likes of Angela to Daredevil.


However, we're always looking ahead, and there's been a few heroes datamined that we're going to run over below.


## All Leaked and Rumored Heroes Coming To Marvel Rivals


While a lot of the attention in the hero shooter landscape in recent weeks has been on the surge of Overwatch 2 and Deadlock once again, Rivals still holds a firm grasp on players.


Rivals has maintained its stance on releasing two heroes each season, and going forward, there's some noted by[X0X_Leak](https://x.com/X0X_LEAK/status/1967052074593775930) that all already have abilities in the backend.


- Professor X
- Gambit
- Rogue
- Elsa Bloodstone
- Locus
- Colossus


Gambit and Rogue are rumoured to be the next crop of heroes to join the game in Season 5, as X0X_Leak indicated that the last lore card for Season 4 references their abilites.
