# Battlefield 6 Season 2: All New Limited Time Modes

[Battlefield 6](https://www.turtlebeach.com/blog/category/guides?tag=Battlefield%206) has been off to a solid start, and players are relatively pleased with all of the changes thus far, for the most part.


Season 2 is now here, and there's going to be soe new Limited Time Modes (LTMs) for players to check out.


Here's all of the new ones for Season 2 in BF6.


## Battlefield 6 Season 2: All New Limited Time Modes


Battlefield 6 has been off to a flying start, as EA is committed to listening to players, and it shows with the smooth release thus far.


While there is not a new BR coming this season, there are going to be some new Limited Time Modes, and these include:


## *VL-7 STRIKE LIMITED-TIME MODE*


**


*Stay alert and go in fast. Clouds of a new psychoactive smoke, VL-7, infiltrate various areas in Multiplayer for a limited time, a result of NATO's new attack against Pax Armata. Load up on masks and filters or risk experiencing its disorienting effects.*


*VL-7 does not do any damage; instead, its hallucinatory effects will make any soldier second guess charging into it without a mask, allowing for unique strategic decisions that create new paths to victory. The VL-7 Strike Limited-Time Event will be a dedicated Multiplayer playlist featuring the new map Contaminated, which was tested through Battlefield Labs at the start of the new year. Regardless of Class, all soldiers will be given a mask to survive the VL-7, with additional masks and filters available around the map.*


## *GAUNTLET ALTERED STATE LIMITED-TIME MODE*


**


*Survive and advance in Gauntlet: a cutthroat, knockout-style elimination mode. Upgraded with new smoke features, squads need to race to complete missions whilst navigating the disorienting effects.*


*This Limited-Time Mode features the VL-7 smoke in Fort Lyndon, adding a psychoactive effect to the already deadly Gauntlet. Out in the field of a Gauntlet Altered State operation, VL-7 may be present around certain objectives, rewarding squads who can keep their head in the game long enough to rack up points.*


## *BATTLE ROYALE - SYNTHESIS LIMITED-TIME MODE*


**


*Smoke engulfs Fort Lyndon, transforming the large-scale battleground. Engage in thrilling new warfare, fight amongst the psychoactive smoke, and loot masks and filters to withstand its devastating effects.*


*Given that Fort Lyndon is the manufacturing site for VL-7, Battle Royale players can find - or earn - a VL-7 Strike Package similar to calling in a smoke or mortar strike. In addition to player-designated strikes, VL-7 smoke may seep out of specific areas where synthesis went awry. Intel suggests that the VL-7 leaks are tied to high-value loot, so the risk of braving its effects may be worth the rewards of a better loadout…*
