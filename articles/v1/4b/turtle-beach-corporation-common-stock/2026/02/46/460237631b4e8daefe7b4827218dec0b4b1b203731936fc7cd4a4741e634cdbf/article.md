# Fable: Can I Make My Own Character? Answered

Fable is a title that many of us have either played or at least heard of while growing up, as the three games by Lionhead Studios have many fond memories with gamers.


Now, Playground Games have been working on their version of a Fable game for quite some time and we're going to run over all we know about the character creation system.


## Fable: Can I Make My Own Character? Answered


While Playground Games has built a reputation over the years as the Forza developers, they're branching out and taking on a mega-project in Fable.


The game has now been fully revealed to us, and the world of Albion has never looked more alive and rife with activities to do and people to talk to.


Initially, when Fable was first revealed to players, there was a general understanding that we would be playing as a character that Playground intended for us to play as. Now, in the recent Xbox Developer_ Direct, the new gameplay footage dove into further detail, and yes, you can indeed create your own character for Fable 2026.
