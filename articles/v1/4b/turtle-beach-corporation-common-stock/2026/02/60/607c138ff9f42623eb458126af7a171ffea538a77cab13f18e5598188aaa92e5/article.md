## Arc Raiders Shrouded Sky Update: Release Date, Content and All We Know


Shrouded Sky will be the next major update for Arc Raiders, as Embark is consistently going to be rolling out major updates each month.


February is bringing us Shrouded Sky, and we suspect that it'll be released in the next couple of weeks, and Embark has confirmed the following content is planned:


- New map condition
- New Arc threat
- Player project
- Raider deck
- Map updates
- Expedition window opens


We aren't too sure what the new Arc will be, but keep in mind that this will also be when the 'wipe' timeframe opens. So, be sure to start saving some Credits to secure those skill points for when you eventually tackle the Expeditions.
