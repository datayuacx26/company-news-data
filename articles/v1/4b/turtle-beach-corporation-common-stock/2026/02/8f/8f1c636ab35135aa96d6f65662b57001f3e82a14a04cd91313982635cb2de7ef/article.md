# Overwatch Season 1: All Stadium Heroes

Overwatch has now been fully rebranded, and Overwatch 2 is no more, and Blizzard is essentially resetting the clock on the game's life cycle.


Stadium is the other premier game mode in Overwatch, and we're going to run over all of the playable heroes thus far.


Here's what we know.


## Overwatch Season 1: All Stadium Heroes


Blizzard releasing five new heroes at the onset of Season 1 was something that no one expected, but here we are.


Season 1 is bringing a ton of content into the game, and not only to the standard Quick Play and Competitive modes. But, Stadium will be getting a little refresh, with more curated recommended item buys and a more user-friendly experience.
