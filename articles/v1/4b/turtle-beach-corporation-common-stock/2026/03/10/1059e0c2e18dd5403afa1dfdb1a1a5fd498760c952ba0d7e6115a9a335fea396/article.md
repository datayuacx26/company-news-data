# World of Warcraft Midnight Raid Release Schedule - Season 1, Voidspire and Dreamrift Raid

World of Warcraft: Midnight is the game's next major expansion, and it's one that implements what might be its most significant changes yet.


The release of the brand new Raids is going to be something to know before diving into the DLC, and we're going to go over the full release schedule that Blizzard has shared.


Here's what we know.


## World of Warcraft Midnight Raid Release Schedule - Season 1, Voidspire and Dreamrift Raid


Midnight is bringing a whole array of changes to WoW, and these stretch from tweaks to all specializations to the full release of player housing.


Raiding is expected to still be as popular as ever, and in Season 1 of Midnight; there's actually going to be three separate Raids for players to enter.


- The Voidspire


- 6 Bosses


- The Dreamrift


- 1 Boss


- March on Quel'Danas


- 2 Bosses


- Final Raid of Season 1


Blizzard is also intending on staggering their release, so that players can complete the first two Raids beforehand.


- ***Week of March 17***


- *Voidspire Raid – Raid Finder Wing 1, Normal, and Heroic difficulties*


- *Dreamrift Raid – Raid Finder, Normal, and Heroic difficulties*


- *Season 1 Dungeons – Heroic and Mythic difficulties*


- *PvP Season 1*


- *Bountiful Delves*


- ***Week of March 24***


- *Voidspire Raid – Story Mode, Raid Finder Wing 2, and Mythic difficulties*


- *Dreamrift Raid – Mythic difficulty*


- *Mythic+ Season 1*


- ***Week of March 31***


- *March on Quel’Danas Raid – Normal, Heroic, and Mythic difficulties*


- *Voidspire Raid – Raid Finder Wing 3*


- ***Week of April 7***


- *March on Quel’Danas Raid – Story Mode and Raid Finder*
