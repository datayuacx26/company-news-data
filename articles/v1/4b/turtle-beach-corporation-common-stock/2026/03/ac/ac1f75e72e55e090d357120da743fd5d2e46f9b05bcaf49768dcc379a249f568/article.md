#### G *auntlet*


*New Mode*


**


*Show what you’re made of in the new Gauntlet mode, pitting two six-player teams against one another in a round of five different game modes played one after the other including Domination, Kill Confirmed, Hardpoint, Team Deathmatch, and Roulette, a high risk vs. reward mode where players fight over control of an Akimbo Hand Cannon. The devastating weapon inflicts massive damage, at the risk of blowing up in your Operator’s hands, dropping the weapon for the next Operator to pick up.*


*Each mode lasts two minutes, immediately moving from one to the next once the timer counts down to zero. Teams are awarded points after each mode ends, based on their performance in that mode. The team with the highest Score at the end of all five modes wins the match. Do you have what it takes to excel no matter the mode? Now’s your chance to prove it.*


#### *Infected*


*Returning Mode*


**


*The fan-favorite Infected Party Game returns! Face off against the Infected horde or join them and eliminate the remaining survivors to win.*


*A single player is randomly chosen to start as the Infected following the match countdown. The infected player’s goal is to eliminate the remaining Survivors, adding to the ranks of the Infected with each fallen Operator. To make matters worse, the Infected are joined by additional undead enemies, increasing the number of swarming Infected. This time, the Infected can earn one of two Mutant Injection Scorestreaks to up their offense against the Survivors, transforming into a Mangler or Disciple to wreak havoc on the living.*


*As an Operator, use your given weapons, Equipment, and Scorestreaks to outlast the horde at all costs. Survive until the end to declare victory for humanity.*
