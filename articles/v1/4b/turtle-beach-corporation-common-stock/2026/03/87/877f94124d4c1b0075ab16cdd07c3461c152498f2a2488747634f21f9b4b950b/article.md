# Warzone Season 2 Ranked Play Resurgence: All Rewards and More

Black Ops 7 Season 2 has now arrived, and while the attention has primarily been around other games as of late, the new season is bound to catch some eyeballs.


Warzone is also getting an abundance of changes, and this includes a brand new Ranked Play Resurgence game mode.


Here's all you need to know about it.


## Warzone Season 2 Ranked Play Resurgence: All Rewards and More


A new COD season is always a good time to dive back into the game, as for numerous years now they've been intent on releasing new weapons each season.


Warzone is getting a brand new Ranked Play iteration on the release of Season 2, and Treyarch has noted that the following rewards will be available to players to earn.


#### *Season 02 Career Rewards*


**


*The following rewards are available as you continue to earn top placements during Season 02 Resurgence: Ranked Play:*


- ***3 Top Placements:** “Honor’s Laurel” Emblem*
- ***10 Top Placements:** “BO7 Ranked Competitor” Operator Skins (2x Skins; Male and Female)*
- ***20 Top Placements:** “Oblivauric” Blueprint (SMG, Dravec 45)*
- ***40 Top Placements:** “Chronicled Wins” Loading Screen*
- ***50 Top Placements:** “Bluecrest” Weapon Camo (Universal)*
- ***100 Top Placements:** “Chimmy” Weapon Charm*
- ***150 Top Placements:** “Gravescale” Large Decal*
- ***250 Top Placements:** “The Last Laugh” Calling Card*
- ***300 Top Placements:** “Ossuary King” Emblem and “Night Reaper” Blueprint (Melee, Knife)*

#### *All Seasons: Rank Skins*


**


*The following Operator Skin rewards are available as you continue to Rank up during Season 02 Resurgence: Ranked Play or any future Season. The reward Skins include two different Skins and are awarded when you are placed in or reach each designated Rank, except for top 250 which is rewarded at the end of the season.*


- ***Reach Gold Division:** WZ Gold Competitor (2x Skins)*
- ***Reach Platinum Division:** WZ Platinum Competitor (2x Skins)*
- ***Reach Diamond Division:** WZ Diamond Competitor (2x Skins)*
- ***Reach Crimson Division:** WZ Crimson Competitor (2x Skins)*
- ***Reach Iridescent Division:** WZ Iridescent Competitor (2x Skins)*
- ***Reach Top 250 Division*:** WZ Top 250 Competitor (2x Skins)*
- ***Reach #1 Overall*:** WZ Top 250 Champion (2x Skins)*


*(* Awarded only to players that have ended the season in the Top 250 or as #1 Overall).*


#### *Season 02: Seasonal Rewards*


**


*The following Blueprint, Large Decal, Calling Cards, and Weapon Camos are only available during Season 02:*


- ***Reach 10 Top Placements in Season 02:** “Chrimerean” Blueprint (Sniper Rifle, Shadow SK)*
- ***Reach 25 Top Placements in Season 02:** “Stone Cold” Large Decal*
- ***Reach Silver Rank in Season 02:** “Satyr” Calling Card*
- ***Reach Gold Rank in Season 02:** “Siren” Weapon Camo (Universal) and “Triton” Calling Card*
- ***Reach Platinum Rank in Season 02:** “Hit List” Weapon Camo (Universal) and “Stoned” Calling Card*
- ***Reach Diamond Rank in Season 02:** “Minotaur” Weapon Camo (Universal) and “Lionizer” Calling Card*
- ***Reach Crimson Rank in Season 02:** “Spartan” Weapon Camo (Universal) and “OpTempo” Calling Card*
- ***Reach Iridescent Rank in Season 02:** “Cyclops” Weapon Camo (Universal) and “Eye Lord” Calling Card*
- ***Finish in the Top 250 in Season 02:** “Herculean” Weapon Camo (Animated, Universal) and “Shock & Awe” Calling Card*
- ***Finish Season 02 #1 Overall:** Monstrum Calling Card*
