Step into the world of Crimson Desert and it quickly becomes clear this is not just one land but many, and Pearl Abyss has crafted an incredible world.


With exploration at the helm of reasons why people are diving into Crimson Desert, we're now going to run over all of the known regions to explore.

## All Regions In Crimson Desert


From windswept plains to war-torn strongholds and untamed wilderness, each region feels carefully crafted to tell its own story, and you'll be exploring them all.


It'll take a long journey to venture throughout the lands of Crimson Desert, and players will be able to come across the following regions as well:


- Hernand


- A region rich with mountains and rivers, also the starting region


- Pailune


- Northernmost region of Pywel, and homeland to the Greymanes


- Demeniss


- Capital of Pywel


- Delesyia


- A region at the forefront of science and technology
