# World of Warcraft The War Within Meta Achievement: Requirements, Rewards and More

World of Warcraft The War Within is nearing it's end, and it feels surreal that this will be the last patch before the Midnight DLC.


Before we head into Midnight, we're going to run over all we know about the new War Within Meta Achievement.


Here's all you need to know.


## World of Warcraft The War Within Meta Achievement: Requirements, Rewards and More


The War Within has been the main focus of WoW since August 2024, and over the course of this time we've been through a lot, from raiding The Undermine to saving K'aresh from the forces of the Manaforge Omega.


Now, heading into Midnight, Blizzard has now released the Meta Achievement for the DLC, which will reward players with the Geargrinder MK. 11.


Image via WoWHead.


In terms of what is required to obtain this reward, you'll need to have the following achievements completed.


- Nerub-ar Palace
- Liberation of Undermine
- Manaforge Omega
- All That Khaz
- You Xal Not Pass
- The War Within Pathfinder
- Glory of the Delver
- Going Goblin Mode
- Owner of a Radiant Heart
- Unravelled and Persevering
