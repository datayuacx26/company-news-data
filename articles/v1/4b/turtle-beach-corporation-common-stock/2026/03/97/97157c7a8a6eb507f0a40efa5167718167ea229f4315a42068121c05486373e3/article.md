World of Warcraft Midnight has been an absolute blast thus far, as the game has been progressing along smoothly with some stunning zones.

## WoW Midnight Season 1: When Does Mythic+ Release? Answered


WoW Midnight is aiming to re-invent World of Warcraft in many facets, as not only are there major changes coming to all 39 specializations in the game, but the revamped Silvermoon has been praised heavily.


Among these changes are also some familiar game modes, such as an end-game pillar in the form of Mythic+. The dungeon-based game mode has become one of the staples of WoW, and many are amped to dive back into the dungeon pool.


In terms of when it'll be released in Season 1, Blizzard has staggered the release date of Mplus to be a week after the raid is released, so March 24th, 2026.
