# Overwatch Stadium: How To Switch Between 1st and Third Person

Overwatch has now been fully rebranded, and Overwatch 2 is no more, and Blizzard is essentially resetting the clock on the game's life cycle.


With the game thriving, and those diving into Stadium for the very first time are going to want to know how to change their camera POV.


Here's all we know.


## Overwatch Stadium: How To Switch Between 1st and 3rd Person


Overwatch is hitting the refresh button on their game once again, as they're removing the '2' and going back to their roots in ways. But, Stadium itself is still alive and well, and the mode has been enjoyed by many thus far.


An aspect of Stadium that is unique compared to the rest of OW is that the camera perspective is naturally set to be in third person, and this is how many play the game mode.


However, for heroes such as Ashe and Widowmaker, it'll be good to always be in first person so that it's easier to aim at opposing players. In order to change this camera angle, players will need to follow the steps below:


- Settings
- Options
- Stadium
- Camera Perspective


You're also able to change this setting depending on the hero, so you can have pre-set settings for certain heroes.
