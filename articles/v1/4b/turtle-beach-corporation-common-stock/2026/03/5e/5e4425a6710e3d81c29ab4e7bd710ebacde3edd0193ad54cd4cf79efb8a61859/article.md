# Marvel Rivals White Fox: Abilities, Ultimate and More

Marvel Rivals has been making waves thus far in 2026, and we're now ready for a whole new season of content from NetEase.


Season 7 is now here, and with this comes White Fox, and we're going to run over all we know about her abilities.


Here's all we know.


## Marvel Rivals White Fox: Abilities, Ultimate and More


Season 7 of Marvel Rivals is once again adding two new heroes to the game, and they're also changing up how ultimates and other mechanics work.


White Fox will be the next Strategist to join the party, and with her kit striking similarities to Sue Storm, many are going to enjoy playing as her.


- Yeowoo Gueseul


- Primary Fire


- Fire a Fox Marble forward that bounces off terrain or heroes, then homes in on nearby heroes. It damages enemies, heals allies, and restores Spirit Tail energy.


- Claw Strike


- Awakened Primary Fire


- While Awakened, slash forward with a flurry of attacks.


- Ninefold Slam


- Primary Fire (Kumiho Form)


- In Kumiho Form, slam the ground, causing an explosive shockwave. Hitting enemies or summons grants One-Time Healing to self and nearby allies.


- Spirit Sanctuary


- Shift


- Teleport to a chosen ally, instantly healing allies in the area while generating a shield.


- Predatory Pounce


- Awakened Shift


- While Awakened, dash forward. Hit an enemy to Slow and flip over them, enabling a second dash that knocks back enemies.


- Fox Form Awakening


- E


- Enter Awakened state, transforming Spirit Tails into physical tails. While active, continuously drain Spirit Tail energy and heal nearby allies. Striking enemies provides additional area healing.


- Spectral Surge


- RMB


- Consume one Spirit Tail to send a spectral fox forward. It heals and grants invulnerability to allies it passes through, damages and Charms enemies, forcing foes to move toward White Fox.


- Tail Sweep


- Awakened RMB


- While Awakened, sweep forward tails to launch up hit enemies.


- Blessed By The Nine


- RMB Kumiho Form


- In Kumiho Form, select an ally to grant Continuous Healing, Unstoppable, and Lifesteal for a short time.


- Kumiho Unleashed


- Q


- Manifest the full Nine-Tailed Fox form and continuously heal nearby allies. Cannot receive healing effects from others while in this form.
