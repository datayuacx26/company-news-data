# Marvel's Wolverine: Release Date, Trailer, PS5 and All We Know

After the barnstorming success of Marvel’s Spider-Man, Insomniac Games is taking on another fan favourite Marvel character with a brand new game based on the adamantium-clawed Mutant, Wolverine.


It’s well documented that a lot of the team at Insomniac love their comics, and it seems they’re also huge fans of Logan too.


While it's been some time since we've last heard about the game, we're going to run over all we know.


## Marvel's Wolverine: Release Date, Trailer, PS5 and All We Know


### Latest News


Insomniac Games has officially revealed a brand new gameplay trailer for Wolverine, and have confirmed the release date of Fall 2026.


### Release Date


Marvel's Wolverine will be released on September 15th, 2026 on PlayStation 5 consoles only.
