## Hytale: How Big Is The Map? Answered


Hytale was originally set to be released back in 2021, just a year shy of when Riot purchased the company, whose game at the time had over 2.5 million registrants.


The game is now playable by everyone, and to our surprise, the game features a ton of unique and interesting locations to explore.


However. While many assume that Hytale's map is the same, this is not the case, as the maps are procedurally generated. Meaning that they're going to be completely random each time, similar to how Minecraft operates.


Biomes are consistent in Hytale, however, and you're going to see the same ones from time to time, and some of these include the following:


- Emerald Grove
- Howling Sands
- Whisperfrost Frontiers
- Devastated Lands
