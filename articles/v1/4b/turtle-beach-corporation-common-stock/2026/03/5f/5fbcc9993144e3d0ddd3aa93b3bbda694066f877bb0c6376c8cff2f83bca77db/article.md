# Marvel Rivals Season 7: All Team Up Changes

Marvel Rivals has been making waves thus far in 2026, and we're now ready for a whole new season of content from NetEase.


Now, we're looking ahead to Season 7 and we're going to run over all we know about the new team-up changes.


Here's what we know.


## Marvel Rivals Season 7: All Team Up Changes


Season 7 of Marvel Rivals is once again adding two new heroes to the game, and they're also changing up how ultimates and other mechanics work.


With a new focus on less ultimate spam, they've also tweaked some of the team-ups we've gotten used to over the course of the season.


The following changes have been announced thus far:


## **TEAM-UP ABILITIES**


### **ADJUSTED OR REMOVED**


**Duality Dance (Adam Warlock - Luna Snow)**
Cosmic symphony on pause. Duality Dance has danced its last dance.


- Adam Warlock loses the Team-Up Anchor 15% Healing Boost.


**Jeff-nado (Storm - Jeff the Land Shark)**
The Jeff-Nado has dissipated.
- Jeff will still cause whirlwinds…of chaos elsewhere.


### **NEW**


**Blessing of the Kumiho (White Fox - Luna Snow)**
Espionage meets idol energy. Swift, precise, unstoppable.


- As the Team-Up Anchor, White Fox gains a 10% increase in healing effects.


- Luna Snow unlocks the new Spirit Fox Accord ability through her Team-Up with White Fox.


**Cosmic Cyclone (Storm - Adam Warlock)**


Wind, lightning, and soul. An astral duet worthy of the cosmos.
- Adam Warlock gains the Heavenly Harmony ability through his Team-Up with Storm.
