# Battlefield 6 Season 2: All New Weapons Coming

[Battlefield 6](https://www.turtlebeach.com/blog/category/guides?tag=Battlefield%206) has been off to a solid start, and players are relatively pleased with all of the changes thus far, for the most part.


Season 2 is now here, and for those diving into the season, or jumping back into the game are going to want to know about all of the new weapons coming.


Here's what we know.


## Battlefield 6 Season 2: All New Weapons Coming


Battlefield 6 has been off to a flying start, as EA is committed to listening to players, and it shows with the smooth release thus far.


Season 1 added a ton of content, such as the Redsec Battle Royal, and Season 2 will be no different, as there are a ton of new weapons for players to obtain.


For Season 2, the new weapons include:


## NEW DESIGNATED MARKSMAN RIFLE: GRT-CPS


*A traditional counterpart to the GRT-BC, this intermediate rifle in a marksman configuration trades damage for controllability and increased ammo capacity.*


Fast-firing, accurate, and able to feed on large magazine attachments, the GRT-CPS demands your full attention to wrestle its recoil to manageable levels. Those who tame this beast will unlock the maximum potential of its mid-to-long-range damage profile, suited for squad wipes in Fort Lyndon, Contaminated, or in any situation that calls for longshots in quick succession.


## NEW ASSAULT RIFLE: VCR-2


*Originating from the Balkans, this high fire rate bullpup is the perfect rifle for close-quarters engagements.*


A rival to the popular KORD 6P67, the VCR-2 can be best described as an SMG acting as an Assault Rifle, able to pulverize targets up-close with a blistering rate of fire and high-caliber bullets. However, any soldier knows the consequences of too much firepower: the recoil is a recruit-level trap, demanding a steady counterbalance in order to make it effective at longer ranges.


## NEW LIGHT MACHINE GUN: M121 A2


*This powerful general-purpose machine gun excels at closer ranges but can extend its reach with competent recoil and trigger management.*


Sensing a theme with Season 2’s new weapons? The M121 A2’s rate of fire is on the high end of its category with a solid standard magazine size. Built for wiping squads, this LMG may have a slightly longer reload cycle and heavier recoil than its peers, but is highly effective for dealing direct damage or suppression in the hands of a capable Support Class soldier.


## NEW ENGINEER GADGET: 9K38 IGLA ACTIVE LOCKING AIR-DEFENSE LAUNCHER


*A man-portable air-defense system designed to target enemy aircraft. Requires lock-on before launch, after which the user must maintain target acquisition by tracking the aircraft with the weapon sight to preserve missile guidance. Missiles can reacquire targets mid-flight if tracking is lost or countermeasures are deployed.*
