# Black Ops 7 Season 2 Reloaded: All New Maps

Black Ops 7 has been progressing smoothly since it was released, and while it does feel like other games have stolen the spotlight as of late, there is still anticipation around the new season.


[Season 2](https://warfareengage.com/black-ops-7-mid-season-2-update-details/) Reloaded is now here, and those diving into the game are going to want to know all of the new maps.


Here's what we know.


## Black Ops 7 Season 2 Reloaded: All New Maps


A new mid-season COD update means a ton of new content is about to be released for players, as we're once again in store for new weapons, maps and game modes.


The Reloaded this timea round will introduce the following maps:


#### *Torque*


*Brand-New, 6v6, 2v2 Small-Sized*


**


*Launch into a breakneck pursuit across this map reminiscent of The Battle of Los Angeles from Black Ops 2. Fight around abandoned vehicles while spotting Wall Jump paths over towering piles of wreckage and spiraling freeway sections jutting into the sky.*


**


*Storm the Acropolis National Bank for tight, close-quarters firefights, or slip along the slim ledge behind it to reach the tilted roadway, where overturned vehicles form platforms spanning a massive gap. In this warped, larger-than-life setting, the rules are out the window; stay sharp and be ready for anything.*


#### *Cliff Town*


*Remaster, 6v6, Medium-Sized*


**


*Drop into a dense urban Multiplayer experience, originally introduced in Black Ops 2 as “Yemen” and now re-envisioned as a cliffside town in Avalon that preserves the spirit of the original design. Anticipate a mix of tight alleyways, raised sightlines, and constricted chokepoints that favor both bold advances and smart tactical play.*


**


*Fight for every inch around the central crevasse and the buildings circling it, creating combat zones where multiple playstyles intersect, turning every rooftop duel and flanking maneuver into a standout moment.*


#### *Mission: Peak*


*Brand-New Skirmish Map, 20v20*


**


*Prepare for an all-new 20v20 Skirmish experience expanding Multiplayer with another large map clash. Battle to secure High Value Sectors scattered through winding medieval streets, all connected by a central, curving roadway, while keeping an eye out for snipers perched atop terracotta rooftops.*


**


*Nearly half of the map’s outer edge is surrounded by water, allowing for amphibious tactics with the help of Tactical Rafts, a compact harbor, and fishery warehouses set along the rocky shoreline beneath the main village. Down there, intense close-quarters fighting erupts from building to building, adding another layer to the intense action.*


#### *Grind*


*Returning from Black Ops 6, 6v6, Medium-Sized*


**


*Originally introduced in Black Ops 2, the iconic skatepark makes its comeback in the Mid-Season update. Engage enemies inside the park itself and the adjacent pro shop or move outside to the sprawling skate bowl and towering halfpipe, where long sightlines and layered verticality keep the action flowing.*


*Leverage the ramps and shifting elevations to gain the upper hand, string together smooth eliminations, and create epic plays, especially with the added Wall Jump routes that open new paths across spaces you’ve long had to run through.*


#### *Firing Range*


*Returning from Black Ops 6, 6v6, Small-Sized*


**


*The fan-favorite Firing Range makes its return in its Black Ops 6 iteration, joining the Black Ops 7 map rotation with Wall Jumps introduced to the layout for the first time. Intense crossfire dominates the central Road where Operators compete to lock down critical sightlines like the Tower and the upper floors of the Shoot House and Garage.*


*Behind the Office and in other spots around the map, moving targets slide along their rails, drawing attention as you seek out your next fight. If you prefer a more indirect route, veer away from the middle and take the Dirt Road instead, keeping your guard up for surprise attacks as you move past the Storage building.*
