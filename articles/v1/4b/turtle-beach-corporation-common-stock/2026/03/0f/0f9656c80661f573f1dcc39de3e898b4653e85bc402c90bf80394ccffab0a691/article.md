# WoW Midnight: When Does Season 1 Begin? Answered

World of Warcraft Midnight has now arrived for players globally, and the next iteration of the classic MMORPG has been a blast thus far, with sprawling new zones to explore.


With the DLC underway, we're now looking ahead into when Season 1 will arrive, which will bring a new Raid, Mythic+ pool and much more.


Here's what we know.


## WoW Midnight: When Does Season 1 Begin? Answered


WoW Midnight has reinvented the game in many ways, as the class pruning has been live for nearly two months now, among other changes.


The kick-off to the end-game content for Midnight will begin with the launch of Season 1, as players are eagerly waiting for the new raid, The Voidspire.


Regarding when Season 1 will be released, Blizzard has confirmed that all of the Season 1 content will become playable on March 17th, 2026.
