# Black Ops 7 Season 2 Reloaded: All New Events Coming

Black Ops 7 has been progressing smoothly since it was released, and while it does feel like other games have stolen the spotlight as of late, there is still anticipation around the new season.


Now, with the new Reloaded update here, we're going to run over all of the new events coming.


Here's all we know.


## Black Ops 7 Season 2 Reloaded: All New Events Coming


A new COD season means a ton of new content is about to be released for players, as we're once again in store for new weapons, maps and game modes.


Much is the case with the mid-season/Reloaded updates, as this extra boost of content will serve us nicely until Season 3.


However, as of now, the following events will be coming to BO7.


#### Event: Altitude Tactics (CM, MP, ZM, WZ)


*Rewards Include: Voyak KT-3 Assault Rifle*


Get hyped for the arrival of Black Ops Royale with the Altitude Tactics Event. Progress through all four game modes to earn new Mid-Season rewards including the new **** Voyak KT-3 Assault Rifle plus the “Rainfall” Weapon Camo, “Pinnacle” Weapon Reticle, “Pinpoint” Calling Card, “Gliders” Emblem, and other altitude-themed items showing you’re ready for the next drop.


#### Event: Counter Skies Event Pass (CM, MP, ZM, WZ)


*Rewards Include: Swordfish A1 Marksman Rifle, Echo 12 Backlash Launcher Kit*


Amass XP across all four game modes to progress and unlock key items like the Swordfish A1 Marksman Rifle and Echo 12 Backlash Launcher Kit in the Free Track or purchase access to the Paid Track for additional rewards like the “Waylay” DS20 Mirage Assault Rifle and “Field Hunt” RK-9 SMG Weapon Blueprints.


**Free Track**


Across all modes, acquire the following rewards for reaching XP milestones:


- One hour of 2WXP
- “Fired Up” Weapon Camo (Universal)
- “Airways” Weapon Charm
- “Guild Rep” Sticker
- “Bot Brigade” Loading Screen
- “Barbed” Emblem
- “Swordfish A1” Marksman Rifle (Base)
- “Echo 12 Backlash Launcher” Attachment
- “Aeronaut” Operator Skin
- “Command Op” Operator Skin


**Rule the Skies with the Premium Reward Track**


Purchase the Premium Track to progress along a second track of rewards featuring the following Premium Rewards earned alongside the Free Track:


- “Walk it off” Gunscreen
- “Maniacal” Weapon Sticker
- “Overpowered” Animated Calling Card
- “Drone Out” Emote
- “Loot Grab” Finishing Mode
- “Field Hunt” RK-9 SMG Weapon Blueprint
- “Bypass” Weapon Blueprint for the Ballistic Knife
- “Hedge” Operator Skin
- “Underbrush” Operator Skin
- “Waylay” DS20 Mirage Assault Rifle Weapon Blueprint


#### Event: Paradox Junction Event (ZM)


*Rewards Include: Weapon Camos, GobbleGums*


Show that you’ve got what it takes to beat the competition in the Paradox Junction Event, offering an assortment of GobbleGum rewards to ease the burden of your subsequent Zombies deployments. Keep climbing to earn additional rewards like the “Outreach” Weapon Camo, the “Citizens” Animated Weapon Camo, and more.
