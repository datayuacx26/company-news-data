Deadlock has been Valve's worst-kept secret since the closed beta became public during the Summer of 2024.

## Is Deadlock Still Invite-Only? Answered


Deadlock is the latest game from Valve, and much like their other titles, this one is diving into a category they aren't specifically known for, which is MOBAs.


With six new heroes slated to come out in the next couple of weeks, players are vying to get into the action, as we can confirm that yes, Deadlock is still strictly an invite-only closed beta.


To get invited to play Deadlock, you'll need to have someone on your friends page send you an invite from the game menu itself.
