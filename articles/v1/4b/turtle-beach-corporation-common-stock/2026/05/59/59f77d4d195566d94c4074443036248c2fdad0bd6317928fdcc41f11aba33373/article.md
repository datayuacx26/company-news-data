# Why Does My Audio Keep Cutting Out in Games? Fixes for Mic & Sound Issues

You are mid-callout, pinging an enemy position, and your mic dies. Or game audio dips just long enough to miss the footstep that gets you killed.


It is almost never a broken headset. A software conflict, a loose connection, or a wrong setting is the culprit.


## Check Your Connection First


For wired headsets, unplug and replug the 3.5mm or USB cable. Check for fraying near the connector. For[wireless headsets](https://www.turtlebeach.com/collections/headsets) , make sure the USB transmitter is seated tightly and away from other USB devices. Bluetooth users should move closer. Walls and distance cause dropouts fast.


If you are on a[Stealth 700 Gen 3](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/stealth-700-gen-3-headset?Platform=Xbox&Color=Black) , check that the correct transmitter is active. It ships with two. On Xbox, use the one labeled XBOX. A solid green LED means active. Amber means standby. For the[Stealth 600 Gen 3](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/stealth-600-headset?Platform=Xbox&Color=Purple) , set the transmitter to XBOX mode before plugging in.


## How to Fix Audio Cutting Out


### Discord


If your Discord mic keeps cutting out but works elsewhere, the fix is inside Discord. Toggle between Voice Activity and Push-to-Talk, then switch back. Disable Krisp noise suppression in Voice & Video settings. Turn off "Automatically determine input sensitivity" and set the slider manually. Confirm Discord is using your headset mic.


Open Windows Sound settings, find your mic's Properties, go to the Advanced tab, and uncheck "Allow applications to take exclusive control." Discord and your game fight over audio access. This is usually why. Our[wireless headset PC and console compatibility](https://www.turtlebeach.com/blog/wireless-headset-pc-console-compatibility) guide covers this further.


Turtle Beach users should also open the Swarm™ II app. Adjust the Sensitivity slider and lower the Noise Gate in the MICROPHONE section if your voice sounds choppy.


### PC


Close background apps using your mic. Update audio drivers through Device Manager. Disable audio enhancements in your playback device's Properties. Set sample rate to 16-bit, 48000 Hz.


On a Stealth 700 Gen 3 or Stealth 600 Gen 3, verify the transmitter shows up in Device Manager under "Audio inputs and outputs" and "Sound, video and game controllers." If not, try a different USB port. If sound goes hollow when Discord opens, your motherboard's audio software (Realtek, Nahimic) may have AI noise cancellation clashing with Discord. Disable it.


### Console


On PS5, go to Settings > Sound > Audio Output for your[PS5 headset](https://www.turtlebeach.com/collections/playstation-headsets) . Confirm the right output device is selected. Disable 3D Audio temporarily. Update controller firmware via USB.


On Xbox, go to Settings > General > Volume & Audio Output for your usual or[Xbox headset](https://www.turtlebeach.com/collections/xbox-headsets) . Set headset format to "Stereo Uncompressed." Update controller firmware through Devices & Connections.


Routing audio through a controller's 3.5mm jack on a low battery causes cutouts. A wireless headset bypasses the controller entirely. The Stealth 700 Gen 3 and Stealth 600 use dedicated 2.4 GHz wireless, so audio never passes through the controller's DAC. See[wired vs. wireless sound quality and latency](https://www.turtlebeach.com/blog/wired-vs-wireless-gaming-headphones-sound-quality-latency) for more.


If your Turtle Beach headset is unresponsive or stuck in a power loop, do a hard reset. On the Stealth 700 Gen 3, hold CROSSPLAY and MODE for 10 seconds. It restarts and re-pairs without erasing settings. Stealth 600 Gen 3 users switching devices should press QuickSwitch. Green LEDs mean wireless mode. Blue means Bluetooth.


## Conclusion


Audio dropping mid-game almost always traces back to a software conflict, a Discord setting, or a bad connection. Disable exclusive mode. Kill noise suppression. Update drivers. Check transmitters.

## FAQs


### Why does my mic cut out only on Discord but not in-game chat?


Discord's noise suppression, input sensitivity, or exclusive mode is likely interfering. Disable Krisp in Voice & Video settings. Turn off automatic input sensitivity and set the slider manually. In Windows Sound settings, uncheck "Allow applications to take exclusive control" under your mic's Advanced tab. Turtle Beach users should also check the Noise Gate in the Swarm™ II app.


### How do I fix audio cutting out between the game and Discord?


This usually happens because both apps are fighting over audio device control. Open Windows Sound settings, right-click your mic, go to Properties, then the Advanced tab. Uncheck "Allow applications to take exclusive control" for both your mic and speakers. Make sure Discord and your game are set to the same output device.


### Is USB or 3.5mm more stable for gaming headsets?


A USB[PC gaming headset](https://www.turtlebeach.com/collections/pc-headsets) is generally more stable. A USB headset processes audio through its own built-in sound card, while a 3.5mm headset relies on the device's DAC. That extra layer introduces more room for interference and quality loss, especially on a PC. If you are getting consistent cutouts on 3.5mm, switching to a USB or wireless connection often solves it.


### Why does my Turtle Beach headset mic not work on Xbox?


First, check that the correct transmitter is connected. The Stealth 700 Gen 3 ships with two, and the one labeled XBOX must be plugged in. A solid green LED means active and unmuted. If the LED is amber, the transmitter is on standby. Also, confirm the mic is flipped forward into the unmuted position.


### Why does my microphone keep cutting out mid-sentence?


Your input sensitivity is likely set too high, causing quieter syllables to fall below the detection threshold. In Discord or your voice app, turn off automatic sensitivity and drag the slider down until your normal speaking voice registers consistently. On Turtle Beach headsets, open the Swarm™ II app and lower the Noise Gate in the MICROPHONE section. A high Noise Gate chops off the softer parts of your speech.
