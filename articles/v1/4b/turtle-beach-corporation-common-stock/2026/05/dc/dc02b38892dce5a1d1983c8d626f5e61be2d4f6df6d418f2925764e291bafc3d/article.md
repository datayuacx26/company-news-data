# Best Fighting Game Controllers

Picking the right controller for fighting games can genuinely improve your play. Whether you prefer a traditional gamepad, a fight stick, or a leverless controller, the best choice depends on the games you play and how you like to input commands.


Here is a breakdown of every controller type for fighting games and what works best.


## Types of Controllers for Fighting Games


Fighting game players use four main controller types, each with distinct strengths.


### Standard Gamepads


The controller you already own. Gamepads work well for fighting games, especially if you are comfortable with D-pad inputs. Many top-ranked players compete on standard controllers.


Best for:


Beginners, casual players, and anyone who already has muscle memory on a gamepad.


### Arcade Fight Sticks


A joystick with large arcade-style buttons. Fight sticks replicate the arcade cabinet experience and give you large buttons that are easy to hit accurately during complex combos.


Best for:


Players with arcade experience, 2D fighters, and anyone who prefers the feel of stick inputs. If you are weighing up your options, this breakdown of


[leverless vs traditional fight sticks](https://www.turtlebeach.com/blog/leverless-vs-traditional-fight-sticks-competitive-play) covers the key differences.


### Leverless Controllers


Directional buttons replace the joystick for movement inputs. Leverless controllers offer faster, more precise directional input and are gaining popularity rapidly in the fighting game community.


Best for:


Competitive players who want maximum input speed and precision.


### Fight Pads


Gamepads specifically designed for fighting games with enhanced D-pads, six-button face layouts, and better ergonomics for fighting game inputs.


Best for:


Players who prefer a pad but want a layout more suited to fighting game inputs.

## Is a Fight Stick Better Than a Controller for Fighting Games?


Neither is objectively better. What matters is your comfort and muscle memory.


Fight Stick Advantages


- Larger buttons are easier to press accurately for multi-button inputs


- Joystick movement feels natural if you have arcade experience


- More satisfying tactile feedback


Controller Advantages


- No extra purchase needed


- Easier to learn for newcomers


- More portable


Leverless Advantages


- Fastest directional input speed


- Most precise movement control


- Eliminates accidental diagonal inputs


Many pro players compete at the highest level on standard controllers. The "best" input device is whichever one you practice with consistently.


## What to Look for in a Fighting Game Controller


### Input Latency


Lower latency means your inputs register faster. For competitive play, wired connections offer the lowest latency. The Victrix Pro KO uses a 3m braided USB-C cable for lightning-fast response times, while the Victrix Pro BFG Reloaded lets you switch between wireless and wired modes depending on whether you are practicing at home or competing in a tournament.


### Button Quality


Quality switches feel crisp and responsive. Cheap, mushy buttons make precise inputs harder and wear out faster. The Victrix Pro KO ships with Cherry MX Speed Silver RGB switches that can be hot-swapped for your preferred switch type, and the Stealth Ultra uses tactile microswitches on face buttons and the D-pad that last up to five times longer than standard buttons.


### Build Quality


A heavy, solid controller stays put during intense matches. Lightweight controllers slide around on your lap or desk.


### Platform Compatibility


Make sure the controller works on your platform. Some fight sticks are PS5-only or PC-only. The Victrix Pro KO is available in both PlayStation and Xbox versions, with PC support on each. The Victrix Pro BFG Reloaded works across PS5, PS4, and PC with a simple mode switch.

## FAQs


#### **What type of controller is best for fighting games?**


No single type is universally best. Gamepads, fight sticks, and leverless controllers all work well. Choose based on your comfort and play style.


#### **Is a fight stick better than a controller for fighting games?**


Not objectively. Fight sticks offer an arcade feel and large buttons. Controllers are more portable and familiar. Both are viable at every competitive level.


#### **What is the best controller for fighting games on PS5?**


The DualSense Edge, premium third-party gamepads like the Victrix Pro BFG Reloaded with its six-button fightpad module, and leverless controllers like the Victrix Pro KO all perform well on PS5.


#### **Are leverless controllers better than arcade sticks?**


Leverless controllers offer faster directional inputs. Arcade sticks offer a familiar joystick feel. Both are tournament-legal and competitive.


#### **What is the best budget controller for fighting games?**


A standard PS5 or Xbox controller with a good D-pad works well. The Rematch Core wired controller offers a reliable budget option with dual rumble motors, impulse triggers, and an ergonomic shape at a low price point.


#### **Can you use any controller for fighting games on PC?**


Most controllers work on a PC through USB or Bluetooth. Xbox controllers have native Windows support. Third-party controllers may need a compatibility check.
