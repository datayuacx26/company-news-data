# Does The Stealth Pro II Work On PS5?

Our Premium headset, the Stealth Pro II, is making a big impact on the standards for gaming headsets. No matter what platform you're playing on, everyone can benefit from owning one. One question many are asking: Does the Stealth Pro II work on PS5?


Xbox and PC players are happy to see the next headset in the Stealth Pro line cover their respective platforms, but does it stretch to other platforms, including PlayStation ®?


Here's what you need to know.


## Does The Stealth Pro II Work On PS5?


The simple answer is yes, the Stealth Pro II does work on PlayStation 5 and even PlayStation 4.


The Stealth Pro II is an extremely versatile headset thanks to its Crossplay 2.0 feature, which allows you to wirelessly switch between up to 4 different audio sources.


So if you own and use an Xbox Series X/S, PC, handheld devices and even PS5, you can quickly switch between them all with just one headset. *(Note: 2 USB transmitters come out of the box, and you can purchase additional transmitters to get to 4).*


You can order either the[Xbox-licensed version or PC version of the Stealth Pro II](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/stealth-pro-ii-headset) , and both will work on the PlayStation 5 via wireless transmitter or Bluetooth.
