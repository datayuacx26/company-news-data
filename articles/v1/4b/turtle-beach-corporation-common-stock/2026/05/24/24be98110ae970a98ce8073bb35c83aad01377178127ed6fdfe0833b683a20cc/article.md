# How to Connect a Wireless Xbox Controller (Sync Guide for All Consoles)

Syncing a wireless Xbox controller takes about 10 seconds. Press the Pair button on your console, hold the Pair button on the controller, and they connect.


Whether you just unboxed a new controller, picked up a spare for local multiplayer, or need to re-pair one that keeps flashing and refusing to connect, the process is the same across every Xbox generation.


Here is the full breakdown for every Xbox console, plus what to do when pairing stops cooperating.


## How to Sync an Xbox Controller to Xbox Series X or S


If you are setting up a controller for the first time on a Series X or S, the whole thing takes three steps.


### First-Time Pairing in 3 Steps


1.


Turn on your Xbox by pressing the Xbox button on the console.


2.


Press the Pair button on the console. On both the Xbox Series X and Series S, the Pair button is the small circular button on the front of the console, near the USB port.


3.


Press and hold the Pair button on the controller. It is the small round button on the top edge, near the USB-C port. Hold it until the Xbox button starts flashing rapidly.


Wait a few seconds. The Xbox button stops flashing and stays lit when connected. You have 20 seconds after pressing the console's Pair button to start pairing.


## How to Sync an Xbox Controller to Xbox One


The process is identical, but the Pair button location varies by model.


-


Xbox One (original): The Pair button is on the left side, near the disc drive.


-


Xbox One S and One X: The Pair button is on the front, near the USB port.


Press the console's Pair button, then hold the controller's Pair button until it connects. Keep in mind that Xbox One controllers use micro-USB, not USB-C, so grab the right cable if you need a wired backup.


## How to Re-pair a Controller That Lost Its Connection


If your controller was previously paired to a different device, it may not auto-connect to your current Xbox.


1.


Turn off the controller by holding the Xbox button for about 6 seconds.


2.


Turn it back on.


3.


If it does not reconnect, go through the pairing steps above.


4.


If it still will not pair, connect via USB cable (USB-C for Series X|S, micro-USB for Xbox One). A wired connection forces a re-pair. Unplug after, and it stays paired wirelessly.


## How Many Controllers Can Connect at Once?


Xbox consoles support up to 8[wireless controllers](https://www.turtlebeach.com/collections/controllers) simultaneously. For the[best couch gaming setup](https://www.turtlebeach.com/blog/ultimate-couch-gaming-setup-wireless-controllers-with-30-foot-range) , make sure each controller has fresh batteries or a full charge.


## Troubleshooting: Controller Won't Connect


If your controller refuses to sync after following the steps above, most pairing issues come down to a handful of causes.


### Common Fixes When Pairing Fails


Check the batteries. Dead or low batteries are the most common culprit.


Move closer to the console. Xbox wireless controllers have a range of roughly 19 to 28 feet, depending on your setup.


Remove other paired controllers. If 8 controllers are already connected, the console will not pair a new one.


Hard reset the console. Hold the power button for 10 seconds to force a full shutdown, then restart.


Update the controller firmware. Connect via USB, go to Settings > Devices & Connections > Accessories, and update.


Try a USB cable. A wired connection works as both a fallback and a fix.


Those steps cover standard Xbox controllers. If you are using a Turtle Beach controller, the connection method is a bit different.


### Turtle Beach Controller Connection Tips


Turtle Beach[Xbox controllers](https://www.turtlebeach.com/collections/controllers) use their own transmitters or dongles instead of the console's Pair button.


### [Stealth Ultra](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/stealth-ultra-controller?Title=Default+Title)


Pairs through its included wireless transmitter, which plugs into the Charge Dock. Plug the USB-C end into the Dock, the USB-A end into the Xbox, and press the Guide button for 2 seconds to power on. Pairing starts automatically. If both LEDs are blinking white but not connecting, hold the transmitter's pairing button for 2 seconds until it blinks rapidly, then use the Connected Command Display: press "+" > Y > CONNECT > WIRELESS XBOX/PC > Y. If the controller will not power on at all, use a paper clip on the hidden reset button on the underside near P3, then power back on.


### [Victrix Pro BFG Reloaded](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/victrix-pro-bfg-reloaded-wireless-controller)


Uses a wireless USB dongle. Plug the dongle into your Xbox or PC, then press the Xbox Button on the controller. The LED ring and dongle LED go solid purple when paired. If both are blinking but not connecting, use a paperclip on the internal connect button on the side of the dongle until the LED blinks rapidly, then hold the Profile button on the controller until both LEDs go solid purple. If unresponsive, hold the Xbox Button for 12 seconds to force a hard reset.


### [Stealth Pivot](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/stealth-pivot-controller?Title=Default+Title)


Connects to Xbox via wired USB-C only. Plug the USB-C to USB-A cable into the controller and console, then hold the power button for 2 seconds.


## Can You Connect an Xbox Controller to More Than One Device?


An Xbox wireless controller can only connect to one device at a time. Pairing to a new device breaks the previous connection. Controllers like the Stealth Ultra support[quick profile switching](https://www.turtlebeach.com/blog/5-stop-hall-effect-clutch-triggers-hair-trigger-mode) through the Connected Command Display.

## FAQs


### How do I sync a new Xbox wireless controller for the first time?


Press the Pair button on the console (the small circular button near the USB port on the front), then press and hold the Pair button on the controller (top edge, near the USB-C port) until the Xbox button flashes rapidly. The controller connects within a few seconds, and the Xbox button stays lit once paired. You have a 20-second window after pressing the console's Pair button to start the controller pairing.


### Why won't my Xbox wireless controller connect to my console?


Start with the basics: check the batteries, move closer to the console, and make sure fewer than 8 controllers are already connected. If that does not work, hard reset the console by holding the power button for 10 seconds, then restart and try pairing again. Plugging in a USB cable (USB-C for Series X|S, micro-USB for Xbox One) forces a connection and often resolves the issue. You can also update the controller firmware through Settings > Devices & Connections > Accessories.


### How do I re-pair an Xbox controller that lost its connection?


Turn the controller off by holding the Xbox button for about 6 seconds, then turn it back on. If it does not auto-connect, use the Pair buttons on both the console and controller to manually re-pair. If wireless pairing still fails, plug the controller into the console with a USB cable to force the connection, then unplug once paired.


### Can I connect an Xbox wireless controller to more than one device?


An Xbox controller can be paired to multiple devices, but it only actively connects to one at a time. Pairing it to a new console or PC automatically breaks the connection with the previous device. You will need to go through the pairing steps again each time you switch between devices.


### How many wireless controllers can connect to one Xbox?


Xbox consoles support up to 8 wireless controllers connected at the same time. Each controller syncs to a different player slot using the same Pair button process. If you already have 8 connected, you will need to disconnect one before a new controller can pair.


### How do Turtle Beach controllers connect to Xbox?


The Stealth Ultra and Victrix Pro BFG Reloaded use their own included wireless transmitters or USB dongles instead of the console's Pair button, so the pairing process is different from a standard Xbox controller. The Stealth Pivot connects to Xbox via a wired USB-C cable only. Check the Turtle Beach Controller Connection Tips section above for step-by-step setup and troubleshooting for each model
