## Back Paddles vs. Back Buttons


Back paddles and buttons sit on your controller's rear where your middle or ring fingers naturally rest, providing extra inputs without lifting thumbs from analog sticks.


Back buttons are positioned where fingers naturally rest, requiring zero repositioning. The Stealth Ultra features four mappable microswitch back buttons rated at 5 million clicks, lasting up to 5x longer than standard buttons.


Back paddles typically sit underneath and require more reach, potentially causing hand fatigue. Turtle Beach's button design eliminates this issue by placing controls in comfortable, accessible positions.


Both options support remapping, letting you customize button assignments instantly for movement techniques in battle royales or combos in fighting games.

## Trigger Stops and Hair Trigger Technology


Trigger stops reduce how far you need to pull triggers to register input. In competitive shooters, shaving fractions of seconds per shot creates significant advantages.


The


[Stealth Ultra's adjustable trigger stops](https://www.turtlebeach.com/blog/5-stop-hall-effect-clutch-triggers-hair-trigger-mode) let you modify throw distance on-the-fly. Hair trigger modes offer near-instant activation where minimal pressure registers as full input. For fast time-to-kill games like Call of Duty, instant trigger response means the difference between victory and defeat.

## FAQs


#### What are back paddles on gaming controllers?


Back paddles are additional buttons on the controller's rear, pressed by your middle or ring fingers. You can remap them to any function, letting you jump or reload without moving thumbs from analog sticks.


#### Do custom controllers improve gaming performance?


Custom controllers provide measurable competitive advantages by reducing input delay, improving ergonomics, and maintaining thumb placement during critical moments. Back buttons let you access functions while keeping your thumbs on the analog sticks.


#### Are controller trigger stops worth it?


Trigger stops are essential for competitive shooters. Reducing trigger travel lets you fire semi-automatic weapons significantly faster. Adjustable stops let you toggle full travel for racing games.


#### How much do custom controller features cost?


Controllers with built-in custom features typically range from $150 to $250. The investment pays off through improved performance, durability, and integrated convenience rather than buying separate accessories.
