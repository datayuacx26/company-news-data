#### How does wireless latency affect gaming performance?


Latency below 20ms is imperceptible and suitable for all gaming. Turtle Beach wireless headsets achieve 12-15ms latency, matching wired performance while providing movement freedom.


#### Can wireless gaming headsets match wired audio quality?


Yes, current Turtle Beach wireless headsets match or exceed wired quality through high-resolution audio support and lossless wireless transmission. Premium models like the Stealth Pro eliminate battery concerns.


#### What battery life should I expect?


Entry-level models offer 30+ hours, while the Stealth 700 Gen 3 provides up to 80 hours. The Stealth Pro's swappable system eliminates battery limitations.


#### Do these headsets work across multiple platforms?


Most Turtle Beach wireless headsets work across PC, PlayStation, Xbox, and mobile using proprietary dongles. CrossPlay technology enables seamless platform switching.


#### Is spatial audio worth it for gaming?


Spatial audio significantly enhances immersion and competitive performance. Turtle Beach's Superhuman Hearing helps locate enemy footsteps and environmental sounds with precision.
