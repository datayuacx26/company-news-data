### Turtle Beach Corporation to Report Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results on Thursday, August 6, 2026


July 23, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/173492113/files/doc_news/Turtle-Beach-Corporation-to-Report-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results-on-Thursday-August-6-2026-2026.pdf)


SAN DIEGO, July 23, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Turtle Beach Corporation](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=d0VconAgolswxlCC74wjqaJjCdvy_iN6wzFQk8F_Le9S3DW-gpU2CoE0E8dvTx5WqoEEyBKsm8ri9uHmHQAAuleW5AIYT3gUGUZw6tduEeE=) (Nasdaq: TBCH) a leading gaming accessories brand, today announced it will report financial results for the second quarter 2026 on Thursday, August 6, 2026, after the close of trading on the Nasdaq Stock Market.


The Company will host a conference call and audio webcast at 4:30p.m. ET / 1:30p.m. PT that same day to review the results. The call will be hosted by Cris Keirn, Chief Executive Officer, and Andrew Clipsham, Interim Chief Financial Officer.


**Conference Call Information**
The live webcast of the call will be available on the “Events & Presentations” page of the Company’s website at[corp.turtlebeach.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=N25d66OXh7t8GS-InkqEnLkubl2jX9a-ztesmO8SFeASJd4ZrAOvU3pmZBV1wMPYV9oat1amisDhXryJGVB65s89vbeEn4KUOVpOFd9bLsNgebd81nmb9snGjjYyJErUbqGVa4wKhbqzijwRZ1v84A==) . Interested individuals may also join by dialing 1-877-407-0792 or 1-201-689-8263. To avoid delays, participants are encouraged to dial into the conference call 15-minutes ahead of the scheduled start time.


A telephone replay of the call will be available through August 20, 2026, and can be accessed by dialing 1-844-512-2921 or 1-412-317-6671 and entering passcode 13761399. A replay of the webcast will also be available on the investor relations website for a limited time.
