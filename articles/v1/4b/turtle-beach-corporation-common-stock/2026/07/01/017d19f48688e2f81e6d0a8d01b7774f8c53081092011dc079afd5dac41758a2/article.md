# Best Warzone Audio Settings: Hear Footsteps Before You're Seen

You know the feeling. You're holding a corner, confidence is high, and then you get beamed from behind by someone you never heard coming. Warzone's default audio buries footsteps under explosions, killstreaks, and ambient noise, and if you haven't touched the audio settings, you're basically playing with earplugs in.


Good news is a few targeted changes make enemy movement significantly easier to track without cranking your volume to migraine levels.


### TL;DR


Set your audio mix to Headphones Bass Boost or Sucker Punch, mute all music, max out Effects Volume, and boost mid-range EQ between 500 Hz and 2 kHz. On PS5, enable Audio Focus with Boost High Pitch. On PC, turn on Loudness Equalization. Pair all of that with a


[wireless gaming headset](https://www.turtlebeach.com/blog/should-i-stick-with-wired-or-try-wireless-gaming-headsets) that delivers clear mids, and you will hear enemies before they see you.

## What Are the Best In-Game Audio Settings for Warzone?


Use the Headphones Bass Boost or Sucker Punch audio mix, set Effects to 100%, drop all music to 0%, and turn Enhanced Headphone Mode off. Open Warzone's Settings menu and head to the Audio tab to dial these in.


### Audio Mix


Headphones, Bass Boost, or Sucker Punch deliver the best results for


[gaming headsets](https://www.turtlebeach.com/collections/headsets) . Sucker Punch compresses the dynamic range more aggressively, pushing quiet sounds like footsteps up while pulling loud sounds like gunshots down. Headphones Bass Boost gives a bit more weight to gunfire without crushing the footstep range.


Try both with your headset and pick whichever makes footsteps easiest to identify.


### Volume Settings


Master Volume at 70 to 85% is the sweet spot. Going higher makes explosions punishing without improving footstep clarity. Gameplay Music Volume and Cinematic Music Volume should both sit at 0%. Background music masks critical audio cues. Dialogue Volume between 40 and 60% keeps operator callouts useful without overpowering footsteps. Effects Volume stays at 100%. Footsteps, gunshots, vehicle sounds, and door interactions all live under this slider.


### Additional Audio Settings


Mute Licensed Music should be set to On, which removes lobby and in-game licensed tracks for a cleaner audio environment. Reduce Tinnitus Sound at On cuts the ringing after flashbangs so you hear footsteps sooner. Enhanced Headphone Mode should be Off. The free Universal profile can muffle certain sounds rather than enhance them.


## How Should You Set Up Audio on Each Platform?


### PS5


Enable


[Audio Focus](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/audio-focus-ps5/) in your PS5 system settings. Go to Settings, then Sound, then Volume, then Audio Focus, and toggle the feature on. Under Focus Type, select Boost High Pitch. High-pitch amplification targets the frequency range where footsteps and metallic sounds live, and many Warzone players report a noticeable improvement in footstep clarity with this preset active. Start with Weak or Medium strength, because Strong can make certain sounds uncomfortably sharp.


### Xbox


Xbox has no Audio Focus equivalent, so Dolby Atmos through the Dolby Access app is the closest option for boosting footstep clarity at the system level. Set your headset format to Stereo Uncompressed under Settings, then General, then Volume & Audio Output for the cleanest signal. If you run Dolby Atmos, make sure no other spatial audio layers are active at the same time. Adjust headset volume and game/chat balance through the Xbox Guide's Audio controls rather than relying only on in-game sliders.


### PC


Enable Loudness Equalization in Windows. Right-click the speaker icon, open Sound Settings, select your headset, go to Properties, then Enhancements (or Advanced on Windows 11), and check Loudness Equalization. Loudness EQ compresses loud sounds and boosts quiet ones, making footsteps stand out during chaotic firefights. Disable Windows Spatial Sound (Windows Sonic or Dolby Atmos) if Warzone's audio mix is already handling spatial processing. Stacking two spatial layers muddies directional audio rather than improving it.


## What EQ Settings Help Footsteps Cut Through?


Cut 60 to 250 Hz by 2 to 3 dB, boost 500 Hz to 2 kHz by 2 to 3 dB, and leave 4 kHz and above mostly flat. Most wireless headsets with companion software (like Turtle Beach's Swarm II app) let you dial these values in through a custom EQ profile.


The bass cut reduces rumble from explosions, vehicles, and ambient sounds that mask footsteps. The mid-range bump targets the frequency range where footstep impact sounds live, making them noticeably more audible. A small high-frequency boost can help with directional awareness, but too much creates harshness during long sessions.


Small, targeted EQ changes work better than dramatic boosts. Over-processing causes listening fatigue and can actually reduce your ability to pinpoint sounds accurately.

## What Audio Mistakes Should You Avoid in Warzone?


Running the master volume at 100% causes listening fatigue, making you less alert to quiet sounds over time. Leaving music on adds nothing to gameplay awareness, so set all music volumes to zero and Mute Licensed Music to On. Stacking multiple spatial audio layers muddies positional accuracy rather than improving it. Using the default headset EQ without adjustment means footstep frequencies get no special treatment.

## FAQs


### What are the best audio settings for Warzone?


Use the Headphones Bass Boost or Sucker Punch audio mix, set Gameplay Music Volume and Cinematic Music Volume to 0%, Effects to 100%, Mute Licensed Music to on, and Reduce Tinnitus Sound to on. On PC, enable Loudness Equalization in Windows Sound Settings for additional footstep clarity.


### What is the best audio mix setting in Warzone?


Sucker Punch compresses the dynamic range most aggressively, pushing quiet sounds like footsteps up and loud sounds down. Headphones Bass Boost is the alternative if you want gun audio to retain more weight. Try both with your headset, because the best choice depends on your specific hardware.


### How do I hear footsteps better in Warzone?


Set Gameplay Music Volume and Cinematic Music Volume to 0%, Effects to 100%, and use the Headphones Bass Boost or Sucker Punch audio mix. Boost mid-range EQ between 500 Hz and 2 kHz on your headset. On PS5, enable Audio Focus with Boost High Pitch under Settings, then Sound, then Volume.


### Is Home Theater or Headphones the best audio mix for Warzone?


Headphones are better for headset users. Home Theater is designed for speaker setups and spreads the soundstage in ways that dilute footstep clarity when listening through headphones. Sucker Punch and Headphones Bass Boost both outperform Home Theater for competitive headset play.


### Does Audio Focus on PS5 help in Warzone?


Yes. Audio Focus amplifies specific frequency ranges through four presets: Boost Low Pitch, Boost Voices, Boost High Pitch, and Boost Quiet Sounds. For footsteps, select Boost High Pitch. Start with Weak or Medium strength, as Strong can make metallic sounds and certain effects uncomfortably sharp.


### What audio settings do Warzone pros use?


Most pros run the Sucker Punch or Headphones Bass Boost audio mix, all music at 0%, Effects at max, and a mild mid-range EQ boost between 500 Hz and 2 kHz. PC players almost universally enable Loudness Equalization in Windows for additional dynamic range compression.
