**


SAN DIEGO – July 23, 2026 – Leading gaming accessories maker[Turtle Beach](https://corp.turtlebeach.com/)[Corporation](https://corp.turtlebeach.com/) (Nasdaq: TBCH), today revealed the all-new[VelocityOne™ Flightstick II](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/velocity-one-flight-stick-ii?Platform=Xbox) ***,***[VelocityOne Dual Throttle](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/velocity-one-dual-throttle?Title=Default+Title) ***,***[VelocityOne H.O.T.A.S.](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/velocity-one-universal-sim-mount?Title=Default+Title) for XBOX and PC, and[VelocityOne Universal Sim Mount](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/velocity-one-universal-sim-mount?Title=Default+Title) . Individually, the ***Flightstick II*** and ***Dual Throttle*** represent among the most advanced flight sim peripherals on the market today. Together, as the ***VelocityOne H.O.T.A.S*** . flight simulation system, they set a new standard for sim enthusiasts striving for richer realism and precision control across all of today's flight simulators.


The all-new ***VelocityOne Flightstick II*** features an ambidextrous design with next-gen 16-bit Hall-effect sensors for ultra-precise stick control, a real-time Flight Management display, and 38 programmable functions that meaningfully increase confidence during landings or dogfights no matter the conditions. The ***VelocityOne Dual Throttle*** delivers ultra-smooth, accurate control through dual levers with 10-bit resolution, 7-axes control in twin-engine or single-engine modes. Together they become the formidable ***VelocityOne H.O.T.A.S.*** system, built for any flight sim fan looking to go deeper — whether flying through clouds or to distant exoplanets.


“When we launched the VelocityOne family, our goal was to deliver greater realism, immersion, and enjoyment for sim fans,” said Cris Keirn, CEO, Turtle Beach. “The evolution of the ***VelocityOne Flightstick II*** and ***VelocityOne Dual Throttle*** ** builds on that vision with major advancements in performance, precision, and accessibility, giving more players the high-fidelity control today’s sim experiences demand.”


The ***Turtle Beach VelocityOne Flightstick II*** , has two models, one built for XBOX and Windows PC and one just for Windows PC. Both feature 16-bit Hall-effect sensors with over 65,000 input counts per axis that deliver smooth, precise control. With 38 programmable inputs, an integrated control dial, and versatile analog stick controls, essential commands are always within reach. The ambidextrous design with adjustable spring response ensures lasting comfort. A full-color display provides real-time feedback and quick adjustments, while customizable RGB lighting enhances your setup. It starts on pre-order today at[www.turtlebeach.com](http://www.turtlebeach.com/) and participating retailers globally at an MSRP of $149.99/€139.99/£129.99 and launches Sep 21, 2026.


The ***Turtle Beach VelocityOne Dual Throttle*** works alongside any Flightstick on Windows PC, or connects directly to the ***VelocityOne Flightstick* II** on XBOX. It offers precision and immersive control through 10-bit Hall-effect sensors that deliver smooth, accurate input with over 1,000 input counts per axis. Dual levers with a lock support both twin- and single-engine aircraft, while swappable detents and adjustable tension customize the feel. Multiple analog controls provide flexibility across sims. The 35 programmable inputs, LED indicators, and RGB lighting, keeps sim fans focused and immersed. As an ideal part of a complete flight setup, the ***VelocityOne Dual Throttle*** is on pre-order now at[www.turtlebeach.com](http://www.turtlebeach.com/) and participating retailers globally at an MSRP of $149.99/€139.99/£129.99 and launches Sep 21, 2026.


The ***Turtle Beach VelocityOne H.O.T.A.S.*** flight simulation system combines the two new controllers into one package, delivering an affordable precision-engineered flight experience for both aspiring and seasoned XBOX and PC sim pilots. Combining ***Flightstick II*** with the ***Dual Throttle*** , this system is built to replicate authentic cockpit control across combat, commercial, and space flight simulations. High-resolution Hall-effect sensors provide smooth, accurate input, while extensive 73 programmable inputs keep essential functions within reach. Adjustable spring response, ambidextrous ergonomics, tactile throttle detents, real-time display, and customizable RGB lighting allow full personalization. Expandability with the ***VelocityOne Rudder Pedals*** and ***Universal*** ***Sim Mount*** makes this a scalable, professional-grade simulation solution. As an ideal foundation for a complete flight setup, the ***VelocityOne H.O.T.A.S.*** system is available for pre-order now at[www.turtlebeach.com](http://www.turtlebeach.com/) and participating retailers globally at an MSRP of $279.99/€249.99/£229.99 and launches Sep 21, 2026.


Also premiering and available for pre-order today is the ***VelocityOne Universal Sim Mount*** . This versatile clamp mount allows flight and racing peripherals, in the VelocityOne range and broad cross-brand compatibility, to be securely integrated into a sim setup, helping bring even more realistic simulation experiences to life. With 240mm of vertical adjustment, **** and broad compatibility it offers flexibility to fine-tune positioning for any setup. Whether it’s positioning the Dual Throttle or Flightstick II lower for a more authentic cockpit feel, placing the Multi-Shift within easy reach for fast gear changes, or mounting the KD3 in an elevated trucking-style position, the ***VelocityOne Universal Sim Mount*** makes it easy. It’s designed to adapt your setup for a more immersive simulation experience across flight, racing, farming, and trucking sims. Starting on pre-order today at[www.turtlebeach.com](http://www.turtlebeach.com/) and participating retailers globally at an MSRP of $59.99/€49.99/£39.99, it launches on September 21, 2026.


Below is the full list of ***VelocityOne Flightstick II*** features designed to deliver exceptional flight sim experiences:


******


**High-Precision Hall-Effect Axes**


16-bit Hall-effect sensors deliver over 65,000 input counts per axis for smooth, accurate control and precise movement in any simulation.


**38 Programmable Inputs**


38 mappable inputs, including 11 new inputs compared to ***VelocityOne Flightstick 1*** , provide quick access to essential commands without breaking immersion.


**Integrated Cockpit Control Dial**


Dual encoder dial with tri-position switch unlocks up to nine inputs for efficient control of navigation and onboard systems.


**Expanded Analog Controls**


Dual levers, precision thumbstick, and a multi-function wheel offer versatile input for throttle, camera, and system control.


**Ambidextrous Ergonomic Design**


Adjustable wrist and thumb rests with swappable inserts support left- and right-handed comfort during long sessions.


**Rudder Twist with Lock Option**


Integrated twist rudder control with lock option lets you switch easily between stick control and dedicated pedals (sold separately).


**Adjustable Stick Tension**


Three tension settings let you tailor resistance for realism, speed, or balanced control.


**Flight Management Display**


Flight Management Display provides real-time flight telemetry and seamless access to onboard systems, letting you monitor instruments, manage configurations, and make in-flight adjustments.


**RGB Lighting**


Customizable RGB lighting lets you match your setup to your cockpit or favorite sim. Adjust colors and effects directly on the display, or unlock deeper customization with the *VelocityOne Flight Hangar* app.


**XBOX and PC Compatibility**


Officially licensed for XBOX Series X|S and Windows 10/11 PCs with seamless plug-and-play performance.


**Expandable Simulation Ecosystem**


Compatible with throttle modules, rudder pedals, a second stick, and mounts to build a complete HOTAS or HOSAS setup.


**VelocityOne Flight Hangar app**


Adjust product performance such as dead zone adjustment, stick response, lighting adjustment, and aircraft-specific profiles using the *VelocityOne Flight Hangar* app available for XBOX and PC.


**Precision Hall-Effect Throttle Axes**


10-bit Hall-effect sensors deliver over 1,000 input counts per axis for smooth, accurate control. Fine inputs translate directly in-game for precise landings, combat, and throttle control.


Below is the full list of ***VelocityOne Dual Throttle*** features engineered for boosted flight sim experiences:


**Lockable Dual Throttle Control**


Dual levers simulate twin-engine control, with a lock for single-engine use. Switch easily between aircraft types without changing your setup.


**35 Programmable Inputs + 7 Axes**


7 axes and 35 mappable inputs and switches keep essential commands within reach, reducing the need to navigate menus mid-flight.


**Interchangeable Throttle Detents**


Swappable throttle detents provide tactile reference points for fighters, airliners, or space sims, including afterburner, idle, and reverse thrust.


**Adjustable Throttle Tension**


Fine-tune throttle resistance from light and responsive to firm and realistic for smooth, controlled flight.


**LED Throttle Bar Indicators**


For select flight simulation titles, LED throttle bar indicators display real-time thrust or sim-assigned data without breaking immersion (on PC only).


**RGB Lighting**


Customizable RGB lighting lets you match your setup to your cockpit or favorite sim. Unlock deeper customization with the *VelocityOne Flight Hangar* app.


**Multiplatform Compatibility**


Compatible with Windows PC via independent USB connection or XBOX when connected to the ***VelocityOne Flightstick II*** designed for XBOX.

High-grip rubber feet keep the throttle stable during desktop use, while pre-drilled mounting holes and included screws enable hard-mount installation in your own cockpit or seamless integration with the ***VelocityOne Universal Sim Mount*** .


**Expandable Hardware**

**VelocityOne Flight Hangar App**


Adjust product performance such as dead zone settings, lighting customization, and aircraft-specific profiles using the *VelocityOne Flight Hangar* app available for XBOX and PC.
