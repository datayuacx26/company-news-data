## How Do Wireless Headsets Connect to PS5?


PS5


[does not natively support standard Bluetooth audio](https://www.turtlebeach.com/blog/connect-wireless-headset-ps5) for game sound. Sony restricts Bluetooth to controllers and select accessories, so regular Bluetooth headphones will not pair as an audio device. You need a headset that connects through one of these methods:


USB wireless dongle (2.4GHz):


Plugs into one of the PS5's USB ports and delivers low-latency, high-quality audio. Every serious gaming headset uses this connection for good reason: near-zero delay, full mic support, and a rock-solid signal.


USB-C wireless dongle:


Same technology, connects to the front USB-C port instead.


Wired 3.5mm:


Plugs into the DualSense controller's headphone jack. Works in a pinch, but routes audio through the controller's basic DAC (digital-to-analog converter), which limits


[sound quality and reduces fidelity](https://www.turtlebeach.com/blog/wired-vs-wireless-gaming-headphones-sound-quality-latency) .


Bottom line: if you want wireless PS5 audio, get a headset that ships with a USB dongle. Bluetooth-only headsets will leave you with no game sound at all.

## What Should You Look for in a PS5 Wireless Headset?


Sound quality


comes first. PS5's Tempest 3D Audio works with any stereo headphones, but headsets with wider soundstages and clear mids make the spatial effect far more convincing. Balanced audio profiles beat extreme bass-heavy tuning for positional accuracy.


Mic quality


matters more than most people realize. Party chat is half the PS5 experience, and a noise-canceling boom mic ensures your squad hears you clearly without picking up background noise. For a deeper look at how connection type affects voice clarity, check out the


[differences between wired and wireless microphone performance](https://www.turtlebeach.com/blog/wireless-vs-wired-headset-microphone-quality) .


Battery life


separates good wireless headsets from great ones. Anything below 12 hours means you are charging mid-session. Mid-range and premium options now push 40 to 80 hours per charge, meaning weekly charging at most.


Comfort


is the sleeper stat. Weight and ear cushion material make or break long sessions. Memory foam cushions with breathable fabric keep your ears cooler than synthetic leather alternatives, especially during four-hour ranked grinds.


Dual connectivity


is the premium feature worth chasing. Some headsets support simultaneous 2.4GHz wireless and Bluetooth, so you can hear game audio from PS5 while taking phone calls or running Discord on your phone.

### What is the best budget wireless gaming headset for PS5?


The Stealth 500 offers 2.4GHz wireless plus Bluetooth 5.2, up to 40 hours of battery, and Superhuman Hearing® at $89.99. For even tighter budgets, the wired Recon 70 plugs directly into the DualSense controller at $39.99 and delivers solid sound quality.


### Are wireless headsets good for gaming on PS5?


Absolutely. Modern 2.4GHz wireless headsets offer latency low enough for competitive play, with audio quality that matches wired headsets at similar price points. The USB dongle connection ensures near-zero delay, full mic support, and a stable signal throughout your session.


### Can you connect a wireless headset to PS5 via Bluetooth?


PS5 does not support standard Bluetooth for game audio. Sony restricts the console's Bluetooth to controllers and specific accessories. You need a headset with a USB wireless dongle for PS5 compatibility, or a third-party Bluetooth adapter with higher latency as a workaround.


### Which wireless gaming headset has the best battery life for PS5?


The Stealth 600 and Stealth 700 Gen 3 both deliver up to 80 hours on a single charge. The Stealth Pro uses a dual swappable battery system with 12+ hours per battery for uninterrupted play across marathon sessions.
