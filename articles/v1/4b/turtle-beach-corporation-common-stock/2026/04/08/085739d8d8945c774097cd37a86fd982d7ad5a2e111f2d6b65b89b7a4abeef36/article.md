Slay The Spire 2 is the direct sequel to the beloved Slay The Spire game, which was originally released back in 2019 and still bolsters an 'overwhelmingly positive' review score on Steam.

## Slay The Spire 2: How Many Acts Are There? Answered


Slay The Spire has been one of the premier indie titles we've seen over the last couple of years, and with the rise in other indie games such as Mewgenics this year, we're amped for this one.


Slay The Spire is a unique roguelike, in the sense that it features a deck-building system that will grant players unique cards, abilites and so much more as they progress further into the game.


Progressing further will require the completion of Acts, and in Slay The Spire 2, there are currently 3 Acts, with many speculating that there will be more added in future patches.


One aspect to keep in mind is that the boss you face at the end of each Act will be different from your previous run.
