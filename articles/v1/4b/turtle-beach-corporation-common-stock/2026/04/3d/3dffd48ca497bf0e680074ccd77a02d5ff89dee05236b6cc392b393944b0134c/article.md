# All Playable Characters In Slay The Spire 2

Slay The Spire 2 is the direct sequel to the beloved Slay The Spire game, which was originally released back in 2019 and still bolsters an 'overwhelmingly positive' review score on Steam.


The sequel has also introduced some brand new playable characters, and we're going to run over all of them below.


Here's what we know.


## All Playable Characters In Slay The Spire 2


Slay The Spire has been one of the premier indie titles we've seen over the last couple of years, and with the rise in other indie games such as Mewgenics this year, we're amped for this one.


Slay The Spire 2 will offer 5 playable characters at the time of it's release, and we can speculate that there will be more later down the line.


- The Ironclad


- Starting Relic: Burning Blood


- Starting Cards: Strike x5, Defend x4, Bash


- Starting Health: 80


- The Silent


- Starting Relic: Ring of the Snake


- Starting Cards: Strike x5, Defend x5, Neutralize, Survivor


- Starting Health: 70


- Unlock Conditions: Play as The Ironclad.


- The Regent


- Starting Relic: Divine Right


- Starting Cards: Strike x4, Defend x4, Falling Star, Venerate


- Starting Health: 75


- Unlock Conditions: Play as the Silent.


- The Necrobinder


- Starting Relic: Bound Phylactery


- Starting Cards: Strike x4, Defend x4, Bodyguard, Unleash


- Starting Health: 66


- Unlock Conditions: Play as the Regent


- The Defect


- Starting Relic: Cracked Core


- Starting Cards: Strike x4, Defend x4, Zap, Dualcast


- Starting Health: 75


- Unlock Conditions: Play as the Necrobinder
