# When Does 2XKO Release On PlayStation 5 and Xbox? Answered

2XK0 is yet another title from Riot Games, and it'll be their first entry into another genre. This time around, they're going after fighting games.


While the game has been playable on PC since the Alpha/Beta, we're going to run over when the fighting game will become available on consoles.


Here's what we know.


## When Does 2XKO Release On PlayStation 5 and Xbox? Answered


Riot is no stranger to diving headfirst into unknown waters, as 2XK0 is aiming to join the likes of Street Fighter and Mortal Kombat as a prestige fighting game title.


2XKO is unique in the sense that they're going to be adding all of the LoL champions that we're all familiar with, and we can expect the roster to grow over time.


Regarding when 2XKO will be playable on both PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, Riot has now released the game onto these platforms with the release of Season 1.


Here's a little snippet of what[Riot had to say](https://2xko.riotgames.com/en-us/news/game-updates/2xko-patch-notes-1-1-1-jan-20-2026/) :


*2XKO is officially live on console in Season 1. You can play on Xbox Series X|S or PlayStation 5 starting January 20-21 (date varies by region). Account progress is shared across all platforms.*


*Some console-specific updates:*


- *2XKO Trophies (PlayStation 5) and Achievements (Xbox Series X|S) are now live.*
- *With PlayStation®Plus, you can now earn a special chroma, avatar item, and Battle Pass level skips.*


- *The chroma and avatar item can also be purchased for Credits via the in-game Collections tab.*
