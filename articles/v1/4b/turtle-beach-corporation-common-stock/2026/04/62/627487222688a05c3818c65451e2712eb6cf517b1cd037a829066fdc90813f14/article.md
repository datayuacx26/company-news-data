# Does Slay The Spire 2 Have A Campaign? Answered

Slay The Spire 2 is the direct sequel to the beloved Slay The Spire game, which was originally released back in 2019 and still bolsters an 'overwhelmingly positive' review score on Steam.


Now, with the second installment here, we're going to run over all we know about the game having a campaign for players.


Here's all we know.


Slay The Spire has been one of the premier indie titles we've seen over the last couple of years, and with the rise in other indie games such as Mewgenics this year, we're amped for this one.


Slay The Spire 2 will feature the iconic deck-building features once again, and the roguelike deck-builder has a ton of content in-game to check out.


However, in terms of offering an actual story, we can confirm that yes, Slay The Spire 2 does indeed have a story. But, you'll need to continue to advance in your runs to progress the main story, so this could take time depending on your playstyle.
