# Overwatch Developers Showcase Anran Visual Rework

Overwatch has now been fully rebranded, and Overwatch 2 is no more, and Blizzard is essentially resetting the clock on the game's life cycle.


New in Season 1 is the DPS hero, Anran, the sister of Wuyang, and there has been some discussion about her design and Blizzard has now officially announced some changes.


Here's all we know.


## Overwatch Developers Showcase Anran Visual Rework


Blizzard releasing five new heroes at the onset of Season 1 was something that no one expected, but here we are.


However, while much of the discussion was positive, the reception to Anran looking strikingly similar to other hero face models such as Kiriko, Juno and more, Blizzard has now decided that the new design will be made live soon.


Executive Director, Aaron Keller also noted the following:


> In Season 2, we’re introducing an updated look for Anran. She didn’t fully land as the fiery and fierce sister of Wuyang that we hoped she would. With this update, our team focused on strengthening her overall presence.
