Black Ops 7 has been progressing smoothly since it was released, and while it does feel like other games have stolen the spotlight as of late, there is still anticipation around the new season.


Now, Season 2 Reloaded is here, and we're going to run over all we know about unlocking the new Swordfish S1.


Here's all we know.


## Black Ops 7 Season 2 Reloaded: How To Unlock The Swordfish A1


A new COD season means a ton of new content is about to be released for players, as we're once again in store for new weapons, maps and game modes.


Weapons are a must each season, and during the Reloaded update, players will now be able to unlock the Swordfish A1, a brand new Assault Rifle to use.


Players will only be able to earn this weapon via a new event, and Treyarch has noted the following:


***Levels: 39***


***MAGS: 3***


***MAG SIZE: 30***


***Mastery Badges: Yes***


***Attachments:** Optic, Muzzle, Barrel, Underbarrel, Magazine, Rear Grip, Stock, Laser, Fire Mods *****


***Customize Options:** Skins, Camos and Reticle Unlocks (CM, MP, ZM, WZ), Accessories (1), Decals (1), Stickers (3). *****


*4-round burst marksman rifle. Fast cyclic fire rate and minimal recoil, but slower mobility and handling.*


*Line up your shot and let loose with a quad burst attack using the Swordfish A1 Marksman Rifle, a weapon offering unrivaled accuracy in its class. Inflict high damage by landing all four rounds, with a possible fifth shot added to each burst after unlocking the weapon’s Penta Burst Prestige Attachment. Using Gunsmith, build the Swordfish A1 with fast handling characteristics to bring the fight up close or opt for precision and distance for battles across larger territories.*
