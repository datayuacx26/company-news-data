## When Does Pokemon Champions Release? Answered


Pokémon Champions is one of the more anticipated Pokémon spin-off games to be released in quite some time, as it promises to bring high-intensity battles to wherever you are.


A game that is all about the actual Pokemon battles is something many of us have been asking for, and thankfully, we won't have to wait much longer.


Pokemon Champions will officially be released on April 8th, 2026 and will be playable on Nintendo Switch devices, alongside mobile ones.
