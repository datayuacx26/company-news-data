# How to Improve Your Aim in FPS Games: Mouse Settings, Sensitivity, and Gear Tips

Aim is the foundation of FPS dominance, and improving it isn't about talent. Good aim comes from the right settings, consistent practice, and gear that doesn't hold you back.


Here's how to dial in your aim on both PC and console.


****


## Find Your Ideal Mouse Sensitivity


Sensitivity is where everything starts. Too high and you overshoot targets. Too low and you can't react fast enough.


### How to Find the Right Sensitivity


Most pro players use 400-800 DPI with in-game sensitivity between 1.5-2.5. Start at medium sensitivity and test it. Place your crosshair on a fixed point, then strafe left and right. If your crosshair stays on target, you're close.


For PC, use 400-800 DPI, which is standard for FPS gaming. For the console, start at the default sensitivity and increase gradually.


### DPI vs In-Game Sensitivity


Your mouse DPI and in-game sensitivity multiply together to determine your actual aim speed. Pick a DPI (400 or 800 is standard) and only adjust in-game sensitivity from there.


## Get Your Mouse Settings Right


Turn off mouse acceleration in Windows (Mouse Settings > Additional Mouse Options > Pointer Options > uncheck "Enhance pointer precision"). Set your polling rate to 1000Hz for the smoothest tracking.


## Practice with Aim Trainers


Use dedicated aim training software for 15-20 minutes, warming up before gaming. Focus on tracking, flicking, and target switching drills that build muscle memory.

## Improve Your Aim on Console


Use the lowest deadzone possible without stick drift. Start at 0% and increase by 1% until the drift stops, then add one more percent. Most competitive players run 3-5% on the right stick.


Learn how your game's aim assist works and adjust sensitivity to complement it rather than fight it.

## Build Consistent Habits


Good aim is a habit, not a one-time adjustment.


- Warm up with aim training before competitive matches
- Keep your sensitivity the same across all games when possible
- Take breaks to avoid fatigue-related accuracy drops
- Review your gameplay to spot patterns in missed shots


Many high-level and professional FPS players set their mouse DPI and sensitivity settings to reach a cm/360 value of roughly 34 cm or greater for consistency across different games.

## FAQs


#### What is the best mouse sensitivity for improving aim in FPS games?


Most pro players use 400-800 DPI with in-game sensitivity between 1.5-2.5. Start in that range and adjust based on comfort and consistency.


#### How do you train your aim in FPS games on PC?


Use dedicated aim training software for 15-20 minutes before playing. Focus on tracking, flicking, and target switching drills.


#### Does your mouse or mousepad affect aim accuracy in FPS games?


Yes. A lightweight mouse with a quality sensor improves tracking accuracy. A large, consistent mousepad provides predictable mouse movement essential for low-sensitivity play.


#### What are the best controller settings for FPS games?


Set sensitivity in the range of 5-8, minimize dead zones to 3-5% on the right stick, and use a linear response curve for consistent tracking.


#### How do professional FPS players practice and improve their aim?


Pros warm up daily with aim trainers, maintain consistent sensitivity across games, and use cm/360 values of roughly 34 cm or greater for consistency.


#### Does a higher refresh rate monitor help with aim?


Yes, higher refresh rates (144Hz and above) show smoother motion that makes tracking moving targets easier compared to standard 60Hz monitors.
