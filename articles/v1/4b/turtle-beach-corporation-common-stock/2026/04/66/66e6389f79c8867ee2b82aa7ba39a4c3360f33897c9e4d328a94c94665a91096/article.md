Black Ops 7 has been progressing smoothly since it was released, and while it does feel like other games have stolen the spotlight as of late, there is still anticipation around the new season.


Now, Season 3 is here, and we're going to run over all of the new weapons that'll be coming for players to use.

## Black Ops 7 Season 3: All New Weapons


A new COD season means a ton of new content is about to be released for players, as we're once again in store for new weapons, maps and game modes.


Weapons are a must each season, and players will get to experience the following ones during the new season:


#### Primary Weapon: MK35 ISR (Launch)


*Assault Rifle, Battle Pass Page 3 HVT, Page 12 HVT Battle Pass Blueprint*


**Levels: 43**


**MAGS: 3**


**MAG SIZE: 30**


**Mastery Badges: Yes**


**Attachments:** Optic, Muzzle, Barrel, Underbarrel, Magazine, Rear Grip, Stock, Laser, Fire Mods


**Customize Options:** Blueprints, Camos and Reticle Unlocks (END, MP, ZM, WZ), Accessories (1), Decals (1), Stickers (3).


*Full-auto assault rifle. Low recoil for excellent accuracy with moderate damage and handling.*


Pepper foes with accurate fire using this Assault Rifle that sports the lowest recoil in its class. In combination with a fast fire rate, the weapon pumps out rounds while staying on target, remaining controllable even through sustained fire. Its clean iron sights are just the icing on the cake, allowing you to focus on other Attachments without giving up a clear view of the enemy.
