# Crimson Desert: How To Parry

Crimson Desert


is an open-world action-adventure game developed by Pearl Abyss


that follows mercenary Kliff as he battles rival factions and mythical threats.


Combat is the cornerstone of the game, and we're going to run over a vital mechanic and how you'll be able to accomplish this.


Here's how to parry in Crimson Desert.


## Crimson Desert: How To Parry


Pearl Abyss has expanded its reach and has released the long-awaited Crimson Desert, a sprawling open-world RPG with breathtaking graphics.


Just by the look of the game, many are going to assume that the game will take ample time to complete, and they're correct.


Parrying will be important for combat, as is the case in most RPGs, and you'll be able to do this in Crimson Desert by hitting the 'Guard' button, L1 on controllers will do the trick!


You'll want to time the parry, though, as doing so will allow Kliff to counter-attack rather quickly, which will be handy in most battles.
