# How To Self Revive In Fortnite

Fortnite Chapter 7 Season 1 has kicked off with perhaps Fortnite's biggest event of all time, and players are thrilled with all of the new changes.


Any new season brings a load of changes, and one of these is the new 'Self-Revive,' and we're going to run over how this works in Fortnite.


Here's what we know.


## How To Self-Revive In Fortnite


Fortnite is a game that has transcended over the years, and there are countless examples of it taking inspiration from other titles and twisting them to match Fortnite.


Self-Revive is the latest, and it is a feature that has been long requested, as it can be quite annoying to solely rely on your allies to revive you.


If players go down when this item is in their inventory, a prompt should pop up on their screen, indicating that they can revive themselves.


Epic has noted the following on this new item:


### *New Self-Revive Device*


*If a **Self-Revive Device** is in your inventory, you can revive yourself while Down But Not Out (DBNO).*


**


### *Knocked? Don’t Stop*


*New DBNO movement options have been added across Fortnite Battle Royale:*


- *● **Roll & Tumble** : Roll to dodge fire or throw yourself down a slope to tumble away from danger.*
- *● **DBNO Sprint** : Move faster while downed by using Energy.*
- *● **Stay on the Move** : While DBNO, you can now use ziplines and ascenders, and even hop into a car’s passenger seat!*
- *● **Reboot Revive** : Crawl, or be thrown, into the back of a Reboot Van to be revived while knocked down*
