# Lamb Weston Announces Inducement Award Under NYSE Listing Rule 303A.08

EAGLE, Idaho--(BUSINESS WIRE)--May 11, 2026-- Lamb Weston Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: LW) announced today that on May 11 **,** 2026, the company granted 15,096 **** restricted stock units (the “Inducement Awards”) to James D. Gray. The company’s Compensation and Human Capital Committee approved the grant of Inducement Awards, made under the Lamb Weston Holdings, Inc. 2026 Inducement Stock Plan, to Mr. Gray as a material inducement to Mr. Gray’s hiring as Chief Financial Officer on April 2, 2026. The restricted stock units were granted as a dollar-for-dollar match on Mr. Gray’s personal investment in Lamb Weston shares and vest 33%, 33% and 34% on May 11, 2027, May 9, 2028, and May 8, 2029, respectively.


The Inducement Awards were granted in reliance on the employment inducement exemption under the NYSE’s Listed Company Manual Rule 303A.08, which requires public announcement of inducement awards.
