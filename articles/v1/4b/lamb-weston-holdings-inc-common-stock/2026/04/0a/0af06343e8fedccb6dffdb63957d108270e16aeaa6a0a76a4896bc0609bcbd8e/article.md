# Lamb Weston Announces Inducement Award Under NYSE Listing Rule 303A.08

EAGLE, Idaho--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Apr. 10, 2026-- Lamb Weston Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: LW) announced today that on April 10, 2026, the company granted 37,923 restricted stock units (the “Inducement Awards”) to James D. Gray. The company’s Compensation and Human Capital Committee approved the grant of Inducement Awards, made under the Lamb Weston Holdings, Inc. 2026 Inducement Stock Plan, to Mr. Gray as a material inducement to Mr. Gray’s hiring as Chief Financial Officer on April 2, 2026. The restricted stock units vest 50% on each of April 13, 2027 and April 11, 2028.


The Inducement Awards were granted in reliance on the employment inducement exemption under the NYSE’s Listed Company Manual Rule 303A.08, which requires public announcement of inducement awards. The company is issuing this press release pursuant to Rule 303A.08.
