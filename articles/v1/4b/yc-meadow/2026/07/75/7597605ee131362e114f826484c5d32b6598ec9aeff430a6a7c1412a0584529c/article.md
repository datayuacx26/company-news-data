## 1. Discount Days/Daily Deals


Daily deals can create excitement and drive consistent traffic. For example, "Munchie Mondays" could feature discounted edibles, while "Wax Wednesdays" offer deals on concentrates. Regular discounts create anticipation and encourage repeat visits. Ensure these deals are well-promoted through various channels and keep the deals easy to remember.


### Here's some inspiration for your Dispensary's Daily Deals:


- **Munchie Monday:** 20% off all edibles, including gummies, chocolates, and baked goods.
- **Top-Shelf Tuesday:** Buy a Top-Shelf 1/8th, get a promotional pre-roll for $1.
- **Wax Wednesday:** 15% off all concentrates, including wax, shatter, and live resin.
- **Thirsty Thursday:** 20% off all cannabis-infused beverages, such as teas, sodas, and tinctures.
- **Flower Friday:** $10 off all eighths of flower, any strain.
- **Sativa Saturday:** 20% off all sativa strains, including flower, pre-rolls, and concentrates.
- **Indica Sunday:** 20% off all indica strains, including flower, pre-rolls, and concentrates.
- **CBD Sunday:** 15% off all CBD-only products, including tinctures, topicals, and edibles.
-


**Happy Hour:** 20% off all orders from 4:20PM - 6:00PM, or 7:10PM-9:00PM.


### Tips for Implementing Daily Deals:


- **Promotion:** Ensure these deals are well-promoted via your website, social media, email newsletters, and in-store signage. Offer deals that are easy to remember so customers don't have to think to figure out what is on sale that day.
- **Rotation:** Consider rotating the specific products or categories featured in each deal to keep things fresh and exciting.
- **Plan accordingly:** Strategically schedule your best deals for slower days to boost traffic when it's typically lower. While offering enticing deals on your busiest days can help you stand out from the competition, focusing on slower days can significantly enhance overall sales.
- **With Meadow POS, you can set it and forget it.** Set discounts to apply on certain categories, brands, or products on days of the week, and during certain timeframes, all within the margin guardrails described above.

## 2. Loyalty Programs


Implementing a robust loyalty program is crucial for the long-term success of your dispensary. While attracting new customers is important, retaining loyal customers is far more critical for sustained growth and profitability. Repeat customers are more likely to make larger purchases, refer friends, and provide valuable feedback.


Loyalty points can be accumulated for discounts, free products, or exclusive access to new product releases, fostering a sense of appreciation and exclusivity. Beyond incentivizing repeat business, loyalty programs also help gather valuable customer data, enabling personalized marketing and improved customer experiences.


##### Here are some loyalty program examples you can implement with Meadow's[loyalty program](https://getmeadow.com/features/loyalty-and-rewards) :


#### Point-Based System


Customers earn points for every dollar spent, which can be redeemed for discounts, free products, or exclusive merchandise.


**How it Works:** Earn points for every dollar spent. **Example:** Earn 1 point for every $1 spent, redeemable for discounts or free products.


#### Tiered Rewards Program


Customers progress through different tiers based on their spending, unlocking greater rewards and benefits at each level.


**How it Works:** Customers earn points and move up tiers as they spend more. **Example:** For every 100 points, receive $10 off. At 200 points, receive $20 off.


#### Visit-Based Rewards


Reward customers for frequent visits to encourage repeat business.


**How it Works:** Customers earn points or rewards for each visit. **Example:** After 10 visits, receive a 15% discount on your next purchase.


#### Product-Specific Rewards


Offer free merchandise or discounted products based on points accumulated.


**How it Works:** Customers can redeem points for specific products. **Example:** For every 10 points, get a free smoking accessory. For 20 points, get a penny pre-roll.


#### Cash-Back on Purchases


Provide a percentage of cash back on purchases, which can be used for future transactions.


**How it Works:** Customers earn a percentage of their purchase amount as cash back. **Example:** Earn 5% cash back on every purchase, redeemable on future orders.


### Tips for Implementing a Loyalty Program:


- **Balance Rewards with Margins:** Ensure that the rewards and discounts offered through your loyalty program do not erode profit margins. Regularly analyze the financial impact to strike a balance between customer satisfaction and maintaining healthy margins.
- **Legal Compliance:** Ensure your loyalty program complies with all relevant cannabis regulations and advertising guidelines. Clearly communicate the terms and conditions to avoid any misunderstandings or legal issues. For example, offering free products is prohibited under some state regulations, so consider requiring a purchase alongside points redemption to avoid a $0 transaction.
- **Keep it Simple:** Ensure the program is easy to understand and use. Complicated rules and processes can deter customers from participating. Integrate the loyalty program seamlessly into your POS system and online store to streamline the process for both customers and staff.
- **With Meadow POS, offering and managing your loyalty program is simple.** Customers can redeem points in-store with a few clicks from the budtender or apply rewards themselves when ordering online.

**Goodie Bag:** Create a discounted bundle of popular products specifically for first-time customers. **Example:** A curated selection of edibles, pre-rolls, and small accessories at a reduced price.

## 4. Referral Programs


Encourage your existing customers to bring in new business with a referral program. Offer both the referrer and the new customer a discount or a free product. Word-of-mouth is incredibly powerful, especially in the cannabis community.


### Here are some suggestions to create a Referral Deal:


- **Mutual Discount:** Offer a 20% discount to both the referrer and the referred customer on their next purchase. This deal incentivizes current customers to bring in friends while providing a great first-time discount to new customers.
- **Exclusive Product Gift:** Provide an exclusive penny product, such as a pre-roll or edible, to both the referrer and the referred customer upon the new customer's first purchase. This adds a unique value to the referral program and creates excitement around trying new products.


### Tips for Referral Programs:


- **Promote the Program:** Regularly advertise your referral program through multiple channels, including social media, email newsletters, and in-store signage, to ensure customers are aware of the opportunity. Have budtenders inform customers about the referral program during their purchase, highlighting the benefits and how they can participate.
- **Provide Printed Cards:** Offering current customers a physical copy of the referral promotion, such as printed cards or informational flyers, can remind them of your referral deal. Include essential shop information, such as phone number, address, and website, making it easy for customers to share with friends and family.
- **Simplify the Process:** Make it easy for customers to refer friends by providing simple referral links, codes, or in-store forms. A seamless process increases participation.
- **With Meadow POS, you can easily track your referral deals with our robust reporting suite.** Create a referral discount and track how often your customers are bringing in new business.

## 5. Holiday Promotions


Capitalize on holidays and cannabis-specific events like 4/20 and 7/10 with special promotions. Limited-time offers, themed products, and festive discounts can drive significant traffic to your dispensary.


### Most Common Holiday Deals for Dispensaries:


- **4/20 (April 20):** Major discounts, special bundles, and exclusive products to celebrate the unofficial cannabis holiday globally recognized by enthusiasts.
- **7/10 (July 10):** Deals on wax, shatter, and other concentrates, along with special events for "Dab Day," focusing on cannabis concentrates.
- **Green Wednesday (Day before Thanksgiving):** Pre-holiday discounts and promotions to boost sales. - **Black Friday (Day after Thanksgiving):** Doorbuster deals, significant discounts, and flash sales to kick off the holiday shopping season.
- **Cyber Monday (Monday after Thanksgiving):** Exclusive online-only discounts and promotions to capitalize on online shopping.
- **New Year's Eve/Day (December 31/January 1):** Special promotions on party-friendly products like edibles, pre-rolls, and beverages to celebrate the new year.
- **Valentine's Day (February 14):** Deals on romantic or self-care products such as infused chocolates, bath bombs, and wellness items.
- **St. Patrick's Day (March 17):** Themed promotions like "pot of gold" deals on select products and festive green bundles.
- **Halloween (October 31):** Spooky-themed products and events, along with discounts on edibles, especially sweets.

## 6. Customer Appreciation Day


Customer Appreciation Day is a recurring event designed to show gratitude to your loyal customers while creating a sense of community and excitement. Held on the same day every month, it turns into a highly anticipated event that customers look forward to, essentially becoming your own holiday.


### Suggestions for Customer Appreciation Day:


- **Consistent Scheduling:** Hold Customer Appreciation Day on the same day every month, for example, the first Friday of the month.
- **Store-Wide Deals:** Offer special store-wide deals and discounts to reward your customers and boost sales.
- **Offer a Referral Deal:** Customer Appreciation Day is a great opportunity for referral deals because it occurs on the same day each month, allowing customers to plan ahead.


### Tips for Implementing Customer Appreciation Day:


- **Maintain Consistency:** Ensure the event occurs regularly on the scheduled day each month to build a reliable event that customers can plan for.
- **Daily Deal Integration:** Decide whether to skip or honor overlapping deals, such as daily deals, to maintain clarity and maximize promotions.
- **Advance Promotion:** Create a dedicated webpage or printed flyer outlining all the deals and distribute or promote it at least a week in advance.
- **Partnered Brand Deals:** Reach out to your partnered brands to create exclusive deals that will be reimbursed on your next invoice. Request promotional products to offer BOGO deals or gifts with purchase with a new purchase order.

## Common Questions


### What are the best daily deals for a dispensary?


The most effective daily deals rotate through product categories on a predictable schedule, like edibles on Monday and concentrates on Wednesday, so customers remember when to shop for what they want. Keep the deal easy to state in one sentence and promote it consistently across your website, social channels, and in-store signage.


### How does a dispensary loyalty program work?


Most dispensary loyalty programs award points per dollar spent, which customers redeem for discounts, free products, or exclusive perks, sometimes with tiers that unlock bigger rewards as spending increases. The best programs integrate directly with your POS so points apply automatically in-store and online.


### What's the best way to structure a referral program?


Reward both the referring customer and the new customer, not just one side, since that's what makes the offer worth sharing. Keep redemption simple with a code or card, and make sure your POS can track which new customers came from a referral so you can measure what's working.


### How do I stop budtenders from accidentally giving away too big a discount?


Set a Maximum Discount Percent as a global cap so no order can exceed it, regardless of how many discounts or loyalty rewards combine on it, and use Global Restrictions to block premium or low-margin products from qualifying for any discount storewide. Automatic discount types can't be manually applied at all, which removes register-level error for those promotions entirely.


### When should dispensaries run holiday promotions?


Beyond the obvious cannabis holidays like 4/20 and 7/10, mainstream retail moments like Black Friday, Cyber Monday, and Green Wednesday (the day before Thanksgiving) consistently drive traffic for dispensaries. Plan inventory and staffing at least a few weeks ahead of any promotion you expect to be a high-traffic day.
