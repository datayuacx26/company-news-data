- **Consistency across locations.** Every store needed to operate with the same high standards, ensuring a familiar and reliable experience for customers no matter which OTC location they visited.


With Meadow’s fast, intuitive transactions, seamless checkouts, and built-in tools for personalized service, Off the Charts has built a customer-focused dispensary chain that feels consistent, reliable, and welcoming across every location.


By prioritizing efficiency without sacrificing quality, they’ve set a new standard for cannabis retail in California—proving that growth and exceptional service can go hand in hand.

## Mastering Multi-Store Inventory


A seamless customer experience starts behind the scenes—with the right products, in the right quantities, at the right time. As Off the Charts scaled, inventory management became a critical pillar of their success, ensuring that every location operated smoothly and stayed compliant.


Without precise inventory control, fast-moving dispensaries risk lost revenue, compliance violations, and poor customer experiences. With 30+ locations and thousands of SKUs to manage, Off the Charts needed a system that could provide real-time visibility and easy inventory workflows across their entire network.


With Meadow’s inventory management tools, Off the Charts can track stock across multiple locations with ease. Managers can instantly verify inventory levels, and streamlined workflows allow staff to quickly add and update products—keeping every store stocked and running efficiently.


Off The Charts SF keeps inventory accurate and compliant with Meadow’s Cycle Counts, allowing staff to verify stock levels in real time without interrupting sales.


But accurate inventory management isn’t just about efficiency—it’s also essential for compliance and loss prevention. That’s why Off the Charts relies on Meadow’s Cycle Counts tool to stay ahead of audits, theft, and inventory discrepancies.


With Meadow’s Cycle Counts tool, OTC staff can:


- Count inventory in real time while stores remain open—no need to pause sales
- Detect loss or theft instantly, catching discrepancies before they escalate
- Ensure compliance with Metrc, with smart tools to track and report inventory adjustments
- Assign multiple employees to count simultaneously, making the process faster and more efficient


By making Meadow’s Cycle Counts a core part of their inventory strategy, Off the Charts has minimized shrinkage, reduced time spent on full inventory counts, and ensured that every store stays compliant with California’s strict inventory regulations. Their ability to scale while maintaining accuracy and compliance is a direct result of this proactive approach.
