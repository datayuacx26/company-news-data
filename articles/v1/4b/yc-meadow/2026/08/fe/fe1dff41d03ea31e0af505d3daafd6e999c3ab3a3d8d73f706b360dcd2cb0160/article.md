## Key Features of Cannabis Delivery Software


When evaluating cannabis delivery software, dispensaries should weigh how each platform handles:


- **Compliance and Delivery Ledgers:** Does it automate strict reporting requirements, like California's Delivery Ledgers?
- **Hub-and-Spoke Model:** Can it facilitate full product selection from a central inventory hub?
- **Dynamic Delivery (Ice Cream Truck Model):** Does it allow faster fulfillment with pre-packed vehicles serving designated zones?
- **Hybrid Model:** Can it support both, giving customers a choice between speed and selection?
- **Driver and Vehicle Compliance:** Does it ensure GPS tracking, age verification, and secure cash handling?
- **Route Optimization and Dispatching:** How efficiently does it manage driver assignments and deliveries?
- **Integrated Ecommerce and Menu Management:** Can you customize your online menu to match your brand, create a seamless shopping experience, and integrate with Weedmaps?
- **Inventory Sync and Accuracy:** Are real-time updates reflected across every menu and system?
- **Reliability and Uptime:** Will it stay up on 420, holiday weekends, and your busiest sales days?


#### What is Dynamic Delivery?


Cannabis delivery is evolving and the Dynamic Delivery model is helping dispensaries reach more customers, faster.


Dynamic Delivery (sometimes called the "Ice Cream Truck" model) allows drivers to carry pre-packed inventory and fulfill delivery orders on the go within specific zones. This approach helps operators speed up delivery times, expand their reach, and operate more efficiently — all while staying fully compliant.


Meadow was the first cannabis software company to launch Dynamic Delivery in California, and it's been featured in TechCrunch as a game-changing tool for delivery operations.

## 7 Questions to Ask as You Evaluate Cannabis Delivery Software


When you're comparing software solutions, here are seven questions that reveal critical differences between platforms:


### 1. How easy is the system to use for both staff and customers?


An intuitive system saves time, reduces training costs, and leads to fewer errors. When your staff can navigate the POS with confidence, your operation runs smoother. And when customers get real-time menu updates and accurate ETAs, they're more likely to come back.


### 2. Will it reduce the time you spend on inventory and compliance?


Delivery introduces extra complexity to inventory and compliance tracking. Look for systems with real-time inventory sync, automated compliance reports (like CA delivery ledgers), and tight Metrc integrations to avoid costly mistakes and time-consuming workarounds.


### 3. Does it support multiple delivery models?


Whether you're running hub-and-spoke, on-demand dynamic delivery, or both, your software should make it easy to support multiple workflows, scale to new zones, manage local tax rates, and add drivers as you grow.


### 4. What does onboarding and training look like?


Switching systems can be painful or painless. Ask about implementation timelines, data migration support, and training resources. A strong onboarding experience keeps business disruption to a minimum.


### 5. What kind of customer support is available?


In cannabis delivery, timing is everything. Waiting hours or days for a response isn't an option. Look for a software partner with responsive, knowledgeable support from people who understand the cannabis industry, compliance requirements, and the unique challenges of delivery operations.


### 6. How reliable is the system, especially on peak sales days?


Ask for specific uptime numbers, especially around 420. A POS outage on your highest-revenue day of the year can cost tens of thousands of dollars and damage customer trust for months. Meadow has maintained 99.99% uptime over the last decade, with 100% uptime on every 420 since 2015.


### 7. How easy is it to manage and customize your online menu?


For delivery operators, ecommerce is your storefront. Your online menu needs to look good, feel like your brand, and always show accurate inventory. Look for built-in ecommerce tools that let you customize your menu, organize products your way, highlight promotions, and integrate seamlessly with Weedmaps. Real-time inventory syncing is non-negotiable.
