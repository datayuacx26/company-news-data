Most shoppers find their next dispensary through Google, AI assistants, personal recommendations, and the channels you already own: your customer data, your website and menu, your loyalty program, and your presence in the community. ## 1. Enhance Your Online Presence, SEO, and AI Search Visibility


#### Attracting: Search-First Shoppers and Tech-Savvy Millennials


Most new customers find a dispensary in 2026 by searching Google or asking an AI assistant. Show up there, and you win the visit. Keep your site fast, mobile-friendly, and full of content that answers real customer questions rather than pure promotional copy.


**SEO fundamentals:**


- Claim and complete your Google Business Profile: hours, categories, service area, photos.
- Host a live, keyword-rich menu on your own domain, not just a third-party marketplace.
- Build a steady stream of genuine reviews across Google, Weedmaps, and Leafly.


**AI search visibility:** AI tools don't crawl your site in real time. They draw on what's already indexed, so consistency and structure matter more than ever:


- Keep your name, address, phone number, and hours identical across your website, Google Business Profile, Weedmaps, Leafly, and Yelp.
- Add LocalBusiness and FAQ schema to your site so AI systems can extract your content accurately.
- Lead each page and FAQ with a direct one-to-two-sentence answer before adding detail. For more, read[Dispensary SEO: The Truth About E-Commerce, Google Rankings, and Online Sales.](https://getmeadow.com/blog/dispensary-seo-the-truth-about-e-commerce-google-rankings-and-online-sales)

Along with SEO+, Meadow's built-in suite of technical SEO tools that automatically optimizes page speed, product metadata, and sitemaps, so your menu is structured to rank without needing a web team to manage it.

## 4. Build Local Goodwill: Events, Workshops, and Community Partnerships


#### Attracting: Community-Oriented Customers, Newbies, and Enthusiasts


Showing up locally builds a kind of trust no ad can buy, and it compounds year over year. That can mean educational workshops for beginners, deeper-dive sessions on cultivation or terpenes for enthusiasts, sponsoring local events, or simply partnering with nearby businesses. Each one gives your dispensary real value to offer beyond a transaction, and each one strengthens your reputation as a knowledgeable, community-invested business.


**Case study:**[Posh Green Cannabis Boutique](https://www.poshgreencollective.com/) , San Francisco's first equity retail cannabis dispensary independently owned by a woman of color, Reese Benton, has built community service into its business model since its founding in 2016.[On January 19, 2023, Posh Green partnered with PAX and other local businesses to host a Day of Peace compassion event](https://getmeadow.com/blog/cannabis-donations-in-california-pax-compassion-case-study) , distributing free cannabis products to veterans, LGBTQ+ individuals, and trauma survivors under the Dennis Peron and Brownie Mary Act (SB-34), which permits tax-free cannabis donations to low-income California patients. That kind of visible, values-aligned commitment is what turns community members into loyal customers.

## 6. Launch a Customer Referral Program


#### Attracting: Friends and Family of Existing Customers


Referrals solve a problem loyalty programs don't: they reward a customer for bringing someone new in, not just for shopping again themselves. In a market where paid acquisition is mostly off-limits, turning existing customers into your sales force is one of the highest-leverage moves available, and it costs you only what you'd already be discounting anyway.


Keep it simple: give both the referring customer and their new friend a reward, redeemable on the new customer's first purchase. A flat discount or a free gift both work; what matters is that the offer is easy to explain in one sentence and easy to redeem in-store.


**How Meadow supports this:** Meadow's built-in, single-use promo codes make referral tracking straightforward: issue a unique code to a customer, share it however you like (text, email, in-store), and redeem it directly at checkout with no separate referral platform needed.

## 7. Leverage Social Media Platforms


#### Attracting: Young Adults and Social Media Enthusiasts


Social media builds the brand that makes every other channel work harder, even though cannabis businesses still can't run paid ads on the major platforms. As of 2026, platforms still restrict cannabis content, and accounts get flagged for violating community guidelines. A few precautions to avoid getting shadow-banned:


1. Be mindful of your hashtags
2. Avoid showing the plant or consumption
3. Highlight scientific research instead
4. Diversify your content and don't post excessively
5. Follow each platform's community guidelines
6. Don't advertise anything that could draw children's attention

## Common Questions


### How do dispensaries attract new customers?


Dispensaries attract new customers most reliably through channels they own and control: a search- and AI-optimized website, targeted email and text campaigns, first-time discounts, loyalty and referral programs, community events, and compliant social media. Paid advertising remains heavily restricted for cannabis, so owned channels outperform.


### How do I get my dispensary recommended by AI tools like ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews?


Keep your name, address, phone number, and hours identical across your website, Google Business Profile, Weedmaps, Leafly, and Yelp, add LocalBusiness and FAQ schema to your site, and build a steady stream of genuine reviews. AI tools draw on indexed, consistent information rather than crawling your site in real time.


### Do referral programs work for dispensaries?


Yes, especially where paid advertising is restricted. A referral program turns existing customers into an acquisition channel, rewarding them for bringing in someone new rather than just for shopping again. Keep the offer simple and easy to redeem in one visit.


### Can dispensaries advertise on Google or Instagram?


As of the writing of this article, mainstream platforms like Google, Meta, and Instagram still broadly prohibit paid cannabis advertising, and organic accounts risk removal for showing products or consumption. Focus on organic SEO, AI search visibility, your own website and menu, review generation, and opt-in email and SMS instead.
