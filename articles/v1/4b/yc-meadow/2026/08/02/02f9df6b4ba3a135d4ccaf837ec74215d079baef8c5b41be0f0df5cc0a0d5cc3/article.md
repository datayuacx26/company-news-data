## The Real Cost of "Good Enough"


A POS that mostly works is quietly expensive. The costs just don't land on one invoice, so they're easy to ignore until they add up.


Slow checkouts are the clearest example. Before[Off the Charts](https://getmeadow.com/blog/off-the-charts-case-study) switched to Meadow, their previous system took up to three minutes to process a single order. That is an eternity when the line is out the door on 4/20.


> **With our previous POS, it took up to three minutes just to process a single order. When you're focused on delivering a great customer experience, that just doesn't cut it.**
>
>
> ***Marissa Molloy, Regional Manager, Off the Charts Northern California Stores***


Multiply that across every register, every busy Saturday, and every peak day, and "good enough" quietly becomes lost revenue and lost regulars.


The rest of the bill adds up fast:


- **Downtime when it matters most.** An outage on 4/20 or a Friday night isn't an inconvenience. It's thousands in lost sales and a room full of frustrated customers who may not come back. This isn't hypothetical. Dutchie's POS went down[nationwide on 4/20 in 2023](https://mjbizdaily.com/cannabis-sales-software-provider-dutchie-goes-down-nationwide-on-420/) and[again on 4/20 in 2024](https://mjbizdaily.com/dutchie-admits-failures-on-420-promises-payout-to-marijuana-retailers/) . In the 2024 outage, Curio Wellness in Maryland estimated roughly **$80,000** in lost sales and nearly **1,000** canceled preorders ([source: mjbizdaily.com](https://mjbizdaily.com/dutchie-pos-system-for-cannabis-retail-crashes-on-420/) ), and Bloom Brothers CEO Nathan Girard put his store's losses at about **$25,000** across the roughly four and a half hours the system was down ([source: highlyobjective.com](https://www.highlyobjective.com/p/dutchie-goes-down-on-420-again) ).
- **Per-ticket support fees.** Some platforms charge every time you need help, so problems you didn't cause become line items at the worst possible moment.
- **Price creep.** A rate that looked fine at signup climbs at renewal, and per-transaction fees scale against you as you grow. You pay more for the same software simply because you're succeeding. (Worth knowing what the market actually charges:[Cannabis POS Pricing: What Dispensaries Actually Pay](https://getmeadow.com/blog/cannabis-pos-pricing) .)
- **Compliance friction.** Manual Metrc fixes and reconciliation eat hours your team doesn't have, and every manual step is a chance for an audit-triggering error.
- **Lock-in.** Long-term contracts keep you paying for a system you've already outgrown. Add it up and "good enough" usually costs more than switching ever would. The question isn't whether you can afford to switch. It's whether you can afford not to.

## Common Questions


### How long does it take to switch dispensary POS?


With a focused, provider-led migration it's typically days, not months. Hardware and data move overnight, so there's no downtime during business hours. Timelines stretch mainly when product data is messy or staff training hasn't started.


### Will I lose my product catalog and customer data?


No. A proper migration moves your products and customer data so you don't start over. Confirm before you sign that your new provider handles the data transfer and that your current provider allows a clean export.


### What happens to Metrc during the switch?


A good migration includes a full Metrc audit and inventory cleanup, so your compliance records are accurate from day one. That cleanup is often the most valuable part of switching, because it resolves discrepancies that have been quietly accumulating.


### Is there downtime when I switch dispensary POS?


Done right, no. Meadow installs hardware and transfers data the night before go-live, so customers never notice.


### Isn't switching too risky during my busy season?


Plan the cutover for a slow window and avoid 4/20 and major holidays. With overnight migration and advance staff training, the disruption is minimal, and the cost of pushing a failing system through peak season is usually higher.


### How much does it cost to switch dispensary POS?


The switch itself is usually the smaller number. Compare total cost of ownership over 12 months, including per-transaction fees, support charges, hardware, and renewal increases, against what you're losing now to downtime, slow tickets, and manual compliance work.[Cannabis POS Pricing: What Dispensaries Actually Pay](https://getmeadow.com/blog/cannabis-pos-pricing) breaks down the models.
