## Why Operators Look for a Flowhub Alternative


Operators leave a working POS for a small number of recurring reasons, and they are rarely about a missing button.


- **Stitched-together tools.** Running separate products and integrations for eCommerce, delivery, and loyalty means separate contracts, separate support queues, and inventory that has to be reconciled across systems instead of syncing once.
- **Pricing structure.** Fee models that scale against the store. Per-transaction costs mean your best month is also your most expensive one.
- **Support responsiveness during peak periods.** A ticket that sits for two days on a Friday afternoon is not a support issue, it is a revenue issue.
- **Day-to-day simplicity.** Every extra click in checkout multiplies by your transaction count. Every extra surface in the back office multiplies by your manager's week. The point is not that Flowhub is wrong for everyone. It is that fit depends on your model, and once you are shopping, it is worth comparing seriously rather than picking the next name you recognize.

## Questions to Ask Before You Sign


Bring these to every demo. The answers separate vendors faster than any feature grid.


#### 1. How does the provider make money?


Subscription only, or subscription plus a cut of every transaction? Ask whether payments revenue is part of their model, because it determines whether you can shop your own processor later.


#### 2. What is the documented uptime, including 4/20?


Every vendor claims reliability. Ask for the specific number and the specific 4/20 record. Public outage history is a matter of record.


#### 3. Is compliance real-time or batched?


Real-time Metrc sync catches an error while the package is still in your hands. A nightly sync catches it after the state already has the data.


#### 4. What is included versus what is an add-on?


Get eCommerce, delivery, loyalty, and reporting itemized in writing. "Integrated" often means "integrates with a product you will pay someone else for."


#### 5. Who answers when something breaks on a Saturday?


Ask for the actual response time target and whether support is free or ticketed. Then ask a current customer whether it holds.


#### 6. Can I export my data and leave?


Ask specifically about customer lists, sales history, and inventory. If the answer requires a conversation with an account manager, that is your answer.


#### 7. What does the migration itself look like?


Who cleans up Metrc, who trains staff, and are you open for business the next morning?
