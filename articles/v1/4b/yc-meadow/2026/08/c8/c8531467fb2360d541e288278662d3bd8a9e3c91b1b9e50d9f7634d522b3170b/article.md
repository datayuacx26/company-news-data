# How The Higher Path Fixed Online Loyalty by Switching to Meadow + Alpine IQ

The phone would ring, and a budtender already knew what it was about before picking up. A regular had just placed an online order and wanted to use their points. Or they wanted to know how many they had. Someone on the team would stop what they were doing, log into the loyalty platform, look up the balance, and add the points to the order by hand. Then do it again an hour later for the next caller.


The points were real. The rewards were real. The problem was the stack. The Higher Path was running three separate systems: Treez at the register, Tymber for its online menu, and Alpine IQ for loyalty. And Alpine IQ didn't integrate with Tymber, even after Tymber was acquired by Blaze. So every point earned or redeemed on an online order had to pass through a staff member's hands.


**This is how The Higher Path replaced three disconnected systems with Meadow + Alpine IQ, and made loyalty work everywhere its customers shop.**

## The Higher Path Dispensary in Sherman Oaks, CA


[The Higher Path](https://thehigherpath.com/) has been on Ventura Boulevard in Sherman Oaks since 2013. Named Best Dispensary in Los Angeles by *LA Weekly* , it has been run for more than a decade by owner Jerred Kiloh as a community fixture "by patients, for patients." It runs a loyalty program regulars genuinely love and pay attention to, with a high-volume operation where every minute of staff time counts. The program wasn't the problem. The patchwork underneath it was.


## The Challenge: Three Systems, One Missing Link


On paper, The Higher Path had every piece a modern dispensary needs: a POS, an eCommerce menu, and a loyalty platform. In practice, the pieces didn't talk to each other where it mattered most. Alpine IQ had no integration with the Tymber menu, so the loyalty program was invisible on online orders. Customers couldn't see their balances. They couldn't redeem their points. And when a program is good enough that regulars actually track their rewards, invisible doesn't mean forgotten. It means phone calls.


**"Before the integration, online loyalty was honestly one of our biggest headaches,"** *says Jessica Martin, COO of The Higher Path* . **"Customers couldn't see their point balances or redeem rewards on online orders, so we were constantly getting phone calls asking us to check balances or explain why they couldn't use their points. It created frustration for customers and extra work for our staff that really didn't need to exist."**


Every one of those calls pulled a team member away from the floor to look up a balance in a separate system or add points to an online order manually. For a shop built on personal service, that was the wrong kind of personal. And the root cause wasn't any single product. It was a fragmented stack where the connection the customers needed simply didn't exist.


The sales floor at The Higher Path in Sherman Oaks, where every purchase now earns loyalty points automatically and budtenders apply rewards right at the register.


## The Turning Point: One Platform, One Integration


The Higher Path consolidated on Meadow, which runs the register and the online menu on a single platform: sales, inventory, compliance, and eCommerce in one system. That consolidation changed the loyalty math. Instead of needing separate integrations for the POS and the eCommerce menu, and hoping both existed, one Meadow + Alpine IQ integration covers every channel the shop sells on.


The integration syncs sales and customer data between Meadow and Alpine IQ in real time. A purchase earns points the moment it happens, online or in store. Balances are visible where customers actually shop. And redeeming is flexible: rewards can be applied online or in store, at checkout or after an order is placed, so a forgotten reward takes moments to fix instead of a phone call to sort out.
