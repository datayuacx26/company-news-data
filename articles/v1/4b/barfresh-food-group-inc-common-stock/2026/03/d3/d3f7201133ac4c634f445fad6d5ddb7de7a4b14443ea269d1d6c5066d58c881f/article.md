# Barfresh to Announce Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2025 Results on March 31, 2026

LOS ANGELES, March 17, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Barfresh Food Group Inc. Listeners can dial (877) 407-4018 in North America, and international listeners can dial (201) 689-8471.
