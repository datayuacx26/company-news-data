# Partnering with SD Times to Help Software Developers Make Better Stack Decisions

We're excited to announce Taloflow's partnership with[SD Times](https://sdtimes.com/) , the leading source for news and analysis in the software development industry.


Taloflow is a leader in leveraging first-party data for software and cloud vendor capabilities, and SD Times is established within the software developer community as a trusted source of industry news and insights. Combined, we are introducing **comprehensive Solutions Guides across multiple categories** hosted on SDTimes.com.


Starting today, visitors to SDTimes.com can access in-depth guides tailored to specific technological needs in 10 new categories such as Platform Engineering, Object Storage, Low Code, iPaaS (Integration Platform as a Service), Data Integration, Cloud Database Management Systems (DBMS), Cloud Cost Management, APM (Application Performance Management) and Observability, API Management, and API Gateway solutions.


Each Solutions Guide is crafted using Taloflow's extensive trove of up-to-date first-party data, and from the guide you can sign up for Taloflow to get more detailed analyses of vendor performance, important category requirements, and trade-offs, ensuring that readers are equipped to make informed decisions.


As part of this initiative, each guide will be regularly updated to reflect the latest developments and trends in the industry. Our joint goal is to serve as your go-to resource, helping software developers navigate the complexities of today's tech landscape with ease and confidence. We also have plans to integrate this data more tightly within the editorial content of SDTimes.
