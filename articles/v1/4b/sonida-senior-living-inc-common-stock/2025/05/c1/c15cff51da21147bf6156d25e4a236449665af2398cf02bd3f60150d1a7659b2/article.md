[Greenbriar Village](https://sonida.wpenginepowered.com/community/greenbriar-village/) held its annual themed Mother’s Day tea, with staff dressed as Alice in Wonderland characters serving tea and treats. Residents celebrated with a lively trivia game, revealing fun facts like their oldest mother being 105 years old and another having an impressive 32 grandchildren.


The celebration created a joyful and whimsical atmosphere where residents and their families could connect, share stories, and enjoy each other’s company.


Thoughtful details, creative costumes, and heartfelt interactions made the day extra special for everyone involved. Residents left with smiles, full hearts, and cherished memories. Great job, Greenbriar team!


## Carnival Fun


[The Waterford at College Station](https://sonida.wpenginepowered.com/community/the-waterford-at-college-station/) hosted its spring carnival this week, bringing joy to residents and their families. The event featured classic favorites like popcorn, cotton candy, a bounce house, and even a train ride through the community. Everyone enjoyed the festive atmosphere, celebrating the arrival of warmer weather with laughter, connection, and fun-filled activities for all ages.


In addition to the tasty treats and exciting attractions, the carnival provided a wonderful opportunity for residents to socialize and strengthen bonds within the community. Families gathered to share smiles and create new memories, while staff ensured everyone felt welcome and included. The lively event truly captured the spirit of spring and left everyone looking forward to future celebrations.


## Bobby’s Dream


Bobby cherishes his memories of deer hunting, so the[West Shores](https://sonida.wpenginepowered.com/community/west-shores/) team knew exactly what to do for his Tree of Dreams experience. They brought him to his favorite deer camp, where they roasted hot dogs over an open fire and surprised him with thoughtful gifts. Afterwards, Bobby took a ride around the grounds, reminiscing about his favorite pastime. The day was filled with joy, reflection, and heartfelt moments.


The team carefully planned every detail to make the experience as meaningful as possible, ensuring Bobby felt comfortable and connected to the outdoors he loves. Stories were shared around the campfire, and the peaceful surroundings brought back fond memories of past hunting trips. This special day not only honored Bobby’s passion but also created new memories he will treasure for years to come.
