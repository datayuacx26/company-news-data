### Kinetik Publishes 2025 Sustainability Report


July 29, 2026


HOUSTON & MIDLAND, Texas--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Kinetik Holdings Inc. (NYSE: KNTK) (“ **Kinetik** ” or the “ **Company** ”) today published its 2025 Sustainability Report (the “ **Report** ”), highlighting its sustainability initiatives, goals, and progress throughout 2025. The Report provides a transparent and comprehensive overview of Kinetik’s progress towards a more reliable, efficient, and sustainable energy future. An electronic version of the Report can be accessed on the Sustainability section of Kinetik’s website or directly at the link below.


[2025 Sustainability Report](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.kinetik.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F07%2FSustainability-Report-2025-final.pdf&esheet=54579609&newsitemid=20260729187574&lan=en-US&anchor=2025+Sustainability+Report&index=1&md5=ce67bb6a255d14937e77be3ce7e9b633)


**Highlights include:**


- Reduced Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas and methane emissions intensities by approximately 19% and 50%, respectively, from the 2021 baseline
- Expanded our carbon management initiatives and low-carbon business opportunities, including the development of carbon sequestration infrastructure
- Completed more than 20,000 hours of employee training with a strong emphasis on environmental, health, and safety
- Contributed over $1.6 million to charitable organizations and community initiatives
- Maintained oversight of sustainability-related risks and opportunities through the Board of Directors’ Governance and Sustainability Committee


The Report was prepared in reference to the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Standards, the Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB) and the Energy Infrastructure Council (EIC) / GPA Midstream ESG Reporting Framework.


**About Kinetik Holdings Inc.**


Kinetik is a fully integrated, pure-play, Permian-to-Gulf Coast midstream C-corporation operating in the Delaware Basin. Kinetik is headquartered in Houston and Midland, Texas. Kinetik provides comprehensive gathering, transportation, compression, processing and treating services for companies that produce natural gas, natural gas liquids, crude oil and water. Kinetik posts announcements, operational updates, investor information and press releases on its website,[www.kinetik.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.kinetik.com&esheet=54579609&newsitemid=20260729187574&lan=en-US&anchor=www.kinetik.com&index=2&md5=b3325dfcd3e474cc3056cdad82f7f85e) .
