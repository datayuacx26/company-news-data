# Now available: Depot Wrapped 2025

We get to see how folks use Depot to make their builds faster and lives easier. It's exciting to see how much time people save by using the things we've built. This time of year we take a moment to reflect on the last twelve months and look back at our metrics.


Every year we talk about doing a Spotify Wrapped-style recap for Depot organizations with metrics we have collected over the year. But, we never actually went and built it. Until now.


This week we pushed out Depot Wrapped 2025! It's a fun way to look back at the year and see what you built with Depot.


## What's in Depot Wrapped 2025?


Getting to your Depot Wrapped 2025 is as straightforward as opening[depot.dev/wrapped](https://depot.dev/wrapped) while logged in to your Depot organization.


Depot Wrapped 2025 for Github Actions


Inside, you will find a summary of your year using Depot. We pulled out the most interesting metrics and put them into a fun modal to flip through.


- **Total activity** — Container builds and GitHub Actions jobs you shipped this year
- **Build & job timing stats** — Average build times, fastest builds, and how much time you saved by using Depot
- **Platform breakdown** — See how your builds split across different architectures
- **Busiest projects & runners** — Your most active build projects and runner labels
- **Peak usage times** — When your team ships the most
- **Cache insights** — Types of caches used and storage breakdown
- **Your builder personality** — A fun title based on your building patterns


In addition, we put a small surprise at the end for all of you to discover. Hold on to it and we'll reach out in the new year so you can redeem a small gift.


## Share your Depot Wrapped 2025


If you want to share your favorite stats, you can download the slide as an image via the share button. If you share your stats on social media, make sure to tag us[@depotdev on X](https://x.com/depotdev) and we will send you a small gift.


Social media not your jam? Hop into our Discord Community and share your Depot Wrapped stats with the rest of the community. We'd love to see what you built with Depot this year![Join our community here](https://discord.gg/MMPqYSgDCg) .
