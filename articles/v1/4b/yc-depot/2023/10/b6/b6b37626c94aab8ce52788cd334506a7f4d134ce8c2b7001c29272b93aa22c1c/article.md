# Depot is now available in the AWS Marketplace

We've been working hard over the past three months to allow folks to leverage other purchasing options for Depot. Today, we're excited to announce that Depot is now available in the AWS Marketplace for customers looking to purchase an Enterprise plan via AWS.


[This is the final announcement for Drop Week #02 — see the rest](https://depot.dev/drop-week)


Since debuting our[Enterprise plan](https://depot.dev/pricing) , we've heard from many folks that they'd like to purchase Depot through the AWS Marketplace, either because they want to leverage credits they have with AWS or they want to centralize SaaS/cloud spend in one place.


Purchasing Depot through the[AWS Marketplace](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/seller-profile?id=seller-atctfdeogez5a) requires an annual contract. With Enterprise plans, we offer additional features like:


- **Dedicated support** — we offer dedicated customer support for Enterprise customers, which can include Slack Connect and priority support.
- **Unlimited users & projectss** — build with as many users and projects as you need.
- **SSO with SAML & SCIM** — custom SAML or ADFS setups with SSO enforcement and SCIM provisioning.
- **Isolated infrastructure** — we offer the option to have dedicated build infrastructure that is a segregated control and data plane in our cloud.


## Chat about an Enterprise plan


If you want to purchase Depot in the AWS Marketplace, we'd love to discuss an Enterprise plan. You canemail us directly to chat with us about your build needs.
