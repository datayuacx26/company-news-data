# Depot is now a member of the CNCF

We've long admired and been heavy consumers of several CNCF projects, including Kubernetes, Prometheus, Flux, and Helm. It's safe to say that those projects fuel hundreds of thousands of new ideas every day, Depot included. So, naturally, we wanted to join them in helping to build and shape the future of the cloud native ecosystem.


We're thrilled to be a part of the[CNCF landscape](https://landscape.cncf.io/) and an incredible community of fellow builders like ourselves. We're looking forward to contributing to several CNCF projects with our experience, knowledge, and performance of Depot.


If you want to learn more about the CNCF, check out their[website](https://www.cncf.io/) . If you're tired of slow Docker image builds and the pain that they bring,[sign up for a Depot account](https://depot.dev/start) and let us help you get faster builds.
