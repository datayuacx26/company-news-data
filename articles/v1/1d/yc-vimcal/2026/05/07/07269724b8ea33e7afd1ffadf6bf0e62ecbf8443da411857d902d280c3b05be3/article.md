## **1 Tech Tip**


### Swipe left halfway on an iPhone notification for Options


Most people either tap a notification (and get sucked in) or swipe it away entirely. But did you know there's a middle option?


Swipe left on any notification but only halfway across the screen, it will reveal Options.


You can now mute that app for 1 hour, mute it for the day, or go straight to its notification settings.


This is the fastest way to do a real-time notification audit without ever going into Settings. The next time a low-priority app sends you a notification, fix it right then and there!
