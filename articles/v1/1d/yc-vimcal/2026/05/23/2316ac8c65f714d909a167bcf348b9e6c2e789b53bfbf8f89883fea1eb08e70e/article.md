## **1 Tech Tip**


### Typing äccénts on Mac


I had to type a lot of accented Irish names this past week for our Dublin meetup invitations and used this trick over-and-over again.


On Mac, it’s super simple: press and hold any vowel and you’ll get a little accent menu.


For example:


1.


Press-and-hold **e** → a menu pops up with options like é, è, ê, etc.


2.


Then just hit the corresponding number (e.g., **2 for é** ) and it drops right in.


If you didn’t know this one, I’m genuinely curious: how were you typing accented letters before? 👀
