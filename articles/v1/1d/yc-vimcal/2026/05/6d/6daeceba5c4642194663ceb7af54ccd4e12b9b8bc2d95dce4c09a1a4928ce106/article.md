## **1 Tech Tip**


### iPhone’s Work Focus mode


I might be very late to the party but I just discovered iPhone’s **Work Focus** feature!


Think of it as a second Do Not Disturb profile.


Yes, regular Do Not Disturb can already silence notifications. The point of Work Focus is that you can create a separate setup for work without messing up your personal DND settings.


That means you can have a separate “allowed list” specifically for work, which only lets execs + key people, Calendar, Slack/Teams notifications get through while muting the rest.


On top of that, you can also have a work-only Home Screen!


You can make it so **when you have Work Focus turned on, your home screen only shows your work apps/pages** .


So when you unlock your phone at work, it’s basically: Calendar, email, Slack, with none of the distracting stuff.


You can set it up by just going to your iPhone’s Settings → Focus → Work


Next week’s tech tip will build on this Work Focus feature so make sure to set it up this week if you want to follow along!
