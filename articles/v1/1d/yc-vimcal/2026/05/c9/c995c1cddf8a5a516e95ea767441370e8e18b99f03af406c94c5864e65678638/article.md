## **1 Tech Tip**


### Auto-turn on Work Focus when you join office Wi-Fi


This is a follow up to[last week’s Work Focus tech tip](https://ccingmyea.vimcal.com/p/thursday-things-may-14th-2026) and will use iPhone’s built-in Shortcuts app.


Once you’ve set up Work Focus, you can make your iPhone turn it on automatically the moment you connect to your office Wi-Fi, and then turn it off when you leave.


1.


Open **Shortcuts** app


2.


Tap **Automation** (bottom)


3.


Tap **+** → Under Personal Automation, tap **Wi-Fi**


4.


Choose your office Wi-Fi network name


5.


Select both **Is Joined** and **Run Immediately** → Next


6.


**Create New Shortcut**


7.


Use the Search Actions box to find “ **Set** **Focus** ”


8.


Focus: **Work** → set to **On** → Next


9.


Done!


Then create another automation with the same steps but turn Work Focus OFF when you leave Wi-Fi.


That’s it. Now your phone automatically goes into “work mode” when you’re on the office network, and returns to normal when you’re not.


Tiny caveat: If your office Wi-Fi is spotty, Work Focus may toggle on/off as you move around. If that’s annoying, you can use a different trigger instead (location, time window, or “when I open Slack”).
