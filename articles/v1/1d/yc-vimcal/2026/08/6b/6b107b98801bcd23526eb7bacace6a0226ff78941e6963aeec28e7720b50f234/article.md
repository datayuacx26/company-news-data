## **1 Tech Tip**


### Gift ideas from 24 readers


2 weeks ago, Jessica asked for client gift ideas that are unique, useful, and budget-friendly (no standard swag). Below are the highlights from the 24 of you wrote in (thank you!!!). The full list, in everyone's own words, is linked at the end.


**Let them pick.**


Goody ([ongoody.com](https://ongoody.com/?utm_source=ccingmyea.vimcal.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=thursday-things-aug-13th-2026) ) lets you set an amount and the recipient chooses; two readers use it. AXOMO (a swag platform) and SugarWish were each suggested once.


**Local & consumable.**


Hampers from Fortnum & Mason, Omaha Steaks and eCreamery, a fresh truffle box, and Rent Mother Nature. One reader does a custom Lego set of their office building (~$45/person); another assembles a box of local goods. Another reader gifted a box of diffuser fragrances.


**Useful.**


Leatherology luggage tags (~$45), Smart Mugs, the Plane Pocket, and logo AirPods (under $200).


**With meaning.**


A donation to the client's charity in their name, gifts made by people with disabilities, or — on a nonprofit budget — a signed sports ball. One reader gifts leadership books chosen by their execs.


**Process, not a product.**


One Chief of Staff's tip: fix the annual scramble by deciding early and locking in the order.
