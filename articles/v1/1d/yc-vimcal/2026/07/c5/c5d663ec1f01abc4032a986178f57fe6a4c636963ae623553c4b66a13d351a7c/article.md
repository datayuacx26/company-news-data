## **1 Tech Tip**


### New in Vimcal EA: Schedule with voice


This tech tip comes from CCing my EA reader[Ramona Justice](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ramona-adair-justice?utm_source=ccingmyea.vimcal.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=thursday-things-jul-2nd-2026) !


“I have a tech tip that might seem obvious, but it was a total game-changer for me.


If you use separate personal and work calendars that don't sync; for instance, if your work Outlook is locked down and can't be added to your phone; you can simply invite your work email address to your personal events.


For example, if you receive a doctor's appointment confirmation on your personal email, adding your work address as an invitee will add it to your professional calendar automatically.


This is a great way to avoid double entries and ensure your schedule is up to date on both ends.”
