## **1 Tech Tip**


### Gmail tip: turn on “Send & Archive”


Did you know Gmail has a built-in Send & Archive button that does both at once? It just saves one click but those clicks add up over time!


How to turn it on


1.


Open Gmail on desktop


2.


Click the gear icon → See all settings


3.


Under General, find Send and Archive


4.


Select **Show “Send & Archive” button in reply**


5.


Scroll down and click Save Changes


Now, when you reply to an email, you’ll see a new send button that sends your reply *and* archives the thread from your inbox.


Unfortunately, Outlook doesn’t really have a clean equivalent here. You can reply, then archive manually, but Gmail gets the satisfying one-click version.
