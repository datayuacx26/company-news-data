## **1 Tech Tip**


### Hide self view in Zoom


This tech tip comes from[Nikki](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nikki-montgomery-1a7a8b13/?utm_source=ccingmyea.vimcal.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=thursday-things-jun-4th-2026) , an EA and CCing my EA reader, in response to this meme I shared a few weeks back.


One of the best pieces of advice given when my company started with Zoom was to turn off the self-view. It's too distracting for some people, it's hard not to worry about what you look like, what shows, is the virtual background holding up or is my room peeking in.... To this day if I even one time look at myself during a video call, I turn off my self-view. I don't want to be "that person" like in the images you shared. LOL


To turn on, hover over the icon of the squares in the upper right corner to access the View menu. You can toggle it off/on there (highlighted in green in the screenshot). Others on the call can still see your video.
