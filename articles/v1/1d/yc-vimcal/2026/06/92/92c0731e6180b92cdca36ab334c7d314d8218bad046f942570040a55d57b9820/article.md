## **1 Tech Tip**


### New in Vimcal EA: Schedule with voice


We just launched a new feature this week that lets you schedule events by voice!


I know it’s a lot of friction to click out of this email just to watch a video, but I swear it’s seriously cool.


I love it because I don’t have to be perfect with my choice of words and it’ll still schedule properly. For example, I can say:


“Schedule a meeting with me and Richard for next Thursday at 1pm. Never mind, I don’t want Thursday. Let’s make it Tuesday, and let’s start it at 3pm instead. Let’s run it for 45 minutes. I would like this to be recurring every 3 weeks. And as a matter of fact, let’s make that meeting only 30 minutes instead.“


This is the exact example I give in the video


And it’ll still create the correct event!
