# The Estée Lauder Companies to Webcast Discussion of Fiscal 2026 Third Quarter Results on May 1, 2026

NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[The Estée Lauder Companies Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.elcompanies.com%2F&esheet=54522682&newsitemid=20260424328041&lan=en-US&anchor=The+Est%26eacute%3Be+Lauder+Companies+Inc.&index=1&md5=e114eb82c1635a8ac2947e1aafc70b2f) (NYSE: EL) will release its fiscal 2026 third quarter results on Friday, May 1, 2026.


On that date, at 8:30 a.m. (ET), the Company will provide a live webcast of its conference call and presentation discussing the results, future prospects and recent corporate developments.


Stéphane de La Faverie, President and CEO, and Akhil Shrivastava, EVP and CFO, will host the call.


Those wishing to access the webcast can visit[http://www.elcompanies.com/investors](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.elcompanies.com%2Finvestors&esheet=54522682&newsitemid=20260424328041&lan=en-US&anchor=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.elcompanies.com%2Finvestors&index=2&md5=d7bf72161ef541f10f01c7ad7d1e9495) . The call will be archived on the Company’s website.


[The Estée Lauder Companies Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.elcompanies.com%2Fen&esheet=54522682&newsitemid=20260424328041&lan=en-US&anchor=The+Est%26eacute%3Be+Lauder+Companies+Inc.&index=3&md5=4754abd671618879f4db5ff31d4b28f9) is one of the world’s leading manufacturers, marketers and sellers of quality skin care, makeup, fragrance and hair care products, and is a steward of luxury and prestige brands globally. The Company’s products are sold in approximately 150 countries and territories under brand names including: Estée Lauder, Aramis, Clinique, Lab Series, Origins, M·A·C, La Mer, Bobbi Brown Cosmetics, Aveda, Jo Malone London, Bumble and bumble, Darphin Paris, TOM FORD, Smashbox, AERIN Beauty, Le Labo, Editions de Parfums Frédéric Malle, GLAMGLOW, KILIAN PARIS, Too Faced, Dr.Jart+, the DECIEM family of brands, including The Ordinary and NIOD, and BALMAIN Beauty.
