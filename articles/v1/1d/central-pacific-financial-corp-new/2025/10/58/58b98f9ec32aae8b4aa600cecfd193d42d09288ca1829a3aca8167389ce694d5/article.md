### Central Pacific Financial Corp. Announces Conference Call to Discuss Third Quarter 2025 Financial Results


October 15, 2025


HONOLULU--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Central Pacific Financial Corp. (NYSE: CPF), parent company of Central Pacific Bank, will release its third quarter 2025 earnings on October 29, 2025, before the open of the New York Stock Exchange. Management will review the results by conference call and live audio webcast beginning at 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time (8:00 a.m. Hawaii Time) on October 29, 2025.


Interested parties may listen to the conference by calling 1-800-715-9871 (conference ID: 6299769), or by listening to the webcast on the company’s investor relations website at[http://ir.cpb.bank](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fir.cpb.bank&esheet=54339304&newsitemid=20251015915767&lan=en-US&anchor=http%3A%2F%2Fir.cpb.bank&index=1&md5=90bcdeecf16456468a1921a4875994cb) . A replay of the call will be available through November 28, 2025 by dialing 1-800-770-2030 (conference ID: 6299769) and on the company’s website.


**About Central Pacific Financial Corp.**


Central Pacific Financial Corp. is a Hawaii-based bank holding company with approximately $7.37 billion in assets as of June 30, 2025. Central Pacific Bank, its primary subsidiary, operates 27 branches and 55 ATMs in the State of Hawaii.
