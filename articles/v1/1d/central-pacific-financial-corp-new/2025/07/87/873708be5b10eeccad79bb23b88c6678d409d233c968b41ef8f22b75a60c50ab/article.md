### Central Pacific Financial Corp. Announces Conference Call to Discuss Second Quarter 2025 Financial Results


July 11, 2025


HONOLULU--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Central Pacific Financial Corp. (NYSE: CPF), parent company of Central Pacific Bank, will release its second quarter 2025 earnings on July 25, 2025, before the open of the New York Stock Exchange. Management will review the results by conference call and live audio webcast beginning at 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time (8:00 a.m. Hawaii Time) on July 25, 2025.


Interested parties may listen to the conference by calling 1-800-715-9871 (conference ID: 6299769), or by listening to the webcast on the company’s investor relations website at[http://ir.cpb.bank](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fir.cpb.bank&esheet=54289098&newsitemid=20250711240424&lan=en-US&anchor=http%3A%2F%2Fir.cpb.bank&index=1&md5=5d531e627d0b377a8445d235a7a6a185) . A replay of the call will be available through August 24, 2025 by dialing 1-800-770-2030 (conference ID: 6299769) and on the company’s website.


**About Central Pacific Financial Corp.**


Central Pacific Financial Corp. is a Hawaii-based bank holding company with approximately $7.41 billion in assets as of March 31, 2025. Central Pacific Bank, its primary subsidiary, operates 27 branches and 55 ATMs in the State of Hawaii. For additional information, please visit the Company's website at:[http://www.cpb.bank](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.cpb.bank&esheet=54289098&newsitemid=20250711240424&lan=en-US&anchor=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.cpb.bank&index=2&md5=f786b57732675d0a72912657091cf240) .
