### Central Pacific Financial Corp. Announces Conference Call to Discuss Fourth Quarter 2025 Financial Results


January 14, 2026


HONOLULU--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Central Pacific Financial Corp. (NYSE: CPF), parent company of Central Pacific Bank, will release its fourth quarter 2025 earnings on January 28, 2026, before the open of the New York Stock Exchange. Management will review the results by conference call and live audio webcast beginning at 1:00 p.m. Eastern Time (8:00 a.m. Hawaii Time) on January 28, 2026.


Interested parties may listen to the conference by calling 1-800-715-9871 (conference ID: 6299769), or by registering for the webcast at the following link:[https://events.q4inc.com/attendee/865518779](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fattendee%2F865518779&esheet=54396084&newsitemid=20260114702336&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fattendee%2F865518779&index=1&md5=ccf180d89eca9d455ea75cc3ce0e6154) . The Company’s investor relations website,[https://ir.cpb.bank](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.cpb.bank&esheet=54396084&newsitemid=20260114702336&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fir.cpb.bank&index=2&md5=7fc0dac5e203e048ff33d732c45c4175) , will also include a link to the webcast and a slide presentation.


A replay of the call will be available through February 27, 2026, by dialing 1-800-770-2030 (conference ID: 6299769) and on the Company’s investor relations website.


**About Central Pacific Financial Corp.**


Central Pacific Financial Corp. is a Hawaii-based bank holding company with approximately $7.42 billion in assets as of September 30, 2025. Central Pacific Bank, its primary subsidiary, operates 27 branches and 55 ATMs in the State of Hawaii.
