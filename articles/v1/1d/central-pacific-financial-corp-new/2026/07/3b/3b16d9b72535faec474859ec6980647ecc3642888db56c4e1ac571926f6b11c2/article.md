### Central Pacific Financial Corp. Announces Conference Call to Discuss Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results


July 7, 2026


HONOLULU--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Central Pacific Financial Corp. (NYSE: CPF), parent company of Central Pacific Bank, will release its second quarter 2026 financial results on July 24, 2026, before the market opens. Management will review the results by conference call and live audio webcast beginning at 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time (8:00 a.m. Hawaii Time) on July 24, 2026.


Interested parties may listen to the conference by calling 1-833-461-5787 (Meeting ID: 719 331 929), or by registering for the webcast at the following link:[https://events.q4inc.com/attendee/719331929](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fattendee%2F719331929&esheet=54566258&newsitemid=20260707979841&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fattendee%2F719331929&index=1&md5=75714dd9b0cfedb1014d58fe368ba984) . The Company’s investor relations website,[https://ir.cpb.bank](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.cpb.bank&esheet=54566258&newsitemid=20260707979841&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fir.cpb.bank&index=2&md5=8dee8aeeb40bd0b8e021c30d1c08faff) , will also include a link to the webcast and a slide presentation.


A replay of the call will be available through July 24, 2027, on the Company’s investor relations website.


**About Central Pacific Financial Corp.**


Central Pacific Financial Corp. is a Hawaii-based bank holding company with approximately $7.50 billion in assets as of March 31, 2026. Central Pacific Bank, its primary subsidiary, operates 27 branches and 55 ATMs in the State of Hawaii.
