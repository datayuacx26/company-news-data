# The Modern Data Stack Upgrade: Announcing Rollstack + Lightdash

The "last mile" of analytics—getting data out of dashboards and into the boardroom—shouldn't be a recurring challenge. Today, we are thrilled to announce that **Rollstack is fully integrated with**[Lightdash](https://www.lightdash.com/?utm_source=rollstack&utm_medium=blog&utm_campaign=rollstacklaunch) **.**


This isn't just another connector; it is the meeting of two product philosophies that prioritize flexibility, code-based workflows, and data democratization.


## **The Bridge Between Data and Decisions**


Lightdash has rapidly become the tool of choice for data teams building on dbt. However, while data teams live in Lightdash, business stakeholders live in slide decks.


Rollstack bridges that gap. This integration combines Lightdash’s modern, developer-friendly exploration with Rollstack’s ability to programmatically generate and update PowerPoint, Google Slides, Emails and Documents in a single click.


## **Customer Spotlight: Octopus Energy**


**Octopus Energy** , a global leader in renewable technology, has been a long-time power user of Rollstack.


As Octopus Energy scales, they are transitioning specific data pods from a legacy BI tool to Lightdash to support a massive internal user base without restrictive licensing. However, the Credit Risk team still needed to deliver high-stakes reports to senior leadership. They couldn't just send a dashboard link; they needed the narrative context of a slide deck.


Octopus uses Rollstack to pull Lightdash data directly into Google Slides. This allows their analysts to add the necessary human layer—commentary, risk assessments, and context—ensuring the C-suite gets the full business story, not just the numbers.


> "As we scale our data strategy with Lightdash to support thousands of users, we needed to ensure our executive reporting didn't skip a beat. Rollstack bridges the gap between modern exploration and the boardroom. It allows us to adopt a flexible, code-based BI tool like Lightdash while still delivering the polished, narrative-driven slide decks our leadership relies on."
