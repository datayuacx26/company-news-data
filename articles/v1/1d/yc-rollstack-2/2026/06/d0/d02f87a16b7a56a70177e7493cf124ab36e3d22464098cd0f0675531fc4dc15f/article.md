## What is dbt?


If you're reading this, you probably already know. But for completeness: dbt (data build tool) is the standard transformation layer of the modern data stack. Analytics engineers use it to model, test, and document data inside the warehouse, turning raw tables into the clean, governed datasets that business intelligence tools query downstream.


dbt has become close to non-negotiable for teams running Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks, or Redshift at any meaningful scale. The dbt community is large, the Slack is active, and the ecosystem of tools that sit alongside dbt has grown into[its own category](https://rollstack.com/articles/modern-data-stack-a-new-category-is-born) .


For data teams, dbt solves the trust problem upstream: clean models, documented logic, tested transformations, and governed metrics. But most stakeholders never open a dbt model. They consume board decks, QBRs, operating reviews, investor updates, and client reports.


That's where[Rollstack](https://rollstack.com/) fits. Rollstack sits downstream of the dbt-modeled warehouse and the BI layer, then turns trusted dashboard data into refreshed PowerPoint, Google Slides, Word, and Google Docs outputs, whether that's a board deck, an investor update, or[automated client reporting](https://rollstack.com/articles/how-to-automate-client-reporting) . For teams heading to dbt Summit, the question isn't only "how do we model the data?" It's also "how do we make sure the modeled data reaches the rooms where decisions actually happen?"


## What is dbt Summit, formerly Coalesce?


Coalesce launched in 2020 as the dbt community's annual gathering. The first event ran fully online (pandemic timing), and by Coalesce 2021 attendance had grown to roughly 7,000 practitioners. It became the largest event for analytics engineers worldwide. For 2026, dbt Labs renamed it to **dbt Summit** .


The rebrand reflects what the conference has become. Coalesce launched when the question was "should we use dbt?" The audience now extends well beyond dbt users to anyone working on the analytics layer of the modern data stack, including the AI and data platform teams that have moved into adjacent territory. dbt Labs positioned the rename as a shift from a community conference into a premier industry summit. Same DNA, broader audience.


What stays the same: the practitioner-forward tone, the analytics engineering community at the center, the dbt Slack as the unofficial second venue, and the dbt Labs team running it. What changes: the name, the framing toward AI-native data work, and the scale of the production.


In brief: dbt Summit is the rebranded Coalesce. Same dbt Labs team, same community, same general format and cadence. The name changed in 2026 to reflect a broader positioning beyond the dbt user community toward the analytics engineering and AI-data audience as a whole. If your team still calls it Coalesce, you're not wrong, you're just a year behind on the branding. The hashtags, the Slack, and the muscle memory will take a while to catch up, and that's fine.


## Who should attend dbt Summit 2026?


dbt Summit is most useful for teams that already have dbt in production or are actively standardizing their analytics engineering workflow. dbt Labs frames the program around three audiences: data practitioners, data leaders, and business leaders or executives.


The conference is worth prioritizing for:


- **Analytics engineers** who want practical patterns for testing, documentation, modeling, semantic layers, CI, and dbt project structure.
- **Data engineers and platform teams** working on scale, governance, cost control, migrations, dbt Mesh, Fusion, and AI-ready data architectures.
- **Heads of data and analytics leaders** who need to benchmark team structure, quality standards, and AI readiness against other modern data teams.
- **BI and reporting teams** whose dashboards, metrics, and stakeholder decks depend on dbt-modeled data.
- **Executives and business leaders** who care less about dbt syntax and more about trusted data, faster decisions, and measurable business outcomes.
- **Partners and ecosystem vendors** who sell into the dbt, Fivetran, warehouse, BI, governance, and data-quality ecosystem.


If your team is brand new to dbt, the event is still useful, but spend your budget on the training and certification sessions rather than the advanced architecture talks.

## Agenda Overview


The dbt Summit agenda is live, and the best way to plan your week is by track rather than by session count. The program spans keynotes, breakouts, peer exchanges, hands-on labs, training, and certification. Here are the tracks to prioritize.


### Analytics engineering best practices


Best for analytics engineers and BI-adjacent data teams. Expect practical sessions on project structure, testing, documentation, quality, governance, and how mature teams keep dbt maintainable as usage grows.


### dbt at scale


Best for platform teams, heads of data, and enterprise analytics teams. Prioritize this track if your biggest problems are ownership, permissions, team boundaries, cost, CI, and scaling dbt across multiple domains.


### Winning with Fusion


Best for teams evaluating dbt Fusion or planning a migration path. This is likely one of the most important tracks for existing dbt teams, because it connects product direction to real workflow changes.


### Powering AI-ready data


Best for teams under pressure to support AI, self-service analytics, conversational BI, or governed data products. The useful question here isn't "how do we use AI?" but "is our modeled data trustworthy enough for AI to use?"


### Building agentic data workflows


Best for technical data teams experimenting with MCP tools, copilots, and AI agents that interact with dbt projects. This is the track to watch if your team wants AI-assisted development without losing lineage, metrics, review, and governance.


### Data modernization and emerging architectures


Best for teams migrating legacy pipelines, consolidating warehouses, evaluating mesh patterns, or modernizing around Iceberg and adjacent architectures.


### Delivering business outcomes


Best for data leaders and executives. These sessions are less about syntax and more about how data teams prove ROI, reduce incidents, control spend, and turn trusted data into decisions.


### Sessions worth shortlisting


If you're building your agenda, start with these:


- **Level Up:** product direction and dbt's agentic-era roadmap.
- **Community Keynote:** dbt Core, Fusion, and community direction.
- **Accelerating analytics with AI:** for teams connecting dbt work to AI-assisted analytics.
- **Accelerating dbt with dbt v2:** for existing teams planning an upgrade.

## Practical Tips for dbt Summit Attendees


- **Build your schedule around the labs and breakouts, not the keynotes.** Keynotes will be on the dbt Labs YouTube channel within weeks. The labs and smaller practitioner sessions are the unrepeatable part, and the hands-on labs are historically the most over-subscribed format. Reserve early.
- **Show up in the dbt Slack before you arrive.** The unofficial schedule, side events, and meet-ups all coordinate there. Use the #dbt-summit-irl channel to connect with people in person. If you're not in the dbt Slack yet,[join the dbt community here](https://community.getdbt.com/) .
- **Claim training and certification at checkout.** Free spots are limited and they go before the event, not at the door.
- **Vegas distance is deceptive.** Give yourself 20 minutes between sessions in different parts of the venue, and longer if you're crossing to another hotel for a side event.
- **Layer up.** Las Vegas conference centers are aggressively air-conditioned. Bring a long sleeve or a hoodie even if you're flying in from somewhere warm.
- **Hydrate.** Vegas air is dry and conference air is recycled. Carry a water bottle. Discovery Hall has meals and the Welcome Reception, but the floor doesn't remind you to drink water.
- **Pace your evenings.** The instinct is to do dinner and a late night every night. Don't. Day 3 is when the best community conversations happen, and you'll regret being exhausted for them.
- **Take notes by topic, not by session.** Future-you needs one synthesis page, not six separate sets of notes on incremental models.
- **Talk to people.** The dbt community is one of the more welcoming ones in data. Sit at a table with people you don't know and ask what they're working on. The sessions are great; the hallway conversations are usually better.
