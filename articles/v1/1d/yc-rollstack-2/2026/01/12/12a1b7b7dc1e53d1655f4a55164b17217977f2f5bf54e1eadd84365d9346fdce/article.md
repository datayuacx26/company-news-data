# FabCon 2026 Overview and Attendee Guide: The Ultimate Resource for Power BI Users

**Date:** March 16 - 20, 2026 **Location:** Georgia World Congress Center, Atlanta, GA


If you are a Power BI developer, data analyst, or BI leader, the landscape of your work has likely shifted dramatically over the last few years. Enter **FabCon 2026** (the Microsoft Fabric Community Conference), the premier event that has effectively succeeded the standalone Power BI summits of the past.


With Microsoft Fabric becoming the unified platform for data analytics, this conference is no longer just an optional "add-on" for Power BI users—it is the central hub for the future of business intelligence. Whether you are looking to master **Copilot** , understand **Direct Lake** mode, or compete in the global **DataViz Championship** , this guide covers everything you need to know to maximize your experience in Atlanta.


## **What is Power BI?**


Power BI is the premier business intelligence platform used by organizations worldwide to bridge the gap between data and decision-making. It allows users to transform raw data—whether from Excel, cloud warehouses, or on-premise databases—into coherent, visually immersive, and interactive insights. Its deep integration with the Microsoft stack and robust modeling capabilities have made it the tool of choice for everyone from individual analysts to enterprise data teams.


The Power BI ecosystem is vast, and specialized tools like[Rollstack](https://www.rollstack.com/) are designed to help you extract even more value from your reports. Rollstack connects Power BI directly to PowerPoint or Google Slides, enabling you to automate scaled reports without ever taking another screenshot or building a hard to maintain custom connection. It even schedules your reports to be automatically updated and shared by email. If you work in[analytics](https://www.rollstack.com/teams/data-analytics) ,[customer success](https://www.rollstack.com/teams/customer-success) ,[finance](https://www.rollstack.com/teams/finance) or[operations](https://www.rollstack.com/teams/business-operations) , it is a critical timesaver that keeps your presentations in sync with your data.


## **What is FabCon 2026?**


**FabCon** , short for the Microsoft Fabric Community Conference, is the largest global gathering dedicated to the Microsoft data ecosystem. While it covers the entire Fabric suite (Data Factory, Synapse, Data Activator, etc.), it remains the spiritual home for the Power BI community.


For 2026, the event moves to the **Georgia World Congress Center in Atlanta** , offering five days of deep-dive workshops, keynotes from Microsoft leadership (including Arun Ulag and Kim Manis), and hundreds of community-led sessions.


## **Why Power BI Users Should Attend**


If you live in Power BI Desktop or the Service, you might wonder if a "Fabric" conference is right for you. The answer is a resounding yes. Here is why:


### **1. The Era of "Agentic" Analytics**


The theme of 2026 is the maturity of AI Agents within BI. Sessions will move beyond basic "text-to-visual" demos and explore how **Power BI Copilot** can act as an intelligent agent that proactively surfaces insights, cleans data, and assists in DAX authoring.


### **2. Direct Lake Mastery**


Import mode is evolving. FabCon is the best place to learn how to leverage **OneLake** and **Direct Lake** mode to analyze massive datasets in Power BI with the performance of Import mode but without the refresh latency.


### **3. Career Future-Proofing**


The line between "Power BI Developer" and "Analytics Engineer" is blurring. Attending FabCon allows you to skill up on the upstream tools (Data Engineering, Real-Time Intelligence) that feed your reports, making you a more valuable full-stack data professional.


## **Agenda Highlights for Power BI Professionals**


With over 200 sessions, building your schedule can be overwhelming. We have curated the "can't-miss" tracks and sessions for the Power BI crowd.


### **Key Sessions to Watch**


- **Power BI Mastery: End-to-End Analytics for Pro Developers:** A deep technical dive (Level 400) focusing on TMDL, PBIP, and version control integration for enterprise-scale deployments.
- [Rollstack](https://www.rollstack.com/) **Session in the Expo Theater:** See live how you can update hundreds of slides with fresh data in a single click, maintain pixel-perfect formatting, and ensure your narrative always matches your numbers - directly in the workflows like powerpoint, email and docs that your stakeholders already operate in.
- **Chat with Your Data:** A hands-on look at customizing Copilot to deliver verified answers and specific instructions to your end-users.
- **The DataViz Championship:** Watch the finalists of the global visualization competition battle it out live. This is often the most energetic session of the week, showcasing the absolute limits of UI/UX in Power BI.


### **The Tracks**


- **Power BI Track:** Pure reporting, DAX, modeling, and visualization.
- **Admin & Governance:** Critical for those managing capacities, tenant settings, and security.
- **Dev Experiences:** CI/CD pipelines, git integration, and automation for Power BI files.


## **Networking Events**


Here is where you can connect with peers:


- **Attendee Celebration (Georgia Aquarium):** Scheduled for Monday night (March 16), this is the marquee social event. Network with MVPs and Microsoft engineers against a backdrop of whale sharks and belugas.
- **Community Lounge:** Located in the Expo Hall, this is the spot to find the "Ask the Experts" booths. Bring your toughest DAX problems or data modeling nightmares; the people who built the tools are often there to help you solve them.
- **MVP & Partner Days:** Tuesday (March 17) offers exclusive content for Microsoft MVPs and Partners, providing a sneak peek into the roadmap before the general keynotes.


## **Logistics: Tips for a Successful Conference**


**1. Stay Close to GWCC** Atlanta traffic can be unpredictable. We recommend booking hotels within walking distance of the Georgia World Congress Center or along the MARTA line for easy access.


**2. Bring Your Laptop for Workshops** Unlike standard breakout sessions, the pre-con workshops (March 16-17) are hands-on. If you are signed up for "Getting Started with Fabric and Power BI," you will need your own device to follow along.


**3. Download the App Early** Session registration can fill up for popular Power BI tracks. Download the conference app as soon as it launches to reserve your seat for the most in-demand sessions.


## **FAQ**


**Q: Is FabCon just for developers?** A: No. There are specific tracks for "Business Decision Makers" and "Business Analysts" that focus on strategy, adoption, and ROI without getting lost in code.


**Q: Will sessions be recorded?** A: Typically, general sessions are recorded and made available to attendees after the event. However, workshops and "Power Hour" sessions are often live-only exclusives.


**Q: What is the cost?** A: Pricing varies by tier (Early Bird vs. Standard). Discounts are often available for groups and previous attendees. Check the official registration page for the most current rates.
