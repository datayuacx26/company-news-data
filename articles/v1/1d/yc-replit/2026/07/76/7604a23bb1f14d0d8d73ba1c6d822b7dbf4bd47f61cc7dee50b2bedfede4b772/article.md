A new creative class is rising: people who see an idea in their mind and refuse to wait to make it.

****

### How Replit Design works


*Ambient Intelligence unlocks the designer in all of us*


**Replit Design** re-invents how AI design works, giving every idea a way to arrive in the world with the same energy, ambition, and emotion it had in your mind. At its core is what we call *Ambient Intelligence* .


AI in code has already made the leap from “complete this line of code” to “build this app for me.” Ambient intelligence brings this leap to design. At every step, you’ll see suggested variations and progressions that you can accept with a single click, turning your idea over in different lights until the thing on screen matches (or beats) the thing in your head.


*Templates as ingredients, not just starting points*


We’ve all had moments when an idea runs into a wall. Replit Design is built to get you over it.


Other AI design tools treat templates as a starting point; in Replit Design they’re an ingredient you can add at any moment. Drop one in at any time, from a library of hundreds made by real designers, to bring fresh inspiration into a project mid-flight. Start from scratch, start from a template, or remix several into something new until it feels like yours.


*A moodboard on demand*


Designers collect inspiration for a living: screenshots, saved links, moodboards. Now everyone gets to start there.


We built[Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/) into Replit Design: the world’s largest UI and UX reference library, trusted by over two million designers and updated weekly with how the best designs work right now. And in an industry first, everyone gets access. No Mobbin account required; it’s just there, wired in.


To use it, just describe what you want to create, and Replit Design draws from more than 600,000 real-world screens from over 1,000 apps to turn your idea into a finished app or site.


*Your brand, everywhere, in one click*


AI design today is fragmented. Prompt in one tool, refine in another, publish in a third. Every handoff strips something out: the colors drift, the type resets, the details that made it yours get lost in translation. What arrives at the end is a watered-down version of what you started with.


Replit Design comes with design systems built in. Upload yours or create one from scratch, and everything you make, on every screen, snaps to your brand in a single click.


Just hit publish, people are going to love it.
