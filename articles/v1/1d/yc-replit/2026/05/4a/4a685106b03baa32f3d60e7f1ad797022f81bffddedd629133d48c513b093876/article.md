# Replit Enterprise, Now Self-Serve

Starting today, any organization can purchase Replit Enterprise directly on our website, configure SSO and SCIM, invite their team, and start building production apps, in minutes. No demo requests, no contract negotiations, no waiting.[Get Started now.](https://replit.com/enterprise-wizard)


## Why we built this


Teams evaluating Replit Enterprise kept telling us the same thing: “We have budget. We’re ready. We just want to get started quickly.”


Self-serve Enterprise removes the friction between deciding to buy and actually building, with setup—from signup to a fully provisioned SSO-enabled Enterprise account—taking less time than most sales calls.


## Who is this for?


This offering is best suited for:


**Growing teams that have outgrown Pro-** You are at the 15-seat cap on Pro and need more seats.


**Any team or org that needs Enterprise controls, but wants to move quickly-** You have the budget, want org-wide seats for Replit, and need Enterprise-grade compliance out of the box for IT approvals. You can evaluate the full security posture without scheduling a single call.


If your needs are more complex: over $200K in spend, custom-term contracts, specific procurement requirements, please[contact our sales team](https://replit.com/enterprise) .


## How to get started


The self-serve flow is designed to get you building as quickly as possible while ensuring your org is set up with full enterprise-grade security from day one.


### 1. Choose your credit commitment, get unlimited seats by default


Visit our pricing page and click "[Get Started](https://replit.com/login?source=self-serve-enterprise&blocked=personal_email&goto=/enterprise-wizard/welcome) " under Enterprise. After a few questions about your Enterprise, you will select your annual credit commitment.


Every dollar you commit becomes a dollar in credits to use across Replit Agent, Deployments, Storage, and more, with unlimited seats. Credits are pooled across your entire organization, so you are paying for what your team actually builds rather than for seats that go unused.


### 2. Check out instantly


Pay with a credit card or ACH bank transfer. Annual billing, paid upfront, with a clear summary of exactly what you are getting before you confirm.


### 3. Your workspace is ready immediately


The moment your payment is confirmed, your Enterprise org is provisioned and you land directly in your new workspace, with an option to configure SSO automatically.


For ACH payments, you will start with an initial credit allocation while the bank transfer clears (typically 3 to 4 business days), after which your full credits become available.


## Enterprise controls, configured in minutes


With self-serve Enterprise, you can configure enterprise-grade security directly during onboarding:


- **SSO (SAML):** Set up SSO with Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, or OneLogin right after checkout so your team can authenticate through your existing identity provider from day one.
- **SCIM directory sync:** Automatically provision and deprovision users through your identity provider—no manual user management required.
- **RBAC:** Define granular role-based access controls across your organization.
- **Audit logs:** Get full visibility into workspace activity for compliance and governance.
- **SOC 2 compliance** and **Enterprise-only connectors:** Included out of the box. See the full Enterprise feature set[here](https://replit.com/enterprise)


## Self-serve purchase, followed by white-glove support


Buying self-serve does not mean you are on your own. Every self-serve Enterprise customer is assigned a dedicated **Account Manager from day one** . You will receive a welcome email introducing your AM, after which your AM becomes your single point of contact for anything you need, right from help with SSO configuration, to product support, to scaling your annual commit.


A sales-led deal and a self-serve purchase end up in the same place: a fully supported Enterprise customer with a dedicated relationship.

We built Replit to make software creation accessible to everyone. Self-serve Enterprise extends that same principle to how organizations buy an Enterprise plan. No friction, no gatekeeping, no delays.
