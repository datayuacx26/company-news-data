**Introduction to PPC and PPC Automation**


Pay-per-click (PPC) advertising is a digital advertising model where the advertisers pay a fee each time their ad is clicked as compared to when their ad is being shown or viewed.


In a nutshell, there are numerous steps involved in launching a PPC ad campaign starting with choosing a PPC platform, choosing a PPC platform, defining objectives of the ad campaign, identifying relevant keywords, creating a landing page, setting up a campaign structure, creating ads, setting up budget and bidding, before finally you can launch the campaign.


PPC Automation leverages AI and Machine Learning (ML) to streamline the complexity of PPC campaigns – automatically optimizing strategies, allocating and adjusting budgets, and generating performance reports almost instantaneously. These tools take over repetitive and data-heavy tasks, freeing up the advertisers to focus on higher-value strategic initiatives.
