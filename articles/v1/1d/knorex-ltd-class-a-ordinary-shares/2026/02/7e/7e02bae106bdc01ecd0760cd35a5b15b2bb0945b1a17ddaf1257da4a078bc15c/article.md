##


**What is Agentic AI?**


Agentic AI refers to artificial intelligence systems capable of:


1. Understanding goals
2. Making decisions
3. Executing tasks autonomously
4. Adapting based on new data and feedback


In online advertising, this means AI agents can:


****


1. Launch campaigns
2. Adjust bids and budgets
3. Optimize audiences
4. Retrieve performance data
5. (Re-)allocate spend dynamically


Unlike rule-based automation, agentic AI continuously learns and acts.


##


**The Infrastructure Problem in AI Advertising**


Despite rapid AI innovation, digital advertising remains fragmented. Currently, majority of marketers operate across:


- Google Ads
- Meta Ads
- Amazon Ads
- Linkedin Ads
- TikTok Ads
- programmatic advertising platforms
- and so on ..


Each platform requires separate APIs, integrations, workflows, and optimization logic. Data is also presented and managed in silo, in different formats, different structure and even different metrics too.


For AI agents, this fragmentation creates:


- Engineering overhead
- Ongoing integration maintenance
- Siloed data environments
- Limited scalability


True agentic advertising requires unified infrastructure.
