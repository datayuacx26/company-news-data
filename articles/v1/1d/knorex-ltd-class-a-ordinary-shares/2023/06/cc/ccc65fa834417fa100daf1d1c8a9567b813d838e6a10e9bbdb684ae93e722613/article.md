# Knorex R&D Team’s Research Paper Titled “Towards Weakly-Supervised Hate Speech Classification Across Datasets” Was Accepted to the 7th Workshop on Online Abuse and Harms (WOAH)

We’re delighted to share that Knorex R&D team’s research paper titled “


[Towards Weakly-Supervised Hate Speech Classification Across Datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.02637) ” was accepted to[The 7th Workshop on Online Abuse and Harms (WOAH)](https://www.workshopononlineabuse.com/) ., the leading workshop for detecting and modelling online hateful content. The workshop is colocated with the[61st Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL’23)](https://2023.aclweb.org/) , which will occur in Toronto, Canada, from July 9th to July 14th, 2023.


In this work, Knorex’s lead research scientist (NLP) Vishakha Kadam collaborated with renowned researchers from


[Pompeu Fabra University](https://www.upf.edu/en/) , the top university in Spain, to detect harmful contents online without requiring labeled data. The project is part of Knorex’s continuous dedication to innovative brand safety technologies to protect brands from unsafe content.
