# Is Allerplex Legit? Review, Side Effects & Alternatives

## What Is Allerplex?


Allerplex is a dietary supplement manufactured by Standard Process, designed to support the body's natural response to environmental allergens. It contains a blend of whole food ingredients, vitamins, and minerals that aim to promote healthy respiratory function and immune system balance during allergy season.


### Key Features of Allerplex


The supplement combines several components to address allergy-related concerns:


• Bovine lung PMG extract for respiratory tissue support • Vitamin A and C for immune function • Calcium and magnesium for overall wellness • Fenugreek seed and tillandsia for traditional herbal support


### How It Differs From Traditional Allergy Medications


Unlike[antihistamines](https://www.wyndly.com/blogs/learn/antihistamines-every-day) that block histamine receptors to reduce symptoms, Allerplex takes a nutritional approach. Standard Process markets it as a whole food supplement rather than a pharmaceutical product. This distinction is important because supplements are regulated differently than medications and don't require the same clinical evidence for effectiveness.


## Is Allerplex a Legit Supplement?


Allerplex is a legitimate supplement sold by Standard Process, a company that has been in business since 1929, but it lacks FDA approval and clinical studies proving its effectiveness for allergies. While the company has a long history, the product's efficacy remains unverified by independent scientific research.


### FDA Approval Status


The FDA does not approve dietary supplements like Allerplex before they reach the market. Unlike[antihistamines](https://www.wyndly.com/blogs/learn/xyzal-vs-allegra) that undergo rigorous clinical trials, supplements only need to meet basic safety and labeling requirements. Standard Process manufactures Allerplex in FDA-registered facilities, but this doesn't mean the product has been evaluated for treating allergies.


### Customer Reviews and Testimonials


Customer reviews for Allerplex are mixed across various platforms. Some users report improvements in their allergy symptoms, while others notice no significant changes. Key points from reviews include:


• Positive feedback often mentions reduced congestion and easier breathing • Negative reviews cite lack of noticeable results after several weeks of use • Some customers prefer it over OTC medications due to its natural ingredients • Price concerns appear frequently, as the supplement costs more than standard allergy treatments


## What Are the Ingredients in Allerplex?


Allerplex contains a blend of whole food-based ingredients, including bovine lung PMG extract, vitamin A, vitamin C, calcium, and various proprietary blends derived from animal and plant sources. Standard Process markets these components as supporting respiratory health and the body's natural response to seasonal challenges.


### Key Active Ingredients


The supplement features several notable components:


• **Vitamin A** (1,490 IU) - supports immune function and mucous membrane health • **Vitamin C** (6 mg) - provides antioxidant properties • **Calcium** (40 mg) - derived from bone meal for mineral support • **Bovine lung PMG extract** - a proprietary ingredient claimed to support lung tissue


### Proprietary Blends


Standard Process includes additional proprietary blends containing dried alfalfa juice, buckwheat leaf, and defatted wheat germ. The formula also incorporates bovine adrenal, kidney, and spleen extracts. Unlike[antihistamines](https://www.wyndly.com/blogs/learn/xyzal-vs-benadryl) that target specific pathways, these whole food ingredients work through less defined mechanisms that haven't been clinically validated for allergy relief.


## How Does Allerplex Work?


Allerplex works by providing whole food-based nutrients that Standard Process claims support respiratory health and the body's natural response to environmental challenges. The supplement combines vitamins, minerals, and animal-derived extracts intended to nourish lung tissue, though these mechanisms lack clinical validation for treating allergies.


### Claimed Mechanisms


The bovine lung PMG extract supposedly supports lung tissue health through a process called protomorphology. Vitamins A and C in the formula may offer antioxidant benefits, while calcium supports general cellular function. Unlike[antihistamines that block histamine](https://www.wyndly.com/blogs/learn/what-is-histamine) receptors to reduce symptoms, Allerplex takes a nutritional support approach.


### Limitations of This Approach


Standard Process has not published peer-reviewed studies demonstrating Allerplex's effectiveness for allergy relief. The supplement does not address the underlying immune dysfunction that causes allergic reactions. For proven symptom management, many people turn to[medications like Xyzal or Claritin](https://www.wyndly.com/blogs/learn/xyzal-vs-claritin) that have undergone rigorous clinical testing.


## What Are the Dosing Recommendations for Allerplex?


The standard dosing recommendation for Allerplex is two capsules per meal, taken three times daily, according to Standard Process guidelines. This amounts to six capsules total each day. However, individual dosing may vary based on a healthcare provider's specific recommendations for your situation.


### Important Dosing Considerations


Standard Process suggests taking Allerplex with meals to optimize absorption of the fat-soluble vitamins in the formula. The capsules should be swallowed whole with water. Some practitioners may adjust the dosage based on the severity of symptoms or individual patient needs.


Unlike clinically tested allergy medications such as[antihistamines or nasal sprays](https://www.wyndly.com/blogs/learn/xyzal-vs-flonase) , Allerplex dosing lacks standardized clinical trials to support its recommendations. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider before starting any supplement regimen, especially if you're already taking other medications or have underlying health conditions.


## Who Should Use Allerplex?


Allerplex is marketed toward adults seeking natural support for seasonal allergies and respiratory health. Standard Process designed this supplement for individuals looking for whole food-based alternatives to conventional allergy medications. Those interested in supporting their body's histamine response through nutritional supplementation may consider this product.

### Who Should Not Use Allerplex


Certain groups should avoid taking Allerplex without medical guidance:


• Pregnant or nursing women • Children under 18 years old • Individuals with known allergies to bovine-derived ingredients • People taking blood thinners or other prescription medications • Those with liver or kidney conditions


Anyone experiencing severe allergic reactions, including symptoms like[shortness of breath](https://www.wyndly.com/blogs/learn/shortness-of-breath) or[wheezing](https://www.wyndly.com/blogs/learn/allergy-wheezing) , should seek immediate medical attention rather than relying on supplements. Allerplex is not intended to treat[anaphylaxis](https://www.wyndly.com/blogs/learn/anaphylaxis) or other serious allergic conditions.


## What Are the Potential Side Effects of Allerplex?


The potential side effects of Allerplex are not well-documented due to limited clinical research on this specific supplement. Individual reactions may vary based on sensitivity to the bovine-derived ingredients.


### Commonly Reported Issues


Users have noted the following potential side effects:


• Mild gastrointestinal upset • Nausea when taken on an empty stomach • Allergic reactions in those sensitive to animal-derived products • Headaches in some individuals


### Important Considerations


Because Allerplex contains organ-derived ingredients from bovine sources, individuals with beef allergies should avoid this product entirely. Similar to other[natural allergy supplements](https://www.wyndly.com/blogs/learn/genexa-allergy) , the lack of FDA regulation means side effect profiles aren't as thoroughly studied as prescription medications. ### Do hot showers help allergies?


Hot showers can provide temporary allergy relief by clearing nasal passages through steam inhalation and washing pollen off your skin and hair. However, they don't treat the underlying allergy. Showering after being outdoors is particularly helpful for removing allergens before they spread throughout your home.


### Is there an allergy medicine that actually works?


Yes, several allergy medications effectively manage symptoms. Antihistamines like cetirizine and loratadine reduce sneezing and itching. Nasal corticosteroids like fluticasone treat congestion. However, these only mask symptoms. Allergy immunotherapy addresses the root cause by gradually desensitizing your immune system to specific allergens for long-term relief.
