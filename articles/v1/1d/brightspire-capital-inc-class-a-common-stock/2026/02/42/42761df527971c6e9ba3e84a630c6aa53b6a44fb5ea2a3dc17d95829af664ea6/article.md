# BrightSpire Capital, Inc. Announces Fourth Quarter & Full Year 2025 Financial Results

[PDF Version](https://ir.brightspire.com/static-files/5a59366d-e54f-45c1-9fcc-334b31547ad9)


180.3 KB


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Feb. 17, 2026-- BrightSpire Capital, Inc. (NYSE: BRSP) (“BrightSpire Capital” or the “Company”) today announced its financial results for the fourth quarter and full year ended December 31, 2025 and certain updates. The Company reported fourth quarter 2025 GAAP net loss attributable to common stockholders of ($14.4) million, or ($0.12) per share, Distributable Loss of ($35.5) million, or ($0.28) per share, and Adjusted Distributable Earnings of $19.3 million, or $0.15 per share. For the full year 2025, the Company reported GAAP net loss attributable to common stockholders of ($31.1) million, or ($0.26) per share, Distributable Loss of ($17.5) million, or ($0.13) per share, and Adjusted Distributable Earnings of $83.6 million, or $0.64 per share. The Company reported GAAP net book value of $7.30 per share and undepreciated book value of $8.44 per share as of December 31, 2025.


Michael J. Mazzei, Chief Executive Officer, commented, “The Company had its strongest quarter of originations since restarting back in late 2024, successfully executed on a new CRE CLO and made substantial progress, including subsequent to quarter end, on the resolution of REO and watchlist loans. The reduction in our book value this past quarter was largely driven by the decision to accelerate REO and watchlist resolutions and redeploy the capital.”


Mr. Mazzei continued, “As we look ahead to 2026, our focus remains on growing the portfolio and earnings through new loan originations while continuing to resolve what remains of our REO and watchlist loans.”

**About BrightSpire Capital, Inc.**


BrightSpire Capital, Inc. (NYSE: BRSP) is internally managed and one of the largest publicly traded commercial real estate (CRE) credit REITs, focused on originating, acquiring, financing and managing a diversified portfolio consisting primarily of CRE debt investments and net leased properties predominantly in the United States. CRE debt investments primarily consist of first mortgage loans, which we expect to be the primary investment strategy. BrightSpire Capital is organized as a Maryland corporation and taxed as a REIT for U.S. federal income tax purposes. For additional information regarding the Company and its management and business, please refer to[www.brightspire.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.brightspire.com&esheet=54427435&newsitemid=20260217014723&lan=en-US&anchor=www.brightspire.com&index=4&md5=36bc251d2bd86e3b07329de7963c97d8) .
