# ADT Safe Places

ADT Safe Places is our corporate philanthropic program, which supports high-impact, mission-aligned organizations that advance community safety and resilience. Our approach combines financial contributions, employee volunteerism and in-kind donations of smart security systems to create measurable impact. ADT Safe Places partners include Requity Foundation, Fight Blight Bmore and the YMCA, among others. ADT Safe Places also provides funding to employee-led groups that drive additional volunteer events and donations.
