# ADT to release second quarter 2026 results on July 30

[ADT](http://www.adt.com/) will release its second quarter 2026 financial results before the market opens on Thursday, July 30. Following the release, management will host a conference call at 10 a.m. ET to discuss these financial results and conduct a question-and-answer session.


Participants may listen to a live webcast through the[investor relations website](https://investor.adt.com/) . Alternatively, participants may listen to the live call by dialing 1-833-461-5787 (toll-free) or 1-585-542-9983 and providing the access code 130714042. A replay of the webcast will be available on the website within 24 hours after the event.
