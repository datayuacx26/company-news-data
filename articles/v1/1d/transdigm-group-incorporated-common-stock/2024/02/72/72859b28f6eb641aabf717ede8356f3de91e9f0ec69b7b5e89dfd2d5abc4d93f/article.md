# Spotlight on Adams Rite


February 12, 2024


2624 Views


Adams Rite Aerospace, headquartered in Fullerton, CA, designs and manufactures a wide variety of custom-engineered components and systems for the aerospace industry. With over 125 years of innovation experience, they break new ground in design and manufacturing in order to make products more precise, durable and aesthetically pleasing. From the initial design of each product, through qualification, certification, manufacturing and support, Adams Rite customers always receive the finest solutions available. In order to design and support such a diverse set of products, Adams Rite employs state of the art technology and has a talented team of highly skilled mechanical and electrical engineers dedicated to providing our customers with innovative solutions. Most importantly, the team focuses on working closely together with the customer design team so that they can offer custom solutions to support unique requirements.


In the over 100 years since its founding, the applications for precision engineered products have expanded dramatically, however, one tradition has remained unchanged and that is Adams Rite Aerospace’s commitment to analyzing each customers unique needs and then custom engineering the most reliable, efficient, attractive and cost effective solution.
