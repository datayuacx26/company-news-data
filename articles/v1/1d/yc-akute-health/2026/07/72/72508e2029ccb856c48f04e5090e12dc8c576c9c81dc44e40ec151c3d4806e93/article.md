# Why Consistent Patient Engagement Matters More Than Ever

## Why Consistent Patient Engagement Matters More Than Ever — And How PatientPulse Makes It Easy


Healthcare outcomes don’t just depend on what happens during visits. They depend on what happens between visits — when patients forget labs, delay screenings, miss follow-ups, or simply lose touch with their care plan.


That’s why consistent patient engagement isn’t just helpful — it’s essential. Practices that regularly reach out to the right patients at the right time see better compliance, stronger relationships, and improved quality metrics.


The challenge? Most teams don’t have the time to manually track who needs what.


That’s where[PatientPulse](https://www.akutehealth.com/patientpulse-intelligent-cohorts) comes in.

##### Step 1: Build Smart Cohorts


Cohorts let you group patients based on real data in the chart — so you always reach the right people.


You can filter by:


-


Age or demographics


-


Diagnoses


-


Lab results


-


Health maintenance items


-


Appointment history


-


Free-text chart entries


Examples of cohorts practices create:


-


Patients with A1c > 7 and no recent lab


-


Patients overdue for mammograms or colonoscopies


-


Patients with hypertension or diabetes


-


New patients who joined this week


-


Patients due for annual wellness visits


Cohorts update automatically as patient data changes, so your outreach always stays accurate.


##### Step 2: Create Reusable Message Templates


Once you know who to contact, the next step is deciding what to say. Message templates let you write outreach once and reuse it anytime.


Templates can be used for:


-


Reminders


-


Education


-


Follow-ups


-


Onboarding instructions


-


Preventative care outreach


Examples:


-


“You’re due for your A1c check. Schedule here.”


-


“Please complete your intake forms before your visit.”


-


“You’re due for your annual wellness exam.”


-


“We don’t have a recent colonoscopy on file. Please update us.”


Using templates keeps communication consistent across your team and ensures patients always receive clear, professional messages.


##### Step 3: Automate Your Outreach


This is where PatientPulse really shines. Automations connect your cohort and your message template and send outreach automatically — on your schedule.

Real-time updates based on chart data

New patient welcome messages

Follow-ups after abnormal vitals

Preventative screening reminders


Once an automation is running, PatientPulse continues working in the background — no manual tracking required.
