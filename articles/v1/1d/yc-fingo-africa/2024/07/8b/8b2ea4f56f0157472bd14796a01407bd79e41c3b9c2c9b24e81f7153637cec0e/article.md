Kenya has become a financial technology leader in recent years, thanks to its vibrant and rapidly expanding fintech industry. However, despite these advancements, many young Kenyans still face difficulties in accessing financial services. Traditional banks often turn away this crucial demographic due to their onerous processes and exorbitant fees. This is where Fingo steps in, a digital bank created by and for young Africans with the aim of revolutionizing the way Kenyan youth manage their finances.


Kenya’s financial industry has made remarkable strides, with M-Pesa and other mobile money services setting the standard for financial inclusion. Despite this progress, the formal financial system remains dominated by traditional banks, which often present challenges for young people who want to acquire loans, save money or open accounts.


Opening an account with a traditional bank can require multiple in-person meetings and numerous paper documents, taking anywhere from 48 hours to two weeks. Sending money can cost between 6% to 10% of the total transaction amount, with additional fees for maintaining an account. To access credit, insurance or savings, people often need to open multiple accounts at different institutions, each with its own tedious onboarding process.


These challenges affect the youth of Kenya, who make up a significant portion of the population. With a median age of only 18, Kenya is one of the youngest countries in the world and by 2050, one-third of all youth will live in Africa. Yet, traditional financial institutions lag behind in offering specialized solutions for this dynamic and rapidly growing group.


Fingo was created to tackle these challenges head-on. In collaboration with Ecobank, the largest bank in Africa, Fingo aims to provide inclusive and seamless financial services across the continent. It’s Kenya’s first digital-only bank.

*Key Fingo Features:*


- **Instant Account Opening** : Users can open a bank account in less than five minutes without waiting in line or filling out paperwork.
- **Free Peer-to-Peer Transactions** : Users can send money to friends and family quickly, without incurring expensive transaction fees.
- **Smart Financial Management** : The app empowers users to take control of their financial well-being with tools like smart spending analytics, savings goals and financial education.
- **Community and Support** : Fingo fosters a sense of community by promoting knowledge sharing and peer support, encouraging collaboration in the pursuit of financial independence and simplifying financial topics.


As Kenya continues to innovate in the financial sector, digital banking solutions like Fingo are essential in bridging the gap between traditional banking and the needs of a younger, tech-savvy population. Fingo is more than just a bank, it’s a driving force for economic empowerment and growth, providing accessible, affordable and user-friendly financial services.
