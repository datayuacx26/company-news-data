# Cohu to Present at Upcoming Investor Conferences

## Cohu to Present at Upcoming Investor Conferences


August 12, 2026


[PDF Version](https://cohu.gcs-web.com/node/19896/pdf)


SAN DIEGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 12, 2026-- Cohu, Inc. (NASDAQ: COHU), a global supplier of equipment and services optimizing semiconductor manufacturing yield and productivity, today announced that management will participate at the following investor conferences:


7th Annual Needham Virtual Semiconductor & SemiCap 1x1 Conference
Location: Virtual
August 19, 2026


2026 Jefferies Semiconductor, IT Hardware & Communications Technology Conference
Location: Four Seasons Chicago, Chicago, IL
August 25, 2026


Portfolio managers and analysts should contact their respective banking representatives to schedule a meeting at these conferences.


Presentation materials will be made concurrently available on the Investor Relations section of the Company’s website,[www.cohu.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.cohu.com&esheet=54588011&newsitemid=20260812845965&lan=en-US&anchor=www.cohu.com&index=1&md5=5754ce7f21af24856f4d1dc56491852a) .


**About Cohu:**


Cohu (NASDAQ: COHU) was founded in 1947 and is a global technology leader supplying test, automation, inspection & metrology products, software analytics solutions and services to the semiconductor industry.
