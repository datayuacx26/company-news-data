# Senstar Smart 3D LiDAR. Your questions, our answers.

LiDAR for physical security applications represent a significant shift, moving from simple intrusion detection to high-precision, spatial awareness. Senstar’s Smart 3D LiDAR, with its robust, three-dimension imaging in real-time addresses the most persistent challenges in physical security, including nuisance alarms, environmental conditions, ease-of-deployment, and privacy restrictions.


Since our introduction of Smart 3D LiDAR, Senstar has on-going discussions about the benefits of LiDAR with system integrators and end-users from many industries, including data centers, corrections, logistics, energy, communications and utilities.


**Here’s our Top 10 FAQ about the use of LiDAR in physical security applications.**


## 1. What are the primary use cases for 3D LiDAR?


Key 3D LiDAR applications include protecting fence lines, outdoor assets, building/facade protection, loading docks, gates, and sally ports, as well as indoor high-value assets such as[data center racks](https://youtu.be/7T_NzInWrY4) or[museum artwork](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=csfM24xj8J8) . Its precise “3D virtual zones” detect approaches, intrusions, over-climbing, loitering, and item removal. It is also used to protect and monitor the position of high-value assets like[aircraft](https://youtu.be/79S75S6FmdU?t=62) without requiring physical barriers or permanent fences.


Smart 3D LiDAR provides precision intrusion detection in a wide variety of applications. True volumetric sensing enables the device to track the size, location, and direction of objects, ensuring that only valid threats trigger alarms.


## 2. What is the relationship between Senstar and Blickfeld?


Senstar Technologies Corporation completed the acquisition of Blickfeld GmbH in January 2026, making it a wholly owned subsidiary. This merger lets us combine Blickfeld’s 3D LiDAR innovations with Senstar’s extensive physical security expertise and global reach, working as a team providing specialized 3D sensors for high-security applications.


## 3. What makes Senstar LiDAR technology “solid-state”?


Unlike traditional LiDAR sensors that use rotating laser sources, Senstar’s Smart 3D LiDAR utilizes a silicon MEMS mirror to steer laser beams. This design eliminates mechanical friction and wear. The result is a compact, reliable device with an 11-year MTBF.


## 4. How does the system perform in fog, rain, or snow?


The system uses 905 nm lasers, multiple returns and filters detections based on reflectivity. Because precipitation has much lower reflectivity than solid objects like humans or vehicles, the LiDAR can distinguish between weather noise and real threats. This ensures reliable detection in conditions where traditional cameras and video analytics often fail.


Senstar’s Smart 3D LiDAR technology retains performance under environmental conditions including rain, snow and fog.


## 5. What is the effective detection range of the sensor?


Standard configurations provide a 90×50 degree field of view with a range of 60 to 65 m (197 to 213 ft) for reliable detection of a human (defined object size 150×50 cm, 25% reflectivity). By using “foveation” to concentrate laser points into a narrower area, the range can be extended up to 80 m. Coverage can be extended by adding more units and merging them via its “swarm” functionality into a continuous 3D space.


## 6. Does the LiDAR require an external server for processing?


No, Senstar’s Smart 3D LiDAR is a true edge-based smart device. While other 3D LiDARs require a separate machine for processing the point cloud data, with Senstar’s Smart 3D LiDAR, all point cloud processing and detection logic occur on the sensor itself. This reduces bandwidth by primarily outputting alarm data and eliminates single points of failure, making it a “drop-in” replacement for other security devices.


## 7. How does the technology address privacy and GDPR compliance?


The system generates a 3D point cloud that provides situational awareness without capturing personally identifiable information. Operators can see an object’s size and position with cm/inch accuracy, but facial features remain anonymous. This makes it fully compliant for use in public and private spaces.


## 8. Can multiple Smart 3D sensors be grouped to cover complex areas?


“Swarming” allows multiple sensors to be fused into one common point cloud. This is ideal for protecting 3D volumes like vehicle sally ports or aircraft, as it eliminates shadows from obstructions. Opposing sensors can cover each other’s blind spots for 100% detection coverage.


The Smart 3D LiDAR’s swarm mode combines the point clouds of multiple devices, enabling it to provide true 360 degree protection without shadows or blind spots. In this example, two devices provide complete detection coverage of a sally port at a correctional facility.


## 09. What are the power and connectivity requirements?


The sensors use standard PoE, requiring a maximum of 15 watts. They are IP67-rated for outdoor durability and connect via a single Ethernet port. For non-PoE setups, 12V injectors can be used, and the device integrates via common protocols like ONVIF and RTSP. Its on-board Flow logic engine that enables connections with virtually any web-based protocol.


## 10. How does the LiDAR integrate with existing VMS platforms?


The device integrates via ONVIF and RTSP, appearing in the VMS like a smart camera. It provides a 2D representation of its 3D data for operators to view. In addition, it’s onboard Flow engine enables custom on-device logic, such as cross-referencing alarms with access control.


The Smart 3D LiDAR’s point cloud can be displayed as a 2D RTSP stream within a VMS such as the Senstar Symphony Common Operating Platform.
