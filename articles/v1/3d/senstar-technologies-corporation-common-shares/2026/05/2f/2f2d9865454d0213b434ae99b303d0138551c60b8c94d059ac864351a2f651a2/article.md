# Stopping Copper Theft Before It Stops You

A thief walks away with $300 in scrap copper. You’re left with a massive repair bill, days of customer outages, a regulator’s letter, and a field crew working the weekend. That asymmetry — small reward for them, outsized damage for you — is the whole problem with copper theft. The place to stop it is at the perimeter, before the first strand is cut.


Perimeter security is the difference between a copper-theft *attempt* and a copper-theft induced *outage* .


## Proactive Security Solutions for Critical Infrastructure


Copper theft is not a petty nuisance; it’s a multi-billion-dollar problem that knocks critical services offline and puts both workers and the public at risk. The ASIS Utilities Security Council called it “a 21st century plague,” and surging commodity prices have only made the target on copper bigger. Anywhere copper is exposed, including electrical substations, solar parks, cell tower compounds, construction storage yards, or data center duct banks, it’s a target (in addition to on-site tools and equipment). Vulnerability is highest at sites under construction, when security systems are not yet fully implemented.


The fallout reaches far beyond the cost of the metal. A single severed feeder can blackout a neighborhood, knock 911 service offline, halt a construction schedule, or cost a hyperscale data center over $9,000 per minute in downtime. And modern thieves don’t always know what they’re cutting — fiber optic cables, with their thick protective jacketing, are routinely mistaken for copper. The thief walks away empty-handed; the operator faces hours or days of fusion splicing.


Sites with large quantities of cables, even fiber optic spools, are at risk from copper theft. This electrical storage site is protected with the Senstar LM100, a hybrid sensor that combines intrusion detection with intelligent lighting.
