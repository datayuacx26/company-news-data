# Senstar Achieves ISO/IEC 27001 Certification, Reinforcing Cybersecurity Commitment When Delivering Physical Security Solutions

**Global leader in physical security solutions strengthens its data protection , risk management, and security of its information systems.**


Ottawa, Ontario, October 31,


2025 —


Senstar


Corporation, a leading provider of physical security and advanced sensing technologies, announces it has achieved


ISO/IEC 2700


certification,


demonstrating


the company’s commitment to the highest standards of information security management. This globally recognized certification validates


Senstar’s


systematic approach to protecting sensitive customer data


and


maintaining


robust cybersecurity protocols across its operations.


**Cybersecurity: Essential to Physical Security Mission**


In today’s interconnected security landscape, cybersecurity and physical security are inseparable. As Senstar provides mission-critical intrusion detection and video surveillance technologies to critical infrastructure, data centers, correctional facilities, energy producers, and transportation hubs like airports, the protection of its internal information assets is paramount.


“Achieving ISO 27001 certification reflects our understanding that physical security systems are only as strong as the cybersecurity framework and organizations that support them,” said Fabien Haubert, Senstar CEO. “Our customers rely on us to protect their most sensitive facilities—from nuclear power plants to airports to data centers. They need assurance that our internal systems, data practices, and information management meet the most rigorous international security standards.”


**Comprehensive Information Security Management**


ISO 27001 is the international standard for information security management systems (ISMS), requiring organizations to demonstrate systematic management of sensitive company and customer information. The certification process involves rigorous evaluation of:


- Information security policies and procedures


- Risk assessment and treatment methodologies


- Asset management and access controls


- Physical and environmental security measures


- Operations security and network management


- Incident management and business continuity planning


- Compliance with legal and contractual requirements


**Integrated Security Approach**


ISO 27001 certification strengthens Senstar’s ability to deliver comprehensive security solutions. “Modern security threats don’t recognize boundaries between physical and cyber domains, nor between security solution providers and their customers” Haubert added. “Our ISO 27001 certification ensures that customers receive solutions supported by world-class information security practices.”


**Building Customer Confidence**


The certification provides Senstar’s customers with independent verification that the company maintains:


- Systematic protection of confidential customer information


- Secure product development and deployment processes


- Robust vendor and supply chain security management


- Documented incident response and recovery capabilities


- Continuous improvement of information security practices


**About Senstar**


With innovative perimeter intrusion detection systems (including fence sensors, buried sensors, and above ground sensors), intelligent video management, video analytics, and access control, Senstar offers a comprehensive suite of proven, integrated solutions that reduce complexity, improve performance and unify support. For over 40 years, Senstar has been safeguarding people, places and property for organizations around the world, with a special focus in utilities, logistics, corrections, and energy markets.
