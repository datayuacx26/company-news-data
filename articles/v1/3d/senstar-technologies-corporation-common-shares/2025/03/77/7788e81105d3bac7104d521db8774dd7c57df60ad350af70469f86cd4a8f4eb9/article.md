# Maximizing Your Time at ISC West 2025

ISC West is the premier event for the physical security industry, bringing together professionals, manufacturers, and system integrators to explore the latest advancements in security technologies. With so many vendors and so much to explore, it’s useful to think about your security goals and what you want to learn and see. Here are five key questions to ask yourself to get the most out of your attendance.


#### **1.**


**Do you have specific security problems you want to solve?**


Before the show, take a moment to define your security challenges. Are you looking to secure a specific site, enhance your general industry knowledge, or find solutions for a specific problem? A bit of pre-show thinking will help you identify vendors who offer relevant solutions while avoiding distractions from technologies that may not be a good fit.


#### **2. Does the vendor understand the problem space?**


A reliable security partner doesn’t just sell products—they understand the complexities of physical security. When speaking with vendors, assess their knowledge of industry challenges. Do they have experience with environments like yours, whether it’s critical infrastructure, corrections, transportation centers, borders, government facilities, and so on? A well-established company like


[Senstar has relevant experience and deep institutional knowledge](https://senstar.com/security-digest/what-should-integrators-consider-when-partnering-with-manufacturers/) , and can provide valuable insights beyond just technology.


#### **3. Does the vendor push a single solution, or do they recommend the best technology for the job?**


Security threats are diverse, different sites have different requirements, and no single technology can address every challenge. Some vendors specialize in one product and try to fit it into all scenarios, while others offer a


[broad portfolio and recommend the right tool for the job](https://senstar.com/security-digest/why-a-broad-security-portfolio-matters/) . Ask us how Senstar can solve your security challenges with a flexible, scalable,


[future-proof](https://cac-word-edit.officeapps.live.com/we/future) solution tailored to your specific needs.

#### **5. Can the vendor show you their products in action?**


Seeing is believing. Ask vendors if they have live demonstrations or real-world applications of their products. Hands-on demos provide a better understanding of how a solution works and whether it meets your operational requirements. Many exhibitors offer interactive experiences—take advantage of these opportunities to evaluate performance and ease of integration.
