CEO John Heller and Loren Jones, Senior Vice President, at the Amentum booth.


The[Farnborough International Airshow 20](https://www.farnboroughairshow.com/)26 has been the perfect stage for spotlighting Amentum’s 70 years of unparalleled expertise supporting UK defence programs and enabling allied readiness around the globe.


At the forefront of our participation is[CEO, John Heller,](https://www.amentum.com/executive-leadership/john-heller/) who paid a visit to the Amentum booth and emphasized the company’s dedication to addressing the most pressing advanced engineering and technology challenges of our time. Heller also spent quality time with members of the Amentum team, including[Loren Jones](https://www.amentum.com/news/amentum-appoints-loren-jones-managing-director-and-senior-vice-president-of-the-international-business-line-for-its-national-security-group/) , Senior Vice President of Amentum International.


Illy Andrews and Justin Goldberg, joined Vago Muradian on the Defense & Aerospace Report podcast.


Another highlight was a series of Amentum media interviews at the Airshow. Illy Andrews, Vice President for Defence International, and Justin Goldberg, Vice President International Defense & Security, joined[Vago Muradian](https://defaeroreport.com/about/vago-muradian/) on the Defense & Aerospace Report podcast for an engaging chat about sustaining defence infrastructure, advancing hypersonic strike technology, and fortifying supply chain resilience.


We invite you to join the conversation! If you missed us at Farnborough or would like to learn more about Amentum’s solutions, visit[our website](https://www.amentum.com/) or reach out to our team directly. Together, we can advance the future and shape the future of defence innovation.
