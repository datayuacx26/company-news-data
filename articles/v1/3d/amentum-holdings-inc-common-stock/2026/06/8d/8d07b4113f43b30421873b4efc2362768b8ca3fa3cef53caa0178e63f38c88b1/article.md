Savannah River Site (SRS), South Carolina


**Chantilly, VA., June 11, 2026** – Amentum (NYSE: AMTM), a global leader in advanced engineering and technology solutions, has been awarded a contract by Savannah River Nuclear Solutions, LLC (SRNS) to provide expert design and engineering services for the Tritium Finishing Facility Project at the Savannah River Site.


“As a trusted partner to the National Nuclear Security Administration, Amentum is excited to deliver innovative solutions that support the Tritium Finishing Facility Project,” said Mark Whitney, President of the Energy & Environment Group. “This award highlights our ongoing commitment to advancing nuclear deterrence and the modernization of our national security facilities for our SRNS customer.”


Under the project, Amentum will oversee the development of the preliminary and final design for the Tritium Finishing Facility. Amentum’s extensive experience in nuclear facilities design and engineering facilities reinforce its position as a key player in supporting national security initiatives.
