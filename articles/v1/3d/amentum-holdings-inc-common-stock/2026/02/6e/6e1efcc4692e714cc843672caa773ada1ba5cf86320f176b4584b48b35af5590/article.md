**What is your role at Amentum?**


I am responsible for the Reliability, Infrared Drone, and Cost Savings programs


**Describe a typical workday at Amentum for you (Day in The Life):**


My typical day starts with approving cost savings projects submitted from our many commercial client sites. I also spend time reviewing root cause analysis (RCA) investigation projects and training personnel on the various Reliability processes. Sometimes I visit our client sites to provide training and assistance on various reliability related equipment failures or to perform drone inspections of client buildings looking for water intrusion and energy efficiency losses.


**What do you enjoy about working for Amentum and what do you find most rewarding?**


I find two things to be the most rewarding about working for Amentum. First, I get to work with a great team at the corporate level who are experts at their jobs and have great attitudes. The second major reward is the variety of technical challenges I get to assist with. With nearly 100 client locations and 10’s of thousands of different assets there is always a new reliability engineering challenge to face.


**What inspired you to pursue a career in engineering, and how has it shaped your journey at Amentum?**


After 8 years in the U.S. Navy as a Nuclear Reactor Operator on submarines, I discovered that I enjoyed understanding how machines work and not only how to fix them, but how to proactively find and prevent a failure from coming back. This led me to pursue Reliability Engineering. My career with Amentum has allowed me to expand my knowledge and share my experience and training with others
