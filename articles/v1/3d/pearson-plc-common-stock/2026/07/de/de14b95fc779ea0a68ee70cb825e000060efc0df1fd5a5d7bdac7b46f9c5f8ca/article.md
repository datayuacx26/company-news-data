# Pearson Statement on SATs Results in England

16 Jul 2026 published at 13:09 PM BST


Pearson confirms that this year's SATs results are available to schools.


We are sorry that the results were not delivered to the original timetable. We know how important SATs are to pupils, parents, and teachers, and recognise the disruption and uncertainty the delay caused to our government partners, teachers, and families.


We are grateful to our markers for their dedication and important work throughout this process, and thank everyone affected for their understanding while we worked to deliver this year's results.


Pearson will cooperate with the review announced by the Department for Education and will engage constructively throughout the process.
