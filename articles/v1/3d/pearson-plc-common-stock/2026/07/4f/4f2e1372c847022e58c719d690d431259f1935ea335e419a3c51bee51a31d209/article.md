# Pearson Revibe Wins CODiE Award for Helping Students Focus and Learn

17 Jul 2026 published at 09:17 AM BST


**AI-Powered Wearable Named 2026 Best Student Learning Capacity-Building Solution**


**HOBOKEN, N.J. — July 17, 2026—**[Pearson](https://plc.pearson.com/en-GB) (FTSE: PSON.L) has won a 2026 CODiE Award for[Pearson Revibe](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/campaign/revibe.html) ®, its AI-powered wearable that helps students build focus and attention— foundational skills for learning. Revibe was named **Best Student Learning Capacity-Building Solution** .


The[CODiE Awards](https://codieawards.com/winners) are the only peer-reviewed awards program to recognize excellence and innovation across education and business technology.


**Why it matters:** For many learners, the challenge isn't comprehending the material; it's staying engaged long enough to benefit from it. Over[seven million](https://www.cdc.gov/adhd/data/index.html) children in the U.S. are living with focus challenges, and millions more struggle with attention in a world of distractions. Educators and families are looking for new ways to help these students while minimizing both stigma and classroom disruption.


**The innovation:** Invented by a school psychologist and brought to scale through Pearson, Revibe combines learning science, behavioral[research](https://www.pearsonassessments.com/en-us/Store/Professional-Assessments/Behavior/Revibe%2C-Pearson%E2%80%99s-wearable-technology/p/P100112000?format=KITS&tab=resources) and AI to help students stay focused. Delivered through a Samsung Galaxy Watch7, Revibe steps in when attention drifts.


- Worn on the wrist, Revibe provides discreet vibration cues and reminders that encourage learners to refocus in the moment.
- Embedded AI learns individual behavior patterns and adjusts prompts over time.
- Behavioral insights in the Revibe app give learners, educators, clinicians and caregivers a clear picture of attention and progress, so they can tailor care accordingly.


**Why it won:** Pearson Revibe stood out for its use of learning science, AI-powered personalization and actionable insights that foster greater student ownership, engagement and time on task, hallmarks of the award category. 2026 CODiE judges described Revibe as "an absolute tool for focus," and that it "merges psychological science with enterprise-grade consumer hardware to tackle the classroom's greatest contemporary barrier…”


**An Ed Tech seal of approval:** "The 2026 CODiE Award winners represent some of the most innovative and impactful solutions in the industry," said Jennifer Baranowski, president of the CODiE Awards. "These organizations are solving meaningful challenges, delivering measurable outcomes, and helping shape the future of technology."


Winners are selected through a rigorous evaluation process led by independent industry experts who assess each solution based on innovation, functionality, market impact, and overall value.


**Clay Richey, managing director, Clinical Assessment at Pearson, said,** "This recognition validates an idea central to how we approach clinical assessment: helping students succeed means understanding and addressing the barriers that stand in the way of learning, then empowering them, and the educators, clinicians and families who support them, with practical tools to make progress. We're proud to see that approach affirmed by the CODiE Awards."
