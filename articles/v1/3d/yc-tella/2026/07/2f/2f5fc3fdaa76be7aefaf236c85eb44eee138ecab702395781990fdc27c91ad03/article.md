## How to choose screen recording software


Start from what happens *after* you hit stop:


- **You want a finished, shareable video with minimal work.** Pick an all-in-one tool with real editing and hosting — this is where[Tella](https://www.tella.com/) is strongest, and ScreenPal is the budget option.
- **You just need free, unlimited local capture.** OBS Studio, no contest. Be ready to bring your own editor and video host.
- **You send quick internal updates all day.** Loom or Cap — record, get a link, paste it.
- **You're on a Chromebook or can't install apps.** Screencastify, or Tella's web recorder — it[records your Chromebook screen with audio](https://www.tella.com/record/screen-recorder-chromebook-with-audio) right from the browser; see also our[Chrome screen recorder](https://www.tella.com/chrome) page.
- **You make cinematic Mac-only product demos.** Screen Studio, or Tella if you also want hosting, analytics, and Windows/web support.
- **You record games or need maximum frame rates.** Bandicam on Windows.

## FAQ


### What is the best screen recording software in 2026?


It depends on the job. For an all-in-one tool that records, edits with AI, and hosts on a shareable page, Tella is our pick. For free, unlimited local recording and streaming, OBS Studio is the standard. For polished macOS demos, Screen Studio is excellent, and for quick async work messages, Loom is popular.


### What is the best free screen recorder?


OBS Studio is the best fully free option for unlimited local recording, though it has no editor or hosting. ScreenRec, ScreenPal, and Screencastify all offer free plans with built-in sharing; note that several free plans add recording-length limits (for example 30 minutes on Screencastify and 15 minutes on ScreenPal). Tella doesn't have a free plan, but every new account starts with a 7-day free trial.


### Which screen recorder works on both Mac and Windows?


Tella, OBS, Loom, ScreenRec, Cap, Camtasia, Bandicam, and ScreenPal all run on both Mac and Windows. Screen Studio is macOS-only, and Screencastify runs as a Chrome extension rather than a native desktop app.


### What is the best screen recorder with built-in editing?


Tella, Camtasia, Screen Studio, Cap, and ScreenPal all include editing. Tella and Screen Studio lean on automatic editing (AI cuts, auto-zoom, auto-layouts) so you can go from raw recording to finished video quickly, while Camtasia offers the deepest manual timeline.


### Do I need to download screen recording software?


Not always. Tella records in the browser as well as via native Mac and Windows apps, and Screencastify runs in Chrome. Others such as OBS, Screen Studio, Camtasia, and Bandicam require a desktop download.
