Yes, Google has a Loom-like product for recording your screen. Built into the Google Workspace package is Google Vids. But, as Sharon Powills states in her[LinkedIn article](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/google-vids-new-loomand-ones-using-sharon-powills-4bt2c#:~:text=The%20landscape%20of%20business%20communication,to%20keep%20paying%20for%20Loom.) , nobody is using it.
