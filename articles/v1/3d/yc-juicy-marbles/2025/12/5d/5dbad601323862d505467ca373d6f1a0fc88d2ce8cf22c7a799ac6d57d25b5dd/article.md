# Orange and Whisky-Glazed Plant-Based Ribs

### Why You’ll Love This Recipe


-


**Sweet and Savoury** **** - A perfectly balanced combination of sweet and tangy orange jam, spicy ginger, and savory plant-based ribs.


-


**Easy To Prepare** **** - This quick and easy recipe is a stress-free festive dish to keep in your back pocket for the upcoming holiday season.


### Key Ingredients for Orange and Whiskey-Glazed Ribs


****


-


**Baby Ribs** pair well with the sweet and tangy glaze and turn perfectly tender in the oven.


-


**Orange Jam**


brings a strong orange aroma, sweetness, and finger-licking stickiness to the glaze.


-


**Whisky**


adds depth and smokiness.


-


**Ginger** brings spice.


-


**Apple Cider Vinegar**


adds tang.

### How To Prepare Orange and Whiskey-Glazed Ribs


****


#### 1. Prepare the Orange and Whisky Glazed


****


Combine orange jam, minced garlic clove, grated ginger, olive oil, apple cider vinegar, salt, pepper, and whisky in a small bowl and whisk.


#### 2. Glaze the Vegan Ribs


Remove the ribs from the packaging and sprinkle with salt on both sides.


Brush the ribs racks with the orange and whisky glaze and place on a parchment-lined baking sheet.


****


#### 3. It’s Time to Bake


****


Bake the ribs in a preheated oven for 25 minutes at 374°F/190°C, until sizzling and caramelized. Remove from the oven, and brush with the remaining glaze. Slice into individual ribs, and serve immediately.


****

### Tips & Tricks


-


**Use Fresh Ingredients** .


Fresh garlic and ginger are more fragrant than dried.


-


**Let It Caramelize**


**.** Give vegan ribs time to caramelize in the oven for extra flavour.


****


-


**Turn It Into a Meal** . Serve the ribs with crunchy salad, crispy potatoes and extra roasted vegetables for a complete meal.


### FAQ


****


#### Can I prepare this recipe without alcohol?


Yes, leave out the whiskey to make this recipe alcohol-free.


#### Is this recipe gluten-free?


Yes, just make sure you are using gluten-free Whiskey (some might contain gluten due to flavoring or barrel cross-contamination).


****


*Note: while Marbles Meats aren’t gluten-free certified, we do not use gluten in our recipe.


#### Can I prepare this ahead of time?


****


Yes, you can prepare the glaze and brush the ribs ahead of time. Store them in the fridge until you are ready to bake.


****


#### Can I use dried ginger and garlic instead of fresh?


You can swap fresh ginger and garlic for dried, but the result will be slightly different, since fresh ingredients are more fragrant.


****


#### What should I serve these ribs with?


****


Serve orange and whisky-glazed vegan ribs with crispy roasted potatoes, charred brussels sprouts, and crunchy salad.
