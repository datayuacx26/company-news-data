# MNTN Bets on AI to Power TV Growth

[News](https://mountain.com/news/)[Press](https://mountain.com/press/)


MNTN CEO Mark Douglas joined Bloomberg Open Interest to talk about the company’s outlook after a post-earnings stock drop. Douglas points to strong margins, record growth, and leadership in AI-driven performance TV. He unveiled QuickFrame Creative A.I., a platform that lets brands create “studio-quality” video ads in minutes. Douglas says MNTN’s AI expansion creates — not replaces — creative jobs.
