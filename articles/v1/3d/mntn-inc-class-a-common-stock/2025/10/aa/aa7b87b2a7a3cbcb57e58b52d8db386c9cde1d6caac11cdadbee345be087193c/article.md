# MNTN CEO Mark Douglas Introduces QuickFrame AI

# MNTN CEO Mark Douglas Introduces QuickFrame AI


Mark Douglas | 3 Min Read


[Product News](https://mountain.com/product-news/)


The TV industry is geared toward the top 200 advertisers. Companies with big budgets and big ad agencies. MNTN is built for everyone else.


MNTN is focused on the millions of other small and medium-sized businesses. In fact, 97% of advertisers on MNTN have never run a TV ad before. They want to use the power of TV to drive returns, yet there’s one pain point we hear from them more than any other:


**How do I make a TV ad?**


Not having assets to upload is often the biggest pain point for our customers. This is exactly the reason I am so proud to announce the beta launch of[QuickFrame AI](https://mountain.com/organic/quickframe-ai) **,** an all-in-one video production platform that lets anyone create complete, studio-quality ads for TV, social and more, in minutes.


## **QuickFrame AI**


QuickFrame AI is a single workspace that unites the best in generative AI. Google’s Veo and Imagen for video, ElevenLabs, and WellSaid Labs for voice, Stability AI, and more. These integrations aren’t just stitched together, they work in concert to script, generate, and voiceover entire commercials in a single, seamless creative environment.


There’s no need to juggle tools, export files, or rebuild creative in other editing platforms. Everything from idea to final cut happens in one place.


**Creating a high-quality commercial used to require weeks, large teams, and big budgets. Now it takes about 12 minutes.**


How It Works


**1. Generate Your Ad |** Choose your style, drop in your brand URL, and QuickFrame AI will generate a complete video ad from scratch, no pre-built assets or script needed.


**2. Edit and Polish |** Use Story View to adjust lighting, camera moves, and scene-level timing. Swap in stock footage, tweak voiceovers and music, and fine-tune copy or visual effects in our Timeline Editor. Share drafts with collaborators or hand off to a creative pro.


**3. Publish and Launch |** Export directly to MNTN Performance TV, YouTube, Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, or TikTok – pre-formatted for every platform.


Because all of this is also all so new, QuickFrame AI also lets you partner with one of our QuickFrame 5,000+ vetted video professionals to help create the ad for you. They’re available for collaboration, refinement, or fully managed production support.


We built QuickFrame AI because video is still the most powerful format in advertising – and we knew it could be faster, smarter, and more accessible. Stay tuned for examples and updates. As with all things AI, our platform and excitement expands by the day.


Lastly, CONGRATULATIONS to the HUNDREDS of talented engineers, developers and artists who helped make today possible. As an engineer, I know how hard it is to turn bold ideas into real, working technology. The fact that this team did it—and made it this seamless—is something I’m incredibly proud of.
