# MNTN’s Anna McMurphy Named an ADWEEK 2026 Architect of Culture

[News](https://mountain.com/news/)[Press](https://mountain.com/press/)


Anna McMurphy, Chief People Officer at MNTN, has been recognized as one of ADWEEK’s 2026 Architects of Culture — an inaugural award honoring senior leaders across marketing, advertising, and technology who are building workplaces where people can learn, thrive, and make a real impact.


Over more than 15 years at MNTN, McMurphy has grown alongside the company with leadership roles across finance, accounting, and HR. That end-to-end perspective has helped her shape a culture that expects high performance grounded in a focus on health, belonging, and growth.


McMurphy has helped foster connection through initiatives including MNTN Cares, which supports local communities where MNTN holds its annual gatherings, and Summit to Success, a program connecting current team members with the future of the industry


MNTN is proud to celebrate McMurphy’s recognition, and the people-first culture she helps lead every day.
