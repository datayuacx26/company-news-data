## **Haven’t Tried Effects Yet? Let’s Get You Up to Speed**


Effects is a feature inside[QuickFrame AI](https://quickframe.com/ai/) that lets any advertiser, regardless of budget or AI experience, produce eye-catching, scene-level video creative in seconds.


Instead of generating and regenerating a scene in hopes the output produces the desired visuals, Effects gives you a curated gallery of pre-designed video effects to choose from. Each effect serves as a powerful visual starting point: a specific style, look, or action that you can then make your own. Once you’ve found the right effect, you simply upload a reference image and QuickFrame AI places your brand directly into the scene.


Adding effects — seasonal or otherwise — in QuickFrame AI is easy:


1. In Story View, choose a scene and hit replace
2. Click “Video Effects” and then browse through the options
3. Select the effect that fits your story
4. Upload a reference image to anchor your product or brand asset inside the moment
5. Hit generate to add the new scene and effect into your ad


QuickFrame AI is creative control without the complexity: more intentional outputs, less guesswork, and a workflow that moves as fast as your campaign needs to.
