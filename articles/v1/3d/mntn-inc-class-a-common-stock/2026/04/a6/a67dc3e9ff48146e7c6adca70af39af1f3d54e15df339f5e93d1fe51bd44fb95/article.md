# Why Supply Quality Matters More in Streaming

[Product News](https://mountain.com/product-news/)


Streaming is different. It’s lean-back, high-attention, and premium by nature, but only if the underlying supply actually delivers on that promise.


Enter MNTN’s curated approach to inventory.


With MNTN Performance TV, advertisers run across a carefully selected set of premium streaming services: the kind that attract passionate, loyal viewership. MNTN prioritizes consistency and quality, giving every impression a better shot at driving the results that performance marketers need. Years of experience have proven that better quality impressions lead to better quality results.


### **Built on Transparent, Trusted, Premium Supply**


Premium performance needs the right partners behind the scenes, ready to connect advertisers with high-quality inventory the moment MNTN detects their audiences are watching.


MNTN works with supply partners, including[Index Exchange](https://www.indexexchange.com/) , to secure direct, transparent paths to publishers. That reduces risk, limits unnecessary intermediaries, and helps ensure each impression reaches a real viewer.


Together, MNTN and Index Exchange make sure inventory is:


- Authenticated and authorized, using standards like ads.txt, sellers.json, and schain
- Directly connected to publishers, cutting complexity and limiting risk
- Fully transparent, so buyers can see exactly where inventory comes from


Index Exchange brings to the table rigorous publisher onboarding, continuous monitoring, and high standards, all of which complements MNTN’s own curation model.


What advertisers get in return is a stronger foundation for performance: inventory you can trust before you even place a bid.


### **Filtering Out Noise Before It Impacts Results**


Low-quality supply wastes spend and distorts performance. Invalid traffic, arbitrage-driven inventory, and other sketchy environments can inflate impressions and clicks without driving meaningful outcomes. That makes it harder to understand what’s actually working.


MNTN works closely with Index Exchange to tackle quality issues upstream, before inventory ever enters the auction. This includes:


- pre-bid invalid traffic filtering
- ongoing supply monitoring and audits
- strict standards for publisher inclusion


By pairing Index Exchange’s supply-side safeguards with MNTN’s curated marketplace, advertisers get cleaner data signals and performance they can actually rely on


### **Cleaner Supply Paths, Stronger Outcomes**


In programmatic advertising, how you access inventory matters just as much as the inventory itself. Every additional intermediary can introduce fees, latency, and risk. That’s why supply path optimization has become essential for performance marketers.


Index Exchange prioritizes direct, transparent connections to media owners, reducing needless complexity. This approach:


- reduces hidden fees
- improves bid efficiency
- eliminates exposure to spoofed or misrepresented inventory
- delivers more consistent campaign outcomes


Simply put: cleaner supply paths, better performance.


### **Where Your Ads Actually Run (And Why That Matters)**


All this work makes it easier to answer a simple question: where exactly will my ads show up?


With MNTN Performance TV, the answer is crystal clear. Your ads run across a curated set of 150+ premium streaming publishers, alongside content people actively choose to watch on well-established platforms, not in questionable or low-value environments. In fact, over the years MNTN has intentionally narrowed the number of publishers it makes available because the long tail simply doesn’t perform as well.


MNTN’s audience-first approach reaches the right customers wherever they’re streaming, keeping campaigns flexible and always focused on performance. On top of that, you’ll get clear visibility into exactly where your campaigns are running, so there are no surprises about where your spend is going.


The result of this combination of MNTN and Index Exchange? Outcomes that actually reflect real viewer behavior.


### **Premium Supply Is the Foundation, Performance Is the Outcome**


Streaming advertising performance doesn’t happen by accident. It starts with premium, transparent supply, enabled by partners like Index Exchange, and is activated through MNTN’s curation and performance-driven approach. MNTN builds on that foundation by focusing every one of those premium impressions on reaching the right audience.


That’s because in streaming, where your ad runs is what makes performance possible.
