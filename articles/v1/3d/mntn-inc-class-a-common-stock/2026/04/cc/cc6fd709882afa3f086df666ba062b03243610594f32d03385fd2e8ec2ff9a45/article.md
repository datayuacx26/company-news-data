# The State of Healthcare Marketing in 2026

Healthcare marketers are facing a defining moment: budgets are under scrutiny, privacy regulations are reshaping data collection, and the pressure to prove ROI has never been greater. Yet most organizations remain in the dark about what actually matters: booked appointments, patient acquisition costs, and downstream revenue impact. Based on survey data from 200 healthcare marketing leaders across providers, payers, DSOs, and urgent care organizations, this report maps where teams are winning, where they’re stuck, and what separates organizations building measurable, scalable growth engines from those flying blind.
