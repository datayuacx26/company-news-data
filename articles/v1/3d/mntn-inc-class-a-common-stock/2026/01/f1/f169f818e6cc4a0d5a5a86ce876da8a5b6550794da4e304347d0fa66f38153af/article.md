## **How Effects Works**


Effects helps teams generate more intentional variation: polished scene-level moments that are easier to build, iterate on, and keep consistent with your brand. That means you can move from concept to usable creative faster, while still maintaining the look and feel you want.


Effects is available directly in QuickFrame AI’s Story and Editor views, with an Effects Gallery that gives you visual starting points for what you want to create. One of our current favorites is “Bullet Time”, which lets you freeze dynamic moments in time, with objects suspended mid-air amidst exploding water droplets, perfect for showcasing products with stylized, action-forward flair.


Here’s a quick breakdown of how you can use Effects:


1. In the Story or Editor view, click Replace, and then select “Generate with Effects.”


2. Choose a pre-designed effect from the gallery. Effects are grouped into categories like Product, Location, Character to make discovery simple. You can also apply transition effects, filter effects and more.


3. Once you’ve selected an effect, provide the required inputs (these vary depending on the effect). You may also upload an optional reference image, like a product shot or brand asset, to insert your brand directly into the selected scene.


4. Generate your scene, and watch the effect come to life instantly.


Because Effects is designed to be explored, you can browse, regenerate, and iterate until you find a look that fits your brand and the story you’re trying to tell.
