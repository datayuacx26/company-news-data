# Live Streaming Ads Get a Performance Upgrade With Magnite and MNTN Integration

[News](https://mountain.com/news/)[Press](https://mountain.com/press/)


*The following contains a snippet from an EMARKETER article:*


Sell-side advertising company Magnite and performance TV marketing platform MNTN announced a new integration Thursday to make live streaming TV ads available to performance-centric advertisers.


- MNTN advertisers will now have access to high-engagement, on-demand streaming content, premium live sports, and breaking news coverage through Magnite’s media owner relationships, per the announcement.
- The partnership also enables MNTN advertisers to take advantage of Magnite’s[programmatic-first approach](https://content-na1.emarketer.com/live-tv-goes-programmatic-with-magnite-s-new-scheduler-tool) to effective streaming ad formats, including pause ads and home screen ads.


**Addressing core issues:** Magnite notes that 97% of MNTN advertisers have never advertised on TV before using MNTN. This integration addresses this gap by expanding access to in-demand inventory, ensuring advertisers can capitalize on the shift to streaming and the high potential of TV ads airing during live moments.


- Viewers find that ads during[live broadcasts are more tolerable](https://chart-na1.emarketer.com/270289/tolerance-ads-on-live-tv-vs-on-demand-among-us-tv-viewers-by-age-nov-2024-of-respondents-each-group?_gl=1*1c030do*_gcl_au*MjA5ODY2ODAyNy4xNzY4NDA2MTU4LjI2OTAwNDEwMi4xNzY4OTI1ODczLjE3Njg5Mjg4Nzg.*_ga*MTc1ODY3MTU5Ny4xNzUzMjkxOTAz*_ga_XXYLHB9SXG*czE3Njg5MjM3OTEkbzM2NiRnMSR0MTc2ODkyOTExNCRqNjAkbDAkaDEzOTQ4NTIxNjQ.) than ads during on-demand content—making live TV a more favorable environment for advertisers struggling to win consumers over.
- Streaming is becoming increasingly relevant to advertisers, capturing nearly half (45.3%) of[ad-supported TV viewing time](https://chart-na1.emarketer.com/c/353471/streaming-captures-nearly-half-of-ad-supported-tv-viewing-353471) , per Nielsen. US adults[spent more time](https://chart-na1.emarketer.com/271890/adults-will-spend-more-time-with-ctv-than-with-traditional-tv-first-time-this-year-average-hrsmins-spent-per-day-with-connected-tv-ctv-vs-traditional-tv-by-us-adults-2020-2027?_gl=1*1hacedz*_gcl_au*MjA5ODY2ODAyNy4xNzY4NDA2MTU4LjI2OTAwNDEwMi4xNzY4OTI1ODczLjE3Njg5Mjg0OTE.*_ga*MTc1ODY3MTU5Ny4xNzUzMjkxOTAz*_ga_XXYLHB9SXG*czE3Njg5MjM3OTEkbzM2NiRnMSR0MTc2ODkyODQ5NyRqMjUkbDAkaDEzOTQ4NTIxNjQ.) with Connected TV (CTV) than linear TV for the first time in 2025, per our forecast—a gap that will continue to widen in 2026 and beyond.
