# MNTN CEO: AI Makes TV Creative Faster, More Affordable

[News](https://mountain.com/news/)[Press](https://mountain.com/press/)


MNTN CEO Mark Douglas joined CNBC’s *The Exchange* to discuss the company’s latest earnings and how AI is transforming TV advertising. Douglas shared that MNTN is using AI across the business — including QuickFrame AI, which helps brands create commercials faster and at lower costs. The bigger goal? Democratizing TV by making high-quality, performance-driven creative more accessible to brands of all sizes.
