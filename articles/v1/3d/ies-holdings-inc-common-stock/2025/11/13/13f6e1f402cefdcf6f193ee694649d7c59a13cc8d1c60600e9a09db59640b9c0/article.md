## IES Holdings Announces Fiscal 2025 Fourth Quarter and Year-End Results Earnings Release Schedule

**About IES Holdings, Inc.**


IES designs and installs integrated electrical and technology systems and provides infrastructure products and services to a variety of end markets, including data centers, residential housing, and commercial and industrial facilities. Our more than 10,000 employees serve clients in the United States.
