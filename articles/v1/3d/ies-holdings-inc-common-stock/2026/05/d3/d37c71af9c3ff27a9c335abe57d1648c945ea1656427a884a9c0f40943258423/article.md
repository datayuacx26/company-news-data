# IES Holdings to Attend Upcoming Investor Conferences

## IES Holdings to Attend Upcoming Investor Conferences


May 18, 2026 at 4:05 PM EDT


[PDF Version](https://investors.ies-corporate.com/node/19391/pdf)


HOUSTON, May 18, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- IES Holdings, Inc. (or “IES” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: IESC) today announced that members of its management team are scheduled to participate in two upcoming investor conferences.


**Stifel Boston Cross Sector 1x1 Conference**


On Tuesday, June 2, 2026, Matt Simmes, the Company’s Chief Executive Officer, and Tracy McLauchlin, the Company’s Chief Financial Officer, are scheduled to host one-on-one investor meetings at the conference in Boston, Massachusetts


**Wells Fargo Industrials & Materials Conference**


On Tuesday, June 9, 2026, Matt Simmes, the Company’s Chief Executive Officer, and Tracy McLauchlin, the Company’s Chief Financial Officer, are scheduled to participate in a fireside chat at 3:00 P.M. CT and host one-on-one investor meetings at the Conference in Chicago, Illinois.


**About IES Holdings, Inc.**


IES designs and installs integrated electrical and technology systems and provides infrastructure products and services to a variety of end markets, including data centers, residential housing, and commercial and industrial facilities. Our more than 11,000 employees serve clients in the United States.
