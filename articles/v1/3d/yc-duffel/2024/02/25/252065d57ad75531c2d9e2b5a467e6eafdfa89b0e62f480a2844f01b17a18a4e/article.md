# Welcome Scott!

LONDON -[Duffel](https://duffel.com/?ref=duffel.ghost.io) , a leading travel tech company, is excited to announce the appointment of Scott Brodows as its new Vice President of Revenue. Brodows, a seasoned professional with a distinguished career in the travel and hospitality industries, will bring his vast experience to Duffel's ambitious mission.


Brodows’ appointment comes as Duffel seeks to accelerate expansion in the US, where he will be based, expanding Duffel’s presence in the market. His proven track record in growing travel technology companies, driving revenue, and leading cross-functional teams makes him an invaluable asset to the company's expanding leadership team.


"Scott's extensive experience across all major verticals in the travel industry and unique skills make him a perfect fit for Duffel. His leadership will be crucial as we continue to make travel selling accessible to everyone," said the Steve Domin, CEO of Duffel. "We're excited to see how his insights and leadership will drive our sales efforts and help us reach new heights."


Brodows’ previous roles include positions at Hopper, Travelocity, and Sabre Holdings, among others. At Hopper, Brodows served as the Head of Third Party Advertising and Head of Flights Marketplace, where he led cross-functional teams and created innovative products.


Duffel, headquartered in London, is on a mission to simplify travel selling through a suite of travel APIs and a plug and play travel booking portal, allowing businesses to construct a cutting-edge embedded travel booking capability into their existing solutions, seamlessly integrated with any other products or services they offer. The company is connected to 300+ airlines, including direct connections to the world’s largest carriers and multiple sources for hotels and accommodations providing access to 1m+ properties. As Duffel's product matures, with the notable launches of their[Stays](https://duffel.com/stays?ref=duffel.ghost.io) and[Links](https://duffel.com/links?ref=duffel.ghost.io) products in 2023, Browder's leadership will be instrumental in increasing awareness and promoting growth.


With a strong focus on Banking and Spend Management solutions, Duffel is regularly onboarding new partners in the Fintech industry. The company's platform allows these type of fintech partners to resell travel directly to their customers through an API or white label integration, enabling fintechs to add extra revenue streams, improve their customers’ experience and encourage innovation. With Brodows’ expertise, Duffel is poised to solidify its position as a tech leading disruptor in the industry.
