# Meet Simple AI at CCW 2026

### Why go to Customer Contact Week?


Contact centers have a consistency problem. Peak seasons expose it. Staffing fluctuations make it worse. Even trained reps have off days. The result is variable customer experiences and conversion rates that shift with whoever picks up the phone.


[Customer Contact Week](http://usesimple.ai/customer-contact-week-2026) is where the people trying to solve that problem (to mention many others) get together. That's why we'll be at Caesars Forum in Las Vegas, June 22-25, and why we're bringing a little bit more than a slide deck.


[Book a Meeting at CCW](https://www.usesimple.ai/events/customer-contact-week-2026)


[Simple AI](http://usesimple.ai/) builds voice agents that run every call at top-performer quality. Whether it's call one or call ten thousand, it maintains the same accuracy, same sales approach, and same customer experience. Our agents already outsell trained live reps by 30% on conversion and upsell.
