# Datavault AI Signs Darryl Strawberry as Brand Ambassador for NIL Vault (NILv)

# Datavault AI Signs Darryl Strawberry as Brand Ambassador for NIL Vault (NILv)


July 27, 2026 9:44am EDT


*Eight-time All-Star, four-time World Series champion, and 1983 National League Rookie of the Year will create original content for the Company’s patented name, image, and likeness exchange*


PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- **Datavault AI Inc.** (“Datavault AI” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: DVLT), a provider of data monetization, credentialing, digital engagement, and real-world asset (‘RWA’) tokenization technologies, today announced that Darryl Strawberry has signed as a brand ambassador for Datavault NIL Vault (NILv), the Company’s new and soon to be launched name, image, and likeness (“NIL”) exchange platform.


Under a services agreement dated June 23, 2026, Strawberry will create and deliver original promotional and marketing content, take part in promotional activity supporting the platform, and participate in revenue attributed to that content. Datavault AI will provide platform onboarding and operational support, tracking and attribution of NILv activity generated by Strawberry’s content, and revenue share reporting and payment administration.


[Strawberry](https://www.baseball-reference.com/players/s/strawda01.shtml) was the first overall selection of the 1980 amateur draft, won the National League Rookie of the Year award in 1983, and was named to eight consecutive All-Star teams from 1984 through 1991. He led the National League in home runs in 1988, hit 335 home runs across 17 major league seasons, and won four World Series titles, with the 1986 New York Mets and the 1996, 1998, and 1999 New York Yankees. The Mets retired his No. 18 in a ceremony at Citi Field on June 1, 2024. In the years since he stopped playing, he has become one of the more visible figures in addiction recovery, writing and speaking on the subject with his wife, Tracy, through their ministry.


That second act is the reason the Company approached him. NILv is being built so that an athlete’s audience, and the content that audience actually watches, can be measured, attributed, and paid out rather than sold once and forgotten. Strawberry played an entire career before any of that existed. Ballplayers of his generation signed away their likeness for a flat fee or gave it away for nothing, and had no mechanism to hold a position in what their name went on to earn. The Company intends NILv to serve figures outside the collegiate system, where most NIL infrastructure has been aimed to date.


That collegiate market is now substantial. Spending on NIL products and services totaled an estimated $918.0 million in the first year of NIL. Opendorse projects it at[$2.75 billion in 2025-26 and $3.15 billion by 2028-29](https://biz.opendorse.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NIL-at-Four-Monetizing-the-New-Reality_July2025.pdf) in its “NIL at Four: Monetizing the New Reality” report published in July 2025. Roughly $995 million of the 2025-26 figure is commercial spending by brands and fans, the segment closest to the work an established public figure performs.


“Darryl Strawberry is one of the most recognizable athletes of his era, and he is candid about a career in which the money and the fame came and went while the name stayed his,” said[Nathaniel T. Bradley](https://app.qwoted.com/sources/nathaniel-t-bradley) , CEO of Datavault AI. “That is precisely the problem NIL Vault is built to solve. We want athletes to hold a measurable, auditable position in what their name, image and likeness generates, and we would rather prove it with someone whose audience is real than with a projection.”


Strawberry joins the platform ahead of its commercial launch. Datavault AI has previously stated its intention to bring a NIL exchange to market as part of a group of specialized data exchange platforms, and additional ambassador and content agreements are expected to be announced as the platform advances.
