# 2020-07-15 Heavyweight Packaging

From potatoes to mattresses and everything in between, at PCA we’re your heavyweight packaging champs.


Serving small, midsize, large and enterprise customers alike, we ensure that each one of our bulk bins and large format boxes are designed to[provide protection and reliability](https://www.packagingcorp.com/packaging-solutions/need/contain-products) —no matter how far they travel.


Industrial customers rely on us to package their products which are heavy, large and sometimes bulky. E-commerce companies turn to us for[intermediate packaging](https://www.packagingcorp.com/packaging-solutions/industry/e-commerce) that allows products to be sorted and moved throughout distribution centers, while[supermarket customers](https://www.packagingcorp.com/packaging-solutions/industry/food-beverage) count on our bulk bins to safely and securely transport many frozen foods, produce, seafood and meat products.


Each product is designed with the function and reliability needed to withstand the rough and tumble nature of global delivery. Whether you’re looking to transport home appliances or[fresh produce](https://www.packagingcorp.com/packaging-solutions/industry/produce) , we’re ready to take on any boxing challenge.
