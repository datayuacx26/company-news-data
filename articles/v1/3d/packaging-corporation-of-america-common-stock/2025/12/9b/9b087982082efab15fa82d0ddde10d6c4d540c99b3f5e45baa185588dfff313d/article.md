# Packaging Corporation of America Schedules Conference Call to Discuss Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Operating Results

LAKE FOREST, Ill.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Dec. 29, 2025-- Packaging Corporation of America (NYSE: PKG) will hold a conference call on Wednesday, January 28, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. Eastern Time to discuss fourth quarter and full year 2025 results. The conference call leader will be Mark Kowlzan. Fourth quarter and full year earnings results will be released after the market closes on Tuesday, January 27, 2026.


To access the conference call, you may pre-register for the conference by navigating to[https://dpregister.com/sreg/10205351/102fa57bc40](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fdpregister.com%2Fsreg%2F10205351%2F102fa57bc40&esheet=54385665&newsitemid=20251229409649&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fdpregister.com%2Fsreg%2F10205351%2F102fa57bc40&index=1&md5=f3b2c0f6a80c6941fe52b4d3b7c41173) . Once you have registered, you will receive your dial-in number. When dialing in the day of the call, please ask to join the Packaging Corporation of America earnings call.


If you prefer not to pre-register, you may dial in the day of the call using one of the following toll-free numbers: (833) 816-1102 (U.S.), (866) 605-3852 (Canada) or (412) 317-0684 (International) by 8:45 a.m. (Eastern Time). Please ask to join the Packaging Corporation of America earnings call.
