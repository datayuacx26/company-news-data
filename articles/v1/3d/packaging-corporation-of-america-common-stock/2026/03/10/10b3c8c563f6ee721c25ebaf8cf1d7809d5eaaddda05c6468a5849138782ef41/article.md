# Packaging Corporation of America Schedules Conference Call to Discuss First Quarter 2026 Operating Results

LAKE FOREST, Ill.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Mar. 16, 2026-- Packaging Corporation of America (NYSE: PKG) will hold a conference call on Thursday April 23, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. Eastern Time to discuss first quarter 2026 results. The conference call leader will be Mark Kowlzan. First quarter earnings results will be released after the market closes on Wednesday, April 22, 2026.
