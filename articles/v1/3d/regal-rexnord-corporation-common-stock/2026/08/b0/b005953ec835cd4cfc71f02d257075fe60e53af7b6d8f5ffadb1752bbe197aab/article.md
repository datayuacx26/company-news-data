# Regal Rexnord Introduces New Cam-Grid® Pro-Form™ Metal Belt


## The weldless design enhances operational uptime, employee safety and food hygiene for spiral applications.


**MILWAUKEE, WIS** . — August 10, 2026 – Regal Rexnord Corporation (NYSE: RRX), a global leader in engineering and manufacturing solutions that power, transmit and control motion, has introduced its innovative Cambridge™ Cam-Grid® Pro-Form Metal Belt. This weldless, metal belt solution is designed to improve operational efficiency and safety and reduce maintenance for food processing facilities utilizing spiral applications for cooling, freezing, proofing, and heating.


The welding process during installation and repairs requires the need for hot work permits, grinding and firewatch, as well as the risk of belt failure and drive bar cap damage due to low-quality welds or component fatigue. The new Cambridge Cam-Grid Pro-Form Belt directly addresses these concerns, offering a unique solution for reliable and hygienic product handling in turn or spiral applications.


"Regal Rexnord understands that our customers need solutions that improve food safety, protect their teams and optimize investments," said Tyler Stohlman, Senior Global Product Manager. "The Cambridge Cam-Grid Pro-Form Belt directly addresses these challenges, offering easier, safer installation without hot work, which significantly reduces risks and downtime. This innovation not only extends equipment life and cuts maintenance costs but also supports critical food safety initiatives, empowering customers to run their lines with greater confidence and efficiency."


This solution eliminates the need for welding during installation and repairs, providing several key advantages:


- Streamlines installation and maintenance
- Reduces downtime
- Enhances safety
- Lowers contamination risk


In addition, the weldless Cam-Grid Pro-Form belt features a flexible mesh overlay support for fragile or delicate products as well as large cuts of meat or poultry. The rods-only design provides maximum air circulation ideal for cookers, proofers and freezers in bakeries.


And because it is built for long-lasting performance, the belt provides the same high fatigue life as metal belts, ensuring consistent and dependable operation in demanding food processing environments.


The Cambridge Cam-Grid Pro-Form Belt is part of Regal Rexnord’s comprehensive portfolio of spiral belts and powertrain options, offering a holistic solution for optimizing spiral cage applications. It is fully compatible with existing Cam-Grid systems, eliminating the need to replace drive bar caps.
