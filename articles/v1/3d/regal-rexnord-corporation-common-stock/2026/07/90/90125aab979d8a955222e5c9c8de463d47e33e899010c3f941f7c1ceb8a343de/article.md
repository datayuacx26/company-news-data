# Set Sail with Our Latest Marine Innovations at Workboat 2024


Discover Regal Rexnord's Marine Solutions and Set Sail with Our Latest Marine Innovations at Workboat 2024!


The Regal Rexnord team is excited to showcase our exceptional manufacturing and reliable product performance for above and below deck applications at the[International Workboat Show](https://www.workboatshow.com/) from November 12th to November 14th. Our world-class solutions-based engineering and innovative excellence enable us to provide reliable and high-quality products for the most challenging marine applications.


For over 100 years, Regal Rexnord has been your trusted global leader, supplying power transmission solutions for above and below deck marine applications. Our legacy has been built on innovation, reliability, and excellence. We provide a comprehensive range of innovative products including flexible & torsional couplings, intermediate shafting systems (including bulkhead seals and bearings), clutches, brakes, propulsion shaft locking systems, system data monitoring and controls from some of the most respected brands in the industry;[Centa™](https://www.regalrexnord.com/brands/centa) ,[Jaure™](https://www.regalrexnord.com/brands/jaure) ,[Stromag™](https://www.stromag.com/) ,[Svendborg Brakes™](https://www.svendborg-brakes.com/) ,[Twiflex™](https://www.twiflex.com/) ,[Stearns™](https://www.stearnsbrakes.com/) , and[Wichita Clutch™](https://www.wichitaclutch.com/) .


Our experts are looking forward to meeting with you in Booth 2543 to answer your questions and help guide you to the right solution. Join us at the Regal Rexnord booth on Tuesday from 3pm to 5pm for food and drinks. See you in New Orleans!


Explore our[marine solutions](https://marine.regalrexnord.com/) !
