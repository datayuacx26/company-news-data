# Regal Rexnord Aerospace Solutions Announces AS9100 Certification for Chihuahua, MX Plant


Regal Rexnord Aerospace Solutions is proud to announce that our Chihuahua, Mexico facility achieved AS9100 certification on January 30, 2025. This accomplishment represents a significant milestone for the Aerospace Solutions team, demonstrating our unwavering commitment to delivering exceptional quality and reliable products to the aerospace industry.


To earn this crucial certification, a third-party assessment was conducted by the Performance Review Institute (PRI). Their thorough audit examined the Quality Management System (QMS) processes at our Chihuahua plant, including Raw Material Receiving, Saw, Machining, Shipping, Purchase Order Review, Purchasing, and the Manufacturing of Bearings.


**What Does This Mean for Our Customers?** The AS9100 certification serves as a hallmark of trust within the aerospace industry. Customers can confidently rely on Regal Rexnord to consistently deliver high-quality parts with the added benefits of expanded capabilities and increased production capacity. This achievement further strengthens our ability to meet First Time Quality and On-Time Delivery commitments.


**Benefits for Regal Rexnord & Aerospace Solutions Group:** The certification amplifies our growth strategy by enhancing capacity, facilitating cross-plant collaboration, and enabling division-level workload balancing. With this new capability, we are better positioned to address backorders and produce even more products to meet growing demand.


**Driving Innovation for the Future:** The combination of our new plant and its AS9100 certification reinforces Regal Rexnord’s role as a trusted partner in aerospace component manufacturing. This milestone builds confidence with our customers, ensuring that we remain their preferred choice for high-quality, reliable components.


We extend our gratitude to everyone who contributed to this achievement, and we look forward to continuing to exceed expectations as we deliver excellence in every part we produce!
