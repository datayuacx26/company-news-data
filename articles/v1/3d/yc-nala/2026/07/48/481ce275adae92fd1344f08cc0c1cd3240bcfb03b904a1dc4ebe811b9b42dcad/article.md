# Loyalty Unpacked – Sephora

Sephora Beauty Insider, the 45 million member program (in North America) that trades in product, access, and experience rather than cash back.


A prestige brand can’t keep discounting without cheapening itself. So instead of coupons and cash, Sephora rewards loyalty another way: Rewards Bazaar samples and limited-stock rewards, invitation-only events, and partner experiences from SEPHORiA to trackside F1 Glam Bars.


It’s one spend doing two jobs: loyalty and marketing, and the Bazaar protects margin in a way a discount never could.
