# Loyalty Unpacked – United Airlines

United MileagePlus, the loyalty program once valued at $21.9 billion, more than the airline that owns it.


The program runs as its own business inside United: most mile sales go to partners, led by the co-brand bank, and since 2024 Kinective Media sells advertising across nearly 100,000 seatback screens and 110 million traveler profiles, with free Starlink Wi-Fi as the login.


In April 2026 the logic reversed. The card now sets the earn rate, and a member without one earns 40% fewer miles on the same fare. The move is financially coherent, and frequent flyers read it as a devaluation. If they stop flying, why keep the airline card?
