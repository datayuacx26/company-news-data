# Loyalty Unpacked – Chipotle

Chipotle Rewards, the 21 million member program that


relaunched in April 2026 with its “Rewards on repeat” strategy.


While most Quick Service Restaurant programs lean on a calendar of heavy discounts (and quietly train customers to be opportunistic), Chipotle is playing a different game. Points that don’t expire, monthly bonus drops, streaks, badges, and gamified challenges, all built to do one thing: motivate people to come back and make specific purchases.


It’s loyalty designed to shape habits, not chase transactions. And that’s a meaningful step toward lasting customer relationships.
