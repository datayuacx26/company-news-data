# Loyalty Unpacked – Ulta Beauty

Ulta Beauty Rewards, 47 million members generating 95% of the retailer’s sales.


The mechanics look simple: points convert to plain dollars off anything, from a drugstore mascara to a salon chair. But at near-total penetration, the program’s real job has moved. It predicts replenishment, personalizes offers, guides store openings, and carries UB Media, where more than 300 brands pay to reach members.


The points are not the strategy. They keep every transaction attached to an identity the whole business builds on.
