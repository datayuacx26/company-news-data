# Loyalty Unpacked – Walgreens

myWalgreens, the free program with more than 134 million members, covering one in three American consumers.


The design is deliberately light: no tiers, no points, and a phone number at checkout is the entire barrier to entry, with a flat 1% back as the whole base cost. Member-only sale prices do the persuading.


In 2026, that coverage has a new job. As Walgreens closes stores, the membership is the bridge: prescriptions transfer, the app redirects, offers re-personalize. The footprint shrinks without the customer base shrinking with it.
