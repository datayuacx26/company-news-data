# Loyalty Unpacked – Walmart

Walmart+, the 28 million member paid program from the world’s largest retailer, where members shop 3x more often and visit e-commerce 7x more than non-members.


The store is open to everyone and already promises the lowest price to everyone, so the fee cannot sell access and will not sell price. It sells time: same-day delivery powered by a store network no purely digital membership can match, with benefits chosen for compounding utility rather than one-off appeal. Members who use multiple benefits are 20% more likely to renew.


The ambition is to become America’s most essential membership. But in a paid program, essential is not declared. It is renewed.
