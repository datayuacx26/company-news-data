# Loyalty Unpacked – Marriott

Marriott Bonvoy, the largest hotel loyalty program in the world, counts nearly 283 million members and filled 75% of US & Canada room nights in 2025. Marriott owns almost none of its 9,900 hotels: its business is fees, and Bonvoy is the demand engine that convinces owners to pay them, while card royalties add a second income guided toward 16% of gross fees this year. Everyone pays for Bonvoy except the member, whose currency is the one variable the marketplace adjusts.
