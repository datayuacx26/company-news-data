# Loyalty Unpacked – McDonald’s

MyMcDonald’s Rewards, the program behind nearly $37 billion in sales across 70 markets, counted in 90-day active users only: 210 million of them.


Most programs report every sign-up they have ever collected. McDonald’s reports members active in the last 90 days, and the design follows the metric: points expire in six months, the first reward is a handful of visits away, weekly deals build the ritual. Everything serves the next visit.


The payoff is frequency. US members visit 26 times a year after joining, up from 10.5 before.
