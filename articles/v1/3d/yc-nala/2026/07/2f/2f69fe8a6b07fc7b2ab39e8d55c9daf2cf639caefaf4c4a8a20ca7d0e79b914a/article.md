# Loyalty Unpacked – KFC

KFC Rewards, the digital-only program that drew more than a million sign-ups in its first month in the US.


KFC came to loyalty late and built it to do what the counter and drive-thru never could. Points accrue only on the app and KFC.com, redeemed for free food from a rotating Secret Recipe Vault, with no tiers and no paid membership. Every reward moves a customer off an anonymous order and into a logged-in one KFC can see, target and personalize.
