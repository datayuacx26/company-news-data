# Loyalty Unpacked – The Fresh Market

For years, grocery loyalty has been built around one simple promise: save customers money.


The Fresh Market is taking a different approach. Its refreshed rewards program combines personalized offers with tasting events, exclusive experiences and customer feedback, moving loyalty beyond transactions alone.


It surpassed **one million members just seven months after launch** , while tasting events have driven triple-digit sales increases on sampled products.
