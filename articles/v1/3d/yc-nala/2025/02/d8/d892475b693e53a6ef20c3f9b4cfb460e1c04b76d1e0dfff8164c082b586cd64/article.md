# 5 Artists to Watch In 2025

####


#### **Alexandre Calvário**


Alexandre Calvário, a luminary in the realm of contemporary art, captivates audiences with his masterful ability to weave narratives through a wide array of artistic mediums. Notable for his intricate sculptures crafted from recycled materials, Calvário’s work transcends mere aesthetics; it serves as a poignant critique of modern consumerism and the urgent issues surrounding environmental degradation.


Each of his meticulously designed pieces invites viewers to engage in meaningful dialogues about sustainability, all while embodying an elegance that reflects the beauty inherent in transformation. Through his art, Calvário not only challenges the status quo but also inspires a renewed appreciation for the potential found in what is often discarded. His sculptures stand as testaments to the intersection of creativity and conscience, urging society to reconsider its relationship with the material world.
