## **3 Big Differences Between HockeyStack and RevSure**


Before diving straight into a side-by-side feature comparison, let’s dig into some of the core differences between HockeyStack and RevSure. We think it’s important to look under the hood and address how both platforms were actually built for two reasons:


1. The underlying architecture impacts everything from features to usability
2. Any solution can push new features, but it’s incredibly challenging to modify the foundations later on.


It’s good to know what you’re getting into from the start, so you can more effectively determine which software best tracks full-funnel B2B journeys.


‍


1. **Data architecture & processing**


HockeyStack’s proprietary data foundation, Atlas, unifies data from every source in your GTM tech stack into a single queryable layer. This feeds everything from AI agent workflows and analyst recommendations to intuitive dashboards, forming a 360 degree view of the entire buyer’s journey—from first anonymous visit to closed-won account. In fact, HockeyStack reveals an average of **4-6X more touchpoints** than traditional CRM-based data models, around 80-120 actions per opportunity. And it does it *fast.*


HockeyStack is built on ClickHouse, a columnar database designed to process enterprise workloads in real-time—we’re talking millions of rows per second, with sub-second latency. OpenAI, Anthropic, Uber, IBM, Cloudflare, Cisco, Lyft, and Twilio are all built on the same ClickHouse architecture.


What truly sets HockeyStack apart is the depth and breadth of its foundational data capabilities, spanning data acquisition, deduplication, identity resolution, categorization, governance, and activation. Insights are surfaced from a single, centralized core, offering unparalleled pipeline visibility based on real-time buyer behavior, engagement, and account activity across any combination of campaigns, channels, and touchpoints. HockeyStack’s real-time processing and action-based model also enables dynamic analysis, with all activity and engagement immediately reflected.


RevSurewas built on an object-based model focused on data acquisition and deduplication. While this may offer some improvement from CRM-native reporting, its architecture lacks more robust data functionality necessary to convert raw data into actionable insights, like categorization, governance, and activation.


Another foundational limitation with RevSure is its batch processing, which introduces latency between when an event occurs and when it appears in your reports. For GTM teams looking to run real-time lead routing, automate workflows, or respond to evolving buyer behavior, this delay creates friction—especially with enterprise data loads. Waiting hours for data to refresh breaks routing workflows, delays decision-making, and makes real-time optimization impossible.

### Multi-Touch Attribution and Identity Resolution


Effective multi-touch attribution not only assigns credit to sales and marketing activities that influence a deal. It also enables GTM teams to consistently answer one simple question: *what actually drove this revenue?* The quality of your answer depends on how many touchpoints you can see.


HockeyStack captures the complete B2B buyer’s journey in a single unified view. Anonymous site visits, ad impressions, content engagement, sales activity, even product usage are all tied together at the account level. You can instantly switch between attribution models and even use predictive attribution to forecast revenue based on real-time buyer behavior.


Beyond standard attribution, HockeyStack also measures incremental lift to reveal the true causal impact of a channel or campaign versus correlation-based credit. This distinction matters because some touchpoints simply happen to be present in winning deals without actually influencing the outcome. Incremental analysis helps you distinguish between channels that drive pipeline and channels that just show up in the journey.


RevSure's touchpoint coverage tends to focus on known contacts and standard CRM activities. This approach may be sufficient for shorter buyer’s journeys but fails to capture early engagement—when B2B buyers engage with ads, visit your website, and consume content before ever identifying themselves.


HockeyStack solves this challenge with its proprietary fingerprinting technology, which captures pre-form-fill engagement through a unique combination of identifiers, including:


- Device type
- Screen height, width, and depth
- Browser
- OS
- Browser language
- Browser version
- Plugins installed
- Extensions
- Country and city data
- Timezone
- API key


‍


This cookieless approach effectively consolidates these data points, which can then be connected to a single person or account for unparalleled visibility.


### AI-Powered Insights and Recommendations


HockeyStack’s AI analysts Odin and Nova use everyday language to make recommendations and surface nuanced insights into pipeline performance. You can ask “Which Q3 assets influenced enterprise pipeline the most?” and get a direct answer with supporting data.


HockeyStack takes it further than most AI driven attribution tools for B2B. Every time you ask a question, specialized agents run an analysis code to calculate metrics. A dedicated evaluation agent then validates the output before it reaches you, which significantly reduces hallucinations.


This ensures every insight in HockeyStack is both traceable and auditable; essential when CMOs and CFOs make million-dollar budget decisions based on AI recommendations. Further, customer data is never used to train global models, and all AI features are opt-in with full data ownership retained by the customer.


RevSure offers predictive scoring and conversion probability features that forecast which deals will close. Teams looking to delve into deeper questions like “What’s actually driving our pipeline?” will likely require more robust analytical capabilities than RevSure was built for.

## **Key considerations for enterprise teams**


Enterprises have additional complexity to consider when determining the best B2B marketing attribution software. These include:


‍


### **AI Accuracy and Enterprise Trust**


AI trust is non-negotiable for enterprise teams. HockeyStack's AI runs in a secure, isolated environment and customer data is never used to train global models. Additionally, all outputs are traceable and auditable.


When evaluating AI features, it's worth asking vendors whether your data trains their models and whether outputs are explainable. Hallucinated insights can be worse than no insights at all—especially when they inform budget decisions.


‍


### **Data Complexity and Quality Controls**


Your attribution capabilities are only as good as the data behind it. Enterprise B2B organizations typically have messy CRMs stuffed with duplicates, conflicting field values, and the same person appearing differently across systems. There’s also multiple marketing tools and complex data structures in the mix.


HockeyStack assumes your data is imperfect by design, automatically normalizing, deduplicating, and reconciling without manual cleanup. So basically, it works with your existing reality rather than requiring you to change it first.


Additionally, HockeyStack was built from the ground up to rapidly process massive volumes of GTM data, without compromising on quality or flexibility—making it a strong choice for enterprise teams. Its ClickHouse architecture features columnar compression, which keeps storage costs stable even as data volume expands.


This prevents costs from spiking unpredictably when you add new data sources or your traffic increases—a common concern with batch-based systems that charge by query volume, like RevSure.


‍


### **Custom Salesforce Object Support**


Enterprise organizations tracking partner activities, product usage, or industry-specific data through custom Salesforce objects require additional support. HockeyStack ingests these natively, so you never lose visibility into your attribution.


‍


### **Complex Account Hierarchy Handling**


HockeyStack handles both heterogeneous and homogeneous hierarchies, including multi-brand organizations and global subsidiaries. Instead of forcing CRM merges or pretending there's one perfect hierarchy, it shows separate brand journeys and also rolls up to parent accounts—so you can gain visibility into enterprise account activity, without restructuring your entire CRM.


‍


### **Touchpoint Governance and Business-Defined Rules**


Unlike rigid attribution tools, HockeyStack lets your team define funnel stages, channel groupings, touchpoint categories, and attribution rules. When definitions change, every dashboard and report updates instantly—no reprocessing or engineering work required.


‍


### **Data privacy and security standards**


HockeyStack maintains rigorous data security protocols and compliance for enterprise teams. The platform is certified SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, ISO 27001.


RevSure is also SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 compliant.
