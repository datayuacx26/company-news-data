# Avista planned power outage in Inchelium

<< Back[PDF Version](https://investor.avistacorp.com/node/30741/pdf)


**Spokane, Wash. June 26, 2026:** Approximately 204 electric customers will experience a planned power outage on Thursday, July 9. Avista customers in Inchelium will be impacted. The planned outage will start at 8:00 a.m. and end at 11:00 a.m. All affected customers will be notified in advance. No lane or road closures are expected during this outage.


During this time, Avista contract crews will perform maintenance and repairs on equipment in the area. Avista is committed to safeguarding the integrity of its infrastructure while enhancing the safety and reliability of the electric system.


For any questions or concerns, customers can contact Avista at (800) 227-9187.
