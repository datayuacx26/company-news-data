# GitLab To Announce Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Financial Results


---


SAN FRANCISCO, August 18, 2026 - All Remote - GitLab Inc., the intelligent orchestration platform for DevSecOps, today announced that it will report its financial results for the second quarter fiscal year 2027, which ended July 31, 2026, after U.S. markets close on Tuesday, September 1, 2026. Management will host a conference call and webcast on the same day to discuss the company’s financial results at 4:30 p.m. ET / 1:30 p.m. PT.


**GitLab Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Financial Results Conference Call and Webcast**


**When** : Tuesday, September 1, 2026


**Time** : 4:30 p.m. ET / 1:30 p.m. PT


**[Earnings Call Registration](https://gitlab-second-quarter-fiscal-2027-financial-results.open-exchange.net/registration)**


**Replay** : A webcast replay of the conference call will be available on the investor relations website for one year.


GitLab uses its Investor Relations website[ir.gitlab.com](https://ir.gitlab.com/overview/default.aspx) and its X feed (@gitlab), among other channels, as a means of disclosing material nonpublic information and for complying with its disclosure obligations under Regulation FD.
