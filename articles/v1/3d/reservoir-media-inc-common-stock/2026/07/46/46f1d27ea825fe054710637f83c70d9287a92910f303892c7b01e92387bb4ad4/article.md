# OAK FELDER EARNS 2026 EMMY NOMINATION

Today the 2026 Emmy Award nominations were announced, with Oak Felder earning a nod for Outstanding Original Music and Lyrics for “The Devil You Know” by Li Jun Li. Felder co-wrote and produced the song for Amazon Prime’s hit show *Spider-Noir* .


The Emmy Awards will be held live on ABC on September 14 at 8pm ET/5pm PT. See the full list of nominees[here](https://www.televisionacademy.com/awards/nominees-winners) .
