# RESERVOIR TALENT CONTRIBUTE TO TWO 2026 VMA NOMINATIONS

Nominations for the 2026 MTV Video Music Awards have been[announced](https://www.billboard.com/lists/2026-mtv-vma-nominations-list-madonna-video-music-awards/all-artists-with-two-or-more-nominations/) . Reservoir talent have contributed to two nominations, including:


**Best R&B**
“Yukon” by Justin Bieber, co-written by 2 Chainz
“Folded” by Kehlani, co-written/co-produced by Khris Riddick-Tynes


[Voting](https://vote.mtv.com/) is open now, and the VMAs will broadcast live from the Peacock Theatre in Los Angeles on Sunday, September 27th at 7:30-9:30 p.m. ET/4:30-6:30 p.m. PT on CBS. The show will simulcast on MTV and stream on Paramount+ in the U.S.
