# RESERVOIR WRITER AVERY ANNA EARNS A 2026 MUSICROW AWARDS NOMINATION

*MusicRow Magazine* announced the nominations for the 2026 MusicRow Awards. Reservoir writer Avery Anna was nominated for Female Artist of the Year. This comes on the heels of her ACM Award win for New Female Artist of the Year and release of her EP ‘forgive, forgot.’


Voting is now open for *MusicRow* subscribers through Friday, August 14 via email. The winners will be revealed on *MusicRow* on Wednesday, September 2.
