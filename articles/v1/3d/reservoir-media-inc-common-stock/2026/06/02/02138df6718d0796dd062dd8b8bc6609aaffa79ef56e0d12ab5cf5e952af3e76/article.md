# RESERVOIR EARNS TOP 10 MARKET SHARE FOR Q1 2026

As reported in *Billboard* ’s[Publishers Quarterly](https://www.billboard.com/pro/music-publishers-quarterly-q1-2026-sony-tops-olivia-dean/?bt_ee=X8HuIvEuV%2BcwOo5M8n8hTqEBSMMD4ifZn9qgO1ebDeoMsVizmIPZobyT4WrMHpqi&bt_ts=1780434796484) , Reservoir has earned a Top 10 U.S. market share for Q1 2026, coming in at #7 on the Top Radio Airplay evaluation with 1.53% market share. Kehlani’s Grammy-winning single “Folded,” co-written/co-produced by Khris Riddick-Tynes, contributed to the Company’s market share, landing at #8 on the Top Radio Airplay Songs for the quarter.
