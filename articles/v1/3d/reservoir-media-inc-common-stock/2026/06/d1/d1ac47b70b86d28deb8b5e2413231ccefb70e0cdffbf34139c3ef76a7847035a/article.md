# GOLNAR KHOSROWSHAHI NAMED ONE OF LA TIMES 2026 ENTERTAINMENT BUSINESS VISIONARIES

LA Times recognized Reservoir Founder & CEO Golnar Khosrowshahi on its 2026 list of Entertainment Business Visionaries. The list features an array of executives and managers “whose expertise continues to shape the direction of the entertainment industry.”


The spotlight on Golnar noted she “manages a diverse portfolio of master recordings and copyrights ranging from Joni Mitchell to the catalog of Miles Davis,” with “expertise \[that\] extends to emerging global markets through the launch of PopIndia and strategic joint ventures with major Jamaican publishers.”


See the full list of Entertainment Business Visionaries[here](https://www.latimes.com/b2b/business-visionaries/entertainment) .
