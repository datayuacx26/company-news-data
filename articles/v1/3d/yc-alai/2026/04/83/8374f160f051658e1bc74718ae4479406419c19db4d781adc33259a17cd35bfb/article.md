# A Guide to Extracting Editable Text from Image-Based Slides

You are sitting with a PowerPoint or PDF presentation in front of you. The slides are full of useful text, data, points, definitions, and important notes. But the whole thing is locked inside images. You cannot select it. You cannot copy it. You cannot edit a single word.


So what do you do?


Most people do the difficult thing, which is retyping the whole file using a keyboard. This requires much time, effort, and attention. And after all that hard work, there are still chances of missing important details and typos in the draft.


The good news is that you do not need to type any of it manually. There are tools built specifically for this. This guide will walk you through exactly how to extract editable text from image-based slides. The method is fast, accurate, and without any technical headaches. Keep reading to learn more.


## What Are Image-Based Slides?


Some slides have text that is typed directly into the slide using a text box. You can select it and edit it. But image-based slides are different.


In these slides, the text is not typed. It is printed on an image that is pasted onto the slide. To the eye, it looks exactly like normal text. But it is not. It is just pixels on a screen. This is why you cannot select it. The text is visually there but digitally locked.


This is a very common problem. Students receive lecture slides where scanned book pages or screenshots are directly pasted onto the slides. Professionals get project presentations where charts, reports, and data tables are embedded as images. Researchers work with academic papers shared as image-heavy PDFs.


## What Is the Easiest Way to Extract Text from an Image Online?


The easiest method is to use an online image to text converter tool.


These tools are based on[optical character recognition technology](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/optical-character-recognition) . This technology can scan the printed or embedded text on the image and convert it into machine-readable and editable text. Modern image to text converter tools offer a high level of accuracy and speed. They can even extract text from low-quality or blurred images with good accuracy.


So this is one of the most practical and time-saving solutions for students and professionals working with image-based slides on a daily basis.


## Step-by-Step Guide to Extract Text from Slides


We will walk you through the complete process of extracting printed text from the images pasted in the slides. Make sure to read the content carefully and follow all the steps to get the best results.


### 1) Save Your Slides as Images


First, you need to convert your slides into images. If your slides are in PPT format, you can convert them into images with ease. Open the PPT in PowerPoint and click on the “File” button in the top left corner. Then click on the “Save as” button and choose the output folder.


A dialogue box will open when you select the folder. Click on the “Save as type” button at the bottom of the box and select “PNG” as output.


All the images in your PPT slides will be saved as separate image files in your selected folder, like this:


If your slides are in PDF format, you can use them as they are. There are certain OCR tools that allow PDF to text conversion. But in that case, you will get one single text file from all the slides. And it can cause confusion for you. So, a better way is to convert PDF to images using an online PDF to image converter.

## Tips to Extract Text Accurately from Image-Based Slides


Well, now that we have discussed the complete process to extract text, let's look at some of the important tips to get the best results out of your image slides.


### 1) Use the Formatted Text Option


This feature is only available in a few tools, including the one we discussed earlier. And it is very useful if your images contain table data. Normal OCR tools convert tabular data into plain text. But if you use the formatted text option, the tool will provide you with the output as a table.


### 2) Upscale the Images


If any of your images are too blurred, misprinted, or contain handwritten text that is unclear, clean them using online image upscaling tools like Canva. This will improve the accuracy of the final results.


### 3) Always Review the Final Output


Even the best tools can miss small details. A quick review helps fix minor errors. Check for spelling and punctuation with care.
