## What Is Napkin AI?


[Napkin AI](https://www.napkin.ai/) is a browser-based text-to-visual tool built for business storytelling. It was created by Jerome Scholler and Pramod Sharma, the founders behind AI gaming company Osmo, and has grown to over 5 million registered users as of 2025. The core premise is simple: you paste text into a document, click a button, and the AI generates visual options for that text in seconds.


What makes Napkin different from design tools like Canva or Figma is where visuals live. In Canva, you go to a design canvas and start building. In Napkin, visuals appear alongside your text in a document-style editor. You write or paste content, highlight a paragraph, and the visual appears for that specific highlighted content. This makes it well-suited for document-first content.


Who it's built for: content marketers producing blog and LinkedIn visuals, consultants who need fast diagrams for client proposals, educators creating lecture materials, and project managers documenting workflows.


## How Napkin AI Works: Step by Step


Napkin's generation workflow runs in five steps:


1.


**Import your source content.** Add your content as notes, ideas or by importing existing files.


1.


**Click the spark icon.** For every piece of content that needs a visual, click the spark option to trigger the AI, which analyses the text for structure and turns them into different types of charts, graphs or visuals.


1.


**Review 4-6 visual options.** The AI typically returns options in 3–8 seconds. Each option represents a different layout interpretation of the same content.


2.


**Pick one and edit.** Once you have a layout finalized, you can also choose between different design styles. Once done, use the editor to make changes to colors, icons, fonts, connectors, layout direction, and spacing.


3.


**Export.** PNG at 1x/2x/3x resolution, SVG, PDF, or editable PPTX. Light and dark mode toggles are available at export. Transparent background is optional.


Credits are consumed at roughly one credit per word of text processed for generation. Editing an existing visual doesn't cost credits on any plan.


Since June 2025, you can use Custom Generation: type the visual type you want before generating (e.g., "horizontal flowchart" or "comparison table") instead of waiting to pick from whatever the AI decides. This is a meaningful upgrade. Before custom generation, abstract input sometimes produced the wrong visual type and required multiple regenerations.


## Napkin AI Features: What You Actually Get


These six features matter the most for presentation builders:


**1. Text-to-Visual Generation: 30+ Types** Flowcharts, mind maps, comparison tables, process diagrams, timelines, Venn diagrams, step-by-step flows, data summaries, org charts. These aren't templated layouts you fill in. The AI generates a visual structure based on the content itself. Paste a six-step onboarding process and you get a flow. Paste a pros/cons list and you get a comparison table. The accuracy is high for structured text. It drops for abstract or loosely written input.


**2. Elastic Design System** Visuals auto-scale as you add or remove elements. Add a seventh step to a six-step process flow and the diagram adjusts without you touching spacing or connector lines. This matters significantly when you're editing content under deadline pressure and don't want to redo the layout every time.


**3. Custom Styles and Brand Kit** Upload your brand colors. Napkin includes an auto-extraction feature that pulls colors directly from an uploaded image, so you don't need hex codes. Upload or choose from 700+ Google Fonts. Save multiple style profiles and apply them in one click across all visuals. Brand style features are tiered: three custom styles on Plus, unlimited on Pro.


**4. Sketch Overlay** Draw directly on any generated visual. Add annotations, freehand arrows, or labels that don't fit standard shapes. Useful for marking up visuals before sending for review.


**5. Export Formats** Free plan: PNG, PPT and PDF, unlimited. Watermark included. Plus plan: Adds editable PPTX and SVG. Watermark removed. Pro plan: Everything in Plus, plus custom font uploads and optional credit top-ups.


The PPTX export is the critical one for presentation workflows. I exported a four-step process flow to PowerPoint and opened it in PowerPoint 365. Text boxes were individually editable, shape colors were retained, and font fallbacks applied cleanly. You can adjust the layout post-export without redesigning from scratch. That's not guaranteed with every tool that claims PPTX export.


Resolution options at export: 1x, 2x, 3x. Light/dark mode toggle. Transparent background on/off. All in one panel.


**6. Collaboration** Real-time commenting on individual diagram elements. Multiple editors can work simultaneously. No API is available as of 2026. No native integrations with Notion, Google Slides, or WordPress. Any workflow connecting Napkin to those tools requires manual file export.

## Napkin AI Review: Does It Actually Deliver Good Presentations?


This is the section no other Napkin AI review covers properly, so here is the direct answer: **Napkin AI is excellent for creating individual presentation visuals. But, it does not build complete slide decks.**


That distinction matters. If you need a polished 12-slide pitch deck generated from a text prompt, Napkin AI is the wrong tool. Go to Gamma or Alai for that. But if you're building a deck in PowerPoint or Google Slides and need to visualize a framework, a process, a comparison, or a roadmap, Napkin AI is faster and cleaner than anything else available.


Here's what that looks like in practice. I pasted a four-step client onboarding description: "Discovery: 30-minute call to understand goals. Proposal: Custom scope sent within 48 hours. Kickoff: 90-minute session to align on deliverables. Delivery: Weekly updates until launch." Then I clicked generate. In six seconds I had a horizontal flow diagram with labeled stages, icons, and clean connector lines. I exported it as an editable PPTX, opened it in PowerPoint 365, and the text boxes were individually editable. I could change fonts, adjust colors, and resize elements without any layout breaking. The whole process took under three minutes.


**Visuals only generate for content you select.** This is worth understanding before you try it. Napkin AI does not scan your entire document and auto-generate visuals everywhere. You highlight a specific paragraph or bullet block, click the spark icon, and the AI generates options for that section only. This matters for presentation workflows because it means you stay in control of which parts of your content become visuals and which stay as text. You're not fighting AI decisions about what to visualize; you're directing them.


**Multiple chart options per content piece, not one interpretation.** When you trigger generation on a selected block, Napkin AI returns 4-6 distinct visual options for that same content. You pick the one that fits the slide you're building into and move on. If none of the options work, you can use Custom Generation to specify the visual type upfront ("give me a comparison table" or "give me a timeline") before the AI runs, instead of hoping the auto-selection is right.


**Style switching across the full visual.** Once you have a visual, you can switch its style with one click. Napkin AI offers multiple style presets (line-based, filled, flat, layered) that completely change the visual treatment without touching the content or layout. A flowchart in the default style is clean and minimal. The same flowchart in a filled style has colored node backgrounds and bolder connectors. This is useful when you need the visual to match the tone of the deck it's going into: a dark, bold investor deck needs a different visual style than a clean internal process doc. You toggle between styles in seconds and the content structure stays intact.


**Brand application is direct and saved.** In Napkin AI's Brand Studio, you upload your brand colors and fonts once. For colors, there's an auto-extraction feature: upload an image (a logo, a brand asset, a slide from your deck) and Napkin reads the hex values directly without you entering a single code. Once saved, every visual you generate can apply your brand palette and typography in one click. On the Plus plan you get three saved Custom Styles. On Pro, unlimited. This means a consultant working across multiple clients can maintain separate saved style profiles and switch between them per project, keeping visuals on-brand without manually adjusting colors each time.

### 4. Gemini Canvas: Best for Google Workspace Users Who Want Zero Friction


[Gemini Canvas](https://gemini.google/overview/canvas/) is Google's AI workspace layer inside the Gemini app. It generates documents, presentations, infographics, quizzes, and code from prompts and lives natively inside the Google ecosystem. If your entire workflow runs through Google Workspace (Docs, Slides, Drive, Gmail), Gemini Canvas is the option that adds AI-assisted presentation creation without adding a new tool, a new account, or a new login to your stack.


**Zero friction entry vs. Napkin AI's dedicated onboarding.** Napkin AI requires a separate account and a new workflow. Gemini Canvas is already inside the Gemini app that Google Workspace users access daily. If someone on your team is already using Gemini for email drafting or document summarization, they can generate a presentation from the same interface they're already in. There is no onboarding, no learning curve for the tool itself, and no credit card required on the free tier.


**Multi-format output from a single interface.** Gemini Canvas generates presentations, documents, code, infographics, and web content from the same prompt bar. Napkin AI generates diagrams only. For a team that uses Gemini Canvas across multiple content types, having presentations covered by the same tool reduces stack complexity.


**Deep Research integration.** Gemini Canvas can pull from Deep Research mode, which runs multi-step web research before generating content. A prompt like "Create a presentation on competitive dynamics in the EV market in 2026" can pull current data directly into the slides rather than relying solely on your input. Napkin AI only works with content you provide; it does not pull live information.


**Native Google Slides export.** Canvas exports directly to Google Slides with one click, where you can use all of Google's existing formatting tools, comment workflows, and sharing permissions your team already knows. Napkin AI requires you to export a PPTX file and manually import it into Slides if you work in that environment.


The honest assessment: Gemini Canvas was not built for presentations. It was built as a general-purpose AI workspace and presentation creation was bolted on afterward. The sequencing shows. Presentations export to Google Slides, not directly to PPTX. Getting a .pptx file requires a two-step conversion (Canvas to Slides, then download as PPTX), and formatting frequently breaks in that process. Design output lacks presentation-specific logic: layouts are functional but flat, closer to 2015-era PowerPoint than what alai or Gamma produces. There is no visual editor. Design quality depends entirely on how detailed your prompt is, which makes it effectively unusable for non-designers trying to produce polished output without prior experience writing design prompts.


Where Gemini Canvas earns its place: free on the standard Gemini plan, zero new-tool friction for Google Workspace users, and adequate for internal drafts under time pressure. For anything going to a client or investor, it needs substantial manual rework in Google Slides after the fact.
