## Gamma AI PowerPoint Export: Why Your Slides Break (And How to Fix It)


Gamma decks break on PowerPoint export because Gamma uses a web-native, card-based layout engine that does not map to PowerPoint's fixed-dimension slide architecture.


My primary reason for adding this as a separate section is because this is the single biggest frustration I hear from Gamma users. In this section, I've covered the exact issue, why it happens and how to solve it.


### What Happens When You Export Gamma AI to PPT


When you download a Gamma AI presentation as a PowerPoint file, the output frequently doesn't match what you see in the Gamma editor:


-


**Non-standard dimensions:** Gamma AI slides often render as square or tall cards, not standard 16:9. When these open in PowerPoint, they don't fit the default slide size.


-


**Text shifts:** Text boxes move from their original positions. Headlines overlap with body text, or get pushed off-slide entirely.


-


**Missing fonts:** Gamma uses web fonts that don't exist in PowerPoint. The result is font substitution that changes your deck's entire look.


-


**Layout collapse:** Gamma's card-based blocks don't map cleanly to PowerPoint's slide structure. Multi-column layouts can stack vertically, destroying the visual design.


### Why This Happens


The root cause is architectural. Gamma AI presentations are built as web-native content using card-based blocks (essentially a scrollable webpage). PowerPoint uses a completely different layout engine with fixed-dimension slides. Converting between these formats is difficult, making Gamma AI PPT exports the casualty.


### Workarounds


-


**Export as PDF instead** if you just need to share (no editing, but formatting holds)


-


**Always open your Gamma PPT export in PowerPoint a day before an important meeting:** I learned this the hard way when text boxes shifted mid-presentation


-


**Budget 20-30 minutes for manual fixes** on any exported Gamma AI deck


-


**Or use an alternative that exports cleanly:** Alai's PowerPoint exports match the editor exactly, and Plus AI works natively inside Google Slides
