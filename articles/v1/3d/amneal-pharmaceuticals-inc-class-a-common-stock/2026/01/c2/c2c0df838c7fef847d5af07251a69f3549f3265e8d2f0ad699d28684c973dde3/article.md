# We believe everyone deserves access to medicine


January 26, 2026


We recently launched our new brand and visual identity which reflects our evolution into a diversified global biopharmaceutical leader and reinforces our purpose of reimagining what’s possible to make medicine accessible for all.


The new brand brings our purpose to life through a modern visual identity grounded in trust, humanity, and access. Our refreshed “Signature” logo is inspired by the act of prescribing, symbolizing the trust placed in healthcare providers and the real-world impact each prescription has on patients. The evolved mark underscores our role in helping bridge access to medicines across increasingly complex therapeutic areas and delivery systems.
