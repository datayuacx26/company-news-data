# Cashel, Ireland Team Advancing our Inhalation Program

# Cashel, Ireland Team Advancing our Inhalation Program


October 2, 2023


Our Cashel Ireland-based team’s hard work and expertise is paving the way for our long-term strategy of shifting our portfolio to increasingly complex product areas. They are supporting this shift by advancing the development and ultimate commercialization of our inhalation/respiratory products. The team operates out of our 200,000 square foot state-of-the-art facility, which includes sterile fill finish and high containment areas, laboratories and office space.


Each team member is committed to Amneal’s growth and advancing our industry-leading reputation for quality. Every activity is guided by quality, from our QC lab to our dedicated janitorial team.


Recently, Co-CEOs Chirag and Chintu Patel visited with this talented team, alongside other company leaders.


It’s truly the people that make Amneal the great company it is today, and our Ireland colleagues are shining examples. Well done, team Cashel!
