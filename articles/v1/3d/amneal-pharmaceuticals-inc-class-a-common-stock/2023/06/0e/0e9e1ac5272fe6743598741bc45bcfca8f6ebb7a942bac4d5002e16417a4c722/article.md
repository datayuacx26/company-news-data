# Recognizing World Environment Day


June 8, 2023


In recognition of World Environment Day on June 5, 2023, we teamed up with[One Tree Planted](https://www.linkedin.com/company/onetreeplanted/) to plant 7,600 trees (one for each Amneal team member) in areas needing reforestation across India, Ireland, and the U.S. National Forests.


Trees play a vital role in our ecosystem. Trees help clean the air we breathe, filter the water we drink, and provide habitat to over 80% of the world’s biodiversity. By planting trees, we can protect and restore the Earth and help make healthy possible for all.


[Transcript](https://amneal.com/videos/transcripts/transcript-one-tree-planted.pdf)


Ready to take action today? We invite you to:


- Make small changes like reducing paper and plastic usage, conserving energy (unplugging electronics when not in use), eating less meat, recycling, and driving less.
- Join One Tree Planted as a[tree ambassador](https://onetreeplanted.org/pages/individuals) to make a difference in your local community.
- Contribute to a local cleanup or tree-planting initiative by searching for local events with[VolunteerMatch](https://www.volunteermatch.org/)


Together, we can build a better and more sustainable world!
