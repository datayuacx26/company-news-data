# Our commitment to our communities


October 23, 2024


This September, Recovery Awareness month, we launched the “


[Taking Control Over It](https://takingcontroloverit.com/) ” initiative to raise awareness, reduce stigma and educate the community about


[Amneal’s Naloxone HCl Nasal Spray](https://amnealnaloxone.com/) and opioid emergencies. We are proud to partner with advocacy organizations such as


[Mobilize Recovery,](https://www.mobilizerecovery.org/)[Young People In Recovery](https://youngpeopleinrecovery.org/) , and


[The Naloxone Project](https://www.naloxoneproject.com/) to donate product and resources to elevate the meaningful work they are doing. And we are proud to be playing a major sponsorship role in iHeart Media’s


[Mobilize Recovery Across America Bus Tour](https://www.mobilizerecovery.org/bus_tour) and their efforts in educate communities and raising awareness that recovery is possible.


## Our Commitment


Since 2000, approximately 1 million people in the U.S. have died of drug overdoses, and that number unfortunately continues to grow. We are taking action. Here are some of our ongoing efforts:


- Our Naloxone HCL Nasal Spray product is available without a prescription under the Amneal brand, and at select major U.S. pharmacies as the store private label product.


- Our government relations team is partnering with government agencies to help drive increased access to this lifesaving opioid overdose reversal medicine.


- We distributed Amneal Naloxone HCL Nasal Spray to all U.S. employees to help educate employees and reduce stigma.


- We’re partnering with organizations including[Mobilize Recovery,](https://www.mobilizerecovery.org/)[Young People In Recovery](https://youngpeopleinrecovery.org/) , and[The Naloxone Project](https://www.naloxoneproject.com/) .


**This is the reason Amneal exists, and we want to do all we can to make sure we get help to those who need it most.**
