# One Extra Call Can Make All the Difference

**By Whitney Hedgpeth, PMF Global CI Manager**


Every organization faces challenges. What often determines success is how people respond when the obvious solution no longer works.


I was reminded of that when a critical shipment of raw materials became stuck in transit during one of our most challenging quarters. The delay threatened production, and despite multiple attempts through traditional channels, there appeared to be no clear path forward.


That is when John Ulcickas, our Procurement Manager in Bedford, Massachusetts, decided that accepting defeat was not an option.


Rather than waiting for the problem to solve itself, John kept looking for alternatives. He thought creatively, tapped into his network, found the right local contact, and ultimately got the shipment moving again. The material arrived the next day, allowing production to continue and helping the team maintain momentum toward important business objectives.


While the shipment itself was important, the experience reinforced a broader lesson about leadership and teamwork.


Innovation is not always about developing new technology or implementing a new process. Sometimes it is simply looking at a problem from a different angle. It is the willingness to ask one more question and make one more phone call, exploring one more possibility when everyone else assumes there are no options left.


John demonstrated that mindset perfectly.


His actions also reminded our team of the power of ownership. He understood what was at stake and took personal responsibility for finding a solution. More importantly, he viewed the challenge as our challenge, not someone else’s. That mentality reinforced a culture where employees support one another and work together to overcome obstacles.


The impact extended well beyond logistics. John’s example showed what is possible when people refuse to be limited by the first obstacle they encounter. It encouraged others to think differently and collaborate more closely, focusing on solutions instead of setbacks.


In today’s environment, every organization faces uncertainty. The employees who make the biggest difference are often those who remain optimistic, adaptable, and determined when the path forward is unclear.


John’s story is a reminder that perseverance matters. Sometimes the difference between a setback and a success comes down to one person who is willing to go a little further than expected.
