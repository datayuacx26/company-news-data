# Why Team Effectiveness Is a Business Advantage

AI is transforming the way commercial teams work, not by replacing expertise, but by helping employees access information and identify opportunities so they can make decisions faster.


Across sales, business development, and product management, AI supports employees in different ways depending on the challenge at hand.


## **Strengthening Customer Relationships**


Strong customer relationships start with preparation. AI helps teams quickly access customer history and meeting insights so they can spend less time searching for answers and more time focused on customers.


It also helps preserve and share knowledge across teams, creating continuity and better-informed customer interactions.


## **Advancing Growth Opportunities**


AI helps commercial teams identify prospects and uncover opportunities within existing accounts, as well as maintain momentum on active opportunities.


By bringing together customer insights, market intelligence, and historical knowledge, employees can engage more effectively and focus on the right opportunities, then replicate successful approaches across their portfolios.


## **Identifying What’s Next**


Beyond supporting day-to-day sales activities, AI is helping teams evaluate trends and identify emerging opportunities.


By accelerating research and turning large amounts of information into actionable insights, AI helps teams make smarter decisions about where to focus future growth efforts.


## **One Technology, Multiple Benefits**


The value of AI-powered selling isn’t tied to a single use case. It shows up across the commercial process, from preparing for customer conversations to evaluating new markets.


By helping employees access knowledge faster, collaborate more effectively, and make better-informed decisions, AI allows commercial teams to spend less time gathering information and more time creating opportunities and delivering value for customers.
