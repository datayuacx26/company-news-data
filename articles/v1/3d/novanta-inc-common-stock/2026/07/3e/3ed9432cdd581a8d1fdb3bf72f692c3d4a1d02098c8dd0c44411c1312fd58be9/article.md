# A Sweet Way to Celebrate a Record-Breaking Quarter

**By Maddie Billings, Office & HR Support Associate**


Success is always worth celebrating, especially when it reflects the hard work and dedication of an entire team.


On July 16, employees at our site in Rocklin, California, enjoyed a special lunchtime treat from the Snowie King truck as part of an employee appreciation event recognizing our highest quarterly revenue on record.


While the snow cones brought smiles and a chance to recharge, the event served a larger purpose: thanking our employees for the collaboration, and perseverance that helped make this achievement possible.


Record-breaking results are built on the contributions of people across the organization. Every team and every project helped drive this milestone.[Through innovation and a shared focus on delivering for our customers](https://novanta.com/about/vision-mission/) , our employees turned ambitious goals into exceptional results.


Celebrations like this are an opportunity to reflect on what makes success possible. Behind every achievement are[talented people who support embrace challenges and work together](https://novanta.com/careers-culture/life-at-novanta/) to create meaningful impact.


We are incredibly grateful for our employees and proud of what we have accomplished together. Thank you to everyone who contributed to this remarkable quarter. We look forward to building on this momentum and continuing our success as one team.
