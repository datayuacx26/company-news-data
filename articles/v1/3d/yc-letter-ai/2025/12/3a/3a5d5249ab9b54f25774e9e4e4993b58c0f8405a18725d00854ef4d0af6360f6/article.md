# Letter AI Earns Highest Momentum in Sales Enablement and 21 G2 Winter Badges

We’re excited to share an important milestone for Letter AI and for the enterprise teams we serve.


In the newly released **G2 Winter 2026 Reports** , Letter AI earned **21 badges** across Sales Enablement, AI Chatbots, AI Coaching, and Digital Sales Rooms — up from 6 badges last winter.


Most notably, we received **the highest Momentum Score of any enablement platform** .


For a fast-growing company serving complex, global organizations, this recognition carries weight. It signals that large enterprises are actively modernizing their enablement ecosystem and are choosing AI-native, unified platforms to power it. And it reinforces what our customers — including Lenovo, Adobe, Netflix Ads, DoorDash, Plaid, and Novo Nordisk — experience every day: scalable impact, rapid deployment, and meaningful time savings across their revenue organizations.


## What This Season’s Results Highlight


### Unmatched Momentum in Sales Enablement


The #1 Momentum ranking reflects a broader shift we’re seeing across enterprise GTM teams — a move away from fragmented point solutions and toward consolidated, AI-native platforms that can keep pace with global scale and operational complexity.


### High Performer Recognition Across Segments


Our strongest performance came in Mid-Market and Enterprise categories, where requirements around governance, localization, integrations, and cross-functional alignment are the most demanding. Letter AI is increasingly becoming the system of record for training, content, coaching, and buyer engagement across distributed revenue teams.


### Customer Experience Wins That Matter


This season, we earned multiple awards across the Enterprise and Mid-Market segment for:


- *Easiest to Do Business With*
- *Best Support*
- *Users Most Likely to Recommend*


For teams managing global rollouts, aggressive timelines, and cross-department workflows, these are the categories that matter most. They reflect the reliability, responsiveness, and partnership experience we aim to deliver to every customer.


## The Bigger Story Behind the Badges


Enterprise enablement teams are under pressure: fragmented systems, inconsistent content governance, long onboarding cycles, and the need to support thousands of reps across regions and languages. Traditional tools weren’t built to solve these challenges at scale.


Letter AI was.


Over the past year, we’ve expanded the platform to meet the demands of modern enterprise GTM teams with:


- **AI-powered content management** that centralizes and governs knowledge across Confluence, SharePoint, GDrive, Notion, and more


- **Multi-modal training and adaptive AI coaching** created in minutes — scalable for global onboarding and continuous learning


- **AI agents** that deliver fast, accurate answers across the tools your reps already live in (Slack, Teams, and Letter AI)


- **AI Sales Rooms** that give buyers a personalized, always-on experience and provide teams visibility into buyer intent


- **End-to-end RFP automation** that eliminates 10–40 hour workflows and improves consistency and compliance


Together, these capabilities give enterprise revenue teams what they need most: clarity, speed, governance, and efficiency.
