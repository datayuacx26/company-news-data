# Celebrating Our Customers: Letter AI’s First G2 Report Recognition

As we close out an extraordinary year of growth and innovation at Letter AI, we’re grateful to announce that our platform has been recognized in the G2 Winter 2025 Report. In our G2 report debut, we’ve earned **six badges** across **five different categories** —a milestone made possible by the incredible support of our customers.


This recognition isn’t just a testament to our efforts; it symbolizes the trust and partnership we’ve built with our users. Our platform is making a real, meaningful difference for revenue enablement teams, and this customer impact fuels our commitment to revolutionizing the future of revenue enablement.


## Our Journey of Innovation and Impact


In 2024, our mission was clear: to deliver impactful, practical solutions shaped by our customers' needs. Almost every new feature we introduced was driven by direct customer feedback and requests. Our platform has revolutionized how teams interact with sales learning and content by streamlining key processes (e.g., onboarding, real-time Q&A) and enhancing user experience (e.g., AI-powered sales rooms, frictionless content discovery) through AI. This transformation has enabled our customers to save valuable time previously spent on tasks like course design, RFP responses, roleplay, knowledge retrieval, and more - all while improving the overall experience of their revenue teams and end customers. Quickly, our customers are experiencing how their focus is shifting from time-consuming workflow tasks to high-impact, strategic initiatives.


Our customer impact is evidenced by our customer feedback and reviews, with one customer testimonial highlighting the holistic transformation Letter AI helped them drive:


“Letter AI is enablement from the future. The platform has supercharged the end-to-end revenue journey…from coaching our sellers to accelerating deal cycles. It has made things possible that just weren't doable without AI, helping make the team more productive and win more customers.‍”


## Looking Ahead with Gratitude


Receiving the G2 recognitions is an honor, especially early in the Letter AI journey, but they signify much more than just awards—they reflect our customers’ enduring trust and partnership. Every day, we are inspired by the challenges our users pose to us, pushing us to innovate and improve constantly.


We couldn’t have reached this point without our dedicated community, and we are thrilled to continue this transformative journey together. As we look toward 2025, we remain focused on delivering exceptional value and pushing the limits of what AI can achieve for revenue enablement teams. Here’s to another year of growth, collaboration, and, most importantly, deep gratitude for the amazing community we’ve built together.
