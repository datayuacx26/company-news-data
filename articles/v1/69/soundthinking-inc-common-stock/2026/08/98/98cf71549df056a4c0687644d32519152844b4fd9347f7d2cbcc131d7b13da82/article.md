# Evolv Alternatives: Options for Weapons Detection

Security directors, hospital administrators, and campus safety leaders evaluating Evolv alternatives are often managing the same compounding problem: existing weapons detection systems create as many operational challenges as they solve. Traditional metal detectors require staff to operate at each checkpoint, generate long queues that frustrate visitors and slow facility operations, and pose a risk to individuals with implanted medical devices such as pacemakers. At the same time, many of these systems lack integrated video capture, leaving security teams without the footage they need for investigative follow-up after an incident.


The stakes extend beyond hardware limitations. Accurately identifying armed individuals in no-firearm zones, without disrupting the flow of visitors, patients, or employees, remains one of the hardest problems in facility security. Add to that the pressure to keep sensitive monitoring data compliant with regulatory requirements, justify cost-per-threat ratios to leadership, and integrate new vendor infrastructure without overhauling internal processes, and the case for re-evaluating your current solution becomes difficult to ignore.


## What is Evolv?


[Evolv Technology](https://www.evolvtechnology.com/) is a weapons detection security screening company founded in 2013 in Waltham, MA. Evolv pioneered and developed a touchless security screening system that uses digital sensors and artificial intelligence to detect threats.


Evolv Technology has two main solutions: Evolv Express® and Evolv eXpedite™. Evolv Express is a weapons detection system that screens individuals at an ingress point, similar to traditional metal detectors. According to Evolv, Evolv Express offers a faster, more efficient threat-screening experience than traditional metal detectors, and Evolv eXpedite works with Evolv Express to detect concealed weapons in bags using X-ray imaging and AI.

Powered by CEIA OPENGATE, the Athena Security weapons detection system comprises an HD active walkthrough metal detector and induction lidar, and includes a visual camera, artificial intelligence technologies, a controller tablet, and a monitor tablet. The system is faster than a typical airport or courthouse scanner but less sensitive, meaning it’s less likely to falsely alarm on something like a belt buckle and could be less likely to detect a weapon.
