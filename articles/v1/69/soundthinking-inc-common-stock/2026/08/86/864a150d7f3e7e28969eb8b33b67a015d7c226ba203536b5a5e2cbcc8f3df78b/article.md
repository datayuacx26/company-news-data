# From Detection to Conviction: Forensic-Grade Gunshot Detection Evidence

Camera-mounted microphones on fixed surveillance cameras serve a different function. They capture whatever sound happens to be near the lens at the time the video is being recorded, which makes them valuable for documenting the immediate surroundings.


That design, however, has important limitations when it comes to recording gunfire. A fixed microphone is a single point of audio capture tied only to that camera, so the usefulness of the footage depends entirely upon the camera being close enough to the shooting in question and actually recording at the moment shots are fired. In contrast, ShotSpotter’s networked array captures the same gunfire from multiple locations, with recording triggered by the sound of gunfire itself rather than by motion sensors or timers commonly used by surveillance and doorbell cameras.


Having multiple recordings also provides an important advantage when evaluating the sound of a shooting. While the recording closest to the shooting often provides the clearest representation of the sound and cadence of the gunfire, the surrounding environment can sometimes introduce echoes or other distracting sounds. In those instances, a recording captured from a different location may provide a clearer view of the gunfire itself.


The advantage of a networked system also extends beyond detection and recording to the process of preserving and accessing the evidence. When gunfire *is* captured on a fixed camera, police must then take affirmative steps to obtain the video – often requiring a search warrant – and also must document the chain of custody for that recording. With ShotSpotter alerts, the ILS and audio are instantly available to the agency via the ShotSpotter app. The report and audio remain available on the app for 30 days and can be accessed through the historical database InSight thereafter. Upon request, SoundThinking can provide the ILS as a[court-admissible Certified Business Record](https://www.soundthinking.com/law-enforcement/forensic-services/business-certification-request-form/) , accompanied by an affidavit that authenticates the record.
