# Carlyle to Present at the Morgan Stanley US Financials Conference

[PDF Version](https://ir.carlyle.com/static-files/10477e29-23f5-48f0-b8aa-2d26a80b5ee8)


120.1 KB


**Washington, DC and New York, NY** – Carlyle (NASDAQ: CG) announced today that its Chief Financial Officer, Justin Plouffe, will present at the Morgan Stanley US Financials Conference on Wednesday, June 10, 2026, at approximately 2:30 PM ET.


A live webcast of the presentation will be available on the Investor Relations section of Carlyle’s website at[ir.carlyle.com](https://ir.carlyle.com/) . For those unable to listen to the live webcast, a replay will be available on the same site shortly after the event.


**About Carlyle**


Carlyle (NASDAQ: CG) is a global investment firm with deep industry expertise that deploys private capital across three business segments: Global Private Equity, Global Credit, and Carlyle AlpInvest. With $475 billion of assets under management as of March 31, 2026, Carlyle’s purpose is to invest wisely and create value on behalf of its investors, portfolio companies, and the communities in which we live and invest. Carlyle employs more than 2,500 people in 28 offices across four continents. Further information is available at www.carlyle.com. Follow Carlyle on X @OneCarlyle and LinkedIn at The Carlyle Group.
