LOS ANGELES


, Aug. 6, 2026


/PRNewswire/ --[Cineverse Corp.](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4746942-1&h=3382111338&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.cineverse.com%2F&a=Cineverse+Corp.) (Nasdaq: CNVS), an entertainment technology company and studio, announced today that it will release its financial results for the first quarter fiscal year 2027 ending June 30, 2026, after the market closes on Thursday, August 13, 2026.


Cineverse will host a conference call discussing these results at **4:30 p.m. ET/1:30** **p.m. PT** that same day. The conference call will be accessible online via the[Cineverse Investor Relations website](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4746942-1&h=1444788637&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.cineverse.com%2Fevents-and-presentations%2Fdefault.aspx&a=Cineverse+Investor+Relations+website) , or by clicking here (listen only). To participate, please register in advance to access the live conference call at[this link](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4746942-1&h=3310650340&u=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fattendee%2F516480513&a=this+link) .


An audio recording of the conference call will be available for replay shortly after its completion. To access the replay, visit the Events and Presentations section of the Cineverse Investor Relations website.


**About Cineverse**


**Cineverse** (Nasdaq: CNVS) is an entertainment technology company and studio. Fiercely innovative and independent, Cineverse develops and invests in technology and content that drives the future of the industry. Core to its business is[Matchpoint®](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4746942-1&h=1554110155&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.matchpoint.tv%2F&a=Matchpoint%C2%AE) – a growing tech ecosystem powered by AI and designed to prepare, distribute, monetize, and continuously improve content across any platform. Matchpoint helps studios large and small operate at scale and improve performance and efficiency in an increasingly fragmented distribution environment. Additionally, Cineverse distributes more than 66,000 premium films, series, and podcasts, across theatrical, home entertainment, and streaming; operates dozens of digital properties that super serve passionate fandoms around the world; and works with leading brands to connect them with audiences they value. From award-winning technology to the[highest-grossing unrated film in U.S. history](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4746942-1&h=2164836697&u=https%3A%2F%2Furldefense.proofpoint.com%2Fv2%2Furl%3Fu%3Dhttps-3A__www.boxofficemojo.com_release_rl2221441025%26d%3DDwMFaQ%26c%3DeuGZstcaTDllvimEN8b7jXrwqOf-v5A_CdpgnVfiiMM%26r%3DB2PNf5C_2XADP6dsTgnr53OvDOYVOPW2rehMq7In2oQ%26m%3D1qE6TsvVFs0moFpQ1oHkDO5J1QdYGaC82jEdPf7iiDru9zQol6LbgKvmK12XCNeA%26s%3DH2-gR0tVdDs9908LbD89uxQYb1GF8meDp04NNOWUHAw%26e%3D&a=highest-grossing+unrated+film+in+U.S.+history) , Cineverse has created a playbook that marries tech and content to redefine the next era of entertainment.
