## Royal Gold Presenting at the Mining Forum Europe 2026 Conference


04/02/2026


[Download (opens in new window) PDF 108 KB](https://s28.q4cdn.com/583965976/files/Mining-Forum-Europe-Announcement-FINAL-LH-Apr-2-2026.pdf)


DENVER--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


**Royal Gold, Inc. (NASDAQ: RGLD)** (together with its subsidiaries, “Royal Gold,” or the “Company”) announced today that management will present at the Mining Forum Europe 2026 conference in Zurich, Switzerland. Dan Breeze, Senior Vice President, Corporate Development, RGLD Gold AG, will present to conference participants on Tuesday, April 14, at 9:50 a.m. CEST (3:50 a.m. ET / 1:50 a.m. MT). The 20-minute presentation will be live streamed on the Company’s website at[www.royalgold.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.royalgold.com&esheet=54478686&newsitemid=20260402967697&lan=en-US&anchor=www.royalgold.com&index=1&md5=af4f70d35e6b4d8794a2e4bc87b08820) , under the Investor Resources / Events and Presentations page on our website.


**Webcast Link:**[https://europe.miningforum.com/member-webcast/4284/](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Furldefense.proofpoint.com%2Fv2%2Furl%3Fu%3Dhttps-3A__europe.miningforum.com_member-2Dwebcast_4284_%26d%3DDwMF-g%26c%3DeuGZstcaTDllvimEN8b7jXrwqOf-v5A_CdpgnVfiiMM%26r%3DBBoXu5tYl6E5pmGq6k9vakdGS68g7eLn66MKmofrnRI%26m%3DqFUfrL3qIUlIgwxcgCSH-qMnmCR44y_4suj3uJiCNn9OUBHhiogeJexfU5zF-g1A%26s%3Dc6dqyHgOM8Jmf3G_g9cP6GLS7olwzTTAyOYznuH2yR0%26e%3D&esheet=54478686&newsitemid=20260402967697&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Feurope.miningforum.com%2Fmember-webcast%2F4284%2F&index=2&md5=16a8c7c81951d39e69e8dfcc51cc5742)


A replay will be available for viewing from Thursday, April 16, at 6:00 a.m. CEST (12:00 a.m. ET / Wednesday, April 15, at 10:00 p.m. MT).


**Corporate Profile**


Royal Gold is a high-margin, large-capitalization company that generates strong cash flows from a large and well-diversified portfolio of precious metal streams, royalties and similar production-based interests located in mining-friendly jurisdictions. Royal Gold shares trade under the symbol “RGLD” and provide growth, value, and income investors exposure to the metals and mining industry.
