# Real-Time Closed-Loop Optimization Delivers 43% Titer Improvement

For decades, bioprocess development has relied on fixed recipes, designed upfront and executed without the ability to adapt. But biology doesn’t follow a script. Conditions shift, variability emerges, and even the best models become outdated the moment a run begins.


What if your process could adapt in real time?


In a collaboration between Ark and Culture Biosciences, adaptive digital twins paired with an API-native bioreactor,[Stratyx](https://www.culturebiosciences.com/stratyx) , enabled true closed-loop optimization, delivering a 43% improvement in titer over the historical best.


This marks a shift from static processes to systems that learn and improve as they run. Read the technical bulletin to learn more.
