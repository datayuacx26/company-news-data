# See More in Every Fermentation: Better Data for Faster Microbial Process Development

Microbial fermentation moves fast. Substrate depletion, oxygen demand, metabolic shifts, and feed responses can happen between routine samples, leaving critical changes invisible until it’s too late.


#### **More Than More Runs. More Insight From Every Run.**


##### **Nexxys™ Services helps microbial teams see more in every bioreactor.**


By combining high-throughput experimentation with mass tracking, off-gas analysis, and high-resolution process data,[Nexxys](https://www.culturebiosciences.com/nexxys) delivers deeper insight into fermentation performance beyond conventional sampling alone.


With Console™, teams can remotely monitor experiments and data in real time, enabling faster decisions, smarter strain and feed optimization, stronger process control, and more confident scale-up—without expanding lab footprint.


Two Culture Biosciences studies demonstrate how these integrated measurements turn every run into more actionable insight.


**Better process decisions start with better mass tracking.**


Culture’s mass-balance approach captures feeds, gas delivery, sampling, evaporation, and manual additions to provide real-time reactor mass estimates and more accurate yield and productivity calculations.


Across 500+ runs, more than 75% achieved 97–103% mass-balance closure, giving microbial teams greater confidence that run-to-run differences reflect biology, not measurement variability.


\[Read the white paper: Data Quality & Mass Balance Calculations\]


#### **See Metabolic Changes in Real Time**


Offline samples capture moments. Continuous off-gas analysis reveals the full metabolic picture.


Nexxys continuously measures O₂ and CO₂ across high-throughput fermentations, enabling real-time tracking of oxygen uptake rate (OUR), carbon dioxide evolution rate (CER), and respiratory quotient (RQ). Performance with


*E. coli* and


*S. cerevisiae* showed strong agreement with a commercial analyzer.


The result, earlier insight into shifts in growth and metabolism, enabling smarter feed optimization, tighter process control, and more confident scale-up.
