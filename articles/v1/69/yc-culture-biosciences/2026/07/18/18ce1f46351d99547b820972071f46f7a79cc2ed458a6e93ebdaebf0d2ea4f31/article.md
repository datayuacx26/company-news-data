# High-Density TFF Perfusion on the Stratyx 250 Platform

As bioprocessing teams explore perfusion to increase productivity and extend culture duration, they need a development platform that makes evaluation simple, scalable, and data-rich.


Our latest Technical Bulletin demonstrates the expanded potential of high-density TFF perfusion on the Stratyx™ platform


through a controlled side-by-side comparison with fed-batch. Using the same CHO cell line, inoculum, and operating conditions, TFF perfusion delivered compelling improvements across every major productivity metric.


**Study Highlights**


- **13× higher volumetric productivity**
- **29× more total IgG produced**
- **35-day culture duration while maintaining >90% viability**
- **3.3× higher cell-specific productivity (Qp)**


These results demonstrate how Stratyx can support the evaluation of high-density perfusion alongside traditional fed-batch workflows on a single automated platform, giving process development teams the flexibility to compare strategies while capturing comprehensive process data.
