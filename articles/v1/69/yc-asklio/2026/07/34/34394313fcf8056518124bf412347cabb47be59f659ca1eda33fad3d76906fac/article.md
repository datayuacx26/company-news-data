# Enterprise Systems Aren’t the Bottleneck: Manual Execution Is

## **The Systems Work. The Work Inside Them Doesn’t Scale.**


Large enterprises already run on powerful ERP & Procurement systems. SAP. Oracle. Ariba. Coupa. These platforms manage data, approvals, workflows, storage, compliance and are the system of record - the operational backbone of the enterprise.


The infrastructure exists. The interface exists.The data layer exists. What doesn’t scale is the manual work happening inside it.


Inside those systems, humans still do the execution. Most companies don’t want to rip out their ERP or procurement suite. These systems already contain critical approval workflows, compliance logic, and financial controls. The problem is that the user experience is often - bluntly - horrible.


So the question isn’t: How do we replace SAP or Coupa? It’s: How do we remove the manual execution happening inside them?
