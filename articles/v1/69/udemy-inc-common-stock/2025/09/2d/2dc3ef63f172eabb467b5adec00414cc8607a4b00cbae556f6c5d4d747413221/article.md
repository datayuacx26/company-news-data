# Introducing Connect 1-1 (beta) – Get Personalized Guidance from Industry Experts


September 25, 2025 By Chad Meirose


[Udemy News](https://about.udemy.com/category/udemy-news/)


NEW! Get the coaching you need, at the right time, with the right coach. Personalized guidance with a real coach who understands your journey.


Connect 1-1 offers personalized support for your upskilling goals — whether it’s to better understand course material, prepare for a certification exam, or get career advice.


You know that moment when you’re working through a course and suddenly hit a wall? Or when you finish learning something new but aren’t sure how to apply it to your specific situation or career goals? You’re not alone. In a


[study done by MIT Sloan](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/ideas-made-to-matter/to-keep-employees-focus-career-advancement) , it was found that “67% percent of the individual contributors surveyed said they want to advance their career, but 49% said a lack of good career advice has hurt their job trajectory.”


That’s why we’re excited to introduce Connect 1-1 — personalized coaching that connects you with your favorite instructors for the course guidance you need.


**Access experts who’ve taught thousands of learners directly and gained practical expertise from being hands on with their topic.**


Whether you need to overcome specific challenges, validate your approach, or get unstuck when you hit roadblocks, you can schedule 1-1 time with the instructor you just took a course from for guidance on your next steps.


**Coaching that fits your everyday life**


Learn on your time with smart scheduling that lets you choose available times that work for you — not when a class happens to meet. Get reminders so you never miss your appointment, plus fair cancellation policies you can review before you book.


**Grow your career with advice from the industry**


[Choose from a growing set of Udemy instructors](https://www.canva.com/design/DAGz6L86tPc/r-ogyjKawQD8vKFJSoGYEg/view?utm_content=DA[%E2%80%A6]hare&utm_medium=link2&utm_source=uniquelinks&utlId=had4cca6428) from a wide variety of industries and backgrounds. These instructors are making themselves available to mentor learners seeking career advice, code reviews, step-by-step career strategies, and more.


**Transparent pricing that works for you**


Pay per session with transparent pricing so you can invest in the sessions you want without long-term commitments.


**Getting started is simple**


Browse available coaches by topic, read instructor profiles and reviews, and book sessions through our streamlined system as you’re taking a course..


With Udemy, you have all the support you need to achieve your goals. Connect 1-1 is now available with select instructors across various topics, with plans to expand based on your feedback.


Ready to accelerate your learning journey?
