# Udemy Accelerates Career Journeys with In-Demand AWS Certification Vouchers


December 16, 2025 By Paxton Mittleman


[Udemy News](https://about.udemy.com/category/udemy-news/)


As a true career companion, Udemy is committed to continuously expanding our offerings so we can help more professionals around the globe accelerate their growth. That’s why we’re thrilled to share that we’ve expanded our resources for AWS certification learners to include exam vouchers for 12 AWS certifications.


Now, learners in the US will enjoy 10% savings on AWS exam vouchers. Learners will also get AWS certification exam reminders through email, and guidance to help them stay on track with exam scheduling.This creates a comprehensive, end-to-end solution that enables our professional community to achieve their goals– proving skills mastery through certification.


#### **Why certifications matter more than ever**


With tech innovations rapidly reshaping what’s needed to succeed in jobs, certifications are imperative for professionals validating their skills mastery.


[Udemy research](http://business.udemy.com/resources/yougov-research-ai-transformation/) found that 59% of US hiring managers rank validation of skill sets through methods like certifications as the most important factor in hiring entry-level talent, far more than those prioritizing traditional college degrees (19%).


In response, professionals are eager to pursue these certifications to demonstrate their skill sets. According to Udemy’s


[2026 Global Learning & Skills Trends Report,](https://business.udemy.com/2026-global-learning-skills-trends-report/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic-search) course consumption for AWS Certified Data Engineer – Associate on Udemy Business increased 183% over the past year. Over half of the top ten cloud computing skills taken on Udemy this past year were linked to AWS certifications.


Yet, the journey to certification remains challenging due to exam costs and learners relying on siloed platforms. With more than 10M certification prep enrollments across Udemy in the past 12 months, offering these AWS vouchers simplifies access for learners to register for several certifications, empowering learners with the tools they need to earn key credentials and accelerate their careers.
