# Reimagine your career with Career Accelerators


May 29, 2025 By Kiran Umapathy


[Udemy News](https://about.udemy.com/category/udemy-news/)


These days, there’s no shortage of information on anything — including career guidance. The real questions are:


**Where do I start?**


**What’s the best use of my time?** **What actually works?**


Sometimes (or all the time), you just want a clear, no-nonsense path that sets you up for success. At Udemy, we’ve spent time listening to our nearly 80M learners and we heard our learners tell us about their desire for outcome-based learning (in this case with the goal of getting a job, changing jobs or increasing earnings potential). So, that’s why we created


[Career Accelerators](https://www.udemy.com/career-accelerators/) .


**What are Career Accelerators?**


All Udemy Personal Plan users can now access Career Accelerators, curated programs designed to help you launch future-ready careers in tech, business, and more (and yes of course there is some AI sprinkled in there). We’ve removed the guesswork to let you focus on building the skills and expertise you need to break into a new career.


**Who are Career Accelerators for?**


Career Accelerators are great for anyone thinking about changing careers, staying competitive, or just starting out. You’ll learn fundamental and practical skills to fast-track an exciting career in IT, Marketing, and more.


They’re built to meet you where you are, with no prior experience required.


**What do Career Accelerators include?**


Each Career Accelerator includes:


- Top-rated courses to help you build job-ready skills


- Hands-on practice exercises to gain real-world experience and confidence


- Interview prep support that helps you stand out and land your dream job


Our content experts use AI and the human touch to curate every course using data-driven insights from our global community of nearly 80 million learners.


You’ll learn at your own pace — and at a price that is way more accessible compared to other career-focused programs and bootcamps.


**What careers does it cover?**


We decided to launch Career Accelerators with six (6) of the most popular and in-demand careers including:


**** **** **Full-Stack Web Developer**


Get all the fundamentals for a


[career in web development](https://blog.udemy.com/what-does-a-web-developer-do/) . Learn JavaScript, React, and Node.js. You’ll also gain an understanding of data structures and algorithms.


**Digital Marketer**


[Get into digital marketing](https://blog.udemy.com/how-to-get-into-digital-marketing/) with essentials like SEO and social ad campaigns. Level up with the latest AI tools to boost your results.


**Data Scientist**


Develop skills in statistics, Python, deep learning, and SQL. Prep for interviews with


[top data science interview questions](https://blog.udemy.com/data-science-interview-questions/) .


**Cloud Engineer**


Learn the basics of


[serverless computing](https://blog.udemy.com/aws-lambda-guide/) and prep for the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner Exam.


**Game Developer**


Learn game design fundamentals, 3D modeling, and much more. You’ll even code your first video game.


**Project Manager**


Learn essentials every organization needs like


[agile project management](https://blog.udemy.com/agile-project-management/) , scrum, and business analysis. You’ll also prep for the Project Management Professional (PMP) exam.


We’ll be adding many more Career Accelerators soon, but we want to learn from you — what Career Accelerator would you like to see next?


Remember, your career isn’t something you find. It’s something you build. If you’re ready to take the next step (and all the ones after that) let Udemy help you on your journey and accelerate your start.
