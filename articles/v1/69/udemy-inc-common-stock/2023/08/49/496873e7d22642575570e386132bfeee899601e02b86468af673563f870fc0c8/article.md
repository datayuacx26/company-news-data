# Learners Flock To Semiconductor And AI Skills Courses, Udemy Finds

The Udemy Q2 2023 Global Workplace Learning Index revealed an uptick in demand for semiconductor and generative AI skills, especially in non-tech sectors including HR, sales and marketing. “We are in the midst of a shift to a skills-based economy where organizations are identifying the skills available within their teams and addressing any gaps,” says Caoimhe Carlos, vice president of global customer success at Udemy.
