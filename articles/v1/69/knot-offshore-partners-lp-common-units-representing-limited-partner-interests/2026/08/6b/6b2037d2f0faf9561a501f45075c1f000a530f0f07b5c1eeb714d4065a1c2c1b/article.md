## KNOT Offshore Partners LP Announces 2nd Quarter 2026 Earnings Results Conference Call

ABERDEEN, Scotland--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


KNOT Offshore Partners LP (NYSE:KNOP) ( *“* *the Partnership”)* plans to release its financial results for the 2nd Quarter of 2026 prior to opening of the market on Friday, September 4, 2026.


The Partnership also plans to host a conference call on Friday, September 4, 2026, at 9:30 AM (Eastern Time) to discuss the results for the 2nd Quarter of 2026. All unitholders and interested parties are invited to join via the live webcast link on the Partnership’s website:[www.knotoffshorepartners.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.knotoffshorepartners.com&esheet=54586819&newsitemid=20260810820766&lan=en-US&anchor=www.knotoffshorepartners.com&index=1&md5=ad71f131dec2da0fc972d4167687ca10) .


A replay of the webcast will be available at the same link following the conclusion of the live call.


Our 2nd Quarter 2026 Earnings Presentation will also be available at[www.knotoffshorepartners.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.knotoffshorepartners.com%2F&esheet=54586819&newsitemid=20260810820766&lan=en-US&anchor=www.knotoffshorepartners.com&index=2&md5=ad8855ed4f045b5931db2177cf452343) prior to the conference call start time.


***About KNOT Offshore Partners LP***


KNOT Offshore Partners LP owns, operates and acquires shuttle tankers primarily under long-term charters in the offshore oil production regions of Brazil and the North Sea.


KNOT Offshore Partners LP is structured as a publicly traded master limited partnership but is classified as a corporation for U.S. federal income tax purposes, and thus issues a Form 1099 to its unitholders, rather than a Form K-1. KNOT Offshore Partners LP’s common units trade on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol “KNOP”.
