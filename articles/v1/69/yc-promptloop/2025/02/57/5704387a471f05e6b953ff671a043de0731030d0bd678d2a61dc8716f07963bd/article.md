# Real-Time Map Data: Scale Local Business Research with AI

For teams targeting local businesses, manual location research has always been a major bottleneck. Sales development representatives often spend hours searching through map listings to identify potential customers, verify business information, and build targeted outreach lists.


Today, we're excited to announce Map Data: a powerful new addition to PromptLoop's research capabilities that eliminates this manual work and helps you identify qualified prospects faster than ever before.


## Transform Geographic Research with AI #


Map Data integrates seamlessly into PromptLoop's existing task framework, enabling you to:


- Search for businesses across specific geographic areas
- Extract detailed location data for thousands of businesses simultaneously
- Keep your customer and prospect data automatically up-to-date
- Validate business information across multiple sources


Our early users are already seeing dramatic improvements: "Using PromptLoop's Map Data, we've cut our location research time by 85% and increased our qualified lead volume by identifying businesses that match our ideal customer profile in specific regions," shares one customer in field services.


## How It Works #


Getting started with Map Data is straightforward:


1. Choose the Map Data task template from our library
2. Specify your geographic area (currently supporting all U.S. locations)
3. Enter your search terms
4. Run the task across your desired dataset


The system handles the complexity of geographic boundaries and overlapping regions automatically, ensuring comprehensive coverage while maintaining data accuracy.


## Built for Scale #


Map Data is designed to handle everything from targeted local searches to nationwide analysis:


- Run searches across multiple geographic areas simultaneously
- Process thousands of locations in minutes
