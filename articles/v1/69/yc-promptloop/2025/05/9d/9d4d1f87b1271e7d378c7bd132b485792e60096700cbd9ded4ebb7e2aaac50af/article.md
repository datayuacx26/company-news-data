# How can you add AI research to HubSpot?

Customer relationship management (CRM) platforms like HubSpot have become the backbone of modern sales and marketing teams. Yet, as the volume and complexity of B2B data grow, teams face a new challenge: how to keep their CRM records accurate, actionable, and up-to-date without spending hours on manual research. The answer lies in integrating advanced AI research tools directly into HubSpot, allowing teams to automate data enrichment, validate leads, and extract critical company insights at scale.


## Why AI Research Matters for HubSpot Users #


**The Data Challenge in B2B Sales**


Sales and go‐to‐market (GTM) teams rely on accurate company data to prioritize leads, personalize outreach, and allocate resources. According to industry research, sales reps spend up to 21% of their time researching prospects and updating CRM records. This manual process not only slows down pipeline velocity but also introduces errors and inconsistencies that can impact conversion rates.


**AI-Powered Research: A New Standard**


Recent advances in AI, including large language models and automated data extraction, have made it possible to process vast amounts of information in seconds. By integrating AI research into HubSpot, teams can:


- Automatically enrich company and contact records with up-to-date data
- Validate sales leads before outreach
- Extract specific datapoints from thousands of web sources
- Reduce manual research time and focus on high-value activities


For example, a sales team can use AI to instantly pull recent funding rounds, technology stacks, or executive changes for every new lead added to HubSpot, ensuring outreach is timely and relevant.
