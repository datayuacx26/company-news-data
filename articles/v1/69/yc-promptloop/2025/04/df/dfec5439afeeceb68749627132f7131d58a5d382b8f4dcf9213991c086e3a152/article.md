# Beyond Basic Contact Data: Next-Gen Lead Enrichment Platforms

Every year, sales teams lose up to 30-50% of their CRM data to decay, leading to missed opportunities and wasted outreach efforts[\[1\]](https://www.cognism.com/blog/lead-enrichment-tools) . In a market where timing and precision drive revenue, relying on outdated or incomplete lead information is a risk few can afford. The new generation of lead enrichment platforms is changing the way B2B teams approach prospecting, validation, and data-driven sales. This article explores how advanced solutions like PromptLoop are setting a new standard for sales data enrichment, and what that means for your go-to-market strategy.


## The Shift from Basic Contact Data to Intelligent Enrichment #


**Why sales teams need more than just names and emails**


Traditional lead enrichment tools focused on filling in missing contact fields—phone numbers, job titles, company names. But as buying cycles grow more complex and personalization becomes the norm, sales teams need deeper insights. Modern platforms now extract firmographics, technographics, intent signals, and even behavioral data from thousands of sources in real time[\[1\]](https://www.cognism.com/blog/lead-enrichment-tools)[\[2\]](https://salesintel.io/blog/lead-enrichment-tools/)[\[3\]](https://www.artisan.co/blog/b2b-data-enrichment-tools) .
