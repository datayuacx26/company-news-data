# 10X Your Sales Pipeline with Advanced Lead Enrichment Tools

Every year, B2B sales teams lose up to 70% of their database value to data decay, according to Salesforce. That means outdated contacts, missed opportunities, and wasted hours spent chasing leads that go nowhere. In a market where speed and accuracy drive revenue, relying on incomplete or stale data is a risk few can afford. The difference between a stagnant pipeline and a thriving one often comes down to the quality of your lead data—and how quickly you can act on it.


## Why Lead Enrichment Is the Backbone of Modern Sales #


### What is Lead Enrichment? #


Lead enrichment is the process of supplementing basic lead information with additional, actionable data points. This includes details like job titles, company size, industry, social profiles, and buying intent. The goal is to transform a simple contact into a complete profile, giving sales teams the context they need to prioritize, personalize, and close deals faster[\[1\]](https://www.cognism.com/blog/lead-enrichment-tools)[\[2\]](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/ai-powered-data-enrichment-software-8299ff4b62) .
