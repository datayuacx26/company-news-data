# Automate Lead Research in 2025 with AI Agents

Lead research has traditionally consumed countless hours of sales teams' time, with professionals manually scouring websites, databases, and social platforms to gather prospect intelligence. However, the landscape of B2B prospecting is undergoing a dramatic transformation as AI agents emerge as the definitive solution for automated lead research in 2025.


## The Lead Research Revolution #


The statistics paint a clear picture of both challenge and opportunity in today's lead generation landscape. The global lead generation solutions market reached $3.1 billion in revenue in 2021 and is projected to reach $15.5 billion by 2031, representing a staggering 17.48% compound annual growth[\[1\]](https://sopro.io/resources/blog/lead-generation-statistics/) . This explosive growth reflects the increasing demand for sophisticated, automated approaches to lead research and qualification.


Yet challenges remain significant. In the last year, 45% of businesses reported struggling to generate enough leads[\[2\]](https://www.saleshandy.com/blog/lead-generation-statistics/) , while 31% of marketers say proving ROI is among their biggest challenges while generating leads[\[3\]](https://axis-intelligence.com/marketing-automation-lead-nurturing-software/) . These pain points underscore the critical need for more efficient, measurable approaches to lead research.
