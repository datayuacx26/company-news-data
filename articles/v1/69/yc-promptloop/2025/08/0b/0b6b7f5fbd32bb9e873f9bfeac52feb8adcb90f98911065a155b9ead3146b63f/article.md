# 5 Smart Moves to Scale Lead Quality Without Extra Work

No matter what industry you're in, data is the engine that drives everything. It can shape your marketing campaigns, guide your sales pitches, and help you make smarter investments.


Even if you have a big, impressive database, there's a catch. For data to be valuable, it has to be current, verified, and accurate. Otherwise, you're just throwing darts in the dark.


The challenge for modern sales teams isn't just collecting data—it's scaling lead quality efficiently without burning out their teams on manual research. Salesforce reports that sales reps spend only 30% of their time selling during an average week, with HubSpot research suggesting sales reps spend only 2 hours per day selling[\[1\]](https://www.salesgenie.com/blog/sales-productivity-statistics/) .


According to a study by Marketo, 80% of marketers believe that lead enrichment is essential for achieving their sales goals[\[2\]](https://superagi.com/future-of-lead-enrichment-5-trends-that-will-revolutionize-your-sales-funnel-in-2024/) , while companies that use lead enrichment tools experience a 25% increase in sales productivity and a 15% decrease in sales cycles[\[3\]](https://superagi.com/10-lead-enrichment-tools-to-supercharge-your-sales-team-in-2024/) .


Here are five smart moves that will transform how your team approaches lead quality without adding to their workload.
