As the HVAC industry continues to grow, sales teams face an increasing challenge: how to efficiently research and qualify thousands of potential customers. Traditional methods of manually visiting websites and gathering information are no longer sustainable. ## The Challenge of HVAC Market Research #


Sales teams in the HVAC space typically need to evaluate potential customers across several key dimensions:


- Company size and service capacity
- Geographic coverage area
- Types of services offered (residential, commercial, emergency services)
- Technology stack (Service Titan, House Call Pro, etc.)
- Company ownership and decision makers
- Multiple location validation


Manually researching these data points across thousands of companies isn't just time-consuming—it's often impossible to maintain accuracy and consistency at scale.
