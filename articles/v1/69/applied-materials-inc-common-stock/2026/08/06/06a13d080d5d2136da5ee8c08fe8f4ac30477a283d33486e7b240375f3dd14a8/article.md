# Applied Materials to Participate in Upcoming Investor Conferences

[PDF Version](https://ir.appliedmaterials.com/node/29566/pdf)


SANTA CLARA, Calif., Aug. 14, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Applied Materials, Inc. today announced that members of its management team will participate in fireside chats at upcoming investor conferences.


Brice Hill, Senior Vice President and CFO, will participate at the Citi 2026 Global TMT Conference on Tuesday, Sept. 8 beginning at 5:50 a.m. PT / 8:50 a.m. ET.


Gary Dickerson, President and CEO, will participate at the Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference on Wednesday, Sept. 9 beginning at 2:25 p.m. PT / 5:25 p.m. ET.


A live audio webcast of each session will be available on the Applied Materials website at:[https://ir.appliedmaterials.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=vBC9-qMwLmkrok8Ez87AlqdXEvCwY690Ya2r4nIktmrJ4asybP6zOVD6bZfg4kOWc-JCBsjppw4fA39EnTQL-xEbSbCybODFPS1uWQgKsKx_iQJB9i3CaJOxJCI3vg-8) with a replay available the same day.
