### **Why Travel Can Change Your Hair**


Travel exposes your hair to a completely different environment than it's used to. Dry airplane cabins can deplete moisture, changing humidity can affect texture and definition, and unfamiliar water may leave your hair feeling different from it does at home. Add in extra sun exposure, chlorine, salt water, and changes to your regular wash routine, and it's easy for your hair to become dry, frizzy, or harder to manage. That's why packing travel-sized versions of your everyday essentials is one of the easiest ways to keep your hair routine consistent wherever you're headed.


One of the biggest challenges is hotel water. Water quality varies from place to place, and many hotels have hard water that contains high levels of minerals like calcium and magnesium. These minerals can build up on the hair, creating a film that makes it harder for moisture to penetrate. After a week, this can leave hair feeling rough, looking dull, and becoming more susceptible to dryness, frizz, and breakage.

### Straight Hair: Keep It Light


Straight hair is naturally prone to oil buildup, especially when travel leads to skipped washes or warmer, more humid weather. Pack travel sizes of


[Straight Hair Shampoo and Straight Hair Conditioner](https://functionofbeauty.com/pages/shop-all-straight-hair) to help maintain your routine. If you're extending time between washes, a travel-size dry shampoo can refresh your roots and absorb excess oil without weighing your hair down.


### Wavy Hair: Help Your Waves Bounce Back


Wavy hair is especially responsive to changes in humidity and moisture, which can leave waves looking flat one day and frizzy the next. Bring your


[Wavy Hair Shampoo and Wavy Hair Conditioner](https://functionofbeauty.com/pages/shop-all-wavy-hair) , along with Zero Gravity Styling Mousse to help redefine your natural wave pattern after washing. Applying mousse to damp hair and scrunching gently can help restore shape and movement throughout your trip.


### Curly Hair: Focus on Hydration


Curly hair naturally loses moisture more quickly than straighter hair types, making dry airplane cabins, unfamiliar water, and changing climates even more noticeable. Pack


[Curly Hair Shampoo and Curly Hair Conditioner](https://functionofbeauty.com/pages/shop-all-curly-hair) , and make room for Super Shape Curl Cream. Applying curl cream after washing helps lock in moisture, define curls, and reduce frizz so your curls stay healthy-looking from the first day of your trip to the last.


### Coily Hair: Lock In Moisture


Coily hair benefits from rich hydration and longer-lasting moisture, both of which can be challenged during travel. Dry cabin air, climate changes, and disruptions to your normal routine can leave coils feeling drier than usual. Pack


[Coily Hair Shampoo and Coily Hair Conditioner](https://functionofbeauty.com/pages/shop-all-curly-hair) , and consider bringing a Custom Hair Serum to help seal in moisture, smooth the hair, and nourish your ends between wash days.
