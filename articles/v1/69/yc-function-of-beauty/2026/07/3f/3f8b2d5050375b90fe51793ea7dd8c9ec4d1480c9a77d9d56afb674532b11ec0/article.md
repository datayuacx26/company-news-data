# How to Get a Salon Worthy Blowout at Home

A great blowout isn't just about technique. It's about working with your hair type, protecting your hair from heat, and creating a routine that builds smoothness, volume, and shine from the start.


Whether your hair is straight, wavy, curly, or coily, understanding how heat changes your hair's structure can help you achieve longer-lasting results with less damage.


## Key Takeaways


-


The best blowouts start with clean, conditioned hair that's matched to your hair type.


-


Heat protection is essential for helping reduce moisture loss and protecting the hair cuticle.


-


Drying your hair in sections creates smoother, longer-lasting results.


-


Allowing each section to cool helps lock in shape and volume.


-


Lightweight styling products help support movement, reduce frizz, and improve longevity without weighing hair down.


## Why Hair Prep Matters


Your blowout starts long before you pick up a blow dryer.


Healthy-looking blowouts begin with hair that's clean, hydrated, and conditioned for your hair type. Product buildup, excess oil, or dehydrated hair can all affect how your style looks and how long it lasts.

## Step 2: Rough Dry First


Instead of immediately reaching for a round brush, begin by rough drying your hair until it's about 70-80% dry.


Use your fingers to lift the roots while directing airflow downward. This removes excess moisture, shortens styling time, and reduces the amount of heat your hair experiences overall.


## Step 3: Choose the Right Tools


Your technique matters, but so do your tools.


Always use a concentrator nozzle on your blow dryer. Focused airflow helps smooth the cuticle for a shinier finish while giving you better control.


Choose your brush based on your styling goals:


-


Large round brush:


Creates volume and movement.


-


Medium round brush:


Adds body with soft bends.


-


Paddle brush:


Creates a sleek, smooth finish.


## Step 4: Work in Sections


Divide your hair into manageable sections before styling.


Working with smaller sections allows heat to reach each piece evenly while maintaining the tension needed for a smooth finish.


Place your brush underneath each section and slowly pull through while following closely with your dryer. Keep the airflow moving from roots to ends to encourage the cuticle to lie flat.


Continue until each section is completely dry before moving to the next.


## Step 5: Let Your Hair Cool


Heat temporarily reshapes the hydrogen bonds inside your hair, but cooling is what helps set your style.


After drying each section, leave it wrapped around the brush or pin it loosely for several seconds before releasing. This simple step helps improve volume, bounce, and longevity.


## Step 6: Finish Your Style


Once your hair has completely cooled, finish with a few drops of

If you want additional volume, gently lift your roots with your fingertips instead of adding more heat.

### Coily Hair

## The Bottom Line


A salon-worthy blowout isn't about using the highest heat or the most products. It's about understanding how your hair responds to moisture, heat, and styling.


When you start with products personalized to your hair type and goals, protect your hair during styling, and use the right technique, you can create a smooth, voluminous blowout that lasts longer while helping keep your hair healthy over time.
