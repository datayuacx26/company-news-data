# Kudos: Give every achievement more visibility

Every recognition has the potential to inspire more participation when it reaches more people. With these updates, the **Kudos** module introduces new ways to highlight achievements across your organization and make the rewards catalog accessible from more devices.


## Help every recognition reach more people


You can now **notify your entire organization** whenever an employee receives a recognition, ensuring achievements extend beyond the Recognition feed.


With this feature, you can:


- **Share every recognition with the entire organization** through a notification.
- **Reach more employees** , even if they don’t regularly visit the Recognition module.
- **Enable or disable these notifications** based on your organization’s communication strategy.


This gives every recognition the opportunity to engage more people and reinforce a culture of appreciation.


## Find kudos faster


To make it easier to locate specific recognitions, we’ve introduced new category filters.


You can now:


- **Filter the Recognition feed by category** to see only the recognitions you’re looking for.
- **Switch easily between categories** or return to the full feed at any time.
- **Browse all available recognition categories.**


This makes it much faster to explore recognition history, even in organizations with a high volume of posts.


## Accede al catálogo de premios desde la web


Ahora, quienes formen parte de un programa de puntos también pueden consultar el catálogo de premios y realizar canjes directamente desde la versión web de Humand.


Desde la web podrás:


- **Consultar los puntos disponibles** acumulados.
- **Explorar el catálogo de premios** antes de realizar un canje.
- **Canjear premios** durante los períodos habilitados por la organización.
- **Revisar el historial de canjes** para conocer el estado de cada solicitud.


De esta forma, cada colaborador puede participar del programa de reconocimientos desde el dispositivo que utilice en su día a día.


## Rewards catalog from the web


Employees participating in a points-based recognition program can now browse the rewards catalog and redeem rewards directly from Humand’s web platform.


From the web, employees can:


- **View their available points.**
- **Browse the rewards catalog** before redeeming points.
- **Redeem rewards** during the redemption periods defined by the organization.
- **Review their redemption history** to track the status of each request.


This allows employees to participate in the recognition program from whichever device they use every day.


## Build a more visible culture of recognition


These enhancements make recognition a more visible part of the everyday employee experience. Whether employees are discovering recognitions, exploring rewards, or redeeming points, they can now do so more easily across both web and mobile.


Explore these new capabilities and continue building a recognition culture that encourages more people to celebrate great work.
