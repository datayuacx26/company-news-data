# Brand guidelines

We are pleased to share our brand guidelines with you to ensure Cardinal Health is clearly and accurately identified in the media.


###


### **Our name is Cardinal Health**


Always refer to our company as “Cardinal Health” in spoken and written communications. Our company name is not “Cardinal” and Cardinal Health is always written as two words. Do not replicate the wordmark portion of the logo.


### **Official logo, tagline and protection**


The Cardinal Health logo is trademarked worldwide to ensure legal protection. Always use official master art for our logo - never recreate it. Official logo files as well as high-res versions for editorial usage by news media can be requested by emailingmedia.relations@cardinalhealth.com or calling 614.757.4250. All other logo requests should go tobrand@cardinalhealth.com .
