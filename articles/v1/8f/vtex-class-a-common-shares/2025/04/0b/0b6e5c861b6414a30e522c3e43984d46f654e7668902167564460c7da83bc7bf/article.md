# Elevating CPG Commerce: Nagarro’s Maya AI and Cashmere on VTEX

The digital commerce landscape for Consumer Packaged Goods (CPG) brands is shifting away from traditional monolithic architectures, which struggle to provide hyper-personalized, omnichannel experiences. To meet this need, Nagarro has introduced **Cashmere** , a composable commerce accelerator built on the VTEX platform, and **Maya** , an advanced AI shopping assistant. Together, they redefine conversational commerce, helping CPG brands launch faster and scale seamlessly.


## The Challenge: Complexity in Modern CPG Commerce


CPG brands face compounding challenges when expanding direct-to-consumer (D2C) and B2B channels. They must manage complex catalogs, high SKU volumes, subscription models, and multi-region support—often while navigating the tension between building direct consumer relationships and preserving retail partnerships. Traditional platforms are too slow and rigid, making a flexible, fast-to-market architecture essential for digital agility.


## The Foundation: Cashmere on VTEX


Cashmere is a pre-configured, modular architecture accelerator built on VTEX, an enterprise commerce platform with a true MACH (Microservices, API-first, Cloud-native, Headless) architecture. This setup decouples the frontend (React/Next.js) from the backend, allowing rapid innovation without disrupting core operations.


VTEX enables Cashmere to integrate smoothly with ERP and CRM systems while providing enterprise-grade security and multi-region capabilities. Key benefits include:

## Maya: Humanizing Digital Retail


Embedded within Cashmere is Maya, an AI-powered conversational shopping assistant that transforms simple personalization into a guided, consultative journey. Maya uses natural language processing (voice and text) and progressive profiling to understand customer needs—such as specific skin concerns or budget constraints—mimicking an in-store advisor.

### Key Features of Maya


- **Guided Consultations:** Adapts conversational flow based on intent, such as pivoting to budget-based filtering for gift shoppers.
- **Inline Recommendations:** Deeply integrated with VTEX, Maya generates rich product cards within the chat featuring “It’s a match ✨” badges, images, and live pricing.
- **Contextual Memory:** Remembers user profiles across the conversation to suggest complementary products and build routines.
- **Dual-Mode Experience:** Acts as a general discovery assistant on the storefront and a focused advisor on individual product pages to increase conversion confidence.


## VTEX as the Strategic Backbone


Maya’s real-time contextual orchestration relies on VTEX’s API-first architecture, which pulls live inventory and pricing data in real time. Furthermore, VTEX captures Maya’s insights (e.g., skin concerns, purchase intent) as first-party data, informing inventory planning and campaign targeting.
