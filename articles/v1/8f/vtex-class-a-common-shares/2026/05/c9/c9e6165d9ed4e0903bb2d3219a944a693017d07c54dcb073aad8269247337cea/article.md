# Modernizing the Fan Experience: Manchester City and VTEX Launch New Stadium Tour Digital Commerce Journey

A new step in the partnership between the global sports organization **Manchester City** and **VTEX** , the backbone for connected commerce, is now **LIVE** with the launch of the updated Manchester City Stadium Tour experience:[mancity.com/store/products/tours](https://www.mancity.com/store/products/tours) .


When a global sports organization like Manchester City evolves its digital brand presence, the priority is clear: creating a foundation built on speed, reliability, and seamless integration across bookings and commerce.

## Engineering a Unified Commerce Foundation


The focus of this project phase is on integrating the Stadium Tour journey with tour add-ons. By leveraging VTEX’s composable technology, the goal is to orchestrate complex business requirements into a single, unified purchase flow.


Rather than viewing different services as separate digital silos, this approach enables a more holistic view of the fan journey. By utilizing a unified commerce architecture, the project aims to achieve:


- **A Streamlined Path to Purchase:** Leveraging a more intuitive flow to ensure the journey from selection to checkout is fast and efficient.
- **Reduced Journey Friction:** Optimizing the number of steps required for fans to complete their bookings and associated tour purchases.
- **Organizational Alignment:** Enabling a shared technical vision across various business units, including tours and experiences, to support an end-to-end commerce strategy.


## Continuous Digital Evolution


This launch represents a significant milestone in our collaborative journey. As digital continues to play a pivotal role in connecting clubs with their global fans, this new foundation provides the scalability needed for future innovation.
