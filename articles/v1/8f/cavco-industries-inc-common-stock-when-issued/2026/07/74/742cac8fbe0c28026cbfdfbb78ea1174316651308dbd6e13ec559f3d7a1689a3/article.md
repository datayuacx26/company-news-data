Here's a number that should stop more people in their tracks: according to the most recent data, the average new[manufactured home](https://www.cavcohomes.com/find-a-floor-plan) sold for


**$115,557** in 2025.


For context, the average onsite-built home in the U.S. costs well over


**$400,000** . That gap is huge – and still widely ignored – and it's exactly why


**20 million** Americans are living in offsite-built manufactured homes right now.


If you're thinking about buying a manufactured home or just trying to cut through the noise about what offsite-built homes actually cost and deliver, this is the information you need to see.


## What Does a Manufactured Home Cost?


The national average price of a new manufactured home in 2025 was $115,557, according to[MHInsider](https://mhinsider.com/) ‘s 2026


[State of the Manufactured Housing Industry](https://mhinsider.com/manufactured-housing-industry-trends-statistics/) report. That breaks down into two product tiers: single-section homes averaged


**$95,074** , while multi-section homes – larger, with more of a traditional residential footprint – averaged


**$156,170** .


The cost-per-square-foot figure is even more telling. New manufactured homes averaged


**$101.20** per square foot in 2025. Multi-section homes came in at


**$110.26** per square foot, single-section at


**$95.17** . Compare that to onsite-built construction costs, which routinely exceed


**$200** per square foot in most markets, and the value equation is hard to ignore.


The average resale price of an existing manufactured home was


**$73,326** in 2025 – a


**2.3 percent** increase year-over-year. Affordability and value hold even in the secondary market.


Infographic courtesy of[MHInsider’s 2026 State of the Industry Report](https://mhinsider.com/manufactured-housing-industry-trends-statistics/)


## How Quickly Do Manufactured Homes Sell?


Pretty fast. Some markets are moving homes in


**under three weeks** .


According to data from Datacomp,


MHVillage


and the[Manufactured Housing Institute](https://www.manufacturedhousing.org/) , the market with the fewest days on market in 2025 was Erie, Pennsylvania at just


**20 days** . Dubuque, Iowa followed at 29 days. Lawton, Oklahoma hit 32 days. Charleston, South Carolina and Oklahoma City both cleared in 38 days.


This means there’s genuine demand for manufactured homes. When homes move that quickly, it reflects a market where buyers are ready, inventory is tight and perceived value is real.

## Do Manufactured Home Owners Actually Like Their Homes?


Yes – and by margins that would make most homebuilders envious.


A 2025 resident satisfaction study found


**78%** of manufactured homeowners are satisfied with their home. Seventy-two percent said they’d recommend one to others. And


**81%** of buyers reported a favorable impression of manufactured housing overall.


That last number matters for anyone still carrying old assumptions about offsite-built homes. These aren’t reluctant buyers rationalizing a compromise. Most came in informed –


**71%** of prospective buyers said they were at least somewhat familiar with the differences between modern manufactured homes and older pre-


**HUD Code** mobile homes – and came out satisfied.


Here’s another statistic that often surprises people: the average resident stay in a land-lease community is more than


**10 years** . Seventy percent of residents planned from the start to stay more than six years. That’s not hard to believe because of the benefits of living in a manufactured home community. Things like amenities, proximity to schools, site maintenance and other perks that make these communities great places to live.


## Do Manufactured Homes Hold Their Value?


More than


**65%** of manufactured homeowners reported their home’s value had either increased or stayed the same since they bought it. Specifically, almost


**37%** reported an increase and 30% reported stable value. So if you’re asking:[Do manufactured homes appreciate in value](https://blog.cavcohomes.com/do-manufactured-mobile-homes-appreciate-in-value/) ,


the numbers speak for themselves.


That statistic debunks the persistent myth that manufactured homes are a depreciating asset. The data says otherwise – and with


**70%** of new manufactured homes now titled as real estate on private land, many buyers are in a position to benefit from the same equity dynamics as other homeowners.

**30%** of new manufactured homes are placed in one of the roughly

**$782 – $751** for all-ages communities and


**$841** for 55+ communities. Occupancy rates averaged


**95%** nationally, a strong indicator of ongoing demand. ## FAQs: What Manufactured Home Buyers Are Searching For


**How much did a new manufactured home cost in 2025?**
The national average selling price for a new manufactured home in 2025 was $115,557. Single-section homes averaged $95,074 and multi-section homes averaged $156,170, according to MHInsider’s 2026 industry report.


**What is the cost per square foot for a manufactured home?**
New manufactured homes averaged $101.20 per square foot in 2025. Multi-section homes averaged $110.26 per square foot, and single-section homes averaged $95.17 per square foot.


**Are manufactured homes a good investment?**
Over 65 percent of manufactured homeowners report their home’s value has held steady or increased. With 70 percent of new manufactured homes now titled as real estate, buyers have a real path to building equity.


**How satisfied are manufactured homeowners?**
78 percent of manufactured home residents reported satisfaction with their home in a 2025 study, and 72 percent said they recommend the lifestyle to others.


**How many manufactured homes were produced in 2025?**
The industry produced 102,738 homes in 2025, built across 147 plants in 28 states, generating $12.7 billion in sales and contributing $6.3 billion to U.S. GDP.


**How long do people typically stay in a manufactured home community?**
The average resident stay in a land-lease community exceeds 10 years. Seventy percent of residents planned from the beginning to stay more than six years.


**What are the fastest-selling manufactured home markets?**
In 2025, Erie, PA led with homes selling in just 20 days on average. Dubuque, IA (29 days), Lawton, OK (32 days), and Charleston, SC (38 days) also ranked among the fastest–moving markets.
