## Why Buyers Want Homes With Porches


A porch adds more than curb appeal. It creates a comfortable outdoor living space that can be enjoyed throughout the year. For many homeowners, a porch becomes the favorite “room” of the house. Not only do porches extend living space, but they are great places to:


- Sip morning coffee, read or watch kids play in the yard
- Entertain family, neighbors and friends all year
- Decorate for every season. (Think pumpkins in the fall, flags on the 4th, wreaths in the winter and flowers in spring!)
- Enjoy plenty of fresh air without leaving your home


Having a porch is one of the easiest ways to make your home feel larger while creating a warm, welcoming first impression — and big curb appeal.


## Today’s buyers expect more from a home than just beautiful interiors.

## Popular Porch Features Available on Select Cavco Homes


Depending on the model and location, Cavco homes with porches may include features such as:


- Covered front porches

- Serving windows for outdoor entertaining
- Outdoor fireplaces or fire features

These features create inviting spaces you’ll enjoy every season while adding timeless curb appeal to your home.
