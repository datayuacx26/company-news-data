## **Lighting That Does More Than Illuminate**


Good bathroom lighting serves two important purposes: it provides bright, accurate illumination for getting ready in the morning and creates a warm, relaxing atmosphere when it’s time to wind down at the end of the day. Cavco bathrooms are designed with both in mind, using layered schemes that combine overhead fixtures with vanity lighting behind mirrors and under cabinets (where available). Some homes even offer recessed lighting in a variety of colors to create a unique look and feel.


---


The result is a comfortable, well-lit space that works as well for a quick weekday morning as it does for a slow, quiet evening wind-down.

## **Primary Bathroom Frequently Asked Questions**


### **Do all Cavco homes include a primary bathroom with these features?**


Primary bathroom features vary by floor plan and home series. Entry-level single-section homes include stylish, functional designs, while double–section and modular homes offer more expansive layouts with upgraded features. Your Cavco retailer can walk you through what’s included at every price point.


### **What is the Ebony Package?**


The Ebony Package is a design package that includes striking black accents on hardware, faucets, fixtures, shower doors and the garden soaking tub. It gives your primary bathroom a modern, stylish edge that is both subtle, yet instantly attention-getting. The Ebony Package is featured in our nationally available[Axis double-section home](https://newhomes.cavcohomes.com/axis/index.html) .


### **Can I customize the primary bathroom in a Cavco home?**


Cavco’s modular homes offer the most flexibility, including options for layout, cabinetry, fixtures and finishes. Some double–section homes also offer design package upgrades. Talk to a[Cavco retailer](https://www.cavcohomes.com/our-retailers/) about what’s available for the floor plan you’re considering.


### **Are freestanding soaking tubs available in smaller floor plans?**


Freestanding tubs are typically found in larger floor plans where the primary bathroom has room to accommodate them. Talk to your[Cavco retailer](https://www.cavcohomes.com/our-retailers/) about which floor plans include soaking tubs.


### **What shower options does Cavco offer?**


Depending on the home model and market availability, Cavco offers a wide variety of walk-in showers that can include ceramic tile options, rain-style showerheads, detachable hose-style sprayers, bench seating, transom windows and much more. See your local retailer for options available in the homes near you.


### **How do I find Cavco homes available in my area?**


Connect with a[Cavco retailer](https://www.cavcohomes.com/our-retailers/) near you. They can show you available inventory, walk you through[floor plans](https://www.cavcohomes.com/find-a-floor-plan) and help you identify which features and upgrades fit your budget.
