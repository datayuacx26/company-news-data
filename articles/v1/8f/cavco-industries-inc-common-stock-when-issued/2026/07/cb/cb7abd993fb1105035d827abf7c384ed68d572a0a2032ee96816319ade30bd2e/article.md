## **How are Halo Homes Built?**


Cavco's Halo homes are built in climate-controlled facilities to the federal **[HUD Code](https://blog.cavcohomes.com/what-is-the-hud-code-for-manufactured-homes/)** set by the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development. Once complete, they're transported to your home site and professionally installed.


Because construction happens indoors, work doesn't stop for bad weather. Materials stay protected from rain, snow, wind and sun, while the building process stays efficient from start to finish. Cavco also purchases building materials in large quantities, which helps lower costs and pass savings directly to buyers.


This efficient building process helps Cavco deliver high-quality homes at prices that make ownership possible for more first-time buyers.


Today's Halo homes meet strict national standards for structural integrity, energy efficiency, fire resistance, plumbing, electrical systems and thermal performance. They're also inspected by independent third parties during construction.
