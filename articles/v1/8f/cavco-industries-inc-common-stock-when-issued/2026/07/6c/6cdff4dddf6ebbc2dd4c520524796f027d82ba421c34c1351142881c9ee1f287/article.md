# Jeff Williams, Cavco Regional Vice President Receives 2026 MHAO Goodman-Ross Award


Published On: July 10, 2026


•


1 min read


•


Bo Radley, Second VP MHAO Board and Jeff Williams, Honoree.


Congratulations to


**Jeff Williams, Regional Vice President of Manufacturing** , Cavco – Texas Region, who was recently recognized with the


**Goodman-Ross Award** by the


**Manufactured Housing Association of Oklahoma (MHAO)** .


The Goodman-Ross Award is one of MHAO’s highest honors, recognizing individuals whose leadership, service and commitment have made a lasting impact on Oklahoma’s manufactured housing industry. The award celebrates those who have helped strengthen the industry while advancing affordable housing across the state.


Jeff has built a career dedicated to supporting the manufactured housing industry through leadership, advocacy and collaboration. In addition to his role with Cavco, he serves on the MHAO Board of Directors, helping guide opportunities for Oklahomans.


This recognition reflects not only Jeff’s professional accomplishments but also his ongoing commitment to the customers, communities and industry he serves.


> ***“I am truly honored to receive this award,” said Jeff. “Everyone in the industry works hard, and to receive recognition makes all the effort worthwhile!***
