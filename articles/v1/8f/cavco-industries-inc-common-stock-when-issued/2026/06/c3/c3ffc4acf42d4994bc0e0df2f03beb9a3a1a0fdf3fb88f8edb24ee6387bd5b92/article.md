# New Affordable Senior Housing Community Breaks Ground in Kissimmee, Florida

### ***Las Margaritas development in Osceola County will provide affordable housing for qualifying seniors aged 62 and older, featuring homes built by Cavco.***


KISSIMMEE, Fla. — Community leaders, housing advocates and project partners gathered May 14 to celebrate the groundbreaking of Las Margaritas, a new affordable senior housing community in Buenaventura Lakes that will provide housing for qualifying residents age 62 and older.


Located in Osceola County, the development is designed to help address the growing need for affordable senior housing in Central Florida. The project will include nine one-bedroom homes built by Cavco's Plant City manufacturing facility, along with a community clubhouse and shared outdoor spaces for residents.


The groundbreaking ceremony was led by Osceola County District 2 Commissioner Viviana Janer and attended by U.S. Congressman Darren Soto, who helped secure federal U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) funding for the project through community funding initiatives.


Representing Cavco at the event were Erik Pederson, Retail General Manager for Cavco Retail – Cocoa; Kevin Law, Vice President of Sales and Sales Manager for Cavco – Plant City and Liz Koehler, Sales Representative for Palm Harbor Homes – Cocoa.


---


L to R: Cavco's Kevin Law and Viviana Janer, Osceola County Commissioner.


---


The development is funded by Osceola County with federal HUD funding secured through Congressman Soto’s efforts. Construction, design and site development are supported by Cavco, Arnco Construction, Marlin Construction and Osceola Engineering.


During the ceremony, Commissioner Janer described Las Margaritas as a project of years in the making and emphasized its importance in helping local seniors age in place safely and affordably.


“Housing is about more than buildings, it’s about peace of mind,” Janer said. She added that some of the most meaningful community projects are those that quietly improve residents’ daily lives.


Las Margaritas is expected to provide a safe, affordable housing option for seniors while supporting Osceola County’s ongoing efforts to expand housing opportunities for residents.


For more information about affordable housing initiatives in Osceola County, visit the county’s housing resources page.
