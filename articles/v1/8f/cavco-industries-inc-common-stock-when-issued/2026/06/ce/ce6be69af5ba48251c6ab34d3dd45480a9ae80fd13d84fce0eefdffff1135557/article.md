# Virginia Takes a Step Forward for Affordable Housing at Cavco’s Rocky Mount Facility

# Virginia Takes a Step Forward for Affordable Housing at Cavco’s Rocky Mount Facility


Published On: June 2, 2026


•


1 min read


•


VA State Governor Abigail Spanberger flanked by VA State Delegate Josh Thomas, VA State Delegate Dan Helmer, Executive Director VA MMHA Randy Grumbine, VA State Delegate Paul Krizek, VA State Senator Schuyler VanValkenberg.


Virginia Gov. Abigail Spanberger made history for manufactured housing advocates on June 1, 2026 — and she did it at Cavco’s Rocky Mount, Virginia production facility.


The governor ceremonially signed two bipartisan zoning reform bills, House Bill 655 and Senate Bill 346, at the Rocky Mount plant, surrounded by state legislators, local officials, and housing advocates. The new laws, which take effect July 1, 2026, require Virginia communities to treat manufactured homes the same as site-built homes under local zoning rules — a significant win for housing access across the commonwealth.


At the signing, Cavco Regional Vice President Wade Wells captured what the moment meant for everyday Virginians:


> *“This legislation accomplishes something meaningful for the people of Virginia — boosting housing supply, expanding where manufactured homes can be placed and creating more pathways for families into affordable homeownership. I want to applaud the Commonwealth for recognizing the quality, energy efficiency and value that today’s offsite constructed homes deliver.”*


Virginia faces an estimated shortage of 200,000 affordable homes. These new laws help chip away at that number by removing zoning barriers that have long kept manufactured housing out of residential areas where comparable site-built homes are already welcome.


Cavco is proud to have hosted this milestone event — and remains committed to advocating for manufactured housing as a real, affordable, and quality solution for American families at the state and federal levels.
