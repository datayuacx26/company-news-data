## **How Are Banner Homes by Cavco Built?**


Banner homes are built the way all Cavco manufactured and modular homes are – inside a climate-controlled facility, to the strict standards of the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) called the[HUD Code](https://blog.cavcohomes.com/what-is-the-hud-code-for-manufactured-homes/) , then transported to customer’s home site and professionally installed.


Building indoors means construction never stops for weather. Materials stay protected throughout the entire build. And because Cavco purchases materials in large quantities, the savings get passed along to buyers in the form of more affordable home prices.


Every home in the[Banner home line](https://www.cavcohomes.com/banner) meets national standards for structural integrity, energy efficiency, fire resistance, plumbing and electrical systems – and is verified by independent third-party inspectors before it ever leaves the facility.

## **Banner Homes Frequently Asked Questions**


### **Who are Banner homes designed for?**


Homes in the Banner home line are designed for growing families and busy households that need room to grow and space to live and want to stay within a realistic budget. They’re a natural fit for families adding members, blending households or individuals who are simply ready for a home that fits the life they’re living now.


They are also perfect for the single member household who simply wants more room for an office, guests or other future plans and wants to invest in their own home.


### **What features come standard in a home in the Banner home line?**


Banner homes include generous kitchen islands, modern durable finishes, smart storage and flexible living spaces – details designed to support the way active individuals, couples and families live.


Your local Cavco retailer can walk you through exactly what’s included in the floor plans available in your area.


### **How long does it take to move into a Banner home?**


Because construction happens in Cavco’s climate-controlled factories rather than on site, homes in the Banner home line can often be completed in a fraction of the time required for on-site-built construction. You can choose a display home already built than can typically be placed and ready in weeks. And once construction begins on a new home, it will usually be completed in about a week. Once the site is prepared and the home arrives, the set up and finish out can begin, depending the timetable established with your local retailer.


### **Can I get financing for a Banner home?**


Yes. Financing options for manufactured homes include home-only loans and may also include financing similar to those available for on-site-built homes, with multiple programs depending on your situation and location. Your local Cavco retailer can walk you through what’s available in your area.


### **Do I need to own land to buy a home from the Banner home line?**


Not necessarily. Many Cavco retailers help buyers find land to purchase or lease – through local real estate referrals, retailer-owned inventory or manufactured home community options. Ask your local retailer what’s available near you.


### **Are Cavco homes energy efficient?**


Yes. Homes in the Banner home line are built to HUD Code energy efficiency standards covering insulation, windows, thermal performance and Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning (HVAC) systems.


### **Are Banner homes well-built manufactured homes?**


Banner homes are HUD Code manufactured homes, built in a climate-controlled facility, transported to your home site and professionally installed. Throughout the process, they must meet strict national standards for construction, safety and energy performance and are inspected by independent third parties throughout the build.
