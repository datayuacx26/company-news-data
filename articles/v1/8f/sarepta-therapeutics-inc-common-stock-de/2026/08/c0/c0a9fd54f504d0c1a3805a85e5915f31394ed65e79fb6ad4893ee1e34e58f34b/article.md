### Sarepta Therapeutics to Report 3-Year Topline Data from EMBARK Study of ELEVIDYS Gene Therapy in Ambulatory Individuals with Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy


01/23/26 4:05 PM EST


CAMBRIDGE, Mass.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jan. 23, 2026-- Sarepta Therapeutics, Inc. (NASDAQ:SRPT), the leader in precision genetic medicine for rare diseases, today announced that on Monday, Jan. 26, 2026, at 8:30 am Eastern Time, the Company will host a webcast and conference call to present 3-year topline functional results from patients treated in Part 1 of EMBARK (Study 9001-301), the global, randomized placebo-controlled Phase 3 study evaluating ELEVIDYS (delandistrogene moxeparvovec-rokl) in ambulatory individuals with Duchenne muscular dystrophy who were aged four to seven at time of treatment.
