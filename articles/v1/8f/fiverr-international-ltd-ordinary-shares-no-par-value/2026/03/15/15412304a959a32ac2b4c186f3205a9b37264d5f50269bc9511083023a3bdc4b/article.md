# Fiverr Files its Annual Report on Form 20-F

NEW YORK, March 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Fiverr International Ltd. (NYSE: FVRR), the company that is transforming the way the world creates and works together, today announced that it filed its annual report on Form 20-F for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, with the Securities and Exchange Commission on March 12, 2026. The annual report on Form 20-F can be accessed on the Company’s investor relations website at[http://investors.fiverr.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=IpVR09ToQiEKODOS1TbSuJscW3CFnQLG9KNGc6j5EDEdCRsWeYgJx8H2To-g5aaGoauNKwUsOEsdNv66NODk1Fxn_p4Stq4dQzPZFYxqzS3SARZ77fmjgIUZa95F-zY2) or on the SEC’s website at[www.sec.gov](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=dx26ycMKel0zV6EgbEDQJL3yG4OExJrCEbGKrfnJP8qbKxgV1yAwOUM2Gc6co6-clWNA7ZEHVefyTiIwIOLC0A==) .


Fiverr will provide a hard copy of the annual report containing its audited consolidated financial statements, free of charge, to its shareholders upon request. Requests should be directed in writing by email to[investors@fiverr.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=sw_TMPjv9ywJfpMvyYQT2pmurc3FkCYDS1uDcFIvIkNV2PGpo7yO40DKKvVnGk8CZTMlyE8WLmtJRyUSDRuZhdpoSdclYuyPmPi1TZKVLQo=) , or by post to Fiverr International Ltd., 8 Eliezer Kaplan St, Tel-Aviv 6473409, Israel.
