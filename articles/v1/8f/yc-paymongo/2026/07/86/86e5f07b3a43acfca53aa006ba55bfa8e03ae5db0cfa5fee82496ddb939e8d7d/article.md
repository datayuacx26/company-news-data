# Best Invoicing Software for Businesses and Freelancers in the Philippines (2026)

The best invoicing software does more than generate invoices; it helps you get paid faster. From online payments and automated reminders to expense tracking and reporting, the right platform can save hours of admin work every month.

## **What to Look for in Invoicing Software**


Before jumping into the list, here's what actually matters when choosing invoicing software for a Philippine business:


1. **Philippine payment methods.** Your invoicing tool is only useful if clients can pay through it. Look for software that supports GCash, Maya, credit cards, and online bank transfers, not just PayPal.
2. **Ease of use.** You shouldn't need an accountant to send an invoice. The best tools let you create and send in under two minutes.
3. **Automatic reminders.** The software should follow up on unpaid invoices for you, so you don't have to.
4. **Free tier or low cost.** Most freelancers and small businesses don't need enterprise features. A free or affordable plan should cover basic invoicing needs.
5. **Payout speed.** If the software collects payment, how quickly does that money reach your bank account?
6. **BIR compliance.** Support for official receipt formats, CAS registration, and ideally EIS readiness for the 2026 mandate.
7. **Philippine tax handling.** Automatic computation of VAT, percentage tax, withholding tax, not just a generic "tax rate" field.
8. **Filipino context.** Designed around how Philippine businesses are structured: freelancers, sole proprietors, SMEs, BIR-registered corporations.

## **The 2026 BIR E-Invoicing Mandate: What You Need to Know**


The Philippine Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) is rolling out a mandatory Electronic Invoicing System (EIS) to modernize tax reporting and reduce manual tax compliance. Under the latest regulations, certain businesses must issue electronic invoices and transmit invoice data to the BIR using approved e-invoicing software by[December 31, 2026](https://www.pna.gov.ph/articles/1262790?utm_source=chatgpt.com&__cf_chl_f_tk=F0UxhulsWbPeAPsF0lHSF.IvVRJWblBD93DM0ExEUHY-1782799285-1.0.1.1-_d67t3nDS8ifLe0v.umhssWUEQx.7tpuKI1dpqhLVBM) .


### What is the BIR Electronic Invoicing System (EIS)?


The EIS is the BIR's digital platform for receiving, validating, and storing invoice data. Instead of simply generating PDF invoices or printed receipts, businesses will be required to create invoices in a structured electronic format (typically JSON) and electronically transmit the sales data to the BIR through an accredited system or API.


### Who needs to comply?


The mandate initially applies to:


- Businesses engaged in e-commerce or online transactions (except micro taxpayers)
- Businesses under the[BIR Large Taxpayers](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%20113-2024.pdf?ref=paymongo-blog.ghost.io) Service (LTS)
- Taxpayers classified as Large Taxpayers under the[Ease of Paying Taxes (EOPT) Act](https://www.bir.gov.ph/EOPT?ref=paymongo-blog.ghost.io)
- Businesses using Computerized Accounting Systems (CAS), Computerized Books of Accounts (CBA) with electronic invoicing, or other invoicing software capable of producing structured invoice data.
- Major corporations with annual sales above ₱1 billion


If you're in this group, global invoicing tools alone won't cut it. You need a platform that generates BIR-compliant JSON invoices and can transmit them to the EIS.


Micro and small taxpayers not covered by the mandate can still voluntarily issue BIR-compliant e-invoices, and doing so early puts you ahead when the mandate eventually expands.


Other taxpayer groups, such as exporters, certain incentive-registered businesses, and POS users, are expected to be covered in later phases once the BIR issues additional regulations.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**


#### What is BIR EIS and when does it take effect?


The BIR Electronic Invoicing System (EIS) is a centralized platform where covered taxpayers must transmit their sales invoices and receipts in BIR-compliant JSON format. The first group of covered taxpayers, large taxpayers, e-commerce businesses, and CAS/CBA users, must comply by December 31, 2026.


#### Which Philippine invoicing software is BIR accredited?


Juan (formerly JuanTax) is the Philippines' first BIR-accredited electronic tax software provider (eTSP). Taxumo is also BIR-accredited for tax filing and e-invoicing. N3 AI Accounting (QNE) holds BIR CAS approval and is EIS-ready.


#### Can I use global tools like QuickBooks or Wave for BIR compliance?


Global tools are not designed for BIR's specific requirements; they don't generate the correct JSON format for EIS transmission or produce BIR-standard forms. You'd need to pair them with a locally accredited solution, or switch to a PH-built platform to be safe for the 2026 mandate.


#### Does PayMongo generate BIR-compliant invoices?


PayMongo's invoicing is designed for payment collection, not BIR tax compliance. For official receipts and BIR-mandated e-invoices, use a BIR-accredited platform like Juan or Taxumo. Pair it with PayMongo for payment collection on those same invoices.


#### What's the difference between an e-invoice and a payment link?


An e-invoice is a BIR-compliant document that records a transaction for tax purposes. A payment link (like PayMongo's) is a way for clients to pay you online. You need both: the e-invoice for BIR, and the payment link so your client can actually pay without going to the bank.
