## **QR Ph: accepting GCash in person**


QR Ph is the national QR standard set by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas. One property makes it useful: a single QR Ph code accepts payment from GCash, Maya, and most Philippine banking apps. The customer chooses the app; you display one code.


There are two grades of QR. A personal QR, generated inside the GCash app, credits into your personal wallet. It works, but wallet limits apply and every payment mixes with your personal money. A business QR routes payments to a business account, with transaction records you can actually reconcile.


You can obtain a business-grade QR Ph code either by applying to GCash directly, or instantly through PayMongo, where the same code also accepts Maya and bank app payments.


Print the code. Place it where the customer already looks when it's time to pay. This is the entire integration.

## **The GCash merchant account**


GCash operates its own merchant program (GCash for Business). Applying gets you an official merchant QR, a business wallet, and GCash's merchant tooling.


Three constraints are worth understanding before you apply. The account accepts GCash and nothing else, thus a customer holding a credit card or a Maya wallet cannot pay you through it. And it is built for in-person acceptance; selling online through it requires separate integration work.


If your business is a physical store where most customers already pay with GCash, this is a reasonable choice. If any part of your selling happens online, it solves half the problem.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**


#### Can I accept GCash payments without a business account?


Yes. A personal GCash wallet accepts payments by QR or send-money. Wallet limits apply, and payments mix with personal funds. For registered businesses, a merchant account or gateway provides higher limits, receipts, and clean records.


#### How do I get a GCash QR code for my business?


Two routes: apply through GCash for Business, or generate a QR Ph code through PayMongo. The PayMongo code also accepts Maya and participating bank apps.


#### Do I need DTI or SEC registration?


Not for a personal wallet. For a GCash merchant account or a PayMongo business account: DTI registration for sole proprietors, SEC for corporations. Freelancers can apply to PayMongo as individuals.
