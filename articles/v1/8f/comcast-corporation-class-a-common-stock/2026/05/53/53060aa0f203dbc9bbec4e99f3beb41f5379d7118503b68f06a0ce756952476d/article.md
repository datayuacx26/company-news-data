# Comcast Declares Quarterly Dividend

PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--May 13, 2026-- Comcast Corporation (NASDAQ: CMCSA) announced that its Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.33 a share on the company’s common stock. The quarterly dividend is payable on July 22, 2026, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on July 1, 2026.
