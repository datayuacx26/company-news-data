# Coincheck Group N.V. To Participate at the KeyBanc Capital Markets Technology Leadership Forum


August 07, 2026 8:00 am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_491dfe346d6283898bd441eb91a34cc6/coincheckgroup/news/2026-08-07_Coincheck_Group_N_V_To_Participate_at_the_KeyBanc__124.pdf)


AMSTERDAM--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Coincheck Group N.V. (NASDAQ: CNCK) (“Coincheck Group” or the “Company”), a Dutch public limited liability company that provides digital asset trade execution, custody, staking and asset management services, today announced that CEO, Pascal St-Jean and CFO, Jason Sandberg, will participate in a fireside chat at the KeyBanc Capital Markets Technology Leadership Forum in Park City, Utah on Tuesday, August 11, at 10:00 a.m. MT.


A live webcast of the fireside chat will be accessible on Coincheck Group website at[www.coincheckgroup.com](https://can01.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.coincheckgroup.com%2Fnews-events%2Fir-calendar&data=05%7C02%7CNathalieB%403iq.io%7Cb93f8203587b4d6d4d3908def18c88ad%7C40457321284f4be2b792399d0fd44831%7C0%7C0%7C639213783070645625%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=VSgHQa%2BJYSfPv4thP3edwWRdzUNyaj8Z49imJccZNAw%3D&reserved=0) . A replay will be available for a limited time following the conference.


**About Coincheck Group N.V.**


Coincheck Group N.V. (NASDAQ: CNCK) seeks to bring together retail scale, institutional capability and resilient infrastructure in one digital finance platform offering. Built on its leadership position in Japan as a retail crypto asset exchange provider, the Company is expanding into institutional services and digital asset infrastructure across multiple markets. Its offerings include trade execution, custody, staking and asset management services alongside ongoing development in on-chain finance.
