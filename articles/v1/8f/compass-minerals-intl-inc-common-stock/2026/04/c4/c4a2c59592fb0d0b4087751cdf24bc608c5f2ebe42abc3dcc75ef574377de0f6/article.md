# Compass Minerals Reinforces its Commitment to Environmental Stewardship During Earth Week

#### Compass Minerals Reinforces its Commitment to Environmental Stewardship During Earth Week


Compass Minerals is committed to environmental stewardship and sustainability, adopting practices that align business operations with environmental responsibility to foster a more resilient future.


The company takes steps to reduce its environmental footprint by responsibly managing water use, optimizing energy consumption and minimizing landfill waste. These measures push the company to operate in a manner that respects and conserves natural resources.


Recognizing that the company relies on natural resources, Compass Minerals focuses on the sustainable utilization of Earth’s natural resources to deliver products that help keep people safe, feed the world and enrich lives, every day.


During Earth Week, April 20-24, employees across the organization participate in activities that demonstrate appreciation for Earth’s natural resources and reinforce environmental stewardship. Activities at Compass Minerals’ sites include cleaning up community parks and shorelines, volunteering with local conservation organizations and community gardens, and hosting informational sessions on making a positive environmental impact.


Through these initiatives, the company not only reinforces its commitment to environmental stewardship but also fosters a culture of collective responsibility to protect the ecology and natural resources surrounding its operations.
