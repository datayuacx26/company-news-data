# Leadership Development Needs a Reset. Here’s What’s New.

Managers today are expected to do far more than drive performance.


They guide teams through constant change, support employee well-being, and keep people engaged in increasingly complex environments. But most leadership development programs still focus on skills alone. They don’t prepare leaders for the day-to-day realities of modern work.


The effects are clear. Leaders feel overwhelmed. Employees feel unsupported. Teams rely on disconnected tools that don’t fully work together.


At the same time, HR and benefits leaders are under pressure to simplify their approach. They need to reduce vendor sprawl while still delivering meaningful outcomes.


‍


## **The growing pressure on managers**


‍


Leadership pressure is rising quickly.


Modern Health’s[workforce research](https://explore.modernhealth.com/2025-trends/elevate-insights-march-2025) shows:


- 77% of managers say their role is harder than ever
- 60% report growing expectations to support their teams’ mental health
- Only 23% feel equipped to do so


External research reinforces the stakes. Management[drives 70%](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/266822/engaged-employees-differently.aspx) of the variance in employee engagement, and strong leadership is[closely tied](https://doaj.org/article/bb4dc00b567047e18d91e681bdd29cd0) to retention and effective talent management.


Left unaddressed, these challenges can affect performance, engagement, and retention across the organization.


‍


## **Introducing Resilient Leadership Pathways**


‍


To help address these challenges, Modern Health is expanding[its Pathways offering](https://www.modernhealth.com/pathways) with a new collection focused on leadership: **Resilient Leadership Pathways** .


The collection is designed to help current and emerging leaders build the skills most programs overlook: resilience, empathy, and the ability to lead through change.


It does this in a structured, focused format that fits into real work.


‍


## **How Pathways work**


‍


Pathways are short, structured programs designed to help employees work through specific challenges with dedicated support.


Each Pathway connects an employee with a Pathway Specialist for four focused, one-on-one sessions. During that time, they work through practical exercises and get strategies to readily apply new skills to real situations at work.


The experience is designed to be simple and intentional:


- One clear topic at a time
- A short, defined structure
- Personalized, direct support
- Practical tools that can be used right away


Pathways are part of Modern Health’s Adaptive Care Model, which connects employees to the right type of support based on their needs, and adapts over time. This includes leadership development, mental health support, family care, and more—all within one platform.


For organizations, this means supporting a wide range of employee needs without adding separate point solutions.


‍


## **Why this approach works**


‍


The Pathways format reflects a broader shift in how support is delivered.


There is growing evidence that short, focused programs grounded in evidence-based coaching techniques can improve both mental health and core workplace skills.


In[a peer-reviewed study](https://www.modernhealth.com/post/coaching-delivers-measurable-mental-health-and-resilience-gains) of working adults using Modern Health’s platform:


- Depression symptoms decreased by 22.5% on average
- Anxiety symptoms decreased by 12% on average
- Participants showed improvements in stress management and emotional regulation


These outcomes are tied to structured, time-bound support—not long-term, open-ended programs.


That’s the model Pathways are built on: focused support that helps employees apply what they’re learning in real time.


Leaders don’t need more content or another course. They need support they can use in the moment.


‍


## **What’s included in the Resilient Leadership Pathways collection**


‍


The collection includes five topic-specific Pathways designed to help leaders build the skills needed in today’s environment:


- Developing Your Leadership Style
- Having a Confident Presence
- Leading with Empathy
- Embracing Change
- Motivating Your Team


Each Pathway is grounded in real scenarios, not hypothetical training, and designed to help leaders apply what they’re learning directly to their teams.


“Leadership today requires emotional resilience as much as technical skill,” said Dr. Jessica Watrous, Chief Clinical Officer at Modern Health.


“Managers are often the first line of support when employees are struggling with stress or burnout, yet many leaders feel unprepared for that responsibility.”


‍


## **Leadership development, rethought**


‍


Organizations continue to invest in leadership development, but expectations are changing. The focus is shifting toward approaches that are more practical, more connected, and easier for employees to use in their day-to-day work.


Resilient Leadership Pathways reflect that shift:


- From fragmented tools to integrated support
- From open-ended programs to focused, structured experiences
- From skill-building alone to development that includes resilience and well-being


In today’s environment, leadership requires more than driving performance. It means helping people navigate complexity without burning out in the process.
