# 7 Things AI Takeoffs Can and Can't Read on Your Construction Plans

## What AI takeoff software can read on your plans


1. **Room dimensions and square footage.** Every room gets measured off the floor plan. That means no manual scaling, no clicking corner to corner for an afternoon.
2. **Symbol counts.** Doors, windows, outlets, fixtures, and connectors get counted across the full set, so nothing gets missed because it sat on page 14.
3. **Schedules and written notes.** Door and window schedules, finish callouts, and margin notes get read and tied to the right line items. If your architect wrote it on the prints, it's in the takeoff.
4. **The whole plan set, across trades.** Architectural, structural, elevations, foundation — framing, roofing, siding, flooring, and electrical all come back marked up and color-coded by trade.
5. **Problems with the plans themselves.** Clashes between sheets, missing information, and any assumptions made during processing get flagged in a written summary, so you know exactly what to verify before the bid goes out.


A takeoff that once took 8 to 16 hours of clicking through drawings comes back in about two hours as a[color-coded PDF you can show a client](https://handoff.ai/ai-takeoffs) . Your AI Teammate does the counting; you do the deciding.


## What AI can't read on construction plans


### The two places your judgment still runs the job


1. **Engineering values that aren't written down.** One custom-home builder asked us point blank: what about snow loads and framing member sizes? Here's the honest answer. If your engineer specified it on the prints, AI reads it and carries it through the takeoff. If it still needs to be calculated, that's a stamped-engineer decision — no software should be making it, and ours won't pretend to.
2. **One-of-a-kind custom work, priced blind (kind of).** A $200,000 custom deck with a roof, custom rails, and under-deck skirting isn't a pattern-match job. AI gets you the measurements and the base scope fast, but your specialty pricing has to come from you which is why you can[load your own catalogs and rates](https://handoff.ai/instant-ai-estimates) so the numbers reflect what your suppliers actually charge, not a national average.


Knowing the limits is what makes the rest trustworthy. Accurate estimates protect contractor margins from the start.

## 5 steps to getting an accurate AI estimate from complex or unusual plans


1. **Upload the full set** , not just the floor plan — elevations, structural sheets, and schedules all feed the takeoff.
2. **Add what only you know.** A voice note or a few typed lines about site conditions, custom details, or your standard exclusions gets read right alongside the drawings.
3. **Load your catalogs and labor rates** so pricing comes from your suppliers and your market, not a generic average.
4. **Read the flagged assumptions first.** The written summary tells you what was inferred and where the plans conflict. Start your review there.
5. **Spot-check the big-ticket lines.** Five minutes on the ten most expensive items catches more than an hour of re-reading everything.


Do those five things and the unusual jobs stop being the scary ones. Handoff removes the guesswork from pricing a job.

## Other FAQs about how AI Takeoffs work for residential construction


**Does AI takeoff work for plans over 5,000 square feet?**


AI Takeoffs are tuned for residential plans up to 5,000 square feet, where accuracy is highest. For larger projects, you can still generate estimates from uploaded files — just plan on a closer review of the output, the same way you'd double-check a new estimator's first big job.


**Can AI read hand-drawn or scanned plans?**


Yes, if they're legible. Which is a very broad term. Our software can read even the worst handwriting. Upload a clean scan or photo as a PDF and dimensions, notes, and counts get extracted. The cleaner the scan, the tighter the takeoff — a coffee-stained fourth-generation copy might need more of your review.


**Do I still need to review an AI takeoff before I bid?**


Yes. The takeoff flags its assumptions so the review takes minutes instead of hours, but the bid carries your name and your margin. Review the flagged items, confirm the big-ticket lines, and send it.
