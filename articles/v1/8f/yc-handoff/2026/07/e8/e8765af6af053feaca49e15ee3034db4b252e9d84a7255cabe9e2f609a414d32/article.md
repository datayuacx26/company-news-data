# How Do Handoff AI Takeoffs Compare to JobTread Takeoffs?

## Stop Measuring. Start Closing.


Every hour you spend measuring plans is an hour you're not selling, not managing your crew, and not running your business. For residential contractors, takeoffs have always been one of most time-consuming part of the bid process because most tools still make you do the measuring yourself.


That's the real difference between[JobTread](https://www.jobtread.com/) takeoffs and Handoff AI takeoffs. One makes manual measurement faster. The other eliminates it.


Here's a full breakdown so you can decide which one fits how you actually work.


## Feature Comparison: JobTread Takeoffs vs Handoff AI Takeoffs


## What Are JobTread Takeoffs? Less time but still manual


JobTread is an all-in-one construction management platform built for small-to-mid-sized contractors.


Key takeoff features:


- Upload a blueprint PDF directly into the platform, calibrate the scale
- However, you manually measure areas, lengths, volumes, and counts using point-and-click digital tools.
- Measurements sync directly into JobTread's budgeting system, so once you've measured, building out your cost estimate is faster. That's the key value prop: tight integration between takeoff and project financials, all in one platform.
- JobTread has also been adding AI features across the platform, but the takeoff itself works the same way it always has. You do the measuring.


JobTread customers are fans, and the time savings are real. One remodeler, Joe Trinidad of Bexar Essentials Remodeling, reports that estimates that used to take him five to six hours now take about one.


But notice what it assumes: the contractor is still doing every measurement. The software made him faster. It didn't do the work for him.


### JobTread Takeoffs: Who It's Built For


JobTread takeoffs are a great fit if:


1. **You're already using JobTread as your core project management tool.** The real value is the tight integration — your measurements flow directly into your budget and job cost tracking without jumping between platforms.
2. If you have a dedicated estimator who's comfortable doing takeoffs but wants a cleaner digital workflow, and you want everything living in one system, JobTread is a solid choice.


## What are Handoff AI Takeoffs? Less time and all automated


Handoff AI takes a different approach entirely. You upload your residential construction plans, up to 5,000 sqft, and the AI does the rest. No calibration. No clicking. No measuring.


In about two hours, you get back:


- A color-coded visual takeoff PDF showing every measured area, length, and item
- A written summary ready to use for pricing and[proposals](https://www.handoff.ai/winning-ai-proposals)
- Material quantities priced against 60M+ SKUs from suppliers like Home Depot and[Lowe's](https://www.handoff.ai/blog/product-update-lowe-s-integration)

Handoff was trained on[100,000+ completed residential estimates](https://www.handoff.ai/1build) . It knows what a kitchen remodel looks like and what a bathroom addition needs, because it has scoped thousands of them.


And because every measurement is marked on the PDF, you can check the AI's work line by line before you bid. The takeoff is autonomous. The numbers are verifiable. You stay in control without doing the measuring.


The framing line that says it all: **Takeoff software helps you measure faster. Handoff AI does the measuring for you.**


### Handoff AI Takeoffs: Who It's Built For


Handoff AI is built for:


1. [Residential contractors](https://www.handoff.ai/remodelers) who don't have a dedicated estimator or who have one but want to free them up from repetitive takeoff work.
2. If you're a general contractor,[remodeler](https://www.handoff.ai/remodelers) , or specialty trade doing residential work up to 5,000 sqft, and you're tired of spending half a day just to get to the bid stage, Handoff is purpose-built for you.

## Other FAQs about JobTread takeoffs and Handoff AI Takeoffs


**Is Handoff a good JobTread alternative?**


For takeoffs and estimating, yes. JobTread is a strong project management platform where you do the measuring and scope-building yourself, while Handoff's AI runs the takeoff for you and turns it into a priced estimate. Contractors who want an all-in-one system with autonomous estimating built in use Handoff for the full workflow:[estimates](https://www.handoff.ai/instant-ai-estimates) ,[proposals](https://www.handoff.ai/winning-ai-proposals) , contact records,[invoices](https://www.handoff.ai/invoicing) , and scheduling.


**Can I use Handoff AI Takeoffs alongside JobTread or another platform?**


Yes. But we don’t recommend it. Handoff is an end-to-end project management and estimating tool. But you can use it for whatever fills your business gaps. Handoff works with whatever workflow you run today, so you can upload plans and use the takeoff output without migrating projects or retraining your team. Many contractors start with AI Takeoffs alone and move more of their workflow into Handoff over time.


**How does AI estimating compare to using Buildertrend or JobTread?**


[Buildertrend](https://buildertrend.com/) requires you to build estimate line items manually — the platform organizes and tracks the estimate once it exists, but the scope-building is still on you. JobTread has added AI-assisted estimating via MCP[integration with ChatGPT and Claude](https://handoff.ai/blog/100-chatgpt-prompts-for-general-contractors-home-remodelers) , which can draft estimates from text prompts — but it requires external tool setup outside the platform. Neither supports generating a scope from site photos, walkthrough video, or uploaded documents. Handoff generates the estimate structure directly from your input — no third-party AI wiring required — which cuts the time from scope to proposal significantly for residential contractors doing their own estimating.


**What is the difference between a takeoff and an estimate?**


A[takeoff](https://www.handoff.ai/blog/what-is-a-quantity-takeoff-in-construction) is the count: the measured quantities of every material and area in a plan set. An[estimate](https://www.handoff.ai/instant-ai-estimates) takes those quantities and adds pricing, labor, and markup to produce the number you bid. Handoff connects the two automatically, so the quantities from your AI takeoff flow into a priced estimate without re-keying anything.
