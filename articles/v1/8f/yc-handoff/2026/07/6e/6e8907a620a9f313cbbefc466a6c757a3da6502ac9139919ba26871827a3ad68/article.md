# How Do Handoff AI Takeoffs Compare to Buildxact Takeoffs?

## Buildxact takeoff software and Blu AI: What it does


[Buildxact](https://www.buildxact.com/us/) is a strong residential construction estimating platform with a full pre-construction suite: takeoff, estimating, proposals, change orders, and a client portal. It's been around long enough to build real credibility in the residential builder space, and its pricing integrations are useful.


Buildxact's Blu is an AI layer across the platform with four core modules:


- **Blu Takeoff Assistant:** AI-assisted scaling and measurement that preps your takeoff faster
- **Blu Estimate Reviewer:** AI double-checks your estimate for missed items and common errors
- **Blu Assembly Assistant:** AI support for building repeatable estimate assemblies
- **Blu Estimate Generator:** creates an estimate framework you then refine


Blu reduces friction in the estimating workflow and helps catch mistakes. But here's the critical detail: **Blu is positioned as an assistant** . It preps and speeds up the work while you stay at the desk to review, edit, and build the estimate. Blu assists. You execute.


### Pricing caveat for Buildxact


One more detail worth knowing before you compare pricing: on[Buildxact's Foundation and Pro plans](https://www.buildxact.com/us/pricing/) , Blu's AI tools are paid add-ons. The full Blu suite comes standard only on the top-tier Master plan. AI that helps you costs extra as you go. Factor that into the comparison.


## The difference between an AI Assistant and an AI Agent for takeoffs


There's a reason the industry distinguishes between[AI assistants and AI agents](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/ai-agents-vs-ai-assistants) . An assistant helps you do something. An agent does it for you.


When you use Buildxact's Blu to scale your blueprint and guide your measurements, you're still clicking through plans. You're still reviewing AI suggestions and confirming them one by one. The cognitive load is lower. The time savings are real. But you're still in the workflow, making decisions, validating outputs.


For contractors with dedicated estimators, this is valuable. The estimator's job gets easier and faster.


For contractors without a dedicated estimator (or for busy GCs who just need the takeoff done) assistance isn't the same as automation.


- Buildxact's Blu AI works alongside you as an **assistant** — it helps, prompts, and assists as you move through the measurement process.
- Handoff's AI works instead of you as a **teammate** — upload your plans, and it handles the entire takeoff autonomously.

Handoff AI doesn't assist. It executes.

No measuring. No clicking. No sitting with the plans. And when it's done, the color-coded PDF lets you check every measurement before the estimate goes out. Autonomous doesn't mean out of your control.


[Handoff was trained on 100,000+ completed residential estimates](https://handoff.ai/ai-takeoffs) . It recognizes floor plans, elevation drawings, and plan sets the way an experienced estimator would, because it has processed thousands of real residential projects across every trade and scope type.

## Which platform should you pick for your takeoffs?


If you have estimating staff and want to make their workflow smarter — Buildxact's Blu is a solid option.


If you're a solo contractor, a small GC, or a[growing remodeling company](https://www.handoff.ai/remodelers) that wants takeoffs done without dedicating staff hours to them — Handoff AI is a different category entirely.


Takeoff software helps you measure faster. Handoff AI does the measuring for you. For contractors whose time is their most valuable asset, that difference is the whole decision.


## Other FAQs about Handoff takeoffs and Buildxact takeoffs


**Should I choose Buildxact or Handoff AI for takeoffs?**


Choose Buildxact if you have estimating staff and want AI that makes their workflow faster. Choose Handoff if you want the takeoff done without staff: upload plans and get back a complete, color-coded takeoff in about two hours. No. Handoff works alongside whatever workflow you run today, so you can use AI Takeoffs without migrating your projects or retraining your team. Contractors who want everything in one place can also run their estimates,[proposals](https://www.handoff.ai/winning-ai-proposals) , contact records, and[invoices](https://www.handoff.ai/invoicing) in Handoff.


**Do AI takeoffs work for remodels or only new construction?**


Both. Handoff was trained on more than 100,000 completed residential estimates, which include kitchen remodels, bathroom additions, and whole-home renovations alongside new builds. Any residential plan set up to 5,000 square feet works.
