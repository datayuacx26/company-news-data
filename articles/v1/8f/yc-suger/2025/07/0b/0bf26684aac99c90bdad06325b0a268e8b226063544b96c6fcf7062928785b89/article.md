# Launching Data Export: Your Central Hub for Reports & Raw Data

Need to get billing data for reconciliation, run advanced analytics, or just export a CSV for your team? Meet[Suger Data Export](https://doc.suger.io/get-started/table-export) , your new go-to tool for flexible, powerful, and automated reporting.


## **One Hub. All Reports.**


Suger Data Export is a centralized report hub designed for:


- Executive Report Viewers
- Report Viewers
- Operations Analysts


You can now export data from any supported program in Insights, whether you’re handling usage metering, billing, or co-sell records all in one place. Access Data Export[here](https://console.suger.io/settings) .


### **What You Can Do**


With Data Export, you can:


- Export billing events, usage data, or co-sell records on demand
- Automate recurring exports for scheduled reporting
- Choose from flexible time ranges, from 30 days to your entire dataset
- Download in CSV, JSON, or PARQUET formats
- Send data to Snowflake, BigQuery, Google Storage, or Databricks


## **Use Case**


**Finance & RevOps**


Easily extract AWS Billing Events or Azure CMA usage data for monthly reconciliation or revenue recognition.


**Data Teams**


Push structured datasets to Snowflake or Databricks for advanced analytics and forecasting.


**Integration Testing**


Set up scheduled exports to validate data pipelines and test downstream tools reliably.


**Ad-hoc Reporting**


Quickly download a CSV to share with your team or audit key data without touching your database.


## How it works


1. Go to Settings > Data Export tab
2. Select your source table
3. Choose the destination (Download, Snowflake, etc.)
4. Pick your export format
5. Set a time range, and select one time or schedule it to run regularly


That’s it. Your data, you way, ready in seconds. Visit our[docs](https://docs.suger.io/) to learn more.
