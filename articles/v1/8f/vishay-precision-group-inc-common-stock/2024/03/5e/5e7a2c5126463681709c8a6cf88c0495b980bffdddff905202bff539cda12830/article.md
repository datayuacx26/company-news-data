# vLite™ innovation for lighter, more sustainable load cells

Every industry is looking for ways to reduce carbon footprint and improve sustainability. Load cells in general can help by providing precise measurement and control that enable more efficient operation of equipment and more effective use of resources.


Now, there is an additional way to help reduce environmental impact – vLite™ technology, which enables lighter-weight load cells that save on materials, waste and energy.


With this revolutionary technology, the VPG Force Sensors team has completely reimagined load cell design. For decades, the solid metal beam was the steadfast foundation of all load cells, mostly unchanged despite advancements in strain gauge technology and evolving application demands. vLite™ introduces a groundbreaking approach by replacing the conventional solid beam with a hollow structure crafted from aviation-grade aluminum. This innovation slashes the weight of load cells by as much as 50% or so, without compromising on quality, accuracy, reliability, or durability.


This reduction in weight has exciting implications for numerous applications, particularly wherever system weight is a prime concern. For example, in the medical field, these lightweight load cells are ideal for infant scales, fluid weighing and monitoring, and hospital beds. Because vLite™ also meets the needs of legal for trade weighing applications, these innovative load cells are also ideal for such as grocery checkout scales, portable retail scales such as those used at farmers markets, postal scales, and packaging and filling machines.


vLite™ technology has been implemented in an initial line of load cells, which are available now for immediate purchase (see below). They are exchangeable with comparable standard load cells, enabling fast and easy integration to create lighter-weight products with existing product designs. Renowned VPG quality and a 2-year warranty together minimize production risks and provide peace of mind for machine builders.


The versatility of vLite™ technology extends beyond those off-the-shelf solutions. It can be seamlessly integrated into any single-point load cell design, offering customized solutions for diverse applications across industries.
