# Similarweb Report Benchmarks AI Brand Visibility Winners and Overachievers

# Similarweb Report Benchmarks AI Brand Visibility Winners and Overachievers


March 03, 2026 8:00 am EST[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_1c4bd553b97ce72d7dd63180f01dc55c/similarweb/news/2026-03-03_Similarweb_Report_Benchmarks_AI_Brand_Visibility_147.pdf)


*GenAI Brand Visibility Index explores how brands are winning favorable mentions from ChatGPT and its competitors across Finance, Travel, Consumer Electronics, Beauty, Fashion, and News*


TEL AVIV, Israel--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- As consumers increasingly turn to ChatGPT and other generative AI tools for answers to their questions, every business needs to understand the findings of the new[Generative AI Brand Visibility Index report](https://www.similarweb.com/corp/2026-genai-brand-visibility-index/) from Similarweb (NYSE: SMWB).


This press release features multimedia. View the full release here:[https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260303878419/en/](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260303878419/en/)


The report reveals which brands are cited most frequently in GenAI answers, which are showing the greatest momentum, and which are overachieving – as in, demonstrating greater visibility in AI answers than in conventional search. Industries covered include Finance, Travel, Consumer Electronics, Beauty, Fashion, and News.


“As advertising begins to be introduced in ChatGPT, understanding how to win organic visibility in AI answers is particularly important,” said Adelle Kehoe, Director of Product Marketing at Similarweb. “As the importance of GenAI visibility grows, some brands may feel compelled to advertise if they want to achieve any visibility at all. Ideally, you want any ad dollars you choose to spend to magnify the impact of the credibility you’ve already gained through organic visibility in AI answers. You want to convert that visibility into sales.”


Not surprisingly, the most cited brands in an industry tend to be familiar names, like Apple in Consumer Electronics, but that’s not the end of the story.


“Years of building trust, deep specialism, and recognizable positioning are now compounding in AI search,” Kehoe said. “At the same time, the inclusion of ‘overachievers’ in this report signals something equally important: AI search represents an opportunity for all, not just a playground for incumbents. Rising players tend to share common traits: high-quality, specialist content, and deep subject-matter expertise.”


In other words, Kehoe said, “Authority, not just scale, is emerging as the differentiator.”


For example, in Consumer Electronics, specialist retailers and support-led platforms such as B&H, Adorama, and iFixit are accelerating their visibility growth and overperforming relative to their branded search demand. In News, the wire service Reuters is the leader, but the much more specialized Science Direct is an overachiever. The report provides detailed breakdowns ranking 113 distinct brands across Finance, Travel, Consumer Electronics, Beauty, Fashion, and News.
