# Lumentum Named to Newsweek’s America’s Greatest Workplaces in Tech 2026

### Lumentum Named to Newsweek’s America’s Greatest Workplaces in Tech 2026


July 20, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s21.q4cdn.com/377324469/files/doc_news/Lumentum-Named-to-Newsweeks--Americas-Greatest-Workplaces-in-Tech-2026-2026.pdf)


SAN JOSE, Calif — Lumentum Holdings Inc. ("Lumentum")


today announced it has been named one of[America’s Greatest Workplaces in Tech 2026](https://rankings.newsweek.com/americas-greatest-workplaces-in-tech-2026) by *Newsweek* and Plant-A Insights Group. Based on an evaluation of publicly available data and a confidential nationwide employee survey, the recognition highlights organizations that prioritize employee well-being, strong leadership, fair compensation, and work-life balance. This is the second consecutive year that the company has received this award.


“Being named one of America’s Greatest Workplaces in Tech for the second year is a testament to our exceptional employees and the strong culture of innovation we continue to build at Lumentum,” said Michael Hurlston, Chief Executive Officer. “As a leader in optical and photonic solutions for AI infrastructure and networking applications, our ability to support customers depends on talented people working together with precision, urgency, and purpose. This recognition reinforces our belief that investing in our people is one of the most important commitments we can make as a technology company.”


"As the tech sector navigates structural realignments and the rapid deployment of artificial intelligence, sustaining a culture of innovation requires an unwavering commitment to talent. Newsweek’s definitive ranking elevates the tech companies that look beyond perks to build environments rooted in clear communication, equity, and meaningful professional development. This list serves as an essential index of the workplaces driving the future of the digital economy," said Jennifer H. Cunningham, Newsweek Editor-In-Chief.


Founded in 2015, Lumentum employs 13,000 people globally. Explore career opportunities[here](https://lumentum.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/LITE) .


**About Lumentum**


Lumentum (NASDAQ: LITE) is a global leader in optical and photonic technologies that power the networks and infrastructure behind AI, cloud computing, and next-generation communications. Built on decades of photonics innovation, Lumentum delivers high-performance lasers, modules, and optical subsystems that enable scalable, energy-efficient data center connectivity, advanced telecom networks, industrial manufacturing, and sensing applications. Headquartered in San Jose, California, the company operates R&D, manufacturing, and sales facilities worldwide.
