### Lumentum Announces Reporting Date for Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year 2026 Results


July 13, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s21.q4cdn.com/377324469/files/doc_news/Lumentum-Announces-Reporting-Date-for-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2026-Results-2026.pdf)


SAN JOSE, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Lumentum Holdings Inc. ("Lumentum") today announced that it will release its fiscal fourth quarter and full year 2026 financial results on Tuesday, August 11, 2026, after the market closes.


Lumentum will hold an audio webcast the same day at 2:00 p.m. PT/5:00 p.m. ET. A live audio webcast of the call and replay will be available in the Investors section of the Lumentum website at[http://investor.lumentum.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Finvestor.lumentum.com%2F&esheet=54569181&newsitemid=20260713790236&lan=en-US&anchor=http%3A%2F%2Finvestor.lumentum.com&index=1&md5=30fb733395f3ae1bf0701241f5ef2c1d) . Analysts planning to participate in the interactive Q&A portion of the webcast are encouraged to pre-register:[pre-registration link](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fanalyst%2F947753374%3Fpwd%3DrFkD1Rec&esheet=54569181&newsitemid=20260713790236&lan=en-US&anchor=pre-registration+link&index=2&md5=edf6f098f55301ed638f7fdf41f10484) .


The earnings press release will be posted at[http://investor.lumentum.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Finvestor.lumentum.com%2F&esheet=54569181&newsitemid=20260713790236&lan=en-US&anchor=http%3A%2F%2Finvestor.lumentum.com&index=3&md5=3290e1114da21ff5e2b19cf7c539cf73) under the "News Releases" section. **About Lumentum**


Lumentum (NASDAQ: LITE) is a global leader in optical and photonic technologies that power the networks and infrastructure behind AI, cloud computing, and next-generation communications. Built on decades of photonics innovation, Lumentum delivers high-performance lasers, modules, and optical subsystems that enable scalable, energy-efficient data center connectivity, advanced telecom networks, industrial manufacturing, and sensing applications. Headquartered in San Jose, California, the company operates R&D, manufacturing, and sales facilities worldwide.
