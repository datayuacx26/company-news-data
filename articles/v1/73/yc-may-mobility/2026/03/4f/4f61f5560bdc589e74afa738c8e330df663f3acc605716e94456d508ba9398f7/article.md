# May Mobility Recognized on Fast Company’s 2026 list of the World’s…

**ANN ARBOR, Mich., March 24, 2026—** May Mobility, a leading autonomous vehicle (AV) technology company, has been named one of Fast Company’s Most Innovative Companies, earning the[No. 2 spot in the Transportation category](https://www.fastcompany.com/91497308/transportation-most-innovative-companies-2026) . This recognition comes in light of May Mobility’s efforts to advance the autonomous vehicle industry with its Multi-Policy Decision Making (MPDM) technology and partnerships with leading ride-hail companies Uber, Lyft and Grab.


May Mobility AVs use a proprietary AI-based reasoning system known as Multi-Policy Decision Making (“MPDM”) that combines two core ideas: an AI world model and reinforcement learning. Analyzing thousands of predictions every second, MPDM selects the safest driving strategy in-situ without having to rely on billions of miles of training data to make the correct decision. The human-like reasoning handles novel situations, solving the industry’s edge-case problem and enabling May Mobility to scale autonomous operations efficiently across the U.S. and international markets. To date, May Mobility’s has deployed driverless deployments in three states using its MPDM technology.


“This recognition marks the difference between memorizing the world and understanding it,” said Edwin Olson, CEO and founder of May Mobility. “Our architecture reasons through thousands of possible futures in real time using a model of the world. Reasoning and understanding, not just data collection, fundamentally change the scaling of autonomy and will move this industry forward.”


Partnerships are key to May Mobility’s Autonomy-as-a-Service strategy, enabling the company to focus on technology development while partners drive demand and manufacturing support for the AV company. May Mobility recently partnered with leading global ride-hail companies Uber, Lyft and Grab to deploy its autonomous technology. In September 2025, Lyft and May Mobility launched their first AV deployment together in Atlanta. May Mobility and Uber plan to deploy in Arlington, Texas, soon.


To determine honorees, Fast Company’s editors and writers review companies driving progress around the world and across industries, evaluating thousands of submissions through a competitive application process. The result is a globe-spanning guide to innovation today, from early-stage startups to some of the most valuable companies in the world.


“Our list of the Most Innovative Companies is about spotlighting organizations that don’t just adapt to change—they drive it,” said Fast Company editor-in-chief Brendan Vaughan. “They are setting the pace for their industries and offering a blueprint for what sustained innovation can achieve.”


Since 2017, May Mobility has successfully given more than half a million autonomous rides and driven more than 1.1 million miles across 24 locations in the U.S. and Japan.

**About May Mobility**


May Mobility is an autonomous vehicle (AV) technology company headquartered in Ann Arbor, Michigan, redefining the future of autonomy-as-a-service (AaaS). Leveraging its patented Multi-Policy Decision Making (MPDM) system, May Mobility built an industry-leading in-situ AI solution, which integrates real-time data every 200 milliseconds to prioritize safety and efficiency, even in unpredictable situations. Operating on-demand vehicles across the U.S. and Japan, May Mobility delivers safer, more efficient transportation across diverse environments, including rural cities, dense urban areas and diverse weather conditions. Backed by strategic partnerships with industry leaders, including Toyota Motor Corporation and NTT, May Mobility is disrupting the autonomous vehicle industry with one of the most practical vehicle offerings available. The company has completed more than half a million rides across 24 deployments worldwide. For more information, visit www.maymobility.com.
