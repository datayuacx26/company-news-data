Endless scrolling has become an addictive behavior, driven by the dopamine spike experienced when opening familiar apps. This seemingly harmless habit has become ingrained in our daily routines.

# The need for effective techniques to prevent the scroll


Implementing various techniques to stop mindlessly browsing apps can lead to a positive change in phone habits, empowering individuals to regain control of their digital lives.
