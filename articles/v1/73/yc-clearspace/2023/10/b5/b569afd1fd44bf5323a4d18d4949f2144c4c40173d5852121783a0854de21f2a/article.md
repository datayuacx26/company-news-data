# Clearspace vs One Sec: Which is the better screen time management app?

# Clearspace vs One Sec: Which is the better screen time management app?


Read Time - 4 min


In the digital age, managing screen time has become crucial for maintaining mental health and productivity. Two notable apps in this arena are Clearspace and One Sec. Both aim to curb excessive smartphone usage, but they employ different strategies to achieve this goal.


# Clearspace: Habit Change Backed by Psychology


### Features of Clearspace:


- **Intentionality Layer** : Users must complete a centering exercise before opening selected apps.
- **Session Lengths** : Users set specific session lengths for each app. Clearspace automatically ends the session when the time is up.


- **Progress Tracking** : Users build up a streak of their progress over time if they are able stay under their budget.
- **Selective App Control** : Enables users to choose which apps to limit.


### **Pros:**


- **Ease of use** : Clearspace is known for its elegant and user-friendly interface.
- **Customization:** Offers advanced features like focus scheduling, social accountability, and alternative unlocking mechanisms like physical activity to unlock apps
- **Data Insights** : Helps users understand their phone habits better.
- **Cross-Platform** : Allows users to get the same features on their computer
- **Long term efficacy:** Users credit Clearspace with permanently reducing mindless scrolling and increasing their digital wellbeing.
- **ADHD Relief:** Found to be particularly beneficial for users with ADHD.


### **Cons:**


- **Limited to Selected Apps** : Only works on apps chosen by the user.
- **Potentially Annoying** : Frequent interruptions might be irritating for some users.
- **Cost** : Offers a premium version, which might be a downside for those looking for a free tool.


# One Sec: An Automatic Interrupt


### Features of One Sec:


- **Automated Mindful Moments** : Triggers a pause (like deep breathing) before opening a selected app.
- **App and Website Blocking** : Can block distracting apps and websites.
- **Intention Setting** : Users set intentions for app usage before entering apps.
- **Focus Sessions** : Blocks all configured apps for a period of pure focus.
- **Data Privacy** : Does not sell or analyze user data.


### Pros:


- **Cross Platform** : Available on both Android and iOS.
- **Long-Term Habit Change** : Focuses on changing user habits, not just temporary fixes.
- **Versatility** : Works on both apps and websites.
- **Privacy-Focused** : Ensures user data privacy.


### Cons:


- **Initial Setup Time** : Many users report difficulty setting up. Requires some technical ability and patience to configure.
- **Interruption Overload** : Constant interruptions could be counterproductive for some users. One sec does not have the ability to automatically end user’s app sessions.
- **Free Version Limitations** : The free version has limited functionalities.


Both Clearspace and One Sec present innovative approaches to screen time management, with their unique features catering to different user needs. Clearspace is ideal for those who prefer a mindful approach to app usage, with its focus on intentionality and session management and ease of use. On the other hand, One Sec is more suited for users looking to interrupt their existing digital habits, offering a way to reassess the necessity of each app or website visit for the technically inclined.


While both apps share the noble goal of reducing screen time, they offer distinct paths towards achieving digital wellness. Users should choose based on their specific needs, whether it's embarking on a habit-altering journey with Clearspace or taking a quick pause with One Sec.
