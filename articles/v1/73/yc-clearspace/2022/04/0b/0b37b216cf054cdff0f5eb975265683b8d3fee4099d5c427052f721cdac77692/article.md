Since the ClearSpace iOS app launched on the App Store last summer, we’ve tracked how much time we’ve helped people save or repurpose by cutting back on their phone use.


We started with a back-of-the-napkin calculation: assuming that our users save 5 minutes for each time ClearSpace helps them realize they didn’t mean to open a distracting app, **we just passed the 10,000 hour mark for time saved by our users** (including nearly 2,000 hours saved during the month of April alone). 10,000 hours is a satisfying and famous number — it translates to over 400 days of time saved, and was famously popularized by Malcolm Gladwell (or Macklemore, depending on your taste) as the amount of hours that it takes someone to become an expert in a given field.

Even better — 5 minutes per intercept is a little simplistic! A[2021 study from FinancesOnline](https://financesonline.com/social-media-usage-statistics) found the average time per open for popular social media apps: 7 minutes and 37 seconds on Instagram, 3 minutes and 32 seconds on TikTok, 10 minutes and 42 seconds on Twitter, 4 minutes and 49 seconds for Facebook, and 15 minutes and 11 seconds on YouTube. For the apps without a listed average visit length, we reverted to our previous assumption of 5 minutes saved per successful intercept.


With our new numbers — buoyed by Instagram’s above-average visit length — **the real amount of time saved is closer to 15,000 hours.** At time of writing, the official number was 14,406 hours (although given the approximate nature of the average time per open, that number isn’t exact. That number puts us just over 600 days of time saved.
