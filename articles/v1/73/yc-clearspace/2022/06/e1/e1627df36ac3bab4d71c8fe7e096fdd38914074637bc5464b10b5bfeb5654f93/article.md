About six weeks ago, we[celebrated](https://blog.getclearspace.com/stat-of-the-week-15-000-hours-saved-3d23e0964320) saving our users 15,000 hours; earlier this week, we broke the 20,000 hour mark. We’ve saved users over 11,000 hours — 458 days — on Instagram alone. At this pace, we could break 30,000 hours by the end of July (about six weeks from now).

One way we’ve enjoyed thinking about this time saved is by thinking about going back in time — 20,000 hours ago was March 6, 2020. To put things in perspective — the US didn’t reach one thousand reported cases of COVID until March 12, almost a week later.

We also like to think about the time we spend working on ClearSpace gets multiplied by the time our users save. Conservatively, the time we’ve put in has been realized five-fold by our users, and that number will only keep improving.


While the 20,000 hour mark is enjoyable to consider in a lump sum, its real application — a few minutes here, a few minutes there—has a different but rewarding feel. Instead of one of our users winning the lottery, we’ve built technology that has scattered countless $5 bills on our users’ paths, and as result, we hope ClearSpacers have been able to find little moments of joy throughout their lives.
