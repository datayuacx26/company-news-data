## **Why look for a Fresha alternative for appointment management?**


Source: PR Newswire


Fresha built a lot of goodwill by being genuinely free for small businesses early on. For some owners, it still feels like a great deal. For others, the frustration starts when the *effective* monthly cost becomes hard to predict as you add staff, messaging, payments or marketplace exposure.


[One reviewer](https://www.softwareadvice.com/retail/shedul-profile/reviews/) says they pay $72/month for basic booking needs. Another describes being charged a 20% marketplace fee for clients who don't even come through their platform.


The most common pain points pushing appointment-based businesses to look for alternatives are:


1.


**The fee model can feel like it keeps shifting.** What starts simple can become layered over time: features change, limits appear and workflows that used to be included end up requiring additional monthly fees or add-ons. Fresha lists its plan pricing, but many owners say the *all-in* total is what’s hard to forecast once you factor in messaging, processing and marketplace fees.


2.


**Marketplace commissions can be hard to control.** Reviewers describe being charged the “new client” marketplace fee even when they believe the client came through Google, referrals or direct contact.


3.


**Extra steps can add booking friction.** Some businesses report client confusion with technical issues like verification steps and account requirements—small hurdles that matter when your goal is “tap, book, done.”


4.


**Support becomes a problem when bookings break.** When clients can’t book and you’re losing appointments in real time, slow or limited support channels get to you—especially for teams managing multiple practitioners or locations.


If these issues are starting to get to you, dive in to our Fresha alternatives comparison with clearer pricing, more control over client relationships and fewer unexpected charges.
