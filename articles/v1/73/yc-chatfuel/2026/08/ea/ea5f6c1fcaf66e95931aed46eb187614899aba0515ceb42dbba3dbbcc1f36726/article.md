##


1. [Chatfuel](https://chatfuel.com/) — best for automation and attribution in one system


**Best for:** agencies that want the automation running the conversation and the attribution reporting on it to be the same product, not two tools stitched together.


Chatfuel runs your WhatsApp and Instagram automation and reports Conversions API events from inside it. It connects only through the official WhatsApp Business API, as a Meta Business Partner, from day one.


**Pros:**


-


No paid gate on the official, trustworthy connection method


-


Detects a sale, booking, or any event you define — not locked to one fixed trigger


-


Reads voice notes and images, not just typed text


-


Automation and attribution run as one system, not two separate tools


**Cons:**


-


Focused specifically on WhatsApp and Instagram — not built as a broad multi-channel attribution suite covering every ad platform at once.


**Pricing:** From $99/mo.


##


1. [Metrito](https://metrito.com/) — best for agencies already on a Brazilian stack tool


Metrito is a Brazilian server-side tracking tool covering both web and WhatsApp, from click to sale. It gives traffic managers a single dashboard to manage multiple ad accounts at once (pause, activate, adjust budgets), with plug-and-play tracking and automatic data enrichment, plus customizable dashboards built on ready-made templates or your own. It's distributed through the Hotmart marketplace and ships detailed documentation for common BR platforms, along with a mobile app and Chrome extension.


**Pros:**


-


Multi-account ad management from one dashboard, not per-platform


-


Plug-and-play tracking with automatic data enrichment


-


Extensive self-serve documentation


-


Distributed via Hotmart, a familiar rail for many BR agencies


**Cons:**


-


Narrower footprint outside the Hotmart-adjacent ecosystem


**Pricing:** From ~$48/mo (R$248).


##


1. [Chatlabs](https://www.chatlabs.com.br/) — best for businesses on a specific vertical platform


Chatlabs is an official Meta partner offering native WhatsApp lead tracking, built around deep integrations with specific vertical platforms — Feegow, Cange, Sienge, IXC, and RD Station. It also supports WhatsApp Coexistence (CoEx), letting a team keep using WhatsApp Business or WhatsApp Web for day-to-day conversations while running the official API in parallel for scale, bulk dispatch, and automation.


**Pros:**


-


Deep, purpose-built integrations for named vertical platforms rather than generic connectors


-


CoEx support means teams don't have to give up the number or app their customers already know while adopting the official API


-


Positioned around plugging into an existing stack rather than replacing it


**Cons:**


-


Value depends heavily on already running one of its supported vertical platforms


**Pricing:** From ~$86/mo (R$449.90).


##


1. [Mercately](https://www.mercately.com/) — best for a broader multi-channel ad stack


Mercately connects Meta Ads, Google Ads, WhatsApp, and CRM in one place. It's backed by Techstars (2023), with press coverage from TechCrunch and LatamList, and one of the more developed partner programs in this category.


**Pros:**


-


Broadest integration scope of the group (Meta Ads + Google Ads + WhatsApp + CRM)


-


Real investor and press credibility (Techstars, TechCrunch)


-


Three-tier partner program for agencies


**Cons:**


-


Broader scope may be more than agencies who only need WhatsApp attribution are looking for


**Pricing:** From $299/mo.


##


1. [Leadsales](https://leadsales.io/) — best for review-credibility and Meta partner status


Leadsales is an official Meta Business Partner, backed by NVIDIA Inception, and was featured on Shark Tank Mexico — reportedly the show's biggest deal to date. Independent Capterra reviews describe it as intuitive enough to skip extensive team training, with responsive customer support and reliable data tracking.


**Pros:**


-


Official Meta Business Partner status


-


Reviewers highlight ease of use and minimal onboarding time, without needing to train a team from scratch


-


High-profile investor and media credibility (NVIDIA Inception, Shark Tank Mexico)


**Cons:**


-


There are limited customization and reporting options. There is no built-in automation, and it occasionally loads slowly during use.


**Pricing:** From $97/mo.


## Your next steps


If official WhatsApp connection and combined automation + attribution matter most to you, start with Chatfuel. If you're already committed to a specific vertical platform or a broader ad stack, Metrito, Chatlabs, Mercately, or Leadsales are worth a closer look against your own setup.
