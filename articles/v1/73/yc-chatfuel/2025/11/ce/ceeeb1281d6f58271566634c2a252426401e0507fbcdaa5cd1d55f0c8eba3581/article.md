-

## **FAQs**


### **Are free salon booking apps worth using?**


Free salon booking apps can be excellent starting points, especially for new beauty businesses or solo practitioners. Platforms like ManyChat or Setter AI offer basic features for free, making them valuable for testing automated booking.


However, they come with limitations: restricted conversation volumes, limited customization options, or commission-based pricing on client bookings. For serious salon operations, the monthly subscription fee usually pays for itself through increased bookings and reduced no-shows.


### **Can salon booking apps integrate with my existing website?**


Yes, most modern booking software, like Chatfuel, offers seamless website integration you can set up in just a few clicks.


It’s as simple as creating an account and installing the plugin, plus a few minutes to customize the appearance and add branding. The best integrations feel native to your website, maintaining your brand aesthetic while providing powerful appointment booking capabilities.


### **What's the difference between salon software and booking apps?**


Salon and spa software are comprehensive systems that manage your entire business and handle appointments, point of sale, inventory management, staff management, and financial reporting.


Booking apps focus specifically on appointment scheduling, solo and group bookings and customer communication, often with lighter feature sets but better specialization in conversion and client experience.


The trend is toward booking apps with CRM capabilities, combining the best of both worlds: specialized booking expertise with broader salon management features.


### **Can I use salon booking apps for multiple locations?**


Most professional booking apps support multiple locations.


Key features for multi-location operations include:


-


Centralized client databases across all locations


-


Location-specific availability and services


-


Unified reporting and analytics


-


Staff management across multiple sites
