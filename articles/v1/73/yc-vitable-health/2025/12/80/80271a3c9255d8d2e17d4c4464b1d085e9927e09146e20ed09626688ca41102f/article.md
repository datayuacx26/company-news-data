# Empowering Clinicians With the Care They Deserve: Vitable Health Partners With DirectShifts

## What the Program Offers


Clinicians using DirectShifts can now sign up for Vitable Primary Care for a simple, flat monthly fee. With no copays, no deductibles, and no surprise bills, Vitable Health’s model is built to support real access, not bureaucracy. Every membership includes:


- Unlimited virtual visits with primary care providers
- $0 routine labs and 1,000+ prescription medications at no out-of-pocket cost
- Preventive and chronic care services, including wellness visits and health coaching
- Access to licensed benefits advisors who can help clinicians shop for individual insurance plans on their state’s marketplace and assist with enrollment


This partnership gives clinicians a streamlined, affordable way to stay on top of their health.


## Why This Matters: Barriers to Care for Clinicians


Even though they spend their careers delivering care, many clinicians face real challenges when it comes to getting care for themselves. Studies show healthcare workers have significantly higher rates of chronic conditions than the general population. Time constraints, burnout, and the complexity of navigating benefits all play a role.


[A study published by Occupational Medicine](https://academic.oup.com/occmed/article-abstract/65/1/49/1432459?redirectedFrom=fulltext) found that 53% of physicians reported trouble getting time or access to personal healthcare, and another[study in the Annals of Translational Medicine](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6694267/) found that nearly half of nurses engaged in self-treatment for their illnesses—and even self-prescribing medications—instead of consulting a physician for their ailments.


These are not isolated cases—they reflect a system that asks too much of its providers while giving too little back.


“Clinicians spend their lives caring for others, yet far too many struggle to access basic care for themselves,” said Sai Kumar Thumuluru, CEO of DirectShifts. “National data shows that more than one in three healthcare workers delay or skip medical visits because of cost, time pressure, or burnout. With over 100,000 clinicians using DirectShifts across the country, we see this challenge every day. Our partnership with Vitable Health gives clinicians convenient and affordable access to the care they need to stay healthy, supported, and able to keep delivering exceptional care to their patients. When clinicians thrive, the entire healthcare system gets stronger.”
