# A Culture of Care That Extends Beyond Benefits

At Vitable Health, we believe health goes far beyond the doctor’s office. Access to nourishing food, supportive environments, and equitable opportunities are just as essential to well-being as healthcare itself. That is why we are proud to partner with organizations like[Urban Growers Collective (UGC)](https://www.urbangrowerscollective.org/) in Chicago and[El Departamento de la Comida in Puerto Rico](https://eldepartamentodelacomida.org/) , both working tirelessly to build a more just and equitable food system.


*Vitable team supporting local food justice in Chicago.*


#### Supporting Local Food Justice in Chicago


Urban Growers Collective, a Black and women-led non-profit, is on a mission to increase access to affordable and nutrient-rich food. Each year,[Vitable donates $15,000 to UGC](https://www.urbangrowerscollective.org/blog/urban-growers-collective-ugc-and-vitable-health-launch-a-new-partnership-with-a-company-wide-volunteer-day-on-july-11) to support their work, and our teams volunteer to strengthen that connection even further.


*Vitable team gives back in Chicago.*


This year, employees traveled to Chicago for a department retreat, where they toured UGC’s farms, harvested green beans, and learned firsthand how the produce is distributed to South and West Side communities. Some of the harvest is sold affordably, while much of it is donated to families in need, creating a direct link between care and community health.
