# Proud to Be A Preferred Vendor for Visiting Angels

At Vitable Health, we believe that better healthcare starts with everyday access, not red tape. That’s why we are proud to continue our role as a preferred partner of[Visiting Angels Living Assistance Services](https://www.visitingangels.com/) , building on a consistent and trusted relationship with their franchise network over the years.


For more than 25 years, Visiting Angels has been known as *America’s Choice in Senior Home Care®* . With more than 600 independently owned and operated franchise locations across the U.S. and internationally, the organization helps tens of thousands of families each year by providing compassionate, non-medical home care services. Their caregivers support seniors with everyday needs such as bathing, meal preparation, light housekeeping, and transportation, as well as specialized services like dementia care and respite care for family caregivers.


Behind this vital care is a dedicated workforce of caregivers, individuals who give their time, energy, and compassion to make aging at home possible. But historically, too many caregivers have lacked access to affordable, meaningful health benefits. That’s where Vitable Health comes in.


#### **Supporting Caregivers with Better Health Benefits**


Through our partnership with Visiting Angels, Vitable Health offers **an ACA-compliant,** **primary-care-first health benefits platform** designed for today’s caregivers. Visiting Angels franchisees can offer their staff benefits that are:


- **Affordable** , with no co-pays, no deductibles, and no hidden fees.
- **Accessible** , with virtual primary care visits, mental health coaching, included prescriptions, and lab testing.
- **Family-friendly** , with dependent coverage available at no extra cost.


These benefits are designed to be actually used, not avoided. For caregivers, this means having peace of mind and easy access to real care. For franchise owners, it means higher caregiver satisfaction, improved retention, and a stronger ability to recruit talent in a competitive industry.


#### **A Growing Partnership Across the Country**


To date, Vitable has partnered with dozens of Visiting Angels franchise organizations nationwide, offering a comprehensive suite of benefits designed to support caregivers and keep businesses compliant. These include:


- [Individual Coverage Health Reimbursement Arrangement (ICHRA)](https://www.vitablehealth.com/ichra) **:** A flexible, tax-advantaged solution that allows employers to reimburse employees for individual health insurance plans, giving caregivers more choice in coverage.
- [Minimum Essential Coverage (MEC)](https://www.vitablehealth.com/mec) **and Minimum Value Plan (MVP):** Affordable plans that help franchises stay compliant with the Affordable Care Act while providing caregivers with access to essential health services.
- [Vitable Primary Care (VPC)](https://www.vitablehealth.com/primary-care) **:** Our core offering that ensures every member has built-in access to virtual primary care without co-pays or deductibles.
- **Dental and Vision Coverage:** Easy-to-use options that give caregivers access to preventive and restorative care for oral and eye health.
- [Occupational Health](https://www.vitablehealth.com/blog-topics/occupational-health) **:** Convenient, compliance-focused services—including tuberculosis (TB) testing, screenings, and physicals—that help caregivers stay healthy, meet regulatory requirements, and continue delivering safe, reliable care.


Together, these options make it easy for organizations of all sizes to find a plan that fits their budget while meeting the unique needs of their workforce.


As an ongoing preferred partner in 2025, we are excited to deepen this relationship and continue supporting Visiting Angels’ caregiver-first mission, ensuring the workforce that delivers hope at home also receives the care and support they deserve.


#### **Building a Better Standard of Care**


At its heart, this partnership represents an alignment of values. Visiting Angels exists to provide compassionate, person-centered care for seniors. Vitable Health exists to provide simple, affordable healthcare for the everyday workers who make that care possible. Together, we are setting a new standard for what it means to support caregivers, because when caregivers are healthier and happier, the people they serve benefit too.


**Better care for caregivers. Better care for seniors. That’s the promise of our partnership.**
