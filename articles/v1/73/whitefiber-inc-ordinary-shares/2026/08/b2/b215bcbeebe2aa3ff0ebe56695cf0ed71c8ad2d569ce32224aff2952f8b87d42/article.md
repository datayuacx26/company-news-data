# WhiteFiber and Krambu Partner to Bring 100MW of High-Density GPU Infrastructure Capacity to Market in 2027

In Q1 we made a decision to pivot the go-to-market strategy for WhiteFiber Cloud. Our differentiation lies with our engineering team’s expertise in designing, deploying, and operating GPU clusters. Supported by the operational strength of the broader team, we’ve successfully landed new customers and expanded existing customers.


‍


Despite the positive momentum, we continue to face the same challenges as the rest of the market: supply chain and access to power. The supply chain is going to do its thing. Access to power is something we believed we could solve for. One piece of the puzzle is our own WhiteFiber data centers where we plan to vertically integrate. As those plans come together, we believe our demand will continue to outpace our own development timelines, so we need a complementary solution.


‍


Today, WhiteFiber announced a strategic agreement with data center developer and operator[Krambu](https://krambu.com/) . This partnership names WhiteFiber Cloud as the exclusive GPU infrastructure operator partner for 100MW of their planned high-density data center capacity, coming online beginning in 2027.


‍


The structure is simple: Krambu builds the facility. We work together to identify high-quality off-takers. Our team designs, deploys, and operates the GPU clusters inside it.


‍


We’ve watched this industry try to solve power and compute as two separate problems, handled by two vendors, on two different timelines. That doesn’t work at the density AI workloads need now. Krambu’s infrastructure is built for direct-to-chip liquid cooling up to 250kW per rack, the density current and next-generation GPU platforms actually require, and more than most legacy data centers were ever designed to handle.


‍


That alignment is the whole point, and it’s why Krambu’s Travis Jank and I keep coming back to the same idea when we talk about this partnership.


‍


> “AI infrastructure needs to be designed from the power source through the GPU cluster. Krambu is building the high-density data center foundation, and WhiteFiber has the expertise to turn that capacity into production GPU infrastructure for demanding AI workloads. Working together gives customers a more direct path from capacity planning to deployment, while allowing us to keep advancing the power, cooling, and rack designs needed for each new generation of AI systems.”
>
>
> ` Travis Jank, President and Founder, Krambu`


‍


When Krambu is designing rack density and cooling architecture at the same time our team is designing cluster networking and deployment timelines, our customers get a direct path from a power-ready site to a fully operating cluster. They don’t have to coordinate two vendors or reconcile two schedules to hit a 2027 deployment date.


‍


We’re also not stopping at this first 100MW. WhiteFiber and Krambu plan to collaborate on research and development for the next generation of AI infrastructure, evaluating data center, cooling, and cluster architecture together as GPU platforms keep pushing more power and thermal load into every rack. We want to design the facility and the cluster as one system from the start, not bolt one onto the other after the fact.


‍


Krambu is pushing the limits of data center design, and that is exactly the kind of partner we want. For our customers, this partnership means the availability of meaningful capacity on a predictable timeline, with optimized, reliable GPU clusters. If your roadmap depends on high-density GPU capacity in the next 18 months.
