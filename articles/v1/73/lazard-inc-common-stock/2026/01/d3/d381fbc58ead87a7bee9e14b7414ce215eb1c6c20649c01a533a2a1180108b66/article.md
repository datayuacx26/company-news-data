# Lazard Asset Management Appoints Eric Van Nostrand as Chief Investment Officer

Eric most recently served as Global Head of Markets and Chief Economist at Lazard Asset Management. Prior to joining Lazard, he served as Acting Assistant Secretary of the Treasury for Economic Policy, where he led the office responsible for analyzing domestic and international U.S. economic policies and received the Alexander Hamilton Award, the U.S. Treasury's highest honor. Previously, he was a portfolio manager and Managing Director at BlackRock, where he managed systematic equity funds and served as Head of Research for Multi-Asset Strategies. Eric holds a JD from Yale Law School and degrees in economics and engineering from the University of Pennsylvania.
