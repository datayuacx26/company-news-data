# Rosalie Berman Joins Lazard Asset Management

Ms. Berman joins LAM from Morgan Stanley Wealth Management, where she spent the last 15 years in various leadership roles. She was most recently Head of Client Engagement, Corporate & Institutional Solutions, where she led the go-to-market strategy for tools on the E*Trade platform, following its acquisition by Morgan Stanley. Over the course of her career at Morgan Stanley, she held various roles developing and guiding strategy to build businesses and operations that serve clients effectively while delivering strong investment outcomes. Prior to joining Morgan Stanley, Ms. Berman held roles at Citigroup and Smith Barney. She holds a bachelor’s degree from Tufts University.
