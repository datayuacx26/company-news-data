# Living in Southern Village, Chapel Hill

# Living in[Southern Village](https://youtu.be/c7vUmP5xb24?si=ZeHJ8nKvCU8D1mPo) , Chapel Hill, NC: Is This Walkable Community Worth the Premium?


Imagine living in a neighborhood where you can walk to your morning coffee, pick up groceries, catch a movie, meet friends for dinner, and even walk your children to school—all without getting in your car.


In most of America, that's a rarity.


In[Southern Village](https://youtu.be/c7vUmP5xb24?si=ZeHJ8nKvCU8D1mPo) , it's simply everyday life.


Located just south of downtown Chapel Hill, Southern Village has become one of the Triangle's most recognizable neighborhoods thanks to its walkability, beautiful architecture, outstanding schools, and strong sense of community. More than 30 years after construction began, demand remains incredibly high, with homes often selling in just a few days.


So, what makes Southern Village so special—and is it worth the price?


Let's take a closer look.


## What Is Southern Village?


Southern Village is a master-planned community located approximately two miles south of downtown Chapel Hill and just over a mile from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.


The neighborhood spans roughly **312 acres** and includes approximately **1,500 residences** , made up of:


- Single-family homes
- Townhomes
- Condominiums
- Apartments
- Retail and restaurants
- Parks and green spaces


Unlike traditional subdivisions built during the 1990s, Southern Village was intentionally designed to create a walkable community where homes, businesses, schools, and recreation are all connected.


That design philosophy remains one of its biggest selling points today.


## The Story Behind[Southern Village](https://youtu.be/c7vUmP5xb24?si=ZeHJ8nKvCU8D1mPo)


Southern Village wasn't created by accident.


Developer D.R. Bryan was inspired by the principles of **New Urbanism** , a movement focused on creating neighborhoods that prioritize people over cars.


After hearing renowned planner Andrés Duany speak about building communities modeled after traditional towns, Bryan traveled to Italy to study **Pienza** , a Renaissance town famous for its walkable design. He also visited **Seaside, Florida** , another influential New Urbanist community.


The result was Southern Village—a neighborhood so different from typical suburban developments that Chapel Hill had to rewrite portions of its zoning regulations to make it possible.


The neighborhood quickly became one of the Triangle's biggest success stories, earning national attention from publications like *Time* , *Better Homes & Gardens* , and *Builder Magazine* .


## Homes in Southern Village


Architecture is one of the first things visitors notice.


Instead of garage doors dominating the street, you'll find:


- Large front porches
- Charleston-inspired homes
- Neo-traditional architecture
- Greek Revival influences
- Rear-entry garages accessed by alleyways


The homes are intentionally positioned close to sidewalks, encouraging interaction between neighbors and creating a more welcoming streetscape.


Housing options include everything from entry-level condos above Market Street to luxury single-family homes, making Southern Village accessible to buyers at a variety of price points.


## Home Prices


Southern Village offers one of the widest price ranges in Chapel Hill.


Recent home sales show:


### Condominiums


- Approximately **$275,000–$550,000**
- Median price around **$340,000**


### Townhomes


- Approximately **$400,000–$825,000**
- Median price around **$630,000**


### Single-Family Homes


- Approximately **$750,000–$1.65 million**
- Median price around **$888,000**


Overall, the neighborhood median sits around **$750,000** , with homes averaging roughly **$340 per square foot** .


While Southern Village certainly isn't inexpensive, buyers are investing in far more than a house—they're buying a lifestyle.


## Small Lots, Big Lifestyle


One of the biggest trade-offs is lot size.


Most single-family homes sit on just **0.10 to 0.30 acres** , with many around **0.16 acres** .


If your dream home includes a huge backyard for entertaining, gardening, or playing football, Southern Village may not be your ideal fit.


Instead, residents trade private outdoor space for shared amenities and unmatched walkability.


For many buyers, it's a trade they're happy to make.


## Why Homes Sell So Quickly


Southern Village consistently has one of the tightest housing markets in Chapel Hill.


Homes frequently:


- Go under contract in about four days
- Sell above asking price
- Receive multiple offers


Inventory is often extremely limited, with only a handful of homes available at any given time.


Adding to the challenge, some homes never reach the MLS at all. Instead, they're sold through neighborhood connections before they're publicly listed.


If Southern Village is your dream neighborhood, working with a local real estate expert can make a significant difference.


## Market Street: The Heart of the Community


At the center of Southern Village is **Market Street** , a true neighborhood town center.


Residents can walk to:


- Weaver Street Market
- Restaurants
- Coffee shops
- Lumina Theater
- Medical offices
- Fitness studios
- Local businesses


Unlike many suburban shopping centers, Market Street feels like a genuine downtown where people naturally gather throughout the day.


It's one of the features that gives Southern Village its unique personality.


## Parks, Trails, and Recreation


Southern Village offers far more than sidewalks.


Residents enjoy:


- Village Green
- Free summer concerts
- Farmers markets
- Outdoor movie nights
- Fan Branch Greenway
- Southern Community Park
- Dog park
- Playground
- Southern Village Club with pool and tennis


Instead of maintaining a large private yard, many families spend their time enjoying these shared community spaces.


## Schools


Education is one of Southern Village's strongest selling points.


The neighborhood is served by the highly regarded **Chapel Hill-Carrboro City Schools** .


Typical school assignments include:


- **Mary Scroggs Elementary School**
- **Culbreth Middle School**
- **Carrboro High School**


Mary Scroggs Elementary is located within the neighborhood itself, allowing many children to walk to school using the Greenway.


This combination of excellent schools and neighborhood walkability continues to attract families from across the Triangle.


## [Who Is Southern Village Best For?](https://youtu.be/c7vUmP5xb24?si=ZeHJ8nKvCU8D1mPo)


Southern Village is an excellent choice for buyers who:


- Want a walkable lifestyle
- Work at UNC or UNC Hospitals
- Value top-rated schools
- Prefer community events over large private yards
- Enjoy restaurants, coffee shops, and neighborhood gathering places
- Appreciate timeless architecture


It may be less appealing for buyers who:


- Want extensive private outdoor space
- Prefer brand-new construction
- Need the lowest home prices in Chapel Hill


Like any neighborhood, it's about finding the right fit for your lifestyle.


## Is Southern Village Worth It?


Southern Village remains one of Chapel Hill's most sought-after neighborhoods because it offers something that's difficult to find almost anywhere else in the Triangle.


It combines:


- Walkability
- Excellent schools
- Beautiful architecture
- Strong home values
- Convenient access to UNC
- A genuine sense of community


For buyers who want to spend less time driving and more time enjoying where they live, Southern Village delivers on that promise.


It's not just a neighborhood—it's a different way of living.
