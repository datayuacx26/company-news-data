# Syrona Health | Blog | Top 5 Media Companies Providing Exceptional Fertility Benefits

The UK media industry has rapidly embraced fertility benefits as a cornerstone of modern employee wellness programmes. With **just 19% of UK organisations currently having formal fertility support policies** and **90% of UK employees identifying a lack of workplace reproductive health support** , forward-thinking media companies are seizing the opportunity to attract and retain top talent through comprehensive fertility programmes.[peoplemanagement+1](https://www.peoplemanagement.co.uk/article/1901533/employers-adopt-infertility-policies-retain-top-talent-report-says)


### The Critical Need for Fertility Benefits in Media


**One in six couples in the UK – approximately 3.5 million people – face fertility challenges** , with **nearly 70,000 IVF treatment cycles carried out annually** . The business case is compelling: **53% of people with fertility problems would stay at their company if employer-funded treatment was available** , while **38% of people undergoing fertility treatment consider quitting their jobs** . With **the average cost of £30,000 to replace an employee** , fertility benefits represent both strategic workforce investment and smart financial planning.


### Top 5 Media Companies with Leading Fertility Benefits


### 1. Daily Mail and General Trust (DMGT) - UK Media Pioneer


**DMGT stands out as a leading UK media company** providing comprehensive fertility support through its innovative partnership with **Healix Health Services and Syrona Health** . Since February 2020, DMGT has worked with Healix to deliver tailored healthcare benefits that include cutting-edge fertility support through Syrona's platform.


DMGT's fertility programme offers employees:


- **Fertility consultations with specialist clinicians**
- **Comprehensive fertility screening and diagnostic services**
- **Discounted fertility treatment at partner clinics**
- **Access to virtual health support and symptom tracking**
- **Quicker treatment access than NHS provision**


The partnership demonstrates DMGT's commitment to supporting its workforce through **flexible, open, and adaptable healthcare administration** , ensuring employees receive personalised care throughout their fertility journey.[healix](https://healix.com/health/insights/case-studies/daily-mail-and-general-trust)


### 2. Channel 4 - UK Broadcasting Leader


Channel 4 has established itself as a fertility benefits pioneer, offering **up to three rounds of IVF treatment through insurance, regardless of gender or sexual orientation** . Their comprehensive programme includes:


- **Coverage for IVF treatments and necessary genetic testing**
- **Access to fertility counselling services**
- **Unlimited leave for fertility treatment appointments**
- **The world's first dedicated pregnancy loss policy launched in April 2021**[employeebenefits](https://employeebenefits.co.uk/healthcare-and-wellbeing/meet-reproductive-health-benefits-trailblazers-in-2021/249646.article)


Channel 4's approach ensures all employees, regardless of their path to parenthood, receive equal support and protection during their fertility journey.


### 3. Netflix - Global Streaming Giant


Netflix provides **global family-forming benefits supporting preservation, fertility, surrogacy, and adoption options** . Their inclusive programme covers **employees and their spouse/domestic partner, regardless of marital status, gender, or sexual orientation** , demonstrating commitment to supporting diverse family-building paths across their international workforce.


### 4. Disney - Entertainment Conglomerate


Disney offers extensive **family-building benefits with up to $75,000 lifetime maximum coverage** . Their comprehensive services include:


- **Fertility planning and care without requiring infertility diagnosis**
- **Egg/sperm and embryo freezing**
- **IVF, IUI, and assisted reproduction technology**
- **Adoption and surrogacy navigation support**
- **Personalised care management throughout the journey**


### 5. Warner Bros. Discovery - Media Entertainment Leader


Warner Bros. Discovery provides **comprehensive fertility benefits, adoption assistance, and surrogacy support** . Their family support extends to **cryopreservation assistance and generous parental leave** , ensuring employees receive ongoing support throughout their family-building and early parenting phases.[builtin](https://builtin.com/company/warner-bros-discovery/benefits)


### Fertility Benefits Statistics


The fertility benefits landscape shows marked differences between UK and US markets:


**UK Market Statistics:**


- **33% of UK young workers believe employers should offer fertility benefits like egg freezing and subsidised IVF**[fertilitynetworkuk](https://fertilitynetworkuk.org/talking-to-your-company-about-fertility-in-the-workplace/)
- **82% of UK organisations expect to enhance their fertility benefits programmes**[fertilitynetworkuk](https://fertilitynetworkuk.org/talking-to-your-company-about-fertility-in-the-workplace/)
- **Job advertisements citing fertility benefits have increased by 700% since March 2022**


**US Market Comparison:**


- **66% of US employers expect to offer fertility benefits, up from 55% in 2017**[raconteur](https://www.raconteur.net/healthcare/fertility-treatment-employee)
- **97% of US employers report that adding infertility coverage hasn't significantly increased medical plan costs**[fortune](https://fortune.com/2022/01/31/companies-fertility-benefits-great-resignation/)
- **75% of companies ranked by Great Place to Work include fertility benefits**[fortune](https://fortune.com/2022/01/31/companies-fertility-benefits-great-resignation/)
- **Average US fertility benefit coverage provides approximately $36,000 lifetime maximum**[fortune](https://fortune.com/2022/01/31/companies-fertility-benefits-great-resignation/)

### Best Practices for UK Media Companies


Leading UK media companies follow key implementation principles:


**Inclusive Design** : Programmes support all family-building paths including IVF, adoption, surrogacy, and LGBTQ+ specific needs, regardless of relationship status or gender identity.


**Comprehensive Coverage** : Benefits extend beyond basic treatment to include counselling, mental health support, diagnostic testing, and flexible leave policies.


**Partnership Excellence** : Companies work with specialised UK providers like Syrona Health to ensure expert guidance, cultural sensitivity, and regulatory compliance.


**Transparent Communication** : Policies are clearly communicated and easily accessible, reducing utilisation barriers and employee anxiety.


**Ongoing Support** : Programmes provide continuous support throughout the entire family-building journey, recognising the long-term nature of fertility challenges.


### The Future of UK Media Industry Fertility Benefits


With **fertility benefits now considered essential rather than optional** , UK media companies failing to offer competitive programmes risk losing talent to more progressive employers. The **700% increase in job advertisements citing fertility benefits since March 2022** demonstrates this shift from luxury to necessity.


Companies like DMGT, through their partnership with Syrona Health, are setting the standard for comprehensive, accessible fertility support that addresses both employee needs and business objectives. As the UK media landscape continues evolving, organisations investing in fertility benefits will build more engaged, diverse, and productive workforces driving long-term success in an increasingly competitive market.
