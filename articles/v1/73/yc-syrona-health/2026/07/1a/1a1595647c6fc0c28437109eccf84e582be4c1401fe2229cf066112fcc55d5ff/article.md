# Syrona Health | News | Syrona Health Launches Innovative Men's Health Proposition

\[London, 20th June 2023\] – Syrona Health, a digital healthcare company committed to empowering individuals through personalised gender-specific healthcare, is proud to announce the launch of its groundbreaking Men's Health offering. Spearheading this exciting initiative is Sonpreet Rai, the newly appointed Men's Health Clinical Lead at Syrona Health.


Syrona Health has gained recognition for its cutting-edge technology and patient-centric approach in addressing underserved healthcare challenges. With the introduction of its Men's Health offering, the company aims to revolutionise the way men approach and manage their health concerns, ensuring improved wellbeing and longevity.


As the Men's Lead, Sonpreet Rai brings a wealth of experience and expertise to Syrona Health as a highly qualified Consultant Urological and Robotic Surgeon with extensive experience in men's health, urology and prostate care.


Rai commented, “Syrona health has developed an amazing virtual healthcare platform in SORA to deliver personalised care plans through its symptom tracker and medically evidenced resources to women in the workforce. The opportunity to work with Syrona health as the Clinical Director for Men’s Health and to develop the Men’s health platform from the ground up is exciting as I share many of Syrona’s values in widening access to healthcare and empowering the individual. I hope through our partnership, we can get men talking and engaging in their health to improve both the short and long term outcomes that will ultimately lead not only to heathy individuals, but also healthier organisations.”


The Men's Health offering by Syrona Health will encompass a wide range of services and resources to support men's health and well-being. From comprehensive health assessments and personalised care plans to telemedicine consultations and health tracking tools, the platform aims to provide convenient and accessible healthcare options for men across all ages.


One of the key highlights of the offering is the incorporation of cutting-edge AI technology, enabling risk assessment and early detection of various men's health conditions. Syrona Health's advanced algorithms leverage large datasets to provide personalised insights, empowering men to take proactive measures to safeguard their health.


Furthermore, Syrona Health's Men's Health offering will feature educational resources and engaging content to raise awareness about common men's health concerns. Through informative articles, interactive webinars, men will have access to valuable information and an opportunity to connect with peers who share similar health experiences.


"We believe that men's health is a crucial area that often goes overlooked. Our aim is to break the barriers and enable men to take charge of their health journey," added Sonpreet Rai. "We are committed to providing a holistic approach to men's healthcare, ensuring comprehensive support at every step."


The launch of Syrona Health's Men's Health offering marks a significant milestone in the company's mission to empower individuals to take control of their health. By combining Sonpreet Rai's expertise with innovative technology, Syrona Health is poised to become a leader in men's healthcare, revolutionising the industry and improving the lives of men around the world.


For more information about Syrona Health and its Men's Health offering, please visit[www.syronahealth.com.](https://www.syronahealth.com./)
