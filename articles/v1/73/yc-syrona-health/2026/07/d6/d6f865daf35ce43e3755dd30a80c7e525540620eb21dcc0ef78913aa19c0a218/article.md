# Syrona Health | News | Syrona Health receives its ISO 27001 certification

### We're ISO 27001 Certified!


After months of hard work led by our Head of Engineering, Tom Hirst-Dunton, and supported by the whole Syrona Health team, we are excited to announce that we are officially ISO 27001 certified!


This is a huge milestone for Syrona Health as a rigorous seal of approval on our information security standards, as we continue to onboard some of the biggest enterprises across the globe.


### Why Is ISO 27001 Certification Important?


ISO 27001 certification is an international security standard, defining how to manage info security in the safest way possible. Recognised as the highest standard of information security, the certification guides businesses on the implementation, support, and measurement of all digital asset management. From scope and leadership, to planning risk management, and improvements, the certification encompasses all areas of information security.


Tom Hirst Dunton (Head of Engineering) - “Compliance with ISO 27001 is about retaining best-practice information security within Syrona Health. It demonstrates our adherence to the highest security standards in handling sensitive information.”


Chantelle Bell - Co-Founder & Director at Syrona Health states that: “ The Syrona Health team has worked extensively over the last few months to achieve ISO 27001. This certification demonstrates our commitment to both information security and quality management. It highlights our dedication to both patients and enterprises who can continue to trust the security controls that Syrona Health has in place.”


### Looking Towards the Future


The achievement of ISO 27001 certification is just the first step in ensuring that as our team grows all client and internal data is kept safe. We are now exploring ways to continually improve information security standards and manage our policies across the whole ecosystem.


We are also working on finding new tools and services which can support the growth of partners - while simultaneously mitigating and reducing risk of security breaches.


Our goal is to set an example that will empower the whole industry to raise the bar when it comes to security principles.
