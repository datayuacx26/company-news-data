# Syrona Health | Blog | 6 Construction Companies Leading the Way in Reproductive Health Benefits

Prioritising employee wellbeing and inclusivity is now a core differentiator for top employers especially in the construction industry, where workforce health impacts retention, productivity, and satisfaction. At Syrona Health, we’re proud to champion forward-thinking construction companies like[Structure Tone](https://stobuildinggroup.com/structuretone/) whose reproductive health benefits are unparalleled. Below are six construction leaders known for their innovative approach.


### **1. Structure Tone (STO Building Group): Pioneering Reproductive Health with Syrona Health**


[Structure Tone](https://stobuildinggroup.com/structuretone/) leads with inclusive health benefits, implementing a partnership with Syrona Health to support the unique needs of both site and office-based staff. This collaboration offers:


- **Virtual consultations** covering fertility, endometriosis, PCOS, menopause and men’s health
- **At-home diagnostic tools** and proactive symptom tracking for early intervention
- **Integrated mental health, menopause, and parental support**
- **Personalised care resources** for challenges faced by construction workers, such as irregular shifts or remote sites


**Why this matters:**
The Structure Tone x Syrona Health partnership brings gender-inclusive, tech-driven, confidential reproductive care directly into the UK construction workplace bridging gaps seen across NHS and private delivery. Employees benefit from faster diagnostic access and specialist consultations, boosting health outcomes and workforce engagement. Learn more[here](https://syronahealth.com/blog/6-uk-construction-companies-leading-the-way-in-rep/www.syronahealth.com)


### **2. Willmott Dixon**


[Willmott Dixon](http://www.willmottdixon.co.uk/news/willmott-dixon-listed-as-one-of-top-50-best-employers-for-gender-equality) is a leader in period dignity, ensuring all sites and offices have accessible, hygienic female facilities, free emergency period products, regular cleaning, and support for social enterprises like Hey Girls. Their sustained focus on gender equality has earned them recognition in the Times Top 50 Employers for Gender Equality.


### **3. Skanska UK**


[Skanska UK](https://www.skanska.co.uk/about-skanska/news-and-events/press-releases/267371/Skanska-UK-invests-in-its-people-with-significantly-enhanced-familyfriendly-benefits) has introduced significant family-friendly benefits: enhanced maternity and adoption leave (26 weeks’ fully paid), increased paternity leave (eight weeks), paid dependent leave, and dedicated support for adoptive and LGBTQ+ parents. They emphasise flexibility and continuous feedback-driven improvement in care policies.


### **4. Balfour Beatty (UK)**


[Balfour Beatty](https://www.balfourbeatty.com/media/iromny0d/balfour-beatty-benefits-leaflet.pdf) goes beyond standard NHS support by offering private medical cover, support for important life events, and a diverse, inclusive benefits framework. Resources and digital tools help staff including those on remote sites access fertility, mental health, and menopause support, reflecting their commitment to diversity, equity, and inclusion.


### **5. BAM Nuttall**


[BAM Nuttall](https://reba.global/content/case-study-how-construction-firm-bam-nuttall-is-addressing-women-s-health-issues/) runs women’s health weeks covering menopause, female cancers, and endometriosis, encouraging open, senior-led discussions and promoting inclusive HR policies. These initiatives directly address the needs of female staff, such as flexible leave for treatments and new menopause policies, helping build empathy and safety at work.


### **6. Gilbane Building Company**


[Gilbane UK](https://www.gilbaneco.com/careers/benefits/family-first/) puts reproductive and mental health at the core of its package offering IVF cycles, adoption and surrogacy assistance, gender-inclusive benefits, and 24/7 virtual support for team members on site or travelling.


### **Why UK Construction Must Prioritise Reproductive Health Benefits**


- **Recruitment & Retention:** With women making up only a small fraction of the UK construction workforce, inclusive health benefits are key to closing the gender gap and attracting diverse talent.
- **Equity in Access:** Tech-enabled solutions bridge long NHS waits, delivering rapid, confidential care—particularly valuable for shift workers and those at remote sites.
- **Boosted Productivity:** Supporting employees through fertility, menopause, and family-building reduces absenteeism and improves loyalty. UK research shows 60% of employees have considered switching jobs for better family benefits.
