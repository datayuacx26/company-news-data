The top fashion and apparel Shopify stores understand their audiences and deliver the products and a shopping experience they expect. From denim to vintage dresses, these Shopify stores are big on style.

### 1.[Hiut Denim Co.](https://hiutdenim.co.uk/)


[Hiut Denim](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CD-C8V8NNlo&feature=youtu.be) is based in Cardigan, Wales, a town that, until 2002, was home to the biggest jeans factory in the UK. Hiut Denim was founded in 2011 to take advantage of the jean-making skills and knowhow that remained with many locals, showcase a dedication to craft, and bring manufacturing back to Cardigan. Dedicated to making the best jeans, not the most jeans, this Shopify site and merchant focuses on details, satisfaction, and quality.


### 2.[Tentree](https://www.tentree.com/)


Inspired by time spent immersed in Hawaii, Dave Luba and Kalen Emsley founded[Tentree](https://www.tentree.com/) as a way to positively impact the environment through commerce. For every item sold, Tentree plants 10 trees, with a goal of planting over a billion trees by 2030. (As of 2025, Tentree has planted more than[110 million trees](https://www.tentree.com/pages/sustainability-nature) .)


### 3.[Maguire Shoes](https://maguireshoes.com/)


[Maguire Shoes](https://www.shopify.com/blog/maguire-working-with-factories) was founded by an industry pro who craved the opportunity to build a shoe brand on her own, inspired by her fashionable surroundings in Montreal, Canada. Myriam Belzile-Maguire started her business by building excellent relationships with factories. Her brand has found success with its unique designs and online store that captures the lifestyle of its ideal customer.


### 4.[The Outrage](https://www.the-outrage.com/)


[The Outrage](https://www.shopify.com/blog/womens-equality-day) was founded by accident when Rebecca Lee-Funk's political[t-shirt design](https://www.shopify.com/blog/start-online-tshirt-business) went viral. Now, the brand supports dozens of causes and raises awareness through its graphic tees and other[merch](https://www.shopify.com/blog/make-sell-merch) emblazoned with social impact slogans. The brand's homepage is consistently updated to reflect timely campaigns and causes.


### 5.[Adored Vintage](https://www.adoredvintage.com/)


Shopify store Adored Vintage[sells vintage clothing](https://www.shopify.com/blog/211022217-sell-vintage-clothing-online) and vintage-inspired modern pieces. The website is like a virtual escape to a romantic French countryside. Owner Rodellee Bas curates and creates pieces out of her affinity for silhouettes of times past. Adored Vintage donates a portion of its revenue to local classrooms, and Rodellee is a regular mentor to other women in business.


### 6.[Goodfair](https://goodfair.com/)


Topper Luciani founded Goodfair to give clothing headed to landfills a second chance. Unlike most vintage or secondhand clothing retailers, Goodfair doesn't list each item individually. Instead, customers buy mystery bundles. This process saves time and money for the company and the savings are passed on to the customers. Combating the culture of fast fashion, this low-carbon initiative is changing the way people are buying preloved fashion pieces.


### 7.[Kirrin Finch](https://kirrinfinch.com/)


[Kirrin Finch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yR6TDdWj3VE) is an apparel company offering gender-defying clothes inspired by menswear but designed to fit a range of female and non-binary bodies. Co-founders Kelly and Laura Moffat source eco-friendly fabrics and implement practices that reduce pollution while partnering with fair labor and ethical manufacturers to produce each garment.


### 8.[Rothy's](https://rothys.com/)


Rothy's transforms[eco-friendly materials](https://www.shopify.com/blog/sustainable-packaging-and-shipping-solutions) into stylish footwear and accessories through 3D knitting technology. The brand also strives for zero waste. Over 200 million plastic water bottles have been turned into bags or shoes by Rothy's, and its shoeboxes are strong enough to ship without additional packaging. They're also biodegradable, made with 85% post-consumer recycled materials.


### 9.[Beefcake Swimwear](https://www.beefcakeswimwear.com/)


[Mel Wells](https://www.shopify.com/blog/beefcake-swimwear-time) took more than two years to craft and launch Beefcake Swimwear, a line of sustainable and ethically made suits inspired by 1920s fashion. These thoughtfully produced, androgynous pieces are manufactured by people making a living wage in Portland, Oregon. Staying as transparent as possible, Mel has openly shared how the cost of making a piece for Beefcake Swimwear breaks down.


### 10.[Suta](https://suta.in/)


Founded by sisters Sujata and Taniya Biswas,[Suta](https://suta.in/) is a fashion house based in Mumbai, India, dedicated to employing weavers and artisans in disempowered communities and to using sustainable fabrics. Mixing unique handmade fabrics with classic saree silhouettes, dresses, and blouses, Suta's pieces represent a meeting of tradition, creativity, and design.


## Art and home décor Shopify stores


The best Shopify stores selling art and home décor are those with consistent design and branding across all customer touchpoints—from online store to social media. These Shopify websites are big on beautiful illustrations and crisp product photography.


### 11.[Uppercase magazine](https://uppercasemagazine.com/)


For more than a decade,[Uppercase magazine](https://uppercasemagazine.com/) has offered interesting articles and photos focused on craft, fashion, illustration, and design, all wrapped up in a beautifully printed package. Creator and editor Janine Vangool uses her platform to feature fellow creators. Since launching her Shopify store in 2009, she has expanded the publication to include *Little U* , a tiny magazine for the young and young at heart, and *The Uppercase Encyclopedia of Inspiration* , a series of books that deep dive on topics like ceramics,[cookbooks](https://www.shopify.com/blog/wil-yeung) , quilting, and printmaking.


### 12.[Artisaire](https://artisaire.com/)


[Artisaire](https://artisaire.com/) is the epitome of a business honing in and dedicating itself to a craft, in this case, the art of wax sealing. From customized wax stamps to premade wax seals, Artisaire has made an old tradition new again. Owning each step of the production process, Artisaire makes all of the items in its Canadian factory. The Thomsen family who run Artisaire still find joy in[selling their art online](https://www.shopify.com/blog/211990409-how-to-sell-art-online) , handwriting their own thank you notes and sealing each one by hand.


### 13.[Terre Bleu](https://www.terrebleu.ca/)


Located in Milton, Ontario,[Terre Bleu](https://www.terrebleu.ca/) 's owners, Ian and Isabelle Baird, left behind the busy city life for the country—to start a lavender farm. Terre Bleu brings a piece of Provence to Canada and[attracts visitors looking to soak in its atmosphere](https://www.shopify.com/blog/seasonality-agritourism-terre-bleu) . The Bairds' popular Shopify store offers lavender-infused beauty, food, and household products.


### 14.[Silk and Willow](https://www.silkandwillow.com/)


Silk and Willow is a sustainable wedding décor[boutique](https://www.shopify.com/blog/start-online-boutique) designed for the environmentally conscious. Founder Shellie Pomery began her career as a graphic designer before finding her calling creating fabrics with natural dyes. Silk and Willow carries plant-dyed silk ribbons, table linens, and handmade stationery, and is a place to get lost within each beautiful detail associated with planning a wedding.


### 15.[GOODEE](https://www.goodeeworld.com/)


GOODEE is a brand founded by twin brothers Byron and Dexter Peart. The fashion industry alums made their foray into home décor using their knowledge of supply chain and building a global brand. The business works with independent artists worldwide, with a focus on fair trade and ethical practices. The brand's website prominently features stories of the artisans, highlighting the uniqueness of each piece of[furniture they sell online](https://www.shopify.com/blog/sell-furniture-online) .
