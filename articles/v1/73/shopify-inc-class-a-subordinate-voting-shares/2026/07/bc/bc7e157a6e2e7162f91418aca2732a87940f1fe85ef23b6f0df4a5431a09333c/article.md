# CRO Checklist: 9 CRO Steps for Ecommerce Stores

A conversion rate optimization (CRO) checklist serves as a practical framework to improve the percentage of site visitors who complete a purchase on your online store.[CRO](https://www.shopify.com/blog/120261189-conversion-rate-optimization) shifts the focus from acquiring raw traffic to improving the[customer experience](https://www.shopify.com/blog/13251973-creative-ways-to-improve-ecommerce-customer-experience-that-also-boost-loyalty-and-sales) so more casual browsers become buyers.


The umbrella brand[Original Duckhead](https://originalduckhead.com/) achieved 100% year-over-year growth in 2024 by auditing its online shopping experience and optimizing their ecommerce website. On an[episode of Shopify Masters](https://www.shopify.com/blog/original-duckhead-retail-strategy-breaking-into-the-market) , founder Morgan Cros explains that optimization led to higher[conversion rates](https://www.shopify.com/blog/ecommerce-conversion-rate) , increased revenue, and a better return on marketing spend.


This CRO checklist outlines actionable steps to optimize your storefront, eliminate checkout friction, and boost conversions. Learn how leading brands use techniques such as strategic layout modifications and deep social proof to improve CRO.


## CRO checklist for ecommerce businesses


- Audit your conversion funnel
- Optimize product pages for clarity
- Strengthen calls to action
- Optimize for mobile shoppers
- Increase site speed and performance
- Build credibility with deep social proof
- Streamline the checkout flow
- Eliminate surprise fees
- Personalize the shopping experience


This CRO checklist provides a practical framework to evaluate your[user experience](https://www.shopify.com/blog/customer-experience-vs-user-experience) , fine-tune your store’s layout, and boost conversions at every stage of the[funnel](https://www.shopify.com/blog/ecommerce-funnel) :


### Audit your conversion funnel


Before adjusting individual elements on a specific landing page, understand how website visitors move through your online store. Auditing your conversion funnel allows you to track the complete[customer journey](https://www.shopify.com/blog/digital-customer-journey) from initial discovery to the final transaction, giving you a macro view of your storefront’s health.


To execute this step properly, use[Shopify Analytics](https://www.shopify.com/analytics) behavior reports and cross-reference them with your[Google Analytics](https://www.shopify.com/blog/14681601-google-analytics-for-ecommerce-a-beginners-guide) dashboard to visualize how site visitors transition through key pages. Identifying exactly where potential customers drop off allows you to focus your optimization efforts on the highest-impact improvements first.


For instance, if you notice low[bounce rates](https://www.shopify.com/blog/bounce-rate) on your[product pages](https://www.shopify.com/blog/product-page-increase-conversions) but a large drop-off during checkout, your energy should be spent fixing checkout friction rather than reshooting product photography. Review your “conversion rate over time” report consistently to measure whether your targeted CRO updates are successfully improving your baseline numbers.


### Optimize product pages for clarity


Effective product pages balance beautiful design with informative, readily accessible data. Focus on these elements:


-


**Visuals.** Feature high-quality visuals and images set against a clean, light background to showcase your items accurately.


-


**Descriptions.** Write thorough product descriptions that highlight the core[value proposition](https://www.shopify.com/blog/value-proposition) and explicitly detail the item’s[perceived value](https://www.shopify.com/blog/perceived-value) .


-


**Key information.** Make essential elements like pricing, shipping costs, return policies, and realistic delivery estimates easy to find.


On *[Shopify Masters](https://www.shopify.com/blog/diaspora-co-changing-the-spice-trade)* , Sana Javeri Kadri, founder of the spice company[Diaspora Co.](https://www.diasporaco.com/) , says she saw her storefront conversion rates increase immediately after auditing her product pages and moving critical information directly into the main image carousel. Because modern online shoppers browse in a highly visual, swipeable format popularized by platforms like Instagram, Diaspora Co. improved its product page usability by building a dynamic carousel of images.


Its checklist for a high-converting carousel includes using five to six dynamic images, explicit size guides, recipe inspiration, and verified customer reviews embedded directly into the image slides so users can see[social proof](https://www.shopify.com/blog/social-proof) without scrolling.


### Strengthen calls to action


A[call-to-action (CTA)](https://www.shopify.com/blog/what-is-a-call-to-action) must leave zero room for ambiguity. If your primary buttons, such as Add to Cart or Buy Now, blend into your background color or are buried beneath dense blocks of text, you will lose users who were otherwise ready to buy.


Ensure your primary CTAs feature strong visual emphasis and clear page layout positioning so they instantly command attention. To keep users engaged and prevent decision fatigue, keep each page focused on a single primary goal.


On[Shopify Masters](https://www.shopify.com/blog/otherhalf-studio-designing-successfully) , Jaz Fenton, cofounder of the design agency[Otherhalf Studio](https://otherhalf.studio/) , recommends treating your entire product page like a continuous landing page experience. If an online shopper scrolls past the initial checkout area, use that lower real estate to dive deeper into product benefits, raw ingredients, and brand ethos to keep selling to them. Test your button placement, phrasing, and sizing over time to uncover what drives high interaction rates.


### Optimize for mobile shoppers


Prioritize a[mobile-first design strategy](https://www.shopify.com/blog/cro-for-mobile) across your entire online store. Review your product pages, collection navigation, and checkout pages on multiple mobile screens to guarantee that all text is completely legible, images scale cleanly, and form fields are effortless to fill out.


Morgan Cros, founder of Original Duckhead, says on[Shopify Masters](https://www.shopify.com/blog/original-duckhead-retail-strategy-breaking-into-the-market) that designing their website layout for mobile screens first made a big difference in their conversion rate, recognizing that mobile is how people actually shop.


### Increase site speed and performance


A slow website will destroy your optimization efforts before a customer even reads your value proposition.[Speed optimization](http://shopify.com/enterprise/blog/site-performance-page-speed-ecommerce) can reduce bounce rates, significantly increase conversion, and improve your visibility on[organic search engines](https://www.shopify.com/blog/organic-search) .


Regularly monitor your website’s performance by tracking your[Core Web Vitals](https://www.shopify.com/blog/how-to-improve-core-web-vitals) through tools like[PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/) . Focus your attention on key metrics such as Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), Interaction to Next Paint (INP), and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) to guarantee that your layout remains stable and responsive.


Take full advantage of Shopify’s built-in performance optimizations, which include global CDN delivery and automated image optimization. You can protect your load speed further by regularly auditing your installed apps, removing legacy third-party scripts, compressing oversized media assets, and stripping away tracking pixels that bloat your store’s underlying code.


### Build credibility with deep social proof


To overcome purchase hesitation, integrate trust signals and authority markers throughout the entire customer journey. Display customer reviews, star ratings, video testimonials, and[user-generated content (UGC)](https://www.shopify.com/blog/user-generated-content) across your ecommerce store. Address common objections before shoppers reach your checkout pages by linking to an FAQ page and detailing your product warranties.


To maximize trust, elevate your[social proof](https://www.shopify.com/blog/social-proof) far beyond the traditional bottom footer. Jaz of Otherhalf Studio advises merchants to actively weave UGC and visual reviews into content blocks across the entire site instead of just dumping an Instagram grid at the bottom of the page.


### Streamline the checkout flow


The checkout flow is the most critical stage of the conversion funnel. According to[data compiled by Uptain](https://uptain.de/en/blog/e-report-cart-abandonment-first-semi-annual-report-2025/?handl_landing_page=https%3A%2F%2Fuptain.de%2Fen%2Fblog%2Fe-report-cart-abandonment-first-semi-annual-report-2025%2F&handl_url=https%3A%2F%2Fuptain.de%2Fen%2Fblog%2Fe-report-cart-abandonment-first-semi-annual-report-2025%2F&wuid=8242684.438608366) , the average ecommerce store loses roughly 70% of its shoppers right at the finish line. Every redundant form field, mandatory account creation screen, or confusing navigation step you include introduces a direct friction point that can stall a transaction.


Transitioning your store to an optimized, single-page checkout reduces the time and effort required to buy, driving down abandonment. Minimize required form fields, provide a guest checkout choice, and set up automated abandoned cart reminders via email and SMS to gently win back users.


You can accelerate this process further by offering[Shop Pay](https://www.shopify.com/blog/shop-pay-checkout) , which securely stores customer shipping and billing details for lightning-fast repeat purchases across millions of ecommerce sites. Ensure your checkout infrastructure supports other popular[payment methods](https://www.shopify.com/blog/payment-options) , including Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay, and standard credit cards. When an online shopper sees their preferred payment option available, they can complete the desired action safely with a single tap.


### Eliminate surprise fees


Surprise fees revealed late in the checkout flow cause immediate friction and contribute to[cart abandonment](https://www.shopify.com/blog/shopping-cart-abandonment) . Surface your shipping fees, estimated local taxes, and delivery timelines clearly before a shopper enters the checkout flow.


On *[Shopify Masters](https://www.shopify.com/blog/kloo-coffee-pre-launch-strategy-tips)* , Claudia Snow, cofounder and CEO of the craft coffee brand[Kloo](https://drinkkloo.com/) , says she discovered through a deep audit of the visitor data that potential customers consistently abandoned their checkouts when hit with unexpected shipping fees at the very end of the funnel. To eliminate this friction point, Kloo reworked its pricing structure.


The company lowered the individual bottle price from $35 to $32, completely eliminated the separate shipping fee, and raised its minimum order quantity to a two-bottle free shipping threshold. This adjustment removed the checkout surprise and increased the conversion rate while maintaining profitable unit economics.


### Personalize the shopping experience


[Personalization](https://www.shopify.com/blog/ecommerce-marketing-personalization) transforms a generic ecommerce website into a tailored storefront that adapts to individual user behavior. When you present relevant items to a customer based on their browsing history, you make it easier for them to discover value.


By using[cross-selling and upselling](https://www.shopify.com/blog/upselling-and-cross-selling) techniques to recommend related resources or complementary items, you keep users engaged and encourage them to purchase multiple products during a single session, raising your average order metrics across the board.


## CRO checklist FAQ


### How do you identify conversion bottlenecks?


To identify conversion bottlenecks, combine quantitative visitor data with qualitative user behavior insights. Audit your funnel metrics in Shopify Analytics to locate the specific pages experiencing the sharpest drop-offs in traffic. Once you have pinpointed the problematic page layout or section, deploy qualitative tools like user heat maps, search logs, and session recordings to observe exactly where website visitors click, stall, or experience frustration.


### What CRO tools can I use to streamline my conversion process?


You can use tools like Shopify Analytics and Google Analytics to monitor traffic behaviors, track all the metrics, and look at conversion rates over time. Google PageSpeed Insights is good for auditing load speed. Heatmapping tools like Hotjar or Lucky Orange provide visual representations of real user behavior.


### What is the fastest way to improve ecommerce conversion rates?


The fastest way to boost conversions is to eliminate hidden fees and deploy accelerated mobile payment options. Ensure that shipping costs are transparent or integrated directly into a free shipping threshold that is publicized clearly on your homepage. Activate single-tap payment methods like Shop Pay to let mobile shoppers complete their purchases instantly.
