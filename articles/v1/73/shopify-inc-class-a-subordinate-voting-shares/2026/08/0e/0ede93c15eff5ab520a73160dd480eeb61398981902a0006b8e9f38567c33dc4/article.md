# Personal Branding for Entrepreneurs: 5 Practical Tips

Personal branding for entrepreneurs applies the principles of professional brand-building to your personal identity as a business leader. It defines how you portray yourself to the world based on traits like your personality, purpose, expertise, and work style.


For entrepreneurs, authentic personal branding is an ingredient in[business growth](https://www.shopify.com/blog/business-growth) . Two out of three American consumers say they’re willing to spend more money on products from founders whose personal brand aligns with their values, and 82% agree companies are more influential when their leaders have a personal brand they know and follow, according to a[2026 Brand Builders Group survey](https://brandbuildersgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Trends-in-Personal-Branding-National-Research-Study-Brand-Builders-Group-Updated-10-2022.pdf?inf_contact_key=e6475727cc6136192d589b69a5351ec37e470d92b8b75168d98a0b8cac0e9c09) .


In this article, you’ll learn what personal branding is, why it’s a valuable endeavor for entrepreneurs, and strategies to build your own brand.


## What is personal branding for entrepreneurs?


[Personal branding](https://www.shopify.com/blog/personal-brand) is a strategic portrayal of yourself to the rest of the world: your expertise, skills, personal values, personality traits, experience, reputation, and more. For[entrepreneurs](https://www.shopify.com/blog/what-is-an-entrepreneur) , it connects the company you founded or the product you created to who you are and what you stand for. An entrepreneur’s personal brand should establish credibility as a business leader and expand influence.


An entrepreneur’s personal brand answers questions like:


-


What am I (or, what do I want to be) known for?


-


What are my values?


-


Why should people trust me and my work?


-


What makes my perspective different from everyone else’s?


-


What kind of opportunities and audiences do I want to attract?


Michelle Razavi, founder of protein snack brand[Elavi](https://elavi.co/) , highlights that a strong personal brand is useful for landing partnerships. When you pitch a major retailer like Whole Foods, the buyers will inevitably Google you and look at your social media platforms—not just your brand’s. Building a strong personal brand online ensures that when buyers “stalk” you, as Michelle says on *[Shopify Masters](https://www.shopify.com/blog/elavi-retail-pitch-sucess)* , they are impressed and want to work with you.


### Personal brand vs. business brand


Your personal brand is centered entirely on you as an individual: your personal story, core values,[professional reputation](https://www.shopify.com/blog/personal-reputation-management) , work experience, and expertise. It’s inextricable from your own name and face, and it will evolve over your career and life.


A business brand applies similar principles to a company. A company’s[brand identity](https://www.shopify.com/blog/brand-identity) is the set of traits that characterize a particular company. It’s centered on how the company speaks to customers, how it presents visually, its place within the market, and its[reputation in its industry](https://www.shopify.com/blog/brand-reputation) .


Personal and business brands can be intertwined, especially when the founder has a public persona.

## Tips for building a strong personal brand as an entrepreneur


- Be authentic
- Share vulnerably with intention
- Lean into your career expertise
- Build your visibility
- Let your personal brand stand alone


Here are approaches to building a personal brand as an entrepreneur.


### Be authentic


Entrepreneur[Alexa Curtis](https://www.shopify.com/blog/alexa-curtis-personal-branding-tips) , founder of Be Fearless, says the foundation of the best personal brands is a genuine understanding of self.


“The way I am in my day-to-day life as a business owner and entrepreneur is the same as how I am in my interpersonal relationships,” Alexa says. She suggests that the way you present yourself and interact with people through your personal brand should be “the same as how you’d be in a random conversation with a person in an airport bathroom.”


To Alexa, a strong personal brand blends the makeup of your core identity: your personality, your values, your “why,” and how you show up in the world. When your personal brand truly reflects who you are as a person, your consistency and authenticity shine through—and it draws people in.


### Share vulnerably with intention


Relational content—like storytelling, humor, and moments of vulnerability—connects with your followers on a deeper level as a human being. “I want you to enter my world, whether it’s business-related, just something funny, or dating life,” Alexa says. “The content I put out is so authentic to me and my message. It’s funny, unfiltered, and fearless.”


You don’t have to share more from your personal life than you’re comfortable with. “Sharing life along with the business stuff is really what shapes my brand; most people who are business owners would not talk about the amount of credit card debt they have, but I did,” Alexa says. For her target audience of aspiring entrepreneurs, being real about her own financial struggles is a way to connect with others navigating the challenges of establishing a new business.


### Lean into your career expertise


[Timm Chiusano](https://timm.work/) built his personal brand around raw, authentic career advice drawn from 25 years in corporate America at companies like ESPN, ABC Sports, and Charter Communications.


Rather than tie his personal brand to a specific company or physical product, Timm focuses on building trust based on his work experience, he says on an episode of the[Shopify Masters](https://www.shopify.com/blog/timm-chiusano-tik-tok-content-pillars) podcast. He’s built a huge personal brand with[1.1 million followers on TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@timmchiusano) , featuring videos like “[How to Chill the F Out at Work and Still Care](https://www.tiktok.com/@timmchiusano/video/7648803147846094093) ” and a[“Pep Talks”](https://www.tiktok.com/@timmchiusano/video/7639778498546093326?) series in which he offers career advice, résumé reviews, project help, and more.


“Corporate America can throw things at you, and not only have I seen it—I've seen it in different flavors and sizes, and I've probably seen it two or three times over at this point,” Timm says. He sees his content as a way to pay that experience forward to others, and “getting people to trust me, which makes the conversation resonate that much more.”


### Build your visibility


To make an impact, your personal brand has to reach others. Identify the online spaces where your[target market](https://www.shopify.com/blog/target-market) spends time: Are they TikTok fans? Do they engage with thought leadership on LinkedIn? Start with one or two platforms where you can begin to build an audience. You can branch out to new platforms and content types as you grow.


Sharing her journey on social media helped[Set Active founder](https://setactive.co/) Lindsey Carter build credibility and secure opportunities that provide even more visibility, like fellow founders inviting her to speak on podcasts. In turn, these podcast interviews lead to appearances like panel discussions, speaking engagements, and written interviews, she says on[Shopify Masters](https://www.shopify.com/blog/set-active-building-community) .


### Let your personal brand stand alone


Your business may be informed by your personality and values, but there’s a fundamental difference between a personal brand and a business brand, says coffee entrepreneur James Hoffman on an episode of[Shopify Masters](https://www.shopify.com/blog/james-hoffmann-niche) . He maintains a massive[YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@jameshoffmann) that he keeps separate from his multiple coffee-related businesses.


To James, this fundamental difference comes down to intent: A personal brand makes a promise of authentic interaction and education, while a brand channel exists to make sales and build long-term customer relationships. He’s careful not to muddy the two, he says.


He’s built a YouTube audience of more than 2.5 million as the platform’s go-to coffee expert, but he doesn’t promote his businesses via his personal brand. “I can’t look like I'm here to build a relationship with you that's just about education, but I’m actually quietly trying to just sell you a product,” James says. “I don't think that's being honest, and I don't think that helps you build a real, meaningful, lasting relationship with your audience that way.”


## Personal branding for entrepreneurs FAQ


### What are the basics of personal branding?


Personal branding is the process of defining, managing, and promoting your reputation. It includes your expertise, your perspective, the values you stand for, and the way you communicate those things through your content, visual identity, and interactions.


### What is an example of personal branding?


One example of personal branding is entrepreneur Alexa Curtis, founder of Be Fearless. She produces content that is authentic to her journey in business, with a tone that’s funny, unfiltered, and fearless.


### How do I start personal branding?


The foundation of your personal brand is a deep understanding of your core identity. Assess your personality, your values, your “why,” and how you show up in the world, and you’ll develop the basis of a personal brand that aligns with your true self.
