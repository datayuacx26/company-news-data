# 8 Infographic Ideas For Ecommerce Businesses

Sometimes, a creative infographic idea can explain your product, process, or motivation better than the crispest ad copy or the most heartfelt newsletter. On an episode of the[Shopify Masters podcast](https://www.shopify.com/blog/diaspora-co-changing-the-spice-trade) , Sana Javeri Kadri, founder of spice company Diaspora Co., explains the value of using visual tools to convey complex information. “Even before I had an operations manager, we worked with a freelance graphic designer and illustrator, because I knew that the easiest way to make things digestible is to draw them.”


Sana goes on to describe how her company uses infographics to illustrate the difference between the traditional supply chain versus diaspora’s simple farm to company to consumer model: “If you draw how a spice goes from a farm to a bunch of different importers and exporters to a manufacturer then to three different grocery stores and then finally to somebody seven years later, you understand inherently that that’s a complicated, dry supply chain that’s not giving you a good product, and is not paying the farmer well. And then if you can just illustrate that beautiful farm, spice company, consumer’s kitchen, you immediately see the difference.”


Because understanding complex ideas like supply chains can be challenging, presenting them visually through infographics can make abstract ideas more digestible. Read on to learn how to use infographics as part of your content marketing strategy, with examples from Shopify merchants to inspire you.


## What is an infographic?


Infographics are visual representations of information. Some use pie charts or bar graphs to illustrate statistical, categorical, or value-driven data. Others use flow charts and illustrations to guide viewers through different procedures and processes, or illustrate geographical networks. Infographics use visual elements to simplify complex ideas like industry trends or scientific findings, making information more accessible to a diverse range of people.


All of this makes infographics particularly useful for businesses as an engaging way to show customers why they should choose your brand—whether that’s your quality ingredients or your social impact. From illustrating your business’s sourcing process like Diaspora does to providing visual content that enriches your website, blog, newsletter, and social media pages, infographics are a great marketing and educational tool for ecommerce businesses.


## How to create a good infographic


A good infographic is visually interesting, easy to understand, and purpose-driven. These tips can help as you begin developing your own infographic ideas for presenting data:


-


**Understand your audience and purpose** **.** Before you begin, be clear on what you want to communicate and who your target audience is. An infographic explaining your supply chain to shipping partners will be different from one you share with customers.


-


**Establish a visual hierarchy.** Guide viewers through your information or data by providing a path for their eyes to follow. Use larger text and imagery for information that is more important, and align visual elements in a way that follows a logical progression. For example, most viewers will begin at the top of the page or graphic, scanning from left to right and top to bottom.


-


**Embrace negative space** **.** Intentional use of negative space keeps viewers focused and prevents distractions. Use blank space so you can highlight what’s most important and prevent visual overload.


-


**Incorporate your branding.** Use your brand’s fonts, icons, and color scheme in your infographics to maintain visual consistency across your website, emails, and social media.


-


**Be precise.** Instead of vague claims like “most customers love our powdered lemonade,” use precise data, such as: “87% of people say our mix tastes better than the leading brand (which has three times the sugar).” Using graphs and charts can help with visualizing statistics and drive your message home.


-


**Design for multiple platforms.** Develop infographics that can be shared on various platforms, including your website,[marketing emails](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/shopify-email) , blog posts, and social media. To do this, you may need to adjust image sizes or break large infographics down into a few separate images.

Infographics are not limited to pie charts and bar graphs. They can take myriad forms and sizes to convey different types and quantities of information. Here are some examples of infographics used by ecommerce businesses to highlight concepts critical to their brand:


### Diaspora


Source:[Diaspora Co.](https://www.diasporaco.com/pages/about)


In this infographic example,[Diaspora Co.](https://www.diasporaco.com/) contrasts its supply chain with the “traditional commodity model.” By introducing its sourcing and distribution model first, Diaspora communicates how it believes such systems should run. It then depicts the traditional model, which has many more steps, thus emphasizing the benefit of choosing their business.


In each section, the different steps are represented by animated icons that fit Diaspora’s aesthetic and are connected by purple lines that match its color scheme. With consistent branding, Diaspora shows viewers how its spices move from farmer to consumer in a quicker, more ethical way that makes for better trade practices and a better product.


### Masienda


Source:[Masienda](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0602/7483/5605/files/2024_SourcingReport_eng.pdf?v=1744237247) .


This geographical infographic example from[Mexican culinary retailer](https://masienda.com/)[Masienda’s](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0602/7483/5605/files/2024_SourcingReport_eng.pdf?v=1744237247) 2024 Sourcing Report shows the extent of the brand’s network. Each of its products is shown as an animated icon linked to the region from where it is sourced. Masienda is a B Corp, a certification that signifies it meets the highest standards of social and environmental performance, public transparency, and legal accountability. The text on the infographic explains how the brand supports responsible, long-term trade partners across Mexico, in keeping with its principles, while the map is an effective way to demonstrate how far its mission has spread.


### Bombas


Source:[Bombas](https://beebetter.bombas.com/impact) .

## Infographic ideas FAQ


### What is a good topic for an infographic?


Supply chains, buyer trends, customer satisfaction rates, price transparency breakdowns, and how-to guides are a few examples of topics that make for interesting infographics.


### What are the seven common types of infographics?


Seven common types of infographics are statistical, informational, timeline, process, geographic, comparison, and hierarchical. There are other use cases for infographics, but these seven are frequently used to visually simplify complex topics, provide viewers with step-by-step tutorials, and support claims or statements.


### What design elements should be on an infographic?


An infographic should include concise text that supports its intended message and some sort of visual depiction of the points being communicated. Infographics often contain numbers to illustrate impact and symbols such as arrows that direct viewers to understand the sequence or timeline of events.
