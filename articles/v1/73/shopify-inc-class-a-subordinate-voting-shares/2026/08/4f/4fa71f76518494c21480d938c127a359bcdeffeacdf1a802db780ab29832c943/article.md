## What is retail marketing software?


Retail marketing software is a collection of digital tools designed to help merchants acquire, engage, and retain customers by managing every aspect of the shopping journey, including personalized promotions and loyalty programs, automated outreach, and data collection. These software programs also help store owners and marketing professionals measure performance and track marketing ROI.


The[omnichannel retail](https://www.shopify.com/blog/omnichannel-retail) software market, valued at[$15.82 billion in 2026](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/5980360/omnichannel-retail-software-market-report?srsltid=AfmBOooetSqQnppRRMNSGRLCsCPF0bE6jim5dtL8R_ne6OytNodmwdTa) , provides the framework for these tools. Rather than operating as isolated apps, integrated platforms connect and synchronize your operations across every customer touchpoint, including physical stores, websites, social media, and marketplaces.


Integrated systems connect your front-end[point-of-sale (POS) systems](https://www.shopify.com/blog/pos-hardware) and ecommerce sites with operational tools, including inventory and order management, and[customer relationship management (CRM)](https://www.shopify.com/blog/crm) software.


## What are the types of retail marketing software?


There are several broad categories of retail marketing software:


### Customer segmentation and CRM software


A CRM stores what you know about each customer, including their orders, spend, location, contact details, and purchase history. Segmentation software slices those records into groups you can market to based on their habits and characteristics.


[Peppermint Products](https://peppermintproducts.com/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=case_study&utm_id=shopify) , a German design brand selling coat hangers and wardrobe hooks, discovered the limits of marketing without segmentation. They had around 50 products across multiple categories, and the team couldn’t tell which audiences to target.


So, using Shopify’s native[customer segmentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/customers/customer-segmentation) , they built a segment for their B2B hotel buyers. They also split customers inside and outside Germany and created a segment for bounced and unsubscribed addresses to keep the active list clean.


“I see a huge advantage in having all my customers together in one platform,”[says](https://www.shopify.com/blog/segmentation-peppermint-case-study) founder Roman Luyken.


Shopify’s customer segments are dynamic, rule-based lists built with Shopify’s query language,[ShopifyQL](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/report-types/shopifyql-editor) . They combine filters such as number of orders, amount spent, last order date, and location, and they update automatically as customers move in and out of the criteria.


You can also use[Sidekick](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin/productivity-tools/sidekick) , Shopify’s AI assistant,to[create customer segments](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin/productivity-tools/sidekick/generate-content#customer-segmentation:~:text=Flow%20with%20Sidekick-,Creating%20customer%20segments%20with%20Sidekick,segment%20for%20my%20Canadian%20customers%20who%20subscribe%20to%20my%20marketing%20emails.%22,-Creating%20discounts%20with) from a natural-language description without ShopifyQL.


### POS and in-store customer capture tools


[A point-of-sale system (POS)](https://www.shopify.com/blog/pos-hardware) records customer transactions. It also connects purchases to customers, so other tools in the stack can use its in-store sales data.


In[an interview with Practical Ecommerce](https://www.practicalecommerce.com/shopify-pos-exec-on-future-of-retail) *,* Shopify head of POS Ray Reddy says that “something as simple as buying a mattress in-store and having it shipped requires more than a basic payment app.”


The process begins at checkout. When staff[add customer information](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/customers) , such as an email or phone number, to a purchase,[Shopify POS](https://www.shopify.com/pos) syncs that data directly with the ecommerce back end. It also aggregates data from customers who shop both in person and online into a[unified customer profile](https://www.shopify.com/enterprise/blog/unified-customer-profiles) .


[New EY research](https://www.shopify.com/resource/ey-known-customer-report?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=retail-ey-growth-claims-campaign_Q225SYDXTA) shows just how much that capture is worth: Store owners using Shopify POS[convert 8 in 10](https://www.shopify.com/enterprise/blog/retail-customer-data) first-time store shoppers into known customers.


### Loyalty, referral, and retention software


The goal of loyalty and retention software is to transform one-time buyers into repeat customers. The software runs the points programs, tiers, and referral mechanics that reward customers for coming back and for bringing others with them.


According to EY’s[research](https://www.shopify.com/resource/ey-known-customer-report?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=retail-ey-growth-claims-campaign_Q225SYDXTA) , known customers spend up to three times as much per order as anonymous shoppers, account for up to 61% of repeat purchases, and drive 76% of in-store GMV growth.

Take Indian luggage brand[Mokobara](https://mokobara.com/) . They built a paid membership program on Shopify, using Shopify Flow to tag loyal customers and automatically trigger their perks. Those members now[generate three times the sales of non-members](https://www.shopify.com/case-studies/mokobara) , and the brand’s customer retention rate has risen 30% year over year.

### Paid acquisition, retail media, and audience software


Retailers use paid acquisition software to find and convert new customers through advertising. This includes building audiences, running campaigns across ad platforms and retail media networks, and measuring each channel’s returns.

Analytics and attribution software connects your business spend to performance outcomes by tracking your traffic, orders, and customer behavior. You can use it to assign credit to the specific channels and campaigns behind each sale.

## How to choose the best retail marketing software


When Gartner evaluated 19 major digital commerce platforms for its[2025 market report](https://www.shopify.com/enterprise/blog/shopify-gartner-magic-quadrant-2025) , Shopify ranked highest for “Ability to Execute.” But a generic ranking shouldn’t be the final word for your business. To choose the right retail marketing features, weigh analysts’ data against your day-to-day operations.


Finding a platform that matches your business complexity requires a close look at its native capabilities. While analysts grade everything from database infrastructure to basic checkout, your retail marketing checklist may look different.


You can use this four-step evaluation process to tailor industry reports to your business’s specific needs:


1. **Map the rankings to your unique business profile.** Generic charts reflect generic buyers. Evaluate how each software provider fits your specific industry vertical, your operational complexity, and the geographic regions where you sell.
2. **Weigh features by your immediate priorities.** Avoid paying for capabilities you don’t need. Global technology company[Manhattan Associates](https://www.manh.com/our-insights/resources/blog/the-magic-quadrant-works-harder-than-most-buyers-let-it#:~:text=Your%20job%3A%20connect%20that%20framework%20to%20your%20actual%20use%20cases.) recommends identifying two or three critical functions—such as how the software integrates with your existing tech stack or whether it provides customers with real-time shipment visibility—and giving those features the most weight in your scoring.
3. **Build vendor demos around software’s weaknesses.** Every software vendor profile in the Gartner report includes a list of product strengths and cautions. Typical vendor presentations focus on strengths, but Manhattan Associates advises using real scenarios from your business that play to a software product’s known weaknesses, so you can better understand risks or capability gaps.
4. **Match the provider’s future road map to your long-term business goals.** Software commitments can last for years. Ensure the platform you choose supports your long-term goals. Consider if the provider’s future tech investments—such as AI,[agentic commerce,](https://www.shopify.com/blog/agentic-commerce) and other automated shopping tools—align with your growth plans.


Connecting your systems to share data across channels automatically pays off. Manhattan Associates’ 2026 Global Unified Commerce Benchmark found that retailers who use unified commerce systems[convert shoppers at 1.5 times](https://www.manh.com/our-insights/resources/articles/what-is-unified-commerce#:~:text=Unified%20commerce%20leaders%20achieve%201.5%C3%97%20the%20conversion%20rate%20of%20basic%2Dmaturity%20peers.) the rate of those with basic tech stacks.

## Retail marketing software FAQ


### What software is used in retail?


A typical retail business runs several categories of software: ecommerce and point-of-sale platforms to process sales, inventory management systems to track stock across locations, customer relationship management tools to hold customer records, and marketing software for email, SMS, loyalty, and ads. Larger retail stores often add analytics and personalization layers on top.


The challenge for retail IT operations is integrating these systems so that data flows automatically between them.


### What is retail ERP software?


Retail enterprise resource planning (ERP) software is a back-office system that manages core operational processes, including finance, human resources, supply chain, procurement, and inventory, on a single platform.


### What is POS software for retail?


Retail POS software processes in-store transactions, including ringing up sales, taking payment, and applying discounts.


As a marketing tool, it also captures customer details at checkout and ties each purchase to a profile, which is how in-store activity feeds the rest of a retail marketing strategy.


### Which retail software is best?


There’s no single best tool, only the one that works best for your retail business. The criteria that tend to matter most are integration with your existing stack, how well the software helps you track customer data, and total cost of ownership. A useful first step is to define where your back office ends and your customer-facing layer begins, then weigh the options against your specific use cases.


### What is a CRM in retail?


A retail customer relationship management (CRM) system stores what a retailer knows about each customer—contact details, purchase history, customer preferences—and assigns it to segments so marketing efforts can target the right groups.


When used well, it supports customer satisfaction, increases customer lifetime value by making campaigns more relevant, and provides staff with the marketing materials and context to serve customers consistently across channels.
