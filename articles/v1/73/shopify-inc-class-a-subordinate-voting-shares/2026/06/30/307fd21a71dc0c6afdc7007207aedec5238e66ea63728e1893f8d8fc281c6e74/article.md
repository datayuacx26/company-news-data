# Two decades building commerce infrastructure: Jeanne DeWitt Grosser joins Shopify’s board

Today,[Jeanne DeWitt Grosser](https://www.shopify.com/investors/board-of-directors) joins Shopify’s board of directors.


Jeanne has spent two decades at the infrastructure layer of commerce. At Stripe, she built the commercial organization from the ground up and scaled revenue across every major function. Now, as COO of Vercel, she's helping build the AI-native foundation for the next generation of developers.


She also brings a clear view of what the AI era demands. Deep expertise matters more, not less. The people who have gone furthest in their craft are best positioned to put AI to work because they can encode real judgment into the systems they use.


That kind of thinking sharpens a company and makes Jeanne a great fit for Shopify.
