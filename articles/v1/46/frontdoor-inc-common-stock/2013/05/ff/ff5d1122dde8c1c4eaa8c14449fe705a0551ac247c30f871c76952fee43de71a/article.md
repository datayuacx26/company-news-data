# American Home Shield Names J.B Mathews Co. of Jacksonville, Fla. 2012 Top Garage Door Contractor

J.B Mathews Co. of Jacksonville, Fla. has been named the 2012 top-performing garage door contractor for American Home Shield, the nation’s leading provider of home protection plans. J.B Mathews Co., which has answered the garage door needs of residents and businesses in the Apopka, Orlando and Jacksonville area since 2003, earned a $20,000 bonus for its performance in American Home Shield’s 2012 Contractor Quality Bonus program.


With a national network of more than 10,000 independent contractors, the American Home Shield program rates and rewards contractors according to customer feedback.


﻿


“Our contractors understand the importance of delivering quality service to our customers every day, and we are proud to recognize J.B. Mathews Co. for providing a best-in-class experience to our customers,” said Mark Barry, president of American Home Shield. The company relies on its contractor network to diagnose and repair system and appliance problems for its more than 1.3 million customers across the nation.


“Our customers in the Jacksonville area consistently told us they would recommend J.B Mathews Co., which is an incredible testimony to the work and service they provide,” said Barry. “This bonus is our way of recognizing and rewarding the quality service J.B Mathews Co. provides our customers every day.”


According to owner John Mathews, the company earned such high regard by its desire to take care of the customer in a timely manner and at an affordable cost.


“Our goal is to serve every customer the way we want to be served in our own homes,” said Mathews. “Clear communication is very important in delivering quality customer service and building trust with customers. You have to tell them when you will be there, be on time, be upfront about the bill and the problem and let them know you are there to answer any questions they have after you leave their home.”


Mathews stated how proud he is of his team. He says the employees he has now are the best he’s ever had.


In all, six contractors from across the country each earned a $20,000 bonus from American Home Shield, one from each of the following trade categories: heating and air conditioning, electrical, plumbing, garage door, pool and spa and appliance. In addition, six contractors from the same trades earned a $15,000 bonus for having the second highest scores. And 164 contractors earned quality bonuses ranging from $1,500 - $15,000. A full list of award winners can be viewed online at


[AHSBigScore.com](http://www.ahsbigscore.com/) .


American Home Shield’s Contractor Quality Bonus program gives contractors opportunities to earn a share of the $1.2 million available in bonuses. Complete information about the American Home Shield Contractor Quality Bonus program, as well as details on how to join the company’s contractor network, are also available online at


[AHSBigScore.com](http://www.ahsbigscore.com/) .
