# American Home Shield Names Starr Electric of Greensboro 2012 Top Electrical Contractor

Starr Electric Company, Inc. of Greensboro, NC has been named the 2012 top-performing electrical contractor for American Home Shield, the nation’s leading provider of home protection plans. Starr Electric, which has answered the electrical needs of residents and businesses in the Greensboro area since 1928, earned a $20,000 bonus for its performance in American Home Shield’s 2012 Contractor Quality Bonus program.


With a national network of more than 10,000 independent contractors, the American Home Shield program rates and rewards contractors according to customer feedback.


“Our contractors understand the importance of delivering quality service to our customers every day, and we are proud to recognize Starr Electric for providing a best-in-class experience to our customers,” said Mark Barry, president of American Home Shield. The company relies on its contractor network to diagnose and repair system and appliance problems for its more than 1.3 million customers across the nation.


“Our customers in the Greensboro area consistently told us they would recommend Starr Electric, which is an incredible testimony to the work and service they provide,” said Barry. “This bonus is our way of recognizing and rewarding the quality service Starr Electric provides our customers every day.”


According to owner and president John Starr, the company earned such high regards by staying true to its core business value of providing exceptional electrical work and customer service.


“We believe in providing the best electrical work and the best customer service to every customer the first time,” said Starr. “We go above and beyond in every job so our customers understand and trust the process. Customer service is about treating people right and respecting the customers’ home, and that is something we take to every job. To be recognized as the best in the nation, based on responses from customers we serve, lets us know we are providing quality service and a positive experience for our customers.”


In all, six contractors from across the country each earned a $20,000 bonus from American Home Shield, one from each of the following trade categories: heating and air conditioning, electrical, plumbing, garage door, pool and spa and appliance. In addition, six contractors from the same trades earned a $15,000 bonus for having the second highest scores. And 164 contractors earned quality bonuses ranging from $1,500 - $15,000. A full list of award winners can be viewed online at AHSBigScore.com.

About American Home Shield


American Home Shield founded the


[home warranty](https://www.ahs.com/)


industry in 1971 and remains the industry leader, servicing more than 1.3 million customers across 49 states. In addition to its core focus on home protection plans, the company also issues and administers


[preventive maintenance contracts](https://www.ahs.com/)


on major home systems and appliances in 48 states across the country. The Memphis-based company operates three customer service centers, employs approximately 1,600 employees and has a national contractor network made up of more than 10,000 independent home-service contractors, which includes more than 40,000 technicians.
