# American Home Shield Names Kane Heating & Air Conditioning of Killeen, Texas 2012 Top HVAC Contractor

Kane Heating & Air Conditioning of Killeen, Texas has been named the 2012 top-performing heating, ventilation and air conditioning contractor for American Home Shield, the nation’s leading provider of home protection plans. Kane Heating & Air Conditioning, which has answered the HVAC needs of residents and businesses in Killeen and Bell County since 2005, earned a $20,000 bonus for its performance in American Home Shield’s 2012 Contractor Quality Bonus program.


﻿


With a national network of more than 10,000 independent contractors, the American Home Shield program rates and rewards contractors according to customer feedback.


“Our contractors understand the importance of delivering quality service to our customers every day, and we are proud to recognize Kane Heating & Air Conditioning for providing a best-in-class experience to our customers,” said Mark Barry, president of American Home Shield. The company relies on its contractor network to diagnose and repair system and appliance problems for its more than 1.3 million customers across the nation.


“Our customers in the Killeen area consistently told us they would recommend Kane Heating & Air Conditioning, which is an incredible testimony to the work and service they provide,” said Barry. “This bonus is our way of recognizing and rewarding the quality service Kane Heating & Air Conditioning provides our customers every day.”


According to owner and president Pat Kane, the company earned such high regard by doing honest work the first time and treating their customers with respect.


“Our focus is delivering quality service on the first visit,” said Kane. ”It’s important for the customer to trust you and the work you do. You have to have the technical expertise but also be able to explain every step of the job to the customer and make them feel at ease. We are thrilled to be recognized as the best and I owe it to my team and their hard work.”


In all, six contractors from across the country each earned a $20,000 bonus from American Home Shield, one from each of the following trade categories: heating and air conditioning, electrical, plumbing, garage door, pool and spa and appliance. In addition, six contractors from the same trades earned a $15,000 bonus for having the second highest scores. And 164 contractors earned quality bonuses ranging from $1,500 - $15,000. A full list of award winners can be viewed online at


[AHSBigScore.com](http://www.ahsbigscore.com/) .


American Home Shield’s Contractor Quality Bonus program gives contractors opportunities to earn a share of the $1.2 million available in bonuses. Complete information about the American Home Shield Contractor Quality Bonus program, as well as details on how to join the company’s contractor network, are also available online at


[AHSBigScore.com](http://www.ahsbigscore.com/) .
