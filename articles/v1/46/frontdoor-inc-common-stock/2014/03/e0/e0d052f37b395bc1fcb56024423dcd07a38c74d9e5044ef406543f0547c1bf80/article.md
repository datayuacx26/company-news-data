# American Home Shield Launches a Second $15000 Sweepstakes and Chance to Win Appliances

Homeowners looking to upgrade their homes can now enter to win new appliances thanks to a $15,000 sweepstakes offered by American Home Shield, the nation’s largest provider of home warranties. The sweepstakes is the second of its kind to be launched in just six months.


“We see that our customers and consumers commonly put off appliance upgrades,” said Philipp von Holtzendorff-Fehling, chief marketing officer for American Home Shield. “This sweepstakes was started as a fun way to engage homeowners and give them a chance to make some of these home improvements with trusted brands like American Home Shield and GE Appliances. The first sweepstakes was so successful that we decided to conduct another one and give homeowners another chance to win.”


As part of the company’s You Pick Two Giveaway, now through June 2, 2014, one lucky winner will be chosen each month to receive their choice of two GE appliances (up to $5,000 total manufacturer’s suggested retail price) plus $200 toward installation. Random drawings will take place on March 31, April 28 and June 2.

About American Home Shield


American Home Shield founded the home warranty industry in 1971 and remains the industry leader, servicing more than 1.3 million customers across 49 states and the District of Columbia. The Memphis-based company operates three customer service centers, employs approximately 1,800 employees and has a national contractor network made up of over 10,000 independent home-service contractors and more than 45,000 service technicians. American Home Shield is a business unit of


[The ServiceMaster Company, LLC](https://www.servicemaster.com/) , one of the world's largest residential and commercial service networks. The company’s brands include Terminix, American Home Shield, ServiceMaster Clean, Merry Maids, Furniture Medic and AmeriSpec.


About ServiceMaster
