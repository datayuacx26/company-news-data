# American Home Shield Awards Habitat for Humanity-Greater Columbus (Ohio) $25,000 in its “Challenge for Change” Campaign

Affiliates in Tacoma/Pierce County (Wash.) and Maumee Valley (Ohio) also win big for communities


After an intense four-month effort that featured multiple lead changes and last minute resurgences, Habitat for Humanity-Greater Columbus came out on top in the American Home Shield Challenge for Change online voting campaign, receiving more than 72,000 votes and earning $25,000 for its Neighborhood Revitalization Initiative.


“We are humbled by and most appreciative of this amazing show of dedicated support from online voters in our central Ohio area and beyond,” said Habitat for Humanity-Greater Columbus CEO E.J. Thomas. “These votes helped rally the community, and now we have $25,000 to build and restore our neighborhoods. We can’t thank them enough.”


Finishing just 5,458 votes behind, Habitat for Humanity-Tacoma/Pierce County, went into the final week of the contest in a virtual dead heat for the lead, and will receive a $15,000 award from American Home Shield. Maumee Valley Habitat for Humanity finished third and will receive a $10,000 award.


“This $15,000 investment will improve the Lakewood neighborhood of Tillicum, and we are extremely grateful to our dedicated supporters and to American Home Shield,” said Maureen Fife, Habitat for Humanity-Tacoma/Pierce County’s Chief Executive Officer. “We are overwhelmed by the outpouring of support.”


The American Home Shield Challenge for Change, which began in late October 2010 and ended January 31, 2011, featured 15 select Habitat affiliates from across the nation rallying supporters to help win one of three prizes totaling $50,000 by voting daily at


[AHSChallenge.com](https://www.ahschallenge.com/) .


“We believe strongly that home ownership and proper home maintenance go hand-in-hand with building strong neighborhoods,” said Dave Crawford, President and CEO of American Home Shield, one of the nation’s leading home warranty companies.” Habitat for Humanity shares this commitment, and American Home Shield is pleased not only to have helped raise awareness about the great work that they’re doing across the nation, but to now also invest in the work of these three affiliates.”


Each award will benefit the affiliate’s ongoing Neighborhood Revitalization Initiative, an effort to improve local housing conditions by partnering with other community organizations to provide services such as home repair and weatherization that enhance the quality of life in struggling neighborhoods.
