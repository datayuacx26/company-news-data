# Clock Ticking on Chance to Enter Go Green Win Green Sweepstakes Sponsored by American Home Shield

Friday's deadline is fast approaching to enter to win a $15,000 Sears gift card that can be used to make your home more energy efficient. The grand prize is part of the Go Green Win Green Sweepstakes sponsored by American Home Shield, the nation's leading home warranty company.


The sweepstakes is just one component in a larger initiative led by the company, known as Live Green Save Green. LiveGreenSaveGreen.com is an online social community for homeowners and enthusiasts, filled with tips and strategies on reducing home energy consumption and living a more eco-friendly lifestyle.


"Preparing your home to be more energy efficient is an investment with short- and long-term results for all homeowners," says American Home Shield President Dave Crawford. "As the nation's largest home warranty company, we want to help homeowners understand the impact that responsible home energy conservation can have on the environment and their wallet. Live Green Save Green is a great resource for those looking for information, or who have tips to share."


Sweepstake entries can be submitted through 11:59 p.m. (CDT) on September 2, 2011 in one of three ways: visiting GoGreenWinGreen.com, clicking on the sweepstakes banner at LiveGreenSaveGreen.com or selecting the "Go Green" tab on the Live Green Save Green Facebook page.


For more information on Live Green Save Green, including sweepstakes rules, visit LiveGreenSaveGreen.com, become a fan of the Live Green Save Green Facebook page facebook.com/livegreensavegreen and follow the effort on Twitter twitter.com/LvGreenSvGreen.


About American Home Shield


American Home Shield helped found the home warranty industry in 1971 and remains an industry leader, servicing 1.4 million customers across 49 states. The Memphis-based company operates three customer service centers, employs over 1,700 employees and has a national contractor network made up of over 11,000 approved, independently insured home-service contractors. American Home Shield is part of the ServiceMaster family of brands, one of the world's largest and most versatile service networks. The company's brands include TruGreen, Terminix, American Home Shield, ServiceMaster Clean, Merry Maids, Furniture Medic and AmeriSpec.
