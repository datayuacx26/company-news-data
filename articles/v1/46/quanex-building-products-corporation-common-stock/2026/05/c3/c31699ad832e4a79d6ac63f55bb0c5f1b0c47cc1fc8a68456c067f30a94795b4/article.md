# Viwinco Customer Testimonial: Scaling For Growth, Built on Partnership

## Quanex and Viwinco Windows


In this Viwinco customer testimonial, we look at how a decades-long relationship evolved to solve a modern production challenge. For more than 40 years,[Viwinco Windows](https://www.viwinco.com/) has built its reputation on delivering high-quality, custom windows and sliding patio doors for the residential market. From new construction to replacement and impact-resistant coastal applications, their products are designed to meet the evolving needs of homeowners and distributors alike.


As a family-owned and operated company, Viwinco combines long-term vision with the agility to adapt. This approach has fueled significant growth in recent years for Viwinco.


### Driving Growth Through Continuous Improvement


Viwinco’s success is rooted in a commitment to getting better every day. By focusing on continuous improvement across every level of the organization, the company has been able to scale rapidly while maintaining the quality and service its customers expect.


“In 2018, we were about a $50 million company, and last year, we were very proud to reach $100 million,” said Mike Duncan, General Manager – Viwinco. “Everybody, every day, is trying to figure out how to get a little bit better.”


That mindset has enabled Viwinco to navigate major industry shifts, invest in new capabilities and continue delivering products that are both energy efficient and visually appealing, all while remaining easy to do business with.


### Expanding Capacity to Support Demand


As demand increased, Viwinco identified a critical constraint within its operations: glass production.


“The glass shop was always a bottleneck here at Viwinco as we grew,” explained Skyler Barnes, Director of R&D, Viwinco.


To support continued growth, the company made the strategic decision to invest in a new glass facility, expanding capacity while improving efficiency and control over insulated glass unit production.


Launching that capability required more than just equipment. It required the right partner.


### A Collaborative Approach to Implementation


Quanex played a key role in helping bring Viwinco’s new glass shop online, providing both production expertise and hands-on technical support throughout the process.


“Being able to work side by side with Quanex to help launch our glass shop was really a benefit,” Barnes explained. “They are the experts in their product. They were able to provide support that not every other supplier would be willing to offer.”


From helping define product requirements to assisting with machinery setup and ensuring optimal performance, Quanex supported Viwinco at every stage of implementation.


That collaboration extended beyond installation, creating a foundation for ongoing process improvements and future innovation.


## Product Performance: Viwinco Customer Testimonial Insights


### Performance That Extends Beyond the Factory


Today, Viwinco utilizes[Quanex Duralite®](https://www.quanex.com/products/single-seal-duralite-warm-edge-spacers/) spacers in its insulating glass units, supporting both product performance and long-term reliability.


“We can only provide a certain level of quality if we have the same type of quality products and components going into the window,” Duncan noted.


The result is improved thermal efficiency, reduced warranty concerns and greater confidence in the final product delivered to customers.


“As we look at developing new products and making our windows more energy efficient, Quanex is going to play a key role in that,” Barnes added.


### Building Toward Something Bigger


For Viwinco, manufacturing goes beyond producing windows, it’s about creating products that enhance the spaces where people live.


“A lot of people think we’re just making white rectangles,” Duncan said. “But what we’re providing is something that is sustainable, beautiful, and thermally efficient for people in their homes.”


That shared perspective is what makes the partnership with Quanex so impactful. Together, the two companies are not only improving manufacturing processes—we’re contributing to better-performing homes and stronger communities.
