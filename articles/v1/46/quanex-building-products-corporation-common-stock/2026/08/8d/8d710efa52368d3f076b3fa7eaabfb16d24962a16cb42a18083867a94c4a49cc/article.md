# MITER Brands Customer Testimonial: One Partner. Multiple Solutions

## Quanex and MITER Brands


MITER Brands is one of the largest privately held window and door manufacturers in the United States, serving customers nationwide with a focus on quality, reliability, and long-term relationships. Operating at that scale requires more than strong internal operations, it depends on trusted partners who can support multiple aspects of the business with consistency and expertise.


For MITER Brands, Quanex plays that role as a multi-service supplier, delivering value across several critical product platforms.


### **Supporting Multiple Product Platforms**


Unlike traditional suppliers that focus on a single component, Quanex supports MITER Brands across a broad range of solutions, from insulating glass spacers to vinyl extrusions and window and doors screens.


That breadth allows for greater alignment, simplified sourcing and more consistent performance across operations.


“We do a lot of Duralite® spacer, work with Mikron® (brand) extrusions, and source screens as well,” Mike Ohline (retired), Senior Vice President, Supply Chain, MITER Brands, shared. “The partnership goes very deep, it works both ways, and that’s important in any partnership.”


From Duralite® spacers used in insulating glass units to Mikron® vinyl extrusions that help form window frames, Quanex products are integrated throughout MITER Brands’ manufacturing process.


### **Consistency Across the Operation**


At a national scale, consistency is critical. Every component must meet performance expectations while supporting efficient production across multiple facilities.


By working with a single partner across multiple product categories, MITER Brands is able to streamline operations while maintaining confidence in the materials and systems they rely on every day.


“We rely on these products day in and day out, and we know they’re supported by Quanex,” Ohlin noted. “Having that kind of relationship and longevity definitely builds trust.”


That trust is built not only on product quality, but also on Quanex’s ability to deliver reliably and support MITER Brands through changing market conditions.


### **Collaboration Beyond Components**


While product breadth is a key advantage, the partnership extends well beyond supply.


MITER Brands and Quanex work closely together to address challenges, refine product performance and explore new opportunities for innovation.


“Sometimes Quanex brings innovative ideas to us that spark creative thinking,” Ohlin explained. “Other times, we bring challenges to them, and we work on solutions together.”


This collaborative approach allows both companies to stay aligned, adapt quickly and continue improving over time.


### **Built on Partnership, Designed for Scale**


MITER Brands has built its business on strong relationships. Relationships with customers, team members, and suppliers alike. That same philosophy defines its partnership with Quanex.


“Supplier partnerships are critical to our success,” Ohlin shared. “We count on our suppliers to bring us innovative products, to deliver when we need it and to help us navigate challenging times.”


By offering a wide range of solutions backed by technical expertise and reliable support, Quanex helps MITER Brands maintain performance across its operations while positioning the business for continued growth.


At its core, the partnership reflects a shared understanding that success at scale requires more than individual components, it requires alignment, trust and the ability to deliver across the full system.


> “We have a saying at MITER Brands that all business is personal—and Quanex understands that”
