# Seamless Transition with a Strong Partnership

## Quanex and Bellmont Cabinet Company


At Bellmont Cabinet Company, craftsmanship, innovation, and family values drive everything they do. Based in Sumner, Washington, Bellmont manufactures custom and semi-custom frameless cabinetry, delivering a modern, European-inspired aesthetic to kitchens across the West Coast, Southwest, and beyond.


That commitment to quality extends beyond their own operations, it also shapes the partners they choose to work with.


### Navigating Change Without Disruption


When Bellmont faced the unexpected closure of a longtime supplier, the team was challenged with maintaining product consistency while working against a tight timeline. It was a critical moment that required both speed and precision.


Quanex stepped in to support the transition—matching existing products while helping Bellmont continue to move forward with new designs in development.


“I can honestly say that it has far exceeded our expectations,” said Tyler Bell, CEO, Sales & Marketing for Bellmont. “As far as our customers are concerned, it’s like they never knew we even made this big of a switch. And at the end of the day, that’s really the best thing I can say about Quanex.”


The ability to make a major operational change without impacting the customer experience speaks to the strength of the partnership and the reliability behind it.


###


### Supporting Innovation in Every Detail


Bellmont has built its reputation on forward-thinking design, specializing exclusively in frameless cabinetry and offering a distinct, modern edge in the marketplace. That approach requires partners who can keep up with evolving styles, materials, and expectations.


Quanex plays a key role in helping bring those ideas to life.


“We’ll come up with a new idea and they really help us achieve what we’re looking for,” shared Casey Bell, CEO – Operations for Bellmont. “In pretty much all cases, we’ve gotten what we’ve been looking for.”


From paint-grade and MDF routed doors to real wood designs, Quanex supplies the cabinet doors that serve as the focal point of Bellmont’s kitchens, where quality, consistency, and craftsmanship are on full display.


### A Shared Standard of Excellence


In cabinetry, every detail matters. The finished product isn’t just functional, it represents a significant investment and a personal vision for the end customer.


> “In our business, 99% is an F, it has to be 100%,” Bell explained. “We’re fulfilling somebody’s lifelong dream with these things.”


That shared commitment to getting every detail right is what defines the relationship between Bellmont and Quanex. Beyond products, it’s about trust, responsiveness and the ability to deliver, no matter the circumstances.


“I think Quanex is absolutely part of something bigger for Bellmont,” they added, “because you can’t have beautiful cabinets without beautiful doors.”
