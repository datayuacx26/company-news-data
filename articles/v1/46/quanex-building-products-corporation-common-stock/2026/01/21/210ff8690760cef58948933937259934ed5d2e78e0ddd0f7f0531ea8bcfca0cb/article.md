# Quanex Appoints New Vice President of Sales for North America

## Quanex Appoints New Vice President of Sales for North America


AKRON, JAN 7, 2026 (NYSE: NX) — Quanex is pleased to announce the appointment of Jack Meluskey to Vice President of Sales, North American Hardware & Extrusions.


Jack brings more than 30 years of experience across sales, operations and manufacturing with a proven ability to align customer needs with business growth. Since joining Quanex in 2017, he has held several leadership roles of increasing responsibility, most recently as Director of Sales, Eastern North America where he consistently delivered results and strengthened key customer partnerships.


In his new role, Jack will lead the North American sales organization for Quanex’s[Hardware](https://www.quanex.com/product/hardware/) and[Extruded Solutions](https://www.quanex.com/about-us/extruded-solutions/) business segments. He will be responsible for accelerating growth initiatives and further strengthening Quanex’s customer-focused approach across North America.


“Jack consistently demonstrates strong leadership and a deep commitment to delivering value-driven solutions for our customer,” said Bob Daniels, President, Hardware Solutions, Quanex. “His industry experience, strategic mindset, and ability to develop high-performing teams make him well positioned to lead our sales organization into its next phase of growth.


## About Quanex


Quanex is a global manufacturer with core capabilities and broad applications across various end markets. The Company currently collaborates and partners with leading OEMs to provide innovative solutions in the window, door, solar, refrigeration, custom mixing, building access and cabinetry markets. Looking ahead, Quanex plans to leverage its material science expertise and process engineering to expand into adjacent markets.
