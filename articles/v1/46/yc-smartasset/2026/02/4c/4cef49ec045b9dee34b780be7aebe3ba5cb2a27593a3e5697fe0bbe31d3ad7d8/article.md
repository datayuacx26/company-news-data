## 2. Blogging or Content Marketing


An[advisor website](https://smartasset.com/advisor-resources/financial-advisor-websites) is a static site that includes information about your advisory business. You may choose to enhance your site by adding informational content in a blog format or as a resource library.


For example, you might write posts that answer specific questions your ideal clients are searching for online. SEO is central to a blogging or[content marketing strategy](https://smartasset.com/data-studies/financial-advisor-marketing-strategies-2022) , as the content you create should be designed to rank in search.


## 3. Email Marketing


Email is a powerful digital marketing tool. You can use it to reach large numbers of people with a single message. Building an email list allows you to collect contact information from current and prospective clients. You can then market your business by sending our regular newsletters or updates and encouraging recipients to get in touch if they have questions or need help.


You email marketing strategy may comprise a combination of the following:


- **Newsletters** : Send out regular newsletters with updates, market insights and useful financial tips.
- **Personalized emails** : Personalize your emails based on the recipient’s interests and needs. This can significantly increase engagement rates.
- **Automated email campaigns** : Use automated email campaigns to nurture leads through the sales funnel. For example, send a series of emails that provide valuable information and gradually introduce your services.

## 5. Social Media


[Social media](https://smartasset.com/advisor-resources/social-media-content-for-financial-advisors) can be another effective way to boost your visibility, but it’s important to select the right channels to focus your attention on. The Broadridge survey found that four in 10 advisors had a social media lead convert into a client. Advisors also reported LinkedIn and Facebook as having the highest conversion rates.


Then again, if you’re interested in offering[advisory services](https://smartasset.com/financial-advisor/financial-advisory-services) to people between the ages of 25 and 40, you might concentrate your marketing on channels like Instagram and X (Twitter).


Studying demographic trends for different social media channels can help you to identify which ones are likely to yield the best ROI from digital marketing.


## 6. Video Marketing


Video is increasingly overtaking written content in the online space, at least from a marketing perspective. According to a HubSpot study, two-thirds of consumers say they’ve viewed a video to learn more about a brand’s products or services.2


If you’re not tapping into the possibilities of video as a digital marketing tool yet, it might be worth considering how you could use it to make an impact.[Starting a YouTube channel or using TikTok](https://smartasset.com/advisor-resources/advisors-video-tiktok-genz-millennials) , for example, could be worthwhile if it allows you to connect with the type of clients you’re hoping to work with.


## 7. Pay-Per-Click (PPC) Advertising


PPC advertising can quickly increase your online visibility and attract potential clients. Google Ads and social media platforms like[LinkedIn](https://smartasset.com/advisor-resources/linkedin-marketing-for-financial-advisors) and Facebook offer robust PPC advertising options.


When relying on PPC advertising, it’s important to use precise targeting to reach your ideal audience based on demographics, interests and behaviors. The success of a PPC campaign also hinges on your ability to write compelling ad copy that highlights the benefits of your services and includes a clear call to action. You’ll also want to ensure that your landing pages are optimized for conversions, with clear information and an easy-to-complete contact form.


## Applying Digital Marketing Strategies in Your Advisory Business


Before diving in with a digital marketing plan, it’s important to first identify your goals and objectives. For example, are you interested in using digital marketing to:


- Refine your brand and increase your visibility in the advisory space.
- Expand your[client base](https://smartasset.com/advisor-resources/financial-advisors-defined-contribution-clients) (or narrow it if you’re moving in a more niche direction with your business).
- Boost conversions or increase engagement.


Understanding that is important before you decide which digital marketing strategies to pursue. The answer can depend on how well you understand your client base. And it can depend on the needs of the clients you’d like to attract. For example, how old is your typical client? What is their net worth? Where are they income-wise and what are their goals or needs? Perhaps most importantly, where do they spend their time online?


Digging into those types of questions can give you clarity on where you should be focusing your[digital marketing](https://smartasset.com/advisor-resources/financial-advisor-marketing-tips) efforts, at least initially. It’s important to remember that nailing down the perfect approach can take time. You might want to try just one or two strategies for a set time period. Then, review the results to see if it’s worth continuing or whether you should shift your attention elsewhere.


## Benefits of Digital Marketing for Financial Advisors


Taking your marketing efforts into the digital realm can offer some distinct advantages. As mentioned, it can be less time-consuming than[traditional marketing](https://smartasset.com/advisor-resources/financial-advisor-marketing-tips) . But there are other benefits, as well.


For example, digital marketing can help you to:


- Gather information about prospective clients to glean insight into what it is they need and want in an advisor.
- Interact with prospects and[current clients](https://smartasset.com/advisor-resources/why-clients-leave-financial-advisors-and-how-to-avoid-it) in an environment that they’re comfortable with.
- Increase brand awareness, while also increasing conversions and building loyalty.
- Showcase what it is that makes you and your business unique.


Digital marketing also affords an opportunity to create a lasting impression and foster trust, without prospects feeling pressured to commit. By experimenting with different digital marketing strategies, you can pinpoint what’s likely to get the strongest response.


## Digital Marketing vs. Traditional Marketing


Digital marketing is a relatively new development. But it’s quickly proven its worth as an effective way for advisors to connect with prospective clients. By establishing an online presence, either through a website, social media or other digital marketing channels, it’s possible to drive client traffic to your business.


That’s a significant difference compared to more traditional marketing methods, such as[cold calling](https://smartasset.com/advisor-resources/cold-calling-scripts-for-financial-advisors) or[networking](https://smartasset.com/advisor-resources/how-to-network-as-a-financial-advisor) in person. Cold calling, for instance, can be a time-consuming process as you need to contact prospects individually. The COVID-19 pandemic, meanwhile, saw a marked shift away from in-person networking toward virtual networking instead.


Digital marketing has made it easier for advisors to make themselves visible to a much broader audience, with a smaller investment of time. It’s possible to experiment with different digital marketing strategies to quantify what’s working or what’s not and adjust your strategy in real time.


## Bottom Line


Digital marketing can play a meaningful role in growing an advisory business, but it’s most effective when paired with a strategy that matches your current stage and resources. For instance, newly established advisors may have limited time or budget to invest in a full website build or ongoing video production. In those cases, directing marketing dollars toward services that connect you directly with qualified prospects can be more impactful, allowing you to focus your time and energy on building client relationships and growing the business.
