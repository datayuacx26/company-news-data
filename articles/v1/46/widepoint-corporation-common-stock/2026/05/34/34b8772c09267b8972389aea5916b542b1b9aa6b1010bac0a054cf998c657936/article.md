# WidePoint Corporation Announces the Rescheduling of its 2026 Annual Meeting of Stockholders

[Download as a PDF](https://www.widepoint.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/WidePoint-WYY-2026-ASM-Announcement_5192026-1.pdf)


**FAIRFAX, VA / May 19, 2026 /**[WidePoint Corporation](https://www.widepoint.com/)[(NYSE American: WYY)](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/WYY?p=WYY) **,** a leading provider of Secure Mobile Management Solutions, announced today that its 2026 Annual Meeting of Stockholders has been rescheduled from Friday, June 19, 2026 to Wednesday, June 17, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. EDT, for administrative reasons.


##### **Important Information About the Meeting**


The Company’s proxy statement related to the 2026 Annual Meeting of Stockholders and other materials filed by the Company with the SEC are available free of charge at the SEC’s website at


sec.gov


or the Company’s website


[www.widepoint.com](http://www.widepoint.com/) .


If you are a stockholder of record, you may vote by proxy by telephone or internet. Proxies submitted by telephone or through the internet must be received by 11:59 p.m. EDT on June 16, 2026. Please see the Notice of Internet Availability of Proxy Materials or proxy card for instructions on how to vote by telephone or internet. Stockholders of record will also be able to participate in the annual meeting online, vote shares electronically and submit questions prior to and during the meeting by visiting:


[www.virtualshareholdermeeting.com/WYY2026](http://www.virtualshareholdermeeting.com/WYY2026) .


If you hold your shares in “street name,” you must either direct the broker, trust, bank or other nominee as to how to vote your shares, or obtain a proxy from the bank, broker or other nominee to vote at the meeting. Please refer to the voter instruction cards used by your broker, trust, bank or other nominee for specific instructions on methods of voting, including by telephone or using the internet.
