# TechCruch selects CellChorus for Startup Battlefield 200 at TechCrunch Disrupt

# TechCruch selects CellChorus for Startup Battlefield 200 at TechCrunch Disrupt


### Fewer than 50 health and bio companies selected to present at TechCrunch event in San Francisco from September 19th to 21st


PRESS RELEASE


HOUSTON, Texas — August 24, 2023 — CellChorus Inc. today announced that TechCrunch has selected CellChorus to present at[TechCrunch Disrupt 2023](https://techcrunch.com/events/tc-disrupt-2023/?promo=battlefield&utm_campaign=disrupt2023&utm_content=battlefield&utm_medium=social&utm_source=cellchorus.com) in the Startup Battlefield 200 program. CellChorus is one of 200 startups—and fewer than 50 health/bio companies—selected from a review of thousands of applicants. This year’s Startup Battlefield participants span artificial intelligence (AI), healthtech, tech-bio, software as a service (SaaS), fintech, security, sustainability, space exploration, and more.


CellChorus® merges visual AI, advanced microscopy, and microscale manufacturing to visually evaluate thousands of cell-cell interactions in parallel. The company applies Time lapse Imaging Microscopy in Nanowell Grids™ (TIMING™) with neural network-based detection to identify which cells perform best—and why. CellChorus customers include leading researchers, developers and manufacturers of cell therapies and antibody therapeutics.


“We are pleased to be recognized by the TechCrunch team for an event the features some of the most exciting high-growth companies across AI, tech-bio, hardware, and other sectors,” said Daniel Meyer, CEO of CellChorus.


TechCrunch Disrupt 2023 will be held from Tuesday, September 19th to Thursday, September 21st at the Moscone Center in San Francisco. The event is known for debuting the hottest startups, introducing game-changing technologies, and discussing what is top-of-mind for the tech industry’s key innovators. Past companies that have presented at Disrupt include Cellino Bio, Cloudflare, Dropbox, Fitbit and Mint.

About CellChorus


CellChorus® is the leader in applying artificial intelligence to quantify the function and performance of cells over time to improve the development and delivery of novel therapies that improve patient care. The company applies Time-lapse Imaging Microscopy In Nanowell Grids™ (TIMING™) with neural network-based detection to identify how cells move, activate, kill, and survive at single-cell resolution. The patent-protected platform can link TIMING data and insights with information from other analysis modalities such as single-cell RNA sequencing to provide a comprehensive understanding of cellular function, state and phenotype. Please visit cellchorus.com for more information.
