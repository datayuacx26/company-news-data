# BioTools Innovator VANGUARD awards CellChorus contract to support AI-enabled pathogen-immune profiling

# BioTools Innovator VANGUARD awards CellChorus contract to support AI-enabled pathogen-immune profiling


### Funding from BioTools Innovator-BARDA partnership to accelerate drug development to combat infectious diseases caused by fungal pathogens


PRESS RELEASE


HOUSTON, Texas — August 25, 2025 — CellChorus Inc., the leader in applying artificial intelligence to visually evaluate how cells perform and interact over time, announced today that[BioTools Innovator (BTI) VANGUARD](https://biotoolsinnovator.org/vanguard/?utm_source=cellchorus.com&utm_medium=pr&utm_campaign=202508-vanguard) has awarded CellChorus® a $200,000 non-dilutive funding contract to support development of AI-enabled pathogen-immune profiling using TIMING™ single-cell assays. The principal investigators on the project are Rebecca Berdeaux, PhD, and Hannah Wilson, PhD.


VANGUARD is a joint initiative between BTI and the Biomedical Advanced Research and Development Authority (BARDA), which is part of the Administration for Strategic Preparedness and Response within the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services. VANGUARD supports startups in deploying innovative medical countermeasures that enhance U.S. readiness for public health emergencies.*


Time-lapse Imaging Microscopy In Nanowell Grids (TIMING) applies artificial intelligence (AI) and robotics to visually evaluate how thousands of individual immune cells perform over time. TIMING single-cell data include immune cell migration, contact dynamics, killing, survival, biomolecule secretion, and other functional readouts. The platform has been extensively validated in oncology for designing more effective therapies, selecting lead candidates for clinical studies, quantifying why certain patients respond in the clinic, and conducting advanced manufacturing analytics. This award will support development of TIMING for pathogen-immune profiling.


“We are excited to have the support from BARDA and BioTools Innovator to accelerate drug development to combat infectious diseases caused by fungal pathogens,” said Rebecca Berdeaux, CSO of CellChorus. “The results of this project will support not only the BARDA mission, but also the HHS priority to replace animal models with AI-enabled experimental modalities.”

About CellChorus


CellChorus is the leader in applying artificial intelligence (AI) and robotics to visually evaluate how thousands of individual immune cells, such as T cells and NK cells, perform over time. The company applies Time-lapse Imaging Microscopy In Nanowell Grids (TIMING™) with neural network-based AI to identify cells and evaluate their activity, including how they move, activate, kill and survive. The patent-protected platform can link TIMING data and insights with information from other analysis modalities such as single-cell RNA sequencing and flow cytometry to provide a comprehensive understanding of cellular function, state and phenotype for the life sciences industry.
