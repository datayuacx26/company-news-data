# Raptor Maps Named on the 2024 Global Cleantech 100

The[Global Cleantech 100](https://www.cleantech.com/the-global-cleantech-100/) is a comprehensive showcase of the most promising private companies in the cleantech ecosystem. This year’s list includes companies from 21 countries, showcasing the international collaboration and impact of cleantech solutions.


Raptor Maps, which is dedicated to enabling solar to scale,[recently reached the milestone](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7117526274717224960) of analyzing 100GW of solar installations, which enabled the restoration of 3GW of power–equivalent to powering 494,000 homes by solar and offsetting 3.9 million tons of CO2 emissions.
