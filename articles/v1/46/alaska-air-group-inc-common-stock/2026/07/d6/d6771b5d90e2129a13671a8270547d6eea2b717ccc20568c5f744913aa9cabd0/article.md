# Alaska Airlines, Inc. selects 737-800s to strengthen Hawaiian Airlines’ Neighbor Island service, enhancing the guest experience and increasing capacity

### Frequently asked questions


##### Why did Alaska Airlines select the Boeing 737-800 to replace Hawaiian Airlines' Neighbor Island Boeing 717 fleet?


The 737-800 was selected because it is a proven, reliable aircraft that is well suited for the unique demands of Neighbor Island operations, which involve short flight segments, frequent daily cycles and a challenging salt-air environment. The aircraft provides greater reliability, provides capacity to support frequent departures throughout the day and delivers a significantly enhanced onboard experience for Hawaiʻi travelers. This investment reflects Alaska’s long-term commitment to strengthening Hawaiian Airlines and serving Hawaiʻi well into the future.


##### How will the guest experience improve with the new Hawaiian-branded 737-800 aircraft?


Guests will enjoy a more modern and premium travel experience. The 737-800s will feature twice as many First Class seats, more than 30 Premium Class seats, fast and free Starlink Wi-Fi, reclining leather Recaro seats, in-seat power and USB charging at every seat, device holders, and additional cargo space for items such as surfboards. These enhancements also create more upgrade opportunities for members of Atmos Rewards and Huaka‘i by Hawaiian.


##### When will we start to see Hawaiian-branded Boeing 737-800s flying Neighbor Island routes?


Our goal is to begin this fleet transition in 2028 and move quickly to bring this additional capacity and enhanced experience to our guests.


##### Does the company plan to operate any Alaska-branded 737 aircraft on Neighbor Island routes?


To sustain frequency and capacity and meet the needs of Hawaiʻi’s communities in the near-term before the transition to the future Hawaiian-branded 737-800 fleet begins, Alaska Airlines will add one Alaska-branded Boeing 737 aircraft to the Neighbor Island schedule. Beginning in October, this aircraft will operate three daily round trips between Honolulu (HNL) and Kahului (OGG). The aircraft will operate from Terminal 1 at Honolulu’s Daniel K. Inouye International Airport, and guests traveling on these flights will check in at Terminal 1.


##### Will Neighbor Island service continue to be operated by local Hawaiʻi-based employees?


Yes. The future Neighbor Island 737-800 fleet will be based in Honolulu, and the core Neighbor Island service will be flown and crewed by Honolulu-based pilots and flight attendants. We will also maintain maintenance capability in Honolulu to support the operation and continue leveraging Hawai‘i-based technical expertise.
