# Investor Relations - Alaska Airlines, Hawaiian Airlines and Horizon Air

# Welcome to the
Alaska Air Group Investor Hub


Alaska Airlines, Hawaiian Airlines and Horizon Air are subsidiaries of Alaska Air Group, and McGee Air Services is a subsidiary of Alaska Airlines. We are a global airline with hubs in Seattle, Honolulu, Portland, Anchorage, Los Angeles, San Diego and San Francisco. We deliver remarkable care as we fly our guests to more than 140 destinations throughout North America, Latin America, Asia, the Pacific and Europe.


Alaska and Hawaiian are members of the


**one** world alliance. Members of our


[Atmos™ Rewards](https://www.alaskaair.com/atmosrewards)


loyalty program can earn and redeem points with


**one** world airlines and our additional global partners that serve over 1,000 worldwide destinations.
