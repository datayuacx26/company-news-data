# About Alaska Airlines


At Alaska Airlines, we’re more than a means of getting from point A to B — we’re a team of passionate people committed to connecting our guests to the world through a remarkable travel experience rooted in safety, care and performance.


Our journey began over 90 years ago connecting remote villages across Alaska. Today, our wings span destinations around the globe, yet our heartfelt dedication to the communities we serve and to delivering genuine, caring service continues to guide us every step of the way.


From where we started to where we’re headed, we’re proud of our story of innovation and adventure — and we look forward to sharing the next chapter with you onboard.

## Our history


For over nine decades, Alaska Airlines and the people behind it have been driven by integrity, care, ingenuity, professionalism and a spirit as rugged and resilient as our roots. That spirit was forged in the wild landscapes of Alaska — where aviation wasn’t a luxury, but a lifeline.


Our story began in 1932, when Linious “Mac” McGee launched McGee Airways by hand-painting the name onto a three-passenger plane and flying essential supplies out of Anchorage. What started as a bold solution to bridge vast, roadless terrain soon grew — through grit and a pioneering mindset — into what became Alaska Airlines in 1944. From those humble beginnings, we’ve charted a course toward innovation, service and connecting people and places with care that’s always grounded in our roots.
