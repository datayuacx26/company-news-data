# Alternatives to Syringe Pumps: Precise Flow Control

Syringe pumps have long been a standard for precise fluid handling, but many modern life science and diagnostic applications now require higher throughput, continuous operation, and system integration. As needs change, instrument developers are looking for alternatives, such as precision dispense pumps, to achieve reliable results for today’s demanding applications.


## What are the Top Five Key Considerations When Replacing a Syringe Pump?


1. Accuracy, precision, and flow performance
2. Regulatory compliance and validation support—especially for life science markets
3. Software, connectivity, and automation
4. Ease of use, training, and reliability
5. Vendor support and total cost of ownership

### Pump Comparison: Syringe versus Precision Dispense Pump


**Pump Capability** **Syringe Pump** **Precision Dispense Pump**


**Volume Capacity**


Limited by syringe size; requires refills


Continuous operation with no refills


**Throughput**


Low; best for small, precise volumes


High: Suited for moderate to large volumes


**Operation Duration**


Interrupted by syringe refill or syringe changes


Continuous, long duration operation


**Maintenance**


Simple fluid path, but will require maintenance to replace syringes


Robust components designed to last the life of the instrument


**Pressure Capability**


Limited; syringes can deform under high pressure


Higher pressure capability based on rugged pump materials


**Chemical Flexibility**


Syringes need to be swapped for different fluids


Choice of robust piston and seals to match solvent compatibility


**Scalability (Multi Channel)**


Bulky; each channel needs its own syringe


Compact and easier to scale. One pump with a solenoid valve can do the equivalent of 6 syringes.
