# Monsoon Flavors Bring IDEX Mumbai Together

With the arrival of the Monsoon season (sometimes called the rainy or wet season), the IDEX Mumbai team marked the transition to cooler weather with a spirited potluck competition.


The event was a feast of flavors, colors, and camaraderie. From homemade classics to inventive surprises, there was so much food, it took two lunch sessions to enjoy it all! The event also allowed for peer-to-peer networking.


50 colleagues across five groups competed for the best food and presentation.


Contrary to the historical proverb, sometimes too many cooks *don’t* spoil the broth; they make it unforgettable.
