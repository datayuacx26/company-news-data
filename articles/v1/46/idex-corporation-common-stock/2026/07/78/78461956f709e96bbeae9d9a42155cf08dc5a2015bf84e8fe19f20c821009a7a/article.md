# IDEX Health & Science Receives Silver Honoree Innovators Award for BrightLine 2.0

**Rochester, New York, December 23, 2025** – IDEX Health & Science Honored by 2025 Laser Focus World Innovators Awards


IDEX Health & Science, a leading innovator in optics and photonics, proudly announces that its cutting-edge Semrock BrightLine 2.0 filter set family has been distinguished among the best in the industry by the esteemed 2025 Laser Focus World Innovators’ Awards. A panel of judges, comprised of experts from the optics and photonics communities, awarded IDEX Health & Science with an outstanding Silver honoree status.


Laser Focus World’s Editor In Chief, Sally Johnson, expressed her congratulations, stating, “On behalf of the Laser Focus World Innovators Awards, I would like to congratulate IDEX Health & Science on achieving a well-deserved level honoree status.” This competitive program enables Laser Focus World to showcase and applaud the most innovative products, projects, technologies, and programs that significantly impact the industry.”


We are thrilled to be recognized for our commitment to excellence and looks forward to continuing to deliver cutting-edge solutions to the market.


Semrock BrightLine filter sets have long been an industry “work horse,” delivering dependable, exceptional performance for a wide range of ever evolving applications and customer needs. BrightLine 2.0 represents the culmination of 20+ years of technological advancement for Semrock optical filters, and they are a prime example of enhancing core Semrock products to meet current industry requirements. With the new BrightLine sets, IDEX Health & Science is setting a new standard for value and performance in fluorescence imaging, fluorescence microscopy, widefield microscopy, and confocal microscopy.
