# IDEX Health & Science Launches New Intuitive™ Torque Limiting Fitting System

Rohnert Park, California, January 22, 2021 – IDEX Health & Science, industry leader in fluidic connections for life science applications, has launched the Intuitive™ Torque Limiting Fitting System which is a patented torque limiting true “Finger Tight” fitting for low pressure connections. The torque limiting technology provides a haptic “click” feedback when it reaches the optimum torque, assuring a perfect connection every time. This universal fitting can be used with 1/16” & 1/8th” OD tubing.


“Fluidic transit is a critical design feature within life science instrumentation that is often compromised at connection sites resulting in increased operational costs and complexity” said Calin Aurel Dragan, PhD Product Manager at IDEX Health & Science. “The torque limiting design feature of the Intuitive connection system ensures a robust and reliable connection when installing tubing assemblies for instrument assembly, as well as for field service operations” continued Dragan.
