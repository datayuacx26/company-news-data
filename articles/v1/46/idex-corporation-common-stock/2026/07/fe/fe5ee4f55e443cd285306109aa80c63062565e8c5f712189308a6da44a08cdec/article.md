# IDEX Health & Science Launches New BrightLine® 2.0 Filter Sets

Rochester, New York, April 29, 2025 – IDEX Health & Science has launched the newly redesigned Semrock BrightLine® 2.0 and BrightLine Basic™ optical filter sets.


Semrock Optical Filters from IDEX Health & Science have stood at the forefront of optical filter technology, consistently pushing the boundaries of what's possible in fluorescence microscopy and analytical instrumentation. Semrock BrightLine filter sets have long been an industry “work horse,” delivering dependable, exceptional performance for a wide range of ever evolving applications and customer needs. Today, the IDEX Health & Science team is excited to announce a significant product advancement designed to provide unparalleled value and performance: the BrightLine 2.0 and BrightLine Basic optical filter sets.


“The market has changed,” said Xavier El Baz, Product Management Director for Semrock Optical Filters. “As limits are pushed for optical systems, we’re creating filters designed to suit those evolving needs.”


Both new BrightLine filter sets represent the culmination of 20+ years of technological advancement for Semrock optical filters, and they are a prime example of enhancing core Semrock products to meet current industry requirements. With the new BrightLine sets, IDEX Health & Science is setting a new standard for value and performance in fluorescence imaging, fluorescence microscopy, widefield microscopy, and confocal microscopy.
