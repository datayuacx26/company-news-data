# Business Units Achieve IDEX Green Certification

Since the program was launched earlier this year, 13 business units have achieved **IDEX Green** certification, demonstrating our commitment to improving sustainability across our manufacturing operations.


These IDEX teams followed a practical, structured framework to better measure and understand their environmental footprint through data logging tools, installation of 100% LED lighting, reduction of water use and waste production, among other improvement efforts.


Sites also must optimize their compressor systems – oftentimes the most energy intensive equipment in a facility. Teams are encouraged to take advantage of free programs offered by local utilities to identify and address opportunities to improve energy efficiency.


Those who achieve these milestones not only reap the benefits of operational efficiency, they build a positive culture of sustainability and engagement in their workforces. Business units can also tout the achievement externally to customers and prospective employees.


Congratulations to the following IDEX Green certified businesses:


- ABEL Pump Technology
- Advanced Flow Solutions (Liquid Controls, Corken, SAMPI)
- Airtech
- Fast & Fluid Management
- Gast
- Godiva
- Hale
- IDEX Health & Science (Bristol, Connecticut & Rohnert Park, California)
- Richter
- Tecan
- Viking Pump (Cedar Falls, Iowa)
- Warren Rupp
