# Feature Release: Refine Summaries Using Selected Papers


Epsilon now lets you refine summaries for a given query. After you make a search, you can star papers that you think are the most relevant and regenerate the answer using only the selected papers.

Epsilon tries to find a diverse set of papers for your query, but this may sometimes give less detailed responses. This feature gives you the power to refine your search using source material that you think is most relevant. This is great for diving deeper into a literature review or exploring new research questions.
