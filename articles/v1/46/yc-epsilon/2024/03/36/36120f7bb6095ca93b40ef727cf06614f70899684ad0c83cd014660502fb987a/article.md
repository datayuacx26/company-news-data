This week, we launched a new feature to help you generate entire literature reviews for any research topic! Here's how it works.


####


**Generate Literature Reviews**


Start by entering a topic in the "Search for Publications" tab. On the given search result, you'll now see a button titled "Generate Literature Review".


When you click this button, Epsilon will create and answer several questions relating to the research topic. It will then synthesize the results into a single review. Here's what the final result looks like:


If you want to deep dive into the information, click on any of the questions at the bottom of the response to view the full information and related sources.


####


**Return to Previous Literature Reviews**


All search results and literature reviews are saved in your search history. Hover over the "History" tab on the left side to see your previous responses.


####


**Adjust Your Results**


You can edit your literature review in several ways.


First, you can filter the set of papers that are used by clicking "Refine Results". In the resulting popup, you can add terms which Epsilon will use to remove irrelevant papers from the search result. You can also expand the list by including all references from the papers in the search result.


You can also modify the questions that are used to generate the literature review by clicking "Modify Questions". Here, you can remove questions entirely or edit them directly. Clicking "Confirm" will generate a new review based on the modified questions.
