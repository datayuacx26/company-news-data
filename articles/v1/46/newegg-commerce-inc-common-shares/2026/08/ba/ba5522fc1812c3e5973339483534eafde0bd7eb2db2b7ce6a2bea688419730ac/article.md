# Best Budget Desktop PCs for Students in 2026

A desktop is the quiet workhorse of student life. It does not go to lectures with you, but it is waiting on your dorm or apartment desk every evening for writing papers, catching up on readings, streaming a lecture recording and handling the light tasks that fill a semester. Because a tower or mini PC uses a separate monitor and keyboard, it also stretches a tight budget further than a laptop: many of the best values here are barebone kits and refurbished office machines that cost a fraction of a new all-in-one. Below are the best budget desktops on Newegg right now, matched to four real student scenarios. All prices were checked on Newegg on August 15, 2026.


## Who Needs a Desktop?


A desktop makes sense if you mostly work from one spot and want the most performance and the biggest screen for your money. You lose portability, but you gain a comfortable dedicated study station, easier upgrades and, often, a lower price than a comparable laptop. A desktop also tends to run cooler and last longer under a heavy workload, and repairs usually mean swapping one inexpensive part rather than replacing the whole machine. One thing to plan for before you buy: several of the picks below are either **barebone kits** , which ship without RAM, an SSD or an operating system, or **refurbished units** that may need a fresh drive or a Windows license. Budget a little extra for those parts, plus a monitor, keyboard and mouse if you do not already own them. Even after adding a screen, these builds usually undercut a new laptop of similar speed.

Right beside it, the[Dell OptiPlex 3060](https://www.newegg.com/p/N82E16883461211?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_best-budget-desktop-pcs-for-students-in-2026) at $240.00 offers the same plug-and-play simplicity from another trusted commercial line. Both arrive ready to run, so first-year students can be online the same afternoon they unbox. Confirm the listing includes Windows and enough storage, and add an inexpensive SSD later if you want more room.


## Scenario 2: The DIY Mini PC


If you want to learn how a PC goes together and keep the footprint tiny, a barebone kit is a rewarding first build. The[ASRock DeskMini X600W](https://www.newegg.com/p/N82E16856158097?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_best-budget-desktop-pcs-for-students-in-2026) at $159.99 is the lowest entry price on this list, a book-sized chassis with motherboard and cooler already installed, waiting for you to add a Ryzen chip, memory, an SSD and Windows. Step up to the[ASRock DeskMeet X600](https://www.newegg.com/p/N82E16856158094?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_best-budget-desktop-pcs-for-students-in-2026) at $209.99 and you get a slightly larger case that still accepts a full-size graphics card down the road, which makes it the more future-proof choice for a student who might add light gaming later.


The DeskMeet X600 barebone leaves room for a full graphics card later.


Remember that both kits are barebone, so factor in the cost of a processor, RAM, an SSD and an OS before you compare them against a ready-made desktop.

The[HP ProDesk 405 G8](https://www.newegg.com/p/N82E16883974001?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_best-budget-desktop-pcs-for-students-in-2026) at $279.99 pairs a modern AMD Ryzen processor with a small-form-factor case, a great fit for students who prefer AMD and want a quiet machine. Both are proven business platforms built to run for years.


## Scenario 4: The Compact All-Rounder


The ASUS NUC 16 Pro packs current-generation power into a palm-sized case.


If you want a brand-new, current-generation machine that disappears behind a monitor, the[ASUS NUC 16 Pro](https://www.newegg.com/p/N82E16856110376?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_best-budget-desktop-pcs-for-students-in-2026) at $697.99 is the premium pick. It delivers modern performance and connectivity in a palm-sized enclosure, making it ideal for a small dorm desk or a shared apartment where space is scarce. It costs more than the refurbished towers, but you get a warranty, the latest platform and near-silent operation, which is worth it for students who want one clean device to last all four years. It also sips power and adds almost no clutter, so it is easy to mount behind a display and forget about until it is time to graduate.
