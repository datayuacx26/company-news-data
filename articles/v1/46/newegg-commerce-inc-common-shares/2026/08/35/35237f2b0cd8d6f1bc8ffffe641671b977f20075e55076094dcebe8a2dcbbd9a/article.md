# Best Laptops for Back to School 2026: Picks for Every Budget

The laptop you choose in August is the one you carry to every lecture, library session and late-night study group until finals. Unlike a desktop, you cannot swap the screen or the keyboard later, so it pays to buy the right machine the first time. The good news is that 2026 is a great year to be shopping: there are genuinely capable laptops under $400, and the money you save can go toward textbooks instead of tech. Below are the best back-to-school laptops on Newegg right now, organized by budget and use case, with the handful of specifications that actually change your day-to-day experience. All prices were checked on Newegg on August 15, 2026.


## Why the Right Laptop Matters


A student laptop has to juggle two very different jobs. During the day it needs to run browser tabs, a word processor and video calls quietly on battery. In the evening it might edit a presentation, compile code or handle photo work for a class project. The trap is overspending on power you will rarely use, or underspending and being stuck with a machine that feels slow by sophomore year. The sweet spot is a laptop with enough memory and storage to stay comfortable for four years, in a chassis that survives daily commuting.


## What to Look For


Four specs matter more than the rest. Start with **memory** : 16GB of RAM is the 2026 floor, and it is the single biggest factor in how many apps and tabs you can run at once. Next is **storage** : a 512GB SSD is a sensible starting point, and 256GB fills up fast once you install a few large apps. Third is the **processor** : a modern Intel Core i5 or Core Ultra, or an AMD Ryzen 5 or 7, comfortably handles coursework, while a Core Ultra 9 or Ryzen 9 adds real headroom for creative work. Finally, the **display** : a 1080p (or higher) IPS panel is easy on the eyes for long reading sessions, and an OLED screen is worth it if you edit photos or video.
