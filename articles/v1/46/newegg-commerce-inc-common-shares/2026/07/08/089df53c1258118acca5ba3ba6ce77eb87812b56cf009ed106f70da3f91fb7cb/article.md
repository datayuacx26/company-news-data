# Samsung 9100 Pro vs Crucial T705: The Fastest PCIe 5.0 SSDs for Creators

PCIe 5.0 storage has matured from a benchmark curiosity into a genuine workflow upgrade, and two drives now define the top of the market: Samsung’s 9100 Pro and Crucial’s T705. Both push sequential speeds around double what the best Gen4 drives manage. Both also demand respect on cooling. Here is how they compare, and who should actually buy one.

## Deep Dive: Samsung 9100 Pro


Samsung’s 9100 Pro leads the rated-speed race at 14,700 MB/s.


The[Samsung 9100 Pro 2TB](https://www.newegg.com/samsung-2tb-9100-pro-nvme-2-0/p/N82E16820147903?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_samsung-9100-pro-vs-crucial-t705-the-fastest-pcie-5-0-ssds-for-creators) ($399.99 on Newegg at this writing) is Samsung’s first consumer Gen5 flagship, rated at up to 14,700 MB/s reads for this capacity. It pairs strong sequential numbers with the consistency and mature Magician software Samsung is known for, and its random I/O performance keeps everyday responsiveness snappy. A heatsink version is available for open motherboard slots.


## Deep Dive: Crucial T705


Crucial’s T705 held the consumer speed crown and still trades blows at the top.


The[Crucial T705 2TB](https://www.newegg.com/crucial-2tb-t705-nvme/p/N82E16820156398?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_samsung-9100-pro-vs-crucial-t705-the-fastest-pcie-5-0-ssds-for-creators) ($392.95) answers with up to 14,500 MB/s reads and 12,700 MB/s writes, numbers PCWorld called the fastest they had tested when it debuted. Micron’s in-house NAND gives Crucial tight control of the whole stack, and the T705 has built a long track record since launch.


## The Cooling Caveat Neither Brand Advertises Loudly


Gen5 controllers run hot. Without airflow or a heatsink, sustained transfers can throttle dramatically; independent testing has shown top Gen5 drives dropping from 13,000-plus MB/s to under half that during long writes with no cooling. Use your motherboard’s M.2 heatsink or buy the heatsink SKU, and treat active airflow over the slot as part of the purchase.


## Who Actually Needs Gen5?


Honest answer: gamers do not, yet. Game load times differ by seconds or less versus a good Gen4 drive. The buyers who feel Gen5 daily are creators and professionals: video editors moving 100GB RAW projects, photographers ingesting cards, AI developers shuffling model checkpoints. For those workflows, doubling sequential bandwidth is measured in saved minutes every single day.

## Frequently Asked Questions


Common questions about high-end PCIe 5.0 SSDs.


Is the Samsung 9100 Pro faster than the Crucial T705? On rated sequential reads the 9100 Pro edges ahead (14,700 vs 14,500 MB/s at 2TB); in real workflows they trade blows.


Do PCIe 5.0 SSDs need a heatsink? Effectively yes. Gen5 controllers throttle heavily without cooling; use a motherboard M.2 heatsink or the heatsink SKU.


Are Gen5 SSDs worth it for gaming? Not really. Load times barely improve over Gen4; the benefit goes to creators moving very large files regularly.


Will a PCIe 5.0 SSD work in a PCIe 4.0 slot? Yes, backward compatible, but capped at Gen4 speeds.
