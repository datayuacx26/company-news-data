# RTX 5070 Ti vs RX 9070 XT: Which High-End 1440p GPU Should You Buy?

The most interesting fight in PC graphics right now is not at the $2,000 flagship level. It is in the high-end 1440p bracket, where NVIDIA’s GeForce RTX 5070 Ti and AMD’s Radeon RX 9070 XT trade blows for very different amounts of money. Both drive 1440p at high refresh rates with ease and both carry 16GB of memory, but they get there in different ways. Here is how they stack up, feature by feature.

## Deep Dive: Where the RTX 5070 Ti Wins


The 5070 Ti is the more complete technology package. DLSS 4 with Multi Frame Generation can multiply smoothness in supported titles, ray-traced lighting runs meaningfully faster, and NVIDIA’s encoder suite remains the choice for streamers and creators. Reviewers at TechSpot found it comfortable not just at 1440p but at 4K in many titles, which gives it more headroom if you upgrade your monitor later.


## Deep Dive: Where the RX 9070 XT Wins


Value, plainly. At roughly $240 less than this 5070 Ti listing, the 9070 XT delivers rasterization performance that trades blows with NVIDIA’s card in traditional workloads, and PC Gamer has repeatedly named the 9070 series the best overall buy for most gamers. FSR 4 closes much of the upscaling quality gap, and 16GB of VRAM means neither card will run out of memory at 1440p any time soon.

## Frequently Asked Questions


Common questions about the RTX 5070 Ti and RX 9070 XT.


Is the RTX 5070 Ti faster than the RX 9070 XT? In rasterization the two trade blows, with the 9070 XT often ahead in raw raster while the 5070 Ti pulls ahead in ray-traced titles and with DLSS 4 Multi Frame Generation enabled.


Which is better value? At verified Newegg pricing of $749.99 vs $989.99, the RX 9070 XT delivers similar 1440p performance for about $240 less.


Do both cards have enough VRAM for 1440p? Yes. Both carry 16GB, ample for 1440p and comfortable for 4K in most current titles.


Which card is better for streaming? The RTX 5070 Ti, thanks to the NVIDIA encoder suite and broader software ecosystem support.
