# AMD Ryzen 7 7700X3D Is Here: The $329 Gateway to High-End AM5 Gaming

After a week of teasers and rumors, it is official: the AMD Ryzen 7 7700X3D launched on July 16 at $329, and for the current quarter it is available exclusively at Newegg in North America. That makes this the single cheapest way to put AMD’s celebrated 3D V-Cache gaming technology into a new AM5 build. Here is everything you need to know before you click Add to Cart.


## Why It Matters


X3D processors have dominated gaming benchmark charts for years, but that dominance always carried a price premium. With DDR5 memory prices climbing across the industry this year, total build costs have crept up, and an affordable X3D chip arrives at exactly the right moment. The 7700X3D lets builders keep the part that matters most for frame rates, the stacked 96MB L3 cache, while trimming spend where it hurts less.


## Meet the 7700X3D


The Ryzen 7 7700X3D: eight Zen 4 cores and 96MB of stacked L3 cache for $328.99.


The[Ryzen 7 7700X3D](https://www.newegg.com/ryzen-7-7700x3d-ryzen-7000-series-raphael-zen-4-socket-am5-amd-cpu/p/N82E16819113941?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_amd-ryzen-7-7700x3d-buying-guide-329-gateway-to-am5-gaming-2) ($328.99 on Newegg at this writing) is an eight-core, sixteen-thread Zen 4 processor for Socket AM5. It runs a 4.0GHz base clock, boosts up to 4.5GHz, and carries the same 96MB of 3D V-Cache L3 as the beloved 7800X3D, within a 120W TDP. You get PCIe 5.0, DDR5 with EXPO memory overclocking, Precision Boost Overdrive support, and basic integrated Radeon graphics for troubleshooting. A cooler is not included, so budget for one.


## What to Look For


Three things determine whether this chip is right for you. First, resolution: X3D cache pays off most at 1080p and 1440p high-refresh gaming, where the CPU is the bottleneck. Second, workload: if you mostly game, the 7700X3D punches far above its price; if you render video all day, a higher-clocked chip with more cores serves you better. Third, platform budget: AM5 requires a new motherboard and DDR5, so factor the whole bill, not just the CPU.

The gap that matters: the[Ryzen 7 7800X3D](https://www.newegg.com/amd-ryzen-7-7800x3d-ryzen-7-7000-series-raphael-zen-4-socket-am5/p/N82E16819113793?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_amd-ryzen-7-7700x3d-buying-guide-329-gateway-to-am5-gaming-2) currently sells for $416, an $87 premium for roughly 500MHz of extra boost clock. Early reviews found the 7700X3D lands only slightly behind its bigger sibling in games, because the shared 96MB cache does most of the heavy lifting. XDA’s launch review measured 175 fps average in Cyberpunk 2077 at 1080p Ultra and around 500 fps in Counter-Strike 2 at 1440p low, awarding the chip a 9/10 and praising the lowest package power draw they had recorded in years.


## The Budget Math


A sensible 7700X3D platform today: the CPU at $328.99, a solid B650 board like the[ASRock B650M Pro RS WiFi](https://www.newegg.com/asrock-b650m-pro-rs-wifi-micro-atx-motherboard-amd-b650-am5/p/N82E16813162131?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_amd-ryzen-7-7700x3d-buying-guide-329-gateway-to-am5-gaming-2) ($119.99), and a 32GB DDR5-6000 kit. With memory prices where they are, the CPU savings effectively pay for most of your motherboard. The chip’s low thermals also mean a $35-50 air cooler is plenty, another quiet saving versus hotter flagships.


## Final Verdict


Best for: gamers building their first AM5 rig, esports players chasing high refresh rates, and small-form-factor builders who value low heat. Skip if: you already own a 7800X3D-class chip, or your workload is production-first. At $329 with Newegg exclusivity in North America, the[7700X3D](https://www.newegg.com/ryzen-7-7700x3d-ryzen-7000-series-raphael-zen-4-socket-am5-amd-cpu/p/N82E16819113941?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_amd-ryzen-7-7700x3d-buying-guide-329-gateway-to-am5-gaming-2) is the value story of this CPU generation so far. Stock at launch has been moving quickly, so if it fits your build, do not wait for a sale that may never need to come.

## Frequently Asked Questions


Answers to the most common questions about the AMD Ryzen 7 7700X3D.


How much does the AMD Ryzen 7 7700X3D cost? It launched on July 16, 2026 at an MSRP of $329 and is available exclusively at Newegg in North America for the current quarter.


Is the 7700X3D good for gaming? Yes. It carries the same 96MB of 3D V-Cache as the 7800X3D and lands only slightly behind it in gaming benchmarks, making it one of the best price-to-performance gaming CPUs available.


Does the Ryzen 7 7700X3D come with a cooler? No cooler is included. Its 120W TDP and notably low real-world power draw mean a quality budget air cooler is sufficient for most builds.


What motherboard does the 7700X3D need? It uses Socket AM5 and works with A620, B650, B650E, B840, B850, X670, X670E, X870, and X870E chipsets. A B650 or B850 board is the value sweet spot.
