# Build Your First Home NAS with Newegg’s NAS Builder: Three Real Scenarios

Cloud storage subscriptions add up, and they never really belong to you. A NAS, network attached storage, is the fix: your own private cloud, sitting on your shelf, accessible from every device you own. The catch has always been choosing one: bays, RAID levels, network speeds. Newegg’s free[NAS Builder](https://www.newegg.com/tools/nas-builder?cm_sp=Cat_NAS_1-_-Shopping-Tools-_-NAS-Builder_1&utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_build-first-home-nas-newegg-nas-builder-scenarios) turns that decision into a guided flow. Here is how it works, through three real households.


The NAS Builder pairs a live model list with a RAID calculator, so you see usable capacity before you buy.


## How the Tool Works


Start by declaring how much total storage you need; the tool filters out enclosures that cannot handle it.


The Builder starts with one question: how much total capacity do you need, from 36TB or less up to 144TB and beyond. It then lists only enclosures that fit, filterable by brand, bay count, memory, and network ports. Add drives, and the built-in RAID Calculator instantly shows how much space you keep, how much protects you from a drive failure, and how much goes unused. A running meter even translates terabytes into minutes of video and numbers of photos.


## Scenario 1: The Family Photo Vault


Two phones full of photos, a shared laptop, and a decade of memories in too many places. A two-bay unit like the[Synology DiskStation DS223J](https://www.newegg.com/synology-ds223j-2-bay-realtek-rtd1619b-processor-diskless-system/p/N82E16822108836?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_build-first-home-nas-newegg-nas-builder-scenarios) ($189.99 verified) with two mirrored drives is the classic answer: one drive can fail and every photo survives. In the Builder, choose “36TB or Less”, filter to 2-Bay, and let the RAID calculator show RAID 1 mirroring. Total spend lands well under most families’ two-year cloud bill.


## Scenario 2: The Media Streamer


Movie collection, home videos, and a Plex habit. Step up to a four-bay: the[Synology DS423](https://www.newegg.com/synology-ds423-4-bay-realtek-rtd1619b-processor-diskless-system/p/N82E16822108830?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_build-first-home-nas-newegg-nas-builder-scenarios) ($399.99 verified) covers streaming duty, while the[UGREEN NASync DXP4800 Plus](https://www.newegg.com/ugreen-dxp4800-plus-4-bay-intel-pentium-gold-8505-processor-diskless-system/p/N82E16822995003?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_build-first-home-nas-newegg-nas-builder-scenarios) ($656.99 verified) adds an Intel 5-core chip, 8GB of DDR5, and a 10GbE port for serious headroom. Four bays mean you can start with two drives and add more as the library grows, and the Builder’s compatibility filter keeps drive choices honest.


## Scenario 3: The Creator’s Studio


Editing 4K video straight off the NAS demands network speed. The tool’s own guide puts numbers to it: gigabit Ethernet tops out around 125 MB/s, while 10GbE delivers roughly 1,250 MB/s, enough to scrub multi-camera timelines remotely. Filter the model list to 10GbE-equipped units, like the DXP4800 Plus above or the six-bay[UGREEN DXP6800 Pro](https://www.newegg.com/ugreen-dxp6800-pro-6-bay-intel-core-i5-1235u-processor/p/N82E16822995004?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_build-first-home-nas-newegg-nas-builder-scenarios) ($1,099.99 verified) with Thunderbolt, then let the RAID calculator balance speed and redundancy across your drive set.

## Frequently Asked Questions


Answers to the most common questions about building a home NAS.


What is Newegg's NAS Builder? A free tool that guides you from total capacity through enclosure and drive selection, with a built-in RAID calculator showing usable versus protective storage before you buy.


How many drive bays do I need in a home NAS? Two bays with mirrored drives suit photo backup; four bays balance capacity and redundancy for media libraries; six or more suit creators who plan to expand.


Do I need 10GbE networking on a NAS? Only for demanding work like editing video directly off the NAS. Gigabit Ethernet is fine for backups and streaming; 10GbE matters for large-file workflows.


How much does a good starter NAS cost? Verified on Newegg, quality diskless two-bay units start at $140.99-$189.99, plus the cost of drives.
