# Upgrading from AM4 to AM5: A Step-by-Step Guide Around the $329 Ryzen 7 7700X3D

If you built your PC in the Ryzen 3000 or 5000 era, you have been watching AM5 from the sidelines for four years. The math finally changed this month: with the Ryzen 7 7700X3D landing at $328.99 as a Newegg exclusive, the total cost of a top-tier gaming platform jump has never been lower. Here is the complete playbook, including what you keep, what you buy, and the order to do it in.


## Background: What Carries Over (More Than You Think)


An AM4-to-AM5 move replaces exactly three things: CPU, motherboard, and memory. Everything else migrates. Your graphics card, SSDs, power supply, case, and fans all work on the new platform. Even better, AM5 kept AM4’s cooler mounting pattern, so most existing coolers bolt straight on; check your model’s maker to confirm. Windows will typically reactivate automatically if your license is linked to a Microsoft account.


## Step 1: The Shopping List


The 7700X3D anchors the cheapest X3D platform jump yet.


Three items. The CPU: the[Ryzen 7 7700X3D](https://www.newegg.com/ryzen-7-7700x3d-ryzen-7000-series-raphael-zen-4-socket-am5-amd-cpu/p/N82E16819113941?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_am4-to-am5-upgrade-guide-ryzen-7-7700x3d) at $328.99 brings 96MB of 3D V-Cache, the same slab as chips costing hundreds more. The motherboard: a B650 micro-ATX covers nearly everyone; the[ASRock B650M Pro RS WiFi](https://www.newegg.com/asrock-b650m-pro-rs-wifi-micro-atx-motherboard-amd-b650-am5/p/N82E16813162131?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_am4-to-am5-upgrade-guide-ryzen-7-7700x3d) ($119.99) and[GIGABYTE B650M GAMING PLUS WIFI](https://www.newegg.com/gigabyte-b650m-gaming-plus-wifi-micro-atx-motherboard-amd-b650-am5/p/N82E16813145502?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_am4-to-am5-upgrade-guide-ryzen-7-7700x3d) ($108.74) are both verified under $120 with Wi-Fi included. The memory: one honest warning below.


## Step 2: Budget Honestly for DDR5


Here is the part nobody enjoys: DDR5 prices have risen sharply this year, and a quality 32GB DDR5-6000 CL30 kit, the sweet spot for Ryzen, now runs around $409 verified, such as the[Patriot Viper Elite 5](https://www.newegg.com/patriot-memory-viper-elite-5-32gb-2-x-16gb-ddr5-6000-pc5-48000-cas-latency-cl30-desktop-memory-white/p/N82E16820225386?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_am4-to-am5-upgrade-guide-ryzen-7-7700x3d) . That makes RAM the second-biggest line item, which is precisely why the 7700X3D’s $87-150 savings over other X3D chips matters: it absorbs most of the memory pain. Total platform cost: roughly $858.


## Step 3: The Physical Swap


Sub-$120 B650 boards with Wi-Fi make the platform tax manageable.


Unplug, ground yourself, and pull the old board with cooler attached; it is easier as one unit. Transfer nothing from the old board except your SSDs. Install the 7700X3D in the AM5 socket, noting the arrow-to-arrow alignment, clip or screw your cooler onto the familiar mounting points with fresh thermal paste, seat both RAM sticks in the slots your manual marks A2 and B2, and rebuild in the case. The whole job is under an hour for a second-time builder.


## Step 4: First Boot and EXPO


First boot on new DDR5 can take a couple of minutes of memory training with a black screen. Do not panic and do not power off. Once you reach the BIOS, do two things: update to the latest firmware for your board, and enable EXPO so your DDR5-6000 kit actually runs at 6000 instead of the conservative default. The 7700X3D supports EXPO and Precision Boost Overdrive if you want to tune further.


## Step 5: Verify the Win


Boot Windows, let drivers settle, and run your most CPU-bound game, an MMO city, a simulation title, or a competitive shooter. Coming from a Ryzen 5 3600 or 5600X class chip, launch-week testing of the 7700X3D suggests you should see dramatically higher frame rates and, just as noticeably, steadier 1% lows. Your GPU did not change; your bottleneck did.


## Summary


Three parts, one afternoon, roughly $858 all-in at verified prices, and your “old” PC exits with a platform that AMD has committed to for years to come. The 7700X3D was built for exactly this move; the only question is whether your AM4 board reads this over your shoulder.

## Frequently Asked Questions


Answers to the most common questions about upgrading from AM4 to AM5.


What do I need to buy to upgrade from AM4 to AM5? Three parts: an AM5 CPU like the $328.99 Ryzen 7 7700X3D, an AM5 motherboard starting under $120, and DDR5 memory. GPU, PSU, case, storage, and most coolers carry over.


Do AM4 coolers fit AM5 motherboards? In most cases yes. AM5 retained AM4's cooler mounting pattern, so the majority of existing coolers install directly; confirm with your cooler manufacturer.


How much does an AM4 to AM5 upgrade cost in 2026? At verified Newegg prices, roughly $858: the 7700X3D at $328.99, a B650 board from $108.74, and a 32GB DDR5-6000 CL30 kit around $409.


Is the AM4 to AM5 upgrade worth it for gaming? If you run a Ryzen 3000/5000 chip and play CPU-bound games, yes. The 7700X3D delivers dramatically higher frame rates and steadier 1% lows than any AM4 non-X3D CPU.
