# 7700X3D vs 7800X3D vs 9800X3D: Which X3D Chip Should You Buy Today?

For the first time, AMD’s X3D lineup offers three eight-core gaming chips at three distinct prices: the brand-new Ryzen 7 7700X3D at $328.99, the fan-favorite 7800X3D at $416.00, and the flagship 9800X3D at $479.00. All three carry the same 96MB of stacked L3 cache that makes X3D synonymous with gaming performance. So where does your money actually go? Let us put them side by side.


## The Contenders


All three are Socket AM5, eight cores and sixteen threads, 120W TDP, with the identical 96MB 3D V-Cache slab. The differences come down to architecture generation, clock speed, and cache packaging. The 7700X3D and 7800X3D are Zen 4 siblings separated by boost clock; the 9800X3D is a Zen 5 part with AMD’s second-generation cache design, which places the cache under the compute die, allowing higher sustained clocks and full overclocking support.

## Deep Dive: Ryzen 7 7700X3D — The Disruptor


The newest and most affordable way into 96MB of 3D V-Cache.


The[7700X3D](https://www.newegg.com/ryzen-7-7700x3d-ryzen-7000-series-raphael-zen-4-socket-am5-amd-cpu/p/N82E16819113941?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_7700x3d-vs-7800x3d-vs-9800x3d-which-to-buy) gives up 500MHz of boost clock versus the 7800X3D, and in cache-sensitive games that costs it surprisingly little. XDA’s launch review measured 175 fps in Cyberpunk 2077 at 1080p Ultra and roughly 500 fps in Counter-Strike 2, praising the lowest package power draw the outlet had recorded in years. It runs cool enough for compact cases and modest air coolers.


## Deep Dive: Ryzen 7 7800X3D — The Squeezed Middle


The[7800X3D](https://www.newegg.com/amd-ryzen-7-7800x3d-ryzen-7-7000-series-raphael-zen-4-socket-am5/p/N82E16819113793?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_7700x3d-vs-7800x3d-vs-9800x3d-which-to-buy) has been the default gaming recommendation for two years, and it remains a superb chip. The problem is arithmetic: at $416.00 it now costs $87 more than the 7700X3D for a single-digit-percentage gaming lead, while sitting only $63 below the newer, faster 9800X3D. Unless you find it on a strong sale, the middle seat is the hardest to justify today.


## Deep Dive: Ryzen 7 9800X3D — The Flagship


Zen 5 plus second-generation V-Cache keeps the 9800X3D on top.


The[9800X3D](https://www.newegg.com/amd-ryzen-7-9000-series-ryzen-7-9800x3d-granite-ridge-zen-5-socket-am5-desktop-cpu-processor/p/N82E16819113877?utm_source=insider&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=insider_article_7700x3d-vs-7800x3d-vs-9800x3d-which-to-buy) is still the fastest widely available gaming CPU. Zen 5 cores, a 5.2GHz boost, and the under-die cache layout that permits real overclocking give it a clear edge, typically around 8-10% over the 7800X3D in reviews, and it holds high clocks under sustained load. If you refuse to leave performance on the table, this is the one.

## Frequently Asked Questions


Answers to the most common questions about choosing between AMD X3D CPUs.


Is the 7800X3D worth $87 more than the 7700X3D? For most gamers, no. The higher boost clock yields only single-digit-percentage gaming gains because both chips share the same 96MB cache. It becomes attractive only on a strong sale.


How much faster is the 9800X3D than the 7700X3D? Reviews place the 9800X3D roughly 10-15% ahead in CPU-limited gaming thanks to Zen 5 cores and higher clocks, for about $150 more at current Newegg pricing.


Do all three X3D chips use the same motherboards? Yes. All are Socket AM5 and work on A620 through X870E chipsets after a BIOS update, so the platform cost is identical.


Which X3D CPU is best for small form factor builds? The 7700X3D. Launch testing found it draws the least package power of the three and runs coolest, making it ideal for compact cases and modest coolers.
