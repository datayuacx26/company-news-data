# Bauma 2025: What to Expect at Enerpac’s Biggest Ever Stand

**Bauma 2025, the world’s leading construction machinery trade fair, is nearly here! From April 14 – 20, Munich will host this premier event for construction management, heavy lifting, manufacturing, and material processing industries.**


This time, Enerpac is making its biggest impact yet, with a record-breaking 500m² stand featuring 11 live demonstrations. If you’re attending to research equipment investments or expand your specialist know-how, make sure to visit the Enerpac stand FS1111/5 for an exclusive look at industry-leading lifting, material handling, and industrial tooling solutions.


#### **Why Visit the Enerpac Stand at Bauma 2025?**


Enerpac’s presence at Bauma 2025 is all about demonstrating how hydraulic solutions can streamline operations, improve safety, and boost productivity. Whether you’re looking for heavy lifting solutions, material handling innovations, or precision bolting tools, you’ll find something valuable.


## **Key Product Highlights at Bauma 2025**


Here’s a sneak peek at some of the must-see exhibits at the Enerpac stand organised by project type


### **NEW Smart Transporter Solutions & In-Factory Material Handling**


For efficient in-factory material handling and industrial moving, explore a range of solutions designed to improve workflow and load management.


**DTA Automated In-Factory Moving Solutions:** Witness the future of material handling with ‘DTA by Enerpac’ Self-Propelled Electric Transporter, one of the many load moving transporters available from the recently Enerpac-acquired DTA Systems. Learn about the AGV (Automatic Guided Vehicles) and ARM (Automated Mobile Robotic) technologies that transport loads up to 1,000 tons with millimetre precision. This is a major highlight on the Enerpac stand, demonstrating the innovation and flexibility Enerpac brings to lifting and moving loads on-site.


**ML40 Mini Gantry:** See the 40-ton entry-level gantry system in action, including how it teams up with the DTA Transporter – perfect for industrial movers seeking a compact, versatile and powerful lifting solution.


**SBL600 Gantry:** Explore the capabilities of the Super Boom Lift hydraulic gantry, offering a stable lifting platform for handling large loads up to 612 tons during factory moves and on-site equipment installations. At Bauma Enerpac will also be previewing the latest updated Wireless Gantry Controller. You can also preview Enerpac’s updated C5 gantry wireless controller that keeps the operator at a safe distance from the lifting operation.


**ETR Electric Trolley & EMLS Electric Load Skates:** Discover how these solutions can simplify the movement of heavy equipment and materials within your facility.


**ETT Turntable:** See how the ETT-Series Turntable, capable of handling loads up to 400T, can rotate heavy loads in tight spaces


### **Solutions For Infrastructure, Bridge Installation & Maintenance at Bauma 2025**


If your work involves infrastructure or bridge projects, here are some of the key technologies on display at Bauma:


**JS250 Jack-Up System:** Learn how this system can lift bridge decks and other heavy infrastructure components with precision and control.


**Strand Jacks** : Explore the power and versatility of strand jacks for infrastructure, offshore energy, tunnelling, and mining projects.


**EVO Synchronous Lifting Systems and Hydraulic Cylinders** : Learn how Enerpac EVO cylinders can be used for precise positioning and movement of heavy loads in bridge construction and maintenance.


**Low-Height Skidding System:** See how heavy loads can be moved horizontally in areas with low headroom clearance.


**SCJ-Series Cube Jacks:** See this compact jack-up system in action, ideal as a safer alternative to climbing jacks and cribbing systems.


### **Heavy Equipment Maintenance**


For those involved in heavy equipment maintenance, these systems offer solutions for safer and more efficient lifting and servicing.


**Cube Jacks for Maintenance:** See how the Enerpac SCJ-Series Cube Jacks, offers a safer, CE-certified approach to lifting and holding an entire cab, boom and arm, allowing the undercarriage to be safely removed.


**Pin Pullers:** Learn about the efficient and safe removal of joint pins from construction machinery using Enerpac’s PPH-Series Hydraulic Pin Pullers.


**BTW Battery Torque Wrench:** Experience the precision and speed of this battery-powered torque wrench, designed for cordless convenience and precision bolting.


**Pow’R-Riser Wheeled Jack** and the **LGH-Series 100-ton Hydraulic Puller** : Explore these tools for lifting loads in-situ, and for pulling heavy shaft-mounted heavy components.


### **Beyond Heavy Lifting at Bauma 2025- Tools for Every Job**


Precision and efficiency matters for smaller tasks too! Enerpac’s versatile hydraulic tools enhance productivity and reliability, ensuring you have the right equipment for every job.


**• Hydraulic Cylinders:** A broad selection for lifting, pushing, pulling, and positioning.
**• Hydraulic Pullers:** For removing stubborn parts and components.
• **Hydraulic Cutters:** Powerful cutting solutions for various materials.
**• Hydraulic Pumps:** A choice of reliable power sources for hydraulic tools.
• **Hydraulic Torque Wrenches:** Ensuring accurate and consistent torque application.
**• Toolbox Sets** : Convenient kits containing pumps and cylinders for on-the-go repairs.
