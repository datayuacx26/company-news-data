# Introducing Chatwoot Affiliates

Over the past few months, we’ve noticed something interesting, many of our paid customers have been bringing in new users. They’ve been telling their peers, communities, and clients about Chatwoot. We’ve never had an affiliate program before, but it felt like the right time to change that.


Today, we’re launching the **Chatwoot Affiliate Program** — a simple way to reward the people who already believe in us.


---


## **How it works**


It’s simple:


1. **Sign up** at[cloud-partners.chatwoot.com](https://cloud-partners.chatwoot.com/?ref=www-internal-blog.chatwoot.com) .
2. **Create your coupon code** — it’s yours to share.
3. **Invite your network** to start using Chatwoot Cloud.


When someone upgrade with your code:


- They’ll get **5% off** their Chatwoot Cloud subscription.
- You’ll earn **20% of the revenue** they bring in for the first 12 months.


## **Why are we doing this**


We’ve always believed in community-driven growth. Most of our milestones — from GitHub stars to customers — have come through word of mouth.


Lately, we’ve seen more teams joining because a customer or partner recommended us. This program is just a way of saying **thank you** to those who help us grow.


## **Who can join**


Anyone who believes in Chatwoot — creators, consultants, agencies, developers, or customers.


If you’re already using Chatwoot and love it, this is an easy way to give others a push to get started — and earn along the way.
