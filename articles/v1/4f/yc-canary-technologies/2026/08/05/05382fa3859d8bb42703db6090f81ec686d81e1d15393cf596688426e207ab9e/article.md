# Canary Technologies and Host Hotel Systems Announce New Integration for Smoother Hotel Operations

*New two-way PMS integration gives hotels across Portugal, Spain and Latin America access to Canary's full agentic AI platform in one connected stack.*


SAN FRANCISCO, CA (August 4, 2026) — Canary Technologies, the leading AI platform for hotel and guest management, today announced a new integration with Host Hotel Systems, providing access to Canary's award-winning guest engagement platform for hotels across Portugal, Spain and Latin America.


The two-way integration directly connects Host PMS with Canary, allowing hotels to automate guest and staff workflows for smoother hotel operations. Mutual customers gain access to Canary's suite of guest and staff management solutions — including Mobile Check-In, Dynamic Upsells and AI Guest Messaging — with real-time syncing. For example, once a reservation lands in Host PMS, the Canary platform handles pre-arrival check-in, in-stay communication, upsell delivery and checkout automatically with real-time deposit-posting and folio updates.


For hotels in Portugal, Spain and Latin America that currently rely on standalone tools for guest engagement, the integration offers a path to consolidating functionality inside a single, fully connected platform.


“Guest expectations have evolved,” said Manolo Diaz, Director of Sales, Southern Europe at Canary Technologies. “Today's guest wants to text the hotel, check in from their phone and be offered the right upgrade at the right moment. Delivering all of that from separate tools is where it gets complicated. Canary runs those workflows end-to-end, and this integration brings even greater connectivity to every hotelier on Host.”


"A property management system holds the operational record every hotel runs on, and every great integration should make that record even more valuable, not more complex,” said Pedro Lara, Head of Partnerships at Host. “Canary is one more piece of that record: adding it to the Host ecosystem lets hoteliers cover check-in, upselling and guest messaging, all while keeping operations connected."


The integration is available now for mutual customers. To learn more, visit[www.canarytechnologies.com](https://www.canarytechnologies.com/) .
