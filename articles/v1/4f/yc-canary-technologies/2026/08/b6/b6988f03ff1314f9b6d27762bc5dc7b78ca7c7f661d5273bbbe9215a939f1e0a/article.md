# Hard Rock Hotel Bali Modernizes Its Iconic Guest Experience With Canary Technologies

*The five-star Bali resort introduces a seamless, mobile-first guest journey with Canary’s AI-powered solutions.*


**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE** **— (August 3, 2026) —** Canary Technologies, the leading AI platform for hotel and guest management, today announced that Hard Rock Hotel Bali has selected Canary’s solutions to modernize the guest journey and elevate service across its world-renowned resort.


Located in the heart of Kuta, Hard Rock Hotel Bali is one of Indonesia’s most iconic hospitality destinations. Known for its vibrant atmosphere, entertainment-led experiences and family-friendly amenities, the resort welcomes travelers from around the world seeking a unique blend of relaxation, music and adventure. With Canary, Hard Rock Hotel Bali is creating a more engaging guest experience through a seamless, mobile-first journey from pre-arrival to departure.


“Hard Rock Hotel Bali has built a reputation for delivering memorable guest experiences that combine world-class hospitality with an energetic, entertainment-driven atmosphere,” said Daniel Mourad, General Manager, APAC at Canary Technologies. “With Canary’s platform, their team can streamline operations behind the scenes while creating more convenient, personalized and seamless experiences for guests throughout their stay.”


Hard Rock Hotel Bali is deploying Canary’s suite of guest engagement solutions across key guest touchpoints. Guests will enjoy a faster arrival and departure experience with Mobile Check-In and Smart Checkout, OCR ID verification and Tablet Registration. During the stay, Canary’s Digital Compendium and F&B Mobile Ordering provide guests with instant access to hotel information, dining options and services directly from their mobile device. And at all points of the guest journey, Canary’s AI Guest Messaging enables instant communication with real-time translation in 100+ languages.


“At Hard Rock Hotel Bali, we are committed to embracing technology that elevates the guest experience without compromising the personal touch that defines our brand,” said Shane Coates, General Manager of Hard Rock Hotel Bali. “By partnering with Canary Technologies, we can offer our guests a more seamless and convenient stay experience while empowering our team to deliver faster, more personalized service through innovative digital solutions.”
