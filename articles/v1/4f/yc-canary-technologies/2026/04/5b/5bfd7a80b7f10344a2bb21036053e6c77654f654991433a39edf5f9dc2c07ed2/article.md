# Sri Panwa Phuket Introduces Mobile-First Guest Engagement With Canary Technologies

*Ultra-luxury Thailand resort digitizes the guest journey with AI-powered, mobile-first guest technology.*


SAN FRANCISCO, CA (April 16, 2026) — Canary Technologies, the award-winning global leader in hotel guest management technology, today announced that Sri Panwa Phuket has adopted Canary’s solutions to bring a digital-first guest experience to its world-renowned luxury resort in Thailand.


Consistently ranked among the top five resorts in Thailand, Sri Panwa Phuket is a beachfront destination located on Cape Panwa. Known for its private pool villas, panoramic ocean views and highly personalized service, the resort delivers an exclusive experience for discerning global travelers seeking exceptional hospitality.


“Sri Panwa Phuket is redefining luxury hospitality with a guest experience that is both highly personalized and seamlessly modern,” said Daniel Mourad, General Manager, APAC at Canary Technologies. “With Canary’s platform, their team can connect with guests instantly, personalize every interaction and streamline operations behind the scenes — delivering exceptional service that matches the expectations of today’s luxury traveler.”


With Canary, Sri Panwa Phuket is digitizing the guest journey from discovery to post-stay. They’re using Canary’s suite of solutions to provide seamless arrivals, AI-powered communication and unrivaled personalization across every add-on offering.


“At Sri Panwa Phuket, we are committed to delivering a level of service that is both intuitive and exceptional,” said Janjira Wiwatwitayawong, Guest Experience Manager at Sri Panwa Phuket. “With Canary, we can anticipate guest needs, communicate effortlessly, and create a more seamless and personalized journey for every guest, all while maintaining the exclusivity and attention to detail our guests expect.”
