### What Is Guest Messaging Software?


Guest messaging software allows hotels to communicate with guests through SMS, WhatsApp and other digital messaging channels before, during and after their stay.


Hotel messaging platforms improve communication between hotels and guests by letting teams:


- Respond instantly to guest questions
- Automate common guest requests with AI
- Reduce front desk workload
- Coordinate service requests across departments
- Improve response times and guest satisfaction


AI guest messaging platforms like Canary allow hotels to manage conversations at scale while maintaining fast, personalized service for guests.
