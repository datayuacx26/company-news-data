**What Is Digital Tipping for Hotels?**


Digital tipping allows hotel guests to tip staff electronically without needing cash.


Digital tipping platforms enable guests to leave tips for services such as:


- Housekeeping
- Valet and bell staff
- Room service
- Spa and wellness services
- Concierge services


Modern digital tipping solutions integrate into the guest journey and offer tipping opportunities in-stay as well as through Mobile Checkout.
