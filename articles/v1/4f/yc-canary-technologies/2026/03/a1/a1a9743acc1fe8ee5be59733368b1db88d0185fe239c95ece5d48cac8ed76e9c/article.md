**What Is Digital Authorization for Hotels?**


Digital authorization allows hotels to securely collect and verify payment details without relying on manual or paper-based processes.


Modern digital authorization solutions allow hotels to:


- Replace credit card authorization forms with secure digital data capture
- Verify guest identity and payment information in advance
- Reduce fraud exposure and unauthorized transactions
- Store sensitive data in a PCI-compliant environment
- Streamline communication with guests for payment collection


By digitizing authorization processes, hotels eliminate common vulnerabilities tied to faxed or emailed forms.
