**What Is Hotel Upselling Software?**


Hotel upselling software allows hotels to present guests with personalized upgrade and add-on offers throughout the guest journey.


Upselling platforms generate ancillary revenue by offering services such as:


- Room upgrades
- Early check-in or late checkout
- Spa treatments and dining experiences
- Parking and transportation services
- Special packages and amenities


Modern hotel upselling platforms automate these offers and present them through digital channels such as pre-arrival emails, mobile check-in and in-stay messaging.
