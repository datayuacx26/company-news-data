# Hotel Review Response Examples with Templates

A hotel’s official star rating only counts for so much if its online reviews are poor. Reviews are one of the most valuable sources of unfiltered guest feedback and[what people value most](https://www.statista.com/chart/28992/influenceing-factors-in-accommodation-booking-online/) when booking — so how you respond to them is a big deal!


The way you respond to reviews has the potential to boost your hotels’ reputation…or make you look bitter and closed off to feedback. Every response you publish shapes how future guests perceive your brand, signaling levels of attentiveness, professionalism and a commitment to service. To make responding to reviews less complex and time-consuming, we’re sharing multiple hotel review response examples for your immediate use in this guide.


**Key highlights:**


- Hotel reviews are publicly shared guest feedback about their stay, covering service, amenities and overall experience.
- Responding to reviews can strengthen a hotel’s reputation while turning guest feedback into actionable operational insights.
- Structured response templates help teams reply efficiently while maintaining a consistent brand voice.
- Canary Technologies enables hotels to centralize guest communication and transform reviews into improved guest experiences across the journey.


## Why Responding to Hotel Reviews Matters


Whether it's a rave review or one that makes you want to hit “delete,” your response to guest reviews is a public affair. Every one shows future guests how seriously you take feedback and whether you follow through on your service standards. Plus, when you consistently engage with reviews, you turn real-time[guest feedback](https://www.canarytechnologies.com/post/hotel-guest-feedback-guide) into insights to improve operations and customer satisfaction long-term.


### You Improve Hotel Review Scores and Visibility


According to[Harvard Business Review](https://hbr.org/2018/02/study-replying-to-customer-reviews-results-in-better-ratings) , increase the number of reviews by 12% and increase ratings by 0.12 stars. This then drives what[Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?sjid=15909266121019757482-NA#zippy=%2Cmanage-respond-to-reviews%2Crelevance%2Cdistance%2Cprominence) calls “prominence,” which is one of three factors that play a direct role in how your property ranks in searches.


Platforms prioritize active, engaged businesses, meaning that by consistently replying to[hotel Google reviews](https://www.canarytechnologies.com/post/hotel-google-reviews) , you can improve both visibility and credibility with prospective guests.


Beyond algorithms, responses influence human behavior. Travelers are more likely to book a hotel that demonstrates attentiveness and professionalism, especially when management engages thoughtfully with both positive and negative feedback. Consistent responses:


- Signal to platforms that your listing is active and trustworthy
- Increase booking confidence
- Encourage more guests to leave reviews
- Demonstrate accountability to prospective guests


### You Strengthen Guest Loyalty Through Personalization


A personalized response means being specific in addressing topics raised, not just using guests’ names. When hotels acknowledge specific details and tailor replies, it creates a sense of recognition that strengthens emotional connection and loyalty.


This approach is especially powerful when paired with the consistent practice of[evaluating guest ratings](https://www.canarytechnologies.com/post/evaluating-guest-ratings) . By understanding trends and what matters most to guests, teams can craft responses that feel authentic rather than transactional. Personalizing your response:


- Displays the level of hospitality and care guests can expect from management
- Builds stronger emotional connections with guests
- Reinforces brand voice and service standards
- Re-engages guests after departure


### You Identify Operational Issues from Guest Feedback


[Reviews](https://www.canarytechnologies.com/post/hotel-review-management-checklist) are among the most accessible and honest sources of operational insight. When aggregated and analyzed, guest feedback can reveal recurring friction points that might otherwise go unnoticed through internal reporting alone.


By responding to reviews, you’ll naturally detect patterns in feedback, even before using tech for support. And once you’re attuned to feedback, you can prioritize improvements that have the greatest impact on guest satisfaction — from housekeeping consistency to front desk responsiveness. Over time, this:


- Surfaces recurring service or operational issues
- Provides real-time insight into evolving guest expectations
- Helps prioritize improvements based on impact
- Aligns teams around data-driven decision-making


### You Protect Revenue by Addressing Negative Feedback Quickly


If negative feedback was presented to you in the lobby with other guests watching, how would you respond? It’s the same online. Having a strong response strategy can turn dissatisfied guests into advocates, and “bystanders” (future guests reading your reviews), into admirers of how you handled the situation.


Timely, thoughtful responses can significantly mitigate risk when it comes to managing guest perception. When handled well, negative feedback becomes an opportunity to demonstrate service recovery and reinforce trust. Acting quickly and adequately helps:


- Reduce the impact of negative reviews on booking decisions
- Demonstrate transparency and accountability
- Increase chances of guest recovery and retention
- Protect long-term revenue and brand perception


## Positive Hotel Review Response Examples


Responding to positive hotel reviews is your chance to reinforce what your hotel does best while encouraging guests to return. It’s a chance to shine, and a little preparation goes a long way. By having a handful of positive review response templates in your back pocket, you can consistently highlight brand strengths and build loyalty, while supporting long-term[hotel brand reputation management](https://www.canarytechnologies.com/post/hotel-reputation-management) .


When done well and with intention, every response to a review becomes a subtle marketing moment, showing future guests exactly what they can expect from your property. Consider using specific words you want associated with your brand as you build on the templates shared below to craft your own response toolbox.


### 5-Star Review with Detailed Praise


When guests leave detailed praise, they’re signaling exactly what stood out. This is valuable insight you can reinforce in your response to amplify those strengths for future guests.


Example Guest Review:


“Absolutely loved our stay! The front desk team was incredibly welcoming, the room was large and spotless and the breakfast selection exceeded expectations. We’ll definitely be back.”


Example Review Response:


“Thank you for your wonderful review! We’re thrilled to hear that our front desk team made you feel welcome and that you enjoyed your room and our breakfast. We truly appreciate your kind words and can’t wait to welcome you back for another great stay.”


Why This Response Works:


- Reinforces specific strengths mentioned by the guest
- Shows appreciation without sounding generic
- Encourages a repeat visit


Template You Can Copy:


“Thank you for your kind words! We’re so glad you enjoyed \[specific mention\]. We appreciate your feedback and hope to welcome you back again soon.”


### Response to 5-star Review, No Written Comments


Even when guests leave only a rating, it’s still important to respond. Acknowledging these reviews shows attentiveness and appreciation, even without detailed feedback.


Example Guest Review:


⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐


Example Review Response:


“Thank you for the 5-star rating! We’re so glad you had a great experience with us and hope to welcome you back again soon.”


Why This Response Works:


- Acknowledges the guest despite limited detail
- Reinforces a positive impression
- Keeps communication consistent across all reviews


Template You Can Copy:


“Thank you for your 5-star rating! We appreciate it and hope to welcome you back again soon.”


### Praise About Your Staff


When guests call out team members, it’s an opportunity to recognize employees and reinforce a service-first culture. Public acknowledgment also strengthens brand perception.


Example Guest Review:


“The concierge team went above and beyond to make our trip special. Truly exceptional service!”


Example Review Response:


“Thank you for your kind words! We’re delighted to hear our concierge team made your stay memorable. We have shared your feedback with them and they were thrilled to hear it. We hope to welcome you back soon!”


Why This Response Works:


- Highlights team performance
- Reinforces service culture
- Shows appreciation to both guests and staff


Template You Can Copy:


“Thank you for recognizing our team! We’re happy \[staff/service mention\] made an impact. We’ll be sure to share your feedback and look forward to your next visit.”


### First-Time Guest Review


First-time guests represent a major opportunity. A thoughtful response can turn a one-time stay into a long-term relationship.


Example Guest Review:


“This was our first time staying here and we were really impressed. Great location and friendly staff!”


Example Review Response:


“Thank you for choosing to stay with us for the first time! We’re so glad you enjoyed our location and team. We hope this is the first of many stays with us and look forward to welcoming you back soon.”


Why This Response Works:


- Acknowledges the first-time experience
- Encourages repeat visits
- Builds a personal connection


Template You Can Copy:


“Thank you for staying with us for the first time! We’re glad you enjoyed \[specific mention\] and hope to welcome you back again soon.”


### Positive Review with Minor Suggestions


Some positive reviews include small suggestions for improvement. The key is to acknowledge them without diminishing the overall positive experience.


Example Guest Review:


“Great stay overall! The room was comfortable and clean, though check-in took a bit longer than expected.”


Example Review Response:


“Thank you for your thoughtful feedback! We’re so glad you enjoyed your room and overall stay. We appreciate your note about check-in and will share it with our team as we continue to improve. We hope to welcome you back again soon.”


Why This Response Works:


- Balances appreciation with accountability
- Shows commitment to improvement
- Maintains a positive tone throughout


Template You Can Copy:


“Thank you for your feedback! We’re glad you enjoyed \[positive aspect\] and appreciate your suggestion regarding \[area of improvement\]. We’re always working to enhance the guest experience and hope to see you again soon.”


## Negative Hotel Review Response Examples


When a guest voices a grievance, the management path usually goes one of three ways: instant defense, acknowledgement or concrete engagement and resolution. That is, unless you count “no response” as a fourth option. In reality, your internal guidebook to handling these situations needs nuance. Strong negative review response templates guide your team in crucial moments with options that demonstrate accountability and focus on both the guest and[managing reputation](https://www.canarytechnologies.com/use-cases/reputation-management) for your hotel.


### Long Check-In Line Feedback


Long check-in lines often point to deeper workflow or staffing inefficiencies. Responding thoughtfully shows guests you’re aware of the issue and actively working to improve the arrival experience.


Example Guest Review:


“Waited over 30 minutes just to check in. Completely unacceptable after a long day of travel.”


Example Review Response:


“Thank you for your feedback. We sincerely apologize for the wait you experienced during check-in and understand how frustrating that can be after traveling. Our arrival processes are under active review to improve efficiency during peak times. We appreciate your patience and hope to provide a smoother experience on your next visit.”


Why This Response Works:


- Acknowledges the issue directly
- Shows empathy without defensiveness
- Signals operational follow-up


Template You Can Copy:


“We apologize for your experience with \[issue\]. We understand how frustrating this can be and are actively working to improve \[insert example of actions taken\]. Thank you for your feedback, and we hope to welcome you back for a better experience.”


### Cleanliness Issue


Example Guest Review:


“The room didn’t feel clean when we arrived. Definitely not what I expected.”


Example Review Response:


“Thank you for bringing this to our attention. We sincerely apologize that your room did not meet our cleanliness standards. This is not the experience we aim to provide, and have addressed this with our housekeeping team to ensure it does not happen again. We appreciate your feedback and hope to have the opportunity to welcome you back.”


Why This Response Works:


- Takes responsibility for the issue
- Reinforces brand standards
- Indicates corrective action


Template You Can Copy:


“We apologize that your experience did not meet our standards. We are addressing this with our team to ensure improvements are made \[or call the guest for more information and indicate in the response gratitude for their additional input and share how you have used it\]. Thank you for your feedback.”


### Noise Complaint


Noise complaints can strongly influence booking decisions, as guests prioritize comfort and rest. Addressing these concerns shows future guests that you take disruptions seriously and whether these are exceptions.


Example Guest Review:


“Could hear an entire wedding party in the hallway and neighboring rooms. Didn’t sleep well at all.”


Example Review Response:


“We’re very sorry to hear about the noise disturbances during your stay. We understand how important a restful environment is and regret that we fell short. We are reviewing room allocation of wedding groups with our team to minimize disruptions for our guests. Thank you for bringing this to your attention.”


Why This Response Works:


- Acknowledges guest discomfort
- Reinforces commitment to guest experience
- Signals intent to improve


Template You Can Copy:


“We apologize for the disturbance you experienced. Guest comfort is very important to us, and we are working to address this issue \[by doing this\]. Thank you for your feedback.”


### 1-Star Review That Is Factually Incorrect


When a review contains inaccuracies, it’s important to correct the record without escalating the situation. Maintaining a calm, professional tone protects your brand while providing clarity.


Example Guest Review:


“This hotel doesn’t even offer Wi-Fi and the staff refused to help.”


Example Review Response:


“Thank you for your feedback. We’re sorry to hear about your experience and would like to clarify that we do offer complimentary Wi-Fi for all guests. We regret any confusion and are reviewing your concerns with our team to ensure better communication and signage moving forward. We appreciate your feedback.”


Why This Response Works:


- Corrects misinformation tactfully
- Avoids sounding defensive
- Maintains professionalism


Template You Can Copy:


“Thank you for your feedback. We’d like to clarify \[factual point\] and apologize for any confusion. We are reviewing this with our team to improve communication.”


### Fake or Malicious Review


In cases of clearly fraudulent or malicious reviews, responses should remain professional while escalating internally and through the review platform.


Example Guest Review:


“This place is a scam. Don’t stay here.”


Example Review Response:


“Thank you for your feedback. We take all reviews seriously but are unable to locate a record of your stay. We encourage you to contact us directly so we can better understand your concerns. We are also reviewing this with the platform to ensure accuracy.”


Why This Response Works:


- Maintains professionalism
- Flags potential inaccuracy
- Signals escalation without confrontation


Template You Can Copy:


“Thank you for your feedback. We are unable to verify this experience and encourage you to contact us directly. We are also reviewing this with the platform to ensure accuracy.”

## Best Practices for Writing Hotel Review Responses


You don’t have to reply to every response; in fact,[Cornell](https://sha.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2019/03/anderson-engaged-consumers.pdf) found that responding to more than 40% yields diminishing returns. However, a consistent and intentional approach to review responses reinforces your brand while supporting broader goals around[managing hotel guest experience](https://www.canarytechnologies.com/post/hotel-guest-experience-management) . The most effective strategies balance speed, personalization and operational follow-through.


### 1. Personalize Every Review Reply


Personalized responses show guests that their feedback was genuinely heard. Reference specific details from the review (positive or negative) to make your response human and show that your team is paying attention.


This level of personalization is also an opportunity to strengthen your brand voice by restating your hotel’s standards. This makes each response a meaningful interaction rather than a transactional reply, building stronger relationships with both previous and future guests. Personalize your reply by:


- Referencing specific details from the guest’s stay
- Using the guest’s name when available
- Matching tone to the sentiment of the review
- Using language that aligns with your brand standards


### 2. Respond Within 24–48 Hours


Hoteliers already know timeliness is critical when it comes to review responses: the amount of time to respond is trending down globally, and currently at three days, according to[Shiji](https://insights.shijigroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Shiji-Reviewpro-Reputation-Q2-2025-Guest-Experience-Benchmark-Update-Infographics.pdf) . Not only do quick replies demonstrate a team attuned to its guests, they also show both the reviewer and future guests that your team is actively engaged.


Fast responses are especially important for negative feedback, where delays can amplify dissatisfaction. A prompt reply helps contain potential reputation damage by swiftly employing service recovery efforts. Quickly responding:


- Shows guests their feedback is a priority
- Helps mitigate impact of negative reviews
- Improves perception of responsiveness
- Encourages ongoing guest engagement


### 3. Avoid Copy-Paste Language


While templates are helpful, overusing identical language can make responses feel impersonal. Guests can quickly recognize generic replies, which may weaken trust and undermine your[online review management](https://www.canarytechnologies.com/post/hotel-online-review-management) strategy.


Instead, use templates as a framework and tailor each response to reflect the specifics of the review. This balance allows teams to stay efficient without sacrificing authenticity. When writing a reply:


- Use templates as a starting point, not a final answer
- Vary phrasing to avoid repetition
- Customize at least one sentence per response
- Maintain brand voice while sounding natural


### 4. Never Sound Defensive


Don’t be defensive — that includes reciting policy and placing blame. Even when feedback feels unfair, responses need to exude calm, be professional and provide a solution.


By focusing on empathy and accountability, you demonstrate integrity and reinforce trust. The goal isn’t to “win” the interaction, it’s to show that your hotel values guest feedback and continuous improvement. Make sure to:


- Acknowledge concerns without arguing
- Avoid blaming the guest or external factors
- Keep tone calm and respectful
- Focus on resolution and improvement


### 5. Close the Loop Internally


Responding to reviews is only half the equation. The real value comes from acting on the feedback. Depending on the issue, you may be able to investigate or take action internally first, and then respond. Other times, sharing insights with relevant teams will happen after a response to prevent recurring issues at the operational level.


Closing the loop internally can enable more meaningful responses and prevent repeat complaints. Over time, this creates a stronger alignment between guest expectations and actual delivery. Act on issues by:


- Promptly sharing feedback with department leaders
- Tracking recurring issues and trends
- Implementing changes based on guest input
- Using insights to improve training and processes
