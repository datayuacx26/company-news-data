# HEI Hotels & Resorts Supercharges Ancillary Revenue Streams with Canary Technologies

*Leading U.S. hotel investment and management company brings personalized, digital add-on offers to guests.*


SAN FRANCISCO, CA (June 4, 2026) — Canary Technologies, the award-winning global leader in hotel guest management technology, today announced that HEI Hotels & Resorts has selected its Dynamic Upsells solution to unlock new revenue opportunities and enhance the guest experience across its portfolio.


HEI Hotels & Resorts is one of the nation’s leading privately held hotel investment and third-party management companies, operating a portfolio of 100+ properties across the United States. Known for its operational expertise and owner-focused approach, HEI continuously invests in technology that increases performance while elevating the guest journey.


“HEI Hotels & Resorts is focused on driving strong ancillary revenue at scale,” said DJ Singh, VP of Global Sales at Canary Technologies. “With Canary’s Dynamic Upsells, HEI can monetize demand more effectively by presenting guests with timely, relevant offers that increase add-on revenue while keeping operations efficient.”


Canary’s Dynamic Upsells solutions lets hotels present guests with relevant upsell offers throughout the guest journey, from pre-arrival to departure. By digitizing and automating add-ons, HEI’s hotels offer guests the ability to customize their stay without adding operational complexity for staff.


“At HEI, we’re always looking for smart, scalable ways to drive revenue and enhance the guest experience,” said Jeremy See, Chief Commercial Officer at HEI Hotels & Resorts. “Canary’s Dynamic Upsells allows us to monetize demand more effectively while providing meaningful add-ons that make guest stays more personal.”
