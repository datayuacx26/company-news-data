# Canary Technologies and Ulyses Cloud by Septeo Partner to Bring Hospitality-Specific AI to Properties Across Spain

*This integration connects Canary’s agentic AI-powered solutions with one of Spain’s leading cloud-based PMS providers.*


SAN FRANCISCO, CA (June 29, 2026) — Canary Technologies, the award-winning global leader in AI-powered guest management technology, today announced a partnership with Ulyses PMS by Septeo, a leading cloud-based Property Management System (PMS) provider in Spain.


Hotels using Ulyses PMS by Septeo can now seamlessly connect Canary's hospitality AI solutions with their PMS to streamline operations, automate repetitive workflows and elevate the guest experience. The partnership further expands Canary's presence in Spain's growing hospitality market, where hotels are increasingly investing in automation to improve efficiency and guest engagement.


"Spain’s hoteliers are increasingly looking for technology partners that deliver both operational efficiency and elevated guest experiences,” said Manuel de la Torre, Head of Sales EMEA at Canary Technologies. "This partnership strengthens our ability to support the Spanish hospitality market with a seamless connection between Canary’s agentic solutions and the Ulyses PMS."
