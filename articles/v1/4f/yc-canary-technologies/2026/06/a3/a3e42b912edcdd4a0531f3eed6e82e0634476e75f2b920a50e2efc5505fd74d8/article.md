# GreenStar Hotels Elevates Sustainable Hospitality with Canary Technologies

*Finland's first carbon-neutral hotel chain elevates the guest experience with Canary's AI-powered solutions.*


SAN FRANCISCO, CA (June 30, 2026) — Canary Technologies, the award-winning global leader in hotel guest management technology, today announced that GreenStar Hotels has selected Canary’s suite of hospitality technology solutions to modernize operations and elevate the guest experience across its portfolio.


GreenStar Hotels is Finland’s first carbon-neutral hotel chain and operates five hotels across the country. Known for combining environmentally responsible hospitality with affordability and comfort, the company has built a distinctive brand around sustainable travel. By adopting Canary’s solutions, GreenStar is enhancing the guest journey while empowering staff with modern, digital-first tools.


“GreenStar Hotels is providing exceptional hospitality by using technology that further advances their mission,” said Manuel de la Torre, Head of Sales EMEA at Canary Technologies. “GreenStar has built something distinctive: hospitality that’s both responsible and exceptional,” said Manuel de la Torre, Head of Sales EMEA at Canary Technologies. “Canary gives their team the tools to deepen guest connections, unlock new revenue, spend less time on manual tasks and invest time in the moments that matter.”


GreenStar Hotels has deployed a suite of Canary solutions across key guest touchpoints. Guests can check in and check out digitally, communicate with the hotel instantly in 100+ languages via AI Guest Messaging, access property information through Canary’s Digital Compendium, and recognize staff with Digital Tipping. Canary’s Dynamic Upsells surface relevant add-ons at the right moment, driving ancillary revenue while keeping the experience seamless.


“At GreenStar Hotels, we are always looking for ways to improve the guest experience while staying true to our commitment to sustainable hospitality,” said Harri Nevalainen, Business Director at GreenStar Hotels. “Canary’s technology helps us create a more convenient, connected guest experience while enabling our teams to operate more efficiently. We believe this investment will help us continue raising the standard for modern, responsible hospitality.”
