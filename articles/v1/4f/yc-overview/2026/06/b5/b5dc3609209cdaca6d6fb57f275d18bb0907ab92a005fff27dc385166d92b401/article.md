# AI Vision Inspection for Military and Defense Manufacturing

Defense manufacturing sits at the intersection of two hard requirements. Parts must meet zero-defect standards because failures are not an option, and the technical data behind those parts is export-controlled and must stay protected. AI vision inspection addresses both, as long as it runs in the right place.


Overview.ai brings high-accuracy defect detection to defense lines while keeping every image and model on-device. Because inference runs on a built-in NVIDIA GPU in each camera, controlled data never leaves your facility. For the compliance side of that story, see our guide to[ITAR-compliant AI vision inspection](https://www.overview.ai/blog/itar-compliant-ai-vision-inspection/) .


## Why Defense Manufacturing Is Different


In most factories, an escaped defect is a warranty cost. In defense work, it can be a safety, mission, and contractual failure. That raises the bar on inspection accuracy, on the documentation behind every accepted part, and on who is allowed to see the data involved. An inspection system for defense has to be precise, auditable, and contained.


Overview.ai is built for exactly that combination. Models train on as few as five images, inspections run at line speed, and every result is recorded locally for traceability. The same edge architecture that delivers the accuracy also keeps controlled technical data inside your facility.


## Defense Manufacturing Applications


### Aerospace and Defense Fasteners


Fasteners and rivets are everywhere in defense assemblies, and a single missing, misseated, or defective fastener can compromise a structure. AI vision verifies presence, seating, and surface condition at speed. See our detailed look at[aerospace fastener and rivet inspection](https://www.overview.ai/blog/ai-aerospace-fastener-rivet-inspection/) .


### Munitions and Ordnance Components


Munitions components demand tight tolerances and flawless surfaces. AI vision catches cracks, deformation, fill and seal issues, and dimensional variation that manual inspection misses under high volume.


### Defense Electronics and PCBs


Circuit boards in defense systems must be inspected for solder defects, missing or misplaced components, and signs of counterfeit parts. AI vision handles high-density boards and flags anomalies that put high-reliability electronics at risk.


### Machined and Airframe Components


Precision-machined metal parts for airframes, missile bodies, and structural assemblies are inspected for surface defects, machining errors, and dimensional accuracy, including features that are difficult to gauge by hand.


### Welds, Composites, and Assemblies


Weld quality, composite layup, connectors, and harness routing all carry mission risk if they go wrong. AI vision provides consistent, repeatable inspection across these complex assemblies, with results documented for every unit.
