# Summerlin® and Bridgeland® Rank Among Top 15 Best-Selling Master Planned Communities Nationwide

Summerlin® and Bridgeland® Rank Among Top 15 Best-Selling Master Planned Communities Nationwide


**Strong home sales underscore continued demand for amenity-rich, thoughtfully planned communities in two of the nation’s fastest-growing regions**


**THE WOODLANDS, TX (January 8, 2026)** – Summerlin® and Bridgeland®, two award-winning communities in the Howard Hughes Communities portfolio, have been ranked among the nation’s top 15 best-selling master planned communities (MPCs) for 2025, according to the year-end report released by national real estate consulting firm RCLCO. Summerlin, located in Las Vegas, ranked #10 nationally with 962 home sales, while Bridgeland, in the Greater Houston area, ranked #11 with 812 home sales. The rankings underscore the continued strength of well-executed master planned communities in two of the country’s fastest-growing regions.


“Strong sales at Summerlin and Bridgeland are not about chasing short-term demand. They reflect decades of intentional planning and long-term stewardship,” said David R. O’Reilly, Chief Executive Officer of Howard Hughes. “When communities are designed around livability, access to nature, and real everyday needs, people respond. These rankings affirm that thoughtful planning, carried out with discipline and care over time, continues to create places people genuinely want to call home.”


**SUMMERLIN®**


Summerlin continues to rank among Nevada’s top-selling communities, with 962 new homes sold in 2025.


Now entering its 36th year of development, Summerlin marked another year of sustained residential, commercial, and community growth, extending a decades-long trajectory defined by disciplined planning and long-term value creation.


Within the residential sector, 2025 saw the opening of 10 new neighborhoods offering dozens of new floorplans, alongside the close-out of six neighborhoods, reflecting both strong demand and healthy absorption. New neighborhoods expanded housing options across multiple villages and districts, including Raven Crest by Toll Brothers, Mockingbird and Sandpiper by Lennar in Kestrel and Kestrel Commons; and Ashland by Taylor Morrison, SHAWOOD at Arcadia, Glenrock by Toll Brothers, Caldwell Park by KB Home, Carlisle by Tri Pointe Homes, Primrose Park by Richmond American Homes, and Brantley by Pulte Homes in Grand Park village.


The community also debuted Astra, a custom homesite enclave set against the La Madre Peaks. Located at one of the highest elevations in the Las Vegas Valley, Astra will ultimately feature 167 expansive custom homesites across 171 acres, offering a rare connection to the surrounding desert landscape.


Summerlin’s commitment to parks, open space, and outdoor connectivity continued in 2025 with the opening of several new amenities, including the final phase of Redpoint Arroyo; Kestrel Creek Arroyo; The Hub in Kestrel Commons; Terrace Park; and the first phase of Grand Park, Council Park, in Grand Park village.


“Summerlin continues to move forward at a steady and thoughtful pace, delivering a wide range of housing options alongside expansive parks and open spaces that deepen residents’ connection to nature,” said Jose Bustamante, President of the Nevada Region for Howard Hughes Communities. “Decades of disciplined planning have established Summerlin as Southern Nevada’s premier master planned community and a national model for long-term development. Continued residential demand, combined with premier retailers, luxury brands, new Class A office space, and sustained interest from growing companies, positions Summerlin for another strong year in 2026.”


Looking ahead, Summerlin is positioned for continued momentum in 2026 with the opening of 11 new neighborhoods, additional parks and open space, and anticipated retail and office tenant announcements, reinforcing its role as the premier community in Las Vegas and a key driver of regional economic growth.


**BRIDGELAND®**


Bridgeland sold 812 homes in 2025, ranking as the #3 best-selling master planned community in Texas and #11 nationally, reflecting sustained demand for diverse residential offerings in Northwest Houston.


Now in its 20th year of development, Bridgeland continues to expand housing options while maintaining a deliberate, thoughtfully paced growth strategy. In 2025, the community opened three new schools, introduced two new residential product lines, and added more than 40 acres of parks and recreational amenities, further reinforcing its focus on livability, connectivity, and long-term community building.


Complementing this residential momentum, Bridgeland Central®, the community’s 925-acre mixed-use district, delivered a year of significant commercial progress. Nearly 60,000 square feet of new walkable office and retail space opened in 2025, introducing new amenities and services to the Northwest Houston region. One Bridgeland Green, Greater Houston’s first Class A mass timber office building, opened in December and now serves as the corporate headquarters for CrossCover Insurance. Additional retail and restaurant openings, including Houston favorite Jonathan’s the rub, further enhanced daily convenience for residents and employees. Bridgeland also broke ground on its first build-to-suit medical office building for Memorial Hermann, scheduled to open in summer 2026.


“Northwest Houston is experiencing remarkable growth, and Bridgeland sits at the center of that momentum,” said Jim Carman, President of the Texas Region for Howard Hughes Communities. “Residents are drawn to the combination of diverse housing choices, meaningful green space, and emerging retail and office destinations. Together, these elements are redefining residential living in the region.”


Today, Bridgeland is home to nearly 30,000 residents across five villages and is projected to grow to approximately 70,000 residents at full buildout.


**About Summerlin®**
Summerlin is a Howard Hughes community spanning 22,500-acres on the western edge of Las Vegas. Home to nearly 130,000 residents, Summerlin also features a robust commercial offering including Downtown Summerlin®, a premier retail, dining, sports, and office destination. The community boasts more than 300 parks, 200 miles of trails, award-winning schools, golf courses, and leading healthcare facilities. Summerlin is pre-certified under the LEED for Cities and Communities program, underscoring its role as a national model for sustainable, large-scale development. Named “Master Planned Community of the Year” by the National Home Builders Association, Summerlin continues to set the standard for integrated residential and commercial development. Learn more at[Summerlin.com](http://summerlin.com/) .


**About Bridgeland®**


Bridgeland is a Howard Hughes community spanning 11,500-acres in Cypress, Texas. Consistently ranked among the nation’s top-selling communities, Bridgeland was named the National Association of Home Builders’ 2024 Master Planned Community of the Year. Home to thousands of residents and designed for long-term commercial growth, Bridgeland includes more than 3,000 acres of parks, trails, and lakes and is pre-certified under the LEED for Cities and Communities program, demonstrating its commitment to sustainable planning and development. The community’s continued evolution is propelled by its growing 925-acre urban hub, Bridgeland Central®, which will deliver retail, office, healthcare, cultural amenities, and more, to the community that is redefining the Northwest Houston region. Learn more at[Bridgeland.com](https://www.bridgeland.com/) .
