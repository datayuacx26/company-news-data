# Amtech Systems Announces Participation in Canaccord Genuity’s 46th Annual Growth Conference

# Amtech Systems Announces Participation in Canaccord Genuity’s 46th Annual Growth Conference


[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_113ec80eb92e20ca00f3ab72c78c0461/amtechsystems/news/2026-07-14_Amtech_Systems_Announces_Participation_in_400.pdf) July 14, 2026 9:00 am EDT


TEMPE, Ariz.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Amtech Systems, Inc. ("Amtech") (NASDAQ: ASYS), a manufacturer of equipment and consumables enabling AI semiconductor device packaging and advanced substrate fabrication, today announced it will participate in Canaccord Genuity's 46th Annual Growth Conference, set to take place in-person from August 11-13, 2026, at the InterContinental Hotel in Boston, MA. The Company is scheduled to present on Wednesday, August 12, 2026 at 11:00 am Eastern time. Management will be meeting with institutional investors on August 11 and 12, 2026.


The investor presentation to be referenced during the event will be made available within the investor relations section of Amtech’s website on the morning of the events. To access the presentation, please[click here](https://www.amtechsystems.com/investors/investor-presentations) . The presentation will be webcast for Canaccord Genuity clients who will not be able to attend the event in-person. For those attending the conference who wish to schedule a one-on-one meeting with Amtech, please make a request with the event host or reach out to Amtech’s investor relations representative, Jordan Darrow of Darrow Associates, using the contact information provided in this press release.


**About Amtech Systems, Inc.**


Amtech Systems, Inc. (NASDAQ: ASYS) provides equipment, consumables and services for AI semiconductor device packaging and advanced wafer substrate fabrication. Our products include advanced packaging and electronics assembly equipment for applications such as AI GPUs and advanced automotive electronics. Consumable and other solutions are used in fabricating semiconductor devices, such as silicon carbide (SiC) and silicon (Si) power devices, digital and analog devices, power electronic packages, advanced semiconductor packages and electronic assemblies. We sell these products to semiconductor device and module manufacturers worldwide, particularly in Asia, North America and Europe. To learn more about Amtech, please visit our website at[https://www.amtechsystems.com](https://www.amtechsystems.com/)


.
