# MID PENN BANK HIRES DANIEL DEVINE AS SENIOR CASH MANAGEMENT OPERATIONS AND PRODUCT MANAGER

# MID PENN BANK HIRES DANIEL DEVINE AS SENIOR CASH MANAGEMENT OPERATIONS AND PRODUCT MANAGER


Posted on Apr 17th, 2026


Millersburg, Pa. – April 17, 2026. Mid Penn Bank (the “Bank”), a wholly-owned subsidiary of Mid Penn Bancorp, Inc. (“Mid Penn”) (NASDAQ: MPB), is pleased to announce that Daniel “Dan” Devine has joined the Bank as senior vice president and senior cash management operations and product manager. Based in Harrisburg at the Bank’s administrative office, Devine will lead operations for the Bank’s cash management team and support product initiatives. He will report directly to Chief Operating Officer Dana Stewart.


“We are pleased to welcome Dan to the team,” Stewart said. “His background in payment systems and operations will support our cash management functions and play a key role in strengthening our processes and customer experience.”


Devine brings more than 20 years of experience in cash management, payment operations and process modernization. Most recently, he served as senior vice president and head of payment strategy and innovation at Customers Bank, where he led efforts to modernize payment systems and support the launch of new payment offerings while improving day-to-day operations. He previously held leadership roles at Silvergate Bank and Santander Bank, where he oversaw payment operations and supported improvements that helped streamline workflows and reduce exceptions.


### About Mid Penn Bank


Mid Penn Bank, headquartered in Millersburg, Pennsylvania, has been serving the community since 1868. The Bank operates 62 retail locations throughout Pennsylvania and central and southern New Jersey, and has total assets of approximately $7 billion. Mid Penn offers a comprehensive portfolio of financial products and services to meet the banking needs of the communities it serves.
