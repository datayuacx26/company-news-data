# MID PENN BANK JOINS THE INDEPENDENCE BLUE CROSS BROAD STREET RUN AS OFFICIAL BANKING PARTNER

# MID PENN BANK JOINS THE INDEPENDENCE BLUE CROSS BROAD STREET RUN AS OFFICIAL BANKING PARTNER


Posted on Apr 20th, 2026


Millersburg, Pa. – April 20, 2026. Mid Penn Bank (the “Bank”), a wholly-owned subsidiary of Mid Penn Bancorp, Inc. (“Mid Penn”) (NASDAQ: MPB), is proud to serve as the Official Banking Partner of the Independence Blue Cross Broad Street Run, one of the largest 10-mile races in the country, which will take place on May 3, 2026, in Philadelphia.


As a Silver-level sponsor, Mid Penn Bank will participate in race weekend activities at the Independence Blue Cross Broad Street Run Expo on May 1 and 2, as well as on race day, May 3. Team members will be on-site throughout the weekend, at the expo, along the racecourse and at both the start and finish lines. Bank employees and leadership will also take part in the race as runners, while additional team members will be stationed along the route to cheer participants. Expo attendees and spectators are invited to visit the Mid Penn Bank booth to meet the team, pick up giveaways ahead of race day and learn more about the Bank’s commitment to supporting its customers and communities.


“While you prepare for race day and look ahead to the finish line, we’re with you every step of the way,” Mid Penn Bank President and CEO Rory G. Ritrievi said. “We look forward to being part of the experience and supporting participants both on and off the course.”


For more than 150 years, Mid Penn Bank has supported its customers and communities in reaching their goals. The Bank offers a full range of financial solutions, including personal and business banking, lending, cash management, insurance and wealth management, working closely with its customers to help them achieve financial well-being. As a community bank, Mid Penn is committed to supporting the communities it serves through partnerships, financial education and initiatives that help create opportunity and long-term impact. With 62 retail locations and an expanding presence throughout greater Philadelphia and southern New Jersey, Mid Penn looks forward to strengthening its ties across the region and building lasting relationships that help customers, businesses and communities move forward with confidence.


### About Mid Penn Bank


Mid Penn Bank, headquartered in Millersburg, Pennsylvania, has been serving the community since 1868. The Bank operates 62 retail locations throughout Pennsylvania and central and southern New Jersey, and has total assets of approximately $7 billion. Mid Penn offers a comprehensive portfolio of financial products and services to meet the banking needs of the communities it serves.
