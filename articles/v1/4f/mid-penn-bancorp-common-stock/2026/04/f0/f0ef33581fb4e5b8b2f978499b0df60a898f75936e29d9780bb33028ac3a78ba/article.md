# MID PENN BANK HIRES ANDREW McGINNIS AS COMMERCIAL LOAN OFFICER


Posted on Apr 6th, 2026


Millersburg, Pa. – April 6, 2026. Mid Penn Bank (the “Bank”), a wholly-owned subsidiary of Mid Penn Bancorp, Inc. (“Mid Penn”) (NASDAQ: MPB), is pleased to announce that Andrew McGinnis has joined the Bank as vice president and commercial loan officer. Based at the Bank’s Newtown office, McGinnis will focus on building and managing commercial lending relationships across the greater Philadelphia area market and will report to Kevin Petzold, first vice president and commercial loan manager.


McGinnis brings extensive experience in commercial lending and relationship management. Most recently, he served as relationship manager and vice president at Firstrust Bank, where he managed a sizable portfolio of commercial and industrial and investment real estate clients across the Philadelphia region. During his tenure at Firstrust Bank, he also served as portfolio manager and banking officer. Earlier in his career, he held credit roles including senior credit analyst and assistant vice president at Penn Community Bank and credit analyst at First Priority Bank.


McGinnis earned a Master of Business Administration from West Chester University and a Bachelor of Science in business administration with a financial services concentration from Penn State Abington. He enjoys spending time with his wife and their two German shepherds, Ryder and Ace, as well as golfing and working on home projects.


### About Mid Penn Bank


Mid Penn Bank, headquartered in Millersburg, Pennsylvania, has been serving the community since 1868. The Bank operates 62 retail locations throughout Pennsylvania and central and southern New Jersey, and has total assets of approximately $7 billion. Mid Penn offers a comprehensive portfolio of financial products and services to meet the banking needs of the communities it serves.
