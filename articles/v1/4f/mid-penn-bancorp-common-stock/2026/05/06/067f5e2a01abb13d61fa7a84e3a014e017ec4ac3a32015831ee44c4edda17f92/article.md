# MID PENN BANK HIRES ERIC WHITE AS COMMERCIAL LOAN OFFICER


Posted on May 5th, 2026


Millersburg, Pa. – May 5, 2026. Mid Penn Bank (the “Bank”), a wholly-owned subsidiary of Mid Penn Bancorp, Inc. (“Mid Penn”) (NASDAQ: MPB), is pleased to announce that Eric White has joined the Bank as vice president and commercial loan officer. Based at the Bank’s Clearfield office, White will support commercial clients with their financing and cash management needs across the region and will report to Dan Baronick, senior vice president and regional president.


White most recently served as assistant vice president of commercial banking at CNB Bank, where he managed commercial relationships and supported lending activity for business clients. During his tenure at CNB Bank, he also served as assistant vice president and market manager, overseeing multiple financial centers, as well as community office manager and assistant manager, where he led day-to-day operations and team performance.


White earned a Bachelor of Business Administration in human resource management and business management from Clarion University of Pennsylvania. He is active in his community, serving as a board member and treasurer of the Children’s Aid Society and as a board member of the Clearfield YMCA, the Moshannon Valley Economic Development Partnership and the Clearfield Revitalization Corporation. He was also recognized as a PA Business Central “Foremost Under 40” honoree and has completed several professional banking programs through the Pennsylvania Bankers Association.


### About Mid Penn Bank


Mid Penn Bank, headquartered in Millersburg, Pennsylvania, has been serving the community since 1868. The Bank operates 62 retail locations throughout Pennsylvania and central and southern New Jersey, and has total assets of approximately $7 billion. Mid Penn offers a comprehensive portfolio of financial products and services to meet the banking needs of the communities it serves.
