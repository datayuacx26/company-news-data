# **Sendwithus Expands Executive Team, Names Jason Han Vice President of Sales**


#### ***Han Joins Sendwithus with over 17 years of Sales Experience as Company Ramps up Enterprise Dyspatch Sales***


**SAN FRANCISCO, CA and VICTORIA, British Columbia** —June 5, 2018—Sendwithus, creators of the cloud-based


[email management solution](https://www.dyspatch.io/platform/) Dyspatch, today announced the expansion of their management team, naming Jason Han vice president of sales. Jason will help advance the company’s growth following the recent launch of Dyspatch


, a GDPR-compliant, cloud-based communications management platform that allows Enterprise organizations to create compelling email experiences on a massive scale.


Jason brings deep software and SaaS industry sales experience in both start-up and Enterprise organizations. Most recently, he was responsible for all direct sales for US West and Canada at Egnyte and led a team of field-based Enterprise Account Executives managing Fortune 500 clients. Jason will be based in the San Francisco office and report to Matt Harris, co-founder and CEO of Sendwithus.


“With nearly two decades of sales leadership positions at IBM, Lyris and Siebel Systems, Jason is a proven leader whose MarTech and Enterprise email software and SaaS expertise aligns with our mission to help companies build increasingly complex, global email programs at scale,” said Matt Harris, co-founder and CEO of Sendwithus. “We look forward to Jason’s contributions and are excited to welcome him to the team.”


Prior to Egnyte, Jason was vice president of sales at Mindjet, responsible for direct sales for US and Canada, where he helped the company achieve 20 percent year-over-year growth. He also served as sales leader, Western Region, for IBM, and vice president of sales for Lyris. Jason holds a Bachelor of Arts in Sociology from the University of California at Berkeley, with a Minor in Asian American Studies.


“Enterprises


are challenged to meet compliance requirements, including GDPR, while centralizing email content management across hundreds of teams, and thousands of users


, making this a very exciting time to be joining Sendwithus


,” said Jason Han, Vice President of Sales, Sendwithus. “I’m looking forward to being a part of the Sendwithus team as the company continues to grow and help Enterprises realize the full potential across the entire spectrum of their automated, transactional, and triggered emails.”

**About Sendwithus**


Sendwithus has been helping leading brands, including Microsoft, Autodesk, and Zillow, craft exceptional communications experiences since 2013. Dyspatch by Sendwithus is a cloud-based,[transactional email platform](https://www.dyspatch.io/platform/) that allows Enterprise organizations to centralize email security, compliance, and reputation management while streamlining collaborative creation, revision, and approval workflows. The GDPR-compliant solution empowers teams to deliver powerful, global communications that engage customers, drive growth, and increase revenue.
