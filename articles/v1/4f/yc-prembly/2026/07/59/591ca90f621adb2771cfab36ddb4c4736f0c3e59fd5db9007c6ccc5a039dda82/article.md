# Prembly Connect Nairobi 2026 Brings Industry Leaders Together to Advance Digital Trust Across Africa

Prembly successfully hosted Prembly Connect Nairobi 2026 in Nairobi, Kenya, bringing together industry leaders, partners, innovators, and decision-makers for an evening of meaningful conversations around digital identity, compliance, customer onboarding, and fraud prevention across Africa.


The event provided an opportunity for professionals across the identity and fintech ecosystem to network, exchange ideas, and explore collaborative approaches to advancing digital trust and innovation across the continent.


Attendees connected with key industry stakeholders, gained practical insights from guest speakers and ecosystem experts, and experienced live demonstrations of Prembly’s identity verification and compliance solutions. Discussions also focused on emerging trends in digital trust, regulatory compliance, and onboarding innovation, emphasizing the need to create secure, seamless digital experiences for businesses and their customers.


Hosted by Prembly’s Kenya team, Victor Otieno and Lorine Odhiambo, the event encouraged meaningful conversations and valuable networking opportunities while reinforcing Prembly’s commitment to supporting organizations with trusted identity infrastructure across Africa.


Prembly Connect Nairobi 2026 reflects the company’s continued commitment to bringing together industry leaders and ecosystem stakeholders to address emerging fraud and compliance challenges, foster collaboration, and help organizations build safer, more trusted digital ecosystems.
