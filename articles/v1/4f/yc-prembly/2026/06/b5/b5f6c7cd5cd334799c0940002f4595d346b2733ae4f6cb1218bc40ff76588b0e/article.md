# Introducing the New Prembly SDK: More Flexibility, Better Branding, Smarter Verification

It is very essential for businesses to build a seamless verification experience for their customers if they are looking to gain trust and onboard efficiently. That’s why we’ve introduced a new and improved version of the Prembly SDK, designed to make identity verification more flexible, personalized, and aligned with your brand.


Whether you’re onboarding new customers or verifying existing users, these enhancements allow businesses to deliver a smooth verification experience to their customers without compromising security and reliability.


## **What’s New in the Prembly SDK?**


The Prembly SDK comes with three powerful features to help you create a better verification journey for your users. Which includes the following:


- **Address Verification:** Businesses can now verify user addresses directly within the SDK, making it easier to confirm customer information without adding extra steps to your onboarding process. This enhances data accuracy, streamlines the verification process, and strengthens compliance efforts.


- **Custom Welcome Notes:** Create a more personalized experience by adding a custom welcome message at the start of your verification flow. So, if you want to greet users, provide instructions, or reinforce your brand voice, custom welcome notes help create a friendlier and more engaging onboarding experience.


- **Company Logo Branding:** It helps your verification flow feel like a natural extension of your product. So, with company logo branding, you can upload and display your logo throughout the verification process, creating a consistent brand experience that helps build user confidence and trust.


## **How These Features Improve Your Verification Experience**


These features were designed to help businesses simplify customer onboarding while delivering a more professional and branded experience. By bringing together address verification, personalised welcome messages, and company branding in a single verification flow, you can improve user trust, reduce friction, and create a verification journey that feels seamless from start to finish.


Whether you’re a fintech company, marketplace, healthcare provider, or any business that relies on identity verification, these updates give you greater control over how users experience your verification process.
