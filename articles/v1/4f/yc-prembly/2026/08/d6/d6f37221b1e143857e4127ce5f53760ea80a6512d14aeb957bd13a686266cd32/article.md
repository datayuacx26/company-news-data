# KYB Compliance: Verifying Ultimate Beneficial Owners

KYB compliance is not only about verifying a company’s registration and legal status. It also involves identifying and verifying the individuals who ultimately own or control the company, known as Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBOs). This process enables businesses and financial institutions to know who they are truly dealing with and to identify potential compliance risks.


Therefore, it is important for businesses to understand that self-declared ownership may not always reveal the complete picture of a company’s ownership, especially when multiple entities or individuals are involved. An effective UBO verification process combines business records, ownership data, and trusted sources to determine who ultimately owns or controls an entity.


This guide explains why UBO verification matters, why self-declared ownership may not be enough, how to identify and verify UBOs against official sources, and how to build an effective UBO verification workflow.


## **What Is KYB Compliance and Why Does UBO Verification Matter?**


KYB (Know Your Business) compliance involves verifying a business’s identity, ownership structure, and risk profile before establishing a business relationship or onboarding the company. Organizations can confirm a company’s proper registration, legal operation, and compliance with applicable laws and regulations through this process.


One of the key components of KYB is Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) verification, which involves identifying the individuals who ultimately own or control a business, even when ownership is spread across multiple entities or layers. It is a crucial step for regulated businesses in preventing money laundering, sanctions evasion, fraud, and other financial crimes. Without proper UBO verification, businesses may rely solely on self-declared ownership information, leaving potential gaps in their due diligence. Hence, it is necessary to verify ownership against reliable data sources to understand who ultimately controls the business and support effective KYB compliance.


## **How Do You Identify the Ultimate Beneficial Owner?**


The process of identifying the Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) requires tracing a company’s ownership and control structure to uncover the individuals who ultimately own or control the business. This often involves looking beyond the company itself to examine parent companies, subsidiaries, and other entities linked to the ownership chain.


Accurate UBO identification is an essential component of KYB and AML due diligence for financial institutions, fintechs, and other regulated businesses. This is because these organizations need to understand who is behind a business, assess potential risks, and meet applicable regulatory requirements. For businesses operating across multiple jurisdictions, UBO identification can become challenging. As a result, putting in place a structured UBO verification workflow can streamline ownership analysis, reduce manual reviews, and make it easier to uncover individuals within complex corporate structures.


## **How do you verify UBO data against official sources?**


Verifying UBOs requires cross-checking self-declared ownership information against reliable and authoritative sources. The specific sources used for UBO verification depend on the jurisdiction and may include government registries, corporate databases, and identity documents. The purpose is to confirm that the individuals listed as UBOs are legitimate, accurately identified, and actually connected to the company’s ownership or control structure.


This independent verification enables compliance teams to detect inconsistencies, missing information, or undisclosed ownership. For organizations conducting KYB and AML due diligence, verifying UBO data against official sources provides greater confidence in the information collected during onboarding.


## **How do you build a UBO verification workflow step by step?**


For organizations to implement a well-designed UBO verification workflow, they need a structured process that goes beyond self-declared ownership data. This helps compliance teams move away from traditional processes that are time-consuming, manual, and difficult to scale by using a workflow that combines business data, ownership records, identity verification, and risk screening into a consistent process.


**Here is a practical step-by-step UBO verification workflow:**


**Step 1: Collect entity and ownership data**


Begin by collecting the company’s registration details, shareholder data, ownership records, and organizational structure. Gather sufficient information to map the full ownership chain, including parent companies and other entities that may sit between the business and its ultimate owners.


**Step 2: Identify the UBOs**


Assess each individual’s ownership interest against the relevant threshold to determine which individuals qualify as UBOs. Then, verify the collected information against corporate registries and trusted commercial databases to uncover ownership structures or connections that may not be reflected in self-declared information.


**Step 3: Verify UBO identities** **independently**


Once the UBOs have been identified, independently verify each individual’s identity. This includes document validation, biometric matching, and screening individuals against sanctions and PEP lists.


**Step 4: Assess risk and document the results**


Evaluate each UBO for potential risks by checking adverse media, sanctions lists, and regulatory enforcement records. Keep a clear audit trail of the information reviewed, verification results, and decisions made during the process. This is necessary because regulators expect auditable records that demonstrate due diligence was performed.


**Step 5: Automate ongoing monitoring**


UBO verification should not end after onboarding. Organizations need to set up ongoing monitoring to track changes in ownership structures, UBO status, and new adverse media findings. Automating these checks can make KYB compliance more consistent and scalable while preventing compliance gaps between onboarding cycles as the business relationship continues.


An effective UBO verification workflow enables businesses to accurately establish ownership, minimize compliance risks, and maintain thorough KYB due diligence throughout the entire business relationship.
