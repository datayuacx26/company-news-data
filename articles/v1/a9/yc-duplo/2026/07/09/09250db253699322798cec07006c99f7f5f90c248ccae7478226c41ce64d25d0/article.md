# Duplo and Wema Bank Partner to Give ALAT Business Customers a Connected Financial Operations Platform

Nigerian businesses have long managed their finances across a patchwork of disconnected tools: spreadsheets for tracking spend, WhatsApp threads for approvals, email chains for invoices, with no single place to see the full picture. That is the problem Duplo was built to solve. And it is the same problem that brought Duplo and Wema Bank to the table.


[Duplo](https://tryduplo.com/) , the number 1 financial operating system for African businesses, has partnered with Wema Bank to give **[ALAT by Wema Bank](https://alat.ng/)** business customers access to a connected platform for managing their financial operations. The partnership was formally signed at a ceremony held at Four Points by Sheraton, Victoria Island, bringing together Wema Bank leadership, industry stakeholders, partners, and members of the[press](https://businessday.ng/companies/article/nigerias-oldest-indigenous-bank-joins-duplo-to-digitise-business-finance/) .

## What This Means for ALAT Business Customers


In practice, this means a business using ALAT by Wema will be able to log into a connected Duplo dashboard and run its everyday financial operations without opening five different tools to do it. Here is what the integration brings together in a single workflow:


• **[Local payments](https://tryduplo.com/direct-debit/)** , powered directly by Wema Bank’s infrastructure, so money moves without leaving the platform a business already trusts.


•[Expense management and approvals ,](https://tryduplo.com/expense-management/) replacing scattered receipts and WhatsApp approval chains with structured workflows and spending limits set in advance.


• **[Invoice generation and collections](https://tryduplo.com/nrs-e-invoicing/)** , so businesses can issue compliant invoices and get paid without a separate system for billing.


• **Vendor payment automation** , built for businesses paying dozens or hundreds of suppliers in a single cycle.


• **Real-time financial reporting** , giving business owners and finance leads one place to see exactly where the money is, instead of reconstructing it after the fact.


• **[Auto-reconciliation](https://tryduplo.com/payment-reconciliation/)** , which matches every payment to its corresponding record automatically, closing what is usually the most time-consuming task on a finance team’s calendar.


None of these are new ideas individually. What is new is that an ALAT business customer will be able to access all of them from one connected dashboard, tied directly to the bank account they already use.


One of the highlights of the event was a live product walkthrough led by Duplo’s Product Lead, **[Remi Ogunsanya](https://www.linkedin.com/in/remi-ogunsanya-24058688/)** , who walked the room through the full experience end to end. The room responded with questions from business owners and banking professionals eager to understand exactly how the integration changes day-to-day financial operations for their customers.


## Beyond the Signing


It is worth stepping back from the ceremony for a moment, because the significance here goes beyond one bank and one platform. For a long time, the assumption in financial services has been that banks and fintechs compete for the same territory. What happened at Four Points by Sheraton was closer to the opposite: a bank leaning on a fintech’s product depth,[and a fintech leaning on a bank’s trust](https://fintech.global/2026/07/21/duplo-teams-up-with-wema-bank-to-automate-business-finances/) , distribution, and infrastructure, to solve a problem neither could solve as well alone.


That is the model Duplo’s leadership pointed to directly: the future of financial services in Nigeria, and across Africa more broadly, will not be built by banks or fintechs working in isolation. It will be built through exactly this kind of collaboration, anchored around a real, specific customer problem rather than a press moment.


For Wema Bank, it means ALAT customers get more reasons to keep deepening a relationship they already have. For Duplo, it means reaching business owners at the exact moment they are already managing money, inside the app they already open every day. For the businesses themselves, it means one less system to manage and a few more hours back every month.


## How the Rollout Works


The integration will not appear for every ALAT business customer overnight. Duplo and Wema Bank confirmed that the experience will roll out first to an initial group of ALAT business customers over the coming weeks, with both teams working closely with that group to refine the experience before it becomes more broadly available.


As part of the launch, existing ALAT business customers will get **three months of complimentary access** to manage their business expenses on the platform, a window designed to let businesses feel the difference before committing.


The afternoon closed with networking and lunch, business cards changing hands, and small groups still talking through the details of what they had just watched on stage.


## This Is What Connected Finance Looks Like


Nigeria’s business landscape does not have a shortage of ambition. What it has had a shortage of is infrastructure that keeps up with it.


For too long, growing businesses have spent too much time stitching together spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads, and disconnected tools to manage their finances. This partnership is a direct answer to that problem: a single connected platform tied to a bank account you already use, handling payments, expenses, approvals, reconciliation, and reporting in one workflow.


Duplo and Wema Bank are not building for the business that is struggling to survive. They are building for the one that is ready to scale, and needs its financial operations to scale with it.


That is the real announcement from the signing ceremony. Not a product launch. A starting point.
