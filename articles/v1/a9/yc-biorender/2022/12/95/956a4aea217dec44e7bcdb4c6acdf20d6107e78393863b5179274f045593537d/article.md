#### How to enter:


1. Create and decorate a science-themed holiday e-card in BioRender. You can start from scratch or use a pre-made holiday e-card template!
2. Share your finished e-card in BioRender by clicking the Share button (located at the top right hand corner of your illustration screen).
3. Share your illustration with us on social media by tagging us @BioRender on Twitter and Linkedin or @BioRender.io on Instagram.


Contest Closes: Thursday, Dec 8th at 11:59PM ET
