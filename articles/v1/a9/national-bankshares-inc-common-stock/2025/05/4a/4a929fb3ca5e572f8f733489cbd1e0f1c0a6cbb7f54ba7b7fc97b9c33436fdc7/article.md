# National Bankshares, Inc. Declares Semi-Annual Dividend

[View All News](https://www.nationalbankshares.com/news/default.aspx)


### National Bankshares, Inc. Declares Semi-Annual Dividend


May 14, 2025


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s203.q4cdn.com/179023092/files/doc_news/2025/May/14/Press-Release-Dividend-5-14-25.pdf)


BLACKSBURG, VA, MAY 14, 2025:


The Board of Directors of National Bankshares, Inc. (NASDAQ Capital Market: NKSH) today approved payment on June 2, 2025 of a semi-annual dividend of $0.73 per share to all stockholders of record as of May 27, 2025. Chairman and CEO F. Brad Denardo commented, “National Bankshares has offered an outstanding dividend for many years, and we are pleased to continue our tradition with this latest payment. We look forward to upholding and enhancing our stockholders’ investment in National Bankshares for many years to come.”


In other business, the Board renewed its authorization of a stock repurchase plan under which management is authorized to purchase up to 250,000 shares of National Bankshares, Inc.’s common stock in the open market.


The authorization extends from June 1, 2025 to May 31, 2026. The Company’s current stock repurchase plan expires on May 31, 2025. The Company’s management has not yet determined how many shares, if any, might be purchased under the continued stock repurchase plan.


**About National Bankshares**


National Bankshares, Inc., headquartered in Blacksburg, Virginia, is the parent company of The National Bank of Blacksburg, which does business as National Bank, and of National Bankshares Financial Services, Inc. National Bank is a community bank operating from 28 full-service offices in southwestern, western and central Virginia, and one loan production office in Charlottesville, Virginia. National Bankshares Financial Services, Inc. is an investment and insurance subsidiary in the same trade area. The Company’s stock is traded on the Nasdaq Capital Market under the symbol “NKSH.” Additional information is available at www.nationalbankshares.com.
