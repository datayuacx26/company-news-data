# Diversion Product Updates: November Highlights (11/25)

Dear community,
Winter’s here - the perfect time to stay in, code, and sync without surprises. We’ve packed this month with lots of features to keep your workflow cozy, clear, and organized - like the perfect version control sweater ;)
Let’s take a look at what’s new!


‍


## 📡 See your sync in action


Sync progress is no longer a mystery!Now you can actually **see** how many files and how much data are left to sync - in real time.It’s the first step toward making sync behavior fully transparent and predictable.


‍


## 🛠️ Admins, this one’s for you


Admins now get **full self-service visibility** - no more guesswork.


- Org admins/owners can view all repos.


- Repo admins/owners can view all workspaces.
Just head to **Avatar → Your Organizations → Repositories / Workspaces** to explore.


‍


## 🎮 UE 5.7 support


Our Unreal Engine plugin is officially live on **Fab** for **UE 5.7** !The easiest way yet to get Diversion working in your Unreal projects.[Download UE plugin here](https://www.fab.com/listings/59d591f2-4026-4e8f-9128-9c8ef1ff9bab)


‍


## 🖼️ Say hello to profile pictures


You asked, we delivered.Customize your avatar right in the app: **Avatar → Your Profile → Edit Icon.**


‍


## 🌿 Cleaner branch lists, happier repos


Branches now group by / and are collapsible - perfect for keeping your repo neat and structured.Organize by developer, feature, or workflow - your call.


‍


## 🖥️ A fresh, unified systray (Windows + Mac)


We gave the systray a complete makeover! It’s now sleek, modern, and works consistently across both platforms.


💡Tip: If you don’t see it, the Desktop app isn’t open (though the agent might still be running in the background).To stay synced automatically, enable “Launch on Startup” in your settings - new installs have it turned on by default


‍


## 🔒Smarter "Access denied" messages (Windows)


On Windows, some apps can hold a file open preventing Diversion from syncing new changes. No more vague “Access Denied” errors. Now you’ll see exactly which app is blocking a sync - like: “Cannot write to src/components/Button.tsx - file is locked by Windows PowerShell.” A huge quality-of-life win for debugging.


‍


## 👋🏻 Leave repos on your own


No need to wait for someone to remove you - now you can leave repos directly from your dashboard. Simple, quick, independent.


‍
