# Diversion Product Updates: August Highlights (08/25)

Dear community,
We hope you had an amazing summer - hopefully with a cold drink in hand by the pool. Meanwhile, we’ve been hard at work making diversion even better for you. Ready to check out what’s new? Let’s go!


‍


### 🏞️ Preview for UASSETS is here!


Exciting news, you can now preview uassets with thumbnails directly in diversion!
A few notes:


- Works with files under 2MB (limit will grow soon!)


- Blueprints coming soon..


Get ready - this is just the start of even smoother workflows!


‍


### 🤝🏻 Horde integration (beta)


Big news for teams using Horde! Diversion now supports Horde in beta. If your studio is interested in early access, hit us up - we’re already onboarding teams.[Click here to read the docs](https://docs.diversion.dev/unreal/horde_setup)


‍


### 📂 Send this version to the workspace


Ever needed a file from an old commit but dreaded the download + re-upload shuffle? Not anymore! With one click, you can now send that version straight into your current workspace. This one’s been at the top of the request list - and it’s finally here.


‍


### **🧹 dv clean** command


Introducing a new CLI command magic trick: dv clean. It wipes ignored and untracked files from your workspace - super handy for CI/CD pipelines that need a fresh state.


‍
