# Diversion Product Updates: July Highlights (07/25)

Dear community, we’ve got some shiny new features ready for action! Whether you’re fixing, building, or exploring, this month’s updates are here to make your dev life smoother. Let’s check them out.
‍


## 🪝 Webhooks are here!


You asked, we built it - webhooks are now live!
Trigger external services when things happen in your repo: perfect for CI/CD workflows, Slack updates, or your own automations.
Set them up via **Your Profile → Repositories → Webhooks** .
Check out the webhooks docs[here](https://docs.diversion.dev/webhooks#webhooks) .


‍


## 📝 Rename repos yourself


No need to ping support anymore - repo owners can now rename repositories directly from the dashboard. Head to "All Repositories", click the three dots, and select Rename. Done!


‍


## 🔐 2FA is now available!


Want extra peace of mind? You can now turn on 2FA for added account security.
Head to **Your Profile → Two-Factor Authentication** to set it up.


‍


## 🎮 Commit button is back (UE 5.6)


The “Commit Changes” button briefly vanished for Unreal Engine 5.6 users - but it’s back now and ready for action. You can commit as usual via the button or the "View Changes" panel.
‍


##
🧠 Enhanced diff view


Review smarter: diffs now show size and modification time for both versions, and you can choose how to view them - **Auto layout** , **Side-by-side** , or **Inline** .


‍


## 📝 Markdown-powered commit messages


Your commit notes just got a glow-up! Now you can sprinkle **bold** , *italic* , lists, links, and even code snippets right in the commit dialog.


‍


## 🔀 In-app merge preview


No more crossing your fingers before you click merge! When you choose “Merge branches” you’ll now see a side-by-side preview of what’s coming in.


‍


## ✉️ Two-way repo invitations


Invites are no longer one-way. Recipients can view and accept pending repo invitations directly from their dashboard. Collaboration: unlocked!


‍
