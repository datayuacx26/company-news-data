# Perforce vs Diversion Comparison


Category Perforce (P4) Diversion


**Performance & Scalability** Prone to bottlenecks under high load (e.g., “Monday Morning Syndrome” with large commits). Requires optimization for stable performance. Modern architecture designed for high-throughput production. Smooth performance at scale without tuning.


**User Experience (UX)** Dated UI. Concepts like changelists, streams, and labels confuse new developers. Clean, Git-like UI. Intuitive for both technical and creative users. Easy for new team members to adopt.


**Branching & Collaboration** Centralized, stream-based model. Lock-heavy workflows. Not ideal for async or team collaboration. Branch-light workflows. Built for safe, async collaboration. Encourages parallel work without overhead.


**History & Audit Trail** Strong changelists and logs, but hard to interpret unless you're a power user. Visual asset change history. Intuitive and accessible to all team members.


**Total Cost of Ownership** Rigid licensing. High per-user cost and IT resource requirements. Flexible per-user pricing. No infrastructure or IT overhead needed.


**Hosting Model** Self-hosted or managed cloud (paid). Requires setup and maintenance. Fully managed and cloud-hosted. No setup or infrastructure required.


**Setup & Installation** Requires P4D install, firewall configs, typemap tuning, SSL, and backup plans. One-click setup. No CLI or config files. Start instantly.


**Team Onboarding** Manual setup per teammate: install P4V, configure server paths and credentials. Just invite users. No setup or local config required.


**Workspace Management** Requires mapping workspaces per user/project. Can confuse new users. Centralized, automatic workspace management. Nothing to configure.
