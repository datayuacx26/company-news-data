# A 360° Version Control and Asset Management Workflow - Now with ArtStash

At Diversion, we believe creative teams deserve tools that move at the speed of ideas. Versioning shouldn’t slow momentum, and managing assets shouldn’t block creativity. That’s why we’re excited to announce a new step forward in the Diversion ecosystem: an integration with[ArtStash](https://www.artstash.io/) , a modern Digital Asset Management (DAM) platform built for creators, game developers, and real-time production teams.


This collaboration brings a complete workflow experience to Diversion users:


✅ Reliable version control


✅ Visual asset organization


✅ AI-powered search and asset grouping


✅ Seamless creative pipeline management


**And even better: ArtStash is free for one year for all Diversion users.**[Sign up here.](https://www.artstash.io/diversion)


‍


## **Why we teamed up with ArtStash**


Diversion empowers teams to version large creative projects with confidence - branches, reviews, Unreal Engine workflows, cloud-native performance, and more. ArtStash adds a visual layer on top of your Diversion repositories, making it faster than ever to browse, locate, and manage large asset libraries.


Now, your assets don’t just live inside repositories - you can explore them visually, organize them by meaning, and find exactly what you’re looking for in seconds.


Together, Diversion + ArtStash deliver a 360° workflow built for creative production.


‍


## **What the integration enables**


### **See all your assets at a glance**


ArtStash automatically pulls assets from your Diversion repositories and displays them in a clean, visual layout. No digging through folders or guessing file paths - you’ll instantly see textures, meshes, animations, materials, audio files, and more in a browsable gallery.


‍


### **Organize with meaningful tags (not just folder names)**


Structure your assets your way. Apply custom tags to organize by workflow, sprint, game feature, biome, character, level, or asset type.


Example tag structures:


- Character > Boss > Frost Giant
- Environment > Desert > Rocks
- UI > Inventory Icons
- Animation > Walk Cycle > Orc


This makes it easier for artists, designers, and developers to navigate large repositories together.


‍


### **AI-powered auto-tagging & discovery**


This is where it gets exciting: ArtStash uses AI to analyze your assets visually and generate relevant auto-tags. It can even detect similar assets across different repositories and intelligently group them.


So even if someone uploads mesh_final_v6_really_final.fbx, you can still find it by searching “character armor” or “NPC human male” - because ArtStash recognizes the content, not just the file name.


‍


### **Built for how teams actually work**


Want better ways to search than just by filename? With ArtStash integration, you can search:


✔ By keywords


✔ By visual similarity


✔ By tags and tag groups


✔ By AI-generated descriptions


This dramatically improves team productivity and creative reuse, especially in large game projects where thousands of assets evolve over time.


It’s a natural evolution - a version control system and an asset management system working together for teams who build visually.


‍


## **Who benefits from this integration?**


If your pipeline includes digital assets - you’ll feel the impact immediately.

## **Get started in minutes**


Connecting ArtStash to Diversion is simple: sign in to ArtStash, connect your Diversion account, and sync your repositories. That’s it.
Learn how to connect ArtStash to your Diversion account[here.](https://www.artstash.io/knowledge-base/connecting-diversion-version-control-to-artstash)
