# Calling All Metal Cutting Creators: The Machinist of the Year Search Begins

## Introducing: The Machinist of the Year


As a global leader in metal cutting solutions, Kennametal is proud to recognize the skill, dedication and innovation of machinists around the world. That’s why we’re launching the Machinist of the Year program, a global initiative celebrating professionals from shops of all sizes and across industries who drive manufacturing forward and settle for nothing less than high performance in their work.


“Machinists are the backbone of manufacturing,” said Eddie McBarnett, Vice President of Marketing & Strategy. “Kennametal’s innovations have always been driven by the needs of these skilled professionals.”


We created the Machinist of the Year program to celebrate those who embody craftsmanship and innovation in the machining field. A winner will be selected in the Americas, EMEA, APAC and India to represent their region as its very best.


## How To Enter


Entering is easy and creativity is encouraged. Just post a brief video on Facebook, LinkedIn or Instagram sharing how you or the person you’d like to nominate uses Kennametal products and why they should be selected as Machinist of the Year. Feel free to name-drop your favorite tools and show off what you’ve made with them, just be sure to tag Kennametal and use the hashtag #KMTMachinist when uploading your video. Each nominee should have actively used Kennametal products at their shop for the past year.
