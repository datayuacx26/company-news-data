# Legacy Meets Innovation: Kennametal Introduces New Products

Fall is a great time to celebrate the change of seasons and embrace new beginnings. In that spirit, we are celebrating by launching new cutting-edge solutions across several platforms including[TopSwiss™](https://www.kennametal.com/us/en/products/metalworking-tools/small-parts-and-swiss-type/swiss-type-turning/od-and-id-turning/topswiss-mbs.html)MBS (Mirco Boring Solid) for small parts turning, while reflecting on our journey of continuous improvement.


Since 1938, Kennametal has been a leader in innovation. And it all began when McKenna Metals that was founded by metallurgist Philip M. McKenna, who after years of research created revolutionary tungsten-titanium carbide alloy cutting tools specifically for working with steel.


As the company evolved, in 1943 McKenna Metals became Kennametal Inc.—a recognized leader in metalworking serving customers across continents and industries including transportation, construction, aerospace and defense, machining and cutting, energy, and general engineering.


“When I joined the company, we were primarily a turning company. We developed and manufactured KC850, a cobalt enrichment grade that changed the industry. It was used everywhere, on every material. It put Kennametal on the forefront,” said Scott Etling, Vice President of Global Product Management.


Having a reputation for building innovative solutions for customers' most challenging applications, Kennametal came out with newer grades called KC9110 and KC9125. Both grades utilizing cobalt enrichment, but a shift was happening in the industry. Companies started to focus on developing advanced coatings, including Kennametal.
