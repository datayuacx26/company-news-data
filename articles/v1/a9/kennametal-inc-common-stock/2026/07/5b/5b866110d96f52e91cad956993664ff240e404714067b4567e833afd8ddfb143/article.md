# First Choice: Essential Tooling for Complex Machining

## Industry Applications


Here are some key industries and practical applications where our heavy hitters are used most:


### Aerospace


For machining intricate components made from materials like titanium and high-temp alloys.[Beyond Evolution™](https://www.kennametal.com/us/en/products/metalworking-tools/turning/profiling/beyond-evolution.html) tooling is a go-to to create precise grooves and for cut-off operations when machining components such as turbine blades, ensuring stability and reducing vibrations during high-speed operations.


### Automotive


For reliably producing precision, complex parts for hybrid and electric vehicles as well as traditional automotive components. For instance,[HARVI™ I TE](https://www.kennametal.com/us/en/products/metalworking-tools/milling/solid-end-milling/high-performance-solid-carbide-end-mills/harvi-i-te-high-performance-solid-carbide-end-mills.html) end mills can be used to machine pieces like aluminum engine blocks with high precision.


### Medical


For manufacturing precision medical devices and implants while navigating high-quality finishes and tight tolerances. The[KenTIP™ FS](https://www.kennametal.com/us/en/products/metalworking-tools/holemaking/modular-drills/kentip-fs-modular-drill-system.html) modular drilling system is perfect for machining accurate holes in stainless steel surgical instruments. This tooling system ensures efficient chip evacuation and reduces the risk of defects, which is crucial for maintaining the integrity and functionality of medical tools.


### Energy


For precision machining in oil and gas and solar industries.[Dodeka™](https://www.kennametal.com/us/en/products/metalworking-tools/milling/indexable-milling/face-mills/dodeka-series.html) face milling inserts can be used to machine high-temp alloy components for wind turbines. These inserts enhance chip formation and evacuation, ensuring efficient machining and contributing to the production of durable and reliable energy.
