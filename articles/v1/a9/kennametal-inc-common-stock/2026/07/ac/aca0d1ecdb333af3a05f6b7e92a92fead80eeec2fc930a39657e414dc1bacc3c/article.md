# TopSwiss Turning Inserts and Small Parts Machining: What You Should Know

Machining small parts calls for high precision, tight tolerances and excellent surface finishes. Kennametal’s TopSwiss platform is designed to meet these demands across industries like medical, aerospace and defense, automotive and general engineering. Here, we have some practical advice to be successful with your Swiss-type turning applications.


## But first, what is Swiss-type turning?


Performed on a CNC Swiss lathe, this method involves a sliding headstock and guide bushing, which rotates the workpiece on a spindle as it is fed across a stationary cutting tool. Parts range from bone screws and hip and shoulder implants to bushings and fuel injectors and can be made from various materials.
