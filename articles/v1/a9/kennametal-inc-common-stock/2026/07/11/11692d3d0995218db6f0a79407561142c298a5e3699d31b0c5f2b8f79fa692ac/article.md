# Celebrating Kennametal’s Legacy in Turning

With deep expertise in developing cutting-edge coatings and grades for turning solutions, Kennametal continually sets the standard for innovation and new technology. It all began in 1972 with the launch of our KC75 physical vapor deposition (PVD) grade, followed by the KC710 chemical vapor deposition (CVD) grade in 1985 – achievements that paved the way for more advancements.


As our PVD and CVD technologies evolved, they culminated in the creation of KENGold,™ a premium coating solution available in both PVD and CVD processes and engineered to meet the demands of next-generation machining.


The legacy continues with[KCP25C](https://www.kennametal.com/us/en/products/metalworking-tools/turning/coating-technologies/kengold.html) , a standout CVD grade featuring an advanced MT-TiCN/Alumina CVD coating. It’s designed for durability and precision, and helps reduce waste by making it easier to spot worn or unused edges when machining carbon, alloy and various other steels.


Complementing everything is our high-performance[KCU10B](https://www.kennametal.com/us/en/products/metalworking-tools/turning/coating-technologies/kcu10b.html) and[KCU25B](https://www.kennametal.com/us/en/products/metalworking-tools/turning/coating-technologies/kcu25b.html) PVD grades. Both deliver outstanding resistance to thermal deformation edge wear across all material groups. KCU10B is the harder, more wear-resistant option, while KCU25B is tougher, catering to different machining needs. These grades excel in machining steels, stainless steels, cast iron and high-temp alloys **.**


*“Turning grades are the backbone of precision machining,” says Etling. “Each new generation improves on the last, ensuring every cut is precise, every edge is resilient, and every operation is efficient. These grades aren’t just materials—they’re engineered solutions that deliver superior performance and durability.”*
