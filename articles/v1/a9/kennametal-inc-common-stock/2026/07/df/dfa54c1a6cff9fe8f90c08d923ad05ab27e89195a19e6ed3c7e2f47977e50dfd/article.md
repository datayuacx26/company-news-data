# Breaking Down Boron Nitride for High-Temperature Vacuum Furnaces – Part 1

Your selection really depends on operating environment, part geometry and performance priorities. Let’s look closer at each:


[BN6000](https://www.kennametal.com/us/en/products/sintec-ceramics/boron-nitride-and-ceramic-components/bn6000.html) – This is hot-pressed, high-purity hBN.


- Highest mechanical strength with lowest thermal conductivity (~21 W/m·K)
- Excellent dielectric strength (~70 kV/mm)
- Stable up to ~2000 °C in inert/vacuum


Best for: precision electrical insulators, feedthroughs and tight-tolerance parts.


[BN8000](https://www.kennametal.com/us/en/products/sintec-ceramics/boron-nitride-and-ceramic-components/bn8000.html) – This is low-density hBN.


- Utilizes near-net shape forming and pressure-less sintering technologies
- Good mechanical strength with moderate anisotropy
- Stable up to ~1700 °C in inert atmosphere


Best for: large plates, shields and setters where weight and machining cost matter.


[BN9000](https://www.kennametal.com/us/en/products/sintec-ceramics/boron-nitride-and-ceramic-components/bn9000.html) – This is medium-density hBN.


- Utilizes near-net shape forming and pressure-less sintering technologies
- Higher thermal conductivity (~36 W/m·K)
- Excellent isotropy and lowest CTE
- Stable up to ~1900 °C in inert atmosphere


Best for: paving plates, setters and fixtures exposed to frequent thermal cycling.
