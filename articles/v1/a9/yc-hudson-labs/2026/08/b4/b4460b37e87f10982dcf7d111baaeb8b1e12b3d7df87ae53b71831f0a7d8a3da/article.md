## Summary and Conclusions


- **NVIDIA is coming off a record Q1 FY2027, with revenue of $81.6B (+85% YoY, +20% QoQ), driven by continued explosive demand for AI infrastructure, especially Blackwell systems.**
- **Q2 FY2027 guidance is for $91B revenue (±2%), with gross margins at 74.9% GAAP/75.0% non-GAAP, and OpEx at $8.5B/$8.3B. Consensus is closely aligned.**
- **The company faces a tough YoY comparable: Q2 FY2026 revenue was $46.7B, so the implied YoY growth for Q2 FY2027 is +96%.**
- **Key focus areas for the upcoming print:**


- **Sustained Data Center momentum:** Data Center revenue was $75.2B in Q1 FY2027 (+92% YoY, +21% QoQ), with hyperscale and AI cloud/enterprise segments now split ~50/50.
- **Vera Rubin ramp:** Management has indicated Vera Rubin is in full production, with shipments to begin in Q3. Investors will look for commentary on initial customer uptake, supply chain execution, and incremental TAM from stand-alone Vera CPUs.
- **Inference share and agentic AI:** NVIDIA claims growing share in inference, with agentic AI driving parabolic compute demand. Look for updates on inference vs. training mix, token economics, and competitive positioning.
- **Supply chain and constraints:** Management continues to highlight tightness in advanced architectures and ongoing large purchase commitments ($145B+). Any update on supply chain risks or easing will be closely watched.
- **CPU traction:** Stand-alone Vera CPU is expected to generate ~$20B revenue in FY2027, opening a new $200B TAM. Progress on CPU attach and ecosystem adoption will be key.
- **Margin sustainability:** Guidance is for mid-70s gross margins for FY2027. Investors will scrutinize cost trends, pricing power, and any signals on input cost inflation or competitive pressure.
- **Capital returns:** NVIDIA announced an $80B additional buyback authorization and raised its dividend to $0.25/share. Execution on capital return targets (~50% of FCF) will be monitored.
- **Segment mix:** The new reporting structure splits Data Center into Hyperscale and ACIE (AI Clouds, Industrial, Enterprise), and Edge Computing. Growth rates and mix shifts between these will be important for forward modeling.

## What to Watch This Quarter


### 1. **Data Center Growth and Mix**


- Is Data Center revenue continuing to accelerate sequentially?
- How does the mix between Hyperscale and ACIE evolve? Is ACIE outgrowing Hyperscale?


### 2. **Vera Rubin Ramp**


- Are initial shipments on track for Q3?
- Any commentary on customer adoption, supply chain readiness, and incremental TAM from stand-alone CPUs?


### 3. **Inference Share and Agentic AI**


- Is NVIDIA maintaining or expanding share in inference workloads?
- How is agentic AI adoption translating into incremental compute demand and token monetization?


### 4. **Supply Chain and Constraints**


- Any updates on supply chain bottlenecks, inventory, or purchase commitments?
- Is NVIDIA able to meet demand, or are constraints limiting upside?


### 5. **CPU Traction**


- Progress on Vera CPU attach rates, stand-alone sales, and ecosystem adoption.
- Is the $20B stand-alone CPU revenue target for FY2027 achievable?


### 6. **Margin Sustainability**


- Can NVIDIA sustain mid-70s gross margins amid rising input costs and competitive pressures?
- Any signals on pricing, cost optimization, or product mix?


### 7. **Capital Returns**


- Execution on $80B buyback authorization and increased dividend.
- Is the company returning ~50% of FCF as planned?


### 8. **Competitive Landscape**


- Any commentary on ASIC/merchant silicon competition, especially in inference?
- How is NVIDIA defending its moat (CUDA, software stack, full-system integration)?

## Management Commentary — Recent Tone


- **On Demand:** "Demand has gone parabolic. The reason is simple, agentic AI has arrived. AI can now do productive and valuable work. Tokens are now profitable, so model makers are in a race to produce more."
- **On Margins:** "We are still expecting to be in the mid-70s \[gross margin\] for the full year."
- **On Supply:** "We remain confident in our ability to support the growth opportunity ahead with our intense focus, scale and long-standing partnerships with critical suppliers continuing to serve us well."
- **On Capital Returns:** "We plan to return roughly 50% of free cash flow to shareholders this year."
- **On CPU Opportunity:** "Vera CPU opens a brand-new $200 billion TAM for NVIDIA, a market we have never addressed before... We have visibility to nearly $20 billion in total CPU revenue this year."

## Conclusion


**NVIDIA enters Q2 FY2027 earnings with extremely strong momentum, but faces a very tough YoY comparable after more than doubling revenue in the past year. The Street will focus on whether Data Center growth can continue at this pace, the Vera Rubin ramp, inference share, and margin sustainability. Supply chain execution, CPU attach, and capital return progress are also key. Any signs of demand moderation, margin compression, or supply constraints could be negative catalysts, while upside in new product ramps or further acceleration in AI infrastructure spend would be viewed positively.**
