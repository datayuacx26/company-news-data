## Most Important Factors to Watch for Q3 2026


### 1. Net Interest Margin (NIM)


- Management continues to guide for "stable to gradually higher" NIM, supported by the tractoring (hedging) strategy, favorable business mix, and product margin discipline.
- Q2 2026 NIM (ex-trading) was 2.05%, up 17 bps YoY, but down 1 bp QoQ due to seasonality.
- Watch for any signs of margin compression from competitive pricing, deposit mix shifts, or mortgage spread pressure.


### 2. Credit Quality and Provisions


- Q2 2026 impaired provisions were $548M, up $28M QoQ, mainly in Canadian Personal & Business Banking.
- Management expects impaired provisions to be "broadly in line with the levels we've seen in the first half of the year."
- Any uptick in delinquencies, net write-offs, or signs of systemic risk would be notable.


### 3. Commercial Loan and Deposit Growth


- Commercial loan and deposit growth has been strong, with Q2 2026 showing 7% YoY growth in both.
- Management guides to "mid- to high single-digit growth year-over-year" for the second half.
- Watch for sustainability of this growth and any commentary on sector/geographic drivers.


### 4. Expense Discipline and Operating Leverage


- Q2 2026 marked the 11th consecutive quarter of positive operating leverage (+4%).
- Expenses were up 10% YoY, but ex-performance comp, up only 4%.
- Management targets continued positive operating leverage on an annual basis.
- Any deviation from this trend or unexpected cost pressures would be a key watch item.


### 5. Capital Deployment and Balance Sheet


- CET1 ratio at Q2 2026 was 13.6%, up 20 bps QoQ.
- Caribbean divestiture (pending close) will add ~25 bps to CET1 but is expected to be slightly dilutive to EPS.
- New 30 million share NCIB (just over 3% of shares outstanding) announced.
- Management reiterates focus on organic growth, active buybacks, and only tuck-in M&A.


### 6. U.S. and Wealth Strategy


- Recent U.S. wealth tuck-in acquisition (&Partners, $54B AUM) aligns with strategy to grow in fast-growing segments.
- U.S. business now reporting strong ROE and growth after years of foundational investment.
- Any update on further U.S. expansion, integration, or performance will be important.

## Management Commentary and Guidance


**Margin Outlook:**


- "We maintain our expectation of a stable to gradual positive bias on net interest margins over time."
- "The hedging strategy... will continue to kick off at the all bank level, we think, in the 1 to 1.5 basis points, gradually falling away at some point in 2027."


**Credit Quality:**


- "As we look to the second half of fiscal 2026, I would expect impaired provisions to be broadly in line with the levels we've seen in the first half of the year."
- "We remain confident in the quality of our portfolio and in our ability to manage through the current environment."


**Expense and Operating Leverage:**


- "We continue to pace our expenses relative to our strong revenue growth and delivered solidly positive operating leverage again this quarter."
- "We do like to have that positive operating leverage. We're not going to -- we target it every quarter. We're not saying we're going to deliver it every quarter. It's nice to have a 10-quarter winning streak for sure, and we intend to continue it. But we do target on an annual basis."


**Capital Deployment:**


- "Organic growth remains our primary focus, we also leverage dividends, share buybacks and select inorganic opportunities to drive long-term shareholder value."
- "We're happy to run with a strong balance sheet. It's there to support our clients. It's there to protect our shareholders and stakeholders."

## What Changed / Intra-Quarter Developments


- **Caribbean Divestiture:** Announced sale of 92% stake in CIBC Caribbean for ~$1.6B (USD), expected to close H1 2027, adding ~25 bps to CET1.
- **U.S. Wealth Tuck-In:** Minority interest acquired in &Partners, a fast-growing U.S. RIA/broker-dealer ($54B AUM).
- **Segment Realignment:** Announced realignment to four SBUs (Personal & Business Banking, Commercial Banking, Wealth Management, Capital Markets) effective Q4 2026.
- **Buyback Upsized:** New 30 million share NCIB announced after completing prior 20 million share program.
- **Commercial Banking Leadership:** Canadian and U.S. commercial banking now unified under one leader to drive North American connectivity.

## Conclusion


CIBC is set to report Q3 2026 results against a tough y/y comparable, with consensus expecting continued but more normalized growth. The most important factors to watch are the sustainability of NIM expansion, credit quality (especially impaired provisions), commercial lending momentum, and expense discipline. Management's guidance remains constructive, with a focus on organic growth, prudent capital deployment, and maintaining positive operating leverage. Any change in tone or deviation from these trends will be closely scrutinized by investors.
