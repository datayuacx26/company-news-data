# Palo Alto Networks Q4 2026 Earnings Preview: PANW Results Preview

## Palo Alto Networks (PANW) — Q4 2026 Earnings Preview


### Key Factors to Watch This Quarter


Factor Details & What to Watch


**Revenue Growth** Consensus: $3,352M (+32% YoY). Guidance: $3,345M–$3,355M. Acceleration from prior quarters; watch for upside.


**NGS ARR** Guidance: $8.90B–$8.95B (+59–60% YoY). Key driver: CyberArk/Chronosphere M&A, organic growth, Prisma AIRS.


**RPO (Backlog)** Guidance: $20.9B–$21.0B (+32–33% YoY). Indicator of future revenue visibility and large deal momentum.


**Non-GAAP EPS** Guidance: $0.96–$0.98. Consensus: $0.97. Margin stability post-M&A is a focus.


**Operating Margin** Guidance: 28.9–29.2%. Watch for integration cost impact and synergy realization.


**Free Cash Flow Margin** Guidance: 37.5%. Execution on 40% FY28 target is a key long-term signal.


**Platformization Progress** Platformized customers, NRR, churn. Management targets >4,000 by FY30; Q3 at ~2,280.


**AI Security/Prisma AIRS** Fastest-growing product ever; >300 customers in Q3. Visibility to $100M ARR in coming quarters.


**CyberArk/Chronosphere M&A** Integration progress, cross-sell pipeline, and margin convergence.


**Hardware/SASE/Software FW** Hardware bookings up nearly 40% YoY in Q3; SASE ARR +40% YoY; software firewall ARR +25% YoY.


---


## Summary and Conclusions


- **Q4 2026 is set up for a strong print:** Consensus and guidance both call for +32% YoY revenue growth, a sharp acceleration from the prior year (+16% in Q4 2025). This is driven by robust demand for AI security, platformization, and the first full quarter with CyberArk and Chronosphere.
- **NGS ARR and RPO are the headline KPIs:** Both are expected to show 59–60% and 32–33% YoY growth, respectively, reflecting both organic strength and M&A contributions.
- **Margins and cash flow in focus:** Despite large acquisitions, PANW guides to stable non-GAAP operating margins (28.9–29.2%) and a 37.5% free cash flow margin, supporting their FY28 40%+ FCF margin target.
- **AI security traction is a differentiator:** Prisma AIRS is scaling rapidly, with management calling it the fastest-growing product in company history and clear line of sight to $100M ARR.
- **Integration execution is critical:** Management claims CyberArk profitability will converge with PANW’s core business 3–6 months ahead of plan; watch for evidence in segment-level results and commentary.
- **Year-over-year comparables are favorable:** Q4 2025 revenue grew just 16% YoY, so the current quarter faces an easier comp, amplifying the expected acceleration.

## Last Year’s Q4 — Context for Comparables


- **Q4 2025 Revenue:** $2,502M (+16% YoY)
- **NGS ARR:** $5.6B (+32% YoY)
- **RPO:** $15.8B (+24% YoY)
- **Non-GAAP EPS:** $0.95
- **Operating Margin:** 28.8%
- **Free Cash Flow Margin:** 38%


**Takeaway:** Q4 2025 was a solid but not exceptional quarter, with mid-teens revenue growth and strong cash flow. The Q4 2026 guide implies a major acceleration (+32% YoY revenue), making for a relatively easy comp and raising expectations for a standout print.

## Intra-Quarter and Management Commentary


- **Bookings and Pipeline:** Management cited “accelerating organic bookings growth” and “record quarterly outperformance” in Q3, with a strong Q4 pipeline.
- **AI Security:** Prisma AIRS is now at 300+ customers (tripled QoQ), with “clear visibility towards $100 million in ARR over the next couple of quarters.”
- **CyberArk/Chronosphere:** Both are exceeding internal benchmarks post-close; CyberArk profitability convergence is now expected 3–6 months ahead of plan.
- **Platformization:** Platformized customer count at ~2,280 in Q3, with a target of >4,000 by FY30. NRR for this cohort is 120% with single-digit churn.
- **Hardware/SASE/Software Firewall:** Hardware bookings up nearly 40% YoY in Q3 (best in a decade); SASE ARR +40% YoY; software firewall ARR +25% YoY.
- **Margin Outlook:** Management remains confident in achieving 40%+ free cash flow margin by FY28, even after integrating large acquisitions.


---


## What to Watch in the Print


- **Top-line Beat:** Any upside to the $3.35B revenue guide will be viewed positively, especially if organic growth is strong.
- **NGS ARR Trajectory:** Watch for continued acceleration and color on organic vs. acquired growth.
- **AI Security Monetization:** Prisma AIRS ARR and customer count, as well as broader AI security pipeline.
- **M&A Integration:** Evidence of cross-sell, margin improvement, and early synergy realization from CyberArk and Chronosphere.
- **Platformization Metrics:** Net new platformizations, NRR, and churn rates.
- **Margin and FCF Delivery:** Ability to maintain or expand margins and FCF despite integration costs and higher share count.
- **Hardware and SASE Momentum:** Confirmation that hardware and SASE tailwinds are sustainable into FY27.


---


## Conclusion


Palo Alto Networks enters Q4 2026 earnings with strong momentum, favorable comps, and high expectations for both organic and acquired growth. The key debate is whether the company can sustain its rapid top-line acceleration, deliver on AI security monetization, and execute on large-scale integrations without margin slippage. Investors will be focused on evidence of durable platformization, AI-driven demand, and early synergy capture from recent M&A.
