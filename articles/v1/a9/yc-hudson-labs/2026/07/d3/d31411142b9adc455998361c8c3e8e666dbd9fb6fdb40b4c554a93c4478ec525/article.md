## Summary and Conclusions


- **Q3 2026 consensus and guidance both point to another record quarter:** Revenue is expected at ~$3.9B, up +34% YoY, with adjusted EPS guided to $3.30 at the midpoint.
- **Growth is broad-based but led by Industrial and Communications:** Industrial is expected to remain ~50% of revenue, with data center/AI-related demand a major driver. Automotive is rebounding faster than expected, especially in China and Europe.
- **Gross margin is at historic highs:** Q2 adjusted GM was 73.0%, but Q3 is guided slightly lower (~72.3% consensus), reflecting the absence of a one-time channel repricing benefit. Mix and pricing remain tailwinds.
- **No signs of double ordering or restocking:** Management continues to emphasize lean channel inventories and order patterns consistent with end demand.
- **Pricing actions are largely complete for 2026:** Q2 saw the full effect of recent price increases; Q3 will not benefit from further one-time price lifts, but ongoing ASP strength is expected from new products and innovation premium.
- **Capacity is not a constraint:** ADI has doubled internal capacity since 2022 and maintains strong external foundry partnerships. Management says they can support up to $20B in annual revenue.
- **Empower Semiconductor acquisition:** Expected to close in H2 2026, bringing proprietary IVR technology for vertical power delivery—potentially a significant TAM expansion in data center/AI.
- **Comparables are favorable:** Q3 2025 revenue was $2,880M, so the YoY comp is not unusually tough; ADI is coming off a period of accelerating growth.

## Management Commentary — Key Themes


- **Demand Environment:** "We continued to see growing demand in the second quarter with record bookings across our B2B markets of Industrial, Automotive, and Communications... These positive demand signals are reflected in our outlook for continued strong growth in the third quarter."
- **Pricing:** "We have increased prices during the course of this year... the pricing impact was pretty much as expected. For Q3, we are assuming about a 50 bps decline in gross margin, largely driven by the absence of that onetime benefit we got from repricing the channel during the prior quarter."
- **Capacity:** "We have the capacity to support up to the $20 billion that we've been talking about as part of our 2030 vision."
- **AI/Data Center:** "Data center, which now accounts for more than 75% of our communications revenue, was up more than 90% year-over-year, driven by both our optical and power portfolios."
- **Empower Acquisition:** "With Empower, we gain cutting-edge proprietary integrated voltage regulator, or IVR, technology... This transaction will expand ADI's total addressable market within the hypergrowth AI accelerator space and further solidify our position as an indispensable hardware partner..."

## Conclusion


- **Expectations are high for Q3 2026:** Both consensus and company guidance imply another record quarter, with broad-based growth and continued margin expansion.
- **Key factors to watch:** Sustainability of AI/data center demand, ability to maintain pricing power, incremental margin upside from mix, and any signs of restocking or double ordering.
- **Management tone remains confident:** ADI is executing well on both cyclical and secular drivers, with capacity and supply chain flexibility to support continued growth.
- **No red flags on comparables:** The YoY comp is not unusually difficult, and the company is not lapping a one-off spike.
- **Upside risks:** Faster-than-expected adoption of vertical power/IVR in data center, further acceleration in auto/industrial, or additional pricing actions.
- **Downside risks:** Macro/geopolitical shocks, sudden demand normalization in AI/data center, or unexpected supply chain constraints.


**Overall, ADI enters Q3 2026 with strong momentum, robust demand signals, and a favorable setup for continued outperformance.**
