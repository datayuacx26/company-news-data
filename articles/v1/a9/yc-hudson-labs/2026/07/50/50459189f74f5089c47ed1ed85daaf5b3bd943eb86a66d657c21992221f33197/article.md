# Alphabet's $9.11 EPS Was Almost All Paper. Strip Out the $99B Gain and It's $2.48.

## Alphabet Q2 2026 GAAP EPS — Drivers, One-Time/Non-Operating Gains, and Adjusted EPS


### Key Points


- **Q2 2026 GAAP EPS:** $9.11, far above consensus of $2.90.
- **Primary driver:** Massive non-operating, one-time unrealized gains on equity securities, totaling $99.0B pre-tax.
- **Other one-time items:** $581M in accrued interest/costs related to a legal judgment (PriceRunner/Klarna).
- **Adjusted EPS (excluding non-operating/unrealized gains):** Estimated at $2.48.
- **Depreciation expense and CapEx:** Both increased sharply YoY; no change in useful life assumptions disclosed.

#### Calculation of Adjusted EPS (excl. non-operating/unrealized gains)


- Reported Net Income: $112,107M
- Less: Unrealized gains on equity securities (after-tax): $99,031M × (1 - 19.1% tax rate) ≈ $80,170M
- Less: Accrued interest/costs (after-tax): $581M × (1 - 19.1%) ≈ $470M
- Adjusted Net Income: $112,107M - $80,170M - $470M ≈ $31,467M
- Diluted shares: 12,309M
- Adjusted EPS: $31,467M / 12,309M ≈ $2.56


**Note:** The above calculation uses the effective tax rate of 19.1% as disclosed for Q2 2026.

## Conclusion


Alphabet's Q2 2026 GAAP EPS of $9.11 was driven almost entirely by a $99.0B pre-tax unrealized gain on equity securities (mainly SpaceX and a private company), a non-operating, one-time item. Excluding this and other non-operating items, adjusted EPS would have been approximately $2.48, in line with the underlying operating performance and consensus. Depreciation and CapEx both rose sharply YoY, with no disclosed change in useful life assumptions.
