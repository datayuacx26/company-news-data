# Arista Networks Q2 2026 Earnings Preview: ANET Results Preview

**Arista Networks (ANET) — Earnings Preview Memo**


---


### Key Preview Takeaways


Factor Details & Signals to Watch


**Q2 2026 Guidance** Revenue guide: ~$2.8B; Non-GAAP op margin: 46–47%; Non-GAAP EPS: ~$0.88; Gross margin: 62–63%


**Full-Year 2026 Guidance** Revenue raised to $11.5B (+28% YoY); AI revenue target raised to $3.5B; Campus target held at $1.25B


**Supply Chain Constraints** Management highlights ongoing industry-wide shortages (memory, silicon, wafers, optics); watch for impact on shipments, deferred revenue, and gross margin pressure. Purchase commitments up sharply to $8.9B.


**AI/Cloud Demand** Demand remains at record highs, especially from hyperscalers, neoclouds, and specialty AI providers. Scale-across networking is now expected to be ~1/3 of AI revenue in 2026.
