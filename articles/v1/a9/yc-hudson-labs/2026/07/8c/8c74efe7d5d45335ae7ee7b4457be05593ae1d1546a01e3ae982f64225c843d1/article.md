### Best-supported “quiet changes”


- **10x Genomics (TXG)** is the clearest example of a **material softening** in a requested category. The prior filing said the company and customers were “dependent on single source and sole source suppliers,” while the new filing reframes this to say only “some” components are single-sourced and adds that it has “qualified second sources for some, but not all” components.
- **Arthur J. Gallagher (AJG)** shows a clear **AI risk-factor expansion** from generic AI risk language to more specific disclosure about **AI-enabled cyberattacks** and **competitors/new entrants gaining advantage from AI** .
- **Sotera Health (SHC)** keeps the limited-counterparty concept but **expands** it with more concrete supplier/material exposure, including **EO, Co-60** , and sanctions-related disruption.
- **Nike (NKE)** shows an **updated AI/privacy/cyber framing** : the older wording emphasized inadvertent disclosure risk from AI features, while the newer wording shifts toward **generative AI regulation** and increasingly sophisticated threat actors.
- **Neptune Insurance (NP)** and **GE Vernova (GEV)** are notable because the retrieved evidence suggests the core risk remained, but the newer filing added **mitigation-oriented language** , which can read as a subtle softening in tone even though the underlying risk statement was retained.


### What the retrieved evidence does **not** support


- No company in the supplied matches has a clearly evidenced **removed** risk factor with quoted prior language and proof of omission in the latest filing.
- Many companies in the batches appear to have **new AI, customer concentration, supplier dependence, or data-center-capacity language** , but the matches usually lack the prior filing text, so they cannot be reliably classified here as added/removed/reworded with before/after quotes.


### Partial-evidence names that look relevant but are not fully supportable here


The batches surfaced many likely candidates—such as **UAL, WDC, SMTC, AMT, DLR, CLS, CRWV, GLXY, NCNO, BLCO, GEHC, SailPoint, SharkNinja, Rubrik, Waystar, and others** —for single supplier dependence, customer concentration, AI competition, or data-center capacity commitments.
