# Elon Musk Predictions & Outcomes: 2019 to 2026

In honour of Tesla earnings, here is a list of predictions and promises made by Elon Musk from 2019 to now. We've included an indicator of which were achieved, which weren't and the eventual date of the outcome, if any. *Coverage window: Feb 19, 2019 to 2026-05-18 — 151 public predictions by Elon Musk about Tesla, assessed as of 2026-07-16.*


*"Result" = whether the prediction came true by its predicted deadline (No / Yes / Yes (qualified) / Partly / Unresolved / Not yet assessable).*
