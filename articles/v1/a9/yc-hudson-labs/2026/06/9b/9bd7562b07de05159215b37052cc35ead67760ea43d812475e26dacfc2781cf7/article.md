# Hudson Labs Deep Dive for Creators, Journalists & Media

*Original, data-driven research — co-branded and free for media partners.*


[Hudson Labs Deep Dive](https://www.hudson-labs.com/blog/introducing-hudson-labs-deep-dive) runs long-running, parallel AI agents across hundreds of documents and web sources to produce original, data-driven research — **the kind of analysis that would cost more than $10,000 in API tokens to reproduce, and that generalist agents routinely get wrong.**


We’re opening it up to creators, journalists, and media at no cost. You bring the question or the angle; we run the Deep Dive and co-brand the result with your name and logo.


## **What you get**


- A complete Deep Dive built to your brief — for example, an analysis of 500+ documents or web sources.
- **Co-branding:** your name and logo appear on the Deep Dive alongside ours.
- **Optional in-platform feature:** we can add your article, newsletter, or note — or a link to it — alongside the analysis on the Hudson Labs platform.
- **No cost.** This program is free for qualifying creators, journalists, and media.


## **Why Hudson Labs Deep Dive**


Complex multi-agent workflows are costly and unreliable. People spend thousands of dollars on generalist, agent-driven analysis that misses the mark — incorrect, noisy, or incomplete results. Deep Dive is our response: agentic analysis at a fraction of the cost and the highest standard of quality.


**Recent Deep Dives:**


- [The AI Ecosystem Stack — Market Map:](https://www.hudson-labs.com/research/the-ai-ecosystem-stack-market-map) 500+ companies mapped by layer, segment and criticality.
- [The Trump Bump](https://www.hudson-labs.com/research/trump-praised-companies?s=2) : Companies that received positive mentions from Trump + the subsequent price change.
- [The Jensen Bump:](https://www.hudson-labs.com/research/nvidia-jensen-company-mentions-computex) Companies mentioned during the Nvidia CEO’s keynote.


## **Sizing your request**


For the fastest approval, keep requests under 500 data points — for example, around 100 companies over less than 5 years. We welcome more involved requests, but they must be high impact to be approved.


## **How it works**


1. Pitch your idea — submit your request[here](https://www.hudson-labs.com/request-deep-dive) .
2. If approved, we confirm the brief and return initial results — straightforward requests within the week.
3. You review and send feedback; we complete and return the full, co-branded analysis.
4. You publish your article, newsletter, or note featuring the Deep Dive.


## **Exclusivity window**


The Deep Dive stays exclusive to you until you publish your article, newsletter, or note about it — or for 2 weeks, whichever comes first. After that, it becomes available to all Hudson Labs subscribers with your co-branding intact, and we can optionally add your piece or a link to it on the platform.


**Available data sources:**


- Conference & earnings calls
- SEC filings
- Fundamental & market metrics
- Consensus estimates
- FRED: Federal Reserve Economic Data
- The Federal Register
- SEC Comment Letters, Enforcement Actions, Litigation Reviews, Stop Orders
- The US Federal Budget
- And more


Restrictions on data sources: Hudson Labs only supports data sources for which we have explicit access and redistribution rights. Your internal data sources cannot currently be incorporated into Deep Dives. We do not engage in unlawful scraping activities.
