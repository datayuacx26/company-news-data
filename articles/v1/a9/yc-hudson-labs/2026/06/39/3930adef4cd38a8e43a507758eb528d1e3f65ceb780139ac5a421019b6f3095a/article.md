# Hudson Labs vs. BamSEC: SEC Filing & AI Research Software Compared

Both platforms make SEC filings, earnings calls, and exhibits fast and easy to navigate. Here is a detailed, feature-by-feature comparison to help you decide which one fits your workflow.


[BamSEC](https://www.bamsec.com/) is SEC filing navigation software that quickly gained popularity among institutional investors thanks to its speed, simplicity, and focused user interface. It remains a gold standard for user-centric design in financial software. BamSEC performs a narrow function — SEC filing navigation and exploration — but it does so with speed and accuracy. BamSEC recently joined the AlphaSense family of applications through acquisition.


[Hudson Labs](https://www.hudson-labs.com/) was founded in 2019 as an SEC-filing-focused AI company, and built the first financial services application fully powered by large language models (LLMs). As the only AI platform with a No Hallucination Guarantee, it is popular among filing-focused analysts who have zero tolerance for error. Like BamSEC, the Hudson Labs Co-Analyst is a focused tool: it ensures that restatement-adjusted numbers, guidance, and materiality-ranked commentary are pulled directly from the source, without AI noise.

## Table Extraction


Both Hudson Labs and BamSEC let you export tables from SEC filings and Exhibits, and both support multi-period table merging.


Unique to Hudson Labs


**Mix and match** tables and commentary across 10-Ks, 10-Qs, press releases, earnings and conference calls, 8-Ks, and more. The Hudson Labs Co-Analyst extracts error-free, restatement-adjusted figures for up to 5 years or 20 quarters. All numbers extracted using Hudson Labs AI are backed by a **No Hallucination Guarantee** .


Hudson Labs: mix-and-match table extraction with restatement-adjusted figures.BamSEC: multi-period table merge.

## Watchlists & Alerts


Both BamSEC and Hudson Labs offer robust watchlist and alerting capabilities.


Unique to BamSEC


BamSEC alerts email you any time a filing for a watchlist company is added to EDGAR.


Unique to Hudson Labs


Hudson Labs starts a series of AI tasks when specific filings are issued or calls are finished. For instance, the Co-Analyst can check for red flags in the 10-K or 10-Q and then email you any findings.


## The Bottom Line


BamSEC earned its reputation for a reason. It is fast, simple, and a benchmark for user-centric design, and for analysts who need clean SEC filing navigation — categorized views, key exhibits, transcripts, PDF export, and EDGAR filing alerts — it does the job well.


If your work goes beyond navigation, the comparison shifts. Hudson Labs delivers the same core filing navigation and table extraction, then adds an AI layer that BamSEC does not offer: advanced AI, consensus estimates, management guidance, fundamental metrics, sentiment analysis, AI agents, peer comparators, catalyst identification, and accounting red flags — all backed by a No Hallucination Guarantee.


Because Hudson Labs includes a two-week free trial, current BamSEC users can put both tools side by side at no cost and decide which one fits the way they actually work.
