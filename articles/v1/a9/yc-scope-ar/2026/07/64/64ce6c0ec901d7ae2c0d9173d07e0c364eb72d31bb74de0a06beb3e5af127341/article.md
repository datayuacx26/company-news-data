# Scope AR Attends Qualcomm 40th Anniversary Party at the Smithsonian

For the second year, Scope AR showcased our groundbreaking augmented reality solutions to the aviation industry at MRO Americas, this year in Atlanta, GA. We were pleased to join our partner FTAI Aviation in hosting a happy hour event, where our teams were able to collaboratively present the excellent work being done with Scope AR's WorkLink to significantly reduce task times at FTAI.
