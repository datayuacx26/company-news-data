By Chris · April 2025


## Introducing automated appointment reminders


Toma has launched a solution designed to reduce no-shows: Automated Appointment Reminders. This conversational AI system makes natural-sounding reminder calls, handles rebooking when cancellations occur, and protects service schedules and revenue. Dealerships lose thousands monthly due to service no-show rates between 18-20%.


## How it works


- Opt-in management: only customers who have opted in receive calls from Toma.
- Fully customizable: configure reminder schedules and messages for all appointments or specific service types.
- On-call rescheduling: AI actively converts potential cancellations into rescheduled appointments during the same call.
