# Solstice Advanced Materials Declares Dividend of $0.075 per Common Share

## Solstice Advanced Materials Declares Dividend of $0.075 per Common Share


July 17, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investor.solstice.com/node/7611/pdf)


MORRIS PLAINS, N.J.


, July 17, 2026


/PRNewswire/ --[Solstice Advanced Materials](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4733217-1&h=331435454&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.solstice.com%2Fus%2Fen%2Fhome&a=Solstice+Advanced+Materials) (NASDAQ: SOLS), today announced that its Board of Directors has declared a regular quarterly dividend payment of seven and a half cents ($0.075) per share of the Company's common stock. The dividend will be payable on September 10, 2026, to shareowners of record as of the close of business on August 27, 2026.


**About Solstice** **Advanced Materials**
Solstice Advanced Materials is a leading global specialty materials company that advances science for smarter outcomes. Solstice offers high-performance solutions that enable critical industries and applications, including refrigerants, semiconductor manufacturing, data center cooling, nuclear power, protective fibers, healthcare packaging and more. Solstice is recognized for developing next-generation materials through some of the industry's most renowned brands such as Solstice®, Genetron®, Aclar®, Spectra®, Fluka™ and Hydranal™. Partnering with over 3,000 customers across more than 120 countries and territories and supported by a robust portfolio of over 5,700 patents and pending applications, Solstice's approximately 4,100 employees worldwide drive innovation in materials science. For more information, visit[www.Solstice.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4733217-1&h=774741879&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.solstice.com%2F&a=www.Solstice.com) .
