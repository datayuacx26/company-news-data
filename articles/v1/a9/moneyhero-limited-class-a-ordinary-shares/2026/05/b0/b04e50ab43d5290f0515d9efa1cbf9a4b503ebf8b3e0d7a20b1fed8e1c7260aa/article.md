# Jacqueline Thong

## Jacqueline Thong


Director


Ms. Thong is a Senior Vice President with Pacific Century Group, responsible for corporate finance, venture investment, and major mergers and acquisitions. Since 2019, she has led the execution of over US$4 billion in capital raising across private equity, bank facilities, and publicly traded fixed income instruments. Her experience also includes private investments in technology ventures spanning fintech, artificial intelligence, and consumer sectors, providing her with a strong perspective on corporate strategy.
