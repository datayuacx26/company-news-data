# MoneyHero Group Announces Resignation of Director

## MoneyHero Group Announces Resignation of Director


29-May-2026


[PDF Version](https://moneyhero.gcs-web.com/node/8041/pdf)


**Hong Kong, May 29, 2026 (ET)** –[MoneyHero Limited](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=W9D-T-wS7cYNyag3UxkBD31xc3zFe0ynpovh3RlWFgwB6c_QFotvFABFdK08-DkT_EGrl5BbjDY5GiDNlY55CxMC6THaH4XYu69mA54mWw8=) (NASDAQ:[MNY](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=0HF0nyNFujg61tOEKJPFpE_O5ANYYY24JSvWwxh92F8TuCnMC0KhHEtYvbrsRPzxq_StHzYBD9ZHITBicaCV9VCc3H5Hnr9R-PSFq79Uf3c=) ) (“MoneyHero” or the “Company”), a leading tech- and AI-powered personal finance aggregation and comparison platform and a digital insurance brokerage provider in Greater Southeast Asia, today announced that the Company’s Board of Directors (the “Board”) has accepted the resignation of Rohith Murthy from his position as a Director of the Company, effective May 26, 2026.


**Steven Teichman, Chairman of the Board** , commented: “On behalf of the Board, I would like to thank Rohith for his years of service to MoneyHero. We are grateful for his contributions to the Company and appreciate the dedication he has shown throughout his tenure. We wish him continued success in his next chapter.”


**Rohith Murthy** added, “Serving MoneyHero has been a privilege. I am proud of what the teams across our markets have built and the progress we have made in strengthening the Company’s foundation for the next phase of growth. I remain confident in MoneyHero’s future, and I wish the Board, the leadership team, and our employees continued success.”
