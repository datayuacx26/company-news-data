- [Boston votes to install 250 curbside EV-chargers on city streets (wcvb.com)](https://www.wcvb.com/article/boston-votes-to-install-250-curbside-ev-chargers-on-city-streets/60013538) --> Boston is moving ahead with a plan to install electric vehicle chargers along city streets. - [StoreDot Unveils Cold-Proof EV Charging - The EV Report](https://theevreport.com/storedot-unveils-cold-proof-ev-charging) --> StoreDot introduces a new battery technology that ensures electric vehicles (EVs) maintain optimal range and performance, even in the coldest weather. This innovation eliminates the common winter range anxiety EV owners face, offering a reliable and efficient charging solution across all seasons.

- [Efficient Fleet Charging with Rightcharge - The EV Report](https://theevreport.com/efficient-fleet-charging-with-rightcharge) --> Rightcharge has introduced a home charging payment solution for fleets, enabling businesses to cover the costs of their employees’ electric vehicle (EV) home charging directly. This innovative service ensures payments are made straight to the employees’ energy accounts, irrespective of their energy supplier, simplifying the process for both employers and drivers.
