# Where to start with AI in property management

Talk about AI is so widespread that even a nursing home from the 1940s might tell you how you can use AI.


There's an overwhelming amount of fluff and this makes it hard to know what's steak and what's sizzle. And yet, you know **there's more to do than using ChatGPT on the job.**


## How other managers are evaluating solutions


We've surveyed over 100 property managers and owners to better understand their pain points, needs and how they evaluate new solutions.


Everyone's process will look slightly different, but overwhelmingly we heard property managers evaluate solutions based on:


- **Impact:** How much time or cost can we save? How much additional revenue can we earn?
- **Time to benefit:** How long does it take to see results?
- **Disruption to existing processes:** How difficult will adoption be?


An existing manager outlined the **benefits of IronLedger** based on this criteria:


⏱️


**Impact** 20+ hours saved and identified 3% wasteful and abusive spend


🚀


**Time to benefit** Rolled out in 2 weeks


🔀


**Disruption to existing processes** No app required. Just text or email IronLedger's agent


## How IronLedger builds high value solutions


The way IronLedger thinks about building features is based on:


🤕


**Customer Pain** How significant is the pain point that we're addressing?


🛠️


**Technical Feasibility** How challenging will it be to deliver the benefits for a given solution?


🌐


**Generalizability** How applicable is a feature for every additional property manager or developer?


We listened to over **100+ property managers and owners** before building our first feature. Customers told us that they were spending **20+ hours for every 100 transactions** and collecting receipts **delayed month end close** .


Now, we help our customers do this automatically. Our AI collects receipts, codes them based on historical data and user input, and uploads them to your property management software for you.
