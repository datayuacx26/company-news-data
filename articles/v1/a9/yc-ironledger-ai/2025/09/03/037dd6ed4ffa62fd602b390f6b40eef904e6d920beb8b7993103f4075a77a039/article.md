# IronLedger Seed Fundraise

AI can't fix your drywall. But it can save you 100s of hours on expenses.


After seeing property managers **lose profits** from missed card expenses **or spend hours** tracking down 100s of receipts from property management teams, IronLedger built software to streamline the process for everyone.


**Today, we're excited to announce that IronLedger raised $2.5M to save property managers time and money on accounting. Our overall vision is to use AI to reduce the cost to manage housing** .


IronLedger is already serving more than 35K multifamily units today and rapidly growing.


Over the last 6 years, home prices have increased 60% and gone from 3x median household income to 5x. Traditionally, 3x median household income is "considered unaffordable" ([Harvard's Joint Center for Housing Studies](https://www.jchs.harvard.edu/press-releases/new-report-highlights-unease-housing-market-amid-worsening-affordability-crisis) ).


New technology will be at the heart of addressing the affordability crisis. IronLedger will focus specifically on driving costs down for multifamily.
