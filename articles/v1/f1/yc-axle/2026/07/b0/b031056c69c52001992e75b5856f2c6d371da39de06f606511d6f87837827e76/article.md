# Introducing Flood Insurance

At Axle, our mission is to make insurance data accessible, secure, and instant for every business and consumer. We started with support for Auto, Home, Condo, and Renters insurance policies, helping our partners verify coverage, confirm policy details, and make confident decisions in seconds.


Today, we’re excited to announce the wide release of Flood Insurance policy support **-** now available to all Axle partners. This expands our capabilities and enabling our partners to access verified flood coverage details through the same API they already use for other policy types.


## Why Flood Insurance?


Flood insurance is a natural extension of our existing Home and Condo policy support. While many homeowners and condo associations carry flood protection as part of a broader coverage plan, these policies are often separate from standard homeowners insurance, making them harder to verify, track, and integrate into workflows.


By adding flood policy support, Axle removes the friction of manually confirming this critical coverage type. Now, property managers, lenders, and insurers can access flood policy data instantly just like they can for Auto, Home, Condo, and Renters insurance, ensuring that properties are fully protected and compliant.


## Benefits for Our Partners


- **Comprehensive coverage verification –** Ensure your users’ properties are protected against flood risk by checking policy status, limits, and coverage areas in seconds.
‍
- **Fewer manual touchpoints –** Skip the phone calls and email back-and-forth with carriers. Axle delivers verified flood policy details directly to your systems.
‍
- **Seamless integration –** Flood insurance support uses the same universal policy specification as all other policy types in Axle, so there’s little additional development work for your team.


## Unlock the Full Picture of Property Coverage


Whether you’re a lender confirming loan requirements, a property manager ensuring tenant compliance, or an insurer bundling coverage types, flood insurance data is now at your fingertips. By bringing flood policies into the Axle platform, we’re making it easier than ever to get a complete view of your customers’ protection.


## Get Started with Flood Insurance on Axle


Flood policy support is now available for all Axle partners. If you’re ready to expand your verification capabilities and access instant, reliable flood insurance data, our team can help you get started.
