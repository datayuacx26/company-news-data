# Introducing Document AI

At Axle, we're constantly looking for ways to improve the user experience and make insurance verification easier and more efficient for all users. That's why we're excited to introduce Document AI to all our customers, a feature that simplifies the insurance verification process for users who are unable to log in to their account.


## **What is Document AI?**


While users have always been able to upload their insurance card or declarations page manually through Axle as an alternative to logging in, Document AI radically improves this process by allowing users to scan or upload their document and automatically extracting policy information to Axle’s universal policy specification. This eliminates the need for manual data entry, speeding up the verification process significantly, ensuring broader compliance, and providing a great experience to every user.


## **Speed up existing document collection processes**


Previously, users or your staff had to manually enter policy information after uploading a document. With Document AI, this information is automatically extracted and available in the Axle Dashboard or directly within your existing systems via our network of supported platforms, saving time and reducing errors.


## **Ensure compliance**


Document AI validates that the uploaded document is an insurance document, not a photo of a pet. Extracting policy information directly from the document ensures that the data entered into your system matches the document, increasing accuracy and enforcing compliance to your requirements.


## **A great experience for every user**


We believe that insurance verification will be as ubiquitous as driver's license verification and used for every transaction. Document AI provides a solution for users who may not be able to complete the connection process, providing an improved user experience and a streamlined verification process.
