# Feature Spotlight: June 2025

This month we're excited to announce our new Platform Roles and Analytics in the Axle Dashboard, along with a number of other improvements!


## Axle Dashboard: Platform Roles


We’re excited to launch a major update to the Axle dashboard for our platform clients: **Platform Roles** .


### What's a Platform?


Our platform partners are typically software providers that provide many services to their customers, and want to offer Axle as a feature or add-on of their platform.


For example, a dealership management system might be a platform with many dealerships as customers. These dealerships want to use Axle to verify insurance information for their own users, but may not want to setup Axle themselves. As these dealerships are already accustomed to using their dealership management system, it's easier for them to use Axle if Axle is embedded into the platform that they're already using.


### What's New?


With **Platform Roles** , users can now navigate across both their platform client and any associated destination clients—all from a single login.


Whether you’re jumping into a specific policy, reviewing ignition data, or accessing a resource from a Slack or email notification, the experience is now faster, simpler, and more intuitive.


### New Roles


We’ve introduced two new roles:


- **Platform Admins**
- **Platform Members**


Users assigned to these roles can now seamlessly navigate between their platform client and any destination client—all from a single login. There’s no need to switch accounts or re-authenticate—just move between clients in a single, unified dashboard experience.


### What stays the same?


While navigation is unified, data remains strictly partitioned. Each client—whether platform or destination—still maintains its own set of resources. There’s no blended or cross-client data view, ensuring clean separation and control.


### Additional controls for platform admins


Platform admins can now invite and manage other platform admins and members, plus admins and members for any destination client, directly within the platform client. This makes it easier to set up and scale your organization’s access—without having to manage credentials across multiple tenants.
