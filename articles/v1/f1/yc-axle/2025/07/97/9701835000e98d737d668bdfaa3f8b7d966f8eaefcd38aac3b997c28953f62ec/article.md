# Feature Spotlight: July 2025

**This month we're excited to announce the launch policy validation templates for our clients and multi-image document scanning in ignition!**


# Policy Validation Templates


Axle’s[validation engine](https://www.axle.insure/product/validation-engine) helps our clients make smarter, faster insurance decisions every day.


Clients use validation engine to validate that insurance policies meet their requirements using configurations of the[validation rules](https://docs.axle.insure/guides/policy-validation#policy-validation) that we’ve built.


We’re finally launching one of our most popular requests and a powerful new way to scale your validation process - **Policy Validation Templates.**


Templates let you define sets of rules once and re-use them across policies, saving time by eliminating the need to manually maintain and update rule lists. You’re still able to provide dynamic inputs like user’s names into the templates.


Additionally,[platform clients](https://www.axle.insure/product/axle-for-platforms) can standardize templates organization-wide by creating shareable templates and ensure consistency in policy validation across all of their downstream clients.


# Multi-Image Document Upload


We’re always looking for ways to make it easier for users to share their insurance with Axle. This is what drove us to build our[Document AI](https://www.axle.insure/product/document-ai) product, which allows users to share their insurance data by uploading an image of their ID card or Declarations page inside an ignition session.


While this works extremely well for most users, some users were struggling to send us pictures of multi-page documents that they were taking real-world photographs of.


That’s why we’ve introduced an improved image upload experience that allows you to upload and manage multiple pages of you document at a time.


This has replaced what was formerly a potential point of confusion for some users with an opportunity to make the insurance sharing process even smoother!
