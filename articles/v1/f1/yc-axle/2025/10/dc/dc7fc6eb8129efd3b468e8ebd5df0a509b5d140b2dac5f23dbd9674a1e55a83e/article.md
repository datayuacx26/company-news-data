# Introducing support for Renters Insurance

At Axle, we’re building the infrastructure to power real-time access to insurance data across every line of coverage. Today, we're excited to announce that **Axle now supports renters (HO-4) insurance** .


With this launch, partners can now verify renters insurance policies alongside auto, home, and flood — helping streamline operations, improve compliance, and enhance the end-user experience.


## **Why Renters Insurance?**


Renters insurance is a standard requirement in residential leasing and property management, but verifying coverage has historically been slow, manual, and unreliable. Property managers and landlords often depend on tenant-provided PDFs or declarations pages — with no way to confirm the policy is active or meets requirements.


By extending support to renters insurance, Axle helps solve that gap, bringing **speed, accuracy, and automation** to a historically offline process.


## **Benefits for Our Partners**


With renters insurance now supported, partners can leverage the same Axle capabilities they already use across other lines of insurance — including Verification, Monitoring, Validation Engine, and DocAI — all built on a universal policy spec that ensures consistency and scalability.


This means you get a single, unified framework for accessing and managing policy data, no matter the coverage type — renters, auto, home, or flood.


Key benefits include:


- **Instant policy verification** via real-time carrier connections
- **Continuous monitoring** for policy changes and ongoing compliance
- **Automated compliance check** (via Axle's Validation Engine)
- **Document intelligence** with DocAI for faster, more accurate document parsing
- **Less manual review** and improved operational efficiency
- **Streamlined onboarding** and an enhanced end-user experience
- **Flexible input methods** , including insurer login and document upload


Whether you’re managing properties, building tenant screening workflows, or embedding insurance into rental platforms, Axle brings the same speed, reliability, and automation you expect — now extended to renters insurance.


## **Get Started with Renters Insurance on Axle**


This is just the beginning. We’re expanding our carrier network to support broader renters insurance coverage in the coming months. Reach out to our team to get started.
