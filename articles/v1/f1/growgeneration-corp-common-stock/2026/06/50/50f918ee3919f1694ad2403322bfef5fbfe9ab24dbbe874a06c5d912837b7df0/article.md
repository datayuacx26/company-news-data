# GrowGeneration to Present at the iAccess Alpha Virtual Best Ideas Summer Investment Conference 2026 on June 23, 2026

DENVER, June 22, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- **GrowGeneration Corp. (NASDAQ: GRWG)** (“GrowGeneration,” “GrowGen” or “the Company”), one of the nation’s largest suppliers of specialty products for controlled environment agriculture (CEA), commercial cultivation, and garden centers, today announced that management will participate in the iAccess Alpha Virtual Best Ideas Summer Investment Conference 2026, to be held on June 23–24, 2026.


Darren Lampert, Chief Executive Officer and Co-Founder of GrowGeneration, will deliver a company presentation on Tuesday, June 23, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time. A webcast of the presentation will be available on the GrowGeneration Investor Relations site at[https://ir.growgeneration.com/news-events/events](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=IQ_6Ya7tdKTiW5w0vrYjUB-ka0Z4YxXocJ6_WE0ocCAyQRatCOk44P6xNfY_fVfaI7EX8N11NfkcPnL8-bu45FOQ6j6eQYtIp5X3xVOGQckxS3yTj27K5BMrdiZPdfue-dAqBH_71IMFbvsTm4NHTUmcP-jhQ8wftwxVwQ_CQ9w=) or via this link:[https://www.webcaster5.com/Webcast/Page/3165/54184](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=IQ_6Ya7tdKTiW5w0vrYjUAfd6Bsstqf6gsajh5Aozir5gNcfv_QYWOz1Jy9t9S9sPAwjIWZIGb_YuEI6AV-BoAloIel_k48F80YJrymCR3pve9DNMIoM0rrtaAZ8sPlekrS5vtpyoP7pWljSm7asOHcf27_ixheYUIggadA2X7M=)

**About GrowGeneration Corp:**


GrowGen is one of the nation’s largest suppliers of specialty products for controlled environment agriculture (CEA), commercial cultivation, and garden centers. GrowGen carries and sells thousands of products, such as nutrients, additives, growing media, lighting, environmental control systems, and benching and racking, including proprietary brands such as Char Coir, Drip Hydro, Power Si, Ion lights, The Harvest Company, and more. The Company also operates an online superstore for cultivators at[growgeneration.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=bPPA_wkP8A2fLZC0XSl0TLQ9ONW3gqWoIoMV4NyDBs6Sf_bdNgi3rvC0Yoc-YOV3rETsw7BFcScqTOUQXSltGJn5zBd2d46wYeRoVlK2ZIc=) , as well as a wholesale business for resellers, and a benching, racking, and storage solutions business, MMI Storage Solutions.
