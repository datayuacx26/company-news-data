# How to Find Cheap Business Class Flights with Points in 2026


A nonstop business class flight from New York to London costs between[$3,200 and $5,000 round trip](https://arangrant.com/en-us/blog/average-business-class-ticket-price/) , depending on the airline and season. That same lie-flat seat can be booked for as few as 50,000 to 60,000 credit card points through the right transfer partner.


Most people assume business class is reserved for corporate travelers or the wealthy. They sit in economy with 100,000 points in their Chase or Amex account, not realizing those points could get them two business class flights to Europe. The value gap is enormous: redeeming through a credit card portal gives you 1 to 1.5 cents per point. Transferring to the right airline program can give you 5 to 10 cents per point on business class, or 2 to 20 times the portal value.


This guide shows you exactly which programs offer the cheapest business class awards, how many points you need, and how to search and book step by step. At Pointhound, we track business class award availability across 200+ airlines and 21 loyalty programs. We surface these deals every day, and one booking typically pays for years of Pointhound Premium.


## What Does Business Class Actually Cost in Points?


Business class award flights start as low as 40,000 points one-way through the right program, for seats that cost $2,500 to $5,000 in cash on transatlantic routes alone.


To put this in perspective, here is what you would pay in cash versus points for business class on popular routes, based on[AranGrant's 2025 fare analysis](https://arangrant.com/en-us/blog/average-business-class-ticket-price/) :


- **U.S. to London:** Cash median $3,200-$3,700. Through Iberia Avios:[40,500 points one-way off-peak](https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/airline-award-chart-sweet-spots/) (81,000 round trip). That is roughly 4 to 5 cents per point in value.
- **U.S. to Paris:** Cash median $3,100-$3,300. Through[Air France Flying Blue: 45,000 to 60,000 miles one-way](https://thriftytraveler.com/guides/points/best-airline-miles-business-class/) . During monthly Promo Rewards, rates can drop as low as 40,000 miles.
- **U.S. to Tokyo:** Cash median $5,100-$5,400 on nonstop carriers. Through[ANA Mileage Club: 100,000 miles round trip](https://thriftytraveler.com/guides/points/best-airline-miles-business-class/) in business class. Through AAdvantage via Japan Airlines:[60,000 miles one-way](https://thriftytraveler.com/guides/points/best-airline-miles-business-class/) .
- **U.S. to Doha (Qatar Qsuite):** Cash median $5,500-$6,300. Through[Qatar Privilege Club: 70,000 Avios one-way](https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/airline-award-chart-sweet-spots/) for one of the best business class products in the world.
- **Domestic U.S. (coast-to-coast):** Through[Aeroplan: around 25,000 points for United Polaris business](https://thriftytraveler.com/guides/points/best-airline-miles-business-class/) on transcontinental routes.


The math is clear. Economy awards to Europe cost 20,000 to 30,000 points. Business class costs 40,000 to 60,000. You pay roughly double the points for a seat worth 5 to 10 times more in cash. The cents-per-point value is almost always higher in business class, making it the single best use of transferable credit card points.

Flies you on Iberia between U.S. gateways and Madrid, with connections across Europe. Avios transfer 1:1 between British Airways, Qatar, Iberia, Aer Lingus, and Finnair. ## When to Book Business Class with Points


Business class awards have two optimal booking windows. Timing your search around these windows improves your chances significantly.


**Early (330+ days out).** Airlines release initial award inventory when schedules open, typically 330 to 360 days before departure. Fixed-chart programs like Aeroplan and Flying Blue offer predictable pricing at this point. This is your best bet for peak travel dates (holidays, summer) when business class fills up fast.


**Last-minute (3-14 days out).** Airlines release unsold premium seats as award availability close to departure. Lufthansa, Emirates, ANA, American, and British Airways all show this pattern. If you are flexible on dates, last-minute business class awards can offer some of the best deals of the year. See our guide on[how to find last-minute flights with points](https://www.pointhound.com/blog/how-to-find-cheap-last-minute-flights) for the full strategy.


**Avoid the middle.** The worst window is roughly 1 to 3 months before departure. Initial award inventory is gone, and airlines have not yet started releasing distressed inventory. If you are searching in this window, check LifeMiles and Flying Blue, which tend to maintain availability longer than other programs.


Two more timing strategies that help:


- **Transfer bonuses.** Credit card issuers periodically offer 20-30% bonuses when transferring points to specific airline partners. A 30% bonus on 60,000 points means you only need to transfer 46,000. These promotions can turn an expensive award into a great deal.
- **Off-peak pricing.** Programs that use peak/off-peak pricing (like Iberia Avios) charge significantly less outside summer and holidays.[January and April are the best value months](https://arangrant.com/en-us/blog/average-business-class-ticket-price/) for both cash fares and award availability.


## What to Expect in Business Class


If you have never flown business class on a long-haul flight, here is what you are paying points for.


On international flights of 6+ hours, business class on most major airlines means a lie-flat seat that converts into a bed. You get a meal service with multiple courses, premium drinks, and often a mattress pad and pajamas for overnight flights. Airport lounge access is included, giving you a quiet place to work or relax before boarding. Priority boarding and baggage mean you skip the lines.


The experience varies by airline. A Qatar Airways Qsuite gives you a fully enclosed suite with a sliding door, a lie-flat bed, and restaurant-style dining. An older American Airlines Boeing 777 business class seat might be a dated recliner. Research the specific airline and aircraft type before you book. The product on a $3,500 Qatar ticket is a fundamentally different experience from a $2,000 domestic first class seat.


For routes under 4 hours, business class is usually a wider seat with more legroom, but not a lie-flat. Keep this in mind when evaluating the points cost for shorter flights.


## Frequently Asked Questions


### How many points do I need for a business class flight?


It depends on the route and program. Transatlantic business class starts at[40,500 Avios one-way through Iberia](https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/airline-award-chart-sweet-spots/) (off-peak). U.S. to Asia starts around 55,000 to 80,000 miles one-way. Domestic U.S. coast-to-coast can be as low as 25,000 Aeroplan points on United Polaris.


### Is it worth using points for business class instead of economy?


Almost always. Economy awards to Europe cost 20,000 to 30,000 points. Business class costs 40,000 to 60,000. You pay roughly double the points for a seat worth 5 to 10 times more in cash. The cents-per-point value is higher in business class, making it the best use of transferable points for most travelers.


### Do I need elite status to book business class with points?


No. Anyone can book business class award flights through any loyalty program they are a member of. Elite status can help with upgrades from economy and waitlist priority, but the award seats themselves are available to all program members.


### Which credit card points are best for business class awards?


Chase Ultimate Rewards and Amex Membership Rewards have the broadest airline transfer partner networks, covering most of the top programs for business class (Aeroplan, Flying Blue, Iberia Avios, and more). Capital One Miles and Bilt Rewards also transfer to strong business class programs including Aeroplan and Avianca LifeMiles.
