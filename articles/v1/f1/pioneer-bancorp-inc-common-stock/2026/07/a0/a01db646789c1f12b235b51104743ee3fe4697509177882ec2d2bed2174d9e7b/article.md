# WNYT: Pioneer ranks No. 79 on Fortune 2025 Fastest-Growing Companies list


*1.6.26*


**


"Pioneer has been named one of Fortune’s 2025 100 Fastest-Growing Companies, ranking No. 79 nationally.


The company, based in New York’s Capital Region, earned its spot due to strong performance in revenue growth, earnings growth, and total shareholder return over three years. Pioneer is the only company from the Capital Region to make the list.


“Being recognized by Fortune reflects the success of a diversified strategy built right here in the Capital Region,” said Thomas Amell, President and CEO of Pioneer."
