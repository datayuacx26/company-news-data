# The Post Star: Pioneer’s 6th annual Mini Golf Tournament raises over $14K


*6.7.26*


**


"The Pioneer Charitable Foundation celebrated a milestone, surpassing $50,000 in total funds raised following its sixth annual Mini Golf Tournament, which brought in more than $14,000 at this year’s event.


The annual tournament was attended by nearly 100 people, including Capital Region business leaders, community partners, and Pioneer volunteers.


The event is designed as a corporate team-building experience with a nostalgic twist, participants are invited to 'be a kid for a day' with 18 holes of mini golf, ice cream, a BBQ lunch, and childhood favorite snacks, all while supporting Pioneer’s Charitable Foundation."
