# The Record: 18 students get summer internships with Pioneer


*6.28.2024*


"Pioneer has accepted 18 college students into its summer internship program. The students come from 13 colleges and universities nationwide, including two local to the Capital Region: University at Albany, Binghamton University, University at Buffalo, Ithaca College, St. Bonaventure University, Siena College, Bentley University, Clemson University, Endicott College, Fairfield University, Providence College, University of Richmond, and University of Tampa.


The competitive ten-week internship will place the students, who are a mix of undergraduates and MBAs, in active roles throughout the organization, including consumer lending, commercial credit and retail; wealth management, IT, financial crimes and compliance; and accounting, insurance, marketing, and business analytics. All will work full-time schedules, both at Pioneer’s headquarters and its branch locations."
