# Capital Region Chamber: Pioneer Celebrates ‘All Together Forward Days,’ Unveils Inaugural Community Impact Report

# Capital Region Chamber: Pioneer Celebrates ‘All Together Forward Days,’ Unveils Inaugural Community Impact Report


*5.22.24*


**


"Pioneer Bancorp, Inc. (NASDAQ: PBFS) (“Pioneer”), a leading financial institution in New York’s Capital Region, has released its first-ever Community Impact Report, which highlights the wide reach and significant impact of the Pioneer Charitable Foundation since its inception in 2018. The unveiling came amid the third annual celebration of “All Together Forward Days,” an intensive community service effort spearheaded by Pioneer employees on May 21 and 22.


According to the report, the Pioneer Charitable Foundation surpassed more than $1.6 million in giving over its first five years. Funding was provided to more than 400 local nonprofits, all of which supply housing, nutrition, or health care, and/or educational, social and recreational programs, to support the Foundation’s mission of “Helping Kids be Kids.” Importantly, Pioneer underwrites all operational costs, so 100% of money raised helps local children, families and organizations in need. "
