# NEWS 10 ABC: New Pioneer branch opens in Hudson


*7.13.26*


**


“Pioneer opening a new full service branch at Hudson City Centre today. The branch will have offices for consultations and feature a drive-thru Interactive Teller Machine (ITM) for extended hours banking. Dozens of people attending today among local leaders including the mayor, giving Pioneer a warm welcome.


‘We are beyond lucky to have Pioneer join this community. So, I just want to say welcome, thank you, and I can’t wait to keep working together’ said Hudson Mayor Joseph Ferris.


According to Pioneer they have committed $30 million in new small business lending in Columbia County.”
