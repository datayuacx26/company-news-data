# The Post Star: Pioneer reports 2025 net income of $20.3 million


*2.1.26*


**


"Pioneer Bancorp Inc., a Capital Region-based financial institution with a local branch on Upper Glen Street, on Friday reported results for the fourth quarter as well as 2025 as a whole.


According to the company, net income for the three-month period that ended Dec. 31, 2025, was $3.7 million, or $0.16 and $0.15 per basic and diluted share, respectively, as compared to $3.3 million, or $0.13 per basic and diluted share, for the same three-month period last year. Net income for the calendar year ended December 31, 2025, was $20.3 million, or $0.83 per basic and diluted share, as compared to $15.3 million, or $0.61 per basic and diluted share, for the fiscal year that ended June 30, 2024."
