# Progress Software to Report Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results on June 30, 2026

BURLINGTON, Mass., June 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Progress Software](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=sEMKJIV3Kg141G2wWOrSBEQwjlXCHMjiVBtMdoqUK_1BDuYRR0zEZIOy-MEFCKepKEysUumK-M_LTHEdEPy2Eu8a9_p219QpPTuxuOHx6PY=) (Nasdaq: PRGS), a small-cap growth and value Russell 2000 stock, today announced that it will release financial results for its fiscal second quarter of 2026 after the market close on Tuesday, June 30, 2026. Progress will host a conference call to review and discuss the results at 5:00 p.m. ET the same day. The company’s second quarter of fiscal year 2026 ended on May 31, 2026.
