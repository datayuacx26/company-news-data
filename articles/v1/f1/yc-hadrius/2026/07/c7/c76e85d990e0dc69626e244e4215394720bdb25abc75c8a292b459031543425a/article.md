# Announcing Hadrius’ Integration with the Claude Compliance API

The SEC has a simple view of what counts as a business record. If your people conduct business on a channel, the communications on that channel are records that you are required to keep.


WhatsApp. TikTok. Telegram. None of these apps were designed with business in mind, much less compliance. But Hadrius supports archiving on all these channels (and 60+ more) for a simple reason: you might be asked about them in an exam.


Today, one of the world’s most important technologies joins the party. We are proud to announce that **Hadrius supports comms archiving with Claude.**


Alongside our existing archiving of ChatGPT, this integration means Hadrius is now able to help you oversee your employees’ communications with two of the major LLMs in use today.


‍


‍ *See our support article on Hadrius’ integration with Claude’s Compliance API*[here](https://support.hadrius.com/articles/8197408781-claude-integration?lang=en) *.*

## How Hadrius connects to Claude’s Compliance API


Claude Enterprise exposes a read-only feed of your organization's activity through Claude's Compliance API. Hadrius connects to it and archives:


- **Every message:** The prompts your employees send and the responses Claude returns, as full conversation threads rather than loose fragments.
- **The metadata:** Who sent it, when, and which conversation it belongs to.


From there, **it lands in the same archive as every other communication.** Email, Slack, Teams, texts, and now Claude live in one place your team already searches and exports from.


> “Your team uses Claude to do their work. Hadrius quietly captures those conversations in the background so your compliance team can search and review them if ever needed.


Note that the Claude account must be Enterprise-tier, as the API we pull from is only available on those plans.


## Compliance built for an off-channel world


Hadrius’ integration with Claude’s Compliance API furthers a global commitment to support audit-ready archiving of every piece of technology your employees use to do work.


Our commitment is to make sure your archive keeps up, so that when your team moves to a new surface, the record is already there instead of being something you have to reconstruct under exam pressure.


Claude is the newest surface, and it won't be the last. If your firm is on Claude Enterprise, the connector is ready now. Talk to your Hadrius account manager about turning it on.
