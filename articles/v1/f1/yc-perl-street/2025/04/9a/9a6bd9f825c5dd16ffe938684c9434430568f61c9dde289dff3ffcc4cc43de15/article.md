# How To Turn Solar Assets into Liquid, High-Return Investments

## Introduction


Distributed Energy Resource (DER) projects are hitting their stride. The last two years have seen the rapid expansion of such projects as more and more companies seek out decentralized renewable solutions. Capacity is expected to[double by 2027](https://www.woodmac.com/) , and by 2030 DERs may provide up to 20% of grid capacity. Solar is leading the DER market and is serving as the foundation for DER growth. And while greenfield projects dominate headlines for growth, the potential of installed solar rooftop assets remains largely untapped. Many of these solar assets are underbanked, illiquid, or overlooked, yet they hold immense value for real estate owners seeking to optimize their financial returns.


‍


## Market Insights


The rapid expansion of rooftop solar presents a major opportunity for real estate asset owners and investors looking to maximize financial returns:


- **Australia** : Rooftop solar photovoltaic (PV) installations are estimated to surpass[25 GW of installed capacity](https://cleanenergycouncil.org.au/getmedia/290496f3-a240-4a12-bc63-7b374f8c71c4/rooftop-solar-and-storage-report_jan-june-2024.pdf) , driven by corporate sustainability goals and rising electricity costs.
- **USA** : The U.S. solar market has exceeded[60 GW of installed capacity ,](https://seia.org/research-resources/solar-industry-research-data/) with commercial real estate owners increasingly integrating solar into their energy strategies


Notably, the majority of these solar assets are owned by real estate companies,[not third-party financiers](https://emp.lbl.gov/sites/default/files/5_tracking_the_sun_2023_report.pdf) . This means real estate CFOs and asset managers must navigate the complexities of optimizing financial returns, managing operational risks, and ensuring long-term value from their energy investments.


By unlocking the financial potential of these existing assets, Perl Street enables real estate owners to monetize their solar investments, standardize performance validation, and position their assets as attractive investments for buyers and aggregators.


‍


## The Problem


Most installed solar assets are not achieving their full financial potential. Their performance is often unchecked or unverified against their initial projections, leaving asset owners and investors without a clear understanding of whether their systems are meeting expected returns. For real estate owners, these financial inefficiencies stem from limited visibility into system performance and an inability to optimize their assets for maximum profitability. Without the right tools to benchmark financial returns or access strategies for enhancing asset value, many owners leave money on the table.


Buyers and aggregators face significant challenges when evaluating these assets for acquisition, including discrepancies between engineering drawings and actual installations, incomplete system performance data, and a lack of standardized due diligence processes. As a result, many aggregators hesitate to acquire these systems despite their potential for strong returns when properly managed.


Perl Street ensures that asset owners and investors can benchmark their portfolios, verify system performance, and make data-driven decisions to unlock the full financial potential of their assets.
