# Webinar: Scaling DER Like Tech Companies

### The presentation sheds light on the challenges faced by distributed energy (DE) companies in scaling their operations.


These challenges include managing diverse data sources, automating tasks, and meeting increasing reporting requirements.


Perl Street offers a software solution designed to address these challenges. The platform provides a centralized hub for managing DE assets, finances, and reporting. By automating tasks like investor reporting, cost allocation, and invoice generation, Perl Street helps DE companies streamline operations, reduce manual work, and improve financial control.


The presentation also emphasizes the importance of a well-designed IT architecture for DE companies. It discusses the need for a robust data architecture to handle the complexities of managing data from various sources and to support the growing needs of the business.
