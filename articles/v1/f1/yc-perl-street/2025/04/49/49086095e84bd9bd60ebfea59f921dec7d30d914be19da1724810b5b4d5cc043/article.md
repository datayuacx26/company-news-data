## Building The Financial Pipes


" *The financial pipes, as they currently exist, are narrow, twisted, and clogged. We need wider, straighter, cleaner financial pipes to facilitate the flow of capital to reach our collective climate goals* ." - Richard Kauffman (NYSERDA), Fixing the Plumbing: Asset Management, Clean Energy Technology, and the Valley of Death ([Settling Climate Accounts](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-030-83650-4) ).


We have been building these straight financial pipes. On the one hand, we have been enabling our customers to develop and finance bankable projects. On the other hand, we have financing customers who leverage our financial management software.
