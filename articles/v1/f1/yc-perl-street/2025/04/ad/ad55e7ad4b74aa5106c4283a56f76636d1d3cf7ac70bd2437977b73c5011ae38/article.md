# Introducing Perl Street’s Project Development Platform

Managing the development of renewable energy projects is no small task. It’s a major juggling act, from overseeing teams to handling mountains of paperwork to maintaining tight deadlines. It’s complex, demanding work.[Perl Street](https://www.perlstreet.com/) ’s new app is designed to streamline these processes and make life easier for developers, engineers, and project managers.


Instead of bouncing between a dozen platforms, Perl Street’s App consolidates tasks and documents in one centralized hub. This integrated approach makes it easier to handle tasks like feasibility studies, legal agreements, and timelines with ease.


‍


## **Why Streamlined Project Management Matters**


Renewable energy projects are logistical nightmares. Juggling multiple, sometimes complicated tasks like site analysis, team coordination, and legal documentation can lead to inefficiency and delays. Perl Street’s app addresses these pain points by offering a centralized solution, integrating every step of the process into a single, easy-to-use platform.


‍


## **Key Features and Workflow**


The Perl Street App is designed to provide an industry-standard framework for developing renewable energy projects. It covers all of the main steps, from checking if a site is feasible to making sure the plant runs smoothly once it’s operational. Here’s a look at how it works at each stage:


### **1. Preliminary Feasibility and Site Selection**


Before breaking ground on a project, you need to know if the site will work - but managing feasibility studies and site selection can be tedious. Perl Street’s app simplifies this. With the app, teams can conduct power connection and zoning studies, identify potential sites, and align them with project needs. Communication with landowners is streamlined and critical documents are accessible in-app. Tasks and documents the app can help with in this stage include:


- Run power connection and zoning checks easily.
- Keep track of potential sites and whether they meet your project needs.
- Organize your reports, documents, and conversations in one spot.
- Manage all communications with landowners without digging through old emails.


### **2. Land Tenure Negotiation and Agreement**


Once you find the right site, it’s time to secure it. This stage of a renewable energy project typically involves a lot of back and forth with landowners and lawyers. With the Perl Street App, negotiating and securing these land agreements is also simplified. Option Agreement negotiations can take place directly within the platform, and teams can easily access documents like land.


- Negotiate land agreements right in the app.
- Track title searches and due diligence with built-in tools.
- Store contracts and legal documents where you can easily find them.
- Keep all communication clear and easy to access.


### **3. Legal and Financial Framework Setup**


The next big step in a renewable energy project is making sure all the legal and financial pieces are securely in place. This means finalizing agreements, running[financial models](https://www.perlstreet.com/platform) , and securing funding to keep the project going.


Instead of chasing down all the details from different sources, the Perl Street App lets you handle it all in one place. Everything from legal documents to financial models is easy to locate when and where you need it.


- Build financial models to track project costs and returns.
- Store legal agreements and financial documents in one place.
- Keep track of funding proposals and investor commitments easily.
- Pull up key documents when you need them for quick decision-making.


### **4. Permitting and Approvals**


If you’ve worked on a renewable energy project before, you’re no doubt familiar with permitting. And more than aware that this is when things can really slow down. From submitting Development Approval (DA) applications to doing environmental impact studies, there’s a lot to manage.


And it’s easy to lose track.


With the Perl Street App, you can organize all your permits and approvals in one place, ensuring nothing falls through the cracks. You can easily track the progress of applications and stay on top of compliance every step of the way.


- Submit and track DA applications.
- Manage environmental studies and other assessments in-app.
- Store permits, approvals, and compliance docs where you can find them.
- Keep track of grid connection applications and approvals.


### **5. Design and Engineering**


With the bulk of the paperwork out of the way, it’s time to focus on the actual design of the renewable energy project. This includes putting together a site layout, picking suppliers, and making sure everything is compliant with grid requirements.


Once again, the Perl Street App simplifies this process by keeping your designs, technical drawings, and equipment contracts organized and easy to access.


- Track design layouts and technical documents in the app.
- Store equipment supplier contracts and specifications.
- Keep track of grid connection requirements and make sure you’re compliant.
- Easily access all technical drawings and site plans.


### **6. Contracting and Procurement**


In the thick of development, you’ve got a lot to juggle - contracts with suppliers, contractors, operations teams, etc. It’s a lot to manage and unfortunately, things sometimes fall through the cracks.


The Perl Street App helps you keep track of contractor agreements, procurement schedules, and supplier contracts.


- Track procurement timelines and supplier contracts.
- Manage EPC contractor agreements.
- Store all your procurement documents in one place.
- Keep O&M contracts organized and easy to access.


### **7. Development Completion and Financial Close**


Before the project can really kick off, you need to tie up all the loose ends. That means finalizing agreements, securing funding, and issuing a Notice to Proceed (NTP).


The Perl Street App helps you keep everything on track during this phase, so you’re ready to move forward without any last-minute surprises.


- Issue NTPs and track Financial Investment Decisions (FID).
- Keep track of all final approvals and funding close documents.
- Make sure lease or purchase agreements are squared away.
- Be fully prepared before construction begins.


### **8. Construction and Commissioning**


During construction, you need to stay on top of progress and make sure everything is in compliance. This is also when you’ll manage commissioning and get ready for grid connection.


The Perl Street App helps you track construction schedules, manage commissioning, and oversee the energization process as the project nears completion.


- Track construction progress and key milestones.
- Manage commissioning steps and compliance.
- Oversee grid connection and energization.
- Keep all construction documents and reports organized.


### **9. Operation and Maintenance**


When the project is complete and the plant is up and running, the Perl Street App shifts to help with daily operations. You can track performance, schedule routine maintenance, and report to stakeholders.


- Monitor plant performance in real-time.
- Keep track of maintenance schedules and compliance.
- Store O&M manuals and compliance records.
- Generate performance reports for stakeholders.
