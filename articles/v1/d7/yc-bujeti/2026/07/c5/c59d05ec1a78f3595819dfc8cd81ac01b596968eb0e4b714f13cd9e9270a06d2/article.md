# Introducing Bujeti Quotes: from quotation to payment, all in one workflow - Bujeti — All-in-One Financial Management Control for Business Growth | Expense & Budget Management

Every payment starts with an agreement. Before a customer pays, they want to know exactly what they are paying for, how much it costs, and the terms of the transaction. That is why quotations are one of the most important documents any business creates.


Yet for most businesses, quoting is still manual. A salesperson prepares a quote in Word or Excel, exports it as a PDF, emails it to the customer, revises it whenever something changes, and once the customer approves, finance recreates the same information from scratch just to raise an invoice. It is a process full of duplicated data, avoidable errors, and delays that slow down how quickly you get paid.


And here is the harder question: how many quotes did your business send last month, how many converted, and do you know your acceptance rate? For most teams that number lives in someone's head, or nowhere at all. When quotes sit in inboxes and follow-ups happen over WhatsApp, you lose visibility into the exact stage of your revenue funnel where deals are won or lost.


Today we are making that process far simpler. Meet Bujeti Quotes: a faster way to create professional quotations, route them for internal approval, track customer decisions, convert accepted quotes into invoices, and move seamlessly from proposal to payment.


###### Why Bujeti Quotes is different


Bujeti Quotes is built differently, because quoting was never meant to be a standalone task. It is the first step in getting paid. So instead of treating it as an isolated document, we connected it to the entire financial workflow that follows. Here is what sets it apart:


1.


**It lives where your money already moves:** Standalone quoting tools force you to bolt on yet another app, then wire it into your invoicing, payments, and accounting. Bujeti Quotes sits inside the same platform that already runs your invoices, approvals, expenses, and payments. No need to integrate another tool or sync.


2.


**Approved quotes become invoices, not more data entry:** With most tools, an approved quote is just a signal to start over: finance re-keys the same items, prices, and terms into a fresh invoice. In Bujeti, one approved quote converts directly into an invoice with the same information carried through. This simply removes the indeed for retyping, version mismatches and pricing errors introduced at handoff.


3.


**One source of truth, not four disconnected steps:** Quote in one tool, approvals in email, invoice in another system, payment somewhere else. That fragmentation is where errors and delays live. Bujeti keeps quote, approval, invoice, and payment in a single connected record, so every team sees the same accurate information at every stage.


4.


**Approval happens before the quote leaves the building:** For businesses that protect margin, a quote should not reach a customer until finance has signed off. Bujeti routes quotes through an internal approval workflow: the creator submits, an approver reviews in-platform, and only approved quotes go out.


5.


**Tracking is built in:** You do not dig through email threads to find out whether a quote was accepted. Acceptance, rejection, and pending status are all visible inside Bujeti, tied directly to the customer and the deal, and shareable however your customers actually work: by email, a copy-paste link, WhatsApp, or a downloadable PDF.


6.


**You finally see your pipeline:** How many quotes are outstanding, what they are worth, and how many convert: these move from guesswork to a real-time view. You can filter by status, track the total value of sent-but-not-accepted quotes, and export for board reporting.


The difference comes down to this: other tools help you make a quote. Bujeti Quotes helps you turn that quote into revenue, with fewer steps, fewer errors, and full visibility from first proposal to final payment.

###### The hidden cost of disconnected quoting


Most businesses never notice quoting inefficiencies, because they happen in small increments. Five extra minutes here. A forgotten email there. One wrong price. One outdated PDF. One customer waiting on a revised version. Individually they seem trivial. Collectively they carry a real cost:


-


**Slower sales cycles:** When revisions require manual work, customers wait longer to decide, and slower cycles mean slower revenue.


-


**Duplicate work:** Finance teams recreate the same information across different systems, consuming time without adding value.


-


**Higher risk of errors:** Manual data entry invites wrong pricing, missing items, inconsistent tax, and outdated customer details, all of which erode customer confidence.


###### What this looks like in practice


Take a management consulting firm sending 40 quotes a month to corporate clients. Today, each one is a Word document emailed as a PDF, and every time a client accepts, the account manager re-types the whole thing into a separate invoicing system. That is roughly two hours of duplicated work per accepted quote, and every re-entry is a chance to fumble a figure.


With Bujeti Quotes, the quote is created once and shared by link or WhatsApp. The client accepts or declines in the portal. One click turns the accepted quote into an invoice, with no re-entry. The two hours disappear, the errors disappear, and the account manager spends that time on the next deal instead of retyping the last one. A contractor who needs internal margin sign-off before quotes go out gets the same benefit on the front end: approval happens in-platform, not in an email thread.
