# Storylane vs Navattic: what are the differences?

Storylane and Navattic are two interactive demo platforms that do the exact same thing on paper. Both **capture your product** , turn it into a clickable walkthrough, and share it via a link or embed it on your website. Naturally, it is easy to hesitate between the two.


The difference lies in the details. It comes down to which team each platform targets, how they capture the product, and increasingly, what they do with AI. In this article, **we are going to compare both tools** in depth to see what type of team fits best with each one.


## What is Storylane?


Storylane is a **no code platform for creating interactive demos** that prospects can explore on their own. Its goal is to transform complex features into clear and easy to follow walkthroughs designed to communicate the product's value without the need for a human presenter.


It is primarily geared toward **marketing and sales teams** that need to scale demo production and adapt them to different audience profiles. Its product is divided into several branches. Guided demos are walkthroughs with hotspots and voiceovers, while sandbox demos are more interactive HTML/CSS environments. The Buyer Hub is a deal room space for centralizing demos and documents, and RepX is a conversational agent that engages and qualifies website visitors.


### Key features of Storylane


-


**No code visual editor:** This feature allows you to build walkthroughs using captures and HTML. You can also edit text, images, and hotspots directly from the platform without technical knowledge.


-


**Lily, its AI assistant:** This assistant helps generate and improve demo scripts, create automatic voiceovers, and translate content into more than 25 languages. It works as a creation aid rather than an agent navigating the live product.


-


**RepX, qualification agent:** This is one of their newest features. It acts as a conversational agent that chats with visitors, answers questions, and qualifies them. It can also open a Storylane demo to guide them through it during the conversation. However, it navigates that demo capture rather than the real interface in real time.


-


**Buyer Hub:** This gathers demos, videos, PDFs, and forms in one place to move deals through the middle of the funnel and provide context to multiple stakeholders.


-


**Flexible distribution:** Demos can be shared via a link or embedded on websites, in emails, and in marketing campaigns.


-


**Behavioral analytics:** It provides replays and engagement metrics like clicks, watch time, and paths taken to understand how each prospect navigates.


-


**GTM Integrations:** The platform connects with CRMs and marketing tools like Salesforce, HubSpot, Marketo, Pardot, and Google Analytics.


-


**Security and enterprise:** It is SOC 2 compliant and includes SSO, custom domains, and whitelabeling options for corporate environments.


## What is Navattic?


Navattic is a **no code platform that captures the product's HTML/CSS** interface very accurately, maintaining hover states, animations, and dynamic elements. It turns this into a clickable experience that prospects navigate in their browser. Its approach is closely tied to generating buyer intent. Every interaction is recorded as a signal that marketing, sales, and product teams can leverage.


It is specifically aimed at **sales and marketing teams** looking to share demos at different stages of the buyer journey and gain visibility into how prospects interact. Its Launchpad module adds demo automation designed for sales teams to share them earlier in the cycle and free up presales time.


### Key features of Navattic


-


**High fidelity HTML/CSS capture:** It replicates the application's actual frontend to provide highly realistic and editable demos. You can change text, blur sensitive data, and remove elements.


-


**AI Copilot:** This feature generates a first draft of the demo simply by navigating the product. It writes the copy, anchors tooltips, and suggests a structure based on best practices.


-


**Agent Demos:** This is a recent addition where an AI agent leads an interactive demo session and answers questions. It is worth noting that it works on the captured HTML demo rather than the live product. Since it is in an early stage, it performs best in structured and predictable flows.


-


**Dynamic personalization:** It uses variables to adapt the demo to each visitor and allows you to route users to specific paths based on their profile.


-


**AI voiceovers, avatars, and translation:** This adds recorded or generated narrations and translates step text into multiple languages.


-


**Real time analytics and alerts:** It tracks clicks, time spent, and dropoff points. It also sends Slack or email alerts when a prospect interacts, making the handoff to sales much easier.


-


**A/B Testing:** This allows you to test different versions of the same demo and compare which one converts better, which is useful for optimizing embedded website demos.


-


**Security and compliance:** It complies with standards like SOC 2 and GDPR while including access controls designed for enterprise environments.


## Storylane vs Navattic: which one should you choose?


Both platforms solve the same underlying problem but with different priorities. The choice ultimately depends on which team leads the demos and what you want to achieve with them.


### Choose Storylane if:


-


Your demo production is led by marketing and you **prioritize speed and ease of use** to get walkthroughs up and running quickly.


-


You are looking for a tool with a **smooth learning curve** and an affordable entry cost.


-


You want to **leverage AI** to quickly generate copy, voiceovers, and translations into multiple languages.


-


You want a deal room space like the Buyer Hub to centralize content and work on deals in the middle of the funnel.


### Choose Navattic if:


-


Your demos **are driven by sales and you need automation** like Launchpad to share them earlier in the cycle and save presales time.


-


You value **a highly accurate HTML/CSS product capture** and an editor with fine control over every element.


-


The **buyer intent analytics and real time alerts** are key to prioritizing outreach and improving handoffs.


-


You want to **experiment with their newest AI features** , such as AI Copilot for building or Agent Demos for automated sessions.
