# The 6 best 1mind alternatives in 2026

Imagine a buyer landing on your website at eleven at night. They do not want to fill out a form or wait two days for a sales representative to call them. They want to **see the product, understand how it works, and decide if it fits** their needs. If all they find is a features page with static screenshots, they will leave.


Artificial intelligence demos exist to bridge that gap. They allow that buyer to interact with the product at their own pace and understand its value without friction or intermediaries.


1mind is one of the platforms operating in this space. It uses "Superhumans": AI agents with a face, a voice, and product knowledge that operate on the web, inside the application, and even on video calls. It is an ambitious proposal, but **it does not fit every situation** or every budget.


If you are evaluating options or want to compare before deciding, these are **the 6 best 1mind alternatives** in 2026.


## Why are users looking for an 1mind alternative?


Although 1mind proposes an advanced approach to sales cycle automation with AI, **many companies look for alternatives** due to a combination of factors. These are the most common reasons:


-


**High and opaque pricing:** 1mind does not publish its prices and does not offer self service plans or a free trial, which puts it out of reach for many companies, especially those still validating the impact of AI demos on their pipeline.


-


**Long and complex implementation:** A typical deployment requires one to two months. There is no self service access to evaluate the platform before making a commitment.


-


**Dependence on the 1mind team:** For tasks like updating the knowledge base or adjusting the agent's content, users rely on the 1mind team instead of being able to manage it autonomously.


-


**Limited reach to owned channels:** 1mind works within the corporate website, the product, and video calls, but it does not cover outbound channels like email, LinkedIn, or SMS, nor does it offer formats like interactive product tours or embeddable demos for landing pages.


-


**Integrations concentrated in one ecosystem:** Following their alliance with Clari and Salesloft, native integrations are focused on that specific stack. Teams working with other CRMs or sales tools may run into limitations.


-


**Change in operating model:** Deploying 1mind is not just installing a chatbot. It requires alignment between marketing, sales, presales engineering, and customer success, which involves a considerable organizational effort.


-


**Avatar perception:** The use of photorealistic avatars with voices can generate pushback from some buyers, especially in B2B contexts where a real human interaction is expected.

### 1. Karumi


[Karumi](https://www.karumi.ai/) has introduced its own category within the demo space:[agentic demos](https://www.karumi.ai/blog/agentic-demos) , an idea that is simple but powerful. An autonomous AI agent takes control of the real product and presents it to the prospect **just like an experienced presales engineer would** . It is not a prerecorded video or a walkthrough using screenshots. It is a live conversation where the agent navigates the interface, answers questions, and modifies the demonstration on the fly based on what the user needs to see.


The fundamental difference compared to 1mind is that **Karumi offers a personalized experience** where the real software interface is shared and displayed based on the prospect's requests. The agent navigates the product live, opens sections, shows specific features, and adapts the walkthrough in real time. In 1mind, the Superhuman operates through video and conversation, but it is unable to show or navigate the product interface in real time.


#### Key highlights


-


**Demonstration on the real product:** The agent operates directly on the software interface, presenting features and answering questions without human intervention.


-


**24/7 availability:** Any prospect can start a demo whenever they want, eliminating bottlenecks in the sales team.


-


**Contextual personalization:** Each session adapts to the user's industry, role, and interests, offering a unique walkthrough instead of a fixed sequence.


-


**Communication in multiple languages:** The agent converses natively in the prospect's language without the need to create separate versions.


-


**Automatic CRM synchronization:** Data from each interaction flows directly into Salesforce, HubSpot, or other tools without intermediate steps.


-


**Purchase intent detection:** It analyzes user behavior during the demo to identify leads with the highest potential.


-


**Automatic reflection of updates:** When the product changes, the demo reflects it without anyone having to intervene manually.


-


**User and permission governance:** It controls who can access, edit, or manage each demo within the organization.


-


**Complete activity log:** Every action is documented to facilitate audits, tracking, and traceability.


-


**Security certifications:** It operates under SOC 2, GDPR, and ISO 27001 standards.

### 2. Storylane


Storylane allows go to market teams to build **interactive product demos** without writing a single line of code. Its visual editor captures the software interface and turns it into clickable walkthroughs that prospects navigate on their own, choosing which features to explore and at what pace.


In 2026, Storylane took a step into the agentic field with Lily, a conversational AI agent that integrates into demos to answer prospect questions, suggest the most relevant demo based on their use case, and qualify leads during the experience. Lily is currently in beta and is only available on the Premium plan.


#### Key highlights


-


**Visual creation without code:** It captures the product interface using a browser extension and generates interactive walkthroughs in minutes.


-


**Lily AI Sales Agent:** A conversational agent that answers questions inside the demo, suggests paths based on the buyer's context, and can qualify leads and book meetings.


-


**Direct content editing:** Text, images, graphics, and data can be modified right inside the demo without needing to rerecord.


-


**Demos adapted by profile:** It allows showing different flows depending on the user type, their industry, or their use case.


-


**AI voiceovers and avatars:** It generates automatic narrations with AI voices and virtual avatars to enrich the experience, although these are paid add ons.


-


**Engagement analytics:** It shows clicks, view time, replays, and drop off points to understand how prospects interact.


-


**Connection with the sales stack:** It integrates with HubSpot, Salesforce, Marketo, Pardot, and Google Analytics to push interaction data directly to the CRM.


-


**Enterprise security:** It complies with SOC 2 and GDPR while offering SSO, custom domains, and access controls for large teams.


### 3. Saleo


Saleo is an AI native demo platform that **combines three modalities into one product** : live demos enhanced with personalized data, interactive product tours, and an autonomous AI agent capable of conducting 24/7 demonstrations.


Saleo's approach is particularly **geared towards presales and solutions engineering teams** who need total control over the demonstration environment. Its ability to inject realistic data directly into the native product during live presentations is one of its most differentiating features.


#### Key highlights


-


**Enhanced live demo:** It allows injecting personalized data into the real product during live presentations, eliminating the reliance on separate demo environments.


-


**AI Demo Agent:** A conversational agent that handles discovery, qualifies interest, and presents the product autonomously and in multiple languages.


-


**AI data generation:** A specific agent automatically creates realistic and contextual demo data based on the prospect's industry.


-


**Interactive product tours:** It allows creating self guided walkthroughs that prospects can explore independently.


-


**Demo analytics:** It provides metrics on which features and sequences generate the most interest in each presentation.


-


**Integrations with sales tools:** It connects with CRMs and marketing platforms to synchronize information.


-


**Security and compliance:** It complies with standards like SOC 2 Type II to guarantee data protection in enterprise environments.


### 4. Navattic


Navattic stands out from other demo tools because of its HTML capture system, which **clones the product's actual front end** instead of taking screenshots. The result is a set of demos that preserve hover states, dropdown menus, animations, and scrolling, offering an experience that is visually indistinguishable from the real application.


In 2026, Navattic launched Agent Demos, **a conversational AI layer** that operates on cloned product environments. These agents guide the prospect autonomously, answer questions, navigate the interface, and generate transcripts of every session for the sales team. This is a recent feature and is still in early stages of adoption.


#### Key highlights


-


**Product HTML cloning:** It captures the real front end code, producing demos with the highest level of interactivity in the category.


-


**Agent Demos:** Autonomous agents that operate within the cloned environment, present features, answer questions in any language, and route qualified leads to the sales team.


-


**AI Copilot for creation:** An assistant that writes copy for the demo, anchors tooltips, and structures the walkthrough while the user captures the interface.


-


**Editing without rerecording:** Once the interface is captured, texts, images, and visual elements can be modified without having to repeat the process.


-


**Account level intent analytics:** It records engagement, syncs it with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Marketo, and triggers Slack alerts when it detects high intent views.


-


**AI voiceovers and translation:** It adds voice narration and multilingual support to demos, although full localization requires additional setup.


-


**Team editing:** Multiple members can work simultaneously on creating and adjusting demos.


-


**Regulatory compliance:** It operates under GDPR and offers enterprise access controls.


### 5. Supademo


Supademo is a tool that **allows you to create interactive demos quickly** . Its browser extension records product flows and transforms them into step by step clickable walkthroughs, with AI generated hotspots and dynamic variables that let you personalize names, logos, and data without duplicating the demo.


In April 2026, Supademo launched Demo Agents, **AI agents that qualify buyers and show demo content** based on the prospect's context. Unlike other agentic solutions, Supademo's agents do not navigate the product live, but rather orchestrate and present existing prerecorded content.


#### Key highlights


-


**Automatic flow recording:** It captures processes directly from the product and generates interactive demos in minutes.


-


**Demo Agents:** AI agents that qualify prospects, answer questions, and show the appropriate demo content based on the buyer's profile and intent.


-


**Personalization at scale with dynamic variables:** It allows changing names, logos, and data in a single demo to adapt it to thousands of prospects without creating separate versions.


-


**Multiple output formats:** Demos can be exported as GIFs, MP4 videos, or embedded directly into websites, emails, and documentation.


-


**Conditional routing:** Prospects choose their own path within the demo, exploring only what interests them.


-


**Heatmaps and analytics:** It shows exactly where users interact, where they drop off, and how many times the demo is shared internally.


-


**CRM integrations:** It connects with HubSpot and Salesforce to centralize engagement data.


-


**Security and compliance:** It complies with SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and CCPA.


### 6. Consensus


Consensus is an **automated demo platform** that allows prospects to discover the product on their own, adapting the content based on their interests and profile. Instead of showing all the information, the tool prioritizes what is most relevant to each user, making the experience more direct and easier to understand.


It is specifically **geared towards sales and presales teams** managing complex buying cycles with multiple stakeholders. By offering personalized demos that are accessible at any time, it helps improve engagement and accelerate the decision making process. Consensus is starting to explore the incorporation of conversational AI agents into its platform, although for now its experience is still based on prerecorded content and dynamic flows without live interaction.


#### Key highlights


-


**Autonomous exploration:** Prospects can interact with the product at their own pace without relying on live demos or static content.


-


**Dynamic personalization:** The demo automatically adjusts the content based on the user's profile and responses.


-


**On demand video demos:** Buyers receive video demos personalized to their use cases and pain points.


-


**Interactive product tours:** Clickable demonstrations that illustrate key features and allow buyers to test the product.


-


**Automated simulations:** Simulated environments that buyers can use to dive deeper into the product's functionality.


-


**Interaction insights:** It allows identifying which parts of the journey generate the most interest, the time spent in each section, and the actions taken during the demo.


-


**Sales and marketing integrations:** It connects with sales and marketing tools to centralize information and improve tracking.


-


**Compliance and security:** It complies with standards like SOC 2 Type II and GDPR to ensure safe platform usage.
