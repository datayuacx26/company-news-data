# The 6 best Saleo alternatives in 2026

Most B2B SaaS buyers want to **try the product on their own before talking to a sales rep** . If your website only offers static screenshots or a contact form, they will likely go to the competition.


Interactive product demos allow users to **explore the software without friction** , quickly understand its value, and move forward in the purchasing process on their own. This leads to better conversion rates and shorter sales cycles.


Saleo is a well known platform for creating live demos with custom data, but it does not always fit the needs of every team. In this article we analyze **the 6 best Saleo alternatives in 2026** to help you find the option that best fits your strategy.


## Why are users looking for a Saleo alternative?


Saleo is a powerful tool for presales teams conducting live demos, but it has certain limitations that lead many companies to explore other options:


-


**High and opaque pricing:** Saleo does not publish its rates on its website. Their annual contracts require a significant initial investment that increases based on the number of users and contracted modules, without offering a free trial or an accessible starter plan.


-


**Slow and technical implementation:** Deploying the platform is complex and can take up to two months before you start seeing results.


-


**Reliance on the support team:** Several users point out that many tasks are not self service. Building tables, adjusting data, or modifying elements frequently requires reaching out to the Saleo team, which slows down demo creation and iteration.


-


**Errors during live presentations:** Glitches and unexpected behaviors during live demos are common, a critical problem when you are in front of a prospect.


-


**Steep learning curve:** The platform requires specific training and an internal manager to guide the team.


-


**Difficulty with complex demos:** When the demonstration includes multiple screens, data flows, or scenarios, building and maintaining them becomes cumbersome according to the users themselves.
