## What is Karumi?


[Karumi](https://www.karumi.ai/blog/agentic-demos) is a platform that has created an entirely new product category:[agentic demos](https://www.karumi.ai/blog/agentic-demos) . Instead of showing pre-recorded videos or relying on a sales rep's schedule, **an AI agent takes control of the demo** and guides the prospect through the product. It answers their questions and highlights the most relevant features based on their specific profile.


What makes this approach different is that **the agent does not work on a static copy of the software** , but rather on the actual interface. This allows it to navigate through product flows, explain every feature in context, and have a genuine conversation with the user. The prospect doesn't have to follow a rigid script. They can freely explore and ask whatever they need at any given time.


The result is **a demo experience that can scale endlessly** : it is available around the clock, tailored to each user, and requires no human intervention to run.


### Key features of Karumi


**Real-time agentic demos:** The AI agent navigates the product, showcases its capabilities, and handles any prospect objections live.


-


**24/7 availability:** Any prospect can take a demo whenever they need it, completely eliminating the reliance on schedules and wait times.


-


**User context adaptation:** The agent tweaks the experience based on each person's industry, role, and interests without any prior manual setup.


-


**Multilingual support:** The AI runs the demo in the prospect's native language, making it easier to expand into new markets without needing local resources.


-


**Automatic product updates:** Any changes made to the software automatically show up in the demo, meaning you don't have to rebuild flows or manually update content.


-


**Automated lead qualification:** The system tracks user behavior during the session and pulls out interest patterns that are highly useful for the sales team.


-


**CRM synchronization:** All activity generated during the demo feeds directly into tools like Salesforce or HubSpot with no extra steps in between.


-


**Advanced permission management:** You can define which users get to see specific parts of the demo by assigning custom permissions based on their role or their stage in the sales process.


-


**Full traceability:** Every single interaction is logged, allowing you to audit platform usage and maintain strict control over shared information.


-


**Enterprise-grade security:** Karumi is fully compliant with SOC 2, GDPR, and ISO 27001, meeting the market's most demanding security and privacy standards.


## What is Saleo?


Saleo is a demo platform designed for SaaS sales teams that need to create high-impact, personalized product experiences at scale. Unlike tools that focus on just a single format, Saleo combines **three product lines** : Saleo Live for live demos on the actual product, Saleo Capture for interactive product tours, and an AI Demo Agent for standalone AI demos.


Saleo is specifically geared toward **mid-market and enterprise companies** with established presales teams that need to scale their demos without losing control over the narrative or data quality.


### Key features of Saleo


-


**Live demos on the native product:** Sales reps can tweak any visible UI element (data, text, charts, logos) right on the actual product, without needing staging environments or engineering support.


-


**Data creation agent:** An AI assistant that generates realistic and contextualized demo data using natural language prompts.


-


**Interactive product tours:** A tool to create captured demos featuring step-by-step guided tours or free-exploration sandboxes.


-


**AI demo agent:** An autonomous agent that delivers personalized demos in real time.


-


**Integrated analytics:** It automatically captures completion metrics, time spent in the demo, drop-off points, buyer questions, and intent signals.


-


**Multichannel distribution:** Demos can be shared as public or protected links, embedded on a website, launched from CTAs, or used as post-meeting follow-up content.


-


**GTM stack integrations:** It connects with over 8,000 applications to sync data with the CRM and the rest of the sales team's toolset.


-


**Enterprise security:** It is SOC 2 and GDPR compliant. It includes role-based access control (RBAC), SSO support, and an architecture with no access to the production backend.

## Karumi vs Saleo: which one should you choose?


Choosing between Karumi and Saleo mainly depends on **the size of your team, the maturity of your demo processes, and the kind of experience** you want to deliver.


Below we review their main differences to help you decide which one best fits your situation.


### Choose Karumi if:


-


**Your priority is letting the prospect see the product without waiting:** You need any lead to be able to access a guided demo the exact moment they show interest, completely independent of your sales team's availability.


-


**You want each demo to adapt itself to the buyer:** You are looking for an experience that adjusts the content and the journey based on who is on the other side, without anyone needing to set it up beforehand.


-


**You need to extract sales intelligence from your demos:** You want the system to spot behavioral patterns and buying signals during the session, and you need that data sent straight to your CRM automatically.


-


**You sell in international markets:** You operate in multiple countries and need the demo to work natively in the prospect's language without duplicating content or assigning local resources.


-


**You don't want to maintain demos manually:** Every time your product evolves, the demo should reflect it without anyone having to jump in to update flows or screenshots.


-


**You are looking to qualify leads before meeting:** You prefer prospects to go through an autonomous demo first to filter their actual interest level, ensuring your sales team only spends time on the highest potential opportunities.


-


**You want to bet on a solution where AI is the engine, not just an add-on:** You are drawn to an approach where artificial intelligence doesn't just assist, but actually leads the entire demo experience from start to finish.


### Choose Saleo if:


-


**You have an established presales team:** You have Sales Engineers or Solution Consultants who need professional tools to prep and deliver top-tier demos for enterprise accounts.


-


**Demo data is your biggest bottleneck:** You spend countless hours prepping custom demo data for each prospect and need to scale that process without losing quality or consistency.


-


**You need an all-in-one demo platform:** You want to manage live demos, interactive tours, and autonomous AI demos from a single platform, rather than integrating a bunch of different tools.


-


**You operate in large enterprise accounts:** You have long sales cycles with multiple stakeholders, and you need to customize the demo narrative for each industry, use case, or buyer profile.


-


**Scale and control are top priorities:** You want your sales team to be able to launch customized demos in minutes, featuring an agent that always keeps the story on track and guides the buyer toward the next steps.
