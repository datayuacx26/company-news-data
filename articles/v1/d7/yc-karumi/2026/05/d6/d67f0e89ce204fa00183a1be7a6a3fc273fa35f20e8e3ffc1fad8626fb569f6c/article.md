# Karumi vs 1mind: what are the differences?

If you are looking for artificial intelligence tools to boost your sales process, it is highly likely that **Karumi** and **1mind** have appeared on your radar. Both platforms use AI to transform how sales teams interact with their prospects, but they do so from very different approaches.


Karumi bets on **agentic demos** , where an AI agent guides the prospect inside the actual product while answering questions and adapting the experience in real time. 1mind deploys what it calls "Superhumans". These are **photorealistic avatars** with emotional intelligence that act as autonomous sales representatives on the web, inside the product, and even in live video calls.


Which of the two fits your strategy better? It depends on the type of experience you want to offer and how your sales team is set up. In this article, **we analyze both platforms in detail** so you can make an informed decision.

## What is Karumi?


[Karumi](https://www.karumi.ai/) is a platform that has created a new product category known as[agentic demos](https://www.karumi.ai/blog/agentic-demos) . Instead of relying on prerecorded videos, static presentations, or the availability of a sales rep, **an artificial intelligence agent takes control of the demo** and accompanies the prospect directly inside the product.


What makes this approach different is that the agent does not work on a static copy of the software but on the real interface. This allows it to navigate the product flows, explain each feature in context, and maintain a genuine conversation with the user. The prospect does not have to follow a rigid script. They can explore freely and ask whatever they need at any given moment.


The result is a **demo experience that scales infinitely** . It is available 24 hours a day, adapts to each user, and requires no human intervention to run.


### Key features of Karumi


-


**Real time agentic demos:** The AI agent navigates the product, shows its capabilities, and resolves prospect objections live.


-


**24/7 availability:** Any prospect can access a guided demo at the exact moment they need it, eliminating the reliance on schedules and wait times.


-


**Adaptation to user context:** The agent adjusts the experience based on the industry, role, and interests of each person without the need for prior configuration.


-


**Multilingual support:** The AI runs the demo in the prospect's native language, facilitating expansion into new markets without the need for localized resources.


-


**Automatic product updates:** Any change in the software is automatically reflected in the demo without having to rebuild flows or manually update content.


-


**Automated lead qualification:** The system tracks user behavior during the session and detects interest patterns that are very useful for the sales team.


-


**CRM synchronization:** All activity generated during the demo is sent directly to tools like Salesforce or HubSpot without intermediate steps.


-


**Advanced permission management:** You can define which users see which parts of the demo, assigning custom permissions based on their role or stage in the sales process.


-


**Complete traceability:** Every interaction is recorded, allowing you to audit platform usage and maintain strict control over shared information.


-


**Enterprise grade security:** It complies with SOC 2, GDPR, and ISO 27001, meeting the most demanding security and privacy standards in the market.


## What is 1mind?


1mind is an artificial intelligence platform aimed at inbound sales that creates photorealistic digital avatars with voice capabilities, which it calls "Superhumans". These virtual representatives **work as autonomous sales reps** that qualify leads, handle objections, and schedule meetings without human intervention.


Unlike solutions that allow the prospect to interact directly with the product, 1mind's Superhumans rely on prerecorded videos and conversations guided by the avatar. This means that **the experience does not adapt in real time** to the specific profile or interests of each buyer, but rather follows a predefined flow with a focus geared more towards presentation than exploration.


Superhumans can act on the company website, inside the product itself, or even join video calls on Zoom, Teams, or Meet as another participant.


### Key features of 1mind


-


**Photorealistic Superhumans:** AI avatars with human appearance, their own name, and high quality voice that represent the company in interactions with prospects.


-


**Autonomous lead qualification:** Superhumans maintain natural conversations to qualify prospects, handle objections, and schedule meetings without human intervention.


-


**Ride Along in video calls:** An AI that joins Zoom, Teams, or Meet meetings as a visible participant, answering technical questions, doing live demos, and presenting slides in real time.


-


**Multiple engagement surfaces:** It operates simultaneously on the web, inside the product, and on video calls, covering the entire inbound buyer journey.


-


**Integrated emotional intelligence:** Superhumans are designed to handle objections empathetically and adapt the tone of the conversation according to the context.


-


**Integration with Salesloft and Clari:** Superhuman conversations natively synchronize with Salesloft Cadences and Clari forecasts for complete pipeline attribution.


-


**Custom implementation:** Each deployment includes persona definition workshops, content ingestion, and avatar production.


-


**Enterprise security:** ISO 27001 and ISO 42001 certified, with AI specific penetration testing and a confirmed policy of not using customer data to train models.

## Karumi vs 1mind: which one should you choose?


The choice between Karumi and 1mind depends fundamentally on the type of experience you want to offer your prospects, your budget size, and the maturity of your sales operation. Below we review their main differences to help you decide which one best fits your situation.


### Choose Karumi if:


-


**Your priority is for the prospect to see the product immediately:** You need any lead to be able to access a guided demo the exact moment they show interest without depending on your sales team's availability.


-


**You want the demo to adapt itself to each buyer:** You are looking for an experience that adjusts the content and journey based on the prospect's interests.


-


**You need to extract sales intelligence from your demos:** You want the system to detect behavior patterns and buying signals during the session and have that data flow directly into your CRM automatically.


-


**You sell in international markets:** You operate in several countries and need the demo to work natively in the prospect's language.


-


**You are looking to qualify leads before the meeting:** You prefer prospects to first go through an autonomous demo to filter their actual level of interest, ensuring your team only spends time on the highest potential opportunities.


-


**You want a solution where AI is the engine:** You are drawn to an approach where artificial intelligence not only assists but leads the entire demonstration experience inside the real product from start to finish.


### Choose 1mind if:


-


**You are looking for a virtual sales rep that goes beyond demos:** You do not just want to show the product, but you also want the AI to take charge of qualifying leads, resolving objections, and scheduling meetings autonomously.


-


**You want an AI to participate in your video calls:** You need a visible technical assistant to join Zoom, Teams, or Meet meetings alongside the account executive to answer complex questions live.


-


**You prioritize the conversational experience over the product experience:** You prefer an avatar with emotional intelligence to present and explain the product before the prospect touches it themselves.


-


**Your stack already includes Salesloft and Clari:** You need the AI conversations to integrate natively with your Salesloft cadences and Clari forecasts without intermediary tools.


-


**Your strategy is 100% inbound:** Your entire sales focus is on capturing and converting inbound demand, and you need an AI that covers everything from the first web interaction to the close.
