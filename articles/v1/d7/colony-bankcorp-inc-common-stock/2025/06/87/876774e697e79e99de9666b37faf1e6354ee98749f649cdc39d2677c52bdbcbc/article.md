# Dexter Lummus Named Atlanta Market President

Colony Bank is pleased to announce the promotion of Dexter Lummus to Atlanta Market President. A seasoned banking professional with more than four decades of experience, Lummus will oversee strategic growth initiatives and strengthen Colony’s commercial banking presence across the Metro Atlanta area.


Lummus most recently served as Managing Director of Commercial Real Estate at Colony Bank, where he focused on delivering tailored financing solutions to Atlanta’s real estate market. He joined Colony in 2021 through the acquisition of SouthCrest Bank and has been instrumental in growing the bank’s commercial presence in the region.


“Dexter’s experience in commercial banking and his knowledge of the Atlanta market make him an ideal choice for this role,” said Kyle Phelps, President of Emerging Markets at Colony Bank. “As we continue to expand our presence in Atlanta, his leadership and commitment to client relationships will help guide our efforts. He understands where the market is headed and how we can be a reliable partner to the businesses and communities we serve.”


Prior to joining Colony, Lummus held senior roles at IBERIABANK/Georgia Commerce Bank and other leading community and regional banks throughout Atlanta. A graduate of the University of Georgia, Lummus resides in the Metro Atlanta area with his wife and two children.
