# Lee Taylor Joins Colony Bank as Douglas Market President

Colony Bank is proud to announce the appointment of Lee Taylor as Market President for its Douglas market, reflecting the bank’s continued investment in the future of Coffee County and its focus on relationship-driven community banking.


In his new role, Taylor will focus on strengthening relationships throughout Douglas and Broxton while continuing to support local businesses, families, and community growth. With several years of commercial banking and strategic leadership experience, Taylor also brings extensive knowledge in agribusiness and small business development, giving him a strong understanding of the opportunities and needs within South Georgia communities.


“Lee is a respected leader who understands that community banking is built on relationships, trust, and showing up for the people and businesses you serve,” said Nat Higdon, President of Established Markets at Colony Bank. “He has deep roots in Douglas and Coffee County, a heart for serving others, and a passion for helping local businesses grow and families succeed. Lee’s leadership reflects exactly who we are as a bank and the type of financial partner we strive to be.”


As Market President, Taylor will work closely with customers, local businesses, and community leaders while helping further Colony Bank’s efforts to support local organizations, schools, businesses, and initiatives that create lasting impact across the region. Colony Bank has a long history of investing in the communities it serves through local leadership, financial education initiatives, nonprofit partnerships, volunteerism, and programs that strengthen our region.


“Douglas is home for me and my family, and I’m incredibly honored to step into this role,” said Taylor. “Colony Bank has built a strong reputation for putting the people and communities it serves first. I’m excited to continue building relationships, supporting local businesses and entrepreneurs, and serving as a trusted resource for families and businesses across our community.”


A longtime resident of Coffee County, Taylor is actively involved in the community and has served on several boards throughout Coffee County and surrounding areas. He and his wife, Courtney (Darwin) Taylor, are active members of First Methodist Church of Douglas and are proud parents to three children: Grayson, Whitley, and Kellan.ivating future banking talent in our local community.
