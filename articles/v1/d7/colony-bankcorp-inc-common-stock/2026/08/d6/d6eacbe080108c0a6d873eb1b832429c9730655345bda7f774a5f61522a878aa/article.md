# Colony Bank Launches Private Banking and Welcomes Colby Cardin as Private Banker

Colony Bank is expanding its financial services with the introduction of Private Banking, a new offering designed to provide personalized banking and financial solutions for individuals and families with more complex financial needs. As part of this expansion, the bank has welcomed Colby Cardin as Private Banker.


“Private Banking is another way we’re expanding the personalized service our customers have come to expect from Colony Bank,” said D Copeland, President of Colony Bank. “As our customers’ financial needs continue to evolve, they want a trusted partner who understands their goals and can connect them with the right financial solutions. We’re excited to welcome Colby to the team and look forward to growing this offering as we continue helping our customers achieve their financial goals.”


Private Banking offers personalized banking and lending solutions tailored to each client’s financial goals while connecting them with Colony Bank’s broader capabilities, including wealth management, insurance, specialized lending and more.


Cardin brings 24 years of experience in the financial services industry with a unique background in both relationship banking and leadership development. His career has included roles in commercial banking, business banking, credit and underwriting, as well as executive coaching and talent development. He earned a bachelor’s degree from the University of Georgia and is a graduate of the Stonier Graduate School of Banking at the University of Pennsylvania. Cardin is also an Associate Certified Coach (ACC) Coach through the International Coaching Federation.


A third generation banker and a lifelong resident of Columbus, Cardin has built his career serving the community he calls home. He currently serves on the board of Valley Healthcare System Inc. and on the faculty for the Georgia Bankers Association GBA Banking School. He previously served on the executive board of Scouts America. Cardin and his wife, Laura, who serves as the Chief Executive Officer of Pastoral Institute, have been married for 26 years and have two children.


“I’ve had the opportunity to work alongside Colby, and I’ve seen firsthand the way he builds lasting relationships and earns the trust of his clients,” said Mitch Watkins, Regional President. “He’s established a strong reputation throughout the Columbus community, and we’re excited to welcome him to Colony Bank as we introduce private banking to our customers.”


The addition of private banking reflects Colony Bank’s continued investment in meeting customers’ evolving financial needs. By expanding its suite of financial solutions, the bank is making it easier for customers to access personalized guidance and specialized expertise while continuing to work with a trusted local financial partner.
