# Financial Institutions and Leaders Recognized at Alkami Co:lab 2026 for Driving Innovation & Relationship Banking - Alkami Technology

*FIsionaries™ Awards and inaugural “For the MANTL” Awards honor leaders accelerating[digital banking](https://www.alkami.com/resources/client-success/case-study/southland-credit-union-uses-the-digital-banking-platform-to-reimagine-member-experience/) technology in the financial services industry*


**PLANO, TX – April 21, 2026 –** Alkami Technology, Inc. (Nasdaq: ALKT) (“Alkami”), a[digital sales and service platform provider](https://www.alkami.com/) for financial institutions in the U.S., today announced the winners of its 2026 customer awards programs, including the FIsionaries™ Awards and the inaugural “For the MANTL” Awards. Presented at[Alkami Co:lab 2026](https://www.alkami.com/event/alkami-colab-2026/) , these programs recognize financial institutions and leaders driving innovation and redefining relationship banking.


The FIsionaries Awards recognize organizations that are pushing the boundaries of digital banking through strategic innovation across digital transformation, data strategy and execution, culture transformation, and user experience. Sponsored by Alkami and hosted by Jim Marous, the[FIsionaries](https://www.alkami.com/digital-banking-podcast/) podcast series highlights regional and community financial institutions operating at the forefront of digital transformation.


The 2026 FIsionaries Award winners are:


- Transformational Culture:[Mountain America Credit Union](https://www.macu.com/)
- Data Strategy & Execution:[Capital Credit Union](https://www.capitalcu.com/)
- User Experience:[First Fidelity Bank](https://www.ffb.com/)
- Digital Transformation:[NASA Federal Credit Union](https://www.nasafcu.com/)


“Each of this year’s FIsionaries winners is pushing the banking industry forward in meaningful ways,” said Jim Marous, host of the FIsionaries podcast and owner of the Digital Banking Report. “They are redefining what’s possible by combining innovation with execution, ultimately delivering stronger experiences, deeper relationships, and greater value to their account holders.”


The “For the MANTL” Awards recognize[banks](https://www.alkami.com/banks/) ,[credit unions](https://www.alkami.com/credit-unions/) , and individual leaders achieving outstanding success with the[MANTL Origination & Onboarding Platform](https://www.alkami.com/mantl-onboarding-account-opening) while advancing relationship banking. These awards celebrate institutions and individuals helping local communities thrive through transformative technology and meaningful customer and member engagement.


The 2026 “For the MANTL” Award winners are:


- Omnichannel Excellence:[ConnectOne Bank](https://www.connectonebank.com/)
- Branch Modernization:[Dupaco Credit Union](https://www.dupaco.com/)
- The Architect Award:[The Atlantic Federal Credit Union](https://www.theatlanticfcu.com/)
- The Change Maker Award:[Financial Plus Credit Union](https://www.myfpcu.com/)
- Time-to-Value Award:[EagleBank](https://www.eaglebankcorp.com/)
- Partner of the Year:[Ascend Bank](https://ascend.bank/)
- Rookie of the Year:[Great Plains Bank](https://www.gpbankok.com/personal/bank)
- The Onboarding Leadership Award: Mary McBride,[Bank of Utah](https://www.bankofutah.com/)
- Legacy of Excellence Award:[Quontic Bank](https://www.quontic.com/)


“These award winners represent the very best of what relationship banking should look like today,” said Dan Milgrom, vice president and chief customer officer, Alkami Solutions Group. “The scale and sophistication of innovation across our rapidly growing customer base is remarkable. These institutions are not only embracing digital transformation but doing so in a way that strengthens human connection, delivering faster, more seamless onboarding experiences while building long-lasting relationships with their customers and members.”


For more information about the FIsionaries Awards and the “For the MANTL” Awards, including categories and the full list of winners, visit[here](https://www.alkami.com/resources/client-success/2026-alkami-customer-awards-financial-institutions-and-leaders-recognized-for-driving-innovation-relationship-banking/) .
