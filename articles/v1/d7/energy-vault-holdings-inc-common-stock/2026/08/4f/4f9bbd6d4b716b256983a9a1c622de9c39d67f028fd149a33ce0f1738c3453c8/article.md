# Energy Vault announces BESS agreement with AI hyperscalers

[Energy Vault Holdings Inc.](https://www.energyvault.com/)


has announced the execution of a “strategic commercial agreement,” under the terms of which it will supply battery systems to hyperscalers for


[AI data centers](https://solarbuildermag.com/featured/data-center-download-how-hyperscalers-are-shaping-the-solar-storage-market/) .


In total, the agreement will support an initial deployment of 1.25 GW of integrated power infrastructure through Energy Vault’s battery technology. The deal aims to establish a replicable platform, the company says, combining dispatchable power generation, intelligent battery systems, turnkey EPC and plant integration, and other factors into a singular integrated energy solution for data centers.
