# Energy Vault signs 1.25GW power supply deal with Texas data center developer

Energy storage firm Energy Vault has signed a deal to supply battery energy storage systems (BESS), grid-forming power conversion systems, and AI infrastructure control software for a 1.25GW integrated power deployment at a hyperscale data center development in Texas.
