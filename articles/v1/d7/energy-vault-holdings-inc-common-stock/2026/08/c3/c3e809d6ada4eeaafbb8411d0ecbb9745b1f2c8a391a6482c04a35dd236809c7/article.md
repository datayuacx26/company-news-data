# Energy Vault to supply 1.25 GW of off-grid BESS for US data center

Energy Vault said it has signed its single largest commercial agreement, winning a deal to supply BESS and grid-forming power conversion systems, for a 1.25 GW integrated power infrastructure deployment serving a hyperscaler AI data center in Texas.
