# Energy Vault to supply batteries, PCS for 1.25-GW Texas power project

Energy Vault Holdings Inc (NYSE:NRGV) has signed an agreement to supply battery storage systems, grid-forming power conversion systems (PCS) and control software for an initial 1.25-GW integrated power infrastructure deployment for a hyperscaler data centre customer in Texas.
