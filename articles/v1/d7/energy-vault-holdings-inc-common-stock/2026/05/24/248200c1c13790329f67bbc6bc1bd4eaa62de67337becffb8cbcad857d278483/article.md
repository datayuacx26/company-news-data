# SA power utility Eskom and Energy Vault sign energy storage agreement

Energy Vault Holdings has signed a deal with Eskom, to deploy energy storage systems in South Africa and across the Southern African region. CNBC Africa spoke with the CEO, of the US energy storage company, Rob Piconi.
