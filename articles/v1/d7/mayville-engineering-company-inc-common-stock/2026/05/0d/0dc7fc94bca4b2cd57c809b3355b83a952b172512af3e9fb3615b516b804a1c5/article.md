# Mayville Engineering Company Announces First Quarter 2026 Results

MEC announced first quarter 2026 results, highlighted by continued momentum in its Datacenter & Critical Power end market.


“We concluded the first quarter with strong momentum, driven by successful project ramp activity in our Datacenter & Critical Power end market. Demand within our Datacenter & Critical Power end market remains robust and continues to be the primary growth catalyst for MEC. In the first quarter alone, we secured approximately $50 million of new Datacenter & Critical Power project awards, surpassing the total awards won in this end market during the entire second half of last year. We expect this momentum to drive profitable growth as we move through 2026. The demand environment in Datacenter & Critical Power end market is creating a meaningful opportunity to invest organically in the business and expand our capacity.”


— Jag Reddy, President & CEO
