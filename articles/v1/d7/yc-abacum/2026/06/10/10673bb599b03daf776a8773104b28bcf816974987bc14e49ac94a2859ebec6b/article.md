# Abacum Is Bringing AI-Native FP&A to the SuiteCloud Developer Network

Finance teams today need more than static budgets and spreadsheet-driven forecasting.


As businesses scale, planning becomes increasingly cross-functional, dynamic, and operationally complex. That’s why Abacum’s “Built for NetSuite” SuiteApp is such an important milestone.


As an AI-native FP&A platform built for modern finance teams, Abacum extends NetSuite with real-time planning, forecasting, reporting, and scenario modeling - all connected directly to ERP data through a native integration.


## Extending NetSuite Beyond the ERP


NetSuite is already the operational backbone for thousands of growing companies. Abacum helps extend that core operation through AI Native FP&A functionality through syncing with NetSuite data.


Financial actuals, dimensions, and operational data stay continuously aligned between systems, giving finance teams a real-time foundation for planning and analysis without manual data wrangling.


This enables teams to focus on faster decision-making and more strategic planning.


## AI-Native Planning for Modern Finance Teams


The Abacum platform was designed from the ground up to support faster forecasting, automated reporting workflows, anomaly detection, and instant scenario modeling, helping finance teams move from reactive reporting toward proactive decision-making.


By combining NetSuite ERP data with operational data across systems like CRMs, HRIS platforms, and data warehouses, Abacum enables organizations to create a more connected planning process across finance, RevOps, and leadership teams. The result is a finance function that can adapt more quickly as the business changes.


## Why This Matters for NetSuite Customers


Finance leaders increasingly want systems that work together in real time, not disconnected tools that create more operational overhead.


Abacum represents a new generation of planning platforms inside the NetSuite ecosystem: modern, collaborative, AI-driven, and built for speed.


For NetSuite customers, that means:


-


Faster budgeting and forecasting cycles


-


Real-time scenario planning


-


Reduced manual reporting work


-


Better cross-functional visibility


-


More agile decision-making


As finance teams continue evolving into more strategic business partners, connected planning platforms like Abacum are becoming a critical extension of the NetSuite core product.


## Looking Ahead


The future of FP&A will be increasingly real-time, collaborative, and AI-assisted.


By bringing modern planning capabilities directly into NetSuite, Abacum is helping finance teams operate with greater speed, accuracy, and confidence.
