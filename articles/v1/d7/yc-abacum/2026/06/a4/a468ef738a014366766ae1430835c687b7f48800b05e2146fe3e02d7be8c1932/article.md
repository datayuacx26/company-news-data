# Abacum and Keansa Solutions Partner to Modernize FP&A for Today's Finance Teams

The way finance teams plan, forecast, and make decisions is changing, and for most organizations, the tools and processes they're relying on haven't kept up. Spreadsheet-heavy workflows, siloed data, and disconnected planning cycles are leaving CFOs and their teams one step behind the business instead of one step ahead.


Abacum and Keansa Solutions are pleased to announce their strategic partnership to help organizations close that gap.


# A Partnership Built for Finance Transformation


With extensive experience delivering FP&A, Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP), and Integrated Business Planning (IBP) initiatives across industries such as manufacturing, retail, consumer goods, pharmaceuticals, financial services, and technology, Keansa brings proven domain expertise to every engagement.


Keansa enables finance and business leaders move beyond traditional planning approaches - not just implementing planning technology, but rethinking how planning connects across the organization.


Combined with Abacum's AI-native FP&A platform, this partnership gives finance teams a modern platform backed by real transformation expertise. Together, Abacum and Keansa combine AI-native FP&A technology with finance transformation expertise to help organizations modernize planning with confidence.


# What This Means for Finance Leaders


For CFOs navigating pressure to improve forecast accuracy, drive cross-functional alignment, and make faster decisions with better data, the Keansa and Abacum partnership offers a clear path forward. Together, we help organizations modernize their budgeting, forecasting, reporting, adopt Integrated Business Planning (IBP) approach, improve collaboration across finance, sales, operations, and supply chain, and build scalable planning capabilities designed to grow with the business.


The goal isn't just a smoother implementation; it's a fundamentally better way of planning.


# Building the Future of Connected Planning


Both Keansa and Abacum share a belief that finance should be at the center of business strategy, not reacting to it. That means building planning environments that are connected, intelligent, and forward-looking where finance leaders have the visibility, collaboration and alignment they need to drive real performance across the enterprise.


We look forward to helping organizations transform the way they plan, collaborate, and make decisions.
