# Abacum and AccountsIQ Partner to Deliver End-to-End Finance Intelligence for Businesses

FP&A teams are only as fast as the data feeding them. For businesses managing multiple entities, currencies, and legal structures, that data too often lives in a separate system - one that requires manual exports, spreadsheet manipulation, and a small systems project just to get actuals into a planning tool.


That's the gap we're closing. Today, we're excited to announce our strategic partnership with[AccountsIQ](https://accountsiq.com/) , the cloud accounting and consolidation platform built for multi-entity, multi-currency mid-market organizations. Together, we're creating a unified finance workflow from record to report to plan - where actuals flow automatically from AccountsIQ into Abacum, and finance teams can spend less time moving data and more time using it.


## A Finance Stack Built for Mid-Market Complexity


Growing businesses operating across multiple entities face a particular challenge: their accounting infrastructure has to handle multi-currency consolidation, intercompany reconciliation, and group reporting - before FP&A can even begin. AccountsIQ was purpose-built for exactly this complexity, serving 35,000+ users across 85+ countries with native multi-entity consolidation, built-in FX conversion across GBP, USD, EUR and more, and an intercompany matrix that handles debtor/creditor reconciliation across entities.


For Abacum, this is a natural fit. Our platform is designed to sit on top of clean, consolidated actuals and turn them into driver-based planning, scenario modelling, and always-on reporting. One of the most common friction points we hear about from finance teams — particularly those at PE-backed companies, investment banks, and scaling international businesses — is the manual handoff between the accounting system and their FP&A layer. This partnership removes it.


## What This Means in Practice


With the AccountsIQ integration, actuals booked and consolidated in AccountsIQ flow directly into Abacum without manual re-keying or custom ETL work. Finance teams get Abacum's planning and reporting layer built on top of AccountsIQ's clean, multi-entity actuals, with FX already converted and intercompany transactions already reconciled.


This is particularly powerful for Abacum's Roll Ups use case: consolidating multi-entity financials in real time, without spreadsheets. AccountsIQ's native consolidation structure is the ideal data source for that kind of group-level planning.


The result is a faster close-to-forecast cycle, without adding headcount or a systems integration project.
