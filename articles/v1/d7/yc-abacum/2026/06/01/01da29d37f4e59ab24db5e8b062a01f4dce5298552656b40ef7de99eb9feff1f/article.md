# Abacum vs. a BI Tool: The Difference and Which to Choose

A BI tool shows what happened. FP&A software decides what happens next. That single distinction is the difference between Abacum and a BI (Business Intelligence) tool, and it drives which one a finance team should choose.


BI tools like Looker, Tableau, and Power BI report on data across the whole company. Abacum is an AI-native FP&A (Financial Planning and Analysis) platform built for finance to plan, forecast, and decide. Most teams end up using both. This guide explains the difference, when to pick each, and how they work together.

-

-


BI dashboards are mostly read-only. Abacum is interactive, so finance edits assumptions and reforecasts without filing a data ticket.


-

-


Choose a BI tool for company-wide operational reporting. Choose FP&A software for budgeting, forecasting, and scenario planning.


-

-

## What Is a BI Tool?


A BI tool collects data from across a business and turns it into dashboards and reports. It answers one core question: what happened? Teams use it to track metrics, spot trends, and share a single view of past performance.


Common BI tools include Looker, Tableau, Power BI, Metabase, and Sisense. They serve every department, from marketing to operations to product. That breadth is their strength.


BI tools are strong at three things:


-


Visualizing large datasets across many sources.


-


Standardizing metrics so everyone sees the same numbers.


-


Serving reports to a wide, cross-functional audience.


Their limits show up in finance work. BI dashboards are built to display data, not to model it. A finance leader cannot change an assumption and see the forecast update in most BI tools. That work usually routes back to the data team, which creates a queue.


## What Is Abacum?


[Abacum](https://www.abacum.ai/) is an AI-native FP&A platform built for finance teams at scaling companies. It answers a different question than BI: what should happen next? Finance uses it for budgeting, forecasting, revenue planning, scenario planning, headcount planning, and investor reporting.


Where a BI tool reports on the past, Abacum turns numbers into decisions. Finance leaders build models, test scenarios, and reforecast directly, without waiting on another team. Abacum automates reporting and surfaces the drivers behind each result.


Abacum connects planning data to the operational data that finance already tracks. The platform is interactive by design, so the owner of the number also owns the model. That is the core reason finance teams adopt an FP&A platform alongside their BI stack.

## When to Choose a BI Tool vs. FP&A Software


The choice comes down to what a team needs the tool to do. The two are not interchangeable.


### Choose a BI tool when:


-


The goal is operational reporting and historical dashboards.


-


Many teams need one shared view of past performance.


-


The work is visualization, not financial modeling.


### Choose FP&A software when:


-


The team needs to model scenarios and reforecast often.


-


Finance wants to work self-serve, without a data-team queue.


-


Planning, headcount, and investor reporting live in one place.


Most scaling companies need both. A BI tool serves the organization. FP&A software serves finance. The mistake is forcing a BI tool to do planning work it was never built for.


## How BI and FP&A Work Together


BI and FP&A are complementary, not competing. A BI tool aggregates and displays operational data. Abacum takes data and turns it into forward-looking plans and decisions.


Abacum integrates with BI tools, including Looker, Tableau, and Power BI. Teams keep their company-wide dashboards and add a finance-owned planning layer on top. Data flows between the two, so finance models use the same numbers the business reports on.


The result removes a common bottleneck. Finance stops waiting on tickets to change a view or test an assumption. Instead, finance plans in Abacum and reports through BI, with each tool doing the job it does best.
