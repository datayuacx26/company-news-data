# Abacum and RSM Announce Strategic Alliance to Deliver AI-Native FP&A to High-Growth Finance Teams

# Combining next-generation FP&A technology with deep advisory expertise to help finance teams scale smarter


At Abacum, we’re focused on helping modern finance teams move faster, plan with greater confidence, and turn FP&A into a more strategic driver of business performance. That’s why we’re excited to announce our strategic alliance with[RSM US LLP](https://rsmus.com/) , bringing together Abacum’s AI-native FP&A platform with RSM’s deep advisory and implementation expertise to help organizations modernize the way they plan, forecast, and make decisions.


Abacum was built for finance teams that have outgrown spreadsheets and legacy planning tools, but still need a solution that can scale with the speed and complexity of their business. Through this alliance, customers can pair Abacum’s intuitive, AI-native planning platform with RSM’s deep Corporate Performance Management (CPM) expertise and strong advisory capabilities to build more connected, data-driven operating models.


For Abacum, this alliance reflects a shared belief that the next generation of FP&A will be more collaborative, intelligent, and action-oriented. By combining purpose-built technology with practical transformation experience, Abacum and RSM can help finance teams improve visibility, increase efficiency, and create stronger foundations for long-term growth.


**Megan Riley, Partner, Data & Digital Services at RSM, says:**


> “We’re seeing strong demand for more AI-enabled FP&A capabilities that help finance teams plan more dynamically and make better decisions. We know successful transformation is about people, process and the right technology working together to help clients drive meaningful, lasting outcomes, and we are excited to have Abacum as part of our technology offering going forward.”


**Ryan McDonald, Head of Partnerships at Abacum, says:**


> “RSM brings deep CPM expertise, strong ERP capabilities, and a global footprint that aligns closely with our customers. Together, we can help finance teams not only implement faster, but continue realizing value as their businesses grow.”


The alliance is designed to support organizations beyond initial implementation -helping teams improve processes, drive adoption, and continue unlocking value as their businesses evolve. With both organizations serving customers globally - together, we are well positioned to support multinational finance teams operating across complex environments.


## About Abacum


Abacum is an AI-native FP&A platform built for modern finance teams. Designed for mid-market and high-growth companies, Abacum helps organizations automate reporting, improve forecasting, and make faster, more informed decisions. With customers across more than 40 countries, Abacum enables finance teams to scale with clarity and confidence.
