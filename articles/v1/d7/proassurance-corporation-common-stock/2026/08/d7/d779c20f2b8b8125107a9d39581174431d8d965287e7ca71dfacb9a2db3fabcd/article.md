# The Doctors Company Assists Members Impacted by Oregon Wildfires

**Napa, California—August 10, 2026—** Due to disruption to member medical practices from the Oregon wildfires, The Doctors Company will defer policy cancellations and nonrenewals for nonpayment.


The policy cancellation deferment grace period applies to Oregon members with either home or practice addresses in the impacted ZIP codes listed below. Additional ZIP codes may be added upon damage assessments. The grace period will run from July 31, 2026, to August 30, 2026, unless circumstances warrant an extension.


The grace period is provided without interest or penalty to allow practitioners to care for patients and attend to their practices in the disaster’s aftermath. Affected members should contact their agent or Member Services at (800) 421-2368 with any questions (press 1 for Member Services).
