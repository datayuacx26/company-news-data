# Upcoming Summer Holiday Closures

With summer approaching, there will be several days when all ProAssurance offices will be closed.


ProAssurance offices will be closed on the following dates.


- Memorial Day: Monday, May 25, 2026
- Juneteenth: Friday, June 19, 2026
- Independence Day: Friday, July 3, 2026
- Labor Day: Monday, September 7, 2026


Ahead of the holidays, all ProAssurance offices will be closing at 2:00 p.m. local time on the following days.


- Friday, May 22, 2026
- Thursday, June 18, 2026
- Thursday, July 2, 2026
- Friday, September 4, 2026
