# ProAssurance Wins at IMCA

The ProAssurance Marketing and Risk Management teams were honored with two showcase awards by the Insurance Marketing & Communications Association (IMCA).


The Marketing department received a gold showcase award in the In-Person Exhibit Booth/Display category for the 2025 ASHRM Annual Conference campaign. The racing themed campaign was inspired by the conference location - Charlotte, North Carolina. It offered a photo opportunity with the ProAssurance branded racecar, themed promotional items and socks, and a customer appreciation event at the NASCAR Hall of Fame.


The Risk Management department received a silver showcase award in the Blog/Article Campaign – Series or Collection category for *Claims Rx. Claims Rx* is our bi-monthly CME publication teaching risk management fundamentals, primarily through closed claim case studies.


The awards were accepted during the IMCA Ignite Conference in June by Risk Management team members Mary-Lynn Ryan and Katie Theodorakis and Marketing team members Cherie Dupure and Andrew Knickerbocker.
