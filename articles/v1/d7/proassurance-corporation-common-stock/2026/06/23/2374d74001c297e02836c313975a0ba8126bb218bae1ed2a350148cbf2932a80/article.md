# ProAssurance Risk Management on Medical Economics Podcast

Two ProAssurance Risk Management team members joined a recent *Medical Economics* podcast episode. Bradley Byrne Jr., JD, CPHRM, and Lesley Lopez Viner, MS, CPHRM, participated in “Navigating AI and Physician Advocacy in the Medical Space.” They discussed use cases for AI, potential risks, and the steps physicians can take to influence the regulation and future use of AI in the clinical space.
