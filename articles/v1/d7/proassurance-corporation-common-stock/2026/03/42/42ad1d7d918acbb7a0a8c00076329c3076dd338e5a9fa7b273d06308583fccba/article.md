# ProAssurance Presenting at 2026 ASHRM

ProAssurance Risk Management has been selected as a presenter at the American Society for Health Care Risk Management (ASHRM) Annual Conference in Phoenix, Arizona!


Risk Management’s Assistant Vice President Mallory Earley, JD, CPHRM, and Southeast Regional Risk Manager Bradley E. Byrne Jr., JD, CPHRM, will be speaking Tuesday, October 6 from 9:00 a.m.-10:00 a.m. on " *Navigating the Digital Shift: How Text Messaging and Digital Communication Are Reshaping Medical Practice Risk.*


" The program will address common challenges faced by physicians and other prescribers who manage patients in the digital age.


What Is ASHRM?


This conference brings together members from all facets of the medical malpractice market. ASHRM is a professional membership group of the American Hospital Association (AHA). ASHRM has nearly 6,000 members comprised of risk management, patient safety, insurance, law, finance, and other related professions. The organization works to promote new, efficient risk management strategies and professional leadership through education, publications, networking, and interactions with leading healthcare organizations and government agencies. With numerous chapters across the county, ASHRM helps members access workshops, ASHRM leadership presentations, and educational resources.
