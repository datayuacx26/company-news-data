# 2026 Loss Prevention Seminar is Now Available!

Risk Management is now offering its Loss Prevention Seminar (LPS) for ProAssurance insureds.


This year’s topic, “System Update Required—Managing Risk in the AI Era,” dives into one of the most significant shifts in healthcare: the growing use of artificial intelligence in clinical practice.


The virtual seminar explores current trends, regulatory developments, and emerging AI-enabled tools through a risk management lens. The seminar takes approximately two hours to complete, and participants qualify for CME credit upon successful completion of the post-test.


For more information on the LPS, you can visit the Risk Management[website](https://riskmanagement.proassurance.com/lps) . If you have questions about how to participate in the LPS, contact the Risk Management team atRiskAdvisor@ProAssurance.com .
