# VIDEO: Getting Started with Mesh MEA - How to Sterilize & Prepare Mesh MEA for Recording

Getting started with Mesh MEA begins with proper preparation. In our new video, Multi Channel Systems is collaborating with Dr. Udo Kraushaar and Tom Stumpp from the NMI (Natural and Medical Sciences Institute) in Reutlingen, Germany to demonstrate how to sterilize and prepare Mesh MEA for organoid electrophysiology experiments.


Designed to support recordings from within 3D cell cultures, Mesh MEA enables researchers to capture more physiologically relevant electrical activity while helping preserve organoid structure. This step-by-step walkthrough covers key handling and preparation considerations, helping researchers set up their arrays with confidence before beginning recordings.
