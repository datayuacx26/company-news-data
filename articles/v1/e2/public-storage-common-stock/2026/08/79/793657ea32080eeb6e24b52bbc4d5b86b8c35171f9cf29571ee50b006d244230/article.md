## What Makes a Self-Storage Facility Convenient in Denver?


The most convenient storage facility may depend on more than distance. Where you live or work, the roads you regularly travel, access hours, and the type of storage you need can all influence which facility works best.


When comparing self-storage facilities in Denver, consider:


- **Location:** Look for a facility near your home, workplace, or somewhere you visit regularly.
- **Major road access:** Consider how easily you can reach the facility from routes such as I-25, I-70, or the roads you regularly travel.
- **Access hours:** Check when you can access your unit. 24/7 Access is available at select Public Storage locations in Denver.
- **Drive-up access:** Drive-up units allow you to park near your storage space, which can make loading and unloading more convenient.
- **Climate-controlled storage:** Climate-controlled units are designed to help maintain more consistent temperature and humidity levels.
- **Vehicle storage:** Car storage is available at select Public Storage facilities in Denver.
- **Elevator access:** At multilevel facilities, elevator access can make it easier to move belongings to and from units on upper floors.
- **Unit size:** Compare available unit sizes based on how much you plan to store and the type of belongings you’re storing.
