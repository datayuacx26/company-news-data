# What Are the Dimensions of Popular Storage Unit Sizes in Orlando?

Popular storage unit sizes in Orlando include


**5×5, 5×10, 5×15, 10×10, 10×15, 10×20, 10×25, and 10×30** . These sizes range from about


**25 square feet** of floor space for a 5×5 unit to about


**300 square feet** for a 10×30 unit.


For folks living in Orlando, picking the right storage unit size usually depends on your location and why you need more room. If you’re in an apartment in areas like


**Downtown Orlando, College Park, or Baldwin Park** , a


**5×5 or 5×10** unit might be all you need for some extra space. However, if you’re moving, renovating, or downsizing, you might want to consider a larger unit like a


**10×15, 10×20, or even bigger** .

## What Should Orlando Residents Consider When Choosing a Storage Unit Size?


When picking a storage unit in Orlando, there are a few things to think about aside from size. You’ll want to consider


**what you’re storing, how long you’ll keep it there, and how frequently you’ll need to access your items** . Start by measuring your larger items like sofas, mattresses, dining tables, and appliances, then figure out how many boxes or storage bins you’ll need for everything else.


And don’t forget about Orlando’s


**heat, humidity, and those rainy days** ! If your belongings are sensitive to moisture or temperature swings, a


**climate-controlled unit** might be your best bet to keep everything safe.


Lastly, think about location—you’ll probably want your storage unit conveniently close to your home, apartment, workplace, or on your regular route. This way, popping in and out for your items will be a breeze!

## Frequently Asked Questions About Storage Unit Sizes in Orlando


**What is the most common storage unit size in Orlando?**


Orlando storage facilities offer a range of sizes, with options commonly including


**5×5, 5×10, 10×10, 10×15, 10×20** , and other configurations. Availability varies by facility.


**How big is a 10×10 storage unit in Orlando?**


A


**10×10 storage unit** provides approximately


**100 square feet of floor space** . Its footprint is roughly half the size of a one-car garage and can provide space for furniture, appliances, boxes, and belongings from multiple rooms.


**What size storage unit do I need for an Orlando apartment?**


For a small amount of apartment overflow, a


**5×5** may be sufficient. A


**5×10 or 5×15** provides additional room for furniture and boxes, while a


**10×10** may be more appropriate when storing the contents of multiple rooms. The right size depends on the amount and dimensions of your belongings.


**What size storage unit do I need for a one-bedroom Orlando home?**


A


**5×10 or 5×15 storage unit** is well-suited to fit the contents of a one-bedroom Orlando home or apartment. This includes bedroom furniture like beds, dressers, and nightstands, as well as small appliances such as microwaves, air conditioners, or televisions. There’s also room for boxes filled with personal items, clothing, kitchenware, and books, along with other household belongings like lamps, small tables, and rugs. Choosing between these sizes will depend on how much stuff you have and the dimensions of each item you’re planning to store.


**What size storage unit do I need for a two-bedroom Orlando home?**


A


**10×10 or 10×15 storage unit** may accommodate the contents of a two-bedroom Orlando home or apartment, including bedroom and living room furniture, appliances, mattresses, and boxes. A 10×15 unit may be a better option if you have larger furniture or need additional storage space.


**What size storage unit do I need for a three-bedroom Orlando home?**


A


**10×20 storage unit** may accommodate the contents of a three-bedroom home, depending on the amount and size of the furniture, appliances, boxes, and other belongings being stored.


**Should I consider climate-controlled storage in Orlando?**


**In Orlando, climate-controlled storage is often a crucial choice** for protecting your belongings from the city’s intense


**heat, high humidity, and frequent moisture** . Items like electronics, wooden furniture, artwork, photographs, important documents, and musical instruments can suffer damage from fluctuating temperatures and damp conditions. When deciding on storage features, think carefully about the nature of what you’re storing and how long it will be there. Opting for a climate-controlled unit can help ensure that your valuables remain in pristine condition, offering peace of mind regardless of Orlando’s weather swings.
