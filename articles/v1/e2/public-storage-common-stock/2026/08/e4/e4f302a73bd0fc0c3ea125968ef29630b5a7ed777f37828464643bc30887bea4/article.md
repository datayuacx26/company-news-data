# What Are the Dimensions of Popular Storage Unit Sizes in Minneapolis?

Popular storage unit sizes in Minneapolis include


**5×5, 7×10, 5×15, 10×10, 10×15, 10×20, 10×25, and 10×30** . These sizes range from about 25 square feet of floor space for 5×5 units to about 300 square feet for a 10×30 unit.


For people living in Minneapolis, the size of the storage unit you need usually depends on your location and purpose. If you live in an apartment in areas like Downtown Minneapolis or Uptown, you may only need a small storage unit, such as a 5×5 or 5×10, for extra items. However, if you are moving, renovating your home, or downsizing, you might require a larger unit, such as a 10×15, 10×20, or even bigger.

## What Are the Dimensions of a 5×5 Storage Unit in Minneapolis?


A


**5×5 storage unit** is approximately 5 feet wide by 5 feet long, providing about


**25 square feet of floor space** .


Comparable to a large closet, this compact size can work well for Minneapolis apartment residents who need extra room for:


- Boxes and storage bins


- Holiday decorations


- Seasonal clothing


- Small furniture


- Sporting equipment


- Small business inventory


For someone living in an apartment or condo in areas such as North Loop, Uptown, or Linden Hills, a 5×5 unit can provide additional storage without renting significantly more space than needed.

## What Should Minneapolis Residents Consider When Choosing a Storage Unit Size?


In addition to dimensions, Minneapolis residents should consider


**what they’re storing, how long they plan to store it, and how often they’ll need access** .


First, grab a tape measure and jot down the sizes of your bigger items like sofas, mattresses, dining tables, and any appliances or furniture pieces. Then, think about how many boxes or storage bins you’ll need to tuck in around them.


Remember, Minneapolis weather can have its mood swings! If you’ve got items that don’t like extreme temperatures or humidity, you might want to think about getting a climate-controlled storage unit.


And don’t forget about convenience—pick a storage location near your home, apartment, workplace, or along your usual routes. It’ll make popping in and out with your stuff a whole lot easier!
