A 5×5 storage unit may accommodate items such as:


- Holiday decorations
- Sports equipment
- Small shelving units
- Business records
- Suitcases and travel gear
- Extra household items


For residents in neighborhoods such as Midtown, Buckhead, Downtown Atlanta, and surrounding areas, a 5×5 unit can provide convenient storage for seasonal items and belongings that aren’t used regularly without requiring a larger storage space.
