# How Do You Store Books?

The best book storage ideas make your collection easy to access while using your available wall, floor, and furniture space efficiently.


Depending on the size of your collection and your home, you can store books on floor-to-ceiling bookcases, floating shelves, narrow shelving units, built-in furniture, or other unused spaces. You can also mix books with framed photos, plants, souvenirs, and other meaningful objects to create shelves that feel personal rather than purely functional.


## What Are the Best Ways to Store Books at Home?


The best way to store books at home depends on the size of your collection and how much space you have. Bookcases and built-in shelves work well for larger collections, while floating shelves, book towers, corner units, and multifunctional furniture can help maximize smaller spaces.


Whichever option you choose, keep books organized and avoid overcrowding shelves so individual books remain easy to access.


## How Can You Use Vertical Space for Book Storage?


Floor-to-ceiling shelving can maximize wall space while giving a large book collection a dedicated place to live. It can work especially well in living rooms, home offices, bedrooms, or reading areas where floor space is limited.


For a home-library feel, pair tall shelving with a reading lamp and comfortable chair. You can also build or arrange shelving around a window and add a window seat to combine book storage with a dedicated reading area.


Floating shelves are another option when you want to use wall space without adding a full bookcase. They can be particularly useful for displaying smaller collections or favorite books.


## How Can You Store Books in a Small Space?


In a small home or apartment, narrow bookcases, rotating book towers, corner shelves, and wall-mounted shelving can provide book storage without taking up as much floor space.


Look for unused areas that could accommodate vertical storage, including corners, hallways, spaces beside furniture, and sections of open wall.


Rotating book towers can provide access to books from multiple sides, while tall, narrow bookcases take advantage of vertical space rather than spreading a collection across the room.


## What Are Some Creative Book Storage Ideas?


Furniture and underused areas of your home can provide additional book storage when traditional bookcases aren’t practical.


Headboards with built-in shelves, for example, can provide bedroom book storage without requiring separate bedside tables. Deep open shelving can serve as both a bookcase and room divider, while leaving sightlines through the space.


Other creative book storage ideas include:


- Under-the-stair shelving
- Built-in wall niches
- Window-seat shelving
- Trough-style shelves for desks or countertops
- Hidden-door bookcases
- Benches or other furniture with built-in shelving


These options can help turn otherwise unused areas into functional storage.


## How Can You Organize Books by Color?


Organizing books by spine color can turn a book collection into a visual feature. Arrange books in groups of similar colors or create a gradual transition from one color to another.


You can also use color on the shelves themselves. Painting shelves and brackets the same color as the wall can create a more seamless appearance and allow the books to become the focal point.


If you prefer a more functional system, organize books by author, genre, subject, or size instead.


## How Do You Style Bookshelves Without Making Them Look Cluttered?


To keep bookshelves from looking cluttered, leave some open space and mix books with a limited number of decorative objects. Framed photos, plants, artwork, souvenirs, and other meaningful items can add variety without filling every available inch.


Try a few simple arrangements:


- Stack three large books horizontally and place a small object on top.
- Group three to five decorative objects with different shapes and heights.
- Lean a framed photo or piece of art against the back of the shelf.
- Arrange several books vertically and finish the grouping with bookends or a plant.


Once you’ve finished, step back and look at the shelf as a whole. Breaking up objects with similar heights, shapes, or materials can create more visual balance.


## How Do You Keep Bookshelves From Looking Crowded?


Avoid filling every inch of a bookshelf if you want your collection to feel more organized. Leaving some open space between groupings can help individual books and decorative objects stand out.


Lining up book spines at a consistent depth can also create a cleaner appearance. If you have books of different sizes, placing some of the tallest or heaviest books on lower shelves can help create visual balance.


If you regularly add books to your collection, periodically reassess what you want to keep on display.


## How Should You Store Books You Don’t Have Room to Display?


Books you don’t currently have room to display can be packed in sturdy boxes or containers and stored in a clean, dry location. Keep books away from moisture and avoid packing them so tightly that covers or bindings become damaged.


Books you want to keep but don’t need regular access to can also be stored away from your primary living space. This can be useful for large collections, inherited books, textbooks, or books you rotate in and out of your home library.


If books and other belongings are taking up too much space at home, a storage unit can provide additional room for items you want to keep but don’t need to display every day. The right book storage setup doesn’t have to hide your collection. By using vertical space, choosing storage that fits your room, and giving your favorite books room to stand out, you can create a space that’s both functional and personal.


## FAQ


**What is the best way to store books at home?**
The best way to store books at home is to use shelving that fits your space and collection size, such as bookcases, floating shelves, or built-in storage. Keep books organized, supported upright, and avoid overcrowding shelves to prevent damage and make them easy to access.


**How do you store books in a small space?**
In small spaces, use vertical storage like tall narrow bookcases, wall-mounted shelves, corner units, or rotating book towers. Look for unused areas such as hallways, corners, and spaces above furniture to maximize storage without taking up floor space.


**Is it okay to store books flat or upright?**
Yes, both methods are acceptable. Most books are best stored upright with proper support to prevent leaning. Larger or heavier books can be stored flat to reduce strain on their spines and bindings.


**How do you keep books from getting damaged in storage?**
Store books in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and moisture. Use sturdy boxes or containers if storing long-term, and avoid packing them too tightly. Make sure books are clean and fully dry before storing.


**Can books be stored in a storage unit?**
Yes, books can be stored in a storage unit as long as they are properly packed. Use durable boxes, keep them off the floor if possible, and avoid damp conditions. Climate-controlled storage is ideal for long-term preservation.


**How do you organize books on shelves?**
Books can be organized by genre, author, subject, size, or color. The best system depends on whether you prioritize function or aesthetics. Many people combine systems, such as grouping by genre and then arranging by color within each group.


**How do you make bookshelves look neat instead of cluttered?**
Leave some open space on each shelf and mix books with a few decorative items like plants, framed photos, or objects of varying height. Avoid overcrowding and group items in small, intentional clusters for a balanced look.


**What should you do with books you don’t have space for?**
Books you don’t have space for can be stored in labeled boxes in a dry, protected area or placed in a storage unit. This is especially useful for large collections, seasonal books, or items you want to keep but don’t need to access regularly.
