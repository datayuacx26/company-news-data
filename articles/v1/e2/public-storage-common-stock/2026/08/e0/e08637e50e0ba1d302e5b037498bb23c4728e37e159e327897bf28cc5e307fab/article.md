**300 square feet** for a 10×30 unit.

**10×15, 10×20, or even bigger unit** to fit all your stuff.

## What Should New York City Residents Consider When Choosing a Storage Unit Size?


When picking a storage unit in New York City, it’s not just about the size—think about


**what you’re storing, how long it’ll be there, and how often you’ll need to grab things** . Start by measuring your bigger items like couches, mattresses, or dining tables, and figure out how many boxes or storage bins you might need around them.


And don’t forget about


**New York’s seasonal weather** ! If your items don’t play nice with temperature changes, humidity, or the winter chill, a


**climate-controlled unit** might be your best bet.


Also, consider


**where** the unit is located. Keeping it close to your home, apartment, workplace, or along your usual route can make popping in and out a whole lot easier!

**Should I consider climate-controlled storage in New York City?**


**Climate-controlled storage** can be a smart choice if you’re storing items that may be sensitive to changes in temperature or humidity. New York City sees


**hot, humid summers and cold winters** , which can create fluctuating conditions throughout the year. Items such as wooden furniture, electronics, artwork, important documents, clothing, and other delicate belongings may benefit from a more consistent storage environment. If you’re planning to keep your things in storage for several months or longer, it’s worth considering whether climate control is the right fit for what you’re storing.
