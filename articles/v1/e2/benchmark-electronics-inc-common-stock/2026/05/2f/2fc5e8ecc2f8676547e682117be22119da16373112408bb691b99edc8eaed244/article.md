# EMSNOW Leadership Spotlight - David Cummings

E


MSNow featured Benchmark’s David Cummings, SVP and Chief Commercial Officer, in an EMS Spotlight interview focused on the evolving electronics manufacturing landscape and the company’s strategic priorities for the future. During the conversation, Cummings discussed how Benchmark is helping customers navigate ongoing supply chain challenges, increasing complexity in advanced manufacturing and shifting market demands across industries such as semiconductor capital equipment, aerospace & defense, medical technologies and industrial markets.


The interview also highlighted Benchmark’s customer-centric approach, global operational capabilities and continued investments in engineering, manufacturing and supply chain expertise to support long-term growth and innovation. Cummings shared perspectives on the importance of flexibility, collaboration and resilience in today’s manufacturing environment, as well as how Benchmark is positioning itself to meet the rapidly evolving needs of customers worldwide.
