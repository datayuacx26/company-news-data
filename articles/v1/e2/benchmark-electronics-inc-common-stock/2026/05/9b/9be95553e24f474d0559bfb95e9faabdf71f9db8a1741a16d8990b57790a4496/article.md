# Robots Are Getting Smarter — But Is Healthcare Manufacturing Ready to Integrate Them?

As healthcare packaging grows more complex, robotics is evolving beyond fixed automation toward more intelligent, adaptable systems. Today’s solutions deliver greater flexibility, improved precision, and stronger resilience in regulated manufacturing environments.


In a recent article in Healthcare Packaging, Benchmark’s Automation Manager Justin Blair shares insights on how intelligent automation, including AI-supported systems and advanced robotics, is helping medtech manufacturers modernize packaging and inspection workflows. The piece explores how these innovations are redefining efficiency, quality, and scalability in healthcare packaging and what it takes to integrate them successfully.


If you're navigating increasing product complexity or evaluating automation strategies, this article offers a valuable perspective on where the industry is headed.
