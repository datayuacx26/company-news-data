# Benchmark’s 2025 Environmental Challenge

In 2025, Benchmark’s Environmental Challenge engaged 9,000+ employees and delivered measurable reductions in electricity (4%) and water (5%), proving that site-led ideas can create scalable sustainability impact.


At Benchmark


[, sustainability](https://www.bench.com/sustainability)


is powered by people. In 2025, our teams across the globe turned employee engagement, innovation, and accountability into measurable environmental progress through our fourth annual Benchmark Environmental Challenge.


Together, participating sites delivered meaningful reductions in energy and water use, exceeded greenhouse gas (GHG) reduction commitments, and reinforced sustainability as a shared responsibility across our global manufacturing operations.


## **What Is the Benchmark Environmental Challenge?**


The Benchmark Environmental Challenge is an annual, global competition among


[all our manufacturing sites](https://www.bench.com/worldwide-locations) designed to reduce energy consumption, waste, and water use while strengthening sustainable resource management. The program empowers employees at every level of our global manufacturing operations to identify and implement practical, data driven solutions that reduce environmental impact and support long term operational efficiency.


The Challenge aligns closely with Benchmark’s purpose statement:


###### “We innovate for a healthier, safer, and better-connected world to create a brighter future.”


## Raising the Bar on Climate Action


For 2025, the Benchmark Environmental Challenge established an ambitious goal:


a 5% absolute reduction in Scope 1 and Scope 2 GHG emissions


at the site level.


More than half of Benchmark’s manufacturing sites met or exceeded their year-over-year emissions reduction targets. Sites achieving or surpassing their 2025 GHG reduction goals included:


Almelo, Austin, Concord, Guadalajara, Huntsville, Penang, Phoenix, Santa Ana, and Tijuana


Thanks to the dedication and creativity of our teams, Benchmark surpassed our commitment to reduce absolute Scope 1 and Scope 2 GHG emissions by 15% from a 2021 base year by year end 2025.
