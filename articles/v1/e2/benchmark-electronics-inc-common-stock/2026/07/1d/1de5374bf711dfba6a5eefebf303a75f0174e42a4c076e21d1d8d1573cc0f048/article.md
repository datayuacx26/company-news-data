# Benchmark Advanced Sensor Architecture Expertise Featured in Military + Aerospace Electronics

Today's defense platforms face an increasingly complex operating environment. Autonomous systems, hypersonic weapons, electronic warfare, and coordinated multi-domain threats require more than faster hardware, they demand smarter sensing. The ability to detect, process, and act on information in real time has become a decisive advantage for modern defense systems.


As a result, sensor technology is evolving beyond standalone electro-optical and infrared systems into intelligent, interconnected architectures capable of delivering actionable information when every second matters.


These trends were recently explored in


Military & Aerospace magazine's feature, "Sensor Technology: The Next Evolution in an Environment of Advancing Threat Capabilities,"


which included insights from Benchmark's Senior Director of Design Engineering, Keith Heinzig. His perspective highlights the growing importance of AI-enabled processing, multi-spectral sensing, and advanced system integration as the next generation of defense technologies takes shape.


To learn more about the industry trends shaping the next generation of defense sensing,[read Keith Heinzig's insights in the recent Military + Aerospace Electronics feature.](https://www.militaryaerospace.com/sensors/article/55376888/sensor-technology-the-next-evolution-in-an-environment-of-advancing-threat-capablities)
