# Breckenridge Distillery Turns Colorado’s Famous Palisade Peaches into a Fresh Pour of Summer with New Peach Whiskey

### The Award-Winning Craft Distillery Expands Its Flavored Whiskey Line with a New Expression that Captures Late Summer in the Rockies.


BRECKENRIDGE, Colo., Aug. 11, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Breckenridge Distillery](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=y3X-vBDJmVeQxLUNsHUDt2dr9a9nSjanKQIlcitYL0FyR-0c_FZT8TOK7j9S1pZtDLMUYDOR1QCd85Y84nASOLdK6Bp2q2Q4DGmCAuOWXaAYxlTJ6u84EVImWK0uD_As) , one of the most awarded craft distilleries in the U.S., and a subsidiary of[Tilray Brands, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=LljvPfX8t4_dYPmVTggezGnY2qLcqstYQm1_xnuvUAAB09UO-TJNzIc81MQGbBLK2wlTWJPFi52GJbPhh50SwV-9lOcWAZpIpHqtfpKUFno=) (NASDAQ: TLRY and TSX: TLRY), today announced the launch of[Breckenridge Peach Whiskey](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=y3X-vBDJmVeQxLUNsHUDtyZJ4Cpy99QEdADV_A9bcNxHMniRarGk8loryurNRAPGIHgVRb_-T8ATm31jaR2rOx0QASra-vUzz9jgFsVJ1Wshp-Rv7ilSDhQ8d2h6DfOFBjazornlnfSsboGGcSMpYvEjdfJ_Acoxp3zlraZ5yTc=) , a new flavored whiskey crafted with locally sourced Palisade peaches, the same legendary peaches brewed in Breckenridge Brewery’s Palisade Peach Wheat for the past five years. Inspired by Colorado’s celebrated late-summer harvest, the new expression delivers vibrant peach character layered with the signature smoothness that has made Breckenridge one of America's most acclaimed craft whiskey producers.


Building on the success of Breckenridge Honey Whiskey, Peach Whiskey continues the distillery's commitment to pairing carefully sourced ingredients with its award-winning whiskey. Bright aromas of ripe white peaches open on the nose alongside hints of vanilla and orchard fruit. The palate is smooth and juicy, delivering fresh peach flavor balanced by soft vanilla sweetness before finishing with a lingering, tangy-fruit note.


“At Breckenridge Distillery, we've always believed the best spirits start with exceptional ingredients and a strong sense of place. Palisade peaches are one of Colorado's most iconic agricultural treasures, and we wanted to showcase them in a way that feels authentic to our home state,” says Bryan Nolt, Founder of Breckenridge Distillery. “This whiskey captures the flavor of a Colorado summer—from the orchard to the glass—and celebrates the farmers, ingredients and craftsmanship that make it possible.”


Bottled at 36% ABV (72 proof), Breckenridge Peach Whiskey is available now in 750ml bottles in Colorado retailers, available nationally for[home delivery where permitted](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=6a4lON8meQEVuL4obuaTuW34FCZgp9p7L1nHiBXUSJuyNJDTqQjcIjJkjBREt8C3M3SP4aPSOjF7AmpmCJbFe5QIlqaCWOgUK6dZ75GGJIXkUhrnNy9tTjl7pMYPTMUG4VjAPjFnSH1ezW7GdC5Uk2Fnk2QN4yQGx8iEv8ukZ3x-AI94SgKalLs_mJ0NZGSV) and available in national retailers Fall 2026.[Find Peach Whiskey near you.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=Y0ZLTv_J3oj7moakWL9SDtI1tb5X1M5OZ6j8F-5h8vsmtPJkZUMY20ohzZl_TovRs03o2lKcR8khdYjAnSL78ev744Y96A1d3KoIjSBugFcVgqYIkdhAGioNBaUvu4kaUulsF7eCOetdCl7-VdPluw==)

**About Breckenridge Distillery**


Founded in Colorado in 2008, Breckenridge Distillery is the “World’s Highest Distillery,” and is best known for its award-winning blended bourbon whiskey, a high-rye mash American-style whiskey. One of the most highly awarded distilleries in the U.S., the Breckenridge Distillery is proudly a 3x Icons of Whisky and 10x winner of Best American Blended at the World Whiskies Awards by Whisky Magazine and a 6x winner of Colorado Distillery of the Year by the New York International Spirits Competition. Most recently, Breckenridge Port Cask Finish was named World’s Best Finished Bourbon at the 2024 World Whiskies Awards, joining Breckenridge High Proof, named World’s Best Blended Whiskey, and Breckenridge Gin, named World’s Best Compound Gin at the World Gin Awards by Gin Magazine. Breckenridge spirits have been awarded 7 Double Golds at the San Francisco World Spirits Competition.


The Breckenridge Distillery is more than award-winning spirits, offering an immersive guest experience. Named one of the country’s Top Visitor Attractions by Whisky Magazine, guests can dine at the award-winning restaurant, enjoy show-stopping cocktails, explore an in-depth tasting, and get an inside look at the active production facility—including the opportunity to blend their own whiskey.


Breckenridge Distillery is a subsidiary of Tilray Brands, Inc. (NASDAQ: TLRY and TSX: TLRY), a leading global cannabis-lifestyle and consumer packaged goods company inspiring and empowering the worldwide community to live their very best life.
