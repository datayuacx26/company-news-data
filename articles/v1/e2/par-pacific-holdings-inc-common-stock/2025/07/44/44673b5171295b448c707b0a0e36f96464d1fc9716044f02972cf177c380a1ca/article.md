# Par Pacific Announces Second Quarter 2025 Earnings Release and Conference Call Schedule

Par Pacific Announces Second Quarter 2025 Earnings Release and Conference Call Schedule

(NYSE: PARR) (“Par Pacific”) today announced that it will release its second quarter 2025 results after the New York Stock Exchange closes on Tuesday, August 5, 2025. This release will be followed by a conference call for investors on Wednesday, August 6, 2025, at 9:00 a.m. Central Time (10:00 a.m. Eastern). **About Par Pacific**


Par Pacific Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: PARR), headquartered in Houston, Texas, is a growing energy company providing both renewable and conventional fuels to the western United States. Par Pacific owns and operates 219,000 bpd of combined refining capacity across four locations in Hawaii, the Pacific Northwest and the Rockies, and an extensive energy infrastructure network, including 13 million barrels of storage, and marine, rail, rack, and pipeline assets. In addition, Par Pacific operates the Hele retail brand in Hawaii and the “nomnom” convenience store chain in the Pacific Northwest. Par Pacific also owns 46% of Laramie Energy, LLC, a natural gas production company with operations and assets concentrated in Western Colorado.
