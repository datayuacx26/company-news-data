# Andersen Rings the Opening Bell at the New York Stock Exchange


December 17, 2025 11:00am EST[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_bc2e721461782d70496c3ee74bcd25dd/andersen/news/2025-12-17_Andersen_Rings_the_Opening_Bell_at_the_New_York_246.pdf)


***Global professional services firm lists under ticker symbol “ANDG”***


SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Andersen Group Inc. (“Andersen”), a leading provider of independent tax, valuation and financial advisory services to individuals and family offices, businesses and funds in the United States, today announced that it is now listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) under the symbol “ANDG.”


Representatives of Andersen’s leadership team rang the opening bell at the NYSE earlier today capping a 23-year journey from startup to publicly traded enterprise.


“Today marks a historic moment for Andersen,” said Mark L. Vorsatz, Chairman and CEO. “From our founding, we set out to build a firm rooted in integrity, independence and exceptional client service. Becoming a public company positions us to help accelerate that vision, invest in innovation and create lasting value for our clients, our people and our shareholders.”


The listing provides Andersen with greater access to capital markets to support its expansion across existing service offerings, including tax, valuation and consulting, and new service offerings, such as legal services.


“Going public is not just a financial milestone — it’s a commitment to our culture, stewardship and transparency,” added Dan DePaoli, U.S. Country Managing Director. “We are proud to join the ranks of global public companies and to bring Andersen’s client-focused model to an even wider stage.”


“Going public isn’t about monetization — it’s about momentum,” Vorsatz said. “With greater access to capital, we can invest more deeply in technology, infrastructure and global integration, broadening our services and creating meaningful opportunities for our people. We’ve never lost sight of those rising behind us. Across generations, our teams are grounded in a culture built on trust and enduring values. Stewardship isn’t a slogan here — it’s a shared commitment, practiced at every level. It’s the foundation of our success and the force that will carry our legacy into the future empowering the next generation to thrive and, in turn, pay it forward.”


"We do not want to be the biggest firm in the world. We want to be the best," Vorsatz said.


**About Andersen**


Andersen is a leading provider of independent tax, valuation and financial advisory services to individuals, family offices, businesses and alternative investment funds in the United States. Andersen’s differentiated approach to client service is rooted in core values that emphasize stewardship, transparency and the seamless delivery of independent, high-quality service. Worldwide, Andersen’s presence spans more than 180 countries through its global platform of member and collaborating firms delivering tax, legal, valuation and consulting services across more than 1,000 locations with over 3,000 partners and 50,000 professionals.
