# SRE.ai Raises $7.2M To Redefine Enterprise DevOps for the AI Era

It’s no secret that enterprise DevOps hasn’t kept up with how businesses actually run today. Platforms like Salesforce, ServiceNow, and Workday have become mission-critical, but traditional DevOps and Reliability tools were never built for them.


But maybe the solution isn’t just more tools. Maybe it’s a **DevOps layer designed for these platforms from the ground up.**


That’s exactly what we’re building at **SRE.ai** , starting with **Salesforce** .


Before SRE.ai, my cofounder and I spent years at Google DeepMind and Google Translate, building AI systems that required rigor, scale, and resilience. We saw firsthand that systems built on solid foundations, not patched together reactively, are the ones that last.


SRE.ai brings that same engineering-grade approach to Salesforce DevOps. Instead of treating it like a second-class citizen, we provide an **AI-native control plane** that:


- Detects and prevents risky changes before they break production


- Orchestrates release cycles across teams and business units


- Answers plain-language questions like *“What changed in the last release that could affect our quoting process?”* with contextual, actionable insights


- Reduces chaos and tribal knowledge, letting teams move faster and more confidently


While Salesforce is our initial focus, the same approach applies to other enterprise systems like Workday and ServiceNow platforms that often suffer from the same complexity and risk. Still, we’re starting where the need is clearest.


Early users confirmed what we suspected: Salesforce teams want an AI-native layer that remembers, orchestrates, and scales their DevOps processes.


We’re grateful for the support of **Salesforce Ventures, Y Combinator, Crane Venture Partners, and our incredible angel investors** , who share our vision for the next generation of enterprise DevOps.


This is just the start. If you’re working with Salesforce and want to see AI-native DevOps in action, come try SRE.ai today.
