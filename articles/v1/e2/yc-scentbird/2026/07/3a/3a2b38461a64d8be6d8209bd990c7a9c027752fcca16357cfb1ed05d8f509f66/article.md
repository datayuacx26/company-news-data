# The Best New Perfume Releases on Scentbird: July 2026

There’s never a dull moment in fragrance, and this month’s new arrivals prove it. July’s perfume releases on[Scentbird](https://www.scentbird.com/) include tropical gourmands and unexpected star notes (magic mushrooms, anyone?), each one offering something for every kind of perfume lover.


If you want to discover something unexpected, these[new drops](https://www.scentbird.com/new-arrivals/colognes) deserve a spot on your radar.


## The Best New Perfume Releases on Scentbird


### [CONFESSIONS OF A REBEL Shore Thing](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/confessions-of-a-rebel-shore-thing-255877321184236)


If your ideal scent this season smells like a beach vacation, Shore Thing delivers exactly that. Coconut water and golden pineapple bring a tropical freshness, while salted petals, tanned skin, and coastal woods recreate the feeling of sunshine and salty ocean air. The fragrance is intoxicating, like the feeling of drinking a cocktail overlooking the sea.


### [BOHOBOCO PERFUME Dark Vinyl Musk](https://www.scentbird.com/brand/bohoboco)


Dark Vinyl Musk is made for lovers of deep, otherworldly fragrances. Sandalwood, incense, balsamic accords, leather, and musk come together to create a smoky, sensual scent that feels mysterious without being too overpowering.


### [BOHOBOCO PERFUME Magic Mushrooms](https://www.scentbird.com/brand/bohoboco)


Magic Mushrooms is, unsurprisingly, one of the collection’s most unconventional creations. Blackcurrant adds a juicy brightness while cannabis, vetiver, moss, and earthy accords create a green, textured fragrance that adds to the fragrance’s mystique.


### [BOHOBOCO PERFUME Coffee White Flowers](https://www.scentbird.com/brand/bohoboco)


Coffee, chocolate, golden rum, and vanilla create an indulgent opening before soft white florals lighten the composition, making it comforting, but also unexpectedly elegant.


### [DRIFT Vanilla Shores](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/drift-vanilla-shores-95810482687386)


Vanilla Shores is proof that vanilla can also be worn by the sea. Airy notes of sea salt crystals and vanilla orchid create a luminous, promising atmosphere. This is balanced out with vanilla crème, amber, and blonde woods for a sun-kissed warmth.


### [FRAGRANCE DU BOIS Tropiques](https://www.scentbird.com/brand/fragrance-du-bois)


Fragrance Du Bois’ Tropiques captures the feeling of an endless summer. Tropical fruits and bergamot create a zesty opening right before lavandin and soft gourmand adds a touch of warmth, making it perfect for basking under the sun this season.


### [FRAGRANCE DU BOIS Lucius](https://www.scentbird.com/brand/fragrance-du-bois)


If you’re looking for a woody scent that’s bright and easy enough for everyday wear, then reach for Lucius. It combines bright bergamot and pink pepper with cedarwood, vetiver, and dry amber to create a fragrance that’s fresh and quietly confident.


### [MICHAEL MALUL Ginger + Mist](https://www.scentbird.com/brand/michael-malul)


Ginger + Mist captures the invigorating feeling of stepping into nature. Bergamot and pineapple brighten the opening while spicy ginger and a refreshing gin accord add a crisp twist. Finally, georgywood grounds the fragrance with a clean woody finish.


### [MICHAEL MALUL Citizen Jack Mohave](https://www.scentbird.com/brand/michael-malul)


Citizen Jack Mohave bottles the spirit of the open desert. Bright bergamot and aromatic cardamom open the fragrance before Egyptian geranium, Virginia cedarwood, and vetiver create a woody, earthy finish that’s effortlessly confident.


### [MICHAEL MALUL Jet Black Horizon](https://www.scentbird.com/brand/michael-malul)


Designed to transition seamlessly from day to night, Jet Black Horizon balances freshness with depth. Bergamot, lavender, and clary sage keep the opening crisp, while amber woods and coffee add warmth, creating a polished fragrance that doesn’t trying too hard.


### [QUIKSILVER Beachpoint](https://www.scentbird.com/brand/quiksilver)


Carefree and incredibly comforting, Beachpoint captures the feeling of lingering by the shore long after sunset. Raspberry and jasmine bring a soft sweetness, while sandalwood, musk, and a touch of cotton candy create a warm, beachy dry-down.


### [QUIKSILVER Offshore](https://www.scentbird.com/brand/quiksilver)


Offshore is made for those who gravitate toward aquatic fragrances. Lemon, pineapple, and a sea salt accord create an invigorating opening before lavender, sandalwood, oakmoss, and musk settle into a clean finish, much like skin-kissed skin after a day by the water.
