# July 2026 Scent Horoscope

July is an invitation to slow down and savor each moment, mostly because Mercury retrograde has other ideas. Instead of fighting the cosmic static, we’re romanticizing the pause with grounding, nostalgic scents. Find your July match below.


## Aries:[Etat Libre d’Orange She Was an Anomaly](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/etat-libre-d%60orange-she-was-an-anomaly-140874885021512)


Pack your bags, Aries, but maybe leave the fireworks to somebody else this year. She Was an Anomaly thrives on the unexpected, all bright twists and creamy woods, which is fortunate because your best July stories won’t come from the trip you planned.


## Taurus:[Strawberry Shortcake x Scentbird Orange Blossom](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/strawberry-shortcake-x-scentbird-orange-blossom-253614853482633?forwardedTradingItemUid=00000000-0000-0000-0000-ee8dc2c8178a)


If your friends can’t find you, tell them to check the nearest campsite. Orange Blossom smells like sunshine, a huge dollop of orange marmelade, and the nostalgia of childhood summers, making it the perfect pick for a month spent ghosting group chats in favor of gazing at actual stars.


## Gemini:[Michel Germain Orange Blossom Garden & French Vanilla](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/michel-germain-michel--orange-blossom-garden-french-vanilla-2457)


Mercury retrograde wants your credit card details. Orange blossom and French vanilla want absolutely nothing from you except a wrist to live on, which is significantly cheaper.


## Cancer:[Maison Louis Marie No.14 Icila](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/maison-louis-marie-no.-14-icila-5224)


Happy birthday, Cancer. Since the stars are practically demanding a wardrobe refresh, you might as well smell the part too. No.14 Icila pairs juicy ruby pamplemousse with dark plum and vanilla flower, like the fragrance equivalent of leaving your old self in last season’s closet.


## Leo:[Love Island x Scentbird Love Island](https://www.scentbird.com/brand/love-island)


The spotlight’s taking a quick coffee break, but don’t get too comfortable backstage. Love Island, with all its peachy flirtation, heavy charm, dripping yuzu, and bare skin musk, is already warming up for your inevitable return.


## Virgo:[Ex Nihilo Spiky Muse](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/ex-nihilo-spiky-muse-264790527771091)


Virgo, listen: Nobody’s questioning your standards, but the stars are encouraging a little emotional availability this month: nothing dramatic, just a tad of entertaining the idea of letting somebody in. Spiky Muse brings juicy strawberry into the mix alongside sharp pistachio and cedarwood, because getting out of comfort zones is fun.


## Libra:[Matière Première French Flower](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/matiere-premiere-french-flower-47934701287179)


The promotion, compliment, opportunity, or lucky break you’ve been manifesting finally shows up, and yes, you should absolutely tell everyone about it. French Flower practically arrives carrying a congratulatory bouquet, filled to the brim with sultry tuberose.


## Scorpio:[Kenzo L’Eau Ambrée](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/kenzo-l-eau-ambree-159897499235662)


Not every masterpiece requires a dramatic drum roll and the whole shebang. L’Eau Ambrée unfolds slowly, amber glowing beneath watery freshness, much like the project you’ve been perfecting in the shadows while everyone else was busy bragging about theirs.


## Sagittarius:[Gisada Donna](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/gisada-donna-59896591689192)


A little extra cash lands in your orbit this month, Sagittarius, and the stars fully support spending some of it on fun. Just a portion. Donna’s blend of juicy fruits, white florals, and vanilla is proof that treating yourself can, in fact, be an investment in morale. We know you’ll spend it on plane tickets, so go ahead, book that trip now.


## Capricorn:[Nopalera Bosque Místico](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/nopalera-bosque-mistico-198847394079360)


Some romances appear all glowing and loud like fireworks, but you’re not like the others. Yours appears to be taking the scenic route, smelling faintly of smoky woods, courtesy of Bosque Místico.


## Aquarius:[DedCool Mochi Milk](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/dedcool-mochi-milk-44486798129480)


Your next big idea will arrive while you’re doing absolutely nothing productive, Aquarius, so leave room for a little daydreaming. Or binge watching. Mochi Milk wraps creamy milk, rice notes, and soft sweetness into a perfume that smells very comfortably posh, which feels very on-brand for you.


## Pisces:[Cayla Gray Water](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/cayla-gray-water-211899259396819)


Your assignment for July is simple, Pisces: NO DRAMA. Water is a cocktail of airy aquatic notes and soft florals, creating a gentle ripple effect that turns self-care into a full-blown lifestyle. All while smelling amazing, to boot.
