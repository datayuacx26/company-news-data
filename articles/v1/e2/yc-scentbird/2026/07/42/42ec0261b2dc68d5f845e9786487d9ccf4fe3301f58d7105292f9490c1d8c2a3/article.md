# Imaginary Authors Perfume: Discover the Brand’s Most Imaginative Scents

Initially, you may not think that perfumes and books have much in common. But[Imaginary Authors perfume](https://www.scentbird.com/brand/imaginary-authors) is here to change that. With a mission to create memories through innovative fragrances and creative storytelling, the niche fragrance house treats every scent like a novel.


Rather than searching for just one signature scent, Imaginary Authors believes your fragrance collection should resemble a bookshelf, with a different story to reach for. If you’re discovering Imaginary Authors perfume for the first time, these[Scentbird favorites](https://www.scentbird.com/scentbird-select) are the perfect place to begin.


## Imaginary Authors Perfumes to Discover on Scentbird


### Saint Julep


Inspired by warm Southern evenings and an icy mint julep, the refreshing Saint Julep is[effortlessly nostalgic](https://www.scentbird.com/blog/90s-perfumes-nostalgia/) . Fresh opening notes of tangerine and fresh mint bend seamlessly with whiskey, magnolia, ambergris, and watery notes, softening into an everyday fragrance that feels crisp.


### A Whiff of Waffle Cone


Cult-favorite A Whiff of Waffle Cone transforms one of life’s simplest pleasures into a surprisingly sophisticated gourmand. With notes of caramel, vanilla, whipped cream, cinnamon, and amyris, it captures the comforting aroma of a freshly made waffle cone, only toned down with its subtle woodiness. Trust us, this is an addictive fragrance you’ll want to wear from morning to night.


### Fox in the Flowerbed


Airy and full of charm, Fox in the Flowerbed captures the feeling of wandering through storybook alpine meadows. Jasmine, tulip, mountain air, and pink pepper create a delicate floral bouquet, while honey and olibanum add a soft warmth. It is, simply put, whimsical without being too in-your-face.
