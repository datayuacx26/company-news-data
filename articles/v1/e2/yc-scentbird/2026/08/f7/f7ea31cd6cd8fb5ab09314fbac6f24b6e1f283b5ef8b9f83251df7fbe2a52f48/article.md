# Ariana Grande Ari vs Ariana Grande Sweet Like Candy: Which Should You Wear?

[Sweet Like Candy](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/ariana-grande-sweet-like-candy-2930) outsells almost everything else on Ariana Grande’s shelf, yet[Ari](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/ariana-grande-ari-2928) , her very first fragrance, keeps a stubbornly loyal fan club. That tension is really what the Ariana Grande Ari vs Sweet Like Candy question comes down to: the two share the same fruity DNA, but they treat sweetness completely differently, and the one you should wear depends on which treatment suits you. Let the best scent win.

## Ari vs Sweet Like Candy: the similarities


Both were built in the same happy universe: juicy berries up top, a soft floral middle, marshmallow somewhere in the base, and a mood that feels like a good hair day. Both wear close and friendly rather than loud, and both take beautifully to being layered with their matching body products. Where they part ways is what happens to all that sweetness an hour in, and that is where the real decision lives.


## Ariana Grande Ari


Ari, composed by Jerome Epinette and Clement Gavarry for the singer’s 2015 debut, is a fruity floral with a powdered finish. The opening is all juice, raspberry, pear, and a flash of grapefruit, but give it an hour and the character changes: rose and vanilla orchid move in, and the fruit settles into something closer to petals dusted with face powder than to candy. The marshmallow in the base behaves like a powder puff, not a bag of sweets. It wears gently for about five to seven hours, fading politely rather than disappearing all at once. The honest trade-offs: it sits so close that by late afternoon it can be a whisper at the wrist, and that powdery finish can feel a touch old-fashioned if your taste runs to crisp, modern florals. Skip it if you want a scent people notice from across the room.


## Ariana Grande Sweet Like Candy


Sweet Like Candy, a 2016 composition by Clement Gavarry, is a fruity gourmand that commits fully to the name. Blackberry and pear open like a spoonful of jam on warm toast, honeysuckle keeps the middle soft, and the marshmallow and vanilla base stays glossy and syrupy from the first spray to the last trace on your collar. It is more linear than Ari, which is part of its charm: what you smell in the first ten minutes is what you get all day. It matches Ari’s five to seven hours but speaks up more in the first two. The trade-offs are just as specific: in summer heat or a stuffy room the marshmallow can turn cloying, and the overall effect reads young. Skip it if candied fruit is not your idea of a good time.

## Which one is better, Ari or Sweet Like Candy?


The fork in the road is texture. Both start at the same fruit stand, but within the hour Ari heads toward the powder compact and Sweet Like Candy toward the candy shop, and they never meet again. That single difference settles almost every practical question: Ari is the one for an office, a classroom, or anywhere you want your fragrance to stay your own business; Sweet Like Candy is the one for weekends, evenings, and days when subtle is not the goal.


The short answer


Choose Ari if you want your fruity sweetness softened into powder and petals, quiet enough for any room. Choose Sweet Like Candy if you want the sugar front and center, playful and unapologetic. They are siblings, not twins, and the texture is what separates them.

## Frequently asked questions


What does Ariana Grande Ari smell like?


Ari is a fruity floral with a powdery finish. Raspberry, pear, and grapefruit open it on a juicy note, then rose and vanilla orchid soften everything into a petal-like, lightly powdered sweetness, with marshmallow and musk underneath. It reads pretty and polished rather than sugary.


What does Sweet Like Candy smell like?


Sweet Like Candy is a fruity gourmand, and it commits to the name. Blackberry and pear open like a spoonful of jam, honeysuckle keeps the heart soft, and a marshmallow and vanilla base stays syrupy from the first spray to the last trace at the end of the day.


What is the actual difference between Ari and Sweet Like Candy?


Texture. They open on similar fruit, then fork within the hour: Ari turns powdery and floral, like fruit dusted with face powder, while Sweet Like Candy stays glossy and syrupy the whole way through. If you think in terms of powder versus syrup, you will pick the right one.


Which lasts longer, Ari or Sweet Like Candy?


They are close, both in the five to seven hour range with soft to moderate projection. Sweet Like Candy makes a little more noise in its first two hours. Layering either with its matching body products stretches the wear noticeably, which helps on long days.


Is Sweet Like Candy too sweet for the office?


At one spray it behaves. Beyond that, the marshmallow can grow heavy in a warm, shared space, so go light or save it for after hours. Ari is the quieter desk-side choice of the two because its sweetness is folded into powder rather than syrup.


Which one is more grown up?


Ari, by a step. The rose and the powdery drydown give it a more composed, classic finish, while Sweet Like Candy is cheerfully young at heart. Neither is formal, though, so it comes down to how playful you want your sweetness to feel.
