# Ariana Grande Cloud vs Ariana Grande Sweet Like Candy: Which Should You Wear?

Search “ariana grande cloud vs sweet like candy” long enough and you will notice most people asking are not choosing between good and bad, they are standing in front of two gourmands from the same artist and trying to figure out which one matches the weather outside.[Ariana Grande Cloud](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/ariana-grande-ariana-grande-cloud-3588) and[Ariana Grande Sweet Like Candy](https://www.scentbird.com/perfume/ariana-grande-sweet-like-candy-2930) both come from the same sweet, dessert-leaning family, but they warm up a room in very different ways.

## Ariana Grande Cloud vs Ariana Grande Sweet Like Candy: the similarities


Both fragrances share a gourmand backbone built on marshmallow-adjacent sweetness, a fruit or fruit-like top note, and a musky base that keeps them from turning cloying. Both were composed by Clement Gavarry for Ariana Grande, both wear as easy, low-effort scents rather than anything that needs careful layering, and both photograph the same way in reviews: sweet, approachable, a little nostalgic. Where they split is in exactly which kind of sweetness they lean on and how much brightness they carry into the first hour.


## Ariana Grande Cloud


Composed by Clement Gavarry for Ariana Grande in 2018, Cloud spends its first few minutes on lavender, pear, and bergamot, a top note pairing that is soft rather than sharp and fades quickly into the background. What is left is a thick, warm middle of coconut cream, praline, and vanilla orchid, closer to the smell of a vanilla latte with toasted coconut stirred in than anything floral, and a base of musk and creamy wood that holds that warmth close to the skin. Wear time lands around 6 to 8 hours, and the projection narrows fairly early, so by the afternoon it reads as a personal warmth rather than something that reaches across a room. The main trade-off next to Sweet Like Candy is that Cloud has almost no fruit or brightness to cut through the sweetness, so it can feel flat on days that call for something fresher. Skip it if you want a top note with real snap, like berries or citrus, instead of a slow, creamy build.


## Ariana Grande Sweet Like Candy


Composed by Clement Gavarry for Ariana Grande in 2016, Sweet Like Candy opens with blackberry, pear, and bergamot, a top note that is tart and juicy rather than soft, more like biting into ripe fruit than smelling a candle. The heart brings in jasmine sambac and honeysuckle for a light floral lift, while marshmallow, black currant, and vanilla round it out into something closer to a berry lollipop dusted with powdered sugar than a straightforward floral. A base of cashmere wood and musk keeps it from turning into pure sugar. Longevity sits close to Cloud’s but runs slightly shorter, around 5 to 7 hours, with the fruit and marshmallow notes softening first and the musk and wood carrying the last stretch alone. The specific downside is that the berry-marshmallow opening is strong enough to overwhelm a small space if you spray more than two or three times, and it can smell a little young in a formal setting. Skip it if you want zero fruit in your gourmand and prefer something rounder and less bright.

## Which one is better, Ariana Grande Cloud or Ariana Grande Sweet Like Candy?


The real difference between these two comes down to temperature and texture rather than quality. Cloud is rounder and warmer, built on coconut, vanilla, and wood, so it reads best in cooler weather or anytime you want something cozy rather than bright. Sweet Like Candy is fruitier and lighter on its feet, with berries and a floral lift that give it more energy in the heat, when Cloud’s creaminess can start to feel heavy. Neither one projects harder than the other; they are both close, moderate wears. The choice really comes down to whether the day calls for a warm coconut latte or a cold berry candy, and which of those two moods matches how you want to smell that day.


The short answer


Cloud is the better pick for cooler months and a rounder, cozier gourmand mood, while Sweet Like Candy is the better pick for warm weather and a brighter, fruitier sweetness. Both last a similar length of time, so the decision is really about temperature and texture rather than performance. If you only want to keep one in rotation, let the season decide for you.

## Frequently asked questions


What does Ariana Grande Cloud smell like?


Ariana Grande Cloud opens with lavender brightened by pear and bergamot, then rounds out into coconut cream, praline, and vanilla orchid over a musky, woody base. It reads more like a warm vanilla-coconut dessert with a soft floral edge than a straightforward floral.


What does Ariana Grande Sweet Like Candy smell like?


Ariana Grande Sweet Like Candy leads with blackberry, pear, and bergamot, moves into jasmine sambac and honeysuckle, and settles into marshmallow, black currant, vanilla, and cashmere wood. The overall impression is closer to a berry lollipop dusted with powdered sugar than a classic perfume.


What is the difference between Ariana Grande Cloud and Sweet Like Candy?


The main difference is temperature and texture. Cloud is warm, creamy, and woody, built around coconut and vanilla, while Sweet Like Candy is brighter and fruitier, built around berries and marshmallow with a floral lift. Cloud reads cozier, and Sweet Like Candy reads more playful and fresh.


Which is better for warm weather, Cloud or Sweet Like Candy?


Sweet Like Candy generally works better in warm weather because its berry and floral notes have more lift and read fresher in the heat. Cloud’s coconut and vanilla warmth can feel heavier once temperatures climb, so it tends to suit cooler months better.


Which lasts longer, Cloud or Sweet Like Candy?


Cloud tends to last a bit longer, around 6 to 8 hours, while Sweet Like Candy typically holds for about 5 to 7 hours. Both have moderate projection that settles closer to the skin after the first few hours, with Sweet Like Candy’s fruit and marshmallow notes fading slightly sooner than Cloud’s musk and wood base.


Who should choose Cloud over Sweet Like Candy?


Cloud suits someone who wants a rounder, warmer gourmand for everyday wear and does not mind a dessert-like sweetness. Sweet Like Candy suits someone who wants a brighter, fruitier scent with more floral lift, and both are available to try on Scentbird.
