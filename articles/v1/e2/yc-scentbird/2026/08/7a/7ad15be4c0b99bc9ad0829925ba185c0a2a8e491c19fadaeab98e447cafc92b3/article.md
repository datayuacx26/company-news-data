So: beginner or collector, decisive or still searching, a cologne subscription is the smart move. The only question is which one’s right for you.


##
How to judge a cologne subscription


### The five things that separate a great service from a forgettable one.


#### Catalog size


The most important factor. If a service only carries a few hundred colognes, your real selection pool—the ones you’d actually want to wear—is smaller than it sounds. Bigger catalogs mean more range across fresh, woody, spicy, and amber territory, and more room to grow as your taste sharpens. Scentbird’s catalog runs to over 1,000 designer and niche fragrances, curated by people who work in fragrance for a living (which is the benchmark the rest of the market gets measured against—and rightly so).


#### Designer names—real ones


If you’re drawn to the big houses, check that they’re actually in the catalog: Gucci, Versace, Prada, Dolce&Gabbana. Then check the fine print. A legitimate service carries 100% authentic fragrances, decanted from the real thing—not “inspired-by” versions of the originals. If a service is vague on this point, walk away.


#### Niche and luxury options


Designer names are the front door; niche is where it gets interesting. Smaller houses and luxury lines are where you find the scent nobody else at the office is wearing—and if part of your goal is learning fragrance properly, you want a service that lets you explore the world beyond the mall counter. A catalog with real niche depth is also a sign the service is run by fragrance people, not box-shippers.


#### Plan flexibility


Never sign up for any subscription without checking three things: can you skip a month, can you pause, and can you cancel anytime? Life changes, travel happens, and some months you just don’t need another vial. A service should also let you scale—one scent a month when you’re settled, two or three when you’re exploring. Scentbird’s plans are flexible on all of these fronts, with a serious customer service team behind them if anything comes up.


#### Vial size


Across the monthly cologne subscription options, vial sizes range from as little as 2 ml up to around 9 ml. 8 ml is definitely the sweet spot: roughly 120 sprays, which works out to 3–4 sprays a day for a month (give or take how heavy-handed you are). It’s also small enough to live in a gym bag or carry-on. Smaller than that and you run dry mid-month—and anything bigger starts defeating the point of monthly variety.


#### Monthly price


Prices vary from entry-level services to luxury-only boxes, with the best value sitting in the middle—services that balance a reasonable monthly cost against a deep catalog of authentic fragrances. Rather than fixating on the overall sticker price, divide it by what you’re actually getting: catalog access, vial size, and flexibility. The lowest price paired with a thin catalog is the worst deal on this list, because before you know it, you’ll run out of colognes worth ordering.
