## Frequently asked questions


### What is a Scentbird scent horoscope?


A scent horoscope pairs each zodiac sign with a fragrance chosen to match that sign’s mood for the month. Scentbird publishes a new one monthly, matching astrology-inspired themes like love, travel, or ambition to a scent profile, so it’s a fun styling guide rather than a scientific prediction.


### How does Scentbird choose which fragrance goes with each zodiac sign?


Each pick is matched by mood, not birth chart. The team pairs common traits associated with a sign, such as Aries’ boldness or Pisces’ dreaminess, with a fragrance’s notes and character. It’s meant as a fun styling suggestion, not an astrological formula.


### Can I wear a different sign’s scent instead of my own?


Yes. These pairings are meant as inspiration, not rules, so wearing another sign’s pick works fine. Choose whichever scent description matches your mood or the season, since fragrance preference comes down to personal taste rather than zodiac sign.
