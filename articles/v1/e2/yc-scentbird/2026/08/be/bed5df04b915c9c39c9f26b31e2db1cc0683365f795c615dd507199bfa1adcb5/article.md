# Maison Louis Marie: Botanical Legacy, Reimagined

[Maison Louis Marie](https://www.scentbird.com/brand/maison-louis-marie) blends clean beauty with centuries of botanical expertise, rooted in an interesting family legacy that began in 1792, and yes, it’s as fascinating as it sounds.


It all started with Louis Marie Aubert du Petit Thouars, a French botanist exiled during the Revolution. While most were dodging politics, he was cataloging over 2,000 plant species across Madagascar, La Réunion, and Mauritius. Ten years (and countless orchids) later, he returned to France a hero of science and was elected to the Académie des Sciences. Just another day in the office.


Fast forward 200 years, and Marie du Petit Thouars is carrying the torch, this time with candles, perfumes, and skincare. She grew up in Belgium, and while some kids had lemonade stands, Marie had botanical experiments in her mother’s greenhouse.


[Maison Louis Marie](https://www.scentbird.com/brand/maison-louis-marie) was born from that same spirit of curiosity and reverence for nature. Using her family’s rich botanical history as both muse and map, Marie creates luxury fragrances that feel refined, modern, and rooted in something real.


Still a family-run brand today, Marie and her husband Matthew Berkson are on a mission to make clean beauty smell incredible. Every product is made in harmony with the environment, using plant-based ingredients, sustainable packaging, and zero animal testing. If it’s harmful to you or the planet, it’s not in the Maison Louis Marie perfume bottle.


Also, fun fact: The brand’s mark? It’s an original botanical drawing by Louis Marie himself, specifically, his discovery of the *Antidris Dryochris* orchid. So if you want your scent to smell amazing and have a great story to tell, you know where to start.
