# 8 Most Complimented Perfumes: New His and Hers Perfume Picks for Date Night

Some perfumes are meant to be worn alone. Others seem to invite a match, complementary *his and hers* perfume ** pairings that read as coordinated, yet still let each one shine on its own. The most complimented perfumes of 2026 include a few classic solo standouts, but also some of the strongest pairs worth knowing about.


## Date night perfumes: why some fragrance notes get all the compliments


Certain notes just do more work than others.[Vanilla](https://www.scentbird.com/note/vanilla) ,[amber](https://www.scentbird.com/note/amber) , and[clean musks](https://www.scentbird.com/note/musks) read as warm and approachable almost universally, which is part of why they show up again and again in the fragrances people can’t stop asking about. There are also a handful of crowd-pleasing florals: think[jasmine](https://www.scentbird.com/note/jasmine) ,[rose](https://www.scentbird.com/note/rose) , or a well-blended white floral that round things out, striking that rare balance of being distinctive without being too polarizing. It’s also why so many of the strongest his and hers perfume pairings lean on the same notes from opposite directions. Once you know what to look for, spotting a good pairing gets a lot easier, starting with the picks below.

## Frequently asked questions


### What perfume notes get the most compliments?


Warm, approachable notes tend to draw the most compliments: vanilla, amber, and clean musks read as inviting almost universally. A few crowd-pleasing florals like jasmine, rose, and well-blended white florals also perform well, since they are distinctive without being polarizing.


### What is a his and hers perfume pairing?


A his and hers pairing is two fragrances chosen to complement each other, often built on the same notes approached from different directions, so they read as coordinated while each still stands on its own. Many of the strongest pairings share warm or floral notes that feel connected side by side.


### What are the best date night perfumes?


Date-night scents lean warm, sensual, and memorable, with notes like vanilla, amber, saffron, and soft florals that invite people to lean in rather than project across the room. Pick something a little richer than your everyday scent, and apply it lightly to pulse points.


### Do men and women have to wear different fragrances?


No. Many of the most complimented scents are unisex and wear beautifully on anyone, which is why several picks here are labeled his and hers. Fragrance has no fixed gender, so the best choice is whatever suits your taste and skin rather than the label on the bottle.
