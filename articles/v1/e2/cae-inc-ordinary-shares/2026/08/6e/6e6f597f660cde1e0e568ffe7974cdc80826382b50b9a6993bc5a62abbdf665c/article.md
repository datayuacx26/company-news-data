# HR Pulse: AI + Human Ingenuity: Powering Progress Together

## AI as a Career Advantage


> *"You don't need to become an AI engineer to succeed in the AI era. What matters is understanding what these tools can do, knowing their limitations, and being comfortable integrating them into your day-to-day work," says Brian Stensrud*


As organizations across industries explore the possibilities of artificial intelligence, CAE is focused on a different question: How do we help people thrive alongside it? According to Brian, the conversation should be less about replacing work and more about transforming it.


While headlines often focus on disruption, Brian encourages employees to view AI as a force‑multiplier, an intelligent assistant that amplifies human capability rather than replacing full roles. He tells us that, “o *ne of the greatest opportunities today is simply learning how to use AI effectively.* Even experts find themselves spending time on tasks that AI can accelerate." He explains that the people who embrace these tools will be better positioned to create value, solve problems, and innovate.


The question is no longer whether AI will become part of everyday workflows, it already is. The opportunity lies in helping employees build the confidence and skills needed to work alongside it effectively.


This mindset aligns with CAE's commitment to continuous learning and innovation. Creating an environment where employees can experiment, adapt, and grow as technology evolves.


## Human Skills Are Becoming More Valuable


With AI adoption accelerating, finding the right balance becomes a priority. Brian notes that the tipping point occurs when AI is mistakenly used as a substitute for human judgment. A “trust but verify” mindset protects teams from this. Employees should feel empowered to use AI for speed and efficiency but still validate outputs with expert review. Maintaining transparency around how AI systems work, ensuring employees retain baseline proficiency in critical tasks, and encouraging healthy skepticism prevents overreliance and protects decision quality.


One of the most exciting realities of AI is that it elevates the importance of uniquely human capabilities. Success in the AI era will not be defined by technical expertise alone. In fact, as AI becomes more capable, uniquely human skills become even more valuable:


- Critical thinking
- Ethical decision-making
- Communication
- Leadership
- Creativity
- Collaboration
- Systems thinking


> *"As AI takes on more routine work, skills like communication, leadership, relationship building, ethical decision-making, and creative thinking become even more important. Those are areas where people continue to make the difference," says Brian Stensrud*


In an AI-enabled workplace, success will increasingly be defined by adaptability, learning agility, problem-solving, and collaboration. These are all capabilities that drive innovation, strengthen teams, and help organizations navigate change.


****


## Building Confidence Through Responsible Adoption


Successful AI adoption isn't just about technology; it is about trust.


Brian advocates for a balanced approach that combines experimentation with accountability.


> *"The right mindset is trust but verify. AI can help us move faster, but human expertise remains essential. The goal isn't to hand over decision-making to AI; it's to empower people with better information and more efficient ways of working," says Brian Stensrud*


This perspective reflects CAE's commitment to responsible innovation. We recognize that employees need more than access to tools, they need guidance, learning opportunities, and governance that allows them to use AI confidently and responsibly.


> *"The greatest risk isn't AI itself; it's using it without understanding it. Training, awareness, and good governance help people use these tools confidently and responsibly," says Brian Stensrud*


For HR leaders, this presents a powerful opportunity. They see AI readiness as both a talent strategy and a business strategy. They focus on equipping employees with the knowledge, resources, and learning experiences needed to build AI fluency while maintaining the high standards of quality, safety, and integrity that define their organizations. Through thoughtful hiring, upskilling, responsible governance, and continuous learning, they are helping their workforce embrace change with confidence.


## Talent for the Future


As AI reshapes industries, organizations must also rethink how they identify and develop talent.


> *"The skills we look for are evolving. Adaptability, curiosity, learning agility, and problem-solving will become increasingly important because technology will continue to change throughout people's careers,” says Brian Stensrud*


At CAE, we are excited by the possibilities this creates. We are building a culture that encourages curiosity, embraces innovation, and empowers employees to continuously learn. Brian believes the future belongs to people who can collaborate effectively with technology while bringing the judgment, perspective, and leadership that only humans can provide.


For prospective talent, this creates an exciting opportunity to join an organization where learning, growth, and innovation are part of everyday work.


****


## Strengthening Culture Through Technology


Perhaps surprisingly, AI may strengthen workplace culture rather than weaken it.


As automation handles routine and administrative tasks, employees gain more time for collaboration, mentoring, strategic thinking, and deeper engagement with colleagues. Brian sees this as one of AI's greatest opportunities.


> *"When AI helps remove repetitive tasks, it creates more room for collaboration, innovation, strategic thinking, and human connection. That's where organizations unlock their greatest potential,” says Brian Stensrud*


## Guided by experts


At CAE, we believe the future of work is not about people versus technology. It is about creating an environment where both can excel together. Integrity and innovation are not competing values; they are inseparable. Our commitment to AI is grounded in a clear purpose: enhancing performance, empowering employees, and creating value through responsible innovation. Guided by our experts, like Brian Stensrud, we are helping shape a future where technology amplifies human potential rather than replacing it.


### As Brian reminds us:


*"Organizations that succeed with AI won't be the ones that adopt it the fastest. They'll be the ones that help their people adapt, grow, and use it responsibly to create meaningful outcomes."*


For current and future talent alike, there has never been a more exciting time to be part of CAE. We are investing in the skills of tomorrow, embracing innovation with purpose, and creating opportunities for people to do their best work.
