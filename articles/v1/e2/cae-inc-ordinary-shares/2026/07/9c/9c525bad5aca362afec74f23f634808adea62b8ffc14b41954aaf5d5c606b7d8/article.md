# HR Pulse: Harnessing Digital Platforms to Elevate your Job Search

In today’s job market, it has become increasingly important to position and promote your online persona and portfolio to present a professional and consistent image that aligns with your career goals. Before diving into your online job search and applying for your next position it is important to set yourself up for success and take some time to first look at how you can optimize your online presence to attract a future employer.


Begin with updating your social media profile. Include your most recent work experience, skills, education and references. If you have a personal website portfolio, ensure you are communicating a compelling “About’ section, highlighting your best work and featuring content that demonstrates your successes. Having an up-to-date online presence will help Hiring Managers find you easily as they search for the ideal candidate.


LinkedIn


LinkedIn’s platform offers its members an opportunity to professionally network, focus on career development, and skills development and helps job seekers find the right job that aligns with their experience level and interest.


Maintaining an active LinkedIn profile up to date is essential to help you stand out to Recruiters. Incomplete profiles can give an impression of inactivity, which may cause Recruiters to miss your profile entirely. Ensuring that you include detailed descriptions of your current role, contact information, and a comprehensive summary of your career will provide a clear picture of your professional background and make you easily accessible to potential employers. Completing your LinkedIn profile by adding recommendations from your supervisors, clients or senior colleagues can also elevate your online presence. Recruiters are seeking the most qualified candidates for the job, and they often look closely at any recommendations.


Engaging with others on the platform is also an essential part of your social and professional “networking’ as it highlights your active participation and will provide you with greater visibility to your peers and Recruiters.

CAE Recruiter Tip!


With outreach on any platform, it is important to identify and inquire about a specific role that is of interest to you. Try to avoid sending resumes and asking if you are a good fit for any role within the company. Targeting specific roles that you are interested in, and tailoring your resume and profile to support that role will indicate to Recruiters that you have completed thoughtful research and are seriously considering the role for your next career move.


Clearance Jobs


For those in clearance jobs, it is especially important to maintain a current and complete profile. This helps Recruiters understand your career goals and better assist you in finding suitable opportunities. Active participation through direct messaging and engagement within clearance job communities can significantly benefit both you and the Recruiter. Your dedication to connecting and staying involved demonstrates your passion for your career and can make a strong impression on potential employers.

Employer Career Websites


Most companies today have a dedicated careers page that features rich information for job seekers, helping them make informed decisions about career opportunities and determine if a company is the right fit for them.


Employer websites are an excellent resource for any job seeker, allowing them to easily get to know the company, its locations, culture, company values and benefits and what to expect, even before an interview. Not only can candidates read company reviews from current and past employees, but it can also connect the job seeker with Recruiters, and provide details of job openings, and upcoming events. Whether you are searching on a desktop or a mobile device, companies optimize their sites so job seekers can get access to the information they need, and so Recruiters can easily gather applications from the broadest pool of eligible candidates.


- **CAE Pro Tip 1:** Add the[CAE USA Careers website](https://www.cae.com/cae-usa/careers-at-cae-usa/) to your bookmarked career sites. There you will discover a company where its people are at the very heart of the organization. Visit their locations or search by demographic, you'll get to read stories from employees and learn how their skills, experience and knowledge are driving innovation and how their passion inspires teamwork and growth.


Applicant tracking systems


To stand out amongst the competition, employers are shifting their focus towards creating an experience that meets the heightened expectations of candidates and communicates the value of joining their organization.


Applicant tracking systems and career site content management systems, (CRMs), are all strategic tools that companies utilize to engage with any potential candidates and create this experience. CRMs empower organizations to welcome candidates with tailored job recommendations and content based on skills, interests and geographic locations. A CRM AI gets smarter the more a candidate shares their information, so they are incentivized to keep sharing, and the result is a better and more personalized experience for the candidate. Having a tool chatbot that can answer questions when Recruiters may not be available is incredibly helpful and provides efficiency. Candidates expect an experience like no other and AI coupled with automation makes it possible by providing personalized, data-based insights to make the process seamless and simple.


By creating an environment that is open and inviting, Talent Acquisition teams can continually connect and meet candidates to fill open roles and collect constructive feedback from applicants about their recruiting experience. Participating in these types of campaigns can be highly rewarding for both the employer and candidates to keep the process competitive and fun.


Joining a talent community!


Organizations build a talent community to quickly help them find ideal candidates for in-demand positions. This opens up a pipeline of either active or passive candidates with a hiring team and is advantageous to any employer and job seeker looking to fill or learn about new and exciting roles.


- **CAE Pro Tip 1:** Research the employer you are interested in and sign up for their talent community. This will allow you to provide your contact information and resume and document your job interests. At CAE,[joining our talent community](https://careers.cae.com/global/en/jointalentcommunity) , provides you with an opportunity to learn and explore CAE’s culture and determine if the organization is a good fit to become your employer of choice.


**The best place to learn about developments in your field, requirements for specific jobs and learn about job openings is in a network where employers share their company’s information.**


Leveraging social media networks and job boards can offer significant advantages for job seekers. Engaging actively on platforms like LinkedIn and ClearanceJobs can enhance your visibility, expand your professional network and provide access to a wealth of industry-specific information. Joining a company’s talent community and adding your information to their CRM can position you as a proactive candidate where you will be able to gain timely access to job openings and career events.


### By being strategic in your job search approach, you are increasing your chances of connecting with future employers who will value your experience, expertise and commitment to your field.
