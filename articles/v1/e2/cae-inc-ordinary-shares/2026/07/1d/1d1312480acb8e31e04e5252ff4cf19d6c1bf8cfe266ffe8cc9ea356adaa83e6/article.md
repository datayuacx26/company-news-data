# CAE USA receives the 2024 Platinum HIRE Vets Medallion Award

HIRE Vets is the only federal initiative recognizing employers that recruit, employ, and retain our nation’s veterans, as well as provide development programs to their benefit. This is the sixth consecutive year CAE USA has received this award and the fourth year of platinum distinction, which signifies that 10% or more of our new hires are veterans.


At CAE we proudly employ over 2,000 veterans, within our global workforce, who through their experience and expertise are helping shape and transform the future of military training for our valued customers.


To learn more about the award program, visit[HIREVets.gov](https://www.hirevets.gov/) .
