# Saia Provides January and February LTL Operating Data

JOHNS CREEK, Ga., March 03, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Saia, Inc. (Nasdaq: SAIA), a leading transportation provider offering national less-than-truckload (LTL), non-asset truckload, expedited and logistics services, is providing LTL shipment and tonnage data for the first two months of the first quarter. In January 2026, LTL shipments per workday declined 2.1%, LTL tonnage per workday declined 7.0% and LTL weight per shipment declined 5.1%, in each case compared to January 2025. In February 2026, LTL shipments per workday increased 0.3%, LTL tonnage per workday declined 2.7% and LTL weight per shipment declined 3.0%, in each case compared to February 2025. Additionally, contractual renewals in January 2026 were 6.6% and remained strong in February 2026 at 5.9%.

With headquarters in Georgia, Saia LTL Freight operates 213 terminals with national service.
