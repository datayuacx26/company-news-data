# Currents: Claynosaurz, Robinhood Chain, NODE and more news Welcome to the OpenSea digest. Let’s look back through the biggest NFT and web3 news of the week. 7.17.2026

## Claynosaurz debuts animated series on Amazon Prime Video and Apple TV


[Claynosaurz](https://www.claynosaurz.com/) , a Solana-based NFT brand, released three two-minute episodes this week on[Amazon Prime Video](https://www.primevideo.com/detail/0JFTT1DXGXHKT0UG5ZIJDMZZ13?ref_=atv_dp_share_cu_r) and[Apple TV](https://tv.apple.com/us/show/claynosaurz/umc.cmc.6260iutjnq391tbpnn93sdwzl) .


Each episode follows four dino-friends named Flea, Bex, Trix and Milo as they navigate growing up in a fictional world called Claynotopia. The series comes from Heeboo studio and is directed by character animator[Daniel Jervis](https://www.claynosaurz.com/team) .


Alongside the launch, Heeboo began selling limited[digital card packs](https://x.com/Claynosaurz/status/2075222937443901870/photo/1) tied to the show, where buyers pay to open a pack and receive a set of collectible cards plus a random amount of HEEBOO tokens, a token tied to the studio's rewards system.

## Robinhood Chain cracks top 5 in DEX volume within two weeks of launch


Robinhood's new blockchain network, called[Robinhood Chain](https://opensea.io/learn/blockchain/what-is-robinhood-chain) ,[reportedly](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/07/13/robinhood-chain-surges-into-top-five-by-dex-volume-bernstein) processed $3.1 billion in decentralized exchange trading over a recent seven-day stretch,[placing it](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/07/13/robinhood-chain-surges-into-top-five-by-dex-volume-bernstein) among the five most active networks by that measure.


More than 65,000 users[were reported](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/robinhood-chain-hits-top-5-in-dex-volume) as moving funds onto the network, which launched July 1 and runs on[Arbitrum](https://arbitrum.io/) , a scaling network built on top of Ethereum.


The chain lets users trade[tokenized stocks](https://opensea.io/learn/token/what-are-stock-tokens-on-opensea) and[stablecoins](https://opensea.io/learn/token/what-is-a-stablecoin) around the clock and connects with platforms including OpenSea.


## NODE premieres Kim Asendorf’s ‘PXL’ digital art exhibition


[NODE Foundation](https://nodefoundation.com/) has opened[Kim Asendorf’s](https://x.com/kimasendorf) “[PXL](https://nodefoundation.com/program/pxl) ” exhibition in Palo Alto, presenting three digital artworks built around the pixel, the smallest unit of a digital image, and $PXL, a crypto token that connects collectors to the evolving pieces.


The exhibition includes three digital artworks that use computer programs to create images that change over time. People who collect $PXL tokens can add pixels that influence how the artworks evolve.


“PXL NET” is so large that it[runs](https://nodefoundation.com/program/pxl) on specialized computers at NODE, allowing visitors to view the work at its full scale. Asendorf’s “PXL” is an ongoing digital art project that combines generative art, meaning art created by programmed rules, with blockchain technology that records ownership and participation.

## Circle receives final approval for national trust bank


Circle has[received final approval](https://www.circle.com/pressroom/circle-receives-final-occ-approval-to-establish-national-trust-bank) from the U.S. Office of the Comptroller of the Currency to launch First National Digital Currency Bank, a national trust bank that will provide digital asset custody, meaning secure storage of digital tokens, and is structured to eventually manage the reserves backing the USDC stablecoin. The approval places Circle's trust bank under direct federal oversight and follows the company's 2025 application.


Circle[said](https://www.circle.com/pressroom/circle-receives-final-occ-approval-to-establish-national-trust-bank) the charter supports its long-term plans for USDC, the world's largest regulated stablecoin, while the company noted reserve management will be added in the future rather than at launch.

## Art duo Operator sets November date for ‘Human Unreadable’ third act


[Operator](https://x.com/operator_______) , the artist duo made up of[Ania Catherine](https://x.com/aniacatherine) and[Dejha Ti](https://x.com/dejhati) , announced that Act III of its “Human Unreadable” project will premiere in New York City from Nov. 19 to 22, 2026, according to a[post](https://x.com/operator_______/status/2076666642403066039) on X.


[Human Unreadable](https://www.operator.la/human-unreadable) is a three-act generative art project that combines code, choreography, blockchain technology and live performance. Act III is the work's closing chapter.


The first two acts, a digital art release on the Art Blocks platform and a follow-up reveal of the underlying choreography behind each piece, wrapped up in 2023. A hosting institution for the November event will be announced in July.

## Adam Weitsman joins Yuga Labs board


Successful scrap-metal executive and Bored Ape Yacht Club collector ​​Adam Weitsman announced he has joined the board of directors at Yuga Labs, the company behind the Bored Ape Yacht Club NFT collection, according to an X[post](https://x.com/AdamWeitsman/status/2077480754078949886) .


Weitsman said he took the unpaid position because he believes in the future of Yuga Labs. He added that skipping compensation lets him stay a neutral go-between for the Bored Ape community and the company.


Yuga Labs CEO Figge announced the appointment in a[separate post](https://x.com/mfigge/status/2077475100522389574) , saying the move reflects an effort to build the right team ahead of the industry's next growth cycle.


The announcement comes a week after Bored Ape Yacht Club revealed a[teaser](https://x.com/BoredApeYC/status/2075218415581581806) for ApeFest 2026, an annual gathering for collection holders, set for Oct. 17 at Beeple Studios in Charleston, South Carolina.
