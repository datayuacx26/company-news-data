## What is Robinhood Chain?


[Robinhood Chain](https://robinhood.com/us/en/chain/) is an Ethereum Layer 2 blockchain built by Robinhood Crypto using Arbitrum technology, designed to bring real-world assets onchain. It launched on public mainnet on July 1, 2026, and delivers fast, low-cost transactions secured by Ethereum. The chain is home to Robinhood Stock Tokens — tokenized assets that track stocks like NVDA, AAPL, and GOOG — alongside a rapidly growing ecosystem of NFT collections and community tokens.


In its first week, Robinhood Chain became one of the most active networks in crypto, ranking among the top chains by DEX volume.
