# OpenSea Mobile Is Here: Everything You Own, Everywhere You Go | OpenSea

## Meet OpenSea Mobile


Everything you own, everywhere you go. The OpenSea mobile app is rolling out now on iOS and Android, and it brings the full OpenSea experience to your pocket: trade tokens and perps across 27 chains, follow the top traders, collectors and creators shaping the space, and view your entire portfolio, NFTs included, from one account.


Here's what you can do with it, and how to get in.


## **Trade across 27 chains from your phone**


OpenSea Mobile supports the same 27 blockchains as OpenSea on the web. That means you can trade memecoins on Solana, tokens on Ethereum, and everything in between, all in a few taps. It also includes[Stock Tokens](https://opensea.io/learn/token/what-are-stock-tokens-on-opensea) , tokenized assets that track stocks, available through[Robinhood Chain](https://opensea.io/learn/blockchain/what-is-robinhood-chain) .


Perpetual futures, or[perps](https://opensea.io/learn/token/what-are-perpetual-futures) , can also be traded directly in the app, with Top Perpetuals surfaced right on the Home tab.


## **An account in seconds, no seed phrase required**


Creating an account takes just an email address. The app generates self-custodial EVM and Solana wallets for you automatically, so there's no seed phrase to write down and no browser extension to install. Already use OpenSea on the web? Link your web profile while you're onboarding or do it at any time in your settings.


Funding your wallet is just as fast: deposit crypto, buy tokens with Apple Pay, or a debit or credit card, right from the Home tab.


## **Your portfolio, and your people**


The **Portfolio** tab shows everything you own across all 27 chains in one place: tokens, balances, and your NFT collection. iPhone users can even add a[portfolio widget](https://support.opensea.io/en/articles/14996854-how-do-i-set-up-the-portfolio-widget-on-iphone-ios) to their home screen.


OpenSea Mobile is also social by design. The Feed tab pairs a For You feed tailored to your interests with a Following feed of activity from accounts you follow. See perps, NFTs, and tokens all in the same feed, or filter to your preferred asset, and watch the app get better the more people you connect with.


## **How to get access**


The app is rolling out gradually on both iOS and Android, so you may see a waitlist screen when you first open it. A[referral code](https://support.opensea.io/en/articles/15232781-how-do-referrals-work-on-opensea-mobile) gets you immediate access, and while you wait, you can reach OpenSea through the[mobile web app](https://support.opensea.io/en/articles/10908478-how-can-i-access-opensea-on-mobile) .


Ready to dive in? Our full getting started guide walks through account creation, funding, and every tab in the app, and the[Help Center](https://support.opensea.io/en/collections/19627377-mobile) has answers to common questions.


Everything you own, everywhere you go. See you in there.
