# OpenSea Mobile #OSMobile Feature Contest — Official Rules | OpenSea

## **1. Sponsor**


Ozone Networks, Inc. (d/b/a OpenSea), 66 W Flagler Street, Suite 929, Miami, FL 33130 ("Sponsor" or "OpenSea").


## **2. Eligibility**


The Contest is open to individuals worldwide who are at least eighteen (18) years old at the time of entry, except residents of countries or regions subject to comprehensive sanctions. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Entrants are solely responsible for determining whether participation is lawful in their jurisdiction.


Employees, officers, and directors of Sponsor, its parent companies, subsidiaries, affiliates, advertising and promotion agencies, and their immediate family members (spouse, parents, siblings, children) and household members are not eligible to participate.


## **3. Contest Period**


The Contest begins on August 19, 2026 at 3PM ET and ends on August 23, 2026 at 12PM ET (the "Contest Period"). Entries submitted before or after the Contest Period will not be eligible.


## **4. How to Enter**


To enter, during the Contest Period, post an original photo or video on X (formerly Twitter) showing an OpenSea Mobile feature, and include both:


- A mention of @opensea, and
- The hashtag #osmobile


in your post.


**Entry Conditions:**


- Your X account must be public at the time of entry and remain public through the winner selection period. Entries from private accounts cannot be viewed by judges and will not be eligible.
- Limit one (1) entry per person per day during the Contest Period. Duplicate posts will not be counted as separate entries.
- All entries must be submitted from a single X account. Entries submitted from multiple accounts by the same person will result in disqualification.
- Entries must be the original work of the entrant.

## **6. Judging Criteria**


Entries will be judged by a panel of OpenSea representatives based on creativity, visual appeal, originality, and relevance to the campaign theme. Judges’ decisions are final.

Three (3) winners will be selected and awarded the following prizes:


- First place: One (1) Doodle NFT with an approximate retail value of $680
- Second place: One (1) mfers NFT with an approximate retail value of $290
- Third place: One (1) Genuine Undead NFT with an approximate retail value of $230

Winners will be selected on or about August 23, 2026 at 5PM ET from among all eligible entries received during the Contest Period. Potential winners will be notified via direct message on X.
