# Top 7 CRMs for Landscaping Businesses 2025

Managing a landscaping business in 2025 is complex - seasonal schedules, crew coordination, equipment upkeep, and client relationships demand efficient tools. A **Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system** simplifies these challenges by centralizing operations, automating tasks, and improving communication. Here's a breakdown of the **top 7 CRMs** tailored for landscaping businesses:


-


[HubSpot CRM](https://www.hubspot.com/) : Free tier available; great for medium to large businesses with marketing and sales needs.


-


[Arborgold](https://arborgold.com/) : Industry-specific features for tree care and lawn services; ideal for small to mid-sized companies.


-


[LMN](https://golmn.com/) : Focused on estimating and project tracking; best for mid to large contractors managing complex jobs.


-


[Aspire](https://www.youraspire.com/) : All-in-one tool for scheduling, billing, and route optimization; perfect for small to mid-sized teams.


-


[SingleOps](https://singleops.com/) : Combines CRM with operational tools; suitable for mid-sized businesses handling diverse services.


-


[Jobber](https://getjobber.com/) : User-friendly with drag-and-drop scheduling; great for small to mid-sized residential service providers.


-


[RealGreen](https://www.realgreen.com/) : Scalable CRM designed for green industry businesses of all sizes, including pest control and snow removal.


Each CRM offers unique tools to streamline operations, boost efficiency, and improve profitability. Whether you're a small startup or a large contractor, there's a solution to help your business thrive.

## 1.[HubSpot CRM](https://www.hubspot.com/)


HubSpot CRM combines customer management, marketing automation, and sales tools into one cohesive platform. While it wasn’t specifically designed for landscaping businesses, its adaptability makes it a solid option for companies aiming to expand their operations and meet the demands of a growing and modernized landscaping industry.


### Key Features for Landscaping Businesses


HubSpot CRM stands out with its **lead tracking and nurturing capabilities** , a must-have for landscaping companies with longer sales cycles. It allows users to follow leads from the initial inquiry all the way to a signed contract, while its contact system ensures customer data is organized and easily accessible.


The platform’s **email marketing tools** are a game-changer for staying connected with clients throughout the year. Automated emails can remind customers about seasonal services, like spring cleanups or winter preparations. Plus, the system tracks email engagement, offering insights into which services attract the most attention.


For managing projects, HubSpot’s **pipeline management feature** is highly effective. Whether it’s recurring maintenance contracts or one-time landscaping projects, businesses can create separate pipelines to track proposals, manage different service types, and analyze lead sources for better decision-making.


### Target Business Size/Type


HubSpot CRM is ideal for medium to large landscaping companies, especially those looking to expand beyond their local market or diversify their services. Smaller businesses might find the platform’s learning curve a bit steep, but its ability to scale makes it a valuable investment for operations poised for growth.


### Benefits for Landscaping Operations


The platform’s **reporting tools** offer valuable insights into key metrics like customer acquisition costs, average project values, and seasonal revenue trends. These reports help landscaping business owners make smarter decisions about pricing, staffing, and which services to prioritize.


HubSpot also integrates seamlessly with accounting software, simplifying tasks like tracking project profitability and invoicing. The mobile app is another standout feature, enabling field crews to access customer information and update project statuses in real time. These tools make it easier to coordinate teams and deliver services efficiently - an essential factor for landscaping companies aiming to thrive in 2025.


### Pricing


HubSpot CRM offers flexible pricing options. The **free tier** includes essential features like contact management and deal tracking, making it a great starting point for businesses new to CRM tools. For companies with more complex needs, paid plans unlock advanced features like enhanced email marketing, live chat, and custom reporting, providing additional tools to streamline operations.

## 3.[LMN](https://golmn.com/) (Landscape Management Network)


LMN (Landscape Management Network) is a specialized business management platform created for landscape contractors. It combines features like CRM, project management, estimating, and financial tracking into one system tailored to meet the unique needs of landscaping businesses. This comprehensive toolset helps simplify and organize every aspect of landscaping operations.


### Key Features for Landscaping Businesses


LMN offers an **estimating engine** that calculates material and labor costs with precision. From initial estimates to final billing, projects can be tracked seamlessly. Clients can also stay in the loop through an online portal, where they can view updates and approve changes. The platform’s **crew management** tools allow dispatchers to assign tasks, monitor productivity, and track time across multiple job sites. Additionally, LMN includes **equipment tracking** features to oversee maintenance schedules and usage expenses.


### Target Business Size/Type


This platform is best suited for **mid-sized to large contractors handling intricate projects** . It’s particularly beneficial for businesses managing multiple teams and requiring detailed cost and project tracking. Companies specializing in areas like landscape construction, snow removal, or large-scale maintenance contracts will find LMN especially useful.


### Benefits for Landscaping Operations


By integrating estimating, scheduling, and accounting functions, LMN minimizes errors and improves communication between office teams and field crews. This streamlined approach reduces duplicate data entry and enhances operational efficiency.


The platform’s **job costing feature** allows contractors to pinpoint which projects are the most profitable and refine their pricing strategies. It tracks actual costs versus estimates, helping to improve the accuracy of future bids. Meanwhile, **automated invoicing** ensures faster cash flow by generating invoices based on completed work stages, cutting down on administrative tasks.


LMN also excels in **reporting capabilities** , offering detailed insights into crew productivity, equipment usage, and project profitability. These reports enable businesses to make informed decisions about resource allocation and growth opportunities.


### Pricing (in USD)


LMN subscriptions start at approximately **$99 per user/month** , covering access to core features. Advanced reporting modules and integrations come at an additional cost. For businesses requiring setup and training, implementation services are available for an extra fee, typically ranging between **$2,000 and $5,000** , depending on the size and complexity of the company.

## FAQs


### What should I look for in a CRM for my landscaping business?


When picking a CRM for your landscaping business, aim for one that helps you **keep client details organized, manage projects effectively, and simplify communication** in a single platform. Key features to consider include **lead tracking, appointment scheduling, and cost management** , all of which can help boost efficiency and profitability.


Make sure the CRM can be tailored to meet your specific business needs and works well with the tools you already rely on. Choose a platform that’s **easy for your team to use** and fits your budget to ensure a smooth transition and a solid return on your investment.


### What makes a landscaping-focused CRM like Arborgold different from a general CRM like HubSpot?


Specialized CRMs, such as **Arborgold** , are built with landscaping businesses in mind. They come packed with features like job scheduling, crew management, and tools tailored to handle seasonal services. These functionalities are crafted to meet the specific demands of landscaping operations.


On the other hand, general CRMs like **HubSpot** offer a wide range of tools, including contact management, marketing automation, and sales pipelines. These platforms are designed to work across multiple industries, making them highly customizable. However, they might not provide the specialized tools necessary for managing a landscaping business efficiently.


If your focus is on industry-specific needs, a platform like Arborgold could be the right choice. But if your business requires broader marketing and customer relationship tools, HubSpot might be a better match.


### Can small landscaping businesses benefit from using a CRM, and which ones are best for them?


Using a CRM can be a game-changer for small landscaping businesses. These tools simplify customer communication, help manage schedules, track sales, and keep day-to-day operations running smoothly - all essential for staying organized and growing your business.


Some top CRM platforms tailored for smaller landscaping businesses include **HubSpot CRM** , **Jobber** ,[Service Autopilot](https://www.serviceautopilot.com/) , and[Zoho CRM](https://www.zoho.com/crm/) .
