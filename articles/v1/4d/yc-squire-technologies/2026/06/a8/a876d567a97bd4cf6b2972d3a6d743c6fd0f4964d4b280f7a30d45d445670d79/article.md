## **What Are Automated Campaigns?**


Automated Campaigns are pre-built marketing emails, texts, and notifications that live directly in SQUIRE Engage and run based on real client behavior.


They don't blast everyone. They don't spam. They don't require you to write anything.


They use your booking data to see:


- Who hasn't rebooked
- Who cancelled
- Who hasn't been back in a while
- Who just visited for the first time


Then they send the right message at the right time.


You turn them on once. They run in the background.


Here are the three campaigns every shop should activate.


## **1. Smart Rebooking**


**Turn one-time visits into regulars.**


Clients forget to book. Smart Rebooking learns their habits and nudges them when they're due.


No chasing. No guessing. No manual reminders.


If you're not using it, you're relying on memory.


## **2. Availability Updates**


**Turn next week's gaps into bookings.**


Every Sunday, this campaign checks your schedule for open spots in the upcoming weeks.


If it finds gaps, it automatically messages clients who don’t have a booking coming up, showing them available time slots.


No manual outreach. No guessing who to message. No scrambling mid-week.


Open spots get surfaced before they turn into slow days.


## **3. Reactivate Lapsed Clients**


**Bring revenue back without hassle.**


Every shop has clients who drift away. This campaign automatically identifies clients who haven't booked in a while and reaches out.


It's easier to win back a client than find a new one.
