## How to Choose the Right Photography Studio in Melbourne


Finding the right studio space depends on what you're shooting, how much gear you need, and how long you'll be there. Here are the main factors to weigh.


### Size and Layout


Melbourne studios range from 130 to over 330 square meters. A headshot or portrait session works fine in a smaller space, but product shoots with multiple setups, fashion editorials with large styling teams, or any shoot involving set builds will benefit from more room. Check ceiling height too, especially if you're working with overhead lighting rigs or shooting tall subjects.


### Lighting


Some studios prioritize natural lighting from large windows, while others offer fully blacked-out spaces with professional strobe and LED equipment included. Studios with built-in lighting grids, like Lithium Studios, save considerable setup time. Studios with large windows and natural light work best for organic, lifestyle aesthetics.


### Equipment Included


Most Melbourne photo studios include basic lighting equipment and backdrops in the hire price. Some go further with grip packages, modifiers, and specialty gear. If your project requires a green screen for compositing work, ask about availability, as some studios offer them as add-on rentals. Check what's included before booking separate equipment hire; the all-inclusive cost might be lower at a slightly pricier studio.


### Cyclorama


If you need seamless white or colored backgrounds, look for a studio with a built-in cyclorama. Sizes vary significantly. Lithium Studios offers an 8-meter corner cyc, SUNSTUDIOS has full cycloramas in all three studios, and K-Studio provides a 3.5-by-5-meter cyc that works well for individual portraits but won't accommodate wide compositions.


### Location and Access


Studios in South Melbourne and Fitzroy tend to be closest to the CBD. If you're transporting heavy equipment or props, check for drive-in access and dedicated parking. Studios like XO Studios and Bohemia Bay Studio offer both.


### Hours and Flexibility


Operating hours vary. Bohemia Bay runs from 7 am to midnight year-round, while K-Studio operates from 8 am to 8 pm, with after-hours availability on request. If your shoot schedule is unpredictable, flexibility should factor into your decision.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### How Much Does It Cost to Rent a Photography Studio in Melbourne?


Photography studio rental rates in Melbourne average $120 per hour. Prices range from about $45 per hour for basic suburban spaces to over $200 per hour for premium CBD studios. Most studios offer discounted rates for half-day (4 hours) and full-day (8 hours) bookings, which brings the effective hourly rate down.


### What Equipment Is Typically Included in a Melbourne Studio Hire?


Most Melbourne photography studios include basic lighting equipment, backdrops, and grip gear in the hire price. Some also include advanced LED panels, strobes with modifiers, reflectors, and product tables. What's "included" varies significantly between studios, so check before booking.


### Do I Need to Bring My Own Camera and Lighting?


You'll almost always need to bring your own camera body, lenses, and memory cards. Most studios provide lighting equipment, but the quality and range differ. Studios like SUNSTUDIOS Melbourne have on-site equipment hire departments where you can rent camera gear and additional lighting on the day.


### What Is a Cyclorama, and Do I Need One?


A cyclorama (or "cyc") is a curved, seamless wall that eliminates corners and edges from your background. It's essential for clean product photography, fashion lookbooks, and any shoot requiring a solid-color or infinity backdrop. If you're shooting lifestyle or environmental portraits, you likely don't need one.


### Can I Hire a Melbourne Photography Studio for Video Production?


Yes. Many studios on this list accommodate both photography and video production. Lithium Studios and XO Studios are particularly well-suited for film work, with high ceilings, sound considerations, and lighting rigs designed for continuous use.


### How Far in Advance Should I Book a Melbourne Photography Studio?


Popular studios in South Melbourne and Fitzroy can book out one to two weeks ahead, especially on weekdays. Weekend availability tends to be more open at some locations, while self-access studios like Bohemia Bay are available year-round with flexible scheduling. Booking at least one week in advance is generally recommended.
