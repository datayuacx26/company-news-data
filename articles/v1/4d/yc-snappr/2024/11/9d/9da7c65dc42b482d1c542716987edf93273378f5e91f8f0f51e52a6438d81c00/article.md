## Types of Product Photography Pricing


There's no universal solution and multiple factors come into play in product photography pricing. It's unwise to prefer one pricing structure over others without careful consideration. Depending on the complexity of the shoot your brand needs, you might want to look further into the pricing structure that would best meet your needs and at the same time utilize your budget.


### Per Photo Pricing


Do you have a clear idea of how many images you need and want a predictable cost? Per photo pricing is where the business owner pays a fixed rate per image. This predictable image pricing allows business owners to precisely budget for their product photography. You may expect a set fee for each final, edited photo. It is recommendable for smaller projects where you would only need a specific number of images.


‍


A flat rate per photo pricing typically ranges from $10 to $100 per image. Sometimes, the pricing is structured in tiers, which might look like this:


‍


1-5 photos: $100


6-10 photos: $160


11-15 photos: $210


‍


The price per image often decreases as you order more photos. This model is particularly cost-effective if you only need one or a few high-quality images, potentially making it more affordable than booking photography services by the hour, day, or product.


‍


Consider per-photo pricing if you want to maintain control over your budget while ensuring you get exactly the number of images you need for your business.


‍

If you unsure about how many product photos you will exactly need but you have a limited time capacity, per hour pricing might be suitable. In this model, you pay for the photographer's time rather than the number of images produced. However, one common challenge with this pricing model is the uncertainty about how many images you'll receive per hour. Different photographers may work at varying speeds based on their experience and the specific production demands of your project. This can make it difficult to predict the final output and cost.

### Per Day Pricing


For larger projects or when you need a full day of shooting, per day pricing might be more economical. This involves a flat daily rate for professional photography studios or independent photographers, typically covering an 8-10 hour day. This can cost between $800 - $3000 per day, depending on the photographer's experience and the complexity of the shoot


‍


While this can be more cost-effective for extensive photo shoots, it can be difficult to budget precisely. Do not forget to inquire the ceiling on costs if the project runs over the estimated time.


‍


In this prciing model, local independent photography studios or freelance photographers might offer more flexible pricing compared to larger professional photography studios.


‍


### Per Product Pricing


Do you have a diverse range of products that require different shooting setups? Per product pricing might be your best bet. Save on the running costs of changing production setups, this model involves a flat fee for images of each product, regardless of how many shots are needed to capture it effectively.


‍


Expect to pay between $15-$300 per product, depending on the complexity of the production and equipment required.


‍


While this model provides clarity on cost per item, it can result in more images being produced than anticipated, making it less desirable for businesses with a large number of similar products. To mitigate this, be sure to ask if there's a limit on the quantity of images per product.


‍


Another consideration with this pricing model is that the photo price per product depends on the product itself. Some items are more challenging to photograph and will be quoted at a higher rate. Complex products or those requiring specialized lighting or equipment may fall into this category.

## Additional Costs to Consider


When budgeting for product photography, it's important to remember that the costs don't end with just taking the photos. There are several additional costs to consider to manage your expectations and avoid feeling overwhelmed when your shoot comes. Below, we've outlined some key expenses so you can better allocate your product photography budget.


### Editing and Post-processing Costs


Editing and post-production can significantly impact your overall product photography costs. Snappr product photography services include basic editing like color correction and lighting adjustments. However, more extensive editing, such as retouching, background replacement, or object removal, incurs additional fees you may avail through Snappr Editing services. Business owners should include these potential costs when calculating the total cost of their product photography.


### Reshoots and Revisions Costs


Reshoots and revisions can also add to your product photography pricing. These costs may involve re-shooting, re-editing, or re-delivering images if the initial results don’t meet your expectations. To avoid these additional costs, it's crucial to plan your shoot carefully. Provide your product photographers with detailed instructions, product pegs, or inspirations, and outline your specific requests well in advance. On the day of the shoot, review the images to ensure they meet your quality standards.


### Model and Talent Fees


Hiring professional models or talent can increase your product photography costs. Depending on your product category, you might need to invest in professional models to showcase your collection effectively.


### Styling and Prop Costs


Styling and prop costs can add up quickly in professional product photography. These expenses might include styling fees, prop rentals, or purchases. If you have a specific setup in mind, you’ll often need to source the props yourself unless they are included in your photography service provider’s package.


### Studio Rental and Equipment Costs


Studio rental and equipment costs can be significant. These costs may include professional studio rental fees, equipment rental fees, and even equipment purchase fees. Although Snappr does not offer studio rentals, Snappr shoots fortunately already include essential equipment such as colored or white background, lighting, cameras, lenses, and microphones during the shoot.


## Factors Influencing Photography Pricing


When it comes to product photography pricing, there's no one-size-fits-all solution. Your business, products, and image requirements all factor into the cost of professional product photography services. Understanding these factors can help you manage your expectations and budget accordingly.


### Type of Photography


Type of photography or shoot style affects the cost of product photography. Snappr lets you choose between studio, natural backdrop, being used in person, or stylized with curated props. In other phothography services, such may significantly affect pricing.


### Quantity and Complexity of Products


The number and complexity of products influence the total cost, with more complex items requiring more time and resources. For example, photographing a simple white t-shirt is less resource-intensive than capturing multiple angles of a huge traffic light signal post that may require specific camera equipment, ladders, and high-level techniques.


### Photographer’s Skill Level and Experience


An experienced photographer often charges higher rates, but their expertise can result in higher quality product images. Their efficiency and understanding of various product categories can sometimes make them more cost-effective in the long run.


### Urgency and Rush Service Fees


Rush services typically incur higher fees. For instance, if you need product photos for your online store within 24 hours instead of the standard turnaround time, you might face an additional fee of 25-50% of the regular cost.


### High Season Rates and Demand


During peak seasons, such as before major holidays when many online shoppers are active, rates may increase due to high demand. A photographer might charge 10-20% more during these busy periods.
