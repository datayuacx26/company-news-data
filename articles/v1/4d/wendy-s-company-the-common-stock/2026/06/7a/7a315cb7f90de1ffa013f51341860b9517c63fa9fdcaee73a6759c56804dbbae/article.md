# How & When to Register for Wendy’s Virtual Frosty 5K

It’s that time of year! Wendy’s® annual Frosty® 5K Run for Adoption is BACK.


[Last year, Wendy’s Frosty 5K](https://players.brightcove.net/993691347001/r7ntaC1wg_default/index.html?videoId=6379735414112) **raised more than $225,000** for our signature cause, the[Dave Thomas Foundation for Adoption](https://www.davethomasfoundation.org/) ®. Importantly, because of our virtual format, we were able to include **more than 1,900 race participants** from all around the world!


We’re thrilled to continue the tradition of raising funds and awareness for the Foundation’s mission to find permanent, loving homes for children waiting in foster care with our 2026 Virtual Frosty 5K Run for Adoption race.
