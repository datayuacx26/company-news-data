# Fueling Fans through Wendy’s Football Club

In Latin America, the Caribbean, and Canada, customers can also score a limited-edition Wendy’s FC collectible cup, a keepsake to remember the moment long after the final whistle. Canadians can fill their cup with an exclusive Coca Cola Freestyle Beverage - ‘Sprite Cherry Kick’, dispensed in Canada’s team colors to keep you refreshed during every exciting moment.


And, back for the second year in a row, fans in the UK can take on the Wendy’s Four Corner Kick Challenge – a football-inspired activation, and a tribute to our iconic square hamburgers. Participants can aim to hit all four corners of the goal (because at Wendy’s, we don’t cut corners!) for a chance to win prizes. Keep an eye on[@wendysuk](https://www.instagram.com/wendysuk) on Instagram to see where it lands next.


#### **Bringing Fans Together**


Wendy’s Football Club is about more than great food. It’s about showing up for fans in the communities where they cheer their teams on.


Across markets, Wendy’s is connecting with communities through youth engagement, local activations, and partnerships that bring people together around the sport. In the U.S., Wendy’s restaurants are teaming up with local youth clubs and small businesses during peak tournament moments, creating even more opportunities for supporters, teams, and communities to celebrate together.
