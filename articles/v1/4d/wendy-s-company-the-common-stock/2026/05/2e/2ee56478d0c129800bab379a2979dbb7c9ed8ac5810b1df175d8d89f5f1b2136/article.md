# Celebrate International Hamburger Day with Wendy’s Around the World

This International Hamburger Day, May 28, we’re celebrating a fan-favorite that’s been part of Wendy’s story since day one: our square hamburgers.


First introduced in 1969, Wendy’s original hamburger set a new standard: fresh beef*, made-to-order and served just the way you like it as a single, double or triple. More than five decades later, that same commitment continues to define every hamburger we serve.


Today, Wendy’s restaurants around the world are bringing that commitment to life in new and exciting ways. From bold, limited-time offerings to locally inspired creations, our menus reflect the flavors our fans love while staying true to what makes Wendy’s, Wendy’s. It’s all part of our ambition to be *Globally Great, Locally Loved* .


No matter where you enjoy Wendy’s, one thing never changes: square hamburgers, made to order and served with pride. It’s a commitment you can taste in every bite, in every market, and in every Wendy’s restaurant around the world.
