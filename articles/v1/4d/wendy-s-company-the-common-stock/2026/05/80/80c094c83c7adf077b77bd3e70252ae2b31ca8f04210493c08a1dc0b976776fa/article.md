# Wendy's® Breakfast Guide

For years, fans begged Wendy’s® to get back into the breakfast game. We set out to serve morning meals that are not only convenient and affordable, but also full of mouthwatering flavor. Breakfast joined Wendy’s menus nationwide in March 2020, and over the past six years, we’ve remained focused on serving up breakfast favorites the Wendy’s way.


Breakfast hours and availability vary across franchisee locations. Fans should visit[Wendy’s location finder](https://find.wendys.com/us/en) to find their nearest Wendy’s restaurant that serves breakfast and go to[Wendy’s menu](https://order.wendys.com/us/en/national/menu) for more specific information about breakfast offerings. With savory staples, sweet treats, satisfying coffee beverages and sensational sides, Wendy’s breakfast has it all.

It’s 7 a.m. You’re tired, hungry and in need of a quick, hot breakfast that will satisfy all your cravings (no cooking required)... that’s what Wendy’s is here for. When that morning craving for a combo of sausage, egg and cheese is all you can think about. When you’re daydreaming about a sweet morning treat to get you through the day. When you could *really* use a cold brew before holding a conversation with your coworker... Wendy’s breakfast is here to help.


First, let’s introduce you to the menu items that just hit different. Maybe it's a fluffy buttermilk biscuit and fresh-cracked eggs, or maybe it’s a boost from bubbly[Sparkling Energy](https://www.wendys.com/blog/get-hyped-wendys-new-coffee-and-sparkling-energy-drinks) * or flavored Cold Brews– all we know is that there’s something about a satisfying breakfast served up the Wendy’s way!


#### **Savory Champions: The Reliable Classics**


- **Breakfast Burrito:** The Breakfast Burrito starts with two fresh cracked eggs and Applewood smoked bacon or grilled savory sausage, seasoned breakfast potatoes nestled alongside two slices of American cheese, all topped with a Swiss cheese sauce. All of these ingredients are wrapped in a tortilla and served with two Cholula® Original Hot Sauce Packets on the side for fans looking for an added spicy kick.
- **Breakfast Baconator®:** Topped with six strips of Applewood smoked bacon, a signature square breakfast sausage patty, a fresh-cracked grade A egg, two slices of American cheese and a Swiss cheese sauce, the Breakfast Baconator is complete on a soft, potato bun. True to the Baconator® name and a culmination of everything we love about the Wendy’s breakfast menu.


#### **Biscuits**


- **Honey Buddy Chicken Biscuit:** Crispy chicken patty on top of a fluffy buttermilk biscuit and slathered with a maple honey butter spread. Perfect balance of savory and sweet, and all of the textures you’re looking for in one bite.
- **Bacon, Egg & Cheese Biscuit:** Fresh-cracked grade A egg on a fluffy buttermilk biscuit with Applewood smoked bacon and melted American cheese. Rise and shine with this hearty favorite.
- **Sausage, Egg & Cheese Biscuit:** Fresh-cracked grade A egg on a fluffy buttermilk biscuit with grilled sausage and melted American cheese. Meet your new morning favorite.
- **Jalapeño Breakfast Biscuit:** For a limited-time only, jalapeños, sausage or bacon, a fresh-cracked grade A egg and American cheese all nestled on a buttery biscuit making for the perfect breakfast spice.


#### **Croissants**


- **Maple Bacon Chicken Croissant:** A juicy, crispy chicken breast, Applewood smoked bacon and maple butter on a flaky croissant bun. A little sweet. A little savory. A lot of good.
- **Bacon, Egg & Swiss Croissant:** Applewood smoked bacon and a fresh-cracked grade A egg covered in creamy Swiss cheese sauce on a flaky croissant bun. A breakfast basic worth waking up early for.
- **Sausage, Egg & Swiss Croissant:** A fresh-cracked grade A egg and grilled sausage covered in creamy Swiss cheese sauce on a flaky croissant bun. There’s a lot going on in this simple sandwich and it’s all delicious.


For those looking for meatless options, any breakfast sandwich can also be ordered without meat.


#### **Sweet Stars: The Morning Delights**


Have a sweet tooth? No problem – Wendy's has it all. Your sweet morning fix can serve as the main event or as a complement to your savory meal. You can’t go wrong with how you indulge in these sweet A.M. treats!


- **Cinnabon® Pull-Apart:** The Cinnabon Pull-Apart, is made of warm, buttery dough bites baked together with world-famous Cinnabon cinnamon and topped with their signature cream cheese frosting.
- **Homestyle French Toast Sticks:** First launched in 2022, the Homestyle French Toast Sticks deliver a mouthful of flavor striking the balance of a soft, custardy interior and crisp, golden-brown crust with delicious notes of vanilla.


What’s a hero without its sidekick? Wendy’s breakfast sides are so craveable, they serve quality and taste all on their own.


#### **Side(Kicks): The Tried & True Bites**


- **Seasoned Potatoes:** Hot, crispy, and perfectly seasoned, these are the perfect complement to one of our delicious breakfast sandwiches, like the Honey Butter Chicken Biscuit.
- **Jalapeño Bacon Breakfast Potatoes:** For a limited-time only, add some kick to the morning with jalapeños topping Wendy's seasoned breakfast potatoes, along with three strips of crispy Applewood smoked bacon and Swiss cheese sauce.
- **Honey Butter Biscuit:** Fluffy buttermilk biscuit spread with maple honey butter.
- **Sausage Biscuit:** Grilled, breakfast sausage on a soft buttermilk biscuit.
- **Sausage Gravy & Biscuit:** A fluffy buttermilk biscuit smothered in white pepper gravy and grilled breakfast sausage. It’s what southern hospitality tastes like.
- **Apple Bites:** A selection of crisp, juicy sliced apple pieces, perfect as a snack or a side. ****


Some folks argue that you can’t have breakfast without coffee or a[Sparkling Energy](https://www.wendys.com/blog/get-hyped-wendys-new-coffee-and-sparkling-energy-drinks) drink. Lucky for you, Wendy’s coffee offerings are one-of-a-kind sips that will level up your day.


#### **Coffee Cravings and Sparkling Energy: The Perfect Pick-Me-Ups**


- **Cold Brew:** With flavor options like vanilla, chocolate and caramel.
- **Hot Coffee:** Dressed how you like it. ****
- **Watermelon Sparkling Energy:** A bubbly energy boost with notes of fresh fruit and candied watermelon.
- **Pineapple Citrus Sparkling Energy:** A sparkling pineapple citrus blend with a caffeinated energy syrup over ice. You know the feeling before your vacation when you’re so excited you can’t sleep? This is what that feeling tastes like.
