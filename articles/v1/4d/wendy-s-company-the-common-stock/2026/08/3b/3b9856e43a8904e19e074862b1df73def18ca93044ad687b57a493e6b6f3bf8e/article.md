# It’s Back! The Pretzel Bacon Pub Cheeseburger Returns Once Again

When Wendy’s® first introduced the Pretzel Bacon Pub Cheeseburger back in 2020, it quickly became a fan favorite. Over the years, Wendy's fans have made one thing clear: they wanted it back.


Beginning today, August 3, the Pretzel Bacon Pub Cheeseburger returns to Wendy’s U.S. restaurants for a limited time after winning the “[Bring it Back](https://www.instagram.com/p/DW6t7thkXv0/?utm_source=ig_embed&ig_rid=AJ7erJGR0cw-ZernSv7m0qP) ” bracket earlier this year. And because one pub-inspired favorite wasn’t enough, it’s joined by the Pretzel Bacon Pub Chicken Sandwich and Bacon Pub Fries, giving fans even more ways to enjoy the savory flavors they’ve been craving.


The origin of Wendy's pretzel bun dates back to 2013. More than a decade ago, we introduced our first pretzel buns to the world, and fans fell in love, even writing social posts that read like love songs. We knew if we were bringing back the Pretzel Bacon Pub lineup, we had to deliver on the flavors that made it a favorite in the first place.


The result is an offering that brings everything you love about gastropub-inspired flavors to Wendy's. Pull up your barstool because with this unforgettable combination of ingredients, we're delivering quality taste that's worth coming back for.


### **Tell Me More**


After leaving menus (but never our hearts) in 2022, the Pretzel Bacon Pub Cheeseburger has re-emerged to remind everyone who the real MVP (Most Valuable Pretzel) is.


Layered between a soft pretzel bun is Wendy's fresh, never frozen quarter-pound beef patty*, topped with Muenster cheese, Applewood-smoked bacon, smoky honey mustard, warm beer cheese sauce, crispy fried onions and pickles. This mouthwatering cheeseburger combines unforgettable ingredients that have earned a loyal following from Wendy's fans.


### **More Ways to Pub**


This year, fans have more than one way to enjoy Pretzel Bacon Pub flavor.


### **Pretzel Bacon Pub Chicken Sandwich**


Craving chicken instead? The Pretzel Bacon Pub Chicken Sandwich delivers the same pub-inspired flavor profile fans love, featuring Wendy's Classic chicken filet with the lineup's signature toppings.


### **Bacon Pub Fries**


Looking for the perfect side? Bacon Pub Fries take Wendy's Hot & Crispy Fries and top them with beer cheese, shredded cheddar cheese and Applewood-smoked bacon crumbles. They're everything you love about the Pretzel Bacon Pub experience, piled high on a bed of fries.


### **Ready to reunite with a fan favorite?**


Visit your nearest participating U.S. Wendy's restaurant or download the Wendy's app to order the Pretzel Bacon Pub Cheeseburger, Pretzel Bacon Pub Chicken Sandwich or Bacon Pub Fries for a limited time.
