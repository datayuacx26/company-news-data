#### **What’s In Wendy’s Chili?**


Made with our delicious beef, a hearty helping of vegetables and (of course) chili beans, this is a tried-and-true classic. What makes it so classic, you ask? Simple: quality ingredients, time-tested recipes, and a whole lot of Wendy’s love.


#### **Facts About Wendy’s Chili**


- **83 million servings** every year in the U.S. alone. That's over 5.5 million gallons—enough for 8.5 Olympic-sized swimming pools!
- **Available** **in 100% of our U.S. locations** as well as in multiple international locations, so you’re never far from a warm bowl.


- Wendy's Canada fans love our Chili so much, they have Chili Cheese Fries and Chili Cheese Nachos on their menu.


- **Loaded with protein:** 19g of protein in a small order and 25g of protein in a large order. It’s perfect for hitting your goals when eating on the go.
- One fan loved our chili so much he challenged himself to eat it **3x a day for 30 days straight** —and *still* came out a fan on the other side!
- **Home state pride runs deep** with Ohio ranking no. 1 followed by Florida, North Carolina, California and Texas **** for the most 2025 chili sales.
- **No. 1 at the Cookoff:** Wendy’s Chili comes in first every time. Rumor has it when one Wendy’s crew member showed up to a local chili cookoff with a batch of Wendy’s Chili, they *won* the whole competition, not to mention saving themselves some time.

Select Wendy’s locations do offer a family size chili, a perfect option for busy weeknights or when you've got a hungry crew to feed.


To see if your Wendy’s offers a family size chili, download the[Wendy’s app](https://www.wendys.com/wendys-app) , enter in your nearby Wendy’s and you can check to see if it is available near you.


#### **What Can You Eat with Chili?**


One of the best things about chili is its versatility. With only 280 calories in a small-serving and 370 calories in a large-serving, it’s a great, protein-packed option that will keep you satisfied. Now, if you’re wondering what to have alongside your chili, we’ve got your back with a few of our favorite pairings:


- **The Classic:** Pair it with a Dave’s Single®, our most-ordered chili combination.
- **The Cozy Comfort Play:** Chili plus Wendy’s baked potato, the ultimate chilly day option.
- **The Crispy Upgrade:** Combo it with Wendy’s Hot & Crispy Fries that are perfect for dipping.
- **The Spicy Challenge:** Pair with our Spicy Nuggets for even more of that irresistible Wendy’s sizzlin’ spice.
