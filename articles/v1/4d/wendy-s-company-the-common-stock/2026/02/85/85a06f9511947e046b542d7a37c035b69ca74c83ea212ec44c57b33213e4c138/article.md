# Wendy's Team Shares Their Girl Scout Stories

The collaboration that’s *mint* to be... Wendy’s®, as a licensed partner of Girl Scouts of the USA (GSUSA) launched the newThin Mints® Frosty® Fusion and returning Thin MintsTM Frosty Swirl®, now available nationwide for a limited time.


The all new Thin Mints Frosty Fusion combines Wendy's Vanilla Frosty with a rich, minty cookie crumble sauce and Girl Scout Thin Mints cookie crumbles, delivering a delicious chocolatey, minty experience with every creamy spoonful. The returning Thin Mints Frosty Swirl takes Wendy's Vanilla Frosty swirled and topped with the same rich, minty cookie crumble sauce inspired by Girl Scout Thin Mints. With both versions available in chocolate and vanilla options, fans can choose their favorite way to enjoy the Thin Mints Frosty.
