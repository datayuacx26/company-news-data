# Wendy’s Canada Serves Up a Canadian Classic — Poutine Now Available for Breakfast

When it comes to comfort food, few dishes are as proudly Canadian as poutine. At Wendy’s Canada, it’s long been a[fan favourite](https://vt.tiktok.com/ZSHEp7JaX/) side – traditionally made with our Hot & Crispy fries, rich gravy and real Canadian cheese curds.


Now, Wendy’s is bringing that same crave‑worthy experience to the morning menu with Breakfast Poutine, delivering big flavour to start the day.


[Wendy's Canada Menu](https://order.wendys.com/ca/en/national/menu)


#### **A Canadian Classic, Made with Canadian Ingredients**


Poutine has earned its place as one of Wendy’s Canada’s most-loved menu items, and our new Breakfast Poutine continues that tradition with Canadian ingredients at its core. Each order starts with seasoned potatoes made from Canadian-grown potatoes and is topped with cheese curds from Quebec - delivering the familiar flavours fans love in every bite. It’s a simple combination, but one that has made Wendy’s Poutine a go-to choice for Canadians craving comfort food done right.


#### **Introducing Breakfast Poutine: A Morning Twist on a Classic**


As part of our commitment to breakfast innovation, Wendy’s Canada is introducing Breakfast Poutine - a limited-time offering that reimagines a beloved Canadian favourite for the morning daypart. Launching nationally on May 4, Breakfast Poutine combines Wendy’s signature breakfast flavours with the indulgence Canadians expect from poutine.


Wendy’s customers can choose from two delicious Breakfast Poutine options:


- **Classic Breakfast Poutine:** Wendy’s Seasoned Breakfast Potatoes topped with gravy and Canadian cheese curds.
- **Swiss Cheese Breakfast Poutine** : Wendy’s Seasoned Breakfast Potatoes layered with a creamy Swiss cheese sauce and Canadian cheese curds.


Fans can also upgrade their Breakfast Poutine by adding two strips of Wendy’s Apple-wood smoked bacon for an extra layer of flavour. Both options can be enjoyed on their own or added as an upgrade to any breakfast combo.

#### **A Canadian Favourite, the Wendy’s Way**


From our classic poutine to our latest breakfast innovation, Wendy’s Canada continues to put a fresh spin on familiar favourites - always made with quality ingredients and local pride. Whether enjoyed in the morning or later in the day, poutine remains a delicious celebration of Canadian comfort food, done the Wendy’s way.


Whether you’re shaking up your breakfast routine or just looking for something a little more indulgent to start the day, Wendy’s Breakfast Poutine proves that some classics are worth enjoying at any hour.
