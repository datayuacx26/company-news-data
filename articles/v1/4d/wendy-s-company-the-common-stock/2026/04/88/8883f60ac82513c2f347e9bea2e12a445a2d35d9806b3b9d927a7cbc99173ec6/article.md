# Wendy’s Spicy Chicken Sandwich Just Got an Upgrade

Wendy's® is taking its iconic Spicy Chicken Sandwich to the next level! Now available at Wendy's restaurants across the U.S., this fan favorite classic gets a crave-worthy refresh with enhanced flavor and premium ingredients that deliver heat that hits.

#### **What's New?**


Inspired by feedback from our fans, the reimagined Spicy Chicken Sandwich features several key upgrades:


- **New Bold Marinade –** Delivers a juicier bite
- **Premium Panko-Style Breading –** Infused with nine distinctive spices for maximum crunch and bold heat
- **Soft Potato Bun –** A slightly sweet bun that complements the updated flavors
- **Rich Homestyle Mayo –** Inspired by classic Southern recipes for added richness and tang
- **Pickles –** Added for extra crunch and flavor balance


#### **Setting the Standard**


As the brand that introduced the Spicy Chicken Sandwich to the quick-service restaurant category, Wendy's continues to lead the way. This elevation honors the sandwich's legacy while delivering the modern, bold flavor today's fans crave.


#### **For Every Taste**


Not ready for the heat? Fans can enjoy the upgraded Classic Chicken Sandwich, which features the same new potato bun and homestyle mayo, giving guests a flavorful option without the kick.


#### **Brand-Wide Upgrades**


Fans will see upgrades, such as the soft potato bun, across Wendy's entire premium sandwich line-up:


- Dave's Single®, Double® and Triple®
- Baconator® and Son of Baconator®
- Big Bacon Classic®
- Asiago Ranch Chicken Club and Spicy Asiago Ranch Chicken Club
- Crispy Panko Fish Sandwich


This refresh isn't just a moment in time. It's Wendy's commitment to raising the bar on quality in everything we do. Behind these changes was a comprehensive and consumer-driven development process to ensure every element delivers on the promise: freshly made, crispy, juicy sandwiches that fans truly crave.
