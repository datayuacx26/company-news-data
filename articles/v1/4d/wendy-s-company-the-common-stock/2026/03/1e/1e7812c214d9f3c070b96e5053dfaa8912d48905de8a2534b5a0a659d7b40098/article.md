# Wendy’s Teams Up with DC for Limited-Edition Kids’ Meal Toys

Calling all DC fans! Starting late March, Wendy's® is partnering with Warner Bros. Discovery Global Consumer Products to drop a limited-edition DC® Rides Kids’ Meal featuring 12 exclusive DC collectible toys available in the U.S. and Canada. With 6 DC Super Heros and 6 Villainous Variants and their DC Rides, this is *the* adventure your family needs.

#### **What are the toys?**


The super line-up includes Batman, Superman, Wonder Woman, The Flash, Green Lantern and Supergirl and their most formidable foes: Zur-En-Arrh, Bizarro, Artemis, Sinestro, Reverse Flash and Bizarro Supergirl. Each figurine also comes with its own DC Ride, designed to excite Wendy’s and DC fans of all generations.


Collect them all to build the ultimate DC team!


#### **How do you collect all 12 toys?**


Here’s where the fun begins: fans who order a Wendy's Kids' Meal® between late March and early June at any participating Wendy's unlock a collectible DC toy with their meal. You can order[in-restaurant](https://order.wendys.com/?site=find%3Futm_source%3Dpaid-search&utm_medium=search&utm_campaign=always-on&gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=129273778&gbraid=0AAAAAD8LVoNeGIZOyCwZvWn2mNpTQgbGW&gclid=CjwKCAiAv5bMBhAIEiwAqP9GuH6F1OIna7IiNolnsGcpKcl5cirl_THL1i5XQuvlDva7Sv45S71kPhoCCrMQAvD_BwE&lang=en_US) at participating Wendy’s locations in the U.S. or Canada, through the[Wendy's app](https://m-wendys.app.link/gm-kids-meal-web-blog-download-app) or on[Wendys.com.](https://www.wendys.com/)


With a toy waiting in every meal, every visit to Wendy's opens a new adventure in the DC universe. The more you collect, the more combinations you can unlock and the more opportunities for endless play!


#### **What’s in a Wendy’s Kids’ Meal?**


At Wendy’s, every meal comes with fresh, never frozen beef* and high-quality ingredients because quality is our superhero.


Every meal features the choice of:


- 2-piece chicken tenders, 4-piece chicken nuggets, a hamburger or cheeseburger (U.S.)
- 4-piece chicken nuggets, hamburger, cheeseburger or 3-piece French Toast Sticks (Canada)
- Jr. Hot & Crispy Fries or apple bites
- Kid's drink
- One DC figurine and their DC Ride


Now, *that’s* a super value for the whole family!
