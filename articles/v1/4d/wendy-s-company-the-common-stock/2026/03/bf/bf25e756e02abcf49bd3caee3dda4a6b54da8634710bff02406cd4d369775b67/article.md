# The Legacy of the Girl Scout Spirit is Strong at Wendy's

It’s no secret that Wendy's Frosty® and Girl Scout Cookies® are an iconic pairing. But for Emily Kessler, Wendy's Manager of Culinary & Innovation, the connection runs deeper than just a delicious dessert. It's part of her DNA.


Emily is a third-generation Girl Scout. Her mom was her Troop Leader when she was a Girl Scout herself, and now she's the Troop Leader for her seven-year-old daughter. The funny part? She didn't volunteer. At troop info night, when they asked for volunteers, her daughter took matters into her own hands, turned around, pointed directly at her mom, and that was that. Emily had been officially "voluntold!"

#### **The Power of Girl Scouts Across Generations**


It wasn't until the first Thin MintsTM Frosty launch in 2025 that Emily reconnected with how formative these experiences had been. Visiting the Girl Scouts of the USA headquarters sparked something profound. Looking back, she realized how much she learned there: CPR certification, life skills, and memories she still talks about decades later. Now, as a troop leader, she gets to create those same moments for the next generation, which feels pretty special.


What's particularly magical is how Emily brings her culinary expertise into her role. She's used her background at Wendy's to teach the girls how to properly "taste" food, analyzing smell, flavor, and texture of the cookies. She's even had them invent and name their own cookies to learn about entrepreneurship.


#### **Why Thin Mints? Why Now?**


This year, Wendy’s brought back the fan favorite Thin Mints Frosty and introduced something even more exciting: the all-new Thin Mints® Frosty Fusion® combining Wendy's Vanilla Frosty with a rich, minty cookie crumble sauce and Girl Scout Thin Mints cookie crumble, delivering a delicious chocolatey, minty experience with every creamy spoonful.


For Thin Mints Frosty Swirl fans, don’t worry, it’s back too! The returning favorite features Wendy's Vanilla Frosty swirled and topped with a rich, minty cookie crumble sauce inspired by Girl Scout Thin Mints.


Fans might ask: why revisit Thin Mints Frosty instead of launching something new? The answer is straightforward. Thin Mints is America's #1 Girl Scout Cookie flavor. In a world where there's always something new and shiny, there's real power in revisiting something that people genuinely love.


#### **More Than Just a Frozen Treat**


With every spoonful of a Thin Mints Frosty (Fusion or Swirl), we hope fans feel the joy that went into its creation, Girl Scouts feel celebrated, and parents reconnect with their own Girl Scout memories, or feel inspired to show up for the Girl Scout in their life.


This Girl Scout Cookie season, Wendy’s is also offering ways to support local Girl Scout troops. As a GSUSA national boothing partner, more than 3,600 Wendy’s restaurants nationwide are ready to host Girl Scouts throughout cookie season to help local troops reach their entrepreneurial goals.
