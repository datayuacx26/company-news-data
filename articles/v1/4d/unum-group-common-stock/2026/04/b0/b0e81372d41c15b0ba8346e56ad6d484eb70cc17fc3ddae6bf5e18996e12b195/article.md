### Unum Group Declares Quarterly Dividend of $0.460 Per Share of Its Common Stock


April 9, 2026


CHATTANOOGA, Tenn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Effective April 9, 2026, the Unum Group (NYSE: UNM) Board of Directors declared a quarterly dividend of $0.460 per share on its common stock to be paid May 15, 2026, to stockholders of record as of April 24, 2026.
