### Unum Group to Release Second Quarter 2026 Results and Host Conference Call


July 13, 2026


CHATTANOOGA, Tenn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Unum Group (NYSE: UNM) will release its second quarter 2026 results on July 28, 2026, at approximately 4:15 p.m. ET. The earnings release and financial supplement will be available in the investors section of the company’s[website](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Funumgroup.com&esheet=54569409&newsitemid=20260713103536&lan=en-US&anchor=website&index=1&md5=7ff91d471758e9347a781a8348c7dfe0) , which can be directly accessed at[https://investors.unum.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.unum.com%2F&esheet=54569409&newsitemid=20260713103536&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.unum.com&index=2&md5=b8466db2d03d8fa26b97f308cfcc803f) .


Members of Unum Group’s senior management will host a conference call on July 29, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. ET to discuss second quarter results. Topics may include forward-looking information, such as guidance on future results or trends in operations, as well as other material information. Interested parties are invited to listen and participate in the question-and-answer segment.

**About Unum Group**
