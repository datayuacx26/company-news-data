### Unum Group Declares Quarterly Dividend of $0.505 Per Share of its Common Stock


July 9, 2026


CHATTANOOGA, Tenn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Effective July 9, 2026, the Unum Group (NYSE: UNM) Board of Directors declared a quarterly dividend of $0.505 per share on its common stock to be paid August 14, 2026, to stockholders of record as of July 24, 2026.
