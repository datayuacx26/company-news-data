### Unum Group’s Board of Directors Votes to Increase Common Stock Dividend


May 21, 2026


CHATTANOOGA, Tenn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Unum Group (NYSE: UNM) announced today that its board of directors has authorized an increase of approximately 10 percent in the quarterly dividend paid on its common stock. The new rate of 50.5 cents per common share, or $2.02 per share on an annual basis, will be effective with the dividend expected to be paid in the third quarter of 2026.
