Madison Square Garden Entertainment Corp. The call will also be available via webcast at[investor.msgentertainment.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.msgentertainment.com%2Foverview%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54585188&newsitemid=20260806470568&lan=en-US&anchor=investor.msgentertainment.com&index=2&md5=f0aa9a099730ad95fa08abde98cf0e17) under the heading “Events.”

**About Madison Square Garden Entertainment Corp.**


Madison Square Garden Entertainment Corp. (MSG Entertainment) is a leader in live entertainment, delivering unforgettable experiences while forging deep connections with diverse and passionate audiences. The Company’s portfolio includes a collection of world-renowned venues – New York’s Madison Square Garden, Infosys Theater at Madison Square Garden, Radio City Music Hall, and Beacon Theatre; and The Chicago Theatre – that showcase a broad array of sporting events, concerts, family shows, and special events for millions of guests annually. In addition, the Company features the original production, the *Christmas Spectacular Starring the Radio City Rockettes* , which has been a holiday tradition for more than 90 years. More information is available at[www.msgentertainment.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.msgentertainment.com&esheet=54585188&newsitemid=20260806470568&lan=en-US&anchor=www.msgentertainment.com&index=3&md5=3b096aded97a64d7f6f5ef49d32c5d0b) .
