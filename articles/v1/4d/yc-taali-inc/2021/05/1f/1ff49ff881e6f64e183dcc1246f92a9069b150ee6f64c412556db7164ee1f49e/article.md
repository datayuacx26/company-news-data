# Taali Makhana - The ingredients of our success

## It’s true what they say


what’s on the inside is what matters. Today, we want to delve into what’s inside our delicious, guilt-free snacks and take you behind the scenes of Taali.


Let’s start with our star ingredient — Makhana. What exactly are they?


Makhana are made from the seeds of water lilies, which are mainly found in the northern regions of India. Once the seeds are harvested, they’re carefully popped and roasted to ultimately become your favorite plant-based snack!


The reason why this superfood snack is a much preferred alternative to greasy, fried snacks is because it has Ayurvedic, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties while also being rich in magnesium, iron and calcium.

## **about the flavors?**


A lot of thought has gone into the selection of our flavors because we didn’t just want to make them delicious. Nutritional benefits are a big part of why we do what we do, so let’s break down the flavors to help you see the goodness of our pops better.


## Ghee & Himalayan
Pink Salt


Salted snacks are a hit already, but we wanted to put our own spin on them, a Himalayan spin, to be precise. The result is a much loved flavor that’s delicious. This salt is sourced all the way from the Himalayan range and is the most natural salt in the world. The combination of Ghee & Himalayan pink salt with our roasted makhana is a match made in superfood heaven, promise!


## Sriracha
Spice


Sriracha is a powerful condiment that gets its spicy flavor from red chilli peppers. These peppers contain capsaicin which improves metabolism and reduces hunger, thus aiding in weight loss. But what really makes Sriracha a hit is its spicy, sweet and tangy profile. We’ve recreated the magic of Sriracha using only natural ingredients that are full of flavor and oh so delicious.


## Tikka
Masala


It’s true, we love our spices. Our mix of tikka masala is a perfect blend of turmeric, garlic, cumin, ginger, and a lot more spices that are known for their health benefits.


- Garlic lowers the risk of heart diseases.


- Cumin seeds aid in weight loss.


- Turmeric is a powerful medicinal herb.


- Ginger contains anti-inflammatory properties.


We’ve taken all these spices and turned them into a pack full of flavor and nutrition.


## Tangy
Turmeric


The benefit is in the name itself. Turmeric (popularly known as haldi) is a popular spice and medicinal herb that is rich in antioxidants; this property helps reduce the risk of many diseases. We’ve combined the goodness of turmeric with the joy of dried mango and tangy tamarind to make our vegan protein snacks high in flavor and health benefits, both!


## **Jaggery**


Made from jaggery and sea salt, this snack is not only a sweet treat to love but also a healthy fix for you!


Jaggery is our favorite desi sweetener because of its many natural benefits. Gur acts as a detox agent in the body and cleanses out toxins. It also helps prevent constipation for a better digestive system.


A natural source of sodium, Sea salt cuts down the excessive sweetness from Jaggery while giving the body a hydrating boost for an energetic day.

Feel the delight of a caramel surprise with every crunch!


What’s better and has fewer calories than caramel popcorn? That’s right, it’s us! Get your binge on with our little pops of happiness. With the best quality of makhanas slow roasted and then coated with golden hues of sweet and buttery caramel; we guarantee that this experience would definitely be your love at first bite!

Who doesn’t love chocolate around here? And who doesn’t hate the calories that come with it? Don’t worry, we’re here to solve your dilemma, with Taali’s dark chocolate!
