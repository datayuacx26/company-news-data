# Welcoming The Winters

##


Shhh... Did you hear that? It’s the Christmas Bells!


With the holidays around the corner, we cannot wait to pull out our favorite jackets and look at all the wonderful lights!With the holidays around the corner, we cannot wait to pull out our favorite jackets and look at all the wonderful lights!


Apart from munching on our Makhana while binge watching all the new shows we have missed, here are 5 things to try out this season!


## 1. **Write A Letter To Santa!:**


No, we’re not just talking to the kids, we’re talking to everyone out there! Writing a letter to Santa can be a great way of journaling. A safe space where you’re allowed to write absolutely anything your heart says!


## 2. **Decorate a Tree:**


C’mon, you knew this was coming. It’s time to get a lil’ creative, and what better way than to make your own Christmas from scratch. You can add any and all features you want, and have a little competition with your friends.


## 3. **Go For a Long Walk:**


The streets are filled with lighting and the chilly weather means you won’t break a sweat. The Ideal way to recharge, and to brace yourself for another year of madness!


##


## 4. **Host a Viewing Party!:**


The winters are always action packed, courtesy of our favorite sports! Ring up your gang, and host a viewing party lined up with your favorite dishes!


## 5. **Stay Indoors For Warm and Cozy Fun :**


Finally to all the people who don’t want to leave the comfort of their house, that’s perfectly alright! Snuggling up with a good book, or binge watching shows are great ways to spend your holidays. Some ‘Me-time’ will get you going perfectly when the holidays end.


There you have it! Get your preparations going because we are all going to dive into December now, and it’s going to be action packed!
