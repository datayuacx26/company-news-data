# Poll Everywhere 2.0 is Coming This August

The next evolution of audience engagement is almost here—and it’s everything you already love about Poll Everywhere, made faster, smarter, and easier to use. Poll Everywhere 2.0 launches this August, and while you **don’t need to do anything right now** , we wanted to give you a glimpse of what’s coming.


### **The same Poll Everywhere you love—just better.**


Poll Everywhere 2.0 builds on the features you trust, delivering **a smoother, more efficient experience** for presenters and participants alike. With improved speed, a refined design, and smart enhancements across the board, 2.0 makes it easier than ever to focus on what matters most: **engaging your audience** .


Whether you're facilitating corporate training or leading a lecture hall, Poll Everywhere 2.0 is designed to reduce friction and amplify impact.


### **What’s new in 2.0?**


- **AI-enhanced poll creation** : Generate smarter questions with AI assistance, so you can spend less time setting up and more time connecting with your audience.


- **Rebuilt PowerPoint integration** : We heard you—and we rebuilt it. 2.0 delivers a seamless experience right inside PowerPoint, so you can create, edit, and present polls without ever leaving your slides.


- **More intuitive workflows** : From easier poll editing to better organization and faster reporting, 2.0 simplifies every step of your presentation flow.


- **New for higher ed** : Full **LMS integration with Canvas** makes it simple to track participation, manage grades, and capture attendance—without ever leaving your LMS.


- **Enhanced accessibility** : Poll Everywhere 2.0 is WCAG 2.2 AA compliant, helping institutions and organizations stay ahead of future standards.


### **When can I try it?**


You’ll be able to start using Poll Everywhere 2.0 in **August 2025** . At that time, you can opt in and try out the new experience on your own terms.


⚠️ **You won’t be automatically switched to 2.0 in August.** We want to give you time to explore. That means there's no pressure--and no disruption to your current workflow.


### **What do I need to do now?**


Nothing yet.


Right now, we’re putting the final touches on Poll Everywhere 2.0 to make sure it delivers the performance, stability, and delight you expect. **There’s no early sign-up** , but we’ll let you know when it’s ready for prime time.
