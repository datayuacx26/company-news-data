# Introducing Poll Everywhere 2.0: The Future of Audience Engagement Starts Now

Poll Everywhere 2.0 is here—and it’s more than just an update. It’s a full reboot of the platform you trust, rebuilt from the ground up for speed, simplicity, and the future of interactive presentations.


Whether you're leading a class, a training, or an executive briefing, Poll Everywhere 2.0 makes it easier than ever to engage your audience, gather insights, and deliver real-time feedback—without missing a beat.


## A Fresh Start, Built for What’s Next


2.0 isn’t just a new version. It’s a new foundation.


Behind the scenes, we re-architected Poll Everywhere to ensure it’s faster to build on, easier to maintain, and more flexible for the future. That means fewer bugs, faster fixes, and smarter updates—so we can respond to your needs more quickly than ever before.


We also built AI directly into the heart of the platform, not as an add-on, but as a seamless part of the experience. With AI-enhanced poll creation, you can go from idea to interaction in seconds. Ask better questions, get better insights, and spend less time on setup and more time on impact.


## Everything You Love, Just Better


Poll Everywhere 2.0 keeps everything you already love—and makes it better:


-


**Faster, smarter poll creation** with built-in AI that helps you craft engaging, targeted questions in real time.


-


**PowerPoint-native experience** , with end-to-end functionality right inside your slide deck. No switching tabs. No interruptions.


-


**Improved reporting tools** that give you instant insights into audience participation and poll results.


-


**Smoother, more reliable performance** with a simplified interface and modern infrastructure.


-


**Frictionless migration** —your data, your polls, your slideware—everything comes with you when you’re ready to switch.


For new users, Poll Everywhere 2.0 is intuitive from the start. For longtime users, it’s a familiar platform with a fresh feel. Either way, it’s designed to make audience engagement effortless.


## Powerful New Features


Poll Everywhere 2.0 brings a host of enhancements that make it easier to create, present, and analyze your interactive content:


-


**AI-Enhanced Poll Creation**
Let AI do the heavy lifting. Generate polls faster, optimize question wording, and get live suggestions based on audience responses—so you can focus on what matters most: meaningful interaction.


-


**PowerPoint Integration**
Create, edit, and present polls directly in PowerPoint. Everything happens in one place, keeping your workflow smooth and your audience focused.


-


**Dynamic Poll Types**
Choose from multiple-choice, word clouds, Q&A, competitions, and more—with visualizations like bar, donut, radar, and likert charts to match your message and style.


-


**Canvas LMS Integration** (Higher Ed)
Run polls, track participation, and sync grades—all from inside Canvas. One-click course sync, in-assignment polling, and participation-based grading make student engagement seamless.


-


**Real-Time Reporting**
Pull engagement and response data in seconds. Understand what landed, what didn’t, and where to go next.


-


**Effortless Migration**
When you're ready, we'll migrate your content—polls, results, and slideware. Your past work stays intact. No rebuilding, no downtime.


## Security That Never Sleeps


Poll Everywhere 2.0 was built with modern security and accessibility standards from day one. It’s **2.2 AA compliant** , **PCI DSS certified** , and protected with **AES 256-bit encryption** , **TLS 1.2** , and **secure cloud storage** on U.S.-based servers.


We also operate a continuous bug bounty program, giving vetted security researchers 24/7 access to test our systems—so you can trust your data is protected at every step.


Coming soon: **SOC 2 Type 2** , **GDPR/CCPA** , and **ISO certifications** , further reinforcing our commitment to security, privacy, and compliance.
