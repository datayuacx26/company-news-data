# Two-Way Lightspeed CRM Sync Is Live

AI summary


Two-way sync with Lightspeed CRM is live. Leads, customers and sales activity move in both directions, so the CRM remains the system of record and your team keeps working where it already works.


A new tool that asks a sales team to abandon the CRM they already live in does not get adopted. It gets ignored, and the data ends up in two places that disagree.


So the sync goes both ways.


##


What moves


Leads, customers and sales activity now sync in both directions between Ekho and[Lightspeed CRM](https://www.ekho.com/integration/lightspeed-crm) . A lead the Sales Agent captures appears in the CRM. Work your team does in the CRM comes back the other way. Neither side is a dead end.


##


Your CRM stays the system of record


This is the point of the release. Ekho does not ask to become the place your team logs its work. The CRM keeps that job, and your reps keep working where they already work. What changes is that the conversations the Sales Agent has at eleven at night are in the CRM the next morning, attributed and complete, rather than sitting in a separate system somebody has to remember to check.


##


Why two-way matters more than it sounds


One-way sync creates a quiet reconciliation problem. A rep updates a customer in the CRM, the other system never hears about it, and within a month the two disagree about who owns the relationship. Two-way sync removes that, so there is one answer to who a customer is and what has happened with them.


##


Where to find it


The integration is available now. See the[Lightspeed CRM integration page](https://www.ekho.com/integration/lightspeed-crm) , or speak to your Ekho contact to switch it on for your group.
