# Welcoming Eugenio Garza Garcia, Forward Deployed Engineer

AI summary


Eugenio Garza Garcia joins as a Forward Deployed Engineer. He led the sports vertical at Polymarket and owned settlements and disputes across most of the platform's volume, after two and a half years at BCG.


Eugenio Garza Garcia joins Ekho as a Forward Deployed Engineer.


##


Where Eugenio comes from


He comes from Polymarket, where he led the sports vertical and owned settlements and disputes across the majority of the platform's volume. Settlement is the part of that business where a technical decision and a commercial consequence are the same decision, and it is unforgiving of a wrong call.


Before Polymarket he spent two and a half years as a consultant at BCG.


##


What Eugenio will do here


Eugenio embeds directly with customers as they onboard. He sits inside the operation, maps how it actually runs rather than how it is described, and shapes the platform to fit it from day one.


##


Why it matters here


A Forward Deployed Engineer needs two things that rarely arrive together: enough commercial fluency to understand why a dealer does something the apparently inefficient way, and enough technical depth to diagnose the system underneath while standing in the room.


That combination is the whole brief, and it is what Eugenio brings.
