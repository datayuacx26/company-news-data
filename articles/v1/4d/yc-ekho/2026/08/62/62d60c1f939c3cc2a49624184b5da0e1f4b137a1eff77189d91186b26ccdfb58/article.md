# Welcoming Ethan Poortenga, Founding Product Manager

AI summary


Ethan Poortenga joins as founding Product Manager. He was the founding PM at Hang, growing it from a handful of customers to hundreds, and led both product and design there.


Ethan Poortenga joins Ekho as our founding Product Manager.


##


Where Ethan comes from


He was the founding PM at Hang, a restaurant loyalty and customer engagement platform. He started with a handful of customers and a half-built product, and grew it to hundreds of customers. He did the design work himself along the way, and eventually led both product and design.


Before Hang he was a product manager at Amazon.


##


What Ethan will do here


Ethan leads the product teams behind the Sales Agent and the Transaction Engine, which between them cover the whole path from a buyer's first question to a titled and registered vehicle.


##


Why it matters here


A founding PM role is not the same job as a PM role at a company that already has a product organisation. The work is deciding what to build with incomplete information, then doing whichever part of it nobody else is doing that week.


Ethan has done that from a half-built product to hundreds of customers once already.
