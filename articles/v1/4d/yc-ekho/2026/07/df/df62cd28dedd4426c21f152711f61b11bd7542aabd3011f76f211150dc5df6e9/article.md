# The Sales Agent Speaks to the Brands You Actually Carry

AI summary


Each rooftop records the brands and categories it carries, and the Sales Agent answers from that. A buyer asking about a brand you do not sell gets a straight answer and a route to what you do sell.


A buyer almost never opens with a model number. They open with a brand, or a category, or a use case.


Until now the agent answered those questions in general terms. It now answers them from what the rooftop in front of the buyer actually carries.


##


What each rooftop sells is now something you record


Every rooftop holds its own list of the brands and categories it sells and services. A group with different franchises at different stores describes each one once, and each store's agent answers accordingly.


##


A straight answer beats a hopeful one


Asked about a brand a store does not carry, the agent says so and points the buyer at the closest thing it does carry. That is a better conversation than an enthusiastic non-answer, and it is a far better one than a buyer arriving to find the thing they asked about was never on the floor.


##


Where to find it


Admin Portal, Settings, Account, then Business Details.
