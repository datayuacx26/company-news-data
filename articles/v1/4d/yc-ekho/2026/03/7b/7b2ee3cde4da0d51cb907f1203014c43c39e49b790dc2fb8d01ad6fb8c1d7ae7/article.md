That framing matters. Ekho wasn't recognized for being the newest or the most feature-rich. We were recognized because dealers using Ekho are seeing real results: more leads, more completed sales, less manual work, and fewer tools to manage.


Being the top-ranked dealer technology platform on this list validates what we hear from our dealer partners every day — that Ekho is helping them grow their businesses without growing headcount.


##


Introducing Ekho AI Agents — Starting with Sales Agent


One of our most exciting recent launches is the **Ekho Sales Agent** (Business Development Center) — an AI-powered assistant that handles dealer website inquiries, SMS conversations, and lead qualification around the clock.


Most dealerships lose leads outside of business hours. Calls go to voicemail. Web forms sit untouched overnight. By the time staff follow up, the buyer has already moved on.


Ekho Sales Agent changes that. It engages every inbound inquiry instantly — day or night — with intelligent, context-aware conversations that qualify leads, answer product questions, and route high-intent buyers to the right salesperson. It works across your website, SMS, and chat channels, so no lead falls through the cracks.


For dealers, this means:


- 24/7 lead engagement without hiring additional BDC staff
- Faster response times that convert more browsers into buyers
- Consistent, professional follow-up on every inquiry
- More qualified leads handed off to your sales team


Early adopters are already seeing strong results, and we're rolling out Sales Agent to dealers nationwide as a standalone product — meaning any dealer can start using it immediately, even without the full Ekho platform.

##


50-State Transaction Engine


At the core of Ekho is the industry's most comprehensive vehicle transaction engine — purpose-built to power compliant, end-to-end vehicle sales across all 50 states.


Selling vehicles online isn't like selling merchandise. Every state has different rules for titling, registration, tax remittance, insurance verification, and franchise compliance. Most dealers either avoid out-of-state sales entirely or piece together manual workflows that are slow, error-prone, and hard to scale.


Ekho's transaction engine automates the entire process:


- **Financing and desking** with real-time lender integrations
- **Titling and registration** across all 50 states
- **Tax calculation and remittance** with jurisdiction-level accuracy
- **Insurance verification** and compliance checks
- **Fraud detection** and KYC workflows
- **Digital signature collection** and sales documentation
- **F&I product presentation** that preserves dealer margin


This is what separates Ekho from traditional website providers and lead-generation tools. We don't just help dealers collect leads — we help them close deals, compliantly, from anywhere in the country.


##


The Unified Dealer Platform


The average dealership runs on a patchwork of disconnected tools — one for websites, another for CRM, another for desking, another for reporting, and more. Each tool has its own login, its own data silo, and its own monthly bill. The result is wasted time, lost data, and frustrated staff.


Ekho replaces that fragmented stack with a single, unified platform that brings everything together:


- **CRM** — manage your leads, customers, and pipeline in one place, with AI-powered prioritization and follow-up
- **Scheduling** — let buyers book test rides, service appointments, and consultations directly from your website
- **Reporting and analytics** — real-time visibility into sales performance, lead sources, conversion rates, and revenue
- **Inventory management** — centralized control over your units, pricing, and merchandising across all channels
- **Communication hub** — unified inbox for email, SMS, chat, and Sales Agent conversations


The goal is simple: reduce the number of tools a dealer needs to run their business while giving them better data, better workflows, and better outcomes than any individual point solution could deliver.
