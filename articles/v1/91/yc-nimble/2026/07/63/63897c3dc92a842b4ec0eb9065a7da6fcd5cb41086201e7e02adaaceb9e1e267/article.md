# 6 Reasons Your District Needs an Applicant Tracking System

Has your district been managing teacher hiring the same way for the past twenty years—posting jobs on your website, emailing applications back and forth, printing resumes and passing folders between principals? Many districts still operate this way, relying on familiar processes. But the hiring landscape has changed a lot in the past couple of decades. To be competitive today, your hiring processes should catch up.


An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software that manages the entire hiring process from a single digital platform—job postings, applications, candidate communications, interview scheduling, and hiring decisions all in one place. Here's why your district should have one.


**1. It keeps everything organized and accessible.** When a candidate applies, their information is captured and organized automatically. Everyone involved in the hiring decision can access the same information in real time, and a complete record of every interaction is preserved—accessible with just a few keystrokes. No misplaced folders. No digging through email threads.


**2. It puts your job postings in front of more candidates.** Jobs posted through an ATS can be pushed to statewide, national, and local job boards, significantly increasing your visibility. And when candidates search for job openings in your area, an ATS helps make sure your postings show up in those results. The more places your jobs appear, the stronger your candidate pool.


**3. It's what candidates expect.** Today's candidates expect to fill out a digital form, upload their documents, and receive automatic confirmation that their application was received. When they encounter a process that requires downloading a PDF, filling it out manually, scanning it, and emailing it to an HR address—many of them won't bother. In a market where there aren't enough teachers to fill all the vacancies, a cumbersome application process is a competitive disadvantage.


**4. It reflects well on your district.** Teachers use sophisticated systems every day to record grades, develop lesson plans, and engage students. If their first interaction with your district is an outdated hiring process, they may sense a poor fit and keep looking. A smooth, modern application experience signals that your district is organized and values people's time.


**5. It saves more time than you might think.** You may be so accustomed to explaining directions to candidates, running email searches, and printing resumes that you don't notice how much time the manual process consumes. Even if your district only hires for three or four positions a year, eight to ten administrative hours per hire adds up to nearly a week of staff time—time that could be better spent on onboarding, professional development, or other priorities. An ATS automates the repetitive parts, keeping candidates informed and stakeholders updated without manual reminders or back-and-forth calls.


**6. Your hiring history becomes a lasting asset.** Every candidate who has ever applied to your district is stored, searchable, and retrievable indefinitely. When a position reopens mid-year, you can immediately pull up everyone who applied for that same role the previous spring. Need historical data for the superintendent? Need to report how many positions were posted last year? It's all in one place, forever.


For districts that have managed hiring close to the vest for a long time, moving to a new system can feel like giving something up. In reality, a well-implemented ATS gives you more visibility into your process, not less. And with the hiring season already underway, there's no better time to make the shift. Every week your district spends in the peak hiring window with a cumbersome process is a week that strong candidates look elsewhere. An ATS is no longer a luxury—it's the standard. The districts finishing this spring fully staffed will be the ones that made it easiest for great teachers to say yes.
