# Viking Takes Delivery of Newest Egypt Ship

# Viking Takes Delivery of Newest Egypt Ship


August 07, 2026 8:00 am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_a43237d33bf8e556ee67e29fe3e07ed9/vikingcruises/news/2026-08-07_Viking_Takes_Delivery_of_Newest_Egypt_241.pdf)


*The New Viking Ptah Joins Award-Winning Sister Ships on the Nile River*


LOS ANGELES--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Viking® ([www.viking.com](http://www.viking.com/) ) (NYSE: VIK) today announced it has taken delivery of the company’s newest river ship in Egypt, the[Viking Ptah](https://www.vikingrivercruises.com/ships/egypt/viking-ptah.html) . The delivery ceremony for the *Viking Ptah* took place at Massara shipyard in Cairo, Egypt.


This press release features multimedia. View the full release here:[https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260807294864/en/](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260807294864/en/)


Viking today announced the delivery of its newest ship for the Nile River, the Viking Ptah. The delivery ceremony took place at Massara shipyard in Cairo, Egypt. Pictured here, a sister ship in Viking’s Egypt fleet. For more information, visit www.viking.com.


“We are delighted to welcome the *Viking Ptah* to our growing fleet on the Nile River,” said Leah Talactac, President and CEO of Viking. “Egypt continues to inspire curious travelers from around the world, and with the addition of the *Viking Ptah* , we are pleased to offer even more guests the opportunity to explore this extraordinary destination in the comfort of a Viking vessel. Purpose-built for the Nile, our elegant sister ships provide an unmatched way to experience Egypt’s rich history and cultural treasures.”


**The *Viking Ptah* & Viking’s Growing Fleet**


Hosting 82 guests in 41 staterooms, the *Viking Ptah* is built specifically to navigate the Nile River on Viking’s popular 12-day[Pharaohs & Pyramids](https://www.vikingrivercruises.com/cruise-destinations/egypt/pharaohs-pyramids/2027-cairo-cairo/index.html) itinerary. Inspired by Viking’s award-winning river and ocean ships, the *Viking Ptah* and her identical sister ships ** feature the elegant Scandinavian design for which Viking is known, including a distinctive square bow and an indoor/outdoor Aquavit Terrace.


Viking remains focused on well-defined, long-term growth plans as part of a strategy to continue leading the way in experiential travel. Based on Viking’s committed orderbook, the company expects to take delivery of 20 additional river ships by 2028, nine additional ocean ships by 2031 and two additional expedition ships by 2031. With these orders, Viking will have 114 river ships in 2028 and 26 ocean and expedition ships in 2031.
