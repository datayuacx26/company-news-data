# Viking Schedules Conference Call on Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results


August 05, 2026 4:15 pm EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_265a027f33044dbd0ff9550d36d20fa2/vikingcruises/news/2026-08-05_Viking_Schedules_Conference_Call_on_Second_239.pdf)


LOS ANGELES--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Viking Holdings Ltd (“Viking” or the “Company”) ([NYSE:](http://www.viking.com/) VIK) has scheduled a conference call at 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time on Wednesday, August 19, 2026, to discuss the Company’s second quarter 2026 financial results which will be released before the stock market opens. The call will be simultaneously webcast on Viking’s investor relations website,[https://ir.viking.com](https://ir.viking.com/) . A replay of the webcast will remain available for 30 days following the call.


**About Viking**


Viking (NYSE: VIK) is a global leader in experiential travel with a fleet of more than 100 ships, exploring 21 rivers, five oceans and all seven continents. Designed for curious travelers with interests in science, history, culture and cuisine, Executive Chairman Torstein Hagen often says Viking offers experiences For The Thinking Person™. For additional information, visit[www.viking.com](http://www.viking.com/) .
