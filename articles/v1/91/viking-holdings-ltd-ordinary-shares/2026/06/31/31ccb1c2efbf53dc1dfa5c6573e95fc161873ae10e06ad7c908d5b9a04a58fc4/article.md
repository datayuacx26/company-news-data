# Viking Takes Delivery of Newest River Ships in Europe

# Viking Takes Delivery of Newest River Ships in Europe


June 11, 2026 4:30 pm EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_6dd2bd05272b2c4e9bb81907cff6a52f/vikingcruises/news/2026-06-11_Viking_Takes_Delivery_of_Newest_River_Ships_in_236.pdf)


*The Viking Annar and the Viking Fjolvar Join Award-Winning Fleet of Viking Longships*


LOS ANGELES--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Viking® ([www.viking.com](http://www.viking.com/) ) (NYSE: VIK) today announced it has taken delivery of its newest river ships, the[Viking Annar](https://www.vikingrivercruises.com/ships/longships/viking-annar.html?msockid=3b2cdb35c54764201318cd7bc46a65d6) and the[Viking Fjolvar](https://www.vikingrivercruises.com/ships/seine-ships/viking-fjolvar.html?msockid=3b2cdb35c54764201318cd7bc46a65d6) . Both vessels were built at Meyer's Neptun Werft shipyard in Rostock-Warnemünde, Germany. The *Viking Annar* will sail Viking's itineraries on the Rhine, Main and Danube rivers, while the *Viking Fjolvar* will sail itineraries on the Seine River.

Viking today announced it has taken delivery of its newest river ships, the Viking Annar and the Viking Fjolvar. The Viking Annar will sail Viking's itineraries on the Rhine, Main and Danube rivers, while the Viking Fjolvar will sail itineraries on the Seine River. Pictured here, a sister ship in the Viking Longship fleet on the Rhine River in Cologne, Germany. For more information, visit www.viking.com.


“We are proud to welcome the *Viking Annar* and the *Viking Fjolvar* to our growing fleet of award-winning river ships,” said Leah Talactac, President and CEO of Viking. “Exploring by river offers an unmatched way to discover a destination’s history, culture and local life, and these new vessels allow even more curious travelers to experience Europe in Viking comfort.”


**The *Viking Annar***


Hosting 190 guests in 95 staterooms, the *Viking Annar* is built specifically to navigate the Rhine, Main and Danube rivers. As a Viking Longship, the vessel features elegant Scandinavian design and the industry-leading innovations for which Viking is known, such as a patented square bow that allows the ship to have three full decks and an indoor/outdoor Aquavit Terrace, as well as asymmetric corridors that enable Viking to offer true two-room suites.


The *Viking Annar* will sail itineraries that include[Rhine Getaway](https://www.vikingrivercruises.com/cruise-destinations/europe/rhine-getaway/2026-amsterdam-basel/index.html?cid=SEM|BNG|999|c_590168145_kwd-77172305883023:loc-190_77172208940197&utm_id=sem:VRC-US-BNG-Brand-Core-Itineraries-Exact&utm_content=VRC-US-BNG-Brand-Core-Itineraries-Exact&utm_term=rhine%20getaway%20viking&gclid=e474ec5059c0159249e8867004e5e408&gclsrc=3p.ds&msclkid=e474ec5059c0159249e8867004e5e408) ,[Grand European Tour](https://www.vikingrivercruises.com/cruise-destinations/europe/grand-european-tour/2023-amsterdam-budapest/index.html?msockid=3b2cdb35c54764201318cd7bc46a65d6) ,[Passage to Eastern Europe](https://www.vikingrivercruises.com/cruise-destinations/europe/passage-eastern-europe/2026-bucharest-budapest/index.html) ,[European Sojourn](https://www.vikingrivercruises.com/cruise-destinations/europe/european-sojourn/2026-amsterdam-bucharest/index.html) and[Christmas on the Rhine](https://www.vikingrivercruises.com/cruise-destinations/europe/christmas-on-the-rhine/2026-amsterdam-basel/index.html) .


**The *Viking Fjolvar***


Hosting 168 guests in 84 staterooms, the *Viking Fjolvar* is built specifically to navigate the Seine River and dock at Viking’s exclusive docking location in the heart of Paris. The vessel features the same design elements as Viking Longships and will sail roundtrip from Paris on Seine River itineraries that include[Paris & the Heart of Normandy](https://www.vikingrivercruises.com/cruise-destinations/europe/paris-heart-normandy/2026-paris-paris/index.html) and the new[Christmastime in Paris & Normandy](https://www.vikingrivercruises.com/cruise-destinations/europe/christmastime-in-paris-and-normandy/2026-paris-paris/index.html) .


**Viking’s Growing Fleet**


Viking remains focused on its well-defined, long-term growth plans and committed to its strategy, continuing to be a global leader in experiential travel. Based on Viking’s committed orderbook, the company expects to take delivery of 22 additional river ships by 2028, nine additional ocean ships by 2031 and two additional expedition ships by 2031. With these orders, Viking will have 114 river ships in 2028 and 26 ocean and expedition ships in 2031.
