# Mayfair Shopping Center & Vintage Marketplace

Mayfair Shopping Center, a ~220K SF center located along Jericho Turnpike on Long Island, is a dominant daily-needs destination anchored by Lidl US, Planet Fitness, and PGA TOUR Superstore and complemented by a strong mix of national tenants including SEPHORA, J.Crew Factory, and more. The property generates approximately 2.4M annual visits and further strengthens our presence on Long Island, bringing our portfolio to 14 centers totaling more than 1.6M SF.


Mayfair is also a milestone transaction for Brixmor, as it marks the first time the Company has used partnership units as currency for a portion of the acquisition price.


Continuing our growth strategy in core markets where we have a large presence, we’ve expanded our portfolio with the acquisitions of Mayfair Shopping Center in Long Island, NY, and Vintage Marketplace in Houston, TX - two grocery-anchored neighborhood centers serving affluent, high-income trade areas.


Vintage Marketplace, a ~72K SF Whole Foods Market-anchored center in northwest Houston, serves a dense and growing suburban trade area with a curated mix of leading food and service tenants. The acquisition expands our Houston footprint to 27 centers totaling more than 3.9M SF.


These acquisitions reinforce our disciplined approach to investing in well-located, necessity-based retail assets in high-growth markets where we have a large presence.
