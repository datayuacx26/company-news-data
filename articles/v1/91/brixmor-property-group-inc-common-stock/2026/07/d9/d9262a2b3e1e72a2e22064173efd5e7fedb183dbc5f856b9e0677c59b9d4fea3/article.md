# Soccer Celebrations in Orlando, FL

This summer, Pointe Orlando became more than a destination. It became a gathering place for the community.


Through a series of fan experiences and match-day celebrations, we partnered with Live! at Pointe Orlando, Orlando City Soccer Club and Visit Orlando to bring residents and visitors together around the excitement of the FIFA World Cup.


As fans from around the globe gathered to celebrate the world's game, Pointe Orlando provided a place to connect, create memories and experience the energy that makes Orlando such a unique destination.


The impact extended beyond the events themselves. During the World Cup celebration period, traffic at Pointe Orlando increased 15% year over year, demonstrating the power of creating experiences that bring people together while supporting our tenants and driving activity across the center.


Thank you to our partners, tenants and the thousands of fans who helped make Pointe Orlando part of this global celebration.
