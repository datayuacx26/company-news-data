## Target opens at at Wynnewood Village


A major milestone at Wynnewood Village. The grand opening of our first ground up Target marks the next phase of this Dallas shopping center redevelopment, bringing a best in class anchor to a historic Oak Cliff community. Years of investment, thoughtful planning, and strong partnerships are coming together to create a modern, community focused retail destination with long term value. And this is just the beginning for Wynnewood Village’s transformation. To learn more about Wynnewood Village, visit the links below.
