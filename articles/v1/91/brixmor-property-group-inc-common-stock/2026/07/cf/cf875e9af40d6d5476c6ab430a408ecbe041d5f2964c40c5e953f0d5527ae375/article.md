CAVA is now open at Block 59 in Naperville, adding another high‑performing brand to one of the area’s most dynamic dining and entertainment destinations. Their opening strengthens a merchandising lineup that consistently drives strong traffic and attracts operators looking to scale in proven, high‑visibility locations.


At Brixmor, we’re focused on creating centers that are the center of the community where national, regional, and local concepts can thrive. Block 59 continues to deliver that formula and the addition of CAVA highlights the demand from top retailers choosing to grow within our portfolio.
