## 3rd annual toy drive


Just before Christmas, Wynnewood Village partnered with Frame House Outreach Foundation for its annual toy drive, and it was a huge success! Thanks to the generosity of our community, we were able to provide toys, bicycles, heaters, coats, shoes, clothing, food, cash, gift cards, and more to families in need. And yes… Santa made his grand entrance on a Harley again! Seeing the joy on parents’ and kids’ faces was truly heartwarming. A huge thank you to everyone who helped make this day so special.
