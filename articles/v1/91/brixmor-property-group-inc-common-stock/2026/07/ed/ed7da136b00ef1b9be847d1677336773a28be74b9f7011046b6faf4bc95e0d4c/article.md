# Celebrating Our Interns

Investing in great real estate starts with investing in great people.


This summer, our interns gained hands-on exposure to commercial real estate through meaningful work, mentorship, and real-world learning opportunities.


From property tours and executive conversations to a visit to the New York Stock Exchange, they experienced firsthand how teams across Brixmor work together to create thriving shopping centers and deliver value for our communities, retailers, and shareholders.


Thank you to our summer interns for the energy, curiosity, and fresh perspectives you brought to the team. We can't wait to see where your careers take you next.


Happy National Intern Day!
