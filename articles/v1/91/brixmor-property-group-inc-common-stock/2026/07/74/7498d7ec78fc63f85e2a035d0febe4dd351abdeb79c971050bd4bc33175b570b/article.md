# Clicks to Bricks

A major milestone in omnichannel retail is now open at Jones Square in Houston, TX.


VEVOR’s first-ever brick-and-mortar store has officially opened, marking a significant step in the brand’s expansion from digital to physical retail.


This opening reflects the continued evolution of omnichannel retail and underscores the importance of well-located, convenience-driven centers that support brand growth. Jones Square delivers the visibility, access, and trade-area strength that make physical retail a powerful complement to e-commerce success.


Congratulations to the VEVOR team on this exciting debut, and to everyone involved in bringing this new concept to life.
