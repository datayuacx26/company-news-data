# Delco Pride

A new point of pride in Delaware County.


We recently unveiled a custom DELCO sign at Pilgrim Gardens in Drexel Hill, created as a placemaking feature that celebrates the people, landmarks, and everyday moments that define this community. Each letter incorporates locally inspired details, bringing together the stories and experiences that generations of residents share.


The response has been immediate. The sign made its debut during Delco Day celebrations and has already generated strong local buzz.


More than a sign, it’s a new gathering point designed to invite people to stop, connect, and see themselves reflected in the place they call home.


Thank you to the team that brought this to life!
