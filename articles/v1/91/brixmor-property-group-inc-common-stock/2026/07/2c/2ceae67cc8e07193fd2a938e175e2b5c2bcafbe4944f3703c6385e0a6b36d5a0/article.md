# New Grocery Anchor

A rapid, strategic repositioning just delivered a stronger Bennetts Mills Plaza.


Gourmet Glatt has officially opened its 67,000‑SF supermarket in Jackson, NJ, their first location within the Brixmor portfolio, following a swift turnaround from lease signing to grand opening. When the previous grocer anchor announced its closure in early 2024, our team immediately activated: assessing market needs, analyzing shifting community trends, and touring competitive grocery formats across NJ and NY.


That work led us to partner with Gourmet Glatt, widely recognized as a leading kosher grocer in the region. The result: a high‑performing anchor that enhances the merchandising mix, reinforces this recently renovated asset, and positions Bennetts Mills Plaza for long‑term traffic growth and value creation.
