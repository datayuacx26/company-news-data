# New Concept Alert

Belk Market is now open at Preston Ridge in Frisco, TX! One of only two locations nationwide, Belk’s new smaller-format concept has opened at Preston Ridge, reinforcing the strength of this asset and Brixmor’s ability to attract innovative retailers to high-performing centers.


This strategic addition complements an already exceptional tenant mix anchored by Nordstrom Rack, Best Buy, T.J.Maxx, Ross Dress for Less, Ulta, and more, positioning Preston Ridge as a premier power center in the Dallas market.


Belk Market’s entry highlights the demand for flexible, curated retail formats and underscores Brixmor’s commitment to delivering long-term value through thoughtful merchandising and adaptability to evolving trends.
