# ICSC Local Philadelphia

Insightful conversations drive better decisions, and yesterday, ICSC Local Philadelphia delivered exactly that.


We welcomed retail real estate professionals to our flagship office at Plymouth Square Shopping Center in where CEO & President Brian Finnegan sat down with Rob McGarrigle, Senior Leasing Representative, for a candid discussion on leadership, career growth, and the trends shaping retail real estate today.


A true Philly native, Brian shared his path in the industry along with timely perspective on market dynamics, the growing role of technology, and the importance of alignment across real estate, operations, and facilities in a shifting retail landscape.


Thank you to Rob for guiding the conversation and to everyone who joined us for a morning of meaningful dialogue and valuable connection!
