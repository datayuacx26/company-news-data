# Local Legends: Arwa Yemeni Coffee

When small businesses create meaningful experiences with their customers, they do more than sell products. They become part of the community. Arwa Yemeni Coffee at Preston Ridge demonstrates the power of creating connection through culture, hospitality, and shared experiences.


Founded by Yazan Soofi, his wife Susan Shihab, his sister Nora Soofi, and her husband Faris Almatrahi, Arwa Yemeni Coffee was built around a simple idea: create a space where customers can experience the rich traditions of Yemen through specialty coffee. What began as a passion for sharing culture has grown into a destination where people gather, connect, and discover something new.


From day one, Arwa has offered more than a cup of coffee. By combining authentic Yemeni flavors, thoughtfully crafted beverages, and an inviting atmosphere, the café creates an experience that encourages conversation and community. Customers aren't just stopping in for coffee; they're exploring a culture, learning new traditions, and returning for the connections they've built along the way.


Located within Preston Ridge, Arwa Yemeni Coffee benefits from a vibrant retail environment that supports local businesses and community engagement. The center's welcoming courtyard and complementary mix of retailers create a destination where people can shop, dine, and connect. For Arwa, the courtyard has become an extension of the café itself, providing an outdoor gathering space that reinforces its role as a community hub and enhances the customer experience.


As the business has grown, the success of its Preston Ridge location has helped inform broader expansion efforts and future growth opportunities. It demonstrates how the right retail environment can help entrepreneurs strengthen customer loyalty, refine their concept, and build a foundation for long-term success.
