# Bringing Western Wear to Ann Arbor

Boot Barn has opened at Arborland Center, strengthening the retail mix and driving additional traffic to one of Ann Arbor’s most established shopping destinations.


Arborland serves as a highly visible, high‑traffic hub along the Washtenaw corridor, home to national brands like Kroger, Nordstrom Rack, HomeGoods, Marshalls, and more, making it a key asset within Brixmor’s portfolio.


Another example of Brixmor’s commitment to curating relevant, in‑demand retail for the communities we serve.
