# Local Legends: Nothing Bundt Cakes at Village at Newtown

When local entrepreneurs build deep roots within their neighborhoods, entire retail centers benefit. Nothing Bundt Cakes at Village at Newtown shows exactly how.


What began as a personal connection to a brand discovered out west became a bold entrepreneurial step, the first Nothing Bundt Cakes location in Pennsylvania. Eight years later, this bakery has grown into a true cornerstone of the center, known for its loyal following, steady foot traffic, and deep community ties.


From day one, the response was overwhelming, lines out the door, customers eager to support a business that delivers more than cake. With a mission to “bring the joy,” Nothing Bundt Cakes has built lasting relationships by turning every visit into a meaningful interaction. It’s not just a transaction, it’s a celebration, a conversation, and a connection.


Following the redevelopment of Village at Newtown, strategic merchandising and a thoughtful leasing vision created the right environment for community‑rooted operators like Nothing Bundt Cakes to thrive. When strong local and owner‑operated businesses choose our commercial retail space for lease, their success amplifies the performance of the entire center and strengthens neighborhood identity.
