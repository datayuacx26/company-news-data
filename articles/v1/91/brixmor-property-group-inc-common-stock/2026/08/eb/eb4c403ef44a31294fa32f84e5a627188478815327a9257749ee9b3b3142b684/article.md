# BRX Coffee Club

Coffee tastes better with conversation.


Each month at our Plymouth Square office in Conshohocken, employees gather for Brixmor Coffee Club, hosted by David Mickleberg, Director of Collections. The club brings colleagues together over a unique coffee selection and conversations that simply couldn't happen over email.


This month's featured roast was Haitian Blue Pine, a rare specialty coffee from Haiti known for its exceptional quality and distinctive flavor.


The coffee may change each month, but the goal stays the same: bringing people together, one conversation at a time.
