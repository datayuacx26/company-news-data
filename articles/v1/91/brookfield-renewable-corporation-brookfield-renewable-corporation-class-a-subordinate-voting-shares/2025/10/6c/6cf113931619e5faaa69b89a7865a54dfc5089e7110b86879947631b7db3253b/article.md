# Brookfield acquires additional 300 MW solar project from SunEnergy1


[News and press releases](https://www.brookfieldrenewablena.com/news-press-releases/)


Brookfield acquires additional 300 MW solar project from SunEnergy1


- *The project is located in the PJM power market, where there is accelerating corporate demand for energy driven by data center buildout*
- *In November of 2024, Brookfield purchased an initial 510 MW portfolio of projects from SunEnergy1, with an option to acquire an additional 330 MW, and this acquisition expands this framework*
- *Leverages the combined capabilities of SunEnergy1’s market leading vertically integrated development and construction capabilities and Brookfield’s commercial contracting competencies and access to capital to deliver new clean power capacity to meet the growing needs of corporate customers*


NEW YORK, NY and BETHEL, NC – October 9, 2025 – SunEnergy1 and Brookfield, announce an agreement to acquire and construct the Cherry solar project, a 300 MW utility scale solar facility in North Carolina.


The transaction is an expansion of the existing framework announced in November 2024 under which Brookfield acquired a 510 MW solar portfolio and received exclusivity over an incremental 330 MW of projects. The Cherry solar project will be built by SunEnergy1 pursuant to a turn-key engineering, procurement and construction contract, providing strong visibility on costs and will serve growing corporate demand for power driven by data center load growth in PJM.


Stephen Gallagher, CEO of Brookfield Renewable North America, said, “Since we signed our initial transaction with SunEnergy1 last year we’ve been impressed with their flexibility, high quality work and ability to get things done. We’re delighted to be building on this relationship in a key market for our corporate customers.”


Kenny Habul, President & CEO of SunEnergy1, said, “As we continue to execute on the active construction of the projects under the initial framework agreement, we are delighted to expand the portfolio of projects from our existing interconnect pipeline that we are selling and constructing for the world-class team at Brookfield to an aggregate of over 1,100 MW.”


### About SunEnergy1


SunEnergy1 is a North Carolina based solar EPC/developer with over 50 utility scale projects built and over 5,000 MW developed in PJM. For over a decade, SunEnergy1 has established a track record of reliably delivering large scale solar projects. SunEnergy1 is vertically integrated and controls all stages of solar development and construction in-house. With the addition of its in-house substation and transmission group, SunEnergy1 now controls all aspects of EPC from design to commissioning.


### About Brookfield Asset Management


Brookfield Asset Management Ltd. is a leading global alternative asset manager, headquartered in New York, with over $1 trillion of assets under management across infrastructure, renewable power and transition, private equity, real estate, and credit. We invest client capital for the long-term with a focus on real assets and essential service businesses that form the backbone of the global economy. We offer a range of alternative investment products to investors around the world — including public and private pension plans, endowments and foundations, sovereign wealth funds, financial institutions, insurance companies and private wealth investors. We draw on Brookfield’s heritage as an owner and operator to invest for value and generate strong returns for our clients, across economic cycles.


Brookfield operates Brookfield Renewable Partners, one of the world’s largest publicly traded platforms for renewable power and sustainable solutions. Our renewable power portfolio consists of hydroelectric, wind, utility-scale solar and storage facilities and our sustainable solutions assets include our investment in a leading global nuclear services business and a portfolio of investments in carbon capture and storage capacity, agricultural renewable natural gas, materials recycling and eFuels manufacturing capacity, among others.
