Kazia Therapeutics Limited (NASDAQ: KZIA; ASX: KZA), an oncology-focused drug development company, today announced that final data from its phase II study of paxalisib in patients with newly diagnosed glioblastoma will be the subject of a poster presentation at the upcoming Annual Meeting of the American Society for Clinical Oncology (ASCO).


The ASCO Annual Meeting will take place virtually and in person from 3-7 June 2022


and will be held in Chicago, IL.


Abstracts from the meeting are expected to be released from 5pm, ET


, on 26 May 2022


, and the poster will be made available by ASCO and via the Kazia website shortly after presentation.


POSTER PRESENTATION


**Title:** Paxalisib in patients with newly diagnosed glioblastoma with unmethylated MGMT promoter status: Final phase 2 study results.
**Date and Time:** Sunday, 5 June 2022


– 8am


– 11am, CDT


**Session:** Central Nervous System Tumors
**Abstract ID:** 2047
