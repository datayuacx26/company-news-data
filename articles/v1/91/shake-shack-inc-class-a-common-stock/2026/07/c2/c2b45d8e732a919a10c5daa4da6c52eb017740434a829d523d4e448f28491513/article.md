# Shake Shack Heads to the Clubhouse with New Pimento Cheese Menu

We’re bringing a taste of the clubhouse to Shacks nationwide with a new menu inspired by the iconic Southern staple: **pimento cheese** , the “caviar of the South.”


Available nationwide on 3/31 for a limited time, the Clubhouse Pimento Cheese menu puts Shake Shack’s spin on the beloved spread. Inspired by the flavors that have become synonymous with golf’s most famous week, we reimagined traditional pimento cheese by swapping mayo and red peppers for our signature ShackSauce and chopped cherry peppers, creating a rich, creamy spread with just the right kick.


## What is on the Shake Shack Clubhouse Pimento Menu?


### Clubhouse Pimento Cheese Burger


A made-to-order quarter-pound* per patty of 100% Angus beef topped with creamy pimento cheese spread made with aged cheddar and spicy peppers. Served with lettuce, tomato and ShackSauce on a toasted potato bun.


**Approximate weight per patty is 4oz before cooking*


### Clubhouse Pimento Cheese Chicken Sandwich


Antibiotic-free, hand-breaded white-meat chicken breast topped with creamy pimento cheese spread made with aged cheddar and spicy peppers. Served with shredded lettuce, pickles and ShackSauce on a toasted potato bun.
