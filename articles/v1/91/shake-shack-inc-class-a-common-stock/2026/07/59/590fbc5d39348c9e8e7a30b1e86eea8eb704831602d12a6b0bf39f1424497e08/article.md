# Shake Shack Celebrates National Rescue Dog Day with Tails That Teach

This National Rescue Dog Day, Shake Shack is celebrating with a giveback that supports dogs and kids alike. On May 20, 2026, we’re donating $1 from every Hot Dog sold* to Tails That Teach, a nonprofit helping promote pet adoption, kindness, and early childhood education.


## **What is National Rescue Dog Day?**


National Rescue Dog Day brings awareness to the need for help for the countless dogs in shelters waiting to be saved.


## **What is Tails that Teach?**


Tails That Teach is a nonprofit organization dedicated to inspiring kindness, empathy, and responsibility among children through the powerful connection between children and animals. They partner with humane societies, rescues, vet clinics, pediatric clinics and community organizations by donating children’s books at no cost for use in humane education programs, pet adoption packets, and school and literacy initiatives.


*Only at company-operated Shacks. Does not include airports, stadiums, and roadways.
