# Shake Shack is Celebrating Teachers and Nurses!

## **Honoring Our Teachers and Nurses: Shake Shack Says Thank You**


At Shake Shack, we’re endlessly grateful for the dedication, heart, and hard work our local teachers and nurses bring to the community every single day. Whether you’re fueling young minds or caring for loved ones, we want to recognize your incredible contributions with something special—right here at your neighborhood Shack.


## **Shake Shack Discount for Teachers**


If you’re a teacher, this week is all about you! From May 4 through May 12, show your valid school ID at your local Shack and enjoy a FREE ShackBurger or Veggie Shack with the purchase of any other menu item. It’s our way of saying thanks for shaping the future and making a difference in our community.


**ShackBurger** : Our neighborhood favorite features 100% Angus beef, crisp lettuce, ripe tomato, and our signature ShackSauce, all on a fluffy potato bun. Crafted with care for the best bite in town.


**Veggie Shack** : Craving plant-based? Enjoy our flavorful patty of mushrooms, sweet potatoes, carrots, farro, and quinoa. We top it with American cheese, crispy onions, tangy pickles, and our creamy ShackSauce, tucked inside a toasted potato bun.


## **Shake Shack Deal for Nurses**


Calling all local nurses—this one’s for you. From May 4 through May 12, bring your valid nurse ID to any neighborhood Shack and enjoy a FREE ShackBurger or Veggie Shack when you purchase any other menu item. Your care and commitment don’t go unnoticed, and we’d love to treat you to a meal as exceptional as you are.


**ShackBurger** : Savor our signature burger made fresh with 100% Angus beef, fresh greens, ripe tomatoes, and classic ShackSauce, all in a soft potato bun.


**Veggie Shack:** Our Veggie Shack is a local favorite, featuring a blend of mushrooms, sweet potatoes, carrots, farro, and quinoa, with all the bold toppings that make it uniquely delicious.


## **Shake Shack Nurses & Teachers Appreciation Week Offer Details**


When: May 4 – May 12


Who: Teachers (with valid school ID) | Nurses (with valid nurse ID)


What: One FREE ShackBurger or Veggie Shack with the purchase of any additional menu item


Where: Your local Shake Shack—stop by and let your neighborhood team serve you! (Excluding rest area, stadium, airport, and museum locations)


Thanks for making our community a better place. We can’t wait to see you at your neighborhood Shack—this one’s for you!
