# Sweet, Crackable or Complicated: Meet the True Love Shake

This Valentine’s Day, Shake Shack is celebrating love in all its forms with the **True Love Shake** : a limited-time Valentine’s Day shake that’s layered, indulgent, and meant to break.


## **What is the Shake Shack True Love Shake?**


The True Love Shake is made with Shake Shack’s signature vanilla frozen custard blended with strawberry purée, then coated in a rich, crackable dark chocolate shell. It’s finished with chocolate whipped cream and strawberry curls for a decadent sip that’s just as dramatic as love itself.


Because Valentine’s Day isn’t one-size-fits-all, we're celebrating love in all its forms.
