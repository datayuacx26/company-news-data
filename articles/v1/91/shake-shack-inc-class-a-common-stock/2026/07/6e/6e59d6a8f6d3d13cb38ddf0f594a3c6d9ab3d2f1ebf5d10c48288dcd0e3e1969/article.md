# The Viral Dubai Chocolate Pistachio Shake is Back for Summer!

Back by popular demand, on **Tuesday, June 9** , the **Dubai Chocolate Pistachio Shake** returns to Shake Shack menus nationwide for a limited time.


A premium, one-of-a-kind experience, the luxurious treat is inspired by the viral Dubai chocolate bar and is made with real pistachio frozen custard blended with toasted kataifi shredded phyllo and topped with chopped pistachios and more crunchy kataifi. It’s carefully coated in a crackable dark chocolate shell — bringing the iconic crack of the Dubai chocolate bar to life in shake form.


Summer is the season to crack open something iconic, and this fan-favorite is back to steal the spotlight.
