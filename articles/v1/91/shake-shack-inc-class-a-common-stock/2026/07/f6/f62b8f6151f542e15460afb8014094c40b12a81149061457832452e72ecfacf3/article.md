# Shake Shack Hits the Road with a New West Coast Burger + Menu

This summer, we’re paying homage to an American classic: California roadside burgers.


Available nationwide on 7/14 for a limited time, the new menu is Shake Shack’s take on the signature tastes that define the West Coast’s legendary burger scene. Featuring savory-sweet, caramelized onions, punchy Secret Sauce, and fresh avocado, we’re bringing the unforgettable flavors of the open road to your local Shack.


## **What’s on Shake Shack’s West Coast Menu?**


### **West Coast Burger**


A made-to-order quarter pound* per patty of 100% Angus Beef topped with American cheese, Shake Shack’s Secret Sauce, caramelized onions and honey mustard pickles served on a toasted potato bun.


**Approximate weight per patty is 4oz before cooking*


### **West Coast Chicken Sandwich**


Antibiotic-free white-meat chicken breast topped with hand-sliced avocado, lettuce, tomato, and Sour Cream & Onion Sauce served on a toasted potato bun.


### **West Coast Loaded Fries**


Crispy crinkle cut fries topped with melted cheddar and American cheese, caramelized onions and Shake Shack’s Secret Sauce.


### **Spicy Fries with Ranch**


Crispy crinkle cut fries spiced with our own hot pepper blend of cayenne, garlic, and paprika served with a side of our Shack Ranch.


### **Spicy Cheese Fries with Ranch**


Crispy crinkle cut fries topped with our cheese sauce and spiced with our own hot pepper blend of cayenne, garlic, and paprika served with a side of our Shack Ranch.


Don’t miss out on these roadside favorites as they’re only available for a limited time! Order ahead on the Shack App or head to your local shack.
