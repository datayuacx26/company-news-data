# Pricing changes

Thank you for being an early Alloy customer. I want to give you a heads-up about a change to how AI usage is priced, what it means for you, and what we've done to make the transition easy.


**The short version:** Until now, one Pro seat included unlimited AI usage for everyone in your workspace. That pricing isn't sustainable for us long-term - our costs scale with usage, and some workspaces cost us several times what they pay. Rather than degrade the product, we're moving to credits for AI usage per user.


## From July 18, 2026


- We're introducing a credits system (metered in $ USD) for AI usage via chat.
- Each Pro seat ($20/month, unchanged) includes $20 of AI credits per user, per month.
- Free collaborators remain unlimited in number, and each free user now gets $5 of AI credits per month.
- Additional usage draws from a shared workspace credit pool you can recharge anytime, with optional auto-recharge.
- If a user runs out of credits, only AI chat pauses - your prototypes, sessions, comments, and sharing all keep working, always.


**To smooth the transition, we've added $50 of bonus credits to your workspace at no charge.**


## What we are not changing


Your monthly bill doesn't change unless you add seats or credits, and access to your workspace, prototypes, and sessions is never restricted.


## Setting up your workspace


If you or your team are frequent users of Alloy, we recommend you take the following actions in[Settings > Billing](https://alloy.app/settings/billing) :


- Review the number of Pro seats in your workspace, and purchase more if needed.
- Confirm Pro seats are assigned to your most frequent users.
- Consider purchasing workspace credits if you anticipate users with frequent usage of the Alloy platform will exceed the personal credit balance.
- Enable automatic recharge to add credits automatically when your workspace balance runs low.


We're also shipping two things to give you more control: a model switcher (use faster, cheaper models for simple requests - available later this week) and credit usage visibility in Settings (available today).


## We're here to help


If anything here doesn't work for your team, contact us and we'll be happy to help. Our goal is to ensure a seamless transition.


You can reach us at any time throughsupport@alloy.app , or via live chat on the app or website.
