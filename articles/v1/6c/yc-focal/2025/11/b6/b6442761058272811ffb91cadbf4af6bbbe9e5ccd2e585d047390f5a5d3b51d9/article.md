# MOVAI Model Improves Temporal Consistency in AI Video

The latest MOVAI model update focuses on reducing flicker, jitter and frame drift in AI generated video. By improving temporal coherence, scenes now maintain smoother transitions and more stable motion over longer clips.Key improvements include:


- Stronger frame to frame alignment
- Reduced texture popping
- More stable lighting and shadows


This creates a more polished and natural viewing experience.
