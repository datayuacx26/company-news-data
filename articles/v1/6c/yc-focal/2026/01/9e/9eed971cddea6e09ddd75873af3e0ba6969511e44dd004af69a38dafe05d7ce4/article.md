## **Recommended Video Format and Output Specifications**


Before generating your first clip, it helps to lock in the technical format so your AI output is consistent and usable across platforms. These settings work well for cinematic Bigfoot videos and short form storytelling.


- Format MP4
- Length 8 seconds
- File size approximately 5MB
- Resolution 1080 by 720 pixels
- Frame rate 24 frames per second
- Audio 48 kHz two channel stereo


You can include these specifications directly in your AI prompt or select them in advanced generation settings, depending on the model you use. Adding these details early helps reduce motion artifacts and improves realism.

## **Step by Step How to Make a Bigfoot AI Video**


## **Plan the Scene Before Writing the Prompt**


Start by visualizing the entire scene as if it were a short film. Think about where Bigfoot is, what just happened before the clip begins, and what emotion he is expressing. Even though the final video is short, the AI performs better when the backstory is implied.


Ask yourself:


- Is Bigfoot tired, surprised, annoyed, or calm
- Is the setting deep forest, ravine, campsite, or mountain trail
- Is the camera observing him or interacting with him


Once this is clear, you can move on to prompt writing.

## **How to Write Prompts for Bigfoot AI Videos**


This is the most important part of the process. Below are **fully written paragraph style prompts** , similar in depth and structure to the example you shared. These are designed specifically for models like Google Veo inside Focal.


### **Sample Prompt 1 Cinematic Awakening Scene**


*Generate a cinematic video of a large Bigfoot wearing a backwards red baseball cap, slowly standing up from a pile of broken sticks and dry forest leaves at the base of a wooded ravine. His fur is dark brown and slightly matted, with small burn marks and singed patches visible on his arms and shoulders. Twigs and debris cling to his fur as he groans quietly and brushes himself off with heavy, deliberate movements. He looks around with tired eyes, slightly hunched, then speaks in a weary Midwestern American accent saying, “Yeah, that was a bit higher than I was expecting.” The environment is a dense forest with tall trees, filtered sunlight passing softly through the canopy, and dust particles floating in the air. The camera has subtle handheld movement, realistic human motion, natural weight shifts, and gentle camera shake. Lighting is soft and cinematic with warm highlights and cool shadows. Audio includes forest ambience, distant birds, rustling leaves, and clear dialogue. Output as an 8 second MP4 video at 1080 by 720 resolution, 24 frames per second, stereo audio at 48 kHz.*


---


### **Sample Prompt 2 Casual Bigfoot Vlog Style**


*Create a realistic AI video of Bigfoot holding a camera at arm’s length as if recording a casual vlog while walking slowly through a forest trail. He is tall and muscular with thick dark fur, wearing a worn backwards baseball cap. His breathing is slightly heavy and his movements feel grounded and human. He occasionally glances at the camera, then back to the trail, speaking in a relaxed conversational tone as if talking to an audience. The forest around him is dense with pine trees, fallen leaves, and soft morning light. The camera movement is imperfect and handheld, with natural sway from walking. Lighting is soft and diffused, with realistic shadows on his face and body. Include subtle environmental sounds like footsteps on leaves and distant birds. Generate a cinematic yet casual feel with realistic motion and pacing. Output as an 8 second MP4 video at 24 frames per second, 1080 by 720 resolution, stereo audio.*


---


### **Sample Prompt 3 Dramatic Environmental Scene**


*Generate a cinematic scene of Bigfoot emerging from a foggy forest clearing just after sunrise. He pauses, stretches his shoulders, and exhales deeply as mist moves slowly around his legs. His fur shows signs of wear, dirt, and small scratches, suggesting long travel. The camera starts low near the ground and slowly tilts upward to reveal his full size, creating a sense of scale. Lighting is atmospheric with golden sunlight cutting through fog and trees. Movement is slow, deliberate, and realistic with subtle shifts in balance. The scene feels quiet and reflective. Include ambient forest audio and light wind. Output as an 8 second cinematic MP4 video at 1080 by 720 resolution, 24 fps, stereo audio at 48 kHz.*
