## Why do you need to use an ad blocker?


If you’re already using an ad blocker you’ll know the benefits it brings, but if you haven’t turned yours on yet, here’s what might motivate you to try it out:


- **Fewer annoyances:** there are some pages that serve you downright annoying and intrusive ads. With an ad blocker, you can say *bye-bye* to those and get a more pleasant browsing experience by eliminating the clutter from your screen.


- **Better video streaming experience:** there’s nothing more anticlimactic than trying to show a funny video to someone else and having to go through several ads that can amount to minutes – it literally kills the mood. Those are the key moments when you want to have an ad blocker on!


- **Better privacy:** Some ads can go beyond simply being annoying; they can track you across the web, even when you haven’t agreed to it. With Opera’s built-in ad and tracker blocker, you can stop all this tracking in its… well, tracks.


- **Faster page loading:** when a website is loading, it has to load in all the ads with it as well, meaning videos, images, complex animations, and more elements have to load alongside the actual content of the webpage you’re visiting. When you block those ads the page loads faster and you can jump directly into the content you’re there for.


- **You can always support your favorite sites:** there are fantastic ads out there that might actually be meaningful to you. That’s why you can whitelist the websites you want with the built-in Opera ad blocker. If you want to see ads on a specific website, or support a specific creator, you can do so while still using the ad blocker everywhere else.
