# Elevate Pay Partners With Befiler: Simplifying Finance and Tax

Elevate Pay continues its mission to simplify finance for Pakistan’s global workforce. This time, we are joining hands with **Befiler** to simplify taxes for freelancers and international wage earners.


Elevate Pay helps you bring your international income home and Befiler helps you file your taxes with expert support and guidance.


## What is this Partnership About?


The collaboration solves two major challenges of the digital economy:


-


**Elevate Pay** for receiving and recording international earnings in USD


-


**Befiler** for guided tax filing aligned with FBR requirements


With Elevate Pay and Befiler now connected, international wage earners can move from earning to filing in one streamlined flow. Verified USD transactions received through Elevate Pay can be shared with Befiler for guided tax filing.


Each payment is recorded, compliant, and ready for documentation, making it easier to qualify for export benefits, maintain official income proof, and stay tax-ready without intermediaries.


## What This Partnership Means for Elevate Pay Users


You can now receive verified USD payments through Elevate Pay and use that same transaction data to file taxes on Befiler, no manual uploads or third-party agents required.
Each payment is automatically documented, making it easier to:


-


File taxes digitally with verified income records


-


Qualify for export-based tax incentives and compliance benefits


-


Maintain official proof of income for loans, visas, and future growth


### Exclusive Benefits for Elevate Pay Users


-


20% discount on Befiler’s tax-filing services


-


Receive USD payments, link verified data, and file returns digitally


-


Save time and cost by removing paperwork, agents, and unnecessary fees


It’s a simpler and credible way to manage your global income and local compliance, all in one place.


## Helping The National Economy


Pakistan’s digital professionals contribute billions in exports each year, but most remain outside the formal tax net.


By combining global payment access with licensed digital compliance, this partnership offers the first complete pathway from earning to filing, a practical step toward financial inclusion and institutional trust.


## Looking Ahead


Elevate Pay and Befiler will soon launch joint educational webinars on freelancer taxation, PRC verification, and export incentives.


Future phases will enable direct one-click tax filing through integrated platforms, allowing users to earn, receive, and file, all in one place.


Together, Elevate Pay and Befiler are simplifying global finance for Pakistan’s freelancers and digital entrepreneurs, building a more connected and compliant digital economy.
