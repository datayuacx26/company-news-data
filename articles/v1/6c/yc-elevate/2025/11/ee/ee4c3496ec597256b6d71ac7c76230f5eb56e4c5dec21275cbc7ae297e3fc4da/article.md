# Wise Business Account Review: Features, Fees, Limitations & More

If you work with international clients or manage cross-border transactions, you already know how tricky global payments can be. Between currency conversion, transfer delays, and hidden fees, handling money across borders often takes more time than it should.


Wise, formerly known as TransferWise, has built a strong reputation as a simple and transparent way to send and receive money globally. But how well does it serve business users in South Asia, especially in Pakistan and Bangladesh, where freelancers, agencies, and exporters make up a fast-growing digital economy?


This review looks at what the Wise Business Account offers, how it performs for users in these two countries, and how it compares to alternatives like Elevate Pay, a newer platform built specifically for businesses in emerging markets.


## What Is a Wise Business Account


A Wise Business Account helps you manage global payments from one place. It’s designed for freelancers, exporters, and companies that deal with clients or suppliers overseas. With Wise, you can:


-


Receive and hold funds in more than 40 currencies


-


Send payments to suppliers, contractors, or freelancers around the world


-


Automate recurring and batch payments


-


Integrate your account with accounting tools such as Xero or QuickBooks


Everything is managed online through a single dashboard, which makes it simple to track transactions and manage expenses without relying on traditional banks.


## Wise Business Account in BD & PK


Wise Business accounts are available in both Pakistan and Bangladesh, but with some important differences and limitations. While you can open and verify an account online, feature availability and compliance conditions vary from country to country.


### Availability and Setup


You can register your business, verify documents, and set up your account online. Once approved, you can receive international payments from clients or platforms such as Shopify, Amazon, Upwork, or TikTok Shop.


However, the onboarding process and feature access can be limited based on local regulations. Many users in Pakistan, for example, have to register under foreign entities or rely on international partners to make full use of Wise’s services.


### Key Features


-


Send and receive payments in multiple currencies, including USD, EUR, and GBP


-


Access batch and recurring payments for payroll or supplier management


-


Connect to business tools for easier bookkeeping and expense tracking


## Limitations and Restrictions


While Wise is reliable for global payments, there are certain limitations that matter if your business is based in the South Asian region:


-


Limited or unavailable debit cards in both Pakistan and Bangladesh


-


Restricted inbound transfers in some cases


-


Focused primarily on international transactions, not local banking


In short, Wise works well for moving money across borders, but not as a full banking solution for businesses that also need local integration or compliance features.


## Who Wise Business Works Best For


Wise is a good fit if you primarily work with international clients or partners. It works best for:


-


Freelancers and agencies receiving global payments


-


E-commerce sellers exporting products through Amazon or Shopify


-


Businesses paying international vendors or remote teams


If you operate globally and only need to send or receive payments, Wise is a solid choice. But if your business also needs payroll, compliance, or local settlement options, you may find the experience limited.


## Wise Business Account in Pakistan


In Pakistan, Wise is often used by freelancers and digital entrepreneurs who receive funds from clients abroad. The setup is simple, but some restrictions apply:


-


You can receive international payments in major currencies


-


Funds can be sent to a local Pakistani bank account, converted to PKR


-


There are no PKR receiving accounts, and debit card availability is limited


-


Some users report difficulty linking Wise to local banks for full functionality


Despite these hurdles, Wise remains a popular option for exporters and freelancers managing foreign income.


## Wise Business Account in Bangladesh


In Bangladesh, Wise allows users to hold and send payments in multiple currencies. It’s widely used by freelancers, outsourcing firms, and small e-commerce sellers. However, like in Pakistan, certain features are limited.


You can link your Wise account to receive payments from marketplaces and clients abroad, but local banking features such as debit cards and BDT receiving accounts are not available. Wise focuses on cross-border payments rather than domestic transactions.


## Pricing and Fees


Wise is known for transparent pricing. You always see the true exchange rate before completing a transfer, which makes it easy to understand how much you’re paying.


Typical fees include:


-


A small percentage per transaction, usually between 0.4% and 1.2%


-


Conversion fees when switching currencies


-


A one-time setup fee in some regions for business accounts


For most users, these fees are lower than what traditional banks charge. However, if your business processes a large number of international payments or frequent currency conversions, the costs can add up over time.

## Alternatives to Wise for South Asian Businesses


While Wise is strong for global payments, many South Asian businesses need more flexibility. They want a solution that combines international payment access with local financial management, especially when it comes to payroll and compliance.


Some global alternatives include Payoneer and Revolut, but both have limitations in local integration and regional support. For a platform built specifically for freelancers, startups, and e-commerce businesses in this region, Elevate Pay stands out as a more complete option.


## Elevate Pay: The Better Choice for BD and PK Businesses


If you are based in Bangladesh or Pakistan and your business earns globally, Elevate Pay is designed for you. It connects international payment rails with the local financial ecosystem, helping you do more than just receive funds.


You can open business accounts that work with major networks such as ACH, domestic wire, and SWIFT. This means you can receive USD directly from clients, platforms, or global marketplaces like Amazon FBA, Upwork, and Fiverr.


### Built for Modern Global Businesses


Elevate Pay supports both US-incorporated businesses owned by Bangladeshi founders and locally registered companies receiving export income. It’s ideal for agencies, SaaS startups, dev shops, remote teams, and freelancers who need access to USD accounts and compliant local operations.


### Simplified Payroll and Payouts


Through its Payroll Power-up, you can send payments to team members locally in BDT or globally in USD. You can run batch payouts, save on fees and conversion costs, and keep every transaction traceable, all within one unified dashboard.


### A Web-First Experience


Everything happens online. You can manage transfers, monitor balances, and handle both incoming and outgoing payments through a clean, simple interface. There’s no need to juggle multiple platforms or tools.


### Why Elevate Pay Fits Better


Platforms like Wise focus on global infrastructure. Elevate Pay focuses on regional usability, helping South Asian businesses connect their international income with local growth. It brings together payments, payroll, and compliance in a way that feels natural for freelancers and digital companies in this region.


## Conclusion


Wise remains one of the most trusted names in international payments. It’s transparent, efficient, and perfect for straightforward cross-border transfers. But for businesses in Pakistan and Bangladesh, the gaps are clear. Wise is built for a global audience, not necessarily for the unique challenges of South Asian entrepreneurs.


If your goal is to simply send and receive payments abroad, Wise gets the job done. But if you want to build a business around your global income, manage teams, and stay locally compliant, Elevate Pay is a better fit.


It brings the entire financial workflow together, from receiving USD to paying your team and tracking compliance, all in one place.
