# How to Create a Nayapay Account? A Step By Step Guide

NayaPay is a Pakistan-based digital wallet that allows users to send and receive money, receive remittances, pay bills, and do more with their finances.[NayaPay](https://www.nayapay.com/) has gained wide popularity among Pakistani users because of its smooth and seamless operations and the overall trustworthiness of the app.


If you are from Pakistan and looking to create a Nayapay Account, we’ll help you. In this article, we’ll walk you through every step of creating an account with NayaPay.


Let’s dive into the step-by-step process.


## Download the NayaPay App


Start by downloading the NayaPay app from the[App](https://apps.apple.com/pk/app/nayapay/id1621286305) or[Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nayapay.app&hl=en) Store.


#### Tap ‘Register’


Open the app and tap on ‘Register’ to begin signing up.


#### Enter Initial Details


Initially, you must enter your first name, last name, and email address. Enter those details and tap ‘Next.’


#### Verify Phone Number


The next screen requires you to enter your contact number and the network service provider. Once you do, you will receive an OTP that you must enter to verify your phone number.


#### Create ID and Password


Once you verify your phone number, create your unique NayaPay ID and password.


#### Tap Create Wallet and Add Personal Details


On the next screen, tap ‘Create Wallet’ and personal details like your location and capture a picture of your face for recognition and verification.


#### Verify Your Email Address


You will then receive an email to verify your email address. You need to click the link in the email to proceed.


#### Create Mobile PIN


The next step will take you to the screen where you create your 6-digit PIN code, which you can use later to log in.


#### Select Security Question


The final step is to select your security question, which you can use to recover your account if you forget your credentials. Just select your security question and type in your answer.


Your account is now ready to use. To get started, deposit Rs.300 into your NayaPay account.
