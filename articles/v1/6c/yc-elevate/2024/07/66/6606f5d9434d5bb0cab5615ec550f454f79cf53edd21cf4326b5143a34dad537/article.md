# How to Create an Elevate Pay Account in the Philippines

A country with over[1.5 million freelancers](https://legacy.senate.gov.ph/lisdata/3784434285!.pdf) **** the Philippines will always need a better solution for seamless transfers. Filipino freelancers have been paying high conversion rates, FX rates, and service fees for receiving their hard-earned money and it was about time someone solved this problem. Elevate Pay is the solution you have been looking for all this time.


Elevate Pay is now available in the Philippines and you can create your account in X simple steps. But before we dive into that let’s help you understand Elevate Pay better.


## What is Elevate Pay?


Elevate Pay is the solution to global USD banking that freelancers and remote professionals have been looking for. Elevate Pay offers a virtual USD bank account. You can use Elevate Pay to store USD, receive payments, send money, and more.


## How To Create an Elevate Pay Account in the Philippines?


Creating an Elevate Pay account is a simple 3-step process. Let’s dive into the guide and help you create your Elevate Pay account:


1.


#### Start by Downloading the App & Signup


Start by downloading the mobile application on your[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.elevatepay.app) or[Apple](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/elevate-pay-usd-banking/id6446812091) smartphone. Elevate Pay does not have a web version for services, yet. So, you’ll have to download the app on your mobile device.


Sign up on the app by submitting your email and mobile phone number. Your phone number must belong to a service provider in the Philippines to be able to sign up.


1.


#### Complete the Verification and Wait for Approval


Elevate Pay highly values security on the platform and therefore requires you to complete the Know Your Customer process. You must take a live selfie, upload your ID, and share a document containing your address ensuring that you live in the Philippines.


Elevate Pay reviews and approves your account typically between 24 to 72 hours. However, excessive demand may result in a longer wait time for profile approval.


1.


#### Receive USD


Once your profile is approved, you can receive USD in your Elevate Pay account. You can receive direct deposits via[ACH or Wire transfer](https://www.elevatepay.co/blog/ach-vs-wire) or through freelance platforms like[Upwork](https://www.elevatepay.co/learn/transfer-funds-from-upwork-to-elevate) and Fiverr.
