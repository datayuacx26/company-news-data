# How to Create a bKash Account? Simple 8-Step Guide

bKash is a mobile financial service in Bangladesh that paves the way to digital financing for the country's unbanked and underbanked. It lets users make digital transactions, including money transfers, bill payments, and mobile recharges. In a country where many people cannot access traditional banking services, bKash provides a convenient way to manage their finances using their mobile phones.


If you are located in Bangladesh and looking to create a bKash account, we have you covered. In this guide, we will walk you through the steps of creating your bKash account and talk about all types of accounts offered by bKash.


Here’s a simple step-by-step guide to creating a bKash account from the comfort of your home:


1.


#### Download the bKash App


Start by downloading the bKash App using your[App](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bkash/id1351183172) or[Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bKash.customerapp&hl=bn) Store.


1.


#### Tap ‘Register’


Once you open the app, you’ll see the button that says ‘Login/Register.’ Tap the button to Proceed.


1.


#### Verify Your Number


Enter your mobile number, select your mobile operator network, and you will receive a verification code via SMS. Enter the code to complete your phone number verification.


1.


#### Agree to The Terms and Conditions


The next step is to read and review the terms and conditions bKash has laid out. You can either read or just tap ‘agree’ to continue.


1.


#### Submit NID Picture


Now, you will be required to take a picture of the front and back of your NID. You need to use the original NID and ensure all the information is readable when taking the picture. After you submit the picture, review the information and proceed.


1.


#### Submit a Selfie


The next step is to photograph your face with your phone’s camera. Make sure you align your face with the boundaries shown in the camera app by bKash.


1.


#### Await Final Verification


Once you’ve submitted all the information, bKash will take up to 48 hours to send you the final verification SMS.


1.


#### Setup Your PIN


Once you receive the verification SMS, you have 72 hours to set up a PIN code for your bKash login. Once you do, you’re all set to sign in and start using bKash.
