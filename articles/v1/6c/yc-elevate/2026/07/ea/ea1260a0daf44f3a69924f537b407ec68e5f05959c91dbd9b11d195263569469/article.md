# Introducing Elev@Tag: A Simpler Way to Send and Receive P2P Payments

Sending money to another Elevate Pay user is now easier.


With the latest Elevate Pay app update, every individual user now has a unique **Elev@Tag** . You can use it to find other Elevate Pay users, add them as payees, and receive P2P payments from them.


## **What is Elev@Tag?**


Elev@Tag is your unique Elevate Pay ID.


Instead of using an email address to search for another Elevate Pay user, you can now use their Elev@Tag.


This makes it easier to find the right user and add them as a payee for P2P payments.


## **What’s changing?**


Previously, you could search for another Elevate Pay user using their email address.


From now on, **Elev@Tag will be the default way to search for and add another Elevate Pay user as a payee** .


The transfer flow remains the same. The only thing changing is how you find and add a new payee.


## **What stays the same?**


You can still send and receive money with other Elevate Pay users as usual.


With Elev@Tag, you can:


-


Search for another Elevate Pay user


-


Add them as a payee


-


Send P2P transfers


-


Receive P2P payments from other users


P2P transfers between Elevate Pay users remain free.


## **Existing payees are not affected**


Any Elevate Pay payees you added previously will remain in your account.


You can continue sending money to them as usual. You only need an Elev@Tag when adding a new Elevate Pay user as a payee.


## **How to find your Elev@Tag**


You can find your Elev@Tag in the Elevate Pay app.


Open the app and go to your **Profile** tab. Your unique Elev@Tag will be shown there.


## **How to receive a P2P payment**


To receive a payment from another Elevate Pay user, simply share your Elev@Tag with them.


They can use your Elev@Tag to find you, add you as a payee, and send the payment.


## **Who can use Elev@Tag?**


Elev@Tag is currently available for personal Elevate Pay accounts.Business accounts do not have Elev@Tags at this time because P2P transfers are not supported for business accounts.


## **Update your app to start using Elev@Tag**


Elev@Tag is available with the latest Elevate Pay app update. Update your app, go to your Profile tab, and check your unique Elev@Tag. Then share it with other Elevate Pay users to receive P2P payments.
