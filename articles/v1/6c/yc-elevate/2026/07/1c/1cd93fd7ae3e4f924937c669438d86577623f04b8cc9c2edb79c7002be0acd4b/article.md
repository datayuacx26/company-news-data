# How to Receive International Payments in Pakistan: Fees, Exchange Rates and PRCs

Freelancers in Pakistan can receive international payments through local bank transfers, digital wallets, global payment platforms, or a USD account. The real cost depends on withdrawal fees, exchange-rate margins, intermediary deductions, conversion timing, and whether the payment is documented with a PRC.


## **How Can You Receive International Payments in Pakistan?**


Common routes include:


-


A direct payout from a freelance platform to a Pakistani bank account in PKR


-


A payout through a global payment platform, followed by a withdrawal to a local bank


-


An international wire to a foreign-currency account in Pakistan


-


A remittance or wallet payout through a local provider


-


A payment to US account details, where the earnings are received and held in USD before being converted


The best route is not always the one with the lowest fee shown on screen. Compare the final amount you receive, the exchange rate, the payment timing, and the documentation provided.


## **The Real Costs of Receiving International Payments**


-


**Withdrawal fee:** The flat or percentage fee displayed by the platform.


-


**Exchange-rate margin:** The difference between the market rate and the rate used to convert your USD into PKR.


-


**Receiving fee:** A charge applied by the payment provider before you withdraw the money.


-


**Intermediary or bank fee:** A deduction by a correspondent bank or the receiving bank.


-


**Conversion timing:** The cost of being forced to convert immediately instead of choosing when to exchange your USD.


-


**Documentation:** The practical cost of receiving money without the PRC or records you may later need.


## **Receiving International Payments in USD Versus PKR**


A PKR payout is converted before it reaches you. This is convenient when you need rupees immediately, but you accept the provider's exchange rate and conversion timing.


A USD payout lets you hold your earnings in dollars and choose when to convert them. It can also let you pay for eligible international expenses in USD without converting the balance first.


The right option depends on how quickly you need PKR, the final exchange rate, transfer fees, and whether the payment route supports the documentation you need.


## **How Exchange Rates and Fees Affect Your Final Payout**


The exchange rate alone does not tell you what a payment route is worth. The number that matters is your total take-home: the final PKR that reaches you after the exchange rate, transfer fee, receiving fee, and any other deductions.


Two providers can advertise similar exchange rates and still deliver very different amounts. Compare the final PKR received, not just the quoted rate. You can also calculate the effective take-home rate by dividing the final PKR amount by the USD amount sent.

## **What Is a PRC and Why Does It Matter?**


A Proceeds Realization Certificate, or PRC, records that foreign earnings were received through recognized banking channels. Freelancers may need it for tax records, income verification, PSEB-related requirements, or other compliance and documentation needs.


PRC issuance depends on the receiving bank and the payment route. Before choosing a withdrawal method, confirm whether a PRC is available, which purpose code applies, and how the certificate is requested or delivered.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**


**Can I receive international payments in a Pakistani bank account?**


Yes. Pakistani banks can receive international payments through local payout arrangements, remittance channels, or international wires. The available currency, fees, exchange rate, and documentation depend on the bank and payment route.


**Can I receive foreign currency in Pakistan?**


Eligible foreign-currency accounts can receive supported foreign-currency payments. Confirm the account type, incoming-payment requirements, fees, and documentation with the receiving bank before sending money.


**Which Pakistani banks accept international payments?**


Many banks support remittances or incoming international payments, but the available route and required information differ. Ask the bank whether it supports the specific payment type, currency, purpose code, and PRC you need.


**Can JazzCash or Easypaisa receive international payments?**


Available international-remittance and payout options depend on the sending provider and current wallet partnerships. Check the wallet and payment provider before initiating the transfer.


**Do freelancers need a PRC in Pakistan?**


A PRC is an important record of foreign earnings and may be needed for tax, PSEB, banking, or income-verification purposes. The exact requirement depends on the freelancer's situation.


**Is it better to receive freelance income in USD or PKR?**


USD provides more control over conversion timing. PKR is more convenient for immediate local spending. Compare the full cost, live exchange rate, payment timing, and PRC availability.


**How can I reduce the cost of receiving international payments?**


Compare the final amount rather than only the visible fee. Check the exchange-rate margin, intermediary deductions, receiving fee, conversion timing, and documentation before choosing a route.
