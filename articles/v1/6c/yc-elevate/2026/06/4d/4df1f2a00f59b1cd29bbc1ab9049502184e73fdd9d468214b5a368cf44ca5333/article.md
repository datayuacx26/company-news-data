# 9186 PRC Certificate Now Available for NayaPay Freelancers

Pakistani freelancers on NayaPay can now request a 9186 PRC on eligible Elevate Pay Salary Transfers.


That means you can request a PRC certificate: official proof of your USD income. It's exactly what PSEB asks for at registration and renewal, and it keeps your tax filing under the correct purpose code.


## **What changed**


With Elevate Pay and NayaPay, you can now request a 9186 PRC on the USD you receive. When you transfer USD into your NayaPay account through the Elevate Pay **Salary Transfer** flow, that transaction becomes eligible for a 9186 PRC, and NayaPay issues it through its partner banks.


So you're not left guessing which purpose code is issued to your transfers, or chasing anyone to fix it afterward. You send the Salary Transfer through Elevate Pay, it lands in NayaPay, and you request your PRC from NayaPay by email.


## **Why the 9186 code matters**


A PRC certificate, short for Proceeds Realization Certificate, is the document that confirms your foreign earnings actually reached Pakistan through a legitimate banking channel. It also carries a State Bank purpose code showing what kind of income it was.


For IT and IT-enabled services, which covers most freelance and remote work, that code is **9186** .


Getting it right matters, because the 9186 PRC is what you lean on for:


-


PSEB registration and renewal


-


FBR tax filing


-


Proof of foreign income


-


The lower tax rate that IT and freelance exports qualify for


If you're going through PSEB freelancer registration, or renewing it, a 9186 PRC is part of the documentation the board expects.


And the gap is real money. PSEB-registered freelancers can see a final withholding tax as low as **0.25%** . Without PSEB registration, the standard final tax sits at **1%** . A PRC stamped with the wrong purpose code can quietly undo all of that, making your filing messier and, in some cases, leaving you paying more tax than you actually owe.

## **Frequently asked questions**


**What is a PRC certificate?**


A PRC, or Proceeds Realization Certificate, is a bank-issued document that confirms your foreign earnings reached Pakistan through a legitimate banking channel. It records the amount, the date, and a State Bank of Pakistan purpose code identifying the type of income.


**What does PRC stand for?**


PRC stands for Proceeds Realization Certificate. In Pakistan's banking system, it's the certificate a bank issues to prove that an inward foreign remittance was received and converted through official channels.


**What does purpose code 9186 mean?**


9186 is the State Bank of Pakistan purpose code for IT and IT-enabled service exports, the category most freelance and remote work falls under. A PRC carrying code 9186 marks your income as IT and freelance export earnings.

**Do I need PSEB registration to get a 9186 PRC?**


No. NayaPay issues the PRC through its partner bank. But if you are PSEB-registered, a 9186 PRC is the proof that qualifies you for the lower 0.25% final tax rate, and it supports your PSEB registration or renewal.


**Why doesn't the Home Remittance flow qualify?**


The 9186 purpose code is for IT and freelance service exports, which is what the Salary Transfer flow records. Home Remittance is classified differently, and a transfer sent that way can't be reclassified as 9186 afterward.
