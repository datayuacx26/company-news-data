### ATEC to Report Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results on August 4, 2026


July 15, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s21.q4cdn.com/310550763/files/doc_news/ATEC-to-Report-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results-on-August-4-2026-2026.pdf)


CARLSBAD, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Alphatec Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: ATEC), a provider of innovative solutions dedicated to revolutionizing the approach to spine surgery, announced today that it will report second quarter 2026 financial results on August 4, 2026, after the market close. The Company will host a live webcast that day at 1:30 p.m. PT / 4:30 p.m. ET.


**Webcast**


To access the live webcast, please use[this link](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fedge.media-server.com%2Fmmc%2Fp%2Fhfnr43wj&esheet=54571399&newsitemid=20260715285345&lan=en-US&anchor=this+link&index=1&md5=09a66a7fa2f7c3eb399b33c0ae15c38b) or visit the[Investor Relations Events & Presentations section](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.alphatecspine.com%2Fevents-and-presentations%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54571399&newsitemid=20260715285345&lan=en-US&anchor=Investor+Relations+Events+%26+Presentations+section&index=2&md5=46f474a0170b3badd1f46b03e618980a) of ATEC’s corporate website.


**Analyst Participation**


To participate in the question-and-answer session, analysts must register in advance using[this link](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fregister-conf.media-server.com%2Fregister%2FBI0363017e15674a16b37d31a015ffc915&esheet=54571399&newsitemid=20260715285345&lan=en-US&anchor=this+link&index=3&md5=6e0774b73652b7f6748f62d8778a77dd) . **About** **Alphatec Holdings, Inc.**


ATEC, through its wholly owned subsidiaries, Alphatec Spine, Inc., EOS imaging S.A.S. and SafeOp Surgical, Inc., is a medical device company dedicated to revolutionizing the approach to spine surgery through clinical distinction. ATEC’s Organic Innovation MachineTM is focused on developing new approaches that integrate seamlessly with the Company’s expanding InformatiXTM platform to better inform surgery and more safely and reproducibly achieve the goals of spine surgery. ATEC’s vision is to be the Standard Bearer in Spine.
