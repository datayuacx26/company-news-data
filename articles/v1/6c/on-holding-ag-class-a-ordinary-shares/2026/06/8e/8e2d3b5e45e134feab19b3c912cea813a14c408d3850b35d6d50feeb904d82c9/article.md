# ON LIGHTSPRAY CLOUDBOOM STRIKE 2

**Launch date:** July 30, 2026


**Category:** Race days, Competitions up to marathon distances


On redefines speed with its fastest shoe ever. Co-created with elite athletes. Proven by PBs and podiums worldwide.


**LightSpray™ Upper**


The seamless, glueless, one-piece upper, sprayed by a robot in just minutes, provides a laceless, “second-skin” fit that eliminates distractions while on the road.


**New Helion™ HF Midsole**


A new super foam material, which is 15% lighter than its predecessor, reduces overall weight while improving performance, slashing minutes off our athletes’ PBs.


**Curved Carbon Speedboard®:**


Sandwiched between layers of dual-density Helion HF Foam, the curved Speedboard **®** is lighter and stiffer than previous versions, providing forward momentum with every stride.


**CloudTec Sphere™ Geometry:**


A brand new cushioning system with precisely engineered channels, which maximizes impact absorption and rebound, for a fresh leg-advantage when the race heats up.

- **Sizes:** 36-49
- **Drop:** 5mm
