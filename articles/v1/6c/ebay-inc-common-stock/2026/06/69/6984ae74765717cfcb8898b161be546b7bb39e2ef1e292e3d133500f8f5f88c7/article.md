# eBay Live Turns Soccer’s Enduring #7 v #10 Rivalry Into a Fan Showdown with Landon Donovan and Jack Wilshere

**SAN JOSE, June 23, 2026** – Today, eBay announces “Showdown: 7s v 10s,” a livestream event taking place on Sunday, June 28, that pits two of soccer’s most iconic jersey numbers head-to-head. Throughout the stream, viewers can make their case for #7 or #10 while bidding on curated trading cards and memorabilia tied to the greatest players ever to wear each number, creating a real-time collecting experience centered around the sport’s most sought-after items.


The interactive event will feature appearances from USMNT and MLS legend Landon Donovan representing #10s and England and Premier League star Jack Wilshere representing #7s. Bringing together renowned athletes and passionate collectors, the livestream gives viewers a front-row seat to the players, stories and collectibles that have shaped the legacy of both numbers.


“Soccer fandom is fueled by rivalry, legacy and shared moments,” said Ashish Chhabra, Vice President, eBay Live. “eBay Live brings those elements together by connecting fans, athletes and collectors around memorabilia that defines the sport. With ‘Showdown: 7s v 10s,’ collectors can do more than watch the showdown unfold, they can take part in it through a marketplace experience only eBay can deliver.”


“The debate around #7 versus #10 has been part of soccer culture for generations, and now fans finally get a chance to settle it together,” said Landon Donovan. “Showdown: 7s v 10s on eBay Live takes that rivalry beyond the field by bringing the soccer community, players and historic memorabilia into one place for a live experience built around the passion and competition that make soccer special.”


“England has been home to a number of historic players wearing both numbers. I had the privilege of playing in both the #7 and #10 and I know how much those jerseys mean to players and fans alike,” said Jack Wilshere. “Wearing #7 for England at a major tournament was one of the greatest honors of my career, and I’m looking forward to seeing the debate #7s v #10s sparks. eBay Live is bringing that debate to the fans and giving them the chance to celebrate the players while weighing in on the showdown themselves.”
