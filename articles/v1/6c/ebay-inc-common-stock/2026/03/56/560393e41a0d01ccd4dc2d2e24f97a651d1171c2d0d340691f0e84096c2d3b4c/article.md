# eBay Launches 48-Hour Livestream Shopping Event Featuring Celebrities, Rare Finds, and $1 Starting Bids

Today, eBay launched[“48 Hours of Drops,”](https://www.ebay.com/ebaylive/channels/J5cXd9dZQrSqKHkQ) a non-stop live stream shopping event happening now through March 27 that features a wide selection of can’t miss steals on pre-loved fashion, collectibles and everything in between. Together with some of today’s favorite pop culture figures —


from fashion icons La La Anthony and Maura Higgins to collectors Xolo Maridueña and Jared McCain —


eBay is inviting enthusiasts to find the things they love, sourced from sellers they can trust, at incredible value.


### Must-Watch Streams


The 48-hour event features five marquee live streams, each designed to bring fans closer to the categories —


and personalities —


they care about most. Viewers can engage in real time, bid live, and secure standout pieces across fashion and collectibles. From Birkins starting at $1 to charity closet cleanouts and vintage Pokémon booster packs, there’s something for everyone.


- **24 Hours of Hermès Drops | March 26, 1 p.m. PT** Maura Higgins of Love Island and The Traitors joins eBay seller Bolt Luxury’s 24-hour Hermès event featuring Birkins and Kellys starting at $1. During her one-hour featured appearance, Higgins will highlight standout pieces, including the 2025 Hermès Rose Mexico Togo Kelly 25 PHW.


- **Goldin Gem Mint Night | **March** 26, 2 p.m. PT** Oklahoma City Thunder guard, Jared McCain, teams up with Goldin to auction off highly coveted sports singles —


including cards that Jared grew up hunting for, like the 2012-13 Panini Kobe Anthology #1 Kobe Bryant Auto (#7:8).


- **Collectibles Breaks with Blez | **March** 26, 3 p.m. PT** eBay seller Blez is breaking 2025 Panini National Treasures and Flawless Football packs. The stream includes buy-one-get-one break spots, conversation around the current NFL landscape, and more.


- **Vintage Pokémon Booster Drops | **March** 26, 5 p.m. PT** Actor Xolo Maridueña joins seller Ryan’s Cardhouse to auction 61 vintage Japanese and English Pokémon booster packs from the franchise’s early years —


all starting at $1. Maridueña will share his personal connection to the hobby and favorite pieces. Proceeds benefit Boyle Heights Arts Conservancy.


- **Live from the Closet with La La Anthony | **March** 27, 5 p.m. PT** La La Anthony invites fans inside her personal collection, featuring iconic pieces from Versace, Manolo Blahnik, Gucci, and The Row. The stream includes storytelling behind each item, audience Q&A, and auctions supporting her nonprofit ThreeSixty, which provides mentorship and rehabilitation resources for young men incarcerated at Rikers Island.
