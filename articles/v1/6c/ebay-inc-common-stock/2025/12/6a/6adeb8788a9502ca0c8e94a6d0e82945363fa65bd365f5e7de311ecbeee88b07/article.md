# Celebrating eBay’s Top Sellers of 2025

To celebrate our sellers’ achievements in 2025, we hosted a series of regional events to recognize the impact they’ve made across the eBay marketplace.


Recently, the HIS team hosted the **Exporter of the Year 2025 (EoY '25)** event in Hanoi, Vietnam, honoring sellers’ entrepreneurial drive and standout accomplishments in global exporting. The two-day event brought together **100 Top Sellers from Global Emerging Markets** , including Eastern Europe, Latin America, the Middle East, India, and Southeast Asia, for connection, collaboration, and shared learning.


Across every winner’s journey, one theme stood out: unwavering praise for the eBay team. Sellers consistently shared how their business growth was directly shaped by eBay’s tools, programs, and problem‑solving support. From day one, they emphasized that eBay has been a partner every step of the way, helping them to adapt, innovate, and thrive in the global marketplace.


Here are a few of the awards presented this year:


### Slovakia — Jan Trucka, J&J Automotive


Honored with the *Parts & Accessories Excellence Award* , Jan Trucka grew his business by leveraging eBay’s research tools to identify new opportunities and scale effectively.


### India — Aanshul Jaiin, Uwash Jewelry


Winner of the *Luxury Excellence Award* , Aanshul Jaiin expanded globally after two decades focused on the domestic market. Leveraging eBay to expand his 25‑year-old jewelry business, he credits the eBay Authentication Program and the use of a local warehouse as key drivers of his success this year.


### Slovenia — Aleksandra Kralj, Ecocool


Recipient of the *Lifestyle Excellence Award* , Aleksandra Kralj exceeded expectations by reaching far more customers on eBay. By prioritizing customer experience, she reduced complaints and returns while strengthening her global presence and driving significant business growth.


### Singapore — Amit Chilgunde, Creationwatches


Awarded the *Fashion & Jewelry Excellence Award* , Amit Chilgunde, a 20‑year seller, actively leveraged eBay programs and shipping tools such as CPaSS to accelerate growth in the watches category.


### Singapore — Dennis Tan, Thypon Machinery


Winner of the *Business & Industrial Excellence Award* , Dennis Tan boosted sales of compact mini excavators by creating detailed listings, moving inventory to local warehouses to improve after‑sales service, and using Ads as a game‑changer for growth.
