### Ameriprise Financial Recognized by Newsweek as One of the “Most Trustworthy Companies in America”


June 29, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/618704162/files/doc_news/Ameriprise-Financial-Recognized-by-Newsweek-as-One-of-the-Most-Trustworthy-Companies-in-America-2026.pdf)


MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Ameriprise Financial, Inc. (NYSE: AMP) has been recognized by Newsweek as one of the “Most Trustworthy Companies in America 2026.” The annual list ranks companies that have earned high levels of trust among customers, investors and employees. For Ameriprise, this recognition reflects a longstanding commitment to building trust through personalized advice and enduring client relationships.


“Earning our clients’ trust is at the heart of everything we do at Ameriprise – and it has been since 1894,” said **Jim Cracchiolo, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Ameriprise** . “In a more complex and uncertain environment, that trust matters even more. Millions of individuals and families count on us to help them make important financial decisions, and we earn that trust every day by delivering advice that’s personal, building lasting relationships and staying focused on what matters most to them over time.”


Newsweek, in partnership with market research and industry ranking provider Statista, surveyed 25,000 U.S. residents. Respondents evaluated companies they are familiar with across three touchpoints of trust – from the perspectives of customers, investors and employees. The ranking also incorporates a social listening analysis of online mentions to assess broader public reputation.

Newsweek partnered with Statista to recognize the Most Trustworthy Companies in America. Eligible companies (headquartered in the U.S. with revenues of at least $500 million) were evaluated September to January 2026 through: An independent survey of 25,000 U.S. residents, which resulted in 101,000 company evaluations, on three touchpoints of trust (customer, investor, employee); and social listening analysis (identification and assessment of what was said about a company on various media segments of the internet). Ameriprise did not pay a fee to be evaluated but did pay a fee to publicly cite the results.
