# Eight Ameriprise Financial Advisors Recognized on Barron’s “Top 100 Women Financial Advisors” Ranking

### Eight Ameriprise Financial Advisors Recognized on Barron’s “Top 100 Women Financial Advisors” Ranking


August 5, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/618704162/files/doc_news/Eight-Ameriprise-Financial-Advisors-Recognized-on-Barrons-Top-100-Women-Financial-Advisors-Ranking-2026.pdf)


MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Ameriprise Financial, Inc. (NYSE: AMP) today announced that eight of its financial advisors have been named to the *Barron’s* “Top 100 Women Financial Advisors” ranking, a prestigious industry list recognizing leading women financial advisors across the United States. The honored advisors exemplify Ameriprise's commitment to delivering personalized financial advice and helping clients achieve their financial goals with confidence.


“We are incredibly proud to see these outstanding Ameriprise advisors recognized among the nation's best by *Barron's* ,” said **Bill Williams, President, Ameriprise Wealth Management Advisor Group.** “I have known and worked closely with many of these advisors over the years, and this recognition reflects what we've long known: they are among the industry's most accomplished advisors, with a proven ability to earn clients' trust and deliver exceptional service. Our role is to help fuel that success through the strength of our brand and continued investments in technology and capabilities that help advisors grow, serve clients more effectively and deliver meaningful outcomes.”


The rankings were based on data submitted by more than 1,400 of the nation’s most successful advisors and evaluated factors such as assets under management, client relationships and retention, growth, industry expertise, professional designations, compliance records, community involvement and team structure.


The full list of ranked advisors can be found[at Barron’s.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.barrons.com%2Fadvisor%2Freport%2Ftop-financial-advisors%2Fprivate-wealth%3Fpage%3D1%26mod%3Dfaranking_subnav_topteams&esheet=54583437&newsitemid=20260805613809&lan=en-US&anchor=at+Barron%26%238217%3Bs.com&index=1&md5=93077cad61e700da452d3a0db84aba86) .
