# A Letter from Our CEO: Launching Solution Scout!

-


### Share:


-
-
-


*To our global learners,*


*I’m pumped to share an exciting product update with you. Chegg has launched Solution Scout, a powerful new tool* ***designed with you in mind*** *. Check out this[video](https://youtu.be/DxY2p4w5TTE) and see how Solution Scout is going to 🤯.*


*We know that your time is precious, and we’re watching students spend too much time flipping between tabs trying to verify information from various AI sources. So, we brought it together on one single page!*


*Solution Scout allows you to easily compare solutions from multiple sources. You now have the ability to compare Chegg’s content with AI models like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Claude. Our tool even highlights key differences between solutions, so you can quickly assess what’s right for you. Chegg’s AI-generated comparison summaries make it easier to see where different solutions align – saving you the time and hassle of cross-checking.*


*You deserve to learn with confidence on a platform that supports your individual learning goals. With Solution Scout, we’re taking a big step forward to help you trust the solutions you rely on, so you can focus on what matters most – your studies.*


*Thank you for your continued trust in us. We’re here to support you and we look forward to continuing to build tools that make your learning experience more personalized, efficient, and reliable.*


*From everyone at Chegg, keep crushing it and give Solution Scout a try –[click here](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250407480473/en/Chegg-Introduces-Solution-Scout-AI-Powered-Tool-to-Help-Students-Learn-More-Efficiently-and-Confidently) .*


*-Nathan*
