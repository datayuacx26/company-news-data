# A Letter from Our CEO: Exciting Changes Ahead

-


### Share:


-
-
-


To our global learners,


As we embark on a new semester, I thank you on behalf of the whole Chegg team for trusting us to support you. Going into this year, our aim is simple: to help you find success in school and beyond, as we work on new and better ways for you to get the most out of our products.


Being a student comes with a whole mix of emotions – it can be exciting, hard, overwhelming, and at times, lonely. No matter how you’re feeling, Chegg is here to be your trusted learning partner, and we have some really exciting innovations for you this spring.


You have told us that you are spending too much time cross-checking and verifying answers from AI platforms, need better and more reliable practice tools, and want mobile support when you’re outside the classroom or on the go. We have been listening. In the next few months, you’ll see several new and innovative updates from us – each designed to provide what you’ve been asking for.


Here’s what we have coming your way this semester so you can really get a grip (BTW… Have you met our new mascot,[Ace the Octopus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1CxvueoSoHM) , who helps students everywhere “Get a Grip” on learning?):


- We’re planning to launch a solution comparison tool so you can see Chegg’s high quality solutions right next to GPT, Gemini, and others. We know you are looking for more than the solution, and this comparison tool will help you synthesize and learn more easily.
- Practice makes perfect, especially when it comes to exams! Our new practice application lets you build practice problems that are tailored to what you are learning, giving you the confidence to go and master those mid-terms and finals.
- Whether you’re in a coffee shop, at the gym, or in the library, we want you to have the best experience with Chegg, no matter what device you’re using. We’ll be improving our mobile app so you can use Chegg more easily on your phones or tablets.


Our goal is to make sure you have the most powerful, personalized learning application at your fingertips! To do this, we’ve combined over a decade of learning science and knowledge of student behavior with the latest AI and machine learning to get the best solution, not just the easiest one. Take our cutting-edge AI and tech, plus a dose or two of human expert help, and you have Chegg’s secret sauce. We have you covered this semester!


We’re excited about these developments and hope you will be too. Make sure you sign up[here](https://forms.office.com/r/6E1vSFxfuH) to be the first to know about new updates and announcements.


As we get into 2025, I am filled with optimism and feel more dedicated and inspired than ever to support you on your learning journey. Your trust in us is the foundation of everything we do, and we are deeply grateful for it. More to come!


-Nathan
