## *Chegg continues to rank among the top companies in the world for workplace culture*


Chegg continues to garner recognition from reputable organizations for our efforts to retain, engage, and support employees. In the first half of the year alone, Chegg has won about a dozen workplace awards underscoring our commitment to fostering a dynamic work environment for employees.


Recently, Chegg has been certified as a Great Place to Work for the seventh year in a row with “[93% of employees](https://www.greatplacetowork.com/certified-company/1293668) ” saying Chegg is a great place to work as compared to “[57% of employees at a typical U.S.-based company.](https://www.greatplacetowork.com/resources/blog/global-employee-engagement-benchmark-study) ” Fortune recognized Chegg among small to medium businesses as one of the “Best Workplaces in the Bay Area” (#5) and “Best Workplaces for Millennials” (#29).


In addition to these accolades, Chegg received multiple awards recognizing the company for our positive work culture, commitment to work-life balance, and top-rated leadership and management teams. Check out the list below to see our positively impacting workplace culture for employees globally.


To learn more about how Chegg puts employees first and Working at Chegg, visit[here](https://www.chegg.com/about/working-at-chegg/) .
