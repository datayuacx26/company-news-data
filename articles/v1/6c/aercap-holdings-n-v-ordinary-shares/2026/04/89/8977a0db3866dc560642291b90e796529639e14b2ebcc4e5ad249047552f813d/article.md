# AerCap Holdings N.V. Annual General Meeting Approves Appointment of New Non-Executive Board Member and Re-appointment of Three Non-Executive Directors

DUBLIN


, April 15, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- AerCap Holdings N.V. ("AerCap") (NYSE: AER) today announced that its Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (AGM) held on April 15, 2026, appointed Doug Parker as Non-Executive Director to the Board of Directors (the "Board"), together with the re-appointment of Non-Executive Directors Stacey Cartwright, Rita Forst, and Robert (Bob) Warden.


Julian (Brad) Branch has retired from the Board with effect from the close of the Company's 2026 AGM.


Aengus Kelly, Chief Executive Officer of AerCap, said, "I am delighted to welcome Doug Parker to the Board. With more than 35 years of aviation industry experience, including his tenure as Chief Executive Officer of American Airlines, Doug brings deep insight and perspective that the Board believes will greatly benefit AerCap."


"I would also like to sincerely thank Brad Branch for his excellent counsel and guidance over his eight years of service to the Board," added Mr. Kelly.


**About AerCap**


AerCap is the global leader in aviation leasing with one of the most attractive order books in the industry. AerCap serves approximately 300 customers around the world with comprehensive fleet solutions. AerCap is listed on the New York Stock Exchange (AER) and is headquartered in Dublin with offices in Shannon, Memphis, Miami, Singapore, London, Dubai


, Shanghai, Amsterdam and other locations around the world.
