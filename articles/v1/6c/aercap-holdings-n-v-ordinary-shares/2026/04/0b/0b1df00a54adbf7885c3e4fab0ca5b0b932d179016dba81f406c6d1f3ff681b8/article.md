# AerCap Holdings N.V. Announces Filing of Interim Financial Report for the First Quarter of 2026

DUBLIN


, April 29, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- AerCap Holdings N.V. ("AerCap" or the "Company") (NYSE: AER) today announced it has filed an interim financial report including its unaudited condensed consolidated financial statements and notes for the first quarter ended March 31, 2026, with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the "SEC"). AerCap's Form 6-K can be accessed on the "Investors" section of the Company's website at[www.aercap.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4676264-1&h=4271076382&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.aercap.com&a=www.aercap.com) , as well as on the SEC's website at[www.sec.gov](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4676264-1&h=3450252943&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.sec.gov%2F&a=www.sec.gov) .


**About AerCap**


AerCap is the global leader in aviation leasing with one of the most attractive order books in the industry. AerCap serves approximately 300 customers around the world with comprehensive fleet solutions. AerCap is listed on the New York Stock Exchange (AER) and is headquartered in Dublin with offices in Shannon, Memphis, Miami, Singapore, London, Dubai


, Shanghai, Amsterdam and other locations around the world.
