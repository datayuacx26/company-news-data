# Susie McCullough

**EVP of Operations**


Susie McCullough is the Co‑Founder and Executive Vice President of Operations at Vivos Therapeutics, where she leads enterprise‑wide operational strategy focused on scaling the business, supporting multi‑state growth, and delivering consistent, measurable performance.
With more than 43 years of experience in dental and healthcare operations, Susie brings deep expertise in building, scaling, and optimizing healthcare organizations. She received her consulting license from HCA Business School in Los Angeles, California, and is widely recognized for translating strategic vision into scalable operating models, disciplined execution, and results‑driven performance management.


In her current role, Susie oversees the startup and expansion of clinical operations across multiple states, while ensuring existing operations run efficiently, consistently, and in alignment with established performance expectations. Her focus is on building the infrastructure, leadership depth, and operational controls required to support growth while maintaining quality, compliance, and financial performance.


Previously, Susie served as Director of Practice Development for one of the nation’s largest Dental Service Organizations (DSOs), supporting operational growth and performance across 165 dental practices nationwide. She also owned and operated a successful consulting practice and was a nationally recognized speaker and advisor, helping healthcare organizations scale efficiently, strengthen leadership teams, and achieve measurable operational and financial outcomes.


Known for her disciplined, people‑centered leadership style and strong execution mindset, Susie plays a key role in positioning Vivos Therapeutics for sustainable growth by aligning strategy, operations, and talent to deliver long‑term value.
