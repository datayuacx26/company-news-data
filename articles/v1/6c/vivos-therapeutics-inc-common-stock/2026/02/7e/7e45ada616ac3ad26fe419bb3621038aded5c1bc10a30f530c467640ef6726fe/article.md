# Nick DeGennaro

**SVP, Mergers and Acquisition Group**


Nick DeGennaro has served as our senior vice president of the medical integration division since October 2019. From September 2015 to July 2019, Mr. DeGennaro served as the director of business development and planning for Liberty Hospital, a community-based health system in Liberty, Missouri. From January 2015 to September 2015, Mr. DeGennaro served as chief development officer at Cerescan Corporation, a neurodiagnostics company. From November 2010 to January 2015, Mr. DeGennaro served as president/owner of PH3 Healthcare Management, a free-standing imaging business with 10 centers across the United States. From July 2004 to November 2010, Mr. DeGennaro served as senior vice president at Nueterra Healthcare, facilitating physician/hospital partnerships in developing ambulatory surgery centers and surgical hospitals. Mr. DeGennaro has lectured at many national healthcare conferences and is regarded as an expert in physician/hospital joint ventures.


Mr. DeGennaro holds a BS in marketing from Colorado State University and an MBA from the University of Saint Mary. Mr. DeGennaro also sits on the healthcare advisory board at the University of Saint Mary and serves as an adjunct professor teaching healthcare leadership, organizational management, small business and entrepreneurial management, operations, and international business.
