# Michael Bruhn

Michael E. Bruhn is an accomplished senior healthcare executive with 29 years of extensive leadership experience across the public, private, and nonprofit sectors. He is currently the Executive Vice President, Business Operations Integration at Vivos Therapeutics, where he oversees and leads integration operations across all Vivos companies, supports corporate strategy development, and directs information systems within the organization.


He previously served as Chief Operating Officer and Executive Vice President of the United States Institute of Peace (USIP), an independent organization funded by the U.S. Congress. Prior to USIP, Mr. Bruhn held senior leadership roles in federal healthcare consulting at IQVIA, supporting agencies including the U.S. Department of Defense, the U.S. Army, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration, and NASA. Earlier in his career, he served more than 20 years in the United States Air Force as a healthcare administrator, obtaining the rank of Colonel prior to retiring.


Mr. Bruhn holds a Bachelor of Science in Economics from the U.S. Air Force Academy, an MBA from the College of William & Mary – Mason School of Business, and a Master’s degree in Military Operational Art and Science from the U.S. Air Force Air Command and Staff College.
