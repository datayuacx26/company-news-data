# Inside the Nu Young Apprentice Program 2026

*Written by: Nubank Editorial*


## **1. The First Step in Your Career**


Rarely in life does a young person get the chance to start their career at one of the fastest-growing technology companies in the world. Nubank is a dynamic place that moves fast and is always full of real challenges — exactly the kind of environment that can accelerate the journey of someone taking their first steps. The 2026 Young Apprentice Program is an invitation to be part of this story from the very beginning, learning firsthand how a global company works and discovering how much you’re capable of growing when you’re given the space to do it.


*“You feel challenged to go further all the time, and the company’s own values push everyone to always look for improvements, even if it means changing methods that are already in place.” — account from a Nubie*


[Check our job opportunities](https://bit.ly/jobs-at-nu)


## **2. A Place to Really Learn**


The pace at Nu is intense, and you feel it from day one. Instead of loose tasks, you join real projects alongside the people building the products that millions of customers use every day. There will be challenges, there will be doubts, and there will be a lot of learning ahead — and that’s how your career picks up speed.


*“It’s an intense environment, full of real challenges.” — account from a Nubie*


## **3. How the Selection Process Works**


The process is the same for everyone, no matter which area you’re applying to (we call it the **Unified Funnel** ). We use the same tools we use to hire people around the world — not to eliminate you, but to understand how you think and learn.


- **Online Test via CodeSignal:** We don’t use this platform to filter out people who already know how to code, but as a cognitive assessment tool. We want to understand your logical reasoning and learning potential.
- **Problem-Solving Exercise:** A space to observe how you collaborate and apply Nu’s values when facing real challenges.
- **Interviews with Leadership:** Where your behavioral profile meets the area that’s the best fit for you.


## **4. Real Ownership: You’re Not Just Here to Watch**


Forget the idea of the apprentice who just watches or makes copies. At Nu, you’re a **Nubie** from day one: you join a real team, with real responsibilities and room to contribute. You quickly realize that your work makes a difference and helps simplify the lives of more than 100 million people.


## **5. Autonomy with Support Networks (The “Buddy” and Leadership)**


We believe in autonomy, but we know no one grows alone. Nu’s culture provides the psychological safety needed for you to have the freedom to try, fail, and learn by doing. To make sure this journey is safe, every Nubie has a solid support network: the **Buddy** (a teammate who acts as your direct guide) and a close leader focused on your development.


## **6. The Nubie Community: A Sense of Belonging That Goes Beyond the Desk**


The journey at Nu is collective. Being a Nubie means being part of a vibrant community that challenges the status quo and brings diverse perspectives to the table. We’ve built a growth ecosystem that includes:


- **Nubie Meetups:** Spaces to exchange experiences and share struggles and wins with people at the same stage as you.
- **Pulses and Active Listening:** Constant feedback cycles to make sure your voice is heard and the program evolves with you.
- **Development Tracks:** Clear technical and behavioral learning paths that accelerate your career.


*“Nubank works in an almost horizontal way, where everyone can exchange thoughts and ideas, and even the young apprentice’s voice is taken into account in several high-impact projects.” — account from a Nubie*


## **7. Basic Requirements (What You Need to Know)**


To be part of the team reinventing financial services, check if you meet the requirements:


- **Age:** Be between 18 and 22 years old.
- **Education:** High school in progress or completed.
- **Language:** Basic English.
- **Location:** Residence in São Paulo or its Metropolitan Region.
- **Work Model:** Availability for a hybrid model and in-person theoretical coursework.
- **Weekly Hours:** 6-hour daily workday.


## **8. Ready to Get Started?**


The 2026 Young Apprentice Program is a real chance to start your career learning, making mistakes, growing, and being part of something big — with people who will be by your side along the way. More than a first job, it’s an invitation to build something together with us.


*“The experience of being a young apprentice at Nu is amazing, exceeding my expectations, which were already high, for such an important and influential bank in the market.” — account from a Nubie*


So, how about building a future where everything is purple, together with us?
