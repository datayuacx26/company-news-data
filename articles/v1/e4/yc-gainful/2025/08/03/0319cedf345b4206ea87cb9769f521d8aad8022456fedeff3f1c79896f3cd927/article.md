Fall Recipe Round-up


### One of our favorite seasons of the year is here and we are making all of our favorite treats with our Personalized Protein. Enjoy!


### Superfoods Smoothie Bowl from @brynley.fitness


**INGREDIENTS:**


1. 1 scoop Gainful Personalized Protein
2. 1 Madagascar Vanilla Flavor Boost
3. 1 frozen banana
4. 1/2 cup frozen strawberries
5. 3 ice cubes
6. 1 tbsp almond butter
7. 1 tbsp honey
8. A splash of almond milk
9. Toppings: cacao nibs, almonds, goji berries, honey & almond butter


**DIRECTIONS:**


Blend all ingredients together, pour into a bowl, add toppings and enjoy!


### Trail Mix Cookie Dough Protein Bites from @rachsrealeats


**INGREDIENTS:**


1. 2 scoops Gainful protein powder
2. 1 cup salted creamy peanut butter
3. ¼ cup manuka honey or maple syrup
4. 1-2 tbsp unsweetened vanilla almond milk
5. 1/3 cup raisins
6. 1/3 cup pumpkin seeds
7. ½ cup mini-sized dairy-free dark chocolate chips


**DIRECTIONS:**


1. Mix all ingredients together in a bowl until well combined.
2. Transfer to a small baking pan and pat down into an even square/rectangular shape.
3. Store cookie dough batter in the fridge for 30-45 minutes.
4. Take out and cut into squares.
5. Store squares in the fridge and enjoy them as the perfect pre- or post-workout snack!


### Sea Salt Caramel Overnight Proats from @siriusly_hungry


**INGREDIENTS:**


1. 1 scoop Gainful Personalized Protein
2. 1 Sea Salt Caramel Flavor Boost
3. ½ cup old fashioned oats
4. 1 tbsp chia seeds
5. ¾ cup unsweetened vanilla almond milk
6. Topping: Diced apples, 2 tsp dulce de leche or caramel sauce


**DIRECTIONS:**


1. Mix together the oats, chia seeds, Gainful Personalized Protein, Flavor Boost and unsweetened almond milk in a mason jar.
2. Stir well, screw the lid on and store in the fridge overnight.
3. When ready to eat, top with the diced apples, take 1 tsp of dulce de leche and mix with 1 tsp of almond milk to thin it out and drizzle over the apples. Enjoy!

With race season kicking off, Kim shares her best tips for runners to make the most of your running whether that’s beating your PR or just overall making the journey one that you enjoy.


## 1. Make Recovery Your First Priority


To me, the workout was the focus— & recovery wasn’t even a thought. To make my work actually WORK, I had to be HELPING my body recover. I did this through— sleep, rolling my legs after workouts, ice baths, getting in enough protein after workouts, taking supplements like BCAA’s to help my body recover!


## 2. Protein Load Right After your Run


I noticed a huge difference in how fast I recovered/how much less sore I was when I started getting in protein RIGHT after a workout, instead of waiting too long to eat. I love my[Gainful protein powder](https://www.gainful.com/product/protein) (customized for YOUR body) & I take it as soon as my run is done.

Basically— they keep it SIMPLE with just the good stuff to help you run your BESTTT! Get that protein in RIGHT after you run to kickstart that post-run recovery


## 3. Run With Friends


Challenge yourself by running with people faster than you. Don’t be intimidated by it--& also, when you’re with a group, stop looking at your watch, relax your body, & just stick in the group.


## 4. Incorporate Strength Training into your Routine


You cannot JUST run— strength work helps you be more POWERFUL & to be able to sustain paces longer. Even 2-3 30 min sessions a week will help BIG time!


## 5. Add Short Speed Intervals Into Your Runs


Maxing out on short speed intervals completely CHANGED my running. It helped me to get faster & improved my threshold- & hit paces I never had before.


## 6. Prioritize Mental Health


You can do all the physical work, but if you neglect your mind, the second things get hard, you’re done. Only I am in control of my mind— & I had to do a better job of controlling my thoughts & keeping them positive & encouraging.
