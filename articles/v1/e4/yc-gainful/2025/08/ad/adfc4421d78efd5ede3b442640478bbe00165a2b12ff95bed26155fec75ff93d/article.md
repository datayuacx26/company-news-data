# 9 Overnight Oats with Personalized Protein Powder Recipes

## What Are Overnight Oats?


Your familiarity with oats may be more associated with oatmeal, which is usually cooked on the stovetop or in the microwave.


However, overnight oats are made with a no-cook method — as the name suggests, it's a method of preparing food that doesn’t require standard cooking methods that use heat; stovetop cooking, roasting in the oven, or heating in the microwave.


Their no-cook method makes them a super easy meal to have in the mornings when you’re short on time.


## How Do You Prepare Overnight Oats?


It takes about two hours of soaking to make oats edible with conventional methods. All you need to do with overnight oats is add your desired liquid — like milk or almond milk — to the oats the night before, and leave them in the fridge overnight.


By the time you get up for breakfast, your oats are saturated and ready for you to enjoy.


Overnight oats are also a versatile option because they serve as a plain base to which you can add a variety of toppings. From chia seeds to fresh fruit or peanut butter, you can make your overnight oats more tasty and nutritious with little effort.


You can also add Personalized Protein Powder to overnight oats for an added boost and benefit.
