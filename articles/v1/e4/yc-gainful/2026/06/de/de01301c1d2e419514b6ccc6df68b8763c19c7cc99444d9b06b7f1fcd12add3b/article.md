Q1: There's a growing overlap between fitness culture and outdoor cooking. Why is grilling such a natural fit for people focused on high-protein nutrition?


****


Adam McMahon:


Grilling is one of the cleanest cooking methods available for lean proteins like chicken breast, salmon, steak, shrimp, and turkey. You're cooking over direct heat without heavy oils or processed coatings, which means you preserve the nutritional integrity of whatever you're preparing. For people tracking macros or following a protein-first approach to eating, that matters.

Q2: How do premium grill features specifically support healthier cooking compared to a basic backyard grill?


****


Adam McMahon:


The biggest differentiator is temperature consistency. Premium Napoleon and Blaze grills offer even heat distribution across the cooking surface, which eliminates hot spots that cause flare-ups or uneven cooking. For proteins like salmon or chicken thighs, that precision is the difference between a perfectly cooked meal and one that's dried out or underdone. Infrared burner technology also lets you sear at high heat quickly, locking in moisture without added fat.
