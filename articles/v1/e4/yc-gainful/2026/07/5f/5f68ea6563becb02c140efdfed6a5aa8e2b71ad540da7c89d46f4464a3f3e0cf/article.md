# Creamsicle Jack O’Lanterns 🎃

Aren’t these the cutest jack o’lanterns you’ve ever seen? 🎃 They also pack a punch of Gainful Custom Protein, antioxidants and vitamins A & C. Create your own festive Creamsicle Jack O’Lantern Ice Cream 🍊


**Ingredients**


*:*


1. 1 cup of frozen coconut cream


2. 3 frozen oranges


3. 1 scoop of[Gainful Customized Protein](https://www.gainful.com/products/customized-protein?variant=42681096667275&selling_plan=1573879947)


4. Madagascar Vanilla Gainful flavor boost


5. ½ cup milk


**Instructions** :


1. Cut off the tops of your oranges and hollow them out with a spoon.


2. Set oranges aside to freeze.


3. Freeze your coconut cream.


4. In a blender, combine coconut cream, milk, oranges, Gainful protein, and Gainful Vanilla flavor boost.


5. Draw your Jack O’Lantern faces on your oranges.


6. Pour creamsicle mixture into each orange.


7. *optional* Place in the freezer for 1 hour for an icy texture.
