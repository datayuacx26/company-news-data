# Electrolyte Slurpee

A healthy 7/11 Slurpee?! Sip on this electrolyte-powered recipe and elevate your gains!


**Ingredients:**


- 2 cups cubed watermelon


- 1 cup lemonade


- 1 cup ice


- 1[Gainful Strawberry Lemonade Hydration Packet](https://www.gainful.com/collections/all-products/products/customized-hydration?variant=42510247985291&selling_plan=1568374923)


- 1/2 lemon, juiced


- Optional: Fresh mint leaves for garnish, additional sweetener to taste


**Instructions:**


1. **Juice the lemon:** Squeeze the juice from half a lemon and set aside.


2. **Blend:** Combine the ice, watermelon, lemonade, Gainful hydration packet, and lemon juice in a blender. Blend until smooth and slushy. If the mixture is too thick, add a splash more lemonade until it reaches your desired consistency.


3. **Taste and adjust:** Sample the slushie. The lemon juice may have added sufficient tartness, but if you prefer it sweeter, add a touch of honey, agave, or your preferred sweetener.


4. **Serve:** Pour the slushie into glasses. Garnish with fresh mint leaves or strawberries.
