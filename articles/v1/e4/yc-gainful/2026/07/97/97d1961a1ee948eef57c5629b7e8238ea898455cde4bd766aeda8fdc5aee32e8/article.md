Tastes like:


a healthy Shamrock Shake — creamy, minty, lightly chocolatey.


### Ingredients


-


1 scoop


vanilla protein powder


(Gainful vanilla works great)


-


1 cup


milk of choice


(dairy, oat, or almond)


-


½ frozen


banana


(for creaminess)


-


½ cup


ice


-


¼ tsp


peppermint extract


-


1 tsp


cacao nibs or mini dark chocolate chips


-


½ cup


spinach


(for natural green color — you won’t taste it)


-


Optional: 1 tsp


honey or maple syrup


### Instructions


1.


Add milk, protein powder, banana, spinach, peppermint extract, and ice to a blender.


2.


Blend until smooth and creamy.


3.


Pulse in cacao nibs for a light chocolate crunch.


4.


Pour into a glass and garnish with:


**or a light whipped topping.**
