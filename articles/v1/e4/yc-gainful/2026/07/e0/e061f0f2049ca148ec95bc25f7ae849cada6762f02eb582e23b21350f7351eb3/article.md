# Pre-Workout Gummies

Taking Pre-Workout gummies to a whole new level 💪


**Ingredients:**


- 4 cups seedless watermelon, cubed


- 1 scoop[Gainful Watermelon Pre-Workout](https://www.gainful.com/products/customized-pre-workout?variant=42516337098891&selling_plan=1568669835)


- 1 tablespoon honey (optional, for extra sweetness)


**Equipment:**


- Blender


- Fine-mesh sieve or cheesecloth


- Baking sheet


- Parchment paper


- Dehydrator OR oven


**Instructions:**


1. **Blend:** Combine the watermelon cubes and Gainful Pre-Workout in a blender until smooth.


2. **Strain:** Pour the mixture through a fine-mesh sieve or cheesecloth to remove any pulp or seeds.


3. **Sweeten (optional):** If desired, stir in the honey.


4. **Prepare for Drying:** Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Pour the strained watermelon mixture onto the parchment paper, spreading it evenly into a thin layer (about 1/4 inch thick).

6. **Cool and Cut:** Allow the fruit leather to cool completely. Then, cut it into desired shapes and store in an airtight container at room temperature.
