# Citrus Ginger Booster Shot

Feeling run down? Give your immune system a kick with this zesty Citrus Ginger Booster Shot!


**Ingredients:**


- 1 packet[Gainful Daily Performance Greens, Lemon Ginger flavor](https://www.gainful.com/products/daily-performance-greens?variant=42681028116619&selling_plan=1572307083)
- 1/2 packet Gainful Mango Hydration
- 1 cup fresh spinach
- 1/2 cup water (can adjust for desired consistency)
- 1-inch piece fresh ginger, peeled and roughly chopped
- 1/4 orange, peeled and seeded (optional)
- 1/4 lemon, peeled and seeded (optional)
- Optional: Pinch of cayenne pepper


**Instructions:**


1. **Prep:** Peel and chop the ginger, orange, and lemon (if using).


2. **Combine:** Add all ingredients to a high-powered blender.


3. **Blend:** Blend until completely smooth and combined. You may need to stop and scrape down the sides once or twice.


4. **Strain (optional):** If you prefer a smoother shot without pulp or ginger fibers, strain the mixture through a fine-mesh sieve.


**Serve:** Pour into small shot glasses and enjoy immediately!
