# Product of the Week - Serums

# Product of the Week - Serums


By Raniyah Basheer and Siya Madayya


3 minute read


There is an extensive range of serums that may contain different benefits for the skin. The main purpose of a serum is that they contain a high concentration of active ingredients that deeply penetrate and hydrate the skin to address problem areas. Most serums are clear gels or liquids that feel lightweight and are quickly absorbed by the skin. They are usually applied after cleansing and before moisturising.


In short, serums do wonders for your skin whatever your skin type or concerns. However, there is such a broad range on the market it can be hard to know which one to try. Here are the top serums recommendations from Clear Users


### The Ordinary Buffet Serum


4.5/5 Stars on Clear


This multi benefit formula contains multiple active ingredients to hydrate, brighten and tighten the skin. Clear users have all reported that it feels light and non-greasy on the skin without any irritation, even to sensitive skin.


### Foreo’s Serum Serum Serum


5/5 Stars on Clear


Foreo’s formula on this serum includes Hyaluronic Acid meant to nourish and hydrate the skin for a softer, plumper complexion. One unique feature that Clear users love about this product is vegan formula that is also free of harmful ingredients. They also commented on the silky feel of this product.


### SkinCeuticals’ Serum 10


4.5/5 Stars on Clear


This serum is an antioxidant and Vitamin C rich serum meant to protect the skin against environmental damage and help with the effects of aging. Some Clear users do say that this formula can be very strong and advise those with sensitive skin to ease the serum into their regular routine. Other than this, many comment on the efficiency of this product, with some users saying they can see the effects in 1-2 weeks! Along with helping with reducing breakouts, many say they saw the serum help with sunspots and hyperpigmentation after regular use.
