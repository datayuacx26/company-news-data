# Product of the Week - Double Cleansers

Everyone on Clear has been talking about ‘double cleansing’. As part of a PM routine, using two cleansers is important as an oil-based cleanser will remove makeup, sunscreen and sebum so a water-based cleanser can more deeply clean the skin.


The top recommendations from clear users are the **GARNIER™ All-in-1 Micellar Water** . This product powerfully yet gently removes make-up and dirt with a single swipe making it better for the skin than abrasive makeup wipes. It is also formulated without fragrance and irritating ingredients, making it perfect for all skin types. To be more environmentally friendly, it can be used with reusable cotton pads.


A highly reviewed oil-based cleanser is **The Inkey List’s™ Oat Cleansing Balm** . It is a gentle balm formulated to remove impurities and makeup while also deeply moisturising and hydrating the skin. It is an especially popular product for doing so much for the skin and offering quite a bit of the product at a low price.
