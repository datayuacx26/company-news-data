# Top Skincare Trends for 2022

Skincare is constantly evolving, with new products and techniques being developed all the time. Keeping up with the latest trends can be overwhelming, but it’s also an exciting way to discover new ways to take care of your skin. Here are some of the top skincare trends that are expected to be big in 2022.


## 1. CBD-infused skincare


CBD, or cannabidiol, is a non-psychoactive compound found in hemp and marijuana plants. It has been shown to have anti-inflammatory and anti-aging properties, making it a popular ingredient in skincare products. From face creams to serums, expect to see more and more skincare products infused with CBD in the coming year.


## 2. Clean beauty


The clean beauty movement is all about using products that are free from harmful chemicals and toxins. Consumers are becoming more conscious of the ingredients in their skincare products and are choosing products that are made with natural and organic ingredients. Expect to see more brands and products promoting their “clean” and “green” credentials in the coming year.


## 3. Personalized skincare


Personalized skincare is all about creating a skincare routine that is tailored to your specific needs and skin type. With the help of technology, consumers can now take online quizzes, use apps, and even consult with virtual skincare experts to create a customized skincare routine that is tailored to their individual needs. Expect to see more brands and products offering personalized skincare solutions in the coming year.


## 4. CBD-infused supplements


Taking care of your skin starts from within, and that’s where CBD-infused supplements come in. These supplements help to improve the health of your skin from the inside out by providing your body with the nutrients it needs. Expect to see more and more supplements infused with CBD in the coming year.


## 5. At-home skincare devices


At-home skincare devices are becoming increasingly popular as they allow consumers to achieve professional-grade results from the comfort of their own home. Expect to see more devices such as LED light therapy masks, at-home microneedling kits, and sonic cleansing tools being used for skincare in the coming year.
