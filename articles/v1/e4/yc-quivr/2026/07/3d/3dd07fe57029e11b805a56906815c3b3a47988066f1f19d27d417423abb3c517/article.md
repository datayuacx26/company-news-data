### Quivr’s New KMS: The Unified Hub for All Your Knowledge!


# **Knowledge Management System: The Unified Hub for All Your Knowledge!**


At Quivr, we’re committed to helping you harness the full potential of your knowledge with advanced, AI-powered solutions. That’s why we’re thrilled to announce the release of our latest feature: the **Knowledge Management System (KMS)** ! Designed to provide a centralized, flexible, and intuitive view of all your information, KMS is set to redefine how you interact with and organize your knowledge. With KMS, say goodbye to the days of searching through multiple platforms or juggling various sources. Whether your data is stored in local files or in the cloud, KMS brings it all together in one seamless, integrated interface—allowing you to manage, organize, and access your knowledge efficiently. Here’s a look at the powerful capabilities that make KMS an indispensable tool for anyone looking to stay on top of their knowledge landscape. ‍


# **Key Features of Quivr’s KMS**


# **1. Unified Knowledge Access Across Sources**


The standout benefit of KMS is the **consolidated view of all your knowledge** within Quivr. From a single interface, you can manage:


- **Local files in Quivr** .
- All synced content from platforms like **Google Drive, Dropbox, SharePoint, and soon Notion** . This unified view lets you access and manage knowledge regardless of its origin, making it simpler than ever to draw from diverse resources when querying information or updating your knowledge base.


# **2. Enhanced Control Over Your Knowledge Organization**


Quivr’s KMS offers unparalleled flexibility when it comes to structuring your knowledge. Here’s how it empowers you to manage content:


- **Folder Organization** : Organize your knowledge by creating folders that can be nested to any depth, allowing you to sort files in a way that best suits your needs. Moving files between folders is effortless, thanks to the drag-and-drop feature.
- **Multi-source Linking** : With KMS, you can link the same content to multiple “brains” or knowledge spaces within Quivr. This means you don’t have to duplicate knowledge; simply link it to any relevant brain to ensure all sources are covered in your searches.
- **Bulk Management** : Whether adding files or folders to multiple brains or removing a selection, KMS enables batch operations, giving you full control over your content at scale.


# **3. Streamlined Syncing with Cloud Sources**


Quivr’s KMS syncs seamlessly with your cloud accounts, so you can:


- **Browse Remote Files within Quivr** : Access your Google Drive, Dropbox, or SharePoint content without switching platforms.
- **Effortless Knowledge Linking** : Link files and folders from these syncs to specific brains, consolidating local and remote knowledge in one place.
- **Read-Only Syncs** : Your files in sync are kept intact on their original platform, with Quivr fetching the latest state for accuracy. These syncs refresh every eight hours, ensuring your knowledge is always up-to-date. This connection to cloud sources is designed to be as efficient as possible, thanks to a 1-minute cache of sync files to optimize browsing performance.


# **4. Sophisticated “Brain” Linking for Advanced Knowledge Management**


Quivr’s brains are customized spaces where you can collect and categorize specific knowledge. With KMS, you can:


- **Add Knowledge to Multiple Brains** : Assign the same file or folder to multiple brains, ensuring it’s available across various relevant categories.
- **Asynchronous Processing** : KMS processes knowledge as you add it to brains. This asynchronous processing makes it fast and efficient, and if there’s an error, you’ll receive instant feedback with the option to retry (with a “Retry” button coming soon!). Additionally, with a new “My Knowledge” toggle, you can easily view all KMS-linked knowledge directly from the brain’s interface, allowing you to add or remove knowledge with a single click.


# **5. Comprehensive and User-Friendly Interface**


The KMS interface is crafted to simplify complex knowledge structures, with tools like:


- **Hierarchy Visualization** : Navigate your knowledge with a hierarchical view in the left panel.
- **Drag-and-Drop Functionality** : Move files and folders around intuitively, whether organizing them in folders or linking them to brains.
- **Top Navigation** : Navigate up through folder levels with ease, thanks to a new top navigation bar. With these features, KMS is designed to put users in full control of their knowledge structure, all while maintaining an intuitive and easy-to-use interface.


# **The Technical Edge: Behind the Scenes of Quivr’s KMS**


Our technical team has packed KMS with powerful features that ensure a fast, reliable, and robust experience:


- **Recursive Processing** : Adding a folder to a brain processes all nested files and folders recursively, ensuring every piece of content is accounted for.
- **Atomic File Processing** : File processing is atomic and parallelized, so each file is processed independently and swiftly—no need to worry about one error halting the entire batch.
- **Redis Caching** : For faster browsing, Quivr uses Redis caching for synced files with a 1-minute Time to Live (TTL), offering faster access to remote files without constantly querying the source. These technical enhancements give Quivr’s KMS a cutting-edge advantage, enabling fast, reliable, and error-resistant knowledge management for all users.


# **What’s Next for KMS?**


Our development roadmap is packed with exciting features for KMS V1, such as:


- **Shift-Select for Bulk Operations** : Streamline actions like deletion or linking by selecting multiple lines.
- **Keyword Search and Filters** : Find specific knowledge sources or search across the entire KMS with upcoming keyword and filtered search options.
- **Tagging and Folder Customization** : Soon, you’ll be able to tag files for better searchability, rename folders, and even assign colors to improve visual organization.
- **Duplicate Management** : When adding content to multiple brains, hints will alert you to potential duplicates, helping you keep your knowledge base clean and organized. Quivr’s KMS is more than just a management system; it’s a complete rethinking of how knowledge should be organized, accessed, and used. By bringing all your knowledge into one accessible and customizable space, KMS empowers you to unlock the full potential of your information. Stay tuned as we continue to enhance KMS with even more features and refinements to keep Quivr at the forefront of knowledge management. With KMS, we’re proud to deliver a tool that not only adapts to your needs but helps you take control of your knowledge—wherever it resides.
