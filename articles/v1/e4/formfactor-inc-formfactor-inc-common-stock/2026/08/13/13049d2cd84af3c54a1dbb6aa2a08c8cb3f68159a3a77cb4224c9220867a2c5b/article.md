# FormFactor and Keystone Microtech Expand Strategic Partnership

August 7, 2026


From AI accelerators and advanced memory to chiplet architectures and next-generation networking devices, FormFactor and Keystone Microtech are committed to helping customers solve increasingly complex test challenges.


The semiconductor industry is moving faster than ever. As AI, high-performance computing (HPC), advanced packaging, and next-generation memory continue to push chip complexity to new levels, manufacturers need more than innovative probe card technology. They also need trusted partners with the manufacturing capacity, technical expertise, and regional support to keep production moving efficiently.


That’s why FormFactor and[Keystone Microtech](https://www.ksmt.com.tw/en/index) have expanded their long-standing strategic partnership. Together, they’re giving semiconductor manufacturers greater access to the technologies and support they need to move new devices from development into production more efficiently.


**** **Meeting the Demands of Next-Generation Devices**


The rapid rise of AI, HPC, advanced packaging, high-bandwidth memory (HBM), and high-speed connectivity is reshaping semiconductor test.


Today’s devices feature:


- More complex architectures
- Higher I/O counts
- Faster data rates
- Tighter manufacturing tolerances


These advances demand more from probe cards than ever before. They must deliver outstanding electrical performance while supporting the speed, precision, and scalability required for high-volume manufacturing.


By expanding their partnership, FormFactor and Keystone Microtech are better positioned to scale manufacturing and provide stronger regional support as customer requirements continue to evolve.


**** **Combining Complementary Strengths**


Under the agreement, FormFactor will continue developing and manufacturing its industry-leading proprietary[probe card technologies](https://www.formfactor.com/products/probe-cards/) while providing advanced semiconductor test expertise.


Keystone Microtech will contribute:


- Design support
- Assembly services
- Product testing
- Repair capabilities
- Distribution for designated products


Each company will continue to own its intellectual property and proprietary technologies while working together to provide faster, more responsive support for customers around the world.


**** **Strengthening Support in a Critical Semiconductor Region**


Taiwan remains one of the world’s most important semiconductor manufacturing hubs. This partnership expands FormFactor’s presence in the region while increasing local production capacity and strengthening customer support.


For semiconductor manufacturers, that means faster turnaround times, greater manufacturing flexibility, and additional capacity as production ramps to meet increasing global demand.


**** **Supporting the Next Generation of Semiconductor Innovation**


As semiconductor devices become more advanced, reliable wafer test is more important than ever. Probe cards provide the critical electrical interface between automated test equipment and semiconductor devices, allowing manufacturers to verify performance, improve yield, and identify potential issues before packaging.


The expanded partnership gives both companies the manufacturing scale and operational support needed to help customers bring the next generation of semiconductor technologies to market.


From AI accelerators and advanced memory to chiplet architectures and next-generation networking devices, FormFactor and Keystone Microtech are committed to helping customers solve increasingly complex test challenges. By combining innovative probe card technology with expanded manufacturing capabilities and regional support, the partnership helps semiconductor companies bring new products to market with greater confidence.
