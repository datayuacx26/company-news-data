### How money laundering happens in gambling


To give a brief overview. Money laundering activities in gambling typically follow three stages:


**1. Placement:** Criminals introduce illicit funds into the gambling system, often through cash deposits, chip purchases, or online account top-ups.


**2. Layering:** They attempt to disguise the money’s origin via repeated bets, chip dumping between accounts, or minimal play followed by withdrawal.


**3. Integration:** Finally, laundered funds are withdrawn as “winnings” or transferred to external accounts, completing the cycle.


Each stage requires a proactive approach, supported by AML screening tools designed and integrated seamlessly into the player experience to reduce the risk of financial crime at every stage of the end-to-end gaming lifecycle.


Operators should ask themselves if they’re following the AML gambling rules:


- Has an AML risk assessment been conducted in the last 12 months?
- Do current CDD and affordability checks capture all relevant risk indicators?
- Is continuous monitoring in place to spot suspicious activity in real time?
- Is adequate PEP and sanctions screening undertaken?
