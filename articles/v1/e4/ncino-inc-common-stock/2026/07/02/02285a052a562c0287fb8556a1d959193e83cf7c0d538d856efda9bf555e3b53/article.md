# How Much Does AML Software Cost?

AML software cost varies significantly depending on the size of the organisation, the volume of checks run, and the features required.


So, what drives AML software pricing? What does best-in-class look like? What are the hidden costs? And what does ROI look like in practice?


## What is AML software?


[AML software mitigates financial crime](https://www.identitysolutions.ncino.com/blog/3-stages-of-money-laundering-explained) by automating AML checks to combat money laundering and terrorist financing.[Financial crime networks grow more sophisticated daily while regulatory frameworks shift globally, requiring organisations to unify sanctions, PEPs, and adverse media screening across complex jurisdictions.](https://www.identitysolutions.ncino.com/blog/which-sectors-need-aml-high-risk-industries-list)


AML software covers three core screening capabilities:[PEPs](https://www.identitysolutions.ncino.com/blog/the-complete-guide-to-peps-and-sanctions) ,[sanctions](https://www.identitysolutions.ncino.com/blog/sanctions-screening-guide) and adverse media. But the broader value proposition is about balancing compliance with client experience by helping compliance teams confidently identify high-risk individuals without creating friction or increasing overhead.


### How’s AML software priced?


AML software is typically priced per verification, per user/licence, or via a platform subscription. But in today’s high-risk environment, the question is not “what does it cost?” but “what does it cost to get it wrong?”


**What factors influence AML software cost, and what are the typical pricing models?**


- **Check volume:** The number of identity verification (IDV) and transaction monitoring checks run monthly is the primary cost lever.
- **Depth of due diligence:** This ranges from basic screening against sanctions and PEP lists to enhanced due diligence (EDD) workflows with ongoing monitoring, adverse media checks, and layered risk assessment.
- **Automation vs manual review:** Platforms with intelligent automation reduce the manual workload on compliance teams.
- **Regulatory scope and geography:** Operating across multiple jurisdictions requires broader data coverage and more complex IDV, which adds to cost.


Most AML vendors price in one of three ways: per verification or IDV check, per user, or on a platform subscription basis.


### What are the hidden costs of AML software?


The licence fee is rarely the full picture. Watch out for:


- **Integration and implementation:** Connecting AML software to existing systems takes time and resource. Some vendors charge separately for API setup or custom configuration.
- **False positive overhead:** Low-quality risk assessment engines generate excessive alerts, driving up manual review costs.
- **Fragmented systems:** Running separate solutions creates duplication, gaps, and higher overhead.
- **Scaling costs:** Per-check pricing that doesn’t flex becomes expensive as a business grows.


#### How to choose the right AML software for your budget


When[evaluating AML software](https://www.identitysolutions.ncino.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-anti-money-laundering-aml-checks) , consider the total cost of ownership, not just the upfront expenditure. The right software will:


- Reduce compliance risk and the cost of regulatory failure
- Automate the highest-volume, lowest-value tasks
- Free up compliance teams for complex work
- Removing the overhead of managing multiple vendors
- Integrate seamlessly with core systems
- Scale without punitive cost increases
