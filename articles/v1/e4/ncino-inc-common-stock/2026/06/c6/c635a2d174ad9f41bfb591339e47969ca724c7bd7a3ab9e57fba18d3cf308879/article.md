# How to Choose the Best Sanctions Screening Software

For financial institutions (FIs), the consequences of inadequate sanctions screening are severe. The[Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation (OFSI)](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/office-of-financial-sanctions-implementation) imposes unlimited penalties for breaches, while the[National Crime Agency](https://www.nationalcrimeagency.gov.uk/what-we-do/crime-threats/bribery-corruption-and-sanctions-evasion) pursues criminal enforcement.


But the risk is not solely a regulatory one. Engaging with a sanctioned entity causes lasting reputational and financial damage.


However, the challenge isn’t[understanding why sanctions screening matters](https://www.identitysolutions.ncino.com/blog/sanctions-screening-guide) ; every compliance team is aware of the risks. The challenge lies in choosing the right sanctions screening software.


This guide provides a practical framework for evaluating the best sanctions screening software for FIs.


## What is sanctions screening software, and why do FIs need it?


Sanctions screening software automates the process of checking individuals, entities, and financial transactions against global sanctions lists. These lists are maintained by bodies including the[United Nations](https://main.un.org/securitycouncil/en/content/un-sc-consolidated-list) , the[European Union](https://data.europa.eu/apps/eusanctionstracker/entities/) ,[OFSI](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-uk-sanctions-list) , and the[US Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC)](https://sanctionssearch.ofac.treas.gov/) .


Under UK legislation, including the[Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/13/contents) , regulated businesses are obligated to ensure they don’t make funds or economic resources available to designated persons or entities. This applies at both the onboarding stage and throughout client relationship management.


In practice, this means screening customers against UK, EU, and UN sanctions lists, OFAC's Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list, and all other relevant jurisdiction-specific lists.


It is worth noting that this is far from a static requirement. The volume and velocity of sanctions changes have increased dramatically in recent years, driven by an unprecedented wave of designations following geopolitical events. Therefore, relying on manual processes or infrequent list updates increases exposure.


Effective sanctions screening software removes that exposure by automating the process in real time.


### What risks does poor sanctions screening create?


Weak or outdated sanctions screening creates compounding risks across compliance, operations, and reputation. The most direct risks are regulatory and financial, with no upper limit on the monetary penalties that can be imposed. The[FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/financial-crime/financial-sanctions) will also take enforcement action against firms whose systems and controls are found to be inadequate.[In 2025 alone, the FCA imposed over £124 million in fines linked directly to AML and financial crime control failures](https://fintech.global/2026/02/16/aml-failures-drive-record-fca-fines-in-2025/) .


But the other critical risk factors are poor client experiences and reputation damage. Poor-quality screening software will generate excessive false positives and bottlenecks that slow down[onboarding](https://www.ncino.com/blog/commercial-onboarding-framework-behind-paperwork) and risk missing genuine matches entirely. Whilst transacting with a sanctioned entity, even unknowingly, attracts public attention that damages client and stakeholder trust.


Typical weaknesses include static or infrequently updated lists, high false-positive rates, siloed screening tools, limited audit trail capabilities and no support for additional[Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) and adverse media screening](https://www.identitysolutions.ncino.com/blog/peps-sanction-checks-adverse-media-screening) .


### So, what features should FIs look for in sanctions screening software?


Here are the 5 essential capabilities sanctions screening software must include:


1. **Real-time data coverage:** The foundation of any sanctions screening solution is the quality of its underlying data. After all, sanctions lists can be updated multiple times per day in response to geopolitical events. The best solutions provide continuous real-time monitoring across all major global sanctions lists and automatic alerts when a client’s status changes.
2. **Sophisticated data matching:** Software that uses simple exact-match logic will generate high volumes of false positives. The best solutions use a range of advanced and configurable matching tools.
3. **PEPs and adverse media screening:** Sanctions screening mustn’t sit in isolation. For effective financial crime compliance, FIs must also screen for[PEPs](https://www.identitysolutions.ncino.com/success-hub/glossary/politically-exposed-person) and[adverse media](https://www.identitysolutions.ncino.com/blog/adverse-media-screening-best-practices) .[The best software will bring PEPs, sanctions, and adverse media screening together on a single platform](https://www.identitysolutions.ncino.com/blog/how-to-successfully-navigate-the-complex-and-ever-changing-peps-and-sanctions-landscape) for a complete view of risk.
4. **Workflow integration and straight-through processing:** Siloed processes increase risk and complexity. The best solutions embed screening directly into onboarding and[KYC](https://www.identitysolutions.ncino.com/products/kyc-software) /[KYB](https://www.identitysolutions.ncino.com/products/kyb-software) workflows, triggering checks automatically and presenting results within a unified case management environment. This ensures straightforward cases are cleared automatically whilst high-risk client requests are escalated for human review.
5. **Audit trail and regulatory defensibility:** The best sanctions screening software maintains a complete audit trail documenting every event, alert, and decision in order to speed up regulatory processes, reduce the cost of compliance, and demonstrate proportionate, risk-based decision-making.


#### How to evaluate the accuracy and effectiveness of sanctions screening tools?


Again, here are 5 clear steps every evaluation process should include:


1. **Test on real data:** FIs must use anonymised samples from their own portfolio to assess real-world performance.
2. **Measure contextual false positive rates:** FIs must ask for false positive rates in comparable environments segmented by transaction volume, customer geography, and industry vertical.
3. **Validate list coverage:** FIs must request a complete list of the sanctions regimes and data sources covered, and cross-reference against their individual regulatory obligations.
4. **Assess configurability:** FIs must seek the ability to tune matching thresholds and rules to their own risk appetite. Configurability ensures the software will remain fit for purpose.
5. **Review the update process:** FIs must explore how vendors update, ingest and validate new designations on a sanctions list.


### How can sanctions screening software integrate with existing systems?


Integration is one of the most common concerns in sanctions screening software procurement; after all, it’s not wise to simply bolt on another siloed solution.


Modern, best-in-class sanctions screening software is built on an API-first architecture, allowing it to connect seamlessly to core systems and workflows. Rather than replacing existing infrastructure, it acts as an orchestration layer coordinating data flow, triggering screening events, and returning results within the compliance environment.


The most powerful implementations go even further. Embedding sanctions screening within a broader[Anti-Money Laundering (AML) platform](https://www.identitysolutions.ncino.com/products/aml-software) . This approach ensures that screening is not a one-time event at onboarding, but a continuous capability that runs throughout the entire client relationship, from initial acquisition, through periodic and event-driven reviews, to ongoing monitoring.


#### How do you measure ROI from sanctions screening software?


The business case for investment in high-quality sanctions screening software extends well beyond regulatory compliance. Quantifiable ROI metrics include:


- **Reduced manual processing time:** Automated screening reduces manual compliance and frees compliance teams for higher-value work.
- **Faster onboarding:** Integrated screening using best-in-class CLM software significantly reduces onboarding timelines.
- **Reduced costs:** A complete, well-structured audit trail dramatically reduces the time and resource costs
- **Avoidance of regulatory penalties:** The cost of non-compliance significantly exceeds the investment in effective screening software.
- **Improved risk detection:** Integrated, data-driven compliance platforms identify more critical risk issues than disconnected or manual processes.


FIs should ask vendors how they stack up across each of these ROI measurements.
