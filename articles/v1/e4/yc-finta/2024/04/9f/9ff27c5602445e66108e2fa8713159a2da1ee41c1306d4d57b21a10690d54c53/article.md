# Updated Syncs Page

The syncs page (previously known as the logs page) has been redesigned to make it easier to see a history of your syncs. The page loads in half the time, and you can now filter by status, trigger, destination or bank connection.


Click on a sync to see exactly how much data was sent to your destination and if there were any errors.
