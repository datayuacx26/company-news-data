# Pitch Deck March Madness

We have selected 16 startup pitch decks and each week you’ll get to vote on the best as they battle for first place. From disruptive tech solutions to game-changing services, these visionary companies represent the forefront of entrepreneurial excellence.


We have a diverse array of industries, ideas, and innovations on display from FinTech to healthcare, social media to realestate – Pitch Deck March Madness promises to be an exhilarating celebration of entrepreneurship and creativity.


Over the remainder of March, you will have the power to cast your votes, determining which startups advance through each round of the competition. So, join us as we embark on this thrilling adventure, celebrating innovation, entrepreneurship, and the boundless possibilities of the startup ecosystem. 🚀


To cast your votes, follow Finta (@trustfinta) on social media and reply with a screenshot of your completed bracket. The winning bracket gets one year of Finta Pro for free! ✨
