# Introducing Finta Investor Intelligence ✨

At Finta, we don’t believe in cold spamming hundreds of investors and hoping against long odds that they’ll respond. We believe in leveraging cutting-edge technology in order to identify dealmakers who are the best fit for your raise. And then help you execute the best practices in fundraising to build meaningful and enduring relationships.


Finta does this over and over again, thousands of times, for each raise on the platform. Our AI-powered CRM, shareable deal rooms, and workflow automation drive deals toward completion to get great companies funded.


Executing a proven approach for fundraising venture capital speaks to the core mission of our company: ***“increasing access to capital for all”*** . In pursuit of that mission we are excited to announce significant updates to Finta, continuing to leverage AI and automation to seamlessly find, communicate, and close the right investors for you. What’s New…


## ‍ **Investor Intelligence ✨**


Earlier this year, we introduced our AI assistant, Aurora, to assist in bridging knowledge gaps and automating repetitive fundraising tasks. Aurora possesses remarkable capabilities, including conducting in-depth web research on contacts in your CRM, providing boutique communications support, searching the internet, and even integrating newinvestors into your CRM for you.


Aurora drafts hyper-personalized email replies to investors, automatically tailored based on their communications history, internet profiles, their stage in the funnel, and your specific deal. Leading to better writing while freeing up time to work on the company’s strategic vision.


## ‍ **Unified Communications Platform 🤝** ‍


The email composer has been redesigned and now enables users to create custom AI-driven prompts. This feature allows users to tailor messages to specific contexts and provides email open tracking to offer insights into engagement. This enhancement enhances the precision of investor communication, making outreach more effective and informed.


## ‍ **Full Synchronization of Emails, Calendars, and Contacts 📅** ‍


The integration of email, calendar, and contact syncing establishes a unified workspace for users to manage all interactions with investors in a centralized location. This comprehensive synchronization streamlines the entire fundraising process, facilitating effortless tracking of communications and schedules.


## **Valuation Modeling 📊**


Finta is meticulously crafted to provide an exclusive experience tailored specifically for the private capital market. We are delighted to announce the recent integration of our cap table feature, which now incorporates valuation and equity modeling capabilities. This enhancement empowers users to project equity outcomes with greater precision and accuracy.
