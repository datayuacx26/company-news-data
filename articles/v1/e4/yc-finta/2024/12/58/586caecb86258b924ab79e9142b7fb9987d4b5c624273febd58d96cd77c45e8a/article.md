# Introducing Finta Networks: The Smart Way to Unlock Warm Intros 🎯

> At Finta we are on a mission to increase access to capital for all.


However, there is a major blocker that gets in the way of fundraising.[Warm introductions](https://www.trustfinta.com/blog/how-to-ask-for-warm-investor-introductions) are the key to closing capital – accounting for 92% of invested dollars.


Yet, warm introductions are notoriously hard to get, often requiring immense effort, persistence, research, and the right network.


‍


## Introducing Finta Networks:


We’re thrilled to announce the launch of Finta Networks—a revolutionary way to unlock capital access.


*"With Finta Networks, navigating from warm intros to closing capital has never been easier."*


With the click of a button, Finta users can now sync their email contacts. Finta will enrich each contact with profile information such as:


- **Job Titles**
- **Organizations**
- **Websites**
- **Social Links**
- **And more**


Each connection is ranked and sorted by relationship strength, allowing users to focus on their most impactful connections. For Finta Pro users, new contacts will sync automatically each week, ensuring your network remains fresh and actionable.


Best of all, Finta Networks are shareable across organizations, so your connections can help multiple teams find the best introduction pathways. Imagine your team seamlessly collaborating with investors and advisors to unlock shared networks—amplifying your fundraising potential.


‍


## From Intros to Capital


With Finta Networks, you can:


1. **Create Your Network:** Sync your inbox to unlock warm introductions to thousands of connections.
2. **Share Networks Across Teams:** Collaborate privately with your team, investors, and advisors to leverage shared connections.
3. **Focus on Strong Relationships:** Automatically sorted by relationship strength to find your warmest pathways **‍**
4. **Populate Your CRM:** Eliminate manual data entry with auto-filled introduction pathways. **‍**
5. **Request Intros Effortlessly:** Let Aurora craft hyper-personalized intro requests to simplify outreach.


The result? A seamless path from your existing relationships to securing the capital your business needs.


‍


## Ready to Get Started?


Take control of your fundraising journey today. Visit the[Prospects](https://app.trustfinta.com/dashboard/prospect) page and click ‘Create My Network’ to unlock your capital connections now. Finta Networks are available now for all Finta customers.
