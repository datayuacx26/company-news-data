# Direct Mercury Integration

Directly connect your Mercury bank account to Finta. The benefits of using the direct connection over our previous Plaid connection are:


-


The direct connection allows for unlimited history, you can sync as far back in time as you like. The Plaid connection is limited to the last three years


-


You can sync recent data with the direct connection. With Plaid, updates are sent on a daily schedule


-


The direct connection has better accuracy and less downtime


To get started, create an API token within your Mercury account. Then, head over to the[Accounts page](https://app.finta.io/accounts) where you can set up your new bank connection.
