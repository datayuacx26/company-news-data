# Connecting to Mercury Just Got Easier

## Connect Mercury with One Click


Connecting your Mercury bank accounts to Finta just got so much easier! You no longer need to hunt down API keys or deal with complicated setup steps. Just click "Add Bank Connection", click "Mercury", sign in to your Mercury account, and you're done. It's faster, easier, and more secure.


## What's Improved


### Cleaner Bank Connection Experience


We've redesigned the bank connection screen to make it easier to find and connect your accounts. The new design features better search, clearer bank logos, and a simpler step-by-step process that guides you through each connection.


## What's Been Fixed


- Account balances now update properly
- Fixed an issue where new transactions would overwrite existing data instead of adding to the bottom of your sheet
- Pending transactions from Mercury are updated
