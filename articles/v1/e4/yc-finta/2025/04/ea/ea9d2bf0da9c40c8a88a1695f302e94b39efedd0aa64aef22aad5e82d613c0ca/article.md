## **Step 1: Everything in One Place**


Traditionally, raising capital involves stitching together multiple disconnected tools:


- Investor databases for leads
- Excel or Google Sheets for contact tracking
- DocSend or Drive for pitch decks and data rooms
- Wire transfers coordinated via email
- Expensive cap table management software
- A chaotic mix of emails, calls, and meetings tying it all together


This fragmented approach is tedious, stressful, and inefficient—words directly echoed by our customers—and frequently stalls deals.


Finta integrates everything into one cohesive, intelligent environment. Central to this environment is **Aurora** , our AI assistant. Aurora relies on a steady stream of timely, context-rich data to make informed decisions. Rather than simply processing spreadsheets of contacts, Aurora comprehensively understands your business, analyzing pitch decks, websites, investor histories, and real-time communications:


- **Company Context** : Aurora reviews pitch decks, websites, and key deal details to fully grasp your business.
- [Investor Intelligence](https://www.trustfinta.com/blog/finta-product-update-introducing-investor-intelligence) : We automatically prospect and enrich contacts, providing Aurora with relevant background and historical data.
- **Real-Time Awareness** : Aurora syncs emails and meetings across your funnel, enabling precise follow-ups and personalized communication. **‍**
- **Expert Knowledge** : Aurora leverages decades of private capital market data, reasoning and acting with the experience of a seasoned dealmaker.


With Finta’s unified system, AI gets **the exact right data at the exact right time** , so you can do in minutes what used to take entire teams (and endless spreadsheets) days or weeks.


We tracked[over half a billion dollars](https://www.trustfinta.com/2024) in capital across more than 20,000 investors last year alone, and we’re just getting started. Finta's Intelligent Environment is the foundation that makes sophisticated deal-making not only possible, but *repeatable* —for any team, anywhere.

## **Step 2: Let Aurora Do the Work**


In traditional software tools, you bear the burden: manually entering data, scheduling communications, and clicking through each task.


Finta flips this paradigm. Built on our intelligent environment, Aurora operates as an AI-driven agent, proactively **thinking, reasoning, and executing** tasks within your workflow:


- **Prospect:** Aurora identifies and ranks top prospects, highlighting the strongest pathways for warm introductions through your networks.
- **Deep Research:** Aurora performs extensive research using enriched data to uncover insights about contacts, tracking relevant backgrounds, news, investment theses, and more.
- **Communication:** Aurora drafts highly personalized emails by synthesizing data across the platform, fostering genuine connections with contacts.
- ‍ **CRM Automation:** Aurora automatically adds new prospects, updates pipeline statuses, and maintains your funnel, minimizing your manual workload.


While many envision an AI powered future, Aurora is already here— **live today** , saving hundreds of hours and accelerating deal closes faster and smarter than ever.
