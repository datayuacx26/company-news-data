# Change Email and Password Reset

## Password Reset


Have you ever forgotten your password? Now you can reset your password on the log in page.


1.


Click on “Forgot Password?”


2.


Enter your email


3.


Click the link in your email


4.


Set your new password


As always, if you know your password but want to change it or if you signed up with Google or a one-time code, you can set a password on your[profile page](https://app.finta.io/settings/profile) .


## Change Email


Now, you can change the email associated with your Finta account. On the[profile page](https://app.finta.io/settings/profile) , enter your new email address and then click on the link sent to you via email.
