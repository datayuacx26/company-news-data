# Finta Now Supports European Banks

## Connect Your European Bank Accounts


Great news for our international users - Finta now supports banks across Europe! Whether you're banking with Revolut, N26, or any of thousands of other European financial institutions, you can now sync your accounts and transactions to your favorite tools.


## What's New


### Thousands of European Banks Supported


Through our partnership with GoCardless, Finta now connects to banks across the European Union. This includes:


- **Major digital banks** like Revolut, N26, and Monzo
- **Traditional banks** across Germany, France, Spain, Italy, and more
- **Nordic banks** in Sweden, Norway, Denmark, and Finland


### Same Great Experience


Connecting a European bank works just like connecting any other bank in Finta:


1. Click "Add Bank Connection"
2. Select your country
3. Search for and select your bank
4. Authorize the connection through your bank's secure portal
5. Start syncing your accounts and transactions


### Full Feature Support


European bank connections support all the Finta features you know and love:


- Automatic account and transaction syncing
- Sync to Airtable, Notion, Google Sheets, and Coda
- Customizable table configurations
- Real-time sync updates


## Get Started


Head to your Finta dashboard, click "Add Bank Connection", and select your country to see the available banks. Your European accounts will sync alongside any existing US or Canadian connections, giving you a complete view of your finances in one place.
