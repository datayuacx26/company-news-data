# Finta for Fractional CFOs

# **Finta: The Best CRM for Fractional CFOs**


Being a Fractional CFO means wearing a lot of hats.


On any given day, you might be:


- Coordinating fundraising conversations with venture capital firms
- Preparing financial models for investors
- Tracking investor introductions across multiple clients
- Managing financial documents, deals rooms, and investor updates


Here’s the challenge: most tools available today weren’t designed for fractional finance leaders working with startups.


Traditional CRMs like Salesforce or HubSpot focus on sales pipelines, not investor relationships or fundraising workflows. As a result, many fractional CFOs end up relying on spreadsheets, inbox searches, and scattered notes to manage critical relationships.


That’s inefficient, and risky when managing high-stakes fundraising conversations. Before long, it becomes difficult to keep everything organized.


That’s where Finta comes in.

Finta is a relationship management and fundraising workflow platform designed to manage investor pipelines, organize financial documents, and streamline communication with stakeholders. For Fractional CFOs, it provides a powerful system to manage client relationships, investor outreach, fundraising workflows, and financial collaboration in one centralized platform.


With tools like secure data rooms, investor pipelines, automated updates, and AI-powered insights through[Aurora](https://www.trustfinta.com/products/aurora-ai) , Finta helps fractional CFOs operate more efficiently while supporting multiple clients.


It combines several critical tools into one platform:


- Investor CRM
- Relationship management
- Secure data rooms
- Investor pipelines
- Investor updates
- AI-powered insights


For fractional CFOs working with startups or venture-backed companies, Finta acts as a central hub for managing investor relationships and fundraising workflows. Instead of juggling multiple systems, manage everything in one organized environment.


# **Finta for Fractional CFO Workflows**


Fractional CFOs typically work across multiple companies simultaneously, which makes relationship management especially complex.


Finta helps streamline several core workflows.


## **1. Investor Relationship Management**


One of the most valuable assets a fractional CFO brings to clients is their network of investor relationships.


Managing these relationships effectively requires a structured system.


Finta allows fractional CFOs to maintain a centralized investor CRM, making it easier to:


- Track investor conversations
- Record introductions and referrals
- Manage warm investor relationships
- Maintain historical interaction data


Instead of relying on memory or scattered notes, CFOs can maintain organized relationship intelligence across their entire network.


This is particularly useful when introducing the right investor to the right startup.


## **2. Fundraising Pipeline Management**


When a client starts raising capital, fractional CFOs often help manage the entire process.


This includes:


- Tracking investor outreach
- Managing pitch progress
- Monitoring interest levels
- Following up with potential investors


Finta provides a fundraising pipeline system that allows CFOs to track investor progress across different stages.


Benefits include:


- Clear visibility into investor pipelines
- Organized outreach tracking
- Structured fundraising workflows
- Easier follow-ups with potential investors


For fractional CFOs supporting multiple startups, this helps keep fundraising organized and transparent.


## **3. Secure Financial Data Rooms**


During fundraising, investors request critical financial documents such as:


- Financial models
- Forecasts and projections
- Cap tables
- Pitch decks
- Company financial statements


Sending these through email can create confusion and security risks.


Finta provides secure data rooms designed for investor collaboration.


With Finta’s data rooms, fractional CFOs can:


- Share financial documents securely
- Control access permissions for investors
- Track document engagement
- Maintain one centralized location for financial materials


This makes the fundraising process more professional and secure for clients.


## **4. Investor Updates and Stakeholder Communication**


Keeping investors informed is essential during and after fundraising.


Fractional CFOs often help founders prepare investor updates that maintain transparency and trust.


Finta helps streamline this process by allowing CFOs to:


- Manage investor contact lists
- Send structured investor updates
- Maintain consistent communication with stakeholders


For CFOs managing multiple companies, this reduces the time spent manually organizing investor communications.
