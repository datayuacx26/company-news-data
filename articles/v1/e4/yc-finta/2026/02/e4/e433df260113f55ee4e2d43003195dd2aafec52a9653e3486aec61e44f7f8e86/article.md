# Restore Archived Rules

## Never Lose a Rule Again


Accidentally deleted a rule you spent time perfecting? Now you can view all your archived rules and restore them with a click. Deleting a rule is no longer permanent — you're always one step away from bringing it back.


## What's New


### Archived Rules Tab


Your rules page now has an **Archived** tab that shows every rule you've previously deleted. Switch between active and archived views to see exactly what's been removed, so nothing gets lost.


### Restore Rules


Found a rule you want back? You have two ways to restore:


- **Single restore** — Open the actions menu on any archived rule and select "Restore" to bring it back instantly
- **Bulk restore** — Select multiple archived rules and use the toolbar button to restore them all at once


Restored rules return to your active rules list and start applying to new syncs immediately.


## Get Started


Head to your rules page and click the **Archived** tab to see your previously deleted rules. Restoring a rule is available now for all destinations that support rules.
