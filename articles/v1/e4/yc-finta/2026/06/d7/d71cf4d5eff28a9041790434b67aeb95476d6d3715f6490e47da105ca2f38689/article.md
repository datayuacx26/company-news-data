# Finta MCP is in Beta

**Finta now speaks the Model Context Protocol. Connect your account to Claude, Cursor, Codex, or any MCP client and ask about your finances in plain English — no exporting, no copy-paste.**
MCP is the open standard that lets AI assistants securely connect to your tools. With the Finta MCP, your assistant can pull straight from the financial data Finta keeps in[storage mode](https://www.finta.io/changelog/introducing-storage-mode) and answer questions about it instantly.


## What you can do


- **Ask in plain English** — "What did I spend on software last month?", "Show my largest transactions this week", "How have my balances trended this year?"
- **Read across your whole picture** — accounts and balances, transactions and categories, balance history, holdings, and investment activity.
- **Tidy up as you go** — have your assistant categorize transactions and manage your spending categories for you.
- **Use it where you work** — Claude, Cursor, Codex, VS Code Copilot, and any MCP-compatible client.
- **Stay secure** — you sign in with OAuth, Finta never moves money, and you choose which accounts to share.


## Get started


Open **Settings → MCP** in Finta and enable it — that turns on storage mode and backfills your connections automatically. Then add Finta to your AI client using the connection guide.
The[MCP docs](https://docs.finta.io/mcp/overview) walk through setup for each client and list everything you can ask.
MCP is in **beta** — we'd love your feedback as you try it. Tell us what works, what's confusing, and what you want it to do next.
