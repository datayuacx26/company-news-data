Let’s face it: helping portfolio companies fundraise is messy. Whether it’s scattered spreadsheets, outdated CRMs, or siloed email chains, early-stage companies spend way too much time and energy managing a process that should be seamless. And for VCs trying to help? That mess impacts speed, transparency, and ultimately fund outcomes like returns to limited partners.


Enter Finta’s latest game-changer: the **“Powered by Finta”** program. A tailored solution for venture capital firms to give their portfolio companies a head start on fundraising from day one.


This isn’t just another SaaS perk or partnerships page; it’s a full-blown fundraising operating system that VCs can plug right into their portfolio’s workflow.

## **What is “Powered by Finta”?**


“Powered by Finta” is a partnership program that allows accelerators, funds, and entrepreneurship centers to offer Finta to all of their portfolio companies at once. Giving founders structured access to warm introductions, shared networks from their existing investors, and deal execution tools, while giving partners visibility and making it easy to be value add.


- Offer portfolio companies structured access to your network and a clean way to request warm intros.
- Give portfolio companies access to Finta’s full fundraising suite
- Provide structured fundraising workflows, complete with a live CRM, easy investor update drafting, and Finta’s Aurora AI
- Help founders generate data rooms and track real-time investor insights.


VC's can embed Finta’s infrastructure into their fund, offering high-leverage support without having to building internal tooling, complicated airtables, or ops workflows from scratch.

## **How Aurora AI Executes the Work**


Let’s zoom in on the real engine powering this experience:[Aurora AI](https://www.trustfinta.com/products/aurora-ai) .


Unlike other fundraising tools that simply organize your data, Aurora actually acts on it. Here's what it does behind the scenes to save founders dozens of hours:


### **Searching for Recommended Leads**


Aurora taps into Finta’s investor network and your CRM data to identify ideal investor matches filtered by stage, sector, geography, and previous investment activity.


### **Deep Researching Contacts**


Before a founder even reaches out, Aurora gathers deep research on each contact — past deals, recent tweets, portfolio preferences — so outreach is informed and relevant.


### **Highlighting Warm Intro Pathways**


Forget cold emails. Aurora scans shared connections across founders, investors, and even other portfolio companies to suggest warm intro paths automatically.


### **Drafting Hyper-Personalized Emails**


Using all that research, Aurora drafts ready-to-send, tailored outreach emails that sound like they were written by the founder, down to the tone, industry jargon, and deal ask.


### **Automating Pipeline Workflows**


From logging investor interactions to updating stages and sending reminders, Aurora keeps the CRM automatically updated. Founders focus on closing, not clicking. In short? Aurora isn’t a passive dashboard; it’s a proactive fundraising assistant that gets work done.
