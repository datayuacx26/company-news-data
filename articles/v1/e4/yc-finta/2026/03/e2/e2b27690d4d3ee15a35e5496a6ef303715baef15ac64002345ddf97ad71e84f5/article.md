Finta is pushing the frontier of workflow automation once again as the “cursor for capital”. Last week, we launched new AI model integrations for[Aurora AI](https://www.trustfinta.com/products/aurora-ai) , our agentic assistant for startups. These upgrades bring the latest and greatest from OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, and Google Gemini straight into your workflows!


Whether you're researching prospects, sending emails, or navigating a deal closing, Aurora is now smarter, faster, and more versatile than ever before.


With the addition of:


- OpenAI’s GPT 5.2
- Anthropic Claude’s Opus 4.5
- Google Gemini’s 2.5 Pro & 2.5 Flash


Aurora now gives you the power of multiple best-in-class AI models right at your fingertips.


## **What is Finta’s Aurora AI?**


Before we dig into the juicy model updates, here’s a quick refresher:


[Aurora AI](https://www.trustfinta.com/products/aurora-ai) is Finta’s intelligent assistant designed to handle complex workflow tasks, from sourcing capital to automating investor relations and managing deal documentation. Think of Aurora as your co-pilot for running capital raising and deal making operations at scale.


With the power of AI + workflows, Aurora helps startups cut down busy work, stay investor-ready, and build with more velocity.


## **What’s New? Meet the New AI Model Lineup in Aurora**


Here’s the big news: Finta just dropped support for five new AI models, available now in Aurora. Let's break 'em down.


### **1. OpenAI GPT 4.1**


**Best for:** Complex reasoning, memory across tasks


With 4.1, Aurora can now handle more complex conversations and remember previous interactions. That means your assistant now gets smarter the more you work with it.


Think multi-step tasks like generating investment memos, cap table analysis, and founder prep.


### **2. OpenAI GPT 5.2**


**Best for:** Overall performance, most capable language model


This is OpenAI’s most advanced model yet. GPT-5.2 excels at nuanced understanding, creativity, and logic. Aurora taps this for high-performance tasks that require top-tier accuracy and contextual awareness.


Use cases: Investor Q&A, deep financial modeling, strategic planning prompts.


### **3. Anthropic Claude Opus 4.5**


**Best for:** Coding tasks, agentic workflows, long-form reasoning


Anthropic’s Claude 4.5 is known for its calm, thoughtful responses and handling of agentic tasks — perfect for when Aurora is coordinating across documents or aligning stakeholder communications.


Ideal for: Smart data synthesis, code generation, team collaboration tools.


### **4. Google Gemini 2.5 Pro**


**Best for:** Research and analysis


Google’s models are a research powerhouse, pulling in structured logic and insights quickly. With this under the hood, Aurora can conduct research tasks, summarize market data, or analyze legal docs with laser focus.


Sweet spot: Competitive benchmarking, term sheet analysis, investor due diligence.


### **5. Google Gemini 2.5 Flash**


**Best for:** Speed-optimized responses


Flash is your go-to for snappy answers and fast turnaround. While it’s lighter than the Pro version, it’s perfect for day-to-day tasks that don’t need deep reasoning.


Great for: Quick updates, meeting summaries, and fast email drafts.

With these integrations, Aurora AI now gives you the flexibility to choose the right model for the right task, in real-time.
