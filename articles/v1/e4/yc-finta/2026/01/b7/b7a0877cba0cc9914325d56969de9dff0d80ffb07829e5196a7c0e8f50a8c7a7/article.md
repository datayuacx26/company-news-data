## Our Proven Three-Phase Playbook


### Phase 1 – Prepare the Deal


We help you clean up your cap table and finalize round terms, craft a magnetic narrative and bulletproof pitch deck, and build a professional-grade data room complete with financial reviews by seasoned experts.


### Phase 2 – Prospect Investors


Our team curates 150+ thesis-aligned investors, mapping out warm introduction pathways. We enrich each investor profile with detailed research and insights to develop tailored outreach that drives contacts to the next stage of the funnel.


### Phase 3 – Execute the Round


Every week, we meet to collaborate closely with you to manage targeted outreach and warm introduction requests directly from your inbox. We handle scheduling investor meetings, capturing feedback, continuously refining your pitch, managing due diligence requests, securing commitments, and meticulously tracking every activity in Finta.
