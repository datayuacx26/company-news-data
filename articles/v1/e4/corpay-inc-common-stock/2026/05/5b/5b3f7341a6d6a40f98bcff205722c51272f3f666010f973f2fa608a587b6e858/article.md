# Corpay and Dooap Partner to Deliver Integrated AP Automation and Vendor Payments for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations

**Corpay and Dooap Partner to Deliver Integrated AP Automation and Vendor Payments for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations**


**ATLANTA, GA and AUSTIN, TX – May, 12, 2026** – Corpay, a global leader in business payments, today announced a new partnership with Dooap to help organizations using Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations modernize accounts payable AP with integrated invoice automation and vendor payment capabilities.


The partnership brings together Corpay’s payment automation technology with Dooap’s AP automation solution, which is purpose-built for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations (F&O). Together, the companies will help finance teams simplify invoice-to-pay workflows, reduce manual work, improve visibility, and create a more connected AP experience within the Microsoft ecosystem.


Dooap is an AI-powered AP automation solution built specifically for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance, enabling organizations to manage invoice capture, approval, and processing. By structuring and controlling the full invoice workflow, Dooap creates the foundation for a seamless transition into integrated vendor payments. Corpay extends this workflow with integrated vendor payment capabilities designed to help businesses streamline payment execution, improve reconciliation, and strengthen control across the payment lifecycle. For organizations paying both domestic and international suppliers, Corpay also helps extend that efficiency across cross-border payments, supporting global disbursements and helping teams manage payment complexity within a more unified workflow.


“At Corpay, we’re focused on helping businesses automate payments in ways that work naturally with the systems they already use,” said **Lindsey Kokosh, VP, VAR Partner Channel & Program.** “Dooap brings deep expertise in Microsoft Dynamics 365 F&O and a purpose-built approach to AP automation. Together, we’re helping finance teams reduce manual effort, improve visibility, and get more value from their Microsoft investment.”


The partnership is designed for organizations that want a more seamless connection between invoice workflows and vendor payments for Microsoft Dynamics 365 F&O. By combining Dooap’s AP automation capabilities with Corpay’s payment technology, customers can create a more efficient, integrated approach to managing invoices, approvals, payments, and reporting.


“We built Dooap to deliver an AI-powered AP experience for Microsoft Dynamics 365 F&O customers,” said Mikko Hytönen, Chief Executive Officer of Dooap. “By partnering with Corpay, we’re extending that experience beyond invoice automation into payments—giving finance teams a single, connected workflow from invoice capture to payment execution within D365 Finance.”


Together, Corpay and Dooap will support organizations looking to modernize AP without disrupting the ERP environment their teams already rely on. The result is a more connected invoice-to-payment process that helps finance teams move faster and operate more efficiently.


**About Corpay** Corpay, Inc. (NYSE: CPAY) is a global leader in business payments, helping organizations control expenses and make payments more safely and securely through modern technology. Corpay’s solutions support accounts payable automation, payment execution, and cross-border payments, helping businesses simplify operations, improve visibility, and manage domestic and international supplier payments more effectively.


**About Dooap** Dooap is an AI-powered AP automation solution built specifically for Microsoft Dynamics 365 F&O. Dooap enables finance teams to streamline invoice processing, improve visibility and control, and deliver a more efficient, user-friendly AP experience for its customers.
