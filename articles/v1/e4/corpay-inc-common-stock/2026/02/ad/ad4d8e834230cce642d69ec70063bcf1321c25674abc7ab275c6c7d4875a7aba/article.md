# Corpay Partners with Healthie

## Corpay Partners with Healthie to Support Modern Healthcare Delivery


## Enabling simpler billing and payments for healthcare organizations


ATLANTA, GA and NEW YORK, NY — February 26. 2026 — Corpay, a global leader in business payments, is excited to announce its strategic partnership with Healthie, a leading infrastructure platform for virtual-first and collaborative healthcare delivery.


Through this partnership, healthcare organizations using Healthie’s platform gain access to Corpay’s payment and spend management solutions, helping simplify billing and payments, improve visibility and control, and reduce friction between care delivery and financial workflows.


“We’re incredibly excited to partner with Healthie and support the next generation of healthcare delivery,” said Joshua Gilbert, Director of Strategic Sales at Corpay. “By combining Healthie’s innovative, API-first platform with Corpay’s payment and spend expertise, we’re helping providers simplify financial workflows, reduce friction between care and billing, and stay focused on what matters most — delivering exceptional patient care.”


An S&P 500 company, Corpay specializes in intelligent spend management solutions, including AP automation and commercial card programs, helping organizations modernize invoice-to-pay, strengthen controls, and mitigate payment fraud risk at scale.


### About Healthie


Healthie provides infrastructure to power the next generation of healthcare delivery. Healthie's API-first EHR, Scheduling, and Engagement solution, coupled with the Healthie Harbor — a marketplace of integrations — makes it easy for organizations of any size to launch and scale virtual care. Healthie was designed for virtual-first, longitudinal, and collaborative care.


Healthie supports thousands of organizations that work with millions of patient lives, including industry-leading health tech companies. Leveraging Healthie, organizations can focus on creating best-in-class patient care experiences. To learn more, visit[https://www.gethealthie.com/](https://www.gethealthie.com/)


### About Corpay


Corpay (NYSE: CPAY) is a global S&P 500 corporate payments company that helps organizations modernize AP automation, manage spend, and pay vendors securely across ACH, virtual card, and check. Through its unified spend management platform, Corpay Complete, Corpay offers solutions including Payments Automation, Invoice Automation, Procure-to-Pay, Expense Management, and Commercial Card programs.


These solutions help finance teams streamline invoice-to-pay, improve visibility and control, mitigate fraud risk, and reduce overall costs. An S&P 500 and Fortune 1000 company, Corpay processes 1.9 billion transactions annually and is the #1 B2B commercial Mastercard® issuer in North America.
