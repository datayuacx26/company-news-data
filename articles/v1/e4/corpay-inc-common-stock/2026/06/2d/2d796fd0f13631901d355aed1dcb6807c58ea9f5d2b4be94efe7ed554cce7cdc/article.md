# Corpay Announces Strategic Partnership with Enterprise Purchasing Group

**Corpay Announces Strategic Partnership with Enterprise Purchasing Group**


New offering enables members to pair best-in-class payments with preferred shipping rates to drive savings, extend terms, and strengthen reporting.


**ATLANTA** , **GA** **and** **ORANGE COUNTY, CA** — **(06/23/2026)** —Corpay, a global leader in business payments, is excited to share news of its strategic partnership with Enterprise Purchasing Group.


"We are thrilled and excited to be adding Corpay as our preferred partner for Corporate Payments and Cash Back Credit Cards. Corpay is the #1 commercial Mastercard® issuer in North America and brings a full suite of payment and spend controls that we know will benefit epg members. Our members can now pair an industry-best payment solution with an industry-best UPS shipping contract to maximize savings, payment terms, and reporting capabilities." says Jordan Zapotechne, Chief Revenue Officer.


In a separate statement, Brad Wegman, Director of Strategic Sales at Corpay, emphasized the partnership’s value for epg members. “Our partnership with Enterprise Purchasing Group is centered on delivering immediate, practical value to their members. By connecting organizations within epg’s network to Corpay’s corporate card solutions, we’re enabling greater visibility, control, and efficiency across their purchasing and payments processes. It’s a focused collaboration that brings innovative financial tools directly to businesses, helping them operate more strategically and drive stronger outcomes,” said Wegman.


**About Enterprise Purchasing Group**


Enterprise Purchasing Group (epg) is the world’s leading group purchasing organization for logistics procurement and supply chain optimization. By combining billions in shipping spend with deep industry expertise, epg helps businesses reduce costs, improve performance, and access enterprise-level pricing and incentives. Built specifically for modern businesses, epg enables small and mid-sized companies to compete more effectively by turning logistics into a driver of profitability and growth.


**To learn more visit www.enterprisepurchasing.com**


**About Corpay**


Corpay (NYSE: CPAY) is a global S&P 500 corporate payments company that helps businesses and consumers manage and pay expenses in a simple, controlled manner. Corpay’s suite of modern payment solutions help its customers better manage vehicle-related expenses (e.g. fueling and parking), travel expenses (e.g. hotel bookings) and accounts payable (e.g. paying vendors).


Through its unified spend management platform, Corpay Complete, Corpay offers a range of solutions including Payments Automation, Invoice Automation, Procure-to-Pay, Expense Management, and Commercial Card programs. These solutions empower clients to automate, secure, and streamline business payments while reducing overall costs. An S&P 500 and Fortune 1000 company, Corpay processes 1.9 billion transactions annually and is the #1 B2B commercial Mastercard issuer in North America. This results in our customers saving time and ultimately spending less.
