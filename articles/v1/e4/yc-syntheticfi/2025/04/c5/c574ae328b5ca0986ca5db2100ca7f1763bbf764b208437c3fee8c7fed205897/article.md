# SyntheticFi Supports International Clients When Banks Are Unwilling to Lend

## Banks won’t lend to international clients


Javier Loreto, Director of Private Wealth Management at Divergent Capital Asset Management, is based in Miami, Florida. **His clients include many HNW and UHNW families based in Latin America.** Divergent uses Charles Schwab as the custodian for most of the client assets.


When planning for the complex needs of his international clients, Javier has often looked for liquidity solutions such as Securities-Backed Lending. **While there are quite a few banks that provide Securities-Backed Lending on the Schwab platform, they all limit their product to US residents.** Because most of Javier’s clients are based in Latin America, they don’t qualify despite sizable investments held at Charles Schwab.


> While Javier’s clients qualify for the margin feature at their custodians, the interest rate on custodian margin features is very high, *"it's just so expensive"* .


## Securities-Backed Lending with international coverage


Javier Loreto discovered SyntheticFi through Sora Finance, a lending partner of SyntheticFi. Javier was initially intrigued by the low interest rate that SyntheticFi advertises, but he quickly discovered that SyntheticFi offers much more.

Apparently, they only work with US residents. SyntheticFi filled this gap for us. Not only does SyntheticFi provide unbeatable interest rates; they support almost every country in Latin America."*

## Bring account over from Interactive Brokers


J and M are a married couple and a financial planning client of Javier. They are business owners with an LLC established in the US. Javier has always wanted to upgrade the relationship and manage more of the investments for this household, including the business accounts.


While Javier's firm primarily uses Charles Schwab as custodian, Javier was not able to convince his client to move their business account from Interactive Brokers to Charles Schwab. J and M use the margin feature at Interactive Broker to provide working capital financing for their business, and really values the low margin rates provided by Interactive Brokers.


**Javier used SyntheticFi to convince the client to move from Interactive Brokers to Charles Schwab** , because SyntheticFi allows the client to get a lower cost of financing at Charles Schwab compared to their existing setup at Interactive Brokers.


> *"I was not sure what it would be like to transfer their LLC margin account from Interactive Brokers to Schwab. SyntheticFi helped me move the account to Schwab without needing to liquidate any investments. It was a seamless process and we were able to quickly transfer the margin balance to SyntheticFi."*
