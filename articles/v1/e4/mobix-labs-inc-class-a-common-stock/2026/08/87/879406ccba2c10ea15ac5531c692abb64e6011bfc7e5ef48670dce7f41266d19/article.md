# Mobix Labs Signs Transformational Rare Earths Acquisition Agreement, Expanding National Security Platform

## Mobix Labs Signs Transformational Rare Earths Acquisition Agreement, Expanding National Security Platform


August 13, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.mobixlabs.com/node/9361/pdf)


IRVINE, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 13, 2026--[Mobix Labs, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.mobixlabs.com%2F&esheet=54588705&newsitemid=20260812356788&lan=en-US&anchor=Mobix+Labs%2C+Inc.&index=1&md5=61e06ce3473bbda9d0732e04576d04a3) (Nasdaq:[MOBX](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.mobixlabs.com&esheet=54588705&newsitemid=20260812356788&lan=en-US&anchor=MOBX&index=2&md5=834d778cae227ba1afb23df8aa2e4f0e) ) has signed a definitive agreement to acquire[Special Project Delivery](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.specialprojectdelivery.com&esheet=54588705&newsitemid=20260812356788&lan=en-US&anchor=Special+Project+Delivery&index=3&md5=681110d94e1a5ee07c4855878d7e770c) , expanding Mobix into rare earth elements, critical minerals, energy and strategic U.S. infrastructure — key resources underpinning America’s defense and advanced-technology supply chains.


Mobix is building a broader national security company spanning defense technology, American-built drones, critical resources and infrastructure — positioning the company across multiple high-priority markets central to America’s security and industrial future.
