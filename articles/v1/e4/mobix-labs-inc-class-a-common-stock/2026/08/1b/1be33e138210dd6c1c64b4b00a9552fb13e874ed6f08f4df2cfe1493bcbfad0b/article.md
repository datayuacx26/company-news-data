# Mobix Labs Guides Q4 Revenue Up Approximately 100% Sequentially at Midpoint

## Mobix Labs Guides Q4 Revenue Up Approximately 100% Sequentially at Midpoint


August 17, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.mobixlabs.com/node/9386/pdf)


IRVINE, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 17, 2026--[Mobix Labs, Inc](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.mobixlabs.com&esheet=54590488&newsitemid=20260817633687&lan=en-US&anchor=Mobix+Labs%2C+Inc&index=1&md5=c08bce7c17354ed1d8dedb162d088c01) . (Nasdaq:[MOBX](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.mobixlabs.com&esheet=54590488&newsitemid=20260817633687&lan=en-US&anchor=MOBX&index=2&md5=96eb7d7ba3257e6386bf3f8351d2e411) ) today highlighted its previously announced fiscal fourth-quarter 2026 revenue guidance of $1.4 million to $1.8 million, representing approximately 100% sequential growth at the midpoint.


The Company expects this growth to be driven by its core defense, aerospace and security businesses, including EMI Solutions and RaGE Systems. Mobix Labs believes the strength of its established operating businesses provides a foundation for its broader National Security Matters strategy.
