# Mobix Labs Hosts Strategic Update Call for Investors

## Mobix Labs Hosts Strategic Update Call for Investors


August 6, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.mobixlabs.com/node/9346/pdf)


**Thursday, August 13 at 4:15pm ET**


IRVINE, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 6, 2026--[Mobix Labs, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.mobixlabs.com%2F&esheet=54584777&newsitemid=20260806589748&lan=en-US&anchor=Mobix+Labs%2C+Inc.&index=1&md5=8e7afee91311aa0d8d2e72d3d8fd5f95) (Nasdaq: MOBX) announced today that it will host a strategic update call for its investors on Thursday, August 13, 2026 at 4:15pm ET.


CEO Phil Sansone will discuss Mobix’s recent progress and strategic repositioning to pursue an expanded array of U.S. national security priorities, through the four pillars of its National Security Matters (NSM) Initiative:


- Critical Resources & Advanced Materials,
- Defense, Aerospace & Autonomous Systems,
- Energy, Water & Critical Infrastructure, and
- Digital Infrastructure & Strategic Technologies.


**Call Details**


Date / Time:


Thursday, August 13, 2026 at 4:15pm ET


Live Webcast / Replay:


A link will be available on the Investor Relations section of the Company’s website, and available for 12 months.


**About Mobix Labs, Inc** . ([www.mobixlabs.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.mobixlabs.com&esheet=54584777&newsitemid=20260806589748&lan=en-US&anchor=www.mobixlabs.com&index=2&md5=1d6f016b1b185c06da6c6f6b96e794fe) )


Mobix is building a diversified platform focused on technologies, resources and infrastructure that advance U.S. national-security priorities. Its strategic focus includes critical resources and advanced materials; defense, aerospace and autonomous systems; energy, water and critical infrastructure; and digital infrastructure and strategic technologies. Mobix Labs also provides advanced connectivity, RF, sensing and electromagnetic-interference technologies for aerospace, defense, communications and other high-reliability markets.
