# Unlock the Power of Visualization in Cytogenetic Analysis with VIA™ Software

[Education](https://bionano.com/blog/education/) | 2024-04-10


Application:


[Cytogenomics](https://bionano.com/application/cytogenomics/)


Product:


[VIA Software](https://bionano.com/product/via-software/)


Share this:


In the realm of cytogenetic analysis, visualization stands as the cornerstone, providing essential context for interpretation that transcends digital representation. VIA™ software takes your data’s narrative to new heights, automatically generating a myriad of visuals and data displays to instill confidence and context into variants across the genome.


Witness a seamless and consistent analysis workflow with VIA software, boasting a unified interface and visualization schema for all compatible data sources. For optical genome mapping (OGM) data, VIA elevates the analysis experience with interactive visuals and illustration of variants passing through the intelligent automation-informed filter pipeline. In a single view, capture all potentially significant variants—copy number variants (CNVs), structural variants (SVs), and allelic events—priming your analysis workflow. VIA software presents a whole genome plot for OGM data akin to single nucleotide polymorphism (SNP) microarrays, featuring log intensity and B-Allele frequency measurements for a comprehensive data perspective.


The interactive VIA Circos plot empowers you to select specific chromosomes or events swiftly. By choosing a variant, its details shine in the Results table, but a deeper dive into the Variant Details dashboard unveils a comprehensive assembly of objective information specific to the event type. Whether it’s a CNV or other event type, the dashboard tailors its display, presenting details such as database similarities, overlap with DGV reports, and gene information. Record notes and interpretations effortlessly for a clear record of your analysis team’s declarations, which are preserved for future case analyses.


Critical annotations come to life in VIA’s chromosome browser, allowing you to drill down to single chromosome, gene, and nucleotide levels for profound analysis and visual context. Witness the power of VIA’s visualizations as it databases all copy number events, providing a unique capability to ‘look back’ through your database for similar cases, offering crucial context for interpretation.


Navigate effortlessly with VIA software—jump from chromosome to chromosome, zoom in/out, and tailor the display to your preferences. The fully customizable interface, saved as a ‘View,’ allows you to set up multiple perspectives effortlessly. Explore different views, compare data, and gain insights with VIA’s powerful visualizations, including an ability to link multiple datasets together or generate a composite view that stacks results for up to 20 samples in a single comprehensive display.


Don’t miss out on the wealth of insights hidden in your data. VIA software transforms your screen into a hub of relevant data, information, and context, providing a single-screen solution for greater understanding and confidence in your results. Elevate your analysis with VIA software.
