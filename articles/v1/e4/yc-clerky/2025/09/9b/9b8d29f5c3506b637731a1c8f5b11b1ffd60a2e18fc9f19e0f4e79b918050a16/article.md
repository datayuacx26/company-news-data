# Release Notes: Passkeys, signup bonuses, and more perks

Now available for Clerky startups: stronger security, bigger signup bonuses from banking platforms, and new and updated perks from top founder tools.


#### **Passkeys**


You can now use passkeys for[two-factor authentication (2FA)](https://www.clerky.com/blog/passkeys-for-2fa) on Clerky.


#### Banking signup bonuses


- [Arc](https://x.com/clerkyinc/status/1970536119155753334) **:** Get a $1,650 bonus when you deposit $20K, or a $4,000 bonus when you deposit $375k (both maintained for 90 days).
- [Brex](https://x.com/clerkyinc/status/1963328201658437871) : Up to 150,000 points. Get 100,000 points after depositing $20K, plus 50,000 points after a $10K spend on the Brex card.
- ‍[Mercury](https://x.com/clerkyinc/status/1849133819519266882) : Up to $1,500 total. Get a $1,000 bonus for depositing $20K, plus $500 bonus for $10K spend on the IO card (both within 90 days).
- ‍[Rho](https://x.com/clerkyinc/status/1899153707549491520) : $1,600 bonus for using Rho as your primary account and maintaining a $20K average daily balance for 90 days.
