Most mortgage point-of-sale tools collect a 1003 and stop. The work that decides cycle time happens one layer down: reading what the borrower entered, catching the missing document while the borrower is still in the session, and knowing the exact moment intake becomes a TRID application with a three-day disclosure clock attached. Where an AI agent sits in the POS, what it is allowed to decide, and the compliance lines it cannot cross at the front door.
