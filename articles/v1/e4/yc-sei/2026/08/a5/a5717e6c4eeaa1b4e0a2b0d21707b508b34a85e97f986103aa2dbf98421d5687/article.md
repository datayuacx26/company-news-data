# AI on the Collateral Desk: The AVM Quality-Control Rule Now in Effect, the Reconsideration-of-Value Process, and the Appraisal-Independence Line the Agent Cannot Touch

Two things changed the collateral desk in the last two years: the interagency AVM quality-control rule that took effect October 1, 2025, and the interagency reconsideration-of-value guidance finalized in July 2024. Both put new obligations on how lenders use automated valuations and how they let borrowers challenge an appraisal. An AI agent can run the collateral review and the ROV intake at volume, but appraisal independence draws a hard line around what the agent is allowed to do to a valuation. Where the agent sits, and where it has to stop.
