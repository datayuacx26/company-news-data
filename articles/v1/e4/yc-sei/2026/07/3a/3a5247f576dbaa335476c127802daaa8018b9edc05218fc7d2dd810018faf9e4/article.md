The Loan Originator Compensation rule at Reg Z 1026.36 prohibits paying an individual loan originator based on the terms of the loan, and it defines 'loan originator' broadly enough to swallow the AI vendor conversation. The person-definition analysis, the compensation-attribution question that a per-loan vendor fee raises, and the contract structure that keeps the AI vendor's economics outside the LO Comp perimeter.
