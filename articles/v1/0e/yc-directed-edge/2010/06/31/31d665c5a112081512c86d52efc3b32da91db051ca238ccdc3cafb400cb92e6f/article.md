# Directed Edge delivering 33% of revenue for The Market Quarter

[Jonathan Briggs](http://www.jonathanbriggs.com/) , who runs[The Market Quarter](http://www.marketquarter.com/) wrote a few months back on[getting up and going with our Shopify app](http://www.jonathanbriggs.com/experimenting-with-shopify-product-recommendation-engine-app,803,AR.html) for recommendations. He reported back today after analyzing the first few months of data:


> What astounded me was how many of my customers (not just visitors) clicked on recommendations. Indeed the ExpressRex referrer is responsible for a full third (33%) of my revenue in the last quarter and has a 36% conversion rate.
