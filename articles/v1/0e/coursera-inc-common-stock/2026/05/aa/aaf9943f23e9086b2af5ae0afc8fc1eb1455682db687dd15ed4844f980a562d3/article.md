# Anthropic launches five free courses on Coursera to help build AI fluency

Today, I’m excited to share that Anthropic is launching five free AI courses on Coursera to help learners worldwide build practical AI skills, with specialized content for educators, students, and nonprofit professionals.


As demand for Anthropic’s AI tools continues to grow, the company remains committed to guiding the world through this major technological shift. Accessible AI education is central to that mission, and we’re proud these courses will be available on Coursera for free as Community Impact courses, which expand access to learning on urgent social needs.


This series of free courses is co-taught by Anthropic instructors alongside Professors Rick Dakan and Joseph Feller, who developed the research-backed AI Fluency Framework, and Giving Tuesday. For the best experience, start with the AI Fluency: Framework and Foundations course before exploring how to apply the framework in real-world industry settings.


### [Anthropic AI Fluency: Framework and Foundations](https://www.coursera.org/learn/ai-fluency-framework-foundations)


Beginner level · 3 hours


Skills you’ll build: AI literacy, Human-AI collaboration, Generative AI


- **[AI Fluency: Framework and Foundations](https://www.coursera.org/learn/ai-fluency-framework-foundations) :** Apply the 4D framework to hands-on projects, and learn how to collaborate more effectively and ethically with AI.
- **[AI Fluency for Educators](https://www.coursera.org/learn/ai-fluency-for-educators) :** Use AI to design courses, create reliable teaching materials, and develop authentic assessments that are grounded in your pedagogy.
- **[Teaching AI Fluency](https://www.coursera.org/learn/teaching-ai-fluency)** : Prepare students for an AI-first workforce by integrating AI into your curriculum and designing assessments that showcase real-world application. You’ll also learn how to better manage AI-enhanced student work.
- **[AI Fluency for Students](https://www.coursera.org/learn/ai-fluency-for-students) :** Leverage AI as a thought partner to enhance learning and career growth while strengthening, not replacing, your critical thinking and creativity.
- **[AI Fluency for Nonprofits](https://www.coursera.org/learn/ai-fluency-for-nonprofits) :** Advance your organization’s mission and improve efficiency across fundraising, outreach, communications, and operations using AI.


With today’s launch, Anthropic is nearly doubling its[course catalog](https://www.coursera.org/partners/anthropic) on Coursera, expanding beyond Claude training to empower learners with broader AI fluency skills.


We’re proud to partner with one of the world’s leading AI companies to make essential AI fluency skills more accessible to learners around the world.
