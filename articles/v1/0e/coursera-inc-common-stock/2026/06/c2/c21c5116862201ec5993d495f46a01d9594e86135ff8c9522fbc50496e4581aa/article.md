# Coursera expands micro-credential catalog with new programs from Google DeepMind, Meta, Microsoft, and more

Today, alongside the release of our[2026 Micro-Credential Impact Report](https://blog.coursera.org/coursera-micro-credentials-impact-report-2026/) , I’m pleased to share 10 new micro-credentials on Coursera from leading university and industry partners around the world.


These credentials play an increasingly important role in the job market. Notably, our new report found that 87% of graduates secure a job in their field, and 94% of employers say they’d offer a higher starting salary to a micro-credential holder.


With micro-credential enrollments up nearly 50% over the last year, partners are responding with new Professional Certificates and Specializations that combine role-specific training with durable skills.


## **New Professional Certificates from HRCI, IBM, MathWorks, Meta, and Microsoft**


Designed by industry leaders, Professional Certificates help learners prepare for specific jobs with hands-on training. Over the last year, Coursera’s Professional Certificate catalog has grown more than 20%, creating new pathways into high-demand fields like AI, business analysis, and engineering.


**Here are the newest Professional Certificates on Coursera:**


- [HRCI Associate Professional in Human Resources-International](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/hrci-human-resource-associate-international) ***:** Develop HR skills in policy, compliance, hiring, onboarding, and employee development while preparing for the aPHRi exam.
- [IBM Man](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/ibm-management-consultant) **[a](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/ibm-management-consultant)**[gement Consultant](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/ibm-management-consultant) ***:** Analyze business challenges, create financial models, design process improvements, and visualize data to produce client-ready assets for your portfolio.
- [MathWorks Electrified Systems Design Engineer](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/electrified-systems-design-engineer) **:** Use MATLAB and Simulink to model electrified systems, simulate power electronics, test motors, and design real-world battery systems.
- [Meta Community Management](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/meta-community-management) ***:** Create engagement strategies, moderate online communities with community guidelines, use KPIs to shape decisions, and leverage AI to work more efficiently.
- [Managing AI Projects with Microsoft](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/microsoft-managing-ai-projects-with-microsoft) **:** Turn AI initiatives into structured project plans using Agile to coordinate teams, RAID tracking to manage risk, and Azure tools to move projects into production.


**Entry-level Professional Certificates require no prior experience and prepare learners for entry-level jobs.*
