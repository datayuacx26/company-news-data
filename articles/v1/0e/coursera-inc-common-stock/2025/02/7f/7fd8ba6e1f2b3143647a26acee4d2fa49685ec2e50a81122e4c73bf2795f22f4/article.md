## What is blockchain?


[Blockchains](https://www.coursera.org/articles/blockchain-technology) are specialized types of databases that are used to store and organize data. Traditional databases use rows and columns that create tables, while blockchains consolidate data into clusters or blocks with limited storage capacity.


Once the capacity is reached, the block will seal itself and establish a chain or connection with the previous block via[cryptography](https://www.coursera.org/articles/cryptographer) . Cryptography creates an immutable timestamp or record when a block links to another, confirming the accuracy of the information. Generally, a traditional database is under a central authority that manages and maintains an organization’s sensitive information.


## What is a blockchain engineer?


A blockchain engineer designs and creates the architecture for decentralized applications used on the blockchain, including network integration and security. Decentralized applications are software programs that aren’t controlled by a single authority. Blockchain engineers typically work for businesses and organizations, such as technology consulting firms or data-holding companies, to create blockchain applications.


### What does a blockchain engineer do?


The tasks and responsibilities of a blockchain engineer may vary depending on where you work, the company you work for, and the applications you’re building. However, some common examples include:


-


Understanding, analyzing, and creating blockchain networks and decentralized applications to run on them


-


Communicating with organizations and businesses to understand client needs and existing infrastructure


-


Testing networks, systems, and applications, and troubleshooting any errors


-


Using cryptography to encrypt systems and secure data


-


Managing teams of blockchain engineers


-


Scheduling application launches and dates for release


-


Keeping up to date with relevant tools and trends to use


-


Handling backend maintenance and maintenance of applications and networks


## Blockchain engineer salary


According to Glassdoor, the median total pay for a blockchain engineer in the US is $152,000 annually \[[1](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/blockchain-engineer-salary-SRCH_KO0,19.htm) \]. This figure includes a base salary of $90,000 to $146,000 and $29,000 to 53,000 in additional pay \[[1](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/blockchain-engineer-salary-SRCH_KO0,19.htm) \]. Additional pay may represent profit-sharing, commissions, bonuses, or other benefits. Remember that your location, experience level, and industry may influence your salary.


### Blockchain developer vs. blockchain engineer


A blockchain engineer and a[blockchain developer](https://www.coursera.org/articles/blockchain-developer) are different but are similar roles that often work together. Blockchain engineers are in charge of the overall architecture and execution of a blockchain application, while blockchain developers work on developing the code that underpins the application.


Both roles require similar skills. For example, it's necessary for a blockchain engineer to understand coding and programming to analyze code and train others.

## How to become a blockchain engineer


To become a blockchain engineer, you must prove you have the required skills and knowledge. This can be through a combination of education, certifications, and experience. The first step is understanding how blockchain works and knowing how to program it.


As a blockchain engineer, you’ll have the option to work in a range of industries, including health care, supply chain, finance, and tech companies.


### Do you need a degree to be a blockchain engineer?


Employers typically require at least a[bachelor’s degree](https://www.coursera.org/degrees/bachelors) to become a blockchain engineer. Relevant majors include computer science, information systems, or engineering. If you want to stand out as a job applicant or have a higher earning potential, consider obtaining a[master’s degree](https://www.coursera.org/degrees/masters) .


### Steps to become a blockchain engineer


To become a blockchain engineer, you’ll need to build the relevant skills, experience, education, and certifications. Here are some steps to take:


1.


**Learn the blockchain basics:**[Study blockchain](https://www.coursera.org/articles/how-to-learn-blockchain) , understand its terminology, and understand its uses. This can be done with a degree, self-study material, or an[online course](https://www.coursera.org/learn/blockchain-basics) .


2.


**Learn how to code and program:** These are essential skills for working with blockchain, and you can develop them through boot camps, a degree, online courses, or self-study.


3.


**Earn a degree:** A degree in blockchain,[computer science](https://www.coursera.org/degrees/computer-science) ,[information systems,](https://www.coursera.org/articles/information-systems-vs-information-technology)[engineering](https://www.coursera.org/collections/degrees-in-engineering) , or similar topics provides experience and hands-on projects to help you build the necessary skills to work as a blockchain engineer.


4.


**Gain experience:** Practical experience is key to demonstrating your skills. It’s essential that you can show an employer you have experience, whether that is through a paid position, personal projects, or hackathons and boot camps.


5.


**Get a blockchain engineer certification:** Gaining a professional certification can give you access to niche skills not included in general[degree](https://www.coursera.org/degrees) coursework. These courses develop your understanding of various blockchain platforms and how to use them in business.


### Blockchain engineer certification

**Certified Blockchain and Finance Professional** by Blockchain Council teaches learners how to create blockchain solutions that influence aspects of finance.
