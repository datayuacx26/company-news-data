## 10 top companies to work for remotely


Increasingly, companies that once offered remote work have instead required employees to return to the office full-time or on a hybrid schedule. However, it's still possible to find a[high-paying remote job](https://www.coursera.org/articles/highest-paying-remote-jobs) . Learn more about remote-first companies, or companies that allow employees to choose the best working environment to meet their needs.


### 1. Dropbox


Dropbox is a cloud storage and file synchronization service that allows users to store, share, and collaborate on files and folders across devices and with others. Dropbox is a "virtual first" company, though employees also have access to Dropbox offices or flexible working spaces.


### 2. Figma


Figma is a cloud-based design and prototyping tool that enables teams to collaboratively create, test, and iterate on user interface designs for websites, mobile apps, and other digital products in real-time. With worldwide office locations, Figma employees have the option to work from a nearby hub or remotely.


### 3. CrowdStrike


CrowdStrike is a remote-first cybersecurity technology company that provides cloud-based endpoint security, threat intelligence, and cyberattack response services. The company is remote-first.


### 4. HubSpot


HubSpot is a developer and marketer of software products for inbound marketing, sales, and customer service. More than 70 percent of HubSpot's employees work from home (called "@home") \[[1](https://www.hubspot.com/careers/remote) \].


### 5. Brightside Health


Brightside Health is a telehealth company that provides online mental health services, including therapy and medication management for depression and anxiety. Brightside offers both corporate and clinical remote positions.


### 6. Nvidia


A leader in AI and[computer graphics](https://www.coursera.org/articles/computer-graphics) technology, Nvidia is reportedly a remote-friendly company. While not everyone works remotely, certain teams are designated as being remote if it makes sense for their work.


### 7. Reddit


Reddit is a social news aggregation, web content rating, and discussion platform where registered members submit content such as links, text posts, images, and videos. Reddit allows employees to decide on the best working model for their needs.


### 8. Pinterest


Pinterest is a visual discovery engine and social media platform that allows users to find, save, and share images and videos. Pinterest employees are encouraged to pick the working style that suits them best.


### 9. Okta


Okta is a remote, cloud-based identity and access management company that provides a platform for secure user authentication, multi-factor authentication, and single sign-on services, helping organizations manage and secure user access to various applications and systems across cloud and on-premises environments.


### 10. Articulate


Articulate is a software company that develops e-learning authoring tools and platforms. The company has always been remote.


### 12. Pearson


Pearson is a multinational publishing and education company that provides a wide range of educational products and services, including textbooks, digital learning tools, and assessment services. Employees at Pearson have the option to work in a way that best suits them.


### 13. Twilio


Twilio is a cloud communications platform as a service (CPaaS) company that provides developers with APIs to integrate various communication channels—including voice, video, messaging, and email—into their applications. Twilio is remote-first.


## What to look for in a remote company


Working remotely is more than being able to do your job from home. For this particular model to be successful, it often requires a company that understands how to support a distributed workforce, enabling employees to be successful with the right virtual tools and processes.


Evaluating if a particular remote-first company is a good fit for you involves considering some of the factors outlined below:


-


**Company culture and support:** A strong remote culture provides employees with the tools, processes, and opportunities for virtual collaboration, as well as a sense of inclusion, support, and growth.


-


**Employee benefits:** Beyond traditional benefits, such as health coverage, some remote-first companies offer specific remote benefits, including home office stipends and internet reimbursement.


-


**Flexibility:** Companies that not only allow employees to work remotely but also offer them scheduling flexibility that reflects any time zone differences and the freedom to work from anywhere can be important.

When applying to a remote-first company or a remote role, it's essential to highlight your ability to work remotely, including your self-motivation and experience with remote-friendly communication tools.

**3. Tailor the cover letter to remote work:** Customize your[cover letter](https://www.coursera.org/articles/how-to-write-a-cover-letter) to reflect your unique ability to thrive in a remote working environment, especially if you can point to past remote work experience.
