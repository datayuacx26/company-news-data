# How to Learn Information Technology

[Information technology](https://www.coursera.org/articles/what-is-information-technology) (IT) has become an integral part of everyday life. Learning about IT can provide personal satisfaction and a greater ability to navigate technology. However, if you want to pursue a[job in IT](https://www.coursera.org/articles/how-to-get-a-job-in-it) , you must cultivate specific IT-related skills.


Identifying these skills and knowing how to acquire them can help you start your IT career path. Use this guide as a starting point to explore the skills you’ll need and how to develop them.


###


## Essential IT skills


IT skills refer to the[technical abilities](https://www.coursera.org/articles/what-are-technical-skills) that professionals within the field need to complete their tasks. Although skills may vary from role to role, the following are standard skills to have to be successful in this career:


### Cloud computing


[Cloud computing](https://www.coursera.org/articles/what-is-cloud-computing) involves using hosted services delivered via the internet by a cloud services provider. Types of cloud services include:


-


[Applications](https://www.coursera.org/in/articles/components-of-cloud-computing)


-


[Computer software](https://www.coursera.org/articles/software-engineer)


-


[Databases](https://www.coursera.org/articles/what-is-database)


-


[Data storage](https://www.coursera.org/articles/big-data-storage)


-


[Networking](https://www.coursera.org/articles/network-security-engineer)


-


[Servers](https://www.coursera.org/articles/server-certifications)


Knowing how to work in cloud platforms like Google Cloud and[Amazon Web Services](https://www.coursera.org/articles/aws-certification) (AWS) can help you become more marketable in the IT career field. Demand for employees with these skills continues to grow because of the expanding need for cloud computing services.


###


### Coding


[Coding](https://www.coursera.org/articles/learning-how-to-code) involves using a language to write code, allowing you to “communicate” with the computer and create programs. IT professionals typically have experience with at least one programming language, which includes popular options like:


-


[C++](https://www.coursera.org/articles/what-is-c-plus-plus)


-


HTML


-


Java


-


[JavaScript](https://www.coursera.org/articles/java-vs-javascript)


-


PHP


-


[Python](https://www.coursera.org/articles/what-is-python-used-for-a-beginners-guide-to-using-python)


-


[Ruby](https://www.coursera.org/articles/ruby-vs-python)


### Data analytics


[Data analytics](https://www.coursera.org/articles/data-analytics) involves using computational techniques and tools to transform raw data into something useful. Through analysis, users gain insights about data, including the relationships and patterns among different data points. Data analysis offers various benefits. For instance, helping businesses boost profits, improving workflows, and enhancing decision-making.


###


### Data visualization


[Data visualization](https://www.coursera.org/articles/data-visualization) involves presenting data in a way that people can understand. Tools for helping people visualize data include charts, infographics, maps, and scatter plots. Employees with[data visualization skills](https://www.coursera.org/articles/data-visualization-jobs) can help employers understand data, resulting in quicker, better decisions.


Data visualization can also help employers:


-


Identify relationships between[data variables](https://www.coursera.org/articles/types-of-data)


-


Share important information with employees or stakeholders


-


Detect general and specific trends (like overall sales and sales per associate)


###


### Generative AI


[Generative AI](https://www.coursera.org/articles/generative-ai-applications) refers to the capacity of artificial intelligence to generate original content like images, text, sounds, or animation based on specific prompts and data. It has proven helpful in many business sectors, including creative arts, entertainment, health care, and manufacturing, marketing.


Employees with knowledge of generative AI and experience with its tools grow increasingly more attractive to employers as this technology evolves. Some of the more common[generative AI tools](https://www.coursera.org/articles/ai-tools-for-work) include:


-


[ChatGPT](https://www.coursera.org/articles/chatgpt) **:** A chatbot created by OpenAI that answers questions, writes content, and more


-


[DALL-E 3](https://www.coursera.org/articles/what-is-dall-e) **:** An AI tool created by OpenAI that creates images from text


-


**MusicGen:** An AI tool created by Meta that transforms text into music


-


[Synthesia](https://www.coursera.org/articles/how-to-use-ai-to-create-videos) **:** An AI tool that creates video from text

### Participate in a coding boot camp.


-


**Format:** Online or in-person


-


**Duration:** Three to six months


A coding[boot camp](https://www.coursera.org/articles/what-are-non-degree-programs) packs a lot of education into a short amount of time compared to an associate or bachelor's degree program. Instead of a theoretical learning approach, you would work through a more project-based curriculum to practice using real skills needed for real jobs. Topics covered in a coding boot camp might include:


-


[Programming languages](https://www.coursera.org/articles/high-level-programming-languages) like[CSS](https://www.coursera.org/articles/css) ,[HTML](https://www.coursera.org/articles/how-to-become-a-web-designer) ,[JavaScript](https://www.coursera.org/articles/java-vs-javascript) ,[Python, and Ruby](https://www.coursera.org/articles/ruby-vs-python)


-


[Application](https://www.coursera.org/articles/application-developer) and[web development](https://www.coursera.org/articles/web-developer-certification)


-


[Computer frameworks](https://www.coursera.org/articles/back-end-developer)


-


[Database management](https://www.coursera.org/articles/database-administrator)


### Learn on your own.

**Format:** Online or in-person

## 3 information technology jobs


To find out how you might put your IT skills to practice, consider three jobs in the field. Learn more about the roles of a[computer systems analyst](https://www.coursera.org/articles/computer-systems-analyst) , a[database administrator](https://www.coursera.org/articles/database-administrator-salary) , and a[web developer](https://www.coursera.org/articles/web-developer-salary) .


### 1. Computer systems analyst


**Median annual US salary:** $103,800 **** \[[1](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/computer-systems-analysts.htm) \]


**Job outlook (2023–2033):** 11 percent annual growth \[[1](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/computer-systems-analysts.htm) \]


**Educational requirements:**[Bachelor's degree in information technology](https://www.coursera.org/articles/how-to-get-into-it-without-a-degree) or related field


As a computer systems analyst, you would evaluate a company's[computer network](https://www.coursera.org/articles/internet-of-things) , systems, and protocols and provide ideas and methods for improvement. Depending on the business field (e.g., engineering, health care, manufacturing), this role may require you to specialize in a specific computer system).


### 2. Database administrator


**Median annual US salary:** $117,450 \[[2](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/database-administrators.htm) \]


**Job outlook (2023–2033):** 9 percent annual growth \[[2](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/database-administrators.htm) \]


**Educational requirements:**[Bachelor's degree in information technology](https://www.coursera.org/articles/it-support-specialist) or related field


As a[database administrator (DBA)](https://www.coursera.org/articles/database-administrator) , you would manage databases to ensure that users have access to needed information. You could also engage in regular database maintenance to ensure proper function.


### 3. Web developer


**Median annual US salary:** $92,750 \[[3](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/web-developers.htm) \]


**Job outlook (2023-2033):** 8 percent annual growth \[[3](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/web-developers.htm) \]


**Educational requirements:** Associate or bachelor's degree or equivalent


As a[web developer,](https://www.coursera.org/articles/web-developer) you create websites using computer coding and programming. You might also ensure that new websites work correctly and efficiently and have visual appeal.
