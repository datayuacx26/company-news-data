# Popular Cities for Cybersecurity Jobs in 2026

An increasing amount of information is stored on digital devices in industries ranging from e-commerce to health care to credit card services. With the rising incidence of[data breaches](https://www.coursera.org/articles/data-security-breach) , protecting that information is paramount. You can take part in building and maintaining security systems with a career in[cybersecurity](https://www.coursera.org/articles/how-to-get-into-cybersecurity) .


Cybersecurity is a growing field; revenue in the industry is likely to grow 10.56 percent per year, reaching $273.6 billion worldwide by 2028 \[[1](https://www.statista.com/outlook/tmo/cybersecurity/worldwide) \]. Read on to discover some of the best cities for cybersecurity jobs and the types of jobs that may be available.

## What is cybersecurity?


[Cybersecurity](https://www.coursera.org/articles/what-is-cyber-security) is the practice of protecting networks, devices, and data to prevent access by individuals who should not have it. This practice ensures the confidentiality, validity, and availability of information.


In many people’s daily lives, technology is ever-present. This is the case, too, with organizations. Businesses often hold sensitive information that they are required by law to protect. Globally, the average cost of a data breach for a company reached $4.88M in 2024, a 10% increase year over year and the highest total ever, according to IBM \[[2](https://www.ibm.com/reports/data-breach?utm_content=SRCWW&p1=Search&p4=43700077724063991&p5=e&gad_source=1&gclid=CjwKCAjwuJ2xBhA3EiwAMVjkVMKAdPVCi3j5RwwTj_beoLen5d0HGlo1wSpmZk67UDAljmd8KQypjhoCYWAQAvD_BwE&gclsrc=aw.ds#:~:text=can%20save%20millions-,USD%204.45%20million,-The%20global%20average) \]. ## What are the best cities for cybersecurity jobs?


Cybersecurity is a growing profession with career opportunities available in cities across the US. Whether you’d like to work in a booming metropolis or a small to midsize community, cybersecurity could offer opportunities in your preferred area.


Explore some popular cities for cybersecurity jobs below, according to Glassdoor and BLS data, as well as an[AdvisorSmith report](https://advisorsmith.com/data/best-cities-for-it-security-analysts) that ranked 181 US cities based on average annual salary, location quotient, cost of living, and size, and CompTIA's[State of the Tech Workforce 2024](https://www.comptia.org/content/research/state-of-the-tech-workforce) report.


### Small cities


#### Idaho Falls, ID


The second-largest city in Idaho outside of the Boise metro area has a lot to offer for cybersecurity professionals. Idaho Falls is a hub for commerce and health care in the eastern part of the state. Idaho National Laboratory and Eastern Idaho Regional Medical Center have headquarters in Idaho Falls, presenting opportunities for cybersecurity jobs. AdvisorSmith, a business information resource, named Idaho Falls among its top cities for cybersecurity careers \[[12](https://advisorsmith.com/data/best-cities-for-it-security-analysts) \].


#### Ithaca, NY


Ithaca, New York, is a small city with affluent prospects for cybersecurity professionals. According to AdvisorSmith, Ithaca ranked seventh among top cities for cybersecurity jobs \[[12](https://advisorsmith.com/data/best-cities-for-it-security-analysts) \]. This city is home to Cornell University and Ithaca College, two schools with respected computer science programs.


### Medium cities


#### Bloomington, IL


Bloomington, a city about two hours from Chicago, is a hub for tech jobs. Companies such as State Farm Insurance and COUNTRY Financial are headquartered here, and 74.862 per 1,000 residents work in the tech sector \[[13](https://www.bls.gov/oes/current/oes_14010.htm#15-0000) \]. AdvisorSmith selected Bloomington as the top overall city for IT security analysts \[[12](https://advisorsmith.com/data/best-cities-for-it-security-analysts) \].


#### Huntsville, AL


Many companies (Invariant Corporation, Nippon Express, Torch Technologies, and more) call Huntsville home, opening opportunities for cybersecurity professionals. Additionally, the significant presence of military and government contractors in Huntsville makes it an ideal center for IT careers.


#### Trenton, NJ


New Jersey ranks number one in broadband connectivity and number two in fastest download speeds, according to CompTIA. A strong talent pool supports the cybersecurity industry in Trenton, with the prestigious Princeton University in the city and a high education level—42 percent of Trenton’s workforce holds a bachelor’s degree or higher \[[14](https://www.comptia.org/content/research/best-tech-cities-it-jobs) \]. Trenton’s tech workforce will grow by 223 jobs in 2024, CompTIA projects \[[17](https://www.comptia.org/content/research/state-of-the-tech-workforce) \].


#### San Jose, CA


San Jose is the largest city in Silicon Valley, and tech jobs are plentiful, with 141.7 tech workers per 1,000 employees \[[15](https://www.bls.gov/oes/current/oes_41940.htm#15-0000) \]. Silicon Valley is one of the country's most important hubs of technology innovation and entrepreneurship. This epicenter for IT careers has remained one of the top five areas for tech jobs, thanks to the thousands of start-up companies that operate here.


#### Boulder, CO


Boulder has a median annual salary of $75,396 for IT jobs, and the city supports technology-focused companies \[[16](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/boulder-information-technology-salary-SRCH_IL.0,7_IM110_KO8,30.htm) \]. More than 17,000 tech workers live in the area. Cisco, IBM, Fanatics, CA Technologies, and Teamsnap all chose to base their operations in Boulder.


#### Austin, TX


Austin is a growing city that's highly ranked for its cost of living and wages for tech workers. It's also home to a growing cybersecurity industry. Employers in Austin advertised more than 45,000 IT job postings in 2023, according to CompTIA \[[17](https://www.comptia.org/content/research/state-of-the-tech-workforce) \]. IT jobs in Austin have a median salary of $81,925 yearly \[[18](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/austin-it-salary-SRCH_IL.0,6_IM60_KO7,9.htm) \].

## How to begin your job search


The beginning of a job search is an ideal time to reflect on your values and aspirations. Considering your career goals, skills, and experience can help you choose an area to look for a job in.


### Decide on your ideal location and type of industry.


Finding the perfect city to call home involves a lot of thought and consideration. Each location you explore for a new job will include advantages and disadvantages. If you're planning to relocate for a cybersecurity job, keep the following factors in mind:


-


**Lifestyle:** Consider the size and feel of the city. Do you crave the energy and variety of a bustling metropolis or the peace and quiet of a small town? Suburbs offer a middle ground.


-


**Climate:** Do you prefer sunshine and warmth year-round or distinct seasons with cooler winters? Think about how the weather will affect your day-to-day life and outdoor hobbies.


-


**Activities and culture:** What are you passionate about? Does the city offer museums, theaters, and events that align with your interests? Are there opportunities for hiking, biking, or exploring nature?


-


**Cost of living:** Research housing costs, groceries, transportation, and taxes. Can you comfortably live on your expected income in this city?


-


**Commute:** How important is a short commute? When considering different areas, factor in public transportation options, traffic patterns, and average commute times.

### Review your qualifications and skills.


Information security analysts typically hold bachelor’s degrees in majors such as computer and information technology. A background in topics such as engineering or mathematics can be helpful in cybersecurity jobs.


### Consider earning a certification.


Certifications can affirm your skills and provide an advantage when applying for cybersecurity jobs. You may consider certification to boost your chances for employment in cybersecurity or take a step toward specializing in a particular area of the profession.


[Security+](https://www.coursera.org/articles/what-is-the-comptia-security-plus-certification) is a common certification that you can earn through CompTIA. It establishes baseline skills and can help an entry-level cybersecurity professional learn more about cybersecurity work. For more experienced professionals, the[Certified Information Systems Security Professional](https://www.coursera.org/articles/cissp) (CISSP) validates experience with managing cybersecurity systems.
