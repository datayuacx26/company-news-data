# Atlantic Union Bankshares Corporation Declares Quarterly Common Stock Dividend and Preferred Stock Dividend

# Atlantic Union Bankshares Corporation Declares Quarterly Common Stock Dividend and Preferred Stock Dividend


January 29, 2026 4:01pm EST[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_5cf031d946cae21c3f8e7b7df6881ced/atlanticunionbank/news/2026-01-29_Atlantic_Union_Bankshares_Corporation_Declares_212.pdf)


RICHMOND, Va.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- The Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Atlantic Union Bankshares Corporation (the “Company”) has declared a quarterly dividend of $0.37 per share of common stock, which is the same as the fourth quarter of 2025 and a $0.03, or an 8.8%, increase from the dividend in the first quarter of 2025. Based on the Company’s common stock closing price of $38.90 on January 28, 2026, the dividend yield is approximately 3.8%. The common stock dividend is payable on February 27, 2026 to common shareholders of record as of February 13, 2026.


The Board also declared a quarterly dividend on the outstanding shares of the Company’s 6.875% Perpetual Non-Cumulative Preferred Stock, Series A (the “Series A preferred stock”). The Series A preferred stock is represented by depositary shares, each representing a 1/400th ownership interest in a share of Series A preferred stock. The dividend of $171.88 per share (equivalent to $0.43 per outstanding depositary share) is payable on March 2, 2026 to holders of record as of February 13, 2026.


**About Atlantic Union Bankshares Corporation**


Headquartered in Richmond, Virginia, Atlantic Union Bankshares Corporation (NYSE: AUB) is the holding company for Atlantic Union Bank. Atlantic Union Bank has branches and ATMs located in Virginia, Maryland, North Carolina and Washington D.C. Certain non-bank financial services affiliates of Atlantic Union Bank include: Atlantic Union Equipment Finance, Inc., which provides equipment financing; Atlantic Union Financial Consultants, LLC, which provides brokerage services; and Union Insurance Group, LLC, which offers various lines of insurance products.
