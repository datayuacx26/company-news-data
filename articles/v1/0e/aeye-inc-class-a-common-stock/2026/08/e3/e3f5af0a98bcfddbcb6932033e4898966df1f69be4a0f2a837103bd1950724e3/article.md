# AEye to Participate in J.P. Morgan Auto Conference

-
-
-
-
-


**PLEASANTON, Calif. — August 7, 2026** —[AEye, Inc.](https://www.aeye.ai/) (Nasdaq: LIDR), a global leader in software-defined, high-performance lidar solutions, today announced that CEO Matt Fisch will participate in a fireside chat at the J.P. Morgan Auto Conference in New York on Wednesday, August 12, 2026, at 2:20 PM ET.


A live audio webcast of the fireside chat will be available here:[Fireside Chat](https://jpmorgan.metameetings.net/events/auto26/sessions/319473-aeye/webcast/public/signin)


A replay and related investor materials will also be made available here:[AEye Investor Relations Website](https://investors.aeye.ai/)


To request a meeting with AEye at the conference, which is running from Tuesday, August 12 to Wednesday, August 13, please contact your J.P. Morgan representative.


### About AEye


AEye offers a suite of unique software-defined lidar solutions that address a wide range of real-world needs including advanced driver-assistance, vehicle autonomy, smart infrastructure, security, defense, and logistics applications. AEye’s flagship product, Apollo™, has been widely recognized for its small form factor and its ability to detect objects at up to one kilometer. In addition to Apollo™, AEye also offers STRATOS™ with the ability to detect objects at up to one-and-a-half kilometers as well as a full-stack solution through its OPTIS™ platform. OPTIS™ provides a complete system that captures a high-resolution 3D image of the world, interprets it, and provides direction to act upon what it sees in real-time.
