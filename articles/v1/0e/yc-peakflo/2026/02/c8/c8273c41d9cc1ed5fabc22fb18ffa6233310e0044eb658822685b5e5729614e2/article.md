## What is CASA Tier 2 Certification?


The **Cloud Application Security Assessment (CASA)** framework was developed by the[App Defense Alliance](https://appdefensealliance.dev/) (a collaboration including Google, Microsoft, and Meta) and leverages the well-respected **[OWASP Application Security Verification Standard (ASVS)](https://owasp.org/www-project-application-security-verification-standard/)** as its technical foundation. CASA Tier 2 is structured to assess a platform’s security controls across a broad set of categories such as authentication, access control, data protection, API security, cryptography, and threat modeling.


Achieving Tier 2 certification requires third-party lab testing by an authorized assessor, validating that application security controls meet or exceed industry security practices, not just developer claims. This certification verifies technical security, reinforces protection of sensitive data, and provides documented proof for enterprise risk reviews and audits.


Want to dive deeper into CASA (framework, tiers, assessor roles, and requirements)? Check out the official source:[App Defense Alliance – CASA](https://appdefensealliance.dev/casa) .

## What Does CASA Tier 2 Certification Mean for Peakflo Products?


Earning this certification goes beyond recognition; it delivers tangible value across our customers, partners, and the broader Peakflo ecosystem:


- **Continued access to restricted Google APIs:** Several Google services, including Gmail and Calendar, require elevated security assurances from applications that access sensitive scopes. CASA Tier 2 certification validates that Peakflo meets these requirements, ensuring uninterrupted and compliant API access.
- **Greater trust for enterprise buyers:** Enterprises, regulated businesses, and public-sector organizations increasingly expect **independent security validation** before approving software vendors. This certification provides third-party assurance that Peakflo’s security practices meet stringent standards.
- **Stronger alignment with global compliance frameworks:** By adhering to CASA’s security framework, Peakflo strengthens its alignment with regulations and standards such as GDPR, ISO, and other data-protection-focused compliance regimes.
- **Clear differentiation in procurement and RFPs:** In competitive procurement cycles, CASA Tier 2 certification enables Peakflo to stand out as a **verified, security-first solution** , reducing friction during vendor risk and IT reviews.
- **Platform stability and long-term reliability:** Certification reduces the risk of unexpected API restrictions or security-driven re-engineering, helping ensure long-term platform continuity and a stable experience for customers.
