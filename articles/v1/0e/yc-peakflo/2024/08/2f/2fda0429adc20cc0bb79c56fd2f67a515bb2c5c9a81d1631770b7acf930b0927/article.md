## Why SOC 2 Matters?


SOC 2 is an external audit created by the American Institute of CPAs (AICPA) to ensure that software platforms properly manage customer data. The AICPA has established strict Trust Services Criteria based on five key principles: security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy.


A SOC 2 Type II certification is given to organizations that show top-notch security practices in these areas during a specific audit period. Achieving this certification is an industry standard that helps build trust in Peakflo’s security and compliance efforts.


## What Does SOC2 Type 2 Certification Mean for Our Customers?


The SOC 2 Type 2 compliance is an independent certification that confirms our high standard of data security.


The detailed audit, conducted over several months, evaluated the effectiveness of our security measures. Our recent renewal shows that Peakflo consistently maintains these strict standards.


1. **Assurance of Data Protection:** The certification confirms that our data security measures are strong and effective, ensuring the thorough protection of our customers’ sensitive information from potential breaches or misuse.
2. **Ongoing Compliance:** SOC 2 Type 2 is not a one-time certification but an ongoing commitment. It involves a detailed audit over time, proving that our company consistently upholds high standards for data management and protection.
3. **Building Trust:** As an industry-recognized standard, the certification reassures customers that we are dedicated to maintaining high levels of security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy.
4. **Transparency:** The certification offers clear insight into our security controls and data management practices, helping customers understand our commitment to protecting their data.
5. **Risk Reduction:** This certification gives customers confidence in our proactive approach to identifying and addressing potential data security risks, reducing the likelihood of data breaches and related disruptions.
6. ****Compliance** Validation:** An independent third-party auditor conducts the certification, ensuring that our security measures are objectively evaluated and validated, offering additional assurance to our customers.
