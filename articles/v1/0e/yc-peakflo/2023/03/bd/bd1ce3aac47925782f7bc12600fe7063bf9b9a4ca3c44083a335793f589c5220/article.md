## **What Are G2 Badges?**


G2 is a major global technology marketplace that assists companies in discovering, assessing, and overseeing the software they need to achieve their full potential. Through G2, verified users can share their perspectives and evaluations of the hardware and software they employ. The site’s objective is to aid 60 million users each year in making informed software selections based on genuine feedback from their peers.


### **Why Are They Important?**


G2 badges are the symbolic representation of product quality and customer satisfaction. These prestigious awards are granted based on the honest feedback from the users to showcase a product’s effectiveness relative to its competitors. Displaying a G2 badge on a website can enhance the trust of visitors and significantly boost the chances of a successful sale.
