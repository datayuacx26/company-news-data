Today, we’re excited to announce Clearline’s integration with PBS Systems. Dealerships can now use Clearline to book service appointments directly into PBS while bringing customer details, vehicle information, service history, and Workplan activity into the conversation.


This is more than a data connection. It closes the gap between talking to a customer and completing the work inside the system the service department already uses.


## From customer conversation to a booked appointment in PBS


A service customer should not have to repeat information the dealership already knows. An advisor should not have to copy notes from a phone call into a separate scheduler. And a manager should not have to reconcile an AI dashboard with the appointment calendar at the end of the day.


With the Clearline PBS integration, the workflow can happen in one continuous path:


1. A customer calls the dealership or responds to an outbound follow-up.
2. Clearline identifies the customer and retrieves the relevant customer and vehicle details from PBS.
3. Clearline uses available service history and Workplan activity to understand the context behind the request.
4. The conversation follows the dealership’s approved scheduling rules, including appointment types, hours, required information, and escalation paths.
5. Once the customer confirms, Clearline creates the service appointment directly in PBS.


The result is a completed booking in the dealership’s operating system, not a message for someone to re-enter later.


## What the Clearline PBS integration supports


### Direct service appointment booking


Clearline can create service appointments directly in PBS during a customer conversation. That matters because a booking is only operationally useful when the service team can see and manage it in the same schedule they already use.


Direct booking also removes a fragile handoff. There is no separate appointment request waiting in an inbox, no duplicate entry for an advisor, and no ambiguity about whether the customer has actually been scheduled.


For a deeper look at this workflow, read our guide to[automating dealership service appointment scheduling](https://www.useclearline.com/blog/automate-service-appointment-scheduling-dealerships) .


### Customer and vehicle context


The integration lets Clearline retrieve customer details and associated vehicle information from PBS. Instead of starting every interaction from a blank screen, Clearline can use the dealership’s existing record to make the conversation more relevant.


That means fewer repetitive questions for the customer and cleaner information for the dealership. When information is missing or needs confirmation, Clearline can gather it as part of the conversation before booking.


### Service history and Workplan activity


Clearline can also retrieve service history and Workplan activity. This context helps distinguish a routine maintenance request from a follow-up already in progress and gives the conversation a clearer starting point.


For example, the workflow can recognize that the dealership has an existing history with the customer and use that context when helping them choose the right next step. If the request falls outside the approved scope, Clearline can route it to the service team with the relevant context intact.


## Why a two-way DMS workflow matters


Integration depth is easy to flatten into a checkbox. In practice, there is a major difference between reading dealership data and completing an action in the dealership’s source system.


PBS describes its partner program as a way to meet shared customers’ data requirements through[API access or a two-way data interface](https://www.pbssystems.com/partners) . Its public[Partner Hub](https://partnerhub.pbsdealers.com/) covers customers, vehicles, appointments, parts invoices, and repair orders, including both querying and updating information.


That two-way model is what makes the Clearline workflow useful. Clearline can use PBS data to handle the conversation with context, then write the appointment back into PBS when the customer commits.


PBS remains the operational source of truth. Clearline is the conversation and workflow layer that helps the dealership turn more calls and follow-up into completed actions.


## What this changes for dealership teams


The value of the integration shows up differently across the service operation.


**For service advisors:** Routine scheduling conversations can be completed without interrupting the customer standing at the counter. Advisors spend less time repeating questions and re-entering appointment details.


**For managers:** Clearline-booked appointments appear in the system the department already runs on. That makes it easier to review the schedule, coach the team, and measure whether calls and follow-up are producing real appointments.


**For customers:** The experience is more connected. They can call when it is convenient, avoid repeating information the dealership already has, and finish with a confirmed next step.


**For dealership groups:** The workflow can be configured around each store’s appointment rules while keeping the underlying process consistent across rooftops.


## Built around each dealership’s service rules


A direct DMS connection does not mean the AI decides how the service department should operate. The dealership still defines the rules.


Before launch, Clearline works with the dealership to configure details such as:


- which appointment types Clearline can book;
- hours, minimum notice, and scheduling windows;
- required customer and vehicle fields;
- when a request needs an advisor or another department;
- how urgent, warranty, recall, or complex requests should be handled; and
- which calls and outcomes managers want to review.


This keeps routine scheduling fast while preserving a clear human path for exceptions. Our[dealership AI rollout checklist](https://www.useclearline.com/blog/dealership-ai-rollout-checklist) covers the decisions teams should make before going live.

## Frequently asked questions


### Does Clearline book service appointments directly into PBS?


Yes. Once a customer confirms an eligible time and the required information is collected, Clearline can create the service appointment directly in PBS.


### What PBS data can Clearline use during a conversation?


Clearline can retrieve customer details, associated vehicle information, service history, and relevant Workplan activity. The exact workflow depends on the dealership’s PBS configuration and approved use case.


### Does Clearline replace PBS?


No. PBS remains the dealership’s DMS and operational source of truth. Clearline handles customer conversations and workflow execution, including creating completed appointments in PBS.


### Can the dealership control what Clearline books?


Yes. The dealership defines bookable appointment types, scheduling rules, required fields, and human escalation paths before launch.
