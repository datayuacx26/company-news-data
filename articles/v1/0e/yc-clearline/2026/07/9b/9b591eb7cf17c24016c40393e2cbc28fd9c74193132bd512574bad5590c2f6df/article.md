## The seven tests every dealership should run live


Do not buy an AI phone solution from a polished scripted demo. Give each vendor the same realistic scenarios and score the result.


### 1. After-hours sales inquiry


Ask about a specific vehicle, then change one requirement. The AI should use current information, offer a sensible next step, and avoid inventing availability or pricing.


### 2. Service appointment request


Request a common service, reject the first time offered, and ask whether transportation is available. Verify what is actually written into the scheduler.


### 3. Existing repair-order question


Ask for a status update that requires dealership data. The AI should either retrieve an approved status or escalate cleanly, not guess.


### 4. Human transfer


Ask for a manager or a specific department. Measure transfer speed and whether the employee receives the caller's context.


### 5. Language and interruption handling


Interrupt, correct a detail, and speak naturally. The system should update its understanding without restarting the call.


### 6. Unsupported or sensitive question


Ask about a financing approval, final price, or policy exception. A trustworthy system should recognize the boundary and route the conversation appropriately.


### 7. No-booking follow-up


End the call without committing. Check whether the summary, lead record, owner, and follow-up action appear in the right place.

## Which AI phone answering solution should you choose?


Choose based on the failure point closest to revenue:


- If calls are going to voicemail, prioritize immediate inbound coverage and appointment completion.
- If service advisors are overwhelmed, prioritize service scheduling, status workflows, and clean escalation.
- If internet leads wait too long, prioritize speed-to-lead and persistent follow-up.
- If managers cannot see what happens on calls, prioritize call intelligence and CRM visibility.
- If several of these problems happen together, prioritize a connected platform over another point tool.

## Frequently asked questions


What is the best AI phone answering solution for a car dealership?


There is no universal winner. Clearline is a strong fit for connected inbound, outbound, appointment, and CRM workflows; Numa is well suited to service-heavy automotive communication; Drivee AI emphasizes inventory-aware sales workflows; Conversica focuses on persistent lead nurture; and CallRail is strongest when call tracking and analysis are the main requirement.


Can AI book dealership appointments directly?


Some platforms can book directly, but integration depth varies. Ask the vendor to create, reschedule, and cancel a real test appointment in your scheduler or CRM during the demo. Capturing a callback request is not the same as completing a booking.


Should a dealership replace its BDC with AI?


Most dealerships should use a hybrid model. AI is structurally strong at immediate response, after-hours coverage, routine qualification, booking, and consistent follow-up. People remain essential for negotiations, exceptions, sensitive conversations, and relationship-driven work.


What integrations matter most for dealership voice AI?


The system needs access to the data required for the chosen workflow. That often includes the phone system, CRM, DMS, inventory feed, service scheduler, sales calendar, and messaging channels. Verify both read and write behavior rather than accepting an integrations logo page.


How should a dealership measure an AI phone pilot?


Track answer rate, speed to answer, completed appointments, call-to-appointment rate, transfer success, show rate, follow-up completion, and manager-reviewed accuracy. Compare the same department and dayparts before and after launch.


Is a generic AI receptionist enough for a dealership?


It can cover basic hours, directions, and message taking. Dealerships usually need deeper handling for inventory, service scheduling, department routing, CRM records, and compliance-sensitive questions. Test those workflows before deciding that a generic receptionist is sufficient.
