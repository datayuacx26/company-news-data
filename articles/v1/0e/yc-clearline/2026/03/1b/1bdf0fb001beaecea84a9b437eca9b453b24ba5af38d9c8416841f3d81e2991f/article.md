## 1. Introduction and ROI Premise


**Why 24/7 AI Voice Agents Matter** for modern auto retail:


- Never miss a lead, especially during after-hours when conventional staff are unavailable.
- Provide instant responses, reducing wait times and improving customer trust.
- Scale effortlessly across multiple locations or a single dealership with minimal disruption.


**Expected 30-Day ROI Trajectory:**


- Revenue lift driven by capturing and nurturing leads that would otherwise be lost.
- Increased customer engagement and appointment conversions.
- Significantly faster response times enhancing CSI scores.
- Luna’s human-like persona fosters trust, improving interaction quality.


## 2. Prerequisites and Alignment


Prior to deployment, align on key aspects:


- **Target use cases:** Post-hours lead capture and qualification, service scheduling, BDC workload relief, test-drive follow-ups.
- **System readiness:** Confirm seamless integration with your DMS and CRM systems. Data governance policies must be clear; escalation and approval workflows should be defined.
- **Guardrails and compliance:** Establish conversation logs, escalation triggers, and response protocols to ensure quality and legal compliance.


## 3. Step-by-Step Deployment Plan


### Phase A (Week 1–2): Discovery & Configuration


- Map out main call flows and personas.
- Set Luna’s tone (warm, proactive) and define escalation rules.
- Configure guardrails, approved responses, and logging.


### Phase B (Week 3–4): Pilot & Scale


- Launch on high-impact queues—e.g., after-hours lead responses.
- Collect telemetry data to monitor performance.
- Optimize based on initial feedback.
- Roll out across all targeted channels to achieve full deployment by Day 30.


**Quick-Start Actions & 14-Day Sprint Checklist:**


- Identify top 3 high-volume call scenarios.
- Configure Luna’s persona and escalation rules.
- Conduct initial testing with internal teams.
- Deploy in parallel with existing workflows for unobstructed performance assessment.


## 4. Configuring Luna & Guardrails


- **Persona Customization:** Adjust Luna’s tone and warmth to match your brand ethos, significantly impacting lead conversion.
- **Escalation Rules:** Define triggers (e.g., complex questions, no-go zone) for immediate transfer to human agents.
- **Response Approval:** Have a repository of approved responses for consistency.
- **Logging & Auditability:** Ensure full call and booking logs are captured, enabling transparent review and compliance.


## 5. Technical Integration & Data Flows


- **DMS & CRM Integration:** Map lead sources, appointment details, and customer data seamlessly, enabling real-time updates and follow-ups.
- **Data Hygiene & Privacy:** Maintain clean, secure datasets; adhere to data privacy laws to build trust.


## 6. KPI Framework & 30-Day ROI Calculation


**Key KPIs:**


- Revenue lift attributable to AI-driven engagements.
- Leads engaged and qualified by Luna.
- Conversion rate from lead to booked appointment.
- Average response time.
- Customer Satisfaction Index (CSI) scores.
- Show rate for scheduled test drives.


## 7. Real-World Impact Examples


- **After-Hours Lead Capture:** Luna answers calls outside business hours, qualifies leads instantly, and books test drives—lifting after-hours conversion rates from below 10% to over 70%.
- **Service Scheduling:** Luna manages overflow, includes appointment confirmations, boosting CSI and revenue.
- **BDC Relief:** Automates repetitive inquiries, freeing staff for high-value tasks, and improving response times.


## 8. Governance & Transparency


- Full conversation transcripts and recordings for review.
- AI interactions are fully auditable to ensure compliance and maintain trust.
- Guardrails activate escalation and prevent miscommunication.


## 9. Common Pitfalls & Mitigations


- **Underestimating Data Readiness:** Prepare clean, integrated data pipelines in advance.
- **Inadequate Guardrails:** Clearly define escalation rules and approved responses.
- **Overcomplex Deployment:** Focus on high-impact queues first for quick wins.
