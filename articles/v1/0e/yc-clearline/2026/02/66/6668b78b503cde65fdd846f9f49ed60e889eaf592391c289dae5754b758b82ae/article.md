# We've raised $2M to power the next generation of dealership operations

Today, we’re excited to share that **Clearline has raised $2M in seed funding** to help dealerships drive more revenue by making sure every customer call is answered, handled, and converted.


The round was backed by **Y Combinator, Garage Capital, Panache Ventures, Union Capital** , and a group of experienced angel investors who deeply understand both software and the automotive ecosystem.


## The Problem We’re Solving


Dealerships miss a meaningful number of inbound calls every day. Not because teams don’t care, but because phones spike during peak hours, after hours, and at the exact moments staff are already stretched thin. Each missed call is a missed appointment, a lost service booking, or a customer who moves on.


Clearline exists to close that gap.


## Our Approach


We’re building a voice AI front desk designed specifically for dealerships. Clearline answers inbound calls, books appointments, handles common questions, and routes conversations to the right human when needed. It works alongside existing teams, stepping in when calls would otherwise go unanswered.


The result is simple and measurable:


- More booked service and sales appointments
- Better customer experience
- Incremental revenue without adding headcount


## A Broader Mission


This funding accelerates our broader mission: **using voice AI to modernize how dealerships operate and communicate with customers** .


Voice is still the most important channel in automotive, yet it has seen the least innovation. We believe AI can transform this channel in a way that feels natural, trustworthy, and operationally seamless, not robotic or disruptive.


Our goal is not to replace people, but to give dealership teams leverage. Clearline ensures no opportunity is lost, even during the busiest moments.


## What’s Next


With this capital, we’re investing in product development, deeper dealership integrations, and expanding our go-to-market efforts across North America. We’re just getting started.


If you’re a dealership operator, partner, or someone excited about the future of voice AI in automotive, we’d love to talk.
