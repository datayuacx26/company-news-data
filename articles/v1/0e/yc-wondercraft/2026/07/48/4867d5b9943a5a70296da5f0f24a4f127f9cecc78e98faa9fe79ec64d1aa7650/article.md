# The next chapter of Wondercraft

When we started Wondercraft two years ago, we had no idea we were building a video company.


Back then, the goal was simple: help anyone create amazing audio content using AI. Podcasts, ads, meditations. If you could write it, you could make it sound incredible.


And for a while, that was enough. We grew to thousands of creators in +100 countries. People used Wondercraft to tell stories, train employees, and bring ideas to life through sound.


But somewhere along the way, something started to feel incomplete.


Because the internet doesn’t speak audio.


The internet speaks **video** .


Over the past year, our team’s been quietly rebuilding Wondercraft from the ground up. Not a facelift. A reinvention.


The question was never, *“How do we add video?”*


It was, *“How would we design a creative studio today, if AI was baked into its DNA?”*


That’s how we got to this moment.


## **Meet the new Wondercraft**


Wondercraft is now the first video editing platform that lets anyone create video simply by chatting with AI.


You can describe what you want, and **Wonda** — your built-in creative director — makes it happen. Cut, trim, animate, add captions, music, voice, design. Everything, conversationally.


It’s a full creative studio, but with the intelligence of AI and the depth of a real editor.


And yes, we still do audio. But now, you can turn that podcast into a launch video, that ad script into a campaign, that training material into a visual story — all without ever touching a timeline.


### **What changed**


This isn’t just a product update. It’s a shift in what Wondercraft is.


We’re no longer “an AI podcast generator.”


We’re the place where anyone can create video and audio **with AI,** **not AI-generated** content.


That distinction matters. The world doesn’t need more auto-generated slop, it needs better workflows that make creation effortless for real humans. That’s our lane.


### **How it works**


- **The Editor:** A full-fledged, timeline-based studio for video and audio.
- **The Models:** Every top model (Runway, Luma, VEO3, ElevenLabs, and more) orchestrated in one place.
- **The Agent:** Wonda — your creative partner that edits, refines, and directs through natural language.


You can start with text, a voice, a script, or nothing at all — just an idea. Chat it into existence.


## **Why we’re doing this**


AI was supposed to democratize creativity. But most tools made it more fragmented: one app for avatars, one for captions, one for voiceovers.


We wanted to build something coherent. Something that feels alive.


If Canva made design simple, and Notion made documentation human, Wondercraft makes creation conversational.


You don’t need to learn editing. You just need to talk to Wonda.


## **What’s next**


On **January 12th** , Wondercraft Video rolls out to everyone.


If you were on the waitlist, you’ll get early access. If not, no worries, it’ll just appear in your Studio.


We’ve also announced the winners of the[Manifesto Challenge](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ddJ8bFzmR7s&list=PLvjA9s_RbXAJw0fC6YUvKgS1hm31_UNvb) , where 10 creators got early access to make our launch videos using Wondercraft itself. The results are wild.


Two years in, it feels like Wondercraft is finally what it was meant to be — a creative home built for the new internet.


We’re just getting started.


The internet speaks video.


Now, everyone can join the conversation.
