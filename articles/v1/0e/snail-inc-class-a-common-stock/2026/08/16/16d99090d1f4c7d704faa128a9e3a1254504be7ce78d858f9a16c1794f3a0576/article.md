# Snail, Inc. Sets Second Quarter 2026 Conference Call for Tuesday, August 11, 2026 at 4:30 p.m. ET

CULVER CITY, Calif., Aug. 06, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- **Snail, Inc. (Nasdaq: SNAL) (“Snail Games” or the “Company”),** a leading global independent developer and publisher of interactive digital entertainment, will hold a conference call and webcast on Tuesday, August 11, 2026 at 4:30 p.m. Eastern time (1:30 p.m. Pacific time) to discuss its financial results for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026.


Snail Games management will host the conference call and webcast, followed by a question-and-answer period.
