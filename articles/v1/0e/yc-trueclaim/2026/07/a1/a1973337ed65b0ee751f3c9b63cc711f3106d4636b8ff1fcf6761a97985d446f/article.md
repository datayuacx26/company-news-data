## **What's great about pharmacy navigation solutions?**


Pharmacy navigation solutions are all about giving you a personalized healthcare experience. They use patients' medical information to find the best ways to prevent or treat any medical issues in a fast but informed way. As summarized byScott Doolittle, chief financial officer of Quantum Health:


‍


> “When we think about what we’re uniquely qualified to do, it’s these complex problems \[pharmacy navigation\] because we have people that are trained in healthcare.”


‍


With such a good understanding of their clients medical situation, these solutions can go ahead and track patients treatment progress, helping them stay on track, purchase cheaper medical equivalents of their prescriptions and navigate them towards appropriate and cost-effective therapies. For instance, if a patient fails to pick up their prescription, they will flag their behavior and contact them to make sure the patient adheres to their treatment.


What is more, patients also receive a team of Patient Care Advocates (PCAs) who will assist them with any type of inquiry or request. They will even advocate for the patients rights, and manage administrative burdens associated with insurance and medication access. What an upgrade from the otherwise multi-step complex process where patients have to contact their PBM, pharmacy, or insurer with questions or issues!


With such support and information, patients are finally sufficiently educated on their healthcare coverage to make well informed decisions about their treatment. This can not only lead to better treatment adherence (as we mentioned many times) but also more confidence when facing new or changing treatments. Patients are more aware of treatment options and resources making them comfortable changing to more cost effective medications or providers.


In essence, pharmacy navigation solutions streamline the healthcare experience, making it more accessible, cost-effective, and less overwhelming for patients. It's a win-win for your employees health and your budget!


‍


## **Risks**


While pharmacy navigation solutions bring a lot of convenience and benefits, they do come with some potential downsides. One thing to be aware of is their heavy reliance on data and technology. These systems are very sophisticated, however they cannot pick up on all patient nuances like an in-person doctor can. For instance, if the system doesn't get a patient's medication history right or overlooks an allergy, it might suggest inappropriate medication, which could lead to some issues or delays in treatment.


It's also easy to get a bit too comfortable relying on pharmacy navigation systems because of their convenience and service selection. They make everything feel super streamlined and easy, but it's important to remember that they're just tools. They’re not a substitute for real healthcare professionals. Doctors and therapists are essential for ensuring patients get the best care possible. So, while these solutions are great for guidance and support, patients should still be reminded about their purpose to fully benefit from their services.
