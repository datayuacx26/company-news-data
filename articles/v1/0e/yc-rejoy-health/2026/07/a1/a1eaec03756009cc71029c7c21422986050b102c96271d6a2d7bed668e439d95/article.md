# Dozens Report Illness After Suspected Food Poisoning Incident at Truck Festival

A major UK music festival has come under attention after dozens of attendees reportedly suffered symptoms linked to suspected food poisoning. The incident happened during Truck Festival in Steventon, Oxfordshire, where thousands of visitors gathered for a weekend of live music, entertainment, and food.


Festival organisers confirmed that they were informed about multiple reports of illness among attendees and launched an investigation with local environmental health officials. A food vendor operating at the event was identified as the suspected source and was removed from service while further checks were carried out.


The incident has raised fresh concerns about food hygiene standards at large outdoor events and the importance of strict safety procedures when serving thousands of customers in temporary catering environments.


## What Happened at Truck Festival?


Truck Festival, one of the UK’s longest running music events, welcomed around 30,000 visitors for its latest edition. The event featured performances from popular artists and offered a wide range of food and drink options for festival-goers.


However, the celebrations were disrupted after a number of attendees reported experiencing symptoms commonly associated with food poisoning. These included stomach problems, sickness, and severe digestive issues.


According to reports from festival visitors, some people became unwell after eating from a Yorkshire pudding food stall. Several attendees claimed that many people who reported symptoms had purchased meals from the same vendor.


The reports led to concerns among visitors, with some describing long queues at festival toilets and increased activity at the medical facilities on site.


## Food Stall Removed Following Complaints


Festival organisers stated that their medical and safety teams responded quickly after receiving reports from attendees. They said a third-party food trader was identified as the suspected source and was removed from operation within a short period after the first complaints were received.


The organisers also confirmed that they contacted the local Environmental Health Officer and are supporting the ongoing investigation.


The food stall reportedly served traditional roast-style meals, including Yorkshire pudding wraps, roast meats, stuffing, gravy, and other popular British dishes. Some customers later raised concerns about food preparation standards, including claims that certain meals may not have been cooked properly.


At this stage, authorities have not confirmed the exact cause of the reported illnesses. Investigations are continuing to determine whether the food served at the stall was responsible.


## Attendees Share Their Experiences


Several festival visitors shared their experiences online after becoming unwell. Some described feeling sick shortly after eating at the event, while others said they saw large numbers of people seeking medical support.


One attendee claimed that many visitors were affected during the final days of the festival, creating difficulties for people staying at the campsite. Others called for improved checks on food vendors operating at large events.


Festival food safety is a major responsibility because temporary catering setups can face unique challenges. Vendors must manage food storage, cooking temperatures, hygiene standards, and high customer demand, often in busy outdoor conditions.


Even small mistakes in food handling can potentially affect large numbers of people when thousands of meals are served over a short period.


## Importance of Food Safety at Festivals


Outdoor festivals depend heavily on catering businesses to provide safe and enjoyable experiences for visitors. Food businesses operating at these events are expected to follow strict hygiene regulations, including:


- Proper storage of fresh ingredients
- Safe cooking temperatures
- Clean preparation areas
- Good personal hygiene practices
- Effective procedures for preventing cross contamination


Food poisoning outbreaks can happen when harmful bacteria, viruses, or toxins enter food supplies. Common causes include undercooked meat, poor temperature control, contaminated ingredients, or unsafe handling practices.


Large events require extra caution because a single food outlet can serve hundreds or thousands of meals in just a few days.


## Investigation Underway


The Vale of White Horse District Council confirmed that it was investigating reports of illness alongside other relevant organisations. The investigation involves working with health authorities and the food business involved.


Officials will examine evidence such as food samples, preparation methods, supplier records, and reports from affected customers. These checks are important to establish whether a specific food item caused the outbreak and whether any food safety rules were broken.


Until the investigation is complete, it is not possible to confirm exactly what caused the illnesses reported by festival attendees.


## What Festival Visitors Should Know


People attending festivals can reduce the risk of food related illness by taking a few simple precautions:


1. Choose food vendors that appear clean and well organised.
2. Check that hot food is served steaming hot.
3. Be cautious with meat, dairy products, and foods that require refrigeration.
4. Wash hands regularly, especially before eating.
5. Report concerns about food quality or hygiene to festival staff.


If someone develops serious symptoms after eating at an event, they should seek medical advice, especially if symptoms include dehydration, persistent vomiting, severe pain, or a high fever.


## Impact on Festival Organisers and Vendors


Food safety incidents can have a significant impact on both event organisers and catering companies. Festivals rely on public trust, and visitors expect food options to meet high safety standards.


Organisers usually work with approved vendors and safety teams to reduce risks, but unexpected incidents can still occur. Quick action, transparent communication, and cooperation with health authorities are essential when problems arise.


The Truck Festival incident highlights why monitoring food operations throughout an event is important, rather than relying only on checks before the festival begins.


## Conclusion


The reported food poisoning incident at Truck Festival has highlighted the importance of food safety at large public events. While investigations continue, festival organisers and health officials are working to understand what happened and whether a specific food vendor was responsible.


For festival visitors, food is an important part of the experience, but safety must always come first. Strong hygiene standards, careful preparation, and fast responses to complaints are essential to ensuring that entertainment events remain safe and enjoyable for everyone.


As authorities continue their investigation, more information may become available regarding the cause of the reported illnesses and any actions that may follow.
