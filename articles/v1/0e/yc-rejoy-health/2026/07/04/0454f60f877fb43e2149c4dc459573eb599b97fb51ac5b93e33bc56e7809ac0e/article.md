# Squishy Dumpling Toy Recall in the UK: Toxic Chemical Warning Every Parent Should Know

## Squishy Dumpling Toys Recalled After Dangerous Benzene Levels Found


Parents across the UK are being urged to check their children's toys after a recall was announced for certain squishy dumpling toys due to a serious chemical safety concern. The UK government's Office for Product Safety and Standards (OPSS) has warned that some imitation versions of these popular toys contain excessive levels of benzene, a toxic chemical linked to both short-term health issues and long-term cancer risks.


The recall has raised concerns because these soft sensory toys have become one of the latest viral trends among children. Their popularity has grown rapidly thanks to social media, blind unboxing videos, and the increasing demand for collectible squishy toys. While genuine versions remain available through approved distributors, authorities are warning consumers to avoid specific imitation products that fail to meet UK toy safety standards.


## Why Were the Squishy Dumpling Toys Recalled?


The recall involves "Squeezy Dumplings" toys sold by Cardiff-based Samsons Cash and Carry Ltd. According to the Office for Product Safety and Standards, laboratory testing found that the outer layer of the toy contained benzene at concentrations exceeding permitted safety limits.


Officials classified the issue as presenting a serious chemical risk. The products do not comply with the Toys (Safety) Regulations, making them unsuitable for continued use by children.


Parents and caregivers are advised to stop using the affected toys immediately and keep them away from children until they can be disposed of safely or returned according to the retailer's instructions.


## What Is Benzene?


Benzene is an industrial chemical widely used in manufacturing processes. It serves as a building block for producing plastics, synthetic fibers, detergents, dyes, rubber, solvents, and many other industrial products.


Years ago, benzene was also used in cosmetics, household products, and even during coffee decaffeination. However, after researchers identified its toxic effects, its use in consumer products became heavily restricted in the UK and many other countries.


Exposure to benzene is now closely regulated because of its known health hazards.


## Health Risks Associated With Benzene Exposure


According to health authorities, breathing in benzene or coming into contact with high levels can cause immediate symptoms such as:


- Eye irritation
- Nose irritation
- Throat discomfort
- Skin irritation
- Burning sensations affecting the digestive system after significant exposure


Although brief exposure does not automatically result in serious illness, repeated or prolonged exposure increases health risks considerably.


Medical research has established a connection between long-term benzene exposure and several forms of blood cancer, particularly acute myeloid leukemia. The International Agency for Research on Cancer classifies benzene as a Group 1 carcinogen, meaning there is sufficient scientific evidence that it causes cancer in humans.


## Why These Toys Became So Popular


Squishy dumpling toys combine several toy trends that have exploded online over the past few years.


Children enjoy the soft, stretchy texture that allows them to squeeze and reshape the toy repeatedly. Many versions are also sold in mystery packaging, making each purchase feel like a surprise collectible.


Videos featuring blind unboxing and stress-relief toys regularly receive millions of views across social media platforms, helping products like squishy dumplings gain widespread popularity among young audiences.


However, the rapid growth in demand has also encouraged imitation manufacturers to release lower-quality copies that may not undergo the same safety testing as legitimate products.


## Genuine Products Versus Imitation Versions


Authorities have stressed that the concern is focused on certain imitation versions rather than every squishy dumpling toy on the market.


The original products distributed by RMS/Crazy Fun are not the subject of this specific recall. The safety issue relates to recalled imitation toys that failed chemical testing.


This distinction is important because many parents may assume all similar-looking toys are unsafe. Instead, consumers should carefully identify where their products were purchased and whether they match the recalled description.


## Identifying the Recalled Toys


One challenge for consumers is that the recalled toys reportedly do not include batch numbers or detailed product identifiers.


The recalled products are described as:


- Soft plastic polymer dumpling-shaped toys
- Packaged inside small basket-style containers
- Manufactured in China


Because identifying marks are limited, parents who purchased inexpensive imitation versions should exercise caution.


If there is uncertainty about whether a toy is affected, it is generally safer to stop allowing children to use it until additional information becomes available.


## What Parents Should Do


If you believe your child owns one of the recalled toys, experts recommend taking the following precautions:


- Stop using the toy immediately.
- Keep it out of the reach of children.
- Contact the retailer for return or refund guidance if available.
- Monitor official government recall notices for updates.
- Purchase children's toys only from trusted retailers that comply with UK safety regulations.


Checking product recalls regularly can help reduce the risk of children being exposed to unsafe products.


## Toy Safety Remains a Shared Responsibility


The recall highlights the importance of product testing and consumer awareness.


Many children's toys sold through established retailers undergo extensive safety checks before reaching store shelves. Problems often arise when imitation products enter the market through unofficial suppliers or imported stock that has not met UK safety standards.


Parents should always examine packaging carefully, look for proper safety labeling, and avoid products with missing manufacturer information.


Although affordable alternatives may seem attractive, they can sometimes pose greater risks if they have not been independently tested.


## Final Thoughts


The recall of certain squishy dumpling toys serves as an important reminder that popular children's products must meet strict safety standards before they are considered safe for everyday use.


While the viral trend surrounding squishy sensory toys continues to grow, families should remain cautious about imitation products that may contain harmful chemicals such as benzene.


Parents who suspect they own one of the recalled toys should stop using it immediately and follow guidance issued by UK product safety authorities. Staying informed about official recalls can help protect children from unnecessary health risks while allowing families to make safer purchasing decisions in the future.
