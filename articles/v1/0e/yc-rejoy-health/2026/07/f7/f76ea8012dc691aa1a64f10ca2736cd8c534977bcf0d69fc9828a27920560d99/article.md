# How to Stay Cool and Sleep Better During UK Summer Heat: Doctor-Approved Cooling Tips That Can Help

Struggling to sleep during hot UK summer nights? Discover doctor-recommended cooling techniques, NHS-inspired methods, and simple bedroom changes that may help you stay comfortable.


## The Best Ways to Keep Cool at Night During a Heatwave


When temperatures rise across the UK, getting a good night’s sleep can become a challenge. Warm bedrooms, uncomfortable bedding, and excessive sweating can leave many people tossing and turning for hours.


Sleep experts often highlight the importance of keeping your body temperature comfortable before bedtime. One simple cooling technique gaining attention involves using cold packs near areas of the body where major blood vessels are close to the skin.


This method has been discussed by former NHS doctor and aesthetics specialist Dr Jonny Betteridge, who explained that targeted cooling was previously used in emergency medicine settings for patients experiencing dangerously high body temperatures.


Although everyday summer discomfort is very different from medical emergencies such as heatstroke, the same principle of helping the body release heat may offer a practical way to feel cooler before sleep.


## The Ice Pack Trick That May Help You Cool Down


Dr Betteridge explained that in hospital environments, rapid cooling methods for severe overheating involved placing ice packs around areas where large blood vessels are located.


These areas include:


- The neck
- Under the arms
- The groin area


The reason this technique works is because cooling these areas may help lower skin temperature more efficiently compared with placing ice on other parts of the body.


For a more comfortable bedtime approach, some people choose to wrap small ice packs in a thin towel or cloth before placing them under their arms for a short period.


This can provide a cooling sensation without putting ice directly against the skin, which may reduce the risk of discomfort or irritation.


However, this method should be used carefully. Ice packs should never be applied directly to the skin for long periods because extreme cold exposure can cause skin damage.


## Why Hot Weather Can Affect Your Sleep


A comfortable sleeping environment plays a major role in achieving good-quality rest. During warmer months, the body naturally tries to reduce its temperature before sleep. High room temperatures can interfere with this process, making it harder to fall asleep.


Poor sleep caused by heat may lead to:


- Increased tiredness during the day
- Difficulty concentrating
- Mood changes
- Reduced energy levels
- Feeling less refreshed after waking


According to sleep researchers, maintaining a regular bedtime routine and creating a cooler bedroom environment can support better sleep habits.


The ideal sleeping temperature varies from person to person, but many experts recommend keeping bedrooms cool, dark, and well ventilated.


## Simple Bedroom Changes to Beat Summer Heat


You do not need expensive equipment to make your bedroom more comfortable during warm weather. Several affordable adjustments can help create a cooler sleeping environment.


### Use Fans Effectively


A fan can improve airflow and make a room feel more comfortable. Some people place a bowl of ice in front of a fan to create a cooling effect, although this works mainly by adding a small amount of extra cooling through evaporation rather than acting like a full air-conditioning system.


For many households, a fan remains one of the simplest and most affordable ways to improve comfort during hot nights.


### Choose Cooling Bedding


Traditional thick duvets and synthetic materials can trap heat. Switching to lightweight bedding made from breathable fabrics may help regulate temperature.


Cooling pillows, mattress covers, and breathable sheets are also popular options for people who regularly struggle with overheating at night.


### Remove Heat Sources


Electrical devices can produce small amounts of heat, which may contribute to an uncomfortable bedroom environment.


The British Red Cross recommends turning off and unplugging unnecessary electrical items where possible, especially devices that are not needed overnight.


Simple steps include:


- Switching off televisions
- Avoiding leaving laptops running
- Turning off unused chargers
- Keeping unnecessary lights off


## Maintaining a Healthy Sleep Routine in Summer


When nights become warmer, it can be tempting to change your normal routine. Some people sleep later, take long daytime naps, or spend more time awake at night.


However, keeping a consistent sleep schedule may help your body maintain its natural rhythm.


Helpful summer sleep habits include:


- Going to bed and waking up at similar times
- Avoiding long daytime naps
- Reducing screen use before bedtime
- Keeping the bedroom calm and relaxing
- Drinking enough fluids throughout the day


While staying hydrated is important during hot weather, drinking large amounts immediately before bed may cause some people to wake during the night.


## Other Ways to Stay Comfortable During a Heatwave


Alongside cooling techniques, lifestyle choices can also make a difference.


Wearing lightweight sleepwear, using breathable fabrics, and keeping curtains or blinds closed during the hottest parts of the day may help prevent bedrooms from becoming too warm.


Opening windows when outside temperatures drop in the evening can also improve airflow.


It is particularly important for vulnerable groups, including older adults, young children, and people with certain health conditions, to take extra precautions during periods of extreme heat.


## When Hot Weather Becomes Dangerous


A warm night can be uncomfortable, but extreme heat can become a serious health concern.


Symptoms of heat-related illness may include:


- Dizziness
- Confusion
- Severe sweating
- Weakness
- Nausea
- A very high body temperature


Anyone experiencing signs of serious heat illness should seek urgent medical advice.


Cooling techniques designed for everyday comfort should not replace professional medical treatment during a heat-related emergency.


## Final Thoughts


Summer nights in the UK can make quality sleep difficult, especially during heatwaves. Simple strategies such as improving airflow, choosing breathable bedding, reducing bedroom heat sources, and using carefully wrapped cooling packs may help create a more comfortable sleeping environment.


The ice pack method discussed by Dr Jonny Betteridge is based on cooling principles used in medical settings, but it is important to remember that hospital treatments for severe overheating are different from everyday methods used at home.


Finding the right combination of cooling habits and consistent sleep routines can help many people rest more comfortably when temperatures rise.
