# Giant 23ft Python Swallows Man in Indonesia After Deadly Attack

A tragic wildlife incident in Indonesia has drawn global attention after a man was reportedly killed by a large reticulated python while walking home alone at night. The incident, which happened in North Maluku, has renewed discussions about human safety in areas where people live close to natural habitats.


According to local reports, the victim, identified as 35-year-old Hasim Lumbessy, disappeared after leaving a rural gathering in the early hours of the morning. Residents later discovered evidence of a snake attack along a forested path, leading to the recovery of his body from inside a large python.


While such incidents are extremely rare, experts say they highlight the importance of understanding wildlife behaviour, especially in tropical regions where large snakes are naturally found.


## Man Reportedly Killed by Large Python in North Maluku


Reports from Indonesia stated that Hasim Lumbessy was walking home alone at approximately 3am when he was attacked in a forested area of North Maluku. The region contains extensive woodland and agricultural areas, where encounters between humans and wildlife can occur.


After Hasim failed to return home, relatives and local residents began searching for him. During the search, villagers reportedly found personal belongings, including bags and sandals, along with signs that suggested a struggle had taken place.


Authorities said the search continued near Fatliwangka Road, where residents discovered a large python with an unusually swollen body. The snake was later examined, and villagers reportedly found the victim inside.


The incident shocked the local community and led police officials to remind residents to take extra precautions when travelling through forest areas.


## Police Issue Warning After Fatal Snake Encounter


Following the incident, local authorities encouraged people living near forest environments to avoid walking alone in areas where dangerous wildlife may be present.


Police officials advised residents who need to enter woodland areas or remote locations to travel with others whenever possible. They also highlighted the importance of staying alert, particularly during early morning or nighttime hours when visibility is reduced.


North Maluku is home to diverse wildlife, and officials stressed that forests remain natural habitats for many species, including large snakes.


The warning comes as communities continue to balance everyday activities, farming, and travel with the need to respect surrounding ecosystems.


## Why Reticulated Pythons Can Become Dangerous


Reticulated pythons are among the longest snakes in the world. They are native to Southeast Asia and are known for their impressive size, strength, and ability to survive in different environments.


These snakes are non-venomous, meaning they do not use poison to kill prey. Instead, they rely on powerful muscles to constrict their targets. Once a python has successfully captured prey, its body strength can prevent the victim from escaping.


Although large pythons are capable of attacking humans, experts explain that such events are uncommon. Most snakes avoid humans and prefer to remain hidden when possible.


Attacks generally occur when a snake feels threatened, is surprised, or sees an opportunity for food. Habitat changes, reduced forest areas, and increased human activity in wildlife zones can increase the possibility of encounters.


## Previous Python Incidents in Indonesia


The latest case follows other reported python-related deaths in Indonesia, particularly in areas where villages and forests are closely connected.


In previous incidents, residents in North Maluku and other regions reported deadly encounters involving very large pythons. These cases have attracted international attention because attacks of this nature are rarely documented.


Indonesia’s tropical environment provides suitable conditions for reticulated pythons. The country’s forests contain abundant wildlife, creating natural feeding opportunities for large predators.


However, experts caution against assuming that every large snake is a threat. Most snakes play an important role in controlling populations of smaller animals and maintaining ecological balance.


## Expert Advice on Staying Safe Around Pythons


Wildlife specialists say prevention is one of the best ways to reduce risks in areas where large snakes live.


People travelling through forested locations should consider the following safety measures:


• Avoid walking alone in isolated areas, especially after dark.


• Carry a torch when travelling through areas with limited visibility.


• Stay on recognised paths rather than entering dense vegetation unnecessarily.


• Be cautious near rivers, farmland, and areas where animals may gather.


• Avoid approaching or attempting to handle large snakes.


Experts also advise people not to panic if they encounter a snake. Sudden movements or attempts to capture the animal can increase the risk of an aggressive response.


## The Importance of Respecting Wildlife Habitats


Human and animal encounters are becoming more common in some regions because communities are expanding into areas that were previously dominated by forests.


Conservation experts often emphasise that protecting natural habitats benefits both wildlife and people. Healthy ecosystems allow animals to maintain their natural behaviour while reducing unexpected contact with humans.


Education is another important factor. Communities that understand local wildlife are better prepared to avoid dangerous situations and respond appropriately during encounters.


Large snakes may appear frightening, but they are also important parts of tropical ecosystems. Their role includes controlling populations of rodents and other animals that can affect agriculture and human health.


## Conclusion


The reported death of Hasim Lumbessy in North Maluku is a rare and tragic reminder of the challenges faced by communities living alongside powerful wildlife.


While reticulated python attacks on humans remain extremely uncommon, the incident highlights the need for awareness, caution, and respect for natural habitats.


Authorities continue to encourage residents and visitors to take sensible precautions when entering forest areas. Understanding wildlife behaviour and following safety advice can help reduce risks while allowing humans and animals to coexist.
