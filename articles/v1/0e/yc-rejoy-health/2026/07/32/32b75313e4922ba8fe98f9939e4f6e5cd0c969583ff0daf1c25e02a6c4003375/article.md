# 1.5 Million OCOOPA Rechargeable Hand Warmers Recalled After Death and Hundreds of Injuries Reported

## OCOOPA Hand Warmer Recall: What Consumers Need to Know About the Fire and Burn Risk


Millions of people use rechargeable hand warmers during cold weather for outdoor activities, travel, work, and everyday comfort. However, a major product safety concern has emerged after OCOOPA Direct announced a recall involving approximately 1.5 million rechargeable hand warmers. The recall follows reports of overheating incidents, fires, serious injuries, and one reported death linked to the products.


The recall was issued because certain OCOOPA rechargeable lithium-ion battery hand warmers may overheat and ignite, creating a risk of fire and burn injuries. Consumers who own affected models are advised to stop using them immediately and check whether their device is included in the recall.


## Why Are OCOOPA Rechargeable Hand Warmers Being Recalled?


According to safety information released in connection with the recall, the problem involves the lithium-ion batteries inside certain OCOOPA rechargeable hand warmers. Lithium-ion batteries are commonly used in portable electronics because they provide high energy storage in a compact size. However, if a battery develops a defect, becomes damaged, overheats, or experiences internal failure, it can create a risk of thermal runaway.


Thermal runaway occurs when a battery rapidly increases in temperature and may release heat, gases, or flames. In portable heating products, this risk is especially concerning because users often keep hand warmers close to their bodies, inside pockets, bags, or near clothing.


The reported incidents associated with the recalled OCOOPA hand warmers highlight the importance of battery safety in consumer electronics. The company recall information states that specific rechargeable hand warmer models are affected due to a potential lithium-ion battery safety issue.


## One Death and Hundreds of Injuries Reported


The recall has drawn significant attention because reports connected to the products include hundreds of injuries and one death. Reports indicate that OCOOPA Direct received numerous complaints involving overheating, fires, and burn injuries.


Safety officials warn that battery-powered heating devices can create serious hazards when a battery fails. A device that appears normal may still contain a battery defect capable of causing overheating or ignition.


Consumers should not ignore signs of battery problems, including:


- The hand warmer becoming unusually hot
- Swelling or changes in the device shape
- Burning smells
- Smoke or unusual sounds
- Difficulty charging
- Sudden loss of battery performance


If any of these warning signs appear, users should stop handling the product and follow proper safety guidance.


## Which OCOOPA Hand Warmers Are Included in the Recall?


The recalled products include several OCOOPA rechargeable hand warmer models. According to the company’s recall information, affected models include:


- UT3053
- UT3056
- ZLS-118
- ZLS-118S
- ZLS-118D
- H01
- H01 (PD)


Consumers should check the model number printed on their device and compare it with the recalled product list. The model number is typically located on the back or bottom of the hand warmer.


## What Should Owners Do If They Have a Recalled OCOOPA Hand Warmer?


Anyone who owns a recalled OCOOPA rechargeable hand warmer should stop using it immediately. This includes stopping charging, operating, or carrying the device until it has been confirmed whether it is part of the recall.


Consumers should:


1. Check the model number of their hand warmer.
2. Confirm whether the product is included in the recall.
3. Stop using the device if it matches a recalled model.
4. Follow OCOOPA’s instructions for the recall process.


The company has provided recall instructions for affected customers, including steps for identifying the product and submitting required information.


## Why Lithium-Ion Battery Safety Matters


Lithium-ion batteries power many modern devices, including smartphones, laptops, power banks, electric tools, and portable heaters. While these batteries are generally reliable, manufacturing defects, damage, improper charging, or aging can increase safety risks.


Consumers should always follow battery safety practices, such as:


- Using the original charger or a compatible charger recommended by the manufacturer
- Avoiding charging damaged devices
- Keeping battery-powered products away from extreme heat
- Not using products with swollen batteries
- Storing rechargeable devices safely when not in use


Battery-powered heating products require extra caution because they combine stored electrical energy with heat generation.


## Growing Concerns Around Rechargeable Consumer Products


The OCOOPA hand warmer recall reflects broader concerns about rechargeable consumer products that rely on lithium-ion batteries. As portable electronic devices become more popular, manufacturers and regulators continue monitoring battery-related safety issues.


Government safety agencies, including the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC), regularly publish recall notices to alert consumers about dangerous products and recommended actions. Consumers can check official recall databases to stay informed about products they own.


## How Consumers Can Stay Safe During Product Recalls


Product recalls are designed to prevent additional injuries and protect consumers. Many people continue using recalled products because they are unaware of the recall or assume their individual device is safe.


To reduce risks:


- Register new products when possible.
- Keep purchase records.
- Follow manufacturer safety announcements.
- Check recall notices regularly.
- Do not sell or give away recalled products.


A recalled product should not be passed to another person because it may expose someone else to the same safety risk.


## Conclusion


The recall of 1.5 million OCOOPA rechargeable hand warmers serves as an important reminder about the importance of battery safety. While rechargeable hand warmers provide convenient warmth during cold conditions, defective lithium-ion batteries can create serious risks.


Consumers who own OCOOPA hand warmers should verify their model numbers, stop using affected devices, and follow official recall instructions. Taking quick action can help prevent further injuries and protect households from potential fire hazards.
