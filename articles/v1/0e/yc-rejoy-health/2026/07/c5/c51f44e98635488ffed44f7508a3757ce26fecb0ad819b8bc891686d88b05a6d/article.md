# FDA Approves New Tylenol with Naproxen for OTC Pain Relief

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has approved a groundbreaking over-the-counter pain relief medication that combines two well-known ingredients into a single product. The newly approved **Tylenol with Naproxen** brings together **650 mg of acetaminophen** and **220 mg of naproxen sodium** , offering consumers a convenient non-prescription option for managing temporary pain.


This approval marks an important milestone because it is the **first FDA-approved over-the-counter fixed-dose combination** of acetaminophen and naproxen sodium. The product is designed to provide quick pain relief while maintaining effectiveness for up to 12 hours, reducing the need for consumers to alternate between different medications.


## A New Era for Over-the-Counter Pain Relief


Pain management has long required consumers to choose between different types of medications depending on the nature and duration of their discomfort. Acetaminophen is commonly selected for its fast-acting pain relief, while naproxen sodium is widely used because of its anti-inflammatory properties and longer-lasting effects.


The newly approved Tylenol with Naproxen combines these benefits into a single tablet. According to the manufacturer, pain relief begins in under 30 minutes and continues for as long as 12 hours.


This simplified approach may help consumers avoid managing multiple medications or coordinating separate dosing schedules.


## Why This FDA Approval Matters


The FDA approval represents more than just another product launch. It expands the range of available non-opioid pain management options for adults seeking temporary relief from common aches and pains.


Many healthcare professionals continue to encourage appropriate use of non-opioid pain relievers whenever possible. With concerns about opioid dependence remaining a public health issue, innovations that improve access to effective non-prescription pain treatments are receiving increased attention.


The FDA has also granted the manufacturer a three-year period of marketing exclusivity for this specific over-the-counter formulation, recognizing its unique combination.


## What Makes Tylenol with Naproxen Different?


Unlike traditional pain relievers that contain only one active ingredient, this medication combines two established medicines into one fixed-dose product.


### Acetaminophen


Acetaminophen helps reduce pain and fever. It is commonly chosen because it works relatively quickly and is generally considered gentle on the stomach when used as directed.


### Naproxen Sodium


Naproxen sodium belongs to the class of medicines known as nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs). It works by reducing inflammation that contributes to pain and typically provides longer-lasting relief than many other over-the-counter pain medications.


By combining these ingredients, the product aims to deliver both rapid symptom relief and extended pain control.


## Clinical Studies Supported the Approval


The FDA's decision was supported by eight clinical studies evaluating the effectiveness of the fixed-dose combination.


According to the company, the studies demonstrated that the combination product provided:


- Better pain relief than acetaminophen alone.
- Better pain relief than naproxen sodium alone at the comparable over-the-counter dose.
- Pain relief beginning in less than 30 minutes.
- Continued effectiveness for up to 12 hours after a single dose.


Additional research findings are expected to be presented during future medical conferences.


## Addressing Consumer Frustration with Pain Management


Research cited by Kenvue indicates that many individuals living with persistent pain remain dissatisfied with currently available treatment options.


A company-sponsored consumer survey conducted in March 2026 found that approximately 75 percent of respondents with persistent pain were not fully satisfied with their current methods of pain relief.


For many people, pain management often involves taking multiple medications at different times throughout the day. A fixed-dose combination may simplify this process by reducing the need to coordinate separate products.


## Supporting Non-Opioid Treatment Options


The approval also reflects ongoing efforts to expand access to non-opioid pain management.


Many public health organizations continue to encourage appropriate use of non-opioid therapies whenever suitable. While opioid medications remain important for certain medical conditions, healthcare providers often recommend considering non-opioid options first for temporary pain whenever appropriate.


Tylenol with Naproxen is completely opioid free and is intended for temporary pain relief without requiring a prescription.


## Availability


The manufacturer has announced that Tylenol with Naproxen will soon become available at major retailers throughout the United States.


Although an official nationwide release date has not yet been announced, consumers should expect the product to appear in pharmacies, grocery stores, and other retail locations in the near future.


## Who Should Use It?


Tylenol with Naproxen is intended for adults seeking temporary relief from common pain conditions that may benefit from both fast and extended pain control.


However, consumers should always read the Drug Facts label before use. Individuals with certain medical conditions, including stomach ulcers, kidney disease, liver disease, or allergies to NSAIDs, should consult a healthcare professional before taking this medication.


Pregnant individuals, people taking blood-thinning medications, and those with chronic health conditions should also seek medical advice before using any new pain reliever.


## A Continued Focus on Innovation


Tylenol has remained one of the most recognized names in pain relief for decades. The approval of this combination product builds upon the brand's long history of developing over-the-counter medications for consumers.


The addition of naproxen sodium to its product lineup reflects a growing interest in combination therapies that offer multiple benefits while simplifying treatment.


As more consumers seek convenient, long-lasting, and non-opioid pain relief options, products like Tylenol with Naproxen may play an increasingly important role in everyday pain management.


## Final Thoughts


The FDA approval of Tylenol with Naproxen introduces a first-of-its-kind over-the-counter pain medication that combines acetaminophen and naproxen sodium into a single fixed-dose product.


With reported pain relief beginning in under 30 minutes and lasting up to 12 hours, the medication offers a convenient alternative for individuals looking to manage temporary pain without prescription medications or opioids.


While the product represents an important advancement in over-the-counter pain relief, consumers should continue following labeled dosing instructions and consult healthcare professionals whenever they have questions regarding the safest treatment for their individual health needs.
