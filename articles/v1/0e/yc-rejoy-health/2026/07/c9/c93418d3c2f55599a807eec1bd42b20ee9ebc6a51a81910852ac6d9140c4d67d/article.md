# Zen Principle Naturals Recalls Moringa Capsules Over Possible Salmonella Contamination

A dietary supplement sold across the United States has been recalled after concerns about possible Salmonella contamination. Zen Principle Naturals has voluntarily recalled a specific batch of its Moringa Capsules after an ingredient supplier notified the company that raw moringa leaf powder tested positive for the harmful bacteria.


The recall adds to a growing list of moringa related product recalls in recent months, raising fresh concerns about ingredient safety and quality control within the dietary supplement industry. While no illnesses have been reported in connection with the affected products, health authorities are advising consumers to stop using the recalled capsules immediately.


## Why the Recall Was Issued


Relay Peak Research LLC, which operates under the Zen Principle Naturals brand, announced the voluntary recall on July 19, 2026. According to information published by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration, the recall involves one production lot of Moringa Capsules that may have been manufactured using contaminated moringa leaf powder.


The company said it became aware of the issue after its ingredient supplier informed them that FDA testing detected Salmonella in a batch of raw moringa powder. Since that ingredient had already been used during manufacturing, the company decided to recall the finished capsules as a precaution.


Sales and distribution of the affected products have been stopped while the company continues working with federal regulators.


## Which Products Are Affected?


The recall only applies to products from a single production lot.


The affected products include:


- Zen Principle Naturals Moringa Capsules
- Lot Number: A6FF4
- Best By Date: November 2028
- Available in 180 capsule bottles
- Available in 360 capsule twin packs


The capsules were distributed nationwide between December 2025 and July 2026.


Consumers purchased the supplements primarily through Amazon and the company's official website. A small number of units were also sold through eBay and Etsy.


As of the recall announcement, the company said it had not received any reports of illnesses linked to the product.


## What Should Consumers Do?


Anyone who purchased the recalled Moringa Capsules should stop using them immediately.


Consumers are advised to safely dispose of the product rather than continue taking the capsules. According to the company, customers do not need to return the product to receive a refund.


Individuals seeking additional information or a refund can contact Zen Principle Naturals through its customer support channels.


## Similar Moringa Recalls Earlier This Year


The latest recall follows two separate Salmonella related recalls announced earlier in 2026.


In May, Mogo Moringa voluntarily recalled two lots of its Moringa Oleifera capsules after potential contamination concerns. Although laboratory testing of retained samples did not detect Salmonella, the company proceeded with the recall in cooperation with the FDA as a precautionary measure.


Another recall involved SKS Copack, a California manufacturer that recalled several beverage powders sold under multiple brands. Those products included matcha, taro, smoothie, cappuccino, and ice cream mixes distributed to cafes and restaurants in more than 20 states.


The contamination in that case was linked to nonfat dry milk powder supplied by California Dairies Inc., which had tested positive for Salmonella.


Like the Zen Principle recall, no illnesses were reported in connection with the affected beverage products.


These incidents highlight the importance of monitoring ingredient safety throughout the food and supplement supply chain.


## Understanding Salmonella


Salmonella is a type of bacteria that can cause foodborne illness in humans. Infection usually occurs after consuming contaminated food or dietary supplements.


Symptoms typically develop within several hours to several days after exposure and may include:


- Diarrhea
- Fever
- Stomach cramps
- Nausea
- Vomiting
- Headache


Most healthy adults recover within a few days without specific medical treatment. However, some infections can become more serious.


Young children, older adults, pregnant women, and people with weakened immune systems face a greater risk of severe illness.


In rare situations, Salmonella bacteria can spread beyond the digestive system and enter the bloodstream. This can lead to serious complications including bloodstream infections, heart infections, joint infections, or infected aneurysms that require immediate medical care.


Anyone experiencing severe symptoms such as persistent high fever, dehydration, bloody diarrhea, or prolonged illness should seek medical attention promptly.


## Growing Attention on Supplement Safety


Dietary supplements are widely used by millions of consumers seeking nutritional support. While many products are manufactured under strict quality standards, contamination events occasionally occur when raw ingredients become compromised before production.


Manufacturers typically issue voluntary recalls after identifying potential safety risks or after receiving notification from suppliers or regulatory agencies.


The FDA encourages consumers to pay attention to recall notices and stop using affected products even when no illnesses have been reported. Acting early helps reduce the risk of exposure while investigations continue.


Consumers should also purchase supplements from reputable retailers and regularly check recall announcements for products they use.


## Final Thoughts


The voluntary recall of Zen Principle Naturals Moringa Capsules serves as another reminder that even widely used dietary supplements can occasionally face manufacturing or ingredient safety issues.


Although there have been no confirmed illnesses associated with the recalled capsules, consumers who purchased products from the affected lot should discontinue use and dispose of them. Staying informed about FDA recalls can help protect consumers from unnecessary health risks and ensure potentially contaminated products are removed from circulation before they cause harm.
