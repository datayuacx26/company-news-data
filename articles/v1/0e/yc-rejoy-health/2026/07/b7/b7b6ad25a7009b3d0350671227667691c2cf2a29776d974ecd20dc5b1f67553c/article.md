# Lidl Cookie Recall 2026: FDA Warns Customers in Nine States About Undeclared Allergens

## Consumers Urged to Check Their Pantries After Shortbread Cookie Recall


A popular imported cookie product sold at Lidl grocery stores has been recalled after federal food safety officials warned that the packaging did not properly identify certain allergens. Customers in multiple states have been advised not to eat the affected product if they have sensitivities or allergies to wheat, soy, milk, or eggs.


The recall involves Eridanous Shortbread Cookies with Chocolate Truffle Coating and Apricot Filling, a product sold in an 11.6 ounce package. The cookies were removed from sale because the ingredient and nutrition information on the packaging was not provided in English, creating a potential risk for consumers who need to avoid specific ingredients.


The United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA) announced the recall after determining that the product contained undeclared allergens. When food labels fail to clearly identify ingredients, people with food allergies may unknowingly consume products that could trigger serious reactions.


## What Product Is Included in the Recall?


The recalled item is:


Product name: Eridanous Shortbread Cookies with Chocolate Truffle Coating and Apricot Filling
Package size: 11.6 ounces
Brand: Eridanous
Store: Lidl supermarkets


The affected cookies were sold between July 15 and July 22, 2026. Customers who purchased the product during this period are encouraged to check their packages carefully before consumption.


The main concern is not that the cookies were found to contain harmful contamination, but that the required allergen information was missing in English. Proper labeling allows consumers to make safe choices, especially those managing food allergies or dietary restrictions.


## Which States Are Affected by the Lidl Cookie Recall?


The recall applies to Lidl stores located in nine states and the District of Columbia. The affected areas include:


• Delaware
• District of Columbia
• Georgia
• Maryland
• New Jersey
• New York
• North Carolina
• Pennsylvania
• South Carolina
• Virginia


Customers in these locations who purchased the recalled cookies should inspect their packages and avoid eating them if they have an allergy or sensitivity to wheat, soy, milk, or eggs.


## Why Undeclared Allergens Can Be Dangerous


Food allergies affect millions of people, and even small amounts of an allergen can cause reactions in sensitive individuals. Symptoms can range from mild issues such as itching, hives, or stomach discomfort to severe reactions that require immediate medical attention.


Common food allergens include milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, and soy. Because of this, food manufacturers and retailers must provide accurate labeling so customers can identify potential risks before purchasing or eating a product.


In this case, the concern was related to missing English allergen information on the package. Consumers who cannot read or understand the label may not realize the product contains ingredients they need to avoid.


## Lidl Provides Refund Information for Customers


Lidl has apologized for the inconvenience caused by the recall and stated that customer safety remains a priority. The company said affected shoppers can return the recalled cookies to their local Lidl store for a refund, and customers do not need to provide a receipt.


Retailers often issue recalls as a precautionary measure when there is a possibility that customers could face health risks. Removing affected products from shelves helps prevent additional purchases while the issue is investigated and corrected.


Consumers who have questions about the recall can contact Lidl customer service or check official recall announcements for updated information.


## What Should Customers Do If They Bought These Cookies?


If you purchased Eridanous Shortbread Cookies with Chocolate Truffle Coating and Apricot Filling, follow these steps:


1. Check the packaging details and confirm whether your product matches the recalled item.
2. Do not eat the cookies if you or someone in your household has allergies or sensitivities to wheat, soy, milk, or eggs.
3. Return the product to Lidl for a refund if you have an affected package.
4. Monitor official updates from food safety agencies for any additional recall information.


Consumers who have already eaten the product and experience symptoms of an allergic reaction should seek medical advice.


## The Importance of Checking Food Recall Notices


Food recalls are an important part of protecting public health. Grocery stores, manufacturers, and government agencies regularly monitor products to identify possible safety concerns.


Although recalls can be inconvenient, they help prevent injuries and allow companies to correct problems quickly. Customers can reduce their risk by regularly checking recall announcements, especially when purchasing imported foods or products from unfamiliar brands.


Imported products may sometimes face additional labeling challenges because packaging requirements differ between countries. In the United States, food labels must clearly identify ingredients and major allergens in a way that consumers can understand.


## Final Thoughts


The Lidl cookie recall highlights the importance of accurate food labeling and consumer awareness. While the recalled Eridanous Shortbread Cookies were not removed because of confirmed contamination, the lack of proper allergen information created a possible risk for people with wheat, soy, milk, or egg allergies.


Customers in the affected states should review their purchases, avoid eating the recalled cookies if they have relevant allergies, and return the product to Lidl for a refund if necessary.


Staying informed about food recalls is one of the simplest ways consumers can protect themselves and their families.
