# UK Holidaymakers Warned Over Unsafe Travel Adaptors Sold on Amazon and eBay

## Thousands of British travellers may have purchased potentially dangerous holiday essentials


British holidaymakers are being urged to check their travel adaptors before heading overseas after safety experts discovered that several models sold through popular online marketplaces could pose a serious electrical risk.


An investigation by Electrical Safety First found that a number of travel adaptors purchased from online platforms, including Amazon Marketplace, eBay, AliExpress and TikTok Shop, failed basic safety checks. The charity warned that some of the products could expose users to electric shocks, fires and, in the most severe cases, potentially fatal injuries.


According to information available online, more than 10,000 unsafe travel adaptors may already have reached UK consumers. The findings have raised concerns about the safety of low-cost electrical products sold through online marketplaces, particularly as millions of people prepare for summer holidays abroad.


## Dangerous faults discovered in travel adaptors


Electrical Safety First tested 14 travel adaptors purchased from major online shopping platforms. Every device examined failed at least one important safety requirement.


The most serious problems were found among universal travel adaptors, which are designed to work in multiple countries. Testing showed that several models allowed more than one set of plug pins to become exposed and live while connected to a power supply.


This means a person could potentially touch live electrical parts and come into contact with mains voltage. The risk could become even greater in situations where someone has wet hands or is standing in a damp environment.


Safety inspectors also discovered that all eight universal adaptors tested failed requirements relating to plug pin measurements and mechanical strength. This creates the possibility that plug pins could break while inside a socket, leaving users at risk if they attempt to remove the damaged device.


Other safety concerns included missing protective shutters, which help prevent accidental contact with internal electrical components, missing fuses that could increase fire risks, and poorly designed plug openings that may allow users to touch dangerous electrical parts.


Some adaptors were also found to accept three-pin plugs without providing a proper earth connection. The earth connection is an important safety feature because it helps protect users from electric shock when electrical faults occur.


## Fire safety concerns raised after testing


The investigation also included additional laboratory testing on six adaptors to examine the fire resistance and flammability of the plastic materials used.


All six products failed the specialist assessment, increasing concerns that some devices may not provide adequate protection if they overheat or develop an electrical fault.


Electrical Safety First said that cheap electrical products can sometimes appear to offer good value, but buyers may face serious risks when safety standards are not properly followed.


The charity’s product safety engineer Giuseppe Capanna said shoppers should avoid taking unnecessary risks when purchasing travel equipment and should choose products from trusted retailers with clear safety information.


He warned that holiday preparation should not involve uncertainty over whether essential electrical items could put consumers at risk.


## Online marketplaces respond to safety concerns


Following the findings, several online marketplaces confirmed that the identified listings had been removed.


AliExpress said it had removed the travel adaptor listings highlighted in the investigation and was contacting affected customers regarding recall procedures. The company also stated that it would review other universal travel adaptor listings displaying similar unsafe features.


eBay confirmed that the reported listings had been removed and that additional checks were being carried out to identify similar products.


Amazon also confirmed that the affected products had been taken off sale.


TikTok Shop removed the reported listings as well.


The responses highlight growing pressure on online marketplaces to improve monitoring of third-party sellers and ensure electrical products meet required safety standards before reaching customers.


## How UK travellers can choose safer travel adaptors


Travel adaptors are often considered a simple holiday purchase, but safety experts recommend taking extra care before buying one.


Consumers should consider the following steps:


• Buy electrical products from established retailers with clear contact details and customer support.


• Check that the product includes appropriate safety markings and information.


• Avoid unusually cheap adaptors that appear significantly cheaper than similar products.


• Look for products designed specifically for the country being visited rather than relying on poorly regulated universal devices.


• Avoid damaged adaptors or products showing signs of poor construction.


• Stop using any adaptor that becomes unusually hot, makes unusual noises or shows signs of damage.


Travellers should also remember that a travel adaptor only changes the physical connection between a plug and a socket. It does not automatically convert voltage. Using the wrong electrical equipment overseas can create additional safety risks.


## Aviation safety advice for holiday travellers


The UK Civil Aviation Authority has also reminded travellers to take care when buying electrical accessories for holidays.


The organisation highlighted that trusted products and responsible use of items such as travel adaptors, power banks and other electronic accessories can help reduce safety risks while abroad.


With more people carrying multiple electronic devices on holiday, including smartphones, tablets, cameras and chargers, reliable electrical equipment has become an essential part of modern travel planning.


## What to do if you own one of these adaptors


Anyone who believes they may have purchased an unsafe travel adaptor should stop using it immediately and check whether the seller has issued a recall notice.


Consumers should avoid attempting to repair faulty electrical products themselves. Damaged or unsafe adaptors should be disposed of responsibly according to local waste guidance.


Checking product reviews, seller information and safety details before purchasing can help reduce the risk of buying unsafe electrical goods online.


## Final thoughts


The warning from Electrical Safety First serves as a reminder that holiday essentials should not be chosen based only on price. Travel adaptors may seem like small purchases, but a faulty device can create serious dangers when connected to mains electricity.


UK travellers preparing for overseas trips should take time to choose reliable electrical products from trusted sources. A few extra checks before departure could help prevent accidents and ensure a safer holiday experience.
