# Elderly Care Scandal Highlights Serious Failures in Home Care Oversight

A recent elderly care investigation has raised serious concerns about the protection of vulnerable people receiving support in their own homes. A woman who relied on professional carers was reportedly subjected to months of poor treatment, including inadequate nutrition, poor communication, medication issues, and failures in basic dignity and safety.


The case has sparked renewed debate about the responsibility of care providers and local authorities that commission home care services. It also highlights the importance of stronger monitoring systems to ensure elderly and vulnerable individuals receive the quality of care they deserve.


According to an investigation by the Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman, serious failures occurred in care arrangements commissioned by Portsmouth City Council. The council later acknowledged shortcomings in its oversight and agreed to apologise to the family involved.


## Elderly Woman Experienced Serious Care Failures


The investigation found that the woman’s home care fell far below acceptable standards over several months. Concerns included failures to follow her care plan, poor support with mobility, inaccurate records, and missed or inadequate care visits.


One of the most disturbing findings was that carers reportedly provided food that was unsuitable for the woman’s needs. The investigation stated that she was given stale or inappropriate food, and in one incident, a worker mistakenly served microwaved dog food that had been stored in a freezer.


The food was recorded incorrectly in care records as a lamb meal. The woman reportedly received several mouthfuls before the mistake was discovered.


The incident has raised questions about staff training, supervision, and the checks used to ensure carers are following proper procedures when supporting elderly people with complex needs.


## Concerns Over Nutrition, Medication, and Personal Care


For elderly individuals with health conditions, nutrition and hydration are essential parts of safe care. The investigation found that carers failed to properly monitor and record the woman’s food and fluid intake.


There were also concerns that she was not always provided with the recommended soft diet. CCTV footage reviewed during the investigation reportedly showed concerns about how food was given to her, with the family describing the approach as forceful.


The report also identified problems relating to medication support. The woman was given medication in a way that meant she could not independently access it, creating further concerns about dignity, independence, and proper care planning.


Basic personal care standards were also questioned. The investigation found that the woman was left for extended periods, including occasions when she was reportedly left wet or in darkness overnight.


These failures demonstrate why elderly care services require regular monitoring and strong communication between care workers, families, healthcare professionals, and local authorities.


## Council Oversight Under Scrutiny


The investigation focused not only on the actions of individual care workers but also on the role of Portsmouth City Council in overseeing the services it had commissioned.


The council had contracted Radis Community Care to provide support. However, the ombudsman found that the council failed to provide sufficient independent oversight after concerns were raised.


The report stated that the council relied too heavily on the care provider’s internal investigation rather than conducting its own thorough review. The ombudsman warned that local authorities cannot avoid responsibility simply because care services are delivered by an external company.


Social care providers are often contracted by councils, but councils remain responsible for ensuring commissioned services meet required standards.


The ombudsman described the situation as a serious failure and stated that safeguarding responsibilities continue even after a care contract has been signed.


## Impact on the Family


The woman’s family said they were deeply affected by what happened and struggled to process their loss after discovering the extent of the problems with her care.


They also found her Do Not Attempt Resuscitation (DNAR) document stored away in a cupboard, with uncertainty over whether care staff were aware of its details.


For families of elderly relatives receiving professional care, trust is a crucial part of the relationship. Many people depend on care workers to provide support, safety, and compassion when their loved ones can no longer manage independently.


Cases involving poor treatment can create significant emotional distress and raise wider concerns among families who rely on home care services.


## Actions Taken After the Investigation


Following the findings, Portsmouth City Council stated that it no longer uses Radis Community Care. The council agreed to apologise to the family, refund care fees covering several months, and provide financial compensation for the distress caused.


Radis Community Care provides social care and support services for vulnerable adults and children in England and Wales.


The case has also contributed to wider discussions about the future of adult social care in England. Questions continue about funding, staffing levels, training standards, and how services can better protect vulnerable people.


## Why Elderly Care Monitoring Needs Improvement


The case highlights several key areas where elderly care services need stronger safeguards:


### Better Staff Training


Care workers need regular training on nutrition, medication support, safeguarding responsibilities, and person-centred care.


### Stronger Care Inspections


Local authorities must actively monitor the services they commission instead of relying only on providers to investigate complaints.


### Clear Communication With Families


Families often notice changes in a loved one’s condition before official systems do. Their concerns should be treated seriously and investigated promptly.


### Accurate Care Records


Detailed and honest records are essential for protecting vulnerable people. False or incomplete documentation can hide serious problems.


## The Future of Adult Social Care


The UK’s adult social care system continues to face significant challenges, including staff shortages, rising demand, and financial pressure. As the population ages, ensuring safe and respectful care will remain a major priority.


The case involving Portsmouth City Council serves as a reminder that elderly people receiving care at home deserve dignity, respect, and protection. Outsourcing care does not remove the responsibility of councils or providers to maintain high standards.


Improving social care requires investment, accountability, and a commitment to putting vulnerable people at the centre of every decision.
