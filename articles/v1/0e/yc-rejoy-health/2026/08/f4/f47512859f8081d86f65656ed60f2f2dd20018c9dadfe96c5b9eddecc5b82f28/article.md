# Surgeon Removed from Medical Register After Serious Surgical Error Put Patient's Life at Risk

Medical professionals are entrusted with carrying out life-saving procedures with precision, care, and accountability. However, when serious mistakes occur in the operating room, the consequences can be devastating for patients and their families.


A recent decision by the UK Medical Practitioners Tribunal Service (MPTS) has drawn significant attention after an NHS locum surgeon was permanently removed from the medical register following a catastrophic surgical error that experts described as one of the worst they had ever encountered.


The case has sparked discussions about patient safety, surgical oversight, professional accountability, and the importance of maintaining high clinical standards across healthcare systems.


## A Surgical Procedure That Went Terribly Wrong


Dr. Yasser Adly Abdel Rahman, an Egyptian-trained surgeon who was working as an NHS locum, performed an emergency bowel operation on a young patient at Royal Oldham Hospital on 25 August 2020.


According to evidence presented during the tribunal hearing, the surgery was carried out incorrectly, resulting in a highly unusual and dangerous reconstruction of the patient's digestive system.


Instead of restoring the bowel correctly, the procedure connected the organs in a way that created a closed loop. This caused bowel contents leaving the stomach to circulate back into the stomach rather than continuing through the digestive tract.


Medical experts stated that this type of reconstruction is not recognized in modern surgical practice and posed an immediate threat to the patient's survival.


## Patient Endured Severe Pain


Following the operation, the patient experienced intense and uncontrollable pain.


Despite clear signs that something had gone wrong, the tribunal heard that Dr. Rahman failed to acknowledge the possibility of a surgical mistake.


Concerns were reportedly raised by:


- The patient's parents
- Hospital medical staff
- Other healthcare professionals involved in the patient's care


Even with these warnings, the surgeon allegedly did not investigate whether complications from the surgery were responsible for the patient's deteriorating condition.


Fortunately, another surgeon later performed emergency corrective surgery, preventing what experts believed could have been a fatal outcome.


## Expert Witness Delivers Damning Assessment


One of the most striking moments during the hearing came from an experienced consultant general and colorectal surgeon acting as an expert witness for the General Medical Council (GMC).


The expert described the operation as something that was "not known to man," highlighting how unusual and medically unacceptable the procedure was.


The tribunal also heard that the mistakes made during surgery were among the most severe surgical errors imaginable.


Such testimony played a significant role in demonstrating the seriousness of the misconduct and its potential consequences for patient safety.


## Tribunal Finds Serious Professional Misconduct


The Medical Practitioners Tribunal Service reviewed both the surgical errors and Dr. Rahman's conduct after the operation.


The panel concluded that:


- The surgical procedure fell well below accepted medical standards.
- The surgeon failed to recognize or properly respond to postoperative complications.
- The patient was exposed to unnecessary suffering and significant risk.
- Confidence in the medical profession would be undermined if such conduct were not addressed appropriately.


Ultimately, the tribunal ruled that the misconduct was fundamentally incompatible with continued medical registration.


As a result, Dr. Rahman was erased from the UK medical register, meaning he is no longer permitted to practise medicine in the United Kingdom.


## Previous Restrictions Were Ignored


The case also involved concerns unrelated to the operation itself.


Following the initial investigation, restrictions were placed on Dr. Rahman's ability to practise medicine in July 2021.


However, tribunal documents stated that he later secured employment as a locum doctor at Affidea Express Care Clinic in Ireland in February 2022 despite those restrictions.


The panel considered this a serious breach of professional obligations and viewed it as an aggravating factor when determining the final sanction.


Ignoring regulatory restrictions can significantly affect disciplinary decisions because it raises further concerns about a doctor's willingness to comply with professional standards.


## Doctor Denied Responsibility


Dr. Rahman did not attend the tribunal hearing and was not represented by legal counsel.


Instead, he maintained that he had become the victim of unfair treatment.


According to tribunal records, he claimed he had been made a "scapegoat" following the operation and described the investigation as a "witch hunt."


Despite these assertions, the tribunal found sufficient evidence to support the allegations of misconduct.


The panel determined that the seriousness of both the surgical errors and the subsequent professional conduct justified permanent removal from the medical register.


## Why This Case Matters


Cases involving surgical negligence are relatively uncommon, but they receive considerable public attention because they directly affect trust in healthcare systems.


Patients undergoing emergency surgery are often in vulnerable situations where they rely entirely on medical teams to deliver safe and effective treatment.


Professional regulatory bodies such as the General Medical Council and the Medical Practitioners Tribunal Service exist to ensure that doctors maintain appropriate standards of competence, ethics, and patient care.


When those standards are not met, disciplinary action helps protect future patients while maintaining public confidence in the profession.


## The Importance of Accountability in Healthcare


Healthcare professionals work in high-pressure environments where complications can occur despite appropriate treatment.


However, regulators distinguish between unavoidable medical complications and conduct that falls significantly below accepted professional standards.


Accountability involves more than technical skill. It also requires:


- Recognizing complications promptly
- Responding appropriately to patient deterioration
- Communicating honestly with colleagues and families
- Following professional regulatory requirements
- Cooperating with investigations


Maintaining these standards helps ensure safer outcomes for patients while reinforcing public trust in healthcare providers.


## Lessons for Medical Professionals


This tribunal highlights several important lessons for surgeons and healthcare organizations.


Continuous training, peer review, adherence to clinical guidelines, and effective postoperative monitoring all play critical roles in reducing preventable harm.


Equally important is a willingness to acknowledge when complications arise and to seek assistance quickly if concerns are raised.


Early intervention can often prevent serious outcomes and improve patient recovery.


## Conclusion


The removal of Dr. Yasser Adly Abdel Rahman from the UK medical register represents one of the most serious disciplinary outcomes available to medical regulators.


The tribunal concluded that the combination of severe surgical errors, failure to recognize postoperative complications, and breaches of professional restrictions made continued registration incompatible with patient safety.


While most medical procedures are performed successfully every day by highly skilled healthcare professionals, this case serves as a reminder of the importance of clinical competence, transparency, accountability, and robust regulatory oversight in protecting patients.
