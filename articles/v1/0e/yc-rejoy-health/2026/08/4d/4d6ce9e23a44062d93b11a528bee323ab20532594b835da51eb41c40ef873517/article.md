# Keep Flies Out of Your Home Naturally With These Simple Kitchen Items and DIY Tricks

Flies can quickly become one of the most annoying household pests, especially during the warmer months when doors and windows stay open for longer. While chemical insect sprays may offer a quick fix, many homeowners are now looking for natural, affordable, and safer alternatives that help keep flies away without filling the house with harsh chemicals.


Fortunately, there are several easy methods that use everyday kitchen ingredients to deter flies or trap them before they become a bigger problem. From roasted coffee beans to homemade vinegar traps, these simple solutions require very little effort and can help reduce unwanted insects around your home.


## Why Flies Enter Your Home


Flies are naturally attracted to food, sweet smells, moisture, and warm environments. Leaving fruit uncovered, overflowing bins, pet food, or standing water can quickly invite flies indoors.


Once inside, flies reproduce rapidly, making it important to deal with them before they become a larger nuisance. Preventing them from entering your home is often more effective than trying to eliminate them afterward.


## Roast Coffee Beans to Repel Flies


One of the easiest natural remedies involves ordinary coffee beans.


According to pest control expert Vicki Hall, roasting coffee beans in the oven releases a strong aroma that flies dislike. While many people enjoy the rich smell of freshly roasted coffee, flies tend to avoid it.


To try this method:


- Spread whole coffee beans evenly on a baking tray.
- Roast them in the oven for around 10 to 15 minutes.
- Allow the aroma to spread naturally throughout your kitchen or living space.


The pleasant coffee scent can help discourage flies from entering through open windows and doors.


Some coffee shops are believed to benefit from this natural aroma, which may contribute to fewer flies being seen inside.


## Make an Apple Cider Vinegar Fly Trap


Apple cider vinegar is another common household product that can help reduce flies indoors.


Flies are attracted to the sweet fermented smell of the vinegar. By adding a few drops of washing up liquid, the liquid's surface tension is broken, making it difficult for flies to escape once they land.


### How to Make the Trap


You only need:


- Apple cider vinegar
- A small bowl, mug, or jar
- A few drops of washing up liquid


Pour a small amount of vinegar into the container and add several drops of washing up liquid. Place the trap near fruit bowls, kitchen counters, or windows where flies tend to gather.


Over time, the trap may capture fruit flies and other small flying insects without using harmful chemicals.


## Create a DIY Bottle Fly Trap


If you have a larger fly problem, a homemade bottle trap can be another effective option.


This simple project requires only a used plastic bottle and a few household ingredients.


### What You Need


- A clean 2 litre plastic bottle
- Apple cider vinegar
- Water
- Scissors


### Instructions


1. Remove the bottle cap.
2. Cut the top section of the bottle approximately two to three inches below the neck.
3. Pour a mixture of apple cider vinegar and water into the bottom half.
4. Turn the top section upside down.
5. Insert it into the lower half to create a funnel.


Flies are attracted into the bottle through the funnel but often struggle to find their way back out.


This inexpensive trap can be placed near bins, patios, kitchens, or other areas where flies frequently appear.


## Install Fly Screens for Long Term Protection


Natural remedies work well, but preventing flies from entering your home in the first place is often the best solution.


Magnetic fly screens fitted to windows allow fresh air to circulate while blocking insects from entering.


These mesh screens are particularly useful during summer when windows remain open throughout the day.


For homes located near gardens, parks, woodland, or farmland, fly screens can significantly reduce the number of insects indoors without affecting ventilation.


## Additional Ways to Reduce Flies Naturally


Alongside traps and repellents, a few good household habits can make your home less attractive to flies.


### Keep Food Covered


Avoid leaving fruit, baked goods, or leftovers uncovered on kitchen counters.


### Empty Bins Frequently


Food waste quickly attracts flies, especially during hot weather. Regularly empty rubbish bins and clean them thoroughly.


### Clean Spills Promptly


Sticky drink spills, crumbs, and grease can all attract insects. Wiping surfaces regularly helps reduce their food sources.


### Store Fruit Properly


Very ripe fruit is particularly attractive to fruit flies. Refrigerating ripe fruit where appropriate can reduce infestations.


### Eliminate Standing Water


Although houseflies mainly seek food, moisture also attracts many insects. Empty trays, buckets, or containers where water collects.


## Are Natural Fly Repellents Effective?


Natural remedies generally work best as part of a wider prevention strategy rather than as an instant cure.


Coffee bean aromas may discourage flies from entering certain areas, while vinegar traps can reduce existing fly populations over time.


Results may vary depending on the severity of the infestation, the cleanliness of the home, and nearby environmental conditions.


For persistent or unusually large infestations, consulting a licensed pest control professional may be the most effective solution.


## Final Thoughts


Keeping flies out of your home does not always require expensive products or harsh chemicals. Simple household ingredients like coffee beans, apple cider vinegar, and washing up liquid can help reduce fly activity while keeping your living space more pleasant.


Combined with good hygiene practices and properly fitted fly screens, these low cost methods offer an easy way to discourage flies naturally throughout the warmer months.


Whether you are dealing with fruit flies in the kitchen or houseflies entering through open windows, these practical DIY techniques can help create a cleaner and more comfortable home.
