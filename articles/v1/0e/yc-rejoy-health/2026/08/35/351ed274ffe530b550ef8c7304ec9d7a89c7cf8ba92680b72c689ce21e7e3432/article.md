# Starbucks Faces Lawsuit After Customer Alleges Hot Tea Spill Caused Severe Injuries

A Starbucks customer in San Diego has filed a lawsuit alleging that a hot tea spill caused serious and permanent injuries after the drink was placed on an unstable table.


The case involves Vladimir Podlipskiy, a 63-year-old oil and gas executive, who claims he was meeting with colleagues at a Starbucks location in San Diego in August 2024 when the incident occurred. According to the lawsuit, a table he was using was unstable, while an employee allegedly asked him several times to move his chair closer to it.


The complaint claims that the combination of an unsecured hot beverage and an allegedly defective table created a foreseeable safety risk.


## Customer alleges hot tea spill caused severe injuries


According to court documents, Podlipskiy had ordered a hot tea while attending a meeting at the coffee shop. He alleges that the drink was placed on a table that was loose or unstable.


The lawsuit states that an employee knew, or should have known, about the alleged condition of the table. Despite this, Podlipskiy says he was repeatedly asked to move closer to the table while the hot beverage remained nearby.


The complaint alleges that the tea eventually spilled onto his lap and groin area, resulting in serious burns.


Podlipskiy claims that his injuries included significant physical pain, emotional distress, disfigurement and lasting nerve damage. His lawsuit also alleges that the injuries affected his sexual function and resulted in continuing medical needs.


These allegations have not been proven in court.


## What does the lawsuit claim?


The lawsuit accuses Starbucks of failing to take reasonable precautions to protect customers from foreseeable risks.


Among the allegations are claims that the company failed to properly inspect and maintain customer tables and seating areas. The complaint also alleges that the hot drink was not adequately secured and that the lid was not properly fitted.


Podlipskiy argues that Starbucks had a responsibility to provide a reasonably safe environment and to take precautions when serving hot beverages.


His legal team says the incident was witnessed by other people who were present at the coffee shop.


Attorney Nick Rowley, who represents Podlipskiy, has previously represented customers involved in hot beverage injury cases against Starbucks. He has argued that companies serving extremely hot drinks must take appropriate steps to prevent spills and serious injuries.


## Starbucks denies the allegations


Starbucks has denied the claims made in Podlipskiy's lawsuit.


In a court filing, the company disputed the allegations and suggested that questions could arise regarding whether the plaintiff took appropriate steps to reduce the effects of his alleged injuries.


The company also raised issues concerning medical treatment and mitigation of damages. In legal terms, mitigation can involve whether an injured person took reasonable steps to limit the consequences of an injury.


The dispute will now proceed through the legal process, where evidence from both sides can be examined.


A joint discovery plan is expected to be filed in September. Discovery can allow lawyers for both parties to gather documents, interview witnesses and obtain other evidence relevant to the case.


## Starbucks has faced previous hot drink litigation


The new lawsuit has attracted attention partly because it resembles other legal cases involving hot beverages served by Starbucks.


In a separate case involving a Postmates driver, a jury previously awarded substantial damages after the plaintiff alleged that a hot tea spilled onto his lap while he was collecting an order from Starbucks.


That earlier case also involved allegations of severe burns and lasting injuries.


The litigation has contributed to wider discussions about the responsibilities of restaurants and coffee chains when handling beverages served at very high temperatures.


Hot beverage lawsuits are not limited to Starbucks. Airlines, restaurants and other businesses have also faced claims involving customers or workers who suffered burns after hot drinks were spilled.


## The wider history of hot coffee lawsuits


One of the best-known cases involving a hot beverage occurred in the United States in the early 1990s.


Stella Liebeck, a 79-year-old woman, was seriously burned after spilling McDonald's coffee on herself while sitting in a vehicle in Albuquerque, New Mexico.


The case became widely discussed in American popular culture. However, the circumstances were more serious than many jokes about the lawsuit suggested. Liebeck suffered extensive burns and required medical treatment.


Her case ultimately resulted in a jury award that was later reduced by a judge.


The lawsuit became an important example in debates about product safety, corporate responsibility and the legal consequences of serving extremely hot beverages.


## What happens next in the Starbucks case?


The Podlipskiy case remains unresolved, meaning there has been no final court determination that Starbucks was responsible for his injuries.


The plaintiff is seeking damages for his alleged physical injuries, emotional suffering, medical expenses and other losses. The exact amount of compensation will depend on the evidence presented and any eventual settlement or court decision.


As the case develops, both sides are expected to present evidence concerning the condition of the table, the way the tea was served, the condition of the cup and lid, witness accounts and the extent of Podlipskiy's alleged injuries.


The case could also draw attention to safety procedures used by coffee shops when customers are served beverages at high temperatures.


For customers, the incident highlights the potential risks associated with hot drinks and unstable surfaces in busy cafes. For businesses, cases such as this demonstrate the importance of maintaining furniture, checking service procedures and responding promptly to potential hazards.


For now, the allegations remain contested. Starbucks has denied liability, while Podlipskiy's legal team maintains that the company failed to take reasonable precautions.


The court proceedings will determine whether the allegations are supported by the evidence and whether the plaintiff is entitled to damages.
