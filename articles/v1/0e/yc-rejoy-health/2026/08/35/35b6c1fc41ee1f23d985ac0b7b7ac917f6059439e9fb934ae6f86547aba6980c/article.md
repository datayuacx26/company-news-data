# Boy Thrown From Tate Modern Balcony Leaves Hospital After Years of Recovery

A young boy who survived one of the most shocking incidents in recent UK history has reached a significant milestone in his long recovery journey. Nearly seven years after being thrown from the Tate Modern viewing platform in London, the child has finally been discharged from hospital, bringing hope to his family and supporters around the world.


The French boy, who was just six years old at the time of the attack in August 2019, suffered catastrophic injuries after falling approximately 100 feet from the gallery's tenth floor. His recovery has required years of intensive medical care, rehabilitation, and unwavering family support. A recent update shared by his family has offered encouraging news, revealing that he has returned home and even managed to briefly attend school before the summer holidays.


## A Remarkable Recovery Journey


The incident occurred during what was meant to be a family holiday in London. The child was visiting the Tate Modern with his parents when he was suddenly attacked by teenager Jonty Bravery, who threw him over the viewing platform.


The fall resulted in life-threatening injuries, including a brain hemorrhage, multiple broken bones, and severe trauma that changed the course of his life forever. Medical professionals worked tirelessly to save him, and his survival was described as extraordinary considering the height of the fall.


Over the following years, the young survivor underwent countless surgeries, rehabilitation programs, and specialist treatments aimed at helping him regain physical and cognitive abilities.


## Family Shares Positive Update


The boy's family recently shared encouraging news through a GoFundMe page established to help cover ongoing medical expenses.


According to the update, the child has now left hospital after a lengthy stay. His parents explained that remaining in hospital had become emotionally difficult because he desperately wanted to return to a normal childhood, spend time with classmates, and continue his education.


One of the happiest moments for the family was seeing him return to school shortly before the summer break. Teachers adapted lessons to meet his individual needs, allowing him to reconnect with friends while continuing his learning.


His family said that seeing him smile again after reuniting with classmates marked an emotional turning point in his recovery.


## Continuing Rehabilitation


Although leaving hospital represents a major achievement, the recovery process is far from complete.


The child continues to participate in several specialist rehabilitation programs, including:


- Physiotherapy
- Speech and language therapy
- Neuropsychological rehabilitation
- Educational support
- Adaptive swimming lessons


Each therapy plays an important role in helping him improve his mobility, communication skills, confidence, and overall quality of life.


Recovery from severe brain injuries often takes many years, and specialists emphasize that progress usually comes gradually through consistent treatment and family support.


## Summer Plans Focus on Recovery and Happiness


Following his discharge, the family hopes to give their son a memorable summer while continuing his rehabilitation.


Among the activities planned are a tandem paragliding experience and a seaside holiday with close friends. These experiences are intended to celebrate his progress while creating positive memories after years spent inside hospitals and rehabilitation centers.


The child also plans to continue adaptive swimming and receive mathematics tutoring to catch up on lessons missed during treatment.


His parents hope these activities will help him regain confidence before returning to school for the new academic year.


## Community Support Made a Difference


Throughout the recovery journey, thousands of people from around the world have offered financial and emotional support.


A GoFundMe campaign established by Surrey nurse Vicky Diplacto has raised more than £500,000 to assist the family with medical costs, rehabilitation, travel expenses, and long-term care.


The fundraiser has become a symbol of international compassion, with donors expressing admiration for the young survivor's courage and determination.


The family has repeatedly thanked supporters for helping make specialized treatment possible during the difficult years that followed the attack.


## The Tate Modern Incident


The attack occurred on 4 August 2019 inside London's Tate Modern gallery.


Jonty Bravery, who was 17 years old at the time, was living in supported accommodation but had been allowed to leave without supervision.


Investigators later revealed that Bravery intended to seriously harm someone that day. He selected the young tourist before lifting him over the viewing platform barrier.


Emergency responders rushed to the scene, and the child was transported to hospital in critical condition.


The shocking attack received widespread media attention both in the UK and internationally because of its seemingly random nature and the age of the victim.


## Criminal Proceedings


Jonty Bravery pleaded guilty to attempted murder and received a life sentence with a minimum term of 15 years.


His case has continued to attract attention due to incidents that occurred while he has been detained.


Earlier in 2026, Bravery was sentenced to an additional 16 weeks in prison after assaulting two nurses at Broadmoor Hospital, a high-security psychiatric facility in Berkshire.


This was not his first violent incident while in custody. In 2020, he also received a prison sentence after attacking members of staff at the same hospital.


The case has continued to raise questions about mental health care, public safety, and supervision of high-risk individuals.


## Public Response


The remarkable resilience shown by the young survivor has inspired people across Europe and beyond.


Many supporters have followed his recovery through periodic updates shared by the family. Messages of encouragement continue to arrive from people touched by his story.


Medical professionals often describe recovery from severe traumatic brain injuries as unpredictable. Every milestone, whether returning to school, speaking more clearly, or regaining physical strength, represents months of dedication and hard work.


For many observers, the family's determination has become just as inspiring as the child's courage.


## Looking Ahead


Leaving hospital marks a major chapter in the young boy's recovery, but his journey is ongoing.


Continued rehabilitation, educational support, and family care will remain essential as he works toward greater independence.


His recent return to school, renewed enthusiasm for learning, and plans for enjoyable summer activities all suggest meaningful progress after years of unimaginable challenges.


While the physical and emotional effects of the attack may remain for years to come, the latest update offers hope that brighter days lie ahead for the young survivor and his family.

Support from medical professionals, educators, charitable donors, friends, and loved ones has played a vital role in helping this young boy continue moving forward. As he prepares for another school year and continues rehabilitation, many people around the world will undoubtedly continue wishing him strength, happiness, and continued recovery.
