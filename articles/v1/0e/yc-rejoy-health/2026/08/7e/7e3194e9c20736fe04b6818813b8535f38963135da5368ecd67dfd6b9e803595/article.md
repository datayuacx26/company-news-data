# Amazon Toddler Step Stool Recall: Thousands of Children’s Towers Pulled Over Serious Safety Risks

Parents and caregivers are being urged to stop using certain toddler step stools sold through Amazon after safety officials announced recalls involving more than 130,000 units. The recalled products have been linked to fall, tipping, and entrapment risks that could lead to serious injuries or, in extreme cases, death.


The recalls involve two popular children’s tower stool brands: Woodure children’s tower kitchen step stools and HARPPA Nordi foldable toddler towers. According to the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC), both products may become unstable during use, creating a danger for young children who rely on them while helping in the kitchen or reaching higher surfaces.


Parents who own one of these products are advised to immediately stop allowing children to use the stools and contact the manufacturers for available repair solutions.


## Woodure Toddler Tower Stool Recall Details


The first recall affects Woodure branded children’s tower kitchen step stools. The recalled models include WD1764, WD1357, and WD1720.


These wooden toddler towers were designed to help children safely stand at kitchen counters, sinks, and other elevated areas. However, reports received by safety officials indicate that some units may become unstable and tip over during use.


The affected Woodure stools are made from plywood and measure approximately 18 inches deep, 16.9 inches wide, and 34.6 inches tall. Consumers can identify the recalled products by checking the model number engraved underneath the bottom step.


Approximately 91,000 Woodure stools were sold through Amazon between July 2024 and June 2026. The products were priced between $60 and $100.


The company has received 22 reports involving instability or tip overs. Among those reports, 15 children suffered injuries including cuts, bruises, and scrapes.


Parents who have one of these recalled stools should move the product away from children immediately. The company is offering repair kits to help address the safety issue.


Consumers looking for additional information can contact Woodure through its recall support service by phone at 844-687-1918, email atwoodure-service@hotmail.com , or through the company’s online recall page.


## HARPPA Nordi Foldable Toddler Tower Recall


A second recall affects HARPPA Nordi foldable toddler towers with model number HANS0002.


These toddler towers were sold in several colour options, including white, black, and blue, with natural wood coloured sections on the platform, step, and safety bars.


The model number can be found on a label located underneath the stool platform.


The recall covers approximately 47,166 units sold through Amazon and the HARPPA website between September 2023 and June 2026. These products were sold for around $130.


According to the CPSC, HARPPA has received three reports of the toddler towers collapsing or tipping over. Two children were reportedly injured, suffering injuries such as cuts, bruises, and scrapes.


The product packaging also includes a warning stating that children have suffered severe head injuries, including skull fractures, after falling from the product. The warning highlights that falls can happen suddenly if children are not properly secured.


Consumers who own the recalled HARPPA toddler tower should immediately stop using it and keep it away from children. The company is providing free repair kits, including shipping, to affected customers.


For further assistance, customers can contact HARPPA at 833-471-9321, emailrecall@harppababy.com , or visit the company’s recall website.


## Why Toddler Tower Safety Matters


Toddler towers have become increasingly popular among families because they allow young children to participate in everyday activities such as cooking, washing hands, and learning new skills. These products can support independence and development when they are properly designed and used safely.


However, because toddler towers elevate children several feet above the ground, stability and construction quality are extremely important. A small design flaw, loose component, or unstable structure can increase the risk of falls.


Experts recommend that parents always check children’s furniture for signs of damage, loose screws, or weakened parts. Caregivers should also supervise children whenever they are using elevated products.


A product recall is an important safety warning that helps prevent further injuries. Even if a child has used a recalled item without problems in the past, continued use may still create a risk.


## What Parents Should Do After a Recall


If you believe you own a recalled toddler stool, follow these steps:


1. Stop using the product immediately.
2. Remove it from areas where children can access it.
3. Check the model number and product details.
4. Contact the manufacturer for repair options or further instructions.
5. Follow all safety guidance provided by official recall authorities.


Parents should avoid attempting unofficial repairs because changes that are not approved by the manufacturer may not fully correct the safety problem.


## Staying Updated on Children’s Product Recalls


Child safety recalls happen when manufacturers, regulators, or consumers identify possible risks with products already available on the market. Staying informed can help families prevent accidents and protect children from avoidable dangers.


The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission regularly publishes recall notices covering toys, furniture, baby products, household equipment, and other consumer goods.


Families who regularly purchase children’s products online should check recall announcements and register products whenever possible. Registration allows manufacturers to contact customers directly if a safety issue is discovered.
