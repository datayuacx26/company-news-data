# Cialis and Erectile Dysfunction Drugs Linked to Possible Glaucoma Risk: What Men Should Know About Eye Health

Medicines used to treat erectile dysfunction have helped millions of men improve their sexual health and quality of life. However, new research has raised questions about whether some of these medications could be linked to an increased risk of certain eye conditions, including glaucoma.


A recent study has suggested that men who use tadalafil, a medication commonly sold under the brand name Cialis, may have a higher chance of developing glaucoma compared with those who do not use the drug. While the findings have attracted attention, researchers have stressed that the study does not prove that erectile dysfunction medicines directly cause vision problems.


Understanding the possible connection between erectile dysfunction treatments and eye health can help men make informed decisions and discuss any concerns with their healthcare providers.


## What Is Tadalafil and Why Is It Used?


Tadalafil belongs to a group of medicines called phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitors, often known as PDE5 inhibitors. These medications work by increasing blood flow to certain areas of the body.


For men with erectile dysfunction, tadalafil helps improve blood flow to the penis, making it easier to achieve and maintain an erection. Unlike some similar medications, tadalafil can remain active in the body for a longer period.


The medicine is also prescribed for some men with symptoms related to an enlarged prostate, also known as benign prostatic hyperplasia. In these cases, some patients may take tadalafil regularly over a longer period rather than only when needed.


Because millions of men experience erectile dysfunction as they get older, medications such as tadalafil, sildenafil, and similar treatments are widely used around the world.


## What Did the Study Find?


Researchers from National Taiwan University Hospital examined health information from approximately 74,000 men over the age of 40. The participants were followed for up to five years to examine whether tadalafil use was associated with glaucoma.


The study found that men prescribed tadalafil appeared to have:


• Around a 22% higher likelihood of developing glaucoma overall
• A higher risk of increased pressure inside the eye
• A higher likelihood of developing primary open-angle glaucoma, the most common form of the disease


The researchers also found that some tadalafil users were more likely to require treatment for eye problems.


However, it is important to understand that these results show an association, not proof of cause and effect. A study can identify a pattern between two factors, but it cannot always explain whether one directly causes the other.


## How Could Erectile Dysfunction Medication Affect the Eyes?


PDE5 inhibitors work by relaxing blood vessels and improving circulation. This process helps increase blood flow and supports erections.


Scientists believe that changes in blood flow could potentially affect pressure regulation inside the eye. If pressure within the eye becomes too high, it may damage the optic nerve, the connection between the eye and brain that allows vision.


Damage to the optic nerve is the main feature of glaucoma. Over time, untreated glaucoma can lead to permanent vision loss.


Researchers have suggested that longer-term daily use of tadalafil may have different effects compared with occasional use. More research is needed to understand whether the medication itself increases glaucoma risk or whether other factors are involved.


## What Is Glaucoma?


Glaucoma is a group of eye diseases that damage the optic nerve. It is one of the leading causes of irreversible blindness worldwide.


The condition is more common in older adults, particularly people over the age of 50. Many people do not notice symptoms in the early stages because glaucoma often develops slowly.


Regular eye examinations are important because opticians can detect warning signs before serious vision loss occurs.


Common symptoms of glaucoma may include:


• Gradual loss of peripheral vision
• Difficulty seeing in low light
• Blurred vision
• Eye discomfort or pain in some cases


A sudden form of glaucoma, called acute angle-closure glaucoma, can develop quickly and requires urgent medical attention.


Warning signs may include:


• Severe eye pain
• Redness of the eye
• Sudden blurred vision
• Seeing coloured rings around lights
• Headache
• Nausea or vomiting


Anyone experiencing sudden vision changes should seek emergency medical care.


## Should Men Stop Taking Cialis or Other Erectile Dysfunction Medicines?


The study does not mean that men should stop taking tadalafil without medical advice.


Erectile dysfunction medicines are considered effective treatments for many men, and doctors prescribe them after considering a person’s overall health, medical history, and potential risks.


Stopping medication suddenly without speaking to a healthcare professional may not be the right approach. Men who use tadalafil and are concerned about eye health should discuss their situation with their doctor or an eye specialist.


Doctors may consider factors such as:


• Age
• Family history of glaucoma
• Existing eye conditions
• Other medications being taken
• How frequently tadalafil is used


## Previous Research Has Produced Mixed Results


The relationship between erectile dysfunction medicines and glaucoma remains uncertain.


Some previous research has raised concerns about possible eye-related effects, while other studies have suggested that PDE5 inhibitors may not increase glaucoma risk and could potentially have protective effects.


Because research findings have not been consistent, scientists continue to investigate how these medications influence eye pressure and optic nerve health.


Further studies involving larger groups of patients and longer follow-up periods may provide clearer answers.


## Protecting Your Eye Health


Regardless of medication use, maintaining good eye health is important, especially as people age.


Helpful steps include:


• Having regular eye tests
• Managing conditions such as high blood pressure and diabetes
• Protecting eyes from injury
• Informing healthcare providers about all medicines being taken
• Seeking medical advice for unusual vision changes


Early detection is one of the most effective ways to prevent serious glaucoma-related vision loss.


## Final Thoughts


New research has suggested a possible connection between tadalafil, a common erectile dysfunction medication, and an increased risk of glaucoma. However, the findings do not prove that the medicine causes the condition.


For most men, erectile dysfunction treatments remain an important and effective option. The best approach is to understand potential risks, attend regular eye checks, and discuss any concerns with a qualified healthcare professional.


As research continues, doctors and scientists hope to better understand how medications that affect blood flow may influence eye health.
