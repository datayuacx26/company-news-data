# Urgent UK Recall: Popular Toy Story Kids’ Water Bottle Pulled From Home Bargains Over Choking Risk

## Parents Warned After Popular Kids’ Bottle Is Recalled Due to Loose Part Hazard


A popular children’s product sold across the UK has been urgently recalled after safety checks revealed a potential choking hazard. Parents and guardians have been advised to stop children from using the item immediately and to keep it away from young children until it has been returned or safely disposed of.


The recalled product is a Toy Story themed water bottle sold by discount retailer Home Bargains. The recall was issued after it was discovered that the bottle’s spout may detach from the lid during use. This creates a small loose component that could become a choking risk for children.


The product was available for purchase from May 2026 as part of a promotional release linked to the launch of Pixar’s Toy Story 5 movie. Customers who purchased the affected bottles are being asked to check the batch information and return any impacted items for a full refund.


## Which Toy Story Water Bottles Are Affected?


The recall applies only to specific batches of the Toy Story water bottle sold through Home Bargains stores.


The affected batch numbers are:


- 11449732
- 11449736
- 11449737
- 11449738
- 11449742
- 11449743
- 11449744


Customers should check the batch code on the product price tag or the care label inside the bottle to confirm whether their item is included in the recall.


At this time, no other batches of the Toy Story water bottle have been reported as affected.


## Why Has the Children’s Bottle Been Recalled?


The main concern identified with the product is the possibility that the spout can separate from the lid. If this happens, the detached piece may become small enough for a child to place in their mouth, increasing the risk of choking.


Young children are particularly vulnerable to choking incidents because they may not recognise unsafe objects or understand the danger of loose parts. Products designed for children are therefore subject to strict safety standards to reduce risks caused by detachable components.


The recall highlights the importance of regularly checking children’s products for damage, loose parts, or signs of wear, even when the item appears to be working normally.


## What Should Parents Do If They Own the Recalled Bottle?


TJ Morris, the company behind Home Bargains, has advised customers to stop using the recalled water bottle immediately.


Parents and carers should:


1. Stop allowing children to use the bottle.
2. Keep the product away from children.
3. Check the batch number to confirm whether it is affected.
4. Return the bottle to the nearest Home Bargains store.
5. Request a full refund.


Customers do not need to have the original packaging to return the item. If they are unsure whether their bottle is included in the recall, they can still take it to a store for assistance.


Home Bargains has apologised to customers for any inconvenience caused by the recall.


## How Product Recalls Help Protect Children


Child safety recalls are an important way of preventing injuries caused by products that may not meet expected safety standards. Companies issue recalls when they discover a possible danger, allowing customers to remove potentially unsafe items from homes.


Parents should stay informed about product recalls, especially for items commonly used by children such as:


- Water bottles and cups
- Toys
- Baby equipment
- Clothing with small attachments
- School accessories


Checking official recall notices can help families identify risks early and take action before an accident occurs.


## Tips for Keeping Children Safe Around Everyday Products


While manufacturers work to ensure products are safe, parents can take additional steps to reduce risks at home.


Some useful safety habits include:


### Inspect Children’s Items Regularly


Look for cracks, broken pieces, loose parts, or damage that could create a hazard. Items used daily can become worn over time.


### Follow Age Recommendations


Always use products according to the manufacturer’s age guidance. Items designed for older children may contain parts that are unsuitable for younger children.


### Remove Damaged Products


If a toy, bottle, or accessory becomes damaged, it is safer to stop using it rather than attempt a temporary repair.


### Stay Updated With Recall Announcements


Government websites, retailer notices, and official company announcements regularly publish information about unsafe products.


## Retailer Response and Customer Advice


TJ Morris has confirmed that affected Toy Story water bottles should be returned to Home Bargains stores for a full refund. The company has encouraged customers to follow the recall instructions and avoid using any affected products.


The recall only applies to the listed batch numbers. Customers who own a similar bottle from another batch should check official updates if they have concerns.
