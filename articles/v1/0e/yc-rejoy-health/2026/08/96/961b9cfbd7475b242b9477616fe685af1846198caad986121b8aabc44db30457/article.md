# Phil Collins Reveals Serious Alcoholism Battle, Hospitalisation and Health Struggles After Genesis Tour

Phil Collins, one of Britain’s most successful musicians, has revealed the devastating impact of a serious alcoholism battle that left him facing a life-threatening health crisis. The legendary singer and drummer said his drinking problems reached a point where doctors and family members feared he might not survive.


In a recent interview with the Sunday Times, Collins opened up about the difficult years following the final Genesis reunion tour in 2021. The 75-year-old musician explained that his struggle with alcohol became severe after he stopped taking Antabuse, a medication previously used to help prevent him from drinking.


Collins said that after stopping the medication, his alcohol consumption increased dramatically. He described drinking wine in the morning and admitted that his dependence on alcohol eventually caused severe damage to his health.


The consequences became critical in November 2023, when Collins was hospitalised for seven months. He revealed that his condition involved serious damage to his pancreas and kidneys, with doctors facing major concerns about whether his body would recover.


“There were some decisions to be made about ‘Do we keep Phil on life support?’ kind of thing,” Collins told the Sunday Times. He explained that his kidneys were failing and that his organs were struggling. According to the musician, people close to him feared they might not see him again.


Collins said he was fortunate to survive the ordeal and confirmed that he has not consumed alcohol since the health crisis.


“I was very lucky to have come out of that. Needless to say, I haven’t had a drink since,” he said.


## Phil Collins’ Previous Battle With Alcohol


This was not the first time Collins had spoken publicly about problems with alcohol. Following his divorce from his third wife, Orianne Cevey, in 2006, Collins previously admitted that he went through a period of heavy drinking.


In a 2015 interview with Rolling Stone, Collins described experiencing emotional struggles after the breakdown of his marriage. He said he spent long periods drinking and watching television, acknowledging that the lifestyle was damaging his health.


The musician explained that he had previously managed to stop drinking and had remained sober for several years before his later relapse.


Collins’ openness about addiction highlights the challenges faced by many people dealing with alcohol dependency. His story also shows how addiction can return even after periods of recovery, particularly during times of emotional difficulty, health problems, or major life changes.


## Health Problems Affecting His Music Career


Alongside his battle with alcoholism, Collins has faced several significant health challenges that have affected his ability to perform.


The singer developed serious problems with his neck and spine, requiring surgery and limiting his ability to play drums. His physical condition meant that during the Genesis reunion tour in 2021, he performed as the vocalist while seated rather than playing drums.


Collins also revealed in 2016 that he had been diagnosed with diabetes, adding another medical challenge during his later career.


Before the Genesis reunion shows, Collins discussed how his health had changed his approach to performing. Although he acknowledged that sitting during concerts was not how he imagined performing, he said audiences continued to respond positively to his music.


Despite these difficulties, Collins continued to connect with fans around the world and complete one of the most anticipated reunions in rock history.


## A Legendary Career Spanning Decades


Phil Collins remains one of the most commercially successful musicians in British music history. His career began with Genesis, where he initially joined as the drummer before becoming the band’s lead vocalist after Peter Gabriel’s departure.


Under Collins’ leadership, Genesis transformed from a progressive rock group into a global mainstream success. The band achieved multiple chart-topping albums and became one of the biggest acts of the 1980s and early 1990s.


Collins also built an extraordinary solo career. His albums produced some of the most recognisable songs in popular music, including “In the Air Tonight,” “Against All Odds,” and “Easy Lover.”


His success included numerous awards, millions of album sales, and a lasting influence on generations of musicians.


His most recent solo album, Going Back, was released in 2010. After taking a break from music, Collins returned to touring in 2015, partly motivated by his desire to share his career with his children.


## Fans Show Support for Music Icon


News of Collins’ health struggles has led to an outpouring of support from fans who have followed his career for decades.


Many listeners have expressed admiration for his honesty about addiction, illness, and recovery. By speaking publicly about his experiences, Collins has helped raise awareness of the serious effects of alcoholism and the importance of seeking support.


His story is also a reminder that even highly successful and internationally famous individuals can experience personal battles away from the public stage.


While Collins’ ability to perform has been affected by his health, his influence on music remains significant. His voice, songwriting, and contributions to Genesis and solo music continue to be celebrated worldwide.


## Conclusion


Phil Collins’ recent revelations about alcoholism and his near-fatal health crisis provide a deeper look into the challenges behind the public image of a global music superstar.


After years of success, personal struggles, and serious medical issues, Collins says he is now focused on recovery and maintaining his health. His decision to share his story offers an important message about addiction, resilience, and the possibility of moving forward after difficult times.


For millions of fans, Phil Collins remains not only a music legend but also a reminder of human strength in the face of adversity.
