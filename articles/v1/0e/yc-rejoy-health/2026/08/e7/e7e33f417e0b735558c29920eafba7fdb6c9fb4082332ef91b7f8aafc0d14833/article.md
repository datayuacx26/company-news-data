# Can Tadalafil Increase Glaucoma Risk? New Study Highlights Possible Link Between Erectile Dysfunction Medication and Eye Health

Millions of men worldwide take tadalafil to treat erectile dysfunction and symptoms associated with an enlarged prostate. The medication has helped improve quality of life for many patients by increasing blood flow and supporting healthy sexual function. However, new research suggests that long term use of tadalafil could be associated with an increased risk of glaucoma, one of the leading causes of irreversible blindness worldwide.


Researchers are urging patients not to panic or stop taking their medication without medical advice. Instead, they recommend that people using tadalafil for extended periods undergo regular eye examinations, especially if they already have glaucoma risk factors.


Here is what the latest research found and what it means for patients.


## What Is Tadalafil?


Tadalafil belongs to a class of medications known as phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitors, commonly called PDE5 inhibitors. It is widely prescribed to treat erectile dysfunction by relaxing blood vessels and increasing blood flow.


Doctors also prescribe tadalafil to relieve urinary symptoms caused by benign prostatic hyperplasia, also known as an enlarged prostate.


The medication is generally considered safe and effective when taken as directed. However, like all medications, it may cause side effects, including some that affect eye health.


## New Study Suggests Higher Glaucoma Risk


A recent study published in the **British Journal of Ophthalmology** examined health records from more than 73,000 men aged 40 years and older who had a history of lower urinary tract symptoms.


Researchers compared two groups over a period of up to five years. One group received tadalafil or another PDE5 inhibitor, while the comparison group used different medications.


To improve accuracy, participants were matched based on several lifestyle and health factors, including:


- Smoking status
- Ethnicity
- Diabetes
- Obesity
- Other underlying health conditions


After analyzing the data, researchers found that men taking tadalafil had a higher likelihood of developing several eye conditions.


The findings included:


- A 22 percent higher risk of developing glaucoma.
- A 32 percent higher risk of ocular hypertension, which refers to increased pressure inside the eye.
- A 33 percent higher risk of primary open angle glaucoma, the most common type of glaucoma.


Although these findings suggest an association, the researchers emphasized that the study does not prove tadalafil directly causes glaucoma.


## Why Researchers Are Not Advising Patients to Stop Treatment


One of the most important messages from the study is that patients should not discontinue tadalafil without consulting their healthcare provider.


The researchers specifically noted that their findings do **not** contraindicate tadalafil use. Instead, the results highlight the importance of monitoring eye health in patients who may already be vulnerable.


This includes individuals who have:


- A family history of glaucoma
- Existing glaucoma
- Elevated eye pressure
- Other eye conditions affecting the optic nerve


Regular monitoring allows doctors to identify potential problems early before significant vision loss occurs.


## What Is Glaucoma?


Glaucoma is a group of eye diseases that damage the optic nerve, which carries visual information from the eye to the brain.


The condition often develops slowly and painlessly, making it difficult for people to recognize symptoms during its early stages.


If left untreated, glaucoma can gradually reduce peripheral vision and eventually lead to permanent blindness.


According to health experts, glaucoma remains one of the leading causes of irreversible vision loss around the world.


## Why Early Detection Matters


One of the biggest challenges with glaucoma is that many people do not notice any warning signs until vision has already been affected.


Routine comprehensive eye examinations remain the best way to detect glaucoma early.


During an eye exam, an optometrist or ophthalmologist may:


- Measure eye pressure
- Examine the optic nerve
- Assess peripheral vision
- Check the drainage angle inside the eye


Detecting glaucoma early often allows treatment to slow or prevent further vision loss.


## Symptoms of Glaucoma


Most people with primary open angle glaucoma experience no symptoms during the early stages.


Because the disease develops gradually, many cases are discovered during routine eye tests rather than after symptoms appear.


However, acute glaucoma is a medical emergency and may cause sudden symptoms, including:


- Severe eye pain
- Red eyes
- Blurred vision
- Rainbow halos around lights
- Headache
- Nausea
- Vomiting
- Tenderness around the eyes


Anyone experiencing these symptoms should seek immediate medical attention.


## Who Should Consider Regular Eye Exams?


While regular eye examinations benefit everyone, they may be particularly important for individuals taking tadalafil over a long period.


People at higher risk include:


- Adults over 40
- Individuals with a family history of glaucoma
- Patients with elevated eye pressure
- People with diabetes
- Those with previous eye disease
- Long term users of PDE5 inhibitor medications


Discussing personal risk factors with both a primary care physician and an eye care specialist can help determine an appropriate screening schedule.


## Limitations of the Research


Although the study involved a large number of participants, it remains an observational study.


Observational research can identify associations between factors but cannot prove one factor directly causes another.


Other variables that researchers may not have measured could also contribute to the increased glaucoma risk observed among tadalafil users.


Additional clinical studies will be necessary to better understand whether tadalafil plays a direct role in glaucoma development and to identify which patients may be most vulnerable.


## What Patients Should Do


For people currently taking tadalafil, experts recommend continuing treatment unless advised otherwise by a healthcare professional.


Instead of stopping medication independently, patients should:


- Schedule routine comprehensive eye examinations.
- Inform their eye doctor about all medications they are taking.
- Report any changes in vision promptly.
- Follow treatment recommendations from their healthcare providers.


These simple steps can help protect long term eye health while ensuring underlying medical conditions continue to be managed effectively.


## Final Thoughts


The latest research suggests there may be a connection between long term tadalafil use and an increased risk of glaucoma, ocular hypertension, and primary open angle glaucoma. While the findings are important, they do not establish that the medication directly causes these conditions.


Healthcare professionals emphasize that tadalafil remains an effective treatment for erectile dysfunction and enlarged prostate symptoms. Rather than discontinuing treatment, patients should focus on regular eye monitoring, particularly if they have existing glaucoma risk factors.


Routine eye examinations remain the most effective way to detect glaucoma early and preserve vision before permanent damage occurs.
