# Joe Biden’s Cancer Has Spread and Is “Very Painful,” Hunter Biden Says

Former President Joe Biden is facing a worsening battle with prostate cancer, according to his son Hunter Biden, who recently spoke publicly about the former president’s health and the emotional impact the illness has had on their family.


In a BBC interview published August 7, 2026, Hunter Biden described his father’s condition as increasingly difficult and said the cancer has spread beyond its original location. He said the illness has become “very painful” and “very debilitating in many respects,” while emphasizing that his 83-year-old father remains active despite his health challenges.


The latest comments provide one of the clearest public updates on Joe Biden’s condition since his prostate cancer diagnosis was announced in 2025.


## Joe Biden’s Cancer Has Progressed


Joe Biden’s prostate cancer diagnosis was first announced in May 2025, several months after he left the White House.


At the time, Biden’s representatives said he had been diagnosed with an aggressive form of prostate cancer that had already spread to his bones. Medical experts generally classify prostate cancer that has spread to distant parts of the body as metastatic or Stage 4 disease. Reuters reported that Biden’s cancer was described as aggressive but hormone-sensitive, a characteristic that can influence treatment options and the expected response to therapy.


Hunter Biden has now offered another update on the former president’s health.


Speaking with the BBC, Hunter said the cancer has continued to spread and that his father is experiencing significant pain. He also described the experience of watching his father deal with the disease as extremely difficult for the family.


The comments have attracted renewed attention because Joe Biden has largely maintained a relatively private public profile since leaving office.


Hunter also suggested that his father does not always openly complain about his physical suffering.


The younger Biden said he sometimes wished his father would complain more because the family can see the toll the disease is taking on him.


## Biden Has Continued Treatment


Following the initial diagnosis, Biden and his family began discussing treatment options with doctors.


By October 2025, a family spokesperson said the former president had begun radiation and hormone treatments. Reuters subsequently reported that Biden was receiving radiation and hormone therapy for metastatic prostate cancer.


The progression of prostate cancer can vary considerably from one person to another. Treatment decisions can depend on factors such as the location of metastases, the characteristics of the cancer and how the disease responds to therapy.


For Biden, the diagnosis has added another difficult chapter to a family that has already experienced a devastating loss from cancer.


## Cancer Has Deeply Affected the Biden Family


Cancer has played a significant role in the Biden family’s history.


Joe Biden’s son Beau Biden died in 2015 after battling brain cancer. Beau was 46 years old. His death profoundly affected the former president and became one of the most painful events in Biden’s personal life.


Over the years, Joe Biden has also connected Beau’s illness to his exposure to toxic burn pits during his military service.


The former president has frequently spoken about cancer research and made cancer prevention and treatment a major part of his public agenda. During his presidency, Biden revived the Cancer Moonshot initiative, an effort aimed at reducing the impact of cancer through research, prevention and improved treatment.


The family’s experience with cancer therefore extends far beyond Joe Biden’s current diagnosis.


## Hunter Biden Also Discussed His Presidential Pardon


Hunter Biden’s BBC interview was not limited to his father’s health.


He also discussed one of the most controversial decisions of Joe Biden’s final weeks in office: the presidential pardon granted to Hunter in December 2024.


Joe Biden issued Hunter Biden a full and unconditional pardon on December 1, 2024. The pardon covered federal offenses Hunter had committed or may have committed between January 1, 2014, and December 1, 2024. It specifically included the federal cases involving his firearm purchase and tax-related prosecution.


Hunter Biden had previously been convicted in a federal gun case and had pleaded guilty to federal tax charges. The pardon prevented further federal punishment connected to the covered offenses.


The decision was controversial because Joe Biden had previously said that he would not pardon his son.


The sweeping nature of the pardon also attracted criticism because it covered more than the specific charges that had already resulted in convictions or criminal proceedings.


## Hunter Biden Acknowledges the Pardon Controversy


In his BBC interview, Hunter Biden did not attempt to dismiss the criticism surrounding the pardon.


Instead, he acknowledged that the decision could reasonably be viewed as damaging to his father’s political legacy.


Hunter reportedly said that the pardon was not good for the Constitution, the American people or his father’s legacy, while acknowledging that it was understandable why people would criticize the decision.


That admission is notable because the Hunter Biden pardon has remained a major part of the debate surrounding Joe Biden’s final months as president.


Supporters of the pardon argued that Hunter had been subjected to unusually intense political and legal pressure because of his relationship with his father. Critics argued that the decision undermined Biden’s previous statements about maintaining the independence of the Justice Department and avoiding presidential intervention in his son’s legal cases.


The pardon remains one of the most consequential and controversial actions of Biden’s presidency.


## A Family Under Extraordinary Pressure


Hunter Biden’s latest comments connect two major parts of the Biden family story: his father’s declining health and the political controversy surrounding his own legal troubles.


For the Biden family, those issues have unfolded against the backdrop of years of intense public scrutiny.


Joe Biden spent decades in public life, serving as a U.S. senator, vice president and president. His final years in office were marked by extensive discussion about his age, health and ability to continue serving. His decision to leave the 2024 presidential race intensified that debate.


Now, more than a year after leaving the White House, Biden is confronting a serious medical challenge away from the pressures of presidential office.


Hunter’s description suggests that the illness has become increasingly difficult for the family to watch.


Despite the progression of his cancer, Biden has continued to make public appearances and remain engaged with issues important to him.


## What Happens Next for Joe Biden?


The latest update from Hunter Biden highlights the seriousness of Joe Biden’s condition, but it does not provide a detailed medical assessment from Biden’s doctors.


Because cancer treatment and progression are highly individual, public statements cannot establish a precise prognosis. The former president’s medical team would be best positioned to explain the current stage of his disease, treatment response and future care.


For now, Hunter Biden’s comments provide a personal perspective on a deeply difficult period for the former president and his family.


Joe Biden’s battle with prostate cancer is also a reminder of the broader impact cancer can have on families. His diagnosis has taken on particular significance because of the Biden family’s previous experience with the disease and the loss of Beau Biden.


As the former president continues treatment, public attention is likely to remain focused not only on his health but also on how his family navigates this private struggle after decades in the political spotlight.
