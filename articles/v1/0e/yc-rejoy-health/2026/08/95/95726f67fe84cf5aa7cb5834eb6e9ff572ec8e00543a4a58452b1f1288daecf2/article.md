# Wegovy and Mounjaro Users Given New Heatwave Warning as Pharmacist Reveals Dehydration Symptoms

People taking popular weight-loss injections such as Wegovy and Mounjaro have been urged to take extra care during periods of extreme heat, with a pharmacist warning that dehydration can become a serious health concern.


The warning comes as parts of the UK experience exceptionally hot weather, with temperatures rising sharply and authorities issuing heat-related health advice.


While staying hydrated is important for everyone during hot weather, people using certain weight-loss medicines may need to be particularly mindful of their fluid intake. Medicines such as Wegovy and Mounjaro can affect appetite and may cause side effects including nausea or vomiting, which can make it harder for some people to maintain their usual food and fluid intake.


According to a report published by the Daily Mirror, highlighted the increased risk of dehydration for people taking these medications during hot weather.


## Why heat can increase dehydration risk


Hot weather causes the body to lose more fluid, particularly through sweating. If someone does not replace the fluids they lose, dehydration can develop.


For people taking weight-loss injections, additional factors may sometimes contribute. These medicines can reduce appetite and may affect how regularly a person eats or drinks. Some users can also experience gastrointestinal side effects such as nausea or vomiting.


This does not mean everyone taking Wegovy or Mounjaro will become dehydrated. However, being aware of the warning signs can help people recognise a problem before it becomes more serious.


The pharmacist advised people using weight-loss medication to pay particular attention to their hydration during periods of extreme heat.


## Common signs of dehydration to watch for


Dehydration can cause a range of symptoms, and the first signs may be relatively mild.


Possible symptoms include:


- Increased thirst
- A dry mouth or sticky saliva
- Headaches
- Darker yellow urine
- Passing urine less frequently
- Feeling unusually tired or weak
- Dizziness, particularly when standing up


These symptoms can have other causes, so they should not automatically be assumed to be caused by dehydration. However, they may be a useful indication that someone needs to pay closer attention to their fluid intake.


As dehydration becomes more severe, symptoms can become considerably more concerning.


The warning highlighted confusion, unusual drowsiness, fainting, a rapid heartbeat and producing very little or no urine as signs that require urgent medical attention.


Someone who is repeatedly vomiting and cannot keep fluids down may also need urgent medical assessment, particularly if they are becoming extremely weak or showing other signs of serious dehydration.


## How much should you drink during hot weather?


The amount of fluid an individual needs varies according to factors such as age, body size, activity levels, health conditions, diet and the temperature of their surroundings.


The advice reported by the Mirror suggested that many adults may need around 2.5 to 3 litres of water a day during very hot weather, compared with a lower baseline amount in more moderate conditions.


However, hydration is not simply about drinking a large quantity of water as quickly as possible.


A more practical approach is to drink regularly throughout the day. People who are exercising, working outdoors or spending extended periods in hot environments may need to replace additional fluid lost through sweating.


It is also important to remember that individual medical circumstances can change fluid requirements. People with certain medical conditions or those taking particular medicines should follow advice from their doctor, pharmacist or other healthcare professional.


## Do not ignore vomiting or persistent nausea


One issue that may be particularly relevant to some users of weight-loss injections is vomiting.


If someone is losing fluid through repeated vomiting and cannot replace it by drinking, dehydration can develop more quickly.


Anyone experiencing persistent vomiting, severe weakness, confusion, fainting or other worrying symptoms should seek appropriate medical advice rather than attempting to manage potentially severe dehydration alone.


People should also avoid making changes to prescribed medication without speaking to a healthcare professional.


Wegovy and Mounjaro are prescription medicines, and they are not suitable for everyone. Their use should be monitored according to the prescribing information and individual medical advice.


## Heatstroke is different from ordinary dehydration


Another important concern during extreme heat is heatstroke.


Heatstroke is a medical emergency and can occur when the body's temperature regulation system becomes overwhelmed. It can involve a very high body temperature, confusion, altered consciousness or loss of consciousness, as well as other serious symptoms.


The Mirror report referenced symptoms including a body temperature above 40C, hot skin, confusion and fainting or unconsciousness.


If heatstroke is suspected, urgent medical assistance should be sought.


People can reduce their exposure to extreme heat by staying in cooler indoor spaces where possible, avoiding strenuous activity during the hottest parts of the day and following official heat-health guidance.


## Practical hydration tips for Wegovy and Mounjaro users


People using weight-loss injections can take several simple steps to reduce their risk of becoming dehydrated during hot weather.


First, keep water readily available throughout the day. Having a bottle nearby can make it easier to remember to drink regularly.


Second, pay attention to changes in urine colour and frequency. Darker urine or noticeably reduced urination can be a warning sign that fluid intake may be inadequate.


Third, do not wait until you feel extremely thirsty before drinking. Regular small amounts can be easier to manage than consuming a large amount of liquid at once.


Fourth, take extra care if exercising or working in hot conditions. Sweating increases fluid loss, so additional fluids may be required.


Finally, anyone experiencing significant vomiting, severe dizziness, confusion, fainting or very little urine should seek medical help promptly.


## When should you contact a healthcare professional?


Not every headache or episode of tiredness means someone is seriously dehydrated. Hot weather itself can make people feel uncomfortable, fatigued and light-headed.


However, symptoms that are severe, worsening or accompanied by confusion, fainting, an unusually fast heartbeat, inability to keep fluids down or very little urine should not be ignored.


If you are unsure whether symptoms could be related to your medication, dehydration or another health problem, speaking to a pharmacist, GP or other qualified healthcare professional can help determine what to do next.


The key message for people taking Wegovy or Mounjaro during extreme heat is straightforward: **do not overlook hydration** .


As temperatures rise, paying attention to fluid intake and recognising the early signs of dehydration may help reduce the risk of more serious complications.
