# Galanz Retro Refrigerators Recalled After Fires, Death Reported: What Consumers Need to Know

## More Than 120,000 Refrigerators Affected in Nationwide Safety Recall


Thousands of consumers across the United States are being warned about a major refrigerator recall involving Galanz retro-style refrigerators after federal safety officials reported multiple fires connected to the appliances. The recall affects more than 120,000 units sold through major retailers, including Amazon and Home Depot stores nationwide.


The recall was announced by the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) after reports indicated that certain Galanz refrigerators could create a serious fire hazard. According to safety officials, internal electrical components in the affected models may short circuit, potentially causing overheating, ignition, fires, and burn injuries.


The reported incidents include more than 30 fires, with at least one death linked to the recalled appliances. Consumers who own one of the affected refrigerators are advised to stop using the unit immediately and follow the company’s recall instructions.


## Details of the Galanz Refrigerator Recall


The recall covers approximately 121,680 Galanz Americas Limited Company retro refrigerators. These appliances were manufactured in China and sold across the United States between approximately 2019 and 2022 through online and retail channels.


The recalled refrigerators are known for their vintage-inspired appearance and compact design. They were marketed as stylish appliances suitable for kitchens, apartments, offices, and other living spaces.


The affected units have the following features:


- 7.6 cubic feet of storage capacity
- Top freezer compartment
- Three adjustable glass shelves
- One storage drawer
- Left or right door opening options
- Retro-style exterior design


The refrigerators were available in several colors, including:


- Black
- Blue
- Red
- White


The units measure approximately 58 inches tall, 24 inches deep, and 21 inches wide.


## Which Refrigerator Models Are Included?


Consumers should check the label on the back of their refrigerator to determine whether their appliance is part of the recall.


The recalled models include:


- BCD-215V-62H
- GLR76TRDER
- GLR76TBKER
- GLR76TBEER
- GLR76TWEER


The affected refrigerators have date codes ranging from December 2018 through December 2020. The date code is usually displayed in a year and month format, such as YYYYMM.


Galanz stated that refrigerators manufactured outside of the listed production dates are not included in the recall.


Consumers can locate the model number and date code on the product label located on the back of the refrigerator.


## Why Are These Refrigerators Being Recalled?


According to the CPSC, the main concern involves electrical components inside the refrigerators. These parts may malfunction and create a short circuit, which can lead to ignition.


Electrical failures in household appliances can be dangerous because they may cause fires without warning, especially when appliances are connected to power continuously.


Refrigerators are among the few household devices that typically operate 24 hours a day, making electrical safety especially important. A malfunction involving wiring, compressors, or internal components can create a risk even when homeowners are away.


Federal safety officials are urging consumers not to ignore recall notices because continued use of a defective appliance could increase the chance of property damage, injuries, or worse outcomes.


## What Should Owners Do If They Have a Recalled Refrigerator?


Consumers who believe they own one of the affected Galanz refrigerators should stop using the appliance and confirm whether their model is included in the recall.


Galanz is offering repair assistance for eligible customers. The company said consumers can register their recalled refrigerator through the recall process and receive a repair kit. Customers may also arrange an appointment for an in-home repair performed by a qualified technician.


The repair service will be provided at no additional cost to eligible consumers.


Customers are encouraged to keep the refrigerator and any required parts accessible before a scheduled technician visit. Repair scheduling may vary depending on location and technician availability.


Consumers should not attempt unauthorized repairs themselves because improper electrical repairs may create additional safety risks.


## Where Were the Refrigerators Sold?


The recalled Galanz refrigerators were available through major retailers, including Amazon and Home Depot locations nationwide.


They were sold at prices generally ranging from about $330 to $520.


Because these appliances were widely distributed, consumers who purchased a retro-style refrigerator in recent years should check their product information even if they are unsure about the brand or model.


Online purchases can sometimes make recall identification more difficult because buyers may not remember the exact model number. Checking the physical label on the appliance remains the most reliable way to confirm whether a refrigerator is affected.


## Importance of Checking Appliance Recalls


Household appliance recalls are issued to protect consumers from potential hazards that may not be obvious during normal use. Many recalled products continue operating normally before a dangerous failure occurs.


Consumers should regularly check for recalls involving:


- Refrigerators
- Washing machines
- Dryers
- Ovens
- Microwaves
- Other electrical appliances


Government safety agencies publish recall information to help families identify dangerous products and take corrective action before injuries occur.


A recall does not necessarily mean every product will fail, but it indicates that a safety concern has been identified and consumers should follow official guidance.


## Consumer Safety Tips for Refrigerators and Electrical Appliances


Experts recommend several basic safety practices for reducing appliance-related risks:


- Avoid using appliances with damaged power cords
- Do not ignore unusual sounds, smells, or overheating
- Keep electrical appliances away from water leaks
- Avoid plugging large appliances into unsafe extension cords
- Register new appliances with manufacturers to receive safety updates
- Check government recall databases regularly


Taking these steps can help identify potential problems before they become dangerous situations.


## Company Response and Ongoing Safety Investigation


Galanz Americas has stated that it is cooperating with the recall process and providing repair solutions for affected customers.


The company explained that only specific models and production dates are included in the recall. Other Galanz retro refrigerators manufactured during different periods are not affected.


Safety officials continue monitoring reports connected to the recalled appliances. Consumers who experience problems, including smoke, burning odors, overheating, or electrical issues, should immediately stop using the refrigerator and report the issue through appropriate safety channels.


## Final Thoughts


The recall of more than 120,000 Galanz retro refrigerators highlights the importance of appliance safety and consumer awareness. While these refrigerators were popular because of their vintage design and compact size, certain models may pose a serious fire risk due to electrical component problems.


Owners should check their refrigerator model numbers and date codes as soon as possible. Those with affected units should stop using them and contact Galanz for repair assistance.


Staying informed about product recalls can help protect homes, families, and communities from preventable safety hazards.
