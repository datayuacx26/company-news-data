# British Surfing Community Mourns as Professional Surfer Arran Strong Is Presumed Dead After Portugal Disappearance

## Arran Strong’s Disappearance During Paddleboard Journey Leads to International Tributes


The global surfing community is mourning the presumed death of British professional surfer Arran Strong, who disappeared during a stand-up paddleboard expedition along Portugal’s Algarve coast. The 28-year-old athlete was reported missing on July 27, 2026, after he became separated from his board while traveling near the southwestern coastline.


Strong was widely respected within British surfing circles and had represented Great Britain in international competitions, including the ISA World Surf Games in 2024 and 2025. His disappearance has prompted tributes from athletes, sporting organizations, friends, and supporters who remembered him not only for his achievements in the water but also for his character outside competition.


According to GB Surfing, the national governing body for Great Britain’s surfing teams, Strong’s passing represents a significant loss for the sport. The organization described him as a talented competitor, a supportive teammate, and a positive influence within the surfing community.


## Search Operation Launched Along Portugal’s Algarve Coast


Strong was taking part in a long-distance paddleboard journey with his coach and close friend José Maria Pyrrait when the incident occurred. The pair were traveling from Monte Clérigo beach toward Carrapateira on Portugal’s Algarve coast when Strong disappeared.


Pyrrait later explained that the two surfers had stopped near Arrifana to rest, drink water, and take photographs before continuing their route. During the next part of the journey, Strong moved ahead while Pyrrait remained behind. Shortly afterward, Pyrrait noticed that Strong’s paddleboard was drifting without him.


After realizing Strong was missing, Pyrrait immediately began searching the surrounding area and contacted people on land to alert emergency services. Portuguese authorities launched a major search effort involving rescue teams and boats in an attempt to locate the missing athlete.


Despite extensive efforts over several days, Strong was not found. Authorities eventually ended the active search operation, and he was presumed dead.


The exact circumstances surrounding the disappearance remain unknown. Officials continue to investigate what may have happened during the paddleboard expedition.


## Tributes From the Surfing World


News of Strong’s disappearance has deeply affected the surfing community in Britain and beyond. Many people have remembered him as a passionate athlete who represented the values of dedication, teamwork, and perseverance.

Strong’s career included representing Great Britain at major international surfing events. His participation in the ISA World Surf Games helped establish him as one of the country’s notable competitive surfers.


Friends and fellow athletes have also shared messages honoring his memory and celebrating the enthusiasm he brought to surfing.


## Strong’s Journey Despite Health Challenges


Throughout his life, Arran Strong faced significant health challenges. He was diagnosed at birth with Alpha-1 Antitrypsin Deficiency, a rare genetic condition that can affect lung function.


Despite these challenges, Strong continued pursuing his passion for surfing and became an inspiration to many within the respiratory health community. The European Lung Foundation recognized his contributions and described him as a valued member of the Alpha-1 community.


His story has been seen by supporters as an example of determination and resilience. Strong’s ability to compete at an international level while managing a lifelong medical condition earned admiration from many who followed his career.
