# Vegan Egg Substitute Recalled Nationwide After Undeclared Egg Contamination Concern

## Plant Based Egg Alternative Pulled From Market Due to Allergy Risk


A vegan baking product marketed as an egg white replacement has been recalled across the United States after the discovery that it may contain undeclared egg ingredients. The recall affects Rooted in RARE Aquafaba Powder, a plant based product produced by Florida company 529 Commerce, LLC.


The recall has drawn attention because the product is designed specifically as an alternative for people avoiding traditional eggs. The discovery of possible egg contamination highlights the importance of accurate food labeling, especially for consumers with food allergies or dietary restrictions.


According to information released through the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), 529 Commerce announced the recall of 3,860 units of its Rooted in RARE Aquafaba Powder after concerns that the product could contain undeclared egg. The company stated that the issue was connected to a supplier and that it has ended its relationship with that supplier.


## Details About the Recalled Aquafaba Powder


Aquafaba is a popular ingredient among vegan cooks and bakers. It is the liquid obtained from cooked chickpeas or other legumes and is commonly used as a substitute for egg whites in recipes such as cakes, cookies, mousses, and whipped desserts.


The recalled Rooted in RARE Aquafaba Powder was sold in flexible foil pouches in two package sizes:


• 4 ounce packages containing 113 grams of product
• 12 ounce packages containing 340 grams of product


The product was distributed throughout the United States through Amazon and direct consumer sales from the company website. The affected products were available between June 2025 and July 2026.


Consumers can identify recalled products by checking the following information:


• 4 ounce package UPC number: 199284530959
• 12 ounce package UPC number: 199284306226


Affected products may also have best by date stickers showing:


• December 14, 2026
• December 12, 2027


Customers who purchased this product are encouraged to check their packaging carefully and confirm whether their product is included in the recall.


## Why the Recall Was Issued


The company began the recall after receiving a report from a customer who experienced an allergic reaction after consuming the product. At the time of the announcement, no additional illnesses connected to the product had been reported.


Food allergies can create serious health risks, particularly when allergens are not clearly listed on product labels. Egg allergies are among the most common food allergies and can affect both children and adults.


For individuals with egg allergies, exposure may result in symptoms such as:


• Skin irritation or hives
• Runny nose or congestion
• Stomach pain and digestive problems
• Vomiting
• Breathing difficulties, including wheezing or chest tightness


In some cases, an allergic reaction can become severe and lead to anaphylaxis. This potentially life threatening condition may cause breathing problems, a rapid drop in blood pressure, and other serious symptoms requiring immediate medical attention.


Because allergic reactions can vary, a person who previously experienced mild symptoms may experience a more serious reaction during another exposure.


## What Consumers Should Do If They Have the Product


Consumers who have purchased Rooted in RARE Aquafaba Powder and have an egg allergy or sensitivity should stop using the product immediately.


The company recommends disposing of the affected product. Customers may request refunds through Amazon if they purchased the item through the online marketplace. Those who purchased directly from the company website may contact 529 Commerce for refund assistance.


For additional questions, customers can contact Rooted in RARE customer service by phone at 1-888-693-1013 or emailinfo@rootedinrare.com . Customer support is available Monday through Friday from 9:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. Central Time.


## Importance of Food Label Accuracy


This recall serves as another reminder of why accurate food labeling is essential. Consumers rely on ingredient lists and allergy warnings when making purchasing decisions. For people with food allergies, even a small amount of an undeclared ingredient can create a serious health concern.


Companies that produce specialty foods, including vegan, gluten free, and allergy friendly products, must maintain strict quality control practices throughout the supply chain. Ingredients can pass through multiple suppliers before reaching the final product, making supplier monitoring a critical part of food safety.


The incident also shows why consumers should stay informed about product recalls. Checking official recall announcements and reviewing product information can help reduce the risk of accidental exposure to allergens.


## Growing Demand for Vegan Food Safety Standards


The popularity of plant based foods has increased significantly in recent years. Many consumers choose vegan alternatives because of personal health goals, environmental concerns, or dietary preferences.


Products such as aquafaba powder have become valuable ingredients for people looking for alternatives to traditional animal based foods. However, as demand grows, manufacturers must ensure that vegan products meet the expectations of consumers who depend on accurate labeling.


A product labeled as vegan should not contain animal derived ingredients, especially when it is purchased by individuals avoiding specific allergens or following strict dietary choices. Proper testing, ingredient verification, and transparent communication are important steps in maintaining consumer confidence.


## Final Thoughts


The recall of Rooted in RARE Aquafaba Powder highlights the importance of food safety, accurate labeling, and consumer awareness. While the company has taken steps to remove potentially affected products from circulation, customers should review their purchases and follow recall instructions if they own the product.


People with egg allergies should remain cautious when using food products and always check official recall notices for updates. Manufacturers, suppliers, and retailers all play an important role in ensuring that food products are safe and properly labeled.
