# Kansas Mother’s Life-Threatening Spider Bite Leads to Rare Medical Battle and Fight for Recovery

A Kansas mother is recovering after a suspected spider bite caused a chain of severe health complications that nearly claimed her life. Britagne Miller of Goddard, Kansas, experienced a frightening medical journey after being bitten during a family outing at Cheney Lake, eventually facing emergency surgery, serious infections, and liver failure.


The incident began on May 28, 2026, when Miller and her family were spending time near a campfire. During the evening, she noticed several spiders crawling on her body and later discovered multiple bites. At first, she believed the injuries were minor and expected them to heal without medical attention.


However, one bite on her calf gradually became much more serious. The area became increasingly swollen, painful, and discolored, causing Miller to seek medical help on June 4. What initially appeared to be a simple spider bite turned into a dangerous medical emergency.


Doctors discovered that Miller was suffering from a severe blood clotting problem in her leg. The condition required immediate surgery to remove dangerous blood clots and prevent further complications. During the procedure, medical professionals reportedly removed nearly 300 milliliters of blood clots from her leg and inserted a drain to support the healing process.


## Suspected Brown Recluse Spider Bite Causes Concern


Doctors were unable to identify the exact type of spider responsible for the bite. However, the location of the injury and the appearance of the wound led medical professionals to suspect that a brown recluse spider may have been involved.


Kansas is home to many different spider species, but only two are commonly considered medically significant because of their venom: the brown recluse and the black widow spider. Brown recluse bites are known for causing tissue damage, swelling, and skin changes in some cases.


Experts note that identifying a spider bite can be difficult unless the spider itself is captured and examined. Many skin injuries blamed on spiders can have other causes, so medical evaluation is important when symptoms become severe.


## A Complicated Medical History Increased the Risk


For Miller, the emergency surgery was especially frightening because she already had several serious health challenges. She said her medical difficulties began years earlier during pregnancy, when she was diagnosed with two blood clotting disorders.


Over time, she also developed liver problems and multiple allergies to foods and medications. These existing conditions made surgery more complicated and increased her concerns about possible risks.


Miller described feeling afraid before the operation, worrying about whether she would survive the procedure. Despite those fears, doctors determined that immediate surgery was necessary to address the blood clotting emergency.


## Serious Infections and Liver Failure Added More Challenges


After the surgery, Miller’s recovery became even more difficult. About five days later, doctors discovered that her wound had developed serious infections, including a staph infection and another bacterial infection.


At the same time, medical teams found that Miller was experiencing end-stage liver failure. Her condition became so critical that doctors warned her family she might have only days to weeks to live.


Miller spent time receiving treatment in both the medical intensive care unit and cardiac intensive care unit. Because of the severity of her condition, she experienced periods she barely remembers.


Despite the seriousness of her situation, Miller continued fighting. She decided against hospice care and focused on recovery, motivated by her family and her desire to return home.


## Family Support Helps Drive Recovery


After weeks of medical treatment, Miller was discharged from the hospital on July 11, 2026. She is now recovering at home while continuing medical care and working toward becoming eligible for a liver transplant.


Miller said her family became her biggest source of strength during the most difficult moments. Although she experienced physical pain, multiple procedures, and emotional exhaustion, she found motivation in her children and loved ones.


Her recovery journey is not complete. Doctors are monitoring her health, including her blood levels, as she works toward meeting the requirements needed for a possible liver transplant.


## Financial Difficulties Add Pressure During Recovery


While Miller continues healing, her family is also dealing with financial challenges. Her husband, Jake Miller, reportedly lost his job while helping care for his wife and their children during her hospitalization.


To help with medical expenses, home healthcare costs, and everyday needs, the family created a fundraising campaign with a goal of $45,000. The funds are intended to support the family as Miller continues treatment and recovery.


The situation highlights how sudden medical emergencies can affect not only a patient’s health but also their family’s financial stability.


## What to Know About Serious Spider Bites


Although most spider bites are not life-threatening, some reactions can become serious. People should seek medical attention if a bite causes:


• Rapidly increasing swelling
• Severe pain or spreading discomfort
• Skin discoloration or tissue changes
• Fever or signs of infection
• Difficulty breathing or allergic reactions


Early medical care can help doctors identify complications and provide appropriate treatment.


Experts also recommend avoiding attempts to identify a spider based only on a bite mark. If possible, safely preserving the spider for identification can help medical professionals determine potential risks.


## Final Thoughts


Britagne Miller’s experience shows how quickly a suspected spider bite can become a major medical emergency, especially for people with existing health conditions. While doctors could not confirm the exact spider species involved, the suspected brown recluse bite and resulting complications created a difficult road to recovery.


Her story also highlights the importance of paying attention to unusual symptoms, seeking medical care when injuries worsen, and recognizing the impact that serious illness can have on entire families.


Miller continues to recover and remains focused on improving her health while preparing for the next stage of her medical journey.
