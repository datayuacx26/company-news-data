# Teenager’s Final Calls to Her Mother During Tragic Siberian Bear Attack

A tragic bear attack in eastern Siberia left a mother facing an unimaginable ordeal after her teenage daughter called her repeatedly while being attacked in the wilderness.


The victim, **19-year-old Olga Moskalyova** , was hiking with her stepfather, Igor Tsyganenkov, in August 2011 when the pair encountered bears near a riverbank in the Kamchatka region of Russia.


The case has continued to attract attention because Olga was reportedly able to contact her mother, Tatiana, several times during the attack. Her calls gave her family a horrifying account of what was happening while they desperately tried to reach her.


## Who was Olga Moskalyova?


Olga Moskalyova was 19 years old at the time of the incident and was reportedly studying psychology.


In August 2011, she accompanied her stepfather on a trip near a river in eastern Siberia. According to reports published after the incident, the pair had gone to retrieve a fishing rod that had been left behind.


The journey took a devastating turn when they encountered bears.


Reports said Igor was attacked first. Olga then fled the area, but she was later caught by the animals.


Unable to escape, she used her mobile phone to contact her mother.


## Olga called her mother during the attack


The most disturbing part of the case was the series of phone calls Olga made to Tatiana.


During the first call, Olga reportedly told her mother that she was being attacked and pleaded for help.


Tatiana initially struggled to understand what was happening. According to accounts published at the time, she believed her daughter could possibly have been joking or playing a prank.


That changed when she heard the fear and distress in Olga's voice.


Tatiana then attempted to contact Igor but could not reach him. She contacted police and relatives in the nearby community, asking them to travel toward the area where Olga and Igor had been hiking.


The family faced a desperate race against time, with Olga still believed to be alive.


## A second call brought more terrible news


Olga reportedly contacted her mother again as the attack continued.


During the second call, she indicated that the bears had returned. Reports said she told Tatiana that the animals were attacking her again.


The calls reportedly became increasingly difficult as Olga's condition deteriorated.


For Tatiana, hearing her daughter in distress while being unable to reach her was an especially traumatic experience. She could do little more than alert emergency services and people nearby.


The incident later became widely reported because of the extraordinary circumstances surrounding the phone calls.


## Olga's final message to her mother


About an hour after the first call, Olga reportedly made another call to Tatiana.


By this point, she appeared to understand that she might not survive.


According to contemporary reports, Olga told her mother that she was no longer experiencing the same level of pain and asked for forgiveness. She also expressed her love for her mother.


The conversation was reportedly the final contact Tatiana had with her daughter.


Police and relatives were already travelling toward the location after Tatiana raised the alarm.


When they eventually reached the area, they found that both Olga and Igor had died.


## Where did the attack happen?


The incident occurred in eastern Siberia, in the wider Kamchatka region of Russia.


Kamchatka is known for its extensive wilderness, forests, mountains and large population of brown bears. The region's remote landscape means that encounters between people and wildlife can sometimes occur far from immediate assistance.


The brown bear is widely distributed across parts of Russia and other areas of the northern hemisphere. While bear attacks on people are relatively uncommon, they can become extremely dangerous when humans encounter a bear unexpectedly, particularly when cubs are present.


## Why do bears attack humans?


Bear behaviour can be influenced by several factors, and attacks do not necessarily mean that an animal is actively hunting people.


Bears may react defensively when they feel threatened, particularly if a person gets too close to a mother and her cubs. They can also become more accustomed to human settlements when food is easily available.


Environmental conditions can influence encounters as well. Changes in habitat, food availability and human activity can bring wildlife and people into closer contact.


Experts generally advise people travelling in bear country to remain alert, avoid surprising animals and follow local wildlife guidance.


## The aftermath of the tragedy


Following the deaths, emergency services reportedly sent hunters into the area. The mother bear and her cubs were subsequently killed.


The decision reflected the authorities' assessment of the danger posed by the animals after the fatal attack.


However, the tragedy also highlighted the difficult relationship between human communities and wildlife in remote areas.


Reports at the time suggested that bear encounters were becoming a concern in some parts of Russia, with wildlife moving into areas increasingly occupied or visited by people.


For Olga's family, however, the wider debate could not lessen the personal loss.


Her mother later remembered her daughter as a cheerful, friendly and warm young woman with a great deal of life ahead of her.


## A tragedy remembered years later


Olga Moskalyova's death remains one of the more hauntingly reported wildlife attack cases because of the phone calls she made during the incident.


The story is not simply about a bear encounter. It is also about a family receiving a desperate call from a loved one and trying to get help from miles away.


The incident demonstrates the unpredictable risks that can exist in remote wilderness areas, particularly where large predators and people share the same environment.
