# TPO Nail Polish Ban UK: How to Check If Your Gel Nails Contain the Banned Ingredient

## UK Gel Nail Ban: The Ingredient Salon Customers Need to Know About


Gel manicures have become one of the most popular beauty treatments in the UK, thanks to their long-lasting shine, durability, and professional finish. However, a major change is coming to the nail industry as authorities introduce restrictions on a chemical commonly found in some gel nail products.


The ingredient customers should look out for is TPO, which stands for Trimethylbenzoyl Diphenylphosphine Oxide. This chemical is used in certain gel nail polishes because it helps the product cure and harden when exposed to UV or LED nail lamps.


With the upcoming ban, many beauty customers are wondering how they can tell whether their salon products contain TPO and whether they should avoid it before the official deadline.


## What Is TPO in Gel Nail Polish?


TPO is a photoinitiator, meaning it helps gel polish transform from a liquid formula into a hard, glossy coating when exposed to light from a curing lamp.


Without ingredients like TPO, many gel nail products would not set properly or achieve the strong finish customers expect from professional manicures.


However, concerns have been raised about the safety profile of TPO, particularly regarding its potential effects under certain exposure conditions. As a result, regulators have decided to remove it from cosmetic products as a precaution.


The ingredient can usually be identified by checking the product label or ingredient list. If a bottle contains the full chemical name Trimethylbenzoyl Diphenylphosphine Oxide, it contains TPO.


## When Will the UK TPO Nail Product Ban Begin?


The restrictions are being introduced in stages.


From August 15, 2026, manufacturers and importers will no longer be allowed to sell cosmetic products containing TPO in the UK market.


However, nail salons will have a longer transition period. From February 14, 2027, salons across England, Scotland, and Wales will no longer be permitted to purchase or use products containing TPO.


This means some salons may still have existing TPO-containing products in their stock before the final deadline arrives.


For customers who want to avoid the ingredient immediately, it is worth asking salons what products they use and checking the ingredient list before booking a treatment.


## Why Has TPO Been Restricted?


The decision follows concerns about the possible risks associated with TPO exposure. The European Union introduced restrictions on the ingredient in 2025 after reviewing available safety information.


Regulators have taken a cautious approach because some studies have suggested possible concerns linked to reproductive health.


Experts have explained that the classification relates to the potential hazardous properties of the substance, particularly when considering high levels of exposure in certain situations.


This does not mean every person who has used a gel manicure containing TPO will experience health problems. Instead, the restrictions are part of wider cosmetic safety measures designed to reduce potential risks from certain ingredients.


## How Can You Check If Your Gel Polish Contains TPO?


The easiest way to find out whether your gel polish contains TPO is to read the ingredient list on the bottle or packaging.


Look specifically for:


Trimethylbenzoyl Diphenylphosphine Oxide


If this ingredient appears on the label, the product contains TPO.


Customers can also ask their nail technician directly whether the salon has switched to TPO-free products.


Many professional salons are already moving towards alternative formulas because of changing regulations and customer demand.


Before your next appointment, consider asking:


• Does the salon use TPO-free gel polish?


• Which brand of gel products do you use?


• Can I see the ingredient information?


A reputable salon should be able to answer these questions and explain the products being used.


## Are There TPO-Free Gel Nail Options Available?


Yes, many beauty brands have already introduced alternatives that do not contain TPO.


The European restrictions encouraged manufacturers to reformulate their products, and several nail companies now offer TPO-free options.


Some brands offering TPO-free gel formulas include OPI, GellifiQue, Glossify, and Mylee.


However, customers should still check product labels because formulas can change and different product ranges may contain different ingredients.


## What Should Nail Customers Do Before Their Next Appointment?


There is no need to panic if you have previously had gel nails. The upcoming ban is a regulatory change aimed at improving cosmetic safety standards.


If you want to avoid TPO before the final deadline, take these simple steps:


1. Ask your nail salon about the products they use.
2. Check ingredient lists before treatments.
3. Choose salons that clearly advertise TPO-free options.
4. Avoid assuming every gel polish product is the same because formulas vary between brands.
5. Keep updated with official safety guidance.


Being informed allows customers to make choices that match their personal preferences and concerns.


## The Future of Gel Nails in the UK


The beauty industry regularly adapts when new safety rules are introduced. Just as brands have changed formulas in response to previous ingredient restrictions, the gel nail sector is expected to continue moving towards alternative ingredients.


For most customers, the biggest change will simply be that salons gradually replace older products with newer TPO-free versions.


Gel manicures are unlikely to disappear. Instead, the focus will shift towards products that meet updated safety requirements while still delivering the long-lasting finish customers enjoy.


## Final Thoughts


The upcoming UK TPO ban means nail customers should become more aware of the ingredients used in their beauty treatments. Checking whether gel polish contains Trimethylbenzoyl Diphenylphosphine Oxide is a simple step that can help customers make informed decisions.


While salons have until February 2027 to fully remove existing TPO products, many businesses are already preparing by switching to alternative formulas.


If you regularly get gel nails, the best approach is to ask questions, check product labels, and choose a salon that is transparent about the products it uses.
