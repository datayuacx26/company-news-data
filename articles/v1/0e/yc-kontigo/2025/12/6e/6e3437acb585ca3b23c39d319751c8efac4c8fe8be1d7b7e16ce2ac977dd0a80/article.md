# Best digital wallets in Colombia: which ones work and how to choose

In Colombia, digital wallets have become a key part of everyday finance. From online payments and international purchases to receiving money from abroad, Colombians increasingly rely on digital wallets to manage their money efficiently.


In this guide, we review the best digital wallets available in Colombia, how they differ, and which factors matter most when choosing one.


## Why are digital wallets popular in Colombia?


Several factors have driven adoption:


- Growth of digital payments and e-commerce
- Strong freelancer and remote work economy
- International remittances
- Demand for alternatives beyond traditional banking


Digital wallets complement the local banking system while enabling global payments and access to digital dollars.


## Types of digital wallets used in Colombia


### Fiat digital wallets


These wallets operate with traditional currencies and are usually linked to bank accounts or cards.


Examples:


- Nequi
- Daviplata
- PayPal


They are convenient for local payments, but often limited for international use.


### Crypto and Web3 wallets


Web3 wallets allow users to store and transfer crypto assets directly on blockchains.


Examples:


- MetaMask
- Trust Wallet


They provide self-custody and global access, but require conversion steps to use funds locally.


### Hybrid wallets


Hybrid wallets combine fiat usability with stablecoins and blockchain infrastructure.


They allow users to save in digital dollars, send money globally, and reduce dependency on banks.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### Is it safe to use a digital wallet in Colombia?


Yes, as long as the wallet is regulated or complies with strong security standards. Many operate under the supervision of Colombia’s Financial Superintendence or are partnered with a recognized financial institution.


### Which wallet allows you to send money abroad from Colombia?


Wallets such as Kontigo, Wise, and PayPal allow international transfers from Colombia. Not all local wallets offer this feature, so it’s important to check whether they support international transfers, as well as their fees and processing times.


### What is the best wallet for freelancers in Colombia who get paid in U.S. dollars?


Kontigo stands out for enabling users to operate with digital dollars (USDC) directly within the app, making it easier to receive payments and preserve the value of their money. Wise and PayPal are also common options, although they often involve higher fees or less flexible processes.


### Do digital wallets replace a bank account in Colombia?


In some cases, yes—but it depends on the user. For basic payments, many people replace their bank account with wallets like Nequi or MOVii. However, for traditional financial products, a bank account is still necessary.


### Which wallets allow top-ups via PSE, Nequi, or DaviPlata?


Kontigo, MOVii, Tpaga, Mercado Pago, RappiPay, and several Grupo Aval apps allow top-ups through popular systems such as PSE or wallets like Nequi and DaviPlata.
