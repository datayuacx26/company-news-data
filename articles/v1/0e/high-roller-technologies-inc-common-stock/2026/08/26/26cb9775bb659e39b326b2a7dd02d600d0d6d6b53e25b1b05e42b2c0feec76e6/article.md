*Company Presentation Scheduled for August 20, 2026, at 12:15 p.m. Eastern Time*


**LAS VEGAS, Nevada, Aug. 10, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --** High Roller Technologies, Inc. (“High Roller” or the “Company”) (NYSE: ROLR), a publicly traded online gaming and prediction markets company, today announced that members of its management team will present at the Sidoti August Virtual Micro-Cap Conference, being held August 19–20, 2026.


High Roller’s virtual presentation is scheduled for Thursday, August 20, from 12:15 p.m. to 12:45 p.m. Eastern Time in Track 1.


Management will also be available for virtual one-on-one meetings with registered investors during the conference. Investors interested in scheduling a meeting with High Roller should submit a request through the Sidoti conference platform or contact their Sidoti representative.


**Presentation Details**


**Event** Sidoti August Virtual Micro-Cap Conference

**Company Presentation** Thursday, August 20, 2026


**Time** 12:15–12:45 p.m. Eastern Time


**Track** Track 1


**Webcast**[High Roller Webcast](https://sidoti.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_PoWy1MCeQuGe5YLmS_-_zg#/registration)


**Conference Registration**[Sidoti Full Conference Registration](https://www.meetmax.com/sched/event_140281/investor_reg_new.html?attendee_role_id=SIDOTI_INVESTOR)
