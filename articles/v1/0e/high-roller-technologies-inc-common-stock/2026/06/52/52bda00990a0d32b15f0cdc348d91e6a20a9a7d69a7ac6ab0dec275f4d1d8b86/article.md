# High Roller Technologies Added to Russell Microcap® Index


June 29, 2026 7:00 am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_30e287017313c7b95ee36bb5025e2b3c/highroller/news/2026-06-29_High_Roller_Technologies_Added_to_Russell_156.pdf)


*Inclusion expected to enhance visibility among institutional investors and index-tracking funds*


**LAS VEGAS, Nevada, June 29, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --** High Roller Technologies, Inc. (“High Roller” or the “Company”) (NYSE: ROLR), a publicly traded online gaming and prediction markets company, today announced that it has been added to the Russell Microcap® Index as part of the 2026 Russell U.S. Indexes reconstitution, effective after the U.S. market closed on June 26, 2026. The Russell Microcap Index is widely used by investment managers and institutional investors for index funds and as a benchmark for active investment strategies. FTSE Russell determines membership for its Russell indexes primarily by objective, market-capitalization rankings and style attributes.


“Inclusion in the Russell Microcap Index marks another important milestone for High Roller as we continue to execute on our strategy, expand our public-market profile and advance our prediction markets and other product initiatives,” said Seth Young, Chief Executive Officer of High Roller Technologies. “We believe our addition to the index can help broaden awareness of High Roller among institutional investors, index funds and the broader investment community.”


The June Russell U.S. Indexes reconstitution captures up to the 4,000 largest U.S. stocks as of April 30, 2026, ranking them by total market capitalization. Membership in the Russell Microcap® Index, which remains in place for half a year beginning 2026, means automatic inclusion in the appropriate growth and value style indexes. FTSE Russell determines membership for its Russell indexes primarily by objective, market-capitalization rankings and style attributes.


For more information on the Russell Microcap® Index and the Russell indexes reconstitution, go to the “Russell Reconstitution” section on the[FTSE Russell website](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=cQFEHFEai97wUV1kP_bD7L9T17KMAS9jSomSB6prkSKQmc4L4fxvlw29g_oDDuVYch9T314HXBQXEI1-rAjMfnIDPZE_h9nl1tDov_dUyZXtZ_sSnR0KuZttJYZxfcE4yDdscd0OF4qBLh50eRgrZZKgNQg_Z4EQp072dlBT8R7aDiayVEClIvaHEKjkD5IDUFYlASvtgbj5UCqXa9ZBDkSf7DbokR3aECTA5WJ7yX1ODkv8Zrdt3QsNDrTdhxAicsczI8L9LpJuKaUnxSUerQ8jmWQV5GXtOvaK38vyc9Q5r-wMLqDlaKhinWNTNfLRmyK4L8_zyrlhYrehiGn4Jw==) .
