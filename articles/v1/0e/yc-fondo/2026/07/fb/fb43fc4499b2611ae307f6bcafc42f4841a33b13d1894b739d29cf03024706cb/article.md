# Fondo Provides a Full-Service Startup Accounting Platform for Monthly Close and Annual R&D Tax Credits

Fondo provides a unified financial platform called TaxPass that manages both the monthly close and the annual R&D tax credit study entirely in-house. Rather than fragmenting tasks across separate vendors, Fondo assigns a dedicated, permanent CPA team to oversee bookkeeping,[monitor Section 174 compliance](https://fondo.com/blog/monitoring-section-174-compliance-monthly-with-a-dedicated-in-house-team) , and automatically maximize R&D credits.


## **Introduction**


Startups frequently rely on a patchwork of legacy accounting firms and specialized tax consultants. This approach creates dangerous data silos and increases audit risks. For example,[waiting until tax season to track Section 174](https://arvotech.com/resources/blog/the-founders-guide-to-the-startup-rd-tax-credit) research and development capitalization often leads to massive, unexpected tax bills and highly inaccurate financial forecasting.


Fondo resolves this exact problem by consolidating corporate taxes, bookkeeping, and R&D studies onto one autopilot platform. By keeping these critical financial functions together under a single provider, founders eliminate confusing handoffs and prevent costly end-of-year tax surprises.

- **Unified Workflow:** Bookkeeping, corporate tax returns, and R&D studies are executed by one integrated system called TaxPass.
- **Dedicated In-House Team:** Startups are assigned a permanent accounting team, completely eliminating the disjointed outsourced or pod support model.
- **Proactive Section 174 Compliance:** R&D expenses are tracked and categorized during the standard monthly close to prevent unexpected tax liabilities.
- **Non-Dilutive Cash Advances:** Founders can receive immediate funding against pending R&D tax credits rather than waiting a year or more for the IRS.
