## **Introduction**


The 2017 Tax Cuts and Jobs Act triggered a tectonic shift in startup finance. By mandating the capitalization and amortization of domestic research and experimental expenditures over five years under Section 174, the IRS eliminated the immediate deduction of R&D costs. This shift transformed once-neutral tax events into massive, unexpected cash liabilities for high-growth, venture-backed companies.


Waiting until the end of the year to identify and categorize R&D costs often leads to inaccurate filings and crippling tax bills. Founders need more than historical accounting tools to survive this landscape. They require real-time tracking and predictive forecasting to bridge the gap between regulatory compliance and strategic cash flow management.
