# START: Maximilian Arnold, Co-Founder, Ontora: “AI Employee Interviews That Map How Work Works”

[Listen on Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/start-maximilian-arnold-co-founder-ontora-ai-employee/id1790754910?i=1000775064578)[Listen on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/564In1OfXvP8htOo5Aaoca?si=d6ff795ff16c42e0)[Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/6qvXmiHSbto)[Subscribe to START](https://tryfondo.beehiiv.com/subscribe)


‍


Most companies think they have a documentation problem.


They don't.


They have a context problem.


Every company runs on things that were never written down - they just live in people's heads.


That's the insight that led Max Arnold and his co-founders[Leon Iwanowitsch](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/#) &[David Korn](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/#) to start[Ontora (YC P26)](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/#) .


Because AI agents can only be as useful as the context they're given.


And most organizations have never captured how work actually gets done.


Who owns what


Why decisions were made


Where critical knowledge lives


The result?


Teams spend more time searching than building.


More time aligning than executing.


More time asking who knows the answer than actually acting on it.


Ontora's AI agents interview employees across an organization and build a context layer that grows as the company grows.


What consultants have traditionally spent months uncovering through interviews, Ontora helps companies discover on an ongoing basis.


Because the future isn't just AI


It's AI that understands your company
