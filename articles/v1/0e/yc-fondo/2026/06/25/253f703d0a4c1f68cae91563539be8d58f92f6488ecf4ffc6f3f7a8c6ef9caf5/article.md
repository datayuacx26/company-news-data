# START: Seb Poole, Co-Founder & CEO, Modern “Secure AI for enterprise IT”

[Listen on Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/start-seb-poole-co-founder-ceo-modern-secure-ai-for/id1790754910?i=1000774766534)[Listen on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/1kSSPFvWWfsPKBphDwoQEJ?si=190d1ef05ad64562)[Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/ia_fLs1TIJ8)[Subscribe to START](https://tryfondo.beehiiv.com/subscribe)

Every company runs on a hidden workforce.


The people who reset the passwords. Clear the MFA requests. Provision the access. Push the tickets through the systems so the rest of the company can work.


Seb Poole wants to give that work to agents.


Not a better way to track the request. A workforce that completes it.


He's seen the problem from the inside.


His last company, Frankie Health, was acquired by Unmind - a leading workplace mental health provider whose customers include Disney and Uber.


At every company he built, the same drag: ServiceNow and Jira tracked the work, but a human still had to do it.


So he and his co-founder Aleks Tomovski built Modern (YC P26). Seb scaled Frankie Health into Unmind; Aleks built developer platforms at Wix and Canva, including Wix's first low-code platform.


Modern is secure agents that understand your business, learn the right workflows, and resolve tickets end to end across the IT function - inside the tools your team already uses, from Slack and Teams to email, voice, WhatsApp, and Telegram.


The result they're seeing today: 80%+ automation across IT team requests.


Not another ticketing system.


The workforce that resolves the ticket.
