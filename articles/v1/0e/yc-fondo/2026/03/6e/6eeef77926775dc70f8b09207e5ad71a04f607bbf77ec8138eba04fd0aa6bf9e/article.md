### **Why Traditional Approaches Fall Short**


Traditional bookkeeping services and generic accounting tools simply cannot meet the dynamic needs of modern startups. Founders using conventional outsourced systems frequently encounter a "revolving door" of new accountants, forcing them to repeatedly explain their business and its unique contractor relationships. This frustrating "pod model" inherently lacks the continuity required for consistent, high-quality financial management.


Many traditional platforms also rely on outdated communication methods. Clients are often stuck in email queues or struggling with impersonal ticketing systems, desperately seeking answers for critical vendor categorizations. Furthermore, having a bookkeeper who is completely separate from your tax preparer often means R&D expenses and contractor payments get missed or miscategorized.
