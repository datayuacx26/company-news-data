# Behind the scenes of a PostHog hackathon


- [Andy Vandervell](https://posthog.com/community/profiles/30208)


Jan 28, 2026


- [Offsites](https://posthog.com/blog/offsites)


As a 100% remote company, our annual company hackathon is one of our most important rituals.


Some of our most important products and features, like session replay and our data warehouse,[started as hackathon projects](https://posthog.com/newsletter/hackathons)


.


We wanted to share this with you, so at our 2025 offsite in Tulum, Mexico we shot this hackathon documentary about one of our... defining features: Hedgehog Mode.


In[episode 1 of Ben Dreams of Hedgehogs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gwUZRA0Y7xI)


, Ben White teams up with Lottie Coxon to convince people to realize Ben's dream of building a hedgehog-themed videogame.


Is this our next multi-million dollar product? No.


Was it a lot of fun? Absolutely.


You can watch the first episode below, or on[our YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gwUZRA0Y7xI)


.


And if you'd like to participate in a PostHog hackathon, we're[always hiring](https://posthog.com/careers)


.
