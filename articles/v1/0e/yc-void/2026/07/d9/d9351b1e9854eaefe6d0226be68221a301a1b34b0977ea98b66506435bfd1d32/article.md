# Latest Breakfast Byte Event Hosted by Digital Catapult and Glass.AI.

Bringing together public sector analysts, data scientists, economists, and consultants to share knowledge!


That was the thinking behind our second Breakfast Byte event, hosted by Glass.AI and[Digital Catapult](https://www.digicatapult.org.uk/) . The event delved into important discussions on developing and tracking the UK’s Industrial Strategy and on how AI can strengthen the evidence base for public policy.


Thanks again to our brilliant speakers:


- **Jonathan Bateman** - Chief Economist - Industrial Strategy Advisory Council.
- **Anna Valero** - Professor and Director of the Growth Programme at the Centre for Economic Performance - LSE.
- **Rajssa Mechelli** - Assistant Director, Economics - Competition and Markets Authority (CMA).
- **Peter Varnai** - Partner, Health and Life Science - Technopolis.
- **David Dorrell** - Executive Director (Data Science & AI) - Frontier Economics.
- **Brian MacAulay** - Director of Economics and Insights, Digital Catapult.
- **Aris Xylouris** - Head of Data Policy Analysis Team - Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT).
- **Cristina Rosemberg** - Managing Partner - Technopolis.
