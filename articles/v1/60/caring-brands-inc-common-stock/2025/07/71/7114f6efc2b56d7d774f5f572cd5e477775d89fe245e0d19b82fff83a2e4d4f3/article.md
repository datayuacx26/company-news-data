# Caring Brands Appoints Christopher M. Galeta, Esq. to Board of Directors

Fort Pierce, FL (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) (OTCQB: CBRA) – Caring Brands Inc. today announced the appointment of Christopher M. Galeta, Esq., a highly experienced Florida attorney and business leader, to its Board of Directors.


Mr. Galeta has been a Member of the Florida Bar for the past 27 years and is admitted to practice in all courts in the State of Florida, as well as the United States District Court for the Southern District of Florida. His early legal career included roles defending insureds for some of the largest national and regional insurance companies. He later served as Associate Counsel for both Travelers Insurance Company and GEICO Insurance Company.


Mr. Galeta’s legal expertise spans property, liability, commercial litigation, and personal injury. In 2012, he founded Christopher M. Galeta P.A., where he continues to represent clients across a range of legal matters. In 2018, he launched Ocean View Title & Escrow in Palm Beach Gardens, Florida, which serves residential and commercial markets. His current law practice includes real estate, aircraft, and marine transaction representation for both individuals and corporations. Mr. Galeta holds the AV Preeminent Rating from Martindale-Hubbell and has been recognized among Florida’s Legal Elite.


“We are honored to welcome Mr. Galeta to the Board,” said Brian John, Chairman of the Board of Caring Brands Inc. “His distinguished legal background, extensive experience across both litigation and transactional matters, and demonstrated leadership make him an invaluable addition. We are grateful for the insight and guidance he brings as we continue executing our growth strategy and expanding our product portfolio.” About Caring Brands Caring Brands Inc. has a growing portfolio of unique, patented, and clinically validated products for skin and hair growth. The Company intends to launch a total of 5 products over the next 2 years in addition to in-licensing additional products. Management has a successful track record of strategic acquisitions, rapid product development, IP development and product licensing. Revenues from the sales of Hair Enzyme Booster for the treatment of hair loss, and Photocil for the treatment of psoriasis and vitiligo, are currently being generated by direct sales in the US and licensees in India. Additional product opportunities include CB-101 for the treatment of eczema, Nostingz, a sunscreen that prevents jellyfish stings, and an at home diagnostic test for Ebola.
