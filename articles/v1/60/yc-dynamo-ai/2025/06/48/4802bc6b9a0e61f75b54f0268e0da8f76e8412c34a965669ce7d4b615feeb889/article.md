# Dynamo AI Co-Founders Named to Forbes 30 Under 30 Asia 2025: A Milestone Year for Innovation and Impact

We’re proud to share that the co-founders of **Dynamo AI,** Vaikkunth Mugunthan and Christian Lau **,** have been named to the[Forbes 30 Under 30 Asia 2025](https://www.forbes.com/30-under-30/2025/asia/ai) list — a recognition of their key contributions to the AI industry, including empowering both enterprises and policymakers to facilitate the development of secure and compliant GenAI.


This recognition comes during a banner year for Dynamo AI, when we've been repeatedly acknowledged as a key innovator in enterprise AI.


In addition to the Forbes honor, **Dynamo AI was featured in the 2025**[Gartner Innovation Guide for Generative AI Technologies](https://www.dynamo.ai/blog/dynamo-ai-recognized-in-the-2025-gartner-r-how-to-secure-custom-built-ai-agents-report) as an *Emerging Visionary* — a testament to our pioneering work in generative AI engineering and custom AI agent development. The company was also highlighted in **Gartner’s 2025 “How to Secure Custom-Built AI Agents” report** , spotlighting our unique approach to building secure, customizable AI solutions tailored to enterprise needs.


Further solidifying its leadership in the AI ecosystem, **Dynamo AI was included in**[Forrester's The AI Governance Solutions Landscape, Q2 2025](https://bit.ly/3FeE5Tu) , showcasing our commitment to responsible, secure, and auditable AI agent deployment at scale.


From redefining how enterprises engineer GenAI to establishing best-in-class security protocols and governance models, Dynamo AI continues to lead with purpose and innovation. The Forbes 30 Under 30 recognition is not just a celebration of our founders — it’s a celebration of what’s possible when cutting-edge technology meets bold vision.


**Congratulations to the Dynamo AI team on a truly transformative year!**
