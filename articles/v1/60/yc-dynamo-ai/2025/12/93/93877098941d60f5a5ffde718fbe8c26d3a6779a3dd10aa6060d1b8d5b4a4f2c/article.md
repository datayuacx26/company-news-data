# U.S. Army Selects Dynamo AI to Advance Scalable AI Risk Management for Mission-Critical Defense Applications

### SBIR Direct to Phase II Award accelerates the deployment of trustworthy AI through advanced evaluation, observability, and risk mitigation solutions.


Dynamo AI, a leading provider of test, evaluation, and custom guardrails, was awarded a U.S. Army SBIR contract focused on Scalable Tools for Automated AI Risk Management and Algorithmic Analysis for mission-critical defense applications.


Through this Direct to Phase II contract, Dynamo AI will help set conditions for secure, scalable AI/ML integration to accelerate the deployment of advanced analytics and deliver trusted AI at speed and scale.


Dynamo AI will develop tailored AI model evaluations to ensure system performance and trustworthiness, as well as guardrails customized to U.S. Army use cases, enabling real-time observability, security, and privacy. These capabilities will be reinforced with automated stress testing to identify risks such as data leakage, adversarial jailbreaking, and hallucination vulnerabilities, ensuring Army AI systems are robust, compliant, and mission-ready.


> *“The Department of Defense is accelerating adoption of AI, but mission success depends on trust – the reliability, security, and accountability of these systems,” said Christian Lau, Co-Founder and Chief Product Officer at Dynamo AI. “Through this contract, Dynamo will equip the Army with scalable AI risk management capabilities that ensure AI systems can perform safely and effectively in the most demanding defense environments.* ”


At project completion, the Army will gain a ready-to-deploy, mission-aligned toolkit that enhances AI risk management, ultimately strengthening the security, reliability, and transparency of AI-enabled systems across critical defense operations.
