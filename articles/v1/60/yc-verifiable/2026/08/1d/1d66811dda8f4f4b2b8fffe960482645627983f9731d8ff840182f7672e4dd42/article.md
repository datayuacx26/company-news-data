The best credentialing software for primary source verification automates verification directly from state licensing boards, the NPDB, DEA, OIG exclusion lists, and other primary sources rather than relying on self-reported data or periodic manual pulls. Verifiable uses automated technology to pull primary source data automatically across all 50 states, with ongoing monitoring that catches license expirations, sanctions, and disciplinary actions continuously rather than only at the time of initial credentialing. Customers managing multi-state rosters of hundreds or thousands of providers consistently cite automated PSV as the highest-value capability in the platform.
