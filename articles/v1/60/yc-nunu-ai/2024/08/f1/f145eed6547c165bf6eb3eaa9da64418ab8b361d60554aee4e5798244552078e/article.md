# Beating the World Record in Pokémon AI Live on Stream

Hi everyone!


Remember how we showed off our AI completing the[Intro Tunnel of Hogwarts Legacy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GHgmBQAy-yI&t) in December? Well, that was cool, but the storyline there is pretty linear. We wanted to really push our AI's limits, so we took on a tougher challenge: Pokémon Emerald.


# Pokémon Emerald and the past world record


For years, research teams have been using Pokémon Emerald to test AI systems, especially for reinforcement learning (RL). It's a great benchmark because it tests all sorts of AI capabilities. Until now, the best RL-based AI bots could only reach the second gym - that was the world record. To get this far, the player needs to explore the map, build a team of Pokémon, and manage their inventory, among other challenges. For context, most humans need about 2-3 hours to get that far.


# Our World Record Stream


To show off what our AI agent May can do in more open-ended games, we decided to livestream everything. We did three streams, one for each gym:


[Gym 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MgHj3ZEHrR4) : 1h45min of gameplay
[Gym 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yF-3FXot1vU) : 2h25min of gamplay
[Gym 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZSHCC-UNgm0) : 1h of gamplay


That's right, we broke the record! 🎉 May reached and beat the third gym in just 5h15min total. That's actually faster than I was when I first played Pokémon!


# Interactability


Here's a cool underappreciated feature: viewers could change May's behavior during the stream by typing commands in the chat (like "!walk a circle"). May would then try to follow these instructions. We've included some of these moments in our highlights.
