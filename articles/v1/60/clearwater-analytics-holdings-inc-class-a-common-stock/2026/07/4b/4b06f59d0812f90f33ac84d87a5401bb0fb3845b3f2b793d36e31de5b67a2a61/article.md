# From Document to Decision: A New Standard for Private Fund Analytics


### Join us for a full product demo to see what private markets intelligence looks like when it’s built on data you can actually trust.


Private markets allocations are growing, but the operational burden is growing faster, with teams overwhelmed by hundreds of quarterly GP documents, manual data cleanup, and error-prone reporting. It’s a critical bottleneck now solved by Clearwater’s Fund Analytics. Join us for a live product demo where we’ll show:


- **Validated data in hours, not weeks.** AI processing at 90%+ accuracy turns GP documents into analytics-ready data.


- **Benchmarks you can defend.** Built on your own validated cashflows. IRR, TVPI, DPI, PME, and peer comparisons that hold up.


- **Full look-through.** Total portfolio to individual holding, with a data quality score on every asset.


- **Model the future.** Forward-looking pacing and allocation comparison to plan deployment before the call is made.


- **Reporting how you need it.** Feed the data into various applications you are using (accounting, research, reg reporting, total portfolio).
