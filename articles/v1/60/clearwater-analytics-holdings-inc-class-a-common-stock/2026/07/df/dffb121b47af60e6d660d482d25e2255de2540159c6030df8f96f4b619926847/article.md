# InvestOps Europe 2026


Meet the Clearwater team at Europe’s leading investment-operations conference — 15–17 September 2026, London.


InvestOps Europe 2026 brings together 500+ senior buy-side operations leaders — COOs and Heads of Investment Operations from asset managers, hedge funds, pension funds and insurers — for three days of practitioner-led discussion on the future of the investment operating model.


Clearwater Analytics is a proud sponsor. Join us on Innovation Day (15 September) for a series of intimate roundtables where operations leaders discuss moving from fragmented, multi-source data to a single source of truth — automating reconciliation, accounting and reporting across the investment book of record. Throughout the conference, visit us at Stand #44-45 to see how leading buy-side teams consolidate their operations onto one platform.


Want a focused conversation on-site? Book time with our team in advance to skip the queue and make the most of your three days at the Hilton London Metropole.
