# Struggling with Menopause Symptoms?

Menopause is a natural part of life, but it doesn’t always feel easy. The changes in your body, mood, and energy can feel overwhelming, and it’s common to wonder if anyone truly understands what you’re going through. At LunaJoy, we’re here to tell you: you’re not alone, and there’s help.


## **What Is Menopause and How Can It Affect You?**


Menopause marks the end of your menstrual cycles, usually happening between ages 45 and 55. But it’s not just about periods stopping—it’s a transition that can bring some tough symptoms, like:


-


Feeling sad or anxious


-


Trouble sleeping


-


Forgetting things or struggling to focus


-


Feeling tired all the time


-


Mood swings or irritability


These changes are completely normal, but that doesn’t make them any less challenging. They can impact how you feel about yourself, your relationships, and your everyday life. That’s why taking care of your mental health during this time is so important.
