# i3 Verticals and the West Virginia Supreme Court Expand Partnership to Modernize Statewide Judicial System

i3 Verticals is proud to announce a major milestone in our long-standing collaboration with the **Supreme Court of Appeals of West Virginia** . Under a newly signed contract, i3 Verticals will deliver its **i3 CourtOne™ Case Management Solution (CMS)** to the state’s Circuit, Family, and Magistrate Courts—advancing transparency, unification, and operational efficiency across the entire judicial system.


This engagement marks a pivotal step in West Virginia’s modernization efforts. By implementing CourtOne™, the state will transition to a **single, unified platform** that simplifies case management, streamlines workflows, and enables citizens to access information with greater ease. The initiative reflects a shared vision between i3 Verticals and the West Virginia Supreme Court: to **build a more connected, accessible, and cost-effective judicial experience** for everyone involved.


> “We are thrilled to expand our partnership with the Supreme Court of Appeals of West Virginia,” said **Dave Graves, Executive Vice President, JusticeTech®** . “For decades, states have attempted to unify their judicial systems in ways that resulted in high costs to their citizens. West Virginia desired something different—an innovative solution that would unify their judicial system and deliver a high-quality product to their citizens, all at a fraction of the historical net cost for these systems. We feel that our collaboration with West Virginia achieves these goals and creates a new model for the state market.”


In addition to the CourtOne™ CMS, i3 Verticals will introduce a suite of **value-added services** designed to enhance transparency and efficiency. The expanded **WVPASS** platform will give citizens broader access to aggregated public court data, while **Resolv 360™** , i3’s Fund Recovery Solution, will streamline financial processes and improve disposition rates across courts.


Chief Justice **William R. “Bill” Wooton** of the Supreme Court of Appeals of West Virginia noted, “The i3 CourtOne™ CMS will unite our Circuit, Family, and Magistrate Courts under a single, more efficient system while also promoting cost savings and increased transparency for the citizens of West Virginia.”


This expanded partnership underscores i3 Verticals’ mission to empower state and local governments through **innovative, cloud-native software solutions** that help them serve their communities more effectively. With thousands of installations across all 50 states and Canada, i3 Verticals remains at the forefront of driving modernization across the public sector.
