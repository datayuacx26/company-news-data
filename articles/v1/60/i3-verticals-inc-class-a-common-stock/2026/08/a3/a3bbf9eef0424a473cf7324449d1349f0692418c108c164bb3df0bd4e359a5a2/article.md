# i3 Verticals & MV Solutions Modernize Kansas Insurance Verification

###### Real-Time Insurance Verification


MV Solutions, a subsidiary of i3 Verticals, has launched[Kansas’ Insurance Verification System (KSIVS)](https://go.i3verticals.com/MVS-KSIVS) .


KSIVS verifies insurance coverage in real time using the Kansas Department of Revenue’s motor vehicle records and data from 317 insurance companies, improving roadside safety and streamlining traffic stops and registration renewals.


“Vehicle insurance is essential to protect citizens from the expenses of a crash,” said Colonel Erik Smith, Superintendent of the Kansas Highway Patrol. “A real-time insurance verification system allows law enforcement to instantly determine whether a motorist has all the coverage necessary for safe travel on Kansas roadways.”


MV Solutions will continue working with Kansas officials to develop an enforcement mechanism targeting uninsured drivers.


###### Raising The Bar


Since its founding in 2006, MV Solutions has raised the standard for real-time insurance verification. i3 Verticals is excited to partner with MV Solutions to continue to bring industry-leading insurance verification to our customers.


i3 and MV Solutions combined expertise and complete, end-to-end insurance compliance program delivers necessary, integrated tools for improving road safety and operations for organizations like KSIVS.
