# The i3 Platform Advantage: Purpose-Built Software for Modern Government

Government agencies rely on technology to manage critical operations, serve constituents, and meet growing compliance demands. Yet many organizations still struggle with disconnected systems that create inefficiencies and data silos.


That’s why i3 Verticals has developed a portfolio of purpose-built software platforms designed specifically for public sector organizations. Each platform serves a distinct government function while providing the tools agencies need to streamline operations, improve service delivery, and maintain accurate records.


###### Purpose-Built for Government


The i3 platform portfolio supports a wide range of government functions, including courts, public safety, citizen services, utilities, transportation, and education. Rather than forcing agencies to adapt to generic software, each platform is designed around the unique workflows and requirements of its users.


- **[i3 CourtOne](https://i3corpupdate.wpengine.com/publicsector/courts/)** – A unified court platform designed to streamline operations and simplify case management.
- **[i3 ResponseOne](https://i3corpupdate.wpengine.com/publicsector/public-safety/)** – A unified public safety platform designed to streamline operations and improve agency efficiency.
- **[i3 CivicCentral](https://i3corpupdate.wpengine.com/publicsector/public-admin/)** – A highly integrative public administration platform with tools and capabilities to simplify processes, eliminate data silos, and provide superior support to constituents.
- **[i3 Unify360](https://i3corpupdate.wpengine.com/publicsector/utilities/)** – An integrative, mobile-ready utilities platform with tools to aid with billing, support, payments, and more.
- **[i3’s Transportation Platform](https://i3corpupdate.wpengine.com/publicsector/transportation/)** – A revolutionary transportation platform designed to replace legacy systems, improve citizen self-service, and automate dealership and government agency operations.
- **[i3 Education](https://i3corpupdate.wpengine.com/education/)** – A powerful, easy-to-use platform that connects nutrition, events, school stores, inventory, reporting, payments and more.


###### What Makes i3 Platforms Different?


Every i3 platform is built on a shared foundation that helps agencies operate more effectively:


- **System of Record Architecture** – A trusted source for operational data, transactions, and workflows.
- **Compliance & Audit Readiness** – Secure, traceable processes designed to support regulatory requirements.
- **Embedded Payments** – Fully integrated payment capabilities that streamline transactions and reduce reconciliation challenges.


###### Supporting Better Government Outcomes


Government doesn’t operate in silos, and technology shouldn’t either. i3 platforms help agencies:


- Improve constituent experiences
- Centralize critical data and records
- Streamline operations and workflows
- Reduce manual processes
- Support compliance and reporting requirements


Together, these platforms form the operational backbone for modern public sector organizations, helping agencies work more efficiently while delivering better services to the communities they serve.
