# Eyes on i3: i3 Verticals Selected by the Arizona Department of Real Estate to Modernize Licensing Processes

We’re excited to share that i3 Verticals has been chosen by the Arizona Department of Real Estate (ADRE) to deliver our Regulatory Licensing and Credentialing Software solution. This partnership will help modernize how real estate licenses are processed across the state, making the experience faster, more efficient, and more intuitive for everyone involved.


###### **Bringing Modern Technology to Real Estate Licensing**


At the core of this effort is our Licensing and Compliance Case Management software—part of i3’s broader Public Administration platform. The goal is simple: reduce manual work, cut down on delays, and give both applicants and staff a smooth, streamlined process.


ADRE is focused on providing a dependable, user-friendly experience for real estate professionals and Arizona residents. The new system will enable ADRE to improve ease of use for applicants and staff while ensuring compliance, transparency, and smooth operations.


###### **A Shared Commitment to Better Service**


“ADRE’s commitment to improving accessibility and efficiency aligns perfectly with our vision,” said Paul Christians, Chief Revenue Officer at i3 Verticals. “Our regulatory licensing platform is built to simplify complex workflows, increase transparency, and deliver better experiences for both staff and applicants. We’re proud to partner with the Arizona Department of Real Estate as it modernizes the way licenses are processed across the state.”


###### **Key Features of the i3 Verticals Regulatory Licensing Solution**


The new system includes a comprehensive suite of tools designed to make licensing more efficient and reliable:


- Streamlined management of fees, licenses, and taxes
- Builtin functionality tailored specifically for regulatory agencies
- Automated creation and printing of certificates, including serialized licenses
- Configurable payment cycles to ensure accurate and timely fund distribution


###### **Supporting the Future of Digital Government**


This partnership reflects i3 Verticals’ continued commitment to helping government agencies modernize operations and better serve their communities. By delivering scalable, cloud-based solutions, i3 empowers organizations like ADRE to operate more efficiently while providing a more accessible and user-friendly experience for constituents.
