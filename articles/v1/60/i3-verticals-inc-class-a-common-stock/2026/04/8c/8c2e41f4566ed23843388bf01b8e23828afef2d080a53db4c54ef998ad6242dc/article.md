# i3 Verticals Strengthens Louisiana Parish Operations with Digital Technology Upgrades

###### Strengthening Parish Operations Across Louisiana


i3 Verticals is proud to continue partnering with parishes across Louisiana as they adopt modern technology that improves efficiency, access, and service for their communities


###### Advancing Efficiency Through Digital Workflows


Through recent technology upgrades, i3 Verticals has supported parish offices statewide in enhancing their ImageFlow systems and transitioning critical processes to fully paperless workflows. These upgrades enable electronic filing, digital signatures, and streamlined document management—helping staff work more efficiently while significantly reducing delays for the public.


###### Replacing Manual Processes with Smarter Solutions


By replacing manual, paper-based processes with secure digital solutions, parish offices gain greater visibility into daily operations, improve record accuracy, and deliver faster, more reliable service to attorneys, agencies, and citizens alike.


###### Expanding Access with ClerkConnect


Many parishes have also expanded services through ClerkConnect, a secure online portal that provides convenient access to public records. Through a single, user-friendly platform, legal professionals and community members can search records, submit filings, print documents, and manage information across multiple offices throughout the state through one unified system.


###### Supporting Ongoing Modernization Efforts


i3 Verticals is proud to support the 13 Louisiana parishes that have recently expanded their services through system upgrades and ongoing modernization efforts. We value the strong partnerships built with Louisiana parishes and sheriffs and deeply appreciate the trust parish leaders place in i3 Verticals as they continue moving toward more connected, technology-driven operations.


Together, these advancements help clerks’ offices modernize records management, reduce operational delays, and expand public access to information—creating a stronger, more efficient foundation for the future of parish services.
