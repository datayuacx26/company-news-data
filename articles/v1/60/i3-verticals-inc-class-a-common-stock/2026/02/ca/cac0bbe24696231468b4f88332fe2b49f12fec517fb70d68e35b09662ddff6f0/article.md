# Eyes on i3: JusticeTech® Momentum Accelerates Across Georgia and Arkansas

###### JusticeTech® Momentum Builds Across the Southeast


JusticeTech® momentum is building across the Southeast, and i3 Verticals continues to lead the charge by expanding modern, cloud-native solutions that improve access, efficiency, and transparency for courts and public safety agencies alike. From eFiling to digital payments, recent activations in Georgia and Arkansas highlight how i3 Verticals is helping agencies modernize operations while better serving their communities.


###### Streamlining eFiling in Georgia


In Georgia, i3 Verticals proudly welcomes Worth County Magistrate Court as the latest court to go live with TrueFiling, i3’s secure, web-based eFiling solution. With TrueFiling now live, Worth County is streamlining its entire filing process—reducing foot traffic, accelerating turnaround times, and freeing up valuable staff resources. Citizens and legal professionals can submit documents securely online 24/7, eliminating paper-based delays and creating a more accessible, responsive court experience for the community.


---


###### Expanding Digital Payments Across Arkansas


JusticeTech® expansion is also accelerating in Arkansas, where i3 Verticals has successfully implemented its solutions across multiple courts and agencies statewide. New implementations include Monroe County District Court – Clarendon Division, Greene County Sheriff’s Office, Marion County District Court – Yellville Division, Marion County District Court – Flippin Division, South Arkansas County District Court – St. Charles Department, and Izard County District Court – Melbourne.


These agencies now benefit from streamlined, secure online payment capabilities—giving staff real-time access to payment data while offering citizens faster, more convenient ways to resolve obligations.


###### Modernizing Fund Recovery with Resolv 360


Meanwhile, i3 Verticals continues to grow its JusticeTech® footprint in Georgia through expanded adoption of i3 Resolv 360, the company’s comprehensive fund recovery solution. Carrollton Police Department and Morrow Police Department have partnered with i3 Verticals to modernize revenue recovery operations.


Resolv 360 enables agencies to offer flexible payment plans, track outreach and communications through a centralized dashboard, and improve recovery rates through secure, technology-driven workflows—all while reducing administrative strain on staff.


###### A Broader Trend Toward Smarter JusticeTech®


Together, these wins reflect a broader trend: courts and public safety agencies are embracing smarter JusticeTech® solutions that simplify operations, enhance transparency, and strengthen community trust. As i3 Verticals continues to expand across Georgia, Arkansas, and beyond, the focus remains the same—empowering agencies with technology that works for the people they serve.
