## Starbucks to Participate in the 6th Annual Evercore Consumer and Retail Conference


06/02/2026


Download (opens in new window)


SEATTLE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Starbucks Corporation (NASDAQ: SBUX) today announced that Brian Niccol, chairman and chief executive officer, will participate in a keynote fireside chat at the 6th Annual Evercore Consumer and Retail Conference on Tuesday, June 9th, 2026, at 11:40 a.m. Eastern Time.


The fireside chat will be webcast live from the company’s Investor Relations website at[https://investor.starbucks.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.starbucks.com&esheet=54546314&newsitemid=20260602473511&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.starbucks.com&index=1&md5=70ce9533f7cc5283e9e8daa38c2f1d28) on the Events & Presentations page.

Today, with a global footprint of more than 41,000 company-operated and licensed coffeehouses and a growing presence in consumer-packaged goods, we are the world's premier purveyor of specialty coffee. Through our unwavering commitment to excellence and our guiding principles, we bring the unique *Starbucks Experience* to life for every customer through every cup.
