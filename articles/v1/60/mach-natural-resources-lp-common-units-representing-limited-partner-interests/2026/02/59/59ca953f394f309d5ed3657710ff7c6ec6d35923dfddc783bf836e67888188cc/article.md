# Mach Natural Resources LP Announces Earnings Release and Conference Call Schedule for Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025

# Mach Natural Resources LP Announces Earnings Release and Conference Call Schedule for Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025


February 12, 2026 4:08 pm EST[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_4d209f0ad0d74e314edc8373d6535e3a/machresources/news/2026-02-12_Mach_Natural_Resources_LP_Announces_Earnings_51.pdf)


OKLAHOMA CITY--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Mach Natural Resources LP (NYSE: MNR) (“Mach” or the “Company”) announced today that it will report fourth quarter and full year 2025 results on Thursday, March 12, 2026, after the close of trading on the New York Stock Exchange. The fourth quarter and full year 2025 earnings release will be available on the Company’s website at[www.ir.machnr.com](https://ir.machnr.com/) .


The Company will host a conference call to discuss its financial and operating results at 9:00 a.m. Central (10:00 a.m. Eastern) on Friday, March 13, 2026. Participants can access the conference call by dialing 877-407-2984. A webcast link to the conference call will be provided on the Company’s website at[www.ir.machnr.com](https://ir.machnr.com/) . A replay will also be available on the Company’s website following the call.


**About Mach Natural Resources LP**


Mach Natural Resources LP is an independent upstream oil and gas company focused on the acquisition, development and production of oil, natural gas, and NGL reserves. The Company operates a diversified portfolio across the Anadarko, Permian and San Juan Basins. For more information, please visit[www.machnr.com](http://www.machnr.com/) .
