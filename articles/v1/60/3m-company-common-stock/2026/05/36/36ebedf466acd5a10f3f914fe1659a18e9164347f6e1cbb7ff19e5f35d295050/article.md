# 3M Board Declares Quarterly Dividend

ST. PAUL, Minn.


, May 12, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- The 3M Company Board of Directors (NYSE:MMM) today declared a dividend on the company's common stock of $0.78 per share for the second quarter of 2026. The dividend is payable June 12, 2026, to shareholders of record at the close of business on May 22, 2026.


3M has paid dividends to its shareholders without interruption for more than 100 years.
