# Marine Faurie joins Finary as VP of Operations

Marine Faurie joins Finary as VP of Operations!


A graduate of **ESSEC Business School** , Marine spent nine years at **HelloFresh** , the global foodtech leader operating in 18 countries and listed on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange.


Based first in Berlin, she held several strategic roles within the group before **launching and leading the Italian market** , which she turned into a success story.


Her unique background — combining experience at **HelloFresh’s headquarters in Strategy & Expansion** , working directly with the C-level team, and hands-on leadership as **Managing Director of HelloFresh Italy** — gives her the rare ability to bridge **strategic vision and operational execution** .


> “Marine shares our culture of performance and brings operational excellence.
> Her dual expertise — both strategic and on-the-ground — will be a key asset as we accelerate our growth in France and across Europe.”
> *Mounir Laggoune, President & Co-founder of Finary*


At Finary, Marine’s mission will be to **turn our strategic vision into flawless execution** , working closely with the founding team.


She will lead all operational teams and support the **structuring of the organization** to sustain our next phase of ambitious growth.


> “I’m thrilled to join Finary at such a pivotal moment in its journey.
> My goal is to structure a hypergrowth company while preserving what makes Finary so special: ambition, innovation, and a relentless focus on the customer.
> I’m convinced that combining strategic vision, technological innovation, and operational excellence will be our greatest lever to become a European leader.” *Marine Faurie, VP of Operations at Finary*
