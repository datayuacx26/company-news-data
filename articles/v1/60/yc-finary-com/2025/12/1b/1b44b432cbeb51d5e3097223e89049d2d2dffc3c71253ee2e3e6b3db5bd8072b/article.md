# November 2025 Product Update

Managing your wealth should feel simple, transparent, and secure. This month, we shipped several improvements designed to give you better visibility over your assets, stronger protection for your account, and more confidence in the data you see every day.


## A complete view of your Finary Life contract


You now have a dedicated page for your Finary Life contract, directly in the app.


It allows you to track all your investments: the value of your contract, a detailed breakdown of your holdings (unit-linked funds and euro fund), as well as the full history of your transactions, with details for each operation.


This new view brings more clarity and readability to your life insurance, and lays the groundwork for a fully manageable life insurance experience within Finary, with management actions coming soon directly in the app.

## Enhanced security for your crypto transfers


To strengthen the security of your transactions and comply with European regulations, we have rolled out the Travel Rule on Finary Crypto.


Each crypto transfer (deposit or withdrawal) now includes consistency checks based on your account information, to ensure that only wallets you actually own can be used.


Depending on the type of wallet:


- transfers to or from an exchange platform are generally verified automatically between service providers,
- transfers to or from a wallet may require, above €1,000, a simple proof of wallet ownership.


Once a wallet has been verified, no additional action is required for future transfers.

## Enhanced security when logging in from a new device


To better protect your account, Finary now verifies any login attempt coming from a device you’ve never used before. If it’s really you, you’ll simply need to confirm the login using a code sent by email (or via your 2FA if you’ve enabled it).


Selon le type de portefeuille :


- les transferts vers / depuis une plateforme d’échange sont généralement vérifiés automatiquement entre prestataires,
- les transferts vers / depuis un wallet peuvent nécessiter, au-delà de 1 000 €, une preuve simple de détention du portefeuille.


Une fois un portefeuille vérifié, aucune action supplémentaire ne sera requise pour les transferts suivants.

To manage your wealth effectively, it’s essential to understand the **actual state of your accounts** and the **freshness of the data displayed** . We’ve therefore worked on clarifying and enriching the information already available around connection management.
