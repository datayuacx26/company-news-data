## Frequently asked questions


### What are the advantages of investing in a defence ETF?


Investing in a defence ETF has several advantages. First of all, defense companies are generally considered to be **Stable** and **less sensitive to fluctuations** economical. This can provide investors with some security, even in times of economic uncertainty.


Second, ETFs allow investors to diversify their investments across multiple defense firms, reducing the risks associated with investing in a single company. Additionally, ETFs are generally less expensive than buying individual stocks, which can be an advantage for investors looking to reduce trading costs.


Finally, defense ETFs can offer investors a way to access international markets that may not be easily available through other means.


### Are there aeronautical ETFs eligible for a PEA?


A PEA (Share Savings Plan) is a French securities account that allows you to invest mainly in European shares with tax advantages. ETFs eligible for PEA must include only European shares and meet certain conditions relating to market capitalization and index composition.


There are indeed aeronautical ETFs eligible for a PEA. However, the options may be limited, as most aviation ETFs invest in companies around the world and are therefore not always eligible for PEA. It is recommended that you do thorough research and consult a financial advisor to identify the aviation ETFs eligible for PEA that best fit your investment goals.
