# Finary secures its Securities Broker License

**Marketing communication*


Today marks a historic moment for Finary. I'm beyond thrilled to announce that we have officially obtained our **Securities Broker License** from the French Prudential Authority, **ACPR** . This milestone is not just another step forward - it's the foundation for the next generation of investing in Europe. With this license, we will soon empower millions of investors across France and Europe to buy stocks and ETFs seamlessly. We are building a comprehensive **European investment platform** - one designed from the ground up for the age of AI.


## The one-stop shop for money


At Finary, our mission is bold yet simple: to make money work for everyone. France has one of the highest savings rates in Europe - **18.5%*** - but remains far behind when it comes to investing. Two forces hold people back: a lack of financial education and the difficulty of getting started.


We've already tackled the first challenge. Finary has become one of the largest financial education channels on YouTube, generating over **15 million organic views every month*** . Knowledge is power, and we've made it accessible to all. But knowledge alone isn't enough. That's why we built a platform that allows anyone to track, budget, and invest effortlessly - in just a few taps.


In 2022, we became a registered **crypto broker (PSAN)** , unlocking digital asset investing for our customers. In 2024, we became a regulated **insurance broker** and launched our **Life Insurance with Generali** .


And now, after years of relentless work, we've secured our **Securities Broker License** . This achievement reflects our resilience, our obsession with excellence, and our ability to meet the toughest regulatory standards. With it, a new world of opportunity unfolds.


## An ambitious roadmap for a new era


The stock market is the heartbeat of the real economy. It's where innovation meets ownership - where savers become shareholders in the world's greatest companies. Over the past century, the leading U.S. index, the **S&P 500** , has returned **10% annually** . The wealth potential is immense, yet most French savers are on the sidelines: **fewer than 10%** own stocks. In the U.S., **over 60%** do.


That changes now.


We're on a mission to democratize investing - to make it effortless, affordable, and empowering. Our upcoming **Brokerage Account** will let anyone invest in stocks and ETFs seamlessly, with minimal fees and no barriers. Freedom to invest. Freedom to grow. Freedom to own your future.


And we're going even further. We'll offer our own **Investment Account (PEA)** - a powerful tool that allows investors to grow their money **tax-free after 5 years** . For years, I've spoken about the PEA as the best-kept secret in investing. Now, we're bringing it to everyone.


But another brokerage account isn't enough. The world has changed. AI is transforming every industry, and finance is no exception. At Finary, we're building **AI-augmented investing** : **smart portfolios** , **automated investing** that adapts to your situation, and **personalized recommendations** based on your entire wealth picture. Not a generic robo-advisor. A **financial copilot** that knows you.


*"The PEA is subject to financial market risks. The value of your investments may fall."*


The Finary method is no longer just a theory: it is becoming a reality.
