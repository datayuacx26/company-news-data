# Finary Life Fee Reduction

## **Finary Life Fee Reduction**


We have great news to share: management fees on the Finary Life insurance contract are going down. Annual management fees are now capped at 0.75%, regardless of your Finary subscription. Unit-linked management fees drop to 0.5% per year. These are among the lowest fees on the market.


## **Among the lowest fees on the market**


As always at Finary, no hidden fees. Here is the breakdown of the new fees:


- Unit-linked management fees: 0.50%
- Euro fund management fees: 0.75%
- Entry fees: 0%
- Arbitrage fees: 0%
- Contribution fees: 0%


The mission remains the same: to offer the best life insurance on the market, at the fairest price.


## **+9.0% in 2025 with the Adventurer profile**


In 2025, Finary Life's Adventurer profile delivered a performance of +9.0%* *, compared to +6.77% for the MSCI World EUR over the same period, representing +2.23 percentage points of outperformance* .


*Investing involves a risk of capital loss. Past performance is not indicative of future results. The performance figures shown correspond to a specific management profile and may vary depending on allocation and the date of investment.


## **Self-managed investing coming soon**


This is one of the most requested features by the community: the ability to choose your own investment supports within your Finary Life insurance. Self-managed investing will be available very soon at the same rate — just 0.5% on unit-linked funds — with a very broad investment universe.


Please note: at launch, it will not immediately be possible to switch between profiled management and self-managed investing, or to make additional contributions under self-managed mode. These features will follow shortly after launch.
