# January 2026 Product updates

Our vision stays the same: make Finary the one-stop-shop to manage your money. This month, we're taking another step forward with the ability to invest in Finary Life directly from the app, a more transparent crypto experience, more flexible tracking, and over 40 fixes for increasingly reliable data.


## 📲 Finary Life: invest without leaving Finary


You can now make one-time deposits into your Finary Life contract directly within Finary.
Invest, track the status of your transaction, and stay in control, all in one place. A seamless, consistent experience, true to the promise of Finary Life.


This new feature is a significant milestone that gets us one step closer to our long awaited self-directed contract (« gestion libre »). Stay tuned!

## ↗️ Fewer surprises when transferring your cryptos


Crypto withdrawals and deposits **above €1,000** are subject to a regulatory requirement that applies to all regulated crypto platforms across the EU: the **Travel Rule** .


We've redesigned the experience to make it clearer and more predictable: you now know upfront when verification is required, and why. The result: a smoother journey when transferring your crypto assets.


## 🎯 Sharper, more flexible tracking


Your tracking is getting deeper. On mobile, PnL now switches between % and € with a single tap, based on your preference. A new 3-month range is available for your performance views, bringing more flexibility to your analysis. On the budget side, the Sankey chart now includes your sub-categories for a detailed breakdown of your spending and income.


The result: more depth, more flexibility, and tracking that adapts to the way you analyse.


[Try sub-categories](https://qxtk.adj.st/cashflow?adj_t=lrgmy9k&adj_campaign=CRM&adj_fallback=https%3A%2F%2Fapp.finary.com%2Fv2%2Fcashflow&adj_redirect_macos=https%3A%2F%2Fapp.finary.com%2Fv2%2Fcashflow&adj_deeplink_js=1)


Connection management is also getting clearer. Already-synced accounts are highlighted when adding an institution, with a possible direct redirect to existing accounts to avoid duplicates. You can also manage your connections at any time from the Synced Accounts page in your settings. The status bar now aggregates all connections requiring action on your end, with maximum context.


## ⚙️ 40+ fixes, more reliable data


Over **40 bugs** were fixed this past month. Goodvest credential errors are resolved. Recurring expense detection has been refined, and budget alerts are now sent and configurable directly from your budget on mobile, so you get notified when significant transactions occur. Securities matching has also been significantly improved (for example with eToro) and over 300 securities (stocks, funds, etc.) have been added to our reference database for broader coverage.


Finally, we're working on two fronts to deliver lasting improvement to sync reliability. Internally, a structural effort is underway to ensure you can maintain reliable tracking even when a sync is temporarily unstable. In parallel, we're actively collaborating with our partners: for instance, Linxea and Yomoni syncs are now operational.
