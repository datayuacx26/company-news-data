## NASDAQ 100: General Information


### Sectoral composition


The NASDAQ 100 is a stock market index composed of **100 largest non-financial companies listed on the NASDAQ stock market** . The sectoral composition of this index is dominated by the technology sector, representing around 50% of the total market capitalization. Other important sectors include health, consumer discretionary, and communication services. Multiple multiples **Nasdaq 100 ETF** allow investors to monitor the performance of this index.


### Historic performance


The NASDAQ 100 Index is known for its impressive performance over the years. It has achieved double-digit growth since its creation in 1985. Several technology giants such as Apple, Microsoft, Amazon, Amazon, Facebook, and Alphabet (Google) are responsible for this sustained growth. The top 100 Nasdaq ETFs allow investors to take advantage of this historic performance.


### An index that does not take into account dividends


It is important to note that the NASDAQ 100 index is a price index, which means that it does not take into account dividends paid by the companies that make it up. NASDAQ 100 ETFs generally track index performance without reinvesting dividends. As a result, the performance of a NASDAQ 100 ETF may be slightly lower than that of the index itself. However, the **Nasdaq 100 ETF** offer an option for distributing or capitalizing dividends, allowing investors to benefit from the payments.


## Why invest in a NASDAQ ETF?


Investing in a NASDAQ ETF has several advantages for investors. First, it provides easy access to a **wide range of leading technology companies** , such **That Amazon** , **Apple** and **google** . These businesses are recognized for their growth potential and their ability to generate high returns.


By investing in a NASDAQ ETF, investors benefit from the diversification offered by the distribution of assets across several companies. Instead of focusing on a single stock, a NASDAQ ETF represents a basket of stocks, which helps to reduce the risks associated with the volatility of an individual stock. Additionally, NASDAQ ETFs generally offer lower management fees than actively managed funds, which can improve long-term returns.


Another advantage of investing in a NASDAQ ETF is the ability to receive or capitalize on **dividends** depending on the companies in which we invest. While dividend returns are not always guaranteed, they do provide additional income for investors. In addition, NASDAQ ETFs often offer high liquidity, making it easy to buy and sell in the market.


Investors can also take comfort in the fact that NASDAQ ETFs are generally managed by reputable and solid asset management companies. Les **funds under management (AUM)** are an indicator of the popularity and confidence that investors have placed in these financial products.


In short, investing in a NASDAQ ETF provides diversified exposure to leading technology companies, while reducing risks and enjoying benefits such as dividends and high liquidity. Choosing a NASDAQ ETF can be an interesting and simple solution to allocate part of your portfolio to this index and take advantage of the growth of the technology sector.

### Disadvantages of NASDAQ ETFs
