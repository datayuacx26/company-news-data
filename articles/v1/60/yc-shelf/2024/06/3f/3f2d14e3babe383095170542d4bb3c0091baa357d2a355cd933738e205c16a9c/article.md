# 6 DIY Home Decor Ideas for Your Flat

Living away from your home in a new city with people you may/may not know can be very challenging. However, you can still make your shared space feel like home, & guess what, with our decor ideas, you won’t even have to spend much!


Here are some creative, affordable, and easy-to-do home decor ideas that you and your flatmates can enjoy together -


#### 1. Lanterns: DIY Illumination Delights


Lanterns: DIY Illumination Delights


Create a charming lantern ball to illuminate your space with this simple DIY project. Made with balloons, twine, cornstarch and glue, the lantern can brighten up all your house parties! Hang them indoors or outdoors to create a warm, inviting atmosphere.


**Procedure:**
1. Prepare the DIY area with a plastic Sheet
2. Blow up a balloon
3. Make a mixture of glue, cornstarch, and warm water
4. Cover the balloon with petroleum jelly
5. Dip twine in the glue mixture and wrap it around the balloon
6. Let it dry for a day
7. After it dries up, spray clear paint on it & then Pop the balloon
8. Insert lights


#### 2. Stick ’n’ Stack Shelf


Stick ’n’ Stack Shelf


An eco-friendly shelf made from recycled ice cream sticks. Its hexagonal shape adds a playful touch to any room. Easy to hang and painted in different colours, it’s great for showing off small plants or decorations. Simple yet stylish, this DIY brings character to your space with ease.


**Procedure:**
1. Layout ice cream sticks in a hexagon shape and glue them together
2. Stack the sticks to the desired depth and let the glue dry
3. Create 3–4 more shelves following the same process.
4. Glue the shelves together in your desired shape
5. Evenly paint the sticks and let them dry
6. Attach hooks and hang the shelf
7. Place small decorative plants or items on the shelf


#### 3. Photo Frame Flower Jar


Photo Frame Flower Jar


Transform a simple jar into a charming photo frame vase with this easy DIY project. Perfect for adding a personal touch to your decor or as a thoughtful gift.


**Procedure:**
1. Take a clean jar of your choice.
2. Cut a piece of paper in your desired shape for a stencil.
3. Paint the jar, using the paper stencil to create a design where the jar remains unpainted. Let it dry completely.
4. Choose a photo you love and place it inside the jar, positioning it so it’s visible through the unpainted area.
5. Add flowers to the jar to create a lovely flower vase.


#### 4. Memory Lane Photo Display


Memory Lane Photo Display


A DIY project to showcase your cherished memories! Crafted with simplicity and creativity, this photo hanger adds a touch of warmth to any room. Customize it with your favourite photos and get creative with decorations to make it uniquely yours. Hang it on your wall and take a stroll down memory lane every time you pass by.


**Procedure:**
1. Prepare 2 pieces of wood preferably in a cylindrical shape of around 40 cm
2. Cut seven equal lengths of string, tie double knots at the top and end, and secure with glue.
3. Next, cut a 50 cm string.
4. Tie and glue it between the first and second strings, then between the sixth and seventh strings to form a triangle.
5. Hang the display and attach photos using washi tape behind each string.
6. Decorate with dried flowers, beads, or stickers if you want
7. Hang it on the wall and enjoy your personalized display.


#### 5. Hanging Rope Shelf


Hanging Rope Shelf


Create a stylish addition to your decor with this hanging rope shelf! Combining rustic charm with a modern touch, you can personalize it with your choice of paint or stain. Using sturdy nylon rope and wooden dowels for secure hanging, it’s perfect for showcasing plants, photos, or other decor items, adding handmade elegance to any room.


**Procedure:**
1. Mark and drill holes in wooden slabs for rope placement.
2. Cut and thread nylon rope through holes for stability.
3. Knot the rope under shelf, add dowels for reinforcement, and trim excess rope.
4. Hang the shelf, then style it with decor items.


#### 6. Living Wall Planter


Living Wall Planter


The Living Wall Planter is a beautiful and functional project that brings a touch of greenery to your home. Using simple materials like glass bottles, string, and a wooden dowel or branch, you can create a wall-mounted vase perfect for money plants or other small plants. This easy-to-make planter enhances your decor and adds a refreshing natural element to any space, making it perfect for small areas.


**Procedure:**
1. Take three small glass bottles and tie a string around the neck of each bottle.
2. Prepare a wooden branch/thick stick.
3. Tie the strings from the bottles to the branch with double knots, securing them with glue.
4. Cut another piece of string. Tie and glue it to the ends of the branch to form a hanger.
5. You can add money plants or any other plant that you may like in the bottles.
6. Your living wall planter is now ready to hang.
