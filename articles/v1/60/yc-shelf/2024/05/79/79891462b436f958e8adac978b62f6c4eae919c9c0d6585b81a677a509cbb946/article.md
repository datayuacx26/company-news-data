# Easy and Quick Recipes to Cook at Home

### 6 Easy & Quick Snacks to Cook with Roommates


It’s fun to live with roommates, especially if you share the same tastes in food. These are a few incredibly simple and tasty recipes that you can quickly prepare. Ideal for times when you want to eat something delicious but don’t have time to spend in the kitchen!


#### **1. Garlic Butter Toast**


Garlic Butter Toast


These toasts make the ideal snack or side dish because they are buttery on the inside and crispy on the outside with a hint of garlic. Easy to prepare and even easier to eat!


**Preparation Time:** 5–7 minutes


**Ingredients:** Bread, Butter, Garlic


**Procedure:** 1. Chop garlic (1 clove per 2 toasts).
2. Melt butter in the microwave.
3. Marinate garlic in butter for a minute.
4. Spread garlic butter on bread.
5. Toast in a pan until golden brown.


#### **2. Cheesy Egg Toast**


Cheesy Egg Toast


A delicious anytime food, these Cheesy Egg Toast feature perfectly cooked eggs, melted cheese, and crispy bread. They’re easy to prepare and make for a fulfilling morning meal.


**Preparation Time** : 5–7 minutes


**Ingredients:** Bread, Egg, Spring Onion, Cheese, Salt & Black Pepper, Butter/Olive oil


**Procedure:** 1. Cut a hole in the center of each bread slice.
2. Heat butter in a pan over medium-low heat.
3. Place the bread in the pan and crack an egg into each hole.
4. Season with salt, pepper, and onion.
5. Add cheese on top and cover with another slice of bread.
6. Press gently, flip, and fry until both sides are golden brown, about 3 minutes.


#### **3. Savoury Oats Pancakes**


Savoury Oats Pancakes


These flavourful and nutritious Savoury Oat Pancakes are a perfect way to start the day or have a substantial meal. They’re a favourite for any time of day because of their crispy edges and cheesy centre.


**Preparation Time:** 10 minutes (including soaking time)


**Ingredients:** 1 Cup Quick Oats, 1 Cup Milk, 2 eggs, Salt & Black Pepper, 1 Tbsp Olive Oil, 30g Mozzarella Cheese, Pinch of Crushed Red Pepper, Pinch of Parsley, Vegetable Oil for Cooking, Black Sesame Seeds


**Procedure:** 1. Combine oats and milk in a bowl and soak for 5 minutes.
2. In a separate bowl, crack and beat the eggs, then season with salt and black pepper.
3. Add olive oil, mozzarella cheese, crushed red pepper, and parsley to the eggs, and mix well.
4. Pour the egg mixture into the soaked oats and mix until combined.
5. Heat vegetable oil in a pan over medium-low heat.
6. Pour the oat mixture into the pan and sprinkle black sesame seeds on top.
7. Cook for 3–5 minutes, then flip and fry for another 2–3 minutes until golden brown.


#### **4. Crispy Paneer Fingers**


Crispy Paneer Fingers


Crispy Paneer Fingers are a quick and delicious snack. Soft paneer slices are seasoned, coated in crunchy bread crumbs, and fried to golden perfection. Enjoy these tasty bites with your favourite dipping sauce!


**Preparation Time:** 10–15 minutes


**Ingredients:** 250 grams paneer, 2 Tbsp cornflour, Juice of 1/2 lemon, 1/2 teaspoon black pepper powder, 1/2 teaspoon dry roasted coriander powder, 50 grams bread crumbs, Salt to taste, Refined oil for frying


**Procedure:** 1. Cut paneer into long slices.
2. In a bowl, mix lemon juice, black pepper powder, coriander powder, salt, and cornflour. Add a little water if needed.
3. Coat paneer slices with the mixture, then coat with bread crumbs.
4. Heat oil in a pan and deep fry the paneer pieces until golden brown.
5. Drain on paper towels and serve hot.


#### **5. Easy-Peasy Whip & Chill Chocolate Delight**


Chocolate Delight


Savour this dreamy, creamy chocolate dessert. Rich, smooth, and completely irresistible, this chilled chocolate delight is ideal for easily satisfying your sweet tooth with little effort.


**Preparation Time:** 5–7 minutes (plus 2–3 hours for chilling, optional)


**Ingredients:** 1/2 cup dark chocolate, 100 ml hot water, 2–3 tbsp whipped cream or butter


**Procedure:** 1. Break dark chocolate into pieces and put them in a bowl. Pour in the hot water and whisk until the chocolate is completely melted and smooth.
2. Place the melted chocolate in a bowl filled with ice or cold water. Whisk vigorously until the mixture thickens and becomes soft and fluffy.
3. Mix in the whipped cream (or butter) gently until fully combined.
4. Spoon your chocolate mixture into a serving bowl. If you can wait, refrigerate for 2–3 hours to let it set or you can have it like this.
5. Sprinkle a little cocoa powder on top before serving.


#### **6. Broccoli Oats Bites**


Broccoli Oats Bite


Delicious and healthful, Cheesy Broccoli Oat Cakes are a great snack. These savory cakes with their combination of broccoli, oats, and mozzarella are ideal for a weeknight dinner.


**Preparation Time:** 10–15 minutes


**Ingredients:** Broccoli, 1 Cup Oats, Garlic, Mozzarella, 2 Eggs, Salt, Turmeric, Black Pepper, Oil


**Procedure:** 1. Cut, wash, and boil broccoli until tender.
2. Mash broccoli in a bowl.
3. Add oats, minced garlic, shredded mozzarella, eggs, turmeric, salt, and black pepper. Mix well.
4. Heat oil in a pan over medium-low heat.
5. Shape mixture into bites and cook for 3–5 minutes per side until golden brown.
