# Flatmates: Family away from Home

Three flatmate sitting : Design by[Freepik](http://www.freepik.com/)


Ups and downs are a big part of everyone’s life. Rough patches are almost an everyday phenomenon: sometimes it’s your work, sometimes it's your partner, sometimes it’s your friends; and all these incidents take a toll on your mental wellbeing. To navigate through all of this you need a good support system and most of the time, it is your family. But what happens when you are living away from them? You can not call them every time, and there are a lot of things you’d not want them to worry about! That’s when you need an additional support system and that’s where having good flatmates comes in!


Living with others can be a great experience! The perks of shared living are numerous, from movie nights and shared meals to always having someone to chat with. The chemistry between flatmates can lead to deep and meaningful friendships, where shared experiences and mutual support create lasting bonds. They are your family away from home!


### Benefits of Shared Living


Shared living offers several benefits that can positively impact your mental health. It provides a support system, helps distribute responsibilities, has a lot of moments of celebration, and fosters a sense of community. These elements collectively contribute to a more balanced and supportive living environment.


#### Support System


One of the key benefits of shared living is having an additional support system. When you’re feeling down, having great flatmates means you have someone who understands your living situation and can lift your spirits. This constant companionship can significantly reduce feelings of loneliness and isolation, which are common triggers for mental health issues. Being away from family for extended periods can be tough, and the loneliness that comes with it can impact your mental well-being. Having flatmates to share your daily life with can help bridge the gap, providing emotional support and a sense of belonging.


#### Distributed Responsibilities


Sharing household tasks with flatmates also lightens the load, both physically and mentally. Chores can be a drag, but tackling them together can be a surprisingly fun bonding experience. When responsibilities are divided, the stress of maintaining a household is reduced, allowing for a more relaxed living environment.


#### Celebrating Victories (Big & Small)


Celebrating victories, both big and small, is another advantage of living with flatmates. Whether you land a dream job or ace an exam, having flatmates to celebrate your wins can make a huge difference. Sharing happiness boosts your mood and strengthens your bond, creating lasting positive memories.


### Maintaining Harmonious Shared Living


#### Have Trust — Open Communication


Open and honest communication is crucial in any relationship, and shared living is no exception. Talking to your flatmates about how you’re feeling and being receptive to their needs can prevent misunderstandings and foster a supportive atmosphere where everyone feels heard and valued.


Three flatmate enjoying Pizza : Design by[Freepik](http://www.freepik.com/)


#### Respect Privacy— Give time to yourself


Everyone needs some alone time to recharge. Communicate with your flatmates about respecting each other’s need for space and privacy. Personal time is essential for mental health, and understanding this balance helps maintain a peaceful living environment.


#### Be Considerate — Resolve Conflicts


Disagreements are inevitable, but approaching them calmly and focusing on finding mutually beneficial solutions is key. Practicing empathy and being willing to compromise helps maintain harmony. For example, if a disagreement arises over chores, consider creating a rotating schedule that satisfies everyone involved.


#### Be Flexible — Creating a Comfortable Living Space


A comfortable living space can positively impact your mental health. Work together to maintain a clean and organized home, and personalize common areas with decor that reflects everyone’s tastes, making them inviting and comfortable. Additionally, having clear rules about guests and friends visiting can prevent misunderstandings. Discussing and agreeing on guidelines ensures everyone feels comfortable with visitors, respecting each other’s privacy and space.


### If you ever feel alone, remember — You’re not.


Living with flatmates can be a fun and enriching experience. By supporting each other and prioritizing mental well-being, you can turn your shared space into a haven of happiness and friendship. But still, life can get overwhelming sometimes, and it’s important to remember that you’re not alone. If you’re struggling with your mental health, don’t hesitate to reach out for help. There are many resources available, like online support groups, mental health professionals, or even campus counsellors if you’re a student. Acknowledging the need for help and seeking it out is an essential step toward maintaining good mental health.

Try embracing the journey of shared living with the right mindset and approach, it can greatly enhance your mental health and overall quality of life, after all they might just be your family away from home!
