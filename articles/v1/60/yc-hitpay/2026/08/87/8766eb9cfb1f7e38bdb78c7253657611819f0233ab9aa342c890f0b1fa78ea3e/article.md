The Philippine digital payments market is growing rapidly. ## What Payment Methods Must a Philippine Payment Gateway Support?


The Philippine market is not card-first. E-wallet penetration — led by GCash and Maya — has outpaced card adoption across most income segments. A Quezon City sari-sari store and a Makati lifestyle brand operate in the same ecosystem and need the same rails.


A credible gateway for the Philippines must support:


-


**QR Ph** — the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas-mandated interoperable QR standard accepted by GCash, Maya, and all major banks


-


**GCash** — the Philippines’ most widely used e-wallet


-


**Maya** — digital bank and wallet with broad consumer adoption


-


**InstaPay and PESONet** — real-time and batch bank transfer rails


-


**Visa and Mastercard** — online and in-person card payments


-


**GrabPay, ShopeePay** — platform-native wallets with loyal user bases


-


**BillEase** — the leading local Buy Now, Pay Later provider for Philippine consumers


-


**Over-the-counter** — Bayad, ECPay, and Palawan for unbanked and underbanked customers


Missing even one of these creates a segment of customers who cannot pay. For deeper context on how e-wallets and gateways differ functionally, the guide on[digital wallets vs payment gateways](https://hitpayapp.com/blog/digital-wallet-vs-payment-gateways) is a useful reference.

Most Philippine payment gateways, including HitPay, support QR Ph and it is strongly recommended for any merchant accepting in-person payments.
