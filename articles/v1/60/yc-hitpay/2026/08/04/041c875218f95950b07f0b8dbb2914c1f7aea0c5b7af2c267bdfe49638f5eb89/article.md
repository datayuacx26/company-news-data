# How Freelancers in SEA Can Manage Payments with HitPay

**Quick Answer:** HitPay is a MAS-licensed payment platform that helps freelancers and service businesses in Singapore, Malaysia, and the Philippines collect payments via payment links, invoices, and local e-wallets — with no monthly fees. Payouts arrive next business day for SGD (Singapore) and PHP (Philippines); T+2 calendar days for MYR (Malaysia). Sign-up is free and approval takes 1–3 business days.


Freelance and services work is growing fast across Southeast Asia. According to[Google-Temasek e-Conomy SEA](https://economysea.withgoogle.com/) , the region’s digital economy continues to expand at pace — and a rising share of that activity involves independent professionals: designers, consultants, tutors, photographers, and personal trainers operating without a fixed business address or retail storefront.


For these operators, payment collection is often the weakest link. Clients pay via bank transfer into personal accounts, receipts are informal, and cash flow is unpredictable. A professional payment system is not a luxury — it is a baseline requirement for running a sustainable service business.


## What Payment Infrastructure Does a Freelance Business Actually Need?


Most freelancers do not need a full e-commerce stack. The core requirements are simpler:


-


A way to request payment from a client without a website


-


A method clients can pay using their preferred local app


-


A record of every transaction for bookkeeping


-


Funds settling quickly into a business bank account


The right payment setup covers all four without monthly overhead.[Understanding the difference between digital wallets and payment gateways](https://hitpayapp.com/blog/digital-wallet-vs-payment-gateways) helps freelancers choose tools that match how their clients actually prefer to pay — whether that is PayNow in Singapore, DuitNow QR in Malaysia, or GCash in the Philippines.


## How Do Payment Links Work for Freelancers?


Payment links are the most practical tool for service businesses. A payment link is a URL that opens a checkout page — no website required. Freelancers share it via WhatsApp, email, or Instagram, and clients pay immediately.


HitPay’s payment links support local payment methods per market, including:


-


**Singapore:** PayNow, GrabPay, ShopeePay, Visa, Mastercard, Atome


-


**Malaysia:** DuitNow QR, FPX, Touch ‘n Go, GrabPay, Visa, Mastercard


-


**Philippines:** GCash, Maya, QR Ph, InstaPay, PESONet, Visa, Mastercard


A graphic designer in Tanjong Pagar can send a payment link to a client who pays via PayNow in under 30 seconds. A personal trainer in Bangsar can collect session fees through Touch ‘n Go without handling cash. A freelance photographer in Bonifacio Global City (BGC) can bill a corporate client by card and receive funds the next business day.


For recurring clients — retainer arrangements or monthly coaching packages —[HitPay’s recurring payment links](https://hitpayapp.com/blog/recurring-payment-link) let freelancers set up automated billing cycles without manual follow-up each month.


## How Should Freelancers Handle Invoicing and Records?


Informal billing creates two problems: clients do not pay on time, and reconciliation at tax season becomes chaotic.


A structured invoicing workflow solves both. HitPay’s invoicing tool lets freelancers generate branded invoices with itemised line items and attach a payment link directly — so clients receive a professional document and a one-click payment option in the same message.


Transactions are logged automatically in the HitPay dashboard, creating a running record of every payment received. This matters for freelancers operating in markets with formal tax obligations: in the Philippines, the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) requires registered self-employed individuals to maintain proper income records. Singapore’s Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) has equivalent requirements for self-employed persons earning above the filing threshold.


For a deeper look at how invoice-linked payments work in practice,[invoice payment workflows for SMEs in Singapore and Southeast Asia](https://hitpayapp.com/blog/invoice-payment) covers the end-to-end process.

## Frequently Asked Questions


Can a freelancer (not a registered company) sign up for HitPay?


Yes. HitPay accepts sole traders and self-employed individuals, not just registered companies. Freelancers in Singapore, Malaysia, and the Philippines can sign up using individual business details, though identity verification documents are required during onboarding.


How do I collect payment from a client in Singapore without a website?


HitPay payment links work without a website. Create a payment link from the HitPay dashboard, set the amount, and send the URL to the client via WhatsApp or email. The client can pay by PayNow, GrabPay, ShopeePay, card, or other supported methods in under a minute.


How long does it take to receive funds after a client pays in Malaysia?


Domestic transactions in Malaysia (MYR) settle to the linked bank account within T+2 calendar days. Cross-border transactions — for example, a Singapore client paying a Malaysian freelancer — also settle at T+2.


What payment methods can Filipino freelancers accept through HitPay?


Freelancers in the Philippines can accept GCash, Maya, QR Ph, InstaPay, PESONet, Visa, Mastercard, GrabPay, and ShopeePay through HitPay. Cross-border acceptance includes PayNow (Singapore), QRIS (Indonesia), and PromptPay (Thailand), among others.


HitPay vs PayPal — which is better for freelancers in Southeast Asia?


HitPay is purpose-built for Southeast Asia and supports local e-wallets (GCash, Touch 'n Go, PayNow, DuitNow) that PayPal does not accept. HitPay also offers next business day domestic payouts in Singapore and the Philippines, no monthly fees, and MAS licensing. PayPal has broader global brand recognition and may suit freelancers billing clients outside Southeast Asia who prefer PayPal's buyer protection. For SEA-based client work, HitPay's local payment method coverage and settlement speed are stronger.


Is there a monthly fee to use HitPay as a freelancer?


No. HitPay charges no monthly fee and no setup fee. The only cost is a per-transaction fee, which varies by payment method and market. Full pricing is available at hitpayapp.com/pricing.


How do I issue a professional invoice and get paid through HitPay?


From the HitPay dashboard, navigate to Invoices, create a new invoice with line items and client details, then attach a payment link before sending. The client receives the invoice by email and can pay directly through the embedded link using their preferred payment method.
