# HitPay vs Airwallex Singapore — Which Platform Fits Your Business?

**Quick Answer:** HitPay and Airwallex are not direct competitors — they serve different business models. HitPay is built for local payment acceptance: PayNow, GrabPay, ShopeePay, Atome, and cards, with no monthly fee and next business day SGD payouts. Airwallex is built for global treasury: multi-currency accounts, FX transfers, and corporate spend management. A Singapore café or online retailer needs HitPay. A Singapore company paying overseas contractors or managing multi-currency cash flow needs Airwallex. Both hold MAS licences; the decision comes down to what your business actually does.


Singapore’s digital payments market is one of the most competitive in Southeast Asia, with consumers regularly switching between PayNow, GrabPay, and card payments at checkout. For a merchant in Tiong Bahru or an online seller based in Bugis, choosing the wrong payment platform means missing transactions — not just paying higher fees.


HitPay and Airwallex are both well-known names in Singapore fintech, but they solve different problems. Understanding that distinction is the first step to making the right choice.


## What does each platform actually do?


HitPay is a[payment gateway and POS platform built for SMBs](https://hitpayapp.com/blog/best-payment-gateway-singapore) across Singapore and Southeast Asia. It handles payment acceptance — online, in-person, and via payment links — with 50+ payment methods including all major local options.


Airwallex positions itself as an intelligent financial platform for global businesses. Its core products are multi-currency business accounts, global FX transfers, corporate cards, and expense management. Payment acceptance (checkout, payment links) is available but secondary to its treasury and spend management offering.


The distinction matters. A café in Orchard Road needs PayNow QR, GrabPay, and a card terminal. A software company paying overseas contractors needs multi-currency accounts and low FX rates. These are different tools.

## How fast do payouts arrive?


HitPay settles domestic SGD transactions on the next business day. Cross-border payments (e.g., a Chinese tourist paying via WeChatPay) settle next business day in SGD. This is a predictable cash flow cycle for retailers and online sellers.


Airwallex’s payout speed depends on the product being used. For payment acceptance, settlement timelines are not uniformly published for SGD domestic transactions at the SMB level.


For a retail operator managing payroll or inventory reorders, next business day SGD payouts from HitPay reduce working capital pressure.

## Practical takeaway


The HitPay vs Airwallex decision is less about which platform is better and more about which problem a business is actually solving. Airwallex is a financial infrastructure platform; HitPay is a payment acceptance platform. Most Singapore retailers, F&B operators, and online sellers need the latter — with local e-wallet coverage, no monthly fees, and reliable next business day payouts. Businesses that have significant international treasury needs or multi-currency expense management requirements will find Airwallex’s product set more relevant.

HitPay settles domestic SGD transactions on the next business day. Cross-border payment methods — such as WeChatPay, UPI, or QRIS — settle at next business day in SGD. Payouts are made directly to the merchant’s Singapore bank account.


Is HitPay regulated in Singapore?
