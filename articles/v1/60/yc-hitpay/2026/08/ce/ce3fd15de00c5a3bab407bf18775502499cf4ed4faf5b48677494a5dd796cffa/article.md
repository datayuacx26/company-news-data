# Tourist Payment Methods for Singapore Businesses (2026)

**Quick Answer:** Singapore merchants can accept payments from tourists and overseas customers using their home-country apps — including WeChat Pay (China), UPI (India), PromptPay and TrueMoney (Thailand), DuitNow (Malaysia), QRIS (Indonesia), QR Ph (Philippines), and KakaoPay/PayCo/LINE Pay (South Korea) — with no currency exchange required at the point of sale. HitPay, licensed by the[Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS)](https://www.mas.gov.sg/) (licence PS20200643), supports all of these cross-border methods for Singapore merchants alongside 50+ local and international payment options, with domestic payouts settling next business day in SGD and cross-border transactions settling next business day in SGD.


Singapore’s retail, food and beverage, and attractions sectors serve a genuinely international customer base every day. A souvenir shop in Bugis, a massage studio in Tanjong Pagar, or a tour operator near Orchard Road may see customers from China, India, Thailand, Malaysia, and Indonesia within a single afternoon. ## How does cross-border payment acceptance actually work at the counter?


The mechanics are simpler than most merchants assume. A single QR code displayed at the counter — or generated via a payment terminal — can be scanned by multiple different apps from different countries. The customer’s app handles the currency conversion on their side. The merchant receives SGD at the agreed exchange rate, with no manual conversion step.


Settlement works as follows:
- **Domestic transactions** (PayNow, GrabPay, ShopeePay, local cards): next business day in SGD
- **Cross-border transactions** (WeChat Pay, UPI, PromptPay, QRIS, DuitNow, QR Ph, Korean wallets): next business day in SGD


Activation timelines vary. Most cross-border methods require 3–5 business days for partner provider processing after submission. PromptPay, TrueMoney, Rabbit LINE Pay, DuitNow, and Korean wallets activate instantly in some configurations — check with your payment provider at setup.

Is Alipay available for Singapore merchants?


Alipay+ is not available in Singapore. Singapore merchants cannot activate Alipay or Alipay+ through any MAS-licensed payment gateway operating in this market. Chinese tourists without WeChat Pay would need to use a card as an alternative.
