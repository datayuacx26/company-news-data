# HitPay vs Airwallex Philippines: Which Is Better for SMEs?

**Quick Answer:** For Philippine SMEs that need to accept GCash, Maya, QR Ph, InstaPay, PESONet, and over-the-counter payments with no monthly fees and next business day payouts in PHP, HitPay is the stronger fit. Airwallex is licensed and operational in the Philippines but is positioned primarily as a global treasury and FX platform rather than a local payment acceptance solution. HitPay supports 50+ payment methods across Singapore, Malaysia, and the Philippines, with free sign-up and approval in 1–3 business days.


Philippine e-commerce and retail are growing rapidly, with digital payments now central to how consumers in Makati, BGC, Cebu, and Davao pay for goods and services. The[Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/) (BSP) has set a target for at least 50% of retail payments to be done digitally — a policy direction that has accelerated merchant adoption of QR Ph, GCash, and InstaPay. Choosing the right payment gateway shapes cash flow timing, checkout conversion, and operational overhead from day one.


## What payment methods do HitPay and Airwallex support in the Philippines?


This is where the two platforms diverge most sharply for local merchants.


HitPay supports the full stack of Philippine payment methods: GCash, Maya, QR Ph, ShopeePay, SPayLater, GrabPay, BillEase, UnionBank Online, InstaPay, PESONet, Visa, Mastercard, and over-the-counter options via Bayad, ECPay, and Palawan. Merchants running a boutique in BGC or a food business in Quezon City can accept payments from virtually every customer segment — banked, underbanked, and those who pay via convenience stores.


HitPay also enables cross-border e-wallet acceptance, so Philippine merchants can receive payments from international visitors using PayNow (Singapore), QRIS (Indonesia), PromptPay (Thailand), TrueMoney (Thailand), LINE Pay (Thailand), DuitNow (Malaysia), and KakaoPay, PayCo, and LINE Pay (South Korea). Cross-border method activation takes 3–5 business days after submission.


Airwallex’s Philippines offering centres on global card acceptance and multi-currency accounts. Its checkout product supports cards and select wallets globally, but its local Philippine e-wallet coverage — GCash, Maya, QR Ph — is not a published core feature of its merchant acceptance product. Airwallex’s strength is in FX transfers, multi-currency corporate cards, and treasury management for businesses with cross-border payment flows.


For a Cebu-based retailer whose customers primarily pay via GCash or QR Ph, the difference in local coverage is material.

## What are the payout timelines for Philippine merchants?


HitPay settles domestic PHP transactions on the next business day. Cross-border transactions (e.g. a Singapore customer paying via PayNow at a Manila merchant’s checkout) settle at T+2. This predictable cash flow timeline is significant for SMEs managing inventory or supplier payments.


Airwallex offers multi-currency account holding and global payouts, which can be valuable for businesses with overseas suppliers or international revenue. However, its settlement timeline for domestic PHP merchant transactions is not published with the same specificity. Businesses that primarily collect from local Filipino customers benefit more from a gateway with transparent, fast domestic settlement.


HitPay’s[payment gateway for the Philippines](https://hitpayapp.com/blog/payment-gateway-philippines) is built around next business day PHP payouts as a baseline, not a premium feature.


## Which platform is better for Philippine SME operations?


[Xendit](https://www.xendit.co/en-ph/) and[Fiuu](https://fiuu.com/) are two other gateways Philippine merchants evaluate — both with local e-wallet support — but the comparison between HitPay and Airwallex is most telling because the platforms serve fundamentally different use cases.


Airwallex is built for businesses managing treasury across multiple currencies, paying overseas suppliers, or issuing multi-currency corporate cards to a distributed team. It is a financial operations platform first and a payment acceptance tool second.


HitPay is built for merchants who need to accept money from Filipino customers — online, in-person, or via payment links — across the widest possible set of local methods. It is PCI DSS compliant, MAS-licensed (PS20200643), and registered with the BSP as an Operator of Payment System. Free to sign up, with approval in 1–3 business days.


For[accepting GCash and QR Ph payments at a brick-and-mortar store](https://hitpayapp.com/blog/hitpay-philippines-scan-to-pay) , HitPay’s Scan to Pay feature allows merchants to generate a QR code from the mobile app and accept payments instantly — no additional hardware required.
