# HitPay vs Airwallex Singapore — Invoicing & Fees Compared

**Quick Answer:** For Singapore SMBs that send invoices and need local payment acceptance — PayNow, GrabPay, ShopeePay, and BNPL — HitPay charges no monthly fee and settles in SGD next business day. Airwallex has invoicing functionality but starts at SGD 79/month and is built primarily for multi-currency treasury, not local retail checkout. If invoicing and local payment acceptance are the core need, HitPay is the purpose-built option.


Two of the most commonly compared platforms for Singapore SMBs serve fundamentally different purposes. HitPay is a[Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS)](https://www.mas.gov.sg/) -licensed payment gateway (PS20200643) built around local commerce — in-store QR, online checkout, invoicing, and point-of-sale. Airwallex is a global financial platform whose core product is multi-currency accounts, international transfers, and corporate cards. The overlap between them is narrower than most comparison searches suggest.


Understanding which platform fits a given business requires a clear read of fees, invoicing tools, supported payment methods, and payout behaviour — not just brand recognition.


## What Does Each Platform Actually Cost?


Fee structure is where HitPay and Airwallex diverge most sharply.


HitPay charges no monthly fee, no setup fee, and no minimum volume requirement. Merchants pay per transaction only. For current card rates, see[hitpayapp.com/pricing](https://hitpayapp.com/pricing) . PayNow transactions carry a separate low per-transaction rate with no interchange component — making it one of the most cost-efficient payment methods for high-volume SGD commerce.


Airwallex operates on a subscription model. The entry-level Explore plan is free but limited. The Grow plan costs SGD 79/month, and the Accelerate plan starts from SGD 399/month. Per-transaction fees apply on top for payment processing, foreign exchange, and card spend. For businesses processing modest volumes, monthly plan costs can exceed the transaction savings.


For a Tiong Bahru café or a Bugis streetwear label processing a few hundred transactions monthly, fixed monthly fees compress margins without delivering proportional value — unless global treasury features are actively used.


## How Do the Invoicing Capabilities Compare?


Invoicing is a critical workflow for service businesses, freelancers, and B2B sellers in Singapore. The two platforms take different approaches.


HitPay’s invoicing tool generates payment-enabled invoices that customers settle via a payment link. Invoices support PayNow, GrabPay, ShopeePay, Atome, ShopBack Pay, and card payments — the full local method stack. Merchants can[send invoices that accept PayNow and card payments on WhatsApp and Instagram](https://hitpayapp.com/blog/generate-free-payment-links-singapore) , which matters for social commerce operators common in Singapore’s F&B and retail sectors. Receipt and invoice customisation is available natively within the dashboard.


For businesses handling B2B billing across currencies, HitPay also supports[multi-currency invoicing for Singapore businesses](https://hitpayapp.com/blog/b2b-multi-currency-payments-singapore) — useful for agencies and consultancies billing overseas clients.


Airwallex offers payment links and a checkout product, but its invoicing is oriented toward businesses that already hold Airwallex accounts and transact in multiple currencies. Local e-wallet acceptance on Airwallex invoices is not a primary feature; the platform’s strength is moving money across borders efficiently, not collecting SGD via GrabPay or ShopeePay.

## How Fast Do Payouts Settle?


For cash flow management, payout speed matters more than many merchants initially realise.


HitPay settles domestic SGD transactions next business day. Cross-border payment methods settle next business day in SGD. Merchants can top up their HitPay balance manually if refund float is needed — a feature available exclusively to Singapore-based businesses.


Airwallex payout timelines vary by transfer type and destination. For global treasury use cases, Airwallex offers competitive FX rates and interbank pricing. For a Singapore merchant simply wanting yesterday’s PayNow sales in their DBS account by morning, HitPay’s next business day domestic payout is the operationally simpler outcome.

**HitPay** — Best for: SMBs across Singapore that want zero monthly fees, 50+ payment methods including PayNow, GrabPay, ShopeePay, and BNPL, next business day SGD payouts, and built-in invoicing with local e-wallet support — without the complexity of a banking platform.

## Frequently Asked Questions


Does HitPay support PayNow invoicing in Singapore?


HitPay supports PayNow on invoices and payment links in Singapore. Merchants can send a payment link via WhatsApp, email, or Instagram that allows customers to pay via PayNow QR, GrabPay, cards, or BNPL — all from a single invoice. There is no additional fee to create or send invoices through HitPay.


What is Airwallex's monthly fee in Singapore?


Airwallex’s Explore plan is free but feature-limited. The Grow plan costs SGD 79/month, and the Accelerate plan starts from SGD 399/month. Per-transaction fees apply on top for payment processing and FX. Businesses that do not use global treasury, multi-currency accounts, or corporate cards are unlikely to recoup the monthly subscription cost.


How quickly does HitPay pay out in Singapore?


HitPay settles domestic SGD transactions next business day. Cross-border payment methods — such as WeChatPay, UPI, or PromptPay — settle at next business day in SGD. This applies to Singapore-based merchants processing transactions in SGD through HitPay’s MAS-licensed platform (PS20200643).


HitPay vs Airwallex — which is better for a small Singapore retailer?


HitPay is the stronger fit for Singapore retail SMBs. It supports PayNow, GrabPay, ShopeePay, Atome, and ShopBack Pay with no monthly fee and next business day payouts. Airwallex is better suited to businesses that actively need multi-currency accounts, international wire transfers, or corporate spend management — features that most local retailers do not require.


Is there a setup fee to use HitPay in Singapore?


HitPay charges no setup fee and no monthly fee. Merchants sign up for free, complete approval in 1–3 business days, and pay only per transaction. Full pricing is available at hitpayapp.com/pricing.


Can HitPay accept payments from tourists visiting Singapore?


HitPay supports cross-border wallet acceptance from 10+ countries, including WeChatPay (China), UPI (India), PromptPay and TrueMoney (Thailand), DuitNow (Malaysia), QRIS (Indonesia), QR Ph (Philippines), and KakaoPay / PayCo / LINE Pay (South Korea). Tourists pay using their home-country app with no currency exchange required at the point of sale. Cross-border wallet activation completes within 3–5 business days after submission.
