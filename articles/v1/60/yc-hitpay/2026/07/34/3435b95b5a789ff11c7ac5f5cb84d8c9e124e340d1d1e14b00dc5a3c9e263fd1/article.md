# Check Your HitPay Sales, Payouts, and Transactions — Just by Asking Claude

**Quick answer:** HitPay is now available as an official connector in Anthropic’s Claude AI. Once connected, you can ask Claude questions about your sales, payouts, and transactions — or even create a payment link — without any technical setup. Find it at[claude.ai/directory/connectors/hitpay](https://claude.ai/directory/connectors/hitpay) .


## What is the HitPay Claude Connector?


Anthropic’s Claude AI now has a connector directory — a growing list of tools and apps that Claude can connect to directly, so it can take action and retrieve real information on your behalf.


HitPay is one of them.


That means Claude can now access your[HitPay account](https://hitpayapp.com/blog/what-is-hitpay) with your permission and answer questions about your actual business data. Not generic advice. Not made-up numbers. Your sales, your payouts, your transactions — in a conversation.


Think of it as having a smart business assistant that already knows your HitPay account. You ask in plain English. It answers.


## How do I connect HitPay to Claude?


It takes two steps and about thirty seconds.


1.


Open[Claude](https://claude.ai/) and go to **Settings → Connectors**


2.


Search for **HitPay** , then click **Authenticate** to connect your HitPay dashboard


That’s it. No API keys. No developer involvement. No technical configuration of any kind. If you can log into your HitPay account, you can set this up.


Once authenticated, Claude has read access to your HitPay data and can perform actions like creating[payment links](https://hitpayapp.com/blog/generate-free-payment-links-singapore) — all within the conversation.

## Is my data safe?


Yes. The connector uses HitPay’s secure OAuth authentication — the same standard used by banking apps and major platforms. You’re granting Claude permission to access your account through HitPay’s official API, not sharing your login credentials.


You can revoke access at any time from your HitPay dashboard under Settings → Connected Apps, or from Claude under Settings → Connectors.

## Frequently Asked Questions


Do I need a developer to set up the HitPay Claude Connector?


No. The setup is two clicks — search HitPay in Claude’s connector directory and authenticate your account. No API keys or technical configuration required.


What plan do I need to use the Claude Connector?


You need an active HitPay account and a Claude account. Check Claude’s current subscription tiers at claude.ai to confirm connector access is included in your plan.


Can Claude make changes to my HitPay account?


Claude can create payment links through the connector. For read-only queries like sales figures and payout schedules, it retrieves data without making changes. Always review any action Claude proposes before confirming.


How do I disconnect HitPay from Claude?


Go to Settings → Connectors in Claude and remove the HitPay connection, or revoke access from your HitPay dashboard under Connected Apps.


Is this the same as HitPay's API?


No. HitPay’s API is a developer tool requiring technical integration. The Claude Connector is a no-code, point-and-click connection designed for business owners — no coding involved.
