## What does a Philippine business actually need from a remittance or payment collection solution?


The term “remittance solution” covers two distinct use cases for businesses:


1.


**Inbound collection** — receiving payments from customers domestically (GCash, Maya, InstaPay, cards, QR Ph) and internationally (PayNow from Singapore, QRIS from Indonesia, PromptPay from Thailand).


2.


**Outbound disbursement** — paying suppliers, staff, or partners via InstaPay or PESONet rails.


Most Philippine SMEs need both. A Cebu-based export retailer collects from local buyers via GCash and from Singapore tourists via PayNow — then disburses supplier payments via InstaPay. A single platform that covers all three rails removes the reconciliation cost of running separate providers.


## What local payment methods must a Philippine SME support?


The non-negotiable domestic rails for any Philippine payment solution are:


-


**GCash and Maya** — the two dominant e-wallets, covering the majority of cashless consumer transactions


-


**QR Ph** — the[Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/) -mandated interoperable QR standard, accepted across all major banks and wallets


-


**InstaPay** — real-time bank transfers, ideal for B2C and B2B collections up to ₱50,000 per transaction


-


**PESONet** — batch bank transfers for larger B2B settlements


-


**Cards (Visa, Mastercard)** — essential for online checkout and in-person retail


-


**Over-the-counter (Bayad, ECPay, Palawan)** — critical for reaching unbanked customers outside Metro Manila


-


**GrabPay, ShopeePay** — growing wallet share among younger urban consumers


-


**BillEase, SPayLater** — buy now, pay later options that increase average order value for retail and lifestyle merchants


For a deeper look at how QR Ph fits into a physical store setup, the[guide to generating QR Ph for your business](https://hitpayapp.com/blog/how-to-generate-qrph) covers the BSP requirements and activation steps in detail.
