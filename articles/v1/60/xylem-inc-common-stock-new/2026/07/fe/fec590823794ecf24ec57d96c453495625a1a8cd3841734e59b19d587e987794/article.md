# MultiLab® Pro IDS Finalist for Best of Industry Award 2025


*Xylem Analytics' innovative multi-parameter instrument recognized among top three in Laboratory & Analytical Technology category*


**Weilheim, Germany – December 2025** – Xylem Analytics is proud to announce that the MultiLab® Pro IDS has been selected as a finalist for the prestigious Best of Industry Award 2025. Our innovation " **Simultaneous measurement of up to four parameters – Efficiency at the highest level** " impressed users in the online voting and secured a spot among the top three finalists in the " **Best of Laboratory & Medicine - Laboratory & Analytical Technology** " category.


The Best of Industry Award recognizes outstanding innovations across the industrial sector. After a five-month voting phase that attracted more than 25,000 votes across 19 categories, the finalists have now been determined based on user preference and industry recognition.


#### Innovation that Convinces


The MultiLab® Pro IDS stands out through its ability to simultaneously measure up to four parameters, delivering unparalleled efficiency in laboratory workflows. This innovation addresses the growing demand for streamlined analytical processes while maintaining the highest standards of accuracy and reliability.


#### Award Ceremony in January


The winner of the coveted award will be announced at a ceremonial gala on January 22, 2026 at the Vogel Convention Center in Würzburg, Germany. Xylem Analytics is honored to be among the finalists and looks forward to this recognition of our commitment to innovation and excellence in laboratory technology.
