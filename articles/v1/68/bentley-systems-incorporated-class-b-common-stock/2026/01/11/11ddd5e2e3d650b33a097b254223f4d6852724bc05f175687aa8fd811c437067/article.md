## Bentley Systems to Announce Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Results and 2026 Financial Outlook on February 26, 2026


EXTON, Pa.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jan. 27, 2026--[Bentley Systems, Incorporated](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.bentley.com%2F&esheet=54405464&newsitemid=20260127872716&lan=en-US&anchor=Bentley+Systems%2C+Incorporated&index=1&md5=d1da25c971339f0f8de301f4f4890261) (Nasdaq: BSY), the infrastructure engineering software company, will release its fourth quarter and full year 2025 results and 2026 financial outlook before the market opens on Thursday, February 26, 2026. **About Bentley Systems** Around the world, infrastructure professionals rely on software from Bentley Systems to help them design, build, and operate better and more resilient infrastructure for transportation, water, energy, cities, and more.
