# From raw data to 3D models – Mastering drillhole and surface mapping with Target Essentials software

This hands-on, on-demand webinar introduces workflows in **[Target Essentials software](https://www.seequent.com/products-solutions/geosoft-target/)** for geoscientists working with drillhole and surface data. You will learn how to import and organize information from over 50 formats, including CAD, GIS, and mine planning software, and apply QA/QC processes to ensure accuracy while calculating economic composites to support reliable decision-making.


The session will also demonstrate how to create plan maps, cross sections, and 3D visualizations, as well as build geological models such as voxels, block models, and isosurfaces. In addition, you will gain practical skills in planning drill holes in both 2D and 3D environments.


**You’ll discover:**


- A deeper understanding of the capabilities and features within Target.


- How to build fundamental skills for surface mapping and plotting geoscience data in Target.


- Ways to integrate your exploration data through import, organization, validation, integrated mapping and analysis, and section mapping and interpretation.
