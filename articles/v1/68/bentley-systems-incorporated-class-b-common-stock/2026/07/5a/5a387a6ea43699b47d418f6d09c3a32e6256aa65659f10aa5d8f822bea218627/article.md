# From core images to RQD in minutes: A look at Imago Image Analysis

In this short, practical 30-minute on-demand webinar, you’ll see how geoscientists can move from core tray images to quantitative RQD and colour data quickly, consistently, and all inside **[Imago](https://www.seequent.com/products-solutions/imago/)** .


Imago Image Analysis brings automated measurement directly into your existing image workflow. You’ll learn how Image Analysis turns core images (new or historical) into trusted, repeatable datasets that feed straight into downstream geology and QAQC processes.


**You’ll discover:**


- **Master Image Analysis:** Learn how to generate consistent RQD and Colour data from your core images.


- **Validate with confidence:** Understand where validation and control fit into the workflow to ensure data you can trust.


- **Visualize your results:** See how your results are visualized within the Imago platform
