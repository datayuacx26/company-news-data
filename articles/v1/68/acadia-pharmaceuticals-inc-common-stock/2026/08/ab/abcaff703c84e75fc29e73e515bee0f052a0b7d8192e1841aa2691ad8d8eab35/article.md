# Learning, Growing, and Finding My Place in Biopharma

**1. What does a commercial project manager in biopharma do?**


It’s a cross-functional role that connects teams, supports product launches, and requires working knowledge of IT systems, regulatory processes, and field operations. It helps to start with something you know, but once you build project management skills, you can apply them across different areas.


**2. Why did you pursue an MBA while working full-time?**


I wanted to learn the business side: strategy, finance, and broader decision-making. That knowledge complements my technical background and allows me to continue growing.


**3. How does Acadia support career development?**


Acadia supports continuous learning through mentorship, professional development opportunities, and benefits such as tuition reimbursement. I’ve been empowered to ask questions, learn new skills, and expand their expertise throughout their careers.


**4. What is Acadia's workplace culture like?**


It’s open, collaborative, and supportive. Leaders are approachable, colleagues are willing to share their knowledge, and employees are encouraged to keep learning and developing.


**5. What advice would you give someone considering a career at biotech or biopharma project management?**


Start in a familiar space, build your core skills, stay open to learning, and pay attention to company culture. It matters more than you think.
