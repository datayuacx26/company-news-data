# Infisical Raises $16M Series A Led by Elad Gil

Some of the most important parts of modern software are the ones you never see — the invisible infrastructure everything else depends on.


Secrets management is a prime example of this. API keys, database credentials, and certificates serve as the glue between all the services, systems, and engineers – keeping things connected and secure. When a secret leaks, expires, or disappears, things break — often in ways that are hard to trace and costly to fix.


We started[Infisical](https://infisical.com/) with a simple belief: secrets are foundational to software — but the way they were managed didn’t reflect that. Managing secrets meant stitching together workflows that were neither secure nor scalable. The existing tools were brittle, heavy, and never built with developers in mind.


### Where we are now


Infisical changed that. We believed there had to be a better way — one that was both secure and easy to use. A solution that stayed out of the way, until you needed it.


Over the last few years, Infisical has grown and exceeded our expectations in ways we never imagined. We went from a simple open source project to securing workloads for Fortune 50 enterprises — across finance, healthcare, AI infrastructure, manufacturing, and the public sector. Secrets management has evolved from something esoteric into something any team can use with ease. And Infisical is becoming the default way engineering organizations manage those secrets.


### Where we are going


Today, we are excited to announce that **Infisical raised a $16M Series A, led by one of the world’s best investors Elad Gil** , with participation from Y Combinator, Gradient Ventures (Google), Dynamic Fund, and some of the world’s best founders — including CEOs of Datadog, Samsara, and Valor.


We didn’t have to raise this round. In fact, Infisical has been cash-flow positive for a long time, and we have more money available than we have ever raised.


But the opportunity keeps getting bigger. Secrets management is just the beginning. Over the past year, we launched Infisical PKI, SSH, KMS, and more. We’re building a complete, open-source security stack for secrets, identity, and access – one that’s designed to support companies in the AI era and beyond.


You can learn more about Infisical by getting a[demo of the product](https://infisical.com/talk-to-us) or by[trying it yourself](https://app.infisical.com/signup) .


Lastly, we are incredibly grateful for our customers and the community. We know that so much of where we are today is because of your support and trust. Thank you and we’re looking forward to continue building for you.
