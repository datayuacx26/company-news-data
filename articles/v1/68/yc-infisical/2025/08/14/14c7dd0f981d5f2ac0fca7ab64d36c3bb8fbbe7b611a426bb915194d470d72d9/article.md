# What to Expect at HashiConf 2025

## Top 5 Tips for Making the Most out of HashiConf


HashiConf 2025 is just around the corner. To help you prepare, we’ve put together some top tips for getting the most out of the conference.


### 1. The Basics — When & Where


HashiConf is happening in San Francisco from September 24–26, at the Fort Mason Center — a waterfront venue in the Marina District.
We recommend arriving in SF a day early to settle in and start the conference feeling ready.


### 2. Decide Which Track Is for You


HashiConf features both Cloud Engineering & Business tracks.


Whether you want deep technical dives on Terraform, Vault, & Nomad or higher-level strategy regarding cloud ROI, developer experience, & risk mitigation, the conference has what you’re looking for.


### 3. Don’t Miss the Chance to Meet Other Folks


Make time to chat with speakers, connect with fellow attendees, and meet teams from across the industry — it’s the best part of the conference. Pro Tip: Come to the happy hour mentioned below!


### 4. Big Themes to Look Out For


HashiConf 2025 builds on last year’s focus, with Infrastructure & Security Lifecycle Management still at the core, plus a growing emphasis on Enterprise Cloud Automation.


Expect plenty of discussion about:


- Multi-cloud setups
- AI in infrastructure workflows
- Insights from teams running this in production


### 5. Take Some Time to Explore SF — A Few Ideas from Locals


- Infisical Happy Hour — 6:30 pm on Thursday, September 25th near Fort Mason. Come join us for good food & conversation.
[RSVP here.](https://lu.ma/kq0v7u47)
- Rent a bike and cruise along the waterfront for views of the Golden Gate Bridge.
- Explore SF’s historic Italian neighborhood, perfect for a scenic walk and some delicious pasta or gelato.
- Enjoy a burrito at Mission Dolores Park overlooking the whole city.
