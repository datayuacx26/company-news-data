# Infisical Launches on AWS Marketplace

Today, we are excited to publicly announce the launch of Infisical Secret Management platform on AWS Marketplace, a digital catalog with thousands of software listings from independent software vendors that make it easy to find, test, buy, and deploy software that runs on Amazon Web Services (AWS).


At Infisical, we are excited to build upon our already strong relationship with AWS and work together further to help meet the business and technology needs of our customers.


By making Infisical available through a private offer in AWS Marketplace, we are able to reach more customers and are better equipped to help organizations efficiently and securely manage their infrastructure credentials. This streamlined purchasing option of Infisical can help AWS customers consolidate their technology purchasing and billing of Infisical solutions, discover partners that can facilitate deployment, and maximize their usage of AWS committed resources.


To access Infisical in AWS Marketplace, please visit[Infisical's AWS listing](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-fuedwvalknaiy?sr=0-1&applicationId=AWSMPContessa) orreach out to our sales team .
