# Infisical 2024 Wrapped – Our Biggest Year Yet

As we wrap up an incredible year at Infisical, we’ve taken a moment to reflect on our biggest product milestones in 2024. From launching 25+ major features to strengthening our global presence, this year has been pivotal on our path to redefining secrets management.


## What Infisical Year Looked Like


### January: New Year, New Backend


Started the year by migrating to Postgres and making our Node.js SDK 90% smaller. Plus, we added secret fallback support to all SDKs and launched our .NET SDK.


### February: Love for Enterprise


[SAML](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/sso/overview) got better with org-scoped authentication enforcement,[SCIM](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/scim/overview#scim-overview) support landed for Okta, Azure, and JumpCloud, and our[Terraform provider](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/Infisical/infisical/latest/docs) got some serious upgrades.


### March: Dynamic Secrets


[Dynamic Secrets](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/dynamic-secrets/overview#dynamic-secrets) made their debut, along with[Additional Privileges](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/access-controls/additional-privileges) – because sometimes roles need a little extra help.


### April: Spring Cleaning Your Access Controls


We introduced[Access Requests](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/access-controls/access-requests#access-requests) and[Temporary Access](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/access-controls/temporary-access#temporary-access) provisioning – making secrets management more self-serve and automated.


### May: May the Force Be With Your Secrets


Cloud native auth methods? Check ([AWS](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/identities/aws-auth) ,[GCP](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/identities/gcp-auth#gcp-auth) ,[Azure](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/identities/azure-auth) ,[Kubernetes](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/identities/kubernetes-auth) ).[Secret sharing](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/secret-sharing#secret-sharing) with encrypted links? You got it. New[Jenkins plugin](https://infisical.com/docs/integrations/cicd/jenkins#jenkins-plugin) ? Absolutely. Plus, we hit[AWS Marketplace](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-fuedwvalknaiy?sr=0-1&applicationId=AWSMPContessa) – making it easier than ever to get started with Infisical.


### June: Summer of Innovation


[Infisical PKI](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/pki/overview#internal-pki) and[OIDC Authentication](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/sso/general-oidc#general-oidc) made their grand entrance, alongside our new[Go SDK](https://infisical.com/docs/sdks/languages/go#infisical-go-sdk) . We also launched our public secret sharing tool –[try it out](https://app.infisical.com/share-secret) !


### July: Independence from Complexity


We released the official[Ruby SDK](https://infisical.com/docs/sdks/languages/ruby) , AWS KMS wrapping (BYOK style), and CLI users got SSO login in non-browser environments. Plus, we launched[Slack Webhooks](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/webhooks) .


### August: Hot Summer Updates


[Azure DevOps integration](https://infisical.com/docs/integrations/cloud/azure-devops) landed, and we gave CLI users the gift of hot-reload variables ([--watch flag](https://infisical.com/docs/cli/commands/run#infisical-run:watch) ). Plus, we made certificate management less stressful with new[alerting features](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/pki/alerting) . Oh, and[Redis](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/dynamic-secrets/redis) joined our dynamic secrets family!


### September: Secret Sauce Gets Spicier


Remember when we made your secrets management even smoother with[Slack integration for access requests](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/workflow-integrations/slack-integration#slack-integration) ? We also launched our[PKI Issuer for Kubernetes](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/pki/pki-issuer#concept) and added support for[older .NET versions](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Infisical.Sdk#supportedframeworks-body-tab) – nobody gets left behind!


### October: Going Global!


We launched[Infisical EU Cloud](https://eu.infisical.com/) , giving our European users their own dedicated infrastructure. Plus, we rolled out organization-wide 2FA and dropped some serious heat with our[Databricks integration](https://infisical.com/docs/integrations/cloud/databricks) and[Infisical KMS](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/kms/overview) .


### November: Reliability, Speed, and Stability


November brought huge reliability, speed, and stability improvements to the whole platform. We also released Dynamic Secrets for[Snowflake](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/dynamic-secrets/snowflake) and many other databases.


### December: A New Product Line


We wrapped up 2024 by releasing[Infisical SSH](https://infisical.com/docs/documentation/platform/ssh#infisical-ssh) to help our users generate SSH credentials and provide secure, centralized SSH access control for infrastructure.


## What's Next?


2024 was just the beginning! Stay tuned for even more exciting features and improvements in 2025. We've got some amazing things planned that we can't wait to share with you. None of this would be possible without our amazing community. Your feedback, support, and trust drive us to keep pushing the boundaries of what's possible in secrets management.


Thank You and Best Wishes! 🙏
