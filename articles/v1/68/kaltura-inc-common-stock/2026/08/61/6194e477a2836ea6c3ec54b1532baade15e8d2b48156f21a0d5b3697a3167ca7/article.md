# Kaltura vs HeyGen (2026): Which AI avatar platform fits your business?

Kaltura sits at the platform end of the spectrum; HeyGen at the production-tool end. Most “which is better” confusion comes from comparing them on the same axis when they’re built for different jobs.


## **What is Kaltura?**


Most organizations don’t struggle to launch an avatar. They struggle to connect that avatar to the systems, content, workflows, and governance requirements that make it useful.


Kaltura (Nasdaq: KLTR) repositioned in 2026 as an “agentic digital experience” company. Its avatar product, Agentic Avatars, became generally available in March 2026 and is built on technology from its acquisition of eSelf.ai.


The defining trait: these aren’t just talking heads. Kaltura’s avatars analyze speech and context in real time, can see a user’s screen and camera, respond with multimodal output, and are grounded in your organization’s knowledge and systems. They run on Kaltura’s video infrastructure and plug into its broader products: video portals, webinars, virtual events, and its Genie agents.


In practice, that means an avatar can advise a prospective student, guide a buyer, run sales or compliance roleplay, or support a customer, while staying inside enterprise governance and compliance guardrails.


Kaltura also ships an Avatar Video Production Studio (for turning enterprise knowledge into avatar-led videos at scale) and an Avatar SDK for developers who want to embed avatars into their own apps.


## **What is HeyGen?**


HeyGen is one of the most popular AI avatar video generators on the market, recognized by G2 as a top fastest-growing product and rated highly across thousands of reviews. Its reputation is built on speed and avatar realism.


The core workflow: write a script, pick or clone an avatar, and HeyGen generates a polished video in minutes. It supports 175+ languages, voice cloning, and AI video translation, which makes it a go-to for marketing clips, product explainers, and localized training content.


HeyGen also offers LiveAvatar, a real-time streaming avatar API using WebRTC for two-way, low-latency conversations. This is important: HeyGen is not async-only. Developers connect LiveAvatar to an LLM like ChatGPT (or a custom model) to build virtual agents, tutors, or support reps. The difference is that you assemble that experience yourself.


## **Real-time Avatars: Where Kaltura and HeyGen actually differ**


This is the section most comparison articles get wrong. They claim HeyGen only does pre-recorded video. Not true in 2026.


Both platforms do real-time, two-way avatars. The real difference is how much is done for you.


With HeyGen LiveAvatar, you get the avatar layer and a streaming API. You bring the brain (your LLM), the knowledge (your data/RAG setup), the orchestration logic, and the integration work. It’s powerful and flexible, but it’s a build-it-yourself developer product.


With Kaltura Agentic Avatars, the avatar arrives already connected to organizational knowledge, intent-based orchestration, screen and camera comprehension, and the surrounding experience stack (portals, webinars, events), wrapped in enterprise governance. It’s a managed experience, not a toolkit.


Rule of thumb:


**HeyGen gives engineers a face to build on. Kaltura gives an enterprise a working agentic experience.**


## **Pricing**


HeyGen has transparent, public pricing:


- Free: $0, 3 videos/month, watermark, 720p


- Creator: about $29/month ($24 billed annually)


- Pro: about $99/month


- Business: about $149/month plus per-seat, adds 4K, custom avatars, SSO


- Enterprise: custom


Note the credit system underneath: premium Avatar IV video consumes roughly 20 credits per minute, so the sticker price understates real cost at volume. The API is a separate pay-as-you-go pool starting around a $5 minimum, billed per second of generated video (roughly $3/minute), independent of your web plan.


Kaltura does not publish self-serve pricing. It’s a sales-led, custom enterprise model. That reflects the buyer: organizations purchasing a governed platform with onboarding, integration, and support, not a monthly seat.


Takeaway: HeyGen wins on transparency and low entry cost. Kaltura is priced as a platform investment, so compare total value (integration, governance, support) rather than a monthly number.


## **Feature breakdown**


**Avatars and realism:** HeyGen is widely praised for avatar quality and easy custom avatar creation from a short video. Kaltura’s avatars prioritize live interaction, expressiveness, and grounding over sheer cosmetic polish.


**Languages:** HeyGen leads for raw video generation (175+ languages). Kaltura’s agentic avatars cover 30+ languages, focused on conversation rather than mass localization.


**Guidance, not just generation:** Kaltura’s avatars don’t just present information. They help users navigate decisions, answer questions, and move toward outcomes. HeyGen leaves grounding and logic to your own stack.


**Integrations:** Kaltura ties into its full video experience cloud (webinars, events, portals, LMS-style learning). HeyGen integrates via API and partners (ChatGPT, Replicate, n8n, and similar).


**Governance:** Kaltura builds enterprise security, compliance, and analytics into the platform. HeyGen offers SSO and enterprise controls on higher tiers.


## **Use cases: Who each is built for**


**If the deliverable is a video, lean HeyGen:** marketing and social clips, product explainers, localized training modules, UGC-style ads, or a developer prototype of a conversational agent.


**If the deliverable is an experience, choose Kaltura:** an avatar advisor on your site, avatar-led sales or compliance roleplay, student onboarding and tutoring, town halls and webinars, or a customer-support avatar grounded in your knowledge base, all under enterprise governance.


A marketing team of three producing weekly localized videos leans HeyGen. A 2,000-person enterprise rolling out an interactive avatar across support, learning, and events leans Kaltura.


## **How to choose**


Ask four questions:


1. Are you trying to create content or create interaction? Files lean HeyGen. Experiences lean Kaltura.


2. Do you have engineers to assemble real-time avatars? If yes, HeyGen LiveAvatar is viable. If no, Kaltura’s managed approach saves months.


3. How important is governance and grounding? Regulated or large enterprises lean Kaltura.


4. What’s your budget model? Predictable monthly tooling leans HeyGen. Platform investment with ROI horizon leans Kaltura.


## **Common mistakes**


**Assuming HeyGen is only pre-recorded:** LiveAvatar does real-time, two-way conversation. The distinction is managed platform vs build-your-own.


**Comparing only the sticker price:** HeyGen’s credit system and separate API billing can multiply real cost at scale. Kaltura’s custom pricing should be weighed against integration and governance value.


**Treating them as direct substitutes:** A video tool and an enterprise experience platform solve different problems. The wrong choice isn’t “more expensive,” it’s “doesn’t fit the job.”


**Assuming realism is the same thing as usefulness:** For many enterprise use cases, grounding, governance, orchestration, and integration matter more than visual fidelity alone.

## **FAQ**


**What is the main difference between Kaltura and HeyGen?**


Kaltura is an enterprise agentic avatar and video experience platform with avatars grounded in your organization’s knowledge. HeyGen is an AI avatar video generation tool with a real-time API. Kaltura is a managed platform; HeyGen is a production tool plus developer building blocks.


**Does HeyGen offer real-time interactive avatars?**


Yes. HeyGen’s LiveAvatar uses WebRTC for real-time, two-way conversation. You connect it to your own LLM and data. It’s a developer product, so you build the experience yourself.


**Is Kaltura more expensive than HeyGen?**


Kaltura uses custom enterprise pricing, while HeyGen starts free and publishes monthly tiers. HeyGen is cheaper to start, but Kaltura is priced as a full platform with integration, governance, and support included.


**Which is better for enterprise training and L&D?**


Kaltura suits organizations wanting governed, integrated avatar-led learning and roleplay across their video stack. HeyGen suits teams producing scripted training videos quickly, especially across many languages.


**Which has more languages?**


HeyGen supports 175+ languages for video generation. Kaltura’s agentic avatars support 30+ languages, focused on live conversation rather than mass localization.


**Can I create custom avatars in both?**


Yes. HeyGen lets you create a custom avatar from a short video clip. Kaltura supports avatar creation through its Avatar Video Production Studio and SDK for enterprise deployments.


**Which should I choose for a website support agent?**


If you want a managed, governed, knowledge-grounded avatar live quickly, choose Kaltura. If you have developers who want to build a custom agent and control every layer, HeyGen LiveAvatar is a strong fit.
