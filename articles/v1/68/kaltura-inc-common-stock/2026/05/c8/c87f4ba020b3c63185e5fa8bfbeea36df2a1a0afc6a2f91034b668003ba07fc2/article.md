# Top 9 ON24 competitors to know in 2026

## What is ON24?


ON24 is a digital, virtual events platform recognized for supporting webinars well. The platform would likely be relevant to mid-to-large-sized businesses or organizations that have small event teams and tend to produce a lot of webinar content. It is specifically geared towards smaller, more intimate online gatherings. Top On24 competitors include Kaltura, SpotMe, and Zoom Webinars.


## ON24 features


ON24 includes a range of tools designed to help teams create, manage, and measure webinar-based events while keeping audience engagement high:


- **Customizable webinar environments** : Branded webinar consoles that allow organizations to design layouts, add logos, and tailor the attendee experience.


- **Audience engagement tools** : Interactive elements such as live polls, surveys, Q&A, chat, and downloadable resources to keep participants involved.


- **On-demand content hosting** : Ability to automatically record webinars and make them available for replay or evergreen content hubs.


- **Registration and landing pages** : Built-in registration forms and landing pages for capturing attendee information and managing sign-ups.


- **Analytics and reporting** : Detailed attendee analytics, engagement scoring, and performance metrics to help measure webinar success.


- **Marketing automation integrations** : Integrations with CRM and marketing platforms (such as Salesforce, HubSpot, and Marketo) for lead tracking and nurturing.


- **Simulated live and automated webinars** : Options to pre-record sessions and present them as live events to scale content delivery.


- **Content hubs and resource centers** : Tools for organizing multiple webinars and related assets into a central library for ongoing audience engagement.


## ON24 reviews


ON24 has received some positive evaluations. However, taking a look at aggregated reviews also points out some shortcomings. One common criticism is that ON24 is subject to its limitations, and while it works well for small webinars, it will quickly show poor performance when scaled to larger events. Secondly, users sometimes find the ON24 user experience to be awkward and unintuitive, leading to difficulties with navigation and usability. A third common complaint is that while ON24 is known to be well documented, when event hosts and users need to solve issues that are beyond the documentation, it has been reported to lack comprehensive and responsive technical support services.


All of this considered, some webinar and virtual events creators might be looking for ON24 alternatives.

## Factors to consider


When analyzing webinar platforms specifically, in addition to thinking about the way your business or organization employs webinars and the size and scope of your events, some key factors include:


- **Scalability and flexibility** – can the product be tailored to your needs whether smaller or larger gatherings are called for? Is it capable of supporting more than one type of virtual event or does it have limited capabilities?


- **Video quality** – this point should be self-explanatory.


- **Customization** – is a bare-bones, utilitarian setup adequate for you, or are you looking for more ability to customize and brand the experience?


- **User-friendliness and user experience** – a significant factor in your event success will be whether attendees have a positive experience and can effectively navigate controls, etc. Further, consider your own needs as far as event setup and hosting; no one wants to get slowed down by a clunky, confusing interface.


- **Engagement features–** a potentially overlooked feature. High engagement is another key to success in virtual events and features that facilitate high engagement, such as interactive activities, live chats, and networking tools can help you get the most out of your online events. Better still if data reporting tools are available to help you post-game.


- **Technical support and customer service** – also (hopefully) self-explanatory. Will the product come with adequate support?


- **Hybrid capabilities** – this point may or may not be relevant to your specific content, but consider whether you want to offer hybrid, mobile, or in-person options for your virtual events. If a product is strictly limited to a single web/online format your attendees have less flexibility.
