# Video marketing tools: Key categories & 10 tools to know in 2026

Video has become a key aspect of digital marketing, helping brands capture attention, explain products, and convert viewers into customers. The right tools make it easier to create, manage, and measure video content across platforms.

## What are video marketing tools?


[Video marketing](https://corp.kaltura.com/blog/video-marketing/) tools include platforms for AI-powered video creation, hosting, and distribution like Kaltura, pure video hosting platforms like Vimeo, and video editing software like Adobe Express. These tools cover the entire video lifecycle, from ideation and creation to hosting, distribution, and performance analysis.


Video marketing tools enable teams to create videos efficiently, maintain brand consistency, track viewer engagement, and adapt strategies based on data. Whether you’re building brand awareness, driving sales, or educating customers, video marketing tools simplify the process of producing and managing video content at scale.

## Categories of video marketing tools


### Video Creation and Enrichment


These tools help users design, shoot, and edit videos, often with minimal technical skills required. They range from beginner-friendly editors with drag-and-drop interfaces to advanced platforms with AI automation, templates, and collaboration features. The goal is to speed up the production process while ensuring professional-quality results.


Some tools offer text-to-video capabilities, automatic video generation, and branded asset libraries, allowing marketing teams to produce videos faster and more consistently.


### Hosting and distribution platforms


Hosting and distribution platforms store your video files and make them accessible to viewers online. They provide streaming infrastructure, embed options, privacy settings, and playback controls.


These platforms also enable teams to manage large video libraries, restrict access, and share videos through websites, emails, or social media. Unlike social media platforms that monetize through ads, professional hosting services often emphasize control, branding, and analytics, making them suitable for business use cases.


### Video analytics and optimization


Analytics and optimization tools track how videos perform across platforms, measuring views, watch time, engagement, and conversion metrics. These tools help marketers understand which content resonates with viewers and where improvements can be made.


Features often include A/B testing, heatmaps, audience segmentation, and SEO insights. Some tools also provide AI-driven recommendations to improve titles, thumbnails, or publishing schedules for better results.


### Automated video content


Automated video content tools generate videos at scale using data inputs, templates, or artificial intelligence. These platforms are designed to create dynamic, personalized, or data-driven videos with minimal manual effort.


Common use cases include generating hundreds of product videos from a catalog, creating personalized sales or onboarding videos using CRM data, or turning blog posts and articles into short-form video content. Automation reduces production time and cost, making it feasible to deliver tailored video experiences across customer segments, channels, or campaigns.
