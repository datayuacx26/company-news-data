## What is a virtual event platform?


Just in case you’re out of the loop, a virtual event platform is a software or app tool that facilitates your digital events. Without an event platform to help your event flourish, it’s just a multi-participant video call! The best virtual event platforms can bring a live event feel into the virtual online world and can include audio, video, and interactive elements, as well as the use of smart technology.


Other than facilitating your event, the goal of a virtual event platform is to help people connect as well as increase audience engagement through interactivity and immersive environments. Full-featured platforms are a means to plan, run, and support virtual events. Certain platforms may also provide support for event planners or hosts on the administrative side and include registration and ticketing.


**Key features to look for in a virtual event platform include:**


- **Live streaming** We all know what this is, but a virtual event platform will give you the capacity to stream larger events and scale.


- **Virtual meetups** An option for smaller, more casual meetups, but within your larger platform, easily able to scale up to a big event or experience.


- **Breakout spaces** Breaking smaller groups out from a large event or meeting is an excellent way to continue specific conversations or facilitate networking.


- **Q&A sessions** Expand it out beyond a text chat window and engage your participants with a real back-and-forth that helps with information retention.


- **Polling** This is an interactive feature, helping to both engage the audience as well as provide a means to measure engagement.


- **Workshop facilitation** Workshop-specific spaces for your virtual event; not everything in your program will be an audience passively watching a speaker or panel.


- **Gamification** This is another great way to simultaneously engage and gather data about your participants.


- **Virtual booths** If an in-person trade show is not an option, you can showcase your products virtually.


## Virtual event platform market trends


### Market size and growth


The virtual events market continues to expand as organizations adopt digital alternatives to traditional gatherings. The


[market size](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/virtual-events-market) has reached USD 16.61 billion and is expected to grow to USD 27.65 billion by 2031, representing a 10.72% compound annual growth rate (CAGR).


### Adoption drivers


Several broader workplace and technology shifts are accelerating the adoption of virtual event platforms:


- **The growth of remote and hybrid work:** This has created distributed teams across multiple time zones. Virtual events make it easier for organizations to host company meetings, training sessions, and partner briefings without requiring travel. Many event agendas are now designed around on-demand content, modular tracks, and recorded sessions to accommodate global audiences.


- **UCaaS:** Another driver is the adoption of Unified Communications as a Service (UCaaS) platforms such as Microsoft Teams, Zoom Workplace, and Cisco Webex. These platforms increasingly include built-in event capabilities, allowing organizations to host webinars and company-wide events without purchasing separate software.


- **Environmental initiatives:** Many corporations now include carbon-reduction targets and ESG reporting in procurement decisions. Virtual events reduce travel-related emissions and help companies meet sustainability goals.


### Technology trends


New technologies are reshaping what virtual events can deliver beyond basic video conferencing:


-


- **Generative AI** is being integrated into event platforms to provide real-time translation, automated captions, and personalized agendas. AI tools can also assist with attendee matchmaking, answering questions, and generating post-event summaries.


- **Agentic AI,** such as Kaltura’s Agentic Avatars, offers registrants and attendees real-time, adaptive guidance at scale regarding session topics, speakers, the event experience and agenda, as well as products and solutions, in the form of conversational digital experiences.


- **Extended reality (XR) event environments** are increasingly being developed. While web-based platforms currently dominate because they require no installation, XR-based event experiences are expected to grow quickly. These environments enable immersive showcases such as product demonstrations, simulations, and virtual walkthroughs.


- **Improved connectivity** is also helping the market grow. The expansion of 5G networks, particularly in Asia-Pacific, enables higher-quality video streaming and smoother real-time interaction during large events.


### Market segments and use cases


Virtual event platforms support a range of formats and industries:


- Webinars currently generate the largest share of event revenue due to their low setup cost and ability to produce reusable marketing content.


- Conferences and summits account for a significant share of the market, often including multiple sessions, networking features, and sponsor participation.


- Product launches are among the fastest-growing use cases because companies can present new products to a global audience simultaneously.


From a revenue perspective, sponsorships represent the largest income source, while ticket sales are growing quickly as organizers introduce tiered access models with premium content or exclusive networking sessions.


## How to choose a virtual event platform


Choosing the right virtual event platform depends on the type of event you are hosting, the size of your audience, and the level of interaction you want to support. Some platforms are designed for simple webinars, while others support large conferences with networking, sponsorship areas, and multiple parallel sessions. Evaluating your needs before selecting a platform helps avoid technical limitations during the event.


When comparing platforms, focus on the following considerations:


- **Audience size and scalability:** Make sure the platform can support your expected number of attendees. Some tools work well for small meetings but struggle with large conferences or thousands of viewers.


- **Event format support:** Consider whether the platform supports the formats you need, such as webinars, workshops, networking sessions, or multi-track conferences. Not all platforms handle breakout sessions or simultaneous sessions well.


- **Audience engagement features:** Look for built-in tools that encourage interaction. Features such as live Q&A, chat, polls, reactions, and networking rooms help keep attendees involved and improve information retention.


- **Registration and ticketing:** Many platforms include event registration pages, payment processing, and ticket tiers. If monetization or attendance tracking is important, these capabilities can simplify event management.


- **Integration with other tools** : Check whether the platform integrates with your existing systems, such as CRM tools, marketing automation platforms, or email software. Integrations help automate attendee communication and data collection.


- **Analytics and reporting:** Post-event analytics are important for understanding attendee behavior. Good platforms provide data on attendance, engagement levels, session popularity, and lead generation.


- **User experience and accessibility:** A platform should be easy for attendees to join without a complicated setup. Browser-based access, mobile compatibility, captions, and language support can significantly improve participation.


- **Technical support and reliability:** Live events require stable infrastructure and quick technical support. Platforms that provide real-time support or dedicated event assistance can reduce the risk of disruptions during the event.

FAQ


- **Where can you host virtual events?**


That’s the genius of virtual events–you can host them anywhere! Host a conference from your bedroom or rent out a concert hall for a hybrid livestream performance.


However, as we’ve run down above, your choice of tools and platform will count for a lot. Additionally, make sure you or your platform provider has adequate network bandwidth and storage capacity to broadcast and manage the content.


- **What kind of events can you do with a virtual event platform?**


In the previous answer, we pointed to a range of virtual event types. Keep in mind that the capabilities of your platform will affect what you’re able to execute: a small-scale networking tool is not the best virtual event platform for a stream that will attract thousands of viewers.


- **What is the difference between a virtual event platform and a video conferencing tool?**


The primary difference is that a conferencing tool tends to host a single speaking session, even if it’s one-to-many or many-to-many, whereas virtual event platforms are designed to allow for multiple live sessions. While conferencing tools often do the trick, virtual event platforms are geared towards handling complex events with configured agendas, and multiple workshops, networking events, or speaking sessions happening at the same time.
