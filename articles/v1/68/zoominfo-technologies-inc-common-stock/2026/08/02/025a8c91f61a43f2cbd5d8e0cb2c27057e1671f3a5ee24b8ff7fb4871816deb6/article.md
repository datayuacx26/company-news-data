# ZoomInfo Sponsors the Cotuit Kettleers, the Cape Cod Baseball League’s Most Decorated Club

***ZoomInfo is backing the winningest team in the historic New England league that has sent hundreds of players to Major League Baseball, and the college athletes who make it run***


WALTHAM, Mass.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 10, 2026-- ZoomInfo (NASDAQ: GTM) today announced that it is a sponsor of the Cotuit Kettleers, one of ten teams in the Cape Cod Baseball League and the most decorated franchise in the league’s history. The sponsorship deepens ZoomInfo’s investment in the New England community where the company runs its East Coast operations, and in the college athletes who compete on Cape Cod each summer.


The Cape Cod Baseball League brings many of the best college players in the country to ten towns on Cape Cod each June through August, where they play with wooden bats in front of scouts from all 30 Major League organizations. The league reports that 400 of its alumni appeared on Major League Baseball rosters during the 2025 season, that more than 1,750 have reached the majors all-time, and that over 70 alumni were named to rosters for the 2026 World Baseball Classic. For generations of players, a summer on the Cape has been the step that turned a college prospect into a professional.


Based in the village of Cotuit, the Kettleers are the league’s winningest club, with a record 17 championships. That tradition, and the team’s role as a direct pipeline to the sport’s highest level, is what drew ZoomInfo, a company built on the same New England ground, to get behind the team.


“Every summer, the future of baseball comes to Cape Cod, and this year ZoomInfo is proud to stand behind the Cotuit Kettleers,” said Henry Schuck, Founder and Chief Executive Officer of ZoomInfo. “This is a program that has helped shape generations of elite talent, and it sits right in our own backyard. Supporting the Kettleers and the college athletes who wear the uniform is exactly the kind of investment we want to make close to home.”


The sponsorship reflects ZoomInfo’s broader support of college athletes in New England, extending the company’s community presence beyond its offices and into the towns where its employees live and work.


**About the Cotuit Kettleers**


The Cotuit Kettleers are a collegiate summer baseball team based in Cotuit, Massachusetts, competing in the Cape Cod Baseball League. With a record 17 league championships, the Kettleers are the most successful franchise in league history.


**About the Cape Cod Baseball League**


The Cape Cod Baseball League is a collegiate summer baseball league on Cape Cod, Massachusetts, made up of ten teams. Dating to the 19th century, it is considered the most prestigious of the country’s collegiate summer leagues and a primary showcase for players eligible for the Major League Baseball Draft.
