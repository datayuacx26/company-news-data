# What you need to know about pest proofing your business

## How to pest-proof your business


Pest activity in buildings has always been a daily problem for businesses. Unwanted pests in your business can cause all types of damage and be a health risk to you, your employees, and your customers. The best way to get ahead of the problem is to prevent these pests from entering in the first place.


Exclusion services are a key part of Rentokil’s[integrated pest management (IPM)](https://www.rentokil.com/us/pest-control/integrated-pest-management) program because they prevent pests from entering your building and prohibit movement. Rentokil’s IPM services are designed to protect your business from unwanted pests by identifying the root of the problem and implementing practical solutions. Our Technicians will work with you to develop a customized plan tailored to your business’s needs.


## **Top pests in your business**


Pests can be a major nuisance to any business and can do more than just cause damage or pose health risks – they can also create a wrong impression for your business. Pests can make your business seem dirty, unsanitary, and unprofessional. Here are a few of the most common pests to look out for:


- **Rodents** , like[mice](https://www.rentokil.com/us/pest-control/mouse-control) and ****[rats](https://www.rentokil.com/us/pest-control/rat-control) **,** can cause major damage by chewing through structures, equipment, electrical wires, etc. Mice can fit through openings as small as ¼”, while rats can squeeze through spaces as small as ½”.


- [Birds](https://www.rentokil.com/us/pest-control/bird-control) **and**[bats](https://www.rentokil.com/us/pest-control/wildlife-control/bats) can be noisy, messy, and unhygienic. Their droppings can carry diseases and pose a potential health risk. Feral pigeons can enter your property through just a two-inch opening, while the English House Sparrows only need a ¾” opening. Bats can squeeze through openings as small as ⅜”.
- [Nuisance wildlife](https://www.rentokil.com/us/pest-control/wildlife-control) , such as[squirrels](https://www.rentokil.com/us/pest-control/wildlife-control/squirrels) ,[opossums](https://www.rentokil.com/us/pest-control/wildlife-control/opossum) , and[raccoons](https://www.rentokil.com/us/pest-control/wildlife-control/raccoons) , can cause significant damage by chewing through wires and insulation and entering buildings through roof vents or open windows.
- **Larger overwintering insects** , such as large[cockroaches](https://www.rentokil.com/us/pest-control/cockroach-control) , brown marmorated stink bugs, and Asian lady beetles, **** can easily damage your business’s reputation. Cockroaches can carry all kinds of bacteria and diseases and only need 1/16” openings to enter your business.
