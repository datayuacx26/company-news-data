# Rentokil achieves CEPA certification (EN16636)

## The value of CEPA certification for Rentokil customers


This industry standard defines processes, action plans, skills and establishes practices for assessments, cause and risk analysis . lt also sets out the competencies required for different roles (administrative, sales, professional user, technical responsible person) that contribute to the delivery of[pest management services](https://www.rentokil.com/services/pest-control-services) .


We understand that is important to our[commercial customers](https://www.rentokil.com/business-sectors) that we prove that everything – from working practices and training to business ethics and values – is aligned to the correct standards.


Being certified by CEPA will guarantee proven protection and reliable service, where our commercial customers can expect:


- **In-depth experience:** We have been providing pest control services to thousands of customers for decades across a range of business sectors around the world.
- **Industry expertise:** We know what it takes to protect your business effectively against a wide range of pests.
- **Integrated Pest Management (IPM):** Our choice of pest control techniques and integration of appropriate measures to restrict pest population development allow us to keep pesticides to a minimum and monitor environmental impact.
- **Trained and certified technicians:** All our service technicians have completed Rentokil’s rigorous training programme to standards unrivalled within the industry.
- **Continuous training:** Throughout their careers, Rentokil pest control technicians are put on training courses to further develop in specific technical aspects of pest control.


As a member of many pest management associations around the world, Rentokil are proud to be recognised as a CEPA-certified pest control company and welcome new standards which serve to harmonize and standards in the pest control industry.
