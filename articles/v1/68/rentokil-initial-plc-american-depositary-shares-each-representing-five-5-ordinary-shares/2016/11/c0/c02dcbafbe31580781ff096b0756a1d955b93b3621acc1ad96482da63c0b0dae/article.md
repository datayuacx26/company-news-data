# Rentokil wins Best IoT Project of the Year 2016

In collaboration with[PA Consulting](https://www.paconsulting.com/) and Google, a cloud-based Internet of Things platform was developed to collect real-time data and send instant alerts of pest activity detected by connected pest control traps. The technology enabled monitoring of a customer’s business 24/7 and allowing visibility for rapid pest control response as well as Big Data analytics to gain new insights on local, regional and global levels.


This connected technology event in our[digital pest control](https://www.rentokil.com/services/digital-pest-control) solutions can be used to remotely identify emerging pest problems to inform customers around potential damaging issues and also precisely where it originated. Using the data provided by our connected devices we are able to upgrade our servicing plans to suit the customer’s needs and apply a targeted solution.
