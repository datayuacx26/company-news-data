# More Power, Same Agent: What's New at CandorIQ This July

That's why we launched the[Job Architecture Agent](https://www.candoriq.com/products/ai-job-architecture-builder) last month. And the response told us we were onto something. Teams that were spending months with consultants building job families from scratch were doing it in hours.


But the feedback was clear: building the architecture is only half the battle. The harder part is defending it.


### **The Job Architecture Agent Just Got Smarter**


When we first launched, the agent could already build complete job families from scratch, structure level definitions with clear criteria across scope, impact, and complexity, and align everything to market benchmarks from the start.


This month we added the two capabilities that comp leaders kept asking for:


**Financial Summary and Analytics for Executives**


Your CFO doesn't want to see job levels and titles. They want to know the financial impact. The agent now generates a clear financial summary built for the conversations that matter most with leadership. Walk into the room with numbers that speak their language, not a spreadsheet that needs 20 minutes of context.


**Compare Pay Strategies Side by Side**


One pay strategy is a proposal. Two pay strategies side by side is a decision framework. You can now create multiple pay strategies and compare them in real time, modeling different approaches before committing to one. No more building three versions of the same spreadsheet to show leadership their options.


This is the shift we keep pushing toward. CandorIQ is not just a system of record. It is a system that reasons with you. Less time pulling data. More time making decisions that move the business.

### **New Partnerships: BambooHR and HiBob**


Your HRIS should not be disconnected from your comp platform. Starting this month, CandorIQ has official partnerships with[BambooHR](https://www.candoriq.com/blog/announcing-our-bamboohr-partnership) and HiBob, deepening how workforce, compensation, and HRIS data flow together across your stack.


When your data lives in one connected ecosystem, your team spends less time reconciling and more time planning.


### **Comp Cycle: More Control Where It Matters**


Merit cycles are complex enough without fighting your tools. This month we shipped several updates to give comp teams more control over the process:


- Preview award letters immediately after upload and catch issues before they reach employees
- Override letters per employee when exceptions come up
- Choose exactly which HRIS data refreshes mid-cycle so nothing changes unexpectedly
- Mass-select eligible and ineligible employees without clicking through one by one
- Manage promotions through a cleaner, refreshed interface


These are the small things that save hours during a cycle and prevent the mistakes that cost trust.


### **Candidate Offers: Built for More Flexibility**


Every offer is different. Your tools should handle that. This month we made candidate offers more flexible:


- Benefits now display with more nuance, giving candidates a clearer picture of their total rewards
- Approvals adjust dynamically based on the offer details, so the right people review the right offers
- You can upload and attach files directly to an offer, keeping everything in one place


### **What This All Adds Up To**


Every update this month comes back to the same idea. The tools your team uses every day should be doing more of the work for you. Not just storing data. Thinking with you. Surfacing insights. Making it easier to have the conversations that actually move the business.
