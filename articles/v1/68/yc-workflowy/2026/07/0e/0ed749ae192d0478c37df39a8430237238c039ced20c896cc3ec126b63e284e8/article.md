# 2026.06: Tables. and more

## Tables


You can now turn any node into a lightweight Table, giving you a more structured way to organize plans, trackers, comparisons, and small databases.


A Table is still a list underneath. A node’s children become rows, and each row’s children become cells. Workflowy also adds a header row with blank cells so you can name your columns and give your notes more structure. You can[turn a Table back into a list](https://workflowy.com/help/change-node-types) at any time.


You can drag and drop to reorganize rows and columns, move cells, or replace one cell with another. You can also sort, duplicate, and use[different node types](https://workflowy.com/help/change-node-types) , such as To-dos, inside headers and cells, so your tables can become working plans, checklists, trackers, or drafts.


You can paste content from Excel, Google Sheets, Markdown, or TSV (tab-separated values) into a Workflowy Table and when you want to share or work with other apps,[export](https://workflowy.com/help/exporting) to HTML, Markdown, or TSV.


To try it, choose “Table” from the “Turn into” option in the node menu, or type /table.


## Other improvements


- Mobile: Sync problems are now detected, shown, and recovered more reliably.
- ​[Calendar](https://workflowy.com/help/plan-with-calendar) : Fixed a Found Date bug that caused a calendar day node to appear inside another calendar day node.
- Fixed: Tag[search](https://workflowy.com/help/search) results now expand parent nodes so matching nodes are visible.
- Fixed: Damaged images and files no longer break[file uploads](https://workflowy.com/help/files-images) or previews.
- Fixed: Moving nodes on mobile no longer leaves a phantom bullet stuck on the screen.
- Added a Bold font, making your content ultra-readable.
- Link and highlight colors work in search and menus for the Vintage, Wood, and Steel themes.
- Fixed: External links in the desktop app now open consistently in your web browser.
- [Export](https://workflowy.com/help/exporting) : Unicode letters are now allowed in export filenames.
- Dropbox Backup now retries if a system error stops it, rather than silently failing.
- Mobile network failures during signup and login are handled more quietly and reliably.
