# 2026.03: Email to Workflowy, Themes for All, Improvements to Calendar, and more

## **Email to Workflowy**


You can now email thoughts, notes, or links straight to Workflowy without opening the app. It’s another way to get things into your Workflowy hub, so everything is there when you need it.


Get a unique email address for key nodes like Today or Inbox, or any node you’ve added a[Shortcut](https://workflowy.com/learn/shortcuts/) to. You control which email addresses are allowed to send to it.


Works on shared nodes too and free for all users.


Set it up here:[https://beta.workflowy.com/integrations/email-to-wf/](https://beta.workflowy.com/integrations/email-to-wf/)


## **Personalise Workflowy**


- Themes are now free for everyone, everywhere (well, on desktop and mobile).
- New font: Geist Mono.


## General Fixes


- [Calendar](https://workflowy.com/learn/calendar/) picker now shows a marker for any Day node with content
- Found Dates load correctly when mirroring “[Today](https://workflowy.com/learn/calendar/) ” to another location.
- [Search](https://workflowy.com/learn/search/) now matches custom formatted dates with a prefix or suffix.
- Pasted Google Calendar event links now open properly.
- mailto:[links](https://workflowy.com/learn/internal-external-links/) open your email client as expected.
- [Video files](https://workflowy.com/learn/files-images/) no longer cause memory issues on Android and play properly.
- Fixed an issue that stopped the page from scrolling with the multi-select menu open.
