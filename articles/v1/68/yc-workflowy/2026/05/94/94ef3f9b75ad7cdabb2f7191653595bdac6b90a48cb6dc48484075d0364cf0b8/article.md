# 2026.05: Timeline, Today Digest, iOS Quick Add, and more

****


## **Timeline**


We’ve added Timeline to the[Calendar](https://workflowy.com/help/plan-with-calendar) , making it easy to plan and organise in the moment.


The idea: stop trying to hold everything in your head, or wrestling with a rigid weekly plan. Deal with things now or push them to the next logical, reasonable, or opportune time, and trust they’ll come back when you need them.


Timeline shows the next 30 days, with quick navigation across the next 12 months. A Tickler file, for our fellow productivity geeks. Drag nodes onto a date or month to schedule them, use **Move to Tomorrow** or **Next Week** in the[slash-command](https://workflowy.com/help/slash-command) or item menus, or multi-select to move several things at once.


**On mobile** , left-swipe a node for quick access to Move to Tomorrow and Next Week. It’s strangely addictive, not that we encourage procrastination, of course.


Available to all users. Requires[Calendar](https://workflowy.com/help/plan-with-calendar) to be enabled.


## **Today Digest**


Start your day knowing exactly what’s on your plate without opening Workflowy.


Today Digest is a morning snapshot of everything on your Workflowy calendar for today, delivered to your inbox at 8 AM.


[Set up Today Digest](https://workflowy.com/today-digest/)


## **iOS Quick Add Shortcut + Action Button Intent**


Capture thoughts in Workflowy without stopping what you’re doing. A native iOS App Shortcut lets you add items to Workflowy from the Shortcuts app or Siri, without opening the app first.


​Say “Hey Siri, Add to Workflowy”, then speak or type your note. It saves to your Inbox node automatically


If your iPhone has an Action Button, you can assign Quick Add directly to it for one-press capture.


[Get the Workflowy iOS app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/workflowy-note-list-outline/id551139514)

## **Other improvements**


- **[HEIC/HEIF photo uploads](https://workflowy.com/help/files-images)** .iPhone photos can now be uploaded directly without converting them first.
- ​ **Video plays immediately after upload.** Uploaded videos now display inline straight away, without requiring a page reload.
- **[Accessible fonts](https://workflowy.com/help/personalize-workflowy) .** OpenDyslexic is now available on mobile and we’ve added Atkinson Hyperlegible for all platforms.
- **Strikethrough completed items in[Markdown](https://workflowy.com/help/markdown) export.** Completed bullets now render with strikethrough formatting when exporting to Markdown.
- **[Google Calendar sync](https://beta.workflowy.com/integrations/calendar/) .** When a synced event has a location, it appears as a detail node beneath the event in Workflowy
- **Google Calendar sync.** Synced event names display in 12-hour format when no time preference has been set.
