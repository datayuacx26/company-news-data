# 2026.04: Google Calendar Sync and more

## **Google Calendar Sync**


[Connect your Google Calendar to Workflowy](https://workflowy.com/integrations/calendar/) and your events will appear automatically in your Calendar node.


With your tasks, notes, and schedule all in one place, Workflowy becomes a single place to plan and organise your day. No switching between apps to figure out what’s next.


Choose which calendars to sync and see event details and join Google Meet calls directly.


Note: requires Workflowy Calendar to be enabled.


[workflowy.com/integrations/calendar](https://workflowy.com/integrations/calendar)


## Improvements


- [Found Dates](https://beta.workflowy.com/help/calendar#view-items-with-date-tags-in-your-calendar) now supports date ranges, including date ranges in the Notes field.
- Fixed a bug where dated[Mirror](https://beta.workflowy.com/help/mirrors) nodes appeared as Found Dates even when the setting was disabled.
- Mirror To Date now works for all node types.
- Fixed: Unsharing a node now correctly removes the blue halo.
- Fixed an issue where users with access to a[shared node](https://beta.workflowy.com/help/sharing) incorrectly saw a “Removed by owner” message.
- Sorting a[Numbered List](https://beta.workflowy.com/help/bullet-types#numbering) now correctly places numbers before non-numeric characters.
- Fixed: The[To-do](https://beta.workflowy.com/help/bullet-types#to-dos) node type now works in the multi-select menu.
- [Keyboard shortcuts](https://beta.workflowy.com/help/hotkeys) are now visible in the multi-select menu.
- Fixed: The @, tag, link, and mirror buttons are now inactive on empty nodes in the mobile keyboard accessory.
- Fixed a scrolling issue with[boards](https://beta.workflowy.com/help/boards) on mobile.
