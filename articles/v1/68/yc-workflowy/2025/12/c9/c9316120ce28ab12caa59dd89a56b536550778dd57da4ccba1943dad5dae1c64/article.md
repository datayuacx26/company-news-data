## Two features that change how you work


**1. Calendar / Today**


Planning shouldn’t mean juggling three apps. Calendar brings daily tasks, journal entries, and future todos into one place: your outline.


- ​Press **Today** to capture what’s on your mind
- Use **/Move to Date** to schedule anything
- Let **Found Dates** surface what matters automatically


​Daily planning, without the filing system.
[Learn about Calendar →](https://workflowy.com/learn/calendar/)


**2. Chat with Your Notes**


Ever have ideas scattered across dozens of notes? **Chat with Your Notes** connects the dots, surfacing everything you’ve written about a topic or subject in seconds. Use **AI Nodes** to brainstorm alongside your thinking, or **AI Actions** to summarize, edit, and improve your notes.


No copy-paste dance. The AI works inside your outline.
[Learn about Workflowy AI →](https://workflowy.com/learn/workflowy-ai/)
