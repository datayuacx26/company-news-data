# 2025.20 – Calendar Found Dates, New Themes, Mirror Improvements, and More

## Calendar Found Dates


Your[Calendar](https://workflowy.com/learn/calendar/) now shows matching Found Dates from anywhere in your account, making it even better for daily planning and note-taking.


Drag any Found Date to your Calendar day to move it there.


## Personalization


Two fresh new[themes](https://workflowy.com/learn/settings/#change-appearance) to help you make Workflowy your own: Robot and Glass.


## Mirrors


[Mirrors](https://workflowy.com/learn/mirrors/) just got a little bit more reliable and useful:


- Mirror nodes now appear in search results even when ‘Show completed’ is turned off, making it easier to find all of your content.
- Moving Mirrors in the search results no longer causes them to disappear.
- Mirrors stay selected after moving a group of them, so you can keep working.


## Search and Organize


- **Friendly date search** .[Searching](https://workflowy.com/learn/search/) for natural language dates like “Today” or “Last week” in JumpTo, MoveTo, MirrorTo, and LinkTo menus now matches their corresponding dated nodes.
- **Improved multi-select dragging on mobile** . Drag and drop for multi-selected nodes is now more reliable and you can drag bullets from different indent levels under a single node, and they’ll flatten correctly as expected.
- **Board** columns no longer glitch when a collaborator is editing a shared board at the same time.
- **Better nested search.** Nested search now supports multiple words. For example, searching` Projects` >` Write draft -today` will match nodes containing both “Write draft” and “-today”.
