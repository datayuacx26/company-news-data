# Streamline your Workflowy Timeline with the “Training Wheels” extension

Over the years, thousands of WorkFlowy users have streamlined their lives using the[WorkFlowy Timeline](https://workflowy.com/the-workflowy-timeline/) method. The system is as simple as it gets — yet it’s underpinned by a modus operandi (a philosophy, really) powerful enough to reduce anxiety, stress and task overwhelm from day one.


Give it a try it and tell me if it’s not so.


The core mantra that maintains the system is to *push everything to the next logical, reasonable, or opportune time on your Timeline* — which allows everything to surface at exactly the right time. It’s a system that makes it literally impossible for things to fall through the cracks. It’s a system that let’s you rest easy, because it’s one you can trust.


The Timeline method helps you align your life with time itself.


## The Training Wheels extension


Today I’m sharing a tool that makes the moving parts of maintaining your WorkFlowy Timeline easier and more automated than ever: a Chrome extension called[Timeline Training Wheels](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/timeline-training-wheels/eldhmeeplhgccaipoekjjlkigedmlpck) (which you can also use on Opera, Edge, Vivaldi, Brave…).


The Training Wheels extension is an overlay that gives you a more “traditional” calendar and daily planner view right inside WorkFlowy. It’s a different way of seeing your Timeline — especially for those who are just starting out.


Timeline Training Wheels helps you to effortlessly:


- Create your calendar in the format of your choice
- Push and pull tasks between your calendar and daily planner


## The life cycle of an extension


You don’t need this extension to create and maintain a timeline in WorkFlowy. It’s simply a helper tool… and ideally, anything useful that it offers would be great to eventually have in WorkFlowy itself. In fact, the team is currently working on a calendar implementation, which you’ll hear more about in the coming days and weeks.
