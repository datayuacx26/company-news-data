# Matidor Wins the SourceForge Spring 2026 Top Performer Award

[Matidor](https://matidor.com/) , a GIS native field operations platform purpose built for oil and gas, environmental consulting, and multi site project management, is proud to announce it has been awarded the **SourceForge Spring 2026 Top Performer Award** in the field operations and project management software category.


SourceForge is the world's largest B2B software review and comparison website, serving nearly 20 million in market software buyers every month. This award recognizes exceptional products that earn a high volume of outstanding verified user reviews, placing them in the top tenth percentile of highly reviewed products out of over 100,000 products on the platform.


## **What the Award Recognizes**


### **Top 10 Percent of Highly Reviewed Products on SourceForge**


To receive the Spring 2026 Top Performer Award, Matidor had to receive enough high rated verified user reviews to rank in the top 10 percent of all favorably reviewed products on SourceForge. The award reflects the volume, recency, and quality of reviews from real users across Matidor's customer base in field operations, environmental services, and oil and gas.


Matidor users on SourceForge highlighted the platform's ease of use, map based project visibility, budget tracking, and ability to manage large multi site portfolios without spreadsheet complexity.


### **Three Spring 2026 Badges Earned**


Matidor earned three Spring 2026 recognition badges:


- **SourceForge Top Performer Spring 2026** — top 10 percent of highly reviewed products on SourceForge
- **Slashdot Top Performer Spring 2026** — recognized across the Slashdot Media network of software review platforms
- **Top Business Software High Achiever Spring 2026** — recognizing consistent high performance across the business software category

## **About Matidor**


[Matidor](https://matidor.com/) is a GIS native field operations platform that unifies project management, budget tracking, location intelligence, and offline first mobile field operations into one platform. Built by a team with Google Earth roots, Matidor replaces disconnected spreadsheets, static maps, and siloed mobile apps with a single live view of all projects, sites, and budgets across any portfolio size.


## **Four Core Capabilities**


- [Location Intelligence](https://matidor.com/product/location-intelligence) **:** Every project, site, and budget mapped in real time with regulatory zones, property boundaries, and environmental constraints overlaid.
- [Project Management](https://matidor.com/product/project-management) **:** Portfolio dashboards, custom workflows, milestone tracking, and contractor portals for multi site teams.
- [Budget and Cost Control](https://matidor.com/product/budget-cost-control) **:** Real time budget tracking with automated alerts at 70, 90, and 100 percent of AFE or budget thresholds.
- [Field Operations](https://matidor.com/product/field-operations) **:** 100 percent offline first mobile app with GPS tagged photos, standardized forms, and automatic sync when connectivity is restored.


Matidor serves customers across oil and gas, environmental consulting, civil engineering, infrastructure, and government in Canada and internationally.
