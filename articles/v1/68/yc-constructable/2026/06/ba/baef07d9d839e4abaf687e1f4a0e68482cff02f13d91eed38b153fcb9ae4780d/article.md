## What is INGENIOUS.BUILD?


[INGENIOUS.BUILD](https://constructable.ai/blog/ingenious-build-reviews-pricing-alternatives) is a construction project management solution built around three core modules: Project Financials, Project Management, and Construction Administration. The system is designed to serve a wide range of users, including real estate developers, architects, engineers, specialty trades, and general contractors.


Where INGENIOUS.BUILD puts its weight is in financial management. Real-time dashboards, budget tracking, and portfolio oversight are central to the product's structure. If you're a developer managing multiple assets across a portfolio, that framing makes some sense.


For a mid-size GC running commercial work day to day, though, the picture gets a little murkier. A system organized around financial modules and broad stakeholder coverage can feel like it was built for the developer's boardroom, not the superintendent's trailer. That's not a knock on the product. It just tells you who it was designed for and what it considers a priority.

### Constructable


RFIs in **Constructable** start on the drawing. You place a pin at the exact location the question is about, and the RFI lives there. Anyone who opens that sheet later sees exactly where the issue is without having to interpret a written description.
