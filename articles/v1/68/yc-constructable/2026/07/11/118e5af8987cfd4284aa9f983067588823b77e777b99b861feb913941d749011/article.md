## What is Procore and How Does it Work?


Procore is a cloud-based construction management software built to connect field and office teams across commercial, residential, and infrastructure projects. General contractors, specialty contractors, and owners all use it to manage the full project lifecycle from a single system.


The software covers a wide range of workflows:


-


Document control and drawing management, so everyone is referencing the same set of plans


-


RFI and submittal tracking to keep the paper trail organized and moving


-


Daily logs and punch lists for field teams to log progress without being chained to a desk


-


Budget tracking, invoicing, and financial management tied directly to the project


-


Quality and safety tools to stay on top of inspections and compliance requirements


-


Team communication and real-time reporting across all project stakeholders


The idea is that everything lives in one place, so the super in the field and the PM in the office are always looking at the same information. Procore runs on a subscription model with custom pricing negotiated per company, and it has mobile apps for both iOS and Android.


That said, Procore is not a light lift. Users consistently report a steep learning curve, especially for teams coming from spreadsheets or simpler tools. The system is broad by design, which means there is a lot to learn before it starts feeling natural. For larger firms with dedicated IT staff and time to train, that tradeoff can work. For mid-size teams where the PM is already handling three other jobs, it is worth knowing what you are signing up for before you commit.
