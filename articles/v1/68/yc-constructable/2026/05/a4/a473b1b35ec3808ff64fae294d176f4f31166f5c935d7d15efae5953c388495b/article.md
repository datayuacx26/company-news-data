## What is RFI Management Software?


RFI management software helps construction teams ask, track, and close out questions that come up during a project. When a detail is unclear, a dimension doesn't match, or the design intent needs clarification, you create an RFI. The software documents who asked, who's responsible for answering, and when a response is due.


Without it, RFIs get scattered across email and printouts. Responses get buried. Nobody knows who has the ball. By the time you find the answer, you've already lost days.


For general contractors coordinating architects, engineers, owners, and subs, RFI software creates a single source of truth. Questions get logged, assigned, and answered in one place. You can see what's open, who's waiting on what, and whether responses actually came back. It's accountability you can point to when schedules slip or costs change.


## How We Reviewed RFI Management Software


We focused on what keeps RFIs from turning into another headache. The goal wasn't to rank feature lists but to see which tools actually close questions without adding steps.


First, we checked drawing integration. If someone has to write a paragraph describing where the issue is instead of dropping a pin on the sheet, you're asking for confusion. Second, we tested field access. Can your super pull up an RFI on site without fumbling through menus, or does it need a desktop and a tutorial?


Response tracking came next. You need to know who's sitting on an answer and when it's overdue, or RFIs just become polite delays. We also looked at how the tool fits with the rest of your project work. If you're uploading the same drawings twice or switching systems to check related submittals, that's wasted time.


Pricing structure mattered too. Per-user pricing often means you avoid adding subs and consultants who need access, which defeats the purpose.

## Procore


Procore handles RFI workflows for large enterprise contractors with complex organizational structures and heavy process requirements.


The system provides customizable RFI workflows with multiple review stages and approval paths, letting external stakeholders respond via email without logging in. It integrates with Procore's broader project management ecosystem and sends automated reminders when RFI responses approach deadlines.


Procore works for large enterprise contractors running standardized processes across multiple concurrent projects who need deep customization and can support the training overhead.


The tradeoff: Procore requires extensive onboarding, often involving multiple training sessions and ongoing user support. Mid-size teams frequently report that the interface feels heavy and click-dense, and that field teams struggle to adopt it without dedicated internal champions. ## FAQ


### How do I choose the right RFI management software for my team?


Start with how your field crews will actually use it: if they can't create an RFI by dropping a pin on a drawing from their phone, adoption will be low. Then check whether unlimited users are included, because adding subs and consultants on a per-seat basis can kill your budget. Finally, look at onboarding time. If it takes months and needs dedicated champions, you're adding overhead instead of removing it.


### Which RFI tool works best for smaller teams versus larger GCs?


Smaller teams and mid-market GCs get the most value from systems that balance field usability with office visibility and do well with tools like **Constructable** or Fieldwire that work from day one with little training. Larger enterprise GCs tend to choose Procore or Autodesk because they have the resources to support longer onboarding.


### What's the difference between RFI software that integrates with drawings versus basic tracking tools?


Tools that pin RFIs directly to drawing locations keep all the context (markups, photos, comments) tied to the exact location of the question on the plan. Basic tracking tools treat RFIs like email threads with file attachments, which means people waste time writing paragraphs describing locations instead of just pointing. When responses come back weeks later, visual context prevents confusion about what was actually being asked.


### Can I include subcontractors and architects without paying per-user fees?


Some systems, like Constructable, offer unlimited users across your entire project team: internal staff, subs, consultants, and owners all get access without additional costs. Most other tools charge per seat, which means you either pay steep fees as your team grows or you restrict access and lose the visibility that makes RFI software worth having in the first place.


### When should I switch from managing RFIs in email to a dedicated software solution?


If you're losing track of who owes responses, can't find answers when you need them, or are spending more than a few hours per week chasing down RFI status, you've outgrown email. Once you're running multiple projects with mixed teams, the accountability and search capability of dedicated RFI software saves more time than the switch costs.
