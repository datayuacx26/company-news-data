## **Courier aur Logistics Industry Ka Scope: Numbers Se Samjho**


India ka logistics market abhi approximately USD 350 billion ka hai aur 2030 tak USD 500 billion se zyada hone ki expectation hai. E-commerce giants jaise Flipkart, Amazon, Meesho, aur Myntra ki growth ne courier companies ko non-stop hiring mode mein rakh diya hai. Festivals ke time (Diwali, Big Billion Days, Great Indian Sale) mein courier companies 30-40% extra staff hire karti hain. Matlab agar aap ready hain, kaam kabhi nahi rukta.

## **Courier Job Ka Daily Schedule: Kya Hota Hai Din Mein?**


Ek typical courier executive ka din kuch is tarah hota hai:


-


Subah 8-9 AM: Hub par pahuncho, parcels sort karo aur apna bag pack karo


-


9 AM - 2 PM: Morning delivery route complete karo


-


2-3 PM: Break, lunch, hub se naye parcels collect karo


-


3 PM - 7 PM: Evening deliveries complete karo


-


7 PM: Daily report submit karo, undelivered parcels return karo


Ek experienced courier executive din mein 50-80 parcels deliver karta hai. Jitne zyada parcels, utni zyada earning. Aur agar per-delivery model hai, to mehnat seedha paisa banta hai.


## **Eligibility: Courier Job Ke Liye Kya Chahiye?**


Bahut simple requirements hain:


-


Age: 18 saal ya usse zyada


-


Education: Koi minimum nahi, 8th pass bhi apply kar sakte hain


-


Vehicle: Bike ya scooter (kuch companies leased vehicle bhi deti hain)


-


Driving Licence: Valid 2-wheeler licence


-


Documents: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, vehicle RC


-


Smartphone: Basic Android phone chalega


-


Physical fitness: Din mein kaafi walking aur riding hoti hai

## **Career Growth: Courier Job Se Aage Kahan Jaayein?**


Courier job sirf ek starting point hai. Ek mehnat karne wale worker ke liye growth ke raste khule hain:


-


Delivery Executive se Hub Supervisor: Rs.25,000 - Rs.35,000/month


-


Area Manager ya Route Manager: Rs.35,000 - Rs.50,000/month


-


Operations Lead ya Cluster Head: Rs.50,000 - Rs.80,000/month


-


Khud ka courier franchise ya pickup point shuru karna


Logistics industry mein experienced workers ki demand bahut zyada hai. Jo log consistently achha kaam karte hain unhe promotions milte hain. Yeh ek career hai, sirf ek job nahi.


## **FAQs: Courier aur Logistics Job Ke Baare Mein Sawaal**


**Q: Kya courier job mein bike compulsory hai?**


Zyada cases mein haan. Lekin kuch companies warehouses aur hubs mein foot-delivery ya cycle-delivery roles bhi offer karti hain dense urban areas mein.


**Q: Shadowfax aur Delhivery mein se kaun sa better hai?**


Delhivery fixed salary model follow karta hai jo stability deta hai. Shadowfax gig model par kaam karta hai jisme flexibility zyada hai. Apni zaroorat ke hisaab se choose karo.


**Q: Courier job ke liye online apply kaise karein?**


Vahan.co/jobs par jaao, courier ya logistics category select karo, basic details fill karo. 2 minute mein application ho jaati hai.


**Q: Festival season mein extra kamaane ka koi tarika hai?**


Haan! Festival season mein companies temporary roles ke liye extra hire karti hain. Us time pe actively available raho aur zyada shifts lo, earning double ho sakti hai.


**Q: Kya courier job mein health insurance milta hai?**


Companies jaise BlueDart aur Amazon Logistics basic health benefits deti hain full-time employees ko. Gig platforms par yeh benefits limited hote hain, isliye join karne se pehle check karo.
