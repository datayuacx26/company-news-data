**Delivery Boy Salary India 2025: Platform-Wise Kitna Milta Hai?**

Ek dedicated delivery rider aasaani se Rs.35,000 se Rs.45,000 per month kama sakta hai, bina kisi degree ya special skill ke. **Delivery Boy Ka Daily Schedule: Kitne Ghante Kaam Karna Hota Hai?**


Ek full-time delivery rider generally 8-10 ghante per day kaam karta hai aur lagbhag 200-250 orders per month complete karta hai. Sabse zyada order milne ka time hota hai:


• Morning Shift: 8 AM - 2 PM


• Evening Shift: 5 PM - 11 PM


• Double Shift: 9 AM - 9 PM (maximum earning ke liye)


Agar aap sirf peak hours (12 PM-2 PM aur 7 PM-10 PM) mein active rehte hain, to ek ghante mein 3-5 deliveries easily ho jaati hain. Part-time riders bhi 4-6 ghante kaam karke acha income earn kar lete hain.

**Delivery Job Mein Career Growth: Kya Future Hai?**


Yeh ek common misconception hai ki delivery job mein growth nahi hoti. Sachai ekdum alag hai. Ek experienced delivery partner ke paas yeh career paths khule hain:


• Delivery Partner se Fleet Manager se City Operations Lead tak


• Khud ka delivery fleet shuru karna (10-15 bikes manage karke Rs.5-10 lakh/month)


• Logistics startup mein trainer ya team lead role


Agle 5 saalo mein India mein drone delivery, EV-based last-mile delivery aur hyperlocal commerce aur bhi tezi se grow karne wali hai. Jo aaj is sector mein enter karte hain, wo kal is growing wave ke top par honge.
