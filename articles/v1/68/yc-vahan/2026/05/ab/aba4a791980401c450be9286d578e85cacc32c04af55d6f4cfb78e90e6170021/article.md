Delivery job mein timing sab kuch hai. Sahi waqt par niklo, orders ki barsaat ho jaegi. India ke top peak hours:

**Food Delivery Job Mein Real Problems: Jo Koi Nahi Batata**


Ab baat karte hain uss cheez ki jo aksar koi nahi batata. Agar aap is field mein aana chahte hain, in challenges ke baare mein pehle se jaanna zaroori hai:


**1. Weather aur Physical Fatigue** Garmi ho, sardi ho, ya baarish, delivery toh karni hi hai. Proper gear (raincoat, sun protection) mein invest karna zaroori hai. Long-term mein ye aapko mushkilon se bachata hai.


**2. Traffic aur Road Safety**


India ke roads pe traffic ek nightmare hai. Delivery partners pe time pressure hota hai. Lekin hamesha safe driving priority honi chahiye, chahe thodi delay ho. Ek accident poori mehnat barbad kar sakta hai.


**3. Customer Complaints aur Ratings**


Kabhi food spill ho jaata hai, order late hota hai, ya restaurant ki galti se item missing hoti hai, blame delivery partner ko milta hai. Polite rehna aur platform ke saath clearly communicate karna best solution hai.


**4. Bike Maintenance aur Petrol Costs**


Delivery partners ko apni bike khud maintain karni hoti hai. Monthly Rs.2,000 - Rs.4,000 maintenance mein lag sakta hai. Iska budget pehle se banao, ye directly aapki net earning affect karta hai.


**5. Income Kabhi Kabhi Unpredictable**


Kuch din orders bahut zyada, kuch din bilkul nahi. Emergency fund rakhna zaroori hai. Consistent working hours rakhoge toh income predictable ho jaati hai.


**6. Health aur Insurance**


Gig workers ko typically company health insurance nahi milta. Ek affordable health insurance plan zaroori hai.
