# Genpact Named a 2026 CSO Award Winner for Tenth Time

*Recognition Honors Genpact’s Commitment to Data Security, Privacy, and Cybersecurity Resilience*


**NEW YORK, May 13, 2026** – Genpact (NYSE: G), an agentic and advanced technology solutions company, today announced that it has been recognized with the 2026 CSO Award from Foundry's CSO, a leading source for news, analysis, and research on security and risk management. See the full list of the 2026 CSO Awards winners[here](https://www.csoonline.com/article/570667/cso-awards-showcase-world-class-security-strategies.html) .


The CSO Awards recognize organizations for security projects and initiatives demonstrating outstanding business value and thought leadership. Genpact’s tenth consecutive CSO honor specifically recognizes its **Data Security reAImagined** initiative, which strengthens enterprise information security by applying AI‑enabled capabilities, enhanced governance, and advanced threat modeling to better protect critical data across cloud, SaaS, and AI environments.


“As enterprises adopt AI at scale, protecting data across cloud, SaaS, and AI environments has become a business‑critical priority,” said **Vidya Srinivasan, Global Leader – Information Security, Infrastructure, and Logistics, Genpact** . “Through our **Data Security reAImagined** initiative, we are strengthening how we govern, monitor, and safeguard critical information to reduce risk and build lasting resilience.”


“This year’s award winners show how security teams have repositioned themselves as strategic business enablers,” said **Beth Kormanik, Content Director of the CSO Cybersecurity Awards & Conference** . “They tackle business challenges by leveraging new technology and ideas and delivering detailed planning and strong execution. Their organizations are stronger for these efforts that protect revenue continuity, improve resilience, and strengthen compliance. We congratulate them and look forward to celebrating them at the CSO Cybersecurity Awards & Conference."
