# Genpact to Report First Quarter 2026 Results

NEW YORK


, April 9, 2026


/[PRNewswire](http://www.prnewswire.com/) / -- Genpact (NYSE: G), an agentic and advanced technology solutions company recognized for its deep industry knowledge, process intelligence, and last-mile expertise, today announced it will report its first quarter 2026 financial results on Thursday, May 7, 2026 after the close of the market.


Following the release, Genpact's management team will host a conference call at 5:00 p.m. ET to discuss the company's performance. Participants are encouraged to[register](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4660689-1&h=178045192&u=https%3A%2F%2Fregister-conf.media-server.com%2Fregister%2FBI2f903c602d8348ac8d2e4a320673cc7b&a=register) in advance to receive a dial-in number and unique PIN for seamless access. While early log-in is recommended, registration and access will be available throughout the call.


A live webcast will be available on the Genpact Investor Relations[website](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4660689-1&h=1854528092&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.genpact.com%2Finvestors&a=website) . A replay and transcript will be posted on the website shortly after the call concludes.
