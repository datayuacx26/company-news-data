# Raising the Standard for Dry Utilities with NWPX Power and Communication Vaults

In modern communities, reliable underground power and communication systems are essential for safety, uptime, and long-term asset performance. NWPX Park and NWPX Geneva, divisions of NWPX Infrastructure, support these needs with precast Power and Communication Vaults designed specifically for dry utilities, providing a robust, secure foundation for underground networks.


###### How NWPX Vaults Support Dry Utilities


NWPX’s Power and Communication Vaults are reinforced concrete structures that form the backbone of safe, resilient underground power and communication systems. Each vault pairs structural strength with best-in-class design to protect critical equipment below grade while creating a controlled, accessible environment for cabling.


- Precast reinforced concrete construction delivers long-term strength and durability.
- A controlled interior space supports organized cabling and simplifies maintenance and future expansion.


Every vault is engineered with security at its core. Lockable hatches provide controlled access for authorized personnel during inspections, maintenance, or repairs, helping protect both workers and equipment. Built to local utility specifications or municipal standards, the vaults streamline permitting and installation across jurisdictions while supporting consistent, reliable underground utility operations.


###### Designed for Compliance, Reliability, and Flexibility


NWPX Power and Communication Vaults are aligned with rigorous safety codes and are designed to meet NEC/CEC requirements for electric equipment enclosures, supporting safe operation and compliance with national standards. For projects in Pacific Power and Rocky Mountain Power service areas, multiple[PacifiCorp‑approved configurations](https://www.nwpipe.com/resources/specifications/rcp-precast-concrete/vaults/) are available to help simplify design and approval.


Flexibility is central to the design. Vaults can be tailored to accommodate different equipment sizes, utility layouts, and access preferences, making them a strong fit for substations, campus complexes, data centers, renewable energy installations, and other secure, underground power and communication applications.


###### Key Features that Enhance Performance


NWPX vaults are built for both durability and functionality, with accessories tailored to project specifications so internal configurations match owner needs.


- Integrated cable management systems with racks, terminators, knockouts, and pulling irons support efficient routing of complex power and communications cabling.
- Optional enhancements such as ventilation, waterproofing, fireproofing, sump pumps, and resistance to seismic or wind loads provide all‑season resilience in demanding conditions.


For sensitive telecom or data infrastructure, specialized grounding and electromagnetic shielding can be added to help protect vital systems from interference. Practical features like secure internal access points and ladders support safe inspections and quick entry for authorized personnel. Manufacturing in a controlled factory environment helps maintain consistent quality and adherence to industry benchmarks.


###### Supporting Long-Term Dry Utilities Infrastructure


NWPX brings unmatched infrastructure expertise to dry utilities, delivering Power and Communication Vaults that combine robust precast construction, advanced engineering, and stringent regulatory compliance to protect critical underground power and communication systems. These vaults support long-term performance, streamlined maintenance, and future expansion across a wide range of applications. Together, these advantages position NWPX as the premier choice for owners and utilities that demand the highest standard in dry utilities infrastructure.
