# Engineers Week 2026: Transform Your Future

From the technologies that power our products to the systems that keep our global operations running with precision, engineering sits at the heart of every breakthrough at Gentherm. Behind each advancement is a team of engineers whose curiosity, creativity and problem‑solving mindset push us toward what’s next.


As we celebrate **Engineers Week** **2026** , we’re taking a moment to recognize the people who make that progress possible. This year’s theme, **Transform Your Future** , reflects the spirit our engineers embody every day: the drive to imagine new possibilities, improve the world around them and build solutions that shape a better tomorrow.


Across our global organization, engineers are turning ideas into real‑world impact. Their work doesn’t just move projects forward, it transforms the future of our company and the industries we serve.


To honor that impact, we’re spotlighting engineers whose perspectives capture the mindset behind meaningful innovation. Their stories remind us that transformation happens through collaboration, curiosity and the courage to keep learning.


## Employee Spotlights


****Gerardo delaGarza | Senior Engineering Manager** | Novi, Michigan, United States**


Gerardo reminds us that engineering is as much about people as it is about technology.


*“Engineering success comes not only from technical skill, but from being yourself and connecting with team members at every level.”*


His leadership philosophy reflects a future where collaboration, authenticity and trust are just as essential as technical expertise.


**Atharva Bodhe | Product Engineer | Novi, Michigan, United States**


Atharva approaches engineering with a mindset rooted in curiosity and practicality.


*“By staying curious, adaptable and grounded in practical engineering principles, I aim to help deliver solutions that add real value to both the product and the customer.”*


Focusing on real‑world impact underscores how engineers transform ideas into meaningful solutions.


**Martin Buechler | Engineer Components | Treuchtlingen, Germany**


Martin sees every challenge as an opportunity to understand systems more deeply.


*“Failures can have a single origin or be the result of several minor effects. Taking time to understand each effect allows you to see the problem in detail.”*


His perspective reflects the patience and analytical rigor that define great engineering.


**Leyla Ozsoy | Product Engineering Team Lead | Novi, Michigan, United States**


Leyla focuses on taking a step back and looking at the basics.


*“Slow down to understand the fundamentals, question why decisions exist, focus on what truly matters and take time to recognize your own achievements.”*


This grounded approach emphasizes the opportunity to engineer holistically, empowering the product and yourself.


****Xin Zhang | Product Development Engineer | Shanghai, China****


Xin reinforces the importance of working with what you’ve got in order to make breakthroughs.


*“Be positive and patient, recognize the recurring pattern, do your best with what’s in front of you right now even if the goal isn’t clear and stay open as you move forward.”*


Her attitude is crucial when working through complex problems as an engineer.


**Sebastian Schürer | Senior Test Engineer | Odelzhausen** , **Germany**


Sebastian highlights the power of teamwork in achieving technical excellence.


*“Solutions and goals can only be achieved as a team, where constant communication, cooperation and open exchange of different approaches with colleagues make optimal component performance possible.”*


His insight reinforces that innovation thrives when diverse approaches come together.


**Tim Li | Application Engineer** | **Tianjin, China**


Tim brings a calm, steady mindset to complex problem‑solving.


*“Be patient and adjust your mindset. With the right perspective, every problem can be solved.”*


His philosophy reflects the resilience and adaptability that fuel engineering innovations.


**Ruchun Zheng | Process Engineering Manager | Tianjin, China**


Ruchun’s experience underscores the impact of collaboration.


*“ *No engineer is a superman, but with teamwork, a solution always comes out, and together the team becomes the superman.* ”*


His story is a reminder that engineering transformation is never a solo effort, it’s a collective achievement.


****Gábor Farkas | Software Architect | Pilisszentiván, Hungary****


Gábor emphasizes the importance of building on strong foundations to create lasting impact.


**“After building a foundation in mathematics and computer science and applying it in real-world industrial environments, this experience shaped how I approach engineering, combining analytical thinking with practicality, and designing systems that remain coherent even under pressure.”**


He reminds us that transformation isn’t just about innovation, it’s about creating systems that remain resilient and reliable in the face of complexity.
