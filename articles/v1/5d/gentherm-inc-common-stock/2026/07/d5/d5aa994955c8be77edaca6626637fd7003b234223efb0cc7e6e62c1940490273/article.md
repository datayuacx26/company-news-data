# Thinking Differently: Celebrating World Quality Week 2025

This World Quality Week, we’re spotlighting the everyday innovations that make a big impact. Under the theme **Think Differently** , our teams around the globe are showing how small changes can lead to smarter processes, stronger collaboration and better outcomes.


## A week of inspiration


From panel discussions to the Global 8D Competition, World Quality Week 2025 is packed with opportunities to learn, connect and grow. Whether on the production floor, in the office or supporting teams remotely — our team members’ ideas matter.


Together, our team will keep thinking differently.
