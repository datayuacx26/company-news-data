# Welcome to the Cedar Blog

Welcome to the Cedar blog. We are starting this space to share three kinds of writing: practical notes on **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** and **Search Engine Optimization (SEO)** as we figure them out for an AI-native product, and **user stories** from the people who actually live in their inbox every day.


## Why now


Email is changing faster than it has in twenty years. Agents read it, write it, triage it, and increasingly act on it. We are building Cedar for that world, and the blog is where we will think out loud about what we are learning.


## What to expect


- Short, opinionated pieces — no SEO filler.
- Posts written to be cited by both humans and AI agents.
- Stories from real users, not personas.


Thanks for reading. More soon.
