### My notes and takeaways

Apr 22, 2021


**All-In Podcast Notes Ep. 29**


**Lessons from Chamath:**


If your company goes public through a non-traditional means, sell when it hits the public market. Trends show prices top tick and go down from there (short term)


Retail stock traders should note this.


Is employee morale affected when stock prices are down? Especially those who have shares locked up


Are share lockups good? Do they allow banks, late stage VC’s and underwriters to make money, while the employee is made to wait?


**GRAB IPO:**


GRAB is going public through a SPAC, and this has boosted the company’s value about 20-30%.


How? GRAB got to choose who got on their cap table, and they chose folks that believed in the business, and they didn’t have to pay exorbitant fees.


High level management in GRAB actually opted to lock up their shares for 3 years (it gets better), all while other employees can freely sell their shares.


**Current Market Conditions:**


Massive drawdowns of growth multiples, but the 10 year yield hasn’t moved much. This isn’t a good sign, because the 10 yr yield moves inversely to growth multiples. This means if the 10 year yield increases, there is more pain down the road for growth stocks.


Don’t allow your short term wins to dictate long term behavior, or analysis of long term events.


Over 5-10 years, the top 25% of tech companies will continue to explode, even though the short term view might be bleak.


Once again, the private market is overvalued companies and the public markets are taking these valuations down.


Buy excellent companies at good prices. Sky high valuations do not necessarily mean you shouldn’t invest IF you like the company and believe in the overarching theme and space this company represents


**Capitalism and Media:**


Capitalism is AWESOME!!! It’s brought us vaccines in record time.


Amazon Prime subscribers create $126B of value for themselves each year. Holy Smokes!!!


The world needs a middle way approach to unions. Right now, unions don’t work as constructed. Create a union that can collectively bargain with data to back up their requests


Post Covid we’ll see a Capitalist vs Socialist throwdown occur between Texas and California, as citizens will vote with their feet and wallets.
