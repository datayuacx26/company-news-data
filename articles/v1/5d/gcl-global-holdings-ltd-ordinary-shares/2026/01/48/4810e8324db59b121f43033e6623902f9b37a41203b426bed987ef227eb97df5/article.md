# GCL Schedules First Half Fiscal Year 2026 Earnings Release and Conference Call Date

SINGAPORE, Jan. 23, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --


[GCL Global Holdings](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=vRXcVDAx7QWYhKZfTjv3AuWXctY9fE9J7FWp1dZZ1j9cN1bX6AB9cIni4MZSYmFImuE1-UbAc-UlpIiPiA1uGjJlBXURnZiUTVwdYrxsYLU=)

##


About GCL Global Holdings Ltd


GCL Global Holdings Ltd. (“GCL”) is a holding company incorporated in the Cayman Islands. GCL, together with its subsidiaries, forms the “GCL Group.” Through its operating subsidiaries, GCL Group unites people through its ecosystem of content and hardware in games and entertainment. This enables creators to deliver engaging experiences to gaming communities worldwide, with a strategic focus on the rapidly expanding Asian gaming market.


###


Our Vision and Mission


Drawing on a deep understanding of gaming trends and market dynamics, GCL Group leverages its diverse portfolio of digital and physical content. We aim to bridge cultures and reach a global audience by introducing Asian-developed IP across consoles, PCs, and streaming platforms. Our mission is to connect creators with gaming communities worldwide and introduce Asian-developed intellectual property to a broader audience.


###


The Future of Gaming


As we look to the future, we see immense opportunities in the gaming industry. The demand for innovative content is growing. Our commitment to quality and creativity will guide us as we expand our offerings. We believe that by fostering collaboration between creators and players, we can enhance the gaming experience for everyone.

[http://www.gclglobalholdings.com](http://www.gclglobalholdings.com/) .
