## Why smart locks matter for Airbnb hosts


Gone are the days of traditional lock-and-key systems. Today’s hosts are embracing smart door locks for their properties, recognizing the benefits they offer:


- **No more key handoffs or lockboxes** – Guests get a code; you skip the coordination
- **Fewer lockouts and rekeying costs** – Lost keys become irrelevant
- **Clear log of who entered and when** – Activity monitoring for security and accountability
- **Scalable solution for multiple listings and staff** – Manage cleaners, maintenance, and co-hosts with unique codes
- **Remote access management** – Grant or revoke entry from anywhere, anytime


## How to choose the right smart lock


When selecting a smart keyless door lock for your Airbnb, consider these factors grouped by category:


### **Hardware and compatibility**


- Door type (standard deadbolt, mortise, euro cylinder)
- Ease of installation (retrofit vs full replacement)
- Battery life and power backup options


### **Connectivity and integrations**


- Connection type (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Z-Wave)
- Integration with property management software like Guesty
- Guesty LocksManager compatibility


### **Guest and staff experience**


- Entry method (keypad, app, or both)
- Backup access options (physical key, lockbox)
- Ability to create staff-specific codes with custom schedules


## Pre-purchase checklist


Before ordering a smart lock, confirm these details:


1. **Measure your door** – Check door thickness and backset distance
2. **Identify your lock type** – Standard deadbolt, latch, or mortise?
3. **Test Wi-Fi signal at the door** – Weak signal = unreliable remote access
4. **Decide on visibility** – Visible keypad or “invisible” smart lock?
5. **Plan your backup entry** – Physical key, keypad code, or nearby lockbox?


Most smart locks can be installed in under an hour with basic tools. However, ensure compatibility with your door before purchase. Many hosts opt for professional installation to guarantee proper functioning and integration with existing systems.


## Educating your guests


While smart locks offer convenience, not all guests are familiar with the technology. Provide clear instructions including:


- Downloading any necessary apps
- Activating Bluetooth or Wi-Fi when approaching the property
- Using backup entry methods (physical keys or keypads)


### **Guest message template**


Hi \[Guest name\],


Your smart lock code for \[Property name\] is **\[CODE\]** . It’s active from \[date/time\] to \[date/time\].


**To enter:**


1. Tap the keypad to wake it
2. Enter your code and press ✓
3. Make sure the door latches behind you


If the lock doesn’t respond, use the backup key in the lockbox at \[location\] with code \[XXXX\].


Safe travels!


### **Do’s and don’ts for guests**


- **Do** test the code shortly after arrival
- **Do** close the door fully before locking
- **Don’t** share the code with anyone not on the reservation
- **Don’t** prop the door open (it may trigger alerts)
