## Why UK hosts should list on more than just Airbnb


Relying solely on Airbnb is like having a shop on only one street in town. You miss out on all the customers walking down other roads. Expanding to other platforms puts your property in front of new audiences who might never have found you on Airbnb.


A multi-channel strategy gives you three distinct advantages:


1. **Increased Occupancy:** You tap into different pools of travelers.[Vrbo](https://www.guesty.com/blog/airbnb-vs-vrbo-for-property-managers/) is popular with families planning longer holidays, while Booking.com has massive global reach and attracts international and last-minute guests. More visibility means fewer empty nights on your calendar.


2. **Higher Revenue:** Different platforms have different demand patterns. You might find that guests on a luxury platform like Plum Guide are willing to pay a premium, or that longer stays from Vrbo guests increase your average booking value.


3. **Reduced Risk:** If your Airbnb account is flagged or your listing’s ranking suddenly drops, your entire income stream is at risk. With bookings coming from multiple sources, a problem on one platform doesn’t cripple your business. You have a safety net.

The biggest fear for hosts managing multiple channels is the double booking. You get a reservation on Vrbo. You race to your Airbnb calendar to block the dates, but a new booking comes through before you can. Now you have two guests, one property, and a massive headache that will likely end in a cancellation penalty and a bad review.


Manually updating calendars is not a scalable solution. Even[iCal syncing is too slow and unreliable](https://www.guesty.com/blog/sync-airbnb-calendar-with-vrbo-calendar/) , often taking several minutes or even hours to update, leaving a dangerous window for double bookings to occur.

You can then see all your reservations from every platform in one place. A[Multi-Calendar](https://www.guesty.com/features/multi-calendar/) gives you a single view of your availability, so you know exactly what’s happening across your business without logging into three different websites.


More channels mean more guest messages. Instead of juggling multiple inboxes, you can manage every conversation from a **Unified Inbox** . ## FAQ about listing on Airbnb alternatives in the UK


### Is it worth listing on sites other than Airbnb in the UK?


Yes. Diversifying reduces your reliance on a single platform, increases your potential occupancy by reaching new guest demographics, and ultimately builds a more stable and profitable hosting business.


### What is the most popular alternative to Airbnb in the UK for hosts?


Vrbo and[Booking.com](https://www.guesty.com/blog/what-is-the-difference-between-booking-com-and-airbnb/) are the two largest and most popular alternatives. Vrbo is excellent for entire homes and family travel, while Booking.com offers unparalleled access to a global audience, particularly for city-based properties.


### Can I list my property on Airbnb and Vrbo at the same time?


Absolutely. This is one of the most common and effective multi-channel strategies. The key is to[use a channel manager](https://www.guesty.com/blog/best-channel-managers/) to ensure your calendars are always synchronized in real time to prevent double bookings.


### Which Airbnb alternative is best for properties in London?


For London properties, Booking.com is a strong choice due to its popularity with international business and leisure travelers. If your property is high-end and design-focused, applying to be on Plum Guide can attract premium guests and higher nightly rates.


### How do I avoid double bookings when using multiple sites?


Do not rely on manual calendar updates or slow iCal connections. The only reliable method is to use a short-term rental management software with a channel manager. It uses a direct API connection to instantly sync your availability across all your chosen booking platforms the moment a reservation is made.
