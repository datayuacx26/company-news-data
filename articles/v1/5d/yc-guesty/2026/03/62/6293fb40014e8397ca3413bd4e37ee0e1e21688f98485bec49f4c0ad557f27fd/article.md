**TL;DR:** Both Guesty and Hospitable automate short-term rental operations, but they’re built for different stages of a business. Hospitable handles the basics well for smaller, simpler setups. Guesty is built for property managers who want to scale — with a channel manager covering 60+ booking platforms, native accounting and CRM tools, embedded dynamic pricing, and enterprise-grade reporting that Hospitable simply doesn’t offer.


[Choosing a vacation rental management system](https://www.guesty.com/blog/how-to-choose-a-pms-for-str/) is one of the highest-stakes decisions you’ll make for your rental business. Switch too early to the wrong platform and you’ll outgrow it fast. Switch too late and you’ve left revenue on the table.


This breakdown cuts through the noise. Here’s exactly what Guesty and Hospitable offer — and where each one falls short.


## **What both platforms do**


Before the differences, the overlap. Guesty and Hospitable share a solid foundation of core PMS features:


- Unified inbox
- Multi-calendar
- Automated and AI-powered messaging
- Task automation
- Automated reviews
- Smart lock integration
- Direct booking capability
- Real-time sync across channels


If you’re running one or two listings and these are all you need, either platform will get the job done.


## **Where they diverge**


The gap opens up fast once you move beyond the basics.


### **Channel reach**


[Guesty’s channel manager](https://www.guesty.com/features/channel-manager/) connects to 60 booking platforms through direct integrations — Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, Expedia, Google Vacation Rentals, Marriott, and dozens of niche marketplaces — plus hundreds more through the Guesty Channel API. Hospitable supports five channels: Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com, Agoda, and Google Vacation Rentals.


More channels means more visibility,[more bookings](https://www.guesty.com/blog/how-to-drive-more-direct-bookings-to-your-str/) , and less dependence on any single platform.

**Revenue and financial tools**


This is where the platforms diverge most sharply. Guesty includes native accounting, trust accounting,[in-house payment processing](https://www.guesty.com/features/guestypay/) , advanced analytics with forecasting, and an embedded[AI-driven dynamic pricing tool.](https://www.guesty.com/features/guesty-priceoptimizer/) Hospitable offers basic metrics on reservations, reviews, tasks, and taxes — and relies entirely on third-party integrations for payments and pricing.


If you’re managing owner relationships, tracking revenue across a growing portfolio, or building a direct booking channel, you need financial infrastructure. Guesty has it built in.


**Automations and workflows**


[Guesty’s automations](https://www.guesty.com/features/automation-tools/) go deeper: custom workflows, smart pricing rules, post-task completion triggers, and compliant accounting automation. Hospitable covers the essentials — messaging, task notifications, reviews, and payments — but customization is limited.


For a business with complex operational needs, the ceiling on Hospitable’s workflows will show up quickly.


**CRM and guest retention**


Guesty includes an embedded CRM with partner integrations (including Mailchimp) to collect and act on guest data — repeat bookings, personalized communication, lead management. Hospitable integrates with Mailchimp for email marketing but doesn’t offer CRM or lead management capabilities.


**Direct bookings and repeat guests are among the highest-ROI levers in this business. A CRM isn’t a nice-to-have.**


## **Which platform fits which operator?**


**Hospitable** is a solid choice if you’re running a small portfolio, you want clean automation without complexity, and your booking strategy stays within the major platforms. It’s designed for simplicity, and it delivers on that.


**Guesty** is built for property managers who are running — or building — a professional operation. Whether you’re at 5 properties or 150, the platform is designed to scale with you. Guesty Lite™ supports portfolios of 1–3 listings with access to Guesty’s core technology at a lower price point. Guesty Pro™ covers larger and more complex operations with advanced customization, multi-unit management, and enterprise-grade reporting. Both tiers come with dedicated onboarding support to get you up and running fast.


The honest question isn’t which platform looks better on a feature list. It’s whether the platform you choose today will still be the right fit when your portfolio doubles.
