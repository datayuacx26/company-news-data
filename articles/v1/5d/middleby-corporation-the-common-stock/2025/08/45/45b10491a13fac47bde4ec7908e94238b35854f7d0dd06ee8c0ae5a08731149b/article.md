At the same time, alcohol-free options like nitro brews, kombuchas, and cold-pressed juices are gaining popularity across every segment, from chain restaurants to college campuses.

That’s where[Middleby Innovation Kitchens](https://www.middleby.com/middleby-innovation-kitchens-mik/) (MIK) can provide great value for operators seeking the right commercial beverage innovations.

### The Brewing Suite: From Grain to Glass


The rise of craft beer and on-premise brewing is transforming how foodservice operators think about their beverage programs. What was once reserved for standalone breweries is now a viable option for a wide range of operations looking to add signature brews, differentiate their offerings, and enhance the customer experience.


At the Middleby Innovation Kitchens, the brewing suite allows operators to bring these ideas to life.[Ss Brewtech](https://shop.middleby.com/brands/ssbrewtech?utm_source=middlebywebsite&utm_medium=learnarticle&utm_campaign=experience-commercial-beverage-innovation-at-the-mik) delivers professional-grade systems that make small-batch brewing both accessible and scalable, which is ideal for first-time brewers or operations looking to add exclusive taps. Deutsche Beverage + Process powers the suite with fully functional 1-barrel and 3.5-barrel brewing systems, complete with fermenters and brite tanks, allowing teams to simulate a real-world brewing process from grain to glass. This suite lets you test recipes, refine production processes, and envision the culinary and branding opportunities that in-house brewing makes possible.
