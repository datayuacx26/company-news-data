# Introducing Middleby Shop: Your Kitchen Quote Hub Is Here


Middleby is modernizing the commercial equipment buying experience. Say goodbye to phone tag, printed catalogs, and long quote requests. **[Welcome to Middleby Shop,](https://shop.middleby.com/?utm_source=middlebywebsite&utm_medium=learnarticle&utm_campaign=introducing-middleby-shop-your-kitchen-quote-hub-is-here)** a quoting platform purpose-built for the foodservice industry.


With Middleby Shop, customers can access Middleby’s full product catalog and request quotes. The website provides start-to-finish support, leveraging channel partners for pricing, delivery, installation, chef consultations, and more. Customers have the flexibility to speak with a live representative via chat or phone, or they can self-serve through guided shopping tools.


## **Elevating A Simple Solution for Complex Products**


The Middleby product portfolio spans an array of commercial restaurant equipment, offering everything from a[panini press](https://shop.middleby.com/commercial-cooking-equipment/commercial-grills/commercial-panini-grills.html?utm_source=middlebywebsite&utm_medium=learnarticle&utm_campaign=introducing-middleby-shop-your-kitchen-quote-hub-is-here) to a triple-stacked[pizza oven](https://shop.middleby.com/commercial-cooking-equipment/commercial-ovens/commercial-deck-ovens.html?utm_source=middlebywebsite&utm_medium=learnarticle&utm_campaign=introducing-middleby-shop-your-kitchen-quote-hub-is-here) . Now, operators can browse the entire Middleby catalog on one site and outfit their entire kitchen with just one quote.


From industry pioneers such as[Southbend](https://shop.middleby.com/brands/southbend?utm_source=middlebywebsite&utm_medium=learnarticle&utm_campaign=introducing-middleby-shop-your-kitchen-quote-hub-is-here) ,[Blodgett](https://shop.middleby.com/brands/blodgett?utm_source=middlebywebsite&utm_medium=learnarticle&utm_campaign=introducing-middleby-shop-your-kitchen-quote-hub-is-here) and[TurboChef](https://shop.middleby.com/brands/turbochef?utm_source=middlebywebsite&utm_medium=learnarticle&utm_campaign=introducing-middleby-shop-your-kitchen-quote-hub-is-here) , to new favorites like Icetro, Marco and Imperial, new product innovations at Middleby are checking all the boxes:


- **Ventless Solutions** – Expand your operation’s footprint beyond traditional kitchen spaces


- **Beverage Service** – Diversify your menu with innovative coffee, cocktail, and soft drink systems


- **Foodservice Automation** – Address labor challenges with intelligent, efficiency-boosting equipment


## **An Evolution in Commercial Restaurant Procurement**


By keeping its channel partners at the core of the business, Middleby Shop delivers a unique experience to its customers. When buying foodservice equipment online, typically, customers are left on their own to figure out the installation, equipment start up and training post-purchase. By connecting the customer with their local product expert, all of these services are quoted upfront in one, transparent quote.


The difference lies in efficiency. Purchasers make their quote requests through Middleby’s approved network of dealers in a streamlined, digital environment. The platform removes friction, eliminates purchasing complexities and delivers a modern online shopping experience while strengthening the role of dealer partners.


## **Why This Matters Now**


The timing couldn’t be more significant. Today’s B2B buyers increasingly expect digital purchasing options that mirror the convenience they experience as consumers. With the foodservice industry facing continued labor challenges and operational pressures, the ability to research, compare, and purchase equipment efficiently is a requirement.
