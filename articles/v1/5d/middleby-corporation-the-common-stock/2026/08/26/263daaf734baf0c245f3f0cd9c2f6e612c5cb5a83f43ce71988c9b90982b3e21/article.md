# Supporting Little City, Making a Big Impact


Published on Aug. 19, 2026


Middleby Cares


Middleby is proud to support Palatine, Ill.-based Little City with equipment for the new Andy’s Café. The Concordia coffee is a hit and our refrigeration keeps everything cool and ice machine ensures cold drinks stay that way. At Little City they do big things, ensuring children and adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities enjoy a vibrant and supportive community.
