# Building Homes, Building Stronger Communities

# Building Homes, Building Stronger Communities


Published on Aug. 19, 2026


Middleby Cares


Middleby Corporate is proud to support Habitat for Humanity, as we helped build a home for a family in our local community. Habitat believes that everyone, everywhere should have a healthy, affordable place to call home. When a family helps build or improve a place they can call home, they are also building a better community, a better life for themselves. Middleby was proud to play a small part in moving their mission forward. Learn more here:[Habitat for Humanity of Northern Fox Valley | Habitat for Humanity](https://www.habitat.org/il/elgin/habitat-humanity-northern-fox-valley)
