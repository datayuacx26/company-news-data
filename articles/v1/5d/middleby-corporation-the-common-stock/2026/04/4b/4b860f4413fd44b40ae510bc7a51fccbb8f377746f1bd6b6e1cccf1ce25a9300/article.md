# Middleby Partners With Ronald McDonald House in Denver


Published on Apr. 15, 2026


Middleby Cares


Middleby is proud to partner with the Ronald McDonald House, supporting equipment upgrades, remodels and new build projects worldwide. In the spring of 2026, we completed the installation of Middleby brands in two Denver-area facilities. This included an Emery-Thompson ice cream maker as part of a specialty dessert area in Aurora to serve families during their stays supporting ill or injured children receiving treatment.
