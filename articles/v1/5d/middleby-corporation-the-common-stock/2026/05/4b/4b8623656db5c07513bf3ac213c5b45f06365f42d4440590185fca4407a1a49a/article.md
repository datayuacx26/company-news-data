# Middleby Runs For A Great Cause at the Chase Corporate Challenge

Team Middleby was proud to participate in the Chicago Chase Corporate Challenge, which supported Association House is a vibrant, inclusive community hub in Chicago that has been cultivating and nurturing the growth of its community for 126 years.


The organization addresses the most urgent needs in local neighborhoods by offering a range of programs under one roof, tackling instability head-on and working in constant communication to support residents.
