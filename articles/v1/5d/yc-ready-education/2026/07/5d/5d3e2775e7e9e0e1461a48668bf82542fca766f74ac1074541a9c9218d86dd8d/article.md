# 4 Reasons Institutions Switch Student Engagement Platforms

Technology plays a critical role in the student experience. The right


[student engagement platform](https://www.readyeducation.com/solutions/student-engagement/) helps students connect with campus life, resources, services, and opportunities throughout their academic journey. It also helps you build community, measure engagement, improve retention, and support student success.


However, many institutions still rely on legacy platforms that struggle to keep pace with the evolving needs of higher education. Common challenges include limited functionality, manual processes, poor support, and a lack of ongoing product innovation.


## **What to Look for in a New Student Engagement Platform**


As student expectations continue to evolve, your institution needs more than a platform that simply manages events and organizations. You need a strategic technology partner that continuously invests in new capabilities, automation, and


[artificial intelligence (AI) analytics](https://www.readyeducation.com/articles/ai-analytics-how-to-make-smart-student-engagement-plans/) .


To succeed, a student engagement platform must be a true enterprise solution. It should integrate with your SIS, ERP, CRM, and LMS. It should provide one place for students, faculty, and staff to communicate, connect, and collaborate across:


- Academics


- Student organizations and clubs


- Social activities


- Leadership and personal development


- Health and well-being


- Athletics


- Career opportunities


- Housing


- Finance


Above all, the platform must be easy to use for students and staff, socially engaging, backed by responsive support, and continuously evolving to meet the future needs of higher education.


Here are the four most common reasons institutions like yours


[upgrade to CampusGroups](https://www.readyeducation.com/articles/how-to-know-when-to-upgrade-your-student-engagement-platform/) , Ready Education’s student engagement platform.


## **Top 4 Reasons Why Institutions Switch Platforms**


### **Reason #1. Limited Community Engagement Features**


A common complaint among institutions is limited functionality. Many legacy platforms focus on a single use case, such as event management, while lacking other engagement tools that your students expect.


Common gaps include:


- A centralized events calendar


- Campus-wide discussion feeds


- Chat and messaging


- Student organization directories


- Mobile-first engagement tools


Without these capabilities, adoption remains low. In some cases, institutions abandon the platform entirely, leaving students without a connected campus experience.


“Our legacy platform was strictly an events app. It did not have features that are key to a


[community engagement](https://www.readyeducation.com/articles/powering-student-success-through-connection-and-community/) platform. Mobile functionality was also extremely limited, which meant that students didn’t use it all that much.”


— Sara E. Thommesen, Senior Coordinator of Engagement, University of Wisconsin-Eau Claire


Institutions that switch student engagement platforms for a more complete solution often see adoption climb quickly, since students finally have one place to find events, groups, and community.


### **Reason #2. Lack of Automation and Reliable Data**


Like many higher ed institutions, your team might struggle with manual processes and disconnected systems. Event management, organization registration, approvals, and engagement tracking often require significant administrative effort.


Some platforms claim to automate these processes, but the reality is often manual workarounds, spreadsheets, and fragmented data. Without reliable data, institutions cannot accurately measure engagement, identify at-risk students, or assess program effectiveness.


“Previously, each of our 9 campuses had its own separate instance of the same student involvement platform. This lack of system integration and automation meant that pulling university-wide stats was very difficult and time consuming.”


— Calvin L. Smith, Jr., Former Senior Director, Leadership Engagement & Experiential Development, Johns Hopkins University


Switching to a platform with built-in automation provides your team with a single place to access your institution’s data, rather than piecing together numbers from separate systems.


### **Reason #3. Poor Performance and Customer Support**


Performance and support issues remain a major reason institutions like yours switch platforms. Administrators frequently report slow systems, lagging performance, and workflows that create inefficiencies rather than reducing them. When issues arise, slow response times and limited support can make matters worse.


A student engagement platform is more than just a software product. It is a long-term partnership that impacts the student experience.


“The customer experience with our previous provider was poor. Performance was a big issue. Sometimes administrators couldn’t physically get their work done in the system due to the lag time. When these issues were raised, technical help and support from the provider was also very slow in their response time.”


A well-run platform with a stellar support team can help save your staff time they’d otherwise spend waiting for technical assistance.


### **Reason #4. Innovation Stalls**


You may be using a platform that has seen little meaningful innovation in recent years. The provider releases new features infrequently, product roadmaps lack transparency, and your administrators have to manage student engagement with technology that no longer reflects student expectations.


Institutions increasingly need mobile-first experiences, real-time analytics, automation, and AI-powered insights. A student engagement platform is a long-term investment. You need confidence that your institution’s technology partner is investing in the future.
