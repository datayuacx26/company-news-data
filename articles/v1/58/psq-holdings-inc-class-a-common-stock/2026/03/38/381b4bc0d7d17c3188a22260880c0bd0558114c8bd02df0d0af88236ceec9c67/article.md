# PublicSquare to Present at 38th Annual Roth Conference

### PublicSquare to Present at 38th Annual Roth Conference


March 17, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s203.q4cdn.com/702081897/files/doc_news/PublicSquare-to-Present-at-38th-Annual-Roth-Conference-2026.pdf)


WEST PALM BEACH, Fla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


PSQ Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: PSQH) (the “Company”), a payments and financial infrastructure company, today announced that Dusty Wunderlich, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, will participate in a fireside chat at the upcoming 38th Annual Roth Investor Conference on Tuesday, March 24, 2026, at 10:30 a.m. PT (1:30 p.m. ET). A link to the webcast will be available at[investors.publicsquare.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Finvestors.publicsquare.com&esheet=54452625&newsitemid=20260317970832&lan=en-US&anchor=investors.publicsquare.com&index=1&md5=807e32c191039ef5301a8a865f0be0e1) . Attendees should log in to the webcast approximately 15 minutes before the presentation starts.


PublicSquare management will also host one-on-one investor meetings with institutional investors at the conference to discuss the Company’s recent financial results, business trends, and growth opportunities.


**About PSQ Holdings**


PSQ Holdings (NYSE: PSQH) is a payments and financial infrastructure company. We build and operate financial infrastructure in highly regulated environments for industries underserved by traditional financial institutions, including businesses, campaigns, and nonprofits that depend on reliable, compliant payment solutions.
