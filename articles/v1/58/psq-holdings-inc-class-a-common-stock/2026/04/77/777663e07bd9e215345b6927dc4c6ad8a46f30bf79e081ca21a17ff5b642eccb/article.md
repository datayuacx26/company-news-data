WEST PALM BEACH, Fla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


PSQ Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: PSQH) (the "Company"), today announced it will host a teleconference and webcast to discuss its first quarter 2026 results beginning at 9:00 a.m. EDT on Thursday, May 7, 2026. The Company will issue a news release containing first quarter 2026 results on May 7, 2026, before the U.S. stock market opens.


The conference call can be accessed live through a link on the PSQ Holdings Investor Relations website at[investors.publicsquare.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Finvestors.publicsquare.com&esheet=54521896&newsitemid=20260423985477&lan=en-US&anchor=investors.publicsquare.com&index=1&md5=3e4924964cc1376a3b8b67593215b0a0) . During the webcast, the company will take both inbound questions received ahead of the call and questions from equity research analysts. Questions may be submitted starting April 30, 2026, through the Say Technologies platform at[app.saytechnologies.com/psq-holdings-inc-2026-q1](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fapp.saytechnologies.com%2Fpsq-holdings-inc-2026-q1&esheet=54521896&newsitemid=20260423985477&lan=en-US&anchor=app.saytechnologies.com%2Fpsq-holdings-inc-2026-q1&index=2&md5=22c830b69b8c115a03277e674d544878) . Attendees should log in to the webcast or dial in approximately 15 minutes before the start of the call.
