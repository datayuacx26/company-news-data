## Our Safety Culture


The safety, excellence and integrity that our drivers demonstrate every day has helped us build a company[culture that puts safety first](https://drivers.jbhunt.com/blog/why-drive-with-j-b-hunt/driver-safety-at-jb-hunt) in everything we do. We believe drivers should be the captain of their ship while they’re behind the wheel, so we provide the training, technology and trust they need to make the best decisions they can out on the road.


Jalen, a regional intermodal driver, has been with J.B. Hunt for almost five years. In his time here, he has been a great representation of our values: integrity, respect, innovation, safety and excellence. And he had an opportunity to step outside of the driver’s seat and put these values into action when he witnessed a motorcycle accident on the road.


Jalen’s safety training and past as a lifeguard allowed him to pull his truck over safely and administer lifesaving first aid until first responders arrived. Jalen’s knowledge and leadership in a time of crisis prevented a tragic outcome and demonstrated a core belief at J.B. Hunt: that people come first. Miranda, Jalen’s operations manager, said that seeing the impact of his actions filled her with pride.


“He likes to make the most out of what he’s doing. He’s the only one that got out of his vehicle to help, and I was so proud to see that happen. He knows the dangers that come with that more personally than someone like me, so he knew instinctively what to do.”


Miranda


Sr. Operations Manager


## Celebrating Our Drivers


You deserve recognition for your part in driving our country forward every day. And more than that, you deserve rewards and appreciation for the milestones you hit throughout your career. It's in our DNA to show our drivers we love them in a big way.


[Driver appreciation](https://drivers.jbhunt.com/culture/appreciation) is a huge part of our people-first culture. Our Million Mile Celebration, company-wide Truck Driver Appreciation events throughout the month of September and service and safety awards are a few ways we reward your achievements. We’re dedicated to recognizing our drivers because we believe they are truly exceptional, and we understand that excellence doesn’t happen by accident – it’s built mile by mile.


## Our Truck Driver Jobs


Life as a truck driver can look different from almost any other career out there. But it could also look like daily home time and weekends off. The truck driver career paths at J.B. Hunt can offer variety,[stability and support](https://drivers.jbhunt.com/benefits/resources) for you, no matter where you are in life.


We have driving jobs available in all of our business units:


- **[Dedicated Contract Services®](https://drivers.jbhunt.com/jobs/dedicated-contract-services)** – Make consistent deliveries along familiar routes to a single customer
- **[Intermodal](https://drivers.jbhunt.com/jobs/intermodal)** – Pick up containers from railyards and enjoy no-touch freight
- **[Final Mile Services®](https://drivers.jbhunt.com/jobs/final-mile-services)** – Find variety with options for home delivery and installation, fulfillment, job site deliveries and more


No matter what business unit you choose,[there are options](https://drivers.jbhunt.com/jobs) for local or regional routes. And with the[Driver Transfer Portal](https://drivers.jbhunt.com/blog/truck-driving-jobs/jb-hunt-driver-transfer-portal) , you have the flexibility to make career changes without leaving J.B. Hunt, meaning[your benefits](https://drivers.jbhunt.com/benefits/company-driver-benefits) can follow you through all of life’s changes.


For Ray, a regional driver, seeing the support and opportunity he’s had in his career has been life changing. Ray began his journey at J.B. Hunt in a warehousing position before becoming a non-CDL-A driver. He then became a Class A driver and made such an impact he was awarded driver of the month. He says that the flexibility and recognition he’s experienced makes him feel valued.


“It’s a huge honor … I just love my job. This year has probably been the best year of my life.”


Ray


Dedicated Contract Services Driver
