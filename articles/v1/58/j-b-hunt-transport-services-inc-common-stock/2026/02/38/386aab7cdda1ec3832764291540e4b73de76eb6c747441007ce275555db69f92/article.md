## Safety Technology


We understand how important safety and efficiency are in your day-to-day operations as a driver. Our driver safety technology is designed with you in mind, and each innovation we make is done to increase your safety and operational efficiency.


By combining our technology with the excellence and dedication of our drivers, we have achieved record-low DOT preventable collisions three years in a row. Innovations like these have transformed our safety culture and empowered our drivers to excel in their careers:


- Advanced adverse weather alerts
- Collision warning system
- Right object detection
- Enhanced rear-view mirror imaging


And with the[J.B. Hunt DRIVE App](https://drivers.jbhunt.com/benefits/drive-app) , our drivers can access almost everything they need in their day-to-day work at the touch of a button. This proprietary technology gives our drivers communication, maps, documentation and training information available directly in the app, along with so much more.


We take driver safety seriously, and every innovation and investment we make in safety technology is made to[give our drivers the support](https://drivers.jbhunt.com/benefits/resources) they deserve.
