## What do you do in a grocery delivery driving job?


Grocery store delivery drivers have a specialized job that involves delivering food products to both small stores and large supermarkets across the country. These jobs can include equipment specialized for specific deliveries, but drivers typically haul a dry van or temp-controlled trailer.


You can[expect safety excellence](https://drivers.jbhunt.com/blog/why-drive-with-j-b-hunt/driver-safety-at-jb-hunt) at J.B. Hunt. And for grocery delivery routes, that includes specialized training in addition to typical onboarding. Drivers on our grocery accounts receive account-specific instruction that can include:


- Safe product handling
- Food safety standards
- Biosecurity protocols
- Compliance with national food safety certifications


Our customers recognize that J.B. Hunt drivers are among the best in the industry, and that trust comes from the dedication to excellence that our drivers demonstrate every day.


Orville, a DCS driver, has been with J.B. Hunt for more than 10 years and has more than one million safe miles. As a grocery delivery driver, he says forming relationships with his customers and seeing that trust build in real time is one of the most rewarding parts of his career.


“Those relationships can make or break your day ... and the friendship and the camaraderie that you put together, you accumulate over time. I really just love what I do.”


Orville
