# MultiSensor AI to Announce Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Results

Houston, Texas--(Newsfile Corp. - March 16, 2026) - MultiSensor AI Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: MSAI) ("MultiSensor AI," "MSAI" or the "Company"), a pioneer[in early threat detection and condition-based monitoring](https://api.newsfilecorp.com/redirect/3K5evheNrz) , plans to report financial results for the fourth quarter and full year 2025 on Thursday, March 19, 2026, after the U.S. stock market closes.


**About MultiSensor AI**


MSAI delivers condition monitoring and continuous early threat detection through a[multi-sensor condition intelligence platform](https://api.newsfilecorp.com/redirect/ZE24JcMqar) for high-throughput, automation-rich, and power-dense industrial facilities. Through a unified edge-to-cloud architecture,[MSAI Connect](https://api.newsfilecorp.com/redirect/an2VLTM0Dv) provides a multi-sensor condition intelligence layer that bridges critical visibility gaps, strengthens system reliability, and improves asset performance. By integrating thermal, visual, vibration, and environmental sensing into a single platform, MSAI detects early signs of mechanical and electrical degradation - enabling organizations to proactively protect uptime, enhance safety, and extend critical asset lifespan.
