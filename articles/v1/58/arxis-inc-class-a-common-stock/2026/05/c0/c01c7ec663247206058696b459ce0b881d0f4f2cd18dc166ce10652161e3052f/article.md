# Arxis Schedules First Quarter 2026 Earnings Release and Webcast

## Arxis Schedules First Quarter 2026 Earnings Release and Webcast


14 May, 2026


[PDF Version](https://ir.arxis.com/node/6866/pdf)


BLOOMFIELD, Conn.


, May 14, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Arxis, Inc. (NASDAQ: ARXS) today announced that it will release its financial results for the first quarter of 2026 after the market closes on Wednesday, May 27, 2026.


Arxis will host a conference call and webcast to discuss its financial results on Thursday, May 28, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. ET. The webcast will be available to the public in listen-only mode.


The webcast link and accompanying slide presentation can be accessed through the Events section of the Company's website at[https://ir.arxis.com/news-events/events](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4688998-1&h=1863558434&u=https%3A%2F%2Fir.arxis.com%2Fnews-events%2Fevents&a=https%3A%2F%2Fir.arxis.com%2Fnews-events%2Fevents) . A replay of the webcast will be available on the Company's website following the call.


**About Arxis**


Arxis is a portfolio company of Arcline Investment Management and a leading designer and manufacturer of proprietary, mission-critical electronic and mechanical components for aerospace and defense, medical technology, and specialized industrial markets. Leveraging significant intellectual property and world-class engineering and operational capabilities, Arxis designs and delivers innovative solutions that address our customers' most complex performance needs.
