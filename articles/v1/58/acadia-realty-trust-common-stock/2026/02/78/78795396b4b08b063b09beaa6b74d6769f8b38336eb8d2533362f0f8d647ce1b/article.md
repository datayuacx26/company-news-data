# Acadia Realty Trust Announces 2026 Annual Meeting Date

RYE, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Feb. 27, 2026-- Acadia Realty Trust (NYSE: AKR) (“Acadia” or the “Company”) today announced the date of the Company’s 2026 Annual Shareholder Meeting.


Acadia’s Board of Trustees has set the meeting date for Wednesday, May 13, 2026 at 1:00 PM, ET. As this will be conducted as a virtual meeting, shareholders will be able to attend, vote and submit questions via live webcast by visiting[www.virtualshareholdermeeting.com/AKR26](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.virtualshareholdermeeting.com%2FAKR26&esheet=54435631&newsitemid=20260227156822&lan=en-US&anchor=www.virtualshareholdermeeting.com%2FAKR26&index=1&md5=3b805a9413efe13e873bac563e3bbc95) . Prior to the meeting, shareholders will be able to vote at[www.proxyvote.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.proxyvote.com&esheet=54435631&newsitemid=20260227156822&lan=en-US&anchor=www.proxyvote.com&index=2&md5=821367d1d6509b6ae9eba434a2a47bcc) .


**About Acadia Realty Trust**


Acadia Realty Trust is an equity real estate investment trust focused on delivering long-term, profitable growth. Acadia owns and operates a high-quality core real estate portfolio (“REIT Portfolio”) of street and open-air retail properties in the nation's most dynamic retail corridors, along with an investment management platform that targets opportunistic and value-add investments through its institutional co-investment vehicles (“Investment Management”). For further information, please visit[www.acadiarealty.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.acadiarealty.com&esheet=54435631&newsitemid=20260227156822&lan=en-US&anchor=www.acadiarealty.com&index=3&md5=ecde3344f2dc14e7a8b25b9b4fcdec3f) .
