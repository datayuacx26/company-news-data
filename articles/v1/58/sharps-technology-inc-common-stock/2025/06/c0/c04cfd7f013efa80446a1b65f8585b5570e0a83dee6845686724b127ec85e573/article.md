# Sharps Technology CEO, Robert Hayes, to Present at the Life Sciences Virtual Investor Forum on June 12 at 9:30AM ET

Published


June 11, 2025


Category


Conference


Source


GlobeNewswire


Summary


NEW YORK, June 11, 2025 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Sharps Technology, Inc. (Nasdaq: "STSS" and "STSSW"), an innovative medical device and pharmaceutical packaging company, today announced that Robert Hayes, CEO, will present live at the Life Sciences Virtual Investor Forum hosted by VirtualInvestorConferences.com, on June 12th, 2025 at 9:30 AM ET.


Full Release


Sharps recently announced that the Company has commenced shipments under three customer orders tied to previously announced purchase agreements. These shipments represent the Company’s first commercial deliveries and its transition to revenue-generating operations.


This will be a live, interactive online event where investors are invited to ask the company questions in real-time. If attendees are not able to join the event live on the day of the conference, an archived webcast will also be made available after the event.
