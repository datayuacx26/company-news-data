# Sharps Technology Becomes SkyAI, Inc. with New Ticker Symbol “SKYA”

SkyAI, Inc. (Nasdaq: STSS), formerly Sharps Technology, Inc., is building the leading Agentic Finance Platform for the Global South. The Company combines stablecoin rails with agentic AI to deliver financial access, education, and actionable intelligence to the billions of underbanked users across Africa, Latin America, and Southeast Asia.


The rebrand from Sharps Technology to SkyAI reflects the Company’s completed strategic transformation from a medical device manufacturer into a technology-forward platform at the intersection of stablecoins, artificial intelligence, and emerging markets. The establishment of overseas operations headquarters positions the Company to execute on its mission of bringing master’s-level financial analysis to every phone in the Global South.


SkyAI’s platform is built on three pillars: programmable stablecoin rails that solve the dollar and infrastructure problem in fragmented markets, autonomous AI agents that execute financial workflows on behalf of users and institutions, and on-chain data intelligence that transforms raw blockchain activity into actionable insight.
