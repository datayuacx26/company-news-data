# How to Boost Testosterone Naturally for Male Fertility

Looking to boost your testosterone levels? You’re not alone.
Testosterone deficiency affects around[20%](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12462828/) of men between 15 and 39 years old. And based on results from the Massachusetts Male Aging Study, low testosterone (aka hypogonadism) impacts up to[2.1%](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12462828/) of men.
Testosterone is vital for so many aspects of male health and fertility. So it’s important to take action if you do have a deficiency. The good news is that there are many accessible ways to increase testosterone in a natural way.
This includes making healthy lifestyle changes to your exercise, sleep, stress management, and diet. You can even consider taking a range of dietary supplements.
Keep reading to learn how to increase testosterone in men naturally!


## Key takeaways


- To improve testosterone levels, consider practices that support overall hormonal balance.
- This can include habits like getting better sleep and exercising regularly.
- Some findings show that certain supplements can have some benefits for testosterone. These include fenugreek, Tongkat Ali, and ashwagandha.
- Other boosters marketed for testosterone have mixed research. Two of these are Tribulus terrestris and D-aspartic acid.
- If you suffer from low testosterone levels or male fertility challenges like erectile dysfunction, it’s important to get medical attention.
- A healthcare provider will help you determine the root cause of your testosterone deficiency.


## What lifestyle changes help boost testosterone naturally?


Lifestyle changes are a great way to improve overall hormonal balance. This may not directly cause an increase in testosterone immediately. But once your hormones are more regulated, you may begin to notice changes to your testosterone levels.
The 4 lifestyle approaches listed below are a great place to start to begin optimizing your hormones and well-being in general.


### **1. Get adequate sleep**


Sleep is a tried-and-true way to support balanced testosterone levels. Your testosterone actually reaches peak levels when you’re[asleep](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18519168/) . Levels are at their highest when you get into REM sleep.


**Here’s what current research has to say about sleep and testosterone levels:**


- Sleeping for only[5 hours](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/1029127) each night for a week can lead to a drop in testosterone levels by 10 – 15%.
- [Sleep deprivation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1087079225000334) and[insomnia](https://blog.inito.com/sleep-and-fertility/) are linked to lower testosterone levels.
- Low levels of testosterone is[connected](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30225799/) to poor sleep and a higher BMI.
- [Obstructive sleep apnea](https://blog.inito.com/sleep-and-fertility/) can impact testosterone levels. And[studies](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/ebiom/article/PIIS2352-3964(26)00038-1/fulltext) show that treating or managing sleep apnea can improve testosterone production.


As you can see from these studies, the bottom line is that sleep disruptions do play a role in testosterone production. But what can be done to prevent (or correct) this?


**Here are some actionable steps to take:**


- Stick to a consistent sleep schedule. This includes when you go to bed and for how long.
- If possible, try to get your sleep in during nighttime hours. (This avoids disrupting your circadian rhythm.)
- Stay away from screens in the hours leading up to bedtime.
- Keep your sleep environment dark and cool.
- Avoid alcohol intake or consuming caffeine in the evening. (Both can disrupt sleep quality.)
- If you have chronic sleep challenges like insomnia or sleep apnea, talk to a doctor. They can help you with treatment options.


**Know more:[Sleep and Fertility: Why Quality, Timing, and Duration Matter](https://blog.inito.com/sleep-and-fertility/)**


### **2. Get plenty of exercise, including resistance training**


Regular exercise is important for overall health. And it’s also a key way to maintain healthy levels of testosterone.


[Moderate to high-intensity](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32297287/) physical activity like high intensity interval training can increase testosterone levels. This boost lasts for up to 30 minutes after exercising. And[strength training](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2796409/) (like lifting weights) can encourage your body to continue producing more testosterone. Mild exercise doesn’t seem to have quite the same effect.


These are the[factors](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41527569/) that seem to have the biggest impact on temporary increases in testosterone:


- Involves larger muscle groups
- Includes multi-joint movements
- Less rest between activity
- Moderate to high in intensity


As noted, these exercise-related changes in testosterone are largely temporary. That said, regular exercise also supports improved muscle mass, hormonal balance, and body mass. So sticking to a consistent exercise regimen is beneficial for healthy testosterone production.


### **3. Keep your stress levels down**


No one wants to feel stressed out all the time. But did you know that stress can actually impact your hormone production, including testosterone?


This is because chronically high levels of stress increase your cortisol levels. And high levels of cortisol can disrupt your body’s ability to produce healthy amounts of other hormones—like testosterone.


In fact,[studies](https://academic.oup.com/jcem/article/111/8/e1951/8663166) show that testosterone dips when your body experiences bouts of:


- Sleep deprivation
- Stress
- Intense psychological strain
- Endurance training without proper nutritional intake


Thankfully, being intentional about your routines can help with avoiding these testosterone disruptions.


**Here are some stress-reducing actions to consider trying out:**


- Exercise regularly
- Get regular, high quality sleep (7 – 9 hours)
- Manage your stress levels. This can involve deep breathing, time in nature, meditation, and other mindfulness practices. (Saying no to non-essential obligations can help too!)


### **4. Consume a healthy diet**


Your diet is another great way to maintain healthy testosterone levels and[hormonal balance](https://web.archive.org/web/20210716135608id_/https://oss.jomh.org/jomh/article/20210409-243/pdf/4-14%20JOMH348.pdf) .
Studies of young men have shown that[eating disorders](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32643144/) (like binge eating) can result in less testosterone.
**A[hormone-balancing diet](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6266690/) that fulfills your nutritional needs usually includes:**


- Quality carbohydrates
- Protein
- Healthy fats
- Enough nutrients and calories to support your energy and hormone needs


If you think you need to make any major changes to your diet, be sure to consult a doctor or licensed dietitian or nutritionist.


Beyond these 4 lifestyle realms, testosterone supplementation may be an option as well. Keep reading to learn about how specific supplements can impact your testosterone levels.

## When to consult a healthcare provider about testosterone levels?


Making lifestyle changes or taking testosterone-boosting supplements can be helpful.
That said, those two steps alone aren’t always enough, especially if your low testosterone symptoms affect your daily life or cause other health problems.


**Here are some[symptoms](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5422691/) that warrant a checkup with your healthcare provider:**


- Low libido or reduced sexual desire
- Erectile dysfunction or sexual dysfunction
- Infertility or difficulty conceiving
- Persistent fatigue or low energy
- Low mood, depression, or difficulty concentrating
- Loss of muscle mass/increased body fat
- Reduced strength or exercise performance


Based on your medical history or blood test results, your doctor may suggest supplements, certain medications, or treatments like testosterone replacement therapy.


Remember, low testosterone is not all that uncommon, and there are steps you can take to see improvements. The sooner you seek support from a licensed provider, the sooner you can come up with a plan for addressing your testosterone imbalance.


## FAQs


What are the symptoms of testosterone imbalance?


Symptoms of testosterone imbalance depend on whether levels are high or low.
Symptoms of[low](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK532933/) testosterone include:


- Low libido
- Fewer spontaneous erections
- Less sleep-related erections
- A reduced testicular volume


For[high](https://www.health.harvard.edu/healthy-aging-and-longevity/testosterone--what-it-does-and-doesnt-do) testosterone (usually from supplements or steroids), symptoms include:


- Acne or fluid retention
- Low sperm production
- Prostate enlargement
- Insomnia
- Headaches
- Increased muscle mass

How to increase testosterone levels?


Making lifestyle changes can help increase testosterone levels. This can include:


- Getting 7 – 9 hours of quality sleep each night
- Taking steps to reduce your stress levels
- Exercising daily (at moderate to high intensity)
- Eating a nutrient-dense and balanced diet
- Supplementing as needed for deficiencies (with physician guidance)


How can I raise my testosterone levels quickly?


There’s no proven way to raise testosterone levels dramatically overnight. Resistance exercise can produce a temporary increase in testosterone after a workout. But long-term improvements depend on consistent sleep, regular exercise, maintaining a healthy weight, and correcting nutritional deficiencies. Treating underlying medical conditions is also important.


How can I get 1000 testosterone naturally?


There’s no guaranteed way to naturally achieve a testosterone level of 1,000 ng/dL. Testosterone levels vary widely between individuals and naturally decline with age. Rather than aiming for a specific number, the goal should be maintaining testosterone within the healthy range for your age and symptoms.


What foods raise testosterone?


No food has been proven to directly raise testosterone. However, foods rich in nutrients that are involved in testosterone production may help support healthy hormone levels. This is especially helpful if you have a deficiency. Some of these foods may include:


- Fatty fish and eggs (high in vitamin D)
- Lean meats and oysters (high in zinc)
- Avocados and spinach (high in magnesium)


How can I raise my testosterone fast?


There’s no true way to raise your testosterone levels fast. That said, diet, sleep, and exercise are great ways to improve your overall hormonal balance. When your hormones are in balance, your testosterone levels are much more likely to be at an optimal level. . Consistency matters though; your daily habits are what create lasting improvements.


Can a man rebuild testosterone?


Yes, in most cases, a man can rebuild his testosterone levels. It’s important to understand the root cause of low testosterone first. But often, changes in diet, exercise, sleep, and stress management can all support hormonal balance. And if needed, supplementation or other treatments can be considered.


What boosts testosterone immediately?


Based on the current status of your hormone levels, there may be no key to boosting your testosterone immediately. That said, the following measures can help:


- Getting consistent quality sleep
- Consuming a healthy diet
- Exercising at moderate to high intensity


Does having sex increase testosterone?


[Evidence](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17320881/) is mixed when it comes to whether or not having[sex increases testosterone](https://blog.inito.com/does-having-sex-increase-testosterone-levels/) . Levels may temporarily change for both men and women, but those increases usually don’t last long.
