# Trying to Conceive? How to Increase LH Levels

Wondering how to increase LH levels? The answer starts with understanding why they’re low.


Luteinizing hormone, or LH, helps your reproductive system work the way it should. In women, LH plays an important role in ovulation. In men, it helps support testosterone production.


Low LH can affect your cycle, fertility, sperm production, and overall hormone balance. Common triggers include chronic stress, over-exercising, poor nutrition, poor sleep, certain medical conditions, or issues with the hypothalamus or pituitary gland.


One common cause in women is functional hypothalamic amenorrhea, or FHA. This stress-related hormone condition accounts for about[30% of secondary amenorrhea cases](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK562219/) in women of reproductive age.


We’ll walk through what LH is, why LH levels can run low, how to support LH naturally for men and women, and when it’s time to talk to a doctor.


## Key takeaways


- Luteinizing hormone helps trigger ovulation in women and supports testosterone production in men.
- Low LH levels can affect periods, ovulation, fertility, sperm production, energy, and sex drive.
- Stress, under-eating, excessive exercise, poor sleep, obesity, medications, and hormone disorders can lower LH.
- There’s no single food, vitamin, or supplement that can reliably boost LH levels.
- Healthy LH levels depend on communication between the brain, pituitary gland, and reproductive organs.
- Lifestyle changes like better sleep, stress management, enough food, and balanced exercise can help.
- Treatments like clomiphene, gonadotropins, or hCG may be used when medically needed.
- If LH stays low or symptoms continue, it’s best to speak with a healthcare provider.


## What is LH?


Luteinizing hormone, or LH, is produced by the pituitary gland, a small hormone-control gland at the base of the brain.


In women, luteinizing hormone helps regulate the menstrual cycle. Its biggest job is triggering ovulation, and after ovulation, it helps support progesterone production.


In men, luteinizing hormone tells the testes to make testosterone. Testosterone matters for sperm production, sex drive, muscle mass, energy, and overall reproductive health.


Women with low LH levels might notice irregular periods or missed ovulation. Men may notice low testosterone symptoms like fatigue or reduced libido. For both, low LH can make fertility more difficult.


Common causes of low LH include:


- Chronic stress
- [Under-eating](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4207953/)
- Excessive exercise
- Obesity
- Poor sleep
- Hormonal imbalances
- Certain medications
- Elevated prolactin levels
- Pituitary disorders like hypopituitarism, pituitary tumors, and[Sheehan syndrome](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK562219/)
- Genetic conditions like[Kallmann syndrome](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK562219/)

## How can you increase luteinizing hormone levels?


There isn’t one food, supplement, or shortcut that can boost LH levels overnight. Healthy levels depend on communication between the hypothalamus, pituitary gland, and reproductive organs.


Lifestyle habits play a big role in hormone production and reproductive health. For most people who want to increase LH levels, that’s the best place to start.


### **Stress management**


[Chronic stress](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11390525/) can cause your brain to reduce the signals needed to produce luteinizing hormone. This can interfere with ovulation, fertility, and overall reproductive health.


Activities like[mindfulness](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38377148/) , yoga, meditation, deep breathing, and relaxation techniques can help regulate reproductive hormones, especially in people with functional hypothalamic disorders.


For women trying to increase LH levels for ovulation, managing stress is often an important place to start.


**Healthy body weight and adequate energy intake**


If you’re wondering how to increase LH levels in females,[energy balance](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK562219/) matters a lot.


When body fat gets too low, or you’re not eating enough for your activity levels, your brain may slow down reproductive hormone signals and cause lower LH levels.


Eating more consistently, getting enough protein, adding healthy fats, and giving your body enough recovery time can all help support healthy LH levels.

### **Sleep and circadian rhythm support**


Inconsistent or disrupted sleep[may interfere](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8764829/) with GnRH secretion, which can affect reproductive hormone signals and LH production.


Prioritize getting[7 or more hours](https://aasm.org/seven-or-more-hours-of-sleep-per-night-a-health-necessity-for-adults/) of restorative sleep each night, maintain a regular sleep–wake schedule, and minimize screen exposure before bedtime.


### **Balanced diet and nutrition support**


No food can directly increase luteinizing hormone. But a[balanced diet](https://blog.inito.com/diet-and-fertility/) can help your metabolism and hormone system work better, which can support healthier LH production over time.


High-fiber foods, healthy fats, protein, and key micronutrients give your body the nutrients it needs for healthy hormone production.


[Fiber](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4025915/) can help regulate estrogen metabolism and support hormonal balance.[One study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00394-011-0207-2) found that dietary fiber can help regulate LH levels.

Omega-3 fats, protein, and micronutrients like zinc and[vitamin D](https://blog.inito.com/vitamin-d-and-fertility/) are also linked with better reproductive hormone function and ovulatory health.

## How to increase LH and FSH levels in males?


For men, increasing LH and FSH levels often starts with improving the factors that affect hormone signaling.


Low LH can reduce[testosterone](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9415259/) production, which may affect sperm production, sex drive, energy levels, and fertility.


Lifestyle habits like poor sleep, chronic stress, overtraining, obesity, and not eating enough can slow down communication between the brain and testes.


## What medications can help increase LH levels?


The right treatment depends on what’s causing your low LH levels. Your healthcare provider may suggest one or more treatment options.


**Clomiphene citrate**


In women, clomiphene helps the brain release more LH and FSH, which can support[ovulation](https://blog.inito.com/what-is-ovulation-induction/) . It may also improve the chances of an[LH surge](https://blog.inito.com/lh-surge-and-ovulation/) . In men, clomiphene helps support testosterone production.


Studies show that about[40% to 45%](https://www.jri.ir/article/120126) of women taking clomiphene become pregnant within six treatment cycles.


**Gonadotropins**


FSH and LH injections may be used when the body isn’t making enough reproductive hormones on its own.


In women, they can help stimulate[follicle growth](https://academic.oup.com/jcem/article/102/5/1413/3077281) and support ovulation during fertility treatment. In men with hypogonadotropic hypogonadism, they can stimulate testicular function and support sperm production.


**Human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG)**


[Human chorionic gonadotropin](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK532933/) , or hCG, acts like LH in the body.


In women, hCG injections are used to trigger ovulation in assisted reproductive treatments. In men with hypogonadism, hCG can stimulate testosterone production and support fertility.

## Can supplements help increase LH levels?


Some supplements and alternative therapies have been studied for reproductive health. However, none have been proven to reliably increase LH levels on their own.


**Vitex agnus-castus (chasteberry)**


Some studies suggest Vitex may influence prolactin and support the[hypothalamic-pituitary-ovarian axis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8459874/) . This may help with luteal phase function and hormonal balance in some people.


A few[studies](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/immunology-and-microbiology/vitex) have also looked at LH and FSH, but the results are mixed. So far, Vitex hasn’t been proven to directly or reliably increase LH levels.


**Maca root**


[Maca](https://blog.inito.com/maca-root-for-fertility/) has long been used as a traditional fertility supplement.[Some studies](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24333960/) suggest it may support fertility and hormone balance, but the research is still limited.


**Know more:[Maca Root for Fertility: What the Science Says](https://blog.inito.com/maca-root-for-fertility/)**


**D-aspartic acid (DAA)**


DAA is a naturally occurring amino acid that’s also available as a supplement. It’s involved in reproductive hormone signaling in the brain and reproductive organs.


[Some studies](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6770039/) suggest DAA may support luteinizing hormone and testosterone production in men. It may also play a role in[follicle development](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17951582/) and egg quality in women, but human research is still limited and inconsistent.


**Acupuncture**


[Some research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6276442/) suggests acupuncture may help regulate reproductive hormones in people with infertility or hypothalamic dysfunction.


However, the results have been mixed, and there isn’t enough evidence to recommend it as a standalone treatment for low LH levels.


While some supplements have been studied, none have been proven to directly and reliably increase LH levels.

## When should you see a doctor?


You should speak with a healthcare provider if you have:


- Missed or irregular periods
- Difficulty getting pregnant
- Symptoms of low testosterone
- Persistently low LH levels on testing


It’s also important to get checked if you have symptoms that may point to a pituitary or hormone-related issue, like severe fatigue, headaches, vision changes, or unexplained hormone changes.


How to increase LH levels starts with understanding what your body needs.


LH can’t be “boosted” by one food, vitamin, or supplement. It depends on healthy communication between your brain and reproductive organs.


Stress, sleep, nutrition, body weight, hormonal imbalances, and medical conditions can all affect LH levels. Depending on the cause, lifestyle changes, medical treatment, or a combination of both may help improve LH.


If your LH levels stay low, speak with a healthcare provider. You deserve clear answers and the right support.


## FAQs


Which vitamins increase LH?


No vitamin has been proven to directly increase LH. But getting enough vitamin D, zinc, and other essential nutrients can support healthy hormone function.


Can low LH levels be fixed?


Yes, in many cases, treating the underlying cause with lifestyle changes, medication, or both can help restore healthy LH levels.


Which food increases LH hormone?


No single food increases LH. Eating a balanced diet with plenty of fiber, healthy fats, proteins, fruits, vegetables, and key micronutrients helps support overall hormone health.


How much LH is needed to trigger ovulation?


There isn’t one number. It’s the LH surge, rather than the exact LH level, that signals ovulation. Ovulation usually occurs about 24–36 hours after the start of the LH surge, and the exact LH value varies among individuals.


How quickly can LH increase?


LH can rise quickly during an LH surge, usually[24 to 36 hours](https://blog.inito.com/lh-surge-and-ovulation/) before ovulation. But if LH is low because of an underlying condition, it may take weeks or months to improve.


What causes low LH in females?


Low LH can be caused by chronic stress, under-eating, excessive exercise, poor sleep, hormonal imbalances, certain medical disorders like hypothalamic amenorrhea, pituitary disorders, hyperprolactinemia, or certain medications.


How can I increase my LH naturally?


Focus on managing stress, eating enough, getting good sleep, maintaining a healthy body weight, and addressing any underlying medical conditions.


Which fruit is best for ovulation?


No fruit directly improves ovulation. But[antioxidant-rich fruits](https://blog.inito.com/28-day-diet-plan-to-get-pregnant/) like berries, oranges, pomegranates, and avocados can support overall reproductive health.


Can I be fertile if my LH is low?


Yes, but it depends on how low your LH is and what’s causing it. Some people with low LH can still conceive, while others experience ovulation or fertility challenges.


How to increase ovulation naturally?


Supporting ovulation starts with healthy habits like eating a balanced diet, managing stress, getting enough sleep, exercising in moderation, and treating any hormone imbalances. These lifestyle measures are most effective when ovulation problems are related to reversible factors such as functional hypothalamic amenorrhea or obesity.
