# AMP Sports Adds Former Packers Pro Bowler Ha Ha Clinton-Dix As Co-Host Of The Tundra – Green Bay Packers Podcast

## **AMP Sports Adds Former Packers Pro Bowler Ha Ha Clinton-Dix As Co-Host Of The Tunddra – Green Bay Packers Podcast**


08.17.2026


**Clinton-Dix Joins Packers Legend John Kuhn; WLUK FOX 11 Sports Director Ryan Wing Also Joins AMP Sports Podcast**


**Green Bay – August 17, 2026** — Sinclair’s AMP Sports today announced former Green Bay Packers safety and Pro Bowl selection Ha Ha Clinton-Dix is joining


**THE TUNDRA: A Podcast on the Green Bay Packers** as co-host alongside Packers legend John Kuhn. Ryan Wing, Sports Director at WLUK FOX 11, is also joining the show, now in its second season, as a co-host.


Beginning August 20, Clinton-Dix will bring fans a firsthand perspective from his five seasons in Green Bay, offering insight into the game, the locker room and what it takes to play for one of the NFL’s most storied franchises. A first-round selection by the Packers in the 2014 NFL Draft, Clinton-Dix emerged as a key part of Green Bay’s secondary and earned Pro Bowl honors in 2016.


Together, Clinton-Dix and Kuhn give The Tundra perspectives from both sides of the ball, pairing Clinton-Dix’s defensive experience with Kuhn’s nine seasons in the Packers offense, which included three Pro Bowl selections and a Super Bowl XLV championship. Wing rounds out the lineup with deep local knowledge and the perspective of a Wisconsin native who covers the Packers and the region’s sports landscape as Sports Director at WLUK FOX 11.


“Ha Ha brings exactly the kind of firsthand experience and personality that makes a show like The Tundra special,” said Pete Gianesini, Director of Network Programming, Podcasts. “He understands what it means to play in Green Bay, compete at the highest level and be part of the Packers organization. Pairing his defensive perspective with John’s experience on offense gives fans tremendous access and insight, and Ryan’s deep connection to Wisconsin sports adds another important dimension to the conversation. We’re thrilled to welcome Ha Ha and Ryan to the show.”


The Tundra is produced in partnership with Sinclair’s WLUK FOX 11 in Green Bay and is part of AMP Sports’ growing portfolio of national and local sports podcasts, bringing together high-profile athletes, coaches, broadcasters and personalities to give fans distinctive perspectives on the teams and sports they follow.


Long-form episodes of The Tundra are available on Apple Podcasts, Spotify and other major podcast platforms, with extended video versions available on YouTube. New episodes are released weekly.


Fans can also follow The Tundra and watch exclusive clips across Instagram, TikTok and X at @TheTundraGB.


**HOST BIOS**


**Ha Ha Clinton-Dix**
Former Packers safety Ha Ha Clinton-Dix built a reputation as one of the league’s most instinctive defensive playmakers during his nine-year NFL career. Selected by the Green Bay Packers in the first round of the 2014 NFL Draft after a standout collegiate career at the University of Alabama, Clinton-Dix quickly emerged as a key contributor in Green Bay’s secondary. He earned Pro Bowl honors in 2016 after recording five interceptions and helping anchor one of the NFL’s top defenses. Later, playing for Washington, Chicago, Dallas, San Francisco, and Denver, Ha Ha brought veteran leadership, versatility, and experience to every locker room. Known for his range, ball skills, and football intelligence, Clinton-Dix remains respected for the impact he made on the field and his commitment to giving back through community and youth-focused initiatives. He is currently the Director of Player Development for the University of Alabama football program.


**John Kuhn**
John Kuhn played nine seasons at fullback for the Green Bay Packers, earning three Pro Bowl selections and was a key member of the Super Bowl XLV Champions as well as five division winners. He is a native of York, Pennsylvania and played his college ball at Shippensburg University. Prior to joining the Packers, Kuhn earned a Super Bowl ring with the Pittsburgh Steelers in 2006 and played for the New Orleans Saints as well before retiring as a Packer in 2019. John currently serves as the sideline reporter for Packers radio broadcasts and as a game analyst on Packers preseason television coverage.


**Ryan Wing**
Ryan Wing is the Sports Director at WLUK FOX 11. Previously, Ryan worked as a videographer/producer for the Badgers Sports Report as well as the weekend sports anchor/reporter at WMTV-TV (NBC) in Madison. Ryan also was the sports director at KTVZ-TV (NBC) in Bend, Oregon where he covered the 2015 U.S. Open at Chambers Bay in Washington and the Oregon Ducks trip to the College Football Playoffs in 2014. His TV career began at WSAW-TV (CBS) in Wausau, WI as a sports reporter/anchor.
