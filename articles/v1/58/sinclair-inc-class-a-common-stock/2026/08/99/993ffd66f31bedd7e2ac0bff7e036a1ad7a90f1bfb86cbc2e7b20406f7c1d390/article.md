# Sinclair Wins Four RTDNA National Edward R. Murrow Awards for Excellence in Local Journalism

[Home](https://sbgi.net/) »[Investor Relations](https://sbgi.net/investor-relations/) »[Press Releases](https://sbgi.net/investor-relations/press-releases/) »[General](https://sbgi.net/investor-relations/press-releases/category/general/)


## **Sinclair Wins Four RTDNA National Edward R. Murrow Awards for Excellence in Local Journalism**


08.13.2026


**BALTIMORE – August 13, 2026** – Sinclair today announced four of its content centers across the country have been honored with RTDNA National Edward R. Murrow Awards, recognizing outstanding achievements in broadcast and digital journalism.


Presented by the Radio Television Digital News Association (RTDNA), the


[Edward R. Murrow Awards](https://www.rtdna.org/2026-national-edward-r-murrow-award-winners) are among the most prestigious honors in journalism, recognizing work that demonstrates excellence in storytelling, reporting and public service.


Sinclair’s 2026 National Edward R. Murrow Award winners are:


**Excellence in Writing**


[Jay Korff Writing Composite](https://youtu.be/iLNlPgHsrUI?si=nefSBdVTnWLNdGAM)
WJLA – Washington, DC


**Breaking News Coverage**


[Confession of a Killer](https://youtu.be/S93YYCmt4F4)
WRGB CBS6 -Albany, NY


**Digital**


[WSET](https://wset.com/news/local/experts-claim-nelson-county-department-of-social-services-neglects-child-safety-concerns-abuse-neglect-communication-commonwealths-attorney-ombudsman-dss-august-2025)
WSET – Roanoke-Lynchburg, VA


**Investigative Reporting**


[Missing Millions: Tax Dollars Vanish](https://nam04.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fyoutu.be%2FY9Y3_d_xrjA&data=05%7C02%7Cjmhart%40sbgtv.com%7Cb1640c9e03e44a5cf1f208de521eaf79%7C897dbc0dc02d43479a713e589c67f8aa%7C0%7C0%7C639038488908508288%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=S4xY%2BxYEIujEL7m2iHXPEmMaNJv4FFeIvSKzcwXMBtM%3D&reserved=0)
KRNV TV- Reno, NV


“These National Murrow Awards reflect the exceptional journalism happening every day across Sinclair’s stations,” said


Scott Livingston, Senior Vice President, News.


“Our cross-platform content centers are designed to produce great journalism for audiences wherever and however they choose to consume it, while giving our journalists the tools, resources and opportunities to pursue ambitious reporting. These awards are a tremendous recognition of the teams who are embracing that mission and setting the standard for impactful local journalism.”


Earlier this year, 12 of Sinclair’s content centers across the country were honored with Regional Edward R. Murrow Awards for Outstanding Journalism. In total, Sinclair brought home a collective 23 regional awards, a testament to the company’s commitment to providing the best in local journalism.
