# AMP Sports Brings Draft Night to Fans With “Cousins Courtside” Vince Carter and Tracy McGrady to Host Live Show and Draft Watch Party on June 23 in New York City

[Home](https://sbgi.net/) »[Investor Relations](https://sbgi.net/investor-relations/) »[Press Releases](https://sbgi.net/investor-relations/press-releases/) »[General](https://sbgi.net/investor-relations/press-releases/category/general/)


## **AMP Sports Brings Draft Night to Fans With “Cousins Courtside” Vince Carter and Tracy McGrady to Host Live Show and Draft Watch Party on June 23 in New York City**


06.08.2026


**NEW YORK** — June 8, 2026- AMP Sports, a division of Sinclair, today announced


**Cousins** ****** **Courtside** , a Draft night experience with NBA legends Vince Carter and Tracy McGrady, co-hosts of the hit podcast


*Cousins* . The live event will take place in New York City on Tuesday, June 23.


Carter and McGrady will bring fans up close for one of basketball's biggest nights with a 30-minute


[Cousins](https://www.youtube.com/@VinceAndTmac) ** live show, followed by a Draft watch party as the two hosts provide live commentary and a Q&A during the live broadcast.


**Cousins** ****** **Courtside** will also stream


**** at


[https://vinceandtmac.com/events/.](https://vinceandtmac.com/events/)


“Vince and Tracy have built


*Cousins* into a destination for basketball fans by bringing the same authenticity, chemistry, and competitive spirit that made them icons on the court.


*Cousins* provides perspective few others can, combining insights of today’s game with firsthand experiences from some of the NBA’s most iconic moments, creating a unique experience basketball fans at all levels can connect with. The NBA Draft has become one of the biggest cultural moments on the basketball calendar. Taking the show live allows audiences to be a part of the excitement in real time and connect with Vince and Tracy in a way that only an event like this can deliver. This will be an unforgettable experience for fans both in person and watching at home,” said Rich Cooke, VP of Audio Programming.


**Cousins Courtside** is presented by Xfinity, with FanDuel serving as an official sponsor.


**Date:** Tuesday, June 23, 2026


**Time:** 6:30 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. ET


**Location:** Sei Less –


156 West 38th Street, NY NY 10018


**Age Requirement:** 21+
