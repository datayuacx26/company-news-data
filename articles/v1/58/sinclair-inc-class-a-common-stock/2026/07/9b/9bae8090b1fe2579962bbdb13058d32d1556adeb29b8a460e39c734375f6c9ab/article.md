**About the Hosts:**


**Candace Parker** has already solidified herself as one of the most influential athletes of this generation. After being selected as the #1 overall pick in the 2008 WNBA Draft by the Los Angeles Sparks, following a champion career at Tennessee, Parker went on to become the first player to earn WNBA MVP and Rookie of the Year honors in the same season, win three WNBA championship titles, and take home two Olympic gold medals. Off the court, first and foremost, she is a wife, mother of three children and three dogs. Since retiring, she serves as a public speaker, activist, entrepreneur, and studio broadcaster for men’s and women’s basketball.


**Aliyah Boston** is a trailblazer in the new generation of athletes. At the age of 12, Aliyah and her sister Alexis moved to Massachusetts from their hometown St. Thomas, USVI to pursue their future in basketball. Aliyah went on to join USA’s national basketball team and became a McDonald’s All-American in high school. After high school, she played for four years at the University of South Carolina where she led their women’s basketball program to their 2nd national championship. Aliyah was drafted as the #1 overall pick in the 2023 WNBA draft to the Indiana Fever. In her rookie season, she became the youngest player ever to start a WNBA All-Star game and went on to unanimously win WNBA Rookie of the Year. Aliyah showcases heart and passion both on and off the court. She is a family-oriented and community-driven individual whose goal is to consistently give back. This goal is demonstrated through her annual Aliyah Boston Basketball Camp in St. Thomas, which draws girls from all of the US Virgin Islands to learn from one of the game’s stars.
