# FNB Proudly Receives Piece of American History with Sojourn 250 Flag

###### COMPANY NEWS


July 08, 2026 - Pittsburgh, PA


Just before dawn on July 4, 2026, The American Legion representatives David Heredia and Kenneth George presented F.N.B. Corporation Chairman, President and CEO Vincent Delie with a commemorative American Flag from the[National Flag Foundation's Sojourn 250](https://nationalflagfoundation.org/sojourn250/) initiative, honoring the Company’s significant support.


Sojourn 250 was a year-long journey of a single American Flag throughout all 50 states, U.S. territories and international U.S. military cemeteries. The presentation at FNB’s corporate headquarters took place minutes before a convoy of hundreds of American Legion motorcyclists departed Downtown Pittsburgh for Washington, D.C., carrying the official Sojourn 250 Flag on the last leg of its journey to join the nation’s semiquincentennial anniversary celebration.


Pittsburgh served as the home of the final full chapter of Sojourn250, hosting a series of community events honoring the people, places and moments that helped shape the nation — from southwestern Pennsylvania’s role in the American Revolution, to its industrial heritage, to its ongoing leadership in innovation, technology and healthcare. More than 40 organizations, government agencies and businesses collaborated to bring the celebrations to life.


In addition to sponsoring Sojourn 250, FNB served as the sole sponsor of Light to Unite, the National Flag Foundation's July 4 initiative that illuminated landmarks and gathering places throughout the U.S. as a visible reminder of the unity, freedom and opportunity represented by the American Flag.


FNB's support of both programs reflects the Company's commitment to the ideals the American Flag represents, including service, sacrifice, freedom, opportunity and respect. During the nation’s 250th anniversary, FNB was honored to help celebrate the shared history and enduring values that continue to unite communities across the country.


**About F.N.B. Corporation** F.N.B. Corporation (NYSE: FNB), headquartered in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, is a diversified financial services company operating in seven states and the District of Columbia. FNB’s market coverage spans several major metropolitan areas, including: Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania; Baltimore, Maryland; Cleveland, Ohio; Washington, D.C.; Charlotte, Raleigh, Durham and the Piedmont Triad (Winston-Salem, Greensboro and High Point) in North Carolina; and Charleston, South Carolina. The Company has total assets of nearly $51 billion and more than 350 banking offices throughout Pennsylvania, Ohio, Maryland, West Virginia, North Carolina, South Carolina, Washington, D.C. and Virginia.


FNB provides a full range of commercial banking, consumer banking and wealth management solutions through its subsidiary network, which is led by its largest affiliate, First National Bank of Pennsylvania, founded in 1864. Commercial banking solutions include corporate banking, small business banking, investment real estate financing, government banking, business credit, capital markets and equipment financing. The consumer banking segment provides a full line of consumer banking products and services, including deposit products, mortgage lending, consumer lending and a complete suite of mobile and online banking services. FNB's wealth management and advisory services include asset management, private banking and insurance.
