# Acadia Announces New Chief Executive Officer

# Acadia Announces New Chief Executive Officer


Posted on


[January](https://www.acadiahealthcare.com/about/news-media-events/2026/01/)[20](https://www.acadiahealthcare.com/about/news-media-events/2026/01/20/) ,[2026](https://www.acadiahealthcare.com/about/news-media-events/2026/)


by


[Acadia Healthcare](https://www.acadiahealthcare.com/about/news-media-events/author/acadia-healthcare)


Acadia is pleased to welcome Debbie Osteen as its new chief executive officer. Ms. Osteen previously served as Acadia’s CEO from December 2018 to March 2022, during which time she led the company through a period of significant progress and evolution, stewarding its strategic vision and overseeing significant growth. Ms. Osteen has also been reappointed to the company’s board of directors, on which she previously served until 2024. Ms. Osteen has held multiple esteemed leadership positions throughout her career, including executive vice president of Universal Health Services, Inc. and president of UHS’ behavioral health division.
