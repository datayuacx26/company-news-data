On August 25, a ribbon-cutting ceremony was held to celebrate the upcoming opening of ECU Health Behavioral Health Hospital in Greenville, North Carolina. The state-of-the-art, 144-bed hospital will offer inpatient and intensive outpatient treatment for children, adolescents, adults, and senior adults.
