# Donaldson Completes Acquisition of Facet Filtration

Donaldson Completes Acquisition of Facet Filtration


We are delighted to share that Donaldson has officially completed the acquisition of Facet following the fulfillment of all closing conditions and regulatory approvals. With more than 80 years of experience, Facet is a globally respected company in fuel and fluid filtration solutions for highly demanding applications. The business brings together over 230 dedicated employees across seven countries and serves customers primarily in Aerospace and Defense, Power Generation, and Hydraulics.


Donaldson and Facet are united by a shared ambition to deliver innovative, high quality filtration products supported by outstanding service. This acquisition represents an important step in accelerating the growth of Donaldson’s Industrial Solutions. Facet’s broad and complementary product portfolio fits naturally with Donaldson’s existing offerings, creating strong opportunities for collaboration, innovation, and long term value for our customers.


"Facet will be an important addition to our company."


Brad Pogalz,


Chief Financial Officer
