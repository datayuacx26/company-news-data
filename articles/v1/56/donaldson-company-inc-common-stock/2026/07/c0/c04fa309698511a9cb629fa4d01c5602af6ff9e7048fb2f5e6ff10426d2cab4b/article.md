# NYSE Closing Bell® Celebration for Donaldson Company

NYSE Closing Bell® Celebration for Donaldson Company


Donaldson celebrated the transition to its seventh CEO, Rich Lewis, in its 111‑year history with members of the executive leadership team ringing the Closing Bell® at the New York Stock Exchange on March 2, 2026


Rich Lewis joined Donaldson in 2002 and became chief operating officer in August 2025. Over the course of his career, he has held a broad range of senior leadership roles across the company, including overseeing global operations as senior vice president and serving as president of both the Mobile Solutions and Life Sciences businesses.


“I am excited about the opportunities in front of us. We have a strong leadership team, a clear strategy, and a culture deeply committed to our customers and operational excellence. I look forward to building on the momentum established under Tod’s leadership and continuing to deliver value for our employees, customers and shareholders.”


Rich Lewis,


President & CEO
