# Donaldson is an Official Partner with the 2026 Special Olympics USA Games

MINNEAPOLIS--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Donaldson Company, Inc. (NYSE: DCI), a leading worldwide provider of innovative filtration products and solutions, announced that it has committed to be a partner of the 2026 Special Olympics USA Games set to take place in June 2026 in Minneapolis, Minnesota.


“Donaldson's partnership with the 2026 Special Olympics USA Games is a testament to their commitment to creating a more inclusive and equitable world,” said Christy Sovereign, CEO, 2026 Special Olympics USA Games. “We are honored to have them join us on this journey to celebrate the power of human potential and inspire a nation.”
