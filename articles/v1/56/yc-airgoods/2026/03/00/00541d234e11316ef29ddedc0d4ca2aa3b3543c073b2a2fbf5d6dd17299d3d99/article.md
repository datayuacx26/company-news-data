# Payment Terms, On Autopilot


### February 2026: Default payment terms, sample reviews, faster quick-add and new categories!


[Aaron from Airgoods](https://substack.com/@aaronfa)


Mar 04, 2026


We shipped several updates this month to make ordering on Airgoods faster and smoother.


As always, these improvements are shaped directly by retailer feedback. If something’s working well – or not – just reply to this email. We respond to every note and use it to guide what we build next.


### Set Default Payment Terms at Checkout


You can now set default payment terms that automatically apply to every order at checkout.


If you adjust terms on an order, you’ll be prompted to save them as your new default – so you don’t have to toggle payment terms each time you place an order. You can update your default anytime in your payment settings.


[Set Your Default Payment Terms](https://airgoods.com/account/buyer/payment)


Need additional payment terms? Apply directly from the same page. We guarantee a decision within 24 hours.


### Improved Quick-Add While You Browse


We redesigned Quick-Add to make adding to cart while browsing faster.


It now takes up less space on product images, so you can browse, scan, and add to cart without opening every product page.


You’ll notice a cleaner, more streamlined experience across marketplace and brand pages.


### Leave Feedback on Your Samples


You can now leave feedback on products you’ve sampled, including brands from the Sample Box Program.


Use reviews to keep notes for yourself – what you liked, what you didn’t, and whether you’d stock it in the future. Your feedback also helps Airgoods better tailor recommendations and rankings to your store over time.


[Leave Feedback on Recent Samples](https://airgoods.com/account/buyer/samples)


### New Categories on Airgoods


We’ve recently expanded Airgoods into three new F&B-adjacent categories:


-


**[Vitamins & Supplements](https://airgoods.com/products?categoryType=Hydration+%26+Supplements)**


-


**[Personal Care](https://airgoods.com/products?categoryType=Personal+Care)**


-


**[Home & Kitchen](https://airgoods.com/products?categoryType=Home+%26+Kitchen)**


You’ll now find a curated selection of essentials – from supplements and skincare to cookware, glassware, and pet accessories – all designed to complement your specialty food & beverage assortment.


Make sure your category preferences are up to date so you see what’s most relevant to your store.


[Update Your Category Preferences](https://airgoods.com/account/buyer/preferences)


We’re continuing to ship improvements every month to make Airgoods faster and easier to use.


More updates coming soon.


The Airgoods Team
