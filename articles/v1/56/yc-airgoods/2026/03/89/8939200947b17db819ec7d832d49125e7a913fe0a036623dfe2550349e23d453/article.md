# Connect to ShipStation in Minutes

# Connect to ShipStation in Minutes


### February 2026: New ShipStation integration, smarter customer segments, clearer payouts, and new categories


[Aaron from Airgoods](https://substack.com/@aaronfa)


Mar 04, 2026


We shipped several updates this month focused on helping you fulfill more efficiently and grow your wholesale business on Airgoods.


As always, these improvements are shaped directly by seller feedback. If something’s working well – or not – just reply to this email. We respond to every note and use it to guide what we build next.


### **New and Improved ShipStation Integration**


We’ve launched a revamped ShipStation integration that’s easier to set up and use.


You can connect your account from the Integrations page in your settings and start syncing orders and samples directly into ShipStation.


[Connect ShipStation](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/settings/integrations)


If you need help getting set up, just reply to this email – we’re happy to walk you through it.


### **Create Customer Segments with More Control**


You can now create customer segments using flexible filters like:


-


**Days since last order**


-


**Amount currently in cart**


This makes it easier to reach the right retailers at the right time – whether you’re following up on churning accounts or nudging buyers with items in cart.


[Create Custom Segments](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/customers/segments)


### **More Transparent Payout Breakdowns**


We’ve made the shipping portion of your payout breakdown easier to understand – especially if you offer shipping discounts or purchase labels through Airgoods.


You’ll now see a clearer breakdown of how shipping charges, label costs, and discounts impact each order payout.


### **New Categories on Airgoods**


Airgoods now includes three new F&B-adjacent categories:


-


Vitamins & Supplements


-


Personal Care


-


Home & Kitchen


If you’ve been holding off on listing products in these areas, they now have a dedicated home on the platform.


We’re continuing to ship improvements every month to help you grow your wholesale business on Airgoods.


More updates coming soon!


The Airgoods Team
