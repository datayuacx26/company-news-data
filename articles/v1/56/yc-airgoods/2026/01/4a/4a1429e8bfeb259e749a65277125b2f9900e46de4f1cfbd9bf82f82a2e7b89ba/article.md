# A Smarter Messaging Experience

# A Smarter Messaging Experience


### January 2026: Messaging upgrades, email controls, and sample box improvements


[Aaron from Airgoods](https://substack.com/@aaronfa)


Jan 30, 2026


Hi there,


We’re bringing back monthly product updates from Airgoods. Going forward, we’ll share a short recap each month highlighting what’s new.


We’re improving Airgoods closely with our sellers. If you have feedback – what’s working, what’s missing, or what feels off – reply to this email letting us know. We use this input directly to shape our roadmap.


Here’s what’s new this month:


### Improved Messaging Experience


We’ve upgraded the in-product messaging experience to give you more context without leaving the conversation.


-


A new side panel shows key details about the retailer you’re chatting with


-


Improved profile images in chat


-


New, richer link previews for orders and samples—making it easier to reference past activity


[Check it out!](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/messages)


This should make it faster to respond, follow up, and stay aligned with your accounts.


### Email Preferences (More Control for Your Team)


You now have full control over which emails each user on your account receives.


-


Add everyone on your team


[here](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/settings/users) who needs visibility into Airgoods activity


-


Customize email notifications so each person only gets what’s relevant to their role (orders, samples, claims, payouts, messages, etc.)


This is especially useful if different teammates handle operations, sales, or finance.


[Manage Email Preferences](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/settings/email-preferences)


### Sample Box Matching, Simplified


If you’ve participated in our sample box program, you know reviewing matched stores can be tedious. We’ve now built this directly into the product so you can:


-


Review matched stores in one place


-


Easily remove stores you already work with


-


Skip the back-and-forth over email


[Click here](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/samples/box) learn more and apply for our sample box program!


We can’t wait to see how these changes will make Airgoods a better platform to grow your wholesale business.


More to come soon!


The Airgoods Team
