# Control Your Store Preferences


### January 2026: Personalized rankings, curated collections, and best seller upgrades


[Aaron from Airgoods](https://substack.com/@aaronfa)


Jan 30, 2026


Hi there,


We’re bringing back monthly product updates from Airgoods. Going forward, we’ll share a short recap each month highlighting what’s new.


We’re improving Airgoods closely with our retailers. If you have feedback – what’s working, what’s missing, or what feels off – reply to this email letting us know. We use this input directly to shape our roadmap.


Here’s what’s new this month:


### Control Your Store Preferences


You can now tell Airgoods what matters to your store – dietary & nutrition needs, cuisine preferences, margin requirements, and more – and we’ll use that to influence how products are ranked across the marketplace.


[Set your preferences](https://airgoods.com/account/buyer/preferences)

### Best Sellers, Upgraded


Our Best Selling page has been revamped!


-


Easier to navigate


-


Rankings expanded from


**25 → 99 products**


-


Each product is now numbered


-


Rankings are based on


**# of orders over the last 30 days**


[View Best Sellers](https://airgoods.com/trending)


### Improved Messaging Experience


We’ve upgraded the in-product messaging experience to give you more context without leaving the conversation.


-


A new side panel shows key details about the seller you’re chatting with


-


New, richer link previews for orders and samples – making it easier to reference past activity


We can’t wait to see how these changes will make Airgoods a better platform for your store.
