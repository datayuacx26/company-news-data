# Retailer Product Changelog – February 9


### Here’s what’s new for retailers on Airgoods this week.


[Aaron from Airgoods](https://substack.com/@aaronfa)


Feb 18, 2026


### **Features**


**Default payment terms**


Retailers can now set default payment terms that apply automatically to all orders at checkout. When you change terms on an order, you’ll be prompted to save them as your default. You can update your default terms at anytime


[in your Payment settings](https://airgoods.com/account/buyer/payment) .


**Feedback on samples**


Retailers can now leave feedback on products they’ve sampled from Airgoods, including items received through the Sample Box Program. Leave feedback on your recent samples in your


[samples dashboard](https://airgoods.com/account/buyer/samples) !


### **Improvements**


**Filtering improvements across the marketplace**


We’ve improved the filtering experience across the marketplace and brand pages. Filters now make better use of screen space and are easier to access – especially on mobile.
