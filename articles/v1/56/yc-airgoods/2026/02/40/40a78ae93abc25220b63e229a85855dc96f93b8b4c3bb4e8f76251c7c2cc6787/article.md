# Seller Product Changelog – February 23

# Seller Product Changelog – February 23


### Here’s what’s new for sellers on Airgoods this week.


[Aaron from Airgoods](https://substack.com/@aaronfa)


Feb 26, 2026


### **Features**


**New categories: Vitamins & Supplements, Personal Care, and Home & Kitchen**


We’ve expanded what independent grocers can stock on Airgoods with three new categories:


-


**Vitamins & Supplements:** Daily vitamins, functional supplements, and wellness staples


-


**Personal Care:** Skincare, haircare, hygiene, and everyday essentials


-


**Home & Kitchen:** Cookware, cleaning products, drinkware, and more


If you’ve been holding off on listing products in these categories, they now have a dedicated home on the platform.


### **Improvements**


**Better visibility into Sample Box timelines**


We’ve improved the


[Sample Box experience](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/samples/box) so you can more easily track key dates, including:


-


When products need to be delivered


-


When retailer match reviews open


This makes it easier to plan inventory and stay on top of upcoming deadlines.


### **Fixes**


**Fixed Shopify integration email links**


We resolved an issue where links in Shopify integration emails could be incorrect.
