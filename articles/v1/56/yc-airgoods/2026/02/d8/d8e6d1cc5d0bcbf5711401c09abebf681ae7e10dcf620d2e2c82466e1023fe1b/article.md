# Retailer Product Changelog – February 23

# Retailer Product Changelog – February 23


### Here’s what’s new for retailers on Airgoods this week.


[Aaron from Airgoods](https://substack.com/@aaronfa)


Feb 26, 2026


### **Features**


**Shop new categories on Airgoods**


We’ve expanded into three new categories – so you can source more of your store in one place:


-


**Vitamins & Supplements:** Daily vitamins, functional supplements, and wellness staples


-


**Personal Care:** Skincare, haircare, hygiene, and everyday essentials


-


**Home & Kitchen:** Cookware, cleaning products, drinkware, and more


Customize what you see (or hide) by


[updating your category preferences here](https://airgoods.com/account/buyer/preferences) !


Does another member of your team handle the purchasing for these categories? Add them to your account here so we can send them a free sample box!


[Invite Team Members](https://airgoods.com/account/buyer/settings/users)


### **Improvements**


**Smarter, more personalized recommendations**


We’ve rebuilt how our recommendations work – so you see more relevant products and brands wherever you browse.


Recommendations now:


-


Adapt to your store’s preferences and ordering behavior


-


Update in real-time based on what you’re viewing


-


Load faster and feel smoother across the app


-


Show more variety (less repetition of the same products and brands)


You’ll see these improvements across the home page, product pages and brand pages.


### **Fixes**


**Improved search navigation**


We fixed an issue where the page could jump after searching – so you now land at the top results every time.


**Fixed order minimum issue at checkout**


We resolved an issue where order minimums could be bypassed when navigating back and forth during checkout. Orders now correctly enforce supplier minimums – so you’ll only be able to check out with valid cart sizes.
