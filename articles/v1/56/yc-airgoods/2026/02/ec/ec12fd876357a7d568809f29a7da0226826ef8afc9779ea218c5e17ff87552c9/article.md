# Seller Product Changelog – February 16


### Here’s what’s new for sellers on Airgoods this week.


[Aaron from Airgoods](https://substack.com/@aaronfa)


Feb 18, 2026


### **Improvements**


**Clearer shipping label error messages**


In-platform shipping label creation errors are now displayed more clearly, making it easier to understand what went wrong and resolve it quickly.


**More transparent payout breakdowns**


We’ve made the shipping portion of your payout breakdown easier to understand – especially if you offer shipping discounts or purchase labels through Airgoods – so you can clearly see how each order payout is calculated.

### **Fixes**


**More reliable payout status updates**


We’ve improved how payout processor failures are handled to ensure your payout data in Airgoods remains accurate and up to date.


**Smoother refunds and discounts**


Adding refunds or discounts to orders is now more reliable, with clearer error messaging if something goes wrong.
