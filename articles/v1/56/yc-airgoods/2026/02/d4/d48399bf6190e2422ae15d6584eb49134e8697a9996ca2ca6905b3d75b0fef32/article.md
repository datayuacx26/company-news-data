# Retailer Product Changelog – February 16

# Retailer Product Changelog – February 16


### Here’s what’s new for retailers on Airgoods this week.


[Aaron from Airgoods](https://substack.com/@aaronfa)


Feb 18, 2026


### **Improvements**


**Improved quick-add while you browse**


We’re upgrading the quick add-to-cart experience on marketplace and brand pages. It’s now more minimal and takes up less space on the product image – so you can scan products more easily and build your order faster without needing to go into each product page.


We are testing this feature with some retailers now, and will roll it out to everyone in the future!


**Faster, smoother home page recommendations**


We’ve improved the performance of recommendation sliders on your home page, making it quicker to see relevant products when you log in.


**Smarter Sample Box auto-pick**


We’ve updated the Sample Box auto-pick to better reflect your store’s preferences—helping surface products that are more aligned with your assortment.


### **Fixes**


**More accurate shipping pricing**


Fixed an issue with shipping profile calculations to ensure you always see the best available shipping rate at checkout.


**Improved search in Sample Box page**


We’ve made the search bar within the Sample Box page easier to use, so you can find and select products more quickly.
