# In-platform shipping labels, order claims updates, and shop by location filters


### Airgoods Product Update – March 11th, 2025


[Airgoods](https://substack.com/@airgoods)


Mar 11, 2025


Hey team,


We have a few new features that we’re excited to share with you! If you don’t see these updates in your account, just refresh the page.


## 1. In-platform shipping labels


*Note: Currently this is only available to Stripe users*


We added the ability to purchase shipping labels for your orders directly in Airgoods!


To create a shipping label, follow the steps you would take to normally fulfill an order, but now you will see an option to “Create Shipping Label.”


To create and print your first label:


1.


Confirm the ‘From’ and the ‘To’ addresses


2.


Confirm the weight of the products included


3.


Select a package and enter the weight


4.


Purchase your label with our


**discounted shipping rates**


The screenshot below is the page you’ll see for these steps.


When purchasing a label, you’ll be given several options, and can select the best carrier option for you and your customer. The cost of the label is passed onto the customer and will be reimbursed to with your payout.


After purchasing the label, you will be navigated to the label detail page where you can print the shipping label in whichever format is most convenient to you. You can also find the option to print the label on the order page itself or on the tracking page.


If you have any questions related to creating, printing, or purchasing labels, feel free to reach out to our team. We’re always open to any and all feedback to improve the Airgoods experience for you!


## 2. Updates to order claims


We’ve updated the way we handle order claims (orders that are missing/damaged/expired). Here’s what you need to know:


1.


**Replacement orders:** Whenever a buyer requests a replacement order because of an issue with their original order, a replacement order with “-R” at the end of it will show as a new entry on your order table. You can fulfill that order the same way you fulfill regular orders.


1.


*Note: Replacement orders currently do not sync with Shopify and ShipStation and must be fulfilled manually*


2.


**Refunds:** When a buyer requests a refund on their order, you can automatically process the refund by going into the report and clicking on “Refund Now”.


3.


**Disputes:** If there is an issue with the refund or replacement request, you can decide to dispute the claim. It is important to note that you have


**10 days** to dispute the claim after it is reported.


This will prompt you to provide an explanation and supporting documents as to why you think the order claim is incorrect or inaccurate. Someone from the Airgoods team will then reach out to resolve it.


### 3. New ‘shop by location’ product filters


We added a few new filters for you to add information to your products. This will allow users to shop by location. We’ve heard from many buyers that they like being able to search for products that are made locally — be sure to add in where your products were made so that buyers see your products when they filter!


You can add these to your product by navigating to your


[Products tab](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/products) , clicking on a product to edit it and then finding the new section on the bottom of the page.
