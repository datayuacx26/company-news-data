# Help Shape Airgoods in 2025 – See What’s New!


### Airgoods product update – March 11, 2025


[Airgoods](https://substack.com/@airgoods)


Mar 11, 2025


Hey team,


We’re excited to share some recent product updates designed to make your Airgoods experience even better. The key updates include:


1.


Expanded pantry categorization


2.


Shop Local discovery tools


3.


Improved order claim process (for damaged / missing / expired products)


We’re also planning what to build for the rest of 2025 – have any suggestions? Please send them over by responding directly to this email.


---


### 1. Expanded Pantry Categorization


We recently expanded our pantry categorization so that it’s easier to find food products on Airgoods.


You can now filter by the following primary categories: Snacks & Confections, Meat, Seafood & Dairy, Spreads & Sweeteners, Cooking & Baking, Grains, Pasta & Packaged Goods.


We also added ‘Hydration & Supplements’ and ‘Kitchen & Barware’ categories.


Let us know if there are other categories you’d like to see!


### 2. Shop Local


We also know that stocking locally made products is an important value for many stores. We just made it easier than ever to find products made nearby – just use our ‘By Region’ marketplace filter to find products made in nearby states or regions.


### 3. Improved Order Claim Process (for damaged / missing / expired products)


We’ve made reporting issues with your order much easier. You can now click on the “Report an Issue” button on your orders page, indicate what the problem was with your order (missing, damaged, or close to expiration) and select how you would like to resolve that issue. You can select one of three options.


1.


🔁


**Replacement:** This will create a new entry in your order table, and you will receive a new tracking number once it ships.


2.


🏦


**Refund to Original Payment Method:** This will refund the payment to your original payment method. Note that this might take 7-14 days to process depending on your payments partner.


3.


💸


**Refund via Instant Credit:** The newest addition is for you to request instant credit to resolve an issue. The credit will be immediately deposited in your account and ready-to-use with any brand on the marketplace. This option is available to buyers who maintain a positive payment reputation on Airgoods.


We’ve also made it easier to report one issue for your whole order (instead of needing to fill out the form product-by-product). You can select the “Entire Order” checkbox to select that all products you’ve ordered have an issue.


You can then select one issue for all the products and provide an explanation and images to support your claim.

## Little Big Updates


-


**Email preferences:** We’ve upgraded our email preferences so you can now get more granular in which emails you or your team receives. Let us know if there are other preferences you’d like to see!


-


**Free shipping filter:** We’ve added a ‘Free Shipping’ filter to our header so you can easily locate all the products on the platform with no shipping costs.


-


**Package tracking in orders table:** We’ve added a new column to your orders table where you can quickly find a status overview for all tracking numbers associated with an order.


-


**Shipping address validation** : We’ve recently added a new shipping address validation flow to reduce the number of mis-deliveries that arise from typos or unique address formats.


-


**New products recommendations email:** We’ve added a new weekly email that sends you personalized product recommendations that we think could be your next best sellers. It goes out on Wednesday mornings!


Thanks for reading! Have feedback or ideas? We’d love to hear from you.


The Airgoods team
