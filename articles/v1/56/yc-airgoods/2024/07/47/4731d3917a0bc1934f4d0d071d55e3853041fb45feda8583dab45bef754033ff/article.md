### **Brand referrals – personalized invites & upgraded tracking**


This month, we upgraded our referral experience to provide you with greater customizability and transparency when inviting brands to Airgoods.


**As a reminder – you earn $50 when an invited brands goes live and $25 for every order they receive in their first 30 days.**


You can now:


-


CC yourself into the invite email by selecting the checkbox below the invite button.


-


Customize the invite to include your own personal message to the brand.


-


Customize all follow-up messages with brands, and opt to be cc’d into those emails.


-


Preview what the full invite email will look like for the brand.


-


Track the brand through their onboarding process so you can forecast future referral credit.


-


Receive email notifications when a referred brands signs-up, launches and a summary at the end of the month with how much credit you earned.


Head over to your ‘Invite a Brand’ tab to check it out!


[Invite a Brand](https://airgoods.com/account/buyer/referrals)

### Little Big Updates


-

-

-
