# 1% cash back on all orders with Airgoods Loyalty


### Airgoods Product Update – July 5th 2024


[Airgoods](https://substack.com/@airgoods)


Jul 05, 2024


Hey team,


We have a few recent product updates that we are excited to share with you. If you don’t see these updates in your account, just refresh the page!


---


### **1. Loyalty Update – 1% Cash Back on All Orders**


This month, we’re updating our loyalty rewards program structure for earning cash back via loyalty rewards.


Going forward, you will earn:


-


1% cash back on ALL ORDERS


-


5% cash back once you hit your loyalty goal each month (you can see this in the


[Loyalty page](https://airgoods.com/account/buyer/loyalty) of your account)


A few other details to keep in mind:


-


You will have access to the credit as soon as an order ships. It’s added to your account automatically, and you can apply it during checkout on any order.


-


You can now view your historical loyalty earnings in your loyalty page.


---


### **2. Marketplace Filters**


(a) We’ve moved the category filters to the side of the marketplace


(b) We’ve added some new filters!


**Cuisine, Margin (including shipping), Certifications & Awards, and Diet.**


(c) You can track your applied filters in one place now too.


---


### Little Big Updates


-


Brand pages with 20+ products now have a search bar and category filter.


-


You can now drag & drop images into damage claims.


-


You can new see average shipping lead time on brand pages.


Thanks for reading! If you need help or have any ideas/suggestions for what we should work on next, just reply to this email!
