# New ways for your products to be discovered on Airgoods


### Airgoods Product Update – July 5th, 2024

Jul 05, 2024


Happy Friday!


Hope you had a great Fourth of July 🇺🇸


We have a few important updates to Airgoods that we wanted to share with you today.


If you don't see this update in your account, just refresh.


---


### 1. New marketplace filters for your products and brand


We recently added several new filters to help your products and brand be discovered more easily. Each of the new fields are listed below:


#### **Product Variables**


1.


**Cuisine**


If your products apply to a specific cuisine, you can know add them to one of the following groups:


*Italian, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Indian, Mediterranean, Latin American, Asian, Middle Eastern, African, Scandinavian, Caribbean, German.*


2.


**Certifications**


If your products have third-party certifications, those can now be indicated on your product page as well. Certifications are officially recognized, and cannot be added unless the product’s packaging indicates the applicable logos and/or labels. The certifications that can be added to your products include:


*Certified Gluten-Free, Certified USDA organic, Certified Vegan, Certified Fairly Traded, Non-GMO Verified, Whole30 Approved*


3.


**Diet** (new options recently added)


This is an existing field, but we recently updated the options. Now you can select from the following:


*Vegan, Gluten-free, Halal, Kosher, Paleo, Keto, Alcohol-free (0.0%)*


4.


**Nutrition** (new options recently added)


We also updated this section, now displaying the following options:


*Organic, High protein, Nootropics, Adaptogens, Mushrooms, Sugar-free, Low sugar, No artificial sweeteners*


In order to add these to your products, head to your


[Products tab](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/products) and use the ‘Edit Product’ option to open the edit product page. You’ll find these four categories on the right side of your page (see image guide below).


#### **Brand Variable**


1.


**Environmental & Social**


We added one brand variable for you to further differentiate yourself in the marketplace. They include:


*Biodegradable, BPA-free, Compostable, Family-owned business, Recycled packaging, Sourced direct from farmers, Supports charity, Upcycled*


In order to add this to your brand, head to your


[My Brand tab](https://airgoods.com/account/seller) and add applicable attributes (see image guide below)


All of these new variables are searchable by buyers in the marketplace using the filters on the left side navigator.


---


### 2. Shipping lead times


We also recently added the ability for you to indicate to buyers how quickly they can expect your products to ship. The more accurate you indicate your shipping time, the better the buyer can time when they should order your products for their stores.


You can indicate your shipping lead time by heading to your


[Shipping tab](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/shipping) and adding it at the top of the page.
