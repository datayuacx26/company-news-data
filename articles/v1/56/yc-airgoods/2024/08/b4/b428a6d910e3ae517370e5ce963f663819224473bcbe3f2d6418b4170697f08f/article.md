# Expanded analytics and new tools to engage your Airgoods Direct accounts

# Expanded analytics and new tools to engage your Airgoods Direct accounts


### Airgoods Product Update – August 23rd, 2024


[Airgoods](https://substack.com/@airgoods)


Aug 23, 2024


Hey team,


We have a few recent platform updates that we are excited to share with you. If you don’t see these updates in your account, just refresh the page!


Separately, we’re in the process of upgrading your ability to create discounts on Airgoods (promotions, discount codes, and volume discounts). If you have specific suggestions or feature requests, feel free to reach out.


---


### 1. Expanded Analytics page


We recently added several new charts and metrics for you to better understand and analyze your sales on Airgoods. Your analytics page now includes:


1.


Total Sales


2.


Customer Breakdown


1.


Total Orders


1.


Number of Cases


2.


Total Samples


Plus, you can now view these metrics by product to compare across your catalog! Check these out for yourself by visiting your


[Analytics tab](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/dashboard) .


---


### 2. New Segment for your Referred Customers with Free Shipping


In your


[Segments tab](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/customers/segments) , you’ll now see a new segment for your referred customers – these are customers that you’ve invited through


[Airgoods Direct](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/referrals) (all customers new to Airgoods that you refer get free shipping for 1 year!).


With this new segment, you can send


*free shipping reminders* to these buyers! Just click on the segment and then “Create Campaign” in the top right corner.


---


### Little Big Updates


-


You can now filter your messages by ‘Unread’ or ‘All’. Check it out in your


[Messages tab](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/messages) !


-


We added a “Delivered” status to the


[Orders table](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/orders) and


[Samples table](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/samples) so you can see when your products have arrived.


-


Fixed a bug in the Airgoods Direct widget for buyers on mobile devices.


-


Fixed a bug that was preventing searches in your


[Products table](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/products) .


Thanks for reading! If you need help or have any ideas/suggestions for what we should work on next, just reply to this email!
