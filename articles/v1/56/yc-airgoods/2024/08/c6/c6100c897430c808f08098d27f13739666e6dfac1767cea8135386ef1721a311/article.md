### **Marketplace Sorting**


This month, we added the ability to sort pages in the marketplace by the following:


-


Featured (personalized to your store)


-


Newest


-


Best Selling


-


Margin


-


Price: Low to High


-


Price: High to Low


Please let us know if there are any other sorting or filtering functions that would improve your ability to discover new products on Airgoods!


[Try It Out](https://airgoods.com/products)


‎


### **Edit Payment Terms & Shipping Address Post-Order**


You can now edit the payment terms and shipping address after an order is placed, up until it’s accepted by the brand.


From the order details page, just click the edit icon next to the payment terms or shipping address to make this change.
