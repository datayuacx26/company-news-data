# New discounting tools, Airgoods Direct update and SEO tags


### Airgoods Product Update – November 6th, 2024


[Airgoods](https://substack.com/@airgoods)


Nov 06, 2024


Hey team,


We have a few new features that we’re excited to share with you! If you don’t see these updates in your account, just refresh the page.


### 1. New Discounting Tools


We added a few new ways for you to offer discounts for your customers on Airgoods. You can now create volume discounts, custom discount codes, and promotions.


**Volume Discounts**


We added the ability to add Volume discounts. These are designed to incentivize buyers to spend more with your brand. You can create a ‘Free shipping over $x’ discount, or create discounts for different ‘tiers’ of spend on the Volume discounts page (pictured below).


Only one Volume subtotal discount will apply at a time, so when someone purchases over the specified threshold for that discount, it will automatically apply. Create your own volume discount


[here](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/discounts/volume) , or go through our


[step-by-step walkthrough](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/discounts/volume?product_tour_id=571401) to learn how to do it.


Once your volume discount is created, it will be visible on your brand page and products throughout the marketplace:


**Discount Codes**


You can now also create your own Discount Codes on Airgoods! They can be applied to the order subtotal, or to the shipping price. You can create a code and send it to any buyer or create a campaign to advertise it. You’ll also find insights on how many times the code was used, and sales it generated, in your


[Codes tab](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/discounts/codes) .


You can customize your discounts by setting a minimum purchase amount, limiting to first-time orders, allowing one-time use only, or making them product-specific.


Get started by creating your first discount code


[here](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/discounts/codes) !


**Brand promotions**


In your


[Promotions tab](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/discounts/promotions) , you can find the existing page for creating Storewide or Product-specific promotions. These are applied automatically to any orders that meet the criteria you set. These promotions are also displayed whenever someone views your product in the marketplace, or views your brand page.


Get started by creating a storewide or product-specific promotion


[here](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/discounts/promotions) !


You can create all of these new discounts under the


[Discounts tab](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/discounts) in your Airgoods account, where you can also see a convenient summary of all your active discounts


The three different discount types can each be applied to an order. So if you have a storewide promotion set and a volume discount set, they will both be applied. The same is true for discount codes and product-specific promotions. If you have any questions about pricing, please don’t hesitate to reach out.


## 2. Airgoods Direct Update


We updated our Airgoods Direct program to make it even easier for you to invite your wholesale contacts and leads to Airgoods. To invite a new or existing retailer, just enter their email and click “Send Invite”.


You can now also customize the email invite you send to your contacts:


Finally, we also improved the way you track your direct relationships. At the bottom of your


[Airgoods Direct page](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/referrals) , you can now see total sales, saved commission, shipping covered by Airgoods, and you can track your customer pipeline.


### 3. Product Tags (SEO)


We added the ability for you to create SEO tags for your products, to make them more discoverable by retailers on Airgoods! This is a great place to add keywords that may be related to your products or brand, so that retailers see your products when searching those words.
