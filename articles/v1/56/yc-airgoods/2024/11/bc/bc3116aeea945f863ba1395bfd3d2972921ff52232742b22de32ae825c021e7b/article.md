# Favoriting products, volume discounts and an upgraded deals page


### Airgoods product update – November 6th, 2024


[Airgoods](https://substack.com/@airgoods)


Nov 06, 2024


Hey team,


We’re excited to share a few recent product updates with you!


If you don’t see these updates in your account, just refresh the page.


---


## Favoriting Products & Following Brands


You now have the ability to favorite products and follow brands! This gives you the option to save products for later if you’re not quite ready to place an order.


**To favorite a product, just tap the heart wherever you see it** ❤️.


**See below for details on finding your favorited products.**


**Check out how to follow brands and find all the brands you follow below.**


We’d love to know if this is helpful! We’ll continue to make it easier for you to find, save, and purchase the products you love.


## Volume Discounts


We’ve added volume discounts to Airgoods! Brands now have the tools to offer you permanent discounts to improve your margins on larger orders. 💰 💸


Specifically, brands can set:


-


Free shipping over a certain order size


-


Subtotal discounts for reaching certain order size tiers


**You can see if a brand has a volume discount set in their brand page.**


**In the marketplace, you can also view volume discounts by hovering over the new volume discount icon.**


**When adding products to your cart, you can track your progress towards reaching volume discounts directly in the product page.**


## New Deals Page


We revamped the deals page to make it easier to find all available


*(and upcoming)* deals on Airgoods. 🥳


This includes: (1) the ability to toggle between a brand view and a product view, (2) search & filter options to tailor your discovery to your preferences, and (3) the ability to follow brands or favorite products that interest you.


[Shop Deals](https://airgoods.com/deals)
