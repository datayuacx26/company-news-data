# ShipStation integration, product collections, and more!


### Airgoods Product Update – December 12th, 2024


[Airgoods](https://substack.com/@airgoods)


Dec 12, 2024


Hey team,


We have a few new features that we’re excited to share with you! If you don’t see these updates in your account, just refresh the page.


## 1. ShipStation Integration


We launched a long-awaited integration with


[ShipStation](https://www.shipstation.com/) , a leading platform for streamlining shipping and order fulfillment!


If you're already using ShipStation to manage your shipping, this integration enables automatic order syncing, shipping status updates, and tracking information to flow smoothly between both systems, helping you save time and reduce manual data entry.


**We have the integration for both products and samples on Airgoods.**


To set up your ShipStation integration, please book a call with our integration team using the button below.


[ShipStation Integration Walkthrough](https://calendly.com/airgoods/airgoods-integrations?month=2024-12)


If you aren’t already using ShipStation but are curious to learn more about how it can help you with order fulfillment and shipping, feel free to schedule a call with our team using the button.


## 2. Brand Page Improvements


**Your brand page got an upgrade!** We’re excited to announce upgrades to our brand pages, designed to make product discovery, re-ordering, and catalog navigation faster and easier than ever. The new design can be seen below (it’s also already live on your own brand page!).


### Easier Navigation


We added some new filters to make it much easier for retailers to find, and re-order your products.


**Top filters**


-


All Products → All your products, sorted by your custom order


-


Best Sellers → Products sorted by those with the most orders over the last 30 days


-


New Arrivals → Products that were added in the last 3 weeks


-


Buy Again → Filter that shows only for buyers that have bought your products before


-


Collections → Custom collections view that you can create


*(more details below)*


-


Search bar → Retailers can easily search for any product


-


Sort (under the search bar) → Retailers can sort your products by (Featured, Newest, Best Selling, Margin: High to Low, Price: Low to High, Price: High to Low)


### Side Filters


These filters will allow retailers to more easily find your products that fit their preferences. The options here show dynamically based on all of your product information.


This is a great opportunity to make sure your product listings have all the relevant information that will help highlight your products. Add relevant filters by going into your


[Products](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/products) tab, and editing a product.


Check out how it looks below!


*Note: The filters are open by default if your brand has more than 10 products.*


### Product Collections


As mentioned above, you can now create custom collections for your products! Collections allow buyers to more easily find your products and serve to group products that go well together.


*Check out how they would look on you brand page!*


*How can I set this up?*


1.


Just go to your new


[Collections tab](https://airgoods.com/account/seller/products/collections) (under Products), and click Create Collection in the top right corner.


2.


Now, just input a Name, Description, Cover Image, and select which products you want in this collection and click Save.


###


### Little Big Updates


-


You can now add shipping rates as a % of product subtotal! This is especially helpful if you have many products with cases that vary a lot in cost. We recommend setting this up to give buyers on Airgoods more shipping cost transparency


-


You can now add your instagram handle to your Airgoods Brand page to allow buyers to check out your Instagram profile. Add it on your My Brand page (see below)


-


We updated how you can add SEO tags to your products. Instead of adding each one individually, just type or paste in a comma separated list of tags you want to add


-


We made some back end improvements to how quickly images load. Your product images on your brand page and in the marketplace should be loading much quicker now!
