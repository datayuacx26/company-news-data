# Airgoods brand pages are now better than ever – See what’s new!

# Airgoods brand pages are now better than ever – See what’s new!


### Airgoods product update – December 12th, 2024


[Airgoods](https://substack.com/@airgoods)


Dec 12, 2024


Hey team,


We’re excited to share some recent product updates designed to make your Airgoods experience even better.


If you don’t see these updates in your account right away, try refreshing the page!


---


## Our Brand Pages Got an Upgrade!


We’re excited to announce upgrades to our brand pages, designed to make product discovery and catalogue navigation faster and easier than ever.


1.


**Collections** Brands can now create collections, making it easier for you to discover curated product groups and browse catalogues efficiently. You will see these popping up over the next few days.


1.


**Filters** Now you can filter brand pages by category, cuisine, dietary needs (e.g., vegan, gluten-free), and much more.


2.


**Best Sellers, New Arrivals, Buy Again**


Easily explore best sellers, new arrivals, and products you’ve purchased before with our new filtering options.


---


## Little Big Updates


-


**Image improvements:** You can now expand images on product pages for a closer look.


-


**Buy Again marketplace filter:** Easily find all the products you’ve purchased in the past.


-


**Mobile invoice downloading:** We’ve resolved a bug that made downloading invoices on mobile tricky for some users.


Thanks for reading! Have feedback or ideas? Reply to this email—we’d love to hear from you. Or visit Airgoods to explore the updates in action.


The Airgoods team
