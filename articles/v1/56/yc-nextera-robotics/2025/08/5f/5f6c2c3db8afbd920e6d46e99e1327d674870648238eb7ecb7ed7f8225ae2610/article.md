# Accelerating Hyperscale Data Center Builds for the AI Era

Data center construction is booming. To meet the infinite demand for AI, Hyperscalers and Builders are tackling extremely ambitious schedules - often delivering multi-million-square-foot advanced buildings in less than a year, a feat that used to take 2-3 years just a decade ago.


For many, **Didge platform and its autonomous fleet have become the key** to delivering **on time and under budget** while **exceeding the highest standards of jobsite safety.**


Didge's advanced AI and robots are hard at work on projects from Arizona to Virginia to North Dakota, providing real-time site digitization and distilling fast-moving, chaotic construction environments into actionable reports and dashboards - with pinpoint accuracy - focusing teams' proactive efforts on what matters most.
