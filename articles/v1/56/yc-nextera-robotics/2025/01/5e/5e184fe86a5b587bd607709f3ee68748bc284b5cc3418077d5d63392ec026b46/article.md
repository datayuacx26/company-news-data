# Nextera Robotics to Present at Health Care Facilities Innovation Conference 2025

We are thrilled to announce that Nextera Robotics has been selected to present at the[Health Care Facilities Innovation Conference](https://www.ashe.org/education/health-care-facilities-innovation-conference) taking place in Columbus, OH on July 27-30, 2025. This opportunity underscores our commitment to revolutionizing construction through cutting-edge robotics and artificial intelligence. We are honored to share the stage with representatives from The Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia and Gilbane Building Co., highlighting our collaborative success on The Morgan Center Project.


# The Morgan Center Project: A Beacon of Innovation


The Morgan Center Project at The Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia stands as a testament to the transformative power of technology in construction. Spearheaded by Gilbane Building Co., this project has embraced the innovative capabilities of our robotic and AI service, Didge, to enhance progress tracking, safety, and precision. Didge has played a crucial role in streamlining the construction processes and ensuring that the highest standards of quality are met.


## Didge: Revolutionizing Construction


Our pioneering service, Didge, integrates advanced robotics and AI to tackle the complexities of modern construction. By automating the collection of data across the entire project, Didge not only accelerates the construction timeline but also enables teams to be take action based on valuable insights. Its sophisticated AI detection gives teams the ability to problem-solving and decision-making with near real-time data available, ensuring that every aspect of the project progresses smoothly and seamlessly.


The collaboration with The Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia and Gilbane Building Co. has been instrumental in showcasing the capabilities of Didge. Together, we have demonstrated how technology can enhance construction efficiency, safety, and quality. The integration of our robotic service into the construction of The Morgan Center has set a new benchmark for future projects, highlighting the importance of innovation in the healthcare construction sector.
