# Full Speed, Zero Incidents: DIDGE.ai on Data Center Builds Across the U.S.

From sunrise to sunset - and through every night shift in between -[DIDGE.ai](http://didge.ai/) is helping deliver high-paced, large-scale data center projects on time and incident-free.


Data center builds are among the most demanding projects in construction today: enormous footprints, compressed schedules, dozens of trades, and multiple crews working around the clock in shifts. On projects like these, no human team can walk, photograph, and verify every area every day - but a[DIDGE.ai](http://didge.ai/) fleet can.


Across data center sites all over the U.S.,[DIDGE.ai](http://didge.ai/) robots autonomously capture the full site at high frequency, and[DIDGE.ai](http://didge.ai/) instantly converts that stream into ground-truth facts: work-in-place progress for every trade, safety observations, and quality flags - prioritized by urgency and delivered to teams through automated reports and live dashboards, shift after shift.


The result is exactly what these high-value projects demand: slowdowns are caught before they become delays, hazards are corrected before they become incidents, and every stakeholder - from the field to the owner - makes decisions from the same unbiased picture of the site. On time, on budget, zero incidents. That's the[DIDGE.ai](http://didge.ai/) standard.
