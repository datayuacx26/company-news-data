# Skanska Spotlights DIDGE.ai Robots on Its Jobsites

We're excited to work with Skanska teams across residential, commercial, industrial, and digital infrastructure projects - and honored to be featured in Skanska's own spotlight on jobsite robotics.


In their post, Skanska highlighted how the DIDGE robot "performs daily site walks, photo documentation and quality/EHS observations - automating a task that once took 20+ hours per week." As Skanska put it, the future of robotics isn't about replacing people - it's about augmenting human expertise with intelligent automation, leading to safer, more efficient, and more predictable builds.


That's the[DIDGE.ai](http://didge.ai/) model in action: robots absorb the repetitive documentation work, while the AI turns every capture into real-time ground-truth insights - progress tracking, safety monitoring, and quality control on one platform - so superintendents and PMs spend their hours building, not photographing.


Skanska's commitment to safety, innovation, and operational excellence is unmatched. It's an honor to collaborate with such forward-thinking teams driving the future of construction.
