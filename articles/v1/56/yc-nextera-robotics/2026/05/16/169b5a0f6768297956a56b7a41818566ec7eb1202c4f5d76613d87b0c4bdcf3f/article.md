# Reliability Wins: DIDGE.ai Live at Oracle's AI and Robotics for the Built World Forum 2026

[DIDGE.ai](http://didge.ai/) took the stage at the Oracle AI and Robotics for the Built World Forum 2026, hosted at the Oracle Construction and Engineering Innovation Lab - presenting, joining the panel, and running a live autonomous demo in front of general contractors, construction executives, owners, and energy leaders.


The demo was the highlight: a[DIDGE.ai](http://didge.ai/) robot navigating the Lab's full-scale indoor jobsite - gravel, block stacks, and steel - entirely on its own, while the platform streamed its captures into live dashboards for the audience. It's the same performance our fleet delivers on real projects: dependable, full-shift autonomy in messy, ever-changing environments, turning every capture into prioritized ground-truth facts that flow straight into Oracle and the other tools crews already work in.


That was the big theme of the day, on stage and off: what builders value most is technology they can count on, solving real problems inside real workflows. Thank you to Oracle for hosting - we left energized and even more confident in where the built world is headed.
