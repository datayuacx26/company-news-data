# Duluth Holdings Inc. to Report Third Quarter 2025 Financial Results on December 16, 2025

MOUNT HOREB, Wis., Dec. 02, 2025 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Duluth Holdings Inc. (dba, Duluth Trading Company) (“Duluth Trading”) (NASDAQ: DLTH), a lifestyle brand of men’s and women’s casual wear, workwear, and accessories, today announced that it will report third quarter 2025 financial results before market on Tuesday, December 16, 2025.


A conference call and audio webcast with analysts and investors will be held on Tuesday, December 16, 2025, at 9:30 am Eastern Time to discuss the results and answer questions.
