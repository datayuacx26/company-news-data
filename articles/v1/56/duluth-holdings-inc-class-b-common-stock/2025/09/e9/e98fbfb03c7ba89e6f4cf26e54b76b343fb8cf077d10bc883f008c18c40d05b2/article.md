# Duluth Trading Strengthens Midwest Footprint, Opens Sixth Minnesota Location in Maple Grove

MOUNT HOREB, Wis.


, Sept. 23, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- Duluth Holdings Inc. (dba, Duluth Trading Company) (NASDAQ: **[DLTH](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4516258-1&h=826527772&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.prnewswire.com%2Fnews-releases%2Fduluth-trading-brings-durable-style-to-kansas-city-with-new-legends-outlets-mall-location-302547807.html%3Ftc%3Deml_cleartime%23financial-modal&a=DLTH)** ) today announced the September 25 opening of its newest retail store in[Maple Grove, Minnesota](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4516258-1&h=747348260&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.duluthtrading.com%2Flocations%2F%3FStoreID%3D068&a=Maple+Grove%2C+Minnesota) . Located in The Shoppes at Arbor Lakes, the store continues Duluth Trading's strong retail presence in the Twin Cities metro area, becoming its fourth location in the region and sixth overall in Minnesota. The new store is located at 12413 Elm Creek Blvd. N.


"Our retail stores are a critical part of our omnichannel approach to bringing this incredible brand to life and have experienced strong momentum this year. We are excited that our second new store to open in a month will be in the northwest Minneapolis suburb of Maple Grove," said Stephanie Pugliese, President and CEO of Duluth Trading. "Minnesota has been a home for our brand since it was founded in Duluth in 1989, and our loyal fan base here continues to grow. With The Shoppes at Arbor Lakes, we're adding a vibrant lifestyle destination where customers can discover our innovative, durable apparel and gear designed for their work and life."


Spanning more than 9,500 square feet, with nearly 7,000 dedicated to shopping, the new store embodies the authentic Duluth Trading style and is stocked for whatever life throws your way. You'll find the latest fall patterns and styles of beloved staples such as men's Fire Hose® pants, women's Heirloom Gardening® bibs, and Buck Naked® underwear next to workshop-inspired fixtures and vintage tools and a fitting lounge with the "Find a Buck" graphic wall.
