# Duluth Trading Brings Durable Style to Kansas City with New Legends Outlets Mall Location

Second Store in State Set to Open on Sept. 11


MOUNT HOREB, Wis.


, Sept. 5, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- Duluth Holdings Inc. (dba, Duluth Trading Company) (NASDAQ: DLTH) today announced the Sept. 11 opening of its newest retail store in[Kansas City, Kansas](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4502924-1&h=4014494573&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.duluthtrading.com%2Flocations%2F%3FStoreID%3D069&a=Kansas+City%2C+Kansas) . Located in Legends Outlets, the store continues Duluth Trading's strong retail momentum and brings the brand's durable, solution-based apparel to one of the Midwest's busiest shopping centers and Kansas's number one tourist destination. The new store is located at 1817 Village West Parkway, Suite S-1, accessible via pedestrian path as well as a newly built entrance next to HomeGoods.


"Our retail stores are a critical part of our omnichannel approach to bringing this incredible brand to life and have experienced strong momentum this year. We are excited to open our first new store since 2021 and are proud that it will be in Kansas City, where we have a lot of longstanding brand fans," said Stephanie Pugliese, President and CEO of Duluth Trading. "Legends Outlets puts us right in the heart of the action — next to Kansas Speedway, Sporting KC, and a community that values hard work and quality. We can't wait to welcome our customers to the store, so they can experience first hand what Duluth is about — our commitment to apparel and gear that is innovative, comfortable and durable."


Spanning more than 16,000 square feet — with nearly 12,000 dedicated to shopping — the new store is built true to Duluth Trading style. Shoppers will find the brand's signature touches throughout, from vintage tool displays and workshop fixtures to two antique showstoppers: a "military mule" M274 and a 1969 Chevy C10 tailgate, both primed for photo opportunities. And because Duluth is built for real life, the store is stocked and ready for fall with fresh colors and patterns in tried-and-true favorites like women's Heirloom Gardening® Bibs, men's Fire Hose® Work Pants, and Buck Naked® underwear for men and women — gear that works as hard as the people who wear it.
