# Aramark to Host Conference Call on Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results

[PDF Version](https://aramark.gcs-web.com/node/24391/pdf)


PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 14, 2026-- Aramark (NYSE:ARMK), a global leader in food and facilities management, announced that it will host a conference call to review its third quarter fiscal 2026 results on Tuesday, August 11, 2026 at 8:30 a.m. ET. A news release containing the results will be issued before the call.


The conference call will be broadcast live on the[Aramark Investor Relations website](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Faramark.gcs-web.com%2Finvestor-overview&esheet=54569651&newsitemid=20260714879751&lan=en-US&anchor=Aramark+Investor+Relations+website&index=1&md5=bddd86652f095acf164b314084d22228) .


Those parties interested in participation via dial-in may register[here](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fnam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com%2F%3Furl%3Dhttps%253A%252F%252Fregister-conf.media-server.com%252Fregister%252FBI3811c94e77d149f08766b8c7b1c0558d%26data%3D05%257C02%257CCleary-Gene%2540aramark.com%257Ccfbee111cb064ccc92e608dec17e8265%257Cb1519f0f2dbf4e21bf34a686ce97588a%257C0%257C0%257C639160946956780698%257CUnknown%257CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%253D%253D%257C0%257C%257C%257C%26sdata%3DDtTGntIg8Iip09XkOG%252BEtcJzy6s2dqujuAs87XN%252FvvM%253D%26reserved%3D0&esheet=54569651&newsitemid=20260714879751&lan=en-US&anchor=here&index=2&md5=6144f76e1e5eea156ea962a909ad91b6) . Once registration is completed, participants will be provided with a number containing a personalized PIN to access the call.


A replay of the call and related earnings materials will be available through the Archives section of the website.
