## Step 2: Run Structured Creative Testing at Scale


Creative is the single biggest lever in Facebook ads performance. A winning creative can cut your CPM by 30% and double your conversion rate. But most teams test too slowly, with too few variations, and without a clear statistical framework.


### How to Structure AI-Driven Creative Tests


Effective creative testing follows a clear hierarchy:


• **Isolate one variable at a time** — test hooks, headlines, visuals, and CTAs in separate experiments so you know what's actually moving the needle


• **Set minimum sample sizes before declaring winners** — 1,000 impressions per variant is a floor, not a finish line; aim for statistical significance at 95% confidence


• **Rotate winning creatives before fatigue sets in** — frequency above 3.5 is a reliable signal that creative fatigue is eroding performance


• **Feed learnings back into production** — document what works (emotional hooks vs. rational benefits, UGC vs. polished video) and brief your creative team accordingly

Your bid strategy should match your campaign objective and margin structure:

## Step 4: Allocate Budget Across Campaigns Dynamically


Static budget allocation is one of the most common and costly mistakes in Facebook ads management. Locking 60% of your budget into a campaign that's underperforming because it was your top performer last month is a guaranteed way to waste spend.


AI-driven budget allocation works differently. It monitors ROAS, conversion volume, and audience saturation across all active campaigns and shifts budget toward the highest-efficiency opportunities in real time.


### Budget Allocation Principles for DTC Brands


• Allocate 70% of budget to proven performers, 20% to scaling tests, and 10% to new audience or creative experiments


• Set automated rules to pause campaigns that exceed your target CPA by more than 20% for 48 consecutive hours


• Increase budgets gradually — 20% increments every 48-72 hours — to avoid triggering Meta's learning phase reset


• Monitor frequency and CPM trends as leading indicators of audience saturation before ROAS drops


Sprites.ai applies these principles automatically, adjusting allocations daily based on live performance data rather than last week's report.
