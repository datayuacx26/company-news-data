## How to Improve Your AI Visibility Score


Tracking your AI visibility score is the starting point. Improving it requires a deliberate strategy. Here's what actually moves the needle:


• **Build authoritative content that LLMs cite.** AI models pull from high-authority sources. Publishing detailed, well-structured content on topics relevant to your category increases the likelihood that LLMs reference your brand.


• **Earn mentions from trusted third-party sources.** Reviews, press coverage, and analyst mentions all feed into how LLMs perceive your brand's authority. The same signals that build domain authority also build AI visibility.


• **Optimize your structured data and entity clarity.** LLMs respond well to clear entity definitions. Make sure your brand, products, and key people are described consistently and accurately across your website and external sources.


• **Monitor competitor citations and close the gap.** Use an AI visibility checker to identify which competitors are being recommended in your category and reverse-engineer why. Sprites.ai surfaces this data automatically.


• **Update content regularly.** LLMs favor fresh, accurate information. Stale content loses ground over time. Treat AI visibility optimization as an ongoing process, not a one-time project.
