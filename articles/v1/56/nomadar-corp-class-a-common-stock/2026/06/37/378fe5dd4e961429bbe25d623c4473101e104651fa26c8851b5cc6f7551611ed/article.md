# Nomadar Added to Russell Microcap® Index

# Nomadar Added to Russell Microcap® Index


by[Press](https://nomadar.com/author/press/)


| Jun 30, 2026


|[News](https://nomadar.com/category/news/) |[0 comments](https://nomadar.com/nomadar-added-to-russell-microcap-index/#respond)


Nomadar Corp. (NASDAQ: NOMA) has been added to the Russell Microcap® Index as part of the 2026 Russell U.S. Indexes reconstitution, marking an important step in the Company’s growing presence in the public markets.


The Russell Microcap® Index is a widely recognized benchmark for the microcap segment of the U.S. equity market and is followed by institutional investors, investment managers, mutual funds, ETFs, and other market participants focused on emerging growth companies. According to FTSE Russell, approximately $10.6 trillion in investor assets are benchmarked to, or invested in, products based on the Russell U.S. Indexes.


Nomadar’s inclusion comes as the Company continues to execute its strategy across sports tourism, destination development, experiential infrastructure, technology-enabled engagement, and international youth sports initiatives. The Company has been classified within the Consumer Discretionary sector, reflecting its position across several global consumer-driven markets.


Since beginning trading on the Nasdaq Capital Market on October 31, 2025, Nomadar has continued expanding into new markets, strengthening international programs, and advancing the digitalization of its methodology, services, and business lines. These initiatives are part of the Company’s broader strategy to build an integrated global ecosystem across sports, tourism, technology, and health.


“This inclusion comes during a transformative period for Nomadar,” said Joaquin Martin, CEO Americas and Global Vice Chairman of Nomadar. “As we continue expanding across sports tourism, experiential infrastructure, and international youth sports initiatives, we believe our addition to the Russell Microcap® Index increases awareness of our business model and strengthens our visibility within the investment community.”


Nomadar continues to advance strategic initiatives across its global platform, including international sports tourism events, new geographic markets, the development of the JP Financial Arena multipurpose event center, and innovative consumer engagement technologies. Together, these initiatives are designed to expand addressable markets, diversify revenue opportunities, and reinforce the Company’s long-term growth strategy.


Membership in the Russell Microcap® Index remains in place until the next applicable reconstitution.
