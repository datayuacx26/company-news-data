# Nomadar Presenting at the 3rd Annual DealFlow Discovery Conference


21 Jan, 2026


|[Press Releases](https://investor.nomadar.com/category/press-releases/)


**MARSHALL, TX / January 12, 2026 /** Nomadar (NASDAQ:NOMA) (“Nomadar” or the “Company”), a U.S.-based company operating at the intersection of sports, technology, tourism, and health, today announced that it will participate in the **DealFlow Discovery Conference, taking place January 28-29, 2026** .


**Joaquin Martin** , CEO Americas & Global Vice Chairman of Nomadar will deliver a company presentation and be available for one-on-one investor meetings throughout the event. Learn more about Nomadar at[www.nomadar.com](https://pr.report/hor0)


Event Details:


- 3rd Annual DealFlow Discovery Conference
- The Borgata Hotel, Casino & Spa
- Atlantic City, NJ
- January 28-29, 2026


Investors interested in scheduling a meeting with the Nomadar management team should request an investor pass to attend the conference (no cost to attend).
