# Nomadar and SID Collaborate to Support International Football Talent Between Asia and Spain

# Nomadar and SID Collaborate to Support International Football Talent Between Asia and Spain


by[Press](https://nomadar.com/author/press/)


| May 19, 2026


|[News](https://nomadar.com/category/news/) |[0 comments](https://nomadar.com/nomadar-and-sid-collaborate-to-support-international-football-talent-between-asia-and-spain/#respond)


Nomadar has signed a collaboration agreement with SID (Spanish International Development), an organization specialized in international football development programs with a presence in Hong Kong and Macau.


Through this agreement, SID will be able to connect international players and sports projects with Nomadar’s high-performance football programs, especially:


- High Performance Training (HPT)
- Football Trials in Spain
- International football camps
- Professional education and training methodology programs


SID is recognized for delivering immersive football development experiences and creating pathways for players through collaborations with Spanish clubs and professional football environments.


As the first activation of this partnership, player Yee Sun NG is currently in Cádiz undergoing a Trial and academy assessment within the Cádiz CF system until May 23rd.


In addition, both organizations are already discussing future international projects, including a possible Strength & Conditioning and professional training methodology camp in Macau.


This agreement marks a new step in Nomadar’s international presence and reflects its ongoing commitment to global football talent development.
