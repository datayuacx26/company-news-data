# 2M Local News Stories on this Indian Website

One of the leading jobs, hyperlocal news[android app Lokal](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=get.lokal.localnews) which has been downloaded more than 5M times has now launched a website where more than 3000 news stories are published on an average daily.


Now you can read hyper local news not just on the[Lokal app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=get.lokal.localnews) but on the websites as well-


[Lokal Telugu – https://telugu.getlokalapp.com/](https://telugu.getlokalapp.com/)


[Lokal Tamil – https://tamil.getlokalapp.com/](https://tamil.getlokalapp.com/)


The website provides information around news, jobs, matrimony, real estate listing, etc in some of the farthest districts and constituencies in[AP](https://telugu.getlokalapp.com/andhra-news) ,[Telangana](https://telugu.getlokalapp.com/telangana-news) and[Tamil Nadu](https://tamil.getlokalapp.com/tamilnadu-news) . Its the only website which provides last-mile news for more than 1700+ districts, constituencies and mandals of Andhra, Telangana and Tamil Nadu.


The website provides minute by minute updates on local happenings along with in-depth analysis of issues that matter to the people of Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Tamil Nadu.


The website’s content is generated through stringers and users and moderated thoroughly before it hits the deck running.


Along with the app and website, the company is backed by Y combinator, 3one4 Capital, Y Combinator, RB Investments, SOMA Capital and existing investor India Quotient among other investors.


It is just not a website for people in South India. It’s a one-stop-shop for local needs for everyone.
