# How to Sell Your HDB Resale Flat in Singapore? (2026 Guide)

Last Updated on June 5, 2026 byYolly Del Prado


**TL;DR / Summary:**
– Your flat must have met its **MOP (five or ten years from key collection, not purchase date)** before you can list it for sale.
– The I **ntent to Sell is valid for 12 months** — if you don’t submit the resale application within that window, you’ll need to re-register.
– Buyers must have a **valid HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter** before you can grant the OTP.
– The **OTP locks you in for 21 calendar days** — no other offers can be accepted during this period.
– The HDB resale process typically takes **eight to twelve weeks** from application to completion.


[Selling your HDB flat](https://ohmyhome.com/en-sg/selling-property-singapore/sell-hdb?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=clickthru&utm_campaign=SellHDBGuide) involves a few key stages: checking your Minimum Occupation Period (MOP), registering your Intent to Sell, finding a buyer, sorting out the Option to Purchase (OTP), and submitting your HDB resale application. There are specific timeframes for each step too, so knowing what comes next really does save you from unnecessary stress (and delays).


The good news? Once you see the full picture, it’s a lot less intimidating than it sounds. We’ve outlined the entire HDB resale process below, so you can move through each stage with the right timing, the right price, and a lot less guesswork.

## Before Selling Your BTO Flat or Resale HDB Flat


Before you get the ball rolling and put your flat on the market, it’s crucial to check if you’ve fulfilled your MOP. If you’re looking to sell your BTO flat or resale HDB, do note that the five- or ten-year period only kicks in from the day you collect the keys, and that any prolonged time you are not residing physically in your HDB flat (for studies or work abroad, for example) is not included in the MOP calculation.


Of course, life can sometimes throw a curveball, and if you need to sell your HDB flat before your MOP is up, you might be able to[appeal to HDB](https://ohmyhome.com/en-sg/property-guides/how-to-appeal-to-sell-your-hdb-before-mop/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=backlink&utm_campaign=SellHDBGuide) .


You might also be happy to know that selling a relatively new BTO flat often yields the highest value in the current market.


## Step 1: Register Intent to Sell


To officially get the ball rolling,[register your Intent to Sell](https://ohmyhome.com/en-sg/blog/how-register-intent-sell/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=backlink&utm_campaign=SellHDBGuide) . To do that, you need to:


1. Go to the[HDB resale portal](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/web/bp28/TimeLine/my-flat-dashboard)
2. Click “I am a Buyer or Seller”
3. Log in with your Singpass


Upon registration, you’ll receive:


- An instant assessment of your eligibility to sell
- The[Ethnic Integration Policy](https://ohmyhome.com/en-sg/blog/what-you-need-know-about-hdb-ethnic-quota/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=backlink&utm_campaign=SellHDBGuide) /Singapore Permanent Resident (EIP/SPR) Quota for your block
- The status of upgrading and billing of upgrading costs
- Recent transacted prices of nearby HDB flats


**IMPORTANT: Your HDB Intent to Sell is valid for only 12 months (1 year)** , so you need to submit the HDB resale application within that time. Otherwise, you’ll have to submit another application.

3. If the buyer intends to get a bank loan, they must have a Letter of Offer before they can exercise the OTP.


## Step 5: Grant Option to Purchase to HDB Buyer


Upon registering your Intent to Sell, you will have a seven-day cooling period before you’re allowed to grant the OTP. You can[download a copy of the HDB-prescribed OTP form](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/webapp/BB24OTPDlWeb/BB24POptionToPurchase.jsp) on the HDB resale portal.


**Remember: Once you have issued an OTP to a buyer, you cannot accept any other offers for about 21 days.** (This is the same amount of time a buyer has to exercise the OTP. More on this below.)


Also, signing the OTP means that you officially accept the buyer’s offer to purchase your flat, so make sure you’re satisfied with the deal before doing so.


After granting the OTP, you may collect an Option fee from the buyer, which is non-refundable should the buyer not go through with the sale. The fee can range from $1 to $1,000, though the market norm these days is $1,000.

## Step 7: Buyer Will Exercise the OTP and Pay the Option Fee


After you’ve agreed on a price with the buyer and discussed any Temporary Extension of Stay terms, the buyer can now exercise the OTP and send you the exercise fee payment. This payment, along with the Option fee, will form the deposit.


The deposit cannot exceed $5,000. Therefore, if the buyer paid $1,000 for the Option fee, the exercise fee should be less than $4,000.


Again, the OTP is only valid for 21 days and must be exercised within that period. Buyers have up to 4 p.m. on the 21st day (weekends and public holidays included) after the date of OTP issuance to exercise the OTP.


### What If the OTP is Cancelled?


If the buyer chooses not to proceed with the purchase, the OTP will lapse and expire. You can then restart the viewing process with other prospects.

## Step 8: Submit HDB Resale Application


Whew! You’re finally on the last leg of the[HDB selling process](https://ohmyhome.com/en-sg/selling-property-singapore/sell-hdb/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=clickthru&utm_campaign=SellHDBGuide) . At this stage, you and the buyer may complete the resale application on the[HDB resale portal](https://services2.hdb.gov.sg/web/bp28/TimeLine/my-flat-dashboard) . But here’s the catch: You and the buyer have to submit your applications separately within seven days of each other, so you’ll have to agree on a timeframe to execute this.


Here’s a list of details you’ll need to provide when filling out the HDB resale application form:


1. Resale flat address
2. OTP details
3. Soft copy of OTP
4. Your personal particulars


**To submit your** **HDB resale** **application, you’ll have to pay an administrative fee** **based on the property** :


- 1- and 2-room flats: $40
- 3-room and bigger flats: $80


You can check the status of your application on the HDB resale portal.

## Step 10: Endorse HDB Resale Documents


You will receive an SMS from HDB within 10 working days informing you when you can accept the terms and conditions for all the documents prepared through the HDB resale portal. You’ll have to endorse all the HDB resale documents within six days of receiving the SMS. Once it’s been endorsed, you will receive an in-principle approval for your HDB flat resale.

### The HDB Flat Inspection May Also Happen at This Stage


Technical Executives from an HDB branch will inspect your flat for any structural damage and unauthorised modifications. This is to ensure that your apartment does not compromise the integrity of the entire building or the safety of the new flat owners.


If anything gets flagged during the inspection, you can’t complete the HDB resale transaction until the flat’s original state has been restored. Should you be out of the country and unable to attend this inspection, you can have another person attend on your behalf, such as a[Power of Attorney](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/residential/buying-a-flat/buying-procedure-for-resale-flats/resale-application/application) .

## Step 13: Prepare for the Resale Completion Appointment


Ok, last few steps!


To prepare for your HDB resale completion appointment, you’ll need to:


1. Fix any unauthorised renovation work.
2. Terminate automated GIRO payments and settle outstanding payments like property tax (payable up to the end of the year) or service and conservancy charges (payable up to the day of the resale completion).
3. Prepare to vacate your flat before the date of completion so the buyer can move in on time.
4. After vacating, ensure that you’re ready to hand the keys over to the buyer during the completion appointment.

## Step 14: Attend Resale Completion Appointment


This is the last thing you’ll have to do to complete your HDB selling process — attend the HDB resale Completion Appointment at HDB Hub. During this appointment, you will witness the following:


1. Signing of the transfer document of the resale flat
2. Signing of the mortgage document/agreement (only applicable for HDB loans)
3. Handover of keys (if the buyer opted for a bank loan, the bank’s lawyer could be authorised to collect the keys on the buyer’s behalf)


You will receive your net sale proceeds at this point. HDB will refund any CPF monies to your CPF account within 10 to 14 working days from the date of the completion appointment.


However, do take note that the HDB resale application will be cancelled if any of the following occur:


1. You or the buyer does not go through the sale or purchase of the flat for any reason whatsoever.
2. You or the buyer withdraws your application by giving notice in writing to HDB.
3. You do not have sufficient CPF savings or cash to finance the purchase of the resale flat.
4. The information given in the resale application by you or the buyer is incorrect, or any information has been omitted by either party.


You or the buyer is not eligible to retain the resale application under HDB’s policies.

## FAQs About Selling Your HDB Resale Flat


### How long is the process or timeline to sell HDB flat?


The entire process of selling an HDB flat typically takes about eight to 12 weeks from the date the OTP is granted to the completion of the sale.


### How long does it take for HDB to approve resale?


HDB usually takes around eight weeks to process and approve the resale application after you and the buyer have submitted all necessary documents.


### Do I need an agent to sell my HDB flat?


While it’s not mandatory to have an agent to sell your HDB flat, many choose to engage one for their expertise, market knowledge, and to handle the paperwork and negotiations.


### Can I sell HDB during MOP?


No, you cannot sell your HDB flat during the MOP, which is typically five years. You must complete the MOP before selling your flat.


### How much goes back to CPF after selling HDB flat?


The amount to be refunded to your CPF account includes the principal amount withdrawn for the property purchase, plus accrued interest, which is calculated at 2.5% per annum.


### How much deposit do you pay for an HDB resale?


The buyer typically pays a deposit of up to 5% of the purchase price upon signing the OTP. This includes an option fee (up to $1,000) and an exercise fee (up to $4,000).
