# Which HDB Town Has the Most Million-Dollar Flats? (2026 Data)

Last Updated on July 17, 2026 byYolly Del Prado


**TL;DR / Summary:**


– Toa Payoh has sold 748 million-dollar HDB flats as of July 2026, the most of any town in Singapore.
– 929 flats have crossed $1 million in 2026 alone, bringing the all-time total to 4,953.
– Chua Chu Kang, Jurong West, Sembawang, and Tengah have never had a single million-dollar sale.


You’ve probably seen the headlines. Some HDB flat somewhere sold for over a million dollars, *again* . What most of those headlines skip is that it’s basically always the same handful of towns doing this. **Toa Payoh has the most** , by a wide margin: **748 flats have sold for at least $1 million as of July 2026** . The next town on the list isn’t even close, less than half that number.


And this isn’t some rare, once-in-a-while thing anymore. **929 HDB resale flats have crossed the million-dollar mark in 2026 so far** . Go back to the very first one in 2012 and add them all up, and you get 4,953 total. Sounds like it’s everywhere, right? It’s not. Some towns are packed with these sales. Others have had exactly zero.


So which camp is your town in? Let’s find out.


***Disclaimer:** Figures and rankings in this article are accurate as of time of publication (July 2026) and are based on available resale transaction data. Property prices and market standings may change over time.*

## Why These Towns Keep Showing Up


It comes down to a familiar mix. These are mostly mature estates, which means they’ve had decades to build up demand, strong MRT connections, and a reputation that buyers are willing to pay for.


And it’s not only about the town itself. Many of the flats crossing $1 million are also larger units, sitting on higher floors, with longer remaining leases than what you’d find in an older block down the road. Put those together in a town with limited resale supply, and prices climb fast.


It’s not really a mystery why Toa Payoh and Bukit Merah keep topping these lists. They check almost every box a buyer is chasing, all at once.


## The Towns With Zero Million-Dollar Flats


Now for the flip side. Four HDB towns have never had a single flat cross the million-dollar mark, as of July 2026: **Chua Chu Kang** , **Jurong West** , **Sembawang** , and **Tengah** .


That doesn’t mean flats there are cheap. It just means these towns are still catching up. They’re newer, further from the city centre, and most of their flats haven’t hit the point where big units, high floors, and long leases start piling up in one place.


Give it a few years. As more flats here clear their Minimum Occupation Period (MOP) with leases still comfortably above 90 years, it’s only a matter of time before one of these towns shows up on this list.


## Ranking High (or Low) Doesn’t Decide Your Flat’s Price


Living in a town near the top of this list doesn’t automatically mean your flat is worth seven figures, and living in one at the bottom doesn’t mean it isn’t worth a good price either. A million-dollar sale still comes down to the specific unit. Floor level, remaining lease, flat size, and how close you are to an MRT station all matter more than which town you’re in.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### Why do some smaller HDB flats sell for more than bigger flat types?


Smaller flats can outsell bigger ones when they sit in premium locations that rarely turn over on the resale market. A handful of 3-room flats have crossed $1 million with average prices higher than 5-room units, simply because so few of them exist in sought-after estates. Scarcity ends up mattering more than square footage.


### If I’m buying for long-term value, should I target a top-ranked town like Toa Payoh?


Not necessarily, since a high ranking reflects past sales, not guaranteed future growth. Towns like Toa Payoh may already be near their ceiling for certain unit types, while a newer town with rising MRT connectivity could have more room to grow. Ranking tells you where the demand has been, not where it’s headed next.


### How much does floor level actually affect a flat’s price in these transactions?


Floor level plays a bigger role than most buyers expect, sometimes adding well over $100,000 to an otherwise similar unit. Higher floors usually come with better views, less noise, and more privacy, which buyers are willing to pay a premium for. Two flats with identical size and lease can still land in very different price brackets.


### Which town is likely to be the next to join this million-dollar list?


Towns with rising MRT connectivity and flats nearing key lease milestones are the most likely next entrants, though no single town is guaranteed. Places like Yishun or Woodlands, both already inching close with recent high sales, are worth watching.
