# Rhonda Wong Shares Ohmyhome Singapore’s New Chapter on CNA938’s Open for Business

Last Updated on July 31, 2026 byYolly Del Prado


Our founder Rhonda Wong recently sat in an interview with CNA 938. She was given the opportunity to talk about recent news involving our company, in relation to the exit from the listed company Ohmyhome Limited.


In the interview, she clarified the situation that had risen from several news reports.

## Key Interview Highlights:


- On the issue of the sale of Ohmyhome Singapore, Rhonda pointed out that the sale was entirely the decision of the board of the listed company.
- Race and Rhonda had already sold their entire interest in the listed company and relinquished control before the debt waiver and sale were approved.
- She also noted that it was the listed company’s new owners who decided to exit the property business.
- Race and Rhonda stepped in to preserve the Singapore operation, its employees and the services provided to its customers.
- Ohmyhome Singapore continues to operate today, serving homeowners and providing employment to 160 people.
