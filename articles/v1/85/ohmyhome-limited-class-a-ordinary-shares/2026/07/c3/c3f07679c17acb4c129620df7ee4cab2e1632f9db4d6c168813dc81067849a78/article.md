# Sim Lim Square Will Be Managed by Ohmyhome Property Management Starting 1 August 2026

Last Updated on July 22, 2026 byYolly Del Prado


[Ohmyhome Property Management (OPM)](https://pm.ohmyhome.com/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=clickthru&utm_campaign=OPMManageSimLimSquare) will officially manage Sim Lim Square starting 1 August 2026, adding one of Singapore’s most recognisable IT and electronics malls to its growing portfolio. It’s a milestone that shows just how far OPM’s reach now extends, from residential condos to large-scale commercial landmarks.


## 25 Years of Keeping Estates Running


Ohmyhome Property Management, or OPM, is[Ohmyhome](https://ohmyhome.com/en-sg/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=clickthru&utm_campaign=OPMManageSimLimSquare) ‘s property management arm, the team behind the scenes making sure estates run the way they should, day in and day out. Over the past 25 years, it’s built a track record managing more than 10,000 units across residential estates, strata-titled properties, malls, and other establishments, all while keeping costs efficient through a tech-powered approach to daily operations.


That’s the same team now taking on Sim Lim Square.


## What Managing Sim Lim Square Involves


Sim Lim Square has been a fixture at 1 Rochor Canal Road since 1987, known across Singapore as the go-to mall for IT, computers, and electronics. The complex spans 6 storeys and 2 basement levels, housing more than 400 shops selling everything from laptops and cameras to phones, audio gear, and computer parts.


Running a mall of this size means managing heavy daily footfall, keeping security tight given the value of stock moving through it, and responding quickly whenever tenants run into issues, since every hour of downtime affects their business.


Starting 1 August 2026, OPM steps in to manage all of that: the footfall, the security, and the day-to-day needs of hundreds of tenants on behalf of the Management Corporation (MCST). The scope covers facility management, tenancy management and leasing, financial administration, security, and regulatory compliance.


It’s a slightly different set of demands from a residential estate, but it’s one OPM’s systems and team are already built to handle.

## Frequently Asked Questions


**Does switching to OPM as the estate manager affect existing tenant leases at Sim Lim Square?**


No, a change in estate management doesn’t affect existing lease agreements, since those are separate contracts between tenants and the mall’s landlord or owner. OPM’s role covers day-to-day operations like maintenance and upkeep, not the terms tenants signed on.


### What’s the difference between a managing agent like OPM and a management council or MCST at a property like Sim Lim Square?


A managing agent like OPM handles the day-to-day operations on behalf of the management council, while a management council or MCST is the governing body that oversees and makes decisions on behalf of owners. OPM works under the direction of Sim Lim Square’s management council, running maintenance and tenant requests, while major decisions still sit with the council itself.


### Is Ohmyhome Property Management (OPM) a separate company from Ohmyhome, or part of the same business?


OPM is Ohmyhome’s property management arm, not a separate company, operating as the team handling estate operations under the wider Ohmyhome business. This means Sim Lim Square’s management ties back to the same company known for property agency services, just working through its dedicated property management division.
