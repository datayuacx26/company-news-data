## Frequently Asked Questions


### Can I sell my HDB flat if I’m overseas?


Yes, you can sell your HDB flat from overseas, though it does require more coordination since viewings, paperwork, and buyer communication need to happen without you physically there. An agent who can manage viewings and buyer questions on your behalf becomes even more important when you’re not around to handle it yourself.


### How is HDB resale pricing affected when a flat has been sitting on the market a long time?


A flat that’s been sitting for months doesn’t need a price cut by default, but buyers browsing older listings do tend to assume something’s off, even if it’s just presentation. That perception alone can suppress offers. Addressing why it’s been sitting, whether that’s photos, marketing reach, or pricing, tends to matter more than the raw number of days on market.


### Does listing on more property portals actually bring more real buyers?


More portals mean more visibility, but visibility alone doesn’t guarantee real buyers, since traffic and genuine interest aren’t the same thing. What matters more is whether your listing reaches people actively searching in your specific estate and flat type, not just anyone scrolling. Reach without relevance tends to inflate views without moving the needle on actual offers.
