## Begin with a Clear Buying Plan


Every milestone Singapore has reached started with a plan, and yours can start the same way.


Before arranging property viewings, it is important to understand your budget, preferred property type and essential requirements. A clear buying plan can help you avoid spending time on properties that do not suit your goals.


Consider the following before starting your search:


- Your estimated purchasing budget
- Your available cash and CPF funds
- Your financing options
- Your preferred property type
- Your ideal neighbourhoods
- Proximity to transport, schools and workplaces
- Your preferred property size and layout
- Your desired purchasing timeline
- Your long-term plans for the property


It may also help to divide your requirements into essentials and preferences. Your budget, financing, and ideal location are usually essentials, since they affect your daily life and your finances directly. Things like unit layout or a specific facility are often preferences, nice to have, but not deal-breakers.


This approach can help you evaluate properties more objectively and recognise a suitable opportunity when it becomes available.

## Make Every Property Viewing Count


Progress looks different up close than it does from a distance, and a home is no different. Online listings provide a useful first impression, but a physical viewing allows you to understand the property more clearly.


During a viewing, consider:


- The condition of the property
- The layout and use of space
- Natural lighting and ventilation
- Noise levels
- Privacy
- Storage space
- Renovation requirements
- The condition of shared facilities
- Nearby transport and amenities
- The overall environment of the estate or development


It can be useful to take notes after every viewing.


A home that looks attractive online may not suit your daily routine. Another property may appear simple in photographs but offer the right location, layout and environment for your needs.
