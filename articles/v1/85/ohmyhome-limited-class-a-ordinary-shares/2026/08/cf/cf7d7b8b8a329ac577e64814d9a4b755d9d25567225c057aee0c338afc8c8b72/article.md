# Does Ghost Month Affect Singapore’s Property Market?

Ghost Month can affect different parts of a property journey, from when families choose to sign property documents to when they renovate or move in. But does it also affect property sales?


Ghost Month runs from 13 August to 10 September in 2026. Since the dates change each year, we looked at previous Ghost Month periods to see whether sales followed a consistent pattern.


They did not. Property activity rose in some months and fell in others, with project launches, interest rates and buyer sentiment also shaping the figures.


We examined three parts of Singapore’s property market from 2023 to 2025:


- New private home sales
- Resale condo and apartment transactions
- HDB resale transactions


Together, these figures offer a broader view of how different parts of the market performed during Ghost Month.

## What Happened to New Private Home Sales During Ghost Month?


Here, “new private home sales” refers to private residential units sold by developers, excluding executive condominiums. These figures can be particularly sensitive to the number and timing of project launches.


Ghost Month also fell on different dates each year, so the August figures are not a like-for-like comparison.


**Year** **Ghost Month** **July sales** **August sales** **September sales**


**2023** 16 August to 14 September 1,412 394 217


**2024** 4 August to 2 September 571 208 401


**2025** 23 August to 21 September 940 2,142 255


The results were different each year. To understand why, we need to consider the number of projects and units launched during the same months.


### 2023: Sales Fell as the Launch Calendar Became Quieter


Ghost Month ran from 16 August to 14 September in 2023.


New private home sales fell from 1,412 units in July to 394 in August, before falling again to 217 in September.


However, **the number of homes launched also dropped sharply** . Developers launched 2,156 units in July and 590 in August. September had no major project launch. The only new project introduced was the 23-unit Shorefront development.


According to CBRE,[68% of August’s new private home sales took place during the first half of the month](https://www.cbre.com.sg/press-releases/cbre-commentary-on-monthly-new-home-sales-for-august-2023) , before Ghost Month began. Developers also did not introduce any new projects after 16 August.


Ghost Month may have influenced the timing of some launches and purchases. However, higher interest rates, cautious market sentiment and the much smaller number of newly launched homes also contributed to the slowdown.


### 2024: New Home Sales Slowed Before Recovering in September


Ghost Month ran from 4 August to 2 September in 2024, covering almost the entire month.


Developers sold 208 new private homes in August, down from 571 in July. There were no major project launches during the month.


Several factors may have contributed to the decline:


- A lack of major launches
- Some buyers waiting because of Ghost Month
- High interest rates
- Cautious market sentiment


New private home sales then recovered to 401 units in September, after Ghost Month ended on 2 September.


The launch of 8@BT and the release of additional units from six existing projects gave buyers more newly released options. JLL also noted that the larger-than-expected interest rate cut on 18 September may have improved buyer sentiment.


Therefore, the recovery cannot be attributed solely to the end of Ghost Month. It also coincided with[more units being released and changing borrowing conditions](https://www.jll.com/en-sea/newsroom/private-residential-units-sold-by-developers-in-september) .


### 2025: Popular Launches Pushed Sales Higher


August 2025 tells a very different story.


Developers sold 2,142 new private homes, more than double the 940 sold in July, according to[CBRE’s August 2025 new private home sales report](https://www.cbre.com.sg/press-releases/cbre-commentary-on-monthly-new-home-sales-for-august-2025) .


Five projects were launched that month:


- Springleaf Residence
- River Green
- Promenade Peak
- Canberra Crescent Residences
- Artisan 8


These projects gave buyers many more new homes to consider and helped lift monthly sales.


However, this does not mean Ghost Month had no effect. The seventh lunar month only began on 23 August, after several of the projects had already held their launch weekends.


Sales then fell to 255 units in September, when most of the month fell within Ghost Month.


However, September also had no new project launches. Developers released only 20 new units for sale, all from the previously launched project The Myst. This was much lower than the 2,496 units launched in August.


Buyers could still purchase remaining units from existing projects, but there were no major new launches to generate the same level of interest seen in August. Therefore, the fall to 255 sales cannot be attributed to Ghost Month alone. It also coincided with a sharp drop in the number of newly released homes.


## What Happened to the Condo Resale Market During Ghost Month?


New private home sales can change significantly depending on what developers launch. To see what happened outside the developer market, we also looked at resale activity for private condominiums.


**Year** **Ghost Month** **July sales** **August sales** **September sales**


**2023** 16 August to 14 September 854 905 731


**2024** 4 August to 2 September 1,099 1,084 1,040


**2025** 23 August to 21 September 1,036 1,091 1,119


*Source: 99.co-SRX condo resale reports. In these reports, “condo” includes non-landed private condominiums and apartments.*


The pattern differed across all three years.


In 2023, condo resale activity rose from 854 transactions in July to 905 in August, before falling to 731 in September. It declined gradually from July to September in 2024. However, in 2025, it increased across all three months, even though most of September fell within Ghost Month.


This does not prove that Ghost Month had no effect on individual buyers. Some transactions recorded during the period may have resulted from viewings and negotiations that began earlier.


However, the figures show that condo resale activity did not consistently decline during Ghost Month.


## What Happened to HDB Resale Transactions During Ghost Month?


HDB resale activity provides another view because it covers existing homes in the public housing market.


Like condo resale activity, it did not follow the same pattern every year.


**Year** **Ghost Month** **July transactions** **August transactions** **September transactions**


**2023** 16 August to 14 September 2,052 2,467 1,974


**2024** 4 August to 2 September 3,036 2,591 2,204


**2025** 23 August to 21 September 2,562 2,203 2,174


***Source:*** *Ohmyhome’s calculations based on*[Resale flat prices based on registration date from Jan-2017 onwards](https://data.gov.sg/collections/189/view)


Transactions rose from July to August in 2023 but fell over the same months in 2024 and 2025. Meanwhile, September 2025 was almost unchanged from August, despite most of September falling within Ghost Month.


However, these figures need to be read carefully. A buyer may view the flat, agree on the price and exercise the Option to Purchase before the transaction appears in HDB’s data. Therefore, the registration month may not be the same month in which the buyer decided to purchase.


The figures cannot tell us exactly how Ghost Month affected buyers’ decisions. What they show is that HDB resale activity did not follow one consistent downward pattern during Ghost Month.


## Does the Data Show that Ghost Month Slows Singapore’s Property Market?


The figures do not show a consistent market-wide slowdown during Ghost Month.


New private home sales were closely connected to the number and timing of project launches. Meanwhile, condo and HDB resale activity moved differently from one year to another.


Ghost Month may influence when some buyers proceed, when families move in and when developers launch projects. However, other factors also matter, including:


- The number and types of homes available
- Interest rates and borrowing costs
- Buyer budgets and loan eligibility
- Market sentiment
- Asking prices
- The condition and location of available homes


The figures do not mean Ghost Month has no effect. They simply show that it is difficult to separate its influence from everything else happening in the market.


## Does Ghost Month Cause Property Prices to Fall?


**Property prices do not automatically fall during Ghost Month.** The figures above show changes in sales volumes, but they do not prove that Ghost Month causes[home prices to decline](https://ohmyhome.com/en-sg/blog/why-singapore-private-homes-selling-at-loss/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=backlink&utm_campaign=DoesGhostMonthAffectSGPropertyMarket) .


A property’s price depends on factors such as:


- Location
- Condition
- Remaining lease or tenure
- Floor level
- Nearby amenities
- Supply of similar homes
- Recent transaction prices


A buyer’s budget, loan eligibility and stamp duties also affect how much they can pay.


## Can Buyers Get a Better Deal During Ghost Month?


Sometimes, but a discount is not guaranteed.


A seller who needs to sell quickly may be more willing to negotiate if fewer buyers are interested. However, a reasonably priced home in a popular location can still attract several offers.


A seller who is not in a hurry may also choose to wait instead of accepting a lower price.


Rather than expecting a “Ghost Month discount”, buyers should compare the asking price with recent transactions and check the home’s condition. Repairs, renovation needs and other costs should also be considered.


A quieter viewing may give you more time to inspect a property. It does not automatically make the property a bargain.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### Does Ghost Month affect Singapore’s rental market too?


It may influence when some tenants prefer to move, but rental activity also depends on factors such as location, asking rent, available units and lease timing.


### Do property developers stop launching projects during Ghost Month?


Not always. Some developers may adjust their launch schedules, but projects can still launch before or during Ghost Month. The timing often depends on project readiness, market conditions, competing launches and the number of units available.


### Does Ghost Month fall on the same dates every year?


No. Ghost Month follows the lunar calendar, so its dates change each year. This makes month-to-month comparisons less straightforward because the period may cover different parts of August and September.
