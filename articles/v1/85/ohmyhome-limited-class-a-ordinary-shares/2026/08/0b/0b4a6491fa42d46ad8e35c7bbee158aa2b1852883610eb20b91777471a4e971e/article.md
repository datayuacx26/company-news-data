# Hungry Ghost Month 2026: How to Keep Your Property Plans Moving

Ghost Month in 2026 runs from 13 August to 10 September, with Zhong Yuan Jie, or the Hungry Ghost Festival, falling on 27 August. During this period, wandering spirits are traditionally believed to roam among the living, giving rise to customs around activities some people prefer to avoid.


For some households, these beliefs extend to property, from starting renovations and moving into a new home to signing contracts and viewing empty homes at night.


Of course, how closely these traditions are followed differs from one household to another. But if you prefer to hold off on certain property milestones, your entire plan doesn’t have to go on pause.


Here are four ways you can use the time to prepare for what comes next.


## 1. Avoiding Night Viewings During Ghost Month? Make Your Daytime Viewing Count


Some people prefer **not to view empty properties at night during Ghost Month** , as dark, vacant spaces are traditionally believed to attract wandering spirits.


If you observe this belief, **let your property agent know so viewings can be arranged during the day** . More importantly, **use the daylight to learn something useful about the home** .


Look at how much natural light enters different rooms and whether strong direct sun reaches certain parts of the unit. At the same time, check the walls and ceilings for water stains or cracks, inspect the fittings and look for signs of repairs that could mean additional costs later.


Then, look beyond the four walls. Open the windows, listen for traffic or surrounding noise and consider how much privacy you would have from neighbouring units.


So rather than simply changing[when you view the property](https://ohmyhome.com/en-sg/blog/6-things-you-need-do-when-viewing-property/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=clickthru&utm_campaign=HungryGhostMonth2026Prepare) , use the opportunity to take a closer look at what you may be buying.


## 2. How to Prepare If You Plan to Move In After Ghost Month


**Moving into a new home is another milestone some families prefer to avoid during Ghost Month** , as doing so is traditionally believed to risk inviting unwanted spirits into the home.


If you would rather move in later, **the preparation around moving day can still begin now** .


Plan when utilities and internet services need to be activated, pack all the things you’re bringing into your new home, and[book professional moving services](https://ohmyhome.com/en-sg/home-services/moving-services/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=clickthru&utm_campaign=HungryGhostMonth2026Prepare) ahead of your preferred move-in date. This gives you time to discuss the arrangements and what help you may need with packing and transportation.


If you are buying furniture or appliances, you can also plan when everything needs to arrive.


Doing this earlier gives you some breathing room if plans change. A delivery may be delayed, renovation works could take longer than expected or you may discover something that needs fixing before you move in.


In other words, your preferred move-in date can be the finish line you are working towards, rather than the day you suddenly start organising everything.


## 3. What Can You Do If You’re Delaying Renovations During Ghost Month?


Hacking, drilling and other noisy **renovation works are among the activities some homeowners prefer to avoid during Ghost Month** , as the noise and disturbance are traditionally believed to disturb wandering spirits.


If you would rather start the physical works later, however, there is plenty you can settle first.


Use the time to[consult an interior design and renovation firm](https://www.thedreamrproject.com/) and start working through your plans. Together, you can discuss and refine the design and scope,[align on your renovation budget](https://ohmyhome.com/en-sg/blog/how-to-save-on-renovation-costs-in-singapore/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=clickthru&utm_campaign=HungryGhostMonth2026Prepare) , explore suitable materials and finishes, and work out what needs to be settled before your preferred start date.


This extra time is also useful for questioning decisions before they become expensive to change. Does the proposed layout work for how you actually live? Which features are worth spending more on? Have you left enough room in the budget for unexpected costs?


That way, waiting to start the works does not mean waiting to make progress.


## 4. Use the Extra Time to Get Your Property Finances in Order


Some people also prefer to avoid **signing property contracts or completing a purchase during Ghost Month** , as the period is traditionally considered less favourable for major commitments.


If you would rather wait before signing, **use that time to take a closer look at the numbers first** .


Start with your upfront costs, then consider what the property could cost you each month. How much cash and CPF might you need? What could your monthly housing payments look like? And how much financial buffer would you have left afterwards?


Remember that the purchase price is not the only expense involved. Depending on your situation, you may also need to account for stamp duties, legal fees, renovation, moving expenses and other costs.


If you need to[sell your property](https://ohmyhome.com/en-sg/property-agent-services/seller/book/address/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=clickthru&utm_campaign=HungryGhostMonth2026Prepare) before buying your next home, this is also a useful time to understand its estimated value and how the sale could affect your budget for the next purchase.


If you are already giving yourself more time before making a commitment, **use some of it to make sure the financial side works for you too** .

## Frequently Asked Questions About Hungry Ghost Month 2026


### Can you buy or sell property during Hungry Ghost Month in Singapore?


Yes. There are no official rules preventing property transactions during Hungry Ghost Month. Whether to proceed is a personal or family decision. However, eligibility requirements, contractual obligations and transaction deadlines still apply, so check the timeline carefully before deciding to postpone any step.


### Does Hungry Ghost Month affect property prices in Singapore?


Hungry Ghost Month does not automatically make property prices rise or fall. A home’s price still depends on factors such as location, condition, demand and recent comparable transactions. While some buyers may delay decisions because of personal beliefs, sellers should assess actual market conditions instead of assuming the period guarantees a discount.


### What if my HDB OTP expires during Hungry Ghost Month?


The deadline still applies. An HDB resale Option to Purchase remains valid for **21 calendar days** and expires at **4pm on the 21st day** , even if the date falls during Hungry Ghost Month. If you intend to proceed, keep the deadline in mind when planning when to exercise the OTP.
