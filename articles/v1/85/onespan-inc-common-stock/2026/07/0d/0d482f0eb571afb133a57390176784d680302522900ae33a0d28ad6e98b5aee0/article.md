## OneSpan to Announce Second Quarter Financial Results on August 4, 2026


July 9, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.onespan.com/node/13896/pdf)


BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 9, 2026-- OneSpan Inc. (NASDAQ: OSPN), today announced it will release its second quarter 2026 financial results after the market close on Tuesday, August 4, 2026. OneSpan will host a conference call that day at 4:30 p.m. ET to discuss the results.


A live webcast of the conference call will be accessible from the OneSpan investor relations website at[investors.onespan.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.onespan.com%2F&esheet=54567515&newsitemid=20260709762977&lan=en-US&anchor=investors.onespan.com&index=103&md5=9e50d13a6138dae6c638ecc393b87147) . Shortly after the conclusion of the call, a replay of the webcast will be available on the same website.


For investors and analysts accessing the conference call by phone, please use this[registration link](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fregister-conf.media-server.com%2Fregister%2FBI46172953dde640c2bed8f80530abd2d7&esheet=54567515&newsitemid=20260709762977&lan=en-US&anchor=registration+link&index=104&md5=49a21937d55b4e2bb6b4129f3198f512) to receive dial-in details. OneSpan encourages participants to dial-in at least 15 minutes before the start of the call.


**About OneSpan**


OneSpan helps organizations build secure, seamless, and trusted digital experiences through two solution portfolios: Cybersecurity and Digital Agreements. Our cybersecurity solutions protect identities, secure mobile apps, and safeguard access through advanced high-assurance authentication, threat intelligence, fraud prevention, and robust mobile app protection, defending users, devices, and applications against sophisticated attacks. Our digital agreement solutions streamline agreement workflows with secure e-signatures, identity verification, and smart digital forms, built to enable speed, compliance and exceptional customer experiences. Trusted by leading global enterprises, including more than 60% of the world’s 100 largest banks, OneSpan processes over 100 million digital agreements and billions of secure authentication transactions across more than 120 countries each year.
