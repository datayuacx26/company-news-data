# Introducing Tetra Workflows: Automate, Orchestrate, and Elevate Your Scientific Data

In modern biopharma, science is only as fast as your data workflows. But too often, critical data is trapped in silos, transcribed manually, or delayed by bottlenecks between instruments and informatics systems. These inefficiencies don’t just slow science—they create compliance risks, increase costs, and delay time to clinic and time to patient.


[Tetra Workflows](https://www.tetrascience.com/platform/scientific-data-workflow-automation) changes that. Built on the Tetra Scientific Data and AI Cloud, Tetra Workflows is a future-ready solution for scientific data workflow automation. It connects your entire lab ecosystem, accelerating and improving scientific outcomes while ensuring data quality, traceability, and compliance. See the full[press release](https://www.tetrascience.com/news/tetrascience-launches-tetra-workflows-to-automate-scientific-data-workflows-at-scale) .


## The Limits of Manual Work and Patchwork Automation


Traditional lab data workflows rely on manual transfers, point-to-point integrations, and fragmented scripts. This leads to:


- **Delays:** Teams waste hours moving files and fixing transcription errors.
- **Errors:** Manual handling increases deviation rates and threatens data integrity.
- **Missed insights:** Inaccessible or inconsistent data can’t easily be leveraged for analytics or AI.


Tetra Workflows eliminates data barriers. By automating the journey from raw instrument output to analysis-ready data in your ELN, LIMS, LES, or analytics platform, you can focus on delivering results instead of wrangling data.


## Inside Tetra Workflows


Tetra Workflows builds on our open, vendor-agnostic, cloud-native platform, pairing it with powerful self-service automation tools to deliver curated, analysis-ready data across your entire scientific data ecosystem.


### Tetra Integrations


Tap into an extensive library of pre-built connectors and agents that handle the heavy lifting of instrument data ingestion and system connectivity. Whether you’re working with chromatography systems, balances, or sequencing platforms, you can move data directly to and from your ELN, LIMS, or LES.[Learn more.](https://developers.tetrascience.com/docs/tetra-integrations)


### Tetra Data Pipelines


Transform raw instrument files into harmonized, queryable datasets using automated data operations. Pipelines can enrich data with contextual metadata, validate it, and push it seamlessly to downstream systems. For specialized logic, low-code options (such as Python-exec steps) let you customize workflows without rebuilding core infrastructure.[Learn more.](https://developers.tetrascience.com/docs/pipeline_overview)


### Recipe Library


Accelerate workflow creation with pre-configured “recipes” that bundle proven pipelines and automations for common use cases. Use them as-is to get started fast, or modify them to match your lab’s exact requirements. Either way, you skip the blank-page problem and build on tested, validated workflows.[See examples (TetraConnect Hub login required).](https://tetrascience.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/categories/27965646400525-Lab-Data-Automation-Data-Usage-Recipes)


### Tetra Data Capture App


Bring simple instruments like balances and pH meters into your digital data ecosystem without expensive upgrades. Simply snap a photo of the readout, and the app automatically uploads and assigns the result to the correct record. Combine these results with metadata from your LIMS or ELN into one analysis-ready dataset.[Watch the demo.](https://www.tetrascience.com/videos/tetra-data-capture-app-weighing-workflow)


### Self-Service Connectors & Pipelines


Create your integrations and pipelines with a guided interface that walks you through mapping fields, generating Python code, and validating your integration or pipeline, so you can connect instruments and applications bidirectionally in hours instead of weeks.[Watch the demo.](https://www.tetrascience.com/videos/self-service-pipelines)


### Visual Pipeline Builder


Use an intuitive, drag-and-drop interface to create or modify pipelines without touching code. Select from validated parsing and transformation components, configure parameters, and deploy changes instantly.[Watch the demo.](https://www.tetrascience.com/videos/visual-pipeline-builder)


### Workflow Creation Assistant


Leverage AI to accelerate the design of new scientific data workflows. The assistant helps you map data, generate scripts, and ensure accuracy through built-in error checks before deployment. It’s powerful enough for IT, yet simple enough for scientists with minimal coding experience to create and update workflow automations.[Watch the demo.](https://www.tetrascience.com/videos/workflow-creation-assistant)


The result: fewer steps, faster execution, and higher-quality data. Customers see **up to 60% fewer workflow steps, 40% more productivity, 75% fewer errors, and 90% reduction in implementation time.**
