# Wynn Enhances Tourism and “Leisure” Bus Service

*Partnering with SMEs to drive visitation to Macao neighborhoods*


**Macao, July 15, 2026** – In active support of the Macao SAR Government’s second phase of the “Tourism and ‘Leisure’ Buses: Exploring Community Charms” initiative, Wynn will further enhance its bus service arrangements by extending the routes and increasing the service frequency. Coupled with the launch of joint consumer promotions with local SMEs, the company aims to encourage both residents and tourists to explore Macao’s diverse tourism offerings and unique neighborhood experiences, thereby injecting greater vitality into the community economy.


As part of its ongoing efforts to enhance Macao’s tourism infrastructure and respond to market demand, Wynn will upgrade its Tourism and “Leisure” Bus service by extending operations beyond weekends to include Fridays during the summer holiday period from July 17 to August 30, as well as by increasing service frequency during the National Day, Christmas, and New Year’s Day holidays. In addition to connecting the two integrated resorts—Wynn Macau and Wynn Palace—the routes will also link a number of distinctive neighborhoods, tourist attractions, and commercial districts across the city. By further expanding the bus service coverage, Wynn aims to offer more convenient and diverse travel experiences to residents and tourists alike. Furthermore, Wynn is partnering with local trade associations to offer consumer promotions at participating merchants along the routes, in a bid to encourage visitors to explore different commercial areas in the community, broaden the economic benefits of tourism spending, and create more business opportunities for SMEs.


Through the continuous enhancement of its Tourism and “Leisure” Bus service, Wynn seeks to further integrate neighborhood experiences into the city’s tourism offerings. These efforts aim to attract more residents and tourists to discover the charm of Macao’s communities, inject new momentum into the local economy, and support the high-quality development of Macao’s tourism industry.
