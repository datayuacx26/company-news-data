# Best Trading Tools for Hyperliquid

### What is Hyperliquid


Hyperliquid is a decentralized perpetuals exchange built for high performance crypto trading. It combines a fully onchain settlement model with a fast matching engine that rivals centralized exchanges. Traders use Hyperliquid to trade perpetual futures with deep liquidity while maintaining self custody of their funds.


Unlike many decentralized exchanges that rely on slow execution or fragmented liquidity, Hyperliquid is designed for active trading. It supports high frequency order placement, tight spreads, and large position sizes. This has made it popular with professional crypto traders who want speed without giving up control.


As activity on Hyperliquid has increased, traders have started to look beyond the native interface. Execution quality, automation, and risk control have become more important as markets move faster and leverage increases.


#### Why traders use external tools on Hyperliquid
