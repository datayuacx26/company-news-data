## What Is E*TRADE?


ETRADE is a U.S. brokerage platform designed to serve a broad range of self-directed investors. Unlike more specialized platforms that focus almost exclusively on active trading or passive investing, ETRADE has built a comprehensive financial hub that can support a wide spectrum of investing styles.


The platform offers access to: Stocks, ETFs, Mutual funds, Options, Futures, Bonds and fixed income instruments, CDs, OTC securities


E*TRADE also supports a broad set of account structures, including standard brokerage accounts, custodial accounts, trusts, SEP IRAs, SIMPLE IRAs, Solo 401(k)s, and other business or retirement vehicles. This breadth is one of E*TRADE’s defining strengths. It is designed not just as a place to place trades, but as a platform that can support the full lifecycle of wealth building, portfolio management, and active market participation.

E*TRADE Web is built for investors who want a clean, intuitive dashboard to manage portfolios, research securities, and monitor markets without being overwhelmed by excessive technical complexity.

Traders can access market breadth statistics such as: advances and declines, new highs and lows, volume flow, sector rotation data. ## Fees and Pricing


E*TRADE follows industry-standard pricing for core equity investing while adding a few policies that are particularly useful for active traders.


Key fees include:


-


Stocks and ETFs: $0 commission


-


Options: $0.65 per contract


-


Futures: $1.50 per contract


-


OTC stocks: $6.95 per trade


One standout feature is the Dime Buyback program, which waives commissions when traders close short options positions priced at $0.10 or less. This encourages responsible position management and makes it easier to remove low-value short exposure before expiration. The main pricing drawback is leverage. Margin rates begin at 12.95% for balances under $25,000, which is meaningfully higher than what more margin-focused brokers offer.
