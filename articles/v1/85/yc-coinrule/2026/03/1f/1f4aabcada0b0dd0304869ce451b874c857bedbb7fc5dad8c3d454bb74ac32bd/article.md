# Coinrule Integrates Public, Trade Smarter

[Public](http://coinrule.com/public-stock-bot) has carved out a distinct position in the brokerage landscape by doing something most platforms struggle with: making investing feel simple without feeling simplistic. From the moment you open the app, the experience is clean, intuitive, and intentionally designed to remove friction rather than add complexity. This philosophy carries through everything Public builds. Instead of overwhelming users with dense data and professional-grade tooling, the platform focuses on accessibility, visual clarity, and new ways to explore investment ideas.


Now, with[Coinrule](https://coinrule.com/) integration, Public users can take that experience a step further by introducing AI-powered automation and structured trading workflows directly into their investing process. By connecting Public to Coinrule, investors can deploy a no-code AI trading bot that executes strategies automatically, helping bridge the gap between simple investing and systematic decision-making.


## What Is Public?


Public is a modern brokerage platform built around accessibility, design, and idea-driven investing. Unlike traditional brokers that prioritize depth, Public prioritizes clarity. The platform offers access to a range of assets including stocks, ETFs, options, cryptocurrencies, and fixed income products like Treasuries and bonds. It also supports fractional investing, making it easy for users to build positions regardless of account size. Where Public stands out is in how these assets are presented. Instead of relying on spreadsheets, dense charts, or technical jargon, the platform emphasizes visual tools and simplified interfaces that make investing feel more approachable.


However, this simplicity comes with trade-offs. Public is not designed to be a full-scale trading workstation. Advanced analytics, complex account structures, and professional-grade execution tools are intentionally limited.


For many investors, that’s exactly the point.


## A Different Approach to Investing


Public doesn’t try to replicate institutional trading platforms. Instead, it rethinks how investors interact with markets.


One of the most notable innovations is its focus on idea generation. Features like AI-driven “Generated Assets” allow users to create themed portfolios using plain-language prompts, transforming abstract ideas into investable strategies. This approach lowers the barrier to entry for investors who may not have the time or expertise to conduct deep fundamental or technical analysis. At the same time, Public has made fixed income investing significantly more accessible. Tools for building Treasury ladders and visualizing yield curves simplify what has traditionally been one of the more complex areas of investing.


The result is a platform that feels modern, creative, and easy to navigate, even if it lacks the analytical depth of more advanced brokers.


## The Public Experience


Using Public feels fundamentally different from using most brokerage platforms.


The mobile-first design is one of its strongest features. Navigation is smooth, layouts are clean, and the overall experience is built for quick interaction rather than deep analysis. Checking positions, exploring ideas, or allocating capital can all be done in just a few taps. Public also integrates AI into the user experience in subtle but meaningful ways. Market summaries, idea generation tools, and visual data representations help users quickly understand what’s happening without needing to dig through complex reports.


That said, the platform’s simplicity becomes more noticeable when you try to go deeper. Technical analysis tools are limited. Charts lack indicators. Options data omits key metrics like Greeks and bid-ask spreads. Real-time quotes are available, but not streaming, which can limit precision for active traders. In short, Public excels at making investing accessible, but it is not built for high-frequency decision-making or advanced strategy execution.


## Fees and Pricing


Public follows a low-cost model on the surface, with commission-free trading for stocks, ETFs, and options. However, the platform introduces a different kind of cost structure through its subscription model. Many features that are standard at other brokers, such as advanced data, extended-hours trading, and premium research, are locked behind Public Premium unless your account exceeds a certain size.


For casual investors, this may not be a major issue. But for more active users, the paywall can become a limiting factor. One area where Public stands out is margin pricing, with relatively low borrowing rates compared to many competitors. This makes it appealing for investors who occasionally use leverage.
