Gold and silver tend to respond to a distinct set of macroeconomic forces. Real interest rates, U.S. dollar strength, inflation expectations, central bank policy, and geopolitical risk all play a role in shaping price action. Gold often behaves defensively during risk-off environments, while silver typically exhibits higher volatility due to its dual role as both a precious metal and an industrial input.


Because of this dynamic, both metals are frequently used in:


• Inflation-hedging strategies
• Macro-driven allocation models
• Volatility and momentum trading systems
• Portfolio diversification alongside equities


These characteristics make gold and silver particularly well suited for systematic and rules-based trading approaches.
