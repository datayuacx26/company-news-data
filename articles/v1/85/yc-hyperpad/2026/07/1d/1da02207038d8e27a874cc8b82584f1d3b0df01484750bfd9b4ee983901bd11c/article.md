# How I Made a Game on hyperPad

## About Me


My name is Yasad, I am a 20 year old entrepreneur, game designer, and graphic artist who loves making enjoyable experiences that expand people’s knowledge and bring creators together.


I started my journey in digital art when I was a kid. Being homeschooled K-12, I got to explore a variety of subjects related to technology, mathematics, and animation freely throughout the day. I was encouraged to get books and online courses surrounding my interests.


For years I wanted to launch my own business, and as a pre-teen, I spent most of the school day tinkering with animation, coding, and graphic design. I was always fascinated with animals, nature, and educational technology, so most of my games and animations from childhood centered on these themes. Today, I am the founder of[YasadCreativeStudios](http://www.yasadcreative.studio/) and my game studio,[Sleepy Turtledove Games](https://www.hyperpad.com/users/ya7) . I first developed these ideas back in middle school and finally got the seed funding to launch it when I was 18. I’m very excited to be using hyperPad’s gaming engine to launch the first game from my company. It’s been a long time in the making!


## How I Found hyperPad


Honestly, I feel like hyperPad found me! It's been a part of my life for years, and I’ve grown and developed beside it. When I first discovered hyperPad in childhood, I dreamed of publishing a game using hyperPad. I first found it on the Apple App Store over a decade ago, and I’ve been using it ever since. This year will be the first year I am going from planning to publishing for a game using hyperPad, and it launches to the App Store soon!


## What is the Game About?


My game is called Star Glide, an action-packed, endless underwater runner. Right now, it’s[available on the hyperPad Hub](https://www.hyperpad.com/projects/uyugqqqx/versions/star-glide-7-462) . You play as an echinoderm—a starfish, sea cucumber urchin etc.—gliding and swimming endlessly through the ocean floor, dodging hazards, and collecting seashells to unlock power-ups.


This marine adventure is also inspired by real life animal behaviors, allowing you to learn about the vast world of the ocean while collecting coins and beating your high score.


## My Inspiration, How I Got Started


The concept of sea life and ocean exploration has always been a huge interest of mine. As a huge fan of the games Super Starfish and Sling Kong (and a fan of the movie Finding Nemo), I started with an octopus game years ago, called High Eight. I couldn’t quite get the game mechanics and graphics the way I wanted them, so I put it on pause as life got busy. Last year, after my sibling had a health emergency, I wanted to do something positive while dealing with this scary moment. That prompted me to get back into gamemaking and leverage my existing passions for design and ocean life. I knew it was important to do something I loved, and Star Glide was officially born!
