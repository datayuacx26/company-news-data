Here’s a clear breakdown of **hyperPad Starter, hyperPad Hub, and hyperPad Full** , so you can decide which version is perfect for you.


## 1. hyperPad Starter (FREE) – Build Your First Project Today


**Download Here:**[hyperPad Starter on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6737631128)


**Best for:** Beginners who want to **try game development immediately**


hyperPad Starter is the **perfect entry point** for anyone curious about making games on an iPad.


### Key Features:


- **Make 1 project** – experiment with ideas, mechanics, and layouts.
- **Full visual editor** – drag, drop, and assign behaviors without coding.
- **Touch-based workflow** – design, test, and play directly on your iPad.


### Why You Should Try It:


If you’ve never built a game before, hyperPad Starter allows you to **get hands-on instantly** . There’s no subscription, no complicated setup—just open the app and start creating. Even one project can teach you the basics of **level design, interactive objects, and simple gameplay mechanics** .


⚡ **Pro Tip:** Start with a small, fun idea like a simple runner or puzzle game. This will give you a **taste of creation without feeling overwhelming** .


## 2. hyperPad Hub (FREE) – Explore, Play, and Get Inspired


**Download Here:**[hyperPad Hub on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1484881474)


**Best for:** Players who want to **experience thousands of community games** and get inspiration before creating.


hyperPad Hub is not a creation tool—it’s a **play-first environment** .


### Key Features:


- Browse **thousands of user-created games** from the hyperPad community.
- Play **interactive stories, platformers, puzzle games, and more** .
- Discover **trending ideas** and see what’s possible before diving into development.


### Why You Should Try It:


If you’re not ready to build yet or want to **spark your creativity** , hyperPad Hub is perfect. Playing community games can give you new ideas, **showcase popular game mechanics** , and demonstrate what’s achievable without writing a single line of code.


💡 **Pro Tip:** Use Hub as a learning tool. Take notes on what works in games you play, and later implement similar ideas in your own projects.

### Why You Should Try It:


If you’re committed to **learning game development** , hyperPad Full removes all restrictions. It’s ideal for:

⚡ **Pro Tip:** Start by recreating a simple game you love, then experiment with unique mechanics. Full access allows **rapid iteration without limits** .
