*We compared the major platforms so you don't have to. Here's what actually matters.*

- **Spott** leads for AI-native depth, with in-house migration in a day to a few weeks, depending on database size and feedback speed
- **Bullhorn** remains the default for large enterprises with deep ecosystems
- **Loxo** and **Manatal** offer the most accessible entry points (free tier and $15/user/month)

Full comparison table and decision framework below.


Every recruiting agency runs on its ATS and CRM. Your applicant tracking system manages the candidate pipeline. Your CRM handles client relationships and business development. Most modern platforms combine both into one system. And whichever one you pick will shape how your team works every single day.


The problem is that the market has become almost impossible to navigate. Dozens of vendors all claim to be the best fit for agencies. Legacy platforms are bolting on[AI features](https://spott.io/resources/ai-in-staffing-a-complete-guide) to stay relevant. Newer entrants are shipping fast but lack depth. And the difference between a platform that's truly AI-native (built around AI from day one) versus one that's just AI-powered (AI added after the fact) is the kind of thing that only shows up after you've already committed.


We cut through the noise. Here's what we found.


## What to Look For in an ATS/CRM in 2026


Before diving into the list, here are the five criteria that separate strong platforms from weak ones this year:


1. **AI depth, not AI marketing.** Every vendor says "AI-powered" now. The question is whether AI is baked into the architecture or[bolted on as a feature](https://spott.io/resources/why-most-ai-powered-recruiting-tools-arent-actually-ai) . Purpose-built AI transforms workflows. Retrofitted AI saves you a few clicks.
2. **Unified ATS + CRM.** Running separate systems for candidate tracking and client management creates data silos. The best platforms unify both so every recruiter sees the full picture.
3. **Migration speed.** Switching platforms shouldn't take four months and a consulting engagement. Ask every vendor:[how long until we're fully operational?](https://spott.io/resources/spott-ats-data-migration-explained)
4. **Workflow fit.** Generic CRM patterns don't match how recruiters actually work. Look for platforms designed around the recruit-to-placement lifecycle, not adapted from sales or HR tools.
5. **Pricing that scales.** Per-user pricing punishes growth. Understand the total cost at 10, 25, and 50 users before committing.

### 2. Bullhorn


**Best for:** Large staffing enterprises that prioritize ecosystem breadth over innovation speed


Bullhorn is the incumbent. With 25+ years in the market and 10,000+ customers, it's the default at many of the industry's largest staffing firms.


**What stands out:**


- Massive integration ecosystem with 300+ marketplace integrations
- Strong automation for high-volume staffing workflows
- Passive communication tracking through Gmail and Outlook integration
- Dedicated VMS integration tools for enterprise staffing


**The trade-off:** Bullhorn's size is both its strength and its limitation. The core dates to 1999 and has grown by acquisition (Herefish, cube19, Textkernel), so automation, analytics, and AI live as separate add-on products rather than one system. G2 reviewers regularly cite slow load times and a dense interface, and enterprise rollouts are typically consultant-assisted.


**Pricing:** Starter $99/user/mo and Core $165/user/mo are published (July 2026); the Pro tier, automation, analytics, and Amplify AI are quote-only add-ons, so real-world totals land above the sticker price.


**Who it's best for:** Large agencies (100+ recruiters) already embedded in the Bullhorn ecosystem, or enterprises where the integration marketplace is a requirement.


### 3. Loxo


**Best for:** Executive search firms and small agencies that want sourcing and ATS in one place


Loxo combines ATS, CRM, and an extensive sourcing database in a single platform. The free tier makes it accessible for solo recruiters and small shops, and the sourcing capabilities (pulling from LinkedIn, GitHub, and other networks) are genuinely strong.


**What stands out:**


- Free-forever ATS and CRM tier
- Large talent database aggregating multiple professional networks
- AI candidate matching that surfaces relevant profiles
- Outreach sequences built into the platform


**The trade-off:** Loxo works well for smaller operations, but agencies scaling past 20-30 recruiters often find the workflows limiting. Enterprise-grade customization and reporting depth don't match what larger firms need. The AI, while useful, is more of a feature layer than an architectural foundation.


**Pricing:** Free tier available. Paid plans with additional features. Contact Loxo for details.


**Who it's best for:** Executive search firms and small-to-mid agencies (under 25 recruiters) that prioritize sourcing capabilities.


### 4. JobAdder


**Best for:** Agencies in the UK and Australia that want a flexible, no-lock-in ATS/CRM


JobAdder is a cloud-based ATS and CRM that's earned a loyal following, particularly among agencies in the UK and Australia. Launched in 2007 and trusted by 26,000+ users, it hits a practical middle ground: broad enough to handle most agency workflows, straightforward enough that teams adopt it quickly.


**What stands out:**


- **No lock-in contracts.** Month-to-month billing with no penalty fees to switch. That's rare in this market.
- **200+ job board integrations** and 150+ technology partners for a flexible, modular tech stack
- **60+ built-in reports and dashboards.** One of the more comprehensive out of the box analytics offerings
- Multi-channel candidate engagement (email, SMS, WhatsApp) with bulk messaging up to 500 profiles
- AI enhancements through Adder Intelligence: smart summaries, smart sync, smart job descriptions


**The trade-off:** JobAdder is a solid generalist, but it's not the deepest platform in any single area. Users report navigation issues and note that advanced reporting still falls short for some workflows. User management requires support intervention for basic admin tasks, and there's no free trial to test-drive before committing. Pricing has also been trending upward.


**Pricing:** Starts at approximately $160/user/month. Custom quotes based on team size and needs.


**Who it's best for:** Agencies (10-50 recruiters) in the UK and Australia that value flexibility, no long-term contracts, and a broad integration ecosystem.


### 5. Recruit CRM


**Best for:** Global agencies that need invoicing, stakeholder feedback, and multi-currency support


Recruit CRM delivers a solid ATS/CRM combo with features that go beyond candidate management. Built-in invoicing, client feedback tools, and revenue dashboards make it a practical choice for agencies managing the full business cycle.


**What stands out:**


- Built-in invoicing and revenue tracking by client and recruiter
- Stakeholder feedback tools for candidate submissions
- Effort-to-revenue dashboards for measuring ROI
- Frequent feature updates driven by user requests
- Transparent pricing with no hidden costs


**The trade-off:** While Recruit CRM covers a lot of ground, its AI capabilities aren't as deep as purpose-built platforms. The platform serves a broad market, which means agency-specific workflows can feel generic in places. Advanced automation requires the higher-tier plans.


**Pricing:** Pro at $85/user/month. Business at $125/user/month. Enterprise at $165/user/month (billed annually).


**Who it's best for:** Global agencies that need business operations features (invoicing, reporting) alongside their ATS/CRM.


### 6. Ezekia


**Best for:** Executive search firms that need a purpose-built CRM with fast onboarding


Ezekia is built specifically for executive search. Not adapted from a generic ATS or CRM. Trusted by 550+ firms globally with a 99.7% customer retention rate over seven years, it focuses on what retained search firms actually need: assignment management, business development tracking, client portals, and branded reporting.


**What stands out:**


- **Purpose-built for executive search.** Workflows designed around retained, interim, and in-house assignments. Not generic recruiting patterns.
- **Fast onboarding.** Teams get up and running in hours, not months. Unlimited human support with no ticket queues.
- **Branded client reporting.** Automated Word/Excel reports shared through a secure client portal with built-in feedback capture.
- **Business development module.** Track opportunities, map relationships, and manage your BD pipeline alongside active assignments.
- OpenAI-powered features for generating insights, writing summaries, and automating tasks
- ISO certified. 8,000+ integrations via Zapier. Multi-language support (English, French, German, Portuguese, Spanish).


**The trade-off:** Ezekia is purpose-built for executive search, which means it's not the right fit for high-volume staffing or temp agencies. Reporting and automation features are adequate but don't match the depth of broader platforms. As firms scale beyond retained search into other business models, they may outgrow the platform.


**Pricing:** Starts at £120/month. Flexible monthly subscription with no long-term contracts. Free trial available.


**Who it's best for:** Retained executive search firms and boutique search practices that want a clean, fast, search-specific CRM without the overhead of a platform built for a different business model.


### 7. Vincere


**Best for:** Large agencies that need a front-to-back office system covering recruitment through payroll


Vincere positions itself as the operating system for recruitment agencies, handling everything from ATS and CRM to timesheets, invoicing, and analytics in a single platform. It covers permanent, contract, and temp staffing models.


**What stands out:**


- Comprehensive front-to-back office: ATS, CRM, timesheets, invoicing, analytics
- Strong support for contract and temp staffing (time management, billing)
- Built-in candidate and client portals
- Detailed analytics dashboards for agency operations


**The trade-off:** Vincere's breadth means complexity. Smaller agencies often find it overpowered for their needs. The platform's architecture hasn't kept pace with newer competitors on the AI front, and the interface can feel dated compared to more modern tools.


**Pricing:** Contact vendor. Generally mid-to-high range.


**Who it's best for:** Mid-to-large agencies (25+ recruiters) running contract/temp desks that need operational tools (timesheets, billing) integrated with their ATS/CRM.


### 8. Recruiterflow


**Best for:** Small-to-mid agencies looking for AI-forward features at a reasonable price


Recruiterflow markets itself as an AI-first ATS and CRM built for recruiting agencies and executive search firms. The platform bundles ATS, CRM, outreach sequencing, data enrichment, and AI agents into a unified experience with visual Kanban-style pipeline management.


**What stands out:**


- AI agents for automating routine recruiting tasks
- Built-in outreach sequencing and data enrichment
- Visual Kanban boards for pipeline management
- Clean, modern interface that's quick to adopt
- Focused specifically on agency workflows


**The trade-off:** Recruiterflow serves a wide range of agency sizes but can lack depth in enterprise features. Advanced reporting, role-based permissions, and compliance tools aren't as mature as larger platforms. Integration ecosystem is smaller than Bullhorn or JobAdder.


**Pricing:** Contact vendor for current plans.


**Who it's best for:** Small-to-mid agencies (5-40 recruiters) that want a modern, focused ATS/CRM without the overhead of enterprise platforms.


### 9. Manatal


**Best for:** Budget-conscious agencies that want AI capabilities without breaking the bank


Manatal is one of the most affordable ATS/CRM platforms with genuine AI features. Starting at $15/user/month, it's accessible to agencies that can't justify enterprise pricing. The platform includes AI-driven candidate recommendations, resume parsing, and integrations with 2,500+ job boards.


**What stands out:**


- Aggressive pricing: starts at $15/user/month (billed annually)
- AI candidate recommendations and scoring
- 2,500+ job board integrations
- Social media profile enrichment (LinkedIn + 20 platforms)
- 14-day free trial, no credit card required


**The trade-off:** You get what you pay for in certain areas. Reporting is basic compared to mid-market and enterprise tools. Resume parsing accuracy can be inconsistent. Customization options for workflows and dashboards are limited. The mobile experience works but isn't polished.


**Pricing:** Professional at $15/user/month. Enterprise at $35/user/month. Enterprise Plus at $55/user/month (all billed annually).


**Who it's best for:** Small agencies (under 15 recruiters) and solo recruiters who need a functional ATS/CRM with AI features at a budget-friendly price.


### 10. Tracker RMS


**Best for:** Growing agencies that need strong CRM and ATS integration with operational tools


Tracker RMS has been serving staffing agencies since 2005 and excels at seamless CRM/ATS integration. Its strength is reducing the friction between business development and recruiting. Your sales pipeline and candidate pipeline live in the same system with no data gaps.


**What stands out:**


- Tight CRM/ATS integration. Sales and recruitment data flows seamlessly
- AI-powered candidate ranking based on engagement and profile data
- Built-in marketing campaigns (email and text)
- Operational tools: timesheets, expenses, onboarding, accounting integration
- VMS automation for enterprise staffing
- Deep integration library (LinkedIn Recruiter, Indeed, QuickBooks, and more)


**The trade-off:** Tracker's depth requires commitment. The learning curve for advanced features is real. The interface doesn't feel as modern as newer competitors. Financial tracking, while comprehensive, is described by some users as overly complex. Pricing isn't publicly listed.


**Pricing:** Contact vendor. Generally competitive for the feature set.


**Who it's best for:** Growing agencies (10-50 recruiters) that need their sales and recruiting operations in one system, especially those running contract and temp desks.

## How to Choose


There's no universally "best" ATS/CRM. The right platform depends on your agency's size, business model, and priorities. Here's a framework:


**If AI is your top priority:** Spott is the only platform on this list built from the ground up as an AI-native system. Every other vendor is adding AI to an existing product. The architectural difference shows up in how the platform handles matching, context capture, and workflow automation.


**If you're a large enterprise locked into an ecosystem:** Bullhorn's integration marketplace is unmatched. If your tech stack already runs through Bullhorn, the switching cost is high and the ecosystem value is real.


**If budget drives your decision:** Manatal at $15/user/month and Loxo's free tier give you functional ATS/CRM capabilities at a fraction of the cost. You'll trade depth and customization for accessibility.


**If you run contract/temp desks:** Vincere and Tracker RMS offer operational tools (timesheets, invoicing, billing) that most ATS/CRM platforms don't include natively.


**If you run a retained executive search practice:** Ezekia is purpose-built for your business model. Assignment management, client portals, and BD tracking without the noise of features designed for temp and volume staffing.


**If you need to switch fast:** Ask every vendor about migration timelines. Some agencies spend months in transition limbo. Spott handles migration in-house with AI data migration tools; most agencies are live in a day to a few weeks, depending on database size and feedback speed.
