# 7 Best Loxo Alternatives for Recruiting Agencies in 2026

## TL;DR


The 7 best Loxo alternatives in 2026 are **Spott** (AI-native, contextual matching, branded client reports), **Bullhorn** (enterprise scale and back-office), **Recruiterflow** (automation and support quality), **Recruit CRM** (budget entry point), **JobAdder** (job board distribution), **Vincere** (UK/APAC back-office suite), and **Ezekia** (retained executive search). The right pick depends on whether you're leaving Loxo over pricing trajectory, AI depth, client-facing deliverables, or regional hosting.


## Why agencies are looking for a Loxo alternative in 2026


Loxo is a genuinely strong product. It scores 4.6 out of 5 on both G2 and Capterra, its built-in sourcing database of roughly 1.2 billion profiles is the most differentiated feature in the category, and reviewers consistently say it feels "built by recruiters for recruiters." For US agencies whose biggest constraint is outbound sourcing volume, it earns its reputation.


The reasons agencies evaluate alternatives in 2026 cluster around four themes.


**The pricing trajectory.** Loxo's Basic plan is now $169 per user per month, and Professional contracts typically land between $250 and $400 per user per month depending on seat count and modules. Multiple Capterra reviewers report automatic 5% annual price increases written into contracts, and contact-credit overage pricing isn't publicly disclosed. The free tier is a great entry point. The exit trajectory is what catches agencies off guard.


**Client-facing deliverables under-deliver.** Reviewers flag resume formatting and CRM function as weak spots, and Loxo's candidate report output has historically been a sore point. When the document you send a client is the product of your work, that matters.


**Integrations feel bolted together.** Several G2 reviewers describe Loxo's integrations as "clunky and half-baked," with some requiring paid add-ons or custom development. Agencies running omnichannel outreach across email, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp tend to hit these edges first.


**Support and hosting.** Loxo's support is chatbot-first, and users report that reaching a human takes effort. The platform is also US-centric, with limited regional data residency for European agencies with GDPR-driven hosting requirements.


If any of that resonates, the seven platforms below are the alternatives most often shortlisted against Loxo in 2026.

## How we evaluated each platform


The platforms below were assessed against five criteria that consistently come up when agencies tell us why they're moving off Loxo:


1. **AI depth.** Is AI part of the core data model, or a layer for sourcing only? Does it understand context (notes, calls, messages) or just keywords?
2. **Total cost trajectory.** Not the entry price, but what you'll actually pay in year two and three once credits, modules, and indexation kick in.
3. **Client deliverables.** Can you send a client a candidate report in your own branding without rebuilding it in Word?
4. **Workflow fit by staffing model.** Perm, temp, contract, and retained search have different needs.
5. **Support and hosting.** Can you reach a human, and can you host data where your compliance team needs it?

## 2. Bullhorn


**Founded:** 1999 | **HQ:** Boston | **Pricing:** Starter $99/user/mo, Core $165/user/mo (published, July 2026); Pro and add-ons quote-only


Bullhorn is the legacy market leader: 10,000+ customers, 300+ integrations, and a proven track record at 500+ seat firms.


**Where Bullhorn stands out vs Loxo:** enterprise scale, deep temp/contract back-office (timesheets, pay & bill, 110+ VMS integrations), and the Amplify AI suite, which is the most substantive AI investment among legacy vendors.

Automation, analytics, and Amplify are typically priced as separate modules, buyer reports and reviews cite implementation fees in the low thousands, and customers report annual price indexation in multi-year contracts. Reviews consistently flag slow, dense UX and support queues.

## 3. Recruiterflow


**Founded:** 2016 | **HQ:** San Francisco | **Pricing:** From $119/user/month, AIRA plan around $149/user/month


Recruiterflow serves 1,700+ agencies and built its name on automation and unusually good support (responses often within minutes, per reviewers).


**Where Recruiterflow stands out vs Loxo:** automation maturity (years of battle-tested sequences and triggers), bundled AI (the AIRA plan includes all current and future agents at one price), and a support reputation that is the opposite of chatbot-first.


**What to keep in mind:** US-hosted only, which rules it out for agencies with EU residency requirements. AIRA is a recent layer on an automation-first product, and LinkedIn messages aren't a native sequence step. Contacts and candidates live as separate records.


**Best for:** Small-to-mid US agencies (10-50 recruiters) that prioritize workflow automation and responsive support.


**Bottom line:** The strongest support-and-automation story on this list. Feature-level breakdown in our[Spott vs Recruiterflow comparison](https://spott.io/comparison/recruiterflow) .

## 5. JobAdder


**Founded:** 2007 | **HQ:** Sydney | **Pricing:** ~$135-160+/user/month, custom quotes


JobAdder brings 26,000+ users, a clean interface, and a 200+ job board ecosystem including native SEEK integration.


**Where JobAdder stands out vs Loxo:** job board distribution breadth (the inverse of Loxo's outbound-sourcing strength), an 18-year track record with 99% CSAT, and a dedicated in-house recruitment edition.

**Best for:** ANZ and UK agencies whose sourcing strategy runs on job board distribution rather than outbound.


**Bottom line:** A sensible swap only if your sourcing model is inbound. Full comparison in our[Spott vs JobAdder comparison](https://spott.io/comparison/jobadder) .

## 7. Ezekia


**Founded:** 2015 | **HQ:** London | **Pricing:** ~£120/user/month


Ezekia is purpose-built for retained executive search: assignment management, polished client portals, BD pipelines, and branded reporting, with 550+ firms and a 99.7% retention rate.


**Where Ezekia stands out vs Loxo:** the exec-search workflow is native rather than adapted, and the client portal experience is the most polished in this list. Loxo serves exec search, but as one model among many.


**What to keep in mind:** Ezekia covers retained exec search only; perm, temp, and contracting are out of scope. It added OpenAI-based features but isn't AI-native, integrations are limited (Outlook, Zapier), and migration fees have been reported as high as £5,000.


**Best for:** Boutique retained executive search firms that want depth in one model.


**Bottom line:** The specialist play. If your firm is 100% retained search and Loxo feels generic, Ezekia fits like a glove; if you run mixed models, it can't follow you. More in our[Spott vs Ezekia comparison](https://spott.io/comparison/ezekia) .


## When Loxo is still the right call


Not every Loxo customer should leave. Loxo remains your best option if outbound sourcing volume is your defining constraint and the 1.2B-profile database genuinely replaces a separate sourcing stack for you, you're US-based with no EU hosting requirements, and your contract economics still work after factoring in the annual increases. The sourcing database is the killer feature, and nothing on this list replaces it one-for-one.


If sourcing isn't your bottleneck, though, you're paying a premium for a database you don't fully use, and the alternatives above will likely fit better.


## How to choose your Loxo alternative


**If AI depth is the driver:** Spott is the most AI-native option on this list, with matching, notes, reports, and outreach designed around AI from inception rather than added to a sourcing tool.


**If you're enterprise temp/contract:** Bullhorn or Vincere. Bullhorn for VMS depth and ecosystem, Vincere for sharper pricing and analytics.


**If you're cost-driven:** Recruit CRM at $85/user/month, with the scalability ceiling as the known trade-off.


**If support quality burned you:** Recruiterflow's human, fast support is the strongest contrast to chatbot-first service.


**If you're retained exec search only:** Ezekia.


**If EU hosting and GDPR are non-negotiable:** Spott (EU-hosted, ISO 27001) is the clearest fit; most other options on this list are US-hosted.
