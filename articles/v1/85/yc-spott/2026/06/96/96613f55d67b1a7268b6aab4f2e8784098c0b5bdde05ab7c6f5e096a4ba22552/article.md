# Why CGP Group Is Consolidating Four ATSs into One AI-Native Platform

**Company:** CGP Group (Cornerstone Global Partners)


**Who:** 200 people, 12 offices


**What:** Executive search, contract staffing, and EOR across APAC, the Middle East, and the US


**Before Spott:** Mercury, Bullhorn, and Vincere, simultaneously, plus a patchwork of point tools


Most agencies replace one ATS. CGP Group is replacing three at the same time.


The firm, 200 people across offices in Asia, the Middle East, and the US, had accumulated Mercury, Bullhorn, and Vincere across different parts of the business, supplemented by a stack of point tools for sequencing, sourcing, and dialing. When CEO Chris Watkins kicked off a company-wide digital transformation, consolidating the ATS layer was only the starting point. The real goal was to put AI at the core of how the firm recruits: matching, outreach, and the data upkeep that eats recruiter hours, handled by the platform itself.


> "By automating and centralizing everything, it makes life easier and delivers real impact for us at scale."
> **Chris Watkins, CEO, CGP Group**


## What a 200-person firm looks for


Chris and his team did the deep dive themselves: dozens of platforms compared, the legacy players they already ran, and the new AI generation alongside them. Their requirements cut the field down fast:


- **AI-native, not AI-bolted-on.** CGP had watched established platforms relaunch with an "AI strategy" layered over fifteen-year-old data structures. They wanted AI as the architecture, not the marketing: candidate matching, automated campaigns, and profiles that keep themselves up to date, built into the platform rather than taped on top.
- **API agility.** A firm at CGP's scale lives on integrations. The platform underneath has to expose real APIs, webhooks, and custom data, not a closed box with a connector marketplace.
- **A partner that moves at their speed.** "We like the fact people are smaller, because we can talk with the founders," Chris said on the first call. "I'm a founder. We can understand each other and help each other build each other's businesses."


## The rollout


CGP is bringing 200 users onto Spott across 12 countries. The cadence tells the story: training sessions run weekly across APAC time zones, and CGP's leadership and engineering team hold a standing weekly sync with Spott's product and engineering side.


That second meeting is the unusual part. CGP's developers ask for API capabilities (webhooks, endpoints, filters) and Spott ships them, often within the week, sometimes the same day. On a recent call, requests from the previous week came back as a checklist: done, done, shipped in today's release.


If you're betting your AI transformation on a platform, what you're really buying is how fast the team behind it moves. Not the initial feature list.


"I think we've nailed the two biggest things I was most concerned about," Chris told the team on a recent sync.


## The bigger picture


CGP's bet mirrors what we see across the industry: the firms winning the next decade are not the ones with the most tools, but the ones whose platform does the work for them, with AI that matches, drafts outreach, and keeps the database clean while recruiters recruit. Four ATSs in parallel was the cost of the old generation. An AI-native platform with open APIs is the entry ticket to the new one.
