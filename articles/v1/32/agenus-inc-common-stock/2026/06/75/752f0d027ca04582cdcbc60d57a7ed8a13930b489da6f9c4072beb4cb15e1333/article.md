### Agenus Announces Virtual 2026 Annual Shareholders Meeting


June 2, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s202.q4cdn.com/949764554/files/doc_news/Agenus-Announces-Virtual-2026-Annual-Shareholders-Meeting-2026.pdf)


LEXINGTON, Mass.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Agenus Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.agenusbio.com&esheet=54546598&newsitemid=20260602350009&lan=en-US&anchor=Agenus+Inc.&index=1&md5=b10c6e9a1c76104083a74ce12453b782) (“Agenus”) (NASDAQ: AGEN), a leader in immuno-oncology innovation, today announced that its Annual Shareholders Meeting will begin at 10:00 a.m. ET on June 16, 2026, and will be conducted in a virtual format only. Registration for attendees will start at 9:45 a.m. ET.


To participate in the Annual Shareholders Meeting, shareholders should visit[www.virtualshareholdermeeting.com/AGEN2026](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.virtualshareholdermeeting.com%2FAGEN2026&esheet=54546598&newsitemid=20260602350009&lan=en-US&anchor=www.virtualshareholdermeeting.com%2FAGEN2026&index=2&md5=e061d12f8d05f0b3534174d87aae1e16) and enter the 16-digit control number found in their proxy materials. Guests may also access the Annual Shareholders Meeting, but in listen-only mode. No control number is required for guests.


**Webcast Information:**


**Date** : Tuesday, June 16, 2026


**Time** : 10:00 a.m. ET


A live webcast and replay will be accessible on the Company’s website at[https://investor.agenusbio.com/events-and-presentations](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.agenusbio.com%2Fevents-and-presentations&esheet=54546598&newsitemid=20260602350009&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.agenusbio.com%2Fevents-and-presentations&index=3&md5=c8f3a152af7fd7d57d81b85eeab8d720) and at[www.virtualshareholdermeeting.com/AGEN2026](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.virtualshareholdermeeting.com%2FAGEN2026&esheet=54546598&newsitemid=20260602350009&lan=en-US&anchor=www.virtualshareholdermeeting.com%2FAGEN2026&index=4&md5=ad95821c280bc37a9aad908029efe286) .

**About Agenus**


Agenus is a leading immuno-oncology company targeting cancer with a comprehensive pipeline of immunological agents. The company was founded in 1994 with a mission to expand patient populations benefiting from cancer immunotherapy through combination approaches, using a broad repertoire of antibody therapeutics, adoptive cell therapies (through MiNK Therapeutics) and adjuvants (through SaponiQx). Agenus has robust end-to-end development capabilities, across commercial and clinical cGMP manufacturing facilities, research and discovery, and a global clinical operations footprint. Agenus is headquartered in Lexington, MA.
