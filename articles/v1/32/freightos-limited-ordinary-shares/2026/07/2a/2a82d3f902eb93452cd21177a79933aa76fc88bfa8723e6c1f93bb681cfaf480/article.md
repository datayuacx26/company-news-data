# Unifying the Freightos Identity: Freightos Consolidates Product Portfolio Under Single Corporate Brand

Streamlined brand portfolio connects carriers, forwarders, and shippers under a centralized, data-driven Freightos identity


**BARCELONA, SPAIN** – Freightos, the leading digital infrastructure platform powering the international freight industry, today announced the strategic unification of its corporate and product brands. Over the past several months, Freightos has streamlined its internal operations, aligning its teams, investments, and accountability behind a common vision to operate as ONE Freightos. Today, the company’s external identity evolves to match this unified operational approach, bringing its comprehensive portfolio under a single Freightos brand.


Operating as a vendor-neutral network, Freightos digitalizes freight execution by transforming manual, fragmented processes into seamless, connected, digital workflows. This structural alignment reflects Freightos’ growing role as the intelligence middleware layer that unifies data and workflow across air, ocean and road freight.


To support this streamlined approach and provide immediate clarity to the market, the Freightos portfolio is now aligned directly with the specific target audiences it serves:


- **Freightos for Forwarders:** The dedicated digital platform for freight forwarders, bringing together Rate & Quote, Booking, Sales Portal, Payments, and workflow solutions into a single environment.
- **Freightos for Airlines:** The comprehensive technology platform enabling air carriers with digital distribution, interlining, eBooking, and digital payment capabilities.
- **7LFreight, a Freightos Company:** The premier North American domestic freight procurement platform, maintaining its deep segment expertise in domestic US pricing, air cargo, and trucking procurement while connecting more deeply to the global Freightos network.
- **Freightos Enterprise:** The enterprise procurement, tender management, and market intelligence platform for multinational importers and exporters.
- **Freightos Marketplace:** The digital marketplace connecting importers and exporters with freight forwarders through instant pricing, booking, and shipment management.


For existing customers and partners, this brand evolution introduces **zero operational friction** . All accounts, user logins, active API integrations, contracts, dashboard configurations, and daily workflows remain exactly as they are today. To simplify access and improve network discovery, the company’s public digital presence is now centralized at a single destination: **freightos.com** .


“Modern logistics organizations no longer look for standalone software to solve isolated operational challenges; they expect connected ecosystems that bring together data, workflows, and decision-making across the entire freight lifecycle,” said Pablo Pinillos, CEO of Freightos. “Unifying our portfolio under the Freightos name is a natural milestone in our journey to build one company. By focusing our investments and accelerating innovation under a single corporate identity, we serve as the intelligence middleware layer that empowers smarter operational decisions, helping our partners automate operations, collaborate with trading partners, and move global cargo more effectively.”


The centralized brand identity is live today. Existing users can continue to access their respective platforms using current credentials without disruption. New logistics service providers, carriers, and businesses looking to leverage the interconnected digital ecosystem can explore the unified portfolio and register for access at **freightos.com** .


## Frequently Asked Questions


Why is the WebCargo name going away?


Freightos has grown rapidly through acquisitions and organic development, creating multiple overlapping product brands. We are unifying everything under a single company name—Freightos—and introducing new names for our solutions (like changing *WebCargo by Freightos Book* to the **Book** module inside **Freightos for Forwarders** ). This makes our ecosystem much easier to understand and navigate.


Will my login credentials or passwords change on July 6th?


No. Your existing username and password will work perfectly on the platform.


What happens if I accidentally type webcargo.co after July 6th?


You will automatically be redirected to the correct, updated destination on freightos.com. However, we highly recommend updating your browser bookmarks to freightos.com for direct access.


Are my rates, data, or active eBookings going to be lost?


Absolutely not. This is strictly a brand identity and product suite renaming initiative. All back-end data, integrations, saved contract rates, customer histories, and live bookings remain completely safe and untouched.


Will there be changes to my agreements and invoices?


Yes, starting in July, invoices will reflect the new product suite and module names. Any new agreements will also be executed under the updated naming convention. Existing agreements, as well as the legal entity, pricing, and payment terms, will remain unchanged.


Will the link I use to log into the platform change on July 6th?


No. The actual web address you and your team use to access the platform, **www.webcargonet.com** , will stay exactly the same. You do not need to change your bookmarks or learn a new login path.


If the login link isn’t changing, what website is being removed?


We are only sun-setting our external corporate marketing sites (webcargo.co) and consolidating our public web presence under **freightos.com** . The operational platform itself remains hosted at www.webcargonet.com.
