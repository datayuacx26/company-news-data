# How To Improve Quality Assurance For Call Centers Using AI

Any systematic process of determining whether a product or service meets specified requirements is referred to as quality assurance (QA).


QA creates and maintains standards for developing or manufacturing reliable products. A quality assurance system is intended to boost customer confidence and a company's credibility while also improving work processes and efficiency, allowing a company to compete more effectively.


Quality assurance assists a company in developing products and services that meet the needs, expectations, and requirements of its customers. It results in high-quality product offerings that foster customer trust and loyalty. A quality assurance program's standards and procedures help to prevent product defects before they occur.


## Quality assurance in call centres


Contact centers are critical for everything from brand loyalty to increasing efficiency and productivity and facilitating growth. According to the 2017 Microsoft State of Customer Service report, 96% of consumers worldwide believe that customer service is a defining factor in brand choice and loyalty.


Customer experience must be prioritized. It is what will set your company apart from the competition. That is why your contact center must be regularly monitored, evaluated, and improved.


You can't rest on your laurels and assume that your customer satisfaction standards are being met. You must be able to measure the quality of your service, which is where quality assurance comes in.


> "A Unique Experience for You." This is the motto of every service provider and brand manufacturer. Around 60,850 contact centers exist today around the world — every day, agents answer calls from various clients, handling a variety of complaints, some new, some old, and some in a different language. How can a business improve its services and customer experience? Quality assurance is the answer.


## QA framework for call centres


A call center QA framework is a set of success criteria and metrics used to measure and manage service quality. Consider it a guide to monitoring and analyzing your contact center's performance.


The right framework can assist you in defining your key performance indicators, identifying goals, and determining how to obtain the most strategic long-term value from your contact center. The right QA framework can essentially determine where your contact center is going and how you'll get there.


Your call center QA framework should provide answers to the following questions:


- What information will you gather?
- How will you evaluate customer interactions (phone calls, emails, live chat, social media, and so on)?
- How will you keep track of and assess communication?
- What will the results of your analyses look like?
- How will you put your quality assurance findings into action?


## How to improve QA with AI


Contact center quality assurance has been an essential component of improving agent performance, ensuring compliance, and improving customer experience for more than 40 years. Despite advances in IVR and chatbot technology, phone support remains an important function. At the end of the day, customers frequently want to speak with a live person.


A US survey found that 44% of respondents still favor voice or phone as their main customer service channel — 21% higher than any other channel, with live chat second at 23%.


Every call center is designed with the customer journey in mind — the path a customer takes from the first time they hear about your brand until they buy. The more interaction you have with your customers, the more loyal they will be to your brand.


So: should you use artificial intelligence, human voice analytics, or a combination of the two?


Call centers benefit greatly from AI — particularly in terms of speed, accuracy, and repeatability. The ability of AI to recognize words, phrases, and voice tone is much faster and more consistent than any human alternative, with a higher accuracy rate. AI provides predictive models and advanced analytics based on customer type, allowing for pinpoint actionable insights — all in a fraction of the time it would take human agents.


Many call center software platforms currently offer AI for lead generation, pre-qualifying leads, quality and compliance monitoring, smart call routing and more. Below are some of the best-known examples of AI and machine learning being adopted by today's call centers.


### Interactive Voice Response (IVR)


Cloud-based intelligent IVR systems use voice response and analytics to automate call routing. If you need to communicate an automated message, an IVR system can send personalized messages to your leads via multiple channels. To reduce the automated feel, use a conversational IVR system to guide customers and leads through a series of options.


### AI virtual agent


AI in call centers today can sound incredibly realistic. A good AI virtual agent employs machine learning, NLP, and advanced speech recognition to conduct natural-sounding conversations. For clients and potential clients, the best IVA solutions provide organic, human-like experiences.


### SMS bots and AI for lead generation


AI implementation can aid in the optimization of your lead generation efforts. While live call centre agents focus on lead conversion, your software can search for more valuable prospects. AI-powered SMS bots can hold entire text conversations with potential customers, opening a completely new channel of productivity.


### AI and call analytics


One of the most common applications of AI in call centres is providing detailed analytics on call times, first-call resolution, and other factors. These technologies can detect trends and access customer data, revealing whether customers are having a positive or negative experience. Because AI measures customer sentiment, tone, and personality, it can provide more comprehensive analytics than a human customer support manager.
