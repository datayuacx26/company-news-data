### Frontier Group Holdings Announces Webcast of Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results


July 22, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s21.q4cdn.com/257736825/files/doc_news/Frontier-Group-Holdings-Announces-Webcast-of-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results-2026.pdf)


DENVER


, July 22, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Frontier Group Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: ULCC), parent company of Frontier Airlines, Inc., will be releasing its second quarter 2026 financial results before the market opens on Wednesday, July 29, 2026.


Management will host a live webcast with analysts to discuss these results the same day at 11:00am ET. The webcast will be available to the public on a listen-only basis at[http://ir.flyfrontier.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4736334-1&h=1348693962&u=http%3A%2F%2Fir.flyfrontier.com%2F&a=http%3A%2F%2Fir.flyfrontier.com) .


A replay of the call will be archived on the company's Investor Relations website for 30 days.


**About Frontier Airlines**


Frontier Airlines, Inc., a subsidiary of Frontier Group Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: ULCC), is a value-focused airline operating one of the youngest and most fuel-efficient fleets in the United States. With a growing network and continued investment in product upgrades, including the introduction of First Class seating, onboard Wi-Fi and enhanced loyalty benefits, Frontier is giving customers more comfort, flexibility, and rewards while keeping fares low.
