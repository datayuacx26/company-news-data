# Frontier Airlines Launches Nonstop Service From Dallas Fort Worth International Airport to Newark, N.J.; Service to Santa Ana, Calif., Begins Later This Month

**DALLAS – May 5, 2026** – Frontier Airlines (


[Nasdaq: ULCC](https://ir.flyfrontier.com/) ) launched daily nonstop service today from Dallas Fort Worth International Airport (DFW) to Newark Liberty International Airport in New Jersey (EWR). This is ahead of new nonstop service to John Wayne Airport in Santa Ana, Calif. (SNA), launching later this month. With these new routes, Frontier will serve 35 destinations from DFW. To celebrate, America’s Low Fare Airline is offering fares starting at $89.*


“We are thrilled to launch these new routes from DFW just in time for summer travel, affordably connecting Dallas-area consumers to both coasts as well as supporting more tourism to North Texas,” said Josh Flyr, vice president of network and operations design, Frontier Airlines.

Frontier Airlines has introduced sweeping changes to its product and customer service offerings, ushering in **‘The New Frontier.’** Among the enhancements is UpFront Plus seating, an upgraded seating option with extra leg and elbow room in the first two rows of the aircraft. Customers in UpFront Plus enjoy a window or aisle seat with extra legroom and a guaranteed empty middle seat. The airline also now offers


[unlimited companion travel](https://www.flyfrontier.com/frontiermiles/companion-travel) for its most loyal customers, with the flexibility to choose a different companion on every Frontier flight. Debuting in 2026, Frontier will begin offering First Class seating, combining unmatched comfort and space at Frontier’s trademark affordable prices.


Frontier continues to innovate with its industry-leading frequent flyer program, **Frontier Miles** ,


** which allows members to earn miles quickly and get rewarded for each dollar spent on Frontier products. Miles accrue based on dollars spent with a standard 10X multiplier: $1 = 10 miles, with multipliers increasing at every elite level up to 20X. Elite status is attainable at only 10,000 points and offers perks such as priority boarding, seat selection and free bag(s) depending on status level. Like the airline, Frontier Miles is also family friendly, offering easy family pooling of miles for Elite members and making it simple for families to enjoy rewards together.


[Joining is free](https://www.flyfrontier.com/myfrontier/frontier-miles?mobile=true) .
