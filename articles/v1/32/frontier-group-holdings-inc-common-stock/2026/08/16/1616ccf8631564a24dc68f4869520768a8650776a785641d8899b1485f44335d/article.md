# Frontier Releases 2026 GoWild Fall & Winter Pass at Just $199 With Immediate Access, Providing Up to Nearly Seven Months of Unlimited Travel

**DENVER – August 4, 2026** – Frontier Airlines (


[Nasdaq: ULCC](https://ir.flyfrontier.com/) ) today launched its 2026 GoWild Fall & Winter Pass with immediate access, offering consumers the chance to purchase the pass now for just $199 and begin enjoying unlimited flights right away, rather than waiting for the typical pass period to begin. With the pass valid through Feb. 28, 2027, this means consumers who purchase now will enjoy nearly seven months of limitless travel with Frontier.


Additionally, now through Aug. 17, consumers who purchase the pass will enjoy even more flexibility as they unlock access to dedicated seats for pass holders on every flight, enabling them to plan ahead for travel through Jan. 4, 2027 with early booking fees as low as $29 (and even book on blackout dates for as low as $79*).


“The GoWild Fall & Winter Pass makes it possible to experience fall foliage in New England, hit the slopes in the Rocky Mountains and escape to the beaches of Puerto Rico – and that’s just the beginning of the limitless adventures available to our pass holders for just $199 for a limited time,” said Bobby Schroeter, Chief Commercial Officer, Frontier Airlines. “GoWild continues to get better, combining the freedom of spontaneous, all-you-can-fly travel with more opportunities to plan ahead for the trips and moments that matter.”


**What you get with the 2026 GoWild Fall & Winter Pass**


· Introductory price of just $199


· Immediate access to the pass, allowing pass holders to begin flying right away and enjoy unlimited travel with Frontier through Feb. 28, 2027


· Only $0.01 in airfare per flight segment (plus taxes, fees and charges)


· Earn miles and Elite Status points on extras like bags and seats


**Limited time special benefits for all GoWild pass holders:**


· Access to blackout dates when you book by 11:59 pm EDT on Aug. 17 (Early booking fees starting at $29 on most dates and $79 on select peak dates may apply*)


· Dedicated seats on every Frontier flight for GoWild pass holders to early book by Aug. 17
