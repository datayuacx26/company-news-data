# Embedded Executives Podcast with Ambiq Founder and CTO

### Embedded Executives: Scott Hanson, Founder, CTO, Ambiq®


Everyone wants their personal device to run longer on a charge. While many of the processor vendors have made incremental improvements, Ambiq seems to have turned the equation on its head, running at performance levels that are more than adequate, but consume far less power. Coming on as a repeat guest of our[Embedded Executives podcast](https://www.embedded-computing.com/embedded-executive) to provide an update on the technology is Scott Hanson, Founder and CTO of Ambiq.
