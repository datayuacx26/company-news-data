# Shenzhen Horn Audio Announces White-Box Headset with Low Power Wake-on-Voice for Amazon Alexa

## Luduan V4 neckband Headset Supports Alexa With Low Power Voice Activation


**Shenzhen China** —Horn Audio, a global manufacturer of audio products, today announced that it has developed a new white-box headset that allows users to interact with Alexa. The Horn Luduan V4 is based on the Alexa Mobile Accessory (AMA) protocol for Bluetooth accessories, and offers high-quality wake word detection with low power. Luduan V4 is a white-box headset, helping brands build voice-enabled devices while reducing development time and increasing speed to market.


The Luduan V4 is built on Ambiq’s ultra-low power[Voice-on-SPOT®](https://ambiq.com/voice-on-spot-vos/) technology, enabling high-quality and low-power voice solutions for battery-powered products.


“As the premier manufacturer and designer of consumer audio products, Shenzhen Horn continues to lead the way by integrating Alexa to their products”, said Aaron Grassian Vice President of Marketing at Ambiq®. “We are delighted that Shenzhen Horn chose Ambiq’s Voice-on-SPOT® platform which provides a low-power voice solution without compromising quality or battery life”.


Luduan V4 is a Bluetooth headset that connects to the Alexa Voice Service (AVS) via the Alexa App for Android and iOS. Customers can just say “Alexa” to interact with Alexa and access features like music playback, smart home controls, and tens of thousands of Alexa skills.


To learn more, visit[http://www.szhorn.com/products/amazon-alexa/neckband-luduanv4/](http://www.szhorn.com/products/amazon-alexa/neckband-luduanv4/)
