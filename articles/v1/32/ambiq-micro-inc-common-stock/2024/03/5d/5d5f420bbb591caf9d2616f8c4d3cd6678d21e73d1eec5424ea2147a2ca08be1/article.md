# Stunning Wearables Graphics Library Powered by Ultra Low Powered MCU

Stunning Wearables Graphics Library Powered by Ultra Low Powered MCU 2


***Live demo of a Qt for MCUs Latest Graphics UX on Apollo4 Plus Display Kit at[Embedded World 2024](https://ambiq.com/events/ew24/) on April 9-11, 2024***


**Ambiq** , a leader in exceptionally energy-efficient semiconductors for enabling AI on edge devices, team up with Qt Group, a global software company that offers cross-platform solutions for the entire software development lifecycle, to present a live demo of the **Apollo4 Plus Display Kit** running powerful and vivid graphics using the latest **Qt for MCU wearables graphics library.**


**Ambiq’s energy-efficient Apollo4 Plus SoC** enables edge devices with high-performing vibrant graphics with a 2D/2.5 graphics accelerator and hardware alpha-blending support via a round 1.4″ AMOLED display with 454×454 pixels.


This powerful collaboration showcases Qt’s impressive and easy-to-use graphics framework with Ambiq’s ultra-low powered GPU capabilities, enabling developers to design visually compelling smartphone-like user experiences on resource-constrained devices embedded with a microcontroller, saving manufacturers time and money to go to market.


The Apollo4 Plus Display Kit demo with the Qt for MCU-Powered Wearable HMI will be on display at Qt’s booth during Embedded World 2024 in **hall 4 booth #258** . Please[book a meeting](https://outlook.office365.com/book/AmbiqeW2024@ambiq.com/) to learn more.
