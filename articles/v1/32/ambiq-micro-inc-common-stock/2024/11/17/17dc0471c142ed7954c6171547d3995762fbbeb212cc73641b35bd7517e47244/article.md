# Ambiq Receives the 2024 Distinguished Golden Bull Award

Ambiq Receives the 2024 Distinguished Golden Bull Award 6


*From L to R: Joshua Zarraga, Matthew Chong, SenthilKumar Jayapal, and Eddy Hartono Halim accept the 2024 Distinguished Golden Bull Award on behalf of Fumihide Esaka, CEO of Ambiq*


**Ambiq®** , a leading developer of ultra-low-power semiconductors and solutions enabling edge AI, wins the **2024 Distinguished Golden Bull Award** on behalf of **CEO, Fumihide “Humi” Esaka** . The award ceremony, hosted in Singapore on November 15, 2024, benchmarks exemplary successful businesses, inspiring more businesses to strive for greater excellence.


Ambiq, a winner of the[2023 Golden Bull Award for Outstanding SME](https://ambiq.com/news/ambiq-receives-singapores-2023-golden-bull-award-for-outstanding-sme/) , has had an outstanding year, most notably the launch of their next-generation Apollo510 MCU, the most capable and low-powered semiconductor yet for enabling more advanced edge AI. They have expanded their market presence with electronics distributor Mouser and Computer Controls, collaborated with technology partners Bosch, TDK InvenSense, Qt Group, and most recently expanded their support for popular development platforms such as Zephyr and edge Impulse.


“As we near the end of the year, we can reflect on the great strides Ambiq has made and the lessons we learned that we will take with us next year,” said Fumihide Esaka, CEO of Ambiq. “This year’s achievements culminated in aligning ourselves with key technology partners, attending industry events, and continuously refining our energy-efficient technology and solutions. I want to thank our customers, investors, partners, and employees who continue to make Ambiq the leading semiconductor company for energy efficiency and edge AI.”

**About Ambiq**


Ambiq’s mission is to develop the lowest-power semiconductor solutions to enable intelligent devices everywhere and drive a more energy-efficient, sustainable, and data-driven world. Ambiq has helped leading manufacturers worldwide create products that last weeks on a single charge (rather than days) while delivering a maximum feature set in compact industrial designs. Ambiq’s goal is to take Artificial Intelligence (AI) where it has never gone before in mobile and portable devices, using Ambiq’s advanced ultra-low power system on chip (SoC) solutions. Ambiq has shipped more than 250 million units. For more information, visit[www.ambiq.com](http://www.ambiq.com/) .
