# Ambiq Micro Announces 2026 Annual General Meeting of Shareholders to Be Held on June 8

Ambiq Micro Announces 2026 Annual General Meeting of Shareholders to Be Held on June 8 2


**AUSTIN, Texas – June 02, 2026 – Ambiq Micro, Inc. (NYSE: AMBQ)** , a technology leader in ultra-low-power semiconductor solutions for edge AI, will hold its annual meeting of shareholders virtually on Monday, June 08, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. Central Time (11:00 a.m. Eastern Time).


The following resolutions, proposed by the Company’s Board of Directors, will be presented during the meeting:


1. To elect Timothy Chen and Ker Zhang, Ph.D., as Class I directors to serve until the Company’s 2029 annual meeting of stockholders and until their respective successors are duly elected and qualified, or until their earlier death, resignation, or removal;


2. To ratify the appointment of KPMG LLP, as selected by the Audit Committee of the Board of Directors, as the Company’s independent registered public accounting firm for the year ending December 31, 2026; and


3. To conduct any other business brought properly before the Annual Meeting.


Ambiq Micro has filed its definitive proxy statement and annual report with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. These materials are available on Ambiq’s investor relations website at[https://ir.ambiq.com/financials/sec-filings/default.aspx](https://ir.ambiq.com/financials/sec-filings/default.aspx) .


The Notice of Internet Availability of Proxy Materials was first made available to shareholders on April 22, 2026. Pre-registration for the annual meeting will be available until 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on June 7, 2026.


Shareholders can register to attend the annual meeting or request paper copies of the proxy materials at[https://proxydocs.com/AMBQ.](https://proxydocs.com/AMBQ)


**About Ambiq**
Headquartered in Austin, Texas, Ambiq’s mission is to enable intelligence (artificial intelligence (AI) and beyond) everywhere by delivering the lowest power semiconductor solutions. Ambiq enables its customers to deliver AI compute at the edge where power consumption challenges are the most severe. Ambiq’s technology innovations, built on the patented and proprietary subthreshold power optimized technology (SPOT®), fundamentally deliver a multi-fold improvement in power consumption over traditional semiconductor designs. Ambiq has powered over 300 million devices to date. For more information, visit[www.ambiq.com](https://www.ambiq.com/) .
