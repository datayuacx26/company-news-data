# Ambiq Showcases Ultra-Low Power Edge AI SoCs and NPU Innovation at Embedded World 2026

## ***Conference Session on Hardware-Optimized Edge AI Runtimes Scheduled for March 10, 10:30–11:00 a.m in the Exhibitor Forum, Hall 3-611***


Ambiq Showcases Ultra-Low Power Edge AI SoCs and NPU Innovation at Embedded World 2026 2


**AUSTIN, TX — March 10, 2026 — Ambiq Micro, Inc. (“Ambiq®”)** , a leader in ultra-low power semiconductor solutions for edge AI, will showcase its latest innovations at Embedded World 2026 in Nuremberg, Germany **(Hall 4, Booth 4-581)** , demonstrating how its technology enables always-on intelligence for wearables, body-worn AI, and edge devices.


## **Advancing Edge AI with Atomiq™**


Ambiq will spotlight its upcoming **Atomiq SoC** —the first SPOT® platform product with an integrated NPU. Built on a 12nm FinFET node, Atomiq introduces a new ultra-low power (ULP) mode operating at as low as **300mV** , the lowest voltage in company history, enabling more efficient always-on audio, vision, and reasoning AI at the edge.


## **Expanding the Apollo Portfolio**


Ambiq will also feature its latest **Apollo510 Lite** and **Apollo330 Plus** SoCs, further extending its SPOT-based portfolio for advanced edge AI processing and ultra-efficient embedded applications.


## **Award-Nominated AI Tools**


Ambiq will demonstrate optimized AI models and development tools from its **neuralSPOT®** SDK, including **heliaAOT™** . This ahead-of-time compiler converts trained models into highly optimized standalone C inference modules. heliaAOT has been nominated for Best AI and Tools at Embedded World 2026.


Dr. Adam Page, Head of AI at Ambiq, will present *“* ***A Faster Runtime: Taking a Granular Approach to Optimizing Edge AI with Ambiq*** *”* on **March 10, 10:30–11:00 a.m., Hall 3-611** .


## **Ecosystem Collaborations**


Booth demonstrations will also feature partner solutions that extend Ambiq’s ultra-low power SPOT platform:


- **Sequans + Ambiq:** Ultra-low power LTE for scalable, secure IoT at the edge.


- **IXANA + Ambiq:** Next-gen body area networking with greater bandwidth and energy efficiency than Bluetooth®.


- **Northern Mechatronics + Ambiq:** Long-range IPv6 mesh connectivity with ultra-efficient on-device AI.


Visitors are encouraged to stop by the booth orschedule a meeting with Ambiq’s technical team .


**About Ambiq**


Headquartered in Austin, Texas, Ambiq’s mission is to enable intelligence (artificial intelligence (AI) and beyond) everywhere by delivering the lowest power semiconductor solutions. Ambiq enables its customers to deliver AI compute at the edge, where power consumption challenges are the most severe. Ambiq’s technology innovations, built on the patented and proprietary subthreshold power optimized technology (SPOT®), fundamentally deliver a multi-fold improvement in power consumption over traditional semiconductor designs.
