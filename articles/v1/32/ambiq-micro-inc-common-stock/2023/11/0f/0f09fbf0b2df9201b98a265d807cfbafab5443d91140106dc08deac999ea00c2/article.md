# Ambiq Receives Singapore’s 2023 Golden Bull Award for Outstanding SME

From L to R: Chin Tong Thia, YewBoon Tan, and Florent Tardy receive the Golden Bull Award on behalf of Fumihide Esaka, CEO of Ambiq


**November 28, 2023, Austin, TX –** Ambiq, an industry leader in ultra-low power semiconductor solutions and technology that provides multifold energy efficiency, earned the **2023 Singapore Golden Bull Award for Outstanding SME** .


Ambiq’s family of cutting-edge products includes a diverse collection of system-on-chips (SoCs) that utilize sub-threshold energy through our patented Sub-threshold Power Optimized Technology (SPOT), enabling sophisticated computing, sensing, Bluetooth® connectivity, advanced graphics, and AI processing – 10X less than the power consumed by other products.


The Golden Bull Award, organized by Business Media International, is a platform that benchmarks successful businesses, inspiring more businesses, both SMEs and corporates, to strive for greater excellence. The nomination selects the best by emphasizing elimination by process. The award has also come to promote the growth of businesses to contribute to economic prosperity by paying tribute to the efforts of businesses for all their hard-won achievements.


“We’re honored to receive the prestigious Golden Bull Award,” **said Fumihide Esaka, CEO at Ambiq** . “Everyone in our organization has contributed so much to make Ambiq the go-to name in intelligent energy-efficient semiconductors, and they are the ones that make an award like this obtainable. Special thanks to our investor at EDBI, who has helped propel our growth and strengthen our presence in Singapore.”


EDBI is a global investor that helps shape Singapore’s future industries by investing in innovative sectors such as emerging technology. Earlier this year, they helped[Ambiq expand its operations in Singapore](https://ambiq.com/news/ambiq-achieves-milestone-of-powering-over-200-million-iot-devices/) to accelerate the development of its next generation of integrated circuit design and meet global demand.

**About Business Media International**


Business Media International is a business enabler and growth partner, that helps businesses in multiple sectors better understand their businesses, make better decisions, promote their businesses, and partner with them to grow their businesses.


It is also an Southeast Asia’s leading B2B publishers and the brands owner of SME Magazine, HR Asia, Capital Asia, SME100, Golden Bull Award and many more. The company has been at the forefront of helping businesses grow, by providing them with the business intelligence and opportunities needed for 20 years.


**About Ambiq**


Ambiq’s mission is to develop the lowest-power semiconductor solutions to enable intelligent devices everywhere by developing the lowest-power semiconductor solutions to drive a more energy-efficient, sustainable, and data-driven world. Ambiq has helped leading manufacturers worldwide develop products that last weeks on a single charge (rather than days), while delivering a maximum feature set in compact industrial designs. Ambiq’s goal is to take Artificial Intelligence (AI) where it has never gone before in mobile and portable devices, using Ambiq’s advanced ultra-low power system on chip (SoC) solutions. Ambiq has shipped more than 230 million units as of October 2023. For more information, visit[www.ambiq.com](http://www.ambiq.com/) .
