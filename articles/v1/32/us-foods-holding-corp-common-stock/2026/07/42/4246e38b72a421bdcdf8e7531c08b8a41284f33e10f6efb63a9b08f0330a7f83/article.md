# US Foods to Host Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results Conference Call and Webcast

### US Foods to Host Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results Conference Call and Webcast


07/23/2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s21.q4cdn.com/460693622/files/doc_news/US-Foods-to-Host-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results-Conference-Call-and-Webcast-2026.pdf)


ROSEMONT, Ill.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


US Foods Holding Corp. (NYSE: USFD) will host a live conference call and webcast to discuss second quarter 2026 results on Thursday, August 6, 2026, at 8 a.m. CDT.


The conference call can be accessed live over the phone by dialing (888) 660-6196. Listeners should dial in 10 minutes prior to the call start time and provide the Conference ID USFDQ226 to be connected.


A replay will be available after the call. To listen to a replay of the conference call, please register by clicking this[link](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.usfoods.com%2Fevents-and-presentations%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54575228&newsitemid=20260723150064&lan=en-US&anchor=link&index=1&md5=fab26983fc32599fa0f44df7b79e6e92) .


The conference call also will be webcast live from the company’s Investor Relations website at[https://ir.usfoods.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.usfoods.com&esheet=54575228&newsitemid=20260723150064&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fir.usfoods.com&index=2&md5=86fcfdd10ac84515f60ec159b3d9c0b8) . The presentation slides that will be reviewed during the webcast will be available in the Events & Presentations section of the Investor Relations website before the webcast begins. An archive of the webcast will be available at the same location beginning at 12 p.m. CDT on August 6, 2026.


**About US Foods**


With a promise to help its customers Make It, US Foods is one of America’s great food companies and a leading foodservice distributor, partnering with approximately 250,000 customer locations to help their businesses succeed. With more than 70 broadline locations and more than 90 cash and carry stores, US Foods and its 30,000 associates provides its customers with a broad and innovative food offering and a comprehensive suite of e-commerce, technology and business solutions. US Foods is headquartered in Rosemont, Ill.
