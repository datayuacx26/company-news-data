# bragg reaffirms commitment to information security with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 recertification


July 27, 2026


We are pleased to announce the successful completion of our


**ISO/IEC 27001:2022 recertification audit** .


Achieving this milestone validates our ongoing dedication to robust information security practices. For bragg, security is far more than a regulatory requirement, it is an operational standard embedded directly into our company culture and daily workflows.


### **Security Beyond Compliance**


Our certified operating entities in Slovenia and Malta have successfully completed their ISO/IEC 27001:2022 recertification audit, a milestone that reflects the strength of the Information Security Management System (ISMS) operated across the bragg group.


Rather than treating security as a static checklist, we maintain an active framework focused on:


- **Proactive risk management:** Identifying and mitigating potential security risks before they impact our systems.


- **Continuous improvement:** Constantly updating our controls and safeguards to adapt to emerging cyber threats.


- **Operational integration:** Ensuring security measures are seamless, effective, and fundamental to how we build and deliver our solutions.


### **What This Means for Our Customers & Partners**


Trust is the foundation of our relationships. Maintaining ISO/IEC 27001:202 standards ensures our clients and partners can operate with full confidence knowing their data is handled with the highest level of care.


Specifically, this recertification guarantees our commitment to the core pillars of information security:


- **Confidentiality:** Protecting sensitive data from unauthorized access.


- **Integrity:** Ensuring information remains accurate, complete, and reliable.


- **Availability:** Guaranteeing that authorized users have seamless access to critical systems when needed.


### **Looking Ahead**


As security threats and business needs evolve, so will we. We remain dedicated to fostering a culture of continuous security innovation, investing in our infrastructure, and upholding the highest international security standards to deliver reliable, resilient, and trusted services.
