### **4.**[Promethease](https://genomelink.io/blog/understanding-genome-with-promethease)


**Price:** $15 (additional files +$4 each) **Type:** Focused on health and traits analysis **Category:** Literature and database retrieval tool **Website Visitors:** 82.6K

Promethease is famous for health and disease-related analysis. The site is positioned as a "literature retrieval system," meaning it connects your raw DNA data file with a site called SNPedia. Researchers are updating this Wikipedia-like database to gather information about genetic markers associated with health conditions. It costs $15, but you can get a comprehensive analysis based on the available information from SNPedia. Reports are typically generated in 10-20 minutes.


**Key Features:**


- Links thousands of genetic variants to scientific literature
- Color-coded risk indicators
- Searchable reports by condition, trait, or medication response
- Data deleted after 45 days (or create an account for longer access)


**Note:** Promethease was acquired by MyHeritage in 2019 and continues to operate independently. However, the site is for educational and research purposes only, so if you are concerned with specific health conditions, I recommend not relying solely on it and talking to your doctor or genetic counselors.

### **8.**[YFull](https://www.yfull.com/)


**Price:** $49 for Y-DNA analysis; $25 for mtDNA **Type:** Raw DNA sequencing analysis and haplogroup placement **Category:** genetic genealogy / deep ancestry interpretation


> "Next-generation sequencing interpretation with detailed haplogroup and variant analysis"


YFull is a specialized DNA upload and interpretation service focused on placing your raw next-generation sequencing (NGS) data—especially full Y-chromosome (Y-DNA) and mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA)—onto comprehensive haplotype trees (YTree and MTree) and reporting detailed variants. Users upload BAM/VCF files from NGS tests such as Family Tree DNA's Big Y or whole genome sequencing and receive deep analysis of:


- **970,570+ known Y-SNPs**
- **780+ STR markers**
- Age estimates for haplogroups
- Terminal haplogroup placement
- Novel "private" SNP discovery


Unlike general DNA upload sites that prioritize relative matching, YFull is designed for advanced genetic genealogy and deep paternal/maternal lineage interpretation, making it particularly useful for serious researchers and citizen scientists who want fine-scale placement in human haplotrees and insights into ancient population structure.
