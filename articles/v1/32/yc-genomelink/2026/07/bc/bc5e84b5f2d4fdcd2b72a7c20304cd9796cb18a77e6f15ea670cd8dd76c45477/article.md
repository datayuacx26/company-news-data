# MyHeritage Review: A Comprehensive Guide

**Are you curious about your family’s past, hoping to connect with distant relatives worldwide, or looking for an affordable yet thorough DNA testing option? This MyHeritage review covers the platform’s main features, pricing, how it compares to other top services, and what real users say about it. By the end, you’ll see whether MyHeritage is the right choice to help you explore your ancestry and preserve your heritage.**

## **1. Service Description**


**MyHeritage caters to a wide range of genealogy enthusiasts, from total beginners to long-time family historians. It focuses on four key areas: building family trees, searching historical records, DNA testing, and photo/storytelling tools.**


### **a. Family Tree Building**


**MyHeritage provides a user-friendly family tree builder. You start by entering basic details—your own name, plus information about parents, grandparents, and beyond. MyHeritage then taps into its large database to locate matching records, suggesting leads to expand your tree faster than manual research alone. An AI-driven Consistency Checker also reviews your entries for possible errors, like mismatched birthdates or duplicated marriages.**


### **b. Historical Record Search**


**With 33.3 billion historical records, MyHeritage is poised to help you uncover key details about your ancestors. The database includes censuses, passenger lists, newspapers, and more. Fresh data is added regularly, meaning new discoveries can appear at any time. One standout collection is the 1950 U.S. Census, fully indexed and free to search—a boon for tracing relatives who lived during that era.**


### **c. DNA Testing**


**MyHeritage’s approach to DNA testing involves a cheek swab, which many find simpler than a saliva sample. The test reveals your ethnic makeup across 2,114 global regions, offering precise insights into your heritage. In addition to standard percentages (e.g., 50% English (Yorkshire), 20% Norwegian, etc.), MyHeritage pinpoints Genetic Groups for even greater detail about ancestral migration patterns. You can also connect with relatives who share segments of your genetic code, confirming family lines or uncovering new branches you didn’t realize existed.**


**Privacy and Security are paramount; all DNA samples are processed in a CLIA-certified, CAP-accredited lab, and user data is protected by multiple layers of encryption. MyHeritage’s terms of service give you control over how your genetic data is used, letting you configure privacy levels to suit your comfort.**


### **d. Photo Enhancement and Storytelling**


**MyHeritage’s photo enhancement tools help bring the past to life. You can colorize black-and-white images, restore faded colors, enhance blurry faces, or animate photos with features like Deep Nostalgia™. DeepStory allows you to produce video narratives of your ancestors’ lives. These features add emotional depth to your research, helping you see historical relatives in a fresh and personal way.**


**In essence, MyHeritage offers a full suite of genealogy resources, from building extensive family trees to animating your great-grandparents’ portraits—all under one roof.**

**We ran an internal survey called “**[Landscape of DNA Tests 2025](https://genomelink.io/blog/landscape-of-best-dna-test-kit-2025) **,” asking 1,123 participants about three leading providers: AncestryDNA, 23andMe, and MyHeritage. - **AncestryDNA: 829**

- MyHeritage: 58%


- **Health Insights & Traits** :


- AncestryDNA: 62%
- 23andMe: 61%
- MyHeritage: 61%


**AncestryDNA leads in genealogy, although MyHeritage is close in some categories, especially Ethnicity & Origins and Health & Traits.**


### **Overall Recommendation**


**When asked which test they would recommend to others, participants answered:**


- **AncestryDNA:** 68.2%
- **23andMe:** 53.5%
- **MyHeritage:** 31.3%


**This suggests AncestryDNA is the top choice, with 23andMe second. MyHeritage trails in recommendation rates, yet it’s still well-liked by its user base.**


### **Which Purpose Is Best Served By Each?**


- **Genealogical Research** : AncestryDNA stands out, supported by a large user base and robust integration with historical records.
- **Exploring Ethnicity & Origins** : AncestryDNA slightly edges out 23andMe, and MyHeritage also does well, matching 59% satisfaction.
- **Connecting with DNA Relatives** : AncestryDNA has a slight lead, possibly due to its scale.
- **Health Insights & Traits** : 23andMe often ranks higher, but MyHeritage’s 61% satisfaction is close behind.


**Overall, AncestryDNA remains the top option for genealogy-heavy users, while MyHeritage excels at ethnicity details and ties with 23andMe on health insights. Despite having a smaller user base, MyHeritage still posted an equal overall satisfaction score (62%), which shows that many who choose MyHeritage are quite happy with it.**

## **4. Real Users’ Reviews: Why MyHeritage?**


**Besides comparing MyHeritage to other services, we asked respondents a direct question: “Why do you recommend MyHeritage?” After removing non-informative answers, we categorized 105 clear responses into five themes:**


### **a. Accuracy / Detailed Ethnicity (~31%)**


- Many praised how MyHeritage clarifies ancestral origins, describing it as “thorough” or “precise.”
- Some noted it verifies migration patterns or health traits through consistent data.
- Users liked the detailed breakdown of their genetic data, which can confirm a family story or reveal unexpected lineage.


### **b. Ease of Use & Interface (~21%)**


- People often described the platform as “fast and easy” or “intuitive.”
- Even newcomers to DNA testing stated they found it “super easy” to navigate.
- Others praised how they can conveniently customize privacy settings under the terms of service, ensuring they remain in control of their details.


### **c. Large Database & Many Matches (~20%)**


- Several users reported more international matches with MyHeritage, particularly in European and Scandinavian regions.
- A big database means a better chance to locate relatives, which can be important for genealogical breakthroughs.


### **d. Genealogy Tools & Integration (~14%)**


- Respondents highlighted helpful features like **clustering maps** , advanced matching, and offline/online tree syncing.
- The ability to **upload raw genetic data** from other services was a bonus for individuals wanting deeper insights.
- Integration of trees, records, and photo tools in one place resonated with hobbyists and serious researchers alike.


### **e. Affordability / Price (~13%)**


- Many users enjoyed the **cost-effective** nature of MyHeritage, especially taking advantage of sales on the DNA kits or discounted subscriptions.
- For those on a budget, the lower overall cost combined with robust genealogical features is compelling.


### **Key Takeaways from Real Users**


- **Detailed Ethnicity Insights** stand out as a strong reason to pick MyHeritage, particularly if you’re interested in exploring multiple global regions.
- The platform’s user interface is simple enough that even beginners won’t feel overwhelmed.
- MyHeritage helps you find relatives all over the world, which is especially valuable if your ancestry is spread across continents.
- Many users cite price as a deciding factor, affirming the platform’s affordability.


**Although MyHeritage comes in third on overall recommendations, plenty of people love it for its thorough results, easy setup, and specialized genealogical tools.**
