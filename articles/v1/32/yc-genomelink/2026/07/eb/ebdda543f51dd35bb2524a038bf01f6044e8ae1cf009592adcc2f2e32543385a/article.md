# The 4 most reputable free ancestry search websites using DNA information

### **How can I get DNA information on ancestry for free?**


**While there is no free DNA test** , several companies do allow you to get many useful features of their platform simply by uploading your previously acquired DNA data. No genealogist? No problem! The companies below will allow you to explore different aspects of your family story simply by setting up a free account and uploading a raw data file!


‍


Here’s some features with free access from various companies: ****


- Search for family members
- Conduct genealogical research
- Use search engine operations to find free family tree information ****


Whether your relatives are Canadian, Indian, Jewish, British, Irish, Native American, or a mix of them all, there are some free tools below which can help you find vital information and records on free genealogy sites. Check it out!


‍ ****


### [Family Tree DNA](https://www.familytreedna.com/) ****


This company accepts tests from:


- 23andMe (Versions 3-5)
- AncestryDNA (Versions 1-2)
- MyHeritage **‍** ‍ **‍**


FTDNA allows you to upload a file from any of the sources above to access their Family Finder Matrix. The Matrix is a program on their platform intended to find your closest relatives. This free tool may help you build your family tree, discover you family history, or conduct simple genealogy research. However, the free upload does NOT give you access to the full range of Family Finder features.


‍


For only $19 you can get access to these extra features which include the Chromosome Browser, myOrigins ethnicity estimate, and the ancientOrigins maternal and paternal haplotype test. You can check out their raw DNA data upload page here:[https://www.familytreedna.com/autosomal-transfer](https://www.familytreedna.com/autosomal-transfer)


[‍](https://www.familytreedna.com/autosomal-transfer) ****


### [MyHeritage](https://www.myheritage.com/) ****


This company accepts tests from:


- 23andMe
- LivingDNA
- AncestryDNA
- FamilyTreeDNA ****


MyHeritage is one of the largest DNA genealogy sites around, competing with Ancestry.com and other testing companies. MyHeritage allows customers to upload data from any of the above companies for free, and free users can get access to living genetic matches to conduct a recent genealogy search. MyHeritage users are largely based in Europe. So, if you are looking for a British uncle, a cousin in Ireland, or a half brother in Germany, this is a good choice. With the free service you can also use the Chromosome Browser, and get an Ethnicity Estimate based on 42 different regions.


‍


Before the days of DNA testing, MyHeritage focused largely on creating software for genealogy resources such as family trees and a database of genealogical records. Records kept in the database include censuses, death records, military records, and passenger lists from European countries. To access these features, however, you must subscribe to the company’s service, starting at $139/year (the price jumps to starting at $189 in your second year of subscription). To learn more about uploading your data to MyHeritage, see the page below:[https://blog.myheritage.com/2018/09/new-myheritage-supports-23andme-v5-and-living-dna-uploads/](https://blog.myheritage.com/2018/09/new-myheritage-supports-23andme-v5-and-living-dna-uploads/) **‍**


**‍**


### [LivingDNA](https://livingdna.com/) ****


This company accepts tests from:


- AncestryDNA
- 23andMe
- MyHeritage
- FamilyTreeDNA
- Gene by Gene
- Geno 2.0 ****


LivingDNA is a genetic genealogy company based mainly in the United Kingdom. Using raw data files from any of the companies above, you can upload your sample to the LivingDNA platform. With a free account you will be able to access the DNA Matching portal, which will give you access to any DNA matches present within the LivingDNA database.


‍


However, with a free upload users will NOT receive the detailed ancestry reports provided to paying consumers. LivingDNA offers autosomal, mtDNA, and Y DNA tests which can analyze everything from your recent ancestry to deep ancestral information such as your DNA haplogroup. For this, you will have to purchase a DNA test kit. To see more information on uploading to LivingDNA, see the following page:[https://support.livingdna.com/hc/en-us/articles/360012509652-What-format-does-my-file-need-to-be-in-to-successfully-upload- ‍](https://support.livingdna.com/hc/en-us/articles/360012509652-What-format-does-my-file-need-to-be-in-to-successfully-upload-)

### [GEDmatch](https://support.livingdna.com/hc/en-us/articles/360012509652-What-format-does-my-file-need-to-be-in-to-successfully-upload-) ****


This company accepts tests from:


- 23andMe
- FamilyTreeDNA
- AncestryDNA
- Most other companies ****


The GEDmatch platform is not nearly as user-friendly as other sites. However, it can be far more powerful if you learn how to use it. A lot of genealogy enthusiasts upload to GEDmatch to look within its expanded pool of people for more DNA matches that may not exist in their original testing company’s database; the ability to transcend the barriers between many testing companies is a clear advantage of the GEDmatch platform.


‍


Plus, many of the most powerful tools for genealogy research are completely free on GEDmatch. These tools include an admixture analysis (aka ethnicity estimate), autosomal DNA comparison with matches, X-DNA comparison, and even tools to compare GEDCOM family tree files created by other sites. This tool could be an extremely powerful free ancestry DNA test if it is used properly.


‍


For a donation of $10/month, the site will also give you access to a number of “Tier 1” tools. These include an enhanced DNA comparison, the ability to separate your maternal and paternal autosomal DNA, and the ability to estimate a deceased person’s DNA using the Lazarus tool. If you’re interested in learning more in specific detail about how to use GEDmatch,[see our blog post on this topic](https://genomelink.io/blog/how-to-use-gedmatch) . **‍**

**How to Check My Ancestry**


‍


There are several ways to check your ancestry using DNA analysis. One of the most popular methods is to take a DNA test. These tests typically involve collecting a sample of your DNA, often through a saliva sample, and sending it to a testing company. The company will then analyze your DNA and provide you with a report that includes information about your ancestry and can also include information about your genetic health, inherited traits, and potential family matches.


‍


There are many companies that offer DNA testing for ancestry purposes, including Ancestry DNA, 23andMe, MyHeritage, and LivingDNA, among others. Each company has its own strengths and weaknesses, so it's important to do some research and read reviews to find the best option for you.


‍


**Ancestry DNA Review**


‍


Ancestry DNA is a popular DNA testing service that provides customers with insights into their family history and genetic makeup. With a database of over 18 million individuals, Ancestry DNA has become one of the most comprehensive ancestry services on the market. One of the standout features of Ancestry DNA is the company's large database of user profiles. With so many users, Ancestry DNA can provide customers with a wealth of information.


‍


Ancestry DNA does not offer free DNA testing, but they sometimes offer discounts and special offers. The regular price for Ancestry DNA testing is $99. Ancestry DNA also offers a subscription service that provides access to genealogical records and additional features, but this is a separate service from DNA testing and requires a monthly or yearly fee.


‍


**Summary of Free Ethnicity Tests**


‍


There is no free DNA test available, but some companies allow you to upload previously acquired raw DNA data and access many useful features of their platform for free. While some DNA testing companies may occasionally offer discounts or promotions on their ethnicity testing services, there are no DNA testing companies that offer a completely free ethnicity test.


‍


The cost of developing and processing the test is generally high, which makes it difficult for companies to offer the service for free. However, some companies may offer free DNA uploads or other free features related to DNA testing, but these may not be related specifically to ethnicity testing. The 4 most reputable ancestry testing companies that offer free services are Family Tree DNA, MyHeritage, LivingDNA, and GEDMatch.


‍


- Family Tree DNA allows you to upload a raw data file and use their Family Finder Matrix to find your closest relatives for free.


‍


- MyHeritage is one of the largest DNA genealogy sites, and free users can get access to living genetic matches.


‍


- LivingDNA accepts raw data from various DNA testing companies, and offers DNA matching for free.


‍


- GEDmatch is another platform that accepts DNA data from multiple companies and offers powerful genealogy research tools, including free admixture analysis and autosomal DNA comparison with matches.


‍
