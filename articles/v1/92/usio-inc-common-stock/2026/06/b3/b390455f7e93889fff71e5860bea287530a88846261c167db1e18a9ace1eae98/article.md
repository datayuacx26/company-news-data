# 7 Unique Employee Rewards For Inspiration

*Powered by Usio Card Issuing*


Let’s be honest. No employee has said, “I was about to accept another job offer, but then management gave us branded stress balls.” And while the occasional coffee mug is nice, most employees already have enough company swag to open their own gift shop.


Today’s employees want recognition that feels immediate, meaningful, and useful. The good news? You don’t have to build a complicated rewards program or hand out giant game-show checks.


With


[Usio Card Issuing](https://usio.com/usio-card-issuing-solutions/) , companies can instantly send rewards, incentives, bonuses, and recognition directly to physical or virtual Mastercards. Employees get rewarded right now, not three payroll cycles from now.


Here are seven creative ways to make your team smile, stay engaged, and maybe even stop stealing each other’s lunch from the breakroom refrigerator.


## 1. The Company Logo Selfie Challenge


Your logo is everywhere: on laptops, coffee mugs, trade show booths, company swag, and office walls. Why not turn it into a game?


Challenge employees to take the most creative photo featuring the company logo. Maybe it’s a selfie at a customer site, a team photo at a volunteer event, or an artistic shot during a business trip. Let employees vote on their favorites and award prepaid card rewards to the winners.


Not only does it create engagement, but you’ll also end up with a library of authentic content that marketing will love.


## 2. The “Caught Being Awesome” Mystery Reward


Everyone enjoys a little surprise.


Each month, managers secretly nominate employees who go above and beyond. Maybe they helped a coworker, solved a customer issue, or stepped up during a crunch.


At the end of the month, winners receive an unexpected reward loaded directly onto their card. No warning. No paperwork. Just an email saying, “You were caught being awesome.”


It’s basically the workplace version of finding money in your winter coat pocket.


## 3. Shark Tank Without the Sharks


Great ideas don’t always come from executives. Sometimes the best ideas come from the employee who’s been quietly fixing the same inefficient process for three years.


Create an innovation challenge and invite employees to submit ideas that save money, improve customer experiences, or streamline operations. Winning ideas receive instant rewards through a Usio-issued card.


The next million-dollar idea might be sitting three desks away from the office snack machine.


## 4. Office Olympics


Who says team-building has to involve trust falls?


Create a month-long Office Olympics featuring challenges like fastest email cleanup, most creative Zoom background, step-count competitions, trivia contests, desk decorating competitions, or customer compliment leaderboards.


Employees earn points throughout the month, and top performers receive rewards instantly loaded onto their cards.


Gold medals are nice. Spending money is nicer.


## 5. The “Teach Us Something” Challenge


Every employee has a hidden talent.


Maybe someone speaks three languages. Maybe someone can bake award-winning cookies. Maybe someone knows how to build furniture, fly drones, or take incredible photographs.


Invite employees to host short lunch-and-learn sessions sharing a skill or hobby. Attendees vote on the most entertaining and useful sessions, and presenters earn card-based rewards.


You’ll build culture, uncover hidden talents, and finally learn why Karen from accounting keeps winning barbecue competitions.


## 6. The Professional Development Jackpot


Every company says they value learning. Few make it exciting.


Instead of simply reimbursing certifications, gamify the process. Employees earn rewards for completing certifications, attending training programs, learning new technologies, or earning professional credentials.


At Usio, employees who earn the Certified Payments Professional (CPP) designation receive a $500 reward. Anyone in the payments industry will tell you the CPP exam isn’t easy. It requires significant study, industry knowledge, and dedication. That’s exactly why the reward is so effective. The achievement is celebrated, employees feel recognized for their hard work, and the company benefits from a workforce with deeper payments expertise.


Create milestones along the way, with larger rewards tied to bigger achievements. Employees gain knowledge. The company gains expertise. Everyone gains money.


It’s one of the few workplace programs where literally nobody loses.


## 7. Holiday Bonuses With a Twist


The holidays already involve enough stress. Travel. Shopping. Family members asking why you’re still single.


Instead of handing out the same holiday bonus every year, create a “Holiday Spin-to-Win” event. Employees spin a digital wheel featuring various reward amounts, extra PTO hours, charitable donations, or surprise prizes.


Whatever they win is instantly loaded onto their Usio reward card.


It’s fun, memorable, and significantly more exciting than another fruit basket.


## Why Employees Love Card-Based Rewards


Here’s the secret.


Employees don’t necessarily want more stuff. They want more choice.


Gift cards tell employees where they can spend. Reward cards let employees decide. Maybe it’s dinner. Maybe it’s concert tickets. Maybe it’s a weekend getaway. Maybe it’s finally replacing that office chair their cat destroyed during a video call.


The point is that the reward becomes personal. And personal rewards create lasting memories.
