# Insurance conferences commercial brokers must attend in 2026: the fall circuit

The short version


- Eleven events between August 28 and November 11, with dates checked against each organizer’s own site in July 2026.
- ITC Vegas (Sept 29 to Oct 1, Mandalay Bay) is the technology show. CIAB, WSIA, and TMPAA are the meeting shows, where the value is a calendar booked weeks ahead.
- ABU’s national convention already passed. The next ABU event is the one-day Southern California Mini-vention on August 28 in Ontario, CA.
- CopyCat will have a booth at ABU and at ITC Vegas.
- November is quiet for commercial P&C. Only one national event lands, and it is life and annuity.


The insurance conference calendar is front-loaded into fall. Between late August and mid-November 2026, a commercial broker can hit a one-day regional trade show, the largest insurtech gathering in the world, the meeting where the biggest brokerage CEOs negotiate carrier relationships, and the two events where wholesale and program business actually gets placed.


You cannot go to all of them. Below is the fall circuit with verified dates, venues, and official links, organized by month, with a note on who each one is genuinely for. Always re-check the organizer page before booking travel, because venues and dates do move.

## October 2026


### [The Council’s Insurance Leadership Forum](https://www.ciab.com/events/insurance-leadership-forum)


October 2 to 6, The Broadmoor, Colorado Springs, CO.


ILF is the premier marketplace meeting for top-tier broker executives in commercial property and casualty. The Council reports more than 1,200 attendees, heavily weighted toward C-suite executives, practice leaders, and decision makers. This is a relationship and negotiation meeting rather than an education conference, and the calendar is wall-to-wall private carrier meetings.


Go if you sit at the top of a brokerage and own carrier relationships. Otherwise this is not your event.


### [WSIA Annual Marketplace](https://www.wsia.org/)


October 11 to 14, Manchester Grand Hyatt San Diego and Marriott Marquis San Diego Marina, San Diego, CA.


The Wholesale & Specialty Insurance Association’s Annual Marketplace is the premier networking and business event for the wholesale, specialty, and surplus lines industry. As with ILF, the value is in the pre-booked meeting schedule, not the sessions.


Go if you place E&S business and want concentrated wholesale market time. Book meetings months ahead, because the calendar fills before you arrive.


### [TMPAA 26th Annual Summit](https://targetmkts.com/national-meeting/26th-annual-tmpaa-summit/)


October 19 to 21, The Westin Kierland Resort & Spa, Scottsdale, AZ.


The Target Markets Program Administrators Association Summit is where program business gets done. TMPAA expects more than 2,000 attendees across three groups: program administrators, carriers, and service providers. It grew from 150 attendees at the first Summit.


Go if you run a program, want to launch one, or need carrier capacity for a niche book.


### [CPCU Society In2Risk](https://www.in2risk.org/)


October 19 to 21, Las Vegas, NV.


The Institutes CPCU Society’s annual meeting and CPCU conferment celebration is three days of peer-led education and designation recognition. It overlaps directly with TMPAA, so pick one.


Go if you are conferring a CPCU, active in a CPCU chapter, or want technical education rather than market access.


## November 2026


November is genuinely quiet for commercial P&C. The fall circuit spends itself by late October and the industry turns to January 1 renewals. The one significant national event is on the life and benefits side.


### [Protectors Vegas](https://protectorsvegas.com/)


November 9 to 11, The Venetian, Las Vegas, NV.


NAIFA and Connectiv have rebuilt NAIFA’s flagship conference as Protectors Vegas, positioned as the largest gathering of the life and annuity industry. It convenes more than 1,500 advisors, carriers, distributors, technology providers, and investors across three streams: distribution, product and strategy, and technology and innovation. NAIFA has offered a complimentary all-access pass to qualified client-facing agents, advisors, and agency principals.


Go if your agency has a life, annuity, or benefits practice. If you are pure commercial P&C, use November to catch up on the renewals you ignored in October.


## How to actually get value out of a conference


Most brokers come home from a big show with a tote bag and no decisions. A few habits change that.


1. Book meetings before you land. At ILF, WSIA, and TMPAA the entire value is the pre-scheduled calendar, and walking the floor cold wastes a flight.
2. Bring real documents to technology shows. Carry the carrier PDF that always breaks, the agency template with the unusual tables, and the renewal that took your team a full day. Ask vendors to run them live, at the booth.
3. Ask what the price actually includes. Implementation fees, per-seat minimums, per-module add-ons, and annual lock-in contracts are where a cheap-looking demo becomes an expensive system.
4. Ask how fast support responds. Not whether they have support, but the actual response time when a live client deliverable breaks on a Friday afternoon.
5. Pick one problem per show. Go in with a single bottleneck you want solved and evaluate everything against it.
