Long-term care insurance is sold through details. A client needs to understand how much a policy may pay, when benefits begin, how long they may continue, which care settings qualify, and what changes if inflation protection or a rider is added. Sending several carrier illustrations does not make that decision easier. It gives the client more pages to decode.


Long-term care and short-term care insurance proposal software should do more than make those pages look polished. It should extract the terms accurately, normalize different carrier formats, explain the tradeoffs, and return a proposal the broker can confidently present. That is the workflow CopyCat is built to handle.


## What is long-term care insurance proposal software?


Long-term care insurance proposal software converts carrier quote documents and illustrations into one client-facing proposal. Instead of rebuilding each option in Word or PowerPoint, the broker uploads the source files and reviews a consistent comparison of the terms that drive the decision.


This category sits between quoting and the client conversation. It is not a carrier rater, CRM, or agency management system. It is the document layer that turns the quotes already produced by carriers into a clear recommendation under the brokerage's brand.


## LTC and STC proposals are an extraction problem first


Care insurance quotes are not standardized for easy comparison. The same concept can appear under different labels, in a different order, or several pages away from the premium. A useful proposal has to normalize meaning, not just copy nearby text.


Decision field What the proposal should make clear


Benefit amount The daily or monthly amount available for covered care


Benefit period or pool How long benefits may last and the total available value


Elimination period When benefits can begin and how the waiting period is counted


Care settings How home care, assisted living, adult day care, and facility care are treated


Benefit triggers The conditions that must be met before benefits become payable


Inflation option Whether and how the available benefit can increase over time


Riders and features What is included, optional, limited, or excluded


Premium The cost of each design and the tradeoff behind it


These are not cosmetic fields. The National Association of Insurance Commissioners advises buyers to compare benefits, covered facilities, coverage limits, exclusions, and premium across policies. Its buyer guidance also explains why elimination periods and inflation protection materially change a policy. The federal Administration for Community Living similarly notes that benefit amounts, benefit duration, and optional inflation protection affect the design and cost of coverage. See the[NAIC long-term care insurance guide](https://content.naic.org/consumer/long-term-care-insurance.htm) and the[Administration for Community Living overview](https://acl.gov/ltc/costs-and-who-pays/what-is-long-term-care-insurance) .

## How better proposals help brokers win more clients


Clients do not choose care coverage because a PDF looked modern. They choose when the tradeoffs feel understandable and the recommendation feels credible. A strong proposal helps the broker create that confidence in four ways:


- It puts the recommendation before the supporting detail.
- It compares policy designs using the same language and structure.
- It keeps important differences visible instead of burying them in illustrations.
- It gives every number a source the broker can verify during the conversation.


The result is a faster path from quote review to a client decision, with less time spent formatting and more time available for advice. That is the practical promise of insurance proposal software.


## What to look for in LTC and STC proposal software


When comparing platforms, run a real carrier file through each one. Do not evaluate a prepared demo alone. Check whether the tool can handle the terminology and layout of the documents your team actually receives.


- **Extraction depth:** Does it capture the benefit design, not just the premium?
- **Source verification:** Can every important value be traced to its page?
- **Template fidelity:** Does the result look like your brokerage created it?
- **Editability:** Can the producer change language and structure before export?
- **Workflow breadth:** Can the same platform support LTC, STC, commercial, personal lines, and employee benefits?
- **Security:** Are documents protected and excluded from model training?
