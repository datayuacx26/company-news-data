# Xacta is Changing Cyber GRC…Again

We’re proud to announce the launch of[Xacta.ai](https://www.telos.com/offerings/xacta-ai) , an innovative artificial intelligence (AI)-powered capability at the core of the[Xacta](https://www.telos.com/offerings/xacta/) cyber governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) platform.


This latest innovation from Telos builds on a legacy of pioneering work in the cyber GRC industry, which began with the launch of Xacta in 2000.


### **The Evolution of Xacta**


Over the years, Xacta has continually pushed the boundaries of what’s possible in GRC.


In August 2000, we launched the first version of Xacta, called Xacta Web C&A (Certification and Accreditation). This web-based tool was designed to automate the most tedious and time-consuming parts of the C&A process.


Demand grew quickly. Within a year, we secured our first enterprise customer. At the time, there was nothing else like it on the market.


By 2002, managing security risk and compliance was still an emerging concept. When we demonstrated Xacta Web C&A to a well-known industry analyst — who would later define what we now refer to as the governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) industry — he credited Xacta as a catalyst in shaping the field.


In 2004, the team behind Xacta pioneered the concept of continuous monitoring. Today, continuous monitoring is not only a critical element of the federal government A&A process but is also[regarded](https://www.isaca.org/resources/news-and-trends/isaca-now-blog/2024/the-never-ending-quest-why-continuous-monitoring-is-crucial-for-cybersecurity) as a best practice in the commercial sector.


Since then, Xacta has evolved into a comprehensive cyber GRC automation platform designed to help organizations meet the complex challenges of managing cyber risk and security compliance.


Now, with Xacta.ai, we’re adding to the powerful functionality of Xacta by giving you the power to get precise, accurate responses to your GRC process questions in seconds.


### **The Challenges of Traditional IT Risk Management and Security Compliance**


IT risk management and security compliance are critical components of cyber GRC, and are areas where AI can have a substantial impact.


Traditional approaches to compliance with security frameworks and regulations often rely on manual processes. This reliance can lead to delays and inefficiencies, making it challenging for organizations to keep pace with emerging threats.


Additionally, manual processes are vulnerable to errors, which can have serious consequences.


The complexity of meeting IT risk and security requirements is further complicated by the need to[navigate a constantly evolving regulatory landscape](https://www.securityweek.com/cyber-insights-2025-cybersecurity-regulatory-mayhem) , manage multiple stakeholders, and balance competing priorities.


### **The Broader Pivot to AI-Enabled Automation**


The launch of Xacta.ai marks an essential step in the wider transition to AI-enabled automation. Organizations across various industries are adopting AI-powered solutions to drive efficiency, reduce costs, and improve decision-making. Within the scope of IT risk management and security compliance, AI-powered solutions like Xacta.ai are being used to streamline and automate manual tasks, identify potential risks, and provide recommendations for mitigation.


### **The Benefits of AI-Powered IT Risk Management**


AI-powered solutions, such as Xacta.ai, are being utilized to streamline IT risk management processes, resulting in increased efficiency, accuracy, and speed. In simple terms, Xacta.ai empowers IT risk and security compliance teams to make informed decisions faster.


By using Xacta.ai, organizations can significantly reduce the time and effort required to implement controls, improving both accuracy and speed. For example, Xacta.ai can help organizations save up to 93% of the time it would take to implement controls manually, allowing teams to focus on higher-priority tasks.


Additionally, AI-powered insights equip organizations to make better-informed decisions and minimize the risk of error, ensuring they are better equipped to manage IT risk. Xacta.ai empowers teams to identify hidden risks by conducting comprehensive analyses of vulnerability-based data, project information, testing results, and external threat intelligence.


### **Where AI-Powered Cyber GRC is Headed**


AI-powered solutions are set to play a crucial role in the IT risk management and security compliance landscape.


In the federal government, the adoption of AI technologies is encouraged. The White House’s Office of Management and Budget (OMB) issued[Memorandum M-25-21 in April 2025](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/M-25-21-Accelerating-Federal-Use-of-AI-through-Innovation-Governance-and-Public-Trust.pdf) , which encourages the adoption of AI by federal agencies, stating “agencies must adopt a forward-leaning and pro-innovation approach that takes advantage of this technology to help shape the future of government operations.”


### **Why Now is the Time for Xacta.ai**


The use of AI in cyber GRC has transitioned from being a novelty to a necessity.


Organizations across various industries face increasing pressure to manage complex risks and comply with evolving regulations. IT risk and security compliance experts in both the public and private sectors need to make fast and accurate decisions while evaluating more data than ever before.


This is precisely the challenge that Xacta.ai was designed to address. With its integrated research and years of innovative development, Xacta.ai provides the equivalent of a team of seasoned experts trained in over two decades of GRC best practices, ready to answer your questions.


Xacta is the proven solution for cyber risk management and compliance among the world’s most security-conscious organizations. Now, with Xacta.ai, you gain the ability to receive precise and accurate responses to your GRC process questions in seconds.


As a member of the team that helped to define the GRC industry over two decades ago, I’m excited to see Xacta poised once again to redefine the future of cyber GRC. This innovation empowers IT risk and security experts to work more accurately and efficiently than ever before.
