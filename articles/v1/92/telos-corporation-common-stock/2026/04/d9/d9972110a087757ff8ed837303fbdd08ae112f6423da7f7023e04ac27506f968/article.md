# Young Travelers Can Save $20 on TSA PreCheck Enrollment Starting May 1

# Young Travelers Can Save $20 on TSA PreCheck Enrollment Starting May 1


April 30, 2026 at 8:29 AM EDT


[PDF Version](https://investors.telos.com/node/13206/pdf)


**Limited-time offer helps travelers 30 and under enroll ahead of peak summer travel**


ASHBURN, Va., April 30, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Telos Corporation](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=vWY8t4ahFO0N_PEd4am48VtP3PL-PMgRQ2HCk2LT5usCwjpsPbbbG5dwcNaA7KV5YH2ISnYIJunt_TCqrs5NiBQy47ohyymI68iHoJypUAU=) (NASDAQ: TLS), an authorized TSA PreCheck® enrollment provider, announced today that the TSA PreCheck $20 Take Off promotion begins May 1, giving eligible applicants 30 and under a discounted way to enroll ahead of the summer travel rush.


From May 1 through May 31, 2026, new TSA PreCheck applicants age 30 and younger can receive $20 off the enrollment fee when they complete their in-person enrollment. The discount is applied automatically – no promo code required.


As travel demand ramps up heading into the summer months, TSA PreCheck offers a faster, more predictable airport experience – allowing eligible travelers to move through security without removing shoes, laptops, 3-1-1 liquids, belts or light jackets.


Telos is offering the $20 Take Off promotion at more than 500 TSA PreCheck enrollment locations nationwide, including convenient in-store options at select USPS and Office Depot® OfficeMax® locations. Earlier this month, Telos launched[TSA PreCheck enrollment at the University of Central Florida](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=HvpC4_ws95L8bE14FcopCH4_PP-Ja4FwOu9a0z-ZfgwLr7iQj1vvrFKJ-D7na1K5wEe5DzBB6CddMuNW2RmIThH8mBTCmT9Ijix5pqOIsNAUeQWTxGTba64uhGuY96bF2DAP1V-Uf2CihcdMjIM8Uupk8V621JRp4rjFcaj5aLt3Jt5vm_1oUbCza7RXSn7CfkfTLptGYhBfiEmJ5sYQXNnN2NrFJ9f0hYUbhKZVZGJKh5Lg1zu_bUdumtKQlab6dExd8CYC2XBaIbXiqp5o5w==) (UCF), bringing on-campus access to more than 70,000 students, as well as faculty, staff, and the surrounding Orlando community.


“With summer travel plans taking shape, TSA PreCheck can make a meaningful difference in how quickly and smoothly travelers move through the airport,” said Tam Phan, vice president of Vetting Services, Telos Corporation. “The $20 Take Off promotion gives young people a simple, affordable way to enroll – and with more than 500 convenient locations nationwide, getting started is easier than ever.”


The $20 Take Off promotion is available only for new TSA PreCheck enrollments and cannot be combined with other discounts. To qualify, applicants must be 30 years old or younger and complete their in-person enrollment between May 1 and May 31, 2026. Applications completed after May 31 are not eligible for the discount, and renewals are not included.


Once enrolled, TSA PreCheck members receive a[Known Traveler Number (KTN)](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=4TudLOLs6a8yxKlmqu82lomFt8_F3WoV6uptKYRzmrDGuKwCCb5LIfA9MgTaDep5ARturgyYvucF_xJZs7sAF6MjVY9-RhIea_gfsZEuCITMx05Lyp5TrwsoIa9xZnjUX0SszDXF63n1sbY5XrbfBGcQRD6vrT5qAB3UOWWB4d4=) and can enjoy expedited security screening at more than 200 participating U.S. airports for five years.


To learn more or schedule an appointment, visit[https://tsaprecheckbytelos.tsa.dhs.gov/](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=YmXVBj9MwlnOHkzitLgCygmj8C-XA15xQxoZKZOL1APKx5wFzRekuPtDhfckTOqd697ULj3TV6Px18KIa9xTAQ6ouqyesTprfyW81zP1-AJk0uJVEiGnimUlmkhYP0ZypJLmOn_f0LNnUuOC483FUA==) .
