# Is TSA PreCheck Worth It? Costs, Benefits, and Time Savings Explained

.authorSpot-more { display: none !important; }


If you’re considering TSA PreCheck, you’re probably asking one question: Is it worth the cost? For most travelers, the answer is yes. At just $85 for a five-year membership – about $17 per year – TSA PreCheck is one of the most affordable ways to make your airport experience more simplified and faster.


Whether you’re a frequent flyer or travel for leisure, here’s what you should know before deciding to enroll in TSA PreCheck.


## **What Is TSA PreCheck?**


TSA PreCheck is a Trusted Traveler Program operated by the Transportation Security Administration (TSA). Members receive access to dedicated TSA PreCheck screening lanes at more than 200 participating airports nationwide.


Benefits include:


- Faster security lines
- Keeping shoes, belts, and light jackets on
- Leaving laptops and other electronics in bags
- Keeping 3-1-1 compliant liquids packed
- A smoother airport screening experience overall


## **How Much Does TSA PreCheck Cost?**


The cost to[enroll in TSA PreCheck](https://tsaprecheckbytelos.tsa.dhs.gov/?utm_source=Telos&utm_medium=website_blog&utm_campaign=tsa_precheck_worth_it) through Telos is **$85 for five years** . Online renewals through Telos are $69.95 for five years, while eligible travelers may also renew in-person for $58.75.


When you break it down, a new TSA PreCheck membership costs just **$17 per year** – or about **$1.42 per month** . Since your membership lasts five years, the value continues to grow every time you travel.


The table below shows how the $85 enrollment fee is spread across the life of a five-year membership. Even if you only take one or two trips each year, the effective cost per trip – and per flight – may be lower than you expect.

## **So, Is TSA PreCheck Worth It?**


For most travelers, yes.


At $85 for a membership that lasts five years, TSA PreCheck costs about $17 per year – or less than many travelers spend on lunch at the airport. Spread across the life of your membership, the cost can be just a few dollars per trip or even less than a dollar per flight.


The value goes beyond the numbers. TSA PreCheck provides access to dedicated security screening lanes at more than 200 airports, and a more convenient, airport experience. Whether you travel for business, family vacations, or an occasional getaway, those benefits can make every trip a little easier.
