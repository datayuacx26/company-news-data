# TSA PreCheck Touchless ID: Everything You Need to Know

If you’re already a TSA PreCheck® member, your next trip through airport security could be even faster. With **TSA PreCheck Touchless ID** , eligible travelers can verify their identity using secure facial comparison technology instead of presenting a physical ID at participating TSA security checkpoints.


If you’re not yet[enrolled in TSA PreCheck](https://tsaprecheckbytelos.tsa.dhs.gov/) , TSA PreCheck Touchless ID is another compelling reason to consider joining. In addition to faster security lines, no need to remove shoes, belts and light jackets, and leaving laptops and 3-1-1 liquids packed away, eligible members can enjoy an even more seamless screening experience at a growing number of airports across the country.


Whether you’re a longtime TSA PreCheck member looking to activate TSA PreCheck Touchless ID or you’re considering enrolling for the first time, this guide explains how TSA PreCheck Touchless ID works, how to opt in, and where you can use it.


## ****TSA PreCheck Touchless ID vs. Standard TSA PreCheck****


Many travelers wonder whether TSA PreCheck Touchless ID is a separate program. It isn’t.


TSA PreCheck Touchless ID is an **optional benefit available to eligible TSA PreCheck members** . It doesn’t replace TSA PreCheck – it simply streamlines one additional step in the screening process.

## **Wh **at Is TSA PreCheck Touchless ID?****


TSA PreCheck Touchless ID is an optional identity verification program offered by the Transportation Security Administration (TSA). Instead of handing your identification to a TSA officer, you simply look into a camera.


Your live image is securely compared with a government-issued photo already associated with your passport and airline profile to verify your identity. Once confirmed, you’ll continue through the TSA PreCheck security lane just as you normally would.


Participation is voluntary, and travelers may choose traditional identity verification at any time.


## ****How to Use TSA PreCheck Touchless ID****


Getting started is simple.


**Step 1:** *Become a TSA PreCheck Member*


If you’re not already enrolled,[apply for TSA PreCheck](https://tsaprecheckbytelos.tsa.dhs.gov/) and receive your Known Traveler Number (KTN).


**Step 2:** *Opt In Through a Participating Airline*


Log into your participating airline account and:


- Save your valid U.S. passport information.
- Add your Known Traveler Number to your traveler profile.
- Complete the airline’s TSA PreCheck Touchless ID opt-in process.


**Tip:** You’ll need to opt in separately with each participating airline you fly.


**Step 3:** *Check In for an Eligible Flight*


After checking in, your airline app or boarding pass will indicate whether TSA PreCheck Touchless ID is available for your trip.


**Step 4:** *Proceed to a Participating TSA PreCheck Touchless ID Checkpoint*


Follow airport signage directing eligible travelers to the designated TSA PreCheck Touchless ID lane.


**Step 5:** *Look at the Camera and Continue Through Security*


When prompted, briefly look into the camera. Once your identity is verified, you’ll continue through the TSA PreCheck screening lane without presenting a physical ID. For most travelers, the identity verification process takes only a few seconds.
