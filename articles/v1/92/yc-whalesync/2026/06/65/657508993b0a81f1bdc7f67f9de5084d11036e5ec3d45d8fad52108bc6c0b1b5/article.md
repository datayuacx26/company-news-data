# How to Sync Notion to HubSpot in 5 Minutes

Notion has become the connected workspace where modern teams keep everything: meeting notes, project trackers, content calendars, and, more often than you might expect, entire databases of leads, customers, and partners. HubSpot is the other center of gravity. It is the CRM where sales and marketing teams manage contacts, run deals through a pipeline, send campaigns, and report on revenue.


The trouble starts when the same customer lives in both places. Someone updates a company record in Notion, but the matching HubSpot record never hears about it. A rep closes a deal in HubSpot, but the Notion tracker your leadership reviews every Monday is still showing last week's numbers. Before long, somebody on the ops team is spending Friday afternoons exporting spreadsheets just to keep the two tools roughly aligned.


Whalesync solves this with a no-code, real-time, two-way sync between Notion and HubSpot. Connect the two apps once, and edits made in either tool flow into the other automatically. In this guide, we will walk through exactly how to set it up, start to finish, in about five minutes.


Let's begin.


## Why two-way sync beats CSV exports and Zapier


The traditional ways of moving data between Notion and HubSpot all share the same weakness: they move data in one direction, once.


CSV exports are a snapshot. The moment you download the file, it starts going stale, and every import is a fresh chance to create duplicates or overwrite good data with old data. If your contacts change daily, a weekly export was wrong six days out of seven.


Zapier and similar automation tools are better, but they are built around one-way triggers: when a record is created here, create a record there. Handling updates is awkward, handling edits that happen on both sides is harder still, and every new field or table means another zap to build and maintain. What you actually want is not a chain of triggers. It is for the two tools to behave like one shared database.


That is what Whalesync does. It keeps records in Notion and HubSpot continuously matched, in both directions and in real time. Update a deal amount in HubSpot and your Notion database reflects it moments later. Fix a typo in a Notion row and the HubSpot record is corrected too. There is no code to write and no zaps to babysit.


## How to sync Notion to HubSpot in 5 minutes


Here is the full setup, step by step.


### **Step 1: Create your Whalesync account**


Head to Whalesync and sign up. Every account starts with a free trial, so you can build and test your sync before paying anything. Once you are in, click 'New sync' and choose Notion and HubSpot as your two apps.


### **Step 2: Connect Notion**


Authorize the[Notion connector](https://www.whalesync.com/connector/notion) and choose which pages and databases Whalesync can access. Granting access is what lets Whalesync read your Notion databases and write updates back to them.


### **Step 3: Connect HubSpot**


Next, authorize the[HubSpot connector](https://www.whalesync.com/connector/hubspot) and select the HubSpot account you want to sync with. Just like with Notion, this gives Whalesync permission to read and update your CRM records on your behalf.


### **Step 4: Map your tables and fields with AI automapping**


Now tell Whalesync which Notion databases should sync with which HubSpot objects, for example a 'Companies' database in Notion paired with companies in HubSpot. Whalesync's AI automapping suggests the table and field pairings for you by matching names and types, so most of this step is just reviewing and clicking confirm. You can map every field or only a handful, and you can add more table mappings to the same sync whenever you need them.


### **Step 5: Turn on your sync**


Before anything moves, Whalesync shows you a preview of how many records will be created in each tool, and existing records are matched up based on the fields you mapped. When it looks right, activate the sync. From that point on, both tools stay in lockstep automatically.


### **Done! Notion and HubSpot are synced**


Add a row in Notion, watch it appear in HubSpot, and enjoy never doing a manual export again.

Whalesync's HubSpot integration covers the objects your team actually works with: contacts, companies, deals, tickets, tasks, notes, calls, meetings, quotes, line items, and products, plus your custom objects. ## Three ways teams use a Notion to HubSpot sync


### **1. A live pipeline view for the whole company**


Not everyone needs a HubSpot seat, but everyone wants to know how the quarter is going. Sync deals into a Notion database and your company dashboard updates in real time, right next to the roadmap and the meeting notes people already read.


### **2. Editing CRM data where the work happens**


Ops, support, and customer success teams often live in Notion. With a two-way sync, they can clean up company records, log account details, or update contact information from Notion, and every change lands in HubSpot instantly. The CRM stays accurate without forcing anyone to switch tools.


### **3. Customer projects tied to real deals**


If you run onboarding or client projects in Notion, sync the relevant deals and companies in from HubSpot. Project pages always show the current deal stage and owner, and notes captured during delivery flow back to the CRM where sales can see them.
