# Why Study in Singapore: Top Reasons to Choose It

[Living Abroad](https://blog.transfez.com/en/living-abroad/)


You have probably scrolled through dozens of “best countries to study abroad” lists and noticed Singapore keeps showing up near the top. And honestly? It deserves the spot. For Indonesian students especially, Singapore feels like the perfect middle ground: close enough to home, yet international enough to completely level up your future. If you are curious about[how to study abroad as an international student](https://blog.transfez.com/en/how-to-study-abroad-as-an-international-student/) , Singapore is one destination you absolutely should not overlook.


But “Singapore is great” is not a good enough reason to uproot your life and spend years studying there, right? You need real, concrete reasons. So let us break it all down, from world-class universities to career opportunities that are genuinely hard to find anywhere else in Southeast Asia.

##


**World-Class Universities That Open Global Doors**


The first and most obvious reason why study in Singapore makes total sense is the sheer quality of its universities. Singapore is home to institutions that consistently rank among the best in Asia and the world.


###


**1. NUS and NTU Are Genuinely World-Ranked**


The National University of Singapore (NUS) and Nanyang Technological University (NTU) regularly appear in the top 20 globally across multiple university rankings. That is not marketing fluff. A degree from either of these institutions carries serious weight with employers around the world.


###


**2. Singapore Management University (SMU) for Business and Law**


If business, finance, or law is your path, SMU has built a strong reputation specifically in those fields. Its curriculum is heavily focused on real-world application and industry collaboration, which means you graduate ready to work, not just ready to study more.


###


**3. SUTD and SIT for Tech and Applied Sciences**


Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD) and Singapore Institute of Technology (SIT) are newer but are carving out strong niches in engineering, design, and applied sciences. These schools have deep ties with industry partners, giving students hands-on experience that is hard to replicate elsewhere.


##


**English Is the Language of Instruction**


One of the biggest practical advantages of studying in Singapore is that English is the primary language used in all universities. For Indonesian students who have been learning English throughout school, this is a huge relief compared to studying in Japan, Germany, or South Korea, where you often need to master a completely new language before even starting your degree.


###


**1. No Language Barrier Stress**


You can focus all your energy on learning your subject instead of struggling to understand lectures in a foreign language. That advantage alone accelerates your academic performance significantly.


###


**2. You Build a Stronger English Portfolio**


Living and studying in a fully English-speaking academic environment polishes your professional English in ways that classes back home simply cannot match. Your writing, speaking, and presentation skills will improve fast, and employers notice that.


##


**Strategic Location and Career Opportunities**


Singapore is not just a great place to study. It is one of the most strategically positioned business hubs on the planet. Thousands of multinational companies have their Asian headquarters there, from tech giants to global banks and consulting firms.


###


**1. Internships With Global Companies**


As a student in Singapore, you have access to internship pipelines that simply do not exist in most other countries. Companies actively recruit from Singapore universities because they trust the talent coming out of those programs.


###


**2. A Gateway to the Rest of Asia**


Singapore sits right at the crossroads of Asia. Once you build your network there, opportunities in Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Hong Kong, and beyond become much more accessible. Think of Singapore as your launchpad, not just your destination.


###


**3. Post-Study Work Options**


Singapore offers pathways for international graduates to stay and work after completing their degree, particularly for students from recognized universities. This makes the investment in your education there even more worthwhile in the long run.


##


**Safe, Clean, and Easy to Navigate**


Let us be real: when you or your parents are thinking about studying abroad, safety is a top concern. Singapore consistently ranks as one of the safest cities in the world. Low crime rates, excellent public infrastructure, and a well-organized healthcare system all make it a comfortable place to live as a student.


###


**1. Public Transport Is Reliable and Affordable**


Getting around Singapore is genuinely easy. The MRT (Mass Rapid Transit) system covers most of the island, and buses fill in the gaps. As a student, you will spend very little time figuring out logistics and a lot more time actually doing things.


###


**2. Close Enough to Home**


For Indonesian students, Singapore is just a short flight away. Jakarta to Singapore takes roughly 1.5 to 2 hours. That means you can be home for important family events without burning through your entire semester break just on travel time.


##


**A Multicultural Environment That Feels Familiar**


Singapore is one of the most multicultural cities in the world, with a mix of Chinese, Malay, Indian, and expat communities all living and working side by side. For Indonesian students, the Malay language and Islamic culture are widely present across the island, which makes the cultural adjustment much gentler than moving to, say, Europe or North America.


###


**1. Halal Food Is Everywhere**


If you are Muslim, finding halal food in Singapore is absolutely not a problem. Hawker centers across the island offer certified halal stalls serving everything from *nasi padang* to Middle Eastern cuisine. You will not struggle to eat well or in accordance with your dietary needs.


###


**2. A Thriving Indonesian Student Community**


There is already a large and active Indonesian student community in Singapore. Student associations, cultural events, and informal networks mean you will find your people quickly and have strong support as you settle in. This is a big part of what makes[the Indonesian diaspora](https://blog.transfez.com/en/what-is-the-indonesian-diaspora/) in Singapore so resilient and well-connected.


##


**Scholarship Opportunities Are Real and Accessible**


One of the most common concerns about studying in Singapore is the cost. Tuition and living costs are higher than in Indonesia, no question. But the good news is that Singapore actively offers scholarships to attract top international talent, and Indonesian students are eligible for many of them.


###


**1. ASEAN Undergraduate Scholarship**


NUS and NTU both offer the ASEAN Undergraduate Scholarship specifically for citizens of ASEAN countries, including Indonesia. This scholarship covers tuition fees and provides a living allowance, making studying in Singapore financially achievable even without a large family budget.


###


**2. Singapore Government Scholarships**


The Singapore government, through various agencies, also offers scholarships and grants for postgraduate studies and research. If you are planning a master’s or PhD, it is worth exploring these options thoroughly. For a broader look at funding your education overseas, check out this guide on[how to study abroad for free](https://blog.transfez.com/en/how-to-study-abroad-for-free/) .


##


**Research and Innovation Culture**


Singapore invests heavily in research and development. If you are interested in cutting-edge research, whether in biomedical sciences, artificial intelligence, sustainable energy, or urban planning, Singapore’s universities and research institutes are doing genuinely exciting work. Students often get the chance to contribute to real research projects, not just observe them.


###


**1. Collaboration With Industry**


University research in Singapore is closely tied to industry applications. That means your work in the lab or studio often has a direct line to commercial and social impact, which is both motivating and great for your resume.


###


**2. Innovation Hubs and Startup Culture**


Singapore has one of the most vibrant startup ecosystems in Asia. Students with entrepreneurial ambitions will find incubators, accelerators, and funding networks that are surprisingly accessible even while you are still studying.
