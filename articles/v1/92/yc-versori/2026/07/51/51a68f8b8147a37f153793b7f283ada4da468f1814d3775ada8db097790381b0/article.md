# Introducing Versori by Avalara

# Versori Joins Avalara to Scale AI-Native Integration Infrastructure for Global Connectivity


MANCHESTER, U.K., March 2, 2026


Versori, a next-generation integration platform built to automate connector development using agentic AI workflows, today announced it has joined the Avalara, Inc. family, the global leader in tax compliance automation.


The deal validates Versori’s vision of transforming how enterprise integrations are built and maintained through AI-driven automation. By joining Avalara, Versori will accelerate the deployment of its technology across one of the world’s largest compliance ecosystems, enabling thousands of commerce, ERP, and financial systems to connect faster and operate with greater reliability. Founded to solve the growing complexity of enterprise integrations, Versori developed a platform that automates connector development and maintenance using agentic AI. The result is dramatically faster integration delivery, simplified lifecycle management, and infrastructure that scales globally without sacrificing reliability.


‍


> “From the beginning, our mission at Versori has been to integrate the world’s most complex enterprises,” said the Versori co-founders. “Joining Avalara allows us to bring that vision to a global platform where integration accuracy and uptime are mission-critical for compliance and commerce.”


‍


Versori’s automation-first architecture enables organisations to build and maintain integrations across complex system landscapes, including ERPs, ecommerce platforms, marketplaces, and financial systems, with significantly reduced development overhead. Its agentic AI workflows automate much of the traditionally manual work involved in building and updating connectors, allowing integrations to adapt as systems evolve. By integrating Versori’s technology, Avalara will accelerate its ability to scale integrations across thousands of commerce platforms while advancing its broader strategy to deliver real-time, always-on compliance within every transaction.


‍


> "Compliance at global scale depends on seamless, reliable integration," said Scott McFarlane, CEO and Co-Founder of Avalara. "Versori's technology and team significantly accelerate our ability to connect into the world's commerce systems quickly, at scale using intelligent, AI-driven automation that meets the reliability and accuracy standards global compliance demands. This acquisition strengthens our unified platform and advances Avalara's vision of AI-native compliance that operates continuously, intelligently, and at scale across the world's most trusted commerce systems."


‍


As part of the acquisition, Versori’s founders and team will join Avalara to continue advancing integration automation and agentic AI capabilities. The platform will operate as Versori, by Avalara, expanding integration capabilities across Avalara’s global compliance ecosystem. With the backing of Avalara, Versori’s technology will now power integrations at a scale previously impossible, bringing autonomous integration infrastructure to the systems that run global commerce.
