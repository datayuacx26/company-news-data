## Versori Vs Workato


### Beyond Tasks: Integrate Without Usage Surprises As You Scale


Workato is known for fast automation wins. But as workflows grow in volume and complexity, organisations often need a platform designed for enterprise-scale integrations.


Versori combines real-time processing, hybrid deployment and predictable pricing so integrations can scale without unexpected operational costs.


‍


### Key Comparisons:


1. **Scale Freely:** Workato's recipe model works well for simple automation but can struggle with high-volume integrations. Versori supports both real-time and batch workflows across ERP, CRM, supply chain and data platforms.
2. **Predictable Economics:** Workato charges per task, ,meaning every step and loop increases cost. Versori pricing is platform-based, allowing teams to scale integrations withoiut worrying about transaction counts.
3. **Enterprise Governance:** Versori includes RBAC, audit logs, encryption and SOC2 compliance built-in, helping teams maintain control across production environments.
