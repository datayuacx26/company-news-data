# Coupang’s statement in response to U.S. House Judiciary Committee’s Report

On July 1, 2026, the U.S. House Judiciary Committee released their findings as part of an[investigation](https://judiciary.house.gov/media/press-releases/chairmen-jordan-and-fitzgerald-demand-information-about-south-koreas) into the Korean government’s treatment of U.S. companies. Coupang, Inc. released the following statement:


“We regret the circumstances that led to the House Judiciary Committee’s investigation, and we remain committed to finding a constructive resolution so Coupang can once again serve as a bridge to strengthen the U.S.-Korea alliance, accelerating trade and investment that benefits both countries.”
