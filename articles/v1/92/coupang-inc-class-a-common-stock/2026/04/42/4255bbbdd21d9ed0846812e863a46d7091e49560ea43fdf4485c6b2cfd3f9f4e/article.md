# Coupang’s Taiwan expansion accelerates U.S. exports

Coupang is launching its fourth smart fulfillment center in Taiwan, showcasing its AI-powered infrastructure, strengthening global supply chains, and helping American businesses expand abroad.


**Key Highlights:**


- Coupang, a U.S. tech company, is expanding its global fulfillment infrastructure and connecting American businesses with even more international customers.
- With its new AI-powered fulfillment center and logistics network, Rocket Delivery now covers 70% of Taiwan’s geography.
- Coupang’s advanced robotics and AI reduce barriers for U.S. sellers entering global markets.


*Coupang’s fourth smart logistics center, located in the Bausan Guanyin Logistics Park in Taoyuan, demonstrates the company’s commitment to AI-driven smart logistics.*


**Building a smarter logistics network in Taiwan**


Coupang has launched its fourth smart fulfillment and logistics center in Taiwan, expanding the company’s advanced global logistics network. The new facility in Taoyuan City enables fast and reliable delivery while opening new pathways for brands, especially U.S. businesses, to scale internationally. As a U.S.-based company, Coupang continues to export American innovation overseas while investing in infrastructure that strengthens global supply chains and supports cross-border commerce.


**AI-powered logistics driving faster delivery**


At the core of Coupang’s expansion is its[AI-driven logistics](https://www.aboutcoupang.com/English/news/news-details/2025/innovation/) . By integrating robotics, machine learning, and end-to-end supply chain coordination across our global network, Coupang connects ordering, fulfillment, and delivery in a single synchronized system. This technology powers Rocket Delivery, which now reaches across approximately 70% of Taiwan’s geography, providing next-day delivery, including weekends, seven days a week.


**Unlocking global growth for U.S. businesses**


Coupang’s fulfillment infrastructure improves delivery speed and enables access. By removing traditional barriers such as complex logistics, long shipping times, and high costs, Coupang makes it easier for U.S. companies to expand internationally. American sellers can reach new customers in Asia and beyond with greater efficiency, reliability, and scale.


**Investing in jobs and local economic growth**


Coupang’s new fulfillment center represents a significant investment in both technology and people. The facility is expected to create more than 3,000 jobs, contributing to local economic development and workforce growth. “We are bringing together American innovation and Coupang’s proven logistics expertise to Taiwan,” said Mark Ross, CEO of Coupang Fulfillment and Logistics. “We will continue expanding our logistics infrastructure and extend our footprint into central and southern Taiwan, reinforcing our long-term commitment to Taiwanese customers.”


**One of the largest U.S. investments in Taiwan**


Coupang has invested in Taiwan across multiple phases, including the largest approved U.S. investment in Taiwan last year and the second-largest approved foreign investment overall. Coupang continues to invest heavily in advanced technologies, including billions in AI, robotics, and logistics automation across the Asia-Pacific region. In 2026, Coupang was again named to the[LexisNexis Top 100 Global Innovators list](https://www.aboutcoupang.com/English/news/news-details/2026/top-innovator/) , with its global patent portfolio growing at an average annual rate of 45% since 2015.
