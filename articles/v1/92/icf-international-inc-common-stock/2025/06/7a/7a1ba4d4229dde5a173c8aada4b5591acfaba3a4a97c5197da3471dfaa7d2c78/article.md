# The AI Advantage: Moving from exploration to impact

**Federal agencies are at different points on the AI adoption journey—but momentum is building fast.** As modernization gains speed, AI is shifting from isolated pilots to a powerful enabler of mission outcomes. The priority now: scaling what works—securely, responsibly, and with measurable results.


To understand how agencies are accelerating progress, we surveyed 200 federal leaders. Their insights reveal clear forward motion—but also persistent barriers, from data quality and infrastructure to workforce readiness and integration. Many are developing use cases and detailed plans, but moving from vision to execution at speed requires the right foundation: the tools, talent, and infrastructure to deliver at scale.
