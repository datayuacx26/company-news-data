# CSXT HDF Fuel Index Rate Adjustment Update for January 1, 2026

# CSXT HDF Fuel Index Rate Adjustment Update for January 1, 2026


---


**Released:** December 15, 2025


Dear CSX Transportation Customer,


Following is the CSX Transportation HDF Fuel Index Rate Adjustment (8661) amount effective January 1, 2026.


**CSXT HDF/Mileage Based Fuel Index Rate Adjustment (8661)**


The highway diesel fuel/mileage based fuel index rate adjustment is published in CSXT 8661 and applies to all line haul freight rates and charges set forth in contracts, and other pricing documents that reference CSXT Publication 8661 dated on or after April 23, 2007.


The CSXT HDF/mileage based fuel index rate adjustment of 42 cents per mile, which became effective December 1, 2025, will change to 46 cents per mile for shipments having a bill of lading dated on or after January 1, 2026.


Following are the 8661 CSXT HDF/mileage based fuel index rate adjustments:


46 cents per mile effective January 1, 2026


The fuel index rate adjustment (contract rates - 8661) amount is based on the "HDF Average Price" of 382.2 cents per gallon for the calendar month of November 2025. The "HDF Average Price" is on U. S. No. 2 Diesel Retail Sales by All Sellers, as determined and published by the U. S. Department of Energy, Energy Information Administration at[www.eia.doe.gov](http://www.eia.gov/) .


Please address any questions you may have to your CSXT Sales Manager.
