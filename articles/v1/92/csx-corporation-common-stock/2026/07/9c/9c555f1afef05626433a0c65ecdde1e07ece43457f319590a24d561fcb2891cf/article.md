# CSX Customer Advisory: CSX Monitoring Tropical Depression Bertha

# CSX Customer Advisory: CSX Monitoring Tropical Depression Bertha


---


July 20, 2026


Please be advised that CSX is closely monitoring Tropical Depression Bertha as it approaches the Gulf Coast. The system, which is expected to become a tropical storm later today, is forecast to bring heavy rainfall, flash flooding, high winds and other severe weather conditions that could affect transportation infrastructure and rail operations across portions of the Gulf Coast region, particularly in and around New Orleans.


As part of regional storm preparations, floodgate closures are expected in the New Orleans area Tuesday, July 21, by 5 p.m., affecting both local and interchange traffic between Mobile and New Orleans. Customers with shipments through the CSX Southeast and Southwest regions should anticipate potential delays.


CSX is implementing measures to protect our employees, customers, and communities. Our team is working diligently to ensure minimal service disruptions while maintaining the highest safety standards.


We will keep you informed of any updates or changes that may affect our operations. Please continue to monitor our CSX Customer Advisories and Intermodal-specific Real-Time Service Advisories for relevant information. Additionally, please notify your CSX contact of any site closures or changes in local service needs due to the weather.


The Customer Solutions team is available to answer questions regarding your shipments. To connect with Customer Solutions, call 1-877-ShipCSX (1-877-744-7279).


- For Carload assistance, select option 2 from the submenu, followed by 1, 5.
- For Intermodal assistance, select option 2 from the submenu, followed by 2, 2.


Customers may also submit a Shipment Problem Resolution request via our website[www.shipcsx.com](http://www.shipcsx.com/) .


We appreciate your partnership as we navigate this weather event together.
