# CSX Customer Advisory: Trevose, Pennsylvania, Derailment May Cause Shipment Delays

# CSX Customer Advisory: Trevose, Pennsylvania, Derailment May Cause Shipment Delays


---


June 30, 2026


CSX customers may experience shipment delays in the Trevose, Pennsylvania, area following a train derailment near the crossing of East Bristol Road and Grave Avenue. The incident occurred at approximately 1:59 p.m. ET on Tuesday, June 30, and involved approximately 16 railcars.


The safety of onsite personnel and the surrounding community is our highest priority. No injuries have been reported, and assessments have confirmed that there are no leaks of hazardous materials. A shelter-in-place order, issued earlier as a precaution, has been lifted. The cause of the incident is under investigation.


CSX crews are on scene and working safely and efficiently to clear the site and restore operations. At this time, the estimated time to resume normal operations has not yet been determined.


We recognize the importance of your shipments and are working to minimize disruptions. Please monitor CSX Customer Advisories and Intermodal-specific Real-Time Service Advisories for updates.


If you have any questions about your shipments, contact Customer Solutions at 1-877-ShipCSX (1-877-744-7279).


- **Carload assistance:** Select option 2, then 1, then 5.
- **Intermodal assistance:** Select option 2, then 2, then 2.


You may also submit a Shipment Problem Resolution request through ShipCSX at[www.shipcsx.com](http://www.shipcsx.com/) .


Thank you for your patience as we work to restore operations safely.
