# CSXT HDF Fuel Index Rate Adjustment Update for April 1, 2026

# CSXT HDF Fuel Index Rate Adjustment Update for April 1, 2026


---


**Released:** March 19, 2026


Dear CSX Transportation Customer,


Following is the CSX Transportation HDF Fuel Index Rate Adjustment (8661) amount effective April 1, 2026.


**CSXT HDF/Mileage Based Fuel Index Rate Adjustment (8661)**


The highway diesel fuel/mileage based fuel index rate adjustment is published in CSXT 8661 and applies to all line haul freight rates and charges set forth in contracts, and other pricing documents that reference CSXT Publication 8661 dated on or after April 23, 2007.


The CSXT HDF/mileage based fuel index rate adjustment of 39 cents per mile, which became effective March 1, 2026, will change to 44 cents per mile for shipments having a bill of lading dated on or after April 1, 2026.


Following are the 8661 CSXT HDF/mileage based fuel index rate adjustments:


46 cents per mile effective January 1, 2026
41 cents per mile effective February 1, 2026
39 cents per mile effective March 1, 2026
44 cents per mile effective April 1, 2026


The fuel index rate adjustment (contract rates - 8661) amount is based on the "HDF Average Price" of 372.2 cents per gallon for the calendar month of February 2026. The "HDF Average Price" is on U. S. No. 2 Diesel Retail Sales by All Sellers, as determined and published by the U. S. Department of Energy, Energy Information Administration at[www.eia.doe.gov](http://www.eia.gov/) .


Please address any questions you may have to your CSXT Sales Manager.
