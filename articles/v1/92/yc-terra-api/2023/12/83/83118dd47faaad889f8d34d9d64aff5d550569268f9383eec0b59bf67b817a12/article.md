# November updates


Even more integrations, Apple Watch workouts, and a new referral scheme! 🤩


## New integrations! 🌐


### What? 🔌


Exciting news for fitness enthusiasts! We are proudly introducing integrations with Insiders and Elite HRV, broadening our array of supported devices.


### How? 🤝


Establishing partnerships with these companies, Terra's developers worked on robust APIs to seamlessly connect these new integrations. The goal was to ensure that users could effortlessly link their preferred wearables and fitness devices through Terra.


### Why? ∑⌚️


Terra's commitment to maximizing sum of integrations is showcased in our expanding integration options. The API aims to accommodate all end users, providing a comprehensive solution for connecting any wearable to their favorite fitness apps. The richness of our integrations is a core aspect of the API's appeal, constantly strengthened by new additions.


## **Create Planned Workouts for your Apple Watch users! ⌚**


### What? 🍎


With the latest SDK version (1.5.3), Terra now supports the creation of Planned Workouts for Apple Watch, leveraging Apple's Workout Kit.


### How? 🛠️


While Apple's Workout Kit introduced this feature, Terra's SDK simplifies the integration process. By handling the intricacies, Terra ensures a smoother experience for developers, enabling them to guide users through step-by-step workouts on their Apple Watch effortlessly.


### Why? 🚀


Following Apple's announcement of this feature at WWDC 23, demand surged. Responding to customer feedback, we prioritized this development. The new capability enhances user experience, providing a more interactive and guided workout session compared to traditional text-based programs.


## **New Terra referral scheme! 🤝**


### What? 🤝


We introduced an enticing referral program accessible through the Terra dashboard. Refer a friend, and you'll receive a complimentary 250 user package – a win-win!


### How? 🛠️


We simplified the referral process by incorporating a user-friendly interface into the dashboard. This allows users to effortlessly refer fellow developers to Terra, with a rewarding system in place.


### Why? 🤔


Community spirit and client engagement are at the forefront of Terra's vision. The referral program aims to incentivize users to share their positive experiences and excitement about Terra. Recognizing the significant impact of referrals, we wish to encourage our community to actively participate in collaborative growth and success! 🥳


As Terra continues to blaze new trails in the fitness tech landscape, these recent updates underscore the our unwavering commitment to innovation and user satisfaction.


Terra's journey is fueled by a dedication to user needs, technological excellence, and the belief that fitness tech should be accessible to everyone. Join Terra on this exciting journey toward a new era of fitness & health! 🌐💪
