# Europe Is Once Again In the Grip of Extreme Heat

Large swaths of Europe are in the grip of another intense heat wave this week, the fifth spell of broiling conditions to hit the continent since May. Forecasters warned that the persistence of extreme temperatures and dry conditions was dangerously increasing the risk of wildfires.


The[scorching weather](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/12/world/europe/europe-fifth-heat-wave.html?searchResultPosition=2) , some of which has been[tied to climate change](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/26/climate/europe-heat-wave-climate-change.html) , has upended summertime in Europe, turning a beloved[vacation season](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/12/travel/europe-travel-tourism-wildfires-heat-waves.html?searchResultPosition=3) into a string of heat warnings, fire-related evacuations, stringent water restrictions and government crisis meetings. Some wonder whether the continent’s summers[will ever be the same again](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/13/world/europe/britain-heat-records.html) .


The heat has forced Romania to partially shut down a nuclear power plant, made conditions unbearable in British hospitals and dismayed some winemakers in France who are scrambling to harvest grapes ahead of schedule.


The intense heat is being driven by a stubborn high-pressure system — also known as a[heat dome](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/22/weather/europe-heat-dome.html) — parked over the heart of the continent. Temperatures this week have already soared to 40 degrees Celsius (104 Fahrenheit) or higher across western parts of Europe. The heat is set to expand in the coming days to Central Europe, including the Czech Republic, Germany and Poland.


[The European Forest Fire Information System](https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/projects-and-activities/natural-and-man-made-hazards/forest-fires/current-wildfire-situation-europe_en) has said that a large area of Central and Eastern Europe, stretching from the Alpine region through Poland and into the Balkans, as well as southern Britain, Ireland, northern France and southern Sweden, will remain under “very extreme” fire conditions through at least next week.


Here’s what to know.


## How hot will it get?


-


High-level heat warnings were in place across parts of Western, Central and Southern Europe on Thursday, with the most severe alerts in Croatia, Italy and Switzerland.


-


In Spain,[AEMET](https://www.aemet.es/es/eltiempo/prediccion/espana) , the country’s weather service, said temperatures would broadly remain above 35 degrees Celsius (95 Fahrenheit) on Thursday and Friday and would climb as high as 42 degrees in parts of the south and northeast.


-


[Météo-France](https://meteofrance.com/actualites/retour-progressif-de-la-chaleur-avec-un-risque-orageux-ce-week-end) , France’s weather service, said the heat was expected to peak in the west of the country on Thursday, with temperatures up to 40 degrees Celsius (104 Fahrenheit) expected, before shifting east on Friday. Cooler air and thunderstorms were forecast to move in from the west by the weekend. Parts of central, northern and southern France were under high-level wildfire warnings for Thursday and Friday.


-


Britain has faced an exceptional run of high temperatures. The Met Office, the country’s weather service,[warned](https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/warnings-and-advice/uk-warnings#?date=2026-08-13&id=27b2ddcc-de43-4935-a544-9eb81e6e4cf8) that temperatures on Thursday could reach 38 Celsius (around 100 Fahrenheit), especially in parts of the Midlands and western England.[It said](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/blog/2026/warmest-uk-summer-on-record-increasingly-likely-as-temperatures-stay-well-above-average) the summer would likely be Britain’s hottest on record in terms of mean temperature, surpassing a record set just last year.


-


Italy has been broiling since last week, when the health authorities placed all 27 cities monitored by its heat surveillance system under red alert. On Thursday, the[Italian Air Force Meteorological Service](https://www.meteoam.it/it/previsioni-testuali) said that temperatures could reach 40 degrees Celsius (104 Fahrenheit) in parts of Sardinia, Campania, Basilicata, Calabria, Umbria and Sicily.


-


In Germany,[the Deutscher Wetterdienst](https://www.dwd.de/DE/wetter/vorhersage_aktuell/vhs_brd_node.html) , the country’s weather service, said to expect temperatures up to 38 degrees Celsius (100 Fahrenheit) in the coming days, especially in the west and southwest on Friday and Saturday.


-


The Czech Republic’s[weather service](https://x.com/CHMUCHMI/status/2087827318731329893) said temperatures would rise through the weekend, possibly reaching 35 degrees Celsius (95 Fahrenheit). In Poland,[the Institute of Meteorology and Water Management](https://x.com/IMGWmeteo/status/2087534156704108878) said temperatures could reach 37 degrees Celsius (nearly 99 Fahrenheit) in some western, central and northern regions on Sunday.
