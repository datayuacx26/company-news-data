# Count Down to Football’s Return With The Athletic’s Advantage Calendar

The Athletic prepares dedicated football fans for kickoff with 12 days of exclusive insights and limited-edition gear, packed in a specialty cooler.


Football season is around the corner, and The Athletic is celebrating with the launch of **The Advantage Calendar** . Inspired by the tradition of holiday advent calendars and powered by The Athletic’’s industry-leading N.F.L. coverage, the first-of-its-kind countdown celebrates football’s most sacred unofficial holiday: the first Sunday of the N.F.L. season.


Arriving in the form of a cooler, The Advantage Calendar includes 12 days of unboxing. Each box reveals a QR code providing access to an exclusive N.F.L. insight from The Athletic’s award-winning newsroom, along with bespoke merchandise to prepare you for the return of football Sundays.


The Advantage Calendar is a limited-edition release. A select number will be available complimentary to fans.


The Athletic is the dedicated sports news app for in-depth football analysis and real-time N.F.L. alerts. Fans can get every angle from the first football Sunday all the way to the Super Bowl with exclusive stories, live game analysis, special-edition features, fantasy guides and more — all on The Athletic app.


### The details that bring you the daily edge


Every box reveals exclusive insights you can’t get anywhere else, including behind-the-scenes stories, expert analysis and scouting details from experts like Jonathan Jones, Robert Mays, Mike Silver, Mike Sando and Jourdan Rodrigue. This calendar will provide the trusted football intelligence that keeps dedicated fans closer to the heart of the game all season long.


“There’s a build-up to every N.F.L. season that feels a lot like the holidays — the countdown, the anticipation, the excitement that grows with each passing day,” said Robert Mays, host of “The Athletic Football Show.” “The Advantage Calendar taps into that feeling, giving fans something new to unwrap every day before kickoff while bringing them the kind of exclusive reporting, analysis and insight only The Athletic can deliver.”


Like any great advent calendar, the fun is in discovering what’s in each box. Alongside exclusive football analysis from The Athletic’s N.F.L. experts, fans will unlock premium game-day essentials — from a candle scent that sets the tone for kickoff, to a mini, in-house referee for your console. By the time the final box opens on the first football Sunday of the season, fans will have everything they need to gain the ultimate advantage on game day.


### Want to unbox your own? Here’s how to join the countdown.


- **Starting today, a limited number of calendars will be available.** Beginning at 12 p.m. Eastern, you can enter for the opportunity to receive The Advantage Calendar by[submitting this form](https://nyt.swoogo.com/advantagecalendar) . Select respondents will be notified and receive the Calendar. See terms for details.
- **Calendars will arrive ahead of the first football Sunday, with plenty of time for unboxing.** Starting Sept. 2, open the day’s box to reveal a surprise and a unique QR code. Scan the QR code to access that day’s exclusive football video content from The Athletic’s N.F.L. experts. Fresh content will be released daily through the first football Sunday, Sept. 13.


Content will be available exclusively to calendar recipients before it is released publicly.
