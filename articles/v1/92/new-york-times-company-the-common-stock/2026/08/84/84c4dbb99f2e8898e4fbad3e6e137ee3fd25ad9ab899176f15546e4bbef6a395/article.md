# The New York Times Is Running for Reporting

The Times is helping support a new generation of journalists by raising money for the Craig Newmark Graduate School of Journalism’s free tuition campaign at the N.Y.C. Marathon.


The Times’s Run for Reporting funds CUNY scholarships to ensure a career in journalism remains accessible to all. Beyond this effort, The New York Times remains deeply committed to supporting and cultivating the next generation of journalists and upholding essential investigative reporting nationwide.
