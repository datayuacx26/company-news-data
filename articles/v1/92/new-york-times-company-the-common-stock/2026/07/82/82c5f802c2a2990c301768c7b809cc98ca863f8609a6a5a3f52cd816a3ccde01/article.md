# New Hires on Audience

Adria Watson, Owen Berg, Cora-Jane Jordon, Emma Grazado and Sarah Bonn have joined The Times. Read more from the Audience team below.


Photo Credit: Earl Wilson


We’re pleased to welcome several new colleagues. Please join us in welcoming them.


Adria Watson has joined the Audience team as our new weekend social editor, based in Los Angeles.


Adria will play a key role in elevating how our journalism informs and engages readers across social platforms. Working alongside Social Visuals editors, Adria will curate and manage our flagship @nytimes accounts, ensuring that both breaking news and our best enterprise are distributed during a crucial window. This includes bringing her expertise to our social video strategy, as Adria will work with a focus on growing the reach and impact of our video journalism off platform. She will also monitor demand as well as competitors to identify opportunities — including via video — for more coverage to grow and engage our audience.


Adria joins us from The Boston Globe, where she helped manage newsroom social accounts, organized Reddit AMAs and built out the newsroom’s social video strategy on Instagram and TikTok. Before she became an audience editor at The Globe, she covered breaking news, managed the education desk’s digital strategy and produced a newsletter. Prior to The Globe, Adria worked at The Connecticut Mirror as a reporter covering K-12 and higher education.


— Alexa Díaz


Photo Credit: Earl Wilson


I am thrilled to announce that Owen Berg has joined the Audience team as an associate SEO editor.


As our audience fellow, Owen thrived on every team he worked with, tackling everything from audience reports for the Business desk to working with reporters on posts and replies in the comments. He does his job with curiosity, expertise and a strong collaborative spirit, and I am so excited to keep working with Owen as he takes on a bigger role in our midterms coverage and with our daily work of finding new audiences for our journalism on Google.


“Owen’s name first crossed my radar two years ago when I read a


[Nieman Lab piece](https://www.niemanlab.org/2024/01/how-to-meet-readers-where-they-are-when-where-they-are-is-offline/) he wrote on community engagement work he had done for The City. It was a smart, creative approach that included testing and iterating on ideas in a way that dovetails well with our approach to audience here,” said Jennie Coughlin, senior Audience editor for National. “When he started as the Audience fellow, he brought that same approach to his work with SEO and Community.”


Owen joins us from Documented, where he served as a social media manager. He holds a B.A. in journalism from Miami University and a master’s degree in journalism from New York University, where he completed the Studio 20 Digital Innovation Program focused on audience engagement, news product and solutions journalism.


— Morgan Greenwald


Cora-Jane Jordon has joined the Audience team as an associate audience editor for Video, based in London.


In this role, Cora-Jane will work closely with journalists and producers to support video distribution and optimization on-site and across off-platform channels. She will surface audience data and competitive insights around major news events and support reporting and analysis that informs how we package and distribute our video journalism.


Before joining The Times, Cora-Jane was a social media journalist at The Times and The Sunday Times, where she led social planning for major editorial projects, analyzed social and video performance to inform editorial strategy and worked across teams to help produce storytelling native to social media platforms. She also spearheaded the launch of the newsroom’s Snapchat presence.


Cora-Jane brings a strong mix of social platform expertise, audience strategy, data-driven decision making and cross-functional collaboration to this role, and we’re excited to have her join the team.


Outside of work, Cora-Jane can be found learning French on Duolingo, running her book club with friends or at a concert.


— Mariya Abdulkaf


Photo Credit: The Washington Post


Emma Grazado has joined the Audience team as an audience editor for Video, based in New York.


In this role, Emma will help shape how our video journalism reaches audiences across platforms. She will work closely with the newsroom Video team to strengthen distribution and optimization across on-site and off-platform channels, surface audience and competitive signals around major and emerging storylines, and help identify opportunities for video coverage based on audience demand and platform trends.


She will also help turn performance insights into actionable recommendations, building on a track record of growing audiences across platforms, social media expertise and thoughtful collaboration across newsroom teams.


Before joining The Times, Emma worked at The Washington Post as the newsroom’s social media coach on the audience strategy team, helping guide social strategy for journalists and working across teams to support growth on social platforms. Prior to that, she was an audience editor at USA Today.


Outside of work, Emma’s baking her way through The New York Times cookie recipe book and learning to speak Portuguese.


— Mariya Abdulkaf

Sarah Bonn has joined the Audience team as an audience editor for Shows, based in New York.


In this role, Sarah will help lead rollout and support performance analysis for Shows across both video and audio platforms, with a particular focus on YouTube. She’ll work closely with hosts, producers, video journalists and visual designers to ensure our shows are packaged and distributed effectively, while helping translate performance insights into recommendations on programming, packaging and audience growth. She will also surface competitive trends, track audience signals and help identify opportunities for our current slate of shows and those we want to pilot.


Before joining The Times, Sarah was a video strategy manager at CakeWorks, where she led end-to-end rollout strategies for video shows and helped shape YouTube programming, packaging and audience growth for a range of clients, including Ellen DeGeneres and Mo Willems. Prior to that, she worked at Fullscreen and National Geographic in roles spanning audience strategy, video production and media operations.


Sarah brings a strong mix of platform fluency, analytical thinking and creative instincts to this role. She is also a singer/songwriter and enjoys biking around Brooklyn.
