# Staff News From Live Team

Amanda Newman joins the team as senior editor, and Randy Pennell is promoted to deputy editor. Read more in the below note from Julie Bloom.


We’re excited to announce staff news from the Live team.


If there’s a desk in the newsroom that covers breaking news, chances are Amanda E. Newman has not merely worked on it but excelled there. She’s had editing stints on International, National, Live, Express, Washington, the News Desk and most recently Politics, and now we’re excited to announce that she is joining the Live team in New York as a senior editor.


Amanda started on the Flex desk in May 2023 and quickly showed her capacity for excellent judgment and hunger for breaking news. During her time on Flex, she worked on this


[story that originated with Graphics](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2025/12/22/us/politics/trump-donors-fundraising-benefits.html) about how hundreds of post-election donors have benefited from President Trump’s return to office. More recently, she’s worked on stories about such wide-ranging subjects as Jeffrey Epstein, Graham Platner, the war with Iran, redistricting efforts across the country and the Artemis II mission.


Pete Blair, the editor of the Flex desk, said: “She is one of those editors who seems to gravitate toward the biggest stories of the day, whether from International, National, Washington, Politics or Express — all of which Amanda has spent extended time on during her three years as a Flex editor, and all of which would tell you she’s unflappable under pressure. With a track record like that, I can’t think of a more logical place for her to go than Live.”


Amanda grew up in New England and began her career at The Citizen of Laconia, a small newspaper in New Hampshire. She then spent nearly five years at The Valley News in West Lebanon, N.H., and joined The Times’s Editing Center in Gainesville, Fla., in 2019. She moved back to the Northeast in 2021 and started on the News Print Hub shortly after.


When big news happens, desk after desk frequently send the same message: “Get me Randy.”


Now, we are happy to announce that Randy Pennell has been promoted to a deputy editor on the Live team based in New York, where he will take on more leadership responsibilities and help us imagine the next evolution of live coverage. During his time on Live, Randy has worked with colleagues around the newsroom to help edit and lead some of our biggest storylines, from the wars in Ukraine and the Mideast to the disappearance of the Titan submersible to Donald Trump’s Manhattan criminal trial.


For the 2024 election cycle, he was detached to Politics to help lead live coverage and more recently he has been a consistent presence on our ongoing live coverage of the second Trump administration. Calm under pressure, Randy can edit and write and rewrite on deadline without losing his sense of humor and he has helped make our live coverage better across many of the most significant stories of the past several years.


Before joining Live, Randy was an editor on the finance team in Business, editing coverage of banking, personal finance and more. He joined The Times as a Sports copy editor in 2016 from The Associated Press, where he was the weekend supervisor for a 10-state region from Maine to Ohio.


Please join us in congratulating Amanda and Randy.


— Julie
