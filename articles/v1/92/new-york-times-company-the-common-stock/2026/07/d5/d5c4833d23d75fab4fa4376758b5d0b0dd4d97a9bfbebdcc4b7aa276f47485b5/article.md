# The Indian Diaspora in New York Joins the ‘Cockroach Party’ Protests

Aarsh Chauhan doesn’t usually go to protests. An Indian citizen studying finance at Baruch College, he describes himself as “apolitical.” But after watching videos of police[in India beating protesters](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/21/world/asia/india-protests-cockroach-janta-party.html) , he joined demonstrations against its government, not in New Delhi but in New York.


Mr. Chauhan, 25, was one of an estimated 500 people who gathered in front of a statue of[Mahatma Gandhi](https://www.nytimes.com/2023/01/30/world/asia/gandhi-assassination-75-years-photos.html) in Union Square on Friday, chanting “we will take our freedom” in Hindi.


It was one of two protests held in the park last week. “There’s a limit to everything,” he said.


After tens of[thousands](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/22/world/asia/india-youth-protest-modi-cockroach-janta-party.html) of Gen Z and millennial Indians took to the streets across India this month, in one of the largest demonstrations against corruption there in years, parallel protests emerged in cities that include New York,[Los Angeles](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DbMJOu7yX7n/) ,[London](https://www.instagram.com/p/DbB7mddI4Nq/) ,[Prague](https://www.instagram.com/p/DbMZgTUCNIF/) ,[Berlin](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/VMQsB_kLaIw) and[Vancouver](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DbCp3txoFNA/) , British Columbia.

“You know the moment in your life when you realize the systems you believe in no longer protect you,” said Shonita Joshi, a 36-year-old actor originally from Mumbai who attended both demonstrations in Union Square. “The young people in India have come to realize that.”


Directed at India’s prime minister, Narendra Modi, and at the country’s education minister, the diaspora protests focused on issues in the Indian education system and the lack of job opportunities. They were inspired by the Cockroach Janta Party, a satirical political party formed in May by a 30-year-old Boston University graduate,[Abhijeet Dipke](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/26/world/asia/india-protests-cockroach-janta-party-abhijeet-dipke.html) .
