# New Studio Video Producer for New York Times Cooking

Emily Daly joins The Times to produce recipe videos for Cooking. Read more below from New York Times Cooking editor Emily Weinstein and producers Anders Kapur and CC Allen.


We’re thrilled to welcome Emily Daly to the team as a studio video producer for New York Times Cooking. In this role, Emily will produce hosted recipe videos and collaborate on a range of other Cooking studio productions.


Emily joins us after 10 years as a video producer at Business Insider, where she found herself lugging camera equipment up mountains in France, into East Asian tattoo shops, around century-old musical instrument ateliers and behind the scenes of fast-paced kitchens in New York. She recently received a News & Documentary Emmy nomination as a producer-editor on the Business Insider series “So Expensive.”


Emily grew up in California wine country and now lives with her two cats in Queens, where she hosts weekly movie nights.


She will report to Anders and starts on Monday. Please join us in welcoming Emily to the team.


— Emily, Anders and CC
