# Was Your Trip to Europe Affected by Heat or Wildfires? Tell Us About It.

SKIP ADVERTISEMENT


As[wildfires](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/07/28/world/europe/france-spain-wildfires-2026-fire-season.html) rage in France, Spain, Greece and elsewhere, and as several devastating[heat waves](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/28/weather/europe-heat-wave-weather-forecast.html) affect major cities like Paris, London and Geneva, Europe is experiencing a[summer of reckoning](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/29/world/europe/summer-heat-wave-fires-climate-change.html) .


Some travelers are questioning whether it’s time to[give up on summer in Western Europe altogether](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/23/travel/europe-summer-heat.html) .


Have your travel plans been affected by the heat or the fires? Have you had to cancel, reschedule or replan your trip? If so, we want to hear from you.


We will read all submissions. We may reach out to you about your story. We will not publish any part of your response without contacting you first.
