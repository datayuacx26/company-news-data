# New Hire in Newsroom Operations

Aubrey Armstrong will be an executive assistant supporting the newsroom’s video expansion. Read more in this note from Juanita Powell-Brunson, director of Newsroom Operations.


I am excited to share that Aubrey Armstrong has joined Newsroom Operations as an executive assistant. In this critical role, she will support Solana Pyne and Charlotte Greensit as the newsroom’s video expansion continues to scale in complexity and output.


Most recently Aubrey was an operations and project manager at Harlem Children’s Zone in New York.


She holds a B.A. in anthropology from the University of Texas at Austin and a master’s in sociology from Arizona State University. She is endlessly curious and considers learning a lifelong pursuit.


Outside of work, Aubrey enjoys baking and sharing the results with friends, running, walking outdoors, and taking barre pilates classes. She also loves getting creative through sewing, jewelry-making, photography and floral design, and is always working on a handmade gift for someone. She is an avid reader, a fan of live performances, a board game enthusiast, and loves hunting down the city’s best bakeries — always accompanied by her Australian shepherd.


Please join me in welcoming Aubrey to the team. She starts today.


— Juanita Powell-Brunson
