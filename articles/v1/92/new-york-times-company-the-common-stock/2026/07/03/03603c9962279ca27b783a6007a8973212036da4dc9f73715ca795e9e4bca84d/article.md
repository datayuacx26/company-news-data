# Lauren Fox Joins Features Design

Lauren, who will be a staff art director, brings experience across brand, editorial and digital design. Read more in the below note from Shannon Robertson and Mary Jane Callister.


We’re thrilled to welcome Lauren Fox to the features design team as a staff art director. Lauren brings multidisciplinary experience spanning brand, editorial and digital design.


Lauren began working at The Times in 2023 as a freelance designer at the Magazine. There, she commissioned illustrations, designed print features and collaborated on


[interactive](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2024/04/17/magazine/first-messsages-love.html)[stories](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2023/11/05/magazine/james-webb-space-telescope.html) . In 2024, she started working with the Features Design team as a freelance art director. She impressed us with her strong eye, talent with illustration and collaborative spirit. During a long stint on Book Review, she commissioned a number of


striking


covers


. One of her


commissions


, an


[animation of Sam Altman by John Provencher](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/05/19/books/review/empire-of-ai-karen-hao-the-optimist-keach-hagey.html) , was a Society of Publication Designers medal finalist this year.


A graduate of Washington University in St. Louis, Lauren has previously worked at Google, Pentagram and a number of other design studios. She just finished an assignment at Bloomberg Businessweek.


Please join us in welcoming Lauren back to The Times!


— Shannon and Mary Jane
