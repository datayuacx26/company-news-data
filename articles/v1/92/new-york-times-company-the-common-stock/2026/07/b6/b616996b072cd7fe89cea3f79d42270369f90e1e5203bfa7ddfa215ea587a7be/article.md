# Yuliya Parshina-Kottas Returns to Graphics

Yuliya returns to The Times as a graphics editor, with a focus on 3-D storytelling, animation and visual reporting. Read more in the below note from Archie Tse and Haeyoun Park.


We are delighted to share that Yuliya Parshina-Kottas is returning to The Times as a graphics editor, with a focus on 3-D storytelling, animation and visual reporting.


Yuliya is an enterprising and creative journalist who shaped some of the newsroom’s most ambitious 3-D work for nine years before leaving to pursue teaching at the end of 2023.


When she was previously at The Times, Yuliya was a lead reporter on a 3-D reconstruction of


[a Black neighborhood in Tulsa destroyed](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2021/05/24/us/tulsa-race-massacre.html) in a 1921 race riot. The award-winning project will be featured in an


[exhibit at The Museum of Modern Art](https://www.nytco.com/press/two-new-york-times-interactive-stories-will-be-featured-at-the-museum-of-modern-art/) this fall.


Yuliya drove many other major projects, including an investigation into the


[Native American boarding school system](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2023/08/30/us/native-american-boarding-schools.html) , an accounting of


Russia’s numerous


[attacks on civilian targets](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2022/03/23/world/europe/ukraine-civilian-attacks.html) and an exploration into how


[sound was restored at Notre Dame](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2023/03/03/magazine/notre-dame-cathedral-acoustics-sound.html) after the 2019 fire. During the pandemic, Yuliya’s reporting and 3-D modeling were essential to service journalism pieces showing public health


experts’


[calls for social distancing](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2020/04/14/science/coronavirus-transmission-cough-6-feet-ar-ul.html) and the


[effectiveness of masks](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2020/10/30/science/wear-mask-covid-particles-ul.html) .


For the last two years, Yuliya has been an assistant professor at N.Y.U.’s Tisch School of the Arts, teaching design, journalism and 3-D storytelling.


She also worked on a project to collect 360-degree street view data in the aftermath of the wildfires at Altadena and Pacific Palisades.


Please join us in welcoming Yuliya back to The Times! She starts today.


— Archie and Haeyoun
