# Focal Systems Illuminates the Future of Retail with Shelf-Level Visibility in 2026

Focal Systems is helping retailers navigate one of the most critical shifts in the 2026 retail landscape: **transforming loss prevention from a lagging metric into real-time operational intelligence** . As margin pressure mounts from inflation, evolving consumer behavior, and tighter labor markets, traditional shrink measurement — based on audits and historical reports — can no longer keep pace with what’s really happening on the sales floor.


In today’s complex store environments, loss is *not just a security problem — it’s a visibility problem* . Without precise, shelf-level insights, retailers are left reacting weeks after losses occur, straining frontline labor, confusing fulfillment and assortment planning, and eroding shopper trust.
