# Focal Systems Welcomes Xavier Carvalho as Head of Innovation

Xavier joins us with an impressive track record of pioneering AI-driven retail technologies. Most recently, he led the development of *Store Intelligence* , a breakthrough solution from RetechLabs / SymphonyAI, where he played a pivotal role in shaping the future of retail through computer vision and intelligent automation. His work has empowered retailers across the U.S. and Europe to enhance operational efficiency, improve on-shelf availability, and drive smarter decision-making through advanced AI.


With deep expertise in computer vision, retail automation, and AI-powered transformation, Xavier is uniquely positioned to push the boundaries of what’s possible. At Focal Systems, he will spearhead innovation efforts focused on elevating shelf intelligence and optimizing store performance at scale.


We’re thrilled to have Xavier on board as we continue to help retailers modernize their operations and unlock new levels of insight, accuracy, and efficiency.


**Welcome to the team, Xavier!**
