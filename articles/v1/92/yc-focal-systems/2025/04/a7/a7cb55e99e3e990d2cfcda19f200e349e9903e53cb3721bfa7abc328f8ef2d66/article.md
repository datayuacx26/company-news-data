# Focal Systems and Morrisons Win 2024 Digital Transformation Project of the Year Award for AI-Powered Retail Innovation

Focal Systems is proud to announce that it has been awarded the prestigious **2024 Digital Transformation Project of the Year** for its groundbreaking partnership with Morrisons. This recognition highlights the largest-scale **computer vision deployment in grocery retail** , revolutionizing store operations and enhancing customer experiences.


Through the installation of **200,000 shelf-edge cameras across 500 Morrisons stores in just eight months** , Focal Systems has helped transform replenishment operations, delivering:


**Over 2% improvement** in customer availability across all stores (with up to 4% in top-performing locations)
**Significant reductions** in waste and shrink
**Enhanced customer and colleague satisfaction**
**Operational efficiencies** , saving hours of manual work daily


Morrisons’ commitment to leveraging cutting-edge AI and computer vision technology has driven measurable results, improving stock accuracy, reducing waste, and elevating the overall shopping experience. As **Morrisons CEO Rami Batieh** noted:


> *“This system is bringing down in-day replenishment times significantly, positively affecting availability, sales, and customer satisfaction.”*


This achievement underscores the power of AI-driven retail innovation. **Focal Systems** extends its gratitude to the Morrisons team for their trust, collaboration, and vision, and to its own dedicated team for making this ambitious project a reality.


As leaders in retail technology, Focal Systems remains committed to shaping the future of grocery retail through continued innovation and strategic partnerships.


**About Focal Systems**
Focal Systems is a leading provider of AI-powered retail solutions, specializing in **computer vision technology** that optimizes inventory management and store operations. By leveraging advanced automation, Focal Systems helps retailers enhance efficiency, reduce costs, and improve customer experiences.
