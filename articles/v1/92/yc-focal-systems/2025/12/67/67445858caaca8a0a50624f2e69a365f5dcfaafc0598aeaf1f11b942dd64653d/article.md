# Focal Systems Named Confluent’s Inaugural Data Streaming Startup of the Year

We’re honored to be recognized as Confluent’s first-ever *Data Streaming Startup of the Year (Americas)* .


Our AI-powered retail automation platform is transforming how modern retailers operate — combining computer vision, AI, and real-time data streaming to keep shelves stocked, improve customer satisfaction, and maximize store productivity. With innovations like Store Walk, powered by Apache Flink on Confluent’s Data Streaming Platform, we deliver instant visibility and actionable insights across thousands of stores worldwide.


This award highlights our mission to transform retail with AI-powered automation and real-time data streaming.
