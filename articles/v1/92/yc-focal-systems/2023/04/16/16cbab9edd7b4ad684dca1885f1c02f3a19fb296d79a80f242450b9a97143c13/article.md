# Focal Systems Wins “Overall Store Management Solution Provider of the Year”

Focal Systems has been awarded the title “Overall Store Management Solution Provider of the Year” at the inaugural RetailTech Breakthrough Awards 2023. This recognition highlights Focal Systems’ innovations in retail technology, particularly its “Self-Driving Store” operating system, which uses AI and camera systems to enhance store operations. This technology optimizes merchandising, ordering, pricing, and staff management, leading to reduced food waste and improved availability and employee morale.
