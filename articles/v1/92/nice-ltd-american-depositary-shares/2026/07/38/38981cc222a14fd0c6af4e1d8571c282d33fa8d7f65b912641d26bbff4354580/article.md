A global customer service leader can see the upside of AI — lower cost-to-serve, faster resolution, more consistent service, and better agent experience. But in regulated environments, the “go-live” decision hinges on a second question: can we scale AI while supporting our digital sovereignty requirements and operating model?


That’s why NiCE is proud to be named a launch partner for the AWS European Sovereign Cloud, a new independent cloud for Europe, and why NiCE plans to make its agentic AI solution, powered by NiCE Cognigy, available on AWS European Sovereign Cloud.


This expands options for European organizations to modernize customer experience with agentic AI in an environment designed to meet digital sovereignty needs – where data is stored, processed, and accessed within the region, complying with data security.


## The impact: Regulated industries can accelerate CX outcomes without trading off control


Customer experience is one of the most immediate places AI can drive enterprise value:


- Reducing avoidable contact through automation
- Improving speed-to-resolution and service consistency
- Increasing agent effectiveness with real-time guidance
- Strengthening operational clarity with continuous optimization


But in regulated industries like public sector, financial services, or healthcare, value only counts if it can be delivered within governance guardrails. That’s why cloud deployment options matter. NiCE Cognigy, deployed on AWS European Sovereign Cloud (when available) will support organizations that need both innovation velocity and regional control.


## The benefit: A new deployment option designed to meet digital sovereignty needs


The AWS European Sovereign Cloud is a fully featured, independently operated cloud infrastructure designed specifically for Europe. It is located entirely within the European Union and operates independently from existing AWS Regions, with strong technical controls, sovereign assurances, and legal protections.


With planned availability on AWS European Sovereign Cloud, NiCE Cognigy customers will be able to:


- Deploy AI-powered customer experience solutions within the EU
- Support data residency and operational autonomy requirements
- Maintain the performance, security, and scalability expected from AWS


This matters for customers who are aligning AI deployment to requirements such as EU data residency and operational autonomy—while still expecting enterprise-grade security, availability, performance, and scalability.


For example, a European healthcare provider could leverage NiCE Cognigy on AWS European Sovereign Cloud to automate patient interactions such as appointment scheduling or billing inquiries. By understanding patient intent and resolving requests proactively, AI agents can streamline administrative workflows and improve patient experience while ensuring sensitive data remains within EU boundaries and supporting strict operational control requirements.
