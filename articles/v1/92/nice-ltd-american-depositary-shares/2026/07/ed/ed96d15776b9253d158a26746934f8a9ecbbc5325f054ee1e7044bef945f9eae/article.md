Big ideas move faster when the right partners put them into action. The 2025 NiCE Channel Partner Awards celebrate the organizations transforming ambition into momentum across the entire NiCE ecosystem.


This year’s winners are opening new markets, accelerating enterprise growth, advancing breakthrough innovation, and helping customers turn complex transformation into measurable progress. Their achievements reflect what becomes possible when deep expertise, bold thinking, and powerful technology come together.


We are proud to recognize the partners raising the standard, redefining what great collaboration looks like, and creating what’s next for NiCE customers around the world.


#### Systems Integrator of the Year | Accenture


Combining transformation expertise and NiCE technology to modernize enterprise customer service.


#### Technology Solution Distributor | Telarus


Activating a powerful advisor network to expand NiCE’s reach and impact.


#### Technology Alliance Partner of the Year | AWS


Making NiCE easier to procure, deploy, scale, and optimize on the cloud.


#### Value Added Reseller of the Year | Carahsoft


Connecting public sector organizations with accessible, tailored NiCE technology and expertise.


#### Trusted Advisor of the Year | WTP


Guiding customers toward the right NiCE solutions with clarity and confidence.


#### Innovation Partner of the Year | Deloitte Digital


Turning bold CX ideas into practical, AI-powered solutions through collaborative innovation.


#### Top Growth Partner of the Year | Trace3


Rapidly expanding its NiCE practice and bringing CX transformation to more customers.


#### Rising Star of the Year | TTEC


Building early momentum through fresh capabilities, commitment, and customer-focused execution.


#### Latin America Partner of the Year | A5 Solutions


Delivering localized expertise and stronger customer outcomes across Latin American markets.


#### Latin America New Partner of the Year | RT4 Mexico


Quickly establishing a trusted regional presence and creating new paths to NiCE.


#### Deal of the Year | Accenture and Edward Jones


Uniting technology, transformation expertise, and shared vision for a landmark enterprise program.


The 2025 NiCE Channel Partner Award winners prove that the strongest ecosystems do more than extend reach—bringing together specialized expertise, local insight, breakthrough innovation, and bold execution to help customers move from possibility to progress.
