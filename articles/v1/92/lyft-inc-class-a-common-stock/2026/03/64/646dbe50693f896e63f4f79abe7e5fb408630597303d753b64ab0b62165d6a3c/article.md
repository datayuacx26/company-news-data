# Lyft Named to Fortune's 2026 Most Innovative Companies

Fortune named Lyft to its[2026 America's Most Innovative Companies list](https://fortune.com/ranking/americas-most-innovative-companies/) and we're proud to join the ranks of those that are leading innovation. Fortune awards companies based on three pillars of innovation: products, processes, and company culture that fosters creative thinking. Judges are a combination of industry experts, an advisory board, and employees, which makes this award even more meaningful.


**Customer Obsession for Riders & Drivers**


In 2012, Lyft disrupted the transportation category with a new solution: rideshare. Since then, the company has grown into a global mobility platform offering a mix of rideshare, taxis, private hire vehicles, executive chauffeur services, car sharing, bikes, and scooters. Our mission guides our customer obsession: to serve and connect riders and drivers to the people and places they love.


We create and build products that enhance people’s lives:


-


[Women+ Connect](https://www.lyft.com/women+) has facilitated millions of rides since launch between women riders and drivers


-


[Lyft Silver](https://www.lyft.com/rider/silver) for older adults nearly doubled rides since launch in May 2025 through December 2025


-


[Wait & Save](https://www.lyft.com/rider/wait-and-save) for those with flexible schedules has helped hundreds of millions of riders save money


These aren't just features – they're direct responses to real community needs because we put people first.


**What this recognition means to us** This recognition is a reflection of the work our dedicated employees put in every day and reinforces our deep dedication to drivers, riders, and the communities we're proud to serve. We're just getting started — and this recognition reminds us of what's possible when innovation is driven by customer obsession.
