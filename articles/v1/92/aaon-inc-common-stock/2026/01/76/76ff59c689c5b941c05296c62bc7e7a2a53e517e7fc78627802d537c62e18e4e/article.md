# Inside AAON Season 2 Episode 01: Why Cabinet Construction Is the Real HVAC Differentiator with Kevin Teakell

Welcome Back to Season 2 of Inside AAON! This season features fresh conversations and new voices as we explore the topics, challenges, and ideas shaping the HVAC industry today. Each episode highlights insights from the people driving innovation across the industry, from engineering and manufacturing to leadership and customer experience.


In this episode, co-hosts, Jeremy Cavness, Executive Director of Marketing, and Whitney Tapp, Marketing Content Producer, dive into our industry-leading cabinet construction with AAON Fellow and Head of Government and Industry Affairs, Kevin Teakell, P.E.


The team explores why cabinet construction is one of the most overlooked differentiators in the industry and its direct effect on efficiency and long-term reliability. If you sell, specify, or own HVAC equipment, this episode will change how you think, and talk, about cabinet construction.


> “It’s the difference between what everyone else does and what AAON does. It’s about the longevity of the cabinet. It’s about putting our BTU’s where we want them...It’s all those things that come together to make that really quality product.” — Kevin Teakell, P.E.
