# Inside AAON Season 2 Episode 05: All About Heat Pumps with Eric Newberg & Ryan Merritt

[We just celebrated one year of Alpha Class Next Generation,](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aaon_one-year-ago-we-launched-aaon-alpha-class-activity-7469750942351966208-eh4l?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAABKetI0BIEeaUM0R9KCk_6rNjWdksxS_DKY) a milestone that revolutionized heat pump technology, and we’re just getting started.


We’re always looking into the future, and that means staying ahead of trends and technology. AAON was the first in market to successfully transition and


[adopt A2L refrigerant R-454B,](https://www.aaon.com/news/aaon-leads-the-industry-with-early-adoption-of-a2l-refrigerant-r-454b) while continuing to push the boundaries of heat pump technology with


[Alpha Class Next Generation.](https://www.aaon.com/news/aaon-pushes-boundaries-with-next-generation-alpha-class-heat-pump-technology-powered-by-copeland-innovation) AAON also achieved the


[industry's first commercially available, AHRI-certified, packaged rooftop unit](https://www.aaon.com/news/aaon-passes-doe-commercial-building-hvac-technology-challenge-with-simultaneous-dual-fuel-technology) with a simultaneous dual fuel heat pump that can operate down to -20°F.


In this episode of Inside AAON, special guests Eric Newberg, AAON Director of Product Management, and Ryan Merritt, AAON Product Manager, dissect the ever-changing industry landscape, the people, and the technology that keep AAON ahead of the curve.


Tune in to hear it straight from the people building it.
