# People-First Storytelling Earns AAON National Recognition

At AAON, our marketing strategy is built on a simple idea: connection matters as much as capability


.


In a technical industry, the secret isn't more specs, but more humanity. Humor, real stories, and


[relatable content](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-NEfR4y5u7A) that makes your audience see themselves in what you sell.


This year, that approach earned us 11 creative awards and nominations across four distinct competitions, recognizing the strength and range of our creative work across the manufacturing industry.


### **ADDY Awards**


The ADDY Awards are one of advertising's most prestigious competitions, drawing over 40,000 entries annually and honoring outstanding creativity. AAON received six ADDY Awards for compelling video work that puts our people front and center—the technicians, engineers, and team members who bring our products to life.
