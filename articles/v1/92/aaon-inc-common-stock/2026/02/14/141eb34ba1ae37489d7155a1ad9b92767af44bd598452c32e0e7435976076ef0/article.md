# Inside AAON Season 2 Episode 02: Why Market-Focused Reps Win with Isaac Simpson

In this episode of Inside AAON, co-hosts Jeremy Cavness, Executive Director of Marketing, and Whitney Tapp, Marketing Content Producer, sit down with Isaac Simpson, DMG Sales Director, to explore what separates good reps from great ones—and how the sales-marketing partnership fuels that success.


Simpson shares how AAON's best reps think beyond individual projects to identify emerging market opportunities, adapt to shifting customer needs, and leverage marketing resources that set them up to win. From understanding market trends to presenting a premium offering with confidence, this conversation reveals the mindset and tools that define excellence in AAON's sales network.


> “The relationship is just part of the puzzle. If you’re not technically there for your customer, if you don’t understand how your product works, you’re not a resource. How many times are they going to call you and have you not know before they just stop calling?” — Isaac Simpson
