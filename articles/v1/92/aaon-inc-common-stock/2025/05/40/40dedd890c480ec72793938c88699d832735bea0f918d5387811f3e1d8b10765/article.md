# Highlights from AAON’s National Sales Meeting: Everything You Need to Know

AAON’s 2025 National Sales Meeting, Boundless, brought together sale reps from across the country for an energizing week of connection, learning, and inspiration. With product deep dives, thoughtful discussions, and leadership insights, the event focused on equipping attendees with the tools to lead in a rapidly evolving market. Whether you were there in person or cheering from afar, Boundless set the tone for the year ahead—one defined by possibility, momentum, and growth.


A major highlight of the event was the unveiling of key advancements to our Alpha Class line of commercial air-source heat pumps and to support the full potential of the EXTREME Series, we introduced Stratus.


##### **Alpha Class Evolution: Three Performance Tiers**


We're thrilled to announce significant advancements to our groundbreaking Alpha Class line of commercial air-source heat pumps, introducing three distinct performance tiers that redefine industry capabilities. As the next generation of heat pump performance,[Alpha Class now features ECO , PRO, and EXTREME Series](https://www.aaon.com/alpha-class) to meet diverse market demands and continue our mission of providing cutting-edge technology with unmatched value.
