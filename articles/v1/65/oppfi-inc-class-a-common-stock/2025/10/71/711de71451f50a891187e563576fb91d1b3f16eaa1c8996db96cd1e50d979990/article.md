CHICAGO


, Oct. 29, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- OppFi Inc. (NYSE: OPFI) ("OppFi" or the "Company"), a tech-enabled digital finance platform that partners with banks to offer financial products and services to everyday Americans, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2025.


"OppFi continued to deliver strong performance in the third quarter 2025, achieving record quarterly revenue, profit and net originations. This continued outperformance has led to raising earnings guidance for the third time this year. Auto approval rates increased to 79.1%, which has improved operating efficiency year over year. We believe we are well positioned strategically with the Model 6.1 refit, risk-based pricing and dynamic seasonal modeling to mitigate exposure during periods of economic volatility," said Todd Schwartz, CEO and Executive Chairman of OppFi.

**About OppFi**


OppFi (NYSE: OPFI) is a tech-enabled digital finance platform that partners with banks to offer financial products and services to everyday Americans. Through this transparent and responsible platform, which emphasizes financial inclusion and exceptional customer experience, the Company assists consumers who are underserved by traditional financing options in building improved financial health. OppLoans by OppFi maintains a 4.4/5.0 star rating on Trustpilot based on over 5,200 reviews, positioning the Company among the top consumer-rated financial platforms online. OppFi also holds a 35% equity interest in Bitty Holdings, LLC ("Bitty"), a credit access company that provides revenue-based financing and other working capital solutions to small businesses. For additional information, please visit[oppfi.com](http://oppfi.com/) .
