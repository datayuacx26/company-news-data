# Build games with your ChatGPT plan


Already paying for ChatGPT? You can now put that plan to work building games. Connect your ChatGPT account to CodeWisp once, and your builds and edits run on your **ChatGPT plan** instead of spending CodeWisp credits — one of the easiest ways to keep creating when your balance runs low.


###


What it is


CodeWisp's AI builds and edits games for you: describe what you want, and the platform writes the code. Normally each build or edit spends CodeWisp credits. The ChatGPT connection adds a second lane: pick **ChatGPT** in the editor's model picker, link your account, and CodeWisp routes your builds and edits through your ChatGPT plan.


Your games, projects, and everything else stay exactly the same — the only difference is what powers the AI turn.


###


Step 1 — Open the model picker


1. Open any project in the CodeWisp editor.


1. Click the **model picker** in the chat composer — the dropdown where you choose your build quality.


1. Choose **ChatGPT (connect)** .


###


Step 2 — Approve on ChatGPT's site


You'll get a short code and a link to ChatGPT's official sign-in page. Approve the connection there — the same kind of secure device linking you've used for TVs and consoles. **No passwords ever touch CodeWisp** , and you can disconnect at any time from the editor.


###


Step 3 — Build


That's it. Whenever ChatGPT is selected in the picker, your builds and edits use your ChatGPT plan and spend **zero CodeWisp credits** . Switch back to CodeWisp's own models any time — it's just a dropdown, per request.


###


What it costs


Builds and edits on the ChatGPT lane use your ChatGPT plan's capacity, just like chatting on ChatGPT does. Heavy building sessions are subject to your ChatGPT plan's own usage limits — if you hit one there, flip back to CodeWisp models to keep going.


###


Why we built this


A lot of creators already subscribe to ChatGPT for school, work, or fun. It never felt right that the plan you're already paying for couldn't help you make games. Now it can:


- **Keep building when credits run low.** The ChatGPT lane doesn't touch your CodeWisp credit balance at all.


- **One subscription, more value.** Get more out of the plan you already have.


- **Full flexibility.** Use CodeWisp models for some turns and ChatGPT for others.


Open a project, click the model picker, and choose **ChatGPT (connect)** — one minute later, you're building games on the plan you already own. We can't wait to see what you make with it!
