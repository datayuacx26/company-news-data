# What do new possibilities mean to those living with SMA?

Scholar Rock recently had the opportunity to speak one-on-one with individuals living with spinal muscular atrophy (SMA) to ask a simple, but powerful question: “What possibilities do you hope to see for those living with SMA?” Their responses—from continuing to play competitive sports, to pursuing a career in musical theater, to living more independently—inspire us to continue working together with the SMA community.
