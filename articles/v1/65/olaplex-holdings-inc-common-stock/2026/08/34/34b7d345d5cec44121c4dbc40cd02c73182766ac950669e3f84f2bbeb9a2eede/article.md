# Heat Styling Tips for Different Hair Types


It’s tempting to reach for a curling wand, flat iron, or blow dryer, but the fear of heat damage can make styling feel risky. While frequent heat styling can lead to breakage, it doesn’t have to stop you from styling your hair. With the right prep, heat styling tips for your hair type, and a repair-focused routine using heat protectant products, you can help prevent damage while creating the look you want. ****


## Does Heat Styling Damage Hair?


Heat styling, when done right, doesn’t always damage hair. But excessive heat styling without protective products can damage hair by weakening the cuticle and stressing internal bonds that help give hair strength and structure. Over time, this can cause:


- Dryness
- Brittleness
- Breakage
- Dullness


How often can you safely heat style your hair? It depends on your hair type,[current damage level](https://olaplex.com/blogs/news/types-of-hair-damage) , and the products you use. Fine, curly, chemically processed, color-treated, or already dry and damaged hair may be more vulnerable to heat damage.


[All hair types](https://olaplex.com/blogs/news/how-to-determine-hair-type-and-texture) , from straight to curly, can benefit from spacing out thermal styling and using bond-repair treatments regularly.

## Heat Styling Tips for Straight Hair


Straight hair may look smooth, but it can still be prone to heat damage, especially when it’s styled frequently for volume, bounce, or curls. Focus on lightweight heat protection, root lift, and limiting the number of repeated passes with hot tools.


### How to Blow Dry Straight Hair for Body and Bounce


1. Prep damp hair with[Volumizing Blow Dry Mist](https://olaplex.com/products/olaplex-volumizing-blow-dry-mist-us) to add lightweight body, bounce, and shine without stiffness.
2. Blow-dry with a round brush to lift the roots, or flip hair upside down while drying to create natural volume.
3. Use focused airflow and avoid repeatedly passing heat over the same section.
4. For extra smoothness and shine, finish with[OLAPLEX N](https://olaplex.com/products/olaplex-n-7-bonding-oil-us)[º .7 Bonding Oil™](https://olaplex.com/products/olaplex-n-7-bonding-oil-us) .


### How to Use a Curling Iron on Straight Hair


1. Start with completely dry hair.
2. Apply the heat protectant of your choice.
3. Choose a curling wand or curling iron with adjustable heat settings.
4. Use the lowest effective temperature for your hair type and desired style.
5. Curl in small sections so each piece takes less time on the hot tool.
6. Let curls cool fully before brushing or separating them to help the style set with less heat stress.
7. Finish with[Shape Set™ Hair Spray](https://olaplex.com/products/olaplex-shape-set-hairspray-77ml-eng) to help hold curls in place and control frizz.


## Heat Styling Tips for Wavy Hair


[Wavy hair](https://olaplex.com/blogs/news/beach-waves-hair) often looks best when you enhance its natural texture first, then use heat only where needed. The goal is to reduce frizz, preserve the wave pattern, and avoid overworking the hair.


### How to Blow Dry Wavy Hair Without Frizz


1. Apply a heat protectant to damp hair that helps enhance waves and reduce frizz, like[N](https://olaplex.com/collections/styling/products/olaplex-n-10-bond-shaper-curl-defining-gel-us)[º .10 Bond Shaper™ Curl Defining Gel](https://olaplex.com/collections/styling/products/olaplex-n-10-bond-shaper-curl-defining-gel-us) or[N](https://olaplex.com/collections/styling/products/olaplex-n-6-bond-smoother-us)[º .6 Bond Smoother®](https://olaplex.com/collections/styling/products/olaplex-n-6-bond-smoother-us) .
2. Work in smaller sections instead of blow-drying all your hair at once.
3. Use medium heat and medium speed, or lower settings if your hair is dry, fine, or damaged.
4. Avoid touching your hair too much while drying, since this can disrupt waves and create frizz.
5. Dry until the wave pattern is set, then stop once hair is dry or nearly dry to help reduce excess heat exposure.

## Heat Styling Tips for Curly Hair


Curly hair can be more prone to dryness, so moisture, sectioning, and lower heat settings are especially important. Whether you’re[diffusing curls](https://olaplex.com/blogs/news/how-to-diffuse-your-hair-for-defined-curls-or-waves) or straightening them, prep hair first and avoid unnecessary heat exposure.


### How to Blow Dry Curly Hair Without Frizz


1. Before blow-drying, gently squeeze out excess water instead of roughing up curls with a towel.
2. Apply a heat-protectant curl styler like[N](https://olaplex.com/collections/styling/products/olaplex-n-10-bond-shaper-curl-defining-gel-us)[º .10 Bond Shaper™ Curl Defining Gel](https://olaplex.com/collections/styling/products/olaplex-n-10-bond-shaper-curl-defining-gel-us) , or a frizz-controlling styler like[N](https://olaplex.com/collections/styling/products/olaplex-n-6-bond-smoother-us)[º .6 Bond Smoother®](https://olaplex.com/collections/styling/products/olaplex-n-6-bond-smoother-us) .
3. Use a diffuser to help preserve curl shape and reduce direct airflow.
4. Choose medium-to-low heat with low airflow, especially if your hair is dry, damaged, or prone to frizz.
5. Limit touching curls while drying to help prevent frizz.
6. Focus the diffuser near the roots for volume, then stop when hair is mostly dry to help avoid overexposure to heat.
7. If your hair is prone to dryness or breakage, use[Rich Hydration Mask](https://olaplex.com/products/rich-hydration-mask-us) weekly to help hydrate medium to coarse hair and support smoother blow-drying.


### How to Straighten Curly Hair


1. Start by gently detangling curls with a wide-tooth comb.
2. Generously apply a heat protectant through the ends and mid-lengths to ensure full coverage, especially for very curly hair types.
3. Blow-dry in small sections with a round brush using moderate heat before flat-ironing.
4. Use a smoothing brush to create tension while blow-drying, which can help reduce the number of flat iron passes needed.
5. Apply[OLAPLEX](https://olaplex.com/collections/styling/products/olaplex-7-shine-serum-oil-mist)[º .7 Shine Serum Oil Mist](https://olaplex.com/collections/styling/products/olaplex-7-shine-serum-oil-mist) through dry mid-lengths and ends before flat-ironing for lightweight heat protection, shine, and polish.
6. Keep flat iron passes to a minimum and avoid holding the tool on one section for too long.
7. Use[Shape Set™ Hair Spray](https://olaplex.com/products/olaplex-shape-set-hairspray-77ml-eng) to help hold down the shape and keep natural curls from bouncing back.
8. Finish with[OLAPLEX N](https://olaplex.com/products/olaplex-n-7-bonding-oil-us)[º .7 Bonding Oil™](https://olaplex.com/products/olaplex-n-7-bonding-oil-us) on the ends to smooth flyaways, enhance shine, and help protect hair from environmental stressors.
