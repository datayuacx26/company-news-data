## What Sets OLAPLEX N°.7 Bonding Oil™ and OLAPLEX °7 Shine Serum Oil Mist Apart

Delivered in an ultra-fine spray, OLAPLEX °7 Shine Serum Oil Mist includes humectants that help draw in hydration. It uses bi-phase technology that fuses a reparative serum with high-shine oils to instantly replenish hair.


On the other hand, OLAPLEX N°.7 Bonding Oil™ offers targeted smoothing and high-impact shine. While it helps smooth dry ends, it does not contain humectants that hydrate.

## Which One Should You Start With?


Both formulas are designed to work together, but if you need to start with one, here’s a helpful guide. ****


### Start with OLAPLEX N°.7 Bonding Oil™ If…


- You prefer more control. The measured-dose bottle makes it easier to apply OLAPLEX N°.7 Bonding Oil™ exactly where your hair needs it.
- You want a concentrated oil. A richer oil format offers more targeted smoothing, softness, and shine.
- You’re looking for maximum frizz control. OLAPLEX N°.7 Bonding Oil™ provides[frizz control](https://olaplex.com/collections/frizz-control-and-smoothing-hair-products) for up to 72 hours . The oil can be mixed with your favorite styling products to blow dry shine and smoothness into you style
- You want maximum shine. OLAPLEX N°.7 Bonding Oil™ provides 125% more shine.
- Your hair needs extra smoothing and softness. OLAPLEX N°.7 Bonding Oil™ works on all hair types, but it can be especially good for hair that tends to feel dry, frizzy, or rough at the ends.


To use OLAPLEX N°.7 Bonding Oil™:


1. Uncap and turn the bottle upside down.
2. Tap the bottom of the bottle gently using your index finger to dispense a drop.
3. Apply 2-3 drops to damp or dry hair.

- You want extended shine. OLAPLEX °7 Shine Serum Oil Mist offers instant shine for up to 96 hours.
- You need a style refresh. When you don’t want to touch your style, such as heat-styled curls, OLAPLEX °7 Shine Serum Oil Mist is easy to spritz anytime, anywhere.

While OLAPLEX °7 Shine Serum Oil Mist works on all hair types, its lightweight formulation is particularly good for those with[low-porosity hair](https://olaplex.com/blogs/news/low-vs-high-porosity-hair-and-how-to-test-it) or hair that is easily weighed down for hydration and shine boost in between wash days.
