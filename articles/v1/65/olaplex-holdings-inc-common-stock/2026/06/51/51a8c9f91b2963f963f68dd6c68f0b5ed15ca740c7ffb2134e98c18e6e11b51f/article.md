# Pre-Wash Hair Oil vs. Hair Styling Oil: What’s the Difference?


Hair oils can treat, style, or do both. It depends on the product, when you use it, and how you use it. Here’s what you should know about these types of hair oils and how to use them.


## What Is a Pre-Wash Hair Oil?


A pre-wash hair oil is an oil or blend of oils, sometimes with other ingredients, applied to hair before shampooing. Depending on the formula being used, pre-wash hair oils can offer a range of benefits for hair, including:


- Hair buffering. For those who wash hair daily, or need to shampoo back-to-back days, a pre-wash hair oil can act as a protective buffer, so the shampoo strips the added oil without completely depleting the hair's natural oils.
- Added shine. Certain hair oils can boost your hair’s shine by smoothing the cuticle and reflecting light.
- Detangling. Pre-wash oils provide "slip," helping reduce tangles and knots. Think of it like applying oil to gears. Friction causes wear and resistance, and oil reduces it by lubricating.


## Does Pre-Wash Oil Hydrate Hair?


A common misconception is that applying oil to hair hydrates it. Hair oil itself cannot hydrate hair as it doesn’t contain water itself or humectants (water-attracting substances) that draw the water into the shaft… unless you’re using a specific formula that contains them.


What hair oil does depends on the type of oil used. Some oils mainly coat the hair's outer surface to help reduce moisture loss and smooth the cuticle, while others may penetrate the hair fiber more readily, depending on their composition.


But that doesn't mean pre-wash hair oils aren't worth it. The right oil blends can help reduce hygral fatigue — gradual damage caused by the hair shaft repeatedly swelling with water during washing and shrinking as it dries.


If you want to use a pre-wash oil that does hydrate, look for formulas that incorporate humectants and water. ****


## How to Use a Pre-Wash Hair Oil


1. Comb your hair. Use a wide-tooth comb to gently remove any knots or tangles.
2. Apply to dry hair. Saturate your mid-lengths and ends with your chosen pre-wash hair oil product.
3. Leave oil on your hair for 20-30 minutes. If you wish, you can also leave your pre-wash oil in your hair overnight. Use a shower cap to prevent oil from getting on your clothes, face, and pillowcase.
4. Wash your hair. Thoroughly wash your hair using a shampoo. ## What Is a Hair Styling Oil?


Unlike pre-wash hair oil, hair styling oil is used after washing hair. You can use hair styling oil before heat or after drying. Hair styling oils help boost shine, reduce frizz, and protect against heat damage.


Hair styling oils tend to be lighter and applied more sparingly than pre-wash hair oils. They also aren’t shampooed out. Using hair styling oil on damp hair after washing helps seal in moisture.
