# Summer Curly Hair Routine for Healthy, Hydrated Curls ****


Naturally curly hair thrives in the summer – but heat, humidity, and environmental stressors can quickly lead to dryness, frizz, and loss of definition. The key to maintaining soft, defined curls all season long is a routine centered on hydration, repair, and humidity defense.


**** From wash day to styling, the right curly hair routine helps support stronger, smoother, more resilient curls – no matter the forecast. ****


## Why Curly Hair Needs Extra Hydration


Curly hair naturally struggles to retain moisture because the spiral shape of each strand makes it harder for oils from the scalp to travel down the hair strand. This is even more common in the summer, because exposure to sun, saltwater, chlorine, and humidity can further disrupt moisture balance.


Without proper hydration, curly hair can become frizzy, brittle, and harder to manage. But a routine that starts with a moisturizing shampoo for curly hair and a curly hair conditioner can help replenish moisture and[bring those curls back to life](https://olaplex.com/blogs/news/read-this-and-olaplex-those-curls-back-to-life) . ****

## Tips for Styling Curly Hair in the Summer


A simplified styling approach helps curls stay defined and manageable, even in heat and humidity.


Here are a few simple tips to get the most out of your curls this summer:


- Start with curly hair shampoo and conditioner to keep your hair hydrated
- Apply styling products to damp hair, which encourages even distribution
- Use the scrunch technique or finger coiling to define curls
- Air dry for softer, more relaxed curls or[diffuse your hair](https://olaplex.com/blogs/news/how-to-diffuse-your-hair-for-defined-curls-or-waves) for more volume and definition
- Avoid excessive heat and over-touching while drying to minimize frizz

## Frequently Asked Questions ****


### Why does curly hair get frizzy so easily?


Curly hair tends to get frizzy because the spiral shape of each strand makes it harder for natural oils to travel down each hair strand. Without enough moisture, curls can become dry, rough, and more prone to frizz.


Using shampoo and conditioner for curly hair can help restore hydration and smooth out hair cuticles, making it much easier to style. ****


### How do you sleep with curly hair without ruining your curls?


Sleeping habits can make a big difference in how curly hair looks the next day. The best way to sleep with curly hair is to protect your curls from friction and flattening while you sleep.


**** Silk pillowcases, bonnets, or wearing your hair in a loose “pineapple” on the top of your head can help preserve curl shape and prevent tangling when sleeping with curly hair. ****


### Should curly hair be washed every day?


Most curly hair types do not need to be washed every day. Because curls are naturally drier, frequent washing can remove essential moisture.


**** Instead use a moisturizing shampoo for curly hair a few times per week and refresh curls between washes with lightweight styling products.
