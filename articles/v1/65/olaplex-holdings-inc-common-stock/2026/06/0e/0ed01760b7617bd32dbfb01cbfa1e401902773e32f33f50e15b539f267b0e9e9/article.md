# How to Use OLAPLEX Signature Hair & Body Mist


Body mist is one of the easiest ways to make fragrance feel like part of your everyday routine, especially when you want a scent you can wear on both hair and body.[OLAPLEX® Signature Hair & Body Mist](https://olaplex.com/products/signature-hair-body-fragrance-mist) brings the brand’s signature scent into a format designed for just that, making it easy to add to your daily routine, use between wash days, or bring along when you want a quick refresh on the go. ## What Is OLAPLEX Signature Hair & Body Mist?


OLAPLEX Signature Hair & Body Mist is a limited-edition hair and body mist featuring the refreshing OLAPLEX signature scent, with key notes of grapefruit zest and sugared mint, plus ginger and crushed sage. In other words, it’s an easy way to wear the scent you already know from OLAPLEX in a format made for both hair and body that doesn’t weigh hair down.


Because this is[a fragrance mist](https://olaplex.com/blogs/news/body-mist-vs-perfume/) , not a hair treatment or skin treatment, OLAPLEX Signature Hair & Body Mist is designed for scent and does not offer hair or skin benefits.


## How to Use OLAPLEX Signature Hair & Body Mist


Use OLAPLEX Signature Hair & Body Mist as the final fragrance step in your routine for an instant boost of that just-left-the-salon feeling in every mist. Here’s how to use it:


1. Hold the bottle 6–8 inches away and mist generously onto hair and body.
2. Reapply as desired whenever you want to refresh your scent.
3. As with any mist, avoid contact with eyes.


## Where Does Hair and Body Mist Fit in Your Routine?


Hair and body mist typically fit into your[routine](https://olaplex.com/pages/routine-quiz) once everything else is done, which is part of the appeal: it’s an easy finishing touch when you want to wear scent on both hair and body without adding another complicated step. It’s easy to fit hair and body mist into daily life: you can make it the final go-to in your routine at home, use it between wash days, and bring it along in a travel bag or handbag when you want a quick scent refresh on the go.


## How to Layer Hair Products with OLAPLEX Signature Hair & Body Mist


Layering hair products with OLAPLEX Signature Hair & Body Mist is straightforward: use your usual[OLAPLEX products](https://olaplex.com/collections/all-olaplex-products) first, then finish with the mist as the final fragrance step. This keeps your routine simple while letting the OLAPLEX signature scent sit on top.


## How Much Body Mist Should I Use?


Use OLAPLEX Signature Hair & Body Mist generously and reapply as desired. In practice, that means you can adjust the amount based on how noticeable you want the OLAPLEX signature scent to feel. For a lighter scent, start with fewer sprays. For a more noticeable scent, build with more sprays as desired.


## How to Make Body Mist Last Longer


Making body mist last longer is less about applying more all at once and more about how you work it into your routine. A few simple habits can help body mist last longer:


- Use it as the final fragrance step in your routine.
- Mist generously onto both hair and body.
- Reapply as desired when you want to refresh the OLAPLEX signature scent.
- Mist over pulse points, such as the wrists, behind the ears, or inside the elbows, where body warmth can help intensify scent.1
- Avoid rubbing mist into your skin after spraying, which can make top notes fade more quickly.
