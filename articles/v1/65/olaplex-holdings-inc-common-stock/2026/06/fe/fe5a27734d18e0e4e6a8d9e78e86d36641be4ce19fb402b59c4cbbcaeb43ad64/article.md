Curly hair definition often starts long before styling products are even applied. From choosing the right shampoo and conditioner for curly hair to using curl-friendly detangling techniques, small changes during cleansing can help support better curls and prevent frizz.

[Whether you have waves, curls, or coils](https://olaplex.com/blogs/news/how-to-determine-hair-type-and-texture) , building a moisture-heavy wash routine may help curls appear more defined and easier to style. ****


## Why Does Curl Definition Start in the Shower?


Curly hair naturally tends to be drier than straight hair because scalp oils have a harder time traveling down the bends and curves of the hair strand. When curls lose their moisture, they can separate, frizz, and lose their shape more easily.


****


That’s why using a shampoo for curly hair, as well as a nourishing conditioner specified for curly hair, can make all the difference during wash day. Hydration helps curls stay more elastic and encourages strands to group together into more uniform curl patterns.


****


The way you cleanse and condition your curls also affects things like:


- Curl clumping
- Frizz appearance
- Shine and softness
- Detangling ease
- Overall curl shape

## 2. Apply Conditioner Generously


Conditioner helps add slip and hydration back into curly hair after cleansing. Applying conditioner evenly from mid-lengths to ends may help curls feel smoother and easier to detangle.


While conditioning:


- Use fingers or a wide-tooth comb to gently detangle
- Work in sections if hair is thick or dense
- Avoid rough brushing that may separate curl clumps
- Add extra water if curls begin to feel dry during detangling


Many curl routines use the “squish to condish” technique, which involves gently pressing conditioner and water upward into curls to encourage hydration and curl grouping.

### 3. Detangle Carefully While the Hair Is Still Wet


Curly hair is often more manageable when detangled while coated with conditioner. Trying to detangle dry curls may lead to unnecessary tension or disruption to the curl pattern.


Start from the ends and slowly work upward toward the roots using:


- Fingers
- A wide-tooth comb
- A curl-friendly detangling brush


Taking your time during this step may help curls maintain a more consistent shape after styling.


### 4. Use Cool Water Towards the End of Your Shower


Rinsing with cooler water at the end of your shower routine may help the hair cuticle lay flatter, which can support smoother-looking curls and reduce the appearance of frizz.


You don’t need freezing cold water, but a cooler final rinse may help curls appear shinier and more defined.


## Common Shower Habits That Can Disrupt Curl Definition


Some wash-day habits may make curls appear less defined, even when using styling products afterward.


### Over-Washing Your Curly Hair


Washing too frequently may contribute to dryness for some curl types. Many people with curly hair prefer spacing out wash days to help maintain moisture and reduce dryness.


### Using Water That’s Too Hot


Very hot water may leave curls feeling drier or rougher by removing moisture too aggressively. Be sure to cool the water slightly when washing your hair. ****


### Rough Towel Drying


Traditional towel drying can disrupt curl patterns and create friction, which damages the hair. Instead, try:


- A microfiber towel
- A cotton t shirt
- Gently squeezing motions instead of rubbing the hair

## How Often Should You Wash Your Curly Hair?


There is no single wash schedule that works for every curl type. Factors like hair density, texture, scalp oil production, lifestyle, and product usage may all influence how often curly hair should be washed.


Some people prefer cleansing:


- A few times per week
- Weekly
- After workouts or heavy product buildup


If curls start to feel dry, brittle, or just difficult to define, adjusting your wash frequency or adding more[hydration focused products](https://olaplex.com/blogs/news/why-your-curly-hair-is-better-with-hair-masks) may help to combat that.
