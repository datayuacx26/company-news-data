# Riding the Wave of AI-Native Devices: Why Efficient User Plane Intelligence Is Becoming Mission Critical

Mobile networks, for decades, were built around a simple premise: people used their phones to talk. Today’s networks have evolved, and user devices have improved with it – they are now AI‑infused companions that listen, respond, predict, generate content, and orchestrate tasks all in real time. This shift is not just redefining what a smartphone is; it is fundamentally changing what a telecom network must be.


**New Devices, New Dynamics**


Despite all the benefits this mobile “renaissance” has introduced, AI-driven devices and user behavior also present sharp, unpredictable bursts of uplink traffic. As devices and applications snap photos, generate mini prompts, share metadata, and synchronize context between devices, they put stress on the network. Traffic that once posed little strain now moves in patterns the network was never designed for, adding new layers of complexity.


These traffic spikes:


- Hit the RAN with sudden uplink surges
- Create short, intense scheduling challenges
- Demand near‑instant prioritization of critical sessions
- Amplify user‑experience sensitivity to micro‑latencies


A GSMA[survey,](https://radcom.com/further-reading/operator-priorities-for-2026-data-automation-and-customer-experience/) in partnership with RADCOM, highlights this stark reality, with data services alone accounting for 45% of customer complaints. And this is not specifically counting AI-native devices. These AI devices require near-perfect consistency. Even minimal jitter becomes noticeable, and when something goes wrong, users don’t care whether the issue is the app, the device, or the network. They tend to blame the operator, risking churn and disloyalty.


**Understanding the Data Plane: Why User‑Plane Visibility Matters**


Without visibility, operators cannot accurately assess performance, allocate resources effectively, or enhance customer experience. Yet extracting these insights is increasingly difficult. Most data services today are encrypted end‑to‑end, making it harder than ever to derive meaningful analytics and understand what is happening across the network. Despite these challenges, **deep, real‑time user‑plane intelligence has become indispensable** .


**What is User-Plane Intelligence?**


In mobile networks, the **data plane** (or user plane) is the layer responsible for carrying all user related traffic i.e. the actual data transmitted between devices and applications. This includes everything from video streaming and online gaming to IoT sensor outputs, enterprise applications, voice calls, and everyday web browsing.


For operators, gaining insights into this user plane traffic is essential for understanding the true quality of experience (QoE) and quality of service (QoS) delivered to customers. User plane visibility provides a direct window into how subscribers actually experience the network, whether their videos stream smoothly, calls remain uninterrupted, or applications respond instantly.


**Why Traditional KPIs Are No Longer Enough**


Classic KPIs were designed for steady, predictable flows. However, AI‑powered devices generate ephemeral, dynamic, per‑session patterns that shift rapidly and behave unpredictably.


To understand these new realities, operators require:


- Per-subscriber visibility
- Real‑time anomaly detection
- Predictive quality management
- End-to-end awareness extending deep into the user plane


This is precisely where next‑generation user‑plane intelligence, and RADCOM’s unique capabilities, become mission‑critical.
