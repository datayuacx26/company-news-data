# Amazfit Signs 2x Olympic Medalist and World Champion Josh Kerr as Elite Athlete Partner

**Milpitas, Calif., February 2, 2026** —[Amazfit](http://www.amazfit.com/) , a leading global smart wearables brand owned by Zepp Health (NYSE: ZEPP), today announced its partnership with Josh Kerr, one of the most accomplished middle distance runners in the world. As Amazfit’s latest elite athlete, Kerr will integrate Amazfit wearables into his daily training, recovery, and competition, bringing real-world validation from the highest level of sport to the brand’s performance ecosystem.


‍


The partnership launches as Kerr returns to indoor competition at the Millrose Games, facing fellow Amazfit elite athlete Grant Fisher in the iconic 2-mile.


‍


As a core part of Amazfit’s performance testing team, Kerr has been training with the Amazfit[Balance 2](https://us.amazfit.com/products/balance-2) and[Helio Armband](https://us.amazfit.com/products/helio-strap) , fully integrating both devices into his daily routine across training blocks, recovery phases, and race preparation.


‍


“Amazfit is a brand that truly understands what elite performance requires. I respect how they support top-level athletes while still creating products that are accessible to everyone,” said Kerr. “As I continue to train smarter and compete against the best in the world, I’m proud to have Amazfit by my side as a key partner in my journey.”


‍


“Josh embodies the mindset we build Amazfit for: discipline, resilience, and precision at the highest level,” said Scott Shepley, Head of Global Marketing at Amazfit. “As he returns to competition, we’re proud to support him with technology trusted by athletes who demand excellence.”


‍


With Josh Kerr joining its growing roster of elite athletes, including Grant Fisher, Derrick Henry, Gabby Thomas, and Ruth Croft, Amazfit continues to establish itself as a performance platform trusted by the world’s best. From championship podiums and comeback moments to world-record ambitions, Amazfit is bringing elite-level performance insight to athletes everywhere.


‍


**About Amazfit**


Amazfit, a global smart wearable and fitness leader is part of Zepp Health (NYSE: ZEPP), a health technology company with its principal office based in Gorinchem, the Netherlands. Zepp Health operates as a distributed organization, with team members and offices across the Americas, Europe, Asia, and other global markets.


‍


Amazfit builds smart wearables designed around movement — training with intention, recovery with balance, and evolution over time. Built for the way people train today, Amazfit blends endurance, strength, and recovery into a single, coherent rhythm to support sustainable progress over time.
