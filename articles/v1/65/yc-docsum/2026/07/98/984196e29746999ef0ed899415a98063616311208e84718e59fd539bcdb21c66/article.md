# 🚀 Product Update: Email Uploads, Smarter Extraction, and Workflow Superpowers

This month’s Docsum release is packed with powerful new features to streamline your contract workflows and deliver even deeper visibility across your agreements.


### **📧** Upload Via Email


You can now upload contracts and documents directly by emailing them to a unique address provisioned for your account. Control access by whitelisting specific addresses or enabling anyone within your organization’s domain to forward contracts—making contract intake seamless, secure, and hands-off for your team.


### 🔗 Source Citations on Extracted Values


Docsum now displays exact citation sources for every extracted value, letting you instantly trace any data point back to the clause or section where it was found. Click the citation to jump straight to the relevant spot in the document, so you can review, verify, and trust every result with confidence.


### 📝 Redline Analysis Report via Email


Simply email in a contract with redlines from your counterparty and receive a detailed AI-powered report in your inbox. Each redline and comment is summarized, with actionable feedback on how to respond—personalized to your playbook and negotiation preferences. No more manual review: get instant guidance to drive smarter, faster negotiations.


### 🔒 Auth Login Facelift


We’ve redesigned the login and team invitation flows to be smoother, more intuitive, and easier on the eyes. Enjoy a modern, streamlined authentication experience for you and your team.


### ⚡ Bulk Reprocess


Quickly re-process multiple documents at once from the dashboard—perfect for when you update custom field configurations and need your data to reflect the latest extraction logic. No more manual reprocessing, just a few clicks for organization-wide updates. Similarity you can declutter your repository by archiving multiple contracts at once. Bulk archive helps you keep your Docsum workspace organized and focused, with easy management of legacy or inactive agreements.


### 🔍 Search Improvements


Find contracts and clauses faster than ever with our upgraded search. We’ve made search smarter, quicker, and more reliable across all your document views—so the information you need is always at your fingertips.
