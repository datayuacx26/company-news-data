# Product Update: Custom Fields

### Custom Fields: Your Contract Data, Your Way


We're excited to announce a major expansion of Docsum's data extraction capabilities, giving you more control and flexibility than ever before.


### Beyond Standard Fields: Create Your Own


While Docsum's standard fields (like Governing Law, Contract Value, and Expiration Date) have served as a foundation for contract analysis, we understand that every organization has unique data needs. Now you can create custom fields to extract exactly what matters to your business.


### Key Features:


-


Define custom extraction fields tailored to your specific contracts


-


Organize related data points with customizable field groups


-


Use AI-powered extraction to automatically pull data from your documents


-


Seamlessly integrate custom fields alongside Docsum Standard fields


### Coming Soon: Expanded Data Types


Currently, custom fields support Text and Clause extractions. We're expanding this capability to include:


-


Currency


-


Date & Time


-


Decimal


-


Duration


-


Lists


-


Single Choice selections


These additional data types will enhance extraction accuracy and enable more powerful filtering and searching capabilities in Views and reports.


### On the Horizon: Source Citations


As your contract data system of record, transparency is crucial. Soon you'll be able to trace any extracted data point—whether from Standard or Custom fields—back to its precise location in the source document. This feature will:


-


Provide clear audit trails for extracted data


-


Enable quick verification of contract terms


-


Enhance confidence in automated extractions


We'll keep you updated as these new capabilities roll out. In the meantime, start exploring custom fields to see how they can streamline your contract management workflow.
