Imagine you have spent weeks negotiating your MSA and order form with your customer and finally got to signature. You completed signing in Docusign, marked your deal closed won in your CRM, and booked revenue — $$$.


But once you’ve signed, there are teams downstream that rely on the data within the contract:


1.


**Customer Success** : Understand the renewal date, auto-renewal provision, negotiated pricing terms, and any defined obligations in the statement of work.


2.


**Marketing** : Understand if the counterparty has provided logo rights and the notification requirements


3.


**Finance** : Understand the negotiated pricing, billing terms, methods, penalties, and obligations to properly recognize revenue.


4.


**Security** : Understand the key SLAs, downtime credits, notification obligations, indemnification provisions, and breach remedies


**How are you going to report on these key items across your customer contracts?** How do you scale this to cover vendor agreements and other contracts you’ve signed within the business?
