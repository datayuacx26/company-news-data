# Transforming Collections With AI-enabled, Omnichannel Engagement

Concentrix led a phased migration to a modern, cloud-native CCaaS platform, moving the operation from voice-heavy outreach to an omnichannel, data-led engagement model.


Digital channels such as SMS, email, self-service, and voice bots were integrated into the collections strategy. AI-driven segmentation and propensity models enabled a “right customer, right channel, right time” approach, while predictive dialling, intelligent routing, automated callbacks, and bot-assisted collections helped improve efficiency and agent focus.


A structured governance framework supported migration planning, compliance validation, business continuity, agent training, and phased cutover execution.
