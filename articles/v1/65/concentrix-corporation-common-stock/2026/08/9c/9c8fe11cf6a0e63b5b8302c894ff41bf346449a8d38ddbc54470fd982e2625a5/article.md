# Turning Operational Data Into 1,600 Productive Hours Every Month

Concentrix leveraged advanced analytics and text mining to transform excess time from an isolated observation into a measurable operational performance metric. The team established a clear definition for Excess Time, identifying chats that remained open for more than 240 seconds after the traveler’s final response, and incorporated this logic into ongoing text-mining analysis.


To help operational teams take action, Concentrix implemented recurring reporting that provided visibility into site-level performance, behavioral trends, and repeat occurrences. This enabled leaders to identify coaching opportunities, monitor progress, and focus improvement efforts where they would deliver the greatest impact.


In addition, Concentrix developed a structured action plan and performance glidepath that translated analytical insights into operational improvements. By combining advanced analytics, governance, and targeted coaching, the client gained a sustainable framework to reduce idle time, improve agent productivity, and optimize handling efficiency at scale.
