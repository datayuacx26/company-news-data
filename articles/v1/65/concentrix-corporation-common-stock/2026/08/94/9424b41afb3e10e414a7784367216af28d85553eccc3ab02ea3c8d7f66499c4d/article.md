# Drive Better Decisions with Project Health

## Turn customer feedback into decisions you can trust.


Your VOC program is only as reliable as the sample behind it, and small data-quality problems can quickly reduce deliverability, participation, and confidence in the results.


Project Health gives your team a clear, always-on view of sample performance, so you can spot issues before they affect your insights. Validate each file, monitor volumes from records received through invitations sent, and check every customer segment for coverage gaps. Go in-depth by tracking delivery, opens, and survey starts to reveal early warning signs.


With automated alerts and self-service monitoring, your team can quickly identify invalid or duplicate records, exclusions, eligibility trends, and unexpected changes. Gain transparency into every stage of the sampling process, protect your sending reputation, and keep customer feedback representative.


Make faster, better-informed decisions while building a stronger foundation for customer experiences, all with Project Health.
