# Concentrix Recognized for Building a People-First Culture Worldwide

## **Company receives multiple honors across seven regions in the 2026 Inspiring Workplaces Awards.**


**NEWARK, Calif. (July, 2026)** – Concentrix Corporation (NASDAQ: CNXC), a global technology and services leader, today announced it has earned honors across seven regions in the 2026 Inspiring Workplaces Awards, reflecting the company’s commitment to creating an environment where people can do meaningful work, grow their careers, and make a lasting impact. The awards highlight Concentrix’ belief that great outcomes start with investing in people.


The Inspiring Workplaces Awards celebrate organizations that place people at the center of their business strategy and demonstrate excellence in creating positive and empowering employee experiences. Winners are evaluated on key areas such as culture and purpose, leadership, wellbeing, inclusion and belonging, employee voice, and employee experience.


Concentrix earned award placements across every major region in which the company operates, including:


- Top 5 Inspiring Workplaces Winner 2026 – Latin America


- Top 15 Inspiring Workplaces Winner 2026 – Asia


- Top 30 Inspiring Workplaces Winner 2026 – Europe


- Top 100 Inspiring Workplaces Winner 2026 – North America


- Top 50 Inspiring Workplaces Winner 2026 – UK & Ireland


- Top 40 Inspiring Workplaces Winner 2026 – Australia


- Top 10 Inspiring Workplaces Winner 2026 – Middle East & Africa


Across more than 70 countries, Concentrix invests in programs that support wellbeing, career development, leadership growth, inclusion and belonging, and community impact. By creating meaningful experiences for its people, the company strengthens its ability to deliver intelligent transformation solutions and exceptional outcomes for clients worldwide.
