#### NIKE CAMPUS


# The Future of CX


### 2nd Annual Executive Gathering


September 22


One Bowerman Drive
Beaverton, Oregon


## An invitation-only, peer-led conversation for CX leaders reimagining care, navigating AI, and redefining differentiated service.


During a moment of real disruption for customer care, CX executives are looking to their peers, not playbooks, for clarity. The Future of CX brings together senior leaders for candid conversations on what’s working, what’s not, and what’s next across customer experience and care.

## What CX leaders are talking about today


### Session topics will include:


- How concepts like unreasonable hospitality are making their way into the strategies of customer care leaders
- Redefining live support and CX excellence in complex, high-stakes environments
- The evolving CX, product, and engineering operating model
- Where and why AI delivers measurable impact in customer care or falls short
- Executive-level lessons from recent AI deployments
