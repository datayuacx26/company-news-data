# Retail Software Solutions Enhance User Experience

We empowered our client to identify technical solutions to optimize the client’s retail platform, deliver on the technology and application needs, and design better processes to ensure continued success.


### Stage 1: Architecture Development


- **Scalability Assessment and Planning:** We conducted a comprehensive assessment of the client’s existing platform and determined that its complexity necessitated a complete code overhaul and the implementation of new technologies.
- **Front-End and Back-End Overhaul:** As part of a comprehensive renewal of the platform, the client was able to redesign the interface to enhance user interaction and aesthetic appeal and upgraded the system to improve performance, scalability, and user-friendliness.
- **Transition to Single-Page Application (SPA):** To ensure scalability, we performed a comprehensive assessment of the platform’s needs and determined that its complexity required a complete code rewrite and the adoption of new technologies.


These new retail software solutions revolutionized our client’s online fashion platform, enhancing its speed, scalability, and user-friendliness. The partnership showed how maintaining swift and effective communication channels solidify trust and ensure seamless collaboration.


### Stage 2: Features Extension and Optimization


- **Support and Expertise:** Throughout the platform extension phase, the client received robust support and technical expertise, facilitating a seamless and efficient transition as new features were integrated.
- **Additional Functionalities:** The client implemented a product ranking automation tool that sorted products by its targeted needs and promoted specific brands. Additionally, it introduced a product information management system to manage all product details.
- **Payment and Shipping:** We integrated Klarna as a payment method and configured payments to be country-based, enabling specific payment methods and bonus schemes. We also enabled new shipping options, customizable by country, with new delivery partners.
- **User Experience Enhancement:** Our “shop the look” feature allowed customers to purchase entire outfits based on website photos, while integration of a search-as-a-service platform enabled users to filter products by brand, color, and size, significantly improving the shopping experience.


The extension and optimization of the platform provided our client with advanced features and tools, greatly enhancing their ecommerce capabilities and elevating user satisfaction. Additionally, the client saw improvements in the look and feel of the website with a new, custom-made UX design, further enhancing the overall user experience.


### Stage 3: Continuous Improvement


To further enhance customer retention, we empowered the client’s SEO expert to implement several key features and significantly boost search engine rankings over an extended period.


We also developed and integrated a sophisticated return system that enabled seamless, automated returns directly through the shop. This enhancement streamlined the return process for both customers and the client.


Finally, we developed a system allowing customers to create multiple wishlists, integrated with automated emails to improve customer retention and engagement, providing personalized recommendations and updates based on their wishlist activity.
