# The Automation Trap: Why Customer Service Automation Might Be Losing You Customers

Generative AI expands what businesses can automate, but automation should be judged by customer experience, not efficiency alone. The piece argues for optimizing effectiveness, ease, and emotion, fixing simpler workflow issues first, preserving human judgment in sensitive moments, and designing automation to detect, limit, and reverse mistakes cheaply.
