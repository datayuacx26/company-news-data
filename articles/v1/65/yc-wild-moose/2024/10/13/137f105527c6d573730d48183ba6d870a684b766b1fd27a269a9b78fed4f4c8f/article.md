# AI as the New First Responder in Production Incident Management

## Have you ever been woken up at 4 a.m. by a production alert, not knowing where to start?


Every engineer has been there. The stress, confusion, and pressure of responding to critical incidents are all too familiar. What if there was a way to tackle this problem head-on—and without dragging engineers out of bed at all?


## The future is here, and it’s powered by AI.


For years, production incidents have been chaotic, costly, and time-consuming. It’s more than just the frustration of late-night wake-up calls—it’s the real risk of lost revenue, damaged customer trust, and major downtime. But with the introduction of AI, that’s all about to change.


## AI: The New First Responder to Critical Incidents


In the past, when an alert was triggered, it was up to engineers to sift through data, check logs, review recent code changes, and manually identify anomalies—all while the clock was ticking.


Now, AI can do that work *before* an engineer even looks at the alert. Here’s how:


🔸 Analyze Logs: AI can instantly scan through logs to detect issues, saving engineers hours of time.


🔸 Identify Metric Anomalies: It can spot unusual patterns in metrics that could indicate a problem, even before it’s escalated.


🔸 Review Recent Code Changes: AI looks at the most recent code updates and cross-references them with the alert to pinpoint possible causes.


And the best part? Actionable insights are provided *immediately* —without any human intervention required.


## Say Goodbye to Wasting Time on Repetitive Alerts


By automating the initial triage process, AI frees engineers from checking alerts that often turn out to be nothing. It eliminates the need for repetitive tasks, enabling your team to focus on more complex, value-driven work.


But that’s just the beginning.


## Harnessing Tribal Knowledge and Turning It Into Powerful Automation


AI can turn your team’s tribal knowledge into valuable automation.


> Engineers and subject-matter experts often carry the deep knowledge of systems that help resolve issues quickly. However, this knowledge is usually siloed and difficult to scale. AI can capture this knowledge and convert it into automation workflows. Now, engineers can independently solve issues based on historical insights—without having to rely on SMEs for every incident.


This is the kind of innovation that ensures your team spends less time fighting fires and more time driving innovation.
