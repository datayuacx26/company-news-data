# Navigating Denver's New Single-Family Rental Compliance and Licensing Inspection Requirements


October 10, 2023


As the rental market in Denver continues to grow, the City of Denver has introduced[new inspection requirements](https://www.denvergov.org/Government/Agencies-Departments-Offices/Agencies-Departments-Offices-Directory/Business-Licensing/Business-licenses/Residential-rental-property/Inspections-and-inspector-information) to ensure tenants' safety and well-being in residential rental properties. These regulations are part of a broader effort to maintain high rental housing standards and provide residents with a safe environment. Here’s what you need to know about Denver’s new inspection requirements:


‍


**I. The Purpose Behind the New Requirements**


The City of Denver's decision to implement these new inspection requirements is driven by a commitment to safeguarding the rights and well-being of tenants. The city aims to identify potential hazards, ensure compliance with building codes, and ultimately create safer and more habitable living spaces by conducting thorough inspections.


‍


**II. Understanding the Compliance and Licensing Process**


Initial Inspection: A rental property must undergo an initial inspection before it can be licensed. This inspection will assess various aspects of the property, including structural integrity, electrical systems, plumbing, heating, and fire safety measures.


Compliance Updates: Depending on the initial inspection findings, landlords may be required to make necessary updates and repairs to bring the property up to code. This might include fixing faulty wiring, addressing plumbing issues, or making structural improvements.


Re-inspection: Once the required updates have been made, the property must undergo a re-inspection to ensure that all compliance issues have been adequately addressed.


Final Licensing: Once the property passes the re-inspection, it will be eligible for licensing as a single-family rental property in the City of Denver.


‍


**III. Key Areas of Inspection**


Structural Integrity: Inspectors will assess the overall stability and condition of the property's structure, including walls, floors, and roof.


Electrical Systems: This involves checking the electrical wiring, outlets, and switches, and ensuring they meet safety standards.


Plumbing: Inspectors will evaluate the plumbing systems, looking for leaks, ensuring proper drainage, and checking water pressure.


Heating and Ventilation: The heating system will be checked for functionality and safety, and ventilation systems will be inspected for proper airflow.


Fire Safety Measures: Include checking smoke detectors, fire extinguishers, and ensuring clear escape routes in emergencies.


General Safety Measures: Inspectors will also look for any potential hazards that could threaten the well-being of the tenants, such as loose handrails, trip hazards, or other safety concerns.


‍


**IV. Timeline and Deadlines**


It's crucial for landlords and property owners to be aware of the deadlines associated with these new requirements. Ensuring that inspections are completed in a timely manner will help prevent any disruptions in rental operations.


‍


**V. Conclusion**


The new inspection requirements for the City of Denver single-family rental compliance and licensing represent a significant step forward in ensuring the safety and well-being of tenants in the rental market. By adhering to these regulations, landlords and property owners comply with the law and contribute to creating a safer, more secure living environment for residents. Staying informed about these requirements and working proactively to address any compliance issues will benefit tenants and enhance the reputation and value of rental properties in Denver.
