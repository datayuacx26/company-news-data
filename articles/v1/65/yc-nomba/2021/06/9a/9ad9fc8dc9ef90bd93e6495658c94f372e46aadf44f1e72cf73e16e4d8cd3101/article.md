The Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) recently released a circular exempting certain organizations and institutions from the Implementation of the Cashless Policy titled[RE: IMPLEMENTATION OF THE CASHLESS POLICY](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/Out/2019/PSMD/Re-%20Implementation%20of%20the%20Cashless%20Policy.pdf) until March 31, 2020.


**What does the Circular mean?**

This circular is a follow up on the processing fees currently in implementation in certain states across Nigeria, including; Lagos, Ogun, Kano, Abia, Anambra, Rivers and the Federal Capital Territory (FCT) on Cash withdrawal and lodgement of ₦500,000 above for Individual accounts and ₦3,000,000 and above for Corporate accounts. The CBN has given exemptions to certain organizations and institutions of including Mobile Money Operators (MMO) on this particular policy till March 31, 2020. This means exempted bodies will not be charged on Cash Withdrawals or Lodgements above ₦3,000,000.


However, this Circular has no connection with the Stamp Duty charges currently being effected on all POS Transactions from ₦1000 and above. Commercial Banks have started and are currently enforcing the charges (₦50 for every POS transaction that is up to ₦1000 and above) on all POS Terminals as directed by the CBN.
