Have you ever tried to update an app and cannot? It can be frustrating. We wouldn’t want you to experience this with the Kudi app especially for Android users. We have compiled a list of reasons your update might be failing.


## STORAGE


If you have insufficient storage SPACE, you might encounter a failed update. You need to delete the apps or files you don’t need on your phone. Your phone might not give you any sort of notification, so you have to check how much space you have by yourself.


## INTERNET SPEED
