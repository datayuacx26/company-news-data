It is common knowledge to own more than one agent outlet, especially when you take into account the[number of unbanked folks](https://www.globaldata.com/central-bank-of-nigeria-looks-to-reach-out-to-60-million-unbanked-population-by-2020-with-payment-service-banks-says-globaldata/) we have in Nigeria. The[actualization](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/FinInc/) of CBN’s goals can only be met with Agents expanding their agent locations.


[Providing financial services to everyone across the country](https://medium.com/agent-banking/how-agency-banking-promotes-financial-inclusion-31bf32e63bc8) as always being the vision of Kudi, and we encourage agents to own more than one outlet.


Growth comes at a cost, you will need capital, personnel in creating new outlets. However, the benefits of owning multiple outlets far outweigh the disadvantages. Managing more than one outlet can be demanding. A lot of planning goes into managing these outlets.


Here are a few tips to help you manage your agent banking outlets;

This is vital because running two or more outlets means you won’t be physically present at all the locations at the same time. A new outlet will take up your time to set up, especially at the infant stage. A way to ensure this is by having the right staff in your current location.


## Have a Standard Operation Procedure


Develop a system or model that your outlets operate with. Rely on the expertise from your original location and use that to build your system for the new outlet. Develop strict guidelines that guide your operations in every location. There is a slight chance that different locations will have peculiarities, and one fast rule might not work for all. Some of these include working hours and customer support rules.


## **Hire Professionals and reliable Staff**


Hire people you can trust with your business. Also, you need to train your team periodically. Make plans to put them through on what to do and how to do it. You can also provide resources on[Agent Banking](https://medium.com/agent-banking) for them. You might never find professionals who have experience in Agent banking, but make sure your hired staff has all the qualities you are looking out for.


## Conduct Regular Evaluations of Your Outlets


Take the time to evaluate your outlets regularly and look out for what can be done better. This way, you can ensure that you can tackle everything that comes your way. Being proactive instead of reactive will help improve your business considerably.


## Use Existing Relationships or Relationship Skills


There are relationships that you already developed while running your first outlet. Don’t let those relationships go to waste. You can use these relationships to build new ones in your new location(s). Sometimes, it is the people skills you need. Use people skills to build new relationships in your new outlet.
