In a previous chapter of his life, Kelvin Okoduwa was handling installations for Cable TVs. Today, he spends his time overseeing a chain of agent banking outlets. He grew up in a family of 21 children, and he is a graduate of Business Administration from Lagos State University. He says, *“When you come from a family like that, you are left alone to fend for yourself”* ***.***


Kelvin has always been industrious. This led him into business, rather than pursuing a career in Business Administration. Kelvin, who started agent banking as far back as 2013, says “It wasn’t that lucrative at that time, all we could do was just bill payments until I heard about **Kudi** .” Meet Kelvin Okodouwa, the agent banker who wants to change the narrative about agent banking.


## **When did you start agency banking and how has the experience been so far?**

*I started agent banking in 2013 and I have never looked back since then. I can’t do any other business apart from agent banking. I have met a lot of people, and I have made so much as well. I think there are only a few businesses like agent banking where you can recoup almost 30% of your initial capital in the first month.*


Though the business comes with a lot of challenges, however, the benefits far outweigh the challenges. Transfers and withdrawals are the primary sources of income for me. One of my goals is to have more outlets in different states across the country.

## What does it take to be an agent?


Press enter or click to view image in full size


Every business has downsides, even though so much can be made from agent banking. Agent banking doesn’t require a lot of capital to start. You can start small. You will gain enough profit to scale your business.


> ***“*** *My advice to fellow agents is to put their customers first* ***”-*** Kelvin Okoduwa.


Also, as agents, there is a need to have empathy towards your customers. Sometimes I send my staff to follow customers to the bank just to rectify issues. The moment you understand that your only means of making money is by treating customers well, you will succeed.


## What is the potential of Agent Banking in Nigeria


Agent banking is a profitable business because it has really created wealth for us in the business. There is a huge customer base, agent banking has really helped families.


Imagine an area where you only have just one bank, and people tell me that “since you started your business I have never been to the bank”. Agent banking has really helped. Agent Banking is not for the rich or poor; it is for everybody **.**
