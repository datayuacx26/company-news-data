In our bid to make[financial services available](https://kudi.com/) to all, we have made our onboarding process for new agents super easy!


Just before we get into that, let’s answer the question; who is a Kudi agent? A Kudi agent is anyone who helps people send money, get cash or make bill payments within his or her local community using Kudi.


Kudi Agents make up for the deficiencies of the commercial banking system in terms of coverage across Nigeria. In other words, a Kudi Agent is like a Bank!

Because we believe Agents play a critical in driving financial inclusion in Africa, we as formidable partners are always working to ensure that they have no reason to worry when processing payments on Kudi; whether it’s helping people send money, receive cash or make a payment on any of the bill options available on Kudi.

Now, let’s look at how to get started on Kudi;


- Download the[Kudi App](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.kudi.agent) **** on Google Playstore.
- Create an Account by registering your Name, Phone Number, Business Name and other required details.
- Your registered phone number becomes your unique Kudi wallet identifier, along with your Kudi PIN, it gives you access to log in securely on Kudi.
- [Fund your wallet](https://kudi.com/faq/transactions-and-wallet/fund-wallet) and start transacting! Easy!


Let’s wrap this up by answering two questions we get asked a lot by new agents;


## Can I perform transactions without a POS Terminal?


Press enter or click to view image in full size


Yes, you can! Right from your Kudi App, you can help people send money to anywhere in Nigeria (Transfers).


Also, our bill payment options — Cable TV (DStv, GOtv, StarTimes), Electricity DISCOS (Ikeja Electric, Eko Electric, Abuja Electric, Kano Electric, Port Harcourt Electric, Ibadan Electric, Jos Electric, Kaduna Electric, and Enugu Electric) and Airtime/Data Recharge (MTN, Glo, Airtel & 9mobile) are transactions you can complete for customers, without using a POS Terminal.


## How do I get a POS Terminal?


Press enter or click to view image in full size


Start by helping people around you make bill payments; your good transaction count will be noticed and will qualify you for a free POS Terminal. Alternatively, you can make a purchase; simply click on the “ **Terminal POS** ” option under the sidebar on the App and follow the instructions to make a purchase.
