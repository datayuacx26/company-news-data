At Kudi, one of our core mission is providing access to financial services. We constantly reinvent the wheel to make sure that those we serve have easy access to our service(s). We are excited to announce that becoming an agent on the Kudi app has been made easy with the new on-boarding experience. We made the process more seamless and faster for our agents.


**Here are three top reasons we love our new on-boarding experience.**


## Easier to Fill


Press enter or click to view image in full size


The new on-boarding experience on the Kudi app is easier to fill. We have provided an easy flow that allows you to move in between pages seamlessly. Starting with a phone number and moving progressively, the flow is easy to relate with. Ensure you have your phone with you to be able to enter your OTP when it requests for it. Once you have this, everything else becomes easy to fill.


## User Experience is Better


The user experience just got a whole lot better. The log-in screen got a new look. We want you to enjoy being with us every step of the way. From the introductory screens to the very last flow of the on-boarding, we ensure that the language is easy to understand and self-explanatory.


## Security is Top-notch


With the new verification and validation process for BVN, your account with us is secure. BVN verification allows us to confirm that no one has stolen your information to access your account or funds. There are also different KYC levels that protect you and your customers.


We also added a fingerprinting feature that allows you to open your Kudi app with your thumbprint (Only those who have the fingerprint feature on their Android phone have access to this feature on the Kudi App).


**Yes, that’s right, we thought of everything! :)**

Here are a few more details to guide you during the new on-boarding process. This is applicable to both existing agents on the platform and new agents who want to register on the Kudi app.

To verify and register your account, you will input the following;


- Your first name
- Last name,
- Date of Birth(DOB),
- Phone number, as you have it on your BVN.


### **Please note that we do not have access to your financial records or credit records. the above information is collated to run a check against the details you gave us. This is to ensure that it is correct.**


- There is a section where you will need to take a picture for your profile. Image Capture of user’s face for data collation. Progressively, this will be used to verify that the person is the same as we have on the BVN data.
- There are 3 KYC levels with different transaction limits. A new user automatically falls into the starter level. To upgrade your KYC access, you can go to the KYC section and click on upgrade. Here you will be required to provide a government-issued identification card.
- You can edit your profile (minus BVN). Your business name and address can also be edited. Once a price of information you provide is verified, you cannot edit it.


**Other features we worked on include:**


- POS Purchase revamp is now located on the settings page in the accounts section. It was previously on the **Side Bar** .
- You can add bank accounts for cashing out in the Settings page. You can also reset your pin in **Settings** sections.
- If your phone has a fingerprint scanner, logging into the app becomes seamless. You can log into the Kudi app with your biometrics. You can also turn on or turn off the biometrics feature.
- A cool additional feature we added to **Transfers** allows you to select from a pool of your last 10 transfers to re-initiate the transaction.
- If you are an old user on the Kudi app, you will have to update your app to enjoy all of the benefits attached to the new on-boarding experience.


**Got any issues? Log them through the “Submit an Issue” tab in the app and have it resolved in record time!**
