Every business transaction requires some level of trust. As a business owner, you are asking people to trust you with their money. Trust like that is sometimes a tough thing to build because of the intrinsic value of money.


It is your duty as agents to get your customers to trust you. Building trust with your customers is a long term goal with even more extended benefits. It ensures continuity and longevity of your business.


Here are various ways to build trust with your customers;


## Excellent Customer Service

Customers are more likely to trust you when they believe that you care about the issues they are currently facing. Excellent customer service assures your customers that you are willing to do right by them, going above and beyond for the people that do business with you.

Your job here is to ensure customer satisfaction at all cost. You can do this through various means like attending to people in good time, excellent communication and a listening ear.


## Ask for Feedback


Agency Banking usually involves a lot of hand-holding. This means that regular contact with the customers is necessary because of the type of business you run. Feedback is the way to know whether or not you are satisfying your customers, or prioritising their issues. When they have a place to voice their opinions, they can tell you what needs improvement in your business.


## Encourage Word Of Mouth in your Neighborhood


Press enter or click to view image in full size


When you have pleased a customer, encourage them to tell others about it. Find ways to encourage and incentivize customers who return to do business with you. What this does is that it allows you to earn the trust of others. When people see that previous customers are pleased with your services, they are more inclined to trust you with their transaction as well.


## Provide Support for Customers


In Agency Banking, A Customer Support system is invaluable. Even more so as an agent, be ready to deliver support for customers that have issues with their transactions. While your support services do not have to be the most sophisticated or extensive, it has to be efficient. Train your staff to handle crisis management and issue resolution.


A trustworthy business demands more business than an untrustworthy one. Invest in getting your customer’s trust today and watch your business grow!
