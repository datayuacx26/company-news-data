# The Logbook Index · Freeport

The Freeport Logbook is the research publication of Freeport Markets. It covers portfolio construction and bet sizing, market microstructure, options and volatility, prediction markets, macroeconomics and asset pricing, corporate finance, and decentralized finance, along with a set of mathematical problems and an interview series with practitioners.
