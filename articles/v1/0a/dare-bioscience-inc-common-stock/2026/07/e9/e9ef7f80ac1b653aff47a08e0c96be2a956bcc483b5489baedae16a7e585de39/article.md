# Daré Bioscience CEO, Sabrina Martucci Johnson, to Participate in Virtual Investor Closing Bell Event

*Live video webcast on Tuesday, July 14* *th* *, at 4:00 PM ET* *Register*[here](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=kD9xlV7QZ0Q-oHWMADRpp6yZgUAohGtsa9Qmns9mo1QPlBEI03jOdvuvNe8EsfBFGPdRw7T6TGZ69nmz0P2hJBMfTqFbpxXmPueNhYLmydOhYRGLt-kNinwETtV2Vt5DnzPiPmd8AqX7dMgbFajW5g==)

(NASDAQ: DARE), a purpose-driven health biotech company solely focused on closing the gap in women's health between promising science and real-world solutions, today announced that it will participate in a[Virtual Investor Closing Bell](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=It8otvHNJbofY8OhlFckeScWeUT2S9iRwdiCNeG92fAVHAqFB2xZOeBWkHhebZknnyDhwpz04J0geGmzTRZsqRl9sE0_ZHWNZhzF25y-Fr2pn2fs70dCPj3uaXbHRe3oLpi6SViC3Ic_aCEXYpMcWoOzz0TYZXuNJ9Blg8AeWbg=) event on Tuesday, July 14, 2026, at 4:00 PM ET.


For the segment,[Sabrina Martucci Johnson, President and Chief Executive Officer](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=q-15JC3leebqYvLo_dlUp6w_r9vDArXC5obHwcx2zeZE484aMu7t1_vQ5r_LOiqsYtnpX0-mtkxLHkXXT51CT2I8Gk1PyJjpDyMlb1sB3Z3gaw9oGjmTxLkXDIwglZfj_v5hjs4vl1QY7VBr8AIo3AzgiGKabP_q23SduaiA7Pt-KPEjBG1ztdxp-xmpHxX40tlZvS--_eVomtV8pFAfGw==) of Daré Bioscience, will provide a brief corporate overview highlighting the recent launch of FloraSync LF5™, the Company's strategy for thoughtfully building its commercial business while advancing its differentiated women's health pipeline, and the milestones that will shape Daré's long-term growth. The discussion will also provide insight into the Company's disciplined approach to capital allocation, commercialization, and execution as it works to create sustainable value for shareholders.


Additionally, there will be a live question and answer session with audience participants. Ms. Martucci Johnson will answer as many questions as possible in the time allowed.

**ABOUT DARÉ BIOSCIENCE, INC.**


Daré Bioscience (NASDAQ: DARE) is a purpose-driven health biotech company solely focused on closing the gap in women’s health between promising science and real-world solutions. Every innovation Daré advances is based in advanced science and backed by rigorous, peer-reviewed research. From contraception to menopause, sexual health to fertility, vaginal health to infectious disease, Daré is working to close critical gaps in care using science that serves her needs. For decades, women have been told to “wait it out” or “live with it,” while innovations that could improve their quality of life languish in the regulatory or funding pipeline. With growing awareness around menopause, sexual health, and vaginal health, the conversation is shifting. However, access to proven solutions is lagging. Daré is working to change that.
