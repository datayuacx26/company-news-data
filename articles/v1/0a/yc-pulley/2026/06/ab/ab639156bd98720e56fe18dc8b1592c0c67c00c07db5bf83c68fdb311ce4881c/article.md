### **ASC 718 reporting and accounting integration**


Your cap table management platform needs to generate the financial reports your auditors and accounting team actually require. ASC 718 compliance demands precise stock-based compensation expense calculations. Manual processes introduce risk during year-end close through restatements, reconciliation delays, and audit findings that are entirely avoidable.


Look for ASC 718 integration that includes:


- **Automated expense calculations** applying the appropriate valuation methodology (Black-Scholes, Monte Carlo, or lattice models) based on award type

- **Modification tracking** that automatically recalculates incremental compensation cost when you reprice options or modify award terms
- **Forfeiture rate analysis** that applies historical data to reduce year-end true-up volatility
- **Audit-ready documentation,** including valuation assumptions, grant date fair values, and expense attribution by period

Finance teams preparing for board meetings or fundraising rounds need the ability to model future scenarios with precision and generate board-ready reports that withstand investor scrutiny. Building analyses manually in spreadsheets introduces error risk on compressed timelines.


Effective scenario modeling delivers:

## **How to select the right cap table software for your finance team**


The evaluation process should be systematic and grounded in your actual needs. The three dimensions you should look at include scalability as your equity structure grows, compliance depth, and integration with the tools your finance team already relies on. So, how can you work through each one?


### **Assessing your current compliance and reporting needs**


A common approach taken by many CFOs is to start with feature checklists, instead of mapping vendor evaluations by compliance gaps. Start by auditing your existing equity tracking infrastructure against what actually matters.


Document every equity event from the past 12-24 months, including:


- Option grants
- Exercises
- Vesting schedules
- Secondary transactions
- Financing rounds


Ask yourself if you can produce a complete audit trail for each transaction with supporting documentation. If your answer involves "I think it's in this folder," you have a compliance gap that will surface during your next audit or financing round.


You should also calculate how many hours your team currently spends each quarter preparing stock options and equity ownership reports and reconciling equity data with your[accounting system](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/consulting/deals/library/new-software-capitalization-standard.html) . If this process requires manual data entry or back-and-forth with legal counsel, you're exposed to audit findings and reconciliation delays that can be avoided.


This assessment reveals whether you need basic cap table tracking or a full[equity management platform](https://pulley.com/guides/best-equity-management-software) with audit-ready controls and accounting integrations.


### **Key questions to ask software vendors during demos**


A demo is your opportunity to push past polished workflows and understand how a platform performs under real-world pressure. Focus on four areas:


- **Data migration and historical accuracy.** How does the platform handle importing existing equity data, including SAFEs, convertible notes, and secondary transactions? A platform that can't cleanly migrate your data without manual reconciliation will create ongoing audit risk.
- **Audit trail depth.** Ask to see the audit log in action. Can you track every change with timestamps and user attribution? Does the system enforce role-based permissions? Finance-grade systems have these capabilities. Basic spreadsheet replacements don't.
- **Integration and reporting.** Does the platform connect to your accounting system to generate ASC 718 journal entries automatically? If it does, ask for a live demo. If you'll be reformatting spreadsheets every quarter, that's a gap worth understanding before you commit.
- **Pricing transparency.** Request a clear breakdown of all costs, including add-ons and support tiers. How the vendor handles pricing discussions during the sales process reflects how they'll handle the relationship long-term. Implementation timelines depend on your cap table complexity. Straightforward cap tables can be live in one to two weeks. Multiple share classes, convertible instruments, or historical transactions spanning several years typically require four to eight weeks or longer.


Migration support quality matters more than speed. Look for dedicated migration specialists who audit your data structure before import, data validation workflows that flag discrepancies before they're locked in, and parallel run periods where you can verify outputs against your current system.

## **Cap table software for finance teams FAQs**


### **What's the difference between cap table software and equity management software?**


Cap table software tracks ownership. Equity management software covers ASC 718 reporting, 409A valuations, compliance workflows, scenario modeling, and accounting integrations. You need the fuller system if audit-ready records and reliable reporting matter beyond a basic ownership snapshot.


### **When should a finance team move from spreadsheets to dedicated cap table software?**


Once you've raised institutional capital, issued SAFEs or convertible notes, or granted options to more than a handful of employees, spreadsheets create liability. If cap table maintenance takes more than a few hours a quarter, switching costs less than staying. For a detailed comparison, see[common cap table mistakes to avoid](https://site.pulley.com/guides/part-iii-is-your-cap-table-broken-mistakes-to-avoid-the-startup-founders-guide-to-equity) .


### **How does cap table software handle SAFEs, convertible notes, and warrants?**


You input each instrument's conversion terms once. The platform models how different financing scenarios affect dilution when those instruments convert, automatically across multiple assumptions simultaneously. This keeps board reporting and investor negotiations accurate under pressure instead of relying on manual reconciliation the night before.


### **Does cap table software integrate with NetSuite, QuickBooks, and other accounting systems?**


Leading software solutions do, though integration quality varies. Look for pre-built connectors that sync ASC 718 journal entries directly into your general ledger without manual data mapping. If a vendor can't demonstrate the connection clearly during a demo, that's a meaningful gap.


### **What security and compliance certifications should finance teams require from cap table software?**


SOC 2 Type II is the baseline. It confirms independent auditing of security controls over time, not just at a single point. For companies with EU stakeholders, verify GDPR compliance. Also, confirm AES-256 encryption for stored data and TLS 1.2 or higher for transmission.
