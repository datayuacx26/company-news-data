# How Much Does Customer Feedback Analytics Software Actually Cost?

Anyone who has tried to budget for customer feedback analytics knows the frustration: almost every enterprise vendor's pricing page says "contact sales." The numbers that do exist online come from competitor blogs and procurement platforms, not from the vendors themselves. That makes it hard to know whether a quote is reasonable before you are three calls deep into a sales cycle.


Here is the honest answer. Customer feedback analytics software spans three price bands: self-serve survey tools from roughly $19 to $50 per month, mid-market analytics platforms from roughly $25,000 per year, and enterprise platforms that typically run from tens of thousands to several hundred thousand dollars per year depending on data volume. Thematic, like most enterprise feedback analytics vendors, prices on usage: comment volume, the number of datasets, how each dataset is analyzed, and the support level, quoted as a custom proposal. The license is only part of the real number. Professional services, implementation, and overage charges can add 50% or more to what you actually spend.


This article breaks down the three bands with verifiable numbers, explains the pricing models behind the quotes, itemizes the hidden costs, and ends with the questions that surface a vendor's real total cost before you sign.


## What "customer feedback analytics software" covers


Customer feedback analytics software analyzes[unstructured feedback](https://getthematic.com/insights/text-analytics-applications) (open-ended survey responses, support tickets, reviews, call transcripts) and turns it into themes, sentiment, and metrics teams can act on. It is a different purchase from survey software, even though the categories overlap at the low end.


The distinction matters for budgeting:


- **Survey tools** collect feedback. Pricing is usually per user or per response, published openly, and cheap.
- [Feedback analytics platforms](https://getthematic.com/insights/customer-feedback-analytics-enterprise-guide) analyze feedback from many sources. Pricing is usually based on data volume, and at the enterprise level it is almost always a custom quote.
- **CX suites** bundle collection, analytics, and workflow. Pricing combines platform tiers with usage and add-on fees.


If a vendor publishes a low monthly price, you are almost certainly looking at a collection tool, not an analytics platform.


## The three price bands, with real numbers


Band Typical price Examples with published or filed prices


**Self-serve survey tools** $19 to $50 per month entry, scaling with users and responses SurveyMonkey from about $25 per user per month; SurveySparrow from about $19 per month; Zonka Feedback from about $49 per month


**Mid-market analytics platforms** Roughly $25,000 to $75,000 per year SentiSum publishes pricing from $3,000 per month, based on channels and feedback volume; enterprise VoC commitments commonly start near $25,000 per year


**Enterprise feedback analytics and CX platforms** Tens of thousands to several hundred thousand dollars per year Qualtrics deal data shows a median near $30,000 per year with contracts ranging past $126,000; Medallia's publicly filed government price schedule listed platform tiers from $20,000 to $168,000 per year


Two reference points deserve special attention because they are unusually verifiable.


**Qualtrics transaction data.** Procurement platform[Vendr reports a median Qualtrics contract around $30,000 per year](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/qualtrics) across 276 deals, with a range from about $6,500 to over $126,000. Averages skew much higher than medians in this market because a small number of very large contracts pull the mean up. When a vendor cites an "average customer," ask for the median instead.


**Medallia's filed government pricing.** Medallia's[public-sector price schedule](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/naspovaluepoint/1632345678_Medallia%20-%20Price%20Scheudle.pdf) (filed around 2021, so treat it as a dated reference point rather than current commercial pricing) listed platform tiers from $20,000 to $168,000 per year, and advanced feature tiers, including text analytics and AI add-ons, from $10,000 to $675,000 per year. The detail worth noticing: the analytics add-ons could cost more than the platform itself. That is what happens when text analytics is sold as a bolt-on to a suite rather than being the product.


## How enterprise pricing actually works


Enterprise feedback analytics quotes are built from a small set of pricing models. Knowing which one you are in tells you how the cost will behave as your program grows.


**Per-interaction or per-record pricing.** Qualtrics moved to[interaction-based pricing in April 2024](https://www.qualtrics.com/news/qualtrics-pricing-packaging/) : CX suites are billed by the volume of survey responses, calls, chats, emails, and social reviews processed, with unlimited users. Medallia prices on["Experience Data Records,"](https://www.medallia.com/pricing/) a similar usage-based unit. The upside is that seats are free, so you can roll insights out widely. The risk is that cost scales directly with feedback volume: a platform priced for today's channels gets materially more expensive as you add sources.


**Volume-and-scope pricing.** Thematic prices on comment volume, the number of datasets, how each dataset is analyzed, and the support level, quoted as a[custom proposal](https://getthematic.com/product/plan-and-pricing/) . This is also usage-based, but scoped to analysis rather than to every interaction touched, and it comes with a specific TCO argument: theme discovery and maintenance happen in the product, so keeping the model current does not require a professional services engagement.


**Platform tiers plus add-ons.** The suite model visible in Medallia's filed schedule: a platform tier sets the base, and capabilities like text analytics, AI features, and journey analytics are priced as separate add-on tiers. The quote looks modest until the add-ons that make the platform useful are included.


## The hidden costs that change the real number


The license is the visible number. Three other line items decide the real one.


- **Implementation and professional services.** Reports on Medallia implementations describe 6 to 12 month timelines with $50,000 to $200,000 or more in services on top of the license. Medallia's own filed schedule listed professional services rates from roughly $110 to $392 per hour. Any platform that needs vendor services to configure taxonomies or build reports carries this cost for its whole life, not just at setup.
- **Overages and escalation.** Usage-priced platforms charge for volume above the contracted band. Analyses of Qualtrics contracts describe first-year hidden costs in the $8,000 to $10,000 range from implementation, overages, and add-ons, plus annual price escalation at renewal.
- **Analyst time.** The quietest cost is the one on your own payroll. A[Forrester Total Economic Impact study](https://getthematic.com/forrester-total-economic-impact-study) commissioned by Thematic quantified it for a composite organization: $652,000 per year in savings from automating manual analysis, representing 4,250 hours of data preparation and the equivalent of two full-time analysts.


If a cheaper tool leaves the coding and reporting work with your team, it is not cheaper.


For teams weighing the[do-it-yourself route](https://getthematic.com/insights/ai-feedback-analytics-build-buy-matrix) instead, Thematic's[build-cost analysis](https://getthematic.com/insights/cost-to-build-an-ai-feedback-analytics-platform/) estimates an in-house AI feedback platform at roughly $1.8 million over five years, against roughly $900,000 over five years for a comparable SaaS deployment. The figures are illustrative, but the two-to-one ratio is the point.


## What the money buys: cost against outcomes


A price is only high or low relative to what it returns. The verifiable outcomes in this category look like this.


The Forrester TEI study of Thematic found a 543% return on investment over three years, with payback in under six months and insight delivery cut from weeks to minutes. Greyhound, the intercity bus network, reduced feedback analysis that took two to three weeks to ten minutes, and its NPS rose about 15 points within a few months. Community Health System, a not-for-profit healthcare network in California's central San Joaquin Valley, produced 250 one-page department reports from employee survey feedback in three days, saving 160+ hours and about $10,000 per reporting cycle. Atom Bank, the UK's first app-only bank, used feedback insights to simplify its mortgage journey and cut related customer calls by 69%.


Numbers like these are why the sticker price is the wrong place to start the evaluation. Start with the cost of the problem: the analyst hours spent coding feedback by hand, the decisions delayed by weeks-long analysis cycles, and the professional services fees attached to your current stack. ## A buyer's checklist for pricing conversations


Seven questions that surface the real cost before you sign:


1. What unit is the price based on: seats, interactions, data records, or comment volume? How is the unit counted?
2. What happens to the price at twice our current feedback volume? Ask for the number in writing.
3. What is included in implementation, and what is billed as professional services? At what hourly rate?
4. Can our analysts build reports and edit the theme model themselves, or does that route through your services team?
5. Which capabilities in this demo are add-ons priced separately from the platform tier?
6. What is the annual escalation at renewal?
7. What overage charges apply, and is there a cap?


A vendor who answers all seven crisply is telling you the real price. A vendor who cannot is telling you something too.


## The short answer


Customer feedback analytics software costs anywhere from $19 per month for self-serve survey tools to several hundred thousand dollars per year for enterprise platforms, with the middle of the enterprise market clustering in the tens of thousands: Qualtrics deal data shows a median near $30,000 per year, and Medallia's filed government pricing listed platform tiers from $20,000 to $168,000. Thematic prices on comment volume, datasets, and support as a custom quote. Whatever the vendor, model the total cost, not the license: implementation, professional services, overages, and your own analysts' hours routinely exceed the subscription line. The fastest test: run the seven checklist questions above in your next pricing call.
