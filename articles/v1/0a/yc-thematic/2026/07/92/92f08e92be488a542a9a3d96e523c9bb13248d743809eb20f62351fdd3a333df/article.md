# 7 Best InMoment Alternatives for Customer Feedback Analytics in 2026

Teams searching for InMoment alternatives in 2026 are usually reacting to one of two things. The first is a set of long-standing frustrations that show up consistently in reviews: implementations that depend heavily on InMoment's services team, custom reports that route through vendor support instead of self-service, and pricing that climbs as add-ons stack up. The second is newer: InMoment is no longer an independent company.[Press Ganey Forsta acquired InMoment](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250514662998/en/Press-Ganey-Forsta-Acquires-InMoment-Accelerating-AI-Innovation-in-Customer-Experience-and-Expanding-Cross-Industry-Expertise) in May 2025, and Qualtrics announced a[$6.75 billion acquisition](https://www.qualtrics.com/news/qualtrics-to-invest-6-75-billion-in-press-ganey-forsta-acquisition-to-advance-ai-powered-experience-management/) of Press Ganey Forsta in October 2025, completing the deal in May 2026. InMoment, Forsta, Decipher, and Clarabridge now sit under a single Qualtrics-owned roadmap.


The best InMoment alternative depends on what you actually need. For enterprise teams whose core problem is analyzing customer feedback (turning surveys, support tickets, reviews, and call transcripts into themes leadership can trust), Thematic is the strongest dedicated alternative. It is a customer intelligence layer, not another all-in-one suite: it sits on top of your existing collection tools and replaces the bolt-on text analytics that bundled platforms treat as a module. Teams that need a full replacement suite, or contact-center-specific voice analytics, have credible options too, and they are on this list.


This guide covers seven alternatives across three categories: enterprise VoC suites, contact center interaction analytics, and dedicated AI feedback analytics platforms. Each entry includes what the tool is genuinely good at, where it falls short, and who should pick it.


## The 7 best InMoment alternatives at a glance


1. **Thematic** : best for enterprise CX and insights teams that need accurate, editable AI theme analysis across every feedback channel.
2. **Medallia** : best for enterprises that want a full VoC suite outside the Qualtrics ownership cluster.
3. **Qualtrics XM Discover** : best for teams already committed to the Qualtrics ecosystem who want conversational analytics.
4. **Chattermill** : best for consumer brands that want multilingual sentiment dashboards across high-volume channels.
5. **Kapiche** : best for mid-market teams that want no-code feedback analysis without an enterprise rollout.
6. **NICE** : best for contact centers that need speech analytics and agent performance management at scale.
7. **CallMiner** : best for teams that want to analyze 100% of contact center conversations in real time.


## Why are teams looking for InMoment alternatives?


InMoment is a capable platform. It holds a[4.7/5 rating across roughly 319 G2 reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/inmoment-experience-improvement-xi-platform/reviews) , and Gartner has recognized it as a Magic Quadrant Leader for Voice of the Customer platforms. But four patterns recur in reviews and buyer conversations:


- **Services dependency.** Implementation is long and resource-heavy, and configuration work often runs through InMoment's services team rather than your own analysts.
- **Slow self-service.** Building custom reports frequently means a request to vendor support instead of a few clicks in the product.
- **Cost accumulation.** The platform is priced for large enterprises, add-ons cost extra, and there is no easy way to trial before committing.
- **Roadmap uncertainty.** After the May 2025 Press Ganey Forsta acquisition and the October 2025 Qualtrics deal, InMoment's product direction is set inside a much larger consolidated portfolio. Buyers who want an independent vendor now have to look elsewhere by definition.


InMoment's text analytics also has a specific lineage worth understanding: it is built substantially on[Lexalytics, the NLP company InMoment acquired in September 2021](https://inmoment.com/news/inmoment-completes-acquisition-of-lexalytics/) . That engine predates the current generation of large language models. Dedicated analytics platforms built around modern AI theme discovery take a different approach, and for many teams that difference is the reason to switch.


## 1. Thematic


**Thematic is a[customer intelligence platform](https://getthematic.com/insights/customer-intelligence-platforms-enterprise-cx-teams) that turns unstructured feedback from every channel into themes that are accurate, editable, and traceable back to the raw comments behind them.** It is the best InMoment alternative for enterprise CX, insights, and research teams whose core need is analysis rather than survey distribution. Thematic connects to the tools you already use for collection (survey platforms, support desks, app stores, review sites, call transcripts) and produces one consistent theme structure across all of them.


The differentiator is how themes are built. Instead of a predefined taxonomy that drifts as customers change how they talk, Thematic discovers themes bottom-up from the feedback itself, and analysts can inspect and edit the theme structure directly. No professional services engagement is needed to keep the model current, which is precisely the maintenance burden InMoment reviewers describe.


The proof points are specific. Atom Bank, the UK app-based digital bank, unified feedback from seven engagement channels across three product lines (app store reviews, Trustpilot, support complaints, call summaries, and their CEM platform) and cut calls on unaccepted mortgage requests by 69%, savings maturities by 43%, and device issues by 40%, while growing its customer base 110% year over year. Vodafone New Zealand replaced manual categorization of NPS verbatims, saved 60 hours per month, and saw a double-digit tNPS increase after nine months. A[Forrester Total Economic Impact study](https://getthematic.com/forrester-total-economic-impact-study) commissioned by Thematic found a 543% three-year ROI with payback in under six months.


**Trade-off:** Thematic is an analytics layer, not a survey tool. If you need to design and distribute surveys, you pair Thematic with a collection tool rather than replacing everything at once. Thematic also[integrates with InMoment directly](https://getthematic.com/integrations/inmoment) , so you can keep collection running while you upgrade the analytics. For teams that want one vendor for everything, a suite may be simpler.


**Best for:** enterprise insights and CX teams that need trustworthy, editable AI analysis across multiple feedback channels, without a rip-and-replace project.


## 2. Medallia


**[Medallia](https://getthematic.com/insights/top-medallia-alternatives) is the most credible full-suite alternative for enterprises that want to stay outside the Qualtrics ownership cluster.** It is an enterprise experience management platform with real-time, multilingual text analytics that detects emotion, sentiment, intent, and themes, plus operational strengths like frontline alerting. In 2025 Medallia added AI features including Intelligent Summaries and Root Cause Assist.


Medallia's strength is operational CX at scale: capturing signals across surveys, mobile, web, and messaging, and routing alerts to frontline teams in real time. For organizations in financial services, hospitality, and telco that want a deeply embedded operational platform, it remains a standard choice.


**Trade-off:** reviewers on Gartner Peer Insights and Software Advice report high total cost of ownership, restrictive customization that often requires admin-level or vendor help, a steep learning curve, and performance lag on very large datasets. Insights teams that need deep, self-service[text analytics](https://getthematic.com/insights/the-best-text-analytics-software) often find the analytics module is the weak layer, the same complaint that drives teams away from InMoment.


**Best for:** large enterprises that want a full, independent VoC suite with real-time operational alerting, and have the budget and admin resources to run it.


## 3. Qualtrics XM Discover


**Qualtrics XM Discover is the conversational analytics product inside the Qualtrics XM suite, built on the[Clarabridge](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20211001005400/en/Qualtrics-Completes-Acquisition-of-Clarabridge) technology Qualtrics acquired in October 2021.** It analyzes conversations across calls, chat, email, social, and reviews, and enriches them with sentiment, effort, and emotion scoring. Qualtrics itself offers the deepest survey design capability on this list.


There is an obvious nuance in listing Qualtrics as an InMoment alternative: Qualtrics now owns InMoment's parent company. Moving from InMoment to XM Discover is a move within the same vendor family, not away from it. For some buyers that is fine, and consolidation onto the acquirer's flagship platform may even be the safe path. For buyers whose concern is roadmap independence or vendor concentration, it defeats the purpose. Those buyers usually end up comparing[Qualtrics alternatives](https://getthematic.com/insights/qualtrics-competitors-and-alternatives) as well.


**Trade-off:** Qualtrics is survey-first and complex, and it moved to interaction-based pricing in April 2024, billed by the volume of responses, calls, and reviews processed. High-volume feedback programs should model costs carefully.


**Best for:** teams already committed to Qualtrics for surveys that want conversational analytics from the same vendor and accept the consolidated roadmap.


## 4. Chattermill


**[Chattermill](https://getthematic.com/insights/chattermill-alternatives-and-competitors) is an AI-native feedback analytics platform aimed at consumer brands with high-volume, multilingual feedback.** Its Lyra AI engine clusters themes and runs aspect-based sentiment analysis across surveys, reviews, support tickets, voice, and social channels in more than 50 languages.


Chattermill sits in the same dedicated-analytics category as Thematic: it is a layer over your collection tools, not a suite. Its multilingual aspect-based sentiment is a genuine strength for global consumer businesses.


**Trade-off:** reviewers describe an action gap (strong dashboards, weaker downstream workflow) and note that preconfigured dashboards and less transparent AI reduce flexibility for research teams that want to interrogate and edit the theme structure. "AI Limitations" is a recurring complaint category in Chattermill's G2 reviews.


**Best for:** consumer brands that prioritize multilingual coverage and polished dashboards over deep theme editability.


## 5. Kapiche


**Kapiche is a no-code feedback analytics platform that auto-builds a theme framework for each dataset and ties themes to metrics like NPS, churn, and revenue.** It is popular with mid-market CX and insights teams because setup is fast and analysts can run it without engineering support.


**Trade-off:** reviewers note it is not built for deep or complex use cases. Advanced statistical and predictive modeling is limited, and the simplicity that makes it fast to adopt becomes a constraint as feedback volume and workflow needs grow into enterprise territory.


**Best for:** mid-market teams that want quick, accessible feedback analysis and do not yet need enterprise-grade governance or workflow depth.


## 6. NICE


**NICE offers contact center interaction analytics (formerly Nexidia Analytics, now part of its Enlighten AI portfolio) that analyzes speech and text from customer interactions at enterprise scale.** It draws on more than 20 years of speech recognition research and holds 70+ patents, and it is strong at understanding sentiment, customer intent, and agent behaviors inside the contact center.


**Trade-off:** NICE is contact-center-centric. It excels at calls and agent performance, but it is not a like-for-like VoC replacement covering surveys, product feedback, and online reviews. Teams replacing InMoment's full multi-channel scope would be narrowing their coverage.


**Best for:** contact center operations that need deep speech analytics and agent performance management, alongside (not instead of) a feedback analytics platform.


## 7. CallMiner


**CallMiner's Eureka platform transcribes, categorizes, and scores 100% of customer conversations across voice, chat, email, and social.** Like NICE, it comes from the contact center world: real-time monitoring, automated scoring, and agent evaluation are its core jobs.


**Trade-off:** reviewers report transcription accuracy issues, limited reporting flexibility, and complex initial setup with meaningful data integration work. It is an interaction analytics tool, not a general customer feedback analytics platform.


**Best for:** teams whose feedback program centers on contact center conversations and who need every interaction analyzed, not a sample.


## How to choose between them


Five questions separate these tools quickly:


1. **Is your problem[collection or analysis](https://getthematic.com/insights/customer-feedback-analytics-vs-management-difference) ?** If your surveys and pipelines work but insights are slow or untrusted, a dedicated analytics layer (Thematic, Chattermill, Kapiche) fixes the actual problem without a migration. If collection is also broken, look at a suite.
2. **Can your analysts see and edit the themes?** Ask each vendor to show theme editing live in a demo, on your data. Black-box categories are the top driver of stakeholder distrust. Thematic makes the theme structure fully editable; several tools on this list do not.
3. **Who maintains the model when customers change how they talk?** If the answer involves a professional services engagement, you are re-buying the InMoment maintenance problem. A good answer is: the themes update from the data, and your team adjusts them in the product.
4. **What happens to your costs at your real volume?** Interaction-based and add-on pricing models behave very differently at millions of feedback items. Model your actual volumes before signing anything.
5. **Does vendor independence matter to you?** After the 2025 acquisitions, InMoment, Forsta, and Clarabridge share an owner with Qualtrics. If concentration risk is on your scorecard, weight Medallia and the dedicated analytics platforms accordingly.
