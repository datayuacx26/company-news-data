# The 8 Best Software Tools to Analyze Customer Support Tickets in 2026

Support tickets are the most honest feedback a company gets. Customers write in with the exact problem, in their own words, at the moment it is costing them something. The trouble is volume. A Gartner survey of 5,728 customers found that[only 14% of customer service issues are fully resolved in self-service](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-08-19-gartner-survey-finds-only-14-percent-of-customer-service-issues-are-fully-resolved-in-self-service) , so the rest turn into tickets, and the patterns inside them stay buried unless something reads every one.


The best software to analyze customer support tickets reads the full ticket text, groups it into themes automatically, and connects those themes to the metrics leaders care about. For enterprise CX and Insights teams that want auditable, editable themes across tickets and other channels, Thematic is the strongest fit. Which tool is right for you depends on whether you need deep analysis and reporting, real-time triage in the queue, or an all-in-one suite.


One distinction to keep in mind while you read. Some tools analyze tickets to find themes and root causes (analytics). Others act on tickets in the queue by routing or auto-resolving them (triage). This list ranks the analytics tools, because that is what "analyze support tickets" means, and it flags where a tool leans toward triage instead.


## The list at a glance


1. **Thematic.** Transparent, editable theme analysis across tickets and other channels, tied to NPS and CSAT drivers.
2. **Chattermill.** Cross-channel VoC analytics that links ticket themes to churn and revenue.
3. **Enterpret.** A self-refining taxonomy trained on support data, built for product-led teams.
4. **SentiSum.** Support-native tagging that quantifies ticket drivers in real time.
5. **unitQ.** A single product-quality score from tickets and app feedback, aimed at consumer tech.
6. **Zendesk (Explore and Advanced AI).** Native reporting and triage for teams already in Zendesk.
7. **Qualtrics XM Discover.** Enterprise text and speech analytics for org-wide programs.
8. **Medallia.** Mature omnichannel text analytics inside a full experience platform.


## 1. Thematic


**Thematic** is an AI customer intelligence platform that turns support tickets into themes an analyst can see, edit, and defend. It reads every ticket, discovers the themes from the language itself rather than a preset list, and ties each theme to score drivers like NPS and CSAT. Native integrations with[Zendesk and Intercom](https://getthematic.com/insights/zendesk-ticket-analytics-intercom-chat-analytics) pull ticket and chat data in directly, and Thematic tags each conversation with the[reasons customers are contacting support](https://getthematic.com/insights/customer-service-insights) .


The differentiator is transparency and control. Every theme traces back to the exact tickets behind it, and analysts can rename, merge, or restructure themes without a data scientist. Serato, an audio software company, uses Thematic's Zendesk integration so its support team can[act on specific issues](https://getthematic.com/insights/identify-root-causes-support-tickets-automatically) instead of sorting comments into five broad buckets by hand. Atom Bank unified support-center complaints and Salesforce call summaries with other channels and cut calls on its top contact reasons by 40 to 69%.


Honest limitation: Thematic analyzes aggregated ticket feedback to produce insight. It is not built to tag, triage, and resolve individual tickets as they arrive in the queue. Best for enterprise CX and Insights teams that want auditable, editable themes across tickets and surveys.


## 2. Chattermill


**Chattermill** is a unified customer feedback analytics platform that treats ticket analysis as one part of a wider Voice of Customer program. It brings surveys, tickets, reviews, social, and call transcripts into one place and connects the themes to business metrics like churn and revenue. It integrates with Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, and Salesforce.


Its genuine strength is cross-channel breadth for enterprise CX teams that need granular theme detection everywhere customers speak. The trade-off is that the wide scope means a heavier setup than a support-only tool. Best for enterprise teams that want ticket themes in the context of every other channel.


## 3. Enterpret


**Enterpret** is a customer-intelligence platform whose taxonomy refines itself as new support data arrives. It ingests Zendesk, Intercom, Front, Salesforce Service Cloud, and dozens of other channels, adds revenue context, and routes findings into the product roadmap.


Its strength is the adaptive taxonomy, which reduces the manual upkeep other tools need, and its fit with product-led SaaS teams that pair customer success with product. The trade-off is that the product-team framing fits pure CX and support reporting less cleanly, and the taxonomy is less directly analyst-editable than Thematic's. Best for product and CS teams that want a taxonomy that maintains itself.


## 4. SentiSum


**SentiSum** is built specifically for support. It tags every ticket by topic, subtopic, and sentiment, then surfaces the drivers behind ticket volume in real time and can route tickets on the way in. It integrates natively with Zendesk and other helpdesk channels.


Its strength is depth within the support queue: purpose-built tagging that quantifies exactly why tickets are coming in. The trade-off is that it works mostly inside support, so it is narrower than a cross-channel VoC platform. Best for high-volume, support-led teams that want to quantify ticket drivers without a broader VoC program.


## 5. unitQ


**unitQ** turns tickets, app reviews, and other signals into a single product-quality benchmark called the unitQ Score. It watches for anomalies in real time and routes issues into engineering workflows, which makes it strong as an early warning system for bugs.


Its strength is speed of detection for consumer tech, gaming, and marketplace products. unitQ itself notes the tool may not be the right fit for B2B or enterprise CX reporting, so the fit is narrower than a general CX platform. Best for consumer product teams that treat support signals as a product-quality feed.


## 6. Zendesk (Explore and Advanced AI)


**Zendesk** offers reporting through Explore and an Advanced AI add-on that assigns intent, sentiment, and language to tickets on the first message. Because it lives inside the helpdesk, there is no data to move, and the operational dashboards are strong.


Its strength is convenience for teams already standardized on Zendesk. The trade-off is scope: Advanced AI is built for triage and routing rather than deep theme discovery, it is a paid add-on on top of the Suite plan, and the analytics stay operational rather than exploratory. Best as a baseline for Zendesk-native teams that want triage plus reporting without adding a vendor.


## 7. Qualtrics XM Discover


**Qualtrics XM Discover** , formerly Clarabridge, is an enterprise text and conversational analytics module. It handles text and speech analytics at large scale with strong governance, and it can ingest tickets, calls, chat, and surveys.


Its strength is depth and governance for organizations running experience management across the whole company. The trade-off is that XM Discover is priced separately from the base Qualtrics platform and carries a heavier implementation. Best for large enterprises that already run an org-wide Qualtrics program.


## 8. Medallia


**Medallia** is a full experience platform with mature omnichannel text analytics. Its NLP has more than a decade behind it, and it pairs analysis with predictive AI and frontline activation across many touchpoints.


Its strength is scale and breadth for global enterprises with complex hierarchies. The trade-off is that it is a broad platform rather than a support-ticket specialist, and it carries enterprise cost and complexity. Best for large organizations that want ticket analysis inside a single omnichannel experience suite.


## How to choose between them


Ask these questions in a demo to separate the tools quickly.


1. Does it read every ticket and discover themes automatically, or do I define the categories first?
2. Can my team see and edit the themes without a data scientist?
3. Does it integrate natively with my helpdesk, whether that is Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, or Salesforce?
4. Does it[connect ticket themes to a business metric](https://getthematic.com/insights/link-feedback-themes-nps-csat-drivers) like CSAT, NPS, or churn?
5. Am I trying to analyze tickets for insight, or to route and resolve them in the queue? Those are different tools.
6. Does every theme trace back to the specific tickets behind it?


## The short version


For enterprise CX and Insights teams that want to analyze support tickets and trust the results, Thematic is the strongest pick, because the themes are transparent, editable, and tied to score drivers across tickets and surveys. If your priority is real-time triage inside the queue, a support-native or helpdesk-native tool like SentiSum or Zendesk Advanced AI fits better. The fastest way to decide: run a demo on your last 90 days of tickets and ask each tool to show you a theme you did not already know was there.
