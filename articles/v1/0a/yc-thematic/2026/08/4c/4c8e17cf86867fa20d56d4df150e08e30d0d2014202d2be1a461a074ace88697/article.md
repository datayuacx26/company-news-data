# 5 Best Lumoa Alternatives for Enterprise Customer Feedback Analytics

If you are searching for Lumoa alternatives, start with the fact that most comparison pages still get wrong: Lumoa is no longer a standalone product.[Netigate acquired Lumoa in March 2024](https://www.eu-startups.com/2024/03/stockholm-based-netigate-acquires-finnish-startup-lumoa-to-give-birth-to-a-leading-experience-management-solution/) , and the independent Lumoa platform has since been folded into Netigate's experience management suite. Teams that liked what Lumoa did well, finding what drives a net promoter score (NPS) up or down without a heavy setup project, are now choosing between staying inside Netigate or moving to a dedicated feedback analytics platform.


The best Lumoa alternative for enterprise customer feedback analytics is Thematic, when the priority is quantifying[which themes moved a score](https://getthematic.com/insights/quantify-themes-moved-nps-score) and being able to show your work. Thematic builds a two-level theme taxonomy from the feedback itself. It links each theme to its[impact on NPS or customer satisfaction score (CSAT)](https://getthematic.com/insights/link-feedback-themes-nps-csat-drivers) . Every theme traces back to the raw comments behind it. Other tools fit other situations. Chattermill suits high-volume multilingual feedback across business units, and Qualtrics XM Discover suits enterprises already committed to Qualtrics. Medallia suits organizations that want text analytics inside a broad experience suite. Kapiche suits mid-market insights teams that want a fast first result.


Below is the shortlist, what happened to Lumoa in more detail, where each alternative genuinely wins, and the trade-off to weigh before signing anything.


## The list at a glance


1. **Thematic** : enterprise CX and insights teams that need to quantify what moved a score and defend the answer.
2. **Chattermill** : large CX-led brands analyzing high-volume feedback across many languages and business units.
3. **Qualtrics XM Discover** : enterprises already running Qualtrics that need omnichannel conversational analytics.
4. **Medallia** : organizations that want text analytics and impact scoring inside a broad experience management suite.

## What happened to Lumoa


Lumoa was a Helsinki-based customer experience platform known for two things: identifying what drives an NPS score up and down, and letting non-analysts query feedback in plain language. It still appears in comparisons of[NPS verbatim analysis tools](https://getthematic.com/insights/best-tool-for-analyzing-nps-verbatims) under the Netigate name. Netigate, a Stockholm-based experience management company, acquired it in an announcement dated March 26, 2024.


Since then the standalone platform has been retired. Netigate runs a page stating that "Lumoa is Netigate and www.lumoa.me can no longer be accessed," that "Lumoa Oy is part of Netigate Group since the spring of 2024," and that the platform "has undergone a re-brand and got a new name (Insights), but the value and functionality remains the same." The lumoa.me domain now returns a redirect to that page.


That matters for three practical reasons:


- **Comparison pages are stale.** Several round-ups still rank Lumoa as a live independent product against current competitors. Any evaluation built on those pages is measuring against something that no longer ships on its own.
- **The roadmap changed owners.** Feature direction now follows Netigate's combined product strategy rather than Lumoa's original one.
- **The original job is still worth solving.** Wanting to know which themes moved your score, without a months-long taxonomy project, is a reasonable requirement. It is the requirement the tools below address.


If Netigate's combined survey collection and analysis suite fits, staying put is a perfectly good answer. The alternatives below are for teams that want a dedicated analytics layer instead.


## 1. Thematic


Thematic is the customer intelligence layer for enterprise CX and insights teams that need to trust and defend their themes. It analyzes unstructured feedback from surveys, support tickets, reviews, and call transcripts. It builds the theme taxonomy from the bottom up, so the structure emerges from the feedback rather than from a template written two years ago.


The closest match to what Lumoa did well is score movement analysis. Thematic links each theme to its impact on NPS or CSAT, so a team reports "shipping delays cost 5 points, support improvements gained 4 points" instead of "NPS dropped 3 points." Every theme traces back to the raw comments underneath it, so a skeptical executive can interrogate the result rather than take it on faith. Analysts can add, delete, and merge themes directly.


The proof is in enterprise outcomes. Atom Bank unified 7 feedback channels across 3 product lines onto a single 1-to-100 Customer Goodwill Score. Those channels included in-product feedback, reviews, complaints, call center notes, and CRM data. Atom Bank then cut calls about unaccepted mortgage requests by 69%. One New Zealand, the connectivity provider[then trading as Vodafone New Zealand](https://media.one.nz/vodafone-new-zealand-to-become-one-new-zealand) , saved 60 hours every month and recorded a double-digit increase in transactional NPS after replacing the manual reading of hundreds of thousands of comments a year. Community Health System, a not-for-profit healthcare network in California's central San Joaquin Valley, delivered standardized reports from its annual employee engagement survey to all 250 departments in a single three-day sprint. Forrester's Total Economic Impact study put the three-year ROI at 543%, with payback in under six months.


The trade-off: Thematic is built for teams that care how themes are constructed and want to maintain them. A team that wants a fully hands-off feed with no human review may find that level of control more than they need.


## 2. Chattermill


Chattermill is the strongest fit for large, CX-led enterprises processing feedback at high volume across many languages. It unifies surveys, reviews, social, support conversations, and voice, then applies deep-learning aspect-based sentiment analysis to surface the drivers behind an experience.


For teams that valued Lumoa's multilingual analysis, this is the closest replacement at scale. Chattermill analyzes feedback in more than 100 languages, with automated translation and transcription built in, and connects with more than 65 feedback channels. It carries the security posture global brands require, including SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001:2022.


The trade-off is that Chattermill is analytics-oriented by design. It is strong at telling a team what is trending and weaker at driving the next action, so it will surface the theme without filing the ticket or closing the loop. Pricing is custom and quoted after a sales conversation.


## 3. Qualtrics XM Discover


Qualtrics XM Discover is the natural choice for enterprises already running Qualtrics that need omnichannel conversational analytics. Formerly Clarabridge, it analyzes feedback across cases, chat, voice transcriptions, email, surveys, and third-party reviews, using natural language understanding to extract intent, emotion, effort, and sentiment.


Where it wins is contact-center-grade analysis at scale inside an established Qualtrics program. Where it loses is cost and complexity. XM Discover is reported to run as a $300,000 to $400,000 per year add-on, on top of a base platform starting around $25,000 per year. Taxonomy setup in Designer requires specialists and ongoing maintenance. Teams moving off a lightweight tool like Lumoa usually find this a significant step up in both cost and administrative overhead.


## 4. Medallia


Medallia suits organizations that want text analytics sitting inside a broad experience management suite rather than as a separate layer. Its in-platform text analytics parses verbatims at the phrase level, sorts them into topic hierarchies, and applies sentiment.


It also offers impact scoring that shows how topics move NPS up or down, which is the capability most directly comparable to what Lumoa was known for. The breadth is real, covering collection, analysis, and program management in one place.


The trade-off is weight. Setup and ongoing taxonomy management can require meaningful internal resources, and teams that came to Lumoa specifically to avoid a heavy configuration project should scope that carefully before committing.


## 5. Kapiche


Kapiche fits mid-market and enterprise insights teams that want fast topic discovery on survey and conversation feedback. It applies natural language processing with no manual code-frame setup, then layers on sentiment, topic clustering, taxonomy classification, and predictive signals such as churn and escalation forecasting.


The appeal is speed to a first result without building a code frame, which is the profile many Lumoa users were buying for. The trade-off is scale. Kapiche prices per project, with row limits running from 50,000 to 250,000 by tier. Its integration ecosystem is narrower than the enterprise incumbents, and some users report slowness on very large datasets. Bronze starts at $1,060 per month, with higher tiers quoted by sales.


## How to choose between them


The right pick depends on what breaks first for your team. Ask these questions during a demo:


- **Can we see and edit the theme taxonomy?** A good answer shows you the themes, lets you merge or split them, and traces each back to the underlying comments. A vague answer means a black box.
- **Can it tell us which themes moved the score, not just which are most common?** Ask for a score change breakdown across two periods. Frequency without impact sends teams to fix the loudest complaint rather than the costliest one.
- **How long until the first reliable report?** Hours or days is strong. Weeks of model training or code-frame setup signals a heavier lift than a Lumoa replacement usually warrants.
- **Will it sit on our existing stack?** Confirm it works alongside your survey platform, warehouse, and BI tool without a rip-and-replace.
- **What does it cost at our volume?** Get pricing tied to your feedback volume and channel count, including setup and professional services fees.
