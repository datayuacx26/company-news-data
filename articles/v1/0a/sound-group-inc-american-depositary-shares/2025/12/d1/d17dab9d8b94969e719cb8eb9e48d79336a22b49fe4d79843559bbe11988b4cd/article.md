# Sound Group’s LIZHI Named Among “China’s Top 100 Internet Enterprises with Comprehensive Strength 2025” List for Seventh Consecutive Year

## Sound Group’s LIZHI Named Among “China’s Top 100 Internet Enterprises with Comprehensive Strength 2025” List for Seventh Consecutive Year


December 30, 2025


[PDF Version](https://ir.soundgroupinc.com/node/8966/pdf)


December 30, 2025 -- Sound Group Inc. (“Sound Group” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: SOGP), a global AI-powered audio company, today announced that its business brand LIZHI has been named to the “China’s Top 100 Internet Enterprises with Comprehensive Strength (2025)” list for the seventh consecutive year. LIZHI was also invited to attend the launch event of the China Internet Enterprises Comprehensive Strength Index (2025), hosted by the Internet Society of China (“ISC”).


The annual ranking is released by the ISC based on authoritative data and a comprehensive evaluation framework. Assessment criteria cover multiple dimensions, including enterprise scale, research and development investment, social responsibility, and international competitiveness. The list is widely regarded as a key benchmark for measuring the comprehensive strength and long-term value of China’s internet enterprises.


Over the past year, LIZHI has continued to enhance its platform capabilities across audio content creation, audio interaction, and ecosystem development, with a focus on improving user experience and supporting content creators in expanding their creative possibilities. Through sustained investment and innovation in audio interaction, content ecosystem, and voice AI technologies, LIZHI has once again earned recognition from industry experts, governments, and research institutions. Its seventh consecutive inclusion underscores its growing influence within the broader internet and digital culture ecosystem.


Looking ahead, Sound Group remains focused on user needs and technology-driven innovation, supporting sustainable business development through a diversified product portfolio and global strategy.
