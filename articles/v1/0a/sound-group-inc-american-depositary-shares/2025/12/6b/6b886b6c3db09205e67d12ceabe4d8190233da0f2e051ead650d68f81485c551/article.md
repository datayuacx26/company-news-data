# Sound Group’s LIZHI Named to “Top 30 Cultural Enterprises in Guangzhou 2025,” Showcasing Innovation in Voice Culture

## Sound Group’s LIZHI Named to “Top 30 Cultural Enterprises in Guangzhou 2025,” Showcasing Innovation in Voice Culture


December 23, 2025


[PDF Version](https://ir.soundgroupinc.com/node/8971/pdf)


December 23, 2025 **–** Sound Group Inc. (“Sound Group” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: SOGP), a global AI-powered audio company, today announced that its business brand LIZHI has been officially selected for the “Top 30 Cultural Enterprises in Guangzhou 2025” list. The list, jointly released by Nanfang Daily, the Guangzhou Cultural and Creative Industry Association, and the Guangzhou Academy of Social Sciences, honors organizations that make outstanding contributions to Guangzhou’s cultural development. LIZHI has also been invited to participate in the opening ceremony of the 2025 Guangzhou Cultural Industry Fair.


The “Top 30 Cultural Enterprises in Guangzhou” list is compiled through multiple rounds ofindustry recommendations, compliance reviews, and expert evaluations. Companies are assessed on scale, innovation, social impact, and brand influence. As the city’s cultural sector continues to grow in both scale and impact, the selected enterprises have become key engines to creativity and economic growth.


As Sound Group’s core business brand, LIZHI has once again earned its place on the list through continued investment and progress in audio content innovation, technology R&D, and community ecosystem building. This recognition underscores LIZHI’s strength in the digital cultural sector and highlights Sound Group’s ability to integrate culture and technology to create influential voice-driven brands.


Since its founding, LIZHI has remained committed to AI-powered innovation, continually strengthening its content ecosystem, expanding its diverse audio products and content offerings, empowering creators to achieve sustainable growth, and advancing innovation in voice culture.


Being named among the “Top 30 Cultural Enterprises in Guangzhou 2025” shines a spotlight on LIZHI’s achievements in audio content innovation and contributions to the cultural sector. Looking ahead, Sound Group will continue to drive technological innovation, expand its global presence, support its creator community, and deliver richer, more engaging audio experiences to users worldwide.
