# Target’s Summer Goals Tour Brings Soccer-Inspired Fun to Families

Target is bringing the excitement surrounding this summer’s global tournament into something families can experience together. The[Summer Goals Tour](https://www.target.com/c/summer-goals-2026-tour/-/N-t7tv0) , a series of high-energy, soccer-inspired events Target is hosting across the country, features interactive challenges, complimentary snacks, drinks and exclusive finds. The experience gives families a fun, playful way to be part of the tournament while showcasing the one-of-a-kind experiences, products and brands guests love from Target.

### Center Circle


As soon as guests hit the Target parking lot, they’re immersed in the excitement of game day on the pitch. Guests will receive a free Goal Getter giveaway bag to kick things off, followed by a walk through the Players Tunnel with the sound of a cheering crowd all around before arriving at the Center Circle, a social hub to gather, meet up and snap photos.


### Play Pitch


The action continues at the Play Pitch, where guests can take part in the fast-paced Target Practice challenge. Each round is 60 seconds, with goals tracked in real time and names lighting up the scoreboard.


### Playmaker Booths


There’s plenty more happening across the experience. Playmaker Booths bring games, samples and surprises from favorite brands like **Chobani** , **Dove** , **Liquid I.V.** and **LEGO** . The Target Playmaker booth wraps things up with a reward and a reason to keep the fun going inside the store.


And once inside, guests can find even more ways to bring the celebration home. From repping favorite teams with the[Boys’ United States Soccer Sweatshirt](https://www.target.com/p/boys-39-united-states-soccer-federation-front-and-back-graphic-hooded-pullover-sweatshirts-red/-/A-95125464?preselect=95050454) and[Women’s Mexico Floral Jersey T-shirt](https://www.target.com/p/women-s-mexico-floral-short-sleeve-graphic-jersey-t-shirt-green/-/A-95101696?preselect=95090448) to game-day snacks like[Lay’s Argentinian-Style Asado Potato Chips](https://www.target.com/p/lay-39-s-argentinian-style-asado-potato-chips-7-75oz/-/A-95022218) , essentials from[Onside](https://www.target.com/b/onside/-/N-q643leoj46m) , and fun finds like the[FIFA World Cup 2026 Blackout Soccer Ball](https://www.target.com/p/fifa-wc26-blackout-tournament-size-5-soccer-ball/-/A-94975482#lnk=sametab) and[LEGO FIFA World Cup Trophy](https://www.target.com/p/lego-editions-fifa-world-cup-official-trophy-43020/-/A-94756855) , there’s something for every kind of fan to discover.


By the end of the day, guests will have felt the energy of the tournament and been part of it, right at Target.
