# Target’s Halloween Assortment Brings Together Style, Fandom and Family Fun at Incredible Value

Whether transforming their homes with richly layered decor, dressing up in Target-exclusive **K-Pop Demon Hunters** costumes or filling their candy bowl with festive favorites, guests will discover an assortment that screams style at Target this Halloween. It’s another example of Target’s merchandising authority in action, bringing the season's biggest trends and culturally relevant moments together, all at an incredible value.


As[guests continue to shop earlier for Halloween](https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/nrf-consumer-survey-finds-halloween-spending-to-reach-record-13-1-billion) , they’ll find even more to love throughout this year’s assortment, with nearly 70% of the collection new and about 60% available only at Target. Just as exciting are the prices, with 60% of decor under $10 and half of all costumes and accessories under $25.

“Our guests are looking for more than simple Halloween decorations or costumes. They want the latest trends, meaningful ways to celebrate with family and friends, and products that reflect the fandoms they’re excited about,” said **Cara Sylvester** , executive vice president and chief merchandising officer, Target. “This year’s assortment does just that, with elevated decor, fan-favorite characters, seasonal treats and creative family activities that make it easy to create memorable Halloween moments without compromising on style or value.”
