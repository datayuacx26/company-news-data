# KATSEYE’s EP “WILD” at Target

Target is celebrating the launch of KATSEYE's new EP, "WILD," with exclusive music releases and an exclusive Kuromi™ x KATSEYE capsule assortment that brings together two fan favorites. Available beginning Aug. 14, the lineup showcases Target's merchandising authority with exclusive products across entertainment, apparel and even food and beverage, creating a curated assortment inspired by KATSEYE, Kuromi and the EYEKONS fan community. Together, the launch turns a major pop culture moment into a can't-miss shopping experience.


## Target-exclusive music assortment


Target will offer exclusive versions of “WILD,” including a Target-exclusive vinyl, CD and individual member CDs.


- [“WILD,” Wild Heart Ver. Designed exclusively for Target, the Kuromi™ x KATSEYE collection brings together Kuromi, the iconic Sanrio® character, and KATSEYE’s bold style through an assortment of exclusive apparel, collectibles, snacks and beverages.

Guests can show off their fandom with exclusive apparel and accessories featuring Kuromi and KATSEYE, with prices starting at $10. Items include:


- Graphic tee featuring Kuromi™ alongside KATSEYE

- Kuromi™ beanie
- Purple clear stadium bag
