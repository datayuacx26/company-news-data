# Press Media FAQs | Target Corporation

### How do I secure an interview or shoot footage at my local Target store?


If you are interested in shooting footage inside a Target store, select “[Find a Store](http://www.target.com/store-locator/find-stores) ” on[Target.com](http://target.com/) to locate and contact your nearest store. Ask to speak with the Leader on Duty to request a visit or interview.


Please note we cannot accommodate all requests.


### Where do I find corporate information like the number of stores and executive bios?


If you want to access basic corporate information about Target, including number of stores and locations, visit[About Target](https://corporate.target.com/about) . Executive officer bios and headshots are available for download from our[Leadership](https://corporate.target.com/about/purpose-history/leadership) page.


### Where can I download the bullseye logo?


We make logo files available for download in our[press multimedia library](https://corporate.target.com/media/collection/b-roll-and-press-materials/target-logos) . Please contact press@target.com with any other requests.


### Where do I find news on sales results and other financial information?


Visit our[Investors](https://corporate.target.com/investors) site for Target’s latest financial news. Comments regarding the company's sales results are available in a pre-recorded telephone message that may be accessed by calling (612) 761-6500.


### What is Target’s return or coupon policy?


Target’s[return policy](https://help.target.com/help/subcategoryarticle?childcat=Returns&parentcat=Returns+%26+Exchanges&searchQuery=search+help) and[coupon policy](https://help.target.com/help/subcategoryarticle?childcat=Coupons+%26+offers&parentcat=Promotions+%26+Coupons&searchQuery=search+help) can be found on Target.com.


### What is Target’s privacy policy?


We value the trust our guests place in us when they shop at Target, and we take our responsibility to protect that trust very seriously. A comprehensive privacy policy can be found on[Target.com](https://www.target.com/c/target-privacy-policy/-/N-4sr7p) , where we share openly how we gather guest information, what we do with it and how we secure it.


### I have a product I’d like to appear in Target stores. Who do I contact?


If you are interested in selling a product at a Target store, call our Target Vendor Hotline at (612) 696-7500.


### I’m a guest and have a question for Target. Who do I contact?


Frequently asked questions can be found on the[Target Help](http://help.target.com/help) page. Guest inquiries should be directed to 1-800-440-0680.
