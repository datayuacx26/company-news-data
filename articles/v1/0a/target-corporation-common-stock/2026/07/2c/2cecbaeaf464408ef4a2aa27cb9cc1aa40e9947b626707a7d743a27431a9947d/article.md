# Back-to-School 2026: Target Partners with Local Organizations to Prepare Students for the School Year

## What students receive


Each participating student receives:


- A $100 Target gift card for back-to-school essentials
- A Target water bottle
- Snacks
- A Champion backpack
- Yoobi school supplies


## Community partnerships


Target is working alongside local nonprofit organizations in cities nationwide to connect students and families with back-to-school resources.


## Participating communities


Back-to-school shopping events will take place throughout July and August in:


- Atlanta - July 14
- Honolulu – July 22
- Phoenix – July 23
- Vegas – July 27

- Houston – July 29
- Austin – Aug. 3

- Baltimore – Aug. 19
- New York City – Aug. 25


## Supporting students and communities


These shopping events are one example of how Target invests in communities through philanthropy, team member volunteerism and trusted nonprofit partnerships, supporting the company’s annual goal of one million volunteer hours and building on our long-standing commitment to giving 5% of its profits to communities in products, cash and through the Target Foundation.*
