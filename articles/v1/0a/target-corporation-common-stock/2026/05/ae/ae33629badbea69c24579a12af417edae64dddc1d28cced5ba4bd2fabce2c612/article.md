# Designed with Guests in Mind — Target’s Reimagined Shopping Cart

Target’s commitment to style and design means that as our guests needs evolve we use our creativity and vision to reimagine how every aspect of our brand can be more easy, inspiring and friendly. From the products on our shelves to the tech they use to simplify their experience — thoughtful design plays an important role in making shopping easier.


One example is the shopping cart, one of the most-used touchpoints in our stores and an everyday essential that continues to evolve alongside guest needs. That’s why we knew a redesigned cart had to be three things: effortlessly functional, made to last and designed with a lower environmental footprint in mind.


> No other cart on the market meets all these aspirations. We’re leading with design and elevating the guest experience down to the last detail.
>
>
> Sarah Deuth
>
>
> Vice President of Store Design, Target


### Made with you in mind


Starting in 2020, the store design team approached the redesign with busy families top of mind — engaging directly with guests to understand what would make their shopping experience easier. That input helped shape a cart designed for real-life use, with features like:


- Smoother maneuverability and ergonomic handles
- A deeper, more comfortable and secure child seat
- Increased basket space for bulk goods
- Larger cupholders for anything from snacks to Starbucks to sippy cups


These features aren’t just functional. They reflect Target’s focus on creating experiences that feel easy, inspiring and friendly and designed for everyday life. The result is a cart shaped by years of guest feedback and testing —designed to improve one of the most-used experiences in our stores.


### The Series 3 All Plastic Cart (APC)


Target’s new Series 3 All Plastic Cart.


A deeper child seat for comfier sidekick seating, and larger cup holders for drinks and tasty treats.


The wider base makes more room for bulk shopping, without impacting ease of steering.


### Sustainability meets dependability


The Series 3 APC is designed with sustainability in mind. Its materials are fully recyclable at the end of their lifespan, helping reduce waste over time. The cart is also built for durability. Its modular design allows for components to be replaced individually, extending its lifespan and reducing the need to replace the full cart.


### An iconic history


Our carts are part of our legacy, one of the most recognizable touchpoints of our brand. The history of our cart shows decades of Target’s commitment to design-forward, guest-centered thinking.


Now, after years of design, testing and refinement, we’re rolling out the Series 3 APC to select stores over time, with some locations already offering the new design, as we continue to evolve the in-store experience. Across every cart design in our fleet, our team remains focused on elevating the guest experience and making every trip to Target easy, inspiring and friendly.


### The Target Cart Through Time

-


**1978:** In 1978, Target stores began replacing all-metal carts with red plastic shopping carts, which were less expensive, easier to keep clean, and lighter weight. The red carts reinforced Target’s growing brand identity.


-


**1995:** In the 1990s, Target further refined the shopping cart design for functionality. This cart promoted the Target Guest Card, our in-store credit card which debuted in 1995.


-


**2006:** In 2006, Target’s shopping cart redesign reflected the company’s broader “Design for All” philosophy, emphasizing ergonomics, brand identity and guest convenience. An additional bonus — the cart was designed to be recyclable.


-


**2014:** In 2014, Target introduced the hybrid cart, before redesigning the All Plastic Carts in stores today.


-


**Today:** The latest redesign of the shopping cart is Target’s most ergonomic and sleek design yet.
