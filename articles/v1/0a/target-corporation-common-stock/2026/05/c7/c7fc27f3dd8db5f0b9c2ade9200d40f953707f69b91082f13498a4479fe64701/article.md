# Local Target Teams Deliver Appreciation to Teachers in Communities Nationwide

Every day, teachers inspire curiosity, confidence and possibility for millions of students. They show up early, stay late and go the extra mile — often with little recognition — to help shape the next generation. This Teacher Appreciation Week, Target is proud to say thank you in meaningful ways that reflect just how much educators matter to us as part of our ongoing commitment to supporting communities and creating growth and opportunity.


### From our stores to their schools: supporting teachers locally


Throughout the week, we’ve been extending our gratitude beyond our stores and into neighborhoods across the country. In select communities, local Target store and supply chain teams personally delivered appreciation packages to nearby schools, filled with thoughtful essentials and “must-haves” to brighten teachers’ days. It’s a small but heartfelt way our teams are showing up locally to recognize the educators who make an impact for students and families every day.


“Our team members are proud to be part of the Dallas community, and we see the incredible impact local educators have on students, families and neighborhoods every day,” says **Adela Acosta** , senior administrative assistant at Target’s Dallas Sortation Center. “At Target, recognizing educators is part of our broader commitment to supporting communities and creating growth and opportunity for all. In recognition of Teacher Appreciation Week, our team hand-selected supplies, classroom essentials and treats for the staff at La Villita Elementary School to honor the difference they make in students’ lives every day.”


Alongside these local efforts, Target is also making it easier for teachers nationwide to save while shopping for the things they need and love. From May 3-9, 2026, verified teachers who are members of our free Target Circle loyalty program can receive 10% off one storewide purchase.* Whether they’re stocking up on classroom supplies, refreshing their summer wardrobe or treating themselves to a little self‑care, the Teacher Appreciation Week discount is designed to support educators both inside and outside the classroom.


That support doesn’t stop with one week. Throughout the year, verified teachers can take advantage of ongoing benefits, including more than 50% off an annual Target Circle 360 membership** — featuring free, fast shipping, same‑day delivery, monthly extras and early access to select sales and collaborations. Target also offers teacher‑focused deals during key shopping moments, including back‑to‑school, with a simplified verification process to make accessing these benefits easier than ever.


Supporting teachers is part of our broader commitment to strengthen communities and create growth and opportunity for all.
