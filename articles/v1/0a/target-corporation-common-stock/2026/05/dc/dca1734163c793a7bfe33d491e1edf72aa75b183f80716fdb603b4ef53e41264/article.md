# Meet Grant McGee, Target’s New Chief Legal and Compliance Officer

As Target’s new chapter of growth continues to take shape, we’re excited to welcome a new member of our leadership team. Grant McGee is joining Target on May 31 as executive vice president and chief legal and compliance officer.


Grant will lead our legal, compliance & risk teams, as well as our government affairs function. Together, these teams shape the policies and standards that help us accelerate progress on our strategy. Their counsel strengthens our business while upholding the responsibilities we have to our team, guests and partners.


Grant comes to the team with 20 years of broad-based legal expertise and experience in large, consumer-facing global companies. His fresh perspective and sharp focus on consumers are a perfect fit for our ambition to be the[most delightful experience in retail](https://corporate.target.com/about/purpose-history/our-corporate-strategy) .


Learn from Grant why he’s joining Target, what he’s looking forward to, and what’s always in his Target cart.


Q:


What excites you about this new challenge at Target?


A:


The pace of change in retail is incredibly high, and products and experiences are evolving to meet consumers where, when and how they want to shop. This creates a significant opportunity to leverage Target’s unique strengths and footprint to meet the moment and deliver the most delightful experience in retail. I’m confident we can achieve this and drive sustainable growth.


Q:


What made you choose Target? What inspired you about this role?


A:


It’s twofold. Personally, Target is a brand that’s been part of my life at every stage — from furnishing my first apartment to everyday essentials for my daughter and gifts and decorations for every holiday. Professionally, this is a transformative moment in retail, and I see tremendous opportunity for Target to deliver on its strategy and create an exceptional experience for our guests.


Q:


What is important to you as you think about legal affairs and the role it plays for Target?


A:


Legal, compliance and government affairs are essential in today’s dynamic operating environment. Whether we’re enabling AI and emerging technologies, adapting to evolving global regulations, or partnering with our team, guests and partners to build lasting value — our team plays a critical role in helping Target deliver its strategy at speed.


Q:


What's always in your Target cart?


A:


Goodfellow & Co. T-shirts (I have a drawer full at home), and Cat & Jack for my daughter — I love shopping for the latest styles as she outgrows each size.
