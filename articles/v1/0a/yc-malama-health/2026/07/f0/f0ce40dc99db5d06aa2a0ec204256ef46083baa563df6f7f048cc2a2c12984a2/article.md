# Malama Expands Doula-Led Tech-Enabled Care Services in Colorado to Expand Equitable Maternal Health Access

**DENVER, CO —** *Malama Medical Group* , a physician-owned corporation focused on improving maternal health outcomes, announced today the launch of Malama’s Extra Support program in Colorado, expanding access to the wraparound pregnancy and postpartum support services for Health First Colorado members statewide. The program, offered at no cost to Health First Colorado members, connects pregnant individuals to culturally congruent, doula-led care navigation and critical health-related social supports including nutritional counseling, behavioral health and postpartum support.


*“Malama’s mission is to make whole-person care the standard for every mom and baby,” said Priyanka Wali, Medical Director of Malama Medical Group. “Healthcare access, quality and affordability shouldn’t depend on who or what you know and through Malama’s programs, we’re helping ensure that every family, regardless of zip code or income, has access to the care, connection, and resources needed to thrive during and after pregnancy.”*


### **Program Highlights**


-


**Comprehensive support:** Doula-led care navigation and individualized assessments for pregnant and postpartum individuals


-


**Extended postpartum care:** Support for up to 12 months after birth


-


**Social and medical care coordination:** Integration of medical, nutritional, behavioral, and community-based resources


-


**Simple digital enrollment:** Patients can sign up in minutes and get matched to free services


-


**Data and quality insights:** Malama’s platform enables seamless coordination and outcomes tracking for providers and health plans

Malama’s Extra Support program is a Medicaid-funded initiative that supports improved maternal and infant health outcomes through coordinated, community-based care. By partnering with local clinics, doulas, and community organizations, Malama ensures that care is accessible, personalized and rooted in trust. Malama’s Extra Support program is live in California, Texas and Colorado.
