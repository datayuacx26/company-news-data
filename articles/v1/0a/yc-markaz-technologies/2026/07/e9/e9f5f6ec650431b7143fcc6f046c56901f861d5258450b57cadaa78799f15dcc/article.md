# China to Pakistan Dropshipping: How It Works

Dropshipping from China means selling a product before you own it. A customer orders from you, you place that order with the source, and the product travels to the customer without ever passing through your hands. No stock, no warehouse, no capital tied up in inventory that might not sell.


The model is simple. What makes it work or fail in Pakistan is almost entirely about one variable: **time** . This guide is about handling that variable honestly.


## The mechanics, plainly


A China dropshipping cycle has four steps:


- You list a product — its photos, description and your price — wherever you sell: WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or your own website.
- A customer places an order with you and you take their address.
- You place that order on[Markaz China](https://www.markaz.app/china) with the customer's address as the delivery address.
- The product ships from China, clears customs, and is delivered to your customer. You keep the difference between your price and the source price.


You never buy stock in advance and you never handle the parcel. Your job is finding the customer and setting the price.


## The 10–17 day window, and why it is the whole game


A China-sourced order typically takes 10 to 17 days door-to-door, with freight and customs handled along the way. Compared with arranging your own import that is fast. Compared with a local order that arrives in three to five days, it is slow — and your customer is comparing it with the local order, not with freight forwarding.


This is where most China dropshipping in Pakistan goes wrong. The seller, worried about losing the sale, implies the parcel will arrive "in a few days". Two weeks later they have an angry customer, a refused delivery, and a review that costs them ten future sales. The delivery window is not the problem. Hiding it is.


What works instead:


- **State the window in the listing itself** , not in a reply when asked. "Imported on order — delivered in 10 to 17 days" filters out impatient buyers before they become unhappy ones.
- **Explain why.** Pakistani customers accept a wait when they understand it buys them a price or a product they cannot get locally. "It comes directly from the factory, which is why it costs what it costs" is a reason, not an excuse.
- **Message once in the middle.** A short "your order is on its way, expected next week" around day seven prevents the anxious cancellation that otherwise arrives around day ten.


## What to sell, and what not to


Products that survive a two-week wait have things in common: they are wanted rather than needed, they are not perishable, they are not required by a specific date, and they are hard to buy locally at the same price. Fashion accessories, gadgets, home items and novelty products fit that profile.


Products that punish you: anything for an imminent occasion, anything a customer could buy from a nearby shop this afternoon, and anything where sizing is critical enough that a return is likely. The[China category tree](https://www.markaz.app/china/categories) is worth browsing with that filter in mind — the catalogue runs to roughly 177,000 products across 38 categories, and the depth sits in accessories, clothing, footwear and household goods.


## Pricing for a longer delivery


Your price has to carry more than your margin. It carries the product cost, the delivery, and the patience you are asking the customer for. Underpricing a China-sourced product is a double mistake: you earn less *and* you signal that the item is ordinary, which makes the wait feel unreasonable.


Work out your full landed cost first, then set your margin on top of it — our guide to[pricing and margin for resellers](https://www.markaz.app/blog-post/how-to-price-products-reseller-margin) sets out the arithmetic. And because a longer delivery raises the odds of a refused parcel, the habits in[reducing COD returns](https://www.markaz.app/blog-post/reduce-cod-returns-pakistan) matter more here than anywhere else in your catalogue.


## China dropshipping versus local dropshipping


Most successful Pakistani resellers run both. Local products from[Markaz Shop](https://www.markaz.app/shop) arrive in days and carry the volume of your business. China products arrive in weeks and carry the margin and the novelty — the things nobody down the road is selling.


A sensible starting split is a catalogue mostly of local products, with a handful of China items that give your page something distinctive. As you learn which China products your audience actually waits for, that share can grow.


## Getting started without risking anything


Order one China product to your own address first. You will learn the real timeline, see the packaging your customer will see, and be able to describe the product honestly because you have held it. That single order teaches you more than any guide, including this one.


Then list it, state the delivery window plainly, and take your first order. Cash on delivery and the 7-day return window mean your customer's risk is low, which is precisely what makes them willing to wait.
