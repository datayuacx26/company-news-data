# How to Price Products: Reseller Margin Guide

Ask a struggling reseller what went wrong and the answer is rarely "nobody wanted the product." It's almost always pricing — margins so thin that one price change or one returned parcel wiped out a week of profit. Pricing is a formula, not a guess. Here it is.


## The door-price formula


What your customer actually pays at the door is:


**Product price + delivery + COD fee + your profit = door price**


In practice on Markaz, most COD orders carry around PKR 165 delivery plus a PKR 50 cash-handling fee (prepaid orders skip the 50). If a product costs PKR 800 wholesale and you want PKR 250 profit, your customer pays roughly PKR 1,265 at the door. Quote THAT number when a customer asks "total kitna?" — surprises at the door are the number-one cause of refused parcels.


## Margin rules that keep you safe


- **Never price below your all-in cost.** Obvious, yet it happens constantly when resellers forget delivery and COD fees.
- **Keep at least 15% or PKR 150 margin** — wholesale prices move, and a thin margin can silently turn negative between the day you list and the day you sell. Always re-check the live Markaz price before placing an order.
- **Use charm prices.** PKR 999 outsells PKR 1,000; PKR 1,499 outsells PKR 1,520. Round your final price down to a 9-ending number and take the margin difference from a slightly higher base.
- **Price the category, not the product.** A phone accessory competes with Daraz listings; an unstitched suit competes with the local bazaar. Check what your customer sees elsewhere before setting yours.
