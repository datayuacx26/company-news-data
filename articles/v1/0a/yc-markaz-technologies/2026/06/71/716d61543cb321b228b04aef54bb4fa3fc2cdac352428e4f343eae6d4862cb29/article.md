## The four stages of your delivery


Understanding the journey helps set expectations. Your parcel passes through four stages between the day you order and the day it arrives:


1. **Supplier pickup in China** — the factory or supplier prepares your item and hands it to Markaz's logistics network.
2. **International freight** — your parcel is consolidated with others and shipped from China toward Pakistan. This leg usually accounts for the largest share of the total time.
3. **Customs clearance** — Markaz clears your parcel through Pakistani customs. You do not file paperwork or pay duties separately.
4. **Last-mile delivery** — a local courier delivers the parcel to your address, where you pay cash on delivery.

## Why a China order takes longer than a local one


It helps to understand why the window is days rather than hours. A local parcel from a Pakistani seller only has to travel within the country, so it skips two of the four stages entirely. A China order, by contrast, must be picked up from the supplier, consolidated and flown or shipped across an international border, and then cleared through Pakistani customs before a local courier can even begin the last-mile leg. Each of those added stages is genuine value — you are reaching factory-direct prices that local stock simply cannot match — but it is also why the realistic end-to-end window is about 10–17 days rather than 3–5. You are trading a couple of weeks of patience for a meaningfully lower price and far wider selection.

## Mistakes that slow your order down


- **An incomplete or wrong address.** If the last-mile courier cannot find you, delivery is rescheduled — double-check your address before confirming.

- **Ordering bulky items when you needed speed.** If timing matters, lean toward lighter products or buy locally instead.
- **Expecting local-order speed.** A China order is an international shipment; planning for the full 10–17-day window keeps the experience stress-free.
