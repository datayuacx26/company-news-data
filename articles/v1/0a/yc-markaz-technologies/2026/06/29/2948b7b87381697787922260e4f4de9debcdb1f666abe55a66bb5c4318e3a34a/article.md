# Affiliate Marketing vs Reselling in Pakistan: Which to Start?

Affiliate marketing and reselling are both earn-from-home programs in Pakistan, but they differ in control and effort. With affiliate marketing you share a referral link and earn a reward when someone buys, while with reselling you choose products, set your own price, and manage the customer. Affiliate is the simplest entry point; reselling gives you more control over your margin.


## How affiliate marketing works


As an affiliate you promote products using a unique link and earn for sales made through it. You never handle stock, pricing or delivery. The[Markaz affiliate program](https://www.markaz.app/affiliate) is free to join, needs no website and no investment, which makes it ideal for complete beginners.


## How reselling works


As a reseller you pick products at factory or best prices, add your own profit margin, and sell to your customers. The platform still handles sourcing, payments via cash on delivery in PKR, and delivery, so you avoid inventory and shipping headaches. Start with[the Markaz reseller program](https://www.markaz.app/reseller-pk) and browse stock in[the shop](https://www.markaz.app/shop) .


## Side-by-side: which fits you?


- **Upfront cost:** Affiliate is fully free to join. Reselling also needs no inventory investment since you only order once a customer buys.
- **Control over price:** Resellers set their own margin. Affiliates earn a fixed reward per sale.
- **Customer relationship:** Resellers own the customer chat and repeat business. Affiliates simply refer.
- **Effort:** Affiliate marketing is lighter; reselling involves managing orders and customers.
- **Scalability:** Reselling can grow into a repeat-customer business, while affiliate income scales with how much you share.


Neither model requires you to hold inventory or arrange delivery yourself, and both run on cash on delivery in PKR across major cities. The real difference is how hands-on you want to be. If you prefer a light touch, stay with affiliate links. If you enjoy talking to customers and want to own your margin, reselling rewards that effort.
