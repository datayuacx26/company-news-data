# Dropshipping Without Shopify in Pakistan (WhatsApp & TikTok)

You do not need Shopify to start dropshipping in Pakistan. You can take orders directly through WhatsApp, Facebook and TikTok, then place each order on a dropshipping platform that sources the product, accepts cash on delivery in PKR, and ships to your customer. This avoids monthly store fees and works perfectly with how Pakistani buyers already shop.


## Why skip Shopify in Pakistan?


Shopify is built around card payments and a hosted online store, which adds monthly costs and friction in a market that runs on cash on delivery and social selling. Most Pakistani buyers discover and order products inside chat apps. Selling where they already are removes the need for a separate website.


The[Markaz dropshipping portal](https://www.markaz.app/dropshipping-portal) lets you list and order products without building any store, so you keep your costs low and your setup simple.


There is also a trust advantage. Pakistani buyers are comfortable messaging a real person, asking questions and paying cash when the parcel arrives. A chat conversation often converts better than a cold website checkout, because the buyer feels they are dealing with someone accountable rather than an anonymous store.


## The social-selling workflow


- **WhatsApp:** Post products on Status and in groups, then take orders through chat with cash on delivery.
- **Facebook:** Use a page or Marketplace to reach local buyers across Karachi, Lahore, Faisalabad and Islamabad.
- **TikTok:** Short product videos and demos drive impulse orders, especially for trending items.
- **Instagram:** Reels and a simple catalogue in your bio help you reach buyers who shop visually.


The advantage of this approach is that each channel is free to start and already full of Pakistani buyers. You are not paying a monthly fee to host a store that nobody has discovered yet. Instead, you go to where attention already exists and meet buyers in the apps they open every day.
