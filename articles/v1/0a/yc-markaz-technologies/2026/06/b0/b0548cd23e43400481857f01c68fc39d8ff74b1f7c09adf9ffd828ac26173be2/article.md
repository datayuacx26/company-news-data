## What is affiliate marketing?


Affiliate marketing is a simple earn-from-home model where you promote products you like and earn for every sale that comes through your personal link. You do not buy, store or ship anything. The marketplace handles the products, payments and delivery, and you focus only on sharing.


In Pakistan this works especially well because many people already shop with cash on delivery in PKR and trust recommendations from friends and family. That word-of-mouth habit is exactly what an affiliate link turns into income. You are simply being paid for the recommendations you would probably make for free anyway.


## Why affiliate marketing suits Pakistan


Three things make this model a strong fit locally. First, cash on delivery removes the payment friction that holds many online buyers back. Second, social platforms like WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok already have huge, active Pakistani audiences, so your reach is built in. Third, trusted Pakistani sellers and factory or best prices mean the products you promote are both credible and affordable, which makes them easier to recommend honestly.
