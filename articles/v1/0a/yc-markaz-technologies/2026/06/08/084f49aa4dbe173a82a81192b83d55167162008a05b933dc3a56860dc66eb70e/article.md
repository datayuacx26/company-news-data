## What matters most for Pakistani sellers


Three things decide whether a reselling business works in Pakistan. **COD** comes first: most buyers won't pay upfront for something they haven't seen, so a platform that collects cash on your behalf removes the biggest barrier to a sale and protects you from non-payment. **Cash flow** comes second: fast payouts and a no-investment model let you reinvest profit into testing and ads quickly instead of waiting for collected cash to clear. **Operational load** comes third: the more a platform handles fulfilment, returns and payments, the more time you spend on the part that actually grows revenue — finding and marketing winning products. A platform built around fast fulfilment is attractive, but speed alone won't help if payouts are slow or COD coverage is thin, so weigh all three together.

## Questions to ask before you commit to any platform


Whether it's AR Dropshipping, Markaz or a newer entrant, get straight answers to these before you scale spending on it:


- **Which exact cities do you deliver to and collect COD in?** Confirm your target areas are covered, not just the big metros.
- **How long after a delivered order is my profit paid, and to which wallet?** Time it on a real order rather than trusting the stated figure.
- **Who handles returns, and within what window?** A platform that processes returns for you removes a recurring cost.
- **Can I set my own selling price?** If margins are fixed, your profit ceiling is fixed too.
- **Is stock availability live and accurate?** Listing items that are actually out of stock burns your ad budget and your buyers' trust.

- **Make sure the margin survives costs.** Your price must comfortably cover product cost, ad spend per sale and the occasional return, because thin margins vanish when a few parcels come back.
