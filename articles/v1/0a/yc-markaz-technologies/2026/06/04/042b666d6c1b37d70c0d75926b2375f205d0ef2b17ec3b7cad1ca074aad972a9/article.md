## What makes a product a good dropshipping winner


You don't need a viral gadget to make money. The most reliable dropshipping products in Pakistan share a few traits. Before you pick anything to sell, check that it ticks these boxes. Look for items that are:


- Low priced, so cash-on-delivery customers feel safe ordering
- Useful in daily life, which drives repeat orders and word of mouth
- Light and compact, keeping delivery costs and damage risk down
- Hard to find in local markets, so buyers can't easily compare prices
- Visual and photogenic, so they perform well on Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp
