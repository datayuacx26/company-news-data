## What "factory-direct" really means


You're buying closer to the source — skipping importers and mall markups — which is why China prices are so low. Normally a product passes through a factory, an exporter, an importer, a wholesaler and finally a retail shop, and each step adds a margin to cover that party's costs and profit. By the time it reaches a mall shelf, the price can be several times what the factory charged. Factory-direct collapses that chain: you order, and the product comes to you with far fewer hands — and far fewer markups — in between.


The hard part used to be the logistics. Buying from a Chinese factory yourself meant arranging international freight, clearing customs, and finding a way to pay across borders — the kind of thing only importers and businesses bothered with. Markaz takes all of that on for you, which is what makes factory-direct buying realistic for a single shopper ordering a single item.

## Why factory-direct is cheaper


The savings come from removing middle costs rather than from any one discount. Each of these is a margin you simply do not pay when you buy direct:


- **No importer or forwarder margin.** Buying direct from China skips the people who would normally buy in bulk and resell to local shops, each adding their own markup.
- **No mall markup.** There is no physical store rent, display stock or staff cost baked into the price you pay.
- **No card fees.** Cash on delivery means you pay in rupees on arrival, avoiding prepayment charges and the risk of paying before you have the goods.


Stacked together, these are why a factory-direct price can sit well below the same item in a local shop. You are paying for the product and its delivery, and very little else.

## Where the savings come from: a simple example


It helps to picture the chain a product travels through before it reaches a shop, because every stop on that journey is a price increase. A typical imported item moves like this: the factory makes it, an exporter ships it abroad, an importer brings it into the country, a wholesaler distributes it, and finally a retail shop sells it — with rent and staff costs on top. By the time it reaches the shelf, several margins have been stacked on the original factory price.


Factory-direct buying removes most of those stops. Instead of the product passing through an exporter, importer, wholesaler and shop, it goes from the supplier in China, through Markaz's freight and customs handling, straight to your door. You pay for the product and its delivery, and almost none of the stacked resale margins. That is the whole reason a factory-direct price can sit well below the same item in a mall — not a one-off discount, but a shorter chain.
