# How to Order from China to Pakistan with Cash on Delivery (2026 Guide)

Today you can shop China-direct prices and simply **pay cash on delivery** when the parcel reaches your door. There is no advance payment, no minimum order, and no customs paperwork to handle yourself. There is **no minimum order quantity** on Markaz China. You can buy a single item to test the quality and the delivery experience before committing to more. ## Step 3: Choose cash on delivery at checkout


At checkout, enter your delivery address and choose **cash on delivery (COD)** . You pay in Pakistani rupees when the order arrives at your door — no credit card, no debit card and no advance payment are required. This is the same familiar COD experience most Pakistanis already use for local online shopping, now extended to products coming straight from Chinese factories.


Double-check your phone number and address before you confirm. Accurate contact details help the last-mile courier reach you on the first attempt, which keeps your delivery on schedule.


## Step 4: Markaz handles freight and customs — one price, door to door


Once your order is placed, Markaz manages the entire cross-border journey for you. There are four stages, and you do not have to lift a finger for any of them:


1. **Supplier pickup in China** — the factory or supplier prepares your item and hands it to Markaz's logistics network.
2. **International freight** — your parcel is consolidated and shipped from China toward Pakistan.
3. **Customs clearance** — Markaz clears your parcel through Pakistani customs. You do not file paperwork or pay separate duties on arrival.
4. **Last-mile delivery** — a local courier delivers the parcel to your address, where you pay cash.


The most important point: because Markaz handles customs and freight, **the rupee price you see at checkout is the all-in price** . There are no surprise customs fees, brokerage charges or hidden costs waiting for you when the parcel lands. This single-price model is what makes ordering from China feel as simple as buying locally.

## Why cash on delivery changes everything for China shopping


Cash on delivery solves the problem that kept most Pakistanis away from China-direct shopping for years: trust. With traditional importing, you wired money abroad, hoped the goods matched the listing, and then faced unpredictable customs charges when the parcel arrived. Any one of those steps could go wrong, and you carried the risk at every stage.


COD flips that. You commit no money until the parcel is physically in front of you. The risk of paying for something that never shows up is removed, because payment and delivery happen at the same moment. Combined with an all-in rupee price that already includes freight and customs, this makes ordering from a Chinese factory feel no riskier than buying from a shop down the road. It is the single biggest reason China-direct shopping is now practical for ordinary households, not just bulk importers.
