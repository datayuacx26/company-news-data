## What actually makes online prices cheaper?


"Cheap" online shopping is less about chasing one magic discount and more about removing the hidden costs that sit between a factory and your hand. A product usually passes through several owners before it reaches a retail shelf, and every one of them adds a margin to cover their own costs and profit. The fewer hands in that chain, the lower the price you pay.


- **Buying direct from sellers** instead of through mall markups. A physical shop has to cover rent, electricity, staff and display stock, and all of that is baked into the price tag. An online seller carries far less of that overhead, so the same item lands cheaper.
- **Cash on delivery** — no card fees, and you only pay when the order arrives. You also avoid the prepayment risk of paying first and hoping the parcel turns up.
- **Factory-direct from China** for gadgets, fashion and home goods, without a freight forwarder or an importer taking a cut along the way. This is where the deepest savings usually sit.


When you stack these together — direct sourcing plus COD — you land close to the lowest possible price for most everyday products. None of it requires a special account, a credit card or any bulk buying. It is simply choosing the route with the fewest middle costs.

## How to read a listing so you don't overpay


The price you see is only part of the picture. A few quick checks on each listing help you judge whether it is genuinely the best value or just the cheapest-looking number:


- **Match the exact variant.** Colour, size and pack quantity all change the price. Make sure you are comparing the same variant across sellers, not a smaller pack against a larger one.
- **Check what is included.** Some listings bundle accessories or extras that justify a slightly higher price, while others are the bare item. The cheaper headline price is not always the better deal once you account for what you actually get.
- **Confirm the delivery window.** If two listings are close in price, the one that arrives when you need it is the better buy. Build the ~3–5 day local or ~10–17 day China-direct window into your decision.
- **Look at the product photos and details together.** Read the description against the images so you order exactly what you expect and avoid a return.


None of this takes long, and it is the difference between a price that looks cheap and a purchase that actually is.
