## How to evaluate any dropshipping platform in Pakistan


Whether you're looking at YourMart, Markaz or anything else, run the platform through the same questions. These are the factors that actually decide whether you make money, not the ones that look good on a landing page.


- **Catalogue size and quality.** More products means more chances to find a winner, but quality and reliable stock matter more than raw count. Can you see real photos, accurate prices and live availability before you list? A catalogue full of out-of-stock items is worse than a smaller, reliable one.
- **Cash on delivery (COD).** COD is the dominant and most-trusted payment method in Pakistan. If a platform doesn't collect COD for you, you carry the cash-flow and non-payment risk yourself — a serious problem for a new seller.
- **Payout speed.** How fast does your profit reach you after a delivered order? Slow payouts choke your ability to reinvest and scale, especially in the first few months when every rupee of profit goes back into testing.
- **What's done for you.** Sourcing, packing, shipping, returns and customer payments are real work. The more the platform handles, the less you risk and the faster you can run.
- **Cost to start and minimum order.** A true zero-investment start with no minimum order lets you test products without buying inventory you might not sell.
- **Margin control.** Can you set your own selling price? If the platform fixes your margin, your profit ceiling is fixed too.
- **Reach and tools.** Which cities are covered for delivery and COD? Are there extras like image search, a Shopify integration, or China sourcing that widen what you can sell?


Write these down, then score each platform out of the seven. The one that wins on more of the points that matter to *your* niche is the one to build on. Don't let a single flashy feature distract you from a platform that's weak on payouts or COD coverage.

## What matters most for Pakistani sellers


For resellers working the Pakistani market, three things tend to decide success more than anything else. First, **COD** : card penetration is still low and many buyers have been burned paying upfront, so they want to pay when the parcel arrives. A platform that collects cash for you removes the single biggest barrier to a first sale and protects you from non-payment. Second, **cash flow** : if you're starting with little capital, fast payouts and a no-investment model mean you can reinvest profit into ads and testing instead of waiting weeks for collected cash. Third, **operational load** : every hour you spend packing, chasing couriers or handling returns is an hour you're not spending finding and marketing winning products. Markaz is built around all three, which is why it suits sellers who want to move quickly without tying up capital.

## How to test both platforms properly


1. Sign up on each platform and browse the actual catalogue — look for products that fit a niche you understand, and check whether stock and prices look reliable.
2. List two or three identical products on each and run a small, equal amount of marketing to each so the comparison is fair.
3. Place a real test order on each to experience delivery speed, packaging and the COD flow exactly as a customer would.
4. Track how long your profit takes to land after a delivered order — time it, don't trust the claim.
5. Trigger a return or a customer query to see how each platform handles problems, because that's where weak platforms quietly cost you money and reputation.
6. Compare the totals: which paid faster, delivered cleaner, and handled more of the work? That platform wins for your business specifically.


Decide with data from your own orders, not marketing copy. A platform that looks great on paper but pays slowly or cancels often will hurt you more than a modest one that's reliable.

## Common mistakes when choosing a platform


- **Chasing the biggest catalogue alone.** A huge product count is useless if stock is unreliable or COD isn't supported in your cities.
- **Ignoring payout speed.** Slow payouts quietly strangle a new business that needs to reinvest quickly.
- **Skipping a real test order.** You only see true delivery quality and return handling when you order as a customer yourself.
- **Forgetting margin control.** If you can't set your own price, your profit is capped by the platform regardless of how well you market.
- **Believing claims without proof.** Treat every published figure as a hypothesis to verify, especially where public detail is thin.


## How to find winning products on any platform


The platform is only half the battle; the products you choose decide the rest. Whichever platform you settle on, the same product-research habits separate sellers who profit from those who don't.


- **Solve a clear problem or feed a strong want.** Products that fix an everyday annoyance or tap a visible trend convert far better than generic items.
- **Check the margin works after costs.** Your selling price needs to comfortably cover product cost, your ad spend per sale, and the occasional return. Thin margins disappear the moment a few parcels come back.
- **Use image search to find what's already moving.** On Markaz you can search by a photo of a trending item to find a sellable equivalent quickly, instead of scrolling endlessly.
- **Avoid fragile, oversized or easily-faulty items** early on — they raise return rates, which hurt most under cash on delivery.
- **Start narrow.** Test three to five products properly rather than listing fifty and marketing none of them well.


Run small, equal tests, keep the products that sell and return cleanly, and cut the rest. Discipline here matters more than which platform's logo is on your dashboard.
