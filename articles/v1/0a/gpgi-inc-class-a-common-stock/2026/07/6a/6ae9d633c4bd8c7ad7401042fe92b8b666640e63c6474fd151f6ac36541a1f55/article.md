# GPGI Announces Date of Second Quarter 2026 Earnings Call

NEW YORK, July 23, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) —[GPGI, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=Id-_sk32icd0_oFPxq16Dz5hlebT2CGCygP-ku3hBeEvZFomoQ6QMmruDV2bkkyDGY7TFGRYx5o5GxHkBEeFtA==) (NYSE: GPGI), a diversified multi-industry platform for companies with great positions in good industries, today announced it will host a conference call on Thursday, August 6, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. Eastern Daylight Time (EDT) to discuss its financial results for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026.
