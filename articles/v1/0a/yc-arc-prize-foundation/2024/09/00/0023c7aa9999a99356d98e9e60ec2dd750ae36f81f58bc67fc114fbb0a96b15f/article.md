# Announcing the ARC Prize 2024 University Tour


ARC Prize is going on tour, visiting over a dozen US universities and enlisting top AI students and researchers to actively move open AGI progress forward.


Want to see ARC-AGI live?[Register](https://arcprize.org/blog/2024-university-tour#tour-dates) .


---


### On Tour


The mission of ARC Prize is to drive open AGI progress. As a global competition (the largest on Kaggle) with a wide audience, we've been thrilled to see incredible talent jump in to participate.


But ARC-AGI is hard.


New ideas are still needed to achieve a leap forward. We know there are innovative thinkers who have outside approaches that can unlock incredible progress.


That's why we're coming to you.


This October, we're visiting more than a dozen top-tier US-based universities on the east and west coasts.


ARC Prize Co-founders Mike Knoop and François Chollet will speak directly to undergrad and graduate students working on AI research to explore the history, motivation, technical approaches, and future direction of the only benchmark that measures our progress towards AGI.


Each event is invite-only and open to university students, faculty, and staff. Check with your school's events organizers for registration details.


---


### 2024 Virtual Tour Stop


In the spirit of open source, for those unable to join live, one tour stop will be a virtual event open to the public. This will feature the same content as the university events.


We'll dive deep into technical approaches designed to defeat ARC-AGI and explore the past, present, and future of ARC Prize.


[Register for the virtual tour](https://lu.ma/txzzf7yk) .


Email team@arcprize.org with any questions.


We hope to see you soon - in-person or online!
