### ARC-AGI-Pub is Live


If you want to test model performance on ARC-AGI no limitations on internet access and few limitations on compute, you can submit your solution to the ARC-AGI-Pub leaderboard.


We are committing $150,000 USD toward a verification fund for this new leaderboard to help reimburse resource costs.


Note: ARC-AGI-Pub is not part of ARC Prize 2024 and has no associated prizes.


Responding to numerous questions on Discord and Twitter - the ARC-AGI-Pub score is measured on the public evaluation set, while the semi-private evaluation set used to check against hardcoding solutions to the public tasks is a new and (for now) hidden dataset.


Read the full announcement:[Introducing the ARC-AGI Public Leaderboard](https://arcprize.org/blog/introducing-arc-agi-public-leaderboard)
