## New high score from MindsAI!


### Breaking the 40% barrier


Hello, AGI enthusiasts!


As I'm writing about the progress in ARC-AGI over the past two weeks, the world record has just been broken — again.


Let's dive into the latest updates!


---


### Activity


A huge congratulations to Jack Cole, Michael Hodel, and Mohammed Osman (Team MindsAI) for breaking the 40% barrier for the first time in the history of ARC-AGI!


New SOTA score: 41%


To learn all about this winning solution approach, watch their recent podcast interview.


As we enter Week 6 of the ARC Prize, only 6 teams have scored higher than the open source solution templates.


Have a novel idea you've been wanting to try? Now is your moment.

### Content


**Reverse Engineering the Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus**


Michael Hodel (Team MindsAI) presented his widely adopted Domain Specific Language for ARC-AGI and how he used it to create tens of thousands of new tasks. The discussion was also joined by dedicated ARC devotee Simon Strandgaard (aka neoneye) and others.


Watch:[YouTube](https://youtu.be/J3_KjLikOU0)


**The ARC Prize: Efficiency, Intuition, and AGI**


Cognitive Revolution Podcast host Nathan Labenz and Mike Knoop delve into ARC-AGI's implications for general intelligence and exploring the nuances of human-like intuition and problem-solving in AI. Listen in for potential hybrid approaches to AGI development and dive deep into the complex interplay between learning, memorization, and generalization in cutting-edge AI models.


Listen:[YouTube](https://youtu.be/XECXoS-D6kQ) ,[Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/5LFNExCAmfLA6Akw4QRlHq?si=tlQNUIe9Qta-wIzMUOX6ew) ,[Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/de/podcast/the-arc-prize-efficiency-intuition-and-agi-with-mike/id1669813431?i=1000662099827&l=en-GB)


**Solving Chollet's ARC-AGI with GPT4o**


ARC-AGI-Pub record holder, Ryan Greenblatt, explains his GPT-4o technique for generating many Python scripts that solve ARC-AGI tasks. He and Machine Learning Street Talk host Tim Scarfe discuss differences in learning and reasoning between AI and humans, and the integration of various techniques to develop smarter AI systems.


Listen:[YouTube](https://youtu.be/z9j3wB1RRGA) ,[Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2lLjVGxDIQJ8WBgKrgGLb6?si=WPKp5AS2QDSjvrlcisdrEg) ,[Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/ryan-greenblatt-solving-arc-with-gpt4o/id1510472996?i=1000661332631)
