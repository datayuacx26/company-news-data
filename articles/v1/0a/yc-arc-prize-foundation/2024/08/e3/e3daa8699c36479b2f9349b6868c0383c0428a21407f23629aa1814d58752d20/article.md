## 13% increase in the ARC-AGI world record


Hello AGI developers,


We've just passed the halfway point of ARC Prize 2024. We're happy to report over 11,000 entrants on Kaggle and a staggering 13% increase in the ARC-AGI world record.


Here's where we stand today.


> 46% -- let's go![https://t.co/btkzUegClX](https://t.co/btkzUegClX)

Experiencing déjà vu? Don't worry. It's not your intelligence at fault. We congratulate Jack Cole and team MindsAI for setting a world record for the third consecutive time! 👏


More teams are stepping up their game. We now have 4 teams with scores exceeding 30%.
