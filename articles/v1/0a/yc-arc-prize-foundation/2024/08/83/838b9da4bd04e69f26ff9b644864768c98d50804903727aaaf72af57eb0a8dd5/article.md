## Chollet at AGI-24 + Zuck, llamas, and ARC at Llamathon


Hello, AGI seekers.


There are 86 days left in this year's competition. MindsAI still tops the leaderboard at 43%. Are you on track to submit your solution?


François Chollet spoke at the AGI-24 conference about defining, measuring, and building AGI.


Watch:[YouTube​](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nL9jEy99Nh0)


Lots of other interesting talks from the conference - @graum in discord mentioned Alexander Ororbia's talk on predictive coding and biomimetic intelligence.


If you discover a new ARC-related insight here or elsewhere, please share it with the community on discord!


Shout out to Vladimir Iglovikov and team for jamming on ARC Prize solutions with Llama models at the South Park Commons Llamathon event which featured real-live llamas (and real-live[Zuck](https://x.com/viglovikov/status/1821693851628872058) .)


What's happened in the past few weeks? Let's dive in.

### Content


**On the "ARC-AGI" $1 Million Reasoning Challenge**


Award-winning author and Santa Fe Institute professor Melanie Mitchell dives into the world of ARC Prize and what it all means.


Read:[Substack](https://aiguide.substack.com/p/on-the-arc-agi-1-million-reasoning)


**Automation to AGI**


Catch Mike Knoop's talk at the Arize AI Observe Conference detailing his AI journey from Zapier to ARC Prize.


Watch:[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mmJYgpbjtDo)


**Putting AI (and Podcast Hosts) to the Test**


Graham Cluley and Mark Stockley give a hilarious live demo of ARC-AGI on the AI Fix Podcast.


Listen:[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OLsIoIuAfKc) ,[Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2b0lTjKg1SDNZVHVFBRrh2?si=_66FAsouQn6mEbylm264jg) ,[Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/an-ai-cookery-dumpster-fire-the-arc-prize-and-a/id1753381111?i=1000664490323)


**LLMs Are a Dead End to AGI**


It's 2030, and AGI is finally here. In one small way, our journey to AGI can be traced back to a $1 million contest that challenged the AI status quo back in 2024.


Read:[Freethink](https://www.freethink.com/robots-ai/arc-prize-agi)


**LLMs Have Stalled the Progress of AGI**


While LLMs are often seen as overhyped in research circles, they are also considered by some as a major roadblock to achieving AGI.


Read:[Analytics India Magazine](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-insights-analysis/llms-have-stalled-the-progress-of-agi/)
