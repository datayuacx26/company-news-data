# 2024 Code Submissions Closed


Code submissions for the ARC Prize 2024 Kaggle competition are now closed as of Nov. 10.


We have begun a leaderboard verification process where both the Kaggle and ARC Prize teams will work to verify winning solutions.


The paper submission deadline is 11:59pm UTC Tuesday, Nov. 12. Make sure to submit your paper by then!


The deadline for teams to open source their solutions is Nov. 24. Only open source submissions are eligible for prizes and leaderboard inclusion.


Winners will be announced on Dec. 6 alongside a paper that will detail novel ARC-AGI solution approaches and learnings from both the ARC-AGI and ARC-AGI-Pub leaderboards gained this year.


[Subscribe to get notified.](https://arcprize.kit.com/bc80575d89)

## Thank You


Thanks to the 1,451 teams who submitted 19,423 code solutions during the 2024 competition!


We're quite thrilled with the progress that has been made on ARC-AGI this year and we can't wait to dig into the code and paper submissions and then share this valuable knowledge with the community.


While there will be no late submissions allowed and the private evaluation dataset will be unavailable during the competition downtime, we encourage you all to continue working on your concepts and solutions.


We have committed to running the competition in 2025 which we plan to launch in the first quarter of next year. You are welcome to keep us posted on your progress in the meantime.


Stay tuned!
