# Amrize Launches $1 Billion Share Buyback Program

# Amrize Launches $1 Billion Share Buyback Program


May 05, 2026[Download as PDF](https://investors.amrize.com/_assets/_63fc600f43b8154f1287ddf415f3cdc7/amrize/news/2026-05-05_Amrize_Launches_1_Billion_Share_Buyback_132.pdf)


CHICAGO & ZUG, Switzerland--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Amrize (AMRZ) will start its previously announced share buyback program on May 6, 2026. The program has been authorized for a total of $1 billion USD until May 5, 2027, with the shares to be repurchased for the purpose of cancellation.


The share buyback program will be executed on a second trading line on SIX Swiss Stock Exchange (Valor: 156 026 729 , ISIN: CH 156 026 729 1 and Ticker: AMRZE).


Concurrently with this press release, Amrize has published the details of the program in a buyback notice. All documentation and weekly updates on the program will be published on Amrize’s investor website:[https://investors.amrize.com/stock-data/share-buyback-programs](https://investors.amrize.com/stock-data/share-buyback-programs)


About Amrize


Amrize (NYSE: AMRZ) is building North America, as the partner of choice for professional builders with advanced branded solutions from foundation to rooftop. With over 1,000 sites and a highly efficient distribution network, we deliver for our customers in every U.S. state and Canadian province. Our 19,000 teammates uniquely serve every construction market from infrastructure, commercial and residential to new build, repair and refurbishment. Amrize achieved $11.8 billion in revenue in 2025 and is listed on the New York Stock Exchange and the SIX Swiss Exchange. We are ready to build your ambition. Learn more at[www.amrize.com](http://www.amrize.com/) .
