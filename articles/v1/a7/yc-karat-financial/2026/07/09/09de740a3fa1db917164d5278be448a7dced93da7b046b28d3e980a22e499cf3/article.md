# Uplevel Your Content Business | Karat Blog

#### 2025 Creator Business Growth Playbook


We broke this out into sections. To watch the full conversations these learnings are from,[join the Discord (replay links inside).](https://link.trykarat.com/DiscordNL)


#### Content


- **• Pick one mission and stick to it.** You don't need to put max effort into everything to grow as a creator.
- ***•* Engage with your community regularly.** Community is your lifeblood as a creator. Respond in the comments. Make sure they feel heard.
- ***•* Your personal brand is everything.** Do these two exercises[Qetsiyah Jacobson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/qetsiyahjacobson/?originalSubdomain=ca) recommends to start creating your personal brand:
- List out everything about yourself (location, jobs, hobbies, etc). Find interest in that. Don't try to be someone else.
- List out **aspiring points vs. relatable points.** You need to be equal parts aspiring and relatable. What makes you relatable to the average person? What makes you stand out from the average person?


#### Brand Deals


#### Pitching:


- ***•* Use**[Justin Moore's](https://www.creatorwizard.com/) **ROPE Method:**


1. R - Relevant: Make your pitch relevant to a campaign that the brand is *currently running or has run in the past.*


2. O - Organic: Showcase organic work that demonstrates how your audience has an affinity for that brand.


3. P - Proof: Show how you've helped other brands achieves these results.


4. E - Easy to Execute: Pitch the brand *exactly* what content you're going to provide them and what rights that brand will have to your content.


- ***•* Include a media kit** even if you don't think you have enough followers. Metrics, past work, testimonials
- ***•* Send 20 cold outreach emails per day** if you’re not getting brand deals. Just do it.
- ***•* Ask for a higher rate.** You’re likely undercharging, ask for more. Start high. You can always come down.


#### Negotiating:


- **• Think about exclusivity, usage rights and licensing** to maximize your income per deal. These can 2x-10x your rates.
- ***•* Exclusivity** - if the brand wants exclusivity over what products you can promote, you can charge a significant fee for that.
- ***•* Usage rights** - if the brand wants to repurpose your content for their own ads and channels, charge 2-3x your content creation fee
- ***•* Licensing** - charge for the rights to use your personal likeness.


#### Revenue Streams:


- **• Sell digital products.** Whether it's a course, template, or ebook, think about what you can package together and sell.
- ***•* Newsletter** - you own your list. Sponsorships are a natural way to monetize a newsletter.
- ***•* Consulting** - if you have an area of expertise, package it up into a consulting offering.
- ***•* Communities** - paid communities can be a great way to monetize a highly engaged audience.
- ***•* Affiliate revenue** - promote other people’s products and take a cut of the sale.
