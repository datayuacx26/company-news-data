# Now Integrate GoHighLevel Calendar With Vapi - Vapi AI Blog

For agencies juggling client appointments, the back-and-forth of calendar management is a familiar friction point. Keeping schedules aligned, capturing lead details, and ensuring bookings are correctly logged in GoHighLevel eats into time that could be spent on higher-leverage work.\\


We’ve been focused on how Vapi’s voice agents can tackle more of these operational loads. That’s why today, we’re rolling out direct integration with GoHighLevel Calendars.


## What This Means


This integration embeds Vapi voice agents directly into your GHL scheduling flow.


Your agents can now:


- Check Availability: Query any connected GHL calendar for real-time openings.
- Create Contacts: Capture caller details and instantly add them as contacts in GoHighLevel.
- Book Appointments: Confirm a time and lock in the booking that is tied to the correct contact.


Imagine an inbound call from a potential client. Your agent can now handle the full interaction: qualify the lead, find a time, and book the slot.


## Getting Started:


We’ve aimed for a straightforward setup, mirroring how you’d connect other tools like Google Calendar within Vapi:


1. Link Your GoHighLevel Account:
Go to[Dashboard → Keys](https://dashboard.vapi.ai/keys) and click Add GoHighLevel. Follow the prompts to authorize the connection.
2. Enable GHL Calendar Tools: Visit[Dashboard → Tools](https://dashboard.vapi.ai/tools) .


You’ll see 4 new tool options:


1. Create Event
2. Check Availability
3. Create Contact
4. Contact Get
5. **Add Tools to Your Assistant**


When building or editing your assistant, attach these tools to its configuration. Then update the system prompt so the agent knows when and how to use them in conversation.


Full setup details are in[the Docs.](https://docs.vapi.ai/tools/go-high-level)


## Why This Matters:


GoHighLevel is core to how many agencies manage client operations. This integration removes the manual appointment workflow and pushes voice agents deeper into day-to-day execution.


What this unlocks:


- **Efficiency** – Automate lead capture and scheduling from the first touchpoint.
- **Accuracy** – Reduce dropped details and mislogged appointments.
- **Scalability** – Let your agents handle more volume without adding headcount.


If your agency builds for local businesses or any team that runs on appointments, this gives your Vapi agents a direct role in driving bookings.
