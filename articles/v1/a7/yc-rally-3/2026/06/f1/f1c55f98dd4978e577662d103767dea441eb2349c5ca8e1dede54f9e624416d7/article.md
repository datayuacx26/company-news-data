# Best Fuel Cards for Companies in France (2026)

The best fuel card for a company in France depends on the route, vehicle type and back-office workflow. A local van fleet may care most about supermarket stations and simple VAT invoices. A transport company needs motorway density, tolls and HGV-friendly stations. A mixed fleet needs fuel, EV charging, tolls, parking and receipts in one place.


This guide compares eight fuel-card and mobility-card options French businesses shortlist in 2026, with a special focus on **multi-enseigne acceptance** , EV readiness and admin work.

## How to compare fuel cards in France


Focus on practical cost, not marketing claims:


- **Multi-enseigne acceptance.** Can drivers use TotalEnergies, Shell, E.Leclerc, Intermarche, Auchan, AS24, Routex partners and motorway sites without detours?
- **Tolls and parking.** French autoroutes can become a separate admin stream if tolls are not consolidated.
- **VAT and accounting.** You need clean, structured invoices, not a pile of pump receipts.
- **EV transition.** Check whether public charging, home charging and mixed fuel/EV fleets fit one workflow.
- **Fees and deposits.** A discount is weaker if monthly fees, service charges or guarantees eat it.

## 2. TotalEnergies Fleet


TotalEnergies is one of the most familiar names for French business fuel cards. It has strong national brand recognition, a large station network and EV charging options through TotalEnergies charging services.


**Best for:** French companies whose routes already fit TotalEnergies stations and charging locations.


**Watch out:** it is still a branded network; compare the terms if you need broad multi-brand acceptance.


**Website:**[TotalEnergies](https://services.totalenergies.fr/)


## 3. Shell Card / Fleetcor


Shell is useful when drivers already fuel at Shell or partner stations and the fleet wants a familiar brand with access to Shell Recharge. It is a stronger fit for route patterns that naturally pass Shell sites.


**Best for:** fleets that want a brand network plus EV charging.


**Watch out:** savings depend on network fit, not universal acceptance.


**Website:**[Shell Fleet Solutions](https://www.shell.fr/)


## 4. AS24


AS24, part of TotalEnergies, is built for heavy vehicles. It focuses on HGV-friendly stations, motorway corridors and transport operators that need diesel, tolls and driver controls rather than a general expense card.


**Best for:** HGV fleets and transport companies.


**Watch out:** less relevant for light commercial mixed fleets that also need parking, EV and employee expenses.


**Website:**[AS24](https://www.as24.com/)


## 5. DKV Mobility


DKV is a strong European card for fleets crossing France, Germany, Benelux, Spain and Italy. It combines fuel, toll services, VAT support and DKV +Charge for EV charging.


**Best for:** cross-border fleets that need mature fuel, toll and VAT workflows.


**Watch out:** pricing and service fees can be complex. Compare your actual routes before signing.


**Website:**[DKV Mobility](https://www.dkv-mobility.com/)


## 6. UTA Edenred


UTA Edenred offers broad service coverage across Europe, fuel and toll integrations, and a familiar transport-company workflow. It is a realistic alternative to DKV for larger fleets.


**Best for:** European operations that need broad service-point coverage and tolls.


**Watch out:** still a network-led fuel-card model, not a universal expense platform.


**Website:**[UTA Edenred](https://web.uta.com/)


## 7. Mooncard Mobility


Mooncard Mobility is closer to a finance and expense-card workflow than a classic fuel network. It can be useful when the business wants cards, limits, receipts and accounting controls across mobility spend.


**Best for:** companies that treat fuel as one category inside wider employee and mobility expenses.


**Watch out:** not a replacement for deep HGV fuel-and-toll networks.


**Website:**[Mooncard](https://www.mooncard.co/)


## 8. Easyfuel and comparison providers


Easyfuel and comparison sites can help buyers shortlist different cards quickly. They are useful if the company is not yet sure whether it needs TotalEnergies, Shell, AS24, DKV, UTA or a modern expense card.


**Best for:** initial market comparison.


**Watch out:** the final fee model belongs to the provider you choose.
