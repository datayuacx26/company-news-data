# Top 7 cheapest refueling apps in Germany (2026 edition)

Fuel is one of the largest operational costs for any European fleet, with prices fluctuating dramatically across countries and even within the same city. In Germany, for example, the *Bundesverband der Energiemarkt-Dienstleister* (bft) reports that diesel prices can vary by up to 20 cents per litre in a single day. For a commercial vehicle using 5,000 litres a month, this difference translates to a potential variance of €1,000 monthly, or €12,000 annually, for just one vehicle. While a consumer **cheap refueling app** helps drivers find the lowest price at the pump, it often creates significant administrative challenges for businesses. Fleets are left with a disjointed process of paying, collecting paper receipts, and manually reconciling expenses, which wastes valuable time and introduces errors.


Effective fleet management also means anticipating and mitigating operational fuel expenses before they hit the monthly close. This 2026 refresh reviews the fuel price apps fleets are most likely to compare in Germany and explains why a consolidated platform is essential for turning fuel data into real, verifiable savings without the operational headache of managing a fleet across the complex European market.


## 1. clever-tanken


As one of Germany's most established fuel price comparison services (since 1999), clever-tanken offers a robust platform for finding the cheapest fuel. It leverages live data from the German Market Transparency Unit for Fuels (MTS-K) to provide real-time pricing for standard fuels, and it also covers premium fuels, LPG, and CNG. Its longevity in the market has built significant trust and a large user base.


This platform is a strong contender for the title of best " **cheap refueling app** " due to its powerful analytical tools. Drivers can set price alerts for preferred stations, view historical price curves to time their refuelling optimally, and use the "magic map" feature to visualise the cheapest areas at a glance. For fleets transitioning to electric, the integrated "clever-laden" feature allows planning for both refuelling and EV charging stops, a crucial advantage for mixed fleets operating in Europe's evolving energy landscape.


### Key Considerations for Fleets


For fleet managers, a key challenge is that while clever-tanken excels at finding low prices, it doesn't solve the payment and reconciliation problem. Drivers still pay out-of-pocket or with various cards, creating a mountain of receipts for the finance team. This manual process is a significant administrative burden, especially for cross-border operations where varying VAT rates and currency conversions complicate accounting. While some[fleet payment card alternatives](https://www.getrally.com/shell-fuel-card-alternative) offer limited station networks, others are emerging to provide broader access and solve these administrative headaches. The free version is ad-supported, with a paid subscription available to remove them.


**Website:**[https://www.clever-tanken.de/](https://www.clever-tanken.de/)


## 2. ADAC Drive (formerly ADAC Spritpreise)


Backed by Germany's largest automobile club, ADAC Drive integrates fuel price comparison with comprehensive route planning and navigation. The app provides real-time pricing for E10, E5, Diesel, and CNG/LPG, sourcing its data from the German Market Transparency Unit for Fuels (MTS-K). Its credibility and association with the trusted ADAC brand make it a popular choice for drivers across Germany.


This platform excels as a "cheap refueling app" by embedding fuel price data directly into its navigation features. Drivers can plan a route and instantly see the five cheapest fuel stations along the way, complete with traffic, toll, and construction information—a crucial feature for navigating Europe's diverse road networks. The app also offers price history charts (24-hour and 7-day) and provides recommendations on the optimal time to refuel. For mixed fleets, it includes filters for EV charging points of interest, detailing power output, plug type, and payment options.


### Key Considerations for Fleets


While ADAC Drive is powerful for route optimisation and price finding, it presents challenges for fleet management. The app does not centralise payments or expense reporting. Each driver must pay individually and submit receipts manually, creating a significant administrative workload. This decentralised approach complicates budget tracking and lacks the spending controls that dedicated fleet payment systems offer. For European fleets, this also means forgoing automated VAT reclaim processes, a complex but essential task for maximising savings when operating across multiple EU member states.


**Website:**[https://apps.apple.com/de/app/adac-drive/id675634893](https://apps.apple.com/de/app/adac-drive/id675634893)


## 3. mehr-tanken


Mehr-tanken is another major player in the German fuel price comparison market, utilising official data from the MTS-K to deliver real-time pricing. It distinguishes itself with an intelligent recommendation engine, known as “Flizzi,” which not only finds low prices but also calculates the optimal balance between cost and the distance required to reach the station. This predictive feature also forecasts the best times to refuel in a specific region, helping drivers plan more effectively.


This platform proves to be a valuable "cheap refueling app" for those who want more than just raw price data. Its route-based search function allows drivers to find the cheapest stations along their planned journey, a crucial feature for logistics and delivery fleets. Furthermore, the app provides extensive filters for fuel type, brand, and accepted payment methods, along with integrated information on EV charging stations, making it a versatile tool for diverse fleets.


### Key Considerations for Fleets


While mehr-tanken offers sophisticated price forecasting and route optimisation, it presents the same core administrative challenge for fleets as other price-finder apps. The process remains disjointed: a driver finds a cheap station but then pays with a personal card or a traditional fuel card, leading to manual receipt collection, expense claims, and complex reconciliation. This creates a significant data gap between the refuelling decision and the final accounting. For organisations seeking to close this gap, exploring why[modern fleet cards beat traditional fuel cards](https://www.getrally.com/blog/why-modern-fleet-cards-beat-traditional-fuel-cards-every-time) can offer a path to integrating payments and expense management. Advanced features, such as the optimal station suggestions and an ad-free experience, are locked behind a paid "Plus" subscription.


**Website:**[https://www.mehr-tanken.de/mehr-tanken-app/](https://www.mehr-tanken.de/mehr-tanken-app/)


## 4. TankenApp / Tanken powered by t-online


Backed by the well-known t-online portal, TankenApp leverages live data from the German Market Transparency Unit for Fuels (MTS-K) to deliver real-time prices for E5, E10, and diesel. A key differentiator is its dual accessibility: a full-featured mobile app and a browser-based tool that requires no installation. This flexibility allows drivers or dispatchers to quickly check prices from any device, whether on the road or in the office.


This platform's predictive capabilities make it a notable contender for the best "cheap refueling app". It offers a 12-hour price forecast, helping drivers decide whether to refuel immediately or wait for a potential price drop. The app also estimates the potential savings in euros for a full tank, making the financial benefit of choosing a specific station immediately clear. For route planning, it integrates directly with navigation apps to guide drivers to the cheapest station along their path.


### Key Considerations for Fleets


For fleet managers, the primary challenge with TankenApp is its focus on price discovery rather than payment and administration. Drivers must pay using their own methods, leading to a fragmented process of collecting and submitting receipts for reimbursement. This creates a significant administrative workload for accounting teams, who must manually verify each transaction. The lack of integration means fleets cannot enforce purchasing policies, such as restricting fuel types or transaction amounts, leaving them vulnerable to out-of-policy spending and potential misuse. While effective for finding low prices, it does not provide the centralised control that integrated fleet payment systems offer.


**Website:**[https://tanken.t-online.de/](https://tanken.t-online.de/)


## 5. Tankerkönig


Tankerkönig serves a unique role in Germany's fuel price ecosystem. Rather than being a consumer-focused app, it is a long-running service that provides the raw, live fuel price data from the Market Transparency Unit for Fuels (MTS-K) through a free, open JSON API. This makes it the data backbone for countless third-party applications, custom dashboards, and independent price comparison tools used across Germany.


While it offers a basic web interface for searching prices, its primary value is for developers, data analysts, and tech-savvy fleet managers who want to build their own tools. For companies looking to integrate real-time fuel pricing into their internal logistics software or create custom alerts, Tankerkönig provides the transparent, reliable data feed to do so. This makes it less of a direct "cheap refueling app" and more of a powerful engine that drives many others.


### Key Considerations for Fleets


For fleet operations, Tankerkönig is not an out-of-the-box solution for drivers. Its website UI is functional but lacks the user-friendly features of consumer apps, and its native app availability is limited. Its real power for a fleet lies in its data accessibility. A company could use its API to analyse historical price trends, identify the most consistently cheap stations on key routes, or even build a dashboard to forecast fuel expenditure. The challenge, as with other price-finding tools, is bridging the gap between identifying low prices and managing the resulting payments and receipts. Even with custom data analysis, drivers still pay separately, leading to the same administrative overhead. ## 6. Benzinpreis‑Blitz


Benzinpreis‑Blitz is a popular consumer-focused app in Germany that extends its reach to six other European countries, including Austria, Luxembourg, France, Portugal, Spain, and Italy. Sourcing its German data directly from the official Market Transparency Unit for Fuels (MTS-K), it provides reliable, live price comparisons. Its multi-country support makes it a practical option for fleets operating near borders or on international routes.


This platform offers essential features expected from a modern "cheap refueling app" such as price alerts for preferred stations, favourite lists, and historical price graphs to help drivers identify optimal refuelling times. The web interface provides a straightforward overview, allowing users to quickly see the cheapest and nearest stations. Its user-friendly design and positive reviews on German download portals highlight its reputation among individual drivers. The app is free but offers in-app purchases to remove advertisements.


### Key Considerations for Fleets


The primary challenge for fleets using Benzinpreis-Blitz is its exclusive focus on price discovery without any integrated payment or expense management capabilities. While drivers can find the cheapest fuel across seven countries, they must still pay with personal or corporate cards. This creates a significant administrative workload, requiring manual receipt collection, verification, and reimbursement. This process is prone to errors, lacks real-time spending oversight, and makes cross-border VAT reclaim unnecessarily complex. The app, developed independently, also relies on app store distribution, which can vary by region and may complicate standardised deployment across a fleet's devices.


**Website:**[https://www.benzinpreis-blitz.de/](https://www.benzinpreis-blitz.de/)


## 7. tanken.de


For those who need a fast, no-frills price check without installing an application, tanken.de offers a streamlined, browser-based solution. It provides immediate access to live fuel prices for E5, E10, and Diesel by leveraging the official German Market Transparency Unit for Fuels (MTS-K) data feed, which is updated approximately every five minutes. Users can instantly search for the cheapest stations near their current location or by entering a specific German address or postal code, making it an extremely low-friction tool for quick checks on any device.


This platform’s strength lies in its simplicity and speed, making it a reliable candidate for a quick "cheap refueling app" alternative. It presents a clear view comparing the cheapest nearby stations versus the closest ones, allowing drivers to make a swift, informed decision. The interface is clean and direct, focused entirely on delivering price information without requiring any registration or login, which is ideal for one-off searches or for users who prefer not to clutter their phones with apps.


### Key Considerations for Fleets


For fleet managers, the simplicity of tanken.de is both a benefit and a drawback. While it provides quick, authoritative price data for basic fuels, it lacks the advanced features found in dedicated applications. There are no price alerts, historical data, or route planning capabilities. Crucially, it only covers standard fuels and omits LPG, CNG, and EV charging information. Like other price finders, it does nothing to solve the core administrative problem for European fleets: the manual collection and processing of paper receipts. Each driver refuels, pays separately, and submits an expense claim, creating a significant reconciliation burden that integrated payment systems are designed to eliminate.


**Website:**[https://tanken.de/](https://tanken.de/)
