# NEW Slack Integration


Do you spend your days on Slack, chatting with your team?


Well… we’ve got good news for you. Starting today, you can get instant notifications of qualified leads, directly to your Slack channels.


When Upcall calls your prospects, you will automatically see this type of notifications in the Slack #channel of your choice:


That way, you can follow-up with the newly called lead even faster.


This feature is FREE for all Upcall plans and is launching TODAY.


How do I enable it? Go to your dashboard, click Settings > Integrations > Slack and follow the instructions. It takes 2 minutes to setup and there is no coding required.


We are excited to launch this feature and hear your feedback.
