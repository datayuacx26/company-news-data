# Erie Indemnity to host third quarter 2025 pre-recorded conference call and webcast


## ERIE, Pa. , Oct. 22, 2025 /PRNewswire/ -- Erie Indemnity Company (NASDAQ: ERIE ) will host a pre-recorded audio webcast with the financial community providing financial results for the third quarter on Friday, October 31 st , at 10 a.m. Eastern Time .


ERIE, Pa.


, Oct. 22, 2025


/PRNewswire/ -- Erie Indemnity Company (NASDAQ: ERIE) will host a pre-recorded audio webcast with the financial community providing financial results for the third quarter on Friday, October 31st, at 10 a.m. Eastern Time. Erie Indemnity will issue a press release reporting its results after the close of the market on Thursday, October 30th.


The pre-recorded audio will be available on the company's Investor Relations website at[www.erieinsurance.com/about/investors.aspx](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4539136-1&h=3097287881&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.erieinsurance.com%2Fabout%2Finvestors.aspx&a=www.erieinsurance.com%2Fabout%2Finvestors.aspx) .


To access the pre-recorded audio via phone, please go to this link ([registration link](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4539136-1&h=376065951&u=https%3A%2F%2Fregister-conf.media-server.com%2Fregister%2FBI46666859c88b400bba726e5b7eae2d1d&a=registration+link) ), and you will be provided details. To avoid delays, we encourage participants to dial into the conference call fifteen minutes ahead of the scheduled start time.

**About Erie Insurance**


According to A.M. Best Company, Erie Insurance Group, based in Erie, Pennsylvania, is the 11th largest homeowners insurer, 12th largest automobile insurer and 13th largest commercial lines insurer in the United States based on direct premiums written. Founded in 1925, Erie Insurance is a Fortune 500 company and the 16th largest property/casualty insurer in the United States based on total lines net premium written. Rated A (Excellent) by A.M. Best, ERIE has more than 7 million policies in force and operates in 12 states and the District of Columbia.
