## PodcastOne (Nasdaq: PODC) Hits Record #6 Podtrac Ranking, Surpassing Disney


07/14/2026


Download (opens in new window)


**Ranks ahead of Disney, Barstool Sports, FOX, CNN and Paramount**


**Delivered 18M+ downloads and streams and 6M unique listeners in June**


LOS ANGELES, July 14, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- PodcastOne (Nasdaq:[PODC](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=yi-lMJYnoRSGgwik_yYoc0lH5Eia0ZKS8MCLk8OUKeVFIWLqhUe8pGdrtFrdrSgSWaEhNd0e9CygughOCAd7DA==) ), a leading publisher and podcast sales network, announced today that it has again been named to the list of Top U.S. Podcast Publishers & Networks for June 2026, by the industry leading podcast metric company Podtrac. PodcastOne ranks #6, up one slot from the previous month’s rankings, with over 18 million downloads and 6 million monthly listeners. Podtrac, which provides the podcast industry’s only ranking of top publishers based on unique audience counts, announced PodcastOne’s ranking in their industry wide communication earlier this month.


“PodcastOne is trending upwards in our ranking on Podtrac’s list of top publishers and it is in direct correlation to our industry setting standard of advertising sales, in house marketing, production and distribution services. Our white glove approach to podcasting ensures that our top ranked podcast hosting talent is positioned to grow their podcasts in the most optimal ways possible. We are excited to continue with our network’s growth strategies,” said Kit Gray, President and Co-Founder of PodcastOne.


PodcastOne’s full roster of top ranked podcasts includes programming across top genres such as news, comedy, true crime, sports and society and culture and includes shows such as *The Adam Carolla Show* , *The Jordan Harbinger Show,* *Karma and Chaos, Stassi with Tay, Off The Vine with Kaitlyn Bristowe, LadyGang, Court Junkie, Pop Apologists* and *Varnamtown.* PodcastOne shows are available through[PodcastOne](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=Y-QpS_b9FbimzlmUd2HXHx1iggxu0tP565N3HqZ9JAPnJ1NsD8fG3BINOyBUI1Dz-nTcXkIzKdO_-CwNpouiAswosG3KLfEhEkNVYber9rQ=) , Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, iHeart, Amazon and wherever podcasts are heard.
