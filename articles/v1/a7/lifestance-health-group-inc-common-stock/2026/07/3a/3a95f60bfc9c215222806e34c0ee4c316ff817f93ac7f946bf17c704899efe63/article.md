# Best Co-Parenting Apps: Tools for Communication, Custody, and Less Conflict

## Popular Co-Parenting Apps


Several co-parenting platforms are available, each with a different emphasis. The right fit depends on your family's needs, your level of conflict, and any requirements set by your court.


-


[TalkingParents](https://talkingparents.com/) provides secure, time-stamped messaging, call records, and document storage. Its robust accountability features are designed to reduce conflict, especially in high-conflict cases.


-


[OurFamilyWizard](https://www.ourfamilywizard.com/) offers a shared calendar, expense tracking, and journals to help parents log events. Its features are recognized for supporting transparent and consistent co-parent communication.


-


[Custody X Change](https://www.custodyxchange.com/) allows parents to create, manage, and modify parenting plans and schedules. This clarity may help reduce tension and missed exchanges.


-


[AppClose](https://www.appclose.com/) stands out for offering a robust free tier, making core co-parenting features accessible at no cost for separated parents who need reliable communication and scheduling support.


Some courts specify a particular platform, so check any orders in your case before you sign up. No app is a substitute for professional support, but the right tool can remove much of the day-to-day friction that typically fuels conflict.
