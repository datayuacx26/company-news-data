# How Liquor Stores Can Keep Up with the Latest Alcohol Beverage Trends In 2026

If you're looking to stay ahead of the curve in the ever-evolving world of beer, wine, and spirits, you're in the right place. The U.S. market is buzzing with exciting trends that can open up new opportunities for your business.


## Premiumization: Quality Over Quantity


Consumers are increasingly opting for premium products, reshaping the alcohol industry. The premium alcohol segment is experiencing noticeable growth, particularly with spirits. From January to October 2024, premium spirits accounted for nearly 25% of spirits sales in the U.S.


**The Opportunity:**
For liquor store owners, stocking up on premium spirits and wines can be a lucrative move. Younger consumers, especially those aged 18-34, are leading this trend, with 54% indicating a preference for premium options. Curate a selection of high-quality products that cater to this demographic, highlighting the unique qualities and stories behind premium brands to attract discerning customers.


## Ready-to-Drink: Convenience Meets Quality


Ready-to-drink (RTD) beverages are taking the market by storm. The RTD segment is benefiting from the premiumization trend, with premixed cocktail sales increasing by 24% and spirits seltzer sales up by 47.7%. Consumers are drawn to the convenience and quality of these products, making them a must-have in your store.


**The Opportunity:**
Stock a variety of RTD options, from classic cocktails to innovative new flavors. Many stores now have dedicated sections for RTD beverages and offer promotions or tastings to introduce customers to this convenient option. Highlight the ingredients and craftsmanship of these products to appeal to quality-conscious consumers.


## Health-Conscious Drinking: Embracing Non-Alcoholic Alternatives


The rise of health-conscious drinking is driving the growth of non-alcoholic and low-alcohol beverages. The no-alcohol segment in the U.S. grew by 29% in 2023 compared to 2022, significantly outpacing the low-alcohol segment, which grew by 7%. Non-alcoholic spirits, in particular, saw an impressive 88% increase.


**The Opportunity:**
As more consumers embrace "sober curiosity" and seek healthier options, offering a range of non-alcoholic alternatives can set your store apart. Interestingly, 82% of non-alcoholic buyers also drink alcohol. Providing non-alcoholic beer, wine, and spirits options expands your sales opportunities, encouraging these customers to return for traditional alcohol products.


By embracing these trends and adapting your offerings, you can position your store as a go-to destination for quality-conscious and health-minded consumers. The market is full of opportunities to expand your business. Stay in tune with your customers' needs, and you'll be in a strong position to grow within your community—and beyond. Santé!
