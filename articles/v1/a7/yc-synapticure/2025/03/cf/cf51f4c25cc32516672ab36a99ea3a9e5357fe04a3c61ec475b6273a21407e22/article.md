# Personalized Care: How Synapticure Assesses and Treats Dementia

A dementia diagnosis can be life-changing, yet many individuals and their families receive it with little to no direction on what comes next. While primary care physicians and general neurologists do their best to support their patients,[85% of dementia diagnoses come from non-dementia specialists](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6874742/) , often leaving families on their own to navigate a complex care system.


At Synapticure, we bridge this gap by providing expert-led, personalized dementia care through virtual assessments and treatment planning supported by a dedicated team that remains with you every step of the way—all through[our virtual model](https://www.synapticure.com/blog/synapticures-virtual-model-bridging-gaps-in-neurodegenerative-care) . Our approach ensures that every patient and caregiver receives a clear path forward and is given the time to understand their history, risk factors, symptoms, and needs.


## A More Personalized Approach to Assessments


Unlike traditional models, which may offer only a brief consultation, Synapticure ensures a thorough evaluation that considers cognitive function, medical history, daily life impact, and caregiver experiences. Our team of neurologists, geriatricians, nurse practitioners, neuropsychologists, and care navigators take the time to understand the whole picture.


Through virtual visits, we conduct detailed cognitive testing, using tools like the[Mini-Mental Status Examination (MMSE)](https://cgatoolkit.ca/Uploads/ContentDocuments/MMSE.pdf) to evaluate memory, attention, and problem-solving skills, among others. Because these tests are administered remotely, patients can complete them in the comfort of their homes and can be scheduled around family members and caregivers availability so they may be present to offer additional insights, even if not living together.


For further evaluation, we may recommend:


- **Genetic Testing & Counseling** – To assess hereditary risk factors and guide treatment options.
- **MRI & Advanced Brain Imaging** – To detect structural changes, stroke history, or atrophy patterns.
- **PET Scans (Amyloid & FDG PET)** – To determine amyloid buildup or metabolic activity in the brain.


## More Than a Diagnosis—A Plan for the Future


After receiving a diagnosis, our team works with individuals and their families to create a clear, actionable treatment plan tailored to each individual’s needs while also supporting their caregivers. By supporting caregivers, we provide an essential aspect of care that can often be challenging to navigate on their own in traditional settings due to limited specialist availability and complex referral processes.


At Synapticure, we provide:


- **Medication & Treatment Plans** – Recommendations based on the latest research, including new[treatments and clinical trials](https://www.synapticure.com/blog/synapticure-on-science-clinical-trials-and-treatments-for-alzheimers)
- **Personalized Therapy Referrals** – Including coordinating cognitive therapy, speech therapy, physical therapy, and in-home assessments **‍**
- **Behavioral & Mental Health Support** – Addressing common challenges that can be associated with memory issues like anxiety, depression, and agitation with counseling, medication, and coping strategies[for both patients and caregivers](https://www.synapticure.com/blog/what-you-need-to-know-about-our-collaborative-mental-health-care-program)
- **Care Coordination & Guidance** – Our team helps families implement brain health strategies, including diet, exercise, and sleep habits while ensuring access to additional services.


## Seamless Collaboration With PCPs & In-Person Care Providers


Unlike many telehealth providers, Synapticure does not operate in isolation. our patients work with the same specialty neurologist and dedicated care team throughout every step of the journey. We also **work directly with their existing primary care physicians, and local clinics to ensure**[coordinated, wrap-around care](https://www.synapticure.com/blog/comprehensive-wraparound-care-coordinating-services-and-mental-health-support-for-neurodegenerative-diseases) **.**


- **Physician Collaboration:** If a patient needs an in-person evaluation or imaging, we facilitate referrals and communicate directly with referring clinicians, eliminating the burden put on caregivers and families to coordinate care.
- **Comprehensive Case Reviews:** Our neurologists conduct care meetings with patients’ PCPs to ensure continuity of care and provide specialized insights.
- **Streamlined Referrals:** We don’t just provide a referral and leave patients to navigate the process—we ensure that when a referral is needed, the appointment is scheduled.
