# Photeine’s Story: Navigating Parkinson’s Through Personalized Virtual Care

Living with a neurodegenerative condition like[Parkinson's](https://www.synapticure.com/specialties/parkinsons-disease) can be a challenging journey, but for Photeine, Synapticure has been a transformative partner in her care.After years of uncertainty and misdiagnoses, Photeine sought a care solution that understood her unique needs. She found that Synapticure’s approach, which combines expert medical guidance from specialists with mental health support and care navigation, provided her with the comprehensive care she had been searching for.


Photeine shares that her neurologist,[Dr. Liz Ferulga](https://www.synapticure.com/providers/elizabeth-ferluga) , has been instrumental in her care, always taking into account Photeine’s perspective on what it means to live well and maintain her quality of life. She values having a say in her care plan and feels heard when providing feedback on medications, which Dr. Ferulga adjusts as needed. This collaborative approach has helped Photeine focus on achieving daily accomplishments and has made her feel supported in her journey. As a part of her multidisciplinary team, Synapticure connected her with mental health services, providing tailored therapy and compassionate counseling. These resources have helped Photeine manage the impact Parkinson’s has had on her mental health, empowering her to take charge of her well-being.


> “Synapticure helps me find joy. They listen to me and that’s all I ever wanted”


Another cornerstone of Photeine’s care has been the flexibility offered by telehealth services. Traveling to multiple appointments became increasingly difficult for her and her family, but with Synapticure’s virtual care model, she can access neurologists, psychologists, and other specialists from the comfort of her home. This convenience has eased the logistical burden and given Photeine a sense of agency in her care plan. For Photeine and her family, Synapticure has provided the tools, support, and guidance needed to face the future with resilience and hope.
