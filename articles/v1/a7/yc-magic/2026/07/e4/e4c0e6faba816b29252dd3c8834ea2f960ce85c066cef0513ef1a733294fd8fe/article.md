# Magic vs BELAY: Pricing and Coverage Compared (2026)

9 min read


## Quick summary


Magic and BELAY are both fully managed services. Each one hires, trains, and replaces your assistant for you. Three things separate them: where the assistants are based, how the pricing works, and whether anyone covers the work when your assistant is away.


Magic charges a fixed weekly rate. Full-time is $540 a week for 40 hours and includes[Magic 24/7](https://getmagic.com/magic-247-assistant/) at no extra cost (part-time is $270 a week for 20 hours). Both plans work out to $13.50 an hour.


BELAY quotes each client after a consultation, so the cost varies and you find out yours on the call. BELAY’s site says US-based assistants cost $30 to $75 an hour.
If you need an assistant based in the US, choose BELAY. If you don’t, Magic costs less and includes backup coverage.


What we found


- **Magic costs less for the same number of hours.** At around 1,000 hours a year, Magic runs $14,040. The same hours at the bottom of BELAY’s stated range come to $31,200.
- **Magic includes backup coverage.** Full-time plans come with[Magic 24/7](https://getmagic.com/magic-247-assistant/) , a shared pool that picks up work when your assistant is unavailable. BELAY provides a dedicated assistant and doesn’t list a backup option.
- **Magic pays for its assistants’ AI tools.** Every assistant gets access to leading LLMs and[training](https://getmagic.com/feature/training/) on how to use them, at no extra charge to you.
- **BELAY hires in the US. Magic hires in the Philippines and Latin America.** Every Magic assistant works US hours.

## Backup when your assistant is away


Assistants take holidays, get sick, and go on leave. What happens to the work during those days is worth checking before you sign with either service.


Every Magic full-time plan includes[Magic 24/7](https://getmagic.com/magic-247-assistant/) , a shared pool of assistants who pick up tasks when your dedicated assistant is unavailable. You send the request and someone handles it.


BELAY provides a dedicated assistant during business hours. Their service list doesn’t include a backup or on-demand option, so you’d need to ask how they cover time off.


## AI tools


Every Magic assistant gets access to leading LLMs and AI tools, plus[training](https://getmagic.com/feature/training/) on how to use them before they start working with you. Magic pays for the tools.


BELAY says its assistants use AI and automation to enhance rather than replace expertise. Their site doesn’t say which tools assistants get or who pays for them, so you’d need to ask on the call.


## Vetting and training


Magic accepts the top 0.5% of applicants. BELAY accepts the top 3% of US applicants.
Every Magic assistant goes through[training](https://getmagic.com/feature/training/) on AI tools before their first task for you. We don’t have information on whether BELAY trains its assistants.


## Contracts and cancellation


Both services run month to month with no long-term contract. BELAY asks for 30 days’ notice to cancel. Magic lets you cancel at any time.


The other difference is when you commit. BELAY’s process is a sales call, then an agreement, then a discovery call. BELAY selects your assistant and introduces them at a kickoff call. With Magic you meet your assistant first, and there’s no charge until you start working together.

## Common questions


How much does Magic cost?


Magic is $540 a week for a full-time assistant at 40 hours a week, which includes[Magic 24/7](https://getmagic.com/magic-247-assistant/) (part-time is $270 a week for 20 hours). Both work out to $13.50 an hour. Plans are billed every four weeks and there’s no charge until you start working with your assistant. See the pricing page.


How much does BELAY cost?


It varies by a significant amount. BELAY’s site says US-based virtual assistants cost $30 to $75 an hour, or $1,200 to $3,000 a month for part-time support. You need to get on a call for a custom quote. Client reports estimate a typical BELAY client pays around $3,800 a month for a little under 90 hours, roughly $42 an hour, though BELAY doesn’t confirm those figures.


Where are Magic assistants based?


Magic assistants are in the Philippines and Latin America, and all of them work US time zones. BELAY hires in the US, across 48 states.


What can a Magic assistant do?


Magic assistants cover executive and administrative work across more than a hundred task types, including inbox and calendar management, travel, research, project coordination, bookkeeping support, and personal tasks. See what an[executive assistant](https://getmagic.com/executive-assistant/) handles day to day. Magic isn’t built for senior specialist roles like a lead developer or designer.


How selective is Magic’s hiring?


Magic accepts the top 0.5% of applicants. Every assistant who gets through goes through[training](https://getmagic.com/feature/training/) on AI tools before their first task. BELAY accepts the top 3% of US applicants.


What happens when my assistant is on leave?


Every Magic full-time plan includes[Magic 24/7](https://getmagic.com/magic-247-assistant/) , a shared pool of assistants who pick up tasks when your dedicated assistant is unavailable. BELAY provides a dedicated assistant during business hours and doesn’t list a backup option.


Do Magic assistants use AI tools?


Yes. Every Magic assistant gets access to leading LLMs and AI tools, plus[training](https://getmagic.com/feature/training/) on how to use them before they start. Magic pays for the tools. BELAY says its assistants use AI and automation, but doesn’t say which tools they get or who pays.


What happens if my Magic assistant isn’t the right fit?


Magic replaces them at no extra cost and your saved preferences carry over, so the new assistant starts with the same context. BELAY offers a free rematch under its Right-Fit Guarantee.


Can I cancel anytime?


Magic has no long-term contract and you can cancel at any time. BELAY also runs month to month and asks for 30 days’ notice.


Which is better, Magic or BELAY?


It depends on whether you need someone based in the US. If you do, choose BELAY. If you don’t, Magic costs less for the same hours and includes backup coverage and paid AI access.
