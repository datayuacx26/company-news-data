# WEX Field Service Management teams up with Bluon to empower HVAC contractors with AI-driven equipment intelligence

### Share


*New integration embeds Bluon’s advanced technical support and database into the WEX FSM platform, streamlining workflows and enhancing on-site technician productivity*


[WEX](https://www.wexinc.com/) **®** today announced the launch of an integration between WEX Field Service Management (FSM) and Bluon, the industry-leading AI support and technical data company for HVAC contractors. This new collaboration embeds Bluon’s robust HVAC equipment intelligence directly into the WEX FSM solution, providing field teams with the critical data and support they need right within their existing workflows.


Through this seamless integration, WEX FSM users gain streamlined access to Bluon’s brand-agnostic database, which is unique in the HVAC industry. Covering over 260 OEMs and nearly 5,000,000 unique model number families, Bluon provides manuals, schematics, original parts (BOM), part specs, and compatible replacement part options for over 35 million specific model numbers found in the field.


In addition to the database, WEX FSM technicians will have access to MasterMechanic, Bluon’s proprietary AI for tech support. Trained on 150,000 live tech support calls handled by some of the best technicians in the trade, Bluon’s AI provides highly nuanced, technical support for virtually any piece of HVAC equipment. The integration also includes Bluon’s PartsConnect™ feature, enabling technicians to quickly locate and source compatible replacement parts carried by their preferred distributors.


“Everything about WEX FSM is designed to help contractors operate more efficiently,” said Dylan Jones, SVP, Growth Mobility. “Bringing Bluon’s trusted HVAC equipment intelligence and capabilities into our platform gives users another competitive advantage to meet the needs of their customers and distinguish themselves within their local markets.”


Serving more than 35,000 contractors, WEX FSM provides access to the critical tools needed to run trade businesses every day. The solution streamlines essential back-office workflows, including scheduling, dispatching, and billing, while equipping field technicians with the tools necessary to enhance on-site productivity. Through the WEX FSM mobile app, technicians can access digital pricebooks and offer flexible customer financing options through leading providers, including GoodLeap, Synchrony, Wells Fargo, and Service Finance.


“As the industry grows more and more complex, technicians need quick and accurate access to model-specific support and information,” said Peter Capuciati, CEO of Bluon. “By integrating Bluon directly into the WEX FSM workflow, we’re making it simpler than ever for technicians to diagnose issues, identify replacement parts and complete jobs more efficiently, without having to change their behavior.”
