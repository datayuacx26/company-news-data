## Fleet Cards 101


### What does a fuel card do?


A strong fleet card drives your business forward by providing advantages and opportunities for efficiencies and smarter fuel expense management.


### How do I save money managing a fleet?


A fleet card provides more to your business than a regular credit card. Fleet cards give businesses of all sizes tools for fuel expense management and fuel payment risk reduction, all in one place.


With many of the highest caliber fleet cards, you can take advantage of the following:


- Rebates/savings
- [Fraud protection](https://www.wexinc.com/products/fuel-cards-fleet/fraud-protection/)
- Fuel expense tracking
- Large location acceptance capacity
- Spending controls
- Automatic reporting/no paperwork
- Online and mobile account access
- Budget estimating tools linking expenses to individual users


### Can I use my fuel card for other purchases?


Fleet cards work for maintenance at accepting locations, and for purchasing certain product types in convenience stores, but most fleet cards’ primary use is for fuel. Unlike corporate credit cards, which are used on a broad range of expenses, fleet cards are usually only used for vehicle expenses. With WEX fleet cards you do have the option to provide your drivers with additional purchasing options through the WEX Edge program.[With the WEX Edge savings network, you can receive discounts on ancillary items like tires, cellular service, hotel stays, and more.](https://www.wexinc.com/products/fuel-cards-fleet/savings-network/)


The best fleet cards available offer more for your business than a credit card by providing ways to track and manage spending, help protect from fraud, help reduce overall fueling costs, and assist in fleet management. You always know who spends what, when, and where.


Fleet cards are safer to use than cash because they are protected and provide security for your dollars in a way cash cannot. Cash is hard to track, easily lost or stolen and isn’t very safe or secure. With cash, you can’t control how much is spent at one time, or at which locations. With a fleet card, driver spending is controllable and will help to avoid surprises on your bill. All you do is set the spending limits that work best for your business.

## How do I use my fleet card?


The best fleet cards provide instant access to a variety of reports, to view online or download to your bookkeeping software. Some fleet cards show you details like who the driver is, purchased fuel grade, and sales tax amounts for every purchase. With a fleet card with insights into card use, you’ll no longer need to go through the arduous task of collecting receipts from your drivers. This removes a time-consuming burden from your workday and alleviates some unwanted stress on both you and your team.


Some fleet card providers enable businesses to build data reports with their transaction data from which they can shape strategy. Choosing a provider offering fleet performance and fueling trend analysis tools, your team can easily strategize where to purchase the least expensive fuel. And if you opt for[GPS tracking (telematics)](https://www.wexinc.com/products/fuel-cards-fleet/gps-tracking/) your fleet card can also help with route optimization.


One example of this is[WEX’s ClearView solutions.](https://www.wexinc.com/products/fuel-cards-fleet/analytics-reporting/) This tool allows fleet managers to review fuel consumption in easy-to-understand charts and graphs, alerting the business to potential spending spikes or irregular spending patterns. For example, if a vehicle is using more fuel than necessary, analytics from ClearView signals that a vehicle is in need of service (or that a driver needs additional training).


When you work with a fuel card provider offering these additional services, fleet management is streamlined and you’ll find you have more time in your day to further expand and fine-tune your business.


## Does a fleet card help me save on fuel expenses?


Fuel cards often provide fleet managers ways to save on fuel expenses. Most fleet cards offer fuel rebates, so you can save a few cents per gallon at the issuing brand. (This means if you get a fleet card from brand X, you get rebates only on fuel purchased at brand X locations.) The more you and your employees drive, the more savings add up. These rebates appear as a credit on your invoice.


With a discount program like[WEX EDGE,](https://www.wexinc.com/products/fuel-cards-fleet/savings-network/) which you automatically get when you sign up for a WEX fuel card, you can take advantage of discounts on tires and hotels that are applied at the point of sale.

When choosing a fuel card provider, look for a card that comes with powerful fuel management tools. ### Can I manage my drivers with a fleet card?


The best fleet cards require drivers to enter identifying information for every purchase, like a PIN number and current odometer reading. This tells you who spends what, where, and when, helping curb theft and misuse. A good card program also lets you control how employees use the card, like limiting purchases by product type, dollar amount, and even the time of day or day of the week your card is used.


With services like WEX’s, your fleet card[helps you prevent fuel slippage or fuel theft](https://www.wexinc.com/products/fuel-cards-fleet/fraud-protection/) from occurring. The security of knowing who spends what, identifying employee theft, and the ability to cancel lost or stolen cards, will help give you peace of mind as you manage your fleet of commercial vehicles.
