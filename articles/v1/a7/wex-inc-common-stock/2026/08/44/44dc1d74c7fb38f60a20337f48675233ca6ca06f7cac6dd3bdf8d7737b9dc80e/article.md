# Fleet management data: Reporting and analytics with your fleet card

### Share


Managing a fleet is dynamic and requires comprehensive oversight. In a data-driven world, statistics and quantifiable information are essential for fleet management. Using data intelligently, you can make smart business decisions and run your fleet with power and control. Leverage fleet card data for a comprehensive overview of all of your spending. At the same time, identify what works and what doesn’t within your fleet. This article explores the most impactful aspects of analyzing fleet card data. It will also help you deliver superior reports while providing insights on the benefits and challenges associated with the process.


## **Gathering fleet card data through smart data analysis techniques**


Before your dive into reporting and analysis, you need to understand where your data is coming from. Data can be sourced from fuel transactions, maintenance expenses, and other fleet-related expenditures. Using robust data collection methods and tools ensures the accuracy and reliability of the information you’re gathering. This data will be the foundation of your analysis so it’s important to make sure your data collection methods are sound.


The art of analyzing fleet card data involves several techniques:


- **Descriptive analysis:** Summarize spending patterns, transaction frequencies, and overall fleet card usage.
- **Problem detection:** Identify irregularities and unusual patterns, potentially indicating fraud, errors, or misuse.
- **Trend analysis:** Predict fuel consumption and maintenance expenses, allowing you to be proactive and mindful in your spending.
- **Benchmarking:** Compare fleet card data against industry standards to assess cost efficiency.
- **Driver behavior analysis:** Evaluate data related to driver spending, fuel efficiency, and road safety, highlighting areas for improvement or extra training.
- **Cost allocation:** Make sure that expenses from fleet cards are allocated correctly to ensure accurate cost tracking.
- **Predictive analysis:** Use historical data to predict future trends, enabling thoughtful decision-making and anticipation of potential issues before they arise.
- **Compliance monitoring:** Share fleet card data use cases with your drivers to encourage compliance. This includes compliance with company policy, regulations, and industry standards, minimizing the risk of penalties or ethical violations.


## **Identify and monitor key metrics to surface opportunities for improvement**


When fleet managers focus on key metrics they gain a thorough understanding of their operations. These metrics include fuel efficiency, maintenance cost per vehicle, and driver spending behavior. The data provides actionable insights on decision-making moving forward. Monitoring these metrics empowers businesses to make informed decisions and optimize resource allocation.

There are several advantages to reporting and analyzing fleet card data. ## **Potential challenges and how to avoid them**


While the information retrieved from fleet card data can bring significant advantages, challenges such as data quality and security can arise. Take action to ensure that your data is accurate and secure, and consider integrating your fleet card data with other systems your business uses. Equally as important is ensuring that your cybersecurity is up to recommended standards to protect your information.


[The Small Business Administration](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/manage-your-business/strengthen-your-cybersecurity) recommends several actions to ensure your business stays safe from cyberattacks:


1. **Train your employees:** Make sure your employees are aware of the latest scams, show them how to spot suspicious emails, and teach them best computer practices like avoiding downloads from external sources.
2. **Secure your networks:** Hide your Wi-Fi network by ensuring your Service Set Identifier (SSID) stays hidden. For remote employees, make sure to use a Virtual Private Network (VPN) to keep data secure while connecting to company accounts.
3. **Use multi-factor authentication:** This safety tool requires any user to securely verify their identity to avoid fleet card skimming and fraud.
4. **Protect your data:** Use the most trusted and validated banks, platforms, softwares, and services to ensure your data is safe from hackers and scammers. Control who has access to your data and regularly back it up.
