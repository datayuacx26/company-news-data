In this article we’ll cover some ways a fuel card program helps you keep costs down, keeps your drivers operating at maximum capacity, and keeps your business running smoothly.

## **What to look for in an OTR fuel card**


At a time when fuel prices continue to fluctuate, shopping around is more important than ever. Most fuel cards offer fueling and maintenance at a network of truck stops nationwide. The perks vary widely, so it’s important to shop around for the right card for your business. Things to look for include:


- Discounts or rewards on fuel and maintenance
- Fraud protection
- Roadside assistance
- Detailed Level III reporting


Other ways to economize and become more efficient through fleet card use include managing truck repair and service costs. These charges add up quickly and become a drain on fleets of any size. However, optimizing your fleet with regular preventative maintenance will catch problems early before a driver is broken down on the road or you’re faced with a large, unexpected repair bill.


## How OTR fuel cards help control costs


#### **Fuel discounts and expense tracking**


Fuel cards are the perfect solution for OTR businesses spending large amounts of money on fuel every year. Not only are these cards convenient for your drivers, they also have easy[expense tracking and reporting features](https://www.wexinc.com/resources/blog/fleet-cards-offer-more-than-just-a-fueling-program/) . You’ll have access to a network of stations and truck stops where your drivers can refuel and get routine maintenance done all in one place.


Fleet cards can be like business credit cards for drivers, offering significant fuel and maintenance discounts and an ability to purchase items beyond just fuel. Fleet managers can place different controls on their drivers’ fleet cards depending on the driver. Cards can be used strictly for fuel and maintenance, or you might have a policy that allows drivers to buy drinks and snacks with their fuel cards. The cards are connected to each driver or truck and[there are fraud protection features that check your driver’s identity at the pump](https://www.wexinc.com/products/fuel-cards-fleet/fraud-protection/) .


#### **Maintenance records and route optimization**


Maintaining a full maintenance record covering each truck in your fleet will help you stay on top of vehicle needs. Ensuring there are extra trucks on hand to replace those in the shop can save fleets lost road time. Scheduling planned maintenance also reduces vehicle wear and tear.


Another cost-saver is delivery route optimization. Saving both time and money, mapping out the most efficient routes and stops will save your business thousands of dollars over a given fiscal year. By leveraging available technology combined with your fleet card program, you are making a valuable investment in your business and in your drivers, making their job easier.


#### **Fleet analytics and reporting**


[WEX analytics and reporting tools](https://www.wexinc.com/products/fuel-cards-fleet/analytics-reporting/) provide easy access to insights that can lead to greater profitability. Available insights include fuel card purchase monitoring and driver tracking. Having access to this information helps fleet managers cut costs, streamline fleet operations, and uncover savings. You’ll find new ways to forecast and budget expenses, spot any unusual activity or misuse, compare performance with other fleets, and[provide accurate fuel tax reporting](https://www.wexinc.com/resources/blog/trucking-companies-tools-to-assist-with-ifta-and-hut-reporting-fuel-tax-prep-and-frequent-tax-changes/) .

#### **Cashing different types of checks while on the road**


Since most banks have limited hours of operation, the ability to withdraw funds or cash a check at a truck stop is essential for your drivers. Truck drivers’ schedules require that services be available 24/7. A limitation is that many of these convenient stores and stations on the highway don’t cash money orders, tax refunds, insurance checks, third-party foreign travelers, or gift checks.


Walmart and other large supermarket chains, on the other hand, will often cash many of these types of checks. At Walmart, go to the customer service desk in the MoneyCenter for check cashing services.They will also cash money orders up to $5,000, charging fees from $4 – $8, depending on the amount. Of the 5,206 Walmart locations across the US, many offer 24-hour check cashing at the service desk.
