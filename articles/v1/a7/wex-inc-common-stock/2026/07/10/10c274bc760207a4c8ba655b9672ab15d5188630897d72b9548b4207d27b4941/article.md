## **A brief refresher on fleet management**


Fleet management involves overseeing and coordinating one or more company vehicles to ensure efficiency, safety, and cost-effectiveness across an organization — from small businesses to large enterprises.


These days, the range of off-the-shelf fleet management products is broad. There are effective, time-tested solutions specifically designed for fleet expense management which provide the best operational efficiency for your business, no matter the size.


Larger businesses and those at the enterprise level likely have one, if not multiple, fleet managers who oversee operations. Fleet management solutions and systems augment fleet managers’ jobs by streamlining things like data collection, tracking, and fuel management.


Typically, to be an effective fleet manager, you have visibility into various aspects of driver behavior, vehicle health, etc. — and tools that can provide it. In most cases, this means oversight of:


- **Vehicle tracking:**[GPS technology helps monitor vehicle location,](https://www.wexinc.com/products/fuel-cards-fleet/gps-tracking/) route efficiency, and overall fleet movement to help keep track of drivers, mitigate fraud, and reduce vehicle misuse.
- **Fuel management:**[Monitoring fuel consumption and optimizing routes](https://www.wexinc.com/products/fuel-cards-fleet/) helps to reduce fuel costs and the environmental impact of your fleet.
- **Driver training/management:** Monitoring driver behavior, ensuring compliance with regulations, and providing training when needed contribute to overall fleet safety and efficiency.
- **Safety and compliance:** Ensuring that vehicles comply with safety standards and regulatory requirements helps avoid legal issues and enhances overall safety.
- **Data analytics and reporting:**[Using data analytics to gain insights into fleet performance](https://www.wexinc.com/products/fuel-cards-fleet/analytics-reporting/) and generating robust reports helps in myriad ways, from enhanced decision-making to better driver training.
- **Telematics:** Integrating telematics technology into your fleet operations makes it easier to collect and analyze data related to vehicle performance, driver behavior, and other relevant factors overseen by fleet managers.

## **Challenges that fleet managers face**


Across all industries and business sizes, fleet managers face challenges that can be difficult to overcome without the right tools and insight:


- **Vehicle downtime:** Unplanned downtime, which sometimes leads to unscheduled maintenance, can increase your operating costs. While it’s tricky to nail down the average cost of unplanned downtime, you can make an informed guess by summing the hourly cost of a driver and hourly profit loss. If the vehicle also needs maintenance, you’ll have to spend even more.
- **Regulatory compliance:** Fleet vehicles are held to a litany of standards and regulations set forth by federal and state authorities, which can be tough to maintain without proper visibility into operations.
- **Fleet maintenance:** Another large — and expensive — hurdle in fleet management, maintenance bills can add up quickly and unexpectedly. Using tools like GPS tracking and telematics, fleet managers can keep track of their vehicles to know when they need service.
- **Driver behavior:** Poor driving habits increase the chances of collisions, injuries, and even death. Not only do poor habits like harsh braking and idling wear down vehicles faster or consume more fuel than necessary, but they can also lead to lawsuits and increased insurance premiums that your business will be responsible for paying.

## **Compliance and regulatory management**


Overseeing commercial vehicles means overseeing many compliance requirements. The best fleet management solutions can help keep your operation organized and in compliance.


For example, in the event of a regulatory audit, fleet management solutions provide accurate and organized data via telematics tracking. Stored and easily accessible historical information makes it easier for organizations to demonstrate compliance during audits.


Furthermore, many regions have strict regulations regarding the maximum hours a driver can operate a vehicle within a specific timeframe. GPS tracking and telematics monitor and log driver hours automatically, ensuring[hours of service (HOS) compliance](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/regulations/hours-service/summary-hours-service-regulations) .
