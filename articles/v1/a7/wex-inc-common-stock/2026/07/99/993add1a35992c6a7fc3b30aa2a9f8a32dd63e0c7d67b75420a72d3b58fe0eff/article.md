# WEX® DriverDash adds CITGO®, accelerating nationwide access to mobile fuel payments for fleets

### Share


**WEX** today announced the integration of its[DriverDash](https://www.fleetdriverdash.com/) ® **mobile payment app** into the[CITGO](https://www.citgo.com/) **merchant network** , highlighting the continued growth of DriverDash as a trusted mobile payment solution for businesses and fleet operators nationwide. DriverDash users can now pay directly at the pump at CITGO stations without swiping a physical card or entering the store, delivering a faster, more effortless fueling experience.


DriverDash expands its fast-growing U.S. station footprint with the addition of approximately **4,000 CITGO locations** . Teaming up with WEX on its card program since 2013, CITGO markets quality motor fuels to independent marketers who consistently rate it as one of the best branded supplier companies in the industry. CITGO branded marketers sell motor fuels through independently owned, branded retail outlets, all located east of the Rocky Mountains.


### **Accelerating momentum and expanding access**


DriverDash is WEX’s mobile payment app for commercial fleet drivers, allowing them to pay for fuel directly from their smartphone. Built on WEX’s closed-loop payments network, the app simplifies transactions, strengthens security, and provides real-time visibility and control for fleet managers.


As demand grows for frictionless payments and digital workflows, DriverDash is quickly becoming a key enabler of next-generation fueling for fleets of all sizes. The latest CITGO integration means DriverDash’s mobile payments system is now available at nearly **45,000 fueling locations nationwide** .


“This collaboration with CITGO represents another step forward in how fleets think about mobility and payments,” said Brian Fournier, senior vice president and general manager, North American Mobility at WEX. “Our goal with DriverDash is to remove friction from everyday fueling while providing intelligent control for fleets. When drivers have an easier way to fuel and fleet managers have more reliable data, everyone operates more efficiently, and that’s the kind of experience we’re focused on delivering.”


### **Built to deliver security, control, and data accuracy**


The solution is powered by the integration of the **DriverDash mobile payment application payment interface (API)** into **CITGO’s point-of-sale (POS) systems** , with transactions validated through the **WEX closed-loop network** .


DriverDash helps address fleet and fuel merchant challenges around security, fraud prevention, and visibility by replacing vulnerable card-based fueling with a secure, data-rich mobile experience. Using encrypted transactions with facial and fingerprint recognition, DriverDash helps reduce fraud risks such as card skimming while capturing accurate in-cab odometer readings and generating automatic electronic receipts for cleaner data and easier reconciliation. Drivers benefit from added convenience by eliminating the need to swipe a card or enter the store, which increases productivity.


“Expanding DriverDash to the CITGO network is about meeting drivers where they are with a faster, safer way to fuel,” said Cecile Zinder, vice president of product management at WEX. “By pairing secure mobile payments with real-time, high-quality data, we’re helping fleets simplify fueling for drivers while giving managers stronger controls across their operations.”


### **Availability**


The DriverDash app is available for[iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/driverdash/id1050166205) **** and ****[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wex.fleet.driverdash&hl=en_US) devices and free to fleets using WEX cards. Drivers can activate the app via a secure invitation from their fleet manager. CITGO locations will begin rolling out DriverDash capability immediately, expanding nationwide over the coming months. Drivers are also encouraged to download the Club CITGO app to begin pairing their fueling sessions with exclusive rewards and savings.
