## Accelerate work with Lucid AI


### Generate a diagram


Rather than manually creating complex visuals, automatically[generate diagrams](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/30324063850516-Boost-productivity-with-Lucid-AI#generate-a-diagram) with[Lucid AI.](https://lucid.co/platform/ai) Use simple text prompts to create robust flowcharts, sequence diagrams, class diagrams, mind maps, and ERDs, network diagrams, and more. But don’t stop there. Continue using AI prompts to iterate on and optimize your original diagram and customize it further. Get more value out of your diagrams by applying[conditional formatting](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/16293796201108-Add-conditional-formatting-in-Lucidchart) to highlight key information or items that need attention.

### Create and optimize process maps


Create detailed process diagrams without the manual work.[Lucid AI’s Process Agent](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/46461638983316-Use-Lucid-AI-s-Process-Agent-to-build-complex-diagrams-faster) does the heavy lifting by automatically generating a comprehensive diagram from a description. By defining clear triggers, owners, and specific steps, you can ensure your process maps are aligned across your teams.


Lucid's AI Process Agent


### Streamline ideation


Unlock your best ideas faster with[Lucid AI](https://lucid.co/lucidspark/use-cases/ai) . Type in a prompt like “hybrid team activity ideas” or “top three use cases for my product,” and instantly be met with inspiration and dozens of new ideas. Similarly, you can generate Lucid boards based on detailed descriptions. For example, you can ask it to set up a sprint retrospective board automatically with frames, text, and any other elements you want.


From there, you can continue to[generate, sort, and summarize](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/30324063850516-Boost-productivity-with-Lucid-AI#lucid-ai-capabilities) ideas with just a few clicks. You can even ask the AI follow-up questions to understand your visuals better.


In addition, you can summarize your diagrams to share overviews with stakeholders and quickly bring collaborators up to speed.

## Switch seamlessly between Lucidchart and Lucidspark


### Universal canvas


Turn ideas into reality with[universal canvas](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/15155938473108-Use-universal-canvas-to-streamline-your-workflow) . Effortlessly switch between Lucidchart and Lucidspark with a few simple clicks! Access the purpose-built features of both products to collaborate across teams and turn your ideas into a reality.


Many teams start their workflow in Lucidspark. Efficiently brainstorm and ideate with your team to bring the best ideas forward. Utilize unique features like[Gather](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/14996551354260-Gather-sticky-notes-in-Lucidspark) to organize ideas and start a[Voting session](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/14995416237460-Use-Voting-sessions-to-collaborate-in-Lucidspark) to decide how to move forward in your workflow.


Once you’ve decided on a path forward, switch over to Lucidchart to build out your ideas within the same canvas. Drop critical information directly into your diagram with[data linking](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/16493391394068-Link-data-to-a-Lucidchart-document) to give team members instant context without forcing them to open new tabs.


If your diagram showcases a lot of information, we recommend using[conditional formatting](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/16293796201108-Add-conditional-formatting-in-Lucidchart?_gl=1%2A1nh3o77%2A_gcl_aw%2AR0NMLjE3NzQyODU2OTYuQ2p3S0NBand5WVBPQmhCeEVpd0FncFQ4UHdnSjZlMzZaQTg2ZGZpSUdPdWxCZ1R4YU1UX283ZVZiZnIyXzc1YUwwN2o0M1otcGVfRFFSb0NXdFVRQXZEX0J3RQ..%2A_gcl_au%2AMTA2Njg5ODU3OS4xNzc4NzAwMTA1&anonId=0.bdb672319a7f9389f1&sessionDate=2026-06-11T23%3A10%3A36.813Z&sessionId=0.889c583619eb8bed366) to quickly highlight important information like key takeaways or items that need attention.


With important items highlighted on your diagram, you and your team can jump back into Lucidspark to ideate further or brainstorm next steps.

Instead of manually transferring project plans from other tools, save time with[two-way sync](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/14943046626964-Integrate-Lucid-Cards-with-Jira#2-way-live-sync) by integrating Lucid with your task management system, such as[airfocus](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/35599271203348-Integrate-Lucid-Cards-with-airfocus) , Jira, Asana, or Smartsheet.


Because of Lucid’s bidirectional sync with these tools, any changes made in Lucidspark will automatically be updated in your task management tool, and vice versa.
