# 9 Lucid features to improve business process documentation

## Key takeaways


- The **Process Accelerator** manages enterprise documentation at scale by centralizing a single source of truth and streamlining stakeholder reviews.
- **Lucid AI’s Process Agent** serves as an AI coworker, automatically generating comprehensive process maps through a conversational approach.

Every business runs on processes whether it’s onboarding new employees, providing customer support, managing IT tickets, and more. If these processes aren’t documented well enough, though, havoc ensues, causing misalignment, misunderstanding, and miscommunication.


But let's be honest: Some of us may be rolling our eyes when we think of business process documentation. All too often, documenting processes can be tedious and ineffective. Hours of searching through documents and messaging coworkers can leave you feeling drained. However,[process documentation](https://lucid.co/diagram/process-map/process-documentation-tutorial) is a powerful resource for teams to work efficiently when done correctly.

In this article, we’ll share nine Lucid that simplify and improve process documentation, helping achieve greater efficiency and alignment within your teams. Ultimately, better documentation will help your organization accelerate work and achieve goals faster.


## Process Accelerator


With the Process Accelerator, you can seamlessly manage enterprise documentation at scale using built-in process repositories, structured approval flows, and dynamic asset libraries.


Here’s how it works:


- **Creates a single source of truth:** Processes, systems, and architecture are centralized in secure repositories so your team can easily find and share official documentation instantly.
- **Streamlines stakeholder review:** Automated workflows enable teams to review, comment on, and approve documentation before it goes live, cutting out delays and driving accountability.
- **Deploys updates with a single click:** Asset libraries enable process owners to automatically roll out changes across many documents at once.
- **Drives compliance and consistency:** Clear standards for visualizing your workflows reduce operational risk and keep your entire organization aligned.

## Actions


When documenting complex processes, information often gets scattered across multiple documents and diagrams, leading to confusion and wasted time searching for answers. With[actions](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/15059514038420-Add-an-Action-or-link-to-a-shape-in-Lucidchart) in Lucidchart, you can create a single source of truth, ensuring everyone has the right content, updated in real time.


This feature allows you to use shapes to efficiently perform certain actions, such as toggling on and off layers, applying conditional formatting rules, or sending emails with a few clicks.
Transparent and connected processes will help your organization make more informed decisions, collaborate effectively, and maintain alignment.


## **Conditional formatting**


[Conditional formatting](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/16293796201108-Add-conditional-formatting-in-Lucidchart) allows you to set specific rules that automatically highlight specific elements of your process documentation. By applying specific rules to your diagrams, teams can identify bottlenecks, optimize processes, and streamline their workflows faster. This feature is particularly useful for lengthy or complex documentation, flagging important information and items requiring attention, based on your specific rules. This makes your diagrams more intuitive and actionable.

## Layers


Optimize space and eliminate confusion by leveraging[layers](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/16296419779476-Add-layers-to-a-Lucidchart-document) in your process documentation. Rather than cramming information into one massive diagram, layers allow you to organize your process by phases. This makes it easy to toggle between phases, focusing on relevant details without overwhelming viewers.


Similarly,[hotspots](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/15059514038420-Add-an-Action-or-link-to-a-shape-in-Lucidchart) allow static elements of your diagram to become interactive. You can switch between documents, toggle layers, link to external content, and more.


## Paths


Ever missed a meeting only to find yourself sifting through endless pages of scattered notes? Or, maybe you’re a new hire and need to review your organization’s critical processes. That’s where[Paths](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/14877877180564-Organize-content-with-Frames-in-Lucidspark) come in. Paths provide a structured way to guide readers through your documentation. As the facilitator or the owner of the document, you can create a pathway that is tailored to how you want people to view your content. This eliminates confusion and ensures team members can easily find and navigate important documentation.
