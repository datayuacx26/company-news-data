## Eliminate duplicative data entry with data linking


With[data linking in Lucidchart](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/16493391394068-Link-data-to-a-Lucidchart-document) , you can connect data sets from Google Sheets, Excel, or a CSV file to specific shapes in your diagrams, effectively eliminating the double data entry.


Connecting data to your diagram is simple:


1. Click **File** > **Import Data** , or access data linking from the right Dock in the editor and click **Link Data** .
2. Follow the prompts to select a Google Sheet, CSV, or Excel file.
3. Select the data you'd like to import.


Along with data linking, Lucid also offers data overlay and data refresh so your data is always up to date.


## Populate org charts automatically


Making an[org chart](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/16463330693268-Create-an-org-chart) does not have to be an exercise in patience. Instead of manually rearranging roles, simply upload a Google Sheet, Excel file, or CSV or import data from apps like[BambooHR](https://lucid.co/marketplace/e722e358/bamboo-hr) . Lucidchart will automatically organize each role within a hierarchy.


Follow these steps:


1. Select **Import Data** from the org chart shape library, or click **File** > I **mport Data** > **Org Chart** .
2. Choose a CSV, Google Sheets, Excel file to upload, or copy and paste data directly from a spreadsheet program like Google Sheets or Excel. You can even import data from BambooHR. Once your data is added, you can format it as necessary using preset or custom fields.
3. After you’ve finished formatting, click **Finish** .


While org charts are typically used to visualize company hierarchy, Group View lets you store and visualize employee-specific information—such as phone numbers, locations, or scrum teams. Group View allows you to arrange your org chart by non-hierarchical factors to gain deeper insight into your people.


Once you’ve created or updated your org chart, you can easily share it with the team, allowing anyone to access the information quickly and easily. Automated[org chart creation](https://lucid.co/diagram/org-chart/ai-org-chart-generator) can save significant time and effort when it comes to keeping everyone on the same page.

## Create UML sequence diagrams with ease


Whether you want to instantly generate your layout with Lucid AI or script it yourself using[UML sequence markup](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/16262874090900-Create-a-sequence-diagram-with-UML-markup-in-Lucidchart) , Lucid gives you the flexibility to build professional sequence diagrams in seconds, without the manual hassle of dragging and dropping every single shape.


### With Lucid AI


Skip the manual work entirely and use Lucid AI to build your UML sequence diagram (and other diagrams like flowcharts, ERDs, and mind maps). Simply open the AI prompt bar and select Build a diagram. Then, describe the diagram you want to make by clicking More > Sequence diagram. From there, you’ll be able to add a few more details and press return. Your editable diagram will appear within your document.

### With markup


Lucid’s built-in sequence markup editor will automatically create UML sequence diagrams from text. Here’s how:


1. Create a new Lucidchart document.
2. Click **More Shapes** .
3. Open the UML Sequence shape library.
4. Select **Use Markup** .
5. Enter your markup in the dialog that appears, and you’ll have a professional sequence diagram in seconds.


Our markup follows common industry standards, keeping diagram creation simple and efficient. If you ever need to edit your diagram, simply click **Use Markup** and adjust your text. Once your sequence diagram is generated, customize it with Lucidchart’s easy-to-use formatting capabilities. And if you can’t give up the dragging and dropping, you can always use our UML Sequence shape library.


## Automate entity relationship diagrams by importing or exporting to SQL


Lucid is a powerful[ER diagram tool](https://lucid.co/diagram/erd) that allows you to create a diagram in Lucidchart and export it to the DBMS of your choice. Use our[ERD](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/16471565238292-Create-an-Entity-Relationship-Diagram-in-Lucidchart) shape library, ER diagram import tool, or Lucid AI to build and customize your diagram before exporting it. You can export single tables or an entire page.


To export a single table:


1. Click on the shape and select **Export ERD** from the advanced shape bar.
2. A dialog will appear. Choose your DBMS from the list, and the text field below will change to the correct create statement.


To export your entire page:


1. In the Entity Relationship shape library found in the left toolbox, click the **Export** button.
2. A dialog will appear. Choose your DBMS from the list, and the text field below will change to the correct create statement for the entire page.


You can also use the[Salesforce schema import](https://help.lucid.co/hc/en-us/articles/16471565238292-Create-an-Entity-Relationship-Diagram-in-Lucidchart) to visualize your Salesforce schema as an ER diagram before implementing.

**What is data linking, and how does it help automate my work?**
Data linking connects data sets from Google Sheets, Excel, or CSV files to specific shapes in your diagrams, eliminating duplicate data entry. Your diagrams stay up to date with data overlay and data refresh features.


**How do I create UML sequence diagrams quickly in Lucidchart?**

**Can I export entity relationship diagrams to Lucidchart?**

**What types of diagrams can I generate with Lucid AI?**
Lucid AI can generate flowcharts, UML diagrams, entity relationship diagrams, and more.


**Are Lucidchart's automation features available on free accounts?**
