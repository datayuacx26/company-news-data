# CELEBRATING NATIONAL WOMEN’S HISTORY MONTH: RECOGNIZING THE WOMEN WHO KEEP AMERICA MOVING

March is National Women’s History Month, a time to celebrate the achievements, resilience, and leadership of women across every industry. In transportation, where strength, precision, and grit meet strategy and innovation, women are not just participating, they are leading, shaping, and transforming the future of trucking.


At Marten, we are especially proud to recognize the incredible women across our organization who keep freight moving safely and efficiently every single day.


**Women in Trucking: Driving Change in the Industry**


Women In Trucking Association (WIT) has played a pivotal role in increasing gender diversity across transportation. From professional drivers and dispatchers to safety leaders, executives, and logistics experts, women are expanding their footprint in every segment of the industry.


Trucking has historically been male-dominated, but that narrative is changing. Today, women are:


- Operating equipment with precision and professionalism
- Leading safety and compliance initiatives
- Managing complex supply chains
- Negotiating contracts and driving business growth
- Serving in executive leadership roles


Their impact isn’t incremental — it’s transformational.


**Marten Recognized as a Top Place for Women to Work in 2025**


We are proud that Marten has been recognized as a Top Company for Women to Work for in Transportation in 2025 by the Women In Trucking Association.


This recognition reflects more than a title, it represents our culture.


At Marten, we are committed to:


- Providing equal opportunity for advancement
- Supporting work-life balance
- Investing in professional development
- Creating safe, respectful workplaces
- Recognizing leadership and performance at every level


From our professional drivers to operations, safety, finance, sales, and executive leadership, women across Marten play a critical role in our success. Their expertise, dedication, and leadership strengthen our organization and elevate the service we provide to our customers.


**Honoring the Legacy and Building the Future**


National Women’s History Month reminds us that progress is built on courage, the courage to step into new roles, challenge norms, lead teams, and raise standards.


The women of Marten embody that spirit every day.


As we celebrate this month, we extend our gratitude to:


- The women who paved the way in transportation
- The women leading today
- The next generation who will redefine what’s possible


Transportation is evolving. Leadership is evolving. And at Marten, opportunity is open to all who are ready to drive forward.


To the women of Marten and to women across the trucking industry, thank you for your strength, professionalism, and the example you set.


**Happy National Women’s History Month.**
