# DRIVING A GREENER FUTURE: MARTEN’S COMMITMENT TO SUSTAINABILITY

**Our journey spans from innovative solutions to sustainable milestones, ensuring a greener future in every mile** . At Marten, sustainability is built into everyday decisions. Progress happens through hundreds of operational choices made across our fleet, facilities, and network, all guided by a steadfast commitment to a greener environment and being a responsible partner for our customers and the communities in which we live. Our customers benefit from a carrier that invests in its people, equipment, and infrastructure with the future in mind.


**Sustainability in Action.** Real sustainability shows up in how work gets done. Over the past year, Marten made measurable improvements by focusing on the fundamentals, such as refining operations, eliminating waste, and making smarter use of resources already in place. For example, teams across the organization worked to reduce unnecessary fuel consumption by tightening operational practices and improving how equipment is utilized day-to-day.


**Smarter Fleet Decisions, Every Day.** Rather than relying on a single solution, Marten focused on incremental improvements across the fleet. This included more disciplined equipment deployment, better alignment between freight characteristics and equipment, and thoughtful lifecycle management of tractors and trailers. Over time, these decisions added up to a fleet that moves freight more efficiently while reducing environmental impact, without compromising service or reliability.


**Excellence Beyond the Fleet.** Sustainability improvements also took place off the road. Marten optimizes opportunities to reduce energy usage through operational adjustments, improved scheduling, smart purchasing, and facility-level efficiencies. Since 2017, Marten has completed 18 solar projects, contributing to reduced purchased electricity demand and supporting our broader sustainability strategy. Our teams are focused on responsible resource management, including water conservation and used oil recycling practices that help reduce waste and support cleaner operations. These practical changes lowered electricity and resource consumption while maintaining productivity and supporting our day-to-day work environments, proving that sustainability and operational excellence go hand in hand.


**Responsible Capacity, Thoughtfully Managed.** Marten’s sustainability efforts extend beyond company-owned assets. Through more intentional planning and oversight of transportation services, we reduced emissions tied to network by focusing on right-sizing solutions and eliminating inefficiencies. Marten is committed to improved environmental performance while continuing to deliver dependable capacity for our customers.


**Measuring What Matters.** Every improvement is tracked, reviewed, and validated against recognized standards. Marten’s sustainability progress is measured using globally accepted greenhouse gas accounting frameworks, ensuring our results are credible, repeatable, and transparent.


**Looking Forward.** Our journey spans from innovative solutions to sustainable milestones, ensuring a greener future in every mile. The progress we’ve made reinforces a simple truth: sustainability isn’t about one big move, it’s about doing the right things consistently, over time.


**STEADFAST COMMITMENT TO A GREENER ENVIRONMENT. At Marten, sustainability is how we operate today and how we prepare for tomorrow.**
