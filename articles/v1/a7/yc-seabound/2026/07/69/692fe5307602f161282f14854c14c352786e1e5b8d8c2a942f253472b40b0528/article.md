# Cracking the code on maritime carbon capture

To complete the installation, we moved the Seabound team to a shipyard in Yalova, Turkey, where the Sounion Trader was undergoing routine maintenance. Over the course of several weeks, we collaborated with the shipyard and the ship’s crew to install our equipment and connect it to one of the ship’s auxiliary engines. We also worked with the American Bureau of Shipping and Lloyd’s Register to gain approval for operating the system onboard and to conduct a detailed risk assessment, respectively. When the ship set sail for the Middle East, we joined the crew for two months, taking turns to run numerous carbon capture trials and gain hands-on experience operating at sea. With each test, we managed to increase the carbon capture rate, culminating in 78% efficiency and ~1 ton of CO2 captured per day.
