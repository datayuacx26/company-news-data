# The Marzetti Company Named a 2026-2027 Best Company to Work For by U.S. News & World Report

To determine its ratings, *U.S. News* analyzed employee perceptions across six factors important to employee well-being. The Marzetti Company earned strong scores in all six areas: quality of pay and benefits; work-life balance and flexibility; job and company stability; physical and psychological comfort; belongingness and esteem; and career opportunities and professional development.


**About U.S. News & World Report’s Best Companies to Work For**


The *U.S. News & World Report* Best Companies to Work For ratings are based on an in-depth analysis of publicly available data including employee reviews, court records, financial strength, and governance metrics. Companies do not submit data, apply, or pay a fee to be considered.


To earn a Best Companies to Work For designation, organizations must score well above average nationally, in its industry, and/or in its region.


Additional information about the Best Companies to Work For rankings can be found at[https://careers.usnews.com/companies/rankings](https://careers.usnews.com/companies/rankings) .
