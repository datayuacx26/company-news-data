# The Marzetti Company Launches Protein Ranch Dressing and Dip

The Marzetti Company announced today the launch of[Marzetti® Protein Ranch](https://marzetti.com/products/#c=protein-ranch) , a first-to-category innovation that delivers the great taste consumers expect from Marzetti Ranch while offering 3-4 grams of high-quality milk protein per serving and fewer calories than Marzetti’s other Ranch offerings.


The new line includes[Protein Ranch Dressing (13 oz. bottle)](https://marzetti.com/products/protein-ranch-dressing/) ,[Protein Ranch Veggie Dip (12 oz. tub)](https://marzetti.com/products/protein-ranch-veggie-dip/) , and[Protein Ranch Veggie Dip Snack Packs](https://marzetti.com/products/protein-ranch-veggie-dip-singles/) .
