# The Marzetti Company Launches New Buffalo Wild Wings-Branded Sauce Flavors and Expands Distribution of Hot Sauces

As part of its exclusive licensing agreement with Buffalo Wild Wings®, The Marzetti Company has expanded its lineup of Buffalo Wild Wings sauces, including new Honey Garlic and General Tso’s.


Honey Garlic sauce, a fan-favorite in restaurants, is now available in retail stores nationwide. General Tso’s – made with soy sauce, ginger, and a touch of sweetness – is available exclusively at Walmart stores.


Additionally, Marzetti has expanded distribution of Buffalo Wild Wings hot sauces, which were introduced in Summer 2025 as an Amazon exclusive. Perfect for spicing up a variety of dishes, the hot sauces are made with the bold, innovative flavors consumers expect from the Buffalo Wild Wings brand. Original, Chipotle, and Habanero are available in grocery stores nationwide, while Blazin’® remains an Amazon exclusive.


**About Buffalo Wild Wings:**


Buffalo Wild Wings, founded in 1982, is the largest sports bar brand in the United States, with more than 1,300 restaurants in nine global markets and a rapidly growing off-premise business through Buffalo Wild Wings GO®. Buffalo Wild Wings is part of the Inspire Brands family of restaurants.
