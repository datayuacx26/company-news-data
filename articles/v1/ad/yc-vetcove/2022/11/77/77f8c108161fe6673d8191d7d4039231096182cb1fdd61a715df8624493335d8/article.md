### Inventory Tip 💡


A day in the life of a veterinary clinic is certainly a busy one! Something we hear often is, “I keep getting interrupted while I’m trying to manage inventory.” We have a tip for you!


“It’s really important to have uninterrupted time to manage your inventory. My favorite little tip is to wear a yellow safety vest so it signifies in a silly way to not interrupt you and let you focus on your inventory tasks.”
