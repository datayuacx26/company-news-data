### Your weekly source for manufacturer promotions, recalls, and industry news from Vetcove!


### ✨Employee Spotlight✨


The Vetcove Employee Spotlight is back, and this week it’s shining on ✨Katie Steininger✨ one of our Partner Account Managers! Take a peek below to get to know Katie!


Q: What inspired you to start working within the veterinary industry?🩺🐾


A: I was one of the many people who got a dog during COVID. It quickly became apparent to me how much stress the veterinary industry is under. The fact that Vetcove can ease some of that pressure is great to see!


Q: Are you a dog person, cat person, or other?🐶🐱


A: Mostly dog, but I always have a soft spot for cats


Q: If you could say one thing to a hospital that doesn’t use Vetcove yet, what would that be?


A: Vetcove is free and saves you time and money. It’s a win-win!🎉


Thank you Katie for all the work you do with our wonderful partners!
