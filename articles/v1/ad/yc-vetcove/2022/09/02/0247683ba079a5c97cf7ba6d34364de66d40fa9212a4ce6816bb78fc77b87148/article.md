## 💡 Vetcove Tip💡


Psst… Here’s a neat little Vetcove Tip!


Use the price change icons as a reminder to update item prices in your PIMS! This will ensure you always have the correct markup in place. 👍


❓🤔 *What are Vetcove’s price change icons?*


This icon will appear on a listing if the price has changed since the last time you purchased it.


The *green graph* will appear if the item is cheaper than the last time you bought it.


Press enter or click to view image in full size


The *red graph* will appear if it is more expensive than the last time you bought it (indicating you may want to revisit the price you’re selling this item to clients for to ensure you still have enough of a mark-up in place).
