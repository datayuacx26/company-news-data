# Respiratory Risk Report


### Week ending in August 1, 2026


[Marisa Donnelly, PhD](https://substack.com/@marisadonnelly)


Aug 07, 2026


Biobot’s national wastewater network continues to show SARS-CoV-2 increasing across all four U.S. regions, with the biggest increases seen in the West. The summer Covid-19 wave continues to grow.


The rest of the respiratory virus picture is quiet. RSV, influenza A, influenza B, and HMPV are all circulating at very low levels, with activity either stable or declining.


**Bottom line**


It’s a good time to be a little more aware, especially if you’re traveling, heading to a crowded indoor event, or spending time with someone at higher risk of severe illness, including older adults, babies, pregnant people, and people who are immunocompromised.


Thankfully, flu, RSV, and HMPV activity remains low.
