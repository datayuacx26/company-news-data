# COVID-19, Influenza, and RSV Wastewater Monitoring in the U.S. | Week of May 9, 2026

### Summary: Week of 5/9/26


Biobot’s nationwide network shows that respiratory virus activity is easing across the U.S. Wastewater trends show COVID-19, influenza A and B, RSV, and HMPV all decreasing nationally, with the same downward pattern seen across every region: the South, Midwest, Northeast, and West.


**The Bottom Line:** It’s great news that we continue to move into a lower-risk period. Still, respiratory viruses have not disappeared—RSV, HMPV, and influenza B are still circulating at low levels in some places. People at higher risk may want to continue using layered protection when needed, especially in crowded indoor spaces or around someone who is sick.
