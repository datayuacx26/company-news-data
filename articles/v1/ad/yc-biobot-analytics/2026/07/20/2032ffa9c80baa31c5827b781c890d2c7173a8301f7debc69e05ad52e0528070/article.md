# Respiratory Risk Report

# Respiratory Risk Report


### Week ending in June 27, 2026


[Marisa Donnelly, PhD](https://substack.com/@marisadonnelly)


Jul 08, 2026


Biobot’s national wastewater network recently picked up an increase in RSV across all four U.S. regions. While less common, sporadic RSV activity can happen outside the usual fall and winter season.


For COVID-19, the South and West each saw small recent upticks, but levels have since declined and are holding steady. We aren’t seeing any major summer wave signals yet.


Other respiratory viruses, including influenza A, influenza B, and HMPV, remain low, and either steady or declining.


**Bottom Line**


Overall, respiratory virus risk remains low.


We are seeing a small RSV bump, but this is not unusual for summer and, at this point, doesn’t point to a broader surge. COVID-19 is also quiet nationally after a few regional blips.


This is still a good time to pay attention to what’s happening locally, especially if you’re traveling, heading to a large indoor event, or spending time with babies, older adults, pregnant people, or someone who is immunocompromised.
