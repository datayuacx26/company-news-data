# Respiratory Risk Report


### Week ending in May 30, 2026


[Marisa Donnelly, PhD](https://substack.com/@marisadonnelly)


Jun 05, 2026


Biobot’s wastewater network shows that respiratory virus activity is still quiet across the country. COVID-19, flu A & B, RSV, and HMPV are all at very low levels nationally. Across regions, the story is the same: these viruses are either declining or holding steady at very low levels.


**Bottom Line**


We’re in a low-risk respiratory virus period. That’s welcome news as we head into a summer with more travel, gatherings, and big events like the World Cup. Respiratory viruses haven’t disappeared, but right now the overall risk picture is calm.


For summer travel and crowded events, the usual tools still help: stay home when sick, test if you have symptoms, and consider a mask in crowded indoor spaces if you’re high-risk or trying to avoid getting sick.
