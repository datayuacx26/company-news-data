# Introducing Supernova 3.0 - Where Vibes Meet Professional Product Development

Today, 7 years after starting our journey to make product development fast and painless for everyone, we're unbelievably proud to announce Supernova 3.0 - the world’s first tool where you can go from idea → exploration → alignment → specs → delivery, all while keeping features you create.


## “From idea to prod in seconds”, said no PM ever.


Today's vibe-coding tools all hype the same fantasy: start from zero and whip up a website, mobile app, or SaaS tool. But real product teams don't work that way—they iterate on live products, leverage existing design and code libraries, refine features as they grow complex, and collaborate to advance ongoing projects. No tool out there delivers this end-to-end experience, which is why we spent the past year building Supernova 3.0—the solution we craved ourselves—to make it real.


Let's break it down stage by stage: how Supernova 3.0 lets you ship your next feature 10x faster, hassle-free.


## From Raw Ideas to Explorations


Dive into quick horizontal iteration with Supernova's AI-powered ideation, and explore different directions before diving deeper. Supernova agents is specifically made for building features - and it brings great taste to the table, generating structured outlines, thinking about your input deeply before creating the result and turning vague vibes into solid concepts that align with your existing product.


Supernova 3.0 also introduces "product contexts"—reusable setups tied to your design and code libraries, brand guidelines or even formalized design systems, if you have them. With product context, every feature is visually consistent, so you don’t spend half of the prompts just tweaking designs or providing info about the product you're building for .


This gets used internally to a great extent - both our design system and product platform have very distinct feels - so two different product contexts are maintained, and product teams just pick from one of them when they start a new exploration.


## From Explorations to Team and Stakeholder Alignment


Threading, branching, and merging keep the back-and-forth smooth—explore ideas in parallel without conflicts, then blend the best into the main flow. It's like Slack for product work: everything's multiplayer, with real-time visibility so everyone sees the same updates.


Bring in multiple disciplines—PMs, designers, engineers—working simultaneously on the same project, refining different aspects without stepping on toes. Easy sharing via one-click invites with smart permissions lets stakeholders view, comment, or even build on prototypes.


Organize multiple features in one project, like "New Authentication" with sub-features for sign-in, sign-up, and forgot password. This sets up seamless transitions to specs, keeping complex projects structured.


## From Alignment to Product Specs


Once aligned, Supernova automates spec creation. AI generates structured PRDs directly from prototypes and inputs, pulling in details like requirements, flows, and guidelines—saving hours of manual writing while keeping everything synced as iterations evolve.


You can also go from prototype, to PRD, to prototype, to PRD.. as many times as you need, before you are ready with the outcome.


## From Specs to Developers — and everyone else


Export is effortless with the recently announced MCP-powered integration, pushing code straight to tools like VS Code or Cursor. Align with your real systems for implementable outputs, or use other export methods to bridge design and dev without rework—faster releases, fewer bugs. Or use our MCP server and connect your documents to ChatGPT or Claude, and generate tickets directly to your project management tools such as Linear or Jira.
