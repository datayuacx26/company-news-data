# Supernova Portal: Unlock Design System Knowledge, Anytime, Anywhere

For years, design systems have promised consistency, speed, and alignment across teams. But many organizations have learned the hard truth: **documentation alone isn’t enough** .


Documentation is essential for best practices, do’s and don’ts, and edge cases. But when engineers or designers just want to find a token, check a component, or confirm a usage pattern, digging through pages of content creates bottlenecks. Design system managers become gatekeepers, answering the same questions again and again.


The result? Slower delivery, frustrated teams, and under-realized value from the design system.


That’s the problem Supernova Portal was built to solve.


## **What Is Supernova Portal?**


Supernova Portal is a **brand-new, standalone platform** — available on web and desktop — that transforms your design system into a living, searchable hub for your entire product team.


Instead of relying solely on documentation, Portal provides:


- **Instant access to design system data** : tokens, components, Storybook stories, assets, and more.
- **AI-powered answers** to everyday questions.
- **Interactive playgrounds** to explore and test entities.
- **Always-up-to-date content** , synced automatically from your design and code sources.


Setup takes minutes, and once enabled, **every team member can use Portal — for free** . AI queries simply use your existing workspace credits.


## Why it matters, and who it helps


We’re entering a new era of design system adoption — one where teams need **action, not just knowledge** .


- **Design system managers** gain back time by removing repetitive Q&A.
- **Designers and engineers** get the autonomy to self-serve the answers they need, when they need them.
- **Leaders** see faster time-to-market, stronger adoption, and better ROI from their design systems.


This shift isn’t just about efficiency. It’s about making the design system a **living product** — one that fuels day-to-day decision-making and accelerates delivery at scale.


## Get started with Portal


Supernova Portal isn’t just a new feature. It’s a new way to think about design systems: **from static documentation to actionable, intelligent, team-wide hubs** .


The future of design systems is about more than building consistency — it’s about unlocking speed, autonomy, and clarity for every product team. Portal is the first step.
