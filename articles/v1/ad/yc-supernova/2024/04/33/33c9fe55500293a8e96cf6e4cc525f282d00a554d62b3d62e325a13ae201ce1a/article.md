# Announcing Supernova's ISO 27001 Certification

Supernova is thrilled to announce that we have successfully achieved ISO 27001 certification, the global standard that specifies the requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an information security management system (ISMS). This certification is testament to our unwavering commitment to data security and protection of sensitive information.


The ISO 27001 standard is developed by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) and the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC). It outlines a risk management process involving people, processes, and IT systems, thereby providing a holistic approach to information security.


This important achievement was made possible through a rigorous audit conducted by the esteemed certification body, United Registrar Systems. The audit confirms Supernova’s compliance with the highest international standards of data security, bolstering our promise to safeguard our customer's data with the utmost diligence.


Obtaining the ISO 27001 certification is not only a reflection of Supernova’s dedication to secure data management but also marks our commitment to continuous improvement in our security practices. To achieve this certification, Supernova undertook several key initiatives:


- Evaluating and mitigating risks to ensure the security and resilience of our operations.
- Implementing comprehensive security controls tailored to the needs of our services and customer data.
- Regularly reviewing and updating our security practices in accordance with international standards.
- Training and engaging our employees to enhance awareness and compliance with our ISMS.


In addition to our ISO 27001 certification, Supernova also complies with the System and Organization Controls (SOC) 2 Type II examination, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Our platform also incorporates advanced security features like Single Sign-On (SSO) and supports integration with Okta, reinforcing the security of our user authentication processes.


At Supernova, we view security as an ongoing journey. We are committed to continuously advancing our security measures to meet and exceed the evolving challenges and needs of our global customer base.
