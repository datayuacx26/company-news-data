# Announcing our SOC 2 Type II Certification

**Supernova** is proud to announce that we have successfully completed the System and Organization Controls (SOC) 2 Type II examination, reaffirming our unwavering commitment to information security and data protection. Developed by the American Institute of CPAs (AICPA), SOC 2 defines rigorous criteria for managing customer data based on five essential "trust service principles" — security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy.


Conducted by the independent auditor, Insight Assurance, the comprehensive examination validates Supernova's adherence to the highest standards of data security, availability, and confidentiality set forth by the AICPA. By undergoing this examination, Supernova provides our customers with an assurance that their data and sensitive information are safeguarded with the utmost care.


The SOC 2 certification not only demonstrates Supernova's dedication to protecting customer data today but also signifies its commitment to maintaining robust security standards for the future. Through a meticulous process, Supernova achieved SOC 2 certification by:


- Assessing the design and operating effectiveness of Supernova's controls
- Conducting thorough examinations of vendor and third-party security
- Establishing comprehensive policies to ensure ongoing protection for customers and employees


Moreover, at Supernova, we take great pride in our robust security posture, which goes beyond the SOC 2 certification. We are fully compliant with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) alongside General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), ensuring that your data is protected according to the highest privacy standards across every region. Additionally, we have implemented Single Sign-On (SSO) functionality, further enhancing the security of your Supernova experience.


To ensure seamless and secure access to our platform, we also support Okta integration, a leading identity provider. This integration enables you to enjoy a smooth sign-in process while adding an extra layer of protection to your account. With Supernova and Okta working together, you can have peace of mind knowing that your data is in safe hands.


At Supernova, we believe that security is a journey, and we are committed to continually improving our practices to meet the evolving needs of design systems.
