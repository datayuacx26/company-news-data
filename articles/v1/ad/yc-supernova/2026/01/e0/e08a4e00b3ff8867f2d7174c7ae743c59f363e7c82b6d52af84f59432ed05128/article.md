# January 2026 Product Updates: AI Prototyping Agent & Code Components

We're kicking off 2026 with improvements to AI-powered prototyping, real code component integration, and enhanced design system management. This release makes prototyping faster and more collaborative for design system teams. Here’s what’s new:


### **Prototyping with your code components**


Prototyping with real code components lets teams build high-fidelity prototypes that look and feel exactly like your product.


The outcome is genuinely game-changing: prototypes are good enough for validation, stakeholder alignment, and engineering handoff. Instead of rebuilding designs from scratch, you work with the same components your developers use in production, ensuring pixel-perfect accuracy and consistent behavior.


Currently available in beta. Interested?[Request a demo](https://www.supernova.io/request-a-demo) today.


### **Iteration status and Agent improvements**


Our **Prototyping Agent is now easier to follow, less distracting, and more informative** , helping you stay in the flow while building prototypes.


#### **Step Previews**


The agent summarizes each action before executing, so you can catch misunderstandings early.


Instead of watching the agent work and hoping it's on the right track, you see exactly what it plans to do next. This preview gives you a chance to course-correct before wasted effort, making the entire prototyping process more efficient and collaborative.


#### **Always-Visible Status**


Clear indicators show what stage the agent is in, even during longer builds.


No more wondering if the agent is stuck or still working. The persistent status display keeps you informed throughout the entire process, whether you're building a simple prototype or a complex multi-screen flow.


#### **Jump to Latest**


Quickly return to the most recent agent activity without scrolling.


When working on complex prototypes with dozens of iterations, finding the latest update used to mean endless scrolling. Now you can instantly jump back to where the action is happening.


👉 *Discover all*[Agent improvements](https://learn.supernova.io/latest/releases/january-2026/iteration-status-and-agent-improvements-Y9m1ljEY-Y9m1ljEY)


### **Public prototype sharing**


Share your prototypes with a single, stable link. No sign-in required. Whether you're sharing with teammates, clients, or stakeholders, getting feedback is now faster and simpler.


Previously, every reviewer needed a Supernova account just to view a prototype. That friction slowed down feedback loops and made external collaboration unnecessarily complicated. Now anyone with the link can view and interact with your prototypes immediately.


👉 *Learn how*[public sharing](https://learn.supernova.io/latest/releases/january-2026/publish-prototype-to-web-NPFXU2Iq) *works*


### **Improved Spreadsheet Experience for Tokens and Components**


Managing large design systems is now **faster and more reliable** thanks to the redesigned spreadsheet.


The new architecture handles hundreds or thousands of design tokens without lag, making bulk edits and system-wide updates significantly smoother. Load times are faster, interactions feel more responsive, and you can finally manage enterprise-scale design systems without performance bottlenecks.
