# You don’t need more tools. Your current setup needs to get smarter.

We built Supernova Create, our AI prototyping platform, because we saw something powerful: when **simple prompting meets deep knowledge** of how your products are built — design, code, and the rules that govern them — prototyping becomes genuinely useful instead of disconnected.


That bet was correct. **Our design system data layer made every prototype more consistent** , more production-aware, and less prone to drift than anything else available at the time. Deep context changes everything.


But as we worked with teams building at scale, a clearer reality emerged.


Most **organizations aren’t looking for another specialized tool** . They’ve already standardized on Claude, Codex, Cursor, and similar platforms. What they need is for the output of those tools to land cleanly in production — with the right context, proper governance, and without the usual handoff chaos or the high costs of tokenmaxxing.


We realize now that the most valuable thing we built wasn’t the prototyping platform itself. It was the **engine that understands how products are built** in your organization. By generalizing it, we can now give that same understanding to every tool in your existing stack — without asking anyone to switch.


### Expanding what we already believe in


Design systems are having their moment. More teams than ever see that serious investment in the system holding their product knowledge is the only way to keep quality, consistency, and speed as they scale. **Those who treat it as infrastructure are pulling ahead** .


We want Supernova to be the platform that makes that investment pay off for every team.


We’re taking the deep understanding we built — design decisions, code patterns, rules, history — and making it available wherever your teams already work. **Supernova quietly supplies the right knowledge to the agents and platforms they use** , captures when something doesn’t land well so the knowledge can improve, and gives managers control over what reaches which teams. Context stays governed, costs stay lower, noise stays out.


**The design system remains the foundation** . What changes is how that foundation reaches the rest of the organization and keeps getting sharper with every use.


This is how **design systems become truly enabling** . And this is what makes me the most excited about the glorious future the design systems and teams building them are marching towards.


### The best results rarely come from the original plan


The best teams experiment, learn from what doesn’t work, and turn those lessons into something more valuable than the original idea.


We tried something ambitious with Supernova Create. It didn’t become the product we expected, but it **showed us where the real leverage sits** . So we took the strongest parts of what we built and redirected them toward what teams need right now.


**That’s what we’re doing here.**


Your agents won’t get smarter by themselves. With Supernova, they will.


— Jiri, CEO, Supernova.io
