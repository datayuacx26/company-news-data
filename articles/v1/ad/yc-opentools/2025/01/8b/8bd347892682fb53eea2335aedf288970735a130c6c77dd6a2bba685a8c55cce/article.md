## Expert Opinions on the O3 Models


OpenAI's[development](https://opentools.ai/categories/development) of the o3 and o3 mini models has generated a whirlwind of expert opinions from across the[artificial intelligence](https://opentools.ai/categories/artificial-intelligence) community. François Chollet, renowned for creating the[ARC](https://opentools.ai/tools/arc) -AGI benchmark, pointed out some of the limitations of these models. He appreciated the high compute performance ratings but noted that the models still falter on tasks humans find trivial, questioning the models' alignment with human intelligence. Chollet highlights the necessity for more rigorous benchmarks to measure AI advancements accurately.


In contrast, Tamay Besiroglu from EpochAI expressed astonishment at the swift progress made with the o3 model, especially with its performance on the Frontier Math benchmark ahead of expectations. This unexpected advancement signifies the model's capabilities, yet it also raises questions about the directional pace of AI[development](https://opentools.ai/categories/development) .


Elvis Saravia provided a tempered view of the advances indicated by the o3 model, cautioning against the excessive hype surrounding its capabilities. Saravia made it clear that while the o3 model signifies considerable progress, it is not the herald of artificial general intelligence (AGI) or a so‑called singularity moment that some might expect.


Furthermore, OpenAI's team of AI safety researchers stressed the importance of integrating safety protocols directly into the model's reasoning processes. This 'deliberative alignment' approach underscores a growing trend among AI developers to prioritize safety as highly as capability advancements, a sentiment echoed in the larger AI community.
