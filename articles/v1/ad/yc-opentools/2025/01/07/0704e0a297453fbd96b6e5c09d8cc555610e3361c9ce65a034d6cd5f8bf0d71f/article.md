## Neural Processing Units: Efficiency and Limitations


Neural Processing Units (NPUs) have emerged as a significant innovation in the field of artificial intelligence, offering a promising avenue for enhancing the efficiency and speed of AI computations. Unlike traditional CPUs, which are designed for general‑purpose processing, NPUs are specialized microprocessors built specifically to handle the vast computations required in AI tasks. This specificity allows NPUs to process data more rapidly and efficiently, significantly reducing the time and energy consumed for AI computations and thus enhancing performance metrics. The adoption of NPUs in AI‑centric tasks is becoming more prevalent as companies strive to optimize their technological infrastructure to keep up with massive datasets and complex models that define modern AI applications.
