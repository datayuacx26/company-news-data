# Leading Data Management Companies for Data Security


The American data center outsourcing market is predicted to continue growing at a CAGR of 6.2% until 2030. So, as more and more businesses integrate cloud‑based operations, AI‑based services, and big data analytics, they are turning to outsourcing to process their data.


Are you looking for a company to handle your data but concerned about its level of security? In this listing, we’ve picked the top 5 data management agencies that:


- Feature ISO 27001 certifications and meet international security standards.
- Have been on the market for around 20 years.
- Worked with big brands in different industries.


Scroll down to find the company that’ll guarantee your data’s protection.


## N‑iX


N‑iX is one of the best[data management companies](https://www.n-ix.com/trusted-data-management-companies/) for enterprises and mid‑sized businesses. The company has been on the market for 23 years, and its professionals work across 25 countries in Europe, the Americas, and India. N‑iX itself is headquartered in Malta and has offices in Poland, Ukraine, Romania, and Bulgaria. A team of 200 data experts handles all stages of the data lifecycle, including strategy, platform design, governance, integration, and analytics.


N‑iX provides various services to help businesses organize data:


- Data governance
- Data lake consulting
- Data warehouse consulting services
- Business intelligence
- Data migration
- Data engineering services
- Data platform modernization


N‑iX backs this with real partnerships too, working alongside AWS, Microsoft, Snowflake, and SAP to build systems that scale without ballooning costs. For example, for a global in‑flight connectivity provider, N‑iX moved legacy on‑premise systems to AWS and built a unified cloud platform with predictive analytics baked in. For a global stock image and media platform, the team modernized an old Hadoop‑based architecture to handle scalable data ingestion.


N‑iX has worked with Siemens, eBay, Bosch, and other big companies. The company is ISO 9001 and ISO 27001 certified and GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI DSS compliant. Also, ISG named N‑iX a Rising Star in Data Engineering for the UK market, and CRN has repeatedly listed the company among leading solution providers in North America.


## BairesDev


BairesDev is a data management firm that can build a full team within two weeks. It employs the top 1% of tech talent in Latin America, who operate in time zones that match US business hours. The company has worked with many clients, including Google, Pinterest, Adobe, and Square.


The service list covers nearly every angle of data work:


- Data architecture design
- Database development
- Data governance
- Data warehousing with ETL services
- Data migration and transformation
- Data security & compliance auditing
- Data backup & disaster recovery planning


The agency conducts compliance audits, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and enforces data privacy standards using IBM Guardium, Collibra, and OneTrust. This process is simple: requirements call, team formation, and then hands‑on delivery with full client oversight. BairesDev is a dependable partner for companies that need their data systems modernized without months of delay by leaning on its combination of fast staffing, broad technical range, and serious attention to compliance.


## Globant


Globant is the company that runs its data management services through the Data & AI Studio. It’s one of several "studios" the company organizes around specific tech domains. Globant has more than 30,000 employees spread across 36 countries, and roughly 1,100 of them focus specifically on data engineering and strategy, working out of North America, Europe, Asia, and Latin America. That's a real depth of specialized talent, not just a side offering bolted onto broader software work.


Its services are built to work with major cloud providers like AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud and include:


- Data strategy
- Data governance
- Data platform design
- Advanced analytics


Globant has previously provided data governance services for Nissan and Coca‑Cola with the focus on something less typical in this sector: developing a real “data culture” within client firms, not merely providing technical infrastructure. That means helping teams verify data quality, catch bias early, and keep governance structures alive long after a project wraps up.


## Itransition


Itransition is a global company that has focused for more than 15 of those years specifically on data management and business intelligence. The company treats data less like a technical chore and more like something to build a solid foundation around. It provides such services as preparation, architecture and modeling, storage, integration, migration, governance, and security. Besides, clients get to choose from three engagement models:


- Full end‑to‑end implementation
- Dedicated team for specific stages
- Team augmentation


Itransition is ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 certified and complies with GDPR and CCPA. The software stack includes Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, Power BI, Tableau, and dozens more solutions for integration and security.


## ScienceSoft


ScienceSoft is another data management company that has been working in data since 1989. That’s why it has an extremely professional team, with specialists such as business analysts, solution architects, data engineers, and developers, each with 12 to 27 years of experience.


The company covers a wide range of services:


- Data governance
- Architecture and integration
- Quality management
- Storage
- Master data management
- Metadata management
- Security
- Data migration


ScienceSoft has successfully delivered 4,200+ projects for over 1,400 customers. It holds Microsoft Solutions Partner status for Data & AI, alongside an AWS partnership since 2017.


## Conclusion


When you’re choosing between data management services provided by various companies, ask each vendor to walk you through a data breach they actually had and how they fixed it. Vague answers are a red flag, while specific, slightly embarrassing details mean real experience. Also, don't skip the boring stuff and ask who owns your data schemas after the contract ends. That question alone has saved companies from painful vendor lock‑in.
