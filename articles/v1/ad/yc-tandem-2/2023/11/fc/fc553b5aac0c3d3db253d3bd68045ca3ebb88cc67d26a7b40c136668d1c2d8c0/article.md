# **Plotting Your NYC Startup Success: Crucial Factors When Leasing Office Space**


So, you're at that exciting stage of launching your small startup in the bustling streets of New York City. Amidst the dreams and visions, there's a critical decision on the horizon: where to set up your office. Don't worry; we've got your back.


Let's dive into the essential considerations every savvy entrepreneur should keep in mind when embarking on the journey of signing that office lease in the Big Apple.


# 1. Budgeting: Balancing Ambition with Affordability


Determine your budget early on. While it's tempting to aim high, make sure your lease aligns with your financial capabilities. The biggest oversight we see is people not factoring in all costs, including utilities, supplies, office management, and so on. These things can add up, depending on the size of your team and where you're located. Be sure to ask the landlord or master tenant how utilities and other expenses will be handled so you aren't caught off guard.


# 2. **Proximity Matters: Location, Location, Location**


Assess where your team and / or clients are predominantly located. Proximity can make or break a deal, for customers and employees alike. If your clients are scattered across the city, a central location might be ideal. If they're concentrated in specific neighborhoods, consider setting up shop closer to them. Regardless, the closer you can be to the subway, the better. For younger, hybrid teams, many are choosing to be close to the L line as more people move from Manhattan to Brooklyn neighborhoods like Williamsburg.


# 3. **Scaling Flexibly: Navigating the Growth Landscape**


Startup life is dynamic, and so should be your lease. Look for flexibility in terms of scaling up or down. Opt for leases that accommodate your growth without locking you into rigid commitments. Most traditional leases will lock you in to a 3-5 year term, while coworking and shared office space options - including Tandem - will offer more flexibility in month-to-month pricing.


# 4. **Amenities and Atmosphere: Crafting Your Workspace Culture**


Identify the amenities that align with your company culture and your employees' needs. Whether it's a creative coworking space in Brooklyn, a bustling hub in Midtown, or the financial flair of FiDi, choose a space that enhances your team's productivity and well-being.


# 5. **Tech and Connectivity: Powering Your Digital Presence**


In the digital age, a robust tech infrastructure is non-negotiable. Ensure the office space is equipped with reliable internet, tech support, and any other essential digital amenities crucial for your business operations. Plug-and-play spaces are becoming increasingly common as teams look for ready-to-go solutions that include internet, monitors, desks, and all conference room needs for hybrid work setups. Check out some spaces with those options on Tandem.


Leasing an office space for your small startup is a significant step toward success. By considering these key factors, you're not just signing a lease - you're making an investment in office space as a means of increasing your team's output. So, embrace the entrepreneurial spirit, weigh your options wisely, and let your office become the launchpad for your NYC startup adventure.
