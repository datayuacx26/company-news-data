# NEW: INCON® Optimizer3 Pre-Assembled Cabinet

The new INCON® Optimizer3 pre-assembled cabinet offers a turnkey comprehensive circuit breaker monitoring solution for rapid deployment. With configurable options available to suit specific application and monitoring needs, the Optimizer3 cabinet comes with all components, sensors, and accessories pre-mounted, pre-wired, and factory tested for streamlined installation.


#### HIGHLIGHTS


Rugged, 16 gauge steel construction enclosure


---


IP66 / NEMA 4 ingress protection against water, oil, dust


---


Sensor inputs pre-wired to test-disconnect terminal blocks


---


Integrated circuit breakers for AC / DC power sources


---


Internal cabinet heater prevents condensation


Built-in hooded cabinet vents allow for proper air flow


---


All components pre-wired and factory tested


---


Eliminates on-site drilling or tapping to mount devices


---


Customize to include cabinet / tank heater monitoring and trip coil isolation


### INTEGRATED COMPONENTS


#### AC / DC Circuit Breakers


The cabinet is equipped with integrated circuit breakers designed to manage both AC and DC power sources. Integrated circuit breakers ensure that both AC and DC circuits are protected from overloads, short circuits, and faults, minimizing the risk of electrical fires and damage to equipment.


####
Test-Disconnect Terminal Blocks


Pre-wired terminal blocks reduce installation time and eliminate potential wiring errors, enhancing overall reliability and efficiency of the system. These terminal blocks simplify testing and maintenance by allowing for quick and safe disconnection of sensors without interrupting the entire system.


#### Climate Control


The integrated heater maintains a consistent internal temperature, protecting sensitive electronics from humidity and temperature-related stress, ensuring reliable operation, and reducing the risk of corrosion and electrical faults.
