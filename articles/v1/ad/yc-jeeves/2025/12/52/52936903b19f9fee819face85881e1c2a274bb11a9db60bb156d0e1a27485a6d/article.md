# How to use Apple Pay for business?

There is no denying the global impact of the iPhone, which stands out, especially in the United States, as the favorite brand among consumers. According to recent statistics, the number of iPhone users in the U.S. reached 155 million in 2024, representing a 1.3% increase compared to the 153 million recorded in 2023.


The rise of digital wallets has also gained prominence in recent years, establishing itself as a fast and convenient payment method for users. In addition to personal use, these transactions have expanded to meet corporate expense needs as well. In this context, the Apple Pay digital wallet stands out as one of the most widely used.


## **What is Apple Pay?**


Launched in 2014, Apple Pay is a digital payment system developed by Apple that allows users to make transactions quickly, securely, and without the need for physical cards or cash.


## **Which devices are compatible with Apple Pay wallet?**


The digital wallet works seamlessly with Apple devices that run the iOS operating system, such as iPhone, Apple Watch, iPad, and Mac.


## **Where can I use Apple Pay?**


Apple Pay can be used in a wide variety of places, making it a versatile and convenient payment method.


**In Stores** Apple Pay works at any retailer that accepts contactless payments. Look for the Apple Pay logo or the Contactless Symbol at checkout. It's widely available in:


- Grocery stores
- Restaurants
- Retail shops
- Gas stations
- Pharmacies


**In Apps** Many apps offer Apple Pay as a payment option, allowing you to pay quickly and securely without entering your card details.


**Online** Apple Pay can be used on participating websites when using Safari® on your Mac®, iPhone, or iPad®. Simply look for the Apple Pay button at checkout. It eliminates the need to fill out lengthy payment and shipping forms.


**Public Transportation** In many cities worldwide, you can use Apple Pay to pay for public transport directly by tapping your device at the turnstile or fare reader.


**Internationally** Apple Pay is accepted globally at any location that supports contactless payment terminals, making it a convenient option for travelers.


## **How do I get Apple Pay?**


Apple Pay can be accessed directly through the Apple Wallet™, which comes pre-installed on eligible iOS devices. When setting up a new device, you will have the option to add cards from Jeeves, for example. Alternatively, you can always open the Apple Wallet app to add your cards conveniently at any time.


## **How do I add my Jeeves cards to Apple Pay wallet?**


You can add your Jeeves cards to Apple Wallet directly through the Apple Wallet.


1. Open the **Apple Wallet** app on your iPhone and tap the **“+” (plus)** icon located in the top right corner of the screen.


2. Select “ **Debit or Credit Card”** and press **“Continue.”**


3. The app will prompt you to capture a photo of your card; instead, choose the **“Enter Card Details Manually”** option.


4. Follow the instructions to manually enter your Jeeves card details. After entering the information, tap **“Next”** to proceed.


5. Complete any additional **verification steps** to finalize the process.


6. **Done!** Your Jeeves card is now in your Apple Pay wallet.


For further questions about adding your cards directly through Apple Wallet, please refer to the[Apple Pay instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118219) .


## **Can I add multiple cards to an Apple Pay wallet?**


Yes, depending on your device model, you can add up to 12 cards to your Apple Pay wallet. You can add debit, credit, and other compatible card types, making it easier to access and manage your payment methods directly on your Apple device. Check Apple support for more information about your device.


## **Is Apple Pay safe and secure?**


Yes, Apple Pay is extremely secure, thanks to tokenization technology, which replaces real card numbers with a unique, encrypted code during transactions. This ensures that your financial data is never shared directly with merchants. Additionally, all purchases require authentication via Face ID, Touch ID, or a password, providing an extra layer of protection.


When you add a Jeeves Corporate Card to Apple Pay, the security is further enhanced. The physical card number is not stored on the device or Apple’s servers, and your data is protected by Jeeves' advanced 24/7 risk monitoring and fraud detection systems. This combination of cutting-edge technology and continuous monitoring ensures secure and reliable transactions, ideal for corporate financial needs.


## **What are the main advantages of using Apple Pay?**


Some of the main advantages of Apple Pay include:


**Convenience:** Contactless payments replace the need for physical cards and cash.


**Security:** Multi-layered security features keep your payment information safe from fraud. It also offers biometric authentication and does not share actual card data with merchants.


**Global Compatibility:** It is accepted in millions of locations worldwide that support contactless payments.
