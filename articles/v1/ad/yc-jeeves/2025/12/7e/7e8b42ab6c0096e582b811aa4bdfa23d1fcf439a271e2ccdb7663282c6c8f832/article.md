# How The Flex streamlined payments and reduced reconciliation time by over 70%

[The Flex](https://theflex.global/) provides fully furnished apartments for short and medium-term stays across major global cities, combining the comfort of home with the flexibility of a hotel. With a presence in multiple countries, it streamlines operations across currencies, local vendors, and service providers to deliver a seamless living experience for business and lifestyle travelers.


## **Cross-border payments and reconciliation: The main challenge for The Flex**


For companies like The Flex that operate across borders, managing operations across multiple countries brings complex challenges, from dealing with long payment settlement times to the difficulty of maintaining visibility and control over spending in different currencies, bank accounts, and regulatory environments. Accurately and quickly reconciling expenses, without slowing down operations, is essential for scaling with consistency.


Before adopting Jeeves, managing expenses meant dealing with a patchwork of banking apps, shared cards, and offline reimbursement processes. “It worked when we were in one market, but once we had teams and suppliers in four countries, it became a headache,” says Michael Buggy, Co-Founder of The Flex.


## **Solution: Jeeves delivers smarter financial infrastructure for The Flex**


With global cards, spend limits, and real-time visibility, Jeeves empowers each team to pay what they need, when they need it, while finance maintains oversight across regions. From cleaning teams to SaaS tools, The Flex keeps budgets tight while scaling its furnished short-term apartments business across borders.


Jeeves enables The Flex to:


• Issue local and virtual cards in multiple currencies


• Set role-specific spending limits per city or department


• Categorise property-level expenses with ease


• Track spend by project, market, or team in real-time


The result? A finance system that moves as fast as the operations team, without losing oversight.


## **How Jeeves made the difference for the Flex Results**


Since partnering with Jeeves, The Flex has transformed the way it manages financial operations across multiple markets. By streamlining reconciliation, enabling precise budget control, and simplifying spend oversight during rapid expansion, Jeeves became a key enabler of their growth.


Since adopting Jeeves, The Flex has:


• Reduced monthly reconciliation time by over 70%


• Issued team-specific cards for each new city launch


• Centralised all operational spending into one dashboard


• Maintained tight budget controls while scaling to over 650 tenants placed


• The system is now a standard part of their market expansion model.


### **Built for global teams, not just startups**
