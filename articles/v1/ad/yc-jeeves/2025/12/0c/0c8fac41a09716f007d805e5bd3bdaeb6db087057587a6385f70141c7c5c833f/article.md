Taylor RC is a British company specializing in the sale of remote control cars (RC cars) and parts for this exciting sport that moves a legion of enthusiasts. The company is recognized for its passion for the hobby and for providing high-quality products that meet the needs of both beginners and professionals in the UK and across Europe.

Recently, Taylor RC faced the challenge of optimizing its financial management and simplifying the payment of suppliers of the final products sold, such as cars, controllers, parts, among others, as well as various bills and support services like facilities and maintenance, both in the UK and in other European countries. Additionally, RC Taylor was disappointed with the cashback programs they used due to various restrictions and earning limits. These programs did not provide significant quantitative benefits or savings for the company, which spends substantial amounts on corporate cards for supplier payments. That was when the company found the ideal partner to transform its financial operations in Jeeves' solutions.


> *“We stumbled upon Jeeves one day via Google search and never looked back.”* - says Mike Taylor, CEO of Taylor RC.
