# Corporate investing: how to generate returns on your business account balance

When it comes to company finances, leaving money idle in non-interest-bearing accounts can be a missed opportunity. Investing available balances wisely can be the key to maximizing profits while maintaining the necessary flexibility to meet business demands.


In this article, we will explore the concept of corporate investing, showing how you can turn your company's idle balance into a source of revenue, as well as presenting high-yield and low-risk alternatives. Discover how to generate significant returns through the use of business accounts, and how this type of investment can drive your business growth.


### **What is corporate investing?**


Corporate investing is simply investing your company's profits or excess cash, rather than keeping it in simple bank accounts that generate no returns, using idle resources efficiently and beneficially.


In some situations, reinvesting extra money in the business itself or distributing it to shareholders may not be appropriate, making corporate investing an attractive option. Moreover, it can be a way to generate returns on working capital without tying it up in long-term investments that make withdrawals difficult when needed.


Learn about some of the benefits corporate investing can bring to your business:


- Creation of multiple revenue streams for the business;
- Potential to generate more money that can be reinvested in the company;
- High security for your funds as it is a low-risk investment;
- Flexibility for withdrawals when your business needs it;
- Give your excess cash a chance to grow, rather than leaving it idle in a savings account with very low interest rates.
