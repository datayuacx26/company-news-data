## **What is 3D Secure authentication (3DS)?**


**3D Secure** is a security protocol that adds an extra layer of protection to online transactions made with credit and debit cards. It involves validating each transaction through various methods, such as entering a specific password or a randomly generated PIN linked to the user’s account and card.


The name "3D Secure" stands for **Three Domain Secure** , as it involves three key parties in the process:


**Issuer domain** : The bank or financial institution that issued the card to the cardholder.


**Acquirer domain** : The online store or merchant where the transaction is taking place.


**Interoperability domain** : The system or infrastructure that connects the two previous domains and verifies the authentication.


## **3D Secure authentication: How does it work?**


When making an online purchase and entering card details at the merchant's checkout, the user will be prompted to confirm card ownership. The process follows these steps:


**Step 1** : After providing the card details and proceeding with the transaction, the system checks whether the payment method has 3D Secure enabled.


**Step 2** : If the 3D Secure protocol is identified, you are redirected to a secure window managed by your card issuer.


**Step 3** : In this window, you must authenticate the transaction. This can be done via a user-specific password for 3D Secure, a code sent via SMS or email (two-factor authentication - 2FA), or even biometric recognition such as fingerprint or facial recognition (on supported devices). Each card issuer implements its protocol according to user preferences.


**Step 4** : After successful authentication, the transaction is approved, and the customer is redirected back to the merchant’s site to complete the purchase process.


## **What is the importance of 3D Secure authentication(3DS)?**


The importance of **3D Secure** extends beyond fraud prevention. It also plays a broader role in fostering trust in e-commerce, ensuring regulatory compliance, and enabling merchants to provide a secure and seamless shopping experience. By balancing security and convenience, 3DS is a vital component in the evolution of online payments.


## **Advantages of 3D Secure authentication**


- **Increased security** : Protects against fraud and unauthorized transactions.
- **Reduction in disputes and chargebacks** : Ensures that the transaction is authenticated by the cardholder.
- **Regulatory compliance** : Facilitates adherence to regulations, such as Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) requirements in certain regions.


## **Disadvantages of 3D Secure authentication**


- **Friction in the purchase process** : In some cases, additional steps may discourage customers from completing their purchases, as they must leave the checkout environment to authenticate.
- **Limited compatibility** : Not all merchants, payment providers, or banks support 3D Secure.
- **Costs** : Merchants may face additional costs to implement the protocol.


The latest version, **3D Secure 2.0** , seeks to address many of these issues by offering a more streamlined and user-friendly experience while meeting modern security demands.
