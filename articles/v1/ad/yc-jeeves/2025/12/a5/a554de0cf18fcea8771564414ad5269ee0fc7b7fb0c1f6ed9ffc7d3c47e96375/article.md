# What is working capital management and how to improve it in your company?

Efficient working capital management is essential to ensure the financial health and sustainable growth of any company, regardless of its size or industry. It represents the necessary balance between available resources and short-term obligations, allowing the business to remain stable, solvent, and ready to seize expansion opportunities. In this article, you will find practical and effective strategies to improve the management of working capital in your company, ensuring healthy cash flow and greater competitiveness in the market.


In this article, we will cover:


- **What is working capital**
- **Why working capital is so important for the company's health**
- **How to improve working capital management in your company**


### **What is Working Capital?**


Working capital is the amount of resources a company has to finance its daily operations, such as purchasing raw materials, paying salaries, suppliers, and other operational expenses. In other words, it is the money needed to keep the company running in the short term.


### **How to Calculate Working Capital?**


Working capital is calculated by the difference between current assets (such as cash, inventory, and accounts receivable) and current liabilities (such as accounts payable and short-term debts). The basic formula for calculating working capital is:


***Working Capital = Current Assets - Current Liabilities***

### **Why is working capital so important for the company's health?**


Working capital is crucial for a company's financial health because it ensures the necessary liquidity to meet short-term commitments, such as salaries, suppliers, and other operational expenses, avoiding disruptions in daily operations. It provides operational stability and financial flexibility to deal with unexpected events and revenue downturns, as well as enabling investments in growth opportunities.


In addition, good working capital management can reduce financial costs by minimizing the need for short-term loans, improve relationships with suppliers, prevent the company from becoming insolvent, and increase the confidence of investors and creditors. It also acts as a buffer against market risks, improves financial planning, and allows for more strategic management of the company's resources.


### **How to improve working capital management in your company?**


‍


#### **1. Optimize your accounts payable process and negotiate with suppliers:**


It's important to have full control of the accounts and suppliers to be paid, to ensure that all commitments are met on time, without incurring interest or penalties that could financially impact your business. Maintain a transparent relationship, especially with the most strategic suppliers, to keep an open channel for negotiating longer payment terms to improve cash flow. This allows the company to use resources for longer before making payments.


Whenever possible, negotiate discounts for upfront or early payments, as long as this does not affect the company's cash flow.


#### **2. Improve accounts receivable management:**


Accelerate cash generation and payment collection by offering discounts to customers who pay in advance or by establishing shorter payment terms. It is also important to analyze the credit profile of customers before granting longer payment terms to avoid defaults and delays.


#### **3. Manage inventory efficiently:**


Idle inventory consumes resources and space. Manage your inventory efficiently to keep only what is necessary, using techniques such as Just in Time (JIT) or ABC inventory control to balance demand and supply. In addition, it is necessary to improve inventory controls. Use inventory management software to monitor and adjust levels in real-time.


#### **4. Improve your expense management and reduce operating costs:**


Identify areas where the company can save, such as cutting unnecessary expenses, renegotiating contracts, or services. Automating finances ensures greater control and mapping of operating expenses, thereby reducing costs and increasing efficiency, freeing up resources for other areas. Finance and payment management platforms, like **Jeeves** , are allies in this control in a simple and intuitive way, reducing financial bottlenecks in companies.


#### **5. Monitor operating cash flow regularly:**


Make periodic cash flow projections to anticipate cash inflows and outflows, allowing you to adjust operations as needed. It is also necessary to understand the company's operating and cash conversion cycles to find opportunities to reduce the time needed to convert assets into cash.


#### **6. Use your available resources for business investments**


Your company's available resources should not remain idle but should ideally generate returns, such as interest from financial investments, for example. This increases your available working capital and keeps your company better safeguarded for future needs.


#### **7. Use business capital funding when really necessary:**


Use revolving credit lines or loans strategically to cover specific cash needs, analyzing the most competitive rates and conditions so as not to compromise the company's financial health.


#### **8. Review prices and profit margins:**


Evaluate the pricing policy to ensure that it covers all costs and generates sufficient profit margins to sustain operational growth.


#### **9. Improve business forecasting:**


It is necessary to establish clear working capital targets and maintain performance indicators (KPIs) to monitor working capital regularly.


#### **10. Monitor your finances and make regular adjustments:**


Considering that the economic scenario changes frequently, it is essential to periodically review financial policies and adjust your working capital strategies to adapt to new conditions.
