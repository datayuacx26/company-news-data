# DocGo Recognized in U.S. News & World Report’s 2025-2026 Best Companies to Work For

NEW YORK, May 29, 2025 − DocGo Inc. (Nasdaq: DCGO) (“DocGo”), a leading provider of technology-enabled mobile health and medical transportation services, today announced that[U.S. News & World Report](https://www.usnews.com/) , the global authority in rankings and consumer advice, has named DocGo one of the 2025-2026[Best Companies to Work For](https://money.usnews.com/careers/companies/rankings?src=usn_pr) .


DocGo was rated among top companies on both the Best Companies to Work For – Health Care and Research and the Best Companies to Work For – Northeast lists on factors contributing to job seekers’ decision-making when choosing the best workplace to meet and support their needs.


“Being awarded as one of the Best Companies to Work For only confirms what we already know – that the DocGo team are some of the most talented, passionate healthcare leaders in our field,” said DocGo CEO Lee Bienstock. “Supporting our employees through career growth opportunities and a supportive, collaborative work environment is a core value at DocGo. We’re grateful that our employees’ strong support of our mission to deliver proactive healthcare qualified us for this recognition.”


U.S. News’ ratings reflect the evolving sentiments that factor into employee decision-making when choosing the “best” company to work for. The ratings then analyze that sentiment against other factors, including quality of pay and benefits, work-life balance and flexibility, job and company stability, physical and psychological comfort, belongingness and esteem, and career opportunities and professional development.


“Workers understand the direct impact of a workplace on their quality of life,” said U.S. News Vice President, Careers, Carly Chase. “The 2025-2026 list recognizes companies that received high scores on multiple metrics that make up a positive work environment and everyday employee experience.”


To calculate the annual U.S. News Best Companies to Work For list, U.S. News only considered the largest 5,000 publicly traded companies as of January 2025 that had more than 75 Glassdoor reviews written between 2021-2025. Relevant data, including employee sentiment and regulatory enforcement data, was gathered from partners[Revelio Labs](https://www.reveliolabs.com/) ,[Good Jobs First’s Violation Tracker](https://violationtracker.goodjobsfirst.org/) and[QUODD](https://quodd.com/) to calculate the six metrics used in the list. For further details on how the metric scores were calculated, see the[methodology](https://money.usnews.com/careers/companies/methodology) .
