# DocGo Successfully Upgrades to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Certification, Further Underscoring its Commitment to Information Security Excellence

NEW YORK, September 4 –[DocGo](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.docgo.com%2F&esheet=53630037&newsitemid=20231018301626&lan=en-US&anchor=DocGo+Inc&index=1&md5=ffaa7a02dd1f45c1915b14dfbd532c70) Inc. (Nasdaq: DCGO) (“DocGo” or the “Company”), a leading provider of technology-enabled mobile health and medical transportation services, today announced that its proprietary mobile health technology platform has successfully achieved the updated ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification, upgrading from its previous ISO 27001:2013 certification. This Information Security Management Systems (“ISMS”) standard establishes a framework and sets the bar for organizations to establish, implement, maintain and continually improve their information security practices.


The certification marks a significant corporate milestone, demonstrating DocGo’s commitment to the highest standards of information security. “Maintaining the integrity of patient data while driving healthcare innovation is a vital business imperative,” said Eiwe Lingefors, Chief Information Officer at DocGo. “This certification demonstrates our commitment to protecting sensitive information, helps strengthen our competitive position, and ensures we continue to meet compliance requirements for our clients and partners.”


The ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Certification is an ISMS standard established by the International Organization for Standardization (“ISO”) that helps organizations ensure they meet customer and other stakeholder needs within statutory and regulatory requirements related to risk management, cyber-resilience, and operational excellence.


“Our partners and our patients trust us with vital healthcare data, and we take the responsibility of protecting that data with the utmost care” said Lee Bienstock, CEO of DocGo. “This achievement reflects the hard work and dedication of our entire team, and is doubly-important as we prepare to offer out technology platform as a stand-alone SaaS solution for health systems seeking a best-in-breed medical transportation management solution.”


The certification further reinforces the company’s position as a premier provider for technology-enabled mobile healthcare services for health systems, payers and providers.
