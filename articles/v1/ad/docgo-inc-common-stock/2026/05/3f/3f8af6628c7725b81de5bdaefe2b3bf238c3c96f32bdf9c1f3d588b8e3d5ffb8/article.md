# DocGo Wins “Best Overall Healthcare Cybersecurity Company” in 2026 MedTech Breakthrough Awards Program

**NEW YORK, – May 8, 2026** –[DocGo Inc.](https://docgo.com/) (Nasdaq: DCGO) (“DocGo”), a leading provider of technology-enabled mobile health and medical transportation services, today announced that it has been selected as winner of the “Best Overall Healthcare Cybersecurity Company” in the 10th annual MedTech Breakthrough Awards program conducted by[MedTech Breakthrough](https://medtechbreakthrough.com/) , an independent market intelligence organization that recognizes the top companies, technologies and products in the global digital health and medical technology market.


DocGo’s cybersecurity program is designed to support the organization’s dynamic, mobile care delivery model while addressing evolving security risks. The program focuses on proactively identifying and mitigating threats, helping to ensure the protection of sensitive patient information across a wide range of care settings. DocGo’s approach emphasizes prevention, continuous monitoring, and strong governance practices. In its most recent independent assessment aligned with the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, the company achieved results that exceeded healthcare industry benchmarks, reflecting its commitment to maintaining a strong and resilient security posture.


A key component of the program is employee engagement, with role-specific training and ongoing awareness initiatives that empower team members to actively support cybersecurity efforts. This approach reinforces a culture of accountability and helps safeguard patient data across the organization.


“Our cybersecurity program is a critical component of our organization and is built on a foundation of strong governance and accountability,” said Lee Bienstock, CEO of DocGo. “By prioritizing prevention and embedding security into every level of our operations, we are able to protect patient information while continuing to deliver high-quality, highly accessible care. We’re proud to be recognized by MedTech Breakthrough for these efforts and remain committed to maintaining the highest standards of security and trust.”


“Patients trust us with some of the most sensitive information they have, and protecting that trust is non-negotiable,” said Eiwe Lingefors, Chief Information Security Officer of DocGo. “Behind this award is a team that treats security as everyone’s responsibility, and the recognition belongs to all of them.”


The MedTech Breakthrough Awards program celebrates excellence and innovation in the health and medical technology industry, recognizing the companies, products, and solutions driving meaningful progress and improving patient care. Spanning a wide range of categories—including Telehealth, Clinical Administration, Patient Engagement, Electronic Health Records (EHR), Virtual Care, Medical Devices, Medical Data & Privacy, and beyond—the awards highlight the groundbreaking work that is transforming the healthcare landscape.
