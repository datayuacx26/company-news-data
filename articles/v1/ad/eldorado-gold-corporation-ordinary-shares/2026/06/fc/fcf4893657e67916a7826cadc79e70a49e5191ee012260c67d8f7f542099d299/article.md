# Eldorado Gold named one of Canada’s Best 50 Corporate Citizens


June 23, 2026


Topic:


Awards & Recognition / Corporate


We’re proud to share that Eldorado Gold has once again been recognized among Corporate Knights’ Best 50 Corporate Citizens in Canada.


For more than two decades,[the Best 50](https://corporateknights.com/issues/2026-best-50-issue/canadas-most-sustainable-firms-are-building-a-more-resilient-economy/) ranking has recognized Canadian companies that are leading the way in environmental, social and governance (ESG) performance. This year’s recognition reflects the continued progress we are making to embed responsible practices across our business and deliver long-term value for our stakeholders.


Our performance this year highlights strong momentum across several key areas and **** reinforces our position as a top-tier performer in the sector. We also saw meaningful growth in sustainable revenue between 2022 and 2024, alongside continued strength in governance, including a high level of alignment between executive compensation and sustainability performance.


Importantly, this recognition also reflects how we operate day to day — with a clear focus on safety, inclusion, environmental stewardship and meaningful engagement with the communities where we operate.


Being named to the Best 50 Corporate Citizens in Canada is a testament to the efforts of our teams across our global operations. Together, we are continuing to raise the bar and integrate sustainability into how we plan, operate and grow our business.
