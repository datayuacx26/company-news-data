# International Women’s Day – Advancing Inclusive Diversity


March 8, 2026


Topic:


People & Culture


Advancing opportunities for women is about strengthening our organization as a whole. When we invest in removing barriers that disproportionately affect women, we create systems that work better for **everyone** . A workplace where women can fully contribute is also one that is safer, more respectful, more collaborative, and more innovative for all employees. By broadening who gets to participate, lead, and thrive, we expand the collective talent, ideas, and perspectives driving our success. Our commitment to women is, at its core, a commitment to building a workplace where every person feels valued, supported, and able to do their best work.


Today, and every day, we ask you to imagine a gender equal world. A world free of bias, stereotypes, and discrimination. A world that's diverse, equitable, and inclusive. A world where difference is valued and celebrated. Together we can forge gender equality.


As we celebrate International Women’s Day 2026 at Eldorado, we honour the accomplishments of women across our global operations and reflect on our progress.


Region


Initiatives & Highlights


Global


-


Multi-year updated Equity, Diversity and Inclusion Strategy launched in 2025 with input from our global employee population and communicated internally across all operations.


-


Continued rollout of Inclusive Diversity roadmaps across all sites and offices, embedding inclusion, equity, and respect into processes, communication, training, and talent systems.


- Gender representation milestones: **50% women on the Board** and **32% women in senior management** .


-


Ongoing gender pay equity reviews using industry‑leading analytics to address systemic bias.


-


Support for women’s development through sponsorships including **International Women in Mining (IWiM)** and the **International Women in Resources Mentoring Program** .


-


Sponsor of Artemis Project


-


Expanded our Personal Protective Equipment “PPE” design and inventory to be more inclusive of diverse people, needs and accessibility requirements.


Canada


-


Active sponsor and participant with Women in Mining Abitibi.


-


Health and wellness committee with diverse representation in support of psychologically healthy and safe work culture.


-


Family‑supportive policies continue at Lamaque, including daycare placement and flexible work options; Eldorado Gold Québec maintains hybrid work and right‑to‑disconnect policies.


-


Women’s representation continues to grow: Lamaque increased from **14% in 2019 to 20% in 2025** , more than doubling the number of women on site.


Greece


-


Hellas Gold signed a **Diversity Charter** , formalizing its commitment to equal opportunity and non‑discrimination.


-


Committed to Gender Equality in Greece as evidenced through achievement of the Share Equality Label award.


-


2024 Sustainability Bronze Award for Gender & Inclusion by the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD).


-


Promotion of women in mining through visibility initiatives, including featuring women shift engineers at Skouries.


-


Recently hired four women as haul truck drivers.


Türkiye


-


Expansion of women’s recruitment into operational and technical roles such as truck drivers and engineers.


-


Growth of internship programs aimed at increasing opportunities for women entering the mining industry.


-


Unconscious Bias training for Management


-


Challenging traditional gender stereotypes for certain roles in mining.


-


Programs in place to support women entrepreneurs in rural development.


At Eldorado Gold, we continue to build on our commitment to creating safe, inclusive, and innovative workplaces. Our philosophy of Inclusive Diversity – placing inclusion at the forefront – remains key to ensuring every employee feels valued and empowered. This foundation enables diversity to thrive across all teams, sites, and functions.
