# Royal Purple & Bel-Ray Launch Stronger, Greener Bottle

[Home](https://calumet.com/) /[News](https://calumet.com/news/) / Royal Purple & Bel-Ray Launch Stronger, Greener Bottle


INDIANAPOLIS *(July 21, 2025)* – Royal Purple® and Bel-Ray® have debuted a new one-gallon bottle design with increased durability and a higher amount of post-consumer recycled resin. The new bottle has a wider neck for enhanced pourability, improved ergonomics with an updated handle design and a larger and clearer label with expanded product information.


“Driven by our commitment to continuous improvement, we are thrilled to unveil our new one-gallon bottle,” said Craig Paterson, VP, Commercial for Calumet. “The bottle is built with our customers in mind and we’re proud to incorporate sustainable materials that align with our dedication to environmental responsibility.”


Royal Purple and Bel-Ray will roll out the new one-gallon bottle across select product lines over the coming months.
