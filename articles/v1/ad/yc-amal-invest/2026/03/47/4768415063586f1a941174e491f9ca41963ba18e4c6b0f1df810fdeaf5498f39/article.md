# Better projections and rebalance emails


March 31, 2026


---


### Improved


#### Projected returns use the fund's real age


The estimate used a one-size-fits-all lookback, which implied a confidence a month-old fund hadn't earned. Projections now use the fund's actual age, capped at four years. Same data, honest window.


#### Rebalance emails redesigned


The monthly rebalance email now leads with what was bought, what was sold, and the net effect on your allocation, instead of burying them.
