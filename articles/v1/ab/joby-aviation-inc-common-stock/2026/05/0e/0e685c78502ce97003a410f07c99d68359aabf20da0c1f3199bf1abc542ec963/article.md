# Joby Reports First Quarter 2026 Financial Results

**About Joby**


Joby Aviation, Inc. (NYSE:JOBY) is a California-based transportation company developing an all-electric, vertical take-off and landing air taxi. Joby intends to both operate its fast, quiet, and convenient air taxi service in cities around the world and sell its aircraft to other operators and partners. To learn more, visit[www.jobyaviation.com](http://www.jobyaviation.com/) .
