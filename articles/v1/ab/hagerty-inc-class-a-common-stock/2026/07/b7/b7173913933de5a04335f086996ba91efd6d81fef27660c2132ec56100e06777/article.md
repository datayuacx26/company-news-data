# Days of Thunder Sequel Gets Green Light

Tom Cruise will soon be back on track as Cole Trickle, as the much-anticipated *Days of Thunder 2* has been given the go-ahead by Paramount Pictures.


*[The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/jonathan-levine-direct-days-of-thunder-2-tom-cruise-1236653926/)* claims that Jonathan Levine will direct the sequel to the 1990 NASCAR movie, fresh from steering the upcoming NFL drama *Mr. Irrelevant* . Sundance Award-winning Levine’s back catalog swerves between horror, comedy, and more serious cinema. He also worked with Cruise’s former *Thunder* co-star (and ex-wife) Nicole Kidman on Hulu’s *Nine Perfect Strangers* .


Beyond Cruise himself, no other cast members have been confirmed, so we don’t know whether Cary Elwes will reappear as Trickle’s rival Russ Wheeler or if Randy Quaid will be back as team owner Tim Daland. Sadly,[Robert Duvall,](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/robert-duvall-everyones-favorite-nascar-crew-chief-and-consigliere-has-died/) who played ace mechanic and pit boss Harry Hogge, passed away this year. Expect an emotional tribute in the storyline somewhere and a repeat of the immortal words, “Rubbin’ is racing.”


The script has been handed over to Will Staples, who made his name writing for video games such as Call of Duty 3: Modern Warfare, before acting as co-writer on the 2021 action movie *Without Remorse* , starring Michael B. Jordan.


The original *Days of Thunder* paired Cruise with *Top Gun* director Tony Scott and took almost $158 million at the box office. 2022’s *Top Gun: Maverick* , meanwhile, achieved more than $1.5 billion globally, suggesting that, even over 30 years later, there’s still no stopping the Cruise missile at the movies.


Production is expected to begin in 2027, giving Cruise, once *Top Gear* ‘s fastest Star in a Reasonably Priced Car, plenty of time to brush up on his driving skills and make sure he’s got a set of tires that are “matched perfect and staggered special.”
