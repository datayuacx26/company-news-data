# Auction-Bound 1993 Range Rover Classic Comes with a Matching 2027 Model

If you’re[a Range Rover fan](https://www.hagerty.com/media/car-profiles/we-drove-every-range-rover-and-one-is-king/) , the perfect two-car garage is headed to auction in less than a month. RM Sotheby’s will soon auction off a 1993 Range Rover Classic that has been fully restored by Range Rover and a matching 2027 Range Rover SV Ultra as a pair.


The 1993 model is a four-door, short-wheelbase model that was restored and modified specifically for the auction. It’s finished in Titan Silver Metallic, a color launched on[the current-generation Range Rover](https://www.hagerty.com/media/new-car-reviews/review-2022-range-rover-se-lwb/) that’s only available on the new SV Ultra trim. We’re told that this highly reflective color features real aluminum flakes that make the body panels look almost like liquid metal.


Range Rover also added newer-style lettering on the hood and the tailgate, fitted a set of 16-inch wheels finished in Satin Platinum Atlas, and removed the plastic cladding from both sides. Inside, you’ll find two-tone Orchid White and Cinder Gray Ultrafabrics upholstery, which is one of the combinations available on the 2027 SV Ultra, and a lot more leather than this SUV was originally built with.

All told, Range Rover Classic claims it spent over 2000 hours making this 33-year-old off-roader like-new. The modifications are only skin-deep, however, so power comes from a stock, 182-horsepower 3.9-liter V-8 linked to a four-speed automatic gearbox. Range Rover’s technicians rebuilt the engine, transmission, suspension, brakes, and steering to give the next owner peace of mind.


The matching SV Ultra will be the first example sent to the American market. It hasn’t been built yet, but it will be pretty much identical to the model pictured in our gallery. Based on the long-wheelbase model and positioned at the top of the Range Rover family, the SUV will feature Titan Silver Metallic paint, Satin Platinum Atlas accents on the 23-inch wheels, and an Orchid White and Cinder Gray two-tone interior. It’s V-8-powered as well, though its eight-cylinder is a 4.4-liter twin-turbo sourced from BMW.


The restored 1993 Range Rover Classic and the matching 2027 Range Rover SV Ultra are scheduled to cross the block as a pair on August 14, 2026, during[the RM Sotheby’s The Monterey Auction 2026](https://rmsothebys.com/auctions/mo26/lots/r0158-range-rover-matched-pair-2027-range-rover-sv-ultra-lwb-1993-range-rover-classic/) . The pair is offered without a reserve, but don’t expect to bag a two-for-one deal. RM set the pre-auction estimate at between $750,000 and $950,000 excluding the buyer’s premium.
