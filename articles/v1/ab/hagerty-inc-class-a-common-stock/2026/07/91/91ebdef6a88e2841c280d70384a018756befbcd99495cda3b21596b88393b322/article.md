## Hagerty to Report Second Quarter 2026 Results and Host Conference Call on Wednesday, August 5, 2026


July 22, 2026

TRAVERSE CITY, Mich.


, July 22, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Hagerty, Inc. (NYSE: HGTY), a business that makes it easier and more enjoyable to be a driving enthusiast, today announced it will report its second quarter 2026 financial results before the market opens on Wednesday, August 5, 2026.


Hagerty will hold a conference call to discuss the financial results at 10:00 am Eastern Time on that day.
