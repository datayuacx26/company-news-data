# Our Two Cents: Cars That Look Shockingly Similar

Thanks to the tales of connective tissue told across the internet for decades, we all have opinions on things that look like other things. Some person looks like another person; that building in Europe looks like one built in Ohio, etc. Cars are no different. But sometimes the similarities come as a significant shock to the system.


That level of shock hit me when I opened my garage to begin my lawn care regimen, only to realize the Ford Mustang and Lincoln Continental in there with my mower looked shockingly similar. Maybe it was the lighting or the position, but it felt worthy of a picture. Upon further reflection, it also felt worthy of Hagerty’s[Our Two Cents](https://www.hagerty.com/media/tags/our-two-cents/) column.


So I asked my co-workers here at Hagerty Media what cars gave them a similar level of shock when they see them together. Have a look at what we thought about, and give us your thoughts in the comments!


## Every Crossover?


Mecum


*(I betcha you forgot about this prototype Buick CUV, so[here’s its likely back story](https://www.hagerty.com/media/car-profiles/this-buick-weaves-a-sordid-tale-of-gms-darker-days/) .)*


“I’ll risk sounding like a grumpy old man and say I’m consistently surprised by how many modern crossovers look like…every other modern crossover.


I’ve always prided myself on my ability to identify vehicles from afar, but I have to get up pretty close these days to determine whether that boxy thing in front of me is a Jeep Cherokee or a Toyota Highlander or a Honda CR-V or a Ford Explorer or a GMC Terrain. I will say these vehicles are all generally handsome and refined, but please, *please* can we see something different?


For this reason I’ve been[fixated of late](https://www.hagerty.com/media/opinion/the-ev-market-is-a-mess-but-the-evs-are-finally-good/) on Hyundai designs, to the point that it probably sounds like they’re paying me (they’re not). Both the three-row Ioniq 9 and two-row Ioniq 5 show it’s possible to make a box that doesn’t look like all the other boxes.” — *David Zenlea*

## 240 Z vs. 2000GT

“Aaron’s submission reminds me of the Italdesign concept that was intended for Jaguar. It was called the Kensington, but Jaguar passed on the idea. It was recycled (upcycled) into the Lexus GS/Toyota Aristo for the high-flying Japanese brand, so I am gonna toss it into the mix, too.
