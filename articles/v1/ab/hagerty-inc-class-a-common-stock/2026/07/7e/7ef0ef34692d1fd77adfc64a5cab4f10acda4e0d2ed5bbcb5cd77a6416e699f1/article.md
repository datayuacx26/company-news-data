# Monthly Hagerty Market Report: June 2026

*Welcome to our monthly market report, where we sum up the previous month’s activity in the collector car market with charts and key talking points using the data we gather every day.*


## Key Takeaways


- The[Hagerty Market Rating](https://www.hagerty.com/media/market-trends/hagerty-insider/how-the-hagerty-market-rating-works/) , which measures the status of the collector car market in terms of activity or “heat,” rose 0.18 from the previous month to 58.77, largely driven by growth in auction sale prices and the number of vehicles sold at auction. The Market Rating is still significantly down from its peak in 2022, however, and it has been trending down for most of the year.
- Global year-to-date total auction sales of $3.17B are up 32% year-over-year through June. The growth rate has been a consistent 32% to 36% since March.
- Total lots offered rose 10%, and lots sold 8% year-over-year—both well below the 32% total sales gain. Appreciation at the top, not volume, is driving growth.
- The global average sale price at auction is up 22% year-over-year to $75K, while the[median sale price](https://www.hagerty.com/media/market-trends/hagerty-insider/data-driven/the-collector-car-markets-widening-split-in-two-charts/) is up just 2% to $26.9K.
- The supercar step-change that started at Kissimmee in January has now held for six months. Records set in Q1 aren’t outliers; they’re the new baseline. June’s auction performance continued the themes of the first five months of the year.
- Macro factors and risks persist. Inflation, equity market volatility, and global events remain swing factors in the second half of the year. A pullback hasn’t hit the top of the market, but would be evident first at August’s Monterey auctions.
