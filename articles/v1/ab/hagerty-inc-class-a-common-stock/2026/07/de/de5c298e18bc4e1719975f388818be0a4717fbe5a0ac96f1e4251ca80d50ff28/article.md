# 2027 Jeep Wrangler Laredo Pairs Retro Looks with Modern Off-Road Capability

Jeep has reached into its deep toy box of heritage trims for the ninth of its[Twelve 4 Twelve](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/2026-jeep-wrangler-willys-392-pricing-pictures-specs/) releases to bring back the Laredo trim for 2027. The 2027 Jeep Wrangler Laredo will offer unique badging and color treatments inside and out, along with outstanding off-road capability thanks to its standard 35-inch tire package.


“The Laredo name represents a pivotal moment in the Jeep brand’s evolution, and bringing it back was an opportunity to build on that legacy,” said Bob Broderdorf, Jeep brand CEO. “With Wrangler Laredo, we set out to create something that stands apart, while staying true to what the Jeep brand does best, delivering proven, trail-rated capability our customers can count on. It blends that capability with distinctive design, premium materials and unmistakable character, creating a Wrangler that feels true to its roots while offering something entirely new.”


© 2026 Stellantis


Like the original Laredo that gave the Jeep CJ a bit of added comfort as well as a Southwest-inspired color palette, the new Wrangler Laredo embraces the spectrum of browns with bronze beadlock-capable wheels, tow hooks, and badges, a tan soft top (a black hard top is optional on both two- and four-door models), and a grille finished in a warm brown called Gobi. Matching two-tone brown graphics cover the hood, accent the fender vent recess, and stretch across the rockers and over the rear wheel opening.


The 2027 Jeep® Wrangler Laredo’s unique Bison Brown Nappa Leather seats.


© 2026 Stellantis


We got a taste for the Laredo at the[2026 Easter Jeep Safari](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/hitting-the-trail-in-jeeps-easter-jeep-safari-2026-concepts/) , where we drove a concept of the upcoming model. The production version keeps the tan top and graphics, but ditched the colorful seat inserts in favor of Bison Brown Nappa leather with Mayan Gold stitching. Contrasting Global Black upholstery covers the console, instrument panel, armrests, and grab handle. Careful inspection will reveal a cowboy hat motif hiding on the HVAC pad prints, which ties into the lasso 4×4 logo on the exterior and the rear swing gate plaque engraved with the coordinates of Laredo, Texas.

The concept Wrangler Laredo gave us a preview of the graphics and grille treatment. We’re sad to see that the seats didn’t make the cut.


Jeep didn’t give an exact MSRP for the 2027 Wrangler Laredo, instead noting that it costs just $1995 more than a Wrangler Willys equipped with the same XTREME 35-Inch Tire Package. That package adds $5495 to the two-door Willys and brings the eight-speed automatic along with it, for a total MSRP including destination of $51,245. The XTREME 35-Inch Tire Package also adds the automatic to the four-door, but costs $5995. The steel power dome hood is also required on the four-door, for an additional $695, bringing the total to $53,625. Orders for the 2027 Jeep Wrangler Laredo open up later this month.
