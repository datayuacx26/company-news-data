# This Bugatti Mistral Marks the End of an Era

It’s curtains for the Bugatti Mistral. The brand has built the final example of the limited-edition, W-16-powered roadster in its Molsheim, France, factory. Bugatti is now turning its attention to[the upcoming Tourbillon](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/bugattis-next-chapter-the-tourbillon/) , which is new from the ground up, but it still has at least two W-16 engines left to assemble.


Pictured here, the last Mistral in the 99-unit run wears a two-tone Pearl and Sparkle paint job with bright-finished wheels. If you see it parked at a concours decades from now, it will be relatively easy to identify as the last Mistral because “the last of its kind” is painted in gold-colored letters under the rear air brake.


The interior is customized as well. Company founder Ettore Bugatti’s signature appears on both headrests and along the door sills, for example. There’s also a falcon’s head embedded into the shifter and a sketch showing the front end of the Veyron, the Chiron, and the Mistral embroidered across the two door panels.

Bugatti says no two Mistrals are exactly alike. Some were not shown to the public and likely won’t be in the foreseeable future, but others were highlighted by the company to demonstrate what its Sur Mesure customization department is capable of.[The Blanc Éternel](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/bugatti-w16-mistral-blanc-eternel-pictures-specs/) model was designed with Berlin-based porcelain maker Königliche Porzellan Manufaktur (KPM), for example, and[the La Perle Rare](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/bugatti-w16-mistral-la-perle-rare-pictures/) took about three years to create because nearly every visual aspect inside and out was customized.


While the Bugatti Mistral is now out of production, a company spokesperson confirmed to *Hagerty* that the W-16 engine isn’t dead yet. “With the W-16,[the Brouillard](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/bugatti-brouillard-one-off-pictures-details/) will be delivered this year, and then[the FKP Homage](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/bugatti-fkp-hommage-pictures-specs/) will come after that,” the spokesperson told us. In the meantime, Bugatti is getting ready to launch production of the Tourbillon, which will replace the Chiron with a new, naturally aspirated V-16.
