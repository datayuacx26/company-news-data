# Hot Wheels and 7-Eleven Team Up for 1:64-Scale Nissan GT-R

If you’re the kind of[Hot Wheels](https://www.hagerty.com/media/tags/hot-wheels) collector who can spot a[Treasure Hunt](https://www.hwtreasure.com/) at Walmart from an aisle away behind half a dozen peg warmers, we’ve got good news. Your favorite brand of 1:64-scale custom cars has once again teamed up with 7-Eleven to create an exclusive model.


The new Hot Wheels is a 2017 Nissan GT-R wearing 7-Eleven’s logo and graphics on its hood and flanks. A part of the Silver Series of Hot Wheels die-cast cars, it features more detail than the brand’s main line of cars, but it isn’t quite as exclusive (or as pricey) as the top-tier Red Line Club offerings. Other Silver Series models include a 1932 Ford salt-flat racer, a patina’d yellow ’65 Mustang 2+2 Fastback, and a 2006 Pontiac GTO with a wheelie bar and mock parachute in back. The GT-R is the second model from Hot Wheels in collaboration with 7-Eleven; the first was a Supra. (Discover more about 7-Eleven’s close connection to Japan[here](https://www.hagerty.com/media/automotive-history/7-elevens-connection-to-car-culture-is-just-going-to-get-better-with-more-japanese-offerings/) .)


[Brandan Gillogly](https://www.hagerty.com/media/author/brandan-gillogly/)


Hot Wheels,[headquartered in El Segundo, California,](https://www.hagerty.com/media/entertainment/mattel-turns-actual-cars-into-hot-wheels-at-the-handler-team-center/) has been bringing production cars and trucks—as well as wild imaginary models—into 1:64-scale reality since 1968. Their popularity has earned them shelf space in venues far beyond toy stores. In fact, the brand’s newest die-cast car can be found exclusively at participating 7-Eleven, Speedway, and Stripes store locations and also at 7Collection.com. The website features an assortment of 7-Eleven merchandise, mostly clothing and accessories, but its latest addition is the new Hot Wheels collaboration, the Silver Series GT-R. It retails, appropriately, for $7.11. The site will also be the exclusive retailer for Hot Wheels and 7-Eleven collaborations on limited-edition merchandise, set to debut soon.
