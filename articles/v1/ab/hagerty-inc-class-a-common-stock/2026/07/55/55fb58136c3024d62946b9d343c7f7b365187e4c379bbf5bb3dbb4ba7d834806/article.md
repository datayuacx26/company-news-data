# Hagerty Drivers Club First-Ever Gearhead Garage Weekends a Huge Success

During the month of April,[Hagerty Drivers Club](https://www.hagerty.com/drivers-club/marketing) celebrated the spirit of[do-it-yourself](https://www.hagerty.com/media/tags/diy/) and car culture with our first-ever Gearhead Garage Weekends. We encouraged Hagerty Drivers Club members to get in the garage, tackle a project, and take advantage of the DIY benefits Hagerty provides. HDC members can log in to their account and access DIY benefits anytime, including: Chilton Library for automotive manuals, diagrams, and educational materials, exclusive access to automotive offers, and help with answering any car-related question from[Ask Hagerty](https://www.hagerty.com/drivers-club/marketing/ask-hagerty) , our team of automotive experts.


The promotion was announced in the Spring issue of Hagerty Drivers Club magazine, supported with email campaigns, newsletters, car club ads, and promotion on the Hagerty website. HDC members could participate by accessing their benefits via login or by sharing on social media.


Each weekend in April, members who logged into their HDC account or posted on social media were entered to win weekly DIY drawings, including: Craftsman 4-drawer rolling tool chest, Griot’s Garage Weekend Therapy Detail Kit, Craftsman Torque Wrench, and Craftsman Cordless Wet/Dry vacuum.
