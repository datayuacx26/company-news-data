# HDC Days 2026 Was a Blast, Celebrating Members!

HDC Days 2026 is now in the rearview mirror, and it was a rousing success! This was our third[HDC Days](https://www.motorsportreg.com/orgs/hagerty/hdc) and it was our best yet, with a weekend of amazing deals on automotive accessories, cool contests, as well as epic events and experiences. Hagerty Drivers Club[introduced HDC Days back in 2024](https://www.hagerty.com/media/events/first-ever-hagerty-drivers-club-days-success/) to celebrate the enthusiast-car hobby. Featuring unique events and experiences, one-of-a-kind giveaways and special offers, HDC Days encouraged our members to get out on the road in their favorite special ride.


Hagerty hosted 15 events throughout the weekend in both the U.S. and Canada. For members who couldn’t attend one of these 15 events, we encouraged members to participate in sweepstakes throughout HDC Days to get involved where they live. Here is a peek at HDC Days by the numbers:


- 1,200 attendees
- 2,406 participants in our Get Out and Drive events
- 1,488,510 miles driven by our US members
- 94,315 kilometers driven by our Canadian members


Courtesy of HDC Members


Members reported driving the entirety of Route 66, visiting Mount Rushmore in South Dakota, and completing a 23-day road trip! Here is what they had to say:


“My grandson and I drove my 1965 Chevy C10 Stepside pickup to Winfield, Alabama, to the Mule Day Car & Bike Show! We had a blast making memories that will last a lifetime!” —member Jack


“I am driving my hubby and me to see a World Cup match in Atlanta. His home country (Haiti) is playing for the first time since 1974, which also happens to be the year he was born.” —member Jennifer


“From Victoria to Calgary, Canada, with stops in Airdrie, Sundre, and Drumheller, our road trip unfolded like a cross‑province adventure stitched together with family, dinosaurs, and big‑sky beauty. Experiencing it all through my daughter’s eyes, especially her first‑ever Stampede, turned the journey into something unforgettable, blending my childhood memories with brand‑new ones we made together.” —member Stephanie


The Hagerty Drivers Club team cannot express enough gratitude to our members who helped make HDC Days 2026 a success. We cannot wait for our next enthusiast weekend coming this October!
