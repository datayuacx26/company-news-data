# Roush-Ram Collab Turns Hemi-Powered Half-Ton into Dirt Devil

After decades of modifying Ford trucks, Roush is[turning its attention to Ram](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/roush-tuned-ram-1500-preview-unveiling-date/) . Sold through official Ram dealers, the 2027 Ram 1500 Direct Connection by Roush receives a more off-road-capable suspension system as well as visual updates inside and out. The engine stays stock, but more power is coming soon.


The[Direct Connection](https://www.dcperformance.com/) by Roush is based on the 2027 1500 Big Horn Crew Cab 4×4 powered by the 5.7-liter Hemi V-8, an engine that Ram[recently brought back](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/oh-snap-the-hemi-v-8-returns-to-ram-pickup-lineup/) thanks to popular demand. Roush started by adding its 2.0 Coil-Over Suspension System, which includes twin-tube hydraulic dampers that help make the big truck more capable off the beaten path. Crucially, this setup allows the truck to retain its advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) and towing capability.

You might be able to hear the truck before you see it thanks to an active cat-back exhaust system that gives the driver several sound settings to choose from. If the truck is parked, here’s a spotter’s guide: Roush added 18-inch wheels wrapped by 33-inch tires, a pair of hood vents, wheel arch flares, and an array of specific graphics. The “RAM”-branded grille is specific to the Direct Connection model as well.


Buyers will have six colors called Bright White, Diamond Black Crystal Pearl-Coat, Forged Blue Metallic, Granite Crystal Metallic, and Molten Red Pearl-Coat, respectively, to choose from. Keep in mind that some of these colors might be optional. Ram charges $295 for Molten Red on the standard 1500, for example.


The interior modifications are largely limited to an emblem on the central armrest and another one on the passenger’s side of the dashboard. However, Ram threw in a long list of standard features, including Mopar leather upholstery, the Protection Group, an electronic locking rear axle, as well as[TRX-sourced](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/jackpot-ram-trx-returns-for-2027-with-777-horsepower/) running boards.


Pricing for the package starts at $15,995, meaning the cheapest version of the 2027 Ram 1500 Direct Connection by Roush carries a base price of approximately $79,000. The truck is available through Ram dealers across the nation, and the Roush parts are backed by a three-year, 36,000-mile warranty plan.


The collaboration between Roush and Ram won’t stop there. Roush told us that it’s currently developing a supercharger kit for the Ram 1500. More details, including specs and pricing, will be announced soon.
