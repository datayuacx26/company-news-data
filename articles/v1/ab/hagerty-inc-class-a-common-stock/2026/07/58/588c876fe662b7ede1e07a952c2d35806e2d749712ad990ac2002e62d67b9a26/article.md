# 1-of-99 2025 Bugatti Mistral up for Auction in Monterey

Bugatti[recently ended production of the Mistral](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/bugatti-mistral-production-ends/) , one of its final W-16-powered cars. The roadster was limited to 99 units and sold out before it was unveiled, but you’re not out of luck if you missed the opportunity to buy one. Broad Arrow’s The Quail Auction 2026 includes[a 2025 Bugatti Mistral](https://www.broadarrowauctions.com/vehicles/ql26_r0158/ql26_email?_cldee=uy6N81gfKA-w9U5r4BvS6gxH2_gTlhkiP1mk4qPIMWs2-dYZUjpsuzd-ZZwJVjqs&recipientid=contact-f66d6500c014f11183417ced8d3c05e5-0f9800886baf46f0a09803dd82d271e7&esid=4a417329-dc85-f111-ab0f-7ced8d3c0a5d) with under 700 miles. The catch is that it could sell for more than what it cost new.


Assigned chassis number VF9SM3V36SM795046, this 652-mile Mistral was delivered new to a dealership called O’Gara Coach Company in Beverly Hills, California. The window sticker shows that it cost €5,565,000 new, including €565,000 in options. Those figures represent roughly $6.3 million and $644,000, respectively, at the current conversion rate. For context,[the Lamborghini Temerario](https://www.hagerty.com/media/new-car-reviews/lamborghini-temerario-review-first-drive/) starts at less than $400,000—this is a hypercar decked out with a supercar’s worth of extra-cost features.


Although the window sticker doesn’t detail every option added to this Mistral, it reveals that the aforementioned figure is split into exterior individualization (€393,000) and interior individualization (€172,000). The carbon fiber body is painted in a combination of Matt Argent and Black Carbon over a striking two-tone Beluga Black and Tangerine interior. Bugatti Light Blue Sport accents on the exterior emblems, the central stripe, and the Dancing Elephant figure embroidered in the headrests add a touch of contrast.

Bugatti went to great lengths to make every Mistral[as unique as possible](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/bugatti-w16-mistral-blanc-eternel-pictures-specs/) ; customers were notably encouraged to participate in their car’s design process. With less than 100 units built, the odds of finding another Mistral identical to this example are low.


The sale includes service records, a clean Carfax report, and the metal chest that Bugatti delivered with every Mistral. The chest is used to store items such as the owner’s manual, the front license plate bracket, a Bugatti-branded battery tender, and a car cover.


This like-new Bugatti Mistral has a sale estimate of between $8M and $10M when it crosses the block on August 14, 2026, in Carmel, California. If that’s too much, there are other, more affordable Bugatti models in[the auction catalog](https://www.broadarrowauctions.com/vehicles?q%5Bbranch_id_eq%5D=37&q%5Bs%5D%5B0%5D%5Bname_dir%5D=minimum_estimated_price.desc) , including[a 2011 Veyron Grand Sport](https://www.broadarrowauctions.com/vehicles/ql26_r0184/2011-bugatti-veyron-16-4-grand-sport) that’s expected to sell for $3.25-3.75M and[a gorgeous 1928 Type 44](https://www.broadarrowauctions.com/vehicles/ql26_r0140/1928-bugatti-type-44-torpedo-by-figoni) estimated at $375K-$450K.
