# Hagerty to Report First Quarter 2026 Results and Host Conference Call on Wednesday, May 6, 2026

## Hagerty to Report First Quarter 2026 Results and Host Conference Call on Wednesday, May 6, 2026


April 22, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investor.hagerty.com/node/6626/pdf)


TRAVERSE CITY, Mich., April 22, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Hagerty, Inc. (NYSE: HGTY), a business that makes it easier and more enjoyable to be a driving enthusiast, today announced it will report its first quarter 2026 financial results before the market opens on Wednesday, May 6, 2026.


Hagerty will hold a conference call to discuss the financial results at 10:00 am Eastern Time on that day. A live webcast of the conference call will be available on Hagerty's investor relations website at[investor.hagerty.com](http://investor.hagerty.com/) . The dial-in for the conference call is (877) 423-9813 (toll-free) or (201) 689-8573 (international).


A webcast replay of the call will be available at[investor.hagerty.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4670459-1&h=2664658361&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.hagerty.com%2Foverview%2Fdefault.aspx&a=investor.hagerty.com) following the call.


**About Hagerty, Inc. (NYSE: HGTY)**
Hagerty is a company built by drivers for drivers, protecting 2.8 million vehicles in the United States, Canada and the UK. We make it easier and more enjoyable for enthusiasts to drive and celebrate the machines they love through innovative insurance products, live and digital auctions, engaging media and events, and the Hagerty Drivers Club, the world's largest membership community of car lovers.
