**8:44 PM:** Charity auctions of new models are not unusual but the price paid above sticker is often surprising. The first auction sale of a[Ferrari Luce at $40 million](https://rmsothebys.com/auctions/mo26/lots/r0150-2026-ferrari-luce-tailor-made/) puts it at a record multiple at over 60x the MSRP. Previously, new vehicles offered for charity have sold at an average of 13x, with some new EVs selling for 24x, and a prior record multiple of 44x. The Ferrari Luce seems destined to break new ground in more ways than one. — *John Wiley, Director of Valuation Analytics*


**8:24 PM:** Another once in a lifetime opportunity in Monterey, this[one of five Corvette Grand Sport](https://rmsothebys.com/auctions/mo26/lots/r0081-1963-chevrolet-corvette-grand-sport/) is the first example to be publicly offered in over seventeen years. The last time one appeared, it did not sell at $4.9 million at RM’s Arizona sale in 2009, that feels incredibly cheap after tonight’s $18.7 million sale, which blew past the $13 million high estimate to become the most expensive Corvette ever sold at auction. Once again, once in a lifetime offerings brought out the serious bidders here in Monterey.— *Greg Ingold, Hagerty Price Guide editor*

**7:12 PM:** It’s been since 2009 that we’ve witnessed a public offering of a Shelby Cobra Daytona, which sold for $7.25 million. This car was personally owned by Carroll Shelby from 1975 to 1998 after an active racing career which lasted through 1968. Recognizing the once in a lifetime opportunity, bidders jumped in and quickly ran the numbers up, after a couple of pauses, the car kept on rising. ## Sunday, August 9th


Monterey Car Week—the annual extravaganza of all things automobile—is upon us. We cover this event (well, events) more than any other, particularly the auctions, with previews, news from the ground, and post-Monterey analysis. There are a few reasons for that.


First, the vehicles filling the consignment lists at the Monterey auctions (there are five sales this year) are some of the rarest, most historically significant, and most valuable automobiles sold anywhere in the world all year. Price records routinely break here, and Monterey is the only auction event where we can see multiple eight-figure cars cross the block and be able to gauge their performance against expectations.


Among the most anticipated this year are a[Ferrari 250 P](https://www.goodingco.com/lot/1963-ferrari-250-p?sortBy=ENDING_SOONEST&pageNumber=2) , a[McLaren F1 GTR](https://rmsothebys.com/auctions/mo26/lots/r0170-1996-mclaren-f1-gtr/) , and multiple[Ferrari Enzos](https://www.broadarrowauctions.com/vehicles/ql26_r0165/2003-ferrari-enzo) . Arguably the two most significant cars on offer this year are home-grown American racers—a[Chevrolet Corvette Grand Sport](https://rmsothebys.com/auctions/mo26/lots/r0081-1963-chevrolet-corvette-grand-sport/) and a[Shelby Cobra Daytona Coupe](https://www.goodingco.com/lot/1964-shelby-cobra-daytona-coupe?sortBy=ENDING_SOONEST&pageNumber=3) . Neither model has been seen at auction since 2009. In addition to those heavy-hitters, there are dozens of other rare and significant prewar greats, vintage racing and sports cars, and modern exotics crossing the block this week.


[Early projections](https://www.hagerty.com/media/market-trends/our-2026-monterey-auction-preview-and-predictions-in-5-charts/) suggest that this year’s sales totals may hit half a billion dollars for the first time ever in Monterey, and that this would mostly be due to more seven- and eight-figure cars being consigned in 2026 than any broad growth in the market or an overall increase in the number of vehicles offered.


We’ve also noted that more modern vehicles and supercars in particular make up a larger chunk of the Monterey consignment lists this year, and that this has been easily the hottest market segment of 2026 so far. If there is to be any pullback or pause at the top of the market, it will be evident this week in Monterey first.


Keep an eye on this post as the week develops. We’ll be posting updates, observations and insights throughout the day, every day of Monterey Car Week. And if you’re on the ground for all the festivities in Monterey, stop us and say hello.
