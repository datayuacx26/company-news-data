# Admit It, You’re BMW Clown Shoe-Curious

Whenever a Skunkworks project escapes an automaker’s back room and actually reaches the buying public, it’s worth marking that moment in history. Inherently, these are fringe products—adaptations of larger ideas that didn’t make sense at the pointy end of the spear. But because they’ve defied the odds to reach customers, we can’t help but love them.


The first[BMW M Coupe](https://www.hagerty.com/media/driving/21-years-on-bmws-clownshoe-m-coupe-strikes-an-even-stranger-cooler-chord/) (along with its less powerful sibling, the Z3 Coupe), more affectionately known as the “Clown Shoe,” is a perfect example of this. Produced from 1998 through 2002, the M Coupe was essentially a BMW M Roadster fitted with an unconventional roofline—a shooting brake-style lid—that offered exceptional performance and increased rigidity. Its silhouette became instantly recognizable as one of the more unconventional shapes BMW ever willingly rolled off an assembly line.
