# Gallery: Every Car from Dawn Patrol at the 2026 Pebble Beach Concours

We kicked off the Pebble Beach Concours d’Elegance with the annual Dawn Patrol ritual of coffee, donuts, and anticipation of the first show cars rumbling onto the show field. The rush for the coveted Dawn Patrol hat went a bit differently this year, as numerous sleeper agents dispersed through the crowd with small parcels of the caps to hand out.[In typical Monterey Bay fashion](https://www.hagerty.com/media/events/gallery-every-car-from-dawn-patrol-at-the-2025-pebble-beach-concours/) , we were met by a bit of drizzle and some lingering marine layer, although the fog kept just offshore and let us all enjoy a great view of the vehicles while softening the light on all of the beautiful sheet metal.

Plenty of the cars were already in place well before dawn, but we captured every car that motored onto the field in the early hours, even if we had to chase them down—that Lancia Stratos is as quick as it looks!
