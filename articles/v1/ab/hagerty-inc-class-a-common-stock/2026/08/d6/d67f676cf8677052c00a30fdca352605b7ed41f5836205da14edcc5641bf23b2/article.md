# Gordon Murray Special Vehicles Previews Its Next Supercar

After his Le Mans GTR[reached a major milestone](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/gordon-murray-le-mans-gtr-dynamic-debut-le-mans-classic-pictures/) in France last month, renowned road- and race-car designer[Gordon Murray](https://www.hagerty.com/media/lists/10-things-you-should-know-about-gordon-murray/) is headed to Monterey Car Week in California to unveil a new car. The model was designed by his company’s Special Vehicles division, so it should be even more extreme than the already high-performance cars built by the boutique English brand.


The preview image released by Gordon Murray Automotive (GMA) prompts more questions than answers. We see the outline of a car that has the proportions we associate with an engine mounted in the middle. The nose is low and the front overhang is short, while the back end looks big enough to house an engine. None of the styling cues are familiar, so odds are this is a new car rather than an evolution of an existing one.


One detail that immediately stands out is the relatively subtle styling. There’s a NACA duct on the doors and a roof-mounted scoop, but the latest Special Vehicles model steers clear of wings, flared wheel arches, and other track-inspired aero parts[adopted by other GMA vehicles](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/gordon-murrays-t-50s-just-destroyed-a-gt3-lap-record/) .


Indianapolis 500 and Le Mans winner Dario Franchitti, who helps Gordon Murray Automotive fine-tune its cars, will introduce the new model. The car will be the centerpiece in a display of vehicles that will include customer-owned[T.50](https://www.hagerty.com/media/driving/exclusive-gma-t-50-finally-meets-its-ancestor-the-mclaren-f1/) models and the Le Mans GTR.


Gordon Murray will unveil the yet-unnamed new model at The Quail on Friday, August 14, at 11:45 a.m. local time. It may be too late to buy one if you haven’t already been assigned a build slot: Production will undoubtedly be limited, and the company proudly pointed out that every vehicle it has announced so far is sold out. We wouldn’t be surprised if this model is, too.
