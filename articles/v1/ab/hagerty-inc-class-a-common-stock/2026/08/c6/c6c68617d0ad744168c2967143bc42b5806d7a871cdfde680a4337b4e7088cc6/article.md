# Lamborghini Revuelto Miura 60° Homage Celebrates the Original Supercar

Acclaimed as one of the cars that set the template for the modern supercar,[the Lamborghini Miura](https://www.hagerty.com/media/market-trends/hagerty-insider/lamborghini-miura-the-original-supercar-turns-60/) is celebrating its 60th birthday in 2026. As part of the festivities, the limited-edition[Lamborghini Revuelto](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/lamborghini-revuelto/) Miura 60° Homage just made its debut as a tribute to the Miura with heritage-laced styling cues, nine available paint colors beamed straight from the 1960s, and a 1001-horsepower plug-in hybrid drivetrain.


Designed jointly by Lamborghini’s[Ad Personam](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/lamborghini-customization-options-studio-pictures/) and Centro Stile departments, the commemorative Revuelto was envisioned as a tribute to the Miura that doesn’t veer too far into retro territory. Mitja Borkert, the head of Lamborghini Centro Stile, previously told *Hagerty* that going full retro is out of the question. His team of designers looks toward the future rather than toward the past.


Buyers will have nine colors to choose from: Rosso Arancio, Arancio, Giallo, Verde Scandal, Verde Metallic, Blu Tahiti, Blu Notte, Nero Noctis, and Bianco Monocerus. Once they’ve selected a color, they’ll need to choose a color for the lower part of the body and the wheels. Options include Oro Elios (a shade of gold) on the lower body and Altanero Shiny Gold wheels and Grigio Nimbus (silver) on the lower body with Altanero Matt Titanium Diamond wheels. Both liveries are inspired by combinations offered on[the Miura](https://www.hagerty.com/media/market-trends/hagerty-insider/miura-magic-how-lamborghinis-classic-supercar-keeps-defying-the-market/) .

If you own a Miura, you’ll be able to order the Revuelto Miura 60° Homage in the same color combination as your car. The sky is the limit when it comes to Lamborghini’s customization options, so we’re looking forward to seeing how these cars are configured.


Beyond the color, the latest limited-edition Revuelto stands out with a “Miura 60” logo above each rocker panel, a gloss black logo on the back end, and gloss black brake calipers. Inside, there’s Classica Trim upholstery, an embroidered “Miura 60” logo, and a carbon fiber plaque on the driver’s side of the cabin. It reads “Miura 60° – Serie Speciale 1 di 99” to underline the car’s rarity.


There are no mechanical modifications, so power comes from a gasoline-electric plug-in hybrid drivetrain built around a naturally aspirated, 6.5-liter V-12. The system also includes a lithium-ion battery pack that sends electricity to three electric motors[for a total output of 1001 horsepower](https://www.hagerty.com/media/video/the-drivers-seat-henry-catchpole-on-the-lamborghini-revuelto/) . Hitting 62 mph from a stop takes 2.5 seconds, and top speed checks in at over 217 mph.


Production of the Lamborghini Revuelto Miura 60° Homage is limited to 99 units globally. Pricing hasn’t been announced yet, but we should learn more when the commemorative model makes its debut during the upcoming 2026 edition of Monterey Car Week.
