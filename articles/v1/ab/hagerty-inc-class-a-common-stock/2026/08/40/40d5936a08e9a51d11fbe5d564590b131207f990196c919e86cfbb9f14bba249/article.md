# Gordon Murray Special Vehicles Reveals the S1, a V-12-Powered, Manual-Equipped Stunner

Gordon Murray Special Vehicles (GMSV), the more bespoke division of Gordon Murray Automotive, just revealed a stunning new supercar at The Quail, a Motorsports Gathering. It’s called the S1, and it’s a V-12-powered, manual transmission-equipped beauty.


The S1 “shares its family DNA” with the[S1 LM](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/gordon-murray-special-vehicles-debuts-a-pair-of-new-supercars/) , another GMSV showpiece that the firm revealed at last year’s rendition of this same event. While the S1 LM boasts a huge rear wing and lots of aerodynamic-focused flourishes, the S1 does away with all of that in favor of a simpler, softer presentation. The company says that every exterior body panel is new for this car.

Although the S1 is meant to prioritize mechanical grip, there is a new active aero system for the rear of the car to help keep things stable at higher speeds or during serious braking events. A revised suspension also ensures that the S1 is more comfortable for long-distance journeys, should a customer feel so inclined.

“This is a car you could drive along the coast from Los Angeles to Monterey in real comfort, still enjoying every gearshift, every corner, and every note of the V-12 when the road opens up,” says[Gordon Murray](https://www.hagerty.com/media/lists/10-things-you-should-know-about-gordon-murray/) , executive chairman of the company.

And what a note it will be. The 4.2-liter Cosworth-developed V-12 pumps out 680 horsepower and revs all the way to 12,100 rpm. This screamer uses plenty of competition-spec components such as silicon carbide-coated lightweight pistons, titanium valves and connecting rods, and a dry-sump oiling system. But because you don’t always want to hit 12K on a grand tour, GMSV notes the engine also makes 80% of its peak torque (368 ft-lb) at just 2500 rpm.

Best of all, the firm has fitted a genuine six-speed manual gearbox to that engine, giving you the conductor’s baton to the symphony placed midship. Sixth gear is “a touring gear,” according to the company, which likely means an overdrive.


Gordon Murray Special Vehicles


Despite a lavish interior, the S1 actually weighs less than the[T.50](https://www.hagerty.com/media/driving/exclusive-gma-t-50-finally-meets-its-ancestor-the-mclaren-f1/) that first put Gordon Murray Automotive on the map a few years back. Credit goes to ultra-lightweight carbon-fiber body panels, lightweight suspension components, and an obsession with trimming weight wherever possible that stems right from the man himself: “Every gram we can save makes the car dynamically better.”


Gordon Murray Special Vehicles


The driver’s seat is back in the middle, something we’ve always loved about these cars, and the[McLaren F1](https://www.hagerty.com/valuation-tools/search?q=mclaren+f1) that this S1 resembles. (Remember, Murray designed both those beauties.) This cabin is more lavish than the S1 LM’s, fitted with premium leather, bespoke fabrics, and comfier seats. Of course, each buyer will commission their S1’s interior exactly to their liking.


Gordon Murray Special Vehicles


One feature that has our attention is the switchable, selectable power steering system. According to the announcement, “the driver can tailor the car’s feel … choosing assistance for relaxed cruising or a more unfiltered connection on dynamic roads.” We’re not sure if that means you can switch the system off entirely for the twisty bits or if that just means you can dial back the level of boost it provides, but either way, that’s a neat piece of kit.

Just 64 S1s will be built, says GMSV. That’s a significant jump from the S1 LM, of which just five units will be produced. Although all five S1 LMs were purchased from the company by the same customer, the first example sold at auction last November for an[eye-watering (and record-breaking) $20,630,000](https://www.hagerty.com/media/market-trends/hagerty-insider/sale-of-the-week/gordon-murray-s1-lm-most-expensive-new-car-sold-at-auction/) . Pricing details for the S1 are undisclosed and likely to stay that way, but you can bet these won’t be cheap. Things this gorgeous seldom are.
