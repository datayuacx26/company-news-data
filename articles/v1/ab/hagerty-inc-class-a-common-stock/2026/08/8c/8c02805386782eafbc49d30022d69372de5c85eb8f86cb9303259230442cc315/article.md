# Final Parking Space: 2005 Saturn Ion Red Line

During its existence from 1991 through 2010 model years, GM’s Saturn division took just a brief foray into building high-performance versions of its vehicles. This was the Red Line program, which involved souped-up versions of the Ion, Vue, and Sky. These cars are junkyard rarities today, but I was able to find this Ion Red Line in[a Denver boneyard](https://www.upullandpay.com/locations/colorado/denver/) last week.


Chris Sundquist


Starting with[the demise of Geo](https://www.autoweek.com/car-life/a61986722/1997-geo-metro-lsi-hatchback-junkyard-treasure/) , continuing with the[termination of Oldsmobile](https://autoweek.com/article/junkyard-treasures/junkyard-treasure-final-500-edition-2004-oldsmobile-alero) , and then accelerating in the wake of GM’s[filing for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2009/jun/01/general-motors-bankruptcy-chapter-11) , quite a few GM and GM-affiliated marques disappeared or left the U.S. market during the 1997-2013 period. Saturn was among them, but apart from some[stalwart Saturnophiles](https://www.hagerty.com/media/people/saving-saturn-a-different-kind-of-car-collector/) , its loss doesn’t inspire the same sense of mourning as we see with enthusiasts of Pontiac, Oldsmobile, Saab, or even[Holden](https://www.hagerty.com/media/automotive-history/down-and-out-down-under-the-fall-of-holden/) . It’s possible that you’re more likely to see[an Oakland](https://www.hagerty.com/marketplace/classified/1927-oakland-greater-six/cedc5019-10a3-43df-b905-373e37bce2b1) (axed by The General in 1931 to make room for Pontiac) than a Saturn at your local car show today.


Saturn started off strong, offering[the SL and SC compact sedan and coupe](https://oldcarbrochures.org/United%20States/Saturn/1991-Saturn/1991%20Saturn%20Full%20Line/index.html) at its 1990 launch for the 1991 model year, followed by[the SW wagon for 1993](https://oldcarbrochures.org/United%20States/Saturn/1993-Saturn/1993-Saturn-Full-Line-Prestige-Brochure/slides/1993_Saturn_Full_Line_Prestige-12-13.html) .


Saturn Corporation, General Motors


There was nothing very radical about the S-Series itself, other than its dent- and rust-proof plastic body panels, but many car shoppers loved Saturn’s no-haggle pricing and pleasant dealership experience.


Behind the scenes, the Saturn Corporation wasn’t subject to as many bureaucratic restrictions as other GM divisions (at first). Sure, car sales across the country got hammered by the early-1990s recession, but Saturn was cultivating fiercely loyal owners as the decade went on.


Saturn even had “Homecoming” events for buyers of its cars. Official Homecoming Edition SLs were sold for[the 1994](https://www.virtualparking.net/post/2793/1994-saturn-sl2-homecoming-edition) and[1999 model years](https://dailyturismo.com/the-saturn-deference-1999-saturn-sl2-homecoming-edition/) , and I’m *still* hoping to find one at a junkyard.


Chris Sundquist


You can identify a Homecoming Edition Saturn by its black fender badges and hood ornament.


GM


The[EV1 electric car](https://www.hagerty.com/media/automotive-history/gm-ev1-true-inside-story/) , though badged as a GM product, was leased and serviced through Saturn dealerships. Saturn sales were never quite as strong as The General had hoped, but the brand enjoyed a distinct and generally positive identity through the 1990s.


But the S-Series was a bit antiquated for the new century, and GM didn’t want to invest in a clean-sheet new Saturn design. Instead, the company spent a billion bucks, more or less, to create a new Saturn based on the[Opel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qit9ISmxbJI) /[Vauxhall](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3_gcvvXADw4) /[Holden](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fBOwLjzfht0) Vectra’s GM2900 platform (upon which the Saab 9-3 and 9-5 dwelled). This was the L-Series, and it boasted Saturn’s plastic body panels but was otherwise more of a European car than an American one. It certainly didn’t win over buyers the way the S-Series had.


2007 Saturn Sky Red Line


GM


The S-Series got the ax after 2002, after which the Saturn lineup lost most of its separate identity. The[Vue compact SUV](https://www.autoblog.com/features/junkyard-gem-2004-saturn-vue-with-manual-transmission/) appeared as a unique-to-Saturn 2002 model, but was joined by uncomfortably similar Chevrolet Equinox and Pontiac Torrent siblings a few years later. The L-Series proved a flop and disappeared after 2005. Then came a Saturnized version of the Chevy Uplander minivan called the Relay, some rebadged Opels (the Astra and Aura), and a cut-rate GMC Acadia with Saturn Outlook badges. At least Saturn dealers were able to sell their own version of[the Pontiac Solstice roadster](https://www.hagerty.com/media/automotive-history/how-bob-lutz-ushered-in-the-solstice-and-sky-even-as-gm-faltered/) before the curtain fell on Saturn in 2010.


[Murilee Martin](https://www.hagerty.com/media/author/mmartin/)


Though I haven’t been able to find a discarded Homecoming Edition Saturn yet, I *can* brag that I documented[a junkyard example of one of the 1037 “Zombie Outlooks”](https://www.thetruthaboutcars.com/cars/down-on-the-junkyard/junkyard-find-zombie-2010-saturn-outlook-xr-l-premium-44514787) built in order to use up existing parts more than a year after GM announced that Saturn was dead.


Saturn Corporation, General Motors


That brings us to the subject of today’s FPS car: the Ion. It was built on the same Opel-designed platform as the Chevrolet Cobalt/Pontiac G5 and Chevrolet HHR. It was sold for the 2003-2007 model years.


[Murilee Martin](https://www.hagerty.com/media/author/mmartin/)


The Ion Red Line was a true factory hot rod, with these cool Red Line badges (now is a good time to mention that the Vue Red Line, unlike its Sky and Ion counterparts, didn’t get any extra engine power versus the base model). It was offered for the 2004 through 2007 model years.


Saturn Corporation, General Motors


The Ion Red Line received a screaming 2.0-liter Ecotec four with an Eaton M62 Roots-type supercharger, producing 205 horsepower and 200 pound-feet. This was the same powertrain that went into the Chevy Cobalt SS Supercharged a year later. This sport compact was both fast and furious, but it faced plenty of sales competition from better-known brands during the mid-2000s.


[Murilee Martin](https://www.hagerty.com/media/author/mmartin/)


Meanwhile, GM was experimenting with gasoline-electric hybrid powertrains on production vehicles. The first was[the 2004 Chevrolet Silverado/GMC Sierra Hybrid](https://www.motortrend.com/reviews/0604lt-2004-chevrolet-silverado-hybrid) , but it lacked regenerative braking and offered minimal improvement in fuel economy. The first true GM production hybrids were[the 2007 Saturn Vue and Aura Green Line](https://www.thetruthaboutcars.com/cars/down-on-the-junkyard/junkyard-find-2007-saturn-vue-green-line-44505739) , which received the same badges as the Red Lines but with a planet-saving green stripe instead of the fuel-swilling red stripe.


Saturn Corporation, General Motors


All the Ion Red Lines were “quad coupe” cars, with short, rear-hinged doors behind the regular-sized front doors. The final few years of the Saturn SC included one of these clamshell doors on the driver’s side.


[Murilee Martin](https://www.hagerty.com/media/author/mmartin/)


Today’s FPS car, like all Ions, was built at Saturn’s original[factory in Spring Hill, Tennessee](https://gmauthority.com/blog/gm/gm-facilities/gm-usa-facilities/gm-spring-hill-plant/) . Today, several Cadillac models are built there.


[Murilee Martin](https://www.hagerty.com/media/author/mmartin/)


It was a recent junkyard arrival when I took these photos, so the Recaro seats were still inside. I assume these are bolt-in swaps for a Cobalt, G5, or HHR.


[Murilee Martin](https://www.hagerty.com/media/author/mmartin/)


Someone, probably the first junkyard shopper to see this car and recognize what it was, pulled the supercharger. Members of the Eaton M62/M90 blower family[are still pretty easy to find in junkyards, along with the Aisin SC12/SC14 and IHI Twin-Screw](https://www.thetruthaboutcars.com/2011/01/we-live-in-the-golden-age-of-cheap-superchargers/) .


[Murilee Martin](https://www.hagerty.com/media/author/mmartin/)


A Getrag five-speed manual transmission was mandatory equipment in the Ion Red Line.


[Murilee Martin](https://www.hagerty.com/media/author/mmartin/)


The keys were still with the car, which suggests that it was an insurance total or unsellable trade-in.


[Murilee Martin](https://www.hagerty.com/media/author/mmartin/)


The space-saver spare with a trio of finger-tight lug nuts indicates that employees at some tow yard needed to deal with a flat in a hurry.


[Murilee Martin](https://www.hagerty.com/media/author/mmartin/)


The[drift-stitch](https://www.reddit.com/r/lifehacks/comments/81zk7m/stitches_for_your_vehicle/) bumper-cover repair saved a few bucks near the end, though the zipties came out prior to arrival here.


[Murilee Martin](https://www.hagerty.com/media/author/mmartin/)


The overall condition is pretty good for a 21-year-old car.


[Murilee Martin](https://www.hagerty.com/media/author/mmartin/)


The MSRP for this car was $20,885, or about $36,574 in 2026 dollars. That undercut[its Acura RSX Type S rival](https://acuranews.com/en-US/releases/release-5ac0f36d8484f9efde1a29004c34c2dd-2005-acura-pricing) ($23,670), but the Acura had ten more horses and a six-speed manual.


[Murilee Martin](https://www.hagerty.com/media/author/mmartin/)


[According to the Red Line Forums](https://www.redlineforums.com/threads/redline-production-s-would-like-help-to-complete-this.14991/) , 1512 Ion Red Lines were built for the 2005 model year. 189 were blue, the rarest color available that year.


[Murilee Martin](https://www.hagerty.com/media/author/mmartin/)


Which is interesting, because I’ve documented just[one other Ion Red Line in a car graveyard](http://autoweek.com/article/junkyard-treasures/junkyard-treasure-2005-saturn-ion-red-line) … and it was a blue ’05 as well.
