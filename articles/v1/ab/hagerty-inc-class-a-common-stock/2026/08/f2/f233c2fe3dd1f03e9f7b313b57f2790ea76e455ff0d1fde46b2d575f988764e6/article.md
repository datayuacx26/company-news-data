# 2027 Dodge Charger Super Bee Argues There’s a Replacement for Displacement

Enthusiasts have been asking Dodge for more power ever since[the current-generation Charger](https://www.hagerty.com/media/new-car-reviews/dodge-charger-550-horse-sixpack-no-hemi-no-problem/) made its debut for 2025. The brand has finally delivered: The 2027 Dodge Charger Super Bee packs more power than the Scat Pack and it’s quicker than[a Hellcat](https://www.hagerty.com/media/new-car-reviews/review-2021-dodge-charger-hellcat-redeye-widebody/) .


We’ll start by answering the question that’s likely buzzing around in your mind: No, the Super Bee is not powered by[a Hemi V-8](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/oh-snap-the-hemi-v-8-returns-to-ram-pickup-lineup/) . Instead, it gets an evolution of the standard Charger’s twin-turbocharged, 3.0-liter straight-six rated at 600 horsepower and 531 pound-feet of torque. That’s an increase of 50 horses over the Scat Pack, while torque stays flat. Dodge is quick to note that the new Super Bee offers a 115-horsepower bump over the 2023 Super Bee and packs a higher power density than the 2015 Hellcat.


The extra power comes from hardware updates, such as larger, 56-millimeter Garrett turbos, as well as software tweaks. Hitting 60 mph from a stop takes 3.7 seconds, which is a split-second faster than the 2015 Charger Hellcat and three-tenths of a second faster than the Scat Pack. The 600-horsepower six also benefits from a beefier cooling system that includes a set of auxiliary radiators.


The engine spins the four wheels via the familiar eight-speed automatic transmission and larger half-shafts. This isn’t an old-school muscle car, however, and there’s a great deal of tech packed in this hive. The driver can switch from all-wheel-drive to rear-wheel-drive on the fly at the push of a button, and Dodge added two new driving modes called Track and Drag, respectively. All of the racing-inspired goodies you’d expect to find in one of Dodge’s muscle cars are accounted for, like line lock and launch control.

Braking is handled by six-piston front and four-piston rear calipers that grip 16-inch Brembo rotors. Dodge claims the Super Bee boasts a brake pad surface area that’s 27% larger than the Scat Pack’s, and brake-cooling ducts should help reduce fade lap after lap. Suspension updates are part of the package as well, including Continuous Damping Control (CDC) dual-valve adaptive dampers, stiffer front-end bushings that were developed specifically for the Super Bee, and increased spring rates on both axles.


Offered as a coupe and as a sedan, the Super Bee stands out from the Scat Pack thanks to trim-specific styling designed jointly by engineers and designers. The front end has been redesigned for improved cooling with a wider air dam, new-look headlights, and a bigger grille. There’s also a front splitter, a wider rear diffuser, and 20-inch wheels. Inside, the Super Bee receives new high-back, cloth-upholstered front seats, a redesigned steering wheel with larger paddles and a big “RWD” button, and dual contrast stitching.


Also inside is Cosworth’s Drive Experience Recorder (DxR), which appears on a straight-six-powered Charger for the first time. It includes a forward-facing, 1080p camera that records 60-frame-per-second videos, and it lets the driver capture laps, launches, and other drives. The SD card slot allows the system to save the footage recorded. Drivers can also export the data to the AliveDrive app to analyze it.


There’s a lot of heritage packed in the Super Bee nameplate, so Dodge is celebrating its long-awaited return with a limited-edition version of the Super Bee called Launch Edition. Pictured here, it’s available in three colors called Sucker Punch, Shady, and Diamond Black, respectively. Sucker Punch is inspired by the yellow offered on previous Super Bee models, and it comes with gloss black exterior accents. Alternatively, buyers who order the Launch Edition in Diamond Black get Sucker Punch accents.


More information about the 2027 Dodge Charger Super Bee, such as pricing and availability, will be announced later in 2026. The only trim level available at launch will be the aptly-named Launch Edition, which will go on sale as a limited-edition model.
