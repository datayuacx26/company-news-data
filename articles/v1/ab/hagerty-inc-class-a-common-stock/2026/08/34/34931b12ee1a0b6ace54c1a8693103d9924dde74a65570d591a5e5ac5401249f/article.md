# Eccentrica V12 Roadster Is a Lamborghini Diablo VT for the 2020s

Italian carmaker Eccentrica made a splash when it unveiled[a Lamborghini Diablo-based restomod](https://www.hagerty.com/media/news/lambo-diablo-gets-a-modern-resto/) in 2023. Fast-forward to 2026, and the brand traveled to Monterey Car Week to show its second car, a Diablo-based convertible called Eccentrica V12 Roadster.


Like the coupe, the V12 Roadster was developed as a modern interpretation of[the Diablo](https://www.hagerty.com/media/market-trends/hagerty-insider/the-lamborghini-diablo-is-outrageous-but-the-market-now-takes-it-seriously/) . Eccentrica tells us that it designed over 3500 model-specific components to bring the Raging Bull’s poster-worthy supercar into the modern era. It starts in the engine bay, where the V12 Roadster is powered by a 5.7-liter V-12 that has been re-engineered to develop 550 horsepower at 7000 rpm and 443 pound-feet of torque at 6500 rpm. Software tweaks give the 12-cylinder a sharper throttle response and faster power delivery.


The engine is linked to a gated six-speed manual transmission, sending the V12 Roadster to a 208-mph top speed. The updates extend beyond the drivetrain: Maurizio Reggiani, Lamborghini’s former chief technical officer, helped Eccentrica develop a body that’s made with steel and carbon fiber. The V12 Roadster weighs 3395 pounds, and it has a 41:59 front-rear weight distribution.

Eccentrica went to great lengths to make the V12 Roadster as analog as possible; it sought to bring the Diablo’s performance into the 21st century while keeping that oh-so-1990s feel. To this end, it fitted hydraulic power steering instead of an electric-assisted system. Brembo provided the massive brakes, while the suspension includes a redesigned geometry and custom semi-active dampers.


Production of the V12 Roadster is limited to 19 units, and Eccentrica claims that no two examples will be identical because it will encourage buyers to customize nearly every part of the interior. From colors to materials to personal touches, the only limit is how much clients are able and willing to spend. Several different wheel designs and paint finishes will appear on the list of options.


The new Eccentrica V12 Roadster will make its public debut at The Quail on August 14, 2026. Pricing has not been announced yet, but nothing suggests it will be cheap: The prior V12 coupe cost over $1 million *before* customization options entered the equation.
