# Gallery: 2026 Rolex Monterey Motorsports Reunion—1966–79 Historic SCCA B & C Sedans

Group 8 at the 2026 Rolex Monterey Motorsports Reunion was filled with high-strung SCCA B & C Sedans from the 1966–79 era. The high-revving four-cylinders typically found in the B-Sedan class earned the class the nickname “Angry Bs.” They make one heck of a racket as they zip around[Laguna Seca’s complex corners](https://www.hagerty.com/media/motorsports/rolex-motorsports-reunion-class-spotlight-bruce-mclaren-trophy-1963-1974-can-am-usrrc-v-8s/) . Comedian and podcaster Adam Carolla has been a longtime fan of Paul Newman’s racing career and has picked up several of the late actor and racer’s notable Datsuns, several of which he has raced in this class at[Rolex Monterey Motorsports Reunion](https://weathertechraceway.com/pages/rolex-monterey-motorsports-reunion) events over the years. He was driving the #85 BRE Datsun 510 this year in a field full of 510s, Alfa Romeos, a couple of 911s, and even a Volvo 122S.


Adam Carolla bought his #85 Datsun 510 about 15 years ago and loves its reliability. “It’s never had the engine out, or the head off, or anything,” Carolla said, “I’m surprised this thing still pulls as hard as it does.”


[Brandan Gillogly](https://www.hagerty.com/media/author/brandan-gillogly/)


Billy Johnson was behind the wheel of Carolla’s other red, white, and blue racer at this year’s Rolex Reunion: the 1977 200SX previously campaigned by Paul Newman.


[Brandan Gillogly](https://www.hagerty.com/media/author/brandan-gillogly/)


We asked Carolla how his Thursday qualifying session went. “Pretty good, I’d say. The car’s working, you know, nothing broke,” he said, noting that the first few laps he “did kind of lead-follow with Billy,” referring to driving instructor, hot shoe, and 2016 IMSA GS champion Billy Johnson. “He was driving my 200SX, which is Newman’s winningest car.” We’ve seen Carolla drive at Willow Springs, so we asked about his thoughts on Laguna Seca and how it ranks on his personal list of favorite tracks. “I’ve not been to Spa, but I love this track … I love the elevation change,” said Carolla. “I love the weather. Because at Willow, you’re going to be sweating.” Carolla contrasted racing at venues that are hotter, dustier, and much harder on man and machine. “This is where you want it to be … this is perfect racing, you know?” He knows there are faster cars and faster drivers in the class. “I come out here once a year, do this race, and try not to make a fool of myself, basically.”


Troy Ermish and his 1969 Datsun 510 were the ones to beat in early qualifying and wound up on top with a best lap of 1:45.430 on Saturday.
