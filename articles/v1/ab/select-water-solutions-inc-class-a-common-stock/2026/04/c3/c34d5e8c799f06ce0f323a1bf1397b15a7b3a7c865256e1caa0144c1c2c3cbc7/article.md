# 2026 Safety Recognition Program Highlights

## A Culture Dedicated to Safety Excellence


Each year, Select Water Solutions brings together its top safety performers from across the country to celebrate a shared commitment: making sure every employee goes home safely—every single day.


The 10th Annual[Safety Recognition Program](https://www.selectwater.com/sustainability/safety-recognition-program/) (SRP), held in Gainesville, Texas, welcomed finalists, leadership, and team members for an afternoon of recognition, reflection, and celebration—culminating in one unforgettable moment.


## Meet the Winner: Oscar Renteria


Oscar Renteria, a CDL driver for Select Chemistry, based in Midland, Texas, isn’t the kind of person to cut corners. For 17 years, he has built a reputation grounded in discipline, consistency, and a deep sense of responsibility; not just for his own safety, but for the people around him.


His commitment shows up in the details: meticulous pre-trip inspections, a zero-incident mindset, and a willingness to step in and improve operations wherever he sees an opportunity.


Oscar’s impact goes beyond his day-to-day role. From helping establish a dedicate return inspection line at his yard to identifying hazards and improving workflows, his influence has helped to raise the standard for safety across his team.


> “Oscar looks out for his teammates, takes ownership of his work, and helps ensure everyone goes home safely every single day. His contributions set a high standard for all Select drivers.”
>
>
> *-Justin Koons, Director of Logistics, Select Chemistry*

Since its launch in 2016, SRP has recognized employees who consistently uphold the highest safety standards in their daily work. Through a tiered system of awards and nominations from supervisors, leadership, and customers, the program highlights those who lead by example and set the tone for safe operations across the organization.


At Select Water Solutions, safety isn’t just a priority—it’s a responsibility shared by every employee, every day. Programs like SRP exist to recognize those who lead by example and to reinforce a culture where doing the right thing is the standard.


Because when[safety comes first](https://www.selectwater.com/about/safety/) , everything else follows.
