# Celebrating 10 Years of Safety Excellence: Announcing the SRP Finalists

For ten years, Select’s[Safety Recognition Program](https://www.selectwater.com/sustainability/safety-recognition-program/) (SRP) has honored the people who make safety more than a policy, they make it a daily practice. What began in 2016 as a way to recognize safe behaviors has grown into one of our most meaningful traditions. This year, we’re proud to celebrate **a decade of recognizing[safety](https://www.selectwater.com/about/safety/) leadership across our organization** .


We’re excited to announce the finalists for the **2025 Safety Recognition Program** , a group of exceptional team members who consistently raise the bar for safety in the field and beyond. This year, **1,881 safety awards** were presented across Select, a testament to the daily choices our employees make to protect themselves, their coworkers, our communities, and our operations.


The program culminates with a **live-streamed truck giveaway** where **fifty-three finalists** will be recognized for reaching the highest level of safety achievement. One finalist will take home the grand prize a new **Ford F-150!**


> *“For ten years, SRP has showcased the commitment our people bring to safety every single day,” said Craig Fric, Vice President of Environmental Health & Safety at Select Water Solutions. “These finalists lead by example. They look out for their teammates, take ownership of their work, and help ensure everyone goes home safely. That’s the foundation of operational excellence.”*

**About the Safety Recognition Program**


Launched in 2016, the Safety Recognition Program celebrates team members who consistently uphold the highest standards of safety in their daily roles. Employees can earn up to five safety awards each year, with finalists representing the top tier of safety performance. Those selected are invited to the annual SRP Finals Event, where they are recognized among peers and celebrated for their leadership.


As we mark **10 years of SRP** , one thing remains unchanged: safety is foundational to who we are at Select. We’re proud of every employee who plays a role in keeping our people safe.
