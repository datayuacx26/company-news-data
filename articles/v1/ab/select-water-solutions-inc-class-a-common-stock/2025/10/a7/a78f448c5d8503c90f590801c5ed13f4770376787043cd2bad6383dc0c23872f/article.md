# Groundwork: The Permian

*Groundwork* is a new documentary-style short film that shines a light on the people, technology, and collaboration that drive energy operations. It’s a behind-the-scenes glimpse into the expertise and problem-solving required to keep the energy industry running smoothly. Whether you’re in the industry or just curious about what it takes to operate at this scale, this episode shows the dedication and innovation behind the work.


This episode follows Justin Wheeler, Senior Director of[Automation](https://www.selectwater.com/technology/aquaview/) , from the early morning start, to meetings with operations teams, to visiting multiple job sites, and even tracking down a key piece of equipment a customer urgently needs. Experience a day in the life of Justin as he navigates the fast pace of innovation in the[Permian Basin](https://www.selectwater.com/produced-water-recycling-in-the-permian/) .


**About Justin Wheeler**
Justin Wheeler’s career in oil and gas began shortly after college, when he took a hands-on role in the poly division of a water transfer company. His strong work ethic and curiosity about emerging technologies quickly propelled him into leadership, eventually overseeing water transfer operations before pioneering automation within the company in 2017.


Now celebrating 14 years with Select, Justin leads the automation team responsible for advancing efficiency and control across complex water systems in the field. Outside of work, he enjoys watching his sons compete in sports, working on cars, and traveling with his family. His advice to those just starting out: *show up early, work hard, stay positive, and help wherever you can; the rest will take care of itself.*
