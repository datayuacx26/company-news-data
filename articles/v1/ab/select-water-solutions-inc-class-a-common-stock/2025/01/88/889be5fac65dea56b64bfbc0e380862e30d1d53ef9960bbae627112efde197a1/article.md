# Select Announces 2024 Safety Recognition Program Finalists

At Select Water Solutions,[safety](https://www.selectwater.com/about/safety/) isn’t just a priority—it’s part of our culture. We are thrilled to announce the finalists for the 2024 Safety Recognition Program (SRP), a celebration of the individuals who exemplify excellence in workplace safety. This year, 1,453 safety awards were presented to team members across our organization, recognizing their commitment to protecting our people, communities, and operations.


The culmination of this program is the highly anticipated, live-streamed SRP event, set to take place on January 29 in Gainesville, Texas. Forty-three exceptional team members have earned their spot as finalists at this exclusive event. In addition to celebrating their achievements, one lucky finalist will drive away with the grand prize: a 2024 Ram 1500 pickup truck.


“Our Safety Recognition Program highlights the remarkable dedication our team shows daily,” said Craig Fric, Vice President of Environmental Health and Safety at Select Water Solutions. “Safety is the foundation of everything we do, and programs like SRP honor our people’s efforts and reinforce our mission to deliver operational excellence to our customers.”

#### **About the Safety Recognition Program**


Launched in 2016, Select Water Solutions’ SRP celebrates team members who consistently uphold the highest standards of safety in their daily roles. Employees can earn up to five safety awards each year, with finalists reaching the top tier. These outstanding individuals are invited to the annual SRP reception, where they’re celebrated for their achievements and given the chance to win incredible prizes.
