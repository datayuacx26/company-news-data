#### **About the Safety Recognition Program**


Launched in 2016, Select Water Solutions’ SRP has recognized over 2,000 employees with nearly 10,000 safety awards since inception. Awards are given to team members who consistently uphold the highest standards of safety in their daily roles. Employees can earn up to five safety awards each year, with finalists reaching the top tier. These outstanding individuals are invited to the annual SRP reception, where they’re celebrated for their achievements and given the chance to win incredible prizes including a new truck.
