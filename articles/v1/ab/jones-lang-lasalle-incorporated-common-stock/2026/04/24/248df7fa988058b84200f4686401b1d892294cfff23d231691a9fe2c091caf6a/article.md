## The art of the possible: How JLL & qbiq are reinventing real estate strategy


By


Daniel Correa


| Published on


April 16, 2026


For decades, the commercial real estate industry faced a persistent bottleneck: the time and dependency required for manual architectural plans. Deals slowed down while stakeholders waited for “test-fits” to determine if a space met their needs. We recognized that to streamline critical deliverables we needed to leverage technology to move faster.


Enter[qbiq .](https://www.qbiq.ai/)


**From vision to global reality**


Recognizing the power of generative AI early on, JLL Spark invested in qbiq with a clear goal: to remove the friction from real estate decision-making. By combining JLL’s deep industry expertise with qbiq’s generative design engine, we didn’t just speed up the planning process. We reinvented it. What started as a vision to empower landlords, brokers, and tenants has evolved into a global standard. Today, our partnership is redefining how spaces are envisioned and brought to life across the globe.


**The impact by the numbers**


This collaboration has moved beyond proof-of-concept into massive, tangible results. Our teams across **40 countries** are now using qbiq’s AI to deliver optimized planning at a scale previously thought impossible.


- **150+ Million square feet** of optimized planning delivered


- **Global reach:** Deployment across Americas, EMEA, and APAC markets


- **Instant visualization:** Generating layout plans and 3D virtual tours in minutes, not days


*“QBIQ has been a phenomenal partner for JLL, powering our 3D Visualization that allows us to show our clients “the art of the possible” and enabling them to make faster, better decisions about space. It’s a great example of how JLL Spark can harness and accelerate the impact of AI innovation in CRE.” – Amit Koren, Leasing Technology CEO, JLL*


**More than just speed**


We take raw data and turn it into human experience—faster than anyone else in the market. Whether it’s agency leasing, tenant representation, or project development, qbiq allows us to provide instant clarity to decision-makers.


*“qbiq changed the way our Project Development Services (PDS) and Design teams engage with clients. We’re generating optimized test-fits in hours instead of weeks — speed that lets us explore more options and give decision-makers real clarity at the speed the business moves while allowing our professionals to consult with their clients on value that matters. qbiq has enabled a fundamental shift in how we deliver test fits.” – Michael Yohnka, Principal Product Management, JLL*


The result? Confident decisions, faster transactions, and the ability to unlock the full potential of any given space instantly.


**The future is here**


This partnership proves what is possible when industry leadership meets disruptive innovation. Together, JLL and qbiq are bringing renders to reality at an unprecedented scale.


*Written by Daniel Correa, Growth Principal based in New York City.*
