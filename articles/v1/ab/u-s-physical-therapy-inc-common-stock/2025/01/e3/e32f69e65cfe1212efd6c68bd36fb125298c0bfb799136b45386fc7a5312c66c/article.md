When you make the decision to sell your physical therapy clinic and partner with **U.S. Physical Therapy (USPh)** , you gain access to a sophisticated, on-staff legal department dedicated to protecting and empowering your practice.


## **Why Legal Support is Important to Physical Therapy Business Owners**


From contracting and network agreements to regulatory compliance and audits, today’s owners face a complex legal landscape that can be difficult to navigate without support. Whether it’s managed care contracts, reimbursement policies, or Medicare-related matters, having a specialized legal team available can help protect business assets and operations.


## **How USPh’s Legal Team Supports Our Partnerships**


-


#### **Contracts & Agreements**


Our legal team helps draft, negotiate, and review a wide range of documents, whether it’s payor contracts, network participation agreements, or employment and non-compete clauses. This ensures you’re getting the best possible terms and are compliant with state and federal guidelines.


-


#### **Compliance Counsel**


With strict requirements around billing, Medicare, Medicaid, and healthcare privacy, clinic operations must meet ongoing regulatory benchmarks. USPh’s legal department works hand‑in‑hand with compliance specialists to identify risks, create action plans, and guide you through audits or reviews.


-


#### **Audit Readiness & Support**


When audits arrive, whether for Medicare reimbursement, recovery, or tax issues, the USPh legal team steps in to help you prepare, respond to inquiries, and provide detailed guidance through every turn.


-


#### **Acquisition & Partnership Transactions**


USPh’s legal professionals help structure transactions, ensure legacy protection, and manage post-closing governance, all while maintaining your identity and autonomy.

Houston, TX, August 1, 2025 – U.S. Physical Therapy, Inc. (the “Company”) (NYSE: USPH), a national operator of
outpatient physical therapy clinics and provider of industrial injury prevention services, announced the acquisition of a
physical therapy practice with three clinic locations, which currently generates approximately $5.3 million in annual
revenues and 28,000 in annual visits. The Company acquired a 60% interest in the practice with the current owners
retaining a 40% ownership interest.
Graham Reeve, Chief Operating Officer-West, said, “We are excited to welcome our newest partners into our USPH
family. The founders have forged a tremendous asset for patients in their communities. On behalf of our entire team, we
all look forward to assisting and supporting them as they work to make a difference in the lives of the patients and families
that they serve.”
About U.S. Physical Therapy, Inc.
Founded in 1990, U.S. Physical Therapy, Inc. owns and/or manages 774 outpatient physical therapy clinics in
44 states. USPH clinics provide preventative and post-operative care for a variety of orthopedic-related
disorders and sports-related injuries, treatment for neurologically related injuries and rehabilitation of injured
workers. USPH also has an industrial injury prevention business which provides onsite services for clients’
employees including injury prevention and rehabilitation, performance optimization, post-offer employment
testing, functional capacity evaluations, and ergonomic assessments.

Running a physical therapy company is a deeply personal and professionally rewarding journey. Whether you’re looking to reduce the administrative burden or find a strategic partner to take your clinic to the next level, selling your practice is a significant decision. At **U.S. Physical Therapy (USPh)** , we specialize in partnering with practice owners like you, bringing over 30 years of experience, a strong nationwide presence, and a collaborative approach that puts your goals first.


## Why Partner with U.S. Physical Therapy?


USPh isn’t just another buyer. We offer something unique: **a true partnership model.**


### You Stay Involved


We don’t buy your practice and change everything. Instead, we work **with** you. We want you to stay on and continue treating patients or help lead and grow the business.


### You Retain Ownership—and Gain a Growth Partner


Unlike traditional acquisitions, you retain significant equity, allowing you to benefit from future growth. As a partner, you are entitled to monthly cash distributions. Also, your partnership’s financials remain separate from any other USPh partnership.


### We Protect Your Legacy


We know how much your practice means to you, your team, and your community. Our goal is not to rebrand or replace—it’s to **elevate what’s already working** , preserve your identity, and build on your success.


### National Reach, Local Focus


With 135 partnerships representing nearly 800 clinics nationwide, U.S. Physical Therapy offers the strength of a national network with the personal touch of local ownership. We bring national resources—but we never take a one-size-fits-all approach. Every market is unique, and every partnership is tailored to the specific needs of the practice and community.
