# Inside Our Co-op Program at Skillz

## Inside Our Co-op Program at Skillz


- by Vishal Kumar
- May. 19, 2026
- 2 min read


-
-
-
-


We’re now accepting applications for our 2026 Co-op Program.


[Click here to apply!](https://www.skillz.com/careers/list/)


Our 2026 Co-op Program is open across four roles this summer: Communications, Creative Design, Social Media Content, and Talent Acquisition. We’re looking for students and early-career talent who are curious, collaborative, and ready to do meaningful work from day one.


Skillz operates at the center of competitive mobile gaming, an industry that continues to evolve around skill-based play, live experiences, and stronger player engagement. With more than 500 games on the platform, Skillz continues to grow by staying focused on fair competition, fast innovation, and building experiences where skill truly matters.


At Skillz, we believe great work happens when people feel supported and challenged in equal measure. Every co-op is paired with a dedicated mentor and has regular one-on-ones with their manager. You’ll be included in team activities, company updates, and the kinds of conversations that give you real context for the work you’re doing.


We operate with a lot of transparency. Co-ops hear directly about where the company is headed, what we’re solving for, and how their team contributes to the bigger picture. That context makes the work more meaningful and the growth faster.


This is a paid, full-time program. Whether you’re curious about what you’ll work on, how mentorship is structured, or what it takes to earn a full-time offer, we’ve designed this program to set you up for a career you’re proud of.


This is your chance to build real skills, work alongside people who are invested in your growth, and take a meaningful first step in your career.
