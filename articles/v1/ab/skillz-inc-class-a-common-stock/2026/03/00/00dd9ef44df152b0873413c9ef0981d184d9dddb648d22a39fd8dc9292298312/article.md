Fast-paced. Competitive. Built for clutch moments. Rumble Arena is the newest game from Skillz, and it brings an action-first battle royale experience designed for players who thrive under pressure.


Inspired by the energy and accessibility of arena brawlers, Rumble Arena drops you into a constantly shrinking battlefield where only one player survives. Every decision matters. Every elimination counts.


## **What Is Rumble Arena**


Rumble Arena is a real-time multiplayer battle royale where eight players enter a single arena. Each match is short, intense, and skill-driven.


You start with three lives. Lose them all and you are out. Outlast your opponents or rack up eliminations to climb the leaderboard and secure the win.


As the match progresses, the arena shrinks. Safe space disappears. Encounters become inevitable. Survival is not passive. You must move, fight, and adapt.


[Play Rumble Arena](https://games.skillz.com/mobile/games/78476)


## **How Matches Work**


Every Rumble Arena match follows a simple but brutal formula.


- 8 players enter the arena


- Each player has 3 lives


- The arena continuously shrinks over time


- The last player standing wins


Placement matters, but aggression is rewarded. Eliminations earn points that push you higher on the leaderboard. Smart players balance survival with calculated risk.


## **Two Paths to Victory. Survival and Kills.**


Winning in Rumble Arena is about mastering two core priorities.


**Survival** keeps you in the game. Positioning, timing, and awareness are critical as the map closes in and escape routes disappear.


**Kills** accelerate your climb. Every elimination adds points that can elevate your final position, even before the last circle closes.


The best players know when to disengage and when to go all in.


## **Meet the Launch Characters**


Rumble Arena launches with three characters, each offering a clear and distinct playstyle.


**Roxy**


A fast and flexible fighter, Roxy thrives on mobility. She uses quick dashes to pressure enemies, poke from range, or dive into close combat.


- Class: Generalist


**Buckler**


A defensive specialist focused on control and evasion, Buckler keeps enemies at a distance and turns aggression into opportunity.


- Class: Evader


**Bull**


Bull pushes enemies out of position and delivers powerful melee hits once he closes the gap.


- Class: Brawler
