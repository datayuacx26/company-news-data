Fast, interactive, and easy to pick up. Bingo Rampage by LevelUp Studios adds a twist to classic bingo by combining it with a slot-style spin system and power-ups.


This guide walks you through how to play and win.


## **The Goal**


Mark numbers on your bingo board to complete a pattern and score a BINGO.


Each match is about spotting numbers quickly, using power-ups wisely, and completing lines before your spins run out.


## **How the Game Works**


1. Tap


**Spin** to roll the number reels.


2. Match the numbers from the spin with your bingo board.


3. Tap (daub) the matching numbers on your board.


4. Complete a full line to get a


**BINGO** .


Stay sharp. The faster and more accurately you mark, the better your chances.

## **Understanding the Board**


Your bingo board contains a grid of numbers.


- When a number from the spin matches your board, you can daub it.


- Completing a full row (or pattern) triggers a BINGO.


## **Wilds and Power Plays**


Bingo Rampage introduces special mechanics to speed up gameplay.


- Wilds let you daub any number within the column they land in, giving you flexibility when you are close to completing a line.


- Super Wilds amplify this effect, allowing you to daub anywhere on the board, helping you fill key spots faster and swing the game in your favor.


- Extra Spin gives you 1 or up to 3 spins instantly


- Multiplier awards you 2X or 3X points for a short duration, this effect does not stack


- Extra time gives you added time to your game clock


And many more!


Use them strategically to fill your board and secure BINGO lines.


## **Tips to Play Smarter**


**Watch every spin closely** **** While the numbers stay in place, your bonus bar does not! Try to daub numbers quickly to achieve more bonus points, stay focused so you do not miss matches.


**Prioritize near-complete lines** **** If you are one or two numbers away from a BINGO, focus there.


**Use Wilds at the right time.**


Do not waste them early. Save them to complete lines or recover from missed numbers. Be aware that they can only hold up to two power-ups at a time.


**Keep track of your spins** **** You have limited spins. Make each one count.
