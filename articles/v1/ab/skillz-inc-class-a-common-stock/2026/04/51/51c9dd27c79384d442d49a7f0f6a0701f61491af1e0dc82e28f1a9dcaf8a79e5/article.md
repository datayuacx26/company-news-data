# How to Play Ball Sort by Feel and Experience Studios

## How to Play Ball Sort by Feel and Experience Studios


- by Vishal Kumar
- Apr. 2, 2026
- 3 min read


-
-
-
-


Simple to learn. Satisfying to master. Ball Sort by Feel and Experience Studios is a relaxing yet surprisingly strategic puzzle game that challenges your logic, patience, and planning.


If you are new to ball sort games or looking to sharpen your skills, this guide will walk you through the basics and help you start solving levels more efficiently.


## **What Is Ball Sort**


Ball Sort is a color-sorting puzzle game where your goal is to organize mixed balls into separate tubes by color.


Sounds easy, right? Don’t be fooled! Every level becomes a fresh puzzle packed with trickier layouts, tighter space, and more strategic decisions. Every move matters as you progress.


## **How the Game Works**


Each level starts with multiple tubes filled with different colored balls.


Your objective is simple.


- Move balls between tubes


- Match all balls by color


- Complete the level when each tube contains only one color


But there are rules.


## **Basic Rules**


- You can only move one ball at a time


- A ball can only be placed on top of the same color or into an empty tube


- You cannot place a ball into a full tube


These simple rules are what make the game both relaxing and challenging.


In Ball Sort, you can play your way. The base mode gives you 10 takebacks per level, so you can undo moves, experiment, and enjoy the puzzle at your own pace.


When you want more of a challenge, switch to harder modes with fewer takebacks, where every move matters.


Unwind and play casually, or turn up the difficulty and test your skills.


## **Tips to Play Smarter**


Success in Ball Sort is not about speed. It is about thinking ahead.


**Plan your moves** Before making a move, think about where that ball will go next. Avoid creating dead ends.


**Use empty tubes wisely:** Empty tubes are your most valuable resource. Save them for when you really need to reorganize.


**Build from the bottom up** Try to complete one color at a time instead of spreading moves across multiple tubes.


**Do not rush:** Rushing often leads to mistakes that force you to restart. Slow and steady wins here.


**Use takebacks strategically:** You have up to 10 takebacks per level. Use them to undo risky moves, recover from mistakes, and test different approaches without restarting.


## **A Relaxing Puzzle That Gets Challenging Fast**


Ball Sort by Feel and Experience Studios is designed to be easy to start and hard to put down.


Early levels help you learn the mechanics, but as you progress, puzzles require more planning, better use of space, and smarter sequencing.


It is a balance of calm gameplay and satisfying problem-solving that keeps each level engaging.


## **Start Sorting**


Whether you are playing to relax or to challenge your brain, Ball Sort offers a simple experience with endless depth.


Jump in, start sorting and let your strategy lead the way.
