Fast-paced, strategic, and built around quick decision-making, Blackjack by Skillz puts a competitive twist on the classic card game formula.


The goal is simple. Clear cards as quickly as possible by building each column to 21 before time runs out.


## **The Basics**


You play across four columns. Each column needs to total exactly 21 to clear. Cards get dealt to you one at a time, and you decide which column each one goes into.


Your goal is to clear all 54 cards within 5 minutes. The faster you clear, the better, because speed feeds directly into your score. Time you save stacks into bonus points, so finishing with seconds to spare beats grinding to the buzzer.


You also have three lives. Send a column over 21 and it busts, costing you a life. Bust three times and the game ends.

## **Scoring: Three Ways to Clear a Column**


There are three ways to clear:


1. Hit exactly 21 with any number of cards.


2. Stack 5 cards in a single column without busting.


3. Play a Wild card


The five-card stack pays more than a plain 21, so when you can keep a column alive with low cards and reach five, take it. Chaining clears builds your point total faster than playing it safe.


## **Tips to Win**


**Use the undo option.** If you place a card in a column you didn’t mean to, take it back. Undo lets you reverse your last move when the placement was wrong, so you can redirect that card to a better column. It also doubles as a way to peek at what’s coming and plan your next placement.


**Pair low counts with face cards for quick clears.** Face cards lock a column near 21 fast. Stack two Jacks and you sit at 20, one Ace away from clearing. Then close it out with an Ace. Because the Ace can be 11 or 1, a single Ace can serve different columns depending on what each one needs, so think before you spend it.


**Spread your cards across all four columns.** Don’t dump everything into one hand. Keeping all four columns active gives you more places to land each card and more chances to clear without forcing a risky play.


**Set up columns at 11 for the most outs.** A column sitting at 11 can be cleared by any card worth 10: a Jack, Queen, King, or a 10. That is the widest set of cards that finishes a hand, so it is the easiest target to hit.


**Track what you’ve already played.** Make mental notes of the cards that have come and gone. Knowing what is still in the deck tells you whether the card you need is likely to show up soon.


**Use face cards to rescue high stacks.** If a column is climbing toward a bust, a low total is no help. Plan placements so you have room to bring a column safely to 21 rather than pushing it over.


**Save undos for the moments that matter.** Burning them early leaves you exposed late, when a single misplacement can cost a life.


**Save wild cards for stuck or climbing columns.** A wild card clears a full column on the spot, so it is wasted on a hand you could finish with a normal card. Keep one in reserve for the column that is one bad card away from busting, and let it bail you out instead of losing a life.


## **Putting It Together**


The win comes from balance: clear columns quickly for the time bonus, reach for five-card stacks when the points are worth it, keep all four columns working, and treat the Ace as your closer. Practice the math until 21 comes instantly, and the clock stops feeling like the enemy.
