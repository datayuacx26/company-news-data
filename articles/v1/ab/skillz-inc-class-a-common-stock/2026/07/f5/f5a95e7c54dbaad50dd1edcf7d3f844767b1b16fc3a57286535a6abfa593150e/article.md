## **How to Play: Find Your Rhythm, Keep It Going**


Drag to aim, release to shoot, and watch the ball bounce off the peggles. Your mission? Clear all orange peggles before your time or balls run out.


Here’s the breakdown:


- Blue peggles are worth +10 points and form the base of your rhythm.


- Orange peggles are worth +25 points and act as mission targets.


- Green peggles are worth +100 points and unlock a booster when hit.


- Purple peggles are worth +250 points and fuel your combo potential.


- Red stars are worth +1,000 points and instantly clear all mission peggles for a huge bonus.


- Grey and brown peggles are unbreakable and can be used to redirect your shots.


Earn extra balls by hitting more than 10 peggles in one turn or by landing your shot in the bucket. The more rhythm you build, the smoother your board clears.


## **Boosters: Build Momentum, Break Barriers**


Boosters amplify your rhythm and reward quick, confident play. Every green peggle you destroy adds one to your stack and you can have up to three per match.


- Yellow Blast: Mid-air detonation that clears nearby peggles. Perfect for resetting your shot tempo.


- Orange Multiball: Splits your shot into three for more bounces, more points, and more rhythm.


- Brown Big Bucket: Expands your catcher by 50% to keep the flow going.


- Blue Clock: Adds +15 seconds for a longer, stronger run.


- Red 2x Multiplier: Doubles every point earned while your streak is hot.


## **Fever Mode: The Rhythm Finale**


Clear every orange peggle, and you enter Fever Mode, a rapid-fire finale where your remaining balls drop automatically for a storm of bounces and bonus points. The more balls you’ve earned, the longer the sequence lasts.
