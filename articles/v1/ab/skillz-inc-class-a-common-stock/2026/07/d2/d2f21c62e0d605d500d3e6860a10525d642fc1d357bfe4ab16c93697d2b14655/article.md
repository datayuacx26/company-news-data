## **How the Game Works**


At the center of your screen, a rotating monster dares you to hit your mark. Here’s how to play:


- **Tap the screen** to throw knives one at a time.


- **Land each knife** on the spinning monster.


- **Watch your supply** : the bottom-right corner shows how many knives you have left.


- **Hit your quota** : the top of the screen shows how many more hits you need to level up.


- **Avoid metal and rock** segments as hitting them costs you a life.


- You’ve got


**two minutes, five lives,** and


**limited knives** . Make it count.

## **Boost Your Blades: Use These Extras Wisely**


This game isn’t just about dodging danger; it’s about capitalizing on opportunity. Here’s how to grab the edge:


- **Gems** give you bonus points and different rewards when struck.


- **Red gems** restore a lost life.


- **Purple gems** count as two hits toward your level progress.


- **Green gems** give you extra knives.


- You get three knives for each monster you destroy.


## **Slip Up and It’s Over: What to Watch Out For**


One wrong move can cost you the game.


- Hit metal, rocks, or other knives and you’ll


**lose a life** .


- **Run out of knives,** and the game ends immediately.


- **Go for accuracy,** as every miss wastes knives and time.


## **Scoring Secrets: How Winners Get Ahead**


Want to climb the Skillz leaderboard? Play like it matters:


- **Go for accuracy** : every miss wastes knives and time.


- **Prioritize bonuses** : gems mean higher scores.


- **Keep lives alive** : the more you survive, the more chances you have to score.


- **Play the long game** : smarter shots and conserving knives increase your chances of winning.
