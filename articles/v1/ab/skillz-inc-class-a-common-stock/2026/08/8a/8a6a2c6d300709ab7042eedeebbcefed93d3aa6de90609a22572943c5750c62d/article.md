At first glance, Fruit Merge looks simple. Drop fruits, merge matching pairs, and watch them grow into larger fruits. But once the board starts filling up, every drop matters.


Whether you’re playing for a new personal best or climbing the leaderboard, understanding the basics and using a few smart strategies can make a huge difference.


###### **How to Play Fruit Merge**


The objective is simple.


Drop matching fruits into the container to merge them into larger fruits. Every successful merge earns points, and bigger fruits are worth significantly more.


The challenge is keeping the board organized before the fruit stack reaches the danger line.


###### **Step 1. Swipe to Aim**


Each turn begins with a fruit waiting above the container.


Swipe left or right to position it exactly where you want it to land.


###### **Step 2. Tap to Drop**


Once you’ve lined up your shot, tap the screen to release the fruit.


Where you drop it matters. Good positioning creates future merges instead of blocking valuable space.


###### **Step 3. Merge Matching Fruits**


When two identical fruits touch, they automatically combine into the next fruit in the evolution chain.


Small fruits become larger fruits, which are worth more points.


Keep merging to unlock the biggest fruit possible.


###### **Step 4. Watch the Danger Line**


As the container fills, you’ll see the danger line near the top.


If fruits remain above this line, the game ends.


Managing space is just as important as making merges.


###### **Fruit Values**


Every fruit you create is worth more than the last.


As your fruits grow, so do your rewards.


###### **Tips for Higher Scores**


- **Plan ahead.** Think about where your next few fruits will land, not just the current one.


- **Build from the bottom.** Keeping larger fruits near the bottom creates more room for future merges.


- **Group matching fruits.** Place similar fruits close together so they’re easier to combine.


- **Leave yourself space.** Don’t fill the board too quickly. Open space gives you more options.


- **Look for chain reactions.** One well-placed fruit can trigger multiple merges and boost your score.


- **Use your Hammer wisely.** Save your Hammer for tricky situations where the board is getting crowded or when removing a single fruit can trigger multiple merges. Clearing the right fruit at the right time can create powerful chain reactions, free up valuable space, and keep your game going longer.


###### **Ready to Merge?**


Fruit Merge rewards patience, planning, and smart placement. Every move counts, so think ahead, keep your board organized, and aim for the biggest fruit to climb the leaderboard.
