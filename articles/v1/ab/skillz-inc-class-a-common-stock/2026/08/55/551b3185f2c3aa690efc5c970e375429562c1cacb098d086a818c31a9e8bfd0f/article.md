# Why Developers Choose Skillz: The Platform Built for Competitive Mobile Games

## Why Developers Choose Skillz: The Platform Built for Competitive Mobile Games


- by Skillz
- Aug. 6, 2026
- 4 min read


-
-
-
-


Mobile gaming has never been larger or more vibrant, with millions of players discovering new experiences every day and developers continuing to push the boundaries of creativity and innovation. As the market has matured, however, reaching new players has become increasingly competitive. User acquisition costs have risen, in-app purchase revenue has become harder to grow even in established genres, and players have more choices than ever, making long-term engagement an increasingly important differentiator. For developers looking for a way to stand out and build sustainable revenue, competitive gameplay has become one of the clearest paths forward. That’s where Skillz comes in.


Skillz has spent years building the infrastructure behind mobile eSports — the matchmaking, tournament logic, fair-play systems, geoprotection, and payments backend that make skill-based competition possible at scale. Developers don’t have to build any of that from scratch or deal with the continual updates needed. Instead, they get a platform purpose-built to turn any game into a competitive experience players want to come back to, again and again.


## **1. Competitive infrastructure, without the infrastructure work**


Building a real-time, skill-based competition system is hard. It means solving for matchmaking fairness, latency, anti-cheat, secure payments, and player disputes problems most studios would rather not spend engineering time on when they could be focused on gameplay. Skillz handles this backend so developers don’t have to: authentication, matchmaking, tournament management, and player data are all available through direct API access, letting teams plug competitive features into their game rather than engineering an entire competition stack in-house.


## **2. Creative control stays with the developer**


One of the most common concerns developers have about game platforms is losing control over their game’s look, feel, and player experience. Skillz has addressed this head-on with its newly announced Pro SDK, a Unity-native framework debuting at GDC 2026. It gives studios the ability to design custom lobbies, tournament flows, match interfaces, and progression systems entirely within their own game, while Skillz manages the infrastructure underneath. With the Pro SDK, competitive play feels like a native part of the game itself, which keeps players in-experience longer and improves load times along the way.


## **3. Multiple ways to build revenue, not just one**


Relying solely on in-app purchases is a fragile strategy in a market where IAP saturation is a real and growing problem. Skillz gives developers room to diversify: meta systems, cosmetic economies, digital collectibles, content bundles, event-driven offers, rewarded ads, and both real-money and free-to-play competitive formats can all be layered together. That flexibility means developers aren’t dependent on a single revenue lever, they can build a monetization strategy suited to their specific audience and game design.


## **4. Live-service tools that keep games fresh**


Powered by Beamable’s infrastructure, the[Pro SDK](http://skillz.com/prosdk/) also brings live-service capabilities directly into the development workflow: virtual economies, real-time content updates, and asset bundle deployment that doesn’t require a full app release. That means developers can ship new tournaments, seasonal content, or balance changes on their own timeline, without waiting on app store review cycles to keep their competitive ecosystem feeling alive.


## **5. Proven in the wild**


Skillz isn’t asking developers to be the first to test unproven tools. Puzzle Blockz is already live as the first implementation of the Pro SDK, giving Skillz a real production environment to refine the platform before wider rollout and giving developers evaluating the platform proof that it works under real player load.


#### **The Bottom Line**


Developers choose Skillz because it solves the hardest parts of competitive mobile gaming fairness, infrastructure, and monetization, while leaving creative control exactly where it belongs: with the studio. Instead of choosing between building a great game and building a sustainable business around it, Skillz gives developers a way to do both, backed by a platform that’s been refined specifically for the demands of skill-based competition.
