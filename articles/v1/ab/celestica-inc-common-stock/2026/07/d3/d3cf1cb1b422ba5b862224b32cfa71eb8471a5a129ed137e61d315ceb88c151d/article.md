Together, we are engineering solutions that seamlessly handle demanding workloads, reduce time-to-value, and improve supply chain resiliency across cloud, enterprise, and research environments.


## Optimized Switching Power: How the DS6010 Powers AMD Helios?


Central to this question is the AMD/Celestica collaboration for AMD Helios and our new standard-setting product: the[Celestica DS6010 networking switch](https://cls.celestica.com/hardware-platforms/ds6010/) , purpose-built for the AMD Helios. Engineered specifically for seamless integration within the AMD Helios architecture, the DS6010 is a 1-OU, cabled-backplane scale-up switch aligned with the Open Compute Project (OCP) Open-Rack-Wide (ORW) open standards. Operators and hyperscalers choose the DS6010 for its elite switching power:


- Massive Performance: It utilizes dual Broadcom Tomahawk 6 ASICs that deliver an astounding 204.8 Tbps of non-blocking capacity to easily handle extreme AI throughput.
- Ultra-Low Latency Interconnects: Features a high-density cabled backplane supporting high-speed 224G PAM4 SerDes to seamlessly interconnect up to 72 GPUs, which dramatically minimizes AI training latency and enables massive, efficient scaling.
- Plug-and-Play Integration: By offering the DS6010 as part of an integrated Helios rack-scale solution inside OCP ORW racks, it streamlines deployment and eliminates the challenges of complex data center integrations.


Furthermore, operators gain complete peace of mind through automated cyber-resilience via Platform Firmware Resilience (PFR) which automatically detects and recovers from malicious firmware attacks. This is backed by a reliable hybrid liquid cooling design and 7+1 fan redundancy engineered for mission-critical uptime.
