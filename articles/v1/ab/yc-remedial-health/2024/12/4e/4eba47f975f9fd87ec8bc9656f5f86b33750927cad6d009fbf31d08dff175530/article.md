One of the biggest challenges pharmacy owners face is dealing with expired drugs. Drug wastage not only affects profitability but also disrupts the smooth running of inventory and[supply chains](https://medium.com/@remedialhealth/how-to-use-remedial-rx-to-fix-supply-chain-challenges-in-africa-752dab7b8057) . The financial implications of expired medications, alongside the ethical responsibility of proper drug management, make it critical for pharmacies and patent medicine stores to adopt strategies to minimize waste.


Here are practical and simple ways to address this issue:


### 1. Adopt a First-Expiry-First-Out (FEFO) System


Implementing a FEFO approach ensures that drugs with the earliest expiration dates are sold or used first. By organizing your stock based on expiry dates, you minimize the risk of newer inventory being used before older stock.


[How to Implement FEFO](https://medium.com/@remedialhealth/how-to-implement-fefo-first-to-expire-first-out-inventory-management-in-your-pharmacy-0a63c0c9b535) **:**


- Label products clearly with expiration dates in visible areas.
- Arrange shelves and storage areas so that older stock is placed at the front.
- Regularly audit the shelves to ensure compliance with FEFO principles.


### 2. Set Up Routine Expiry Checks


Regularly inspecting your inventory for nearing expiry dates is essential. By conducting routine checks:


- You can identify products close to expiration and take corrective actions, such as discounts or promotional offers to clear them.
- You can separate items nearing expiry from the general stock to prioritize their sale.


**Tip:** Assign specific team members the responsibility of monitoring expiry dates weekly or monthly.


### 3. Accurate Demand Forecasting


Overstocking is one of the leading causes of drug expiry. Proper demand forecasting allows pharmacies to[stock up](https://medium.com/@remedialhealth/how-to-stock-up-your-pharmacy-at-no-cost-ff1ff7d7a3c7) the right quantity of products at the right time, reducing excess inventory that risks expiration.


**Steps for Better Forecasting:**


- Use historical sales data to predict future demand.
- Identify seasonal trends (e.g., flu season, malaria spikes) to adjust procurement levels.
- Leverage inventory management software to automate forecasts and procurement decisions.


### 4. Leverage Inventory Management Software


Manual inventory management often leads to errors that result in overstocking or stockouts. Advanced inventory management systems help track stock levels, monitor expiration dates, and provide automated alerts for products nearing expiry.


**Benefits of Using Inventory Software:**


- Real-time monitoring of stock movement.
- Automated notifications for replenishment and near-expiry products.
- Insights into fast-moving and slow-moving items, aiding better ordering decisions.


### 5. Collaborate with Suppliers for Better Procurement


Establishing clear communication with your suppliers is critical to preventing waste. Work closely with them to order smaller quantities of drugs that have shorter shelf lives and negotiate flexible procurement terms.


**Actions to Take:**


- Request smaller, frequent deliveries instead of bulk orders when appropriate.
- Return slow-moving items to suppliers if possible.
- Discuss expiry-date thresholds for delivered stock.


### 6. Monitor Slow-Moving Inventory


Drugs that sit on shelves for long periods are more likely to expire. Identifying slow-moving items allows you to take action before they expire.


**Solutions for Slow-Moving Stock:**


- Offer discounts or promotions to encourage faster sales.
- Bundle slow-moving products with fast-moving items as a sales strategy.
- Reduce reordering quantities for slow-moving products in future procurement.


### 7. Adopt Digital Tools for Expiry Alerts


Pharmacies should leverage digital tools (like[the Remedial Health app](https://medium.com/@remedialhealth/boost-your-profits-during-inflation-with-the-new-remedial-health-app-f1843c91bcaa) ) that provide notifications for drugs nearing their expiry dates. These alerts allow you to take action promptly, such as reducing prices or notifying customers of promotions.


### 8. Educate Staff on Effective Inventory Practices


Train your team to be vigilant about expiration dates and proper stock management. Educated staff can play a significant role in implementing FEFO, conducting regular checks, and ensuring best practices are followed.


**Key Training Areas:**


- Proper labelling and shelf arrangement.
- Identifying slow-moving and near-expiry stock.
- Using inventory software efficiently to track and forecast stock.


### 9. Partner with Organizations for Drug Redistribution


If permitted by regulations, unused and unexpired medications can be redistributed to organizations or healthcare facilities that need them. This not only reduces waste but also contributes to healthcare accessibility.
