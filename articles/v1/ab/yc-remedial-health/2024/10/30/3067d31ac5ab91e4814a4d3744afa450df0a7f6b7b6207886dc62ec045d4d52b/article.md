Pharmaceutical[procurement](https://medium.com/@remedialhealth/2023-best-procurement-practices-for-pharmacy-and-patent-medicine-stores-in-africa-21e34faaea29) is necessary to maintain a strong healthcare system. A well organized procurement system ensures pharmaceuticals, and medical devices are in constant supply.


It is essential for managing public health challenges such as disease outbreaks and other healthcare emergencies. Apart from medicines being made available, efficient pharmaceutical procurement practices can reduce the risk of substandard or counterfeit drugs which is a menace to the Nigerian healthcare system.


Despite the advantages of efficient pharmaceutical procurement systems, Nigeria is found lacking. This is due to economic, political and social factors and these factors impede quality healthcare provision.


In this article, we discuss if convenient pharmaceutical procurement in Nigeria is a possibility. Let’s delve further into procurement problems.


## Overview of the Challenges faced in the Current System


In Nigeria, a lack of well-equipped facilities and insufficient medical supplies hinder access to quality healthcare. This deficiency in infrastructure affects the timely and effective delivery of healthcare services.


The funding of health systems in Nigeria relies solely on foreign aid. While aid is important, this dependency on external assistance can trigger instability and uncertainty regarding the availability of medicines.


For instance, foreign aid was limited during the COVID-19 pandemic because all countries were focused on catering to the needs of their citizens.


Additionally, healthcare interventions in Nigeria are often characterized by short-term and crisis-driven approaches. These responses only address immediate health needs but tend to overlook sustainable long term solutions.


These challenges include:


- [Supply Chain](https://medium.com/@remedialhealth/how-to-use-remedial-rx-to-fix-supply-chain-challenges-in-africa-752dab7b8057) **Disruptions:** Inconsistent supply chains often lead to shortages of pharmaceuticals which can disrupt the continuity of care, posing serious consequences for public health.
- **Quality Assurance Concerns:** Ensuring the quality of pharmaceuticals is another hurdle. The global market for fake or counterfeit drugs is estimated at[$200 billion](https://apps.who.int/iris/bitstream/handle/10665/311460/9789290234135-eng.pdf) per annum. The deteriorating economic conditions in Nigeria has increased the influx of[fake drugs](https://medium.com/@remedialhealth/how-to-identify-fake-drugs-in-africa-19ff81d0fd56) . Purchasing medicines can consume a large part of one’s income. This increases the sale of cheap drugs sourced from unregulated outlets. These counterfeit medications pose risks to patient safety.
- **Financial Constraints:** Limited budget allocations for healthcare procurement can restrict the ability to procure an adequate quantity of medications. This can compromise the overall quality of healthcare services.
- **Lack of Transparency:** Lack of transparency in procurement processes can contribute to corruption and unethical practices. Transparent procedures are essential for building public trust and ensuring that resources are allocated efficiently.
- **Infrastructure Challenges:** Inadequate infrastructure, including storage and transportation facilities, can affect the proper handling and distribution of pharmaceuticals. This may lead to product degradation or delays in reaching healthcare facilities.


In light of these challenges, addressing the pharmaceutical procurement crisis demands an effort from governments and international organizations to enhance infrastructure, develop sustainable and long-term strategies to improve the overall health and well-being of the population.


To address these challenges in the current pharmaceutical procurement system in Nigeria, these solutions can be considered:


- **Supply Chain Management:** Implementing advanced supply chain management systems to ensure a more consistent and reliable flow of pharmaceuticals, reducing the risk of shortages.
- **Quality Assurance Measures:** Strengthening quality control and assurance mechanisms to detect and prevent the circulation of counterfeit drugs to ensure the safety and efficacy of medications.
- **Increased Budget Allocations:** Advocating for higher budget allocations to healthcare procurement and better access to[financing](https://medium.com/@remedialhealth/5-mistakes-to-avoid-in-financing-your-pharmacy-263eab9b2018) to enable the procurement of an adequate quantity of medications.
- **Transparency and Accountability:** Establishing transparent procurement processes, leveraging technology for real-time tracking and reporting, to reduce corruption and unethical practices, fostering public trust.
- **Infrastructure Development:** Investing in infrastructure improvement, including storage and transportation facilities, to enhance the proper handling and distribution of pharmaceuticals, minimizing product degradation and delays.


By addressing these areas, Nigeria can work towards creating a more efficient and effective pharmaceutical procurement system that better serves the healthcare needs of its population.
