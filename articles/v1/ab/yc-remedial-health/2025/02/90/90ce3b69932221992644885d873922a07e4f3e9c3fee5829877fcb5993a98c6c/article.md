### What are Counterfeit Medications?


[Counterfeit medications](https://medium.com/@remedialhealth/how-to-identify-fake-drugs-in-africa-19ff81d0fd56) are broadly defined as drugs that contains inadequate amounts of active ingredients, contain the wrong active ingredients, be contaminated with harmful substances, and/or be falsely labelled. Scary Statistics of Counterfeit Medicine In the last few months, illegal operations across the country have been freely circulating fake and substandard drugs as well as unwholesome food and drinks. This fraudulent practice, is putting the lives of many Nigerians on the line.


In August 6, 2024, the NAFDAC informed healthcare providers and the public of a report confirming the presence of counterfeited Perjeta in Nigeria. Also, on November 21, 2024, NAFDAC announced the circulation of counterfeit Combiart Dispersible Tablet 20/120mg in the country. In December 18, 2024, NAFDAC alerted the public to the sale and distribution of counterfeit Paludex tablets and suspensions across Nigeria. NAFDAC reports that approximately 17 per cent of pharmaceutical products in circulation are counterfeit, putting millions of lives at risk.


To put it more succinctly, the World Health Organization estimates that[1 in 10 medicines](https://www.who.int/news/item/28-11-2017-1-in-10-medical-products-in-developing-countries-is-substandard-or-falsified) in low- and middle-income countries failed quality control tests, suggesting the product is substandard or falsified.


That’s how serious this issue is.


As a matter of fact, Hospitals are overwhelmed with cases of poisoning and organ failure as direct effects of ingesting these counterfeit medications.

The picture is getting clearer, right?


### How to Combat Counterfeit Medications


Solutions Insights into solving the problem? Okay, let’s go:


1, The use of technology in combating the issue. Provide modern technology for detecting counterfeit products, and expand enforcement powers.


2, Verification system using QR codes could allow consumers to verify the authenticity of products.


3, Anti-counterfeiting measures such as holographic labels and track-and-trace systems


4,Sensitization: Consumers needs to be aware of the risks associated with counterfeit goods and how to identify them. These measures would to a large extent curb this menace.
