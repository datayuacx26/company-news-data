# Els Club Vilamoura ready to unveil elevated on-course experience with The Toro Company


###


May 06, 2025 |[Golf](https://newsroom.toro.com/en-gb/golf)


Vilamoura, Portugal — Els Club Vilamoura, which was announced in December 2024, has named The Toro Company, a global leader in innovative solutions for the outdoor environment, as its exclusive supplier of turf maintenance equipment and irrigation systems. A landmark partnership that promises to elevate golf course conditioning standards in the Algarve.


Partnering with Irrimac, Toro’s local distributor in Portugal, this agreement combines cutting-edge technology, sustainability, and expertise to support the opening of the Algarve’s first private members’ golf club in Summer 2025.


Designed by four-time Major winner Ernie Els, the new private club carrying his name is set to redefine the golfing experience in the Algarve setting a new benchmark for luxury and hospitality.


In this exclusive partnership, Toro will provide a comprehensive support package with regular ongoing inspections to guarantee the quality of its golf course maintenance equipment which will include GREENSMASTER® eTriFlex® 3370, REELMASTER® Fairway Mowers, and WORKMAN® MDX Lithium-Ion Utility Vehicles.


Greenkeeping staff will also benefit from certified on-site technical training, ensuring the highest standards of course maintenance, while advanced connectivity technology will enable hardware and software tracking for the entire fleet of equipment, maximising operational efficiency.


**Nuno Sepulveda, Co-CEO of DETAILS** , said “At the Els Club Vilamoura, we are committed to creating a golf experience unlike any other in the Algarve. Partnering with Toro and Irrimac ensures we are equipped with the best tools and technologies to maintain our world-class standards while prioritising sustainability and innovation.”


“In heart of the Portuguese Algarve, a masterpiece is being created that showcases DETAILS’ and its parent company, Arrow Global’s aim to elevate the prestigious Portuguese golf market,” added **Andy Brown, Toro - Senior Manager, Global Business Development & Relationships** . “Innovation, efficiency, and aftermarket support are the main reasons Toro has been chosen. We are honoured to play our part to help maintain the Els Club Vilamoura, DETAILS’ flagship golf course.”


Arturo Moran, **Toro’s regional business manager for Southern Europe** , commented: “It’s a privilege to collaborate on such a prestigious project. Our cutting-edge equipment is perfectly suited to support The Els Club Vilamoura in achieving its vision of sustainability and excellence. Toro’s Greensmaster eTriflex, Reelmaster Fairway mowers, and Workman MDX Lithium are integral to maintaining the course’s exceptional quality.” **Xavier Agustí, area sales manager, golf irrigation products** , added: “In addition to our equipment, Toro’s advanced water management solutions will play a crucial role in ensuring efficient irrigation. This highlights our dedication to both environmental stewardship and top-tier performance.”


**Henrique Duarte, General Manager at Irrimac** , stated “Over the last 30 years, Irrimac has played a key role in Portugal’s national golf industry by providing irrigation systems, a full range of machinery for golf course maintenance, as well as training, technical assistance, and consultancy services. Being part of such an innovative and technologically advanced project, which is also strategically significant for Portuguese golf, is the ultimate recognition for our years of dedication.”


Sustainability will play a big role in the collaboration. Sponsored by Toro and Irrimac, solar panels are set to be installed, providing clean energy to recharge the electric equipment used on the course.


This initiative, paired with the provision of electric handheld tools such as mowers and rapid chargers, underscores the commitment to environmental stewardship. Together, these efforts will reduce the overall carbon footprint of the on-course maintenance while continuing the impeccable landscaping standards for which Toro is renowned.


The Els Club Vilamoura will open its doors later this year, introducing a high-end experience for members. With an innovative concept and strategic partnerships, the club is set to become a game changer in the region, redefining golf in one of Europe’s most prestigious destinations.
