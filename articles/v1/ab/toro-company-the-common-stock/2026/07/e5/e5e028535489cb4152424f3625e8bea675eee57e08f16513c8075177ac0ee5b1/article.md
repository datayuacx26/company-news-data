# A Legacy of Innovation: Toro Brings Unified Landscaping Solutions to Equip Expo


### From autonomous mowers to versatile compact utility loaders, Toro will showcase a powerful lineup of landscape innovations designed to tackle today’s toughest challenges


Oct 23, 2025 |[Professional Contractor](https://newsroom.toro.com/en/professional-contractor) |[Rental](https://newsroom.toro.com/en/rental) |[Construction](https://newsroom.toro.com/en/construction)

BLOOMINGTON, Minn. (October 23, 2025) — Since 1914, The Toro Company has operated with a simple goal — to help our customers enrich the beauty, productivity and sustainability of the land. At this year’s Equip Expo, Oct. 21-24, in Louisville, Kentucky, we will honor more than a century’s worth of innovation and highlight the equipment that helps landscape contractors work faster, easier and smarter than ever before.


“For more than 100 years, Toro has focused on building quality relationships with customers,” said Edric Funk, president and chief operating officer at The Toro Company. “This tradition not only fosters trust but sparks innovative ideas across all our businesses and teams. We’re proud to honor and continue that legacy at Equip Expo.”


Products from nearly every Toro division will be on site to present a complete overview of the company’s diverse offerings. Visit us at booths 7148 and 7600D for an up-close look at the powerful machines that define Toro’s legacy and help landscape pros conquer modern day challenges.


**Equipment to Boost Efficiency and ROI**


No matter the time of year, crews are expected to do more with less — and they need equipment that rises to the occasion.


The new **GrandStand® MULTI FORCE® EVO** is powered by a rugged Kawasaki EVO 34.5 HP engine and can be equipped with various attachments for year-round efficiency and revenue. For hands-free precision, the new **Autonomous PROLINE™ AMI** uses intelligent technology to deliver consistent results, freeing up operators for other tasks. Similarly, the **TurfMaster® HDX** features a new caster front end on its 30" deck, giving operators the mobility to work in tight spaces and navigate diverse properties with ease.


Toro’s lineup of **Dingo®** compact utility loaders give landscape contractors the power and versatility to dig, haul, trench, grade, auger and more. Whether it’s the nimble TX 525, the high-torque TX 1000 TURBO or the powerful TXL 2000, there’s a model to meet every crew and jobsite need. Paired with the Ultra Buggy 2500 — designed for fast, efficient material transport — landscape contractors can overcome the toughest jobsite demands.


Customize the **Workman® UTX** to meet the exact needs of each operation. This versatile 4-wheel drive utility vehicle is made for tough work, featuring commercial-grade components and the highest payload in its class.


Above all, customer satisfaction is always a priority, and the **Eagle™ Plus** irrigation controller delivers with superior efficiency and water savings. An enhanced and intuitive internet-connected interface equips the user with real-time irrigation control from anywhere. Plus, the Eagle Plus controller comes in a variety of configurations, including multiple enclosure options.


**Battery-Powered Solutions**
Toro’s expanding lineup of battery-powered equipment provides electric versatility to meet challenges like noise and emission restrictions.


The Toro **eDingo** ® and electric **Ultra Buggy®** family offers compact, clean and quiet solutions for high-output tasks. The eDingo 500 and eDingo TX 750 are built to navigate tight spaces while powering through hauling and loading jobs. For material transport, the electric Ultra Buggy 2500-TS offers precise placement with a 180-degree swiveling tub, while the 2500-THL adds high-lift capability — easily clearing a 30-yard dumpster for fast, efficient debris removal.


Toro’s battery solutions also extend to grounds care with the **Groundsmaster** ® **e3300** . Built on the same rugged chassis and commercial-grade deck as its diesel-powered predecessor, it can be equipped with up to 17 HyperCell™ batteries for all-day power and productivity in the most demanding conditions.


“Our goal is simple: make it easier for customers to deliver their best work,” said Funk. “As your business grows and changes, Toro evolves with you. Whether you’re dealing with labor challenges, tight timelines or jobs that require more power, you can count on Toro tools and resources to get it done.”


To learn more about Toro’s latest innovations and explore how our integrated solutions can help you and your crew work more efficiently and productively, visit us at[toro.com](https://toro.com/) .

##### About Toro


With roots dating back to 1914, Toro is a leading worldwide provider of innovative solutions for the outdoor environment including turf and landscape maintenance, snow management, rental and construction equipment, and irrigation solutions. Through a strong network of distributors, dealers, retailers and rental stores in more than 125 countries, Toro helps customers care for golf courses, sports fields, public green spaces, commercial and residential properties, construction sites and agricultural operations.
