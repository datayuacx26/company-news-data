# Toro Introduces EagleTM Plus Series Irrigation Controller with Expanded Options


### Smart, Flexible and Economical Controller Boasts Elevated Interface and App Connectivity


Sep 29, 2025 |[Professional Contractor](https://newsroom.toro.com/en/professional-contractor)


-
-
-


BLOOMINGTON, Minn


. (September 29, 2025) — Toro introduces the new


**** EagleTM Plus Series, a


commercial-grade irrigation controller d


esigned for centralized, internet-based irrigation management. T


he Toro Eagle Plus Series is an expanded version of the previous Rain MasterTM Series controller and now


includes an enhanced user interface, expanded Smart AlertsTM and full iCentralTM and mobile maintenance app connectivity.


This powerful and scalable irrigation controller is optimized for professional grounds maintenance teams and contractors for a variety of properties from the smallest city parks to sprawling college campuses and municipalities, plus everything in between.


Eagle Plus simplifies system management while helping to maximize water efficiency.


Toro Eagle Plus users can seamlessly connect to the iCentral web-based platform that allows two-way remote control and monitoring. The ProMaxTM Connect app enhances convenience by allowing maintenance control, diagnostics and monitoring from anywhere via smartphone or tablet. Users can run tests, activate stations and programs, and troubleshoot in real time right from a mobile device.


The Eagle Plus Series also features Smart Alerts that notify users of flow errors, wire faults, or rain shutdowns via text or email. ET-based scheduling adjusts irrigation based on local weather with ZipETTM to optimize water usage without compromising turf quality.


“The Toro Eagle Plus is a testament to our commitment to providing powerful and accessible solutions to our customers,” says Sergio Ramos, irrigation central control marketing manager at Toro. “By including a long-term subscription to our iCentral platform and providing the ProMax Connect app at no cost, we’re delivering immense value and a new level of convenience for irrigation professionals.”


Designed with flexibility and cost in mind, Eagle Plus supports both conventional and 2-wire configurations with scalable support for up to 200 stations. It is available in a variety of enclosure options, including a new,


more economical plastic cabinet to suit a wide range of needs.


Customers with the Rain Master Eagle Plus controllers will continue to experience the same performance and reliability they have come to expect, now with the added support, service, and replacement parts backed by Toro’s century of proven expertise in turf and irrigation.


New Toro Eagle Plus customers will receive a 60-month free subscription to the iCentral system when purchased with iCentral card. The ProMax app is free to download for all android and Apple devices.


The Toro Eagle Plus Series is available through Toro’s authorized irrigation distributor network. To learn more, visit


[toro.com/eagleplus](http://www.toro.com/eagleplus) .
