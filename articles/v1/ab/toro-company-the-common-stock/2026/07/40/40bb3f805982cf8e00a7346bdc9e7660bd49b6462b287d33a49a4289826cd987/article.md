# Toro and FEGGA Extend Long-Standing Partnership to Support Greenkeeping Across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa


###


Jul 02, 2025 |[Golf](https://newsroom.toro.com/en-gb/golf)


BLOOMINGTON, Minn. (July, 2025) — Toro, and the Federation of European Golf Greenkeepers Associations (FEGGA) are proud to announce the extension of their historic partnership—one that has been in place since FEGGA’s formation in 1996. This renewed commitment reinforces both organisations’ shared dedication to supporting greenkeeper associations across the EMEA region.


This enduring collaboration continues to provide valuable guidance on common challenges facing the profession, while advancing education, environmental stewardship, and professional recognition in golf turf management.


“FEGGA has been a vital force for professional growth and advocacy in our industry,” said Andrew Ihrke, senior marketing manager, International at The Toro Company. “We are proud to extend our support and continue working together to promote excellence in greenkeeping throughout the world.”


FEGGA, headquartered in Sweden, plays an active daily role in promoting educational standards, EU policy awareness, and the recognition of greenkeepers across its member associations. By working closely with industry partners like Toro, the Federation helps elevate the profession through initiatives in sustainability, learning, and community building.


“Our relationship with Toro has been instrumental in the success of our programs,” added Joel Nunes, Executive Officer at FEGGA. “This partnership has helped us expand our impact across the region and tackle some of the most pressing challenges facing greenkeepers today.”


As two long-standing supporters of the industry, Toro and FEGGA look forward to continuing this impactful collaboration and driving positive change in golf course management across the EMEA region.
