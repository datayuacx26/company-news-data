# Toro’s Autonomous Solutions Create Time-Saving Efficiency


### Toro is always thinking of and creating new ways to help golf course superintendents and crews save what matters most — time. Meet two new innovations that do just that: the Turf Pro™ 300/500 Series and the Range Pro™ 100.


May 13, 2025 |[Golf](https://newsroom.toro.com/en-gb/golf) |


**Toro’s Autonomous Solutions Create Time-Saving Efficiency**


Toro is always creating new ways to help golf course superintendents and crews maximise their most valuable resource — time.


At the 2025 BTME and GCSAA Conference and trade shows, Toro showcased a complete electric and autonomous portfolio for the entire course — from fairways to roughs and clubhouse grounds to the driving range — to help free up time for tasks that require specialised skills.


Among these innovations are the[Turf Pro™ 300/500 Series](https://www.toro.com/en-gb/product/Turf-Pro-300-500-Series?srsltid=AfmBOoqU-FohyHWsJDan6QXToNY9_kJC1KMs67EV7qo5JLyiRWN-g1KA) and the[Range Pro™ 100](https://www.toro.com/en-gb/product/30931?srsltid=AfmBOorA-TprCld_sXIt0uGdeykjr9qtNx0mu2epCLbX1ty0MMymp6ZT) . Both are equipped with cutting-edge technology, offer quiet operation and deliver consistently superior results.


**Turf Pro 300/500**


The Turf Pro 300/500 autonomous mower provides a truly hands-free mowing experience. The Turf Pro 300 efficiently manages up to 45,000 m2 (11 acres)* with a 64 cm (25 in) mowing width, while the Turf Pro 500 handles up to 75,000 m2 (18.5 acres)* with a wider 103 cm (41 in) cut.


Unlike many autonomous mowers, no boundary wires are required for setup. Instead, RTK technology produces ultra-precise navigation, enabling both models to mow consistent patterns day after day. Wireless connectivity enables anytime monitoring, control and customisation of working perimeters, no-go zones, mowing patterns, cutting height, transport paths and more. When it’s time to recharge, the Turf Pro automatically returns to its charging station.


**Range Pro**


The Range Pro is an autonomous ball collector that can gather up to 350 balls at a time and an impressive 19,500 balls in a 24-hour period. This labour-saving device operates in defined patterns and can be set to target high-density areas. Utilising the same RTK technology as the Turf Pro for wireless setup and control, these two units can even operate simultaneously, allowing operators to monitor individual machines or entire fleets from their fingertips. At the end of its run, the Range Pro automatically returns to its charging station.


Discover more about the Turf Pro, Range Pro and other autonomous innovations, including the[GeoLink](https://www.toro.com/en-gb/product/GeoLink-Solutions-Autonomous-Fairway-Mower-Series?srsltid=AfmBOorV82-dzRtRVZeLm-EQilT2B8EOZppWx4hQd-IENE5YejP65ZDx)[®](https://www.toro.com/en/product/GeoLink-Solutions-Autonomous-Fairway-Mower-Series?srsltid=AfmBOorV82-dzRtRVZeLm-EQilT2B8EOZppWx4hQd-IENE5YejP65ZDx)[Solutions™ Autonomous Fairway Mower](https://www.toro.com/en-gb/product/GeoLink-Solutions-Autonomous-Fairway-Mower-Series?srsltid=AfmBOorV82-dzRtRVZeLm-EQilT2B8EOZppWx4hQd-IENE5YejP65ZDx) , at toro.com.
