# Bitmovin joins NVIDIA Inception program

**Denver – Colorado, October 10, 2024—**[Bitmovin](https://bitmovin.com/) **,** the leading provider of video streaming solutions, today announced it has joined[NVIDIA Inception](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4007246-1&h=2855583600&u=https%3A%2F%2Fnvda.ws%2F2BvtUc9&a=NVIDIA+Inception) , a program that nurtures startups revolutionizing industries with technological advancements so it can drive further AI-led innovations in the media and entertainmentment technology industry.


Bitmovin is a market leader in video streaming technologies for global digital media companies and service providers and ensures superior viewing experiences for global audiences. Bitmovin’s suite of solutions includes Playback, VOD Encoding, Live Encoding, and Streams, all designed to reduce operating expenses while maximizing revenues and retaining and growing subscribers.


NVIDIA Inception is a program designed to help startups evolve faster and accelerate the development of Generative AI applications and services. By joining the program, Bitmovin will benefit from access to NVIDIA’s technology and technical expertise, including the latest GPUs to efficiently handle extensive language models, such as large language models (LLMs) and domain-specific models. Joining the program also means Bitmovin will be connected to a community of startups and industry leaders who are advancing the possibilities of AI.


“NVIDIA is powering innovations that have ushered in the era of AI, and by joining the NVIDIA Inception program we can help accelerate AI use cases in the world of video streaming,” said **Stefan Lederer, CEO and co-founder of Bitmovin** “We’re already working with the University of Klagenfurt on Project ATHENA to see how AI can optimize quality in video streaming, and launched AI-led innovations such as our Analytics Session Interpreter, so joining the program and having the opportunity to work with forward-thinkers on more AI initiatives will help us deliver more impactful solutions in video streaming.”
