# Statement Pieces: How to Make Your Space Unforgettable

Your home should tell a sto­ry. Not a qui­et one, but a bold, inter­est­ing sto­ry that makes peo­ple stop and say, “ *where did you find that?* ” That’s the pow­er of a state­ment piece.


State­ment pieces are con­ver­sa­tion starters. They trans­form a room from “nice” to “wow.” And the best part? They don’t have to cost a fortune.


## **What Makes a Statement Piece?**


OK, let’s start with the basics. What the heck is a state­ment piece any­way, and who decides what makes the cut? While style might be sub­jec­tive, we can all agree that fur­ni­ture (or art) that com­mands atten­tion or evokes sur­prise is what a state­ment piece is all about. Here are a few examples.


- **Unapolo­get­i­cal­ly bold in col­or.** Think emer­ald green vel­vet sofa or a sun­shine yel­low credenza.
- **Dis­tinc­tive in shape.** Such as a sculp­tur­al accent chair, a sun­burst mir­ror, or a geo­met­ric cof­fee table.
- Unique­ly designed. This could be a vin­tage find, mid-cen­tu­ry clas­sic, or hand-made piece with real character
- Lay­ered in tex­ture. Try a woven wall hang­ing, a rat­tan book­shelf, or a brass-legged din­ing table.
- Pure visu­al inter­est. Abstract wall art, a pat­terned accent wall, or a show-stop­ping light­ing fixture.


What do they have in com­mon? They make a room feel inten­tion­al and personal.


## **Why Go Bold?**


Play­ing it safe might be eas­i­er. But it’s also for­get­table. Com­mit­ting to just one or two state­ment pieces in a room can embold­en you and ground your space in your per­son­al­i­ty. Here’s what happens:


### Your space feels more “you”


A bold choice is an act of self-expres­sion. It says you have taste, you’re con­fi­dent in your style, and you’re not afraid to take a cre­ative risk. Your home becomes a reflec­tion of who you are, not a cat­a­log page. (Remem­ber Phoebe and Rachel’s apothe­cary table?)


### One piece sets the tone for everything


A great state­ment piece anchors a room. Once you have it, build­ing the rest of your décor becomes eas­i­er. Col­ors, tex­tures, and oth­er pieces nat­u­ral­ly fall into place around it.


### It’s a conversation starter


Friends notice and ask ques­tions. Your space becomes a back­drop for sto­ries, which is part of the mag­ic of bold design. It’s going to make you want to throw par­ties so more peo­ple will see it. (No, seriously.)


### You can save money on quality


Here’s the sec­ond­hand secret: a stun­ning mid-cen­tu­ry cre­den­za or design­er chair that is pre-loved costs a frac­tion of the new retail price. Your room looks high-end like an Insta­gram show­room, but with­out the high-end price tag.


## **How to Style Statement Pieces Like a Pro**


Resist the urge to sur­round your state­ment piece with oth­er bold ele­ments, giv­ing it some space to shine. Pair a dra­mat­ic sofa with neu­tral walls and a sim­ple area rug. Hang strik­ing wall art with plen­ty of white space around it. The min­i­mal­ism makes the bold­ness even more powerful.


Pull col­ors or tex­tures from your state­ment piece into small­er décor items. If you have a jew­el-tone vel­vet chair, echo that col­or in throw pil­lows or a piece of art. If you have a tex­tured wood cre­den­za, repeat that warmth in a wood­en mir­ror frame or shelv­ing. These con­nec­tions cre­ate harmony.


Shop­ping vin­tage is a great way to intro­duce a state­ment piece. They’re one-of-a-kind and already car­ry his­to­ry. Pair a vin­tage state­ment chair with mod­ern side tables. Mix a sculp­tur­al brass lamp with min­i­mal­ist wall shelves. This con­trast keeps spaces from feel­ing dat­ed or over­ly themed.


***Pro tip:*** *One state­ment piece does­n’t have to be fur­ni­ture. A strik­ing piece of wall art, an over­sized mir­ror, or a sculp­tur­al floor lamp can anchor a space just as effec­tive­ly as a sofa or cre­den­za. Think beyond tra­di­tion­al furniture.*
