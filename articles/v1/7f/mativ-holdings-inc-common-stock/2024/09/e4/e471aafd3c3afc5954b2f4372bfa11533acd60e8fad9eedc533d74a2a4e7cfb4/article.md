# Mativ To Showcase Innovative Label Solutions

ALPHARETTA, Ga.–(BUSINESS WIRE)– Mativ Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: MATV), a global leader in specialty materials, is showcasing its enterprise solutions for the label industry at the upcoming LabelExpo Americas at booth 1205 from September 10 – 12 in Chicago.


“We are excited to highlight our breadth of capabilities that enable us to collaborate, innovate, and most importantly, support our customers’ customers,” said Shae Hinman, Director of Sales, Americas, Mativ. “This collective display is a tremendous opportunity for us to show customers how our suite of solutions helps to connect, protect, and purify our world.”


Mativ’s label solutions are aligned under the brands of Indafor, Itasa and Neenah and support a wide range of applications, including high performance labels that stand up to the harshest environments, disposable and efficient food rotation labels, premium image wine and spirit labels, and a broad array of release liner products that serve end markets such as hygiene, industrials and composites.


“Our extensive product and technology portfolio combined with a solution-driven team of experts gives customers access to unique capabilities for premium applications,” said Jeffrey Kirk, Sr. Manager, Product Management, Indafor. “As a trusted partner, we continue to provide industry-leading products and technologies to our customers around the world.”


One such technology is our Dispersa ® label for food rotation labels. Dispersa ® is a cellulose-based material engineered to disperse in water, along with any imprinted or handwritten graphics. Rather than recycling after use, this lightweight product disperses in water.


“This is a great opportunity for our customers to see labels made from Dispersa ® in action with our live demonstration,” Kirk said. “We want to do our part in helping customers reach their sustainability goals.”
