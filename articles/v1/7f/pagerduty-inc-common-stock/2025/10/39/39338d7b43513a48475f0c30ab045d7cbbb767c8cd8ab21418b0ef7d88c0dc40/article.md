# PagerDuty Strengthens Momentum in Asia Pacific Region with Appointment of Callum Eade as Vice President, APAC Sales


**SYDNEY, Australia – OCTOBER 21, 2025** –


[PagerDuty, Inc.](https://www.pagerduty.com/) (NYSE:PD), a global leader in digital operations management, announced the appointment of Callum Eade to the position of vice president, Asia Pacific Sales. Callum will lead PagerDuty’s next phase of growth in the Asia-Pacific region, bringing the PagerDuty Operations Cloud and modern incident management to customers across the region to solve their biggest digital operations challenges.


“We are thrilled to appoint Callum as vice president, Asia Pacific Sales since he brings a wealth of enterprise sales relationships and years of experience growing this region,” said Jennifer Tejada, Chairperson and CEO, PagerDuty. “His leadership has already delivered strong results while architecting a high-performance culture grounded in operational discipline and championing the customer.”


Eade joins PagerDuty with over two decades of enterprise technology leadership experience and has worked at companies such as Citrix, VMware, and UiPath. Eade brings a proven track record of driving excellence-driven environments and customer-centric strategies across complex, high-growth markets.


“It is an incredibly exciting time to be at PagerDuty as I’m dedicated to ensuring teams are empowered to deliver value at every stage of the customer journey,” said Callum Eade, vice president Asia Pacific Sales. “Leading the Asia Pacific team as we help our customers transform their digital operations is both a privilege and a responsibility I deeply value. We’re building something truly special, anchored in trust, high performance and unwavering commitment to deliver meaningful outcomes for our customers.”


He holds a Bachelor of Business Management from Lincoln University.
