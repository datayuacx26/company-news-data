# Huron Earns Great Place To Work Certification™ for Second Consecutive Year

*87% of employees say Huron is a great place to work*


CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Apr. 14, 2026-- Global professional services firm Huron (NASDAQ: HURN) today announced it has been[Certified™ by Great Place To Work®](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.greatplacetowork.com%2Fcertified-company%2F7040776&esheet=54498629&newsitemid=20260414612998&lan=en-US&anchor=Certified%26%238482%3B+by+Great+Place+To+Work%26%23174%3B&index=1&md5=764c7d053501366eb285d641f879dd5e) in the United States, Canada, India, Singapore, and the United Kingdom for the second consecutive year. This recognition is based on employee feedback and reinforces Huron’s commitment to fostering a workplace where people feel supported, engaged, and able to do their best work.


"We are honored to be Great Place to Work-Certified™ again this year," said[Mark Hussey](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.huronconsultinggroup.com%2Fcompany%2Fexperts%2Femployees%2Fmark-hussey&esheet=54498629&newsitemid=20260414612998&lan=en-US&anchor=Mark+Hussey&index=2&md5=2e92eb1ac599a27d3b0b3eeffd5a8f1a) , CEO and President of[Huron](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.huronconsultinggroup.com%2F&esheet=54498629&newsitemid=20260414612998&lan=en-US&anchor=Huron&index=3&md5=7791a043e381194ecc4654dd37dba8df) . “Earning this recognition for a second consecutive year reflects the consistency and strength of our culture and the dedication of our people. It is especially meaningful because it comes directly from the responses of our employees, reflecting their experiences, their engagement, and their belief in the work we do together to create impact for our clients and in our communities.”


Huron continues to invest in programs that support professional and personal growth and enhance the employee experience, including leadership development, flexible work arrangements, and well-being initiatives. The company prioritizes a culture of collaboration, inclusion, and continuous learning, empowering its people to bring forward their best ideas and build meaningful careers. This focus supports stronger client outcomes and contributes to long-term value for both its people and the business.


Great Place To Work® is the global authority on workplace culture, employee experience, and the leadership behaviors proven to deliver market-leading revenue, employee retention and increased innovation. According to[Great Place To Work research](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.greatplacetowork.com%2Fresources%2Fblog%2Fjob-seekers-are-4-5x-more-likely-to-find-a-great-boss-at-a-certified-great-workplace&esheet=54498629&newsitemid=20260414612998&lan=en-US&anchor=Great+Place+To+Work+research&index=4&md5=3fb92ac4ee95710b00b979c35814f578) , job seekers are 4.5 times more likely to find a great boss at a Certified great workplace. Additionally, employees at Certified workplaces are 93% more likely to look forward to coming to work.
