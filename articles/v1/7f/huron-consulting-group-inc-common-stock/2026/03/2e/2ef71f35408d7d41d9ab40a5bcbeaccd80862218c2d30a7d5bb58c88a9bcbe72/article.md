# Huron Announces Ekta Singh-Bushell Will Step Down From its Board of Directors at the Completion of Her Current Term

CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Mar. 20, 2026-- On March 20, 2026, Ekta Singh-Bushell, a director of Huron Consulting Group Inc. (the "Company") since 2019, notified the Company of her decision to step down from the Board of Directors (“Board”) following the completion of her current term. Consequently, Ms. Singh-Bushell will not stand for re-election to the Board at the Annual Meeting of Stockholders, which the Company anticipates holding on May 8, 2026.


"We are grateful for Ekta’s many contributions, including her service as chair of our inaugural Technology & Information Security committee, and chair of our Nominating & Corporate Governance, and Compensation committees. While we will miss her insights and expertise, we wish her continued success in her future endeavors," said Hugh Sawyer, Non-Executive Chairman of the Board.


"It has been a privilege and pleasure to serve Huron and work alongside such a dedicated team," said Ms. Singh-Bushell.
