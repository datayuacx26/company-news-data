# Huron Leaders Named 2026 Consulting Magazine Top Consultants

*John DiDonato recipient of the Lifetime Achievement Award*


*Paul Praveen recognized in the Global & Cross-Border Consulting category*


*Andy Waldeck recognized in the Strategy and Transformation category*


CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jun. 29, 2026-- Global professional services firm Huron (NASDAQ: HURN), today announced that three of its senior leaders have been recognized by *Consulting Magazine* as 2026 Top Consultants, underscoring the depth of expertise, innovation, and leadership that continues to drive value for its clients and support the firm's long-term growth strategy. John DiDonato received the Lifetime Achievement Award, Paul Praveen was honored in the Global & Cross-Border Consulting category, and Andy Waldeck was recognized in the Strategy and Transformation category.


“John, Paul, and Andy each reflect the depth of expertise, leadership, and commitment to client impact that define our company,” said[Mark Hussey](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.huronconsultinggroup.com%2Fen%2Four-experts%2Fmark-hussey&esheet=54561857&newsitemid=20260629348172&lan=en-US&anchor=Mark+Hussey&index=1&md5=eb10404cff751e7739c4682b29a69771) , chief executive officer and president of ****[Huron](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.huronconsultinggroup.com%2F&esheet=54561857&newsitemid=20260629348172&lan=en-US&anchor=Huron&index=2&md5=8eb3331a59078eb8e5daa7bcfb45c640) . “Through their strong leadership, innovation, and contributions they have expanded our global capabilities, helped organizations navigate complexity, accelerate transformation, and achieve meaningful outcomes. I congratulate them on these well-deserved recognitions.”


John DiDonato received Consulting Magazine's Lifetime Achievement Award in recognition of a distinguished career spanning more than four decades. A pioneer in restructuring and operational transformation, DiDonato has guided organizations through critical inflection points, helping leaders address complex challenges and unlock value. His entrepreneurial vision played a foundational role in establishing Huron's business advisory capability, creating a platform that has become a key driver of the firm's impact.


Paul Praveen was recognized in the Global & Cross-Border Consulting category for his leadership in scaling Huron's global operations and delivery capabilities. As Huron's India country leader, Praveen has built high-performing teams and expanded integrated delivery models that enhance client service, strengthen operational efficiency, and support the firm's ability to deliver transformative results at scale. His leadership has been instrumental in advancing Huron's global footprint and creating greater value for clients across the globe.


Andy Waldeck was recognized in the Strategy and Transformation category for his leadership in helping organizations identify new avenues for growth and build strategies that endure amid disruption. As leader of Huron's Innosight business, Waldeck advises executives and boards on innovation, growth strategy, and transformation initiatives that position organizations for long-term success. His work has helped clients translate complexity into a clear strategic vision and measurable business outcomes.


Consulting Magazine’s annual Top Consultants awards recognize professionals who have demonstrated exceptional leadership, innovation, and impact in helping organizations solve complex challenges and achieve strategic objectives.
