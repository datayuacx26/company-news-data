# VenoStent Accepted Into J&J JLabs @ TMC

[JLabs is an innovation center](https://jlabs.jnjinnovation.com/locations/jlabs-tmc) founded by Johnson & Johnson to help healthcare companies grow and innovate. It is comprised of numerous medical device, pharmaceutical, biotech, and diagnostic companies who are hoping to help patient populations around the world.


JLabs will provide VenoStent with a massive network of investors, mentors, and space for testing and innovating.


As VenoStent plans to develop our current product and grow into an advanced biomaterials company, we are so pleased to have a partner in Johnson & Johnson to help us with our mission.
