# We’re Going Contactless!

We’re excited to share that contactless technology is coming to Bank7 Visa® Debit Cards.


This upgrade will allow you to simply tap your card to pay at participating retailers — making checkout faster, easier, and just as secure as inserting or swiping your card.


**What to Expect**


We’ll be rolling out contactless cards in phases over the next few months.


***Beginning Now:***
All new Visa® Debit Cards ordered at a branch will be issued as contactless cards and mailed directly to customers.


***Coming in Late March:***
Contactless cards will be available through Instant Issue at our branches*, allowing you to receive a contactless card on the spot.


*Where instant issue is available


***Starting in April:***
We will begin reissuing contactless cards to existing debit cardholders. Cards will be replaced automatically up to 60 days before their current expiration month, regardless of the year.


*Example:* If your current debit card expires in June 2027, you’ll automatically receive a new contactless card in April 2026.


**Do I Need to Do Anything?**


No action is required.


If you are a current debit cardholder, your contactless card will be mailed to you automatically based on your expiration month. You’ll keep the same card number — just with upgraded tap-to-pay technology.


If you order a new debit card after March 1, your card will already be contactless-enabled.
