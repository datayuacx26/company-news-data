# Bank7 Corp. Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend on Common Stock

June 4, 2026 – Bank7 Corp. (NASDAQ: BSVN), the parent company of Oklahoma City-based Bank7, today announced the declaration of a quarterly cash dividend of $0.27 per share on its outstanding common stock. The dividend will be paid on July 7, 2026, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on June 19, 2026.
