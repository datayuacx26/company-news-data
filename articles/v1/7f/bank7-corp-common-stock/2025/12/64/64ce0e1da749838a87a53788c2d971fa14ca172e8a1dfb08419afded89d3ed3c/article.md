# Bank7 Helps Fulfill Christmas Wish Lists for 80 Foster Children

Bank7 partnered with Citizens for Caring Children this holiday season to help make Christmas brighter for foster children throughout Oklahoma. Through this collaboration, our team worked together to fulfill 80 wish list, ensuring they each receive gifts chosen just for them.


We’re grateful for the opportunity to support an organization that brings hope and comfort to children in need. Community giving remains at the heart of who we are, and we’re proud to play a small part in making the season a little more joyful.
