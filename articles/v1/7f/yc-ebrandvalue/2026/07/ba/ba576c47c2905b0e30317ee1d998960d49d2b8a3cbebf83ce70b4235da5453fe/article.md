Introducing our latest update to our software, featuring the new "Author Enrichment" feature. This powerful tool allows brands to gain valuable insights into their target audience by scraping authors' public content from social media platforms.

With this feature, brands can identify authors who have a strong following and are highly respected in their field. By building relationships with these authors, brands can leverage their influence to reach a larger audience.
