Camden Property Trust, an S&P 500 Company, is a real estate company primarily engaged in the ownership, management, development, redevelopment, acquisition, and construction of multifamily apartment communities. Camden owns and operates 176 properties containing 59,676 apartment homes across the United States. Upon completion of 3 properties currently under development, the Company’s portfolio will increase to 60,838 apartment homes in 179 properties. Camden has been recognized as one of the 100 Best Companies to Work For® by FORTUNE magazine for 19 consecutive years, most recently ranking #13.
