## Why DIY Conversions Are Hard


Most EV conversion projects fail at the same points: fitting batteries safely, managing heat, and getting it street legal. You can buy a motor and controller easily enough. The hard part is making everything work together. Battery boxes that fit the floor pan, coolant routing, crash protection, getting it certified.


Every vehicle is different. Different floor geometry, different suspension mounts, different crash structures. Unless you have serious automotive engineering experience, you're guessing your way through critical safety systems.
