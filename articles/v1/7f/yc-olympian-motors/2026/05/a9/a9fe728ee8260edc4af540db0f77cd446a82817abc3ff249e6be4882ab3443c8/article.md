SECTION 02


## A New Chassis for an Old Idea


Replica building is one of the oldest acts of love in car culture. A builder studies a silhouette, sources a donor drivetrain, fabricates a tube frame, and spends two thousand hours putting it together in a garage. The result is something the factory never sold — a personal answer to a question the original car asked decades ago.


The tradition is alive. The donor drivetrain is dying. Carbureted V8s are getting harder to source, harder to register, and harder to live with. Emissions law is closing in. The next generation of builders wants the silhouette without the apology.


The Olympus Platform is the replacement for the donor car. A price that lands inside what serious builders already spend on a crate engine and a transmission — and a chassis that outperforms the donor in every measurable way.
