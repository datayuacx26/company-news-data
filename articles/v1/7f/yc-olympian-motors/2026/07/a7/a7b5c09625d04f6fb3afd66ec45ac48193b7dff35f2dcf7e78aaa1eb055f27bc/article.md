# Convert Classic Car to Electric: Modern EV Conversion Guide

**Convert classic cars to electric** without compromising what makes them desirable. Keep the design. Get rid of carburetor problems and constant maintenance.


## Why Electric Makes Sense


Classic cars look great. They're also unreliable, hard to maintain, and underpowered by modern standards. Carburetors that need constant tuning. Cooling systems that overheat. Brakes that barely work.


Electric conversion fixes this. 174 HP instantly available. Starts every time. No maintenance beyond tires and brakes. Daily driver reliable.


### Performance Upgrade


Full torque from zero RPM. Batteries in the floor drop the center of gravity dramatically. Less body roll, better handling. Optional traction control prevents wheelspin.


Vintage look, modern reliability


## The Platform Method


Instead of gutting the original car and cramming batteries into the floor, mount the classic body on a purpose-built[electric chassis](https://olympianmotors.com/blog/electric-chassis) . The[Olympus Platform](https://olympianmotors.com/olympus-kit) handles weight distribution, cooling, safety. The body stays original. Learn more about[EV conversion technology](https://olympianmotors.com/blog/ev-conversion) .


### How It Works


1. **Remove body from original chassis**
2. **Configure Olympus wheelbase** — 183-196" to match proportions
3. **Fab mounting brackets** — Interface vintage body to modern chassis
4. **Wire and test**


## What Stays Original


The body, chrome, paint, badging, trim. All original. Interior can stay period-correct or integrate modern screens. Your choice.


## Range and Charging


42 kWh battery: 155-200 miles. 54 kWh: 200-255 miles. DC fast charge in 30-40 minutes. Level 2 overnight.


## Cost


Olympus Platform: $40k. Body adaptation: $15k-$30k. Total: $55k-$70k for a complete electric conversion with zero maintenance. Competitive with traditional restoration that still needs constant work.
