# Electric Vehicle Chassis | EV Skateboard Platform

## Modular EV Platform Architecture


The Olympus **electric chassis** utilizes a modular architecture. Wheelbase adjusts from 183" to 196" for different vehicle segments without altering the core powertrain.

## Integration for OEMs


The pre-engineered **EV skateboard platform** reduces development cycles by 24-36 months. Standard CAN bus protocols simplify integration with body electronics.
