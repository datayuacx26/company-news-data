# Build Retail Commercial Vehicles with Olympus EV Platform

What about the body? Do you build that too?


We sell the rolling chassis. You add the body. Our engineering team can recommend upfitters or help with custom body design, or you can work with your own fabricator. We provide CAD files and mounting specifications.


How long does a full build take from platform to finished vehicle?


Platform delivery takes 6-12 weeks. Body fabrication and installation varies by complexity—a simple box body might take 4-6 weeks, a full food truck buildout could be 3-4 months.


What happens if I need service or warranty work?


We handle warranty service directly from our California facility. For routine maintenance, any EV-certified shop can work on the platform. We provide technical documentation and support to your local service provider.


Can I power equipment from the vehicle battery?


Yes. The 400V system can supply auxiliary equipment. For food trucks, this means running cooking equipment directly from the main battery. For service vehicles, you can power tools and compressors. We provide integration support for high-power accessories.


What's the realistic range with a heavy commercial body?


Expect 100-150 miles with a full food truck or box body at maximum payload. For lighter service vehicle configurations, 150-200 miles is realistic. DC fast charging gets you from 30% to 80% in under 30 minutes if you need to extend your day.


Do you offer fleet discounts?


Yes. Volume pricing is available for orders of 5+ platforms. Contact us directly for fleet quotes. We also offer technical partnership programs for manufacturers building multiple units.


DC fast charging specs?


CCS2 connector. 30% to 80% in under 30 minutes.


What's the warranty coverage?


3-year or 100,000-mile warranty covering the drivetrain, battery system, and vehicle controls. Service handled directly from our California facility.


Is there a minimum order?


No. Single unit orders accepted.


Where is the platform built?


Designed and assembled in Irvine, California.


Can I see one in person before ordering?


Yes. Schedule a test drive at our Irvine, California facility. You can drive the platform, inspect the chassis, and discuss your specific application with our engineering team.
