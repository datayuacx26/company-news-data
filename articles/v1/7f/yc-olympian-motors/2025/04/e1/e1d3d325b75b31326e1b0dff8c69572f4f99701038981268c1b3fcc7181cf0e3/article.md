# Build Industrial Utility Vehicles with Olympus EV Platform

**100% Electric.** Zero emissions. Zero fuel costs.


**Customizable Dimensions.** Length, width, and ground clearance can be configured to match your application. Wheelbase options from 105 to 153 inches. Configure your platform and purchase directly from the[Olympus Kit page](https://olympianmotors.com/olympus-kit) .


**Fully Mobile.** Complete drivable vehicle. No flatbed required for transport. Max speed 121 mph (within safety and hazard standards).


**Faster and Affordable.** Traditional EV development takes 8-18 months and costs millions. Our Lite platform starts at $27.5K. The Olympus platform is ready to drive. Mount your body, equipment, or machinery on a proven, tested, automotive-grade chassis. If needed, you can make it L4 autonomous.


**Single Unit Orders.** No minimum order quantities. You can purchase 1 platform for your business or sixty for your fleet. We'll partner with you when you are ready to scale to higher volume production.


**Open Architecture.** CAD design files, electrical schematics, pin diagram, T-Box telematics, ECU access, SDK access, BLE module, and CAN bus communication included. Olympus is an open platform, flexible with various upper-body vehicle configurations. It covers class 2, class 3, and class 4 vehicle categories across chassis, flat bed, VAN, and mini trucks.


**L4 Autonomous (Optional).** All platforms are drive-by-wire and brake-by-wire, ready for autonomous integration. Olympian is working with NVIDIA/Alpayamo to deliver L4 autonomy by 2027.


**Build and Customize Your Own.** Depending on your business requirements, operational and technical needs, you can build your own company vehicle. Our engineering team is ready to help with concept, design, prototyping, production, and assembly. We have been proudly working with clients and partners across the United States and Canada, helping them build new electric mobility solutions powered by the Olympus Platform.
