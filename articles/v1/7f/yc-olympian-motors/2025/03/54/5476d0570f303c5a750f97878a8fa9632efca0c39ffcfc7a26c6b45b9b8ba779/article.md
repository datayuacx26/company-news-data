## Why Build Your Own Car?


Some people want a car that does not exist. Not a modified production vehicle. Not a restomod. Something entirely new that matches a vision only they can see.


Others want the experience of creation — the satisfaction of building something with their hands that actually works. Driving a machine you made yourself hits different than driving something off a lot.


And some want capability that no manufacturer offers. A specific wheelbase. A unique body style. Performance characteristics tuned exactly to their needs.

## Frequently Asked Questions


Do I need automotive engineering experience? ▼


No. The platform handles all powertrain complexity. Basic fabrication skills or access to a custom shop are enough to build a body and mount it. You focus on the creative work.


Can I register and drive my custom car legally? ▼


Registration requirements vary by state and country. Many jurisdictions have kit car or special construction vehicle categories. The platform provides documentation to support registration processes.


What if I want to build multiple cars? ▼


The platform scales from single builds to production volumes. Custom shops and small manufacturers use Olympus as a foundation for limited-run vehicles.


How much does it cost to build your own car? ▼


The platform starts at $27,500. Total build cost depends on body complexity and materials. Simple bodies add $15,000-30,000. Complex custom work can exceed $100,000. Still far less than developing a vehicle from scratch.


What tools do I need? ▼


Basic hand tools, a welder for body mounting, and standard automotive equipment. The platform does not require specialized EV tools — battery and motor systems are sealed and serviced by Olympian Motors if needed.


Can I build a car in my home garage? ▼


Yes. The platform fits in a standard two-car garage. You need 240V power for charging and enough space to work around the vehicle. Many builders complete their projects in home workshops.


Is there a builder community? ▼


Yes. Olympian Motors maintains a builder network where owners share progress, solutions, and fabrication tips. We also connect new builders with experienced custom shops when needed.
