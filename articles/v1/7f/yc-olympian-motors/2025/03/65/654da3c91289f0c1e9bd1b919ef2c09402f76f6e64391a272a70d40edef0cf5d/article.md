SECTION 01


## Why Electric Food Trucks


Traditional food trucks run on two engines. One moves the truck. Another powers the kitchen — usually a loud, fuel-hungry generator bolted to the back. That generator burns through diesel while you serve, creating noise and fumes that affect everyone nearby.


An electric food truck eliminates the generator entirely. The same battery that drives the truck powers your kitchen equipment. Silent operation. Zero local emissions. Dramatically lower fuel costs.

## Better Venue Access


Many venues ban food trucks because of noise and emissions. Corporate campuses, indoor events, hospitals, schools, and upscale locations often have strict policies. An electric food truck opens doors that stay closed to diesel.


Indoor events are the biggest opportunity. Trade shows, conventions, and private events inside buildings are off-limits to combustion vehicles. Electric food trucks can operate anywhere a golf cart can.


### Indoor Events


Convention centers, expo halls, and indoor markets. Serve hot food where diesel trucks cannot even enter.


### Corporate Campuses


Tech companies and offices with sustainability requirements. Electric trucks fit their environmental policies.


### Hospitals & Schools


Locations with strict air quality requirements. Zero emissions means zero objections.


### Residential Areas


Neighborhoods with noise ordinances. Silent operation means no complaints, longer hours.

## Frequently Asked Questions


Can the battery power kitchen equipment all day? +


Yes. The 42-72 kWh battery options provide enough capacity for a full service day plus driving to and from your location. Most operators charge overnight and have plenty of power for 8-12 hours of kitchen operation.


What kitchen equipment works with electric? +


Any standard commercial kitchen equipment with electric power. Induction cooktops, electric grills, fryers, refrigeration, ventilation. The platform provides clean, stable power that is actually better for sensitive equipment than generator power.


How much does an electric food truck cost? +


The Olympus platform starts at $38,500. Kitchen build-out costs depend on your equipment and design choices. Total cost is comparable to a traditional food truck when you factor in the eliminated generator, lower fuel costs, and reduced maintenance.


What if I run out of battery during service? +


Proper planning prevents this. The battery management system shows remaining capacity and estimated run time. For extended events, you can plug into venue power to supplement. Most operators find they return home with 20-40% battery remaining after a full day.


Can I run propane cooking equipment alongside electric? +


Yes. Many operators use propane for high-heat cooking like woks or grills while running refrigeration, lighting, and ventilation on battery power. This hybrid approach gives you flame cooking without the generator noise and emissions.


What permits are needed for an electric food truck? +


Standard food truck permits apply. Some jurisdictions actually have easier permitting for electric vehicles due to zero emissions. Health department requirements for kitchen equipment remain the same regardless of power source.


How do I maintain the battery with daily commercial use? +


The battery system is designed for commercial duty cycles. Charge overnight using standard Level 2 charging. The battery management system handles cell balancing and thermal management automatically. Annual inspections are recommended but routine maintenance is minimal.
