## What Is an Open Source Electric Car?


An open source electric car is more than just a vehicle. It is a complete framework that gives you access to all the technical information needed to build, modify, or repair an electric vehicle yourself. This includes detailed CAD files for every mechanical component, electrical schematics for the entire power system, software source code for the vehicle computer, and comprehensive assembly documentation.


Traditional automakers treat their vehicles as black boxes. You buy the car, but you never truly own the knowledge behind it. Open source changes this completely. You get the same level of technical detail that engineers use to design the vehicle in the first place.

SECTION 02


## Why Open Source Matters for EVs


The automotive industry has operated on secrecy for over a century. Proprietary designs, locked software, and dealer-only repairs have created a system where car owners are dependent on manufacturers for everything. Open source electric cars break this model entirely.


I


### Independence from Manufacturers


When you have complete documentation, you are not dependent on a single company for parts or repairs. Components can be sourced from multiple suppliers.


A


### Accelerated Innovation


Open source projects benefit from collective intelligence. When hundreds of builders work with the same platform, improvements happen faster.


L


### Lower Costs


Open source documentation allows competitive manufacturing. Multiple suppliers can produce the same component, driving prices down through competition.

SECTION 05


## Assembly Process


Building an open source electric car follows a structured process. The platform arrives as a complete rolling chassis with all major systems installed. From there, you add body panels, interior components, and any customizations.


1


### Platform Preparation


Inspect the chassis and verify all components against the documentation. Connect diagnostic equipment and run system checks. The platform should drive under its own power at this stage.


2


### Body Integration


Mount your body design using the documented attachment points. Route any additional wiring for lights, doors, and accessories. Install glass, seals, and exterior trim.


3


### Interior Completion


Install seats, dashboard components, and interior panels. Connect HVAC ducting and cabin controls. Complete final wiring for accessories and entertainment systems.


4


### Testing and Calibration


Perform full system diagnostics. Calibrate steering and brake systems. Test all safety features. Complete road testing and final adjustments.

SECTION 08


## Frequently Asked Questions


What does open source mean for an electric car? +


Open source means you receive complete documentation including CAD files, electrical schematics, and assembly instructions. You can build, modify, repair, and improve the vehicle using this information. Nothing is hidden or proprietary.


Do I need engineering experience? +


The Olympus platform arrives as a complete rolling chassis. Basic mechanical skills are sufficient for body integration. The documentation is detailed enough for motivated builders without formal engineering training.


Can I manufacture parts myself using the CAD files? +


Yes. The CAD files include full specifications and tolerances. You can manufacture replacement parts, modifications, or entirely new components using the documentation provided.


Is the software also open source? +


The vehicle control system uses documented CAN bus protocols. You can develop custom software, integrate autonomous systems, or modify vehicle behavior through the standard interfaces.


How does this compare to buying a traditional EV? +


Traditional EVs are closed systems. You cannot access technical documentation, manufacture parts, or make significant modifications. Open source gives you complete ownership and control over your vehicle.
