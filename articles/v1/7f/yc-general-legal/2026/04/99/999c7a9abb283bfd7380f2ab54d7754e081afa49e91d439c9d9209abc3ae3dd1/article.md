# Optimizing Legal Operations to Ensure a Frictionless Client Experience

If you read our CPO JP Mohler’s recent piece on[why we built a law firm](https://general.legal/blog/we-build-for-clients) , you already know our core thesis: we are entirely obsessed with client outcomes, not preserving legacy legal workflows. But as someone who sits in Client Operations handling success and billing here at[General Legal](https://general.legal/) , I look at that obsession through a slightly different lens. While our engineers and attorneys are busy leveraging frontier AI stacks to turn around commercial contracts in hours instead of weeks, my job is to ensure that everything *after* the legal work feels just as seamless, fast, and respectful of your time.


Traditional outside counsel has made a habit of hiding behind the opaque wall of the billable hour, making simple answers take days and invoicing feel like an exercise in financial anxiety. We think that’s an incredibly broken way to treat founders and operators. Because we operate as an AI-native firm, we’ve completely eliminated the friction of the intake-to-answer pipeline. When a client pings us via Slack our experienced attorneys deliver precise, real-time answers right away. No waiting for a junior associate to search a dusty database, and no paying for the time it takes them to do it.


That same commitment to radical efficiency extends directly to how we bill. We have integrated Stripe to automatically send direct, transparent invoices straight to our clients for our outcome-based, flat-fee pricing per matter. Traditional law firms love to send confusing, 20-page line-item PDFs thirty days late, forcing your finance team to audit why they were charged $150 for a five-minute phone call. With our transparent pricing structure and direct Stripe invoicing, paying for your legal services takes seconds. You get[clear, predictable flat fees](https://general.legal/pricing) —like our standard $500 contract negotiations—delivered straight to your inbox via an invoice. It turns billing from an administrative headache into an afterthought.


Ultimately, we measure the success of these operational choices using the only metric that actually matters: whether our clients love working with us. Right now, our Client Satisfaction (CSAT) score sits at a remarkable 95%. In an industry where outside counsel is usually viewed as a bottleneck to closing deals, a 95% CSAT tells us that our combination of elite human judgment and automated operations is working. We aren’t just making legal advice better, faster, and cheaper; we’re building a firm where the operational reality finally matches the speed of the businesses we serve.


If you are tired of opaque hourly billing, slow email threads, and administrative bottlenecks, it is time to experience legal counsel built for the way you operate.
