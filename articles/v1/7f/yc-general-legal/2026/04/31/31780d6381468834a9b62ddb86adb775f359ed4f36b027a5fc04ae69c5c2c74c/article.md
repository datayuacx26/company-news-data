If you’re evaluating Eudia AI alternatives, this guide breaks down the **top seven options** , pointing out where they outperform Eudia and which teams they’re best suited for.

## Alternative #1: Harvey


Harvey is one of the most widely adopted legal AI products on the market, serving **over 142,000 professionals across more than 1,500 law firms** and in-house legal teams worldwide.


It’s also used by more than 75 Am Law 100 firms, making it one of the most established legal AI vendors in the enterprise market.


Harvey is particularly well-suited for these major law firms because it was designed for **high-volume legal work at scale** , combining legal research, drafting, due diligence, and collaboration workflows into a single platform that can support hundreds or even thousands of legal professionals simultaneously.


### Why choose Harvey as an Eudia AI alternative


Harvey outperforms Eudia in several areas.


Its Harvey Vault product can analyze up to 10,000 documents simultaneously during large M&A transactions, making it a stronger choice for **large-scale due diligence projects** . It also offers deeper legal research capabilities through partnerships with providers like LexisNexis, giving users access to case law, regulations, and legal authorities across multiple jurisdictions.


This makes it a strong Eudia AI alternative for law firms, transaction-heavy legal teams, and organizations that **prioritize legal research, due diligence, and cross-border legal work over company-specific knowledge management.**


That said, Eudia remains a better fit for in-house legal departments looking to operationalize institutional knowledge, enforce internal playbooks at scale, and combine AI workflows with human legal services under a single platform.


In addition, Harvey is also known for high pricing, enterprise seat minimums, and lengthy implementations, so it isn’t the right alternative if your biggest issue with Eudia is cost or procurement friction.


## Alternative #2: CoCounsel


CoCounsel is Thomson Reuters’ AI legal assistant, built on top of the **same legal content ecosystem that powers Westlaw and Practical Law** . More than one million users rely on the platform, which is designed around Thomson Reuters’ Fiduciary Grade AI framework—a model focused on verifiable outputs, authoritative legal sources, and the security standards required in regulated professions.


### Why choose CoCounsel as an Eudia AI alternative


The biggest advantage CoCounsel has over Eudia is its **access to Thomson Reuters’ legal research ecosystem** . While Eudia is optimized around a company’s internal documents and playbooks, CoCounsel can search millions of court opinions, statutes, regulations, and secondary sources, producing research-backed answers with traceable citations.


As a result, CoCounsel is often the better choice for litigation teams, highly regulated industries, and legal departments whose work revolves around finding, validating, and applying legal authority.


If your lawyers spend more time researching statutes, reviewing case law, drafting memoranda, or responding to regulatory questions than managing large-scale contracting workflows, you’ll likely get more value from CoCounsel’s research stack than from Eudia’s broader operational approach.


However, teams that don’t already rely on Westlaw or Practical Law may find the economics less compelling since much of **CoCounsel’s value comes from its deep integration with the Thomson Reuters ecosystem.**


## Alternative #3: Spellbook


Spellbook is a **contract-focused AI assistant** that works directly inside Microsoft Word, helping lawyers draft, review, redline, and negotiate agreements without leaving their existing workflow. The platform is trusted by **more than 4,500 law firms and in-house legal teams across over 80 countries** , making it one of the most widely adopted AI tools for transactional legal work.


Unlike broader legal AI platforms that attempt to centralize legal operations across an organization, Spellbook is designed around a much narrower goal: helping lawyers work faster inside the contracts they’re actively handling.


### Why choose Spellbook as an Eudia AI alternative


Spellbook’s biggest advantage is **simplicity** . Because it lives inside Microsoft Word, lawyers can start using it immediately without lengthy implementations, training programs, or workflow redesigns. This makes it particularly appealing for **smaller legal teams, boutique firms, and transactional attorneys** who want practical drafting assistance rather than a large-scale legal operations platform.


The platform also excels at clause-level work. Whether you’re drafting a new provision, evaluating proposed redlines, or looking for alternative language during negotiations, Spellbook can provide suggestions directly within the document.


Its ability to learn from saved precedents and drafting preferences is another advantage for lawyers who want AI assistance tailored to their personal drafting style.


However, this also means that teams looking for enterprise-wide workflow automation, large-scale document analysis, or complex legal research will likely find Spellbook **too specialized.**


## Alternative #4: Eigent AI


Eigent AI is an **open-source AI agent platform** that automates complex workflows through teams of specialized agents capable of researching information, analyzing documents, browsing the web, writing code, and executing tasks in parallel.


The company claims its multi-agent architecture can reduce **up to 500 hours of work to roughly 50 minutes** while allowing a single user to handle workloads traditionally spread across entire teams.


Because it can be self-hosted, supports both commercial and open-source models, and gives organizations complete control over where their data is stored and processed, Eigent is particularly well-suited for **security-conscious enterprises** that can’t rely on vendor-managed AI infrastructure.


### Why choose Eigent AI as an Eudia AI alternative


Ownership is the main reason teams choose Eigent over Eudia.


Rather than committing to a managed platform with vendor-controlled infrastructure, organizations can deploy Eigent on their own systems, select the models they want to use, and connect agents directly to internal tools and data sources. This level of control is especially valuable for **enterprises with strict security, compliance, or data residency requirements.**


Eigent also takes a dramatically different approach to pricing. It offers a **free tier, transparent pricing, and self-serve access** . Teams can start testing workflows immediately instead of navigating procurement reviews and implementation projects before seeing value.


The tradeoff is that Eigent requires **significantly more technical ownership** . Configuration, integrations, governance, and ongoing maintenance remain the responsibility of the organization, making it a better fit for technically sophisticated teams than legal departments looking for a fully managed solution.


## Alternative #5: Kira


Kira is one of the most established **contract intelligence platforms** in the legal industry. Trusted by **70% of the world’s top 50 law firms** , including four of the UK’s Magic Circle firms, Kira has spent more than a decade refining its AI models for legal document review.


This focus on accuracy has made Kira a staple of **high-stakes transactional work** .


Its multi-layer AI architecture combines generative AI with proprietary models trained on more than 45,000 lawyer hours, enabling legal teams to identify key clauses, assess risk, and review thousands of agreements with a level of precision that has made it a standard tool in corporate M&A and due diligence workflows.


### Why choose Kira as an Eudia AI alternative


Kira’s main strength is **scale** .


When legal teams need to review thousands of contracts during an acquisition, divestiture, financing, or corporate restructuring, speed matters, but consistency matters even more.


Kira was built specifically for these scenarios, with pre-trained models capable of extracting hundreds of contract provisions across large document sets and turning unstructured agreements into structured data that can be analyzed, reported on, and shared across deal teams.


The platform is also particularly effective when dealing with legacy contracts. Strong OCR capabilities, detailed audit trails, and highly traceable extraction results make it well-suited for situations where legal teams need **defensible outputs rather than conversational AI assistance.**


For organizations evaluating Eudia alternatives, Kira is the better choice when the primary challenge is **understanding what’s buried inside thousands of existing agreements** .


The limitation is that Kira remains **highly focused on analysis** . Teams looking for AI-powered drafting, negotiation support, or day-to-day legal workflow assistance may find it too narrow, particularly outside major transaction and due diligence projects.


## Alternative #6: Luminance


Luminance is an **AI-powered contract lifecycle management platform** that allows legal teams to review, negotiate, analyze, and manage contracts throughout their entire lifecycle.


Built by Cambridge mathematicians and adopted by many of the world’s largest enterprises, the platform combines contract intelligence, due diligence, compliance monitoring, and automated negotiation within a single system.


Its focus on high-volume legal work has delivered **significant efficiency gains** for customers, including 99% faster responses to business queries, 80-page master service agreements reviewed in under five minutes, and more than 500 hours saved on contract generation. These capabilities have made Luminance particularly popular among **contract-heavy businesses.**


### Why choose Luminance as an Eudia AI alternative


Luminance is built around the idea that much of legal work can be automated **before a lawyer ever touches the document.**


Its strongest differentiator is[contract review automation](https://general.legal/blog/contract-review-automation) . The platform can review incoming agreements, compare them against approved positions, generate redlines, identify risks, and support negotiation workflows across large contract portfolios. For organizations processing hundreds or thousands of agreements each year, these efficiencies can create a meaningful operational advantage.


Luminance is also one of the few alternatives that **competes with Eudia on institutional intelligence** . Its contract analysis engine continuously learns from prior agreements, negotiation history, and approved language, helping legal teams maintain consistency across large-scale contracting operations.


The downside is that some of the same frustrations buyers have with Eudia still apply here.


Luminance is an enterprise platform with a substantial implementation process, sales-led procurement, and pricing that is difficult to justify for smaller teams. Companies looking for a lightweight, self-serve solution may find themselves trading one enterprise commitment for another.


## Alternative #7: iManage Work


iManage Work is a **document and email management platform** designed to help organizations store, organize, secure, and retrieve institutional knowledge at scale.


Used by **more than one million professionals across 4,000 organizations worldwide** , it serves as the system of record for many of the world’s largest law firms and corporate legal departments.


In recent years, the company has expanded beyond traditional document management by **introducing AI-powered capabilities** , allowing users to search, summarize, analyze, and extract insights from their document repositories.


This combination of knowledge management and AI-powered search makes iManage particularly valuable for companies that manage large volumes of documents and emails and need a reliable way to surface information **across years of institutional history.**


### Why choose iManage Work as an Eudia AI alternative


For many organizations, Eudia’s centralized Company Brain is the reason they adopted the platform. If you appreciate the idea of having institutional knowledge available on demand but want that **intelligence built directly into the system** where your documents and emails already live, iManage is one of the strongest alternatives available.


Governance is another major differentiator. Features like permission-aware search, audit trails, threat monitoring, and native OCR capabilities make iManage especially attractive to large enterprises with strict compliance and information governance requirements.


However, this also means that iManage **solves a different problem than most legal AI platforms.**


While it has added AI-powered search and analysis, its core strength remains document management rather than workflow automation. Plus, organizations hoping to escape long implementations, enterprise procurement cycles, or complex deployments may find that iManage introduces many of the same operational challenges they were trying to avoid in the first place.
