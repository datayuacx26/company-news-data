# 8 Best Startup Tools for Managing Contracts [Comparison]

If you only manage a dozen contracts, a proper Contract Management System (CMS) is likely overkill. A simple stack of Google Docs, a shared Drive folder, and a spreadsheet of renewal dates is all you need to track key obligations and deadlines.


However, add another dozen contracts, a few customers, vendors, and hiring agreements, and that **system starts showing cracks fast.**


That’s because a standard contract lifecycle has[over 40 friction points](https://www.deloitte.com/content/dam/assets-zone3/us/en/docs/services/tax/2024/us-tax-roi-of-contracting-excellence.pdf) where delays, missed handoffs, and poor visibility can slow deals down or create risk. Contract disputes are also the[single most common type of civil litigation businesses face](https://www.uscourts.gov/file/document/institute-advancement-amer-legal-system-civil-litigation-survey) , which is a fight most startups don’t have the cash or the runway to take on.


That’s why more founders are **investing in dedicated contract management software.**


In this guide, we’ll break down the **eight best startup tools for managing contracts** , from full contract lifecycle systems to specialized platforms that solve specific parts of the contracting workflow.


## Key takeaways


- **Poor contract management creates real business risk**
Companies lose revenue, miss obligations, and struggle to find important agreements when contract processes break down.
- **The best contract tool depends on your stage**
Startups should choose software based on contract volume, team size, and workflow complexity rather than feature count alone.
- **Different tools solve different problems**
PandaDoc is strong for sales workflows, Juro helps teams self-serve contracts, and Ironclad is built for more complex operations.
- **AI can help, but it doesn’t replace legal judgment**
AI tools can summarize contracts and flag issues, but they can’t reliably assess negotiation leverage, business risk, or market-standard terms.
- **General Legal can be a better fit than contract software for some startups**
If your main challenge is reviewing, drafting, and negotiating contracts rather than storing them,[General Legal](https://general.legal/) combines attorney-led legal support with AI-powered speed, without requiring a dedicated legal team or complex software implementation.

That’s why contract tools are no longer judged solely on their ability to collect signatures. Founders need systems that can keep contracts searchable, track obligations, flag upcoming renewals, automate approvals, and **create a reliable system of record as the business grows.**

## 1. PandaDoc: Best for sales-led startups that turn proposals into signed contracts at high volume


PandaDoc is a **document workflow platform** that combines proposal creation, contract generation, approvals, e-signatures, and document management in a single system.


It’s particularly well-suited for sales-led startups because it **sits directly inside the revenue workflow** . Strong integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, and other CRM platforms allow teams to generate agreements from existing deal data, reducing manual work and helping reps move prospects from quote to signature faster.


### Standout features


PandaDoc keeps contract execution tightly connected to the sales process, with tools like:


- **Drag-and-drop document builder:** Create branded proposals, quotes, and agreements from reusable templates without relying on design or legal resources for every document.
- **Document tracking and analytics:** Monitor when recipients open, review, and sign documents to prioritize follow-ups and identify stalled deals.
- **Built-in payment collection:** Accept ACH and card payments directly from signed documents to shorten the gap between closing a deal and getting paid.


### Pricing


PandaDoc uses a **tiered subscription model** , with plans ranging from a free e-signature offering to more advanced packages that include workflow automation, integrations, and additional administrative controls.


## 2. Juro: Best for fast-growing startups that want business teams to self-serve contracts


Juro is a **CLM platform** that lets teams draft, negotiate, approve, sign, and manage agreements entirely within a browser. Its design prioritizes collaboration across sales, HR, operations, and legal teams rather than serving as a tool used exclusively by lawyers.


Juro shines for startups that want business teams to self-serve contracts because it **removes legal bottlenecks without sacrificing control.** Conditional templates, approval rules, and built-in guardrails allow non-legal employees to generate compliant agreements on their own, freeing legal teams and founders from routine contract requests.


### Standout features


Juro keeps contracting work inside a single environment, with capabilities like:


- **Browser-native editor:** Draft, negotiate, approve, and sign agreements without moving between Word files, PDFs, and separate e-signature tools.
- **Collaborative redlining:** Review edits, negotiate terms, and hold internal discussions alongside counterparties without creating version-control headaches.
- **AI contract intelligence:** Extract key metadata, identify unusual clauses, and search contract repositories using natural-language queries.


### Pricing


Juro doesn’t publish standard pricing on its website, requiring prospective customers to contact the sales team for a **custom quote.**


## 3. Ironclad: Best for scaling startups that need enterprise-grade contract workflows before they become enterprise-sized


Ironclad is a **CLM platform** built for organizations with increasingly complex contracting requirements. It combines contract creation, negotiation, approvals, repository management, and AI-powered analysis in a system designed to support legal, finance, procurement, and sales teams as they scale.


It’s a strong fit for startups preparing for rapid growth because it **brings structure to processes that often become chaotic** during later funding rounds, larger customer deals, and expanding compliance requirements. Teams can establish sophisticated controls early rather than rebuilding their contract operations every time the business doubles in size.


### Standout features


Ironclad helps standardize and govern contract operations across the company, with features like:


- **No-code workflow designer:** Configure conditional approval paths that automatically route agreements to the right stakeholders based on deal terms, contract value, or risk level.
- **Clickwrap management:** Deploy legally binding click-to-accept agreements across products and websites while maintaining a detailed audit trail for compliance purposes.
- **Slack and Salesforce integrations:** Launch, track, discuss, and approve contracts within the systems teams already use every day.


### Pricing


Ironclad follows a **custom enterprise pricing model** , with costs available only through direct discussions with its sales team.


## 4. Contractbook: Best for lean startups that automate operational workflows from signed agreements


Contractbook is a **cloud-based CLM platform** that helps startups create, manage, store, and automate agreements from a single workspace.


Fast-moving teams will benefit from Contractbook because it **treats signed agreements as operational data rather than static documents** . This makes it easier to connect contracts to onboarding, finance, HR, and other business processes without adding administrative work every time a document is executed.


### Standout features


Contractbook focuses heavily on turning contract data into actions across the business, with capabilities including:


- **AI-powered contract import:** Convert legacy PDFs and scanned agreements into searchable records by extracting key metadata automatically.
- **Questionnaire-based document creation:** Generate customized agreements from structured forms that populate contract fields without manual drafting.
- **Renewal tracking and task management:** Assign responsibilities, monitor obligations, and receive reminders before important deadlines are missed.


### Pricing


Contractbook offers **several subscription tiers** , but organizations typically need to speak with the company directly to get accurate pricing.


## 5. ContractSafe: Best for small teams that need a reliable system for tracking renewals and obligations


ContractSafe is a **contract repository and management platform** focused on storing, organizing, and retrieving agreements without the complexity of a full enterprise CLM system.


It’s particularly well-suited for small teams because it solves one of the most common contract management problems: **forgetting what happens after the agreement is signed** . Automated alerts and clear visibility into key dates help founders and operators stay ahead of renewals, notice periods, and contractual commitments before they turn into expensive surprises.


### Standout features


ContractSafe prioritizes visibility and accessibility over heavyweight workflow customization, with tools like:


- **AI data extraction:** Pull key terms, dates, and contract details from uploaded agreements to reduce manual data entry.
- **Advanced search and tagging:** Locate specific contracts, clauses, counterparties, or terms in seconds instead of digging through folders and inboxes.
- **Custom dashboards and reporting:** Surface contract activity, upcoming milestones, and repository insights in a format that’s easy for non-legal teams to understand.


### Pricing


ContractSafe publishes starting prices for its plans, with costs ultimately determined by the features required and the volume of active contracts being managed.


## 6. Concord: Best for budget-conscious startups that want full CLM without enterprise pricing


Concord is a **CLM platform** that brings drafting, negotiation, approvals, e-signatures, and contract storage into a single workspace.


Lean startups will get the most value from Concord because it delivers broad functionality at a **cost that’s easier to justify early on** . Teams can replace a patchwork of separate tools and manual processes with a single platform while avoiding the implementation burden and overhead that often come with larger systems.


### Standout features


Concord emphasizes cross-functional collaboration and day-to-day usability, with capabilities including:


- **Unlimited viewer access:** Give stakeholders visibility into agreements without purchasing additional licenses for every employee who needs occasional access.
- **Version history and audit trails:** Track every edit, comment, and document revision to maintain accountability throughout the negotiation process.
- **Live contract collaboration:** Review, discuss, and revise agreements with internal teams and counterparties directly within a shared workspace.


### Pricing


Concord publicly lists its **subscription tiers** , making it easier for startups to estimate costs before engaging with sales.


## 7. SpotDraft: Best for startups that need legal to support the business without becoming a bottleneck


SpotDraft is an **AI-powered CLM platform** that combines contract creation, review, approval workflows, repository management, and legal operations tooling.


Startups with lean legal resources will benefit from SpotDraft because it helps **distribute routine contract work across the business** while maintaining consistent legal standards. Structured request flows, automated review processes, and standardized playbooks make it easier for legal teams to focus on higher-value work instead of repetitive administrative tasks.


### Standout features


SpotDraft is geared toward operationalizing legal processes within the organization, with capabilities including:


- **VerifAI contract review:** Identify non-standard clauses and receive fallback language recommendations based on predefined legal playbooks.
- **DraftMate AI:** Convert existing Word agreements into reusable contract templates with automatically generated variables and fields.
- **Smart data capture:** Extract key contract terms and metadata from executed agreements to build a searchable, audit-ready repository.


### Pricing


SpotDraft offers **customized pricing** based on factors like user counts or contract volume, with quotes provided through its sales team.


## 8. LinkSquares: Best for startups that need AI-powered visibility across hundreds of existing contracts


LinkSquares is an **AI-driven CLM platform** that allows companies to organize, analyze, and manage large contract portfolios.


It’s particularly well-suited for startups with expanding contract volumes because its **AI can quickly surface the information hidden inside executed agreements** . Rather than digging through folders, PDFs, and shared drives, teams can identify key obligations, renewal dates, risk areas, and business commitments across their entire contract portfolio in a fraction of the time.


### Standout features


LinkSquares centers contract management around extracting actionable insights from legal data, offering tools like:


- **Autonomous risk scoring:** Flag potentially problematic clauses and prioritize agreements that require closer legal review.
- **Agentic AI redlining:** Generate proposed edits and contract revisions based on predefined legal standards and clause preferences.
- **Smart tags and metadata extraction:** Categorize agreements automatically and pull key contract details into a searchable database for faster reporting and due diligence.


### Pricing


LinkSquares doesn’t publish standard pricing, instead providing **customized proposals** based on each company’s requirements and contract management needs.


## What is the best startup tool for managing contracts?


The best startup tool for managing contracts mainly depends on your **contract volume** , **internal legal resources** , and the **complexity of the agreements** you’re signing.


For example, a Series A SaaS startup with a small legal or operations function may find a platform like Juro particularly useful. Its browser-based workflow, approval controls, and self-service capabilities help business teams generate routine agreements without creating constant legal bottlenecks.


On the other hand, a Series C company with multiple departments, complex approval chains, and growing compliance requirements may be better served by a platform like Ironclad. Its workflow automation, governance controls, and scalability are designed for organizations that are starting to operate more like enterprises.


However, for many startups, **contract management software isn’t the biggest problem that needs solving.**


Early-stage and growth-stage companies often struggle less with storing contracts and more with reviewing, negotiating, drafting, and turning them around quickly.

## FAQ


### Is CLM the same as CRM?


No. Contract lifecycle management (CLM) software manages contracts, while customer relationship management (CRM) software manages customer and sales relationships.


### What is the best contract management software?


The best contract management software depends on your contract volume, legal resources, and business needs, but Juro, Ironclad, and PandaDoc are among the strongest options for startups.


### Which AI tool is best for contracts?


There’s no single best AI tool for contracts. The right choice depends on whether you need contract review, contract management, drafting, or negotiation support. In some cases, an AI-native law firm like General Legal may be a better fit than software alone.
