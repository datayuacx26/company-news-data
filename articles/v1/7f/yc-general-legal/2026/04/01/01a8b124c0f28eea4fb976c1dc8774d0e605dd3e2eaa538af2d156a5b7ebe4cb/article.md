# 7 Best Contract Review AI Tools for Legal Teams [Compared]

Contract review is the process of **analyzing a contract** before signing to identify risks, validate terms, and ensure the agreement actually protects your business.


Startups might not handle[over 50,000 contracts per year](https://clp.law.harvard.edu/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/ey-contracting-report-june-2021.pdf) like the busiest enterprise legal teams do, but they still[routinely negotiate contracts](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/startups/contract-and-legal-traps-to-avoid-startup-founder-sales-series-part-11/) such as[Master Service Agreements](https://general.legal/library/master-services-agreement-msa) (MSAs) and SaaS terms. Getting those contracts wrong can create significant **financial, legal, and operational risk.**


However, manual contract review creates a **costly bottleneck** , with **** delays, unfavorable contract terms, and operational friction eroding[up to 9% of annual revenue](https://info.worldcc.com/contract-management-aug-2025) .


That’s why legal teams are increasingly **adopting artificial intelligence** (AI) to accelerate review cycles, identify risky clauses, and reduce negotiation bottlenecks.


If contract review is starting to delay deals for your business, the **seven contract review AI tools** below are worth evaluating.


## Key takeaways


- **The best contract review AI tool depends on your needs** Enterprise legal teams, startups, and small businesses often require different solutions based on contract volume, workflow complexity, and legal resources.
- **AI speeds up review, but it doesn’t replace legal judgment** Contract review tools can identify risks and suggest edits, but important legal and business decisions still require human expertise.
- **Workflow integration is critical** The best tools fit into existing systems like Microsoft Word, Slack, and CLMs, making contract review easier to adopt and scale.
- **Growing companies often outgrow basic AI tools** As legal needs become more complex, software alone may not provide the breadth of expertise required across different legal matters.
- **General Legal offers an alternative to software-only solutions**[General Legal](https://general.legal/) combines attorney-led legal services with AI-enabled workflows, helping startups and growth-stage companies access legal expertise without building a large in-house team.


## How we chose the 7 best contract review AI tools


There’s **no single “best” contract review AI tool** for every organization, as different teams operate under different constraints. Some need enterprise-grade review infrastructure; others require fast self-service reviews, outsourced legal support, or tools that integrate into their existing workflows.


So, rather than ranking tools based on marketing claims or feature volume, we selected platforms that **solve different operational problems** across startups, in-house legal teams, and enterprise organizations.


The following factors played the largest role in our selection process:


- **Quality of contract review (25%):** We prioritized tools that improve review accuracy, identify meaningful risks, surface problematic clauses, and help legal teams review contracts faster without sacrificing legal judgment.
- **Fit for different company sizes (20%):** Some tools are built for lean startup teams handling a handful of agreements a week. Others are designed for enterprise legal departments reviewing thousands of contracts per year. We included both.
- **Level of legal involvement required (15%):** We included both AI copilots designed for legal professionals and solutions geared toward founders, operators, procurement teams, and businesses seeking more hands-on legal support.
- **Workflow integration (15%):** Contract review happens inside operational workflows, not in isolation. We looked closely at integrations with Microsoft Word, Slack, CLM systems, e-signature platforms, approval workflows, and existing legal processes.
- **Practical usability and adoption (15%):** A powerful platform nobody adopts is useless. We favored tools that reduce friction, accelerate negotiations, and fit naturally into existing workflows.
- **AI realism instead of hype (10%):** Many legal AI tools promise full automation, but very few deliver it. We prioritized platforms that understand where AI is genuinely useful and where human legal judgment remains essential.

## 1. Harvey AI: Best for enterprise legal teams and large law firms


Harvey AI is an **enterprise-grade legal AI platform** that supports contract analysis, legal research, drafting, due diligence, regulatory work, and large-scale document review inside a centralized AI workspace.


This platform is geared toward **large legal departments and Am Law firms** handling substantial volumes of unstructured legal data while maintaining strict security, governance, and compliance standards.


Organizations managing **highly specialized matters** spanning multiple practice areas will find more value in Harvey than those looking for a point solution focused solely on routine contract review.


### Key features


Harvey supports large-scale legal analysis and workflow automation through features like:


- **Vault document analysis:** Extract and compare key contract terms from thousands of agreements to identify renewals, compliance risks, and operational inconsistencies at scale.
- **Review workflow builder:** Build multi-step legal review processes that automate repetitive analysis, document assessment, and information extraction.
- **Knowledge and playbook management:** Apply internal precedents, templates, and negotiation standards consistently across legal teams and matters.


### Pricing


Harvey operates on a **custom enterprise pricing model** with costs varying based on seat count, integrations, and workflow complexity.


The platform is designed for large firms and enterprise legal departments rather than smaller legal teams or startups, and its pricing reflects that positioning.


## 2. Spellbook: Best for legal teams working primarily in Microsoft Word


Spellbook is an **AI contract review copilot** built directly into Microsoft Word. It helps legal teams review, redline, and negotiate agreements without leaving the document itself.


This makes it a strong fit for **transactional lawyers, small firms, and operators** who spend most of their time **** reviewing contracts inside Word rather than managing complex enterprise CLM systems.


### Key features


Spellbook streamlines in-document review and negotiation support through tools like:


- **AI-powered redlining:** Generate contract edits and negotiation markups directly inside Word to accelerate review cycles.
- **Custom playbooks:** Apply pre-approved legal guidance and fallback language automatically across agreements.
- **Risk and drafting detection:** Surface risky clauses, drafting inconsistencies, and missing terms before agreements move forward.


### Pricing


Spellbook uses a **custom pricing model** based primarily on team size and organizational requirements.

## 4. goHeather: Best for SMBs reviewing routine contracts


goHeather is a **lawyer-trained AI contract review platform** that helps users review, edit, and redline agreements through plain-English issue spotting, risk analysis, and guided negotiation suggestions.

### Key features


goHeather makes deep contract review more accessible to non-lawyers through features like:

### Pricing

## What is the best contract review AI tool?


The best contract review AI tool for your team depends on **how your company handles contracts internally** and **where the review process tends to break down.**


For large legal departments with complex approval chains, compliance requirements, and high contract volume, platforms like Harvey AI and Docusign IAM are often the most suitable choice. Smaller teams reviewing relatively straightforward agreements may find tools like goHeather, Legly, and Spellbook sufficient for issue spotting, redlining, and basic analysis.


**The challenge often emerges in the middle.**


Many startups and growth-stage companies have outgrown lightweight review tools but aren’t yet large enough to support a full in-house legal function.


In that situation, software alone only solves part of the problem. AI can identify clauses, suggest edits, and accelerate reviews, but someone still needs to make legal judgments, negotiate terms, and understand **how**[employment](https://general.legal/employment) **, fundraising, commercial,**[privacy](https://general.legal/data-privacy) **, and**[regulatory](https://general.legal/financial-regulatory) **issues interact.**


Hiring a single attorney can help, but their expertise may not cover every practice area the company encounters.


So, how do companies solve this problem without continuously adding headcount? The answer lies in **AI-native law firms.**

## FAQ


### Which AI tool is best for contract review?


The best AI tool for contract review depends on your company’s size, contract volume, and legal workflow requirements. Harvey AI is generally better suited to enterprise legal departments, while tools like Spellbook, Juro, goHeather, and Legly are often a stronger fit for smaller teams handling routine contract reviews.

### Can ChatGPT review a contract?


Yes. ChatGPT can summarize clauses, identify potential issues, and suggest edits. However, it can’t provide legal advice and shouldn’t replace professional legal review for high-stakes agreements or complex negotiations.
