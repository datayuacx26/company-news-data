# Best Fractional General Counsel for Startups: 6 Top Options

Running out of cash and making the wrong hires are among two of the biggest challenges for startups. Hire in-house counsel too early—or hire the wrong one—and you’ve combined both risks into a single line item.


Founders are already **delaying hiring more than ever** , with the median time to the first hire increasing[from 214 days in 2019 to 284 days in 2024](https://carta.com/data/resources/state-of-startups-2025/) . Given that median General Counsel (GC) pay sits at[$296,000 as of early August 2026](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/general-counsel-salary-SRCH_KO0,15.htm) , it’s easy to see why bringing legal in-house is a decision many startups just aren’t ready to make.


That’s why some founders choose to **start with fractional GC instead** , getting senior-level legal judgment on demand and at a fraction of the cost.


If you’re considering that route, too, this guide compares the **six best fractional General Counsel for startups.**


## Key takeaways


- **Choose the right fit, not the cheapest option**
The best fractional GC is the one that matches your startup’s stage, industry, and legal needs, not simply the lowest-priced provider.
- **Fractional GC becomes valuable once legal work is constant**
If you’re regularly negotiating contracts, hiring employees, raising funding, or dealing with compliance, ongoing legal support can prevent costly mistakes.
- **Different firms solve different startup problems**
Some firms specialize in AI, fintech, fundraising, or revenue-stage businesses, so picking a provider with relevant experience matters.
- **Look beyond contract review**
A strong fractional GC should act as a strategic advisor, supporting fundraising, governance, employment, commercial contracts, and long-term growth.
- **General Legal is the best all-around choice for most B2B startups**
If you’re a Seed to Series B company scaling commercial operations,[General Legal](https://general.legal/) combines startup expertise, embedded legal support, and predictable pricing in a model designed to grow with your business.


## How we chose the 6 best fractional general counsel for startups


According to DiliTrust, fractional GC can cost[as little as $60,000 per year](https://www.dilitrust.com/fractional-general-counsel/) , making this legal model an **obvious place to start for startups still managing burn and extending runway.**


But choosing the cheapest option misses the point, as a fractional GC isn’t just another legal vendor. They’re the person reviewing your enterprise[Master Services Agreements (MSAs)](https://general.legal/library/master-services-agreement-msa) , helping structure fundraising, advising the board, and keeping product, sales, and hiring decisions from becoming legal problems six months later.


That’s why the real question isn’t “Who’s cheapest?” but “ **Who’s most suitable for the kind of startup I’m building** ?”


That’s the lens we used to evaluate more than 25 providers before selecting the six firms that best represent different startup stages, industries, and legal operating models. These were the criteria that carried the most weight in the selection process:


- **Startup Specialization & Founder Fit (30%):** We prioritized firms with demonstrated experience serving specific startup sectors or growth stages, including AI, SaaS, deep tech, venture-backed companies, and regulated industries.
- **Fractional GC Offering & Legal Coverage (25%):** We assessed whether firms provide true ongoing GC services rather than project-based legal work, including support for startup contracts, fundraising, board governance, employment, privacy, intellectual property, and compliance.
- **Pricing Model & Engagement Structure (20%):** Preference was given to firms offering predictable engagement models, such as flat-fee retainers or structured subscriptions, along with delivery models that fit different founder needs, from embedded legal partners to flexible outside General Counsel for startups.
- **Attorney Expertise & Startup Experience (15%):** We examined founder and attorney backgrounds, focusing on teams with concrete startup, in-house, or BigLaw experience and a demonstrated track record advising venture-backed companies.
- **Scalability & Operational Fit (10%):** We considered how well each provider could support a startup as it grows, including responsiveness, geographic reach, technology adoption, and the ability to scale from early fundraising through later-stage commercial growth.

A fractional General Counsel is an experienced attorney who provides ongoing legal leadership on a part-time or outsourced basis, giving businesses executive-level legal support without hiring a full-time General Counsel.

A startup should hire fractional General Counsel once legal work becomes continuous—for example, when signing customer contracts, raising funding, expanding the team, or entering regulated markets.
