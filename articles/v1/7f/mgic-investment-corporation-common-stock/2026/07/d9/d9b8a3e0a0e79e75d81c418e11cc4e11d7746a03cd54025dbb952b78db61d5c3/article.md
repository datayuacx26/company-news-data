# MGIC Investment Corporation Schedules 2nd Quarter 2026 Earnings Call

MILWAUKEE


, July 2, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- MGIC Investment Corporation (NYSE: MTG) has announced plans to release its second quarter 2026 financial results after the market closes on Wednesday, July 29, 2026. A conference call/webcast has been scheduled for 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time on Thursday, July 30, 2026, to discuss the Company's results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026.

The call is also being webcast and can be accessed via the Company's Investor website found at[http://mtg.mgic.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4724513-1&h=1913024447&u=http%3A%2F%2Fmtg.mgic.com%2F&a=http%3A%2F%2Fmtg.mgic.com) under Newsroom.
