# MGIC Investment Corporation Announces Quarterly Dividend of $0.15 Per Share

MILWAUKEE


, Jan. 27, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- MGIC Investment Corporation (NYSE: MTG) announced that its Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.15 per share, payable on March 6, 2026, to shareholders of record as of February 17, 2026.
