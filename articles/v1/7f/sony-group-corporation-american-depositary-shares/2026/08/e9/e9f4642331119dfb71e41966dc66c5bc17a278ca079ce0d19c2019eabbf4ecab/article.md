# Take A Deep Dive Into The New Sony 100-400mm f/5.6-8 Lens

The[Sony 100-400mm f/5.6-8](https://electronics.sony.com/imaging/lenses/full-frame-e-mount/p/sel100400?utm_source=AlphaUniverse&utm_medium=Web&utm_campaign=Article) brings super-telephoto reach to a notably compact and lightweight package. Built for photographers who want to explore wildlife, birds, sports and distant subjects without carrying a large, heavy kit – the full-frame E-mount zoom covers an exceptionally useful focal range while prioritizing portability and value.


Let’s take a closer look. In this hands-on video from the[Alpha Universe YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@SonyAlphaUniverse) , Sony Artisan[Miguel Quiles](https://alphauniverse.com/member/#/miguel-quiles/) takes a closer look at the lens’ design, autofocus performance, optical construction and real-world handling when paired with his Sony Alpha camera.


#### A Different Kind Of 100-400mm


The first thing that stands out about the new 100-400mm f/5.6-8 is its size. This is a lens designed to make long-lens photography feel more approachable. At approximately 1.44 lb. (654g), it is remarkably light for a full-frame 100-400mm zoom.


That’s a difference that matters in the field. For photographers moving into bird and wildlife photography – or anyone who wants flexible reach without committing to a larger pro-level telephoto zoom – that portability can be a major advantage.


#### Essential Controls, Built For The Outdoors


Despite its compact size, the[100-400mm f/5.6-8](https://electronics.sony.com/imaging/lenses/full-frame-e-mount/p/sel100400?utm_source=AlphaUniverse&utm_medium=Web&utm_campaign=Article) includes the controls photographers expect for quick adjustments in the field. On the exterior, you’ll find dedicated focus and zoom rings, a focus mode switch, a focus-hold button, a SteadyShot switch and a zoom lock switch.


The lens is also built with dust- and moisture-resistance in mind. Its seams are sealed, while the buttons and switches use silicone-rubber gaskets. A rubber seal around the lens mount adds another layer of protection when working in changing outdoor conditions.


Those details make the lens a practical option for photographers who are often shooting outside – waiting for wildlife, following action or simply working through the unpredictable conditions that come with nature photography.


#### Optical Design Made For Clean, Natural Rendering


Inside, the lens uses 15 elements in 10 groups, including two Extra-low Dispersion glass elements to help suppress chromatic aberration and two aspherical elements designed to minimize other optical aberrations.


The result is image quality that feels well suited to the type of subjects this lens invites you to photograph. From distant wildlife to compressed landscapes, the lens delivers a clean, natural rendering across its focal range.


A nine-blade circular aperture also helps create a nice and smooth bokeh. At longer focal lengths, that background separation can give images a more immersive feel, helping isolate a subject from distracting surroundings. In hands-on shooting, the out-of-focus rendering had a pleasingly natural character – particularly useful when photographing wildlife in busy environments.


#### Fast, Accurate Autofocus For Moving Subjects


A long zoom lens is only as useful as its ability to keep up with a moving subject, and the[100-400mm f/5.6-8](https://electronics.sony.com/imaging/lenses/full-frame-e-mount/p/sel100400?utm_source=AlphaUniverse&utm_medium=Web&utm_campaign=Article) is equipped with two linear motors for fast, accurate autofocus.


That performance is especially important for birds, wildlife and action. The lens can keep pace with the 120 fps burst shooting capability of the Alpha 9 III, giving photographers the speed needed to capture brief, unpredictable moments.


Pairing responsive autofocus with Sony’s subject-recognition technology makes this lens an appealing entry point for photographers looking to shoot fast-moving subjects with greater confidence. Whether tracking a bird in flight or photographing wildlife from a distance, the lens is designed to respond quickly when the moment happens.


#### Extend The Reach With Teleconverters


The[100-400mm f/5.6-8](https://electronics.sony.com/imaging/lenses/full-frame-e-mount/p/sel100400?utm_source=AlphaUniverse&utm_medium=Web&utm_campaign=Article) is compatible with Sony’s[1.4X teleconverter](https://electronics.sony.com/imaging/lenses/all-e-mount/p/sel14tc?utm_source=AlphaUniverse&utm_medium=Web&utm_campaign=Article) and[2.0X teleconverter](https://electronics.sony.com/imaging/lenses/all-e-mount/p/sel20tc?utm_source=AlphaUniverse&utm_medium=Web&utm_campaign=Article) , giving photographers even more flexibility when the subject is farther away. That compatibility opens up additional possibilities for bird and wildlife photographers who need more reach without adding another lens to their bag.


That is one of the lens’s biggest strengths: it makes long-range shooting easier to bring along. A lightweight camera-and-lens setup is more likely to be with you when an opportunity presents itself – and, for wildlife photographers, being ready is often half the battle.


#### A Versatile Long Lens For More Photographers


The[100-400mm f/5.6-8](https://electronics.sony.com/imaging/lenses/full-frame-e-mount/p/sel100400?utm_source=AlphaUniverse&utm_medium=Web&utm_campaign=Article) offers a compelling combination of reach, portability and autofocus performance. Its compact design makes it easy to pack and carry, while its optical construction, nine-blade aperture and teleconverter compatibility give photographers room to grow.


For those getting started with bird and wildlife photography, it is an approachable way to step into the 100-400mm range. For experienced shooters who want a lighter long-lens option for travel, hikes or everyday outdoor work, it is a versatile addition to your Sony Alpha kit.
