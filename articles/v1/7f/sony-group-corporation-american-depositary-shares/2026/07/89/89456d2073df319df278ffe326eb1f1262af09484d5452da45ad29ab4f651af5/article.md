# Travel Photography Checklist: What To Know Before You Go With Your Sony Alpha

Preparing for a photography trip that requires travel involves careful planning to ensure you have everything you need to capture great images. Here’s a checklist of gear-related tasks and items to consider before you go:

#### 2. Select and Pack Gear:


As Katrin mentions in the video above, it's all about packing light...but you of course still want to be prepared. The types of photography you'll be doing on your travels will help determine your gear. Check out some gear ideas below to help plan your travel photography kit:


**Cameras:** Decide which camera bodies to bring. Many traveling photographers reach for more compact options like the[Sony Alpha 7CR](https://electronics.sony.com/imaging/interchangeable-lens-cameras/all-interchangeable-lens-cameras/p/ilce7crb?utm_source=AlphaUniverse&utm_medium=Web&utm_campaign=Article) or[Sony Alpha 7C II](https://electronics.sony.com/imaging/interchangeable-lens-cameras/all-interchangeable-lens-cameras/p/ilce7cm2b?utm_source=AlphaUniverse&utm_medium=Web&utm_campaign=Article) . But as Sony users know, all of the cameras are pretty compact and suitable for travel. So if you have the[Sony Alpha 7R VI](https://electronics.sony.com/imaging/interchangeable-lens-cameras/full-frame/p/ilce7rm6b?utm_source=AlphaUniverse&utm_medium=Web&utm_campaign=Article) ,[Sony Alpha 7 V](https://electronics.sony.com/imaging/interchangeable-lens-cameras/all-interchangeable-lens-cameras/p/ilce7m5b?utm_source=AlphaUniverse&utm_medium=Web&utm_campaign=Article) and[Sony Alpha 1 II](https://electronics.sony.com/imaging/interchangeable-lens-cameras/all-interchangeable-lens-cameras/p/ilce1m2b?utm_source=AlphaUniverse&utm_medium=Web&utm_campaign=Article) , or the like, you still have that lightweight package with next-level image quality for on-the-go photography. Explore other Sony Alpha cameras to find what's best for you at[alphauniverse.com/cameras](https://alphauniverse.com/cameras/) .


**Lenses:** Choose which lenses to pack based on the type of photography you will be shooting (e.g., wide-angle, telephoto, macro). If you're not sure what you'll come across or don't have a major focus on what you'll be photographing, think about packing a versatile zoom like the[Sony 24-70mm f/2.8 G Master II](https://electronics.sony.com/imaging/lenses/all-e-mount/p/sel2470gm2?utm_source=AlphaUniverse&utm_medium=Web&utm_campaign=Article) – a popular choice for travel photographers. If you want to see more great lenses for travel, check out[The Best Lenses For Travel Photography In 2026](https://alphauniverse.com/stories/the-best-lenses-for-travel-photography-in-2026/) . Explore all and compare all of the Sony lenses to find what's best for you at[alphauniverse.com/lenses](https://alphauniverse.com/lenses/) .

**Tripod:** The best tripods for travel photography balance weight and stability, which is especially important if you'll be hiking or traveling long distances. Adventure photographer and glacier guide Tori Harp prefers the Carbon Fiber Peak Design Tripod. "For nights out shooting astrophotography I use this tripod which always gets the job done. I originally added this tripod to my kit for longer backcountry trips but it is also my go-to tripod for everything else." See what else she packs in[What’s In My Bag: A Glacier Guide’s Camera Gear For Ice Climbing Adventures](https://alphauniverse.com/stories/whats-in-my-bag-a-glacier-guides-camera-gear-for-ice-climbing-adventures/) .


**Filters:** Ensure you have the necessary filters, such as polarizing filters for enhancing skies and reducing reflections, ND filters for long exposures, and UV filters for lens protection.[Alpha Collective member Vince Lim](https://alphauniverse.com/member/#/vincent-lim/) brings a variety of them to cover everything he wants to shoot. "Depending on the situation and what I’m shooting, I use both drop-in and screw-on filters. Since I’m often by the water, I always bring a circular polarizer to reduce glare. I’ve been really enjoying halation filters, like the Black Pro Mist, which diffuse light for a unique effect. The way light sources appear with this filter adds an extra dimension to my photos." Read more in[What’s In My Bag: An Alpha 7R V & G Master Kit For Landscape, Travel & Lifestyle Photography](https://alphauniverse.com/stories/whats-in-my-bag-an-alpha-7r-v-and-g-master-kit-for-landscape-travel-and-lifestyle-photography/) .

[Sony HVL-F60RM2](https://electronics.sony.com/imaging/imaging-accessories/all-accessories/p/hvlf60rm2?utm_source=AlphaUniverse&utm_medium=Web&utm_campaign=Article) flashes in his travel portrait photography kit. "Using these two portable flashes with my[Sony Alpha 9 III](https://electronics.sony.com/imaging/interchangeable-lens-cameras/all-interchangeable-lens-cameras/p/ilce9m3b?utm_source=AlphaUniverse&utm_medium=Web&utm_campaign=Article) allows me to get way more light with a fraction of the size." Check out the other items in his travel kit in[Best Travel Portrait Photography Gear: See What's In Scott Robert Lim’s Bag](https://alphauniverse.com/stories/best-travel-portrait-photography-gear-see-whats-in-scott-robert-lims-bag/) .

[Sony TOUGH CF Express Cards](https://electronics.sony.com/imaging/imaging-accessories/media/p/sfm128t-t1?utm_source=AlphaUniverse&utm_medium=Web&utm_campaign=Article) , which she keeps secured in her F-Stop Gear Memory Card Case. See her complete travel kit in[What’s In My Bag: 2 Cameras & 4 Zoom Lenses Make A Dream Kit For Travel](https://alphauniverse.com/stories/what-s-in-my-bag--2-cameras-and-4-zoom-lenses-make-a-dream-kit-for-travel/) .


**Cleaning Kit:** Bring a comprehensive cleaning kit, including lens cloths, air blowers, and cleaning solutions, to maintain your gear.


**Chargers and Cables:** Pack all necessary chargers and cables for your devices.


**Laptop, Phone or Tablet:** For editing on the go, if needed.


#### 3. Check and Maintain Equipment:


**Clean Gear:** Thoroughly clean your camera lenses and sensors to avoid any unwanted spots in your images.


**Firmware Updates:** Update the firmware on your Sony Alpha cameras and Sony lenses. You can check for any updates at[alphauniverse.com/firmware](https://alphauniverse.com/firmware/) .


**Label Equipment:** Use labels or tags for easy identification and to prevent mix-ups with other photographers.


#### 4. Plan for Power Needs:


**Spare Batteries:** Bring ample spare batteries for your camera, flash and other electronic devices to avoid running out of power at crucial moments. You'll more than likely want to bring some extra batteries for your Sony Alpha camera(s).


**Universal Power Adapter:** This depends on your destination, but if you're headed to a different country - make sure you have the appropriate power adaptor.


**Power Bank:** A high-capacity power bank can be a lifesaver, providing additional power for your devices when you're on the move.


#### 5. Organize and Protect:


**Camera Bag:** Select a camera bag that offers excellent protection and easy access to your gear. Consider a waterproof or weatherproof bag for harsh conditions. Travel and landscape photographer[Hudson McDonald](https://www.instagram.com/hudsonmcdonaldd/) prefers the Peter McKinnon 25L Backpack. "Stylish and versatile, this backpack keeps all my gear organized and protected, making it perfect for both urban shoots and outdoor adventures." Explore everything he fits inside in[What’s In My Bag: The Perfect Camera & Zoom Combo For Landscape & Travel Photography](https://alphauniverse.com/stories/whats-in-my-bag-the-perfect-camera-and-zoom-combo-for-landscape-and-travel-photography/) .


**Waterproof Covers:** Pack waterproof covers or cases for your equipment to guard against rain, dust, and other environmental hazard **s.**


**Security Measures:** Add GPS tracking tags, like Apple Airtags, to your bags and equipment.


Photo by Stan Moniz.


#### 6. Insurance and Documentation:


**Insurance:** Ensure all your photography equipment is covered by insurance that includes travel protection.


**Documentation:** Keep a detailed list of your gear, including serial numbers, in case of loss or theft.


**Permits and Licenses:** Don't forget to obtain any permits required for photography at your destination.


#### 7. Backup and Data Management:


**Backup Plan:** Have a reliable backup plan to safeguard your photos daily, such as using portable hard drives or SSDs.


**Cloud Storage:** Consider cloud storage solutions for additional backups and easy access to your photos from anywhere.

By following this checklist, you can ensure that you are well-prepared for your photography assignment and can focus on capturing stunning images without worrying about equipment issues.
