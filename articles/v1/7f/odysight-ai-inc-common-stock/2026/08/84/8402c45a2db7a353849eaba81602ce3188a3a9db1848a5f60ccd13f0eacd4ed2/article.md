# Odysight.ai signs agreement with Boeing to demonstrate AI-Powered capabilities

***The agreement will showcase vision-based technology at two Boeing sites in laboratory and test environments***


**Ramat Gan, Israel, Aug. 13, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --** Odysight.ai Inc. (NASDAQ/TASE: ODYS), a leader in AI‑powered visual sensing and predictive maintenance (PdM) solutions for the aerospace, defense, and industrial markets, today announced the signing of a Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) with Boeing. This agreement will enable Odysight.ai to demonstrate its computer vision‑based predictive maintenance platform in select laboratory and test environments at two Boeing sites.


This collaboration forms part of the industrial cooperation agreement between Boeing and the Industrial Cooperation Authority (ICA). It complements Odysight.ai’s expanding portfolio of aerospace and defense engagements, which includes operational deployments and contracted programs with the Israeli Air Force (AH‑64 Apache, SH‑60 Seahawk, Heron TP UAV), a Cooperative Research and Development Agreement (CRADA) with the U.S. Navy’s Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division Lakehurst (NAWCAD), and a proof‑of‑concept with Honeywell Aerospace’s APU Division. Collectively, these initiatives position Odysight.ai across a broad spectrum of OEMs and operators, focusing on platforms where its technology delivers significant impact. Notably, Odysight.ai’s vision‑based monitoring system has been operational on the AH‑64 with the Israeli Air Force since 2023.


*“This agreement with Boeing is a significant step forward and a real honor for Odysight.ai,”* said **Yehu Ofer, Chief Executive Officer of Odysight.ai.** *“We look forward to demonstrating the versatility of our visual sensing and predictive maintenance capabilities.”*


**About Odysight.ai**


Odysight.ai, incorporated in Nevada, U.S., with subsidiaries in Europe and Israel, is advancing the Predictive Maintenance (PdM) and Condition‑Based Monitoring (CBM) markets through an AI platform for critical systems across aviation and aerospace, transportation, energy, and industrial sectors. By combining advanced visual sensing, real‑time analytics, and AI‑driven insights, Odysight.ai helps organizations improve safety, efficiency, and operational intelligence.
