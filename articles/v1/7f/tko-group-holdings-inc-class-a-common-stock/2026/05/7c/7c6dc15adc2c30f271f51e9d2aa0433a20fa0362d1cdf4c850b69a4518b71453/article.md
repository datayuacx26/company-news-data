# adidas Becomes Event Supporter and Official Footwear Supplier of The 2026 Solheim Cup


Today the Ladies European Tour is excited to announce adidas as the Official Footwear Supplier of the 2026 European Solheim Cup Team for the event at Bernardus Golf in the Netherlands, from September 7-13.


The agreement also includes adidas as an Event Supporter and Official Footwear supplier of the 2026 Solheim Cup.


adidas join an illustrious list of brands taking official partner positions for one of the biggest events on the sporting calendar with staging partners IMG previously announcing global partners including PING, Skyscanner and Rolex and official golf course and turf maintenance machinery partner, John Deere.


adidas will supply footwear for the captains, players, caddies and helpers of Team Europe, as well as tournament event staff. The range being showcased includes a limited‑edition women’s shoe that will be sold exclusively onsite and worn by both players and staff throughout the event. All styles will be presented in white, featuring the iconic 3-stripes and design details in the blue and yellow colours of Team Europe.


The European PING Junior Solheim Cup team members will play in the same footwear, in the under-18s event immediately preceding the Solheim Cup, at the same golf course and, as Official Licensed Footwear Supplier, the footwear models will be on sale in the merchandise area at the Solheim Cup.


“The iconic adidas brand is world renowned for its leading sports products and I am confident that the footwear will be well received by the players, for its comfort, style and versatility,” said European Solheim Cup Team Captain, Anna Nordqvist.


European Solheim Cup player and adidas athlete, Linn Grant, added: “I love competing in adidas shoes week in, week out on Tour, and I’m beyond excited that the iconic 3-stripes will be represented at the Solheim Cup this year. It’s incredible to be part of a brand that is openly and proudly supporting the women’s game.”


Building on its existing partnerships with Sky Sports Golf, the Ladies European Tour, and the AIG Women’s Open, adidas continues to demonstrate its long‑term commitment to elevating the women’s game. These collaborations have already played a pivotal role in increasing visibility, inspiring the next generation, and creating more opportunities for fans to engage with golf at every level. By expanding its network of partners, adidas is further strengthening its mission to champion the sport, support female athletes, and drive greater participation across the global golf community.


Chris Hedderman, Marketing and Product Director for Europe at adidas Golf, commented: “We are incredibly proud to become the Official Footwear Supplier of the 2026 Solheim Cup and to be supporting some of the world’s best players as Official Footwear Supplier of the 2026 European Solheim Cup Team. This partnership underlines our commitment to elevating women’s golf at every level and I can’t wait to see the players, caddies, staff and helpers in our footwear at Bernardus Golf in September.”


Further details on product availability will be announced in due course.
