# 11 first-ever Michelin Stars in Dubai have been revealed!

The first-ever Michelin Stars in Dubai have been unveiled! Foodies, get your bookings in quick because we’re sure these stars are going to be fully booked for a while.


‍


### **Who received this prestigious recognition?**


‍


On its inaugural edition of the MICHELIN Guide Dubai 2022, 2 restaurants got 2 Michelin Stars, and 9 restaurants got 1 Michelin Star! That’s a Michelin star restaurant for you to try almost every month of the year!


During an exclusive ceremony on June 21 at Dubai Opera, the inspectors bestowed this award to a selection of stars after months of evaluating the kaleidoscopic culinary map of Dubai.


‍


### **The criteria**


‍


1. The quality of the ingredients
2. The mastery of cooking
3. The harmony of flavours
4. The personality of the chef reflected through the cuisine
5. The consistency both over time and across the entire menu
