# CircuitHub's New Funding Round

‍


We are pleased to announce that CircuitHub has recently raised an additional $4 million in capital in order to further our mission of making it possible for hardware companies to design and iterate as fast as software companies.


‍


Our goal ever since launching our manufacturing platform in 2014 has always been to give engineers the ability to quickly and cost-effectively move a design from prototype to high volume.


‍


Since then we have made leaps and bounds in the PCB assembly space and have built circuit boards for hundreds of engineers that go into end products like self driving cars, satellites, 3D printers, robotics, medical devices and more.


‍


We are happy to be able to say that over the past year CircuitHub has reached a point that we are now operating cash flow positive. We have doubled our team size in both our Massachusetts and London locations and plan to continue to grow rapidly over 2020.


‍


More specifically, the funding will allow us to put the accelerator pedal down on something we are extremely excited to finally be able to announce in the coming weeks (apologies for the suspense!)


‍
