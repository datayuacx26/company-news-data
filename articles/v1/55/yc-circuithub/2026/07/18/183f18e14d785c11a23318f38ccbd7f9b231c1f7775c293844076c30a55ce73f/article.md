# CircuitHub's new metrics page: real-time production stats

The service quality provided by a contract manufacturer is crucial for hardware companies. Without accessible concrete data, customers often struggle to assess quality, complicating the evaluation of potential new suppliers. Our[Metrics page](https://www.notion.so/41bc1b7f2bfb4c3f94505dbb4d6cccfa?pvs=21) addresses this issue by offering real-time data on the percentage of orders shipped on time, defect-free orders, among others. To our knowledge, we're the first in our industry to publicly disclose such data. This unprecedented transparency not only underscores our commitment to excellence but also distinguishes CircuitHub from its competitors.
