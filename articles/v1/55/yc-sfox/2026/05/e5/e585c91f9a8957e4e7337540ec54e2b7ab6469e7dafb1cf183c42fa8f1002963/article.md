# PAX Gold (PAXG) on sFOX: The Rise of Digital Commodities & Tokenized Gold

## Bringing Tokenized Gold Into Institutional Digital Asset Infrastructure


sFOX is strengthening institutional access to digital commodities by listing PAX Gold (PAXG), its first digital commodity offering.


As institutions increasingly explore tokenized real-world assets (RWAs), digital commodities are emerging as a compelling bridge between traditional finance and blockchain infrastructure. PAX Gold offers investors a digitally transferable asset fully backed by physical gold, providing security, transparency, and easy access to real-world value.


The addition of PAXG signals sFOX’s commitment to advancing institutional-grade tokenized financial markets.


## What Is PAX Gold (PAXG)?


PAX Gold is a gold-backed asset from Paxos. Each token represents one fine Troy ounce of London Good Delivery gold in professional vaults.


PAXG, an ERC-20 token on the Ethereum blockchain, blends gold’s legacy as a store of value with blockchain’s programmability and accessibility.


Unlike synthetic commodity exposure or unbacked digital assets, PAXG is designed to provide direct exposure to allocated physical gold while enabling:


- 24/7 trading
- Fractional ownership
- Blockchain settlement
- Global transferability
- Integration into digital asset infrastructure


Paxos affirms each token is backed by LBMA-approved vaulted gold and is redeemable for physical gold.


## Why Digital Commodities Matter


Tokenized commodities are changing capital markets. Access to gold used to require:


- Physical custody
- Specialized brokers
- Settlement delays
- Geographic limitations
- High minimum investment thresholds


Tokenization changes this. Digital commodities allow illiquid, intensive assets to move quickly and flexibly across blockchains, enabling:


- Faster settlement
- Greater accessibility
- Improved portability
- Fractional ownership
- Integration into modern trading and treasury systems


Gold’s global reserve status makes it the leading example of tokenized commodities for institutional portfolios seeking inflation hedges.


Analysts view tokenized gold as part of the broader RWA movement reshaping digital finance.


## The Growing Market for Tokenized Gold


*Rising gold prices and increased market volatility have contributed to growing interest in tokenized gold products such as PAX Gold (PAXG), as investors seek more flexible, blockchain-enabled exposure to commodities.*


Demand for tokenized gold is rising as institutions seek efficient, blockchain-enabled access to gold.[Reuters notes rapid growth](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/precious-metal-price-fluctuations-could-test-fast-growing-gold-token-market-2026-02-03/) in the market cap of tokenized gold, highlighting its appeal as a modern, flexible commodity investment. At the same time, the infrastructure surrounding tokenized commodities continues to mature:


- Institutional custody solutions are improving.
- Secondary market liquidity is expanding.
- Standards around reserve transparency are evolving.
- Derivatives markets tied to tokenized commodities are emerging.


This convergence links traditional commodity markets with digital asset platforms.

PAX Gold reflects the shift toward tokenized real-world assets.


Stablecoins brought fiat to blockchains. Digital commodities extend this to assets like gold and other resources.
