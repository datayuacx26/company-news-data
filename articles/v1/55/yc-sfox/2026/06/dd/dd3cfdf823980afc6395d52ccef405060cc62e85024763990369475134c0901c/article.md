# Optimizing Modern Payment Stacks with sFOX Connect

### Scaling Global Payouts: The Missing Piece in Your Payment Stack


Building a cross-border payment network typically demands a frustrating compromise: endure high foreign exchange (FX) fees and slow settlement times over legacy banking networks, or undertake a massive, risky infrastructure overhaul to compliment existing digital asset rails. For institutional payment providers, platforms, and enterprises operating in fragmented markets like LATAM, the technical debt of replacing core systems is often a non-starter.


The challenge isn’t simply access to banking relationships or on- and off-ramps. The bottleneck is the absence of an institutional-grade layer capable of routing, pricing, liquidity, and settling transactions efficiently behind the scenes.


### What is sFOX Connect?


sFOX Connect is a universal API designed to serve as the dedicated execution and liquidity layer for modern payment flows. Rather than replacing your current local banking rails, it operates seamlessly behind them to bridge traditional fiat systems and digital assets. By aggregating deep liquidity and automating execution, it helps enterprises maximize transaction margins, streamline operational overhead, and isolate counterparty risk.

**Execution Optimization:** Connects to more than 30 liquidity venues simultaneously.
