# Scalable Crypto Infrastructure for Asset Managers

## Moving Beyond the Fragmented Crypto Stack


For years, institutional wealth managers looking to offer digital assets faced a frustrating dilemma: either stitch together a fragile patchwork of disparate vendors or embark on a multi-million dollar engineering overhaul. The result was almost always an inefficient “sidecar” system that sat completely isolated from core investment workflows.


A truly scalable approach requires a fundamental shift. Instead of treating digital assets as an exotic exception, firms need a cohesive foundation that harmonizes execution, deep pool liquidity, and regulated custody under a single operational umbrella. By embedding these critical layers into a unified framework, asset managers can finally eliminate structural friction, mitigate counterparty risk, and treat crypto with the same operational rigor as traditional equities or fixed income.

### Streamlining Portfolio Workflows and Governance


Achieving institutional-grade scale isn’t just about secure storage; it’s about operational fluidity. True integration means having the capability to seamlessly rebalance model portfolios, execute bulk block trades across thousands of client accounts simultaneously, and maintain centralized oversight from a single dashboard.


When compliance safeguards, rigorous audit reporting, and fiduciary-level routing are built directly into the underlying tech layer—rather than clumsily layered on top—firms unlock a highly defensible, turnkey solution. This allows investment professionals to focus entirely on asset allocation and client alpha, rather than managing back-office complexity.
