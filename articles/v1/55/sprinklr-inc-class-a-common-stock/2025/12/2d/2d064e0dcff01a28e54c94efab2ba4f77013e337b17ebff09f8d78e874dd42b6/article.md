### Trend 1: AI agents redefine brand-to-consumer interactions


AI agents are transforming how brands engage customers across social platforms, turning social media into a measurable growth channel. From Instagram DMs to WhatsApp, TikTok, and Messenger, hybrid human-AI workflows allow brands to respond instantly, personalize recommendations, and escalate complex issues without increasing budgets.


[Source](https://www.cbnews.fr/digital/image-maia-lvmh-met-son-ia-generative-au-service-du-luxe-84652)


A prime example of this in action is LVMH, which has integrated a[generative AI agent](https://www.voguebusiness.com/story/technology/whats-agentic-ai-and-what-should-brands-know-about-it) named MaIA across its 75 maison brands, including Louis Vuitton, Dior, Dom Pérignon.


MaIA assists over 4,000 employees with multilingual, brand-aligned responses while maintaining ethical and human oversight. It has reduced manual workflows and improved conversion velocity across customer touchpoints, while ensuring a personalized experience for employees and customers.


**Next moves:**


- Start by defining how your agents should communicate, when to escalate issues and which compliance rules must be followed
- Route routine questions to bots while escalating complex issues to humans to lower contact center load and boost customer satisfaction
- Continuously A/B test scripts to maximize retention and personalized follow-ups
- Make sure every AI interaction feeds your[CRM](https://www.sprinklr.com/blog/contact-center-crm/) so you can track outcomes.
- Train your agents on your brand voice and past conversations, experiment with scripts and optimize next-best actions


**Best channels to prioritize:** Instagram DMs, WhatsApp and Messenger for high-volume conversational engagement; TikTok comments to guide Gen Z users to products.


**KPIs to measure:**


- **Instagram DMs** : AI response handling rate, time to first reply, conversion from automated sizing or product queries
- **WhatsApp / Messenger** : Resolution speed, retention lift, repeat purchase rate via AI sequences
- **TikTok** : Engagement with AI-driven prompts in comments or polls, click-throughs on bot-recommended links


**Pro Tip:** Train your AI agents on historical conversations and brand FAQs so they understand typical customer questions while responding in your[brand’s voice](https://www.sprinklr.com/cxm/brand-voice/) . **Best channels to prioritize** : TikTok and Instagram Reels for trend discovery, Pinterest for visual search and YouTube Shorts for video-based searches.


**What to measure:**


- **TikTok** : Views and clicks from discovery hashtags, trending sound adoption, participation in hashtag challenges

### Trend 3: Short-form video evolves into interactive and transactional media


Passive short-form video is losing effectiveness. Audiences expect engagement through gamified stories, polls, quizzes, AR filters and shoppable content. Failing to adapt would lead to lower engagement, missed conversions and losing your audience to competitors who provide interactive experiences.


[Source](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/owala-review)


A strong example is Owala, known for its insulated stainless-steel water bottles, which gained popularity among Gen Z through TikTok virality. Their colorful, mix-and-match FreeSip bottles became a trend, demonstrating how interactive short-form video content can drive both product discovery and sales, turning passive viewers into active customers.


**Next moves:**


- Launch gamified challenges and shoppable videos on Tiktok to increase participation and in-platform sales
- Embed AR filters and shoppable stickers in Instagram reels/ stories to shorten conversion paths and raise transaction velocity
- Add interactive CTAs and product links on YouTube Shorts to drive purchases and maximize revenue per view


**Platforms to prioritize:** TikTok and Instagram Reels/Stories for interactive short-form content, YouTube Shorts for tutorials and shoppable videos


**What to measure:**


- **TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts** : Participation rate in interactive polls, AR filters or gamified content, conversion from shoppable elements
- **Instagram Stories** : Swipe-ups and tap-through rates for shoppable Stories
- **AR Filters / Gamified Features** : Engagement rate and drop-off points in interactive experiences

### Trend 4: Authenticity and trust metrics drive partnerships and engagement


Credibility now drives[influencer marketing](https://www.sprinklr.com/blog/influencer-marketing-strategy/) success more than reach. Brands must place trust above follower counts and superficial metrics. Ignoring authenticity wastes spend on influencers and campaigns that may appear impressive but fail to generate measurable conversions, a key focus in current social media trends.


[Source](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DO9CflvjKse/)


You can take a cue in this regard from Pandora Charms. Known for their charm bracelets, Pandora leveraged micro-influencer activations to promote new charm collections. On TikTok and Pinterest, jewelry micro-influencers received limited-edition charms and shared creative styling ideas, generating authentic engagement and inspiring purchase behavior.


**Next moves:**


- Partner with micro-influencers who resonate with Gen Z on TikTok to drive authentic engagement and measurable conversions
- Collaborate with credible Instagram influencers for Reels and Stories to strengthen trust, boost conversions and maximize ROI
- Work with YouTube/Pinterest creators producing long-form or visual content to track engagement depth and ensure campaigns drive both awareness and purchase behavior


**Platforms to prioritize:** TikTok and Instagram for micro-influencers, YouTube and Pinterest for deeper, authentic content


**What to measure:**


- **TikTok / Instagram** : Engagement with influencer content, conversions, creator credibility score
- **YouTube / Pinterest** : Depth of audience interaction and content shares


**How to benchmark the use of social media trends against direct competitors?**


Use competitive dashboards to compare engagement rates, post frequency and sentiment across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and LinkedIn. Evaluate conversion velocity and CTRs to uncover content gaps and high-performing formats. Benchmarking this way identifies gaps, highlights top-performing formats, and reveals opportunities to adopt trends that drive measurable outcomes in your industry.


### Trend 5: Rise of niche communities as strategic engagement hubs


Public feeds are fragmenting into smaller, private communities where brand loyalty is built through sustained, meaningful exchange. Discord, Slack, LinkedIn, Telegram and WhatsApp are emerging as high-value engagement hubs.


[Source](https://discord.com/invite/jackinthebox)


Jack in the Box exemplifies this strategy. In June 2025, they sponsored a network of servers, including the Fortnite community "NA Practice Scrims," boasting approximately[114,000 members](https://digiday.com/media/brands-turn-to-discord-servers-as-a-means-to-reach-niche-influencer-channels-in-their-own-communities) . The collaboration aimed to drive attention and engagement toward a sponsored livestream with Faheed “T-Pain” Najm.


Additionally, during San Diego Comic-Con, Jack in the Box hosted a virtual after-party on Discord, featuring interactive channels, exclusive content and live events, resulting in over 7,000 members joining the server and more than[27,000 messages](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/why-jack-in-the-box-hosted-a-comic-con-afterparty-on-discord/604357/) exchanged. This proves that community spaces can rival public feeds in engagement intensity.


**Next moves:**


- For consumer brands, run Discord servers to offer early access or test products. Host live events, AMAs and product previews to increase participation, retention and community-driven sales
- Use private LinkedIn groups for thought leadership and client co-creation if you are a B2B company. Run co-creation projects and discussions to improve engagement depth and influence
- Share exclusive content or early access offers on Telegram/WhatsApp to drive repeat purchases and advocacy
- Measure[social media engagement](https://www.sprinklr.com/blog/social-media-engagement/) depth, retention, community-driven sales and[share of voice](https://www.sprinklr.com/blog/share-of-voice/) to track success and optimize ROI
- Set clear[community moderation](https://www.sprinklr.com/blog/community-moderation/) policies and actively moderate discussions to maintain a safe, engaging environment, encourage meaningful interactions and protect brand reputation.


**Platforms to prioritize** : Discord for Gen Z and live events, Slack and LinkedIn for B2B, Telegram and WhatsApp for private, high-value groups


**KPIs to measure:**


- **Discord** : Active participation, retention, messages exchanged
- **Slack / LinkedIn groups** : Contribution to discussions, repeat visits, co-creation initiatives
- **Telegram / WhatsApp** : Click-throughs on exclusive offers or early access content


**Which social media trend will give the biggest ROI for SMBs in 2025?**


For SMBs, short-form interactive video content is likely to deliver the highest ROI. Platforms like TikTok and Instagram Reels drive engagement and shares while keeping production costs manageable. Pairing these formats with micro-influencer collaborations or targeted paid campaigns amplifies reach, generating both leads and conversions. Discord communities can complement this by fostering loyalty and advocacy, creating a direct line to high-value customers and boosting repeat sales.

Future forecasts and what to watch in 2026


Besides these five trends, there are several other shifts you should be ready for in 2026. Emerging technologies, changing consumer behaviors and new content formats will create fresh opportunities. Preparing for these developments now will help you stay ahead and drive engagement across platforms:


### 1. Transparency in AI usage becomes a trust imperative


A survey by Forbes Advisor found that[76% of consumers](https://www.forbes.com/advisor/business/artificial-intelligence-consumer-sentiment/) worry about misinformation generated by AI tools. Consumers will demand clear disclosures about AI-generated content and data usage, making transparency a key factor in maintaining brand trust and loyalty.


Enterprises will need to disclose where and how AI contributes to content creation or moderation. Integrating AI transparency dashboards into CRM and analytics tools can help track when generative assets are used, communicate data governance clearly and preserve brand credibility.


**Impact** : Brands that disclose AI use proactively will see higher trust and engagement scores, especially in regulated industries like finance and healthcare.


### 2. Omnichannel strategies replace single-platform dependence


Dependence on a single social channel exposes brands to algorithmic volatility and uneven reach. As user activity fragments across TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube and WhatsApp, integrated omnichannel strategies will become essential.


Leaders should centralize campaign planning and measurement in unified systems that track cross-platform KPIs (reach, retention, ROI) in real time. This ensures consistent messaging and customer experience even when platform algorithms shift.


**Impact** : Brands that diversify touchpoints reduce cost per acquisition and protect engagement stability across changing platforms.


### 3. Sustainability is no longer optional


Consumers will[increasingly prioritize](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/consumer-packaged-goods/data-shows-shoppers-prioritizing-sustainability-and-values) brands that demonstrate genuine commitment to sustainability on social media. You should embed sustainability reporting into marketing content. You can also utilize eco-friendly supply chain practices and measure social engagement tied to sustainability campaigns to quantify impact.


**Impact:** Brands integrating sustainability content into performance dashboards can link reputation lift directly to revenue and loyalty metrics.


### 4. Voice commerce gains traction


Nearly[one-third of U.S. households](https://www.statista.com/topics/5406/voice-commerce-in-the-united-states/?srsltid=AfmBOop2BdW776O566csSMWtaU4YONXno_HJJN3mmvZ_QccfnMvdvDlH) own smart speakers or other connected devices. Social platforms and e-commerce systems are already adapting, integrating voice-enabled search and conversational checkout.


Enterprises should optimize metadata and content for voice discoverability (“near me,” “buy now” or “product comparisons”) and sync inventory to voice trends. AI-driven voice analytics will become a new measurement layer, revealing intent patterns that traditional dashboards miss.


**Impact** : Voice commerce will open a measurable path from spoken discovery to purchase, expanding ROI opportunities across connected ecosystems.
