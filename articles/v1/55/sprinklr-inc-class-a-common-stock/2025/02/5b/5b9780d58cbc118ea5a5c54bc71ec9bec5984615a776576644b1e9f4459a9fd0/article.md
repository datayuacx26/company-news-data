# Social Share of Voice: How to Measure and Improve It

**Key Takeaways:**


- Social media share of voice (SOV) measures how much of your industry's online conversation your brand owns versus competitors, making it a stronger indicator of competitive momentum than follower count or reach alone.
- SOV is only as useful as the sentiment behind it — a spike in mentions driven by negative conversations can inflate your share of voice while actively hurting brand perception, which is why volume and sentiment must be tracked together.
- Brands that consistently outpace their SOV relative to their market share are statistically more likely to grow market share over time, making SOV a leading indicator of future business performance, not just a marketing vanity metric.
- Increasing SOV doesn't require a bigger budget: tapping into real-time trends, activating employees as advocates, and benchmarking content against competitors are high-impact levers that compound over time.


A strong social media share of voice signals greater brand awareness, higher audience trust, and more influence over purchase decisions.


In this guide, we break down how to calculate social media share of voice, why it matters for your marketing strategy, and actionable ways to increase it using the right tools and tactics.

##


What is social media share of voice?


Social media share of voice (SOV) is the percentage of total industry or competitor conversations that mention your brand on social platforms. It factors in[brand mentions](https://www.sprinklr.com/social-media-glossary/brand-mention/) , visibility, influence, and authority across social channels to give you a measurable picture of your competitive position.


97% of consumers, in a[Goodfirms survey](https://www.goodfirms.co/press-releases/online-reviews-influence-purchase-decisions) , say online reviews influence their purchase decisions — making your brand's share of online conversation a direct driver of business outcomes. By analyzing SOV, you can evaluate competitive position, identify gaps in visibility and uncover opportunities to increase reach and engagement. It also helps connect social performance to business outcomes like brand awareness, demand generation, and customer loyalty.

For example, if your brand receives 800 mentions and the total industry conversation adds up to 4,000 mentions, your SOV is 20%. Tracking this consistently helps you spot shifts in competitive momentum, identify content gaps, and find opportunities to grow your presence before rivals do.

***What is a good social media share of voice benchmark?***


There's no universal benchmark — a "good" SOV depends on your industry size, number of competitors, and brand maturity. A general rule of thumb is: if your SOV exceeds your market share, you are in a position of strength and more likely to gain ground on competitors over time.


For example, a brand with 15% market share but 25% SOV is outpunching its weight and building future demand. For most mid-sized brands competing in crowded categories, an SOV above 20–25% is considered competitive. The more important signal is directional; whether your SOV is trending up or down quarter over quarter.


##


Why social media share of voice is a must-have for growth


In a saturated social media space, brands compete not just for attention but for dominance in conversations. Share of voice on social media indicates how much of that conversation your brand controls and surfaces opportunity to shape consumer perception. Tracking SOV isn’t just about measuring mentions — it’s about understanding your competitive standing, gauging engagement and finding opportunities to strengthen your presence.


Here’s what measuring SOV can help you with:


### 1. Tracking brand growth


Social media share of voice gives you long-term insights into your brand’s visibility and growth online. If mentions and SOV increase, it means your marketing campaigns and strategies are paying off to boost awareness, positioning you as a key player.


### 2. Understanding audience engagement


[Social engagement](https://www.sprinklr.com/blog/social-media-engagement/) provides an early signal of how effectively your brand is capturing attention and shaping conversations in the market. Rising mentions, interactions and conversation velocity often indicate that your messaging, campaigns or thought leadership are gaining traction with target audiences.


However, engagement should not be measured by volume alone. The quality of interactions matters just as much. By pairing share of voice with sentiment analysis, brands can understand whether increased visibility is reinforcing trust, driving advocacy or surfacing risk, giving leaders a clearer view of audience perception and brand health.


**Also Read:**[How To Perform Social Media Sentiment Analysis](https://www.sprinklr.com/cxm/social-media-sentiment-analysis/)


### 3. Refining content strategies


Tracking share of voice on social media can help identify campaigns or topics that drive the most conversation. With this, you can pinpoint the types of content that resonate with your audience, whether it's certain themes, formats or messaging styles. You can then fine-tune your[social media content strategy](https://www.sprinklr.com/blog/social-media-content-strategy/) to engage your audience better, ultimately improving your content marketing.


### 4. Conducting competitive analysis


Perhaps one of the most important benefits of tracking share of voice in social media is to understand how your mentions compare to competitors.


Say your competitor consistently clocks more mentions, understanding why — whether it’s due to better engagement, superior content or effective campaign strategies — can inform your approach to your own social media marketing strategy.[Social media competitor analysis](https://www.sprinklr.com/blog/social-media-competitor-analysis/) helps you get an edge over competitors, whether that’s by improving niche conversations or doubling down on high-impact topics.


***What is the difference between social media SOV and overall share of voice?***


Social media share of voice specifically measures your brand's share of mentions, conversations, and engagement across social platforms relative to competitors. Overall share of voice is broader — it covers paid advertising (impression share), organic search visibility (SEO), earned media (PR and press mentions), and social, combined. While social SOV is a strong real-time signal of brand perception and audience engagement, a complete picture of your competitive standing requires tracking SOV across all these channels together.

Tips to increase your share of voice on social media


If you are dissatisfied with your social media share of voice, you can improve it to boost your visibility and carve out a strong[social media presence](https://www.sprinklr.com/blog/building-social-media-presence/) . All you need is an actionable strategy, bolstered by the right tools. Below are four tips to actively boost your share of voice:


### 1. Tap into social media trends


Engaging with the latest[social media trends](https://www.sprinklr.com/blog/social-media-trends/) is one of the fastest ways to amplify your brand’s share of voice. Active social media users often catch on trending topics, and when you ride that wave, it can significantly expand your reach and brand visibility. It helps your brand become part of a larger cultural movement, increasing its potential for greater engagement.


***Tips to leverage trends:***


- **Monitor trending hashtags:** Use a[social media analysis tool](https://www.sprinklr.com/blog/social-media-analytics-tools/) to monitor hashtags and trending topics on platforms like Twitter, Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn to spot opportunities for participation.
- **Watch your competition:** Tracking what your competitors are talking about and how much engagement it is getting can help you become part of that conversation.
- **Make it unique:** If you want your brand to stand out, put a spin on the trend and align it with your brand’s voice for authentic involvement.


For instance, during[Pokémon's 30th anniversary campaign](https://www.polygon.com/pokemon-30-year-anniversary-logos-all-pokedex/) , The Pokémon Company released over 1,000 unique Pokémon logos distributed via random drops on X — turning a simple hashtag into a viral collecting game. Brands like[Uniqlo](https://www.uniqlo.com/my/en/spl/ut/pokemon-30th-Anniversary) and[LEGO](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-news/the-lego-group-reveals-first-ever-lego-pokemon-sets-and-trainer-challenge) jumped in with co-branded product launches, each riding the cultural wave to boost their own share of voice without creating a trend from scratch.

### 2. Craft share-worthy content


Content that resonates with your audience on an emotional level or provides clear value will have higher[social media interactions](https://www.sprinklr.com/blog/social-media-interaction/) and be more share-worthy. When users share content with their networks, it amplifies your brand’s reach, increasing visibility and engagement. This can directly boost your share of voice. To make your content share-worthy, create posts that are entertaining, educational, inspiring, and/or relatable.


***Tips to create shareable content:***


- **Understand your audience:** The first step to create great content is to understand audience demographics, their interests, pain points, and behavior. You can then create content that speaks to them directly.
- **Experiment with formats:** Try different formats like text, images, infographics, videos, reels and memes on different social platforms to see what resonates with your audience. For instance, short-form video continues to dominate engagement across platforms, with platforms like LinkedIn and Instagram prioritizing video content in their algorithms, making it a key driver of visibility and reach.
- **Make sharing easy:** Encourage your audience to share your content easily by including social sharing buttons and prompts.

According to Aspire's 2026 State of Influencer Marketing report,[69%](https://www.aspire.io/blog/10-influencer-marketing-stats#:~:text=The%20State%20of%20Influencer%20Marketing%202026%20is,influencer%2Dgenerated%20content%20performs%20better%20than%20brand%2Ddirected%20content.) of marketers say influencer-generated content outperforms brand-directed content, and 74% plan to increase influencer budgets this year. Influencer partnerships and brand collaborations provide a powerful opportunity to expand your reach and boost SOV. By partnering with well-known figures or brands that align with your values, you tap into their established audiences, increasing your SOV through their advocacy.

- **Leverage AI:** AI-powered tools can comb through millions of influencer profiles including scanning their demographics, topics of interest, industries, content themes and social channels to find the right match to ensure the[social media collaboration](https://www.sprinklr.com/blog/social-media-collaboration/) feels authentic.
- **Use data-driven insights:** Use data analytics and[social media KPIs](https://www.sprinklr.com/blog/social-media-kpis/) like engagement metrics or audience demographics to find the right content to share a content strategy with influencers for targeted collaborations.
