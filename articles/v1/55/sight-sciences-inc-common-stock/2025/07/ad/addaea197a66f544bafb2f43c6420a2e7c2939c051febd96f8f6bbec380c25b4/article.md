# Introducing the New Sight Sciences Brand

At Sight Sciences, our mission has always been clear: to develop transformative, interventional technologies that allow eyecare providers to procedurally elevate the standards of care – empowering people to keep seeing.


As our company continued to grow and our impact expanded, we felt it was time for our visual identity to evolve alongside us. We are proud to unveil the new Sight Sciences brand and logo.


**Why the Change?**


This is more than just a logo update—it’s a reflection of who we are today and the future we’re building. Since our founding, we’ve evolved from an ambitious startup to a leader in interventional ophthalmology. Our new brand honors that journey while signaling a bold, forward-looking commitment to innovation and progress.


**The Meaning Behind the New Brand**


The updated design features clean, modern typography and purposeful visual elements that reflect precision, clarity, and forward momentum. The flowing lines in the logo convey the energy and innovation that drive our work—and the strong, collaborative relationships we foster between science and medicine, providers and patients, and within our own team.


**Watch the Transformation**


To celebrate this milestone, we created a series of short videos that highlight the evolution of our logo and the story it tells about where we have been—and where we are going.


**What’s Next?**


You will begin to see our new identity across all Sight Sciences platforms and materials—our website, product literature, social channels, and event branding. While our look is new, our mission remains unchanged: to transform vision care through advanced interventional solutions that support providers in protecting long-term eye health.


Thank you for being part of the Sight Sciences journey. We’re excited for what’s ahead—and grateful to have you with us.
