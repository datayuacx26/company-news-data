# NETSCOUT Recognized as Technology Innovation Leader by Frost & Sullivan

NETSCOUT’s unwavering commitment to technological innovation has been recognized again by Frost & Sullivan, this time for cybersecurity best practices in the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region. By integrating artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) into the core cybersecurity strategy, NETSCOUT is redefining the industry by analyzing large volumes of network traffic in real time while identifying anomalies and triggering automated countermeasures to stop threats in their tracks.


Since 1961, Frost & Sullivan has sought to bring together the brightest minds in business to track emerging trends. The company identifies true innovators, disruptors, and leaders via independent and primary research and market analysis. Its Best Practices Award recognizes unique excellence in the recipient’s field, in this case cybersecurity in the MENA region.


For both enterprise and service provider networks, NETSCOUT’s[nGenius](https://www.netscout.com/solutions/ngenius-enterprise-performance-management) ,[Omnis Cyber Intelligence](https://www.netscout.com/product/cyber-intelligence) , and[Arbor DDoS protection](https://www.netscout.com/arbor) product lines can be integrated to provide a unified network data solution for network visibility, performance management, and security. The integration capabilities of NETSCOUT’s Visibility Without Borders platforms help create a holistic strategy for managing and securing the most complex networks in the world.


NETSCOUT’s visibility doesn’t end within the core network. With our[Business Edge Observability](https://www.netscout.com/solutions/network-observability) suite, enterprises can improve network visibility to remote sites and devices, which is imperative for many industries including[healthcare](https://www.netscout.com/industries/healthcare) ,[utilities](https://www.netscout.com/industries/utilities) , retail, and more.


With the continuous innovation NETSCOUT delivers in our evolving product lines, enterprises and service providers can reap new benefits from their investments in technology. The seamless integration between performance management and cybersecurity improves visibility, availability, digital resilience, and operational efficiency.


Thanks to key partners across a variety of industries, including telcos, enterprises, and internet service providers, NETSCOUT is able to drive innovation by solving unique customer problems. No network is too large or complex for NETSCOUT to deliver Visibility Without Borders.
