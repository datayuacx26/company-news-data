# NETSCOUT to Have a Strong Presence at Cisco Live

When it comes to everything artificial intelligence (AI), the network is key. That is why this year’s Cisco Live theme is— the network as the foundation of the AI era!


And in a bit of a twist this year, the old marketing slogan “What happens in Vegas, stays in Vegas” gets turned on its ear, because this seminal event will help shape a lot of innovation that sure won’t stay on the convention floor or meeting rooms at Mandalay Bay.


Every year, Cisco Live stands as the undisputed gathering place for the people who keep the digital world running. From May 31 through June 4, 2026, tens of thousands of IT and networking professionals will descend on this year’s event to learn, connect, and chart the course of their organizations’ technology futures. Network engineers, security architects, operations leaders, cloud strategists, and C-suite executives all find common ground here, embracing a shared mission to build infrastructure that is faster, smarter, and more resilient than ever before.


One thing nearly every attendee has in common is facing challenges, such as rising complexity, escalating cyberthreats, and the relentless pressure to deliver flawless digital experiences, which demand more than any single team can solve alone.


### **NETSCOUT Is Proud To Be an Important Part of the Conversation**


For organizations responsible for the performance, security, and availability of complex digital ecosystems, NETSCOUT has been a trusted partner for decades. That’s exactly why Cisco Live is such a natural fit for us. The operators, leaders, and technical teams who attend this event are the same professionals we work alongside every day, and this year, we’re bringing some of our most exciting innovations to the conference center floor.


At the heart of our presence is a simple but powerful idea: AI is only as good as the data feeding it. As enterprises race to deploy AI-driven operations and AIOps platforms, the quality, completeness, and context of underlying data have never mattered more. NETSCOUT’s answer is NETSCOUT Smart Data, which is structured, enriched, AI-ready metadata derived from deep packet inspection at wire speed and enterprise scale. This data is not sampled, nor inferred. It is observed.


Smart Data is the foundation of the[NETSCOUT AI-ready data platform](https://www.netscout.com/platform) , which captures every interaction, every transaction, and every experience, driving businesses in real time. It’s a single source of truth, generated once at the network layer and consumable across an organization’s entire technology ecosystem. What’s more, NETSCOUT’s Smart Data powers[observability](https://www.netscout.com/observability) and[cybersecurity](https://www.netscout.com/cybersecurity) applications for platforms such as Splunk, ServiceNow, and beyond.
