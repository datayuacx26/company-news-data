[NETSCOUT SYSTEMS, INC.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.netscout.com%2F&esheet=54520225&newsitemid=20260422791375&lan=en-US&anchor=NETSCOUT+SYSTEMS%2C+INC.&index=1&md5=b6d21418198a612d3b30910eac50b199) (NASDAQ: NTCT), a leading provider of enterprise network observability, carrier service assurance, cybersecurity, and Distributed-Denial-of-Service (DDoS) protection solutions, plans to announce its fourth quarter and full fiscal year 2026 financial results for the period ended March 31, 2026, on Thursday, May 7, 2026, at approximately 7:30 a.m. ET. NETSCOUT will host a corresponding conference call and live webcast on the same day at 8:30 a.m. ET.


The number for the conference call is (800) 267-6316, or (203) 518-9783 for international callers. The conference call ID is NTCTQ426.
