# NETSCOUT Earns Eight Leader Badges in the G2 Spring 2026 Grid Reports

NETSCOUT is proud to be recognized with eight badges in the G2 Spring 2026 Grid Reports, reflecting strong customer satisfaction and market presence across multiple security and observability categories.


G2 is one of the world’s largest and most trusted software marketplaces, where rankings are based on verified customer reviews and independent data about market presence. Because these reports reflect the experiences and feedback of real users, they offer valuable insight into how solutions perform in real-world environments.


This spring, NETSCOUT solutions were recognized across several categories spanning distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) protection, network management, web security, and enterprise monitoring, reinforcing the value our customers see in our ability to protect and assure complex digital infrastructure.

## **How G2 Grid Reports Work**


G2’s Grid Reports evaluate software products based on two primary factors: customer satisfaction and market presence.


### **Customer Satisfaction**


Customer satisfaction scores are derived from verified user reviews submitted by real customers. These reviews assess several factors, including:


- Ease of use
- Ease of administration
- Implementation experience
- Quality of support
- Product capabilities and reliability
- Likelihood to recommend the product


This feedback provides valuable insight into how well solutions perform in real operational environments.


### **Market Presence**


Market presence reflects the overall footprint and adoption of a product, calculated using publicly available data and third-party metrics such as:


- Company size and growth
- Product adoption and customer base
- Market share indicators
- Social and web presence
- Team member scale and momentum


Products that score highly in both customer satisfaction and market presence are positioned in the Leader quadrant of the Grid.


### **Momentum and Usability Reports**


In addition to the main Grid rankings, G2 also publishes specialized reports that evaluate additional product characteristics.


Momentum Grid Reports measure the growth trajectory of a product, taking into account factors such as review growth, review ratings trends, and market engagement.


Usability Index Reports evaluate the overall user experience, including ease of setup, ease of use, and ease of administration.


These reports highlight products that deliver both strong performance and a streamlined experience for users.
