# Intellyx Names NETSCOUT to Prestigious 2026 Digital Innovator Award List

Earning industrywide recognition two years in a row is very rewarding. Achieving that same honor six consecutive times makes a statement. Being selected by Intellyx for its[2026 Digital Innovator Award](https://intellyx.com/2026/06/09/2026-intellyx-digital-innovator-award-winners-announced-2/) as an industry disruptor and innovator is a validation of NETSCOUT’s long-held commitment to blazing new trails.


> At Intellyx, we’re always looking to speak to the most innovative and disruptive software vendors around the world,” said Jason Bloomberg, managing director of Intellyx BV. “We’re happy to call out those vendors who successfully brief us with our Digital Innovator Award.


Intellyx, a respected industry analyst firm, confers its Digital Innovator Award on those leading-edge vendors deemed worthy of watching. Following a thorough selection process and a successful briefing, the firm honors those companies that meet its exacting standards.


NETSCOUT continues to distinguish itself through its innovations for both enterprise and communications service provider (CSP) markets. A few examples of recent developments include:


#### **Enterprise Innovations in Observability**


- NETSCOUT’s[new observability capabilities](https://ir.netscout.com/news/news-details/2026/NETSCOUT-Enhances-Resiliency-and-Responsiveness-With-New-Observability-Capabilities/default.aspx) close[remote-site monitoring](https://www.netscout.com/solutions/remote-site-observability) gaps in locations without local IT staff to assure quality performance and user experience.
- Real-time[SSL certificate visibility](https://www.netscout.com/blog/shrinking-lifespan-ssltls-certificates) detects expirations, surfaces unknown certificates, and heads off the outages, compliance failures, and security risks that accompany today’s shorter certificate lifespans.
- NETSCOUT extends continuous[observability into large, multicluster Kubernetes deployments](https://ir.netscout.com/news/news-details/2025/NETSCOUT-Extends-Visibility-Into-Kubernetes-Containers-With-Continuous-Observability-Innovation/default.aspx) , eliminating the blind spots that make it hard for IT teams to keep service performance, user experience, and application availability consistent across complex cloud environments.


#### **Communications Service Provider Innovations in Observability**


- NETSCOUT brings its[AI-ready Smart Data](https://ir.netscout.com/news/news-details/2026/NETSCOUT-Delivers-AI-Ready-Smart-Data-for-Communications-Service-Providers/default.aspx) to CSPs, turning raw network traffic into curated feeds that power scalable agentic AI for better customer experience, predictive maintenance, and stronger network security at lower cost and risk.
- NETSCOUT delivers[end-to-end visibility into 5G standalone network slices](https://ir.netscout.com/news/news-details/2026/NETSCOUT-Ensures-Optimal-Customer-Experiences-for-5G-Network-Slicing-Services/default.aspx) , giving CSPs the real-time monitoring and auditable service-level agreement (SLA) performance needed to keep services such as immersive gaming, large-scale live events, and remote surgery consistently reliable.


NETSCOUT delivers the AI-ready data foundation behind modern observability, cybersecurity, and AIOps. Powered by its Visibility Without Borders strategy, the NETSCOUT Data Platform produces Smart Data, the trusted, actionable intelligence enterprises and service providers rely on to resolve disruptions faster, strengthen resilience, and power their AI initiatives.
