# Large attachments

Earlier this year,[we released attachments](https://www.usewaypoint.com/changelog/attachments) on Waypoint. Since then, customers have loved the ability to include files with their emails.


However, one key limitation has been size: emails with standard attachments are capped at **5 MB** .


Starting today,` Growth` customers can send emails with attachments up to **10 MB** and` Pro` customers up to **20 MB** . This unlocks including larger files such as custom reports, presentations, detailed invoices, or higher-quality assets.


[Learn more about sending with large attachments](https://www.usewaypoint.com/docs/sending-with-attachments#using-large-attachments)
