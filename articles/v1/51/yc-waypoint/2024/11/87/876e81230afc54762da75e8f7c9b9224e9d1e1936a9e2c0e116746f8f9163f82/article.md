# Webhooks

One of our most requested features is now available –[webhooks](https://www.usewaypoint.com/docs/webhooks) . Webhooks are how services notify each other of events. On Waypoint, customers can use webhooks take action after an email event like a ‘bounce’ or ‘delivery’ has happened.


For example, posting a notification in a Slack channel whenever an email bounces or is marked as spam.


[Add custom webhook endpoint URLs](https://www.usewaypoint.com/docs/webhooks#adding-a-webhook-endpoint) or use our[Slack and Zapier](https://www.usewaypoint.com/docs/webhooks#slack-and-zapier-integrations) integrations to make it even easier to connect and transform your data as needed.
