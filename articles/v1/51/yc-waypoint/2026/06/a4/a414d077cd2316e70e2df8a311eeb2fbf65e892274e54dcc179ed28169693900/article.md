# Export email activity


You can now export email message activity from your workspace as a CSV file. Use the filters on the[Activity page](https://www.usewaypoint.com/docs/activity-and-metrics) to narrow down the messages you need, then click **Export CSV** to download the results for further analysis or reporting.
