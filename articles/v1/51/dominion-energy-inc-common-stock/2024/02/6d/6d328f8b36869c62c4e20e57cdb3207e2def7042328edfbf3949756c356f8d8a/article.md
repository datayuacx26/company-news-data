### Protect Yourself: Dominion Energy Shares Signs of Utility Scams as Spring Approaches


02/08/2024


[Download this Press Release (opens in new window) PDF](https://s205.q4cdn.com/611413639/files/doc_news/Protect-Yourself-Dominion-Energy-Shares-Signs-of-Utility-Scams-as-Spring-Approaches-02-08-2024-2024.pdf)


**CLEVELAND, Feb. 8** – Milder temperatures bring with it the potential for an increase in scam activity door-to-door, by phone or via text. Dominion Energy is reminding customers to stay vigilant in the face of the upcoming changing seasons.


“Scammers never seem to take a day off. We continually hear about new ways scammers are approaching our customers to get their sensitive information,” said **Bob Metzinger** , **general manager** of **Customer Service at Dominion Energy Ohio** . “It’s important for everyone in our service area to understand how we work with our customers so they can keep their information safe and help stop these criminals.”


**Know the warning signs of a utility scam:**


1. Dominion Energy will never demand payment with a credit or debit card by phone or ask a customer to wire a transfer, send a money order or buy a prepaid or gift card to pay a utility bill.
2. Dominion Energy does not call and threaten service disconnection if a customer does not make a payment immediately.
3. Utility scammers are known to falsify their caller ID to make it appear the call is from a Dominion Energy customer service number. When in doubt, hang up.
4. Utility scammers often target small businesses and threaten service disconnection during peak business hours to instill fear and a sense of urgency.
5. Customers should not allow individuals claiming to be Dominion Energy employees to enter their homes without proper identification, an appointment or a reported emergency.


**What to do if customers receive a suspicious call:**


- **Hang up** . Do not provide account, payment or any other personal information.
- **Verify** . Scammers do not typically know the customer's account balance. Customers can verify their account balance and payment due date online or on the Dominion Energy app.
- **Ignore** . Do not respond to suspicious emails or text messages. Do not click on links or attachments prompting energy bill payment.
