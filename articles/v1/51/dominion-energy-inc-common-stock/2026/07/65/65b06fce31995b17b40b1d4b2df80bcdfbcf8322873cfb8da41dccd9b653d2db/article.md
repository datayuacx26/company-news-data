# Dominion Energy Brings Free Energy-Saving Program to Santee and Elloree Customers

### Dominion Energy Brings Free Energy-Saving Program to Santee and Elloree Customers


07/15/2026


[Download this Press Release (opens in new window) PDF 488 KB](https://s205.q4cdn.com/611413639/files/doc_news/2026/07/15/Dominion-Energy-Brings-Free-Energy-Saving-Program-to-Santee-and-Elloree-Customers.pdf)


**CAYCE, S.C. (July 15, 2026) –** Dominion Energy customers in the Santee area recently attended a kickoff event for the Neighborhood Energy Efficiency Program (NEEP), with an additional kickoff event scheduled for eligible customers in Elloree on July, 23. The program offers eligible customers in both communities free energy-saving products and services, including home assessments, on-site education and installation of energy-efficient products.


Customers began receiving information on how to participate in June, with installations in homes beginning in early July and continuing through September. Dominion Energy representatives will complete installations at no cost.


“Programs like NEEP help bring practical energy-saving solutions directly to customers,” said Derek McDaniel, program manager. “By combining education with no-cost products, we’re helping families lower their energy use and make their homes more comfortable and efficient.”


Energy-saving products available to program-eligible residential customers include the following:


- LED light bulbs
- Faucet aerators and low-flow shower heads
- Electric water heater wraps and pipe insulation
- Advanced smart power strips
- Door sweeps and weatherstripping for doors and windows
- HVAC air filter replacement
- Digital wall thermometers


In addition to NEEP, customers had the opportunity to talk in person with a Dominion Energy customer assistance representative and learn about the company’s available programs and resources.


Since its launch in 2013, NEEP has helped Dominion Energy customers across the state reduce electricity usage through direct-install improvements and energy education. Over the past decade, customers have saved enough electricity to power approximately 112,000 homes for a year.


Dominion Energy offers a comprehensive portfolio of EnergyWise programs that work together to increase overall energy efficiency and reduce energy usage. For more information about these programs, visit[DominionEnergy.com/Savings](https://www.dominionenergy.com/savings) or call toll-free at 877-510-7234.
