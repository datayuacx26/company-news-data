# QuantumScape Reports Second Quarter 2026 Business and Financial Results

## QuantumScape Reports Second Quarter 2026 Business and Financial Results


July 22, 2026


[PDF Version](https://ir.quantumscape.com/node/12706/pdf)


SAN JOSE, Calif., July 22, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- QuantumScape Corporation (NASDAQ: QS), a global leader in next-generation solid-state lithium-metal battery technology, today announced its business and financial results for the second quarter of 2026, which ended June 30.


The company posted a letter to shareholders on its Investor Relations website,[ir.quantumscape.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=IK1KInUtAtjmSV-_KWb_ehrvNmlH3jH5YPYgEVShdCF77Ti_dLKCJ22DjHmnLVYzQuvc7AKJBD8_oI5eG92Zfg0FUE09Oa0ZrTkpJLp4eb0=) , that details second-quarter financial results and provides a business update.


QuantumScape will host a live webcast today at 2 p.m. Pacific Time (5 p.m. Eastern Time), accessible via its[IR Events page](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=H2gTUpMAW8vi3F08hXa30KTf_3BHq6iFByr7fEaGCxd-nM7BN4XUSBzzIC3dQQJcl_CANPaFlm8yGKVAoOjal-_5heD1xtQ7p3M1Nhy5e1kHi5-yqzPVx5AVred-tvj7oFUXqKKTuMPezISPih0a16wTtwTdxcrb2tNJra0_nEg=) . Siva Sivaram, chief executive officer, and Kevin Hettrich, chief financial officer, will participate on the call.


An archive of the webcast will be available shortly after the call for 12 months.
