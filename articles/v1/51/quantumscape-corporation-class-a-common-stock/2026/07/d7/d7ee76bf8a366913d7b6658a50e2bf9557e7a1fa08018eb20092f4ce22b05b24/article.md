# QuantumScape Announces Timing of Second Quarter 2026 Business Results and Webcast

## QuantumScape Announces Timing of Second Quarter 2026 Business Results and Webcast


July 8, 2026


[PDF Version](https://ir.quantumscape.com/node/12691/pdf)


SAN JOSE, Calif., July 08, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[QuantumScape Corporation](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=QT23pt_5g3E7U9voB9RDp8x66jVcG5THjIPeGLqezWGMciZ89pOlDl12NLKB0R6VMzRzdhwb8mCMF6qtxg2Ts2-E8q2eMzq7_P9ljfBgXYA=) (NASDAQ: QS), a global leader in next-generation solid-state lithium-metal battery technology, today announced it will release its 2026 second-quarter business results after market close on Wednesday, July 22, 2026. This will be followed by a conference call at 2 p.m. Pacific Time (5 p.m. Eastern Time). Siva Sivaram, chief executive officer, and Kevin Hettrich, chief financial officer, will participate on the call.


Starting today, July 8, shareholders can submit questions ([here](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=PsN2YbxE9PBzOFVP6E1LjXFSQAaZpARFzQz3BIIKzk4J__F05jRhoxOnTxNmFufnX3NdQybKff71I6Wr_lnToLzUUuVEij8jivrhpDrmxmUZbq7k8LcVCTZok5RERTWK2lZcq7sDGs8uOAeLZQaX56PC9yeVzI0QOO9sdTw63YXrI0VminK6m6ppM2997GKE) ) they would like addressed on the call. QuantumScape management will respond to a selection of the submitted questions. The company will accept questions until Tuesday, July 21, at 2 p.m. Pacific Time (5 p.m. Eastern Time).


The call will be accessible live via a webcast on QuantumScape’s[IR Events Calendar page](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=BeKA23eNUhSxIwf5Va0Qz13LkrQ4cKPdTDES6o7r5GdnybYreTduddztluOvO9fS8gPmSKQvyLJVquuatw61vhPTNsNKj8J7ODPeyZU3wPqcv_pHHRNUZkKljOaLcX7O) . An archive of the webcast will be available shortly after the call for 12 months.

The company’s next-generation batteries are designed to enable greater energy density, faster charging and enhanced safety to support the transition away from legacy energy sources toward a lower carbon future.
