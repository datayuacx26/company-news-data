# QuantumScape Reports First Quarter 2026 Business and Financial Results

## QuantumScape Reports First Quarter 2026 Business and Financial Results


April 22, 2026


[PDF Version](https://ir.quantumscape.com/node/12431/pdf)


SAN JOSE, Calif., April 22, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- QuantumScape Corporation (NASDAQ: QS), a global leader in next-generation solid-state lithium-metal battery technology, today announced its business and financial results for the first quarter of 2026, which ended March 31.


The company posted a letter to shareholders on its Investor Relations website,[ir.quantumscape.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=bRiTLpOUBQf0KY822p_d1lSWvM4uZ5vplNSK2S66M6vZ0A-lZwVQupHTWjJOlPaZO45EqyMN_-UJ_FFLbmc7Vx-Z1WX1fj-uEmuZSkuBVJE=) , that details first-quarter financial results and provides a business update.


QuantumScape will host a live webcast today at 2 p.m. Pacific Time (5 p.m. Eastern Time), accessible via its[IR Events page](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=J9aCGz1JgmRyqJ3hvdW8jv1SVvA6yT4JCu3chN8WKG90fh3FPNRLAtCCAXSN1_hO6xW5vI9T2egaEy5KWFkGvg0tsEML2Pxa4FJOdLIuJPSU-kYiQtentIiP7ufT3r0ySYxbwSSZ03r2jR0aFgpMcoiGoHmHmaRD7l_-i6ZtusI=) . Siva Sivaram, chief executive officer, and Kevin Hettrich, chief financial officer, will participate on the call.


An archive of the webcast will be available shortly after the call for 12 months.


**About QuantumScape Corporation**
