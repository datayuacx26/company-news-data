# QuantumScape Reports Fourth Quarter 2025 Business and Financial Results

## QuantumScape Reports Fourth Quarter 2025 Business and Financial Results


February 11, 2026


[PDF Version](https://ir.quantumscape.com/node/12096/pdf)


SAN JOSE, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Feb. 11, 2026-- QuantumScape Corporation (NASDAQ: QS), a global leader in next-generation solid-state lithium-metal battery technology, today announced its business and financial results for the fourth quarter of 2025, which ended December 31.


The company posted a letter to shareholders on its Investor Relations website,[ir.quantumscape.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fir.quantumscape.com%2F&esheet=54422535&newsitemid=20260211029862&lan=en-US&anchor=ir.quantumscape.com&index=1&md5=152373d590f9044b34cec147ed5f6186) , that details fourth-quarter financial results and provides a business update.


QuantumScape will host a live webcast today at 2 p.m. Pacific Time (5 p.m. Eastern Time), accessible via its[IR Events page](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.quantumscape.com%2Fresources%2Fevents-and-presentations%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54422535&newsitemid=20260211029862&lan=en-US&anchor=IR+Events+page&index=2&md5=b2dab05678e7c89feff2971d7c09072b) . Siva Sivaram, chief executive officer, and Kevin Hettrich, chief financial officer, will participate on the call.


An archive of the webcast will be available shortly after the call for 12 months.


**About QuantumScape Corporation**


QuantumScape is on a mission to revolutionize energy storage to enable a sustainable future. The company’s next-generation batteries are designed to enable greater energy density, faster charging and enhanced safety to support the transition away from legacy energy sources toward a lower carbon future.
