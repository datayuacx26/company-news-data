# Amazon Pharmacy now delivers GLP-1 medications to eligible Medicare patients for $50 a month

---


## Key takeaways


- Eligible Medicare Part D patients can now get GLP-1 medications like Wegovy, Zepbound, and Foundayo for $50 per month through Amazon Pharmacy with free home delivery and in-office kiosk pickup.


- [Amazon Pharmacy](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/tag/amazon-pharmacy) automates eligibility verification, prior authorization, and billing—patients just need to add their Bridge insurance plan and Medicare ID to get started.


- Same-day delivery is available in more than 3,100 U.S. cities and towns, with no Prime membership required.


---


[Amazon Pharmacy](https://pharmacy.amazon.com/) is now delivering GLP-1 weight management medications—including Wegovy (injectable and oral pill), Zepbound KwikPen, and Foundayo—to eligible Medicare patients for $50 per month with free home delivery. Same-day delivery is available in more than 3,100 U.S. cities and towns, with no Prime membership required.


[Amazon One Medical introduces weight management program with upfront GLP-1 medication costs](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-glp1-management-program)


The new program combines primary care, pharmacy, and virtual options to treat obesity as a chronic condition—all in one place.


The medications are available through the Medicare GLP-1


[Bridge Program](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coverage/prescription-drug-coverage/medicare-glp-1-bridge) , a short-term federal initiative running through December 31, 2027, that enables eligible Medicare Part D members who meet specific clinical criteria to access weight management prescriptions at a predictable, accessible cost.


Many submitted claims have already resulted in complete orders, with patients receiving their medications quickly and without administrative burden.


## How does Amazon Pharmacy make it easier for Medicare patients to access and sign up for GLP-1 medications?


For years, cost has been the biggest barrier keeping Medicare patients from accessing GLP-1 medications. Now that the Bridge Program has addressed that, Amazon Pharmacy is focused on removing the remaining friction: confusing paperwork, prior authorization delays, and long waits at the pharmacy counter.


Once a clinician sends an electronic GLP-1 prescription to Amazon Pharmacy, customers can add their Bridge insurance plan and Medicare details to their Amazon Pharmacy account and Amazon Pharmacy takes care of the rest—eligibility verification, prior authorization, and starting the enrollment process on the customer's behalf. There's no need for patients to navigate enrollment steps, chase down approvals, or wait at a counter.


“We want to make sure nothing stands in the way—no confusing paperwork, no prior authorization delays, no waiting at a pharmacy counter,” said Tanvi Patel, vice president and general manager of Amazon Pharmacy. “Once a patient's clinician sends the prescription, we take care of the rest and deliver their medication to their door.”


[How Amazon Pharmacy has helped customers save more than $100 million on prescription medications like GLP-1s and insulin](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/glp-1-drugs-savings-discount-coupons-amazon-pharmacy)


Amazon Pharmacy automatically applies manufacturer-sponsored coupons directly to eligible orders, making medications more affordable.

[4,500 by the end of 2026](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-pharmacy-same-day-delivery-medication-expansion)

[Amazon Pharmacy kiosks](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-pharmacy-kiosks-one-medical) at select One Medical locations, where newly approved Wegovy and Foundayo GLP-1 oral pills are also dispensed

[trusted individual to manage medications](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-pharmacy-caregiver-medicare-pillpack) on behalf of a loved one through their own Amazon account
