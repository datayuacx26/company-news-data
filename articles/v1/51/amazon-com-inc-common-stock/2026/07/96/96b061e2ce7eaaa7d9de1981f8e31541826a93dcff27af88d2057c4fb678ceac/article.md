# 'The Oprah Podcast' is coming to Prime Video on July 21, with new episodes twice weekly

---


## Key takeaways


- The Oprah Podcast


expands to twice weekly episodes on Tuesdays and Thursdays starting July 21.


- Episodes will be available to watch on Prime Video as part of a new partnership between Amazon and Oprah Winfrey’s Harpo Entertainment.


- New guests include Ryan Leak, Dr. Siddhartha Mukherjee, and


The Daily Stoic


author Ryan Holiday.

## What is 'The Oprah Podcast'?


Exploring timely themes including renewal, fulfillment, resilience, and connection,


The Oprah Podcast


aims to meet listeners on their path to live their best lives with select episodes taking place in front of a live audience.


Oprah talks to everyday people about topics that reflect the cultural conversation, including tech addiction, divorce after a long marriage, estranged families, coping with caregiving and grief, financial health, how to build community and improve relationships, our unbreakable bond with dogs, and more.


## What guests will be on this season of 'The Oprah Podcast'?


Special guests this season include best-selling authors Ryan Leak, Ryan Holiday, Allen Levi, and Virginia Evans, as well as acclaimed physician Dr. Siddhartha Mukherjee, with more to be announced in the coming weeks.


Notable previous guests include Misty Copeland, Hugh Jackman, Kate Hudson, Mel Robbins, Leanne Morgan, Jeremy Allen White, Adam Grant, Serena Williams, Ina Garten, Scott Galloway, Maria Shriver, and Esther Perel.

## Who makes 'The Oprah Podcast'?


The Oprah Podcast


is produced by Harpo Entertainment, executive produced by Oprah Winfrey and Tara Montgomery. Brian Piotrowicz and Brad Pavone are also co-executive producers.


In its first year, the podcast won a Gracie Award for Video Podcast Host, earned four Ambie nominations, and was nominated for a Webby Award.
