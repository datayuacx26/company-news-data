## Who is behind the new 'Thomas Crown Affair'?


Jordan is the director. The screenplay was written by Drew Pearce (


[The Fall Guy](https://www.primevideo.com/detail/0P0AX4TEJ0R0ZXSM44B7RDXUDG?pageTypeId=B0CVWTWHY9&qid=1785166329509&ref_=atv_sr_fle_c_sr62ef6f_1_1_1&pageTypeIdSource=ASIN&sr=1-1) ) and Jason Hall (


[American Sniper](https://www.amazon.com/American-Sniper-Clint-Eastwood/dp/B0D84F2D72) )


,


based on a story by Alan R. Trustman and Pearce, and based on a screenplay by Trustman.


Producers include Jordan and Elizabeth Raposo for Outlier Society and Charles Roven for Atlas Entertainment. Patrick McCormick and Toberoff Productions' Marc Toberoff also produce. Trustman serves as an executive producer.
