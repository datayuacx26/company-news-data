# How do I transfer stock, change the address on my shareholder account?


If your shares are held in street name (by your broker), you must notify the securities firm holding your stock. If you hold stock certificates in your name, address changes should be submitted in writing by U.S. Mail to our transfer agent: VStock Transfer, 18 Lafayette Place, Woodmere, NY 11598.
