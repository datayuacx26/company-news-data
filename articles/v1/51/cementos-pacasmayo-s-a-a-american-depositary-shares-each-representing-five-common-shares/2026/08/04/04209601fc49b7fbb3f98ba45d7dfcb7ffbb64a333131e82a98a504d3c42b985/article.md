# Uzbekistan’s cement production rises in first six months of 2026

**Uzbekistan:** Cement plants in Uzbekistan produced 10.1Mt of cement in the first six months of 2026, according to the National Statistics Committee. Compared with the same period of 2025, cement production increased by 0.2Mt (2%).
