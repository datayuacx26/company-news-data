# INSEE Vietnam completes expansion project at Can Tho cement plant

**Vietnam:** INSEE Vietnam has commissioned the expansion of its Can Tho cement plant in an inauguration ceremony on 31 July 2026. The project includes the commissioning of four new silos with a total capacity of 1000t, in addition to the four existing silos with a capacity of 600t, bringing the total capacity of the plant to 1600t. According to local press, the expansion will enable INSEE to respond more quickly and flexibly to market demands and ensure a stable supply for its customers throughout the Mekong Delta region.
