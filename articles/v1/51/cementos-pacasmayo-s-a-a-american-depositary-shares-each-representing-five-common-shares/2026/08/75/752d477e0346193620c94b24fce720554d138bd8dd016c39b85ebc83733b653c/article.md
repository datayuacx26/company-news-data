# Vietnamese cement exports increase in July 2026

**Vietnam:** Vietnam exported 3.18Mt of cement and clinker worth US$120m in July 2026, up by 5% in volume year-on-year, according to the latest figures from the government’s National Statistics Office. In the first seven months of 2026, Vietnam exported 22.5Mt of cement and clinker, up by 14% year-on-year.
