# West China Cement expects first-half profit to drop in 2026

**China:** West China Cement expects to record a net profit of US$49.9 – 55.4m in the first half of 2026, down by 50 – 55% year-on-year. The producer attributed the anticipated decline to a drop in its cement sales volumes and selling prices.
