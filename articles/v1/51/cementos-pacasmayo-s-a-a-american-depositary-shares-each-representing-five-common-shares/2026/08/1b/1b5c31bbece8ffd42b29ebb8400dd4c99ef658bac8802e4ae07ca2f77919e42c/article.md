# ES Processing launches kiln optimiser at Sibline Cement

**Lebanon:** ES Processing has launched its fully autonomous Kiln Process Optimiser (KPO) at Sibline Cement’s Kiln 2, a SECIL Group subsidiary. The equipment produced a 2% rise in kiln throughput, a 3% fall in specific electrical energy consumption, a 2.5% fall in thermal energy consumption and a 15% fall in essential climate variable-based variability.
