# JSW Cement’s revenues grow in first quarter of 2027 financial year

**India:** JSW Cement’s revenues increased by 22% year-on-year to US$3.66bn in the first quarter of the 2027 financial year. Earnings before interest, taxation, depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA) fell by 8% to US$577m. Nonetheless, the producer swung to a net profit of US$310m, from a loss of US$262m in the first quarter of the 2026 financial year. The former loss had been due to an exceptional charge of US$2.83bn, according to the producer.
