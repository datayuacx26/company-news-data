# Cement producer hires AVG Logistics for electric truck logistics in North East India

**India:** AVG Logistics has won a five-year contract worth US$1.89m/yr, extendable by three years, to deploy 30 electric heavy-goods vehicles for a cement producer's inbound and outbound transport of raw materials and cement. Capital Market News has reported that the vehicles will operate along ‘industrial corridors’ in North East India, carrying loads of 38 – 41t of materials. Additionally, AVG Logistics will build and operate electric vehicle charging stations to support the fleet.
