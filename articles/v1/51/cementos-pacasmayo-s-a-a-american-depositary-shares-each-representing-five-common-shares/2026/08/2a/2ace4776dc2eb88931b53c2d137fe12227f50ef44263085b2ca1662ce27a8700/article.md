# Moroccan cement sales decline in July 2026

**Morocco:** The cement market experienced a slight decline in the first seven months of 2026. Deliveries reached 8.22Mt at the end of July 2026, compared to 8.28Mt for the same period in 2025, representing a decrease of 0.75% year-on-year, according to data from the Moroccan Cement Industry Association (APC). In July 2026, cement deliveries reached 1.42Mt, compared to 1.4Mt in July 2025, an increase of 2%.
