# Mangalam Cement raises first-quarter sales in 2027 financial year

**India:** Mangalam Cement’s sales rose by 0.8% to US$47.8m in the first quarter of the 2027 financial year (begun 1 April 2026). Operating expenditure also rose, by 7% year-on-year to US$42.2m. The producer attributed this primarily to a rise in its inventory value, to US$2.58m. As a result, profit before tax fell by 38% year-on-year.
