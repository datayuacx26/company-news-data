# Thomas Zement to install new grinding mill at Karsdorf cement plant

**Germany:** Thomas Zement has broken ground on the construction of a new cement grinding mill at its 1.4Mt/yr Karsdorf cement plant in Saxony-Anhalt. The mill will use thermal energy derived from waste heat from the plant's clinker cooler. It is intended to reduce the Karsdorf plant’s CO₂ emissions and clinker and power consumption.
