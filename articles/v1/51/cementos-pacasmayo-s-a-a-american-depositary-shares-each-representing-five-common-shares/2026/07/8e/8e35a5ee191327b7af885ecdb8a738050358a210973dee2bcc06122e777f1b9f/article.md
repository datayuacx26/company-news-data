# UltraTech Cement records positive financial results for June 2026 quarter

**India:** UltraTech has reported its highest ever June quarter for sales volumes, revenues and net profit. It recorded a 12% year-on-year increase in cement sales volumes to 41.3Mt in the period from April to June 2026, according to the company. Net sales increased by 16% year-on-year to US$2.5bn from US$2.18bn in the previous corresponding period. Profit after tax was US$2.7bn, up by 17% year-on-year. Capacity utilisation reached 81% following the expansion of domestic cement capacity to 200Mt/yr. Its global cement capacity stood at 205Mt/yr at the end of June 2026.
