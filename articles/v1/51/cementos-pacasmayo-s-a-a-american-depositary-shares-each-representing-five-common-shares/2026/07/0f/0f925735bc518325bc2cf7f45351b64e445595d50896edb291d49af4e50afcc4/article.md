# Chui region produces 1Mt of cement in first five months of 2026

**Kyrgyzstan:** Cement plants in the Chui region produced 1Mt of cement during the first five months of 2026. This is 1.5 times more than the 0.68Mt produced in the same period of 2025, according to the National Statistical Committee. For the January-May 2026 period, cement plants operating in the Chui region accounted for 53% of Kyrgyzstan's total national cement output.
