# Hyesan cement plant increases production

**North Korea:** The Hyesan cement plant has increased its production. According to Korean News, the plant has taken measures to solve problems arising in the production process, developing the ‘initiative and ingenuity of its employees.’ Cement production has reportedly increased by 120% year-on-year.
