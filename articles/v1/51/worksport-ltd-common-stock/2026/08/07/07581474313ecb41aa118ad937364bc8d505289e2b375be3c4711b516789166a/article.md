## ⛽ The trouble with gas generators


Gas generators feel simple until you live with one. The fuel adds up. You are stuck buying, hauling, and storing gasoline that goes stale in a few months. The noise is real. Most portable units run at 65 to 75 decibels, about as loud as a vacuum cleaner that never turns off. That is a fast way to annoy every neighbor and campsite around you.


Then there is the safety problem. Gas generators give off carbon monoxide, so you can never run one indoors, in a garage, or in a closed tent. They need oil changes and tune ups. And they only make power while they are running and burning fuel.


For a lot of people, a generator is loud insurance they hope to never use. A solar and battery setup flips that. It works quietly in the background, refuels itself from the sun, and is safe to use right next to you.
