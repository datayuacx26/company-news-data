# Best Tonneau Covers for the Honda Ridgeline: Complete Worksport Buying Guide


## 🚛 Introduction


The Honda Ridgeline is not quite like any other truck on the market. It is unibody, all-wheel drive standard, and comes with a feature no other production pickup offers: a waterproof In-Bed Trunk built directly into the bed floor. These quirks make the Ridgeline a uniquely capable lifestyle vehicle — and they also make tonneau cover shopping a slightly different experience than it is for any other truck in this guide.


This guide covers every Worksport tonneau cover available for the 2017–2026 Honda Ridgeline, with direct attention to the question every Ridgeline owner asks first: "Will this cover block my In-Bed Trunk?"


---


## 🛻 The Honda Ridgeline: What Makes It Different


The second-generation Ridgeline (2017–present) is built on Honda's unibody platform shared with the Pilot and Passport. Unlike conventional body-on-frame trucks — the F-150, Silverado, Tacoma, and every other truck in this series — the Ridgeline does not have a separate frame. This gives it a car-like ride, standard AWD, and a more maneuverable footprint, at the cost of towing capacity and off-road articulation relative to body-on-frame rivals.


What the Ridgeline uniquely gains is the In-Bed Trunk (IBT) — a 7.3 cubic foot lockable, waterproof storage compartment accessed by lifting a section of the bed floor. The IBT is large enough for a spare tire, a full set of grocery bags, or gear you want completely concealed and protected from the elements. It is opened from within the bed, meaning the tailgate and bed floor surface above it must remain accessible.

## 📏 One Bed Size — No Configuration Choices


The Ridgeline's bed measures approximately 5'4" (64") in physical length and is fixed across all trim levels and cab configurations — there is only one Ridgeline body style. In the Worksport vehicle selector, this bed is classified as a 5'0" bed. This is the simplest fitment situation in the entire Worksport buying guide series: you only need to confirm your model year, not your bed size or cab type.

## ✅ The Big Question: Do Worksport Covers Block the In-Bed Trunk?


This is the first question every Ridgeline owner asks, and the answer across Worksport's lineup is generally positive. Because Worksport's covers clamp to the bed rail and fold toward the cab rather than using full-length sliding rails that run along the bed floor edge, they are designed not to interfere with the IBT lid operation.


✅ IBT COMPATIBILITY — CONFIRMED FOR[AL3](https://worksport.com/products/al3) AND[AL4](https://worksport.com/products/al4)


Honda Ridgeline Owners Club forum users confirm that both the[AL3](https://worksport.com/products/al3) Pro and[AL4](https://worksport.com/products/al4) do not interfere with opening the In-Bed Trunk lid. The[AL3](https://worksport.com/products/al3) 's rail system does not have a latch or clamp point between the second and third tri-fold sections that would block the IBT area. The[AL4](https://worksport.com/products/al4) , which opens as a single panel flipping toward the cab, clears the IBT lid when folded. Owners can access the IBT normally with either cover installed.


For the[SC3](https://worksport.com/products/sc3) and[SC4](https://worksport.com/products/sc4) , IBT compatibility has not been independently confirmed via forum reports in current research — verify these via the live selector or Worksport support before publishing this claim for those covers.


It is worth noting that the IBT lid opens upward from the bed floor, not from the tailgate end. Covers that clamp flush to the rail and fold toward the cab — like all Worksport designs — naturally leave the bed floor area unobstructed from underneath, which is why IBT compatibility is achievable across the lineup. A cover that used a crossbar system running the full bed width at regular intervals could potentially block the IBT lid; Worksport's panel designs avoid this.

## 🔧 Ridgeline-Specific Installation Tips


The Ridgeline's bed rail profile and tie-down system differ from conventional body-on-frame trucks. A few things to know before you order:


**Tie-down cleats and the AL4:** The[AL4](https://worksport.com/products/al4) on the Ridgeline mounts using the existing factory tie-down cleat positions rather than traditional foam rail clamps. You will need a Torx T-50 socket (not a standard hex or Phillips) to remove and reinstall the cleats. Plan for this before starting the install.


**Packaging labels:** As noted in the[AL3](https://worksport.com/products/al3) section, Worksport's packaging may reflect an older year range. If your Ridgeline is a 2023 or newer, call Worksport directly at 1-888-554-8789 or confirm via the vehicle selector before assuming the cover will not fit. Platform continuity from 2017 through 2026 means fit is very likely, but the packaging may not say so.


**IBT access:** All Worksport covers are installed on the top rail, not across the bed floor. The IBT lid opens vertically from beneath the cover's mounting plane, which is why the two are compatible. If you are using a non-Worksport cover with crossbar systems that span the full bed width at frequent intervals, measure carefully before assuming IBT access is maintained.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions


### Can I still open the In-Bed Trunk with a Worksport cover installed?


Yes — at least for the[AL3](https://worksport.com/products/al3) and[AL4](https://worksport.com/products/al4) , where forum confirmation exists. Both covers mount to the top rail and fold away from the bed floor, leaving the IBT lid accessible from inside the bed. The[SC3](https://worksport.com/products/sc3) and[SC4](https://worksport.com/products/sc4) use the same rail-mount architecture and are expected to be compatible, but independent confirmation should be gathered before publishing this claim for those covers.


### The Ridgeline is not a 'real truck' — does it even need a tonneau cover?


This is a common debate in Ridgeline circles. The Ridgeline's 5'4" bed may be shorter than most full-size trucks, but it is used actively by owners who haul gear, protect cargo from weather, and secure valuables. A tonneau cover is useful for the same reasons on a Ridgeline as it is on a Tacoma or Ranger — if anything, the In-Bed Trunk makes it more important to keep the rest of the bed weather-tight so the whole cargo system works together.


### Does Worksport make covers for the first-generation Ridgeline (2006–2014)?


No confirmed Worksport fitment was found for the first-generation Ridgeline (2006–2014), which used a different bed profile and the original IBT design. All confirmed fitment in this guide is for the second-generation Ridgeline (2017–present). If you own a gen 1 Ridgeline, contact Worksport directly to ask whether any cover in their catalog is compatible.


### Will the AL3 or AL4 fit my 2025 or 2026 Ridgeline even though the box shows older years?


Yes — both the[AL3](https://worksport.com/products/al3) and[AL4](https://worksport.com/products/al4) are confirmed for 2017–2026 via the Worksport live vehicle selector, so any current-production Ridgeline is in the covered range. Some older packaging labels may show a narrower year range; this reflects outdated print runs rather than actual fitment limits. The[SC3](https://worksport.com/products/sc3) is confirmed through 2025. Always confirm your exact year via the live selector at worksport.com if in doubt.
