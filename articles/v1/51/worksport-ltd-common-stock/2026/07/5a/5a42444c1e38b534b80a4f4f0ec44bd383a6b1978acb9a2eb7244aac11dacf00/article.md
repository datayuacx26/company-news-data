# Best Tonneau Cover for Ford Ranger & Ford Maverick (2026 Buying Guide)


The Ford Ranger and Ford Maverick represent two very different takes on the compact truck. The Ranger is a body-on-frame mid-sizer built for capability and towing. The Maverick is a unibody, hybrid-first compact that made truck ownership accessible to a whole new group of buyers. Different vehicles, different buyers — but both benefit from the same thing: a well-built tonneau cover that protects cargo, improves fuel efficiency, and looks like it belongs on the truck.


This guide covers the right Worksport cover for each. The Ranger and Maverick each come in a single bed size, which simplifies the buying decision — but you still need to confirm your year and which cover format works best for how you use your truck. We cover that here.


---


## 🚙 Ranger vs. Maverick: two trucks, one guide


Ford Ranger (2019–2026) — the Ranger returned to the US market in 2019 after a nearly decade-long absence. It’s a proper body-on-frame truck with real towing capacity, available 4WD, and a 5’0” truck bed. The 2024 model year brought a significant exterior refresh and an updated interior. If you need a truck that genuinely hauls and tows but don’t need a full-size footprint, the Ranger is the right tool.


Ford Maverick (2022–2026) — Ford’s entry-level compact truck is a unibody design that starts as a standard hybrid — one of the only trucks in America to do so. The Maverick targets urban buyers who want a truck’s practicality without the size or cost of a full-size or traditional mid-sizer. Its 4’5” bed is the smallest in this guide, but that just means the cover is purpose-sized for it.


The key thing both trucks have in common: each comes in a single bed size. There’s no short bed vs. standard bed choice to make. The Ranger has one bed (5’0”) and the Maverick has one bed (4’5”). That means once you know your truck, you just need to pick the right cover style.


---


## 🛡️ Hard vs. soft tonneau covers for the Ranger and Maverick


Hard covers are built for security and long-term durability. Thick aluminum panels don’t flex or rattle, and they make it significantly harder for anyone to get into your bed without permission. For Ranger owners using their truck as a work vehicle — or Maverick owners who park on the street — a hard cover earns its price quickly.


Soft covers offer a lighter, lower-cost entry point. They fold out of the way quickly when you need full bed access and are a solid fit for owners who don’t need maximum security but want basic weather protection and a cleaner look.


Both the Ranger and Maverick are popular as daily drivers, which makes the flip-up access format — available in both the aluminum[AL4](https://worksport.com/products/al4) and soft vinyl[SC4](https://worksport.com/products/sc4) — particularly useful. Full bed access without removing the cover is the right default for a truck you use every day.

## ❓ Frequently asked questions


### 🚛 Does the Ford Ranger come in different bed sizes?


No. The current generation Ford Ranger (2019–2026) comes with a single 5’0” bed size. There is no long bed or short bed choice to make — every Ranger in this generation uses the same bed, which simplifies the cover selection process.


### 🚙 Does the Ford Maverick come in different bed sizes?


No. The Ford Maverick has a single 4’5” bed across all trims and model years. Worksport covers for the Maverick are sized specifically for this bed and use different part numbers than Ranger covers.


### 🔁 Can I use the same Worksport cover on both a Ranger and a Maverick?


No. The Ranger (5’0”) and Maverick (4’5”) have different bed lengths and require different covers. While the same model names appear in both lineups, each truck uses a dedicated part number sized for its specific bed. Do not interchange them.


### 📦 Does the[AL4](https://worksport.com/products/al4) work with a bed liner in my Ranger or Maverick?


Yes. Worksport hard covers mount to the bed rail, not the bed floor. The[AL4](https://worksport.com/products/al4) and[AL3](https://worksport.com/products/al3) are compatible with drop-in mats and spray-in liners on both the Ranger and Maverick.


### ☀️ Does Worksport make a solar tonneau cover for the Ranger or Maverick?


Yes. Worksport’s[SOLIS](https://worksport.com/products/solis) solar tonneau cover is available for both the Ford Ranger and Ford Maverick. The Maverick’s 4’5” bed qualifies for the Small tier (up to 300W / $1,999). See the[SOLIS](https://worksport.com/products/solis) buying guide for full specs and use-case details.


### 🛡️ Is the Ford Maverick too small for a hard cover?


Not at all. Worksport has engineered the[AL4](https://worksport.com/products/al4) and[AL3](https://worksport.com/products/al3) specifically for the Maverick’s 4’5” bed.
