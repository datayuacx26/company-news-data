# Best Tonneau Cover for the Rivian R1T (2026 Buying Guide)


The Rivian R1T does not follow the rulebook. It is electric, it weighs over 7,000 pounds, and it can tow five tons on charge alone. Its buyers are a different kind of truck owner — tech-forward, adventure-minded, and willing to spend on equipment that matches the truck’s quality. But behind all that technology sits a real truck bed. A 4’6” load floor that gets rained on, loaded with gear, and left in parking lots just like any other truck on this list.


That bed needs a cover. This guide covers every Worksport tonneau available for the Rivian R1T, why the R1T lineup runs hard covers only, and exactly which one fits the way you actually use the truck.

## ⚡ Why the Rivian R1T deserves its own buying guide


Most tonneau cover guides treat all trucks the same. The Rivian R1T earns its own entry because it differs from every other truck in this series in three ways that actually affect how you choose a cover.


**Electric powertrain.** No exhaust, no fuel, no traditional engine bay. The R1T’s front trunk replaces the engine compartment with an additional 11 cubic feet of storage. This does not affect tonneau fitment directly, but it changes how the truck is used — and therefore what kind of bed cover makes sense. R1T owners are not running to job sites to pick up lumber. They are packing technical gear, camp setups, and adventure equipment that needs to stay dry, secure, and organized.


**The Gear Tunnel.** A lockable, waterproof pass-through storage compartment running beneath the bed between the rear wheels — one of the R1T’s most distinctive features. It is accessed from the lower rear corners of the truck via two outward-opening doors, not from the bed surface or tailgate. This means any tonneau cover mounted to the bed rails has zero interference with Gear Tunnel access. The[AL4](https://worksport.com/products/al4) or[AL3](https://worksport.com/products/al3) can be fully closed and locked while the tunnel doors open and close normally.


**One bed. No variants.** Unlike the F-150 or Tundra, which ship in multiple bed lengths, every R1T comes with the same 4’6” bed. No cab configuration changes your bed length. Fitment for the R1T is the simplest in this entire guide: confirm your model year, and you’re done.

## 📏 Rivian R1T bed size: one configuration, no guesswork


The R1T bed measures 4’6” in length across all model years and trims. There is no long bed option and no short bed variant. If you own an R1T, you have a 4’6” bed, and both the[AL4](https://worksport.com/products/al4) and[AL3](https://worksport.com/products/al3) are sized for it.


The Gear Tunnel housing sits in the lower portion of the bed but does not reduce the surface area available to a tonneau cover. The cover mounts to the bed rails above and does not interact with the tunnel structure below.

## ❓ Rivian R1T tonneau cover FAQ


### Will a tonneau cover block access to the Gear Tunnel?


No. The Gear Tunnel is accessed from the lower rear corners of the truck via outward-opening doors that sit below the bed rail line. Worksport covers mount to the top of the bed rails and do not interact with the tunnel doors in any position — open, mid-fold, or fully closed. You can have the[AL4](https://worksport.com/products/al4) or[AL3](https://worksport.com/products/al3) locked on the truck and still open the Gear Tunnel normally.


### Does the R1T come in more than one bed size?


No. Every Rivian R1T ships with the same 4’6” bed regardless of trim level or configuration. There are no short bed or long bed variants. Fitment on the R1T is one of the simplest in the Worksport lineup.


### Can I use a tonneau cover and still access the front trunk (frunk)?


Yes. The frunk is at the front of the vehicle and is completely independent of the bed cover. You can open and close the frunk with the tonneau in any position.


### Will a closed tonneau cover improve my R1T’s range?


Potentially, yes. An open truck bed creates aerodynamic drag that increases energy consumption at highway speeds. A closed hard cover reduces that drag and can modestly extend range on longer drives. The improvement varies by speed and conditions, but the physics are the same for the R1T as for any other truck on this list — a covered bed is more efficient than an open one.


### Are Worksport covers compatible with the R1T’s Gear Guard security system?


Yes. The[AL4](https://worksport.com/products/al4) and[AL3](https://worksport.com/products/al3) are physical cargo security layers that work alongside — not instead of — the R1T’s built-in Gear Guard camera system. The cover locks the bed from the tailgate using cable-operated Quick Latches; Gear Guard monitors the truck’s exterior with cameras. Both run simultaneously and independently.


### Is the SOLIS solar cover also available for the R1T?


Yes. The Worksport[SOLIS](https://worksport.com/products/solis) solar tonneau cover is confirmed for the R1T: 2021–2026, 4’6” bed, Small tier (up to 300W output). Paired with the[COR](https://worksport.com/products/cor) modular battery generator,[SOLIS](https://worksport.com/products/solis) turns the R1T into a zero-emission power station for camp, jobsite, or off-grid use — see the dedicated[SOLIS](https://worksport.com/products/solis) buying guide for the full breakdown.
