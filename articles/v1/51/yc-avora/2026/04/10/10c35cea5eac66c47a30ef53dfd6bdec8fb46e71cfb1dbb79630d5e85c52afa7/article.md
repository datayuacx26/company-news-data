# Why we're building Avora

Three years ago, my co-founder Justin and I moved to San Francisco to work in voice AI. I created the first version of[Wispr Flow](https://wisprflow.ai/) , and Justin worked in healthcare, automating critical RCM workflows at Infinitus.


There's a magic with voice. At Wispr, we saw how voice changed the way people interact with computers. Engineers designed better specs. Students posed better questions. It became obvious that the best way to use AI is with your voice.


Consumers have widely adopted voice, but the people who need it most have not. Half of the healthcare workers in America report feeling burnt out. Our typical dental practice creates **20,000 words** worth of documentation per week.


That time spent on documentation is time not spent with patients. Patients who can't spend time with their providers don't trust them — it's part of the reason **13% of adults over 65 have no natural teeth left** .


We're building Avora to make dental healthcare better. Our clinicians spend more time with patients and get back to their families faster. We do that with exceptional voice AI products.


Today, dental teams across the country use Avora to take notes, coach providers, and grow case acceptance —[read what they're saying](https://getavora.ai/testimonials) .
