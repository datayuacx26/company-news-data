# Best AI dental charting software in 2026

Before choosing a dental AI scribe, ask yourself: **will this genuinely fit into the way my practice currently documents?**


It seems straightforward, yet this is where many AI documentation tools fall short.


Dental documentation isn't one-size-fits-all. Hygiene appointments differ greatly from scaling and root planing (SRP). A limited exam isn't documented like a new patient exam, and restorative procedures have entirely different needs compared to crown seats, extractions, consultations, or treatment coordinator interactions. Each scenario requires unique note structures, terminology, speakers, and precise integration points within your practice management system (PMS).


This complexity means the best dental AI scribes do far more than simply "record and summarize." They seamlessly transform real conversations into structured documentation using your practice's existing templates. These tools effectively handle diverse workflows, support perio charting, offer customizable prompts, clickable choices, and respect the nuanced preferences your providers have developed over time.


Equally important, the right tool significantly reduces clicks and workflow friction.


Even if an AI produces a decent summary, the value diminishes quickly if your team must manually copy, paste, reformat, select templates, or re-enter data. The success of adopting an AI scribe hinges on subtle yet crucial factors: how intuitive is the interface? Does it automatically detect appointment types and apply the right templates? Does it seamlessly integrate into multiple PMS fields? And importantly, can every member of your team easily and consistently use it — not just your most tech-savvy staff?


This review takes a practical approach, evaluating leading dental AI scribes based on these critical, real-world questions:


- Does it integrate smoothly into daily dental workflows?
- Does it utilize your existing documentation templates?
- Is it effective across diverse appointment types?
- Can it facilitate live, mid-appointment handoffs?
- Can it accurately write back information to the correct PMS locations?
- Is it easily adoptable across your entire team?


Features, integrations, and pricing noted here reflect information publicly available as of April 2026. Since the market evolves rapidly, we recommend directly verifying details with each vendor and testing the software thoroughly in your practice.


## Key considerations for selecting a dental AI scribe


### 1. PMS integration


Seamless PMS integration is crucial. If your AI scribe requires frequent manual adjustments, formatting, or copy-pasting, it undermines productivity. Look for a tool capable of automatically inserting notes directly into multiple PMS areas, such as progress notes, perio charts, patient communications, and treatment records.


### 2. Flexibility with existing templates


Each practice — and sometimes each provider within a practice — has unique documentation styles. The ideal AI scribe respects these differences, enhancing your current templates rather than replacing them. The best platforms enable customizable prompts, clickable selections, and provider-specific preferences.


### 3. Comprehensive appointment support


Real-world dental schedules are varied and unpredictable. Your AI scribe should reliably handle everything from basic exams and hygiene appointments to more complex procedures like restorative treatments, ortho visits, and emergency care. Tools limited to basic appointment types often struggle to achieve consistent adoption.


### 4. Document accuracy and traceability


Accuracy is essential, but equally valuable is the transparency of how information was gathered. Quality AI scribes provide clear citations or timestamps linking directly back to recorded conversations, facilitating quick reviews and audits while enhancing provider confidence.


### 5. Real-time handoff capability


Dental visits frequently involve multiple staff members interacting with patients sequentially. A good AI scribe goes beyond creating final notes — it can summarize preliminary interactions or patient concerns in real time, ensuring smoother handoffs and preventing repetitive patient discussions.


### 6. Ease of adoption and scalability


Ultimately, the most effective AI scribe is one your entire team willingly and regularly uses. For solo practices, simplicity is paramount. For group practices or DSOs, consistent template control, training support, adoption tracking, and scalability are vital.


Of course, HIPAA compliance, secure data management, business associate agreements (BAAs), and robust encryption protocols should be considered standard across all platforms.

## Frequently asked questions


### How much does an AI dental scribe cost?


Pricing typically lands between $99 and $399 per provider per month, with discounts at multi-provider and DSO scale. Some vendors price per location instead of per provider; some bundle scribe with imaging or analytics. Always confirm whether voice perio charting, custom templates, and PMS write-back are included or are paid add-ons.


### How accurate are AI-generated dental notes?


Accuracy depends on the model, the dental specificity of the training data, the chart-note template used, and microphone setup. The most reliable accuracy signal is auditability: vendors that link every line of the generated note back to a timestamped moment in the recording let you spot-check accuracy in seconds rather than reading the whole note line by line.


### Do I have to ask patients for consent to record?


Recording-consent laws vary by state and by recording context, so verify current requirements with counsel before rollout. Best practice is to disclose recording in your intake forms, post a brief notice in operatories, and use a vendor that documents recording retention and consent workflows.


### What's the difference between an AI dental scribe and a general medical scribe?


Dental-specific scribes are trained on dental terminology — tooth numbers, surfaces, CDT codes, perio measurements — and write back into dental PMS systems (Dentrix, Open Dental, Eaglesoft, Denticon, Ascend). General medical scribes typically don't recognize dental anatomy, don't follow dental chart-note formats, and don't integrate with dental PMS.


### Will hygienists actually adopt voice-driven workflows?


The biggest predictor of hygienist adoption is whether the system understands natural language vs. requiring command-style speech. Legacy voice-perio systems (e.g., older Dentrix Voice Perio, Florida Probe voice modules) require keyword prefixes that disrupt the rhythm of the exam. Modern AI voice perio — see our[voice perio charting guide](https://getavora.ai/voice-perio-charting) — captures measurements in the way hygienists already speak.


### Which dental PMS systems are best for AI integration?


Dentrix, Open Dental, and Eaglesoft have the deepest write-back support across vendors because they expose stable APIs or local desktop integrations. Cloud PMS systems (Denticon, Dentrix Ascend, Curve) vary more widely — coverage is improving but is not yet universal. If you're on a less-common cloud PMS, ask vendors to demonstrate write-back live, not just claim support.
