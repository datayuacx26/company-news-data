And, like the customers we serve, we’re proud to call Columbus home.

## Supporting local families in need with the Healthy New Albany Food Pantry


The cost of living has risen over the last several years and many families have felt that pressure most clearly at the grocery store. As a result, many households are having trouble making ends meet. And approximately one in nine residents in Franklin County — nearly 150,000 people — now


[struggle with food insecurity](https://local-matters.org/columbus-food-rescue/) .


"Food insecurity affects more families than many people realize, including households that may not qualify for assistance but are still stretched thin. When children and families have reliable access to food, it supports health, stability and the ability to thrive,” Hapney says.


Northwest supports the Healthy New Albany Food Pantry, which provides local families with grocery staples and personal care items including fresh produce, dairy, meats, bread and shelf-stable foods.


“The Healthy New Albany Food Pantry’s mission aligns with our values of service and community partnership. We’re honored to contribute to their work and look forward to deepening our relationship with the New Albany community,” said TK Creal, Chief Credit


o


fficer.


To help, the bank donated funds to cover the cost of a full month’s supply of fresh produce for pantry clients. Corporate headquarters employees added to the impact by donating more than 300 food and household items and volunteering at the pantry — assisting with unloading and organizing a delivery from the Mid-Ohio Food Collective.


-


[Learn more about our work with the Healthy New Albany Food Pantry.](https://www.northwest.bank/link/0b9127725744449c900c5c0acf0e3270.aspx)

## Supporting Westerville families during the holidays


While school cafeterias close over the winter break, hunger doesn’t take a holiday. And families teetering on the brink of food insecurity can be pushed over the edge with the loss of school-funded nutrition programs.


That’s why we partnered with Westerville Area Resource Ministry (WARM) to help families in the area access meals over the holidays.


Throughout the school year, WARM distributes weekend food bags to students who qualify for free or reduced-cost lunches, ensuring they have access to nutritious meals when school is not in session. Our team stepped in to help continue support over the extended holiday break, funding and preparing 1,000


bags for families in need.


“I am deeply grateful for the partnership between WARM and Northwest Bank,” said Chad Maxeiner, WARM e xecutive director


. “Their generosity will have a wonderful impact on our community and our work at WARM, and I eagerly anticipate the positive changes ahead."


-


[Learn more about our partnership with WARM — and how you can get involved](https://www.northwest.bank/link/d02dc598000d4f2a978c59b3605f7ab9.aspx)

## Building stronger communities with Habitat for Humanity, one home at a time


One of our missions at Northwest is to help families settle into homes they love. And supporting Habitat for Humanity is a central pillar of our philanthropy in the four states we serve. Since 2021, employees across our footprint have volunteered over 1,500 hours for Habitat for Humanity. We’ve also donated more than $1 million to the nonprofit and manage more than 500 loans for Habitat homeowners.


Our teams across the Columbus region are eager to carry on that tradition


. We recently had the opportunity


to join


Habitat for Humanity’s


[Playhouse Project](https://www.habitatmidohio.org/what-we-do/the-playhouse-project.html) ,


which builds outdoor play spaces for young families.


"Participating in Habitat for Humanity’s Playhouse Build gave me a meaningful opportunity to give back to the communities we serve," said Tarika Lewis, Regional Vice President of Community Lending at Northwest. "It was rewarding to see how our time and effort could create something that brings joy, comfort and a sense of belonging to a family. While a playhouse may seem like a small addition, it creates a place for children to play, imagine and grow, reinforcing the importance of investing in our communities and supporting families as they build brighter futures."


-


[Learn more about our work with Habitat for Humanity](https://www.northwest.bank/link/68de7ac0225946cea6ff510e2c584335.aspx)
