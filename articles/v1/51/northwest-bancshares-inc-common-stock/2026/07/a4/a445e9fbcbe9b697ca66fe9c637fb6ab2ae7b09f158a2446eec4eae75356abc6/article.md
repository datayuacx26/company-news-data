**Key takeaways:**

You know all about the benefits of shopping locally. But did you know that


*banking*


locally offers many of the same advantages?

##


## How is a local bank different from a national bank?


As the name suggests, national banks have a


nationwide


, or even an international, footprint. That can offer some advantages: For example, you’re likely to find a bank ATM from virtually anywhere in the country.


-


**Pro tip:** We're


part of the


[AllPoint ATM network](https://www.allpointnetwork.com/) , a network of more than 55,000 ATMs located around the world. That means you can find an ATM to withdraw money without surcharges, even if you’re far from home. ****


But a national presence can also create a “big bank” atmosphere. Because national banks serve customers across many markets, the experience can feel less connected to the needs of a specific community. With such a broad footprint, it can be harder to shape the experience around what matters most in a specific market.


Local banks take a different approach. They typically operate locations within specific areas — anywhere from a handful of states to a network of local communities — which means they’re deeply


rooted


in the communities they serve.


“Local banking is all about creating a


welcoming


, people-first atmosphere and serving as a cornerstone of the neighborhood,” says Hapney. “That kind of relationship shows up in small but important ways, from how you’re


greeted


when you walk in to how conversations and recommendations are handled.”

**New Albany, Gahanna, Westerville** and **Columbus** . We're excited to offer tailored support to families across the


area.

## How does having a local banker actually impact your banking experience?


Beyond providing a friendly community atmosphere, keeping your money close to home can lead to a better banking experience, too. Here’s why:


### Products, services and solutions tailored to you


One benefit of local banking is working with a bank that can better reflect the needs of the people and businesses in the community it serves.


“As local bankers, we can have more relevant conversations because we understand the market people are living and working in," says Hapney. "That matters whether someone is buying a home, opening an account or running a business here in Columbus.”


-


**Get generation-specific insights:** Learn about


[smart money habits for Gen Z](https://www.northwest.bank/link/fb5639f8e3684fd18591a9dbcdbaff1e.aspx) , as well as


[financial wellness for Millennials.](https://www.northwest.bank/link/593cbcc5860648d1a4d22de20b6802f7.aspx)

### Convenient service that fits your daily life


Beyond helping you find solutions personalized to your needs, a **local bank** makes it convenient for you to manage your financial health. One clear benefit: Your local bank is right in your neighborhood. So it’s easy to stop in when you're shopping


nearby


or coming home from a family adventure at the Blendon Woods Metro Park.


-


We're opening financial centers in New Albany, Gahanna, Westerville and


North High Street


.


[Click here to learn more about our expansion across the Columbus area.](https://www.northwest.bank/link/2c75437060bb4648b8fcce9626c12b5b.aspx)


Local banks may also offer faster service, particularly when it comes to loans, mortgages and other forms of credit. “At a local bank, the person processing your application might be just down the hall, which makes it easier for your banker to keep you in the loop throughout the process,” says Hapney. “At a national bank, that person could be hours away, which can make the application process feel less transparent — and, for some, much more intimidating.”

###


### A bank that just


*gets it*


Putting down deep roots in the community offers another significant advantage: The ability to hit the ground running when you come in for financial advice.


“We’re on top of what’s happening in the


community and trends impacting business owners and households,” says Hapney. “And not just because we’re helping other local customers going through similar experiences. We have a personal stake in the community because we live here too. We’re seeing the same challenges as you, and we’re looking for opportunities to help.”
