# Nephros Introduces New Convenience Kits

# Simplifying Filter Installations


Nephros recognizes and has responded to the need for expanded resources to accommodate a broader range of plumbing setups, environmental requirements, and applications. We are proud to introduce three new convenience kits complementing the[DSU-H](https://www.nephros.com/products/infection-control-filtration/in-line/dsu-h-dual-stage-filter/) and[SSU-H](https://www.nephros.com/products/infection-control-filtration/in-line/ssu-h-single-stage-filter/) in-line infection control filter installations.


> We are pleased to introduce these new kits to the market” said Robert Banks, President & CEO of Nephros. “Not only will these kits reduce the need for additional labor expenditures, but they also provide an opportunity for Nephros filters to be used in applications or areas that were once deemed inaccessible.”


## Install Kit – In-Line, Push-Fit (70-0290J)


The[push-fit kit](https://www.nephros.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/KIT_60-8049_REV00_In-Line_Push-Fit-Kit_012925.pdf) is the first kit to contain 3/8 plastic push-fit tubing as an alternative to braided flex-hose lines. Nephros in-line infection control filters can easily be installed on equipment with existing PEX, John Guest, or similar tubing without the need for a full water line replacement.


## Drill-Free, C-Clip Anchor Kit (70-0345)


This kit allows for Nephros filters to be mounted on tiled, metal, or other smooth, non-painted surfaces without drilling. The[adhesive tabs](https://www.nephros.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/KIT_60-8050_REV00_Drill-Free-Anchor-Kit_012925.pdf) are mounted on the surface, and the c-clips are then secured in place with a screwdriver. The risk of damaging walls, existing hardware, or plumbing lines is removed.


## Install Kit – Bottle Filler / Fountain (70-0346)


[Bottle fillers and drinking water fountains](https://www.nephros.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/KIT_60-8048_REV00_Bottle-Filler_-Fountain_012925.pdf) are applications that are often overlooked but are susceptible to the same biological risks commonly found at sinks, showers, and ice machines. This convenience kit comes equipped with all the components necessary to support in-line filter installations, allowing for the protection of end users.


To learn more about if any of these kits are the right fit for your facility, reach out to us atcustomerservice@nephros.com .
