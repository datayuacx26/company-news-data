# Nephros Expands HydraGuard Product Line

The Nephros[HydraGuard 20″ UltraFilter](https://www.nephros.com/products/infection-control-filtration/in-line/hydraguard-ultrafilter/) (70-0121) is an extension of the HydraGuard 10” UltraFilter and the newest addition to our infection control product line. The 20” cartridge provides double the surface area making it ideal for installation on high-volume equipment in environments such as laboratories, manufacturing, and sterile processing. In addition to the high-capacity filtration, it offers the same microbiological retention through size exclusion as all of Nephros’ FDA-cleared in-line filters.


> The 20″ model of our HydraGuard 222 O-ring cartridge allows us to support new markets and industries where high-volume infection control and superior endotoxin retention capabilities are needed most.
> – **Brianne McGuire, Nephros’ Director of Business Development**


HydraGuard filters provide customers with a continuous filter life of up to 180 days (6 months). The 0.005-micron pore size filter media allows for effective and consistent protection against endotoxins, bacteria, and viruses and meets or exceeds the standards for water defined by ANSI/AAMI ST108, ASHRAE 514, and VHA Directive 1061.


As a complement to the HydraGuard 20″ UltraFilter, Nephros offers a 20″ Housing Kit (70-0127), to accommodate the unique reverse flow of the filter.
