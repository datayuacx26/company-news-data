# Knight-Swift Transportation Named a 2026 HDT Top Green Fleet!

Heavy Duty Trucking (HDT) has once again named Knight-Swift Transportation a Top Green Fleet for 2026, recognizing the company’s continued leadership in[sustainable](https://investor.knight-swift.com/Sustainability/default.aspx) trucking.


This year marks Knight-Swift’s fifth consecutive year – and ninth overall – receiving this prestigious honor, reinforcing its long-standing commitment to environmental responsibility and operational excellence.


## Recognizing Leadership in Sustainable Trucking


HDT’s Top Green Fleets program spotlights fleets that are leading the industry in sustainability. In 2026, 20 fleets were selected based on a range of criteria, including:


- Use of alternative fuels and powertrains
- Fuel economy
- Freight efficiency
- Facility improvements
- Overall sustainability goals


Knight-Swift continues to distinguish itself by implementing **practical, scalable strategies** that reduce emissions while maintaining strong efficiency and service.


## A Continued Commitment to Sustainability


[Knight-Swift’s](https://www.knighttrans.com/corporate-responsibility/) ongoing inclusion on the Top Green Fleets list reflects its focused approach to innovation and sustainability.


Across its operations, the company is actively working onto minimize environmental impact while driving performance improvements.


Through strategic investments, operational efficiencies, and forward-thinking initiatives, Knight-Swift remains committed to shaping for a more sustainable future of the trucking industry.


Our Swift trucks are pictured at the **Jurupa Charging Stations** , actively charging as part of our commitment to cleaner, more efficient transportation.


## From Heavy Duty Trucking: How Knight-Swift Is Delivering Real-World Results


In its 2026 feature,[Heavy Duty Trucking,](https://www.truckinginfo.com/articles/top-green-fleets-2026-how-fleets-are-reducing-emissions-in-the-real-world?utm_source=Newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=20260518_NL-HDT-HEADLINENEWS_BBM260515013&utm_content=01&omeid=4468C2065134G5Y&oly_enc_id=4468C2065134G5Y) highlighted Knight-Swift’s data-driven approach to emissions reduction across one of the largest fleets in the industry, with approximately 25,000 heavy-duty trucks.


Since 2019, Knight-Swift has achieved a 9% reduction in CO₂ emissions per mile, driven by measurable improvements in fuel economy, network efficiency, and equipment technology.


A key differentiator is our in-house Environmental Management System, which evaluates the real-world performance of technologies such as renewable diesel and battery-electric trucks – ensuring investment decisions are guided by operational data.


HDT also noted Knight-Swift’s continued progress in fleet electrification, including the development of charging infrastructure and a microgrid in Southern California to support electric trucks and yard operations. At the same time, the company is expanding its use of renewable diesel and biodiesel wherever feasible.


Beyond fleet operations, Knight-Swift is making strides across its facilities through solar energy generation, energy efficiency initiatives, and recycling programs.


Looking ahead, we continue to test and scale a range of solutions as it works toward its goal of reducing CO₂ emissions per mile by 50% by 2035.
