# Knight Named Home Depot’s 2025 Carrier of the Year

Knight Transportation is proud to announce that we have been named Home Depot’s 2025 Carrier of the Year. This recognition reflects our commitment to service, reliability, and strong partnership.


This award is a testament to the power of teamwork across our organization. From planning and coordination to execution on the road, every team member plays a role in delivering the level of service our customers expect.


We want to give credit where credit is due. This achievement would not have been possible without the dedication and hard work of our people.


The award was presented at the **[Executive Carrier Council](https://corporate.homedepot.com/page/home-depot-leadership-0) (ECC) event** in where **Shelley Billions, Regional Sales Manager** , proudly accepted the honor on behalf of Knight.


## Team Behind the Success


We’d like to extend a special thank you to those who directly support our[Home Depot](https://corporate.homedepot.com/page/about-us) partnership:


- **Account Managers:** Alex Dezember & Alexis Gunn
- **Sales Representative:** Shelley Billons
- **Our Operations Team:** Every driver, driver manager, and planner who helped transport Home Depot freight with on-time performance and exceptional service


Your commitment and consistency continue to set the standard for excellence.


## Thank You Home Depot


Receiving Home Depot’s Carrier of the Year award is incredibly meaningful to our team. It represents the trust Home Depot places in us to keep their supply chain moving.


Thank you to Home Depot for recognizing[Knight](https://www.knighttrans.com/our-services/#services_description) as the Carrier of the Year for 2025! And most importantly, thank you to our Knight team members who make achievements like this possible every day.
