# Knight Transportation Named One of America’s Most Patriotic Companies in 2026 by Newsweek

Knight Transportation is proud to announce that Newsweek has named the company one of[America’s Most Patriotic Companies in 2026](https://rankings.newsweek.com/americas-most-patriotic-companies-2026) , a prestigious recognition awarded to organizations that demonstrate a strong commitment to American values, community support, ethical business practices, and patriotism.


This national recognition reflects Knight’s ongoing dedication to supporting the men and women who have served our country, fostering opportunities for veterans, and creating a workplace culture built on service, respect, and leadership.


## Honoring Veterans Through Meaningful Career Opportunities


Veterans have long been an important part of the[Knight Transportation](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/knight-transportation_driveknight-america250-knightlife-activity-7478840002332901376-NhyZ?utm_source=li_share&utm_content=feedcontent&utm_medium=g_dt_web&utm_campaign=copy) family. Every day, veteran drivers and employees bring leadership, discipline, teamwork, and a mission-focused mindset that fuels our nationwide success.


At the same time, we recognize that veterans often face unique challenges when transitioning from military to civilian careers.


With that in mind, Knight helps veterans build meaningful careers through opportunity, support, and a workplace that values experience.


As a result, veterans make a lasting impact by driving, supporting operations, leading teams, and serving customers daily.


## What Makes a Company Patriotic?


According to Newsweek, evaluators selected America’s Most Patriotic Companies based on consumer feedback,[corporate responsibility](https://www.knighttrans.com/corporate-responsibility/) , community impact, and American values.


Additionally, the ranking also considered each organizations’ support for military personnel, veterans, first responders, and local communities.


Ultimately, this recognition reinforces Knight Transportation’s commitment to doing more than moving freight. It reflects our dedication to supporting the people and communities that keep America moving.


## Supporting Veterans Beyond Their Service


We proudly recognize military service through our Fleet of Heroes, a series of custom-wrapped trucks honoring veterans, military families, wounded warriors, and first responders. Each truck serves as a moving tribute to the sacrifice, courage, and dedication of those who’ve served.


Each December, Knight participates in[Wreaths Across America](https://www.wreathsacrossamerica.org/) , helping transport and place wreaths at veterans’ cemeteries nationwide. Together, these efforts reflect our ongoing commitment to honoring veterans, remembering their sacrifices, and supporting them long after their military service ends.


We also offer a[Military Scholarship Program](https://www.knighttrans.com/military/) that helps cover CDL school tuition and housing costs for veterans, reservists, National Guard members, and military spouses. Through our Veteran Apprenticeship Program, eligible veterans can receive GI Bill benefits while gaining valuable on-the-job training.


## A Culture Built on Service, Respect, and Gratitude


At[Knight Transportation](https://www.knighttrans.com/military/) , we believe our success is built by the people who make a difference every day.


This recognition reflects our veterans, drivers, and employees, and more importantly, the culture of respect, integrity, and service at Knight.


As America marks 250 years, we proudly recognize veterans whose sacrifices helped preserve our nation’s freedoms and opportunities.


Moreover, their dedication to service extends beyond the military, and we proudly support their successful careers at Knight.


We extend our sincere gratitude to all veterans across Knight Transportation and the Knight-Swift family of companies.


Finally, thank you you for your service, leadership, and contributions. We’re honored to work alongside you and celebrate this achievement together.
