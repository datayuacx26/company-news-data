### 2. Du Chinese — Bite-sized Stories for Reading Practice


⭐ Best for: Casual daily reading at an intermediate level


**Du Chinese** offers a large library of short stories and articles with pinyin, audio, and English translations. It's user-friendly and great for supplementing your daily practice, though it’s more passive than interactive.


-


Adjustable pinyin and translations


-


Read at your own pace


-


Mostly casual topics like travel, culture, and daily life


### 3. HelloChinese — Structured Lessons Beyond the Basics


⭐ Best for: Gamified, structured grammar and speaking drills


While **HelloChinese** is known for beginners, their more advanced courses (Premium+ plan) include HSK 4–6 level content, grammar explanations, and speaking challenges using voice recognition.


-


Gamified exercises to keep motivation up


-


Good grammar focus


-


Helpful for oral fluency, but limited reading depth


### 4. SuperChinese — AI-Personalized Grammar and Listening


⭐ Best for: Strengthening listening and grammar comprehension


**SuperChinese** uses AI to adapt lesson plans based on your mistakes and strengths. It’s particularly useful for learners who need more listening and grammar-heavy practice at intermediate levels.


-


AI identifies weak points automatically


-


Strong listening practice across accents


-


Less focused on deep reading or writing


### 5. The Chairman’s Bao — News-Based Learning Platform


⭐ Best for: Serious learners preparing for exams or professional use


If you want structured reading practice based on *real-world news* , **The Chairman’s Bao** is a classic. It offers graded news articles written for learners from HSK 1–6+.


-


Great for HSK exam preparation


-


Business and academic news topics


-


Heavy text focus, but less interactive
