## The Bad: Where AI in Language Learning Falls Short


### 1. Risk of Incorrect Information


AI can sometimes misinterpret language nuances or generate **inaccurate translations** and explanations, especially with less common phrases or cultural references.


-


A mistranslated idiom can reinforce bad habits.


-


A poorly explained grammar point can cause confusion.


### 2. Losing the Human and Cultural Touch


Languages are deeply tied to **culture, history, and human emotion** .
Over-relying on AI can create an "empty" learning experience — one that feels mechanical instead of truly immersive.


-


Slang, jokes, humor, and emotional expression can get lost.


-


Regional accents, gestures, and context might be overlooked.
