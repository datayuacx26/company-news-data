# Quince Therapeutics to Participate at Investor Events in December 2025

[PDF Version](https://ir.quincetx.com/node/9636/pdf)


SOUTH SAN FRANCISCO, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Nov. 24, 2025-- Quince Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: QNCX), a late-stage biotechnology company dedicated to unlocking the power of a patient’s own biology for the treatment of rare diseases, today announced that senior management will participate at three investor events in December 2025:


- Dirk Thye, M.D., Quince’s Chief Executive Officer and Chief Medical Officer, will participate at the Piper Sandler 37th Annual Healthcare Conference on Wednesday, December 3, 2025, with a fireside chat beginning at 10:30 a.m. Eastern Time. Access to the company’s fireside chat will be accessible on the Events page of Quince’s Investor Relations website[here](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.quincetx.com%2Fevents&esheet=54362203&newsitemid=20251124942104&lan=en-US&anchor=here&index=1&md5=f6b586d77b27a3f22a22ab91be7bccea) . An archive of the webcast will be available shortly following the end of the live event.
- Charles Ryan, J.D., Ph.D., Quince’s President, will participate at the Oppenheimer Movers in Rare Disease Summit on December 11, 2025. This event will include participation on a panel showcasing rare disease companies with key near-term, potentially stock-moving catalysts as Quince moves toward pivotal Phase 3 NEAT clinical trial topline results expected in the first quarter of 2026.
- Dirk Thye, M.D., Quince’s Chief Executive Officer and Chief Medical Officer, and Brendan Hannah, Quince’s Chief Operating Officer and Chief Business Officer, will participate in a virtual Lunch with LifeSci Featuring Quince Therapeutics fireside chat hosted by LifeSci Advisors on Tuesday, December 16, 2025, beginning at 1:00 p.m. Eastern Time. Please register for the Lunch with LifeSci event[here](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Flifescipartners.zoom.us%2Fwebinar%2Fregister%2FWN_kp_R_DXFQROKh1yjOi7vQg%23%2Fregistration&esheet=54362203&newsitemid=20251124942104&lan=en-US&anchor=here&index=2&md5=e86997f6f47947bafea7b2e6d2554770) .
