# Introducing Lifecycle Studio: Go from idea to personalized campaigns, in minutes.

When Anthropic dropped Claude Code, software engineering had a permanent redefining moment.


The breakthrough was that engineers finally had a harness that understood how their work actually gets done. One that could navigate codebases, use tools, learn from context, and help move projects from idea to production.


**We believe marketing is next.**


The future of marketing will be defined with software that deeply understands a marketing discipline and can help teams accomplish real work from start to finish. Software that acts more like an agency than a platform, where you define the business goal and the system helps get it done. Reliably, on-brand, and grounded in your data.


Today, we're launching Lifecycle Studio to give lifecycle and CRM teams that capability, reinventing what it means to run lifecycle marketing.


## The lifecycle workflow hasn't changed in a decade


Every lifecycle marketer knows the process. An idea comes up. Someone writes a brief. Data science pulls the audience. Creative gets a request. Copy goes through review. Design builds the email. Ops assembles the journey. QA runs the checks. Four to six weeks later, the campaign finally launches.


Workflows can’t keep up with your campaign ideas.


Over the last decade, marketing technology made individual steps more efficient. Segmentation got faster. Content blocks became more dynamic. Journey builders became easier to use.


But the workflow itself stayed mostly the same.


The best ideas still wait in queues. Campaigns still depend on half a dozen handoffs. Teams still choose between speed, personalization, and quality because they rarely have the capacity to do all three.


## Lifecycle Studio reinvents the way marketing teams work


Lifecycle Studio frees marketers to go from an idea to fully-built, personalized campaigns, in minutes.


Marketers set the campaign goal, and agents draft the campaign brief, recommend audiences, generate content and creative, configure journeys, and prepare messages for activation—all grounded in the full context of your customer data, brand guidelines, creative history, campaign performance, and marketing stack.


With Lifecycle Studio, marketing teams can now self-serve on the work that used to require a half-dozen dependencies without filing a single cross-team request.


Thumbtack cut campaign cycles from six weeks to days, reduced cross-team effort by 75%, and launched programs they simply couldn't run before.


## The campaigns you've always wanted to run


Most lifecycle teams are not short on ideas. They are short on the time, resources, and cross-functional bandwidth to actually get those ideas into market.


Lifecycle Studio is built to help teams launch more campaigns, faster, without sacrificing quality or personalization. Here are a few ways marketers are using it today:


-


**Ship your full backlog.** Every lifecycle team has campaigns they know are worth running but never quite get to: new onboarding tests, winback ideas, loyalty moments, better cross-sell journeys. Lifecycle Studio gives teams the capacity to finally move those ideas out of the queue and into market.


-


**Optimize the campaigns already running.** The work does not stop once a journey is live. Teams still need new variants, better segments, refreshed copy, and more ways to improve performance. Lifecycle Studio makes it easier to keep improving live campaigns without turning every iteration into a new cross-functional project.


-


**Launch in the moments that matter.** Sometimes the best campaign ideas are driven by moments. A sporting event, cultural viral trend, or breaking news. Lifecycle Studio helps teams move while the moment still matters, compressing the time from idea to live campaign from weeks to minutes.


-


**Turn market signals into campaigns.** Instead of waiting for teams to find every opportunity manually, agents proactively surface opportunities and turn them into ready-to-launch campaigns. The result is a workflow where marketers are not just executing more ideas, they are discovering more of the right ones.

## What Lifecycle Studio does


Lifecycle Studio operates a series of agents with access to your customer data, brand context, creative assets, and marketing stack. These agents help teams move from planning to launch without the traditional handoffs.


**Ideation.** Agents draft briefs with audience segment recommendations, journey strategies, and messaging angles grounded in your customer history and engagement signals. Marketers can prompt directly in Lifecycle Studio, through Claude or ChatGPT via MCP.


You define your campaign goals and preferred channels, the agents do the heavy lifting.


**Generation** . Agents build every message variant, pulling from your DAM, product catalog, and past campaign templates to generate on-brand content at scale. Brand guidelines, compliance checks, dark-mode rendering, and ESP-specific templating are all handled in the same pass.


Generate on-brand, production ready campaigns.


**Orchestration.** After marketers review, refine, and send off for final approval, agents activate across email, push, and SMS through the marketing stack you already have.


Once a campaign is approved, activate through your existing ESPs.


**Measurement.** Every campaign feeds the next one. Agents pull engagement, conversion, and revenue data from every send, surface the patterns worth acting on, and recommend the content to inform the next campaign.


Agents surface patterns and unforseen opportunities to act upon.
