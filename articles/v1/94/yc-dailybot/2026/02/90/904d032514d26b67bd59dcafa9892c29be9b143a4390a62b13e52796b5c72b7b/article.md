# Dailybot | The most powerful AI assistant for team check-ins out there · Changelog

We’re excited to announce **AI-Powered Smart Summaries** — a breakthrough feature that transforms how teams consume check-in data. Dailybot’s AI now generates intelligent summaries of your team’s responses, highlighting the most important updates, flagging blockers, and surfacing action items automatically.


No more scrolling through dozens of individual responses. With Smart Summaries, team leads and managers get an instant bird’s-eye view of what’s happening across the team — in seconds, not minutes.


## How to use Dailybot’s AI


Smart Summaries work automatically with your existing check-ins. Once enabled, Dailybot’s AI will analyze team responses and generate a summary after all participants have submitted their updates. You can also trigger summaries on-demand by asking Dailybot AI in your chat platform.


- **Automatic summaries** — generated after all check-in responses are collected, delivered to the channel or via DM
- **On-demand summaries** — ask Dailybot AI to summarize any check-in, time period, or team at any time
- **Blocker detection** — AI identifies and highlights blockers, risks, and items that need attention
- **Action item extraction** — automatically pulls out next steps and to-dos from team responses
- **Trend analysis** — compare summaries over time to spot patterns and track progress


## Improved chat assistance. Powered by AI


Along with Smart Summaries, we’ve significantly upgraded Dailybot’s AI chat capabilities. The AI assistant now understands context better, provides more accurate responses, and can handle complex queries about your team’s data. Whether you’re asking about participation rates, looking for specific updates, or need help configuring workflows, Dailybot AI is smarter and faster than ever.


Smart Summaries are available on all plans, with advanced customization options on Advanced and Enterprise tiers. Enable them today in your Dailybot dashboard or ask our AI assistant for help getting started.
