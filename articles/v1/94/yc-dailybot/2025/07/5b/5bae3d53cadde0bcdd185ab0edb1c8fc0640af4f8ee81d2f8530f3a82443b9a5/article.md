# Dailybot | Meet the new Jira + Dailybot integration · Changelog

Dailybot’s rebuilt Jira integration brings one-click setup, chat commands, and live issue updates so dev teams can ship faster. No tab juggling needed — manage your Jira issues, get status updates, and keep the team aligned without leaving your chat platform.


The new integration is designed for developer workflows, with support for standups, sprint updates, and issue tracking directly in Slack, Teams, or Discord.
