# Dailybot | Google Calendar Sync Is Live · Changelog

Dailybot now supports calendar sync. Automatically pull in your meetings to async check-ins, ask about your schedule in chat, and even schedule meetings with teammates without switching tools. Your calendar context is now part of your team’s async workflow.


This makes it easier for teams to understand availability, avoid scheduling conflicts, and keep check-in responses relevant to what’s actually on everyone’s calendar.
