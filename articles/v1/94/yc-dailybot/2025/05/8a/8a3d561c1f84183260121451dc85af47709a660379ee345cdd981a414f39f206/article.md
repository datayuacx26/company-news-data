# How to win friends and influence AI search results

The search landscape is undergoing its most significant transformation since Google first appeared. AI-powered search engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google’s Gemini are revolutionizing how people find information online. Instead of scrolling through blue links, users are receiving direct, conversational answers to their queries.


Naturally, a change like this demands a new approach to SEO—one that positions your content to be referenced, cited, and featured in AI-generated responses. Welcome to the world of “AI SEO” (also known as “Generative Search Optimization” or “GSO”).


## Why AI SEO matters now


AI search tools digest and synthesize your content into direct answers versus simply displaying your website or linking to it. If your optimization strategy hasn’t evolved for this new reality, you risk becoming invisible in an increasingly AI-mediated world.


Consider this: when someone asks ChatGPT or Perplexity a question related to your industry, will your expertise be represented in that answer? Or will your competitors’ content be the source of truth?


## Traditional SEO vs. AI SEO


Aspect Traditional SEO AI SEO


**Goal** Rank high in search results to earn clicks Be included or cited in AI-generated answers


**Success** Your URL appears as a blue link Your content is synthesized into the answer itself


**Metrics** Rankings, organic traffic, CTR AI citations, brand mentions, post-AI referral traffic


Many traditional SEO fundamentals still apply—they just need to be enhanced with AI-specific strategies.


## Your AI SEO optimization playbook


### 1. Structure content for AI digestibility


AI systems love clear, well-organized content that directly answers specific questions. Your first action steps:


- Restructure content into a Q&A format with clear question headings
- Answer the main question concisely in the first 1-2 sentences of each section
- Use descriptive H2-H4 headers that mirror how people ask questions
- Focus each section on a single question (avoid cramming multiple questions together)


Instead of a section titled “Product Features,” use “What Features Does \[Product\] Offer?” Then immediately provide the answer before elaborating.


### 2. Implement structured data and schema markup


Schema markup helps AI models better understand your content’s context and purpose. Add JSON-LD schema markup for:


- FAQs (FAQ schema)
- Articles (Article schema)
- Products (Product schema)
- Authors (Person schema)
- Organization information


Ensure consistent entity information across all pages. While there is some debate about schema’s direct impact on AI results, this practice helps build your presence in knowledge graphs that AI systems reference.


### 3. Establish E-E-A-T signals for AI trust


AI models prioritize content from trusted, authoritative sources. Here’s what you can do now:


- Add visible author bios with credentials and expertise
- Link author profiles to LinkedIn or professional websites
- Include citations to reputable sources (.edu, .gov domains, peer-reviewed research)
- Update content regularly (AI favors fresh, current information)
- Create comprehensive topic coverage that demonstrates subject mastery


**A real-world example:** Investopedia is frequently cited in AI responses for financial queries because of its established authority and clear, structured content.


### 4. Optimize for traditional SEO as your foundation


Since many AI models are trained on web data and rely on search indexes, traditional SEO is still a priority:


- Maintain technical SEO excellence (site speed, mobile-friendliness, crawlability)
- Continue building quality backlinks (they signal authority to both search engines and AI)
- Create comprehensive internal linking structure
- Optimize for featured snippets (they often correlate with AI answer selection)


This is the foundation upon which your AI SEO strategy builds—don’t cut corners here.


### 5. Explore API access and data feeds


A more forward-thinking approach is making your content directly accessible to AI systems through APIs:


- Consider implementing a public API for your website content
- Explore the emerging llms.txt protocol (similar to robots.txt but for AI crawlers)
- Offer structured data feeds for your content


This is one of the most novel approaches, positioning you at the forefront of AI integration.


### 6. Create beyond-the-basics content value


Since AI can satisfy basic informational queries, your content needs to provide value beyond what an AI summary can offer:


- Include unique insights, proprietary research, or exclusive data
- Develop tools that complement your content
- Add value through community engagement or expert commentary
- Create multimedia experiences that AI can reference but not replicate


Remember: if an AI can completely satisfy a user’s need with a summary of your content, you need to offer something deeper to earn the click-through.


## Measuring AI SEO success


With no direct equivalent to position tracking for AI visibility, new measurement approaches are needed:


1. **Monitor AI citations:** Regularly query AI tools with keywords related to your content and track whether your brand/content is mentioned
2. **Track direct brand searches:** Increases may indicate users discovering you through AI mentions
3. **Set up analytics segments:** Create segments to identify traffic coming from AI platforms
4. **Measure post-AI engagement:** Focus on conversion rates from AI-referred traffic rather than just volumes


AI SEO measurement is still a young field, and as it develops, better tools will emerge.


## Challenging “the black box”


One of the biggest issues in AI SEO is the opacity of how these systems select content:


- Results can vary based on prompt phrasing or context
- There’s minimal control over how your content is presented
- Different AI models may weight factors differently
- There’s no direct feedback mechanism to measure improvement


This uncertainty doesn’t mean we’re flying completely blind, but it does force a mindset shift from precise optimization towards more comprehensive influence-building. The key is focusing on quality, authority, and clarity rather than trying to “game” specific AI algorithms.


## Things to look out for in 2025 and beyond


As AI search continues to evolve, several trends are emerging:


**Zero-click searches will increase.** Users will get answers without visiting websites, making brand presence in AI responses crucial.


**Success metrics are changing.** The emphasis will move from traffic volume to post-click engagement and conversion.


**Content distribution will diversify.** Brands will focus on maintaining presence across multiple platforms that feed AI systems.


**New tooling is already emerging.** Expect better analytics for AI search visibility and citation tracking.


## Your action plan for today


1. **Audit your current content:** Evaluate how well your existing material is structured for AI consumption
2. **Prioritize high-value pages:** Focus first on optimizing cornerstone content that represents your core expertise
3. **Implement Q&A restructuring:** Reorganize content to directly answer key questions in your industry
4. **Add schema markup:** Start with FAQ, Article, and Author schema on priority pages
5. **Monitor your AI presence:** Test how your brand appears in AI responses


AI SEO represents not just a challenge but an opportunity. The organizations that adapt earliest will establish valuable positions in this new search paradigm.


AI optimization isn’t about abandoning traditional SEO principles but enhancing them. Focus on creating genuinely helpful, authoritative content that’s structured for machine understanding while delivering unique value no AI can replicate.


The future of search belongs to those who can effectively communicate with both humans and machines. Implement these strategies now and you won’t just be optimizing for today’s AI tools—you’ll be future-proofing your digital presence for whatever comes next.
