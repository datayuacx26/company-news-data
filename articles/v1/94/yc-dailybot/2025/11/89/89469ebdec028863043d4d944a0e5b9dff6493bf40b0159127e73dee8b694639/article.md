# Dailybot | Introducing Summary Reports - clearer insights from your team's check-ins · Changelog

Dailybot now gives managers a faster way to understand team progress. Generate Summary Reports across teams and check-ins in one clean view. No more digging through individual responses — get a consolidated overview of what your team has been working on, key highlights, and action items in seconds.


Summary Reports help team leads and managers stay informed without the overhead of reading every check-in response. Perfect for weekly reviews, stakeholder updates, and keeping a pulse on team momentum.
