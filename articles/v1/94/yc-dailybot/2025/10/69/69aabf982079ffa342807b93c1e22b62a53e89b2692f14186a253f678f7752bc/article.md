# Dailybot | Enhanced Team Analytics Dashboard · Changelog

We’ve redesigned the Team Analytics Dashboard with powerful new capabilities to help you understand and improve team engagement. The enhanced analytics include participation trends over time, sentiment analysis of team responses, response time tracking, and exportable reports for sharing with stakeholders.


These improvements give managers and team leads better visibility into how their teams are engaging with check-ins and where they can provide support or intervention.
