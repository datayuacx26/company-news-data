# Dailybot | Access Dailybot from any web page with our Browser Extension · Changelog

Introducing the **Dailybot Browser Extension** — now you can access Dailybot from any web page without switching to your chat platform. Whether you’re reviewing a pull request on GitHub, reading documentation, or browsing the web, Dailybot is always just a click away.


The Chrome extension provides a lightweight popup interface that shows your pending check-ins, lets you submit responses, and delivers real-time notifications. It’s the fastest way to stay on top of your team updates without context switching.


## Key features of the Browser Extension


The extension is designed to be lightweight and unobtrusive. Click the Dailybot icon in your browser toolbar to access everything you need in a clean popup interface.


- **Quick check-in popup** — view and respond to pending check-ins without leaving your current tab
- **Real-time notifications** — get badge alerts when you have pending check-ins or new team updates
- **Team activity feed** — browse recent team check-in summaries and kudos in the extension sidebar
- **Quick actions** — give kudos, view dashboards, and jump to your Dailybot settings with one click
- **Minimal footprint** — the extension uses minimal resources and only activates when you interact with it


## Available for Chrome and Chromium browsers


The Dailybot Browser Extension is available now on the Chrome Web Store and works with all Chromium-based browsers including Chrome, Brave, Edge, and Arc. Firefox support is coming soon.


[Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/dailybot-%E2%80%94-standups-check/dpgmlpodaidnpnddlfmpeplmdngmnkdm) , sign in with your Dailybot account, and never miss a check-in again. It’s free on all Dailybot plans.


See the[Chrome extension connector page](https://www.dailybot.com/connections/browser-extension) for setup steps and feature details.
