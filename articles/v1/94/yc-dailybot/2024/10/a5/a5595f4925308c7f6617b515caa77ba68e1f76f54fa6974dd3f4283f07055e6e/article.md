# Dailybot Now Supports Gmail Accounts · Changelog

Dailybot now supports Gmail accounts! Invite Gmail users to your team and collaborate effortlessly via Google Chat, no GSuite needed. This makes it easier for teams to get started with Dailybot without requiring a Google Workspace subscription.


Whether your team uses Gmail for personal or work email, everyone can now join Dailybot and participate in check-ins, kudos, and workflows.
