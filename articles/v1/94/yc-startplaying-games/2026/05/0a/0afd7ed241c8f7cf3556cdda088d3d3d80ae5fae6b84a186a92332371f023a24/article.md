# Dark Lord Stat Blocks Shown, Vampire's Next Project, WotC Employee Union Update – TTRPG News 5/29/2026

Dungeons & Dragons dominated the headlines this week, both for good and bad reasons. In this week’s tabletop RPG news: *Ravenloft, The Horrors Within* previews show off stat blocks, *Vampire: The Masquerade* gets big updates at Gen Con, and WotC employees claim the company is sending them anti-union letters.


### **Ravenloft: The Horrors Within Previews Show DnD Cthulhu Stat Block**


[Dungeons & Dragons](https://startplaying.games/play/dungeons-and-dragons-5e) put out a[State of the Game](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ipsLQIwemeo) video today to give developer insights on the upcoming **Ravenloft book** . Among the pages previewed were **stat blocks for the Darklords** themselves, something many criticized was missing from *Van Richten’s Guide to Ravenloft* .


Strahd himself got the 5.5e treatment complete with an auto-grapple attack and the flexibility to mix in spellcasting with his multiattack. Little is a surprise, remaining faithful to how he’s statted in[Curse of Strahd](https://startplaying.games/play/dungeons-and-dragons-5e/curse-of-strahd) . A bit more novelty went into **Cthulhu** , who uses a lot of action economy to fold space, both teleporting and knocking enemies around. *Ravenloft: The Horrors Within* releases on June 16.


### **Big Vampire: The Masquerade News Coming During Gen Con 2026**


via White Wolf


Around this time last year,[White Wolf was revived](https://startplaying.games/blog/posts/psion-class-dnd-2024-white-wolf-return-2025-svavelvinter-remaster-dragonbane) as the steward of the **World of Darkness** TTRPG arm. Now they are ready to reveal what’s next for[Vampire: The Masquerade](https://startplaying.games/play/vampire-the-masquerade-5e) . The[studio announced](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/world-of-darkness/news/vtm-gencon2026) that it has plans to bring VTM to **Gen Con 202** 6 in a big way. They teased a new project that emphasizes player agency as well as some advancement of the **VTM** **metaplot** .


The post also reveals that there will be a presentation on the future of World of Darkness, an actual play featuring new playtest material, and playtesting opportunities. Tickets for all of these Gen Con events are on sale now.


### **Wizards Of The Coast Sends Letters Urging Employees Not To Unionize**


via[@xib.bsky.social](https://bsky.app/profile/xib.bsky.social)


A group of **WotC employees** working on ***Magic: The Gathering Arena***[unionized earlier this month](https://startplaying.games/blog/posts/new-justice-league-ttrpg-astarion-prequel-novel-wotc-employees-unionize) and now find the company urging them not to. The company was given the invitation to recognize the union and[ignored it](https://startplaying.games/blog/posts/dnd-beyond-drops-wotc-declines-union-stormbringer-ttrpg-free-league) . WotC gave statements to the press, but union organizers claimed that they were never given an official response. Now, employees are[telling PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/3-years-on-from-laying-off-over-1-000-people-hasbro-and-wizards-of-the-coast-are-sending-daily-emails-and-physical-letters-encouraging-the-survivors-to-not-unionise/) that WotC is **sending daily emails** describing how unions don’t guarantee fair deals and make negotiations take longer. Employees shared these emails on social media, and are staying resolute on voting “Yes” for a union.
