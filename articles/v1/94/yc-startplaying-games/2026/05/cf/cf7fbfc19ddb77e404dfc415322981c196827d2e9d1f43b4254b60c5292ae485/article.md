# Cancelled D&D Video Game, Daggerheart Anniversary Announcements, Dungeon Crawler Carl BackerKit Overtime – TTRPG News 5/22/2026

Fresh off a showing of the ***Mandalorian & Grogu*** , here I am with this week’s tabletop RPG news! (Also if you’re hankering for some Star Wars TTRPG action,[we got you](https://startplaying.games/play/star-wars-rpg-ffg) .)


This week: Hasbro passes on DnD video game pitch from prominent game director, *Daggerheart* marks its first year with a lot of announcements, Paizo shares more of its horror TTRPG *13 Omens* , and *Dungeon Crawler Carl* joins the multi-million crowdfunding club.


### **Hasbro Cancels D&D Video Game From Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order Dev**


A **Dungeons & Dragons video game** helmed by the *Fallen Order* director was passed on by Hasbro. As[per Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-19/hasbro-cancels-dungeons-dragons-game-from-star-wars-veteran?embedded-checkout=true) , Wizards of the Coast is choosing to **deny “an early concept”** from Giant Skull, a studio helmed by director Stig Asmussen. According to Asmussen, however, relations are still good between the two companies and **WotC is considering other pitches** from Giant Skull. Hasbro has several other D&D video games in development, including a[town builder from Gameloft](https://www.gameloft.com/newsroom/gameloft-montreal-dungeons-and-dragons-pc-console) and the action-adventure game[Warlock](https://www.warlockthegame.com/en-US) *.*


### **Daggerheart Anniversary Special Promises Content For All Tiers**


via Critical Role


Darrington Press celebrated[Daggerheart](https://startplaying.games/play/daggerheart) *’* s first anniversary with several announcements for players, GMs, and creators. For players, products were revealed such as a **Duality Dice set** and holder. Game Masters[can now download](https://shop.critrole.com/products/the-wish-thief-a-daggerheart-birthday-adventure) a **free Tier 1 adventure** called *The Wish Thief* , with promises that more **free adventures that span all tiers** are planned for 2026 and 2027. As for creators, Darrington revealed that content from the upcoming *Hope & Fear* expansion will be added to the **Daggerheart SRD** . Finally, another ***Critical Role*** miniseries set in the Age of Umbra begins on July 9.


### **Paizo Horror TTRPG 13 Omens Uses Shared Dice Pool**


via Paizo


Paizo revealed some of the mechanics behind its upcoming **horror RPG *13 Omens*** via its mailing list. EN World[shared the update](https://www.enworld.org/threads/new-deets-about-paizos-13-omens-horror-ttrpg.719218/) , which goes into how dice work. Players use a shared pool of d6 kept in a bag. Some of the dice are safe, whereas others are labelled as **Omen dice** . The Omen dice are responsible for negative outcomes and more are added to the pool as a session goes on. *13 Omens* aims to be a narrative-focused, modern horror game.


### **Dungeon Crawler Carl RPG Takes On Most-Funded Kickstarters**


via Renegade Game Studio


The crowdfunding campaign for the[Dungeon Crawler Carl Roleplaying Game](https://startplaying.games/blog/posts/dungeon-crawler-carl-rpg-paizo-horror-ttrpg-daggerheart-expansion-release-date) ** hit its end on[BackerKit this week](https://www.backerkit.com/c/projects/renegade-game-studios/dungeon-crawler-carl-rpg-unstoppable) , finishing strong at **over $13 million** . This activated BackerKit’s Overtime mode that allows extra time for late pledges. That final funding tally puts Carl in a list of legends. Literally, as[Avatar Legends](https://startplaying.games/play/avatar-legends-the-rpg) and[Cosmere RPG](https://startplaying.games/play/cosmere-roleplaying-game) ** stand as the top TTRPG earners from crowdfunding. ***Dungeon Crawler Carl*** didn’t quite hit the $15 million of ***Cosmere*** but still made a very impressive showing. Now the wait begins to actually play the thing.
