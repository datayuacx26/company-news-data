# WotC Employees Achieve First Union, Invincible TTRPG Release, Arcana Unleashed Subclasses – TTRPG News 6/26/2026

This week in StartPlaying TTRPG news: *Arcana Unleashed* subclasses previewed in new actual play series, *Magic Arena* employees officially formed the first union ever at WotC, Invincible TTRPG gets release details, and *Outgunned* pre-orders open for two new books.


### **Arcana Unleashed Subclasses Confirmed Via Dungeon Masters Show**


The second season of the official DnD Actual Play,[Dungeon Masters](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/2198-dungeon-masters-campaign-2-experience-the-magic-of) , will preview four subclasses from the upcoming ***Arcana Unleashed* book** . The four subclasses,[according to EN World](https://www.enworld.org/threads/four-subclasses-confirmed-for-arcana-unleashed.719654/) , are **Arcane Archer Fighter, Transmuter Wizard, Vestige Warlock, and Warrior of the Mystic Arts Monk** .


The season itself tells the story of a group of Misfits who must traverse the Anauroch Desert to ensure ancient magical forces stay sealed. Like how season one previewed elements of *Ravenloft: the Horrors Within* , season two looks to offer peeks at *Arcana Unleashed* including Play-Along packs for viewers to access on D&D Beyond. It features the same cast, with the first episode airing **July 8** .


### **First Union Within Wizards Of The Coast Gets Its Official Start**


The *Magic: The Gathering Arena* workers got their union. The group is called **United Wizards of the Coast** , and was successfully voted into being with 79 saying yes and 16 for no. **WotC publicly accepted** the results of the vote, and the bargaining process will now begin.


This union is the first to exist within WotC. It came about after *Arena* employees grouped up to fight for better pay, crunch, and concerns over AI. WotC did not recognize the first announcement of this union, and[many employees received letters](https://startplaying.games/blog/posts/dnd-dark-lord-stat-blocks-vtm-gen-con-wotc-employee-union-news) these past few weeks downplaying the effectiveness of unions.


### **Invincible TTRPG Comes Out July 30 At Gen Con**


The *Invincible RPG* set its official launch for **Gen Con** on **July 30** . Thankfully, for those not attending the giant convention, you can still pre-order on[Free League’s website](https://freeleaguepublishing.com/shop/invincible/core-rulebook/) . PDFs will be distributed via **DriveThruRPG** on launch day. Those looking for a physical edition can either order it online to get it shipped or find it in your local game store. Physical items include a starter set, core rulebook, GM screen, power cards, and a branded d6 set.


### **Outgunned Superheroes Comes To Store Pre-Orders**


Free League put ***Outgunned Superheroes*** up for pre-order after its highly successful Kickstarter. While the PDF is already available on DriveThruRPG, this pre-order is for the hard cover book’s eventual release in book stores, local game stores, and online. As reported by[ICv2](https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/62719/free-league-places-two-outgunned-rpg-books-onto-preorder) , *Outgunned Superheroes* retails for $49.99. There will also be *Action Flicks Vol. 3* which adds action movie settings for *Outgunned* including mythology and time travel.
