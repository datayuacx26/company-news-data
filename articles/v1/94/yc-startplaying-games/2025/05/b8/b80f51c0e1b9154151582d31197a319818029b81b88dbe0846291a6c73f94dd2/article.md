# What Is The Best VTT For Werewolf: The Apocalypse 5th Edition?

When it comes to playing[Werewolf: The Apocalypse 5th Edition](https://startplaying.games/play/werewolf-the-apocalypse-5th-edition) online, choosing the right[Virtual Tabletop (VTT)](https://startplaying.games/blog/posts/a-comprehensive-list-of-virtual-tabletops) can make or break your gaming experience. While there are several excellent options available, Roll20 emerges as the best choice for running W5 due to its accessibility, robust features, and more.


### Why Roll20 is the Best VTT for Werewolf: The Apocalypse 5th Edition


#### Dynamic Lighting & Atmospheric Tools


*Werewolf: The Apocalypse* thrives on mood and immersion. Roll20’s dynamic lighting, fog of war, and customizable maps help create the dark, tense atmosphere that W5 demands. Whether you're stalking through an urban sprawl or battling in the Umbra, Roll20’s visual tools enhance the experience.


#### Easy-to-Use Dice Roller & Automation


Roll20’s built-in dice roller supports the Storyteller System’s mechanics, including dice pools, successes, and critical failures. The system automatically calculates results, saving time and reducing manual tracking—perfect for fast-paced combat and frenetic Rage-fueled action.


#### Seamless Audio & Video Integration


Since *Werewolf* is a highly narrative game, clear communication is key. Roll20 includes integrated video and voice chat, eliminating the need for third-party apps like Discord. This keeps everything in one place, making it easier to focus on the story.


#### Cross-Platform Accessibility & Free Tier


Unlike some VTTs that require hefty downloads or subscriptions, Roll20 is browser-based and works on almost any device. The free tier is robust enough for most groups, while the paid tiers unlock even more features like advanced macros and storage space.


### Active Community & Workshop Support


[Roll20](https://startplaying.games/search?gamePlatforms=roll20) has a large, active community of World of Darkness fans, meaning you can easily find pre-made assets, maps, and macros tailored for Werewolf: The Apocalypse. The marketplace also offers additional tools and artwork to enhance your game.


#### Final Verdict: Roll20 is the Best Choice for W5


For *Werewolf: The Apocalypse 5th Edition* , Roll20 stands out as the best VTT due to its official support, ease of use, immersive tools, and strong community resources. Whether you're a new Storyteller or a seasoned Garou, Roll20 provides everything you need to bring the savage world of the Wyrm’s corruption to life.
