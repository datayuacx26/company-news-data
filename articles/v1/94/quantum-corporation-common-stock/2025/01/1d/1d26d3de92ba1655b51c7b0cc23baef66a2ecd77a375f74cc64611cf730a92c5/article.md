# Quantum’s “one-two” punch for AI applications recognized by analysts

**By Bob Fine, Analyst Relations Director**


Without question, AI is the most transformative technology we’ve seen in the last few decades. It has the power to transform traditional workflows in powerful new ways and yield discoveries that we can barely imagine today. As we start 2025, we’re at the dawn of the AI era and we can’t wait to see what discoveries AI will bring.


While AI will undoubtably transform every business, leveraging the power of AI requires a new type of infrastructure. The networks, computers, storage and servers of a decade ago are insufficient to meet the demands of today’s AI workloads. Quantum recognized this trend, and we’ve developed a targeted set of products unmatched by other vendors. This blog will highlight two of our key products, both of which have been recently highlighted as Leaders by analyst firms including[Coldago Research](https://www.coldago.net/) .


Coldago is an independent analyst firm covering storage and file systems. Over the last few years, they’ve released an annual assessment in key product areas to determine which vendors stand out in a crowded market. Coldago’s[Research Maps](https://www.coldago.net/mapobj) assess key customer criteria and evaluate vendors to determine which vendors stand out – these are considered Leaders. Two of Quantum’s products that are ideal for AI workloads are now positioned as Leaders. These are[Quantum’s Myriad all-flash file system](https://www.quantum.com/en/products/file-and-object/) and[Quantum’s ActiveScale](https://www.quantum.com/en/products/object-storage/) object storage system.


“Coldago’s Research Maps represent a year-long effort to determine key players in various market segments,” said Philippe Nicolas, founder and principal at Coldago. “Each map evaluates vendors by scrutinizing product announcements, success stories, and partnerships. among other things. For products, criteria include product/market fit, evolution, roadmap, frequency of releases, IP and patents, product design, and architecture. Leaders demonstrate a combination of factors that position them above other players in their markets. With the significant software shift and development effort that occurred within the company for a few years, inviting it to play premier roles in these domains, we’re pleased to recognize Quantum as a Leader in multiple Research Maps”.


AI applications are very different than traditional workloads. First and foremost, they require massive amounts of data to create key insights. Quite simply, more data yields better results. At Quantum, we like to say, “Your Difference is in Your Data”. However, building a powerful AI infrastructure requires two very different data engines. These systems require an extremely scalable, high-performance file system for massive data ingest. In addition, storing huge amounts of reference data is best suited for an exabyte-scale object storage system, ideally coupled with a cold storage component. This provides an extensive data archive at a lower cost and less power requirements. Quantum offers a one-two punch to address these diverse needs.


**Myriad – Fast and Scalable**
Quantum Myriad® is an all-flash, software-defined storage platform designed for legacy-free direct NVMe access, RDMA interconnects, and smart fabrics in a single, highly intelligent storage environment that fuels data-intensive, insight-driven workflows.


**ActiveScale – Deep and Cost-effective Object Storage**
Quantum ActiveScale® is a simple, reliable object storage platform built for high-performance access, storage efficiency, and the long-term durability of massive data sets. With built-in policy-based lifecycle management, ActiveScale helps build, scale, and manage massive data lakes for training AI models on your unique data for differentiating advantage and S3-compatible private and hybrid storage clouds for flexibility, predictable costs, and data security. To help you prepare for the AI era, we’ve developed a one-two punch of key products ready for the new set of workflows. Design your systems with confidence knowing they are recognized as Leaders by independent analyst firms such as Coldago.
