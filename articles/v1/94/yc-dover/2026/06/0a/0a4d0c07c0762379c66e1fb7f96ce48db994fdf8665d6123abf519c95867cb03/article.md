# Best Startup Recruitment Software to Know in July 2026

### What's the best recruitment software for a seed-stage startup with no dedicated recruiter?


For seed-stage teams, a free ATS with job board distribution and simple pipeline tracking is enough. Dover fits this stage: setup takes under five minutes, posts go out to over 100 boards automatically, and the free tier has no limits on jobs or users. For harder searches, fractional recruiter support is available at $2,000 to $7,000 per hire, no retainer required.


### Ashby ATS vs. Dover for an early-stage startup: which makes more sense?


Ashby targets scaling teams with dedicated recruiting operations. Its Foundations Plan for companies with up to 100 employees starts at $400 per month, with a 10% discount available for annual commitments, and it does not offer a permanent free tier. Dover is built for founder-led hiring at seed and Series A, where setup speed and cost matter more than deep analytics. For teams hiring their first five to ten people without a full-time recruiter, Dover's free ATS and on-demand fractional support are a better structural fit.


### Do I need a recruiter if I'm already using an ATS?


An ATS handles pipeline organization, job distribution, and candidate communication, but it does not source candidates or manage judgment calls in a competitive search. For roles where inbound is sufficient, it is often enough. The case for adding a recruiter builds when the role is senior or specialized, outreach is required, or hiring bandwidth is thin. Fractional recruiters can work directly inside the same ATS pipeline, giving both sides real-time visibility without coordination overhead.
