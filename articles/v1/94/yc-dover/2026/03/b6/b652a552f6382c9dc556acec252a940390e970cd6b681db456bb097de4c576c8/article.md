## FAQs


### How much do Austin startup recruiters typically charge?


Traditional agencies charge 20-30% of first-year salary as placement fees, which can cost $20,000-$40,000 per hire for senior tech roles.[Fractional recruiters typically charge $75-$125 per hour](https://www.dover.com/blog/fractional-recruiter-costs) (up to $250/hour for specialized roles), allowing startups to control costs based on actual work performed instead of successful placements.


### How long does it take to fill tech roles in Austin?


Average time-to-hire for tech roles in Austin ranges from 4-8 weeks, depending on role seniority and requirements. Specialized positions like senior engineers or technical leads often take longer due to limited candidate pools and multiple competing offers.


### Should early-stage startups work with recruiters?


Early-stage startups benefit from recruiter expertise when they lack internal recruiting resources or need specialized talent. The key is choosing flexible models that align with startup budgets and hiring patterns.


### Do I need an ATS before bringing in a fractional recruiter?


An ATS isn’t a hard requirement, but it changes how well fractional recruiting works in practice. When a recruiter (or a fractional recruiting agency) and your team operate from the same pipeline, both sides get real-time visibility into sourcing activity, candidate status, and interview feedback, with no coordination overhead or duplicate outreach to manage. Without a shared system, the recruiter works in one tool while your team tracks progress separately, which often creates gaps in communication and slower decision-making. Some fractional recruiting systems include a free ATS as part of the same infrastructure, so the agency or recruiter and your internal team work from a single source of truth from day one.
