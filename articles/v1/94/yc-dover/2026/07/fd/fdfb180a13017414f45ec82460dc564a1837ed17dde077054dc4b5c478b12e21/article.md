# How Does a Fractional Recruiter Work? (July 2026)

### What's the difference between a fractional recruiter and a contingency agency recruiter?


A fractional recruiter bills hourly ($75-$125/hour) and works inside your hiring process as a dedicated partner, while a contingency agency recruiter charges 10-30% of the hired candidate's first-year salary only when a placement is made. That fee structure pushes agencies toward speed over fit, whereas a fractional engagement ties the recruiter's time directly to your search quality.


### Should I use a fractional recruiter or hire a full-time in-house recruiter for my Series A startup?


A full-time recruiter makes sense when you anticipate continuous, high-volume hiring that warrants a loaded annual cost often exceeding $150K, and most Series A companies aren't there yet. A fractional engagement at 15-20 hours per week gives you structured sourcing and interview coordination without fixed overhead, and the engagement ends cleanly once a hiring sprint is complete.


### What types of roles benefit most from fractional recruiting?


In-demand technical and specialized roles, such as software engineers, product managers, and product designers, tend to benefit most, since finding strong candidates requires sourcing expertise and domain knowledge that most founding teams don't have in-house. Entry-level positions with straightforward requirements and deep applicant pools (SDRs, customer support) are generally easier to manage without dedicated recruiting support.
