## What Is Cadre?


Cadre is a boutique firm that connects startups with software engineers, deliberately capping their team at fifteen recruiters or fewer. They focus on building actual relationships with clients through clear communication, trust, and only surfacing opportunities that genuinely fit.


They work with early-stage startups and tech companies looking for engineering talent, with clients ranging from Pre-Series A startups to publicly traded tech companies and Venture Capitalists. Cadre also gives you access to their broader entrepreneurial community, so you gain the engineers you need now plus valuable connections down the road.


The key difference between network-based firms like Cadre and fractional recruiting platforms like Dover is ownership. With Cadre, you’re dependent on their specific network and relationships. ## Cadre History and Background


Founded in 2007 by Jason Stomel, Cadre has built its reputation around a network-based approach to tech recruiting. The firm specializes in technology startups across multiple sectors including information technology, media, automotive, restaurant technology, robotics, drones and augmented reality.


They have evolved from traditional contingency recruiting toward a more relationship-focused model that focuses on long-term connections within the tech ecosystem. Positioning itself as “not a traditional agency, but rather a network,” Cadre connects clients with renowned entrepreneurs and engineers. They play the long game, with some candidates having half of their career with companies Cadre has placed them at.


While Cadre has built itself up in the tech recruiting space, startups today are looking for more flexible solutions like Dover. Companies want solutions that adapt to their growth patterns without forcing them into rigid contracts or dependencies.

## FAQs


### How much does Cadre charge for recruiting services?


Cadre typically operates on a contingency model, charging 20-30% of the hired candidate’s first-year salary. You pay nothing upfront, but face major costs when making hires, especially for senior engineering roles.


### What’s the difference between fractional recruiting and traditional agencies?


Fractional recruiters work on an hourly basis and integrate with your team, while traditional agencies operate independently and charge contingency fees. Fractional recruiting offers more transparency, control and typically lower overall costs.
