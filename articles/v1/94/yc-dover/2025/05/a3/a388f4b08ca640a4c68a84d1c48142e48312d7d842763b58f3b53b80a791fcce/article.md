# Best Recruiting Agencies for Startups: July 2026

### Dover vs. Paraform for early-stage startup hiring: which model actually fits?


Dover gives you an integrated ATS plus a dedicated fractional recruiter who works as part of your team, while Paraform sends your role to multiple independent recruiters competing on placement. If you want a single recruiter who builds context on your company over time, Dover fits better; if you want maximum candidate volume fast and are comfortable managing coordination yourself, Paraform works for urgent, well-defined roles.


### Can I manage multiple recruiting agencies through a single system without losing track of candidate submissions and fees?


Yes. Dover's Agency Portal gives founders a centralized dashboard showing every candidate submitted by external agencies, their current status, and associated fees, so you can run contingency firms in parallel without placement-fee disputes that arise when two vendors find the same candidate.


### What information do I need before engaging a startup recruiting agency?


At minimum: a clear job description, a defined compensation range covering salary and equity, a realistic hiring timeline, and a ranked list of must-have versus nice-to-have qualifications. A compelling pitch on why a strong candidate should leave their current role matters just as much, since recruiters are selling your opportunity to passive candidates, and the clearer that pitch is from day one, the faster a search moves.
