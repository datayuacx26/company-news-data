# LinkedIn Recruiter Cost, Review and Alternatives (July 2026)

### How much does LinkedIn Recruiter actually cost per year?


LinkedIn Recruiter Corporate runs $9,600 to $12,960 per license annually, though LinkedIn does not publish pricing publicly and only reveals the final number after a sales conversation. Beyond the base fee, each additional InMail costs $10 to $15, and active teams can burn through their monthly allocation quickly. That's before factoring in recruiter time spent filtering irrelevant results and working around daily profile view caps.


### When should startups consider alternatives to LinkedIn Recruiter?


If your team is hitting InMail limits, filtering irrelevant candidates, or paying close to $1,000 a month without proportional results, LinkedIn Recruiter is likely not the right fit. Its fixed subscription model is a poor match for startups that hire in bursts or focus on a handful of roles per quarter, since you keep paying even during quiet periods. If recruiters are spending more time wording Boolean queries and filtering noise than engaging candidates, the tool is creating work instead of reducing it. Look at alternatives before costs compound across multiple renewals, not after locking into another annual contract.


### How do Dover's costs compare to LinkedIn Recruiter for startups?


Dover's ATS is free, with no license or subscription fee. Fractional recruiting services run $75 to $125 per hour, with per-hire costs generally landing between $2,000 and $7,000 depending on the role, and you only pay when actively hiring. Compare that to LinkedIn Recruiter Corporate at $9,600 to $12,960 per seat annually, locked in whether your pipeline is active or quiet, with no recruiting expertise built in. Dover ties costs to actual hiring activity; LinkedIn Recruiter charges a flat fee regardless of how many hires you make.
