## Frequently Asked Questions


### How accurate is AI candidate scoring compared to manual resume review?


AI scoring tools can process hundreds of resumes in seconds while maintaining consistent evaluation criteria, unlike manual review which varies by reviewer fatigue and bias. Some vendors cite[accuracy rates](https://www.hiretruffle.com/blog/best-ai-recruitment-statistics) in the 80% range when properly configured with job-specific requirements.


### What’s the main difference between free and enterprise application scoring tools?


Affordable tools like Dover, whose ATS is free and whose AI scoring runs $199 per month, offer core scoring functionality with basic customization, while enterprise solutions provide advanced features like video analysis, predictive analytics, and extensive bias reduction tools. However, for most recruiting teams, the lower-cost options deliver comparable candidate ranking accuracy without the complexity.


### How long does it take to set up an AI scoring system?


Setup time varies dramatically by tool. Dover takes under 5 minutes to start scoring candidates, while enterprise solutions like HireVue and Eightfold require 2-8 weeks for full implementation. For fast-growing companies that need to hire quickly, immediate deployment features are important.


### Can AI scoring tools help reduce hiring bias?


Yes, AI scoring tools can reduce human bias by applying consistent evaluation criteria to all candidates and focusing on job-relevant qualifications instead of subjective impressions. However, the AI must be properly trained and[regularly audited](https://secureprivacy.ai/blog/ai-bias-audit-requirements) to make sure it doesn’t perpetuate existing biases from historical hiring data.


### Do I need a recruiter if I’m already using an AI scoring tool?


AI scoring tools handle ranking and filtering, but getting a top candidate to say yes often requires human judgment. Outreach, interview coordination, salary negotiation, and offer management are areas where a recruiter’s experience moves faster than software alone. If your team has bandwidth for those steps, an AI scoring tool may be all you need. When hiring volume spikes or a role is hard to fill, Dover’s fractional recruiting layer lets you bring in vetted recruiters at $75 to $125 per hour with no retainer, so you add human capacity exactly when you need it, without a long-term contract.
