# Dover vs MarketerHire (August 2026)

When you’re looking for marketing talent, the choice often comes down to hiring freelancers or building an in-house team. MarketerHire and Dover take very different approaches: MarketerHire connects you with freelance marketers, while Dover gives you access to[fractional recruiters](https://www.dover.com/recruiters) who help you build your own full-time team. At its core, the decision is about whether you want to rent expertise or invest in long-term capabilities, and both paths come with trade-offs that founders often overlook. In this post, I’ll share a framework to help you choose the right fit for your team.

## What MarketerHire Does and Their Approach


MarketerHire is a freelance talent marketplace that matches businesses with pre-vetted marketing professionals. The[freelance platforms market](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/freelance-platforms-market-report) has grown considerably, and MarketerHire occupies a specialized corner of it, focusing only on marketing roles like SEO, paid ads, content, and email marketing.


They use specific information to find someone in their network who has already solved that exact type of problem for businesses like yours. They don’t just match keywords. They look for relevant experience and fit.


MarketerHire now uses a subscription model with plans starting at $5,000 per month. They send you one recommended marketer with a brief overview of their background, what types of brands they’ve worked with, and why they’re a fit.


This is where MarketerHire is different: you don’t get flooded with choices. The service operates through monthly retainers where companies pay for the marketer’s time instead of a recruiting fee.


The approach focuses solely on marketing talent, which means deep expertise in that specific area. But it also means you’re limited to marketing roles when your hiring needs likely extend beyond just marketing specialists.

## When to Use MarketerHire vs Dover


MarketerHire is best for companies that don’t have time to vet freelancers and want to work with senior-level marketers who can start delivering right away. Their model works well for short-term marketing projects, specific campaign needs, or when you need immediate marketing support without the commitment of hiring full-time staff.


MarketerHire makes sense when you need fractional marketing talent for 3-6 month projects, have a specific marketing challenge that requires expert intervention, or want to test new marketing channels without hiring permanently. This pricing model removes hourly guesswork, but it also means you’re committing to a monthly marketing budget of at least $5,000.


Dover is the better choice when you’re looking to hire full-time employees, build your internal team, or need recruiting help across multiple roles. It is also a strong fit when you need fractional marketing expertise right now but want that work done inside a platform that can transition into full-time hiring later. Dover’s startup experts can fill that immediate marketing gap while a fractional recruiter works on sourcing a permanent hire in parallel.


Recruiting can quickly absorb[30-40% of a founder’s time](https://www.indexventures.com/scaling-through-chaos/start-with-founder-led-recruiting) , pulling you away from product development and business strategy in ways that hurt company growth. Dover helps you hire the people you need instead of providing temporary solutions.

## FAQs


### What’s the Main Difference Between Dover and MarketerHire?


MarketerHire provides pre-vetted freelance marketers on monthly subscriptions starting at $5,000, while Dover offers fractional recruiters who help you hire full-time employees across all roles. Average hiring costs per role typically range from $2,000-$7,000.


### When Should I Choose MarketerHire Over Dover?


Choose MarketerHire for short-term marketing projects (3-6 months), testing new marketing channels, or when you need immediate marketing expertise without hiring full-time staff. It’s ideal for specific campaigns or temporary marketing support.


### How Much Does It Cost to Hire Through Dover Compared to MarketerHire Long-Term?


MarketerHire costs $60,000 annually ($5,000/month), while Dover offers a more flexible solution with average hiring costs per role typically ranging from $2,000-$7,000. Dover becomes much more cost-effective for marketing needs lasting longer than 6 months.


### Can Dover Help Me Hire Roles Beyond Marketing?


Yes, Dover’s fractional recruiters help companies hire across all roles including engineering, sales, operations, and executive positions, while MarketerHire focuses exclusively on marketing talent.


### Do Fractional Recruiters Work Better with an ATS?


Fractional recruiters work best when paired with a shared applicant tracking system that gives both the recruiter and the hiring team real-time visibility into the same candidate pipeline. Without a shared system, coordination overhead can slow things down and duplicate outreach becomes a real risk. Dover’s model pairs fractional recruiters with a free ATS so both sides work from the same pipeline from day one. The recruiter operates directly inside your system instead of maintaining a separate database, which means candidate data and sourcing history stay with your company when the engagement ends.
