# Top 20+ Recruiting Metrics That Drive Hiring Success (October 2025 Edition)

### How do I start tracking recruiting metrics if I'm currently not measuring anything?


Start with three core metrics: time-to-fill, cost-per-hire, and source effectiveness. Track your current performance for 30 days to create a baseline, then set up weekly 15-minute reviews to discuss trends and make adjustments.


### What's the difference between time-to-fill and time-to-hire?


Time-to-fill measures days from posting a job to offer acceptance (top startups hit 25-30 days), while time-to-hire tracks from first candidate contact to offer acceptance. Time-to-fill shows how well the overall process works, while time-to-hire reveals interview bottlenecks.


### When should I consider using fractional recruiters instead of hiring full-time?


Consider fractional recruiters when you need to scale hiring capacity quickly without full-time overhead, especially if your internal recruiters are managing more than 10-12 open positions simultaneously. Fractional recruiters often exceed 3-4 hires monthly while reducing cost-per-hire by 35%.


### Why do employee referrals consistently outperform other recruiting sources?


Employee referrals deliver 82% retention rates and 55% faster hiring times, because referred candidates have better cultural fit and more realistic job expectations from insider knowledge.


### How often should I review my recruiting metrics to see meaningful improvements?


Weekly reviews drive the best results. Companies reviewing metrics weekly see better improvement rates than monthly reviews. But, keep sessions to 15 minutes focused on identifying trends, bottlenecks, and immediate tactical adjustments.
