# Ashby ATS Review: Features, Pricing, and Alternatives (June 2026)

Dover combines a free ATS with access to experienced fractional recruiters, all operating within the same system. The ATS includes job distribution to 100+ boards, pipeline tracking, candidate scoring, and scheduling at no per-seat cost. For teams that find Ashby's pricing or implementation requirements hard to absorb, Dover delivers the core recruiting workflow immediately without monthly software fees or annual contracts.


Beyond the ATS, Dover serves as the infrastructure layer that fractional recruiters and recruiting agencies use to manage client hiring. Pipeline data, sourcing activity, candidate feedback, and outreach history remain in the client's ATS account, so knowledge and recruiting momentum carry forward across engagements. This gives organizations long-term ownership of their recruiting data while agencies and recruiters work from a shared system. Recruiters on the platform are backed by verified reviews from real hiring teams, providing greater transparency and confidence when selecting recruiting support.


Organizations can engage fractional recruiting expertise on a flexible basis, scaling support up or down as hiring needs change. Because recruiting activity occurs directly within Dover, teams maintain visibility into every stage of the hiring process while benefiting from specialized recruiting expertise. Whether partnering with an individual fractional recruiter or a recruiting agency, companies retain a centralized system of record that supports continuity, accountability, and consistent execution.
