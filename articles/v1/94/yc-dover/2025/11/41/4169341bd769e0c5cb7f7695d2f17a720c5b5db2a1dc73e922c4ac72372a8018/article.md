# Lever Reviews, Pricing, and Alternatives (July 2026)

### How much does Lever actually cost compared to free alternatives?


Lever charges $6-$8 per employee per month with annual costs ranging from $3,500 to over $140,000 depending on your team size and features needed. Free alternatives like Dover provide unlimited ATS access, AI résumé scoring, and job board distribution at no cost, with optional fractional recruiting services averaging $2K-$7K per hire. SHRM's recruiting benchmark puts the 2025 median cost per hire for nonexecutive positions at approximately $1,200, although actual costs vary considerably by role, organization size, and hiring method.


### What's the main difference between an ATS with CRM features and a recruiting tool?


An ATS with CRM (like Lever) focuses on software for tracking applicants and nurturing passive candidates, requiring you to handle recruiting yourself or hire separately. A recruiting tool combines the software with access to vetted recruiters who work as part of your team, giving you both tools and expert help in a single package.


### When should I consider switching from Lever to another solution?


If you're facing high costs that strain your budget, struggling with limited reporting capabilities, or need recruiting expertise beyond just software, it's worth assessing alternatives. Startups especially benefit from switching when they need faster setup, more flexibility, or want to avoid multi-year contracts.
