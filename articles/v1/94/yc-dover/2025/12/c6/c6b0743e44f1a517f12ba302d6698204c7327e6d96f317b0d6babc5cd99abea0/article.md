## What Is Fractional Recruiting?


[Fractional recruiting](https://www.dover.com/blog/what-is-a-fractional-recruiter) means hiring an experienced recruiter on a part-time or project basis. These recruiters work a set number of hours per week or month, handling sourcing, screening, and closing without a full-time commitment. Early-stage startups use this model to fill their first key roles. Growing businesses with lean HR departments use it to manage hiring surges without adding permanent headcount.


For[startups](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/s/startup.asp) , this solves a common problem: you need recruiting expertise to scale your team, but you’re not ready to commit to a full-time recruiting headcount.


The key difference from traditional[contingency agencies](https://www.dover.com/blog/contingency-recruiting-placement-fee-incentives) is focus and alignment. Contingency recruiters typically juggle multiple client searches and only get paid when they place someone. This creates an incentive to move fast and place candidates wherever possible.


Fractional recruiters work more like an extension of your team. They take on fewer clients at once, learn your company deeply, and get paid hourly instead of per-placement fees. You get[dedicated attention](https://www.dover.com/blog/dedicated-vs-multi-recruiter-hiring-model) without the overhead of a full-time salary and benefits package.


The coordination between a fractional recruiter and your internal team works best when both sides share the same applicant tracking system. A shared ATS gives real-time visibility into sourcing activity, candidate status, and pipeline progress, so the recruiter knows what your team has seen and your team knows exactly what’s in motion. Without this shared infrastructure, updates happen through email chains and check-in calls, pulling the recruiter’s focus away from the actual work of finding and closing candidates.

For startups, that means a fractional recruiter working part-time hours can cover more ground per engagement than was possible a few years ago, sourcing candidates, drafting outreach, and tracking pipeline faster across every open role.

## FAQs


### Can fractional recruiting work alongside an existing HR team?


Yes. Many growing businesses pair fractional recruiters with an in-house HR team during high-volume hiring periods. The fractional recruiter handles sourcing and screening while the internal team manages onboarding and culture fit decisions. This keeps recruiting costs predictable without adding full-time staff.


### Can I scale fractional recruiting support up or down as my hiring needs change?


Yes, most fractional recruiting services offer month-to-month or hourly engagements without long-term contracts. You can increase hours during hiring sprints and reduce support when you’re not actively recruiting. This flexibility makes fractional recruiting ideal for startups with unpredictable hiring patterns.


### Do I need an ATS before bringing in a fractional recruiter?


Not necessarily, but a shared ATS makes the engagement considerably more effective. When a fractional recruiter and your team work from the same system, both sides see the same pipeline in real time: sourcing activity, candidate status, and outreach history in one place instead of split across separate tools. Some fractional recruiting services include a free ATS the recruiter uses alongside your team from day one, which removes the setup friction and keeps all candidate data on your side of the relationship.
