# How to Hire AI Engineers for Startups in July 2026

A thoughtful onboarding process is critical when building an AI team, including creating a structured 90-day plan with technical environment setup, codebase orientation, team integration, and increasing responsibility with dedicated mentorship.


Set up their development environment before day one. AI engineers need access to data, computing resources, and specialized tools. Having everything ready shows professionalism and respect for their time.


Pair them with a technical mentor, even if it's the CTO or a senior engineer from another team. AI engineers benefit from understanding your data infrastructure, business context, and technical constraints.


Create early wins by identifying projects they can complete and deploy within their first month. This builds confidence and shows the impact they can have at your startup.


Retention requires providing continuous learning opportunities and clear career progression paths. AI engineers want to stay current with rapidly evolving techniques and see how their role will grow with the company.


Regular feedback and career development conversations are important. Discuss their interests, provide challenging projects, and create opportunities for them to mentor others as your team grows.


The[candidate experience](https://www.dover.com/blog/why-candidate-experience-matters-in-hiring) you create during hiring sets expectations for their employee experience, so maintain that same level of attention and communication.
