# The Best Kicker in Madden 26 is not who you would expect

## **Jason Sanders**


After Brandon Aubrey had his reign as the best kicker in *Madden 26,* there is a new king of the kickers. Former Dolphins kicker Jason Sanders is now the best kicker in *Madden 26* Ultimate Team, despite not being the highest-rated kicker. That is because his kick power is the highest at 93. What makes Aubrey even better is that you can use a strat card to boost his kicking power to 98, making you able to kick field goals from longer distances than ever before in Madden 26 Ultimate Team. Jason Sanders is also relatively affordable on the MUT Marketplace, with an average price of about 300k coins. Will Jason Sanders be your Ultimate Team’s kicker?

## **Shedeur Sanders gets a 93 overall card**


Shedeur Sanders is by far one of the most polarizing players in the NFL, and after winning his first-ever start against the Las Vegas Raiders, Madden has taken notice. Sanders has received a 93 overall card with an impressive 91 speed. What makes the Sanders card so good is that it has no weaknesses. Sanders has over a 90 in every major quarterback stat and will be one of the best quarterbacks in *Madden 26* for months to come.[The 93 overall Shedeur Sanders](https://www.mut.gg/players/14770-shedeur-sanders/26-37014770/) is currently going for around 3 million coins. Will Shedeur Sanders be your Madden Ultimate Team quarterback?
