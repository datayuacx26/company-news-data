## **Switch Stick**


The switch stick feature has become a staple in Madden and allows users to play defense at a level previously unseen in Madden! Switch Stick is so important due to the ability to switch onto any defender on the field. In past Madden’s a user could only control their user defender on a play. This allowed certain routes to be gamebreaking routes that an offense could revolve around. While you can still do this to an extent, the switch stick feature allows users to take away certain routes manually much easier than prior Madden’s. Once you master the Switch Stick feature, your Madden game will truly level up.
