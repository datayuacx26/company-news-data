# The Biggest Changes Coming to College Football 26

## **Cross Play Between Xbox and PlayStation Available**


**** The Dynasty game mode will always be a staple in the College Football franchise. One of the biggest complaints of College Football 25 was that dynasty mode did not have cross-platform capabilities. Xbox players could only play other Xbox players and vice versa with PlayStation. EA listened to the feedback and in College Football 26, Dynasty will be cross-platform between Xbox and PlayStation! A massive win for college football gamers, with College Football 26 releasing in under a month, on July 10th!


## **High School Mode Returns to Road to Glory**


Road to Glory is one of the most popular game modes in College Football. With EA reviving the beloved franchise with College Football 25, gamers were disappointed with the lack of any high school gameplay in the Road to Glory franchise. EA listened to the feedback and gamers will be excited to know that in College Football 26, the Road to Glory franchise will make a return to the franchise in College Football 26! This mode will come equipped with high school football gameplay, as the ability to commit and decommit to colleges as you try to raise your ranking for better schools to recruit you! Will you be playing Road to Glory on College Football 26?


## **Formation Subs are in College Football 26**


Another common criticism of College Football 25, was the lack of formation subs. Similar to Madden, you will now have the ability to set substitutions based upon the formation that you select. This will allow for easier formations, and the ability to have specific players for specific formations, thus making substitutions based upon formations throughout the game a thing of the past!
