## **Cameron Ward’s shockingly Low Rating**


When the Titans selected Miami standout Cam Ward as the first overall pick in the 2025 NFL Draft, it came as no surprise. Ward put up remarkable numbers during his last season in Miami, throwing for an insane 39 touchdowns with 4,319 yards to go alongside it. With that being said, Madden was not impressed, with Cam Ward coming into Madden 26 just as a 72 overall. To put that into perspective, the number 2 pick in the draft Travis Hunter, is an 84 overall. While all rookies are a work in progress including Ward, there are several rookies that are rated much higher than Ward who were selected even in the later rounds. This will only add fuel to Ward’s fire.
