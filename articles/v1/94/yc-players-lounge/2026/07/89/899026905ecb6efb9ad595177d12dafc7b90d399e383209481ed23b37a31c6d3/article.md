# The Madden 26 99 Overall Club

## **The 99 Overall Club Revealed**


The 99 overall Club for Madden 25 features 7 players! Madden 26 cover athlete and Philadelphia Eagles running back Saquon Barkley highlights the field, followed by quarterbacks Josh Allen and Lamar Jackson, receivers Justin Jefferson and Ja’Marr Chase. Offensive tackle Lane Johnson and the only defensive player in this year's 99 overall club, defensive end Myles Garrett, rounds out the group. Be on the lookout for Lamar Jackson in Madden 26, as he is bringing an impressive 96 speed rating with him to Madden 26, that will undoubtedly make him one of the hardest quarterbacks to contain in the new Madden.


## **No Patrick Mahomes**


The biggest story coming out of the Madden 26 club is not who made the list, but who didn’t. 3 time Super Bowl champion Patrick Mahomes was not included on this year's 99 overall club. Noticeably, fellow quarterbacks Josh Allen and Lamar Jackson were 99 overalls, making it the first time in over 4 years that Mahomes did not enter Madden as a 99 overall. While Mahomes will not be a 99 overall when Madden 26 launches, there is always a chance he can become a 99 overall as the season progresses and the ratings update. Do you think Mahomes should have been a 99 overall?


## **Madden 26 has its release date**


Originally leaked by “billbil-kun,” of Dealabs and[covered by Players’ Lounge](https://www.playerslounge.com/blog/possible-madden-26-release-date-leake) , it has now been confirmed that Madden 26 will release on August 14th. While the release of Madden 26 will be on August 14th, players who pre-order the Deluxe Edition or are EA Play Pro members, can get early access on August 11. Madden 26 will be available on PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and the Nintendo Switch 2! Which system will you be playing Madden 26 on?
