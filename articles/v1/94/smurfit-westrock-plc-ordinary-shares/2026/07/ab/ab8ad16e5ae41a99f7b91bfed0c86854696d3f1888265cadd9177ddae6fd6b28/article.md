# Smurfit Westrock Achieves Top Supplier Recognition from Roca with Five-Star Rating

Smurfit Westrock, global leader in sustainable, innovative paper-based packaging solutions, has been awarded a prestigious five-star supplier rating by Roca, one of Europe’s leading bathroom manufacturers.


Roca, renowned for producing high-quality bathroom furniture, shower trays, bathtubs, sanitary fittings and ceramic products, recognised Smurfit Westrock for its outstanding performance across a comprehensive supplier evaluation process. The assessment covered multiple criteria including product quality, delivery performance, relationship management and sustainability.


Smurfit Westrock achieved the highest overall score amongst all suppliers, receiving excellent ratings in every category and securing top marks for both local and central account management. The award reflects the strength of the partnership across multiple markets, with Smurfit Westrock providing services to Roca in Germany, Portugal, Poland, Spain, Switzerland, the UK, Morocco and Austria.


Nobert Faunie, Key Account Manager, at Smurfit Westrock, said: “We are proud to be recognised by Roca with this five-star rating, particularly as it reflects excellence across such a broad range of performance criteria. Achieving the highest score among all suppliers is a testament to the dedication of our local and central teams, who consistently deliver high-quality, innovative and sustainable packaging solutions across multiple markets.


“This recognition reinforces our commitment to supporting our customers through close collaboration, strong account management and continuous innovation, ensuring we meet their evolving needs while advancing our shared sustainability goals.”


Roman Köhler, Corporate Category Manager at the Roca Group, added: “Smurfit Westrock has demonstrated exceptional performance across all aspects of our supplier evaluation. From product quality and reliable delivery to strong relationship management and sustainability leadership, they have set a benchmark for excellence. Their ability to combine innovative design with sustainable solutions has been instrumental in supporting our operations across Europe.


“We value their partnership and look forward to continuing our collaboration as we work together to drive innovation and sustainability in our industry.”


This award further strengthens Smurfit Westrock’s position as a trusted partner for leading manufacturers, reinforcing its commitment to delivering best-in-class packaging solutions that combine performance, design excellence and environmental responsibility.
