# Forbes again names Smurfit Westrock as one of the world’s most successful companies

Smurfit Westrock has once again been included on the[Forbes Global 2000 list](https://www.forbes.com/lists/global2000/) of the world’s most successful public companies.


The prestigious ranking measures global impact and financial strength and is determined by criteria including sales, assets and market value.


Tony Smurfit, President and Chief Executive Officer, said: “We’re honored to be included on the Forbes list for a second year running. This recognition is testament to the tremendous skill and talent of my colleagues and our global leadership in innovative and sustainable packaging.”


The 2000 companies on this year’s Forbes Global 2000 list account for $56 trillion in annual revenue, $5.5 trillion in profit and $121.9 trillion in market value.


Smurfit Westrock was also recognized this week as one of TIME magazine’s World’s Most Sustainable Companies.


Smurfit Westrock operates in 40 countries worldwide and has approximately 97,000 employees.
