# PSMA x CD3 T-cell engagers show preclinical efficacy for the treatment of prostate cancer

Bergqvist P, et al. American Association for Cancer Research® (AACR) Annual Meeting, April 2025.


This poster describes preclinical data on AbCellera’s PSMA x CD3 T-cell engagers (TCEs) for the treatment of metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer. TCEs were generated by pairing diverse CD3- and PSMA-binding antibodies and using empirical testing to assess how TCE parameters impact function.


‍ **Together, the data presented show:**


- Potent *in vitro* tumor-cell killing ***,*** with one molecule showing ****** ~10x lower EC50 than benchmark
- Sustained *in vitro* activity across four rounds of serial T-cell killing
- Preclinical *in vivo* efficacy, with significant tumor growth inhibition in a xenograft mouse model
- Potential for enhanced potency when combined with costimulatory PSMA x CD28 bispecifics
