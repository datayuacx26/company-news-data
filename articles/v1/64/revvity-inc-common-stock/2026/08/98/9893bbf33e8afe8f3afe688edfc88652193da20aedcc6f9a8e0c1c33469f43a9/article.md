### Revvity Launches SuperFlex Prenatal Screening System, Expanding Access to Preeclampsia Testing


08/10/2026


[Download this Press Release (opens in new window) PDF 99 KB](https://s202.q4cdn.com/908952201/files/doc_news/Revvity-Launches-SuperFlex-Prenatal-Screening-System-Expanding-Access-to-Preeclampsia-Testing-2026-LgYyMR-53t-2026.pdf)


- *Revvity’s new maternal and fetal biochemistry testing platform*
- *Designed for accessibility, simplicity, and flexibility*
- *Purpose-built for small to mid-sized labs and clinics to enable this capability in-house for the first time*


**WALTHAM, Mass. — August 10, 2026 —**[Revvity, Inc.](http://www.revvity.com/) today announced the launch of the[SuperFlex™ prenatal screening system](https://www.revvity.com/category/preeclampsia-testing#superflex) , a compact, CE-IVDR certified immunoassay instrument designed to bring high-quality screening to laboratories and clinics seeking reliable, rapid solutions that fit lower-volume workflows.


The system supports testing for preeclampsia, a condition that affects between 2% and 8% of pregnancies and is increasing globally due to rising maternal age and conditions such as diabetes and hypertension. Despite international guidelines recommending screening, access has historically been limited to high-throughput centralized laboratories. SuperFlex addresses these gaps by delivering three core advantages:


- **Accessible entry** — affordable instrument cost with just one annual maintenance, and no reagent wastage even when sample volumes are low
- **Operational simplicity** — a user-friendly design that minimizes training requirements, to be seamlessly integrated with[Revvity’s LifeCycle™](https://www.revvity.com/category/risk-calculation-software) software for streamlined risk assessment and LIMS data transfer
- **Flexible throughput** — continuous sample loading delivers results in as little as 14 minutes per test, accommodating both urgent and routine workflows


"This SuperFlex offering represents a significant addition to our prenatal portfolio," said Anna Godenhjelm, vice president and general manager of reproductive health at Revvity. "By removing the financial and operational barriers to in-house prenatal screening, we are giving more women access to potentially life-saving testing regardless of where they receive care."


The platform also includes a test menu for both first- and second-trimester biochemical prenatal screening, enabling a versatile, cost-effective, random-access solution well-suited for decentralized testing.


The SuperFlex platform will initially be available in all markets that accept CE-marked instruments, with expansion into Asia-Pacific planned for late 2026.
