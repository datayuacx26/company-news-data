# What YC Looks For: Ankit Gupta on Turning Ideas Into Paying Customers

Over the weekend at[HackMIT 2025](https://hackmit.org/) , Dedalus Labs joined a standout group of sponsors and sat down with[Ankit Gupta](https://x.com/GuptaAnkitV) , Y Combinator’s newest General Partner.


In a conversation with Dedalus Labs CEO,[Cathy Di](https://x.com/itsCathyDi) , Ankit Gupta shares how he works with founders, what YC looks for in a founding team, and the startup trends he has observed.
