**The Member Question:** During a welcome call, the member mentions an upcoming root canal and asks if his dentist is in network. AI recognizes this requires verification.


**AI Response:** "That's important to verify, especially with a procedure coming up. I'll have our team check this immediately and call you back with confirmation."


**Dashboard Integration:** The question appears in your careCycle dashboard with complete context: member information, plan details, call transcript, and the specific question asked.


**Resolution Options:** After verifying through carrier portals, your team has two choices:


**Dispatch careCycle Callback:** Send the AI assistant to call the member back with the verified answer. Result: "Hi Alex, this is Sarah again. I checked with Jason about Dr. Johnson in San Francisco - he's indeed in network for your root canal. You're all set."


**Manual Resolution:** Handle complex situations with a direct call using careCycle's integrated softphone system, with full recording and transcription. Result: Personal conversation with complete documentation for compliance.


Both options ensure members receive accurate, verified information while maintaining appropriate human oversight based on question complexity.
