# Introducing The careCycle Softphone: Call Members Directly From Inquiries

‍ **Watch how the softphone eliminates tab-switching and keeps every member conversation in one place.**


You're in the inquiries dashboard. The question is right there. The member's number is right there.


Copy. Paste into your phone system. Switch tabs. Take the call. Come back. Type what happened.


**There's a better way.**


‍


### We Heard The Feedback


Inquiries resolve thousands of member questions automatically. AI handles the callback. Tracks the resolution. Works great.


But some questions need a real conversation. Follow-ups. Nuanced explanations. The human touch.


That's when you told us:


- *"I keep my phone system open in another tab"*
- *"The call history doesn't sync back to careCycle"*
- *"I'm tracking the same member across three systems"*


Your member's full story should live in one place. Not scattered.


‍


### What Changes Today


See a manual inquiry. Click **Call** right there in the dashboard.


The softphone dials. Your member picks up. You talk.


**While you're talking:**


- Every word gets transcribed
- The recording saves to the inquiry
- Resolution status updates automatically
- The member timeline grows


When the call ends, you're done. No logging. No switching tabs. The inquiry is resolved. The history is complete.


‍


## Why This Matters


**Your agents move faster.** No tab switching. No manual logging. Click. Talk. Done.


**Your data stays complete.** Every conversation tracked. No gaps in the member story. When they call back, you see everything.


**AI gets smarter.** It learns from real conversations. It becomes a better teammate.


**You stay compliant.** Every call recorded. Every interaction documented. Audit trail built automatically.


‍


## When Members Call You Back


You control the routing:


- Send calls to specific agents
- Route to AI assistants after hours
- Set custom rules by inquiry type


One platform. Your rules.


‍


## See It In Action


Member calls confused about insulin costs. AI creates an inquiry. Realizes it needs a human touch.


**The old way:** Copy number. Switch to phone system. Call. Hang up. Switch back. Type summary. Mark resolved.


**The new way:** Click Call. Answer question. Done. (AI transcribes everything.)


The inquiry closes automatically. The transcript saves. Next time this member has a question, whoever helps them sees the full context.
