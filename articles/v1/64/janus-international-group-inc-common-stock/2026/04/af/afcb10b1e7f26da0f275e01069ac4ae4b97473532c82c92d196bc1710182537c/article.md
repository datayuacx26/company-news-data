# When to Upgrade: A Guide to Self-Storage Smart Lock Retrofits

The self-storage industry has entered a new era — one where tenant expectations, operational efficiency, and security standards are being reshaped by smart technology. At the center of that shift is self-storage smart lock technology. For owners of existing facilities, the question is no longer if you should modernize access, but when.


Retrofitting with the[Nokē Smart Entry](https://www.janusintl.com/products/noke) ecosystem, including[Nokē Infinitē](https://www.janusintl.com/nok%C4%93-infinit%C4%93) and[Nokē Ion](https://www.janusintl.com/noke-ion) , offers a way to future-proof your property without the cost and disruption of a full rebuild. Understanding the right moment to make that investment can significantly impact revenue, tenant satisfaction, and long-term asset value.


### The Market Has Shifted and So Has The Market For Smart Storage Access


Today’s tenants are accustomed to managing nearly every aspect of their lives from a smartphone. From banking to travel to home security, mobile-first experiences are the norm. Self-storage facilities are no exception.


Nokē Smart Entry's self-storage smart lock system allows tenants to access gates and individual units using a mobile app instead of keys, padlocks, or memorized gate codes. Whether through battery-powered options like Nokē Infinitē or hardwired solutions such as Nokē Ion, the experience is frictionless and keyless. This level of convenience is increasingly viewed as a baseline expectation rather than a premium perk.


> " We have cameras everywhere. One of the biggest things for us has been the Nokē Smart Entry product. Access continuity was really critical. With a tech background, I was really focused on people opening one single app to access everything that they need. Janus delivers that product, and they deliver it very, very well." - Nick Fletcher, Fletcher Storage


Facilities still relying solely on traditional locks can feel outdated to prospective renters comparing options online. If your facility operates in a competitive market, the right time to begin a smart lock retrofit may be when customer expectations have already outpaced your infrastructure.


### Operational Strain Is a Clear Signal Your Facility Needs a Smart Lock Upgrade


As portfolios grow and staffing models evolve, many operators are managing more locations with fewer on-site personnel. Manual lock checks, lost keys, cut locks, and after-hours access issues create operational strain that does not scale well.


Nokē's cloud-based smart lock system automates many of these tasks across both Nokē Infinitē and Nokē Ion deployments. Digital lock checks, real-time alerts, and remote access management reduce the need for physical walkthroughs and manual verification — giving teams visibility and control across a single site or an entire portfolio.


If your staff is spending too much time managing access-related issues, or if labor efficiency is becoming a concern, that is a strong signal that a self-storage smart lock retrofit is the right next step.


### Security Concerns Should Prompt Proactive Action


Security is one of the strongest drivers of tenant trust and brand reputation. Traditional padlocks can unintentionally signal whether a unit is occupied, delinquent, or vacant. That visibility can create unnecessary risk.


Smart locking solutions like Nokē Infinitē and Nokē Ion bring a consistent look across units while providing detailed digital activity logs. Features such as automated alerts, motion detection options, and time-stamped access records give operators more insight and control than traditional systems can offer.


If your facility has experienced break-ins, lock tampering, or recurring access disputes, or if you want to reduce risk before an incident occurs, the right time to retrofit is before security becomes a problem rather than after.


### Capital Improvements Create Natural Smart Lock Retrofit Opportunities


Smart lock retrofits are most effective when aligned with broader asset improvement strategies. If you are already planning door replacements, electrical upgrades, security enhancements, or a shift toward contactless operations, integrating smart access at the same time increases return while minimizing disruption.


Nokē Infinitē is designed for streamlined retrofits where battery-powered flexibility is preferred, while Nokē Ion offers a hardwired solution for facilities that want constant power and expanded onsite features. Both options are compatible with many existing door configurations, allowing owners to modernize without large-scale structural changes.


*Can you retrofit smart locks in phases?* Yes. The flexibility of Nokē's ecosystem makes it straightforward to upgrade in phases or align smart lock installation with other capital projects — giving you full control over scope, timeline, and budget.


### Increasing Asset Value and Long-Term Flexibility


Smart access is no longer just an operational upgrade. It is an asset-level differentiator. Facilities equipped with modern, cloud-based self-storage smart lock systems are viewed as more resilient, scalable, and future-ready by investors and buyers alike.


The Nokē Smart Entry platform integrates with major property management systems and supports a mix of battery-powered and hardwired locking solutions. Whether you plan to hold the asset, expand your portfolio, or prepare for a future sale, modern smart lock access control strengthens your overall value proposition.


> “We’re definitely attuned to making a long-term investment in technology that’s going to reduce our operating costs and improve our customer experience.” - Luke Corsiglia, Farragut Investments


### Smart Lock Unlocking Premium Pricing Opportunities


Many operators are rethinking unit tiering and looking for features that justify higher rates. Self-storage smart locks and enhanced security can be positioned as premium offerings — particularly for high-value storage, business customers, or climate-controlled units.


Digital key sharing, automated overlocking, and detailed access history all add perceived value for tenants who prioritize convenience and control. Retrofitting with Nokē Infinitē or Nokē Ion can open the door to new pricing strategies without expanding your physical footprint.


### The Best Time Is Often Before You Are Forced To Act


One of the most common patterns in facility upgrades is reactive decision-making. Operators wait until systems fail, competitors pull ahead, or tenants complain. The most successful retrofits are proactive.


The Nokē Smart Entry ecosystem is designed for both new construction and existing facilities, giving owners the flexibility to modernize on their own timeline instead of under pressure.


### Is a Self-Storage Smart Lock Retrofit Right For Your Facility?


Retrofitting your storage facility with Nokē, whether through Infinitē, Ion, or a hybrid approach, is not just a technology upgrade. It is a strategic decision about how your facility operates, competes, and grows over the next decade.


If your facility is feeling operational strain, struggling to keep pace with shifting tenant expectations, or planning for repositioning, the right time to invest in a smart lock retrofit may be now.


Self-storage smart locks are not the future of the industry. They are the present.
