# Self-Storage Offices that Drive More Rentals: Converting, Renovating, and Modeling

If your self-storage facility is underperforming or visually outdated, a targeted office remodel is a great first step toward improving the trust and perception of quality of your facility to both current and future tenants.


## When to Consider a Self-Storage Office Renovation


Remodeling self-storage offices is less about aesthetics and more about operational efficiency, tenant experience, and cost control. The design should support leasing velocity, security, and staff productivity. If your facility is more than 15 years old and hasn't been renovated or remodeled since opening, there's a strong change that you could be unintentionally sending a message to customers that your facility doesn't lacks the quality, security, and level of care that they're seeking. Dated carpeting and ceilings, older technology, clutter, exposed wiring, poor lighting and lack of visible security monitoring are all tell-tell signs that your office may be off-putting to renters. The good news is, office remodels or renovations are a lower cost, high impact way to help driver higher rates at your facility.


## What to Prioritize in a Self-Storage Office Remodel


The office operates as a hybrid of retail checkout, security control room, and administrative workspace. That hybrid function should dictate the entire floor plan, furniture selection, technology layout, and even finishes. In other words, the space has to sell, secure, and support operations simultaneously—every square foot should be working for you.


A well-performing office does three things:


1. Converts walk-ins to tenants quickly


The reception and leasing area should function like a high-efficiency retail counter. Clear sightlines to the front door, intuitive queuing, and a clean, organized countertop all help staff greet prospects immediately and move them through the leasing process without friction. Digital tools—such as online reservations, smart entry demonstrations, and clear unit availability displays—reinforce professionalism and make it easy for customers to say “yes” on the spot.


2. Enables staff to monitor the facility continuously


Because the office also serves as a security hub, staff should have direct visual and digital oversight of key access points, drive aisles, and high-traffic corridors. Strategically positioned windows, an ergonomic monitoring station, and properly sized, wall-mounted surveillance screens allow managers to keep eyes on the property while handling leases, payments, and tenant inquiries. Integrating modern access control systems and smart entry technology further streamlines monitoring and reduces blind spots.


3. Highlights security and commitment to cleanliness and quality visually


The first impression of your office should immediately communicate that the facility is well managed and secure. Bright, uniform lighting, clean surfaces, updated finishes, and visible security elements—such as cameras, smart keypads, and smart unit security—signal to tenants that their belongings will be protected. Thoughtful merchandising of packing supplies, clear signage, and clutter-free work areas reinforce a culture of organization, safety, and long-term asset care.


## Office Remodels and Renovations ROI


### Higher Rental Rates


If your facility competes with newer properties:


- A modern office supports **rate justification**


- Customers anchor perception of the entire facility based on the front office
- You may be able to raise rents **5–15%**


, especially for climate-controlled units


### **ROI Example**


Assume:


- Remodel cost: $40,000
- Facility: 500 units
- Avg rent: $120/month


Post-remodel impact:


- 5% rent increase → +$3,000/month
- 5 additional rentals/month → +$600/month
- Retail/insurance uplift → +$400/month


**Total monthly gain: ~$4,000**
→ Annual: ~$48,000


**ROI ≈ 120% in year one**


This is a *good-case scenario*


, but not unrealistic in underperforming properties.
