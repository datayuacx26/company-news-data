# Revolutionizing Storage: Titan Mega Storage's Automated Experience

When operator Larry Keller set out to build **Titan Mega Storage** in Bakersfield, CA, he wasn’t aiming for incremental improvement—he was aiming for a breakthrough. By rethinking unit design, access control, and tenant movement through the property, he created a facility that feels seamless, intuitive, and modern.


## **A Smarter Way to Move Through Storage**


Titan Mega Storage’s signature feature is its **large, dual‑ended units** , each equipped with both a walk door and a roll‑up door. Tenants can enter from either side, pull through, or stage their move however they want—turning what’s normally a hassle into a smooth experience.


## **100% Phone‑Based Access—No Codes, No Keypads**


Every gate and door is controlled through **smartphone access** , with adjustable activation distances that make each entry effortless. As tenants approach, doors open at just the right moment—no stopping, no fumbling, no guesswork.


Even better, the system stays functional **during power outages** , keeping operations steady when other facilities grind to a halt.


## **Low‑Maintenance Power with Direct‑Drive Doors**


To handle the oversized roll‑up doors, Larry chose the **Pantheon direct‑drive system** from Janus International. With no belts, pulleys, or complex bracing, it delivers smooth, reliable operation while reducing long‑term maintenance costs.


## **Safety and Awareness Built In**


The facility layers multiple safety technologies, including:


- High‑precision photo‑eyes


- Thermal motion sensors


- 24/7 exterior camera coverage


Everything responds instantly and intelligently to keep both equipment and tenants protected.


## **One Integrated Ecosystem, Zero Friction**


Working with **Janus International** , Larry built a fully connected system combining smart access, door automation, safety optics, and real‑time monitoring. The result is a facility that runs cleaner, breaks less, and delivers a premium tenant experience without added complexity.
