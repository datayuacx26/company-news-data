# Issues Comprehensive Business Update


Cricket’s LA28 Olympic inclusion and growth at US collegiate club level provide strategic context for platform development


LAFAYETTE, Colo., April 27, 2026[(GLOBE NEWSWIRE)](https://www.globenewswire.com/) -- urban-gro, Inc. **(Nasdaq: UGRO)** (“urban-gro” or the “Company”), which operates its sports and media business under the Flash brand, today is providing a comprehensive business update to investors regarding (i) the Company’s strategic transition into a sports, media, and experiential marketing platform, (ii) the restoration of its Nasdaq listing compliance as previously disclosed in Forms 8-K, (iii) the Company’s near-term operating focus around Lanka Premier League (“LPL”) Season 6, and (iv) strategic context for the Company’s cricket platform, including and separately, the inclusion of cricket at the Los Angeles 2028 Olympic Games and the expansion of cricket at the US collegiate club level.
