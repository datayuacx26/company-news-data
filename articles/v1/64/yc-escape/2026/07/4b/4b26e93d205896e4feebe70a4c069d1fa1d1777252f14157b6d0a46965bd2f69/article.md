# Introducing Escape's Partner Program

**Building offensive security into every stack, together.**


The best security outcomes happen when small teams aren't working alone. Our partners play a critical role in getting offensive security engineering into the hands of the teams that need it most, helping them cover the vast surfaces they could never cover themselves.


They identify where Escape fits in a customer's stack, integrate it into existing workflows, and build the processes that make continuous testing stick for each customer's specific engineering org. It's a community united by one goal: helping security teams do more with the people they already have.


We're excited to introduce the next chapter of that community — the Escape partner program, officially open as of June 1, 2026. It's built to help partners of every type grow alongside Escape while delivering real, measurable impact to customers. This program builds on the relationships we've already started and gives partners a clearer, more flexible path to deliver that impact at scale.


## A partner program designed around mindshare and being frictionless


Escape’s channel program is built with partners first. We want to be easy to remember and easy to work with. This means every partner gets sales and technical training, access to a sandbox environment, and a clear plan to achieve joint business goals.


> "At Technium, we’re always searching for technology partners that complement our consultative approach to enterprise security. Escape's Channel Program gives us another way to bring innovative application and API security capabilities into customer conversations, while working alongside a partner that's truly committed to our success. We're looking forward to exploring where Escape fits within our customer security strategies as we create new, mutually beneficial opportunities." - **Technium**


Every partner who joins the Escape partner program can expect:


- Deal registration and conflict rules that are fair and enforced
- Guaranteed margins
- Dedicated sales and technical reps from Escape on every customer call
- Free POCs for every customer
- Assistance with quoting
- Renewal and expansion strategy
- Competitive differentiation training
- Marketing and demand generation support


There's no certification requirement or multi-tier maze to climb before you get real support. You join, you get trained, and you hit the ground running.


> "Our clients are building software faster than their security teams can manually keep up with, and that gap is where real risk lives. Escape gives us a way to bring continuous, offensive coverage of their application and API attack surface to the table, not another point-in-time scan. Partnering through Escape's channel program lets EverSec lead these conversations with technology our customers actually need, and deliver outcomes we can stand behind." - **Stephen Harrison, Managing Partner & CEO, EverSec Group**


### Partnerships are a two-way street


A partnership only works if it flows both directions. Escape is committed to sharing leads with partners, not just asking partners to source them. For us to truly scale security operations where it matters most, we want partners who grow with us and customers who win because of it.


> "What stood out about Escape is that it fills a genuine gap in the offensive security conversations we're already having with clients, and it does so in a way that's easy for our teams to position and deliver. Their channel program was built to make partners successful, not to treat us as a transaction. For ConRes, that means a differentiated offering and a real services motion. For our customers, it means continuous, evidence-backed coverage they can trust. Contact Escape and ConRes team for a comprehensive AI security conversation." - **Aaron Leiberman, Chief Technology Officer, ConRes IT Solutions**


### Led by someone who's built channel programs from the ground up


Jeff Miller is running the program as Escape's Head of Channels. Before joining Escape, Jeff started a channel security services company from scratch and ran Northeast U.S. and Canada channel partnerships for Arctic Wolf and N-able Technologies. He knows what makes a partner program work seamlessly so every new partner is in the safest of hands with the Escape team.


> "The channel program at Escape is built on a simple belief: the best way to help a security team is through a partner who already has their trust. AppSec has been underserved by vendors more interested in renewals than outcomes. We built our channel program around mutual revenue growth and actual customer results, not partner tiers and training mandates. That distinction matters to how we recruit, enable, and support every partner in the program." - **Jeff Miller, Head of Channels, Escape**
