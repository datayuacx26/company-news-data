Most Indian employers have heard of the UAN. They know it has something to do with provident fund and that employees need it for PF withdrawals and transfers. What most employers do not realize is that the UAN is one of the most powerful hiring tools available to them, and it is hiding in plain sight.


The Universal Account Number is not just a PF identifier. It is a verified, government maintained record of every formal employer a person has ever worked for. Every company that contributed PF on behalf of an employee is logged in the EPFO database against that UAN, along with dates of joining, dates of leaving, member IDs, and contribution status.


This means that when a candidate tells you they spent three years at a reputed company, you can verify that claim in minutes. Not by calling their former HR department and hoping someone picks up. Not by inspecting an experience certificate that could have been printed from a template. But by checking the actual EPFO database, which records real monetary contributions made by real employers.


If the employer actually contributed PF for this person during the claimed period, the record will be there. If they did not, it will not be. No story, no forged letter, and no rehearsed interview answer can change what the database shows.


## Why Employment Claims Are the Hardest Thing to Verify Traditionally


Of all the things a candidate can lie about, employment history is the hardest to catch through traditional methods. Here is why.


Education fraud is relatively straightforward to detect. You contact the university, they check their records, and they confirm whether the degree was issued. Criminal records can be searched through court databases. Address verification can be done through field visits or digital checks.


But verifying employment history traditionally requires calling the candidate’s previous employer’s HR department and asking them to confirm dates, designation, and reason for leaving. This sounds simple in theory. In practice, it is a nightmare.


Many companies, especially large ones, have policies that restrict what HR can share with external callers. They will confirm that the person worked there and provide dates of employment, but nothing more. No performance details, no reason for leaving, no information about whether the person was a star performer or a walking liability.


Small companies are even harder. The “HR department” might be a single person who handles everything from payroll to office supplies. They may not respond to calls or emails from unknown numbers. The company may have shut down entirely. The person listed as the reference might be a friend of the candidate who agreed to play the role.


And then there are the candidates who create entirely fictional employers. In an era where anyone can register a domain name, set up a professional looking website, and print letterhead, fabricating a previous employer is disturbingly easy.


This is where UAN verification changes the game completely.


## How UAN Verification Works and What It Shows


Every employee in India who earns up to Rs 15,000 per month (the current wage ceiling for mandatory PF) receives PF contributions from their employer. This contribution is recorded under the employee’s UAN in the EPFO database. Over time, the UAN accumulates a complete employment trail.


When you verify a candidate’s UAN through a platform like ID Verify by SalaryBox, the EPFO database returns a set of information that is remarkably detailed.


You see the employee’s name as registered with EPFO, which you can immediately compare against the name they gave you during the application process. A mismatch here is a significant red flag because EPFO names are verified during KYC linking.


You see a list of all employers who have contributed PF for this person, identified by their establishment IDs and names. Each employer entry includes the date the employee joined and the date they left (if applicable). This gives you a complete timeline of their formal employment history.


You see whether each employment record is active or inactive, which tells you whether the person is still receiving PF contributions from a current employer. If they are applying for a job with you while still actively employed somewhere else and did not disclose it, the UAN will reveal it.


You also see the KYC status, which indicates whether the employee has linked their Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account to their UAN. A fully KYC compliant UAN adds another layer of identity validation.


## Six Things UAN Verification Catches That Interviews Cannot


### Fabricated employment history


This is the most straightforward catch. A candidate says they worked at Company X for three years. You check their UAN. No PF contributions from Company X appear anywhere in the record. The employment was either completely fabricated, or they worked there on a cash basis with no formal employment relationship, which contradicts the “senior role” they described in the interview.


### Inflated tenure


A candidate claims five years at a company, but the UAN shows PF contributions from that company spanning only two years and three months. They did work there, but they significantly exaggerated how long. This is one of the most common forms of resume fraud because candidates assume nobody will verify the exact dates.


### Concealed employers


Sometimes the interesting thing is not what the UAN confirms but what it reveals that the candidate did not mention. If their resume shows three employers over the past eight years, but the UAN shows PF contributions from five different companies during that period, there are two employers the candidate chose to hide. Why? Usually because they were terminated, or their tenure was embarrassingly short, or something happened that they do not want you to know about.


### Dual employment


The UAN can show overlapping employment periods. If PF contributions were being made by two different employers during the same months, the person was either working two jobs simultaneously, or something unusual was going on with their PF records. Either way, it warrants a conversation.


### Misrepresented job level


While the UAN does not directly show job titles, it reveals the size and type of establishment through the employer’s registered name and ID. If a candidate claims they were a “Regional Manager” at a company, but the UAN shows that the establishment is a small proprietorship with fewer than 20 employees, the “regional” part of that title deserves questioning.


### No formal employment despite claims


Some candidates claim years of experience but have never been in a formal employment relationship. Their UAN either does not exist, or it was created recently with no contribution history. This is particularly telling when the candidate has described roles that would certainly have PF contributions, such as working at a retail chain, a hospital, or a manufacturing plant.


## When UAN Verification Is Most Valuable


Not every hire requires the same level of scrutiny. UAN verification is most valuable in specific situations.


When hiring for roles that require genuine experience. If you are hiring an accountant, a store manager, a nurse, or a machine operator, the candidate’s claimed experience directly affects whether they can do the job safely and competently. UAN verification confirms whether that experience is real.


When the candidate’s resume seems too good to be true. A candidate with a perfectly linear career progression, all at well known companies, with no gaps and steadily increasing responsibilities, might be the real deal. Or they might have crafted a fiction. UAN verification settles the question quickly.


When you are hiring someone who will handle money, inventory, or sensitive data. People in positions of trust need verified backgrounds. If a candidate claims three years as an accounts executive and the UAN shows nothing from that employer, you do not want this person anywhere near your finances.


When you are hiring at a level where checking references is impractical. Many small business owners do not have the time, experience, or confidence to conduct professional reference checks. UAN verification provides much of the same information automatically, without the awkward phone calls and uncertain reliability of spoken references.


When hiring in high turnover industries. Retail, hospitality, logistics, and healthcare see frequent job changes. Candidates in these sectors may have worked at five or six employers in the past few years. Manually verifying each one would take weeks. UAN verification shows the entire trail in minutes.


## How to Use UAN Verification in Your Hiring Process


Making UAN verification part of your hiring workflow is straightforward.


During the interview or application stage, ask the candidate for their UAN along with other identity details like PAN and Voter ID. Most formally employed candidates know their UAN because they have used it for PF balance checks, withdrawals, or transfers.


If a candidate says they do not have a UAN, pay attention to the context. A genuine fresher straight out of college or ITI will not have one, and that is perfectly normal. But a candidate claiming five years of experience at formal employers should have a UAN. If they say they do not, ask why. The answer will either be reasonable (they worked entirely in cash based roles) or suspicious (they are trying to avoid having their employment claims checked).

## Frequently Asked Questions


### Can HR at a previous company see that I checked a candidate’s UAN?


No. UAN verification through authorized platforms queries the EPFO database. It does not notify the candidate’s current or previous employer. The check is confidential between you and the verification platform.


### What if the candidate worked at a company that did not pay PF?


Some small employers do not contribute PF, either because they fall below the employee threshold or because they operate informally. In such cases, the UAN will not show contributions from that employer. This does not necessarily mean the candidate lied, but it does mean you cannot independently verify that particular employment claim through UAN. You may need to follow up with a direct reference check for that specific employer.


### Can candidates manipulate their UAN records?


No. UAN records are maintained by EPFO and reflect actual monetary contributions made by employers. An employee cannot add, remove, or alter employer entries in their UAN record. This is what makes UAN verification so reliable. It is based on money that actually changed hands, not documents that can be fabricated.


### What if the candidate is a freelancer or worked on contract?


Freelancers and independent contractors typically do not have PF contributions because they are not classified as employees. If a candidate tells you they freelanced for three years and their UAN shows no contributions during that period, it is consistent with their story. UAN verification is most useful for verifying claims of formal, salaried employment.


### Do I need the candidate’s permission to check their UAN?


Yes. Under the DPDP Act 2023, you must obtain written consent from the candidate before processing their personal data, which includes running verification checks. A simple consent form explaining which checks will be conducted is sufficient.


### How far back does UAN history go?


UAN was introduced in 2014. For employment before that, EPFO records may be linked through older member IDs, but coverage is less comprehensive. For any employment from 2014 onwards at a PF registered establishment, UAN records are generally reliable and complete.
