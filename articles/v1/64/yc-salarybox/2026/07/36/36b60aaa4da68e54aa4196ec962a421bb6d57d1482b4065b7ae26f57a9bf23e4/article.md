## Why Employee Identity Verification Is Non-Negotiable in 2026


India’s job market is growing faster than ever, and so is the sophistication of identity fraud. According to a 2025 EY study on employment fraud, discrepancies were detected in 30 to 35 percent of resumes screened across industries. The BFSI sector alone saw a high incidence of employment history discrepancies, including inflated tenures, undisclosed exits, and fabricated past employers.


For employers, the consequences of hiring someone with a fake identity are severe. A single fraudulent hire can lead to financial embezzlement, data theft, regulatory penalties, and even criminal liability under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.


The good news is that identity verification has become dramatically easier. Government databases for PAN, Aadhaar, Voter ID, and Driving Licence are now accessible through authorised digital platforms. What once required physical visits to government offices and weeks of waiting can now be completed in minutes.


This guide walks you through each identity check, explains what it reveals, and shows you the fastest way to run all of them in a single workflow.


## The 4 Identity Documents Every Indian Employer Should Verify


### 1. PAN (Permanent Account Number)


**What it verifies:** Tax identity, full legal name, date of birth, and PAN status (active, inactive, or deactivated).


**Why it matters:** PAN is the most reliable single document for confirming a candidate’s legal identity. It is issued by the Income Tax Department and linked to the candidate’s financial history. Employers deducting TDS on salary must verify employee PANs, using incorrect PAN details can trigger mismatch notices from the Income Tax Department and attract a higher TDS rate of 20 percent under Section 206AA.


**What a PAN check reveals:** – Full name as registered with the Income Tax Department – Date of birth – PAN status (active or deactivated) – Whether the PAN belongs to an individual, company, or other entity type


**Red flags to watch for:** Name on PAN does not match the name on the resume or offer letter. PAN status shows as deactivated or inoperative (which happens when PAN is not linked with Aadhaar). Multiple PAN numbers associated with the same individual, this is illegal under the Income Tax Act.


### 2. Voter ID (Electoral Photo Identity Card — EPIC)


**What it verifies:** Electoral identity, residential address, age, and photograph.


**Why it matters:** The Voter ID is one of the most widely held identity documents in India, issued by the Election Commission. It provides an independent address proof and age confirmation that can be cross-referenced against other documents. Unlike Aadhaar, the Voter ID is not linked to biometric data, making it a useful secondary verification source.


**What a Voter ID check reveals:** – Full name and father’s or husband’s name – Residential address as registered with the Election Commission – Age or date of birth – Assembly and parliamentary constituency


**Red flags to watch for:** Address on Voter ID significantly different from the address declared by the candidate. Name spellings that do not match across documents. Voter ID number that does not return a valid record, this could indicate a forged document.


### 3. Driving Licence (DL)


**What it verifies:** Transport identity, address, date of birth, and licence validity.


**Why it matters:** A Driving Licence is especially critical for roles involving driving, delivery, field operations, or the use of company vehicles. But even for non-driving roles, the DL serves as a strong identity proof because it is issued after in-person verification at the Regional Transport Office (RTO). Verifying a DL also confirms whether the licence is currently valid, expired, suspended, or cancelled.


**What a DL check reveals:** – Full name, date of birth, and address – Licence number and issuing RTO – Vehicle classes authorised (two-wheeler, four-wheeler, commercial) – Validity period and current status – Any endorsements, suspensions, or cancellations


**Red flags to watch for:** Expired or suspended licence, especially for roles that require driving. Licence class does not match the type of vehicle the role requires. DL number does not return a valid record from the transport database.


### 4. UAN (Universal Account Number)


**What it verifies:** Provident fund identity, employment history, and employer linkage.


**Why it matters:** The UAN is issued by the Employees’ Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) and is linked to every PF account the employee has ever held. Verifying a candidate’s UAN is one of the most effective ways to cross-check their claimed employment history. Each employer they have worked with would have made PF contributions under this UAN, creating a verifiable employment trail.


**What a UAN check reveals:** – Full name and date of birth as registered with EPFO – Linked PF account numbers – Current and previous employers who contributed to the PF account – KYC status (Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account linking)


**Red flags to watch for:** UAN does not exist or returns no records, the candidate may have never been formally employed, which contradicts their resume claims. Employer names linked to the UAN do not match the employment history on the resume. KYC details under the UAN do not match the candidate’s submitted documents.


## Manual vs. Digital Verification: Why Manual Methods Fail


Many small businesses in India still verify documents manually — asking candidates to bring original documents for physical inspection. This approach has three critical flaws:


**It cannot detect sophisticated fakes.** Modern document forgery has become remarkably advanced. High-quality fake PAN cards, Voter IDs, and even Driving Licences can pass visual inspection. Digital verification queries the actual government database, which a forged document cannot fool.


**It is slow and error-prone.** Manual verification depends on the HR person’s ability to spot inconsistencies across multiple documents. With hiring volumes increasing, the chance of human error grows proportionally. A digital platform returns results in seconds with zero subjectivity.


**It creates no audit trail.** If a verified employee later turns out to have submitted fake documents, manual verification provides no defensible record of what was checked and when. Digital platforms generate timestamped reports that serve as compliance evidence under the DPDP Act 2023.


## How to Verify Employee Identity Online: Step-by-Step


Here is the practical workflow for running all four identity checks digitally:


**Step 1: Collect candidate details.** Gather the candidate’s full name, date of birth, mobile number, and the identity document numbers you plan to verify (PAN number, Voter ID number, DL number, UAN).


**Step 2: Obtain written consent.** Under the DPDP Act 2023, you must secure explicit consent from the candidate before running any identity checks. Use a clear consent form that specifies which documents will be verified and why.


**Step 3: Choose your verification platform.** You need a platform that queries government databases directly, not one that relies on visual document matching or OCR alone. The platform should support all four checks (PAN, Voter ID, DL, UAN) in a single workflow, so you do not need to juggle multiple tools.


**Step 4: Run the checks.** Enter the candidate’s details and select the checks you want to run. A good platform will process all four simultaneously and return results within minutes.


**Step 5: Review the cross-record analysis.** The real power of running multiple checks together is cross-referencing. Does the name on the PAN match the name on the Voter ID? Does the date of birth on the DL match the UAN record? Discrepancies across records are a stronger fraud signal than any single check in isolation.


**Step 6: Download and store the report.** Generate a PDF report of the verification results and store it securely in the employee’s digital file. This report serves as your compliance documentation.


## What Happens When You Skip Identity Verification


The cost of not verifying is not hypothetical. Industry data paints a clear picture:


A single bad hire costs Indian companies between Rs 5 lakh and Rs 50 lakh when you factor in recruitment costs, training investment, lost productivity, potential legal fees, and the cost of re-hiring. For small businesses, this can be the difference between profitability and closure.


Beyond financial loss, hiring someone with a fraudulent identity exposes your business to legal liability. If that employee causes harm, whether through financial fraud, data theft, or workplace misconduct and you failed to conduct basic identity verification, you can be held liable for negligent hiring.


In regulated industries like BFSI, failing to verify employee identities can result in regulatory sanctions from RBI or SEBI, including fines and loss of operating licences.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### Is online identity verification legally valid in India?


Yes. Digital identity verification through authorised platforms that query government databases (Income Tax, Election Commission, Transport, EPFO) is legally valid and widely accepted. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 provides the legal framework for collecting and processing personal data for employment verification, provided you obtain written consent from the candidate.


### How much does online employee identity verification cost?


Costs vary by provider and the number of checks. Basic identity verification (single document) can cost as little as Rs 50 to Rs 150 per check. ### Can I verify employee identity without Aadhaar?


Yes. While Aadhaar is a powerful verification tool, not all employees may be willing to share it due to privacy concerns. PAN, Voter ID, Driving Licence, and UAN collectively provide robust identity verification without requiring Aadhaar. ID Verify by SalaryBox supports all four of these checks.


### How long does digital identity verification take?


Digital identity checks typically return results within minutes. PAN and Voter ID verification are near-instant. Driving Licence and UAN checks may take slightly longer depending on database response times, but are usually completed within the same session.


### Is identity verification enough, or do I need a full background check?


Identity verification is the essential first layer it confirms that the person is who they claim to be. For most small businesses, running PAN, Voter ID, DL, and UAN checks covers the critical risk areas. Full background checks (employment history, education verification, criminal records) are recommended for senior roles, positions with financial authority, or roles in regulated industries.


### What if the verification finds a discrepancy?


A discrepancy does not automatically disqualify a candidate. Minor differences, such as a slightly different name spelling across documents are common and may be resolved by asking the candidate for clarification. Major discrepancies such as a PAN that does not exist or a UAN showing completely different employers are serious red flags that warrant further investigation or offer withdrawal.
