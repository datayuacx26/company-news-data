## Why Background Verification for Domestic Help Is No Longer Optional


India is a country where over 50 million people work as domestic help across households. That includes maids, cooks, drivers, nannies, gardeners, and security guards. For most urban families, especially nuclear families and households where both parents work, domestic help is not a luxury. It is a necessity.


But think about what you are really doing when you hire domestic help. You are inviting a stranger into your home. This person will have access to your valuables, your personal spaces, and in the case of a nanny or babysitter, your children. They may know when you leave for work, when you come back, and when the house is empty.


Despite all of this, the vast majority of Indian households hire domestic staff based on nothing more than a verbal reference. There is no identity check, no address verification, no criminal record search. The reference itself is often from another household that did the same thing.


This approach worked reasonably well when communities were smaller and people knew each other. But in modern urban India, where people move between cities frequently and domestic workers often come from different states, a verbal reference tells you almost nothing about a person’s actual background.


### The Real Risks of Skipping Verification


The risks are not hypothetical. They fall into several clear categories.


**Theft and burglary.** This is the most common issue. Domestic workers who have access to your home can easily identify valuables and may steal small items over time, making it hard to notice until the losses add up significantly.


**Safety of children and elderly family members.** If you have a nanny caring for your infant or a helper looking after elderly parents, you need to know that this person has no history of violence or abuse. This is not something a casual reference can tell you.


**Fraud and impersonation.** There have been cases where individuals provide fake identity documents, use someone else’s Aadhaar card, or give false addresses. Without proper verification, you have no way to confirm that the person standing in front of you is actually who they claim to be.


**Legal liability.** If an unverified domestic worker causes harm to someone in your household or neighbourhood, you could face questions about why you did not take basic precautions. While India does not have a specific law mandating domestic help verification in all states, several cities including Delhi, Mumbai, and Bangalore strongly recommend or require police verification for domestic workers.


## What Does Background Verification for Domestic Staff Include?


A comprehensive background check for domestic help typically covers these key areas.


### Identity Verification


This is the foundation of any background check. It involves verifying the person’s government issued identity documents to confirm they are who they say they are. The most commonly verified documents include PAN card, Voter ID (EPIC), Aadhaar card (when consent is provided), and Driving Licence (especially important for drivers).


Modern digital verification platforms can validate these documents against government databases in real time. For example, a PAN verification check confirms whether the PAN number matches the name and date of birth provided by the candidate. A Voter ID check confirms the person’s identity and registered address through the Election Commission database.


### Address Verification


Knowing where your domestic help actually lives is crucial. Address verification confirms that the residential address provided by the worker is genuine. This can be done digitally through database checks or through physical verification where someone visits the address to confirm the person lives there.


### Criminal Record Check


This involves searching court records and police databases to check whether the person has any criminal cases registered against them. In India, this can be challenging because there is no single centralised criminal database. Checks typically need to cover multiple jurisdictions, especially if the worker has lived in different states.


### Previous Employment Verification


Speaking with previous employers can reveal a lot about a domestic worker’s reliability, behaviour, and work ethic. However, this is often the hardest check to complete for domestic staff because previous employers may not keep formal records and contact information may not be available.


### Police Verification


Many cities in India have formal police verification processes for domestic help. In Delhi, for instance, you can apply for police verification through the Delhi Police website or your local police station. The police conduct their own background check and provide a verification certificate.

## City Wise Guidelines for Domestic Help Verification


Different cities in India have different requirements and processes for verifying domestic staff.


### Delhi


The Delhi Police has an online portal for domestic help verification. Employers can register their domestic workers through the portal, and police conduct background checks. The Tenant and Domestic Help Verification (TDHV) system has been active in Delhi for several years, and many RWAs (Resident Welfare Associations) now require it.


### Mumbai


The Mumbai Police runs a similar verification program. Building societies and housing complexes in Mumbai often mandate that all domestic workers be registered and verified. The verification typically includes an address check and criminal record search.


### Bangalore


The Bangalore City Police has implemented domestic help verification drives, encouraging residents to get their helpers verified. The city also has several private agencies that offer door to door verification services.


### Other Cities


Most state police forces now have some form of domestic help verification program, though the level of enforcement varies. Regardless of local requirements, running your own digital identity verification is always a good idea.

## Red Flags to Watch For During the Verification Process


Even before you run a formal background check, there are warning signs that should make you more cautious about a potential hire.


**Reluctance to share documents.** A genuine candidate should have no problem providing basic identity documents. If someone consistently avoids sharing their ID or makes excuses about why they do not have any documents, proceed with caution.


**Inconsistent personal details.** If the name on their PAN card does not match the name on their Voter ID, or if the dates of birth are different across documents, that is a serious concern. It could indicate a fake document.


**Vague employment history.** If someone claims to have worked for several families but cannot provide even one contact number or reference, that is worth noting.


**Pressure to skip verification.** If a placement agency or the candidate themselves pressures you to skip the verification process, treat that as a red flag. Legitimate workers and agencies welcome verification because it works in everyone’s favour.


**No permanent address.** While many domestic workers in cities live in rented accommodations and move frequently, they should still be able to provide a current address that can be verified.


## How Background Verification Protects Your Domestic Staff Too


Background verification is not a one sided process that only benefits employers. It actually helps domestic workers in several important ways.


First, it creates a formal record of their employment. Many domestic workers in India operate in the informal economy with no documentation of their work history. When you verify their identity and keep records, you are helping them build a formal employment trail.


Second, verified workers command more trust and often better pay. Families are willing to pay more for domestic help that comes with verified credentials. A worker who has been through background verification can use that as a selling point with future employers.


Third, it protects them from false accusations. If a theft or incident occurs in the household, verified workers have documented proof of their identity and background. This protects them from being wrongly blamed.


## Building a Culture of Verification in Your Community


One family verifying their domestic help is good. An entire residential community doing it is transformative.


If you live in an apartment complex or gated community, consider proposing a community wide verification program for all domestic workers. Many RWAs across Indian cities have already implemented this successfully.


The approach is simple. The RWA collects identity documents from all domestic workers entering the complex. Documents are verified through a digital platform. Verified workers receive an entry pass or ID card. New workers are verified before they begin work.


This kind of systematic approach creates a safer environment for everyone. It also makes verification more affordable because costs can be shared across the community.


## Frequently Asked Questions


### Is background verification of domestic help legally mandatory in India?


There is no central law that makes it mandatory for all households across India. However, several cities like Delhi and Mumbai have police verification programs for domestic workers, and many housing societies and RWAs require it as a condition for allowing domestic staff entry. Regardless of legal requirements, it is strongly recommended as a basic safety measure.


### What documents should I ask my domestic help to provide?


At minimum, ask for one government issued photo ID such as PAN card, Voter ID, or Driving Licence. For more thorough verification, ask for two or three different IDs so you can cross reference the details. Also ask for their current residential address and a photograph.


### How long does it take to verify a domestic worker’s background?


Digital identity verification through platforms like ID Verify by SalaryBox takes just minutes. You enter the document details and get verified results almost instantly. Police verification through official channels can take one to four weeks depending on the city and current backlog.


### Can I verify someone’s background without their knowledge?


No. You should always inform the person that you are conducting a background check and get their consent before using their personal documents for verification. This is both ethically correct and legally required under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.


### What should I do if the verification reveals a red flag?


It depends on the nature of the issue. A minor spelling difference in a name across two documents might just be a data entry error. But a completely different name or date of birth, or a criminal record, warrants serious concern. In such cases, discuss the findings with the candidate. There may be a legitimate explanation. If the explanation is not satisfactory, it is better to continue your search for a different candidate.


### Is online verification reliable for domestic staff?


Yes. Online platforms that verify documents against government databases like the PAN database, Election Commission records, and VAHAN (for Driving Licences) provide highly reliable results. The data comes directly from the issuing authority, making it more reliable than verbal references or paper based checks.
