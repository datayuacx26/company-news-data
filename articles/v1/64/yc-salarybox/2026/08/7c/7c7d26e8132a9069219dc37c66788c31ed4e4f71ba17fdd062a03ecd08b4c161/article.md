## The Four Pricing Models in Indian Background Verification


### Model 1: Pay Per Check (No Subscription)


You pay only for the checks you run. No monthly fees, no annual contracts, no minimum volume requirements.


**Best for:** Startups, SMEs, companies with irregular hiring patterns, and any business that does not want to pay during months when they are not hiring.


**How it works:** Visit the platform, enter candidate details, select checks, see the price, pay, and get results. Every check has a clear, fixed price that you see before checkout.


**Example:** ID Verify by SalaryBox (available at compose1.com/verify) uses this model. You pay per check with complete pricing transparency before checkout. No surprises on your invoice.


### Model 2: Monthly or Annual Subscription


You pay a fixed monthly or annual fee for access to the platform and a set number of checks per month.


**Best for:** Companies with predictable, consistent hiring volumes (for example, 20 to 50 hires per month, every month).


**The catch:** If your hiring volume drops, you still pay the subscription. If you exceed your monthly allocation, you pay overage charges that are often higher than the per check rate.


### Model 3: Per Candidate Package Pricing


You pay a bundled price per candidate that includes multiple checks (identity, address, education, employment, criminal).


**Best for:** Companies that need the same set of checks for every candidate and want one simple price.


**The catch:** You pay for checks you might not need. If a role only requires identity verification, you still pay for the full package that includes education, address, and criminal checks.


### Model 4: Enterprise Contract


Custom pricing negotiated based on volume commitments, typically with annual contracts.


**Best for:** Large enterprises verifying 500 or more candidates per year.


**The catch:** Volume commitments. If you commit to 1,000 checks per year and only use 600, you have prepaid for 400 checks you did not need.


## Cost Breakdown by Check Type


### PAN Card Verification


**What it does:** Checks the PAN number against the Income Tax Department database. Returns the name and date of birth associated with the PAN.


**Digital platform cost:** Rs 50 to Rs 200 per check.


**Traditional agency cost:** Rs 300 to Rs 500 per check.


**Time:** Digital platforms return results in minutes. Traditional agencies take 1 to 3 business days.


**Why digital is better value:** The government database returns the same authoritative data regardless of who queries it. Paying Rs 500 for a field agent to do what an API does in seconds is paying for overhead, not accuracy.


### Voter ID Verification


**What it does:** Validates the EPIC number against the Election Commission database. Confirms identity and registered address.


**Digital platform cost:** Rs 50 to Rs 200 per check.


**Traditional agency cost:** Rs 300 to Rs 500 per check.


**Time:** Digital results in minutes. Traditional takes 1 to 5 business days.


### Driving Licence Verification


**What it does:** Checks the DL number against the VAHAN/SARATHI database. Confirms licence validity, vehicle class, and any suspensions.


**Digital platform cost:** Rs 50 to Rs 200 per check.


**Traditional agency cost:** Rs 300 to Rs 500 per check.


**Time:** Digital results in minutes. Traditional takes 1 to 3 business days.


### UAN Employment History Verification


**What it does:** Checks the Universal Account Number against EPFO records. Reveals previous employer names and employment dates through PF contribution history.


**Digital platform cost:** Rs 200 to Rs 500 per check.


**Traditional agency cost:** Rs 500 to Rs 1,000 per check.


**Time:** Digital results in minutes to hours. Traditional takes 3 to 7 business days, and this gap has widened since EPFO’s 2025-26 data masking rules made manual portal viewing unreliable.


### Criminal Record Check


**What it does:** Searches court records for criminal cases matching the candidate’s name and details.


**Digital (eCourts database) cost:** Rs 300 to Rs 800 per check.


**Traditional (physical court visit) cost:** Rs 500 to Rs 1,500 per check.


**Time:** Digital takes 24 to 72 hours. Traditional takes 7 to 14 business days.


**Note:** India does not have a centralized criminal database. Searches are conducted per jurisdiction, so costs increase with each additional district or state searched.


### Address Verification


**What it does:** Confirms the candidate’s current residential address.


**Digital (database based) cost:** Rs 100 to Rs 300 per check. Voter ID verification provides registered address as a proxy.


**Physical (field agent visit) cost:** Rs 500 to Rs 1,500 per check, depending on location.


**Time:** Digital is instant (through Voter ID address data). Physical takes 3 to 10 business days.


### Education Verification


**What it does:** Confirms degrees and certifications with the issuing institution.


**Digital cost:** Rs 300 to Rs 800 per check.


**Traditional cost:** Rs 500 to Rs 1,500 per check.


**Time:** 3 to 15 business days, depending on how quickly the institution responds.


### Credit Check (CIBIL)


**What it does:** Pulls the candidate’s credit report to assess financial behavior.


**Cost:** Rs 500 to Rs 1,000 per check.


**Time:** 24 to 48 hours.


**Typically used for:** Financial services roles, accounting positions, and senior management.

## Hidden Costs That Inflate Your Verification Bill


### Platform Access Fees


Some agencies charge a monthly or annual “platform access fee” of Rs 5,000 to Rs 25,000 just for the right to submit verification requests through their portal. This is on top of per check charges.


### Rush Processing Surcharges


Need results faster than the standard timeline? Many agencies charge a 30 percent premium for expedited processing. With digital platforms that return results in minutes, there is no concept of rush processing because every check is already fast.


### Minimum Volume Commitments


Annual contracts often require minimum volumes. If you commit to 100 checks per year and only use 50, you pay for 100. This is a guaranteed way to overpay.


### Re-verification Charges


When a check returns “unable to verify” due to a database being temporarily unavailable, some agencies charge for the re-check. Digital platforms typically allow retries without additional charges.


### Report Generation Fees


Some agencies charge separately for generating PDF reports, on top of the verification check fee. ID Verify includes downloadable PDF reports with every verification at no additional cost.


### Integration and Setup Fees


Enterprise verification platforms sometimes charge Rs 50,000 to Rs 200,000 for API integration and setup. Self serve platforms like ID Verify require no integration and no setup fees.


## How to Reduce Your Verification Costs Without Cutting Quality


### Use Digital for Identity, Traditional for Depth


Run PAN, Voter ID, Driving Licence, and UAN checks digitally for every candidate. These checks are faster, cheaper, and more accurate through API based platforms. Reserve traditional agency services for criminal record deep dives, physical address verification, and detailed reference interviews that genuinely require human involvement.


### Match Checks to Roles


Not every role needs every check. A frontline worker needs identity verification. A financial controller needs identity, employment history, criminal records, and credit checks. Matching the verification depth to the role’s risk profile avoids paying for unnecessary checks.


### Avoid Annual Contracts If You Hire Irregularly


If your hiring pattern is unpredictable (busy some months, quiet others), annual contracts with volume minimums will always result in overpayment. Pay per check models let you match spending to actual hiring activity.


### Consolidate on One Platform


Using multiple verification vendors for different checks multiplies administrative overhead and sometimes duplicates checks. Using a single platform like ID Verify that handles all identity checks in one workflow is more efficient and often cheaper.
