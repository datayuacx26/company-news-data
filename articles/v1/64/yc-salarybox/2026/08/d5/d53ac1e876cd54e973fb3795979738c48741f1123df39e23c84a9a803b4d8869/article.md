Background verification costs in India typically range from ₹500 to ₹8,000+ per candidate in 2026. The exact amount depends on which checks you run, how many candidates you screen, which provider you use, and how quickly you need results.


This guide breaks down real-world pricing across different check types, company sizes, and providers — so you can budget accurately for your hiring process without overspending on checks you don’t need.


## Background Verification Cost Breakdown by Check Type


Each component of a background check carries a different cost. Here is what Indian employers typically pay in 2026:


### Identity Verification (₹150–₹1,500 per candidate)


Identity checks validate government-issued documents against official databases. This is the most affordable layer of verification and the one every employer should run.


Check Typical Cost Turnaround


PAN verification ₹50–₹200 Instant–minutes


Voter ID verification ₹50–₹200 Instant–minutes


Driving Licence verification ₹50–₹200 Instant–minutes


UAN/EPF verification ₹100–₹300 Instant–minutes


Aadhaar verification (OTP-based) ₹100–₹300 Instant


Bundled ID checks (e.g., Compose1 Verify) ₹500–₹1,500 Minutes


**Cost-saving tip:** Platforms like[Compose1 Verify](https://compose1.com/verify/) bundle multiple ID checks (PAN, Voter ID, Driving Licence, UAN) into a single transaction, which is typically cheaper than running each check individually through separate providers. The bundled approach also saves time by cross-referencing identity data across all documents simultaneously.


### Employment Verification (₹500–₹2,000 per employer checked)


Employment checks confirm a candidate’s previous job titles, employment dates, and sometimes reason for leaving. The cost varies based on the verification method.


Method Typical Cost Turnaround


Digital/database verification ₹500–₹800 24–48 hours


Phone-based verification ₹800–₹1,200 2–4 days


Physical verification (field agent visits) ₹1,200–₹2,000 4–7 days


Most candidates have worked at 2–3 previous employers, so budgeting ₹1,500–₹4,000 for employment history verification is realistic for a standard check covering the last two employers.


### Education Verification (₹500–₹1,500 per degree)


Education checks confirm that degrees, diplomas, and certifications are genuine.


Check Type Typical Cost Turnaround


Digital university database check ₹500–₹800 24–48 hours


Direct university verification ₹800–₹1,500 3–10 days


International degree verification ₹2,000–₹5,000 5–15 days


Education verification is most critical for roles where specific qualifications are legally required — medical professionals, chartered accountants, engineers, and similar.


### Criminal Record Checks (₹800–₹3,000 per candidate)


Criminal screening in India is more complex and expensive than identity checks because it often requires physical court record searches.


Check Type Typical Cost Turnaround


Digital criminal database search ₹800–₹1,500 24–48 hours


District court record search (single district) ₹1,000–₹2,000 3–7 days


Multi-district court search ₹2,000–₹3,000 5–10 days


High Court and Supreme Court check ₹1,500–₹2,500 3–7 days


Criminal checks are essential for positions involving financial responsibility, access to vulnerable populations, security roles, and leadership positions.


### Address Verification (₹500–₹1,500 per address)


Address verification confirms that a candidate actually lives (or has lived) at the address they provided.


Method Typical Cost Turnaround


Digital/postal verification ₹500–₹800 2–5 days


Physical field verification ₹800–₹1,500 3–7 days


### Drug Testing (₹1,000–₹3,000 per test)


Drug screening is less common in India than in Western markets but is increasingly required by multinational employers and in safety-sensitive industries.

## Cost Comparison by Provider Type


The type of provider you choose significantly affects pricing:


### Self-Service Platforms (Most Affordable)


Platforms like[Compose1 Verify](https://compose1.com/verify/) operate on a pay-per-check model. You create an account, add candidate details, choose your checks, and pay at checkout. There are no sales calls, no contracts, and no minimum commitments.


**Typical cost:** ₹500–₹1,500 per candidate for identity checks.


**Why this model works for small businesses:** You only pay when you hire. During months when you are not recruiting, your verification costs are zero. There is no monthly subscription fee and no minimum spend.


### API-Based Platforms (Mid-Range)


Platforms like IDfy, OnGrid, and Surepass charge per API call and typically offer volume-based discounts. These work well for companies with consistent hiring volume and the technical capability to integrate APIs.


**Typical cost:** ₹100–₹500 per individual check via API, but requires development resources for integration.


### Full-Service BGV Providers (Premium)


Companies like AuthBridge, SpringVerify, and HireRight offer end-to-end background verification with dedicated account management, compliance consulting, and customized reporting. These providers charge per candidate or through annual contracts.


**Typical cost:** ₹2,500–₹10,000+ per candidate for comprehensive screening packages.


**Why enterprises pay more:** Full-service providers handle verification that requires human judgment — interpreting court records, conducting field visits, speaking with references. This manual work drives costs higher but provides deeper verification than purely digital checks.


## How Company Size Affects BGV Costs


### Startups and Small Businesses (1–50 Employees)


**Recommended approach:** Use a self-service tool like Compose1 Verify for identity checks on every hire. Add manual reference checks by calling previous employers directly. Reserve comprehensive BGV for sensitive roles only.


**Estimated annual cost:** ₹10,000–₹50,000 (assuming 10–30 hires per year, primarily identity checks).


### Growing Companies (50–200 Employees)


**Recommended approach:** Implement standard-level verification (identity + employment + education) for all hires. Use a platform with HRMS integration like SpringVerify to streamline the workflow.


**Estimated annual cost:** ₹1,00,000–₹4,00,000 (assuming 40–80 hires per year at ₹2,500–₹5,000 per candidate).


### Enterprises (200+ Employees)


**Recommended approach:** Comprehensive verification for all hires, with enhanced screening for sensitive roles. Enterprise contracts with providers like AuthBridge or First Advantage typically offer volume discounts of 15–30%.


**Estimated annual cost:** ₹5,00,000–₹25,00,000+ depending on volume and check depth.


## 7 Ways to Reduce Background Verification Costs


**1. Start with identity verification for every hire.** A bundled identity check through Compose1 Verify catches the most common forms of candidate fraud — fake PAN numbers, forged Voter IDs, misrepresented identities — at a fraction of the cost of a full BGV. Reserve comprehensive checks for roles that genuinely require them.


**2. Use a risk-based approach.** Not every role needs the same level of screening. An entry-level data entry operator does not need the same verification depth as a finance manager handling company funds. Define two or three verification tiers based on role sensitivity.


**3. Bundle checks with a single provider.** Running PAN verification through one platform, Driving Licence through another, and employment checks through a third multiplies your costs through separate transaction fees and subscription charges. Bundled platforms offer better per-check rates.


**4. Negotiate volume discounts.** If you hire more than 50 people per year, most BGV providers will offer volume-based pricing. Even self-service platforms sometimes offer bulk credit packages at reduced rates.


**5. Verify at the right stage of hiring.** Run identity checks after the final interview but before extending an offer letter. Do not pay for background verification on candidates who are still in early screening rounds.


**6. Avoid unnecessary international checks.** If a candidate’s entire career has been in India, international screening adds cost without adding value. Only run global checks for candidates with international education or work history.


**7. Automate where possible.** Digital verification is faster and cheaper than manual processes. Platforms that use API-based document verification can deliver results in minutes at a lower cost than providers that rely on field agents.


## Hidden Costs to Watch For


**Setup and integration fees.** Some enterprise BGV providers charge ₹25,000–₹1,00,000 for initial setup, API integration, and platform customization. Self-service tools like Compose1 Verify eliminate this cost entirely.


**Minimum monthly commitments.** Contract-based providers often require a minimum monthly spend, typically ₹10,000–₹50,000. If your hiring is seasonal or irregular, you end up paying for verification you don’t use.


**Re-verification charges.** When a check returns inconclusive results and needs to be re-run, some providers charge full price for the second attempt. Clarify re-verification policies before signing a contract.


**Compliance consulting fees.** Some providers charge separately for compliance advisory services, regulatory updates, and audit support. Ask whether these are included in the per-check price.


## Frequently Asked Questions


**What is the cheapest way to do background verification in India?**
The most affordable approach is using a self-service identity verification platform like Compose1 Verify for basic ID checks (PAN, Voter ID, DL, UAN) at ₹500–₹1,500 per candidate, supplemented by direct reference calls to previous employers. This costs a fraction of full-service BGV while catching the most common forms of identity fraud.


**Is it worth paying for background verification for every employee?**
Yes, but the depth of verification should match the role. Identity checks are inexpensive enough to run on every hire. Comprehensive background screening — including criminal, education, and employment checks — is most cost-effective when reserved for mid-level and senior roles, positions handling sensitive data, and roles with financial responsibility.


**Do background verification costs vary by city in India?**
Physical verification components (address checks, court record searches) cost more in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities where provider networks are thinner and field agent travel costs are higher. Digital identity checks, however, cost the same regardless of geography because they validate documents against centralized databases.


**Can I do background verification in-house to save money?**
You can handle reference calls and basic document review internally, but identity document verification against government databases requires access to verification APIs or platforms. The cost of building and maintaining these integrations internally almost always exceeds the cost of using a platform like Compose1 Verify.


**How often should I re-verify existing employees?**
Annual re-verification is recommended for employees in sensitive positions (financial handling, data access, compliance roles). For other employees, re-verification at the time of promotion or role change is sufficient. Continuous monitoring services from providers like HireRight and First Advantage offer automated post-hire screening.
