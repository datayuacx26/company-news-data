## The 10 Point Evaluation Checklist


### 1. Does It Check Against Government Databases Directly?


This is the single most important technical question. There are two fundamentally different approaches to document verification in India.


**API based verification** sends the document number directly to the relevant government database (Income Tax Department for PAN, Election Commission for Voter ID, VAHAN/SARATHI for Driving Licence, EPFO for UAN) and receives an authoritative response. The result comes from the source of truth. It cannot be fooled by a forged document because it does not look at the document. It checks the number against the database.


**Manual or field agent verification** involves a person visually inspecting the document and making a judgment about whether it appears genuine. This approach cannot detect AI generated forgeries, digitally altered certificates, or sophisticated fakes that look identical to real documents.


In 2026, API based verification is the standard for identity checks. Any provider that relies primarily on visual document inspection for PAN, Voter ID, or Driving Licence verification is using an obsolete approach that misses the most common forms of modern document fraud.


**What to ask the provider:** “Do you verify PAN, Voter ID, and Driving Licence through direct API integrations with government databases, or through manual document inspection?”


### 2. How Fast Are Results?


Speed matters for two reasons. First, candidates with multiple offers will accept the employer who confirms fastest. A two week verification delay loses you talent. Second, roles that need to be filled urgently (seasonal hiring, replacement hires, growth phase onboarding) cannot wait for traditional timelines.


**Benchmark for 2026:**


Identity checks (PAN, Voter ID, Driving Licence) should return results in minutes, not days. Any provider quoting 24 to 48 hours for a PAN check is using manual processes that add unnecessary delay.


UAN employment history should return within minutes to a few hours for digital platforms.


Criminal record checks take 24 to 72 hours through digital eCourts database searches. Physical court visits take 7 to 14 business days.


Education verification takes 3 to 15 business days depending on institutional response times. This is the one area where speed is largely outside the provider’s control.


**What to ask the provider:** “What is the turnaround time for each check type? Specifically, how long does a PAN or Voter ID verification take?”


### 3. Does It Offer Cross Record Analysis?


A single document check tells you whether that one document is valid. Cross record analysis compares details across multiple documents and highlights inconsistencies.


For example, if the PAN card returns “Amit Kumar, born 15 June 1992” and the Voter ID returns “Amit Kumar Singh, born 16 June 1992,” a single document check would show both as valid. Cross record analysis flags the name variation and the date of birth discrepancy, prompting further investigation.


Without cross record analysis, your HR team must manually compare details across every document for every candidate. This is time consuming and error prone.


**What to ask the provider:** “Do you offer automatic cross record analysis when multiple documents are verified for the same candidate? Is this included in the price or charged separately?”


### 4. What Does the Pricing Structure Look Like?


Pricing structures in the Indian verification market vary widely. Understanding the structure is as important as understanding the price.


**Pay per check (no subscription):** You pay only for checks you run. No monthly fees, no annual contracts, no minimum volumes. Best for companies with variable hiring patterns.


**Subscription with included checks:** Monthly or annual fee includes a set number of checks. Overages are charged at a higher rate. Best for companies with predictable, consistent volume.


**Annual contract with volume commitment:** Custom pricing based on committed annual volume. Penalties or wasted spend if you do not meet the commitment.


**Red flags in pricing:**


Platform access fees charged on top of per check prices. Rush processing surcharges for faster results (digital platforms should not need this because all results are fast). Separate charges for PDF report generation. Setup or integration fees. Minimum volume requirements that do not match your actual hiring pattern. Prices that are only revealed after a sales call.


**What to ask the provider:** “Is the per check price all inclusive, or are there additional fees for platform access, report generation, rush processing, or setup? Is there a minimum volume commitment?”


### 5. Can You See the Price Before You Pay?


This seems basic, but many verification providers require you to talk to a sales representative before revealing their pricing. They want to qualify you as a lead, understand your volume, and negotiate a custom price.


In 2026, pricing opacity is a red flag. The best platforms show you the exact price for each check before you complete the transaction. You know what you are paying before you commit.


**What to ask the provider:** “Can I see the complete price for each check on your website or platform before talking to a sales representative? Is the quoted price the final price, or are there additional charges?”


### 6. How Good Are the Reports?


A verification result is only useful if your HR team can understand and act on it. Evaluate the report quality on these dimensions.


**Clarity.** Are statuses clearly labeled (verified, mismatch, unable to verify)? Can a non specialist understand what each result means?


**Completeness.** Does the report show what was checked, what the government database returned, and whether the returned details match the candidate’s claims?


**Format.** Is the report available as a downloadable PDF? Can it be attached to the employee’s file for compliance and audit purposes?


**Consistency.** Are reports structured the same way for every candidate, making it easy to compare results across multiple verifications?


**Delivery.** Are reports delivered automatically (by email or through the platform), or do you have to request them separately?


**What to ask the provider:** “Can I see a sample report before signing up? Are PDF reports included in the price, or charged separately?”


### 7. Is the Platform Self Serve?


The verification industry has historically operated on a relationship model: you talk to a sales representative, sign a contract, and submit verification requests through an account manager. This model works for large enterprises but adds unnecessary friction for smaller companies.


Self serve platforms let you create an account, enter candidate details, select checks, pay, and get results without ever talking to a sales representative. This is faster, simpler, and gives you complete control over your verification process.


**What to ask the provider:** “Can I create an account and run my first check without talking to a sales representative or signing a contract?”

### 8. How Does It Handle Data Security?


Background verification involves sensitive personal data: PAN numbers, Voter ID numbers, dates of birth, employment histories, and criminal records. How the provider handles this data matters.


**Account bound access.** Verification data should be visible only to the account holder who initiated the check. Shared dashboards where multiple users can see all verifications create unnecessary data exposure.


**Masked identifiers.** Sensitive document numbers should be masked until the appropriate point in the workflow. Displaying full PAN or Voter ID numbers throughout the process is a data security risk.


**Secure checkout.** Payment processing should use industry standard encryption. Your payment details should never be stored on the verification platform.


**Data retention policies.** The provider should have clear policies on how long verification data is retained and how it is eventually deleted.


**What to ask the provider:** “Where is verification data stored? Who can access it? Are document numbers masked during the workflow? What is your data retention policy?”


### 9. What Is the Coverage?


Coverage has two dimensions: geographic and check type.


**Geographic coverage.** For Indian companies hiring Indian candidates, the key question is whether the provider checks against national databases. PAN, Voter ID, Driving Licence, and UAN are national databases, so any provider with API access covers the entire country. For criminal record checks, coverage depends on which courts and jurisdictions are included in the search.


**Check type coverage.** Evaluate whether the provider offers the specific checks you need. Most companies need some combination of identity verification (PAN, Voter ID, Driving Licence), employment history (UAN), criminal record checks, education verification, and address verification.


**What to ask the provider:** “Which government databases do you have API access to? For criminal record checks, which states and districts are covered?”


### 10. What Happens When Something Goes Wrong?


Even the best verification platforms occasionally encounter issues: a government database is temporarily unavailable, a check returns an unexpected result, or a candidate disputes a finding.


**Customer support.** Is support available by email, chat, or phone? What is the typical response time? Is support included in the price, or is it a premium add on?


**Failed checks.** What happens when a check returns “unable to verify” due to a technical issue? Is the retry free, or do you pay again?


**Dispute resolution.** If a candidate disputes a verification finding, does the provider assist with resolution?


**What to ask the provider:** “What happens if a check fails due to a database being unavailable? Am I charged for failed checks? How do you handle candidate disputes?”


## Comparing Provider Types


### Large Traditional Agencies


Examples include AuthBridge, First Advantage, and HireRight.


**Strengths.** Comprehensive check types including physical verification, global coverage for multinational companies, established processes for regulated industries.


**Weaknesses.** Slower turnaround (7 to 21 days standard), higher costs (Rs 1,500 to Rs 5,000 per candidate for standard packages), annual contracts with volume minimums, require sales calls to get started.


**Best for.** Large enterprises with predictable high volumes, regulated industries requiring comprehensive screening, and companies needing global coverage.


### Digital API Based Platforms


Examples include ID Verify by SalaryBox (compose1.com/verify), and other digital first providers.


**Strengths.** Instant to same day results for identity checks, significantly lower per check costs, self serve access with no sales calls, pay per check pricing with no minimums, government database API verification that catches document fraud.


**Weaknesses.** May not offer physical address verification through field agents, limited reference interview capabilities, some specialized checks (CIBIL, directorship) may not be available on all platforms.


**Best for.** SMEs, startups, companies with variable hiring volumes, frontline and operational hiring, and any organization that values speed and cost efficiency.


### Regional or Niche Agencies


Smaller agencies with specialization in specific regions or industries.


**Strengths.** Local knowledge, personal service, sometimes better reach in specific geographic areas for physical verification.


**Weaknesses.** Limited technology, inconsistent quality, may not have API access to government databases, limited scalability.


**Best for.** Companies with very specific local verification needs that national providers cannot address.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### Should I choose a large agency or a digital platform?


For identity and employment verification (PAN, Voter ID, DL, UAN), digital platforms are faster, cheaper, and more accurate. For comprehensive checks including physical verification and detailed references, large agencies add value. Many companies use both: digital for most hires, agency for senior roles.


### How do I know if a provider actually uses government database APIs?


Ask directly: “Do you verify PAN through the Income Tax Department API?” If the answer involves field agents, document scanning, or visual inspection, they are not using API based verification. API based results arrive in minutes. Manual results take days.
