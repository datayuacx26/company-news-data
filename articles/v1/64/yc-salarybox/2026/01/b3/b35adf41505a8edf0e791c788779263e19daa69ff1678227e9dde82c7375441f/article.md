## Understanding Challenges in Power-Prone Areas


India's construction industry is booming, contributing significantly to GDP growth. However, sites often operate in remote locations with unreliable electricity supply. Frequent power outages, sometimes lasting hours, can halt operations, including attendance tracking. Manual registers become prone to manipulation, leading to payroll inaccuracies and disputes.


Traditional card-based or manual systems fail here because they rely on constant power or human intervention. Biometric attendance solves this by using tamper-proof biometric authentication, but without battery backup, even these can fail during cuts.


Enter a biometric attendance machine with battery backup. These devices store data locally and continue functioning on internal batteries, ensuring seamless attendance marking for workers in dusty, hot environments typical of the construction industry.


Moreover, in extreme weather conditions like heavy rains, dust storms, or scorching heat common in Indian sites, standard devices may malfunction. This calls for rugged biometric scanners that are dust resistant biometric, heat resistant biometric, and ideally weatherproof biometric or even IP67 rated biometric for dust and waterproof biometric protection.
