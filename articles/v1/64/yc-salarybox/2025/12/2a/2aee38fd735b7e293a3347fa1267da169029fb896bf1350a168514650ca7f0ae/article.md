## What is Biometric Attendance?


Biometric attendance refers to the use of unique physical or behavioral characteristics to verify identity and record attendance. Common types include fingerprint attendance systems, iris recognition, and facial recognition systems. Unlike traditional methods, biometric attendance systems eliminate the need for cards or manuals, providing a secure attendance system with fake check-ins prevention and time theft prevention.


In hybrid workplaces, biometric attendance combines office-based devices with mobile attendance apps for remote workers, supporting remote attendance tracking and geofencing attendance.


## What is Facial Recognition Attendance?


A facial recognition attendance system uses face recognition technology to identify employees by analyzing facial features. It's a form of contactless biometric attendance that's hygienic and efficient, especially post-pandemic. Key advantages include no swipe card attendance, no manual register attendance, and integration with door access control.


Popular in India for facial recognition India needs, these systems support attendance for offices, attendance for factories, and multi-location attendance systems.
