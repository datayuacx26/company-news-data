## What is a Biometric Attendance System?


A


[biometric attendance system](https://salarybox.in/biometric-devices/) is an automated tool that uses unique physiological or behavioral characteristics to verify and record an individual's presence. Common methods include fingerprint scanning, facial recognition attendance, iris recognition, and palm vein patterns. Unlike manual registers or card-based systems, biometrics ensure tamper-proof records by eliminating issues like buddy punching prevention and time theft.


These systems are widely used in biometric attendance system schools, corporate offices, healthcare facilities, and government organizations. In India, they comply with labor laws and often integrate with payroll software integration for accurate wage calculations.
