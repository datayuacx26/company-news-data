# Tiptap is now a YC-backed company.

Dear Tiptap Community,


we are incredibly excited to share some major news with you today. Tiptap, has been accepted into[Y Combinator's Summer 2023](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tiptap) batch, one of the most renowned startup accelerators in the world!


[Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/) has a storied reputation for nurturing some of the most innovative startups around the globe - Dropbox, Airbnb, Stripe, to name just a few. To become a YC backed company is a testament to the hard work and dedication of our entire team and community.


Being a part of the YC S23 batch means more than just recognition. It's an opportunity for us to refine our product, scale our operations, and expand our reach, with guidance from some of the most successful entrepreneurs and experts in the startup scene.


As an open-source project, the core of our mission has always been rooted in the strength of community. It's your unwavering support, invaluable feedback, and active contribution that have brought us here. Rest assured, as we take this significant step forward, our commitment to maintaining our open-source ethos remains stronger than ever.


We believe this opportunity will bring us even closer to our goal of making the web more collaborative and growing Tiptap into the ecosystem for developers to build great collaborative editing apps. We're beyond excited for this journey and can't wait to see where this takes us.


As we embark on this new chapter, we extend our deepest thanks to Y Combinator, and to you, our users, contributors, and supporters. We'll continue to keep you updated as we progress and look forward to sharing more exciting news very soon!


Thank you for being part of this journey with us 🫶


Yours Nick, Patrick, Philip, Sebastian, Sven and Timo


Any questions? Please write us athumans@tiptap.dev .
