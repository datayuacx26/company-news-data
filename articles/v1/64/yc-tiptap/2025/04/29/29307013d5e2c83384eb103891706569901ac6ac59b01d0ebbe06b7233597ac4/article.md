# Q1 '25 Hackathon recap: what we built and shipped

## New features and updates


- **Tiptap V3:** Significant progress, with the` v3.0.0-next.7`[branch](https://github.com/ueberdosis/tiptap/releases) available for early testing.
- **Tiptap v2.11.6:** Multiple minor improvements released ([release notes](https://github.com/ueberdosis/tiptap/releases/tag/v2.11.6) ).
- **Docx Import/Export:** Major[improvements released](https://tiptap.dev/docs/conversion/import-export/docx) for smoother document handling.
- **AI Suggestions:** Added a[new feature](https://tiptap.dev/docs/content-ai/capabilities/suggestion/overview) to help users write faster and smarter.
- **Decorations API:** Initial version completed to enhance editor functionality.
- **Tiptap Cloud:** Made notable progress on our new cloud platform and refined the pricing model.
- **AWS Marketplace:** Advanced integration efforts to list our services on AWS.
- **Tiptap AI Agent PoC:** Built a new experimental feature exploring AI integration ([live demo](https://ai-agent.tiptap.dev/) ).


## Technical enhancements


- Improved documentation, rigorous testing of new features, and comprehensive release support.
- Enhanced collaboration capabilities, added read-only comments, and introduced server-side content manipulation examples.
- Explored advanced AI vector search features and expanded support for on-premises deployments.
- Developed essential[UI React components](https://tiptap.dev/product/ui-components) and significantly improved overall UI/UX.
- Provided ongoing troubleshooting and support for customers and team.


## Team milestones


- Closed two significant multiyear deals.
- Smoothly organized and executed the entire event.


## Special mention

We're excited about what's next, especially our upcoming Q2 Hackathon, combined with our annual retreat in Spain!
