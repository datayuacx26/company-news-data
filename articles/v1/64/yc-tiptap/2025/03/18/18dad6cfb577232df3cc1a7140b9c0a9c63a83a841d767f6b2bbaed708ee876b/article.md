# Introducing Tiptap AI Suggestion

## **How It Works**


The AI Suggestion extension securely sends your document content (HTML) and defined suggestion rules to the Tiptap API endpoint, which then returns precise proofreading and editing suggestions directly into your editor interface. Neither the document content nor rules are stored in Tiptap Cloud, providing maximum privacy and security.


[Read the docs](https://tiptap.dev/docs/content-ai/capabilities/suggestion/overview)

## **Key Technical Features**


- **Fully Customizable Rules:** Define rules globally, per-document, per-user, or allow your end-users to create their own.
- **Fully Customizable UI:** Tailor exactly how suggestions appear in your editor’s interface to match your app’s design and UX.
- **Multiple Replacement Options:** Present multiple AI-generated suggestions, letting users choose the best fit for their content.
- **Prevent Certain Content:** Lock specific content to exclude it from AI-generated suggestions.
- **Custom AI Models:** Effortlessly integrate your own AI models alongside traditional methods (e.g., dictionary-based rules).
- **Optimized Performance:** Preload suggestions for minimal latency and improved UX.
- **Security & Privacy:** Secure API-generated suggestions. Your rules and content are never stored in Tiptap Cloud.
- **Real-time Suggestion Updates:** Suggestions update automatically as users write, delivering smooth, responsive interactions.
- **Easy, Instant Setup:** Get started in minutes using our ready-to-go example code.


## **Real-Time Suggestions in Your Editor**


Suggestions generated by AI Suggestion appear directly in the editor, allowing users to easily accept or reject recommended changes without interrupting their workflow.


## **Ensure Consistent Quality**


By leveraging customizable suggestion rules, developers can ensure consistently high-quality content across their applications, save users time and improve content quality.
