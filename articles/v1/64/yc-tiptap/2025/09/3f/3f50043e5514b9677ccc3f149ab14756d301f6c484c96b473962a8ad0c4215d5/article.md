# Structured, paginated, real: meet Tiptap Pages alpha

Most editors treat documents like one long scroll. But business documents- contracts, proposals, policies, exams - have structure. Pages, limits, rules.


If you’ve tried building those workflows in a modern editor, you’ve probably hacked together page breaks with CSS, or bolted on a PDF exporter just to meet formatting requirements.


**Tiptap Pages** is developed and maintained in-house, it plays nicely with all Tiptap Pro and Cloud extensions and gives your editor a real sense of structure.


[Open in new window →](https://feature-pages-extension--tiptap-pro.netlify.app/src/extensions/pages/react/)


## **What it does**


With Tiptap Pages, your editor becomes aware of pages. It is not just pixels.


- [Page layouts](https://tiptap.dev/docs/pages/core-concepts/page-format) :
A4, Letter, Legal, Tabloid, A3, A5, or custom sizes
- [Visual page breaks](https://tiptap.dev/docs/pages/core-concepts/page-break) :
Know exactly where one page ends and the next begins
- [Dynamic headers & footers](https://tiptap.dev/docs/pages/core-concepts/page-header-footer) :
Add page numbers, section titles, or custom content
- **Smart pagination** :
Content flows across pages correctly … and you can configure your custom extensions to break accordingly


If your users export to DOCX, print documents, or just care where things land on a page. This matters.


## **Why we built it**


Tiptap has always been about developer control. But until now, that stopped at the page level.


We’ve seen developers trying to build:


- Insurance workflows where addendums must appear on the correct page
- Edtech tools that separate exams from answer keys
- Proposal software that needs to warn if an RFP runs too long
- Legal apps that insert exhibits between exact pages without splitting signature blocks


These aren’t edge cases, they’re normal cases, for structured content. Pages is the foundation for all of it.


## **Where it’s going**


This isn’t just a layout feature, it’s the beginning of a document intelligence layer that understands real-world document constraints.


We’re working on page-aware AI that can:


- Reflow or rewrite documents to stay within limits
- Respect page-sensitive formatting and sections
- Insert, split, or annotate content with layout in mind


If you’re building AI-native workflows, this unlocks a whole new set of capabilities.
