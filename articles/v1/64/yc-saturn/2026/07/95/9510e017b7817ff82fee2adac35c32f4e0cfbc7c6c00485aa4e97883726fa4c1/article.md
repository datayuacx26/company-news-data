# Advice Will Always Be Human | Emmelia Powell

Whilst we build collaboratively with our partners, we continue to realise just how much financial advice is and has always been about people. Beyond the admin and regulation, its real connection, value, and empathy that allows advisers to guide clients through life’s complexity. These are all stories that Saturn exists to support and share.


> “If you can make the mundane more efficient, you have more time to explore leadership, to have human connections, to be creative thinkers, to be problem solvers, and go out to see clients face to face.”


– Emmelia Powell advises.


‘Advice Will Always Be Human’ is an original Saturn Studios production that will follow a series of advisers to hear about their stories so far and how they’re integrating technology into their businesses to make a tangible difference to peoples lives.


Delivered with Emmelia’s characteristic candour and passion to serve other human beings as they navigate their lives and finances. Emmelia cuts through the noise and breaks down the challenges Premier Wealth Solution’s faces, how technology enables them to service the mundane, and how advice should be delivered.


Amal Jolly, Saturn CEO, said:


> “Behind every financial plan is a human story.
>
>
> Advisers and their teams quietly change lives, giving families confidence and peace of mind.
>
>
> By sharing these powerful personal stories, we show a future where it can scale to hundreds of millions while staying human at its core, amplified by technology.


Advice Will Always Be Human will continue with the story of Rory Albon.
