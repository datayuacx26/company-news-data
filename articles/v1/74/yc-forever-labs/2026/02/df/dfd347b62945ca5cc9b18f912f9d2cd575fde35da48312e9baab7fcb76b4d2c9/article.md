# Member Spotlight: Walking the walk with Forever Labs CEO Kevin Virgil

As the CEO of Forever Labs, Kevin Virgil spends his days talking about the future of regenerative medicine. But for Kevin, stem cell banking isn't just a professional mission; it's a deeply personal one. Recently, Kevin transitioned from being a long-time advocate to a patient who successfully utilized his own biology to reclaim his quality of life.


### **A Life in Motion: The Reality of Joint Wear and Tear**


Between playing sports and serving in the military, Kevin spent decades actively putting his body through its paces. While those experiences were fulfilling, they left behind a common byproduct: physical wear and tear.


By the time Kevin reached his early 50s, the damage became undeniable. An MRI revealed that he had almost no cartilage left in either of his knees, leading to a diagnosis of knee osteoarthritis. The pain was significant, but his desire to keep on going was even greater.


### **Stem Cell Banking: A "Back to the Future" Solution**


Faced with the diagnosis, Kevin was presented with traditional orthopedic treatments: lidocaine injections for temporary pain management or a full knee replacement. At 52 years old, Kevin felt he was far too young for such an invasive surgery.


That's when his “type A” personality paid off: Kevin had prepared for this moment years in advance. He and his wife had banked their mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) with Forever Labs in 2019. These "special cells" are capable of generating new connective tissue, such as cartilage, and Kevin decided it was time to put his own biological assets to work.


### **Results of Regenerative Therapy: From Knee Pain to a Five-Mile Run**


Just eleven months ago, Forever Labs’ Chief Medical Officer Tom Nabity performed a regenerative procedure, injecting Kevin’s stem cells directly into his knees to help regenerate the missing cartilage.


The results were nothing short of life-changing. In February of last year, Kevin was struggling with joint pain during short walks. By November—just nine months after the procedure—he successfully completed a five-mile trail run with no knee pain whatsoever:


> *"I went from not really being able to walk down a flight of stairs without feeling joint pain... to being able to run a five-mile trail run*

> *It’s just a life-changing quality of life improvement."*


### **Your Biology as Your Greatest Asset**


For Kevin, this experience has solidified his belief in the power of "biological insurance." By freezing his cells when they were younger and more potent, he was able to reintroduce that vitality into his body exactly when he needed it most.


Kevin’s story is a powerful reminder that while none of us are getting any younger, we now have the ability to use our own biology to feel and look healthier. Whether it's returning to the sports you love or simply moving through the world pain-free, banking your stem cells ensures your future self has the tools to stay in the game.
