The modern orthopedic experience is often point-of-care, meaning treatments are provided only after an injury has occurred. This "one-size-fits-all" approach—relying heavily on steroid injections that can lead to bone loss—fails to account for the unique biological symphony of the individual.


Dr. Turner, who honed his expertise treating elite professional athletes at the Andrews Institute, advocates for a move toward Precision Medicine. This philosophy recognizes that true health isn't just the absence of pain; it’s the preservation of high-level function. The goal is to ensure you can move freely, without restriction or pain, for decades to come.
