# New Partnership Makes Adult Stem Cell Banking and Regenerative Therapies More Accessible


Two leaders in the


**regenerative medicine**


industry, AcCELLerated Biologics and Forever Labs, have joined forces to make


**adult stem cell banking**


and


**personalized biologic treatments**


more accessible to patients across the country.


This collaboration connects advanced clinical treatments with secure, long-term cell storage, paving the way for exciting possibilities in longevity and healthy aging.


## Key Points


-


**Your own cells for healing:**


The partnership focuses on


*autologous*


therapies, which means using your own body's cells rather than relying on a donor.


-


**Pausing the biological clock:**


Patients can now seamlessly undergo a minimally invasive procedure to collect and freeze their youthful stem cells for future use.


-


**Advanced lab capabilities:**


Thanks to progressive medical guidelines, specifically in growing hubs like Florida, compliant facilities can harvest, store, and safely multiply your cells for specialized treatments.


## Why This Matters for Stem Cell Banking


As we age, our stem cells age with us. By banking your adult stem cells while you are as young and healthy as possible, you effectively "freeze time" for those cells.


If you experience


**joint pain**


, injuries, or age-related conditions later in life, accessing an inventory of your own "younger" cells can dramatically improve the effectiveness of regenerative treatments. Having your cells preserved in highly secure, properly accredited labs gives you a personal biological reserve for your future healthspan.


## The Science Behind It


### How It Works


Through a simple outpatient procedure, your physician collects stem cells, typically from bone marrow or


*adipose*


(fat) tissue. These cells are then transported to an advanced laboratory for processing and


*cryopreservation*


—a method of deep-freezing them at precise temperatures to keep them perfectly preserved and viable for decades.


### Expanding Your Healing Potential


What makes this new partnership particularly exciting is the integration of personal cell storage with cutting-edge clinical treatments. If a patient needs therapy later on, these stored cells can be safely thawed and even cultured (grown into much larger numbers in a lab). Care providers can then combine your expanded stem cells with other advanced treatments, such as


**platelet-rich plasma**


(


*PRP*


) and


*exosomes*


(tiny cellular messengers that instruct your body to reduce


**inflammation**


and heal), to create highly customized therapies.


## What This Means for You


This collaboration bridges the gap between advanced cell laboratories and your doctor's office. It empowers you to take an active, preventative role in your long-term wellness. By banking your cells today, you are giving your future self access to a toolkit of personalized therapies that can help maintain your vitality, manage


**physical wear-and-tear**


, and combat the biological effects of aging.
