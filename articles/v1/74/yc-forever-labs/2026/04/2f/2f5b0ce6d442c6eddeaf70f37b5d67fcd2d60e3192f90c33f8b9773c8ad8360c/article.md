# Forever Labs and Southern Surgical Arts to Reshape Longevity | Forever Labs

The future of health is no longer reactive. It is becoming more personalized, proactive, and powered by your own biology, allowing you to age on your terms and own your years.


The world of aesthetics is shifting beyond the era of surface-level beauty and entering the age of biological optimization. Today, the most discerning individuals are no longer just looking for a physical transformation, but also seeking a biological one.


This level of transformation requires a collaborative effort. That’s why we’re thrilled to announce a milestone partnership between


[Forever Labs](https://www.foreverlabs.com/) , the world’s first regenerative biotechnology platform, and


[Southern Surgical Arts](https://www.southernsurgicalarts.com/) , one of the nation’s premier destinations for aesthetic and reconstructive surgery.


### **The Science of Now**


Aesthetic procedures at Southern Surgical Arts can provide immediate, life-changing results. This new partnership with Forever Labs extends benefits to patients by enabling the long game of healthspan and lifespan.


Our bodies are powered by mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs), an internal repair crew responsible for helping regenerate bone, cartilage, skin, and more. But these cells are a finite resource: As we age, our stem cells age with us, declining in both number and potency. The cells we possess today are the youngest and most vital resources we’ll ever have.


### **Comprehensive Transformation, from Aesthetic to Regenerative**


For Southern Surgical Arts patients undergoing body contouring procedures such as liposuction, tummy tucks, or skin reductions, this partnership offers a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity.


During these procedures, adipose (fat) tissue containing a high concentration of MSCs is typically discarded. Now, patients can choose to have a sample of that tissue collected and sent to Forever Labs’ ISO-accredited facilities in the United States to be processed and cryopreserved for a lifetime of future use.
