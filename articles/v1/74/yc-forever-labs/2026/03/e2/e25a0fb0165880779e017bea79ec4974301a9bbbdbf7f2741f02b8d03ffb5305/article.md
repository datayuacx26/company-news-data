# The Future of your Biology with Forever Labs and The Michigan Center for Regenerative Medicine

What if the best solution for your aging joints was actually a younger version of you? That was the core theme of The Michigan Center for Regenerative Medicine’s


[recent webinar](https://regenerativemedicinemichigan.com/webinar-registration/) featuring Forever Labs CEO Kevin Virgil.


The conversation bridged the gap between personal patient success and the cutting-edge regulatory shifts making advanced stem cell therapy accessible right here in the U.S.


### **From Bone-on-Bone Osteoarthritis to Trail Running**


The session kicked off with Kevin Virgil sharing his personal experience as an MCRM patient. Suffering from end-stage osteoarthritis and having lost 75% of his knee cartilage, Kevin was told a total knee replacement was his only option. Instead, he opted for an


**autologous stem cell procedure**


(using his own stem cells, from his own bone marrow).


The results? Gradual but profound. Nine months post-procedure, Kevin was back to running five-mile trails pain-free, a feat that seemed impossible just a year prior.


### **The Power of Culture Expansion**


The "big reveal" of the night centered on stem cell culture expansion. Currently, most U.S. procedures are limited to the number of cells harvested in a single day. However, with shifting right to try regulatory frameworks emerging in states


[like Florida](https://www.foreverlabs.com/articles/regulatory/floridas-new-stem-cell-law-what-it-signals-for-physicians-patients-and-the-future-of-regenerative-me) , Forever Labs is currently building a state-of-the-art lab to change that.


By "expanding" or growing your own stem cells in a controlled environment, doctors will be able to:


- **Increase dosage**


to deliver significantly more cells to a single site for potentially better outcomes.


- **Treat multiple areas**


and start addressing knees, shoulders, and the spine all in one session.


- **Help patients own their future**


by enabling them to


[store their youngest, healthiest cells today](https://www.foreverlabs.com/articles/blog/why-store-your-young-msc-stem-cells) to use for age-related conditions decades down the line.


### **Why Autologous Matters**


The doctors emphasized a critical distinction:


[Autologous (your own) vs. Allogenic](https://www.foreverlabs.com/articles/blog/why-your-own-stem-cells-outperform-exosomes?utm_content=410394327&utm_medium=social&utm_source=facebook&hss_channel=fbp-100213109290950) (someone else's) cells. While medical tourism often leads patients to clinics overseas using umbilical or placental cells, the team stressed the safety and quality control of


[using your own genetic material](https://www.foreverlabs.com/articles/blog/why-your-own-stem-cells-outperform-exosomes?utm_content=410394327&utm_medium=social&utm_source=facebook&hss_channel=fbp-100213109290950) , which helps reduce the risk of rejection or exposure to unknown viruses.


### **Other Key Takeaways**


- **Florida is the New Frontier:**


With recent legislation,


[Florida is rapidly becoming a hub for advanced stem cell therapies](https://www.foreverlabs.com/articles/regulatory/floridas-new-stem-cell-law-what-it-signals-for-physicians-patients-and-the-future-of-regenerative-me) (like culture expansion) that previously required traveling abroad.


- **One visit for a lifetime of therapies:**


Banking your stem cells once allows you to multiply them on demand for future orthopedic or age-related needs.


- **Function over film:**


While Kevin’s MRIs showed remarkable improvement, the doctors noted that improved function and pain relief are the true markers of success, as imaging doesn't always tell the full story of how a patient feels.


- **Safety remains paramount:**


Autologous treatments prepared in cGMP-certified labs (like the one Forever Labs is building) offer the


[highest level of safety](https://www.foreverlabs.com/articles/blog/is-stem-cell-therapy-safe-five-myths-debunked) and protections.
