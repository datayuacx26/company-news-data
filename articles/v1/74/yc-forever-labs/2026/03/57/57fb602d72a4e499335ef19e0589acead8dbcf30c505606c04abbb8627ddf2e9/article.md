### **Building a Foundation Through Prehab and Movement**


A core pillar of maintaining lifelong mobility is the concept of prehab. Rather than waiting for a structural failure, prehab focuses on strengthening the body’s intrinsic support systems and forming proper functional movement habits early on.


By learning about the nuances of our bodies and identifying areas of weakness, practitioners can help patients build a "buffer" that protects the joints from the wear and tear of repetitive activity. Preparing for your future self means training today for the eventual physical shifts that occur as we age, ensuring we maintain the muscle mass and strength necessary for a high quality of life as we age.


### **Precision Diagnostics and the Regenerative Path**


To provide truly proactive care, we can use advanced tools to see the body in motion. Dr. DeWald highlights the importance of dynamic ultrasound, a live diagnostic tool that enables physicians to observe how tendons and ligaments interact in real-time during the specific movements that define your sport or lifestyle.


When early signs of degeneration are detected, regenerative therapies such as Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) and autologous stem cell treatments offer a way to stimulate the body's natural healing processes and help slow the progression of conditions like arthritis. Both specialists agree that banking your younger, more potent stem cells with providers like Forever Labs is a critical biological resource for the future. As Dr. DeWald explained: “The cool thing now is that we can use those \[stem cells\] right away, but we can also bank them. And patients can have those stored. So if they want to use them in ten years and 20 years, they have those there".


### **It Takes a Village to be Your Best Self**


Achieving optimal health is a collaborative effort between the patient, physician, physical therapist, and other holistic professionals. Dr. DeWald often notes that a medical procedure represents only 50% of the solution. The remaining 50% rests on the patient’s commitment to rehabilitation and corrective exercise.


Just as professional athletes rely on a dedicated team of specialists to optimize their performance, every individual should feel empowered to build their own performance team. By partnering with experts who understand your unique bodily quirks and long-term goals, you can navigate your health journey with confidence and precision.
