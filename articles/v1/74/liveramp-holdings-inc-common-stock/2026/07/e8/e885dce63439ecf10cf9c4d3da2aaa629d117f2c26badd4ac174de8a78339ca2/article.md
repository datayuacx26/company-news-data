## FAQs about deterministic identity coverage


### What is a people-based identifier?


A people-based identifier connects customer interactions across devices and channels using authenticated signals instead of third-party cookies. These identifiers help marketers recognize audiences more securely and consistently.


### How does deterministic identity differ from probabilistic identity?


Deterministic identity is supported by authenticated, verified signals to recognize individuals, while probabilistic identity uses statistical models and inferred connections. Deterministic approaches provide greater accuracy and consistency for audience activation and measurement.


### Why is first-party data important for identity?


First-party data provides the authenticated customer information needed to build identity strategies. When combined with identity resolution, first-party data helps marketers connect customer interactions, activate audiences, and measure campaign performance across channels.


### How does identity support cookieless advertising?


Identity solutions help marketers recognize and engage audiences in environments where third-party cookies are unavailable or limited. This enables audience activation and measurement across browsers, devices, and other omnichannel environments while relying less on third-party cookies.
