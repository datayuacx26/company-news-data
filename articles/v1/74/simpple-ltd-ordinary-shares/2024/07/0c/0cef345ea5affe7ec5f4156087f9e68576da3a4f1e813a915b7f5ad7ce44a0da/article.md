###


SIMPPLE Ltd. Unveils Key Milestones at CleanEnviro Summit Singapore 2024 (CESG)


SIMPPLE Ltd. (NASDAQ: SPPL) recently showcased three pivotal organizational advancements at the CleanEnviro Summit Singapore (CESG) 2024, a premier global event for the Environmental Services industry held biennially. Hosted at the Marina Bay Sands Expo and Convention Centre from June 19-21, 2024, this year's summit was a platform for innovation and collaboration, featuring cutting-edge technologies and forward-thinking solutions for environmental management.


###


Key Milestones Announced


**1. Partnership with CenoBots and Launch of Spot Floor Cleaning Robot**


SIMPPLE Ltd. announced a strategic partnership with CenoBots, introducing Singapore’s first spot floor cleaning robot. This innovative solution aims to enhance workforce productivity and public hygiene standards. The robot, equipped with advanced cleaning capabilities, represents a significant leap forward in the automation of routine cleaning tasks, ensuring cleaner and safer environments in public spaces.


**2. Facelift of the Multifunctional Robot**


The company unveiled a revamped version of the world’s first multifunctional robot, which now integrates security, concierge, and cleaning functions. This updated model is part of the Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA) Advanced Digital Solutions (ADS) scheme, allowing end users to adopt this revolutionary technology at a subsidized rate. The robot’s multifunctionality offers a comprehensive solution to facility management, addressing various operational needs within a single platform.


**3. Onboarding of RATSENSE to the SIMPPLE Partner Ecosystem**


SIMPPLE Ltd. welcomed RATSENSE into its Partner Ecosystem, aiming to enhance pest management and control within its Integrated Facility Management (IFM) platform. This collaboration will enable in-depth analysis and improved pest control measures, making the SIMPPLE Software a more holistic and efficient solution for facility managers.


###


Distinguished Guests and Technological Innovations


The event was graced by notable dignitaries, including Grace Fu, Minister for Sustainability and the Environment, Senior Ministers of State Dr. Amy Khor, Dr. Poh-Koon Koh, and Senior Parliamentary Secretary Baey Yam Keng. These esteemed guests, along with representatives from the National Environment Agency (NEA) and international delegations, explored the latest technological advancements presented by SIMPPLE Ltd.


Reflecting on our showcase at CESG 2022, SIMPPLE Ltd. highlighted the progress made over the past two years. The company demonstrated how Artificial Intelligence (AI) can drive automation through the Detection, Decision, and Deployment of Gaussian Robotics cleaning robots. This year, with the award of their process patent, SIMPPLE Ltd. showcased the interoperability of different robot brands, including Gausium Official, LionsBot International, CenoBots, and KABAM Robotics. This capability underscores SIMPPLE’s commitment to creating an IFM platform that future-proofs building operations and maintenance.


###


Looking Forward


SIMPPLE Ltd. remains dedicated to expanding its ecosystem by onboarding more robot brands and integrating progressive technology solutions into its open-platform SIMPPLE Software. This approach ensures added value for end-users and addresses the evolving challenges in building operations and maintenance.


As CESG 2024 concluded, SIMPPLE Ltd. extended heartfelt gratitude to its partners, clients, and visitors, including IFSC PTE LTD, Gaussian Robotics, and those who experienced their innovations at the Experiential Zone.
