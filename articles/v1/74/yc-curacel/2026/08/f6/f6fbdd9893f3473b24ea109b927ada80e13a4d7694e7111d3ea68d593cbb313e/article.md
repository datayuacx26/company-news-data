# A presenter at AI Compass Lagos 2026 speaking beside a slide titled From Support AI to the Super Workforce.

AI is easy to demonstrate and hard to trust at scale. A model can impress in minutes, yet a financial institution still has to connect it to live systems, protect customers, prove the business case and decide who is accountable when it fails.


That tension shaped[AI Compass Lagos 2026](https://aicompass.crl.to/) , held on 13 August at Radisson Blu Anchorage in Victoria Island. The final check-in reconciliation verified at least 42 external participants, including speakers, with 24 named organisations represented.


The room included NITDA, Access Bank, FCMB, Wema Bank and ALAT, ARM, AXA Mansard, Cornerstone Insurance, Coronation Insurance, Consolidated Hallmark Insurance and CHI Life, NEM Health, SUNU Health, Avon Healthcare, Bastion Health, HCI Healthcare, Lifeworth HMO, Zenor Healthcare, Lendsqr, Seamless Technologies, ShipBubble, Suplias Technologies, Comply54, SpectreNG and other technology and professional-services participants.


## Policy Set the Opening Context


The formal[programme](https://aicompass.crl.to/agenda) opened with Barr. Abdullahi Abubakar Aliyu, representing NITDA, speaking on AI policy and the future of digital financial services. Starting with policy placed every later discussion inside the same reality: AI adoption in finance has to satisfy public trust, accountability and market development at the same time.


The keynote opened the formal programme with AI policy and the future of digital financial services.


## Curacel Centre Stage Moved from Tools to Workflows


Curacel Centre Stage followed with *The Super Workforce* . The presentation moved the conversation beyond assistants that answer questions towards agentic systems that can carry repeatable work across documents, support, payments, reconciliation and operations.


The important distinction was workflow design. Institutions still have to define the job, the approval point, the exception path and the measure of success. Agentic AI works only when the operating model around it is clear.


Curacel moved the conversation from support tools to a Super Workforce built around agentic AI.


## Insurance Put Claims, Risk and Trust on the Table


The first panel brought together Dr Sanni Asishana, Group Head of Medical Services at NEM Health; Oluwarotimi Adediji, Chief Technology Officer at Cornerstone Insurance; and Segun Aboderin, Chief Technology Officer at Consolidated Hallmark Insurance and CHI Life.


Their session framed AI through claims, risk, distribution and trust, the points where automation meets customer outcomes and regulated decisions. A short Curacel Auto demonstration followed the panel, keeping the agenda connected to practical application.


Dr Sanni Asishana, Oluwarotimi Adediji and Segun Aboderin joined the AI in Insurance panel on claims, risk, distribution and trust.


## Banking and Finance Shifted the Focus to Operating Leverage


The next panel featured Oladipo Alabede, Divisional Head of Payments and Merchant Solutions at FCMB; Evi Jessy Emeurude, Head of Engineering at ALAT, Wema Digital Bank; and technology and digital-transformation leader Oladipupo Aina.


The banking discussion centred on payments, risk operations, service delivery and resilience. It was followed by a Curacel Support AI demonstration, showing how the day moved from sector questions into a specific operating workflow before breaking for lunch and networking.


The AI in Banking and Finance panel focused on payments, risk operations, service delivery and resilience.


## Embedded Finance Connected AI to Distribution


After lunch, Grace Effiom, Head of Enterprise at Lendsqr; Ms Oke Egbi, Senior Director of Embedded Finance at Seamless Technologies; and Jordan Ajibola, Co-Founder and CEO of ShipBubble, examined AI in embedded finance and lending infrastructure.


Their mix of lending, enterprise technology and logistics-platform experience widened the discussion beyond traditional institutions. The question became how AI can support distribution, credit and new financial products across the platforms customers already use.


Grace Effiom, Ms Oke Egbi and Jordan Ajibola brought lending, enterprise-technology and platform perspectives to the embedded-finance session.


## The Day Closed with How Leaders Use AI at Work


The final feature was a fireside conversation with AI strategist and digital-transformation leader John Orakwe, moderated by Jake. It brought the agenda back to people: how individual AI habits become team workflows, where AI fails, and where human judgement must remain final.


That sequence mattered. AI Compass moved from policy to infrastructure, then through insurance, banking, embedded finance and leadership practice. The flow showed that production AI is not one model or one department. It is a connected operating decision.
