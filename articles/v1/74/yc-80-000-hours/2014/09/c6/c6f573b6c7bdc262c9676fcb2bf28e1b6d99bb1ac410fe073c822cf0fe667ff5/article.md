# Earning to give

### What if you want to donate part of your income but don’t want to earn to give?


If you don’t want to change your career in order to donate more (or aren’t ready to), but you still want to use your income to help others, you can start by donating some of it to effective charities.


[Here’s](https://www.givingwhatwecan.org/best-charities-to-donate-to-2024) some advice on where to donate.


You might even consider taking the[🔶10% Pledge](https://www.givingwhatwecan.org/pledge/) from Giving What We Can — where you pledge to donate 10% of your income over your working life to whichever charities you think are most effective. (Or, to test it out,[pledge 1% of your income for a limited time](https://www.givingwhatwecan.org/get-involved/trial-pledge) .)


This can be a way to ramp up to earning to give, or just a way to have an impact without affecting your career choice. For example, many of the staff at 80,000 Hours have taken the 🔶10% pledge — we’ve found it to be a meaningful way to help others, even outside of our day-to-day work.


You can take the pledge in just a few minutes. It’s likely to be the most significant thing you can do **right now** to do more good with your career.
