# Vertiv Appoints Li Ning as Chairman of Greater China and Promotes Edward Cui to President of Greater China

**Columbus, Ohio** \[March 1, 2022\] - Vertiv (NYSE: VRT), a global provider of critical digital infrastructure and continuity solutions, today appointed Li Ning as Chairman of Greater China and promoted Edward Cui to President of Greater China. Both appointments are effective immediately.


Vertiv provides critical digital infrastructure solutions and services, including power, cooling and infrastructure management systems, for data centers, communication networks, and commercial and industrial facilities.


Li Ning has spent more than 25 years with Honeywell in APAC, most recently as Chairman of Honeywell Greater China, and serves as a strategic consultant to Vertiv. He holds a Bachelor’s Degree from Xi’an Jiaotong University and an EMBA Degree from China Europe International Business School. As Chairman of Vertiv Greater China, Li Ning will assist the China leadership team in reaching growth targets.


Edward Cui has been with Vertiv for nearly 25 years and has extensive management experience in critical infrastructure fields. He has been leading the Greater China supply chain, business units, marketing, sales, and APAC R&D organizations during the past years. Cui holds a Bachelor’s and a Master’s Degree from Tsinghua University and an EMBA Degree from St. Louis University. As President of Greater China, Cui will be fully responsible for the business of Vertiv Greater China.


Li Ning and Edward Cui will lead the Vertiv Greater China team with a focus on our customers and the concept of "In China, For China," while continuing to create value for customers, partners, and society. They will do this through innovative technologies, solutions, and services, together with customers, to build a green and low-carbon digital future and energy world.


Robert Zhu retires after four years as President of Vertiv Greater China, having led the team to continuous growth of the business in the region.


**About Vertiv**


Vertiv (NYSE: VRT) brings together hardware, software, analytics and ongoing services to ensure its customers’ vital applications run continuously, perform optimally and grow with their business needs. Vertiv solves the most important challenges facing today’s data centers, communication networks and commercial and industrial facilities with a portfolio of power, cooling and IT infrastructure solutions and services that extends from the cloud to the edge of the network. Headquartered in Columbus, Ohio, USA, Vertiv employs approximately 21,000 people and does business in more than 130 countries.
