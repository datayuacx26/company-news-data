# Vertiv Announces PowerHouse Group as Vertiv Hub For Western Canada

**Columbus, Ohio** \[March 1, 2022\] – Vertiv (NYSE: VRT), a global provider of critical digital infrastructure and continuity solutions, announced[PowerHouse Group](https://www.powerhousegroup.com/home) as its new Local Vertiv Office (LVO) for the entire Western Canada region, effective today.


As an LVO, PowerHouse Group will be the exclusive Vertiv representative providing Vertiv’s data centre and communications infrastructure products, solutions, technical knowledge, application expertise and local support for Western Canada, from British Columbia to Manitoba, and areas in the Canadian North. The partnership strengthens Vertiv’s presence across the region with a consistent voice and shared resources to better serve customers. The existing and long-established Vertiv™ Services organization will continue to provide service, maintenance, warranty and parts support in the region.


“PowerHouse Group’s consulting capabilities and project management knowledge add immense value to our customers and to our partnership. This expertise, backed by Vertiv’s portfolio, factory support and local service organization, will take the regional experience to a higher level,” said John Hewitt, President of Vertiv, Americas.


PowerHouse Group is a Calgary-based, Western-Canada-focused data centre and communications infrastructure company that offers data centre design, project management, turnkey capabilities and mission-critical commissioning services.


“With Vertiv, we have an exciting opportunity to work with an industry leader, expand capabilities to both Vertiv and PowerHouse customers, and Vertiv customers with the world-class support they’ve come to expect over the years,” said Michele Meston, President and CEO of PowerHouse Group. “We look forward to growing the Vertiv presence in Western Canada, leveraging our deep experience in the data centre industry to provide customers with the continuity and support they need to succeed.”


For information about Vertiv’s portfolio of thermal management, power management, IT management and monitoring, racks, integrated systems, and services, visit[Vertiv.com](https://www.vertiv.com/en-us/) .
