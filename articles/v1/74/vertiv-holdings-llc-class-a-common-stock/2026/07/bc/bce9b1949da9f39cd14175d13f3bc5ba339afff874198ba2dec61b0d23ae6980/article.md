# Vertiv introduces backup power for personal devices and business applications

**COLUMBUS, Ohio** \[January 29, 2026\] – Vertiv (NYSE: VRT), a global leader in critical digital infrastructure, today introduced a new generation of compact uninterruptible power supply (UPS) systems for connected homes and workspaces. The[Vertiv™ PowerUPS 200](https://www.vertiv.com/en-us/products-catalog/critical-power/uninterruptible-power-supplies-ups/vertiv-powerups-200-line-interactive-desktop-series/) family, available in Essential, Standard, and Premium versions with capacities from 600 to 1500 VA, combines modern design with enhanced usability for workstations, point-of-sale systems, gaming consoles, smart devices, and home entertainment setups. These 120V models are now available in North America.


“As the way people work and live continues to evolve, so do their expectations for reliable, easy-to-use power protection,” said Ramesh Menon, vice president of IT systems solutions at Vertiv. “The Vertiv PowerUPS 200 family combines our proven expertise in single-phase power with a new design focused on user experience, extending enterprise-level reliability to homes, offices, and modern connected spaces.”


Designed for ease of use and everyday reliability, the Vertiv PowerUPS 200 Essential, Standard and Premium Line Interactive Series include practical benefits that make power protection simpler and more intuitive. Among the most notable features are user-replaceable batteries, which allow users to safely extend product life, and a straightforward interface featuring runtime LED indicators on Vertiv PowerUPS 200 Essential Line Interactive Series models, a color LCD display on Vertiv PowerUPS 200 Standard Line Interactive Series models, and a full color rotatable LCD display screen on the Vertiv PowerUPS 200 Premium Line Interactive series for clear system visibility.


All models include Type-A and Type-C USB charging ports, providing convenient power for mobile and connected devices. The new design also integrates details such as a dedicated mute button for alarm management and recyclable packaging, complementing proven performance features including automatic voltage regulation (AVR) for stable output, optimized internal design for consistent performance across a wide voltage range. The Vertiv PowerUPS 200 Premium model also provides pure sinewave output, making it the ideal choice for sensitive devices and gaming consoles.


The Vertiv™ PowerUPS 200 Essential Line Interactive Series, Vertiv™ PowerUPS 200 Standard Line Interactive Series, and the Vertiv™ PowerUPS Premium Line Interactive Series include models with up to ten total outlets, depending on configuration. Compact dimensions are suitable for modern workspaces, while the three-year limited warranty, including battery coverage, provides added confidence for users.


The Vertiv PowerUPS 200 Series expands Vertiv’s single-phase desktop UPS portfolio, which also includes the network class Vertiv™ PSI5 Line-Interactive UPS and Vertiv™ Liebert® GXT5 UPS. Together, these solutions form part of Vertiv’s power management ecosystem, supporting reliable, efficient, and future-ready digital operations across home, office, and edge environments.


For more information about the Vertiv™ PowerUPS 200 series and Vertiv’s portfolio of power and thermal management solutions that extend from the desktop to the edge, visit[Vertiv.com](https://www.vertiv.com/en-us/) .
