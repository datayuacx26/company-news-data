# Vertiv Announces New Rack Cooling System for Edge IT Deployments

**Atlanta, Georgia** \[Jan. 14, 2019\] – Vertiv introduced new and upgraded thermal management products at AHR Expo today, headlined by the new Vertiv™ VRC rack cooling system, designed to fit easily into racks in small server rooms, network closets and similar edge computing environments. The Vertiv VRC can be configured and installed to meet the space restrictions of any edge site. It will be available globally in Spring of 2019.


The Vertiv VRC delivers 3.5 kW of IT cooling, uniquely deploying load-matching, variable-capacity compressors and fans, making it one of the industry’s highest capacity, most efficient cooling systems for IT cabinets. It comes in three heat rejection options: a self-contained configuration, with options for room or ceiling plenum heat rejection discharge, and a split system using a refrigerant loop and outdoor condenser.


Vertiv also introduced enhancements to the ceiling-mounted Liebert Mini-Mate precision cooling system, the Liebert DCD rear door heat exchanger, and the Liebert DSE free-cooling economization system. The[Liebert Mini-Mate](https://www.vertiv.com/en-us/products-catalog/thermal-management/room-cooling/liebert-mini-mate-ceiling-mounted-variable-capacity-cooling-17.5kw/) , for small IT and edge spaces, uses 30 percent less energy than previous models, features variable capacity compressors and fans, and Liebert iCOM™ for remote monitoring and control. The Liebert DCD Active is a powered fan unit for high-density rear rack doors and supports capacities up to 50kW per rack without significant modifications to existing data center infrastructure.[Liebert DSE Optimization](https://www.vertiv.com/en-us/about/news-and-events/articles/videos/save-even-more-money-with-the-liebert-dse/) is a control enhancement that increases unit efficiency by up to 50 percent.


“The edge of the network is increasingly critical, requiring IT infrastructure solutions that deliver higher levels of visibility, control and performance,” said John Schneider, vice president and general manager, Thermal Management, at Vertiv. “With the introduction today of the Vertiv VRC and important enhancements to the Liebert Mini-Mate, we are delivering best-in-class capabilities to ensure reliable, efficient cooling of today’s smaller IT spaces.”


The ceiling-mounted Liebert Mini-Mate features remote monitoring, management and alarm troubleshooting, available through the integrated[iCOM CMS Monitoring and Control System](https://www.vertiv.com/en-us/about/news-and-events/corporate-news/2019/vertiv-announces-new-rack-cooling-system-for-edge-it-deployments/~/link/21487dc6b6ad428f97f8eb4f95a43a37.aspx)


. The iCOM CMS can be accessed through building management systems, secure desktops or a mobile app.


The Liebert Mini-Mate is available in 3, 4 and 5 ton configurations. It is available now in the U.S.


The Liebert DCD Active unit uses powered fans to pull server exhaust air through the cabinet to increase capacity. It can operate with the rear-door Liebert DCD, a passive chilled water heat exchanger. Used separately or together, the two offerings deliver clear access to rack equipment and are suitable for hot aisle/cold aisle and irregular configurations. Liebert DCD Active will be available in February in the U.S.


The Liebert DSE Optimization is an enhancement to the controls used by the cooling unit, providing finer tuning of liquid refrigerant temperature and pressure setpoints, leading to a reduction in power consumption by condenser fans during mid-to-high outdoor ambient temperatures.


The Vertiv VRC, Liebert Mini-Mate, Liebert DCD, Liebert DCD Active and Liebert DSE Optimization are featured at AHR Expo at booth B2521. For information on products and solutions from Vertiv, visit[VertivCo.com](https://www.vertiv.com/en-us/)
