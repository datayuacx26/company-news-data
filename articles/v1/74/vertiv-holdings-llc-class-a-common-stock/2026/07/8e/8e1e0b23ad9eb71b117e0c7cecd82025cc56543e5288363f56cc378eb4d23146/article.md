# Emerson Network Power Wins Gold Awards for Thermal and Power Products

**Columbus, Ohio** \[September 15, 2016\] – Emerson Network Power, a business of Emerson (NYSE: EMR) and the world's leading provider of critical infrastructure for information and communications technology systems, today announced that the Liebert® iCOM™-S Thermal System Control and the Liebert eXM Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) have been chosen as best new products of 2016 in their respective categories by the readers of Consulting-Specifying Engineer (CSE).


Emerson Network Power earned the two honors in the 12th annual CSE awards that celebrate innovation and technological advancement in engineering in 15 different categories. The Liebert iCOM-S Thermal System Control took the Gold award in the Building Automation Systems (BAS), Controls, Energy Management classification, and the Liebert eXM UPS received the Gold award in the Emergency, On-Site, Standby Power category.


"We are very proud of these awards, especially since Consulting-Specifying Engineer readers determine which products stand out from the crowd," said Steve Hassell, president of data center solutions for Emerson Network Power in North America. "Emerson Network Power will continue to drive innovation throughout the data center with technologies that improve data center efficiency, availability, productivity and flexibility."


The Liebert iCOM-S Thermal System Control manages and controls the entire data center cooling system for up to 50 percent higher efficiency, improved protection and greater productivity. The thermal control system provides a single point for managing the entire cooling infrastructure and gaining quick access to actionable data, system diagnostics and trending.


The Liebert eXM UPS is a high efficiency power system optimized for midsize applications. The three phase UPS combines economical ownership with the scalability, flexibility, intelligence and reliability that ensures optimal performance to power a range of critical loads.


For more information about Liebert iCOM-S, the Liebert eXM UPS and other data center infrastructure solutions, visit[www.EmersonNetworkPower.com](http://www.emersonnetworkpower.com/) .
