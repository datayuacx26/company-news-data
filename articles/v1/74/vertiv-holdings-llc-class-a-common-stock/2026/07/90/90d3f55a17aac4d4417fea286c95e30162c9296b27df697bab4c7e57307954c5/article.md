# Vertiv Receives Frost & Sullivans 2025 Latin American Data Center Infrastructure for AI and HPC Company of the Year Recognition for Excellence in Best Practices

**Sao Paulo, Brasil** . \[March 11, 2026\] – Vertiv (NYSE: VRT), a global provider of critical digital infrastructure, announced today that it has been named **Latin American data center infrastructure for AI and HPC Company of the year 2025** by Frost & Sullivan, as part of its Best Practices awards program.


The recognition is based on an independent market evaluation conducted by Frost & Sullivan's team of analysts, which analyzed Vertiv's performance in terms of portfolio, regional execution capability, alignment with AI and HPC demands, and its focus on supporting clients who are designing and scaling high-density infrastructures in Latin America.


"Artificial intelligence and HPC workloads are redefining the way data centers are designed, operated and scaled in the region," stated Alex Sasaki, sales vice president at Vertiv in Latin America. "This recognition from Frost & Sullivan validates our regional strategy and our ability to support clients in one of the most relevant moments of technological transformation the industry has experienced. At Vertiv, we are focused on providing businesses with the capacity, scalability and speed of deployment that are required for AI and HPC, supported by the industry’s most complete portfolio of critical digital infrastructure solutions, global and local service and support capabilities, and consistent execution throughout Latin America."


Vertiv offers integrated power, cooling, racks, monitoring and services solutions, designed to support mission-critical environments with growing requirements for density, efficiency and resilience, associated with the deployment of AI and HPC.


In Latin America, Vertiv continues to identify market growth opportunities, strengthen relationships with clients from key industries and support infrastructure modernization and digital infrastructure expansion projects. This strategy seeks to align Vertiv's solution portfolio and service capabilities with real market needs, particularly in sectors that are accelerating the adoption of artificial intelligence, HPC and high-density architectures.


The Frost & Sullivan Company of the Year award recognizes organizations that demonstrate consistent leadership, effective execution and a solid competitive position in specific markets.
