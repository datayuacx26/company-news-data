# Thanking Our Customers

As we continue to navigate these challenging times, we’d like to take a moment to say thank you to our valued customers. They are integral in keeping our world connected, protected, equipped and provided for during this global pandemic. We are proud to partner with them to keep critical digital infrastructures operating around the world.


Vertiv and many of our customers have been designated as essential businesses by international authorities and government agencies. Together, our operations continue so that we can keep important data centers, communications networks, healthcare systems, governments and other essential services running and connected.


We are proud to partner with so many great companies and organizations to ensure the continuous flow of products and support. Our employees are working every day – from wherever that happens to be – to make sure we are doing all we can to bring the necessary products and support to keep our customers operational during these difficult times.


We appreciate all that our customers and partners are doing to keep the world moving forward. We are dedicated to ensuring that, together, we’re able to continue the vital roles we all play to support our global community.


Stay safe and stay healthy.


-Your friends at Vertiv
