# Serial consoles: Deploying resilient and connected AI-ready IT networks

High-performance computing (HPC) workloads, such as artificial intelligence (AI), are making IT networks and management more complex than ever. To keep operations running smoothly and support high-density infrastructure and applications, organizations must now focus on building networks that can withstand failures and adapt to changing demands.


Whether managing enterprise data centers, edge computing facilities, or hybrid environments, this guide provides actionable insights for creating resilient networks that can support businesses’ current needs while preparing for future challenges, such as:


- Managing distributed IT environments across multiple locations
- Maintaining security in complex hybrid environments
- Enabling efficient remote management capabilities
- Facilitating business continuity during network disruptions
- Addressing the needs of remote and hybrid workforces
- Meeting compliance requirements across different regions
