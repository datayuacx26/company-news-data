# Vertiv Names Tech Data as Authorized Distributor of Converged Solutions in Latin America

*Agreement comes amidst growth in the data center market in Central America and the Caribbean*


**Costa Rica** \[August 7, 2019\] –[Vertiv](https://www.vertiv.com/en-us/) today announced the expansion of its relationship with[Tech Data](https://www.techdata.com/) , the world’s leading end-to-end distributor of technology products, services and solutions. Amidst the increased demand for data center solutions in the Central America and Caribbean regions, the agreement with Tech Data broadens their reach outside of the US and EMEA markets and now extends into markets across the Central America and the Caribbean regions.


This agreement, now extending into new and emerging territories in Latin America, includes access to Vertiv’s data center infrastructure portfolio, including Vertiv critical power management, thermal management, infrastructure monitoring, IT management, integrated solutions, racks and power distribution units.


“Countries across the Central America and Caribbean regions are increasingly adopting IoT and edge computing. As a result, we’re seeing a great demand for data centers that are easy to build, quick to deploy and efficiently manage,” said Ricardo Duque, vice president of channel strategies for Vertiv in the Americas. “We work closely with our partners to ensure continuity and achieve breakthrough outcomes, from design and commissioning through deployment, operation and service”.


Tech Data’s understanding of the logistics of the sales process in the region and extensive network of resellers will help Vertiv promote its portfolio and expand its footprint in Latin America. Vertiv brings experience, technology leadership and scale to allow distributors in the region to pursue larger and more lucrative projects.


“Tech Data uses best practices to enable our resellers and vendors to engage the market. By expanding our distribution agreement with Vertiv, we can provide our partner ecosystem in the region with world-class solutions for power, cooling and data center infrastructures,” said Jan Michael de Kok, managing director, Northern Cone Region, for Tech Data in Latin America.
