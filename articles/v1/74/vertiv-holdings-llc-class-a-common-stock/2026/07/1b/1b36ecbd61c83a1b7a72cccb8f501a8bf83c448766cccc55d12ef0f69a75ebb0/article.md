# Vertiv Names Magdalena Mardones as Channel Sales Manager for Chile

**Santiago, Chile \[February 18, 2019\]** – Vertiv has announced the appointment of Magdalena Mardones as channel sales manager for Chile. Mardones is charged with leading the sales function within the local market, in addition to supporting Vertiv’s business partners.


Mardones has extensive experience in business development and marketing, as well as in the management of logistics and budgets. Her new position will allow her to lead the Vertiv strategy at the local level, with the objective to grow market share, solidify and grow the distribution network and develop commercial partnerships designed to strengthen the company’s offering. Through these synergies and greater efficiencies, she hopes to increase the visibility of Vertiv and its service portfolio to clients.


Mardones earned her business degree from Queen’s University in Belfast, Northern Ireland, and holds a Diploma in Business Administration and Marketing Strategy from the Universidad del Desarrollo *.* She previously worked as the Business Development Manager at Vertiv for the Southern Cone region.
