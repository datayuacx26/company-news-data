# Vertiv AI Roadshow in Atlanta, GA highlights AI infrastructure imperatives for future-ready AI factories

**COLUMBUS, Ohio** \[September 9, 2025\] – Vertiv (NYSE: VRT), a global leader in critical digital infrastructure, today announced the next stop of its global AI Solutions Roadshow in Atlanta, Georgia, will showcase how data centers can adapt to the growing demands of artificial intelligence (AI) through key infrastructure imperatives, including advanced cooling to manage extreme thermal loads, scalable power architectures to support accelerated compute, and integrated IT systems designed for rapid deployment and reliability. The event, scheduled for October 9, 2025, targets end customers including hyperscalers, cloud, neocloud, enterprise, consulting engineering firms, contractors, and channel partners.


“The growth of AI is transforming data center design and operation across every segment, from hyperscale to enterprise,” said Tony DeSpirito, vice president of enterprise sales at Vertiv. “Through the Vertiv AI Solutions Roadshow, we’re meeting directly with customers to discuss real strategies and technologies that are already helping them adapt for high-density AI environments while balancing efficiency, scalability, and reliability.”


The Atlanta event will also feature a fireside chat with John Dumler, senior vice president of data center design and engineering at DC BLOX, exploring the impact of AI on data center design and development. Dumler will share insights on how DC BLOX is addressing the region’s growing demand for high-performance, scalable digital infrastructure and how emerging AI workloads are driving opportunities for innovation, collaboration, and growth in the Atlanta area.


“The Southeast is emerging as a hub for digital innovation, and AI is accelerating that growth,” said John Dumler, senior vice president of data center design and engineering at DC BLOX. “I’m looking forward to sharing the advantages of designing facilities that not only meet the power and density requirements of AI workloads but also strengthen the digital backbone that supports regional industries and communities.”


The Atlanta stop, supported by local Vertiv representative, Joe Powell & Associates, is part of Vertiv’s AI Solutions Roadshow, which brings education, collaboration, and solution demonstrations to key innovation hubs in North America.


For more information about Vertiv’s AI Roadshow and solutions for AI-driven infrastructure, visitVertiv.com .
