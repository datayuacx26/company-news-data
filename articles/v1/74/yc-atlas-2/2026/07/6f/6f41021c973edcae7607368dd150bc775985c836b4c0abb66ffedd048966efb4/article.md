# change-log-2024-12-20

Dec 20th, 2024


AI Launch Week, Day 5 🚀


For **Day 5 of AI Launch Week** , we’re putting Zeus AI to work right inside your inbox. With **AI-Sorted Inbox Priorities** , your incoming tickets are now automatically categorized and prioritized based on urgency and importance. No guessing. Just focus on what matters most.


Here’s what is new:


## 📥 AI-Sorted Priorities


Zeus AI now reviews incoming tickets in real time, assessing **urgency, importance, and customer sentiment** . It then **auto-assigns a priority** to each ticket, so you know which ones to tackle first.


## 🚦 Auto-Prioritize Tickets Based on Urgency


Critical issues are flagged as top priority, while routine inquiries are set at a lower priority. Your team can focus on the most urgent matters without wasting time sorting tickets manually.
