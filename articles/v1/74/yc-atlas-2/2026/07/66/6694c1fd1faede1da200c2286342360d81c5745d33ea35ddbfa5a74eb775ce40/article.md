Mar 4th, 2025


🎮 Atlas + Discord: Bringing structure to community support


Atlas now integrates with **Discord** , making it easy to manage support conversations without losing track of important messages. Whether you're handling public discussions, private requests, or structured forums, you can now bring all your Discord interactions into Atlas.


### ▶️ See how it works in this quick video


### Key features


- **Seamless ticketing workflow** – Convert Discord messages into Atlas tickets automatically or manually.
- **Multi-channel support** – Connect general chat, forum channels, private VIP threads, and dedicated support forms.
- **Bidirectional sync** – Receive and respond to Discord messages directly from Atlas.
- **Multi-threaded conversations** – Keep multiple discussions organized with private ticket threads.
- **Real-time updates** – Messages sync instantly, so nothing gets lost.


### Getting started


---


- Go to[App Configuration > Integrations](https://app.atlas.so/configuration/discord) in Atlas.
- Select **Discord** and connect your server.
- Set up your preferred ticketing flow.
- Done—your Discord support is now fully integrated into Atlas.


Try it out today and see how much smoother Discord support can be with Atlas!
