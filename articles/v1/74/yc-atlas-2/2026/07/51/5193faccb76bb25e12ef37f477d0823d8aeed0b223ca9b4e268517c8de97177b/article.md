Feb 4th, 2025


📢 Atlas Broadcast


We've just rolled out Broadcast - a powerful tool to send announcements instantly to your preferred group of customers. Here’s what you get with the latest release:


🚀 **Multi-channel flexibility** – Reach customers where they are, whether it’s Slack, SMS, or email.


🎨 **Rich formatting** – Add images, GIFs, and styling to make your announcements pop.


⚡ **Scheduled delivery** – Ensure your updates get sent out as per your convenience, instantly or in the future


**📊 Analytics** - Analyze campaign effectiveness with real-time data


Let’s get into the details.


---


### Multi-channel


You can send announcements via Slack, SMS, or email, ensuring every message lands where it matters most. Whether it’s a critical outage update, a new feature launch, or a compliance reminder, Broadcast helps you reach the right people, on the right platform, at the right time.


---


### How it works


1. Find[Broadcast](https://app.atlas.so/broadcast) in the menu or hit` 'G + O'`
2. Click 'Add campaign' and select your channel & audience.
3. Craft your message, preview it, and schedule or send instantly.


Once you’ve sent out the campaign, you can also use Broadcast to view the campaign analytics.


---


In customer support, communication is everything. We hope Broadcast makes that a bit easier for you.
