# change-log-2025-03-18-1

[Back](https://atlas.so/changelog)


Mar 18th, 2025


Usability Upgrades


We’re rolling out a series of small but crucial upgrades to make Atlas smoother, faster, and more intuitive. Here’s what’s new:


### Zoom In/Out on Images


---


You can now zoom in and out of images right inside your Atlas inbox for better visibility. Perfect for inspecting screenshots, receipts, and detailed documents.


### Choose Your Notification Sound


---


Customize how Atlas notifies you! Pick between:


🎵 **Waterclick** - A crisp, subtle notification


🔔 **Doorbell** - A classic, attention-grabbing chime


Head to **Profile Settings > Notifications** to make your choice.


### New Hotkeys for Faster Ticketing


---


We’re making it easier (and faster) to navigate your inbox:


- **Close a ticket** with **E** (We’ll phase out “C” soon)
- **Change ticket status** with **S**


### Better Ticket Selection for Bulk Actions


---


We’ve increased the click area for easier multi-ticket selection—making it faster to handle multiple requests at once.


---


These updates are now live in Atlas! Try them out and let us know what you think.
