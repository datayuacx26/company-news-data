# Carlsmed, Inc. To Participate in the 2026 Canaccord Genuity Musculoskeletal Conference

CARLSBAD, Calif., Feb. 23, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Carlsmed, Inc. (Nasdaq: CARL) (“Carlsmed” or the “Company”), today announced that Mike Cordonnier, CEO and Chairman, will be presenting at the 2026 Canaccord Genuity Musculoskeletal Conference on March 2, 2026 at 11:30 AM CST in New Orleans, LA.


A live webcast will be available to registered participants. Following the presentation, the webcast replay will be available at[https://event.summitcast.com/view/d69xRqMspwymhwGUDdQoes/JC9qqvWhWggYawxGnDNtmz](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=5McKLtYUtdr4OgomW1bU334Fry3a74O7zY8E9SICR6GHi-w09yMfrITLFgsAWpwg9LoVchThFzWxAmmpSzUXG2l2IrC-ayPYXgCZyoH2uMCSi4ZiajH9rG2Z_QTAbR6A3nRMpz-qOqWRqGZT8RxkMqIu9WalZWxAVWQU0yaYgKHHPQsAiJq9PICejzVSHUu0HZZb8H7tsQplhct1qKkXRYIBGjLuxfxSSkqfPWzy3zOtIUCJilJVc21f-TqM6g7arLG3s7lZXDS-_V2LiOFEJg==)
