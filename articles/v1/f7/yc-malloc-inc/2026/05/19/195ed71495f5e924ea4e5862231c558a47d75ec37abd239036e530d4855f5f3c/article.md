Instead of wrapping your internet traffic in a loud protocol that screams “VPN,” our new system completely camouflages your connection. Threat actors frequently record encrypted traffic today so they can use future quantum computers to decode it later. Our new transport layer natively integrates **post-quantum encryption standards** , making your data permanently uncrackable to both present and future computing threats.
