Malloc LTD Selected for the Prestigious European Defence Innovation Scheme (EUDIS) Business Accelerator Cohort #3


**NICOSIA, CYPRUS — July 2026**


Malloc LTD, an AI-driven mobile cybersecurity company specializing in advanced spyware blocking and secure communications, today announced its selection into Cohort #3 of the European Defence Innovation Scheme (EUDIS) Business Accelerator. Out of a record-breaking 499 applications from across the European Union and Norway, Malloc is one of only 20 high-potential companies chosen to participate in this highly competitive, eight-month program.


Initiated by the European Commission and the European Defence Agency, the EUDIS Business Accelerator is designed to bolster Europe’s sovereign defense and dual-use technological base by fast-tracking breakthrough commercial innovations into the defense sector.

The program, which officially kicks off in September 2026, provides selected scale-ups with intensive, tailored commercial coaching, direct mentorship from defense industry specialists, and access to advanced technical testing facilities. Over the next eight months, Malloc will participate in five immersive, regional bootcamps across Europe, connecting directly with defense leaders, end-users, and specialized institutional investors. Upon successful completion of the milestones, Malloc will also receive a €120,000 seed-funding voucher to further accelerate its dual-use product trajectory.


> *“Securing a spot in Cohort #3 out of nearly 500 applicants is a powerful validation of Malloc’s core technology,” said Maria Terzi, CEO of Malloc. “Advanced spyware blocking and completely secure mobile communications are no longer just corporate necessities — they are critical requirements for modern defense infrastructure. Through the EUDIS framework, we look forward to adapting our AI-driven privacy solutions to meet the rigorous demands of the European defense ecosystem and contributing to a safer, more resilient Europe.”*


Malloc’s inclusion highlights the growing urgency for intelligent mobile defense software capable of intercepting sophisticated, zero-day spyware threats and safeguarding sensitive data channels in real time.


## About Malloc LTD


Malloc LTD is an AI-driven mobile cybersecurity company specializing in spyware blocking and secure communications. Utilizing proprietary, on-device machine learning models, Malloc monitors mobile environments to detect and block unauthorized surveillance, prevent spyware execution, and encrypt communication channels in real time. Malloc helps individual users and enterprises worldwide protect their data and safeguard sensitive mobile intelligence. Learn more at[mallocprivacy.com](https://www.mallocprivacy.com/) .


## About the EUDIS Business Accelerator


The European Defence Innovation Scheme (EUDIS) Business Accelerator is a landmark initiative powered by the European Defence Fund (EDF). Implemented alongside major European defense ecosystems and industrial partners like Starburst Aero, the program aims to close the gap between commercial tech innovators and European defense requirements, providing up to 20 exceptional companies per cohort with funding, elite mentoring, and strategic go-to-market channels. Learn more at[www.eudis-business-accelerator.eu.](http://www.eudis-business-accelerator.eu./)
