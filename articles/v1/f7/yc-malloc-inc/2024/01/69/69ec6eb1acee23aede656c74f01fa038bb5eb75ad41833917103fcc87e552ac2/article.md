Exciting news, Malloc users!


We’re thrilled to share some groundbreaking updates about the Malloc VPN app that will take your online protection to a whole new level. At Malloc, we’ve been hard at work, focusing on delivering an enhanced user experience with improved privacy and lightning-fast performance.


What’s Changing?


We’re excited to introduce a significant shift — a move towards a more advanced on-device VPN approach. This upgrade is designed to bring you not only heightened privacy but also a substantial boost in speed. Here’s what you can expect:


1. 🌐 More Privacy: With our new on-device VPN, your data stays where it belongs — on your device. Forget about connecting to specific countries; enjoy a truly private online experience with your information remaining secure and local.

Why the Change?

How Does This Benefit You?


• Privacy Empowered: Your data stays on your device, enhancing your online privacy.
