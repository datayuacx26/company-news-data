# Fun Family Reunion Ideas & Activities

# Make Memories with These 10 Family Reunion Ideas


Every once in a while, it’s crucial to pause the bustle of modern life and take time for family. Family is what grounds us, brings joy, and makes life meaningful.


Family reunions hold significance as they reinforce bonds, bridging generational gaps and sustaining familial connections. These gatherings offer a platform to cherish shared histories, stories, and traditions, preserving cultural heritage.


They provide a rare opportunity for dispersed family members to interact, nurturing a sense of belonging and mutual support. Reunions are an opportunity for family members to reconnect — or perhaps meet new family members for the first time.


So often, family reunions happen around a special event, like a wedding or the holidays. But having time dedicated to only family — not distracted by other priorities — means more quality bonding. Is it time to start planning your family reunion?


## 10 Fun Family Reunion Ideas


Whether your family is big or small, we have whipped up 10 family reunion ideas that all ages will enjoy.


### Cook Up Memories with Cooking Classes


Not sure how to plan a family reunion? Start with a structured activity. If your family is based near New York City, or wants a reason to visit, consider attending a[group cooking class](https://cocusocial.com/cooking-classes/NYC) taught by top New York chefs! Making and enjoying food is one of the best ways to bond as a family.


Learn a culinary skill together, then recreate it at the next reunion. Experiment with a new cuisine, or learn a new way to make an old classic.


A CoCuSocial cooking class is a hands-on experience that lasts around two hours. Professional chefs teach attendants how to make dishes like dumplings, tapas, or French pastries.


Bring out some friendly competition to see who makes a sushi roll best! Or pretend you’re on a televised cooking show and judge each other’s creations. Most importantly, enjoy a homemade meal or dessert together at the end.


Plus, New York is an excellent destination for a family reunion weekend, with much to do once cooking class is over. Bon appétit!


### Mix it Up with Mixology Classes


For those more interested in learning the art of the cocktail, consider a[group mixology class](https://cocusocial.com/cooking-classes/NYC/Mixology) when planning a family reunion. Because sometimes, the same old beer and wine selection can get old. Switch it up and learn from the best pro mixologists in town! Plus, if your family has any[new 21-year-olds](https://cocusocial.com/blog/21st-birthday-ideas/) in the house, this is a fun way to welcome them to adulthood.


This fun, NYC-based mixology classes offer private instruction for families of up to twenty. All participants partake in their handcrafted concoctions at the end of the class.


An Old Fashioned, Mai Tai, or Margarita may become your new specialty if you host the next family reunion. Whether you prefer shaken or stirred, with flavored syrup or a simple garnish, get ready to impress your friends once you nail these refreshing cocktails. Of course, you also apply the techniques you learn to craft mocktails. Cheers!


### Plan Some Picnic Fun in the Sun


Family reunion games, outdoor scenery, and picnic food— this kind of party is always a crowd pleaser. With low cost and low maintenance, a picnic-style family reunion is simple and timeless. When you have an outdoor family reunion, no one has to use their “inside voice” — kids are free to run around. Plus, minimal cleanup is required afterward!


First, decide if you would like to host an at-home backyard picnic or opt for an easily accessible spot on the beach, in a public park, or at a scenic overlook. A public place means less pressure on the host. However, popular public places may require reserving a picnic table beforehand.


Additionally, make sure bathrooms are accessible for all attendees. And don’t forget to bring sunscreen, bug spray, and plenty of water to all summertime picnics.


The casual nature of a family picnic means folks can drop by and leave as time allows. The outdoors puts people at ease, especially with a gentle breeze and beautiful nature to admire.


Finger food (like sliders) and picnic games (like cornhole) keep the party going all day. Whether it is catered or everyone packs their food, all generations love a classic family picnic.


### Connect Digitally with Virtual Classes


It is not always possible for family reunion activities to occur in person. Families are often spread out over state lines and time zones, with commitments that make it tough to plan a day that works for everyone.


That’s why some families opt for a virtual reunion or virtual component during an in-person reunion. That also accommodates those with travel, health, or mobility concerns.


So, what kinds of virtual activities bring people together? Browse[virtual cooking classes](https://cocusocial.com/cooking-classes/Online) and see what will entice your family. See something everyone loves to eat, but no one knows how to cook? Learn how to make ravioli from scratch or homemade empanadas over Zoom!


Depending upon the chosen cooking lesson, participants will need to gather some basic cooking supplies and ingredients. On the day of the class, set aside a couple of hours for quality instruction, and enjoy the fruits of your labor when finished!


### Crown the Winner at Game Night


Whether your family is playful and silly or fiercely competitive, playing games is a fun way to spend a family reunion. What will be a crowd-pleaser for your family: board games or video games?


Perhaps a homemade backyard obstacle course, a card game, or an improv game will forge new bonds and connections. Pick the most inclusive family reunion games for your family’s size and age range.


Playing a low-stakes game can lighten the mood and make everyone laugh. Consider factors like time limits and age to find the best games for your group.


For large families, choose a game with a round-robin element to ensure everyone participates. Conclude the event with a closing ceremony where you award a fun prize or recognition, like a homemade paper crown signed by all family members. Make it an annual tradition!


### Explore Nature Together


Whether planning an all-day family hike or a simple reunion outdoors, exploring nature together is a low-cost, high-reward way to spend time together. It’s perfect for the no-frills family who doesn’t need anything fancy to have a good time. After all, there’s nothing better than nature’s beauty to put your family in a state of wonder and awe.


Gauge your family’s fitness level and plan accordingly. Not all family members will enjoy a high-exertion experience. Consider what is available locally, like parks and recreation departments, nature museums, and state forests. Or seek out a destination reunion, like a favorite beach or mountain lake.


Is the weather not cooperating? Take the family to the zoo or an aquarium for an indoor nature experience.


Whether your family catches summertime fireflies, picks flowers in the spring, skis in the winter, or loves to walk amongst autumn leaves — nature awaits you. Just be sure to dress accordingly and leave no trace!


### Unleash Creativity with Arts and Crafts


Is your family creative? Creative family reunion themes like arts and crafts give people something tangible to do while reconnecting. Whatever you make will be a memento for years to come, especially when you incorporate the reunion year on the homemade keepsake. Choose a safe craft that all ages can do, or offer a few different ones.


For a sentimental activity, decorate picture frames. Set aside time at the reunion to pose for a family photo to put inside the homemade frame. Then sign each other’s frames!


For holiday reunions, make Christmas ornaments. Make matching tye-dye family shirts or decorate matching baseball caps for a summertime reunion. Not into matching? Have everyone paint their own birdhouse or individual mug.


Some arts and crafts projects are easy to do at home; others are best completed at an art studio. Whatever you make together, it will become a cherished keepsake. Family arts and crafts time will nurture creativity in children and bring out the kid in everyone else.


### Host the Family Olympics


On your marks, get set, go! Get ready to host family reunion outdoor games — Olympics-style. Just as all countries come together in the name of good sportsmanship, all branches of the family tree will reunite for some friendly competition between in-laws. Go all out with opening and closing ceremonies, toy medals, and playing the favorite song of each winner instead of a national anthem.


Now, it’s time to choose the games. Select a combination of simplified backyard races or activities — or make up your own games. Opportunities abound to get both creative and athletic when designing your family Olympics. And for those not up for heavy movement, the family Olympics always need announcers, scorekeepers, and cheerleaders! Everyone can get involved, even if they don’t want to exert themselves.


Family Olympics may take place over a day, week, or weekend. Ensure everyone stretches, hydrates, and doesn’t get hurt attempting an overly ambitious feat. Document the event with “athlete” interviews, behind-the-scenes training, and other fun videos.


### Spend Quality Time at Monthly Potluck Parties


Who says family get-togethers can only happen once a year? Spend quality time with family every month at a potluck party!


When there is no pressure for one person to cook food for everyone, much of the stress of hosting a family reunion is removed. Decide if one person would like to host or if your family would prefer rotating hosting duties between different family members’ homes each month.


When family reunions are monthly, there is less pressure to make it perfect. The more quality time you spend together, the more authentic connections form, and the tighter knit you will be as a family. And if someone can’t make it, there’s always next month! Of course, monthly reunions are best for families who live within driving distance of one another.


To ensure it all runs smoothly, create a meal signup so that there is an even balance of entrees, desserts, drinks, and sides with no repeat dishes. A potluck with only chips and dip won’t do! That will also ensure that everyone likes at least one dish served.


### Plan a Family Camping Trip


Does your family love the great outdoors? Take a family camping trip somewhere beautiful. Spend a night, weekend, or — for the most in-depth family bonding time — a whole week immersed in nature.


Has your family been feeling too tied to technology lately? Unplug and see how it brings the family closer. Eliminate modern distractions and just focus on each other’s company.


As you hike, fish, or tell stories around the campfire, generations will learn and laugh with one another. Pick a time of year that suits everyone’s schedules and promises good weather.


Alternatively, you can try glamping. Glamping refers to “glamorous camping.” It is the perfect middle ground if your family loves nature but prefers to maintain some comforts of home, like mattresses. Whether you find the ideal glamping accommodations or prefer to rough it the old-fashioned way, camping is a great way to enjoy the beautiful outdoors with family.
