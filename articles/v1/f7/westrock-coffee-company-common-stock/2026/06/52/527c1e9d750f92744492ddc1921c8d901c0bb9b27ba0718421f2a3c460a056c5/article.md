# Westrock Coffee Releases 2025 Sustainability Report, Achieving 100% Responsibly Sources Coffee and Tea

ON
**9 de junio de 2026**


** 3 min read


New report marks completion of the company’s landmark responsible sourcing goal and details priorities for supply chain resiliency.


LITTLE ROCK, Ark., June 9, 2026 – Westrock Coffee Company (Nasdaq: WEST), a leading manufacturer of private label coffee and tea in North America, today released its 2025 Sustainability Report, announcing the achievement of a foundational company goal set in 2021: 100% of its green coffee, soluble coffee, and tea purchases are now responsibly sourced through second- or third-party verified programs.


The 2025 report highlights progress in sustainable manufacturing and Westrock Coffee’s three responsible sourcing pathways—Raíz Sustainability™, Real Roots™, and third-party certifications—and outlines the company’s progress toward its 2030 goals across three strategic pillars: Sustainable Supply Chain & Ecosystems, Education & Empowerment, and Transparency & Traceability.


“This report reflects our 2025 progress—but it also reflects our vision,” said Brooke Cantrell, Vice President of Sustainability & Insights for Westrock Coffee. “We are building a company designed for change, grounded in partnership, and committed to creating lasting value for the people who make it all possible.”


Looking ahead, Westrock Coffee’s 2026 strategy advances progress across all three of its 2030 goal pillars. The company will expand farm resiliency and gender equity programming across Raíz Sustainability™, broaden sourcing versatility, deepen farmer-to-customer connections and traceability, and continue driving manufacturing efficiency and ecosystem restoration in the communities where it sources and manufactures coffee and tea.

**About Westrock Coffee Company**
Westrock Coffee is a leading integrated coffee, tea, flavors, extracts, and ingredients solutions provider in the U.S., providing coffee sourcing, supply chain management, product development, roasting, packaging, and distribution services to the retail, foodservice and restaurant, convenience store and travel center, CPG, non-commercial, and hospitality industries around the world. With offices in 10 countries, the Company sources coffee and tea from 35 origin countries. For more information, please visit WestrockCoffee.com
