# Westrock Coffee Showcases Advanced Beverage, Extract, and Flavor Solutions at IFT Expo 2024

ON
**July 3, 2024**


** 3 min read


At the event, Westrock Coffee’s innovative beverage offerings will be sampled and displayed for expo attendees.


“We are excited to return to the IFT Expo, a premier event for the food and beverage industry that highlights the innovations Westrock Coffee is proud to offer,” said Kyle Newkirk, Executive Vice President – Global Sales at Westrock Coffee. “From our hot coffee and innovative frozen frappes and slushies to our responsibly sourced energy products, Westrock Coffee is recognized for capturing consumer preferences across the beverage spectrum. The IFT Expo is the perfect platform to present our products to key decision-makers, and we are thrilled to showcase how our solutions meet the evolving demands of beverage consumers.”


As the “brand behind the brands,” Westrock Coffee currently provides over 20 million cups of coffee to the world daily and is a leader in emerging beverage categories including energy, cold coffee, and ready-to-drink (RTD) products. The Company’s expertise in beverage and flavor trends allows customers to bring the most unique and distinct products to market across a range of product categories and formats. Visitors to the Westrock Coffee booth at the IFT Expo can experience the following products, among others:


- **100% Arabica Cold Brew:** A delicious cold brew coffee made with our Rainforest Alliance Certified 100% Arabica concentrate. This medium roast delivers hints of chocolate, nut, and caramel for a versatile and sustainable cold brew experience.
- **Premium Iced Americano:** Enjoy barista-quality iced americanos in seconds! Our concentrated espresso shot alternative is unsweetened and requires no fancy equipment. Just pour, dilute, or pump for a delicious medium roast with chocolate, nutty, and citrus notes.
- **Iced Chai Latte:** Skip the steeping! Our 5:1 black tea-based Chai All-in-One Concentrate is used to craft delicious, iced chai lattes in seconds. Just pour, dilute, or pump and enjoy authentic chai flavor anytime, anywhere.
- **Energy Refresh & Focus Green Tea:** Unsweetened green tea for a natural energy boost and sharpened focus, available in dispenser or grab-and-go bottle formats.


To learn more and experience the range of Westrock Coffee innovations and beverage offerings, IFT Expo attendees can visit the Westrock Coffee display at Booth #1312.
