## About GSX


The Global Security Exchange (GSX) is one of the world’s largest annual security conferences and trade shows. Powered by ASIS International, it brings together thousands of physical security, cybersecurity, and emergency management professionals to learn, network, and discover new security technologies. The event is designed to tackle everything from corporate security challenges to emerging global threats.


Be there when the worldwide security industry reconvenes in September to stay informed, connected, and prepared for what’s next.
