## 1. What Cloaked Does Well


Cloaked deserves credit on several fronts, based on what its own site and independent coverage confirm.


- **A genuinely bundled privacy suite.** Cloaked pairs data broker removal with unlimited email and phone aliases, a password manager, spam call blocking, VPN, dark web and SSN monitoring, and $1 million in identity theft insurance, all in one subscription. For someone who wants a single app rather than several separate tools, that is a real convenience.


- **Simple household pricing.** Cloaked’s Family plan covers up to four people for $24.99 per month billed annually, with the full feature set included for everyone on the plan. For a household that wants aliases and light removal together, this is easy to understand.


- **Verified security certifications.** Cloaked’s trust page documents ISO 27001 and ISO 27701 certification, a completed PCI DSS v4.0.1 audit, and SOC 2 Type 2 compliance. These are checkable credentials, not just marketing language.


- **Included identity theft insurance.** Cloaked’s homepage states coverage of up to $1 million in financial losses from identity theft and fraud, included standard rather than sold as an add-on. For users who place a premium on this kind of backstop, this is a real differentiator from removal-only competitors.


- **A large total site directory.** Cloaked’s own site-coverage page lists 1,000+ individual sites it monitors and works to remove data from, spanning both data brokers and other companies (retailers, employers, and similar organizations) that may hold a person’s contact information.


For users who mainly want a bundled privacy app, aliases for everyday sign-ups, and basic broker cleanup as one part of a bigger toolkit, this may be enough.

## 4. Verification and Reporting for Personal Data Removal


Verification is the difference between a service telling you it removed your data and showing you it did.


Optery’s Exposure Reports show where a person’s profile appears at data broker and people-search sites, using what the company calls “patented view links” so users can see the profile without necessarily contacting the broker directly themselves. Optery adds Removals Reports approximately every 90 days that include before-and-after screenshots which is the only personal data removal service that provides detailed exposure and removal reports, complete with screenshots, to demonstrate the effectiveness of its services.


Cloaked’s dashboard, per its help center, shows a running “Total records removed” count, “Days Protected,” and in-progress versus completed broker scans. This gives a user a sense of overall progress, but it is a different kind of evidence than a screenshot showing a specific profile disappearing from a specific site.


Optery’s key advantage here is not just that it says removals happened, they also show where profiles were found and, later, screenshot evidence tied to the removal. That is a meaningfully more auditable model for a privacy professional who needs to document that a specific exposure was actually resolved, and it is also easier for an everyday consumer to understand at a glance.
