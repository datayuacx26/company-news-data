# ProhostAI now integrates with OwnerRez

**ProhostAI now officially integrates with OwnerRez. If you host on OwnerRez, you can connect your account to ProhostAI in a few minutes and add AI-powered guest messaging, automated cleaning scheduling, smart upsells, AI reviews, AI workflows, team chat with an AI teammate, and digital guidebooks on top of your existing OwnerRez setup — no migration required.**


We’re excited to announce that ProhostAI now officially integrates with[OwnerRez](https://www.ownerrez.com/?utm_source=prohost&utm_medium=website&utm_campaign=prohost) , a popular property management platform used by vacation rental hosts and managers worldwide.


If you’re an OwnerRez user, you can now connect your account to ProhostAI and start using AI-powered guest messaging, automated cleaning scheduling, smart upsells, AI reviews, AI workflows, team chat with an AI teammate, digital guidebooks, and more.


## About OwnerRez


[OwnerRez](https://www.ownerrez.com/?utm_source=prohost&utm_medium=website&utm_campaign=prohost) is widely recognized in the vacation rental industry for channel management, CRM, property management, accounting, messaging, and websites. It integrates with major vacation rental channels to sync availability, rates, rules, and listing content, while also giving hosts fast direct-booking websites, payment processing, inquiry management, guest communication, and renter agreements.


OwnerRez is also one of the few independent PMS platforms in the space, with no outside investors steering product decisions. Its PeopleFirst approach shows up in how closely the team listens to customer feedback and ships improvements for working hosts and property managers.


## What this means for OwnerRez hosts


Once you connect your OwnerRez account, ProhostAI syncs your listings, reservations, and guest messages. From there, the AI starts learning your properties, house rules, and hosting style through AI Memory.


Here’s what you unlock:


- **AI Guest Messaging** : Automate 90%+ of guest messages with instant, context-aware replies across your channels. Turn on Autopilot for 24/7 responses or review every draft before sending.
- **Automated Cleaning Scheduling** : Turnover cleanings are scheduled automatically, assigned to the right cleaner, and tracked with timestamped photos and checklists.
- **AI-Powered Upsells** : ProhostAI detects gap nights and sends offers to extend stays. You can also promote late checkout, gear rentals, or local experiences to increase revenue from each booking.
- **Task and Maintenance Management** : The AI detects maintenance issues from guest messages and reviews, creates tasks, and routes them to the right team member automatically.
- **Digital Guidebooks** : Give guests property-specific guidebooks with check-in details, house rules, and local recommendations.
- **AI Reviews** : Analyze guest reviews for actionable feedback and generate personalized host review drafts with AI.
- **AI Workflows** : Build automations that trigger on bookings, guest messages, or completed cleanings. Describe what you want in plain language and the AI Builder creates the workflow for you.
- **Team Chat** : Coordinate with your team and your AI teammate in real time with built-in chat, read receipts, presence indicators, shared photos, and linked operational context.


## Getting started takes 2 minutes


Connecting is simple:


1. Log in to your OwnerRez account in your browser.
2. In ProhostAI, go to **Listings → Add listings → Add connection → OwnerRez** .
3. Click **Authorize ProhostAI** in the popup.
4. Your listings import automatically and the AI starts learning right away.


That’s it. No technical setup and no complicated configuration.


For a detailed walkthrough, read our[OwnerRez connection guide](https://www.prohost.ai/learn/getting-started/topic/connecting-listings/ownerrez) .
