# From reviews to revenue: AI Review Analyzer

## Why reviews are the ultimate feedback loop


**ProhostAI ingests your Airbnb reviews as they arrive and uses AI to surface themes, sentiment, and recurring issues across your whole portfolio. Instead of reading every review one by one, you see what guests consistently praise and what to fix — and the AI can draft on-brand responses for you to approve.**


"88 % of guests read at least three reviews before booking (AirDNA, 2025)."


## Pipeline breakdown


1. **Data ingestion** : pulls Airbnb reviews instantly as they come in
2. **AI engine** : AI instantly analyzes each review
3. **Task generation:** If a guest mentions actionable feedback, AI automatically generates a task in the ProhostAI Tasks feature
4. **Auto assignment:** From there, the task gets automatically routed to the right team member via the auto-assignment feature


## Case study: beachside duplex


**Issue:** recurring "musty smell." **Result:** AI Task auto-created HVAC filter replacement. Post-fix, negative mentions dropped 95% and occupancy rose 8% in shoulder season.
