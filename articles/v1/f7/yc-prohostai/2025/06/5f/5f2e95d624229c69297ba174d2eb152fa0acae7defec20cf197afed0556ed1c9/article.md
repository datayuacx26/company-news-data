# Ask AI: your always‑on hosting copilot

## Information overload for multi‑property hosts


**Ask AI is ProhostAI's conversational assistant trained privately on your portfolio — reservations, listings, messages, tasks, cleanings, finances, and reviews. Instead of digging through Google Docs, PDFs, and Slack threads, your team types a natural-language question and gets an instant, accurate answer drawn from your own operation.**


Google Docs, scattered PDFs, Slack threads—tribal knowledge lives everywhere. Team members waste 5-7 h/week hunting answers.


## What sets Ask AI apart


Think of it as ChatGPT *trained exclusively on your portfolio* —and kept private. Type natural‑language questions and receive:


- **Direct answers** ("Check‑out time Villa Aurora?" → "10 a.m.")
- **Drafted messages** ("Write late checkout reply in Spanish…")
- **Operational insights** ("Which property gets most Wi‑Fi complaints?")


## Under the hood


- **Vector database** stores embeddings of every doc, rule, and past message.
- **Realtime sync** keeps new reservations and rule changes searchable within 60 s.
- **Role‑based access** so cleaners only see housekeeping SOPs, not owner revenue.


## Two minute onboarding


1. Upload docs (.pdf, .docx, .md) or let Prohost scrape Airbnb descriptions.
2. Invite teammates via email.
3. Start asking questions—web or iOS/Android.
