# Introducing ProhostAI Autopilot

## The late‑night ping problem


**ProhostAI Autopilot answers guest messages 24/7 on Airbnb, Hostaway, and Guesty. It reads each message, pulls facts from your AI Memory, drafts a reply in your voice in under a second, and scores its confidence — high-confidence replies auto-send, while anything uncertain waits for your approval, so you stay off your phone at 2 a.m.**


Every seasoned host knows the 2 a.m. drill: a guest can't find the Wi‑Fi password; another's flight is delayed and they're begging for a midnight check‑in. Respond late and your communication rating tanks; stay glued to your phone and *your* quality of life tanks. Legacy "canned responses" help, but they're blunt instruments that ignore context, language, and tone.


## How AI Messaging Autopilot can help


ProhostAI's **AI Messaging Autopilot** connects to your inbox on Airbnb, Hostaway or Guesty—listening in real‑time. Instead of using messaging templates, it:


1. **Interprets intent & sentiment** using a hospitality‑tuned AI.
2. **Searches your AI Memory** (listing rules, prior conversations, house manual).
3. **Drafts a reply** in under 700 ms, mirroring your brand voice.
4. **Scores confidence** ; high‑confidence replies auto‑send, others wait for your thumbs‑up.


> "We cut median response time from 9 min to **38 sec** while doubling five‑star comms reviews." — *Sara K., Superhost (12 units)*


## Deeper dive: the two‑layer AI Memory


- **Static layer** : property facts—door codes, amenities, check‑in times.
- **Dynamic layer** : conversation history, recent guest issues, and micro‑feedback. The AI learns from every message you send and analyzes your edits to improve from every message. No need to do anything special to "train" the AI.


Our nightly retraining job fine‑tunes the model, so the more you host, the smarter it gets—no prompt engineering or manual knowledge base maintenance required.


## Setting it up


1. **Connect listings** via our official Airbnb connection or Hostaway/Guesty integrations in less than 1 min.
2. **Import house rules** automatically detected from Airbnb.
3. **Send test messages** ; watch suggestions appear.
4. **Watch AI learn everything about your listings and how you host** in AI Memory. You will see thousands of memories created about your business.
5. Over the course of a week or two, you will see the accuracy of messages grow rapidly, just like a human employee, but way faster at learning & perfect memory!
6. **Enable Autopilot once message accuracy hits 90%+ —or keep manual approval.**

## FAQ & troubleshooting


**Will guests know replies are AI‑generated?** No; messages appear as native host replies.


**Multi‑language?** 32+ languages supported; model understands any major language. Replies will be in English, but will be autotranslated by Airbnb for the guest.


**Edge cases?** Escalation rules route unusual asks to you.
