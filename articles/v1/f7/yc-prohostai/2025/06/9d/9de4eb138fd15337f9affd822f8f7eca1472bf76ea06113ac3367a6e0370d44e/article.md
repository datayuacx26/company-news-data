# Guidebooks that update themselves

## PDF Guidebooks get never read


**ProhostAI generates dynamic AI guest guidebooks from your listing details — check-in steps, Wi-Fi, house rules, and local tips — that stay current instead of going stale like a PDF. Guests open them from a link on any phone with no app to download, so they self-serve the basics and you field fewer repetitive messages.**


Static PDFs become outdated quickly, causing guest frustration and increasing message volume.


## Enter AI Guidebooks


ProhostAI creates dynamic guidebooks using AI that:


- Automatically populate content
