## **Is This A Good Transfer Bonus?**


Avianca LifeMiles is one of those transfer programs that is not immediately well-known to the average Joe, but is a fan favorite amongst those who are deeply invested in the points game. LifeMiles redemptions can be extremely high value, with the ability to fly intra US for just 7,500 miles or to Europe for just 35,000 miles.


## **U.S. <> Europe In Business Class For 63k Pts**


LifeMiles nonstop business class fares to/from Europe typically price at just 63,000 pts each way, with minimal taxes/fees (fuel surcharges are not passed onto the user). These can be easily found and booked via the LifeMiles website. Additionally, they can be booked over email or phone.


Notably, economy prices are not half bad either. Prices for economy flights can vary, but you can expect to see up to 30k miles one way, with reports of as low as 16.5k one way (for certain airport combinations).


## **Intra United States For 7,500 Miles**


LifeMiles splits the U.S. into three different zones:


- **Region 1:**


Connecticut, Delaware, District of Columbia, Kentucky, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, Vermont, Virginia


- **Region 2:**


Alabama, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Iowa, Kansas, Louisiana, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, Montana, Nebraska, North Dakota, Oklahoma, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Wisconsin


- **Region 3:**


Arizona, California, Colorado, Idaho, Nevada, New Mexico, Oregon, Utah, Wyoming


Flights within each zone start at just 7,500 miles each way. Going across zones isn't too bad either, with rates crawling up to the 13,500 miles bucket when going from Zone 1 / Zone 3.


## **JFK To LIS In Business Class For 35k Pts**


Some things in life make little sense, and this offering makes no sense when looking at LifeMile's award chart. However, this is an insanely high value booking you could snag to fly business class to Europe.


However, this booking is not as friendly on the way back, costing the expected 63k in business.


## **U.S. <> New Zealand In Business Class For 80k Pts**


You can book Air New Zealand flights using LifeMiles for just 80,000 miles with minimal taxes/fees (fuel surcharges are not passed onto the user). Unfortunately, Air New Zealand award flights are considered extremely difficult to find, but can provide some incredible value otherwise.


## **U.S. <> Istanbul In Business Class For 63k Pts**


Even with Turkish Airline's direct devaluation of its own points, you can still get some high value redemptions flying on Turkish metal using LifeMiles. Business class flights from various U.S. airports go for as high as 63k one way, making it a fantastic & high value way to experience Turkish culture in style.


## **Conclusion**


Avianca LifeMiles have tremendous value when used correctly, but do note some of the hidden difficulties with making some of these bookings come to life. LifeMiles is known to sometimes have IT issues here and there, but if you can score seats, you're in for some tremendously outsized value.
