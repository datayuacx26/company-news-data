### How to maximize your rewards


To wring every last dollar of value out of that $395 annual fee, you need a deliberate strategy centered on global spend, pass timing, and point pooling.


- **Funnel all overseas spend to the card:**


Because foreign transactions earn an uncapped 3x points, make this card your primary driver for every international hotel stay, transit ride, meal, and shopping trip abroad.


- **Stack with Bank of America:**


Link an eligible Bank of America checking or savings account to score an automatic 10% points bonus on all card purchases. That instantly supercharges your 3x foreign spend, dining, and airfare categories to a whopping


**3.3x points per dollar**


!


- **Pace your quarterly passes:**


The 8 lounge passes and 8 Wi-Fi passes arrive in batches of two every calendar quarter. Space out your travel throughout the year so you don't let these valuable vouchers expire.


- **Pool points for maximum value:**


Take advantage of free point sharing with up to 10 friends or family members with no transfer fees. Stacking points makes it significantly easier to book high-value, long-haul partner flights using your annual 25,000-point Global Companion Award.


### Things to consider before applying


While the Atmos Summit card offers incredible upside, there are a few structural caveats to keep in mind before you apply:


- **Strict "Use-It-or-Lose-It" Pass Pacing:**


Rather than giving you all eight lounge and Wi-Fi passes upfront, they drop as two per calendar quarter. If you only take one major trip a year, you will forfeit unused passes from previous quarters.


- **Locked-In Loyalty Currency:**


Points accumulate directly within the Atmos Rewards ecosystem. Unlike flexible credit card currencies like Chase Ultimate Rewards or Amex Membership Rewards, Atmos points cannot be transferred to external hotel loyalty programs or non-partner airlines.


If you do not regularly fly Alaska, Hawaiian, or their Oneworld partner network, offsetting the annual fee becomes a lot tougher.


### The Verdict


The


[Atmos Sumit card](https://www.bankofamerica.com/credit-cards/products/alaska-airlines-infinite-credit-card/?utm_source=roametravel) is hands-down one of the absolute best airline loyalty cards on the market.


If you frequently fly Alaska or Hawaiian Airlines, or if you drop serious cash on foreign transactions while traveling abroad, adding this card to your wallet is a total no-brainer.
