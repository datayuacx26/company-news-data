## **What is a points transfer bonus?**


A transfer bonus is a limited-time multiplier applied to a program’s standard transfer ratio. Ratios vary by issuer, card, and partner: many are 1:1, while current examples include Amex to Hilton at 1:2, Citi to Accor at 2:1 for many cardholders, and Capital One to EVA at 4:3. Chase to Hyatt can also be 1:1 or 4:3 depending on card and account eligibility. The table above shows the effective partner points produced by each live bonus instead of assuming every transfer starts at 1:1.


Why they matter: a transfer bonus is one of the only ways to durably increase the redemption value of points you have already earned. Award space is priced in the partner’s currency, so a bonus directly lowers the cost of every award flight or hotel night you book at that partner during the campaign window. The catch is that once points leave your card portal, they are bound to the partner’s program rules, including its award chart, fees, and expiration. Bonuses reward people who already know what they want to book.


## **When should you transfer points?**


Transfer to a partner only when you have a specific redemption in mind, the award space is showing live, and the partner’s price for that redemption, after the bonus, beats the next best alternative (your card’s travel portal, a different transfer partner, or paying cash and earning miles). Once you transfer, you cannot reverse the move, and the miles you receive sit under that partner’s rules: they may expire, the partner may devalue its award chart, or the route you were targeting may disappear.


In practice, the strongest plays are time-sensitive: a transfer bonus to a partner whose award space you have already confirmed, for a trip you would book this week or next. Speculative transfers, moving points to “stock up,” are a common and expensive mistake, because the partner miles you collect are no longer flexible across other programs.


## **How to use a transfer bonus, step by step**


1. Find a live bonus in the table above whose source program you have points in.


2. Search the partner award you want to book on


[Roame SkyView](https://roame.travel/) . Confirm the seat or room is bookable at a reasonable mileage price.


3. Transfer just enough points to cover the booking. Never transfer for “later.”


4. Book within minutes, before the seat is gone or the partner re-prices the award.


Historical example (promotion expired June 30, 2026): an Air France business-class JFK–CDG award priced at 50,000 Flying Blue miles required 40,000 Membership Rewards points during the former 25% Amex-to-Flying-Blue bonus.
