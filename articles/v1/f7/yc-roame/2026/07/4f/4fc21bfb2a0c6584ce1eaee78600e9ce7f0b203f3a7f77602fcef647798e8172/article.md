### Why We Like This Card


The Atmos™ Rewards Business Card is a high-value tool for small business owners who frequently fly Alaska Airlines or Hawaiian Airlines. With a low annual fee of $70 for the company plus $25 per card, it quickly pays for itself through core operational travel savings: you and up to six companions get a free checked bag ($70 round-trip value per person) and preferred boarding on every flight booked with the card. Meeting the $6,000 annual spend threshold unlocks an annual $99 Companion Fare, making it effortless to offset the annual fee on a single trip. You earn 3x points on Alaska and Hawaiian flight purchases, 2x points on common business expenses like gas, EV charging, shipping, and local transit (including rideshares), and 1 status point for every $3 spent toward earning Atmos elite status. Company owners with a Bank of America business checking or savings account also receive an automatic 10% rewards bonus on all card purchases.
