### Why We Like This Card


Bank of America lets you pick where your best cash back rate lands, and for the first year that rate is 6%. You choose one of six categories: gas and EV charging stations, online shopping, dining, travel, drug stores and pharmacies, or home improvement and furnishings. Groceries and wholesale clubs earn a flat 2% with no setup on your end, and every other purchase earns 1%.


The cap is what decides whether this card is worth a slot in your wallet. Only your first $2,500 of combined quarterly spending gets the 6% and 2% rates, so the ceiling is $150 back per quarter, or $600 across the year, if you push all of it through your choice category. Past $2,500 you drop to 1%. The 6% rate is also a first-year promotion. In year two the choice category pays 3%, which turns that same maxed-out quarter into $75.


Travel is one of the choice categories, which means you can point 6% at flights and hotels for twelve months. Cash back is still cash, though. It will not move to an airline or hotel partner the way Chase Ultimate Rewards or American Express Membership Rewards points do, so this card earns its keep next to a transferable-points card rather than instead of one. If your checking or brokerage balances already sit at Bank of America, enroll in BofA Rewards and every purchase picks up a bonus on top of the cash back.

Good for your first 60 days, then 5% on later transfers. Balance transfers cannot be used to pay another Bank of America account.

- **Digital wallet support:**

- **Alerts and paperless statements:**


Set email or text alerts for balances and due dates, and switch off paper statements from Online Banking.
