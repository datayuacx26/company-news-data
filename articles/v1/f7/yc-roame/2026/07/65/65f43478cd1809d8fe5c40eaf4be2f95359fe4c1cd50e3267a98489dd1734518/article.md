## The Key Rule of Allegris First Class Tracking


When hunting for this product, you must keep one golden rule in mind:


**Allegris First Class is currently exclusive to select Airbus A350-900 aircraft flying out of the Munich (MUC) hub.**


While Lufthansa also flies its newly designed Allegris


*Business Class*


out of Frankfurt on its Boeing 787-9 fleet, those specific planes do not carry a First Class cabin. Additionally, legacy First Class cabins continue to fly on older Boeing 747-8, Airbus A380, and Airbus A340-600 aircraft. If you want the fully enclosed Allegris suites, you must target the A350 departing from or arriving in Munich. To understand how this fits into the broader alliance landscape, check out the


[Roame Star Alliance guide](https://roame.travel/blog) to maximize your booking strategy.

## The Ground Experience: Munich vs. Frankfurt


Flying First Class on Lufthansa unlocks some of the finest ground services in commercial aviation. However, your experience will look slightly different depending on your routing:


- **Departing from Munich (MUC):**


Because your Allegris flight departs here, you will have access to the dedicated Lufthansa First Class Lounge. If your plane is parked at a remote stand rather than a gate, you will be driven directly to the aircraft tarmac in a private luxury vehicle.


- **Connecting through Frankfurt (FRA):**


If you are booking a connecting itinerary that starts or ends with a leg through Frankfurt, you can still visit the world-renowned, standalone Lufthansa First Class Terminal. Here, you bypass the main airport entirely, enjoy a private security screening, dine à la carte, and receive a guaranteed limousine or Porsche transfer straight to your connecting flight.


## How to Find and Book Lufthansa Allegris First Class?


Unlike legacy Lufthansa First Class cabins, the new ultra-private Allegris First Class suites can only be booked through Lufthansa's own Miles & More program. Because there are only three of these exclusive suites on select Airbus A350-900 aircraft, partner airlines like Air Canada Aeroplan and Avianca LifeMiles are completely blocked from accessing this specific award space. You can explore supported programs on the


[Roame Airline Loyalty Programs overview](https://roame.travel/programs) to see how different alliances handle space, but your best bet is checking the dedicated


[Lufthansa Miles & More overview page on Roame](https://roame.travel/programs/lufthansa) for program-specific rules.


The tough news for US award travelers is that Miles & More does not partner with any major flexible credit card point currencies like Chase, Amex, or Capital One. To see which cards map to which programs across the board, you can reference the


[Roame Transfer Partner Cheat Sheet](https://roame.travel/transfer-partners-cheat-sheet) to plan your earning strategy. If you want to book these suites using rewards, your options are limited to:


- **The Lufthansa Miles & More Credit Card:**


Earning miles directly through daily spending on the US-issued Barclays co-branded card.


- **Marriott Bonvoy Transfers:**


Converting Marriott hotel points over to Miles & More at a 3:1 ratio.


Because award seats are exceptionally rare and highly restricted, you should be prepared to monitor the Miles & More calendar exactly 360 days out. When you are ready to check real-time seat availability, you can use the


[Lufthansa search tool on Roame](https://roame.travel/programs/lufthansa) to track down open space or look for cash upgrade opportunities on existing Business Class fares.
