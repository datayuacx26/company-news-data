### Why We Like This Card


The Atmos™ Rewards Summit Visa Infinite® Card is an exceptional choice for frequent flyers and global travelers invested in the unified Alaska Airlines and Hawaiian Airlines Atmos™ Rewards program. It stands out by rewarding international spending, like earning 3x points on all foreign transaction purchases made abroad alongside 3x points on worldwide dining.


It offers unmatched airline perks, including 8 Alaska Lounge passes per year (issued 2 per quarter), an annual 25,000-point Global Companion Award, and a free checked bag for you and up to six guests on the same reservation. Plus, Bank of America deposit account holders receive an automatic 10% rewards bonus on all points earned from card purchases.
