### Why We Like This Card


The Atmos™ Rewards Ascent Visa Signature® Card is a practical mid-tier option for anyone who flies Alaska Airlines or Hawaiian Airlines even once or twice a year. With a $95 annual fee, the card easily pays for itself through core flight perks: you and up to six companions get a free checked bag ($70 round-trip value per person) and preferred boarding whenever you book flights with the card. Spending $6,000 on the card annually unlocks a $99 Companion Fare, making it simple to recoup the annual fee on a single trip. You also earn 3x points on Alaska and Hawaiian flight purchases, 2x points on everyday transit, gas, EV charging, cable, and streaming services, plus 1 status point for every $3 spent toward earning Atmos elite status. For Bank of America account holders, an automatic 10% rewards bonus pushes those earning rates even higher.
