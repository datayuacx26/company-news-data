# Air Canada Aeroplan and World of Hyatt Launch Major Loyalty Partnership

# Air Canada Aeroplan and World of Hyatt Launch Major Loyalty Partnership


By


[Sophie Langford](https://roame.travel/guides/sophie-langford)


-


Updated: July 16, 2026


Air Canada’s Aeroplan and World of Hyatt have launched a major reciprocal partnership featuring 2:1 points transfers, unique cross-program redemptions, and reciprocal elite status fast-tracks. Most notably, Aeroplan elite members can now secure top-tier World of Hyatt Globalist status by completing a shortened 20-night fast-track challenge within 90 days.


Air Canada’s Aeroplan and World of Hyatt have joined forces, introducing a comprehensive reciprocal loyalty partnership. Effective July 15, 2026, members who link their accounts can access two-way point transfers, reciprocal redemption options, status fast-track challenges, and accelerated credit card earning rates.


The collaboration represents a significant bridge between one of North America's most versatile airline currencies and a highly regarded hotel loyalty program.


## Direct Points Conversions & Redemptions


Historically, transferring points between airline and hotel programs has yielded poor conversion rates. This partnership establishes a direct two-way bridge, though the conversion values differ depending on your direction:


- **Aeroplan to Hyatt:**


Aeroplan Elite Status members can convert Aeroplan points to World of Hyatt points at a


**2:1 ratio**


(minimum transfer of 500 points). This is capped at 100,000 Aeroplan points per day or 250,000 points per week.


- **Hyatt to Aeroplan:**


Any World of Hyatt member can convert Hyatt points to Aeroplan points at a


**2:1 ratio**


starting at 5,000 points. Alternatively, members can redeem 50,000 Hyatt points for a


**30,000-point Aeroplan flight reward certificate**


.


### Redemptions on the Ground and in the Air


The collaboration also introduces unique cross-program award bookings:


- **Hyatt Free Nights via Aeroplan:**


Aeroplan members can redeem Aeroplan points directly for World of Hyatt Free Night Awards (Category 1–4 and Category 1–7) starting at


**25,000 Aeroplan points**


.


- **Airline Stay Preference:**


Aeroplan members can choose to earn a flat


**500 Aeroplan points per stay**


at participating Hyatt properties in lieu of standard World of Hyatt points.


## Status Challenges and Fast Tracks


Elite status holders in both programs receive reciprocal benefits, with several lucrative fast-tracks rolling out.


### For Aeroplan Elite Members (Fast-Track to Hyatt Status)


Aeroplan Elite Status members (and eligible premium credit cardholders) can fast-track to World of Hyatt status through a highly competitive


**annual 90-day challenge**


:


- **Discoverist Status:**


Complete


**4 qualifying nights**


(normally 10 nights required).


- **Explorist Status:**


Complete


**10 qualifying nights**


(normally 30 nights required).


- **Globalist Status:**


Complete


**20 qualifying nights**


(normally 60 nights required).


**Why this matters:**


A 20-night fast-track to World of Hyatt Globalist—which grants complimentary breakfast, club lounge access, and suite upgrades—is one of the most generous status shortcuts available in the hotel industry.


### For World of Hyatt Elite Members (Fast-Track to Aeroplan Status)


- **Air Canada Flight Credits:**


Discoverist, Explorist, and Globalist members receive an


**annual $20 CAD Air Canada flight credit**


upon linking accounts.


- **Aeroplan Status Challenges:**


Scheduled to launch later in 2026, World of Hyatt Explorist and Globalist members will gain access to dedicated challenges to fast-track to


**Aeroplan Elite Status**


.


## Co-Branded Credit Card Boosts


Canadian-issued Aeroplan co-branded credit cardholders receive unique point multipliers on the ground. When using an eligible premium Aeroplan credit card at participating Hyatt hotels and resorts, cardholders can earn up to


**2.5 Aeroplan points and 2 World of Hyatt Bonus Points**


per eligible Canadian dollar spent.
