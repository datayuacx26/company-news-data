# Best Ways to Redeem Bilt Rewards Points

The quick answer


## What to actually do with your Bilt points


Bilt points transfer 1:1 to a short but unusually good roster — World of Hyatt plus a set of airlines you can't reach from most other cards. That's the whole game. Redeem for statement credit or rent and a point is worth around half a cent. Move it to Hyatt or Alaska and it's worth 2¢, 4¢, sometimes more. The floor is low and the ceiling is high, so the exit is everything.


The four rules that matter


**Transfer to travel partners.** Never take statement credit, rent, or the Bilt Collection.


**World of Hyatt is the crown jewel:** 1:1, with award nights from 3,500 points.


**On airlines, lead with the rare ones:** Alaska, American, Flying Blue, Turkish, Aeroplan.


**Skip** the Bilt Collection, Amazon, Lyft, rent, and Hilton/Marriott transfers — all well under 1¢.

## What your Bilt points are actually worth


A point has no fixed value — it depends entirely on the exit. Bilt's built-in options set a brutal floor: the Bilt Collection merchandise store can price a paper-towel roll at 3,000 points, and statement credit hovers near 0.55¢. Transfers are the whole reason to hold the currency. Here's the spread you're choosing between every time you redeem.

## The worst ways to use Bilt points


Bilt makes these effortless because they're the least valuable to you. Each hands back most of your points' worth for the sake of convenience. If you're doing any of them, stop.


Bilt Collection


~0.3¢


The merchandise store is the single worst option — everyday goods priced at multiples of retail. A paper towel for 3,000 points is not a deal.


Statement credit


0.55¢


Wiping card charges hands back nearly half your points' value. There is no upside and no bonus tier. Never.


Rent redemption


0.7¢


Using points toward rent lands around 0.7¢ (1.05¢ on Rent Day). Convenient, but a fraction of transfer value.


Amazon "pay with points"


0.7¢


The most efficient non-travel exit — which is damning. Pay with the card and keep the points for a transfer instead.


Lyft, fitness, student loans


~0.7¢

Hilton & Marriott transfers


<1¢
