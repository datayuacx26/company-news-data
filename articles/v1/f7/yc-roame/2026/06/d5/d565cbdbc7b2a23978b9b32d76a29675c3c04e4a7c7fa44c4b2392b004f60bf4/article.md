## **Important things to know**


- Miles expire after just 12 months of inactivity. You can keep them alive by transferring more points or booking an award.


- Change and cancellation fees are expensive, though you can avoid them by signing up for a Lifemiles+ subscription at the Basic level or higher. The Basic plan costs $50 monthly but includes free cancellations up to 72 hours before departure.


- The booking website can be clunky. Sometimes you need to search multiple times or try different airports to find availability. Also make sure to search using Star Alliance search.


- Transfers are permanent. You can't move points back to Amex once you transfer them to


[LifeMiles](https://roame.travel/programs/lifemiles) .

1. Log into your Amex account and navigate to the Membership Rewards section.


2. Select "Transfer Points" and find Avianca Lifemiles in the airline partners list.

4. The bonus applies automatically. You'll see 1,150 LifeMiles credited for every 1,000 points transferred.
