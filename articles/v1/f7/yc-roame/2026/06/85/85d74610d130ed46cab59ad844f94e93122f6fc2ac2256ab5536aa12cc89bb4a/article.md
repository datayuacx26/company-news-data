The Roame Team

Updated: June 23, 2026


Bilt Rewards has expanded its travel loyalty network by introducing Preferred Hotels & Resorts as its latest transfer partner. This addition marks the twenty-fourth overall partner and the seventh hotel loyalty program to integrate with the platform, giving members immediate utility for their rewards across an extensive selection of boutique properties.


Members can convert their Bilt Points into the I Prefer Hotel Rewards currency at a lucrative one-to-two ratio. This means that a transfer of 1,000 Bilt Points will result in 2,000 I Prefer points deposited into a member's hotel account.


## Program Details and Moving Points


The transfer capability is live within the Bilt Rewards application and online dashboard. Moving points requires linking a valid hotel account before initiating the process. The standard conversion guidelines depend slightly on account standing:


- **General Minimums:**


Members holding Silver, Gold, or Platinum status can process transfers starting at a 1,000-point minimum. Blue status members face a slightly higher entry point of 2,000 points.


- **Incremental Rules:**


All conversions must be executed in even increments of 1,000 points.


- **Finality:**


Like all standard credit card rewards programs, points conversions are entirely permanent and cannot be reversed or returned to Bilt once finalized.


## Earning Bilt Points with the Bilt Mastercard


To take advantage of this new partnership, travelers must first build up a balance of Bilt points. The easiest way to accumulate these rewards is through the Bilt Mastercard, which is uniquely built to reward consumers on everyday living and housing costs.


The card features a $0 annual fee and serves as a highly accessible way to earn up to 1.25x points on monthly rent and mortgage payments without incurring any transaction fees. It also expands your earning capability by pairing housing rewards with 3x points on dining, 2x points on travel, and 1x points on everyday purchases. Just keep in mind that you need to use the card at least five times each billing cycle to earn points.

## Scope of the Preferred Hotels Network


Preferred Hotels & Resorts acts as a curated umbrella organization for independent lodging rather than a traditional corporate hotel chain. The portfolio encompasses over 700 distinct boutique properties, upscale resorts, and luxury residences scattered across 85 countries globally.


The properties are organized into tiered categories that reflect different styles of travel. These include the ultra-luxury Legend collection, which features notable destinations like Montage properties and the exclusive Necker Island, alongside the well-positioned urban properties of the LVX collection and the approachable luxury found in the Lifestyle tier. This unique structure allows members to target highly individualistic hotels that are typically omitted from major corporate rewards platforms.


## Evaluating Redemption Value


While a one-to-two conversion ratio appears generous on the surface, the overall value relies entirely on how the I Prefer program sets its award pricing. Standard reward nights at participating properties generally start at 15,000 points per night and can quickly climb well past 100,000 points for top-tier luxury stays. The program also provides a points-plus-cash structural alternative starting at 3,000 points per night to reduce out-of-pocket point requirements.


Because I Prefer points generally carry a lower baseline value than highly competitive hotel currencies like World of Hyatt, this new option is best utilized when targeting specific independent hotels that lack other coverage options. The matching one-to-two transfer ratio also aligns with existing partnerships that I Prefer maintains with Capital One and Citi ThankYou Rewards, meaning travelers can easily combine balances across multiple credit card ecosystems to lock in a premium independent hotel stay.
