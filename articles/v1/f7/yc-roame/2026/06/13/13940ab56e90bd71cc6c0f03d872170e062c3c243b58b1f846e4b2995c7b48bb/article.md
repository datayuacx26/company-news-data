The transfers must be executed in even increments of 1,000 points, and the points typically clear into your airline account immediately.

## The Partner Pricing Switch: ANA First Class vs. Business Class


The absolute highest value redemption available through Virgin Atlantic involves booking travel on its partner airline, All Nippon Airways (ANA). Because Virgin uses a specialized distance-based award chart for ANA flights, the inclusion of a 30% transfer bonus creates a unique pricing crossover for flights connecting the United States and Japan.


Under normal pricing conditions, an ANA Business Class ticket from central or eastern US hubs like Chicago or New York to Tokyo requires 60,000 Virgin Points one-way. However, a top-tier ANA First Class ticket from West Coast hubs like Los Angeles or San Francisco requires a baseline of 72,500 Virgin Points.


When you compute the 30% Chase transfer bonus, the point costs drop significantly:


- **ANA Business Class (East Coast to Tokyo):**


47,000 Chase points


- **ANA First Class (West Coast to Tokyo):**


56,000 Chase points


- **ANA First Class (East Coast to Tokyo):**


66,000 Chase points


Because of this specific math, booking a flagship ANA First Class suite from the West Coast actually costs fewer transferable credit card points than booking a regularly priced business class seat from the East Coast. Securing a premium first class cabin with a private suite and world-class service for just 56,000 points is an incredible use of credit card rewards.


## Other Notable Sweet Spots


If your travel plans do not involve Asia, the 30% bonus can still be applied effectively to flights across Europe and the United States:


### Premium Cabins to London


Virgin Atlantic's own Upper Class business class seats can be booked from the US East Coast to London for as low as 29,000 points one-way during off-peak dates. The 30% bonus reduces your out-of-pocket cost to a mere 23,000 Chase points. While you will still face significant carrier-imposed taxes and fees on flights to the United Kingdom, the low point requirement remains highly competitive.


### Regional Delta Flights


As a member of the SkyTeam alliance, Virgin Atlantic provides access to domestic flights operated by Delta Air Lines. Short regional flights under 500 miles require just 7,500 Virgin Points, which translates to exactly 6,000 Chase points each way after applying the promotion.


## Evaluating the Move


The standard rule of thumb for award travel is to avoid transferring points speculatively. Once you move your Chase points over to Virgin Atlantic, they are permanently locked into that airline program and cannot be returned to your credit card account. However, if you have a specific near-term booking window in mind, utilizing this 30% promotion before the July 14 deadline is an excellent way to extract maximum value from your Chase balance.
