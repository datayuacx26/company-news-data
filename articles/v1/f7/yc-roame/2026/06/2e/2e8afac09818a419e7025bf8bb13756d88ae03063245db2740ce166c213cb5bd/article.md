# New Citi ThankYou with 50% Transfer Bonus for ALL Accor


By


The Roame Team


-


Updated: June 23, 2026


The Citi ThankYou Rewards network has introduced a compelling 50% transfer bonus when cardholders shift their points to ALL – Accor Live Limitless. This time-sensitive promotion allows participants to boost their hotel redemption balances significantly. Moving flexible credit card points at anything less than a 1:1 parity can often feel counterintuitive, but this particular promotional window bridges that gap heavily, making Accor stays far more lucrative for premium Citi cardholders.


Under standard terms, Citi ThankYou Points route to Accor at a baseline 2:1 ratio, meaning 1,000 credit card points translate into 500 hotel rewards points. During this campaign, the temporary conversion adjusts to an optimized 4:3 rate, yielding 750 Accor Live Limitless points for every 1,000 Citi points converted. Because Accor utilizes a completely fixed-value currency structure, where a block of 2,000 points universally slices €40 (approximately $45 USD) off a retail lodging bill, the 50% bonus elevates the return on your ThankYou points to an impressive 1.74 to 2.0 cents apiece depending on current currency valuations.

By utilizing the strengths of these respective cards to build a point balance, travelers can more effectively fund their upcoming lodging. This strategic promotion serves as an exceptional mechanism for travelers holding precise itineraries at high-end Accor properties, such as Fairmont, Sofitel, or SLS hotels, particularly within European markets where the brand footprint is massive. Because award redemptions are tied directly to cash room rates without any blackout restrictions or convoluted category charts, capitalizing on this bonus delivers predictable, outsized financial savings. However, since point transfers are entirely irreversible once initialized and must land in a loyalty account bearing the cardholder’s exact name, speculative point-moving should be avoided in favor of immediate, active bookings.
