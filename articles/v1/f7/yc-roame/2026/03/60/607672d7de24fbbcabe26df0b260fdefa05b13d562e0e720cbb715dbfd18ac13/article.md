**Why JAL miles matter**


JAL Mileage Bank is one of the most underrated programs out there. You can use JAL miles to book JAL flights, partner airline awards, and even Emirates business class from New York to Dubai for around 80,000 miles each way. JAL also has a generous stopover policy, which makes it easy to build a complex itinerary through Japan and beyond.


Japan Airlines is part of the oneworld alliance, so partner options include American Airlines, Alaska Airlines, British Airways, Cathay Pacific, and Qantas. The program has its own award chart for partner flights, and there's real value in there if you know where to look.
