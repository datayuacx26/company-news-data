## **Best Wells Fargo Transfer Partners To Check First**


### **Cathay Pacific Asia Miles**


Cathay Pacific is Wells Fargo's first transfer partner focused primarily on Asia. That makes Wells Fargo points more useful for travelers looking at Hong Kong, mainland China, Southeast Asia, Australia, and other Cathay-served regions.


Because the announced Wells Fargo to Cathay transfer ratio is 1:1, Asia Miles is worth checking when Cathay or its partners have award space that beats a cash redemption.


### **Air France/KLM Flying Blue**


Flying Blue is one of our favorite transfer programs because it frequently offers useful award pricing, broad Air France and KLM route coverage, and occasional promotional award pricing.


It can be especially compelling for U.S. to Europe trips when award space is available, so this is often one of the first airline partners to compare against a cash fare.


### **Choice Privileges**


Choice Privileges can be useful because Roame's Wells Fargo transfer data shows a 1:2 ratio, turning 1 Wells Fargo point into 2 Choice points. That ratio can create outsized value when cash rates are high and award prices are reasonable.


For example, a stay with a high cash rate but a moderate Choice award price may clear more than 1 cent per Wells Fargo point after the 1:2 transfer ratio. Always compare the all-in cash price, taxes, fees, and cancellation rules before transferring.


## **Should You Transfer Wells Fargo Points?**


Transfer Wells Fargo points when a partner award gives you meaningfully more value than a cash redemption or Wells Fargo travel booking. If a flight or hotel would cost roughly the same number of points either way, keeping points flexible is usually better.


The safest workflow is simple: find award space first, confirm the transfer ratio and timing in Wells Fargo Rewards, compare against the cash price, and then transfer only the points you need for that specific booking.
