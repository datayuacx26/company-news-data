# How to book Air France First Class (La Première) using points


By


[Tim Qin](https://roame.travel/guides/tim-qin)


-


Updated: June 25, 2026


Air France First Class is impossible to book with points alone, but there's a clever workaround.


Photo Credit: Air France


## **TLDR**


Only Flying Blue Platinum or Ultimate elite members can book Air France First Class (called La Première) directly using points. The cabin which costs around 200,000-300,000


[Flying Blue miles](https://roame.travel/programs/flyingblue) .


The work around is to book a business class award ticket using points, then upgrade with cash at check-in for around $1,500 to $2,500.


An Air France first class seat regularly sells for $10,000 to $20,000, but can pay 60,000 points for business class plus $1,500-$3,000 for the upgrade.


## **Step 1: Book Air France business class with points**


Flying Blue has a published tool that lets you price out the lowest possible miles prices for entry fares. Using this tool, we can see that Europe award flights to/from the contiguous 48 United States, Canada, and Mexico all cost


[45,000 to 60,000 points](https://roame.travel/guides/air-france-business-class) one way when flying on Air France or KLM.


Alternative, you can search for Air France award flight deals using the


[Roame reward flight finder](https://roame.travel/) .


Air France La Premiere. Credit: Air France


## **Step 2: Upgrade to Air France First Class**


Now here's where it gets interesting. Air France sometimes offers paid upgrades from business class to first class, including on award tickets. This is most commonly available at check-in, though expect it to be pricey. Upgrade costs will rarely be as low as $1,000, and it's much more common to see upgrade offers


in the $1,500-3,500 range.


### **Timing is everything**


The magic window opens 30 hours before departure. That's when Air France releases unsold La Première seats for upgrades. Just log in to Flying Blue, go to "My Travel," go to your booking, and check for upgrade offers. This may require multiple tries, so make sure to check on all four channels: the Air France app and website as well as we the KLM app and website.


**Upgrade pricing patterns**


Based on recent data points from travelers:


- Paris to Dubai: ~$1,500 to $1,900


- Paris to Los Angeles: ~$2,500 to $3,200


- Paris to Miami: ~$2,300


- Paris to New York: ~$2,600


- Paris to SAn Francisco: ~$3,000


### **Important restriction**


As of July 1, 2023, Air France has implemented a policy whereby you can no longer upgrade business class award tickets to first class.


However, this policy has been inconsistently enforced, and many travelers report still receiving upgrade offers at check-in. Your mileage may vary.

## **How the math works**


Let's do the math. A typical La Première ticket from New York to Paris costs around $15,000 one-way. Using our strategy:


- Business class award: 60,000 Flying Blue miles + $300 in taxes


- Cash upgrade: $2,600


- Total cost: 60,000 points + $2,900


## **Maximizing your chances**


- Pick flights with empty cabins: You can use Google Flights to check which Air France La Première flights have the most empty seats. There are only 4 seats in first class.


- Be flexible with dates: Mid-week flights and off-season travel typically have better upgrade availability. Avoid school holidays and peak summer months if possible.


- Consider originating in Paris: You will want to experience the full Air France experience, including chauffeur pickupfrom your Paris hotel.

## **Final Thoughts**


The points + upgrade strategy remains one of the most accessible ways to experience La Première without elite status or paying for a full cash fare. Yes, there's some uncertainty involved, and yes, you'll need to pay a cash component.
