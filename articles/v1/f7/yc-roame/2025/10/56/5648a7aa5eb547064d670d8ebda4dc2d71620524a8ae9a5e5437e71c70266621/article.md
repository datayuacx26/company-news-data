## **Singapore Suites vs Singapore Airlines First Class**


Singapore Airlines uses two different premium labels that people often mix up. Singapore Airlines Suites are the A380 product. Singapore Airlines First Class on Boeing 777 aircraft is a separate first class cabin, not Suites. If your goal is the private Suite with the separate chair and bed, filter for the Airbus A380.


The current A380 Suites cabin has six suites. The adjoining Suites 1A and 2A, or 1F and 2F, can be booked together for the double-suite setup when both seats are available. The product is still subject to aircraft changes, so a route that normally has A380 service is not enough on its own.


### **Does Singapore Airlines first class have a shower?**


No. Singapore Airlines Suites do not have an onboard shower. The A380 Suites cabin instead has two spacious lavatories, including a larger lavatory with a sit-down vanity and premium toiletries. If you are comparing Singapore Suites with Emirates A380 First Class, the shower is a key difference.

## **Booking tips for Singapore Suites availability**


- Filter by aircraft, not just cabin. A Singapore Airlines first class result on a 777 is not Singapore Suites.


- Use Delhi and Mumbai as practical starting points if London and Sydney have no Saver space.


- Check seasonal routes early and often. A short seasonal A380 window can disappear or change aircraft.


- For two travelers, look specifically for adjoining Suites 1A/2A or 1F/2F if you want the double-suite experience.


- Recheck the aircraft before departure. Singapore Airlines notes that Suites may be unavailable if there is an aircraft change.
