## **Current Routes and Schedules**


[Cathay Pacific](https://roame.travel/programs/cathay) currently operates Aria Suites on six routes from Hong Kong with nine aircraft retrofitted as of October 2025. The limited fleet means not every flight on these routes features the new product, making seat selection crucial.

## **How to Identify Aria Suite Flights**


Check Google Flights for "Individual suite" designation versus "Lie flat seat" for older products.


On Cathay Pacific's website, look for "77J" aircraft codes and confirm 45 business class seats instead of 40. The seat map should show rows extending to row 14 and four seats in row 20, both unique to Aria configurations.


Remember that aircraft assignments may change before departure, so make sure to verify as your travel date approaches.

## **Award Availability Reality on Cathay Pacific**


Cathay Pacific rarely releases premium cabin award space to partner programs these days. Recent searches found partner availability only on Hong Kong-Beijing, with long-haul routes much harder to find unless you are close-in.

## **Sweet Spot Redemptions**


Pricing starts at 28,000 Asia Miles for Hong Kong-Beijing, 58,000 for Sydney, and 84,000 for London, while Vancouver costs 88,000 miles after an April 2025 increase.


If you want to fly Cathay Pacific business class, then the best way to redeem is via Cathay Pacific's own loyalty program.
