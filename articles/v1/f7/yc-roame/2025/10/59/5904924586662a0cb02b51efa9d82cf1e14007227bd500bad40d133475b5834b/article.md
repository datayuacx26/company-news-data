# United cuts partner airline award redemption rates by 9%

# United cuts partner airline award redemption rates by 9%


By


[Tim Qin](https://roame.travel/guides/tim-qin)


-


Updated: May 27, 2026


After years of watching devaluations pile up, United just slashed partner award pricing across the board.


Photo Credit : United Airlines (New United Polaris Studio)


The new pricing affects virtually all Star Alliance partners. Business class redemptions on partner airlines like Lufthansa, Turkish, Austrian, or TAP Air Portugal that previously cost 88,000


[United miles](https://roame.travel/programs/united) each way are now down to 80,000 miles across the board. You'll see similar drops on Pacific routes too.


Transpacific business class on ANA and EVA Air went from 110,000 to 100,000 miles. That's a solid ~9% reduction, bringing the cost down to the credit card member discounted rates.

## **Much better, but still not the best pricing**


Even with these reductions,


[United](https://roame.travel/programs/united) isn't winning any awards for value. You can book the exact same Lufthansa flight for 70,000 points via


[Air Canada Aeroplan](https://roame.travel/programs/aeroplan) instead, potentially even less when departing from the East Coast.


New York to Frankfurt: Lufthansa Business Class


But if you're sitting on a pile of


[United miles](https://roame.travel/programs/united) (and only


[United miles](https://roame.travel/programs/united) ), this is definitely welcome news. Not everyone has the flexibility to transfer points between programs.
