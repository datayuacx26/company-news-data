#### **Key Differences Between Roame and Seats.aero**


##### **1. User Interface: Easy Search vs Table Filtering**


Roame offers an interface that feels familiar (like Google Flights or Kayak). Clean inputs, intuitive filters, and flexible date views.


Roame search interface


Seats.aero uses a data pivot table format requiring filters, sorting, and airport codes. It’s functional, but less beginner-friendly.


##### **2. Route Support: Roame Searches Everything**


Roame allows you to search any route on Live Search, including connecting itineraries and complex routings — perfect for planning flexible trips or finding rare availability. With Roame, you can custom add routes to be tracked on SkyView as well.


Seats.aero only supports routes with direct or nonstop flights. That’s fine for simple trips, but limits options if your departure or destination isn’t served nonstop.


##### **3. Search Window: Roame Goes Further on Free Tier**


Roame is free to search, with a Pro plan offering Super Alerts, and SkyView Pro search.


Seats.aero’s free users can search up to 60 days ahead; the full year is unlocked only with a paid subscription.


##### **4. Alerts: Persistent vs One-and-Done**


Roame’s


[Super Alerts](https://roame.travel/guides/alerts) stay active until you delete them — ideal if your first alert doesn’t work out or you want to track ongoing availability.


Seats.aero auto-deletes alerts after one match, requiring you to set them again to continue tracking.


##### **5. Community & Support**


Roame is backed by a


[160,000+ member Facebook community](https://www.facebook.com/groups/roame) (Award Travel by Roame), sharing deals, tips, and support daily. Roame also provides step-by-step instructions for booking and guides for many points-related and booking topics.


Seats.aero has a ~10,000-member Discord, offering active discussions but on a smaller scale and a limited knowledge base to learn from on the website.


#### **Who Should Use Roame?**


- You want to search any route or connection, not just nonstops


- You value a clean, easy-to-use search interface


- You want alerts that persist and cover wide search windows


- You’re newer to award travel and want help navigating transfers/redemptions


- You want step-by-step guides to help book flights you find


- You want to access 365-day search on the free tier


#### **Who Should Use Seats.areo?**


- You’re searching for direct flights only


- You need quick availability snapshots


- You prefer airport code searches and a data table format


- You’re okay with alerts deleting after one match


#### **Final Thoughts: Roame vs Seats.aero – Which Is Better for Me?**


##### **🏆 Winner: Roame**


Seats.aero is a powerful tool for direct flights and advanced users who prefer fast, table-based searches. But for travelers who want more flexibility, easier search tools, and help with booking, Roame is the better option — especially if you’re newer to points or want to search more broadly and actually complete redemptions with ease.
