# Topline Pro: Giving home service businesses an edge

Since day one, Topline Pro set out to improve the lives of hard working home service pros. These pros have little spare time, so our commitment has been to build a product that actually *does* work for them: helping them get discovered, win jobs, and manage operations day to day, so they can focus on their customers, their people and their craft.


We started on this journey by doing the work for pros to build trust and get found online. Our product worked incredibly well, and over 6,000 pros have built real businesses with Topline Pro to date.


This year, we’ve taken a big leap, harnessing advancements in AI to bring the full product vision to life. We’re now live with an AI teammate that works for pros while they're on roofs or under sinks: answering calls, sending follow-ups, building quotes, advising on scope, and even collecting payment on their behalf. Our product has grown from running quietly behind a website and has started working directly for and with the pro, day in and day out. As our impact has evolved, we wanted to bring our brand up to date, as well.


So, today we'd like to introduce the new identity system that reflects our voice, our visual identity, and a clear answer to who we back and why.


## Who we stand for


There are 2.6 million small home service businesses in the United States. About 200,000 are PE-backed rollups with real teams and infrastructure behind them, and tech has always found its way to those. The other 2.4 million haven't: solo operators, husband-and-wife teams, and lean crews that big-box enterprise software was never built for.


This is who we're building for. The local service pro: someone who learned to do the job right long before they had a business to run, whose growth has come from referrals and reputation built within their community, and who takes pride in being a trusted, reliable tradesperson every hour of the day, even when it takes time away from their family.


Our pros got into the trades to work with their hands. Running the business side, quoting, scheduling, invoicing, chasing reviews, should be a part of growing the business, not the whole job. The bigger the business gets, the more it depends on them personally to hold it all together. Meanwhile, pros also have had a hard time trusting technology as many have been repeatedly scammed by software that promises to be a partner and turns out to be just another vendor. We had to say plainly that we exist to carry that weight instead of adding to it: **Topline Pro backs local service pros in every corner of their business to supercharge their hard work with intelligence that unleashes their ambition.**


We built the brand and the product side by side rather than in sequence, so the identity would fit what the product actually delivers. We partnered with[Red Antler](https://www.redantler.com/) on the brand side, to help turn the strategy into a visual system that could actually hold up.


## It's all in the Edge


Our old mark sat inside a circle. Safe, rounded, a little forgettable. We cut the circle away and sharpened the symbol with more intent underneath it: two shapes, one standing just behind the other, backing it up so it can push further than it could on its own. That's the Edge. It's the same relationship we'd already written into our positioning: Topline Pro backing a Pro.


The rest of the identity system was built from the Edge, and became an extension of our pros' teams: bringing a fresh perspective, but built from the same world they already work in, so Topline Pro felt like someone they'd trust to have their back, not another piece of tech they'd have to learn.


## Cut from the same material


The palette is rooted in the world of our pros. Brick, a hot red, carries the logo and every primary brand moment, picked for accessibility and to hold up under harsh sunlight. It's supported by Cement and Slate. Sun, Sky, Land, and Grass round out a secondary palette that builds out the world around the pro and lives inside the product.


We also layered in textures and materials familiar to our pros into our identity system, like grass, cement, sky, glass, copper, and drywall, using them to frame product moments in real environments instead of on a plain background. We're not trying to be a brand for every small business. We're doubling down on the trades, and the materials say that before a single word does.


Photography carries the same discipline. Every image in the system is a real pro, framed inside a defined container system with a consistent crop and edge. It sounds like a small rule. In practice it's the reason a photo of a landscaper in Ohio and a photo of a roofer in Texas both read as unmistakably Topline Pro, without either of them looking staged. Our tech is AI, but our mission is human, a real team serving real people , putting actual pros in our imagery is how the home service community sees themselves in it.


Similarly, for type we chose Mikro, which carries the same cut, angular edges as the mark itself, closer to the geometry of tools Pros use, supported by Antarctica for legibility and a warmer feel in product interactions.


## Creating a flywheel


Sidekick, our personalized AI teammate, plays a huge role in the evolution of our brand, and we needed a symbol that felt just as unique and approachable as the pros who'd grow their businesses alongside it. We built it directly out of the Edge, extended into something dynamic that signals forward momentum, because Sidekick runs on everything Topline Pro already knows about a given pro and gets sharper the longer it works with them. Anyone who sees the Sidekick mark is seeing Topline Pro underneath it, on purpose.


We thought hard about how to treat AI visually without turning Topline Pro into a tech brand. In the app, a gradient border shows up specifically when Sidekick is listening or actively working, a small, functional cue rather than a stylistic flourish. But the rest stays grounded in the world of the pro, not the world of tech. Sidekick needed to feel like it belonged on a job site, so we encourage pros to name their Sidekick and treat it like what it is: a real extension of their team, always in their corner.


## One thing we didn't touch


Not everything about Topline Pro needed rebuilding. One line survived the entire process untouched: **More jobs. More customers. No bullsh*t.** It was true when we were helping pros get found, and it's true now that we're powering the growth of their entire businesses.


## Where it's already working


We worked tirelessly with our Pro Feedback Council and tested the new identity live inside the app for months. We launched the new brand system on July 14 at Topline Pro AI Day to hundreds of pros, industry leaders, and investors, alongside the broader launch of our new native mobile app with Sidekick. Joshua Valdez of JV Paint summed up the day: *"As a small business owner, opportunities like this don't come around often. Being able to learn from you, connect with other incredible pros, and see the future of where AI can take our business left me inspired and motivated."*


More than 6,000 pro businesses have now booked more than $1.2B in revenue through Topline Pro. That's billion with a "B." With our new brand identity and Sidekick's growing capabilities, we're turning that "B" into a "T." Every dollar in that number is a pro who got to spend Saturday with their family instead of behind a desk.
