### How To Get Pressure Washing Leads


#### 1. Use Yard Signs


You're probably operating on a tight budget if you've just started your power washing business. Therefore, you need to work smart to reach as many people as you would using other lead generation strategies.


One of the ways you can accomplish this goal is by putting up yard signs. Yard signs are a cost-effective and short-term way of marketing your pressure washing business. You can put them up in strategic locations such as busy intersections, shopping malls, and other high-traffic areas.


To increase the chances of people seeing your yard sign, make sure it is big and visible. Also, include a call to action such as "Call us for a pressure washing quote!" or "Get 50% off pressure washing services!"


Another way to increase the visibility of your yard sign is to put it up on a stand. This method will allow more people to see the sign, especially if it's placed in a busy area.


Make sure that your yard sign does not have any clutter to make it easy for people to see details such as pricing and phone number. A clear sign will also help prospects see important information clearly when they take photos while in traffic.


You can also use pressure washing magnets to attach your contact information to people's cars. These magnets are a great way to generate power washing leads, as it ensures that people will see your information even when they're not looking for pressure washing services.


Although yard signs are cheap, they can be time-consuming to implement because you have to put out lots of them to cover a wide area, and you'll need about 500 signs. Another challenge you might encounter is that they get easily pulled off by vandals or stolen by people who want to use them for their own pressure washing businesses.

#### 2. Build a Proper Website


A website acts as the core of all your online marketing efforts as it's the first thing that prospective leads will see when they search for pressure-washing businesses in their area or get redirected from social media.


The first element you need to pay attention to when building your website is its design. A proper website is built for the customer. Its design is user-friendly, easy to navigate, responsive, fast-loading, and mobile-friendly.


The second element that will help generate more pressure washing leads is[local SEO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_search_engine_optimisation) . This type of SEO will help your website rank higher in search engine results pages when people search for pressure washing businesses in your area.


For local SEO to be effective, you need to include NAP (Name, Address, and Phone Number) on your website. You should also add images and videos to showcase your work. Also, create blog content relevant to pressure washing and your local area.


Your website should have relevant and keyword-rich content to rank high on search engines. Do keyword research to find out what keywords people use to search for pressure washing businesses in your area. Include those in your content to help it rank higher on search engines.


It would help if you also focused on building backlinks to your website. Backlinks are links from other websites that link back to yours. They act as a vote of confidence and tell search engines that your website is relevant and trustworthy.


If you're wondering how to get pressure washing leads from your website, include a lead capture form to collect leads from prospects interested in your pressure washing services. You can offer a discount or a free pressure washing quote in exchange for their contact information.


Another essential element of your website is testimonials from past clients. Testimonials are a great way to build trust and credibility with potential customers. Ensure that the testimonials are from actual clients and that they're placed in an easily accessible area on your website.


There are a few other things that you can do to generate pressure washing leads from your website. They include the following:


- **Use keyword-rich titles and descriptions on your website** : When prospects search for pressure washing businesses in their area, they'll see your website in the search results. Make sure that your website's title and description include relevant keywords to help it stand out from the competition.
- **Use header tags (H tags) on your website to indicate the hierarchy of your content** : H tags help structure your content and make it easy to read. They also help search engines understand your content hierarchy, which can impact your website's ranking.
- **Use call-to-action (CTA) buttons on your website** : CTA buttons are a great way to encourage prospects to take action. Include CTA buttons on your website so prospects can request a pressure washing quote or contact you for more information. Make sure that your website's call-to-action (CTA) buttons are visible and easy to find. The CTA should be clear and concise, such as "Request a pressure washing quote now!"
- **Optimize your images by including keywords in the file name and alt text** : When you upload images to your website, make sure that you include keywords in the file name and alt text, as doing so will help your website's images rank higher on search engine results pages, leading to more traffic and pressure washing leads.


- **Use internal links to help Google understand the structure of your website** : Internal links are links from one page on your website to another. They help Google understand the structure of your website and can influence your website's ranking.
- **Add a blog to your website and write articles about pressure washing topics in which your potential customers would have interest** : A blog is a great way to add new content to your website and keep it updated. Write articles about relevant pressure washing topics, such as tips for pressure washing your home or how to choose a pressure washing company.

#### 3. Use Google My Business


Google My Business is a business listing that businesses create on Google, and it's free to sign up. Once you're verified as the business owner, you can add photos, respond to reviews, and add other information like your business hours and contact information.


When creating your GMB listing, include keywords in your business name and description that potential pressure washing customers may use when searching for a pressure washing company. For example, if you pressure wash houses in Los Angeles, you want to include something like "Los Angeles pressure washing" or "house pressure washing Los Angeles" in your business name and description.


If you're a pressure washing company that serves a specific city or town, include that in your GMB listing as well.


By optimizing your GMB listing for pressure washing keywords, you'll make it easier for potential customers to find you when they're searching for pressure washing services online.


One of the great things about GMB is that it allows customers to leave reviews. These reviews improve your chances of ranking higher in local search results, but they also help build social proof and increase your chances of getting power washing leads.


To encourage customers to leave reviews, you can send them follow-up emails after they've had a chance to use your services. In these emails, you can include a link that takes them directly to your GMB listing, where they can leave a review.


Below are a few other tips that can help you generate power washing leads for your business through GMB:


- **Use high-quality photos** : People searching for pressure washing companies will be able to see your GMB listing before they even click on it. Make sure your GMB page includes high-quality photos that show off your pressure washing work.
- **Respond to reviews** : Google reviews can significantly impact whether or not someone decides to use your pressure washing services. When you respond to reviews, potential customers will see that you're responsive and that you care about providing a good customer experience.
- **Keep your business information updated** : Make sure your GMB listing has up-to-date information, such as your pressure washing prices, service area, and business hours.
