#### 1. Build a website


If your company does not already have a website, build one. Websites are vital for every business as they provide potential customers with important information about your company. Include pictures of previous pressure washing projects, a list of services, and customer testimonials.


##### Tips for building a website that will get you more clients


When building a website, there are certain elements you'll want to include to make sure it's effective. Here are a few tips:


• Use white space: White space is the area on a webpage that doesn't include text or images. It can help make your website more visually appealing and easier to read.


• Include calls to action: A call to action is instruction or invitation for users to take some desired action, such as "contact us" or "learn more." Calls to action should be included on every page of your website.


• Make it mobile-friendly: More and more people use their phones and tablets to browse the internet. Make sure your website is designed to be viewed on all devices.


• Add relevant keywords: Keywords are words or phrases that describe your business. Include them in your website content, titles, and tags to help potential customers find your site.


• Use attractive visuals: Include images and videos on your website to make it visually appealing. People are more likely to remember information when it's presented with visuals.


Now that you have a website, you need to get traffic.

#### 2. Generate traffic to your website


To get more traffic to your website, ensure it is optimized for the search engine. Search engine optimization (SEO) is the process of optimizing your website to rank higher in search engine results. The higher your website ranks, the more likely people are to find it.


##### To optimize your site for the search engine:


• Use keywords: As we mentioned before, keywords are words or phrases that describe your business. Include them in your website content, titles, and tags to help potential customers find your site.


• Get listed in directories: Directories are websites that list businesses in specific categories. Getting listed in directories can help increase traffic to your website and improve your search engine ranking.


• Publish relevant and authoritative content: Relevant and authoritative content is the key to a successful website. Write blog posts that answer questions your potential customers are searching for.


• Add social media buttons: Include social media buttons on your website so people can share your content. The more people who share your content, the more traffic you'll get to your site.


Do not ignore local SEO


Local SEO is especially crucial if you want to get more pressure washing leads. Local SEO refers to optimizing your website to rank higher in local search results. Meaning, that if someone in your business' area of operation searches for pressure washing services, your business will appear higher in the search results.


There are several ways to optimize your website for local SEO, but one of the most important is to include your city and state in your website's title tags, meta descriptions, and content. Additionally, be sure to claim your business on Google My Business.
