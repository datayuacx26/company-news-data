# New CompanyCam Integration for Streamlined Photo Workflows

## What is CompanyCam?


CompanyCam is a photo-based project management tool tailored for home service entrepreneurs. It helps document and manage projects with ease by offering:


- Unlimited cloud storage
- Time and location-stamped photos
- In-app communication
- Photo categorization and so much more!


These features enable streamlined workflows and maintain an organized portfolio, making it easier to deliver exceptional service.


‍


### How Does the Topline Pro - CompanyCam Integration Work?


The Topline Pro and CompanyCam integration eliminates the hassle of manual photo management on your website and helps you create high-performing marketing content effortlessly. Here’s how it works:


- **Tag and Sync Automatically:** Create a tag for all website photos in CompanyCam, and they will automatically sync to your website and the Topline Pro app. Say goodbye to tedious cross-platform uploads!
- **Organized Content at Your Fingertips:** Easily access and manage your photos in one place, ensuring a seamless workflow.
- **Effortless Showcasing:** Highlight your best projects on your website and social media, presenting a polished and professional portfolio to potential clients.

### How to Get Started


Getting started with the CompanyCam integration is simple:


1. Log in to your[Topline Pro app.](http://app.toplinepro.com/)
2. Navigate to the[Settings](https://app.toplinepro.com/settings) menu and select the[Connections](https://app.toplinepro.com/settings/connections) tab.
3. Connect to your CompanyCam account in just a few clicks.


Once connected, you can effortlessly upload photos from CompanyCam to use in your marketing campaigns.
