# Velo3D to Participate in Upcoming Investor Conferences

FREMONT, Calif.


, May 28, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Velo3D, Inc. (Nasdaq:[VELO](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/velo3d-to-announce-first-quarter-2026-results-on-may-12-2026-302756214.html#financial-modal) ) ("Velo3D" or the "Company"), a leader in additive manufacturing ("AM") technology known for transforming aerospace and defense supply chains through world-class metal AM, announced today that management will participate in the following upcoming investor conferences:


- RedChip Future Tech (Virtual)


- **Date** : June 10, 2026
- **Time** : 12:00 p.m. ET
- **Format** :[Webcast presentation and Q&A](https://www.redchip.com/webinar/redchip/89763628854)


- Planet MicroCap (Bellagio Hotel, Las Vegas)


- **Date** : June 17, 2026
- **Time** : 4:30 p.m. PT
- **Presentation Room** : TRACK 2 - Da Vinci 2
- **Format** :[Webcast presentation](https://event.summitcast.com/view/bpjo3VVjZ25pp6SXpUua92/guest_book?session_id=oNTzKj7bZ6smvasxA8WtxZ) and[one-on-one meetings (June 17–18](https://www.meetmax.com/sched/event_130817/conference_register.html) )


To learn more about the events or to schedule a one-on-one meeting with management, please contact your conference representative or emailjames@haydenir.com .


**About Velo3D:**


Velo3D is a metal 3D printing technology company that enables customers to build mission-critical metal parts. The fully integrated solution includes the Flow print preparation software, the Sapphire® family of printers, and the Assure quality control system—all of which are powered by Velo3D's Intelligent Fusion® manufacturing process.
