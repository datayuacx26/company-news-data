# Smith-Midland Secures $10 Million Contract for I-81 VDOT Project

# Smith-Midland Secures $10 Million Contract for I-81 VDOT Project


*August 6, 2026* |[Smith-MIdland Virginia](https://smithmidland.com/blog/category/smith-midland-virginia/) ,[SoftSound / Sound Walls](https://smithmidland.com/blog/category/sound-walls/)


MIDLAND, VA – August 6, 2026 – MIDLAND, Va. — Smith-Midland Corporation (NASDAQ: SMID), a leading manufacturer of innovative precast concrete products for transportation, infrastructure, and building construction, has been awarded a transportation infrastructure project valued at approximately $10 million to manufacture precast concrete SoftSound®, retaining wall components, and pre-stressed columns for Virginia Department of Transportation (VDOT) improvements along Interstate 81 near Roanoke, Virginia.


This project, the third largest in Smith-Midland’s history, includes the manufacturing of Smith-Midland’s proven SoftSound® a proprietary sound absorptive concrete face that absorbs traffic noise into the soundwall, along with retaining wall elements and structural sound wall columns that will enhance the I-81 corridor improvements. Designed for long-term durability and efficient installation, the precast systems help accelerate construction schedules while providing lasting performance in demanding transportation environments.


“This award reflects the continued confidence transportation agencies and contractors place in Smith-Midland’s ability to deliver large-scale infrastructure solutions,” said Matthew Smith, Vice President of Sales and Marketing for Smith-Midland Corporation. “Projects like the I-81 improvements are an important part of our long-term strategic growth plan, expanding our transportation portfolio while reinforcing our commitment to enhancing Virginia’s infrastructure. We look forward to working alongside Triton Construction and VDOT to deliver products that continue to benefit communities and travelers across the Commonwealth for years to come.”


Beginning in mid-2027 and continuing through 2029, Smith-Midland will manufacture and supply precast concrete SoftSound® sound wall, precast concrete retaining walls, and precast columns, reinforcing the company’s longstanding partnership with VDOT in delivering innovative transportation infrastructure solutions across Virginia.
