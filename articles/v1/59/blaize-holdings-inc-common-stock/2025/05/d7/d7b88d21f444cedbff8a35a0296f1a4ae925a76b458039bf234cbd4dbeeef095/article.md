# Blaize Expands Global Presence with New Cooperation Office in Chungbuk

Blaize has officially opened a new cooperation office in Chungbuk, South Korea, aiming to enhance the global competitiveness of the local AI industry. This strategic initiative marks a significant step for both Blaize and the Chungbuk Institute of Science and Technology Innovation, fostering digital innovation and collaboration.


Renowned for cutting-edge technology in high-performance, low-power AI edge computing, Blaize specializes in real-time AI analysis and decision-making solutions across various sectors, including smart cities, healthcare, automotive, and national defense. The new office in Chungbuk is designed to transform local industries through AI while advancing Blaize’s global expansion in partnership with the Chungbuk Science and Technology organization.


### **Strategic Partnership with a Regional Impact**


The establishment of the Chungbuk Cooperation Office[follows a business agreement signed](https://www.blaize.com/press/cbist-selects-blaize-to-power-its-2025-smart-city-development-project/) in September between Blaize and Chungbuk Province. This initiative is part of the “Digital Innovation Base Creation Project,” which aims to serve as a central hub for AI technology collaboration and accelerate the digital transformation of industries in Chungbuk and beyond.


Ko Geun-seok, the Director of the Chungbuk Science and Technology Institute, articulated the regional implications of the newly established Blaize Cooperation Office noting that the opening of the Blaize Cooperation Office is a concrete achievement for Chungbuk to transition to an AI-centric digital industry. This sentiment underscores the commitment of the Chungbuk region to embracing cutting-edge technology and fostering an environment conducive to innovation. By establishing this office, Chungbuk is not only making strides in adopting artificial intelligence but is also positioning itself as a key player in the rapidly evolving digital landscape.


Building strong partnerships with global innovation leaders is key to helping local businesses stay competitive and boost their digital skills. These collaborations bring in valuable resources, expertise, and real-world best practices that local companies need to succeed in today’s fast-moving digital world. By teaming up with international companies known for cutting-edge technology, Chungbuk is working to give its local businesses the tools and knowledge they need to innovate, grow, and thrive.


This initiative is closely tied to the “[Local Digital Innovation Base Creation Support Project,](https://www.investkorea.org/cb-en/bbs/i-1310/detail.do?ntt_sn=491323) ” which was identified last year by the Ministry of Science and ICT. This project aims to bolster the region’s digital infrastructure, facilitating an environment that supports technology commercialization and nurtures digital excellence among local firms. By focusing on these areas, the project seeks to empower businesses in Chungbuk to capitalize on digital opportunities, ultimately driving economic growth and enhancing the region’s overall competitiveness.


As Chungbuk continues to invest in its digital infrastructure and forge strategic collaborations, the opening of the Blaize Cooperation Office symbolizes a pivotal step forward in its journey toward becoming a hub for AI and digital innovation. This effort reflects a broader commitment to ensuring that local enterprises are not only participants in the digital economy but also leaders within it.
