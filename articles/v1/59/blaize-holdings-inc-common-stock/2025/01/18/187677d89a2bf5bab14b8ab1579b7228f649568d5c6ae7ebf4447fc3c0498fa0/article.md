# Physical AI: Blaize’s Approach to Smarter, Faster, and More Sustainable Tech

Blaize, a Sacramento-based tech innovator, is leading the AI industry with its advanced edge computing solutions, processing data locally and eliminating the need for distant servers to handle analysis. Blaize provides high-performance hardware and software solutions that power real-time AI applications. From autonomous vehicles to medical equipment, the company is meeting the growing demand for smarter (and more energy-efficient) technologies by processing data at the source—ensuring critical decisions, like avoiding a car accident, are made instantly.


Under the leadership of CEO Dinakar Munagala, Blaize is advancing AI technology with a focus on improving sustainability and efficiency. The company was named one of the Top 10 Companies with Global Potential at the Forbes India D Globalist Entrepreneur Mobility Summit (DGEMS) 2024. This recognition reflects Blaize’s ongoing work to drive AI innovation and its growing impact across industries.


**Physical AI: Meeting Real-Time Demands**


In today’s tech landscape, using physical AI directly on devices or local systems, instead of relying on distant servers, is crucial for many applications. Whether it’s self-driving cars making critical decisions or IoT devices needing real-time data analysis, low-latency (faster) computing is crucial. Blaize meets this need by providing a complete solution that combines hardware and easy-to-use software, allowing businesses to implement AI more quickly and efficiently.


Blaize’s focus on edge AI places it at the heart of the Fourth Industrial Revolution, where new technologies are changing industries and helping businesses make more informed decisions based on data. By designing energy-efficient AI chips, Blaize is providing businesses with the tools to process data locally, which reduces reliance on the cloud and enhances security and privacy.


Blaize’s recent recognition extends beyond product innovation. At the SacramentoINNO 2024 Awards, Munagala was honored with the prestigious INNO Award for leadership in physical AI, a testament to his strategic vision and the company’s growing influence in the tech space. Additionally, Blaize secured $106 million in funding earlier this year, making it the most-funded company in the Sacramento region.


**Reducing AI’s Carbon Footprint**


As AI systems grow more powerful, they also consume vast amounts of energy. Data centers, responsible for powering AI applications, account for a significant portion of global electricity usage—a problem Blaize is actively working to solve. By prioritizing energy efficiency in its AI chips, Blaize is reducing the energy footprint of AI systems, without compromising performance.


This commitment to sustainability is crucial as the world shifts towards greener technologies. Blaize’s architecture is designed to help businesses meet their sustainability goals, enabling them to process data more efficiently while reducing energy consumption.


**Empowering the Workforce & Democratizing AI**


Blaize is also focused on ensuring that the benefits of AI are accessible to all. In addition to creating innovative technologies, the company invests in tools and software that promote upskilling and reskilling. By investing in training resources, Blaize aims to empower individuals and organizations to participate fully in the AI-driven future.


“Physical AI brings tremendous benefits to everyone in all walks of life,” Munagala said. “But to ensure that no one is left behind, Blaize believes in investing in people through a variety of training approaches to fully realize an open and accessible AI.”


**Looking Ahead: Next-Gen Chips for Autonomous Systems**


Blaize’s forward-looking vision includes next-generation chips designed for emerging applications in autonomous systems and enterprise edge servers. With these innovations, Blaize is poised to further cement its position as a leader in edge physical AI, helping industries tackle new challenges and unlock new opportunities.


Driving the future of AI at the edge, Blaize is offering smarter, faster, and more energy-efficient solutions that power many market verticals. Through their focus on sustainability, innovation, and democratizing access to AI, Munagala is helping to shape the next generation of technology. As the company continues to push the boundaries of what’s possible, it is paving the way for a smarter, more sustainable version of artificial intelligence.
