# What You Missed at ISC West: How Blaize is Powering the Future of Edge AI

### **Smart City AI, Simplified** ****


From traffic analytics to advanced incident detection, our **PictureSmart Video Concept** demonstrated how Blaize enables real-time video AI directly at the edge. Municipalities can scale safer, smarter infrastructure without needing a cloud-heavy, high-cost backend.


### **Developer-Ready: Blaize on Raspberry Pi** ****


We showcased our **M.2 Dev Kit running on Raspberry Pi** , performing live intrusion detection using edge inference across multiple models. It’s a low-power, easy-to-integrate solution—ideal for developers building early-stage edge AI products.


### **Video Analytics at Scale: Blaize + OrionVM** ****


In partnership with **OrionVM and CloudCo** , we demonstrated a scalable, camera-dense solution for Smart City video services. It’s designed for ISPs and providers looking to stand up AI-powered surveillance with **lower infrastructure cost** and **faster time-to-service** .


### **Smarter AI with Adaptive Inference**


Our **adaptive inference demo** showed how Blaize’s GSP can dynamically tune performance—dialing down idle zones and ramping up where precision is needed. The result: real-world efficiency that doesn’t compromise intelligence.
