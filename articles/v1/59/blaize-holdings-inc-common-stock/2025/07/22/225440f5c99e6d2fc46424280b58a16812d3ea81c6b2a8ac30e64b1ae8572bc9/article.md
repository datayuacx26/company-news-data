# Blaize and Starshine: Pioneering AI Infrastructure for a Smarter Asia Pacific

At Blaize, we’re committed to pushing the boundaries of AI architecture, delivering highly energy-efficient, low latency, and multimodal inference support. Our mission is to enable robust, scalable AI solutions that transform industries and cities. As part of this mission, our collaboration with strategic partner, Starshine, is shaping the future of AI in the Asia Pacific region. We are dedicated to fulfilling this vision through innovative solutions and strong collaboration, ensuring a lasting impact on the region’s technological landscape.


### **Driving Innovation Together**


Starshine is a technology pioneer specializing in high-performance computing and AI infrastructure, with a strong focus on regional deployment across Asia Pacific. Their expertise in system integration, edge computing, and AI service platforms complements Blaize’s vision. By combining our advanced architectural solutions with Starshine’s extensive industry experience, we are building a robust ecosystem capable of supporting real-world AI applications that drive tangible impact across the region.


### **Optimizing Edge AI Performance**


Our collaboration focuses on integrating Blaize’s advanced architecture into Starshine’s heterogeneous computing platform. This integration is designed to optimize Edge AI performance by enhancing processing efficiency and reducing latency at edge nodes. Additionally, it significantly decreases power consumption in data centers, leading to more sustainable operations. These improvements enable faster deployment of AI-driven solutions across a wide range of sectors, including smart transportation, urban development, industrial automation, energy management, and agriculture, ultimately supporting smarter, more connected communities and industries.
