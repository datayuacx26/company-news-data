## Steps to Create Your Data Scientist Resume


### 1. Get Started


Start by selecting your job type (Data Science) and experience level. This helps tailor the resume to the specific role you're targeting and highlights your most relevant experiences.


### 2. Personal Details


Include essential personal information such as your name, city, contact details, email address, LinkedIn profile, GitHub (if applicable), and portfolio links showcasing your projects or publications


### 3. Educational Background


List your academic qualifications, including degrees in fields such as Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, or Data Science.


### 4. Work Experience


Add any professional work experience, including full-time, part-time, or freelancing roles. Prioritize roles related to data science, such as positions where you worked with big data, machine learning algorithms, data visualization, or predictive modeling.


### 5. Internships and Projects/h3>


Showcase your internships, research projects, or personal data science projects. Focus on key tasks such as building machine learning models, analyzing large datasets, or automating data pipelines. Highlight metrics and results to quantify your impact.


### 6. Skills and Certifications


Include technical skills such as Python, R, SQL, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn, or Hadoop. Also, mention any relevant soft skills like problem-solving, communication, and teamwork. If you have certifications (e.g., AWS Certified Machine Learning, TensorFlow Developer), list them here to boost credibility.
