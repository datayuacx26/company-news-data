# How to Get a Job at Infosys as a Fresher

## Eligibility Criteria


Before applying, check if you meet these requirements:


### Education:


- - 60% or above in 10th and 12th
- - 65% or above in B.Tech/B.E/MCA/M.Sc
- - No active backlogs


### Accepted Streams:


- - BE/BTech/ME/MTech (all streams)
- - MCA, MSc (CS, Electronics, Maths, Physics, Statistics)


Note: A maximum gap of two years in education is permissible.


## Infosys Job Application Process


You can apply for Infosys jobs through:


- - **Online Application:**[Infosys Careers](https://www.infosys.com/careers/graduates.html)
- - **Campus Placement:** Check with your college’s placement cell

## Infosys Recruitment Process for Freshers


### Online Assessment Test


- - **Reasoning Ability:** 15-20 questions (25 mins)
- - **Mathematical Ability:** 10-15 questions (35 mins)
- - **Verbal Ability:** 15-20 questions (20 mins)
- - **Pseudocode:** 5-10 questions (10 mins)
- - **Puzzle Solving:** 4-5 questions (10 mins)

### Technical Interview


Focus on:


- -Programming (C, C++, Java, Python)
- -Computer Fundamentals (OS, DBMS, Networks)
- -Projects & Internships


### HR Interview


Common questions:


- -Why do you want to join Infosys?
- -Tell me about yourself.
- -Can you work under pressure?
