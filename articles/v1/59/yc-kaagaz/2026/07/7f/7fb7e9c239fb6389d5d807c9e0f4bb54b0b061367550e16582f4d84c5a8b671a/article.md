# How to Become a Social Media Manager at Zomato (Step-by-Step Guide)

## Eligibility Criteria


Before applying, check if you meet these requirements:


**Education**


- - Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, Journalism, or a related field.
- - MBA in Marketing (preferred but not mandatory).


**Experience**


- - 0-2 years of experience in social media management or digital marketing.
- - Prior internship or freelancing experience in content creation, branding, or social media is a plus.


**Skills Required**


- - Strong writing and communication skills.
- - Knowledge of social media trends and analytics.
- - Basic design and video editing skills (Canva, Adobe Suite, etc.).
- - Familiarity with SEO and online marketing strategies.


## Zomato Job Application Process


You can apply for social media jobs at Zomato through:


- - **Online Application:**[Zomato Careers](https://www.zomato.com/careers)
- - **Referrals:** Connect with current Zomato employees on LinkedIn.
- - **Internships:** Zomato often hires interns who later get full-time offers.


## Zomato Recruitment Process for Social Media Managers


### Online Assessment Test


- - **Creative Writing & Copywriting:** Short-form and long-form content writing.
- - **Social Media Analytics:** Understanding engagement metrics and reporting.
- - **Case Study Round:** Develop a content strategy for a Zomato campaign.


### Technical & Marketing Interview


Focus on:


- - Your knowledge of Zomato’s social media presence.
- - Experience with social media tools (Hootsuite, Buffer, etc.).
- - Ability to craft viral and engaging content.


### HR Interview


Common questions:


- - Why do you want to work at Zomato?
- - How would you handle a PR crisis on social media?
- - Can you share a time you ran a successful campaign?
