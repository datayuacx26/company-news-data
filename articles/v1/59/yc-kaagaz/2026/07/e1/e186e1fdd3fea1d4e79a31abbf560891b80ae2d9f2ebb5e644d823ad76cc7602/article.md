# How to Get an Internship at TCS, Infosys, or Wipro

## Eligibility Criteria


Before applying, ensure you meet the following requirements:


### Education:


- - Minimum 60% in 10th, 12th, and ongoing degree program
- - Pursuing B.Tech/B.E, MCA, M.Sc (CS, IT, Electronics, etc.)
- - No active backlogs


### Accepted Streams:


- - BE/BTech/ME/MTech (all streams)
- - MCA, MSc (CS, Electronics, Maths, Physics, Statistics)


## Ways to Apply for Internships


You can apply for internships through multiple channels:


- - **TCS Careers:**[TCS NextStep Portal](https://nextstep.tcs.com/)
- - **Infosys Careers:**[Infosys Internships](https://www.infosys.com/careers/internships.html)
- - **Wipro Careers:**[Wipro Internship Program](https://careers.wipro.com/)
- - **Internship Platforms:** Apply through portals like[Internshala](https://internshala.com/) ,[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/) and[Naukri](https://www.naukri.com/) .
- - **College Tie-Ups:** Check with your college’s placement cell for any collaboration with these companies.

## TCS, Infosys, Wipro Internship Selection Process


### Online Assessment Test


Most companies conduct an aptitude-based assessment covering:


- - **Logical Reasoning:** 15-20 questions (20 mins)
- - **Quantitative Ability:** 10-15 questions (30 mins)
- - **Verbal Ability:** 15-20 questions (20 mins)
- - **Coding Challenge:** 1-2 problems (30-45 mins)


### Technical Interview


Key areas to focus on:


- -Programming (C, C++, Java, Python)
- -Database & Operating System fundamentals
- -Projects & Certifications


### HR Interview


Common questions include:


- -Why do you want to intern with us?
- -What are your career aspirations?
- -Are you comfortable with remote work?
