# Top 5 Resume Mistakes Freshers Make (And How to Fix Them)

Your resume is the first impression you make on recruiters, but many freshers unknowingly make mistakes that hurt their chances of landing a job. A poorly structured resume can lead to rejection, even if you have the right skills.


In this guide, we’ll discuss the top **5 resume mistakes freshers make** and how to fix them, ensuring your resume stands out in India’s competitive job market.


## Mistake 1: Using a Generic Resume Format


Many freshers use the same resume for every job application, which **reduces their chances of getting shortlisted** . Recruiters prefer tailored resumes that match the job description.


### How to Fix It?


- - **Customize Your Resume** – Highlight skills and experiences relevant to the job role.
- - **Use Keywords** – Extract important terms from the job description and incorporate them.
- - **Keep It ATS-Friendly** – Many companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter resumes.

## Mistake 2: Poor Resume Formatting & Length


A resume that is too long, poorly structured, or cluttered makes it difficult for recruiters to find key information quickly.


### How to Fix It?


- - **Stick to One Page** – A fresher resume should be **concise and well-structured** .
- - **Use Clear Headings & Bullet Points** – Break content into sections for easy readability.
- - **Choose a Professional Layout** – Avoid fancy fonts or excessive colors.
