# How to Get Hired at Deloitte India

## Eligibility Criteria


Before applying, check if you meet these requirements:


### Education:


- - 60% or above in 10th and 12th
- - 65% or above in B.Tech/B.E/MCA/MBA/Other relevant degrees
- - No active backlogs


### Accepted Streams:


- - BE/BTech (CS, IT, ECE, EE) or MCA, MBA, B.Com, BBA for specific roles
- - M.Tech, MSc (CS, IT, Analytics) for advanced roles


Note: A maximum of one year’s education gap is allowed.


## Deloitte Job Application Process


You can apply for Deloitte jobs through:


- - **Online Application:**[Deloitte Careers](https://southasiacareers.deloitte.com/go/Deloitte-India/718244/)
- - **Campus Placement:** Check with your college’s placement cell
- - **Employee Referrals:** Reach out to current Deloitte employees for a referral

## Deloitte Recruitment Process for Freshers


### Online Assessment Test


- - **Logical Reasoning:** 15-20 questions (20 mins)
- - **Quantitative Aptitude:** 15-20 questions (30 mins)
- - **Verbal Ability:** 15-20 questions (25 mins)
- - **Coding Round (for technical roles):** 2-3 coding problems (45 mins)
- - **Essay Writing (for consulting roles):** One case-based or opinion-based essay (20 mins)

### Technical Interview


Focus on:


- -Programming (C, C++, Java, Python)
- -Data Structures & Algorithms
- -System Design & Cloud Computing
- -Projects & Internships
- -Role-specific knowledge (Finance, Analytics, Consulting, or Technology roles)


### HR Interview


Common questions:


- -Why do you want to work at Deloitte?
- -What do you know about Deloitte’s work culture?
- -Describe a challenging situation and how you handled it.
- -Are you open to relocation and flexible work hours?
