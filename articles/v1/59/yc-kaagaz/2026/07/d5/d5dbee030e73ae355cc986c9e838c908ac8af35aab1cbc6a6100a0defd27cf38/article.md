# How to Land a Job at Jio in 2025

Reliance Jio, one of India's leading telecom giants, is a dream company for many fresh graduates. With its fast-paced work environment and abundant opportunities, landing a job at Jio can be a great start to your career. This guide will take you through the step-by-step process to secure a fresher job at Jio.


## Why Work at Jio?


- - Competitive salary packages
- - Excellent career growth opportunities
- - Learning-driven culture
- - Exposure to innovative projects


## Eligibility Criteria


To be eligible for a fresher job at Jio, you typically need:


- - A **Bachelor’s degree** in engineering, business, or a related field
- - A good academic record (generally 60% or above in 10th, 12th, and graduation)
- - Strong analytical and communication skills


For more details on eligibility and hiring criteria for other companies, check out our guide on[How to Get a Job as a Fresher in India (Without Campus Placement)](https://kaagaz.app/blog/how-to-get-a-job-without-campus-placement) .


## Jio Hiring Process for Freshers


Jio follows a structured recruitment process to select fresh talent. Here’s what you can expect:


### 1. Online Application


- - Visit the Jio Careers page and browse openings for freshers.
- - Submit your resume and required details.
- Ensure your resume is **ATS-friendly** to get shortlisted.[Use our guide on How to Create a Resume That Passes ATS](https://kaagaz.app/blog/how-to-create-ats-friendly-resume) for tips.


### 2. Online Assessment


- - Includes **aptitude, logical reasoning, and technical questions** (for IT roles).
- - Practice sample aptitude tests to improve your chances.


### 3. Technical Interview


- - Focus on basic coding concepts (for IT roles) and subject knowledge.
- - Be prepared for scenario-based problem-solving questions.
- -Read our guide on[How to Get an Internship in India Without Any Experience](https://kaagaz.app/blog/how-to-get-an-internship-in-india-without-experience) to gain relevant skills before applying.


### 4. HR Interview


- - Standard questions on strengths, weaknesses, and career aspirations.
- - Showcase confidence, communication skills, and eagerness to learn.
