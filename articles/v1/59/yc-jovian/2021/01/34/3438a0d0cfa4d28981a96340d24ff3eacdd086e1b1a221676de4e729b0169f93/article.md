## Schmidhuber’s blog post on World Models and Artificial Curiosity


**1990: Planning & Reinforcement Learning with Recurrent World Models and Artificial Curiosity**


**From the article:**


> What does all of this have to do with the seemingly elusive concepts of **consciousness** and **self-awareness** ? My[first deep learning machine](http://people.idsia.ch/~juergen/deep-learning-miraculous-year-1990-1991.html#Sec.%201) of 1991[\[UN0-UN3\]](http://people.idsia.ch/~juergen/world-models-planning-curiosity-fki-1990.html#UN0) emulates aspects of consciousness as follows. It uses unsupervised learning and *predictive coding*[\[UN0-UN3\]](http://people.idsia.ch/~juergen/world-models-planning-curiosity-fki-1990.html#UN0)[\[SNT\]](http://people.idsia.ch/~juergen/world-models-planning-curiosity-fki-1990.html#SNT) to compress observation sequences. A so-called *“conscious chunker RNN”* attends to unexpected events that surprise a lower-level so-called *“subconscious automatiser RNN.”* The chunker RNN learns to *“understand”* the surprising events by predicting them. The automatiser RNN uses a neural[knowledge distillation procedure](http://people.idsia.ch/~juergen/deep-learning-miraculous-year-1990-1991.html#Sec.%202) of 1991[\[UN0-UN2\]](http://people.idsia.ch/~juergen/world-models-planning-curiosity-fki-1990.html#UN0) (see[Sec. 2](http://people.idsia.ch/~juergen/deep-learning-miraculous-year-1990-1991.html#Sec.%202) of[\[MIR\]](http://people.idsia.ch/~juergen/world-models-planning-curiosity-fki-1990.html#MIR) ) to compress and absorb the formerly *“conscious”* insights and behaviours of the chunker RNN, thus making them *“subconscious.”*


**Link:**[http://people.idsia.ch/~juergen/world-models-planning-curiosity-fki-1990.html](http://people.idsia.ch/~juergen/world-models-planning-curiosity-fki-1990.html)
