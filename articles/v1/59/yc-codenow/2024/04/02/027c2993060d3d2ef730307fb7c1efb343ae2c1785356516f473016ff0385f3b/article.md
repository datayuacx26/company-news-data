# AI's Practical Impact: Insights from Petr Svoboda at Systeum Meetup 2024


Petr Svoboda shares insights on AI's practical impact at Systeum Meetup 2024.


## Overview


At the Systeum Meetup, industry leaders discussed how AI applications are reshaping business, society, and employment trends. The event featured perspectives from multiple experts examining AI's nuanced influence across various sectors.


## Key Speakers and Insights


### Katerina Lesch - Human Values in AI


Data analytics specialist Katerina Lesch emphasized that "human touch and emotional intelligence" remain vital as AI technologies advance. Her message highlighted the importance of preserving human-centered values within an AI-driven landscape.


### Jan Tyl - Digital Twins and Education


Researcher Jan Tyl, a pioneer in digital twin technology, shared innovative educational approaches. He demonstrated how digital simulations can teach democracy and human rights by leveraging neurological patterns that inspire AI design. Tyl encouraged organizations to begin by developing "digital experts" with domain-specific knowledge.


### Petr Svoboda - IT Job Market Transformation


CEO of CodeNOW, Petr Svoboda addressed AI's significant impact on entry-level IT employment. His analysis revealed emerging specialized roles requiring both technical skills and industry-specific expertise. He also highlighted strategic movement toward hybrid cloud environments as organizations balance AI capabilities with data management challenges.


## Panel Discussion Themes


The broader conversation explored philosophical and ethical dimensions, including:


- Potential for AI consciousness
- Parallels between artificial and human neural networks
- The concept of "digital hormones"


These discussions underscored the ethical responsibilities accompanying AI technology adoption.
