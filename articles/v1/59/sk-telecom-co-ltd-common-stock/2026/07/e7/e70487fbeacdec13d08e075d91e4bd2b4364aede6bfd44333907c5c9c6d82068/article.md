# SK Telecom Launches Dedicated AI Data Center Business Development Company to Kick Off ‘Asia’s AI Infrastructure Hub’ Push

- Facebook
- X
- Email
- Copy URL
- Print


**· New subsidiary ‘SK Hyper’ established to pursue 15GW AI data center business in earnest**
**· Investment of KRW 750 billion by 2030 to secure key foundations such as site acquisitions and substation construction**
**· Chung Suk-geun, CEO of SK Hyper: “Through systematic and rapid execution, we will contribute to Korea’s advancement to AI G3”**


SK Telecom (NYSE: SKM, hereafter “SKT”) announced that its Board of Directors has approved the establishment of ‘SK Hyper,’ a new company dedicated to AI data center (AIDC) business development, along with an investment of KRW 750 billion by 2030 to lay the groundwork for the business.


The name ‘SK Hyper’ embodies the meaning of transcending all limits to unlock infinite possibilities, and reflects the company’s determination to rapidly expand the AIDC business to hyperscale.


SK Hyper will focus on business development to enable the build-out of 15GW of AIDC capacity over the mid-to-long term. It will be responsible for securing sites, constructing and operating substations, acquiring customers, and commercializing the AIDC business


SKT will retain 100% ownership of SK Hyper and provide phased capital contributions as needed through 2030, within the approved investment limit.


With the newly established company, SKT is expected to accelerate the build-out of AIDCs to help achieve the government’s mega project. Centered on SK Hyper, SKT targets a phased opening of 5GW of AIDC capacity in 2029 as the first phase.


Taking market demand and other factors into account, SKT plans to commence sequential expansion toward a total capacity of 15 GW in 2035.


Recently, SKT also established the AIDC Integrated Development Division reporting directly to the CEO to consolidate the Group’s full‑stack AI capabilities.


SKT plans to build out AI infrastructure by region, starting with a GW‑class AIDC cluster in Ulsan and adding additional GW‑class AIDCs in the Chungcheong and Honam regions.


On the same day, SK Hyper’s Board of Directors appointed Chung Suk-geun as the inaugural CEO of SK Hyper. CEO Chung is recognized for deep expertise in the AIDC business, currently serving as SKT’s Head of the AI CIC (Company-in-Company). He also heads SKT’s AIDC Integrated Development Division, tasked with connecting SK Group’s AIDC execution organizations and accelerating the delivery of the project.


“SK Hyper’s role is to turn SK Group’s blueprint for Asia’s AI Infrastructure Hub into reality,” said Chung Suk-geun, CEO of SK Hyper. “Through systematic and rapid execution, we will secure core infrastructure and customers, and contribute to Korea’s advancement to AI G3.”
