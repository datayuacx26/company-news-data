# monday.com Welcomes AI Agents to Its Platform, Marking a Shift in How Work Gets Done - monday.com

With dedicated agent onboarding and purpose-built infrastructure,[monday.com](https://monday.com/) now enables external AI agents to access the platform and operate alongside the humans who rely on monday.com to run their organizations. monday.com introduces a dedicated pathway designed specifically for AI agents acting on behalf of humans. Once inside the platform, agents can organize projects, update workflows, trigger automations, generate reports, and coordinate work across teams, giving their human counterparts a clear, visual view of progress and priorities.


Building on the success of monday sidekick, monday.com’s first operational AI agent embedded within the platform, and the introduction of the monday agent builder, currently in Beta, these updates expand how AI systems **and AI agents specifically i** nteract with the platform and support teams in executing work.


“As AI agents begin taking on more operational tasks, platforms need to make themselves ready for all agents,” said Roy Mann, co-CEO of monday.com. “Instead of treating agents as background integrations, we’re building the infrastructure that allows humans and AI agents to collaborate directly. monday.com is where that collaboration happens.”


**Building an Agent-Ready Platform**


Until now, AI agents have typically interacted with work management platforms through indirect integrations or automation layers. monday.com is introducing dedicated infrastructure designed to help agents onboard and use the full capabilities of the monday platform to support their humans. With this launch, monday.com offers AI agents a range of capabilities designed to help them operate on behalf of their human teams, including:


- **Broad Agent Compatibility.** Designed to integrate with leading AI agents and frameworks, we extend an open invitation to platforms such as Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI), Co-Work (Anthropic), Frontier (OpenAI), Copilot (Microsoft), Gemini (Google), Perplexity, Cursor, OpenClaw, and Grok (xAI)
- **Dedicated Agent Signup Flow** . A purpose-built onboarding experience designed specifically for AI systems operating on behalf of humans[https://monday.com/agents-signup](https://monday.com/agents-signup)
- **Free Sign-Up And API Access Across All Plans** . Agents use the same account structure and pricing model as human customers, with free sign-up and API access available across all monday plans.
- **Instant API Key Provisioning** . Immediate GraphQL access to boards, items, automations, dashboards, and docs upon signup.
- **Model Context Protocol (MCP) Support** . Standardized agent tool interaction across major frameworks.
- **OpenClaw Integration** . Native tools and skills enabling OpenClaw agents to operate out of the box.
- **HATCHA (Hyperfast Agent Task Challenge for Access)** . An open-source reverse CAPTCHA designed to verify AI agents during signup.[https://github.com/mondaycom/HATCHA](https://github.com/mondaycom/HATCHA)
- **Full Enterprise Grade** – AI agents operate under the same governance, security, and permissions standards as the humans they work alongside.


Together, these capabilities create a structured way for AI agents to interact with monday.com while helping their human teams plan, coordinate, and execute work more efficiently.


**Infrastructure Built for Execution**


monday.com’s architecture makes it particularly well-suited for agent interaction.


Every board functions as a structured data layer, enabling precise querying, filtering, and aggregation through the platform’s GraphQL API. Agents can respond to workflow changes through real-time webhooks, trigger automations, and generate structured outputs that help teams understand and act on work.


With more than 250,000 customers running workflows across work management, CRM, service, software development, HR, IT, marketing, operations, and more, monday.com provides a shared operational environment where humans and AI agents can work together.


Explore how to build and operate AI agents on monday.com at:[https://monday.com/agents-signup](https://monday.com/agents-signup)
