# monday.com to Announce Fourth Quarter 2025 Financial Results on Monday, February 9, 2026 - monday.com

NEW YORK & TEL AVIV, Israel–([BUSINESS WIRE](https://www.businesswire.com/) )–[monday.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fmonday.com&esheet=54398044&newsitemid=20260120772128&lan=en-US&anchor=monday.com&index=1&md5=275b127855075a8f33c561f93e59a859) (NASDAQ: MNDY) today announced it will report its financial results for the fourth quarter of 2025 on Monday, February 9, 2026. monday.com management will host a conference call and webcast to discuss the results that morning at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time.


Information about monday.com’s financial results, including a link to the live webcast of the conference call, will be made available on monday.com’s investor relations website at[https://ir.monday.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.monday.com%2F&esheet=54398044&newsitemid=20260120772128&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fir.monday.com&index=2&md5=c6cf21fcddac70bb4e80c9bf1cf159aa) . The live call may also be accessed via telephone at +1 (646) 968-2525 or +1 (888) 596-4144 (toll-free). Please reference conference ID: 1347415. An archived webcast can be accessed from the News & Events section of monday.com’s Investor Relations website following the call.
