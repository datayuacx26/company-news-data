# Introducing Complete's Direct Integration with Carta

We are excited to unveil our new direct API integration with Carta. This integration enables our clients to access their equity data directly within the Complete platform. By connecting Complete.so with[Carta’s Developer Platform](https://carta.com/api/) , users can now obtain a comprehensive view of their compensation and equity information in a single, unified interface, thereby simplifying the management of topline compensation spend.


‍


### Key Benefits of the Complete.so and Carta Integration


- Seamless Integration: Connect your Carta account with Complete effortlessly through our direct API integration.
- Unified Dashboard: Access all your compensation and equity data in one place, providing a holistic view of your team's financials.
- Enhanced Decision-Making: Make informed decisions with real-time equity data at your fingertips, ensuring fair and transparent compensation plans.
- Time-Saving: Eliminate the need for manual data entry and reduce administrative overhead, allowing you to focus on what matters most – growing your startup.


‍


### Getting Started


1. Sign Up: If you have not already done so, please sign up for a demo[here.](http://complete.so/)
2. Connect: Use the API integration setup within Complete to link your Carta account on the integrations tab.
3. Sync: Synchronize your equity data and begin exploring the unified dashboard.
