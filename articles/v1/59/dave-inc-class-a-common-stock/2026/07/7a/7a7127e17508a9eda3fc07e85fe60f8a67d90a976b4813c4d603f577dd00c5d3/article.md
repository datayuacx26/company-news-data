##### Benefits of transformative banking technology


Mobile banking apps offer products that make banking more accessible, save time, and empower consumers to make informed financial decisions. Here are a few ways the digital transformation in banking has made it easier to manage how you save and spend.


###### 24/7 remote access to your bank account


Gone are the days of standing in long queues at the bank or waiting on hold for a customer service representative. Mobile banking apps provide you with 24/7 access to your savings and checking account information and the ability to manage it from anywhere, anytime. You can check your balance, transfer funds, pay bills, and even deposit checks in a few simple taps—all from the convenience of your smartphone.


###### Budgeting and expense tracking


The best mobile banking apps go beyond basic account management and offer additional financial tools that help you track expenses, budget, take an[overdraft](https://dave.com/about-extra-cash?) 1, and[set financial Goals](https://dave.com/goal-account) . These features help you understand how you're spending your money, where you could save some cash, and how to strengthen your finances, both short-term and long-term.


###### Enhanced security and fraud protection


With the increased use of mobile banking,[security has become a top priority](https://dave.com/blog/7-tips-for-mobile-banking-safety) for mobile banking apps. These apps usually have security features such as:


- **Biometric authentication** : Fingerprint or facial recognition can be more secure than passwords and PINs, as the account holders have to be physically present to authenticate access.
- **Encrypted data transmission** : This keeps transactions safe and protects personal information from being stolen. It also prevents unauthorized access, which can help users feel more confident that their privacy is protected
- **Two-factor authentication** : In case a user’s data is compromised, two-factor authentication prevents user accounts from unauthorized access and potential fraud. It adds an extra layer of verification.


These measures ensure the safety of your sensitive financial information and provide an additional layer of protection against fraud.


###### Customized alerts and real-time notifications


Mobile banking apps are great at keeping you up-to-date with your finances and in control of your money. You can set up alerts for different transactions, account balances, and upcoming payments. That way, you can always be on top of your finances and catch any suspicious activity right away.
