# Spartan RV Chassis and Love's Travel Stops Partner to Bring Professional Chassis Service to RV Owners across America

The RV industry is booming, and America's roads are home to more motorhome owners than ever. Serving them well means combining world-class vehicle engineering with a service network they can rely on. Through AtlasCare™, Spartan RV Chassis already delivers that promise with 24/7 support and more than 500 authorized service locations nationwide. The partnership with Love’s now expands that network further, adding one of America’s most trusted names in travel and truck care, with more than 670 locations in 42 states.


"Spartan owners invest in the best chassis on the road and they deserve a service they can count on", said Douglas Searles, vice president & general manager, Spartan RV Chassis. "Partnering with Love's means our customers can pull into a trusted location across the country any time and know their chassis will be cared for the right way."


Through the agreement, participating Love's Truck Care locations now offer Spartan owners comprehensive maintenance packages, chassis inspections, lubrication service, and precision wheel alignment—all performed to Spartan's exact specifications. Spartan's technical standards and service expertise will be embedded directly into Love's network, giving motorhome owners a convenient, consistent option for chassis care wherever their travels take them.


### **Proven Expertise from Coast to Coast**


Love’s Truck Care is a natural fit for Spartan’s AtlasCare™ program due to its deep expertise in servicing large, heavy‑duty vehicles. With proven experience maintaining commercial trucks, Love’s teams routinely work on systems found in premium Class A motorhome chassis, including braking, suspension, steering, axles, tires, and long‑wheelbase alignment.


"RV owners are a valued and growing customer segment for Love’s," said Eric Daniels, vice president of truck care for Love’s. "This partnership with Spartan is a great addition to our amenities for RVers, and underscores our commitment to help our customers get road ready so they can focus on the journey ahead."


With a growing network of more than 130 Love’s RV Stops, customers have access to convenient overnight RV hookups nationwide, featuring amenities such as Wi-Fi, electric and water hookups, RV dump stations, and – at select locations – additional offerings such as sport courts, fire pits and grills. Love’s is the only travel stop network that allows RVers to book, pay for and turn on amenities on their phones through the Love’s Rewards App.
