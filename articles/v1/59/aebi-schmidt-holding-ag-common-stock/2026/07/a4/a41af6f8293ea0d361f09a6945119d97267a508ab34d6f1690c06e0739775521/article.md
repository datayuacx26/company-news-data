# Breaking Tradition: Meyer Products Unveils Limited-Edition Blackout Plows

As Meyer enters its next century, the brand is doing something unexpected: breaking tradition. The Limited-Edition Blackout Plows combine the legendary performance Meyer is known for with a sleek design and exclusive production run of just 100 units.


This goes beyond a cosmetic shift. It reflects a clear understanding of where the industry is headed and what end users value today.


Fleets, contractors, and operators take real pride in how their equipment looks. Alongside quality and performance, appearance matters.


The Blackout Series leans directly into that shift. Sleek. Bold. Purpose-built to stand out. It delivers a sharp presence that pairs naturally with today’s trucks, and the pride operators take in their equipment choices.


The Drive Pro Blackout Edition delivers an aggressive look for homeowners who tackle snow and ice removal and want to do it in style.


### **A Century-Old Icon Gets a Glow-Up**


Internally, the decision to move away from Meyer Yellow generated immediate excitement.


“The reaction was overwhelmingly positive,” said Nate Kallay, Sales Director at Meyer Products. “Our team was energized by the opportunity to do something different. Meyer Yellow is an important part of our heritage, but the Blackout Series gave us a chance to create something unexpected and exciting for our customers during our 100th anniversary year.”


That enthusiasm quickly translated into the product itself. The Blackout finish delivers a striking look that stands out in a category where performance has traditionally taken center stage.


“Black has become one of the most popular colors for truck accessories, and we saw an opportunity to bring that trend to the snow and ice industry,” Kallay explained. “The Blackout Series gives customers something unique that they can't get every day. With only 100 units available, there's an exclusivity factor that creates excitement and a little bit of FOMO among buyers.”


By limiting production to just 100 plows, Meyer created a collector-worthy anniversary edition designed to spark conversation, generate demand, and give customers the opportunity to own a distinctive piece of Meyer history.


The Blackout Series is built for a new generation of buyers. Those who want a plow that performs and looks just as striking as the trucks they drive.


### **Built for a New Generation of Buyers**


While Meyer has earned the loyalty of generations of snow professionals, the Blackout Series is attracting attention from a new audience.


Dealers and end users alike are responding to the opportunity to own something unique. Early feedback has been positive, with strong interest from customers who take pride not only in the performance of their snowplow but also in its appearance.


The Blackout Series delivers a head-turning look that pairs naturally with today's trucks, especially the growing number of operators who invest time and money into customizing and maintaining their vehicles. The snowplow is part of the overall snow-fighting identity.


“We're seeing excitement from customers who want their equipment to reflect their personal style as much as their work ethic,” said Kallay. “The Blackout Series gives them a premium, distinctive look without sacrificing the durability and performance Meyer is known for.”


From contractors looking to make a statement on the new generation of plow operators, the Blackout Series offers something different. It stands out from the crowd.


The Pro-Mount Attachment System delivers fast, secure mounting, standard on all Blackout Edition plows.


### **Engineering a Fresh Shift**


From an engineering perspective, the idea of moving away from Meyer Yellow carried a different set of emotions.


For a brand that has spent a century building recognition around its iconic yellow plows, introducing a black edition wasn't a decision that came without hesitation.


“To be honest, my first reaction was apprehension,” said Marty Warchola of Meyer Engineering. “Our yellow plows are instantly recognizable, and in an industry that doesn't always embrace radical change, there was naturally some concern about how customers would respond.”


But as prototypes began to take shape, that apprehension quickly gave way to excitement.


“Once we saw the plows come together, the perspective changed,” Warchola said. “The Blackout Series brings a fresh look to the product while still delivering everything people expect from a Meyer plow.”


What became clear throughout the development process was that the Blackout Series was about demonstrating that a strong brand is bigger than any single design element.


“The brand is built through decades of innovation, quality, reliability, and the trust we've earned from our customers,” said Marty. “Color is certainly part of that identity, but only one piece of the story. Meyer’s legacy has always been one of continuous evolution and introduction of new ideas to adapt to the needs of the market.


That philosophy helped guide the project from concept to reality. The Blackout Series honors the legacy that made Meyer an industry leader while introducing a contemporary look that reflects the next chapter of the brand. It's a reminder that innovation doesn't always mean starting over. It means finding new ways to build on a century of success.


The Lot Pro Blackout Edition delivers contractor-grade performance paired with a head-turning look built to stand out on the jobsite.


### **Designed to Stand Out. Built to Perform.**


The Limited-Edition Blackout Series is more than a new finish. It's a statement.


After 100 years, Meyer is still willing to challenge expectations, embrace the latest ideas, and create products that generate excitement across the industry.

For dealers, it's an opportunity to offer something truly exclusive. For end users, it's a chance to own a piece of Meyer history. And with only 100 units available, once they're gone, they're gone.


As Meyer celebrates a century of helping customers take on Mother Nature, the Blackout Series serves as a reminder that strong brands don't stand still. They evolve, innovate, and continue finding new ways to deliver progress.
