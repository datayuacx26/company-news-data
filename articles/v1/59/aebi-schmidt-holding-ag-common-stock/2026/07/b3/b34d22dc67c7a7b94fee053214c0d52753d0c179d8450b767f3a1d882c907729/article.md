# 50 years of Terratrac: Aebi celebrates an original

At the foot of the two Mythen mountains, Aebi Schmidt hosted an event to mark the 50th anniversary of the Terratrac. The event demonstrated how practical experience, technical curiosity and close dialogue with users have given rise to a vehicle concept that continues to be developed to this day. The highlight of the dealer and press event was the world premiere of the new Aebi TT 212 – and with it, a glimpse into the next chapter of the Terratrac’s history.


### An occasion for the Terratrac family


The Blüemlisberg Alp provided the perfect setting for an anniversary closely linked to mountain farming throughout the Alpine region. Swiss market partners, international dealers and media representatives gathered on 7 July 2026 to look back on 50 years of Terratrac and, at the same time, look ahead to the future. Some dealers travelled over 2,000 kilometres for the event – the longest journey was from Sweden to the Blüemlisberg Alp. For dealers from Austria and South Tyrol, a separate event followed on 8 July, featuring a dealers’ evening at the Aebi Schmidt Austria site in Inzing, Tyrol.


Barend Fruithof, CEO of the Aebi Schmidt Group, welcomed those present and emphasised the importance of the Terratrac for Aebi and the Aebi Schmidt Group. In his speech, he expressed particular thanks to the dealers. Their close relationship with customers, their understanding of the market and the knowledge they have gained from day-to-day use have played a key role in the ongoing development of the Terratrac over five decades.


### From the idea to the Terratrac


Aebi celebrated 50 years of the Terratrac on the alp, but the story began even before the launch of the first Terratrac in 1976. Aebi set out to find ways of making heavy agricultural work easier, more efficient and safer.


As early as 1912, the company had been attempting to reduce physical strain and increase area output with its Aebi self-propelled mowers. Further experiments and developments followed, including the HM96 hay baler from the early 1970s.


Two technical ideas ultimately led to the breakthrough for the first Terratrac concept: one was the Terra tyres, which the Aebi family discovered during a trip to America and integrated into the new machine design. The other idea was to adapt a drum mower from a tractor for use on the Terratrac, thereby replacing the cutter bar. This combination of soil-conserving tyres, excellent hill-climbing ability and new mowing technology made the Terratrac a practical vehicle concept.


In 1976, the time had come: the first Aebi Terratrac TT 77 was launched – a vehicle developed specifically for use on slopes. Compact, manoeuvrable, safe and powerful – characteristics that still form the core of the Terratrac’s DNA today.

Since the first TT 77, the Terratrac has undergone continuous development across numerous model generations. Increased power, continuously variable drives, all-wheel steering, improved visibility, more powerful lifting mechanisms and modern operating concepts have expanded the possibilities in mountain farming and[municipal](https://www.aebi-schmidt.com/en/products-solutions/products/municipal-solutions/) applications. Throughout, the Terratrac has always remained true to its core philosophy: to be a reliable and safe machine for demanding tasks. Several historic models on display at Blüemlisberg illustrated this development, showing how the concept has evolved over five decades – and how consistently Aebi has carried it forward.


### The new Aebi TT 212: the next chapter


With the world premiere of the new Aebi TT 212, the focus was deliberately set on the future. The model succeeds the tried-and-tested TT 211+ and marks an important step forward in the development of the small Terratrac series.


The TT 212 is based on a completely newly developed vehicle platform. New axles, a new chassis design, decoupled mounting of the engine and cab, robust braking technology, a modern drive system and significantly increased lifting forces provide greater performance reserves and enhanced comfort in day-to-day use. At the same time, the TT 212 remains compact, manoeuvrable and designed for safe operation on slopes.


Further details on the new Aebi TT 212 can be found in the[separate blog article on the machine.](https://www.aebi-schmidt.com/en/news/2026/07/07/aebi-launches-the-new-terratrac-tt-212/)


### 50 years of experience – and a clear vision for the future


The event at Alp Blüemlisberg demonstrated that the Terratrac is more than just a product. It is a piece of Aebi’s history, an essential tool for many users and an example of how technical development arises from practical experience.
