# Built in Kielce. Trusted around the world.

Founded in 1993, the plant began like many successful companies: modestly, with local roots, and growing step by step. Today, the site stands as a testament to the strength of European manufacturing and the combined expertise of the Aebi Schmidt Group. Every year, more than 1,800 snow ploughs are produced here for customers in over 50 countries worldwide, all manufactured to perform reliably even under the most demanding winter conditions.


In Kielce, the decades of experience of Schmidt, Arctic, Tellefsdal and Beilhack come together – ensuring that every plough meets the high standards of winter[service](https://www.aebi-schmidt.com/en/products-solutions/maintenance-service/) professionals around the world. Continuous investment in automation, robotics and ISO 9001-certified processes guarantees high efficiency, flexibility and consistent compliance with international quality standards. More than 300 employees – engineers, technicians and production specialists – work every day to turn innovative solutions into reality for customers around the globe.


Whether on motorways, mountain passes, at airports or on local road networks: the snow ploughs manufactured in Kielce are designed for durability, efficiency and reliable performance – even under the harshest winter conditions.
