# Netflix Reveals Trailer for Swedish Crime Thriller 'Blood Sacrifice'

[DOWNLOAD IMAGES HERE](https://assetshare.netflixstudios.com/561b5fb7-0293-48ab-80b9-5326a3f4265e)


[WATCH THE TRAILER](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YjgT9NMq3ww)


Netflix today unveils the trailer for a gripping new Swedish crime thriller series, premiering on August 20. Created by George Kay (


Lupin


,


Hijack


), the five-part drama introduces a tense and emotionally charged hunt for a killer targeting the police.


Jakob Oftebro (


Hamilton


) stars as lead investigator Thomas, forced to work with his estranged father Alfred, a former detective played by Peter Andersson (


The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo


). As bodies mount and the attacks draw closer to home, father and son must put decades of resentment aside to stop a brutal killer before time runs out. The series is directed by Kristoffer Nyholm (


The Killing


,


Taboo


).


Actor Jakob Oftebro:


“


Blood Sacrifice is an intense, emotional journey. Thomas is a man who has dedicated his life to the police, but when the attacks start targeting his own world, he’s forced to confront not only a brutal killer, but also the complicated relationship with his father. Playing him was both challenging and incredibly rewarding - it’s a story about justice, loyalty and what happens when the past refuses to stay buried


.”


Actor Peter Andersson:


“


What drew me to Blood Sacrifice was the powerful mix of crime thriller and family drama. Playing a detective felt very close to my heart, especially as my own son is a police officer. I also really enjoyed working with Jakob - my fictional son this time - and I can’t wait for audiences to discover the tense series


.”


About Blood Sacrifice


It is summer time in Stockholm, when the darkness never really creeps in. A 911 call is received, speaking of a home invasion. Two police officers are sent to the address, a summer house in the archipelago. Only to find there is no one there, at least not that they can see. The next day the bodies of the officers are found, a brutal crime. This is the start of a series of terrifying murders, all seemingly targeting police officers. The lead detective needs to ask his father, a retired detective, for help in cracking the case.
