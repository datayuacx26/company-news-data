# How ‘The Polygamist’ Is Breaking Records and Opening Doors in South Africa and Around the World

At Netflix, we’ve seen over and over again how local stories can travel the globe. From South Korea (


[Teach You a Lesson](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/teach-you-a-lesson-release-date-news) ), from France (


[Lupin](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/lupin) ), from Brazil (


[Rulers of Fortune](https://about.netflix.com/en/news/rulers-of-fortune-netflix-gathers-cast-to-celebrate-the-start-of-season-2-production) ) and now, from South Africa with


[The Polygamist](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/the-polygamist-release-date-news) .


Based on the 2012 book of the same name by Zimbabwean author Sue Nyathi,


The Polygamist


debuted on Netflix June 12th and quickly became one of our biggest African hits ever. The 22-episode


super


novela has reached #1 in


21 countries


and the Top 10 in 62, amassing


27.6M views


and counting. Since opening at #2 on the


[Global Top 10 Non-English Shows list](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/top10/tv-non-english) , the show has spent seven consecutive weeks on the list, the most ever for a Netflix title from South Africa.


The series’ impact has extended beyond the screen, with book sales tripling following


The Polygamist’s


Netflix debut, over a decade after the book was first published. “It feels great and it is an affirmation of my work,” Nyathi said of the world’s reception to her story. “Of all my books,


The Polygamist


was dismissed as fluff. … To hear people call it a masterpiece and being able to draw so many life lessons from it is incredible.”


The series, which centers on a social media darling whose cheating husband's conquests ignite a scandalous emotional meltdown, was filmed with Stained Glass Productions over


nearly


100 days


in South Africa. Roughly


150 cast and crew


and over


70 extras and daily hires


were employed on the production, in addition to more than


45 local vendors


.


"We were so specific and so particular in the story we're telling," said Gugu Zuma-Ncube, executive producer at Stained Glass Productions. "It's set against a backdrop of polygamy, which is very African — and the more authentic we were to that, the more universally appealing and relatable the themes became. Polygamy may not be the most common framework for a global audience, but the themes that the show covers - love, adultery, betrayal, struggling with feelings of self-worth, kids feeling they aren’t being met emotionally by their parents, coming‑of‑age - they definitely have universal appeal."

Beyond


The Polygamist


, Nyathi says the Netflix series has helped “broaden” her readership. The same week


The Polygamist


hit #1 on the


[Exclusive Books’ Top 10 Bestsellers list](https://exclusivebooks.co.za/collections/top-10-bestsellers) in South Africa, her 2020 novel


A Family Affair


reached #6. The first run of her 2024 work,


An Angel’s Demis


e, also sold out following


The Polygamist’


s Netflix debut, after only selling 2,500 copies in its first two years of release.


The show’s popularity “has opened doors for me,” Nyathi said. “This series has given me the kind of visibility that writers from the deep south only dream of.”

is one of several adaptations currently heating up our Global Top 10 lists, also including
