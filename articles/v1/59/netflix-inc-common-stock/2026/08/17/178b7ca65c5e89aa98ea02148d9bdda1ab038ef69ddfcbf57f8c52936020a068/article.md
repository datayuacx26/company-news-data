Through series, docs, and games, and more, Netflix’s football slate captured a historic tournament and kicked off the countdown to the FIFA 2027 Women’s World Cup™.


It’s been two weeks since the final whistle blew, but we’re still thinking about all the epic moments from the FIFA 2026 Men’s World Cup™. While it’s still another year until our broadcast of the


[2027 Women’s World Cup](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/features/fifa-womens-world-cup-2027) in the US and Canada, fans couldn’t contain their excitement. Across series, films, documentaries, talk shows and games, our football slate lit up right alongside the tournament:

Game On: Inside the Official FIFA Game


This World Cup, we invited fans to star in their own match with the


[FIFA World Cup: Launch Edition](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/fifa-mens-world-cup-2026-game-on-netflix) game. Members could feel the thrill of the pitch instantly, playing for their country and stepping into real-life stadiums as the world’s best athletes. During the World Cup window, it was the #1 most-played Netflix game. Even after the final whistle of the championship, fans came back to the game in force — surging more than


40%


above normal.


And which teams were most popular in


FIFA World Cup: Launch Edition


? Brazil took the lead, with Mexico and Argentina close behind. Players in Brazil were most likely to choose their home country, unlike in the US, where most players chose to play as Argentina:


In the game, players can upgrade their favorite soccer stars using in-game currency that boosts attributes and unlocks new skill moves. From Vinícius Júnior in Brazil to Raúl Jiménez in Mexico, most gamers chose a hometown hero except for the US, where players spent most of their upgrades on Portugal’s Cristiano Ronaldo.
