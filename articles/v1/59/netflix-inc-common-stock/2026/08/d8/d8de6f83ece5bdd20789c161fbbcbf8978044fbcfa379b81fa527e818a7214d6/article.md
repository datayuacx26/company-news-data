# From Screen to Stitch: Sybylla's Cardigan From ‘My Brilliant Career’ Is Yours to Knit

### Inspired by the on-screen writing cardigan worn by Sybylla,


My Brilliant Career


's fiercely ambitious heroine, Netflix has teamed up with female-owned knitwear label


[Cardigang](https://cardigang.com.au/?tw_source=google&tw_adid=717177306850&tw_campaign=21814097983&tw_kwdid=kwd-315070262871&gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=21814097983&gbraid=0AAAAABupUDkjT39M0986IcNT89RZXupbY&gclid=CjwKCAjw1vXTBhB-EiwAEKr_k1nl_eQPLtsedSBBu-N0PVjD41k7hf6Aj5pM1P3H_zCzqvqwZLJ57hoCMsEQAvD_BwE) to release a free knitting pattern so fans can knit a piece of the story for themselves.


Fans of


My Brilliant Career


who've watched Sybylla Melvyn (Philippa Northeast) write, dream and defy convention in her signature pink cardigan can now knit their own. Netflix has partnered with Cardigang to release The Sybylla - a free pattern inspired by Sybylla's hero writing cardigan - available to download from today.


Oversized, threaded with soft silk ribbon and pulled on every time Sybylla puts pen to paper, the cardigan serves as Sybylla’s creative permission slip. Cardigang has paid homage to it with its own simplified pattern, inspired by the piece's silhouette and spirit - tested from cast-on to bind-off. Whether you've been knitting for years or are picking up needles for the very first time, this one's made for you.


"Everything shifts for Sybylla when she arrives at Five Bob Downs and falls under Augusta's spell,” said Mariot Kerr, costume designer on


My Brilliant Career


. "Through her, Sybylla begins to see that intelligence, creativity and boldness can pair beautifully with style."


"The writing cardigan had to feel like something Augusta herself would give to Sybylla — generous, glamorous and a little whimsical, while also warm, comforting and nurturing. Oversized and threaded with soft silk ribbons, it creates a cocoon where Sybylla can write, dream and discover her own voice."


Want everything you need to make The Sybylla in one go?
