# Netflix Expands Young Adult Slate with New Series 'Sid & Zoey,' 'Sunshine Girls' and More

##


From new titles and release dates to casting news and first looks, discover what’s next in Netflix’s coming-of-age universe.


Netflix is the ultimate destination for Young Adult (YA) entertainment, telling stories that speak to anyone who’s ever navigated first loves, big risks, or moments that shape who you become — whether living the experience right now or feeling nostalgic about the past. As we gear up for our biggest YA slate yet — the return of fan-favorites


Outer Banks


and


Ginny & Georgia;


new series


Crew Girl, A Different World, The Body, Poser


and


The Sticks;


and films


Don’t Say Good Luck


and


Best of the Best —


we spent the week celebrating all things YA with a series of announcements, debuts, bringing authentic storytelling that resonates with audiences and delivers fresh global perspectives. And we’re just getting started…


New Series and Film Announcements


Today, Netflix announced two new scripted series,


Sid & Zoey


and


Sunshine Girls


. Dark, seductive, and unapologetically strange — gothic in its bones and electric in its romance —


Sid & Zoey


arrives precisely as the cultural signals become unmistakable: a generation of audiences hungry for bold, creator-driven genre storytelling that is emotionally alive. This new genre-bending, YA series is equal parts supernatural mystery and romance from writers/showrunners Jon Kasdan and John Shiban, executive produced by Blumhouse Atomic Monster and Bad Robot. Find more details on


Sid & Zoey


[here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1F0xMSX8YOoywZQrAMKGk4qYzYOGX8UUx/view?usp=sharing) .


Sunshine Girls


follows Olivia’s fresh start in the suburbs of Valley Hills, California after her move from New York. Starring Alma Marian, Isabella Fowler and Erin Rose Park and from creators Rob Fishman and Nuria Anson, this series will debut exclusively on Netflix in December. Find more details on


Sunshine Girls


[here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1s7K2ty3_8QJ8SmidzR7YI5BvGhzUzSsf/view?usp=sharing) .


The previously announced


Minimum Wage


keeps it closer to home, following teens juggling part-time jobs and love triangles on the way to graduation. Today, Netflix announced that Randall Park will join the cast, shared a


[first look](https://assetshare.netflixstudios.com/85ffa82b-c39d-429a-9b3e-5e8a90853b61) at the show and confirmed that the first 14 episodes will debut this year, only on Netflix. Find more details on


Minimum Wage


[here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1GCgBccUAKGXa2klHebXTRNvCAyxRM_dN/view?usp=sharing) .


For


Finding Her Edge


fans, Season 2 is edging closer to its grand encore. Today, we announced that Season 2 begins production later this month with Madelyn Keys, Cale Ambrozic and Olly Atkins reprising their roles for new twists and turns on and off the ice. Read more


[here](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/finding-her-edge-season-2) .
