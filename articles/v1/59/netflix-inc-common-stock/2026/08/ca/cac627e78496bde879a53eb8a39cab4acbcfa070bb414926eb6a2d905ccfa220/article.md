# Netflix Confirms Production of New Family Action Comedy Film ‘Two Cops and Five Kids’

Entertainment


05 August 2026


Global


South Korea


Link copied to clipboard


Netflix has announced the production and cast of


Two Cops and Five Kids


, a new family action-comedy film starring Cha Tae-hyun and Uhm Ji-won.


Two Cops and Five Kids


follows a detective who suddenly finds himself solely in charge of all his five children when his wife leaves on a mission disguised as a trip. Just as he’s trying to juggle parenting and preparing for his promotion exam, the whole family becomes unexpectedly involved in an operation to take down a criminal organization.


Cha Tae-hyun (


Moving


,


[Along with the Gods: The Two Worlds](https://www.netflix.com/tm/title/80214451) ) takes on the role of Dong-sik, a detective and father of five. As both a cop and dad, Cha Tae-hyun is set to deliver a mix of relatable moments that evoke empathy and lighthearted laughter.


Uhm Ji-won (


[Dear Hongrang](https://www.netflix.com/title/81708925) ,


[Little Women](https://www.netflix.com/title/81610895) ) plays Kyung-ah, Dong-sik’s wife and the mother of their five children, who also happens to be a former ace police officer. After spending 10 years focused on raising her family, she’s drawn back into the field when she accepts a secret mission from the National Intelligence Service.


The film marks the first on-screen collaboration between Cha Tae-hyun and Uhm Ji-won, two of Korea’s most beloved and respected actors. Beyond their much-anticipated pairing, audiences can also look forward to a family chemistry made richer by the five distinctive sibling characters: Spring (Do Young-seo), Summer (Kim Na-eun), Autumn (Jung Han-sol), Winter (Sim Ji-an), and April (Kim Ra-hee) — hinting at the playful, close-knit family dynamic at the heart of the story.


Directed by Kim Jong-hyun (


Mr. Gam’s Victory


,


[Run Off](https://www.netflix.com/title/80131732) ), known for balancing strong ensemble casts, relatable humor, and warm storytelling,


Two Cops and Five Kids


promises a blend of action, comedy, and family love.


Stay tuned for more updates on


Two Cops and Five Kids


, only on Netflix.
