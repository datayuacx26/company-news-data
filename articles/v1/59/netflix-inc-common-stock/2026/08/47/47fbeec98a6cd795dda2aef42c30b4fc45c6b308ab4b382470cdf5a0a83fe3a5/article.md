# Netflix Releases Official Season 3 Trailer for Highly Anticipated Series 'Graveyard'

Netflix has released the official season 3 trailer for the highly anticipated series


Graveyard


, directed by


Abdullah Oğuz


, starring


Birce Akalay


and


Olgun Toker


. The new season reunites Inspector Önem and her team, and will premiere only on Netflix, on


August 28


.

The series is produced by


Abdullah Oğuz


and


Evren Oğuz


, with


Abdullah Oğuz


and


Ömer Baykul


sharing directing duties on the new season. The screenplay is written by


Fulya Turhan


,


Ahmet Saatçioğlu


, and


Evren Oğuz


. The cast of the third season includes


Birce Akalay


and


Olgun Toker,


joined by


Şehsuvar Aktaş, Hakan Meriçliler, Sezgin Uzunbekiroğlu, Cem Sürgit, Baran Güler, Arbil Tabur, Yeşim Çelebi, Elif Sevinç


, and


Berke Gündem


.


The team reunites to uncover the truth behind the mysterious boxes sent to women, embarking on the search for a shocking truth in the new season of Graveyard. Inspector Önem, meanwhile, finds both her team and herself at the center of a dark threat.
