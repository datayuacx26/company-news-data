# Netflix Celebrates Mexican Cinema Day With Iconic Characters, Bold Stories, and the Richness of the local Cinema

Entertainment


13 August 2026


Global


Mexico


Link copied to clipboard


Mexican cinema brings together perspectives, emotions and characters that invite us to see ourselves on the screen and connect us wherever we are. On August 15, we celebrate Mexican Cinema Day by doing what we’re most passionate about: partnering with the country’s audiovisual industry to amplify the diversity, quality and cultural impact of films made in Mexico.


> Carolina Leconte, Vice President of Content for Mexico & Licensing for Latin America at Netflix,
>
>
> said:
>
>
> “We love being a creative partner to local talent and seeing the many faces of Mexico on screen. Our goal is to tell bold stories that entertain and offer an authentic perspective on Mexican life.”


The film selection we're announcing today ranges from explosive action stories to bold documentaries that connect us to real life and, of course, classic gems that remind us why we love cinema so much.


> “We want to take Mexican action movies to the next level with heroes who inspire us: characters with huge hearts, who are brave and very resourceful—characters whom the audience will root for and whose resilience will stay with us long after the credits roll. That’s the strength of Mexican cinema, and we want more and more people to discover it,”
>
>
> continued Leconte.

## Celebrating Mexican Storytelling


Starting August 13, members will be able to access the exclusive section


[Celebrating Mexican Storytelling](https://www.netflix.com//celebratingmxstories) . This page is dedicated to celebrating all content made in Mexico, and also coincides with Netflix's 15th anniversary in the country.


This collection on Netflix will invite members to rediscover Mexican content that reflects the richness and diversity of the country: from award-winning films and comedies to dramas, love stories, and documentaries that portray the reality of the country. It serves as a gateway for members to find, all in one place, a wide selection of Mexican titles.
