# Netflix Confirms Production and Cast of New Period Action Film ‘Dochabi’ (WT)

Entertainment


13 August 2026


Global


South Korea


Link copied to clipboard


Netflix has announced the production and cast of a new period action drama film,


Dochabi


(WT)


, which is the native Korean word for


dokkaebi


, the Korean goblin.


Dochabi


(WT) is a period action drama about Tae-san, a former soldier who lives in hiding deep in the mountains with a Jurchen woman named Ahai, near the borderlands where farmers have formed settlements. When the commander Yi Do-gwan hunts these farmers under a mask, Tae-san fights back, coming to be known as “Dochabi.”


Kim Do-hoon


(


Moving


,


Your Honor


,


[Love Scout](https://www.netflix.com/kr-en/title/81988231) ) plays Tae-san, bringing his signature mix of emotional nuance and high-intensity action to the role. Tae-san becomes entangled in a mysterious conspiracy by masked figures and is framed as the invaders’ ringleader. Amid desperation, he survives like a monster and fights like a ghost, eventually becoming the "Dochabi" that protects people. The versatile


Jun Jong-seo


(


[Project Y ,](https://www.netflix.com/kr-en/title/82738450)


[Ballerina](https://www.netflix.com/title/81572011) ,


[The Call](https://www.netflix.com/title/81342505) ) takes on the role of Ahai, a Jurchen woman in hiding with Tae-san, whose superior agility and martial prowess shine as she fights alongside him. The charismatic and sharp


Lee Joon-gi


(


Moon Lovers: Scarlet Heart Ryeo


,


[Flower of Evil](https://www.netflix.com/title/81357268) ) transforms into Yi Do-gwan, the commander of the border region who harbors a deep hunger for power.


Lee Sang-yi


(


[Bloodhounds](https://www.netflix.com/title/81444051) ,


[GOOD BOY](https://www.netflix.com/kr-en/title/81908823) ) showcases a strong performance as Park Se-ryong, an inspector investigating rumors of masked figures appearing in the north. Harboring suspicion towards Yi Do-gwan, Park Se-ryong adds tension to the story.


Bae In-hyuk


(


[Under the Queen’s Umbrella ,](https://www.netflix.com/title/81610893)


[Cheer Up](https://www.netflix.com/nz/title/81610940) ) intricately portrays the Crown Prince Yeolhwagun, who carries a deep sense of responsibility and compassion for the people suffering at the hands of invaders.


Dochabi


(WT) is the newest film from director An Tae-jin, whose debut


[The Night Owl](https://www.netflix.com/kr-en/title/81673948)


received much critical acclaim. The film is written by Hwang Sung-gu — known for the in-depth character work and solid storytelling of


The King’s Warden


and


[Anarchist from Colony](https://www.netflix.com/title/80197491) , and produced by Ondaworks, the company behind Korea’s second-highest-grossing film of all time,


The King’s Warden


. With impactful characters, a compelling story, and high-quality production,


Dochabi


(WT) promises to deliver a fresh and powerful period action drama.


Dochabi


(WT) is coming, only on Netflix.
