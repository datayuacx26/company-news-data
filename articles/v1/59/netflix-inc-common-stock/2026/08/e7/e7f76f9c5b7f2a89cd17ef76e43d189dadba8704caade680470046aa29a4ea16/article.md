# The Next Brilliant Career: Netflix Launches Nationwide Search for Australia's Next Great Screenwriter

To celebrate the upcoming release of


[My Brilliant Career](https://www.netflix.com/au/title/81655060) and the legacy of pioneering author Miles Franklin, Netflix ANZ, in partnership with the Australian Writers' Guild and Australia's eight State and Territory Screen Agencies, is today launching 'The Next Brilliant Career'.


The initiative invites emerging Australian writers who identify as women or non-binary to submit original, authentically-Australian scripted concepts for the chance to receive invaluable feedback directly from Netflix in developing their story for screen.


Following application and eligibility check via the state Screen Agencies dedicated online portals, an independent Australian Writers' Guild panel will shortlist applications. One finalist from each state and territory will then be invited to pitch their concept live at Netflix ANZ headquarters in Sydney in October, and will receive direct feedback from Netflix in developing their story for screen.


Eligibility


To be eligible, applicants must:


-


Be emerging writers, defined as holding 1-3 reportable writer credits on a commercially released short film, film or television series productions longer than 10 minutes, that has screened at a recognised film festival and/or been supported by a state or federal screen agency


-


Not be a full-time student


-


Be an Australian resident


-


Be aged 18 or over


-


Identify as a woman or non-binary


-


Be available to attend a pitch day in Sydney on 6 October 2026


Submissions


Applications open today 4 August 2026 and close 18 August 2026 23.59.


Submissions must include scripted stories that are authentically Australian, and include a brief professional biography, working title, logline, intended audience description, genre, an explanation of the work's cultural relevance and personal connection, a one-page synopsis and a five-page writing sample.


Writers retain full ownership of their original work throughout the Program. Participation does not transfer any rights in a submitted story to Netflix, the Australian Writers' Guild or any Screen Agency. Selection as a Program participant does not guarantee that Netflix will develop a participant's story, or provide funding or ongoing support beyond the Program.

The judging process


Applications will be assessed on the strength and distinctiveness of the writer's voice, the clarity and compelling nature of the central premise, and whether the narrative demonstrates potential to sustain a series or feature format.


Judges will look for a strong command of genre, an awareness of the current screen marketplace, and work that is demonstrably and authentically Australian.

The Next Brilliant Career


is a development program from Netflix, launched alongside its series


My Brilliant Career

It's designed for emerging Australian writers who identify as women or non-binary, giving eight participants the opportunity to pitch an original story idea directly to Netflix and receive structured feedback.


Who can apply?


To be eligible, you must: (i) be 18 years of age or older at the time of application; (ii) be an Australian resident; (iii) identify as a woman or non-binary; and (iv) hold at least one, and no more than three, reportable writer credits on a commercially released short film, film or television series that has screened at a recognised film festival and/or been supported by a state or federal screen agency. Full-time students are not eligible.


What counts as a “reportable writer credit”?


A writer credit on a commercially released short film, film or television series longer than 10 minutes that has either screened at a recognised film festival, or been supported by a state or federal screen agency. You need between one and three such credits to be eligible: no credits, or more than three, won't meet the criterion.


For South Australia only, please note that a “recognised film festival” is defined in the South Australia Film Corporation’s


[Terms of Trade](https://www.safilm.com.au/download/9281) (see p. 11).

Are there word or page limits I need to stick to?


Yes. Your biography should be no more than 150 words, your logline no more than 30 words, and your audience description no more than 200 words. Your synopsis should be one page, and your screenwriting sample five pages in total. These limits help keep the assessment process fair and consistent across every application.


What is the assessment process?


Your relevant screen agency first checks your application against the program's eligibility requirements. Eligible applications are then reviewed by an independent assessor panel appointed by the AWG, which shortlists up to five applications per state or territory. Netflix reviews each shortlist and selects one participant per state or territory, eight in total, for the program.


What is the panel looking for?


The Assessor Panel and Netflix assess applications against a consistent set of criteria: (i) a clear and distinct writing voice; (ii) a compelling central premise; (iii) potential for the story to sustain a series or feature format; (iv) understanding of the chosen genre; (v) an authentically Australian sensibility in tone, character or setting; (vi) a clearly identified intended audience; (vii) awareness of where the project sits in the current screen marketplace; and (viii) how the project advances diversity, whether through on-screen representation, themes, narrative or key creatives.


When will I know if I've been selected?


Selected participants will be notified by phone and/or email on 28 September 2026. If you can't be reached by 5:00pm (AEST) on 30 September 2026, or you decide not to proceed, Netflix reserves the right to select another participant in your place. All participants will be confirmed by 2 October 2026.


Will I receive correspondence or feedback if I'm not selected?


Given the expected volume of applications, individual correspondence and/or feedback isn't provided to unsuccessful applicants. If you do not hear from us, you have not progressed in the program.


What happens if I'm selected as a participant?


If you're selected, you'll be contacted by a Netflix representative on 28 September 2026 who will provide further details of the Pitch Day and help to coordinate the transport/accommodation logistics depending on where you live. In advance of the Pitch Day, you will also be asked to provide a high-resolution headshot. Netflix will announce all eight participants nationally and regionally. By submitting your application, you consent to Netflix and its partners publishing your full name and headshot as part of the announcement(s).


What does a Program participant receive?


Participants are invited to a pitch day with Netflix executives in Sydney on 6 October 2026 (Pitch Day), where they'll present their story directly and receive in-room feedback. Depending on where they live, Netflix will arrange return flights or train travel and up to two nights' accommodation. After the Pitch Day, participants can refine their concept and resubmit it by 6 November 2026, and Netflix will provide final written feedback by 1 December 2026. For the avoidance of doubt, being selected doesn't guarantee that Netflix or its partners will develop your story, or provide funding or ongoing support.


What if Netflix is interested in further developing my story?


If Netflix is interested in developing your story, discussions and development will proceed through Netflix's usual channels and processes.
