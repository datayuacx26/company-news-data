# Lee Chang-dong’s ‘Possible Love’ Sets November 6 Netflix Release Ahead of Venice and Toronto Premieres

Entertainment


27 July 2026


Global


South Korea


Link copied to clipboard


Possible Love


has unveiled its date announcement poster, confirming the film’s arrival on Netflix worldwide on November 6. It will also be released in cinemas in Korea on September 23, followed by Australia on October 22, and the US, Canada, UK, Ireland and Japan on October 23rd.


The film follows two married couples — a laid-off worker and his wife, and a documentary filmmaker and her husband — whose paths cross when the filmmaker sets out to film them, bringing both marriages face to face with their different lives and hidden desires.


Making headlines with its official invitations to Competition at the 83rd Venice International Film Festival and the Special Presentations section of the 51st Toronto International Film Festival,


Possible Love


will first meet audiences in theaters. After its theatrical run,


Possible Love


will be available globally on Netflix for audiences worldwide to enjoy the film.


The date announcement poster features Mi-ok (Jeon Do-yeon) and Ye-ji (Cho Yeo-jeong), standing apart beneath moonlit trees at dawn, creating an unsettling atmosphere that hints at the reckoning both couples are about to face


.


Director Lee Chang-dong’s first film in eight years,


Possible Love


starsJeon Do-yeon, Sul Kyung-gu, Zo In-sung, and Cho Yeo-jeong, marking one of the biggest ensembles of powerhouse Korean talent to appear together in a single film.


Possible Love


arrives globally on Netflix on November 6.
