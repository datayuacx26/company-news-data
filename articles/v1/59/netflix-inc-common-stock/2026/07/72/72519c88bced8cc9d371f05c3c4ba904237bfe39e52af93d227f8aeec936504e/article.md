# Netflix Is Your Ultimate Summer Sidekick

###


The Ultimatum


Co-Host Vanessa Lachey Shares New Series, Films, Games, and More Ways to Be Entertained Whether Chilling or Venturing Out


This summer, Netflix is all about helping you escape into your next favorite story — whether you’re on a staycation, a plane, a road trip, or relaxing at the beach. From big new shows and movies to kid‑friendly games, Netflix is the perfect sidekick for summer downtime, travel days and family nights in.


To help you map out your seasonal escape, Vanessa Lachey, co-host of Netflix’s


Love is Blind


and the upcoming fourth season of


The Ultimatum


(streaming July 15), spotlighted a robust lineup of TV shows, movies, games and experiences designed to match every summer vibe.


'Love Is Blind' and 'The Ultimatum' co-host Vanessa Lachey.

For the Gamer


We’re turning living rooms and beach towels into interactive play spaces. Members can turn their living room into a World Cup arena with Netflix’s


[brand-new FIFA game](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/fifa-mens-world-cup-2026-game-on-netflix) to score the winning goal, or let the kids explore alongside their favorite characters with


[Netflix Playground](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/playground-kids-game-hub-news) . For backyard hangs, fire up


[Netflix Party Games](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/netflix-party-games-play-on-tv) like


Boggle Party


,


Pictionary


, and a chaotic special edition of


Overcooked


featuring characters from


KPop Demon Hunters


and


Stranger Things


.


For the Reader


Prefer a beach read? Head over to our “


[Watch Your Favorite Books](https://www.netflix.com/browse/genre/82819173) ” hub to discover riveting adaptations like


Ransom Canyon


and


Remarkably Bright Creatures


. Or go analog with page-turners like


East of Eden


and


The Whisper Man


before they come to life on screen later this year. It’s the perfect way to move seamlessly from your summer library to ours.


For the Traveler


Don't let spotty Wi-Fi or a loud beach ruin your next cliffhanger. Stream smarter with these quick travel shortcuts:


-


Save entire story arcs before takeoff with


[downloads](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/54816) ,


[smart downloads](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/122916) , or the ‘


[Season Download](https://about.netflix.com/en/news/introducing-the-season-download-button) ’ button.


-


Catch every line in crowded areas using


[dialogue-only subtitles](https://about.netflix.com/en/news/introducing-a-new-way-to-experience-subtitles) .


-


Tap ‘


[Remind Me](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/101522#:~:text=On%20Netflix%20you%20may%20see,also%20appear%20in%20My%20List.) ’ for instant notifications when new titles launch.


-


Clip and text your favorite scenes directly to the group chat with ‘


[Moments on Mobile.](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/210664027435620) ’


-


Claim total peace of mind on long trips with kids' profiles and robust


[parental controls](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/guide-to-netflix-parental-controls) .


For the Adventurous


Beyond the screen, Netflix is offering fans real‑world ways to immerse yourself in your favorite stories. Choose your own adventure at


[Netflix House](https://www.netflix.com/house) in Dallas or Philadelphia, or catch


[Stranger Things: The First Shadow](https://broadway.strangerthingsonstage.com/) on Broadway. You can even bring the action home with


[LEGO ONE PIECE sets](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/one-piece-season-2-lego-sets) , as well as


[KPop Demon Hunters singing dolls and NERF swords](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/kpop-demon-hunters-products-guide) , and


Stranger Things: Tales From ‘85


action figures — creating even more ways to experience Netflix stories throughout the summer.


From new seasons and must‑watch movies to games and live experiences, Netflix has countless ways to make this summer more entertaining, wherever you are.
