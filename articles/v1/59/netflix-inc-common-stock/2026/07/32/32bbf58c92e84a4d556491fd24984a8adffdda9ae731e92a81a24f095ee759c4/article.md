# What We Watched the First Half of 2026

Today, we’re sharing what our members watched on Netflix from January to June 2026.


Engagement for Netflix is about winning the most valuable moments of truth — that moment when people pick up their remote or phone, decide to press play on something they’ve discovered on Netflix and stay to watch.


We measure engagement not only by the


quantity


of hours watched, but also the


quality


of that experience for our audiences. To be successful, we also need to have a


variety


of programming that appeals to different tastes and moods.


Since 2021, we’ve made it easier for audiences to see the viewing numbers behind what’s popular on Netflix, starting with our


[weekly Top 10](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/top10) and


[Most Popular Lists](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/top10/most-popular/tv) . We built on these efforts with our


What We Watched


reports to better capture the quantity of all hours viewed on Netflix over a six-month period. Starting in Q1 2027, we will move from a six-month to a yearly snapshot.


For this report, our audiences watched more than


97B hours


on Netflix — our highest view hours to date for a half — across a variety of genres and languages. Here’s what we found:


Our audiences love to discover and watch new series


-


Five of the top 10 most-watched shows premiered in the first half of this year, with


[I Will Find You](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/harlan-coben-i-will-find-you)


(64M) and


[Teach You a Lesson](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/teach-you-a-lesson-release-date-news)


(48M)


from South Korea both reaching the top 10 less than a month after their releases.


-


We saw big audiences for new stories such as


[His & Hers](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/his-and-hers)


(104M)


—


the #1 most watched show in the half —


along with


[Run Away](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/run-away-harlan-coben-plot-cast-release-date-news)


(50M) and


[Nemesis](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/nemesis)


Season 1 (33M), which was recently renewed for a second season.


New seasons consistently drive discovery and viewing of past seasons of a series


-


The return of


[Bridgerton](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/bridgerton)


Season 4 (100M) nearly tripled viewing of every earlier season of the beloved franchise (including


[Queen Charlotte: A Bridgerton Story](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/queen-charlotte) ), when compared to the second half of 2025. Together, the entire


Bridgerton


franchise brought 180M views in the first half of this year.


-


We also saw a boost in viewership for past seasons of series including


[ONE PIECE](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/one-piece)


(69M views across seasons),


[The Night Agent](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/the-night-agent)


(63M views across seasons),


[Virgin River](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/virgin-river)


(54M views across seasons) and


[The Lincoln Lawyer](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/the-lincoln-lawyer)


(59M views across seasons)


.


Netflix original films continue to draw in huge audiences across genres


-


Our action film slate performed particularly well this half, with


[The Rip](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/the-rip)


(136M)


,


[Apex](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/apex)


(129M) and


[Thrash](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/thrash-release-date-cast-news) (100M) among our most-watched.


[War Machine](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/war-machine-release-date)


was our biggest title in the half with 147M views.


-


Animated films are becoming hits.


[Swapped](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/swapped-cast-release-date)


came in at 131M views in the half and is on track to become our second most-watched original animated film ever, behind


[KPop Demon Hunters](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/kpop-demon-hunters)


(130M) — which came in at #4 despite premiering over a year ago.


-


We’re seeing real fandom for our book-to-screen adaptations, with successful films including


[People We Meet on Vacation](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/people-we-meet-on-vacation)


(78M) and


[Remarkably Bright Creatures](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/remarkably-bright-creatures) (51M).


-


Rom-coms like


[Office Romance](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/office-romance)


(58M) and


[Voicemails for Isabelle](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/voicemails-for-isabelle)


(53M) also moved audiences.
