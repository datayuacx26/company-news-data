# Production Begins on 'Queenpin'

Entertainment


15 July 2026


Global


Spain

Netflix has begun production on


Queenpin


, its new series created by


Daniel Écija


(


Paco’s Men, Locked Up


) and starring


Elena Rivera


(


Alba, Heirs to the Land


),


Maxi Iglesias


(


Valeria


,


Velvet


),


Laura Weissmahr


(


Se tiene que morir mucha gente, Salve Maria


) and


Lola Rodríguez


(


My Dearest Señorita, Welcome to Eden


).


Queenpin


is based on the novel of the same name by Ulises Bértolo and is freely inspired by the true story of the drug trafficker responsible for the largest cocaine seizure in Spain in the 1990s.


The series is a


Good Mood Productions Mediawan


. production for Netflix and will consist of eight episodes. Created by


Daniel Écija


, who also co-wrote the screenplay with


Pablo Fajardo


,


Andrés Martín Soto


(as script coordinators)


,


Ignacio Campón


, and


Olga Salvador


, it will be directed by


Esteban Crespo


and


Oskar Santos


.


Daniel Écija


will serve as executive producer alongside


Andrés Arriaga


,


Javier Lorenzo


, and


Ana Gómez


as co-executive producers, and


Daniel Aranyó


will serve as director of photography. Filming will take place in Madrid, Galicia, and Porto (Portugal), among other locations. The cast also includes Myriam Gallego and Óscar de la Fuente, among others.


About


Queenpin


Queenpin


tells the incredible story of Diana García, a young woman who flees to Madrid to escape a terrible past and who, together with her friends Penélope and Maricarmen, ends up leading one of the most ruthless drug cartels in 1990s Spain. In a world ruled by men, Diana prevails where they fall: she negotiates, she leads, and she survives. But her power begins to waver when Detective Medina — the only one capable of seeing the woman behind the criminal legend — crosses her path. The relationship between Diana and Medina, as intense as it is impossible, will put their loyalties in check: as the police and the cartel tighten the noose, both must choose between justice, revenge… or a love that could doom them both.
