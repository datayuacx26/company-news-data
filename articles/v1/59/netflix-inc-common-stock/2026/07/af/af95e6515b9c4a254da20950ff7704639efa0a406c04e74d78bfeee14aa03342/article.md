# Made in Brazil: ‘Brazil ‘70: The Third Star’ Scores for the Local Economy

World Cup may be in its final days, but


[Brazil ‘70: The Third Star](https://about.netflix.com/en/news/limited-series-about-the-greatest-soccer-team-of-all-time-netflix-releases-the-trailer-and-poster-for-brazil-70-the-third-star) has left a lasting impact.


The limited series, which debuted May 29 on Netflix, follows the 1970 Brazilian national team — and famed players Pelé, João Saldanha, and Zagallo — as they attempt to become the first three-time world champions.


Brazil ‘70: The Third Star


not only celebrates Brazilian culture — it’s also boosted the Brazilian economy, generating approximately


R$100 million (Brazilian reais) in economic impact


1 . In total,


3,000 people


and


1,200 local vendors


worked on the production, which spanned cities in Brazil like São Paulo, Carapicuíba and Cotia, as well as Mexico’s Guanajuato and the country’s capital.


To ensure historical accuracy, production also tapped into local vendors like


[Athleta](https://athletabrasil.com/) , which created the original jersey for the Brazil national team. The family-owned company recreated the 1970 uniform specifically for the series.


The five-episode sports drama hails from Brazilian directors Paulo Morelli (who also served as showrunner), Pedro Morelli and Quico Meirelles, and stars local talent like Rodrigo Santoro (


The Son of a Thousand Men, Good Morning, Verônica, 7 Prisoners


), Bruno Mazzeo (


Rulers of Fortune


) and Lucas Agrícola in his acting debut.
