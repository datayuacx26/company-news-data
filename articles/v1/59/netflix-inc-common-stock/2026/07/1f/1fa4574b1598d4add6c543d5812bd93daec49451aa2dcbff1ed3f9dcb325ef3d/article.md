# Netflix Awards Catalog: Complete List of Academy Award®, Emmy®, Golden Globe® and BAFTA Wins and Nominations

We’re incredibly proud of the Netflix films, series and specials that have received awards over the years. They are testaments to the immensely talented artists in front of and behind the camera who bring their very best work to Netflix, making us a global destination for exceptional storytelling.


On July 8, 2026, Netflix received the following 111 Primetime Emmy® Award nominations across 34 new and returning titles and 12 different program categories. Across our nominations,


BEEF


Season 2 led with 16 nominations, followed by


The Beast in Me


with 9 nominations.


Rafa


also became the first non-English title to be nominated for Outstanding Documentary Series in Emmy history.
