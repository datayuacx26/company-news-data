# Netflix Releases the Trailer for the Spin-Off 'Nando Between Two Worlds - A Sintonia Film,' Premiering August 12

São Paulo, July 20, 2026 —


Hey, fam! Netflix releases the official trailer for


Nando Between Two Worlds - A Sintonia Film


, a feature premiering


August 12


and marking the end of the journey for one of the franchise’s most iconic characters. The trailer gives a peek at a story filled with tension, action and emotion, and highlights the return of


Christian Malheiros


as Nando and


Júlia Yamaguchi


as Scheyla, who has been part of Nando’s journey from the start and now finds herself at the center of the family conflicts driving this new film. The trailer also features


Duda Pimenta


as the teenage version of Bruninha, the couple’s daughter, and introduces Moicano, a new character played by


Henrique Santana


and a key figure in this new chapter of the story. The film takes place five years after Nando’s rise to power in the criminal underworld. Living in Serbia, far from everything he built, he sees his life change drastically when his family finds themselves in danger and he has to return to Brazil. Back home, Nando tries to rebuild his bonds with Scheyla and his children while continuing to run the business from afar alongside Moicano, his partner who remained in Serbia. But keeping these two worlds separate becomes increasingly difficult, and danger soon closes in on the people he loves most. Torn between family life and his obligations to the criminal underworld, Nando must make an impossible choice — who deserves his total loyalty? Produced by Gullane,


Nando Between Two Worlds - A Sintonia Film


features special guest appearances by


Bruna Mascarenhas


and


Jottapê


, who return as Rita and Doni in this farewell to the world of


Sintonia


. The feature expands the universe of the hit series, with one final original story that brings a long-awaited end to Nando’s journey, with a moving family drama about choices and consequences. Based on an original idea by KondZilla and developed in partnership with Felipe Braga and Guilherme Quintella, the film brings Nando’s journey to a close in a story driven by choices, conflict and the desire to protect the people he loves most. Directed by Johnny Araújo, the feature is written by Guilherme Quintella, Denis Nielsen and Léo Todeschini. The film is produced by Caio Gullane and Fabiano Gullane, with Caio Gullane, Ana Saito, Marina Puech Leão and Cristiane Façanha serving as executive producers, and Johnny Araújo and Viviane Zangrossi as co-executive producers.
