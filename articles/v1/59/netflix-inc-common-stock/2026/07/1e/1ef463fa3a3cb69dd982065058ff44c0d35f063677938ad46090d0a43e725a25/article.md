# Netflix Releases Trailer and Premiere Date for 'Elize: Shadows of a Woman,' Starring Lorena Comparato

São Paulo, July 8, 2026 –


A well-known case seen through a new fictional lens. Today, Netflix released the trailer for


Elize: Shadows of a Woman


, premiering on July 22


.


Starring Lorena Comparato, the feature film from acclaimed author and screenwriter Raphael Montes (


Good Morning, Verônica


) in collaboration with Mariana Torres (


Good Morning, Verônica


Season 3), is inspired by the real-life case of Elize Matsunaga, who murdered and dismembered her husband in the couple’s apartment, shocking all of Brazil. In a way that only fiction can, this dramatic psychological thriller, directed by Felipe Vellas (


Criminal Code


) explores Elize’s story, the inner workings of a turbulent relationship and the choices that led to a horrifying crime. The cast also includes Henrique Kimura, Miwa Yanagizawa, Julia Shimura and Denise Weinberg. “This is not just a movie about a crime. The aim was to tell a story that would also make people think. We wanted to open up a conversation about violence — what led Elize to commit such a brutal act?” said Montes. “We wanted to show this woman’s duality. It's a story people can relate to in many ways, but unfortunately with the worst possible outcome,” adds Torres. Produced by Boutique Filmes, the production company behind the true crime documentary series


Elize Matsunaga: Once Upon a Crime


, also available on Netflix, the film is produced by Gustavo Mello, with executive production by Renata Artigas, Marcelo Máximo, and Adriana Gaspar, and also features Raphael Montes as an associate producer.

About Boutique Filmes


For fourteen years, the company has been dedicated to telling impactful Brazilian stories. With a strong presence in the international market, it is home to top independent production talent and a partner of the most important players in the industry, such as Netflix, Globoplay, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+ and Max. Boutique Filmes has received three International Emmy Award nominations and was one of the pioneers of streaming production in Brazil, with the international hit 3%. In 2021, it produced Netflix’s first Brazilian true crime series, Elize Matsunaga: Once Upon a Crime. The company also produced the fiction series Summer Heat (Temporada de Verão, Netflix) and Rota 66 (Globoplay). Currently, it has been investing in major projects such as Véspera (Max), Mila in the Multiverse (Disney+), Negociador (Prime Video), Sutura (Prime Video), Passinho – The Rhythm of Dreams (Disney+), the feature films The Genius of Crime (O Gênio do Crime), Everything Is River (Tudo é Rio), The Five-Star Mystery (O Mistério do Cinco Estrelas), Adriano, Emperor, as well as releasing the documentaries PCC: The Secret Power (HBO Max), Flordelis: Question or Worship (Globoplay), The Invisible Victim – The Eliza Samudio Case (Netflix), and Caçadores de Marajás (Globoplay). In 2025, it released its first feature film in theaters, based on the book of the same name, The Genius of Crime.
