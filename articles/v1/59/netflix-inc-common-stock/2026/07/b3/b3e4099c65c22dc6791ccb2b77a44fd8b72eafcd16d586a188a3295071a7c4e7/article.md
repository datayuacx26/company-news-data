# Meet the 13-Year-Old Fan Whose Letter Became a 'RIV4LRIES' Episode

###


A handwritten note from Toulouse, France, to the cast of the Italian pre-teen series turned into the first episode made just for one fan .


Some fan letters get a reply. Hailé's got an entire episode.


The 13-year-old from Toulouse, France, had watched every twist of


RIV4LRIES


— the Italian pre-teen series that turned a school rivalry into must-watch television — more times than she could count. So, when the show wrapped its latest run, she did what generations of devoted viewers have done before her: she picked up a pen. In a handwritten letter addressed to the cast, Hailé poured out what the show meant to her, describing the kind of unfiltered, specific affection that only a true fan can put into words.


What happened next was a twist no one saw coming: The letter made its way to Netflix and the


RIV4LRIES


team, and instead of filing it away as a heartwarming keepsake, they brought it to the show's writers. Together, they turned Hailé's words into the foundation of an official


RIV4LRIES


extra episode, available on YouTube and TikTok, set in her hometown.


Hailé didn't just inspire the episode. She's in it. Production wove her cameo directly into the story.


Then came the big surprise. Cast members Kartika Malavasi, Samuele Carrino, Joseph Figueroa and Duccio Orlando traveled to Toulouse and showed up, unannounced, at Hailé's front door. The moment — the door opening, the recognition landing, the realization that the actors she'd written to were suddenly standing in her home — was captured on camera and became the emotional centerpiece of an immersive vlog available on YouTube, chronicling the entire journey, from the letter's arrival to her day of filming.


It's a story that crosses borders just like the show itself.


RIV4LRIES


is an Italian production, and Hailé is a French fan — proof that a good story doesn't stop at a subtitle. The cast's trip to Toulouse became a small act of cultural exchange in itself, with an Italian set soon arriving in southern France to bring one viewer's letter full circle.


The experience left a mark beyond Hailé herself. Afterward, her mother wrote to the Netflix team to thank them for the experience, describing how the project had gently pushed her daughter outside her comfort zone and left her with, in her words,


“a memory she'd carry for a long time.”


For a show built on rivalry, the making of this episode told a different story: about what happens when a fan's voice doesn't just get heard, but gets written into the show she loves. Hailé went from watching


RIV4LRIES


to being part of it — letter, cameo, and all.
