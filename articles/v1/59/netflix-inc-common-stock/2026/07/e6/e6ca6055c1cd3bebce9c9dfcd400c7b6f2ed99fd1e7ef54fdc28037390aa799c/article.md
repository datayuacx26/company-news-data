Netflix


has today announced the release date, the first images and the teaser poster for


A Day Like no Other


, the biographical series based on the life of the renowned Spanish singer Raphael, which will premiere exclusively on Netflix in


Spain


and


Latin America


on


9 October


.

,


El jardinero

1992

Francesco Carril


(


Los años nuevos

A Day Like no Other


is an eight-episode biographical series that recounts the life of Raphael, from his humble beginnings in post-war Spain to his conquest of the world’s greatest stages. The series portrays the unstoppable rise of a one-of-a-kind artist: his most intimate passions, his greatest hits, his personal struggles and the demons that have haunted him throughout his life. Set against backdrops ranging from Madrid to Las Vegas, via Moscow, Paris and Acapulco,


A Day Like no Other


is an emotional journey through five decades full of highs and lows. A moving story that reveals the man behind the legend.

About Caracol Televisión


Caracol Televisión is Colombia’s leading television channel for Spanish-language content, having produced several of the industry’s most successful and innovative programmes and formats over the last 20 years. With over 1,900 hours of content produced annually and extensive international sales experience spanning more than 180 countries across five continents, Caracol Televisión is a global pioneer. Today, it is a veritable content production powerhouse, having expanded its influence worldwide through its international business division. With offices in Miami, Madrid and Bogotá, Caracol Internacional distributes the telenovelas, series and entertainment formats created by Caracol TV, which have transcended all cultural and linguistic barriers to become globally recognised products.
