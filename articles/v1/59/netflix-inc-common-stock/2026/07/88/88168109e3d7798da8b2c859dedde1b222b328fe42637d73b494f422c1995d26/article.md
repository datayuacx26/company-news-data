# The Pods of 'Love is Blind: Poland' Will Open Once Again — Netflix Confirms the Second Season of the Hit Show

Love is Blind: Poland


is coming back! The first season of the show won viewers’ hearts and had everyone talking for weeks. Netflix has announced that the pods will once again open for single women and men ready to follow their hearts in search of love. Casting for the new season is already underway.


The first Polish edition of Netflix’s global hit invited participants – and viewers – into a love experiment in which first impressions give way to conversation, emotions, and the question: can love be blind? The stories of the couples who took part in the show and opened their hearts to one another sparked huge emotions. From the day of its premiere,


Love is Blind: Poland


remained in the Top 10 most-watched titles on Netflix in Poland for six consecutive weeks. Today, Netflix has officially confirmed that fans will get a second season.


“Since its premiere, L


ove is Blind: Poland


has stayed at the top of the most-watched titles on the service. Viewers across the country experienced this experiment together with the participants – their tears, doubts, and decisions. Such incredible engagement from our audience makes us want to keep delivering high-quality stories and genuine emotions. The second season will be another chance to see whether, in today’s world, love can start from just a conversation and truly be blind,” says Ola Nadzieja, manager responsible for the development of entertainment formats and non-fiction productions at Netflix.


Casting for the second season of


Love is Blind: Poland


is now open.

ABOUT


LOVE IS BLIND: POLAND


Love is Blind: Poland


is the Polish version of Netflix’s global hit, built around a love experiment that challenges modern dating standards. Its participants have the chance to find a soulmate who loves them for who they are, not what they look like. They meet in so-called “pods” – rooms separated by a thin wall – where they talk and get to know each other better every day. When the emotional bond they build this way turns into deep feelings, they can decide to get engaged. Only then do they see each other for the first time. Next, the couples put their relationship to the test in everyday life – they move in together, meet each other’s loved ones, and plan their wedding. It is at the altar that they decide whether to say “I do” and prove that love can be blind, or walk away from each other forever.
