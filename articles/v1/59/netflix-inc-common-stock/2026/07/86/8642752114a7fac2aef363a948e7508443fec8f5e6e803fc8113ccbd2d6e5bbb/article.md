# Prepare Your Hearts! Miguel Lallo and Diego Lira Meet With the Cast of 'Heartstopper' in London to Announce the Premiere of 'Here the Whole Time' on Netflix

São Paulo, July 14, 2026 —


Netflix heard the fans loud and clear!


Here the Whole Time


, an adaptation of Vitor Martins's bestseller starring


Miguel Lallo


and


Diego Lira,


will be available for streaming worldwide on


August 19


. To celebrate the news, Netflix brought the stars of the movie to London for a get-together with Kit Connor and Joe Locke, stars of the feature


Heartstopper Forever


, which premieres on July 17 and marks the end of the hit franchise inspired by Alice Oseman's graphic novel.


(Photo Credit: Netflix)


Based on books that have captivated readers around the world,


Here the Whole Time


– which fans lovingly call the “


Brazilian Heartstopper” —


and the UK show share themes such as youth, LGBTQIA+ identity, belonging and the discoveries of first love. The actors' meeting highlighted the connection between the stories and celebrated the new Netflix releases.


After moving audiences in theaters,


Here the Whole Time


will be available to watch, rewatch and binge on Netflix whenever you want, as many times as you like, starting August 19.

About


Here the Whole Time


Synopsis:


Felipe (Miguel Lallo), a sensitive teenager with a passion for pop culture, just wants to survive the summer break — staying away from the school bullies, binge-watching his favorite shows and forgetting about the outside world. But his plans go awry when his self-assured neighbor Caio (Diego Lira), the popular guy at school and his old crush, moves in under the same roof for two weeks.


About


Heartstopper Forever


Synopsis:


Nick (Kit Connor) and Charlie (Joe Locke) are inseparable, but with Nick about to leave for university and Charlie discovering a new independence at school, the reality of a long-distance relationship starts to weigh on them. Doubts begin to set in, and their relationship faces its biggest challenge yet. Meanwhile, the couple's friends are also navigating the ups and downs of love and friendship, facing the pains and joys of growing up and forging their own paths. Can first love really last forever?


About Conspiração


Conspiração is one of Brazil’s leading independent production companies, founded by filmmakers and built around Brazilian artistic creation. With 10 International Emmy nominations – the most for any independent production company in Latin America – Conspiração co-produced


I'm Still Here


, winner of the Academy Award® for Best International Feature Film and the Golden Globe® for Best Actress – Drama. The production company is also behind five of the biggest box office hits in Brazilian cinema. Conspiração's projects have premiered at major international festivals, including Cannes, Berlin, Sundance, Venice, and Toronto. With more than 50 feature films, 30 series, and 45 documentaries produced, totaling more than 30 million tickets sold at the box office and over 300 awards, the company combines a strong artistic vision with projects that reach both Brazilian audiences and the international market, rooted in the strength of Brazilian storytelling.


About Manequim Filmes


Manequim Filmes, Vitrine group's commercial distribution label, was created to bring the best of Brazilian cinema to wider audiences. In just three years, Manequim has already established itself in the audiovisual market with Brazil’s highest-grossing national release of 2023:


Our Dream – The Story of Claudinho and Buchecha


. In 2026, it released


The King of the Internet


, the story of Brazilian hacker Daniel Nascimento and


Here the Whole Time

,

The Trial
