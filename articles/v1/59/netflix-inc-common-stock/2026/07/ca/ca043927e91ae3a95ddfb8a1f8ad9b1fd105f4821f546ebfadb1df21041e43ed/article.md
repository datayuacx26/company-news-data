# 'I Will Find You’ Tracks Down Most Popular List as Book Sales Soar

Audiences are having no trouble tracking down Harlan Coben’s new mystery series on Netflix.


[I Will Find You](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/harlan-coben-i-will-find-you) , the latest adaptation from the best-selling author’s library, has officially entered our


[Most Popular Shows of All Time list](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/top10/most-popular/tv) at #10. Since its June 18 premiere, the limited series has racked up


101.8M views


and counting.


I Will Find You


marks our


[biggest scripted series debut of the year](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/top-10-june-15-2026) and has hit #1 in


81 countries


. The show spent five weeks at #1 on the


[Global Top 10 English TV list](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/top10/tv) , the first series to do so since


Wednesday


Season 2 in September 2025.


And I


Will Find You’


s popularity extends far beyond the screen. Even before the series launched on Netflix,


[anticipation for the adaptation](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/harlan-coben-i-will-find-you-series-adaptation) sparked a surge in popularity for the source material three years after its publication. "Since the series announcement and teaser trailer drop on June 1, sales of


I Will Find You


have increased over 500%,” said Beth de Guzman, SVP Deputy Publisher, Grand Central Publishing.


I Will Find You


is just the latest chapter in our long-standing creative partnership with Coben, dating back to 2018. The Harlan Coben Universe — or the HCU as we like to call it — spans


[13 adaptations in 6 countries](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/harlan-coben-shows) , including:


-


The UK:


Fool Me Once,


[Missing You](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/missing-you-harlan-coben-release-date-news) , Run Away, Safe,


[Stay Close](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/stay-close) , The Stranger


-


France:


Gone for Good


-


Poland:


[Hold Tight](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/hold-tight-netflix-cast) , The Woods


-


Argentina:


[Caught](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/caught-release-date-news)


-


Spain:


The Innocent


-


The US:


I Will Find You


Since the debut of the Netflix Top 10 in 2021, our Coben adaptations have made Netflix’s weekly Global Top 10 list


41 times


and reached the Top 10 in


93 countries.


Since 2023, Coben's shows have amassed over


450M global views


.


I Will Find You


follows the 2024 adaptation of Coben’s


[Fool Me Once](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/fool-me-once-release-date-news) , which also previously appeared on the Most Popular Shows list. Earlier this year, our adaptation of his novel


[Run Away](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/run-away-harlan-coben-plot-cast-release-date-news) spent four weeks on the Global Top 10 English Shows list, reaching the Top 10 in 84 countries and hitting #1 in 18.


Britt Lower and Harlan Coben behind the scenes of ‘I Will Find You.’ (Courtesy of Netflix © 2026)


I Will Find You


is just the latest 2026 release to break into our most popular lists, following the gripping limited series


[His & Hers](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/his-hers-tessa-thompson-netflix-series)


(previously #10 on the Most Popular Shows) and the animated film


[Swapped](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/swapped-cast-release-date) (#10 on the


[Most Popular Movies of All Time](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/top10/most-popular) ).


In addition to


I Will Find You


and


His & Hers


, other series breakouts this year include


[Nemesis](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/nemesis-release-date-cast-news) , which


garnered 30M views, spent five weeks on the Global Top 10 English Shows list and appeared in the Top 10 in 89 countries. And


[Bridgerton Season 4](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/bridgerton-season-4-cast-release-date-news) spent nine weeks in the Global Top 10 English Shows list, reached #1 in 85 countries and drew 100M views in the first 6 months of 2026, nearly tripling the viewership of every earlier entry in the franchise — including


Queen Charlotte: A Bridgerton Story.


I Will Find You


also joins an illustrious list of adaptations on our most popular lists, such as multiple seasons of


[Bridgerton](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/bridgerton) ,


[Leave the World Behind](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/leave-the-world-behind-release-date-photos) and


[The Queen’s Gambit.](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/the-queens-gambit-limited-series) In 2025,


[book adaptations](https://about.netflix.com/en/news/how-netflix-adaptations-are-heating-up-the-top-10-and-bestseller-lists) drove over


9B global views


and represented nearly


20% of total view hours


on Netflix.


And the story’s far from over. We recently renewed our partnership with Coben, which continues with a new UK adaptation of


[The Woods](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/harlan-coben-the-woods-release-date-cast-news) and


[Myron Bolitar](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/myron-bolitar-series-harlan-coben) , based on Coben’s longest-running series.


I Will Find You


— and the entire HCU — is another testament to our ability to not only faithfully adapt novels for the screen, but to turn these stories into culture-defining moments that spark new fandoms, expand worlds and bolster book sales and audiobook streams. (Learn more about our latest adaptations


[here](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/new-book-adaptations) .)
