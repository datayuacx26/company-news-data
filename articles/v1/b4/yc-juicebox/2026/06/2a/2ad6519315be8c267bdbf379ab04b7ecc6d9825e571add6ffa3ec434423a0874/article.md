## **SeekOut is focused on its recruiting service**


This is the most important thing to understand before comparing features.


SeekOut today operates in two areas: Recruit, the search platform recruiters use themselves, and Spot, a service that pairs AI agents with SeekOut's own recruiters to deliver interview-ready candidates to hiring managers in about two weeks. Spot is priced and positioned against recruiting agencies, and when SeekOut's founder-CEO handed the company to a new chief executive in May 2026, coverage reported that Spot is expected to be the major focus going forward, alongside potential recruiting-agency acquisitions.


There is nothing wrong with a service business. But if you're evaluating SeekOut as software, you should know that Recruit is becoming a secondary business. The roadmap, the engineering attention, and the company's stated strategy now point at outcomes delivered by SeekOut's recruiters, not at making your recruiters more capable.


Juicebox is the opposite bet: agents and sourcing are the product. Everything we ship (Agents 2.0, Research Signals, Hiring Manager Workflows, network sourcing) exists to make your team's own sourcing radically better. Your pipeline stays your capability, not a line item you rent back from your vendor's recruiters.


## **What "AI agent" actually means**


**Juicebox Agents 2.0** are autonomous sourcing agents your team directs. You brief an agent like a sourcer: discuss trade-offs, challenge its plan. It then runs continuously, adjusting strategy in real time as results come in and as you redirect it in plain language, writing multi-step outreach in your team's voice, and reporting through a dashboard that runs many searches at once.


**SeekOut's agentic AI** , in its own words, keeps "a human in control, surfacing recommendations you approve." Its fully hands-off option is Spot, where the autonomy is completed by SeekOut's own human recruiters delivering candidates as a service. Between those two poles, there is no sourcing agent your team can brief, redirect mid-search, and scale on its own.


In each demo, ask to see:


- A must-have criterion changed *after* the search has started, and how the agent updates its strategy in response.
- The agent explaining what it searched, what it learned, and what it will try next.
- Several candidate searches running in parallel, managed from one dashboard.
- A search adjusted through conversation, without rebuilding the job description or workflow.


Only Juicebox can do all four.

## **SeekOut hosts stale data**


SeekOut advertises a billion-profile index. Juicebox searches 800M+ profiles across 30+ sources. Raw index size matters less than whether the profile is current and the email connects, and that's where SeekOut's own reviewers keep raising flags. Recent Capterra reviews report that candidates often show at old jobs, that "not every email is accurate," and that international coverage is thin.


The fair test: run both platforms on the same 50 profiles, check the current-role accuracy, and count the email bounces.
