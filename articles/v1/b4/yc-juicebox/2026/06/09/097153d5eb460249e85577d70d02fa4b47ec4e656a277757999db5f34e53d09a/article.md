## **The LinkedIn automation problem**


Pin's LinkedIn documentation is explicit about how its connection works. Step one: "Allow Pin to access cookies." Your LinkedIn cookies are what authenticate your session. With this, any actions Pin takes will be tied to your account.


*Pin’s documentation, screenshot made in July 2026*


[LinkedIn's automated-activity policy](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1340567) prohibits third-party software and browser extensions that automate activity on LinkedIn, and prohibits using automated methods to access it.


If you're evaluating Pin anyway, ask: Is the LinkedIn integration officially authorized by LinkedIn? (LinkedIn publishes its[official hiring integration partners](https://business.linkedin.com/hire/hiring-integrations#recruiter-system-connect-partners) ). Why does setup require granting the extension access to my cookies, and what does Pin do with my session?


Juicebox outreach runs on channels we can operate compliantly and at scale, with deliverability and sender reputation managed for you. Nothing runs inside your LinkedIn account.


## **Only Juicebox’s Agent is autonomous**


Both companies use the word "agent." The products behind the word are not the same thing.


**Juicebox Agents 2.0** are autonomous sourcing agents. You brief an agent like you'd brief a sourcer: discuss trade-offs, refine the profile, challenge its strategy. The agent then works continuously, adjusting its sourcing strategy in real time based on what it finds and what you approve or reject, writing personalized multi-step outreach in your team's voice, and reporting back through a unified dashboard where you can run multiple agents across multiple searches.


‍ **Pin's agent** , based on its public product materials, runs a search against a job description and attaches automated sequences to the results. It's automation, but there is no strategy loop, no conversational recalibration, no agent-level reasoning about *how* to source differently when a search isn't converging.


Don't take our word for it. If you demo Juicebox and Pin, ask to see:


- A must-have criterion changed *after* the search has started, and how the agent updates its strategy in response.
- The agent explaining what it searched, what it learned, and what it will try next.
- Several candidate searches running in parallel, managed from one dashboard.
- A search adjusted through conversation, without rebuilding the job description or workflow.


Only Juicebox can do all four.


## **Juicebox helps you find and close candidates**


In addition to candidate sourcing, Juicebox provides additional features for a full fledged recruiting platform.


- **Network Sourcing.** Juicebox surfaces potential warm intros your team has to a candidate, so outreach can start with an intro instead of a cold message.
- **Hiring Manager Workflows.** Calibrate and review candidates in Juicebox instead of across multiple Slack channels.
- **Research Signals.** Get market compensation, company details, and talent-flow mapping so recruiters are better prepared.
- **A recruiting CRM with granular permissions** , built for teams where multiple recruiters, coordinators, and hiring managers work the same pipeline.


Pin offers none of this layer. It's designed to run one recruiter's funnel (sourcing, sequences, scheduling, inbox), which is a legitimate product, but a different and smaller one.


Juicebox is constantly releasing new features as well. Agents 2.0, Research Signals, and Hiring Manager Workflows all launched in the past year.
