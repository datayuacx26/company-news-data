# A recruiter's guide to Juicebox vs Gem

Juicebox and Gem both serve recruiting teams, but they're built on fundamentally different bets about where recruiting is headed and solve different problems.


Gem began as a sequencing add-on for your ATS and has expanded into many additional products: ATS, CRM, applicant review, scheduling, analytics. Juicebox is, at its heart, a sourcing platform: AI-assisted search and fully agentic sourcing, that plugs into the stack you already run.


Choosing between Gem vs Juicebox depends on what you're actually buying software to fix: more workflow managing existing candidates, or building an outbound recruiting engine for finding candidates you don't have.


## **TL;DR**


- **Gem widened; Juicebox deepened:** Gem started as a sequencing add-on and now spans ATS, CRM, applicant review, scheduling, and analytics. Juicebox is a sourcing platform at its core, offering AI-assisted search plus fully agentic sourcing to own your top of funnel that plugs into your existing stack through 35+ ATS/CRM integrations.
- **Agents that learn vs. list** :
Juicebox Agents take a briefing, adjust strategy mid-search, and respond to feedback through a chat conversation. Gem's AI Sourcing Agent takes a role description and surfaces matches for review, but it doesn’t learn and apply your feedback continuously over time.
- **Features for advanced sourcing:** Juicebox shows what candidates actually earn and where talent is moving, with market comp data and talent-flow mapping in every search, plus network sourcing for warm intros and hiring manager workflows. Gem's depth runs towards optimizing for pipeline ops and candidate management.
- **Sourcing beyond LinkedIn:** Gem's sourcing workflow works around LinkedIn (extension adds, InMail sequencing), so for many Gem teams, discovery still happens in LinkedIn Recruiter. Juicebox does the finding itself: natural-language search across 800M+ profiles from 30+ sources, with best-in-class email and phone contact data.
- **Secure, and shipping:** Juicebox is SOC 2 Type II certified and ISO 42001 ready, with an independent[AI audit center](https://trust.warden-ai.com/juicebox) , backed by $115M+ including an[$80M Series B in March 2026](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260310781820/en/Juicebox-Raises-%2480M-at-%24850M-Valuation-to-Help-Businesses-Reach-Top-Talent-Before-Anyone-Else-Does) . Gem last announced funding in 2021.

## **The all-in-one bet, examined**


Gem started life as a sequencing and CRM layer for your ATS, a system for nurturing candidates you'd already found. From there it expanded outward: its own ATS, applicant review, scheduling, analytics.


Today, they focus on a consolidation product angle - fewer vendors, one data model, a claimed 30-50% saving on tooling. But a platform that grew this wide is only as strong as the module you purchase in your Gem package. The breadth cuts both ways: spreading across sourcing, CRM, ATS, and analytics leaves each piece competing for focus, and none of them fully owning its category.


Most recently, the company introduced a tier that lets customers buy in without adopting Gem's ATS at all, a tacit admission that the anchor module isn't yet the reason people come.[Recent G2 reviewers](https://www.g2.com/products/gem/reviews/gem-review-12234239) describe the ATS side as early-stage relative to its price.


Two practical details matter if you're keeping your ATS:


- On Gem's published pricing, the ATS connector to Greenhouse, Workday, Lever, and others requires **custom pricing** .
- Companies with more than 100+ employees fall outside Gem's listed plans and must negotiate custom pricing to **access the full AI suite** .


Juicebox takes the opposite approach. We connect with 50+ ATS/CRM integrations like Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workday, starting the week you sign up. You keep your existing system of record and upgrade the part of the stack that brings in candidates to the pipeline.


## **What "AI agent" actually means**


Both companies ship AI sourcing agents. The interaction model is where they diverge.


**Juicebox Agents** work like a sourcer you brief through a conversation. You discuss the profile, challenge the strategy, and redirect mid-search in plain language. The agent adjusts in real time (adding new must-have skills or changing critera without having to restart your search), writes multi-step outreach in your team's voice, and reports through a dashboard running across multiple searches at once.


**Gem's AI Sourcing Agent** , based on its public materials, works from your role description: tell it who you're hiring for, and it searches and surfaces the strongest matches for your review. That's useful, but the steering model is brief-and-review. Gem's broader agent lineup (application review, fraud detection) is oriented to inbound pipeline processing, with recruiters approving outputs.


When assessing whether Gem fits, a few capabilities are worth pressure-testing:


- If it can change a criterion *after* the search has started with an AI Agent
- Whether the agent can explain what it searched, what it learned, and what it'll try next
- Running multiple searches in parallel
- Whether the AI features being shown are actually included in the tier you're quoted rather than gated behind a higher or custom plan.


## **Deep features for advanced use cases**


Juicebox surrounds sourcing with an intelligence layer that isn't in Gem's public product line:


- **Comp data and talent-flow mapping:** See what candidates actually earn and where talent is moving (compensation ranges, company context, talent-flow maps) before you write the first message.
- **Network sourcing:** Juicebox surfaces the warm paths your team already has to a candidate, so outreach can start with an intro instead of a cold email.
- **Hiring manager workflows:** Structured calibration and review loops inside the platform. Their feedback produces a summary you can convert into a search or push to an agent in a single click.
- **Research signals:** Identify top research talent from their publication and citation record, making it easy to find deep technical experts and rank them by real scientific impact.


Gem's strengths run a different direction: pipeline operations, rediscovery, scheduling, analytics on your funnel. But when the question is which candidates to pursue and how to reach them - that's the layer Juicebox built.


Our pace backs this up. We’ve shipped a few major capabilities this year: Juicebox Agents, Research Signals, Network Sourcing, Hiring Manager Workflows — all launched within the past year, and with bi-weekly updates on[our blog](https://juicebox.ai/blog/product-updates) .


## **Where your candidates actually come from**


Strip away the modules and ask the only question that fills roles: where does your pipeline originate?


Gem's sourcing workflow grew up around LinkedIn: a Chrome extension for one-click adds from LinkedIn profiles, InMail sequencing, LinkedIn-centric flows. It organizes and nurtures that pipeline well. And for Gem teams that don’t have an AI Seat add-on in their pricing package, the actual *finding* still happens in LinkedIn Recruiter, one search at a time. Gem is where candidates go after you've found them.


Juicebox is built to do the finding. Natural-language search across 800M+ profiles from 30+ sources (GitHub, research publications, the open web, well beyond LinkedIn), with best-in-class phone and email data, and Agents that run entire searches themselves.

## **When Gem might fit**


If your goal is vendor consolidation, meaning you want to replace your ATS and CRM with one system, you're comfortable growing with a newer ATS, and pipeline operations matter more to you than sourcing depth, Gem's all-in-one is a coherent choice, and its rediscovery and analytics are well regarded.


If sourcing is the job (finding people your competitors haven't, reaching them at scale, with comp intelligence and hiring managers in the loop), Juicebox is the better choice for you.


## **Frequently asked questions**

Gem is an all-in-one recruiting suite that began as an ATS sequencing add-on and now spans ATS, CRM, applicant review, scheduling, and analytics. Juicebox is a sourcing platform at its core: natural-language AI search across 800M+ profiles, fully agentic sourcing, comp data and talent-flow mapping, network sourcing, and hiring manager workflows, integrated with 50+ existing ATS/CRMs instead of replacing them.

Yes. Teams on Gem's ATS or CRM run Juicebox as their sourcing and outreach layer, syncing candidates through the integration stack. No. Juicebox spans across a fully automated multi-step outreach, a recruiting CRM with granular permissions, internal talent rediscovery, comp and talent-flow intelligence, network sourcing, and hiring manager workflows.
