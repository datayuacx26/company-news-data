# Introducing Juicebox for Slack

Today, we’re introducing **Juicebox for Slack** , bringing the sourcing power of Juicebox inside your Slack Workspace.


Recruiters and hiring managers can now stay aligned, review candidates, and move searches forward using your context from Slack.


Here's a first look at what you can do:


## **⚡ Start an Agent From a Slack Thread**


*Turn a hiring conversation into a candidate shortlist in minutes*


Most searches start with a conversation about what good looks like. Now, that conversation can become the search itself. **‍**


**Kickoff a Juicebox Agent directly from a Slack thread:**


- **Start with full context:** The Agent pulls in the requirements, preferences, and tradeoffs your team already discussed in the thread
- **Calibrate before it runs:** Review sample profiles and tell the Agent what to prioritize
- **Refine as you go:** Approve or pass with a reason, and your feedback syncs to Juicebox to make each sourcing batch stronger


Go from a hiring conversation to your first candidate leads right where your team already works.


## **🔔 Stay on Top of Every Search**


*Get notifications the moment a candidate replies or leads are ready for review*


Candidate replies and review requests are easy to miss when they live in another tab.


Juicebox now sends those updates straight to your hiring channels, so your team catches every reply and lead the moment they land.


**Add Juicebox to any channel to get updates on, including:**


- New Agent leads ready for review
- Candidate replies
- Sourcing and sequencing activity
- ATS connection changes, and more


## **🤝 Partner with Hiring Managers**


*Get hiring manager feedback right where they already work*


The best recruiting team move fast when recruiters and hiring managers stay in sync. Now feedback doesn't have to wait for the next check-in.


With Juicebox for Slack, your team can:


- **Review Agent leads:** Your Agent notifies the channel when it finds candidates. Approve or pass right in Slack.
- **Hiring manager reviews:** Send candidates for review to hiring managers in Slack, and their feedback syncs back to Juicebox in a summarized view.


Every review syncs back to your Juicebox project, so nothing gets lost in the thread.


## **📣 Tag @Juicebox to** **Take Profile Actions**


*Shortlist candidates, add tags, and pull pipeline stats from anywhere*


Juicebox for Slack doesn't just deliver notifications. When a candidate comes up in conversation, you can take actions that sync to Juicebox right in Slack.


Tag @Juicebox to:


- **Shortlist candidates:** Paste a LinkedIn URL or directly add the profile to a project
- **Add context:** Leave notes and tags for your team
- **Get quick summaries:** View candidate and lead updates on demand


Shortlist, tag, and review from your phone, between meetings, or wherever hiring happens.
