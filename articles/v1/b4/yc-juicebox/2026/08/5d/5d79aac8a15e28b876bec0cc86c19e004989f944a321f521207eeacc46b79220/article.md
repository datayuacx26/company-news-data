# Introducing Juicebox Agent 4.0

Today, we’re launching[Juicebox Agent 4.0](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/david-paffenholz_today-were-launching-juicebox-agent-40-ugcPost-7495902140033376256-geo8/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAB54djcBnYDGtVqKZM6rRo66Z6Rhyy7-vAE) **,** built on our new Context Intelligence Layer.


For the first time, your Agent will understand everything your team already knows, before you ever have to explain it. We believe it's the most advanced sourcing agent for recruiting teams yet.


Here's everything that's new in Agent 4.0:


## **🧠 Context Intelligence Layer**


*Agent 4.0 now sources with full context from across your recruiting stack*


**Agent 4.0 brings context intelligence into Juicebox.**


Using[MCP connectors](https://docs.juicebox.ai/juicebox-agents#context-intelligence) , your Agent connects to the context across your tools, including your ATS, meeting notetakers, productivity apps, and your team's organizational memory in Juicebox.


Before it sources a single profile, the Agent will first:


- Research your context from your connected tools
- Study patterns across your past hires and feedback


Then it builds the search from that context to **help get to your ideal candidates faster** .


Every first search should reflect months of your team's decisions, so you never have to start a new role from scratch again.


## **🗺 Real-Time Market Mapping**


*Juicebox Agents can now test the market before choosing a strategy*


Finding talent is only part of a sourcing workflow.


The harder challenge can be knowing where to look, how wide to go, and when the strategy needs to change.


Starting today, Agent 4.0 can help take on more of that judgment by performing its own market map and:


- **Simulating multiple searches:**[Test different versions of your sourcing strategy](https://docs.juicebox.ai/juicebox-agents#market-mapping) against real-time market data
- **Showing its reasoning:** The Agent will now recommend a strategy, and you can see exactly how your Agent reached its recommendation


Instead of committing to one search and finding out later how it performed, you can see how each version of the search shapes the market before your Juicebox Agent sources anyone.


## **🔗 Connected Workflows**


*Take actions in your favorite apps, right from Juicebox*


With Agent 4.0, **Juicebox becomes the central place** to run your top of funnel sourcing.


Your Agent can pull in context from the tools your team already uses, take action across them, and carry the work from initial sourcing all the way through to a finished deliverable.


For example, you can even ask it to " **Build a talent market map for this role in Notion.** "


You can see[more examples here](https://docs.juicebox.ai/juicebox-agents#chat-stays-with-you-while-sourcing) , but the workflow possibilities are endless!


We can’t wait to see what you build!


## **🗄 The Knowledge Vault**


*The context your team has built, applied to every search*


Juicebox builds context with every search your team runs and team member you hire. Now you can see exactly what the product knows.


[The Knowledge Vault](https://docs.juicebox.ai/knowledge-vault) holds the context your Agents apply searches everyday:


- **Org context:** How your company hires, the bars you set, and the copy you use
- **User context:** Each recruiter's patterns, preferences, and saved criteria
- **Recent hires:** Who you actually hired, the strongest signal for who to find next


To see what Juicebox knows about your company, **head to your account settings and navigate to Knowledge Vault** .


Explore your company memory, connected tools, and the context your Juicebox Agents are already using for sourcing.
