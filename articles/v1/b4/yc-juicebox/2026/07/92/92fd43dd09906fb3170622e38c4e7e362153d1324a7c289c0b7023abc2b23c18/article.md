# Introducing Hiring Manager Workflows in Juicebox

Great recruiting depends on a strong partnership between recruiters and hiring managers.


Today, we’re introducing **Hiring Manager Workflows** , a shared way for recruiters and hiring managers to review candidates, learn from feedback, and refine the search together. *‍*


*Business plan customers get unlimited Hiring Manager Seats. Contact your CSM for more information.* ‍


Here's a look at what's new in Juicebox:


## **Share Shortlists and Collect Feedback**


*Get clear, actionable feedback on every candidate profile*


Starting today, you can request a shortlist review from Hiring Managers directly in Juicebox.


Hiring managers get access to a simple, guided experience where they can collaborate on a search without needing to learn the full product.


Hiring Managers can:


- **Approve, skip, or reject:** Collect a clear decision on every candidate profile in a guided review flow for Hiring Managers
- **Add notes to individual profiles:** Keep specific feedback connected to the candidate instead of buried in a Slack, emails, and more


With this new rollout, you can walk into your next hiring manager conversation with more clarity on where the talent pipeline stands for every role.


## **Get Structured Feedback Summaries**


*Understand the patterns behind which profiles were approved and rejected*


Knowing which candidates a hiring manager liked is helpful, but understanding why is what moves the search forward.


Once a hiring manager finishes a review, Juicebox summarizes the feedback across the shortlist and highlights the patterns across the reviewed profiles.


You can now quickly understand:


- **Overall results:** See how many candidates were approved, rejected, or skipped
- **Which signals matter most:** Identify preferences around seniority, company background, research depth, industry experience, and technical skills
- **Where the search needs to change:** See which criteria to prioritize, broaden, or reconsider in the next round


Instead of spending time interpreting scattered opinions, hiring teams can now start with a shared understanding of what the team is looking for before kicking off their next search.


## **Turn Feedback into a New Search**


*Kick-off a new Search or Agent with hiring manager feedback in one click*


Once you understand the feedback, you can act on it directly in Juicebox.


You can now **launch a new Search or Agent** using the hiring manager’s feedback **in just one-click** .


Juicebox carries the insights from the review into this new search, so you can build on a hiring manager's feedback instead of rebuilding from scratch. Every round of shortlist reviews gives recruiters and hiring managers a clearer picture of the ideal profile to make the next round of sourcing even more targeted.


## **Add Hiring Managers as Agent Collaborators**


*A new way for Hiring Managers to review Agent leads and follow progress*


Outside of the core search shortlist capabilities, you can also add hiring managers as collaborators on an Agent.


Hiring managers can now:


- **Review incoming leads:** Approve, reject, or skip candidates as the Agent finds them
- **Help calibrate the search:** Share feedback that keeps the Agent aligned with what they are looking for
- **View analytics:** Know when there are new candidates waiting for review and follow its progress


Users can add hiring manager collaborators directly from Agent settings.
