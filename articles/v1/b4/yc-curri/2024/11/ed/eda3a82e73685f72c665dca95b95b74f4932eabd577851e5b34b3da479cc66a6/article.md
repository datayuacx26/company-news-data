Corken Steel Products, a leader in distribution of HVAC, Fabrication, and Roofing Products, operates 18 locations across Kentucky, Indiana, and Ohio. With a fleet of 80+ vehicles and a driver count to match, they deliver a range of products, from a small box of filters to truck loads of fabricated metal. Their fleet includes a mix of commercial and non-commercial vehicles such as sprinters vans, box trucks, flatbeds, and moffett trucks. A key competitive advantage for Corken Steel is their customer service and efficient delivery service, which they aim to manage as a value-added service rather than an additional charge for customers.


*Corken "Good Service" Steel Products location in Ohio*


## **Challenges**


Jon Hobbs, Regional Operations Manager, was tasked with increasing fleet utilization across multiple locations and divisions while improving on-time deliveries and minimizing operational inefficiencies.


Historically, Corken Steel relied on a manual, pen-and-paper system to manage 240+ daily deliveries across multiple locations, often resulting in overlapping of routes, missed opportunities for route optimization, and unsatisfactory customer satisfaction.


Jon first heard from Curri at[HARDI’s](https://hardinet.org/) (HVAC association) annual event in December 2023, and the fact that Curri is also an[AD buying group member](https://www.curri.com/ad-buying-group-distributor-members) played in Curri’s favor too.


*Jon Hobbs, Regional Operations Manager at Corken Steel Products*


## **Solution Implementation**


Corken Steel implemented Curri’s[fleet management system](https://www.curri.com/route-planner) , starting with an initial deployment at two locations and 25 vehicles.


The seamless onboarding and ease of use enabled Corken Steel to quickly scale operations with Curri’s platform. Within a month, seven locations and nearly 40 trucks are integrated with Curri's platform, representing approximately 50% of the company's fleet.


The team plans to add 20 more vehicles by summer 2025.


## **Impact and key metrics**


**1. Cost Savings** : Corken Steel anticipates that Curri’s data insights will allow the company to reroute vehicles and balance fleet distribution more effectively. By mid-2025, they expect to reduce fuel costs and operational expenses by relocating vehicles based on delivery trends and customer needs.


**2. Improved On-Time Delivery** : Curri’s digital delivery management has significantly increased Corken Steel’s delivery efficiency. Drivers now have real-time access to delivery expectations and customer contacts, reducing missed or delayed deliveries. This visibility ensures that all parties are aligned on delivery timelines, leading to greater customer satisfaction and fewer phone calls. Corken Steel’s customers particularly appreciate the proof-of-delivery photos as well as the live-tracking feature. These features enable customers to be ready for unloading materials, contributing to a seamless all-around service.


**3. Optimized Route Planning** : The ability to manage routes digitally has reduced manual errors. The system now allows Corken Steel to organize deliveries more effectively, enhancing fleet utilization and optimizing driver assignments across locations.


**4. Faster, Scalable Onboarding** : Curri enabled Corken Steel to onboard 25 vehicles in the first month, and within three months, an additional 15 were brought online without interrupting daily operations. This rapid deployment has allowed Corken Steel to scale quickly, leveraging Curri’s intuitive system and support team.


## ‍ **Conclusion**


[Curri’s fleet management platform](https://www.curri.com/route-planner) has enabled Corken Steel Products to streamline operations, reduce administrative burden, and scale their fleet utilization with minimal disruption. With real-time data insights and robust route management features, Corken Steel is well-positioned to enhance their delivery radius and optimize costs in the near future.
