Curri allowed Hajoca to operate more efficiently, by only investing at their time of need.

Curri implementation had tangible results for Hajoca, including increased customer satisfaction and serviceability. By strategically utilizing Curri, Hajoca penetrated the market effectively, even amid customer emergencies and competitive pressures.
