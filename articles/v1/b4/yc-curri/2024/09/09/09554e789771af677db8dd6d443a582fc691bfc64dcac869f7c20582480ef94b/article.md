# What Is Crowdsourced Delivery?

Crowdsourced delivery has emerged as a possible solution for businesses seeking faster and more flexible shipping options. It brings agility to deliveries, offering same-day and even on-demand services that align with today's fast-paced market.


In this article, we’ll discuss what crowdsourced delivery is, its benefits, and how different industries can use this flexible model to get their goods where they need to go.


## **What does crowdsourcing delivery mean?**


Crowdsourced delivery is a fulfillment method where contractors or independent drivers use their own vehicles to transport products. These drivers act as couriers, responding to delivery requests in real-time through an app or platform.


The model fits various shipping needs—whether it's the last mile (getting products to a customer’s doorstep), the middle mile (moving items between warehouses or distribution centers), or the first mile (from the manufacturer to the warehouse).


For example, a construction company working on multiple job sites across a city often needs a set of power tools delivered quickly. Instead of relying on a limited number of company trucks or dealing with scheduling delays from traditional courier services, the company uses a crowdsourced delivery platform. Independent drivers with trucks or vans pick up materials from suppliers and deliver them directly to the job sites as needed.

## **Advantages of crowdsourced moving for businesses**


Crowdsourced delivery offers several benefits that can help businesses streamline their shipping processes. Here are some of them:


### **Cost efficiency**


Crowdsourced delivery is cost-effective because it eliminates the need for a full-time fleet. Businesses save on expenses like purchasing or leasing, insurance, fuel, and maintenance. Instead, they pay only for the delivery services they use, helping to control costs and improve their bottom line.


It also reduces labor costs, as companies aren’t responsible for hiring, training, or employing drivers—drivers are independent contractors, paid per delivery.


### **Scalability**


Crowdsourced delivery services allow businesses to scale up or down based on their delivery needs. During peak periods like large projects or sudden demand spikes, companies can easily increase the number of drivers without worrying about fleet limitations.


It also means there’s no need to invest in additional vehicles or hire temporary drivers, making it both practical and cost-effective.


### **Flexibility for last-minute needs**


In industries where last-minute changes and urgent requests are common, like construction, having a flexible delivery solution like crowdshipping is essential.


Crowdshipping provides quick access to a network of drivers ready to take on last-minute deliveries, often with same-day or even on-demand services.


### **Expanded delivery coverage**


Crowdsourced delivery networks cover a broad geographical area, often reaching locations that traditional courier services may not serve efficiently.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**


### **How is crowdsourced delivery different from gig economy services like ride-sharing?**


While both rely on independent drivers, crowdsourced delivery focuses on transporting goods rather than passengers. Crowdsourced delivery platforms match deliveries with the right drivers and vehicles based on load size, timing, and distance.


### **How are delivery reliability and timing maintained in a crowdsourced model?**


Delivery reliability is ensured through intelligent matching, innovation, and real-time tracking. Different delivery platforms offer dedicated routes and live tracking so you always know where your deliveries are.


Drivers are vetted and trained to adhere to strict guidelines, and customer support is available around the clock to address any issues.


### **Can crowdsourced delivery services handle large or fragile items?**


Yes, crowdsourced delivery can handle a variety of items, including large, heavy, or fragile materials. With access to a fleet ranging from sedans to semi-trucks, platforms like Curri can match delivery needs with the right vehicle and provide trained drivers to handle specialized loads.


### **How sustainable is crowdsourced delivery compared to traditional delivery services?**


Crowdsourced delivery can be more sustainable by leveraging existing vehicles and local drivers, reducing the need for a dedicated fleet that might run empty or inefficient routes.
