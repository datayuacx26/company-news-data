# June 2025: Preferred Drivers feature is here

We’ve released a new feature that gives you more control over your driver network. This month’s update starts with a simple but powerful shift—putting you in charge of who’s behind the wheel.


## **New feature: Preferred Drivers**


Every great team has a starting lineup—and now, your deliveries can too.


With our new **Preferred Drivers** feature, you can rebook drivers who impressed you most—along with the vehicle they operate. Whether they handled a complex route, arrived ahead of schedule, or simply made your day easier, you can now add them to your personal roster with one click.
