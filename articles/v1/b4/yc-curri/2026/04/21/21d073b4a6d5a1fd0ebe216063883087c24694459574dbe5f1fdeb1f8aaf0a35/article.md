# New features built for faster delivery booking

In Q1 2026, Curri introduced new features designed to help construction and industrial distributors streamline delivery booking, reduce manual entry, and improve visibility across every shipment. Together, these updates simplify booking workflows and help teams move from order to delivery faster with fewer manual steps.


Curri is delivery management software built for construction and industrial distributors to manage deliveries across ****[hotshot](https://www.curri.com/hotshots) ,[LTL freight](https://www.curri.com/ltl) , and[dedicated routes](https://www.curri.com/dedicated) in one platform.

### **1. Smart Import: Automatically build bookings from order documents**


Manual data entry slows shipping teams and increases the risk of errors. With Smart Import in Curri, users can upload an invoice, bill of lading (BOL), or shipping document directly into the platform. This helps automate shipment data entry and reduce errors in delivery booking.


Smart Import automatically extracts shipment details from uploaded shipping documents to build delivery bookings with BOL numbers, addresses, and order information.


### Smart Import extracts:


- Pickup and delivery addresses


- Contact information


- Order and item details


‍[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BdT2yCbGeGs) **how Smart Import converts order documents into delivery bookings.**


### If you are searching for:


- How to reduce manual entry in shipping

- How to import shipment details into a delivery platform


Smart Import is built to solve those challenges.


### **2. Save Drafts: Start a booking now and finish it later**


Not every delivery is ready to be booked immediately. Teams often need to confirm details or coordinate internally before submitting.


The save drafts feature lets users save delivery bookings in progress and continue booking later from the Drafts dashboard.


### With Save Drafts in Curri, users can:


- Save a booking at any stage


- Access saved bookings from the Drafts Dashboard


- Reopen drafts with all information preserved


This prevents rework and supports real-world workflows where bookings aren’t always completed in one session.


### **3. Driver Reviews: Support high standards across every booking**


Reliable delivery requires consistent performance. With Driver Reviews in Curri, users can leave quick feedback directly within the booking flow after a completed delivery.


Driver reviews let users rate delivery drivers after completed deliveries to maintain consistent service quality.


### This supports:


- Recognition of strong performance


- Accountability across deliveries


- High service standards over time


It helps maintain consistent delivery performance across teams and locations, especially for distributors managing multiple branches or high shipment volumes.
