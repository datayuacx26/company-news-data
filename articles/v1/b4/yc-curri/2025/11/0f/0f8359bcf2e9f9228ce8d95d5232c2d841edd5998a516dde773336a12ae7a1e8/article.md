## The features that changed the game for Johnstone


Curri is more than a delivery service. It’s a comprehensive logistics solution. Some of our most notable features include:


- **Live-tracking:** Curri sends you a live tracking link for each delivery booked, so bookers always know the delivery status for any trip. Users can even share the tracking link with relevant stakeholders and opt in to notifications for ultimate visibility.
- **Proof of delivery:** Curri drivers send bookers a proof of delivery photo and signature. No need to wonder if your order got to where it needs to go. The proof is in the photo.
- **Live traffic forecasting:** Heavier traffic due to an unaccounted-for event? Road closures? Road conditions creating congestion? Nothing will be a surprise thanks to Curri’s traffic forecaster. Re-route and re-plan with ease.

## The requirements to drive for Curri


If you’re looking to get started with Curri, here’s what you’ll need after downloading the Curri Driver app:


- You need to be at least 18 years old, with a valid Social Security Number (SSN) or an Employer Identification Number (EIN).
- You’ll also need a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN).
- You need, of course, a valid United States driver’s license.
- You’ll need to prove you have an active auto insurance policy that meets your jurisdiction’s requirements.
- You need a vehicle in good working condition, that’s clean, cleared, and ready for loading.


Each Curri driver also needs to complete a background check. Driver activation and clearance will also depend on your MVR checks. Once complete and accepted, onboarding will be based on the demand for your delivery area.


### Onboard quickly and start getting paid


Once accepted, new Curri drivers will be put on the waiting list until we confirm there’s delivery demand in your area. From there, make sure your Curri Driver App is active, like Jonathan's, so you don’t miss new notifications. In the app, you can document your deliveries with photos, collect customer signatures, and sort your pickup and drop-off locations.


Payments are typically made based on vehicle type and driver mileage. The longer your route is, the more you’ll earn. Drivers can be strategic with their routes, like Jonathan, and accept gigs that work with their schedule to best maximize profit.


And if you’re worried about sticky contracts or being locked into specific terms, fear not. If you want to “quit,” you simply stop accepting gigs. You’re an individual contractor, and while you’re a part of the Curri team, you’re also the one in the driver’s seat. You call the shots.
