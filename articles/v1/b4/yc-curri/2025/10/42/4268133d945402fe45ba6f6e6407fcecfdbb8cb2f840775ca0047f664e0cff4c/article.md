When demand for your product grows through your marketing efforts, it’s a beautiful thing. But if you can’t deliver to the areas you need to? It’s a total nightmare. Here’s what those delivery bumps in the road might look like, and how to solve them with[Curri](https://www.curri.com/) .


## The problem: Delivery was more of a challenge than you anticipated


Thirsty people + great beverage product = a recipe for business success, right?


Well, not so fast. There are a number of challenges that beverage businesses face when it comes to delivering the product to the consumer. Some of those hurdles include:


- Needing quick delivery for drinks with a short shelf life
- Parcel carriers like UPS and FedEx restricting the delivery of liquids
- Freight carriers with LTL services deny delivery of beverages due to the leak risk
- Heavy beverage truckloads mean high shipping costs, eating away at your margins
- No tracking or no real-time tracking


And if you’re a new or growing brand, especially, you might not have a grasp on where your demand will spike. One region might be jonesing for your product, or you could see a boost in demand across the country. Then, you have to worry about overstocking one warehouse while others run low.


Consumers themselves have begun to expect fast, cheap shipping (like Amazon), but those are big shoes to fill. This creates a margin versus customer satisfaction dilemma.
