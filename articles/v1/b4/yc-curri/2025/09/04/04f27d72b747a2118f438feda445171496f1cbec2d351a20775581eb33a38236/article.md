# How Standard Electric Supply uses Curri to cover delivery logistics gaps and cut costs

At[Standard Electric Supply Co.](https://www.standardelectricsupply.com/) , providing exceptional customer service is a must. They’re a fast-growing and innovative distributor of electrical and automation products that helps businesses streamline their operational costs and scale. It’s a high-pressure mission, but one they continue to uphold thanks to airtight delivery logistics processes.


[Jim Daly](https://www.linkedin.com/in/james-daly-2736a233/) , warehouse manager, has watched the company nearly double in volume since starting over 13 years ago. With that growth, however, came bumps in the road with delivery. But thanks to[AD Buying Group](https://www.curri.com/ad-buying-group-distributor-members) , Jim’s team was introduced to a better, modern solution through their network for Midwest route coverage.


That’s where we came in. Enter:[Curri](https://www.curri.com/) .


## The problem: materials delivery in the Midwest gets tricky


At Standard Electric, Jim utilizes a small fleet of in-house couriers, but it doesn’t entirely fill the delivery gaps. If Jim had to rush materials to a location his drivers didn’t have time for, or to a remote and harder-to-reach location, he had to rely on FedEx or UPS. The result? Overly expensive shipping costs and no guarantee that the supplies would make it to the customer on time. And if FedEx weren’t an option, someone from Jim’s team would make the delivery themselves.


This meant frustration for everyone. The workers at Jim’s warehouse felt exhausted from a lack of reasonable options, and the customers were frustrated by having to wait for deliveries. The team knew that[hotshot delivery](https://www.curri.com/hotshots) , straightforward shipments from point A to point B, existed. However, they recognized these options as overly complex and lacking dedicated customer support.


## What makes Curri Jim’s right-hand partner in the warehouse


Now, the previous frustrations with outdated carrier options are a thing of the past. Jim relies on Curri instead, almost every other day, for critical just-in-time shipments. Curri quickly became the trusted partner for their Madison location when they proved they could surpass the speed of UPS and FedEx, at a fraction of the cost.


For Jim, Curri is essential for urgent and long-distance deliveries. They frequently make stops in towns like Greenbay or Watertown, Wisconsin, and when Jim’s team’s resources are stretched, Curri is available to run the ball past the goal posts. Curri assists with[dedicated routes](https://www.curri.com/route-planner) when their internal team needs the coverage, and they use hotshot delivery when they need those quick deliveries turned around.


> *“Some customers are a pretty far drive for our trucks, and that’s where Curri comes into play. When my team is going one direction, I can trust Curri to go another and get that customer the material they need. Curri is one of our best tools to ensure we can meet our customers' requirements and achieve a 99% or better on-time delivery rate.”*
