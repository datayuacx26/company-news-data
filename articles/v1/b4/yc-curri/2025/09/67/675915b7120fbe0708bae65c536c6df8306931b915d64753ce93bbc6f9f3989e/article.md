# Why FleetPride switched to Curri for reliable, flexible, cost-effective industrial equipment delivery

When you work in construction, timing is everything. On projects big or small, every moving part needs to be meticulously planned. For[Jarrod Kaup](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jarrod-kaup-7135ab11b/) , branch manager at[FleetPride](https://www.linkedin.com/company/fleetpride/) , that means scheduling free deliveries in the Midwest at the drop of a hat.


FleetPride is America’s largest distributor of trailer and truck parts in the independent heavy-duty aftermarket. Offering over 300 locations, including more than 75 service centers, customers rely on FleetPride to get the service and truck parts they need with a quick turnaround. Their mission? To be the most reliable parts supplier in every market they serve, and to provide outstanding service and value to their loyal customer base.


For Jarrod, that means not only executing efficient delivery, but also doing so in a way that reflects their reputation for exceptional service. And their old courier service simply wasn’t cutting it.


Luckily, they switched to[Curri](https://www.curri.com/) .


## Most third-party heavy-duty parts couriers are unreliable and inefficient


Jarrod’s team operates with two or three in-house drivers, but they need some additional support to fill the gaps. If a FleetPride driver calls in sick, breaks down on the road, or is delayed, the supplementary company needs to be fast, flexible, and reliable.


For a while, Jarrod’s team used Roadie for that extra support. While it worked in a pinch, they found the service to be unreliable, lacked visibility, and didn’t offer support for single-order deliveries. On top of that, the team couldn’t rely on Roadie drivers to always be presentable and professional. In other words, they were open to alternative solutions.


With Curri, Jarrod found he could put that trust in third-party deliveries again. Curri offers[hotshot deliveries](https://www.curri.com/hotshots) , which are simple point A to point B deliveries, so a single order gets where it needs to be, fast. He also found that Curri was a game-changer in terms of communication. He had full visibility into delivery status and could count on seamless contact with drivers or dispatch.


> *“It’s a night and day difference between Roadie and Curri. The drivers, for one, the value is unmatched. Curri drivers are presentable, professional, kind, and on time. We trust them to stay dedicated to that order we’re passing off. With Roadie, things would fall short or get cancelled. All without letting us know. We got to a point where Roadie wasn’t helping our image and brand. Curri feels like an extension of our company.”*
