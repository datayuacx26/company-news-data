# Why OmniCable forgoes traditional construction couriers and how Curri helps streamline their Denver-area operations


When we think new construction, we think of the big-picture elements that go into the build. The envelope, the roofing, the windows, the paneling work. While it is important, of course, it’s the smaller elements that really make a house a home.


[OmniCable](https://www.linkedin.com/company/omni-cable/) is a premier redistributor of wire and cable, electrical products, and value-added services. Since its founding in 1977, they only sell to electrical distributors. For larger commercial projects, orders are spooled and palletized, and in smaller residential projects, the wiring is simply boxed and shipped. The company is dedicated to providing every customer with an exceptional experience.


Bobby White, Distribution Center Team Lead at OmniCable’s Denver distribution center, is a big part of that experience. Every time an order makes its way from OmniCable’s Denver distribution center to a job site, Bobby was likely involved in its processing and shipping. And with orders of all sizes coming from different sectors, staying organized with[delivery logistics](https://www.curri.com/platform) could be a nightmare. But thankfully, Bobby and his team use[Curri](https://www.curri.com/) .


## The problem with traditional construction supply delivery systems


Bobby’s job is primarily centered around all-things shipping at OmniCable. He receives, books, and confirms orders, and ensures everything is delivered as expected in his[Denver distribution](https://www.curri.com/location/denver-colorado) center. It’s a big job, and conventional delivery planning doesn’t quite cut it.


With traditional couriers, many businesses don’t have any visibility into[route status](https://www.curri.com/route-planner) , and are often left in the dark about whether or not a shipment made it. If there are delays on the road, neither the supplier nor the builder are kept up to date, meaning flexible rescheduling or pivoting is impossible.


On top of that, if OmniCable needs to modify or book a last-minute delivery, it’s not something a conventional delivery service could support. These bookings typically need to be made days in advance and can throw off execution timelines and inflate the labor budget unnecessarily.


Bobby’s team, however, has been using Curri since before he joined OmniCable in 2023. Here’s how Curri mitigates all the issues created by outdated construction delivery systems.


## How Curri makes materials delivery in Denver a breeze


Bobby’s team needs to book deliveries multiple times per day. No two construction projects are exactly alike, so it would be impossible for them to work on a routine delivery schedule that keeps every builder satisfied. The only option for them to scale their operations? Flexible, reliable delivery.


*“We use Curri daily, and sometimes multiple times a day and enjoy their flexibility and dependability.*

In a few words, Bobby would describe Curry as flexible, dependable, and reliable.
