With[conventional couriers](https://www.curri.com/article/local-courier-alternatives-how-curri-compares) , you get no visibility into the delivery status and often no confirmation that the delivery arrived at all. It’s a bumpy experience end-to-end, leaving both the supplier and the customer in the dark.

## Handling construction supply delivery is a challenge for remote teams


Olivia is the primary operator for delivery logistics for ILC Studios. She has to oversee every shipment and confirm deliveries, all without seeing drivers face-to-face or spending time in the warehouse. Rigid conventional trucking solutions offer no visibility into route status, whether the delivery was made on time, if there was a delay, or even if everything on the BOL was picked up correctly.


On top of that, ILC studios recently expanded their operations. Scaling up meant more deliveries that Olivia would have to orchestrate around the country. Outdated courier options simply wouldn’t cut it.

With Curri, Olivia has a flexible, modern solution for supply delivery.
