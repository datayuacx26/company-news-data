# How Gemaire uses Curri to increase sales and supercharge HVAC material delivery in the Phoenix Metro

When working in HVAC, being reliable and on time are key to retaining customers and growing your business. And getting equipment delivered on schedule? That’s half the battle. HVAC deliveries are also particularly important in hot climates like[Phoenix, Arizona](https://www.curri.com/location/phoenix-arizona) .


That’s where[Brett Garner](https://www.linkedin.com/in/brett-garner-b6210993/) at[Heating and Cooling Supply](https://www.gemaire.com/about-heating-and-cooling-supply) , a subsidiary of Gemaire, part of Watsco, knew he needed an upgrade. Their branch covers 90% of the[Phoenix metro](https://www.curri.com/location/phoenix-arizona) area, and is the largest independent dealer of HVAC equipment and supplies in the country. Brett’s existing solution for delivering to licensed HVAC contractors at job sites and shops was costly and unreliable. That’s where we came in.


Here’s a closer look at why traditional couriers create blockers in Brett’s day-to-day, and how[Curri](https://www.curri.com/) streamlined operations, saving the business time and money.


## Why Phoenix-area suppliers struggle with conventional couriers


We asked Brett about the roadblocks (no pun intended) with his old delivery system and found the same issues many encounter with conventional courier services. They’re expensive, they’re inefficient, and often, they’re unreliable.


These services are rigid, meaning you have little to no choice in the type of vehicle available for pickup, and when they can complete the route. And once your supplies are handed off, professionals like Brett have no visibility into the status of the delivery. If there are delays in the route or the driver gets lost, neither Brett nor his customers are notified. This causes workflow delays and can strain client relationships.


## The solution? Shipping visibility and flexibility with Curri


Brett and his team needed a modern solution for his growing branch. Enter:[Curri](https://www.curri.com/) .


Curri is a[unified logistics platform](https://www.curri.com/platform) that puts distributors in the trades in control of their deliveries. Our best-in-class route planning software allows users to leverage a[nationwide fleet of drivers](https://www.curri.com/fleet) for almost any delivery type, including[hotshot deliveries](https://www.curri.com/hotshots) for quick turnaround.


Luckily for Brett’s team, a representative from Curri had been by with a brochure and business card, which were forwarded right to head office. Brett’s branch was one of the first in the Arizona area to make the switch, and Curri has since been rolled out to other locations around the U.S. One of the most immediate wins Curri brought to the team was delivery visibility with live tracking.


> *“I immediately liked that the drivers take pictures of the process from start to finish. We took comfort in seeing in real time that the delivery had been completed in a satisfactory manner. And if there was an issue on the road, I could be proactive about it with my customer. Full visibility is important for maintaining our customer relationships.”*


Now, Brett’s team uses Curri’s Intelligent Matching to schedule box trucks with liftgates for 80% of their deliveries. Curri understands Brett’s delivery needs and sends vehicles best suited for palletized heavy equipment deliveries that lack forklift access at specific job sites. Our flexible vehicle options also allow Brett to schedule a quick pickup truck or sprinter van for one-off large items or parts deliveries. And previously hard-to-reach clients were no longer a problem once Curri stepped in.


*“The ability to pre-schedule either one-off deliveries or routes is very beneficial for my business. And when we send Curri drivers up 200 miles north, they’re very attentive and conscientious. Google Maps often gets other couriers lost. But I can rely on Curri drivers to follow the address on the bill of lading, and take comfort in knowing those customers up north are going to receive their deliveries. It’s a well-oiled machine that used to be a giant headache with the previous delivery service.”*
