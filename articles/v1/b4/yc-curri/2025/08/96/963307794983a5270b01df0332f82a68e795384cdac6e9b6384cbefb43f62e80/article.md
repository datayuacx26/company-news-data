# Greentech Renewables speeds up solar deliveries across Los Angeles with Curri

At[Greentech Renewables](http://greentechrenewables.com/) in Orange County, mornings start before most people’s alarms. By six a.m., crews are already loading pallets of solar panels, racking, and batteries onto trucks bound for neighborhoods across Los Angeles and Southern California.


The day looks organized on paper: five or six Greentech trucks, each carrying four or five jobs, carefully routed to cover thirty or forty deliveries. But by mid-morning, Dalton Lohman, an account manager at the branch, knows the phone will ring.


A contractor in Santa Ana realizes they forgot to order roof attachments. An installer in Pasadena is short on racking. A project manager in Riverside calls in a panic—his crew is on-site and waiting, but the inverters didn’t make it onto the morning truck.


That’s when Dalton opens his laptop and books a Curri[Hotshot](http://curri.com/hotshots) .


> “Curri is Uber for materials. It’s quick, user-friendly, and it saves our butt when we need something delivered right away.”
>
>
> — **Dalton Lohman, Account Manager, Greentech Renewables**


## **Keeping pace with solar’s demands**


[Solar projects](https://www.curri.com/industries/solar-materials-delivery) move fast. Installers in Los Angeles and Orange County often schedule crews weeks in advance, with homeowners expecting panels installed in a single day. Any missing part—whether a box of attachments or a full pallet of panels—can derail a project, costing labor hours and delaying activation.


Greentech has its own trucks, but those are spoken for with planned routes. There’s no room for last-minute mistakes, let alone jobs two hundred miles away in Central California or across the border into Arizona.


Before Curri, Dalton’s only option was a traditional courier service. The process was clunky: call in an order, recite every detail over the phone, then wait for confirmation. If the driver got lost or delayed, no one knew until the customer called frustrated.


“It wasn’t user-friendly,” Dalton recalls. “And we don’t have time for inefficiency when our customers are waiting on-site.”


[Curri](https://www.curri.com/) changed the game.


## **Why Curri works for Greentech**


With Curri’s digital platform, Dalton can book a Hotshot in less than two minutes. He inputs the pickup, the drop-off, and the load details—and gets an instant quote. From there, Curri finds the right vehicle and driver for the job.


For a fast-moving solar distributor, the benefits are clear:


- **Fast booking** – Urgent deliveries are scheduled in under two minutes, no phone calls required.
- **Flexible vehicle types** – Cars and vans for small boxes of electrical parts; box trucks with liftgates for palletized panels; flatbeds for larger deliveries.[Curri's fleet](https://www.curri.com/fleet) can handle it all.
- **Extended reach** – Coverage across[Los Angeles](https://www.curri.com/location/los-angeles-california) , Orange County, Riverside, San Bernardino, and as far as Central California and Arizona.
- **Customer visibility** – Installers receive live tracking links and proof-of-delivery photos. Crews can plan their day with confidence.


## **The business impact**


Today, Greentech Orange County books 15–20 Curri Hotshots per week. Dalton himself uses it three to five times personally. For his team, Curri has become a daily part of keeping projects on schedule.


The results speak for themselves:


- **10% sales lift** – Retaining one-off customers who might otherwise walk away.
- **Saved labor hours** – Installers don’t have to reschedule crews or waste time driving back to the warehouse.
- **Fleet relief** – Greentech’s trucks stay focused on planned routes while Curri covers last-minute exceptions.
- **Problem-solving flexibility** – Curri even helped when a delivery accidentally blocked access to a homeowner’s entryway. Dalton booked a driver just to move the pallet, solving the issue in hours.

For contractors across Los Angeles, Curri doesn’t just save time—it saves money. Every missed delivery risks:

By filling the gaps, Curri helps Greentech’s customers stay efficient. Crews spend more time installing and less time waiting. Projects move forward smoothly, and contractors know they can trust Greentech to come through—even if something was overlooked.
