# 2025 NMFC LTL freight class changes: Curri has you covered

### **New rules, more risk — unless you’re using Curri**


On **July 19, 2025** , the[National Motor Freight Traffic Association (NMFTA)](https://nmfta.org/) rolled out major updates to the **National Motor Freight Classification (NMFC®)** system. These changes affect how[Less-Than-Truckload](https://www.curri.com/article/ltl-for-construction-delivery) (LTL) freight is classified and priced across the shipping industry.


### **What changed under the 2025 NMFC update:**


- A new **13-tier density scale** is now used to determine freight class
- Over **2,000 commodities** were reclassified
- Accurate **weight and dimensions** are now essential
- Missing or incorrect data can lead to **reclassifications, billing disputes, or delivery delays**
