# Curri’s top features of 2025: Smarter delivery tools and streamlined workflows

### **A year of smarter tools, smoother workflows, and stronger delivery performance.**


In 2025, Curri introduced a powerful set of tools designed to help construction and industrial distributors deliver materials faster, manage routes with more confidence, and reduce slowdowns across every branch. Whether teams support multiple warehouses, deliver to busy jobsites, or manage an internal fleet, these updates were built around one goal: **keeping materials moving with fewer delays and less manual effort for every community.**


Below is a breakdown of the most impactful features Curri released in 2025.


### 1. LTL arrives at Curri


### More shipment options for palletized freight and oversized materials.


**‍** 2025 marked the launch of[Less-Than-Truckload (LTL)](https://www.curri.com/ltl) shipping. Distributors can now quote palletized freight, classify materials accurately, and manage LTL deliveries alongside Hotshot deliveries—all within one platform.


Use built-in NMFC guidance to classify palletized freight with confidence


#### ‍ **LTL features and benefits**


Feature How it helps distributors


NMFC classes 50–500 Classify palletized freight correctly and reduce costly reclasses


Built-in classing guidance Helps bookers choose the correct NMFC class


Side-by-side LTL rate shopping Compare cost and transit speed across multiple carriers for the best option


BOL generation in-platform Auto-fill shipment details to cut paperwork time and reduce manual entry errors


### 2. **Hotshot booking gets smarter**


### Faster estimates, better visibility, and a more seamless booking flow.


**‍**[Hotshot delivery](https://www.curri.com/hotshots) is essential for urgent materials, emergency restocks, and time-sensitive jobsite needs. Curri introduced several improvements in 2025 that make Hotshot booking faster, clearer, and more predictable.


Stay ahead with alerts when specific vehicle types may take longer to secure


#### **Hotshot features and benefits**


Feature How it helps distributors


Upfront estimated totals See estimated pricing by vehicle to choose the most cost-effective option


Low fleet alerts Adjust expectations or vehicle selection when certain vehicle types may take longer to secure


Street view for pickup and drop-off Confirm jobsite access points to help drivers move more efficiently


Traffic visibility during booking See potential slowdowns in advance and keep customers informed with accurate ETAs


### 3. **Route Planner enhancements**


### Smarter weekly planning and better multi-stop coordination.


[Route Planner](https://www.curri.com/route-planner) saw meaningful updates that help distribution teams plan and coordinate routes more effectively. These[improvements](https://www.curri.com/article/the-top-5-benefits-of-using-an-ai-powered-route-planner-in-construction-logistics) support dependable coverage across multiple branches and busy distribution schedules.


Account for driver rest and break times to keep planned routes accurate


#### **Route Planner features and benefits**


Feature How it helps distributors


Calendar view Visualize upcoming routes across days and drivers to avoid overlaps or gaps


Driver break stops Keep planned routes accurate by accounting for fuel breaks, lunch breaks, and driver rest


Commercial navigation Provide route drivers with safer, more reliable directions for larger vehicles


Hotshot deployment Instantly cover any gaps in routes by deploying Curri Hotshots as needed


### 4. **Dedicated route improvements**


### Better coverage management during peak demand.


[Dedicated routes](https://www.curri.com/dedicated) remain one of the most reliable ways to secure predictable, short- and long-term coverage. In 2025, Curri expanded these capabilities to[help distributors](https://www.curri.com/article/how-holt-cat-uses-curri-to-streamline-operations-and-scale-the-business) keep up with the quick turnaround trend and provide consistent operations during the busiest periods.


Track and manage dedicated routes directly from Route Planner


#### **Dedicated route features and benefits**


Feature How it helps distributors


Route extensions with reminders Avoid sudden end-of-day coverage gaps by getting notified before a route expires


Expanded vehicle options Compact vehicles are now available for more cost-effective options for Dedicated coverage


Earlier start times Route coverage can now start as early as 12 a.m.


Manage dedicated routes in Route Planner Track progress, driver status, and completion without switching interfaces


### 5. **Everyday booking and workflow tools**


### Small improvements that create smoother workflows across branches.


Curri introduced several foundational enhancements that help bookers move faster and stay organized day to day. These updates reduce repetitive work, support streamlined billing, and keep teams aligned across branches.


Track and manage deliveries with shared visibility across branches using Teams


#### **Everyday tools and benefits**


Feature How it helps distributors


Sortable billing history Filter invoices by date, delivery type, or cost for faster lookup and more organized billing


Clear “Bill to” selection Ensure charges go to the correct customer or branch every time


Quick cancellations from delivery history Cancel mistaken bookings without contacting support


Streamlined teammate invites Add colleagues quickly to join your branch team on Curri and maintain shared visibility on deliveries
