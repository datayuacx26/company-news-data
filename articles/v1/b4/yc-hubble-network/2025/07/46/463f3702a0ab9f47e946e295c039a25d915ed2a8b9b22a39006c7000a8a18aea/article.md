# Introducing the Hubble Bluetooth® Finding Network

We just launched something I've wanted to build for a long time: A global Bluetooth® Low Energy (LE) finding network that gives **enterprises** the same power that Apple’s Find My, Google’s Find My Device, and Samsung’s SmartThings Find gave to consumers.


But unlike those networks, Hubble’s Bluetooth Terrestrial Network is built specifically for businesses **with full control, zero infrastructure, and global scale** baked in from the start.


Today we’re also launching[hubble.com](https://hubble.com/) . If you’re a developer or enterprise looking to integrate Bluetooth into your products or operations, you can[join our waitlist](https://hubble.com/contact-us) and get access to the network right away.


## **What We Built**


Consumer networks proved how powerful Bluetooth can be for tracking and discovery. But they were never built for enterprise. They’re closed ecosystems. They don’t give you your data. And you’re subject to their rules.


We’ve flipped the model.


The **Hubble Terrestrial Network** gives you:


-


**Global Coverage** : We’re live today across **88 million+ passive scanners** , phones, gateways, and infrastructure already deployed in homes, cities, highways, warehouses, and more.


-


**Zero Infrastructure** : No hardware. No installers. Just update your Bluetooth Low-Energy (BLE) advertisement format. You’re live in minutes.


-


**Full Ownership** : You own the devices, the data, the APIs, the analytics.


-


**Secure by Design** : Rotating IDs, encrypted payloads, verified endpoints, we built this for enterprise use from day one.


-


**Open SDKs & APIs** : Developer-first integration, no proprietary lock-in.


## **What You Can Do With It**


This is a finding network that actually works for the real world:


-


**Asset Tracking** – Pallets, tools, containers, equipment.


-


**Fleet Management** – Know what’s in each vehicle or trailer, in real time.


-


**Cold Chain Monitoring** – Combine Bluetooth Low-Energy (BLE) sensors with location telemetry.


-


**Workplace Safety** – Track badges or wearables in hazardous areas.


-


**Equipment Sharing** – Enable shared access, usage data, and location history across sites.


And we’re just scratching the surface.


## **It’s Live And You Can Build on It Now**


If you want to start building with the network, we’re now onboarding early developers and customers.


[Visit docs.hubble.com](https://docs.hubblenetwork.com/) There, you’ll find technical details, architecture, and access to our waitlist. If you’re ready to build, we’ll get you in fast.


We’ve already seen Fortune 500s use this platform to track assets at scale with no added infrastructure. We’re opening up that same power to the rest of the world.


## **What’s Next**


In March 2024, Hubble became the first to connect a Bluetooth device directly to a satellite. That breakthrough changed the game.


Our vision is simple: Connect billions of Bluetooth devices everywhere on Earth, even off the grid.


Today’s launch is a huge step toward that. If you want to be part of it as a developer, a partner, or just someone curious, check out[hubble.com](https://hubble.com/contact-us) and join the network.


Let’s build something big.
