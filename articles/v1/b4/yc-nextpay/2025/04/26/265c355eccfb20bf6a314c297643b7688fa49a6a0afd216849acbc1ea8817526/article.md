# BIR 2303 and 2307: What Philippine Businesses Should Know

BIR Form 2303 and BIR Form 2307 are often requested during vendor onboarding, invoice processing, and tax filing. They sound similar, but they do different jobs.


In simple terms:


**BIR Form 2303**


The taxpayer’s Certificate of Registration. It helps confirm that a business or professional is registered with the BIR.


**BIR Form 2307**


The Certificate of Creditable Tax Withheld at Source. It documents income payments and creditable withholding tax deducted by a withholding agent.


This guide is for business owners, founders, and finance teams. It is not tax advice. Withholding rules depend on taxpayer type, transaction type, rate, ATC, registration, and current BIR issuances. Confirm details with your accountant, tax adviser, or RDO.


## Quick Comparison


Form What It Is Who Usually Provides It When It Matters


BIR Form 2303 Certificate of Registration The taxpayer/vendor provides a copy to counterparties when needed Vendor onboarding, bank/account setup, tax profile verification, invoice review


BIR Form 2307 Certificate of Creditable Tax Withheld at Source The withholding agent/payor issues it to the income recipient/payee When creditable withholding tax is deducted from a payment or payable income


The practical difference: Form 2303 helps verify the taxpayer profile. Form 2307 helps document creditable tax withheld from income payments.


**Business Takeaway**


Treat Form 2303 as vendor identity and tax-registration evidence, and Form 2307 as withholding evidence. Neither one replaces your own review of tax treatment.


## What is BIR Form 2303?


BIR Form 2303 is the Certificate of Registration issued by the Bureau of Internal Revenue after a taxpayer registers a business, profession, branch, or other taxable activity.


It usually helps identify:


- the taxpayer’s registered name;
- Taxpayer Identification Number or TIN;
- registered address and RDO;
- line of business or registered activity;
- registered tax types and filing obligations;
- other registration details applicable to the taxpayer.


The BIR NewBizReg portal says new business registrants receive a schedule for pick-up of the Certificate of Registration and buying of BIR printed receipts or invoices, or authority to print if applicable, after the application is processed.


For businesses paying vendors, a copy of Form 2303 is useful because it helps align the vendor’s BIR-registered name, TIN, address, and tax profile with the vendor record in your accounting and payment system.


## When Should You Ask for a Vendor’s Form 2303?


Ask for it during onboarding, before the first payment if possible.


A practical vendor file should include:


- BIR Form 2303 or other tax registration evidence;
- business registration documents, such as DTI or SEC documents when applicable;
- invoice format and registered name;
- bank account or payout destination details;
- contact person for finance and tax documents;
- withholding tax status or accountant-confirmed treatment.


Do not use Form 2303 alone as proof that every invoice, receipt, tax rate, or withholding treatment is correct. It is a registration document, not a full tax clearance.


## What is BIR Form 2307?


BIR Form 2307 is the Certificate of Creditable Tax Withheld at Source. The BIR form shows the payee, payor, income payments, tax withheld, ATC, period covered, and signatures.


For the payee, Form 2307 supports the creditable withholding tax that may be claimed against income tax due, subject to the rules for the applicable return and attachments.


For the payor, issuing Form 2307 is part of maintaining evidence that withholding was handled and communicated to the payee.


Use careful wording internally: Form 2307 is not the income tax return itself. It is also not a substitute for recording income. It is evidence of withholding that should tie back to invoices, books, returns, and remittance records.


## When is Form 2307 Issued?


Under BIR withholding regulations, the withholding agent generally furnishes the payee a withholding tax statement using BIR Form 2307 for creditable withholding tax. RR No. 11-2018 describes the certificate as showing monthly income payments, the quarterly total, and the amount of tax withheld.


The timing should be managed by your accountant, but the common compliance workflow is:


1. Determine whether the payment or payable income is subject to creditable withholding tax.
2. Apply the correct rate and ATC based on the transaction and payee.
3. Remit the withholding tax using the applicable BIR return and deadline.
4. Issue BIR Form 2307 to the payee within the required timing, commonly within 20 days from the close of the quarter, or simultaneously with the income payment if requested and applicable.
5. Keep your own copy with the invoice, payment record, and withholding return.


EOPT-related rules also affected the timing of withholding. RR No. 4-2024 should be reviewed for current treatment of when withholding obligations arise, especially where invoices, accrued expenses, or payable amounts are involved.


## What Payees Should Check on a 2307


If you receive a Form 2307 from a client or platform, check it before using it as a tax credit.


Review:


- your registered name and TIN;
- the payor’s name and TIN;
- the covered period;
- income payment amount;
- tax withheld;
- ATC or nature of income payment;
- signatures or authorized e-signatures;
- whether the amount ties to your invoice and books.


If the name, TIN, period, or amount is wrong, ask for correction early. Waiting until income tax filing can create reconciliation problems.


## What Payors Should Check Before Paying Vendors


Before paying suppliers, contractors, freelancers, or professional service providers, build a repeatable workflow:


1. Collect vendor documents.


Get the vendor’s Form 2303, business registration details, invoice details, and bank or payout information.


1. Match names across records.


The registered name, invoice name, TIN, and payout account should make sense together. Mismatches should be resolved before releasing payment.


1. Confirm withholding treatment.


Do not guess the rate from a blog post. Confirm the applicable withholding tax rule, ATC, exemption, or special treatment with your accountant.


1. Record the invoice or payable correctly.


Keep the invoice, purchase order, contract, approval, and payment record linked to the vendor transaction.


1. Issue Form 2307 when withholding applies.


Prepare the certificate using the BIR-prescribed form, accurate payee/payor details, correct period, and correct amounts.


1. Store evidence.


Keep the Form 2307, filed withholding return, proof of remittance, invoice, and payment proof together for future review.


## Common Mistakes


### Treating Form 2303 as a Tax Clearance


Form 2303 confirms registration details. It does not prove that the vendor has filed all returns, paid all taxes, or issued every document correctly.


### Saying Form 2307 Means the Tax was Definitely Remitted


Form 2307 documents withholding information. The withholding agent should also keep remittance records and returns to support what was filed and paid.


### Issuing 2307 for Every Vendor Automatically


Not every payment is subject to the same withholding rule. Check the payee, transaction type, rate, threshold, exemption, and ATC.


### Forgetting That 2307 Affects the Payee’s Filing


The payee may need the certificate to claim creditable withholding tax in the correct quarter or year. Late or incorrect certificates can create avoidable tax filing problems.


### Promising Automated Tax Compliance Without Review


Payment tools can improve records and approvals, but withholding tax computation, remittance, and Form 2307 issuance still need tax review and proper setup.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### Is BIR Form 2303 the Same as a Business Permit?


No. Form 2303 is a BIR tax registration document. A business permit is usually issued by the local government unit. Many businesses need both, depending on the activity and location.


### Is BIR Form 2307 Proof That I Filed My Income Tax Return?


No. Form 2307 is a withholding certificate. You still need to report income and claim tax credits through the applicable income tax return and supporting schedules.


### Who Issues Form 2307?


The withholding agent or payor issues it to the income recipient or payee when creditable withholding tax is withheld.


### Can Form 2307 Use an E-signature?


BIR RMC No. 29-2021 allows e-signatures on certain BIR forms and certificates, including Form 2307, subject to the circular’s rules. The certificate should still follow the latest BIR-prescribed form and validity requirements.


### Should I Withhold Tax From Every Contractor Payment?


Do not assume that. Withholding depends on the taxpayer, transaction, rate, exemption, and current BIR rules. Ask your accountant before setting the rate or issuing the certificate.


## Bottom Line


Form 2303 and Form 2307 solve different problems.


Use Form 2303 to verify vendor registration details before payments become messy. Use Form 2307 to document creditable withholding tax when withholding applies. Keep both tied to invoices, payout records, returns, and accountant-reviewed tax treatment.
