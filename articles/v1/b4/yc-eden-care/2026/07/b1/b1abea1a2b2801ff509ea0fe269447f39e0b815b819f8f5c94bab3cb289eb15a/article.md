# Eden Care Partners with Mediheal Diagnostic and Fertility Centre to Enhance Healthcare Access for Members

**Kigali, Rwanda – Jan 13th, 2025** - Eden Care Healthcare Rwanda Ltd proudly announces its new partnership with Mediheal Diagnostic and Fertility Centre, bringing members expanded access to premium healthcare services. This collaboration aligns with Eden Care's mission to deliver high-quality, specialized health solutions tailored to individuals and families across Rwanda.


Located in Kimihurura at 7 KN 14 Avenue Kigali, Mediheal Diagnostic and Fertility Centre is a renowned facility recognized for its advanced medical diagnostics, women's health, and fertility care. Through this partnership, Eden Care members gain access to a broad spectrum of specialized services, including:


- Advanced Diagnostics and Imaging: Comprehensive laboratory, testing, MRI, X-ray, CT scan, and ultrasound.
- Obstetrics and Gynecology: Full-spectrum maternal and women's health services.
- Fertility Care: State-of-the-art fertility treatments, including in-vitro fertilization (IVF)


> "This partnership with Mediheal underscores our commitment to redefine healthcare access in Rwanda" said Kevin Rudahinduka, CEO of Eden Care Rwanda. "By integrating Mediheal's advanced services into our network, we're are empowering our members with personalized, high-quality healthcare solutions that address their diverse needs"


## About Mediheal Hospital and Fertility Centre


Mediheal Hospital and Fertility Centre, established in 2014, is a leading healthcare provider in Kigali, Rwanda, offering a wide range of specialized medical services. The hospital is equipped with modern facilities, including operating theatres, a CT scan, ICU, NICU, and a fertility clinic. It offers services in areas such as Internal Medicine, Obstetrics and Gynecology, Pediatrics, Urology, Nephrology, and Dentistry, prioritizing patient-centered care and utilizing advanced technology for optimal outcomes.
