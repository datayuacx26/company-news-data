# Content Engineering: The Category B2B Companies Didn't Know They Needed

You spent two years finding product-market fit.


You talked to hundreds of users. You iterated relentlessly. You optimized every feature until people couldn't live without your product.


Then you get on LinkedIn to tell people about it.


And... crickets.


12 likes. Half from your co-founders. Zero pipeline.


So you try again. Same result. You hire a ghostwriter. They sound nothing like you. You use ChatGPT. It's worse—obvious bot garbage that makes you cringe.


Eventually, you just stop posting.


Here's the problem: You're treating content like a checkbox instead of a product.


## What If Content Worked Like Product?


Imagine if you built your product the way most founders "do content."


You'd ship a feature. Get no usage. Then ship the exact same feature again next week. No iteration. No measurement. No improvement.


You'd never do that with your product.


So why do it with your content?


## Content Market Fit


We call it content market fit.


It's when your content consistently generates qualified sales conversations. Not likes. Not engagement. Pipeline.


One of our clients gets 200+ qualified inbounds per month from content.


Another closed a $5M deal from a single post.


Another generated $40K in inbound revenue in 1.5 weeks.


That's not luck. That's content market fit.


And it requires the same discipline you used to find product-market fit.


## The Gap in the Market


Right now, B2B companies have three options for content:


**Option 1: Do it yourself** You don't have time. Your posts are inconsistent. You sound like everyone else.


**Option 2: Hire a ghostwriter** They don't understand your product. They can't capture your voice. They're expensive and slow.


**Option 3: Use AI tools** Generic output. Obviously robotic. Actually damages your brand.


None of these work.


That's why we built a fourth option: Content Engineering.


## How Content Engineering Works


### Week 1: Deep Research


We don't start with prompts. We start with research.


Every article you've written. Every podcast. Every sales call transcript. Every investor pitch. Your competitors' content. Your customers' pain points.


We build what we call your Brand DNA. Documents half the length of a Harry Potter book.


This is the context that makes everything else work.


### Week 2+: Forward Deployed Content Engineers


Here's where we're different.


We don't give you a tool and wish you luck. We forward deploy content engineers who work embedded with your team.


Every week:


- Ship 5 pieces of content
- Measure what converts (not likes—pipeline)
- Iterate based on data
- Optimize until you hit content market fit


Same process you used to find PMF.
