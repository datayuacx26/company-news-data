# Stop Double Bookings: 3 Ways to Sync Your Availability

##### Stop Double Bookings: 3 Ways to Sync Your Availability


Double bookings cost you money, damage your reputation, and waste precious time on damage control. For small tourism businesses juggling[Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.co.uk/) ,[Booking.com](http://booking.com/) , and your own website, keeping availability synchronised is critical but often overwhelming.


##### The Real Cost of Poor Synchronisation


Before diving into solutions, consider what one double booking actually costs:


-


Refunds or relocating guests to competitor properties


-


Negative reviews that impact future bookings


-


Hours spent resolving the issue


-


Lost trust with guests who may never return


For most small properties, a single double booking can cost $200-500+ in direct losses, not counting the long-term reputation damage.


##### Your Three Synchronisation Paths


##### Path 1: iCal Links


Think of iCal as your availability's universal translator. You generate a calendar link from one platform and plug it into others, letting them "read" your bookings.


**The reality:** iCal is free and simple to set up, making it perfect for properties with fewer than 10 rooms or activities. However, it only shares availability - not pricing or guest details.


**Bottom line:** If you're on 2-3 booking platforms and have manageable booking volume, iCal gets you 80% of the way there at zero cost.


##### Path 2: Channel Managers


Channel managers like[Cloudbeds](https://www.cloudbeds.com/) connect all your booking channels through real-time, two-way communication. When a guest books on Airbnb, your Booking.com and Expedia listings update instantly—along with rates, restrictions, and minimum stays.


**The reality:** You'll pay $50-250 monthly depending on your property size, but you gain automated workflows, centralized reporting, and zero manual calendar updates. Cloudbeds connects to 300+ channels and offers comprehensive property management tools in one dashboard.


**Cloudbeds meets Tab:** Connect Cloudbeds with Tab's Checkout Flow to process payments directly through your website while Cloudbeds handles all your OTA distribution. Your guests book commission-free on your site, payments flow through Tab, and Cloudbeds keeps everything synchronized across all channels—no manual intervention needed.


**Bottom line:** If you're managing 5+ channels or serious about scaling, channel managers pay for themselves by preventing errors and reclaiming your time.


##### Path 3: Direct Booking Widgets (Best for Reducing OTA Commissions)


OTA commissions eat 15-25% of every booking. A booking widget on your website lets guests book directly, keeping that revenue in your pocket.


**Tab's Checkout Flow** is built for exactly this purpose:


-

-


Sync availability through iCal integration (perfect for properties with named, individual rooms)


-


Accept payments with secure card processing


-


Display multiple room types or activities with specific pricing


-


Maintain real-time availability without double bookings


**For Cloudbeds users:** The Tab-Cloudbeds integration creates a powerful combination. Use Cloudbeds to manage your property and all OTA channels, then let Tab handle your direct website bookings and payments. You get enterprise-level channel management with commission-free direct booking capability.


**Bottom line:** Even if you start with just iCal connections, adding Tab's widget to your website creates a direct booking channel that compounds in value as you build your online presence.
