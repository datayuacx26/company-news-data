# Ceragon Receives Approximately $120 Million in Orders from Operators in India Year-to-Date

*Approximately $120 million in bookings achieved in just over six months reflect* *continued momentum in a strategic growth market*


**Rosh Ha’ain, Israel, July 28, 2026 -**


Ceragon (NASDAQ: CRNT), a leading solutions provider of end-to-end wireless connectivity,


today announced that it has received and booked approximately $120 million in orders year-to-date from operators in India. These booked orders represent an increase from the approximately $86 million announced in May and reflect continued strong demand from two of the country’s leading mobile operators.


The orders support the continued expansion and modernization of nationwide 4G and 5G transport networks, helping operators increase capacity, accelerate Fixed Wireless Access (FWA)


deployments, and meet the growing demand for high-speed connectivity. The bookings include Ceragon’s advanced microwave and millimeter wave wireless transport solutions, including the IP-50EXA and IP-50CX platforms, which enable operators to deploy high-capacity networks quickly and cost-effectively.


“We are very pleased with the continued momentum we are seeing in India, with approximately $120 million in bookings achieved in just over six months,” said Doron Arazi, Chief Executive Officer of Ceragon. “The sustained pace of follow-on orders reflects the confidence our customers place in Ceragon’s technology and our ability to execute at scale. India remains one of our most important strategic markets, and we believe we are well positioned to support operators as they continue investing in next-generation broadband infrastructure.”


The year-to-date bookings build on Ceragon’s long-standing relationships with India’s leading telecommunications providers and further improve the Company’s visibility into 2026. India continues to be one of the world’s fastest-growing telecommunications markets, driven by rising mobile data consumption, expanding broadband access, and ongoing investments in nationwide 4G and 5G infrastructure.
