# Dropback Unveils Integration with Top Basketball Analytics Platform

March 24, 2025 — Today, Dropback (pro-grade “front office” software for college sports) announces our integration with Synergy Sports (industry-leading insights and analytics for basketball), to further embed basketball scouting workflows with the roster building tasks of the Post-House era.


“General Managers help their teams win by maximizing every dollar spent on their rosters. Our Synergy integration brings the power of the most popular basketball data platform to Dropback–helping GMs innovate their roster construction process, and go from prospect to proposed contract in minutes,” said Luke Bogus, Co-Founder and CEO at Dropback.


> "\[Dropback's\] Synergy integration brings the power of the most popular basketball data platform to Dropback–helping GMs innovate their roster construction process, and go from prospect to proposed contract in minutes"


Dropback’s ‘collegiate front office’ tech empowers General Managers and Personnel staff to translate their athlete evaluations into roster valuations ($)—managing their roster financially to secure top talent while optimizing usage of their NIL and Revenue Sharing budgets.


This collaboration connects Dropback users with Synergy Sport’s powerful data & insights for over 50,000+ prospects across NCAA, NAIA, Grassroots, High School, and International—directly within Dropback. Now, GMs can leverage the power of Synergy’s player tracking data in Dropback’s “Valuation Modeler” to build even richer custom, defensible athlete valuation formulas via program-specific data, metrics, baselines, and weights.


Dropback partnered with NFL & NBA front office advisors to bring the fully-custom & advanced tech the pros use today, to the ‘collegiate front office’. Now, college basketball GMs and DBOs can build their most competitive rosters against budget with Dropback’s tech—leveraging the power of college basketball’s #1 data provider: Synergy Sports.


For more information about eligibility for the Synergy + Dropback integration, chat with your Synergy Sales Representative. For more info on how elite programs running their GM operations on Dropback’s technology, visit our[website](https://www.dropback.com/) .
