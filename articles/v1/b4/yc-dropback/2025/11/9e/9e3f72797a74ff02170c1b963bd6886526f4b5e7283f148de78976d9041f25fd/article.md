# CBB Analytics and Dropback Partner to Power Data-Driven Decisions for Basketball GMs

CBB Analytics is now available in Dropback’s Data Library, providing more analytical power for men’s & women’s basketball General Managers to make data-backed valuation & roster decisions in the rev-share era.


“GMs trust Dropback to construct competitive, cost-efficient rosters,” says Luke Bogus, Co-Founder and CEO of Dropback. “It's been great working with Nick and the CBB Analytics team to bring our integration to life. Now, men's and women's basketball GMs can supercharge their decision-making with the power of the CBB Analytics platform–directly inside Dropback.”


## Advanced Analytics for Enhanced Roster Valuation Workflows


Dropback’s CBB Analytics integration provides real-time access to 300+ unique men’s and women’s basketball metrics for thousands of DI athletes. CBB Analytics is the most comprehensive college basketball analytics platform in the game, covering every men’s and women’s program across Divisions I, II, and III. They offer advanced statistical insights that help programs evaluate talent, optimize rosters, and gain a competitive edge.


"This is an exciting step forward for college basketball," said Nick Canova, Founder of CBB Analytics. "Dropback has built an incredibly sharp product, and we're eager to see how their users leverage our player data to innovate. As college programs become more professionalized, Dropback is going to be a huge part of the future–and we’re thrilled to contribute.”


Starting today, General Managers can connect to CBB Analytics data directly within Dropback to leverage numbers GMs know and trust, such as:


- All-in-one player valuation metrics, including CBB Analytics' proprietary RAPM and related performance models.
- Detailed shooting analytics - tracking performance by zone, by shot clock, by assisted or unassisted or by play context such as half-court, transition, and putbacks.
- Expanded playmaking and contextual data, including assists by zone, foul drawing stats, and other advanced metrics.


GMs can now supercharge their Dropback valuation insights & roster construction workflows with the power of CBB Analytics data directly within Dropback—no additional integration or setup steps required.


## Powering the Future of Roster Construction


This strategic partnership makes it easy for GMs and analytics staffs to leverage CBB Analytics’s industry-leading basketball analytics dataset, alongside Dropback’s comprehensive front office platform, in real time.


“Dropback is democratizing NBA-grade front office tools, at a fraction of the effort & cost,” says Nick Siscoe, CTO and Co-Founder of Dropback. “By integrating with CBB Analytics, an industry-leading data provider that GMs know and trust, we're reducing the friction for elite college front offices to make data-decision roster decisions.”
