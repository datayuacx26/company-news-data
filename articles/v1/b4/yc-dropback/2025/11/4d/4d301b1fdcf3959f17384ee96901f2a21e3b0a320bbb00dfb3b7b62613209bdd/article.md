# NEW: Dropback For Basketball

Today, we’re graduating Dropback for Basketball out of beta—the next step in our mission to bring pro-grade decision infrastructure to every college sport.


Football may have been our starting point, but the challenges we’ve been solving there are universal:


- GMs are being asked to make faster, sharper, and more defensible decisions about their rosters.
- College programs now operate in an environment that looks more and more like the Pros—constant roster movements with annual expectations to construct championship-caliber teams


Teams that thrive in this new era will be the ones who bring pro-level structure to the way they evaluate players and manage their rosters. Basketball is no different.


And Dropback is here to help.


### Why We Built Dropback for Basketball


Basketball is the most analytics-heavy sport in college athletics. And from our dozens of GM conversations, thousands of data points may exist for on-the-court analysis—but translating that data to off-the-court decisions (roster construction, contract valuations, etc) is near-impossible.


Dropback for Basketball helps elite men's and women's basketball GMs to translate on-the-court analytics to off-the-court roster decisions.


We’ve taken the same technical foundation that powers our football product—modern data pipelines, no-code modeling infrastructure, and real-time collaborative workflows—and built a basketball-specific experience that allows staffs to evaluate talent, shape roster strategy, and manage the cap within one, private, pro-grade infrastructure.


### Elite Infrastructure for Elite General Managers


Programs can finally work from a modern, unified front-office workspace that’s purpose-built for the Rev-Share Era—bringing together the data they already trust with the tools they now need.
