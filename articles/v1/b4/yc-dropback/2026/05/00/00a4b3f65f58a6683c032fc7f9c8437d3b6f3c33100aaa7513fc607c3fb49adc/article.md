# Dropback and Evollve Partner to Power Volleyball's New Era of Roster Construction

Today, Dropback announces a strategic partnership with Evollve, one of volleyball's most open and comprehensive statistics platforms. Now, the same kind of analytical infrastructure that has reshaped roster decisions across other major college sports will be available to college volleyball teams for the first time.


"Volleyball is one of the fastest-growing sports in the country, and its emerging front offices deserve the same caliber of data-driven tools that have transformed roster decisions in other sports," says Luke Bogus, Co-Founder and CEO of Dropback. "With Evollve's Dropback integration, volleyball staff now have a level of roster intelligence that simply hasn't existed in this sport until now."


This partnership brings the power of Evollve directly into Dropback—enabling real-time athlete evaluation and valuation workflows powered by a robust volleyball statistical database.


"We are tremendously excited to begin this partnership with Dropback," says Mike Beuoy, Co-Founder of Evollve. "By combining our statistical framework with Dropback's workflows, we look forward to enabling better front office decision making and improved evaluation of player value."


## Data-Driven Roster Decision Frameworks, Built for Elite Volleyball Programs


As investment in volleyball programs continues to accelerate across the country, making roster spend decisions backed by real data has never been more critical.


Dropback's custom Front Office Database solutions help elite teams combine personnel and financial data into one private, streamlined workflow—to make defensible, data-driven athlete compensation decisions.


Starting today, volleyball staff can experience the power of Evollve's comprehensive, high-quality statistical data within Dropback. By enabling the data connector in Settings, staff will gain access to Evollve's NCAA women's volleyball statistical database of:


- Traditional set-level box score statistics (kills, digs, blocks, assists, aces, and more)
- Advanced performance metrics and analytics
- Historical season-over-season data


Evaluating, comparing, and valuing talent has historically been a time-intensive process, scattered across numerous systems. Now, by combining Evollve's robust data platform with Dropback's front office workflows, the future of athlete acquisition and talent management is here.
