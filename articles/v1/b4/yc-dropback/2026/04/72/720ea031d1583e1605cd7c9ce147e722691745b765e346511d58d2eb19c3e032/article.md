# Dropback and Cerebro Sports Partner to Power College Basketball's New Era of Roster Construction

Today, Dropback announces a strategic partnership with Cerebro Sports, basketball's most comprehensive player database. This brings the power of over 1.5 million player records across high school, college, grassroots, and international levels—directly inside Dropback.


"General Managers trust Dropback to construct competitive, cost-efficient rosters," says Luke Bogus, Co-Founder and CEO of Dropback. "Cerebro Sports delivers an unmatched breadth and depth of high-quality roster data. By partnering with Cerebro, we're supercharging elite GMs' roster construction & compensation workflows."


This partnership brings the power of Cerebro directly into Dropback—enabling real scouting and valuation workflows across the largest player database in basketball.


"Dropback is building exactly the kind of workflow infrastructure modern college basketball needs," says Ryan Girardot, Co-Founder of Cerebro Sports. "We're excited to bring Cerebro's global player data to a platform that helps General Managers evaluate talent, compare options, and make smarter roster decisions faster. Together, we're helping programs operate with more context, more confidence, and a far more complete view of the player market."

## About Cerebro Sports


Cerebro Sports is building basketball's most comprehensive player data and intelligence platform.
