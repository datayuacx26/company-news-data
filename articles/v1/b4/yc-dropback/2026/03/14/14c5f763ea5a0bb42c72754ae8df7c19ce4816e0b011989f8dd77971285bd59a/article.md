# Sports Info Solutions and Dropback Partner to Power Data-Driven Roster Valuations for College General Managers

Dropback partners with Sports Info Solutions (SIS), a premier FBS data and analytics engine, to power data-driven roster construction for college football front offices.


SIS is coming to Dropback's Data Library, delivering the gold standard in football analytics directly into GM workflows—at no additional cost for existing Dropback clients.


"Every decision in the revenue-sharing era of college sports needs great data to drive it," says Luke Bogus, Co-Founder and CEO of Dropback. "We're thrilled to directly integrate SIS's trusted data feeds into Dropback. Now, every valuation model clients build in Dropback starts from a strong, democratized, analytical foundation."


With today's launch, Dropback's infrastructure to strategize, forecast, and compete in the revenue-sharing era just got even better.


## Essential Football Data, Now Available in Dropback


Sports Info Solutions provides statistics and actionable insights for every FBS snap. This is based on tracking done by SIS's trained team of Data Scouts, who do rigorous data tracking to ensure the best and most accurate information.


"Front offices are being asked to make increasingly high-stakes roster and revenue allocation decisions, and those decisions demand objective, trustworthy data," said Corey March, Director of Business Development at Sports Info Solutions. "We're excited to partner with Dropback to bring our FBS data and analytics directly into the workflows college GMs rely on to build smarter, more informed roster strategies."


For all existing Dropback clients, SIS data will soon be available with no additional cost and no setup required. This is a direct investment in the quality of the data powering Dropback's platform and the valuations dozens of teams create every day.


Dropback Plus and Pro clients can unlock access to SIS's advanced insights, including its all-encompassing player value stat, Total Points, and its injury data—enabling Front Offices to build even richer custom, defensible athlete valuation models.


## Powering Data-Driven Decision Making for Front Offices


Revenue sharing has raised the stakes for every roster decision. Elite Front Offices are evolving beyond the gut feel when allocating millions across a roster, and toward translating actionable data into defensible financial decisions.


With 250+ SIS datapoints now available in Dropback's Data Library, GMs get exactly that: a comprehensive, scout-verified football dataset in the FBS, embedded directly into the valuation and roster construction workflows they've built on top of Dropback.


"Dropback democratizes pro-grade front office tools for college sports. We unify internal datasets, with best-in-class data sources, into one private workspace—so GMs don't have to wrangle data across a dozen platforms to make great decisions," says Nick Siscoe, CTO and Co-Founder of Dropback. "Integrating SIS's advanced datastreams will immediately enrich every client's custom Dropback database, upgrading their analytical workflows overnight."


## About Sports Info Solutions


Sports Info Solutions partner with Major Leagues, Teams, Media, Sportsbooks, Odds Providers, and Fans to help them win by enriching and optimizing their decision-making process.


SIS is a pioneer in the sports analytics space. The company was founded in 2002 by John Dewan and his partners as Baseball Info Solutions and changed its name as it evolved into working with other sports and businesses.


## About Dropback


Dropback is the pro-grade front office data workspace for elite college programs.


Trusted by 30+ elite teams across every major conference, Dropback's infrastructure empowers General Managers to build cost-efficient teams with customizable analytics. GMs can build a custom "athlete valuation engine" based on their priorities, values, and budget in a unified, private database.
