## 🧑‍💻 Who


We’re a team of builders, hardware-meets-software enthusiasts, and techno-optimists on a mission to make it radically easier to build physical products.


---


## 💡 Why


Our team has helped design and ramp up manufacturing lines for some of the most iconic products out there — iPhones, Google Nest devices, Oculus headsets ... . After living through that chaos firsthand, we asked: “Why does this still suck?”


That question became our founding flame. So we started Autumn Labs to lay down the infrastructure to streamline the future of manufacturing and take the pain out of bringing hardware products to life.


---


## 🛠️ What


Our first product makes it seamless to monitor machines, robots, and entire manufacturing lines in real time — just like in the *Hello World* demo above.


Factories today are packed with advanced equipment, but the infrastructure to monitor them is still stuck in the past. Data is often delayed, scattered, or incomplete—which makes it hard to keep things running smoothly. Manufacturing shouldn’t feel like flying blind.


We’re here to fix that.


Some of the goodies:


- **Real-Time Monitoring** — Instantly see machine status and see how its spending time: active, idle, error
- **Data Collection** — Pull station, unit, and process level data to find patterns and bottlenecks.
- **AI-Powered Insights** — Use our insight engine to crunch through the data to provide KPIs and actionable steps.
- **Dependency Management** — Define control variables and critical limits that each station might need as input
