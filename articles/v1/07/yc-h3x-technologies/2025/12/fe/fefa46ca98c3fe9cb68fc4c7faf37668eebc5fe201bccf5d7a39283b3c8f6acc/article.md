# PRODUCT LAUNCH: HPDM-500 Multisector Integrated Motor Drive

Today, we‘re unveiling the HPDM-500 — the next step in the evolution of our ultra-high power density electric motors.


The HPDM-500 delivers 500 kW (670 hp) in a package compact enough where two units fit inside a standard carry-on suitcase.


This product is stackable, allowing it to scale into megawatt-class applications. More announcements related to this soon.


The unit also incorporates dual, fully isolated integrated inverters to support fault-tolerant operation.First production batch of 20 units ship to our flagship customer in 26Q2 for an advanced hybrid turboelectric propulsion system.


Full specs on the unit can be found[here](https://www.h3x.tech/products/hpdm-500)
