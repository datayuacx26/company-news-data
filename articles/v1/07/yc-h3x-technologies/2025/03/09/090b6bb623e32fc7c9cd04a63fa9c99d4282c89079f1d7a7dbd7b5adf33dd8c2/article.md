# HPDM-30 Gen 4 Updates

H3X has announced the release of its Gen 4 HPDM-30, a high-performance integrated motor drive engineered to meet the demanding requirements of aerospace and defense platforms.


Key enhancements in the Gen 4 HPDM-30 include:


- ✅ Tested to 45,000 ft altitude
- ✅ 800V-capable architecture
- ✅ Improved EMI performance
- ✅ Enhanced corrosion protection
- ✅ New high-reliability connectors
- ✅ Streamlined manufacturing processes


These improvements enable broader environmental compatibility, improved manufacturability, and streamlined integration into advanced aerospace and defense platforms. Production is ramping, and units are already shipping to customers.
