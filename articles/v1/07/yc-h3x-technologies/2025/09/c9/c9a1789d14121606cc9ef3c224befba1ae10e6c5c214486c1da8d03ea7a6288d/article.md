# HPDM-180D Direct-Drive Version Now Available

H3X Technologies is excited to introduce the **HPDM-180D** , a direct-drive variant of the HPDM-180. This high power density integrated motor drive features a 20,000 RPM shaft speed and is well-suited for generator applications.


With a **system-level power density of 13.8 kW/kg (continuous)** , the HPDM-180D is the most power-dense product in our portfolio to date.


The HPDM-180D is now open for **early access programs** . To learn more or request evaluation units, please contact the H3X team.


For more information, click on the '180D (Direct Drive)' tab in the[HPDM-180 product page](https://www.h3x.tech/products/hpdm-180) .
