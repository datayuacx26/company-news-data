# H3X Hits 5 Year Anniversary

5 Years At H3X! 🎉🍾


Five years ago, H3X was just an idea. All we had was a basic cad model and a bold vision for an electrified future powered by H3X motors.


Today, we’re shipping real hardware to customers building everything from supersonic jets to hydrofoiling boats to electric aircraft. Along the way, we’ve assembled a world-class team of engineers and builders who bring ambitious ideas to life everyday with relentless execution and focus.


We are building the enabling technology that will radically transform not just one industry, but several.


Thank you to everyone who came out to celebrate with us last week! This is only the beginning 🚀
