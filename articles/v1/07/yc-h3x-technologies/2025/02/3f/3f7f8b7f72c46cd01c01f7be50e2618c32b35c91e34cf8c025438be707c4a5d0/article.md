# H3X Partners with SailGP to Power Its State-of-the-Art Jet Propulsion System

We’re proud to power the state-of-the-art jet propulsion system that will help SailGP's fleet of identical F50 catamarans foil in light wind conditions - currently under development for seasons ahead. Developed by[HamiltonJet](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hamilton-jet/) , our electric motor drives the Pod-Jet system providing greater certainty of world-class racing for both athletes and fans.
