[Published by Afamefuna Onyeogulu](https://www.psychiatrist.com/news/nrx-100s-breakthrough-in-suicidality-treatment/) | March 24, 2025


*Clinical Relevance: For millions struggling with suicidal thoughts, the difference between life and death could come down to a single breakthrough treatment.*


- Every 11 minutes someone in the United States takes their own life, with 1.6 million Americans attempting suicide in 2022.
- Mood disorders, such as various forms of depression, strongly associate with the risk of suicidal ideation.
- NRX-100 is an IV ketamine formulation being submitted to regulators as an alternative treatment for acute suicidal crises in certain types of depression
