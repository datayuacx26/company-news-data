# How Pitney Bowes can upgrade your office operations

## Convenient Self-Service Pick-Up


**Situation:** **Packages keep arriving at the office for employees who are now working from home. The reception desk is piled up, and you’re worried about items getting misplaced or stolen.**


**Solution:** Flexible, self-service options are key to making everyone happy. Rerouting packages is an option—and it’s easy to do when you have PitneyTrack, but for local package pickups or sensitive asset transfers, it’s hard to beat a[smart locker system](https://www.pitneybowes.com/us/shipping-and-mailing/parcels-and-packages/smart-parcel-lockers.html?cm_sp=officeoperations_blog_link_lockers_040422) . When a package is placed in a ParcelPoint™ Smart Locker, the recipient automatically receives an email with a unique, scannable code for claiming it, which they can do securely at a time that’s convenient for them. They can even use the ParcelPoint Mobile App or their employee badge to securely access their items. The locker system tracks every activity and with options such as signature confirmation or photo capture, so you’ll have full accountability of who picked up what and when.


**Take it to the next level:** Smart Lockers do more than just deliver, they can be used for multiple uses. Utilizing your smart lockers as a way for hybrid workers or visitors to temporarily store personal items while visiting the office, provides a self-service, convenient way to protect personal belongings without the need for keys, locks and inefficient manual processes. It’s a practical solution that provides flexibility and peace of mind while turning your smart lockers into a multifunctional solution.
