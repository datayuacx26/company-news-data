## **What is a shipping label?**


A shipping label contains the essential details—addresses, tracking number, barcodes, and service type—that carriers need to deliver and track your package accurately. It’s a crucial part of every shipping workflow, ensuring each parcel reaches the right destination. For a deeper look, explore our full guide on what is a shipping label


## **What you need to create and print shipping labels**


Getting started with shipping from home is easier than you might think. Most of the tools you need to create and print shipping labels are already in your workspace — and you don’t need any fancy setup to begin.


At minimum, you’ll want a computer or mobile device, a printer, and paper or adhesive labels. A digital scale is a smart addition for weighing packages accurately, which helps you get the best USPS,UPS, or FedEx rates without overpaying.


You can print shipping labels on standard 8.5x11 paper or on 4x6 thermal labels using a label printer or thermal label printer for a more professional look. These options make it simple to match your printing setup to your shipping volume and budget.

For the ultimate upgrade, the[PitneyShip Cube™](https://www.pitneybowes.com/us/shipping-label-printer.html) combines a built-in scale and label printer in one compact device, perfect for fast, accurate, and affordable label creation.


### **Use a carrier website (USPS, UPS, FedEx)**


Carrier websites are one of the most straightforward ways to create shipping labels and manage postage online. If you already have a USPS account, you can use[USPS Click-N-Ship®](https://www.usps.com/ship/online-shipping.htm) to prepare Priority Mail®, Priority Mail Express®, and international shipments directly from your browser. Flat Rate options—like Priority Mail Flat Rate Boxes®—are great for flat rate shipping since you pay one set price regardless of weight, as long as it fits the box.


[UPS.com](https://www.ups.com/us/en/home) and[FedEx.com](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/home.html) offer similar tools to help you generate postage labels, compare packaging supplies, and even schedule package pickups. Each of these shipping carriers supports domestic and international label creation and lets you print labels at home or in your office for quick drop-offs.


While these platforms offer convenience and reliability, they’re limited to one carrier’s services and discounts. For greater flexibility and savings, third-party shipping software like PitneyShip® lets you compare rates across carriers, print[prepaid shipping labels](https://www.pitneybowes.com/us/blog/how-do-you-send-prepaid-shipping-label.html) , and access exclusive discounts—all from a single dashboard.


### **Use an online marketplace (eBay, Etsy, Amazon)**


If you sell through an online marketplace like eBay, Etsy, or Amazon, you can create shipping labels and print shipping labels directly from your seller dashboard. These marketplaces include built-in tools for order fulfillment, making it easy to manage sales, track shipments, and fulfill orders all in one place. Each platform partners with at least one shipping carrier—often USPS or UPS—and offers discounted postage or shipping discounts through negotiated rates.


This approach is fast, familiar, and beginner-friendly, especially for part-time sellers or those just starting their ecommerce journey. However, it can limit flexibility since you’re typically tied to one carrier and set of rates. For growing businesses that ship higher volumes, using shipping software like PitneyShip lets you compare rates across multiple carriers, automate fulfillment, and scale beyond marketplace tools for greater savings and efficiency.


### **Use PayPal Shipping**


PayPal Shipping is a convenient option for anyone with a PayPal account, not just eBay sellers. It lets you print labels and purchase postage for USPS shipping labels and UPS shipping labels directly within your PayPal dashboard—no separate store setup required. You can easily manage shipments, track deliveries, and handle basic order management tasks in one familiar interface.


This tool is great for home-based or casual shippers who need quick, reliable shipping options for smaller or occasional ecommerce operations. However, it doesn’t support FedEx or offer expedited shipping, batch label creation, or multi-carrier rate comparison. In short, PayPal Shipping is a fast and simple starting point, but not scalable enough for growing businesses that need advanced automation or broader carrier integration.
