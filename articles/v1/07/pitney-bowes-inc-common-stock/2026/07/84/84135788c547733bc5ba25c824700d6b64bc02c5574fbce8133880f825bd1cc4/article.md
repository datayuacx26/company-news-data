# Pitney Bowes among the top 3 workplaces in India

Pitney Bowes has been named one of[India’s Top 3 Great Mid-Size Workplaces For 2024](https://www.greatplacetowork.in/indias-great-mid-size-workplaces) by the Great Place to Work®️ Institute. The announcement was made on June 20 at a ceremony in Mumbai and members of the Pitney Bowes India team were there to receive this prestigious award. Pitney Bowes joins the list for the 11th time alongside industry leaders.


“Our commitment to excellence, inclusion, and trust has brought us here, and it will propel us forward. Each one of our employees plays an important role in shaping our culture and contributes to making us a great place to work every single day.” – Pankaj Sachdeva, Vice President Data Science & Analytics and Managing Director, Innovation.


Pitney Bowes ranked 3 in the largest workplace culture study in the country. It is a matter of pride to be consistently recognized for our people-first culture. Pitney Bowes has earned this recognition for creating a Great Place to Work FOR ALL employees and has excelled on the 5 dimensions of building a High-Trust, High-Performance Culture™ – Credibility, Respect, Fairness, Pride, and Camaraderie.


In 2024, Pitney Bowes was also named India’s Best Organizations for Women - 2024 by ET Now, a division of The Economic Times, one of India’s leading business publications. We were also recognized as one of India’s Top 25 Best WorkplacesTM Building a Culture of Innovation by All 2024 - Mid-size and India’s Top 50 Best WorkplacesTM in Health and Wellness 2023 by the Great Place to Work® Institute for fostering a holistic approach to employee health and wellbeing and for our industry-best innovation and people practices, respectively.


Together, we continue to create an environment where everyone feels valued, thrives, and contributes to our shared success. To learn more about Life at Pitney Bowes visit[pitneybowes.com/careers](https://www.pitneybowes.com/content/pb/us/en/careers) .
