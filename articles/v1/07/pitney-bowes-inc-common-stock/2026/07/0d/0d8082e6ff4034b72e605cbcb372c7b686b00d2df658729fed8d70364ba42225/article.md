# July USPS® Changes Explained: How to Avoid Surprise Mailing and Shipping Costs

The USPS July 12 rate change brings more than just higher prices. From dimensional-weight calculations and hazardous material fees to certified mail updates and package measurement requirements, these changes could significantly impact mailing and shipping costs for businesses of all sizes.


Whether you send invoices, legal notices, statements, product samples, or e-commerce orders, understanding these changes now can help you avoid unexpected costs and improve operational efficiency.


Here’s what’s changing and what your business can do about it.


**Another stamp increase? Here’s what it means for your mailing budget**


Starting July 12, the price of a First-Class Mail® Forever stamp increases by 4 cents.


While four cents may seem minimal, the increase adds up quickly for organizations sending hundreds or thousands of mail pieces every month.


The good news? Businesses using a postage meter or online postage software continue to receive the 4-cent discount compared to retail stamps, helping offset the impact of the increase.


**Why it matters**


- Higher mailing costs directly impact budgets.
- High-volume mailers can see substantial annual cost increases.
- Metered mail and online postage remain a simple way to reduce spending on every eligible mail piece.


**Certified Mail just got more expensive - Here’s how to save on every piece**


Similar to First-Class stamp prices, the base USPS Certified Mail cost is increasing.


Organizations that use Certified Mail for legal notifications, compliance communications, collections, tax notices, or customer documentation should pay close attention.


One bright spot: the *savings* you’ll receive by using Digital Certified mail that provides an Electronic Return Receipt (ERR) are increasing to $1.74 per piece.


**Why it matters**


Electronic Return Receipt offers more than cost savings:


- Lower cost than Green Card service
- Digital (PDF) proof of delivery
- Searchable records and signatures
- Faster access to delivery documentation
- Reduced manual handling and filing
- Long-term record retention


Instead of waiting for a physical card to be returned and manually stored, proof of delivery becomes immediately accessible in a digital format.


For organizations that rely on Certified Mail regularly, switching to an electronic workflow can significantly reduce administrative effort while improving compliance and visibility.


**Oversized boxes could cost you more: Understanding USPS’s new DIM weight rules**


One of the most significant shipping changes involves USPS dimensional weight (DIM weight) pricing.


USPS is changing its DIM divisor from **166 to 139** , aligning with FedEx and UPS.


What does that mean?


Large, lightweight packages will generate higher billable weights, even if the actual package weight doesn't change.


**Example**


A package that previously qualified for lower pricing based on its physical weight may now be rated at a much higher dimensional weight, resulting in a higher shipping charge.


Additionally, each dimension must now be rounded up to the nearest whole inch, so a measurement of 12.3 inches becomes 13 inches. Even small dimensional differences can affect shipping costs.


**Why it matters**


Businesses shipping everyday items like marketing kits, apparel and promotional materials may see costs increase based solely on the size of the box, without changing a single thing about their product. The size of the box now matters more than ever.


**New USPS package rules could trigger extra fees. Are you ready?**


The Postal Service is also placing greater emphasis on package dimension accuracy.


Packages meeting certain size thresholds now require additional dimension reporting, and the USPS plans to expand dimension requirements in future phases beginning in January 2027. It’s important to prepare now for these changes.


Failure to provide accurate dimensions could result in additional fees.


**Why it matters**


Organizations that estimate box sizes rather than measure them accurately may experience:


- Non-compliance charges
- Billing adjustments
- Increased shipping costs
- More time spent resolving carrier disputes


Accurate measurements are no longer optional, they're becoming essential to controlling shipping spend.


**Ground Advantage™ Commercial ounce-based pricing is going away**


Another major change affects USPS Ground Advantage Commercial pricing.


Historically, lighter-weight shipments benefited from ounce-based pricing tiers. Under the new structure, USPS is eliminating those ounce-based breaks and moving to pound-based pricing.


This means many lightweight shipments will cost more.


**Most affected shipments include:**


- Small merchandise
- Samples
- Promotional items
- Accessories
- Lightweight e-commerce orders


**Why it matters**


Many organizations have optimized shipping strategies around lightweight Ground Advantage packages.


As ounce breaks disappear, the economics change. Businesses should reevaluate packaging sizes, service selection, multicarrier shipping strategies and shipping software that offers discounted carrier pricing.


**Hidden HazMat fees are coming: What shippers need to watch for**


USPS is introducing new hazardous materials (HazMat) handling requirements and penalties.


**New fees include:**


**HazMat handling fee**


- Applies to Priority Mail Express® and Priority Mail® shipments containing hazardous materials
- Fee: $7.50 per qualifying package


**HazMat non-compliance fee**


- Applies when packages aren't properly declared or labeled
- Fee: $50 per package


Examples of products that may qualify as hazardous materials include:


- Lithium batteries
- Nail polish
- Nail polish remover
- Certain aerosols
- Perfume
- Mouthwash containing alcohol


In some cases, businesses may unknowingly trigger a penalty by reusing boxes that still display HazMat **** markings.


**Why it matters**


HazMat **** compliance is no longer something businesses can afford to overlook.


Incorrect labeling can be costly, and the USPS is increasing enforcement as it focuses on transportation safety, particularly for shipments moving via air transportation.


**5 steps to minimize the impact of the July USPS changes**


The July USPS changes reinforce an important reality:


**Operational decisions now have a greater impact on shipping and mailing costs than ever before.**


Organizations should review:


✅ Mailing methods for First-Class Mail


✅ Certified Mail processes


✅ Package dimensions and packaging selection


✅ Ground Advantage and light-weight package strategies


✅ HazMat **** compliance procedures


✅ Shipping technology currently in use


Small adjustments can generate meaningful savings and help avoid unnecessary fees.
