# German American Bank Strengthens Louisville Commercial Banking Team with Addition of Cory Bybee

German American Bank’s financial services team serving the Louisville business community has grown with the addition of Cory Bybee. As a Senior Vice President of Commercial Banking he will drive the delivery of German American Bank’s innovative banking solutions with a focus on client relationships and quality local service.


Cory comes to German American Bank with over 20 years of experience in commercial banking. He brings extensive knowledge in the areas of business development, commercial lending and real estate investment. He is a graduate of Louisville’s Trinity High School and holds a bachelor’s degree from Eastern Kentucky University. Cory recently finished 10 years of service on the Metropolitan Business Development Corporation board for the City of Louisville. He’s actively involved as a member of St. Bernadette Catholic Church, the Trinity Alumni Business Circle, Building Industry Association of Greater Louisville, and as a Kentucky Certified Commercial Investment Member.


“Cory is a valuable addition to our financial services team. His experience and knowledge of the area business community further strengthens our commitment to being a top provider of business solutions in Louisville,” states Jason Adler, German American Bank Louisville Market President. “He has a relationship-driven approach to helping his clients that make him an excellent fit for German American Bank."


“I am excited to join the team here at German American Bank,” says Cory Bybee. “The company’s strength and stability speak for its historical successes, and I am thrilled to be a part of the continued growth.”


Cory may be contacted at 502-276-0849 or by email atcory.bybee@germanamerican.com . For more information about German American Business Banking, visit[germanamerican.com/business](https://germanamerican.com/business) .
