# German American Bank announces the addition of Casey Delgado to the Evansville Commercial Banking Team

German American Bank’s financial services team serving the Evansville business community has grown with the addition of Casey Delgado. As a Commercial Banking Officer he will drive the delivery of German American Bank’s innovative banking solutions with a focus on client relationships and quality local service.


Casey Delgado is a graduate of Austin Peay State University with a background in sales and business development. He has spent the past several years working in commercial lending, helping businesses navigate their financial needs and growth opportunities. In his spare time, he pitches for the Evansville Otters baseball team and enjoys playing golf. Originally from Miami, Casey played professional baseball for 12 years, reaching Double-A with the New York Mets.


“Casey is a valuable addition to our financial services team. His experience and knowledge of the area business community further strengthens our commitment to being a top provider of business solutions in Evansville,” states Lindsay Botsch, German American Bank Evansville Market President. “He has a relationship-driven approach to helping his clients that make him an excellent fit for German American Bank.


“I am excited to join the team here at German American Bank,” says Delgado. “The company’s strength and stability speak for its historical successes, and I am thrilled to be a part of the continued growth.”


Casey may be contacted at812-759-2330 or by email atcasey.delgado@germanamerican.com . For more information about German American Business Banking, visit[germanamerican.com/business](https://germanamerican.com/business) .
