# Travis Russell joins German American Bank as Regional President

German American Bank announces Bowling Green native, Travis Russell, has joined the company as Regional President. In this role, he will lead the Company’s financial team serving Warren, Simpson, Barren, and Hart Counties in South Central Kentucky in delivering relationship-focused commercial and personal banking and wealth management services. Travis joins the local leadership team of Kim Thomas, Senior Vice President of Retail Banking, and Todd Kanipe, Regional Credit Officer.


“Travis brings a tremendous amount of talent to an already strong and dedicated team of professionals in Bowling Green, Glasgow, Franklin, Horse Cave, and Munfordville,” states Neil Dauby, German American Bank Chairman and CEO. “His experience as a community leader makes him a great fit for German American Bank and our focus on local decision making, relationship-building, and strong community involvement. I look forward to watching the leadership team of Travis, Kim and Todd grow our presence in the South Central Kentucky area.”


Russell brings over 10 years of commercial banking experience to this role, holding various positions at American Bank and Trust and J.P. Morgan Chase Bank in the Bowling Green area. He is a graduate of Western Kentucky University with a degree in Finance and obtained an MBA from the University of Kentucky. As a lifelong Bowling Green resident, Travis has deep rooted connections and presence in his community. He is currently a member of the Cornerstone Committee for LifeWorks, and is a graduate of Leadership Bowling Green and the Kentucky Bankers Association Emerging Leader Program. He has also served as a Board member and President of the Bowling Green Country Club. Travis and his wife Alyssa live in Bowling Green with their son, Chapman, and daughter, Elsie Blair.


"I’ve always been impressed with German American Bank’s reputation for community involvement. The company’s strength and stability speak for its historical successes. I am joining a seasoned and strong team of commercial and personal bankers and I am thrilled be a part of our continued success and growth in our local communities," said Russell.
