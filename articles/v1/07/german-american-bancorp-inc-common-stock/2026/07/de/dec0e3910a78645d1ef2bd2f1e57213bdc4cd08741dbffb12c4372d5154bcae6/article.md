# German American Bank Strengthens Louisville Commercial Banking Team with Addition of Kyle Kulyan

German American Bank has announced that the financial services team serving the Greater Louisville business community has grown with the addition of Kyle Kulyan. As a Regional Vice President of Treasury Management, Kyle will drive the delivery of German American Bank’s innovative banking solutions with a focus on client relationships and quality local service.


Kyle has fifteen years of experience in banking, specializing in business development, treasury and relationship management serving commercial and corporate businesses. He is a 6-year board member for the Parks Alliance of Louisville, and spent the past ten years volunteering as a team lead for the WHAS Crusade for Children donation drive.


“Kyle’s knowledge of the Louisville business community and relationship-driven approach to banking make him an outstanding addition to our team,” states Jason Adler, German American Market President.


“His expertise in the financial services field further strengthens our commitment to being a top provider of business solutions,” adds Chris LeBeau, Vice President, Director of Treasury Management.


"I am motivated to be a trusted partner for my clients as they work to achieve their objectives,” says Kulyan. “German American Bank is a highly respected community bank and I look forward to building upon their success in the Louisville area by focusing on the unique needs of the individuals and businesses we serve.”


Kyle can be contacted at 502-805-0893 or by email at kyle.kulyan@germanamerican.com
