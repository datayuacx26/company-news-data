# Luxfer Annual General Meeting of Shareholders to be Held on June 11, 2026


### 20 April 2026


**RIVERSIDE, CA, April 20, 2026 – Luxfer Holdings PLC (NYSE: LXFR)** (“Luxfer” or the “Company”), a global industrial company innovating niche applications in materials engineering, today confirms that its Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (the “AGM”) will be held on Thursday, June 11, 2026, at 8:30 a.m. BST at the Company’s registered address, particularly the Luxfer MEL Technologies site, located at:


Lumns Lane
Manchester
M27 8LN


United Kingdom


Please refer to the AGM Location Map, available for download below, for further details on the AGM location. The formal notice of the AGM and details regarding the business to be acted upon at the AGM are set forth in the Proxy Statement. The Company’s Proxy Statement on Form DEF 14A, Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025, and U.K. Annual Report and Accounts (including the Directors’ Remuneration Report) for the year ended December 31, 2025 are also available for download below.


Please be advised that this website cannot be used for any communication, voting, or proxy appointment in connection with the AGM. The documents available below are provided for information purposes only.


**About Luxfer Holdings PLC**


Luxfer is a global industrial company innovating niche applications in materials engineering. Using its broad array of proprietary technologies, Luxfer focuses on value creation, customer satisfaction, and demanding applications where technical know-how and manufacturing expertise combine to deliver a superior product. Luxfer’s high-performance materials, components, and high-pressure gas containment devices are used in defense and emergency response, healthcare, transportation, and general industrial applications. For more information, please visit[www.luxfer.com](http://www.luxfer.com/) .


Luxfer is listed on the New York Stock Exchange and its ordinary shares trade under the symbol LXFR.
