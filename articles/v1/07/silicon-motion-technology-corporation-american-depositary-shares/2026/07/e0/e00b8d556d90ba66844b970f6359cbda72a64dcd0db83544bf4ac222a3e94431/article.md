# Blog-Silicon Motion Named a Clarivate Top 100 Global Innovator 2026-Silicon Motion

[Contact Us](https://www.siliconmotion.com/support/contact/sales)


Silicon Motion has been named one of the Clarivate Top 100 Global Innovators 2026, marking a significant milestone in our journey of continuous innovation. This recognition highlights organizations that consistently deliver high-impact inventions and shape the future of technology across industries.


As a global leader in merchant NAND flash controller design, innovation has always been at the core of what we do. From advancing storage performance and efficiency to strengthening our intellectual property strategy, we focus on turning R&D breakthroughs into meaningful, real-world impact.


Our inclusion in this year’s list reflects not only the scale of our patent portfolio, but also the quality and influence of our innovations—particularly in areas such as grant rate and downstream impact. It underscores our ability to translate technology leadership into strong, defensible IP.


On April 9, we had the honor of attending the Clarivate Top 100 Global Innovators 2026 Awards. Alex Chou, Senior VP of Enterprise Storage & Display Interface Solutions, represented Silicon Motion on stage as we celebrated our first inclusion—selected from over one million organizations worldwide.


We are also proud to be recognized alongside leading innovators from Taiwan, including TSMC, MediaTek, Foxconn, ASUS, AUO, Delta, Realtek, Nanya Technology, Wistron, ITRI, and Coretronic. Together, these companies represent the strength and global impact of Taiwan’s technology ecosystem.


As AI continues to redefine the boundaries of innovation, we remain committed to advancing storage technologies that support the next generation of workloads—delivering higher performance, greater efficiency, and scalable solutions for customers around the world.


This recognition is not just a milestone, but a motivation to keep pushing forward.
