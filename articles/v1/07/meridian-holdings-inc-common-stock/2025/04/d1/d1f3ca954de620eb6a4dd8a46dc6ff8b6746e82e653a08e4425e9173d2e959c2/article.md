## Highlights from the ESG 2024 Report


- **293 CSR campaigns** across 25 markets
- Healthcare, education, sports, and social support initiatives
- 5,027 hours of employee volunteer work
- 18,004 direct and indirect beneficiaries


### Workforce and Diversity


- Total employees: **1,224** across 18 jurisdictions
- **63% female representation** , including 63% in managerial roles
- 39 employees with disabilities
- Average **18.26 hours of training per employee**


### Environmental Impact


- Energy usage: **4,289,352 kWh** (down from 2022)
- Water usage: **8,576 m³** (continued decline)
- A4 paper use dropped to **4,487 packs**
- **8,437 kg** of recycled e-waste – more than double last year


### ESG Governance and Licensing


- Fully licensed operations in **18 markets** , including South Africa, Brazil, Serbia, and Peru
- Active role in shaping EU and international gaming regulation through **EOGL**
- Integration of ESG into risk management, tech deployment, and product development


### Technology-Driven Responsibility


- Launch of **AI Bet** and **AI Casino Recommender**
- 120+ in-house developers maintain a fully proprietary platform
- Member of the **global 5% club** : one of few gaming firms with end-to-end tech ownership


### Recognized Impact


- **IPO Edge praise** for CSR and ESG innovation
- Global expansion of **Meridian Donate** platform: 43 campaigns in 14 markets
- Collaboration with governments and NGOs on key healthcare and education initiatives


### Sustainability in Action


- Partnership with **Nature Trust Malta** for wildlife and habitat conservation
- Global afforestation efforts and urban tree planting
- Scaled community recycling programs in all markets
