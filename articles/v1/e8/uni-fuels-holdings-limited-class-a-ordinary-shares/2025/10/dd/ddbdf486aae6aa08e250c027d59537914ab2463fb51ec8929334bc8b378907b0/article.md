# Uni-Fuels Announces 2025 Interim Financial Results

**Uni-Fuels (NASDAQ: UFG)** reported interim results for the six months ended June 30, 2025, showing significant operational expansion and revenue growth.


Key metrics: **revenues** of ~US$114.6M (+54% YoY), **cost of revenues** ~US$112.6M (+55% YoY), **gross profit** ~US$2.1M (+42% YoY), and **net income after tax** ~US$90k (down 11% YoY). Volumes and coverage: **217,000 MT** delivered (+90%), **502 transactions** (+75%), **359 vessels** served (+80%), **103 ports** (+98%), and **179 customers** (+106%).


Corporate moves: ISCC EU/ISCC PLUS certification (Feb 12, 2025), new subsidiaries/offices in Dubai and Shanghai, and two oversubscribed commercial paper issuances raising US$3M each (Series 001 repaid Oct 17, 2025; Series 002 closed Oct 17, 2025 and listed Oct 18, 2025) to bolster liquidity.
