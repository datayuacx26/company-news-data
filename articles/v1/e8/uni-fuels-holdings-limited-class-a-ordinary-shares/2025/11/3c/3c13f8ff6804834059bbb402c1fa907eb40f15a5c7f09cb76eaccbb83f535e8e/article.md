# Uni-Fuels Continues to Strengthen Global Operations with A New Office in Limassol, Cyprus

**Uni-Fuels (NASDAQ: UFG)** announced the opening of its first European office in **Limassol, Cyprus** on **November 11, 2025** . The Limassol office joins new 2025 offices in Dubai and Shanghai as part of the company's global expansion to provide localized marine fuel expertise, enhance operational agility, and strengthen supplier diversification and supply resilience.


The company said the Cyprus location offers closer access to Mediterranean trading hubs, local price signals, regulatory updates, and green-shipping corridors, and supports coordination of low-emission and alternative fuel sourcing to meet evolving customer and regulatory needs.
