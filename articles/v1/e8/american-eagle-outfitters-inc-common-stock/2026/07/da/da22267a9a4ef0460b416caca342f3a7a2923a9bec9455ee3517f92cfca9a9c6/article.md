# AEO Announces 2026 Steven A. Davis Scholarship for Social Impact Recipients

##### *Meet the newest cohort of 17 inspiring associate scholars driving real change.*


Real change doesn’t just happen—it’s driven by people bold enough to stand up for inclusion and equity. Which is why we are thrilled to introduce 17 incredible associates from across the U.S., Canada and Mexico who are doing exactly that in their local communities.


Through AEO’s $5 million Steven A. Davis Scholarship commitment, we are investing in these change-makers to help fund their higher education and amplify their impact. **Since 2021, this program has fueled the education of 96 associate scholars with more than $2.5 million in total funding.**


Join us in celebrating the 17 recipients who are using their voices and passion to build a better, more inclusive future.


---


*“I never asked for arthritis, but through my advocacy I can ensure that no one will ever have to feel as alone as I did. I am a witness to the fact that with early intervention and fair policy, we can change lives. The world needs to know: Kids get arthritis, too.”*


**Alana** is an AE Brand Ambassador entering her freshman year at Texas A&M – College Station as a political science major. As a Junior Ambassador for the Arthritis Foundation, she lobbied Texas Senators and Representatives for health equity and insurance policy reform at the Advocacy Summit in Washington, D.C. Locally, Alana has driven positive impact by raising awareness for juvenile arthritis and mentoring newly diagnosed peers in her community.


**Alex** is an AE Brand Ambassador and a junior at McMaster University in Canada studying midwifery. Deeply committed to reproductive justice, she has challenged hospital labor induction norms and spoken at a provincial press conference for free contraception and expanded midwifery care. Alex supports marginalized groups by organizing postpartum supply drives and serving as a student representative on the Association of Ontario Midwives’ Disability Equity Committee.


*“I am excited to receive this scholarship because it means an opportunity to follow my dream of becoming a lawyer.”*


**Camilla** is a Sr. Brand Ambassador at AE and will major in political science at Florida International University. As president of the Hispanic & Latino Student Union, she fostered cultural belonging and community while launching a school initiative to provide free menstrual hygiene products. She also advanced youth civic empowerment through a social justice fellowship with Rhizome, focusing on increasing youth voter engagement.


*“Change to me looks like stepping outside one’s comfort zone and embracing experiences that challenge one’s abilities. It means being open to new opportunities, learning from setbacks and adapting to unfamiliar situations. Although change can feel uncomfortable at first, it often leads to growth, confidence and new possibilities!”*


**Chloe** is an AE Brand Ambassador and an incoming pediatric pharmacy student at Duquesne University. She is a 3-year mentor for the Outdoor Odyssey Leadership Academy, where she helps at-risk youth from vulnerable backgrounds to build confidence, resilience and teamwork skills. Chloe dedicates her free time to organizing community resource distributions for families in need and assisting with global education initiatives.


*“You are responsible for being the change you want to see. Change comes from taking action, staying determined and refusing to let doubt dictate possibility.”*


**Dana** is an AE Brand Ambassador and plans to pursue a dual degree in general management and public health at the University of Lethbridge in Canada. After revitalizing her school’s Interact Club, she went on to lead community projects and fundraisers—bringing clean water to Uganda and distributing winter blankets to unhoused individuals. Dana drives social impact by coordinating youth STEM outreach and serving as a public relations representative for the Muslim Youth of Lethbridge.


**Fiona** is an AE Brand Ambassador and a recent high school graduate with plans to study political science. As the treasurer of her school’s Eco Club, she spearheaded local trash cleanups, community upcycling events and sustainability workshops for middle schools. She has also dedicated over 100 hours to volunteering with Glamour Gals, bridging generational gaps and combating social isolation among senior citizens.


*“In a world where polarization is at an extreme, community is the blanket of integration.”*


**Inaya** is an AE Brand Ambassador and a recent high school graduate planning to major in finance and minor in legal studies. Through her work with South Asian SOAR, she actively combats cultural stigmas around gender-based violence. She also fosters representation and youth civic engagement by volunteering with LearnAAPI to support Asian American advocacy.


**Isabelle** is a Sr. Brand Ambassador at OFFLINE and a junior at the University of Texas at Austin majoring in social work. She is the Vice President of Socks for the Sole, a student-run non-profit organization dedicated to humanizing and empowering Austin’s unhoused population. In this role, Isabelle manages fundraising events and coordinates essential item distributions to support displaced immigrants, unhoused veterans and domestic violence survivors.


**Jayla** is an Aerie Brand Ambassador and a recent high school graduate preparing to attend DePaul University as a biochemistry major. She supports her community as an academic peer tutor and mentor — helping students rebuild their confidence and self-esteem. During her internship at a local community healing center, she organized peer support groups and led outreach to normalize conversations around mental health.


*“Change to me means creating spaces where every voice is valued, every person feels represented, and every community is supported with the care and opportunities they deserve.”*


**Kaycie** is an AE Brand Ambassador pursuing a nursing degree in her junior year at Old Dominion University. As the College Engagement Coordinator for the Nursing Careers and Pathways Program, she mentors middle and high school students from underserved backgrounds to explore STEM and health professions. Through her advocacy and mentorship, Kaycie works to break institutional barriers and increase diverse representation within medical fields.


*“My social work is important to me because it allows me to make an impact on communities one generation at a time.”*


**Kaliah** is a Brand Ambassador at OFFLINE and a pre-med sophomore majoring in biology at Xavier University of Louisiana. As a social and environmental justice advocate, she served as vice president of her high school diversity club, orchestrating inclusive cultural celebrations. Kaliah currently supports community rebuilding and sustainability in New Orleans by volunteering with The Green Project to recycle discarded building materials.


*“A listening ear and an open mind facilitate change in ways simple brainstorming often cannot produce- solutions should be created in the eyes of those they impact.”*


**Kathryn** is an AE Brand Ambassador and a senior studying biological sciences on the pre-med track. She supports her community through her work with the Seneca-Babcock Food Pantry on Buffalo’s East Side, a critical resource center addressing food scarcity. Through direct engagement with residents, Kathryn works to dismantle socioeconomic stigmas and foster an environment of mutual respect and dignity.


*“I am excited to receive this scholarship because it helps me achieve my goal of using my education and resources to make lasting changes in my community.”*


**Kaylee** is an AE Brand Ambassador and a recent high school graduate heading to Georgetown University to study public policy. As a queer woman from a rural area, Kaylee actively challenges local prejudice and advocates for equitable institutional policies. She has spent her summers volunteering in communities across the rural South—rebuilding porches, supporting youth literacy programs and delivering essential heating fuel to low-income families.


**Manny** is an AE Brand Ambassador and a freshman studying social work at Wilfrid Laurier University’s Brantford Campus. As a volunteer responder for the Crisis Text Line, he provides critical emotional support to youth experiencing severe distress. Manny also serves as a youth ambassador at The Grove, championing early mental health intervention and working to remove systemic and financial barriers for marginalized populations.


*“My social justice work is important to me because I believe diverse communities deserve spaces where people feel genuinely welcomed, represented and connected. I’m incredibly grateful to receive this scholarship because it supports both my education and my commitment to creating more inclusive spaces rooted in empathy, equity and belonging.”*


**Moksha** is an AE Brand Ambassador and a senior at the University of Calgary pursuing an honours degree in psychology. She has served nearly 200 hours as a hospital patient experience volunteer alongside years of ongoing service in local soup kitchens. She actively supports the growth of Girls Who Like to Do Things, a Calgary-based social club for female-identifying individuals focused on building community, connection and belonging. As a university research assistant, Moksha contributes to systemic equity by examining workplace harassment, postpartum healthcare inequities and immigrant experiences.


*“I am excited about my education path because it will allow me to help reduce healthcare disparities in society and help provide equal resources for all.”*


**Nimrit** is an AE Brand Ambassador pursuing a public health degree at the University of Texas at Austin. Inspired by her grandfather’s experiences, she volunteers at community blood pressure drives to translate and provide health education for Sikh elders facing language and cultural barriers. Nimrit also co-founded Mentor Medicine, an organization bridging equity gaps by pairing aspiring medical students with experienced healthcare professionals.


*“I am excited about my education path because it gives me the tools to turn passion into impact. I want to grow not only for myself, but so I can uplift others along the way!”*


**Rewayete** is an AE Brand Ambassador attending Virginia Commonwealth University to study marketing and fashion merchandising. She works to raise public awareness about the ongoing human rights crisis affecting the Uyghur community through research and advocacy. Rewayete also contributed to a school-based academic research project exploring genocide awareness and its psychological effects on youth to advocate for mental health equity.
