# Forbes: 50 Over 50 Honors Jennifer Foyle

Edited By: Maggie McGrath |[Link to article](https://www.forbes.com/sites/maggiemcgrath/2026/08/11/2026-50-over-50-lifestyle/)


***50 Over 50: Lifestyle***


Veteran actor Tracee Ellis Ross made her Broadway debut at 53. At 61, CeCe Winans is the most awarded female gospel artist of all time. And at 83, fashion founder Betsey Johnson continues to shape style while doing the splits (literally). Age is an advantage for these and the 47 other women who influence how we sing, eat, dress and experience culture.


---


Jennifer Foyle
59 | President, Executive Creative Director – AE & Aerie
Jennifer Foyle transformed Aerie from a $225 million intimates brand into a $2 billion business while making authenticity central to the brand’s identity. She championed the retailer’s pioneering 2014 AerieREAL campaign, which pledged to stop retouching models in its advertising years before body positivity became an industry norm. More than a decade later, she extended that commitment into the AI era, spearheading Aerie’s pledge not to use AI generated people or bodies in its marketing. As president and executive creative director of AE & Aerie, she continues to shape the brand’s creative vision and long-term strategy.
