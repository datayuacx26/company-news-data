# Interlink Electronics Announces Participation at Southern Manufacturing & Electronics February 4-6, 2025

Interlink Electronics, Inc. Announces Participation at Southern Manufacturing & Electronics February 4-6, 2025 IRVINE, Calif., Feb. 3, 2025 /PRNewswire/ -- Interlink Electronics, Inc.


(Nasdaq: LINK), a leading provider of sensor and printed electronics solutions, is pleased to announce it will be exhibiting at Southern Manufacturing & Electronics 25, a cornerstone event in the manufacturing and electronics industry, from February 4 through February 6, 2025, in Farnborough, England.


Southern Manufacturing & Electronics is a dynamic hub where industry leaders, innovators, and experts converge to share ideas, discover new technologies, and forge long-lasting partnerships.


Interlink will be showcasing our broad portfolio of smart printed sensor solutions including resistive force and pressure, HMI solutions, piezoelectric sensors, as well as our broad range of electrochemical gas and environmental sensors.


In addition, you will have the opportunity to learn about our recently acquired innovative conductive transfer technology which enables the integration of electronics and sensors into textiles and fabrics enabling a true "textronics" revolution. This award-winning technology is targeted at smart fabric and textile applications in the wearables, consumer, medical and automotive markets.


About Interlink Electronics, Inc. Interlink Electronics is a leading provider of sensors and printed electronic solutions, boasting nearly 40 years of success in delivering mission-critical technologies across diverse markets. Our blue-chip customers benefit from our robust instruments and printed electronics solutions, which span various markets, including industrial, medical, consumer, and automotive.


Our expertise in materials science, manufacturing, embedded electronics, firmware, and software enables us to create custom solutions tailored to our customers' unique needs (www.interlinkelectronics.com).


About Southern Manufacturing & Electronics Southern Manufacturing & Electronics is a dynamic hub where industry leaders, innovators, and experts converge to share ideas, discover new technologies, and forge long-lasting partnerships. The show provides invaluable insight through technical seminars, connections with over 500 leading suppliers, and hands-on demos of solutions that can transform business operations.
