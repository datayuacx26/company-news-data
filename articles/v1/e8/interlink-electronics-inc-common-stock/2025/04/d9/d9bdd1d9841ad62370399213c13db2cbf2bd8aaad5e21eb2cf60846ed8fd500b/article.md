# Interlink Electronics to Participate at the Planet MicroCap Showcase April 23-24, 2025

Interlink Electronics to Participate at the Planet MicroCap Showcase April 23- 24, 2025 FREMONT, Calif., April 7, 2025 /PRNewswire/ -- Interlink Electronics, Inc. (Nasdaq: LINK), a leading provider of sensor and printed electronics solutions, will be participating in the Planet MicroCap Showcase: VEGAS 2025, being held April 22-24, 2025, at the Paris Hotel & Casino in Las Vegas, Nevada.


Interlink Chairman, President, and CEO Steven N. Bronson will deliver a company presentation on Wednesday, April 23, 2025, and host one-on-one meetings with investors on Thursday, April 24, 2025. Presentation Details Date: Wednesday, April 23, 2025 Time: 8:00 a.m.


Pacific time Location: Paris Hotel & Casino in Las Vegas, Nevada Webcast: Here Bronson will provide an overview of Interlink's recent operational progress and key strategic achievements, including the acquisition of UK-based Conductive Transfers Limited and the awarding of two SBIR grants to accelerate product innovation.


He will also highlight the company's progress in scaling operations, expanding its global footprint, and strengthening its leadership team to support long-term growth. In addition, Bronson will outline Interlink's 2025 growth priorities across its core verticals: environmental sensing, printed electronics, and smart textile solutions.


With strong revenue momentum, healthy margins, and growing institutional interest, Interlink views 2025 as a transformative year as it continues to execute its strategic expansion into high-growth end markets.


Participation in the Planet MicroCap Showcase—held in partnership with MicroCapClub—supports Interlink's commitment to enhancing investor visibility, expanding its shareholder base, and maintaining consistent engagement with the financial community. For additional information or to schedule a one-on-one meeting with Interlink management, please contact the Gateway team at LINK@IESensors.com.
