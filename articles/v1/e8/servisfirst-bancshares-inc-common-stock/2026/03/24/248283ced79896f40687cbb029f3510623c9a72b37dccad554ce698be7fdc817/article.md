# ServisFirst Bank Huntsville Announces New Headquarters

## ***ServisFirst Bank Begins Construction on Downtown Huntsville Office***


**HUNTSVILLE, Ala.** –[ServisFirst Bank](https://servisfirstbank.com/) , a subsidiary of ServisFirst Bancshares (NYSE:SFBS), is pleased to announce the construction of its **new Huntsville headquarters** , further solidifying the Bank’s growing presence in one of Alabama’s most dynamic markets.


Located at 515 Meridian Street N. in Downtown Huntsville, the new **four-story, 25,000-square-foot** facility is designed to support modern, relationship-driven banking while honoring the character of the surrounding historic district. The building will accommodate approximately 50 banking professionals and feature premium office space, a large community room, flexible meeting areas, and a 1,200-square-foot covered rooftop patio with views of Huntsville’s historic downtown and Monte Sano Mountain.


“ServisFirst Bank has experienced meaningful growth in the Huntsville market, and this new headquarters reflects our long-term commitment to the community,” states Jim Holtkamp, Regional CEO of ServisFirst Bank Huntsville. “We are proud to invest in a space that not only supports our team and clients, but also creates opportunities to gather, connect and continue building strong relationships throughout the region.”


The project is being built by[Fite Building Company](https://www.fitebuilding.com/) , with design by[Matheny Goldmon Architecture + Interiors](https://mathenygoldmon.com/) . Additional project partners include[SSOE Group](https://www.ssoe.com/) ,[PEC Structural Engineering](https://pecstructural.com/) ,[Schoel](https://schoel.com/) , and[GTEC](https://gteccorp.com/) . Construction is currently underway and is expected to be completed in 2027.


ServisFirst Bank currently operates two locations in Huntsville, at 401 Meridian Street, Suite 100 and 1267 Enterprise Way, Suite A.


For more information regarding ServisFirst Bank’s recent announcement in Huntsville, please contact Krista Conlin at Krista@KCProjects.net. For more about ServisFirst Bank, please visit[www.servisfirstbank.com](http://www.servisfirstbank.com/) .


#### **ABOUT SERVISFIRST BANK**


ServisFirst Bank is a full-service commercial bank focused on commercial banking, correspondent banking, treasury management, private banking and the professional consumer market, emphasizing competitive products, state-of-the-art technology and a focus on quality service. Recently, the Bank announced that its assets exceed $18 billion. The Bank offers sophisticated treasury management products, Internet banking, home mortgage lending, remote deposit express banking, and highly competitive rates.


ServisFirst Bank was formed in May 2005, and has offices in Alabama, Florida, Georgia, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee, and Virginia. In April 2015, and annually thereafter, ServisFirst Bank has earned investment- grade ratings and a stable outlook from Kroll Bond Rating Agency (KBRA), which measures companies’ financial fundamentals.
