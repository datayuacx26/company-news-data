Miller is the President and Founder of Miller CPAs & Advisors, a growing accounting and advisory firm providing tax, audit, and consulting services to a diverse client base. With more than two decades of leadership experience in public accounting, he is recognized for his disciplined approach to financial stewardship, sound judgment, and ability to guide organizations through complex business environments with clarity and confidence.


> “We are pleased to welcome Mike to our Northwest Florida Advisory Board,” states Rex D. McKinney, Regional CEO of ServisFirst Bank Northwest Florida. “His extensive background in public accounting, combined with his strong relationships across the construction, real estate, and utility sectors, brings valuable perspective to our team. Mike’s commitment to transparency, accountability, and long-term growth aligns well with our mission as we continue to expand in the region.”


In his current role, Miller leads the firm’s strategic direction, oversees financial performance, and mentors a team of professionals. As a trusted advisor to business owners, contractors, and individuals, he offers deep expertise in strategic tax planning, financial reporting, and organizational decision-making, with strength in the construction, real estate, and utility industries.


Prior to founding Miller CPAs & Advisors, Miller held leadership roles within a large regional accounting firm in the Southeast, where he provided advisory services across multiple markets and industries. Earlier in his career, he also served in both nonprofit financial leadership and public accounting roles, gaining broad experience in financial management, planning, and client service that continues to inform his approach today.


Miller is actively engaged in industry and community leadership. He has served as Treasurer of the Northwest Florida Utility Contractors Association, providing financial oversight and strategic guidance, and currently serves as Chair of the Membership Development Committee for Associated Builders and Contractors – North Florida Chapter. He is also active with the Home Builders Association of Northwest Florida and the Florida Surety Association.


A Certified Public Accountant, Miller earned degrees in Accounting and Management from Pensacola Christian College and the University of West Florida. Originally from Lewisburg, West Virginia, he has called Pensacola home since 1988 and maintains strong ties to the local business and civic community.
