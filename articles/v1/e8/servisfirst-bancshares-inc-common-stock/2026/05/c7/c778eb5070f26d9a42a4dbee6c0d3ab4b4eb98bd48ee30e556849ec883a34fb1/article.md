# ServisFirst Bank Opens New Panama City Office

## ***ServisFirst Bank Expands Commitment to Northwest Florida***


**PANAMA CITY, Fl.** –[ServisFirst Bank](https://protect.checkpoint.com/v2/r01/___https:/servisfirstbank.com/___.YzJ1OnNlcnZpc2ZpcnN0YmFuazE2ODQ4NDI5NDUwNTU6YzpvZmZpY2UzNjVfZW1haWxzX2F0dGFjaG1lbnQ6NmE3Yzk4NTEzZWYxM2EzMjQ3M2FmMzBkZDNiZGZlMTY6Nzo0NzMzOmM5MzAzOTEzMDU1MDljMTNmMmQ1ZGJiY2FhOGViNWRiZDcyMDJjYTIyNzdlNGMxZjEwZDJkNDhmNzg2Y2ViODA6cDpUOkY) , a subsidiary of ServisFirst Bancshares (NYSE:SFBS), is pleased to announce the **opening of its new Panama City office** , located at 2402 State Avenue, Suite 101, Panama City, FL 32405. The new full-service banking office officially opened on Friday, May 15, further strengthening the Bank’s presence and long-term investment in the Northwest Florida market.


> “We are excited to officially open the doors to our new Panama City office and continue building on the momentum we’ve experienced in this market,” stated Steven Aase, Market President of ServisFirst Bank Panama City. “This new location reflects our commitment to providing exceptional service, convenience and long-term support to our clients and the Panama City community. We are proud to continue investing in this growing market and creating a space that allows our team to better serve our clients for years to come.”


The **new 5,000-square-foot facility** was financed by ServisFirst Bank and designed to better serve the growing needs of clients throughout the Panama City area. The office features **two drive-thru lanes** , **a drive-thru ATM** , **on-site mortgage loan assistance and treasury management services** , offering both convenience and comprehensive banking solutions for businesses and individuals alike. The building was developed by Gulf Coast Plaza, led by Dr. Daniel “Skip” Daube of Gulf Coast Facial Plastics and ENT Center.


> “ServisFirst Bank has been an outstanding partner throughout this entire process,” stated Dr. Daube. “Their team understands the value of relationships and brings a level of professionalism, responsiveness and personal attention that made this project seamless from start to finish. We are proud to welcome ServisFirst Bank to Gulf Coast Plaza and know they will continue making a positive impact in the Panama City community.”


ServisFirst Bank Panama City currently employs seven associates dedicated to delivering personalized banking solutions and exceptional client service throughout the region. The Bank has served the area for more than three-and-a-half years and continues to experience strong growth within the market. The new office replaces the Bank’s previous location at 1022 W. 23rd Street, Suite 600, Panama City, FL 32405. Construction on the new facility began at the start of 2025.

## **ABOUT SERVISFIRST BANK**


ServisFirst Bank is a full-service commercial bank focused on commercial banking, correspondent banking, treasury management, private banking and the professional consumer market, emphasizing competitive products, state-of-the-art technology and a focus on quality service. Recently, the Bank announced that its assets exceed $18 billion. The Bank offers sophisticated treasury management products, Internet banking, home mortgage lending, remote deposit express banking, and highly competitive rates.


ServisFirst Bank was formed in May 2005, and has offices in Alabama, Florida, Georgia, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee, and Virginia. In April 2015, and annually thereafter, ServisFirst Bank has earned investment- grade ratings and a stable outlook from Kroll Bond Rating Agency (KBRA), which measures companies’ financial fundamentals.
