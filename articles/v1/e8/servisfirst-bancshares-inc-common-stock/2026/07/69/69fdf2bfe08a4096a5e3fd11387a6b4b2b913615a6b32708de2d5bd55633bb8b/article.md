# ServisFirst Bank Hosts SBA Roundtable to Advance Small Business Access to Capital

## ***Financial Leaders from across Alabama Convene at ServisFirst Headquarters***


**BIRMINGHAM, Ala.** –[ServisFirst Bank](https://protect.checkpoint.com/v2/r01/___https:/servisfirstbank.com/___.YzJ1OnNlcnZpc2ZpcnN0YmFuazE2ODQ4NDI5NDUwNTU6YzpvZmZpY2UzNjVfZW1haWxzX2F0dGFjaG1lbnQ6OGMwMDE4YjcwYjRkMjcyNjE5MGNjYzNiMmNjODgzNDM6NzpjZTg4OmQyNTU4MTY5Nzg3MWNmMzI5ZWE0M2U3ODJlZmZiNTlhNjhhZjJiZDJiZThiNTZhNDc3NWE0MmQ0M2QyYWU3Nzg6cDpUOkY) , a subsidiary of ServisFirst Bancshares (NYSE:SFBS), is pleased to announce that it hosted a roundtable discussion in partnership with the[U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA)](https://protect.checkpoint.com/v2/r01/___https:/www.sba.gov/___.YzJ1OnNlcnZpc2ZpcnN0YmFuazE2ODQ4NDI5NDUwNTU6YzpvZmZpY2UzNjVfZW1haWxzX2F0dGFjaG1lbnQ6YzQ0MTAzNjc0MjY4N2Y4YWE0M2QxNjEzYzBjYjE5ZTk6NzowMGFiOjU4YzJlNTJiY2IxYzlkZWIwNTQ3OGIxYzZkYTRmMDk0NDU0NzczYTY5YWQ0MTU0Zjc4MGQ3MWI5MzBhNmIxNTQ6cDpUOkY) , bringing together representatives from 12 financial institutions to discuss strategies for expanding access to capital for small businesses across the country.


Held June 16, 2026, at ServisFirst Bank's headquarters in Birmingham, the roundtable provided an opportunity for banking leaders and SBA officials to exchange ideas on strengthening SBA lending programs and identifying opportunities to improve the effectiveness and reach of these initiatives. Discussions centered on enhancing access to capital, streamlining lending processes and supporting the continued growth of America's more than 36 million small businesses.


ServisFirst Bank welcomed **Bill Briggs** , Deputy Administrator of the U.S. Small Business Administration, and **John Rich** , Deputy Regional Administrator of the U.S. Small Business Administration, who shared insights into the SBA's current priorities and future direction. Their remarks underscored the importance of strong partnerships between the SBA and financial institutions in helping entrepreneurs access the resources they need to start, grow and sustain their businesses.


> “Community banks and lending partners are essential to fueling local economies, connecting small businesses with the capital they need to succeed,” states Bill Briggs, Deputy Administrator of the U.S. Small Business Administration. “Working together, we can continue to strengthen SBA capital access programs, lower barriers for borrowers, and create jobs. Ensuring entrepreneurs across Alabama, and across the country, have the resources needed to grow and support their communities.”


The event also featured participation from **Brad Bolton** , President, CEO & Senior Leader of Community Spirit Bank and Past Chairman of the Independent Community Bankers of America, who emphasized the value of collaboration among financial institutions and the SBA.


> “Having Deputy Administrator Briggs visit our great state shows the importance that community banks and SBA lending partners play in helping our local economies,” states Brad Bolton, President, CEO & Senior Leader of Community Spirit Bank. “His commitment to meeting directly with lenders and borrowers creates meaningful opportunities to improve SBA programs and better serve small businesses. I appreciate the hospitality that Rodney and the entire staff of ServisFirst Bank showed us as we gathered at their corporate headquarters to discuss important issues and learn how the SBA is modernizing to better serve lenders and borrowers. It was a great day for our state!”


ServisFirst Bank regularly partners with organizations that promote economic development and support entrepreneurs, reinforcing the Bank's commitment to helping businesses of all sizes thrive in the communities it serves.


> “On behalf of everyone at ServisFirst Bank, I want to thank Bill Briggs, John Rich, Brad Bolton, and the U.S. Small Business Administration for partnering with us and making this important discussion possible,” states Rodney Rushing, ServisFirst Bank Executive Vice President and Chief Operating Officer. “We're grateful for everyone who joined us to share ideas and collaborate. Together, we had impactful conversations about strengthening SBA lending programs, improving access to capital and creating more opportunities for the entrepreneurs and small businesses that drive our communities forward.”

The U.S. Small Business Administration helps power the American dream of entrepreneurship. As the leading voice for small businesses within the federal government, the SBA empowers job creators with the resources and support they need to start, grow, and expand their businesses or recover from a declared disaster. It delivers services through an extensive network of SBA field offices and partnerships with public and private organizations.
