#### ***John Mazyck, CEO, The Frazer Lanier Company***


John Mazyck serves as CEO of The Frazer Lanier Company, an Alabama-based investment banking firm. An investment banker since 1999, Mazyck has underwritten public finance issues across the Southeast and has advised numerous domestic and international corporations on debt financings. In addition to his professional work, Mazyck has remained active in civic and business leadership throughout Alabama. He previously served as Chairman of the Business Council of Alabama in 2020, Chairman of the Montgomery Area Committee of 100, and as a board member of the Montgomery Area Chamber of Commerce. He is also a graduate of Leadership Alabama (2019). Mazyck earned a Bachelor of Science degree from Vanderbilt University and an MBA from Auburn University at Montgomery.
