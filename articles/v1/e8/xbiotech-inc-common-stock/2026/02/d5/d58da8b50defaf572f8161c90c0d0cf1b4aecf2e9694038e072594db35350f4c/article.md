# Advancing Infectious Disease Innovation: New Microscopy Insights from Our UT Austin Collaboration

Here at XBiotech, we're pleased to share a significant publication recently released in the *Journal of Microscopy* (DOI: 10.1111/jmi.70038), which highlights our ongoing collaboration with UT Austin to advance our infectious disease pipeline through refined imaging techniques.


This steady progress complements our broader efforts, as our Research and Development team maintains a clear focus on key infectious disease areas such as shingles, C. *difficile* , influenza, S. *aureus* , and JC virus infections, while our Manufacturing and Clinical teams continue their work in oncology and rheumatology as we progress into 2026.
