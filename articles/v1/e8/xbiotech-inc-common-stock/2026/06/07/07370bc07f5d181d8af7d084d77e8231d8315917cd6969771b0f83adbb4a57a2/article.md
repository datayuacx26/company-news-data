# From Lab to Cover: XBiotech Scientists Earn Cover Recognition in the Texas Journal of Microscopy

We’re pleased to highlight a recent achievement from our team: an image co-created by our scientists—a scanning electron micrograph of a *Clostridioides difficile* biofilm fragment—was selected as the cover art for a publication by the Texas Society for Microscopy. This work reflects our ongoing collaboration with University of Texas at Austin, where advanced imaging techniques are being applied to better understand complex infectious processes. The recognition underscores the depth of our scientific capabilities and our continued focus on applying innovative tools to advance programs targeting serious infections, including *Clostridioides difficile* .
