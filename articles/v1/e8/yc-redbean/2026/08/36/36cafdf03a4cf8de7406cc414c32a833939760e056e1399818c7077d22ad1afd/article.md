FAQ

###


What is Redbean?


#


**Redbean is a free mobile sandbox** (iOS and Android, optional IAP), built around character creation and relationship/story-driven gameplay. You can create original characters (OC) or recreate ones you already love, design their pixel town, and play through it: talk to them, do quests and follow storylines you write yourself. A small world that is *yours* , filled with *your characters* and runs on its own. You can also visit towns other players built and meet the characters they made in “Explore” tab.


###


What is Redbean’s challenge, and is it an official Animal Hospital art challenge?


#


**Redbean’s Animal Hospital Town Check-in Challenge** is a community challenge, not an official Animal Hospital or Roblox event. The challenge simply invites fans to turn their Animal Hospital fanart or OC ideas into playable characters with Redbean, visit our Animal Hospital-inspired town and share the results.


Redbean isn’t an official Animal Hospital Anomaly game and has no partnership with Animal Anomaly or Roblox. All Animal Hospital Towns in Redbean are fan-built versions, inspired by the game and characters, and the same Explore tab also holds towns built around other fandoms if you want to keep wandering.


###


**What makes Redbean different from character/OC maker apps?**


#


Some character/OC maker apps are strong for designing characters and customizing detailed features, but the characters stay static and never own a distinct persona or lore. In Redbean, **your OCs don’t just exist, they feel alive:** They can think, talk, move around, visit places, remember what happens, and have relationships with each other. You can also build a living multiverse inside Redbean where your OCs and all your favorite fandom characters could actually live together.


###


Do I need to be a good artist to join?


#


No. A rough sketch or a reference photo, even a Roblox screenshot, is enough to start. Redbean builds the pixel character from whatever image you upload, so the challenge is about the idea, not your drawing skill.


###


Can I submit an original OC or a character from another fandom instead of an Animal Hospital character?


#


Yes. Original character you draw, new anomalies, hospital staff, or any other new OC all count, and so do characters from other fandoms placed alongside your Animal Hospital cast. Several Redbean users already mix casts from different series into one town, so an Animal Hospital character standing next to someone from a completely different show isn’t unusual.
