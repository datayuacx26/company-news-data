#


Start with the pattern the show itself uses. Pick one word tied to your character’s theme, an emotion, a food, a color, and add “-ping” to the end. From there, a Teenieping OC maker like Redbean’s turns that name and theme into a mascot-style design you can keep refining.

Plenty of fans design their own “Royal-style” OC as a tribute, and that’s a fair creative choice. It stays a fan title, not a canon one.
