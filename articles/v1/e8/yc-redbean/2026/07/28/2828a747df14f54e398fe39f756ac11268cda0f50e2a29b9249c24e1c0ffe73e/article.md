##


Roblox’s Animal Hospital OC Maker: Create Your In-Game Class


#


Inside[Roblox Animal Hospital Anomaly](https://www.roblox.com/games/78515283254292/Animal-Hospital) , the closest thing to an official character maker is[the Class system](https://animal-hospital.fandom.com/wiki/Classes) , which players treat as their in-game identity and their role in the hospital. Each Class changes your avatar’s uniform and equips you with unique abilities, stat boosts or starter items to survive hospital shifts.


The game currently features **11 distinct classes** , which you can unlock from the yellow “Classes” circle in the lobby, or buy them using either Animal Coins earned from completed shifts or Robux (Roblox currency).


- **Free-to-Play** (Animal Coins): Intern, Nurse, Secretary, Paramedic, Firefighter, Security, Psychologist, Doctor and Surgeon (considered the best free class, which grants sanity and speed burst)
- **Pay-to-Play** (Robux): **Head Nurse** (190 Robux) gives a massive inventory boost, while **Secret Agent** gives you a powerful gun to combat anomalies.


Picking a Class is really the closest thing to shaping Animal Hospital OC ideas within the base game: an Intern reads as a nervous new hire, a Secret Agent reads as someone who knows more than they let on, a Psychologist reads as someone playing a dangerous game with their own sanity for a bigger payoff. None of this gives you a name, a persona, or a backstory. It is a role you perform for one shift, not a character you keep.


##


Animal Hospital Roblox OC Maker: Customize Your Roblox Avatar


#


The second path is your actual Roblox avatar rather than your in-shift Class, and this is closer to what most people mean by “ *Animal Hospital Roblox OC* “: a character that looks like it belongs in the hospital, that you can screenshot, pose, and use across other Roblox experiences.


To recreate a character from *Animal Hospital* (like Ratthew, Barney, Liz, or a custom hospital staff member) as your actual Roblox avatar, you need to combine the right body base, custom layered clothing, and specific accessories from the[Roblox Marketplace](https://www.roblox.com/catalog) (You can only buy them using **Robux** ).


For example, you can get a “[3D Ratthew](https://www.roblox.com/bundles/154639907343676/Ratthew-Animal-Hospital-Work-2D-3D-Clothing) ” from Animal Hospital with matching gloves, tail, ears for 175 Robux, or browse individual components and accessories from fan-made “Animal Hospital Outfits” or “Animal Hospital Avatars”. Mixing catalog pieces (a rat tail here, a lab coat there) is the fastest way to build an Animal Hospital Roblox OC look without waiting on an official character bundle for every cast member.


The honest limit: an avatar outfit changes how your character looks, not how it acts. It will not talk back, will not remember what you told it, and will not have a personality as defined in the main game lore. For a genuine OC with a unique persona and story, you still have to build on somewhere else.
