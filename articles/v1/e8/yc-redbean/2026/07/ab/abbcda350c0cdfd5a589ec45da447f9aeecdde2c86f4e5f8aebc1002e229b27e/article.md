###


Is there an official Animal Hospital roleplay game?


#


**No** . *Animal Hospital* is a[Roblox survival horror game](https://www.roblox.com/games/78515283254292/Animal-Hospital) with “spot the anomaly” puzzle gameplay (inspired by Phasmophobia) rather than a role-playing game. Besides the main gameplay, the developers haven’t released a dedicated roleplay mode. Most[Animal Hospital roleplay games](https://www.roblox.com/discover/?Keyword=animal%20hospital%20roleplay) currently available are fan-made, built by other Roblox creators.


###


Who is Animal Hospital Rathew?


#


In the Roblox *Animal Hospital* , ******[Ratthew](https://animal-hospital.fandom.com/wiki/Ratthew)****** (a portmanteau of “rat” and “Matthew”) is a central story NPC and a most-beloved character. He acts as a sincere and trustworthy assistant who always warns players of anomalies, unlike other characters who only appear to make demands. With a deep storyline and a cute appearance, he captivates many players, making them want to stick with him even after the game ends.
