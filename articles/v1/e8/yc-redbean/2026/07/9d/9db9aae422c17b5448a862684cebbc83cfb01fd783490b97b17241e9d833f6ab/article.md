Is The Amazing Digital Circus an actual game?


#


**No** . ***The Amazing Digital Circus*** is an[animated dark comedy series](https://youtube.com/@GLITCH) from Glitch Productions about six humans trapped in a circus-themed VR simulation run by a wacky AI. Across nine episodes, it follows Pomni, Jax, Ragatha, Gangle, Kinger, and Zooble as they cope with ringmaster Caine, ending with the finale *“Remember”* released on YouTube and Netflix in June 2026.


Glitch Productions hasn’t announced the official Amazing Digital Circus game on any platform yet. What you’ll find instead is a mix of unofficial fan games, mobile knockoffs that reuse the name with no connection to the show, and image creation tools for “Amazing Digital Circus character creator” that generate character art but aren’t playable.

**The Amazing Digital Circus: Lost Demo**


#


*Platform: Windows (itch.io) · Free (or name your own price)*


This is one of the most polished games played by many TADC fans, built by[Yaman Studios](https://yaman-studios.itch.io/the-amazing-digital-circus-lost-demo) in Unreal Engine 5. You play as Pomni stepping through the circus halls, running short exploration quests, a 3D platformer sequence with Gloink, and a small shop for cosmetic items from Bubble. The gap: Windows-download only, and it requires DX11/12, so older systems, Mac or mobile devices are not compatible.


###


**Digital Circus: Parkour Game**


#


*Platform: Browser (CrazyGames, Playgama) · Free*


A[free browser parkour game](https://www.crazygames.com/game/digital-circus-parkour-game) where you help Pomni navigate levels built by Caine, no download required. It’s one of the more approachable amazing digital circus games if you just want something to click into during a break, built for quick sessions rather than long-term play. The gap: it’s a single mechanic, parkour, with no story branching or character interaction beyond Pomni.


###


**CircusLife RP: A Digital Circus Fan Game**


#


*Platform: Roblox · Free*


[CircusLife RP](https://www.roblox.com/games/15068745965/CircusLife-RP-A-Digital-Circus-Fan-Game) is the most popular TADC game created on Roblox with 20+ locations from the show for open-ended roleplay, including a claw machine, a bowling alley, and the circus grounds themselves, with customizable character morphs. Among Amazing Digital Circus games built by fans, it’s the closest thing to actually living inside the show’s world alongside other people right now. The gap: it runs on Roblox’s chat and emote system standing in for real character dialogue, not scripted or freeform conversation. You can also explore other role-playing games inspired by the show on[Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/discover) .

Can I play Amazing Digital Circus games free on mobile or PC?


#


**Yes** , all Amazing Digital Circus fangames here are free to play directly on your browser or PC, and Redbean is free to play on iOS and Android. Watch out for generic Android and iOS apps like “Amazing Digital Circus Game 3D”: these are made by independent developers and have no connection to the official production.
