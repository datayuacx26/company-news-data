What Is Roblox Animal Hospital? How To Play It on Mobile?


#


**Animal Hospital Anomaly** launched on May 10, 2026 and quickly became[one of Roblox’s biggest horror hits](https://www.eurogamer.net/roblox-animal-hospital-all-characters) within two months. It runs on PC (Windows and macOS), consoles, and mobile (iOS and Android). To play on your phone, install **Roblox** first and search for[“Animal Hospital” from Animal Anomaly](https://www.roblox.com/games/78515283254292/Animal-Hospital) (not other copycat games).


Gameplay centers on check-in duty during a night shift at a veterinary clinic: you guide animal patients to the correct treatment, but some are secretly anomalies, and missing the signs can trigger a haunting that ends your shift. Surviving pays out campaign money plus permanent Animal Coins to buy ten *Classes* in-game.


Its story later reveals a shocking truth about their corporate employer: they’re deliberately infecting animal patients to transform them into lucrative, monstrous anomalies. Assisted by[game characters and NPCs](https://animal-hospital.fandom.com/wiki/Characters) , the player must gather evidence to expose the company’s dark experiments and survive the night.


*The game captures an **addictive mix of multiplayer simulation and survival horror** , a trend that has massive appeal on Roblox (image source: eurogamer)*

Meet Animal Hospital Characters: Ratthew, Dr. Harlow, Ron and Others


#


###


Ratthew


#


[Animal Hospital Ratthew](https://animal-hospital.fandom.com/wiki/Ratthew) is a small black rat with a white snout, two large white buckteeth, and a long curled pink tail. Paranoid but kind-hearted, he is the only character to maintain eye contact with the player and usually warns the player not to trust anyone in the hospital. Little is known about Ratthew’s background, his story centers on Ron and a missing twin sister, Rattina. He appears as an unsolved mystery rather than a jumpscare, the secret he’s hiding is left open enough that the community keeps arguing over what it means.


###


Dr. Harlow


#


**[Dr. Harlow](https://animal-hospital.fandom.com/wiki/Dr._Harlow)** is a light-orange, deer-like doctor in blue scrubs and a white coat, recognizable by his stethoscope, head mirror, and the black suitcase he carries everywhere. He plays as a calm doctor and player’s supervisor, checking in after every shift to grade the player’s performance and opening new areas as the run progresses. While initially seen as a helpful character, lore and updates reveal him as a mysterious figure tied to a dark secret, which makes many fans keep speculating about him.


*From left to right: Dr. Harlow, Ron from Accounting, Ratthew, Barney*


###


Ron from Accounting


#


[Ron from Accounting](https://animal-hospital.fandom.com/wiki/Ron_from_Accounting) is one of the players’ favorite Animal Hospital characters, appearing as a blue bunny with short, wooped-back brown hair. Working in Animal Corporation, he knows about the hospital’s anomalies and casually reveals company secrets, while remaining calm and pragmatic. Carrying a “TOP SECRET” briefcase around, he often complains about his work and seems exhausted with constant headaches. He also shares an uneasy, suspicious relationship with Ratthew, which is tied to the hospital’s dark secrets.


###


Barney


#


Barney appears as a white rabbit, with a mustache and black slit eyes, his[anomaly](https://animal-hospital.fandom.com/wiki/Anomalies) form has a sharp-toothed mouth and big, black hollow eyes. He is a serial killer who randomly walks up and friendly asks the player for a cup of coffee, but turns demanding and threatening if his requests are denied. If you hide Barney without turning him in to Officer Duckman, he will reward you with an extra coffee machine.


###


Officer Duckman


#


Officer Duckman is a minor character in Roblox Animal Hospital who arrives at the reception office after Shift 6 to search for and arrest Barney. He appears as a black, duck-like creature who wears a blue shirt, and little is known about his background. If you choose to turn in Barney, Officer Duckman will yell for you to arrest him. Unlike Barney, Officer Duckman does not reward the player with a gift in exchange for turning Barney in, he simply asks you to report any suspicious activity.


###


Liz


#


Liz appears later in the game (shift 11-12) and reveals more game lore. She is a light-brown fur caracal and disgraced former news reporter, always exaggerative and energetic. Liz used to work for the Animal Corporation, but she was fired for reporting on the Anomaly infection, which the corporation dismissed as “fake news”. She asks the player to help investigate the case and give her proof using a special UV Camera to take some photos of specific patients.


###


Other Roblox Animal Hospital characters


#


The rest of the cast takes on different roles in the hospital’s story and contributes to Roblox Animal Hospital’s unique appeal: **Secretary** and **Sam** are helpful characters who give player sanity every check-in or help player clean slime and get rid of room fires. **Rattina** does not physically appear in the game as an NPC, but she remains a mysterious character as Ratthew’s twin sister and the keeper of the Animal Corporation’s secrets (player can later find a hidden photo of her during the game)

Frequently Asked Questions About Roblox Animal Hospital Anomaly


#


###


**Is Roblox Animal Hospital free on mobile?**


#


**Yes** . The game runs on both iOS and Android, and you need a Roblox account to play the game. This game is completely free to play on Roblox (which is also free to download). Optional in-game purchases are available using **Robux** (Roblox’s currency).


###


**Can I play Animal Hospital without Roblox?**


#


**No** . Animal Hospital is a Roblox experience, so it requires the Roblox app or platform account to play, though Roblox itself is available on PC, consoles, and mobile.


###


Is Roblox Animal Hospital a role-playing game?


#


**No** . Roblox Animal Hospital is a survival horror game inspired by titles like *Scary Shawarma Kiosk* and *Dandy’s World* , with “spot the anomaly” puzzle gameplay – you check in animal patients and spot disguised monsters (like the Skinwalker or Stalker) before they attack you. It’s also a **multiplayer** game where you can team up with friends, though you can experience its secrets on your own.


###


**Beginner tips on how to play Roblox Animal Hospital Anomaly**


#


Here are some Roblox Animal Hospital practical gameplay tips to help you manage sanity, detect anomalies and deal with NPCs:


- Grab **coffee** at the start of every shift (refills ~3 minutes); take 2 sips before handing it to NPCs who need it (e.g Barney) to keep your Sanity buff, and coffee can also extinguish candles during rituals.
- Revolvers, tasers, and fire extinguishers can handle most anomalies; extinguishers also clear slime fast and reveal invisible ghosts (but cannot kill), and you can give the **wrong medicine** to kill anomaly patients instead of refusing them.
- Carefully inspect **eyes, ears, face, and seizures** —small differences often reveal anomalies, even patients on fire. **Black eyes** or **abnormal seizures** = guaranteed anomaly; confirm before inspecting, since a cursed shot costs -10 Sanity.
- Some disguises (like Walker) are extremely convincing, so monitor suspicious behavior rather than appearance alone.
- Specific anomalies need specific counters: syrup or organs for the under-the-bed monster, coffee for the head-banging one, and ignoring ceiling eyes.
- Always help Barney instead of reporting him for a reward (a coffee machine).


###


**Is Animal Hospital Ratthew an anomaly?**


#


**No** . **Ratthew** is **not** an anomaly, even though he sometimes behaves strangely and his prolonged eye contact with the camera reads as unsettling. Among main Animal Hospital characters, Barney is an anomaly, but he’s harmless to players. You can find[instructions on detecting anomalies in the game here.](https://www.ign.com/wikis/animal-hospital-roblox/All_Anomalies)


###


What is the relationship between Ratthew and Rattina?


#


They are twin rat siblings in the game. Rattina worked for the Animal Corporation before vanishing without explanation, and Ratthew is on a quest to find her, which is tied to an unresolved mystery that fans are still theorizing about.
