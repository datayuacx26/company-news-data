**How Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream’s Mii Character Creation works?**


#


*[Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream](https://miiwiki.org/wiki/Tomodachi_Life:_Living_the_Dream)* puts players in the role of a ‘God’ – or a caretaker: populate an island with your own Mii characters, decide Miis look and personality, then watch them build relationships, develop rivalries, and stumble into situations entirely on their own. The game supports up to **70 Miis** total.


The Tomodachi Mii Maker allows you to create your Miis in two modes: **“Get Help”** generates Miis based on your answers, while **“From Scratch”** opens the full Mii Editor to customize each feature: skin tone, eyes, nose, hair, height, voice, etc. You can also create new Miis based on any existing island Mii. *Living the Dream* expanded the Tomodachi Mii Maker beyond the original game, adding pronouns, gender options and same-sex dating preferences. For step-by-step Tomodachi Miis tutorials, you can check out[Nintendo’s official guide here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v3sc_bYLXJ8) .


*Customize each feature of your Mii from face shape, eyes, nose, to skin color, hair and more (image: game8)*


*Besides the name, you can set your Mii’s birthday, height, voice and each of their relatives on the island *(image: game8)**


###


How To Make Your Miis Look Exactly the Way You Imagined?


#


Most *Tomodachi Life Miis* come together fine through the standard editor, but **Face Paint** is where *Tomodachi Life* character creation really opens up. *Easy Mode* applies flat color blocks; *Advanced Mode* gives you a blank canvas to draw Mii characters that match your Tomodachi Life Mii ideas, though it can look daunting to non-artists or those who demand accurate, realistic clones of their favorite fictional characters.


*You can draw anything you want on your Mii’s face in Face Paint, whether it’s a unique marking, non-human detail or unusual eye shape (image: game8)*


*Tomodachi fans enjoy bringing their favorite celebrities and anime/movie characters into the game (image: ScreenRant)*


Advice from community members for **creating your own Tomodachi Life Miis** : proportions matter most (face shape, eye and nose position, body scale). You can read the megathread on Mii creation guides/tutorials/tips[here](https://www.reddit.com/r/tomodachilife/comments/1se5rql/mii_creation_masterpostmegathread_tutorials/) , or get more Mii inspiration with player-made Mii characters on[TomodachiShare](https://tomodachishare.com/) or[TomodachiMiis](https://tomodachimiis.com/) .

**What is Tomodachi Life Mii Personality and How It Affects Your Mii?**


#


A[Tomodachi Life Mii Personality Chart/Guide](https://www.reddit.com/r/tomodachilife/comments/1s3y1vf/new_personality_chartguide_for_ltd/) shaped by community has summarized **16 distinct personality types** for your Tomodachi Life Miis, categorized into four main groups (Easy-Going, Energetic, Reserved, and Confident). You can set each Mii personality through five sliders: Movement, Speech, Energy, Thinking, and Overall (only affects their dreams), and your choice will influence each character’s quirks, their dialogue style, and relationship tendencies – from friendship and romance to rivalries.


*In Tomodachi Life Mii personality setting, you can only see its personality after you are done creating, which means you **cannot adjust it later***

Can I play Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream if I can’t draw?


#


**Yes** . Impressive Tomodachi Life works get shared most on social media, making the game look more drawing-dependent than it actually is. *Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream* is meant to be a playground with your unique Mii characters. Optional Face Paint tools add good details to your Miis, but if you’re a non-artistic player, you can still use the Standard Editor with basic features.


###


**Can I import a photo or sketch to create my Mii?**


#


**No** . *TL: Living the Dream* has no photo or sketch import, Miis are built from scratch in the editor. Some 3rd-party tools help map pixel colors from artwork into the Face Paint grid, useful for detailed work but complicated and time-consuming to set up.


If recreating specific fictional characters or uploading your OC’s sketch is what you’re after,[Redbean](https://www.redbean.ai/) supports it: in-game pixel character looks are built directly from a reference image.


*Redbean is a free mobile game with pixel character creation, players can upload their OCs or recreate any fav characters from an image ([Redbean Character Gallery](https://www.redbean.ai/characters) )*

**How do I get my Mii to look accurate when recreating a favorite character?**


#


Keep a reference image open while you build. Face Paint’s Advanced Mode handles what the standard tools can’t capture. You can also check out some recreation guides on[TomodachiShare](https://tomodachishare.com/) or[TomodachiMiis](https://tomodachimiis.com/) for specific characters. (not affiliated with Nintendo) These also showcase Tomodachi Life Mii ideas for inspiration, and you can share your own Mii character designs.

###


**Can I create my original characters in Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream?**


#


**Yes** . The Tomodachi Mii Maker tools work well for OCs. For creators who have their characters living in their heads for years, *Living the Dream* is currently the best game to thrust their OCs into a world and see how they interact with each other. However, Tomodachi only has adjustable sliders based on pre-existing personalities, with no space for written lore or backstory.

**Platform** ***:** Android, browser |* **Price** ***:** free to play*
