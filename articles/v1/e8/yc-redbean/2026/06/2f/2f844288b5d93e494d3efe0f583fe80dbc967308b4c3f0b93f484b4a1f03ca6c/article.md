**BitLife: Write Your Character’s Life Story From Scratch**


#


*Platform: iOS & Android | Price: Free (optional Bitizenship in-app) | Google Play*


[BitLife](https://goodgamestudios.com/company/press/releases/72-million-virtual-lives-in-one-year-bitlife-impresses-internationally/) is a text-based life simulator by Candywriter LLC, free on Android. You start with a character (named, gendered, born in a country of your choosing) and make every decision from that point: what to study, who to date, whether to pursue a stable career or take risks. The life unfolds through text choices, year by year, from childhood through old age.


*BitLife life simulator on Android with text-based character choices and life decisions (image source: Goodgamestudios)*


In fact, what it gets right is narrative ownership: no preset story, just the life you chose to build. In BitLife, your character doesn’t develop a personality independently, you choose every outcome. While BitLife offers an engaging text-based life simulation experience, it doesn’t really stand out as one of[iOS or Android games similar to Tomodachi Life](https://www.redbean.ai/blog/mobile-games-like-tomodachi-life) for fans who value character customization and autonomy.

**Gacha Life 2: Build Your Anime Cast and Tell Their Stories**


#


*Platform: iOS & Android | *Free (with IAP)* |[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details/Gacha_Life_2?id=air.com.lunime.gachalife2)*


**Gacha Life 2** is a free anime character creation and scene-building game by Lunime, available on Android. You can create up to 20 characters with deep visual customization: outfits, hairstyles, expressions, and poses. The Studio mode lets you build multi-scene stories, place your characters in backgrounds, and attach custom chat bubbles to them.


*Among free mobile games like Tomodachi Life, Gacha Life is the strongest pick for visual creation*


For players who loved defining every aspect of how each Tomodachi resident (Miis) looked, **Gacha Life 2** goes deep in the visual customization with a wide range of anime-style options. However, it’s more like a dress-up game, not a cozy life simulator. Your characters don’t develop personalities or carry memory between scenes, they remain figures waiting to be posed. For players who want a free *Tomodachi Life Android alternative* where characters themselves feel alive or interaction depth, it stops short.
