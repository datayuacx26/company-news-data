# Immunome to Present at the Goldman Sachs 47th Annual Global Healthcare Conference

BOTHELL, Wash. –


**[Immunome](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fimmunome.com%2F&esheet=54542502&newsitemid=20260527728826&lan=en-US&anchor=Immunome&index=1&md5=6c04a5b4952e08839b53d84589f183a1) ,** Inc. (Nasdaq: IMNM), a biotechnology company focused on developing first-in-class and best-in-class targeted cancer therapies, today announced that members of Immunome’s management will present at the Goldman Sachs 47th Annual Global Healthcare Conference on June 9, 2026, at 10:40 a.m. EDT.


Interested parties can access the live audio webcast for this conference from the Investor Relations section of the company’s website at[www.immunome.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.immunome.com&esheet=54542502&newsitemid=20260527728826&lan=en-US&anchor=www.immunome.com&index=2&md5=f61a376aa8a9c1fb8f2ec66296bd6c01) . The webcast replay will be available after the conclusion of the live presentation for approximately 30 days.


**About Immunome, Inc.**


Immunome is a clinical-stage targeted oncology company committed to developing first-in-class and best-in-class targeted cancer therapies. We are advancing an innovative portfolio of therapeutics, supported by a leadership team with deep experience in the design, development, and commercialization of cutting-edge therapies, including antibody-drug conjugates. Our pipeline includes varegacestat, an investigational gamma secretase inhibitor for which an NDA has been submitted to the U.S. FDA; IM-1021, a clinical-stage ROR1 ADC; IM-3050, an IND-cleared FAP-targeted radiotherapy; and IM-1617, an IND-cleared solid tumor ADC. We are also advancing a broad portfolio of early-stage ADCs pursuing undisclosed solid tumor targets.
