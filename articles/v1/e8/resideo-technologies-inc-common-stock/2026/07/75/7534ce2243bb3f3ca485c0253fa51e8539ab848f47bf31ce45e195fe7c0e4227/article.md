**SCOTTSDALE, Ariz.,** Feb. 20, 2026 –[Resideo Technologies](https://www.resideo.com/us/en/?utm_source=pr&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=IBS_industry_award_nr) , a leading global provider of home comfort, safety and security solutions, announced it received two industry awards for the[Honeywell Home ElitePRO™ smart thermostat](https://www.resideo.com/us/en/pro/elitepro-smart-thermostat-series/?utm_source=pr&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=IBS_industry_award_nr) and the[Honeywell Home Whole Home Dehumidifier,](https://www.resideo.com/us/en/pro/whole-home-dehumidifier/?utm_source=pr&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=IBS_industry_award_nr) underscoring the company’s continued leadership and innovation in smart home and indoor air quality technology. The awards were presented in conjunction with the 2026 International Builders’ Show.


The Honeywell Home portfolio received:


- [Pro Builder MVP Award](https://www.probuilder.com/products/mvp-awards/article/55355696/pro-builder-magazines-8th-annual-most-value-product-mvp-awards) for the Honeywell Home ElitePRO smart thermostat
- [NAHB Global Innovation Silver Award](https://online.flippingbook.com/view/929702067/90-91/) for the Honeywell Home Whole Home Dehumidifier


Together, the awards highlight a new era of innovation for Honeywell Home - the brand that invented the thermostat - and continues to set the standard for comfort, performance and reliability in connected home solutions.


“These industry awards are powerful validation of our commitment to innovation that serves our HVAC technicians and our builder partners,” said Pat Tessier, Vice President of Product Development for Honeywell Home and First Alert solutions at Resideo. “From advanced contractor tools to next-generation indoor air quality solutions, our award-winning Honeywell Home innovations are redefining comfort in ways that are practical, powerful and designed for real-world use.”


## ElitePRO Smart Thermostat Awarded for Elevating Home Comfort


The Honeywell Home ElitePRO smart thermostat was honored with the Pro Builder MVP Award for going beyond the expectations of a thermostat to provide an intuitive user interface on a sleek, five-inch touchscreen. Designed to help contractors deliver next-level comfort and convenience, the ElitePRO S1200 smart thermostat streams live video from compatible doorbells, offers seamless indoor air quality (IAQ) monitoring and control, and is ideal for customers seeking a stylish, smart and energy-efficient solution.


## Whole Home Dehumidifier Honored for Innovative Flexibility


The Honeywell Home Whole Home Dehumidifier won the NAHB Global Innovation Silver Award after being re-designed from the ground up to deliver the ultimate performance, efficiency and quality. The Honeywell Home dehumidifier features a tri-directional outlet for flexible, tight-space professional installation. It offers serviceable components, advanced diagnostics to help eliminate guesswork during a service call and the 65-PPD unit is the only product in its class with ENERGY STAR® certification.
