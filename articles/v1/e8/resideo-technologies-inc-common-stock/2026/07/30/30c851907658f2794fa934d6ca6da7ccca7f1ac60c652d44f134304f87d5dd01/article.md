# Resideo Recognized for Award-Winning Training and Loyalty Programs

Resideo, a leading global provider of home comfort and security solutions, was recently recognized by the Association for Talent Development (ATD) and Loyalty360 for its continued leadership in Pro enablement and customer training programs.


## Award-winning Training


Resideo won an[ATD Excellence in Practice Award](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/atd-recognizes-exceptional-achievements-in-talent-development-302775089.html) during the ATD International Conference & Exposition in Los Angeles held during May 17-20, 2026. This global program recognizes organizations that deliver impactful and innovative talent development initiatives.[Resideo Academy](https://www.resideo.com/us/en/pro/resources/resideo-academy/) earned honors for driving measurable business results through high-quality training and development programs. Resideo Academy delivers expert-led training for HVAC, plumbing, and security professionals through online, in-person, and on-site options. Courses are built to expand product knowledge, increase efficiency, and reduce callbacks by focusing on real-world applications and practical learning.


“This recognition from ATD reflects our commitment to helping our professional partners grow through meaningful engagement and world-class learning experiences,” said Jamie Quanrud, Learning Solutions Senior Manager at Resideo. “We remain focused on delivering programs that not only strengthen contractor expertise, but also support their long-term success.”


## Innovative Loyalty Program


Additionally, Resideo was honored during the[2026 Loyalty360 Awards](https://loyalty360.org/content-gallery/daily-news/2026-loyalty360-awards-winners-recognized-at-loyalty-expo) ceremony on May 18th, 2026, for its innovative customer experience and loyalty strategies. The company’s[Resideo Pro PERKS™](https://www.resideo.com/us/en/pro/resideo-pro-perks/) program is a tier-based loyalty program for HVAC contractors that combines financial and non-financial benefits, unlocking more value as members move up tiers.[First Alert® Premier Pro Partner Program](https://www.resideo.com/us/en/pro/Security-dealer/) is an invitation-based program for security dealers that provides sales and marketing tools, co-op advertising support, lead generation opportunities, and expert guidance to support business planning and sales strategies, helping drive business growth.


“Industry recognition is meaningful because it reflects the impact that our programs have on the contractors we serve every day,” said Brad Pierson, Senior Channel Marketing Manager at Resideo. “Our loyalty programs are designed to go beyond rewards by helping pros grow their businesses, increase visibility and build long-term success.”


These recognitions reinforce Resideo’s leadership in building strong partner relationships while investing in the skills and capabilities that support growth across its professional ecosystem.
