# Resideo Debuts Leviton Smart Lighting Integration at Design & Construction Week 2026


## New integration delivers seamless lighting control, automation, and design flexibility for homeowners and builders


[Company News](https://www.resideo.com/us/en/corporate/newsroom/all-articles/#f:tags=[Company%20News])


[Smart Home](https://www.resideo.com/us/en/corporate/newsroom/all-articles/#f:tags=[Smart%20Home])


2/17/26


**SCOTTSDALE, Ariz., Feb.17, 2026** –[Resideo Technologies](https://www.resideo.com/us/en/) , a leading global provider of solutions for home comfort, security, and safety, announced a new platform integration with Leviton smart lighting controls into the[First Alert® app](https://www.resideo.com/us/en/first-alert-app/) during[Design & Construction Week 2026](https://designandconstructionweek.com/) . The integration expands the app’s ability to manage lighting alongside[First Alert smart smoke and carbon monoxide alarms](https://www.firstalert.com/pages/sc5-smart-smoke-co-detection?utm_source=Newsroom&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=leviton_announcement) ,[water leak detectors](https://www.resideo.com/us/en/pro/lseries/?utm_source=Newsroom&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=leviton_announcement) , and[security cameras](https://www.resideo.com/us/en/pro/vx-series/?utm_source=Newsroom&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=leviton_announcement) ,[Honeywell Home smart thermostats](https://www.honeywellhome.com/products/elitepro-s1200-smart-thermostat?utm_source=Newsroom&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=leviton_announcement) , Schlage and Kwikset smart locks, and Genie smart garage door openers – giving homeowners a single app to control and monitor their home.


“We’ve designed Resideo's First Alert app to bring smart home solutions together into one seamless platform – without the need for a hub,” said Pat Tessier, Vice President of Product Development for First Alert and Honeywell Home solutions at Resideo. “This lighting integration with Leviton gives builders a flexible, easy-to-deploy option for connected homes and gives homeowners the convenience of managing comfort, safety, security, and lighting from a single app.”


The enhanced app experience continues to expand as a unified smart home platform and helps[builders](https://www.resideo.com/us/en/pro/builder-solutions/?utm_source=Newsroom&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=leviton_announcement) streamline installation and everyday use for homeowners while enabling intuitive control and automation across key smart home categories.


Through the integration, homeowners can manage compatible Leviton smart lighting products directly from the First Alert app, as well as major voice assistants, adding convenient automation and personalization to everyday living. Lighting solutions available include:


- Leviton Decora Smart Wi-Fi Dimmer
- Leviton Decora Smart Wi-Fi Switch
- Leviton Decora Evolve Smart Switch and Smart Dimmer Modules


“Leviton is proud to bring a leading smart lighting control company to the First Alert app,” said James Shurte, Lighting Controls Product Manager at Leviton. “This integration gives homeowners and builders flexible, easy-to-install and use options for connected lighting.”


The First Alert app and new lighting integration will be showcased at the annual Design & Construction Week in Orlando, Feb. 17–19, at booth #S8085, along with the new[Honeywell Home ElitePRO™ smart thermostats](https://www.resideo.com/us/en/pro/elitepro-smart-thermostat-series/) and[Honeywell Home Whole Home Dehumidifier](https://www.honeywellhome.com/products/whole-home-dehumidifier?variant=43065186877542&utm_source=Newsroom&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=leviton_announcement) . For more information, visit[Resideo.com](https://www.resideo.com/us/en/) .
