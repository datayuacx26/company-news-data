# How to Open a USD Account as a Freelancer or Remote Worker from Bangladesh

### Introduction


This article explains how to open a USD digital account as a Bangladeshi freelancer so you can receive USD payments from clients for free and easily send money home to your local BDT account for only $1. By the end of this guide, you’ll understand step-by-step how to manage USD payments in a simple and transparent way.


### Why it Matters


If you're a Bangladeshi freelancer working directly with clients or offering your services on platforms like Fiverr, Upwork, Freelancer.com, PeoplePerHour or others, you know the struggle. Clients want to pay you in USD, but without a USD account, you may not be able to hold USD directly. As a result, funds are often converted to BDT (Bangladeshi Taka) for rent, groceries, and other expenses, often losing extra money in conversion fees.


### Get your free USD Account


The steps below show you how to open a free USD digital account using only your ID, without complicated paperwork or in-person visits. This account allows you to:


- Receive USD ACH transfers for free. ACH transfers are a quick, secure, and cost-free way to receive USD payments from clients or platforms.
- Send money home to a BDT account for only $1. Transfers can be sent to Bangladeshi bank accounts or supported mobile wallets including JazzCash, bKash, or traditional banks. (Check our Local payout guide for Bangladesh)
- Spend online in BDT, USD, EUR, or GBP with a multicurrency debit card.
- Access an optional investment feature that allows users to Invest in U.S. stocks, bonds, and ETFs.


### Step-by-Step Guide


#### 1. Download the nsave App


Open the App Store or Google Play Store and download the “nsave” app.


#### 2. Sign Up with Your Phone Number


Create your nsave account using your email address and phone number. The sign-up process typically takes a few minutes.


#### 3. Verify Your Identity with Your ID or Passport


Verify your identity using your national ID or passport to activate your USD digital account. This step usually takes less than five minutes.


#### 4. Get Paid in USD


Share your account details with your employer or receive transfers from platforms such as Fiverr, Upwork, Truelancer or others. For the most common freelancer platforms, we’ve created separate step-by-step payment guides to help you receive USD payments smoothly. You can find them \[here\].


By opening a verified USD account with nsave, freelancers from Bangladesh can receive USD payments, send money home to their BDT account for only $1, and manage international payments securely and efficiently using a single digital platform.


### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


1. Can freelancers receive USD payments directly from clients? Yes. With a verified USD account, freelancers can receive USD payments directly from clients or platforms through ACH transfers. nsave does not charge a fee for receiving ACH payments. ‍


2. What documents do I need to open a USD account? A valid ID or passport is required to verify your identity and activate your account. ‍


3. How long does it take to open the account? The entire process takes under 10 minutes, about three minutes to sign up and five minutes for ID verification. ‍


4. How can I send money to my local BDT account? Once USD is received, funds can be converted and sent to a Bangladeshi BDT account or supported mobile wallet for only $1. ‍


5. Can I use the account for online spending & subscriptions or investing? Yes. You can use the multicurrency debit card to spend online in BDT, USD, EUR, or GBP, and even invest in U.S. stocks, bonds, and ETFs without any fees.
