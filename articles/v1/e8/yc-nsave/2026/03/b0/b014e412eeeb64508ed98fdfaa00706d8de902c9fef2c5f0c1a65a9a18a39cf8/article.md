# The Global Payroll Revolution: Pay International Teams in USD or Local Currency for $0


Jonas Auernhammer


Mar 4, 2026


As teams become more distributed, paying people across countries has become a practical challenge for many founders.


You may have:


- One developer based in Nairobi
- A small team spread across three countries
- Or a larger group working across multiple markets


The question remains the same: how do you pay everyone efficiently and reliably?


For many businesses, existing options introduce cost delays, or complexity that scale poorly. This is often referred to as the global Payroll problem, particularly for businesses earning internationally but operating from emerging markets.


### The Cost of Current Solutions


Let’s look at a common example.


You run an agency based in Lagos. Your team includes:


- A developer in Karachi ($2,000/month)
- A designer in Accra ($2,000/month)
- A project manager in Cairo ($1,500/month)


Monthly payroll: $5,500 ‍‍


Option 1: Wire transfers


- 3 wire transfers × $40 each = $120 per month
- Settlement times of 3–5 business days
- Additional correspondent banking fees may apply


Estimated annual cost: $1,440, excluding indirect or hidden fees ‍‍


Option 2: Payoneer batch payouts


- Payoneer charges per transaction, no standard batch pricing for SMBs
- Each person's withdrawal: ~2.75% FX markup
- $5,500 × 2.75% = $151.25/month
- Local receiving bank fees may also apply


Estimated annual cost: $1,815 or more


Over time, these costs accumulate. For a growing business, payroll processing can become a meaningful operational expense rather than a background task.


### What Global Payroll Is Expected to Provide


In principle, global payroll should allow businesses to:


- Pay people in multiple countries from a single account
- Pay in their choice of currency USD or local currency
- Pay reliably and on time, maintaining a predictable schedule
- Pay with minimal or no fees, reducing reliance on manual wires or repeated conversions


In practice, achieving this depends heavily on the underlying payment infrastructure.


### The Revolution: $0 Cost Global Payroll


Imagine you could pay your entire international team for essentially no cost. This isn't wishful thinking. This is possible when you build local payout rails specifically for payroll.


In this setup:


- Team members do not need to join the same platform
- Funds are sent from the business account to local accounts
- Currency conversion, where required, happens as part of the payout flow


Fees, settlement times, and availability vary by country and payment method, but this approach can reduce the friction associated with traditional wires or platform withdrawals.


### Why This Matters Operationally


The most visible impact is cost. Reducing per-payment fees can add up over a year.


Less visible, but equally important, are operational effects:


- Fewer delays reduce the need for manual follow-ups
- Predictable payment timing supports planning on both sides
- Administrative effort around payroll is reduced


Over time, smoother payroll processes can make it easier to manage distributed teams without payroll logistics becoming a constraint. But the deeper benefit is team morale and reliability.


When payments are fast, reliable, and predictable, your team trusts you. They know they'll be paid on time, in their local currency, without hassle.


### What This Changes


With a more streamlined global payroll setup:


- Hiring across different markets becomes easier to evaluate
- Teams can be paid using familiar local banking arrangements
- Payroll processing requires less manual oversight
- Cash-flow planning becomes clearer


Rather than being a recurring source of friction, payroll becomes a more predictable part of operations.
