If you work with international clients, you’ve probably heard the term ACH. ACH (Automated Clearing House) is one of the most common ways to send and receive payments within the United States. Many companies, payroll platforms, and freelance marketplaces prefer ACH because it’s relatively fast and cost-effective.


However, for freelancers in Pakistan, getting access to ACH payment details used to be difficult.


Today, platforms like nsave allow freelancers and remote workers to open a USD account with ACH payment details, making it easier to receive payments from US clients.


This guide explains how ACH works and how freelancers in Pakistan can get started.


### What Is an ACH Account?


An ACH account functions similarly to a US bank account and includes:


- An ACH routing number
- An account number


These details allow you to receive USD payments from:


- US clients
- Payroll platforms
- Freelance marketplaces
- Agencies or remote companies


For many businesses, ACH is one of the simplest ways to send payments directly in USD.


### Why Freelancers in Pakistan Use ACH


Freelancers often prefer ACH payments for several reasons:


Receive payments directly in USD: ‍Funds can arrive in USD instead of being automatically converted to PKR.


Compatible with major platforms: ‍Many global platforms support ACH transfers, including Fiverr, Upwork, and Deel.


Lower transfer costs compared to SWIFT


Flexibility to convert or withdraw funds later


This gives freelancers more control over when and how they convert their earnings.
