# How to send money from upwork to nsave

### Introduction


If you are a freelancer or remote worker getting paid through Upwork, you can withdraw your earnings directly into your nsave account. This is especially useful if you work internationally, live abroad, or need a stable place to receive and hold your money in USD or other foreign currencies.


This guide explains the step by step process on how to transfer your Upwork payout to nsave, using either ACH or Wire Transfer, what details you’ll need, how long it takes, and common questions freelancers ask when connecting Upwork to nsave.


By the end, you’ll know exactly how to set up your payment method and receive your earnings smoothly.


Why Upwork Freelancers Use nsave


#### Freelancers rely on nsave because it gives them:


- A reliable way to receive payments in USD, for free
- Easy conversions when they need local currency
- Low transfer fees when sending money home
- Stability compared to home-country banking systems
- A simple, mobile-first experience for holding and managing earnings


### Step-by-Step Guide: How to Send Money From Upwork to nsave


#### 1. Log in to Upwork and open “Get Paid”


Inside your Upwork dashboard, go to:


Settings → Get Paid → Add Payment Method.


This is where you choose how Upwork will send your earnings.


#### 2. Select the payment method


Upwork supports several withdrawal methods, but the two options that work with nsave are:


- ACH (Direct to US Bank)
- Wire Transfer (USD via SWIFT)


Both methods work; the right one depends on what you prefer.


#### 3. Open your nsave app and find your USD account details


Go to your USD account inside the nsave app.


Choose the details that match the payment method you plan to use on Upwork.


- ACH (Routing + Account Number)
- Wire Transfer (IBAN / SWIFT)


Copy these from your nsave account and paste them exactly into Upwork.


#### 4. Confirm your withdrawal method on Upwork


Review the information and click Confirm.


Upwork may ask you to verify the method by email.


Once confirmed, your nsave account becomes an official withdrawal option for your Upwork earnings.


#### 5. Withdraw your earnings into nsave


Go to: Get Paid → Withdraw Now


Choose your nsave transfer method and confirm the payout.


Processing times:


- ACH: Usually 1–3 business days
- Wire: Usually 2–10 business days


Your USD will arrive directly into your nsave account balance.


### Summary


Sending money from Upwork to nsave is simple:


- Go to Upwork → Get Paid
- Choose ACH or Wire Transfer
- Copy the matching nsave USD account details
- Confirm the payment method
- Withdraw earnings to nsave anytime


Connecting Upwork to nsave gives freelancers and remote workers a fast, reliable, and stable way to receive international earnings. Whether you withdraw via ACH or Wire Transfer, you can hold your funds in USD, convert when it’s best for you, and send money home easily.
