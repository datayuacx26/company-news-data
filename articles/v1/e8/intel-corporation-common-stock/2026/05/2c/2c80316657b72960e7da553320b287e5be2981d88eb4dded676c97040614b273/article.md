# Intel at Computex 2026: Advancing the Next Era of AI-Driven Computing

## Intel outlines progress across the AI compute ecosystem—highlighting open platforms, partners and real world momentum from silicon to software to systems.


In this article:


SANTA CLARA, Calif., May 5, 2026 – At Computex 2026, Intel will share its vision for the next era of AI-driven computing and spotlight the ecosystem progress powering it.


Intel’s presence – anchored by a keynote from CEO Lip‑Bu Tan on June 2 – underscores momentum across compute, from AI PCs to the edge, data center, and cloud. It also shows how silicon innovation, open platforms, and strong ecosystem collaboration help customers deploy and scale AI with confidence.


As AI workloads scale across the compute continuum, Intel will reinforce the resurgence of the CPU as a critical engine for AI – complementing GPUs and accelerators to enable powerful, efficient, and scalable systems. Intel will also highlight progress in silicon innovation and the enduring role of x86 as a foundational architecture for AI across the world’s largest installed base and broadest ecosystem.


Building on this platform foundation, Intel leaders and ecosystem partners at Computex will demonstrate how Intel technologies align at the system level—helping customers deploy AI more efficiently from the client to the data center.


Computex 2026 attendees are invited to tune into the keynote online, engage with Intel leaders and ecosystem partners through invitation‑only sessions and demonstrations, and explore Intel technologies in action.
