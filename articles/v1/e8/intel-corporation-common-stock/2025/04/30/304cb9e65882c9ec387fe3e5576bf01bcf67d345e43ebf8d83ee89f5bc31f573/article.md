# Intel Foundry Direct Connect Keynote: Earning Customers’ Trust (Replay)

## See Intel Foundry leaders and Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan speak to customers and partners about progress and priorities for the company’s foundry offerings.


In this article:


Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan delivered an opening keynote to more than 1,000 customers and partners today at Intel Foundry Direct Connect 2025. Tan outlined Intel Foundry’s progress and priorities as the company drives the next phase of its foundry strategy. He was joined onstage by ecosystem partners Cadence Design Systems, Siemens EDA and Synopsys – all to highlight Intel Foundry’s collaboration in serving customers.


Naga Chandrasekaran, Intel Foundry chief technology and operations officer, and Kevin O’Buckley, general manager of Foundry Services, also delivered keynotes during the morning session, sharing process and advanced packaging news while highlighting Intel Foundry’s global manufacturing and supply chain.
