# Introducing Mozart AI

‎We’re excited to introduce Mozart AI to the world. Over the past few years, Generative AI has made significant strides in enhancing workflow efficiency across various tasks such as drafting documents or emails, organizing notes, and even writing SQL and interpreting results. To enable our customers to fully take advantage of the power of AI in their data workflows, we are incorporating OpenAI’s GPT model into the Mozart Data product.


## **What is Mozart AI?**


Mozart Data brings an **AI-powered, all-in-one data platform experience** via Mozart AI. This initiative is designed to incorporate AI in a way that is helpful by ensuring the interactions are appropriate to every customer’s data engineering, analysis, and management processes. What truly differentiates Mozart AI is its **context awareness** . By leveraging the metadata stored in the customer’s data warehouse, Mozart AI can make custom experiences that are unique to each customer’s needs. This ensures that every interaction is tailored, relevant, and beneficial.


##


### **Check out a suite of AI-powered features available in the Mozart Data platform in four key initial areas:**


**1.** Meet **AI Assistant** , a bot designed to interact with users in creating useful SQL queries for data analysis or modeling (data transformation). AI Assistant indexes all the metadata from the existing tables and views in your data warehouse to generate accurate SQL outputs tailored to your needs. **Simply ask a question. Get instant answers.**


‎ **Use-case examples**


Use-case Description Examples


Data Analysis Describe a metric or analyze trends Please analyze the monthly marketing spends on Facebook Ads and Google Ads and report on the ROI (e.g. clicks, visits)


Data Modeling Create a table for recurring use-cases Build a SQL query that returns row-level information about each user and their product usage trends and demographic information from \[insert table name(s)\]


Edit SQL Query Add, remove, or change columns or logic on an existing query Add date_of_birth and is_active as new columns to the query. A user is considered active if they last signed in within the past 7 days


Optimize SQL Query Improve the SQL for efficiency Make edits to the query to optimize the run-time while returning the same results


**2. Fix SQL:** Can’t figure out why your query is not running? Mozart AI takes the guesswork out of resolving SQL errors by providing immediate fixes to your queries with just a simple click. **Never get stuck with a bug again.**


‎


**3. AI Table Description:** This initial feature allows users to quickly draft description about the tables in your data warehouse. This feature generates useful descriptions about the customer’s tables based on important metadata, such as the query as well as the relevant information about the upstream and downstream tables. Stop spending your valuable time creating detailed descriptions. **Automate high-quality documentation of your tables!**


‎ **4. Explain SQL** analyzes your SQL queries and generates concise, easy-to-understand summaries. We aim to facilitate better communication between technical and non-technical team members by providing accessible summaries, so any data users within the company can quickly grasp the purpose and actions of any SQL query without needing deep technical expertise or contextual background. **Save time by eliminating the need for manual explanations to your stakeholders or team members.**


## ‎Looking Ahead: The Future of Mozart AI


As we continue to invest in our suite of AI features, we are actively exploring new ways to integrate AI into various facets of our product and enhance the overall data experience. Our commitment remains in three key areas:


- **Productivity:** Enable users to significantly reduce the time spent on manual tasks, allowing them to focus on more valuable activities and move faster.
- **Helpfulness:** Bridge the skills and knowledge gaps by addressing everyday workflow pain points. Our AI features are designed to provide immediate support and solutions, making complex tasks more manageable and accessible for all users.
- **Usefulness:** In a landscape filled with AI hype, our priority is to ensure that the Mozart AI experience is genuinely beneficial and relevant to your business needs. We focus on delivering practical, impactful AI solutions that enhance your operations.


As we look to the future, we remain dedicated to advancing AI technologies that drive productivity, usefulness, and overall value for our users. Stay tuned for more innovative features and improvements in Mozart AI!
