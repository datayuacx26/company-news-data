# BigBear.ai to Report First Quarter 2026 Results on May 5, 2026


April 21, 2026 • 4:15 PM EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_ab748a2c1f9ab7d9c5e72f39779d2873/bigbear/news/2026-04-21_BigBear_ai_to_Report_First_Quarter_2026_Results_140.pdf)


MCLEAN, Va.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- BigBear.ai (NYSE: BBAI), a specialized defense technology company, providing mission-ready AI for national security and travel & trade customers, today announced that it will publish its first quarter earnings release on Tuesday, May 5 at approximately 4:15 pm ET and will host an earnings call at 4:30 pm ET. The earnings release will be accessible on the Company’s investor relations website:[https://ir.bigbear.ai](https://ir.bigbear.ai/) . Additional details on the earnings call will be made available on the investor relations website on May 5, 2026.


**About BigBear.ai**


BigBear.ai is a specialized defense technology company, developing and deploying mission-ready AI solutions and services. Customers and partners rely on BigBear.ai’s artificial intelligence and predictive analytics capabilities in highly complex, distributed, mission-based operating environments. Headquartered in McLean, Virginia, BigBear.ai is a public company traded on the NYSE under the symbol BBAI.
