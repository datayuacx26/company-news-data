# VergeSense Launches the Large Spatial Model (LSM): Spatial Intelligence for Workplace

## Workplace leaders don’t need more dashboards. They need to know what’s going to happen next.


Today, VergeSense announced the Large Spatial Model (LSM,) a new AI foundation built so real estate and workplace teams can get predictive insights integrated directly into the real estate & workplace platforms they already use.


Trained on eight years of real-world workplace behavior across 200+ million square feet of office space, the VergeSense LSM brings spatial intelligence to the workplace technology ecosystem, enabling a new generation of predictive, scenario-driven tools.


### **From reporting the past to predicting what’s next**


Most workplace analytics answer questions like:


- *What was our peak occupancy last month?* **
- *Which spaces were underused?* **
- *How has utilization changed over time?* **


But today’s offices are constantly shifting, by day of week, season, team behavior, and policy changes. That means yesterday’s trends can quickly become tomorrow’s blind spots.


Powering our recently launched[Predictive Planning](https://www.vergesense.com/products/decision-intelligence/predictive-planning) product, the VergeSense LSM changes this historical approach, turning workplace strategy into a modeling exercise where teams can simulate outcomes before making decisions.


Instead of reacting after the fact, organizations can plan proactively around the questions that matter most:


- *What happens if we reduce desks on one floor?* **
- *What if we change our attendance policy?* **
- *Where will we hit capacity first, and when?* **
- *How do we right-size without disrupting employee experience?* **


### **Built for the workplace technology ecosystem**


The Large Spatial Model is available to partners via API, making it easy to embed spatial intelligence directly into the platforms customers already use.


With VergeSense LSM, partners can unlock capabilities like:


- **For IWMS & planning platforms partners:** Scenario modeling, right-sizing, and breakpoint forecasting


- **For workplace experience app partners:** Predictive busyness signals and smarter space recommendations


- **For smart buildings & operations systems partners:** Proactive optimization based on predicted demand


- **For design & space planning tool partners:** Layout simulation and design performance scoring before build-out


The result: workplace technology that doesn’t just measure space, it anticipates demand, adapts to change, and helps teams make better decisions faster.


### **Available now**


The[VergeSense Large Spatial Model is available now via API](https://vergesense.ai/) for technology partners. Customers can access LSM-powered insights through our[Predictive Planning product](https://www.vergesense.com/products/decision-intelligence/predictive-planning) .
