# Unlock AI-Powered Planning with VergeSense + Microsoft Places

We're thrilled to announce an expanded integration with Microsoft Places, giving joint customers a direct path from the floor plans they already manage in Places to AI-powered Predictive Planning. Microsoft Places customers can now activate VergeSense Predictive Planning using their existing floor plans, with no additional onboarding or sensor deployment required.


Request more information


This release takes the collaboration from descriptive to predictive, powered by VergeSense's Large Spatial Model, the industry's largest behavioral benchmark of enterprise workplace usage, trained on more than eight years of real-world data across 250+ million square feet.


Key Benefits:


- **Plan with Greater Confidence:** Right-size portfolios and redesign workplaces based on AI-driven utilization forecasts, not just historical trends, for faster and more defensible planning decisions.
- **Skip the Manual Setup** : Export your existing Microsoft Places floor plans directly into VergeSense Predictive Planning for an immediate foundation for AI-powered space planning.
- **Simulate Before You Commit** : Model layout changes and headcount scenarios to understand the downstream impact of design and occupancy choices before making costly capital commitments.
- **Keep Systems in Sync** : The integration also runs in the other direction, so VergeSense customers can also export digitized floor plans into Microsoft Places, seeding Places as their floor plan source of truth from day one.


Interested in setting up this integration? Request more information below to see how you can turn the floor plans you already manage in Microsoft Places into confident, AI-powered space planning decisions.
