# Unlock Occupancy Insights and Plan Smarter with VergeSense + Logitech

We’re thrilled to announce that VergeSense has joined the


[Logitech Collaboration Program](http://www.logitech.com/lcp) , designed to give joint enterprise customers a richer, more complete view of how every space is used. This new integration brings person count data from Logitech workplace solutions like Rally Bar and Rally Bar Mini directly into VergeSense, where it powers conference room utilization reporting and strengthens Predictive Planning recommendations.


Request more information ->


By combining Logitech’s device-level occupancy signals with VergeSense Occupancy Intelligence and Predictive Planning, workplace, real estate, and IT leaders can move faster from data to decision, with sharper planning forecasts and a more complete picture of how their spaces support their people.


For enterprises that already have Logitech Rally Bar hardware deployed at scale, this integration is the fastest path to richer Predictive Planning coverage, no additional sensor deployment required.


**Key Benefits:**


- **Plan with Greater Confidence:** Power portfolio scenarios, lease decisions, and space allocations with VergeSense Predictive Planning, now enriched with Logitech occupancy signals from rooms already in use.


- **Refine Your Occupancy Picture:** Understand how conference rooms are actually used, with Logitech person count data feeding real-time utilization insights in the VergeSense platform.


- **Maximize Existing Hardware:** Extend occupancy intelligence to every conference room already equipped with Logitech video conferencing hardware—without the cost or complexity of additional sensors.


- **Act on Continuous Insights:** Optimize space, improve employee experience, and drive measurable real estate outcomes with a unified view of occupancy data across your workplace technology stack.
