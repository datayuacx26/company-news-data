# A New Era for Occupancy Intelligence

## Unify and analyze data from every source to make faster, more confident decisions from a single platform.


Today we’re excited to unveil the next-generation of the[Occupancy Intelligence Platform](https://www.vergesense.com/occupancy-intelligence/platform) , marking a pivotal evolution in how real estate and workplace leaders manage and optimize their spaces. By unifying and analyzing occupancy data from various existing building systems data such as WiFi, space booking systems, with sensor data, VergeSense now offers a single platform for real estate and workplace decisions. Plus, leaders can now leverage AI-powered insights and take advantage of additional AI capabilities from Workplace Assistant, now integrated in the Occupancy Intelligence Platform.


### Unified Data Analysis for Streamlined Decision-Making


The Occupancy Intelligence Platform now supports the analysis of existing building systems data, allowing you to get more information from your existing WiFi networks, space booking systems, and more. Not all spaces need sensors, and this approach allows you to use data from your existing building systems to understand trends across your portfolio. Additionally, you can streamline how you make real estate and workplace decisions by leveraging a single platform for occupancy intelligence.


### Support for WiFi Data Analysis


[By analyzing your WiFi data](https://www.vergesense.com/occupancy-intelligence/data/wifi) with the Occupancy Intelligence Platform, you can unlock insights about space utilization and movement trends using your existing infrastructure. Automatically ingest WiFi data from your network provider to understand people count at the building, floor, and neighborhood levels to make more informed portfolio evaluation and neighborhood planning decisions.


### Support for Space Booking Systems Data Analysis


[With support for space booking data](https://www.vergesense.com/occupancy-intelligence/data/space-booking) , you can use the Occupancy Intelligence Platform to understand how your booked spaces are actually used to measure the effectiveness of space booking policies. Compare space booking data with real-time usage information from VergeSense area sensors, and then refine your room booking policies or identify opportunities to optimize underutilized areas.


### AI-Enabled Insights and Decision Support from Workplace Assistant


We’re also excited to share that you can now use[Workplace Assistant](https://www.vergesense.com/occupancy-intelligence/platform/workplace-assistant) to analyze data from other existing building systems, such as badge data or PIR sensors. Upload your data and receive actionable recommendations, analysis, and insights in minutes. This allows you to spend less time manually analyzing data across disconnected systems, and more time experimenting, iterating, and optimizing.


Plus, you can now leverage AI-enabled Insights in the Occupancy Intelligence Platform, powered by Workplace Assistant. This feature is designed to provide personalized guidance for customers on attendance, efficiency, and space shortage trends. By proactively highlighting these trends, you can quickly capitalize on optimization opportunities, eliminate manual data analysis, and make better, faster decisions.


### The Future of Occupancy Intelligence


As workplaces continue to evolve, VergeSense is at the forefront of delivering the tools leaders need to make data-driven real estate and workplace decisions. With a single platform for occupancy intelligence, organizations can harness the power of all their occupancy data—no matter the source—enabling smarter, faster decisions that drive better outcomes for their people and their real estate investments.
