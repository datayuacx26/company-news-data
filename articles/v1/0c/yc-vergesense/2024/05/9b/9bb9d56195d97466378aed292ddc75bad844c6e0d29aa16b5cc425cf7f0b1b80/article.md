# VergeSense Unveils Exclusive Event Series: VergeSense Connect

*VergeSense Connect is a series of in-person events bringing together local real estate, and workplace professionals from around the world to network, share knowledge, and gain valuable insights.*


SAN FRANCISCO, Ca.— May 15, 2024


[VergeSense](https://www.vergesense.com/) , the leader in occupancy Intelligence, is excited to announce the launch of


[VergeSense Connect](https://www.vergesense.com/events/vergesense-connect) , an exclusive event series crafted to create and foster regional communities of innovative workplace leaders. These intimate gatherings are designed for those who are actively shaping the landscape of modern workspaces: workplace strategists, space planners, space designers, and real estate leaders.


VergeSense Connect will be hosted across the United States and Europe, with dates in London, Dallas, New York, Singapore, and Amsterdam already set.


"We've witnessed the incredible value that comes from connecting workplace leaders to share their insights and their strategies at our recent virtual Occupancy Intelligence Summit events" said Dan Ryan, CEO of VergeSense. "With VergeSense Connect, we're bringing that spirit of collaborative learning into the physical realm, creating localized networks where workplace innovators can unite, share, and lean on one another for advice. This series is an embodiment of our belief in the power of community-driven progress in redefining workplace environments."


### A Forum for Exchange, Learning, and Empowerment


At the heart of VergeSense Connect is a commitment to collaborative learning and growth. Each gathering will be centered around a facilitated round table discussion about common workplace topics and challenges like workplace design, the state of hybrid work, how to get executive buy-in, and more. Local leaders will come together to discuss challenges, share experiences, and exchange best practices. These working sessions are meant to build communities around the world where workplace leaders can voice their thoughts and have meaningful conversations about strategies and insights.


“We believe that our global network of workplace leaders is actively shaping the future of work and transforming the modern office experience. It is our privilege to cultivate such a dynamic community, empowering leaders to connect, exchange insights and drive innovations that significantly enhance the workplace for all" said Ryan.


To learn more about VergeSense Connect, VergeSense’s exclusive event series,


[visit this page](https://www.vergesense.com/events/vergesense-connect)


for more information.
