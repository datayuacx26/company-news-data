# VergeSense Unveils 4th Occupancy Intelligence Index Highlighting 2024 Workspace Trends

By: VM Blog


VergeSense has released the 4th edition of its Occupancy Intelligence Index, showcasing global workspace trends and capacity usage during the first half of 2024. Based on analysis of over 180,000 spaces across 200+ companies and 140+ million sq ft of real estate, this edition delivers critical benchmarks on collaboration vs. focus spaces, passive occupancy rates, and ghost meeting phenomena—equipping workplace teams with actionable insights for strategic space optimization.
