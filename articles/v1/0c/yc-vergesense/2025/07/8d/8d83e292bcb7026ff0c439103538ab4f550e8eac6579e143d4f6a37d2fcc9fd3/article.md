# VergeSense Unveils AI-Powered Workplace Assistant for Smarter Facilities Optimization

By: FacilitiesDive


VergeSense has launched Workplace Assistant, a generative AI‑driven virtual assistant designed to turn occupancy data into actionable space optimization recommendations. Available via its Occupancy Intelligence Platform, Workplace Assistant empowers facilities teams to make informed decisions on portfolio evaluation, neighborhood planning, and dynamic operations within minutes—rather than weeks—using a natural language interface and enterprise‑grade data privacy.
