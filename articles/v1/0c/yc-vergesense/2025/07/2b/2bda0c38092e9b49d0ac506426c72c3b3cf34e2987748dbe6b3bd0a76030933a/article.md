# The Future of Workspaces: Data‑Driven Design and VergeSense’s Occupancy Intelligence

By: IntelligentBuild


In this IntelligentBuild feature, VergeSense VP of Product Kanav Dhir explains how the Occupancy Intelligence Index helps architects and workplace planners use anonymized sensor data to inform design decisions. By analyzing trends around ghosted meetings, active space usage, and collaboration patterns, VergeSense empowers design professionals to avoid overbuilt layouts and create adaptive, modern office environments that align with real employee behavior and organizational goals
