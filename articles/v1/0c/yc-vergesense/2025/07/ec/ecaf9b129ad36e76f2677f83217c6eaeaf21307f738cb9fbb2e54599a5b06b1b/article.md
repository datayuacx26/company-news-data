# VergeSense Expands Microsoft Places Integration to Streamline Workplace Design & Efficiency

By: TechRSeries


VergeSense has deepened its collaboration with Microsoft Places to offer seamless floor‑plan conversion and enhanced occupancy intelligence. This integration allows users to export up‑to‑date VergeSense floor plans—converted into IMDF format—into Microsoft’s AI‑powered app. The result: smarter space visualization, real‑time booking automation, and improved optimization across hybrid workplaces.
