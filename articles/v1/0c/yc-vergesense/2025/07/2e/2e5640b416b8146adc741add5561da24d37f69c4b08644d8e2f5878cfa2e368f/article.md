# Data-Driven Commercial Cleaning: Infogrid Integrates VergeSense for Smarter Maintenance

By: Facilities Dive


Discover how Infogrid’s Smart Cleaning Pro uses IoT sensors and wearable devices — including those from VergeSense — to trigger targeted cleaning based on real-time occupancy. This system guides cleaning teams with LED wayfinding, automatically logs tasks for validation, and visualizes usage patterns to optimize facility efficiency. Perfect for facilities managers tackling labor shortages, hybrid-space volatility, and sustainability goals.
