# VergeSense Integrates with Microsoft Places to Elevate Workplace Optimization

By: AITech365


VergeSense has expanded its partnership with Microsoft Places, enabling customers to seamlessly export accurate, up-to-date floor plans and occupancy data into Microsoft’s AI-powered workplace ecosystem. This integration enhances space planning, automated usage tracking, and booking efficiency—empowering facilities teams with unified real estate intelligence to make better decisions, improve space utilization, and drive a modern connected workplace.
