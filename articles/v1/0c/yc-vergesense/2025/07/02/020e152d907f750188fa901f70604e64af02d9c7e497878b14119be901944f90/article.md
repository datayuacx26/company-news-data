# VergeSense Unveils 4th Edition of Occupancy Intelligence Index Benchmarking Global Workspace Usage

By: IT News Online


VergeSense has released the 4th edition of its Occupancy Intelligence Index, drawing from over 180,000 spaces and 140+ million sq ft across 200+ global enterprises. This edition benchmarks workplace trends and capacity usage for the first half of 2024, offering enterprises actionable data on space mix, utilization, and design strategies. The Index provides critical insights to help CRE and facilities teams optimize efficiency, right-size spaces, and elevate workplace strategy.
