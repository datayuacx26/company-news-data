# Introducing Breakpoint Analyzer: The First Step Toward Smarter, Faster Space Planning

In today’s workplaces, real estate and workplace teams are under pressure to do more with less. Whether you're consolidating offices, rethinking space design, or navigating hybrid attendance patterns, one challenge remains the same: how do you confidently plan your footprint when the stakes are high?


Too many teams are still wasting valuable resources on expensive, lengthy consulting engagements that rely on static ratios, outdated benchmarks, and gut feel to inform their space planning processes **.** But in a world where every square foot counts, guesswork is no longer good enough.


That’s why we’re excited to announce Breakpoint Analyzer, now available in beta.


## From Gut Feeling to Ground Truth


Breakpoint Analyzer is a new AI-powered tool that reveals how your office space supply actually performs against demand. Leveraging a proprietary AI model from VergeSense trained on over 200M SQFT of occupancy data, Breakpoint Analyzer leverages your floor plans and occupancy data from sensors, Wi-Fi data, or simulated data for unmeasured spaces to predict which spaces on a floor plan will fill up first, identify where you have too much of a space type, and where you can rebalance spaces to reduce space waste and improve efficiency.


From the start, our mission was to uncover the behavioral fingerprint of the modern workplace—because we knew it would unlock truly predictive planning. Every sensor deployed, every model trained, and every customer insight gathered has led to this moment. With more than 200 million square feet of real-world occupancy data behind it, Breakpoint Analyzer finally delivers what teams have long needed: data-driven clarity into capacity, scenario planning, and the hidden inefficiencies in workplace design.


The result? A clear, data-backed view of your design inefficiencies, opportunities to rebalance your floor plans or room sizes, and a better understanding of your true capacity and space bottlenecks.


*Breakpoint Analyzer in action*


## **Know Where Your Space Will Break - Before it Does**


With Breakpoint Analyzer, you can:


- **Identify opportunities to rebalance** **** Learn how changing layout or reallocating square footage could unlock hidden capacity.
- **Quantify wasted space** **** See how much space is lost to misaligned room sizes and ineffective mix of space types.
- **Understand your true capacity** **** Get a realistic view of how many people your space can actually support based on usage—not assumptions.
- **Pinpoint bottlenecks** **** Identify which space types (like meeting rooms or focus areas) fill up first and start impacting experience.
- **Act with confidence—fast** **** Get clear recommendations and automated insight—without waiting on consultants or time-consuming analysis.


**How It Works**


Breakpoint Analyzer follows a simple, powerful process to help you understand how you can make your space work better for your teams:


1. **Inventory your space** **** Upload your floor plans—Breakpoint Analyzer identifies room types, sizes, and seating counts.


2. **Model space behavior** **** Using your occupancy data or our benchmark dataset, it creates a proprietary usage fingerprint that reflects how employees actually use your office.


3. **Calculate capacity and breakpoints** **** We compare expected demand to space inventory, surfacing where bottlenecks will emerge and how much capacity is being wasted.


4. **Deliver actionable insights** **** Get clear, prescriptive recommendations for redesign or space reallocation.


Even if you don’t have full occupancy measurement, the Breakpoint Analyzer fills gaps with high-confidence simulations based on the largest workplace occupancy dataset in the industry.
