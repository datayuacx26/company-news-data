# Altana and Rune Technologies Partner to Strengthen Defense Supply Chains and Logistics with AI

**WASHINGTON — Sep. 4, 2025** – Altana, the world’s only product network connecting industry and government on a common platform for global trade, and Rune Technologies, the leader in AI-enabled predictive software for operational military logistics, have partnered to secure and strengthen defense supply chains.


By uniting Altana’s collaborative AI-powered platform with Rune’s flagship TyrOS platform, global defense programs can move beyond reactive, fragmented analysis to proactively identify and mitigate bottlenecks, geographic concentrations, and adversarial dependencies — as well as simulate the impact of disruptions — to take informed action to ensure the continuity of critical resources and secure warfighting readiness fully from the tactical to strategic level.


“Our partnership with Rune will allow us to provide critical, multi-tier visibility, collaboration, and action into the world’s defense supply chains that supercharge logistics support to warfighters,” said Andrew Keene, Altana’s Vice President of Federal. “Together, we are committed to enabling faster, more strategic decision making for the military and intelligence community.”


“Rune excels at AI-driven logistics built for the operational force, and Altana provides the largest and most accurate view of the entire global supply chain required to shift the JLEnt to a proactive posture. Together we provide logisticians with a modern solution that enables visibility, machine-speed decisions and predictive capabilities at echelon, from the DIB to the tactical edge,” said David Tuttle, Co-Founder and CEO of Rune Technologies.


Altana’s product network equips agencies, allies, and industry partners with a trusted network for visibility, collaboration, and action — delivering a faster, more effective way for governments and industry to secure and strengthen defense supply chains. Thousands of agents today use Altana at the U.S. Department of Defense, U.S. Customs and Border Protection, U.S. State Department, UK Ministry of Defence, NATO, and more top national security organizations.


Rune's TyrOS transforms how the military tracks, predicts, and manages supplies by replacing manual spreadsheets and whiteboards with an intelligent logistics platform that works even without reliable connectivity. When deployed on tactical edge devices, TyrOS functions through a sophisticated mesh networking capability that allows units to operate independently when disconnected and synchronize when connectivity is temporarily restored.
