# Diniz Adient opens new facility in Ismailia, Egypt


8/13/26

Diniz Adient has opened its new trim facility in Egypt, marking an important milestone in the company’s regional growth strategy.


Diniz Adient Egypt Automotive LLC was launched in March 2026 in the Free Zone, Ismailia, with hiring starting in May 2026. Supported by Diniz Adient board members, the project represents the company’s official entry into manufacturing operations in Egypt.


The new site expands Diniz Adient’s manufacturing footprint, increases production capacity, supports customer growth, and creates new employment opportunities in the region.


The project also highlighted the value of cross-functional teamwork, structured planning, and strong stakeholder communication — practices that can be replicated by other plants and teams during future expansion initiatives.


As the first phase of Diniz Adient’s footprint diversification strategy, the project lays the groundwork for future growth in Egypt.


“The launch of our new facility in Ismailia marks a significant milestone for Diniz Adient and reflects our long-term commitment to Egypt. We are proud to contribute to the growth of the automotive components industry in the country by bringing new manufacturing capabilities, creating employment opportunities, and supporting local talent. With our future expansion plans, we are confident this project will deliver lasting value for our customers, employees, and the local community”, said Engin Kopal, Country Manager, Diniz Adient Egypt.
