# Adient receives fifth consecutive GM Supplier of the Year Award


5/21/26


-
-
-
-
-


-
-
-


Adient has been named a Supplier of the Year by our customer General Motors (GM) for a fifth consecutive year.


The Supplier of the Year award recognizes distinguished global suppliers that exceed GM’s requirements, in turn providing GM customers with innovative technologies and the highest quality in the automotive industry.


“Adient is proud to continue our strong partnership with General Motors, which dates to 1987. It was a total team effort that allowed us to be recognized as a seating Supplier of the Year. This recognition speaks to the strength, capabilities and commitment of our manufacturing and support teams,” said Jim Conklin, executive vice president of Adient Americas.


Congratulations and thank you to the GM Business Unit and the many plants and employees supporting our customer GM.
