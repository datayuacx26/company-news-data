Adient recently participated in the 22nd J.P. Morgan Global China Summit in Shanghai. James Huang, executive vice president, APAC, and Linda Conrad, vice president of FP&A and investor relations, together with the Adient China management team, met with investors and J.P. Morgan auto research team.


During the meeting, James Huang shared Adient China’s business highlights over the past year, underscoring continued strong performance and sales growth outpacing the market. This momentum is driven by new business bookings, high-content innovative solutions and rapid commercialization, as well as automation and AI-enabled digitalization to enhance efficiency and optimize cost. He also highlighted the company’s global footprint combined with local capabilities, and disciplined capital allocation to deliver long-term shareholder value in China.


The presentation is available on the investor section of the Adient website:[J.P. Morgan Investor Meeting](https://investors.adient.com/~/media/Files/A/Adient-IR/reports-and-presentations/jp-morgan-china-summit-adient-presentation.pdf)
