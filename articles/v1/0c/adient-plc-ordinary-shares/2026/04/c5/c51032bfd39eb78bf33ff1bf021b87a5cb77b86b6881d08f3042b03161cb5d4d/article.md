Every year on Earth Day, we take a moment to reflect on our shared responsibility — and our shared progress — in creating a more sustainable future. At Adient, sustainability is not a one day event. It’s something our teams around the world bring to life every day through innovation, continuous improvement, and care for our people and our planet.


This Earth Day, we are celebrating what Adient employees have already achieved together:


**Greenhouse gas emissions**


- 42% reduction in Scope 1 & 2* greenhouse gas emissions since 2019
- Equivalent to removing ~15,800 passenger vehicles from the road for an entire year


**Water conservation**


- 67,559​ cubic meters of water conserved through FY25 improvement projects​
- Enough to fill 27 Olympic-sized swimming pools


**Waste reduction**


- 4,671​ metric tons of waste diverted from landfills via 609 waste-saving projects in FY25​
- Equivalent to the weight of ~467 African elephants


**Energy efficiency**


- 72.6M​ kWh of energy conserved annually through 575 energy-saving projects in FY25​
- Enough electricity to power ~6,700 average American homes for a year


**Fuel conservation**


- 13.4M​ kWh-equivalent of fuel saved annually through 99 fuel-reduction projects in FY25​
- Equivalent to taking ~900 gasoline-powered cars off the road for a year


​ **Renewable energy**


- 30% of Adient’s total electricity came from renewable sources across 68 of Adient’s 200+ global manufacturing sites in FY25​
- Enough clean energy to power a city of ~50,000 homes


**Total energy reduction**


- 23 million kWh reduction in total energy consumption year-over-year in FY25​
- Equivalent to the annual electricity consumption of ~2,100 U.S. households


**Scope 3 emissions**


- 3% year-over-year reduction in Scope 3** emissions in FY25, preventing ~147,000 metric tons of CO₂e​
- Equivalent to the carbon sequestered by 172,000 acres of U.S. forests in one year​


**Water savings**


- 6% reduction in total water withdrawals **** year-over-year in FY25​
- Savings equivalent to filling ~59 Olympic-sized swimming pools​


**Waste recovery**


- 82% waste recovery rate **** in FY25, recycling or reusing 120,000+ metric tons of waste​
- Equal to the weight of ~850 fully-loaded Boeing 737 aircraft​


**Carbon footprint innovation**


- 6,000+ product carbon footprints analyzed with Adient’s proprietary Product Carbon Footprint Tool in FY25​
- Each analysis contributes to lighter, more sustainable vehicle seats


**Continuous improvement**


- 1,990 continuous improvement projects completed by Adient employees in FY25​
- An average of 5+ sustainability projects completed every single day​


* Scope 1 covers direct emissions from owned or controlled sources, while Scope 2 covers indirect emissions from purchased energy​
** Scope 3 covers all other indirect emissions across an organization’s value chain, both upstream and downstream, beyond its direct operations and purchased energy​


**Happy Earth Day.**
