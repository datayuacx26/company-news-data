# Introducing Openlayer Governance

November 24, 2025


[Introducing Openlayer Governance](https://www.openlayer.com/changelog/introducing-openlayer-governance)


Introducing Openlayer Governance: the fastest way to track and enforce rules for your AI systems. With custom frameworks, you can define rules like:


- Test every system for PII leakage + prompt injection
- Require monitoring, tracing, logs, and production alerts
- Enforce owner, risk level, and approval status


Rules are tracked across projects and workspaces so you can easily see progress and spot gaps. Collaboration is made easy through assigning rule owners, comment threads, and uploading evidence that can be accessed by the whole team.


Governance is now available to everyone on Openlayer, so you can increase trust in your AI systems today.
