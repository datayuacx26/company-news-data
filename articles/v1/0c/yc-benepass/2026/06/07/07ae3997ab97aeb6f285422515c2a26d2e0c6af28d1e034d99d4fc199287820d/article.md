# Benefit Claims Reviews That Move At Modern Speed

Employees can order dinner, hail a ride, or transfer money in seconds. But when it comes to getting reimbursed for benefits, many wait *days* for a human to manually review their receipts.


This experience of feeling “stuck” doesn’t match employees’ expectations. And frankly, it shouldn’t.


That’s why Benepass has implemented a near-instant, automated review of reimbursement and card transaction claims, helping employees get faster approvals while reducing friction for HR and benefits teams.


## Benefits Spending Has Changed


Employees use their benefits in more ways than ever before. Hybrid work stipends. Wellness allowances. Home office setups. Global teams submitting expenses in multiple languages and currencies.


But most reimbursement workflows were built for a different era.


Employees increasingly expect workplace technology experiences to match the ease and convenience of consumer apps. Yet, reimbursement workflows often involve long review windows and confusing follow-ups that cause unnecessary delays.


The traditional process is painfully familiar:


- Upload a receipt
- Wait several business days
- Receive vague follow-up questions
- Resubmit documentation
- Wait again


## Claims Reviewed In Seconds


Benepass now supports near-instant AI-powered review for many reimbursement claims. Claims that previously took days may now be approved in as little as three to five seconds.


Here’s what’s happening behind the scenes:


- AI and traditional rules-based engineering extract receipt data automatically
- Claims are validated against employer program rules
- Eligible expenses are auto-approved
- Low-confidence or ambiguous claims route to human specialists
- Ongoing audit reviews validate AI decisions for accuracy and compliance


Today, a growing share of[post-tax claims](https://getbenepass.com/blog/what-is-a-lifestyle-spending-account-a-complete-guide-for-hr-teams) are fully autonomous. But this isn’t about removing humans from the process altogether. Instead, AI handles the repetitive validation work, so specialists can focus on more complex tasks, like handling edge cases, making nuanced decisions, and overseeing compliance.


## Real Life, Global Examples


This new claims process isn’t confined to a single country, language, or expense type. Best of all, employees don’t need to become reimbursement experts just to access their benefits.


Philipe in Brazil submits his monthly internet bill for his hybrid work benefit. The claim is reviewed and approved in seconds.


Amy uploads her recurring gym membership receipt to her Wellness LSA. Benepass recognizes the recurring expense and processes it automatically.


Tim in Tokyo submits expenses for a desk, chair, and lamp using a screenshot receipt from his phone. Coming soon, Benepass will notify him instantly if the image is blurry or missing required details before he submits the claim.


## Faster Reimbursements, Less Friction


For HR and benefits leaders, faster claims review is not just an employee experience upgrade. It creates a smoother, more scalable way to manage benefits globally.


| What Employees Gain | What Employers Gain | |---|---| | **Decisions in seconds, not days** | **Fewer reimbursement support tickets** | | **Support for receipts in multiple languages** | **More confidence in program accuracy and consistency** | | **Clear denial reasons with actionable next steps** | **Greater auditability and compliance oversight** | | **Less back-and-forth frustration** | **Better employee trust in the benefit program** | | **Faster access to their money** | **Faster reimbursement cycles** |


Benepass continuously audits a percentage of autonomously reviewed claims. In many cases, AI is already significantly more accurate than its manual review equivalent.


That combination of speed, transparency, and oversight is what modern benefits infrastructure should look like.
