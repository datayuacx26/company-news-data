# Venta AI launches four new AI sales employees

Munich, September 23, 2025: Venta AI, an up-and-coming Munich-based start-up in the field of sales automation, is today launching four new AI employees to support German sales teams. Just three months after its $2 million pre-seed financing round, Venta AI is putting its vision into practice by creating AI colleagues who not only automate processes but also contribute their own knowledge and actively support users in acquisition.


## Four new AI employees for sales


Venta AI's AI colleagues are based on the agent principle: instead of simply working through individual tasks according to pre-programmed patterns, they act independently like specialized team members. Each agent pursues a clear goal, plans the necessary steps, executes them independently, and draws on knowledge and external data sources. This creates a new form of collaboration between humans and AI – with significantly more initiative and proactive added value.


A total of four new AI employees have been developed. They cover key tasks in new customer acquisition and enhance classic tools with independent intelligence:


#### Company and contact search


This AI employee searches various databases according to specified criteria and provides qualified companies with suitable contact persons. It then enriches this information with relevant data and creates customized approaches. This allows contacts to be addressed in a personalized manner in no time at all.


#### Monitoring press articles


By monitoring thousands of news sources daily, this AI employee finds relevant company news about potential new and existing customers as well as competitors. This helps sales staff find the right time to approach them.


#### Preparing for trade fairs


By simply entering a trade fair website, this AI employee automatically creates a list of exhibiting companies and checks them against individual criteria. This means that appointment requests can be sent out in just a few minutes for a trade fair visit.


#### Monitoring job postings


New job postings are often an important indicator of new potential business relationships. Venta AI monitors specified job advertisements and automatically creates appropriate messages.

## Customers are already relying on Venta AI


Venta AI's solutions are already being used successfully by start-ups and established medium-sized companies alike. Customers include medium-sized companies such as the ARNO Group, UnternehmerTUM, and a&o hostels. They use Venta AI to tap into suitable customer groups, make their sales processes more efficient, and discover new business opportunities.


## A step toward smarter AI colleagues


With these four AI employees, Venta AI is expanding its platform for the “sales of the future.” Unlike traditional automation solutions, Venta AI colleagues create additional value: they contribute contextual knowledge, develop independent ideas, and thus open up new business opportunities for users.


“Our vision is to develop AI colleagues that are more than mere automations. They should be experts in their field, develop their own ideas, identify opportunities, and help people in sales to work more successfully and creatively. With our new AI employees, we are taking a decisive step in this direction,” says Lucas Spreiter, founder of Venta AI.

## About Venta AI


Venta AI is a European startup that develops GDPR-compliant AI employees for sales teams who can take over the cold calling process from identifying target customers to approaching them via legally compliant channels. This increases sales productivity and the number of meetings booked. Founded in 2022 and accepted into Y Combinator in 2023, Venta AI is revolutionizing sales technology with a focus on compliance and data quality for Europe. Founders Stefan and Lucas have been working on AI automation for European companies since 2018 and began with sales automation after experiencing the weaknesses of traditional sales processes themselves.
