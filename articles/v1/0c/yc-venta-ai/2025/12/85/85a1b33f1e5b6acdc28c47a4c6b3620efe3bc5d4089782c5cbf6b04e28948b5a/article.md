Venta AI is an end-to-end AI SDR platform that doesn’t just automate parts of outbound—it manages the entire SDR workflow for companies in the DACH region with precision, depth, and local optimization.

Venta AI produces complete multichannel outreach sequences. These can be based on your own templates with personalization fields or fully hyper-personalized using sources such as:

- trade show participation
- and more


Within hyper-personalization you can configure tone, CTA style, subject line approach, reference examples, and automated follow-ups.


Another core feature is the intelligent CRM engine: Venta detects existing contacts, ongoing conversations, prevents double outreach (unless permitted), and syncs only new contacts back to your CRM—avoiding pipeline errors from day one. Lead replies are automatically categorized, and opt-outs are handled when needed.


Because GDPR compliance is critical in the DACH region, Venta AI is built natively for European standards:

Venta AI is currently the strongest fit for the DACH market. For Mittelstand firms, SaaS companies, agencies, and B2B service providers, Venta AI functions as a reliable “AI SDR Co-Pilot” that consistently generates qualified meetings.
