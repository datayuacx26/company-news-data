# Concentra Expands Arizona Presence with New Goodyear Medical Center

# Concentra Expands Arizona Presence with New Goodyear Medical Center


June 24, 2026 9:00am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_6b3571f727f38be47e5aa6c2415194e2/concentra/news/2026-06-24_Concentra_Expands_Arizona_Presence_with_New_205.pdf)


*New Goodyear medical center brings Concentra's Arizona footprint to 17 locations, increasing access to occupational health services*


DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Concentra® (NYSE: CON), the nation's leader in occupational and workforce health services, today announced the opening of its first medical center in Goodyear, Arizona, expanding access to occupational health services for employers and employees throughout the West Valley. The new facility is in Pebble Creek Plaza Shops at 2403 North Pebble Creek Parkway, Suite 101, Goodyear, Arizona 85395.


The new medical center, located in one of Arizona's growing industrial areas, will provide occupational health services for employers across a wide array of industries, including logistics, transportation, manufacturing, warehousing, distribution and construction. The facility is intended to help address workplace health and safety needs across the region.


"The West Valley continues to see significant growth across a range of industries, and access to occupational health services is an important part of supporting that workforce," said Dr. Scott Cole, senior vice president of medical operations at Concentra. "This medical center allows us to provide care closer to where employees live and work while helping employers address workplace health needs in a timely manner."


The medical center will provide a comprehensive suite of workforce health services, including work injury care, physical therapy, drug testing, Department of Transportation physicals, pre-placement exams, and medical surveillance programs. Employers will also have access to Concentra HUB, the company's digital-enabled platform that streamlines injury management and communication.


As Arizona continues to experience growth across key industries, employers increasingly need workforce health partners that can help reduce workplace injuries, support compliance and accelerate return-to-work outcomes. With more than four decades of experience in occupational health, Concentra helps employers build healthier, safer and more productive workforces through coordinated workforce health solutions that meet the needs of employers and their employees at convenient times and locations.


To learn more about Concentra and its nationwide network of medical centers, visit concentra.com.


**About Concentra**


Concentra is the largest provider of occupational health services in the United States by number of locations, with the mission of improving the health of America's workforce, one patient at a time. Our approximately 13,000 colleagues and affiliated physicians and clinicians support the delivery of an extensive suite of services, including occupational and consumer health services and other direct-to-employer care. We support the care of approximately 54,000 patients each business day on average across 47 states and the District of Columbia at our 632 occupational health centers, 411 onsite health clinics at employer worksites, and Concentra Telemed as of March 31, 2026.[www.Concentra.com](https://www.concentra.com/)
