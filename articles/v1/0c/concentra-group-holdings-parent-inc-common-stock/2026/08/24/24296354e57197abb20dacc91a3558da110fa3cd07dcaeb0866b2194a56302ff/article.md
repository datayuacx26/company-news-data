# Concentra Appoints Dr. Jill Rosenthal as Chief Medical Officer to Advance Clinical Innovation and Workforce Health

# Concentra Appoints Dr. Jill Rosenthal as Chief Medical Officer to Advance Clinical Innovation and Workforce Health


August 18, 2026 9:00am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_47a0b20d8d1fe4446266126866f78624/concentra/news/2026-08-18_Concentra_Appoints_Dr_Jill_Rosenthal_as_Chief_212.pdf)


DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Concentra® (NYSE: CON), the nation's leader in occupational medicine, today announced that nationally recognized occupational medicine leader Jill Rosenthal, MD, MPH, will join the company Sept. 1 and assume the role of chief medical officer, effective Jan. 1, 2027. Returning to the organization where she previously served in physician leadership roles, including area medical director, **** Dr. Rosenthal will lead Concentra's national clinical strategy, physician leadership, quality and patient safety initiatives, evidence-based care programs, and medical innovation as employers increasingly seek integrated workforce health solutions. She succeeds longtime chief medical officer, Dr. John Anderson, who previously announced his retirement effective Dec. 31, 2026, after 33 years with Concentra.


This press release features multimedia. View the full release here:[https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260818786254/en/](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260818786254/en/)


Dr. Jill Rosenthal


Dr. Rosenthal returns to Concentra after more than 16 years as chief medical officer at Zenith Insurance Company, where she led medical strategy, clinical innovation, and quality initiatives supporting employers and injured workers. She also serves as the 2026 president of the American College of Occupational and Environmental Medicine (ACOEM), bringing national leadership and a broad perspective from both provider and payer organizations.


Working closely with Concentra's clinical and operational leaders, Dr. Rosenthal will drive clinical strategy with service excellence, advancing consistent, high-quality care across the organization's national footprint. This integrated approach further strengthens Concentra's position as the nation's leading occupational health care provider while helping employers build healthier, safer, and more productive workforces.


"Dr. Rosenthal brings an exceptional combination of national leadership, payer expertise, and deep knowledge of occupational medicine," said Matthew DiCanio, president and chief financial officer at Concentra. "She understands Concentra's clinical model and what has made us the nation's leading occupational health provider, and her experience will help us continue evolving our clinical capabilities while delivering even greater value to the employers and employees we serve."


Throughout her career, Dr. Rosenthal has focused on improving the quality, coordination, and effectiveness of occupational medicine. She has worked extensively with employers, clinicians, insurers, and industry leaders to advance evidence-based practices that improve recovery, support safe return-to-work, and strengthen workforce health.


“Returning to Concentra is an opportunity to build on the extraordinary legacy Dr. Anderson and Dr. Fogarty created and the standard they helped establish for occupational medicine,” Dr. Rosenthal said. “Their commitment to clinical excellence and understanding employers’ needs helped shape Concentra into the organization it is today. I’m honored to carry that legacy forward, while continuing to innovate, strengthen employer partnerships, and advance the specialty.”


For more than four decades, Concentra has helped employers improve workforce health through coordinated occupational health services, injury care, physical therapy, onsite health services, telemedicine, and advanced data analytics. The organization partners with employers nationwide to help reduce workplace injuries, accelerate recovery, improve productivity, and support healthier workplaces through evidence-based occupational medicine.


To learn more about Concentra and its nationwide network of medical centers, visit[www.concentra.com](http://www.concentra.com/) .


**About Concentra**


Concentra is the largest provider of occupational health services in the United States by number of locations, with the mission of improving the health of America’s workforce, one patient at a time. Our approximately 13,000 colleagues and affiliated physicians and clinicians support the delivery of an extensive suite of services, including occupational and consumer health services and other direct-to-employer care. We support the care of approximately 54,000 patients each business day on average across 46 states and the District of Columbia at our 633 occupational health centers, 415 onsite health clinics at employer worksites, and Concentra Telemed as of June 30, 2026.
