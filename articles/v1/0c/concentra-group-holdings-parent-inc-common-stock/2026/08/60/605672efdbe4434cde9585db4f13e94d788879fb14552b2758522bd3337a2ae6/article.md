# Concentra to Present at Upcoming Investor Conferences


August 10, 2026 9:30am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_bba9dc60f1c5bca3c8730feb89a6f474/concentra/news/2026-08-10_Concentra_to_Present_at_Upcoming_Investor_210.pdf)


DALLAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Concentra Group Holdings Parent, Inc. (NYSE: CON), the nation’s largest provider of occupational health services by number of locations, today announced that executive and senior management are scheduled to attend the following upcoming investor conferences:


- **Bank of America Virtual SMID Cap Conference,** Aug. 11, 2026
**Location:** Virtual


- **RBC Nashville Healthcare Bus Tour,** Aug. 12, 2026
**Location:** Nashville, Tenn.


- **Wells Fargo Healthcare Conference,** Sept. 9-10, 2026
**Location:** Boston, Mass.


- **Jefferies Healthcare Services and Technology Conference,** Sept. 15, 2026
**Location:** Nashville, Tenn.


- **Deutsche Bank Healthcare Summit,** Sept. 16, 2026
**Location:** New York, N.Y.


Concentra leadership will present at several of the conferences, and webcast replays of the presentations will be made available where possible at[www.ir.concentra.com](http://ir.concentra.com/) . For additional information about the conferences and management presentations, please reach out to Bill Chapman, Vice President of Strategy & Investor Relations, atir@concentra.com .


To learn more about Concentra and its nationwide network of medical centers, visit[www.concentra.com](http://www.concentra.com/) .


**About Concentra**


Concentra is the largest provider of occupational health services in the United States by number of locations, with the mission of improving the health of America’s workforce, one patient at a time. Our approximately 13,000 colleagues and affiliated physicians and clinicians support the delivery of an extensive suite of services, including occupational and consumer health services and other direct-to-employer care. We support the care of approximately 54,000 patients each business day on average across 46 states and the District of Columbia at our 633 occupational health centers, 415 onsite health clinics at employer worksites, and Concentra Telemed as of June 30, 2026.
