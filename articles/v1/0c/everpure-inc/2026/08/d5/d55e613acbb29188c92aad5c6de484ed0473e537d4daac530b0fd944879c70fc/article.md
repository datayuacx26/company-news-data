Everpure turns storage into an active cyber resilience layer, helping organizations detect threats sooner and recover clean data at scale with immutable snapshots, AI-driven anomaly detection, and integrated recovery workflows.

For too long, organizations have treated storage as a passive element, that simply stores data, and the security and recovery of the data is enabled through cyber security controls and backup and recovery platforms. But in today’s threat climate, passive isn’t enough. Storage should be an active defender, capable of adding to an organization’s security posture and providing instantaneous data recovery to speed threat remediation and recovery.


Everpure’s cyber resilience strategy includes a comprehensive approach that transforms storage to reduce the risk of disruption and reduce the impact of disruption. These capabilities ensure the organization will survive cyber disruptions and reduce overall cyber risk.

## **Ensure Data Surivability: Native resilience in the storage platform**


The foundation of active defense begins at the data layer itself. Everpure delivers built-in, native resilience directly through[FlashArray](https://www.everpuredata.com/products/block-file-object-storage.html) ™ and[FlashBlade](https://www.everpuredata.com/products/unstructured-data-storage.html) ®. By utilizing[SafeMode](https://www.everpuredata.com/solutions/cyber-resilience/ransomware/safemode.html) ™, we provide immutable, indelible snapshots that prevent unauthorized deletion, even if administrative credentials are fully compromised. These are coupled with extensive zero-trust principles to ensure only the authorized use of the platform, the access of data and the control of settings and policies.


## **Detect Threats Faster: Integrating threat detection into your security ecosystem**


Storage shouldn’t exist in a silo. Everpure provides native signals that provide data and user anomalies directly into the tools your SecOps team uses every day. Everpure integrates seamlessly across the entire security stack,including[SIEM](https://blog.everpuredata.com/perspectives/need-better-roi-from-siem-put-data-to-work/) , SOAR, XDR from leading threat detection platforms like[CrowdStrike](https://www.everpuredata.com/partners/technology-alliance-partners/crowdstrike.html) .


Instead of forcing your team to jump between separate dashboards, we feed storage anomalies straight into your organization’s existing central nervous system. This means a storage alert can automatically trigger a SOAR playbook, correlate with endpoint telemetry in your XDR, and immediately help your analysts determine response and remediation.


When Pure1 detects anomalous behavior or a partner’s security platform flags suspicious user activity and data changes, that vital context is immediately shared across your tools. Security teams can correlate storage-layer anomalies with endpoint, identity, network, and application telemetry. This allows teams to trigger automated workflows, tag affected volumes or snapshots, and drastically accelerate investigations. This means faster containment, better visibility into the blast radius, and more confidence about which data is compromised. **
