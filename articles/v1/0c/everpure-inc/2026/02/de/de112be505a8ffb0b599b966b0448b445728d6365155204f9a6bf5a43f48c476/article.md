# Pure Storage Announces Date and Conference Call Information for Fiscal Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2026 Financial Results

**Santa Clara, Calif. – February 4, 2026 —** Pure Storage (NYSE: PSTG), the IT pioneer that delivers the world’s most advanced data storage technology and services, today announced it will host a conference call on Wednesday, February 25, at 2:00 p.m. PT to discuss its financial results for fiscal fourth quarter and full year 2026 ended February 1, 2026. This conference call will be held following the release of Pure Storage’s financial results.
