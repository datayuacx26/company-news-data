# Everpure Announces Date and Conference Call Information for First Quarter Fiscal 2027 Financial Results

**Santa Clara, Calif. – May 6, 2026 —** Everpure® (NYSE: P), the company revolutionizing storage and data management, today announced it will host a conference call on Wednesday, May 27, at 2:00 p.m. PT to discuss its financial results for first quarter fiscal 2027 ended May 3, 2026. This conference call will be held following the release of Everpure’s financial results.


**First Quarter Fiscal 2027 Conference Call Details**


A live audio broadcast of the conference call will be available at the Everpure Investor Relations website at[investor.everpuredata.com](https://investor.everpuredata.com/) . A replay will be available following the call on the Everpure Investor Relations website, or for two weeks at 1-800-770-2030 (or 1-647-362-9199 for international callers) with passcode 5667482.
