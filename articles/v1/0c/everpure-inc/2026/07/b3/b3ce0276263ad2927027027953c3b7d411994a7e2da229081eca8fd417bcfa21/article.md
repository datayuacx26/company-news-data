## What is the Everpure Enterprise AI Factory with AMD?


The AMD-powered Everpure Enterprise AI Factory, powered by AMD, is a fully integrated AI infrastructure platform built on AMD Instinct MI350 Series GPUs and Everpure™[FlashBlade](https://www.everpuredata.com/products/unstructured-data-storage.html) ®. It’s purpose-built for enterprises that need to run large-scale AI workloads—training, fine-tuning, inference, and data pipelines—without stitching together point solutions or managing constant tuning cycles.


At its core, the platform combines:


- AMD Instinct MI350 Series GPUs—next-generation AI accelerators built on the CDNA 4 architecture with massive HBM3E memory capacity, native FP4/FP6 support, and the open source ROCm 7 software stack for immediate compatibility with PyTorch, JAX, and vLLM
- Everpure[FlashBlade//S](https://www.everpuredata.com/content/dam/pdf/en/datasheets/ds-pure-storage-flashblade-s.pdf) ™ R2—the industry’s leading unified fast file and object (UFFO) storage platform, engineered from the ground up for flash and purpose-built for AI data pipelines
- An enterprise-grade fabric built on RDMA over Converged Ethernet (RoCE) with Arista 7060DX4 switches—delivering low-latency, high-throughput connectivity across every node in the cluster


The result is a system that functions as a unified AI factory: multi-user, always-on, and capable of managing the full lifecycle of your AI data—from ingestion through training to deployment.


## Why storage is the hidden bottleneck in enterprise AI


Most AI infrastructure conversations start with compute. But experienced AI infrastructure leaders know that storage is where AI projects quietly die. High-resolution data sets, model checkpoints, training logs, and inference pipelines all place extreme and simultaneous demands on your storage layer.


FlashBlade was built to handle exactly this. Unlike legacy storage systems retrofitted for AI, FlashBlade//S R2 delivers multi-dimensional performance—meaning it handles any file size, sequential or random I/O, batched or real-time jobs, and tens of millions of metadata operations per second—all simultaneously, without tuning.


> High-resolution computer vision data sets can exceed 30TB. LLM checkpointing demands peak read/write throughput simultaneously. FlashBlade handles both without compromise.
