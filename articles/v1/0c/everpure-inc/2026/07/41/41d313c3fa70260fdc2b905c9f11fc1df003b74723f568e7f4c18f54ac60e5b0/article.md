# Everpure Celebrates Partner Excellence at Pure Accelerate 2026

### Summary


At Pure Accelerate 2026, Everpure celebrated partner excellence and highlighted how our partner ecosystem is helping customers simplify operations, modernize infrastructure, and harness AI through the Enterprise Data Cloud.


Pure Accelerate 2026 was a milestone moment—not just for the[groundbreaking announcements](https://blog.everpuredata.com/products/ai-strategy-stuck-behind-architecture/) that defined this year’s event, but for the incredible partner community that makes it all possible. From inspiring sessions and meaningful conversations to major product launches and well-deserved recognition, Pure Accelerate 2026 was a celebration of where we’ve been and a bold look at where we’re headed.


*“Pure Accelerate 2026 was a defining moment for our ecosystem. The energy in the room said it all—the future of data infrastructure will be built in partnership. Our partners extend our mission, and investing in their success will always be a top priority.”* –Lyanne Paustenbach, AVP, Global Partner Marketing, Everpure


At Everpure, we believe our partner ecosystem is one of our greatest strengths. Together, we’re helping customers simplify their operations, unlock the full value of their data, and embrace the era of AI. That spirit was at the heart of everything we did last week—and nowhere was it more evident than in the partners we had the privilege of recognizing.


## **A week of big announcements**


This year’s Pure Accelerate introduced our vision for the new data dynamic via the[Enterprise Data Cloud](https://www.everpuredata.com/enterprise-data-cloud.html) (EDC)—the most significant evolution of the Everpure Platform to date. This new data-centric operating model moves customers away from application-centric silos and toward a unified, intelligent approach to managing data across every environment: on premises, cloud, and edge.


Built on three pillars—the[Unified Data Plane](https://blog.everpuredata.com/products/era-of-one-workload/) , the[Intelligent Control Plane](https://blog.everpuredata.com/products/self-managing-data-estate/) , and[universal data intelligence](https://blog.everpuredata.com/products/ai-advantage-is-in-your-data/) —the EDC represents an unprecedented services and growth opportunity for our partner ecosystem. Customers can no longer navigate the complexities of modern AI, infrastructure costs, and governance challenges alone. They need trusted partners to help them re-architect their data strategy, and our ecosystem is uniquely positioned to lead that charge.
