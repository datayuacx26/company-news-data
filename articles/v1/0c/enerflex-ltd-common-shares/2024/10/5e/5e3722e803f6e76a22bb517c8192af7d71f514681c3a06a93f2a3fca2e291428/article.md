# Enerflex Shines at the GPA Midstream Convention in Texas - Enerflex Ltd.

At Enerflex, we are proud to have a team of dedicated experts committed to building a better energy future. Driven by their passion, they are constantly seeking innovative solutions to tackle the industry’s greatest challenges.


One area we are focused on is the Permian Basin. As there are resources left to produce in the Permian, the industry is reporting an increased gas-to-oil ratio and an increased amount of CO2 in the associated gas.


Tim Oneal – Lead Process Engineer for New Product Development in the USA, delved into how Enerflex can help our client partners navigate the challenges of producing in the Basin at the recent 2024 GPA Midstream Convention in San Antonio. Tim shared his insights on whether operators should remove the bulk of water with a TEG system and polish it with a small molecular sieve system or if they should invest for a fully-saturated mole sieve system and forego the TEG system altogether.


His presentation ignited thought-provoking discussions and evoked inspiring insights on how we can collaborate to address this.


You can read his full presentation “Optimizing Gas Processing in the Permian Basin: Choosing Between TEG with Molecular Sieve Polishing or Fully Saturated Molecular Sieve Dehydration”[here](https://www.enerflex.com/wp-content/uploads/Optimize_Gas_Processing_in_PB_TEG.pdf) .
