# 6.8MW Modular Power Island Supports Continuous Midstream Operations - Enerflex Ltd.

#### Reliable Prime Power for Remote Permian Basin Operations


A midstream operator in Lea County, New Mexico needed reliable prime power for a remote site operating in extreme heat with limited space and strict emissions requirements. Enerflex supplied four 2,500 hp modular power units, delivering a total of 6.8MW (10,000 hp) of continuous power. The compact design simplified installation, supported rapid deployment, and helped the customer meet operational and environmental requirements while staying on schedule.
