# FDA Drug Approvals Targeting GPCR Mechanisms, 2021 to 2026

## FDA drug approvals targeting GPCR mechanisms, 2021 to 2026


G-protein-coupled receptors (GPCRs) remain the single most productive target family in small-molecule and peptide drug development, and the FDA's approval record since 2021 reflects that. Across Drugs@FDA, at least eighteen distinct products approved between 2021 and mid-2026 act primarily through a GPCR, spanning class B secretin-family receptors (the incretin and glucagon receptors), class A aminergic and peptide receptors, lipid-sensing receptors, and chemokine receptors. The therapeutic footprint is unusually broad: cardiometabolic disease, migraine, schizophrenia, insomnia, multiple sclerosis and ulcerative colitis, gynecology and menopause, ANCA-associated vasculitis, chronic-kidney-disease pruritus, hepatorenal syndrome, stem-cell mobilization in multiple myeloma, and opioid-overdose reversal.


The clearest theme is the dominance of incretin biology. Beyond that, the period produced several genuine first-in-class GPCR agents, including the first muscarinic-agonist antipsychotic (Cobenfy), the first oral C5a-receptor antagonist (Tavneos), and the first neurokinin-3 receptor antagonist for menopausal vasomotor symptoms (Veozah).

### Cardiometabolic and endocrine: the incretin era


The largest cluster of GPCR approvals targets the incretin and glucagon receptors, all class B (secretin-family) GPCRs. Semaglutide (Wegovy) is a GLP-1 analogue that "acts as a GLP-1 receptor agonist that selectively binds to and activates the GLP-1 receptor"[523](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=523) ; it entered the window as an obesity therapy in June 2021[375](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=375) . Tirzepatide is a dual "GIP receptor and GLP-1 receptor agonist"[460](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=460) , first approved as Mounjaro for glycemic control in type 2 diabetes in May 2022[459](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=459)[354](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=354) and then as Zepbound for chronic weight management in adults with obesity or overweight in November 2023[378](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=378)[357](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=357) .


The period closed with two notable additions. Orforglipron (Foundayo), approved April 2026[479](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=479) , is an orally administered agent whose labeling identifies "glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonist activity"[480](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=480) , extending the incretin class beyond injectable peptides into a small-molecule oral format. Dasiglucagon (Zegalogue), approved March 2021[435](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=435) , works on the other side of glucose homeostasis: it is "a glucagon receptor agonist, which increases blood glucose concentration by activating hepatic glucagon receptors"[520](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=520) , indicated for severe hypoglycemia in patients with diabetes weighing at least 20 kg[520](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=520) .


### Neurology and headache: CGRP-receptor gepants


Migraine has become a showcase for CGRP-receptor pharmacology, another class B GPCR. Atogepant (Qulipta) is a "calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP) receptor antagonist"[234](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=234) indicated for preventive treatment of migraine in adults[246](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=246) , approved September 2021[225](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=225) . Zavegepant (Zavzpret), the same mechanism[243](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=243) , was approved March 2023[231](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=231) as an intranasal spray for acute treatment of migraine with or without aura[248](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=248) . These small-molecule "gepants" sit alongside erenumab (Aimovig), the monoclonal antibody directed at the CGRP receptor itself rather than the ligand, which received a supplemental efficacy approval in this window[254](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=254) . The oral gepants rimegepant (Nurtec ODT) and ubrogepant (Ubrelvy) also received labeling and indication supplements during the period.


### Psychiatry and CNS: the first muscarinic-agonist antipsychotic


Cobenfy (xanomeline/trospium), approved September 2024[36](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=36) , is the first antipsychotic to work through muscarinic rather than dopaminergic receptors. Its labeling attributes efficacy to xanomeline's "agonist activity at M1 and M4 muscarinic acetylcholine receptors in the central nervous system," with the co-formulated trospium acting as a peripheral muscarinic antagonist to limit cholinergic side effects[39](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=39) . It is indicated for schizophrenia in adults[39](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=39) . Both components engage class A muscarinic GPCRs, but as agonist and antagonist respectively.


### Sleep medicine: dual orexin-receptor antagonism


Daridorexant (Quviviq), approved January 2022[332](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=332) , acts "through antagonism of orexin receptors," blocking orexin A and orexin B at the OX1R and OX2R receptors to suppress wake drive[336](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=336) . It is indicated for adults with insomnia characterized by difficulties with sleep onset and/or maintenance[340](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=340) . Orexin receptors are class A peptide GPCRs, and the dual-antagonist approach continues a mechanism established by earlier agents in the class.


### Immunology and neurology: sphingosine-1-phosphate receptor modulators


Two S1P receptor modulators, both acting on class A lipid-sensing GPCRs, were approved in the window. Ponesimod (Ponvory), approved March 2021[271](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=271) , is "a sphingosine 1-phosphate (S1P) receptor 1 modulator" that blocks lymphocyte egress from lymph nodes[286](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=286) , indicated for relapsing forms of multiple sclerosis[540](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=540) . Etrasimod (Velsipity), approved October 2023[259](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=259) , is a broader "S1P receptor modulator that binds with high affinity to S1P receptors 1, 4, and 5"[289](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=289) , indicated for moderately to severely active ulcerative colitis in adults[289](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=289) . The two illustrate how receptor-subtype selectivity within one GPCR family is being tuned for different autoimmune indications.


### Women's health and menopause


Relugolix, formulated with estradiol and norethindrone acetate as Myfembree, is a gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) receptor antagonist[536](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=536) , approved May 2021[199](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=199) for heavy menstrual bleeding associated with uterine fibroids and later for moderate-to-severe pain from endometriosis[214](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=214) . The GnRH receptor is a class A peptide GPCR. Distinct from hormonal suppression, fezolinetant (Veozah), approved May 2023[66](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=66) , is "a neurokinin 3 (NK3) receptor antagonist that blocks neurokinin B binding on the KNDy neuron" to modulate the thermoregulatory center[530](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=530) , indicated for moderate-to-severe vasomotor symptoms due to menopause[532](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=532) . Veozah was the first NK3-receptor antagonist approved for this indication.


### Rheumatology, nephrology, and dermatology


Avacopan (Tavneos), approved October 2021[185](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=185) , is "a complement 5a receptor (C5aR) antagonist that inhibits the interaction between C5aR and the anaphylatoxin C5a," blocking C5a-mediated neutrophil activation[188](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=188) . It is an adjunctive treatment for severe active ANCA-associated vasculitis (granulomatosis with polyangiitis and microscopic polyangiitis)[188](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=188) , and was the first oral C5a-receptor antagonist. Difelikefalin (Korsuva), approved August 2021[90](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=90) , is "a kappa opioid receptor (KOR) agonist"[96](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=96) indicated for moderate-to-severe pruritus associated with chronic kidney disease in adults on hemodialysis[96](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=96) . As a peripherally restricted kappa-opioid agonist, it exploits a class A GPCR for an antipruritic rather than analgesic effect.


### Hepatology and critical care


Terlipressin (Terlivaz), approved September 2022[75](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=75) , is "a synthetic vasopressin analogue with twice the selectivity for vasopressin V1 receptors versus V2 receptors"[533](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=533) , acting on class A vasopressin GPCRs to raise renal blood flow by reducing portal hypertension. It is indicated to improve kidney function in adults with hepatorenal syndrome and rapid reduction in kidney function[534](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=534) .


### Hematology and oncology support


Motixafortide (Aphexda), approved September 2023[41](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=41) , is "an inhibitor of the C-X-C Motif Chemokine Receptor 4 (CXCR4)" that blocks binding of the ligand CXCL12/SDF-1[518](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=518) , disrupting stem-cell anchoring in the marrow. It is indicated in combination with filgrastim to mobilize hematopoietic stem cells for autologous transplantation in multiple myeloma[518](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=518) . CXCR4 is a class A chemokine GPCR, a target more familiar from HIV and oncology biology than from conventional small-molecule pharmacology.


### Addiction and emergency medicine


Nalmefene (Opvee), approved May 2023[113](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=113) , "is an antagonist at opioid receptors" with a longer duration of action than naloxone[522](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=522) , indicated for emergency treatment of known or suspected opioid overdose in patients aged 12 and older[522](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=522) . A nalmefene autoinjector (Zurnai) followed in August 2024[136](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=136) . Opioid receptors are class A GPCRs, and several buprenorphine reformulations acting as partial mu-opioid agonists (for example Brixadi, approved May 2023[100](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=100) ) also reached the market, alongside the meloxicam/rizatriptan combination Symbravo (January 2025[35](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/fda-drug-approvals-targeting-gpcr-mechanisms-2021?showSourceNumber=35) ), whose triptan component engages 5-HT1B/1D serotonin GPCRs.


### What the pattern shows


Three observations stand out for regulatory and competitive-intelligence purposes. First, class B secretin-family receptors (GLP-1, GIP, glucagon, CGRP) drove both the highest-volume approvals and the most commercially significant ones, and the arrival of an oral GLP-1 receptor agonist signals a shift away from injectable peptides. Second, several approvals were deliberate first-in-class moves against long-validated but previously undrugged GPCRs, notably muscarinic agonism for schizophrenia, C5a-receptor antagonism for vasculitis, and NK3-receptor antagonism for menopause. Third, receptor-subtype selectivity, rather than a new target entirely, increasingly differentiates products within a class, as seen across the S1P modulators and the CGRP-receptor gepants. A reader tracking a specific indication or receptor subfamily would find it worth asking Rhizome directly for the underlying review documents, pivotal-trial endpoints, and any post-marketing or exclusivity details tied to each of these approvals.
