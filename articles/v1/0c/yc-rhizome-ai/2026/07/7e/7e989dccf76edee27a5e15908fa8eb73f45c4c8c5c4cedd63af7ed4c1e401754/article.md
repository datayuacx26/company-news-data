# Primary Endpoints for Phase 3 Huntington's Disease Trials

## Primary endpoints for Phase 3 Huntington's disease trials: what regulators have accepted


### The core distinction: what is the trial trying to show?


There is no single "best" primary endpoint for HD. The defensible choice depends on the therapeutic claim, and the regulatory record splits cleanly into two archetypes:


1. **Symptomatic trials targeting chorea** (VMAT2 inhibitors). Here the established, FDA- and EMA-accepted primary endpoint is the **UHDRS Total Maximal Chorea (TMC) score** .
2. **Disease-modifying trials** (antisense oligonucleotides, small molecules aiming to slow progression). Here sponsors have used **Total Functional Capacity (TFC)** , **Total Motor Score (TMS)** , or the **composite UHDRS (cUHDRS)** as the primary measure of global clinical decline.


### Symptomatic (chorea) trials: UHDRS Total Maximal Chorea


The TMC score is the through-line across all three approved chorea drugs. It is UHDRS Part I, item 12 (a–g), rating chorea in seven body regions from 0 to 4 each, for a total range of 0 to 28[1](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=1)[2](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=2)[22](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=22)[25](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=25) . The primary analysis in each pivotal trial was the change from baseline to a "maintenance"/end-of-treatment window defined as the average of two late visits, which dampens visit-to-visit variability.


- **Tetrabenazine (Xenazine), TETRA-HD (Protocol TBZ 103,004):** primary endpoint was change in UHDRS TMC from baseline to the maintenance phase, defined as the average of Week 9 and Week 12 scores[1](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=1)[2](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=2)[6](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=6)[11](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=11) . Key secondary endpoints were the Clinical Global Impression (CGI), UHDRS Total Motor Score, the UHDRS Functional Assessment Checklist (Part IV), and a UHDRS gait item[11](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=11)[19](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=19) .
- **Deutetrabenazine (Austedo), First-HD (SD-809-C-15):** primary endpoint was change in UHDRS TMC from baseline to maintenance, with maintenance defined as the mean of the Week 9 and Week 12 scores[28](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=28)[29](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=29)[35](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=35) . Secondary endpoints were Patient Global Impression of Change (PGIC) and Clinical Global Impression of Change (CGIC) at Week 12, SF-36 Physical Functioning, and the Berg Balance Test[28](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=28)[29](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=29)[35](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=35) . EMA's assessment of the same program identified the identical UHDRS-TMC primary endpoint and maintenance-window definition[39](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=39) .
- **Valbenazine (Ingrezza), KINECT-HD:** primary endpoint was change in UHDRS TMC from baseline to end of treatment, with end of treatment defined as the average of Week 10 and Week 12[22](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=22)[25](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=25) . Key secondary endpoints were CGI-C and PGI-C responder/impression analyses[127](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=127)[128](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=128) .


Takeaway for a chorea program: UHDRS TMC (baseline to an averaged end-of-treatment window) is the endpoint with the deepest regulatory precedent, and clinician- and patient-rated global impression scales (CGI-C, PGI-C/PGIC) are the conventional key secondaries that support the clinical meaningfulness of a TMC change.


### Disease-modifying trials: TFC, TMS, and the composite UHDRS


For interventions claiming to slow progression rather than suppress chorea, single-symptom scales are too narrow, and sponsors have anchored on measures of global functional/clinical decline. The registry record shows three approaches in Phase 3:


- **Total Functional Capacity (TFC):** change from baseline in TFC has been used as a Phase 3 primary endpoint in early HD, with timepoints seen at 52 weeks in the EU register[120](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=120)[121](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=121)[122](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=122) . TFC captures global daily independence (work, chores, finances, activities of daily living)[88](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=88)[89](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=89)[92](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=92) .
- **Total Motor Score (TMS):** used as a primary or co-primary motor measure, with a 26-week timeframe noted in one EU Phase 3 record before extension to TFC over 52 weeks[121](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=121)[122](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=122) . TMS spans oculomotor function, dysarthria, chorea, dystonia, gait, and postural stability[82](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=82)[88](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=88)[89](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=89) .
- **Composite UHDRS (cUHDRS):** used as the Phase 3 primary in the tominersen program, specified as change from baseline in cUHDRS (with TFC as the primary for U.S. participants) at 16 months[78](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=78) ; a related EU record specifies change from baseline to Week 65 in cUHDRS total score in the mITT population[120](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=120)[123](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=123) .


Registry text also shows cognitive and secondary measures (SDMT, Stroop Word Reading, TMS) tracked alongside the primary in the tominersen Phase 3 at 16 months[78](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=78) , and a separate Phase 3 metformin study using a composite UHDRS **cognitive** score (built from SDMT, verbal fluency, color naming, word naming, and Stroop interference) as its primary endpoint[80](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=80) . This illustrates that "the primary endpoint" in disease-modifying HD trials is a design choice among validated global or domain-specific composites, not a fixed regulatory standard.


### The composite UHDRS (cUHDRS) in detail


Because the cUHDRS is increasingly the default global endpoint for disease-modifying trials, its construction matters. It is an **equally weighted composite of four UHDRS-based measures** : TFC, TMS, SDMT (Symbol Digit Modalities Test), and Stroop Word Reading. Each component is converted to a z-score and the z-scores are summed[87](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=87)[89](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=89)[90](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=90) . The components cover:


- **TFC** – global daily functioning/independence[88](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=88)[89](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=89)[92](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=92)
- **TMS** – motor impairment (oculomotor, dysarthria, chorea, dystonia, gait, postural stability)[82](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=82)[88](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=88)[89](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=89)
- **SDMT** – cognitive processing speed, attention, working memory, psychomotor speed[88](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=88)[89](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=89)[92](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=92)
- **Stroop Word Reading** – attention, processing speed, mental flexibility/psychomotor speed[82](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=82)[88](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=88)[89](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=89)


The rationale for using it as a primary endpoint is that it captures multidomain clinical decline (motor, cognitive, functional) rather than a single domain, tracks underlying progressive brain changes, and relates to change in daily functional ability[89](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=89) . It is also sensitive to decline; a change of at least 1.2 points has been characterized as a meaningful worsening[87](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=87)[90](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=90) .

### Practical considerations for a Phase 3 design


- **Match the endpoint to the mechanism and label you want.** A VMAT2-type chorea suppressant has a clear, precedented path with UHDRS TMC and global-impression secondaries. A disease-modifying agent needs a global or multidomain measure (TFC or cUHDRS) and a longer readout (roughly 12 to 16 months) to detect a slope difference.
- **Use averaged end-of-treatment windows for chorea.** Every approved chorea drug averaged two late visits for the TMC primary, reducing measurement noise; regulators have accepted this consistently[1](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=1)[29](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=29)[22](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=22) .
- **Pre-specify how clinical meaningfulness is supported.** CGI-C and PGI-C/PGIC have served as the anchor secondaries that translate a TMC point change into a patient-relevant effect[28](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=28)[127](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=127)[128](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=128) . For cUHDRS, a pre-specified meaningful-change threshold (for example, ≥1.2) helps interpretation[87](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=87)[90](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=90) .
- **Cognitive scales are usually components or secondaries, not standalone primaries** , except where cognition is the explicit therapeutic target (as in the metformin cognitive-composite design)[78](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=78)[80](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=80) .


### Limitations


The chorea (symptomatic) endpoints are drawn from FDA review/label records and the EMA EPAR and are well established. The disease-modifying endpoints are drawn from trial-registry protocol text (ClinicalTrials.gov, CTIS, EudraCT); registry excerpts sometimes state the scales and visit schedule without the full primary-endpoint wording, so exact estimands (analysis population, comparison timepoint) should be confirmed against each trial's statistical analysis plan[56](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=56)[78](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=78)[120](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/huntingtons_disease_clinical_trial_endpoints?showSourceNumber=120) .
