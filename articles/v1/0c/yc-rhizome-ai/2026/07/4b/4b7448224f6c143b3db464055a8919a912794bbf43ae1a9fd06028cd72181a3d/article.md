# Patient-Reported Outcomes in CDER Prescribing Information

## Patient-reported outcomes in CDER-approved prescribing information (2021 to 2026)


### Short answer


Yes. Patient-reported outcomes (PROs) appear in the prescribing information (PI) of multiple products approved or relabeled by CDER over the past five years. They show up in two ways: as formal efficacy endpoints described in Section 14 (Clinical Studies), and as supportive symptom measures. The most consistent uptake is in dermatology (itch scales), acute migraine (most bothersome symptom freedom), acute pain, and inflammatory bowel disease. Examples below span new molecular entities, new therapeutic biologics, and labeling supplements to older products.


### PROs used as efficacy endpoints in Section 14


The clearest signal is products where a patient-reported instrument served as, or directly supported, the registrational efficacy endpoint.


**Dermatology (itch as a co-primary or primary endpoint).** Itch is inherently patient-reported, and CDER has accepted validated single-item itch scales as pivotal endpoints.


- DUPIXENT (dupilumab) for prurigo nodularis: efficacy was assessed by the proportion of subjects achieving a reduction of at least 4 points on the Worst Itch-Numeric Rating Scale (WI-NRS), a single-item 0 to 10 scale, in the PRIME and PRIME2 trials, described as a primary efficacy endpoint[2](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=2) . For atopic dermatitis, Dupixent labeling reports the Peak Pruritus NRS[4](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=4)[5](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=5)[6](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=6)[7](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=7)[8](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=8)[12](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=12)[13](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=13)[20](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=20) .
- NEMLUVIO (nemolizumab-ilto), a CDER-reviewed biologic approved December 13, 2024[101](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=101) , uses the Peak Pruritus NRS (PP-NRS), a weekly average of daily 0 to 10 maximal-itch scores, in prurigo nodularis (OLYMPIA 1/2) and atopic dermatitis (ARCADIA 1/2). In ARCADIA, a PP-NRS improvement of more than 4 points at Week 16 was a key secondary endpoint alongside the IGA/EASI-75 co-primaries[9](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=9)[15](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=15)[95](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=95) .
- LYNAVOY (linerixibat) for pruritus in primary biliary cholangitis, approved March 17, 2026[136](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=136) , is built around a Worst Itch NRS: patients rate worst itch twice daily on a 0 to 10 scale, aggregated into Worst Daily, Weekly, and Monthly Itch Scores[1](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=1) .


**Acute pain (numeric pain rating).** JOURNAVX (suzetrigine), a non-opioid NDA approved January 30, 2025[98](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=98) , measured pain intensity with a patient-reported 11-point Numeric Pain Rating Scale (NPRS) and evaluated efficacy by the time-weighted sum of pain intensity difference over 48 hours (SPID48) versus placebo in acute pain after abdominoplasty and bunionectomy[62](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=62)[64](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=64) .


**Acute migraine (most bothersome symptom freedom).** "MBS freedom" at 2 hours is a patient-reported co-primary endpoint now standard across the gepant and ditan classes, and it is carried in current labeling for:


- NURTEC ODT (rimegepant), pain freedom and MBS freedom at 2 hours[43](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=43)[48](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=48)[51](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=51)[52](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=52)[54](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=54) (original approval February 27, 2020[135](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=135) );
- UBRELVY (ubrogepant), pain freedom, pain relief, and MBS freedom[47](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=47)[55](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=55)[56](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=56) (December 23, 2019[124](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=124) );
- REYVOW (lasmiditan), pain freedom and MBS freedom[57](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=57)[58](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=58) (October 11, 2019[148](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=148) );
- VYEPTI (eptinezumab-jjmr), headache pain freedom and absence of MBS on infusion day[59](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=59)[61](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=61) (February 21, 2020[142](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=142) );
- ELYXYB (celecoxib oral solution), pain freedom and MBS freedom[44](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=44) (May 5, 2020[149](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=149) );
- SYMBRAVO (meloxicam/rizatriptan), pain freedom and MBS freedom at 2 hours, approved January 30, 2025[45](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=45)[46](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=46)[49](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=49)[50](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=50)[96](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=96) .


Several of these migraine products were originally approved just before the five-year window, but the MBS PRO content is present in their current, in-window labeling.


**Inflammatory bowel disease.** OMVOH (mirikizumab-mrkz), approved as a biologic on October 26, 2023[119](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=119) , reports patient-reported abdominal pain (improvement as early as Week 6), stool frequency (by Week 12), and fatigue measured by the FACIT-Fatigue scale at Week 12 in its Crohn's disease program; the label also references a patient-reported clinical-response definition used to re-randomize placebo non-responders[75](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=75)[76](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=76)[82](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=82) .


### PROs as supportive measures


A second group reports PROs descriptively rather than as the registrational endpoint.


- COSENTYX (secukinumab): in plaque psoriasis trials PsO1/PsO2, labeling reports Week 12 improvements in itching, pain, and scaling versus placebo using the Psoriasis Symptom Diary[65](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=65)[66](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=66)[71](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=71) .
- XYWAV (mixed oxybate) for idiopathic hypersomnia reports the Epworth Sleepiness Scale and Patient Global Impression of change, alongside the Idiopathic Hypersomnia Severity Scale[23](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=23)[26](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=26)[31](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=31) (original approval July 21, 2020[115](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=115) ).
- CYMBALTA / DRIZALMA SPRINKLE (duloxetine) fibromyalgia labeling reports Fibromyalgia Impact Questionnaire results and Patient Global Impression of Change[36](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=36)[39](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=39) .
- XALKORI (crizotinib) reports patient-reported lung-cancer symptoms (dyspnea, cough, chest pain) as exploratory measures in ALK-positive metastatic NSCLC[25](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=25)[27](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=27)[28](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=28) .
- ZOLOFT (sertraline) social anxiety disorder labeling includes patient-rated instruments such as the Marks Fear Questionnaire Social Phobia Subscale[30](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=30)[41](https://rhizomeai.com/articles/patient_reported_outcomes?showSourceNumber=41) .

### Reading the pattern


Two things stand out for a labeling strategist. First, where the disease is defined by a symptom the patient alone can report (itch, pain, migraine bother), CDER has accepted a validated single-item PRO as a primary or co-primary endpoint, and that endpoint reaches Section 14 verbatim: WI-NRS and PP-NRS in dermatology, MBS freedom in acute migraine, and NPRS/SPID in acute pain are now effectively expected. Second, in areas where PROs supplement objective endpoints (IBD, psoriasis, oncology, sleep, psychiatry), the instruments still appear in labeling but as descriptive support rather than the basis of the indication. The distinction between an original approval within the window and a labeling supplement matters here: several migraine and sleep products carry in-window PRO labeling despite original approvals in 2019 to 2020.


### Caveats and suggested follow-ups


This survey is illustrative, not exhaustive. PRO terminology varies across reviewers, and some instruments (for example generic quality-of-life questionnaires) are more likely to sit in review documents than in the final PI. If you need a defensible, complete list, useful next questions to put to Rhizome directly include: a full enumeration of Section 14 PRO endpoints for a specific therapeutic area; whether a given PRO was labeled as primary, key secondary, or supportive for a named product; and how a specific instrument (say, WI-NRS or FACIT-Fatigue) was validated and described across the reviews that supported approval.
