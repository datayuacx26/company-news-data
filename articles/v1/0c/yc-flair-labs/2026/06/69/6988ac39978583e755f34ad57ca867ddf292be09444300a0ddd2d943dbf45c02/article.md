# Compliance as a feature

Voice AI in mortgage isn't a novelty — it's automated outreach in one of the most heavily regulated industries there is. If it isn't compliant, it isn't usable. So we treated compliance as a design constraint from the first line of code, not a disclaimer to bolt on at the end.


That decision shapes the whole product. When compliance is an afterthought, it becomes a manual checklist someone has to enforce on top of the system. When it's built in, the safe path and the easy path are the same path.


## Consent-first by default


Every call and message honors consent state and contact-time rules, and opt-outs are respected automatically. The standard is simple: never put a lender in a position they'd have to explain after the fact. Consent isn't assumed from a stale record — it's checked before the system reaches out.


## Disclosures that always get made


Required disclosures are easy for a human to forget on the busy call and impossible for the system to skip. The conversation carries the disclosures it's supposed to, every time, in the right place. Consistency is one of the quiet advantages of automation done carefully — the script doesn't get tired or rushed.


## Recorded and traceable


Every interaction is recorded, transcribed, and logged into a complete, reviewable record. Compliance teams get traceability they can actually use, and borrowers get a consistent, professional experience. When a question comes up later, the answer isn't a guess — it's a transcript.


- Consent and contact-time rules checked before every interaction.
- Opt-outs honored promptly and across channels.
- Required disclosures delivered consistently, never skipped.
- A full, searchable record of every call and message.


Done right, automation stops being a risk you manage and becomes infrastructure you can stand behind. That's the bar we hold ourselves to, because anything less doesn't belong anywhere near a regulated business.
