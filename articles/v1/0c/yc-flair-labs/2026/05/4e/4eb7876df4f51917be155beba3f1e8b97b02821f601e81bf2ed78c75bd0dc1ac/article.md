# Connecting to your CRM

Most AI features arriving in mortgage share a quiet limitation: they only work inside the platform that ships them. If your CRM bundles an AI add-on, that intelligence lives and dies inside that CRM. Switch tools, or run more than one, and it doesn't come with you. We took the opposite approach. Flair runs on top of the stack you already have.


## Your CRM stays the source of truth


Flair doesn't ask you to migrate, rip and replace, or learn a new system of record. It connects to the CRM you already run and treats it as the source of truth. New leads flow in, conversations and outcomes flow back, and your team keeps working in the tool they already know. The worker handles the conversations on top of the system, not instead of it.


## A closed loop, not a dead end


A connection only earns its keep if it works in both directions. When Flair qualifies a lead, books an appointment, or learns something on a call, that has to land back in the CRM where the team will see it — not get stranded in a separate dashboard nobody opens. The point of the integration is a closed loop: the CRM stays current without anyone copying notes by hand.


## One worker across the whole journey


Because Flair isn't tied to a single platform, the same worker can qualify a new lead today, help coordinate the next step in processing later, and answer a servicing question down the road — even when those stages live in different systems. The borrower gets one continuous relationship instead of a handful of disconnected features stitched across tools.


Your systems should serve your borrowers, not the other way around. An AI worker that respects the stack you've already built is one you can actually adopt without tearing anything down.
