# Noninvasive Scale Measurement of Stroke Volume and Cardiac Output Compared With the Direct Fick Method: A Feasibility Study, Journal of American Heart Failure, JAHA

With this research, we demonstrate that a connected cardiac scale is able to approximate stroke volume and cardiac output compared to the direct Fick method. The Bodyport Cardiac Scale has the potential to be deployed in the home setting to easily and longitudinally monitor cardiac function.
