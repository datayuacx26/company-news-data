# Vessels

######


Who better to tell the story of ages than those who’ve held the Earth tightly in their grasp? Clutching the secrets of seasons and centuries. Cradling color. Time. Treasure.


These keepers of history and heritage are actually roots of trees. Writing in ringed-lines and sap stains, they’ve become the natural world’s most unassuming scribes.


Only like souls could coax from these massive woods their stories. And these souls do exist. We know because we’ve met them. They are a family of artisans. A father and his sons. Javier. Rodrigo, Pablo, and Alejandro. Each a thoughtful listener. Attentive to the lives lived in each tree and recorded in their roots.
