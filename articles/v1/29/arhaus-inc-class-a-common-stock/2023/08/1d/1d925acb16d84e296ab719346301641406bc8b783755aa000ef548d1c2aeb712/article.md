# A Laboratory of Logs

######


Rousing color from a hidden hollow takes skill and study. A proficiency gained through a lifelong mastery of woodworking. And through continual trial and error.


One particular quest for learning takes place most every Saturday, when a busy countryside sawmill transforms into a craftsman’s laboratory. Here, Javier experiments. With wood, techniques, and finishes. In whatever direction his mind chooses to wander. His creatively imprecise efforts share a singular goal: to encourage the beauty and characteristics of the wood to reveal themselves. “Our father has always said to let nature lead,” says his son, Pablo.


In this fascinating lab of logs, Javier toys with chemistry, history, and artistry. Often by unexpected means and methods.
