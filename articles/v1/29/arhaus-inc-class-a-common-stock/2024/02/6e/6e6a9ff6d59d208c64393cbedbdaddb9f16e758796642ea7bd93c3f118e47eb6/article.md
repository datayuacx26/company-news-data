# The Artistry of Our Italy

One of our first partnerships in Italy began more than 30 years ago. I had been traveling through Northern Italy to meet with another potential partner, who suggested it would be worth my time to take the small detour required to meet Rino, a furniture builder, painter, and antique collector.


I found myself as moved by his work as I was by Rino himself. Hands in constant motion as he told stories, his head thrown back in laughter, Rino and I talked about the importance of craftsmanship and family as his young daughters played nearby.


We curated a small assortment of tables and cabinets that would eventually become known as our Bell’Arte Collection, and it wasn’t long before our customers fell in love with the lived-in look and whimsical, hand-painted detailing of Rino’s work. It was unlike anything they had ever seen—and the incredible quality couldn’t be found anywhere else.


Over the years, as we added more artisan partners, our Italian Collection gradually expanded to include gold-leaf lighting that glistens with hand-tied crystals, the expressive forms and inviting curves of hand-built seating, and so much more. Each handcrafted piece reflected the spirit of Italy—and echoed our commitment to the artistry of its makers.


Somewhere along the way, over the course of years and visits and lingering, pass-the-plate dinners, those makers started to feel more like friends…and eventually, like family. We were welcomed, arms open, into sun-dappled studios and bustling workshops. To see. To explore. To share and be inspired. This is, after all, the Italian way… and I believe it can be felt and seen in every piece we offer to this day.


Rino is still creating the pieces of our Bell’Arte Collection, by the way. Only now, his daughters are working beside him, carefully preserving the artistry they share. It’s this artistry that I am so proud to share with you—the same artistry that takes me back to Italy again and again, even if only in my mind.


###### JOHN REED, ARHAUS CO-FOUNDER
