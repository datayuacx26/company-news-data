# Propexo CEO Joins Real Estate Tech Panel at Thesis Driven Event

Remen Okoruwa, Co-Founder and CEO of Propexo, recently shared his perspectives on the state of real estate technology in a panel event moderated by Thesis Driven's Brad Hargreaves on Tuesday, April 8. The event was hosted at Gunderson Dettmer's office in New York City.


The discussion covered a wide range of topics, including the current fundraising environment, proptech sectors that are heating up and cooling down, as well as the exciting AI applications that have the greatest potential to transform real estate.
