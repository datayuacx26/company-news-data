# Propexo CEO Speaks on Panel for ULI TREC Product Council

Remen Okoruwa, Co-Founder & CEO of Propexo, yesterday participated as a panelist for the Urban Land Institute's Technology in Real Estate Council (TREC) during the Spring Meeting in New York City.


The mission of TREC is exploring how technology impacts development and development trends as well as how the real estate industry can help drive technology for better land-use decision-making.


To learn more about the Urban Land Institute, visit their website:[americas.uli.org](https://americas.uli.org/)
