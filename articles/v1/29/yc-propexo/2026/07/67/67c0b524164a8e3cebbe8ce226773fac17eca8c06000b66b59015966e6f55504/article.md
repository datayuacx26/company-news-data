# Propexo CEO Featured in Article "The Future of the Property Management System"

Remen Okoruwa, Co-founder and CEO of Propexo, was recently featured in an in-depth analysis exploring the evolution and future of property management systems (PMS).


Drawing from his experience as a former HubSpot Senior PM, Okoruwa highlighted the stark contrast between traditional software platforms and current PMS solutions, noting that proptech companies spend significant engineering resources on PMS integration maintenance.


In the article, Okoruwa advocated for a more open, API-first approach similar to platforms like Salesforce and HubSpot, while explaining how Propexo's unified API solution helps companies efficiently connect with multiple property management systems.


The feature also explored Propexo's unique position in the ecosystem, where they maintain both collaborative and arms-length relationships with PMS platforms as they work to modernize real estate technology integration.
