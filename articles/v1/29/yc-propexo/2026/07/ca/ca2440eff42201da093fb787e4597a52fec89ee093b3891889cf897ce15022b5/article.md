# Propexo CEO Named to Inaugural Thesis Driven Innovation 100 List

Remen Okoruwa, Co-Founder and CEO of Propexo, has been named to the prestigious Thesis Driven Innovation 100, a curated list recognizing the most influential individuals shaping the future of real estate.


Selected by a distinguished panel of real estate media executives including leaders from Ten31 Media, LoopNet, and Propmodo, Okoruwa was honored for his pioneering work in simplifying property management system integrations through Propexo's innovative plug-and-play platform.


The recognition highlights Propexo's disruptive approach to addressing one of proptech's most significant challenges: the complexity of integrating with multiple property management systems.


The selection committee specifically noted Propexo's impact in reducing barriers for new software products entering the multifamily space.


The complete Thesis Driven Innovation 100 list can be viewed at[Thesis Driven](https://www.thesisdriven.com/p/thesis-driven-innovation-100-51-100) .
