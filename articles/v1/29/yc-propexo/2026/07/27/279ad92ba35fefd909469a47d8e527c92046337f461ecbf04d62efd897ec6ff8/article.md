# Propexo Announces Partnership with MRI Software

**Boston, MA - January 31, 2025** - Propexo announced today it has partnered with MRI Software, a global provider of real estate software solutions that transform the way communities live, work and play. Propexo offers an integration platform that allows technology vendors to integrate their products with multiple property management systems. The collaboration with MRI will offer enhanced integration service capabilities to new partners, helping them quickly leverage the broader MRI ecosystem.


"MRI has been a trailblazer in property management software for decades and remains a top integration target for multifamily technology vendors," said Remen Okoruwa, Co-Founder & CEO of Propexo. "With this partnership, we're better able to help new vendors looking to become MRI partners deeply integrate their products with MRI's solutions, accelerating time-to-market and reducing engineering costs."


"We're thrilled to welcome Propexo to the MRI Partner Connect program," said Sean Slack, VP of Partnerships at MRI Software. "The enhanced integration capabilities support our shared mission of enabling real estate firms to achieve their goals through open, connected and flexible solutions."


Since January 2023, Propexo has processed billions of monthly data records for its customers in the United States and Canada. Propexo powers integrations used by more than half of the NMHC Top 15 Managers.


**About Propexo**


Propexo is a product integration platform that offers unified APIs to empower technology companies to integrate with multiple property management systems and rental CRMs seamlessly. Propexo empowers companies to win deals faster and save engineering costs.


Propexo is backed by Prudence, FifthWall, Y Combinator, and RXR. Propexo was founded in 2022 and is headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts. For more information, visit Propexo's website and follow the company on Linkedin.


**About MRI Software**


MRI Software is a leading provider of real estate software solutions that transform the way communities live, work and play. MRI's open and connected, AI-first platform empowers agents, owners, operators and occupiers in commercial and residential property organizations to innovate in rapidly changing markets. MRI has been a trailblazer in the PropTech industry for over five decades, serving more than six million users worldwide, including the public and affordable housing sector. Through innovative solutions and a rich partner ecosystem, MRI gives real estate companies the freedom to realize their vision of building thriving communities and stronger businesses.
