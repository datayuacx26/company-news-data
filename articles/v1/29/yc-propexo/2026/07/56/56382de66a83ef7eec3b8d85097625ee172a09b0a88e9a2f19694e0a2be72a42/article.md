# Propexo CEO Shares Entrepreneurial Journey on Humans of Multifamily Podcast

Remen Okoruwa, Co-founder and CEO of Propexo, recently appeared on the "Humans of Multifamily" podcast with host Shiv Gettu to share his entrepreneurial journey in proptech. In the conversation, Okoruwa traced his path from growing up in Iowa to attending Harvard, and his transition from HubSpot product leader to startup founder. The discussion offered candid insights into overcoming startup challenges, including pivoting the business model and finding product-market fit in multifamily technology integrations. Okoruwa shared his team's experience being accepted into Y Combinator and their vision for transforming property management system integration.
