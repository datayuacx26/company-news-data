# Propexo Announces Middleware and Integration Consulting Partnership with Entrata

Propexo announced today it has partnered with Entrata, a leading AI-powered operating system for the multifamily industry. Propexo offers an integration platform that allows technology vendors to integrate their products with multiple property management systems using an industry-first unified API.


"Entrata has been a trailblazer in multifamily integrations, focusing on building an expansive vendor ecosystem. So we're delighted to support technology vendors looking to integrate their products into Entrata," said Remen Okoruwa, Co-Founder & CEO of Propexo. "With Propexo, these vendors can go to market quickly with multiple integrations while significantly reducing engineering costs."


"Partnering with a leading player like Entrata is a big opportunity for us," Remen Okoruwa added. "This partnership will allow us to better support the hundreds of existing Entrata integration vendors and new vendors looking to join this product ecosystem."


Since January 2023, Propexo has processed billions of monthly data records for its customers in the United States and Canada. Propexo powers integrations used by more than half of the NMHC Top 15 Managers.


**About Propexo**


Propexo is a product integration platform that offers unified APIs to empower technology companies to integrate with multiple property management systems and rental CRMs seamlessly. Propexo empowers companies to win deals faster and save engineering costs.


Propexo is backed by Prudence, Fifth Wall, Y Combinator, and RXR. Propexo was founded in 2022 and is headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts. For more information, visit Propexo's website and follow the company on Linkedin.


**About Entrata**


Entrata is the leading operating system for multifamily communities worldwide. Setting the bar for innovation in property management software since 2003, Entrata offers solutions for every step of the leasing lifecycle and empowers owners, property managers, and renters to create stronger communities. Entrata currently serves over three million residents across more than 20 thousand multifamily communities around the globe. Learn more at[www.entrata.com](https://www.entrata.com/) .
