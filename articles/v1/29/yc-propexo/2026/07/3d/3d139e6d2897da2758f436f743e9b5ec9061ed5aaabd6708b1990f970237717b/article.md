# Propexo CEO Featured in Tangent Proptech podcast

Remen Okoruwa, Co-founder and CEO of Propexo, recently appeared on the Tangent podcast to share insights on streamlining property management technology integration in the multifamily real estate sector.


During the interview, Okoruwa discussed Propexo's mission to become the "Plaid for PropTech" and their innovative approach to connecting property management software systems. The discussion covered crucial industry topics including Propexo's customer-centric product roadmap, the current state of PropTech integration, and its impact on resident experience and NOI improvement.


Okoruwa also shared valuable perspectives on recent industry developments, including the RealPage antitrust case and its implications for the PropTech ecosystem.
