# Propexo Announces Integration Partnership with ResMan

**Boston, MA - February 6, 2025** - Propexo announced today it has partnered with ResMan, a leading property management system helping multifamily managers operate more efficiently and deliver higher returns to investors. Propexo offers an integration platform that allows technology vendors to integrate their products with multiple property management systems using an industry-first unified API.


"Multifamily technology vendors recognize the importance of integrating into ResMan and other property management systems, but building and maintaining these integrations is often costly and time-consuming," said Remen Okoruwa, Co-Founder & CEO of Propexo. "With Propexo, technology vendors can launch multiple integrations quickly while reducing ongoing maintenance costs. This allows our customers to speed up go-to-market and focus their engineering resources on their product rather than 3rd party integrations."


"Partnering with a leading system like ResMan is an exciting opportunity for our company," Remen Okoruwa added. "This partnership will allow us to support the hundred-plus vendors already integrated with ResMan in addition to new vendors looking to become ResMan partners."


Since January 2023, Propexo has processed billions of monthly data records for its customers in the United States and Canada. Propexo powers integrations used by more than half of the NMHC Top 15 Managers.


**About Propexo**


Propexo is a product integration platform that offers unified APIs to empower technology companies to integrate with multiple property management systems and rental CRMs seamlessly. Propexo empowers companies to win deals faster and save engineering costs.


Propexo is backed by Prudence, Fifth Wall, Y Combinator, and RXR. Propexo was founded in 2022 and is headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts. For more information, visit Propexo's website and follow the company on Linkedin.


**About ResMan**


ResMan is the preferred growth partner that drives profitability and efficiency for nearly a thousand property management companies across the U.S. ResMan delivers the property management industry's most innovative technology platform, making property investments and operations more profitable and easier to manage. ResMan's platform unlocks a new path to growth for property management companies that deliver consistent NOI improvement and brilliant resident experiences easier than ever before.
