# Propexo CEO Moderates Multifamily AI Panel at OPTECH Conference

Remen Okoruwa, Co-Founder and CEO of Propexo, recently moderated a distinguished panel exploring artificial intelligence's impact on multifamily real estate at the National Multifamily Housing Council's 2024 OPTECH Conference.


The session, "Unleashing the Power of AI: Opportunities and Challenges for Multifamily," brought together an elite group of industry leaders including Willow Bridge COO Margette Hepfner, RPM Living SVP Operations Matt Rogers, Yardi Industry Principal Paul Yount, and AvalonBay Communities VP of Product Pratik Dhebri.


The panel delved into how leading multifamily organizations are leveraging AI to transform property operations and enhance resident experiences. Okoruwa guided the discussion through critical topics including current AI applications in property management, emerging use cases for operational efficiency, and strategies to accelerate industry-wide AI adoption.


The conversation highlighted both the transformative potential and implementation challenges of AI technology in multifamily real estate, providing attendees with actionable insights for their own AI initiatives.


More information about the OPTECH 2024 conference can be found[here](https://www.nmhc.org/meetings/calendar/OPTECH/2024-optech-conference-expo/) .
