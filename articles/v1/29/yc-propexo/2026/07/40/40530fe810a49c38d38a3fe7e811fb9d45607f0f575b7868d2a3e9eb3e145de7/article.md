# Propexo CEO Analyzes Impact of Revenue Management Lawsuits on Proptech Innovation

Remen Okoruwa, Co-Founder and CEO of Propexo, recently appeared on "Tech Talk with 20for20," hosted by industry expert Dom Beveridge, to discuss the far-reaching implications of recent revenue management lawsuits on multifamily technology innovation.


During the conversation, Okoruwa provided unique insights into how regulatory actions are reshaping data sharing practices in the multifamily industry, particularly highlighting the challenges these changes pose for AI solutions that rely on aggregated data.


The discussion explored how property management organizations are proactively adjusting their data policies in response to legal pressures, while Okoruwa emphasized the growing importance of industry collaboration in advancing technological innovation.


The Propexo CEO also addressed potential solutions, including the development of proactive industry standards to protect valuable technologies, while sharing his company's perspective as a key player in property management system integration.
