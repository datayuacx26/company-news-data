# Propexo CEO Speaks on Panel Discussing Multifamily Tech Stacks at Digible Summit

Remen Okoruwa, Co-Founder and CEO of Propexo, recently participated in a thought-provoking panel discussion on multifamily technology integration at the 2023 Digible Summit in Denver.


The session, "Your Tech Stack: Working in Harmony," brought together industry leaders including executives from The Life Properties, PERQ, and RevolutionRE to explore the challenges and opportunities in modern property technology adoption.


During the discussion, moderated by proptech consultant Khet Richards, Okoruwa shared insights on the unique integration challenges facing the multifamily industry, particularly the complexities of API integration and data synchronization across multiple platforms.


The panel emphasized the importance of balancing artificial intelligence with human interaction in property operations, noting that while the average multifamily operator now manages six or more technology vendors, successful implementation requires careful pilot programs and clear success metrics.


The conversation highlighted the critical need for better data integration in multifamily, with Okoruwa and fellow panelists providing practical guidance on evaluating and implementing new technology solutions while maintaining focus on core KPIs and operational efficiency.
