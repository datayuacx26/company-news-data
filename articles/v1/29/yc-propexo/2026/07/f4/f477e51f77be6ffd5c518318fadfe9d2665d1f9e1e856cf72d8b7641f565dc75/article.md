# Propexo CEO Discusses Multifamily APIs and Integrations on Podcast

Remen Okoruwa, Co-founder and CEO of Propexo, recently appeared on the popular multifamily technology podcast "Tiff Talks Tech" to discuss the fundamental role of APIs in revolutionizing property management workflows.


In the episode titled "APIs for Dummies: Making the Most of Your Integrations to Create Efficient Workflows," Okoruwa broke down complex technical concepts into accessible insights for multifamily professionals.


The discussion explored how APIs function as universal translators between different software applications, enabling seamless data flow and operational efficiency in property management.


During the conversation, Okoruwa shared valuable perspectives on common integration challenges faced by vendors and provided practical strategies for maximizing the effectiveness of property technology solutions.


The episode offers essential knowledge for both technology novices and industry veterans looking to leverage APIs for improved data management and enhanced customer experiences in the multifamily space.
