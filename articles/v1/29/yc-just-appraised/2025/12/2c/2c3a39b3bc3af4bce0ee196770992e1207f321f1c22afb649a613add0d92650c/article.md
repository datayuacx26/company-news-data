# Why building Just Appraised is the most meaningful work of my career

#### How we work together matters


We recently ran an internal engagement survey, and the results meant a lot to me. Participation was high, and the feedback reflected what I have always hoped this company would be.


People feel accountable for their work because they care.
People feel seen as humans, not just as roles.
Managers lead with empathy, and teammates look out for one another.


We are not perfect, and we have room to improve. One clear area of focus coming out of the survey is recognizing great work more consistently and creating more opportunities for connection across teams. But the themes that stood out most were kindness, accountability, and gratitude. Those are the values that shape how we operate.


As a founder, that is what matters most to me.
