# The Future of the Cruise Industry Takes Center Stage at CLIA Cruise Summit


Jun 8, 2026


Share


Turning ambition into action took center stage at the Cruise Lines International Association (CLIA) UK Cruise Summit, where Carnival Corporation’s Richard Cooke joined industry leaders to tackle one of cruising’s biggest opportunities: decarbonization.


Representing the company on a panel focused on practical solutions, Cooke brought a clear message – progress happens when everyone rows in the same direction. From expanding shore power to exploring future fuels, the discussion spotlighted the importance of alignment between ports, policymakers and operators to move the industry forward.


By working closely with ports and destinations, Carnival Corporation is helping turn shared ambitions into real-world progress – supporting infrastructure development, advancing new technologies and creating pathways to scale solutions that benefit both communities and the cruise industry.
