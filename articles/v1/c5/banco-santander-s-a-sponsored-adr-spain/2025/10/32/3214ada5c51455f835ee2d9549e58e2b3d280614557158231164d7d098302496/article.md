# Driving our transformation with the creation of the Global Data & AI function

Artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming the world and the banking sector. At Santander, while we’ve already made significant steps, we’re taking it further: we want to transform our way of working to become an organization that thinks and acts with data, with AI a key ally in every decision and customer interaction.


Our aim is clear. We want to create more value, grow faster, be more efficient, and offer our customers an outstanding experience. To achieve that, we’ve created the new Global Data & AI function, which will enable us to scale our capabilities, collaborate better across the entire Group, and increase our impact quickly.


At Santander, we’re not just using AI. We’re becoming an “AI-native” institution, with the trust, scale and human touch that banking requires.


Ricardo Martín Manjón, Head of Data & AI at Banco Santander


We base our Data & AI strategy on three key pillars:


1. Transforming customer experience and our operations through artificial intelligence.
2. Making smarter decisions that draw on reliable data.
3. Accelerating impact on the business through analytical models and tangible solutions.


We’re working on a global, secure and ethical ecosystem, with robust platforms and strategic partnerships with such leading enterprises as OpenAI, Microsoft, and Amazon. Moreover, we’re going to create global AI hubs, which we’re already delivering training on and organizing our people for; and we’ll continue to strengthen our ties with startups to scale up faster and better.


AI with Ricardo Martín Manjón


But the real transformation isn’t just in the technology: it starts with us. Because AI empowers people. It opens the doors to a new way of working, making decisions, and creating value together. And when we combine data, technology and talented people, the sky’s the limit.


The future is under way. And we’re building it together.
