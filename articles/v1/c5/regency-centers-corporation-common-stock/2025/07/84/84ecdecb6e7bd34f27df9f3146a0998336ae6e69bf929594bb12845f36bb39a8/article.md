# Regency Centers Completes $357M Portfolio Acquisition in Southern California

Today, Regency Centers announced the acquisition of a portfolio of five premier suburban shopping centers, all located within the 23,000-acre Rancho Mission Viejo (“RMV”) master-planned community in Orange County, CA. The acquired portfolio consists of Bridgepark Plaza, Mercantile West, Mercantile East, Terrace Shops, and Sendero Marketplace, comprising close to 630,000 square feet in aggregate.
