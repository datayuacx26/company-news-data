# Southbury Green Honored for Outstanding Landscape Design in Southbury, CT

Regency is proud to share that[Southbury Green](https://www.southburygreenct.com/) has been recognized by the Southbury Garden Club “in recognition and appreciation for outstanding landscape design to enhance the beautification of Southbury."


Each year, the[Southbury Garden Club](https://southburygardenclub.org/) honors local businesses whose landscaping efforts make a lasting impact on the community. This recognition reflects the club’s mission to enhance Southbury’s beauty and promote landscaping excellence across town.


#### A Commitment to Curb Appeal


For Regency’s Property Manager Mistie Certain and Landscape Manager[Andrew Herold (NYM Group)](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andrew-herold-21631171/) , the award is a testament to their dedication to keeping Southbury Green vibrant year-round. From seasonal flowerbeds to neatly manicured walkways, every detail is designed to welcome visitors and reflect the charm of Southbury’s Main Street.


> We know that a beautiful, well-maintained property isn’t just nice to look at — it’s part of what makes people want to visit, shop, and spend time here,” said Mistie. “It’s an honor to be recognized by the Southbury Garden Club, and we share this recognition with our entire team.


####


#### A Community Gathering Place


Located on Main Street South, Southbury Green serves an affluent customer base with a curated mix of national retailers, local favorites, dining, and essential services. Anchored by ShopRite, the center features lifestyle brands like[J.Crew Factory](https://factory.jcrew.com/) ,[LOFT](https://www.loft.com/) , and[Chico’s](https://www.chicos/) , alongside community staples such as[Barnes & Noble](https://www.barnesandnoble.com/) ,[HomeGoods](https://www.homegoods.com/) ,[Starbucks](https://www.starbucks.com/) ,[Heritage Deli & Café](https://heritagedeliandcafe.com/) ,[Foot Solutions](https://footsolutions.com/) , and more.
