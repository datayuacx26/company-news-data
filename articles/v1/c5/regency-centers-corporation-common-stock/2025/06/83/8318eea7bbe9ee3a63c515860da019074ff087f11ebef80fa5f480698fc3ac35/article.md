# Regency's Westbard Square Celebrates Grand Opening in Bethesda, MD

Regency Centers’[Westbard Square](https://www.westbardsquare.com/) in Bethesda, MD, recently marked a major milestone with its official grand opening — a vibrant, two-day event that brought together local artisans, live entertainment, and the community at large. The event was hosted in partnership with[URBNmarket](https://www.urbnmarket.com/) , the D.C. region’s premier artisan market organizer known for curating unique shopping experiences that spotlight small businesses and handmade goods.


**A Celebration of Craft and Community**


Set along Zenith Outlook — the shopping center’s main street — URBNmarket transformed Westbard Square into a bustling pop-up market filled with creativity and community spirit. Dozens of local vendors set up shop, offering everything from handmade jewelry to small-batch pantry staples and pet treats. Highlights included:


- [Amano Real Fruit](https://amanorealfruit.com/) – delicious handcrafted preserves
- [Barbie’s Doggie Bakery](https://www.barbiesdoggiebakery.com/) – all-natural treats for furry friends
- [Bianca + Jean](https://www.biancaandjean.com/) – handmade thoughtful fabric design
- [Blazing Bella Balsamic & Olive Oil](https://blazingbella.com/collections/olive-oil?srsltid=AfmBOopGDK-xdxRfYiUI5zGsb4j2r-iYUsWdPpbz191VPbalVcm-rUmK) – gourmet oils and vinegars
- [Golden Bond Jewelry](https://www.goldenbondjewelry.com/) – fine permanent jewelry
- [True Honey Teas](https://www.truehoneyteas.com/) – locally blended teas and infusions
- [Violet Red Studio](https://violetredstudio.com/) - wall art and stationary


**A True Neighborhood Gathering Spot**


Throughout the weekend, guests of all ages — and even their pets — explored Westbard Square’s newly completed green space. The open-air plaza featured lounge seating, family-friendly games like cornhole, and live music performances on the newly inaugurated stage. The event brought Westbard’s vision to life: a welcoming, community-first destination where people can shop, connect, and unwind.


> Westbard Square’s grand opening celebration was absolutely fantastic,” said[Krista Di Iaconi](https://www.regencycenters.com/employee/2043/Krista-Di-Iaconi) , Managing Director. “I enjoyed meeting so many of the vendors—several of whom lightened my wallet—and was impressed with the variety and quality of offerings. It was especially meaningful to see the vision we’ve held for this project for more than eight years come to life.


**A Fresh Retail Mix**


Already home to a curated mix of national and local favorites including[Giant](https://giantfood.com/) ,[Starbucks](http://www.starbucks.com/) ,[Tatte Bakery & Cafe](https://tattebakery.com/) ,[Citgo](https://citgo.com/) ,[District Dabble Lab](https://www.districtdabblelab.com/) ,[Oak Barrel & Vine](https://pikedistrict.org/go/montgomery-county-liquor-and-wine) ,[U.S. Post Office](https://www.usps.com/) ,[Silver & Sons BBQ](https://www.silverandsonsbbq.com/) ,[Westbard Dental](https://westbarddental.net/) ,[Stretch Zone](https://www.stretchzone.com/) ,[Kumon Math & Reading Center](https://www.kumon.com/) , and[1 to 1 Fitness,](https://1to1fitness.com/) Westbard Square is poised to become a staple neighborhood hub.[Piccoli Piatti Pizzeria](https://piccolipiattipizzeria.com/) is set to open its doors in the coming days, with more tenants to follow.


> We couldn’t be happier with how this grand opening celebration unfolded,” said Tiffany Greenidge, Property Marketing Manager. “Working with URBNmarket was seamless, and we’re thrilled to continue this partnership. The future is bright for this property, and we’re so excited for the community to finally experience everything Westbard Square has to offer.


**More Than a Shopping Center**


Spanning 22 acres in one of the D.C. area’s most affluent neighborhoods, Westbard Square is more than a retail destination—it’s a community cornerstone. With thoughtfully designed public spaces and a strong commitment to supporting local businesses, it’s built to meet the daily needs of its neighbors while offering something more: a place to gather, celebrate, and connect.
