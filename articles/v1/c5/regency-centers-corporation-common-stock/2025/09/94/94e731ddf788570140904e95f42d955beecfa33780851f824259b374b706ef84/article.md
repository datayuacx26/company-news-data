# Regency Centers Unveils Ellis Village Center in Northern California

Regency Centers is proud to announce the development of Ellis Village Center, a premier open-air shopping destination poised to become the heart of retail in Tracy, California. Anchored by a 23,000-square-foot[Sprouts Farmers Market](https://www.sprouts.com/) , and supported by a carefully curated mix of shops, restaurants, and service retailers, Ellis Village Center is designed to meet the needs of a rapidly growing and affluent community.


> Ellis Village Center reflects Regency’s strategy of aligning with high-growth, master-planned communities,” said[John Mehigan](https://www.regencycenters.com/employee/1734/John-Mehigan) , Senior Vice President of Investments at Regency Centers. “This project is a long-term investment in a dynamic region, bringing high-quality retail to serve expanding residential neighborhoods with lasting value.


Strategically located at the gateway to the Ellis Master-Planned Community at Corral Hollow Road and Ellis Town Drive, the center will serve more than 10,000 new homes planned in the trade area. Ellis Village Center South will feature over 25,000 square feet of additional retail, as well as a prominent drive-thru restaurant opportunity. A second phase, Ellis Village Center North, offers further leasing potential and is in the planning phase.


> Ellis Village Center is designed to meet the everyday needs of Northern California families through a retail environment that fosters connection and creates a true sense of arrival,” said Craig Ramey, Development Partner for Regency Centers. “By curating the right mix of tenants and emphasizing thoughtful design, we’re creating a destination that supports both community life and regional growth.


The center’s vibrant design and merchandising strategy focus on grocery, wellness, dining, and service uses, key retail categories driving foot traffic and dwell time. The surrounding market is anchored by an affluent shopper base, with an average household income of more than $150,000 within a 5-mile radius.


Construction is scheduled to complete in late 2026. With over 12,000 new homes still to be built in the area and direct access to I-580 and I-205, Ellis Village Center is well positioned to meet long-term demand in this high-barrier, high-growth market.


Regency Centers brings its proven approach to merchandising, placemaking, and community connection to this flagship project. Ellis Village Center continues the company’s track record of pairing top-tier tenants with vibrant, well-located centers in the nation’s most desirable markets.
