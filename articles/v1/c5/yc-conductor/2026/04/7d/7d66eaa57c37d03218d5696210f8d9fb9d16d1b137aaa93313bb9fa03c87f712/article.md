# cmd joins Conductor


I'm very excited to announce that[cmd](https://getcmd.dev/) is joining Conductor!


[Guillaume Sabran](https://www.linkedin.com/in/guillaume-sabran/?locale=en) created cmd to bring AI into Xcode. He previously worked 6 years as a staff engineer at Airbnb, where he managed the iOS Platform team.


We first encountered Gui in our feedback portal. He was one of our earliest power users, and wasn't afraid to let us know about our bugs :)


As we got to know each other I became increasingly impressed. Gui's got a special mix of deep technical expertise and startup scrappiness, and he's worked on dev tools for the past decade.


But perhaps most importantly: working together was just *fun* . Gui is thoughtful, wise, and likes moving extremely fast.


Conductor is quickly growing from a desktop app to a platform, available everywhere from your phone to your VPC, and joining forces with cmd will help us get there faster.


Welcome to Conductor!


We're hiring product engineers and designers.[Join us](https://www.conductor.build/join-us) !
