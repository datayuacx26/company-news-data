# Alokai and Vincit announce strategic partnership to accelerate digital transformation

We are excited to announce a strategic partnership between Alokai and Vincit, combining our efforts to drive extensive business optimization for merchants running on SAP Commerce Cloud on the Nordics market. The goal of this collaboration is to deliver enhanced value to our clients and their customers without having to replatform from their SAP CC backend.


Vincit, with its exceptional track record in software development and IT services, specializes in turning complex business challenges into seamless digital solutions. Their approach to business-driven technology aligns perfectly with our mission to transform businesses by enhancing their digital capabilities.


In the words of Mikko Seppä, the Offering Development Director at Vincit:


*Joining forces with Alokai represents a significant milestone for Vincit. Their expertise in digital experiences complements our strategic focus on business transformation through technology. This partnership will enable us to expand our offerings and deliver even more comprehensive solutions to our clients.*


## Getting the partnership off the ground


To initiate this partnership, Alokai’s developer experience team led by our CTO and co-founder has facilitated on-site workshops in Helsinki to showcase some of the best practices for implementing Alokai in their clients’ future projects. We are hoping that through dedicated collaboration and knowledge exchange we can drive innovation to the ecommerce vendors in the Nordics.


Through this partnership, Vincit will enhance its digital transformation capabilities with Alokai's frontend as a service solution, while Alokai will benefit from Vincit's extensive expertise in user experience and digital strategy. Together, we are poised to redefine industry standards and drive unprecedented growth and innovation.


We look forward to a fruitful partnership that creates meaningful impacts across industries.


For more information, please visit[Alokai](https://alokai.com/) and[Vincit](https://www.vincit.com/) .
