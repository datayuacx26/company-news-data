- SAP Customer Experience (CX) Suite offers a cohesive, cloud-based platform integrating marketing, sales, customer service, and commerce to provide seamless and consistent customer interactions.
- Key components of the SAP CX Suite include SAP Marketing Cloud, SAP Commerce Cloud, SAP Sales Cloud, SAP Service Cloud, and SAP Customer Data Cloud, each designed to enhance specific aspects of the customer journey.

## How SAP CX supports sustainable business practices


Sustainability is not just a buzzword but a core principle that SAP CX embodies through initiatives like SAP Recommerce. By facilitating trade-in programs and managing the resale of preowned inventory, SAP Recommerce nudges businesses towards a circular economy, fostering sustainable consumption and opening new revenue streams. This approach not only aligns with environmental values but also resonates with consumers who are increasingly conscious of their ecological footprint.


Moreover, SAP CX’s predictive replenishment leverages AI to streamline restocking processes, thereby minimizing the environmental impact of overstocking and out-of-stock scenarios. In conjunction with SAP Business AI, companies can forge supply chains that are not only efficient and agile but also inherently sustainable, ensuring that environmental considerations are woven into the fabric of business activities.
