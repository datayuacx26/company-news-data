# SharkNinja Launches Jailbreak LIVE, a Global AI Hack Week

At SharkNinja, we’ve always set an unimaginably high bar for what’s possible. Ten years ago, that instinct led the entire company to shut down for a week for its first company-wide hack. By the end of it, an entirely new business category was born.


Today, we are living through the biggest technological transformation of our lifetimes – and we’re meeting it the same way. All 4,200+ team members across the globe are participating in Jailbreak LIVE, a four-day company-wide hack where we are suspending business as usual. Every function, every market, and every role is thinking without boundaries.


We’re also extending it beyond our own walls. Through Jailbreak EDU, we’re inviting university students on-site for two days to hack alongside our team – bringing fresh perspective from the next generation of problem solvers.


Ten years ago, a week like this showed us what this company is capable of. When 4,200+ people stop their day jobs and go all in together, anything is possible.
