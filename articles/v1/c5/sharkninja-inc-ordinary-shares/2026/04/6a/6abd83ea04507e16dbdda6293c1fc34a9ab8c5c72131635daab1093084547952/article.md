# SharkNinja Unveils Limited-Edition Shark® ChillPill™ in SKYLRK “HEAT”

*First-ever ChillPill™ brand collaboration features a custom SKYLRK colorway, “Heat”*


SharkNinja has announced its first-ever brand collaboration for Shark® ChillPill™ with SKYLRK. The limited-edition “Heat” colorway nods to peak desert inferno and will be available soon in limited quantities via


[SharkNinja.com](http://sharkninja.com/) and SKYLRK.


Since launching this spring, ChillPill™ has quickly become one of the most sought-after personal cooling devices. Built for heat, movement, and all-day wear, it’s designed to keep up from the first set to the last—making it ideal for festival season. The limited-edition SKYLRK “Heat” colorway is desert-ready for anyone who wants to keep their cool, wherever they are.


“We built ChillPill™ for the moments when life heats up, and there’s no better test of that than a full day in the desert,” said Neil Shah, Chief Commercial Officer at SharkNinja.


ChillPill™ combines a high-speed bladeless fan, an indoor-outdoor dry-touch evaporative mister, and a cryo-inspired InstaChill™ cooling plate that lowers skin temperature by up to 16°F in seconds*. Wear it, clip it on, or set it down hands-free—with up to 11 hours of battery life on low speeds— so it moves with you all day.


The limited-edition SKYLRK “Heat” colorway drops soon on SharkNinja.com and via SKYLRK, while supplies last.


The full ChillPill™ lineup — Carbon, Dragonfruit, Glacier, Haze, Iced Latte, Matcha, and Rose Gold — is available now for $149.99 at SharkNinja.com and on Amazon.
