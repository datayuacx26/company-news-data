# SharkNinja CEO Mark Barrocas Named EY Entrepreneur Of The Year® 2026 New England Award Winner

SharkNinja is proud to announce that CEO Mark Barrocas has been named an EY Entrepreneur Of The Year® 2026 New England Award winner — one of the most prestigious business honors for entrepreneurs anywhere in the world.


For more than four decades, the Entrepreneur Of The Year® program has built a legacy of recognizing bold, transformative business leaders who don’t just build companies — they reshape industries, push the boundaries of innovation, and invest in the people and communities around them. Recipients are chosen by an independent panel of past winners, top CEOs, and business leaders.


Mark has spent close to two decades cultivating a company culture where ambition is encouraged, and people are consistently challenged to exceed what they thought was possible.


Being named an Entrepreneur Of The Year® winner is a reflection of that enduring commitment — and of SharkNinja’s mission to make a positive difference in the lives of people around the world.
