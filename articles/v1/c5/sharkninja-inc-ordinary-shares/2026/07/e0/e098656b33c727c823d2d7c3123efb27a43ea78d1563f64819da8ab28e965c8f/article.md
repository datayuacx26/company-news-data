# Jordan Chiles Partners with SharkNinja to Introduce the new Shark® PowerDetect® Transformer™

Two-time Olympic gymnast Jordan Chiles is teaming up with SharkNinja to showcase the new


[Shark® PowerDetect® Transformer™](https://www.sharkninja.com/shark-transformer-true-pet-upright-vacuum/LX501UPG1.html) ,


the best overall cleaning upright^ that transforms into a lightweight stick vacuum and powerful handheld.


The partnership launches with a


[social media video](https://www.instagram.com/p/DadlVG2APJM/?hl=en) featuring Chiles using the PowerDetect® Transformer™, highlighting the product’s ability to adapt to a range of cleaning tasks—from everyday floor care to hard-to-reach spaces—with a single click.


“As an athlete, I know what it takes to adapt, stay focused, and keep moving,” Chiles said. “That’s why I love the Shark® PowerDetect® Transformer™. Whether it’s a big mess, a quick cleanup, or those hard-to-reach places, it transforms to meet the moment without slowing you down.”


The all-new, 3-in-1 vacuum combines the deep-cleaning performance of a full-size upright vacuum with the portability of a lightweight stick vacuum and the convenience of a handheld. Powered by Shark’s Detect and Reveal Technology, it automatically responds to dirt levels, floor types, edges, and cleaning direction while illuminating hidden debris to help deliver a thorough whole-home clean.


Additional features include Auto-Empty


capabilities


, HEPA Filtration with Anti-Allergen Complete Seal Technology®, a self-cleaning brushroll for No Hair Wrap, Odor Neutralizer Technology, and DuoClean Detect™ Brushrolls.
