# The Best Used Small SUVs in 2026

Crossovers and SUVs have dominated the automotive landscape for well over a decade, which means today's used-car market is packed with outstanding options to suit virtually every buyer. If you're shopping for a used small SUV, we recommend widening your search to include the 2017 model year — that's when many small SUVs truly came into their own and began establishing the segment's modern standards.


Whether you're drawn to an Acura, an Audi, a GMC, or a Volvo, today's new SUVs are more capable and refined than ever — though that quality comes at a cost. One smart strategy is to let the original owner absorb the steepest depreciation and step in at the right moment.


A used SUV won't come wrapped in a new-car warranty, but later-model examples may qualify for certified pre-owned (CPO) coverage. Some low-mileage finds might even remain within their original factory warranty windows. Here are our top picks for the best used small SUVs available right now, covering a wide price range and a diverse lineup of manufacturers.

## 2018 Honda CR-V


The Honda CR-V is a perennial bestseller that underwent a full redesign in 2017, but its most meaningful update for the 2018 model year was the welcome return of a rotary volume knob for the audio system. The CR-V has built its reputation on comfort, practicality, and long-term reliability. The 2018 model's 75.8 cubic feet of cargo space ranked it at the top of its competitive class, and it was equally impressive for occupants, with ample head- and legroom in both rows.


The 2018 CR-V offered a choice between a 184- or 190-hp four-cylinder engine. Neither prioritized outright performance, but both returned strong fuel economy numbers. Standard equipment included a 5-inch infotainment touchscreen, with an available 7-inch unit that brought Apple CarPlay and Android Auto into the mix. Buyers could also option in Honda's well-regarded Sensing suite of driver-assistance technologies.

## 2019 Honda HR-V


The HR-V is built for comfortable, spacious, no-fuss daily driving. Beyond its smooth and composed ride, the 2019 edition of Honda's smallest crossover was surprisingly generous with interior room, offering 58.8 cubic feet of cargo space with the rear seats folded flat. With the rear seats upright, both rows provided plenty of headroom and legroom for passengers of most sizes. The HR-V also featured the clever Honda Magic Seat system, which unlocked multiple cargo and passenger configurations to suit a variety of hauling needs. Apple CarPlay and Android Auto became standard features for 2019, adding even more appeal to an already strong value proposition.


The HR-V's 141-hp engine won't quicken anyone's pulse, but it delivered highway fuel economy on par with the Mazda CX-3, and Honda refined the 2019 model's continuously variable transmission (CVT) to improve its responsiveness and overall feel. The HR-V leaned firmly toward on-road comfort rather than sporty handling, which is exactly what most buyers in this segment are looking for. One caveat worth noting: higher trim levels came with an infotainment interface that some owners found less than intuitive. Opting for the base trim with its more straightforward controls may be the wiser choice.

## 2019 Jeep Renegade


The 2019 Jeep Renegade carved out a distinct identity among subcompact SUVs in both appearance and real-world capability.


Its tall, upright proportions translated into excellent outward visibility and a commanding driving position. At 50.8 cubic feet, cargo capacity fell short of some rivals, but the Renegade's squared-off rear hatch opening made loading bulkier items considerably easier. The only American-made SUV on this list received a well-timed refresh for the 2019 model year, bringing updated styling that felt more current. It also arrived a year after Apple CarPlay was introduced to the Renegade lineup — though it remained an optional feature, so it's worth verifying the equipment list on any specific example you're evaluating.


Powertrain options for the 2019 Renegade included a 2.4-liter four-cylinder producing 184 hp and a then-new 1.3-liter turbocharged four-cylinder rated at 177 hp. Each had its strengths and compromises. The nine-speed automatic transmission drew some criticism for its tendency to hunt between gears in everyday driving.


For buyers with genuine off-road intentions, the Renegade Trailhawk is the trim to seek out. It carried a price premium but delivered skid plates, an enhanced four-wheel-drive (4WD) system, and increased ride height over standard models.

## 2018 Mazda CX-3

## 2020 Mazda CX-5


Much like its smaller CX-3 sibling, the 2020 Mazda CX-5 delivered polished performance and a premium interior experience. The CX-5 was fully redesigned for the 2017 model year, and by 2020 it had matured into something of a bargain luxury vehicle. As a used compact SUV, the 2020 CX-5's design still reads as fresh and sophisticated — and it's the kind of styling that should continue to age gracefully. The exterior is clean and contemporary, though the prominent C- and D-pillars do compromise rear and side visibility to some degree.


Cargo space was arguably the CX-5's weakest attribute, but it more than compensated with its exceptional cabin quality. The seats were genuinely supportive and comfortable, and the fit and finish rivaled vehicles commanding significantly higher prices. The MazdaConnect infotainment system used a rotary dial in the center console, echoing the approach taken by premium European brands. The CX-5 also delivered confident acceleration and handling dynamics more commonly associated with a luxury SUV than a mainstream compact. For 2020, Mazda made its i-Activsense suite of advanced driver-assistance systems standard across the entire trim lineup.

## 2018 Nissan Rogue


Few compact SUVs can rival the 2018 Nissan Rogue when it comes to passenger and cargo flexibility. It stood as one of the only compact SUVs available with an optional third row of seats. For buyers who only need two rows, the Rogue's 70 cubic feet of cargo space placed it firmly among the class leaders. The third row was tight but workable in a pinch, while the first and second rows offered generous head- and legroom for everyday use.


The 2018 Rogue's control layout was refreshingly user-friendly, with physical dials for the radio and climate system positioned exactly where you'd expect them. There was a noticeable price step between the 2017 and 2018 models, but it was justified: Nissan added standard Apple CarPlay and an expanded suite of advanced safety features for 2018. If those are important to you, the 2018 model is the clear target. If not, the 2017 Rogue still represents solid value and includes several meaningful driver-assistance features, among them adaptive cruise control and automatic emergency braking.

## The Best Used Small SUVs in 2026 FAQs


**What is the best used small SUV to buy?**


There's no single right answer, but the 2017 Toyota RAV4 and 2020 Mazda CX-5 stand out as especially well-rounded picks. The RAV4 offers Toyota's legendary reliability, generous cargo space, and standard safety technology, while the CX-5 delivers an upscale interior and confident driving dynamics at a mainstream price. The best choice ultimately depends on your priorities — whether that's practicality, driving enjoyment, or long-term dependability.


**What model year should I look for when buying a used small SUV?**


The article recommends casting your net back to at least the 2017 model year, as that's when many small SUVs matured significantly in terms of features and refinement. Many 2017 and newer models came standard with driver-assistance technology and Apple CarPlay or Android Auto connectivity. Some low-mileage examples from this era may even still be within their original factory warranty periods.


**Which used small SUVs are best for families who need extra seating?**


If you need more than five seats but don't want to move up to a midsize SUV, the 2018 Nissan Rogue and the 2019 Volkswagen Tiguan are your best bets — both offered an optional third row of seats, a rare feature in the compact segment. The Rogue also delivered up to 70 cubic feet of cargo space in its two-row configuration, making it one of the most versatile options in the class. The Tiguan added European styling and a turbocharged engine to the mix, giving it a distinct character alongside its practical credentials.


**Which used small SUV is the best choice for off-roading?**


The 2019 Jeep Renegade is the standout pick if you plan to venture off the pavement, particularly in Trailhawk trim. That version came equipped with skid plates, an upgraded four-wheel-drive system, and additional ride height compared to standard models. The 2017 Subaru Forester is also worth considering, as it came standard with all-wheel drive and a capable 8.7 inches of ground clearance.
