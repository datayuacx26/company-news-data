# CarGurus Dealer | Fee Transparency Update

##### **1. How can I disclose my fees or confirm I don’t charge any?** ‍


We’ve created an easy way for you to disclose fees, or confirm that you charge no fees, to maintain your Deal Ratings. The process takes less than five minutes in the CarGurus Dealer Fee Setup tool (Dashboard → Settings → Dealer Fee Setup).


If you do not charge any fees, please toggle OFF the button “My dealership charges dealer or document fees”.


‍


##### **2. Do I need to disclose fees if I am sending all-in prices in my inventory feed?**


Yes. You still need to go into Dashboard → Settings → Dealer Fee Setup and toggle on "Fees included in my inventory feed." Without that, you're still at risk of losing Deal Ratings.


‍


##### **3. Which fees do I need to disclose?**


The FTC sent warning letters to a number of dealer groups in March 2026, stating that prices they advertise should be the total price, including all mandatory fees, that a consumer is required to pay.


If you have questions about what fees you should disclose to comply with applicable law, please consult your legal counsel. Your state dealer association may also have helpful resources for its members.


‍


##### **4. Does this apply to all dealers?**


Yes, this applies to any dealer that lists used inventory on our platform.


‍


##### **5. When will you change IMV to be calculated based on all-in price?**


In the weeks following July 14, we plan to move our IMV and Deal Rating calculations to be based on all-in price (inclusive of fees). We plan to communicate any changes with dealers so they can be prepared.


##### **6. Do I need to disclose fees for new vehicles too, or just used?** ‍


Yes. New vehicles do not have Deal Ratings, but you can separate our new vs. used vs. CPO in the Dealer Fee Setup tool. The July 14 shift to No Rating is only for used vehicles.


‍


##### **7. How is IMV being calculated right now?**


In the meantime, IMV is calculated based on the base price. We’re reinforcing all-in pricing through consumer site updates, including fee badging and a dedicated search filter, providing dealers who disclose fees more credibility and visibility.
