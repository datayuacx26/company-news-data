## Toyota RAV4 Hybrid


**Best Fuel-Efficient SUV Under $20,000:** Toyota RAV4 Hybrid **Priced from:** $15,000


We'd love to steer you toward a Lexus RX 450h within a $20,000 budget—and technically, it's possible. But you'll be looking at one with well north of 100,000 miles. The smarter move at this price point is the Toyota RAV4 Hybrid Limited, which delivers most of what the Lexus brings to the table, and within this budget you can realistically find one with fewer than 70,000 miles on the odometer.


In the $15,000 to $20,000 range, expect to be shopping RAV4 Hybrid Limited models from around the 2016 model year. What makes the RAV4 such a compelling choice is its sheer versatility—it's equipped to handle almost anything life demands, short of hauling more than four additional passengers. The Mitsubishi Outlander plug-in hybrid and Nissan Rogue are also worth considering in this space, though finding lightly used examples within this budget is trickier.


The RAV4 Hybrid Limited arrives well-equipped from the factory, with leather upholstery, the largest available touchscreen, a power sunroof, and heated seats. The hybrid powertrain also delivers class-competitive fuel economy, rated at 34 mpg city and 30 mpg highway by the EPA.

## Kia Sportage

A $20,000 budget can unlock some surprisingly recent used vehicles, and the Kia Sportage is a standout example. You won't be getting into a top-tier trim, but if the LX in cloth with all-wheel drive (AWD) suits your needs, you can find a 2023 Sportage LX AWD with under 50,000 miles—potentially with some factory warranty still in effect. ## Subaru Outback


**Best AWD Crossover Under $20,000:** Subaru Outback **Priced from:** $15,000


The Subaru Outback began its life as a station wagon, but today it stands fully established as a crossover SUV in every meaningful sense. In fact, previews of the 2026 model suggest the Outback will finally shed the last remnants of its wagon DNA altogether.


Standard AWD has long made the Outback—much like its Forester sibling—a go-to choice for drivers in the snowy Northeast, the Pacific Northwest, and throughout the Rocky Mountain region. Subaru has refreshed the Outback repeatedly over the years, steadily expanding interior space for passengers and cargo alike while advancing its suite of safety technology with each generation.


The 2.5-liter flat-four found in most Outbacks may leave performance-minded drivers or high-altitude commuters craving a bit more, but it's a smooth, well-matched unit paired to a continuously variable transmission (CVT). Combined with the Outback's capable AWD system, it makes for a well-rounded package for anyone who regularly faces harsh weather. Drivers wanting more power could opt for the available 3.6-liter H6 or, in later models, a turbocharged 2.4-liter four-cylinder.


New Outbacks now start near $30,000 and climb well past $40,000, but previous-generation examples deliver the same rugged character and most of the same mechanical substance at a significantly lower cost. Starting from the 2015 model year, there's no shortage of quality options available under $20,000.

## Lincoln MKT


**Best Large Crossover Under $20,000:** Lincoln MKT **Priced from:** $18,000


We've steered countless family-hauler shoppers toward the Ford Flex over the years, and those who followed the advice have come back nothing but pleased. The Lincoln MKT shares its bones with the Flex but trades that vehicle's boxy, armored-truck silhouette for a more polished, upscale look.


Like the Flex, the MKT largely flew under the radar during its production run. Many examples ended up in livery fleets after Lincoln retired the Town Car. Part of the issue was a lofty MSRP when new—but the used market has corrected that dramatically. For $20,000, you can find a meticulously maintained, fully loaded, low-mileage MKT complete with a heated steering wheel, backup camera, navigation, Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, lane departure warning, and more. The MKT brings luxury-level appointments typically associated with far pricier SUVs, making it one of the most underappreciated values in the used market today.


One important note: Given the MKT's history in livery service, always pull a vehicle history report and arrange a pre-purchase inspection—regardless of how spotless the listing appears. Fleet operators tend to stay current on maintenance, but verifying that firsthand is always worth the effort.

## Honorable mentions


Because our list is organized around specific categories, a handful of deserving models didn't earn a dedicated spot—but they absolutely merit your attention.


This is particularly true in the midsize crossover segment, where the volume of strong contenders makes comprehensive coverage impossible. Two standouts worth singling out are the Mazda CX-5 and the Honda CR-V. ## The Best Used SUVs Under $20,000 in 2026 FAQs


**What is the best used SUV to buy for under $20,000?**


The best pick depends on your priorities, but there are strong options across every category at this price point. Highlights include the Toyota RAV4 Hybrid for fuel efficiency, the Kia Sportage for a reliable compact crossover, the Dodge Durango for a capable midsize family hauler, and the BMW X3 if you want a touch of luxury without the luxury price tag.


**Can you find a reliable used SUV under $20,000 that still has warranty coverage?**


Yes — a 2023 Kia Sportage LX AWD with under 50,000 miles can be found within a $20,000 budget and may still carry some factory warranty coverage. Kia also backs non-certified used models with a five-year/60,000-mile powertrain warranty, which adds a meaningful layer of protection for used buyers.


**Which used SUVs under $20,000 are best for families who need three rows?**


The Dodge Durango and Chevrolet Tahoe are the strongest three-row options at this price, with both offering spacious cabins and capable powertrains. The article also notes that the Toyota Highlander, Hyundai Santa Fe, and Kia Sorento are solid three-row choices, though finding clean, late-model examples of those within a $20,000 budget is more challenging.


**Is buying a used luxury SUV under $20,000 a smart move, or will maintenance costs eat up the savings?**


A used BMW X3 can be had for around $13,000 and up, offering genuine driving refinement and a quality interior at a fraction of its original price. The key is doing your homework — the article recommends a similar approach for the Lincoln MKT, advising buyers to always pull a vehicle history report and schedule a pre-purchase inspection before committing to any used luxury vehicle.
