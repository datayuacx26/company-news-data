# What's my car worth?

The difference between these terms relates to how you plan to sell your car and the type of valuation you're looking for:


Trade-in value: This is the amount a dealership is likely to offer when you trade in your car. This option is often lower because dealers need to cover their business costs.


Private sale value: You can expect to get more for your car if you sell it directly to someone yourself, rather than trading it in to a dealer. However, this requires a good amount more work on your part.
