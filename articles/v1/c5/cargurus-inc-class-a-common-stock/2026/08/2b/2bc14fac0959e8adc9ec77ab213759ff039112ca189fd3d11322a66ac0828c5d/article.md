The Honda Pilot is a masterclass in three-row family SUV practicality, and the 2026 update gives it a brawnier new look to match its already-impressive utility. One of its standout features remains the removable second-row center seat, which gives families the flexibility of a bench or captain's chairs depending on the day—and when not in use, it stows neatly under the cargo floor. ## 9. Volkswagen Atlas


**CarGurus expert rating:** 6.8 out of 10
**CarGurus user rating:** 4.7 out of 5
**Percentage of Good and Great Deals available on CarGurus:** 34.6%
**Overall CarGurus rating:** 4.5 out of 5


Pros Cons


Stylish design Poor ride quality


High-end interior Unintuitive technology

The Volkswagen Atlas is one of the most visually striking three-row SUVs at its price point, with an interior that punches squarely into luxury crossover territory—particularly in upper trims, where quilted diamond-pattern leather, metallic accents, and warm wood trim create an ambiance that rivals vehicles costing significantly more. Every Atlas comes standard with heated and ventilated front seats, leatherette upholstery, and roof rails, while the full IQ.Drive safety suite—including forward-collision warning, automatic emergency braking, blind-spot monitoring, adaptive cruise control with stop-and-go, and semi-autonomous Travel Assist—is standard across all trim levels, a notable value proposition. Passenger and cargo space are genuine competitive strengths: the second-row captain's chairs tip forward without collapsing for easy third-row access, the rear bench sits high enough off the floor to provide real thigh support, and cargo capacity of 20.6 cubic feet behind the third row and 96.6 cubic feet total puts it near the top of the class. With base pricing starting at $40,785—undercutting the Honda Pilot, Hyundai Palisade, and Toyota Grand Highlander at their respective entry points—the Atlas offers a compelling style-to-value ratio that's hard to ignore.


On the road, the turbocharged 2.0-liter four-cylinder delivers 269 hp and 273 lb-ft of torque with a wide, usable powerband that keeps highway driving smooth and unflustered, and the 4Motion AWD system is available across the lineup and standard on the Peak Edition and above. A five-star NHTSA overall safety rating and a prior IIHS Top Safety Pick+ designation add further peace of mind. The main trade-offs are consistent ones: ride quality on larger wheel-and-tire packages can feel stiff over rough pavement, and the native MIB3 infotainment system is genuinely frustrating to navigate—touch-sensitive HVAC and volume sliders become invisible at night, and basic tasks require too many menu dives. Fortunately, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto work flawlessly as a daily workaround, and fuel economy of around 21–22 mpg combined is adequate, if unspectacular in an era of increasingly available hybrid alternatives. For shoppers who prioritize style, space, and a premium interior feel over driving refinement, the Atlas remains one of the segment's most distinctive offerings.

## Best Midsize SUVs in 2026: FAQs


### Which midsize SUV is best for families?


The Kia Telluride, Hyundai Palisade, and Honda Pilot are consistently ranked as top choices for families. The Telluride and Palisade offer near-luxury interiors, spacious three-row seating, and robust safety features. The Honda Pilot is praised for its practical interior, flexible seating with a removable second-row center seat, and proven reliability.


### What is the most reliable midsize SUV?


The Toyota Highlander and Honda Pilot have long-standing reputations for reliability. The Jeep Wrangler is also noted for its durable build and dependable off-road performance.


### Which midsize SUV has the best gas mileage?


Hybrid and plug-in hybrid (PHEV) models offer the best fuel economy. The Lexus RX Hybrid lineup is a strong contender, with the RX 450h+ PHEV delivering up to 83 MPGe. The Mazda CX-90 also offers an efficient plug-in hybrid powertrain. Among gasoline-only models, the Hyundai Santa Fe and Chevrolet Traverse offer competitive fuel economy for their size.


### Which midsize SUV is best for off-roading?


The Jeep Wrangler is the undisputed champion for serious off-roading, thanks to its body-on-frame construction, solid axles, and standard 4WD. For buyers who want a more comfortable daily driver with some off-road chops, the Chevrolet Traverse Z71, Hyundai Palisade XRT, and Kia Telluride X-Pro trims offer increased ground clearance and all-terrain tires.


### What's the difference between the Hyundai Palisade and Kia Telluride?


While they share the same platform and V6 powertrain, the Palisade and Telluride have distinct differences. The Telluride often has a slight edge in cargo space and a more rugged exterior design. The Palisade focuses more on a luxurious and comfortable ride with a sleeker design and, in some trims, more advanced tech features.
