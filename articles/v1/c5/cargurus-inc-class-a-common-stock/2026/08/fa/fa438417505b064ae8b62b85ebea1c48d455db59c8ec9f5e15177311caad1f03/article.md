# The Best Luxury SUVs with 3 Rows in 2026

Finding a luxury SUV that genuinely seats three rows of passengers—without sacrificing the refinement, technology, and driving experience that justify the premium price—is a narrower search than it might first appear. Many vehicles in this segment technically offer a third row, but the real-world usability of that space varies considerably from model to model. Some, like the Cadillac Escalade and Mercedes-Benz GLS-Class, are purpose-built for family hauling with adult-friendly rear seating and generous cargo room. Others, like the Genesis GV80 and Audi Q7, include a third row as more of an occasional-use option. Both have their merits.


Beyond seating capacity, buyers in this segment are weighing a wide range of priorities: towing capability, fuel economy or electric range, off-road hardware, driver-assistance technology, and how well the infotainment system holds up in daily use. The Lexus GX, for instance, brings impressive towing numbers and a body-on-frame build, while the Lucid Gravity takes a completely different approach with an all-electric powertrain and up to an estimated 437 miles of range. The Range Rover and BMW X7 appeal to those who want a more driver-focused experience, while the Volvo XC90 and Mercedes-Benz GLS lean into comfort and safety credentials. This guide covers 10 of the best luxury SUVs with third-row seating currently available to help you find the right fit for your family and budget.

## The Best Luxury SUVs with 3 Rows FAQs


**Which luxury three-row SUV has the most cargo space?**


Among the vehicles in this guide, the Cadillac Escalade leads the pack with 121 cubic feet of total cargo space when both rear rows are folded, and 25.5 cubic feet behind the third row with all seats up. The BMW X7 is also competitive, offering 90.4 cubic feet with the second and third rows folded. At the other end of the spectrum, the Range Rover offers just 11 cubic feet behind its third row, and the Audi Q7 provides 14.2 cubic feet in the same configuration—so cargo capacity varies significantly across this segment and is worth checking carefully before you buy.


**Which luxury three-row SUVs offer the best towing capacity?**


If towing is a priority, the Lexus GX leads this group with a maximum towing capacity of up to 9,063 pounds. The Range Rover is close behind at 8,200 pounds, followed by the Cadillac Escalade at 7,900 pounds (with 4WD) and the BMW X7 at 7,500 pounds. The Mercedes-Benz GLS-Class is rated at 7,700 pounds, while the Genesis GV80 tops out at 6,000 pounds and the Cadillac Vistiq at 5,000 pounds. The Lucid Gravity's towing rating was not specified at publication time.


**Which luxury three-row SUVs have the most usable third rows for adults?**


Third-row usability varies considerably in this segment. The Cadillac Escalade, Mercedes-Benz GLS-Class, and Lucid Gravity are generally considered the most adult-friendly in terms of headroom and legroom in the third row. The Range Rover's long-wheelbase three-row configuration also offers generous headroom across all rows. By contrast, the Audi Q7, Volvo XC90, and Genesis GV80 have tighter third rows that are better suited to children or occasional short-trip use for adults.


**Are there any electric or plug-in hybrid options among luxury three-row SUVs?**


Yes. The Lucid Gravity is the only fully electric option in this guide, with an estimated range of up to 437 miles for three-row models. For plug-in hybrid options, the Range Rover offers a PHEV with an EPA-rated 53 miles of electric range, and the Volvo XC90 T8 provides up to 33 miles of electric range. The Cadillac Vistiq is also fully electric, with Cadillac estimating at least 300 miles of range. The remaining models—Audi Q7, Lexus GX, Mercedes-Benz GLS-Class, BMW X7, Cadillac Escalade, and Genesis GV80—are conventional gasoline-powered vehicles, though several use mild-hybrid systems to improve efficiency.


**Which luxury three-row SUVs offer hands-free highway driving?**


Hands-free highway driving is available on a select few models in this segment. The Cadillac Escalade includes Super Cruise as standard equipment, making it one of the more accessible options for this feature. The Lucid Gravity offers hands-free highway driving as an available upgrade through its DreamDrive system. The Genesis GV80 includes standard Highway Drive Assist, though it is not a fully hands-free system. The BMW X7 requires the Driving Assistance Professional Package for its Highway Assistant feature. The Audi Q7, Mercedes-Benz GLS-Class, Volvo XC90, and Range Rover do not currently offer a hands-free highway driving system.
