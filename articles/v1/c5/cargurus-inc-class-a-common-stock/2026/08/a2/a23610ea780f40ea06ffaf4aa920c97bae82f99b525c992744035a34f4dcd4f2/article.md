# 2027 Nissan Kicks Pricing, Specs, and Release Date

The Nissan Kicks was completely redesigned in 2025 with a more upright design, added tech, and available all-wheel drive. It sits in the competitive subcompact crossover class and squares off against the Chevrolet Trax, Hyundai Kona, Honda HR-V, Kia Seltos, and Toyota Corolla Cross. The 2027 Nissan Kicks will have four trims: S, SV, SR, and Rock Creek.

## What’s New for the 2027 Nissan Kicks


Since the Nissan Kicks was all-new in 2025, it doesn’t receive any big sweeping changes across the lineup for this model year. However, what Nissan does do for 2027 is add the Rock Creek trim to the Kicks lineup. Built off of the SV trim, the Rock Creek adds more features and technology designed for off-road driving. The new Kicks Rock Creek will come standard with all-wheel drive, an exclusive Off-Road driving mode, and hill descent control as well as some unique design treatments.


## Design


The 2027 Nissan Kicks has an upright stance with sharp edges and a unique vertical stack front lighting signature that echoes the one found on the Nissan Murano. The base S trim comes with 16-inch steel wheels, but 17- and 19-inch wheels are available as you level up in trims. The Rock Creek model is differentiated by exclusive 17-inch black alloy wheels, gloss black exterior accents, a roof rack with cross bars that can hold up to 165 pounds, and amber daytime running lights.


## Powertrain


The Nissan Kicks is equipped with a 141-horsepower, 2.0-liter, four-cylinder engine paired with a continuously variable transmission. Front-wheel drive is standard, and all-wheel drive is available on all trims. EPA estimates that the FWD models will get 28 mpg in the city and 35 mpg on the highway, with AWD models getting 27 mpg and 34 mpg, respectively. All trims of the Kicks have 8.4 inches of ground clearance, but the new Rock Creek adds an Off-Road mode with unique traction tuning and hill descent control to aid drivers in mild off-road situations.


## Interior


The interior of the Nissan Kicks is a mix of modern and traditional with a large infotainment screen combined with physical-touch buttons and dials for HVAC and audio controls. Technically, the Kicks is a five-passenger vehicle, but four passengers will be more comfortable in this subcompact crossover. Cloth seats are standard with leatherette seating surfaces available in the top SR trim. The Rock Creek adds TailorFit (synthetic leather) seats and steering wheel as well as Rock Creek-branded floor mats. Though a moonroof isn’t standard on any trim, it is available in both the SV and SR premium packages. For a vehicle on the small side of the spectrum, it’s worth noting that the Kicks has a decent cargo area with up to 29.2 cubic feet of space behind the second row and up to 58.5 cubic feet behind the first row in the FWD models. AWD cargo space drops slightly to 23.9 and 50.1 cubic feet, respectively.


## Technology


One of the surprising things about the 2027 Kicks is the amount of technology you’ll find on this affordability-focused crossover. Standard features include a 12.3-inch infotainment screen, a four-speaker audio system, two front USB-C ports, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, and a standard 7-inch digital color display. Available features include a wireless phone charger, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, two USB-C rear ports, heated front seats, heated steering wheel, a Wi-Fi hotspot, NissanConnect services, a myQ Connected Garage Door opener, a 10-speaker Bose sound system, and a 12.3-inch digital cluster.

## Safety


Standard safety features on the 2027 Nissan Kicks include automatic emergency braking with pedestrian detection, lane departure warning, blind spot monitoring, rear cross-traffic alert, adaptive cruise control, and automatic high beams. Available safety features include automatic reverse braking, front and rear parking sensors, lane departure prevention, emergency lane keep assist, blind-spot intervention, traffic sign recognition, ProPILOT assist, and a 360-degree camera system. At the time of this writing, the 2027 Kicks had not yet been crash tested by either the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration or the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety. However, the 2026 model received an overall safety rating of four out of five stars from the NHTSA, and it gets mixed Good and Marginal crash-test scores from IIHS.


## Pricing and Release Date


The 2027 Nissan Kicks will go on sale later this summer. At the time of this writing, Nissan lists the starting price for the S trim at $24,335. The SV and SR will start at $26,035 and $28,535, respectively. The AWD versions of these three trims start at $25,835, $28,035, and $30,335. The Rock Creek with standard AWD starts at $30,385. Outside of the SV Premium ($950) and SR Premium ($1,600) packages, the Kicks has some premium paint as well as two-tone paint options that range in price from $450 to $1,000. Plus, a cold weather package available on SV and SR trims ($300) adds heated front seats, heated steering wheel, heated side mirrors, and rear floor heater ducts.
