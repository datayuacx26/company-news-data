## Benefits of Leasing a Hyundai


When you lease any new car, you are paying only for the depreciation for the term of the lease. Because you are paying that smaller amount—not the full MSRP—your monthly payment is lower than financing a car over the same 24 to 36 month term. This often means you can stretch your budget by leasing a Hyundai.


If you are concerned with reliability, leasing is a great option as the vehicle will be under warranty for the entire lease period. Depending on your state, electrified vehicles can also be highly incentivized and provide great value.

## How to Get the Best Hyundai Lease Offers


Take time to cross shop the various models across the Hyundai lineup. If you are considering an SUV, Hyundai offers a wide range of options in different price categories, from subcompact to full-size, with gas engines, hybrid powertrains, and battery electric powertrains. Additional savings can be found by switching to an electric vehicle.


Outside of picking the best Kia for your driving needs, remember that factors like a strong **[credit rating](https://www.cargurus.com/research/articles/bad-credit)** and the ability to pay a down payment are essential when it comes to scoring the best deals. It is also important is to understand that not every lease deal you see advertised online is available across the country. Check with your local dealers to find the best deals near you.


## Research Available Financing Options


Financing a Hyundai may be the right decision for you. Owning a car outright means you do not have to worry about mileage limits or penalties from wear and tear. And there may be some special offers or bonus cash for this option as well.


Look into offers from your local bank, local dealership, and Hyundai Motor Finance. Depending on which model you are shopping for, zero percent interest rates are available and are being advertised by Hyundai for well-qualified buyers. Get an offer from your bank before walking into a dealership, having a pre-approved loan is tremendously helpful in negotiating the best deal.


## Leverage Trade-Ins and Additional Incentives


Whether you are leasing or financing a vehicle, consider trading in your old car. Trading in a used vehicle can help lower your sales tax burden, can have a significant impact on your down payment, and can lower your monthly payment.


Use available online resources to know what your trade-in is currently valued, and speak to local dealerships directly to understand its price-saving potential.


If you are purchasing an electrified vehicle, always inquire about any additional city and state incentives. Electric vehicles and plug-in hybrids may qualify for additional cash back or tax incentives depending on your location.


Finally, always ask if there is any flexibility for a given offer. For a lease, this could mean increased yearly mileage limits, complimentary scheduled maintenance, or adjusting your down payment.
