# 2026 Buick Envision Pricing, Specs, and Release Date

The Buick Envision is a small crossover with luxury aspirations, a bit more premium than some of Buick’s other vehicles. Competitors include the Acura RDX, Lincoln Corsair, and BMW X3.

## What’s New for the 2026 Buick Envision


For 2026, the Buick Envision gets a price hike of about $3,000 across the lineup, which erodes its former value proposition compared to other compact luxury crossovers. Otherwise, the Envision gets just an updated exterior color palette and some other minor updates. Quartz Blue Metallic and Midnight Opal are new, while Calypso Blue Metallic, Smokey Amethyst Metallic, and White Frost Tricoat are gone. The Envision no longer includes a standard cargo organizer or first aid kit. It was last refreshed in 2024 and redesigned in 2021.


## Design


The Envision is a stylish and cheerful little crossover, a more polished version of the Chevrolet crossovers with which it shares design and engineering. It has a sleeker nose and roofline than most of its rivals, which have largely evolved back toward more upright and blunt front ends and greenhouses. The Envision comes standard with 18-inch alloy wheels on the Preferred trim and 20-inch wheels on the Sport Touring and Avenir trims.


## Powertrain


Every Envision features a turbocharged four-cylinder engine rated at 228 horsepower and 258 pound-feet of torque, with a nine-speed automatic transmission and all-wheel drive (AWD). It’s neither quick nor fast, though it’s adequate for everyday driving. The steering and handling both lack feedback, but the ride is compliant and comfortable. The Envision is EPA-rated for 22 mpg, 28 mpg highway, and 25 mpg combined, which is about average for the class.


## Interior


You’ll find more space inside the Buick Envision than you might expect, with 40.4 inches of front legroom and 38.9 inches of rear legroom. This might be the Envision’s best trick, along with its about-average cargo capacity of 25.2 cubic feet behind the second row and a maximum of 52.7 cubic feet with the second row folded down. Standard features include power-adjustable front seats and a leather-wrapped steering wheel. Options include heated front seats, ventilated front seats, heated rear outboard seats, a heated steering wheel, synthetic leather upholstery, and quilted and perforated leather upholstery.


## Technology


Though the Envision’s cabin is a bit disappointing for what Buick claims is a luxury vehicle, the infotainment system is a step in the right direction and the focal point of the interior. The system spans 30 combined inches across the dash, incorporating both a digital instrument display and a touchscreen with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. Other standard features include wireless charging and a nine-speaker Bose audio system.

## Safety


Most of the Envision’s safety features come standard. The list includes a surround-view camera system, forward-collision warning, front and rear automatic emergency braking, pedestrian and cyclist detection, adaptive cruise control, intersection assist, blind-spot monitoring, lane-departure warning, lane-keep assist, a head-up display, rear parking sensors, rear cross-traffic alert, a rear-seat alert system, and General Motors’ Teen Driver safety settings system. The only option is a rear-camera mirror, which projects an unobstructed camera view from behind the vehicle onto the rearview mirror. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) awarded the Envision a five-star overall safety rating. The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) has not evaluated the Envision since 2020.


## Pricing and Release Date


The 2026 Buick Envision is available now. As of this writing, the base Preferred starts at $41,000, the Sport Touring starts at $43,500, and the top Avenir starts at $50,700. The Envision has a whopping destination fee of $1,995 on top of the prices seen here.
