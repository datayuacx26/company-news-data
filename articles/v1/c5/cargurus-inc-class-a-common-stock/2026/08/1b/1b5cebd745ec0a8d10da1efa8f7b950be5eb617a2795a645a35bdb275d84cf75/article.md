# 2027 Dodge Charger Pricing, Specs, and Release Date

The Dodge Charger name has been around for decades. The original dates back to the 1960s muscle car era, then it was reborn in front-wheel-drive (FWD) form in the 1980s, and again in 2006 as a quintessentially American rear-wheel-drive (RWD) performance sedan. The Charger relied on that formula through the 2023 model year before an all-new model hit dealerships in 2024 in coupe form and with an electric powertrain. Fast-forward to 2026, and the Dodge Charger is offered with two or four doors and both electric and gasoline powertrains.

## What’s New for the 2027 Dodge Charger


While rumors persist that Dodge will bring the Hemi V8 engine back to the Charger lineup, the automaker has yet to confirm it. Still, there are quite a few updates for the 2027 model, including an optional Satin Black painted hood, graphics packages that include Charger blade stripes or dual racing stripes in red, white, or black, and optional black or orange Brembo brake calipers. Inside, the 2027 Charger gains Petrol Blue leather seats with a Digital Slack perforation pattern that joins red and black, and optional Demonic Red seat belts. Dodge has also added a new 600-horsepower Charger Super Bee Launch Edition to the lineup—a SIXPACK High Output-based performance variant positioned as the quickest, fastest, and most powerful Super Bee ever, arriving to mark the Charger's 60th anniversary. Pricing, ordering, and availability details will be announced later this year.


## Design


Few cars are now offered in both two- and four-door body styles, but the Charger is. In either guise, it looks long, lean, wide, and sporty, with a rakish roofline, blunt-faced front, rear fascias, and standard front and rear LED lighting. Dodge offers a wide array of exterior colors, including imaginatively named options such as Bludicrous, Destroyer Gray, Green Machine, Redeye, and Peel Out (orange). The 2027 Charger comes standard with 18-inch alloy wheels, while several 20-inch options are available. With two-tone exterior paint schemes and multiple graphics packages, it's possible to customize the Charger's look in many different ways.


## Powertrain


The standard engine in the 2027 Dodge Charger Sixpack is a turbocharged 3.0-liter inline-six with 420 horsepower and 468 pound-feet of torque, which is enough for a 0-60 mph time of 4.6 seconds and a quarter-mile time of 12.9 seconds. The Charger Scat Pack gets a high-output version of the inline-six engine with 550 hp and 531 lb-ft, resulting in a 0-60 mph time of 3.9 seconds and a quarter-mile time of 12.2 seconds. An eight-speed automatic transmission is standard, as is all-wheel drive (AWD). Buyers keen on roasting tires can make use of the Charger's Drift/Donut mode and its ability to lock into RWD, along with the Scat Pack's Line Lock, which locks the front brakes to aid burnouts. Launch Control is also offered to maximize straight-line acceleration.


The range-topping 2027 Charger Super Bee Launch Edition takes SIXPACK performance further, with a high-output tune producing 600 hp and 531 lb-ft of torque — enough for a 0-60 mph time of 3.6 seconds and a quarter-mile time of 11.8 seconds, quicker than the Scat Pack. It uses larger 56mm turbochargers (compared to the Scat Pack's 54mm units) and, like the R/T and Scat Pack, comes standard with AWD and an on-demand 100% RWD mode. The Super Bee also gets 16-inch Brembo brakes with six-piston front and four-piston rear calipers standard — a first for a SIXPACK-powered Charger — along with exclusive Sucker Punch and Shady exterior color options, in addition to Diamond Black.


In addition to the two gasoline inline-six engines, Dodge offers the Charger Daytona Scat Pack with a 670-hp electric powertrain.


## Interior


The Dodge Charger is a big car, and that equals a roomy interior. There's plenty of space inside for adults in the front and rear seats, though the low-cut roofline can make it tough to get in and out of the back—even in four-door models. The Charger is actually a hatchback with folding rear seats, so there is more cargo capacity than buyers might expect. Sporty interior features include a leather-wrapped steering wheel with flattened top and bottom portions, a console-mounted starter button and shift lever, optional high-back bucket seats, and available Attitude Adjustment Lighting that illuminates the doors and instrument panel from below with interesting textures in one of 64 selectable lighting colors.


## Technology


All 2027 Dodge Chargers feature a 12.3-inch touchscreen infotainment system in the center of the dashboard running parent company Stellantis's latest Uconnect 5 software. Depending on the model, either a 10.3-inch or 16-inch digital gauge cluster sits in front of the driver. A head-up display is optional and wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto are standard. Dodge's Performance Pages are accessible from the infotainment screen and record metrics such as acceleration times, maximum cornering numbers, and lap times. Dodge Connect, which requires a subscription or trial offer, allows smartphone app connectivity to view alerts, the car's location, and more.

## Safety


Dodge's Active Drive Assist technology comes standard on all 2027 Charger models. It is a semi-autonomous package that includes adaptive cruise control and lane-keep assist while still requiring the driver to keep their hands on the wheel. Also standard are forward-collision warning and automatic emergency braking with vulnerable road user detection, active lane management, adaptive cruise control with stop-and-go, blind-spot monitors with rear cross-path detection, traffic-sign recognition, and a driver-attention monitor. At the time of writing, the latest Dodge Charger had not been crash-tested by either the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) or the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA).


## Pricing and Release Date


The 2026 Charger was still available at dealerships at the time of writing. Dodge hadn't said exactly when it would switch to selling 2027 models, but the company indicated pricing would remain similar. With an MSRP of $51,990 (including destination), the 2027 Dodge Charger R/T in two-door form is the least expensive model. Opting for four doors raises the price to $53,990. Any color other than black costs extra. There are several noteworthy options offered in 2026 that are likely to carry over to 2027, including a Driver Convenience Group that adds upgraded cloth-and-leatherette seats, a heated steering wheel, front and rear parking sensors, steering-wheel paddle shifters, and an upgraded Alpine stereo system for a reasonable $1,495. A Performance Handling Group for $2,995 includes a lot of extra gear, such as Brembo brakes, launch control, performance pages, and an upgraded suspension setup. The two-door Charger Scat Pack with the 550-hp engine starts at $56,990, while the four-door Scat Pack costs $58,990. The new Charger Super Bee Launch Edition will be a limited-production model; Dodge has not yet announced pricing, ordering, or availability details for it.
