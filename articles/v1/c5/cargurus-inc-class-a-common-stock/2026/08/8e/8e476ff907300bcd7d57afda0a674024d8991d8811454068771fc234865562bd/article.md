# 2026 Honda Civic Hatchback Pricing, Specs, and Release Date

Long recognized as one of the most practical and the most fun vehicles in the compact car segment, the Honda Civic hatchback takes on vehicles such as the Toyota Corolla, the Mazda3, and the Volkswagen GTI. The Civic is also available in a sedan body style and hybrid variants, which we review separately.

## What’s New for the 2026 Honda Civic Hatchback


The Honda Civic hatchback was refreshed for 2025 with updated exterior and interior styling, bringing nothing new of note into 2026. That’s fine, since it’s about on par with most of its competitors in terms of lifecycle updates. Honda should update the Civic’s infotainment system sooner rather than later, though, particularly if it wants to maintain its status among the brand’s and the segment’s top sellers.


## Design


The Civic hatchback has a jauntier, more distinctive look than its sedan sibling, though the two are largely identical except for the rear ends. It’s available in a handful of bright colors, offset by the black grille and lower fascia trim. Since the Civic hatchback starts in Sport trim, it gets 18-inch alloy wheels.


## Powertrain


The Civic hatchback and sedan share the same powertrain in their base trims, a 2.0-liter four-cylinder engine with 150 horsepower and 133 pound-feet of torque. A continuously variable automatic transmission (CVT) sends power to the front wheels. It’s peppy enough for most driving scenarios and agile enough to be enjoyable. The Civic hatchback is rated by the EPA at 30 mpg city, 38 mpg highway, and 34 mpg combined.


## Interior


The Civic hatchback seats five passengers across its two rows, and though some hatchbacks compromise rear-seat space in favor of style, that’s not the case here, because it’s just as spacious as the sedan. The seats are comfortable and well-padded in both rows, with cloth upholstery, but there is no option to upgrade the upholstery or add heated front seats because they're reserved for the Civic Hybrid. There’s 24.5 cubic feet of storage space behind the second row, which opens up to a maximum of 46.2 cubic feet with the second row folded down. A retractable cargo cover is standard.


## Technology


The Civic’s infotainment system is modest but easy to use. It’s a simple 7-inch touchscreen with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto connectivity, plus a pair of USB-C ports and a smattering of physical controls for the most commonly used functions. There’s also a 7-inch digital instrument display and a four-speaker stereo system. Most competitors have bigger screens and more modern interfaces, as well as more options. The Civic’s only available upgrade in this area is an eight-speaker stereo.

## Safety


The Civic hatchback is well-equipped with safety gear, including a rear-view camera, forward-collision warning, forward automatic emergency braking, adaptive cruise control, lane-departure warning, lane-keep assist, traffic-jam assist, driver-attention monitoring, and a rear-seat alert system. Options include blind-spot monitors and rear cross-traffic alert. In National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) testing, the 2026 Civic hatchback earned a five-star overall safety rating. In Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) testing, it earned a Top Safety Pick rating and Good scores in all three tests conducted.


## Pricing and Release Date


The 2026 Honda Civic hatchback is available now. At the time of writing, the Civic hatchback was available in just one gas-only trim, Sport, which started at $27,895 before a $1,195 destination charge. The Civic hatchback is also available in Sport Hybrid and Sport Touring Hybrid trims, which for reference, start at $30,595 and $33,595, respectively.
