# 2021 Ford F-150 Trims & Specs Comparison

The **[2021 Ford F-150](https://www.cargurus.com/research/2021-Ford-F-150-c30674)** launched the 14th generation of America's best-selling pickup, bringing significant enhancements in technology, interior capability, and powertrain options. Key generation upgrades include the standard Sync 4 infotainment system with an available 12-inch center touchscreen, a flat rear load floor, and a folding gear shifter that enables an available interior work surface. The 2021 model year also introduced the 3.5-liter PowerBoost Full Hybrid V6 paired with Pro Power Onboard, an integrated generator system capable of powering job sites and tailgates.


The 2021 F-150 lineup spans eight distinct trim levels, catering to everything from rugged, fleet-ready work trucks to ultra-luxurious highway cruisers and high-performance off-road rigs.

## 2021 Ford F-150 Trim Levels Breakdown


### Ford F-150 XL


The XL serves as the entry-level work truck trim in the 2021 F-150 lineup, offering essential utility and modern connectivity:


- 3.3-liter Ti-VCT V6 engine generating 290 horsepower and 265 lb-ft of torque
- 8-inch capacitive touchscreen with Sync 4, wireless Apple CarPlay, and Android Auto
- Cloth or heavy-duty vinyl seating options
- Ford Co-Pilot360 technology with Pre-Collision Assist and Automatic Emergency Braking
- Rearview camera with dynamic hitch assist


The XL is tailored for budget-minded buyers or fleet operators who need a durable, straightforward work vehicle.


### Ford F-150 XLT


The XLT is the core volume-seller in the lineup, adding key comfort, convenience, and styling upgrades over the base XL:


- 17-inch silver-painted aluminum wheels
- Power windows, power door locks, and cruise control
- Color-coordinated carpeted flooring
- Available 12-inch center touchscreen with enhanced voice recognition
- Ford Co-Pilot360 2.0 suite with Blind Spot Information System and trailer coverage
- Lane-Keeping System


### Ford F-150 Lariat


The Lariat represents the transition into mid-level luxury and higher-tier standard performance:


- 2.7-liter EcoBoost V6 engine producing 325 horsepower and 400 lb-ft of torque
- 12-inch center touchscreen and 12-inch digital productivity gauge cluster
- Leather-trimmed, heated and ventilated 10-way power front seats
- Dual-zone automatic climate control
- Ambient interior lighting


### Ford F-150 King Ranch


The King Ranch brings Western-themed luxury combined with strong standard V8 muscle:


- 5.0-liter Ti-VCT V8 engine generating 400 horsepower and 410 lb-ft of torque
- Del Rio leather upholstery with Kingsville Western accents
- Bang & Olufsen 8-speaker sound system
- Heated rear outboard seats and heated leather-wrapped steering wheel
- Power tailgate with integrated tailgate step and tailgate work surface


### Ford F-150 Platinum


The Platinum emphasizes modern, urban luxury with high-end interior and exterior touches:


- 20-inch polished aluminum wheels and satin-chrome exterior accents
- Carmelo or Black leather seating with multi-contour massaging front seats
- Power-deployable running boards
- Intelligent Adaptive Cruise Control and upgraded driver assistance


### Ford F-150 Limited


As the top-of-the-line flagship, the Limited delivers the highest tier of technology and refinement:


- 3.5-liter EcoBoost V6 engine producing 400 horsepower and 500 lb-ft of torque
- 22-inch polished aluminum wheels
- Exclusive Admiral Blue and Light Slate leather-trimmed interior
- 18-speaker B&O Unleashed sound system by Bang & Olufsen
- Twin-panel moonroof
- Standard Pro Power Onboard with 2.0 kW output


### Ford F-150 Tremor


The Tremor bridges the gap between daily driving utility and serious off-road performance:


- 33-inch all-terrain tires
- Off-road-tuned suspension with twin-tube shocks
- Increased ground clearance and upgraded approach, breakover, and departure angles
- Locking rear differential


### Ford F-150 Raptor


The Raptor is the high-performance desert runner at the top of the off-road lineup:


- High-Output 3.5-liter EcoBoost V6 generating 450 horsepower and 510 lb-ft of torque
- Next-generation Fox Live Valve internal bypass shocks
- 5-link rear suspension with extra-long trailing arms
- Available 35-inch or 37-inch all-terrain tires


If trail capability is the priority, the Raptor is the one to get.

## Powertrain & Specs: Engine Options, Towing, and Pro Power Onboard


The 2021 F-150 offers six engine choices, so you can match power output and towing capability precisely to your needs.


### Gas and Diesel Engines


- 3.3-liter Ti-VCT V6: 290 hp, 265 lb-ft, max towing 8,200 lbs
- 2.7-liter EcoBoost V6: 325 hp, 400 lb-ft, max towing 10,100 lbs
- 5.0-liter Ti-VCT V8: 400 hp, 410 lb-ft, max payload 3,325 lbs, max towing 13,000 lbs
- 3.5-liter EcoBoost V6: 400 hp, 500 lb-ft, max towing 14,000 lbs with the Max Trailer Tow Package
- 3.0-liter Power Stroke turbo-diesel V6: 250 hp, 440 lb-ft, max towing 12,100 lbs


### The 3.5L PowerBoost Full Hybrid V6 and Pro Power Onboard


The PowerBoost makes 430 hp and 570 lb-ft and tows up to 12,700 lbs, the strongest all-round combination in the lineup. It comes standard with a 2.4 kW Pro Power Onboard system and offers a 7.2 kW output option. The 2.7-liter EcoBoost, 3.5-liter EcoBoost, and 5.0-liter V8 offer an available 2.0 kW output, giving you mobile generator capability directly from the truck bed.


## Which 2021 Ford F-150 Trim is the Best Value?


For most used truck buyers, the XLT strikes the best balance of capability, technology, and value. Pair it with the 2.7-liter or 3.5-liter EcoBoost V6 and you unlock strong towing limits and performance, while optional packages add the 12-inch touchscreen and blind-spot monitoring without luxury-trim pricing.


If premium comfort is the priority, step up to the Lariat. It includes leather seats, the 12-inch digital gauge cluster, and the 2.7-liter EcoBoost as standard equipment, delivering a near-luxury experience at a much lower cost than the King Ranch or Platinum.


**Our pick:** Start with the XLT for maximum versatility per dollar. Move up to the Lariat for daily luxury, or choose the Tremor or Raptor if trail capability is your main requirement.
