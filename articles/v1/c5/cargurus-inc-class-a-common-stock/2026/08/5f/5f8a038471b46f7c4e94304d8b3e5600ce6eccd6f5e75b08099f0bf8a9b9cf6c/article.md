# 2026 Buick Envista Pricing, Specs, and Release Date

The Buick Envista is a subcompact crossover SUV that hints at a premium experience above its mainstream competitors. Competitors include the Hyundai Kona, Kia Seltos, and Mazda CX-30.

## What’s New for the 2026 Buick Envista


The Buick Envista was introduced for the 2024 model year and has seen few changes since then. That tracks for 2026, with one shade of red exterior paint swapped out for another. Buick has also cut the first aid kit, cargo organizer, and premium floor mats from the standard features list.


## Design


The Envista is a subcompact crossover with a somewhat misleading sporty profile. The tapered nose and raked roofline look great from the side, but hint at a better-performing vehicle than the Envista actually is. Furthermore, the roofline cuts into the second-row passengers’ headroom. The Envista comes standard with 17-inch alloy wheels, and it’s available with 18- and 19-inch wheel designs. The top Avenir trim swaps black exterior trim for body-color trim and side mirrors.


## Powertrain


Every Envista is equipped with a modest turbocharged three-cylinder engine that makes 137 horsepower and 162 pounds-feet of torque. Front-wheel drive (FWD) comes standard, and unlike some options in the class, all-wheel drive (AWD) is not available. A six-speed automatic transmission also comes standard. Driving dynamics are reasonably athletic, though the Envista isn’t particularly powerful. The Envista’s fuel economy ratings are about average for the segment, with ratings of 28 mpg in the city, 32 mpg highway, and 30 mpg combined.


## Interior


The Envista seats five passengers across its two rows. The front seats are comfortable, and the second row has adequate leg room but, again, scant head room. Standard features include manually adjustable front seats with cloth and synthetic leather upholstery. Options include a moonroof, synthetic leather upholstery, black upholstery with tan stitching, a power-adjustable driver’s seat, heated front seats, and a hands-free power liftgate. The Envista’s cargo space is just about average, measuring 20.7 cubic feet behind the second row and a maximum of 42 cubic feet with the second row folded down.


## Technology


Tech features are simple, with most coming standard across the Envista lineup. The Buick Envista features an 8-inch digital instrument display, an 11-inch touch screen infotainment display, wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay, and a six-speaker audio system. There are few options or upgrades on the menu, but wireless device charging is available.

## Safety


The Buick Envista comes standard with a rearview camera, forward-collision warning, forward automatic emergency braking, pedestrian detection, lane-keep assist, lane-departure warning, a rear-seat alert system, General Motors’ Teen Driver safety settings system, and a system that requires seatbelts to be buckled to drive. Options include adaptive cruise control, blind-spot monitoring, and rear cross-traffic alert, but the Envista isn’t available with some of Buick’s more desirable safety features, like the Super Cruise semi-autonomous driving system. In Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) testing, the Envista earned top Good scores in the side and small overlap front evaluations, but the moderate overlap front evaluation yielded the lowest Poor rating. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) hasn’t crash-tested the 2026 Envista, but an early test of the 2025 Envista yielded an overall rating of four out of five stars.


## Pricing and Release Date


The 2026 Buick Envista is available now, in Preferred, Sport Touring, and Avenir trims. As of this writing, the Envista starts at $24,700, moves up to the Sport Touring for $26,200, and starts at $29,500 for the Avenir. These prices do not include the Envista's $1,795 destination fee.
