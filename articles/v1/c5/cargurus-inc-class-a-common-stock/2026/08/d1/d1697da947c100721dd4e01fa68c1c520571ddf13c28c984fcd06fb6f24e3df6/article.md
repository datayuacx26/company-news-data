## What are 0% APR Car Deals?


Learning some lingo helps when finding the best deals on cars. APR stands for annual percentage rate, and tells you how much interest you’ll pay on the loan over the course of the loan term. So 0% APR car deals are offers to borrow money from automakers or dealerships that don’t require you, the borrower, to pay any interest over the duration of your loan; the only thing you’re paying off is the value of the loan itself. Though interest rates change over time, once you sign the contract for your new vehicle, the rate is locked in for the duration of the car loan.


## Pros and Cons of 0% APR Car Loans


**Pros:** Paying off only the value of the loan keeps you from overspending. Zero-percent financing can save you hundreds or thousands of dollars over the course of the loan. It also means you don’t have to do a lot of math to figure out how much more money you’re going to have to factor in to your monthly payments. Lastly, you can sometimes save enough money on a zero-percent financing loan to upgrade to a higher trim.


**Cons:** Zero-percent car loans are hard to find right now. And when you can pin one down, the dealership is going to be less likely to negotiate on price or add on any further incentives or discounts. In addition, the loans themselves are usually for a shorter period of time, which in turn makes the monthly payments a bit higher. Lastly, you must have an excellent credit score and good credit history to qualify for zero-percent financing.

## How to Get the Best 0% APR Car Deal


**Research Available Financing Options**


Start with our guide when researching 0% APR car deals, as it provides a comprehensive list of offers from many manufacturers. Then, be sure you consider other types of financing offers, and consider low APR options in particular. You may find a good offer from your bank, for example. As always, whether you’re looking at a no-interest loan or a traditional loan, be sure to calculate any interest, incentives, down payment, and the value of your trade-in if you have one.


It’s important to keep in mind that a deal with the lowest APR isn’t always the best one. And a great deal on a vehicle that isn’t the best car for you isn’t necessarily the best choice. Just because Toyota Financing gives you a great financing deal on a new AWD plug-in hybrid, it doesn’t work if you were really needing the utility of a new Toyota Tacoma pickup truck.


**Leverage Trade-Ins and Additional Incentives**


A great tool to use for leverage in a 0% APR is a trade-in vehicle, if you have one. Dealerships might be motivated to offer a better price if you have a trade-in. Also, though additional incentives aren’t likely with a 0% APR deal, it’s important to ask. Ask the local dealer if you have access to a loyalty program or if you’re financing an EV, be sure you ask about potential state-level tax credits. Look for words in ads or on the manufacturer and local dealer websites like “bonus cash,” “cash offers,” “customer cash” to recognize financing deals that you can take advantage of. Don’t be shy about negotiating, and definitely don’t be afraid to walk away if the deal and the auto loan isn’t the best fit for you. Nothing’s worse than getting home with your new car and realizing that your monthly payment is higher than you planned.
