## Benefits of Leasing a Lexus


A monthly lease is a great way to stretch your budget. This is because when you lease a new vehicle, you are paying off the depreciation over the lease term (usually 24 to 36 months), not the full MSRP. The result is a lower monthly payment compared to financing the vehicle over the same period of time.


When applied to leasing a Lexus, this could mean a higher trim or even a different model is within your budget. Your Lexus will be under warranty for the entire lease period as well, and all new Lexus leases come with LexusCare, which means your first and second scheduled maintenance services at Lexus dealers are complimentary. Finally, leasing is a great way to experience a new Lexus every two to three years.

## How to Get the Best Lexus Lease Offers


There are no hidden handshakes or special tricks when it comes to getting the best lease specials on a new vehicle. The best tip is to be patient, consider what you want most in a vehicle and prioritize things like tech features, fuel economy, cargo volume, and safety equipment.


It’s also smart to learn the terminology that comes with leasing or financing a vehicle. This avoids feeling confused or cornered when you’re at the dealership and ready to agree on a deal. ## Research Available Financing Options


If you plan on keeping your car long-term, financing a Lexus could be the better option for you. While you can always purchase a vehicle at the end of the lease, it is not as cost-effective as financing from the start. For those who prefer to own a car outright, financing makes a lot of sense.


Another advantage to financing a vehicles is not having to worry about wear and tear and yearly mileage limits. Most leases limit your mileage to 8,000 to 12,000 miles a year, and going over these limits can incur significant penalties.


Familiarize yourself with current available interest rates. You may already be pre-approved by your local bank or credit union, and your dealership may be able to match or improve on these rates. For well qualified buyers, Lexus financial services also offer attractive financing rates.


## Leverage Trade-Ins and Additional Incentives


No matter if you’ve decided to lease or finance your ideal Lexus, there are important steps to **[get the most for your money](https://www.cargurus.com/research/articles/everything-you-need-to-know-about-leasing)** . For example, trading in your current vehicle can significantly lower the initial down payment, or the entirety of your fixed-year monthly payments.


Use all available online resources to know what your trade-in is currently valued, and speak to local dealerships directly to understand its price-saving potential.


Remember to ask if there is added flexibility possible with a given offer. In terms of leasing, this could be higher annual mileage caps, or adjusting a down payment balanced with monthly payments. If you’d prefer to have more cash in-hand at the time the deal is done, a lower down payment could **[offset the uptick in future monthly payments](https://www.cargurus.com/sell-car/?pid=c2d_articles)** .
