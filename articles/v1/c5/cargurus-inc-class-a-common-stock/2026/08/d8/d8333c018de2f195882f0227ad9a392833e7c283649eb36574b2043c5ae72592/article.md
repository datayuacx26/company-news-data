# 2024 Toyota Tacoma Trims & Specs Comparison

The **[2024 Toyota Tacoma](https://www.cargurus.com/research/2024-Toyota-Tacoma-c32923)** kicked off a fourth generation for America's best-selling midsize pickup. Re-engineered from the ground up on Toyota's high-strength TNGA-F global truck platform, the 2024 Tacoma replaces its long-running V6 with a lineup of 2.4-liter turbocharged four-cylinder engines, including the high-output i-Force Max hybrid powertrain.


Beyond the power upgrades, the redesigned Tacoma introduced a multi-link coil-spring rear suspension on Double Cab models for a far smoother on-road ride, standard Toyota Safety Sense 3.0, and an overhauled cabin with available 14-inch touchscreens and a JBL Flex portable Bluetooth speaker. The 2024 lineup spans eight trim levels, covering everything from fleet-ready work trucks to high-speed desert racers, luxury highway cruisers, and factory-built overlanding rigs.

## 2024 Toyota Tacoma Trim Levels Breakdown


### Toyota Tacoma SR


The SR is the foundational entry-level model, delivering durable utility and modern cabin essentials:


- 2.4-liter turbocharged i-Force 4-cylinder engine producing 228 horsepower and 243 lb-ft of torque with the 8-speed automatic
- Available 6-speed manual transmission with automatic rev-matching, which raises output to 270 hp and 310 lb-ft
- Available in XtraCab with a 6-foot bed or Double Cab with a 5-foot bed
- 8-inch Toyota Audio Multimedia touchscreen with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto
- Smart Key System with push-button start and LED headlights
- Heavy-duty composite cargo bed with Class IV trailer hitch receiver
- Toyota Safety Sense 3.0 driver-assist suite


The SR covers turbo performance and essential safety tech for budget-conscious buyers and fleet work.


### Toyota Tacoma SR5


The SR5 expands cab configurations and unlocks higher engine tuning alongside key comfort features:


- Upgraded 2.4-liter i-Force turbo engine generating 278 horsepower and 317 lb-ft of torque
- 17-inch alloy wheels
- Available multi-link coil-spring rear suspension on Double Cab models
- Bed rails with movable tie-down cleats and LED bed lighting
- Keyless entry on all doors
- Available Blind Spot Monitor with Rear Cross-Traffic Alert


The SR5 is the mass-market volume seller, hitting the right balance of price, power, and daily versatility.


### Toyota Tacoma TRD PreRunner


Inspired by desert-reconnaissance trucks, the TRD PreRunner is a two-wheel-drive performance trim built exclusively in the two-door XtraCab configuration:


- 278-hp 2.4-liter i-Force turbo engine with 8-speed automatic transmission
- Lifted front suspension and 17-inch dark gray TRD alloy wheels
- Electronically controlled locking rear differential
- Underbody fuel tank protection and front skid plate
- Lockable rear cab storage and a 6-foot bed
- Leather-wrapped steering wheel and digital instrument cluster


### Toyota Tacoma TRD Sport


The TRD Sport trades off-road hardware for sharper street handling and sporty styling:


- 278-hp i-Force engine, with an available 6-speed manual rated at 270 hp
- 18-inch TRD alloy wheels and TRD mesh grille with hood scoop
- TRD sport-tuned suspension dampers for reduced body roll
- Fabric sport seats with unique trim accents and aluminum pedals
- Standard coil-spring rear suspension on Double Cab models
- Available 14-inch touchscreen and power moonroof


### Toyota Tacoma TRD Off-Road


The TRD Off-Road is engineered for rough terrain and rocky trails:


- Part-time 4WD with two-speed transfer case
- Bilstein remote-reservoir suspension dampers
- Electronically locking rear differential and Multi-Terrain Select with Crawl Control
- Underbody armor for the engine, transfer case, and fuel tank
- 17-inch alloy wheels wrapped in all-terrain tires
- Optional front stabilizer bar disconnect system for maximum wheel articulation


### Toyota Tacoma Limited


The Limited is the flagship luxury trim, focused on highway comfort and premium technology:


- Adaptive Variable Suspension for a smooth, composed ride
- 18-inch chrome-finished alloy wheels and chrome exterior accents
- SofTex synthetic leather upholstery with heated and ventilated front seats
- 14-inch touchscreen with 10-speaker JBL premium audio and removable JBL Flex speaker
- Power up/down tailgate and power-deployable running boards
- Head-up display and 360-degree Panoramic View Monitor


### Toyota Tacoma Trailhunter


The Trailhunter is purpose-built for overlanding, with the 326-hp i-Force Max hybrid powertrain as standard:


- Old Man Emu 2.5-inch forged monotube position-sensitive shocks with remote rear reservoirs
- 33-inch Goodyear Territory rugged-terrain tires
- ARB sport bar and steel rock rails
- High-mount air intake
- Rigid LED lighting
- Integrated 2400W AC inverter


### Toyota Tacoma TRD Pro


The TRD Pro is built for high-speed desert running and also runs the 326-hp i-Force Max hybrid powertrain:


- Fox 2.5-inch QS3 internal bypass shocks with remote reservoirs
- Front sway-bar disconnect
- High-clearance front bumper with ARB steel rear bumper
- Heritage Toyota front grille with integrated LED light bar
- IsoDynamic Performance Seats with oil-air shock absorption to stabilize driver vision over rough terrain


If maximum factory-built capability is the priority, the Trailhunter and TRD Pro are the two to get.

## Powertrain & Specs: i-Force vs. i-Force Max Hybrid


The 2024 Tacoma streamlines its engine choices into two turbocharged 2.4-liter architectures.


### The 2.4L Turbocharged i-Force (228 to 278 hp)


Standard on SR, SR5, TRD PreRunner, TRD Sport, TRD Off-Road, and Limited. Output ranges from 228 hp and 243 lb-ft on the base SR up to 278 hp and 317 lb-ft on higher automatic trims, or 270 hp and 310 lb-ft with the 6-speed manual. Fuel economy reaches an EPA-estimated 21 mpg city, 26 mpg highway, and 23 mpg combined. Maximum towing is 6,500 lbs, with a maximum payload of 1,705 lbs.


### The 2.4L Turbocharged i-Force Max Hybrid (326 hp)


Standard on Trailhunter and TRD Pro, and available on TRD Sport, TRD Off-Road, and Limited. It pairs the turbo four with a 48-hp electric motor built into the 8-speed transmission for 326 hp and 465 lb-ft of torque from just 1,700 rpm. You get instant low-end response with strong efficiency and a maximum towing capacity of 6,000 lbs.


## Which 2024 Toyota Tacoma Trim is the Best Value?


For the average daily driver and weekend warrior, the SR5 Double Cab offers the strongest baseline value. It gets you the full-output 278-hp engine, multi-link coil rear suspension, alloy wheels, and the key convenience features without premium pricing.


If off-roading is on your radar, the TRD Off-Road is the sweet spot of the lineup. It includes Bilstein remote-reservoir shocks, all-terrain tires, skid plates, and a locking rear differential out of the box, delivering near-TRD Pro trail performance for far less money.


**Our pick:** Choose the SR5 for everyday utility, the TRD Sport for street performance, or the TRD Off-Road for serious trail capability. Step up to the i-Force Max-powered Trailhunter or TRD Pro if you want the ultimate factory-built overlanding machine.
