## Benefits of Leasing a Subaru


When you lease a Subaru—or any vehicle—you’re essentially paying for its depreciation rather than the full MSRP. This typically results in lower monthly payments compared to financing over the same term. Most lease agreements span 24 to 36 months, keeping you well within the factory warranty for added peace of mind.


Leasing also allows you to drive a brand-new Subaru every few years, ensuring you always have access to the latest technology and features. Another advantage is the flexibility at the end of your lease—you can simply return the vehicle and start a new lease, or if you’ve grown attached to it, you have the option to buy it outright.


## Subaru Lease Trends in 2026


Practical SUVs remain in high demand, especially when paired with Subaru’s renowned off-road capability. Freshly updated for the 2025 model year, the Forester is an excellent choice for those seeking an easy-to-drive SUV with generous cargo space. Meanwhile, the Impreza continues to offer budget-conscious buyers a capable AWD hatchback at an accessible price point.


For those considering an EV, the all-electric Subaru Solterra is a strong value. Featuring a dual-motor AWD powertrain, it delivers the familiar Subaru driving experience with distinctive styling. Although the Solterra didn’t qualify for the Federal EV Tax Credit (which was phased out after September 30 anyway), Subaru offsets this by providing its own $7,500 incentive, making it a compelling option in the electric SUV segment.

## Research Available Financing Options


Financing a vehicle may be the better choice if you prefer to own your car outright rather than lease. One major advantage of buying is the freedom from mileage restrictions and potential fees for excess wear and tear. If you plan to keep your vehicle long-term, financing from the start is generally more cost-effective than purchasing it at the end of a lease.


Before heading to a dealership, check for current financing offers from Subaru and your bank. Subaru is currently offering 0% interest rates on select models for well-qualified buyers, and your bank may pre-approve you for a competitive rate. In many cases, dealerships can match or even beat your existing rate, and having a pre-approval in hand can give you extra leverage in negotiations.


## Leverage Trade-Ins and Additional Incentives


If you're replacing your current vehicle, trading it in can help reduce your upfront costs and lower your monthly payments. To get a realistic estimate of your car’s value, use tools like CarGurus’ “What’s My Car Worth?” so you have a clear idea of what to expect when negotiating your trade-in.


Don’t hesitate to ask for added flexibility in your lease deal. You may be able to negotiate a higher mileage allowance or even complimentary scheduled maintenance. If you're considering an all-electric Solterra or the plug-in hybrid Crosstrek, check for additional state or local incentives. Many states offer rebates or cashback programs for leasing eco-friendly vehicles, potentially adding to your savings.
