## Benefits of Leasing a Kia


The main advantage to leasing a new Kia vehicle is a lower monthly payment compared to financing over a similar term. This is because when you lease, you are paying for the depreciation of the vehicle and not the full MSRP. Leasing is a great way to stretch your budget.


If reliability is a concern, leasing is a great option since the vehicle will be under warranty for the entire lease period. Leasing is also a great way to find out if an electric vehicle is right for you over 24 to 36 months. And with how quickly technology advances, you can keep pace by regularly getting into the latest and greatest Kia models.

## How to Get the Best Kia Lease Offers


Take time to cross shop the various models across the Kia lineup. Are you ready to jump into an EV? Choosing an EV9 over the Telluride can result in significant savings in your monthly payment.


Outside of picking the best Kia for your family’s needs, remember that factors like a **[strong credit score](https://www.cargurus.com/research/articles/bad-credit)** and the ability to pay a down payment are essential when it comes to scoring the best deals. It is also important is to understand that not every lease deal you see advertised online is available across the country. Check with your local dealers to find the best deals near you.


## Research Available Financing Options


If you prefer to own your car outright, financing your purchase could be the better option for you. Owning your car means you do not have to worry about mileage limits or penalties from wear and tear. And Kia frequently offers financing offers alongside lease specials, depending on the model.


Look into offers from your local bank, dealership, and Kia finance. Depending on which model you are shopping for, zero percent interest rates are available and are being advertised by Kia for well-qualified buyers. Get an offer from your bank before walking into a dealership, having a pre-approved loan in hand is a tremendous help in negotiating the best deal for you.


## Leverage Trade-Ins and Additional Incentives


Whether you are leasing or financing, consider trading in your old car. Trading in your old car towards a new car can help lower your sales tax burden, can have a significant impact on your down payment, and can lower your monthly payment.


Use available online resources to know what your trade-in is currently valued, and speak to local dealerships directly to understand its price-saving potential.


If you are purchasing an electrified vehicle, always inquire about any additional city and state incentives. Electric vehicles and plug-in hybrids may qualify for additional cash back or tax incentives depending on your location.


Finally, always ask if there is any flexibility for a given offer. For a lease, this could mean increased yearly mileage limits, complimentary scheduled maintenance, or adjusting your down payment.
