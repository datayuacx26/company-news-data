# 2026 Toyota Highlander Pricing, Specs, and Release Date

Next year, an all-new Toyota Highlander will hit the American market with a fully electric powertrain. For 2026, though, Toyota’s three-row crossover SUV returns in its familiar form with a gasoline-fueled engine (a hybrid powertrain is also available in 2026). Smaller than the Grand Highlander, the regular Highlander offers a third row of seats sized for occasional use for households that don’t always need to seat a full complement of seven or eight passengers.

## What’s New for the 2026 Toyota Highlander


Now in the sixth year of its current fourth-generation form, the Toyota Highlander sees only a few small tweaks to its lineup for the 2026 model year. The base LE trim has been dropped (which oddly means the larger Grand Highlander that’s still offered in LE form has a lower starting price than the smaller Highlander), leaving the XLE, sportier XSE, Limited, and Platinum models. All-wheel drive (AWD) is now standard on all Highlander models, as well. This is the final year for the Highlander as we currently know it. In 2027, the nameplate will be redesigned as a **[fully electric vehicle](https://www.cargurus.com/research/articles/2027-toyota-highlander-pricing-specs-and-release-date)** .


## Design


The 2026 Toyota Highlander’s aggressive split-grille design and deeply contoured bodysides are its most distinguishing design features. Highlander XSE models have a sportier exterior look with a more aggressive front fascia, 20-inch wheels, dual exhaust outlets, and black accents. Limited and Platinum Highlander models have more exterior brightwork for a classy, upscale appearance.


## Powertrain


A 2.4-liter turbocharged four-cylinder engine comes standard in the 2026 Toyota Highlander, sending 265 horsepower and 310 pound-feet of torque to all four wheels through an eight-speed automatic transmission. The EPA estimates the Highlander’s fuel mileage at 21 mpg in the city, 28 mpg on the highway, and 24 mpg combined. There’s a Highlander Hybrid model as well that delivers 35 mpg combined.


While all 2026 Highlanders feature standard AWD, XSE, Limited, and Platinum models get a more advanced system with torque vectoring that can vary power to each individual wheel for improved handling performance.


The 2026 Toyota Highlander can tow up to 5,000 pounds.


## Interior


The Toyota Highlander’s modern-looking interior has excellent fit and finish. All Highlander models feature seating for seven passengers, and XLE and Limited models can be optioned with a second-row bench for eight-passenger seating. A leatherette called SofTex is standard in the XSE and XLE, while Limited and Platinum models get leather. Limited models feature heated and ventilated front seats, while the Platinum extends heat to second-row passengers.


## Technology


An 8.0-inch infotainment screen and a 7.0-inch digital gauge cluster come standard on Highlander XLE models, while Limited and Platinum trims get a much nicer 12.3-inch touchscreen (the larger unit can be optioned on all Highlanders) and a matching 12.3-inch cluster in front of the driver. The XSE trim level pairs the smaller touchscreen with the larger digital gauge cluster. Apple CarPlay and Android Auto are supported regardless of screen size.


A six-speaker audio system is standard in Highlander XLE and XSE trim levels, while Limited and Platinum models benefit from an 11-speaker JBL Premium Audio system.

## Safety


Toyota Safety Sense 2.5+ is standard on all 2026 Highlanders. The suite of safety technologies includes a pre-collision system with pedestrian detection, lane-departure alert with steering assist, automatic high beams, lane-tracing assist, road-sign assist, and full-speed range dynamic radar cruise control. A blind-spot monitor with rear cross-traffic alert is also standard.


While the Highlander was awarded a Top Safety Pick award by the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) in 2024, it lost the award in 2025 and 2026 due to a Marginal rating in the agency’s updated moderate overlap front crash test. The Highlander did score the IIHS’ highest rating of Good in the original moderate overlap test, along with the small overlap front and updated side crash tests. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) awarded the Highlander with its top five-star overall safety rating.


## Pricing and Release Date


If you’re in the market for a Toyota Highlander powered by a gasoline-fueled engine, 2026 is your last chance, as the 2027 model is all-new and fully electric. As of this writing, pricing for the 2026 Highlander starts at $47,865 (including destination) in XLE trim. The sporty Highlander XSE starts at $49,935, and the Limited model carries an MSRP of $52,320. At the top of the Highlander range sits the $55,520 Platinum model.
