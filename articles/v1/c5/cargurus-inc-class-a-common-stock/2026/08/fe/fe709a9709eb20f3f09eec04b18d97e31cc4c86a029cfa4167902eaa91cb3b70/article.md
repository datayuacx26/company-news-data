# 2026 Honda CR-V Trims & Specs Comparison

The **[2026 Honda CR-V](https://www.cargurus.com/research/2026-Honda-CR-V-Hybrid-c34078)** is a hugely popular compact crossover SUV, with lineup enhancements that boost value, connectivity, and off-road capability. Every 2026 CR-V gets an upgraded standard 9-inch infotainment touchscreen with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, plus a standard wireless phone charger across the range. The 2026 model year also marks the debut of the all-new TrailSport Hybrid, injecting rugged, trail-ready hardware into Honda's hybrid lineup.


The 2026 CR-V hierarchy spans seven trim levels, evenly split between responsive 1.5-liter turbocharged gas models and fuel-sipping 2.0-liter two-motor hybrid configurations. Whether you want a practical daily commuter or a feature-packed hybrid cruiser, there's a CR-V trim built for it.

## 2026 Honda CR-V Trim Levels Breakdown


### Honda CR-V LX


The LX is the foundational entry point in the 2026 lineup, delivering strong core technology and driver-assist features:


- 1.5-liter turbocharged 4-cylinder engine generating 190 horsepower and 179 lb-ft of torque
- Available Real Time AWD with Intelligent Control System
- 9-inch color touchscreen with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto
- Wireless phone charging pad
- Honda Sensing safety and driver-assistive suite
- 17-inch steel wheels


The LX covers the essential connectivity and safety tech for budget-conscious SUV shoppers.


### Honda CR-V EX


The EX adds the key daily comfort and convenience features over the base trim:


- 18-inch silver-painted aluminum alloy wheels
- Heated front seats with a 10-way power-adjustable driver's seat
- One-touch power moonroof with tilt feature
- Dual-zone automatic climate control
- Smart Entry with Walk Away Auto Lock
- Heated side mirrors with integrated LED turn signals


### Honda CR-V EX-L


The EX-L turns the gas-powered CR-V into a refined, near-luxury crossover:


- Leather-trimmed seating surfaces
- 4-way power-adjustable front passenger seat
- 10.2-inch digital driver instrument cluster
- Power tailgate with programmable height
- 8-speaker, 320-watt audio system
- Front and rear parking sensors

## Turbo Gas vs. Hybrid: Performance, Fuel Economy, and Cargo


Choosing between the two powertrains comes down to your priorities for commuting, efficiency, and cargo.


### The 1.5L Turbocharged 4-Cylinder (190 hp)


Standard on LX, EX, and EX-L. It makes 190 hp and 179 lb-ft of torque, returns an EPA-estimated 28 mpg city, 33 mpg highway, and 30 mpg combined in front-wheel-drive form, and tows up to 1,500 lbs. It also offers the most cargo room, up to 76.5 cubic feet with the rear seats folded.


### The 2.0L Two-Motor Hybrid (204 hp)


Standard on Sport, TrailSport, Sport-L, and Sport Touring. It makes 204 hp and 247 lb-ft of torque for quicker low-speed acceleration, returns an EPA-estimated 43 mpg city, 36 mpg highway, and 40 mpg combined in front-wheel-drive form, and tows up to 1,000 lbs. Maximum cargo space drops to 71.8 cubic feet.


## Which 2026 Honda CR-V Trim is the Best Value?


If you're shopping the gas lineup, the EX-L delivers the best balance of value and luxury. It upgrades the cabin with leather seating, a power tailgate, and the larger 10.2-inch digital driver display without stepping up to hybrid pricing.


For commuters focused on long-term savings, the Sport Hybrid or Sport-L Hybrid are the smartest buys. You get 204 horsepower, up to 43 mpg in the city, and a strong set of comfort features that lower running costs.


**Our pick:** Choose the EX-L if you prefer a traditional gas engine and maximum cargo volume. Choose the Sport-L Hybrid if you want premium interior comfort paired with high-efficiency hybrid driving.
