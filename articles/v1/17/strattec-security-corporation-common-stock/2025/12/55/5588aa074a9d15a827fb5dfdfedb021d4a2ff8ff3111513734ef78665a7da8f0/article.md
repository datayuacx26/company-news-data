# Strattec to Participate in Upcoming Virtual Investor Conferences

## Strattec to Participate in Upcoming Virtual Investor Conferences


December 2, 2025


[PDF Version](https://ir.strattec.com/node/14091/pdf)


MILWAUKEE, Wis.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Dec. 2, 2025--[Strattec Security Corporation](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.strattec.com%2F&esheet=54366321&newsitemid=20251202026734&lan=en-US&anchor=Strattec+Security+Corporation&index=1&md5=53956ca142e5fd83ff23277f7a788ed5) (Nasdaq: STRT) (“Company”), a leading provider of smart vehicle access, security and authorization solutions for the global automotive industry, today announced that Jennifer Slater, President & CEO, and Matthew Pauli, Chief Financial Officer, will participate in two upcoming virtual investor conferences: the iAccess Alpha Virtual Best Ideas Winter Investment Conference and the Sidoti Year End Virtual Investor Conference.


***Investor Conference Details:***


- **December 9 & 10, 2025** – iAccess Alpha Virtual Best Ideas Winter Investment Conference; management will webcast a presentation at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time on December 9th and will virtually host one-on-one meetings on December 10th.
- **December 11, 2025** – Sidoti Year End Virtual Investor Conference; management will virtually host one-on-one meetings and webcast a presentation at 1:00 p.m. Eastern Time.


Live audio webcasts of both events, with accompanying slides, will be available at[STRT Investor Relations](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.strattec.com%2Fcalendar-of-events&esheet=54366321&newsitemid=20251202026734&lan=en-US&anchor=STRT+Investor+Relations&index=2&md5=995be31399e60a514bc64aef5bc5e206) . Archives of the presentations will be available at the same link following the conference.


**About Strattec**


Strattec is a leading global provider of advanced automotive access, security & authorization solutions for leading vehicle manufacturers, primarily in the U.S. With a history spanning over 110 years, Strattec has consistently been at the forefront of innovation in vehicle security, transitioning from mechanical to integrated electro-mechanical systems. Its highly-engineered products include power access solutions, latches, vehicle start systems, keys, fobs & accessories, locks & locksets, door handles and other access products. Power access solutions provide the motion control for power liftgates, sliding power doors and power tailgates.
