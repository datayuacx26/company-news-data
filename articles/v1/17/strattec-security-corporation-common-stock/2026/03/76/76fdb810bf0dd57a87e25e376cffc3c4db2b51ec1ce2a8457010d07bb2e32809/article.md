# Strattec to Present at the Sidoti Small Cap Conference

## Strattec to Present at the Sidoti Small Cap Conference


March 11, 2026


[PDF Version](https://ir.strattec.com/node/14281/pdf)


MILWAUKEE--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Mar. 11, 2026--[Strattec Security Corporation](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.strattec.com%2F&esheet=54445335&newsitemid=20260311140719&lan=en-US&anchor=Strattec+Security+Corporation&index=1&md5=f32cafec7a819aa36d06c38a364a4860) (Nasdaq: STRT) (“Company”), a leading provider of smart vehicle access, security and authorization solutions for the global automotive industry, today announced that Jennifer Slater, President & CEO, and Matthew Pauli, Chief Financial Officer, will virtually present and host investor meetings at the Sidoti Small Cap Conference on Wednesday, March 18, 2026.


The Company presentation is scheduled to begin at 1:00 p.m. Eastern Time. A live audio webcast of the event with accompanying slides will be available at[STRT Investor Relations](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.strattec.com%2Fcalendar-of-events&esheet=54445335&newsitemid=20260311140719&lan=en-US&anchor=STRT+Investor+Relations&index=2&md5=152ff3541794f34e220b72d7feaa1f9f) . An archive of the presentation will be available at the same link following the conference.


**About Strattec**


Strattec is a leading global provider of advanced automotive access, security & authorization solutions for leading vehicle manufacturers, primarily in the U.S. With a history spanning over 110 years, Strattec has consistently been at the forefront of innovation in vehicle security, transitioning from mechanical to integrated electro-mechanical systems. Its highly-engineered products include power access solutions, latches, vehicle start systems, keys, fobs & accessories, locks & locksets, door handles and other access products. Power access solutions provide the motion control for power liftgates, sliding power doors and power tailgates.
