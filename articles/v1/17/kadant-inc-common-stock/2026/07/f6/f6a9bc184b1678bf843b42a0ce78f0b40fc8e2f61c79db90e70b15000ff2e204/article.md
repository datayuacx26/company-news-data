# Kadant Carmanah Design Awarded Multiple Orders for Wood Processing Systems

SURREY, B.C., Canada, June 4, 2025 – Kadant Carmanah Design announced it received orders from three North American wood processing companies in the second quarter of 2025 for oriented strand board (OSB) processing equipment and related technologies.


The orders include SmartRING stranders with batch feeder systems, rotary debarkers, and straight knife drum chippers. The Argus smart-connected technologies packages with fines measurement and log fill optimizer systems will also be provided.


“These orders reflect our strong history of supplying reliable and efficient equipment to the wood processing industry,” said Todd Macey, president of Kadant Carmanah Design. “Whether it’s to improve strand geometry, minimize fines, reduce debarking fiber loss, or maximize chip quality, our objective is to enable our customers to maximize the use of their raw materials.”


The SmartRING strander, when paired with Kadant Carmanah’s batch feeder and log clamping system, is the preferred technology for processing cut-to-length logs into strands for OSB production. Integrating Kadant Carmanah’s Argus-FMS fines measurement system and Argus-LFO log fill optimizer systems provides OSB producers with the data they need to produce better quality strands while improving strander throughput.


The company’s straight knife drum chipper produces pulp quality chips from a variety of materials, including lumber trim ends, slabs, veneer trims, edgings, blocks, log ends, oversized chips, and solid wood waste.


About Kadant Carmanah Design


Kadant Carmanah Design, a division of Kadant Canada Corp., a subsidiary of Kadant Inc., is located in Surrey, B.C. Canada. Kadant Carmanah designs and manufactures equipment for the panel and engineered wood products and sawmill industries. Providing leading-edge technology and equipment to optimize fiber use to produce wood-based panels, Kadant Carmanah's products include SmartRING stranders, SmartDISC stranders, rotary debarkers, ARGUS smart-connected technologies, and chippers.
