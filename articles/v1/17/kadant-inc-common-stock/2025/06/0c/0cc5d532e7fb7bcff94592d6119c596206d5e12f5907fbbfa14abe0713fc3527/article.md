# Proactive Maintenance for Uninterrupted Production

Do you know that Kadant not only designs and manufactures equipment used to manage flow in industrial processes, but also installs and services the equipment? Partnering with Kadant ensures your hardware is fully integrated into your process for maximum performance and a single source of accountability.


Across industries where steam, heat, and pressure are an essential part of production, plant and production managers rely on Kadant for strategic services beyond components. From planned rotary joint maintenance to full refurbishment projects and inventory management, Kadant’s field specialists keep your systems operating at peak efficiency, every day.


Our technically trained staff conduct hands-on inspections and servicing of high-performance rotary joints, within tight timeframes and challenging industrial environments. From ensuring safety, lubricating bearings, or pinpointing improvement areas in flow control systems, Kadant delivers proven results.


In addition to rotary joints, doctoring, cleaning and filtration systems we supply and service solutions including syphons, flexible metal hoses, doctor blades, and Turbulator® bars. Our audits detect early signs of wear and deliver practical recommendations to boost efficiency, cut energy waste, and prevent unplanned downtime.


> "Our goal is to create real value”


Our spare parts inventory management service ensures that essential components are always in stock, reducing the risk of production stoppages and supporting continuous operations with real-time valuations and parts procurement planning.


Each recommendation is customized; from high-speed rotary joints for fast-moving machines to vibration-resistant models for critical applications. Our goal is to create long-term value by optimizing systems, not just replacing parts. Kadant’s maintenance services include:


1. Machine auditing
2. Preventive maintenance and repairs
3. Components installation
4. Inventory management
5. Maintenance training


Contact our team to learn how Kadant’s maintenance programs can keep your systems running at peak performance.
