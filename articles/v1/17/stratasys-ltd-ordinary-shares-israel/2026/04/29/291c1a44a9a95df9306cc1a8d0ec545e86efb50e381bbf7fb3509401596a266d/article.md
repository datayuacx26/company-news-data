# DirectAssist™

It is designed to make the website experience more interactive and useful, giving visitors another way to explore possibilities, compare options, and learn more about what Stratasys Direct can support.
Visitors can use DirectAssist™ to ask practical questions such as:


-


What material might be a good fit for use under the hood of a car?


-


Can you build something 8 feet long?


-


What process is best for a strong, lightweight part?


-


Can you build a complex part that would replace aluminum?


-


What should I know before choosing one material over another?


You can even have a little fun with it by asking questions such as:


-


What material would work best for a superhero gadget?


-


Can you help me build something wildly overengineered for no good reason?
