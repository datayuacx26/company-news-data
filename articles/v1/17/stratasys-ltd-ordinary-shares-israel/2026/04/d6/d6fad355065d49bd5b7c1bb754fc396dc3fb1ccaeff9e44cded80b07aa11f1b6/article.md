# TrueVoxel: Advanced Aesthetics for 3D Printed Dentures with TrueDent

### The 3D Printed Denture Has Come a Long Way


When Stratasys introduced[TrueDent](https://www.stratasys.com/en/dental/applications/dentures/) ®, it changed how dental labs made dentures. Instead of needing to assemble separate pieces such as gluing teeth onto a base, TrueDent digital dentures are printed as one continuous piece. The base and teeth come out of the printer as a single, seamless monolithic polychromatic unit. That's a big deal for fit, functional occlusion, stability and the way a denture wearer responds to complete prosthetic treatment.


TrueDent uses five base colors that are jetted together in precise ratios during printing to match the natural shades of teeth and tissue. The result is a denture that looks realistic with better digital denture aesthetics and true-to-design fit. Plus, labs can scale production efficiently and reliably, without sacrificing quality.
