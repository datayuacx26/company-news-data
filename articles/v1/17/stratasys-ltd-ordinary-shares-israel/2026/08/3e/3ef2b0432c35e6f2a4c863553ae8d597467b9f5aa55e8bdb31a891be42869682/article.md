# Inspiring the next generation at the 2026 SkillsUSA additive manufacturing competition

At the 2026 SkillsUSA Additive Manufacturing Competition, students from across the United States demonstrated what's possible when creativity, engineering, and advanced manufacturing come together. As a longtime partner of SME and SkillsUSA, Stratasys is proud to support this competition by providing the professional-grade 3D printing technology that helps transform student ideas into real-world solutions.
This year's challenge asked middle school, high school, and post-secondary teams to tackle an aerospace-inspired design problem, requiring them to think like engineers while applying additive manufacturing principles to optimize performance, precision, and functionality. Throughout the multi-day competition, students designed, iterated, and produced their solutions using Stratasys 3D printers—the same technology trusted by leading manufacturers across industries.
Preparing Students for Tomorrow's Workforce


At Stratasys, we believe hands-on experience is one of the most effective ways to prepare students for careers in advanced manufacturing. Competitions like SkillsUSA give students the opportunity to move beyond the classroom by solving authentic engineering challenges under real-world constraints.


This year's event also placed a strong emphasis on workforce readiness. Ninety-six high school and post-secondary students completed the Additive Manufacturing Fundamentals Certification Exam during the competition, validating their knowledge of industry-standard additive manufacturing concepts and strengthening their credentials as they prepare to enter the manufacturing workforce.
