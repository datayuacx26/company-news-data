# 3D Printing for Automotive Parts and Tooling Production

### Stratasys 3D Printing Technologies for Car Parts and Tooling


Stratasys technologies support


different stages


of automotive development and production, from design validation and prototype parts to custom tooling,


short-run


production parts, and PPAP-supported manufacturing through Stratasys Direct Manufacturing.


#### FDM® for Functional Parts and Production Tooling


[FDM® technology](https://www.stratasys.com/en/guide-to-3d-printing/technologies-and-materials/fdm-technology/) is widely used for functional prototypes, production tooling, and durable production parts. Its engineering-grade thermoplastics support automotive applications that require strength, dimensional stability, and reliable factory-floor performance. Manufacturers commonly use FDM® for jigs, fixtures, end-of-arm tooling, and low-volume production components.


#### SLA for Prototype Parts and Tooling


[Stereolithography](https://www.stratasys.com/en/guide-to-3d-printing/technologies-and-materials/stereolithography-technology/) is used for accurate prototype parts, aerodynamic models, and tooling patterns that require smooth surfaces and strong dimensional accuracy.


Automotive manufacturers use our Neo® systems during vehicle development to support faster testing and design iteration.


#### PolyJet


™ for Visual Prototyping and Design Reviews


[PolyJet™ technology](https://www.stratasys.com/en/guide-to-3d-printing/technologies-and-materials/polyjet-technology/) produces highly detailed prototypes with smooth surfaces, fine feature detail, and realistic textures. Automotive design teams use PolyJet™ for interior concepts, fit-and-finish reviews, ergonomic studies, and design validation where appearance and accuracy are important.


#### SAF™ for Volume Production Parts


[SAF™ technology](https://www.stratasys.com/en/guide-to-3d-printing/technologies-and-materials/polyjet-technology/) supports repeatable production of polymer production parts at higher volumes. It is designed for manufacturing environments where consistency, throughput, and part repeatability are important, making it suitable for low-volume production and bridge manufacturing applications.


#### P3™ for Injection Mold Quality Short Runs


[P3™ technology](https://www.stratasys.com/en/guide-to-3d-printing/technologies-and-materials/p3-technology/) supports highly accurate parts with strong surface quality and fine detail.


Automotive manufacturers use P3™ for prototypes, tooling, and short-run production parts where injection mold-like quality is needed without the cost and lead time of traditional tooling.
