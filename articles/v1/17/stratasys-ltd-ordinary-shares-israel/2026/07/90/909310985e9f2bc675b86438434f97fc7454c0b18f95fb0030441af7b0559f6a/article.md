# 3D Printing Materials for Realistic Medical Modeling

### **Medical models on Stratasys J5 Digital Anatomy system**


A recent study between Stratasys and CollPlant Biotechnologies looks to explore what is thought to be additive's next frontier: regenerative medicine. The companies are embarking on a pre-clinical study to develop a 3D bioprinting solution for regenerative breast implants, printed on an[Origin system](https://www.stratasys.com/en/3d-printers/printer-catalog/p3/origin-two-printer/) using rhCopllagen-based bioinks, to test the implant's ability to promote natural tissue growth and comletely degrade over time – resulting in real breast tissue.


“That's the future of 3D printing, in my opinion,” Bitan said. “It’s basically replacing the human body with the human body.”
