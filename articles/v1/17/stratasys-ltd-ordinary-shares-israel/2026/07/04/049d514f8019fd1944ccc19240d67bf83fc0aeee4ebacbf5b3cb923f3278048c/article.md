# Stratasys Expands DentaJet Workflow with Soft Relax HTM

### Soft Relax HTM automated washing system further reduces labor and streamlines digital dental workflow, to be carried exclusively by Stratasys in North America


Stratasys has strengthened its end-to-end unattended digital dentistry workflow with the introduction of the Soft Relax HTM automated washing system. This new system is a significant step toward increased automation in dental application production. The Soft Relax HTM complements the existing GrabCAD® software-driven automated nesting and the DentaJet 3D Printer Series unattended printing and curing capabilities, further reducing manual intervention in the dental part production process.


The Soft Relax HTM system washes batches of up to 20 dental parts, automating support removal. As with the manual waterjet process that the system replaces, the Soft Relax HTM uses water to remove support material - no IPA is needed. This integration enables dental laboratories and production facilities to achieve higher throughput with minimal labor. The Soft Relax HTM will be carried exclusively by Stratasys in the United States and Canada.


"The addition of the Soft Relax HTM system is a critical advance toward an increasingly automated dental production workflow," said Erez Ben Zvi, Vice President, Healthcare at Stratasys. "By automating waterjet support removal, we're eliminating a significant manual bottleneck and further streamlining the workflow, while maintaining the highest quality standards our customers expect. We believe that the integration of Soft Relax HTM with our[DentaJet series of printers](https://www.stratasys.com/en/dental/printers/dentajet-series/) will enable our customers to unlock further growth.”


The enhanced workflow now provides a seamless progression from digital design through production, featuring:


- Automated nesting and support generation through GrabCAD software
- Unattended printing with integrated curing via DentaJet series printers
- Automated support removal through the new Soft Relax HTM system


This comprehensive solution allows dental laboratories to reduce labor costs and increase production efficiency while maintaining consistent quality across all applications. The system's ability to process multiple applications simultaneously aligns with Stratasys' commitment to scalable production solutions for the dental industry.
