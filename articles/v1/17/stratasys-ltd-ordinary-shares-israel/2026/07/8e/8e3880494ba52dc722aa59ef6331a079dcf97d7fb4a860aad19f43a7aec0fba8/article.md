# Cost-Effective Flame-Retardant 3D Printing for Rail Applications

### Optimized for Fortus Systems in Rail Additive Manufacturing


FDM PA6/66-GF30-FR is optimized for use on Fortus® 450mc and F900 systems, enabling consistent, high-quality results with industrial-grade repeatability.


#### **Key benefits include:**


-


- Excellent stiffness and dimensional stability from glass fiber reinforcement
- High-quality surface finish with reduced visible layer lines
- Reliable print performance across production builds


These capabilities make it well-suited for both functional prototypes and certified end-use production parts.


### **Accelerating Adoption of Additive Manufacturing in Rail and Transportation**


The rail industry is rapidly expanding its adoption of additive manufacturing, driven by the need for faster, more flexible production. Stratasys is uniquely positioned to support this shift by combining industrial hardware, and proven workflows.


#### **With FDM PA6/66-GF30-FR, manufacturers can confidently:**


-


- Meet regulatory requirements
- Reduce production costs
- Scale additive manufacturing across more applications


### **A Smarter Path to Certified, Cost-Effective Rail Production**


FDM PA6/66-GF30-FR represents the next step in making additive manufacturing practical for regulated, production environments.


By delivering flame certification, structural performance, and cost efficiency in a single material, it empowers manufacturers to expand their use of additive, without compromise.
