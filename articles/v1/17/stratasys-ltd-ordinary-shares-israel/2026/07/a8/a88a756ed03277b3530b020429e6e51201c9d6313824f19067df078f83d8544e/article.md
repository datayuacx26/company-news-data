# A Step-by-Step Guide to Rapid Prototyping with 3D Printing

#### For functional testing.


Functional prototyping is used to test how a part performs under real-world conditions. This often includes evaluating mechanical strength, durability, and fit, as well as validating specific functional requirements such as ESD properties, high-temperature performance, or flame, smoke, and toxicity (FST) compliance.


Technologies such as **FDM** , **PolyJet** , **SAF** , **SLA** , and **P3 DLP** support functional testing by offering a broad range of materials with different performance characteristics, helping teams confirm that a design meets both mechanical and application-specific requirements before moving to production.
