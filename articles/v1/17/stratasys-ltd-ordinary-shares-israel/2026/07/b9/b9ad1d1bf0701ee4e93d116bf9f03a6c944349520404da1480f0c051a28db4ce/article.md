# How 3D Printers Have Accelerated the Automotive Industry

### 3. Customized 3D Printed Parts


The ability to create highly customized parts is essential in automotive manufacturing. Such customization is not otherwise possible with traditional manufacturing methods which depend on legacy methods of mass production, outsourcing, long lead time, and higher cost.


Radford Motors, for example, relied heavily on additive manufacturing in order to prototype and build the Radford Lotus Type 62-2 production vehicle. 3D printers enabled an agile development and production process so they could focus on high craftsmanship, customization and performance.
