# A Guide to High Performance Resin 3D Printing

## **A Few Things to Consider When Choosing a Resin 3D Printing Solution**


Here’s the truth from the trenches: you don’t pick a printer. You pick an outcome.


You want parts that feel real. Or a workflow that scales. Or regulatory compliance without chaos. Start there.


- **Volume:** Do you need to produce large, single parts, or a high volume of smaller components? What matters more: build size or throughput?


- **Budget:** How does the cost per part compare to your current process – like CNC machining, injection molding, or hand-finishing prototypes? What are your trade-offs between capital investment and operational savings?


- **Application:** Are you creating CMF (color, material, finish) prototypes, functional test parts, or production parts? How critical are aesthetics, material performance, accuracy and precision or regulatory compliance?


- **Scalability:** Does the solution integrate with your existing workflow – from CAD to production? Can it scale with your team, your output, and your business over time?


## Stratasys Resin 3D Printing Technologies in Depth


Stratasys offers three distinct resin 3D printing technologies – each designed to solve different challenges across prototyping, tooling, and production.


#### Stereolithography (SLA) – For Detail


SLA resin 3D printing is often the entry point into the world of photopolymer printing – and for good reason. It uses a laser to cure resin layer by layer, delivering parts and prototypes with ultra-smooth surfaces and crisp detail.


- **Printer range:** the[Stratasys Neo® line](https://www.stratasys.com/en/3d-printers/printer-catalog/stereolithography/) , including the and Neo®450 systems. Designed for industrial reliability, large build volumes, and open resin flexibility, making them ideal for engineering and prototyping environments.


- **Best for** : High-detail functional prototypes, visual models, and fit-check parts


- **Why it’s a favorite** : The ability to print large parts (as well as small ones!) with consistently smooth surfaces and excellent aesthetics, excellent geometric accuracy and fine-feature resolution.


- **Resin capabilities:**[SLA resins](https://www.stratasys.com/en/materials/materials-catalog/stereolithography-materials/) offer high clarity, fine feature resolution, and smooth surface finish. Our users typically rely on clear, tough, and castable materials – ideal for visual models, light transmission, and investment casting.


- **What we’ve seen** : Product development teams use the Neo 3D printer to speed up concept validation with parts that look client-ready straight off the printer. The combination of sharp **** features, smooth surfaces, and repeatable output makes it a go-to choice for prototyping where looks and function both matter.


#### P3 DLP Technology – For Precision


P3 is Stratasys’ evolution of DLP printing, designed for repeatable high accuracy (50 micron and below) and tight tolerance, production-grade parts with minimal post-processing. It cures each layer with a digital light , while tightly controlling exposure, temperature, and separation forces to maximize accuracy and repeatability.


- **Printer range:** The **Stratasys Origin® Two.** Built for industrial performance and scalability, this system offers closed-loop process control, fast print speeds, and access to high-performance materials.


- **Best for:** Functional parts, jigs and fixtures, elastomeric components, and medical or dental devices.


- **Why it’s a favorite:** High accuracy and precision production, in a broad range of engineering-grade, validated materials for regulated environments, and an open material license for flexibility.


- **Resin capabilities:** P3 supports a growing library of functional[materials](https://www.stratasys.com/en/materials/materials-catalog/p3-materials/) – from tough and high-temperature resins to elastomers and ESD-safe options. It's engineered for production environments, with validated resins for regulated applications like medical and aerospace.


- **What we’ve seen:** Engineering teams rely on P3 to replace traditional tooling and streamline low-volume production. It consistently delivers tight tolerances, smooth surface finishes, and repeatable accuracy – even for complex geometries. It’s a favorite for manufacturers moving toward digital production without compromising performance.


#### PolyJet Technology – For Hyper-Realism


PolyJet technology jets multiple resins at once, curing them with UV light to produce multi-material, full-color parts with unrivalled realism. From prototypes that simulate overmolding to anatomical models with lifelike textures, it brings your designs to life, exactly as envisioned.

Resin 3D printing that’s optimized for industries like healthcare, consumer goods, and product design, offering a wide range of material combinations and finish options.
