## Radiant Logistics Announces 100% Renewable Electricity Across Company-Owned Operations In North America And Asia Pacific


**Achievement advances company climate strategy and will be reflected in fiscal 2026 sustainability reporting**


**Radiant Logistics, Inc.** (NYSE American: RLGT), a leading provider of technology-enabled global transportation and logistics solutions, today announced that its company-owned operations in North America and Asia Pacific are now powered by 100% renewable electricity, another measurable step toward the company's long-term climate objectives.


In its 2025 Sustainability Report, renewable electricity accounted for 36% of electricity consumption across company-owned facilities. Operating on 100% renewable electricity reflects continued improvement in reducing the company's operational environmental footprint while supporting a stronger, more resilient global organization.


The transition was achieved through the procurement of renewable electricity and certified Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs), consistent with GHG Protocol accounting standards. The applicable certificates will be retired on Radiant's behalf in accordance with the purchase agreements.


"Meaningful progress isn't defined by a single event or a single announcement. It's built through consistent action over time," said Bohn Crain, Founder and CEO of Radiant Logistics. "Reaching 100% renewable electricity across our company-owned operations is one milestone, but more importantly it reflects our ongoing commitment to doing our part — as a company and as a global citizen — to help build a more sustainable future.”


This move supports the company's previously announced goal of reducing Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions by 30% by 2027, compared to a 2022 baseline. As reported in its 2025 Sustainability Report, Radiant has already achieved a 23% reduction toward that goal while continuing to expand emissions measurement and reporting across its operations.


The impact of this renewable electricity procurement will be reflected in the company's fiscal 2026 sustainability reporting, including market-based Scope 2 emissions, following completion of the annual greenhouse gas inventory and retirement of the applicable Renewable Energy Certificates.


As a technology-enabled, non-asset-based logistics provider, Radiant leverages its network of company-owned and independently operated agency locations, together with a broad portfolio of transportation partners, to create efficient supply chain solutions worldwide. Powering company-owned facilities with renewable electricity represents one element of the company's broader approach to reducing operational emissions while collaborating with customers, carriers, suppliers and agency partners to help build more sustainable supply chains.


### About Radiant Logistics, Inc.


Radiant Logistics, Inc. ([www.radiantdelivers.com](http://www.radiantdelivers.com/) ) (NYSE American: RLGT) is a publicly traded third party logistics company providing technology-enabled global transportation and value added logistics solutions primarily to customers based in the United States and Canada. Through its comprehensive service offering, Radiant provides domestic and international freight forwarding along with truck and rail brokerage services to a diversified account base including manufacturers, distributors and retailers which it supports from an extensive network of Radiant and agent-owned offices throughout North America and other key markets around the world. Radiant's value-added logistics services include warehouse and distribution, customs brokerage, order fulfillment, inventory management and technology services.
