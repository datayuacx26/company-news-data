## **The Science Behind Rice Water**


**** The water that's filtered from the rice after soaking or fermentation is known as “rice water,” and it has skin-loving compounds such as antioxidants, vitamins B and E, amino acids and inositol. Fermented rice water toners do one step more and break down the rice molecules into smaller molecules that can be absorbed more easily. ## **How to Choose the Best Rice Water Toner for Your Skin Type?**


However there is no universal single product of rice toner that will fit all skin types. Here is a brief guide to help you choose one according to your needs.


#### **For Dry Skin**


-


You should choose rice ceramide toner, since ceramides help lock in moisture and repair the skin barrier.


-


Choose formulas enriched with hyaluronic acid to provide deeper hydration.


-


Avoid alcohol-based rice toners as they remove natural oils.


-


Go for a rice and ceramide moisturizing toner for an extra hydration boost.


#### **For Oily & Acne-Prone Skin**


-


You need a light-weight rice water toner which would not clog your pores.


-


Select the niacinamide-infused options to control the maximum oil and minimize pores.


-


Avoid heavy, cream-based toners that can clog pores.


-


Fermented rice water toners work well since they help balance **[sebum](https://www.clinikally.com/blogs/news/understanding-sebum-causes-and-effective-remedies)** production.


#### **For Combination Skin**


-


Select an ideal mixture that is moisturizing to dry area while not leaving the T-zone oily.


-


An exfoliating rice toner will help balance the skin texture.


-


Look for ingredients like **[niacinamide](https://www.clinikally.com/collections/niacinamide)** or **[licorice root extract](https://www.clinikally.com/blogs/news/licorice-root-extract-in-skincare)** for tone correction.


#### **For Sensitive Skin**


-


Opt for fragrance-free and alcohol-free rice toners.


-


Choose fermented rice water for its gentle, calming properties.


-


Patch test any new black rice or rice ceramide toner before full use.


-


Avoid toners with added acids or strong actives.

## **Safety Tips and Common Mistakes to Avoid**


We believe that all natural products are good, gentle and offer great benefits, but some things can go wrong which can diminish the effectiveness or cause skin irritation. Here are some tips that you should keep in mind to use them safely and correctly:


-


**Skipping the Patch Test:** Some skin types may be irritated by even natural ingredients such as rice water, particularly sensitive skin. There's a reason why it's important to always patch test before regular use.


-


**Using Expired Homemade Rice Water:**


Rice water toner is not ideal to keep at room temperature. If it is not preserved properly it will begin to go rancid. So, use it within a few days, or consider a preservative free store bought version.


-


**Over-Exfoliating:** Some rice toners have mild exfoliants; using them alongside other exfoliating products can irritate the skin.


-


**Ignoring Ingredient Lists:**


Not all rice toners are created equal. Check for added alcohol, fragrance, or fillers that could not be appropriate for your skin type.


-


**Applying on Broken Skin:** Avoid using any toner, including rice water toner on open cuts, active breakouts, or irritated skin.
