# Floor &amp; Decor Announces New Store Opening in Schererville, IN

# Floor & Decor Announces New Store Opening in Schererville, IN


[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_4ae08789bb83845627cadee6bf764508/flooranddecor/news/2026-06-11_Floor_Decor_Announces_New_Store_Opening_in_299.pdf) June 11, 2026 8:35am EDT


*—Leading high-growth retailer specializing in hard-surface flooring brings its expansive in-stock selection and warehouse-format shopping experience to the Schererville, Indiana area—*


ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Floor & Decor (NYSE: FND), the leading high-growth retailer specializing in hard-surface flooring for homeowners and professionals, today announced the grand opening of its newest warehouse store and design center in Schererville, Indiana, located at 1516-30 U.S. 41.


The Schererville store will open with a team of approximately 40 associates and is led by Michael Albro, the store's Chief Executive Merchant.


Floor & Decor operates more than 275 warehouse-format stores and five design studios nationwide and offers a broad assortment of in-stock hard-surface flooring, including tile, wood, laminate, vinyl, and natural stone, along with decorative accessories and wall tile, installation materials, and related categories at everyday low prices.


“Bringing Floor & Decor to Schererville is an exciting milestone for our team,” said Albro. “For more than 25 years, we’ve focused on making quality, trend-forward hard surface flooring accessible to everyone, and we’re proud to extend that commitment to this community. We look forward to serving our new customers and helping bring their ideas to life.”


**30-Day Grand Opening Celebration**


Schererville will be the first new store location to debut Floor & Decor’s 30-Day Grand Opening Celebration, kicking off on Thursday, June 18, 2026, with daily deals, giveaways, vendor demonstrations, and special events running through July 17, 2026.


**Ribbon Cutting Ceremony**


Floor & Decor will host a Ribbon Cutting Ceremony on Thursday, June 18 at 10 a.m. CDT, in partnership with the Crossroads Chamber of Commerce. Cheesie’s food truck will be on site offering free lunch for the first 200 customers, starting at 10 a.m. In addition, starting on June 18 through June 24, 2026, all Installation Materials will be 15% off, only at the new Schererville location.


**Free Daily Giveaways for the First 100 Customers**


New Floor & Decor giveaway items will rotate weekly throughout the celebration, free for the first 100 customers each day:


- June 18–20: Free Totes
- June 21–27: Free Buckets
- June 28–July 4: Free Lunch Box
- July 5–11: Free Hat
- July 12–17: Free T-Shirt


**Weekly $1,000 Gift Card Giveaway**


Every week throughout the 30-Day celebration for a total of five weeks, Floor & Decor will give away a $1,000 gift card. Customers can enter daily during each store visit for additional chances to win.


**Free Morning Treats**


Kick off your week with Free Breakfast Mondays, offering a complimentary breakfast every Monday morning from 7–10 a.m., running June 22 through July 13.


To wrap up each week on a sweet note, stop by for Free Muffins & Coffee Friday, complimentary muffins and coffee every Friday morning from 7–10 a.m., June 19 through July 17.


**Daily In-Store Vendor Demonstrations from 7 a.m.–12 p.m.**


Supply representatives will be in store from 7 a.m. to noon throughout the celebration, providing live product demonstrations, answering questions, and sharing installation expertise.


Featured vendor weeks include:


- Mapei: June 22–26
- Schluter: June 30–July 2
- Laticrete: July 6–10
- wedi: July 13–17


Plus, a National Tile Contractors Association (NTCA) training session will be held on Thursday, July 16 at 8 a.m. CDT at the Schererville store. Registration is available at:[https://www.tile-assn.com/events/EventDetails.aspx?id=2064860](https://www.tile-assn.com/events/EventDetails.aspx?id=2064860) .

**About Floor & Decor Holdings, Inc.**


Floor & Decor is a multi-channel specialty retailer of hard surface flooring and related accessories and a commercial flooring distributor. At the end of the first quarter of fiscal 2026, the Company operated 276 warehouse-format stores and five design studios across 39 states. The Company offers a broad in-stock assortment of laminate and vinyl, tile, wood, and natural stone flooring, installation materials, decorative accessories, and adjacent categories at everyday low prices. Founded in 2000, Floor & Decor is headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia.
