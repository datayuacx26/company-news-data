# Floor & Decor Announces New Portland Area Store in Clackamas, OR


[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_4ae08789bb83845627cadee6bf764508/flooranddecor/news/2026-04-09_Floor_Decor_Announces_New_Portland_Area_Store_in_293.pdf) April 09, 2026 8:35am EDT


—Leading high-growth retailer specializing in hard-surface flooring brings its expansive in-stock selection and warehouse-format shopping experience to the Portland metro area—


ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Floor & Decor (NYSE: FND), the leading high-growth retailer specializing in hard-surface flooring for homeowners and professionals, today announced the grand opening of its newest warehouse store and design center in the Portland metro area, located in Clackamas, Oregon, at 10174 SE 82nd Ave in Happy Valley, OR.


The Clackamas store will open with a team of approximately 30 full-time and part-time associates and is led by Brianna Martin, the store’s Chief Executive Merchant.


Floor & Decor operates more than 270 warehouse-format stores and five design studios nationwide and offers a broad assortment of in-stock hard-surface flooring, including tile, wood, laminate, vinyl, and natural stone, along with decorative accessories and wall tile, installation materials, and related categories at everyday low prices.


“Bringing Floor & Decor to Clackamas is an exciting milestone for our team,” said Martin. “For more than 25 years, we’ve focused on making quality, trend-forward hard surface flooring accessible to everyone, and we’re proud to extend that commitment to this community. We look forward to serving our new customers and helping bring their ideas to life.”


**7 Days of Deals at the Clackamas Floor & Decor**


In celebration of the new store opening, starting Thursday, April 16 through Wednesday, April 22, Floor & Decor will host its 7 Days of Deals promotion along with daily events at the Clackamas store, featuring giveaways, installation demonstrations from leading industry brands, and the chance to win a $1,000 Floor & Decor gift card each day throughout the promotion. Full details are available at[prograndopening.com/clackamaspro/register.aspx](http://www.prograndopening.com/clackamaspro/register.aspx) .


**The week-long celebration at the Clackamas location will feature daily events, vendor demonstrations, giveaways and Floor & Decor’s “7 Days of Deals,” offering customers 10% off when they buy 10 or more participating products* from Schluter, Laticrete, Mapei and wedi through April 22, only at the Clackamas location.**


**Thursday, April 16 — Ribbon Cutting Ceremony**


- Floor & Decor will host a Ribbon Cutting Ceremony on Thursday, April 16 at 10 a.m. PDT, in partnership with the North Clackamas Chamber of Commerce. A complimentary food truck will be on site from 7 a.m. through 11 a.m., and free Floor & Decor branded buckets will be given to the first 200 customers.


**Friday, April 17 — Schluter Featured Day**


- Schluter will be onsite with installation demonstrations and product tours. Free hats will also be available for the first 200 customers on Friday.


**Saturday, April 18 — Family-Friendly Super Saturday Event**


- The celebration continues from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. PDT, featuring local businesses including succulents from Vanessa’s Floral Shop and a variety of seasoning salts and dip mixes from Crumb and Get It. During Super Saturday, visitors can also participate in Floor & Decor’s Crack the Code game*, where participants will have a chance to win up to $100,000 by correctly entering a five-digit code.


**Sunday, April 19 — Home Trend Presentation from Top Designers**


- Floor & Decor’s top designers will present the latest trends, and free samples will be available with a design appointment. Plus, a complimentary ice cream truck will be available from 2 p.m. to 4 p.m.


**Monday, April 20 — Laticrete Featured Day**


- Laticrete will be onsite with installation demonstrations and product tours. A free tumbler will also be given to the first 200 customers on Monday.


**Tuesday, April 21 — Mapei Featured Day**


- Mapei will be onsite with installation demonstrations and product tours. T-shirts will also be given away to the first 200 customers on Tuesday.


**Wednesday, April 22 — wedi Featured Day**


- wedi will be onsite with installation demonstrations and product tours. A tote bag will also be given to the first 200 customers on Wednesday.

**About Floor & Decor Holdings, Inc.**


Floor & Decor is a multi-channel specialty retailer of hard surface flooring and related accessories and a commercial flooring distributor. At the end of fiscal 2025, the Company operated 270 warehouse-format stores and five design studios across 39 states. The Company offers a broad in-stock assortment of laminate and vinyl, tile, wood, and natural stone flooring, installation materials, decorative accessories, and adjacent categories at everyday low prices. Founded in 2000, Floor & Decor is headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia.
