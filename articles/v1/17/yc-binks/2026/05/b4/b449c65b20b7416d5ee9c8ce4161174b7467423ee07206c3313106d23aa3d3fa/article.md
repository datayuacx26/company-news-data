## Tips to Choose the Right Bollywood-Inspired Lehenga


Selecting an ideal lehenga online depends largely on whether the garment suits the wearer. Below are some guidelines to consider before purchasing a lehenga set.


### 1. Match with your body type


A flared lehenga creates volume at the bottom and looks beautiful on petite frames. For the curvy body types, an embroidered structured skirt along with a fitted blouse will create a clean outline.


### 2. Consider the function & venue


A daytime outdoor event calls for more lightweight fabrics as well as subtle colours. At the same time, an evening grand party event is an opportunity to go all glamorous.


### 3. Balance trend with comfort


Simply because a particular design is trending does not mean it has to suit you. Go for the style you can comfortably wear throughout the day.
