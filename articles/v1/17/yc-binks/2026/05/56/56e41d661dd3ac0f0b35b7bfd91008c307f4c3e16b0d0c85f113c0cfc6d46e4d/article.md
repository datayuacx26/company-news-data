## Colour Trends to Avoid in 2026


We know how thrilling it can be while experimenting with different colours. However, in 2026, there are a few colour trends that, honestly, you should avoid at all costs.


Starting with overly dull shades such as muddy browns and greys. They can easily fade you out in photos, along with lacking the vibrancy that weddings demand. Overly neon tones are famous for overpowering the natural features of the wearer on camera. In addition, try to avoid outdated colour combos like orange-purple or lime green-pink. They give off outdated vibes and lack the sophistication required in 2026 weddings.


Remember that your wedding photographs are eternal. Colours that do not go with your accessories or even the surroundings may look daring at first, but do not age well.

## FAQs


### 1. Which lehenga colour is trending in 2026?


Light pastel colours such as pink, lavender, and ivory are the new trends, along with classic colours such as red, emerald green, and royal blue.


### 2. Is red still in trend for weddings?


Of course! Red will always be a perfect colour for brides at their wedding ceremony and is never out of fashion.


### 3. Which colour is best for a mehendi function?


Traditionally, the best colour for mehendi is green; however, light pastel colours such as pink and ivory have also gained popularity recently.


### 4. How do I choose a lehenga colour based on skin tone?


Fair skin tones can opt for pastel and vibrant colours, whereas medium skin tones look elegant in deep jewel tones, maroon, red, and other rich hues.


### 5. Can I wear pastel lehengas for night weddings?


Although pastel colours are more suitable for daytime events, one can consider using dark pastel lehengas or heavily embroidered pastel lehengas for evening functions.
