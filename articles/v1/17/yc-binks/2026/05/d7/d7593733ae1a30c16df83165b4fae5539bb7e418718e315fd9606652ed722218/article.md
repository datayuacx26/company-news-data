# What to Wear to a Temple Wedding - A Complete Style Guide

Temple weddings are deeply rooted in tradition and culture. Unlike destination weddings or big banquet hall celebrations, they are more about rituals, purity, and timeless grace. Thus, the temple wedding outfit calls for modest and elegant dressing.


So, whether you are a bride, a bridesmaid, a close family member, or a wedding guest, this blog will help you select the perfect temple wedding outfit for women. The proper wedding ethnic wear will not only respect the culture and the venue, but will also give you a balance between style and comfort.


## Understanding Temple Wedding Dress Code


We all can agree that modesty is very important while attending a temple wedding ceremony. It means choosing appropriate traditional wedding attire with proper coverage on the shoulders and hemlines. Hence, it is best if you avoid dresses that are too revealing, such as deep necklines, backless blouses, and short hemlines. Temple weddings require wearing dresses that suit the traditional dress code of the place and are elegant at the same time.


Moreover, one important fact people often overlook is footwear, which is a part of an outfit. Since temples require guests to take off their shoes before entering, it is better to pair traditional wedding outfits with easily removable footwear, like flats and juttis.

## What Not to Wear to a Temple Wedding


Not all styles are temple wedding-approved. When selecting an outfit, avoid anything that is over-revealing, like deep necklines and backless blouses. Additionally, do not opt for very heavy party wear like shiny embroidery that will look out of place. Also, skip uncomfortable outfits that can restrict your movements or are difficult to wear during longer periods of time.


## Outfit Ideas Based on Wedding Functions


Temple weddings generally span over multiple events that require different kinds of outfits.


### 1. Temple Wedding Ceremony


The main event demands the most traditional version of yourself. Sarees or plain lehengas in basic colours will reflect your grace.


### 2. Pre-Wedding Rituals


Mehendi or sangeet rituals call for kurta sets or light lehengas.


### 3. Post-Wedding Celebration


A party or dinner after the temple event is perfect for you to wear somewhat fancier outfits, such as lehengas and decorated sarees.


## Smart Styling Tips for Long Wedding Rituals


Long rituals call for smart clothing decisions. Opt for breathable outfits which don’t make you feel sweaty or stuffy. Easy movements are very important because the ceremony will include sitting, standing, and performing other rituals.


Be prepared for the weather by checking the forecast and dressing appropriately. Bring a dupatta that will help cover up when required.


## Frequently Asked Questions


### 1. Can I wear black to a temple wedding?


Although black is not considered an auspicious colour for weddings, it's accepted to some extent because of fashion trends. However, for temple weddings, stick to Indian wedding colours as a sign of respect to the divine place.


### 2. Can I wear a lehenga to a temple wedding?


Yes, you can wear a light lehenga with adequate coverage for temple weddings as bridesmaids or wedding guests.


### 3. What makeup is suitable for a temple wedding?


Minimalistic and natural-looking makeup is recommended for temple weddings. For instance, you may choose natural colours and soft hues for makeup that enhances your natural features.


### 4. What should I avoid wearing to a temple wedding?


Avoid wearing revealing clothes, Western attire such as party wear dress, stilettos, and casual clothes.


### 5. Can I wear Indo-western outfits?


Yes, you can wear an Indo-western outfit like a lightweight dress for a wedding guest. Just keep in mind to avoid dressing immodestly when opting for Indo-Western wear.


### 6. Which saree is best for a temple wedding?


Kanjeevaram or Banarasi sarees are most appropriate for temple weddings, although silk cotton sarees are preferable in summer.


### 7. How do I stay comfortable during long temple wedding rituals?


Wearing comfortable and light clothes as per the occasion and weather is recommended.


### 8. What hairstyle suits a temple wedding?


Buns, decorated with gajra, braids, or simple updos, are excellent hairstyles to consider for your temple wedding outfits.


### 9. Are heels allowed in temples?


Since you must remove your footwear when entering the temple, heels become a little impractical. Opt for safer options like flats and juttis.


### 10. What are the common mistakes to avoid at a temple wedding?


Some common mistakes include overdressing, wearing uncomfortable outfits and choosing the wrong fabric. It is better to plan ahead and prioritise comfort along with tradition.
