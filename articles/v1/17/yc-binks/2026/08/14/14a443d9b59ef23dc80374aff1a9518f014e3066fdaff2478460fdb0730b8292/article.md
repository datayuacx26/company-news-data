# Trending Saree Blouse Designs You'll See Everywhere This Season

Saree blouse design moves fast, what was a niche request two seasons ago is now the first thing customers search for. Here's what's actually trending right now, and how to shop it without losing comfort along the way.


Pictured:[Nargis x Rozaana | Corset-Style Blouse with FlexiFit™ Elastic Back](https://getbinks.com/products/nargis-saree-blouse-corset-style-with-comfy-elastic-back)


## 1. Corset-style structure


Structured, waist-defining blouses, built with boning or panelling rather than just a stretch back, have moved from bridal wear into mainstream festive shopping. See our[corset style blouse guide](https://getbinks.com/pages/corset-style-saree-blouses-guide) for how to wear the look, and our broader[SculptFit™ guide](https://getbinks.com/pages/sculptfit-structured-support-guide) for the mechanics behind it.


## 2. Front-open closures


Front-open blouses are trending because they solve a real problem: no one wants help hooking up a blouse at a wedding. Buttons, knots or tie-ups at the front are both functional and a design feature now. Full range in our[blouse front design gallery](https://getbinks.com/pages/blouse-front-design-gallery-binks) .


## 3. Deep necklines with statement jewellery


As more brides and guests invest in statement neckpieces,[deep neck blouses](https://getbinks.com/pages/elegant-deep-neck-blouse-designs-by-binks) are trending right alongside them, the open neckline is designed to let jewellery lead.


Pictured:[Zohra x Luxe | Black Blouse with Gold Hand Embroidery and FlexiFit™ Elastic Sides](https://getbinks.com/products/zohra-black-gold-blouse-with-elastic-sides)


## 4. Puff sleeves, still going strong


We called this one last season and it's still true:[puff sleeves](https://getbinks.com/pages/puff-sleeve-blouse-style-guide-binks) remain the easiest way to make a simple saree feel festive.


## 5. Sweetheart necklines for a softer look


Not every bride wants a plunging neckline. The[sweetheart neck](https://getbinks.com/pages/sweetheart-neck-blouse-designs-guide) has become the go-to for a romantic, universally flattering alternative.
