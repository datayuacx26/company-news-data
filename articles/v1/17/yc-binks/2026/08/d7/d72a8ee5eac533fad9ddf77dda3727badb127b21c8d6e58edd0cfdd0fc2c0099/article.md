# New Blouse Sleeve Designs to Try This Season

Ask anyone who's shopped for a saree blouse recently: the neckline gets all the attention, but the sleeve is what actually changes how a blouse feels to wear, and how it reads visually. A few sleeve styles are having a real moment right now.

## Puff sleeves, the current favourite


Puff sleeves remain the most requested blouse sleeve style for festive wear. The volume at the shoulder adds a statement silhouette without needing extra embellishment elsewhere, which is why they work so well on simpler saree fabrics. See our full[puff sleeve blouse style guide](https://getbinks.com/pages/puff-sleeve-blouse-style-guide-binks) for how to wear them.


## Elbow-length sleeves, the quiet workhorse


Not every occasion calls for drama. Elbow-length sleeves have quietly become the most-worn everyday option, enough coverage for the office, cool enough for daily errands, and easy to layer jewellery over.


## Sleeveless, back for warm-weather events


With summer wedding season in full swing,[sleeveless blouses](https://getbinks.com/pages/sleeveless-blouse-design-ideas-and-styles) are back as the practical choice for daytime functions. They also happen to be the best canvas for arm jewellery, bangles, armlets, the works.


## How to choose based on your event


- **Wedding or sangeet:** puff sleeves or a fitted full sleeve with heavy cuff detailing.
- **Daytime function or puja:** elbow-length or sleeveless, depending on the weather.
- **Office or everyday:** a simple elbow or three-quarter sleeve in a solid colour.
