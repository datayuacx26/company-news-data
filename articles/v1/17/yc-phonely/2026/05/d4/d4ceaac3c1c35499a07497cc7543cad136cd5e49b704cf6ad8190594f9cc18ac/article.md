# Best Retell AI Alternatives for Voice AI Software

## Why are teams moving away from Retell AI?


[Retell AI](https://www.retellai.com/) is a capable voice AI platform with sub-second latency, a visual agent builder, and per-minute pricing starting at $0.07. For developer-led teams running voice pilots, it's a solid starting point.

### Which Retell AI alternatives work without developers?

Phonely uses a visual workflow builder where about 70% of businesses go live in under five minutes. Synthflow has a visual Flow Designer. Bland AI and Vapi are developer-first. PolyAI, Cognigy, and Five9 are enterprise platforms where setup typically involves the vendor's team.


### Can I use a Retell AI alternative for healthcare or other regulated industries?


Yes, but compliance coverage varies significantly across platforms.


Phonely holds **SOC 2, HIPAA, CCPA,** and **PCI** certifications. PolyAI covers **SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS,** and **ISO 27001** . Sierra lists **SOC 2, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, HIPAA,** and **the EU AI Act** .


Other platforms have restrictions. Synthflow only offers HIPAA on its enterprise plan. Vapi charges $1,000/month for HIPAA as an add-on. Bland AI describes itself as HIPAA-ready rather than HIPAA-compliant. Always verify directly with the vendor before deploying in a regulated environment.


### How does Retell AI's pricing actually break down?


Retell AI's headline rate starts at[$0.07 per minute](https://www.retellai.com/pricing) , but total cost depends on your LLM, TTS provider, and add-ons.


Branded caller ID adds $0.10 per outbound call. Verified numbers cost $10/month each. AI quality assurance runs $0.10 per minute on top. At high volumes, these layers make monthly spend significantly harder to forecast than the base rate suggests.


Retell offers $10 in free credits to start, though actual minutes depend on your LLM and voice provider. Phonely offers 100 free minutes upfront, no configuration needed.
