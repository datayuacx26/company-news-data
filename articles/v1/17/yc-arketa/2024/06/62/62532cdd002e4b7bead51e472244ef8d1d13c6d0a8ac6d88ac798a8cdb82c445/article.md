# NEW: Updated Branded Mobile App

## **Exciting News: Your Branded Mobile App Just Got Even Better!**


## **Why Customize Your Mobile App?**


Your mobile app is more than just a tool—it's an extension of your brand's identity and a powerful way to engage with your clients. By customizing your app to reflect your unique offerings and style, you create a memorable user experience that fosters loyalty and sets you apart from the competition. Arketa is currently the only platform allowing partners to fully customize their app. Say goodbye to cookie-cutter apps, that all look the same.


## **What's New?**


**Fully Customizable Homepage:** Now, you have complete control over what your clients see when they open your app. Our new homepage customization feature allows you to choose from a variety of widgets including 'Milestones', 'Promo Card', 'Upcoming Reservations', 'Favorites', and more. You can also opt for a minimalist look with no homepage at all—it's all about what suits your business best.


**Styling Options:** Consistency is key when it comes to branding. With our updated app builder, you can effortlessly customize the colors of your app to align with your brand's palette. Upload your logo as the app icon and select background images that resonate with your brand story—all with just a few clicks.


**Customizable Menu Bar:** Tailor your app's navigation to fit your specific offerings. Choose which tabs to include on your menu bar—whether it's 'Home', 'Pricing', 'Schedule', 'Community', or any other feature that matters most to your clients. This flexibility ensures that navigating your app is intuitive and seamless. Not only choose what goes on your navigation bar, but how it is displayed. Choose from a variety of icons that best match your studios branding and toggle on-and-off headings under each icon.


**Easy Updates in Real-Time:** Managing your app's content and appearance is now easier than ever. Within your Arketa dashboard, you can instantly toggle features on and off, rearrange widgets on your homepage with a simple drag-and-drop interface, and experiment with different color schemes—all with real-time updates reflected immediately in your app.
