# The Wait is Over. Introducing Arketa Rewind.

Early morning flows. Lunchtime HIIT sessions. That Sunday class you almost skipped but showed up to anyway. 2025 was a year of showing up, and now it's time to look back at the year that made you healthier and stronger, one class at a time.


We're thrilled to announce the first ever **Arketa Rewind** , a personalized year-end celebration for the studio owners who built the spaces, the instructors who led the classes, and the members who showed up, day after day.


Whether you're teaching the 6 AM class or the one who actually makes it there, we've got a Rewind experience for you.


## **What's Inside Your Rewind**


**For Studio Owners:** See how your community grew, which classes packed the room, and how many lives your studio touched this year.


**For Instructors:** Discover your biggest fans, the total number of people you impacted, and how many hours you spent teaching.


**For Members:** Find out how many hours you spent moving, who your favorite instructor was, and which personality type matches your class choices.


## **How to Access Your Rewind**


Your personalized Rewind is available now in your Arketa-branded studio app or website. Just open it up and see what your year looked like - sweat, stats, and all.


## **Why We Built This**


Arketa Rewind isn't just about numbers. It's about reflection, pride, and momentum. It's looking back on the year you committed to yourself and your community, then using that energy to push even harder in the year ahead.


Because whether you're running a studio or running to class, you showed up. And that's worth celebrating.


## **Ready to See Your Year?**


Check out your 2025 Arketa Rewind now. Celebrate your wins, your comebacks, and those days you didn't feel like going but went anyway.


Then share it. Tag your studio, hype up your favorite instructor, and show your friends what real consistency looks like.


Here's to the year you moved.
