# Introducing Arketa Elevate: Helping You Open and Grow Your Studio with Confidence

Opening or expanding a studio is one of the most exciting steps you can take as a fitness or wellness professional. At the same time it can also be overwhelming. Between finding funding, navigating leases, setting up operations, and figuring out how to attract clients before day one, the process can feel like too much to take on alone.


That is why we created **Arketa Elevate** , a new program designed to give studio owners and future studio owners the tools, resources, and expert guidance they need to launch and grow successfully.


## Why We Built Arketa Elevate


We have seen too many talented professionals struggle when making the leap from teaching classes to running a business. They know how to create an incredible client experience but the business side including leases, financing, payroll, legal, staffing, marketing, and pricing structures can be a different story.


Arketa Elevate bridges that gap. Our mission is to remove the guesswork so you can focus on your passion while knowing your business is built on a foundation that works.


## What the Program Includes


Through Arketa Elevate, you will get direct support from our team plus access to industry experts who have helped launch and grow hundreds of studios. Together we will guide you through every step of the process, including:


**- Real estate guidance** : Evaluate spaces, negotiate leases, and design a build-out that maximizes revenue


**- Financing and financial support** : Explore funding options and build a path toward sustainable profitability


**- Branding and marketing** : From your launch campaigns to ongoing marketing, we will help you create a brand that stands out and drives memberships


**- Operations and systems** : Payroll, staffing, legal, SOPs, and the right software to keep your business running smoothly


**- Hiring and staffing** : Build the right team from the start with proven playbooks and strategies


**- Launch and growth strategy** : Run campaigns that fill your classes before opening day and scale confidently once your doors are open


**- Ongoing support** : We are here for your questions at every stage, from opening day through expansion


## Who Arketa Elevate Is For


**- Aspiring studio owners** : If you are currently teaching online or in studios and dream of opening your own brick-and-mortar, Arketa Elevate gives you the framework to do it with confidence


**- Existing studios looking to expand** : Already running a successful location? We’ll help you replicate and scale your success into another space without repeating costly mistakes


## Your Partner From Day One


Owning a studio is about more than running a business. It is about creating a community, building a brand you are proud of, and sharing your passion on your terms. Arketa Elevate ensures that journey does not feel overwhelming or lonely.


With the right partner and proven playbook, you can skip the costly trial and error and step into studio ownership with an experienced partner in your corner.
