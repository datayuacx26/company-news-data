# Thinking Bigger About What’s Possible in the Home

The home environment is one of the least visible yet most influential drivers of outcomes and utilization, but traditional models rarely capture these risks in a structured way. As trusted companions in the home, Papa Pals regularly observe day-to-day challenges that might otherwise go unnoticed. In fact,[one in four Papa Pals](https://www.papa.com/resources/blog/pal-idi-results) we spoke with for recent in-depth interviews said they “often” or “always” notice a risk to the health, safety, or well-being of members in the home.
