# What is a Microschool and How Do I Start One in Arizona?

## Microschools are shaking up the education world in the best way possible! 🎉


These small, community-based schools prioritize personalized learning, flexible pacing, and student empowerment—giving kids an education that actually fits their needs. Unlike traditional schools, microschools offer a more dynamic, hands-on approach where kids can thrive socially and academically.


Microschools are growing fast, and Prenda is at the forefront of this movement! As one of the nation’s largest and most trusted microschool networks, Prenda makes it easy and fun to start your own microschool whether you have a professional education background or not (teachers, homeschool mamas, and public school parents- you’re perfect, let’s do this!). While you can absolutely start a microschool independently, it’s a lot harder to do on your own. That’s why we built Prenda—to get all the tricky stuff out. of. your. way. So you can focus on creating a rigorous, *yet whimsical* , learning environment. 🤯Yes, it’s possible.


This guide will walk you through Prenda’s simple 10-step process for launching a microschool in Arizona—plus, we’ll cover Arizona’s Empowerment Scholarship Account (ESA) program so your students can pay your microschool feels with little to no out-of-pocket cost. Yay!


So grab your favorite snack, take a deep breath, and let’s dive into how you can start your microschool adventure in Arizona! It’s going to be amazing. *You’re* going to be amazing. 🚀

You're already doing step 1! 🎉 Reading up on microschools in your area is essential. We recommend checking out these informative articles:


- [What Are Microschools?](https://www.edchoice.org/engage/microschools-what-are-they-what-do-they-cost-and-whos-interested/) (EdChoice)
- [What’s a Microschool and Why Focusing on how Kids Learn is Changing Education.](https://standtogether.org/stories/education/whats-microschool-why-focusing-how-kids-learn-changing-education) (Stand Together)
- [The Rise of Millennial-Powered Microschools](https://www.gettingsmart.com/2024/03/07/the-rise-of-millennial-powered-microschools/) (Getting Smart)


Consider your "why" and what type of learning environment you want to create. A significant part of this decision is your educational pedagogy and approach. At Prenda, we believe in empowering students to become self-directed learners who have strong skills and an intrinsic drive to pursue their goals.


That said, there are many wonderful and beautiful ways to approach educating children. If Prenda's learning approach doesn't match your vision, that's completely okay! We want the future to be full of diverse educational options that meet everyone's unique needs. So even if you want to go it alone, we hope this process overview is a support to you in your journey! 🧳


### **Step 2: Set Up Your Microschool**


To get started, simply click "[Start My Microschool](http://discover.prenda.com/) " on prenda.com (or open that there link in a new tab…go on now, you got this). Starting this process in no way commits you to starting a microschool IRL. You can explore this whole idea with no commitment and no cost. You’ve got nothing to lose!


After you click Start My Microschool, you'll be asked some get-to-know-you questions to help tailor the process to your needs. Follow the prompts to create and account and don’t forget to verify your email. ### **Step 3: Choose Who You Want to Serve**


Will you open your school to other people's children (the norm), or do you want to start with just your own kids in more of a homeschool setting with Prenda's support? This decision will shape your microschool's structure and growth potential. If you just want to homeschool with Prenda, select "Single-family Microschool," and welcome aboard! But if you want to earn an income as a microschool guide in your community, you’ll select the “Multi-family Microschool” option.


### **Step 4: Select Your Funding Source**


In Arizona, you have two primary funding options: direct pay or the Arizona Empowerment Scholarship Program (ESA).


The ESA program works by redirecting state education dollars to parents, allowing them to decide what type of education works best for their student. The average award amount varies depending on your school district but is approximately $7,000 per student per year. Prenda acts as the vendor and manages all the invoicing and payment collection, so guides (that's what we call our microschool teachers!) don't have to worry about it.


All K-12 students in Arizona are eligible for this scholarship, and funding is universally given to anyone who applies. This makes Arizona one of the most microschool-friendly states in the country! 🌵


For more detailed information about Arizona's ESA program, check out:


- [Arizona's Empowerment Scholarship Account Program: Everything you need to know](https://www.prenda.com/post/arizonas-empowerment-scholarship-account-program-everything-you-need-to-know)

Think about creating an environment where kids can feel comfortable, be creative, and play. Many Prenda microschools operate out of homes, giving them an intimate, cozy vibe. Others use dance studios, tutoring centers, or even barns and outdoor spaces! 🏡 Here are just a few of the locations where people have run Prenda microschools...

Once you have a location, it's time to decide what your income will be. Prenda guides set their own per-student rate, allowing each microschool founder to control their income. Once you have a location and your guide fee is set, you can use Prenda's profile builder to create your microschool profile. You can also use your profile link to text to friends or post on social media.

As you find interested families, all you have to do is send them an invite to join your school. They'll fill out a quick form, and bam—you'll see them pop up in your Prenda account, ready for school!
