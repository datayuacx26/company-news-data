# From Burnout to Balance: The Real Reasons Teachers Are Making the Switch to Microschools


Burnout is real, folks. 😓From overcrowded classrooms to standardized testing pressure and low compensation, many teachers are left feeling drained, frustrated, and disconnected from the passion that led them to education in the first place. Research puts K-12 classroom teacher[at the top of the list when it comes to stress](https://news.gallup.com/poll/393500/workers-highest-burnout-rate.aspx) . But here’s the exciting news- the microschool movement is helping thousands of teachers escape the daily grind and rediscover the joy of teaching. 🎇


What is a microshool, pray tell?


Microschools are small (think 5-12 kids) independent schools that focus on personalized learning, inquiry, and mastery. Let’s explore why so many teachers are ditching the traditional classroom for microschools and how Prenda (one of the nation's leading microschool networks) makes it easy to make that switch.


## Microschools Give Teachers Flexibility and Autonomy


Imagine your life with no more rigid bell schedules, no more district-mandated curriculum, and no more constant test prep. Microschools allow teachers to **take control of their teaching environment** . With Prenda, teachers—known as “guides”—can create a schedule that fits their lifestyle, set their own compensation, and adjust lessons to match each student’s needs.


**You call the shots** :


- Want to start your day later? Go for it.
- Want to take breaks for project-based learning or field trips? Absolutely.
- Need to adapt your curriculum mid-year to better meet students’ interests? No one’s stopping you.


This freedom to **teach without the constraints of standardized testing** or curriculum schedules is a game-changer. Traditional school systems often leave little room for personalization, as teachers are forced to march through predetermined lessons to stay on schedule. In a microschool, however, you can focus on what matters—helping each student grow at their own pace. 😍


Microschool guides can also **decide their own compensation** , giving them financial control that traditional teachers often lack. With Prenda’s support, they have access to state funding and scholarships that help cover costs and allow many families to attend with no out-of-pocket-cost, making it easier to **run a sustainable school** ​​. This autonomy allows teachers to strike a healthy balance between work and life—something every educator deserves. ## Fewer Students, Stronger Connections


Many teachers say they entered the profession to **inspire students and make meaningful connections** , only to find themselves overwhelmed by the demands of managing 30 or more kids at a time. Microschools solve this problem by keeping class sizes small, usually between **8-12 students** . You get to create the class!


In this setting, microschool guides can **build deep, trusting relationships** with their students—relationships that spark genuine learning and growth. There’s time to learn each student’s interests, strengths, and challenges, and to help them set personalized goals. As a guide, you’re no longer just managing behavior—you’re **mentoring and inspiring young minds** .


And the impact goes both ways. We all know that students thrive when they feel connected to their teachers and peers​​. In microschools, kids are more engaged and motivated because they feel seen, heard, and supported. 🤝These strong relationships foster **curiosity, creativity, and a love of learning** —qualities that often get lost in larger traditional classrooms.


## No More Late-Night Lesson Planning and Weekend Grading


One of the biggest frustrations for traditional classroom teachers is the **endless hours spent outside of school planning lessons, grading papers, and prepping for the next day** . It’s not unusual for teachers to spend weekends buried in paperwork, leaving little time to recharge or enjoy life outside of work.


Microschools change that. With Prenda’s learning system, guides have access to a **research-backed, engaging curriculum** that emphasizes mastery-based learning and inquiry. Rather than teaching to the test, guides implement hands-on, project-based learning experiences that engage students and encourage them to explore topics deeply​.


The beauty of this model is that students work at their own pace, mastering concepts as they go. As a guide, your role shifts from being a taskmaster to **facilitating learning and guiding inquiry** .[Learn more about our approach to learning here!](https://www.prenda.com/learning-experience) Prenda provides tools to track student progress, so there’s no need to spend hours grading or preparing traditional lesson plans. This frees up your evenings and weekends, giving you more time to **focus on what matters most—whether that’s family, hobbies, or just some well-earned rest** ​.
