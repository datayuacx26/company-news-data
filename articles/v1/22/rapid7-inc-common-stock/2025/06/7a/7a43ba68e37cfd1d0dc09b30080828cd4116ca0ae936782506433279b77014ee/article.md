# Rapid7 Recognized by Boston Business Journal as a Best Place to Work

We’re excited to share that Rapid7 has been recognized by the


*Boston Business Journal*


as one of Boston’s Best Places to Work for the 14th consecutive year. We ranked an impressive #6 in the extra-large company category for 2025.


This year’s jump—two spots higher than our 2024 ranking—is a reflection of the intentional culture we continue to build and nurture. Our recognition is based entirely on anonymous employee feedback, evaluating everything from career growth and meaningful work to leadership support and day-to-day engagement. And once again, our people spoke loudly and proudly.


"Our work lives and personal lives are shaped by people and culture," said Katie Kulikoski, Rapid7’s Chief People Officer. "The people at Rapid7 are passionate about performing their best daily, leading to positive outcomes for customers and colleagues. This shared passion and commitment further enhance our company culture, empowering all people to build incredibly rewarding careers. This award celebrates their continuous dedication to fostering an outstanding workplace."


### **What Makes Rapid7 a Best Place to Work?**


Our global headquarters at 120 Causeway Street is more than just a home base. It’s a hub for connection, creativity, and collaboration. Located next to TD Garden and North Station, the space was designed with accessibility and flexibility in mind. Employees work on a hybrid schedule, spending three days in the office and two working remotely. This model supports both productivity and work-life balance.


Inside the office, employees can choose from a variety of thoughtfully designed workspaces. Quiet zones, private rooms with Zoom-enabled technology, and whiteboard-equipped spaces support focused or strategic work. Community areas such as our Barista Bar, speakeasy lounge, and open collaboration zones encourage spontaneous interaction and cross-functional energy.


The Boston office is home to teams across every function. That includes engineering, sales, marketing, finance, and people strategy. Together, they create a unified, mission-driven culture that defines the Rapid7 experience.


### **A Culture That Empowers**


For existing team members, this recognition is a moment of shared pride. For those considering joining us, it’s a powerful signal of what life at Rapid7 is really like: fast-paced, supportive, innovative, and deeply human. It’s a place where you can bring your whole self to work, grow a career with purpose, and make a lasting impact on the security landscape.


We’re proud of what we’ve built. We’re even prouder of the people who make it all possible.
