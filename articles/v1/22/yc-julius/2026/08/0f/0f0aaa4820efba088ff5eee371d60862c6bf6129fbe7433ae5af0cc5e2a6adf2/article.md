## What is Slidesgo?


[Slidesgo](https://slidesgo.com/) is a Google Slides and PowerPoint template library


. You browse templates by topic, style, and color, then open them directly in Google Slides, PowerPoint, or Canva.


It also includes an


[AI Presentation Maker](https://slidesgo.com/ai/presentation-maker) , which drafts an editable deck once you describe a topic and pick a theme.


Every template gives you a finished layout to start from, so you skip the blank slide and start editing right away.


## Key Slidesgo features


Slidesgo has grown well past a plain template downloader, and most of what makes it useful shows up once you start editing. Here's what the platform offers:


-


Template library:


Browse a large library of presentation templates for business, education, and creative use cases.


-


AI Presentation Maker:


Enter a topic and choose a style, and Slidesgo generates an editable deck using hundreds of template designs.


-


Extensive customization:


You can change colors, fonts, images, and layouts on most templates, while some design elements are controlled from the master slides.


-


Built-in visual assets:


Slidesgo includes access to icons and other design resources, including Flaticon content, to make it easier to update visuals while editing.


-


Data visualization:


Slidesgo templates include editable charts, infographics, and other visual elements you can update with your own data.


-


Multi-format export:


Many Slidesgo templates are available for Google Slides or PowerPoint, and selected templates can also be opened in Canva.


-


Free and Premium access:


Registered free users get 3 template downloads a month and must keep the attribution slide, while


[Premium](https://slidesgo.com/pricing) removes attribution and raises downloads up to 150 per month.

## The billing and cancellation complaints (and how to cancel Slidesgo)


The single loudest theme in Slidesgo reviews is money. Users report being charged after canceling and still getting invoices for months. A few describe a 500 error inside the "My Subscription" menu that blocks cancellation outright.


I can't verify every individual charge, but the pattern is too consistent to wave off. So if you subscribe, protect yourself.


Here’s what to keep in mind:


⚠️ Problem


✅ What to do

Cancellation won't go through

You're still getting invoices

## Is Slidesgo safe, or is it a scam?


Slidesgo is not a scam. It's a legitimate product from Freepik, a well-established design company, and millions of people use its templates without incident.


The real risk is the subscription itself: the billing and cancellation complaints are documented and repeated, and there are scattered reports of spam email and data-handling gripes. Fritz.ai puts it well -


Slidesgo is legitimate, but the billing clarity needs serious work


.


Use it with the same caution you'd give any auto-renewing subscription. Use a disputable card, monthly billing, and a saved cancellation confirmation.


## My personal take on Slidesgo


Slidesgo gets you a finished-looking deck fast, and that speed shows up the moment you open a template and start typing over the placeholder text.


The template library does the heavy lifting


a blank deck usually demands of you, and the design consistency held up better than I expected going in. Fonts, colors, and icon styles stay matched within a single template, so a 20-slide deck built from one design doesn't look stitched together from five different sources.


Once I'd built more than a handful of decks,


originality became the weak spot


. I kept reaching for the same card shapes, and realized my "unique" deck probably looked familiar to anyone who'd sat through a class presentation or a startup pitch in the last year.


The AI Presentation Maker carries the same trade-off. It earns its place as a starting point that saves you the blank-slide stall, but the content needs a rewrite before it's ready to show anyone, and the free plan's attribution slide is awkward to explain away on a client deck.


The billing side is where my confidence dropped the most.


I didn't personally get charged after canceling, but the pattern across reviews is consistent enough that I'd treat it as a real risk. Canceling early and screenshotting the confirmation isn't optional advice here, it's a precaution worth taking.


Here's my verdict:


Slidesgo earns its keep for teachers, students, and anyone who needs a clean deck fast and doesn't mind a template someone else has probably used too. If you're billing a client for the work or building something that needs to look unmistakably yours, budget extra time to rework the design, and keep a close eye on your subscription from day one.

## Frequently asked questions


### Is Slidesgo free?


Yes, Slidesgo is free with limits


. Registering gets you 3 template downloads a month with an attribution slide and limited AI access. A Premium subscription raises your download limit to 150 templates a month and removes the attribution slide.


### Is Slidesgo safe to use?


Yes, Slidesgo is safe to use


. It's a legitimate product from Freepik, a well-known design resource company. The main risk is subscription billing, so pay monthly and keep a cancellation record.


### How do I cancel a Slidesgo subscription?


Cancel a Slidesgo subscription from your account under "My Subscription,"


then screenshot the confirmation. If invoices keep coming, email support with your account email, and dispute the charge through your card issuer as a last resort.


### Can you use Slidesgo templates for commercial work?


Yes, you can use Slidesgo templates for commercial work,


but free-tier downloads require keeping the Slidesgo attribution slide


, which many clients won't accept. Premium removes the attribution requirement, though you should check current license terms before final use.


### Is Slidesgo worth it?


Yes, Slidesgo is worth it for teachers, students, and quick internal decks


, since it saves time without requiring design skill. It's a weaker fit for client or agency work, where the attribution requirement and overused templates work against you.
