## Decktopus pricing plans breakdown


Decktopus keeps its plan lineup short, which makes it easy to compare. The catch is that credits, more than the base price, drive most of what you'll spend.


Here's what each tier gives you:


### Free: $0


-


What's included:


A small pool of free credits at signup, enough to build one deck and try the AI generation flow.


-


Best for:


Kicking the tires before you commit any money.


-


Pros:


You can test the AI slide generation without entering a card.


-


Cons:


This credit pool only comes around once (at signup), and doesn't refresh monthly. There's no ongoing free plan.


### Pro: starts at $9.99 per month, billed annually


-


What's included:


Presentations and carousels, 750 AI credits a month, PDF and PPT export, one-click sharing, watermark removal, AI image generation, and prompt-based slide editing.


-


Billing options:


$9.99 per month billed annually, $19.99 per month on a 3-month plan, or $24.99 per month billed monthly.


-


Best for:


Solopreneurs, freelancers, and creators who build decks often enough to use a monthly credit refresh.


-


Pros:


750 credits a month covers about 150 slides, which is plenty for someone building a handful of decks each month.


-


Cons:


AI edits spend credits too, so heavy editing can use up your slide count faster than you'd expect.


### Business: starts at $19.99 per seat per month, billed annually


-


What's included:


Everything in Pro, 1,000 credits per seat each month, a custom domain, a brand kit, a shared team workspace, and slide-level analytics.


-


Billing options:


$19.99 per seat per month billed annually, $39.99 per seat per month on a 3-month plan, or $49.99 per seat per month billed monthly.


-


Best for:


Small teams that need consistent branding and shared decks in one place.


-


Pros:


Each additional seat gets its own credit allocation, so one heavy user doesn't drain the rest of the team's credits.


-


Cons:


Credits don't pool across seats, so a team member who runs out mid-month can't draw from a teammate's unused allocation.


### Enterprise: custom pricing


-


What's included:


Corporate branding systems, dedicated onboarding, and support for scaled teams.


-


Best for:


Organizations that need brand control and hands-on setup across a lot of seats.


-


Pros:


Custom pricing lets larger orgs negotiate a plan that fits their exact seat count and support needs.


-


Cons:


Custom branding systems and dedicated onboarding add setup steps before a team is up and running, which can slow down how quickly new team members start creating decks.

## Costs and limits to watch for


Decktopus's pricing page doesn't mention everything that affects what you'll pay or give up.


A few of the costs below only showed up once I started using the product myself:


-


Edits burn credits too:


Iterating on a deck isn't free. Editing a slide with AI or Edit Text costs 5 credits per edit, the same rate as generating it in the first place, so a heavy revision cycle can cost as much as the original deck did.


-


Logo removal is paid-only:


The free signup credits leave Decktopus branding on your deck. Removing it means upgrading to Pro or higher.


-


PowerPoint editing has limits:


You can export to PPT, but some reviewers note that the exported files aren't always cleanly editable afterward.


-


Integrations run through Zapier:


Decktopus natively connects to Flaticon, Freepik, Icons8, Vimeo, and YouTube for design assets. Zapier covers everything else, letting you trigger deck generation from a new CRM lead, a form submission, or another app event.


## Which Decktopus plan should you choose?


The right plan comes down to how many decks you make and whether anyone else touches them.


### Choose Pro if you:


-


Work solo and build presentations regularly.


-


Need Decktopus branding gone from client-facing decks.


-


Make somewhere around 10 decks a month or fewer.


### Choose Business if you:


-


Work on a team that shares decks and needs consistent branding.


-


Want a custom domain, brand kit, and slide analytics.


-


Want the best per-seat rate by paying annually, though monthly and 3-month options exist too.


### Choose Enterprise if you:


-


Run a larger org that needs fully corporate-branded output.


-


Want dedicated onboarding as you roll the tool out.


-


Are rolling Decktopus out across many seats.


### Skip Decktopus if you:


-


Only need a deck once in a while (trial credits or PowerPoint will do).


-


Don't want to manage a credit system on top of a subscription price.


## Is Decktopus worth the cost?


Decktopus can be worth the cost if you build decks often enough that the credit refresh actually gets used. It also helps if you want AI handling the first draft of layout so you're editing rather than starting blank.


I'd evaluate it based on how much you create:


-


It's worth it if:


You produce client-facing decks or internal presentations on a regular basis and want AI to handle the first draft of layout and design.


-


It's best for:


Solo creators and small teams who need branded, shareable decks and interactive elements like forms and quizzes.


-


Skip it if:


You only make the occasional presentation. The free signup credits or a standard tool like PowerPoint will cover you for less.


From what I've seen after testing it,


Decktopus earns its price once you're building more than a couple decks a month


. At that pace, the 750 monthly credits on Pro cover you with room left for edits, and $9.99 a month starts looking cheap compared to the hours you'd spend formatting slides by hand.

## Frequently asked questions


### Is Decktopus free?


No, Decktopus doesn't have an ongoing free plan


. New users get a small pool of free credits at signup, enough to build a small deck, and then need a paid plan or top-up credits to keep going.


### How much does Decktopus Pro cost?


Decktopus Pro costs


$9.99 per month on annual billing ($119.88 up front)


, $19.99 per month on a three-month cycle, or $24.99 month-to-month. Every Pro option includes 750 AI credits a month.


### How do Decktopus AI credits work?


Decktopus spends 5 credits to generate a slide and 5 more each time you edit one with AI


. Pro includes 750 credits a month (about 150 slides), and you can buy top-up packages from $4.99 that never expire.


### Can I pay monthly instead of annually?


Yes,


Decktopus offers monthly, three-month, and annual billing on the Pro and Business plans.


### Is Decktopus worth it?


Yes, Decktopus is worth it for solo creators and small teams who build decks regularly.


It stops being worth it once you're building decks so rarely that the monthly credit refresh goes unused.
