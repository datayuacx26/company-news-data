## Prezi plans and pricing breakdown


Basic and Standard both cap you at 500 one-time AI credits, and that cap disappears the moment you hit Plus. Premium builds on that with presentation analytics, and Teams adds the branding and collaboration tools a group needs to stay consistent.


Each plan builds on the one below it,


so let's break down exactly what you get at every tier:


### Basic: Free


-


What's included:


Up to 5 shareable visual projects and


[500 one-time Prezi AI credits](https://support.prezi.com/hc/en-us/articles/35653601291543-How-do-Prezi-AI-credits-work) . You can earn more credits through referrals.


-


Best for:


Anyone testing Prezi before committing to a paid plan.


-


Pros:


You get real access to Prezi AI without entering a card number, so you can build something functional before paying anything.


-


Cons:


Every project you make is visible to anyone with the link, so it's not a good fit if you need private drafts.


### Standard: $7 per month, billed annually


-


What's included:


Unlimited projects, 500 one-time Prezi AI credits, and advanced privacy controls. You can earn more credits through referrals.


-


Best for:


Personal use with light presentation needs.


-


Pros:


Privacy controls let you keep projects hidden, which the free tier doesn't offer at all.


-


Cons:


Your AI credits stay capped at the same 500 you'd get for free, so heavy AI use still runs out fast.


### Plus: $19 per month, billed annually


-


What's included:


Unlimited Prezi AI, PowerPoint import, PDF export, presentation recording, and offline access through the desktop app.


-


Best for:


Individual and business users who present often and need to work without an internet connection.


-


Pros:


Removing the AI cap and adding offline access solves the two biggest limits on the cheaper tiers.


-


Cons:


You still can't see how your audience engages with a presentation once you've sent it out, since analytics aren't included here.


### Premium: $29 per month, billed annually


-


What's included:


Everything in Plus, plus presentation analytics, advanced online training, and priority technical support.


-


Best for:


Professionals who want to track how a presentation performs after it goes out.


-


Pros:


Analytics let you see whether people are actually opening and finishing what you send them, which Plus can't show you.


-


Cons:


There's no shared branding or collaboration tools, so a small team relying on Premium alone can end up with decks that don't match each other.


### Teams: $39 per user per month, billed annually


-


What's included:


Everything in Premium, plus collaboration tools, commenting, Slack integration, a custom brand kit, and secure single sign-on.


-


Best for:


Businesses that need shared branding and a consistent look across multiple presenters.


-


Pros:


A shared brand kit and commenting keep multiple presenters working from the same look and the same feedback loop without passing files back and forth.


-


Cons:


Most of what you're paying for here is built around coordinating a group, so a single presenter working alone won't use much beyond what Premium already covers.


## Prezi EDU pricing breakdown


I checked this track separately since it runs on its own pricing, and it's meant for students and educators with a valid .edu email. The features mirror what you'd get on the Individual and Business tiers already covered,


just at a lower price:


-


EDU Plus ($4 per month billed annually):


Matches Individual and Business Plus, with unlimited Prezi AI, offline access, and PDF export.


-


EDU Pro ($8 per month billed annually):


Adds the same analytics as Premium, though here it tracks progress across a whole class, since Prezi built that feature with classroom use in mind. Premium's version stays focused on a single presentation.


-


EDU Teams ($19 per user per month billed annually):


Brings in the same collaboration tools and custom brand kit as Teams, aimed at school departments running several accounts under one roof.


## Prezi pricing gotchas to watch


A few things about Prezi's pricing aren't obvious until you're already in the checkout flow.


These are the ones worth knowing before you pay:


⚠️ Gotcha


💡 What it means for you


Annual billing only


Prezi doesn't offer month-to-month billing on paid plans. The rates require paying for a full year up front, and there's no lower-commitment option to test a plan before locking in.


The 500-credit AI cap


Basic and Standard both start you with 500 Prezi AI credits, and that balance doesn't refresh. If AI generation is your main reason for using Prezi, you'll burn through it fast and need Plus for unlimited access.


Offline is paywalled


The desktop app, offline access, and PDF export don't exist below Plus. Present somewhere without internet and you're on the $19 tier by default.


Analytics is Premium-only


Presentation analytics only appears on the $29 Premium plan and above.


Teams pricing is per seat


Business Teams runs $39 per user per month, so the total cost scales directly with how many people you add.


## Which Prezi plan should you choose?


The right plan comes down to how often you present, whether you rely on Prezi AI, and whether you ever work offline.


### Choose Basic if you:


-


Just want to test the zooming format before spending anything.


-


Make presentations rarely and don't need offline access.


-


Can live with a starting pool of 500 Prezi AI credits that doesn't refresh, though you can earn more by referring friends.


### Choose Standard if you:


-


Present regularly but stay light on AI generation.


-


Want privacy controls and revocable links for client-facing decks.


-


Don't need the desktop app or offline mode.


### Choose Plus if you:


-


Want unlimited Prezi AI without watching a credit counter.


-


Present in places where you can't count on Wi-Fi.


-


Need PDF export, recording, or live remote presenting.


### Choose Premium if you:


-


Track how people engage with your presentations.


-


Want priority support behind you.


-


Already need everything in Plus, and analytics is the deciding factor.


### Skip Prezi entirely if you:


-


Build decks in a standard slide format and don't need a zooming, non-linear layout.


-


Share files with people who expect a native PowerPoint or Google Slides file.


-


Present as a team and need shared branding without paying for the Teams tier.

## Frequently asked questions


### Is Prezi free?


Yes, Prezi has a free Basic plan


that doesn't require a credit card. It includes the online editor and one-time 500 AI credits, but unlimited AI, offline access, and PDF export all require a paid plan.


### Is Prezi worth it?


Prezi is worth it if the zooming, motion-based presentation format helps your ideas land better


than static slides. If you only need standard slides, a cheaper or free tool will likely do the job without the learning curve.


### Does Prezi have a student or education discount?


Yes, Prezi offers discounted Edu Plus and Edu Pro plans


for students and educators with a valid education email.


### Can I cancel Plus AI anytime?


Yes, you can cancel anytime through the billing portal


, but Plus AI doesn't issue refunds on subscription payments. Cancel before your next renewal date to avoid another charge.


### Does Plus AI work in both PowerPoint and Google Slides?


Yes, one Plus AI subscription includes the add-in for both PowerPoint and Google Slides


, so you can use it in whichever app you're working in.
