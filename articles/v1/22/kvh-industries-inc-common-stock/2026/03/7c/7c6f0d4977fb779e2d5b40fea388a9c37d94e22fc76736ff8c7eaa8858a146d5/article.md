# MOL Chemical Tankers Signs Up for KVH Link

***KVH and MOL Chemical Tankers deploy KVH Link, the world’s leading crew entertainment service, across MOLCT’s*** ***in-house managed*** ***fleet***


KVH Industries has today announced that, together with MOL Chemical Tankers (“MOLCT”), they are deploying KVH Link across their in-house managed fleet. MOLCT has contracted MOVIElink™, TVlink™, MUSIClink™, and YOURlink™ to create a compelling crew content package onboard for their crew. With a wide selection of movies, including Hollywood blockbusters, Bollywood hits, major Pinoy releases in addition to top TV series and documentaries delivered each month, crew will have access to quality entertainment options during designated off-duty hours, complementing established work and rest requirements.


Speaking with Mr. Louis Liu, who heads Infrastructure & Cyber Security at MOL Chemical Tankers, he underscored the organization’s position, stating, “At MOL Chemical Tankers, we believe that our seafarers are the lifeblood of our business. We were eager to offer them a genuine morale-boosting service that would help enrich their downtime while onboard, as part of our comprehensive crew wellbeing program. KVH Link with its breadth of high-quality content, makes it ideally suited for the diversity of our global crews. Beyond delivering entertainment content, KVH Link doubles as a powerful onboard communication tool, where its “YOURlink” service allows us to distribute important corporate news, announcements, and updates directly and seamlessly to our ship crews. It operates much like a digital signage system, facilitating timely and consistent communication from the office to our vessels.”

“We’re thrilled to be working with MOLCT to enhance their already comprehensive crew wellbeing program” said Keng Teen Phang, Regional Sales Director APAC. “We look forward to harnessing our 70 years of experience in providing entertainment and news to MOLCT’s seafarers while also providing vital head office updates in a way that is both accessible and stimulating for everyone onboard.”


KVH Link content is constantly refreshed with the latest releases, and is available to crew, individually or in a group, on multiple devices simultaneously, and delivered over-the-air to the linkHUB media server using onboard SATCOM or 5G/cellular services. KVH Link content is licensed from copyright holders for crew viewing. Thanks to multiple secure delivery methods, KVH Link also reduces the risk of cyberthreats that could be brought onboard via unlicensed content and USB drives.
