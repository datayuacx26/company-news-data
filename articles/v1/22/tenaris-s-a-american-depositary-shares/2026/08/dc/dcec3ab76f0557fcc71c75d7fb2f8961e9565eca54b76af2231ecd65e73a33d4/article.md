# Toyota TLAC recognizes Tenaris for quality excellence

PUBLISHED ON 08/06/2026


Share


Share


PUBLISHED ON 08/06/2026


Tenaris’s seamless steel pipe mill in Campana, Argentina, has been recognized by Toyota for quality excellence, highlighting the company’s outstanding performance throughout 2025.


The award places Tenaris among Toyota’s top-performing supply chain partners in the region, recognizing consistent results for quality, one of the three key pillars used by Toyota to evaluate suppliers supporting its manufacturing network.


“This recognition reflects the commitment and dedication of our entire team,” said **Andrea Previtali,** President of Tenaris for the Southern Cone. “Meeting Toyota’s demanding quality standards is a source of pride for us, and this award reinforces our position as a trusted first-tier supplier. It also motivates us to continue strengthening our partnership and supporting Toyota’s success in the years ahead.”


The recognition further strengthens Tenaris’s position as a reliable partner for the automotive industry in Latin America, delivering high-quality products backed by the technical expertise, service and logistical support required by leading automotive manufacturers.
