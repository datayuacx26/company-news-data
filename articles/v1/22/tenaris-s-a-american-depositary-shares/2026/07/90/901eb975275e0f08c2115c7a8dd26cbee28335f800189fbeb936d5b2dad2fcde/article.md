# Tenaris contributes to the construction of the Aquatics Center of Matagorda County

PUBLISHED ON 07/20/2026


Share


Share


PUBLISHED ON 07/20/2026


Tenaris announced on July 20, 2026, that it will donate $50,000 toward the construction of the Aquatics Center of Matagorda County. The Aquatics Center represents an important step in the county's long-term vision to enhance wellness and recreational opportunities for residents.


Located in central Matagorda County, the facility is intended to serve residents throughout the county and neighboring communities. In addition to competitive swimming, fitness, therapy, and family recreation opportunities, the center will support critical water safety and swim instruction programs aimed at reducing drowning risks.


"Strong communities are built through collaboration and shared commitment," said **Guillermo Moreno** , Tenaris U.S. President. "We are pleased to support a project that will create lasting value for the people of Matagorda County, and we look forward to seeing its positive impact for years to come."


The contribution to the Aquatics Center reflects Tenaris's broader approach to community investment. Through partnerships with local organizations and institutions, the company supports initiatives that promote education, well-being, economic development, and quality of life across the communities where it operates.


In Matagorda County, those efforts include the recent celebration of the[10th anniversary of the Manufacturing Technology Program with Wharton County Junior College](https://www.tenaris.com/en/news/2026/tenaris-and-wcjc-celebrate-10th-anniversary-of-manufacturing-technology-program) , a partnership focused on workforce development and technical education, as well as the[awarding of more than $200,000 in Roberto Rocca scholarships to local high school seniors.](https://www.tenaris.com/en/news/2026/tenaris-awards-more-than-360-000-in-roberto-rocca-scholarships-to-us-high-school-seniors)
