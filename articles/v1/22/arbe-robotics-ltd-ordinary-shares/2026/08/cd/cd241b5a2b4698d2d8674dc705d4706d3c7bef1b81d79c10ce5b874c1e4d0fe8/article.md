# Arbe to Present at Canaccord Genuity's 46th Annual Growth Conference

TEL AVIV, Israel


, Aug. 3, 2026


/PRNewswire/ --[Arbe Robotics Ltd.](https://arberobotics.com/) (NASDAQ: ARBE) (TASE: ARBE), a global leader in ultra-high-resolution radar solutions, today announced that Kobi Marenko, President, will present at and meet with investors during Canaccord Genuity's 46th Annual Growth Conference, taking place at the InterContinental in Boston on August 11-13, 2026.


**Format:** In-person presentation and one-on-one meetings
**When:** Presentation on Tuesday, August 11, 2026 at 3:00 pm ET
**Location:** InterContinental Boston, 510 Atlantic Avenue, Boston, MA


Investors who want to see the live presentation can view the presentation at the following webcast link at or after the presentation time:[https://event.summitcast.com/view/WuFmFdTcA9mVsUGHZJFU62/Rq47eXXogLFsoy95XKdUYF](https://event.summitcast.com/view/WuFmFdTcA9mVsUGHZJFU62/Rq47eXXogLFsoy95XKdUYF)


Investors who want to schedule a one-on-one meeting with management during the conference should contact their contact person at Canaccord Genuity or contact Arbe's investor relations team atinvestors@arberobotics.com .


**About Arbe**


Arbe (NASDAQ: ARBE), a global leader in ultra-high-resolution radar solutions, is redefining radar as a core sensing platform for next-generation mobility and advanced sensing applications. Arbe's complete radar technology stack includes proprietary automotive-grade RF transmitter and receiver chips, a high-definition radar processing chip, the Phoenix radar system with 2,304 virtual channels, and advanced AI algorithms that transform radar data into a perception-ready layer. By delivering an exceptional level of detail, real-time processing, and scalable system performance, Arbe enables OEMs, Tier-1s, and technology partners to build more capable perception systems for passenger vehicles, robotaxis, heavy machinery, defense, and additional advanced sensing markets. Headquartered in Tel Aviv, Israel, the company also operates offices in the United States, Germany, and China.
