# [Precision Ag | Part 2] Precision Farming with John Deere: How Is It Different?

Farming is a complex and demanding task that requires careful monitoring and management of crop growth to ensure maximum yield. However, traditional farming techniques often rely on guesswork and estimation, which can result in inefficient use of resources, lower crop yields, and higher costs.


This is where John Deere's precision ag technology comes into play, offering a range of solutions that help farmers maximize crop yield, reduce waste, and minimize costs. With over 186 years of experience in using technology for farming, John Deere has a proven track record of innovation in the industry. From the invention of the first self-scouring steel plow by the company's founder, John Deere, to the introduction of the fully autonomous tractors in 2022, John Deere has been a leader in the field of precision ag technology.


So, what makes John Deere's precision ag technology stand out? Let's take a closer look at some of the key differentiators.


**Save Inputs**


One of the biggest challenges faced by farmers is the efficient use of inputs such as fertilizers, pesticides, and water. Applying too much of these inputs can lead to wastage, while applying too little can result in reduced crop yield. John Deere's precision ag technology helps farmers optimize their use of inputs by providing real-time data on crop growth, soil moisture, and weather conditions. Farmers can use this information to adjust their inputs accordingly, ensuring that they apply just the right amount for maximum yield.


**Get More Done**


Another major challenge faced by farmers is the need to cover large areas of land in a short amount of time. Traditional farming techniques involve a lot of manual labor and can be very time-consuming. John Deere's precision ag technology helps farmers get more done in less time by providing advanced automation features. For example, the technology can automatically control the speed of the tractor, adjust the height of the cultivator, and even steer the vehicle along predetermined routes.


**More Operator Comfort**


Farming can be a physically demanding job, and operators often spend long hours in the field. John Deere's precision ag tech helps make the job more comfortable by providing a range of features that reduce operator fatigue. For example, the technology can automatically adjust the speed of the tractor based on the terrain, reducing the need for manual intervention and allowing farmers to focus more on work and less on driving.


**Never Stop**


Finally, John Deere's precision ag tech is designed to keep farmers productive even in difficult conditions. For example, the technology can operate in low visibility conditions, such as during a dust storm, using advanced sensors and cameras to navigate the field. It can also operate in adverse weather conditions, such as heavy rain or snow, allowing farmers to keep working even when traditional techniques would be impractical.


Overall, John Deere's precision ag tech is a game-changer for the farming industry. The technology aids farmers in maximizing yield, minimizing waste, and lowering costs by providing real-time data on crop growth, automating crucial tasks, and enhancing operator comfort. With a range of features designed to keep farmers productive even in adverse conditions, John Deere's precision ag technology is sure to become an essential tool for farmers around the world.
