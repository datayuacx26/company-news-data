## Key risks


As with any investment, there are risks involved when investing in CoinShares’ crypto ETPs and equity ETFs.


-


Volatility:


crypto prices are highly volatile, and this volatility flows through to the price of crypto ETPs and, indirectly, to the shares held by crypto-adjacent equity ETFs.


-


Structure:


CoinShares’ crypto ETPs are generally structured as debt securities, not equities. CoinShares Equity ETFs, by contrast, are structured as funds holding equities and do not directly hold or track the price of any cryptocurrency.


-


Company and market risk:


because equity ETFs invest in operating companies, their returns also depend on company specific factors and broader equity market conditions, and may diverge from the price of the underlying cryptocurrency.


-


No guarantees:


past performance is not indicative of future returns.


-


Risk of capital loss


: ETPs involve a risk of capital loss and the capital invested is not guaranteed.
