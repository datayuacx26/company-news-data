# By the way, what is double spending?


1 min read


Double Spending is not only CoinShares’ new animated series designed to educate audiences on the historical, fun, and sometimes tragic stories behind money as we know it: namely, fiat currencies. This term, which inspired our series, was popularised by Mr or Mrs. Satoshi Nakamoto in the Bitcoin whitepaper.


In this episode, we discuss why the Internet used to be a challenging place to exchange money flawlessly, and why Bitcoin solved this.


Illustrated by artist Alberto Miranda (The Guardian Weekly, The Economist, El País, Barron’s Magazine, Capital Magazine) and animated by CoinShares motion designer Marine Adenis, Double Spending offers insight into why Bitcoin may, after all, have a place in modern society.
