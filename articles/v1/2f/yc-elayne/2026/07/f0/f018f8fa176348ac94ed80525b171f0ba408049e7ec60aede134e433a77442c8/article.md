# How to Delete Cash App for Deceased Users: July 2026

A Cash App account belonging to someone who has died stays open until an executor or authorized family member formally requests closure. If the deceased had a balance, recurring payments, or a linked bank account, it remains active until the estate completes the closure process. If you're settling an estate that includes a Cash App account, this article covers many of the key points to be aware of.


**Key Takeaways:**


- Cash App accounts stay active after death; closing one requires formal action through Cash App's Estate Services process.
- Only an executor, administrator, or immediate family member with legal documentation can request account closure.
- It's helpful to have a certified death certificate, Letters Testamentary or Letters of Administration, and a government-issued photo ID before contacting Cash App.
- Elayne helps families identify which digital accounts need attention and work through the documentation required to close them.


## Why Cash App Ends Up in Estate Administration


Cash App accounts don't close automatically when someone dies. The account stays active, funds remain held, and any recurring payments or subscriptions tied to it continue running until someone formally notifies Cash App and requests closure.


For families settling an estate, this creates a concrete administrative responsibility. The account may hold a balance that belongs to the estate, and closing it is often required before the estate can be fully settled.


### Why This Comes Up So Often


Cash App has grown into a common way for people to send money, receive direct deposits, and hold a spending balance. Many users also hold Cash App Card accounts or invest through Cash App's stock and bitcoin features. When someone dies, any of these active features can leave funds in limbo until the estate steps in.

## Who Has the Legal Authority to Close the Account


Only[an executor](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/information-for-executors) , administrator, or immediate family member with proper documentation has the legal authority to close a deceased person's Cash App account.


Cash App does not allow informal requests. Whoever contacts support will need to show they have standing to act on behalf of the estate.


### Who Qualifies


- An executor named in a will carries court-recognized authority to manage and close financial accounts, including Cash App. A copy of the[Letters Testamentary](https://www.elayne.com/resources/what-are-letters-testamentary) issued by the probate court is the document that confirms this role.
- An administrator appointed by a court steps in when no valid will exists.[Letters of Administration](https://www.elayne.com/resources/what-is-letter-of-administration) serve the same function as Letters Testamentary and carry equal weight.
- An immediate family member may be able to request account closure in limited cases, though Cash App's process for non-executor family members is less defined and may require additional verification.


### What Documentation to Have Ready


- Government-issued photo ID for the person making the request
- A certified copy of the death certificate
- Letters Testamentary or Letters of Administration from the probate court
- Any documentation showing your relationship to the deceased, if acting as a family member instead of a named executor


## Cash App's Estate Services Process, Step by Step


Cash App handles deceased account requests through a dedicated[Estate Services process](https://cash.app/help/US/EN-US/6496-deceased-customer-estate) in its Help Center. Two things need to be verified: that the account holder has died, and that the person making the request has legal authority to act on behalf of the estate.


Once documentation is in hand, you can:


1. Contact Cash App through the official Deceased Customer Estate Services page in the Help Center.
2. Submit documentation as Cash App directs during intake. The process specifies how and where to send materials.
3. Wait for review. Verification typically takes several weeks once all required documents are submitted. The account stays open and funds remain held until that review is complete.

## What Happens to the Balance After Someone Dies


Any balance in a Cash App account at the time of death belongs to the estate. Cash App does not offer beneficiary designations or transfer-on-death features, so funds cannot pass directly to an heir outside the estate process. The balance moves only through estate settlement.


Before Cash App closes the account, the balance must be zero and all pending transactions must clear. Once the estate request is approved, Cash App transfers the funds to a designated estate account or the linked bank account on file. That transfer can take several business days. Also, it's important to keep the confirmation as part of the estate's financial records.

## Common Challenges


### Proof Requirements


Cash App typically requires documentation before releasing funds or closing an account. If those records aren't organized or readily available, the process slows considerably.


### Funds May Not Be Immediately Accessible


Even after Cash App verifies the account holder's death, any remaining balance may need to pass through the estate before it can be distributed. If the estate is in probate, that timeline can stretch from several months to over a year depending on the state and the estate's overall complexity.


### Linked Bank Accounts


Closing the Cash App account does not automatically close or unlink any connected bank accounts or debit cards. Those must be managed separately with the respective financial institutions.

## FAQ


### How do you close and delete a Cash App account after someone dies?


Accounts can be closed through Cash App's official Estate Services page in the Help Center, not through standard in-app chat. You'll need a certified death certificate, Letters Testamentary or Letters of Administration from the probate court, and a government-issued photo ID for the person submitting the request. Submitting all documents at once is key; incomplete submissions can extend the review timeline by weeks.


### What happens to the money in a Cash App account when the account holder dies?


The balance belongs to the estate and stays held until Cash App completes its review. Cash App has no beneficiary designation or transfer-on-death feature, so funds cannot pass directly to an heir; they move only through the estate settlement process. Once the account is approved for closure, Cash App transfers the remaining balance to a designated estate account or the linked bank account on file.


### Can a family member log into a deceased person's Cash App account to retrieve the funds?


No. Logging in with the deceased's credentials violates Cash App's terms of service and may constitute unauthorized access under federal law. The right path is Cash App's Estate Services process, regardless of whether the family has the login information. An executor or court-appointed administrator with proper documentation is the only party with legal standing to request account closure and fund transfer.


### What documents do I need to close a deceased person's Cash App account?


You'll need a certified copy of the death certificate issued by a state or county official records office, Letters Testamentary or Letters of Administration from a probate court, and a government-issued photo ID for whoever is submitting the request. Having the deceased's full legal name and the email or phone number linked to their Cash App account helps Cash App locate the correct account quickly. Some estates without formal probate may also need a small estate affidavit, which varies by state.


### Does Cash App delete the account automatically after someone dies?


No. The account stays active until the estate formally requests closure through Cash App support. Balances remain held and any recurring payments continue in the meantime.


### What if the deceased had a Cash App business account?


The closure process follows the same general steps. Additional documentation related to the business entity may be required depending on how the account was registered.


### What happens to transaction records after the account is closed?


Cash App retains transaction records for regulatory compliance after closure, but the estate loses in-app access to them. Families should download any needed transaction history before the account is formally closed.


### Can a Cash App account be transferred to a beneficiary instead of closed?


No. Cash App does not support account transfers. The account must be closed and any remaining balance directed to the estate through the settlement process.
