# 4 Best Estate Admin Software Tools for Attorneys (August 2026)

The estate administration space includes a range of well-built tools, each designed to serve a specific audience well. Some tools are built for personal representatives who want to manage the settlement process independently, with step-by-step guidance and self-service tracking built around their workflow. Others are built for families navigating loss, offering emotional support, educational resources, and general guidance through a difficult time. Another category is built for financial advisors and wealth managers, and is designed to help with tasks such as estate planning visualization and analysis. For attorneys managing estate matters, some of the key priorities often include: credentialed asset discovery, an organized estate record, fiduciary deadline tracking, and a workflow layer that's easy to use for both legal teams and their clients. In this article, we'll focus on four services, and how they support both families and attorneys.

## Alix


Alix estate is built around a dedicated concierge care team of settlement specialists and licensed probate attorneys. Families receive hands-on coordination across probate, debt negotiation, and asset recovery, with a care team guiding them through each stage. For attorneys whose clients want a fully delegated, human-centered settlement experience, Alix brings a credentialed team to support that work.


Key points:


- Full-service settlement covering probate coordination, debt negotiation, and asset recovery, delivered through a dedicated concierge care team of settlement specialists who guide families through each stage of the process from estate opening through final distribution.
- Licensed probate attorneys are part of the concierge model, giving families access to credentialed legal support and professional oversight across probate filings, creditor coordination, and asset recovery.
- A mobile app connects family members and the care team throughout the settlement process, supporting document uploads, real-time progress tracking, and communication in one place.
- Asset recovery support draws on state missing-money databases to help locate accounts and funds on behalf of the family.


## Empathy


Empathy is a grief-support and estate guidance service distributed through life insurance carriers and employer benefit programs. Families receive access through their insurer or employer. For attorneys whose clients are covered through one of these programs, Empathy provides a structured source of emotional and educational support.


Key points:


- Care Managers provide emotional support and guidance on probate questions, giving families a dedicated human point of contact.
- Educational articles, guides, and checklists through a consumer app give families clear orientation on the settlement process and help them understand what comes next.
- Assistance identifying survivor benefits and life insurance claims supports families in locating and accessing eligible benefits tied to the decedent's accounts and policies.
- Care Managers assist with canceling consumer accounts, helping families manage recurring charges and account closures through a supported, guided process.


## Sunset


Sunset is a financial asset discovery service built for families working through estate settlement. The service is free for families and is funded by participating bank partner relationships rather than charged to the estate. Sunset focuses on locating unknown or forgotten financial accounts. For attorneys whose clients need dedicated financial account discovery support, Sunset provides an excellent resource at no cost to the family.


Key points:


- Financial account discovery focused on locating unknown or forgotten accounts, with coverage designed to help families surface assets that may have been overlooked.
- SOC 2 Type II certified, with annual independent audits, third-party penetration testing, and encrypted API connections keeping all uploaded documents and personal information protected throughout the process.
- Free for families through participating bank partner relationships, with no charge to the estate or the personal representative.
- Probate document generation across all 50 states, giving families access to state-specific foundational paperwork as part of the settlement process.
