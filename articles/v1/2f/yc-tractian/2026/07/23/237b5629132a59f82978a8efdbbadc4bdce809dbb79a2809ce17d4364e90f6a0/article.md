# Tractian Earns Spot on Forbes AI 50: A Testament to Innovation and Excellence in Industrial AI

We're thrilled to share some exciting news: **Tractian has been recognized in the prestigious Forbes AI 50 list for 2024** , which highlights the most promising privately-held artificial intelligence companies. This accolade cements our position as a leader in the industrial AI space, underscoring our commitment to revolutionizing maintenance with cutting-edge technology.


## Recognition on a Global Stage


Forbes’ AI 50 list is a highly coveted recognition, designed to spotlight companies at the forefront of AI innovation. This year, the list was more competitive than ever, with nearly 1,900 submissions-more than double the previous year’s count.


Tractian’s inclusion is a reflection of our relentless pursuit of excellence in deploying AI solutions that are not just groundbreaking, but also deeply practical for the industrial sector.


Our journey began in 2019, inspired by real-world challenges faced by maintenance technicians, and it’s this grounded approach that has fueled our rapid growth.


In just a few years, we've expanded to serve over 500 clients, including industry giants like John Deere, Procter & Gamble, and Caterpillar.


Our AI-driven technology redefines industrial maintenance by offering an all-encompassing solution that includes intelligent sensors for real-time equipment condition monitoring, energy meters paired with an intuitive **EMS (Energy Management System)** platform, and comprehensive **CMMS/EAM** to streamline maintenance operations.


These tools work together seamlessly to predict issues, optimize energy use, and ensure that maintenance teams have the actionable insights they need, anytime and anywhere.


## Driving Innovation in Industrial Maintenance


At Tractian, our mission is clear: **to empower maintenance teams with the tools they need to prevent failures before they occur** .


Our technology suite exemplifies our mission, integrating proprietary sensors, intelligent algorithms, and real-time monitoring systems to safeguard industrial equipment and optimize performance.


This not only reduces downtime but also drives significant cost savings for our clients by preventing costly failures.


The recognition from Forbes comes at a pivotal time in our journey. The AI sector is more competitive than ever, with companies across the globe racing to integrate AI into their operations.


Our inclusion in the AI 50 list is not just a badge of honor but a validation of the impact our solutions are making in the industrial landscape.
