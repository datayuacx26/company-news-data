# What’s new in OpenText Content Aviator

OpenText™ Content Aviator puts an[AI content assistant](https://www.opentext.com/aviator-ai/content-aviator) into the hands of business users: one that can act on that content, not just search and summarize content in place. Built on a fully governed, secure foundation, Content Aviator brings secure information management for AI to every user and process. It is available across all[OpenText Content Cloud](https://www.opentext.com/products/content-cloud) platforms for content management and archiving.


## July 2026: What’s new in OpenText Content Aviator 26.2


The[26.2 release](https://blogs.opentext.com/modernize-your-content-foundation-for-the-era-of-agentic-ai/) of Content Aviator brings a major new set of capabilities and a strong governance foundation for[agentic AI](https://www.opentext.com/what-is/agentic-ai) . Safety and AI governance are critical to the success of engaging agentic AI in your overall strategies. The combination of agents, new skills, and process-centric knowledge graphs gives your teams what they need to decide, act, and transact safely on your content.


Content Aviator 26.2 puts our vision into practice: governed content now connects to more of the AI systems your teams already use. It goes beyond search and summary to take intelligent action on your content. It becomes the secure, agentic foundation for accessing your information, either directly or through integrations with Microsoft Copilot, Salesforce Agentforce, and Google Gemini Enterprise, providing governance and trust your users can depend on.


Here’s what’s new in 26.2.


### Agentic AI opens up a new level of AI content management


Content Aviator expands well beyond search, summary and draft to act on your content. With[OpenText Content Management](https://www.opentext.com/products/content-management) 26.2, here are just a few things you can achieve now with Content Aviator and agentic AI:


**Ground your work on the right content through the knowledge graph:** Content Aviator orients your work to what you’re working on intelligently, giving a 360-degree view of the most up-to-date information available at any time.


*Through the knowledge graph, OpenText Content Aviator understands relationships across a network of connected workspaces*


**Put agentic AI to work within your processes with business workspace management:** Create new workspaces, update metadata, and create relationships between workspaces.


**Manage documents and records with ease:** Rename, move, copy and change permissions on documents.


**Records management:** Classify documents, set legal holds, and apply markings or security clearances.


**User agent:** Look up users, favorite items, and review recently accessed documents.


**Generate templated, finished documents, not just drafts:** The document generation agent lets users define templates for common business documents and generate a completed document from a single natural-language request; no manual searching or setup required.


*New document generation agent powered by OpenText Content Aviator*


**Kickoff new workflows:** Define prescribed workflows and initiate them from Content Aviator.


The throughline across all of it: less time hunting for information, more time acting on it.


### Integrate Content Aviator with leading AI systems for the ultimate context and control


Integrating with the AI systems your organization uses gives users the flexibility to stay “in the zone” and get work done efficiently, while benefiting from safe access to your organization’s critical content when and where they need it. Earlier, we introduced AI integration with[Microsoft Copilot](https://www.opentext.com/products/content-cloud-integrations-for-microsoft-365) and[Salesforce Agentforce](https://blogs.opentext.com/content-in-context-is-the-foundation-your-ai-actually-needs/) .


Now, with **Google Gemini Enterprise** (available with[OpenText Core Content Management](https://www.opentext.com/products/core-content-management) 26.2), integration gives teams using Gemini Enterprise and Google Workspace direct access to Content Aviator, ensuring proper security permissions and important AI governance controls are applied during AI use on their critical content. Users tap into Content Aviator directly from the Gemini chat interface, keeping content safe beyond policy-driven permissions enforced on every response.


*Access OpenText Content Aviator directly inside the Gemini chat interface*


**Microsoft Copilot:** Inside[Microsoft Copilot](https://www.opentext.com/products/content-cloud-integrations-for-microsoft-365) , users can now see inline citations pointing directly to source documents in[OpenText Document Management platforms,](https://www.opentext.com/products/document-management) a fast way to validate information and locate the business workspace it came from, without ever leaving Copilot.


**Salesforce Agentforce:** And in[OpenText Core Content Management for Salesforce](https://www.opentext.com/products/content-cloud-integrations-for-salesforce) 26.2, users can now open business workspaces directly inside Salesforce Agentforce using natural language. Content Aviator identifies and opens the correct workspace without having to navigate away from their train of thought.


*AI content assistance in the flow of work without switching apps*


### OpenText Documentum Content Management 26.2 brings new flexibility for Content Aviator users


Several key improvements are coming to Content Aviator users with OpenText Documentum Content Management:


**AI-driven natural language search:** Content Aviator now drives a natural language search, making complex search simple and combining keyword precision with semantic understanding.


**Mobile app support:**[OpenText Documentum Content Management](https://www.opentext.com/products/documentum-content-management) 26.2 now includes Content Aviator within the mobile app for AI content assistance on the go.


### Expanded deployment flexibility for deploying OpenText Content Aviator with OpenText Information Archive


[OpenText Information Archive](https://www.opentext.com/products/information-archive) 26.2 extends OpenText Content Aviator’s reach with support for more hyperscalers, models and hybrid deployment scenarios, built for organizations with complex environments.


## January 2026: What’s new in OpenText Content Aviator 26.1


### OpenText Content Aviator is now available from Microsoft Copilot


OpenText Content Aviator is now available as a Microsoft Copilot agent, bringing trusted, AI powered content intelligence directly into Microsoft 365. By enabling the OpenText Content Aviator agent from the Microsoft Agent Store, users can select it as their Copilot agent source and converse with OpenText Content Aviator using natural language to search for information, uncover key facts, and generate insights directly from content stored in OpenText Core Content Management, OpenText Content Management, and OpenText Documentum Content Management.


This AI-to-AI integration allows users working in Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint to access governed, context-rich enterprise content without leaving the app. The result is faster research, more informed drafting, and greater productivity—while maintaining the security, permissions, and compliance controls enterprises expect from OpenText content management. Learn more about the OpenText Content Aviator agent in the[Microsoft Agent Store](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/marketplace-offers/ai-agent-overview) .


OpenText Content Aviator is accessible from Microsoft Copilot.


### Upgrade to GPT 5 model on Microsoft Azure


OpenText Content Aviator now gives customers access to OpenAI GPT 5 model when deployed on Microsoft Azure.


### Off-cloud deployments now support the hyperscaler-hosted model


With 26.1, customers connect OpenText Content Aviator 26.1 to a hyperscaler-hosted model, giving customers complete control over their environment, end to end, but supporting the efficiency and cost management of a hyperscaler-hosted model.


## November 2025: What’s new in OpenText Content Aviator CE 25.4


### Deploy OpenText Content Aviator off-cloud in your own data center with Meta LLM


OpenText Content Aviator CE 25.4 introduces a new off-cloud deployment option, giving organizations the ability to use GenAI-powered content management while meeting strict data residency and compliance requirements. Off-cloud customers of OpenText Content Management and OpenText Documentum Content Management can now deploy OpenText Content Aviator alongside OpenText Aviator Model Services using a fully packaged, production-ready installer. This deployment approach provides everything IT teams need to run GenAI safely, predictably, and securely in highly regulated environments.


### OpenText Content Aviator now available on Microsoft Azure with OpenAI LLM


OpenText Content Aviator CE 25.4 can also be deployed natively on Microsoft Azure using the OpenAI frontier model. By purchasing through the[Microsoft Azure Marketplace](https://marketplace.microsoft.com/en-us/product/saas/opentextglobal.opentext_aviator?tab=Overview) , customers gain expanded options for technical integration, compliance alignment, and data governance while being supported by a single vendor relationship and a unified SLA. This arrangement also helps organizations maximize their existing Azure commitments with AI-powered content management.


### Bring key metadata into the conversation


This release further improves answer quality through metadata enrichment. OpenText Content Aviator can now retrieve and use metadata from connected business applications to enhance contextual understanding and support queries where key information resides in metadata fields.


### Direct LLM Access API enables custom applications


App developers gain access to a new Direct LLM Access API, enabling them to add basic LLM-driven capabilities directly into their applications. This API reduces reliance and uncertainty around using Chat API for simple, repeatable tasks such as generating document summaries.


## August 2025: What’s new in OpenText Content Aviator CE 25.3


### OpenText Content Aviator: smarter answers, richer visuals


OpenText Content Aviator continues to evolve as the embedded AI content assistant inside OpenText Content Management:


- **See your data differently.** Aviator can now generate charts such as bar, line, and pie directly in the chat panel, turning complex information into quick, clear visuals without leaving the platform.


- **Better results with tables.** Improved table handling provides more context, producing more accurate answers when your content is structured in rows and columns.


- **Choose your AI engine.** In addition to GCP and AWS, customers can now deploy Aviator on Azure OpenAI, expanding compliance and regional hosting options.


Together, these advances reduce the friction of finding, interpreting, and presenting information, helping knowledge workers make better decisions in less time.
