## **It’s all about you**


We’ve carefully curated OpenText World 2026 to cater to the needs of our attendees. Whether you’re an executive looking to understand the impact new technology can have on your organization, a decision maker tasked with choosing the right products to suit specific needs, or a technical user who wants to learn about the latest upgrades, OpenText World 2026 is the place for you.


One of today’s most pressing business questions is *What do I do with my data* ? OpenText World 2026 is where that question gets answered. From new product announcements to live capability demos and real use cases, this is your chance to see what the future of your organization could look like when powered by enterprise-grade AI built on secure, contextual data you can trust.


We’ll help you answer other important questions: How do I move to the cloud? What kind of cloud is right for me? Which AI model should I use? Our solutions create a single, trusted data foundation that is flexible enough to suit your specific needs, priorities, and goals.

- Keynote speeches from OpenText leaders, including our new CEO Ayman Antoun, offering insights into the latest innovations and putting them in real-world context.
