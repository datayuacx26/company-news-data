**Common Challenges of Traditional SDR Coaching**


Coaching SDRs traditionally involves reviewing recorded calls, one-on-one feedback sessions, and role-playing scenarios to improve sales techniques.


While these methods are effective, they often fall short in today's fast-paced, remote work environment. Here's why:


1. **Limited Time for Managers** : Sales managers are busy, and it’s not always feasible to provide frequent, one-on-one feedback to every SDR on the team. When they do provide feedback, it’s often in a structured setting like weekly meetings or quarterly reviews, which doesn’t allow for the kind of real-time correction that can drastically improve performance.
2. **Delayed Feedback** : Traditional feedback tends to be delayed, coming after the call or interaction has already taken place. SDRs might struggle to remember the context of the call, making it harder to apply the feedback effectively.
3. **Overlooked Areas for Improvement** : No matter how skilled the manager is, human error and time constraints mean that certain areas for improvement might be overlooked. This is especially true when managers are juggling multiple tasks and priorities, leaving SDRs with unaddressed gaps in their skills.


These challenges make it difficult to **coach SDRs** effectively, creating a gap in the learning and development process. Real-time AI sales coaching aims to bridge this gap by providing instant, personalized feedback during sales activities.
