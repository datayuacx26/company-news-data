Elric Legloire, the Global SDR Manager at Agorapulse, employs a bold strategy that gives power back to the prospect.


**Opener:** “Hi Sally, it’s Elric from Agorapulse; this is a cold call. You want to hang up now or give me 30 seconds to explain why I’ve called?”


**Why it works:** This disarmingly honest approach flips the script, acknowledging the nature of the call and putting the decision in the prospect’s hands. More often than not, curiosity wins out.

Nick Cegelski, founder of “30 Minutes to President’s Club,” uses recent events or news related to the prospect to create relevance.


**Opener:** “Emma, I just finished reading your press release about the new product launch. My name is Nick, and I know you didn’t expect me to call you this afternoon. Mind if I steal a minute to tell you why that press release prompted me to call you, and then you can tell me whether or not it’s worth a chat?”


**Why it works:** This personalized approach shows that your call is based on genuine interest and recent developments, making the prospect feel valued and understood.


#### 4. Ask if They’ve Seen Your Email


Will Falkenborg from Champify is back with another strategy that blends email and phone outreach to break the ice.


**Opener:** “Hey, This is Will from Champify; you may have seen my email earlier; you got a minute? I’ll be brief.”


**Why it works:** There’s a pre-existing connection, even if small. The prospect may have seen your name in their inbox, which makes them more likely to engage.


#### 5. Add Humor Where You Can


People buy from people they like. Will Falkenborg of Champify uses humor to create a relaxed and open atmosphere.


**Opener:** “Hey, this is Will from Champify. Can you hear me, okay? Might be on a little bit of a bad line, or there might be a gorilla in the room.”


**Why it works:** It catches people off guard in a pleasant way, making them more open to what comes next.


#### 6. Ask for Permission to Speak for 30 Seconds


Benjy Aryeh, Senior Business Development Representative Team Lead at Similarweb, uses a respectful approach that has boosted his success rate.


**Opener:** “Do you have 30 seconds to share why I’m calling, and you can tell me if it makes sense to continue?”


**Why it works:** This permission-based opener shows respect for the prospect’s time and conveys confidence in the value of what you have to say.


#### 7. Say You’ve Not Spoken Before


Honesty can be refreshing. Shabri Lakhani from Trellus shares a cold calling script that has significantly increased engagement.


**Opener:** “Hi, this is Shabri calling from Trellus. We haven’t spoken before…”

#### 8. Ask the Prospect How They Are

**Opener:** “Hey \[name\], Nate from Productside - how are you doing today?”

#### 9. Be Upfront That It Is a Cold Call

**Why it works:** This candid approach disarms the prospect and piques their curiosity, setting the stage for an open conversation.


#### 10. Congratulate the Prospect on a Recent Win


Art Sobczak, President of Business By Phone Inc., recommends leading with a prospect’s recent achievement.


**Opener:** “Hi \[name\]. Congratulations on the \[achievement\] I had read about in \[media\].”

## How to open a Cold Call?


Opening a cold call effectively is key to capturing the prospect's attention and setting the tone for a productive conversation. Here’s a step-by-step guide on how to open a cold call:


### 1. **Start with a Strong Introduction**


- **Introduce Yourself** : State your name and the company you represent.
- **Be Clear and Concise** : Keep the introduction brief to respect the prospect's time.


**Example** : "Hi \[Prospect's Name\], this is \[Your Name\] from \[Company Name\]. How are you today?"


### 2. **Acknowledge the Prospect’s Time**


- **Respect Their Time** : Ask if it's a good time to talk. This shows consideration and sets a professional tone.


**Example** : "Is now a good time for a quick conversation?"


### 3. **Personalize the Conversation**


- **Do Your Research** : Mention something specific about the prospect or their business to show you’ve done your homework.
- **Establish Relevance** : Tailor your approach based on the prospect’s industry, role, or challenges.


**Example** : "I saw that your company has been expanding its digital marketing efforts. We’ve helped similar businesses improve their lead generation by 30%."


### 4. **Introduce the Value Proposition**

### 3. **Focus on Building Rapport**


- **Be Friendly and Approachable** : Build a connection early on by being polite, empathetic, and conversational. People are more likely to listen to someone they feel comfortable with.

### **Top Cold Call Qualifying Questions to Increase Your Conversion Rates**


Cold calling can be a challenging yet rewarding sales strategy when executed correctly.


One of the most important aspects of a successful cold call is knowing which qualifying questions to ask. These questions help you determine whether the prospect is a good fit for your product or service, saving both your time and theirs.


By asking the right questions, you can increase your chances of turning a cold lead into a warm prospect, ultimately boosting your[conversion rates](https://www.trellus.ai/post/understanding-cold-calling-campaigns-average-conversion-rates-and-strategies-for-success) .


#### **Why Qualifying Questions Matter**


Qualifying questions help you identify prospects who are more likely to buy, allowing you to focus your efforts on high-value leads. They also help build rapport and trust, as you’re showing genuine interest in solving the prospect's pain points. The key is to ask questions that provide valuable insights into their needs, budget, decision-making process, and timeline.


#### **Top Qualifying Questions to Ask During a Cold Call**


1. **"What prompted you to take my call today?"**


- **Why it works** : This open-ended question allows the prospect to share their reasons for engaging, giving you insight into their immediate needs or challenges. It also helps you gauge their level of interest.


2. **"Can you tell me about your current \[product/service\] and how it's working for you?"**


- **Why it works** : This question helps you understand the prospect's current situation and whether there’s an opportunity to offer a better solution. It also opens the door to discussing pain points and areas for improvement.


3. **"What challenges are you currently facing in \[relevant area\]?"**


- **Why it works** : By identifying their pain points, you can position your product or service as the solution. It also allows you to understand the urgency of their needs.


4. **"Who is involved in the decision-making process for \[product/service\]?"**


- **Why it works** : Understanding who is involved in the decision-making process helps you identify key stakeholders and avoid wasting time with prospects who don’t have the authority to make purchasing decisions.


5. **"What is your budget for \[product/service\]?"**


- **Why it works** : Knowing the prospect’s budget helps you determine if they can afford your solution. It also allows you to tailor your pitch to their financial constraints, ensuring you offer a solution within their price range.


6. **"What is your timeline for implementing a solution?"**


- **Why it works** : Understanding the prospect’s timeline helps you gauge their level of urgency and whether they’re a good fit for your sales cycle. It also allows you to prioritize leads based on when they’re ready to make a decision.


7. **"How do you typically evaluate \[products/services\] like ours?"**


- **Why it works** : This question helps you understand the prospect’s buying process and what factors are most important to them. It allows you to tailor your pitch to meet their specific evaluation criteria.


8. **"Have you used a similar solution in the past?"**


- **Why it works** : If the prospect has experience with a similar solution, you can address their previous pain points and explain how your product or service improves upon what they’ve used before.


9. **"What would make this solution a success for you?"**


- **Why it works** : This question helps you understand the prospect’s expectations and what success looks like for them. It allows you to position your offering as the best solution to meet their goals.


10. **"What’s your biggest priority right now in \[relevant area\]?"**


- **Why it works** : By aligning your solution with their top priority, you demonstrate that you understand their needs and are offering a relevant solution that directly impacts their business goals.


#### **How to Use These Questions Effectively**


- **Be Natural** : Don’t rush through your questions. Let the conversation flow naturally, and listen carefully to the answers. This will help you build rapport and trust with the prospect.
- **Follow Up** : Use the answers to these questions to guide the rest of your conversation. Ask follow-up questions based on what the prospect shares, and adjust your pitch accordingly.
- **Be Respectful** : Always be mindful of the prospect’s time. If they’re not interested or not ready to buy, be polite and thank them for their time. You can always follow up later if circumstances change.


#### **Conclusion**


Asking the right cold call qualifying questions is a crucial step in increasing your conversion rates.


These questions help you qualify leads, uncover pain points, and position your product or service as the solution to the prospect’s needs.
