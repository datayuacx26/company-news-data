# **What Is Sales Rep Software?**


A sales rep software is part sales management tool, part sales enablement program, and a workflow optimizer for SDRs, BDRs, and whatnot.


Think of it as the operating system for your entire sales organization. But it has to be pretty darn good at that, in terms of features, deliverability, integrations, technical perks, etc. It’s not every day that companies can change a sales rep software, just because its use is tied to bottlenecks.


The decision comes with a price that’s not only tied to the monetary aspect but also to the time it’ll take to onboard clusters of sales teams, training and ramp time reduction. From that point of view, we can say that modern platforms have evolved far beyond traditional means of operation.


You’re looking at advanced features, better CRM for sales representatives integrations, AI coaching, interactive reporting dashboards that can help you pick a needle from a haystack, and of course, tons of automations to make things more efficient.


Similarly, for field sales teams, many solutions also include **field sales management software** capabilities like customer visit scheduling, GPS navigation, territory management, digital order capture, and offline access through a **mobile sales app** .


That pretty much sums up a sales rep software.
