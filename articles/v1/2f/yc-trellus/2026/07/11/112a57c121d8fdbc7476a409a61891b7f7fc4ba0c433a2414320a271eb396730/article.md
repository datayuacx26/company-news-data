## Frequently Asked Questions


### 1. What is a Parallel Dialer?


A parallel dialer is a technology that enables sales reps to dial multiple phone numbers simultaneously. Rather than waiting for each call to connect or ring out before dialing the next prospect, the dialer manages multiple calls in progress at once. When a live person answers, the call is routed to an available rep. Voicemails and disconnects are handled automatically. This approach significantly increases the number of meaningful conversations reps can have per day, often by 3-5x compared to traditional one-at-a-time dialing. Parallel dialers are particularly effective in high-volume sales environments where maximizing conversations per hour is critical to hitting pipeline targets.


### 2. How Does Trellus's Parallel Dialer Differ from Salesfinity's?


Both platforms offer parallel dialing, but with different approaches and capabilities. Trellus's parallel dialer is designed to integrate directly into your existing sales engagement platform (Salesloft, Outreach, Apollo, etc.), providing a seamless experience within your current workflow. This integration-first approach means reps don't need to switch between tools. Salesfinity's parallel dialer is a more standalone feature that can dial up to 5 numbers simultaneously and includes advanced contact enrichment and voicemail detection. Trellus emphasizes integration and coaching, while Salesfinity emphasizes dialing efficiency and data quality. For teams already invested in a specific sales engagement platform, Trellus's approach may feel more natural. For teams looking for a comprehensive dialing solution, Salesfinity's feature set may be more compelling.

### 5. How Does AI Objection Analysis Enhance Sales Calls?


AI objection analysis works by listening to prospect statements during calls and identifying common objection patterns. The AI then surfaces relevant counterarguments, case studies, or talking points that have proven effective in similar situations. This real-time guidance helps reps respond to objections more effectively without needing to think on their feet. Over time, the AI learns which responses work best for your specific industry and product, making suggestions increasingly accurate and relevant. This capability significantly improves close rates and reduces the number of calls lost to unhandled objections. For new reps, AI objection analysis provides a safety net that builds confidence and accelerates learning. For experienced reps, it provides data-driven insights that improve their already-strong skills.
