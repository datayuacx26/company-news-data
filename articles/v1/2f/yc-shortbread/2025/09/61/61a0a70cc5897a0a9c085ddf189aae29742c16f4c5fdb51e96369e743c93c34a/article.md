## Why this book hits so hard


H.D. Carlton's "Haunting Adeline" absolutely consumed me when I first picked it up, and let me tell you, I was NOT prepared for the emotional roller coaster that is Zade Meadows. If you've been on BookTok at all in the past couple years, you know exactly what I'm talking about. The obsession, the psychological cat-and-mouse games, the morally gray (okay, let's be honest—morally black) anti-hero who sometimes makes you root for him anyway.


The way Carlton crafts that delicious tension between Adeline and her stalker had me questioning my own sanity, and I mean that in the best possible way. The gothic atmosphere of the Parsons Manor, the found diary entries from Adeline's great-grandmother, and that absolutely unhinged devotion that borders on worship? Chef's kiss. Pure dark romance perfection.


If you're like me and found yourself completely addicted to the twisted dynamic between predator and prey (with some seriously questionable consent along the way), then here are 10 romance books like Haunting Adeline you'll devour. Between my deep dive into dark romance corners of the internet and my ever-growing collection of morally questionable book boyfriends, I've got the perfect recommendations—10 best dark romance stories to feed your newfound obsession.
