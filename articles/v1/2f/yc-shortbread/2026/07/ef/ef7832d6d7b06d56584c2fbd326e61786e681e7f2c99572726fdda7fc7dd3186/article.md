## Story Summary


Twisted Love is a steamy grumpy x sunshine, brother's best friend romance between Alex Volkov, a cold, brilliant businessman scarred by tragedy, and Ava Chen, a sweet, artistic college student haunted by childhood trauma. When Ava's brother asks Alex to look after her while he's abroad, their forced proximity sparks a passionate, forbidden romance filled with steam, angst, jealousy, and healing. As their love deepens, buried secrets resurface, putting their relationship—and their lives—at risk.

## Related Books: The Twisted Series & Similar Reads


If you're obsessed with Twisted Love – whether it's the billionaire alpha hero, the sunshine heroine, or the mix of angst and spice – there are plenty of books to keep the vibes going. First, let's look at Ana Huang's own series, and then some other BookTok-favorite romances with similar energy:


#### Twisted Games (Book 2 of the Twisted Series, 2021)


The sequel follows Princess Bridget von Ascheberg and stoic bodyguard Rhys Larsen in a sizzling forbidden romance full of palace intrigue. It's heavier on angst and slow burn, but just as spicy.


#### Twisted Hate (Book 3, 2022)


A fiery enemies-to-lovers story between Jules Ambrose (Ava's sharp-tongued friend) and her longtime nemesis Josh Chen. Expect banter, hate sex, and one of Ana Huang's most beloved couples.


#### Twisted Lies (Book 4, 2022)


Stella Alonso's story with Christian Harper, a morally gray billionaire who pretends to be her boyfriend in a fake-dating setup. The spice is high, the tension delicious.


### Other BookTok-Recommended Reads:


#### King of Wrath by Ana Huang


The first in her Kings of Sin series, featuring ruthless billionaire Dante Russo and heiress Vivian Lau in an arranged marriage romance. It's angsty, glamorous, and steamy – perfect for fans of Alex Volkov.


#### Love, Theoretically by Ali Hazelwood


While lighter in tone, Hazelwood's trademark grumpy x sunshine dynamic mirrors Ava and Alex's vibe, with academia as the backdrop.


#### Magnolia Parks by Jessa Hastings


A toxic, angsty, high-drama romance that BookTok readers often pair with Ana Huang's books. If you liked the push-pull of Alex and Ava, this delivers emotional chaos.


#### The Fine Print by Lauren Asher


Part of the Dreamland Billionaires series, this book follows a broody billionaire and a sunshine heroine in a theme park empire setting. The banter, spice, and grumpy x sunshine pairing make it a natural fit for fans.


#### Terms and Conditions by Lauren Asher


The second Dreamland book, featuring marriage-of-convenience with a grumpy CEO – another must-read if you loved Alex Volkov's icy exterior hiding deep passion.


From Ana Huang's own interconnected universe to billionaire bad boys and BookTok's most dramatic romances, these reads will keep you swimming in angst, spice, and swoony heroes long after you close Twisted Love.
