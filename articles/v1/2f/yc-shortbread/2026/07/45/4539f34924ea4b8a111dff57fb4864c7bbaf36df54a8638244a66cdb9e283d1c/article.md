At the end of the book, Violet and Xaden share one last night together before everything changes.

## Goodreads Reader Reactions


Readers loved Fourth Wing for its balance of danger, fantasy, and slow-burn romance. It became an instant BookTok favorite for its mix of dragons, training, and emotional depth.

Many readers praised Violet as a unique fantasy heroine — smart, stubborn, and refreshingly real. Xaden's morally gray character and the tension between them were major highlights.
