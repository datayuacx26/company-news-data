## **1. The Fine Print by Lauren Asher**


If you loved the grumpy-sunshine and billionaire hero vibes in the Twisted books, this one delivers. Expect a rich, cocky hero, a woman who won’t roll over, and all the tension of “should we even be together?” It hits that mark for BookTok readers looking for that same kind of rush.
