# **10 BookTok-Obsessed Dark Romance Books You *Need* to Read**


If there’s one place that thrives on unhinged anti-heroes, toxic tension, and romances that drag you straight into emotional chaos, it’s **BookTok** . Dark romance has taken over the platform — with readers recommending their favorite twisted love stories at lightning speed.


Whether you’re new to the genre or already deep in its morally-grey madness, this list highlights **ten BookTok-beloved dark romances** pulled from the most popular titles on Goodreads.


These books are intense, addictive, taboo, and absolutely unforgettable. Check trigger warnings first — BookTok always will — and prepare to fall into the kind of messy, chaotic love stories that readers can’t stop screaming about.


## **1. The Ritual (L.O.R.D.S., #1) – Shantel Tessier**


One of the **most viral BookTok dark romances** , *The Ritual* pulls you into a world of dangerous secret societies, deadly rituals, and an anti-hero who practically defines “obsessed.” BookTok readers *devour* this book for its power dynamics and ritualistic tension — and then immediately beg for more Tessier.


## **2. God of Malice (Legacy of Gods, #1) – Rina Kent**


Rina Kent is practically **BookTok royalty** , and *God of Malice* is one of the books that cemented it. Expect a morally corrupt hero, psychological intensity, and obsessive devotion that BookTok loves to spiral over in aesthetic edits and long, chaotic rants.


## **3. The Sweetest Oblivion (Made, #1) – Danielle Lori**


BookTok has crowned this one of its **favorite mafia romances** , and it’s easy to see why. Elena and Nico’s deliciously tense dynamic, forbidden chemistry, and mafia danger make it a staple on “Books That Ruined My Life” TikToks everywhere.
