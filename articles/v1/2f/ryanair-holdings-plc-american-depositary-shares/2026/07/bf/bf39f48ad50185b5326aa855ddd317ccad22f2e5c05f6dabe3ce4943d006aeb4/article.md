## RYANAIR LAUNCHES RECORD WINTER 2026 SCHEDULE WITH 80M SEATS ACROSS EUROPE


Ryanair, Europe’s No.1 airline, today (16 Jul) launched a record schedule for Winter 2026, with 1,700 routes across 35 countries, including over 140 new routes. Ryanair’s record Winter 2026 schedule offers customers even more choice for their Winter 2026 getaways, including top winter sun destinations, ski trips, Christmas markets, or city escapes, at the lowest fares in Europe.


Ryanair’s full Winter 2026 schedule is available to book now via the Ryanair App / website.
