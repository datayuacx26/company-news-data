## RYANAIR ADDS EXTRA FLIGHTS FOR PREMIER LEAGUE MATCHES IN LIVERPOOL & MANCHESTER


Ryanair, Europe’s No.1 airline, today (30 Jul) added extra flights (3,000 extra seats) between Dublin and Liverpool/Manchester ahead of the highly anticipated Premier League taking place in Anfield in Liverpool (29 Aug & 12 Sept) & in Old Trafford in Manchester (30 Aug & 13 Sept). Ryanair’s Premier League extras are available to book via the Ryanair app now.


**Ryanair’s Director of Comms, Jade Kirwan said:**


*“Ryanair is pleased to offer extra flights (3,000 extra seats) to/from Liverpool and Manchester for the Premier League matches in Aug & Sept to give fans in Ireland the opportunity to experience the incredible match atmosphere in person. We expect these extra Ryanair flights to sell out fast, so don’t miss out, head over to the Ryanair app now to book your flights before they’re gone.”*
