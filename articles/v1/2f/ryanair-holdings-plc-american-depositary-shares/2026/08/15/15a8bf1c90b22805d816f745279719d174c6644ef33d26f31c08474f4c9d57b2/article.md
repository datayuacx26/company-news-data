## RYANAIR LAUNCHES RECORD MOROCCO WINTER SCHEDULE


**2 NEW AIRCRAFT ($200M INVESTMENT), 17 NEW ROUTES, &** **5.3M LOW FARE SEATS**


Ryanair, Europe’s No.1 airline, today (Thurs, 6 Aug) announced its biggest ever Winter schedule in Morocco, offering 5.3m low-fares seats to/from 156 destinations, including 17 exciting new routes. These include new connections from Rabat to major European cities such as Stockholm and Krakow, from Marrakesh to Wroclaw, and from Agadir to Milan, Nuremberg, Bratislava and Gdansk.


Ryanair’s record Winter 2026 schedule will connect Morocco with 14 countries, giving millions of European visitors the opportunity to discover the Kingdom’s imperial cities, Atlantic and Mediterranean coastlines, Atlas Mountains and desert landscapes while enjoying year-round sunshine. This record schedule is underpinned by the opening of Ryanair’s new Rabat base – its 5th in Morocco – and flights across 13 Moroccan airports, delivering enhanced connectivity, inbound tourism and economic growth to communities across the Kingdom, including Fez, Tetouan, Essaouira, Ouarzazate, Dakhla and Nador.


**Ryanair’s Record Morocco Winter 2026 Schedule will deliver:**


- 5.3m low fare seats – providing an additional +580k (+12%) vs. last Winter.
- 156 routes – 17 new routes incl. Rabat-Stockholm, Marrakesh-Wroclaw, and Agadir-Milan.
- 16 based aircraft – 2 new at Rabat – representing a total inward investment of US$1.6bn.
- Supporting over 8,500 local jobs (incl. 480 highly paid pilots and cabin crew jobs).


**Ryanair’s CEO, Eddie Wilson, said:**


*“Ryanair is delighted to announce our biggest ever Winter schedule for Morocco, delivering 5.3m low-fare seats across 156 routes, including 17 exciting new routes and the opening of our new Rabat base for Winter. This latest investment brings Ryanair’s Morocco operation to 16 based aircraft, representing a US$1.6bn investment and supporting over 8,500 local jobs.*


*Morocco has become one of Ryanair’s fastest growing and most important markets. Our record Winter 2026 schedule will boost year-round connectivity across the Kingdom, drive inbound tourism from across Europe, support regional economic development, and help deliver Morocco’s ambitious tourism growth strategy ahead of the 2030 FIFA World Cup.*


*We thank the Moroccan National Tourist Office, in addition to the Moroccan Government and ONDA for their continued partnership and shared ambition to grow Morocco’s tourism sector. Ryanair remains committed to further long-term investment in Morocco, and we look forward to continuing to grow traffic, aircraft and routes, bringing even more visitors, connectivity and low fares to communities across the Kingdom.”*
