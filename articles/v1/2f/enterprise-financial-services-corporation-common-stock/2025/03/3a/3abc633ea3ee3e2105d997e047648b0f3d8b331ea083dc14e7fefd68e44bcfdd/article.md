# Enterprise Bank & Trust Names Industry Veteran As Las Vegas Market President

Enterprise Bank & Trust, the banking subsidiary of Enterprise Financial Services Corp (NASDAQ: EFSC), is pleased to name Vjollca Tarjan to the position of Las Vegas Market President.


A proven and trusted commercial banker with more than two decades of industry experience, Vjollca brings demonstrated leadership skills in successfully managing client relationships and driving sales growth in competitive markets including Las Vegas.


In her new role, Vjollca will build upon a culture of excellence, integrity and continuous improvement with the experienced and knowledgeable banking team at Enterprise. She will provide informed guidance in the development and implementation of innovative financial solutions to meet the evolving needs of small and midsize businesses.


"Vjollca is a seasoned banking leader who is highly respected throughout Las Vegas and the surrounding region, and we’re excited for her to build on the momentum of the business and brand Enterprise Bank & Trust has established in the region," said Jeff Friesen, President, West Region. “She is a highly-respected expert and community leader who will be an excellent representative for us among organizations and business leaders looking to grow.”


Vjollca will focus on driving Enterprise’s organic growth in Las Vegas and the surrounding region through the evaluation of business prospects, execution of growth strategies and active pursuit of the bank’s community-focused objectives.


“I’m honored to join Enterprise and look forward to advancing the bank’s mission of supporting the financial well-being of businesses throughout Las Vegas and the surrounding region,” Vjollca said. “I look forward to leveraging and expanding upon the bank’s successful operational model through collaboration with a remarkable team.”
