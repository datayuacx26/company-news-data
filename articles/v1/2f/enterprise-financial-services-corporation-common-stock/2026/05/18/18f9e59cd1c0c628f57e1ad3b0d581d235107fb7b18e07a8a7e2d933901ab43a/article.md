# Enterprise Bank & Trust Promotes Emily Weber to St. Charles Market President

Enterprise Bank & Trust, the banking subsidiary of Enterprise Financial Services Corp (NASDAQ: EFSC), is pleased to announce the promotion of Emily Weber to the position of Market President & Director of Business Banking. Weber’s Market President role specifically covers the area of St. Charles County, MO.


During her 25 years at Enterprise, Weber’s leadership has made impactful contributions to the bank’s growth throughout St. Louis and surrounding regions. This strategic appointment reflects her exceptional capabilities and underscores the bank’s commitment to building on its successes in the high-priority St. Charles County market.


“I’m honored to have this opportunity to support Enterprise’s deepening investment in serving clients throughout St. Charles County,” Weber said. “Building on our longstanding commitment to this important market, I look forward to working with the exceptional local team to further advance our role as a go-to partner for businesses throughout the county.”


Weber retains her previous duties leading the business banking division, overseeing and leveraging a talented leadership team across multiple markets to ensure continued success for the bank and current and prospective clients.


“Emily’s accomplishments have made a meaningful impact on our success throughout the region, making her an excellent choice to bolster and lead our expert teams in this priority market,” said Doug Bauche, Senior EVP, Chief Banking Officer.
