# Forum Energy Technologies Announces Timing of Second Quarter 2026 Earnings Conference Call

## Forum Energy Technologies Announces Timing of Second Quarter 2026 Earnings Conference Call


July 13, 2026


[PDF Version](https://ir.f-e-t.com/node/15416/pdf)


HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 13, 2026-- Forum Energy Technologies, Inc. (NYSE: FET) announced today that it will host its second quarter 2026 earnings conference call at 10:00 a.m. Central Time on Friday, July 31, 2026. FET will issue a press release reporting its second quarter 2026 earnings prior to the conference call.


The call will be webcast through the Investor Relations link on FET’s website at[ir.f-e-t.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.f-e-t.com%2F&esheet=54569602&newsitemid=20260713022222&lan=en-US&anchor=ir.f-e-t.com&index=1&md5=e18985edd597da8848360cda4f15dfed) . Participants may also join the call by registering[here](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fregister-conf.media-server.com%2Fregister%2FBI77d80ed1ed354dd8bd90f7b88c154c16&esheet=54569602&newsitemid=20260713022222&lan=en-US&anchor=here&index=2&md5=b9d04135910c9602f296c13109a80394) . A replay of the call will be available on the Investor Relations website after the completion of the call at approximately 5:00 p.m. Central Time.


*FET is a global manufacturing company, serving the oil, natural gas, defense, and renewable energy industries. With headquarters in Houston, Texas, FET provides value added solutions aimed at improving the safety, efficiency, and environmental impact of our customers’ operations.
