# **Engineered in the USA**


**Milwaukee, WI**


In 1958 John C. Koss invented the world’s first SP/3 Stereophone in Milwaukee, WI marking the beginning of a legacy rooted in over 65+ years of precision engineering and innovative audio design. That same commitment continues today with A/550, designed, engineered and refined at Koss World Headquarters in Milwaukee, WI.


From acoustic engineering to material selection to design, ensuring that the A/550 upholds the tradition of inventing headphones that deliver iconic Koss sound, durability, and craftsmanship.


# Hearing is Believing


**Over 65 Years of Sound Innovation**


At the heart of the A/550 is Koss’s engineered 54-ohm RLX44 dynamic driver system. Each driver features a multilayer polycarbonate diaphragm for exceptional rigidity and control, a precision-machined neodymium magnet for high efficiency and responsiveness, and an ultra-light copper-clad aluminum voice coil for rapid transient response.


Housed within a reinforced 44 mm resin frame designed to minimize resonance and preserve sonic purity, A/550 delivers open-back, high-fidelity sound across a wide frequency range of 12 Hz to 22,000 Hz that reproduce audio with remarkable clarity, balance, and depth.
