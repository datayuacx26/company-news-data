# New Recall.ai Pricing for 2026: $0.50 per Hour of Meeting Recording

## **Why is Recall.ai lowering prices?**


We’re[lowering prices](https://www.recall.ai/pricing) to make Recall.ai accessible to more startups and companies building products around conversation data. Recall.ai is an essential infrastructure layer for companies that work with meeting data, spanning industries like sales, notetaking, healthcare, legal tech, incident management, and more. Ultimately, lower pricing helps more teams test, launch, and scale products built on top of Recall.ai.


## **Breakdown of Recall.ai’s updated pricing**


### Recall.ai now charges $0.50 per recording hour


As of early 2026, Recall.ai has dropped prices for the Pay As You Go Plan from $0.70 per recording hour to $0.50 per recording hour. This change applies to both our[Meeting Bot API](https://www.recall.ai/product/meeting-bot-api) and[Desktop Recording SDK](https://www.recall.ai/product/desktop-recording-sdk) , which are priced at the same rate.


### Recall.ai has no platform fee


We no longer charge a monthly platform fee –[our usage-based pricing model](https://www.recall.ai/pricing) means our customers only pay when they’re actively using Recall.ai. This change makes it easier for startups to build a proof of concept without a financial commitment.


### Calendar API included for free


Unlike other companies, we don’t charge for our[Calendar API](https://www.recall.ai/product/calendar-integration-api) , which connects Recall.ai with your users’ Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook accounts. This lets you automatically send meeting bots to select events on your users’ calendars, while enriching meeting recordings with calendar information like participant emails and meeting titles.


### Flexible transcription pricing


To[get meeting transcripts](https://www.recall.ai/product/transcription-api) , you can use Recall.ai’s built-in transcription engine for $0.15 per recording hour. Based on your preferred balance of cost, language support, accuracy, and latency, you can also pick from one of the many transcription providers we partner with and pay them directly.


### Storage and playback


You can[store meeting audio, video, and transcripts](https://docs.recall.ai/docs/storage-and-playback) for up to 7 days for free on Recall.ai’s platform. If you need to store your data for longer, we charge $0.05 per recording media hour for every additional 30 days of storage.
