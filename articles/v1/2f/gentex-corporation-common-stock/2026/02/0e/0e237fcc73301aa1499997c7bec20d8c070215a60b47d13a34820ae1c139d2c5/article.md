# Gentex Announces First Quarter 2026 Cash Dividend

## Gentex Announces First Quarter 2026 Cash Dividend


February 24, 2026


[PDF Version](https://ir.gentex.com/node/17611/pdf)


ZEELAND, Mich., Feb. 24, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Gentex Corporation (NASDAQ: GNTX), the Zeeland, Michigan-based supplier of digital vision, connected car, dimmable glass, fire protection technologies, medical devices, and consumer electronics, today announced that its Board of Directors recently declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.12 (12 cents) per share that will be payable April 22, 2026, to shareholders of record of the common stock at the close of business on April 8, 2026.


About the Company
Founded in 1974, Gentex Corporation (The NASDAQ Global Select Market: GNTX) is a leading supplier of digital vision, connected car, dimmable glass, fire protection technologies, medical devices, and consumer electronics.
