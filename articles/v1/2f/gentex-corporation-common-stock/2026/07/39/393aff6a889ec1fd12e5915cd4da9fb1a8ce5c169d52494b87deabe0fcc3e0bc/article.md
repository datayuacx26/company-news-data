# GENTEX DELIVERS A FEAST FOR THE EYES AND EARS AT THE 2026 MICHIGAN INTERNATIONAL AUTO SHOW


Gentex's booth at Michigan International Auto Show will feature the Ringbrothers' OCTAVIA, a fully bespoke 1971 Aston Martin DBS.


ZEELAND, MICHIGAN, January 27, 2026 – When the[Michigan International Auto Show](https://showspan.com/mas/) kicks of Thursday afternoon, visitors to[Gentex Corporation’s](https://www.gentex.com/) (NASDAQ:[GNTX](http://www.nasdaq.com/symbol/gntx) ) booth will be able to experience[premium car audio](https://www.klipsch.com/automotive) from the company’s newly acquired[Klipsch](https://www.klipsch.com/) Audio brand, and take in the critically acclaimed, awe-inspiring design of[OCTAVIA](https://www.caranddriver.com/news/a65783978/ringbrothers-octavia-aston-martin-dbs-revealed/) , a fully custom 1971 Aston Martin DBS from the world-renowned Ringbrothers.


Gentex is a technology company and long-time supplier of electronic products for the global automotive, aerospace, fire protection, medical, and consumer electronics industries. The company is Ottawa County’s largest employer and has expanded into Grand Rapids with a satellite manufacturing facility in the Madison Square neighborhood and a tech-hub engineering center downtown.


> “This year, we’re offering a feast for the eyes and ears,” said Craig Piersma, Gentex vice president of marketing and corporate communications. “For the first time, our booth will feature a vehicle with our Klipsch Premium Audio system. And we’re once again showcasing a custom car from Ringbrothers, the nation’s preeminent custom car builder. OCTAVIA is a ‘must see’ -- the most celebrated Ringbrothers vehicle yet created!”


[OCTAVIA](https://www.newspressusa.com/releases/public-view/single-release/83711) is indeed the biggest, most ambitious, and highly acclaimed[Ringbrothers](https://www.ringbrothers.com/) build to date. It’s a fully bespoke 1971 Aston Martin DBS, engineered to reimagine this icon of British motoring as a wholly modern and tech-rich car. It was unveiled in August at The Quail, A Motorsports Gathering in Carmel California, part of Monterey Car Week, and was displayed to the public for the first time at the[SEMA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-C7fD6JEsaY) car show in Las Vegas in November.


> “We’re excited to bring Ringbrothers back to Grand Rapids,” said Piersma. Octavia combines American muscle with English sophistication. Plus, it features special tech from Gentex like dimmable sun visors, a camera-based digital rearview mirror, custom exterior mirrors, and more.”


The Klipsch Premium Audio system is featured in an Infiniti QX80, courtesy of Infiniti of Grand Rapids. The system includes a segment-first, 24-speaker[Klipsch Reference Premiere Audio system](https://www.klipsch.com/news/all-new-2025-infiniti-qx80-to-feature-premium-klipsch-reference-premiere-audio-system-powered-by-panasonic) (standard on Sensory/Autograph trims) delivering 1,200 watts, 24-channel amplification, and 3D surround sound. Key features include titanium tweeters, an 8-inch TriPower™ subwoofer, and front-seat headrest speakers for personalized audio that isolates phone calls/navigation to the driver. Gentex obtained the Klipsch brand last spring when it[acquired](https://ir.gentex.com/news-releases/news-release-details/gentex-announces-closing-voxx-international-acquisition)[VOXX International](https://www.voxxelectronics.com/) .


Gentex’s booth at the show will also highlight some of its digital vision, connectivity, sensing, and dimmable glass technologies. Gentex HR representatives will be on hand should anyone be interested in exploring employment opportunities. Gentex is known for its world-class facilities, excellent benefits, tremendous work perks, generous bonus structure, and a unique corporate culture that values innovation.


This year’s Michigan International Auto Show runs January 29 through February 1, 2026, at DeVos Place in downtown Grand Rapids. The show is hosted annually by the Grand Rapids New Car Dealers Association and produced by ShowSpan, Inc. It showcases hundreds of production vehicles from more than two dozen global manufacturers, including sedans, vans, SUVs, trucks, hybrids, sports cars, and more. Vintage cars will also be well represented thanks to a display of collection favorites from the Gilmore Car Museum.


##### **About Ringbrothers**


Based in Spring Green, Wisconsin,[Ringbrothers](http://www.ringbrothers.com/) has established itself in the automotive aftermarket as a premium parts builder and world-class car designer. Among its many accolades are several Chevrolet Best in Show awards, Goodguys Street Machine of the Year awards, Mothers' Shine awards and SEMA Battle of the Builders wins in 2019 and 2022. Ringbrothers proudly designs, fabricates, packages and ships its products from its headquarters in the United States, focusing on quality materials, craftsmanship and customer service. Revolutionizing the way resto-mods are done, Ringbrothers continues to set the standard with its innovative products and builds. Learn more about Ringbrothers products and builds at Ringbrothers.com.
