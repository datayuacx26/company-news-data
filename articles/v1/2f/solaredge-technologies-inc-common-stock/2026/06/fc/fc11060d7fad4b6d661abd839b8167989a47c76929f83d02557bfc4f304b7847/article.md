# SolarEdge Nexis Earns Wave of U.S. Financing Approvals Across Leading Residential Solar Platforms

#### *Leading U.S. residential solar financing platforms approve SolarEdge Nexis as domestic manufacturing ramps in Texas and Utah*


**MILPITAS, Calif. - June 8, 2026** –[SolarEdge Technologies, Inc.](https://www.solaredge.com/us/) (“SolarEdge”), a global leader in smart energy technology, today announced that SolarEdge Nexis, its next-generation residential solar and storage platform, has earned approval from a growing roster of leading U.S. residential solar financing platforms, including Palmetto Light Reach, Terra Energy, Maxwell Power (formerly HDM Renewable Finance), Participate Energy, Skylight Lending, and IGS Solar.


Together, these platforms represent a broad cross-section of U.S. residential solar financing — spanning TPO, prepaid PPA, and loan products — giving installers and homeowners more ways to access SolarEdge Nexis regardless of how they choose to finance. The milestone comes as SolarEdge ramps up U.S. manufacturing of the solution in Austin, Texas, and Salt Lake City, Utah, strengthening the company’s domestic manufacturing footprint as demand grows for its American-made energy technologies and solutions aligned with domestic content.


“SolarEdge Nexis was designed to redefine what homeowners and installers should expect from a residential solar and storage system, from installation simplicity and scalability to long-term performance and energy resilience,” said Amir Cohen, General Manager of SolarEdge North America. “With broad financing support now in place alongside expanding U.S. manufacturing, we are helping remove key barriers to adoption and enabling installers to deploy advanced home energy systems at scale confidently.”


“SolarEdge Nexis brings together the installation simplicity, modular storage, and whole-home backup capability that our financing customers are actively looking for,” said Dustin Dunaway, Chief Revenue Officer at Maxwell Power (formerly HDM Renewable Finance). “For homeowners who want to own their system, having a U.S.-manufactured, FEOC-compliant option matters — both for confidence in the technology today and for resilience against whatever comes next. SolarEdge Nexis is exactly the kind of product we want to be financing.”


“We’ve been watching the SolarEdge Nexis platform closely, and the combination of installation simplicity, modular storage, and full-home backup capability is exactly what the residential market has been waiting for,” said Jaime Martinez, CEO of Terra Energy. “Having this product approved across leading financing platforms removes the last major barrier to deploying it at scale — and we’re excited to start putting it in front of our customers.”


"IGS Solar has been serving home energy customers across the Midwest, Northeast, and Florida for many years, and we understand what it takes to earn their trust," said Brandon Childers, President at IGS Solar. "SolarEdge Nexis is built to last — the modular storage architecture means homeowners can start where they are and grow over time, and the whole-home backup capability answers the question every customer is asking right now. We're excited to bring it to the markets we know best."


“The U.S. grid is changing fast, and homeowners’ energy needs are evolving with it,” said Thomas Dungan, CEO of Skylight Lending. “The flexibility and modularity of SolarEdge Nexis give us confidence that this is a product built to stand the test of time — and that’s exactly what we look for when we decide what to finance.”


[Launched commercially in Germany](https://investors.solaredge.com/news-releases/news-release-details/solaredge-launches-its-next-generation-solaredge-nexis) for the 3PH grid in March 2026, SolarEdge Nexis is the company’s next-generation residential solar and storage platform, featuring a newly designed high-efficiency inverter architecture, modular “LEGO-like” battery system, full-home backup capabilities, and a simplified installation experience. The company is accelerating its North American rollout as installer demand and financing momentum continue to grow.


“Domestic manufacturing is becoming a competitiveness requirement, not just a policy checkbox,” added Cohen. “What the market needs most right now is clarity, not more complexity. Installers and homeowners want trusted technology, dependable financing pathways, and confidence that their systems will continue delivering value for years to come.”


For installers, SolarEdge Nexis introduces a streamlined installation and commissioning experience through an entirely redesigned system architecture featuring:


- Single SKU high-efficiency residential inverter technology supporting up to 13kW;14.5kW during backup


- Modular Simple-Click™ battery architecture with 5kWh blocks, scalable up to 80 kWh


- Simplified four-component installation design


- Full-home backup capabilities


- Advanced energy optimization and monitoring capabilities


Additional financing partner announcements, AVL expansions, and North American availability updates are expected throughout 2026.
