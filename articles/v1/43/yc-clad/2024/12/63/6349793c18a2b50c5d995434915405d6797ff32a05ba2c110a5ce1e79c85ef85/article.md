# Vendor Management Quick Actions

### A more capable design to onboard vendors and ensure ongoing compliance


Monitor and take action on your subcontractor’s compliance, with tools for procurement power users.


### What’s new


1.


**Quick Peek** - View vendor compliance status through a custom progress indicator and detailed hover card, showing the status of each work requirement at a quick glance.


2.


**Vendor Grid Mode (beta)** - A compliance-specific dashboard to easily see all of your vendor’s onboarding and compliance status at once. You’ll also be able to take actions directly from this screen - override a requirement, request missing information, and upload documents.


### Why we built this


We've seen strong adoption of our vendor management tools. Procurement, operations, and admin teams are using them to automate vendor onboarding and compliance tracking. But, as people used these capabilities more and more, we noticed that many of the key workflows were taking too long.


We believe administrative work shouldn't eat up your day. That's especially true for vendor managers and procurement leaders who have more strategic priorities to focus on. These updates make it faster to handle common compliance tasks. Whether you're tracking subcontractor documentation or reviewing vendor requirements, you can get what you need and move on.


We’re excited to keep improving Clad to make it the best tool for construction vendor management.
