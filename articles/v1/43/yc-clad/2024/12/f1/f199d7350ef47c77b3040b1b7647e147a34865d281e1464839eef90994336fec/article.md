# Connected Markups

### Fast and accurate field updates, linked to project financials.


It is now faster to update field progress in Clad. Crews can mark up construction prints with custom shapes that are connected to your company’s billable item codes.


### Why we built this


Infrastructure contractors face a core challenge - how to quickly get accurate data from the field.


This is mission critical because the question “what work was completed today?” drives so much - from customer billing to inventory tracking to subcontractor payments.


When field documentation tools are hard to use, daily production updates become delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate (plus, your field crews won't be too happy).


Connected Mark Ups is our next step to making field reporting easier. This lets crews spend less time on data entry and more time on actual work. And, your back office benefits too - making it a cinch to gather the information contractors need to get paid faster, track materials, or pay subcontractors.


### What’s new


1. You can now build a custom shape library linked to billable items
2. Shapes are automatically populated with default quantities
3. The update is automatically linked to the project's progress and budget trackers.
