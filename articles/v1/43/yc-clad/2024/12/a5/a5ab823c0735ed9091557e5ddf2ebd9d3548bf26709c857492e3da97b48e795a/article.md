# Invoice Management: AI Extraction & Custom Approvals

### Job costing without manual data entry; Approvals without messy emails


Cut down your time managing invoices by over 75%. Clad invoice management now comes with AI data extraction and custom approval flows to save time and gain real-time visibility into project spend.

#### AI Invoice Extraction


Upload any invoice and Clad will extract all relevant information. The invoice is then automatically linked to a project’s budget. This gives instant visibility into project costs.


#### Invoice Approval Flows


Create custom approval workflows to streamline invoice reviews. Monitor approvals in real-time, eliminate messy email chains between finance and operations, and maintain a clear audit trail.


With these features, we’ve seen teams cut down their time to process invoices by >75%.


### Why we built this


We saw ISPs painstakingly copying details from PDF invoices into spreadsheets.


This was needed to do job costing and track project-level spend, since traditional ERPs and finance systems don’t help out of the box. Meanwhile, invoices and approvals frequently were getting lost in email threads, leading to delayed payments and frustrated subs.


Job costing and invoice approvals are both critical for effective construction management, yet teams are stuck with inefficient, manual processes. It was clear that we could save everyone time, while still getting finance and operations teams the information they need.


The updates we’re announcing today eliminate the need for duplicative work. Operations teams can treat Clad as the ‘front door’ to process vendor invoices. This gives them the data for detailed cost tracking (without manual data entry) and avoids approval confusion at the same time.
