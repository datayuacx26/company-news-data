# Project Insights

### A powerful engine to understand production and spend across your projects.


We’ve built a powerful tool to build the reports and dashboards you need to monitor production and project spend, across all of your projects.


### What's new


Your browser does not support the video tag.


- **Report Types:** Track project performance through two distinct views. The first shows Production or Spend on a daily, weekly, or monthly metrics. The second lets you compare Budget vs. Actual to compare planned vs actual production, budgeted or actual spend.
- **Filtering:** Navigate from high-level project status to granular details with a broad set of filters, enabling analysis from project-wide metrics down to individual crew productivity on a specific line item.
- **Saved Reports and Pinned Favorites:** Get quick access to your favorite reports through saving configurations and pin important views to your dashboard.
- **Data Export:** Export any report to CSV format for external analysis and reporting


### Why we built this


Especially in broadband, we know that it’s important to get a quick snapshot of your entire construction program, across projects, contractors, and regions.


Project Insights lets operations teams at ISPs or contractors get the core information they need without needing to set up complex templates or be an excel whiz.


Our customers are already using Insights in a variety of ways - to monitor crew productivity, evaluate project health, and get the data needed to pay subcontractors.


We’re excited to see how more people continue to use these reports over time!
