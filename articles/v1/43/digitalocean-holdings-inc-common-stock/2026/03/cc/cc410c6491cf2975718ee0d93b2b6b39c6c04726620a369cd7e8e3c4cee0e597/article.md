# DigitalOcean India: Inside Our Growing Hub for AI and Cloud Innovation

At DigitalOcean, our philosophy, ‘We ship in hours and days, not months or quarters,’ delivers real results for our customers. This is the exact velocity that has defined DigitalOcean’s India region over the past year. Since expanding into the Hyderabad market exactly twelve months ago, the broader India team has rapidly evolved into a core engine for our global business, shipping high-impact products at record speed. Time flies when you are having fun! As we celebrate this anniversary, we recognize a year of unparalleled execution and look forward to the region’s next massive milestone: building DigitalOcean’s[Agentic Inference Cloud](https://www.digitalocean.com/products/gradient) .


## A history of high-velocity growth


DigitalOcean recently achieved[record growth](https://investors.digitalocean.com/news/news-details/2026/DigitalOcean-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2025-Financial-Results/default.aspx) as a $1B global inference cloud company, supporting over 640K customers in 190 countries across[18 globally distributed data centers](https://docs.digitalocean.com/platform/regional-availability/) , including one in Bangalore. Over time, we have supported larger, more complex AI and Digital-Native workloads for companies like[Character.ai](https://www.digitalocean.com/blog/technical-deep-dive-character-ai-amd) and[Workato](https://www.digitalocean.com/blog/workato-nvidia-technical-deep-dive-agentic-inference-cloud) , and have established partnerships with[AMD](https://www.digitalocean.com/blog/technical-deep-dive-character-ai-amd) and[NVIDIA](https://www.digitalocean.com/blog/building-ai-factory-for-agentic-era-nvidia-gtc) . Our team in India has grown alongside the company, becoming a critical hub for product-led innovation. In the last year alone, we have doubled in size with over 370 employees in-region as we develop the next generation of AI inference and position the company for accelerated growth.


“DigitalOcean is entering a new phase of accelerated growth driven by AI. AI inferencing and agentic workflows will power the next generation of applications, and our India teams are strategic to our product and technology innovation, helping build these AI native capabilities across the platform,” says **Venkatraghav Parthasarathy, Vice President, India General Manager** .


## Building the AI inference cloud


DigitalOcean has brought together talent from across India - from Engineers to Technology Evangelists, Product Managers, and UI experts - to build DigitalOcean’s Agentic Inference cloud. This includes products like GPU Droplets, Gradient AI, and our App Platform, which enable customers to perform inference at scale. Over the last year, teams here have contributed to launches across[networking](https://www.digitalocean.com/products/networking) ,[storage](https://www.digitalocean.com/products/spaces) ,[security](https://www.digitalocean.com/security) , and more, supporting new capabilities for the cloud stack. Our[values](https://youtu.be/QGgigyucaaw) of simplicity, velocity, and experimentation mean employees are given ownership and can make an impact from Day 1. Engineers here work closely with teams to test new ideas and build ambitious solutions that power DigitalOcean’s platform.


“ **The Inference Cloud built for scale - without complexity or surprise costs** . That last part of removing complexity is what my team is focused on, whether it is customers trying to access DO through APIs, where over a billion requests are made every week, or organizing their massive organizations through various accounts to get the best use of DigitalOcean’s infrastructure. We are also focused on alleviating the pain points of developers within DO as well. After much dedication, the features developed by the team were showcased in[London Deploy 2025](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLbPZ_kA1AJr-lfixBwA6jH7-IetlmqSFa) , which included account hierarchy and single sign-on that served a thousand customers in just three months,” notes **Ravi Grandhi, Senior Director of Engineering** .


## A flexible environment for extraordinary talent


In India, we believe that in order to perform at a high level, employees need an environment that offers both flexibility and purpose. DigitalOcean provides a foundation for employees to do the best work of their careers with a commitment to both remote work and in-person collaboration (at our office in[Hi-Tech Corridor](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ERzX8KM1abfW2pHa6) ). Our goal is to provide a great place to work by creating a sandbox for innovation where you can sharpen your skills on the latest tech stack through hands-on experience and a variety of development programs - from unlimited LinkedIn Learning access to career pathing to manager training. Beyond the work, the team in India is a community where individuals feel proud of their contributions, enjoy meaningful relationships with their colleagues, and receive rewards and recognition for their efforts. We empower our teams with competitive cash compensation and future-focused equity, generous wellness benefits, and in-office and global perks, like our internal recognition platform and local social meetup program.


“India has always had an incredible pool of engineering talent. What makes DigitalOcean special is that it gives that talent the right platform, a place where engineers can contribute meaningfully to global products, see their work impact millions of developers, and grow while doing it,” shares **Sudhanshu Desai, Sr. Director, Cybersecurity.**


## The horizon is bright for DigitalOcean India


As we reflect on our growth in the region, our vision for India remains clear: To do what DigitalOcean has always done best - deliver powerful technology that is simple and reliable at scale, and do the best work of our career along the way. The talent within our India team is actively shaping DigitalOcean’s next chapter. We are excited to scale and deepen our roots in the region, and to build the future of AI and the cloud together.


[We’re hiring](https://www.digitalocean.com/careers) **!** If you are a builder ready to move toward high-impact AI innovation, we want to meet you.
