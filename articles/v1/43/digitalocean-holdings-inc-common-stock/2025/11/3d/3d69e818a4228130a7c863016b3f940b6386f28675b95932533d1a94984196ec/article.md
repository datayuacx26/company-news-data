# Leading the Cloud With Curiosity : Spotlight on Pranav Nambiar, SVP, AI/ML & PaaS

Pranav Nambiar is no stranger to big tech, but what inspired him to join DigitalOcean was to be closer to the builders. He also wanted to work in a place where ideas move fast, people stay humble, and real breakthroughs happen. Today, he leads our[AI/ML](https://www.digitalocean.com/products/gradient) and[PaaS](https://www.digitalocean.com/products/app-platform) portfolios with curiosity, empathy, and a belief that innovation should lift others up.


## What made DigitalOcean the right place for you?


“Having spent a lot of time in large companies, I was eager to experience a smaller, more agile company and broaden my horizons. DigitalOcean stood out for three main reasons:


**First** , the fundamentals are incredibly strong. A company that moves from startup to IPO and now serves more than 640,000 customers—that’s an incredible achievement. It clearly signals strong fundamentals at DigitalOcean.


**Second** , the opportunity to make a big impact is real. With generative AI transforming how innovation happens, and developers becoming the new drivers of change, DigitalOcean’s developer-first product heritage and simplified cloud focus position us to make massive waves.


**Last but not least** , the culture is what brought me here. People build amazing products with a strong focus on simplicity and sophistication—this is what DigitalOcean is about. All of these combined made me feel this was the right place to join the journey and create a meaningful impact.”

## How is your team shaping the future for our customers?


“I lead two major organizations at DigitalOcean: one is AI/ML and the other is[Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS)](https://www.digitalocean.com/resources/articles/what-is-paas) . To simplify: my teams build great infrastructure that customers can leverage with modern technology to create amazing business experiences.


On the AI/ML side, we’re focused on delivering next-generation generative AI products and services, products that will likely change how people look at AI and build intelligent apps.


On the PaaS side, we build highly scalable, reliable platforms enabling companies to launch business-critical applications without needing to do all the heavy lifting that typical cloud services may demand.


Overall: our teams help customers innovate and realize their full potential with technology, while staying true to the simplicity and sophistication that DigitalOcean is known for.”


## What makes DigitalOcean a special place to build and innovate?


“DigitalOcean is now an extremely well-known brand, one of the cloud providers that developers think of. Also, we have a strong developer community across the board.


What excites me most is that you get to work with cutting-edge technology to build something amazing that can change how companies around the world operate. I’m really excited about the products we’re working on, and I believe they can make a world of difference for developers everywhere.


If I had to sum it up: we have a golden opportunity to build something industry-transforming. And here’s the great part: you get to do this with a team of professionals who are passionate, dedicated, collaborative, and above all, truly humble. If you’re looking to build something magical and keep learning both professionally and intellectually, DigitalOcean is where you want to be.”
