# Best IFTTT Alternatives: Find the Right Automation Tool for Your Needs

If This Then That (IFTTT) has been a go-to for simple automations for years. It's great for connecting apps and devices in straightforward ways – like getting a notification if rain is forecast or saving your Instagram photos to Dropbox. But what happens when your "if this" gets more complex, or your "then that" needs more power? You might start looking for IFTTT alternatives.


Many users find themselves needing more than IFTTT's applet-based system can offer, especially as their automation needs grow or become more business-focused. If you're one of them, you're in the right place.


## Why Look for an IFTTT Alternative?


While IFTTT excels at simplicity, several factors might lead you to explore other options:


-


**Pricing Changes & Limitations:** IFTTT's Pro plan introduced new limits, and for more extensive use, the costs can add up, or the free tier might feel restrictive.


-


**Need for More Complex Logic:** Business processes often require more than a single trigger and action. You might need[conditional logic](https://docs.workflow86.com/docs/components/cond_logic) (if X happens, do Y, but if Z happens, do A), multi-step sequences, or the ability to[branch paths](https://docs.workflow86.com/docs/components/branch_paths) in your workflow.


-


**Desire for Advanced Features:** Things like detailed error handling, data transformation,[AI-powered steps](https://docs.workflow86.com/docs/ai/ai-intro) , or[human-in-the-loop approvals](https://docs.workflow86.com/docs/forms-and-tasks/assign_task) are often missing in simpler tools.


-


**Specific Integration Gaps:** While IFTTT has many integrations, it might not connect with every niche or specialized business application you use.


-


**Scalability for Business:** As businesses grow, their automation needs become more sophisticated, requiring tools that can handle higher volumes and more intricate[workflows](https://docs.workflow86.com/docs/workflows/workflow_intro) .


## Key Features to Consider in an Automation Tool


Choosing the right automation platform can feel daunting. Here's a checklist of what to look for to help you decide:


-


**Connectivity:** How many apps and services does it connect with? Does it support the specific tools critical to your tasks? Check out a platform's[integrations list](https://www.workflow86.com/integrations) .


-


**Complexity Handling:** Can it manage multi-step automations? Does it offer conditional logic, branching,[loops](https://docs.workflow86.com/docs/workflows/loops) , and parallel processing?


-


**User Interface (UI) & Ease of Use:** Is it intuitive? Does it offer a visual builder? Is it truly no-code, or does it require some technical know-how? Can you easily[edit your workflows](https://docs.workflow86.com/docs/getting-started/edit_workflow) ?


-


**Pricing:** How does the pricing scale? Is there a free tier that's genuinely useful? What are the limits on tasks, users, or connected apps? Does it offer good value for your specific needs? (See an example[here](https://www.workflow86.com/pricing) ).


-


**Advanced Features:** Does it support AI components, advanced data manipulation ([like parsing JSON](https://docs.workflow86.com/docs/components/parse_json) ), robust error handling, or the ability to[run custom code](https://docs.workflow86.com/docs/components/run_code) ?


-


**Human-in-the-Loop:** Can you easily incorporate manual steps, such as approvals or[task assignments](https://docs.workflow86.com/docs/forms-and-tasks/assign_task) , into your automated processes?


-


**Support & Community:** What kind of support is available? Is there an active user community for help and inspiration?


-


**Use Cases:** Is it better suited for personal productivity, small business tasks, or enterprise-level[solutions](https://www.workflow86.com/solutions) ?


## Top IFTTT Alternatives to Explore


Let's look at some popular and powerful IFTTT alternatives:


### 1. Zapier


-


**Brief:** A giant in the automation space, known for connecting thousands of web apps.


-


**Pros:** Massive library of app integrations ("Zaps"), user-friendly interface for simple to moderately complex automations.


-


**Cons:** Can become expensive quickly as your task volume or complexity increases. The free tier is quite limited.


-


**Best for:** Users needing to connect a wide array of common web services for tasks that don't involve extremely intricate logic.


### 2. Make (formerly Integromat)


-


**Brief:** Offers a highly visual and flexible "scenario" builder for more complex automations.


-


**Pros:** Powerful data manipulation tools, visual debugging, more granular control over workflow logic than Zapier for certain tasks.


-


**Cons:** The interface can have a steeper learning curve for beginners. Pricing is based on operations, which can be tricky to predict.


-


**Best for:** Users who need to build visually complex workflows with detailed data handling and don't mind a bit of a learning curve.


### 3. n8n.io


-


**Brief:** A source-available (often called open-source) workflow automation tool that can be self-hosted or used via their cloud offering.


-


**Pros:** Cost-effective, especially if self-hosted. Node-based visual editor offers flexibility for developers and technical users. Growing list of integrations.


-


**Cons:** Self-hosting requires technical setup and maintenance. The number of native integrations is smaller than Zapier or Make, though it's expanding.


-


**Best for:** Tech-savvy users, developers, and businesses looking for a customizable, potentially low-cost solution, especially if they can self-host.


### 4. Microsoft Power Automate (formerly Microsoft Flow)


-


**Brief:** Microsoft's automation platform, deeply integrated into the Microsoft 365 and Azure ecosystems.


-


**Pros:** Excellent for automating tasks within Microsoft applications (Outlook, SharePoint, Teams, Dynamics 365, etc.). Strong enterprise features and RPA capabilities.


-


**Cons:** Can be less intuitive or efficient when working with non-Microsoft services. Licensing can be complex depending on your Microsoft subscriptions.


-


**Best for:** Businesses heavily invested in the Microsoft ecosystem.
