# What Does a Sales Representative Do in Software Sales?

The best salespeople sell something they believe in — something they know can make a difference in their clients’ lives and businesses. When it comes to software sales, the bar is even higher, as sales professionals introduce prospects to tools that can shift the whole trajectory of their organization. At Paycom, a leading S&P 500 HR software company, our Sales Representatives drive businesses forward around the globe.


Working in software sales is incredibly rewarding. At Paycom, for example, our Sales Representatives know their role is about much more than selling payroll tech. The work they do each day as part of our Sales team ensures people get paid accurately and on time. It helps their neighbors afford their groceries, medication and daily expenses without worry. It helps the businesses they work for grow and succeed, too. Our work is truly work worth doing.


Paycom’s technology powers economies, empowers lives and supports businesses, households and futures. As a company recognized for its culture, we believe in rewarding those dedicated to winning, innovating and making a difference with us. We invite you to read on to learn more about our Sales Representative role on our Sales team.


## What does an Sales Representative do?


Paycom’s[Sales Representatives](https://www.paycom.com/careers/job-categories/sales/account-executive/) are confident, adaptable and efficient sales professionals who initiate new business, close deals and work directly with each business’s C-suite and HR leaders to set them up for long-term success.


Sales Representatives, also known as sales reps, are crucial to company growth. These members of the Sales team connect with potential clients and, at Paycom, introduce organizations to our innovative HR and payroll tools.


Transitioning to a new software provider is a major decision, and Sales Representatives help ease the concerns of key decision-makers, guiding them to understand the benefits of switching. They work hand in hand with C-suite leaders to understand their challenges and demonstrate how Paycom’s technology addresses their needs.


## Building relationships as a sales leader


Sales Representatives build and maintain strong relationships, moving prospects through each stage of the sales cycle — from first contact to the deal’s close — and assisting in onboarding new clients. At Paycom, we instill confidence in prospects that we are a trusted provider not only for the software transition, but for long-term business success.
