# Driven to Succeed: A Peek Inside Paycom’s Sales Team

What if you could find a company as passionate about sales as you? At Paycom, our Sales team is the driving force behind our company’s success. Our industry-leading HR and payroll tech makes work better for millions. Fueling our winning culture is a Sales team supported by a company that invests in your development, providing the tools, training and support you need to succeed in an ever-changing sales landscape.


## Ready to be challenged and grow?


Paycom’s sales culture prioritizes growth, innovation and excellence. Here, you can truly take ownership of your career. It’s just one of the reasons Paycom was named to Selling Power’s 2025 list of the[60 Best Companies to Sell For](https://www.paycom.com/about/press-room/selling-power-recognizes-paycom-as-a-top-sales-organization/) .


## It starts with our top-tier training


Our Sales team is continually seeking ways to innovate and enhance its sales strategies. At Paycom, we offer intensive training from your very first day.


“The professional development that you get at Paycom — even in the first three weeks of training — has not only taken me outside of my comfort zone and grown me personally, but it has also sharpened my sales skills and grown me professionally more than I ever expected,” said[Savanna G., an Executive Sales Representative](https://www.paycom.com/careers/resources/blog/behind-the-badge-savannas-story/) .


We are proud to offer our Sales Junior Executive internship, which helps sales interns break into the industry, as well as our Executive Representative training, where Sales team members connect with and learn from colleagues across the country.


[Paycom’s sales training](https://www.paycom.com/careers/resources/blog/the-best-never-rest-paycoms-approach-to-exceptional-sales-training/) includes lessons on our strategy, software and analysis. This training provides proven talk tracks, role-playing opportunities and multiday intensives on everything from the one-minute pitch and phone canvassing to educating prospects on our tech and closing a sale. The Paycom difference is that everyone from the Sales team, including executives, participates in the same comprehensive training to help each team member sell effectively.


We provide the opportunities and resources employees need to succeed, helping them continuously improve their skills and knowledge. And it’s just *one* of many perks our Sales team members enjoy.

The members of our Sales team are hungry for success and driven to achieve their goals. - Winners’ Circle — monthly events celebrating quota attainment

Our sales community is built on the principles of collaboration, innovation and mutual support. We proudly cultivate a culture of giving back at Paycom. As our employees discover success and advance in their careers, they also give back to their communities. Fulfillment is more than personal success — it’s about how we all win together.
