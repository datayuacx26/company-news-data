# Better Alerts

[Tangia’s Better Alerts](https://app.tangia.co/twitch/alerts) lets you setup awesome custom alerts for new follows, new subscribers, gift subs, bit cheers, raids, and more! The alerts include the *entire Tangia content library* of thousands of memes, AI TTS, Ask AI, **custom **3D scenes,** custom pop-ups, and more. Tangia provides you with unparalleled control over your stream, and your viewers are going to love it!


## Alert Flows


### Triggers


Better Alerts lets you create Alert flows. Alert flows lets you build and tailor all of the different pop-ups, memes, 3D Spline Scenes, TTS messages, and whatever else you want to have happen when an alert gets triggered on your stream! Tangia offers triggers for almost everything like new **subscribers, resubs, gifted subs, raids, follows, bit cheers** , **and more!** The Alert flow builder is super easy to use and you can set up as many alert flows as you would like.


### Conditions


Once you select a trigger for the alert flow, you will be able to add in` conditions` and` actions` . Conditions let you specify what conditions must be true for the actions to play. For example if you have raid alert, you may want to set a condition that alerts only play if the raid is larger than a certain number of viewers, or if you want a special alert to play if someone has re-subbed for a year, you can do that too! Conditions give you total control of which alert flows actually play!


### Actions


Actions are the actual media pieces that play when an alert flow trigger is fired and a condition is met. Actions can be almost anything including: Alert Pop-up, TTS & Ask AI, any of the Tangia overlay interactions, a custom video/sound you upload, a **3D Spline scene** , or even an entire website that is iFramed and displayed on stream. You can add as many actions to an alert flow as you want, and you can have alerts play at the same time as each or or sequentially. This means you can have an combo/order of Alerts as you can imagine!


### Variables


Control doesn’t just stop there, we make every value the could be associated with a certain trigger available to you for use in **any** the actions! So you can have something as simple as an AI TTS read out messages for re-subs or you can have something complex a entire 3D scene that spawns in 3D parachutes for every raiders in a raiding party. You can see a ton of examples and get scenes from our[knowledge base!](https://help.tangia.co/3f21bc44e0954144b4a988260432cdbb)


## 3D Alerts With Spline


Tangia lets you turn your Spline scenes into 3D Twitch alerts and interactions with a simple copy + paste![Spline](https://spline.design/) is browser based 3D design software thats free, and freakishly easy to use. You can make full blown 3D animations, add physics to a scene, and best of all it works with Tangia! All you have to do to turn a spline scene into an alert or interactions is give us your Spline scene link, and just like with all of the other alerts, you can pass in variables to Spline to create insanely dynamic scenes.


Spline also offers a ton of out of the box assets so if you are looking to make sick 2D scenes with custom fonts and text colors, Spline can do that too! We offer a ton of ready to go scenes that you can copy and paste right away into your alerts flow, but we have also published our spline scenes so you can go in and make whatever changes you would like.
