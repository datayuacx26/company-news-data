# Custom Tangia Parties

Custom Tangia Parties are live! You can now create and upload your own custom Tangia Parties that will get played once chat reaches a Tangia party level. Tangia Parties have always been a great way to reward chat for supporting you through Tangia and streamers who use Tangia parties tend to get sent a lot more interactions! If you are wondering what a Tangia party even is,[check this out!](https://clips.twitch.tv/AwkwardTentativeBulgogiDansGame-h8omYEMcnwj9oGDS)


We plan to continue adding to our library of over 20 existing Tangia parties. Now with custom Tangia Parties, you can add your own channel exclusive parties to mix as well. Custom Tangia parties will be much more likely to play over regular Tangia parties so there is no shot your custom party will get buried under our existing library. But if you are ready to start uploading you can head over to the new[Tangia Party settings tab.](https://app.tangia.co/twitch/settings/parties)


To upload your own Custom Party you are going to want to export your video files as a .webm. The .webm file will preserve transparency, if you have it enabled. Once your custom party is uploaded, thats it you are good to go! You will see preview appear in the custom parties box so you will know how it appears on stream as well. If you have any more questions specifically about Tangia parties you can check out our[knowledge base here](https://help.tangia.co/fd4e1e6ca2984e6aa03a5250d6fa8bfe) . And if you run into any issues or have any specific editing question, feel free to hit us up via live support and we will gladly help out.
