# Tangia Media Share

Tangia Media Share is now live for everyone to use![Tangia Media share is new tab](https://app.tangia.co/twitch/media_share) you will find in the Tangia Dashboard where you can play back media shared with you. Your viewers will be able to share Youtube videos, Youtube shorts, TikTok videos, and even other Twitch clips through your Viewer page!


Tangia media share also offers a ton of quality of life features such as sharing the current media link in chat via a bot message, the ability to autoplay through shared media pieces, a real time public queue that anyone can view at any time, and no more “media math”, use a trimmer to trim content just as god intended. If you give us a link with a timestamp in it, we start it there too ;)


You can set a minimum price per media shared, a price per second of video ranging from free to whatever works for your stream. Priority queuing also a feature that allows you to set higher price points for that users content to play first from the priority queue.


Viola! You and your viewers can watch the shared media together with no hassle or any extra set up all just one flick of a button! We have also created a brand new Tangia challenge around media share so you can snag some extra rep for receiving media shares.
