# Better Subathon Timer

You asked, so we made it! The[Tangia Better Subathon Timer](https://app.tangia.co/twitch/settings/subathon) is now live for everyone! The subathon timer takes 1 click to setup and is fully customizable from the dashboard! You can easily set bit cheers, Tangia interactions, and tiered subs to increase the timer by a specified amount. If that isn’t enough control for your we also have a public API url you can use to let anything outside of Tangia increase the timer too!


Subathons are a great way to engage your community, try out some fun new content pieces, play different games, and overall just spice things up! With the Tangia Subathon timer, starting a subathon is a no brainer. You don’t have to download any special software, setup any new overlays. You just click` enter subathon mode` and thats it!


We hope you really enjoy this new feature, and if there is anything you think its missing, just let us know via email, live support chat, or on Twitter AKA X!
