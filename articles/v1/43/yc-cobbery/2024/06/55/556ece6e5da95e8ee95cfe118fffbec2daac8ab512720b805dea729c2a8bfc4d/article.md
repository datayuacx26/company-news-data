# The Tangia Grid


With[Tangia Grid](https://app.tangia.co/twitch/settings/grid) viewers can easily upload their own images or search popular memes on Tenor, purchase the grid cells directly through Tangia, and have the image show up on stream! Giving your viewers the option to share any piece of content is a great way to build up unique community lore, and share some nice community specific memes!


## Setting up The Tangia Grid


Tangia Grid also gives you a whole suite of customizations and moderation tools so that you can ensure the content is appropriate for your stream, and nothing shows up where you don’t want it to. Check it out!


Tangia Grid works GREAT with the[Tangia Monitor Overlay](https://www.tangia.co/monitor-overlay) if you want to incentivize chat to make your gameplay more difficult. Or if you want to have Tangia enabled but have a more chill stream sesh, the Grid works great for that too because Tangia grid has no audio support, and you can block off any space on the stream you don’t want covered.


Tangia Grid comes with a comprehensive set of moderation tools that lets you and your mods quickly and accurately vet the content submitted to your grid. Beyond just a standard preview, we also have included a GIF frame inspection tool which lets preview every frame of a GIF individually. This will prevent anyone from trying to sneak in an inappropriate frame that might get missed at first glance.
