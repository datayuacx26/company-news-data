# Tangia Dungeon

Tangia Dungeon is a brand new mini-game, available in Tangia, that your chat can play on the corner of your stream with a simple 1-click setup! Your viewers can create custom avatars, raid dungeons with other viewers, earn loot, and free interaction vouchers! The dungeon and dungeon loot are fully customizable, so you can make your dungeon experience completely unique to your stream. Tangia Dungeon is a great way to keep chat entertained during starting screens, downtime during Subathons, anytime you step away for a quick break, or even during normal stream time!


To enable Tangia Dungeon just head to the[Tangia dashboard → Settings → Dungeon](https://app.tangia.co/twitch/settings/dungeon) and click enable, and that's it! Your viewers will be able to redeem the Dungeon interaction in the Tangia extension, the viewer page, or through channel point redeems. The chatter who starts a raid gets a guaranteed spot, the remaining 5 spots go to the viewers who type “!join” fastest in chat. Every dungeon raider has a chance to earn loot, gain XP, or possibly perish during the raid.


Viewers keep all of the loot that they find while raiding dungeons and can equip the loot as avatar customizations all within the extension. Loot also provides unique attributes that actually add a layer of strategy to the dungeon raids! You can also add up to 100 custom pieces of loot in the dungeon settings.


All of the raid stats are saved in your community leaderboard! So you can see who the top dungeon raiders are in your stream. Other raiders can also get a good look at all of the sick avatars and all of the loot currently equipped by each raider.
