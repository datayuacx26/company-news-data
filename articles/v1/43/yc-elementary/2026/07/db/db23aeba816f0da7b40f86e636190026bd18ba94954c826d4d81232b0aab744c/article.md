# Elementary Now Available on AWS Marketplace | Elementary Data

**Buying Elementary Just Got a Lot Easier**


We’re excited to announce that Elementary is officially available on the AWS Marketplace, making it easier for teams to procure our data reliability platform directly through their AWS accounts.


For many of our customers, Elementary is not a tool they try for a quarter. It’s infrastructure they commit to long-term. The majority of our revenue is in multi-year contracts, reflecting this is a strategic investment for teams, who rely on Elementary as a core part of their data stack.


By making Elementary available on AWS Marketplace, we’re aligning how teams buy data reliability with how they already budget for critical infrastructure.


**What this changes**


- **Simpler procurement:** Buy Elementary directly through your AWS account, using existing approval and procurement workflows.
- **Infrastructure budgeting:** Pay for Elementary as part of your AWS spend, alongside the systems it protects.
- **Easier long-term planning:** Make data reliability a planned, strategic investment, not an exception line item.


Data reliability underpins dashboards, models, and AI systems. It belongs in the same category as the infrastructure that runs them.


Now, buying Elementary is easier, faster, and better aligned with how modern data teams operate.
