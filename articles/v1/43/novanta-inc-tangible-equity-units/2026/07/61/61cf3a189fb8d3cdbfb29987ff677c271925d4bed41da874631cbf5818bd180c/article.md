# Illuminating Discovery: How FUSIONultra Supports the Future of Personalized Medicine

By: Amanda Savas Yesensky, Senior Director, Global Sales


When people hear about lasers in life sciences, they often think about the technology itself. What excites me most is what that technology makes possible.


Every day, researchers around the world are working to better understand disease, unlock insights hidden within our genes, and develop therapies tailored to individual patients. The answers they’re searching for often exist at a level invisible to the human eye, buried within cells, proteins, and genetic material. Helping bring those answers to light is where FUSIONultra comes in.


We developed FUSIONultra to support the advanced imaging systems researchers depend on to study biology and understand disease pathways, accelerating scientific discovery. While it may be one component within a larger instrument, it plays a critical role in helping researchers collect the high-quality data they need to make confident decisions.


## **Helping Researchers See What Matters**


Many modern life sciences tools rely on fluorescence imaging.


Researchers use fluorescent tags to mark specific genetic material, proteins, or cellular structures. When those tags are illuminated with laser light, they glow, creating signals that can be captured and analyzed. The resulting data helps scientists understand how genes are expressed and how diseases develop, exploring how potential therapies interact with biological systems.


Those insights are fueling advances across genomics, sequencing, drug discovery, proteomics, and personalized medicine. As applications become more sophisticated, researchers need imaging systems that deliver both speed and accuracy. They need confidence that the data they’re collecting truly reflects what’s happening within their samples.


## **Better Data Leads to Better Answers**


One of the biggest challenges in life sciences research is data quality.


If imaging systems produce inconsistent results, researchers may struggle to draw reliable conclusions. Poor data integrity can slow development efforts and delay important discoveries, increasing the time required to bring new innovations to market.


According to Konstantinos Saroglou, Product Line Director, that challenge was a key driver behind FUSIONultra’s development. The team wanted to create a highly configurable platform that could be optimized around each customer’s application rather than forcing researchers to work within the limitations of a standard solution.


FUSIONultra combines multiple laser wavelengths into a single, integrated platform that simplifies implementation for OEM customers while providing the performance needed for demanding life sciences applications. Its configurable architecture and ULTRALOQ™ technology help deliver the stability and throughput researchers need to produce consistent, high-quality results.


## **Supporting the Future of Personalized Medicine**


What makes this work especially exciting is where the science is headed.


The healthcare industry is moving toward a future where treatments can increasingly be tailored to an individual’s unique biology. As researchers gain deeper insights into genetics, protein expression, and disease mechanisms, they’re unlocking new opportunities to identify which therapies may be most effective for specific patients.


That future depends on data.


Researchers need tools that help them process samples faster, improve confidence in their findings, and generate meaningful insights more efficiently. Faster data acquisition and improved data quality can help reduce costs and improve throughput, ultimately providing researchers with better information to analyze.


Every improvement in imaging quality helps move science closer to answers that could support new therapies and expand access to personalized medicine.


## **Innovation That Matters**


At its core, FUSIONultra reflects a simple idea: Meaningful innovation starts with understanding customer challenges.


Researchers don’t need technology for technology’s sake. They need solutions that help them generate reliable data and accelerate discovery in order to move closer to answers that can improve lives. By combining configurable design along with advanced stability and simplified integration, we developed FUSIONultra to help customers overcome real-world obstacles and focus on what matters most: the science.


Our goal remains: creating technology that helps enable better outcomes for researchers, clinicians, and patients. That’s innovation that matters.


## **Looking Ahead**


For me, FUSIONultra represents more than a product launch.


It reflects our commitment to helping customers solve increasingly complex challenges and supporting the researchers driving the next wave of scientific discovery. Through its integrated design along with the configurable architecture and stable performance, FUSIONultra helps provide the foundation customers need to transform data into insight and insight into impact.


We may only be one piece of the puzzle, but it’s exciting to know that the technology we develop today could help enable the personalized treatments of tomorrow.
