# Preserving a Local Treasure in the Franconian Forest

**By Nadine Prühß, Assembler II and ERG Member**


Giving back to our communities often starts with small actions that make a lasting difference.


On July 24, a group of employees from our site in Ludwigsstadt, Germany, participated in a Volunteer Time Off (VTO) day dedicated to maintaining a local hiking trail that we proudly sponsor. Fourteen employees from four departments joined forces to help keep the trail safe, accessible, and enjoyable for visitors.


Throughout the day, volunteers cleared fallen trees and branches, trimmed back overgrown vegetation, and mowed sections of the trail. Their efforts helped improve the hiking experience while supporting the long-term preservation of a valued community resource.


The day was also an opportunity to strengthen connections among employees. During a break, the group shared sandwiches and refreshments and enjoyed spending time together in the beautiful surroundings of the Franconian Forest.


One of the most encouraging discoveries was that there was almost no litter to collect along the route, reflecting the care and respect local visitors have for the area.


Stretching through scenic valleys, alongside streams and fish ponds, and across the higher elevations of the Franconian Forest, the trail offers a peaceful place for recreation and relaxation. By volunteering their time and effort, our employees helped ensure that others can continue to enjoy this remarkable landscape.


Community involvement is an important part of how we create a positive impact beyond our sites. This volunteer day demonstrated what can be accomplished when employees come together with a shared purpose and a commitment to preserving the places that matter most.
