# What MACHINA Summit Paris Revealed About the Future of Physical AI

We recently attended the inaugural MACHINA Summit in Paris, where robotics leaders, AI innovators and automation experts gathered to discuss the future of physical AI.


The event was packed with presentations and discussions, but the most valuable insights came from understanding how the industry is evolving beyond innovation and into large-scale deployment.


Across sessions and conversations, one question surfaced repeatedly: How do we take today’s[advanced robotic systems](https://novanta.com/robotics-automation/) and make them scalable and ready for widespread adoption?


## **The Next Challenge: Scale**


Many robotics companies have already demonstrated what their technologies can do. The conversation is no longer about proving concepts. It is about industrializing them.


Leaders across the industry discussed manufacturing readiness and supply chain strategies along with production models that can support large-scale deployment. The focus has shifted from exploring what is possible to determining how quickly these technologies can move into real-world applications.


## **Safety Is No Longer Optional**


Another consistent theme was safety.


As robots increasingly operate alongside people, organizations are placing greater emphasis on systems that can enable safe and effective human-robot collaboration. This focus extends across hardware, sensing technologies, controls and system design.


The industry’s message was clear: Intelligent robots must also be trusted robots.


## **Data Is Driving the Future**


Data emerged as a third major area of focus.


Companies are investing heavily in collecting and utilizing high-quality data to improve model training and reliability. As AI capabilities continue to evolve, access to meaningful data will remain a critical differentiator.


For companies supporting the robotics ecosystem, this creates an opportunity to deliver technologies that contribute to both performance and intelligence.


## **Why These Conversations Matter**


Beyond the formal presentations, the summit provided opportunities to connect directly with customers and industry peers.


These conversations often reveal as much as the sessions themselves. They help uncover emerging priorities and provide perspective on market direction, creating a stronger understanding of the challenges organizations are working to solve.


One takeaway was particularly clear: Long-term success will depend on delivering[meaningful value](https://novanta.com/about/vision-mission/) . As technologies become more accessible, differentiation increasingly comes from precision, performance, safety and reliability.


## **Looking Ahead**


The robotics industry continues to advance at an impressive pace, creating new opportunities across industrial automation, workforce automation and physical AI applications.


While the MACHINA Summit did not introduce a dramatic shift in direction, it provided something equally valuable: confirmation that the industry is focused on scaling deployment and improving safety while leveraging better data to create more capable robotic systems.


Staying close to these conversations helps us better understand customer needs and continue developing technologies that support the future of robotics. The opportunities ahead are significant, and the momentum across the industry is undeniable.
