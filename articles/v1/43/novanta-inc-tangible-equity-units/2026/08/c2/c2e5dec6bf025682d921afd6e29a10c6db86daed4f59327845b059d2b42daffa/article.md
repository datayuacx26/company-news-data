# The Future of Precision Manufacturing Starts with People

When people think about innovation, they often picture breakthrough technologies or next-generation products. What they do not always see is the combination of customer insight, engineering expertise, manufacturing excellence, and collaboration that makes those breakthroughs possible.


Across the industries we serve, requirements are evolving rapidly. The growth of AI infrastructure, advanced electronics, and additive manufacturing is increasing demand for systems that deliver exceptional speed, precision, stability, and reliability. Customers are no longer willing to compromise between performance and accuracy. They expect both.


Recognizing this shift, our teams saw an opportunity to rethink what was possible. Rather than pursuing incremental improvements, they undertook a comprehensive redesign of our flagship[Lightning™ beam steering platform](https://novanta.com/precision-manufacturing/product-category/laser-beam-steering/) . Engineers reimagined key elements of the system across mechanics, optics, electronics, and firmware to create a platform designed for the next generation of customer applications.


A key contributor to this effort has been Brendan Boyd, Beam Steering R&D Manager, and his team, whose work continues to advance beam steering technologies while helping customers meet increasingly demanding performance requirements. Guided by close collaboration with OEMs and end users, the team evaluates emerging technologies and gathers real-world feedback while refining solutions that create[meaningful value for customers](https://novanta.com/about/vision-mission/) .


Innovation also extends far beyond product design. Delivering breakthrough performance consistently requires advanced manufacturing processes, sophisticated testing capabilities, automation, and a relentless focus on quality. Through ongoing investments in these areas, we have built manufacturing capabilities that enable precision and scale.


Andy Eccles, Manufacturing Engineering Manager, and his team play a critical role in turning innovative designs into products that can be built efficiently and consistently. Advanced fixtures, automated assembly systems, enhanced test equipment, and continuously improving processes help ensure every product performs to the highest standards. Equally important, engineers and operators[work closely together](https://novanta.com/careers-culture/life-at-novanta/) to transform practical experience into improvements in efficiency and performance.


This commitment to manufacturing excellence has become a meaningful competitive advantage.


At the same time, we are building the foundation for future growth across our global manufacturing network. Through modular automation platforms, standardized test systems, and the continued expansion of Fusion, our proprietary manufacturing platform, we are creating a global factory approach that supports traceability and operational excellence across regions.


David Keng, Senior Manager, Sustaining & Advanced Engineering, and his automation and test engineering team are helping drive this work by developing scalable automation and digital infrastructure that can be deployed across our manufacturing network. Fusion provides a common digital framework that connects production and test data, improves visibility, accelerates problem-solving, and enables innovations developed in one location to be adopted more quickly across the organization.


What makes all of this possible is our people. Engineers, manufacturing specialists, automation experts, operators, quality professionals, and operations teams work together to solve complex challenges and support one another beyond traditional organizational boundaries.


The result is more than a new platform or manufacturing capability. It is[a stronger foundation for long-term innovation.](https://novanta.com/precision-manufacturing/)


As we continue strengthening capabilities, we remain focused on investing in what matters most: technical expertise, customer partnership, manufacturing excellence, digital innovation, and a culture of collaboration.


Technology may be the outcome, but people make innovation possible. Together, they are helping shape the future of[precision manufacturing](https://novanta.com/capabilities/precision-manufacturing/) and building the foundation for our next chapter of growth.


***By Jens Hupkau,*** ***VP of Engineering***
