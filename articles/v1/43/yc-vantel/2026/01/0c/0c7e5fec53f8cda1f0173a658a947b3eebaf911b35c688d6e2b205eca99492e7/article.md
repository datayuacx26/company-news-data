# Vantel Joins Brokerslink: Empowering the Global Broking Community with AI - Vantel

The commercial insurance landscape is becoming increasingly complex. Brokers are often buried under a mountain of paperwork: comparing quotes, searching for client information, processing hundreds of emails daily. This manual workload doesn’t just take time; it creates room for error and distracts from what brokers do best: managing risk and advising clients.


The Vantel platform was built to change that. By joining the Brokerslink network, we are bringing our tools for workflow automation and document intelligence to a global audience. Our technology allows teams to:


-


**Rapidly compare quotes** with precision.


-


**Identify coverage gaps** in seconds rather than hours.


-


**Analyze complex policies** to ensure the best possible client outcomes.


-


**Focus on strategy** , reallocating time toward building deeper client relationships and specialized risk management.
