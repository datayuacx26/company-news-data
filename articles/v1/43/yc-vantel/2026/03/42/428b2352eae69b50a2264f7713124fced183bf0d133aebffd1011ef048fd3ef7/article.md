# RENOMIA partners with Vantel to bring AI‑powered workflows to Central & Eastern Europe - Vantel

RENOMIA partners with Vantel to bring AI‑powered workflows to Central & Eastern Europe.


As one of the region’s leading commercial insurance brokerages, managing over €1B in premium, RENOMIA has a track record of investing early in what works. This is true for AI as well.


The partnership focuses on AI‑assisted quote comparison, helping brokers work faster and more consistently across markets while bringing more value and dedicated time to clients.


As Daniel Kohout, Head of Operations at RENOMIA, puts it:


> AI‑powered tools are a natural next step in how we serve clients across countries. Working closely with business colleagues and technology leaders we have built a dedicated AI team to ensure that AI brings value first to our clients. Our project with Vantel is one of many AI initiatives.


The Vantel team is excited about the partnership.
