# Notion Capital selected Vantel among 200,000 European B2B software companies for their annual Cloud Challengers report - Vantel

Vantel has been named one of Europe's Top 100 Cloud Challengers for 2026 by[Notion Capital](https://www.notioncapital.com/report/cloud-challengers-2026) .


Each year, Notion Capital screens roughly 200,000 European B2B software companies to identify the 100 most promising seed-stage businesses on the continent. The report, now in its fifth year, has historically captured every B2B European unicorn that fits its methodology, including Synthesia, Lovable, and Aikido Security before they broke out. Companies on the list have a 3x higher chance of raising a follow-on round.


Vantel builds the intelligence layer for commercial insurance brokers, turning unstructured documents into structured coverage comparisons so brokers can advise faster and with more confidence. We're an agile, Y Combinator-backed team, and being recognized alongside some of Europe's strongest early-stage companies is meaningful to us.


We're just getting started. If you're interested in what we're building,[you can learn more here](https://www.vantel.com/) .
