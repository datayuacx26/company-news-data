# Vantel raises $2M Seed from Y Combinator, Inception Fund, Rebel Fund & top Angels - Vantel

What makes this round special is the mix of perspectives behind it. Our angels include founders, AI experts, and insurance insiders who understand both the technical complexity and the operational reality of commercial insurance. Among them are Lukas Saari, co founder and CEO of Tandem Health, Axel Ericsson, Thiel fellow and co founder of Callin, and Oliver Jung, one of the most successful angel investors of all time. We’re also fortunate to have angels from institutions like Lloyds of London and Anthropic supporting us. This depth of experience challenges us to build at a higher bar.
