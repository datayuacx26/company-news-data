# Catching what matters before it becomes a claim: How Renewable Guard uses Vantel in real client work - Vantel

The client had already been shown two quotes, compared using Vantel. When the client later requested higher limits and confirmation around sewer backup, Keaton pursued a third market and, at the client’s request, ran it through the tool again.


> “I told the insured I had another quote coming in and they said run it against your tool.”


Without that comparison, the exclusion could have gone unnoticed until after a loss. For a broker, that risk is not abstract.


> “If I did not send that policy comparison in the beginning, and the insured didn’t ask me to run a new report, and they had a loss due to this, I’m out of my job.”
