# Feature Spotlight: Interactive Highlighting & Source Proof - Vantel

We believe that for AI to be useful in insurance, it must be **verifiable.** Every time Vantel extracts a data point, whether it’s a premium amount, a specific exclusion, or a complicated limit, it creates a digital breadcrumb.


-


**Click-to-Source.** Click any value in your Vantel dashboard, and the platform instantly opens the original PDF and highlights the exact sentence where that data originated.


-


**Context Matters.** You don't just see the number; you see the paragraphs surrounding it, ensuring you understand the context of the coverage.


-


**Audit-Proof Workflow.** When your client asks where a specific figure came from, you don't have to go hunting. You show them the source in one click.


We aren't asking you to trust our AI blindly. We’re giving you the tools to give the right advice faster than ever before.
