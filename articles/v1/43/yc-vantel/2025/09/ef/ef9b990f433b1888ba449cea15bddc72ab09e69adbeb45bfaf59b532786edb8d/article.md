# Stop Flipping Tabs: Introducing Vantel Quote Comparison - Vantel

Our new feature allows you to upload multiple quotes and instantly generate a standardized side-by-side comparison:


-


**Standardized Language.** We normalize carrier-specific jargon so you’re comparing apples to apples.


-


**Instant Gap Analysis.** Highlighted differences in deductibles, limits, and exclusions across all quotes.


-


**Export-Ready.** Move from "raw quotes" to a "client-ready presentation" in minutes, not days.


Brokers shouldn't be valued for their data entry skills. They should be valued for their advice. We want to give brokers the time back to actually be brokers, focusing on the human part of the job.
