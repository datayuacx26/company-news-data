- Lifetime Delivery Value with Nash: $3,500,000+

### **The Meat N’ Bone story**


Meat N’ Bone began with a simple problem: premium-quality proteins weren’t easy to access. Founders Gabriel and Luis wanted to combine education, quality, and convenience into a new kind of butcher experience.
‍


> *"We realized there wasn’t a semi-premium player in the space. We wanted to educate the consumer on what they were buying, offer a product they couldn’t get anywhere else, and make it convenient to access." ‍*


With just $10,000, they grew Meat N’ Bone into an $11 million business in five years. Today, they operate seven retail stores in Florida, ship nationwide, and are expanding into major cities like Houston and Austin.
‍


> *"We hand-butcher every single piece we sell. Customers trust us because of consistency. Once they try it, they understand the importance of what they’re putting in their body."*


#### **The challenge**


From the beginning, Meat N’ Bone saw delivery as central to its model.
‍


> *"We started as a delivery company. Same-day and convenience were always part of the vision."*


But scaling delivery wasn’t simple. Managing drivers, insurance, and payroll made expansion into new markets time-consuming and expensive. At the same time, Shopify’s local delivery tools weren’t designed for businesses with perishable, high-value inventory.
‍


> *"When we launched, we had to develop our own widget for delivery. Shopify was light years away from solving this. Local delivery was a challenge." ‍*


#### **Meat N’ Bone + Nash**


Enter Nash. With direct integration into Shopify, Meat N’ Bone could launch delivery in new markets in minutes, without hiring fleets or adding complexity.
‍


*"We had it in our business plan that we’d need to figure out drivers in Tampa. With Nash, we just turned it on. Within minutes, we were delivering in a whole new market." ‍*


How it works:


- Customers place an order through the Shopify site.
- At checkout, they see local delivery options with date and time windows.
- Nash quotes directly in the platform and dispatches a driver.
- Customers receive tracking links; the Meat N’ Bone team monitors everything in one dashboard.
‍


*"The funnel is pretty simple. Customer inputs their address, gets delivery options, and Nash handles the rest. It’s been a proven model in all our markets." ‍*
