# Say Hello to Nash Pick & Deliver: The Smarter Way to Scale First-Party Grocery Delivery

Delivery has become the fastest-growing channel in grocery. In May 2025, online grocery delivery sales hit $3.9 billion… a 70% year-over-year increase, while overall grocery sales grew just 3%. Delivery now represents 45.4% of all eGrocery sales, and leaders like Walmart and Amazon have raised expectations for speed, accuracy, and reliability.[(Brick Meets Click) ‍](https://www.brickmeetsclick.com/insights/unpacking-may-2025-egrocery-performance-key-insights-for-retail-leadership)


For regional and enterprise grocers, this shift is both a challenge and an opportunity. Customers are making delivery their *primary* way of engaging with grocery brands, but most store teams are already stretched thin. Hiring to peak demand is expensive, and without flexible item-level picking and delivery, first-party programs risk breaking down at the busy moments when customers rely on them most.
‍


## That’s why Nash launched our brand new **Pick & Deliver** product.


**What Pick & Deliver solves**


Many grocers face the same bottlenecks:


- Store teams overwhelmed during promotions, weekends, or poor weather
- Long staging queues that delay orders and miss promised ETAs
- Labor costs that rise when too many associates are pulled into picking
- Customer dissatisfaction when substitutions are inaccurate or items are missing


Pick & Deliver solves these problems by adding[courier](https://www.nash.ai/glossary/courier) -led picking and delivery capacity. It allows grocers to handle spikes without extra headcount and to maintain order accuracy without overloading store teams.
‍


### **How Pick & Deliver works**


Nash synchronizes store catalogs and inventory, then enables trusted third-party service providers to complete guided picking with barcode scans,[substitution](https://www.nash.ai/glossary/substitution) rules, and location hints. Once the order is ready, the same courier transitions directly to delivery.
‍


This eliminates handoffs, reduces idle staging, and keeps accountability with one courier from shelf to doorstep. Grocers and customers both get real-time status updates, while headquarters sees a unified audit trail across providers. All through a single integration.
‍
