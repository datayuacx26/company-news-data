# Dynamic Dispatch with Velocity


May 18, 2025


Our Dynamic Dispatch tool now evaluates allocation decisions at a deeper level than ever before. These powerful new optimization options give you unprecedented control over how deliveries are assigned and prioritized across your provider network.


‍


##### Dispatch with Door-to-Door Velocity


Automatically allocate more jobs to your fastest providers for time-sensitive deliveries. Our system now intelligently prioritizes actual performance over provider estimated arrival times, ensuring the quickest possible deliveries for on-demand use cases.


‍


##### Precision Timing for Catering


Optimize catering deliveries with our new "just right" timing algorithm that rewards providers who arrive neither too early nor too late with more job allocations. Perfect for when timing is critical and food needs to arrive exactly when expected.


‍


##### Customizable Priority Balance


Configure the relative importance of ETA adherence versus delivery velocity in your allocation algorithm. Specify precise ratios (50/50, 20/80, etc.) to align provider performance metrics with your business objectives and service level agreements.


‍


##### Personalized Scoring System


Create your own scoring criteria based on the delivery milestones that matter most to your business:


- Penalize late pickups
- Add consequences for inaccurate estimates (early or late)
- Define custom rules to measure provider performance your way
