# Qureight Raises $20M Series B for AI Clinical Imaging

*Fundraise Insider tracks newly funded startups each week and delivers verified[B2B leads list](https://fundraiseinsider.com/) of C-suite contacts straight to your inbox, so you reach the right people while the funding is still fresh.[See pricing.](https://fundraiseinsider.com/pricing)*


Qureight, a Cambridge company building an[AI](https://fundraiseinsider.com/blog/ai-startups/) imaging platform for clinical trials, has closed a $20 million[Series B](https://fundraiseinsider.com/blog/series-b-startups/) round led by Molten Ventures. The financing was announced on July 29, 2026.


Existing investors Hargreave Hale AIM VCT, XTX Ventures, Guinness Ventures, Meltwind and Ascension also took part. Qureight provides imaging and precision endpoints for trials, with a focus on lung and heart disease.


The company plans to expand its 3D deep learning imaging portfolio and grow commercially. Part of the money will go toward building an AI imaging laboratory to house its 3D chest imaging foundation model, which it says cuts both the data and the time needed to develop new disease models.


With the new capital, Qureight aims to bring products to market in areas including asthma, pulmonary hypertension, bronchiectasis and drug induced lung toxicity, adding to its existing work in lung fibrosis.
