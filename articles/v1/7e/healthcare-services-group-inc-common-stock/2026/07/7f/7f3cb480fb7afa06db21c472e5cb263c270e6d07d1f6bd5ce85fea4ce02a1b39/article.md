# Behind Every Compliant Facility is a Well-Trained Team


[Corporate](https://www.hcsgcorp.com/blog/category/business/corporate/) ,


[Training](https://www.hcsgcorp.com/blog/category/training/)


Healthcare is one of the most highly regulated industries, and keeping up with evolving standards requires more than ‘checking the box’ with annual training. Regulations change, expectations shift, and organizations must ensure that employees have the knowledge and confidence to deliver safe and high-quality service every day.


With five decades of serving the healthcare industry, Healthcare Services Group (HCSG) understands the importance of remaining vigilant and compliant. As a result, a dedicated Training Department was established to ensure employees remain prepared and give clients peace of mind. Through innovative learning opportunities, ongoing education, and compliance-focused resources, the department helps build confident teams that consistently meet the high standards expected across healthcare environments.


**About HCSG Training Team**


The HCSG Training Department supports approximately 38,000 employees across the organization by developing and maintaining the educational resources that help teams stay informed and prepared in an ever-changing regulatory environment. **Jonathon Flancher, Operations Regulatory Compliance Counsel,** says staying compliant starts with constantly monitoring changes across the healthcare landscape. He explains, ***“Our team continually monitors changes in regulations, pending changes in regulatory updates, individual state requirements, and client requests to ensure our operating procedures and policies remain compliant.”***


On average, the team develops 40-60 new training courses each year, with that number growing to 80 or more during years with major company initiatives or new program launches. By taking a proactive approach to continuously updating training to reflect changing regulations, the department helps ensure employees have the knowledge to deliver high-quality service.


**Meeting Employees Where They Learn Best**


Everyone learns differently, which is why HCSG delivers training through multiple formats designed to support learning across all roles and experience levels. Employees learn through instructor-led education, hands-on training, digital learning modules, educational videos, and ‘Train-the-Trainer’ programs that prepare leaders to coach their own teams. This blended approach allows employees to build knowledge and stay current as requirements evolve.


Keeping employees informed also means delivering training in ways that are timely and accessible. As Jonathon explains, ***“Our learning management system allows us to push out new courses and send out updates in real time whenever we need to.”***


Together, these resources help employees strengthen their knowledge while making learning accessible, engaging, and practical.


**Building Confidence for Clients**


While the Training Department directly serves HCSG employees, its impact extends far beyond training.


When healthcare organizations partner with HCSG, they trust that their support services will be delivered by knowledgeable, well-prepared teams who can adapt to changing regulations and the unique needs of each facility. HCSG develops training and operational procedures that align with each client’s environment, helping facilities remain compliant while supporting consistent, high-quality care. As a result, HCSG partner communities experience reduced compliance risk, improve survey readiness, and high standards across housekeeping, dining, and nutrition services.


**Director of Training Production Emanuel Falade** emphasizes that flexibility is one of the department’s greatest strengths. ***“We handle the unique operational needs of each client. We can customize the training we provide and tailor it to satisfy their requirements.”***


By ensuring employees receive consistent, up-to-date training, the focus has evolved to better support the unique needs of each facility HCSG serves. This commitment allows clients to focus on providing quality care for residents by providing peace of mind knowing their support service teams are backed by trained professionals who are continuously educated and trained.


Training is about building a culture of learning, accountability, and continuous improvement. As regulations evolve and clients’ needs change, HCSG’s Training & Compliance department remains committed to developing resources that help employees grow professionally while supporting operational excellence across every facility we serve.


**Whether through instructor-led education, digital learning, or innovative training tools, HCSG continues to invest in our people because well-trained employees create stronger teams, better outcomes, and greater confidence for every client.
