# Decoda Health Raises $4.5M Seed Round

Healthcare has advanced clinically, but the software powering clinics hasn't kept up. Outdated Electronic Medical Records (EMR) force teams into manual work, fragmentation, and burnout. Clinics need technology that thinks with them, not tools built decades ago.


Today, we’re excited to share that Decoda Health has raised a **$4.5M seed round led by[Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/)** , with participation from[The Fintech Fund](https://www.thefintechfund.com/) ,[Graphene Ventures](https://graphenevc.com/) , and experienced operators across healthcare. This funding allows us to accelerate our mission: building the **AI-native operating system** for modern clinical care.


## **Why Now**


AI is transforming how work gets done. In healthcare, where workflows are dense and interconnected, the gap between AI-powered clinics and those relying on manual operations will widen quickly. Practices can't sustainably hire more administrative staff for every additional patient, they need intelligent systems that automate and coordinate work.


Read more


Administrative costs account for up to 25% of U.S. healthcare spending and physicians spend nearly 6 hours a day in legacy PMSs. AI for complex workflows is only now ready for healthcare, while clinics cannot keep hiring more administrative staff as they grow. The timing could not be better for a platform built from the ground up with intelligent automation.


## **Our Solution**


Decoda is an AI-native Operating System (OS) built around intelligent agents rather than legacy workflows. We started with two core products:


- **[AI Receptionist](https://decodahealth.com/products/ai-front-desk)** that answers calls, books appointments, and updates records automatically
- **[AI Scribe](https://decodahealth.com/products/scribe)** that generates structured clinical notes and extracts key details like procedures and follow ups


From there we built the full set of modules every clinic needs. Decoda includes[scheduling](https://decodahealth.com/products/scheduling) ,[intake and consent forms](https://decodahealth.com/products/forms) , a unified[communication suite](https://decodahealth.com/products/communications) ,[charting and documentation](https://decodahealth.com/products/notes) ,[inventory and checkout](https://decodahealth.com/products/inventory) ,[patient messaging](https://decodahealth.com/products/chat) , and[reporting and analytics](https://decodahealth.com/products/analytics) .


The result is a platform that goes far beyond a simple LLM wrapper. Decoda is already delivering meaningful impact at scale:


- **$20M additional revenue** captured for customers using AI
- Over **5M patient interactions** handled
- **$100M dollars** in payments processed annually
- Live in **150 clinics** across the US


Because these agents and modules live inside the same platform, every workflow becomes connected and actionable.

Examples:


- If a patient calls about a service but doesn't book, Decoda tags them so clinics can follow up.
- If the AI Scribe detects a procedure, the system auto-populates checkout, inventory changes, and future reminders.


This makes Decoda not just any regular PMS - but the operating system for modern clinics.

What happens to my clinic's patient data if I decide to leave Decoda?


Your data is always yours — Decoda does not hold it hostage or charge you to export it. You can take your full patient records with you if you ever decide to leave, which is a commitment many legacy EMR vendors quietly avoid making.


AI-native EMR vs. legacy EMR with AI added on — what's the real difference for a med spa?


A legacy EMR with AI bolted on can only do what its original rigid architecture allows — the AI sits on top of forms and clicks that were never designed for intelligence. An AI-native system like Decoda is built from the ground up so the AI understands context across your entire patient journey, from the first phone call to the follow-up reminder, which produces meaningfully better automation.


How does Decoda handle telehealth and virtual follow-up visits inside the platform?


Decoda lets you configure virtual locations and conduct virtual appointments, such as post-operative follow-up calls, inside the same scheduling and charting environment you use for in-person care. You can also restrict virtual visit types so patients cannot self-book them, keeping that workflow fully staff-controlled.


What is the fastest way to get support when something breaks during a busy clinic day?


Every Decoda customer gets a dedicated Slack channel with a sub-5-minute response time — you can call, text, or message anytime. If a feature exists, the team walks you through it; if a setting needs adjusting, they configure it for you; and if something needs to be built, they scope and ship it in days, not weeks.


Can Decoda's AI receptionist handle calls in languages other than English?


Yes — Decoda's AI front desk operates with multilingual capability, so patients who prefer to communicate in a language other than English are not left stranded. This is especially relevant for elective-care practices in diverse metro markets where a significant share of patients may not be fluent English speakers.


How does the AI scribe protect my practice if a patient disputes what was discussed during a visit?


Because the ambient scribe generates a structured, time-stamped clinical note of what was said and decided during the appointment, you have a reliable record to reference in any dispute. Many providers using Decoda independently discover this as a layer of protection against "he said / she said" situations before the benefit is ever formally pitched to them.


Should I replace all my existing tools at once or can I add Decoda modules gradually?


Decoda is built as a full-stack system where every module shares one patient record, so the compounding benefits come from consolidation — clinics running scheduling, billing, notes, and communications in one place see the biggest gains. That said, Decoda's onboarding team handles the migration over 3 to 4 weeks so the switch does not have to feel like ripping out everything overnight.


How does lab ordering work inside Decoda, and which lab networks does it connect to?


Decoda supports native lab ordering directly within the platform, including a native integration with Quest Diagnostics, so lab orders and results are accessible inside the patient profile without a separate tool or login. This keeps the full clinical picture in one place instead of forcing providers to toggle between systems.


What kind of membership configurations can Decoda actually support for a med spa or weight-loss clinic?


Decoda supports advanced memberships with per-item rollover, tiered discounts, multi-frequency services, and credits, with billing cycles that include 6-week, 10-week, and 12-week options alongside standard cadences — designed specifically for GLP-1, structured wellness, and aesthetic treatment programs. Membership configuration depth is consistently the feature that stops demos cold, with practice owners noting that no other platform they evaluated could match it.


How does Decoda's inventory tracking work for high-volume injectables like Botox units?


Decoda tracks inventory down to the unit and ties deductions directly to patient treatments, so Botox units drawn for a specific patient automatically reduce your stock count without manual entry. You can also take a photo of a packing slip or product box to auto-populate inventory on receipt, which cuts out one of the most tedious parts of restocking.
