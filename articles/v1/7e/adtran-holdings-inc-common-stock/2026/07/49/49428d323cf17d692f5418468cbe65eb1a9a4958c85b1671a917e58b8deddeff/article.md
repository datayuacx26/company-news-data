# Winning the broadband battle with experience-driven services

**The broadband experience gap**


For years, broadband providers measured success by network expansion: Homes passed, premises connected and faster service tiers.


But as fiber deployments expand and Gigabit services become commonplace, connectivity alone is no longer enough to stand out. Speed creates the potential for a great service, but subscribers judge their provider by what they experience after the connection reaches the home.


As Ben Connor of Jackson County REMC explained during a recent Adtran webinar:


“Nobody ever calls in and says 900 Megabits isn’t fast enough. They call in and say my TV buffered, my laptop doesn’t work on the deck, or my video call keeps dropping.”


This is the broadband experience gap: the difference between the service delivered to the home and the experience subscribers receive throughout it.


Wi-Fi coverage, latency, device onboarding, reliability and support now play a major role in customer satisfaction. Closing the gap has therefore become a strategic priority for broadband providers.


**A new operating model: experience-driven services**


This requires a fundamental shift. Experience-driven services focus on outcomes rather than connectivity alone.


Success is measured through service reliability, coverage quality, resolution times, customer satisfaction and long-term retention. Building this model requires providers to:


- Establishing next-gen Wi-Fi 7 and mesh as the in-home foundation
- Use data, visibility and automation to improve operations
- Move from reactive support to AI-driven proactive assurance
- Give subscribers greater control through self-service
- Packaging better experiences into revenue-generating services


Together, these capabilities can transform broadband from a commoditized connection into a proactive, intelligent and personalized service.


**Build the foundation with Wi-Fi 7 and mesh**


As broadband speeds continue to increase, the most significant performance limitations are increasingly found inside the home.


Wi-Fi 7 and intelligent mesh networks provide the foundation for consistent coverage, lower latency and greater reliability across a growing number of connected devices.


As[Christoph Glingner, CTO of Adtran, said](https://www.adtran.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/20260513-adtran-expands-sdg-8700-series-to-deliver-wi-fi-7-at-scale) :


“Service providers are telling us the decision to adopt Wi-Fi 7 has already been made. Competitive pressure is accelerating Wi-Fi 7 standardization, and providers want to deliver high-quality experiences to their full subscriber base, not just premium tiers.”


For latency-sensitive applications such as cloud gaming, immersive entertainment and real-time collaboration, Wi-Fi 7 can deliver more consistent performance. When combined with intelligent mesh nodes, it can also eliminate dead zones and extend reliable connectivity throughout the home and surrounding property.


**Turn visibility and data into strategic assets**


Closing the experience gap requires providers to understand what’s happening inside the subscriber’s home.


Traditional support remains largely reactive. A subscriber experiences a problem, contacts support, and, if the root cause cannot be identified remotely, an uninformed truck roll often follows.


Managed Wi-Fi platforms provide the visibility needed to diagnose issues more quickly and distinguish between problems involving the access network, gateway, Wi-Fi environment or individual subscriber devices.


As an Adtran Managed Wi-Fi customer explained:


“Intellifi provides the **visibility** to **resolve issues faster** , delivering a more secure, seamless experience that drives satisfaction, **loyalty** and l **ower support costs** .”


Open APIs are also critical. They allow experience data to flow into service assurance, customer care, OSS and AI platforms, creating a more unified view of the subscriber.


The visibility enables faster issue resolution, more efficient workflows and fewer unnecessary truck rolls.


> By packaging services around customer outcomes rather than speed alone, providers can turn the subscriber experience into a sustainable growth engine.


**Move from reactive support to proactive experience management**


An experience-driven operating model shifts the focus from troubleshooting symptoms. It identifies root causes and helps prevent issues before subscribers are affected.


By applying AI and analytics to telemetry from the home, access network and service infrastructure, providers can build a unified view of service performance and direct support teams toward an effective resolution.


Adtran’s Mosaic One Clarity combines network and subscriber data to provide predictive insights, guided remediation and proactive assurance. This can reduce troubleshooting time, avoid unnecessary truck rolls and help teams focus resources where they can have the greatest impact.


As[Sean McGrath, GM and CEO of ACE Fiber, said](https://www.adtran.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/20251118-ace-fiber-uses-adtrans-mosaic-one-clarity-ai-solution-for-proactive-assurance) :


“With Adtran’s Mosaic One Clarity, we can anticipate issues and resolve them before they disrupt service. That shift means fewer on-site visits, faster fault resolution and greater reliability for our customers.”


**Empower subscribers through self-service**


Self-service is another essential part of closing the experience gap.


Modern subscriber applications can simplify gateway and mesh setup, display network health, help optimize coverage, manage guest access and provide services such as parental controls and cybersecurity.


Giving subscribers greater visibility and control improves the customer experience while reducing operational costs.


**Turn experience into revenue and retention**


As competition increases, providers need new ways to grow revenue and differentiate beyond faster service tiers.


Experience-based bundles can create recurring revenue while strengthening customer loyalty. These services can be tailored around subscriber needs and personas.


Families may value whole-home coverage, parental controls and cybersecurity. Gamers and remote workers may pay more for multigigabit services, Wi-Fi 7 and more consistent low-latency performance. Mesh Wi-Fi can be offered as a value-added service for larger homes or properties with difficult coverage areas indoors and outdoors.


By packaging services around customer outcomes rather than speed alone, providers can improve satisfaction, reduce churn and turn the subscriber experience into a sustainable growth engine.


**Experience is the new competitive advantage**


The broadband market is entering a new phase of competition.


Providers were once differentiated primarily through network reach and speed. Today, those capabilities are increasingly expected. The next competitive advantage will come from using advanced Wi-Fi technologies, data intelligence, automation and AI to deliver personalized, proactive and continuously optimized services.


The question is no longer whether providers should adopt an experience-driven services model. It is how quickly they can close the experience gap.


Connectivity brings customers onto the network. Experience is what keeps them there.
