# Stefan Voll discusses AI-driven data center investment and connectivity opportunities at ANGA COM 2026

- Speakers: Stefan Voll, senior director of business development at Adtran
- Topics: AI-driven data center investment: New connectivity demands and opportunities for European service providers
- When: Tuesday, May 19, 3:45 p.m. CET
- Where: ANGA COM 2026, Koelnmesse, Cologne, Germany[https://angacom.de/startseite](https://angacom.de/startseite)


Investment in AI infrastructure is driving significant growth in data center capacity, with increasing demand for high-performance connectivity between hyperscale, metro and edge locations. These shifts are changing how networks are designed, requiring greater scalability and support for large and dynamic data flows across distributed environments.


In this session, Stefan Voll will present on how AI-driven data center investment is creating new connectivity requirements and opportunities for European service providers. The discussion will focus on evolving traffic patterns, implications for network architecture and the role of service providers in supporting data center interconnect and cloud access. Attendees will gain insight into how these trends are shaping infrastructure development across the region.
