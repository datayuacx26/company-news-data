# Paul Breakman discusses strategies for identifying network coverage gaps at Fiber Connect 2026

- Speakers: Paul Breakman, VP of Americas partners and business development at Adtran; Ron Westfall, VP and practice leader for infrastructure and networking at HyperFRAME Research (moderator); Patrick Mulhearn, director of government affairs at Ripple Fiber; Lauren Marcucci, senior project manager at BHC; Cole McAndrew, senior policy analyst at the Virginia Office of Broadband
- Topics: Identifying gaps in existing network footprints
- When: Monday, May 18, 1 p.m. ET
- Where: Fiber Connect 2026, Gaylord Palms Resort & Convention Center, Kissimmee, Florida, USA[https://fiberconnect.fiberbroadband.org](https://fiberconnect.fiberbroadband.org/)


Even the most advanced fiber networks can contain hidden coverage gaps that leave some communities underserved and limit growth potential. These blind spots impact service quality and can restrict opportunities for expansion if they are not addressed through more accurate planning and analysis.


In this session, Paul Breakman will join a panel of industry leaders to discuss strategies for identifying gaps in existing network footprints. The discussion will cover how providers are using data analysis, mapping techniques and community input to uncover underserved areas and inform planning decisions. Attendees will gain practical insight into approaches that support more effective expansion while maximizing return on investment.
