# Greg Luhman discusses how AI can act as a force multiplier for broadband providers at Core Tel Forum 2026

- Speakers: Greg Luhman, business development manager at Adtran
- Topics: AI and your broadband network
- When: Thursday, August 20, 10:10 a.m. CDT
- Where: Core Tel Forum 2026, Brainerd, Minnesota, USA[https://www.coretelecom.net/tel-forums](https://www.coretelecom.net/tel-forums)


Artificial intelligence is rapidly moving from experimentation to everyday use, influencing how people work, learn and make decisions. As AI tools become more capable and accessible, service providers are exploring new ways to apply them across their organizations to improve efficiency, accelerate decision-making and simplify routine tasks.


In this session, Greg Luhman will explore the growing role of AI in broadband operations and discuss how emerging tools can help service providers work more effectively. From answering questions and assisting with everyday tasks to supporting network management and operational workflows, he will examine how AI can act as a force multiplier for teams responsible for the care and operation of broadband networks. Attendees will gain insight into practical applications of AI and the opportunities it presents for service providers.
