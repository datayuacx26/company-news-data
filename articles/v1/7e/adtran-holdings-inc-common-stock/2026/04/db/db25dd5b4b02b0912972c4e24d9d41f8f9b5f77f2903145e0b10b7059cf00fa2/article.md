# Stefano Fogli joins FTTH Conference 2026 workshop on the impact of the Digital Networks Act and Cybersecurity Act

- Speakers: Stefano Fogli, director of technology and solutions at Adtran and VP of strategy at FTTH Council Europe; Feyo Sickinghe, principal regulatory counsel at Bird & Bird; Ingrid Malfait-Guilbaud, director of EU affairs at Iliad; Lucrezia Busa, head of unit at DG Connect, European Commission; Rory Ardagh, director at Enet
- Topics: DNA impact – standardized wholesale and Cyber Security Act
- When: Tuesday, April 14, 11:30 a.m. BST
- Where: FTTH Conference 2026, Excel London, London, UK[https://ftthconference.eu](https://ftthconference.eu/)


The Cybersecurity Act is raising important questions for Europe’s fixed fiber networks, particularly around security expectations, technology choices and long-term resilience. As discussions continue at EU level, cybersecurity considerations are increasingly part of technology and network planning conversations.


In this session, Stefano Fogli will share Adtran’s perspective as a European vendor, focusing on technology and network considerations related to the Cybersecurity Act and its potential relevance for fixed fiber networks.
