## What Are Metal Protection Fluids?


Metal protection fluids are specially formulated to prevent corrosion by forming a barrier that blocks moisture, air, and other corrosive elements from reaching the metal surface. They're used in nearly every stage of the metal lifecycle:


- Post-machining
- Pre-shipping
- During storage
- Between manufacturing steps


These fluids can be oil-based, water-dilutable, or solvent-based, depending on the application. Many modern options, like[FERROCOTE® 5856 BF](https://store.quakerhoughton.com/ferrocote-5856-bf-t1-na) , are barium-free to meet evolving health, safety, and environmental standards.


## Why Corrosion Preventatives Matter


Corrosion can lead to costly part failure, safety concerns, and rejected components. Using the right protection fluid helps:


- Maintain product quality and appearance
- Ensure readiness for final assembly or coating
- Reduce rework and scrap rates
- Protect parts during international or long-term storage


Whether you're protecting a pallet of castings or individual CNC-machined parts, corrosion preventatives are essential.
