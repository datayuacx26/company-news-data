**About PACS™**


PACS Group, Inc. is a holding company investing in post-acute healthcare facilities, professionals, and ancillary services. Founded in 2013, PACS Group is one of the largest post-acute platforms in the United States. Its independent subsidiaries operate 324 post-acute care facilities across 17 states serving over 31,900 patients daily.
