# Multiple Birth: Planning for Triplets and Beyond

## What is a Multiple Birth?


A multiple birth refers to the delivery of three or more babies. Like twin births, multiple births are considered high-risk and typically require more medical intervention, including the likelihood of a scheduled C-section.


### Planning for a Multiple Birth


If you're expecting triplets or more, your birth plan should outline your preferences for pain relief, delivery methods, and the medical care each baby will need immediately after birth. Multiple births often occur earlier than single births, so discuss the likelihood of a premature delivery with your provider.
