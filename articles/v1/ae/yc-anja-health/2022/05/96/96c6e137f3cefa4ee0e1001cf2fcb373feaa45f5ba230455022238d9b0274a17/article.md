Anja Health also offers lifetime storage plans for families who want long-term peace of mind. Parents can choose between flexible 47-month interest-free payment plans or paying 100% upfront, depending on what works best for their family.


Beyond pricing, Anja Health focuses on a high-quality banking experience from start to finish. Our collection kit is delivered before birth, collected by your healthcare provider, and shipped to our experienced lab in New Jersey for processing and cryostorage. We work with a lab that has over 40 years of experience in blood processing, and our lab director is an AABB accreditor, helping ensure a careful and well-run operation.


All monthly payment plans start in the second month after delivery. The enrollment fee is $199 at checkout. An initial collection fee is due at the time of birth: $300 for cord blood, $400 for cord blood and cord tissue, and $500 for cord blood, cord tissue, and placenta. No hidden fees.
