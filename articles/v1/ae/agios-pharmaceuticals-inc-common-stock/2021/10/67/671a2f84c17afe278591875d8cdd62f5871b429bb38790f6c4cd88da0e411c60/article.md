# Our Approach to Partnering with Patients on CheatCodes: A Sickle Cell Podcast

Our Head of Patient Advocacy, Holly John, and our then-Chief Commercial Officer, Darrin Miles, were guests on Cheat Codes: A Sickle Cell Podcast. In this episode, they share more about why they were drawn to work with and on behalf of people with sickle cell disease, their passion for improving health equity, and Agios’ approach to partnering with patient communities. Holly and Darrin were interviewed by special guest host and patient advocate Blaze Eppinger.
