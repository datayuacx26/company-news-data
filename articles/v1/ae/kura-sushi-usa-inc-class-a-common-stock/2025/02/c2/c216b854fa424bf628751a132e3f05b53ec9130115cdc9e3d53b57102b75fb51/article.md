# ESCAPE TO ISLAND VIBES WITH KURA SUSHI’S PARADISE POKE


Tropical flavors await with Kura Sushi’s limited-time Paradise Poke and "Fly to Paradise" sweepstakes


February 5, 2025


[Kura Sushi USA, Inc.](https://kurasushi.com/) , a trailblazing leader in technology-driven dining experiences and authentic Japanese cuisine, **invites guests to sway into an island state of mind as the brand announces today its latest limited-time offering:**[Paradise Poke.](https://kurasushi.com/paradise-poke) Available from Feb. 5 through March 12, 2025, Paradise Poke combines the perfect blend of high-quality fish, a flavorful marinade and tropical inspiration to create a vibrant dish that bursts with umami goodness in every bite.


Crafted with tender slices of salmon and tuna, real snow crab meat, creamy avocado, kazunoko and green onion, Paradise Poke is served over Kura’s specialty sushi rice and drizzled with poke inspired ishizawa sauce. This refreshing and savory creation is available at Kura Revolving Sushi Bar locations nationwide for dine-in only, priced at $11.50 while supplies last.


“At Kura Sushi, we’re committed to creating experiential dining moments that captivate and delight our guests,” said Newton Hoang, Vice President, Head of Marketing at Kura Sushi USA. “Paradise Poke captures the spirit of adventure and indulgence with its island-inspired flavors, providing a taste of paradise while delivering the exceptional quality and authentic flavors that define Kura Sushi.”


**To complement this limited-time dish, Kura Sushi invites guests to participate in the "Fly to Paradise" sweepstakes. From Feb. 5 to Feb. 17, guests who dine in and spend $65.00 or more will receive an entry form for a chance to win a grand prize of four round-trip tickets to Oahu, Hawaii, valued at up to $4,999.00.** For Official Rules of the sweepstakes, visit[kurasushi.com/flytoparadisesweepstakes](https://kurasushi.com/flytoparadisesweepstakes) . Entries must be postmarked by February 17, 2025. No purchase necessary to win, see Official Rules for alternative method of entry. The sweepstakes is void where prohibited.


**Rounding out the sweet specials this month, Kura Sushi Rewards Members can enjoy a unique Valentine’s Week promotion. From Feb. 9 to Feb. 16, members who spend $15.00 or more in-restaurant will receive a complimentary dessert, making this the perfect time to celebrate with loved ones.**


For more information and to find a location near you, visit[kurasushi.com](https://kurasushi.com/) and follow Kura Sushi USA on[Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/kurasushiusa) ,[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/kurasushi_usa/) and[Tiktok](https://www.tiktok.com/@kurasushiusa) .


**About Kura Sushi USA, Inc.**


Kura Sushi USA, Inc. is the largest revolving sushi bar chain stateside renowned for its trailblazing technology-driven eater-tainment experiences and authentic Japanese cuisine with 70 locations in 20 states and Washington D.C. Utilizing advanced technology to enhance service speed and accuracy, guests have a wide variety of more than 100 menu items at their fingertips via conveyor belt service. Kura Sushi USA, Inc. is dedicated to serving REAL ingredients free of artificial colorings, preservatives, seasonings and sweeteners. Kura Sushi USA, Inc. was established in 2008 as a subsidiary of Kura Sushi, Inc., a Japan-based revolving sushi chain with more than 600 restaurants internationally and 45 years of brand history.
