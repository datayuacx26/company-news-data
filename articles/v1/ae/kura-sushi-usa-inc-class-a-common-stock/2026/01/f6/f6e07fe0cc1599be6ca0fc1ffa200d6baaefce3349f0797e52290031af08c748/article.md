# Ring In the New Year with Matcha at Kura Revolving Sushi Bar

# Ring In the New Year with Matcha at Kura Revolving Sushi Bar


Enjoy a flavorful start to 2026 with Uji matcha Kura Reserve menu items


January 9, 2026


[Kura Sushi USA, Inc.](https://kurasushi.com/) , the nation's largest brand of revolving sushi restaurants, is celebrating the new year and winter season with a Kura Reserve limited-time menu designed to bring a sense of calm and indulgence with earthy flavors of Japanese matcha from the Uji region of Kyoto, Japan. Six matcha-infused dishes and beverages are now available at all 83 Kura Revolving Sushi Bar locations, while supplies last.


Savor something a little greener with these comforting dishes:


- **Matcha Seared Scallop:** Seared Hokkaido scallops topped with house made Uji matcha mayo and matcha furikake on Kura specialty rice.
- **Matcha Mayo Caterpillar Roll:** Roasted eel and cucumber wrapped in dried seaweed and Kura specialty rice topped with house made Uji matcha mayo and matcha furikake.
- **Matcha Umami Oil Salmon:** Norwegian salmon dressed with house made Uji matcha umami oil on Kura specialty rice garnished with microgreens.
- **Matcha Umami Oil Sea Bream:** Imported sea bream from Ehime and Kochi, Japan prefectures dressed with house made Uji matcha umami oil on Kura specialty rice garnished with microgreens.
- **Kyoto Style Matcha Sundae:** Uji matcha ice cream from Japan paired with warabimochi, red bean paste, strawberries and house made matcha sauce.


Sip away on a fruity, sparkling green tea:


- **Sparkling Uji Matcha White Strawberry:** Carbonated water with organic cane sugar, Uji matcha, white strawberry puree and green tea.


“Get ready to elevate your sushi experience with Uji matcha, a premier ingredient of the highest-grade quality sourced from the Uji region of Kyoto,” said Newton Hoang, Vice President of Marketing at Kura Sushi USA, Inc. “This January, we are excited to start the new year with flair as we’re adding a combination of matcha flavored nigiri sushi, as well as a dessert and sparkling beverage selection to our Kura Reserve menu lineup.”


These authentically vibrant selects join Kura Reserve Standard nigiri sushi favorites **Bluefin Tuna Otoro** --tender bluefin tuna belly and **Kumamoto Wagyu** --marbled Japanese wagyu beef topped with garlic chips, making this Kura Reserve January menu an absolute delicacy.


Kura Reserve is a limited-time culinary experience platform that features seasonally inspired creations and complements Kura Sushi’s always-on menu. These unique menu selections are available for dine-in only starting Jan. 9, while supplies last.


For more information about Kura Reserve’s limited-time Uji matcha menu and to make a reservation at participating restaurants, visit[kurasushi.com](https://kurasushi.com/) and download the[Kura Sushi Rewards app](https://kurasushi.com/rewards) to roll into delicious loyalty perks.


**About Kura Sushi USA, Inc.**


Kura Sushi USA, Inc. is the nation’s largest brand of enjoyably fun revolving sushi restaurants, renowned for its trailblazing technology-driven dining experience and authentic Japanese cuisine with more than 80 locations in 22 states and Washington D.C. Utilizing advanced technology to enhance service speed and accuracy, guests have a wide variety of more than 100 menu items at their fingertips via conveyor belt service inclusive of sushi, sashimi, tempura, noodles, desserts, fountain drinks, sake, beer, and more. Kura Sushi USA, Inc. is dedicated to innovating its menu and guest experience, serving fresh, high-quality ingredients free of artificial colorings, preservatives, seasonings and sweeteners. Kura Sushi USA, Inc. was established in 2008 as a subsidiary of Kura Sushi, Inc., a Japan-based revolving sushi chain with more than 600 restaurants internationally and 45 years of brand history.
