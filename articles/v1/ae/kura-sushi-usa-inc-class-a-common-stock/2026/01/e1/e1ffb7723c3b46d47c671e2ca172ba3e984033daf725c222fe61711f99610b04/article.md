# Kura Sushi USA, Inc. Unveils New Perfect Pair Lunch Combo


Leading revolving sushi restaurant chain offers limited-time weekday special featuring select ramen and hand roll combinations for $13.75 at participating locations


January 20, 2026


[Kura Sushi USA, Inc.](https://kurasushi.com/) , the nation's largest brand of revolving sushi restaurants, is providing warmth and comfort this winter with the return of its Perfect Pair limited-time lunch combo available at participating Kura Revolving Sushi Bar locations exclusively for dine-in only from Jan. 20 through Mar. 31.


Guests can choose their hearty and flavorsome Japanese lunch with 9 Perfect Pair available options that include pairing a select ramen*— **Shoyu Ramen, Tantanmen, Tonkotsu Ramen** and a select hand roll— **Crunchy Hand Roll, Spicy Patagonian Scallop Hand Roll, Spicy Tuna Hand Roll** during the chilly months for only $13.75 plus applicable tax Monday through Friday from open to 5 p.m. local time.


“We’re excited to bring back last year’s highly successful Perfect Pair lunch combo and deliver an unbeatable flavor experience that features our steaming ramen noodles and crisp hand rolls,” said Newton Hoang, Vice President of Marketing at Kura Sushi USA, Inc. “This limited-time special is the ultimate way to enjoy a satisfying, high-quality meal at an incredible value. Nowhere currently can you get a quality lunch offering like this at this price.”


Perfect Pair is a limited-time weekday lunch combo drawn from Kura Sushi’s always-on menu that serves to provide a hearty and delicious dining combination for an irresistible price.


For more information about the limited-time Perfect Pair lunch combo and to make a reservation at participating restaurants, visit[kurasushi.com](https://kurasushi.com/) and download the[Kura Sushi Rewards app](https://kurasushi.com/rewards) to roll into delicious loyalty perks.


**Availability may differ by location and is not guaranteed. Kura Revolving Sushi Bar in Salt Lake City, Utah does not currently serve ramen options; will offer Perfect Pair with udon variety—Beef Udon, Kitsune Udon, Shrimp Tempura Udon. Kura Revolving Sushi Bar in Cerritos, Calif. will not offer Shoyu Ramen.*


**About Kura Sushi USA, Inc.**


Kura Sushi USA, Inc. is the nation’s largest brand of enjoyably fun revolving sushi restaurants, renowned for its trailblazing technology-driven dining experience and authentic Japanese cuisine with more than 80 locations in 22 states and Washington D.C. Utilizing advanced technology to enhance service speed and accuracy, guests have a wide variety of more than 100 menu items at their fingertips via conveyor belt service inclusive of sushi, sashimi, tempura, noodles, desserts, fountain drinks, sake, beer, and more. Kura Sushi USA, Inc. is dedicated to innovating its menu and guest experience, serving fresh, high-quality ingredients free of artificial colorings, preservatives, seasonings and sweeteners. Kura Sushi USA, Inc. was established in 2008 as a subsidiary of Kura Sushi, Inc., a Japan-based revolving sushi chain with more than 600 restaurants internationally and 45 years of brand history.
