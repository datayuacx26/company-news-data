# The Ultimate Summer Comfort: Kura Sushi USA Launches Tonkotsu Mazemen Packed with Umami Flavor on the Express Lane


Experience the made to mix savory brothless ramen available for a limited time at Kura Revolving Sushi Bar


May 13, 2026


Unlock new umami flavor![Kura Sushi USA, Inc.](https://kurasushi.com/) , the nation's largest brand of revolving sushi restaurants, is adding a savory brothless ramen to indulge this summer with the limited-time **Tonkotsu Mazemen** now available at participating Kura Revolving Sushi Bar locations.


The dish, best enjoyed well-mixed, includes tender braised chashu pork, crispy chicken, a soft-boiled egg marinated in a salty-sweet soy sauce blend (ajitama), green onion, fried red onions, tempura flakes, ramen noodles, and tonkotsu sauce.


"Our new Tonkotsu Mazemen leans into everything ramen enthusiasts will appreciate and features a robust blend of ingredients designed to deliver a complete and satisfying meal without the heat,” said Newton Hoang, Vice President of Marketing at Kura Sushi USA, Inc. “Get ready to unlock a whole new level of deliciousness and dive into this incredible brothless ramen combination of braised pork, crispy chicken, and our special tonkotsu sauce.”


The new Tonkotsu Mazemen is available now through July 31, 2026, at participating locations nationwide.


For those who can take the heat and favor traditional ramen bowls with broth, **Miso Ramen, Shoyu Ramen, Tantanmen, and Tonkotsu Ramen are available on Kura Revolving Sushi Bar’s everyday menu to enjoy.**


Kura Sushi USA is committed to quality and menu innovation valuing authentic Japanese craftsmanship and serving thoughtfully sourced ingredients, providing guests with opportunities to explore new tastes.
