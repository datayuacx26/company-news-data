# Kura Sushi USA Delivers a Burst of Sunshine with a Curated Zesty Yuzu Menu


Starting July 10, premium culinary series Kura Reserve returns with tangy Japanese citrus flavors


July 10, 2026


[Kura Sushi USA, Inc.](https://kurasushi.com/) , the nation's largest brand of revolving sushi restaurants, announces the launch of four Kura Reserve limited-time menu items infused with authentic Japanese yuzu now available at participating Kura Revolving Sushi Bar locations, while supplies last. This curated collection of fresh ingredients sourced from Japan enhanced with tangy, citrus flavor shines bright for a refreshing summer escape.


**A Burst of Sunshine with Every Bite:**


- **Seared Yuzu Jalapeño Alfonsino (Kinmedai):** Enjoy the rich and buttery texture of Alfonsino (Kinmedai) from Kagoshima, Japan. Each slice is seared, topped with yuzu jalapeño and layered over Kura specialty rice.
- **Seared Yuzu Dashi Oil Squid:** House-made yuzu dashi oil drizzled over seared squid from Aomori, Japan topped with bonito flakes layered over Kura specialty rice.
- **Yuzu Dashi Oil Yellowtail:** Decadent cuts of yellowtail from Kagoshima, Japan drizzled in house-made yuzu dashi oil, topped with bonito flakes and layered over Kura specialty rice.
- **Yuzu Honey Jelly Parfait:** Tangy yuzu jelly, creamy vanilla ice cream, and sweet frozen strawberry, topped with a drizzle of honey.


“Summer is all about fresh and vibrant energy, and that is exactly what this new yuzu-themed Kura Reserve menu brings to our restaurants,” said Newton Hoang, Vice President of Marketing at Kura Sushi USA, Inc. “We meticulously balanced the acidity of the fruit with rich, premium cuts of fish to create an unforgettable flavor experience that our guests cannot find anywhere else.“


Rounding out this limited-time menu are **Seared Squid nigiri** —lightly salted seared tender squid with a lemon wedge, and Kura Reserve Standard nigiri sushi favorites **Bluefin Tuna Otoro** —tender bluefin tuna belly and **Kumamoto Wagyu** —marbled Japanese wagyu beef topped with garlic chips.


Kura Reserve is a limited-time culinary experience platform that features seasonally inspired creations and complements Kura Sushi’s always-on menu. These unique menu selections are available for dine-in only starting Jul. 10, while supplies last.


For more information about the Kura Reserve limited-time menu and to make a reservation at participating Kura Revolving Sushi Bar restaurants, visit[kurasushi.com](https://kurasushi.com/) and download the[Kura Sushi Rewards app](https://kurasushi.com/rewards) to roll into delicious loyalty perks.


**About Kura Sushi USA, Inc.**


Kura Sushi USA, Inc. is the nation’s largest brand of revolving sushi restaurants that serves authentic Japanese cuisine and delivers a joyful dining experience with more than 90 locations in 24 states and Washington D.C. Utilizing advanced innovation to enhance service speed and accuracy, guests have a wide variety of more than 100 menu items at their fingertips via conveyor belt service inclusive of sushi, sashimi, tempura, noodles, desserts, and more, made with high-quality ingredients free of artificial colorings, preservatives, seasonings and sweeteners. Kura Sushi USA, Inc. was established in 2008 as a subsidiary of Kura Sushi, Inc., a Japan-based revolving sushi chain with more than 600 restaurants internationally and 45 years of brand history.
