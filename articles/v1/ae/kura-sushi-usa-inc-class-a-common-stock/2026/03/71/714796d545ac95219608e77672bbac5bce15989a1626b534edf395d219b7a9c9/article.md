# Kura Sushi USA to Announce Fiscal Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results on April 7, 2026

[PDF Version](https://ir.kurausa.com/node/9156/pdf)


### The Company to Participate in the 38th Annual Roth Conference on March 23-24, 2026


IRVINE, Calif., March 17, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Kura Sushi USA, Inc. (“Kura Sushi” or the “Company”), (NASDAQ: KRUS), a technology-enabled Japanese restaurant concept, today announced that it will host a conference call to discuss fiscal second quarter 2026 financial results on Tuesday, April 7, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. ET. A press release with fiscal second quarter 2026 financial results will be issued that same day after the market closes. Hosting the conference call and webcast will be Hajime “Jimmy” Uba, President and Chief Executive Officer, Jeff Uttz, Chief Financial Officer, and Benjamin Porten, SVP Investor Relations & System Development.


Interested parties may listen to the conference call via telephone by dialing 201-689-8471. A telephone replay will be available shortly after the call has concluded and can be accessed by dialing 412-317-6671; the passcode is 13759165.


The webcast will be available at[www.kurasushi.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=zRr_wmsdot1hO8ybkXYnsAL9gmlLbAh9AJg-Uloo_rGPYthvC-WxnchaNX6qOtGg2OWlip4fo8oThpUpYfR2q0spt9iIcAs4jfBaur2AErs=) under the Investor Relations section and will be archived on the site shortly after the call has concluded.


**Investor Conference Participation**

**About Kura Sushi USA, Inc.**
Kura Sushi USA, Inc. is a technology-enabled Japanese restaurant concept with 85 locations across 22 states and Washington DC. The Company offers guests a distinctive dining experience built on authentic Japanese cuisine and an engaging revolving sushi service model. Kura Sushi USA, Inc. was established in 2008 as a subsidiary of Kura Sushi, Inc., a Japan-based revolving sushi chain with over 550 restaurants and 40 years of brand history. For more information, please visit http://www.kurasushi.com.
