# It’s A Cool Summer: Kura Sushi USA Celebrates the Sunny Season with Ice Cream Floats, Debut of a New Kura Reserve Lineup with Hirame, and International Sushi Day Specials

# It’s A Cool Summer: Kura Sushi USA Celebrates the Sunny Season with Ice Cream Floats, Debut of a New Kura Reserve Lineup with Hirame, and International Sushi Day Specials


Strawberry Puree, Green Apple, and Cotton Candy Floats, alongside a refreshing Kura Reserve lineup featuring Japanese flounder, and more to enjoy for a limited time at Kura Revolving Sushi Bar


June 10, 2026


[Kura Sushi USA, Inc.](https://kurasushi.com/) , the nation's largest brand of revolving sushi restaurants, is taking in the sunshine and celebrating the playfulness of June festivities like the beautiful game by introducing **new ice cream floats, refreshing Kura Reserve sushi plates featuring Japanese flounder, and in recognition of International Sushi Day, offering high-quality nigiri and sushi rolls for an incredible value** at all Kura Revolving Sushi Bar locations.


**Fizzy, Fun and Made for the Moment:**
In time for the season’s biggest games, feel the joy in this refreshing summer lineup of ice cream floats paired with Sprite® for a crisp, lemon-lime finish through Jul. 31.


- **Strawberry Puree Float:** Strawberry puree blend with Sprite® topped with vanilla ice cream.
- **Green Apple Float:** Green apple blend with Sprite® topped with vanilla ice cream.
- **Cotton Candy Float:** Cotton candy blend with Sprite® topped with vanilla ice cream.


**A Vibrant Selection Inspired by Sunny Coastlines:**
Embrace the aromatic flavors of the season with June Kura Reserve, where bursts of sweet and spicy pair with delicate hirame (flounder) from Sanriku, Japan and tender tofu for a refined tasting journey. These unique menu selections are available for dine-in only starting Jun. 12, while supplies last.


- **Flounder with Soy Jalapeño:** Delicate cuts of subtly sweet hirame layered over Kura specialty rice topped with soy-marinated jalapeño and sesame seeds.
- **Flounder with Spicy Crunchy Garlic:** Enjoy the mild taste of hirame over Kura specialty rice, balanced with house-made spicy crunchy garlic oil and green onion.
- **Shrimp Wrap with Spicy Crunchy Garlic:** This taco-style wrap features jumbo shrimp over Kura specialty rice with pickled carrot. It’s topped with avocado, mayo, dressed with house-made spicy crunchy garlic oil, and wrapped in green leaf lettuce and soy paper.
- **Crispy Tofu Roll:** This delectable, vegetarian-friendly roll includes pieces of crispy tofu, cucumber, avocado, pickled carrot, and green leaf lettuce with miso mayo wrapped in seaweed and Kura specialty rice.


Rounding out this limited-time menu are Matcha Soy Milk Pudding—made with matcha powder from Shizuoka, Japan, shiratama mochi, red bean paste and matcha sugar, and Kura Reserve Standard nigiri sushi favorites Bluefin Tuna Otoro—tender bluefin tuna belly and Kumamoto Wagyu—marbled Japanese wagyu beef topped with garlic chips.


**A Day to Celebrate the Iconic Japanese Dish:**


At Kura Sushi, the obsession with sushi is next level, and International Sushi Day is the perfect occasion to satisfy the craving. On Jun. 18., indulge in a variety of sushi specials available at the standard sushi plate price.*


- **Fan Favorite Trio (3 pieces):** A single piece of Salmon nigiri, Tuna nigiri and Seared Salmon with Japanese Mayo.
- **Salmon Trio (3 pieces):** Savory slices of salmon layered over Kura specialty rice.
- **Tuna Trio (3 pieces):** Savory slices of tuna layered over Kura specialty rice.
- **Seared Salmon Trio (3 pieces):** Seared salmon drizzled with creamy Japanese mayo layered over Kura specialty rice.
- **Golden Crunchy Roll (6 pieces):** Shrimp mayo and avocado wrapped in dried seaweed and Kura specialty rice, drizzled with spicy mayo, sweet soy sauce and topped with panko and sesame seeds.


“We curated a summer menu for guests to feel like they’re experiencing a vibrant celebration with light, refreshing, and high-value dining choices made with seasonal ingredients,” said Newton Hoang, Vice President of Marketing at Kura Sushi USA, Inc. “As inspired by the playfulness that summer brings, we want to deliver for all the sushi lovers and bring joy to every table.”


Since establishing U.S. roots in Irvine, California in 2009, Kura Sushi USA, Inc. has become a must-visit dining destination with more than 600 million sushi plates served, and proud to be the nation’s largest brand of revolving sushi restaurants.

**About Kura Sushi USA, Inc.**


Kura Sushi USA, Inc. is the nation’s largest brand of revolving sushi restaurants that serves authentic Japanese cuisine and delivers a joyful dining experience with more than 90 locations in 22 states and Washington D.C. Utilizing advanced innovation to enhance service speed and accuracy, guests have a wide variety of more than 100 menu items at their fingertips via conveyor belt service inclusive of sushi, sashimi, tempura, noodles, desserts, and more, made with high-quality ingredients free of artificial colorings, preservatives, seasonings and sweeteners. Kura Sushi USA, Inc. was established in 2008 as a subsidiary of Kura Sushi, Inc., a Japan-based revolving sushi chain with more than 600 restaurants internationally and 45 years of brand history.
