# Paymentus to Participate in Upcoming Conferences in March

## Paymentus to Participate in Upcoming Conferences in March


February 25, 2026


[PDF Version](https://ir.paymentus.com/node/6611/pdf)


CHARLOTTE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Paymentus Holdings, Inc. (“Paymentus”) (NYSE: PAY), a leading provider of cloud-based bill payment technology solutions, today announced that Dushyant Sharma, Chief Executive Officer, and Sanjay Kalra, Chief Financial Officer, will participate in the following investor conferences:


- Raymond James 47 th Annual Institutional Investors Conference in Orlando, Florida on Tuesday, March 3 rd where Mr. Sharma and Mr. Kalra will participate in a fireside chat at 8:40 am ET
- 2026Wolfe Research FinTech Forum in New York on Wednesday, March 11 th where Mr. Sharma and Mr. Kalra will participate in a fireside chat at 3:40 pm ET


The fireside chats will be webcast live and available for replay on Paymentus’ investor relations website at[ir.paymentus.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.paymentus.com%2Fhome%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54433481&newsitemid=20260225627070&lan=en-US&anchor=ir.paymentus.com&index=1&md5=e9afda73a440a0748330ca39a2005a8f) .


Management will host investor meetings at the conferences. For further information, please contact your Raymond James and Wolfe Research representatives or emailpaymentus@kcsa.com .


**About Paymentus**


Paymentus is a leading provider of cloud-based bill payment technology and solutions for billers and financial institutions across North America. Our omni-channel platform provides consumers with easy-to-use, flexible and secure electronic bill payment experiences through their preferred payment channel and type. Paymentus’ proprietary Instant Payment Network™, or IPN, extends our reach by connecting our IPN partners’ platforms and tens of thousands of billers to our integrated billing, payment, and reconciliation capabilities.
