# Paymentus to Participate in Upcoming Investor Conferences

## Paymentus to Participate in Upcoming Investor Conferences


August 17, 2026


[PDF Version](https://ir.paymentus.com/node/9816/pdf)


CHARLOTTE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 17, 2026-- Paymentus Holdings, Inc. (“Paymentus”) (NYSE: PAY), a leading provider of cloud-based bill payment technology solutions, today announced that Dushyant Sharma, Chief Executive Officer, and Sanjay Kalra, Chief Financial Officer, are scheduled to participate in the following investor conferences:


- Deutsche Bank 2026 Technology Conference on Wednesday, August 26th in Dana Point, California
- Goldman Sachs 2026 Communacopia + Technology Conference on Thursday, September 10th in San Francisco, where Mr. Sharma and Mr. Kalra will participate in a fireside chat at 6:45 pm ET


A live webcast and replay of the Goldman Sachs fireside chat will be available on Paymentus’ investor relations website at[ir.paymentus.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fir.paymentus.com%2F&esheet=54589547&newsitemid=20260817468266&lan=en-US&anchor=ir.paymentus.com&index=1&md5=f04b2107499da7b0990dca796e704a65) .


At each conference, management will host investor meetings throughout the day.


For further information, please contact your Deutsche Bank or Goldman Sachs representative or emailpaymentus@kcsa.com .


**About Paymentus**


Paymentus is a leading provider of cloud-based bill payment technology and solutions for billers and financial institutions across North America. Our omni-channel platform provides consumers with easy-to-use, flexible and secure electronic bill payment experiences through their preferred payment channel and type. Paymentus’ proprietary Instant Payment NetworkTM, or IPN, extends our reach by connecting our IPN partners’ platforms and tens of thousands of billers to our integrated billing, payment, and reconciliation capabilities.
