# Paymentus to Participate in Upcoming Investor Conferences in May and June

## Paymentus to Participate in Upcoming Investor Conferences in May and June


May 5, 2026


[PDF Version](https://ir.paymentus.com/node/9541/pdf)


CHARLOTTE, N.C.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--May 5, 2026-- Paymentus Holdings, Inc. (“Paymentus”) (NYSE: PAY), a leading provider of cloud-based bill payment technology solutions, today announced that Dushyant Sharma, Chief Executive Officer, and Sanjay Kalra, Chief Financial Officer, will participate in the following investor conferences:


- J.P. Morgan 2026 Global Technology, Media and Communications Conference in Boston on Monday, May 18th, where Mr. Sharma and Mr. Kalra will participate in a fireside chat at 11:45 am ET
- Baird Global Consumer, Technology & Services Conference on Tuesday, June 2nd in New York


A live webcast and replay of the J.P. Morgan fireside chat will be available on Paymentus’ investor relations website at[ir.paymentus.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fir.paymentus.com%2F&esheet=54529024&newsitemid=20260505824389&lan=en-US&anchor=ir.paymentus.com&index=1&md5=a23be7e99fe95ef5a33fddd210bc9bbf) .


At each conference, management will host investor meetings throughout the day.


For further information, please contact your J.P. Morgan or Baird Research representative or emailpaymentus@kcsa.com .


**About Paymentus**


Paymentus is a leading provider of cloud-based bill payment technology and solutions for billers and financial institutions across North America. Our omni-channel platform provides consumers with easy-to-use, flexible and secure electronic bill payment experiences through their preferred payment channel and type. Paymentus’ proprietary Instant Payment NetworkTM, or IPN, extends our reach by connecting our IPN partners’ platforms and tens of thousands of billers to our integrated billing, payment, and reconciliation capabilities.
