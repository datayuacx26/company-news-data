# Pulte Homes Announces Grand Opening of West Village of Brighton

Pulte Homes is once again growing in Metro Detroit with the grand opening of West Village of Brighton, a new community offering a variety of townhomes just two blocks west of downtown Brighton. The community offers a unique opportunity for homebuyers seeking a lively, low-maintenance lifestyle with new floor plans to the market.


“We are excited to introduce our brand-new two-story townhomes to Michigan, especially in Brighton, where residents will be within walking distance of the charming downtown area,” said Kevin Christofferson, President of PulteGroup’s Michigan Division.


West Village of Brighton will include the rear-loaded Haven (1,907 sq. ft.) and Enclave (1907 sq. ft.) floor plans, each with three bedrooms, two and a half baths, and an oversized rear-load two-car attached garage. These new floor plans feature sleek modern craftsman exteriors and are equipped with smart home technology.


Residents will enjoy exceptional walkability to the local shops and restaurants on Main Street. Downtown Brighton is known for its charming, lively heart that hosts several events and festivals throughout the year. Mill Pond Park, with its small amphitheater and playground, serves as the hub of summer activities. For winter sports enthusiasts, Mount Brighton, a five-lift ski hill, is just a four-minute drive away. The community also benefits from highly rated Brighton schools and easy access to both I-96 and US-23.


West Village of Brighton will consist of 107 units upon completion, with prices starting in the low $400s. For more information, please visit
