# PulteGroup’s Northeast Corridor Division Helps Build a New Future for Veteran Family


When Sandro Moreta and his family arrived at their new home in the[Spring Lake Community](https://www.pulte.com/homes/pennsylvania/philadelphia/glen-mills/spring-lake-211173) , the welcome was warm and well-earned. But what stood out most wasn’t the ceremony. It was what this home represents for their future.


This home and the moment surrounding it were made possible by the dedication and care of PulteGroup’s Northeast Corridor Division, whose team, along with many trade partners, brought the vision to life and showed up in full force to support the family. Through our Built to Honor program and in partnership with Operation Homefront, this isn’t just a home being handed over. It’s a shift. A release. A chance to move forward without the same weight.


For Sandro, a Marine Corps veteran and Purple Heart recipient, that weight has been real. And while no home can change the past, this one creates space for healing, for presence, for simply being a dad.


The impact this home will have is evident in the way he talks about everyday things. Lying on the floor with his kids. Watching them play. Being able to slow down and just be together.


For Destiny, it’s about stability in a way that changes everything. A safe place for their children to grow up. A neighborhood where they can walk outside, go to school nearby, and build friendships that last. The kind of environment that shapes childhood.


And for their children, it’s simpler. It’s space to run. A yard to play in. A home where they can just be kids without limits.


That’s the part that’s hard to fully capture in a ceremony. The long-term impact. The generational shift. What it means when a family no longer has to carry the same uncertainty, and instead can start building something steady.


As the keys were handed over and the family stepped inside, the moment spoke for itself.
A new home, a fresh start, and a future with more room to grow.

**About Built to Honor®**


For the last decade, PulteGroup’s Built to Honor® program has recognized and thanked returning military personnel who have been injured during their term of service by providing mortgage-free homes to veterans and their families. Launched in 2013, the program has built and donated over 80 homes, gifting more than $30 million in value, across the country. Built to Honor works in partnership with nonprofit organizations to identify veteran candidates. For more information about Built to Honor, go to
