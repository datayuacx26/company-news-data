# PulteGroup's Built to Honor Program Helps 10 Veteran Families Build Stronger Futures in 2026

For the veterans and families who receive a Built to Honor® home, the greatest impact begins after the ribbon is cut, when homeownership creates stability, opportunity and peace of mind for years to come.


That's exactly how U.S. Navy Petty Officer


[Robert Orr,](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DGl17Y41JSwU&esheet=54576642&newsitemid=20260727662377&lan=en-US&anchor=Robert+Orr%2C&index=1&md5=3d0ebf9ac98549afcd074497c07b87d8)


who received a Built to Honor home in 2024, describes the experience: "This home has been far more than a place to live. It has been the foundation that has allowed our family to grow, heal and build a future."


Built to Honor provides mortgage-free homes to injured veterans and their families. Last year, the program surpassed its


[100th mortgage-free home delivery](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DCR7EBHXdG5c%26t%3D14s&esheet=54576642&newsitemid=20260727662377&lan=en-US&anchor=100th+mortgage-free+home+delivery&index=2&md5=1dd33975371caf93ecf99d8d5898c117) , marking a major milestone in its 15-year history. In 2026, nine PulteGroup divisions are leading 10 home projects for veterans across seven states, all united by a single purpose: helping veterans build stronger futures for their families.


"The true impact of the Built to Honor program isn't measured on the day we hand over the keys," said Ryan Marshall, PulteGroup's President and CEO. "It's measured in the years that follow, as veteran families build their lives, become ingrained in and strengthen their communities, and create new opportunities for generations after them."


Several of this year's projects are unique and feature distinctive community connections.


PulteGroup's West Florida Division will cut the ribbon on not one, but two mortgage-free homes right next door to one another this fall. Two veterans will become lifelong neighbors, marking the program's second delivery of adjacent homes.


Through projects led by PulteGroup's Coastal Carolinas and Minneapolis divisions, two veterans will soon call Del Webb Savannah and Bellwether by Del Webb home. As PulteGroup's renowned 55+ brand, Del Webb communities offer connection, friendship, and support that can be especially meaningful for veterans.


Another veteran family will receive a mortgage-free home at


[The Grow](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Flifeatthegrow.com%2F&esheet=54576642&newsitemid=20260727662377&lan=en-US&anchor=The+Grow&index=3&md5=7317714aea72155ebad1f4c3624a6d60) , PulteGroup's first agrihood community. Rooted in agriculture, wellness and community, The Grow offers a unique and tranquil setting for the Central Florida Division's veteran recipient.


Built to Honor projects are underway or complete across seven states in 2026, including PulteGroup's Central Florida, West Florida, Michigan, Coastal Carolinas, Northeast Corridor, Indianapolis, Dallas, Minneapolis, and San Antonio divisions.


Since its founding, Built to Honor has helped military families build more than homes. Mortgage-free homeownership creates lasting financial stability while opening doors to new opportunities for veterans and their families. Together with thoughtfully designed communities, the program provides a beautiful home and place of belonging, but also the foundation for everything that comes next.
