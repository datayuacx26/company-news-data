# Del Webb Announces Grand Opening of The Lake House, Amenity Center at Kensington Ridge

Del Webb, the nation’s leading builder of active adult communities for those 55 and older, is proud to announce the grand opening of the highly anticipated clubhouse and amenity center, The Lake House, at Kensington Ridge by Del Webb. This 16,800-square-foot facility provides residents with a world-class, resort-style living experience.


“The Lake House is the vibrant heart of Kensington Ridge,” said Kevin Christofferson, president of PulteGroup’s Michigan division. “This facility offers a perfect mix of function and fun; from social gatherings to fitness classes to creative workshops, we are excited to unveil this fantastic space.”


Situated on the shores of a scenic 43-acre lake, The Lake House offers a variety of amenities designed for an active and social lifestyle. Residents can enjoy a state-of-the-art fitness center with locker rooms, an indoor heated pool, a golf simulation room, and a range of versatile craft and multipurpose rooms. A full-time lifestyle director is available to coordinate a wide range of clubs and activities.


Beyond The Lake House, Kensington Ridge offers residents numerous outdoor activities, including scenic walking trails, tennis and pickleball courts, bocce courts, a future dog park and two fishing docks coming soon. The community also provides lake access for paddleboarding and kayaking, and it is adjacent to Kensington Metropark, a large 4,481-acre recreational park.


Since opening in the summer of 2023, Kensington Ridge by Del Webb has been very popular, with over 300 of the 611 homes planned for the community already sold. The community offers 10 different home designs from Del Webb’s consumer-inspired GenYou™ collection, focusing on livability and flexibility.


Kensington Ridge by Del Webb is situated at 1554 Tranquility Drive, Milford, Michigan 48381. Prospective buyers are encouraged to visit the community and tour the three fully furnished model homes and brand new clubhouse. For additional details, visit www.delwebb.com/kensingtonridge or call (248) 920-9130.
