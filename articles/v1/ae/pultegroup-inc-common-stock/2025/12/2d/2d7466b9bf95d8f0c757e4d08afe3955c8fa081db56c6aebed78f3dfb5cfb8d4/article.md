# PulteGroup to Attend the 2025 Goldman Sachs Industrials and Materials Conference


In conjunction with its upcoming attendance at the 2025 Goldman Sachs Industrials on Thursday December 4, 2025, PulteGroup, Inc. (NYSE: PHM) has posted a new investor presentation to its website. PulteGroup’s new and completely updated presentation provides a detailed review of the Company’s investment thesis, operating platform, business strategies and competitive advantages.


Interested investors can access PulteGroup’s presentation by logging onto the Company’s website at


[pultegroup.com](http://www.pultegroup.com/) and selecting the Events & Presentations link under the Investor Relations tab.

**About PulteGroup**


PulteGroup, Inc. (NYSE: PHM), based in Atlanta, Georgia, is one of America’s largest homebuilding companies with operations in more than 45 markets throughout the country. Through its brand portfolio that includes Centex, Pulte Homes, Del Webb, DiVosta Homes, American West and John Wieland Homes and Neighborhoods, the company is one of the industry’s most versatile homebuilders able to meet the needs of multiple buyer groups and respond to changing consumer demand. PulteGroup’s purpose is building incredible places where people can live their dreams.
