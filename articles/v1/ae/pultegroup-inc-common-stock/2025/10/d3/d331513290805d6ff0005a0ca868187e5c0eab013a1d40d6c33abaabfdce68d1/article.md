# PulteGroup Named to 2025 Best Workplaces for Women List by Fortune Media and Great Place To Work


PulteGroup, Inc. (NYSE: PHM), America's third-largest homebuilding company, is proud to be named to[the 2025 Fortune Best Workplaces for Women™ List](https://www.greatplacetowork.com/best-workplaces/women/2025?category=large) by Fortune Media and Great Place To Work. This is PulteGroup’s sixth consecutive year being named to this prestigious list, this year coming in at #26.


The Best Workplaces for Women list is highly competitive. Survey responses reflect a comprehensive picture of the workplace experience. Honorees were selected based on their ability to offer positive outcomes for women regardless of job role, race, sexual orientation, work status, or other demographic identifier.


“We’re proud to be recognized on the Fortune Best Workplaces for Women list for the sixth year in a row,” said Kevin Henry, EVP and Chief People Officer at PulteGroup. “This honor underscores our deep commitment to fostering a workplace where women are supported, valued, and empowered to grow both personally and professionally. From paid parental leave and flexible work options to caregiver support, women’s leadership programs, and mentorship opportunities, we’ve built a culture that champions equity and enables our team members to thrive. This recognition speaks to the inclusive environment our employees help shape every day.”


Great Place To Work measured the experience of women at participating companies and compared those results to their peers within the organization. The best companies create great work experiences for all women, from the frontline to the C-Suite. “Congratulations to the Fortune Best Workplaces for Women,” says Michael C. Bush, CEO of Great Place To Work. “These companies show that when women thrive in the workplace, everybody else does too. That’s what it means to be a For All company, where the expectation is that every employee can have a great experience and reach their full potential.”


In 2025, PulteGroup was also named to the 2025 Fortune Best Workplaces in Construction™ List, the 2025 PEOPLE Companies that Care list, ranked as one of the Fortune 100 Best Companies to Work For®, earned the 2025 Great Place To Work Certification™, and in 2024 was honored on the Fortune Best Workplaces for Millennials™ list.

**About the Fortune Best Workplaces for Women**


Great Place To Work selected the 2025 Fortune Best Workplaces for Women List by analyzing the survey responses of nearly 605,000 women employees who work for Great Place To Work Certified companies that also meet the criteria for this list. To be eligible, a company must employ at least 50 women, have least 20% of non-executive managers who are women, and have at least one female C-suite executive. Company rankings are derived from 60 employee experience questions within the Great Place To Work Trust Index™ Survey. Read the full methodology. To get on this list next year, start here.
