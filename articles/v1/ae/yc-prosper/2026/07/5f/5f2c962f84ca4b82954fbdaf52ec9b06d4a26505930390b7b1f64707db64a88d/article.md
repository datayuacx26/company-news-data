## Artera


Artera is a patient communication tool built around messaging, appointment reminders, and two-way texting. It works across multiple channels, including SMS, email, and web chat, letting patients confirm, cancel, or reschedule appointments directly from a reminder message without calling the front desk.


It integrates with many major EHRs and is used across a wide range of specialties. For OB-GYN practices managing high appointment volumes, the reminder automation can reduce inbound confirmation calls meaningfully.


That said, Artera focuses on messaging workflows. It does not handle inbound phone calls, prior auth, or benefits verification, so front desk staff still own those call types manually. ## Assort Health


Assort Health is an AI-powered scheduling tool built for high-volume specialty practices. It focuses on[patient self-scheduling software](https://www.getprosper.ai/blog/patient-self-scheduling-software-guide) and slot optimization, letting patients book appointments through an online portal without calling the front desk.


The software integrates with several major EHR systems and can match patients to the right provider based on insurance, visit type, and availability. Practices report fewer scheduling errors and reduced front-desk call volume after implementation.


That said, Assort Health is largely a scheduling point solution. It handles the calendar well, but leaves prior auth, benefits verification, and after-hours call handling to staff.


## Hello Patient


Hello Patient is a patient communication and scheduling tool built for OB-GYN and women's health practices. It focuses on appointment reminders, two-way messaging, and self-scheduling so front desk staff spend less time on routine confirmations.


### What it does well


- Two-way text messaging lets patients confirm, cancel, or reschedule directly from an[appointment reminder](https://www.getprosper.ai/blog/appointment-reminders-reduce-no-shows-guide) without calling the office.
- Self-scheduling gives patients a portal to book available slots based on provider rules the practice configures.
- The interface is straightforward, which keeps staff training time low.


### Where it falls short


Hello Patient works well as a reminder and messaging layer, but it covers a narrow slice of the front desk workload. Billing inquiries, insurance questions, and after-hours calls still route to staff. Practices with high call volume often find they need additional tools to cover what Hello Patient leaves unhandled.


## EliseAI


Founded in 2017 in property management, EliseAI entered healthcare in 2023. Its architecture pairs an LLM for speech generation with a workflow engine that enforces linear call flows instead of goal-driven conversation management.


### What they offer


- Voice and text automation for scheduling and administrative inquiries

- Capability quality that varies by deployment configuration


Good for: Practices with low-variance scheduling workflows and no requirement for RCM or payor-facing call handling.


Limitation: Linear call flows can break down on mid-call topic pivots, off-script insurance questions, and the complex multi-turn conversations common in prenatal and high-risk OB calls, a broader architectural challenge covered in this guide to[AI voice agents for healthcare](https://www.getprosper.ai/blog/ai-voice-agents-for-healthcare-complete-guide) . Scripted-workflow tools generally reach an all-inbound resolution ceiling of roughly 25 to 40%.


Bottom line: Scripted-workflow tools can be adequate for predictable scheduling patterns, but architecture and deployment variance tend to leave gaps for practices with high call complexity or RCM requirements.


## Luma Health


Luma Health markets its voice AI product as "Spark." In practice, it is an SMS and text workflow tool with voice AI that has limited documented production deployment.


Here is what they offer:


- SMS and text automation for patient reminders, confirmations, and cancellations
- SMS-to-form deflection for a subset of scheduling inquiries
- Voice AI with limited published production case studies, focused on cancellations
- Text-based patient communication campaigns


Good for: Practices whose primary need is reducing no-shows through SMS reminders for simple appointment types.


Limitation: SMS-first tools typically lack EHR write-back and outbound voice. SMS deflection rates in the 15 to 25% range are not the same as end-to-end call resolution, and voice capabilities in this category are often limited to narrow use cases such as cancellations.


Bottom line: A workable starting point for SMS-based no-show reduction. OB-GYN practices that need to handle scheduling, insurance verification, and billing calls end-to-end will find it falls short of that workload. ## FAQ


### How do I choose between Prosper AI, EliseAI, and Assort Health for my OB-GYN practice?


Start by mapping your actual call mix across three months of data. If your volume is limited to predictable appointment scheduling, Assort Health or EliseAI may be adequate. If your front desk also handles insurance verification, billing inquiries, referral status, and after-hours calls, Prosper AI is the only option on this list with documented production coverage across that full range, resolving over 60% of inbound calls end-to-end based on Prosper AI's customer deployment data.


### When does Luma Health or Hello Patient make sense instead of a full AI voice agent?


Luma Health and Hello Patient work well when your primary problem is no-show reduction through SMS reminders and your inbound call volume is low enough that staff can handle what text automation misses. If your practice fields high call volume across scheduling, insurance, and billing, both tools hand the majority of that work back to staff, since neither handles inbound phone calls or EHR write-back at the level a full voice agent does.


### What should I test before selecting any OB-GYN scheduling software?


Call a live customer number for each vendor you are vetting and test three things: whether the agent can answer questions it was not scripted for, whether it can follow a caller who changes topics mid-call, and whether it handles a mid-call language switch. These tests take 10 to 15 minutes per vendor and expose capability gaps that demos typically do not show.


### Is Prosper AI a good fit for smaller OB-GYN practices or independent clinics?


Prosper AI is built for outpatient groups in the 10 to 150 location range. Independent single-site practices may find the product's scope exceeds their current call volume, and tools like Hello Patient or Artera may offer a lower-friction entry point for reminder and messaging workflows. Practices with 6 or more locations and high mixed call volume are a stronger fit.


### How does after-hours coverage differ across these OB-GYN scheduling tools?


Most tools on this list, including Artera, Luma Health, and Hello Patient, do not provide after-hours AI call handling. Prosper AI runs the same workflow continuously, so calls outside business hours receive the same handling as peak-hours calls. For OB-GYN practices with patients calling after hours about prenatal concerns or appointment changes, the gap between a tool that covers business hours only and one that covers 24/7 is where call abandonment accumulates.
