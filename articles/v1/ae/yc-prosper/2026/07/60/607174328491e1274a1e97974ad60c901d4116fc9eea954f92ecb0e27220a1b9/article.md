## What are AI voice agents for cardiology practices?


AI voice agents for cardiology practices are phone-based AI systems that handle patient calls autonomously, without routing to a human staff member for routine requests. They can schedule appointments, answer questions about referrals and prior auth requirements, collect insurance information, and send follow-up reminders, all over a live phone call.


Cardiology front desks field a high volume of calls that are repetitive but time-sensitive.[Cardiology voice AI](https://www.getprosper.ai/outpatient-groups/cardiology) handles that call load without pulling staff from urgent work. A patient calling to confirm a stress test or ask about a Holter monitor return is not a complex clinical case, but that call still pulls a staff member away from higher-priority work. AI voice agents handle that call end to end. In cardiology,[nuclear, echo, and stress test prior auths](https://www.cliniqhealthcare.com/blog/cardiology-prior-auth-imaging-procedures) represent the highest-volume authorization category, a call type that most scheduling-only tools leave entirely to staff.

## Assort Health


Assort Health is an AI voice agent built for health systems and large specialty groups. It focuses on inbound call handling, appointment scheduling, and some post-visit follow-up workflows, with EHR integrations across a handful of major systems including Epic and Cerner.


The product handles scheduling calls well in structured environments, where appointment types are predictable and insurance rules are relatively uniform. Based on Assort Health's publicly documented feature set, the tool focuses on scheduling and reminders; coverage for call types such as billing questions, referral coordination, and prior auth status checks is not described in their public materials.


Assort Health suits larger organizations that need a scheduling-focused voice layer and have staff available to handle the remaining call types manually.


## Hyro


Hyro is an AI voice agent built for large health systems, with publicly referenced deployments at institutions including Cedars-Sinai and Providence. It handles inbound call routing, appointment scheduling, and FAQ responses across high call-volume environments.


Where Hyro performs well is scale. Health systems fielding tens of thousands of calls monthly get consistent call containment across multiple service lines. The architecture is designed for enterprise rollouts, not single-specialty clinics.


For cardiology practices, that scale orientation is a tradeoff. Based on Hyro's publicly documented implementation approach, adding new call types or specialty-specific workflows requires vendor engineering rather than self-service configuration, which can extend timelines for practices scoping changes after go-live. Smaller or mid-size cardiology groups may find that onboarding overhead outpaces their actual call volume.


If your practice sits inside a large health system already running Hyro, the fit may be reasonable. For independent or specialty cardiology groups reviewing standalone options, the enterprise-oriented build may not align with the speed and flexibility those groups typically need. ## Artera


Artera is a patient communication tool built around two-way messaging, appointment reminders, and care gap outreach. It gives patients a way to confirm, cancel, or reschedule directly from a reminder message, which reduces some manual follow-up for front desk staff.


For cardiology practices, the appeal is in reducing no-show rates through automated outreach. But Artera's core design is messaging-first. Phone call handling falls outside its primary scope, so practices fielding high inbound call volumes for referral intake, prior auth status checks, or post-procedure follow-up questions still need staff to cover that ground.


### Where it fits in a cardiology workflow


- Appointment reminders with two-way reply options work well for routine scheduling confirmations, particularly for practices running high visit volumes across multiple cardiologists.
- Care gap outreach can flag patients overdue for echo follow-ups or stress tests, though acting on those flags still requires a staff touchpoint.
- Based on Artera's publicly documented features, inbound call handling, insurance verification, and referral coordination fall outside its core scope, leaving a meaningful share of daily call volume unautomated.


Artera suits practices that want to cut no-shows through better messaging but aren't looking to reduce inbound call load or front desk staffing. If your primary pain point is inbound phone volume, Artera's messaging-first design may not cover that call load based on its documented feature set.

## Frequently asked questions


### What should I look for in an AI voice agent for a cardiology practice?


Call containment depth matters more than appointment scheduling alone. Cardiology practices field a high volume of calls around benefits verification, billing, and prior auth status. An agent that handles only scheduling leaves most of that call mix to staff. Look for EHR integration, configurable call type coverage, and documented[voice AI deflection rates in healthcare](https://www.getprosper.ai/blog/voice-ai-deflection-rates-healthcare-benchmarks) from cardiology or comparable specialty deployments.


### How does an AI voice agent handle cardiology-specific calls like prior auth inquiries?


The better agents connect directly to your EHR or PMS to pull real-time authorization status and relay it to the caller without staff involvement — a core feature of[automated prior authorization](https://www.getprosper.ai/blog/automated-prior-authorization-complete-guide) workflows. Weaker implementations route the caller to a voicemail or staff queue the moment the request falls outside a narrow scheduling scope. Ask vendors to walk through exactly what happens when a patient calls asking about a pending stress test auth.


### Will patients find it hard to talk to an AI voice agent?


Many patients who interact with well-configured AI voice agents report not noticing a meaningful difference from speaking with staff. The gap tends to show up with older patient populations or complex multi-issue calls, where escalation to a live person matters most. A well-designed agent escalates gracefully, without looping or dropping the caller.


### How do I choose between Prosper AI, Assort Health, Hyro, Artera, and Clarion for a cardiology practice?


Start with your call mix, not your feature checklist. Narrow-scope tools like Artera handle reminders well but leave inbound call volume to staff, while options like Assort Health and Hyro cover scheduling but tend to stop short of prior auth and benefits verification workflows.
