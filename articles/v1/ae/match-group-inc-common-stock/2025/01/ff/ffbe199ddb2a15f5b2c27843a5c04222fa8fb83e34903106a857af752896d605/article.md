# Match Group, Tinder Donate to Fire Relief, Support Emergency & Rebuilding Efforts

Jan 16 2025


Match Group, Tinder Donate to Fire Relief, Support Emergency & Rebuilding Efforts


Written by Match Group


The Match Group and Tinder teams are heartbroken by the fires that continue to have a devastating impact across the Los Angeles community. The majority of our Tinder employees are based in Los Angeles, a region we’re proud to call home. The health and safety of our colleagues remains our top priority, and we are doing everything we can to provide support for our employees, their loved ones, and everyone who calls the City of Angels home.


We’ve set up donation boxes in all of our California offices to collect essential items for displaced individuals. These items will be distributed in partnership with[It’s Bigger Than Us](https://www.itsbiggerthanusla.org/) ,[South Pasadena YMCA](https://www.ymcala.org/community-response) , the[Pasadena Humane Society](https://pasadenahumane.org/) , and also available for our impacted team members. The Match Group Workplace Experience team is also collaborating with our local vendor Bruce’s Kitchen to provide meals and supplies to firefighters and volunteers on the frontlines.


Beyond the direct aid we are providing to our employees, Match Group and Tinder are committed to supporting the communities impacted by this tragedy, and are matching and double matching donations to several employee-chosen local organizations.


We are also making direct financial contributions to disaster-relief nonprofits performing incredible work –[World Central Kitchen](https://wck.org/?gad_source=1&gbraid=0AAAAApcxQJuWMvdDGHO_lpplir5UBasci&gclid=Cj0KCQiAkJO8BhCGARIsAMkswyifCAgXm1YKkkxHSeLdoP7b_vMnNuiLy5cSdr3bfuO8s32RdfOEPj0aAtEvEALw_wcB) and the[Los Angeles Fire Department Foundation](https://supportlafd.org/) . If you would like to join us in donating to the Los Angeles Fire Department Foundation or World Central Kitchen, you can do so online[here](https://supportlafd.kindful.com/) and[here](https://wck.org/donate) .


Our thoughts are with the LA community and everyone who has been impacted by this unbelievable tragedy and will continue to provide information and resources. We are committed to being a source of strength and support for our community in this time of need.
