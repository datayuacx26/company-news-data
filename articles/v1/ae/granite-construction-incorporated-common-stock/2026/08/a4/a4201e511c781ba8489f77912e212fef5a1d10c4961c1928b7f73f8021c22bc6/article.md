# United Contractors Announces R.E.A.L. Safety Award Winners

UCON’s Safety Awards Program recognizes excellence in jobsite safety, leadership, and innovation across California’s union construction industry.


Safety is more than a priority—it's the foundation of every successful construction project. United Contractors (UCON) announces the recipients of its 2025 R.E.A.L. Safety Awards, honoring the companies, projects, and safety professionals whose leadership continues to raise the standard for jobsite safety across California's union construction industry.


Recognizing Excellence, Awareness, and Leadership in safety, the program, now in its third decade, the R.E.A.L. Safety Awards celebrate companies and individuals who demonstrate an unwavering commitment to protecting their workforce, preventing injuries, and fostering a culture where safety is a core value.


This year, UCON received one of the largest applicant pools in the program's history, reflecting the industry's continued investment in safety excellence. Applications were reviewed anonymously, with each submission evaluated against established criteria to recognize outstanding leadership, innovation, and measurable safety performance.

**About the R.E.A.L. Safety Awards**
Established in 2005, the R.E.A.L. Safety Awards are guided by UCON's Safety & Insurance Committee and recognize contractors and individuals who exemplify Recognizing Excellence, Awareness, and Leadership in safety. The program promotes continuous improvement, innovation, and a culture where every employee goes home safely.


**About United Contractors (UCON)**
United Contractors is a member-driven association representing nearly 800 union-signatory construction firms and affiliate members across California. UCON is dedicated to protecting, educating, and empowering California’s union contractors through labor relations, political advocacy, HR support, safety & regulatory services, professional & leadership development, and industry networking.
