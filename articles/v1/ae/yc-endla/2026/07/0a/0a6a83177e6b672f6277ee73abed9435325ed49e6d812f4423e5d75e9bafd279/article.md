# What is a Digital Twin?

The digital twin is your asset's most powerful predictive tool. It mirrors the real world in software, runs experiments faster than coffee brews, and gives teams a calmer, clearer way to make decisions.


A living twin stays in sync with measurements, literature, and the way your site actually behaves.


## A Short Story


Morning shift. You open the dashboard and your twin whispers: "Here's what's likely to happen today, and the easy wins." At first it was just a calculator with a clipboard. Then it became an oracle with a live feed. Now it's a co-engineer that problem solves while you grab a coffee.


That journey, from basic to oracular to agentic, is the path we're on at Endla. Below is how each step works in practice.


## Basic Digital Twin


Start with the essentials: measurements and literature feed a scenario, the scenario drives a model, and the model produces results you interpret. It's what-if testing without any bells or whistles.

## Agentic Digital Twin


The next step is agency. The twin doesn't just warn; it tries options. It identifies upcoming bottlenecks, applies business logic, devises candidate solutions, and runs autonomous what-if testing inside the model. You get ranked, top-performing solutions, not a pile of plots.
