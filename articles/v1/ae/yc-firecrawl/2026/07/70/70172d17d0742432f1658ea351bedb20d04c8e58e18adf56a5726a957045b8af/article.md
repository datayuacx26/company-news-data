# How Minimal Builds AI Support Agents That Know Every Merchant's Store

*Minimal runs nearly six million storefront pages a month through Firecrawl, turning live product and policy content into LLM-ready data its AI support agents can answer from.*


**What is Minimal?**[Minimal](https://gominimal.ai/) is a YC-backed AI customer support platform for e-commerce brands. Its AI agents resolve up to 90% of customer support tickets for growing brands including XXL Nutrition, Cloudpillo, and Boombrush.


> Storefronts come to us in every shape imaginable, and no matter the complexity, Firecrawl turns each one into clean, structured data our AI support agents can answer from.


*— Niek Hogenboom, Co-Founder, Minimal*


## Why is the store itself the best training data for an AI support agent?


Almost every answer a customer needs is already published somewhere on a merchant's website. The challenge is that it is rarely all in one place. Sizing details live on product pages, refund rules in the returns policy, and delivery estimates in the shipping terms.


For an AI support agent to resolve tickets at human-level quality, it needs a deep understanding of that entire business. Minimal starts with the merchant's own storefront, using Firecrawl to turn scattered products, policies, and help content into clean, structured data its agents can use.


That foundation helps Minimal's AI support teams resolve up to 90% of incoming tickets end to end while staying grounded in the information customers see on the live store.


## How does Firecrawl handle every storefront platform?


Minimal's merchants run on Shopify, Magento, Lightspeed, and storefronts built in-house. Each platform renders pages differently, and every merchant customizes its storefront with its own theme and layout.


Building scraping for all of that in-house would turn every onboarding into an engineering project, when Minimal's promise to merchants is setup in hours, not weeks.


Instead, Minimal sends storefront pages through Firecrawl's[/scrape](https://docs.firecrawl.dev/features/scrape) endpoint. Firecrawl handles the differences between platforms and returns clean markdown no matter how the underlying site is built. Minimal can feed that output into its existing data pipeline without writing a new scraper for every merchant.


## What does Firecrawl look like at Minimal's scale?


Minimal's Firecrawl usage has grown 13x in fourteen months, reaching more than six million pages a month. Every new merchant adds another storefront, product catalog, and set of policies that Minimal needs to keep current.


Scheduled refreshes re-scrape those storefronts as products, promotions, and policies change. Instead of answering from a snapshot captured during onboarding, Minimal's agents stay aligned with the live store.


In June 2026 alone, Minimal sent more than six million requests through Firecrawl across more than 1,000 domains. Firecrawl gives the team one dependable ingestion layer across that entire footprint, so Minimal can keep focusing on the quality of its support agents rather than the complexity of scraping each storefront.
