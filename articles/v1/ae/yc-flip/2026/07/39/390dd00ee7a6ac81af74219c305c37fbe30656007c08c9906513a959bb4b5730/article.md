# Forward 2026 by Flip: Transforming the Employee Experience

Program Highlights


-


**Opening Keynote** – “ **Reimagining Work and the Workforce in the Age of AI” Marcus Berret** (Roland Berger) opens with a look at how organizations need to be reimagined for an AI-enabled world of work.


-


**REWE Group on Stage – “Employee Experience. At Scale.”** **Julia Herrmann** and **Mareike Heidemann-Jarosch** demonstrate how digital enablers are transforming work at one of Europe’s largest decentralized organizations.


-


**Science Meets Practice – “Rethinking the Human Side of AI at Work” Prof. Dr. Katharina Hölzle** (Fraunhofer IAO / University of Stuttgart) on the human side of AI in the workplace.


-


**Panel – “HR Transformation & Psychological Safety in the Age of AI** ” With **Bala Sathyanarayanan** (Greif, formerly Schindler), **Prof. Dr. Katharina Hölzle, Khaled Thaler** (Managing Director Hirschtec) and **Sebastian Ullrich** .


-


**Customer Stories from the Frontline**


Real-world case studies from **HELLA** (Operational Excellence in Manufacturing), **toom Baumarkt** (Retail Transformation), **CSL** (wired & unwired united on one platform).


-


**Product Premieres from Flip**


Flip CPTO **Luke Talbot** and team reveal what’s coming next.


-


**Flip Awards 2026** In the grand finale, the **Employee Experience Awards for Frontline Teams** will be presented for the second time – recognizing outstanding transformation projects from industry, retail, and services.


Why Forward 2026 Matters for the Press


**1. AI on the frontline is THE HR topic of the year.** While Generative AI in the office is widely covered, the bigger question remains unanswered: How does AI reach the 80% of workers who don’t sit at a desk? Forward 2026 is one of the first conferences in Europe to answer this question concretely – with live demos, case studies, and research insights.


**2. Real numbers, real brands, real results.** Instead of buzzwords, leaders from **REWE, HELLA, toom Baumarkt, CSL** and other major organizations share how they digitized their frontline – including measurable impact on productivity, employee retention, and operational processes.


**3. Scientifically grounded.** With **Prof. Dr. Katharina Hölzle** (Fraunhofer IAO) and **Marcus Berret** (Roland Berger), Flip brings together top academic and strategic voices on the topic of “work in transition” – a relevant crossover between research, consulting, and practice for business editors.


**4. Product news with market relevance.** Flip will announce the next step in its platform strategy from the stage: from a pure communications tool to a **System of Action** for the frontline.


**5. A stage for award-winning best practices.** The **Flip Awards 2026** make visible successes that often fly under the radar in public debate: concrete examples of how German and European companies are improving the work of their operational and frontline teams.
