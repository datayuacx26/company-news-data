# Flip accelerates global expansion with strategic partnership in the UK

**LONDON, November 22 2024** –[Flip](https://www.getflip.com/) , Europe’s leading frontline employee super-app, today announced a partnership with[Work Networks](https://www.worknetworks.co.uk/) in the UK. This news follows Flip’s recent collaboration with[Enablo](https://www.enablo.com/) , a provider of cutting-edge communication and productivity solutions, marking another pivotal step in the company’s global growth strategy. This partnership further Flip’s mission to transform the employee experience for deskless and frontline workforces worldwide.


## **Global Presence, Local Expertise**


In the UK, Flip’s partnership with Work Networks, a pioneering digital transformation firm and one of the first Workplace by Meta partners, unites Flip’s powerful frontline employee platform with extensive regional expertise in complex enterprise transitions. With a client portfolio that includes organisations like Danone, AstraZeneca, the World Health Organization (WHO) and the National Health Service (NHS), Work Networks shares Flip’s commitment to creating significant organisational impact through technology and cultural transformation.


## **Experienced Partners for Workplace by Meta Transitions**


“Moving a large, diverse workforce to a new communication platform can be risky, especially to employee engagement and adoption,” says Benedikt Brand (né Ilg), CEO of Flip. “Our partnerships with Work Networks brings localised expertise to help guide large organisations successfully through this transition. Worknetworks knows Workplace’s ecosystems inside and out, and they chose Flip because they see us as the ideal solution for former Workplace customers. Together, we’re delivering a powerful, next-level platform that strengthens internal communication and operational efficiency for businesses worldwide”


With Flip’s intuitive interface and robust functionalities, frontline workers across sectors, from retail to manufacturing, can streamline communication, manage HR tasks, and foster stronger team connections—all in one platform.


Nick Crawford, CEO, at Work Networks, says “Our collaboration with Flip is an exciting opportunity to redefine how organisations connect with their frontline employees in the UK. Having been at the forefront of Workplace from Meta, we understand that successful transitions require more than technology; they demand a strategic focus on employee engagement and support. Together with Flip, we're driving a cultural shift that enables companies to cultivate strong connections and foster a truly inclusive digital workplace.”


These partnerships come on the heels of Flip’s recent acquisition of[The Bot Platform](https://www.getflip.com/newsroom/press-releases/flip-acquires-the-bot-platform/) and two key appointments from Workplace by Meta. Flip is trusted by over 400 global companies, including Bosch, McDonald’s Germany, Rossmann and Porsche to reach, engage, and transform their frontline workforces.


**About Flip**


Flip is the employee experience platform that’s transforming the day-to-day of deskless workforces across the globe. Its white-label app creates a single point of contact between businesses and their frontline employees, bundling internal communications and HR tools into one easy-to-use, employee-friendly interface. From onboarding to up-skilling and shift planning to vacation requests, Flip gives employees instant and equal access to the company information, services, and opportunities relevant to them. Flip is trusted by the world’s top companies, including Bosch, McDonald’s Germany, Porsche, and MAHLE.


**About Work Networks**


With a focus on fostering stronger connections among teams, Work Networks believes that a great digital employee experience should enhance actual employee experiences, driving smarter and more connected ways of working. Work Networks is a leading reseller and service partner for digital employee experience, intranet, and internal communications platforms, connecting organisations from HQ to the frontline. Specialising in the selection, implementation, and optimisation of secure, efficient communication and collaboration tools, Work Networks enables AI-driven collaboration and staff engagement accessible from any device.
