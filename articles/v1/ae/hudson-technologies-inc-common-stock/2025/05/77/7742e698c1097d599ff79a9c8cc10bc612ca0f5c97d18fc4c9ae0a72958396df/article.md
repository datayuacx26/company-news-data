# Building the Future Together: Hudson Technologies Partners with Rockland Community College

At Hudson Technologies, we believe in investing not only in sustainable solutions—but also in the people and communities that help make them possible. That’s why we’re excited to announce our new partnership with Rockland Community College (RCC), a collaboration aimed at fostering education, innovation, and career development right here in our backyard.


This partnership represents a shared commitment to building a stronger, more sustainable future. Together with RCC, we’ll work to create opportunities that support local students through workforce training and educational initiatives focused on environmental stewardship and clean technology.


For us, this is about more than just business—it’s about giving back and helping shape the next generation of leaders in sustainability and HVACR technology. RCC’s dedication to hands-on learning and community empowerment aligns perfectly with our mission, and we’re proud to stand alongside them in this effort.


We’re looking forward to all the possibilities this partnership will bring—from nurturing emerging talent to strengthening the local economy and accelerating progress toward a greener tomorrow.


**To our team, our partners, and the students of RCC: the future is bright—and we’re excited to build it with you.**
