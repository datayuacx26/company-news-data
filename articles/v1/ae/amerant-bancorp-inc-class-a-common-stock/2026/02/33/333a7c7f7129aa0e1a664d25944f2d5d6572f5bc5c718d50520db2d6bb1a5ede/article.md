# AMERANT BANK AND MIAMI HEAT TEAM MEMBERS SUPPORT ENVIRONMENTAL RESTORATION DURING 16TH ANNUALHEAT GREEN SPACE INITIATIVE

## *The volunteer effort continues a long-standing partnership focused on environmental stewardship across Miami-Dade County*


[Amerant Bank,](https://www.amerantbank.com/) Florida’s bank of choice with a growing footprint throughout the state, joined forces with the Miami HEAT on Jan. 29th for the annual HEAT Green Space Initiative, a volunteer-driven environmental cleanup and restoration initiative at Matheson Hammock Park. Presented by Amerant Bank, the event brought together Amerant team members and HEAT representatives in support of local South Florida conservation efforts as part of the NBA Green initiative.


The HEATGreen Space Initiative has become a[long-standing collaboration between the Miami HEAT and Amerant](https://www.amerantbank.com/about-us/sports-partnerships/) , reflecting a shared commitment to protecting South Florida’s natural resources. In prior years, Amerant volunteers have supported ecological restoration projects across Miami-Dade County, including tree planting initiatives in partnership with the Miami-Dade County Division of Environmental Resources Management and conservation work at sites such as Oleta River State Park and the Tamiami Pineland Complex Addition.


“At Amerant, supporting the communities we serve goes beyond banking,” said Mariola Sanchez, Chief Administrative Officer of Amerant Bank. “Our volunteer efforts during the HEAT Green Space Initiative reflect our commitment to environmental responsibility and to making a positive impact across South Florida through hands-on team efforts.”


The HEAT Green Space Initiative aligns with broader[sustainability initiatives supported by Amerant Bank](https://www.amerantbank.com/impact/) and the Miami HEAT, engaging employees, community partners and volunteers in meaningful environmental action throughout the region.
