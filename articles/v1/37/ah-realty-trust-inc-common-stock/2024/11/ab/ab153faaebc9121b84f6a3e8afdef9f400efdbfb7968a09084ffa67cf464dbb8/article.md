# Promoting Environmental Stewardship in Urban Developments


AH Realty Trust views[sustainability](https://ahrealtytrust.com/sustainability/) as a fundamental aspect of our responsibility to shape the future of urban spaces. With a focus on responsible growth, we maintain that every project aligns with the goal of minimizing environmental impact and enhancing our local communities. Our dedication to sustainable practices is an integral part of our development process, contributing to both environmental and economic success.


### **Strategic Leadership in Sustainable Development**


Our sustainability efforts are driven by clear, measurable objectives. For instance, we aim to reduce energy consumption by 15% and lower greenhouse gas emissions by 10% across our portfolio by 2032. These targets are part of a broader initiative that incorporates[energy-efficient designs](https://www.google.com/aclk?sa=l&ai=DChcSEwj85Kqe5aSJAxUlYEcBHYJiIN4YABAAGgJxdQ&co=1&ase=2&gclid=Cj0KCQjwveK4BhD4ARIsAKy6pMJGXGGwXvHe52wtTDLb_m33PgwCUrrUc5vDryHh3raZ9uyMkaZY6CgaAqBGEALw_wcB&sig=AOD64_1xevJfZ5PXryj5Tf68GH_KRPjloQ&q&nis=4&adurl&ved=2ahUKEwiks6Se5aSJAxWxFmIAHbCWBxcQ0Qx6BAgUEAM) , renewable energy solutions, and waste reduction practices into each development.


Our mixed-use development,[Harbor Point](https://www.harborpoint.com/) in Baltimore, is an excellent example of our commitment to these principles. As the city’s first eco-district, it features[LEED-certified](https://www.usgbc.org/leed) buildings, electric vehicle charging stations, and extensive green spaces. The project demonstrates that sustainable urban developments can foster both livability and environmental[responsibility](https://www.reit.com/investing/reits-sustainability) , setting a new standard for environmentally conscious design.


### **Extending Structural Longevity**


Our approach to sustainability also emphasizes[adaptive reuse](https://www.mbharch.com/post/a-complete-guide-to-adaptive-reuse-in-2023) , which allows us to extend the life cycle of existing structures. By modernizing older buildings with energy-saving technologies, we preserve their character and significantly reduce resource consumption. A prime example within our portfolio is[Chronicle Mill](https://thechroniclemill.com/) in Belmont, North Carolina. This property takes elements of a[historic building](https://ahrealtytrust.com/preserving-the-past-while-building-the-future-the-benefits-of-adaptive-reuse/) that has been revitalized with modern efficiencies. This project highlights our ability to honor architectural history while simultaneously contributing to a sustainable future.


### **Sustainability as a Competitive Advantage**


Our sustainability initiatives also deliver tangible[financial benefits](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/sustainability/our-insights/sustainability-blog/how-the-e-in-esg-creates-business-value) . Energy-efficient buildings reduce operational costs and meet the growing demand for eco-friendly living and work environments. This helps to enhance tenant satisfaction and creates long-term value for shareholders. As the market increasingly favors sustainable development, our efforts position us to remain competitive and align economic returns with environmental responsibility.


### **Developing for a Changing Future**


Beyond energy efficiency and adaptive reuse, AH Realty Trust’s developments are designed to tackle environmental challenges to guarantee they remain functional and safe for years to come. We incorporate features that mitigate climate risks, such as flood prevention measures and designs capable of withstanding extreme weather conditions. These strategies further strengthen the long-term viability of our properties.


### **A Continued Commitment to Sustainable Growth**


Sustainability will continue to guide our growth strategy, driving innovation and ensuring we meet the needs of both current and future generations. Through developments like Harbor Point and adaptive reuse projects like Chronicle Mill, we remain dedicated to building urban spaces that prioritize environmental stewardship without compromising quality or community engagement.
