# The Gathering Spot Expands to The Interlock: A Strategic Win for AH Realty Trust


AH Realty Trust continues to strengthen its presence in Atlanta with an exciting new addition at[The Interlock](https://theinterlockatl.com/) in Atlanta, Georgia. The Gathering Spot, a private, members-only city club, has chosen this premier mixed-use development as the home for its new headquarters. With 38,000 sq. ft. of rooftop space and 20,000 sq. ft. of office space, this expansion solidifies The Interlock as a dynamic destination for professionals, entrepreneurs, and creatives.


### **A New Hub for Innovation and Community**


Since its founding in 2016,[The Gathering Spot](https://theinterlockatl.com/directory/the-gathering-spot/) has built a reputation as a go-to space for collaboration, networking, and cultural engagement. Its expansion into The Interlock brings even more energy to[West Midtown](https://discoveratlanta.com/explore/neighborhoods/midtown/west-midtown/) , with amenities including:


- A rooftop with skyline views and a pool
- Two full-service restaurants and bar ([WYE](https://www.atlantadowntown.com/go/the-gathering-spot) ), featuring locally sourced cuisine
- Flexible workspaces and private event venues
- A state-of-the-art event space equipped with cutting-edge technology


Opening in summer 2025, this location will provide members with a unique environment to work, connect, and create.


### **Why The Interlock?**


> *Our team was able to quickly fill vacant space at The Interlock, demonstrating the strength of our portfolio and continued demand we see for highly-amenitized and well-located real estate, underscoring our mixed-use strategy.”* – Shawn Tibbetts, CEO & President of AH Realty Trust


Located in the heart of West Midtown Atlanta,[The Interlock](https://ahrealtytrust.com/properties/the-interlock/) has become a top-tier mixed-use development, offering a blend of office space, retail, dining, and entertainment. AH Realty Trust’ vision for the property is rooted in community-driven growth, ensuring that each addition adds long-term value. The Gathering Spot fits seamlessly into this vision, enhancing the development’s appeal to forward-thinking businesses and professionals.


### **Mixed-Use Development: The Future of Real Estate**


As cities evolve, mixed-use developments like The Interlock continue to redefine urban landscapes. These spaces prioritize:


- Convenience & Accessibility: Bringing work, lifestyle, and entertainment under one roof
- Community & Engagement: Creating destinations that foster collaboration and cultural vibrancy
- Sustainability & Innovation: Integrating smart design and eco-conscious practices


AH Realty Trust has long championed mixed-use projects that drive economic growth and enhance livability. Properties like[Town Center of Virginia Beach](https://www.towncentervb.com/) ,[Harbor Point](https://www.harborpoint.com/) in Baltimore, and The Interlock highlight our dedication to building spaces that support business success and strengthen communities.


### **What’s Next for The Interlock?**


With The Gathering Spot anchoring a significant portion of its rooftop and office space, The Interlock is positioned for continued success.
