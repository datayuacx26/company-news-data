# Power Financial Credit Union selects NCR Atleos Allpoint Network to expand cash access

ATLANTA --[NCR Atleos](https://www.ncratleos.com/) Corporation (NYSE: NATL) (“Atleos”), a leader in expanding self-service financial access for financial institutions, retailers and consumers, today announced that[Power Financial Credit Union](https://www.powerfi.org/) has selected the NCR Atleos Allpoint and Allpoint+ networks to expand cash access and deposit locations for its members.


For 75 years, Power Financial Credit Union has served as a trusted partner for South Florida families and small businesses. Founded in 1951, the credit union provides the personalized financial tools and guidance necessary to help its members thrive. Recently, Power Financial Credit Union was recognized as one of America’s Best Regional Banks and Credit Unions 2026 by *Newsweek* and voted the Best Credit Union in Miami-Dade for three straight years by the *Miami Herald.*


As a new Atleos customer, Power Financial Credit Union enabled the Allpoint Network to support its strategic priority of expanding ATM access for its growing membership. Through this deployment, members can now access their cash without surcharge-fees at more than 40,000 Allpoint ATMs nationwide, and 15,000 outside the U.S., as well as complete convenient cash deposits at approximately 3,500 Allpoint+ cash deposit locations.


“Access and convenience are critical to how we serve our members,” said Wanda Ferrer, EVP and Chief Financial and Strategy Officer at Power Financial Credit Union. “By leveraging the Allpoint and Allpoint+ networks, we’re able to provide our members with more locations to access their cash, make deposits, and enjoy a seamless self-service experience.”


In addition to expanding access, Power Financial Credit Union is using Allpoint Screen Branding, enabling the credit union to ensure members experience the Power Financial Brand when using their card throughout the transaction. This capability is designed to strengthen brand awareness while delivering a consistent, trusted experience when members transact at major retailers nationwide.


“Atleos supports financial institutions in extending their reach beyond traditional branches through unmatched scale, reliability and innovation in self-service banking,” said Steven Nogalo, General Manager of North America for Atleos. “We’re proud to support Power Financial Credit Union as it expands access and convenience for its members through the Allpoint Network.”


The collaboration reflects the focus by Atleos on helping financial institutions address operational efficiency and evolving expectations for digital‑first financial services.
