# Prediction Markets Deserve Better Deposit Infrastructure

Prediction markets have gone mainstream. Platforms like[Polymarket and Kalshi](https://news.kalshi.com/p/election-contracts-kalshi-vs-polymarket) proved that real-money markets can attract millions of users and generate billions in volume. But behind the rapid growth is a persistent problem: getting money in the door is still way too hard.


## Speed is everything


Prediction markets are uniquely time-sensitive. A user sees breaking news, opens the app, and wants to place a position *right now* . Every second of delay in the deposit flow is a lost trade — and a lost user.


Yet most platforms still force users through multi-step deposit flows: connect wallet, check if you're on the right chain, bridge if you're not, approve the token, confirm the transaction, wait for finality, then finally see your balance. By the time the funds arrive, the odds have moved.


The platforms winning this category have figured out that **deposit speed is product speed.**


## The numbers speak for themselves


We've been working with prediction market platforms to rebuild their deposit flows from the ground up. The results:


Metric Before Unifold After Unifold


Deposit completion rate ~55% ~90%


Time to account credit 2–15 minutes < 5 seconds


Chains supported 1–2 10+


Deposits from non-primary chains < 5% > 35%


That last metric matters the most. Over a third of deposit volume now comes from chains that platforms previously didn't support. That's not optimization — that's new revenue that didn't exist before.


## What the best platforms do differently


The prediction market platforms seeing the highest deposit conversion share a few patterns:


- **They accept any token from any chain.** A user with ETH on Base can deposit just as easily as a user with USDC on Ethereum. No bridging required.
- **They credit balances instantly.** Users see their funds within seconds, not minutes. They can trade immediately.
- **They absorb gas costs on small deposits.** For a $25 bet, even a $0.50 gas fee feels like friction. The best platforms make deposits feel free.
- **They treat deposit UX as a core product surface** , not an afterthought buried in settings.


## Deposits are the growth lever


As prediction markets expand beyond crypto-native users, deposit UX becomes the primary conversion bottleneck. Mainstream users won't bridge tokens. They won't wait for block confirmations. They'll compare the experience to Venmo or Apple Pay — and if it doesn't measure up, they'll leave.


The platforms that win will be the ones that make depositing as invisible as swiping a credit card. We're building the infrastructure to make that possible.
