# How to migrate mobile lines with no disruption to your customers

If you’re reading this, you’re probably facing a decision you didn’t plan for.


Maybe your current MVNE is shutting down. Maybe your connectivity wholesaler has been acquired. Maybe your setup simply can’t support the products you want to build next. Either way, you’re now responsible for migrating thousands, even millions, of lines without disrupting customers or overloading your team.


Whatever brought you here, your concerns are probably the same:


-


How long will this take?


-


How much resource will it require?


-


What happens to my customers during the transition?


The prospect of migrating all your customer lines onto a new tech stack can sound daunting. But it doesn't have to be. Here's why.


## **Gigs isn’t a traditional MVNE. It’s a modern telecom OS.**


Most MVNEs give you access to a network and a patchwork of OSS/BSS tooling. You still manage the complexity and carry the operational burden.


Gigs is different. Think about what Shopify did for ecommerce or what Stripe did for payments. Those platforms didn't just give access to infrastructure. They abstracted away complexity entirely and made something previously painful feel simple.


That's what Gigs does for telecom.


## **Migration without chaos**


Moving tens of thousands of lines can be seamless and straightforward. Here’s how.


### **1. Dedicated migration support**


You don’t have to go it alone. We provide a dedicated team that manages the migration execution according to your timeline, including a Project Lead, Account Manager and Implementation Manager. From planning and provisioning to validation and cutover, we handle the heavy lifting. You don’t need to hire a new telecom operations team, build custom tooling or pause product development.


Our team has a wealth of experience at companies like Stripe, Apple and Airbnb, and our focus is always on providing the very best experience for you and your customers.


### **2. Fast, seamless transfers that meet your deadlines**


Existing lines can be transferred in days, not months. There’s no service interruption for end users. Customers keep their numbers, their plans and their experience.


Our plans are fully customizable, so we can mirror the plans your customers are currently on to minimize disruption. We can also support the transfer in multiple ways, depending on what works best for your customers and your business.


### **3. Get new activations back up and running instantly**


One of your biggest concerns is likely the risk of losing the ability to activate new subscribers.


With Gigs, that won’t be an issue. We can spin up activation journeys in a matter of days, so you can keep onboarding new customers without interruption to your business or your acquisition targets.
