# Revolut Mobile starts rolling out in the UK: Unlimited 5G data, calls & texts for an introductory offer of £12.50 a month

**Revolut Mobile starts rolling out in the UK: Unlimited 5G data, calls & texts for an introductory offer of £12.50 a month.**


-


Revolut Mobile offers unlimited 5G data, calls, and texts with a generous 20GB of roaming data in the EU and US for a special early-bird rate of only £12.50. Customers joining from 30th of March 2026 onwards will pay £14.99/month.


-


Customers can activate a plan in seconds and choose to keep their existing mobile number or move to a new number with a range of additional features to create a truly personalised plan.


-


Revolut Mobile delivers fast, reliable 5G coverage across the UK and abroad and will be powered by Gigs.


**London, United Kingdom, 16th December 2025**


Revolut, the global fintech with 12 million customers in the UK, has started rolling out Revolut Mobile to waitlist customers with general access available in January 2026. At a special early-bird rate of just £12.50 a month for customers who sign up before 30th of March, Revolut Mobile offers unlimited 5G data, calls, and texts at home, with a generous 20GB roaming allowance across Europe and the US.


Revolut Mobile is built on a foundation of simplicity, security, and global utility. The entire service is managed within the Revolut app, allowing customers to activate a plan in seconds, keeping their existing number or selecting a new one. Powered by Gigs, the operating system for mobile services, Revolut Mobile delivers fast, reliable coverage across the UK and abroad.


The new product goes beyond basic connectivity, offering a truly personalised and differentiated phone plan that offers, global access and flexibility:


-


Global messaging: customers travelling to more than 80 countries (beyond EEA and US), can stay connected with Messaging Pass, an allowance that provides low-bandwidth data for messaging and other apps.*


-


Multiple numbers, one plan: customers can have up to three separate numbers on a single plan (starting from £2 extra per month). Perfect for easily separating work and personal life, or for obtaining a number just for merchant sign-ups, protecting your primary number from unwanted calls/texts.


-


VIP numbers: customers can select new numbers from a range of exclusive VIP numbers for an enhanced personal touch, at £10 per month.


-


Security: all plans include a complimentary NordVPN subscription to ensure secure and private connections.


For even more value, Revolut’s loyalty program, RevPoints, seamlessly integrates with mobile plans, allowing customers to pay for or discount their plan using their points.
