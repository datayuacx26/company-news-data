# Security at Gigs: SOC 2 Type II Audit Report

Security is central to everything we do at Gigs. Our customers entrust us with critical data and operations, and we take that responsibility seriously.


As part of our commitment to delivering the gold standard in security and transparency, Gigs completed the SOC 2 Type II audit on January 15th 2025 through auditing firm


[Johanson Group, LLP](https://www.johansonllp.com/) .


To download the


report,


head over to the Gigs Trust Center at


[trust.gigs.com](http://trust.gigs.com/) .


## **What is SOC 2?**


SOC 2 is an auditing standard developed by the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) to ensure service providers, like Gigs, correctly manage their security, availability and confidentiality commitments.


Gigs was audited by Johanson Group LLP in 2024/2025 for three service criteria:


-


Security


-


Availability


-


Confidentiality


The audit was supported by


[Vanta](https://www.vanta.com/) , the industry-leading security and compliance platform. Vanta continuously monitors Gigs’ service infrastructure and processes, ensuring we hit the highest standards.
