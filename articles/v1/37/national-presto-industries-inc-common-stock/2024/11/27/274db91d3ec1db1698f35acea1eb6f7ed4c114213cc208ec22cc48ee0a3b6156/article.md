As we're well underway during football season across the country, football fans are hungry for exciting new match ups, old rivalries, and every second of intense competition to come. For those hungry for a little more—say, favorite appetizers, shareable snacks, and tailgate treats—Presto® has you covered. Discover a whole new way to share your team spirit while beating your halftime hunger with Presto's Gameday Lineup of home appliances!


**A Revitalized Roster**


Now arriving on the field sporting a sleek new green-and-gold paint job, the Gameday family of Presto home appliances features four proven MVP's of parties past. Pop fresh popcorn for some easy snacking with the Poplite® Hot Air Popper. Put a savory spin on your crispy, thin-crust pizza on the Pizzazz® Plus Rotating Pizza Oven. butter melter, or apply your favorite toppings like sweet caramel, cheddar cheese, or onion-dill seasoning. Plus, since the PopLite doesn't use oil, the base and cover simply wipe clean. Try some of our recommended recipes for an easy win this season!
