# Game Day Ready Snacks

The biggest football game of the season is just around the corner, and preparing delicious snacks and finger foods can be stressful. Finding the right recipes is half the battle, and we are here to offer some suggestions. Whether you are excited for the game, the commercials, or the company of friends and family, you’ll score big with these delicious and easy-to-make recipes for football or commercial fans alike!


**Kick-off appetizers**


****


[Baked Brie in Puff Pastry](https://www.gopresto.com/recipes/baked-brie-in-puff-pastry)


A flaky puff pastry wrapped around a creamy brie cheese wheel, topped with a sweet-and-spicy chili jam; this shareable appetizer is a crowd pleaser. Simply preheat your[Digital AirDaddy®](https://www.gopresto.com/product/digital-airdaddy-4-2-quart-electric-air-fryer-03422) air fryer, prepare, and bake for 15 to 16 minutes. Looking for a sweet take? Substitute the chili jam with apricot preserves or raspberry jam for a sweeter flavor.


[Hot Mustard Snack Mix](https://www.gopresto.com/recipes/hot-mustard-snack-mix)


Nothing beats kicking back on the couch and watching kickoff with a nice bowl of popcorn made with your[Presto® popcorn popper](https://www.gopresto.com/product/category/popcorn-poppers) . Spice it up with this simply delicious snack mix featuring game day favorite flavors! A combination of mixed nuts, popcorn, and shredded wheat cereal tossed in a buttery mustard, thyme, and cayenne pepper coating.


**Halftime eats**


[Spicy Chicken Strips](https://www.gopresto.com/recipes/spicy-chicken-strips)


Bring a little heat to the halftime performances with these flavorful chicken strips. Marinated in a mixture of buttermilk, hot sauce, salt, and pepper, then coated in a cayenne and black pepper bread crumb mixture — these spicy chicken strips are ready to be enjoyed right out of the air fryer or paired with your favorite dipping sauces.


[Fully Loaded Pizza](https://www.gopresto.com/recipes/fully-loaded-pizza)


Pizza is a classic football favorite, and this fully loaded pizza is a sure win with how easy it is to cook on your Presto[Pizzazz® rotating pizza oven](https://www.gopresto.com/product/presto-pizzazz-plus-rotating-oven-03430) . Topped with bacon, portobello mushrooms, artichoke hearts, Italian sausage, and balsamic vinaigrette, this pizza has it all.


**Second half treats**


****


[Peanut Butter & Jelly Thumbprint No-Bake Cookies](https://www.gopresto.com/recipes/peanut-butter-jelly-thumbprint-no-bake-cookies)


Delicious peanut butter-coated popcorn topped with your favorite jam or jelly — This no-bake cookie is an easy treat to score big points with your guests. Looking for a bit more variety, substitute the jam or jelly in the center with a chocolate kiss.


[Fruit Turnovers](https://www.gopresto.com/recipes/fruit-turnovers)


Sweet or savory—this versatile, flaky pastry is sure to delight as the clock ticks down the final minutes of the game. Fill with your favorite jam flavors for a sweet bite or make it savory with a pepper jam or cream cheese. Easy to make a variety of flavors in a matter of minutes!
