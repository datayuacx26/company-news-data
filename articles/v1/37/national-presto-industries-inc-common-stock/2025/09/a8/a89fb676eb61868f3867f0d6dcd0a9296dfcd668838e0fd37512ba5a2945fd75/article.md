# Preserve the Last of Summer

As summer winds down and gardens overflow, it’s the perfect time to fire up the pressure canner, preserve the last jewel-toned stone fruits, and stash away tomato sauce so thick it tastes like August. If you’ve been eyeing a digital pressure canner to ease your canning load, the Presto Precise® line has four excellent models—each with strengths depending on how much you preserve, what jars you use, and how much kitchen space you have. Here’s a guide to help you choose *and* some inspiration for what to can while the season’s still generous.


---


## Why Go Digital


Presto Precise® digital pressure canners **sense temperature** , display guides for each phase, and maintain the **exact processing temperature** you need. You don’t need to fuss with regulating heat or watching pressure gauges every few minutes—they do the heavy lifting.

## Which One Should You Choose?


Depending on what you’re preserving now and what you expect to preserve in future, here are some heuristic suggestions:


-


If your preserving tends toward **small batches of jam or pickles** , the 12-quart model likely fits your needs.


-


If you haul in bushels of tomatoes, peaches, plums, or stock, go for **a 17-quart or 23-quart pot** so you don’t spend all evening doing batches.


-


If you occasionally need quart jars but also want something a bit lighter, the **16-quart hybrid** is the “best of both worlds.”


-


Think about where you’ll store it, where you’ll use it, and if you’ll move it often—weight and size matter, especially when hot and full.

## Late-Summer Preserves You’ll Love


To put these tools to good use, here are some of my favorite late-summer preserves—perfect for any of these canners, depending on capacity.


-


**🍏** **Apple Butter** — Use Jonathan, Winesap, Stayman, Golden Delicious, McIntosh, or other tasty apple varieties for good results.


-


**🍅 Tomato Salsa** — Best with paste tomatoes such as Roma. Slicing tomatoes, such as Beefsteak, require a much longer initial cooking time to achieve a desirable consistency


-


**🌶️** **Spicy Cranberry Salsa** — A fresh take on a tart, sweet, spicy, and super flavorful salsa, oh my.


-


**🍝** **Spaghetti Sauce with Meat** — A “heat and eat” home-canned pure, ready for spaghetti sauce.


-


**🥕** **Carrots** — Leave them whole, sliced, or diced.


-


**🎃** **Pumpkin and Winter Squash** — Great for pies and quick, tasty sides.
