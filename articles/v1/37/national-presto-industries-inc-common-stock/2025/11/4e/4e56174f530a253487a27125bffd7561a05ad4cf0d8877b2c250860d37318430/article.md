# Gather, Give Thanks & Let Presto® Do The Cooking

As we approach the holiday of gratitude, we at Presto are reminded of everything we're thankful for—the warmth of family and friends around the table, the laughter echoing through the kitchen, and the joy of passing around dishes that bring everyone together.


This Thanksgiving, we're shining a spotlight on our favorite traveling slow cookers that makes sharing delicious meals effortless: the **[Nomad® Traveling Slow Cooker](https://www.gopresto.com/product/nomad-8-quart-traveling-slow-cooker-06017) (8-quart, model 06017)** . Designed not just for cooking, but for connecting: it's got a locking lid, a cool-touch exterior, and a swing-up handle—so you can easily move tasty side dishes or warm casseroles from the kitchen to the buffet table (or even to a friend or family's house).


**Why it's perfect for your Thanksgiving spread**


- **Big capacity** — 8 quarts is enough for mashed potatoes, green-bean medleys, stuffing, or whatever your family tradition calls for.
- **Quick clean-up** — The porcelain-enameled cooking pot is removable and fully immersible, making cleanup easier after the big meal.
- **Spill-friendly and portable** — With a locking lid and wide-profile design, you can carry dishes through the house (or to a shared potluck) without worrying about messes.


**A Little Thanksgiving Recipe Idea**


Try setting the Nomad on *Low* early in the morning and tossing in peeled and cubed sweet potatoes, a splash of maple syrup, a pinch of cinnamon, and a handful of pecans. By the time family arrives, the sweet potatoes are tender, fragrant, and ready to serve—without you standing over a stove. Meanwhile, you can be mingling, indulging in pumpkin pies, or stealing seconds of cranberry relish.


**From our Kitchen to Yours**


We're grateful for each one of you—our customers, our community, and the home chefs who trust us to bring warmth, ease, and deliciousness to special occasions. This holiday, we hope your kitchen is full of aromatic steam, full hearts, and full plates.


Happy Thanksgiving from all of us, and may your day be rich in flavor, fellowship, and gratitude.
