# Second Season, Same Passion: Packers & Presto Bring Game Day Home

It’s been a full season—and then some!—since we kicked off our partnership with the Green Bay Packers, and we’re excited to be in **year two** of cheering on our team together. We’ve been busy bringing the spirit of Lambeau Field into kitchens everywhere with the new Game Day line of Packers-themed appliances.

Our Game Day collection has everything you need for the perfect homegate, watch party, or game day feast. Think big, bold, fun, and always Packers-green and gold.


Some of the standouts:


-


The[Nomad ® 8-quart Traveling Slow Cooker](https://www.gopresto.com/product/nomad-traveling-slow-cooker-06019) — keeps your chili, pulled pork, or brats warm all game long.


-


The[FryDaddy® Electric Deep Fryer](https://www.gopresto.com/product/frydaddy-electric-dep-fryer-05427) — for those wings, cheese curds, or anything else that’s better deep-fried.

The[Pizzazz ® Plus Rotating Pizza Oven](https://www.gopresto.com/product/pizzazz-plus-rotating-pizza-oven-03434) — spin your pizza, bake it evenly, and bring cheesy goodness.

The[PopLite ® Hot Air Corn Popper](https://www.gopresto.com/product/poplite-hot-air-corn-popper-04870) — popcorn = the ultimate snack, hands down.


Every appliance is designed with game day in mind: built to serve, built to impress, built for Packers fans.

Year two of this partnership is about more than nostalgia. It’s about bringing fans into the kitchen—not just into the stands. Whether you’re frying, slow-cooking, popping, or spinning pizzas, these appliances let you mix fandom and food in a way that’s fun and functional.


If you’re looking for a gift for a Packers fan or want to gear up your own game day setup, this[Game Day line](https://www.gopresto.com/product/category/game-day) has you covered. Here’s to full stadiums, full bellies, and full hearts—Go Pack Go!
