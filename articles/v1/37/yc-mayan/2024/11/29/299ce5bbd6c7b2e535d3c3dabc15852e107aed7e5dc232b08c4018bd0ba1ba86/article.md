# How to Sell on TikTok in 2024: Best Practices for Amazon Sellers

TikTok has become a dominant social media force, but it's more than dance crazes and viral trends. It has also become a powerful sales tool for FBA sellers. With over a billion active users, TikTok lets you reach an engaged audience and drive serious sales.


Whether you are already posting TikTok videos or have never even tried, this guide will help you get the most out of your video marketing efforts. Read on to learn what every FBA seller needs to know about TikTok as a sales tool.


## Creating an FBA seller TikTok Profile


As an FBA seller, your TikTok profile is like your storefront for that platform. Don't be tempted to jump into posting product videos without creating a bio that connects with your target audience.


### Set up a Pro Account


A Pro Account is free, but it still gives you access to valuable insights about your audience and content performance. As every FBA seller knows, the best strategies start with strong data, which is how you get that data on TikTok.


### Craft a Compelling Bio


Your bio is your first impression. Keep it concise, informative, and engaging, but make sure you include important content and information such as:


- **A clear brand identity** means not just your brand name but also what you offer and the categories of items you sell. (e.g., "Eco-friendly kitchen gadgets")
- **Your Amazon store** should be center stage and linked to from your bio, along with a few details about what you sell that entice them to click.
- **Relevant keywords** that you used to draw in customers on Amazon can help potential customers find you TikTok as well.
- **A call to action** in your bio will encourage users to visit your Amazon store. Remember to update your CTA for seasonal sales and special promotions.


### Choose the Right Visuals


- **Your profile picture** should include a high-quality image of your logo or a representative product for your brand.
- **A cover video** takes your profile to another level and is a great way to showcase your products or brand personality.


### Example Bio:


✨ Sustainable kitchenware for a healthier planet ✨


Shop our eco-friendly products on Amazon! ➡️ \[Link to your Amazon Store\]


#ecofriendly #kitchen #sustainableliving


## Content Strategies for FBA Sellers


Your TikTok needs a content strategy that excites your audience and gets them to click over to your Amazon storefront. A few examples of sure-fire content that sells include:


- **Product demos** show your products in action and highlight key features and benefits. It's important to be informative, but don't forget that TikTok is about entertainment. You can even try a bit of humor, but remember to keep it professional.
- **"Did You Know?" videos** educate your audience while highlighting your products. The video can talk about an unusual product feature, or it can address a common problem and set up your product as the perfect solution.
- **Customer review spotlights** use positive reviews from your Amazon listings to build trust and social proof. You can even duet with a review video from to showcase what others think of your products.
- **Behind-the-scenes content** can give your audience a feeling of connection and is a great way to draw attention to sustainability practices or your dedication to selling quality-manufactured products. Videos may give a look at how your products are made or how you choose the best products for your store.
- **Relevant trends** that pop up on TikTok can really boost your signal if you jump on at the right time. Keep an eye on trending topics each day for opportunities to draw more views to your existing videos.
- **Relevant keywords** in your TikTok captions and hashtags will help to attract the right audience.


### Example


If you sell a unique kitchen gadget, you could create a dynamic video set to a trending song with a demonstration of the gadget in use and a text overlay that highlights key features. Don't forget a clear call to action such as, "Shop now - link in bio!"


## Engage the TikTok Community


TikTok is a social platform, not a one-way sales channel. That means building relationships with potential customers, but don't worry, this doesn't have to be another full-time job. Some examples of ways to keep things social on Tik Tok include:


- **Be responsive** to comments and questions. The TikTok algorithm prioritizes accounts with high engagement, and responsiveness also shows your audience you value their interactions, helping you to build a loyal following.
- **Participate** in relevant trends and challenges to increase your visibility and reach a wider audience.
- **Find your people** by collaborating with creators in complementary niches. Cross-promotion exposes your brand to a new audience and adds variety to your content.
- **Go live** with your product demonstrations or host Q&As. You can encourage engagement by offering exclusive deals or bundles, and you can also use live videos to premiere new products.
