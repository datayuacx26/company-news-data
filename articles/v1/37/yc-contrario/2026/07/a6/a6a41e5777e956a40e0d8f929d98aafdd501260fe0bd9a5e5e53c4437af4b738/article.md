# How AfterQuery uses Contrario to hire across every core function

AfterQuery was founded with a clear thesis: the best foundation models need the highest quality data, and curating that data requires an exceptional team to match.


In under one year, co-founders Spencer Mateega and Carlos Georgescu grew their team from 3 people to 30+. In April 2026, they announced their $30M Series A at a $300M valuation.


AfterQuery's hiring profile is deliberately diverse. Engineers from leading technology companies work alongside analysts from quantitative hedge funds and operators from top consulting firms.
