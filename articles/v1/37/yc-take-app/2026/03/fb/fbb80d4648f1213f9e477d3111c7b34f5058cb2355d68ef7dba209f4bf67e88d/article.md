# Neraj: Powering WhatsApp Commerce with Take App

## A New Chapter Built Around WhatsApp


For Neeraj Jajodia, the rise of WhatsApp Business APIs in India opened the door to an entirely new career path. Restarting his career at 42, Neeraj saw an opportunity where his strengths and curiosity for technology could truly make a difference for businesses.


As he began working with companies exploring WhatsApp for marketing and sales, he noticed a recurring challenge.


“I saw that most businesses were either (a) treating WhatsApp like a spam cannon or (b) getting lost in tools without a strategy, so I stepped in as the person who builds human, compliant, revenue-focused conversation systems end-to-end.”


Over time, Neeraj evolved from simply implementing tools to becoming a WhatsApp Growth Strategist, helping businesses design structured customer journeys that turn conversations into conversions.


## Why Take App


As Neeraj worked with more businesses, he needed a platform that could help him launch WhatsApp commerce systems quickly while still providing the right set of features for real operations. Take App became that platform.


“I chose Take App because it let us launch faster without sacrificing the essentials: team inbox, automation, and compliant messaging, so clients could start seeing ROI quickly.”


For businesses adopting WhatsApp commerce, speed to launch matters. Complex setups can slow down implementation and delay results. With Take App, Neeraj was able to deploy complete WhatsApp commerce systems in a fraction of the time compared to heavier solutions.


The platform also got the right balance between capability and simplicity. Teams could manage conversations, orders, and workflows without needing extensive technical training, which made adoption much smoother.


## Building a WhatsApp Storefront for Chainaram


One of the businesses that benefited from this approach is Chainaram, a sweets brand based in New Delhi.


The team was receiving many enquiries on WhatsApp but needed a clearer system to convert those conversations into actual orders. Neeraj implemented Take App as the foundation of their WhatsApp ordering experience, transforming scattered chats into a structured commerce flow. What surprised Neeraj most was how quickly everything could be launched.


“I can confidently say that TakeApp is one of the most robust and reliable tools I’ve ever used. I was able to commit to a full end-to-end go-live in under 24 hours setup, cataloguing, configurations, everything and TakeApp handled it flawlessly.”


## Opening New Opportunities


The reliability and speed of Take App have also helped Neeraj grow his own consulting work.


“Because of this tool, I’m now getting new business opportunities where people want to partner with me for building their systems while they run their business. TakeApp has genuinely opened doors for additional ventures and long-term collaborations.”


He is now expanding his work further by exploring partnerships with delivery providers that could integrate with these systems, enabling same-day or next-business-day delivery for businesses operating through WhatsApp.


## Advice for Businesses Starting with WhatsApp Commerce


Based on his experience helping companies implement WhatsApp commerce systems, Neeraj encourages businesses to stay focused on outcomes rather than tools alone. Start with one clear objective, such as converting leads or handling orders through WhatsApp. Build a simple but structured system around that goal, and expand gradually.


Most importantly, treat WhatsApp as a long-term growth channel rather than a short-term marketing tool.


“Your edge is lived experience + execution, launch small, iterate fast, and keep the feedback loop tight.”


For Neeraj and the businesses he works with, Take App plays a central role in making that possible. By turning WhatsApp conversations into structured ordering experiences, Take App helps businesses move from simple messages to scalable commerce.
