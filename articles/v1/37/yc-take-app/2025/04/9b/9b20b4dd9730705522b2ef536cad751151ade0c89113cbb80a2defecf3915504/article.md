# Smart Customer Management with Take App

With Take App, businesses can manage customer information, streamline communication, and improve loyalty all in one place. From targeted promotions to automated workflows, these tools can significantly **improve customer relationships** and **increase sales** . Here are some ways you can take advantage of Take App’s customer management features to create a seamless shopping experience.


## 1. Centralizing Customer Data with Profiles


Every time a customer places an order, Take App automatically creates a **customer profile** . These profiles store key details such as name, phone number, email, birthday, address, and order history, including total spent and average order value. Businesses can also add private notes to track preferences, special requests, or important details about each customer. Additionally, Take App allows you to assign wholesale prices and store credits to specific customers, making it easy to manage pricing tiers for different buyer segments.


##### **How to Use This Effectively**


-


**Personalized marketing:** Use customer data to offer birthday discounts, targeted promotions, or special deals based on order history.


-


**Better customer service:** Keep track of notes to remember preferences, dietary restrictions, or frequent requests.


## 2. Targeted Marketing with Tags and Broadcasts


Rather than sending generic messages to all customers, Take App enables businesses to categorize their audience using **tags** such as **VIP, Wholesale, High-Spender, Inactive** , etc. This allows businesses to send broadcast messages that are relevant to specific customer segments, increasing engagement and response rates.[Learn more.](https://help.take.app/en/articles/10987117-whatsapp-broadcast-beta)


##### **How This Helps Your Marketing**


-


**More personalized promotions:** Send exclusive discounts or early access deals to VIP customers.


-


**Win back inactive customers:** Offer special deals or incentives to re-engage customers who haven’t ordered in a while.


-


**Segment your audience effectively:** Ensure the right messages reach the right people, making marketing campaigns more effective.


## 3. Encouraging Loyalty with Membership and Rewards


Take App’s **Membership** feature allows businesses to control access to their store while rewarding loyal customers. You can require customers to log in to browse your store or limit access exclusively to approved members. Additionally, the store credit rewards system enables businesses to automatically offer customers a percentage of their purchase back as credit for future orders. This keeps customers engaged and encourages repeat purchases.[More info.](https://help.take.app/en/articles/10632226-membership-loyalty-rewards)


##### **How to Make the Most of It**


-


Convert first-time buyers into repeat customers by rewarding purchases with store credits.


-


Create a sense of exclusivity by offering membership-based access for premium customers.


## 3. Offering Wholesale Pricing for B2B Customers


For businesses selling to other businesses (B2B), Take App makes it easy to assign wholesale pricing to specific customers. When these customers log in, they will automatically see their discounted prices, ensuring a smooth purchasing experience. This feature eliminates the need for manual price adjustments and negotiations, allowing businesses to efficiently serve their wholesale clients.[Learn more.](https://help.take.app/en/articles/10632376-wholesale-price)


## 4. Managing Conversations with the Inbox Feature


Customer interactions are the heart of any business, and Take App’s Inbox feature ensures that conversations are organized and efficient. The Inbox provides full customer insights while chatting, allowing businesses to view client’s order history next to their chat, add internal notes, and respond to inquiries with full context. This helps businesses provide quick, informed, and personalized support.[See more.](https://help.take.app/en/articles/10847494-manage-whatsapp-business-chat-inbox)


**Why This Improves Customer Service**


-


Faster responses improve customer satisfaction and increase the likelihood of conversions.


-


Context-aware support allows you to handle inquiries efficiently without needing to ask for details repeatedly.


-


Better follow-ups ensure customers feel valued and understood, leading to stronger relationships.


## 5. Importing and Exporting Customer Data for Seamless Management


For businesses migrating from another platform or managing a large customer base, Take App simplifies the process with CSV import and export features. Customer data can be easily transferred, ensuring a smooth transition without manual entry.


##### **Best Uses for Import & Export Features**


-


Easily onboard customers from another system or add prospective customers to start engaging with them.


-


Send targeted broadcasts to newly added customers via WhatsApp, encouraging them to place their first order.


-


Leverage customer lists for external email and WhatsApp marketing campaigns.


-


Analyze spending patterns to refine promotional strategies and product offerings.


By importing customer data into Take App, businesses can immediately start building relationships and driving sales through personalized outreach.


## 6. Keeping Customers Engaged with Automated Workflows


Automated workflows save time by keeping customers informed without requiring manual follow-ups. Businesses can use these workflows to send order updates via WhatsApp and email, request reviews from satisfied customers, and offer discount codes for future purchases.


##### **Pro Tips for Workflows**


-


Automate payment reminders to recover abandoned orders and increase conversion rates.[See more.](https://help.take.app/en/articles/10632318-payment-recovery-workflow)


-


Set up post-purchase review requests to encourage customer feedback, boosting credibility and engagement. This is a great opportunity to offer discount codes to incentivize repeat purchases.[See how.](https://help.take.app/en/articles/10632201-review-request-workflow)


## 7. Gaining Insights with Customer Analytics


Understanding **customer behavior** is key to optimizing business strategies, and Take App provides valuable analytics to help businesses track and act on insights. Key metrics include:


-


**Customers by orders:** Identify your most frequent buyers and reward their loyalty.


-


**Inactive customers:** Spot customers with no purchases in the last month or more and target them with re-engagement campaigns.


-


**Customers who refund frequently:** Detect patterns and address potential issues to reduce refund rates.


-


**New customers by date:** Track the daily number of customers making their first orders to measure acquisition trends and marketing effectiveness.


##### **How to Act on This Data**


-


Focus on high-value customers and tailor special promotions to retain them.


-


Re-engage inactive buyers with personalized incentives.


-


Use refund data to identify product or service improvements and enhance customer satisfaction.


-


Monitor new customer trends to evaluate the success of marketing efforts and optimize acquisition strategies.
