# Don Pastel: Where Peru Meets Cuba in Every Bite

Ariel, the passionate founder of Don Pastel, has created a sweet fusion of Peruvian and Cuban flavors, bringing his innovative pastry business to life. Specializing in desserts and pastries that blend Cuba’s rich traditions with Peru’s exotic fruits, Don Pastel offers treats that are as unique as they are delicious. Each bite tells a story of resilience, cultural fusion, and a deep love for authentic flavors.


## A Business Rooted in History and Flavor


Ariel’s journey began 17 years ago when he emigrated from Cuba to Peru. Back in Cuba, he had to make sweets in secret due to the government’s restrictions on private businesses. Upon arriving in Peru, he discovered a country full with vibrant flavors and exotic fruits. Inspired by this new world of ingredients, he combined his Cuban heritage with Peruvian influences, creating desserts that are both nostalgic and innovative.


## The Choice of Take App: Simplicity and Efficiency


Managing an online store was initially a challenge for Don Pastel. Before discovering Take App, Ariel struggled with the technical difficulties and unexpected costs of running a WordPress store. Switching to Take App transformed the business—making management simpler and more accessible. The platform’s rapid improvements, including integrations with popular regional payment methods like Mercado Pago, have significantly streamlined transactions, allowing Ariel to focus on what matters most: delivering high-quality products to customers.


## Strengthening Customer Relationships


Ariel mentions that in Latin America, many consumers prefer to place orders via WhatsApp rather than navigating a traditional website. Take App enabled Don Pastel to implement a digital catalog that enhances the customer experience, simplifying communication and minimizing order errors. The ability to send orders via WhatsApp has not only increased sales but also fostered greater customer trust, making interactions feel more direct and personal.
