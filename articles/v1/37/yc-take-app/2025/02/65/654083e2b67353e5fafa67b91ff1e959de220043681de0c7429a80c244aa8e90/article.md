# The Best Free Tools for Small Businesses Selling Online

Running an online business doesn’t have to be expensive. With the right tools, you can manage sales, marketing, and customer support efficiently without breaking the bank. Whether you’re selling through WhatsApp, social media, or a website, these free tools can help you streamline operations and grow your business.


## 1. Take App – Hassle-Free Online Store for WhatsApp Sellers


If you sell online,[Take App](https://www.take.app/) is a powerful tool that simplifies the process of setting up an online store and receiving orders. Unlike other platforms that charge transaction fees, Take App ensures you keep 100% of your profits. Key features include:


-


Free online store setup


-


Easy order management


-


Seamless payment integrations, supporting 70+ payment methods, including local options like UPI in India and PayNow in Singapore


-


Built-in local delivery features, such as proximity-based fees and scheduling


For those who rely on WhatsApp for customer communication, Take App allows customers to send you their orders details directly through WhatsApp. You can also send automated WhatsApp messages for order confirmations, low stock alerts, and customer reviews. Additionally, Take App is optimized for mobile, making it an ideal solution for entrepreneurs looking for a smooth and intuitive selling experience.


## 2. Canva – Create Stunning Marketing Graphics


Marketing visuals are essential for attracting customers.[Canva](https://www.canva.com/) offers free templates for social media posts, product catalogs, and promotional banners. Key benefits:


-


Free stock photos and icons


-


Customizable templates for businesses


-


Drag-and-drop design tools


No design experience? Canva makes it easy to create professional-looking graphics in minutes. You can also collaborate with team members in real-time and save your designs for future edits, making it a great long-term tool for consistent branding and marketing efforts.


## 3. WhatsApp Business – Streamline Customer Communication


If you're using WhatsApp for sales, the[WhatsApp Business](https://business.whatsapp.com/) app is a must. It offers:


-


Automated replies for "greeting" and "away messages


-


Business profile with store hours and location


-


Labels to organize chats and customers


-


Quick replies for faster responses


This helps you provide better customer service and manage conversations efficiently. Additionally, the app allows you to set up a catalog where customers can view your products.
