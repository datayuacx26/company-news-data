# Take App vs. Shopify: Which E-commerce platform is right for you?

When it comes to launching an online store, choosing the right e-commerce platform can make all the difference. Two popular options are Take App and Shopify, each offering unique features tailored to different business needs. In this article, we’ll compare these two to help you make an informed decision.


## Plan Pricing: Affordability Matters


One of the biggest differences between Take App and Shopify is pricing. Shopify offers four main plans, with the cheapest, the Basic plan, costing **$29 per month** .


In contrast, Take App provides a more budget-friendly approach with three plans:


-


Free Plan – Ideal for businesses just starting out


-


Business Plan: $50/month


For entrepreneurs watching their budget, **Take App’s free plan** provides a way to start selling online without any upfront investment, whereas Shopify requires a monthly commitment from the start.


## Transaction Fees: Understanding the Costs


While Shopify’s subscription plans have a flat monthly cost, there’s an additional cost—transaction fees. Shopify takes a cut from every sale, which varies depending on the plan. For example, on the Basic plan, Shopify charges 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction.


Take App, on the other hand**, does not charge any additional transaction fees for unlimited orders.** You only pay for your subscription plan (if you choose a paid one), ensuring more predictable costs. This difference can significantly impact your profits, especially for businesses with high sales volume.


## ​​WhatsApp Integration: Take App’s Key Advantage


One of Take App’s most powerful features is its **WhatsApp integration** —a game-changer for businesses looking to connect directly with customers. With Take App, you can:


-


Receive and process orders with your existing WhatsApp account.


-


Send automated WhatsApp messages to your customers and staff, including order confirmations and updates, low stock alerts, and review requests.


-


Connect your **WhatsApp Business number** to send messages from your own brand name and logo, creating a professional e-commerce experience.


Shopify does not offer direct WhatsApp integration, meaning businesses that rely heavily on WhatsApp for communication and sales may find Take App a better fit.


## Shared Features: What Both Platforms Offer


Both Take App and Shopify provide essential e-commerce features:


-


**Unlimited Products** – Sell as many products as you want


-


**Staff Accounts** – Manage your team with multiple accounts


-


**Custom Domain** – Strengthen brand identity with a personal domain


-


**Third-Party App Integrations** – Expand functionality with plugins


However, Shopify has an edge in:


-


Customization – Offers more design flexibility


-


Advanced Analytics – Shopify provides 60+ reports, while Take App offers 20


-


Scalability – Better suited for larger enterprises with complex needs


If you require advanced customization and in-depth reporting, Shopify may be the better option.


## Ease of Use: Simplicity vs. Complexity


Many entrepreneurs struggle with setting up and managing an online store, and Shopify, while powerful, comes with a steeper learning curve. Shopify requires more time and technical knowledge to set up a store, manage inventory, and configure settings.


Take App **prioritizes simplicity** , making it easy for solo entrepreneurs and small businesses to get online quickly. No coding skills are required, and the platform is intuitive enough for anyone to create a professional-looking store fast.


With Take App, you can **launch your website in just a few minutes** , making it an ideal choice for business owners who want to start selling right away. Once your store is up, you won’t even need to access the admin dashboard—you can seamlessly take orders and manage your business directly through WhatsApp.


Take App is also **optimized for mobile** , offering a smooth experience for both merchants and customers who rely on their smartphones for online shopping.


## Payment Gateways and Localization: Catering to Your Market


Payment options and localization are vital for reaching your target audience. Take App supports 70+ payment methods, including credit/debit card payment gateways as well as a focus on local payment methods—such as UPI in India and PayNow in Singapore. On the other hand, Shopify offers over 100 payment options, primarily international and credit card-based.


Moreover, customers do not need an email or credit card to place an order on Take App, making the purchasing process more accessible and frictionless. If your business targets markets with diverse local payment preferences, Take App's localized approach provides a significant advantage.


Additionally, Take App is **optimized for local businesses** , with built-in local delivery features, including:


-


Proximity-based delivery fees


-


Delivery scheduling


-


Daily order capacity limits


This makes it easier for small businesses to manage deliveries without needing complex third-party integrations.


## **Conclusion: Making the Right Choice**


Take App is the perfect solution for **small businesses and solo entrepreneurs** seeking an **affordable, easy-to-use platform with WhatsApp integration** and localized payment options.


If you’re running a larger business and need advanced customization and scalability, Shopify may be the better choice. However, if your focus is on simplicity, affordability, and direct customer engagement, Take App stands out as the best option.
