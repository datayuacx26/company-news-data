## SEO and Trackings


Search Engine Optimization (SEO) helps improve your store’s visibility on search engines like Google, making it easier for potential customers to find your products or services.


It's an important marketing strategy because good SEO drives organic traffic to your online store, saving you money on paid ads while increasing your reach and potential sales.


**How to improve your SEO:**


-


Use descriptive keywords in your product titles and descriptions. For example, instead of “Gift Box” write “Customizable Gift Box for Birthdays and Anniversaries”.


-


Include your location in descriptions to attract nearby customers. For instance, “Artisan Coffee Roaster in Singapore”


-


Regularly update your store with new products or content, as fresh updates can improve rankings.


-


Use tools like[Google Keyword Planner](https://ads.google.com/home/tools/keyword-planner/) to identify popular search terms in your niche.


**How to do it with Take App
**You can enhance your SEO by going to **Settings > SEO and Tracking** in your Take App admin panel. For a detailed tutorial, click[here](https://help.take.app/en/article/seo-and-trackings-tya4sz/) .


## Request Reviews


With Take App, you can request customer reviews that appear directly on your store, showcasing trustworthiness and customer satisfaction. Reviews build trust and influence new customers to make purchases.


**Tips:**


-


After fulfilling an order, send a friendly review request. For example, “We hope you loved your purchase! Could you take a moment to leave us a review?”


-


You can automate this with Take App. You can set up a *Review Request Workflow* , to collect customer reviews automatically after order. Offer an incentive. Provide small discounts or loyalty points in exchange for honest reviews.


**How to do it with Take App

## QR Code Download


A QR code linked to your Take App store provides a quick and convenient way for customers to access your store. Whether printed on packaging, flyers, or displayed at your physical location, QR codes bridge the gap between your offline and online presence. They’re a modern, user-friendly tool to attract and engage customers.


**Tips for using QR codes effectively:**


-


Place strategically: Add your QR code to packaging, receipts, business cards, or flyers so customers can easily scan and access your store.


-


Use in ads: Include the QR code in offline ads, such as posters, to drive traffic to your store.


-


Label the QR code with a call-to-action like “Shop Now!” or “Scan to Browse Our Store” to encourage scanning


**How to do it with Take App
**Go to your admin menu, and click on “Marketing”. Go to the “Download QR code” section and click on the “Download” button. You can see a detailed tutorial,[here.](https://help.take.app/en/article/download-store-qr-code-mfl0ci/)


## WhatsApp Auto-Reply


A WhatsApp auto-reply is a pre-written message automatically sent to customers when they contact your business. Whether it’s a greeting, acknowledgment, or essential information, this feature ensures customers receive an immediate response without needing your constant attention.


Auto-replies help you maintain excellent customer service, even during busy times or outside business hours. Quick responses improve engagement, build trust, and enhance the overall customer experience, making your business appear professional.


**Tips:**


-


Include your Take App store link in your welcoming message. For example, “Hi there! Thanks for reaching out. We’ll get back to you within a few hours. In the meantime, check out our store here: \[insert link\].”


-


Include FAQ’s: Address common questions, like “What are your delivery times?” or “What are your payment options?”

Learn how to set it up[here](https://help.take.app/en/article/whatsapp-auto-reply-okx2pw/) .

## Link in Bio


A clickable link in your social media bio directs customers straight to your online store, making it easy for them to explore your products or services. It’s one of the most effective tools for converting social media traffic into store visits, helping you drive sales and build brand awareness. Without it, you risk losing interested customers who may not know where to find you online.


**Tips:**


-


Include your Take App store link in your link-in-bio, so customers can access your store easily. Learn how to do it[here](https://help.take.app/en/article/add-take-app-link-to-instagram-link-in-bio-1c0ws2c/) .


-


Use a short, memorable Call to Action (CTA) in your bio. For example, “Shop our handmade goods 👇” or “Order your favorite treats here!”


-


Regularly update your bio to match current promotions. For example, “New arrivals just dropped! Click below to explore.”


-


Highlight the link in posts or stories. For example, “Want to order? Link in bio!”


-


Track clicks. Use a URL shortener or analytics tool to see how many people are clicking your link.

Add links to your social media profiles or other important resources to drive traffic. ## Membership - Rewards


The Membership feature allows you to enhance customer engagement by requiring customers to create an account and log in to access your store. Additionally, you can activate rewards, enabling customers to earn store credits based on a percentage of their total purchase amount. These credits can be accumulated and redeemed as a form of payment for future purchases.


Membership programs encourage repeat purchases by fostering customer loyalty and rewarding their ongoing support. By offering exclusive benefits, you differentiate your store from competitors and build long-term relationships with your customers.


**Tips:**


-


Promote your loyalty program**:** Highlight the program on your store and social media. For example, include a banner on your page saying, “Earn 5% back in points with every purchase!” or post on Instagram, “Don’t forget to collect loyalty points with every order.”


-


Personalize offers**:** Send messages to regular customers when they have enough points to redeem. For instance, “Hi Alex, you’ve earned 100 points! Treat yourself to a discount today!”


**How to do it with Take App
**Go to Admin > Settings > Membership, and enable the rewards feature. Set a percentage that works for your profit margins (e.g., 5% back on every order). To see a step-by-step tutorial, click[here](https://help.take.app/en/article/membership-loyalty-rewards-115z1j/) .
