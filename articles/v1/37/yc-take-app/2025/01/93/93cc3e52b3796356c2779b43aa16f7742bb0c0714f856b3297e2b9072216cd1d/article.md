# Take App 2024 Review

2024 was a year of remarkable growth at Take App. We welcomed **100k+ new registrations from 150+ countries** and processed over **1M orders** . Here's a recap of what we achieved.


## Global Expansion


We expanded our global reach with support for 10 languages, including French, Indonesian, and Spanish.


We introduced[30+ new payment methods](https://www.take.app/features/payments) to accept local payments, including Wise, Revolut, SEPA, Mercado Pago, Pagadito, Paystack, DPO, Airtel, MTN, Tap Payment, Instapay, Yoco, WigWag, IkhoKha, DuitNow, Razorpay (Curlec), Payex, QRIS, Pocket Pay, Pix, OXXO, UPI, and more.


## Ecommerce for WhatsApp


We expanded our platform to support general e-commerce businesses alongside F&B stores.


Key improvements included[storefront design enhancements](https://help.take.app/en/article/edit-storefront-design-3ei44c/) such as Saved Cart for returning customers,[announcement banners](https://help.take.app/en/article/announcement-banner-to-let-customer-join-whatsapp-group-182pq2d/) ,[customer reviews](https://help.take.app/en/article/customer-review-z8m75q/) ,[custom menus](https://help.take.app/en/article/add-custom-menu-f191g7/) , and a dedicated search page.


We introduced essential e-commerce features like[store-wise automatic discounts](https://help.take.app/en/article/create-automatic-discount-eg0a48/) ,[membership features](https://help.take.app/en/article/membership-loyalty-rewards-115z1j/) , and[advanced fulfillment tracking](https://help.take.app/en/article/delivery-tracking-2t62j0/) ,[in-house drivers workflow](https://help.take.app/en/article/fulfillment-workflow-1weuvkh/) , low inventory alerts for staff, and[WhatsApp reminders for payments](https://help.take.app/en/article/payment-recovery-workflow-2237rz/) ,[reviews](https://help.take.app/en/article/review-request-workflow-15x9azy/) , and[delivery schedules](https://help.take.app/en/article/delivery-reminder-automated-whatsapp-y3we9v/) .


## Enterprise Features


We scaled our services to meet the demands of enterprise customers with advanced requirements.


We introduced[pay-as-you-go billing](https://help.take.app/en/article/usage-quota-and-pay-as-go-billing-4y1tqh/) for scaling operations,[platform API](https://platform.take.app/) and[webhooks](https://help.take.app/en/article/webhook-7su1a9/) for ERP and internal system integrations, and new integrations with Zapier, Shopify, Google Sheets, and Lalamove.


We launched[the official WhatsApp Business Platform integration](https://help.take.app/en/article/whatsapp-business-integration-1350wpc/) , enabling verified accounts, auto-replies, and WhatsApp Catalogs.


## Enhanced Checkout Flexibility


We made checkout more versatile and feature-rich. Customers can[upload payment proof](https://help.take.app/en/article/payment-proof-upload-8qin2e/) and provide additional details like pickup locations easily. We added support for[map-based delivery](https://help.take.app/en/article/enable-map-location-pin-location-rcp4e2/) using Google or OpenStreetMap and introduced options for[service charges](https://help.take.app/en/article/adding-service-charge-1fpabwg/) ,[tips](https://help.take.app/en/article/enable-tip-function-18mk3mr/) , and[payment processing fees](https://help.take.app/en/article/payment-processing-fee-1gk2mu8/) .


We enabled payments with[store credits and partial payment options](https://help.take.app/en/article/store-credits-partial-payment-a0j5mm/) .
