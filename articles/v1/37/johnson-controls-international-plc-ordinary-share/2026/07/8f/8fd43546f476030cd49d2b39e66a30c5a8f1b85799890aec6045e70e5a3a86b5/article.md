Johnson Controls was named to[the Wall Street Journal’s inaugural list of the 2026 Best Companies for the Future](https://www.wsj.com/rankings/best-companies-for-the-future/full-rankings-2026?mod=ig_bestcompaniesforthefuture&asset_id=ADVOCACY_205_6a296f72ad6c1447b2b01f8c&cpost_id=6a3c1c72e7fd9e2eeae742e7&post_id=20792165232&sn_type=LINKEDIN&user_id=6a2affb947e03c0e20697e8f) . The list evaluates how large corporations stack up related to AI readiness, innovation, talent readiness, financial fitness, resilience and agility. Companies listed on the S&P 500 were scored based on 30 indicators. Of note, Johnson Controls ranked in the top 10% for AI readiness, reflecting the value the OpenBlue AI-powered digital ecosystem has provided to customers and the company’s results in leveraging AI internally.
