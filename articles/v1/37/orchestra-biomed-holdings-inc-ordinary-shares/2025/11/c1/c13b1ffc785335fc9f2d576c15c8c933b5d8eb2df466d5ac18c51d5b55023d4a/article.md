#### Virtue SAB: Advancing a Differentiated Approach to Coronary Treatment


In late October, Orchestra BioMed announced that first patients were enrolled


in the Virtue Trial


, a U.S. pivotal IDE study evaluating the Virtue® Sirolimus AngioInfusion™ Balloon (Virtue SAB) against Boston Scientific’s AGENT™ paclitaxel-coated balloon for the treatment of coronary in-stent restenosis (ISR). Coronary ISR is a difficult-to-treat and serious complication of coronary stenting that increases the risk of life-threatening heart problems, affecting an estimated 100,000 patients in the U.S. annually.


The Virtue Trial marks a significant step toward U.S. regulatory approval for Virtue SAB and reflects a broader paradigm shift in coronary interventions toward “leave-nothing-behind”


strategies that utilize drug delivery balloons and avoid using stents, permanent metal implants.


Virtue SAB stands apart from existing drug delivery balloons which are all drug-coated balloons. Virtue SAB was designed specifically to provide important, fundamental differentiating features:


- It delivers a **large liquid dose of** Orchestra BioMed’s proprietary **SirolimusEFR™** formulation — designed for optimal arterial uptake and extended-release to achieve sustained therapeutic tissue levels of sirolimus, the proven “gold standard” drug used for many years on drug eluting stents, during the critical healing period following the interventional procedure.
- It enables drug delivery through our novel, patented, microporous balloon delivery system, meaning it is **non-coated** and avoids particulate embolization and drug loss during navigation while optimizing drug delivery at the time of inflation.
- It demonstrated **best-in-class** results in prior pilot data in coronary ISR (SABRE study), with a 12-month target lesion failure rate of only **2.8%** and **no reinterventions through 36 months** .


The Virtue Trial will enroll approximately 740 patients at up to 75 U.S. centers, with enrollment completion targeted for mid-2027.


As Mr. Hochman noted during the call, Virtue SAB represents “…a fundamentally different, and we believe, superior approach to arterial drug delivery…We believe Virtue SAB is a very exciting pivotal-stage asset that offers a substantial future value creation opportunity, which we are driving toward from a position of financial, operational, and strategic strength.”


####


#### AVIM Therapy: Broadening Clinical Impact Through the BACKBEAT Study


AVIM (Atrioventricular Interval Modulation) Therapy


continues to advance in the BACKBEAT global pivotal study


, which is designed to evaluate its potential to treat uncontrolled hypertension in patients indicated for or recently implanted with dual-chamber pacemakers.


The therapy — delivered as proprietary firmware programming — is designed to substantially and persistently lower blood pressure


while improving cardiac function in hypertensive heart disease.


Key 2025 milestones include:


- **FDA Breakthrough Device Designation** for the treatment of patients with uncontrolled hypertension who have elevated cardiovascular risk and preserved ejection fraction — encompassing an estimated **7.7 million patients in the U.S** .
- **Implementation of an FDA-approved protocol update nearly complete** , expanding eligible patients and simplifying enrollment processes across sites, enabling faster recruitment and broader representation.
- **Expanded collaboration with Medtronic** , providing a pathway for potential integration of AVIM Therapy into future leadless pacing technology.


Recent peer-reviewed publications have also reinforced the therapeutic promise of AVIM Therapy as a potential treatment for hypertensive heart disease, a condition where heart muscles become damaged over time due to prolonged or chronic high blood pressure:


- **JACC: Advances** published data from the **MODERATO II study** , showing significant reductions in blood pressure and improvements in ventricular function in patients with diastolic dysfunction.
- **JACC: Clinical Electrophysiology** reported acute and chronic clinical results demonstrating sustained blood pressure reductions and improved cardiac function across multiple measures using standard and conduction system pacing methods.
- New data presented at **HRX Live 2025** confirmed that AVIM Therapy’s effects in a pilot study were **sustainable, reversible, and reproducible** over multi-year follow-up — emphasizing its potential long-term clinical utility and safety profile.
