### Company to Host Conference Call with Prepared Remarks and Q&A at 1:30 p.m. PT


LAS VEGAS, April 27, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[HF Foods Group Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=hATo3QmHL6HNcs_8KORvHctJtIugbKZHrzchRyEcm2GhFzI8q5xDu_2fPXa_JXzdFgWzkRnSrL1qCQK4MOXuAyW_b6z52wKfM_K2-ufKuiU=) (NASDAQ: HFFG) (“HF Foods” or the “Company”), a leading distributor of international foodservice solutions to Asian restaurants and other businesses across the United States, today announced it will report financial results for the first quarter ended March 31, 2026, on Monday, May 11, 2026, after market close.


The Company will host a conference call to discuss its financial results on May 11, 2026, at 1:30 p.m. PT (4:30 p.m. ET). The link to the webcast will be available on the “Events” section of the Company’s Investor Relations website at[https://investors.hffoodsgroup.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=qwE3JM4XM6TmIZETRq4ZP5SNLn-DH7zYpfhPUOAffBi5KJS3V5wRbSJZgvqh9EqWrpjj5yNpLGMOBahbQO8p4ukY9voZ_TCm2b1tKQulvy2FdCBpK9yF-N68vcDG2Zkq) . Those interested in participating in the live call can dial 1-877-407-0752 or 1-201-389-0912. The webcast will be archived and available for replay.


**About HF Foods Group Inc.**
HF Foods Group Inc. is a leading marketer and distributor of fresh produce, frozen and dry food, and non-food products to primarily Asian restaurants and other foodservice customers throughout the United States. HF Foods aims to supply the increasing demand for Asian American restaurant cuisine, leveraging its nationwide network of distribution centers and its strong relations with growers and suppliers of fresh, high-quality specialty restaurant food products and supplies in the US and Asia. Headquartered in Las Vegas, Nevada, HF Foods trades on Nasdaq under the symbol “HFFG”.
