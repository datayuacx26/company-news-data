# Granite Point Mortgage Trust Inc. Announces Fourth Quarter 2025 Common and Preferred Stock Dividends and Business Update

# Granite Point Mortgage Trust Inc. Announces Fourth Quarter 2025 Common and Preferred Stock Dividends and Business Update


December 17, 2025 5:05pm EST[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_ac38d8b18554be35b441966ae40f554a/gpmtreit/news/2025-12-17_Granite_Point_Mortgage_Trust_Inc_Announces_Fourth__167.pdf)


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Granite Point Mortgage Trust Inc.](https://www.gpmtreit.com/) (NYSE: GPMT) (“GPMT,” “Granite Point” or the “Company”) today announced that the Company’s Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.05 per share of common stock for the fourth quarter of 2025. This dividend is payable on January 15, 2026, to holders of record of common stock at the close of business on December 30, 2025.


The Company’s Board of Directors also declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.4375 per share of the 7.00% Series A Fixed-to-Floating Rate Cumulative Redeemable Preferred Stock for the fourth quarter of 2025. This dividend is payable on January 15, 2026, to the holders of record of the Series A Preferred Stock at the close of business on December 30, 2025.


***Fourth Quarter Business Update***


- During the quarter, the Company has funded approximately $4.8 million in unpaid principal balance on existing loans and has realized about $39.0 million in full and partial principal repayments.
- Refinanced Maynard, MA, REO with a first mortgage of $18.0 million and a financing spread of S+3.05%.
- Further reduced the secured credit facility borrowings by $7.5 million.
- As of December 16th, the Company carried approximately $66.9 million in unrestricted cash.


**About Granite Point Mortgage Trust Inc.**


Granite Point Mortgage Trust Inc. is a Maryland corporation focused on directly originating, investing in and managing senior floating-rate commercial mortgage loans and other debt and debt-like commercial real estate investments. Granite Point is headquartered in New York, NY. Additional information is available at[www.gpmtreit.com.](https://www.gpmtreit.com/)
