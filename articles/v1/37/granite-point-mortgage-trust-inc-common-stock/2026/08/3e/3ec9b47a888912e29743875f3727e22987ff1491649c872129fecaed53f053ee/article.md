# Granite Point Mortgage Trust Inc. Announces Refinance of Legacy CLO Assets, Extension and Upsize of JPMorgan Repurchase Facility

# Granite Point Mortgage Trust Inc. Announces Refinance of Legacy CLO Assets, Extension and Upsize of JPMorgan Repurchase Facility


August 03, 2026 8:00am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_ac38d8b18554be35b441966ae40f554a/gpmtreit/news/2026-08-03_Granite_Point_Mortgage_Trust_Inc_Announces_177.pdf)


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Granite Point Mortgage Trust Inc.](https://www.gpmtreit.com/) (NYSE: GPMT) (“GPMT,” “Granite Point” or the “Company”) today announced that it refinanced the assets that had been financed in its two remaining CRE CLOs, GPMT 2021-FL3 and GPMT 2021-FL4, on its JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. repurchase facility, which it recently extended to July 2028, with three 1-year term extension options. In connection with the refinance, the Company upsized its JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. repurchase facility to $651 million.


As of June 30, 2026, the Company’s two CRE CLOs had combined debt obligations outstanding of $521 million, with a weighted average cost of funds of SOFR+2.38%. Refinancing the legacy CRE CLO assets decreases the weighted average cost of funds by approximately 38 basis points to SOFR+2.00%. As of July 31, 2026, GPMT held approximately $35.1 million in unrestricted cash.


**About Granite Point Mortgage Trust Inc.**


Granite Point Mortgage Trust Inc. is a Maryland corporation focused on directly originating, investing in and managing senior floating-rate commercial mortgage loans and other debt and debt-like commercial real estate investments. Granite Point is headquartered in New York, NY. Additional information is available at[www.gpmtreit.com.](https://www.gpmtreit.com/)
