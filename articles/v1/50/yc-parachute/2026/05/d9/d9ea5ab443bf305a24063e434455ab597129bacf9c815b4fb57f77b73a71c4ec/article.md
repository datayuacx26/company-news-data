# Reply, drafted

Ask Parachute to draft, write, or reply to an email and it now composes the email right there in the thread. You get an editable rich-text body, a subject, and to/cc fields. Adjust anything that needs it, then copy the formatted or plain-text version straight into your mail client.


It’s built for the everyday reply: forward an email into Parachute, ask for a response that hits the points you care about, and send the result. No switching tools to write the reply you just reasoned through.


## Also in this release


- **Bulk thread management.** Move several threads into a matter at once from your work view.
- **Country at onboarding.** New organisations choose their country up front, so jurisdiction-aware answers work from day one.
- **More reliable PDF reading** , plus a clearer thread layout and a more visible in-app help button.
