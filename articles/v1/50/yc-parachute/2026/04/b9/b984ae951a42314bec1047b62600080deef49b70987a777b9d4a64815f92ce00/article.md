# Smokeball integration

If you use Smokeball to manage your practice, Parachute now connects directly to it. Pull in your matter details, contacts, and documents so the AI has the context it needs, then push the work back when you’re done.


## Pull your practice data into Parachute


Link a Parachute matter to its Smokeball counterpart and the full picture is available to the AI: matter details, parties, documents, billing history, events, and workflow stages. You can also search across your Smokeball matters and contacts at any time. No more switching between apps to find what you need.


## Push the work back to Smokeball


Once the work is done, send it straight back. Upload a document you’ve drafted in Parachute to the linked Smokeball matter, log a time entry, create a task, add a memo, or schedule an event. It all goes back to Smokeball without breaking your flow.


## Safe by design


Every action that modifies your Smokeball data requires your explicit confirmation before it goes through. Parachute can read and write to Smokeball, but it never deletes records, and nothing changes without you approving it first.


## Getting started


Head to **Integrations** in Parachute’s sidebar, find Smokeball, and hit **Connect** . Authorise through Smokeball and you’re ready to go. To unlock matter-specific tools, link a Parachute matter to a Smokeball matter through the chat.


[Full setup guide in the docs](https://help.goparachute.ai/integrations/smokeball/) .
