# Connect everything

Parachute keeps getting better at fitting into the tools you already use. This update adds another practice management integration and cloud storage access.


## Clio integration


If your firm runs on Clio, Parachute now connects directly to it. Link a Parachute matter to its Clio counterpart and the AI has your matter details, contacts, and documents at hand. When the work is done, push documents, time entries, tasks, and calendar events straight back to Clio.


Like the Smokeball integration, every action that modifies your Clio data requires your explicit confirmation. Nothing changes without you approving it first.


[Full setup guide in the docs](https://help.goparachute.ai/integrations/clio/) .


## OneDrive


Connect your Microsoft OneDrive account and browse, search, and import files directly into Parachute. When you’re done working on a document, save it back to OneDrive without leaving the app. No more downloading, editing, and re-uploading.
