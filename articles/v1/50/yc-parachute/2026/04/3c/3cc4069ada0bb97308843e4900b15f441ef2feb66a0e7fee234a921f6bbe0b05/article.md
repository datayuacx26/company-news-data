# Fresh coat of paint

A visual refresh across the board. New navigation, updated branding, and a few things to help you get more out of Parachute from day one.


## Redesigned sidebar


The sidebar has been rebuilt with clearer sections for chats, legal sources, and firm settings. Law firms get a dedicated verifications section with a badge showing how many requests are waiting. The sidebar collapses into icons when you need more screen space.


## Updated branding


New logo, new brand marks, new favicons. The updated identity rolls out across the app, login screens, and admin panel. Same Parachute, sharper look.


## Guided setup


The home page now shows setup cards that walk you through getting started: build your legal sources, establish precedents, centralise your workflow, and standardise your house style. Each card tracks your progress and adapts as you complete steps.


## Verification notifications


A notification badge in the sidebar shows pending verification requests at a glance. No need to check a separate page to see if something’s waiting for you.


## Client document uploads


You can now upload client documents directly to a matter from the sidebar panel. Documents appear with previews and can be removed with a single click.


## Trial status at a glance


If you’re on a trial, the sidebar footer shows how many days you have left. When it’s time to add a payment method, clicking the badge takes you straight there.
