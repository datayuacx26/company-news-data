# ISO 27001 certified

Parachute is certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022, the international standard for information security management.


The certification was issued by Compass Assurance Services, an accredited independent certification body, and covers the provision and operation of our platform for legal research, document drafting and document review. Certificate number 8818-4353-01. A copy is available on request fromsecurity@goparachute.ai .


Our security practices also align with SOC 2 controls covering security, availability and confidentiality.
