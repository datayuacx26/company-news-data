# Smarter document edits

Following the editor rebuild, the AI’s document editing has taken a big step up.


## Bigger edits, in one pass


The AI can now carry out large, multi-step edits in a single pass rather than breaking them into repeated round-trips. Complex changes are faster and more reliable, and edits are style-aware, so formatting, block styles, and highlighting all come out right.


## See exactly what changed


Every change the AI makes is recorded in a trace you can scroll through. Click any entry and the editor jumps to that block and briefly highlights it, so you can follow precisely what happened and where.


## Cleaner tracked changes in Word


- AI redlines are clearly attributed to Parachute, so it’s obvious at a glance what the AI changed.
- Each suggestion you apply lands as a single, clean tracked change, and respects your document’s existing Track Changes setting.


## Also in this release


- **Multiple documents from one precedent.** The AI can produce several documents from a single precedent in one request, kept separate across a session.
