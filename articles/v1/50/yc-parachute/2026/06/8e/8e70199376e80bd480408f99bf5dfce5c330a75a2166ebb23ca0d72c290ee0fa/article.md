# Onshore AI

Some firms need their AI processing to stay in the country. Parachute can now run inference on Australian-resident infrastructure for the firms that require it.


## AI inference in Australia, if you need it


For firms that require AI processing to stay onshore, Parachute can now route inference through Australian-region infrastructure - the models that draft and review, the embeddings that power search, and document conversion. It’s optional, and we enable it for firms that need it.


## The trade-off


The most capable models aren’t always hosted in-region, so keeping inference in Australia can mean a trade-off in speed or model choice on some tasks. We’ll walk through what that means for your firm before switching it on.
