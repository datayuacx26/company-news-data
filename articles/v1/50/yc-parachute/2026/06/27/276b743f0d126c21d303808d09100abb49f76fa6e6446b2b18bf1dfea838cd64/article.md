# NetDocuments integration

If your firm stores its documents in NetDocuments, Parachute now connects directly to it.


## Import from NetDocuments


Pick documents straight from your NetDocuments cabinets and bring them into Parachute - from a chat, a matter’s documents, your knowledge base, your precedents, or a tabular review. Parachute pulls the file in so the AI can read, review, and draft from it alongside everything else. Re-importing the same document won’t create duplicates.


## Save your work back


Export a document you’ve worked on in Parachute back to NetDocuments, so your firm’s system of record stays up to date.


## Enabling it


A firm admin turns NetDocuments on under Firm settings > Integrations. Document versions and your NetDocuments region are supported out of the box.


[Full setup guide in the docs](https://help.goparachute.ai/integrations/netdocuments/) .
