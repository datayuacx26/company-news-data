# Built for barristers

Barristers and chambers work differently from firms, and Parachute now fits the way they work.


## A setup made for chambers


Sign up as a barrister and Parachute shapes itself around chambers rather than a firm. The language it uses, the details it asks for, and the way it’s organised all reflect how you actually practise, so there are no firm-shaped fields to work around.


## Roles that match the bar


Junior Counsel and Barrister are now available as roles, so Parachute pitches its work at the right level from the first conversation.


## Also in this release


- **Matter search by keyword** , making it faster to find the right matter.
- **Sharper legislation handling.** Parachute recognises named Acts by their full title and year more reliably, and can pull Commonwealth Acts directly from the Federal Register of Legislation.
