# Chris Wiseman

Chris Wiseman is Senior Vice President, Operations of MGP and is responsible for all aspects of operations across the organization. Mr. Wiseman joined MGP in October 2025.


Mr. Wiseman has nearly two decades of deep manufacturing experience and supply chain expertise, cross-functional leadership skills, and a track record of operational excellence. Most recently, Mr. Wiseman served as the Vice President of Manufacturing at Schwan’s Company, where he held increasing roles of responsibility from 2017 to 2025. Before Schwan’s, he led multiple facilities for Bimbo Bakeries USA from 2012 to 2017.


Mr. Wiseman received a bachelor’s degree in bakery science and production management from Kansas State University.
