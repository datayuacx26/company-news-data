# Unilever Updates Demerger and Share Consolidation Timetable


Unilever PLC (" **Unilever** ") today announces a revision to the timetable for the demerger of The Magnum Ice Cream Company N.V. (the " **Demerger** ") as a result of the ongoing US federal government shutdown. Read the full announcement


[here](https://otp.tools.investis.com/clients/uk/unilever/rns1/regulatory-story.aspx?cid=129&newsid=1998638) .

The Magnum Ice Cream Company – as Unilever Ice Cream is now known ahead of the demerger in November 2025 – is the world’s largest ice cream company. With an unrivalled portfolio of brands including global power brands Magnum, Ben & Jerry’s, Wall’s and Cornetto, and with a global fleet of nearly 3 million freezers, our products are available in 80 countries. The company generated €7.9 billion in revenue in 2024. For more information, visit the new
