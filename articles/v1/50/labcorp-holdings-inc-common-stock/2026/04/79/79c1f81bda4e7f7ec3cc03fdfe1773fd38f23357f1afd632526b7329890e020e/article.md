# Becker's Healthcare Podcast: Advancing Pediatric Care through Diagnostic Innovation and Collaboration

Labcorp leaders joined pediatric diagnostics experts from Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia at the Becker’s 16th Annual Meeting to discuss how strategic partnerships are accelerating innovation in pediatric testing. The conversation focused on co‑development models that combine academic clinical insight with Labcorp’s national laboratory infrastructure to translate specialized diagnostics into scalable clinical solutions.


**Deborah Boles, Ph.D.** , vice president, research & development, Labcorp, **Mindy Nye, Ph.D.** , MT (ASCP), D (ABMM), vice president, Diagnostics, Labcorp, and CHOP experts explain that by combining strong science with the ability to deliver tests at scale, these partnerships help clinicians diagnose conditions earlier and more accurately and make it easier for health systems to use advanced pediatric tests in everyday care, leading to better decisions and improved patient outcomes.
