# Digital Health News: Labcorp Advances Alzheimer’s Research Through AI‑Driven Data Innovation

Labcorp is being recognized for its leadership in applying artificial intelligence and real‑world data to accelerate Alzheimer’s disease research. In a recent *Digital Health News* article, Labcorp’s newly launched AI‑powered real‑world data platform is highlighted for its ability to help researchers analyze large, de‑identified healthcare datasets in minutes rather than months. Developed in collaboration with Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Datavant, the platform brings together diagnostic, genomic and claims data to support faster scientific discovery, improve clinical trial recruitment and streamline drug development.
