# 360Dx: How Labcorp’s Collaboration with CHOP Is Advancing the Future of Pediatric Diagnostics

A recent *360Dx* article spotlights Labcorp’s new strategic collaboration with Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia (CHOP) to accelerate the development of specialized pediatric diagnostics and expand access to innovative testing nationwide. The agreement brings together CHOP’s deep pediatric research and clinical expertise with Labcorp’s national diagnostic infrastructure, scientific capabilities and commercialization reach—helping move promising pediatric tests from discovery to broader patient access.


In the article, Marcia Eisenberg, Ph.D., Labcorp’s chief scientific officer, underscores the importance of partnering to advance pediatric testing, noting that while Labcorp can develop diagnostics internally, collaborations like this enable the company to help bring novel pediatric tests to patients at scale and extend the impact of academic innovation beyond individual institutions.
