# Medical Device Network: Labcorp Launches ColoSense Nationwide, Expanding Access to At-Home Colorectal Cancer Screening

A *Medical Device Network* article highlights Labcorp’s nationwide launch of ColoSense, the first FDA-approved RNA-based at-home screening test for colorectal cancer. The test is designed to detect biomarkers linked to both cancer and precancerous advanced adenomas in adults aged 45 and older at average risk.


ColoSense features a simplified, patient-friendly collection process intended to improve screening participation by reducing discomfort and complexity. Backed by strong clinical performance and expanded Medicare coverage, the test reflects Labcorp’s commitment to increasing access to convenient, effective cancer screening options.
