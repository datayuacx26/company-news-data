# Turning down the volume: Advancing microsampling within bioanalysis

This webinar will explore the necessity, challenges and advancements of microsampling in both preclinical and clinical bioanalysis. Microsampling has the potential to enhance patient experience, support the 3R principles, enable decentralized clinical trials and allow more frequent assessment of bioanalytical drug effects. While the benefits are compelling, challenges such as sample integrity, capillary vs. venous comparisons, bridging studies and regulatory acceptance have previously limited broad adoption.
