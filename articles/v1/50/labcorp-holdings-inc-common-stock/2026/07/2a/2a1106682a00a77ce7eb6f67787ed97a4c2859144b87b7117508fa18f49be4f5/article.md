# Bloomberg: Labcorp CEO Adam Schechter Talks Quarterly Performance and Future Growth

In an interview with *Bloomberg* , Adam Schechter, president and chief executive officer at Labcorp, discussed the company’s second quarter performance and future growth.


In the interview, Schechter highlighted growth across several high-demand specialty areas, including oncology as well as consumer-initiated testing and digital health solutions. He noted that Labcorp OnDemand is experiencing “strong double-digit growth” and emphasized the company’s continued focus on expanding innovative testing options and strengthening relationships with hospitals and regional laboratories.
