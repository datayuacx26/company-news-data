# Wall Street Journal: Labcorp CEO Adam Schechter Talks AI’s Impact on Healthcare

In an interview with *The Wall Street Journal* , Adam Schechter, president and chief executive officer at Labcorp, discussed how rapidly advancing technology, particularly AI, is reshaping healthcare and diagnostics.


In the interview, Schechter emphasized that AI has the potential to improve diagnostics, enhance treatment decisions and accelerate the shift toward more personalized medicine.
