# Today's Clinical Lab: How RNA Biomarkers Are Shaping the Next Generation of Colorectal Cancer Screening

In an article for *Today's Clinical Lab* , Dr. Brian Caveney, chief medical and scientific officer at Labcorp, discussed how advances in RNA biomarkers are helping shape the next generation of colorectal cancer (CRC) screening and support earlier disease detection.


In the article, Caveney highlighted the role of RNA-based testing in providing additional insight into cancer-related biological activity and expanding noninvasive screening options for patients. He also emphasized the importance of pairing scientific innovation with a positive patient experience, noting that simpler at-home testing approaches may help improve screening participation and outcomes.
