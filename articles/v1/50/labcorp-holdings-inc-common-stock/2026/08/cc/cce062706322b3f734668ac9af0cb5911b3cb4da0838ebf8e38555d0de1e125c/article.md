# Expanding clinical research efficiency through intelligent data flow: The IgniteData approach to healthcare innovation

*This is part of a series:*[Brief interviews with healthcare innovators](https://www.labcorp.com/education-events/series/brief-interviews-with-healthcare-innovators)


*Welcome to our “*[Brief interviews with healthcare innovators ”](https://www.labcorp.com/education-events/series/brief-interviews-with-healthcare-innovators) *series highlighting the cutting-edge companies in the Labcorp Venture Fund portfolio. The mission of the*[Labcorp Venture Fund](https://www.labcorp.com/venture-fund) *is to strategically invest in innovative businesses making healthcare more personalized, accessible, and convenient.*


*These innovators use technology to satisfy their mission of improving health system operations and patient experiences while simplifying and reducing the growing duties of physicians, healthcare staff, and administrators.*


*This month, Kristen Andrews, head of research site enablement at Labcorp, interviewed Zach Taft, CEO of*[IgniteData](https://ignitedata.com/) *, to discuss the company's mission, innovative solutions, and measurable impact. Labcorp is proud to be an investor in IgniteData, a groundbreaking platform that automates the flow of clinical data from source directly into sponsor systems, eliminating the manual bottlenecks that slow clinical trials and delay patient access to potentially lifesaving treatments.*


*Their technology allows research sites and sponsors to collaborate with greater speed, accuracy, and confidence, reflecting our shared commitment to advancing clinical research so breakthrough therapies reach patients faster. IgniteData was founded in 2014 with the mission to make hospital-to-sponsor clinical data flow speedy, reliable, and research-ready so studies can run smarter and faster.*


*“We’re excited to invest in IgniteData so we can help them deliver smarter, faster studies and better patient outcomes.” – Kristen Andrews, Head of Research Site Enablement, Labcorp*


## **Origination of innovation**


**Kristen Andrews:** Tell us the founding story of IgniteData and its mission.


**Zach Taft:** IgniteData was founded with a straightforward but powerful ambition: unlock healthcare data that’s often trapped in electronic health record (EHR) systems and put it to work improving clinical research and patient outcomes. Our founders, Rich Yeatman and Dan Hydes, were initially inspired by observing the data silos and inefficiencies involved in running clinical trials at large research hospitals, particularly the challenges of accessing and transferring high-quality data.


In the company's early years, we built a highly successful patient-recruitment business, helping sponsors enroll thousands of patients into clinical trials. That work gave us deep insight into how research operates in the real world and led to partnerships supporting real-world evidence studies with major life science organizations.


Time and again, however, the same fundamental bottleneck surfaced: transferring high-quality clinical data from hospitals to research teams was slow, manual, unreliable, and error prone. By 2019, the ecosystem was ready for change.


Emerging interoperability standards, evolving regulation, and growing acceptance of secure cloud-based software made it possible to build something genuinely new. Early validation with partners including AstraZeneca and University College London Hospitals proved our approach, while a[codevelopment partnership](https://ignitedata.com/ignitedata-advances-clinical-trial-efficiencies-with-successful-deployment-at-leading-cancer-center/) with Memorial Sloan Kettering (MSK) Cancer Center helped accelerate our platform in one of the world's leading clinical research environments.


Today, our mission is simple but powerful: enable seamless hospital-to-sponsor data flow so clinical trials can run faster, more efficiently, and with higher-quality data.


**Kristen Andrews:** What was the inspiration behind starting this venture?


**Zach Taft:** The inspiration is both personal and professional. Our founders, Rich and Dan, each lost their mothers to cancer. I also lost my mother at a young age.


What began as a deeply personal commitment to improving outcomes for patients through better clinical research evolved into a professional calling for all of us. At the same time, our collective experience in technology, healthcare operations, and research made it clear that many barriers in clinical trials are avoidable.


We’re united by the belief that progress in research matters and it shouldn’t be slowed by:


- Technology
- Manual processes
- Avoidable barriers
- Fragmented data
- Operational inefficiencies
- Therapeutic area


Our purpose is to remove friction from clinical research to help the healthcare ecosystem deliver smarter, faster studies and better outcomes for patients.


## **New technology in healthcare**


**Kristen Andrews:** What specific problems in the healthcare system does IgniteData address?


**Zach Taft:** We address a major inefficiency at the intersection of healthcare and clinical research: the manual, time-consuming process of re-entering patient data fromEHRs into research systems. Research teams spend countless hours transcribing information that already exists in the EHR, leading to:


- Delays
- Higher costs
- Data-quality issues
- Staff burnout
- Limited site participation


Every single day that data is delayed represents a $1-$8 million[opportunity cost](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10565190/) to the sponsor. By eliminating weeks from each data transfer, we unlock significant opportunities for pharmaceutical companies to de-risk those costly delays and connect patients with viable opportunities faster.


**Kristen Andrews:** Describe your innovative solution and how it works in practice.


**Zach Taft:** Our core solution, Archer, connects directly into a site's existing source for a simple implementation that can be completed in less than 90 minutes, although broader organizational onboarding may take a few weeks. Once live, Archer automates the flow of clinical data from the source to sponsor using modern interoperability standards such as HL7 FHIR and API-driven integrations.


It maps study-specific data to source fields and continuously extracts relevant information as care is delivered, sending it directly into the sponsor's system with full traceability back to the source. The results are measurable:


- 99.999% data accuracy
- 6.8 times faster movement of data
- 87.15% reduction in data queries
- Faster study execution


Research staff are freed from transcription work to focus on patient care, while sponsors gain faster, higher-quality data to drive study execution.


**Kristen Andrews:** What makes Archer unique compared to existing alternatives?


**Zach Taft:** The platform is built with a singular focus on quality and efficiency. We’re not trying to do everything. We’re focused on one critical problem and solving it at scale.


However, three things set us apart.


**1. It’s system agnostic.**


Archer works across different health systems, research platforms, and sponsor environments without requiring organizations to adopt a specific technology stack. Archer can accept data inputs from any source and transfer data outputs to any sponsor system. No other tool delivers full agnosticism, making us flexible and uniquely accessible to all sites and sponsors globally.


**2. It doesn’t store patient data.**


Archer acts as a secure, real-time conduit that moves data from source to destination with full traceability, simplifying compliance and meeting HIPAA, GDPR, GCP, and other regulatory requirements worldwide, all while not persisting any patient data. This reduces risk and simplifies adoption.


**3. It has a premium site network.**


Archer’s proven success has resulted in a rapidly growing network of top-ranked, global sites. More than 80% of National Cancer Institute–designated clinical research sites, including eight of the top 10 ranked cancer institutions, are engaging with Archer and prioritize sponsors engaged with IgniteData.


## **How technological innovation improved operations and patient outcomes**


**Kristen Andrews:** How has Archer improved patient care and operational efficiency in hospitals and health systems?


**Zach Taft:** Within hospitals and health systems, our primary users are clinical research coordinators. Before Archer, these teams spent a significant portion of their time manually transcribing and validating data, a process that’s error prone and a well-documented source of frustration and burnout.


Archer automates that work, allowing coordinators to focus on patient-facing activities, data review, and study management. Coordinators report a satisfaction score of 92%, most commonly citing usability, data quality, and their collaborative experience with IgniteData as primary sources of satisfaction.


By reducing data-transfer durations from 30 days down to three, we’re connecting patients with data-driven decisions in near real time, allowing sponsors to expand or suspend trials based on confirmed outcomes. In 2025, we saw a:


- 676% increase in enrolled patients
- More than 10-fold increase in exported data points
- 489% increase in live studies


For health systems, this translates into increased capacity and more sustainable research operations. Sites can run more studies in parallel, which also expands patient access to clinical trials.


For sponsors, faster and higher-quality data assists better decision-making. Reducing delays in data access can significantly reduce the cost of clinical trials, where each day of delay can represent millions of dollars in[opportunity cost](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10565190/) .


From a patient perspective, faster data flow means quicker insights, more responsive trials, and improved access to innovative treatments.


**Kristen Andrews:** Can you share a case study that demonstrates your impact?


**Zach Taft:** Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center is our most comprehensive and long-standing example. Through a[codevelopment partnership](https://ignitedata.com/ignitedata-advances-clinical-trial-efficiencies-with-successful-deployment-at-leading-cancer-center/) , MSK has enabled Archer across approximately 60 studies, automatically transferring more than 150,000 clinical data points directly from the EHR into sponsor and investigator research systems.


In a timed validation, Archer-supported data entry was 58% faster and 99% less error prone than manual abstraction, and research staff expressed a strong preference for the automated workflow. MSK leadership now views Archer as part of their standard operating model and has articulated a long-term strategy to transition all eligible studies into the platform.


From a sponsor perspective, AstraZeneca has reported a 70% reduction in manual data entry and an 87% reduction in data queries. Johnson & Johnson has established an enterprise contract and is actively scaling Archer across dozens of studies at leading institutions. As Amy Cramer of Johnson & Johnson noted, "We're at the tipping point; with Ignite, we're scaling sites and data to make EHR-to-sponsor the norm."


## **The future of innovation in healthcare**


**Kristen Andrews:** What are your plans for future innovations?


**Zach Taft:** Our ultimate intention is to transfer 100% of the data necessary for comprehensive decision-making on clinical trials, across all data domains and data types, both structured and unstructured, spanning all therapeutic areas. Our next major area of innovation is unstructured clinical data, such as clinical notes and imaging.


While many companies claim to support unstructured data in clinical trials, delivering the consistency, auditability, and scalability required for regulated research environments is an entirely different challenge. Through close partnerships with leading research sites and sponsors, we’re applying large language models in a targeted way to specific, high-value data types where accuracy, traceability, and repeatability can be validated in real-world environments.


This capability will expand incrementally to ensure it meets the rigorous standards required in clinical research, extending Archer's ability to reduce manual effort and unlock deeper clinical insights while maintaining the governance and scalability that underpin the platform today.


**Kristen Andrews:** How do you plan to expand your reach and impact?


**Zach Taft:** Our growth strategy centers on scaling the network while remaining the only genuinely agnostic solution in the market. We’re expanding across more research sites, more sponsors, and more therapeutic areas, deepening the value we deliver within each study.


As more data domains flow through our pipelines, we reduce operational friction for both sites and sponsors, which in turn increases site capacity and allows more studies to run in parallel. This creates a reinforcing cycle:


- Measurable efficiency gains drive broader adoption
- Broader adoption generates stronger evidence of impact
- Continuous expansion is supported


Ultimately, we aim for this cycle because increased adoption leads to more data, better outcomes, and broader scalability across the clinical research ecosystem.


Partnerships play a central role in this strategy. Our collaboration with Labcorp, for example, has the potential to link central laboratory data at scale directly into sponsor EDC systems and back into site workflows, significantly reducing manual reconciliation for research teams.


**Kristen Andrews:** How do you stay ahead of emerging trends in healthcare?


**Zach Taft:** Our most valuable insights come directly from our partners. We sit directly between research sites and sponsors, giving us a real-time view into evolving challenges and opportunities as we have continuous, in-depth conversations about trial execution, data quality, operations, emerging technology, and patient needs.


That engagement allows us to identify meaningful trends early and focus our innovation efforts on areas where new technologies can deliver practical, validated impact in clinical research. This constant feedback loop allows us to focus on practical innovation that delivers immediate value, rather than chasing theoretical use cases.
