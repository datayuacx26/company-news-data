# Fierce Biotech: Labcorp Advances AI-Powered Personalization with Launch of MyLabcorp App

In a *Fierce Biotech* article, Labcorp’s launch of its MyLabcorp app is highlighted as part of a broader push toward AI-powered lab result personalization. The app combines diagnostic results with AI-generated insights and clinical content to help patients better understand their health, responding to growing consumer use of AI tools, often without full clinical context.


The article highlights that MyLabcorp uses clinically grounded, contextual AI alongside Labcorp’s expertise to provide more meaningful explanations, track trends over time and support more informed patient-provider discussions, positioning the company alongside others moving quickly into AI-enabled diagnostics tools.
