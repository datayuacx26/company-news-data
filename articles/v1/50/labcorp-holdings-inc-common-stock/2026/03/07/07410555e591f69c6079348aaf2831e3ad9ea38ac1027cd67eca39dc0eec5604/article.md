# MedTech Dive: Labcorp Scales Digital Pathology Through Expanded PathAI Collaboration

A new *MedTech Dive* article covers Labcorp’s expansion of its collaboration with PathAI to implement the FDA-cleared AISight® Dx digital pathology platform across its U.S. its national network of anatomic pathology labs and hospital collaborations. The piece highlights how the rollout supports fully digital slide review for primary diagnosis, enabling pathologists to manage cases remotely, enhance collaboration, and integrate AI-enabled tools into routine workflows.
