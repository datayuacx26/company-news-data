### Why translation is a ceiling, not a bridge


The habit of translating is one of the most common reasons learners plateau at the beginner level. Converting every Spanish sentence into English before processing it adds a cognitive step that real conversation will not accommodate. Native speech moves at roughly 150 to 200 words per minute, and the mental detour through English creates a lag that compounds sentence by sentence until comprehension collapses. The fix is to build direct meaning associations between Spanish words and the concepts they represent, bypassing English altogether.
