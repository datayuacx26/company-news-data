## What Are Spanish Idioms and Why Do They Matter?


**Spanish expressions** carry meaning the way jokes do: you either get it or you don't, and *no amount of dictionary work* closes that gap.[An idiom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idiom) is a fixed phrase whose meaning goes **beyond its individual words** . ### Why does passive knowledge fail at natural speed?


Flashcard-based learning creates what linguists call passive knowledge.
