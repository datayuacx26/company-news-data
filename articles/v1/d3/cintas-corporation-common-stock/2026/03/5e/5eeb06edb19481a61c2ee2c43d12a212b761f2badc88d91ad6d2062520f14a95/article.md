# Cintas Earns Newsweek’s America’s Greatest Workplaces for Entry Level 2026 Award

**CINCINNATI, Ohio** –[Cintas Corporation](https://www.cintas.com/) (Nasdaq: CTAS) is proud to have earned a spot on[Newsweek’s America’s Greatest Workplaces for Entry Level 2026](https://rankings.newsweek.com/americas-greatest-workplaces-for-entry-level-2026) list. The ranking recognizes the best employers dedicated to supporting new employees.


“At Cintas, we believe strong careers begin with opportunity, training and support from day one,” said Todd Schneider, President and CEO of Cintas. “We’re committed to creating an environment where entry level employee partners can learn, grow and build meaningful careers. This recognition from Newsweek reflects the importance we place on developing talent early and investing in the future of our organization.”


A nationwide survey that gathered more than 610,000 company reviews from more than 37,000 employees aged 18 to 28, along with more than 75,000 interviews from previous surveys, helped determine the ranking.


Cintas’ commitment to supporting these entry-level employee-partners is reflected in its approach to early career development, including Cintas’[Management Trainee (MT) Program](https://careers.cintas.com/content/Campus-Recruitment/?locale=en_US) , which provides recent graduates with hands on experience across key areas of the business. Through structured training, mentorship and exposure to operations, sales and service, the program is designed to help employee partners build foundational skills, gain a broad understanding of the organization and prepare for long term career growth within Cintas.


Cintas’ strong record of supporting entry level employee partners is reflected in multiple national workplace recognitions, including:


- [Newsweek’s America’s Greatest Workplaces for Gen Z 2025](https://www.cintas.com/newsroom/details/news/2025/05/08/newsweek-names-cintas-corporation-as-one-of-america-s-greatest-workplaces-for-gen-z/)
- [Newsweek’s America’s Greatest Workplaces for Job Starters 2024](https://rankings.newsweek.com/americas-greatest-workplaces-job-starters-2024)
- [Forbes Best Employers for New Grads 2024](https://www.cintas.com/newsroom/details/news/2024/05/31/cintas-recognized-as-one-of-america-s-best-employers-for-new-graduates-by-forbes/)


#### About Cintas Corporation


Cintas Corporation helps more than one million businesses of all types and sizes get **Ready™** to open their doors with confidence every day by providing products and services that help keep their customers’ facilities and employees clean, safe, and looking their best. With offerings including uniforms, mats, mops, towels, restroom supplies, workplace water services, first aid and safety products, eye-wash stations, safety training, fire extinguishers, sprinkler systems and alarm service, Cintas helps customers get **Ready for the Workday®** . Headquartered in Cincinnati, Cintas is a publicly held Fortune 500 company traded over the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol CTAS and is a component of both the Standard & Poor’s 500 Index and Nasdaq-100 Index.
