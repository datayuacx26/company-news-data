# Cintas Earns Spot on Newsweek's America's Greatest Workplaces for Culture, Belonging | Cintas& Community 2026 List

Cincinnati, Ohio -[Cintas Corporation](https://www.cintas.com/) (Nasdaq: CTAS) announced it has been recognized by Newsweek as one of America's Greatest Workplaces for Culture, Belonging & Community. Earning a spot on the 2026 list highlights Cintas' commitment to maintaining a postive workplace culture for all employee-partners.


Cintas’ corporate culture is paramount to the company’s success, anchored by its corporate characteristics, including humility and respect for all employee-partners. Cintas employee-partners work shoulder to shoulder, collaborating closely to provide valuable products and excellent service to customers, while taking great care of each other and the communities where the company operates.


“I’m proud of the strong culture we’ve built for our nearly 50,000 employee-partners,” said Todd Schneider, President and CEO of Cintas. “Creating an environment where employee-partners know they are valued while continuing to grow in their careers is of utmost importance for our continued success and we are grateful for Newsweek’s recognition of these efforts.”


The 2026 award evaluated companies with more than 1,000 employees and recognized employers based on several metrics relevant for women, minorities and diverse demographic groups. The study included a national survey, desk research and third-party data.


### About Cintas Corporation


Cintas Corporation helps more than one million businesses of all types and sizes get Ready™ to open their doors with confidence every day by providing products and services that help keep their customers’ facilities and employees clean, safe, and looking their best. With offerings including uniforms, mats, mops, towels, restroom supplies, workplace water services, first aid and safety products, eye-wash stations, safety training, fire extinguishers, sprinkler systems and alarm service, Cintas helps customers get Ready for the Workday®. Headquartered in Cincinnati, Cintas is a publicly held Fortune 500 company traded over the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol CTAS and is a component of both the Standard & Poor’s 500 Index and Nasdaq-100 Index.
