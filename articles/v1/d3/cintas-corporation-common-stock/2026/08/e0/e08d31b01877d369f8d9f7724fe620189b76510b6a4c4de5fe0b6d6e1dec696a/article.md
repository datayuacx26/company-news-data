# Global Recognition Highlights Cintas’ Commitment to Accessibility

When Kerri Taylor arrives at Cintas’ Ontario, California, location each day, she does so with confidence and the support of her service dog, Buffy. Her story is one example of how accessibility and teamwork help create opportunities for employee-partners to succeed across the company.


For the seventh consecutive year, Cintas has been recognized as a World’s Top Disability Inclusive Business based on its performance on Disability Index®. This recognition reflects Cintas’ commitment to building workplaces where all employee-partners can contribute and drive long-term success.


Kerri, who is blind, experiences Cintas’ culture of respect every day. When she shows up to work, her service dog, Buffy, leads Kerri to her workstation that her production team has adapted to suit her needs. This allows her to excel at her job as a Bulk Folder, bagging garments, fastening buttons and supporting the location’s largest healthcare account.


Stories like Kerri’s reflect the culture Cintas strives to build: one where every employee-partner has the opportunity to contribute, grow and succeed. The company’s recognition as a World’s Top Disability Inclusive Business reinforces its ongoing focus on creating an environment where accessibility and support are woven into the fabric of the organization.
