# Me and All Hotel Leipzig Opens in Germany

**CHICAGO, IL (July 20, 2026)** – Hyatt Hotels Corporation (NYSE: H) today announced the opening of Me and All Hotel Leipzig, a 282-room hotel that combines Bauhaus-inspired design, local culture and social experiences in the heart of Leipzig. Operated by the Lindner Hotel Group as part of its strategic collaboration with Hyatt, the hotel reflects the successful combination between Hyatt’s global brand expertise and Lindner’s deep understanding of the German hospitality market. The opening marks the continued expansion of the Me and All Hotels brand in Germany, further strengthening its position within Hyatt’s Lifestyle portfolio *.*


Located in Leipzig Zentrum-Ost, just moments from Leipzig Central Station and the historic city centre, Me and All Hotel Leipzig places guests within easy reach of popular attractions, cultural institutions and business hubs. The historic Old Town, Leipzig Market Square, St. Thomas Church and the renowned Gewandhaus concert hall are all nearby, while Leipzig Central Station provides convenient connections across Germany and beyond.


**Michel Morauw, Managing Director, EAME North, Hyatt** , said, “ *Leipzig has emerged as one of Germany's most exciting urban destinations, attracting creative talent, entrepreneurs, international businesses and cultural travellers alike. Me and All Hotel Leipzig perfectly captures the energy of the city through its vibrant social spaces, design-led approach and strong connection to the local community. The opening reflects Hyatt's continued focus on expanding our Lifestyle portfolio in sought-after destinations across Europe* .”


**In the heart of Leipzig's creative and cultural scene**


The surrounding neighbourhood reflects Leipzig's evolving identity as a hub for creativity, innovation and culture. Independent cafés, galleries, bars and event venues sit alongside a growing startup ecosystem, creating an energetic atmosphere that appeals to both visitors and locals. The city is also internationally recognised for major events including the Leipzig Book Fair, music festivals and a thriving arts scene. Through collaborations with local partners, the hotel aims to become a vibrant meeting place within Leipzig’s creative scene, offering guests authentic connections to the destination beyond a traditional hotel stay.


**Bauhaus-inspired design with a contemporary twist**


Designed by Kitzig Interior Architects in collaboration with Grimbach Nogales Hochbauarchitekten, Me and All Hotel Leipzig draws inspiration from the Bauhaus movement, one of Germany's most influential design traditions. The hotel blends clean architectural lines with organic forms, creating spaces that feel both contemporary and welcoming.


A dynamic palette of colours, fluid shapes and thoughtfully selected materials runs throughout the hotel, reflecting the Me and All Hotels philosophy of individuality, creativity and local connection. Bauhaus-inspired artistic influences can be found throughout the property, creating an environment that celebrates both Leipzig's cultural heritage and its forward-looking spirit.


**A social hub for guests and locals alike**


At the heart of the Me and All Hotel Leipzig is Biladi & All, a vibrant social hub where hotel guests and locals come together to enjoy handcrafted drinks, authentic Neapolitan pizza and unforgettable experiences. The culinary focus is on authentic Neapolitan pizza, prepared using traditional methods with carefully selected ingredients and long-fermented dough, then baked at high temperatures to achieve its characteristic light, airy crust and unmistakable flavour.


As the hotel's lively bar and event venue, Biladi & All comes alive throughout the day and well into the evening. Guests can enjoy expertly crafted cocktails, local beers and a carefully curated drinks selection, while regular DJ sessions, live entertainment and community events create an energetic yet welcoming atmosphere. More than just a restaurant, Biladi & All is a place where food, music and people come together—reflecting the vibrant spirit of Leipzig and the Me and All Hotels brand.


The relaxed atmosphere reflects the brand's community-driven approach, with regular programming and social experiences creating a dynamic environment throughout the day and into the evening. Me and All Hotel Leipzig is designed as a true gathering place where locals and travellers naturally come together to work, meet, relax and experience the city.


**Designed for modern urban travellers**


The hotel features 282 guestrooms across a range of categories, including standard rooms, twin rooms, studios and boarding houses. Designed with comfort and functionality in mind, accommodations feature premium bedding, large flat-screen televisions, minibars, in-room coffee stations and keyless entry technology for a seamless guest experience. In addition to its guestrooms, the hotel offers studios and long-stay apartments, providing contemporary accommodations for everything from weekend city breaks to extended stays.


**Flexible spaces for collaboration and connection**


With 250 square metres of meeting and event space across four rooms, equipped with smart layouts and modern technology, Me and All Hotel Leipzig is well-placed to host business meetings, workshops and private events. The largest meeting room spans 100 square metres, offering flexibility for a variety of event formats. The hotel's central location further strengthens its appeal for corporate travellers and event organisers.


**Wellness in the city**


Guests have access to a 150-square-metre fitness centre equipped with a comprehensive range of cardio and strength-training equipment, alongside a 70-square-metre sauna designed for relaxation after a day in the city. Complementing the hotel's vibrant social spaces, these wellness facilities create a balanced experience for both business and leisure travellers.


**Kenneth Hein, General Manager, Me and All Hotel** **Leipzig** added, “Opening Me and All Hotel Leipzig is an exciting moment for our team and for the city. Leipzig is known for its creativity, openness and entrepreneurial spirit, and we wanted to create a hotel that reflects that energy in every detail. From stylish guestrooms and engaging social hubs to community experiences and central location, the hotel offers the perfect base for guests to relax, connect and explore the city. Whether staying for one night or several weeks, guests will experience a hotel that combines the international strength of Hyatt with the individuality and character of Leipzig.”
