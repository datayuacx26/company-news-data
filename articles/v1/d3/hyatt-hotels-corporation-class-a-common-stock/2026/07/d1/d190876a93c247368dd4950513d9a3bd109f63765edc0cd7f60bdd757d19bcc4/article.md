# The Lowline Hotel Opens Its Doors on Charleston’s King Street, Bringing the JdV by Hyatt Brand to The Palmetto State

**CHICAGO, IL \[July 15, 2026\]:** The JdV by Hyatt brand, alongside Highline Hospitality Partners, is thrilled to announce the opening of[The Lowline Hotel](https://www.hyatt.com/jdv-by-hyatt/en-US/chszh-the-lowline-hotel) , now welcoming its first guests to Charleston's newest lifestyle destination.


Living within the JdV by Hyatt portfolio, a collection of independent, neighborhood-inspired hotels within Hyatt’s Lifestyle collection, The Lowline Hotel is a reimagination of the former Hyatt Place Charleston Historic District, which was acquired by Highline Hospitality Partners with the adjacent Hyatt House hotel in November 2024.


Located on iconic King Street, The Lowline Hotel places guests within walking distance of Charleston's acclaimed restaurants, cocktail bars, boutiques, galleries, and historic landmarks. Whether shopping, dining, or exploring the city's vibrant neighborhoods, the hotel serves as an ideal home base for experiencing the best of Charleston.


"The Lowline Hotel was created to embody the best elements of one of the country's most historic and spirited cities,” said Chuck Pomerantz, Managing Partner of Highline Hospitality Partners. “Every detail was considered with precision, purpose, and intent to create an experience that feels distinctly Charleston. We wanted to create a hotel that feels rooted in Charleston while reflecting the way people travel today. Whether guests are visiting for a weekend getaway, celebrating a special occasion, or discovering Charleston for the first time, we hope The Lowline becomes part of the memories they make here."


**Design Rooted in the City and the Sea**


Designed by Ealain Studio, The Lowline Hotel draws inspiration from Charleston's maritime heritage, historic architecture, and the movement of the North Easterly Trade Winds. Rich navy tones, warm oak finishes, sculptural forms, and layered materials subtly reference ships at sea while celebrating the craftsmanship and character found throughout the city.


A winding check-in desk anchors the arrival experience as a nod to coastal movement, while handcrafted details, wrought iron-inspired elements, and thoughtfully designed transitions evoke Charleston's hidden gardens and intimate courtyards. Outdoor spaces further extend that feeling, offering moments of calm and discovery just off the energy of King Street.


Guest rooms are designed to feel more like private residences than traditional hotel accommodations, pairing rich navy tones with warm wood accents, generous seating areas, and thoughtful details throughout. Each room is centered around a custom headboard designed by Ealain Studio featuring stylized sandhill cranes native to the region, while a marble beverage cabinet finished with brass hardware offers guests a place to unwind before dinner or after a day exploring Charleston.


"The hotel serves as a living reflection of the city," said Jacqueline McGee, Principal and Design Director of Ealain Studio. "It's a modern interpretation inspired by Charleston's history and Southern culture. With each decision, we focused on creating experiences that feel distinctive to guests staying at The Lowline Hotel."


**Dining, Gathering, and Social Spaces**


Designed to be as much for locals as hotel guests, The Lowline's collection of dining and social spaces offers something for every part of the day. At the heart of the hotel,[Leeward Restaurant](https://www.opentable.com/r/leeward-charleston) and Leeward Patio serve approachable Lowcountry-inspired cuisine in a lively, welcoming setting designed for everything from pre-dinner cocktails to late-night gatherings. **Lowline Spirited Coffee & Provisions** features coffee from Savannah-based PERC Coffee alongside grab-and-go offerings, while **The Long Game at Leeward** offers a private social entertainment space featuring a golf simulator, large-screen TVs, and full access to Leeward's food and cocktail menu. The space can be reserved for celebrations, group dinners, and special events. Guests can also enjoy rooftop views of the Charleston skyline at **Pour Taproom** , which offers a curated selection of local and regional craft beers. Specialty programming, including a daily 4 p.m. Martini Hour and 11 p.m. Snack Hour, adds another layer to the guest experience.


Together, these spaces reflect The Lowline's neighborhood-inspired spirit, creating inviting places to dine, connect, and unwind from morning through night.


**Property Details**


**The 197-room property** also features[Sterling Hall](https://www.hyatt.com/jdv-by-hyatt/en-US/chszh-the-lowline-hotel/weddings) , the hotel's dedicated meetings and events venue, offering nearly **8,000 square feet** of flexible indoor event space for weddings, corporate meetings, celebrations, and social gatherings.


Additional amenities include an **indoor pool, fitness center, and valet parking** . Guests can now book stays at The Lowline Hotel website[here](https://www.hyatt.com/jdv-by-hyatt/en-US/chszh-the-lowline-hotel) .
