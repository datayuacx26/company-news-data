# Covenant Logistics and Landair Earn Partner-Level Status in John Deere's Achieving Excellence Program for Second Consecutive Year

Chattanooga, TN \[May 15th, 2026\] - Covenant Logistics and Landair, a Covenant Logistics Group company, have earned Partner-level status for 2025 in the John Deere Achieving Excellence Program — the second consecutive year both entities have received this distinction. Partner-level status is John Deere's highest supplier rating, awarded to companies that demonstrate exceptional quality, service, and a commitment to continuous improvement. The award was accepted on behalf of Covenant Logistics and Landair, a Covenant Logistics Group company, during formal ceremonies held at Deere & Company World Headquarters in Moline, Illinois.


Suppliers who participate in the Achieving Excellence program are evaluated annually in several key performance categories, including quality, delivery, process alignment, value creation, and relationships. John Deere Supply Management created the program in 1991 to provide a supplier evaluation and feedback process that promotes continuous improvement.


> “For our team to receive this recognition for the second straight year is something we don’t take lightly,” said Bo Cordell, VP Contract Logistics Sales “This is truly a team award. It reflects the collective effort of everyone across our organization who shows up every day committed to delivering excellent service, driving value for our customers, and maintaining the highest standards in everything we do — from execution and billing to the relationships we build and sustain.”


**About Covenant**


Covenant Logistics Group, Inc., through its subsidiaries, offers a portfolio of transportation and logistics services to customers throughout the United States. Primary services include asset- based expedited and dedicated truckload capacity, as well as asset-light warehousing, transportation management, and freight brokerage capability. In addition, Transport Enterprise Leasing is an affiliated company providing revenue equipment sales and leasing services to the trucking industry. Covenant's Class A common stock is traded on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol, “CVLG.”
