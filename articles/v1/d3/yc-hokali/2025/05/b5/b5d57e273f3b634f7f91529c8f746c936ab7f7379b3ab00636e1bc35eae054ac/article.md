# Empowering Excellence: Retaining and Motivating Instructors through Community and Support

Across the country, after-school and enrichment providers are facing persistent challenges in keeping instructors engaged and committed, key players in delivering quality learning experiences. From emotional burnout and lack of recognition to administrative overload and professional isolation, these issues directly affect program consistency, student participation, and long-term outcomes.


The insights presented in this article were gathered during the 2025 RTM - April National Superintendent Forum, an event that brings together K-12 education leaders to explore innovative strategies and tackle system-wide challenges. A dedicated session surfaced key challenges shared by program leaders, instructors, and education advocates, including low instructor engagement, high turnover, and the need for stronger communities of practice.


HOKALI offers an opportunity to address these challenges through a model that supports instructors at every step of their journey. Through our HOKALI Community, we provide ongoing training, personalized follow-up, and day-to-day support, creating an environment where instructors feel connected, empowered, and part of something bigger. This approach helps strengthen after-school programs from the inside out by caring for the people who bring them to life.


## Key Challenges in Instructor Retention


-


**Lack of Community and Connection** : Instructors often feel isolated, especially in part-time or freelance roles, with little opportunity for collaboration or mentorship.


-


**Limited Recognition and Growth Opportunities:** Many programs do not offer professional development, feedback, or pathways for advancement.


-


**Administrative Overload:** Instructors frequently shoulder scheduling, communication, and reporting tasks, leaving less time and energy for teaching.


-


**Emotional Burnout:** Without adequate support systems, instructors face fatigue and disconnection, leading to high turnover.
