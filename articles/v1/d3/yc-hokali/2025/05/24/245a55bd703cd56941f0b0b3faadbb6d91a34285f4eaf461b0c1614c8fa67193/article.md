# HOKALI Partners with Capitanes de Barrio to Empower Youth Through Sports and Education

At HOKALI, we believe that every child deserves the opportunity to explore their passions and build a brighter future. That’s why we are thrilled to announce our new partnership with Capitanes de Barrio, a nonprofit organization dedicated to creating equal opportunities for children in underserved communities through sports, values-based education, and community engagement


## Who Are Capitanes de Barrio?


[Capitanes de Barrio](https://www.capitanesdebarrio.org/landing-donacion) is a community-driven organization focused on using sports as a tool for social inclusion and personal development. By combining athletic activities with educational programs, they empower children and teenagers to develop essential life skills, build self-confidence, and stay connected to their communities.


Their mission is to break the cycle of inequality by providing access to quality programs that foster growth, teamwork, and resilience.


## What Does Our Partnership Mean?


In collaboration with[Civic House](https://www.civic.house/) , we are supporting Capitanes de Barrio through a strategic initiative aimed at strengthening their organizational capacity and achieving long-term financial sustainability.


We are launching a fundraising campaign targeting businesses and individuals in the U.S. to help expand Capitanes de Barrio’s impact. This campaign will enable the organization to invest in vital resources, develop new projects, and continue nurturing young leaders within their communities.


## Building Brighter Futures Together


As an ed tech company focused on expanded learning, HOKALI understands the transformative power of extracurricular engagement. Our partnership with Capitanes de Barrio aligns perfectly with our values and vision to empower youth through meaningful opportunities outside the classroom.


By supporting this initiative, we are contributing to a sustainable model that ensures children in vulnerable neighborhoods have access to the tools they need to thrive, both on and off the field.
