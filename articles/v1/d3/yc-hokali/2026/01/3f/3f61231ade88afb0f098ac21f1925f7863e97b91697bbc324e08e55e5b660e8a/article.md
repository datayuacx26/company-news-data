# What Makes HOKALI Summer Camps Special!

## Overview


Summer camp is a season of growth, connection, and meaningful experiences that extend well beyond traditional academic learning. Across the United States, thousands of children participate in summer programs each year that promote social, emotional, and cognitive development, as well as lasting memories.


Recent national research shows that in 2023, **about 55%** of parents reported their child participated in at least one structured summer activity, **while 45%** did not, often due to barriers like cost or limited availability, illustrating an ongoing gap in access to enriching summer experiences.


[Our Summer Camps](https://www.hokali.com/summer-camps) bring together enrichment, play, and growth through high-quality programs designed to work seamlessly for schools and families, helping expand access for more children.


## The Value of Summer Camps


Summer camps have been recognized as spaces where kids expand their horizons socially and intellectually. Research and industry reports identify several key benefits associated with summer camp participation:


### 1. Social and Emotional Development


High-quality camp experiences foster belonging, engagement, and positive peer-adult relationships, helping children strengthen social awareness, emotional confidence, and independence.


### 2. Growth in Personal Skills


Beyond social connection, summer camps provide hands-on opportunities for students to develop practical skills such as teamwork, communication, leadership, and problem-solving.


Through collaborative activities, students practice taking initiative, navigating challenges, and working toward shared goals in real-world settings.


### 3. Well-Being and Balance


Camp environments often offer a chance to step away from screens and routines, encouraging children to engage with peers and nature in more active ways. Participation in camp has also been linked to positive mental health outcomes, including reduced anxiety and improved emotional well-being.
