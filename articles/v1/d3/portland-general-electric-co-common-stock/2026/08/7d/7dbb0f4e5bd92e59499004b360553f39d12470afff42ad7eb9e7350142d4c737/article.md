# PGE shares ways to keep cool, reduce energy costs as hotter summer temperatures settle in

**Portland, Ore.** — The National Weather Service has issued an excessive heat warning from Tuesday to Wednesday that affects the Portland General Electric (PGE) service area. With near-record high temperatures in the forecast, customers may be looking to stay cool and keep energy costs low.


**WHAT** : Media availability - PGE spokesperson discussing ways customers can stay cool during summer heat.


**WHEN** : Tuesday, August 4, 11 a.m.


**WHERE** : 1 World Trade Center, 121 SW Salmon Street, Portland – Plaza


**Ways to help keep your home cool**


-


Run appliances such as ovens, stovetops, dishwashers and clothes dryers at night or in the early morning.


-


Close curtains on sun-facing windows or use awnings during the day and when running AC units.


**Check your thermostat**


-


Set central air thermostats to 76 degrees, rather than 70 degrees.


-


Upgrade to a programmable or[smart thermostat](https://pgemarketplace.com/Thermostats-and-Temperature/) to help you save season after season.


**Weatherize your home**


-


[Keep the heat out](https://portlandgeneral.com/save-money/save-money-home/weatherization) by making sure ducts are sealed or insulated. Use weather-stripping or caulking around doors, windows, pipes and cracks.


-


Community action agencies offer income-qualified PGE customers[Weatherization Assistance](https://portlandgeneral.com/save-money/save-money-home/weatherization/weatherization-assistance) (even for renters). If you’re a DIY-er,[Community Energy Project](https://www.communityenergyproject.org/) has free workshops and other weatherization resources.


**Sign-up for**[Flex Power](https://portlandgeneral.com/energy-shifting/flex-power) **programs that help you save energy and reduce strain on the power grid during times of high demand**


-


[Peak Time Rebates](https://portlandgeneral.com/save-money/save-money-home/peak-time-rebates) rewards customers for shifting energy use during peak times – when electricity demand and prices are highest.


-


When you’re enrolled in the[Smart Thermostat](https://portlandgeneral.com/save-money/save-money-home/smart-thermostat-program) program, your thermostat automatically adjusts a few degrees to shift your electricity use away from peak times.


-


Do less between 5-9 p.m. weekdays and save with[Time of Day](https://portlandgeneral.com/about/info/pricing-plans/time-of-day) – our unique option that offers you lower energy prices for using less energy during high demand hours.


-


[Smart Charging](https://portlandgeneral.com/energy-choices/electric-vehicles-charging/charging-your-ev/charging-your-ev-at-home) rewards you for allowing PGE to pause your EV charging for a few hours every weekday when energy demand is high.


**The PGE Customer Dashboard can help**


-


PGE customers can[sign in to their online account or create one](https://portlandgeneral.com/auth/sign-in) to access their account dashboard where they can take steps to maximize energy savings, gain insight into their bill, and get personalized recommendations for Flex Power programs.


-


Set up[text or email notifications](https://portlandgeneral.com/secure/account/manage-alerts) to get weekly estimated bill amounts.


For more ways to use energy more efficiently and participate in energy-saving programs,[visit our website](https://portlandgeneral.com/save-money/save-money-home/no-cost-and-low-cost-tips) .
