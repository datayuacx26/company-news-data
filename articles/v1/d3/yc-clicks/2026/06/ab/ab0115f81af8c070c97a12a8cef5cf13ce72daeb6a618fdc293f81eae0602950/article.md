# Introducing Clicks Health: AI agents for healthcare operations

Clicks Health is our dedicated healthcare automation offering for RCM companies, orthopedic practices, hospitals, and healthcare operators that still rely on manual queues across EHRs, PM systems, payer portals, spreadsheets, and internal tools.


Healthcare teams do not need another dashboard or another queue. They need the repetitive work to move through the systems they already use, with the controls, approvals, and audit trails that healthcare operations require.


## What Clicks Health automates


Clicks Health can automate patient credit refunds, eligibility and benefits checks, prior authorization packets, claim status checks, denials follow-up, claim submission cleanup, payment posting, and audit-ready exception queues.


For one live patient refund workflow, Clicks is helping reduce processing cost by 60%+ while keeping approval rules, escalation paths, and final exception decisions under the team's control.


## Built for the way healthcare work actually runs


Clicks Health can use APIs where they exist, or operate through the same UIs teams already use. Every action is logged, exceptions can route to a person, and sensitive workflows stay governed with HIPAA-compliant PHI handling and SOC 2 Type 2 controls.
