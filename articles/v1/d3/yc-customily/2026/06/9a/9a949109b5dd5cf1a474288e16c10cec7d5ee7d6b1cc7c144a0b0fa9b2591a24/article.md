[Customily](https://www.customily.com/) is built around that insight. ## **Why Personalization Drives Sales in Print-on-Demand**


The personalized gifts market has been growing steadily for years - and in print-on-demand, it represents some of the highest-margin, lowest-return product categories available to online sellers. The reason is straightforward: personalized products are bought with intent.


When a customer is designing a custom star map of their wedding anniversary, they're not browsing; they're buying. That emotional commitment at the point of personalization is what makes these products convert at rates that generic items rarely match.


For sellers, the challenge has traditionally been execution: how do you offer a rich personalization experience without building custom software, managing design files manually, or hiring a developer? That's exactly the gap **Customily** fills.


### **Customily's Personalization Suite: What It Covers**


Customily is not a single tool, it's a complete[personalization platform](https://www.customily.com/our-features) . Here's what merchants have access to:


**Live Preview**


Every change a customer makes - text, photo, color, layout, effect - updates the product mockup instantly on the page, before checkout. This real-time feedback loop is one of the most powerful conversion tools available in e-commerce. Customers can see exactly what they're getting, which reduces hesitation, lowers cart abandonment, and dramatically cuts post-purchase confusion and returns.


**AI-Powered Photo Effects**


Customers can upload any photo and apply professional-grade effects automatically - no design software, no technical skills required. Available effects include cartoon filter, watercolor style, oil painting, face cut, and background removal. These effects are especially popular for pet portraits, family photos, and couple gifts, and they transform a simple photo upload into a premium product experience.


**Specialty Generators**


Customily includes built-in generators for some of the best-selling personalized product types:


- Star maps: exact rendition of the night sky for any date and location in the world
- Street maps: a stylized view of any address or city
- Custom crossword puzzles: built from words and clues the customer provides
- And more, with new generators added regularly


All of these are built into Customily: no third-party tools, no extra subscriptions, no developer work required.


**Full Design Toolkit**


Beyond the specialty tools, Customily gives merchants control over every design element customers can interact with:


- Custom text fields with font selection and styling

- Shapes, borders, and layout controls
- Conditional logic: show or hide elements based on customer selections


Merchants configure which options are available and how they appear; customers get a guided, intuitive experience without seeing anything they don't need.


‍ **Print-Ready File Generation**


One of Customily's most important capabilities is automatic print-ready file generation. Once a customer completes their personalization and places an order, Customily produces a production-ready file built to the exact specifications of the product: correct dimensions, resolution, color profile, and print area.


These files are built for any production method - printed, engraved, embroidered, or otherwise - and can be routed in different ways depending on your fulfillment setup:


- Sent automatically to your connected POD partner (like merchOne) via API
- Downloaded by the seller for manual fulfillment or local production
- Forwarded to a third-party manufacturer or print shop of your choice


This flexibility means Customily works whether you're fully automated through a POD partner or managing your own production workflow.

## ‍ **How the Order Flow Works: From Personalization to Production**


One of the most important things to understand about the[Customily + merchOne](https://merchone.com/customily-integrations/) setup is that it's fully automated after the customer completes their personalization. Here's what happens:


- Customer personalizes the product in the live preview on your storefront
- Customer completes checkout as normal
- Customily automatically generates the print-ready file from the customer's inputs
- The order routes to merchOne via API integration - no manual steps required
- merchOne produces the item in one of its in-house facilities (US or EU)
- Tracking syncs back to your store automatically


No manual file preparation, no copy-pasting order details, no chasing tracking numbers. The seller's job is to set the product up correctly; after that, the system handles everything.


For sellers, this means they can build personalized products across wall art, home décor, apparel, and pet products without having to manage printing, packing, or shipping manually.


Once an order is placed, merchOne helps move the product from personalization to fulfillment automatically, with unbranded packaging and integrations through Shopify, Amazon, Etsy, and WooCommerce.


This is supported by merchOne’s broader production network, including in-house facilities for key wall art and home décor items in Europe and the US, HP Latex water-based inks, FSC-certified wood frames, up to 2.5 million units of daily production capacity, and a 95% on-time shipping target.
