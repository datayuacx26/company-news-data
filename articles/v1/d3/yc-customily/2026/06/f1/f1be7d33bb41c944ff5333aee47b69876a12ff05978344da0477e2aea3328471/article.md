# 7 Best Shopify Apps for Pet Product Customization

**The global custom pet products market hit $8.5 billion, and it's growing at 8.4% annually.** Pet owners aren't just buying for their animals anymore; they're buying *expressions of their bond* . Engraved collars with their dog's name. A blanket printed with their cat's face. A custom portrait of a rescue they adopted last spring.


For[Shopify](https://shopify.pxf.io/customily) merchants, this emotional spending power translates directly into higher average order values, lower return rates, and fiercely loyal repeat customers. But to capture it, you need the right tools.


This guide covers the **7 best Shopify apps for pet**[product personalization](https://www.customily.com/) , not the generic roundup you've seen everywhere, but a curated list of apps that actually work for pet-focused stores, whether you're selling custom dog collars, personalized pet portraits, engraved ID tags, or photo-printed apparel for proud pet parents.


## Why Pet Product Personalization Is a Winning Strategy Right Now


Before we get into the apps, let's talk about why you're here in the first place.


**Pet humanization is the defining trend in the $320 billion global pet industry.** Consumers are increasingly treating their pets as family members, and spending accordingly. **25% of pet parents say they're more likely to buy a new outfit for their pet than for themselves.** Demand for breed-specific designs grew 41% between 2022 and 2026.


The personalization angle compounds this naturally. When a customer uploads their golden retriever's photo and watches it appear on a custom blanket in real time, they've already decided to buy. Merchants using product customization apps report:


- **20–30% higher average order values** on personalized vs. standard products
- **15–25% lower return rates** on co-created items
- **Higher repeat purchase rates** , because no one forgets where they got that perfect gift


The products driving the most revenue? Engraved collars and ID tags (21% of pet accessory sales), personalized pet portraits and wall art, photo blankets and pillows, custom feeding bowls, and memorial keepsakes.


Now let's look at the tools that make it happen.

### 3. JetPrint: Best for Personalized Pet Accessories & Jewelry


**Best for:** Merchants wanting a POD supplier + personalizer combo specifically for pet accessories, watches, and jewelry


[JetPrint](https://jetprintapp.com/) is a print-on-demand platform with Shopify integration and a standout catalog of personalizable pet products, particularly ID tags, custom collars, pet-themed jewelry (like pet portrait necklaces for owners), and home décor. Customers can add their pet's name, a photo, a QR code (linking to medical info or contact details), and even custom patterns, all visualized in real time. For merchants focused on the booming pet accessories segment, JetPrint covers ground that most generic POD apps don't.


**What makes it stand out for pet stores:**


- 400+ pet product catalog including ID tags, collars, pet-themed jewelry, and home goods
- QR code personalization for smart pet ID tags, a growing niche
- Real-time design preview built into the shopping experience
- Worldwide shipping with branding options
- Pet portrait products for owners (necklaces, keychains), a high-margin gifting category


**Ideal use cases:** Custom pet ID tags with QR codes, engraved pet collar charms, pet portrait jewelry, personalized pet photo frames and canvas prints

These aren't products for the pet, they're for the pet *owner* , and they sell exceptionally well as gifts (birthdays, holidays, new pet, memorial). ShineOn's fulfillment is US-based and fast, which helps with conversion on gift-driven purchases.

Why does this belong in a pet product personalization list? Because personalization isn't just about the product - it's about the experience. Imagine sending a birthday email to a customer that says "Happy birthday, Max! 🐾 Here's 15% off a personalized treat bag for your 3-year-old beagle." That level of targeting converts at a fundamentally different rate than a generic sale email.


**What makes it stand out for pet stores:**


- Collects pet names, breeds, birthdays, and photos from store visitors
- Enables hyper-targeted email campaigns around pet birthdays and milestones
- Creates emotional connection between your brand and the pet owner
- Useful data layer for recommending breed-specific or personalized products
- Works for dogs and cats (multi-pet households supported)


**Ideal use cases:** Birthday email campaigns for personalized gift reminders, breed-targeted product recommendations, loyalty program segmentation, new pet owner welcome flows


**Pricing:** Plans starting at $25 per month


### 7. Seal Subscriptions: Best for Building Recurring Revenue from Personalized Pet Products


**Best for:** Pet stores selling consumables, treats, grooming supplies, or curated subscription gift boxes, and merchants who want to lock in repeat buyers after a personalized first purchase


Here's the angle most pet store guides miss entirely: personalization gets the first sale, but subscriptions keep customers for life. Seal Subscriptions is one of Shopify's highest-rated apps (4.9 stars, 2,400+ reviews) and it's particularly well-suited to pet stores because pet owners are naturally recurring buyers, they replenish food, treats, and grooming products on predictable schedules.


The strategic play for personalized pet stores is to pair a customization app (like **Customily** or Shopaw) with a subscription layer. A customer who buys a custom-engraved collar is an ideal candidate for a monthly "new pet bandana" or "seasonal treat box" subscription. Seal makes it straightforward to set this up, and its flexibility is genuinely impressive: mixed subscriptions (different intervals for different products in one order), gifting options, skip/pause controls, and tiered loyalty discounts that reward long-term subscribers.


**What makes it stand out for pet stores:**


- Mixed subscriptions: customers can subscribe to treats monthly and grooming supplies every 6 weeks in the same checkout
- Gifting subscriptions: customers can gift a subscription to another pet owner, turning gift recipients into new subscribers
- Tiered loyalty discounts that deepen over time, perfect for building brand lock-in with emotional pet buyers
- Skip, pause, and swap controls reduce churn (53% of shoppers are more likely to subscribe if they can pause easily)
- Integrates with Klaviyo and Google Analytics for segmented, data-driven follow-up campaigns


**Ideal use cases:** Monthly personalized treat boxes, recurring pet food or supplement orders, seasonal custom accessory subscription boxes, "new pet welcome" subscription starter kits


**Pricing:** Free plan available (up to 50 subscriptions); paid plans from $5.95/month

### Frequently Asked Questions


**What is the best Shopify app for selling personalized pet products?** ‍


Customily is the most complete option for pet-focused Shopify stores. It combines a live product personalizer with automated POD fulfillment, supports photo uploads, text, and engraving files, and integrates directly with suppliers like Printify, ShineOn, and JetPrint.


**Can I sell custom pet collars and ID tags on Shopify?**


Yes. Apps like Customily, Shopaw, and JetPrint let customers personalize collars and ID tags with their pet's name, contact details, and even QR codes, with a live preview before checkout and automated print-ready file generation on the backend.


**Do I need a print-on-demand supplier to sell personalized pet products on Shopify?** ‍


Not necessarily. POD suppliers like Printify, JetPrint, and ShineOn handle production and shipping for you, which is the easiest entry point. But if you already hold inventory, a standalone personalizer like Customily handles customization without requiring a POD partner.


**What personalized pet products sell best on Shopify?** ‍


The highest-converting categories are engraved ID tags, custom name collars, pet portrait wall art, photo blankets, and memorial keepsakes. Pet-themed jewelry for owners - like photo projection necklaces - also performs strongly as a gift product.


**How do I get repeat customers for my pet store on Shopify?** ‍


Combining a personalization app with a subscription tool like Seal Subscriptions is the most effective approach. Personalization drives the first purchase; subscriptions - monthly treat boxes, seasonal accessory drops - keep customers coming back on autopilot.


**Can I collect pet names and birthdays in my Shopify store?**


Yes, Petlytics is built specifically for this. It lets you collect each customer's pet profile (name, breed, birthday, photo) and use that data to trigger personalized email campaigns, birthday discounts, and breed-specific product recommendations.
