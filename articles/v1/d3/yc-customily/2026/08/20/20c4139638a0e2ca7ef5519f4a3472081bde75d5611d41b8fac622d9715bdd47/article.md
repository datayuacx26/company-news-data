# How to get the best out of Q4: Tips to end your year strong

IT'S ON 🔥 We're about to reach the most wonderful (and stressful) time of the year. The last push. The last effort. The time that can determine how good our year is going to end. And in 2026, the stakes are higher than ever: new selling channels, smarter shoppers, and more competition than we've ever seen. The long-awaited Q4 starts in a month 🤯


As you know, most customers put all their energy and time into buying products and services during the last part of the year, so businesses should use these buying habits to their advantage.


In this post, we'll cover:


- How to organize your business before the rush hits


- Sales and marketing strategies to maximize the Q4 window


- New selling channels to reach more customers in 2026


Here are some tips that will help you get ready and cross the finish line by the end of the year.


## **Don't underestimate the importance of being organized**


If you want your Q4 season to run smoothly, it's essential to be exceptionally organized 📅


The best thing you can do is look back to understand where you stand today. Analyze how this quarter has impacted your business over the last few years, how it differs from the rest of the year, what worked before 👍 and what didn't 👎


### **1. Set goals**


What do you want to accomplish by the end of the year? 🎯


Put the[numbers over the table](https://www.customily.com/post/7-ecommerce-kpis-you-need-to-know) . Set clear, ambitious, but achievable goals.


The numbers back it up: U.S. online holiday sales reached $257.8 billion in 2025, a 6.8% increase over 2024. And the 2025 holiday season was the first ever to cross $1 trillion in total retail sales. Use these benchmarks as a reference when setting your targets, and don't leave Q4 without a clear number to aim for.


### **2. Prepare a budget**


Make sure you set up a plan and stick to the budget 💰


Operating costs may increase during this peak season due to higher shipping costs and other factors. It's important to stay on budget while making sure you're getting maximum profit.


### **3. Watch your stock**


Stocking up adequately for the fourth quarter is more important than you might think. Inventory is an aspect that's often overlooked, sometimes salespeople don't factor in actual sales projections when deciding what needs restocking.


Considering delivery times, shipping costs, and planning in advance is critical. While it's important to stock up and meet expected high demand, you also need to be careful not to overstock.


### **4. Put your customer first**


Companies that focus too much on numbers and statistics sometimes forget about the customer. That's why it's important to look at how your customers actually interact with your store.


- Offer flexible payment options. The more ways a customer has to pay you, the better.


- Look at what customers said last year and what they're saying about your business now 🗣️ Evaluate the positive and negative reviews equally.


- Analyze how you can improve the customer experience during this busy time, and plan how you'll implement those improvements.


## ‍ **Prepare your sales and marketing strategies**


For marketing and sales teams, this is the most important time of the year 📈 Anticipating and paving the way for them will have a positive impact on your business.


Let's look at some options:


### **1. Plan your marketing calendar**


The fourth quarter is fast-moving, especially in e-commerce. Within a small window of time, sellers have the opportunity to get their products to customers on[Halloween](https://www.customily.com/post/5-awesome-ideas-to-boost-your-halloween-sales) , Black Friday, Cyber Monday,[Christmas](https://www.customily.com/post/top-print-on-demand-products-you-must-sell-this-christmas) , and more.


By making a plan for each of these occasions, you can make the most of every opportunity to increase sales. Use the holiday calendar as a planning tool, and match these dates with the strong offers you tested earlier in the year 😉 In 2026, also map out your channel strategy, which dates are best for TikTok Shop live events, which products go on Amazon for Prime fall deals, and which campaigns live on your own store.


### **2. Encourage customers to visit frequently**


Since consumers are in a buying mood during the last few months of the year, encourage them to come back often! Give them reasons to check out your shop, pull out their wallet, and buy your products.


Use discount codes or coupons that expire within a set number of hours or days. You can also give them a coupon redeemable at a later date that pulls them back for a second purchase.


### **3. New and trendy products**


Q4 is a good time to test new, innovative, or interesting products. Your competitors will do the same, or introduce higher-quality products in the same category to compete.


Pricing and promotional strategy also play a big role during the holidays. Focus your efforts on items with the highest potential to stand out, whether they solve a specific problem or are simply visually appealing.


### **4. Host events that attract customers**


To boost sales, organize events that bring in both loyal and new customers. This gives customers a chance to see your exclusive seasonal items, and gives your team a chance to connect with loyal fans and make a strong first impression on new ones.


A[happy customer](https://www.customily.com/post/best-tips-to-increase-customer-engagement-and-loyalty) is more likely to spend more on your business, and will promote your brand to their friends and family.


### **5. Fries with your burger?**


Bundling complementary or similar products into a single package and marking them "Frequently Bought Together" is a popular resource for selling online 🍔🍟. Bundling drives impulse buys as customers feel they're getting a great deal, which increases average order value and revenue.


To succeed with bundling this Q4, get comfortable packaging complementary products together and displaying them clearly in your store, so customers don't have to scroll through multiple listings to find each item.


### **6. Test your offers in advance**


Go into the season with strong offers that attract new customers and maximize sales from existing ones. Run A/B tests on different offers and discounts to see what works best. Test multiple variations, items in a bundle, size or color options, etc. and use the results to inform your Q4 campaigns.


### **7. Sell where your customers already are: TikTok Shop, Amazon & beyond 📱**


One of the biggest shifts heading into Q4 2026 is the rise of social commerce and new selling platforms. If you're only selling on your own store, you're leaving a massive audience on the table. The winning strategy today is omnichannel, meeting shoppers wherever they discover products.


TikTok Shop has evolved into a full commerce engine in 2026. Over 65% of Gen Z shoppers now start product discovery on social video platforms rather than search engines, and TikTok Shop is where they buy. For Q4, that means running LIVE selling events during peak moments like Black Friday, partnering with creators through the affiliate program (you only pay commission on actual sales), and making sure your best gift products are optimized for TikTok's visual-first format. Products that can be shown and explained in a few seconds, personalized gifts, home goods, apparel, perform especially well.


Amazon remains critical for Q4, especially for personalized and gift products. With Amazon's second Prime event landing in October and holiday traffic surging through December, sellers need to submit FBA inventory early, missing inbound deadlines can cost you the Prime badge during Cyber Week and tank visibility. Invest in short product videos (shoppers are 73% more likely to buy after watching one), and use influencer or user-generated content in your Amazon Brand Store to make listings feel more authentic and gift-ready. Consumers have shown they're willing to pay a real premium for products that feel personal and individual.


The smart play in 2026 is combining all three: your own store for margins and customer ownership, Amazon for reach and Prime shoppers, and TikTok Shop for discovery and impulse buys. Each channel has its own peak moments in Q4, plan for them now rather than scrambling in November.


### **8. Increase sales by adding personalization**


Differentiation matters more than ever, and it's tough to achieve. That's why customization plays such a key role across every business, don't miss this wave 🌊 Every customer has their own needs, and the best way to meet them is customization.


**‍** Customers are getting more demanding, and they're starting to demand[personalized products](https://www.customily.com/post/customization-the-new-strategy-to-increase-your-sales) over mass offerings. Large companies started introducing personalizable products in recent years with great success. Personalization doesn't just increase sales, it builds brand loyalty by creating something unique together with the customer.


Customily has rolled out several new features in 2026 that make this easier than ever:


- Multi Image Upload: lets customers add multiple photos to a single product, opening the door to richer, more personal gifts.


- Multi Preview for Product Bases: shows buyers how their customization looks across different product variants before they commit, reducing hesitation and returns.


- AI Filters: bring a new creative layer to personalization, letting customers apply artistic effects to their designs in seconds.


- Word Search Puzzles: add a completely new product type to your catalog, personalized puzzles where shoppers enter their own names and words, turning a simple product into a meaningful custom gift.
