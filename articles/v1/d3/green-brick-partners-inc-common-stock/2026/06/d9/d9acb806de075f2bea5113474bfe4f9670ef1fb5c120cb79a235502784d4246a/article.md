# Atlanta St. Jude Dream Home® Open for Tours

## Atlanta St. Jude Dream Home® Open for Tours


June 10, 2026


Download (opens in new window)


*One Lucky Ticket Buyer Will Win a Brand-New Home Built by The Providence Group*


CUMMING, Ga.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[The Providence Group](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Ftheprovidencegroup.com%2F&esheet=54551750&newsitemid=20260610434221&lan=en-US&anchor=The+Providence+Group&index=1&md5=506fc4c73f2bfb46cbc6cdc9b7c688e2) , a subsidiary of Green Brick Partners, Inc (NYSE: GRBK) and one of metro Atlanta’s leading lifestyle homebuilders, along with dozens of generous trade partners, suppliers and sponsors, has completed the 2026 Atlanta St. Jude Dream Home® Giveaway house and is inviting the public to tour the home during select open house weekends this June.


Located at 604 Silva Street in The Providence Group's[Palisades](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Ftheprovidencegroup.com%2Fnew-homes%2Fga%2Fcumming%2Fpalisades%2F14636%2F&esheet=54551750&newsitemid=20260610434221&lan=en-US&anchor=Palisades&index=2&md5=dbecd527f0ce435d3f418fab7b100be7) community in Cumming, the 2026 Dream Home showcases the award-winning builder's commitment to exceptional design, craftsmanship and livable luxury while helping support the lifesaving mission of St. Jude Children's Research Hospital®.


The home will be open for tours on Saturdays and Sundays starting June 6 through June 28 from Noon to 5 p.m. Visitors will have the opportunity to explore the professionally designed home and learn more about how the Dream Home Giveaway helps ensure that families never receive a bill from St. Jude for treatment, travel, housing or food, allowing them to focus on their child.


Tickets for the 2026 Atlanta St. Jude Dream Home® Giveaway are available for a $100 donation. One lucky ticket holder will win the home, while additional prize drawings throughout the campaign provide opportunities to win other exciting prizes. Every ticket purchased directly supports St. Jude Children's Research Hospital and its mission of finding cures and saving children battling cancer and other life-threatening diseases.


The 2026 Dream Home is located within[Palisades](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Ftheprovidencegroup.com%2Fnew-homes%2Fga%2Fcumming%2Fpalisades%2F14636%2F&esheet=54551750&newsitemid=20260610434221&lan=en-US&anchor=Palisades&index=3&md5=f1016c8afe9f5d1e50fedd1db3c92ad6) , one of Forsyth County's most sought-after communities. Just minutes from the vibrant Cumming City Center, Sawnee Mountain Preserve, shopping, dining and entertainment destinations, the neighborhood offers residents a connected lifestyle with walkable amenities and convenient access to everything North Georgia has to offer.


The Dream Home itself features The Providence Group's signature attention to detail, blending timeless architecture with modern functionality. Thoughtfully designed living spaces, designer-selected finishes and carefully curated interior details create a home that is both beautiful and welcoming for the future homeowner.


"The St. Jude Dream Home is one of the most meaningful projects we build each year," said Dusty Talbert, Director of Marketing for The Providence Group. "This home represents the incredible generosity of our trade partners, suppliers and sponsors who come together to support a cause that impacts families around the world. Every ticket sold helps St. Jude continue its lifesaving work, and we are honored to be part of that mission."


Now in its fourth year as Atlanta's St. Jude Dream Home® builder, The Providence Group and its trade partners have helped raise more than $5.3 million to support the lifesaving mission of St. Jude Children's Research Hospital since bringing the program to Atlanta in 2023. Since its inception, the St. Jude Dream Home® Giveaway has raised more than $650 million nationwide to support research and treatment efforts.


"This project demonstrates the power of our industry coming together for something bigger than ourselves," said Talbert. "From the first shovel in the ground to the final design selections, hundreds of people have contributed their time, talents and resources to create a beautiful home while helping make a difference in the lives of children and families facing unimaginable challenges."


The 2026 Atlanta St. Jude Dream Home® campaign officially launched in January with a ceremonial groundbreaking that brought together trade partners, subcontractors, vendors and sponsors who donated their services and materials to the project. The campaign will culminate on July 9, when one lucky winner receives the keys to the home.


For more information about the 2026 Atlanta St. Jude Dream Home® Giveaway, please visit[The Providence Group](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Ftheprovidencegroup.com%2Fst-jude-dream-home%2F&esheet=54551750&newsitemid=20260610434221&lan=en-US&anchor=The+Providence+Group&index=4&md5=d7bb1c4a01a3a96cde6c76bb4cbf79aa) .

**About Green Brick Partners, Inc.**


Green Brick Partners, Inc (NYSE: GRBK), the third largest homebuilder in Dallas-Fort Worth and one of Fortune Magazine's fastest growing companies, is a diversified homebuilding and land development company that operates through its seven subsidiary homebuilders in Texas, Georgia, and Florida. Green Brick owns five subsidiary homebuilders in Texas (CB JENI Homes, Normandy Homes, Southgate Homes, Trophy Signature Homes, and a 90% interest in Centre Living Homes), as well as a controlling interest in a homebuilder in Atlanta, Georgia (The Providence Group) and an 80% interest in a homebuilder in Port St. Lucie, Florida (GHO Homes). Green Brick also retains interests in related financial services platforms, including Green Brick Title, Green Brick Mortgage, and Green Brick Insurance. Green Brick and its affiliated builders are engaged in all aspects of the homebuilding process, including land acquisition and development, entitlements, design, construction, marketing, and sales for its residential neighborhoods and master-planned communities.

**About St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital®**


St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital is leading the way the world understands, treats and defeats childhood cancer and other life-threatening diseases. Its purpose is clear: Finding cures. Saving children.® It is the only National Cancer Institute-designated Comprehensive Cancer Center devoted solely to children. When St. Jude opened in 1962, childhood cancer was largely considered incurable. Since then, St. Jude has helped push the overall survival rate from 20% to more than 80%, and it won't stop until no child dies from cancer. St. Jude shares the breakthroughs it makes to help doctors and researchers at local hospitals and cancer centers around the world improve the quality of treatment and care for even more children. Because of generous donors, families never receive a bill from St. Jude for treatment, travel, housing or food, so they can focus on helping their child live.
