# ING wins Best Bank awards at Euromoney

## Making banking simpler


In Retail Banking, the focus is on making banking faster, easier and more intuitive for 41 million customers in nine markets.


“Our ambition is to be the best bank in Europe. For our customers, that means making it easier to achieve the things that matter most to them, whether buying a home, planning for the future or reaching important life goals. Being recognised as Europe’s Best Retail Bank and the World’s Best Bank for Mortgages/Home Loans reflects the trust our customers place in us and the dedication of our talented teams around the world. We are proud of this recognition, and it motivates us to keep raising the bar for ourselves every day,” said Pinar Abay, Head of Retail Banking at ING.


## Supporting clients internationally


In Wholesale Banking, ING was named Europe's Best Bank for Large Corporates – a testament to how we support clients in over 100 countries navigate changing markets and transition to a more sustainable economy.


"Being recognised as Europe's Best Bank for Large Corporates reflects the trust our clients place in us and the dedication of our people across Wholesale Banking. Our role goes beyond banking. By bringing together the full breadth of ING's expertise through our international network, we help clients navigate change, unlock opportunities and support the competitiveness and resilience of the European economy," said Ljiljana Čortan, head of Wholesale Banking at ING.


Euromoney’s Awards for Excellence, the first of their kind in the global banking industry, were established in 1992.
