# Meet Nova, your AI agent

Nova has spent the last nine months in beta, built hand in hand with a group of event organizers who put it to work on their real events. It's been tested, shaped, and sharpened with them, and now it's available to every organizer on Let's Do This.


### What you can do with Nova


You don't need to set anything up or learn a new tool. You ask, in plain language, and Nova answers.


The best way to understand Nova is to simply try asking it something! A few examples of what it can do:


- **A good place to start is simply by asking**["What can Nova do?"](https://www.letsdothis.com/dashboard/nova?prompt=What%20can%20Nova%20do%3F)
- **Ask the how-to questions** , like["How do I set up Payment Plans?"](https://www.letsdothis.com/dashboard/nova?prompt=How%20do%20I%20set%20up%20Payment%20Plans%3F) The things you'd normally save for a call with your account manager, Nova can talk you through.
- **Get help with the admin.**["Build me a CSV of lapsed runners."](https://www.letsdothis.com/dashboard/nova?prompt=Build%20me%20a%20CSV%20of%20lapsed%20runners.) You could even build a dashboard, prepare your startlist exports, or set up a marketing segment.
- **Dive into your event performance.** Try["How is this event pacing versus last year?"](https://www.letsdothis.com/dashboard/nova?prompt=How%20is%20this%20event%20pacing%20versus%20last%20year%3F) or["Where did last week's registrations come from?"](https://www.letsdothis.com/dashboard/nova?prompt=Where%20did%20last%20week%27s%20registrations%20come%20from%3F) Nova gives you a visual answer with the numbers behind it, then lets you dig deeper.
- **Plan with the data behind you.**["What should I price my early-bird at?"](https://www.letsdothis.com/dashboard/nova?prompt=What%20should%20I%20price%20my%20early-bird%20at%3F) Nova draws on your past events and anonymized industry benchmarks so you can see what worked before you set your next price.


Want more ideas? Check out the[Prompts](https://www.letsdothis.com/dashboard/nova/prompts) section of your Let's Do This dashboard.


If you'd like a hand getting started, our team is always happy to run a live session with you.


### How it works with your data


Nova can only access the data that's already on your account, plus anonymized industry benchmarks. Your team won't see your conversations, but you can choose to share a chat link with them if you spot something interesting.


### Feedback? We're listening


We're improving Nova every day, based on how organisers use it. If an answer isn't quite right, use the thumbs up or thumbs down feedback buttons in any conversation to tell us.
