# Track your goals with real clarity, powered by Nova

Event management pulls you in a hundred directions at once.


From watching registrations, to tweaking campaigns and managing budgets. And somewhere in the middle of it all, you’re trying to make good decisions, fast.


You shouldn’t have to hunt for clarity to know how you’re doing. So we’ve made it easier to keep track of your goals with Nova.


---


### **Introducing goals in Nova**


You can now set clear registrations goals with Nova, so your focus stays aligned to what you’re actually trying to achieve. In the future, Nova will also help you stay on target when it comes to reaching other goals such as selling out faster than the year before or reaching a certain number of charity donations.


**‍**


### **‍** How it works


Goals can be added in two simple ways:


- Via the new Goals section on your personalised homepage


OR


- Directly in Nova chat, just by saying what you want to achieve
*For example ‘I want to reach 14,500 participants for the Half Marathon by event day.’*


Nova monitors your goals in the background, provides helpful visuals of your progress, and surfaces relevant insights if your trajectory starts to go off track.


### ‍ **What changes day to day**


When you ask Nova how your event is performing, the answers are more focused.


Instead of just showing raw numbers, Nova frames everything against your goals. Are you on track or falling behind? And if so, what could help you course-correct?


Setting goals ensures Nova provides the most tailored insights for your specific focus.


### **Why this matters**


When you ask Nova about your event performance, it doesn't just give you numbers, it focuses on how you're tracking against your specific goals.
