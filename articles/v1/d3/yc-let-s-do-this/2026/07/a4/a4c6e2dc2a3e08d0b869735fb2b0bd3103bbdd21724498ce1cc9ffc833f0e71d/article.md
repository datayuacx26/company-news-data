# Teach Nova how your business works with Instructions

Every organizer has their own way of working. Their board metrics, their acquisition targets, their definition of a loyal participant. Until now, you'd need to give Nova all of that key business context each time in order to get the most informed insights and recommendations. That's about to change.


### Introducing Instructions for Nova


You can now teach Nova about your business. Share your metrics, your strategic priorities, your internal shorthand. Nova holds onto all of it and applies it across every conversation, every analysis, every recommendation.


Think of it like onboarding a new analyst. Except you only have to do it once.


### What you can teach it


**Your metrics** - Every organizer measures success differently. Now you can tell Nova exactly what your numbers mean.


*"When I ask for board metrics, I want total registrations, revenue, avg ticket price and YoY growth. Run this every month for any event over 1,000 participants."*


**Your priorities** — Strategic context shapes every recommendation Nova gives. Tell it what matters most to your business.


*"Our mission is getting more people active. Prioritize new participant acquisition over retention. Registration targets are secondary."*


**Your processes** - If you have recurring analyses or reports, you can define them once and call them up with a single phrase.


*"When I ask for a loyalty discount list, find everyone who's done the same event three years running. $50 discount each. I'll need the list as a CSV."*


### How it works


Access Instructions through Nova's settings, or tell Nova directly in chat to remember something for future conversations. Write instructions as plain text, as specific as you like. The more context you give, the better Nova performs.


You can review, edit, or remove instructions at any time. They are only visible to you and are never shared with other team members.


### Less explaining. More doing.


The best insights come when Nova already understands your world. Instructions gets it there faster, so every conversation starts from a smarter place.
