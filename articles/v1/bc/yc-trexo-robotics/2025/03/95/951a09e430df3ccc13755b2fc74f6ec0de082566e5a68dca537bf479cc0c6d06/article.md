## The First Steps In A Journey of Possibility


Our new Trexo Trial Experience isn’t like our typical Open Houses. This is a personal, moment in time, designed to inspire, support, and empower your family. The most important part of your time with us is seeing your kiddo in a Trexo. Watching their face as they feel their weight shifting, and they legs taking steps. This is when you experience the excitement and wonder of your kiddo walking in robotic legs and when we determine if Trexo is a good fit for your kiddo.


## Your Exclusive Trexo Trial Experience Will Include:


🤖 **Personalized Trexo Trial** Guided by our compassionate, knowledgeable and fun experts, your child will experience the magic of Trexo robotic legs – taking steps that will likely be confusing, exciting and ultimately fun for your kiddo.


🏭 **Behind-the-Scenes Tour** Peek into the soace where innovation happens, where each Trexo device is built, seeing the team that is behind the Trexos that we ship out daily to excited families.


☕ **Comfort & Connection** Relax in our welcoming reception area, enjoy refreshments, and feel the warmth of a community that truly understands your journey.


💬 **Time for Your Questions** Our team is here to listen, support, and answer every question with genuine care and a tonne of knowledge.


## Celebrating Every Milestone


Every step is a triumph. That’s why we’ve created special touches to honor your child’s incredible moment:


- **Trexo Achievement Certificate** : A personalized keepsake celebrating your child’s unique journey, signed by our team. It will include number of steps taken and initiation percentage.
- **Goodie Bag** : Fun Trexo swag to remember this magical day
- **Photo Opp** : We will make sure we get some great photos and videos of your kiddo (with your consent of course)
