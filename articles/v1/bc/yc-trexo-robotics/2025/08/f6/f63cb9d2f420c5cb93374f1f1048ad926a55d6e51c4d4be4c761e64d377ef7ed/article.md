# Trexo Walking Club: Building Community, Creating Memories

# Trexo Walking Club: Building Community, Creating Memories


by[Jasmeet Dhillon](https://www.trexorobotics.com/author/jasmeet/)


| Aug 25, 2025


|[Thoughts](https://www.trexorobotics.com/category/thoughts/) |[0 comments](https://www.trexorobotics.com/trexo-walking-club-building-community-creating-memories/#respond)


**One of the best parts of my role as Marketing Coordinator at Trexo Robotics is getting to see families and kiddos in the Trexo and the joy it brings. Every time families gather at our office, the energy is contagious – kids walking in their Trexo, parents cheering them on, and smiles all around. These moments remind me why we do what we do, and why creating more opportunities for community building, connection and happiness is so important.**


**Trexo Walking Club**


Recently, our Walking Club visited two amazing venues: the Butterfly Conservatory and the Toronto Zoo. While I wasn’t able to attend in person, my team shared just how special these events felt. These moments highlight what makes the Walking Club so powerful: it’s not only about movement, but also about creating inclusive experiences in places where our community can truly feel connected. We want to extend a big thank you to the Toronto Zoo – they went above and beyond to make this day incredible.


For families new to Trexo, the Walking Club is more than just events – it’s a chance to meet others, share stories, and feel the power of community. The laughter, the encouragement, and even the quiet moments when a child takes their steps in the Trexo – side by side with other kiddos, are all special.


**Looking Ahead**


We’re planning some unforgettable experiences at iconic places like Ripley’s Aquarium, and even the CN Tower. These aren’t just outings; they’re opportunities for families to make memories in spaces that are committed to accessibility .We’re hopeful that more venues will expand accessibility, opening the door for our community to explore even more places.


If you’re reading about our GTA Walking Club and would love to see something similar in your own area, we’d be happy to hear from you – reach out and let us know, and we’ll do our best to help make it happen. The Walking Club is growing, and we can’t wait to bring our community together again soon! Every step with Trexo is a milestone worth celebrating.





“The Milton Walking Club at the velodrome was such a powerful reminder of what community looks like in action. Watching kids walk side by side in their Trexos, cheered on by their families, filled the space with so much joy. Capturing those moments for our community online was an absolute privilege. I’m looking forward to the exciting events we have planned for the future”


– Sristhi, Social Media Manager
