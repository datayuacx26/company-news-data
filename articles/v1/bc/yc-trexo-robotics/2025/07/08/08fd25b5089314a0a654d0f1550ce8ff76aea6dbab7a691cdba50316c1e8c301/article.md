# Trexo And Brar’s Come Together to Help More Kiddos Take Their First Steps

# Trexo And Brar’s Come Together to Help More Kiddos Take Their First Steps


by[Srishti](https://www.trexorobotics.com/author/srishti/)


| Jul 4, 2025


|[Thoughts](https://www.trexorobotics.com/category/thoughts/) ,[Trexo News](https://www.trexorobotics.com/category/trexo-news/) |[0 comments](https://www.trexorobotics.com/trexo-and-brars-come-together-to-help-more-kiddos-take-their-first-steps/#respond)


Pediatric mobility data in Canada is sparse, leaving many kiddos underserved, as a result. Together Trexo and Brar’s are taking steps to change that.


For our readers that don’t know; Brar’s is one of Canada’s most loved ethnic food brands, known for bringing authentic, high-quality food to families across the country. With a long history of being part of family traditions, celebrations, and everyday moments, they understand that food is more than just nourishment. It’s about connection, culture, and family.


When we met Brar’s at an event, an organic partnership began and w hen two community-driven companies come together, the impact does not double – it has a multiplier effect. This was the thinking behind our recent partnership with Brar’s. The collaboration aims to amplify our mission of helping as many children as possible to walk and Brar’s mission to give back and make a difference in their community. A huge thank you goes to Brar’s for believing in this vision and committing to donate two Trexo Plus devices to two different pediatric physical therapy clinics in 2025.


## **Opening Doors to Pediatric Mobility With the Support of Brar’s**


This partnership means so much more than a business agreement. It means numerous families will now have access to Trexo technology, thanks to the generosity of[Brar’s](https://www.brars.com/) . Parents will be able to book appointments and let their children experience walking with the Trexo consistently. For many of these children, this represents an opportunity to feel the joy of walking for the very first time.


Brar’s is donating two[Trexo Plus devices](https://www.trexorobotics.com/trexo-plus/) , one to a pediatric physical therapy clinic in mid-town Toronto and one to a clinic in BC (stay tuned for the announcement of exact location) The profound impact this device will have on families in the Toronto area is something special that will continue to grow. When kiddos visit for trials, their faces illuminate with a special kind of happiness that’s impossible to describe. The joy that beams from the parent’s hearts is truly unbelievable – an emotional response unlike any other.


Brar’s commitment to the community extends beyond this donation, they will be working with us on various initiatives to give back.


## **From Signature to Impact**


With the paperwork now signed, the next step is getting our robot builders to work on the device. The first device will soon be delivered to its destination, with the second following in the winter.


Through this collaboration, countless more families will experience that transformative moment of seeing their child take supported steps. How many people get to see their kiddo walking in robot legs?! It’s an amazing thing and a win for pediatric mobility.


We are incredibly excited about this partnership and look forward to sharing updates as this initiative unfolds. We’re huge fans of Brar’s and deeply admire their mission and the incredible work they do in our community. What makes this partnership even more special is how much family means to Brar’s – their commitment to bringing families together for celebration and joy aligns perfectly with our mission. There’s a beautiful connection between the family moments Brar’s creates and the family milestones we help achieve when a child takes their first steps.


Having had the pleasure of welcoming the Brar’s team to our office, we’ve experienced firsthand their enthusiasm for making a difference. Beyond this device donation, we’re exploring new ways to support our communities, side by side. This partnership is just the beginning of what we believe will be a long and meaningful collaboration.


Thank you again to the Brar’s team – we enjoy working with you, and our hearts are filled with joy, as we think of the kiddos that will benefit from these devices.


Have a wonderful summer and stay in touch!


*— Srishti, Trexo Robotics*
