# Colgate-Palmolive Webcasts Fireside Chat at the dbAccess Global Consumer Conference

NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--May 20, 2026-- Colgate-Palmolive (NYSE:CL) Chief Operating Officer, Americas, Shane Grant and Executive Vice President, M&A and Special Projects, John Faucher will participate in a fireside chat at the dbAccess Global Consumer Conference in Paris on Wednesday, June 3, 2026 at 8:45 am ET.


Investors may access a live webcast of this fireside chat on Colgate’s website at[www.colgatepalmolive.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.colgatepalmolive.com&esheet=54531350&newsitemid=20260507118815&lan=en-US&anchor=www.colgatepalmolive.com&index=1&md5=266e3b73d88902752ae404b3a177585d) . For those unable to participate during the live webcast, a recorded version of the webcast will be made available through the Investor Center section of Colgate’s website.
