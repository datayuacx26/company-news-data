# Colgate-Palmolive Announces Webcast of its 2026 First Quarter Earnings Conference Call May 1, 2026 - Now at 8:00 a.m. ET and its 2026 Annual Meeting of Stockholders on May 8, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. ET

NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Apr. 27, 2026-- Due to a scheduling conflict, Colgate-Palmolive Company (NYSE:CL) will now provide a live webcast of its 2026 first quarter earnings conference call on Friday, May 1, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. ET (previously 8:30 a.m. ET). The call will be hosted by Chairman, President and CEO, Noel Wallace, Chief Financial Officer, Stan Sutula, Executive Vice President, Investor Relations, Claire Ross, and Executive Vice President, M&A and Special Projects, John Faucher. Investors may access the earnings press release, prepared materials and the live audio webcast on Colgate’s website at[https://investor.colgatepalmolive.com/events-and-webcasts](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.colgatepalmolive.com%2Fevents-and-webcasts&esheet=54523726&newsitemid=20260427180176&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.colgatepalmolive.com%2Fevents-and-webcasts&index=1&md5=581cb0ce810a3e6234897f25b075b966) . For those unable to participate during the live webcast, a recorded version of the webcast will be made available through the Investor Center section of Colgate’s website.


Separately, the Company also announced it will provide a live video webcast of its 2026 Annual Meeting of Stockholders on Friday, May 8, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. ET. Investors may access the live video webcast at[http://www.virtualshareholdermeeting.com/CL2026](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.virtualshareholdermeeting.com%2FCL2026&esheet=54523726&newsitemid=20260427180176&lan=en-US&anchor=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.virtualshareholdermeeting.com%2FCL2026&index=2&md5=da9891e8ff4f3abe8fba6770dc97bd96) . Access will be available beginning at 9:30 a.m. ET. For those unable to participate during the live webcast, a recorded version of the webcast will be made available on the Company’s website approximately 24 hours after the meeting concludes.
