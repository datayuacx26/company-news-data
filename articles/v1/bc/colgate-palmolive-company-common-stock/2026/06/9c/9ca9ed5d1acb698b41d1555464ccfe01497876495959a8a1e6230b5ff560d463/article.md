# Colgate Declares Regular Quarterly Dividend

NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jun. 11, 2026-- The Board of Directors of Colgate-Palmolive Company (NYSE:CL) today declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.53 per common share, payable on August 14, 2026, to shareholders of record on July 20, 2026. The Company has paid uninterrupted dividends on its common stock since 1895.
