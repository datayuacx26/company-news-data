# Celebrating Women in Leadership: Anne Trobaugh's Impact at American Woodmark

-
-
-


Anne's journey is remarkable, characterized by a commitment to excellence and a passion for elevating customer experiences. With years of experience across various sectors, including notable roles at Cummins Inc. and Flowserve Corporation, Anne brings a wealth of expertise and strategic insight to her position at American Woodmark.


Beyond her professional accomplishments, Anne is pivotal in advancing and encouraging other women within the organization. As a female leader in a predominantly male-dominated industry, she actively champions initiatives to attract and develop female talent, fostering an inclusive and supportive work environment for all.


Under Anne's guidance, American Woodmark has embraced a culture of innovation, drive continuous improvement, and fostered team collaboration. Her dedication to the company's principles - Customer Satisfaction, Integrity, Teamwork, and Excellence - is evident in every aspect of her work.


As we celebrate Anne's accomplishments, we invite you to learn more about her journey and the impact of her leadership in an exclusive video interview. Join us in recognizing the invaluable contributions of women in leadership!
