# Celebrating Excellence: EASE Data-Based Champions for Quality Awards

-
-
-


April 9, 2025


We are proud to announce that American Woodmark has been recognized in the latest Champions for Quality Awards program. This program evaluates performance based on EASE usage metrics and benchmarks and acknowledges outstanding achievements in operational efficiency, quality improvements, and team leadership across various categories.


Our[South Branch Plant](https://americanwoodmark.com/our-story/locations/south-branch-plant) has been honored with the **Plant of the Year** award. This award is given to the plant that demonstrates excellence in maintaining quality standards, focusing on the timely completion of plant floor audits, and efficiently addressing findings. The South Branch Plant’s strong performance in these areas highlights its commitment to operational excellence and dedication to fostering a culture of continuous improvement.


This prestigious recognition reflects the tireless efforts of the South Branch team, who work diligently to ensure that quality is prioritized at every step of the production process. Their ability to consistently meet and exceed expectations for audit completion, problem-solving, and process improvements has been a key factor in securing this award. The South Branch Plant sets a high standard for the entire company, exemplifying how quality initiatives can lead to tangible, measurable success.


We are also proud to have received an **Honorable Mention** for the Influencer category, specifically recognizing **Donnie Thysell** from our[Riverside, California plant](https://americanwoodmark.com/our-story/locations/riverside-plant) . Donnie has been instrumental in generating excitement and driving the adoption of quality initiatives across his team. His efforts to encourage peer buy-in for quality improvement processes have made a significant impact, helping to strengthen the company’s overall commitment to excellence.


These awards underscore the dedication and hard work of our teams in driving quality and operational excellence at American Woodmark. Congratulations to the South Branch Plant for earning the Plant of the Year award and to Donnie Thysell for his Honorable Mention in the Influencer category. We look forward to continuing our efforts to improve and innovate in the months and years ahead, setting new standards for quality in the industry.
