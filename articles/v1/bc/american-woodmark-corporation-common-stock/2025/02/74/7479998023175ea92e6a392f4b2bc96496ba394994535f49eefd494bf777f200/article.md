# Celebrating 20 Years of Excellence at Allegany Plant

-
-
-


February 3, 2025


We recently marked a significant milestone at our Allegany Plant in Cumberland, MD, as the facility celebrated its 20th anniversary! This momentous occasion underscores the hard work, dedication, and teamwork that have made Allegany a vital part of our operations for two decades.


*“Celebrating 20 years of success at Allegany is a proud moment for all of us—we’ve built something truly special here. The sense of teamwork at Allegany has always been what sets us apart, and this anniversary is a perfect reminder of how much we've accomplished together.”*


> -Tim, Director of Manufacturing, Allegany


As one of our Made-To-Order assembly plants, Allegany plays a crucial role in our operations. Within a single day, all the cabinetry components—frames, doors, panels, and more—are picked, assembled, packaged, and shipped to our customers. This streamlined process ensures that each order is completed with precision and care, allowing us to meet the specific needs of our clients. Serving the Northeast, Allegany not only supports our home center business but also plays a vital role in fulfilling orders for our Builder Direct business, ensuring that both retail and professional customers receive the high-quality, customized cabinets they need exactly when they need them.


This achievement wouldn’t have been possible without the efforts of the dedicated individuals who contribute to the plant’s daily operations. The Allegany team has continually upheld high standards of quality and efficiency, consistently exceeding expectations while maintaining an environment of collaboration and innovation.


A big thank you to everyone who joined us in celebrating this milestone and to the team members who work tirelessly every day to ensure that Allegany continues to thrive. Here’s to another 20 years of success, growth, and excellence at the Allegany Plant!
