# Celebrating 45 Years of Excellence at American Woodmark

-
-
-


May 15, 2025


This month marks a monumental milestone for American Woodmark as we celebrate **45 years** of innovation, quality, and growth. From our humble beginnings to becoming a leader in the cabinetry industry, we’ve come a long way—thanks to the dedication of our employees, the trust of our customers, and the guidance of visionary leadership. As we reflect on this incredible journey, we are filled with pride for everything we’ve accomplished and excitement for the future ahead.


#### A Proud Moment at NASDAQ


To commemorate our 45th anniversary, American Woodmark’s leadership team had the honor of ringing the **closing bell at NASDAQ** in New York. This exciting event symbolized a major achievement for our company, showcasing the hard work and dedication that has brought us to this point. It was a moment of reflection on how far we’ve come and a celebration of all those who have contributed to American Woodmark’s success.


This event was not only a proud moment for our team but also a reminder of the incredible journey ahead. As we continue to innovate and expand, the NASDAQ bell ringing was a tangible representation of how far we’ve come and the bright future that lies ahead.


#### Thank You to the American Woodmark Family


As we reflect on 45 years of growth, we want to take a moment to thank all of the **employees, customers, and partners** who have been part of our journey. From the first cabinet we built to the products we’re making today, it’s the people who make American Woodmark what it is. Your commitment, hard work, and loyalty have helped us reach this incredible milestone.


To our employees, especially those who have been with us for many years, thank you for your dedication. To our customers, thank you for trusting us to deliver the best in cabinetry solutions. And to our partners, thank you for your continued collaboration and support.


#### Looking Forward: The Next 45 Years


While we celebrate our past achievements, we’re already looking forward to the next 45 years. American Woodmark will continue to push the boundaries of design, quality, and service. We remain committed to sustainability, innovation, and community involvement as we work towards an even brighter future.


Here’s to the next chapter of American Woodmark— **45 years of excellence...and counting!**


*Celebrating at our Corporate Headquarters in Winchester, VA*
