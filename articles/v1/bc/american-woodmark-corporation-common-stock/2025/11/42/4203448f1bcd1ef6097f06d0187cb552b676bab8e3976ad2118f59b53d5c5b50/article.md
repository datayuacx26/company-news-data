# Embedding Quality at Every Step – How Our Partnership with EASE Is Transforming American Woodmark

-
-
-


With 18 facilities, our teams once relied on disconnected, often manual systems to manage audits and monitor quality. But as we expanded our digital capabilities and doubled down on customer experience, it became clear that we needed a more unified and forward-looking approach.


> “We had all this audit data, but we couldn’t do anything about it. We couldn’t prove when we acted on it and whether that action was effective, because our process audit systems didn’t have that capacity.”


> *— Shawn Daly, Corporate Quality Systems Manager*


# That’s where EASE came in.


After evaluating multiple platforms, we partnered with EASE to implement a single, sustainable audit system across all facilities. The results have been significant. In the first quarter of 2025 alone, we conducted over 184,000 audits and opened more than 13,000 findings, each one tracked, addressed, and visible at every level of the business.


EASE also enabled us to standardize audit content across our Made to Stock and Made to Order business units, improving governance and alignment across teams. Real-time alerts, mobile accessibility, and integration with Microsoft Power BI have empowered supervisors, analysts, and executives alike to make faster, more informed decisions.


> “Delivering a great customer experience meant shifting from product inspection at the end to focusing on the way we build every step of the process. True quality comes from doing things right the first time, not just catching issues at the finish line. EASE gave us the structure to make that shift possible.”


> *— Anne Trobaugh, Vice President of Quality and Customer Experience*


Even more impactful is the cultural shift we’re seeing across the company. Quality is no longer viewed as a standalone department, it’s a shared discipline that touches every team member and every stage of the process.


As we look to the future, we’re exploring even more ways to extend this momentum, from deeper integration with training systems to AI-powered audit insights. The path forward is clear: when quality leads, everything else follows.
