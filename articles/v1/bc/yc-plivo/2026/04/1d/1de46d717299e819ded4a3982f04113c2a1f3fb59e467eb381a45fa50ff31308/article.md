## **FAQs**


1.


**How quickly can I get an AI voice agent live?**


Most SMBs can launch a basic call flow for essentials like after-hours call handling or lead qualification within days. Full production setups usually take a few weeks, depending on integrations.


1.


**Do I need technical or AI expertise to use this?**


No, most platforms offer no-code or low-code setup for common use cases. Technical help is only needed if you want custom workflows or deeper integrations.


1.


**Can the AI handle real phone calls, not just chat?**


Yes but not all tools do this equally well. Some rely on third-party calling services while others run on native telephony which is more reliable at scale.


1.


**What happens if the AI can’t answer a customer?**


The best setups transfer the call to a human when needed with full context like caller details, conversation summary and intent.


1.


**Is this affordable for a small business?**


Pricing typically scales with usage. The safest options use transparent, pay-as-you-go pricing so costs grow predictably as call volume increases.
