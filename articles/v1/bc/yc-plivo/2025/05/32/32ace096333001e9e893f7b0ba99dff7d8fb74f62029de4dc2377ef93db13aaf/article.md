# Exploring the Advancements and Cost Efficiency of OpenAI's GPT-4.1

Artificial Intelligence continues to revolutionize how we interact with technology, pushing boundaries and setting new standards. OpenAI’s latest release, **GPT-4.1** , epitomizes this progress—bringing significant advancements over its predecessor, GPT-4o. From improved performance in complex tasks to cost-effective deployment models, GPT-4.1 sets new benchmarks in the AI landscape.

## Significant Performance Improvements


GPT-4.1 demonstrates marked improvements across a variety of benchmarks:


- **54.6% on the SWE Benchmark** , a **21.4-point increase** over GPT-4o.
- **38.3% success rate on multi-step tasks** like table generation—compared to GPT-4o’s 10.5%.


These performance gains reflect GPT-4.1’s advanced reasoning and its growing reliability in solving complex challenges.


## Versatility in Code Generation


OpenAI's GPT-4.1 is also a strong performer in programming-related tasks:


- **52.4% in full code generation**
- **53% in code editing** on the Aider Polyglot benchmark


While it still trails top competitors like Gemini 2.5 Pro, GPT-4.1 remains a **powerful AI assistant for coding and debugging** .

## Cost-Effective Models: Mini and Nano


To broaden accessibility, OpenAI introduced **Mini and Nano** versions of GPT-4.1. These models:


- Offer **similar intelligence to GPT-4o**
- Provide **83% cost savings**
- Deliver **nearly 2× the processing speed**


They also support **up to 1 million tokens of input** and **32,000 tokens of output** , enabling high-volume tasks without compromising performance.
