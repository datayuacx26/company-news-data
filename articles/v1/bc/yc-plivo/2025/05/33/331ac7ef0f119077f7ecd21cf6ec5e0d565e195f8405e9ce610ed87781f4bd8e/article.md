## Accessing Google Veo 2: The Basics


Google Veo 2 provides a unique way to create videos using AI-generated images and prompts. The best part? You don't need a Freepik subscription to access it. To get started, simply open the YouTube app on your device. Yes, you read that correctly—Google Veo 2 is accessible directly through YouTube. Once you're in the app, tap on the create button to begin your video-making journey.


‍


### The New User Interface


When you tap on the create button, a new user interface designed specifically for Google Veo 2 greets you. This interface is intuitive and user-friendly, making it easy for anyone to start creating videos. You’ll see an option to input a prompt. This is where the magic begins. By entering a simple text prompt, you can instruct the AI to generate visual content based on your description.


## From Prompt to Video: The Creative Process


Once you enter your prompt, Google Veo 2 gets to work. The app generates four distinct images from your input, each offering a unique interpretation of your description. This variety allows you to choose the image that best matches your vision for the video. After selecting your preferred image, the app seamlessly transitions into video creation mode.


### Crafting Realistic Videos


The transition from image to video is where Google Veo 2 truly shines. Using advanced AI algorithms, the app animates the selected image, bringing it to life with stunning realism. The resulting video is not just a simple animation but a lifelike representation that captures the essence of your original prompt. This entire process is free, making it accessible to anyone with a creative spark and a smartphone.
