This tight integration streamlines workflows, boosts creativity, and makes real-time visual feedback a reality.

Compared to other AI tools like **Grok, Gemini Flash 2.0** , and **Reve** , the 4o model delivers **superior visual performance** . It can create:

Its ability to handle a wide array of creative styles positions it as a top-tier tool for both casual and professional designers.


## Contextual Image Generation: Enhancing Quality


The 4o model goes a step further with **contextual image input** —allowing users to feed up to **four reference images** for more coherent and tailored outputs. This is especially powerful for:


- Product packaging
- Ad creatives
- Brand consistency


It ensures that the generated visuals are aligned with the intended look and feel.
