# Google's Veo 2: Free Access to AI Video Generation

Imagine a world where creating a video is as simple as typing a sentence or uploading an image. Thanks to Google's latest innovation, Veo 2, this is now a reality. Google has made the powerful AI video generation model, once exclusive to a paid partnership with Freepik, freely accessible to everyone through Google AI Studio.

## Introducing Veo 2: A Leap in AI Video Generation


Veo 2 represents a significant advancement in AI technology, offering users the ability to generate short, AI-driven videos using a simple text or image prompt. Google designed this model to democratize video creation, making it accessible to anyone with an internet connection.


Previously, a paid integration with Freepik limited Veo 2's reach. Now, Google has opened the doors to creativity by making it freely available, allowing users to explore the potential of AI in video generation without the need for expensive tools or software.

## How Veo 2 Works


The process is straightforward: users input a text or image prompt, and Veo 2 generates a video clip that lasts between 5 to 8 seconds. The model supports two aspect ratios, 16:9 for traditional widescreen and 9:16 for mobile-friendly vertical videos. While the resolution is capped below 1080p with a maximum frame rate of 24fps, the quality suffices for social media and quick content needs.

Veo 2 allows users to generate up to two videos simultaneously, with a limit of nine generations every 24 hours. This setup ensures that while the service is free, it remains accessible and fair for all users. For those needing higher limits or commercial use, Veo 2 is available via the Vertex AI API at a cost of $0.50 per second, providing a scalable option for businesses and content creators.
