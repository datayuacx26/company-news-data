# SIP Trunking Wins TMC 2023 Communications Solutions Products of the Year Award

Global media company TMC just named[SIP Trunking](https://plivo-webflow.webflow.io/sip-trunking) , Plivo’s cloud-based SIP trunking solution, a 2023[Communications Solutions Product of the Year](https://www.tmcnet.com/topics/articles/2023/07/07/456401-tmc-announces-recipients-the-2023-communications-solutions-products.htm) . It’s a threepeat for us - in 2021 and 2022 we won for our[SMS API](https://plivo-webflow.webflow.io/sms) .


SIP Trunking has evolved in the last year, capped by[announcements](http://plivo-webflow.webflow.io/blog/introducing-new-zentrunk-apis) of our new Trunk Properties API and Call Object API. A recent[blog post](http://plivo-webflow.webflow.io/blog/console-tour-zentrunk) shows you how easy it is to manage SIP Trunking from the Plivo console.


In its blog post announcing the award, TMC says, “Winners represent prominent players in the market who consistently demonstrate the advancement of technologies. Each recipient is a verifiable leader in the marketplace.”


“This list encompasses the true leaders within their market segments,” says Rich Tehrani, CEO, TMC. “Honorees truly represent the best-of-the-best communications products and solutions available today.” He called SIP Trunking “truly an innovative product and is amongst the best communications products and services available on the market today.”
