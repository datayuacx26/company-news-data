**Key takeaways:**


- KinderCare Learning Companies’ Kids Scholarship program is awarding $100,000 in scholarships to 20 former students of KinderCare Learning Centers, Champions and The Crème School for the 2026–27 school year.

LAKE OSWEGO, Ore.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


KinderCare Learning Companies, Inc. (NYSE: KLC) (“KinderCare”), a leading provider of high-quality early childhood education and care, today announced the recipients of its 2026–27 KinderCare Learning Companies’ Kids Scholarship. KinderCare is awarding 20 former students with $5,000 scholarships to support their education at an accredited two- or four-year college, university or vocational program.


The KinderCare Kids Scholarship honors alumni of KinderCare’s programs, including KinderCare Learning Centers, Champions and The Crème School, who have demonstrated academic achievement, leadership and a commitment to serving their communities. Since its launch in 2019, KinderCare has awarded more than $700,000 in scholarships, helping former students continue building the confidence and skills they need to thrive.


This year's scholarship recipients are pursuing higher education across 14 states. From Oregon and California to North Carolina, Texas and Florida, these students are pursuing studies in education, social work, engineering, law, dentistry and other in-demand fields.


"Learning doesn't stop when children leave our classrooms," said Tom Wyatt, chief executive officer of KinderCare Learning Companies. "Our KinderCare Kids Scholarship reflects our commitment to supporting children and families beyond their early years. These remarkable alumni represent the lasting impact of high-quality early childhood education, and we're proud to invest in their futures.”
