# Dorm Desk Glow-Up: How to Style Your MacBook Neo Setup for $60

As you gear up to head back to campus, your new $699 MacBook Neo is likely already packed and ready for lectures and study groups. But while it delivers incredible portable power between classes, relying strictly on a small trackpad and display during late-night study sessions can quickly lead to neck strain and wrist fatigue.


The good news? Changing up your study space doesn’t mean blowing your budget or settling for dull, generic colors. You can upgrade your entire desk setup with a complete $60 peripheral combo or pick out individual color-matched essentials starting from $30 to $60. Give your desk the glow-up it deserves so it works as hard, and looks as good, as you do.


#### **Color-matched hardware transforms your dorm desk**


While some generic office gear has been stuck in a sea of grey, you can now match your laptop accessories directly to your personal style. Pairing your MacBook Neo with clean, minimalist peripherals, in Tonal Rose, Tonal White, Tonal Graphite or Tonal Blue, turns a basic dorm desk into a setup that actually inspires you to get work done.


#### **Instant Bluetooth pairing keeps your workflow moving**


Tangled cords and lost dongles waste your time when you’re rushing between the library, dorms and study groups. Multi-device Bluetooth lets you pair instantly without plugging in extra hardware attachments. Just flip a switch on your wireless keyboard and start typing immediately, keeping your momentum going wherever you decide to work.


#### **Compact gear and laptop risers improve your posture**


Hunching over a low laptop screen can force your neck into an awkward position, especially if you are working in cramped places. Adding an external keyboard and mouse lets your shoulders relax and keeps your wrists in a neutral position. Placing your laptop on a simple riser or stand can also help, bringing the display right up to eye level.
