# Building the Future Together: Logitech Celebrates the Inaugural Southeast Asia Partner Connect and 2026 Partner Award Winners

From June 9–11, we hosted the first-ever Southeast Asia Partner Connect in Hanoi, Vietnam, bringing together partners from across the region for three days of strategy, collaboration, and celebration.


As we continue to invest in our partner ecosystem throughout Southeast Asia, this inaugural event created a new forum for partners to connect directly with our leadership, share ideas, strengthen relationships, and align on the opportunities that lie ahead in Fiscal Year 2027.


The event began with an evening welcome reception, where partners reconnected with colleagues and built new relationships in a relaxed setting before two days of learning and networking.


The second day featured business updates and strategic sessions led by our CEO Hanneke Faber, Global Channel Chief Crystal Ferreira, regional executives, and global business leads. Together, they shared our vision for the future of work, highlighted business priorities for FY27, and discussed how deeper collaboration across our partner ecosystem will help customers create better workplace experiences.


That evening, partners gathered for the Southeast Asia Partner Awards Dinner, recognizing organizations whose innovation, commitment, and outstanding customer focus helped drive our success throughout Fiscal Year 2026.


The event concluded with a memorable cruise through the breathtaking landscape of Ha Long Bay, providing partners and our leaders another opportunity to strengthen relationships, exchange ideas, and celebrate the collaborative spirit that defines our channel community.


### Celebrating the Power of Partnership


“Our partners are at the center of everything we do,” said Crystal Ferreira, Global Channel Chief. “Hosting our first Southeast Asia Partner Connect is an important milestone as we continue investing in this growing region. The organizations we recognized represent the very best of our partner community through their innovation, customer commitment, and collaboration. We’re excited to continue building on this momentum together in FY27.”
