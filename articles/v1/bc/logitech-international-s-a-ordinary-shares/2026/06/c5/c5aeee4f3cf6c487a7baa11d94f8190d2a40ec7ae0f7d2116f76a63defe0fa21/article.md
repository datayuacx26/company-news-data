# Is Dictation Faster Than Typing? How To Use Voice To Add To Your Typing Productivity

For decades, the keyboard was the physical extension of human thought. Today, a voice-first trend is bringing a new kind of productivity to our lives.


The rhythmic clicking of keys has long been the drumbeat of the modern office. But as we move through 2026, another sound has joined the chorus. A softer, more conversational murmur. It’s the song of vibe coding, brainstorming, WhatsApp messaging and Slack chats.


This change is not just a niche trend. After long years of promise, finally, voice is going mainstream. At least, according to


[The Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/tech/typing-is-being-replaced-by-whisperingand-its-way-more-annoying-a804fee7) , there is a rising trend of office workers chatting to AI to dictate their messages which is “just a little awkward”.


Awkward or not, it’s clear the knowledge workers are looking for other human ways to interact with the digital world, at work and in daily life.


**Makes more sense: adding speech to the sense of touch**


The keyboard has been our primary connection to the digital world for over a century. But even the best typists are limited by their fingers. Most of us type between 40 and 60 words per minute. Our thoughts move much faster than that.


This is where speech comes in. We speak at a pace of 130 to 150 words per minute. Research from


[Stanford University](https://readmultiplex.com/2017/03/27/stanford-university-voice-first-is-3x-faster-than-typing/) shows that dictation is now three times faster than typing. It is also more accurate than ever. Modern AI tools now reach


[a 99% accuracy rate](https://www.transcribetube.com/blog/ai-transcription-accuracy) .


Adding speech to your workflow does not mean throwing away your keyboard. It means using the right tool for the right task. Use your keys for data, editing long-form and precision. Use your voice away from your desk, for short-form messages, informal chats, drafting and brainstorming.


This hybrid approach balances cognitive and physical effort. While skilled touch-typing is second nature, typing complex or long thoughts still introduces limits – it is slow. When you speak, that friction disappears. Dictation allows you to communicate your thoughts at the speed of conversation. This makes it perfect for brainstorming or rapid AI prompting. It is why doctors and lawyers have used dictation for years.


Now, technology has made voice accessible for everyone. Instead of tediously drafting a 300-word AI prompt, you can spill it out in seconds, allowing AI to react to your intention as only AI can. In the same way, voice is a great way to brainstorm, with AI as your sparring partner.


Logitech tools make for a smooth transition back and forth, from voice to keyboard to voice. The MX Keys S, for example, features a dedicated talk-to-text key. You do not have to change your habits to start speaking. You just tap a button and start talking.


The popular Wispr Flow app does the same thing – push a button on any keyboard and you’re up and running. Or rather talking. Bottom line, at your desk, keyboards bring the power of your voice to your fingertips.

**A future we can touch and hear**


For over a century, we have expressed our thoughts through the reliable key. Today, we are moving away from a world where we only touch our technology. The keyboard did not replace the mouse. Nor will voice replace the keyboard. Each is just a powerful new partner for the other, working in symphony together.


At the heart of all these input devices is a powerful truth – they help us extend our potential through our human senses. Touch, voice, hearing, sight. Each has its role and is incremental to the next.
