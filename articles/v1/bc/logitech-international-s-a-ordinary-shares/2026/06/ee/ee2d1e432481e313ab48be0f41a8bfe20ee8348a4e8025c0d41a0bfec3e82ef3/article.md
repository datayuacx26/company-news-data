# From Code to the Swiss Alps: Meet the Logitech DevStudio Challenge Finalists

The journey that began with a call to “code your way to Switzerland” has reached its most exciting milestone yet. After a period of intense development and tough deliberation, we are proud to unveil the six finalists of the first-ever


[Logitech DevStudio Challenge](https://www.logitech.com/blog/2026/02/05/code-your-way-to-switzerland-join-the-first-ever-logitech-devstudio-challenge/) . These teams have stood out from a global field of innovators, pushing the boundaries of what is possible with our latest hardware: the MX Creative Console and MX Ink.


**Why We Build: The Vision Behind DevStudio**


At Logitech, we believe that the best hardware is only as powerful as the software and ecosystems that support it. The DevStudio Challenge isn’t just about finding clever shortcuts; it’s about open innovation. By opening our ecosystem to the global developer community, we are inviting a diverse range of perspectives to help define the next generation of productivity. For Logitech, this is an investment in the future of work and creation and collaborating with the community allows us to discover use cases we haven’t even imagined yet, ensuring our tools like the Actions SDK evolve alongside the real-world needs of users.


**Meet the Finalists**


These six teams, hailing from Greece, Turkey, the USA, India, Germany, and Australia, represent the pinnacle of over 250 global submissions.


**Category 1: MX Creative Console + Actions SDK**


- [Access Ring](https://devstudiologitech2026.devpost.com/submissions/972947-access-ring) **(India):** A precision workflow tool designed to bridge the gap between tactile hardware and digital efficiency.


- [VoxRing – Voice-to-Everything Plugin](https://devstudiologitech2026.devpost.com/submissions/977789-voxring-voice-to-everything-plugin) **(Germany)** : An innovative plugin that uses voice commands to trigger complex system and application actions seamlessly.


- [AgentDeck – Command Your AI Fleet](https://devstudiologitech2026.devpost.com/submissions/1000614-agentdeck-command-your-ai-fleet) : A specialized interface for managing and commanding multiple AI agents directly from your console.


**Category 2: MX Ink (MR Stylus for Meta Quest)**


- [ReframeXR](https://devstudiologitech2026.devpost.com/submissions/950837-reframexr) **(USA):** Pushing the boundaries of Mixed Reality with an immersive design and spatial framing experience.


- [DentiXR](https://devstudiologitech2026.devpost.com/submissions/949833-dentixr-dental-training-simulator) **Dental Training Simulator (North Macedonia):** A high-precision medical simulation that proves the power of the MR stylus in specialized professional training.


- [QalamMR](https://devstudiologitech2026.devpost.com/submissions/977703-qalammr) **(Turkey):** An exploration of spatial creativity, redefining how we interact with 3D and 2D worlds in immersive environments.


**What is at Stake**


For these six teams, the trip to Switzerland is a door to the future. Beyond a paid trip to the Logitech headquarters in Lausanne, each category winner will receive as a team USD 10,000. Perhaps most importantly, they gain the opportunity to bring their applications into the


[Logi Marketplace](https://marketplace.logi.com/plugins/en/4?sort=mostRecent&platform=10) . This offers them a direct path to reach over 40 million users worldwide, turning a competition project into a potential global standard for productivity.


**A New Era of Creativity**


As we head toward the finals in Lausanne on June 10th – 11th, we aren’t just looking for a winner; we are looking at the future of how people will create and work. Stay tuned to see who takes home the top prize in Switzerland!
