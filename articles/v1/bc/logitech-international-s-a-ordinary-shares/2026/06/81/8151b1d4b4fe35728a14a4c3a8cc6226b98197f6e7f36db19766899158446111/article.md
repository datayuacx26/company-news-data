# Celebrating Excellence: Logitech G’s PRO X2 SUPERSTRIKE Nominated for Best Product Innovation in Esports

Esports thrives on innovation, and Logitech G is honored to be shortlisted for Best Product Innovation in Esports at the inaugural[Esports Leaders Honors’ List](https://leaders.esportsradar.gg/#best-product-innovation) . This prestigious recognition shines a spotlight on the[PRO X2 SUPERSTRIKE](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/pro-x2-superstrike-mouse) and Logitech G’s relentless dedication to advancing science-driven esports performance.


The Esports Leaders Honors’ List is a celebration of the people, organizations, and technologies reshaping the industry. Encompassing six key categories, it highlights those who exemplify ingenuity and inspire others to elevate what’s possible. For Logitech G, being on this shortlist is a testament to our pursuit of excellence in a rapidly evolving industry.


Innovation is the foundation of everything we do, and the PRO X2 SUPERSTRIKE epitomizes this ethos. In the high-stakes arena of esports, milliseconds can mean the difference between victory and defeat. PRO X2 SUPERSTRIKE, “The Fastest, Fully Customizable Click” delivers unparalleled speed, precision, and adaptability with its groundbreaking[Haptic Inductive Trigger System (HITS)](https://www.logitechg.com/discover/technology/hits) , reducing click latency by up to 30ms.


Our commitment goes beyond products. Backed by rigorous scientific research and collaboration with hundreds, if not thousands, of pro athletes, the PRO X2 SUPERSTRIKE is a prime example of our continuous innovation to meet the demands of competitive esports, redefining how the game is played and won.


As we look forward to the GEIW ceremony on June 19th in Cologne, we are reminded of how far we’ve come and how much further we can go. Regardless of the outcome, the PRO X2 SUPERSTRIKE is a bold statement of what’s possible when passion meets innovation.


Here’s to shaping the future of esports, one millisecond at a time. Keep Playing!
