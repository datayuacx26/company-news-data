# Working From Home at Logitech: Everyday Routines and Must-Have Gear

When you think about working from home, what comes to mind? Is it the technology bridging the physical gap or the cultural shift allowing us to design work around our daily lives?


Today marks National Work From Home Day in the United States and because we make the products that make hybrid work possible, we believe in it too. We help teams collaborate, learn and grow from anywhere, so it should be no surprise that many of our jobs are able to be remote from most locations. Our hybrid work model allows many employees to work from home and the office throughout the week.


While we focus on designing tools intended to keep you productive, comfortable and connected, we’re turning the camera inward to see exactly how our team brings hybrid work to life, what it unlocks for them and what favorite WFH gear keeps them going at home.

**Designing the Future of Work Together**


National Work From Home Day serves as an excellent reminder that the way the world operates has fundamentally shifted. Modern hybrid work isn’t about replicating the corporate office at home; instead, it’s about creating an environment that adapts to you—your family, your habits, your comfort and your life.


Our engineering and design teams build tools that help extend human potential in work and play, and observing our own workforce use these instruments to thrive provides vital insights for our future product pipelines.
