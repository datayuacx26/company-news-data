# How We Are Using Logitech MX Ink to Transform Dental Education with DentiXR

For us at Cythero, the trip to Lausanne was much more than a competition. Coming from Bitola, North Macedonia, with a small but very dedicated team, it was a special feeling to stand among the top projects globally and present DentiXR.


We came with excitement, but also with a lot of respect for the opportunity.


We took this opportunity very seriously from the very beginning, because we understood that the


[Logitech DevStudio Challenge](https://www.logitech.com/blog/2026/02/05/code-your-way-to-switzerland-join-the-first-ever-logitech-devstudio-challenge/) was not only a challenge, but a chance to learn, improve, and present DentiXR in front of people who deeply understand innovation and product development.


From the first moment, the welcome we received from the Logitech team was exceptional. What impressed us most was not only the professionalism, but the warmth, openness, and genuine interest in every project. The lab visits, debriefing sessions, discussions, and the chance to see how much science, engineering, design, and care goes into Logitech products gave us a completely different perspective. It felt like being welcomed by people who truly wanted to help small teams grow and bring their ideas closer to the world.


Presenting DentiXR during the challenge was a very emotional moment for us.


This project started from a clear problem we saw in dental education: students need more access to realistic, repeatable training. The Logitech MX Ink gave us a new way to shape that idea into something more practical, accessible, and natural for students. So, for us, DevStudio was not just a pitching event, it was a moment where our vision felt understood.


Winning the challenge was honestly unforgettable. We were proud, grateful, and a little overwhelmed. Not because of the award alone, but because of the people, the conversations, the support, and the feeling that a small company from our region could be seen and respected on such a stage. It gave us motivation, confidence, and also a strong sense of responsibility to continue developing DentiXR in the right way.


Thank you again to you and the whole Logitech team for the trust, warmth, and support.


This is an experience we will never forget.
