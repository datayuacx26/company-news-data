# What Your Desk Says About Your Return-to-Work Style

The transition back to work after summer holidays hits hard. You try to recapture pre-break momentum, but your success is dependent on your level of preparation. Are you managing a busy home while finishing reports or logging in from cafes? Answer these three questions to find your desk archetype and tips on improving your workflow for the final stretch of the year.


1. **What best describes your workspace right now?**
a. A desk covered with kids’ drawings and sticky note reminders


b. An ergonomic chair, water bottle and plants


c. Multiple monitors and a mechanical keyboard


d. A backpack, cramped table and cafe menu


2. **What is your primary focus as you start the workday?**
a. Surviving the sheer volume of unread emails and updates


b. Keeping your shoulders relaxed and avoiding immediate physical burnout
c.


Using AI to generate your daily to-do list
d.


Finding an electrical outlet and flat surface to actually start working


3. **Which item is non-negotiable for your workday survival?**
a. A notebook to keep track of your daily to-dos


b. A supportive palm rest for typing comfort


c. A high-precision mouse with customizable buttons


d. A stand for your laptop or tablet


#### **Mostly As: The Chaos Coordinator That Masters Multitasking**


You balance family schedules and prep dinner while catching up on email. The environment is hectic and sometimes not even a traditional desk. But you are not alone! A recent Logitech survey on work-life realities* found that 41% of professionals have “hot-desked” from a kids’ bedroom. Still, you make it work even if your desk is a dresser. According to the


[Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/lifestyle/careers/why-work-from-home-is-here-to-stay-53d11eaa) , feeling like you have control over your workspace keeps you more engaged and productive. So look for


[tools](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/shop/p/signature-comfort-plus-m850l) that help you manage the chaos and keep you comfortable amidst the year-end whirlwind without cluttering what little surface space you have left.


#### **Mostly Bs: The Comfy Connoisseur Prioritizing Posture and Wellbeing**


It’s likely your setup has an ergonomic chair, plenty of plants and maybe a blanket for chilly mornings. When Logitech surveyed more than 5,000 people across 11 countries,** 8 out of 10 people wished they knew how to optimize their workspace for comfort and ergonomics. You are already ahead of the curve, but beware of staying glued to your comfy corner as you catch up on post-holiday emails. Combine


[ergonomic tools](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/discover/c/ergo-series) with wellbeing habits like taking regular breaks, stretching and staying hydrated.


#### **Mostly Cs: The Speed Strategist Streamlining Daily Workflows**


You build efficiency into your workday. You’ve already taken advice from


[Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91337250/best-windows-11-keyboard-shortcuts) and memorized keyboard shortcuts to save time every day. You may have even mapped keys with custom shortcuts to automate tasks. You have your trusted


[hardware](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/shop/p/mx-master-4) , but software can help you further save time. For example, Actions Ring through Logi Options+ can help you save up to 33% of time*** by putting favorite app shortcuts right under your cursor as you edit photos or update spreadsheets.


#### **Mostly Ds: The Digital Nomad Packing Light While On The Go**


You rarely spend time at your actual desk, if you even have one. Your office is a commuter train, an airport lounge or a local coffee shop, so packing light is a requirement. Logitech research found that 72% of people working from public spaces own a mouse, but only 26% actually pack it because mice can be bulky and add extra weight to their bag. You need ultra-portable


[gear](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/shop/p/mobi-fold-mouse) that slips into your pocket or bag to keep you efficient while on the move.


Aligning your workspace with your daily workflow sets you up for success when returning from holidays. What does your desk say about how you will handle the return to work?
