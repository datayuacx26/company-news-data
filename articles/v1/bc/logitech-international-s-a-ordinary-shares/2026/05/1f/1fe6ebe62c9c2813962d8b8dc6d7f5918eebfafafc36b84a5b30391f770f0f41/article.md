# Signature Comfort Plus: More comfort. Less distraction. Workdays Easier.

##### **Work and life overlap at the same desk, this mouse and keyboard are built to make it more comfortable.**


Work and life now share the same desk. One minute it’s a meeting, next it’s a message from home. A quick task between calls, a list, a reply, a scroll, a reset. The day rarely comes in neat blocks anymore. That’s exactly the reality Logitech designed for with


**Signature Comfort Plus** , a new comfort-first mouse and keyboard lineup built to make long, full desk days feel more manageable.


The new M850 L mouse brings Logitech’s first palm cushion to help your hand feel more supported through long hours and quick in between moments. Pair it with the keyboard that comes in


[MK880](https://logi.link/gsgjza) Signature Comfort Plus Combo, with a soft palm rest and naturally comfortable typing angles, and you get a setup that feels easier to stay with all day.


Silent mouse clicks and a softer typing sound help reduce distractions, so your space feels calmer even when your day isn’t. Switch between work and home devices and even a phone with a tap. Customize buttons for what you use most. Launch AI tools, mute your mic, and move between tasks without losing momentum.


**Why it matters:** Because long, full days deserve more comfortable tools. And comfort shouldn’t be a luxury, it should be your baseline.


Available as – Signature Comfort Plus M850 L cushioned mouse, MK880 keyboard and mouse combo, and M840 L mouse variant without cushion. Available in graphite, off-white, and black (selected channels).
