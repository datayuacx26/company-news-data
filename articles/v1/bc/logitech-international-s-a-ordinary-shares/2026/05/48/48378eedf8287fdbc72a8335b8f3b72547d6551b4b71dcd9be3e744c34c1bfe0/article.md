# Logitech G x McLaren Racing Live: Miami – The Recap

**A Celebration of Speed and Legacy at McLaren Racing Live: Miami!**


From April 29th to May 3rd, Miami’s Regatta Harbour transformed into a motorsport and gaming hub as[McLaren Racing](https://www.logitechg.com/discover/partnerships/mclaren) Live brought fans together to celebrate their historic 1000th Grand Prix campaign. The event combined F1-inspired activities, cutting-edge racing technology, and unforgettable performances on and off the track, putting the thrill of motorsport directly in the hands of fans.


**Logitech G Turns Fans Into Racers**


[Logitech G](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/c/racing-wheels-pedals) made an impactful contribution by giving fans the opportunity to step into the driver’s seat with professional-grade sim racing setups. Eight advanced racing rigs, including an ADA-accessible system, invited attendees to test their driving skills and experience life on the grid.


****


With Logitech G’s[RS50 Direct Drive Wheelbase](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/rs50-system) featuring[TRUEFORCE technology](https://www.logitechg.com/discover/technology/trueforce) , paired with McLaren-inspired hardware like the[RS Formula Wheel](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/rs-formula-wheel-mclaren-racing-edition) and[Playseat Formula Instinct](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/playseat-formula-instinct) , the rigs delivered spine-tingling realism. Every bump, turn, and acceleration could be felt, recreating the sensation of high-speed racing.


This was an invitation for everyone, from first-time drivers to racing fanatics, to connect with the essence of motorsport and feel the excitement firsthand.


**Challenges and Rewards: Hands-on Action**


Fans’ competitive spirit was on full display as they took on lap challenges and week-long competitions. Each lap was an opportunity to push personal limits, with participants competing for exclusive prizes, ranging from McLaren Racing merchandise to coveted Logitech G gear.


These challenges turned friendly rivalry into electric moments of camaraderie, celebrating the shared passion for both real-world and virtual racing.


**A Collector’s Dream: The PRO X Victory Edition Mouse**


Logitech G added exclusivity to the event with the[PRO X Victory Edition mouse](https://www.logitech.com/blog/2026/02/26/prox_victory_edition_logitechg_mclaren/) ; a stunning collector’s item limited to only 25 units globally and crafted as a tribute to McLaren’s Constructor’s Championship triumphs.


With carbon fiber detailing and displayed in a sleek glass plinth, the mouse represented the perfect fusion of Logitech G’s gaming innovation and McLaren’s motorsport heritage, a powerful symbol of the iconic partnership between two brands that redefine excellence in technology and motorsport.


**McLaren’s Double Podium Finish on the Big Stage**


While Logitech G brought fans closer to the virtual grid, McLaren delivered a show-stopping performance on race weekend. The team recorded two back-to-back double podium finishes during the Miami GP, highlighting their prowess on the track and amplifying the sense of celebration.


On Saturday, Lando Norris claimed the top spot with a commanding 1st-place finish in the Sprint Race, followed by Oscar Piastri in an impressive 2nd. The momentum carried into Sunday’s Grand Prix, where the duo added a 2nd and 3rd to McLaren’s tally.


**Final Thoughts: A Partnership Rooted in Innovation**


McLaren Racing: Live showcased the power of collaboration, blending tradition with forward-thinking innovation in motorsport and beyond. Logitech G’s involvement added another dimension to the festivities, proving how gaming technology can bring fans closer to the action and complement the legacy of a team like McLaren.


From thrilling sim racing experiences to unforgettable double podium finishes, the event was a dream for fans of racing and gaming alike. As McLaren continues its journey in motorsport and Logitech G pushes the boundaries of immersive gaming, the alliance between these two worlds promises an exciting road ahead.


The journey doesn’t end here, stay tuned for what’s next, and let’s keep driving innovation together.
