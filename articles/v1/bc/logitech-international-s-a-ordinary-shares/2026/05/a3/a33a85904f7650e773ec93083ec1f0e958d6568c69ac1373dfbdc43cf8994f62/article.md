# Built for the Real Classroom: How the New Rugged Combo 4c Keyboard Cases Keep Students Learning and iPads Safe

Today’s classrooms deserve technology that can keep pace with dynamic teaching and learning environments. Logitech has added new options to the


[Rugged Combo 4 keyboard case](https://www.logitech.com/rugged-combo-4) lineup with the new Rugged Combo 4c and Rugged Combo 4c Touch to better fit the different ways schools work today. Designed to support seamless uninterrupted learning, these cases make it easier for IT teams to scale deployment and for teachers to keep their lessons moving.


We spent time in the classroom with teachers and tech leads for extensive research and understanding of what are the needs and challenges in these settings. The result? The Rugged Combo 4c. It’s built to handle more than just a desk drop. It’s made for the spills, backpacks,and bus rides that actually happen, so you can focus on the lesson, not the hardware.


Compatible with iPad (A16) and iPad (10th generation), the Rugged Combo 4c introduces features that expand the versatility of iPad. An additional USB-C connector allows students to charge their iPad and use a wired headset simultaneously, supporting consistent lesson flow. As audio becomes increasingly central to literacy, language learning, and digital assessments, the Rugged Combo 4c ensures that technology seamlessly supports the lesson. Rugged Combo 4c ensures students stay fully immersed in their lessons without having to worry about battery life or unplugging their headphones.


All Rugged Combo cases feature a magnetic latch that provides a secure place to store a digital pencil, and, additionally, Rugged Combo 4c and 4c Touch have an updated holder design to allow for Apple Pencil magnetic attachment. Scaled deployment is made easy through the large asset tag window and QR code for rapid S/N scanning.


Additional features include:


- **Military-Grade Drop Protection** : Drop protection up to 6.6 ft., and backpack drop-tested 10,000 times so iPad stays protected for tumbles and falls.


- **Smart Connector:** Instantly pairs with iPad for seamless setup, powered directly by iPad so charging of the Rugged Combo 4c is never required.


- **Multi-touch Trackpad** : The Rugged Combo 4c Touch features a large, high-precision trackpad so students can seamlessly navigate and engage with content in a natural, intuitive way.


- **Sealed, Spill-Resistant Keyboard:** Delivers a comfortable and silent typing experience while protecting against spills in busy classroom environments.


- **Easy to clean** : Designed with durable and easy-to-clean material, tested to withstand over three years of daily cleaning at schools.


- **Four use modes:** Type, View, Read, & Sketch.
