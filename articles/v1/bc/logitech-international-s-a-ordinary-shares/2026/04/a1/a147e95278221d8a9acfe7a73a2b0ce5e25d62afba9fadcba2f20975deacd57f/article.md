# Turning Fans Into Drivers: Logitech G Powers Racing Experience at McLaren Racing Live: Miami

Feel the roar of engines, the pulse of competition, and the thrill of precision engineering as McLaren Racing Live: Miami takes over Regatta Harbour from April 29th to May 3rd. This five-day spectacle transforms the harbour into a McLaren motorsport haven with support from[Logitech G](https://www.logitechg.com/discover/partnerships/mclaren) , the world’s leading global gaming brand.


As part of McLaren Racing’s 1000th Grand Prix campaign, this event is more than just a milestone. It is a celebration of the legacy, passion, and precision that define McLaren Racing, highlighting not only a historic achievement but an unrelenting drive toward championship performance.


Whether you are an F1 fanatic, a gaming enthusiast, or someone eager to soak up the Miami sun, this event offers an unforgettable fusion of speed, technology, and culture.


## **Welcome To The Grid**


Logitech G invites you to experience the thrills of sim racing at McLaren Racing Live: Miami. Step into the action on eight state-of-the-art racing rigs, including a fully ADA-accessible PC rig, and discover what it means to take your place on the grid.


**The Setup**


Powering this experience is Logitech G’s Racing Series hardware, featuring the[RS50 Direct Drive Wheelbase](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/rs50-system) with[TRUEFORCE](https://www.logitechg.com/discover/technology/trueforce) , paired with the[RS Formula Wheel McLaren Racing Edition](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/rs-formula-wheel-mclaren-racing-edition) ,[A50X McLaren Racing Edition](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/astro-a50-x-mclaren-edition) , and[Playseat Formula Instinct McLaren Racing Edition](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/playseat-formula-instinct) . This Collection delivers next-gen force feedback so precise, you’ll feel every bump, corner, and burst of acceleration as if you’re on the track.


**Drive to Win**


Skill-based prizing and giveaways ramp up the excitement, rewarding driving performance with exclusive prizes. It is a moment where your love for racing and gaming take center stage. Think you have what it takes?


## **Spotlight: PRO X Victory Edition**


The[PRO X: Victory Edition mouse](https://www.logitech.com/blog/2026/02/26/prox_victory_edition_logitechg_mclaren/) is the ultimate celebration of Logitech G and McLaren Racing’s partnership, blending carbon fiber construction with design inspired by McLaren’s F1 cars to create a stunning masterpiece of gaming innovation. Limited to just 25 globally and displayed in a premium glass plinth, this rare collectible is a must-see for fans of racing, gaming, and exceptional design.


## **Mark Your Calendar**


April 29th to May 3rd is your chance to unleash your skills, immerse yourself in sim racing, and explore gear redefining the boundaries of gaming innovation.


Do not just witness history. Drive it.


We’ll see you in Miami.
