# Logitech G and Le Mans Ultimate: Where Community Meets Cutting Edge Tech

Few names carry the weight and history of Le Mans, and now, that legacy meets cutting-edge gaming technology. Logitech G and Le Mans Ultimate, The Official Game of the 24 Hours of Le Mans, are joining forces to deliver realism, immersion, and community experiences to sim racers worldwide.


“We’re proud to partner with Logitech G, a brand that shares our commitment to pushing the boundaries of simulation and delivering truly immersive racing experiences. Together with Logitech G we’re excited to continue to engage with players through engaging technology and exciting online competition” Said Ben Rossiter-Turner, VP Studios


**TRUEFORCE Technology: Bringing Reality to Your Hands** ****


At the center of this partnership is[TRUEFORCE](https://www.logitechg.com/discover/technology/trueforce) , Logitech G’s proprietary high-definition force feedback system. Unlike conventional systems that use simple rumble effects, TRUEFORCE connects directly to in-game physics and audio engines to create precise, real-time haptic feedback.


In Le Mans Ultimate, driving a Logitech G wheel means more elements of the car and track come alive, delivering a sensory experience that’s as close to real racing as you can get. Logitech G hardware like the[G923](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/g923-trueforce-sim-racing-wheel) ,[RS50](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/rs50-system) , and[PRO Racing Wheel](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/pro-racing-wheel) takes full advantage of this technology, delivering unmatched precision and responsiveness.


**Feel The Tires**


Through TRUEFORCE, you feel every nuance of your connection to the track. When you push too hard through a corner, tire scrub becomes tangible; you feel the friction grow as grip fades away.


**Feedback Where It Matters Most**


From the jolt of a gear change to the faint, rhythmic vibration of the pit speed limiter engaging, TRUEFORCE translates those subtleties into touch. You’ll feel when traction breaks, sense the texture of rumble strips under your wheels, and understand your limits like never before.


**Engine Haptics**


TRUEFORCE connects you to the engine itself. Every rev, shift, and idle vibration channels through the wheel to your hands, giving you a visceral sense of the car’s power. It’s not just about hearing the engine; you feel its pulse through every straight and corner.


**Logitech McLaren G Challenge: A New Dawn**


The[Logitech G Challenge](https://www.logitechgchallenge.com/en) has long been recognized as the premier esports competition for sim racing, offering an accessible platform for casual drivers and aspiring racers of all skill levels. With Le Mans Ultimate now at the core of the online G Challenge competition, this year’s event promises to deliver the same thrills you’re familiar with alongside new tracks, cars, and incredible prizes.

**A Community Built for Players**


Beyond technology and competition, this partnership is about bringing people together. Logitech G and Le Mans Ultimate are committed to building inclusive, community-driven experiences for sim racers worldwide.


The path ahead for endurance racing is more exciting than ever. Master the track. Feel every tire carve its story across the tarmac. Hear the engine roar in a symphony of speed and precision. And with TRUEFORCE, feel it all, because this is racing as it was meant to be.


The future of endurance racing starts now.
