Whether you were testing your speed, racing for glory, or meeting your favorite creator, every moment delivered both excitement and innovation. Here’s the full rundown of what made the Logitech G experience unforgettable.

Whether for competition or pure immersion, this segment offered unforgettable moments for each individual driver.

Behind the scenes, our product development team joined DreamHack Birmingham, engaging with pro players in private sessions to test upcoming Logitech G innovations.

Community was at the heart of DreamHack Birmingham, and Logitech G helped bring creators and fans together in meaningful ways. Attendees had the chance to meet some of their favorite gaming personalities, share stories, and even exchange tips for gear setups.


## **Capturing the Spirit of DreamHack**


The Logitech G booth was about more than the latest technology; it was about creating memorable moments for the community. Whether posting high scores, mastering turns on the track, or meeting their gaming heroes, attendees embraced every opportunity to connect, compete, and level up.
