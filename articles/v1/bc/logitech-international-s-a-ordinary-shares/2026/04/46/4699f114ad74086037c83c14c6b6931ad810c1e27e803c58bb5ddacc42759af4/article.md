# Making Your Productivity and Creativity Seamless with the MX Ecosystem That Follows Your Ideas

The Logitech MX series has always been about more than just hardware. It’s an ecosystem of hardware and software products that seamlessly work together to bring your vision to life. We continually evolve that experience, and today we are bringing several important improvements that will benefit both our new and existing users.


“Our goal is to enhance the workflows of advanced users, while making the technology behind it invisible,” said Maxim Bondar, head of MX business and marketing at Logitech. “By introducing community-requested features like Enhanced Easy Switch and our new productivity plugins, including for Microsoft Office, I am happy we continue to add more ‘superpowers’ to the MX Ecosystem.”


#### **New MX Ecosystem features:**


- **Enhanced Easy Switch:** Based on direct feedback from our MX community, we’ve created a more seamless way to move your devices between computers. Now, you can switch your MX Keys S or MX Keys Mini keyboard to a different workstation, and your MX mouse and MX Creative Dialpad follow automatically. One key press simultaneously moves three devices, so no manual re-connecting is required. This “magic” is compatible with all Logi Bolt devices. While MX Keys S and Mini are ready today (make sure to be on the latest firmware release), MX Mechanical and MX Mechanical Mini will receive the required firmware update this summer.


- **Actions Ring is across the entire MX portfolio:** The power of the Actions Ring is no longer limited to select devices. It is now supported across the entire MX lineup, including the MX Master 4, MX Master 3S, MX Anywhere 3S, MX Keys S, MX Keys Mini, MX Mechanical, and MX Mechanical Mini.


- **Folders in Actions Ring:** Stay organized by grouping your digital shortcuts into nested folders for a cleaner, more efficient workflow.


- **Updated icon editor:** Personalize your workspace by creating custom styles for your Actions Ring and MX Keypad, then apply them across your entire setup with one click.


**More Plugins for Productivity:** We are introducing a new suite of Productivity Plugins designed to enhance not only the


[MX Creative Console](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/shop/p/buy-mx-creative-console.920-012661?sp=1&searchclick=Logitech) , your dedicated hardware controller, but also for the entire MX Ecosystem, bringing the most important actions and tools to your cursor via the Actions Ring.


- **Microsoft Office:** Navigate Excel sheets, style Word documents, and manage PowerPoint slides with full control and precision.


- **Notion for Desktop:** Keep your ideas connected and your pages organized via dedicated controls through LCD screens or digital overlay menus.


- **Slack:** Jump into huddles, switch workspaces, and manage threads with a single tap or click.


The future of work is enabled by the MX ecosystem that helps you stay in the flow and create at the speed of thought.
