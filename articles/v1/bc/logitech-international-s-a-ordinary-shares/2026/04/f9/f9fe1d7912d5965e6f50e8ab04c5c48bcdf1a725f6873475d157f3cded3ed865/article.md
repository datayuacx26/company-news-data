# Spring Refresh: 4 Ways to Style Your Desk For Spring

Spring’s warmer weather has you dreaming of spending the day outdoors, but chances are your desk is still your daily destination. Just like swapping winter wear for spring staples, refreshing your desk can make those 9-to-5 (or 9-to-whenever) hours feel way less routine.


Here are the 4 trends shaping the “Deskie” this season:


### **1. Bold Aesthetics**


Move over millennial grey, color is claiming its rightful spot at the desk. Curating a space that reflects your creative flow means warm lighting, softer pastels, and earthier tones.

It’s all about texture to create a workspace that feels as good as it looks. Think soft desk mats on wooden surfaces or smooth scrolling with tactile typing. Experiment with contrast until you find the perfect combination for focus and inspiration.

Say goodbye to sharp, aggressive angles and hello to soft-minimalism. 2026 design is all about human-centric shapes with curved furniture and rounded peripherals finding home at the workspace. When you spend 8+ hours at the desk, you need it to feel comforting and welcoming.

Trinkets, figures, and collectibles are moving off the shelf and onto the desk. Gone are the days of hiding your personality; it’s time to make everyday spaces – and everyday tech – your own.
