# Logitech G Racing Series: Unlocking Precision in Every Lap

Since its introduction in 2024, the[Logitech G Racing Series](https://www.logitechg.com/discover/c/r-series) has set a new standard for sim racing. With precision engineering, immersive technology, and modularity at its core, this ecosystem adapts to racers of all skill levels, empowering beginners and professionals alike to evolve their setups at their own pace. From responsive Direct Drive Wheelbases with[TRUEFORCE](https://www.logitechg.com/discover/technology/trueforce) to finely tuned pedals, Logitech G’s Racing Series is giving sim racers and enthusiasts the tools to feel every bump, every turn, and every thrill.


Now, in 2026, Logitech G takes the Racing Series ecosystem to the next level with the launch of the[RS H-Shifter](http://logitechg.com/rs-h-shifter) , a 7+R manual shifter built to deliver unparalleled realism, tactile control, and extended durability. Designed for racers who crave authenticity, it’s the perfect addition for those seeking a deeper connection to the thrill of manual driving.


**Racing Series Ecosystem: The Heart of Sim Racing**


At the center of the Racing Series lies the[RS50 Direct Drive Wheelbase](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/rs50-system) , a masterpiece of engineering capable of delivering a powerful 8Nm of torque, designed to bring the track to life with precision and immersive force feedback. Powered by TRUEFORCE feedback technology, the RS50 processes up to 4,000 in-game updates per second, replicating every shift in grip, the roar of the engine, and the bumps on the road with extraordinary precision. Whether you’re navigating high-speed straights or winding through rally stages, the RS50 transforms realism into full immersion.


Adding modular versatility, the[RS Wheel Hub](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/rs-wheel-hub) connects seamlessly across platforms, while different wheel options, such as the[RS Track Wheel](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/rs-track-wheel) and[RS Round Wheel](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/rs-round-wheel) , allow racers to fine-tune their rigs for any motorsport style. Logitech G’s modular design ensures that every racer can build their setup step by step, whether they’re starting fresh or upgrading their existing gear.


Complementing the base are the[RS Pedals](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/rs-pedals) , designed with precision that rivals professional setups. With the load-cell brake pedal capable of detecting up to 75kg of applied pressure, the RS Pedals emulate the feel of real-world braking with unmatched accuracy. The Hall Effect accelerator provides smooth and consistent throttle application, while customizable brake resistance lets racers personalize their setup to their unique driving style.


Further enhancing performance is the[RS Shifter & Handbrake](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/rs-shifter-handbrake) , with dual-mode functionality, allowing it to operate as either a sequential shifter or a handbrake. For racers who want to maximize control and specialization, it’s possible to purchase two units; one dedicated entirely to sequential shifting and the other to handbrake operations, providing peak performance in rally and drifting setups. With adjustable actuation points, tunable response curves, and a durable steel construction, the RS Shifter & Handbrake delivers unmatched realism and reliability across all racing disciplines.


**Introducing the RS H-Shifter: Completing the Experience**


For racers who crave the mechanical thrill of manual control, the RS H-Shifter introduces a new level of immersion. Its advanced 7-speed (+Reverse) gearbox replicates the tactile feel of real-world gear changes, accompanied by push-through lockouts for confident transitions during high-pressure races. Hall Effect sensors eliminate wear and ensure long-term durability, while its robust aluminum and steel construction stands up to the intensity of competitive sessions.


Adaptable mounting options make the RS H-Shifter ideal for both table setups and rigs, offering incredible versatility for all racers. From novice drivers exploring manual setups to seasoned competitors perfecting their technique, the RS H-Shifter bridges the gap between simulation and reality.


Richard Neville, Head of Product for Simulation, states: “The RS H-Shifter delivers the kind of immersive realism that racers have been asking for. Every gear change feels intuitive, exciting, and incredibly engaging, it completes the Racing Series ecosystem in every way.”


**Expanding Immersion with the McLaren Racing Collection**


While the Racing Series ecosystem delivers unparalleled modularity, Logitech G takes immersion even further with the[McLaren Racing Collection](https://www.logitechg.com/discover/partnerships/mclaren) . Featuring the already available[A50 X McLaren Racing Edition](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/astro-a50-x-mclaren-edition) , as well as the soon to be released[RS Formula Wheel McLaren Racing Edition](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/rs-formula-wheel-mclaren-racing-edition) and[Playseat® Formula Instinct Logitech G McLaren Racing Edition](https://www.logitechg.com/shop/p/playseat-formula-instinct) , this range brings the thrill of formula racing to sim racers everywhere.
