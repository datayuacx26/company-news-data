# The 6 Best Keyboards for Screenwriters in 2026

Stars took the stage last night at the 98th Academy Awards to celebrate excellence in cinema, where storytelling plays a key role. For writers who aspire to be the next[Ryan Coogler](https://www.theguardian.com/film/2026/mar/16/ryan-coogler-wins-best-original-screenplay-oscar-for-sinners) or[Paul Thomas Anderson](https://deadline.com/2026/03/paul-thomas-anderson-oscar-win-adapted-screenplay-speech-1236755061/)


, there’s one tool you can’t overlook – your keyboard.


At Logitech, we know keyboards are a personal preference and there is no one-size-fits-all solution. If you’re spending hours writing, now is the time to find what best supports your style. From mechanical to membrane, full-size to compact, and wavy to low-profile, we’ve chosen our favorites.


Without further ado, the winners for best keyboards for screenwriters goes to…


#### [Keys-to-Go 2](https://www.logitech.com/shop/p/keys-to-go2-universal) **, for writing anywhere, anytime.**


Inspiration knows no boundaries and ideas can emerge in the most unexpected places. When you’re writing on planes, trains, or at cafes, or on tablets, phones or laptops, you need a keyboard that is super slim, light, and easily fits in your bag. Keys-To-Go 2 is our most portable keyboard ever and a great companion for on-the-go writing.


#### **[MX Keys S](https://www.logitech.com/shop/p/mx-keys-s) , for late-night writing.**


When those late-night ideas hit, you need a keyboard that instantly knows you’re there. MX Keys S has smart illumination, so those backlit keys light up when your hands approach the keyboard. And with keys shaped for your fingertips, it minimizes mistypes. Better flow with fewer corrections.


#### **[Signature Slim Solar+](https://www.logitech.com/shop/p/signature-slim-solar-plus) , for uninterrupted writing.**


Tech should be the last thing to get in your way when you sit down to write. Low battery warnings and tangled cables are now a thing of the past. Signature Slim Solar+ is a keyboard powered by solar and artificial light, so you don’t have to worry about charging it. As the ideas flow, it stays on and powered without the interruptions – day or night.


#### [Wave Keys](https://www.logitech.com/shop/p/wave-keys-ergonomic-wireless) **, for all-day writing comfort.**


Long days spent writing can put strain on your body. If you feel fatigue after hours at the desk, it might be time to switch to an ergonomic keyboard. Wave Keys provides all day support and comfort with its signature wave design and cushioned palm rest. Trust us, your wrists, forearms, and posture will thank you.


#### [Alto Keys K98M](https://www.logitech.com/shop/p/alto-keys-k98m) **, for satisfying sound.**


Sometimes the typing experience is enough to keep you in the scene. That satisfying sound that supports your writing rhythm. Alto Keys K98M is a mechanical keyboard with linear switches that absorbs vibrations as you type, so you still get that tactile feel without losing focus. When your keyboard sounds this good, you won’t want to stop writing.


#### **[Combo Touch](https://www.logitech.com/shop/p/combo-touch-ipad) , for focused writing on iPad.**


****


For writers who rely on their iPad for on set script revisions or travel days, you need a tool that keeps you focused and productive while on-the-go. Combo Touch is a keyboard case that offers comfortable typing with backlit keys all while keeping your iPad protected. And with an adjustable kickstand, you can get writing done no matter where you do it.


These are our 2026 winners, but we encourage you to choose what works best for you. At the end of the day, it’s about what keeps you comfortable and productive while supporting your creative flow to bring stories to life.
