# Modernizing Defense Collaboration: How Logitech Helps Agencies Deploy Microsoft Teams Rooms on Windows

#### **Strengthening Secure Collaboration for Defense Agencies**


In defense environments, every communication channel, meeting space, and device must meet the highest standards for security and reliability. That’s why Microsoft Teams Rooms on Windows joining the Department of Defense Information Network (DoDIN) Approved Products List (APL) is such a meaningful milestone—one that expands what’s possible for secure, simple collaboration across defense environments.


For defense organizations adopting this platform, L


ogitech’s comprehensive portfolio of hardware solutions certified for Microsoft Teams Rooms plays a pivotal role in enabling secure, compliant, and flexible deployment across defense environments. As a trusted Microsoft partner, Logitech provides

#### **Why Microsoft’s DoDIN Approval Matters**


The DoDIN APL represents the Department of Defense’s trusted gateway for approved communications technologies. Each listed solution has undergone rigorous evaluation to meet DoD cybersecurity standards and operational requirements.


With Microsoft Teams Rooms on Windows now approved, defense organizations gain access to a collaboration platform built for classified and mission-critical operations. Paired with Logitech’s video conferencing and room solutions, agencies can securely integrate modern communication workflows without compromising on usability or compliance.

These all-in-one systems including Logitech video bars, controllers, and mic pods, deliver a plug-and-play experience that simplifies setup and minimizes IT intervention. From command centers to field offices, Logitech’s hardware helps ensure rigorous security standards are met.
