# Restoring Access to Options+ and G HUB on macOS

If you are unable to launch Logitech Options+ or G HUB on your Mac today, you are in the right place. First, we are truly sorry for the disruption and inconvenience. Second, we have quickly delivered a solution. We have a patch available now to get you back up and running.


Instructions on how to manually download the solution can be found below.


**Do NOT uninstall your current app** – doing so will erase your customizations. Simply run the patch installer over your existing version to keep all your profiles and customizations safe.


Note: if you did uninstall your app, please run this update regardless. If you use Settings Backup in Options+ you’ll be able to restore your settings. If not, or on G HUB, you’ll need to select your customizations again.


**How to Fix It**


The process is slightly different depending on which app you are using.


**For Logitech Options+**


1. **Download:** Get the updated ‘patch’ installer for Options+ \[


**[Link](https://download01.logi.com/web/ftp/pub/techsupport/optionsplus/logioptionsplus_installer.zip)** \].


2. **Install:** Double-click the downloaded file.


3. **Launch:** The installer will automatically close, and Options+ will launch. Your devices and settings should be fully restored.


**For Logitech G HUB**


1. **Download:** Get the updated ‘patch’ installer for G HUB \[


**[Link](https://download01.logi.com/web/ftp/pub/techsupport/gaming/lghub_installer.zip)** \].


2. **Install:** Double-click the downloaded file.


3. **Exit:** Once the message


**“software already exists”** appears, exit the installer.


4. **Launch:** Open G HUB manually. Your devices and settings should be fully restored.


**Compatibility**


The fix is currently available for:


- macOS 26 Tahoe


- macOS 15 Sequoia


- macOS 14 Sonoma


- macOS 13 Ventura


We are working on updates for earlier macOS versions and will share timing for those as soon as possible. Windows users are not affected.


More information is available on support page **[here](https://support.logi.com/hc/en-us/articles/37493733117847-Options-and-G-HUB-macOS-Certificate-Issue)**[.](https://support.logi.com/hc/en-us/articles/37493733117847-Options-and-G-HUB-macOS-Certificate-Issue)
