**Logitech’s latest Rally Cameras are here, and they’re packed with brilliant optics, AI-powered video intelligence, smart automation, and sleek design features—now for bigger, more complex spaces.**


Logitech is building on the quality of its fan-favorite Rally Camera to bring you two new smart models:


- **Rally AI Camera Pro:** Perfect for larger spaces, like all-hands meeting and training rooms. Its dual camera system, optical and digital, means even your biggest meetings get up-close and personal.


- **Rally AI Camera:** Sleek, digital, and designed to fit right into your room aesthetic—especially with its low-profile in-wall mount option.
