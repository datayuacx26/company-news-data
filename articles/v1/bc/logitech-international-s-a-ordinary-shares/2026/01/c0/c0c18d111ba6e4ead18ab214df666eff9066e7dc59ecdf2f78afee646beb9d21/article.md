# What is Logitech Bringing to ISE 2026?

Pack your bags, comfortable shoes, and an extra battery pack, because Logitech is heading back to Barcelona for ISE 2026


with a booth so packed with tech, you’ll have to visit twice!


From February 3rd to 6th, Logitech will be at the Fira de Barcelona,


Booth #2K100,


to show how our smarter, simpler, and more sustainable solutions make life easier for employees, IT and AV teams. Whether you’re working from a coffee shop, boardroom, phone booth, home office, open office, or a classroom, we’ve got a solution that


*just works* .


What can you expect from Logitech at the show?


- No two workspaces are the same — that’s what we design for. This is your chance to get hands-on with flexible solutions that adapt to how your team works and modular systems that scale with your business


.


- A true work-from-anywhere experience, where Logitech turns conference rooms and coffee shops alike into centers of productivity.


- Tailored solutions for Public Sector, including Logitech for Education’s return of the popular Connected Classroom.


- Alliances like Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Zoom, and many more listed below–on full display so you can see firsthand how our partners make the tech behind the scenes feel frictionless.


- Everything we do is driven by a relentless commitment to sustainability, so you can feel as good about your hardware as you do about that extra serving of tapas.


**PRODUCT SHOWCASE** **** Think of booth #2K100 as your official home base for the modern workplace. We’re packing the floor with everything from the small-but-mighty (headsets, mice, and Spot) to Logitech’s largest device yet (ready to flip out again with Rally Board 65?). If you’re an IT or AV pro, this isn’t just a pit stop—it’s a “must-see, must-touch, must-experience” destination


**.**


***NEW* Rally Camera AI & Rally Camera AI Pro** **** Get up close and personal with these two newest showstoppers. Logitech’s most advanced camera yet, Rally Camera AI Pro, brings dual-camera sophistication with AI-powered RightSight 2 to complex rooms like town hall spaces, large boardrooms, and training facilities. And Rally AI Camera is a clever option for designers who take aesthetics seriously–an in-wall mount lets the camera blend seamlessly into the background.


**Logitech Spot** **** This isn’t just a pretty face; it’s a modern three-in-one powerhouse that detects occupancy with pinpoint accuracy to automatically claim or release room reservations the moment you walk in. But the real magic happens when it starts “reading the room, monitoring environmental conditions to nudge you with suggested actions that keep your team comfortable, energized and your energy bills low. Come see how Spot turns a standard meeting space into a high-performance, healthy workspace.


**Zone Wireless 2 ES for Business** **** Swing by the booth to slip on this “gaming-inspired” headset and witness how Adaptive Hybrid ANC and AI-powered noise suppression turn chaotic background noise into a distant memory. Whether you want to test out the 170-foot wireless range by doing a victory lap or just want to feel the “plush-and-pillowy” comfort of a headset designed for marathon workdays, we’ve got a pair waiting for you. Plus, you can see how it effortlessly swaps between devices, proving that high-performance tech can finally be high-convenience, too.


**Rally Board 65** **** As Logitech’s biggest device, the Rally Board 65 represents a major shift in how AI and hardware are being used to solve the “open office” dilemma. Rally Board 65 can be flipped 180 degrees, allowing the camera to be positioned at the bottom for eye-level contact when used in a standing configuration or on a mobile cart. A stunning piece of industrial design you have to see to believe!


**So much more** **** That’s just the tip of the iceberg. You’ll also see Logitech Rally Bar family, MeetUp 2, Tap Scheduler, Tap IP, Logitech Sight (and dual camera Daisy Chaining), Logitech View, RoomMate, Logi Dock Flex, MX Master 4, MX Creative Console, MX Brio 505 and 705, MX Keys for Business, Lift for Business, and more!
