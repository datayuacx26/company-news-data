# Meet the G325: A Sibling to G321 and Your Next Big Decision

Your headset is the most prominent piece of gear in your arsenal. It’s the one peripheral you actually wear, so we are thrilled to add another member to our family of gaming headsets, bringing you another choice for headgear. Introducing the


[Logitech G325 LIGHTSPEED Wireless Gaming Headset](https://www.logitechg.com/en-eu/g325) , made to fit seamlessly into your life, wherever you play.


Designed for superior comfort and wireless play anywhere, the G325 delivers premium performance in a sleek, modern design that you’ll be proud to show off at home or on the go. Weighing in at just 212 grams, it offers a “barely there” fit and a sophisticated silhouette that looks as good as it feels.


Joining our 3 Series, the G325 isn’t arriving to stand all alone. It lands just five months after its sibling, the


[Logitech G321 LIGHTSPEED Wireless Gaming Headset](https://www.logitechg.com/en-eu/shop/p/g321.981-001563) , made its debut.


*Side by side: G325 meets G321*


So with two incredible lightweight wireless headsets now sitting in the G3 Series, you might be asking: “Which one is the right fit for me?” The good news is, you can’t go wrong with either. Both are designed for PC gaming excellence, offering premium audio, all-day comfort, and LIGHTSPEED wireless reliability—all at a price that leaves you plenty of budget for new games.


But while they share some of the same DNA, they each have unique attributes that fit different styles of play. Let’s break it down so you can find your perfect match.


### **G325: For the All-Around Gamer Who Plays Anywhere**


If you think of gaming as a lifestyle that doesn’t stop when you leave your desk, the G325 is your new best friend. We designed this headset for the PC player who values versatility, comfort and style above all else.


- **Comfort and Style On The Go:** The G325 features a streamlined, modern design with no external boom arm, swapping it for a built-in microphone. This means it looks just as good on the street or in a coffee shop as it does in your battle station. It’s the headset you wouldn’t be embarrassed to wear outside the house.


- **More Control:** If you have a knack for customizing your tech, G325 is for you. Through


G HUB software and the mobile app, you can adjust a 10-band EQ or choose from game-optimized presets to personalize your audio experience.


- **Versatility:** While built for PC excellence, the G325 offers total freedom. Connect seamlessly to mobile via Bluetooth to blast your favorite playlist, or take advantage of the impressive 30-meter LIGHTSPEED wireless range to stretch your legs without missing any action from the game.


### **G321: For the First-time Gamer Who Hits the Ground Running**


If the G325 is the stylish hop-around, the


**G321** is the reliable, high-performance daily standard. Easy to use, it’s the perfect choice for someone looking for their first serious wireless PC gaming headset.


- **Ready Out-of-the-Box:** The G321 is for players who want to plug in and instantly immerse themselves in the game. It doesn’t dangle any customization options, as it’s perfectly tuned for excellent performance right from the start.


- **The Boom Mic:** The G321 rocks a 16 kHz flip-to-mute boom microphone. It’s a classic feature for a reason—there is something incredibly satisfying (and quick) about flipping your mic up to mute when you need to.


- **Broad Compatibility:** While it shines on PC, the G321 offers the added bonus of working across a massive range of platforms via its USB receiver, including Xbox Series X|S, PlayStation, and Switch. If you primarily game on PC but occasionally jump to consoles, the G321 has you covered.


**A Headset for Every Gamer**


Deciding which headset is right for you ultimately comes down to how and what you play. The G325 and G321 are both premium PC gaming headsets offering superior comfort at a great price. They are proud members of the 3 Series and part of a much bigger headset family designed to fit every type of gamer.


Meet the rest of the squad:


- **The headsets that rule them all:** For console-first players, the living room often reigns over gameplay, but not always. The A20 X, A50, and A50 X all feature PlaySync technology for effortless platform switching.


- **A go-to for legendary gameplay:** Players consistently reaching new heights can look to the G522 for advanced performance, RGB flair and proper bragging rights.


- **The competitor’s choice:** For those chasing the win, the PRO X 2 Wireless delivers esports-grade technology used by the world’s best athletes.


With a portfolio meticulously engineered for every type of player—from the comfortable new G325 to the competitive-level PRO X 2—Logitech G is here to ensure you never miss a beat. The gear is ready. The choice is yours. Keep Playing.
