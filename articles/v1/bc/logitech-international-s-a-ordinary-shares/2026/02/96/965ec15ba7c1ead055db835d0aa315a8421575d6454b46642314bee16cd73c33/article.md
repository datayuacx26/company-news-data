# When Performance Wins Championships & Becomes Design

2025 was a landmark year for McLaren Racing and a defining moment for our partnership. McLaren claimed its 10th Constructors’ Championship, while Lando Norris secured his first Drivers’ Championship, cementing a season marked by relentless performance, precision engineering, and fearless execution.


At[Logitech G](https://www.logitechg.com/en-gb/discover/partnerships/mclaren) , we couldn’t imagine a better opportunity to celebrate what happens when two performance-driven brands inspire and advance each other.


To honor this achievement, Logitech G created something truly rare, a collectible mouse dubbed the ‘PRO X: Victory Edition’ in celebration of our friends in papaya. Even more exciting, there are only 25 units in existence worldwide:


- Logitech G’s first-ever carbon fiber mouse


- Hand-assembled with obsessive attention to detail


- 3 grams lighter than our award winning PRO X SUPERLIGHT 2


- Engineered under an accelerated timeline, made possible by swift decisions and a small, elite team


Every curve, surface, and material choice reflects the principles that power McLaren on the grid: reduce weight, increase strength, and eliminate anything unnecessary.


This mouse will not be available at retail. G Challenge winners will have the chance to earn one, with a limited number also appearing via[Logitech G and McLaren social giveaways](https://gleam.io/mlgvb/logitech-g-pro-x-victory-edition-giveaway) . Just like racing, access is all about performance.


Our partnership with McLaren is more than a badge or special edition—it’s a deep, ongoing collaboration rooted in shared values, design excellence, engineering rigor, and performance under pressure. From product development to community events, we continuously translate motorsport thinking into gaming innovation.


McLaren builds race cars measured in milliseconds. Logitech G builds gear measured in microns and latency. Different arenas, same mindset.


Welcome to the grid.
