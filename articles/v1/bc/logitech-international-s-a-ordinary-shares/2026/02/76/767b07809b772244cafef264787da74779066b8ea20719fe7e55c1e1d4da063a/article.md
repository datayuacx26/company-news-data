# Logitech Wins 7 iF Design Awards for Innovation Across Categories

Logitech has been honored with 7 iF Design Awards for 2026.


In the 36 years since iF first recognized Logitech’s design excellence, the company has garnered 171 awards, with a recognition every year since 2010.


Founded in 1954, the iF Design Awards is among the world’s most prestigious and important design competitions. Each year, an independent jury of leading international design and sustainability experts evaluate submissions through a rigorous review process. This year, more than 10,000 entries from 68 countries were considered. The iF label honors designs that dare to “challenge conventions, rethink systems, and elevate everyday experiences into meaningful impact.” Products, packaging, UX, and UI are all considered disciplines in the selection process.


The Logitech products recognized for creative excellence this year include: The G325 Lightspeed Wireless Gaming Headset, Rally Camera Streamline Kit, Signature Slim Solar+ Wireless Keyboard K980, MX Ink, MX Master 4, Miniroll, and Flip Folio.


This breadth of recognition reflects Logitech’s ongoing commitment to thoughtful design that drives superior performance across work and play – enabling users to stay creative, productive, and connected.


Thank you to iF Design for the continued recognition.
