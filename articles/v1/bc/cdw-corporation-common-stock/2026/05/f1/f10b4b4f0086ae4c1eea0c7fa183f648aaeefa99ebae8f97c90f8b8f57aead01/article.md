# CDW to Participate in the J.P. Morgan 2026 Global Technology, Media & Communications Conference

### CDW to Participate in the J.P. Morgan 2026 Global Technology, Media & Communications Conference


May 5, 2026


VERNON HILLS, Ill.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


CDW Corporation (Nasdaq: CDW) today announced that Christine A. Leahy, chair and chief executive officer, and Albert J. Miralles, chief financial officer and executive vice president, enterprise business operations, CDW, will participate in a question and answer session at the J.P. Morgan 2026 Global Technology, Media and Communications Conference in Boston, Massachusetts on Tuesday, May 19, 2025, at 10:25 a.m. CT/11:25 a.m. ET. The session will be webcast live on[investor.cdw.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.cdw.com%2Foverview%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54528926&newsitemid=20260505419463&lan=en-US&anchor=investor.cdw.com&index=1&md5=194a025d5c0e242f05cd0415a9d337f0) . An archived copy of the webcast will be available on the same website for 30 days following the completion of the event.


**About CDW**


CDW Corporation is a leading multi-brand provider of information technology solutions to business, government, education and healthcare customers in the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada. A Fortune 500 company and member of the S&P 500 Index, CDW helps its customers to navigate an increasingly complex IT market and maximize return on their technology investments. For more information about CDW, please visit[www.CDW.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.CDW.com&esheet=54528926&newsitemid=20260505419463&lan=en-US&anchor=www.CDW.com&index=2&md5=7480981b07a9be7ec44d80501b9d051a) .
