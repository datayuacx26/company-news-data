# Stoic Update: Custom Journaling Templates & Weekly Themes

## Custom Journaling Templates Are Here


This week’s update brings something big — and highly requested:[Feature request #2](https://feedback.getstoic.com/2) ‍


You can now create your own **custom journal templates** in stoic.
Design your own guided journals from scratch. Add the prompts that matter to you. Track the things you want to track. Save them. Reuse them. Build your own practice, your own way.
We can’t wait to see what you create with it.


‍


## How to Create a Custom Journal in Stoic


1. Open Stoic and go to the Library Tab
2. Scroll down to "Your Templates" → then + New
3. Add your own prompts, scales, and tracking elements
4. Tap Save — and it’s ready to use anytime
5. You can add it to favorites and it will be available in quick access (under the main big + button)
‍


## Weekly Themes


We’ve also refreshed the **Weekly Themes** section in the library. It’s now easier to browse and more visually clear — so you can dive into inspiration faster.


‍


## New Completion & Streak Screens


Every time you finish an activity, you’ll now see a cleaner, more celebratory **completion screen** — and your **streak** will feel even more motivating.
Because reflection deserves a little recognition.


‍
