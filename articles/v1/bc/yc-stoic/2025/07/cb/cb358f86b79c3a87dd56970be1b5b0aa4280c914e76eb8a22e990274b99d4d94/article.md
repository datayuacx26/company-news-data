# Sadness Journaling Prompts: 10 Reflections to Help You Heal

Sadness is not a flaw — it’s part of being human. It can arrive slowly or hit hard, but either way, it’s asking you to pause and listen. These 10 journaling prompts, created by[Jon Filitti](https://www.getstoic.com/authors/jon-filitti) (Licensed Mental Health Counselor), are designed to help you honor that feeling, explore what it’s trying to teach you, and gently guide yourself toward healing.


These prompts are also included in the Stoic app’s Emotion Check-In — a space to process your emotions in real time, with clarity and compassion.
