# World ADHD Awareness Month in Stoic.

This October, explore stoic collection of **lessons and journals** designed to help you understand and embrace ADHD.


Inside Stoic, you’ll find guided reflections that unpack what ADHD really means, from symptoms and everyday challenges to tools for focus, perspective, and self-compassion.


## Collection includes:


- **What Is ADHD?** — an overview of symptoms and treatment
- **FAST MINDS** — daily challenges and how to manage them
- **The Big Three** — hyperactivity, impulsiveness, and attention
- **Gaining Perspective** — understanding and supporting others
- **Do I Have It?** — a short self-assessment to start exploring


You can find all lessons and related reflections in the **Library → Collections -> On ADHD** section (or you can simply follow[this link](https://getstoic.com/journaling?collection=on-adhd) )


Take a few minutes each day to learn, reflect, and write — and use this month to reconnect with how your mind really works.

## How to Explore the ADHD Awareness Collection


1. **Update Stoic** to the latest version.

3. Tap **On ADHD** to open the collection.
4. Choose a lesson — or start a reflection tied to each topic.
5. Learn and write freely, everything is private and secure.
