# Stoic’s Foundation Model AI Features: Personalized Reflections, Smarter Prompts, and Adaptive Insights

With iOS 26, Stoic introduces **Foundation Model AI** — a new wave of intelligent, on-device features that make journaling more personal, reflective, and private than ever.


All of these features run **entirely on your device** , meaning your reflections never leave your iPhone or iPad.


Here’s how Foundation Models are transforming Stoic.


‍


## 1. Personalized Uplifting Messages & Gratifications


Stoic now responds to your mood and sleep reflections with tailored, compassionate messages.


When you log a **low mood** or **poor sleep** , Stoic offers gentle encouragement to help you reframe the day with kindness.


‍


‍


‍


> “Remember, it’s totally okay to have tough days. You’re doing the best you can, and that’s something to be proud of.”


We’re also testing a version that includes your **first name** , for an even more personal touch:


> “Remember, Maciej, you’ve overcome difficult days before — you can do it again.”


When you log a **positive mood** or **great sleep** , Stoic celebrates your moment with short, affirming notes.


> “Your positive vibes are truly inspiring! Keep shining bright and embracing this incredible feeling.”


These uplifting moments are designed to appear **unpredictably** , so they stay rewarding instead of routine — a principle rooted in positive reinforcement research.


‍


## 2. AI-Generated Journaling Prompts Based on Quotes & Affirmations


Instead of simply reading daily quotes, you now get **reflective prompts** inspired by them.


For example, from the quote:


> “Enjoy when you can, and endure when you must.”


Stoic now adds:


> “When life feels good, do you let yourself enjoy it fully?”


Each day, your quote feed transforms into **interactive inspiration** , helping you connect Stoic philosophy with your own experience.


‍


‍


## **3. AI-Suggested Sentence Beginnings**


The blank page can be intimidating. Foundation Models help you start strong.


Every journaling prompt now includes **context-aware sentence starters** that make writing easier and more natural.


Example prompt:


> “Is there something small and simple in your life that means a lot to you?”


Suggested beginnings:


- “There’s something simple that…”
- “A small thing that I cherish is…”


These subtle cues make it easier to flow into your thoughts — especially on days when finding words feels hard.


‍


## **4. Personalized Journaling Prompts**


Your reflections now shape what Stoic asks next.


Foundation Models analyze your recent entries (privately, on-device) to generate **hyper-personal prompts** that build on your themes, progress, and struggles.


No two journeys are alike — and now, neither are your prompts.


‍


## **5. Context-Aware Notifications**


Stoic now sends **smart reminders** tied to your current mood or recent reflections.


Instead of generic nudges, you’ll see suggestions like:


> “You mentioned feeling stuck earlier this week. Want to unpack what changed since then?”


These reminders evolve with your journey, helping you reflect more meaningfully when it matters most.


‍


## 6. Smart History


Your journal history will now **group related entries** into meaningful chapters.


Instead of a plain list, Stoic uses Foundation Models to recognize patterns and topics — like “Work Challenges” or “Moments of Gratitude”.


You can see what you were reflecting on most — and how your mindset shifted over time.


‍


## 7. Smart Search Suggestions


Foundation Models also power **context-aware search** , suggesting relevant topics as you type.


For instance, if you’ve been journaling about “change” or “stress,” search will surface related entries, tags, and themes — helping you rediscover insights instantly.


‍


## 8. Personalized Greetings & Completion Messages


Every time you open Stoic, your **home screen** feels alive with dynamic greetings and closing messages that rotate daily.


They’re small touches — but they remind you that Stoic isn’t static. It’s a companion that evolves alongside you.


‍


‍


## Why It Matters


All of this intelligence runs **entirely on-device** using Apple’s **Foundation Models framework** , meaning:


- Your reflections never leave your phone.
- You get faster, contextual responses.
- Stoic adapts to your emotions in real-time — with privacy built in.


We believe this is the future of emotional technology: **deeply personal, yet fully private.**


‍


## How to Use Stoic’s Foundation Model Features


1. **Update Stoic to the latest version** on iOS 26 or iPadOS 26.
2. **Open the app** — you’ll notice new dynamic greetings on the home page.
3. **Start your Daily Check-In** — after selecting your mood, you might see an **uplifting message or gratitude reflection** .
4. **Browse your quotes or affirmations** — tap one to unlock an **AI-generated journaling prompt** that helps you reflect on it.
5. **Begin writing** — Stoic will suggest **sentence beginnings** that make journaling easier.
6. **Review your past entries** — under History, Foundation Models group your reflections into **Smart Search suggestions** .
7. **Stay consistent** — the more you write, the smarter and more relevant your insights become (still 100% on-device).
