# Do Solar Panels Increase the Value of My Home? | SunPower®

One consideration people have when making an investment in solar is whether or not solar panels increase the value of their home.


Many worry adding solar could affect the resale value of their home, even if it reduces utility bills. Adding solar is a home improvement project that changes how energy is used and keeps giving back to the homeowners, pre- and post-sale.


In addition to saving money on your monthly electric bill, adding solar to your house can increase its value.[According to a 2019 Zillow study](https://www.zillow.com/research/solar-panels-house-sell-more-23798/) , homes with solar sell for an average of 4.1% more than homes without solar.2 For example, on a $500,000 home that could mean an additional $20,500 of value from solar alone, increasing the value of your home. In fact, more than[80% of homebuyers](https://www.ecowatch.com/solar/solar-panels-increase-home-value) say energy-efficiency features (such as solar panels) are important to them. And when you take into account the possibility of added home value, plus any applicable incentives,1 the solar investment can quickly pay for itself.
