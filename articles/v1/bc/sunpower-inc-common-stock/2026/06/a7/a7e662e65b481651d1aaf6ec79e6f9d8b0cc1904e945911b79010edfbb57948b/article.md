# How to Choose Your Solar Panel Installer?

Photovoltaic solar panels are installed in more than 25 million homes around the world ([Source: International Energy Agency](https://www.iea.org/reports/approximately-100-million-households-rely-on-rooftop-solar-pv-by-2030) ). Each year, new installations are built across the country, driven by the desire to achieve long-term savings and adopt more environmentally friendly energy production.


Are you also considering switching to solar energy? Wondering who you can trust to carry out this type of project on your roof?


Choosing the right solar panel installer is the key step in any successful solar project. A good installer must be able to provide the expertise required not only for installing the panels but also for designing the system from A to Z, including connection to the grid. Whether you work with a local artisan or a large company, here are the essential criteria to make the right choice.


## Prepare your solar project by asking the right questions


Before contacting a solar installer, review your electricity bill. This is the starting point: your consumption data will allow your installer to correctly size your solar system. A properly calibrated self-consumption system prevents you from overpaying for an installation that produces more energy than your household can use.


Also consider your home’s location. Some roofs may not be suitable for a photovoltaic installation: they may be too shaded, poorly oriented, or too steep.[A professional installer](https://www.sunpowerglobal.com/uk/find-installer) will assess this potential during the first visit. Solar radiation also varies from one region to another in the UK: a local installer will design a system suited to your geographic location and seasonal trends. Properly oriented installations maximize power output and optimize system performance over its lifetime.


A solar energy system includes several components: photovoltaic panels, an inverter, and sometimes[a storage battery](https://www.sunpowerglobal.com/uk/homeowners/products/storage-battery) . Your installer will guide you through each element. Understanding the basics will help you compare offers and communicate confidently with professionals.


## Understand pricing and request more than one quote


The cost of a solar panel installation depends on the system’s capacity in kWp (kilowatt-peak), the type of photovoltaic panels, and the complexity of the work.


*High-efficiency SunPower Shingled Panels installed on a residential roof, delivering optimized performance, long-term reliability, and better value for your solar investment.*


According to UK government data in 2025 **:**


- A 3 kWp installation costs between €4,500 and €6,000


- A 6 kWp installation costs between €9,000 and €12,000


These prices include equipment, labour and standard components.


Never settle for the first quote. Compare several installers by checking:


- Total installation cost


- Company certifications


- Warranties


- Included services (grid connection, energy monitoring, after-sales service, building authorisations)


Many top installers offer free simulations in just a few minutes. This is the easiest way to estimate pricing, required panel numbers, and system capacity (kWp and efficiency), often with no obligation.


There are also plug-and-play solar kits that are easy to install yourself and more affordable. While they don’t replace a full photovoltaic installation, they are an accessible first step toward self-consumption and can cover a limited portion of your needs.


## Solar panels: How to assess quality?


Performance, efficiency, and warranties vary significantly between solar panel brands. Be cautious of cheap panels, which often have a much shorter lifespan.


A useful technical indicator is the **fill factor** , which measures the real efficiency of photovoltaic cells under everyday conditions. The higher the fill factor, the better the panel quality.
