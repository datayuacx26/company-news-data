# How to Clean Solar Panels: Best Practices for Maximum Energy Output

You’ve invested in solar panels to generate your own electricity? An excellent decision! But did you know that simple, regular maintenance can make a real difference to your energy production? Discover how to clean your photovoltaic panels easily and enjoy optimal performance all year round.


## Why Is It Important to Clean Your Solar Panels Regularly?


Your panels work for you every day, exposed to natural elements such as rain, wind, dust, pollen, and pollution. Over time, these particles build up on the surface, forming a thin layer of dirt that blocks sunlight. The result? Your system produces less energy than it should.


### The Tangible Impact on Your Energy Production


The numbers speak for themselves: without regular cleaning, the efficiency of your photovoltaic panels can drop by **5% to 25%** , depending on your environment and the type of soiling involved. By contrast, a well‑maintained solar installation can retain **up to 95% of its production capacity even after 20 years of operation** .


In practical terms, if your system normally produces **4,000 kWh per year** , dirty panels could cause an annual loss of **200 to 1,000 kWh** . ## What Causes Solar Panels to Get Dirty?


Solar panels are designed to be highly durable, but their surface is still exposed to various forms of soiling that reduce efficiency.


*SunPower solar panels installed on a residential rooftop, benefiting from regular cleaning to maintain optimal energy efficiency and long‑term performance.*


### The Invisible Enemies of Your Solar Production


- **Dust and pollen** : particularly abundant in spring, they form a film that reduces sunlight absorption
- **Fallen leaves** : in autumn, they accumulate and create shaded areas that negatively impact performance and can be harmful to panel health
- **Bird droppings** : stubborn and opaque, they locally block photovoltaic cells
- **Urban pollution** : in cities, fine particles leave a greyish layer on equipment
- **Salt and sand** : in coastal areas, sea air covers the surface with saline deposits


Even if your panels are equipped with a self‑cleaning coating, these elements inevitably accumulate over time, gradually reducing the efficiency of your system and your overall energy production.


## When Should You Clean Your Solar Panels?


Timing is essential to optimise performance while avoiding unnecessary effort.


### How Often Should You Clean Your System?


For most photovoltaic systems, **cleaning twice a year** is enough to maintain optimal production. However, certain environments require closer attention:


- **Near farmland or agricultural zones** : dust and pollen increase dirt build‑up (3–4 cleanings per year recommended)
- **Dense urban areas** : pollution may require more frequent cleaning
- **Coastal locations** : salt and sand demand regular maintenance
- **Flat roofs** : a tilt of less than 15° allows dirt to accumulate, as rain runoff is insufficient


For **large commercial & industrial (C&I) flat roofs** , this issue is even more pronounced due to larger surface areas and higher exposure to atmospheric pollutants. In these cases, **professional quarterly cleaning** is strongly recommended. C&I installations also require specialised equipment and enhanced safety protocols for working at height.

## What Is the Best Time of Day to Clean Solar Panels?


Never clean your panels in direct sunlight.


### Morning Is Your Best Ally


Clean your panels **early in the morning** , before sunrise if possible. At that time, the surface is still cool. During the day, panel temperatures can reach **80°C** , while cleaning water is much cooler - this thermal shock can damage photovoltaic cells and leave marks.


Additionally, midday sun causes water to evaporate too quickly, leaving mineral residues that reduce cleaning effectiveness and impact energy production.


## How to Clean Your Solar Panels Safely


Cleaning your panels is a simple task that requires minimal equipment to maintain peak performance.


*Clean SunPower solar panels installed on a sloped residential roof, demonstrating how regular maintenance helps maximise solar energy efficiency and long‑term performance.*


### Recommended Cleaning Equipment


#### Basic tools you’ll need:


- Lukewarm water (not cold, not hot - ideally room temperature)
- A soft cloth or non‑abrasive sponge
- A telescopic pole or soft brush (for roof‑mounted systems)
- Foam squeegee (optional, for stubborn stains)


**Professional tip:** use **de‑mineralised water** to avoid limescale stains. Tap water can leave a white residue that reduces cleaning efficiency.


### The 4‑Step Cleaning Method


1. Wet the surface with lukewarm water
2. Gently wipe using circular motions with a soft cloth
3. Rinse thoroughly with clean water
4. Allow to air‑dry or use a foam squeegee


Rinse your cloth every square metre to prevent spreading dirt back onto clean panels.


### What Should You Absolutely Avoid?


To protect your solar system and maintain performance, never:


- Use a **high‑pressure washer** (even low pressure can damage seals and cells)
- Use **hard brushes** or **scrapers**
- Walk on the panels
- Apply **chemical cleaners** or **detergents**
- Clean during **extreme heat**


For stubborn stains, be patient and gentle - lukewarm water and a foam squeegee only.

If your panels are easy to reach and you’re comfortable with basic DIY tasks, cleaning them yourself is an economical option.
