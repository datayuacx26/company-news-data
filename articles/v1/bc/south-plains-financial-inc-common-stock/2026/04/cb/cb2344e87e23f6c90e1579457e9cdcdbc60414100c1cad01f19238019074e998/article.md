# James D. Stein

James D. Stein


Director and Houston Market President – BOH


Mr. Stein joined the boards of directors of the Company and the Bank in April 2026 following the Company’s acquisition of BOH. Mr. Stein began his banking career in 1981. He formed BOH in 2017 and then acquired The Dublin National Bank. The bank headquarters were relocated to Houston while renaming the bank to Bank of Houston. Mr. Stein has previously served on the boards of other banks and bank holding companies. Additionally, Mr. Stein has served four terms on the Board of the Texas Bankers Association and many community, church and civic boards including the Houston Symphony and Chapelwood United Methodist Church. Mr. Stein attended the University of Texas in Austin, Texas.
