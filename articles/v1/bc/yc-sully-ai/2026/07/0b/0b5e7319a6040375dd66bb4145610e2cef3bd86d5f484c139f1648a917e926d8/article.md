## What Heidi Health Does and Why Clinicians Look for an Alternative


Heidi is one of the most popular self-serve scribes on the market, and for good reason. Being clear about what it does well makes the gaps easier to see.


### Where Heidi Is Strong


Heidi runs a real free tier and a fast self-serve signup, so a clinician can be drafting notes the same day. It has adaptive voice recognition, highly customizable templates, and a multi-product suite that adds citation-backed clinical answers and audio capture around the core scribe. It carries HIPAA, ISO 27001, and SOC 2, and users report saving around 1.5 hours a day \[2\]. For a solo physician who wants to stop typing notes at night, Heidi does the job well.


### Where Heidi Falls Short for Groups and Health Systems


The gaps show up the moment more than one provider is involved. Here is how one group practice put it on a call with our team:


> A roughly 16-provider group had already tried Heidi, Freed, and a few other scribes and was still shopping. What they were chasing was not a flashier note. It was value for the money across the whole practice and notes they could fully customize to their own workflow. A single-clinician scribe never quite added up for a group that size.


That lines up with three patterns the research keeps surfacing.


**Native EHR write-back is tier-gated.** How deeply the note writes into the chart depends on the plan and the EHR, with the deeper native integration reserved for higher tiers \[2\]. Sully writes natively across four major EHRs on one integration, with no per-tier upcharge for the connection.


**Coding is not the same as a submitted claim.** Heidi has added coding and CDI support, but it stops short of autonomously submitting a clean claim \[2\]. Turning the finished note into a paid claim still falls to your revenue-cycle staff.


**The pieces do not act as one workforce.** Heidi has expanded beyond the note into coding, patient communications, and referral triage, but they run as separate add-ons rather than agents that share context and hand off a case. For a group, someone still stitches the steps together between the note and the next visit.
