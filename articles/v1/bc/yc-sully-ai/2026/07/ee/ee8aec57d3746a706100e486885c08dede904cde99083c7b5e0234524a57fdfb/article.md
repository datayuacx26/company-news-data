## What Freed AI Does and Why Clinicians Look for an Alternative


Freed is the best-known simple scribe on the market, and it earned that spot. Being clear about what it does well makes the gaps easier to see.


### Where Freed Is Strong


Freed is fast to set up, has a clean, distraction-free interface, and produces strong primary-care notes in a minute or two. It starts at 39 dollars a month, offers a no-credit-card trial, and carries HIPAA and a BAA \[2\]. For a solo physician who just wants to stop typing notes at night, Freed is one of the easiest tools to pick up and keep using.


### Where Freed Falls Short for Groups and Health Systems


The gaps show up the moment a group tries to standardize on it. Here is how one medical director described it on a call with our team:


> A 30-plus-provider group was already running on Freed, and the director's read was blunt: everyone was basically fending for themselves with a solo tool. He said he would switch and roll the whole team onto something else the moment it could integrate more deeply and let clinicians customize their notes properly. The scribe was fine for one doctor. It was never built to run across a group.


Freed has added products since then, but three gaps still matter for a group.


**EHR write-back is a one-click push, not deep enterprise integration.** Freed pushes notes into browser-based EHRs with a one-click step \[1\]. Sully integrates natively across Epic, Cerner, Meditech, and Athenahealth on one connection, the depth a health system needs across its whole stack.


**Coding stops at suggestions.** Freed surfaces ICD-10, CPT, and E&M suggestions inside the note \[1\], but applying them and submitting the claim still falls to your staff. Sully's Coder submits the clean claim end to end.


**The add-ons are separate products, not one workforce.** Freed now sells a Front Desk receptionist and clinical decision support, but each is a separate tool bought and run on its own \[1\]. On one platform, Sully's agents share context so the note, the code, the claim, and the follow-up move as one.
