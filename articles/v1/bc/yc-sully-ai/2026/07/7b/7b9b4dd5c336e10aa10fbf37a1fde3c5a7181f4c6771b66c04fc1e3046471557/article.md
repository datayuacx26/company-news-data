# How to Add an AI Scribe to Office Practicum in 2026

You run a pediatric practice on Office Practicum, the pediatric-only EHR built around well-child care, immunizations, and growth tracking, and documentation is pulling you away from a room where a parent and a child are both talking. Physicians spend close to two hours on the EHR and deskwork for every hour of direct patient care \[1\], and log about 86 minutes of nightly "pajama time" finishing charts \[2\]. Pediatric visits add their own twist: the note has to follow a three-way conversation and slot into well-child and vaccine structure.


Here is the fact worth knowing up front: Office Practicum has its own pediatric AI scribe, OPIE AI, so the real decision is no longer whether to add a scribe. It is whether the native scribe covers everything you need, or whether you also want the parent-facing work around the visit handled. This guide covers how a scribe connects to Office Practicum, a step by step rollout, and the options worth shortlisting.

## What an AI Scribe Adds to the Office Practicum Workflow


An[AI scribe](https://www.sully.ai/medical-scribe) listens to the visit and turns the conversation into a structured note. The pediatrician stays present with the family. The scribe drafts the note in seconds, and the clinician reviews it instead of typing it.


Pediatric documentation is harder than it looks, because the scribe has to follow a parent, a child, and a clinician all talking, then organize the result into well-child and immunization structure. The numbers below are the burden a scribe is meant to remove.


The difference on Office Practicum is that a native pediatric scribe already exists. So the decision shifts from "do we add a scribe" to "does the native scribe cover everything, or do we also want the parent-facing work around the visit handled."


### Ambient Documentation Versus Manual Charting


The old flow is typing well-child and sick-visit notes in Office Practicum during or after the visit. Either way the clinician is still doing data entry.


The ambient flow is listen, draft, review, sign, and ambient scribes have cut documentation time in multi-site studies \[7\]. The pediatric nuance that matters is multi-speaker capture: the transcript has to attribute parent history, child statements, and clinician observations correctly \[4\]. And the question that decides real value is what happens after the note, across recall, scheduling, triage, and claims.


### Office Practicum Has a Native Scribe, So the Question Is What Comes Next


Office Practicum offers OPIE AI, a native pediatric ambient scribe built into the workflow, alongside its 200-plus pediatric templates, AAP Bright Futures questionnaires, and VacLogic+ immunization forecasting \[3\].


It documents pediatric visits, and it does that with real pediatric tuning. So the decision is not native versus nothing. It is whether documentation alone is enough, or whether you also want the parent-facing work, recall, reminders, triage, and claims, handled. That framing drives the rest of this guide.

### The Native Option, OPIE AI


OPIE AI is Office Practicum's native pediatric ambient scribe, tuned for pediatric visits and living inside the workflow with the pediatric templates and vaccine forecasting \[3\].


This is a strong fit for practices that want an in-workflow pediatric note with nothing to integrate. The honest limit is scope: it documents, and it is not a receptionist, a nurse line, or a coder that submits claims.

Freed, Commure Scribe, and Sunoh.ai are cross-EHR ambient scribes, and some handle multi-speaker pediatric encounters well \[4\]\[5\]\[6\].

## How to Add an AI Scribe to Office Practicum Step by Step


Five steps take you from decision to a validated note in the chart.


### 1. Confirm Your Office Practicum Setup and Goals


Confirm whether you have enabled OPIE AI, and identify the clinical and practice-manager owners. Decide what you want: an in-workflow pediatric note, which OPIE AI may already cover, or the parent-facing work around the visit, recall, reminders, triage, and claims, which points to a workforce platform.


That goal determines the route.


### 2. Choose Your Integration Method


Map the methods to that goal. OPIE AI gives you an in-workflow pediatric note. A cross-EHR scribe documents alongside Office Practicum. A workforce platform handles documentation, claims, recall, and triage through one integration.


The decision rule: if you want an in-workflow pediatric note, OPIE AI gets you there. If you want the parent-facing work handled, recall, vaccine reminders, the nurse line, and clean claims, you need a workforce platform.


### 3. Connect the Scribe


For the native option, enable OPIE AI in Office Practicum \[3\]. For a cross-EHR or workforce tool, have the vendor connect to your Office Practicum environment.


Whichever route you pick, two things are non-negotiable before any visit audio is recorded: a signed BAA and HIPAA-compliant data handling, plus parental consent for recording a child's visit, reviewed with your compliance team.


### 4. Map Notes and Coding to Office Practicum


Configure how notes land in the right Office Practicum fields, including well-child and immunization structure, and how codes attach to each problem. A scribe drafts the note; a workforce platform also[writes coded, claim-ready data](https://www.sully.ai/help-center/articles/how-does-sully-ai-s-two-way-integration-with-ehrs-work) .


Note that CVX codes, lot numbers, and registry export remain EHR functions \[4\], so verify the handoff against your real well-child and sick-visit templates, not a demo.


### 5. Pilot on Well-Child and Sick Visits, Measure, and Scale


Pilot across both well-child and sick visits, since they document very differently. The hardest visit is the one that reveals gaps.


Confirm the note lands correctly and attributes speakers, measure the[time saved](https://www.sully.ai/help-center/articles/what-is-the-roi-of-an-ai-medical-scribe) \[7\], and measure the work after the note: is recall handled, are claims cleaner. Validate consent and governance before a practice-wide rollout.


## What to Look For in an Office Practicum AI Scribe


Four criteria separate the options on the shortlist.


### Pediatric Documentation Fit


Multi-speaker capture, well-child structure, and immunization context are specific to pediatrics. OPIE AI is native and pediatric-tuned \[3\], and any third-party tool has to prove it handles a three-way visit and your well-child templates \[4\].


Test on a real well-child visit, not an adult demo.


### The Parent-Facing Work Around the Visit


Pediatric practices live on the phone: well-child recall, vaccine reminders, sick-call triage, and scheduling. A scribe documents; it does not make those calls.


This is the clearest gap between a scribe and a workforce. Sully adds an AI Receptionist and an AI Triage Nurse for exactly this work, plus an[AI Coder](https://www.sully.ai/agents/medical-coder) that submits clean claims.


### Coding and Claims on High-Volume Visits


Pediatric visits are high-volume and often low-margin, so clean claims matter. A scribe drafts the note, and some tools suggest codes, but submitting the claim is a separate job.


Sully's AI Coder extracts every ICD-10 and CPT code and submits clean claims through one integration, so the visit turns into a submitted claim rather than a note somebody bills later.


### Security, Consent, and HIPAA Compliance


The non-negotiables:[HIPAA compliance](https://www.sully.ai/help-center/articles/are-ai-scribes-hipaa-compliant) , a signed BAA,[encryption and de-identified PHI handling](https://www.sully.ai/trust) , MFA, SSO, role-based access, and clear retention controls.


Pediatrics adds one more: parental consent for recording a minor's visit, and heightened sensitivity for children's records. Ask where audio is processed, how long it is retained, and who can access transcripts before the pilot starts.
