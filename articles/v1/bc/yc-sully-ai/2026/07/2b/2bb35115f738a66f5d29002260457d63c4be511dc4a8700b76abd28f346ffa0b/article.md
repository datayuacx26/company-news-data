## What Suki AI Does and Why Teams Look for an Alternative


Suki is the toughest ambient tool to beat at the point of care, so it is worth being precise about what it does before talking about where it stops.


### Where Suki Is Strong


Suki has deep, real-time integration with the four major EHRs, Epic, Oracle Health, Athenahealth, and Meditech, and calls itself the most embedded ambient AI for a reason \[1\]. It documents across 100+ specialties, takes voice commands, answers questions about the chart, stages orders, and suggests billing codes \[1\]\[2\]. The voice-command workflow sets it apart from a plain scribe. As a point-of-care assistant for a physician, it is arguably the best there is.


### Where Suki Falls Short for Health Systems


The gaps are not about note quality. They are about everything around the note.


**It does not run the front desk.** By its own description, Suki does not handle patient communication, call answering, appointment scheduling, or triage \[2\]. Those stay with your staff.


**Its coding is assistive, not autonomous.** Suki suggests ICD-10, HCC, CPT, and E&M codes for the clinician to review and apply \[2\]. It does not submit or validate the claim, so the revenue-cycle work still lands on people.


**It is a single assistant, not a workforce.** Suki is a single-agent clinical assistant focused on documentation and voice commands, not a multi-agent platform that automates the full practice workflow \[2\]. It is also among the most expensive options, roughly 299 to 399 dollars per provider per month and higher on enterprise contracts \[2\].


## How to Evaluate a Suki AI Alternative


Because Suki matches the field on documentation and integration, the decision should turn on scope and autonomy, not on whose note is a shade better.


### Assistant for the Clinician or Workforce for the Practice


An assistant makes one clinician faster. A workforce removes work across the whole practice: the front desk, intake, documentation, coding, claims, and follow-up. That is the fork in the road, and it matters more than any single feature.


### Assisted Coding Versus a Submitted Claim


Assistive code suggestions still leave a person to apply them and submit the claim. Autonomous claim submission is a different outcome for the revenue cycle \[2\]. Ask which one you are actually buying.


### Total Cost Across the Practice


Suki is premium-priced per provider \[2\]. For a practice, the honest comparison is not one scribe seat against another. It is the cost of the whole administrative workload, which Sully prices at[80 to 90 percent below the human equivalent](https://www.sully.ai/help-center/articles/what-is-the-roi-of-an-ai-medical-scribe) per role.
