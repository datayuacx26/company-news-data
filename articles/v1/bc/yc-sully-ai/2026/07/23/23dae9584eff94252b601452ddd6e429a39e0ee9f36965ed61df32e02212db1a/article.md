# How to Add an AI Scribe to Netsmart EHR in 2026

You run a behavioral-health, human-services, or post-acute organization on Netsmart, whether that is myAvatar in a community mental health center, myEvolv in human services, or the broader CareFabric platform. Documentation here is uniquely heavy: long narrative notes, treatment plans, and compliance language, consuming up to 40% of clinician time in behavioral health, nearly two full days a week \[3\]. Across medicine, physicians spend close to two hours on the EHR and deskwork for every hour of direct patient care \[1\], and log about 86 minutes of nightly "pajama time" finishing charts \[2\].


Here is the fact worth knowing up front: Netsmart has a native ambient scribe, Bells Virtual Scribe, so the real decision is no longer whether to add a scribe. It is whether to stop at the note or also automate the work that happens after it. This guide covers how a scribe connects to Netsmart, a step by step rollout, and the options worth shortlisting.


## Key Takeaway


Netsmart has a native ambient AI scribe, Bells Virtual Scribe, part of the CareFabric platform, which documents the session inside the Netsmart workflow. That covers the note, but not the work after it. Behavioral-health documentation specialists like Eleos also stop at the note. A workforce platform goes further.[Sully.ai](https://www.sully.ai/products) integrates with Netsmart and adds the[AI Coder](https://www.sully.ai/agents/medical-coder) that submits clean claims, plus reception and triage, across Netsmart and more than 20 other EHRs.


## What an AI Scribe Adds to the Netsmart Workflow


An[AI scribe](https://www.sully.ai/medical-scribe) listens to the session and turns the conversation into a structured note. The clinician stays present with the client. The scribe drafts the note in seconds, and the clinician reviews it instead of typing it.


Behavioral-health and human-services teams feel the charting load more than almost anyone, and it is a known driver of clinician turnover. The numbers below are the burden a scribe is meant to remove.


The difference on Netsmart is that a native scribe already exists. So the decision shifts from "do we add a scribe" to "do we stop at the note, or automate the work that comes after it."


### Ambient Documentation Versus Manual Charting


The old flow is typing long narratives into myAvatar or myEvolv after each session, often at the end of the day. Either way the clinician is still doing data entry.


The ambient flow is listen, draft, review, sign. Behavioral-health sessions are conversational and often involve more than two people, which is exactly what ambient capture is built for. The question that decides real value is what happens to that note after it is written, because the note feeds coding, billing, scheduling, and follow-up.


### Netsmart Has a Native Scribe, So the Question Is What Comes Next


Netsmart offers Bells Virtual Scribe, a native ambient documentation assistant in the CareFabric platform. It transcribes and summarizes the session in near real time, tags ICD-10 and SNOMED terminology, maps generated sentences back to the transcript for transparency, and supports multi-participant sessions, which matters for group work \[4\]\[5\]. Bells also includes an AI quality coach for documentation.


It documents, and it does that with real behavioral-health tuning. So the decision is not native versus nothing. It is whether documentation alone is enough, or whether you also want coding-to-claim, scheduling, and intake handled. That framing drives the rest of this guide.

### The Native Option, Bells Virtual Scribe


The zero-friction path for documentation inside the Netsmart stack. Bells documents the session in the native workflow and coaches documentation quality \[4\]\[5\].


This is a strong fit for Netsmart shops that want an ambient note and documentation-quality coaching and nothing more. The honest limit is scope: it is documentation and quality tooling. It does not submit claims, schedule the follow-up, or run intake.


### Behavioral-Health Specialists and Cross-EHR Scribes


Eleos is a behavioral-health AI documentation specialist, purpose-built for therapy and community-behavioral-health workflows \[6\]. Twofold and Freed are cross-EHR scribes popular with therapy practices and lean teams \[7\]\[8\].


These document well, and Eleos adds behavioral-specific session intelligence. The tradeoff is the same one Bells has: they are documentation tools, and they stop at the note.


### Workforce Platforms


The broadest tier. A workforce platform integrates with Netsmart and adds agents across the session and everything around it.


Sully.ai[integrates with Netsmart](https://www.sully.ai/integrations) once, and then its agents share that connection: the AI Scribe documents, the AI Coder submits the claim, the AI Receptionist handles scheduling, and the[AI Triage Nurse](https://www.sully.ai/agents/triage-nurse) runs intake and follow-up. This is the option that goes past the note, and it also covers any systems your organization runs beyond the Netsmart stack.


## How to Add an AI Scribe to Netsmart Step by Step


Five steps take you from decision to a validated note in the chart.


### 1. Confirm Your Netsmart Footprint and Goals


Confirm what you run, myAvatar, myEvolv, and which CareFabric modules, and identify the clinical-informatics owner. Decide what you want: an ambient note inside the Netsmart workflow, where Bells is the quick path, or the work after the note, coding into claims, scheduling, and intake, which points to a workforce platform.


That goal determines the route.


### 2. Choose Your Integration Method


Map the methods to that goal. Bells is the fastest in-workflow note plus quality coaching. A behavioral specialist or cross-EHR scribe documents, sometimes with behavioral-specific intelligence. A workforce platform handles claims, reception, and triage through one integration.


The decision rule: if you want an ambient note inside the Netsmart workflow, Bells gets you there. If you want the work after the session handled, the claim submitted plus scheduling and intake, across every system you run, you need a workforce platform.


### 3. Connect the Scribe


For the native option, enable Bells in your CareFabric environment \[4\]. For a specialist or workforce tool, have the vendor integrate with your Netsmart environment.


Whichever route you pick, two things are non-negotiable before any session audio is recorded: a signed BAA and HIPAA-compliant data handling. In behavioral health, add consent workflows for recording therapy sessions and the heightened confidentiality rules that apply to substance-use records, reviewed with your compliance team.


### 4. Map Notes and Coding to Netsmart


Configure how each note section maps to the correct myAvatar or myEvolv fields, how treatment-plan links attach, and how codes attach to each problem instead of landing as one block. A scribe focuses on the note; a workforce platform[writes coded, claim-ready data](https://www.sully.ai/help-center/articles/how-does-sully-ai-s-two-way-integration-with-ehrs-work) .


Verify it against the note types you actually run, individual, group, and case management, not a demo.


### 5. Pilot, Measure, and Scale


Pilot on a high-volume program. The heaviest documentation caseload is the one that reveals real value.


Confirm the note lands in the right Netsmart sections, measure the[time saved](https://www.sully.ai/help-center/articles/what-is-the-roi-of-an-ai-medical-scribe) , and measure the work after the note: are claims cleaner, is follow-up handled. Validate consent workflows and governance before an agency-wide rollout.


## What to Look For in a Netsmart AI Scribe


Four criteria separate the options on the shortlist.


### Behavioral-Health Documentation Fit


Long narratives, group sessions, treatment plans, and compliance language are different from a primary-care SOAP note. Bells and Eleos are tuned for this \[4\]\[6\], and any tool you pick must handle your real note types.


Test with a group session and a treatment-plan update, not just an individual visit.


### Depth of Work After the Note


Does the tool turn the session into linked ICD-10 and CPT codes and a clean claim, and handle scheduling and follow-up, or just document? Bells documents and coaches quality. Eleos documents with behavioral intelligence. Workforce platforms go further.


This is where the real value sits for an agency running its own billing. Sully pairs its[AI Scribe](https://www.sully.ai/agents/scribe) with an AI Coder that extracts every ICD-10 and CPT code and submits clean claims, plus front-desk and triage agents.


### Coverage Beyond the Netsmart Stack


Many organizations run more than one system across programs and service lines. A native scribe covers only Netsmart. A workforce platform that integrates with Netsmart and more than 20 other EHRs covers the whole organization through one approach.


Weigh coverage against your real footprint, not just the system in front of you today.


### Security, Consent, and HIPAA Compliance


The non-negotiables:[HIPAA compliance](https://www.sully.ai/help-center/articles/are-ai-scribes-hipaa-compliant) , a signed BAA,[encryption and de-identified PHI handling](https://www.sully.ai/trust) , MFA, SSO, role-based access, and clear retention controls.


Behavioral health adds two more: consent workflows for recording sessions, and the heightened confidentiality expectations around substance-use treatment records. Ask where audio is processed, how long it is retained, and who can access transcripts before the pilot starts.
