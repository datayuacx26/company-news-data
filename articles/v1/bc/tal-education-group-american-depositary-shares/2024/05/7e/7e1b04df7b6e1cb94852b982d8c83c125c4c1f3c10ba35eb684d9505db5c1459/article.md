# TAL Education Group Files Its Annual Report on Form 20-F

BEIJING


, May 31, 2024


/PRNewswire/ -- TAL Education Group ("TAL" or the "Company") (NYSE:TAL ), a smart learning solutions provider in China


, today announced that it filed its annual report on Form 20-F for the fiscal year ended February 29, 2024 (the "Annual Report") with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the "SEC") on May 31, 2024, U.S. Eastern Time.


The Annual Report can be accessed and downloaded on the Company's website at[https://ir.100tal.com/](https://ir.100tal.com/) , as well as on the SEC's website at[http://www.sec.gov](http://www.sec.gov/) .

**About TAL Education Group**


TAL Education Group is a smart learning solutions provider in China


. The acronym "TAL" stands for "Tomorrow Advancing Life", which reflects our vision to promote top learning opportunities for students through both high-quality teaching and content, as well as leading edge application of technology in the education experience. TAL Education Group offers comprehensive learning services to students from all ages through diversified class formats. Our learning services mainly cover enrichment learnings programs and some academic subjects in and out of China


. Our ADSs trade on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol "TAL".
