# Sony Pictures’ Spider-Man: Brand New Day to Expand in IMAX® Across North America Beginning August 6

***Following a Super $23 Million IMAX Opening across China, Japan, and South Korea,*** ***Spider-Man* *Swings into IMAX Locations Across North America***


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 3, 2026-- IMAX® Corporation (NYSE: IMAX) today announced that Sony Pictures’ *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* will expand across the IMAX network into North America beginning Thursday, August 6. The move follows a strong IMAX opening for the film in China, Japan and South Korea, where it generated $23 million at the IMAX box office.


- In China, the film earned $19 million in IMAX, delivering the biggest IMAX opening weekend in *Spider-Man* franchise history, while accounting for a franchise-best 16% of the film's opening weekend box office.
- In South Korea, the film set a new record for the biggest IMAX opening weekend ever in the market, earning $2.4 million and accounting for 9% of the film's opening weekend box office.
- Despite playing on a shared IMAX schedule in Japan, the film earned $2.2 million, with IMAX accounting for an impressive 18% of the film's opening weekend box office.


“Spider-Man has been among the most bankable superheroes at the global box office for over two decades — with the franchise delivering $365 million in IMAX receipts to date — and *Brand New Day* is taking that legacy to new heights with its record-breaking opening weekend,” said Rich Gelfond, CEO of IMAX. “We believe more Spidey fans around the world will want to experience his latest adventure in IMAX — and we hope to be a major driver of both new and repeat viewing for the latest hit in this incredible year for moviegoing.”


*Spider-Man: Brand New Day* will share the IMAX global network with Christopher Nolan's *The Odyssey* across select digital IMAX locations in North America, while *The Odyssey* continues to play across all 41 IMAX 70mm Film locations worldwide. With more than $221 million in IMAX global box office to date, *The Odyssey* will continue its IMAX rollout over the coming weeks with debuts in South Korea (August 5), China (August 14), and Japan (September 11).
