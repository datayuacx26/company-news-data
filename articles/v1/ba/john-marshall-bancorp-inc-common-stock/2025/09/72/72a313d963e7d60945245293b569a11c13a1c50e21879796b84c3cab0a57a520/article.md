**John Marshall Bank Hires Charles Kapur as SVP, Director of Deposit Services to Develop and Execute Growth Strategy**


Reston, VA —[John Marshall Bank](https://www.johnmarshallbank.com/) (“JMB” or the “Bank”), subsidiary of[John Marshall Bancorp, Inc.](https://investor.johnmarshallbank.com/corporate-profile/default.aspx) (the “Company”) (Nasdaq: JMSB), is proud to announce the hiring of Charles Kapur as Senior Vice President, Director of Deposit Services. In his role, Mr. Kapur will develop and implement both long and short-term strategies to increase core deposits and market share. He will also work in a business development role to establish new commercial relationships while supporting the financial needs of existing clients. The Director of Deposit Services position has been created to accelerate the Bank’s growth while continuing to provide exceptional financial and customer service to its clients.


An established figure in the community, Mr. Kapur most recently served as the President and CEO of the Greater Reston Chamber of Commerce for almost 6 years. His commitment to the local business community is second-to-none as he is dedicated to the success of the businesses he serves and the people who operate them. His position as a community leader reflects the Bank’s position as a community bank with a team that leads, lives, and supports the same communities they work in.


Mr. Kapur also has a strong and established career in banking, his most recent banking role having been with the Bank as VP, Treasury Services Sales Manager prior to joining the Reston Chamber. His over 18 years of banking experience and his history with John Marshall Bank places him in a prime position to foster growth for the Bank and its clients. He will use his banking history in tandem with the skills he developed as the CEO of the Reston Chamber to facilitate the Bank’s forward-thinking strategies.


“We’re excited to welcome Charles back to the JMB team in a newly created role he is well-equipped to thrive,” stated Andrew J. Peden, SEVP, Chief Banking Officer. “His extensive community involvement, proven leadership capabilities, and strong banking background exemplifies JMB’s commitment to hiring market leaders who value the success of our clients, the community, and the Bank.”
