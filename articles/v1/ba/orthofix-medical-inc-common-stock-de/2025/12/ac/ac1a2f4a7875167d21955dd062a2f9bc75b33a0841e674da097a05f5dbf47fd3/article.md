# Orthofix Academy and ABS Websites Go Live with a New Look: Education Across Specialties and Anatomical-Based Solutions

We are proud to announce that two of our primary digital platforms are now live with a completely refreshed design, offering healthcare professionals an enhanced experience and easier access to resources.


## OrthofixAcademy.com – Your Hub for Continuous Learning Across Specialties


[Orthofix Academy](https://www.orthofixacademy.com/) is designed to support healthcare professionals in multiple areas of orthopedic care. The platform offers:


- Comprehensive educational resources across various treatment domains
- Interactive courses, webinars, and case-based learning led by experts
- Personalized content recommendations based on your profile and interests


Whether your focus is spine, limb reconstruction, or other orthopedic specialties, Orthofix Academy provides the tools and knowledge to help you deliver the best possible patient outcomes.


## abs.orthofix.it – Anatomical-Based Solutions for Limb Reconstruction


[ABS](https://abs.orthofix.it/) stands for Anatomical-Based Solutions, and this platform is designed to guide you through Orthofix’s complete offering for limb reconstruction. The navigation process is intuitive and clinically oriented:


- Select the patient type (adult or pediatric)
- Choose the anatomical site requiring treatment
- Define the clinical condition or treatment type
- Explore Orthofix solutions, including external and internal fixation systems


The site also provides in-depth content on key topics around the four Limb Reconstruction pillars: Limb Lengthening, Limb Preservation, Complex Fracture Management, and Extremity Deformity Correction.


ABS is your go-to resource for understanding all our available limb reconstruction treatment options and how they can be applied to meet your patients’ needs.
