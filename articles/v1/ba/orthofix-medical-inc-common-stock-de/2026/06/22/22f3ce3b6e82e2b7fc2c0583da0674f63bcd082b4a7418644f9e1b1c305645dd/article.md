# From Nerve Pain to Full Recovery: Jason’s CervicalStim Device Experience

> “I’m back to my normal life – and I’m so grateful.”
>
>
> Jason, CervicalStim™ device patient


[Jason](https://orthofix.com/patients/patient-stories/jason/) developed progressive neck and shoulder pain that led to nerve compression and loss of hand function. Imaging revealed severe cervical disc herniation at a challenging spinal level, and cervical fusion surgery was recommended.


To support healing after surgery, Jason used the[CervicalStim](https://orthofix.com/solutions/therapeutic-solutions/bone-growth-therapies/spinal-fusion-therapy/cervicalstim-spinal-fusion-therapy/) ™ device, the only FDA‑approved device for cervical fusion. With post‑operative therapy and bone healing support, his neurologic function steadily improved.


Today, Jason has returned to work, daily activities, and an active lifestyle with restored function.
