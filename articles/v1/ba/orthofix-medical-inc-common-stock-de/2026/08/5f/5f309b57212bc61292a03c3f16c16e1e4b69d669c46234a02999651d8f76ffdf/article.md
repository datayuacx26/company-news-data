# Carolyn’s Experience with Spine Surgery and Recovery

After living with progressive scoliosis and undergoing two spine surgeries, Carolyn used the SpinalStim bone growth therapy device as part of her prescribed recovery. Hear how she approached healing and returned to the activities she enjoys.


> “Be a partner in your recovery. Ask questions. Do everything you can to give yourself the best chance to heal, physically and mentally.”
>
>
> Carolyn, SpinalStim device patient


Carolyn shares her experience with spine surgery and using SpinalStim as part of her prescribed recovery.


Carolyn Griesemer has a long history of scoliosis that progressed over time and eventually led to significant pain and functional limitations. After exploring several treatment options, she worked with orthopedic spine surgeon Greg Mundis, MD, an orthopedic spine surgeon, to pursue a surgical approach that addressed her symptoms while preserving mobility.


Carolyn underwent two spine surgeries nearly a decade apart. Following both procedures, her physician recommended bone growth therapy using the[SpinalStim device](https://orthofix.com/solutions/therapeutic-solutions/bone-growth-therapies/spinal-fusion-therapy/spinalstim-spinal-fusion-therapy/) to support spinal fusion during recovery. The device became part of her prescribed postoperative routine.


Over time, imaging confirmed successful fusion after each surgery. Carolyn was able to return to daily activities, travel, and physical exercise, reporting relief from the pain that had previously limited her quality of life.
