# How Surgeons Achieve Comprehensive Spinal Fusion with Facet Fixation

## Cervical Spine: NorthStar Facet Fusion


[NorthStar™ Facet Fusion](https://orthofix.com/solutions/biologic-solutions/orthofixbiologics/northstar-facet-fusion/) was designed to support two critical objectives of posterior cervical fusion: lateral mass fixation and robust fusion. Demonstrated in the all-in-one Facet Prep Instruments that simultaneously guide lateral mass screw trajectory, facilitate facet joint decortication, and integrate biologic delivery.


Together, Facet Prep Instruments and[OsteoStrand™ Cervical Pellets](https://orthofix.com/solutions/biologic-solutions/orthofixbiologics/osteostrand/) enable surgeons to reproducibly **locate, decorticate, and fuse** in the cervical spine.


### Why do surgeons choose
NorthStar Facet Fusion?


- A complete cervical fusion kit
- 2x more fusion surface area compared to standard methods1
- Reproducible lateral mass screw pathway prep2
- Zero-prep, integrated delivery of precision-shaped biologics
- [OsteoStrand™ fibers](https://orthofix.com/solutions/biologic-solutions/orthofixbiologics/osteostrand/) with demonstrated fusion results3,4


### Locate


**Improved screw trajectory**


**Reduced rates of screw breach**


Sagittal screw angle measured relative to inferior facet joint


Freehand


Facet Fusion


### Decorticate


**NorthStar Facet Fusion**


**Standard of Care**


Efficient, repeatable method maximizes the decorticated surface area inside the facet joint


Burring can be inconsistent and limited to the facet joint line


### Fuse

## Lumbar Spine: FLASH Facet Fusion


Navigated MIS procedures for lumbar spinal fusion have grown as navigation systems become more readily available. Fusion in MIS presents unique challenges to surgeons due to limited visibility and access to the posterior elements.


[FLASH™ Facet Fusion](https://orthofix.com/solutions/biologic-solutions/orthofixbiologics/flash-facet-fusion/) is a navigation-compatible system that accesses the facet joints and acts as a working channel for the entire facet fusion procedure. FLASH Facet Fusion Instruments and FLASH Facet Pellets offer surgeons a technique to reproducibly **locate, decorticate, and fuse** in minimally invasive lumbar procedures.


### Why do surgeons choose FLASH Facet Fusion?


- Navigation-compatible instruments for minimally invasive lumbar spine surgery
- 3x more fusion surface area compared to standard methods1
- Zero-prep, integrated delivery of precision-shaped biologic
- [OsteoStrand™ Plus fibers](https://orthofix.com/solutions/biologic-solutions/orthofixbiologics/osteostrand-plus/) with patented[Accell™ Technology](https://orthofix.com/solutions/spine/accell/) for both immediate and sustained release of growth factors4


### Locate


Seamless integration with[7D FLASH Navigation](https://orthofix.com/solutions/surgical-navigation/7d-flash-navigation-system/)


Real-time tool projection provides a geometric representation of the Lumbar Inserter to help target the facet joints


### Decorticate


The navigated Lumbar Inserter guides the Facet Drill during decortication


- Inserter forks center cannula on the joint line
- Enables equal decortication of superior and inferior facets


- Drill creates a contained defect within the inserter forks
- Inserter designed to control drill depth


### Fuse


FLASH Facet Pellets contain[Accell Technology](https://orthofix.com/solutions/spine/accell/) for immediate and sustained release of growth factors4


Post-op CT shows centered decortication


Fusion identified in the facet joint by CT at 10 months
