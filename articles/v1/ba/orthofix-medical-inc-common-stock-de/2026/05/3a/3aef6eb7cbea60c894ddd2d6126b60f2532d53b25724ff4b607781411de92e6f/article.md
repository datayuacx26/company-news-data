# Orthofix Therapeutic Solutions: Redefining Recovery

At Orthofix, recovery has always been more than a single moment in time. It is a journey—one that patients and providers walk together from diagnosis through healing and beyond. That belief continues to shape everything we do, guiding the evolution of our business and the way we support patients across the full continuum of care.


As our organization has grown,[Therapeutic Solutions](https://orthofix.com/solutions/therapeutic-solutions/) has evolved to unite our noninvasive[bone growth therapies](https://orthofix.com/solutions/therapeutic-solutions/bone-growth-therapies/) , advanced[bracing solutions](https://orthofix.com/solutions/therapeutic-solutions/bracing-solutions/) , and patient-focused services under one streamlined business unit—creating a more connected, simpler recovery experience for everyone involved.


We are redefining recovery by making it more consistent, more supported, and more effective.


Therapeutic Solutions is dedicated to advancing musculoskeletal health through innovative, noninvasive therapies that help patients heal faster and recover better. Our solutions are designed to:


- Promote bone healing
- Restore mobility
- Improve overall quality of life


By delivering clinically proven technologies, we help accelerate recovery timelines, reduce the need for invasive surgeries, and support better outcomes for patients and providers alike.


## A Comprehensive, Connected Approach to Recovery


Our Therapeutic Solutions portfolio brings together the technologies, services, and expertise needed to support healing at every stage.


### [Bone Growth Therapies](https://orthofix.com/solutions/therapeutic-solutions/bone-growth-therapies/)


Fusion and fracture healing depend on consistency and biology. That’s why we are redefining recovery with proven[PEMF](https://bonegrowththerapy.com/for-physicians/therapies-and-how-it-works/stim-pemf/) and[LIPUS](https://bonegrowththerapy.com/for-physicians/therapies-and-how-it-works/how-lipus-works/) therapies—clinically validated, noninvasive solutions designed to support bone healing when it matters most.


### [Advanced Bracing Solutions](https://orthofix.com/solutions/therapeutic-solutions/bracing-solutions/)


Stability plays a critical role in healing and movement. Our bracing systems are designed to protect the healing process while supporting mobility—helping patients recover with confidence and comfort.


### Streamlining the Patient Journey


What truly differentiates Therapeutic Solutions is how we support patients and providers beyond the device itself.


We offer streamlined digital services, reducing complexity, and saving time for care teams. Our simple, digitalized claims processing through the Orthofix team and Provider Portal is built for ease of use—making administrative steps more efficient and intuitive.


Patients receive full-service, personalized care that includes fitting, education, and ongoing support—ensuring a seamless recovery experience and optimal outcomes. This hands-on approach reflects our commitment to *every patient, every time.*


With timely access to state-of-the-art, clinically proven technology, patients can begin therapy sooner—supporting faster recovery and better adherence. The result is consistently high patient satisfaction, not only with outcomes, but also with the Orthofix team and the user-friendly tools designed to support them throughout their journey.


## We are Redefining Recovery


Recovery doesn’t end after a procedure—it continues every day with the right support, consistency, and care. Through expanded offerings, deeper partnerships, and solutions designed to make recovery easier. Orthofix streamlines musculoskeletal recovery for patients through comprehensive service and offerings.
