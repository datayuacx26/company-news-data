# Carrot Collective: Highly vetted products, lower prices — all in one place

Carrot continues to drive value for both organizations and their people, and our latest resource is no exception.


Introducing the **Carrot Collective:** a curated marketplace of clinically vetted products and services that help members improve their fertility and hormonal health.


Our members are always looking for smarter ways to reach their health goals. By connecting them with trusted recommendations and offering exclusive discounts — all within the Carrot app — the Carrot Collective empowers members with tools to positively impact whatever journey they’re on.


For members, the Collective removes barriers to care. For customers, it enhances the Carrot experience with no additional cost or complexity, delivering immediate and ongoing value.

We’ve brought together established Carrot partners and brand-new offerings to provide a wealth of helpful products and services to all members across a variety of journeys.


**Oura:** A smart ring that tracks sleep, activity, stress, and more to help members stay connected to what their body needs and provides insights on overall health and wellness
