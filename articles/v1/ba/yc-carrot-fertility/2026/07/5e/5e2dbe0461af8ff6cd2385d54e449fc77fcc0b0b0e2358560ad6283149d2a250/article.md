# Carrot launches first-of-its-kind global doula network: Trusted support for pregnancy & postnatal care

From skyrocketing cases of postpartum depression to a troubling increase of maternal mortality in the U.S., the truth is clear: we are in the midst of a global maternal-health crisis. This crisis is fueled by a broken healthcare system that has historically deprioritized women’s health during one of the most critical moments in their lives.


The need for deeper, more attentive care is paramount, and doulas can bridge the gap. Doulas are non-medical professionals who provide vital education, advocacy, and emotional support for people who are pregnant, giving birth, and recovering from labor.


Research shows that engaging a qualified doula during or after pregnancy can:


- Improve live-birth rates
- Reduce the frequency of expensive C-sections
- Lower rates of postpartum depression and anxiety
- Decrease complications in labor and delivery
- Shorten recovery time from labor, getting parents back to work sooner


But not all doulas are created equal, and there isn't a standardized vetting process to ensure quality care from one professional to another. This quality gap can create risks to the health and safety of women, babies, and families.


Carrot has always recognized the valuable role these caregivers play, by helping our members afford and access their services. And now, we’re making it even easier to connect with the most trusted professionals in this field.


Carrot is proud to announce **our new doula network** : a global, in-network program of highly qualified caregivers — rigorously vetted by Carrot — to ensure the highest quality experience for every member. Members can now more easily access the best professionals possible for prenatal and postnatal care, because all doulas within Carrot's network complete a rigorous vetting process.


## The benefits of Carrot’s doula network


By building our doula network, Carrot offers value to members and employers alike:


- **Cost savings:** Carrot’s doula network eliminates erroneous claims, meaning customers can rest easy knowing the services truly add value for members.
- **Global access:** The doula network is currently available in the U.S., the U.K., Canada, Germany, Ireland, India, and Australia — with plans to expand to even more countries in the near future. Carrot’s global eye ensures in-network doulas meet the same criteria, no matter where they practice.
- **Peace of mind:** Carrot is the only solution that offers a curated network of highly trained, vetted doula professionals. Members can connect with the very best support for their needs the first time, instead of having to search independently and hope for the best.
- **Virtual and in-person care:** Members can connect virtually with doulas or find a provider in their local community, expanding the reach of this life-changing care.
- **Healthier pregnancies:** Doula support has been shown to reduce unnecessary interventions and improve the mental health of future and postpartum parents, all of which means better outcomes for all.
