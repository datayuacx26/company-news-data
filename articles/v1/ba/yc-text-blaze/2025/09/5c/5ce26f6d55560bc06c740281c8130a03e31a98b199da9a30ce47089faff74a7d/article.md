## What is EditGPT?


[EditGPT](https://editgpt.app/) is an[AI writing assistant](https://blaze.today/blog/best-ai-writing-assistant-software) that helps you rewrite, revise, and polish AI-generated text. It highlights changes between drafts and gives you more control over edits than tools that just spit out a new version.


It's mainly used by students, content writers, and anyone trying to clean up AI text to sound more natural. Though, it may not work for everyone, so keep an eye out for alternatives.


### How to Use EditGPT


EditGPT is simple to use: you paste your original text and your edited version, and it highlights the exact changes between them. This makes it easy to review revisions without guessing what was altered.


Students often use it to compare AI-generated drafts, see how a rewrite improved clarity, or track formatting edits. It's also helpful for AI proofreading, checking grammar tweaks, or spotting awkward phrasing.


If you're humanizing AI-generated content, it shows how your text has been adjusted to sound more natural. It can even be used to monitor changes when reformatting citations or restructuring academic writing.


While it's not a full writing tool, EditGPT works well as a side-by-side comparison editor for anything you[rewrite or rework with AI](https://blaze.today/blog/best-ai-rewriters/) .

### 2. Grammarly


[Grammarly AI](https://www.grammarly.com/) is known for grammar correction, but it also helps rewrite and restructure sentences. It works directly in Google Docs and other platforms, so it fits into most workflows.


Grammarly flags unclear or robotic-sounding phrases and suggests more natural alternatives. It’s good for students who want better grammar and smoother writing.


### 3. Wordvice AI


[Wordvice AI](https://wordvice.ai/) focuses on academic writing. It checks grammar, sentence flow, and formal tone. It’s useful for students working on essays or research papers who want something more polished.


Wordvice also includes paraphrasing features to help with rewriting awkward sentences.


### 4. PaperPal


[PaperPal](https://paperpal.com/) is another AI writing assistant focused on academic use. It rewrites your text to match formal academic standards. You paste your paragraph, and it gives you suggestions for clarity and flow.


It’s especially useful if English isn’t your first language or you struggle with academic tone.


### 5. Samwell AI


[Samwell AI](https://www.samwell.ai/) is a browser-based AI humanizer built specifically for rewriting AI-generated content to sound more natural. You paste your draft, and it rewrites the text to bypass AI detectors while keeping your original meaning.


[Tools like Samwell AI](https://blaze.today/blog/samwell-ai-alternatives/) are good for polishing up ChatGPT responses before submitting assignments.


### 6. PaperRater
