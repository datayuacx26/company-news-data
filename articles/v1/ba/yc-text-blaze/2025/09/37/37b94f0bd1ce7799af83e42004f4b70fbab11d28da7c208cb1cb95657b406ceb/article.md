## What is SMMRY AI?


[SMMRY](https://smmry.com/) is a free online tool that shortens long text into a few key sentences. You can paste in a block of text, and it will automatically generate a condensed version.


It's mostly used by students and researchers who need to process large volumes of reading material quickly. While it’s simple, it lacks some of the more advanced features newer tools offer.

[TLDR This](https://www.tldrthis.com/) lets you paste in text or a URL and then generates a quick summary. It works well for articles, blogs, and essays, showing both a short version and key bullet points.


You can choose between basic and detailed summaries depending on what you need.[Tools like TLDR This](https://blaze.today/blog/tldr-this-alternatives) are a good pick for students doing research or reviewing reading assignments.


### 3. Dumb It Down AI


[Dumb It Down AI](https://dumbitdown.ai/) rewrites complicated text into simpler summaries. You can paste in text and choose how much to simplify it, which helps if you're working with dense reading material.


[Tools like Dumb It Down AI](https://blaze.today/blog/dumb-it-down-ai-alternatives/) are useful for high school or college students trying to break down academic content.
