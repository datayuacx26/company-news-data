## What is Quizard AI?


[Quizard AI](https://www.usequizard.com/) is an[AI study tool](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-study-tools) that helps students solve homework problems by scanning and answering questions. It's mostly used for subjects like math, science, and history.


Students can upload photos or type in questions to get instant answers with explanations. It’s meant to save time and help students better understand difficult topics.


However, it may not be the best fit for every student because of the price or features available. That's why alternatives are worth looking into.

## Prompts to Use With an AI Homework Helper


Getting the right answers starts with asking the right way. A more detailed prompt helps the AI give a better explanation or result.


> Instead of just asking for the answer, try including context, subject, or instructions on how you want the response.


Here are some useful prompts you can use with an AI homework helper:


-


Solve this algebra problem step-by-step and explain each step: 2x + 5 = 13


-


Summarize this paragraph from my science textbook in 3 simple sentences: \[paste text\]


-


Identify the main idea and two supporting details from this article: \[paste article or paragraph\]


-


Balance this chemical equation and explain the process: H2 + O2 → H2O


-


Rewrite this answer in casual language so it sounds like a high school student wrote it: \[paste answer\]


-


Create 5 multiple choice quiz questions based on this history chapter: \[paste content\]


-


Check my grammar and suggest a clearer version of this sentence: \[paste sentence\]


-


Define photosynthesis in simple terms and give one real-life example
