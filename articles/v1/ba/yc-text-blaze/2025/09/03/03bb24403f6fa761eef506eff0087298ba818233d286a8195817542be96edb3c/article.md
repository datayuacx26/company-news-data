## What is HARPA AI?


[HARPA AI](https://harpa.ai/) is a ChatGPT Chrome extension designed to automate tasks directly in your browser. It interacts with web pages[using ChatGPT](https://blaze.today/blog/how-to-use-chatgpt-effectively/) to help you read content, summarize text, write responses, and more.


It's often used by people who want to automate web-based tasks without leaving their current tab.


### How is HARPA AI Used?


HARPA AI is mainly used to summarize content, generate responses, and answer questions directly on websites. It adds a ChatGPT assistant to your browser so you can interact with text on the page without copying or switching tabs.


It’s useful for research, writing, and reading tasks, especially if you need quick insights. Though, the interface and features might feel limited for users who want more customization or better integration with their workflow.

## Things to Look for in ChatGPT Chrome Extensions


Using ChatGPT through an[AI extension](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-chrome-extensions/) should feel quick and helpful. If you're using it for writing, summarizing, or answering questions, the right features can make it much more effective.


Here are a few things to look for in ChatGPT Chrome extensions:


-


**Website Compatibility** - Lets you use ChatGPT naturally across sites like Gmail, Google Docs, and Notion.


-


**Prompt Management and Customization** - Allows you to save, edit, customize, and reuse your most common prompts with fewer clicks.


-


**Instant Access Anywhere** - Trigger ChatGPT with a shortcut or click, without needing to open a new tab.


-


**Context Awareness** - Understands the page you're on and adjusts its responses without needing extra input.


-


**Flexible Input Modes** - Works with text selection, full-page content, or even typed commands depending on what you need.


These features are what separate a helpful tool from one that just takes up space. Make sure the extension fits the way you already work online.
