## Copy-and-Paste These Prompts for AI Tutors


To get better answers from AI tutors, it helps to be specific. Instead of typing something vague, use detailed prompts that give context and tell the AI exactly what you need.


Here are 20 copy-ready prompts—split into homework help and study prep—to save you time and improve the results you get.


#### Homework Help Prompts


-


Explain how to solve this math problem step by step and show your work


-


Break down this science question and help me understand the key concept


-


Rewrite this answer in clearer language a 10th grader would understand


-


Fix this paragraph and make it sound more natural for a class assignment


-


Tell me what’s wrong with this answer and how to improve it


-


Solve this equation and explain each step in plain language


-


Help me write a better conclusion for this homework question


-


Show me how to format this short answer to meet my teacher’s expectations


-


Turn this list of facts into a proper paragraph for my assignment


-


Compare these two answers and explain which one is better and why

Summarize this topic and highlight the most important points I should remember

-

-


Turn these notes into flashcards with a question and answer format


-


Give me 3 example questions that test my understanding of this topic


-
