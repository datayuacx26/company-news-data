# 10 Free CiteFast Alternatives to Generate APA & MLA Citations

Students often struggle to format citations correctly when working on academic assignments. APA and MLA styles have strict guidelines, and even small mistakes can lead to lost marks.


> **[Studies show](https://www.plagiarism.org/article/plagiarism-facts-and-stats) that one out of three students have admitted to plagiarizing in an assignment** .


While CiteFast is a free tool to handle citations, it’s not the only option. There are many great citation generators out there for you to use to generate APA or MLA citations.


In this article, we'll list 10 free alternatives to CiteFast that simplify creating accurate citations, saving students time and effort while ensuring proper formatting.


## What is CiteFast?


[CiteFast](https://www.citefast.com/?s=APA7#_Webpage) is a free online citation generator designed to help users quickly create citations in APA, MLA, and Chicago styles. The tool lets users input sources like books, articles, and websites and generates properly formatted citations in seconds.


While CiteFast is efficient, it may lack some advanced features students seek, such as in-depth plagiarism checks or integration with other writing tools. It also doesn’t offer as many customization options or additional support for less common source types.


## Things to Look for in an APA or MLA Citation Generator


Choosing the right citation generator can make writing assignments less stressful. To find the best tool, focus on features that save time and help you meet academic requirements.


Here are a few things to look for when choosing an APA or MLA citation generator:


-


**Accuracy** - The generator should follow the latest APA or MLA guidelines so you don’t lose points over small mistakes.


-


**Ease of Use** - It should have a simple design and quick[options like autofill](https://blaze.today/blog/autofill-chrome-extensions) , so you spend less time figuring out how to use it.


-


**Source Variety** - The tool should support citations for books, articles, websites, and less common sources like podcasts or interviews.


-


**Affordability** - Free tools or affordable plans are perfect for students working on a tight budget.


Keeping these in mind will help you pick a tool that makes your academic work smoother and faster. That way, you can focus more on your ideas and less on citation headaches.

### 2. EasyBib


[EasyBib](https://www.easybib.com/) is a tool that allows users to generate citations in APA and MLA styles. It simplifies the process by letting students input source details and retrieve properly formatted citations.


Beyond citation generation, it includes features like bibliography organization to help keep sources well-ordered. EasyBib also supports educational resources for students who want to learn more about citation styles.


### 3. Cite This For Me


[Cite This For Me](https://www.citethisforme.com/) helps students create APA and MLA citations by automating much of the process. Users can input source information manually or autofill citations by pasting URLs.


It also provides the option to save citations for later use, making it easier to compile a bibliography over time. The tool is accessible on both desktop and mobile, offering flexibility for students working from various devices.


### 4. Citation Machine


[Citation Machine](https://www.citationmachine.net/) focuses on making the citation process efficient for students using APA or MLA styles. It offers customization options, allowing users to tweak citations based on specific formatting needs.


The tool also includes a plagiarism checker, which can help students ensure their work meets academic integrity standards. Citation Machine is suitable for quick citation creation and long-term bibliography management.


### 5. MyBib


[MyBib](https://www.mybib.com/) is a free citation generator designed to handle APA and MLA formatting. It allows students to organize multiple citations into comprehensive bibliographies.


The tool supports both manual entry and autofill for common sources like websites and books. It is particularly useful for students who need to manage and export references for large projects.
