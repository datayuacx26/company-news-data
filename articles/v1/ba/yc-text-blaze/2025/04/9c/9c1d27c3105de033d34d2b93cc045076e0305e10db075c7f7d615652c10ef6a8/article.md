## What is PaperRater?


[PaperRater](https://www.paperrater.com/) is an[AI essay checker](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-essay-checkers-and-graders) that helps students analyze their writing for grammar, spelling, and plagiarism issues. It offers automated proofreading, word choice suggestions, and a basic plagiarism checker.


The tool is web-based and does not require downloads or installations. Users can paste their text into the platform and receive feedback within seconds.


While it’s useful for quick checks, PaperRater lacks advanced[AI writing analysis](https://blaze.today/blog/best-ai-writing-assistant-software/) and has restrictions on free usage.


### Is PaperRater Safe & Legit?


PaperRater is a legitimate AI essay checker that has been used by students for years. It provides automated proofreading and plagiarism detection without requiring an account for basic features.

#### GPTZero


[GPTZero](https://gptzero.me/) is designed to detect AI-generated text, but it can also help students analyze their writing for originality.


It provides insights into sentence structure, complexity, and whether an essay appears to be written by AI. This can be useful for students who want to ensure their writing looks natural and authentic.


While it doesn’t check for grammar or plagiarism, it helps verify that an essay is entirely human-written.


#### Quillbot AI


[Quillbot AI](https://quillbot.com/essay-checker) is primarily known for its rewriting tool, but it also includes a grammar checker and plagiarism detector.


Students can use it to rephrase awkward sentences, fix grammar mistakes, and improve word choice. The free version allows basic rewriting and grammar checking, while the plagiarism checker requires a premium upgrade.


It’s useful for refining essay content and making sure writing is clearer and more natural.


#### Scribbr AI


[Scribbr AI](https://www.scribbr.com/) specializes in plagiarism detection, making it a helpful tool for students who need to check their work before submitting it.


It scans essays against academic papers and online sources, highlighting any potential matches. The tool also includes a basic grammar checker to fix common writing mistakes.


It’s designed for academic writing, so it’s useful for students who need a more detailed plagiarism report.


#### Grammarly AI


[Grammarly AI](https://www.grammarly.com/ai-writer) is a popular tool that checks essays for grammar, punctuation, and clarity.

## Prompts to Use With an AI Essay Checker


Using an AI essay checker is more effective when you provide clear and detailed prompts.

-
