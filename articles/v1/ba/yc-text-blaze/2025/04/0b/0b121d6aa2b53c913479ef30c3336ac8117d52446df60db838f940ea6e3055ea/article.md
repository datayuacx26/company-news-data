## 5 Tips for Using AI to Generate & Summarize Your Notes


Using AI to generate and summarize notes can greatly improve productivity, but there are a few strategies that can help you get the most out of these tools.


Following these tips will ensure your AI-generated notes are both accurate and useful.


#### Be specific with your input


The quality of AI-generated notes often depends on the clarity of the input. Make sure the text or material you provide to the AI is organized and free from unnecessary distractions.


> By giving the AI a well-structured document, you increase the chances of getting a focused and relevant summary.


#### Use clear prompts


The AI will perform better when it receives clear instructions on what you want from the output.


For example, you can instruct it to extract key points, highlight action items, or summarize in a specific format like bullet points. Tailoring your prompt helps the AI generate the notes in the exact style and depth you need.


#### Start with shorter summaries


If you're unsure about the AI's capabilities, start with a shorter summary and build from there. Many tools allow you to adjust the length of the output, so requesting a brief overview first can help you quickly assess the quality.


Once you're satisfied with the initial output, you can expand the summary for more detail if needed.


#### Always review the output


Even though AI is efficient, it’s not infallible. There might be important context or subtle points that the AI misses.


Take a moment to review the generated notes to ensure accuracy, and make any necessary edits or additions to complete the information.


#### Organize notes into folders


Most AI note-taking tools offer ways to organize your generated notes, such as folders or tags. Take advantage of these features to keep your notes categorized by topic, project, or date.


> This will make it easier to retrieve and reference your notes when needed, especially if you deal with large volumes of information regularly.


### Example Prompts


Using the right prompts can make a big difference in how effectively the AI generates and summarizes your notes. Below are some example prompts that can help you get specific outputs tailored to your needs.


Here are a few example prompts you can use to get started:


-


Summarize the key points from this research paper, focusing on the main arguments, supporting data, and any conclusions or recommendations made by the authors, in a bullet-point format.


-


Provide a detailed meeting summary, highlighting important decisions, key discussion points, action items, and assigned tasks with deadlines, and organize the notes by topic.


-


Generate concise notes from this lecture transcript, focusing on the main concepts discussed, key terms defined, and any examples used to explain complex ideas.


-


Identify and summarize the recurring themes from this document, making sure to include how each theme is developed and supported throughout the text.


-


Create a brief yet comprehensive overview of the important topics covered in this report, including any recommendations or future actions proposed.


Using these prompts ensures that the AI delivers summaries and notes that are relevant to your specific objectives. Tailoring your prompt makes it easier to get the exact information you need from the AI.
