# 7 EasyBib Alternatives for Quick APA and MLA Citations

Students often rely on citation tools to save time and ensure their references are formatted correctly. However, many tools like EasyBib can be cluttered with ads, have limited free features, or make mistakes in formatting.


> **[Studies show](https://academicintegrity.org/resources/facts-and-statistics) that ~14% of students have paraphrased or copied text from a source without properly citing it** .


Finding a free and accurate alternative is essential for producing quality academic work without extra hassle. There are a lot of citation generators out there that can help you generate accurate citations quickly (and even use AI).

## What is EasyBib?


[EasyBib](https://www.easybib.com/) is a widely used online citation generator that helps students create bibliographies and citations in various formats, including APA and MLA.


However, while EasyBib is helpful, it’s not the only option out there. Some users might want tools with fewer ads, better user experiences, or more citation styles.


Looking for an alternative to EasyBib? Read on for things to look for in alternatives and our recommendations for some great citation generators:


## What to Look for in EasyBib Alternatives & Citation Generators


When picking a citation generator, it’s important to find one that meets the everyday needs of students. From formatting essays to handling last-minute deadlines, the right tool can make a big difference.


Here are the key features to prioritize:


-


**Citation Styles** - Ensure the tool supports the formats your teachers require, especially APA and MLA.


-


**Ease of Use** - Choose a tool that’s simple to navigate and doesn’t waste time with unnecessary steps.


-


**Accuracy** - Look for a tool that consistently generates correct citations without errors.


-


**Cost** - Free options are ideal for students, so prioritize tools that provide core features without charging.


-


**Export Options** - Check if the tool lets you easily transfer citations to your essay, like exporting to Google Docs or Word.


-


**Ad-Free Experience** - Ads can be distracting and slow you down—tools without ads are much more student-friendly.


By focusing on tools that are free, accurate, and easy to use, you can save time and reduce stress when working on assignments. The right citation generator can help you stay organized and meet your deadlines.

### 2. CiteFast


[CiteFast](https://www.citefast.com/?s=APA7#_Webpage) is an online generator designed to create citations quickly and efficiently. It supports APA, MLA, and Chicago styles, making it a flexible choice for different academic tasks.


The tool’s auto-save feature allows students to revisit their work within a set time frame. It also enables basic citation edits before exporting to meet specific project requirements.


### 3. Scribbr


[Scribbr](https://www.scribbr.com/) provides citation support for APA, MLA, and other styles while also offering educational resources on referencing. Students can use it to create formatted citations or learn proper citation techniques through its guides.


Scribbr includes a plagiarism checker, which can be useful for maintaining originality in academic work. Its combination of citation and writing support tools makes it a practical resource.


### 4. Citation Machine

### 5. MyBib


[MyBib](https://www.mybib.com/) is a free citation tool that provides support for APA, MLA, and other styles with a user-friendly interface. It allows students to collaborate by sharing citation lists, making it helpful for group assignments.


The tool includes export options to Google Docs, streamlining the process of adding references to essays. Its simplicity and functionality make it appealing for students managing multiple assignments.
