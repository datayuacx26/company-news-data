### Cover Letter Format


Before using AI to generate a cover letter, it's important to understand the basic format that most hiring managers expect. Having a structured template in mind can help guide the AI and make the customization process smoother.


Here’s a general cover letter format that AI tools often follow:


1.


**Contact Information** : Your name, phone number, email, and optionally, your address. Include the date and the employer's contact information as well.


2.


**Greeting** : Address the hiring manager by name if possible, or use "Dear Hiring Manager" as a default.


3.


**Introduction** : State the job you’re applying for and briefly mention why you’re interested in the position.


4.


**Body** : Highlight your relevant skills and experience. Focus on how your background aligns with the job requirements.


5.


**Closing** : Thank the hiring manager for their time and express your interest in further discussing the position.


6.


**Signature** : End with a polite sign-off like “Sincerely” followed by your name.


This format ensures that the cover letter flows logically and includes all necessary information. By following this structure, your AI-generated cover letter will appear professional and organized.

### What to Include in Your AI-Generated Cover Letter Prompt


When using AI to generate your cover letter, the key to getting a high-quality output is providing a clear and detailed prompt. The more specific you are in your instructions, the more personalized and accurate the cover letter will be.


Here are some key things to include in your prompt:


-


**Job Title** - Make sure to include the specific job title you are applying for.


-


**Skills and Experience** - Highlight your most relevant skills and experience to guide the AI in focusing on what matters.


-


**Tone** - Specify whether you want the letter to sound formal, professional, or more conversational.


-


**Achievements** - Mention any notable accomplishments or metrics that could strengthen your cover letter.


-


**Company Details** - If possible, include the company's name and any specific information about their values or culture.


By providing these details, you can ensure that the AI-generated cover letter is not only relevant but also stands out to hiring managers. The prompt is your opportunity to steer the AI toward creating something that truly reflects your qualifications.
