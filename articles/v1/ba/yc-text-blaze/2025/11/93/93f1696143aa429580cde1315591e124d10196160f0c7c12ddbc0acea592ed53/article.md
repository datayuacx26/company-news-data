## What is TLDL?


[TLDL](https://tldl.club/en) is an[AI study tool](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-study-tools/) that students use to turn long content into short, organized notes. These tools have become popular because many students want faster ways to study without spending hours taking notes manually.


### What Does TLDL Do?


TLDL helps students break down information into shorter and easier study formats. Here are the main things it can do:


-


**Notes** - Create short notes from longer content.


-


**Studying** - Turn class material into quick study references.


-


**Transcribe Lectures** - Convert recorded lectures into text you can review.


-


**Study Guides** - Build structured guides based on the major points.


### Is TLDL Free?


TLDL offers a free plan, but it has a limit on how many notes you can create. If you want unlimited, you have to upgrade to a paid plan.


> TLDL's Unlimited plan (unlimited note generation) costs $19.99/month or $89.99/year.


While TLDL is useful, it's limited free plan might cause some students to look into other tools that offer more expanded free usage.

## How to Choose a TLDL Alternative


Choosing the right option depends on how you like to study and what features support your workflow. You should look for tools that make it easy to create organized notes without adding extra steps.


Here are the main things to pay attention to:


-


**Your preferred study style** - Pick a tool that matches how you learn and the type of notes you want to create.


-


**No file uploads required** - Choose a tool that lets you generate notes without uploading documents.


-


**More free access** - Look for options that give you enough free usage to study consistently.


-


**Interactive study sessions** - Check if the tool supports back and forth studying or deeper topic breakdowns.


-


**Writing help** - Make sure the tool can help you rewrite or improve content when needed.


These points make it easier to compare options and find the tool that supports your study habits. Trying a few tools is the best way to see what fits your routine.
