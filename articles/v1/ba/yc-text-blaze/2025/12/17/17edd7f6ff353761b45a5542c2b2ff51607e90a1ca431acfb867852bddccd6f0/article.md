[Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com/) is a writing tool that highlights[complex sentences](https://blaze.today/blog/what-is-a-compound-sentence) ,[passive voice](https://blaze.today/blog/active-and-passive-voice-checkers) , and adverbs to make your writing more readable.


It’s known for its color-coded feedback that pushes writers to simplify and improve clarity. Many use it to make blog posts, essays, and emails easier to read without losing meaning.
