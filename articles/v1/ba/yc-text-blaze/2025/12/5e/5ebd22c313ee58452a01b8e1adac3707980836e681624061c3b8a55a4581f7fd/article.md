## What is ProWritingAid? What Does It Do?


[ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/) is a writing assistant designed to help you improve your grammar, clarity, and overall writing quality. It reviews your text for errors and offers real-time suggestions to make your writing more polished and consistent.


Here’s what ProWritingAid can do:


-


**Grammar checking** - Finds and fixes grammar, spelling, and[punctuation mistakes](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-punctuation-checkers) as you write.


-


**Paraphrasing** - Suggests different ways to reword sentences for better readability or tone.


-


**Proofreading** - Scans your text for style, flow, and structural improvements to make it sound more natural.

## How to Choose a ProWritingAid Alternative


Not every[grammar checker](https://blaze.today/blog/best-free-ai-grammar-checkers) or AI writer offers the same features. Some focus on accuracy, while others make editing faster and more creative. When choosing a ProWritingAid alternative, think about what matters most to your writing goals.


Here are a few things to look for:


-


**More free usage** - Pick a tool that lets you use key features without hitting limits.


-


**Advanced AI rewriting and paraphrasing** - Choose one that helps improve sentence flow and tone with AI suggestions.


-


**Privacy focus** - Make sure the tool doesn’t store or share your writing without permission.


-


**Works anywhere** - Go for one that integrates with browsers, documents, and emails for quick editing.
