# 20 Chrome Extensions Every Student Needs to Install Today

In recent years, remote and hybrid learning have become more prominent in schools, as online tools make teaching and learning easier and more accessible.


Whether you are studying, writing, taking notes, or doing assignments, online tools make it easier for you to learn. This is especially true for students who use Google Chrome and apps like Google Drive and Docs.


> [One study](https://www.kajeet.net/chromebooks-classrooms-connectivity-the-age-of-chromebooks/) showed that 85% of schools and districts have a program in place where students can use Chromebooks daily at school.


[Chrome extensions](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/category/extensions) are useful tools these students can use to make working online easier.


That’s why we wrote this article on the 20 best Chrome Extensions for students that help with writing and editing, studying, screen/tab management, and productivity.

### Grammarly


[Grammarly](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/grammarly-grammar-checker/kbfnbcaeplbcioakkpcpgfkobkghlhen?hl=en%27) is an incredibly useful writing tool for any student. Correct grammar is important regardless of what you are writing.


With Grammarly, you can write essays and emails with confidence, knowing that your writing will be free of errors.


### LanguageTool


[LanguageTool](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/grammar-spell-checker-%E2%80%94-l/oldceeleldhonbafppcapldpdifcinji?hl=en%27) is another writing tool that you don’t want to work without. LanguageTool is a writing assistant that highlights errors in different colors, marks style and grammar issues with underlines, and helps you quickly make changes to your work by going to the error and clicking the correction.


### Cite This For Me


Writing essays is an essential part of many classes at most levels of education. Creating citations is an important, yet challenging (and sometimes infuriating!) part of essay writing.


[Cite This For Me](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/cite-this-for-me-web-cite/nnnmhgkokpalnmbeighfomegjfkklkle?hl=en%27) is the tool for you if your classes require you to create citations in your writing. Cite This For Me takes the frustration out of creating citations by having a simple 1-click citation extension you can use while writing your essays.


### Kami


Studying is one of the most important aspects of learning, yet finding good study tools can be difficult.


[Kami](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/kami-for-google-chrome/ecnphlgnajanjnkcmbpancdjoidceilk?hl=en%27) is one of the premiere study tools for students, as it helps with many aspects of learning. Kami’s document annotation feature can help you edit while you read, make highlights and comments, and learn in a way that works for you.


### Highlight This

[HighlightThis](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/highlight-this-finds-and/fgmbnmjmbjenlhbefngfibmjkpbcljaj?hl=en%27) makes highlighting text simple so that you can read and study important terms with ease. You can create word lists and use different colors to read, study, and learn in your own unique way.


### Web Highlights - PDF & Web Highlighter


[Web Highlights - PDF & Web Highlighter](https://web-highlights.com/) is another online highlighter tool that makes online highlighting, note-taking, and tagging super easy.

## Extensions for Studying


### Zorbi


When you think of studying, one of the first things that likely comes to mind is creating flashcards. It’s a time-tested study strategy that is proven to work.


[Zorbi](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/zorbi-flashcards-from-pdf/cionjeaneeijknieabfgfiidhhjlkbcm?hl=en%27) takes flashcards to the next level. With dynamic flashcards that can include images, formulas, and even audio, Zorbi can help you prepare for that big test efficiently.


Want to save hours of repetitive typing for free?


Join thousands of students who are using Text Blaze templates.


### Speechify


People learn in many different ways. One way is to listen to written text out loud. This can help with catching errors and retaining information.


[Speechify](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/speechify-text-to-speech/ljflmlehinmoeknoonhibbjpldiijjmm?hl=en%27) is a tool that reads out any text online to you. If you prefer to study by listening, Speechify can be a game-changer for you.


### Scribe


You might not be an auditory learner, and that’s okay! Here’s a tool for people who are natural visual learners.


[Scribe](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/scribe-%E2%80%94-documentation-so/okfkdaglfjjjfefdcppliegebpoegaii?hl=en%27) helps you create step-by-step guides that you can refer to and study. If you need to remember how to do something online regarding your classwork, just click record and create your Scribe guide to help you remember it.


### Otto


It might sound counterintuitive, but a crucial part of studying is actually taking breaks. Studying without breaks can lead to you retaining less information and burnout. Taking breaks can help you focus and fully learn the information you’re studying.


[Otto](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/otto-%E2%80%93-pomodoro-timer-and/jbojhemhnilgooplglkfoheddemkodld?hl=en%27) is a Pomodoro timer that helps you take breaks and stay productive while studying. Otto’s time-tracking and website-blocking can help you study effectively without overdoing it.


## Extensions for Screen/Tab Management


### Loom


If you’ve ever tried to describe a technical issue online with a professor or peer, sometimes describing it in an email just doesn’t work.


[Loom](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/loom-%E2%80%93-free-screen-record/liecbddmkiiihnedobmlmillhodjkdmb?hl=en%27) solves this problem by allowing you to quickly create screen captures. You simply click record and your screen and actions are recorded until you click stop. Plus, once you’re done, Loom quickly converts your recording into a video that you can access and share.


### Screencastify


[Screencastify](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/screencastify-screen-vide/mmeijimgabbpbgpdklnllpncmdofkcpn?hl=en%27) is another screen capturing tool that offers more options regarding the editing of your captures.


If you need to create a video with only a tab, a browser, or just a webcam, Screencastify is perfect for you. Not only that, but you can edit to enhance your video visually with objects and shapes.


### Dualless


Having multiple screens is very convenient for school, but it is not always possible.


[Dualless](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/dualless/bgdpkilkheacbboffppjgceiplijhfpd?hl=en%27) is a tool that solves this problem by allowing you to split your screen in half and do two different things at once. Watch a video and take notes, write an essay while checking the dictionary, or even listen to a lecture while studying the textbook. Dualless helps you study and learn while multitasking.


### Workona


Having messy tabs can be a real problem if you have many assignments or texts to read.


[Workona](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/workona-tab-manager/ailcmbgekjpnablpdkmaaccecekgdhlh?hl=en%27) is a tab manager that helps you stay organized and manage all of your tabs while working. With Workona, you can keep track of your work and never lose that one pesky tab again.


## Extensions for Staying Organized


### Apploye


If you are a student who is struggling with poor study habits and wasting time on unnecessary activities is a concern for you, then[tracking time with chrome extension](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/apploye-time-tracker/chdpgihnbjecnplaebiafkecebcdffig) is the way to combat your instinct.


[Apploye](https://apploye.com/) is a time tracking software, a must-to-have companion for students struggling to keep pace with the mounting pressure of study and approaching assignment deadlines. Here you can track your time opening tasks for different subjects and see how much time you spend per topic.


You will be able to see the weekly time spent. Furthermore, you will get a Pomodoro timer that can help you re-energize yourself and focus with a higher level of concentration.


### Stay Focused


Being productive while doing schoolwork online can be difficult if you find yourself easily distracted.


[Stay Focused](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/stay-focused/nnlgodiccogbpcfnhmclaicljjgfmekd?hl=en%27) is a productivity tool that helps you minimize online distractions by limiting your access to websites you choose during hours that you want to work. You can turn distracting websites off when you’re working and on when you aren’t, making it easier for you to focus.


### Todoist


Staying organized is an important part of productivity. One way to stay organized is by creating to-do lists.


[Todoist](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/todoist-for-chrome/jldhpllghnbhlbpcmnajkpdmadaolakh?hl=en%27) is one of the most popular to-do apps, and for good reason, as it can help you create effective lists and manage upcoming due dates. With Todoist, you can keep track of your assignments and their due dates effectively.


### Momentum


Finding motivation to do schoolwork can be difficult. Sometimes, you need a small reminder to help you get back on track.


[Momentum](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/momentum/laookkfknpbbblfpciffpaejjkokdgca?hl=en%27) is a Chrome extension that gives you motivational messages when you open a new tab. Not only that, but Momentum can help you focus and get back to work on the stuff that matters most.


### Toggl


Procrastination is one of the worst habits a student can have. Time-tracking is one method to combat procrastination.


[Toggl Track](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/toggl-track-productivity/oejgccbfbmkkpaidnkphaiaecficdnfn?hl=en%27) helps you keep track of the time you spend working on assignments and homework so that you know when to take breaks. Toggl makes it easy to track your time and combat procrastination.
