## 5 Tips for Building Resumes With AI


AI resume builders can help streamline the job application process, but it's important to use them effectively.


Here are a few tips for building resumes with AI:


-


**Tailor Your Resume to Each Job** - AI tools can generate resumes quickly, but[tailoring them to match specific job descriptions](https://blaze.today/blog/tailor-resume-to-job-description-ai) improves your chances of passing ATS scans and getting noticed.


-


**Highlight Key Skills and Achievements** - Instead of relying on generic AI-generated content, emphasize accomplishments that align with the role you’re applying for.


-


**Keep Formatting Simple** - Avoid overly complex designs or graphics that could make your resume harder for ATS to process.


-


**Rewrite and Check with AI** - Use AI to[rewrite resumes](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-resume-rewriter) to improve clarity and[check your resume](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-resume-checker) for grammar issues.


-


**Verify Industry-Specific Terminology** - AI tools may not always include the right technical terms, so ensure your resume accurately reflects industry expectations.


Using AI effectively can save time and improve the quality of your job applications. With the right approach, AI tools can help create resumes that are both optimized and tailored to your career goals.
