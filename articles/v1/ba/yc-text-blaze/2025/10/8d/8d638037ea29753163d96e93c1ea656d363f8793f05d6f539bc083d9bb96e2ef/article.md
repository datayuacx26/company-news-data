## What is SciSpace AI?


[SciSpace AI](https://scispace.com/) is a tool designed to help students, researchers, and professionals analyze academic papers.


It offers features like chatting with PDFs, AI-driven insights, and citation management. This makes it easier to break down complex[research papers](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-essay-research-paper-writers-checkers/) and extract key information without manually skimming through hundreds of pages.

#### 2. Unriddle AI (Anara)

#### 3. Coral AI


[Coral AI](https://www.getcoralai.com/) helps students manage research by providing summaries, citation tools, and interactive features for understanding papers.


The Chat with PDF function allows users to highlight sections and ask AI-powered questions about specific details. This is helpful for analyzing studies and extracting relevant information for essays and projects.


Some[tools like Coral AI](https://blaze.today/blog/coral-ai-alternatives) also include notetaking features, which can make organizing research more efficient.


#### 4. Jenni AI


[Jenni AI](https://jenni.ai/) is designed for students who need AI-assisted writing support along with research tools. It includes an AI citation generator that formats references in multiple styles, helping students avoid manual citation errors.


While it doesn’t focus on analyzing research papers, it provides AI-generated writing suggestions that can help structure essays and academic work.


For students who need assistance with both writing and citations,[tools like Jenni AI](https://blaze.today/blog/jenni-ai-alternatives) provide features that can make drafting assignments easier.


#### 5. Consensus AI


[Consensus AI](https://consensus.app/) specializes in gathering insights from multiple research papers and summarizing key findings.


It’s useful for students working on literature reviews or research-heavy assignments, as it scans academic studies and extracts evidence-based conclusions. The Chat with PDF feature allows users to ask detailed questions about uploaded papers, making it easier to understand complex topics.


This tool can help students quickly find and compare research without reading through every study in full.


#### 6. Scite AI


[Scite AI](https://scite.ai/) is a research tool that helps users evaluate the credibility of scientific papers by showing how they’ve been cited in other works. It classifies citations as supporting, contrasting, or mentioning the original paper, giving deeper insight into how the research is viewed by the academic community.


It also provides smart citation metrics, dashboards, and browser integrations to make academic exploration more efficient.


#### 7. Powerdrill AI


[Powerdrill AI](https://powerdrill.ai/) is designed to help users analyze and understand research papers quickly. It uses AI to summarize long or technical documents into clear, digestible points while maintaining the paper’s original meaning.


Users can ask questions about the text and get instant, relevant answers without reading the entire paper.

#### 8. Julius AI


[Julius AI](https://julius.ai/) is an AI assistant built for data analysis and research support. It can interpret data from spreadsheets, visualize patterns, and explain results in simple language.

#### 9. Semantic Scholar AI


[Semantic Scholar AI](https://www.semanticscholar.org/) is a free academic search engine developed by the Allen Institute for AI. It indexes millions of scientific papers and uses AI to highlight key concepts, influential citations, and related research topics.


The platform also helps users find papers based on relevance, recency, or impact rather than just keyword matches.
