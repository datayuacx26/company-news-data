### How Do AI Resume Rewriters Optimize Resumes for ATS?


[AI resume](https://optimcareers.com/expert-articles/best-ai-resume-builder) rewriters help optimize resumes by scanning job descriptions and picking out keywords that the ATS is likely to prioritize.


The ATS is programmed to filter out resumes that don’t match specific keywords or phrases used in job descriptions. AI resume tools can detect these keywords and strategically insert them into your resume, making sure it matches the job criteria.


Here are a few examples of keywords AI might identify and include:


-


**Project Management** - A common keyword for roles related to overseeing projects.


-


**Data Analysis** - Often required for jobs involving data interpretation and insights.


-


**Customer Service** - Frequently used in job descriptions for client-facing roles.


-


**Sales Strategy** - A typical keyword for sales positions involving planning and execution.


By adjusting the layout and ensuring the text is readable by ATS systems, AI tools maximize your resume’s chances of being seen. This means you get both relevant content and optimized structure, helping your resume stand out in the first round.
