## Can ChatGPT or AI Write a Resume?


Yes, AI tools like ChatGPT can help you write a resume, (or even[rewrite existing ones](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-resume-rewriter) ) but there are some important things to keep in mind.


ChatGPT is great at generating text based on the information you provide, but it doesn’t have the expertise of a professional resume writer. It can create a basic structure, suggest phrases, and help format your experience, but the quality depends on the input it receives.


> AI-generated resumes might miss nuances or industry-specific keywords that a human expert would know. It’s crucial to provide clear instructions and review the AI’s output to ensure it’s tailored to the job you’re applying for.


Using AI for resumes is a collaborative process; it does the heavy lifting, but your final touches are essential. ChatGPT can certainly help, but think of it as a starting point, not the final product.


Our suggestion is to use[AI to tailor your resume to each job](https://blaze.today/blog/tailor-resume-to-job-description-ai) that you are applying for!


#### How About Using AI to Generate a Cover Letter or a Resume Summary?


AI can also help with creating cover letters and resume summaries, two areas where many job seekers get stuck. A[cover letter generated by AI](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-cover-letter-generators/) can be structured, clear, and tailored to the job description, making the process much faster.


For resume summaries, AI can help create concise and impactful statements that highlight your key strengths. By feeding AI with details about your skills, experience, and job goals, you can get a solid draft that you can refine further.


While AI is excellent at drafting these components, remember that **customization is key** . A generic cover letter or summary won’t stand out to recruiters.


## How to Use ChatGPT to Write a Resume


Writing a resume, like[doing anything with ChatGPT](https://blaze.today/blog/how-to-use-chatgpt-effectively/) , can streamline the process, but it’s essential to approach it with a clear plan.


Here's a step-by-step guide to help you use ChatGPT effectively for resume writing.


#### 1. Outline Your Resume Sections


Start by outlining the main sections of your resume, such as Contact Information, Professional Summary, Work Experience, Skills, and Education.


Clearly define what each section should include based on your job goals. This outline serves as a roadmap for ChatGPT, ensuring that the AI focuses on the key areas you want to highlight.


#### 2. Create a Detailed Prompt


Craft a detailed,[effective ai prompt](https://blaze.today/blog/how-to-write-effective-ai-prompts) for each section, specifying the type of information you want ChatGPT to generate.


For example, for the work experience section, include your job title, company, dates of employment, and key responsibilities.


Read more below on what to include in your prompt.


#### 3. Feed the AI with Your Job History and Achievements


Provide specific details about your work experience, including achievements, skills used, and impact made. The more detailed your input, the better the AI will tailor the content.


> For example, instead of just listing your job title, include what you accomplished in that role, like “Increased sales by 20% through targeted marketing campaigns.”

#### 4. Ask for Formatting and Language Suggestions


Use ChatGPT to help refine the wording and structure of your resume. You can ask for suggestions on how to phrase job responsibilities or highlight skills effectively.


ChatGPT can also recommend ways to organize your resume to make it more readable and visually appealing.


#### 5. Generate Multiple Versions for Different Job Applications


To tailor your resume to various job opportunities, ask ChatGPT to create multiple versions based on different job descriptions.


Modify the focus areas and keywords for each version to match the specific industry or role. This approach saves time and allows you to quickly adapt your resume for different applications.


#### 6. Review and Edit the Output


Carefully review the AI-generated content to ensure accuracy and relevance. Edit sections to better reflect your unique skills and experience, and adjust any generic language to make the resume feel more personal.


It’s essential to refine the final document so it accurately represents you.


#### 7. Optimize for ATS Compliance


Use ChatGPT to check if your resume includes the right keywords and formatting for ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems). Ask for tips on optimizing each section to ensure it passes through software filters that recruiters use. This step enhances your resume’s chances of being seen by hiring managers.


Following these steps will help you effectively use ChatGPT to build a professional and customized resume. Remember that while ChatGPT provides a solid foundation, your final edits will be crucial in making the resume truly stand out.

## 5 Key Things to Include in Your ChatGPT Resume Prompt


Crafting an effective prompt is essential when using ChatGPT to build a resume. A well-detailed prompt helps the AI generate relevant and tailored content that closely matches your needs.


Here are 5 key things to make sure you include:


-


**Your own work experience** - Include detailed job history, accomplishments, and skills. This gives the AI context and helps it write a better resume for you.


-


**The resume format to follow** - There are a lot of new ways to format resumes. Whether you are going with a new format, or a tried and true format, mention it to the AI (chronological, functional, hybrid).


-


**ATS keywords** - Add industry-specific keywords in your prompt to improve ATS compliance. This makes sure the AI includes them in your resume.


-


**Strengths & skills** - Is there anything you do well that you want to highlight in your resume? If there are any of these you want emphasized, don't forget to add them to your prompt.


-


**Instructions for specific sections** - Don't want a summary? How about a deeper focus on your skills? Tell the AI what to include/not include based on your preferences and the job you're applying for.


Use the AI's output as a starting point and refine the resume to better fit your experience and job goals. Your prompt’s details directly impact the quality of the AI-generated resume.
