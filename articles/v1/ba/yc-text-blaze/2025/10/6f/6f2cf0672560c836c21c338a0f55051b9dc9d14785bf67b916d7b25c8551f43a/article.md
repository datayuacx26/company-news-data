## What is MathGPT?


[MathGPT](https://math-gpt.org/) is an[AI math solver](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-math-problem-solvers/) that answers math questions using natural language. It’s built to handle complex equations, word problems, and even advanced topics like calculus or statistics.


Students use it to get explanations, not just answers, which can be helpful when trying to understand how to solve a problem.


It works by combining math-specific reasoning with a large language model. This helps it solve questions in a way that feels more like talking to a tutor.


However, it may not always be free or accessible depending on where you're using it from.


#### Is MathGPT Free?


MathGPT has some free features, but not everything is free. Users may hit limits or need to pay for full access depending on the platform.


Some websites might wrap MathGPT into their premium tools, so students looking for a truly free AI calculator or[AI homework helper](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-homework-helper/) often look elsewhere.

Here are a few things to check for when looking for an alternative to MathGPT:

Can it handle the level of math you’re working on? (basic math vs. college-level)


-


Does it explain the steps clearly?


-


Is it actually free, or does it ask you to pay after a few questions?

### Mathos


[Mathos](https://www.mathgptpro.com/) is a free AI calculator that solves math questions from basic algebra to calculus. It accepts both typed and image-based inputs, so students can use it with textbook problems or handwritten notes.


The tool explains answers clearly, making it easier to follow each step. Mathos also works in a browser, which makes it easy to access from laptops or Chromebooks during study time.


#### Price


- Offers a free plan but has limitations. The Basic plan is $4.99/week and the Prime plan is $9.99/week.


### Mathway


[Mathway](https://www.mathway.com/) gives students quick answers to a wide range of math problems, including algebra, geometry, and calculus. Users can type in a problem or snap a photo for an instant solution.


While step-by-step explanations require a paid version, it’s still useful for checking answers or figuring out where a mistake was made. Mathway is available as both a mobile app and web tool, so it’s easy to use on the go.


#### Price


- Mathway offers a free version, but there are no explanations on the free plan. To get full access, you have to upgrade to Mathway+ which is $9.99/month.


### Symbolab


[SymboLab](https://www.symbolab.com/) focuses on solving and explaining math problems with detailed steps. It supports topics like equations, integrals, and graphing. Students can enter a problem and see how each part of the solution is broken down.


Some features are behind a paywall, but the free version still works well for common math topics.[Tools like Symbolab](https://blaze.today/blog/symbolab-ai-math-solver-alternatives/) are often used by students who want more than just the final answer.


#### Price


- Offers a free plan but has limitations on saved notes. The Pro plan offers full access for $9.95/month (or $39.95/year).

### Julius AI


[Julius AI](https://julius.ai/) is designed to help with both math problems and data-related assignments. It works with natural language, so students can ask math questions in regular sentences.


This is helpful when a problem involves word-based instructions instead of just numbers. The tool runs in a web browser and doesn't need any downloads. While there are usage limits, it’s still a helpful MathGPT alternative for quick math support.


#### Price


- Julius AI offers a free trial, but its Lite plan is $20/month and its Standard plan is $45/month.


### Solvely AI


[Solvely.ai](https://solvely.ai/) gives fast answers to math problems by letting students type questions or upload images. It supports common subjects like algebra, trigonometry, and geometry.


The tool gives short explanations that help students check their work or understand how to start a problem. Solvely is lightweight and free to use, making it a good option for students looking for simple, no-signup math help.


#### Price


- Offer a free trial, but its Unlimited Solver is $12.99/month (or $59.88/year).
