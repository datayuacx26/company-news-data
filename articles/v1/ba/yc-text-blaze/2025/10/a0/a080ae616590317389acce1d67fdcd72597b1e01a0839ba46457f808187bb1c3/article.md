#### Mathway


[Mathway](https://www.mathway.com/) is a long-standing AI math solver that can handle a wide range of topics from basic arithmetic to calculus. You can either type in your problem or take a photo to get quick results.


It gives answers instantly, but step-by-step breakdowns often require a paid version. Even so, many students use the free version to check homework and confirm their approach.


##### Price


- Mathway offers a free version that doesn't include explanations with answers. To get full access, you have to upgrade to Mathway+ which is $9.99/month.


#### GauthMath (aka Gauth AI)


[GauthMath](https://www.gauthmath.com/) uses AI to solve math problems from images or typed input, with support for subjects like algebra and statistics. It also offers a live chat feature where students can connect with real tutors.


This makes it helpful when you want both an AI explanation and human guidance.[Tools like GauthMath](https://blaze.today/blog/gauth-ai-alternatives) are useful for students who need answers quickly.


##### Price


- GauthMath has a free plan that has pretty strict limits. Gauth Plus is $11.99/month or $99.99/year.


#### Symbolab


[SymboLab](https://www.symbolab.com/) solves math problems while showing detailed steps for many types of equations. It's especially useful for learning how to solve the problem, not just getting the final answer.


Students often use it to prepare for tests by reviewing how each step works. Some features are locked behind a subscription, but many core functions are free.


##### Price


- Symbolab a free plan that has limitations on saved notes. The Pro plan unlocks full access for $9.95/month (or $39.95/year).


#### MathGPT


[MathGPT](https://math-gpt.org/) is designed specifically for solving math questions using a version of ChatGPT trained on math content. You can paste in complex or simple problems and get full explanations with math formatting.


It's geared more toward students who want to understand the logic and process behind a solution. The interface is simple, and it focuses on clarity in responses.


##### Price


- MathGPT offers a free trial, but MathGPT Unlimited is $9.99/month.

#### Julius AI


[Julius AI](https://julius.ai/) isn’t built only for math, but it can still help with math problems, especially ones that involve data or word-based logic. It lets you input questions, upload spreadsheets, or describe what you're trying to solve.


The tool can break down the math behind your question and walk you through the logic. While it’s not a traditional step-by-step solver, it’s useful in cases where the problem needs context or explanation.

#### Solvely AI


[Solvely.ai](https://solvely.ai/) helps students solve math problems by letting them upload a photo or enter equations directly. It quickly detects the math type and provides clear solutions with explanations.
