## How to Use AI to Summarize Articles


When using AI to summarize articles, the key is giving clear prompts and specifying how you want the summary formatted. Instead of just pasting text and asking for a summary, you can guide the AI to produce results that actually help you study.


Here’s how to use AI to summarize articles effectively:


1.


**Start with a clear prompt** — Tell the AI exactly what kind of summary you want (for example: “Summarize this article in 5 bullet points focusing on key findings”).


2.


**Add context** — Mention what type of content it is (research paper, blog post, or news article) so the AI knows what to focus on.


3.


**Specify the output format** — Choose how you want the summary written, such as bullet points, short paragraphs, or[a study guide](https://blaze.today/blog/study-guide-creators) format.


4.


**Set the tone and detail level** — Indicate whether you want a simple overview or a detailed explanation with examples.


5.


**Review and refine** — If the result isn’t quite right, adjust your prompt or ask follow-up questions to make the summary more useful.
