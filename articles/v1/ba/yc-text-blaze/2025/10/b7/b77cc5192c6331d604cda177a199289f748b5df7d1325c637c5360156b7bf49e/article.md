## What is Recall AI?


[Recall AI](https://www.getrecall.ai/) is an[AI summarizer](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-summarizer/) that records and summarizes meetings, lectures, and online content. It captures information, converts it into text, and creates a summary or transcript.


People use it to save time on note-taking and reviewing long sessions. It helps summarize PDFs, articles, YouTube videos, documents, and more.


## How Does Recall AI Compare to Other AI Summarizers?


Recall AI is helpful for summarizing different types of content, including meetings, notes, and articles. It does a good job of turning long material into short, clear summaries.


However, beyond summarizing, it can be a little limited. Other AI summarizers offer more features for studying, research, and writing, making them better suited for users who want to do more than just condense information.
