## What Do AI Document Readers Do?


AI document readers use natural language processing to help you interact with research material. Instead of reading everything manually, these tools summarize content, answer questions, and support study workflows.


Here are the main ways AI document readers help with research:


-


**Summarize content** - Generate short and clear summaries from long papers or articles.


-


**Answer your questions** - Ask specific questions about a document and get relevant answers instantly.


-


**Help you do research** - Extract insights, identify important sections, and support research projects.


-


**Generate citations** - Create proper references and citations automatically from your sources.


## How to Choose a Sharly AI Alternative


When choosing a Sharly AI alternative, focus on how easily the tool fits your study habits and research needs. Some work better for[summarizing PDFs](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-pdf-readers/) , while others are designed for question-answering or writing help.


Here are some features/important points to consider:


-


**Easy to use for students** - The tool should have a simple, distraction-free layout that makes it quick to upload and read files.


-


**Supports multiple file types** - Look for tools that can handle PDFs, research papers, web articles, and class notes without conversion.


-


**Lets you ask questions** - Choose one that allows you to ask follow-up questions directly about your document or study material.


-


**Has a useful free plan** - Make sure the free version gives you enough uploads or summaries to use regularly for schoolwork.


-


**Helps you study smarter** - Tools that include citations, note exports, or highlight key ideas can make your study sessions much more effective.
