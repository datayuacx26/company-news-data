# 15 Best Job Search Apps For Job Seekers in 2026

Job searching can feel like a full-time job itself. Sending out dozens of applications without hearing back is frustrating, and manually tailoring resumes and cover letters for each job takes too much time.


Job search apps and AI tools can make the process easier by automating applications, optimizing resumes, and helping candidates stand out.


> Whether you need a job board, an AI job application tool, or a resume builder, the right app can save time and improve your chances of landing interviews.


In this guide, we'll list out the 15 best job search apps to help streamline your search. Let's dive in!


## 15 Best Job Search Apps


Finding the right job search app depends on what you need help with. Some platforms focus on listing job openings, while others assist with applications, resumes, or interview preparation.


There are even tools that help with[tracking time off](https://attendancebot.com/) ! Regardless of what you're looking for, there's a tool available for your needs.


Here are the 15 best apps for different parts of the job search process:


### Job Search Engines


Job search engines collect job listings from multiple sources, making it easier to find and apply for positions in one place. These platforms let users filter job openings based on location, industry, experience level, and more.


Here's our three favorite job search engines:


#### 1. LinkedIn


[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/) is a networking platform that also functions as a job search engine. It allows users to apply for jobs directly, message recruiters, and research company profiles.


The Easy Apply feature speeds up applications by using a saved resume and profile details. LinkedIn also provides insights into job listings, such as how many people have applied and the skills employers are looking for.


#### 2. Indeed


[Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/m/jobs) aggregates job postings from company websites, job boards, and staffing agencies. Users can upload a resume, create job alerts, and apply directly through the platform.


The site includes salary comparisons and company reviews to help job seekers make informed decisions. Indeed also offers a mobile app for on-the-go job searching.


#### 3. Wellfound


[Wellfound, formerly AngelList](https://wellfound.com/) , is a job search engine[focused on startups](https://blaze.today/blog/best-ai-tools-for-startups/) and tech companies. It connects job seekers with hiring managers and startup founders, often without needing a traditional resume.


Salary ranges and equity details are listed upfront, helping candidates understand compensation before applying. Wellfound also supports remote job searches for those looking for flexible work options.
