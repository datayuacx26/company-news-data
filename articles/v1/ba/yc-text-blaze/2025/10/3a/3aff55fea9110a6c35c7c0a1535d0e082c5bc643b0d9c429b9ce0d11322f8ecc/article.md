## Does LinkedIn Have a Resume Builder?


Yes, LinkedIn has a built-in resume builder, but it is only available to LinkedIn Premium users.


To access it, go to your profile, click “More,” and select “Build a resume.” You can choose a template, make edits, and download the resume.


However, since this feature is locked behind a Premium subscription, free users must rely on other methods, such as using an[AI resume builder](https://blaze.today/blog/best-ai-resume-builders/) to take their profile and turn it into a resume.


### Is LinkedIn's Resume Builder Good?


LinkedIn’s resume builder is only available to LinkedIn Premium users, making it inaccessible for those using the free version.


While it provides a quick way to[write a resume](https://blaze.today/blog/how-to-write-a-resume-with-ai/) , it has several limitations. The templates are basic, customization is minimal, and it simply transfers your LinkedIn profile details without optimizing them for readability, applicant tracking systems (ATS), or specific job applications.


> For users who don’t want to pay for LinkedIn Premium,[free AI job search tools](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-job-application-tools) can be a better option.


AI tools do more than just format your LinkedIn profile—they analyze job descriptions, suggest keyword improvements, and rewrite bullet points to highlight your most relevant skills and experience. They can even[generate cover letters](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-cover-letter-generators/) !


If you need a tailored resume that stands out, an AI resume builder provides more flexibility and customization without the cost of LinkedIn Premium.

## Pro Tip: Tailor Your Resume to Each Job Description


A generic resume won’t stand out to recruiters or pass applicant tracking systems (ATS).


[Tailoring your resume](https://blaze.today/blog/tailor-resume-to-job-description-ai) to each job description increases your chances of getting noticed by highlighting the most relevant skills, experience, and keywords.


> Many companies use ATS to filter applications, so an optimized resume ensures your application reaches a hiring manager.


AI tools can make this process easier by analyzing job descriptions and suggesting improvements. They can compare your resume to a job posting, identify missing keywords, and rewrite bullet points to better match the role.


Here’s what to tailor in your resume:


- Adjust job titles or descriptions to match the language used in the job posting
- Add relevant keywords to improve ATS compatibility
- Highlight key achievements that align with the role’s responsibilities
- Reorder bullet points to emphasize the most relevant skills first
- Modify the summary section to reflect the specific job opportunity


Customizing your resume for each job ensures you present yourself as the best candidate for the role while improving your chances of passing resume screening systems.
