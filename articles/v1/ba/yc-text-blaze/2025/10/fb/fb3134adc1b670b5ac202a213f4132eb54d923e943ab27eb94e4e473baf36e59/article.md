## How to Have AI Review Your Resume


Using AI to review your resume is a straightforward process that helps improve its quality and effectiveness. Most AI resume checkers follow similar steps, from giving your resume to AI to receiving actionable feedback.


Here’s a breakdown of how to use AI for resume review.


#### Give Your Resume to the AI


Most AI resume checkers require you to provide your resume in a supported format, and some can read it directly from the page.


The AI scans the document to assess structure, formatting, and readability. Some tools also allow direct text input, making it easy to paste and analyze resume content.


Ensuring your resume is in an accepted format helps the AI provide an accurate review.


#### Make Sure Your Resume Is Tailored to the Job You Are Applying For


Before submitting your resume, make sure it is[tailored to the job](https://blaze.today/blog/tailor-resume-to-job-description-ai) you’re applying for.


AI can help identify missing skills, irrelevant details, and areas where your experience doesn’t match the job description. Some tools compare your resume against job postings and suggest industry-specific keywords to improve relevance.


If your resume lacks key qualifications, AI may recommend ways to highlight transferable skills.


#### Get AI Feedback and Recommendations


After analysis, the AI provides detailed feedback on strengths and weaknesses. It may suggest keyword additions, formatting changes, or sentence restructuring.


Some tools offer job-specific recommendations to help tailor your resume for specific roles. Reviewing this feedback helps you identify necessary changes for a stronger resume.


#### Make Changes Based on AI Suggestions


Use the AI’s recommendations to update your resume effectively. Focus on fixing any formatting issues, weak phrasing, and missing keywords.


Ensure that your resume remains professional and easy to read while improving its alignment with job descriptions. Applying these changes increases your chances of passing ATS scans and impressing recruiters.


#### Use AI to Rewrite or Create a New Resume


If your resume needs more than just small fixes,[AI can rewrite](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-resume-rewriter) sections or generate a completely new version. Some AI tools can restructure your resume to improve clarity, highlight achievements, and ensure proper formatting.


Not only can[AI check resumes](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-resume-checker) but it can even[build resumes](https://blaze.today/blog/best-ai-resume-builders/) and[cover letters](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-cover-letter-generators/) too!


> If you don’t have a resume yet, AI can create one from scratch by asking for your experience and skills. This makes it easier to build a professional resume quickly.


#### Recheck for Final Optimization


After making edits, give your resume to the AI again for a final review. This step ensures that the adjustments improved your resume’s readability and keyword optimization.


Some AI tools allow multiple scans to refine your resume further. Running a second check helps confirm that your resume is polished and job-ready.

When using an AI resume checker, you can get the best results by guiding it to focus on key areas. Clearly telling the AI what to check for ensures your resume is optimized for job applications.
