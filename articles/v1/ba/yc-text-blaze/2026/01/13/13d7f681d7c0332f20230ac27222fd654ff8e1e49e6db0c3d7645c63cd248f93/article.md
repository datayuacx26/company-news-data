College application essays carry a lot of pressure, and it is easy to second-guess every sentence you write. You want your essay to sound clear, honest, and polished, but catching your own mistakes after reading the same draft over and over is tough.


Using an AI essay checker can help you review your application essays more carefully by pointing out grammar issues, unclear phrasing, and areas that need stronger structure.


A college essay checker or AI proofreader gives you feedback that helps you refine your ideas without changing your voice.


In this article, we’ll cover the 5 best free GradGPT alternatives for college essays in 2026.


## What is GradGPT?


[GradGPT](https://www.gradgpt.com/) is an[AI essay checker](https://blaze.today/blog/gradgpt-alternatives/blaze.today/blog/college-essay-checkers-and-reviewers) designed to help students review and improve their academic writing. It focuses on checking grammar, clarity, tone, and structure to make essays stronger and more readable.


Students use it as a college essay reviewer when they want feedback that feels more specific than basic spell check. It can also be used as an[AI proofreader](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-proofreaders/) to catch small errors before turning in assignments.


### Is GradGPT Good?


GradGPT can be useful when you want quick[feedback on grammar](https://blaze.today/blog/best-free-ai-grammar-checkers/) , clarity, and basic structure. It works well as a starting point for reviewing drafts and catching obvious mistakes.


However, some students look for tools that go deeper into writing quality, such as stronger sentence suggestions, better organization feedback, or more detailed explanations.


Others want an AI essay checker that provides more guidance on improving arguments and flow. That is usually when students start exploring alternatives with more advanced proofreading and feedback features.


### Is GradGPT Free?


GradGPT offers a free plan, but it only includes its AI counselor and application guides. To get access to essay editor features, you have to upgrade to a paid plan.


> To get full access to GradGPT, you have to upgrade to a paid plan, which is $24/month or $144/year.


For students who write often, these limits can become noticeable. That is why many students look for other free AI essay checker options.


## How to Choose a GradGPT Alternative


When choosing a GradGPT alternative, it helps to focus on tools that support your full editing process, not just quick corrections.


Here are some things to check when looking for a GradGPT alternative:


-


**How much you can use it for free** - Look for tools that let you review multiple essays without running into strict limits too quickly. This matters when you are working on drafts, revisions, and final versions.


-


**How well it catches your mistakes** - A strong alternative should catch grammar errors, spelling issues, and awkward phrasing that you might miss on your own.


-


**How helpful the writing suggestions are** - The tool should give ideas for improving clarity, sentence structure, and flow, not just point out what is wrong.


-


**Whether you can ask it questions** - Being able to chat with the AI about your essay helps you understand feedback and make smarter revisions.


Focusing on these features helps you find a tool that supports real improvement. The best option should guide your writing while still leaving you in control of your work.
