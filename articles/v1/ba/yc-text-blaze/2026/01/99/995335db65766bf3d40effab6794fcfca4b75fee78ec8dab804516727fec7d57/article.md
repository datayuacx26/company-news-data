## Prompts to Use With an AI Study Helper


Using the right prompts can make AI study tools more effective. The quality of the prompts you give the AI matters.


> Instead of asking vague questions, providing clear and specific instructions helps the AI generate better responses.


Here are some useful prompts to get the most out of an AI study helper.


-


Summarize this article in five bullet points, focusing on key concepts and important details.


-


Explain this topic in simple terms, using examples to make it easier to understand.


-


Create a study guide based on this document, organizing the information into sections with key takeaways.


-


Generate 10 flashcards with questions and answers to help memorize this topic.


-


Break down this problem step by step, explaining each part clearly so I can understand how to solve it.


-


Compare and contrast these two concepts, highlighting their differences and similarities.


-


Rewrite these notes in a clearer and more concise way, making them easier to review later.


Giving specific instructions like these helps AI study tools provide better explanations, summaries, and study materials.
