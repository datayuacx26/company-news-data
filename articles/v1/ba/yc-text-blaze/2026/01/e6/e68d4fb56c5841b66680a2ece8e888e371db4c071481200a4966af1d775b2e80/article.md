[StudyBuddy AI](https://www.studybuddy.gg/) is an[AI study tool](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-study-tools) that helps students with homework, explanations, and productivity. It works as an[AI Chrome extension](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-chrome-extensions/) that appears on school websites to offer help on questions and assignments.


Students can[get quick answers](https://blaze.today/blog/best-free-ai-answer-generators/) , writing support, and even[make study guides](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-study-guide-maker) right inside their browser. It’s meant to make studying more efficient without needing to jump between tools.

## How to Choose an Alternative to the StudyBuddy Extension


Not every[AI study helper](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-study-helpers) works the same, and some just aren’t worth your time. If you’re switching from StudyBuddy AI, there are a few key features that make a real difference when it comes to homework and studying.


If you are looking for an alternative to StudyBuddy's Chrome extension, keep these features in mind:


-


**Free to Use** - You shouldn’t need to pay just to get basic help with questions or assignments.


-


**Helps with Multiple Subjects** - A good tool should support math, science, writing, and more.


-


**Works Anywhere in Your Browser** - Chrome extensions are easier to use while you're already on school websites.


-


**Explains the Answer, Not Just Gives It** - Look for tools that actually show how the answer was found.


These features make it easier to stay focused, save time, and actually learn while doing your homework. The more helpful the tool, the less stressed you’ll be during the school week.

#### 2. Coursology


[Coursology](https://coursology.com/) is a homework helper that supports questions in math, science, and English. Students can upload problems or type them out to get step-by-step explanations.


[Tools like Coursology](https://blaze.today/blog/coursology-ai-alternatives) focus on making tough topics easier to understand rather than just handing over an answer. Coursology has a free version with limitations and paid plans for full access.


#### 3. TurboLearn AI


[TurboLearn AI](https://www.turbolearn.ai/) helps students study smarter by turning notes and materials into flashcards and summaries. Instead of just answering homework questions, it breaks down the material so it’s easier to review.


You can paste in reading passages or class notes, and the tool will explain key points. This is especially helpful for test prep or end-of-unit reviews. TurboLearn AI has a free plan with some limitations as well as paid plans that give access to all features.


#### 4. YouLearn AI


[YouLearn AI](https://www.youlearn.ai/) is designed like a study partner that chats with you to explain concepts. It can help with writing, grammar checks, and even generating practice quizzes.


The tool keeps things conversational, which can make it easier to understand difficult subjects. Students can use it to work through questions step-by-step rather than copying answers. YouLearn has a free plan that offers two chats a day. To get more, you have to upgrade to a paid plan.


#### 5. SmartSolve AI


[SmartSolve AI](https://smartsolve.ai/) focuses on walking students through solutions instead of just giving them. It supports typed questions and image uploads for homework across various subjects.

#### 6. StudyX

#### 8. Gauth AI (GauthMath)


[Gauth AI](https://www.gauthmath.com/) is mainly used for math help and lets you take a photo of the problem to get a breakdown of how to solve it. It supports everything from basic algebra to more advanced math topics.

It’s mainly useful for reviewing content before a test or organizing study sessions. Study Fetch is free for students to use.
