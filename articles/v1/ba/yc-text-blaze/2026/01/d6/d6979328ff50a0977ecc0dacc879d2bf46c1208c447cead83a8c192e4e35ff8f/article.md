## What is Coral AI (now Ivory Mind)?


[Coral AI](https://www.getcoralai.com/) , now known as Ivory Mind, is an AI document tool designed to help you read, analyze, and summarize files. People use it to ask questions about PDFs, extract key points, and understand long documents faster.


It’s commonly used for research, work documents, and study materials. The goal is to reduce the time spent reading and manually pulling information from files.


### Is Coral AI Free?


Coral AI offers a free plan, but it only includes 2 free file uploads. After that, you'll be prompted to upgrade to a paid plan.


> To get unlimited file uploads, you have to upgrade to a paid plan, which is $30/month or $144/year.


If you rely on AI document summarizers often, these limits can become a problem. Check out the alternatives below for tools with more affordable pricing options.


## How to Choose a Coral AI Alternative


When comparing a Coral AI alternative, it helps to think about how you actually work with documents day to day. Free access is important, but so is how much freedom you have before running into limits.


Here are a few key things to consider:


-


**Free usage that doesn’t feel restrictive** - Look for tools that let you upload more files and keep using them without hitting walls right away.


-


**File types you already work with** - A solid option should[summarize PDFs](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-pdf-summarizer) , Word documents, and other common formats without extra steps.


-


**No page or document caps** - Tools that don’t limit how many pages or documents you can summarize are much easier to rely on over time.


-


**Asking real questions about your files** - Being able to ask follow-up questions and get clear answers from the document makes summaries far more useful.


Focusing on these areas makes it easier to pick a tool that fits your workflow and doesn’t slow you down as your document needs grow.
