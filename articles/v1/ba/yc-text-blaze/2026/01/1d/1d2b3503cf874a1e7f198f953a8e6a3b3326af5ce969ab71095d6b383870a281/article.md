### 2. Study Fetch


[Study Fetch](https://www.studyfetch.com/) helps students turn their class materials into study tools like flashcards and quizzes. You can upload notes, textbooks, or slides and then ask the AI questions based on your own content.


[Tools like Study Fetch](https://blaze.today/blog/study-fetch-alternatives/) can be helpful when you want your study sessions to stay focused on what’s actually going to be on the test. It works best if you already have some materials ready to upload.


### 3. Revisely


[Revisely](https://www.revisely.com/) focuses on reviewing written work like essays or homework assignments. It gives feedback on sentence structure, clarity, and grammar, helping students clean up their writing before submitting it.


While it doesn’t build study guides or flashcards, it’s a good option for students in writing-heavy classes. It can also be used to practice improving drafts and spotting mistakes.


### 4. Studyable


[Studyable AI](https://studyable.app/) turns your notes and documents into interactive learning tools. It can make quizzes and flashcards based on what you upload, giving you new ways to study the same material.


You can track your progress and organize study content by subject.[Tools like Studyable](https://blaze.today/blog/studyable-ai-alternatives) are helpful if you want to stay active while reviewing and not just reread notes.


### 5. Penseum AI


[Penseum AI](https://penseum.com/) creates study outlines, revision plans, and short summaries based on any topic or class material. Students can paste in their content, and the tool breaks it down into smaller chunks that are easier to study.


It’s good for setting up study sessions that feel more structured. If you prefer a more visual and organized way to prep for tests, this tool can help keep things on track.

## 15 Prompts for Studying With AI


Getting the most out of an AI study helper depends on how you ask questions. The more specific you are, the more helpful the response will be.


These prompts are designed to help you review topics, understand concepts, and prep for tests more effectively. Feel free to copy them so you can get more out of your AI tool:


1.


Summarize this topic in a way that makes it easier to review before a test: \[insert topic\].


2.


Turn these notes into 5 flashcards with clear questions and answers: \[paste notes\].


3.


Make a list of key terms and definitions from this chapter: \[insert topic or text\].


4.


Break down this concept into 3 simple parts I can remember: \[insert concept\].


5.


Create a 5-question quiz to help me test my understanding of this topic: \[insert topic\].


6.


Help me understand the difference between these two ideas: \[insert concepts\].


7.


Turn this material into a short study guide I can review in 10 minutes: \[insert content\].


8.


List the top 5 things I should focus on when studying this topic: \[insert topic\].


9.


Make a study schedule for the next 3 days using these topics: \[insert list\].


10.


Explain this in simple terms, like you're helping a friend who missed class: \[insert topic\].


11.


Give me an example problem and walk me through how to solve it: \[insert subject/topic\].


12.


Create a comparison chart that shows how these ideas are similar and different: \[insert topics\].


13.


Generate 3 practice questions that cover the key points from this lesson: \[insert notes\].


14.


Help me organize this information into categories I can memorize more easily: \[insert list\].


15.


Highlight the most important points from this article or textbook page: \[insert text\].
