## What is Quizlet?


[Quizlet](https://quizlet.com/) is a tool that helps you study with study guides, flashcards, and practice tests for different school subjects.


It's a popular study app that students use to practice for upcoming exams or just study certain topics by generating study materials.


Here’s a quick overview of what Quizlet does:


-


**Creates flashcards** – Lets you make or use pre-made flashcards to review key terms and concepts.


-


**Generates practice tests** – Automatically builds quizzes and tests to help you prepare for exams.


-


**Offers study modes** – Includes games and adaptive learning modes to improve memorization.


-


**Supports collaboration** – Allows students and teachers to share study sets and work together.
