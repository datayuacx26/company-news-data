## Magical


### Overview


[Magical](https://www.getmagical.com/) is a tool that focuses on[workflow automation](https://blaze.today/blog/workflow-automation/) and agentic AI. It used to be focused on text expansion, but has since pivoted to make workflow automation & data transfer features its primary offering.


[Tools like Magical](https://blaze.today/blog/magical-text-expander-alternatives/) are typically used by people are interested in integrating AI into their workflows in an attempt to save time and cut down on repetitive work.


### Pricing


Magical no longer has a free plan. It uses a subscription model for both individuals and teams.

### Advanced Features


In addition to regular[text expansion](https://blaze.today/blog/best-text-expanders-chrome-windows-mac) , Magical recently added a new AI feature that helps you transfer data and automate workflows.


For example, it can help you extract data from a page (like a CRM), and then transfer it to another page (like a form or document).


If you're looking for an[AI assistant](https://blaze.today/blog/best-ai-writing-assistant-software/) , it's a solid option for automating your work.


### Portability


Magical is currently only available as a Chrome extension and as a[Microsoft Edge extension](https://blaze.today/blog/microsoft-edge-extensions/) .


### Team collaboration


On Magical's Advanced plan, you can share folders with your team and assign permissions. This is useful for teams who want or need admin controls over their team-wide folders.


If you need custom usage reporting, you'll have to inquire about Magical's Enterprise plan.
