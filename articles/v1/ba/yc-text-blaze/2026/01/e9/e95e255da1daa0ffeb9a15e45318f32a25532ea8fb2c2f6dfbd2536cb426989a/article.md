## What is Studocu?


[StuDocu](https://www.studocu.com/en-us) is an online platform where students can find and share study materials, including lecture notes, summaries, and exam guides. It provides access to user-generated content from various universities worldwide.


The platform allows students to upload their own notes in exchange for access to premium materials. It also offers[AI tutoring](https://blaze.today/blog/best-ai-tutors) ,[notes summarization](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-notes-generator-and-summarizer/) , and study assistance.


### Is Studocu Legit?


Yes, Studocu is a legitimate platform that has been around for a while. It provides real study materials from students and verified contributors. Many users find it helpful for exam prep and assignments.


### How Much is Studocu?


Studocu offers both free and premium access. The free plan provides limited access to documents, while the paid plan unlocks full access.
