## Prompts for Using AI Math Problem Solvers


Getting accurate and useful answers from an AI math solver depends on how you phrase your questions.


> Being specific and including details can help the AI provide step-by-step solutions instead of just an answer.


Whether you're solving algebra equations, finding derivatives, or working with limits, structuring your prompts correctly makes a difference.


Here are some example prompts organized by math topic:


#### Algebra


- Solve for x in the equation 3x² - 5x + 2 = 0 and show all steps.
- Factor the expression x³ - 4x² + 5x - 2 and explain the process.
- Simplify the algebraic expression (2x + 3)(x - 4) - (x² - x + 2).
- Expand and simplify (x - 2)² + (x + 3)(x - 3).


#### Derivatives


- Find the first and second derivative of f(x) = 5x⁴ - 3x² + 7 and explain each step.
- Determine the critical points of f(x) = x³ - 6x² + 9x and classify them as maxima, minima, or points of inflection.
- Compute the derivative of f(x) = sin(x) * e^x using the product rule.
- Use implicit differentiation to find dy/dx for the equation x² + y² = 25.


#### Integrals


- Evaluate the definite integral from 0 to 2 of (3x² - 2x + 1) dx and show all steps.
- Solve the indefinite integral ∫ (x³ - 4x + 7) dx and include the constant of integration.
- Compute the integral of e^(2x) sin(3x) dx using integration by parts.
- Find the area under the curve f(x) = 4 - x² from x = -2 to x = 2.


#### Calculus


- Find the equation of the tangent line to f(x) = x² - 3x + 2 at x = 4.
- Determine the local and absolute extrema of f(x) = x³ - 3x² - 9x + 5 on the interval \[-2, 4\].
- Compute the Taylor series expansion of f(x) = ln(1 + x) around x = 0 up to the third-degree term.
- Find the arc length of the function f(x) = sqrt(x) from x = 1 to x = 4.


#### Limits


- Evaluate the limit as x approaches 0 of (sin(x)/x) and explain why it works.
- Determine whether the function f(x) = (x² - 4)/(x - 2) has a limit at x = 2 and justify the answer.
- Compute the limit as x approaches infinity for (5x³ - 2x + 7) / (3x³ + 4x² - 1).
- Use L'Hôpital's Rule to evaluate the limit of (e^x - 1)/x as x approaches 0.


Using specific and detailed prompts ensures that AI math solvers provide clear and accurate solutions.
