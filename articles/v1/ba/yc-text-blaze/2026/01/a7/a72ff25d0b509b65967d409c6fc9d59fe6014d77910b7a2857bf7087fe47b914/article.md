## What is the Feynman Technique?


[The Feynman Technique](https://subjectguides.york.ac.uk/study-revision/feynman-technique) is a learning method that helps break down complex concepts into simpler explanations. The goal is to teach a topic as if explaining it to someone else, ensuring a deep understanding.


This technique follows four steps:


1. **Choose a Concept** – Pick a topic you want to understand.
2. **Teach It Simply** – Explain it in your own words as if teaching a beginner.
3. **Identify Gaps** – Find areas where your explanation lacks clarity and refine them.
4. **Simplify Further** – Keep breaking it down until it’s easy to understand.


Many AI study tools, including Feynman AI, use this approach to help students learn more effectively.

Users can input a topic or ask questions, and Feynman AI will break it down in easy-to-understand terms. It also identifies gaps in knowledge and offers suggestions for improvement. This makes it a useful tool for studying, reviewing notes, and preparing for exams.
