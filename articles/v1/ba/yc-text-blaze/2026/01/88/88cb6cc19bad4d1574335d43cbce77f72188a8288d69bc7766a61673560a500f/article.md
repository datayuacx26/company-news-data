## What is Humata AI?


[Humata AI](https://www.humata.ai/) is an[AI document summarizer](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-document-summarizers/) that lets you upload PDFs and ask questions about them. Instead of reading the full document, you can ask for summaries, explanations, or specific details.


It’s commonly used for research papers,[legal documents](http://blaze.today/blog/legal-workflow-automation-software) , and reports. The goal is to save time and make long documents easier to understand.


### Is Humata AI Free?


Humata AI offers a free plan, but it comes with usage limits. It only includes access to basic features, and you can only summarize up to 60 pages and get 10 answers.


If you work with large PDFs or need frequent summaries, those limits can become a blocker. If that's your situation, check out the tools below for options with cheaper plans.


## How to Choose a Humata AI Alternative


Not all AI PDF summarizers work the same way, so it helps to know what to look for before switching. The right tool depends on how often you[summarize documents](http://blaze.today/blog/ai-document-summarizers) and what features matter most to you.


Here are a few things to look for:


-


**Free usage limits** - Some tools reset daily, while others cap pages or questions per document.


-


**PDF interaction** - Look for tools that let you[chat with PDFs](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-tools-to-chat-with-pdfs/) instead of only generating summaries.


-


**File support** - Check whether the tool works with scanned PDFs, large files, or multiple documents at once.


-


**Ease of use** - A simple upload and chat flow makes a big difference if you use it often.


Thinking through these points can help you avoid switching tools again later. A good Humata AI alternative should fit naturally into how you already work with documents.
