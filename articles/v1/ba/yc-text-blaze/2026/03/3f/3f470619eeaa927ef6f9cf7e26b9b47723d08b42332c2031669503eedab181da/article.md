### Merlin AI


[Merlin AI](https://www.getmerlin.in/) lets students use[ChatGPT for homework](https://blaze.today/blog/chatgpt-homework-solvers/) across any website to ask questions, summarize articles, or solve problems. It's helpful when you're reading something confusing and need a quick breakdown.


Because it works across tabs, it fits naturally into your study flow. It’s often used to clarify reading assignments or get writing support on the spot.


### HIX AI Tutor


[HIX AI Tutor](https://hix.ai/) focuses on teaching, not just solving. When students enter a problem, the tool explains how to work through it step by step.


[Tools like HIX AI](https://blaze.today/blog/hix-ai-alternatives/) are especially useful for math and science topics where the process matters. HIX also provides review questions to reinforce what you’ve just learned.


### Coursology AI


[Coursology AI](https://coursology.com/) gives real-time answers by letting you upload or take pictures of your homework. It supports a wide range of school subjects and responds with both a solution and an explanation.


[Tools like Coursology](https://blaze.today/blog/coursology-ai-alternatives) are often used for math and science, but it can also help with word problems and written questions. It’s a go-to for students who prefer scanning worksheets instead of typing.


### MathGPT


[MathGPT](https://math-gpt.org/) is built specifically for solving math problems and showing the logic behind each step. You can paste in equations or upload screenshots of your work.


Instead of just giving the final answer, it explains how to reach it. That makes it useful for learning how to show your work in class.


### Caktus AI


[Caktus AI](https://caktus.ai/) combines writing, coding, and subject help in one place. Students often use it to draft essays, generate code for assignments, or break down textbook questions.


It's not a direct Q&A tool, but more of a support tool for longer homework tasks. Many use it to save time on multi-part projects that involve writing or research.


### Quizard AI


[Quizard AI](https://quizard.ai/app) helps students create flashcards and quizzes from class material. You can snap a photo of a worksheet or paste in notes, and it turns that content into questions.


[Tools like Quizard](https://blaze.today/blog/quizard-ai-alternatives/) are more focused on review than solving, so it works well for studying before tests. Students often use it to remember facts or practice with vocabulary.


### Solvely AI


[Solvely AI](https://solvely.ai/home) solves homework problems fast while giving a clear explanation. You can upload questions by taking a photo or typing them out.


It’s used mostly for math and science, especially when you need a quick way to understand the answer. The tool is built for speed but still shows how it got the solution.
