## What is N8N?


[N8N](https://n8n.io/) is an open-source tool designed for[workflow automation](https://blaze.today/blog/workflow-automation/) . It allows you to connect different apps and services to run processes automatically, without needing to code everything from scratch.


The platform also supports AI agents, giving you the ability to integrate AI into your workflows for smarter automation.


### What is N8N Used For?


N8N is mainly used for building automations that save time and reduce manual effort. It offers flexibility through different features, including AI agents, workflows, and templates.


##### AI agents


N8N lets you create AI agents that handle tasks like summarizing content, answering queries, or analyzing data automatically.


##### Workflow automation


You can connect apps and services to[build workflows](https://blaze.today/blog/workflow-automation-examples) that run in sequence, replacing repetitive manual work. Example: onboarding a new employee (email, adding to relevant channels, etc).


##### Templates


N8N provides ready-to-use templates that help you set up automations quickly without starting from scratch. It also offers many different integrations to connect with sites your team uses.


N8N can be a really powerful tool, but it can be difficult to learn if you don't have an engineering background. That's why many people look for tools with lower learning curves.
