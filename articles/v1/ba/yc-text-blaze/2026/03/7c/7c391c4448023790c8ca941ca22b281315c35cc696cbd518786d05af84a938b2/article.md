## What is Automation Anywhere?


[Automation Anywhere](https://www.automationanywhere.com/) is a[workflow automation software](https://blaze.today/blog/workflow-automation/) focused on robotic process automation, often called RPA. Businesses use it to automate repetitive, rule-based tasks that would otherwise require manual effort.


It is commonly used by operations, finance, HR, and[IT teams](https://blaze.today/blog/it-process-automation-tools) to handle high-volume processes. The platform is designed for both attended and unattended automation scenarios.


It's primarily intended for enterprise-level organizations, which can often leave smaller teams wanting more.


### What Does Automation Anywhere Do?


Automation Anywhere allows teams to build software bots that interact with applications the same way a human would. These bots can log into systems, move data between tools, and trigger actions based on predefined rules.


Organizations use it for tasks like invoice processing, data entry, and report generation. It also supports workflow automation that connects multiple systems together.


### Automation Anywhere Pricing


Automation Anywhere requires you to book a demo to get learn their updated pricing.


For many[small businesses who want to automate workflows](https://blaze.today/blog/workflow-automation-software-for-small-business/) , this price is too steep. It's mainly geared toward teams who are ready to implement enterprise-level automation.


If you work for a small team and are looking for ways to automate workflows, the tools below might work better for you.
