## AI Notes FAQ


### Types of Notes to Generate With AI


AI-powered note generators are versatile tools that can produce different types of notes based on the user’s needs.


Here are some of the most common types of notes that AI can help generate:


-


**Lecture Notes** - AI can capture and condense key points from classroom lectures or presentations, making it easier for students to review content.


-


**Study Notes** - Generates summaries from textbooks or documents, turning lengthy content into quick[study notes](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-study-notes-generators) .


-


**Meeting Notes** - Provides a summary of discussion points, decisions, and action items from business meetings, ideal for keeping teams aligned.


-


**Clinical Notes** - Transcribes patient conversations and relevant information for healthcare professionals, aiding in documentation.


-


**Brainstorming Notes** - Captures ideas during brainstorming sessions, helping users organize thoughts and follow up on specific points.


-


**PDF to Brainrot** - Some students like to use AI to turn PDFs (and complex topics) into simple, easy to understand videos or summaries, also known as brainrot.


These note types make AI tools adaptable to various professional and academic contexts. By understanding these types, users can select the best AI tools for their unique note-taking needs.
