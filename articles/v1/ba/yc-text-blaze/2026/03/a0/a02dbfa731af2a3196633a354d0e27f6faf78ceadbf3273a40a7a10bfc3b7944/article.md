[StealthWriter AI](https://stealthwriter.ai/) focuses on rewriting text to make it sound less like AI and more like you. Instead of major changes, it applies light but smart edits to make writing feel less robotic.


[Tools like StealthWriter](https://blaze.today/blog/stealthwriter-ai-alternatives) modify sentence structure and vocabulary, often improving the flow in the process. You don’t get a lot of customization, but they work well for making AI writing sound more human.


#### Walter Writes AI


[Walter Writes AI](https://walterwrites.ai/) offers a quick way to humanize content by focusing on sentence rhythm and word choices. It scans your input and rewrites it in a tone that feels more personal and less automated.


The output usually sounds casual and natural, which helps reduce the chance of being flagged. While[tools like Walter Writes AI](https://blaze.today/blog/walter-writes-ai-humanizer-alternatives) don't allow much control over the style, the simplicity is part of what makes them work.


#### Humanize AI


[HumanizeAI](https://www.humanizeai.io/) is a simple tool that rewrites AI-generated content. It adjusts phrasing, sentence structure, and vocabulary to better reflect how people actually write.

[Phrasly AI](https://phrasly.ai/) gives users multiple rewriting styles to choose from, including casual, formal, and neutral. This makes it useful for tailoring content to fit the tone of different assignments.
