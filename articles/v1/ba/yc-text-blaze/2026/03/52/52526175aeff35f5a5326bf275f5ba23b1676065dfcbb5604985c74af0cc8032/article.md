## Example AI Prompts for Grading, Feedback, and Lesson Planning


AI tools are only as helpful as the prompts you give them. Make sure you give high-quality prompts to get good output.


Here are a few prompts you can try out:


-


Grade this student’s paragraph and explain the score using a 4-point rubric. Include comments on grammar, structure, and clarity.


-


Write a short, personalized comment for a 6th grader who needs help organizing their ideas. Keep the tone supportive and offer one clear suggestion.


-


Create a 3-day lesson plan about ecosystems for 5th grade. Include one hands-on activity, a short quiz, and a wrap-up discussion.


-


Summarize this 8th grade reading passage in under 100 words. Focus on the main idea and two key details using student-friendly language.


-


Rewrite this assignment prompt to be more clear and age-appropriate for 4th graders. Keep the instructions simple and avoid complex vocabulary.


-


Translate this classroom announcement into Spanish for parents. Use polite, clear language that’s easy to understand.
