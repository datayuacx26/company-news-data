### 2. Class Companion


[Class Companion](https://classcompanion.com/) focuses on grading and feedback for written assignments. It gives teachers pre-written comment suggestions that can be tweaked and reused across students.


You can also set up rubrics and have AI assist in aligning feedback to each criterion.[Tools like Class Companion](https://blaze.today/blog/class-companion-alternatives/) are especially helpful when dealing with long-form student writing.


### 3. Eduaide AI


[Eduaide AI](https://www.eduaide.ai/) is designed for lesson and content generation. You can use it to quickly brainstorm lesson objectives, activities, or bell ringers.


It also has a bank of instructional strategies that can be tailored to your subject or grade level. It’s most useful for planning lessons faster without starting from scratch.


### 4. Brisk Teaching AI


[Brisk Teaching](https://www.briskteaching.com/) integrates directly with platforms like Google Docs and Google Classroom. It helps generate student feedback, adapt reading levels, and track writing progress over time.


[Tools like Brisk Teaching AI](https://blaze.today/blog/brisk-teaching-ai-alternatives) highlight areas for improvement and offers suggestions you can review and edit.

### 6. Khanmigo


[Khanmigo](https://www.khanmigo.ai/) is an AI tutor and assistant created by Khan Academy. It supports both students and teachers, offering explanations, lesson help, and teaching tips.


Teachers can use it to guide students during assignments or plan instruction using Khan Academy materials. It’s best for classrooms that already use Khan Academy regularly.


### 7. Twee AI


[Twee AI](https://twee.com/) is geared toward English and language teachers. It helps generate grammar exercises, vocabulary quizzes, and other classroom activities.


You can also create speaking prompts and warm-up tasks to fit your lesson goals. It’s a practical choice for teachers looking to save time prepping materials.
