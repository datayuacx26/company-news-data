# 7 Thank You Email After Interview Examples

Sending a thank you email after an interview is a small but significant gesture that can make a big difference in the job application process.


> Thank you emails after an interview can really set you apart, as[nearly one-third of people don't bother to send them](https://topresume.com/career-advice/post-interview-thank-you-importance) .


Though, crafting the right thank you email can be challenging, especially when you're unsure what to include.


In this article, we'll cover when to send thank you emails after an interview, how to write them, and provide 7 examples you can use today.


## When to Send Thank You Email After Interview


Timing is crucial when sending a thank you email after an interview. Ideally, the email should be sent within 24 hours of the interview.


> The goal is to ensure that the email arrives while the interview is still recent enough for the interviewer to remember you clearly.


Sending it too late can make the email seem like an afterthought, which could diminish its impact. Being timely also demonstrates your ability to follow up and manage time effectively, both of which are valued in any job.


#### How Soon to Send Thank You Email After Interview


Knowing when to send a thank you email after an interview is key to making a good impression.


Here's a short guide on the best timing based on common interview times:


-


**Morning Interviews (8 AM - 10 AM)** : Send the thank you email by early afternoon, around 1 PM. This shows promptness and keeps you fresh in the interviewer's mind.


-


**Late Morning Interviews (10 AM - 12 PM)** : Send the email by late afternoon, around 3-4 PM. This timing is perfect as it ensures the interviewer receives it before wrapping up their day.


-


**Early Afternoon Interviews (12 PM - 2 PM)** : Send the thank you email by the evening, around 6-7 PM. This gives you time to reflect on the interview and craft a thoughtful message.


-


**Late Afternoon Interviews (2 PM - 4 PM)** : Send the email the next morning, ideally before 10 AM. This allows you to write a well-considered email without rushing, while still being prompt.


-


**Evening Interviews (4 PM and later)** : Send the email the next morning, by 10 AM. This timing ensures your message is one of the first things the interviewer sees when they start their day.


By following these guidelines, you can ensure your thank you email is timely and impactful, increasing your chances of leaving a positive impression.

## How to Write a Thank You Email After an Interview


Writing a thank you email after an interview involves several important steps to ensure it leaves a positive impression. The email should be professional, yet personable, and it should clearly convey your appreciation and interest in the position.


Here’s a breakdown of what to include in your thank you email:


-


**Clear subject line** : Include your name and the position you interviewed for.


-


**Express gratitude** : Start by thanking the interviewer for their time and the opportunity.


-


**Mention specifics** : Refer to something specific from the interview to show attentiveness.


-


**Reiterate enthusiasm** : Highlight your interest in the role and how your skills align with the job.


-


**Professional closing** : End with a polite closing, expressing your hope to hear from them soon.


-


**Proofread carefully** : Ensure there are no typos or errors before sending.


Following these steps will help you create a thank you email that is both effective and memorable. It’s a simple yet impactful way to strengthen your candidacy and leave a lasting impression.


Crafting this email thoughtfully can be a key factor in moving forward in the hiring process.
