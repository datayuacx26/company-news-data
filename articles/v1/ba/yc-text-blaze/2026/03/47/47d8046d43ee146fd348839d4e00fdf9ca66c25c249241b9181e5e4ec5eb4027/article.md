## What is Make.com (Formerly Integromat)?


[Make.com (formerly Integromat)](https://www.make.com/en) is a[workflow automation platform](https://blaze.today/blog/workflow-automation/) that helps people connect apps and move data without coding. It was previously known as Integromat before being rebranded as Make.


The tool is built around visual scenarios, where different apps and services are linked together to complete tasks automatically. Make is used to simplify processes, cut down on repetitive work, and streamline how their tools interact.


### What is Make Used For?


Make is used to build and[automate workflows for businesses](https://blaze.today/blog/business-automation-tools) that move information between apps. It can sync data across CRMs, spreadsheets, and email platforms, automate marketing and communication tasks, generate reports from multiple sources, and coordinate project updates.


It is also applied in customer support workflows, where it can trigger actions like ticket creation or[data entry](https://blaze.today/blog/data-entry-software-programs/) . The tool is generally used when there is a need to reduce manual handling of repetitive digital tasks.


### Is Make Free?


Make offers a free plan, but it only includes 1,000 credits/month. If you want to setup fully automated workflows, you'll hit that limit pretty quickly.


While it is useful, the limited free plan leads some users to look at other tools that might have a more expansive free plan.

## How to Choose a Make Alternative


Choosing the right Make alternative depends on your needs. Some tools focus on simple workflows, while others are better for advanced AI[workflow automation](https://blaze.today/blog/workflow-automation-examples) .


Here are a few things to consider before making a choice:


-


**How easy it is to use** - The setup should feel straightforward so you can build workflows without spending too much time learning.


-


**Whether it works on the sites and platforms you use daily** - A smaller set of relevant integrations is more valuable than hundreds you’ll never touch.


-


**AI features that actually help your work** - If you need AI workflow automation, check whether the platform includes the type of AI support you’ll use.


-


**Free plan limits** - Each platform sets different restrictions, so make sure the free version gives you enough room to test and run workflows.


Taking a moment to review these factors will make it easier to pick an alternative that actually fits the way you work.
