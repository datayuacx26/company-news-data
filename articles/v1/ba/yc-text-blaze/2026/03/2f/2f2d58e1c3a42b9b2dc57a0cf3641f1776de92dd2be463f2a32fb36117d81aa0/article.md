## How to Use AI to Improve Your Writing


If you're trying to make your AI-generated writing sound more human, there are a few simple steps that can help. These tips break the process down into clear actions you can take.


Here's how to use AI to improve your writing:


#### Write or Generate Your First Draft


Start by writing or generating a first draft of your content. It helps to just get it all written before working on improvements.


Focus on getting your ideas down clearly without worrying about sounding too polished. You’ll refine the tone and wording in the next steps.


#### Rewrite Using a Humanizer


Paste your text into a tool that helps you improve it. These tools reword your content to use more human-like phrasing and sentence flow.


> Rewriting your content using an AI humanizer helps you improve your writing and make it sound less robotic.


#### Check the Output and Content


Use tools to scan the rewritten text. These will grade your content and sometimes give suggestions for improvement.


You can also made some slight changes yourself. If there's something you don't like, try adjusting it so that it fits what you're looking for.


#### Make Light Manual Edits


Even after using a rewriting tool, adding your own tweaks can help, even if it's just a couple changes.


Change up sentence starters, simplify long phrases, or adjust the tone. A few human touches can make a big difference.


#### Test and Repeat as Needed


Sometimes you’ll need to test and tweak your content a few times to get to a good final draft.


Don’t rely on just one rewrite. The more natural the final text reads, the better chance it has of passing.
