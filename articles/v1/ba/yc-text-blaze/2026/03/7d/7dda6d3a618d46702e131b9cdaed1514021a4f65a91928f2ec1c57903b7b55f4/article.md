## 20 Useful AI Prompts for Homework, Writing, and Studying


To get the most out of any AI homework helper or AI study tool, it helps to give specific and clear prompts.


Here are 20 improved prompts you can use for studying, writing, and assignments.


1. Solve this math problem step-by-step and explain why each step is necessary: \[insert problem\]
2. Summarize this article in 3 bullet points and include the main argument: \[insert article or text\]
3. What’s the main idea of this paragraph, and why is it important in the overall topic? \[insert paragraph\]
4. Turn these notes into flashcards with definitions and examples: \[insert notes\]
5. Create an outline for a 5-paragraph essay on this topic: \[insert topic\]
6. List 3 arguments for and 3 against this topic, with one sentence of support for each side: \[insert topic\]
7. Explain this science concept in simple terms and give a real-world example: \[insert concept\]
8. Turn this list of terms into a quiz with multiple-choice questions and correct answers: \[insert terms\]
9. Give 5 key takeaways from this reading and explain why they matter: \[insert reading\]
10. Create a study guide from this material and organize it into sections: \[insert content\]
11. Write an introductory paragraph for an essay on this topic, including a clear thesis: \[insert topic\]
12. Check this paragraph for grammar and clarity, and suggest better sentence structure: \[insert paragraph\]
13. Simplify this explanation so it’s easier to remember, and add a short summary: \[insert explanation\]
14. Compare and contrast these two ideas with a table showing 3 differences and 2 similarities: \[insert topics\]
15. Based on these notes, what questions should I expect on the test? \[insert notes\]
16. Generate 5 multiple-choice questions and answers based on this text: \[insert lesson or reading\]
17. Write a thesis statement and 3 supporting points for this essay topic: \[insert topic\]
18. Find two examples or facts that support this argument and explain how they help: \[insert argument\]
19. Write a short conclusion paragraph for an essay on this topic: \[insert topic\]
20. Based on this chapter, what are the most important concepts I should review for the exam? \[insert chapter notes or text\]
