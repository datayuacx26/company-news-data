## What Are Report Card Comments?


Report card comments are short, personalized statements teachers use to describe a student’s academic performance, behavior, and overall progress.


They help communicate strengths, highlight areas for improvement, and give clear guidance to both students and parents. Effective comments are specific, easy to understand, and focused on helping students continue to grow.


For example, report card comments are great for subjects like[math](https://blaze.today/blog/math-report-card-comments/) or[ESL](https://blaze.today/blog/esl-report-card-comments/) , as they help students learn what they can do to improve.


## How to Write Report Card Comments (Best Practices)


Writing effective report card comments is about being clear, balanced, and helpful. Following a simple structure can make the process faster while improving the quality of your feedback.


Here's how to write report card comments:


1.


**Start with strengths** – Begin each comment by highlighting what the student is doing well to set a positive tone.


2.


**Be specific** – Focus on particular skills, behaviors, or patterns rather than general statements.


3.


**Keep it balanced** – Include both strengths and one or two areas for improvement.


4.


**Use simple language** – Make comments easy for both students and parents to understand.


5.


**Stay concise** – Keep comments clear and to the point so the main message is easy to follow.


6.


**Personalize when possible** – Tailor comments to each student instead of relying on overly generic phrasing.


Using these best practices helps ensure your comments are meaningful, easy to understand, and useful for both students and parents.


## Common Mistakes to Avoid in Report Card Comments


Even experienced teachers can fall into patterns that make comments less effective. Being aware of common mistakes can help you write clearer and more[useful feedback](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-feedback-generator) .


Here are some common mistakes to avoid in report card comments:


-


**Being too vague** – Comments like “doing well” or “needs improvement” don’t provide helpful insight.


-


**Focusing only on negatives** – Even struggling students should have strengths acknowledged.


-


**Writing overly long comments** – Too much detail can make feedback harder to understand.


-


**Using repetitive phrasing** – Repeating the same structure across comments can feel impersonal.


-


**Not giving actionable feedback** – Comments should help guide improvement, not just describe performance.


-


**Overusing generic templates** – Templates should still be customized to reflect each student accurately.


Avoiding these mistakes will make your report card comments more clear, balanced, and effective.
