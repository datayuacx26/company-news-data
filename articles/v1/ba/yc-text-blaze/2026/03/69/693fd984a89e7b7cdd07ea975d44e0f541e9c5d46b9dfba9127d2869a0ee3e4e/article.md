## What Does Data Extraction Software Do?


Data extraction software takes unstructured information from emails, PDFs, receipts, and other documents and turns it into clean, usable data. This helps reduce manual entry, improve accuracy, and speed up workflow automation.


Here are the main things data extraction software can do:


-


**Pull data automatically** - Capture details from emails, PDFs, and scanned files without manual copy-paste.


-


**Organize information** - Convert raw text into structured fields that fit your workflow.


-


**Transfer data into apps** - Send extracted details straight into spreadsheets, CRMs, or automation platforms.


-


**Save time on repetitive work** - Eliminate hours of manual entry for recurring documents like invoices or receipts.


-


**Improve accuracy** - Reduce the risk of typos and human errors in your workflows.


By using data extraction tools, you free up time for more important work while keeping your processes accurate and efficient.

[Docparser](https://docparser.com/) specializes in parsing PDFs and documents into structured data. Users can set up parsing rules to automatically extract fields such as names, dates, or amounts.

#### 3. Mailparser


[Mailparser](https://mailparser.io/) focuses on email-based data extraction. It allows users to capture data directly from incoming emails and attachments.


Once extracted, the[data can be exported](https://blaze.today/blog/autofill-chrome-extensions) to spreadsheets or connected to apps through integrations. This makes it especially valuable for businesses that receive a lot of order confirmations, lead emails, or support tickets.
