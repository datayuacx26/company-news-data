## Tips & Prompts for Generating Summaries For Your LinkedIn Profile


Crafting a LinkedIn summary can feel overwhelming, but AI tools make it easier by providing a strong starting point.


To get the best results, it’s important to guide the AI with clear prompts and tailor the output to align with your goals.


Below are practical tips and example prompts to help you create a LinkedIn summary that captures attention and highlights your strengths.


#### 1. Generate a Summary From Your Personal Resume


Your resume is an excellent starting point for generating a LinkedIn summary. Give your resume to an AI tool and prompt it to create a summary based on your professional experience.


Focus on adapting your resume’s content to a conversational tone. Unlike a resume, your LinkedIn summary should feel approachable and engaging.


> Ensure the tone aligns with your industry—for instance, a creative professional may use a more casual style, while a corporate executive may prefer formal language.


Finally, review the generated summary to ensure it reflects your goals and personal brand.


#### 2. Highlight Your Career Goals


A great LinkedIn summary doesn’t just focus on past achievements; it also looks to the future. Use an AI tool to generate a summary that aligns with your aspirations.


Be specific about what you want to achieve, whether it’s mastering new skills, entering a new industry, or advancing in your current field. This helps recruiters understand your ambitions and match you with relevant opportunities.


Don’t be afraid to show enthusiasm—expressing passion for your goals can make your summary more compelling. Always review the AI output to ensure it accurately represents your vision.


#### 3. Use Keywords Strategically


LinkedIn’s search algorithm relies heavily on keywords, so make sure your summary includes terms relevant to your industry. An AI generator can help identify and incorporate these keywords effectively.


Including the right keywords improves your profile’s visibility to recruiters and hiring managers.


> However, avoid keyword stuffing—your summary should still flow naturally. Focus on terms that describe your skills, tools you use, or industries you specialize in.


Once the AI generates your summary, cross-check it against job descriptions in your field to ensure you’ve covered the essential terms.


#### 4. Tailor the Tone to Your Audience


Your LinkedIn summary should resonate with your target audience, whether it’s recruiters, clients, or collaborators. Use AI tools to adjust the tone of your summary based on who you want to impress.


Consider your industry norms when choosing the tone. A tech professional might lean towards concise and technical language, while someone in creative fields might opt for a more conversational style.


Don’t hesitate to experiment with different tones using the AI tool, but always ensure the final version feels authentic to you.


#### Example Prompts for Generating LinkedIn Summaries


The results are only as good as the prompts you give the AI, so make sure to use the tips above to write good prompts.


Here are some example prompts to use with AI LinkedIn summary generators:


-


**Resume-Based Prompt:** Using my resume, create a LinkedIn summary that highlights my achievements in project management, focusing on delivering projects on time and within budget.


-


**Career Goals Prompt:** Write a LinkedIn summary that emphasizes my goal of transitioning into a leadership role in the tech industry, showcasing my problem-solving skills and team management experience.


-


**Keyword-Focused Prompt:** Generate a LinkedIn summary with a focus on digital marketing, including keywords like SEO, content strategy, and analytics, to improve profile visibility.


-


**Tone-Tailored Prompt:** Create a professional yet approachable LinkedIn summary for a marketing consultant, reflecting creativity and expertise in client engagement.


-


**Custom Combination Prompt:** Write a LinkedIn summary that combines my past experience in sales with my future goal of building a career in product management, ensuring a conversational tone and including relevant keywords like customer engagement and product strategy.
