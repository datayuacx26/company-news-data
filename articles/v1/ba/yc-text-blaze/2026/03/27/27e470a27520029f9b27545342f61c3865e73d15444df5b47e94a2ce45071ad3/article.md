## What is an Email Parser?


An email parser is software that scans emails and pulls out important details like names, dates, or tracking numbers. It automates the process of sorting and structuring data so it can be used in spreadsheets, CRMs, or other[workflow automation tools](https://blaze.today/blog/workflow-automation/) .


It helps you take data from emails and quickly transfer it to other place that you need it in. They reduce the burden of manual[data entry](https://blaze.today/blog/data-entry-software-programs/) /data transfer.


### Are Email Parsing Software Worth Using?


Email parsing software is often worth using if you rely on email as a key source of information. It eliminates repetitive manual work and ensures important details are captured consistently.


Here are the benefits of using email parsing software:


-


**Save your time on repetitive tasks** - Automating extraction reduces the need to copy and paste information from emails.


-


**Cut down on mistakes in your data** - Parsing reduces human error by consistently identifying and pulling key fields.


-


**Make your workflows run smoother** - Parsed data can automatically trigger actions in tools like CRMs or project management software.


-


**Keep all your email details organized** - Email data can be structured and stored in one system for easy access.


By automating these steps, email parsing software makes workflows smoother and more reliable.


## What is Mailparser?


[Mailparser](https://mailparser.io/) is an email parsing software that extracts data from emails and attachments. It allows users to set custom parsing rules and direct the data into apps, spreadsheets, or databases.


Think of it as the[Zapier](https://blaze.today/blog/zapier-alternatives/) of email data transfer. It automatically takes data from your emails and transfers it somewhere else for you.
