## What is Grubby AI?


[Grubby AI](https://grubby.ai/) is an[AI humanizer](https://blaze.today/blog/best-free-ai-humanizers) that rewrites text to make it sound more human.


It’s designed to change the tone, sentence structure, and vocabulary of AI-generated text. You can paste in text or upload files, then the AI will analyze it and edit it for you.


### What Does Grubby AI Do?


Grubby AI takes text and rewrites it in a way that sounds more human. Here are a few of the primary use cases:


-


Humanizing text (making it sound more natural)


-


Checking/proofreading with AI (checking for mistakes)


-


Rewriting (writing new versions of text according to your requirements)


These types of tools are especially useful for students submitting AI-assisted work. It doesn’t write essays for you but helps you rewrite them.
