## How to Use AI to Check & Grade Essays & Papers


Using AI to check and grade your essays is straightforward, but there are a few steps you should follow to get the most out of these tools.


Below are key tips on how to effectively use AI essay checkers to improve your writing.


#### Choose the Right AI Tool


The first step is selecting the right tool that fits your needs. Some AI checkers focus more on grammar and spelling, while others provide detailed feedback on structure and readability.


Make sure to pick a tool that offers the features most relevant to your essay or paper. If possible, choose one that includes a plagiarism checker as well.


Check out our suggestions for thebest free AI essay checkers & graders .


#### Add Your Prompt


When using AI tools, many allow you to add a prompt or specify the focus of your essay. This could include keywords, the essay question, or a specific instruction for the tool to follow.


By adding your prompt, you give the AI more context to analyze your essay accurately. This step helps tailor the feedback to your specific writing goals or assignment guidelines.


These tools can even[generate full essays](https://blaze.today/blog/best-ai-essay-generators/) for you if you use them properly.


#### Review Feedback Carefully


After the AI analyzes your essay, it will provide feedback on areas that need improvement. This might include grammar corrections, sentence restructuring, and suggestions for improving clarity.


> Take the time to carefully review each suggestion before making changes. Some feedback may not always apply to your writing, so it’s important to use your judgment.


#### Revise and Run Again


Once you’ve made revisions based on the AI’s feedback, consider running the essay through the tool a second time. This allows you to catch any new errors that may have been introduced during editing.


It also ensures that your revised essay meets the tool's criteria for quality and clarity. Re-running the essay can be a quick way to double-check your work before submission.
