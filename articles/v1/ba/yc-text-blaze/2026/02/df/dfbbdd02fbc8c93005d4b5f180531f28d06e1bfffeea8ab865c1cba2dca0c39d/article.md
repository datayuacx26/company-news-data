## What is Liner AI?


[Liner AI](https://getliner.com/) is a tool that helps users highlight,[summarize](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-summary-generator/) , and search information across the web. It's an AI research tool and search engine that helps streamline the process of academic research.


Though, it may not work for everyone. Some students needs cheaper options, or just require a tool with different features.


### What Does Liner Do?


Liner offers a few key features that are useful for students trying to save time and stay organized during research. It combines multiple tools into one interface to help with reading, searching, and note-taking.


Here are a few things that Liner offers:


-


**AI search engine** - Gives short, AI-generated answers from the web when you type in a question.


-


**AI research** - Helps you explore topics by pulling relevant summaries and related info.


-


**AI summarizer** - Shortens long articles, papers, or websites into quick, digestible takeaways.


-


**Answer questions** - Lets you ask follow-up questions for clarity without needing to search again.


These features can make research easier, especially when you're working with limited time or juggling multiple sources.
