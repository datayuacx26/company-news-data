## How to Generate an Essay or Outline With AI


If you're not sure how to guide an AI essay writer, it helps to break the process down. Giving clear instructions and making small edits can go a long way in getting better results.


Follow these steps to generate an essay or[outline](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-essay-outline-generator) with AI:


##### 1. Start With a Detailed Prompt


Begin by typing a specific, detailed prompt. Include your topic, the type of essay, and any points you want it to cover.


##### 2. Add Your Thesis or Key Ideas


If you already have a thesis or main arguments, include those. This helps the AI stay on topic and support your main points.


You can list them as bullet points or in a sentence. AI works better when it knows what direction you’re going in.


##### 3. Choose the Output Format


Decide whether you want an outline, body paragraphs, or a full essay. An outline is good for planning, while a full essay saves time.


Pick what works best for where you’re stuck. You can always ask for more later.


##### 4. Read and Edit the Output


Don’t just copy and paste the results. Read through what it gave you and fix anything that feels off.


You can also run it through a grammar checker or[rewrite tool](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-essay-rewriter) if it feels robotic. Think of it as a first draft, not a final version.


##### 5. Use Citations When Needed


If you're writing about facts or[research in your essay](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-essay-research-paper-writers-checkers/) , ask the AI to add citations. Some tools can format in APA or MLA for you.


Just be sure to double-check the sources—it doesn’t always get them right. Adding real citations makes your essay stronger.


### Pro Tip: Humanize Your AI-Generated Essay


AI-generated essays can sometimes sound too stiff, repetitive, or just plain awkward.


That’s why we recommend using AI humanizers. They can make your writing feel more natural and less like it was written by a robot.


Just be careful: don’t rely on AI to do all the work. Always read through the final result, check facts, and make sure it still reflects your thoughts.
