### 2. LazyApply


[LazyApply](https://lazyapply.com/) automates job applications by applying to multiple postings with one click. It works with job boards like LinkedIn and Indeed, autofilling application forms to save time.


Users can upload different resumes and cover letters to target various job types. The platform also includes basic application tracking to help users keep track of where they’ve applied.


This tool is useful for job seekers who prioritize speed and volume in their job search.


### 3. Jobright AI


[Jobright AI](https://jobright.ai/) focuses on improving the quality of job applications with AI-driven resume and cover letter customization.


It scans job descriptions and optimizes resumes to align with specific role requirements. The tool also helps users identify missing skills and suggests content improvements.


Some versions include interview preparation, offering AI-generated questions based on job postings. For job seekers who need stronger application materials, Jobright AI provides AI-powered assistance.


### 4. Sonara AI


[Sonara AI](https://www.sonara.ai/) continuously scans job listings and submits applications automatically. Users set job preferences, and the tool applies to matching roles on their behalf.


It also customizes resumes and cover letters to increase application relevance. The platform provides real-time notifications on application progress but does not offer detailed tracking.


Sonara AI is an option for job seekers who want applications sent out consistently without manual input.
