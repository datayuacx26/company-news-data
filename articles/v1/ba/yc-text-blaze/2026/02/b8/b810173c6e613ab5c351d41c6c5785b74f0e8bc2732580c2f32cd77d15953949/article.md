## What is Microsoft Power Automate?


[Microsoft Power Automate](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/products/power-automate) is an automation platform that connects apps and services to handle repetitive tasks. It is part of the Microsoft ecosystem and integrates directly with Office 365, Teams, and other tools.


Users create[automated workflows](https://blaze.today/blog/workflow-automation/) that can move data, trigger notifications, and manage approvals.


### What Does Power Automate Do? How to Use Power Automate


Power Automate connects different apps and services to automate routine work without the need for coding. You can build workflows, known as "flows," that save time by running tasks automatically once they are set up.


For example, you could create a flow that saves every email attachment you receive in Outlook directly to a specific folder in OneDrive. This way, instead of manually downloading and organizing files, the system handles it for you every time an email comes in.

Power Automate offers a 30 day free trial, but beyond that, you have to upgrade to a paid plan. While the free trial is nice, if you want to build useful automations, you likely need more than 30 days (building, testing, implementing, etc)

Prebuilt templates that let you set up common workflows quickly.

Performance issues when handling large amounts of data or complex processes.
