It’s mainly used for academic writing, emails, and research support. The tool can help[structure essays](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-essay-writer) ,[summarize articles](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-summary-generator/) , and give writing suggestions to save users time.

### Is HyperWrite Free?
