## Prompt Tips for Using AI to Generate Summaries of Text


Crafting the right prompts can significantly improve the quality of AI-generated summaries. By following these tips, you can ensure that your summaries are accurate, relevant, and tailored to your needs.


Here are a few things we recommend to do when writing prompts for generating summaries with AI:


#### Be Clear and Specific


Clearly state what you want the tool to summarize and the level of detail you need. Ambiguous prompts can lead to incomplete or irrelevant summaries.


> For example, specifying “summarize this article into three bullet points” provides the AI with clear guidance on the expected output.


#### Provide Context


Include additional details about the text or the purpose of the summary. Letting the AI know whether the summary is for a report, presentation, or quick review can improve the results.


Context helps the tool focus on the most relevant points based on your intended use.


#### Include Keywords


Incorporate important keywords or phrases related to the text to guide the AI’s focus. Keywords help the tool identify the main topics or themes of the text.


> For example, asking the AI to "summarize this article about renewable energy, focusing on solar power advancements" can refine the output.


#### Specify the Format


Let the AI know how you want the summary presented, such as bullet points, a paragraph, or numbered steps. Specifying the format helps ensure the summary is immediately usable without additional reformatting.


For instance, asking for "a 5-sentence paragraph summary" can produce a concise, cohesive output.


#### Request a Focus Area


If the text covers multiple topics, tell the AI to prioritize a specific area of interest.


For example, in a long article about technology trends, you can prompt, “Focus the summary on advancements in artificial intelligence.” This ensures the output aligns closely with your needs.
