## What is Monica AI?


[Monica AI](https://monica.im/) is a ChatGPT assistant that works across websites to help users write, search, and summarize content. It acts like a personal[AI writing assistant](https://blaze.today/blog/best-ai-writing-assistant-software/) , giving you quick replies, rewriting suggestions, and summaries without switching tabs.


It integrates into your browser and works in Google Docs, Gmail, and more. Monica AI runs on GPT-4 and other large language models to deliver smart, contextual help. It’s one of many[GPT Chrome extensions](https://blaze.today/blog/best-gpt-chrome-extensions/) built to make daily tasks easier with the help of AI.


However, it may not work best for everyone. Some people may want a cheaper option or a more seamless experience. That's why alternatives are worth looking into!


### Is Monica AI Free?


Monica AI has both free and paid plans. The free version comes with usage limits. If you want unlimited access, you will have to upgrade to a paid plan.


Users looking for more flexibility often explore other tools with fewer restrictions or better integrations.

### 2. Wordtune


[Wordtune](https://www.wordtune.com/) focuses on helping you rewrite sentences to make them clearer, shorter, or more formal depending on your needs. It offers real-time suggestions while you write in apps like Gmail and Google Docs.


The tool is centered around improving sentence structure and tone rather than generating long-form content. It's useful if you're mainly looking for help polishing your writing.


### 3. Jenni AI


[Jenni AI](https://jenni.ai/) is designed for long-form writing, especially for essays, reports, and blog posts. It provides sentence completions, content suggestions, and citation tools to help you build out ideas while staying focused.


The assistant works inside a dedicated editor, offering more structure than a browser overlay.[Sites like Jenni AI](https://blaze.today/blog/jenni-ai-alternatives/) are best suited for users who want an AI tool built around guided content creation.


### 4. Rytr
