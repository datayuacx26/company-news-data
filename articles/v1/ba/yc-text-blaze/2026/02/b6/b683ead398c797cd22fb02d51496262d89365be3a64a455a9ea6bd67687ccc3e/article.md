## What is Merlin AI?


[Merlin AI](https://www.getmerlin.in/) is an[AI Chrome extension](https://blaze.today/blog/ai-chrome-extensions/) that gives you[ChatGPT features](https://blaze.today/blog/best-gpt-chrome-extensions/) on any website. It’s built to help users quickly summarize content, generate replies, and answer questions without needing to switch tabs.


Merlin also includes tools like an AI detector,[writing assistant](https://blaze.today/blog/best-ai-writing-assistant-software/) , and search summarizer. Students use it mostly for research, writing, and studying.

#### How to Use Merlin AI


Merlin AI works directly in your browser. Once you install the extension, you can use shortcuts to activate it while browsing.
