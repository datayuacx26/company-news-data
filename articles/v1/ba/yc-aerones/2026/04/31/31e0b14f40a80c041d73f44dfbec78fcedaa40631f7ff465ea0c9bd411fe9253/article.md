## Aerones News & Updates: Bigger Facilities. Better Results. New Technology.


Welcome to the latest episode of Aerones News & Updates. This time, we are proud to share the next phase of growth – from expanded testing capabilities and record-breaking field performance to new international deployments and certified technology advancements at Aerones.


Watch the video below to explore the latest updates.


In this episode, you’ll learn about:


- Expanded R&D testing with four full-scale blades
- Launch of our Denton office and first U.S. Workshop & Demo Day
- Successful leading-edge repairs in France for TotalEnergies & Vestas, achieving 1.31 blades/day
- New robotic arm tested in Spain through the ROMAIN project
- Qualifoudre LPS certification for advanced lightning protection technology
