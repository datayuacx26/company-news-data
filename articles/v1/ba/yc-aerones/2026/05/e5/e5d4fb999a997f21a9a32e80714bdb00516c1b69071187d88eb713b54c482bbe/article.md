## Aerones TechTalk: Biggest challenges in leading edge repairs


Leading edge repair is evolving fast and two things are changing the game: robotic application method and application material. In this[#AeronesTechTalk](https://www.youtube.com/hashtag/aeronestechtalk) video, we break down why precision automation and the right material selection are critical for achieving consistent, high-quality repairs that improve durability, efficiency, and repeatability.


We’re also sharing our rain erosion test results for different materials, giving you a closer look at how various solutions perform under demanding conditions and why material choice matters in long-term protection.


Watch the full video to see how advanced processes, material performance, and testing are shaping the future of leading edge repair.
