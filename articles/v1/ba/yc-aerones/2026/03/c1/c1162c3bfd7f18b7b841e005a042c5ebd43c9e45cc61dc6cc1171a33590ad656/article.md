# Aerones Annual Workshop & Demo Day 2026 Recap

March 17, 2026


## Aerones Annual Workshop & Demo Day 2026 Recap


At the end of February, we hosted our first Aerones Annual Workshop & Demo Day at our newly opened facility in 📍 Denton, Texas, welcoming wind industry professionals for a day of insights, technology, and live demonstrations.


The program covered topics such as robotic leading edge repairs, AI in blade repair planning, safety in operations, and blade health management, along with a live demo of the Aerones Customer Platform and Visual Inspection Studio.


In the afternoon, guests saw our robots in action during LIVE leading edge repair demonstrations, showcasing the full process from grinding to final LEP application.
