- **Share buyback program**


On November 13, 2024, Cosan S/A approved its new Share Buyback Program, valid for 18 months.


The shares acquired through the Share Buyback Program will remain in treasury for subsequent sale or cancellation.


[Click here](https://www.cosan.com.br/en/investor-relations/disclosure/notices-and-material-facts/material-facts/) to access the Material Fact regarding the Share Buyback Program.


OPERATION DEADLINE:


18 months


MAXIMUM NUMBER OF SHARES THAT CAN BE REPURCHASED IN THE PERIOD:


Up to 115,000,000 (one hundred and fifteen million) shares representing
approximately 6.16% of the total shares and up to 9.99% of the
Outstanding Shares¹.


OUTSTANDING SHARES ON THIS DATE¹:


1,150,987,430 common shares


COMPANY’S PROFIT RESERVE:


R$ 7,894,393,676.38


*Note 1: On 10/31/2024*
