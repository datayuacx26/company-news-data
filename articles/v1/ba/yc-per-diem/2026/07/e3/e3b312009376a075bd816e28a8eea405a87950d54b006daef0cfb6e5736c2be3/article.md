# Per Diem Announces Mobile Ordering Launch for Oakberry Canada

‍


Per Diem announced the launch of the official[Oakberry Canada mobile ordering app](https://www.tryperdiem.com/mobile-app/oakberry-ca) . The app went live in June 2025 and is now used across multiple Oakberry locations in Western Canada. The project marks a significant step in Per Diem’s international growth and supports one of the most recognizable acai bowl franchises globally.


‍


[Oakberry Canada](https://www.oakberrycanada.ca/) is operated by Snowbank Brands Inc. Snowbank acquired the master franchise rights from Oakberry USA LLC to grow the Oakberry brand throughout Western Canada. The franchise group selected Per Diem to build a branded mobile experience that supports customer loyalty, ordering ahead, and fast payment.


Per Diem created a branded app that connects directly with Square POS, giving Oakberry’s customers a seamless way to order acai bowls and smoothies ahead of time, with pickup options and fast payment, including Apple Pay. The app also features points-based rewards and referral rewards. Oakberry uses the Per Diem marketing tools to run in-app promotions. **‍** ‍


Oakberry was[founded](https://oakberry.dk/en/om-os/) in Brazil in 2016 and focuses on a health-driven fast food concept built around organic vegan and gluten-free acai. The brand now operates in many countries, including the United States, Australia, China, Spain, Portugal, Peru, Saudi Arabia, and the United Arab Emirates. The global chain has nearly one thousand stores.


The Canadian expansion reflects the rapid growth of Oakberry in North America. The new mobile ordering app, powered by Per Diem, is designed to help the franchise group scale with consistency and give customers the ordering comfort they are used to from large chains across all their locations. The app aligns with Oakberry’s focus on convenience and quality while helping each store provide fast service during busy hours.


Per Diem builds branded mobile ordering and online ordering systems for restaurants, bakeries, and coffee shops that use Square POS. The platform gives merchants a clear way to offer ordering ahead, loyalty, rewards, and fast payment in one place. Per Diem works with independent and multi-location brands in the United States, Canada, Australia, and France.
