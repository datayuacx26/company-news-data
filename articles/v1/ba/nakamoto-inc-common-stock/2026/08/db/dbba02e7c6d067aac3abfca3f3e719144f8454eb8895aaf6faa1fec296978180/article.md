# Nakamoto Launches Intelligence 2027, a New Flagship Event for the AI Economy

*A Defining Forum for the Builders, Investors, and Institutions Driving the Future of AI*


**NASHVILLE, TN – August 12, 2026:** Nakamoto Inc. (Nasdaq: NAKA) (“Nakamoto” or the “Company”) today announced the launch of Intelligence 2027 (“i27”), which is anticipated to be an annual forum for leaders across the artificial intelligence economy. The inaugural flagship event will take place on April 22 – 23, 2027 at the Palace of Fine Arts in San Francisco, California bringing together founders, investors, enterprise executives, developers, policymakers, creators, and institutions shaping the next era of AI, from frontier models and infrastructure to enterprise adoption, consumer products, capital markets, and more.


The launch of i27 marks Nakamoto’s expansion of its industry leading events platform into one of the world’s largest and fastest-growing technology markets. Through its subsidiary BTC Inc., the Company has spent more than a decade building The Bitcoin Conference into the marquee annual gathering of the global Bitcoin ecosystem. i27 will apply that same operating model to AI through high-caliber programming, culturally relevant speakers, and platforming the most important conversations to allow all levels of attendees access to learning more about a technology that has and will continue to shape our future.


AI is no longer solely a software cycle. It is a revolution touching every corner of the global economy. i27 is designed to reflect that breadth, covering the full AI landscape, including business adoption, consumer products, developer tools, robotics, enterprise software, media and creative tools, investment, policy, education, security, infrastructure, and the cultural shifts created by increasingly capable intelligent systems. The event is expected to convene the full range of participants driving and responding to that transformation, from the companies building frontier systems to the enterprises deploying them, the investors backing them, and the policymakers governing them.


“We view i27 as a natural extension of the capabilities we have built within our events and media business. Artificial intelligence represents a massive and rapidly evolving market, and we believe there is a significant opportunity to create a premier annual gathering for the companies, investors, developers, and institutions driving its growth. Our team has a proven track record of building industry-defining events, and we are excited to bring that experience to one of most consequential technological shifts of our time.” says David Bailey, CEO and Chairman of Nakamoto.


Additional details regarding i27, including programming themes, speakers, sponsors, and ticket availability, will be announced in the coming months. For more information, visit[i27.ai](https://i27.ai/) .
