# $1.9M Funding Round

## *Our seed round includes an incredible group of investors and angels. We’ve raised from Y Combinator, Collab Fund, Transpose, Orange Collective, Pioneer Fund, and The 23 Fund. We’re also proud to welcome angels and operators from companies such as Amazon, Figma, Anthropic, Loom, Netflix, Meta, and OpenAI.*


#### **What We Do**


Overlap builds multimodal AI agents for media companies and video creators. Our first AI agent - a video marketing agent - can autonomously understand, search, and edit videos. This agent now helps dozens of companies and creators by instantly transforming their long videos into clips for social media. Over the last few months, our customers have seen a 10x increase in engagement while saving over 20 hours per week.


#### **So, What’s Next?**


Our seed round accelerates our mission to define the role AI will play in video workflows. With this capital, we’re gearing up to:


-


**Accelerate Product Development:** Enhance our multimodal AI agents and roll out new agents that make video editing even easier.


-


**Expand Our Team:** Bring on additional talent to accelerate our pace and support our growing community of customers.


-


**Scale Our Impact:** Reach more media companies, marketing teams, and podcasters, helping them drive even greater engagement with their audiences.
