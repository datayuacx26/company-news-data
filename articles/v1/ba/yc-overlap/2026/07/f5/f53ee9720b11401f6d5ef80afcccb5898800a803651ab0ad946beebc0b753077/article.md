# Frequently asked questions


## How does AI video editing help marketers?


It reduces repetitive production work like clipping, captioning, resizing, and formatting so teams can publish faster.


## Can these tools handle multiple aspect ratios?


Most of them can. Strong support for 9:16, 1:1, and 16:9 is now table stakes for marketing use cases.


## What matters most when choosing a tool?


Not the length of the feature list. The real question is whether you need editing assistance, repurposing, or workflow automation.


## What if AI makes mistakes?


Most tools allow manual review and adjustment. The best systems reduce the amount of cleanup required, but none eliminate review entirely.


## How do teams scale video production?


Teams scale by standardizing formats, reusing brand rules, and automating as much of the workflow as possible. That is where workflow-level tools outperform standalone editors.


## Which tool is best for a high-output marketing team?


If you want a tool that helps one person edit faster, a clipper or browser editor may be enough. If you want the output capacity of a much larger team, you need a platform built around automation, not just editing features.
