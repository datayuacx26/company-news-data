# Invesco Ltd. Announces July 31, 2026 Assets Under Management

ATLANTA


, Aug. 11, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Invesco Ltd. (NYSE:IVZ )1, a leading global asset management firm, announced today preliminary month-end assets under management (AUM) of $2,447.1 billion, a decrease of 0.9% versus previous month-end. The firm delivered net long-term inflows of $8.6 billion in the month. Money market net inflows were $22.8 billion. AUM was negatively impacted by unfavorable market returns which decreased AUM by $59 billion. FX increased AUM by $4.6 billion. Preliminary average total AUM for the quarter through July 31 was $2,453.0 billion, and preliminary average active AUM for the quarter through July 31 was $1,216.9 billion.
