# First Interstate Named Among America’s Top-Performing Banks

American Banker recognized First Interstate BancSystem, Inc. for its 2025 financial performance, disciplined growth, and long-term value creation.


Recognition Reflects 2025 Performance


First Interstate BancSystem, Inc. has been named to American Banker’s 2026 list of top-performing banks with $10 billion to $50 billion in assets.


American Banker developed the annual ranking with Capital Performance Group. The ranking evaluates banks using financial measures such as profitability and shareholder returns. The 2026 list is based on performance during 2025.


The recognition reflects our focus on relationship-based banking and disciplined, organic growth. Across our footprint, we help individuals, families, businesses, and agricultural producers work toward their financial goals. We also invest in the communities we serve.


Focused on Long-Term Strength


“We are honored to be recognized among the nation’s top-performing banks,” said Jim Reuter, President and Chief Executive Officer of First Interstate. “This recognition reflects the strength of our relationship-focused approach and our commitment to quality, organic growth. By staying focused on our clients, supporting our communities, and operating with discipline, we continue to build a strong foundation for long-term success. We are grateful to our clients for their trust and to our employees for the dedication that makes achievements like this possible.”


We remain focused on serving our clients, strengthening our communities, and creating long-term value for our shareholders.
