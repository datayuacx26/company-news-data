# First Interstate Earns Gold Badge on 2026 Where You Work Matters List

First Interstate earned a Gold badge on the 2026 Where You Work Matters List, a national rating that looks at how large U.S. employers create jobs with strong pay, career growth, and retention outcomes.


The Where You Work Matters List evaluates results across more than 1,750 employers and nearly 55,000 occupations, using career progression patterns drawn from more than 12 million workers.


“Great banking starts with great people,” said Rachel Turitto, Chief HR Officer at First Interstate. “When we invest in our employees’ growth and support, they can bring more skill, focus, and care to the work they do for clients and communities.”


What the List Measures


Where You Work Matters describes its list as a data-based look at job quality that focuses on real-world outcomes — including pay, mobility, and retention — rather than company-submitted surveys.


How We Support Employees


First Interstate offers[compensation and benefits](https://www.firstinterstatebank.com/company/about/employee-benefits) designed to support employees over the long term, including:


- A 401(k) plan with a 100% match on the first 6% contributed
- A company-paid child care assistance program
- A student debt repayment program
- A wellness program, along with other traditional benefits


Clients can learn more about the Where You Work Matters List and view the 2026 rankings at[whereyouworkmatters.org](https://www.whereyouworkmatters.org/) .
