# First Interstate Named to Two Newsweek America's Greatest Workplaces Lists

We earned recognition on two of Newsweek's 2026 America's Greatest Workplaces lists, ranking among the top community banks in the country for benefits and entry-level career opportunity.


About the Rankings


Newsweek and Plant-A Insights Group release the America's Greatest Workplaces rankings each year. The 2026 lists draw on independent research, including hundreds of thousands of employee surveys and millions of publicly available data points evaluating workplace culture, compensation, benefits, and career development.


We placed on two lists:


- **America's Greatest Workplaces for Perks & Benefits** — ranked 5th in banking nationally and 1st in Montana
- **America's Greatest Workplaces for Entry Level** — ranked 14th in banking nationally and 5th in Montana, the only Montana-based bank on the list


What This Means for Our Team


"Being recognized on two national Newsweek lists reflects what we aim to do every day at First Interstate — meet people where they are and help them get to where they want to be. This includes not only our clients and communities, but also our teammates," said Jim Reuter, President and CEO. "We have measurable goals for engagement and positions filled by internal promotions, with the idea being what we measure is what we move."


Benefits Built for Every Stage


In addition to competitive salaries and performance pay, we offer a benefits program designed to support team members throughout their careers:


- 401(k) with a 100% match on the first 6% contributed
- Company-paid childcare assistance
- Student debt repayment program
- Wellness program and other traditional benefits


Start Your Career With Us


Recognized as one of the top-ranked banks to work for in Montana, we're focused on building a workplace where people grow — from entry-level roles to leadership. Whether you're launching your career or looking for your next opportunity, explore open positions and learn what it's like to be part of #TeamFirstInterstate at firstinterstatebank.com/company/about/careers
