# Select Nebraska Branches Transitioning to Security First Bank in April 2026

As announced in October 2025, First Interstate Bank entered into an agreement for Security First Bank to acquire select First Interstate branches in Nebraska.


The transaction is scheduled to close on Friday, April 10, 2026. On Monday, April 13, 2026, these locations will open as Security First Bank branches.


What’s Changing


When the transaction closes, Security First Bank will assume the physical buildings, retail and business deposit accounts, loans, and safe deposit boxes associated with the transitioning locations.


Branches Opening as Security First Bank on April 13, 2026


- Alliance
- Atkinson
- Broken Bow
- Burwell
- Callaway
- McCook
- Norfolk
- O’Neill
- Ord
- Scottsbluff
- Sidney


What’s Not Changing


Wealth Management relationships, credit card accounts, and some mortgage loans will remain with First Interstate.


Key Dates


- **Friday, April 10, 2026:** Transaction closes.
- **Monday, April 13, 2026:** Listed locations open as Security First Bank branches.
