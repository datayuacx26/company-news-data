# Announcing Our Partnership with Foursquare: Launching the First AI Agent on the FSQ Places Engine

We're excited to announce our partnership with **Foursquare** to help them build the world's most accurate, open places dataset. Reprompt has built the **first AI-powered agent** integrated directly into Foursquare's **Places Engine** , designed to work alongside community Placemakers to verify and maintain place data with unmatched efficiency.


## Watch: Lukas and Vikram sit down to discuss the future of Location Data with AI Agents


## How Reprompt's AI Agent Improves Places


Reprompt's AI agent uses large language models to extract, verify, and propose updates to POI data. By sourcing information from the web and social media and validating it across multiple independent sources, the agent ensures every update meets Foursquare's high standards for accuracy.


Key highlights:


- **Multi-Source Verification:** Attributes require corroboration from three separate sources before being accepted.
- **Speed:** Tasks that once took 20 workdays can now be completed in under an hour.
- **Accuracy:** Our agent consistently achieves 97% accuracy, verified through human oversight.


## AI + Humans: A Winning Combination


This collaboration doesn't replace human contributors—it amplifies them. While the AI agent handles large-scale data extraction and validation, human raters focus on edge cases and complex decisions. Together, we're building a dataset that's accurate, dynamic, and ready for the real world.


## What's Next


This is just the beginning. As Foursquare opens up their **Placemaker Agent API** in private beta, we're laying the groundwork for other organizations to develop their own AI agents on top of the Places Engine.
