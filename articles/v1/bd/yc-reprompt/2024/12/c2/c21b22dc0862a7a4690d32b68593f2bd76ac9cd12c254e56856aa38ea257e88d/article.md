# Foursquare OS Places vs. Overture Maps

The rise of open POI (Points of Interest) data has transformed how businesses access location intelligence. Two major players have emerged: Foursquare OS Places and Overture Maps. In this post, we'll compare these datasets and show how Reprompt can help you leverage both.


## Overture Maps


Overture Maps is a collaborative effort backed by major tech companies to create an open, interoperable map dataset. Key characteristics:


- **Global coverage** with 60+ million places worldwide
- **Open license** allowing commercial use
- **Regular updates** from multiple data sources
- **Standardized schema** for easy integration


### Overture Challenges


- Data quality varies by region
- Limited attribute coverage
- Some categories have sparse data
- 4.5% of businesses have incomplete addresses


## Foursquare OS Places


Foursquare OS Places provides access to Foursquare's comprehensive places database. Key characteristics:


- **100+ million places** globally
- **Rich attributes** including hours, ratings, and categories
- **Verified data** from real user check-ins
- **Deep category taxonomy** with 900+ categories


### Foursquare Challenges


- 40% of global records may need refreshing
- Some regions have better coverage than others
- Certain niche categories are underrepresented
