# First Advantage Honored by Marketplace Risk Excellence Program for Background Screening

First Advantage has been honored by Marketplace Risk for their Excellence Program. Read the full press release by Marketplace Risk below.


---


*Top solution providers recognized for innovative strategies and technologies that strengthen the capabilities of marketplaces and digital platforms to protect consumers on the internet.*


“The Solution Provider Excellence Program reflects the critical role these providers play in enhancing trust & safety, and security across the internet. Our honorees develop solutions that tackle complex challenges and support business resilience in an increasingly connected world.” — **Jeremy Gottschalk, Founder, Marketplace Risk**


**SAN FRANCISCO, CA, UNITED STATES, May 28, 2026** — Marketplace Risk announced the honorees of its 2026 Solution Provider Excellence Program, celebrating leading figures in risk, trust & safety solutions for internet marketplaces and digital platforms. This annual initiative highlights tech companies with proven track records and extensive expertise in addressing the complex challenges these platforms face when protecting their users.


This year’s honorees provide innovative solutions dedicated to protecting businesses, customers, and stakeholders from dynamic business risks. They continue to set new industry standards through advanced strategies and technologies that strengthen the capabilities of marketplaces and digital platforms to protect their communities and the internet as a whole.


The program’s rigorous selection process, overseen by the Marketplace Risk Advisory Board, evaluates nominees on multiple criteria, including case studies demonstrating customer satisfaction and meaningful contributions to internet marketplace and platform improvement.


Jeremy Gottschalk, Founder of Marketplace Risk, commented: “The Solution Provider Excellence Program celebrates the essential work of solution providers who empower marketplaces to navigate complex risks effectively. These honorees combine expertise, data-driven insights, and innovative technologies to deliver measurable impact across trust & safety, compliance, and operational resilience.”


The 2026 recipients not only lead in their respective fields but also demonstrate ongoing commitment to innovation, leveraging analytics and cutting-edge technology to equip businesses with tools for proactive risk identification, mitigation, and strategic growth.


Marketplace Risk provides a global platform for education, networking, and collaboration among industry experts, leaders, and innovators. The Solution Provider Excellence Program offers honorees the opportunity to showcase achievements, share insights, and promote excellence in risk management, trust & safety, regulatory compliance, technology innovation, and legal strategy.


As businesses confront increasingly complex global challenges, the 2026 honorees stand as exemplars of success, enabling marketplaces and digital platforms to navigate uncertainty with confidence.


For more information about the Marketplace Risk Solution Provider Excellence Program and its honorees, please contactinfo@marketplacerisk.com .


### Marketplace Risk Solution Provider Excellence Program Honorees (2026)


- **Screening / Background Checks:** First Advantage
- **General Trust & Safety:** Concentrix
- **Fraud Prevention / Chargebacks:** Socure
- **Identity Authentication / Verification:** Prove, Veriff
- **Compliance / Legal Services:** LegitScript
- **Risk Management / Insurance:** G2 Risk Solutions
