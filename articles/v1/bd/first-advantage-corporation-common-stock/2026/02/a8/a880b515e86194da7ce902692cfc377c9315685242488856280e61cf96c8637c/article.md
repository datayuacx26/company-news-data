# Cybersecurity Expert and Legendary FBI Operative Eric O’Neill to Keynote First Advantage’s 10th Annual Collaborate Conference

*O’Neill Will Present “Spies, Lies & Cybercrime: Protecting Data in a Dark Web Economy”*


First Advantage has officially announced that cybersecurity expert and legendary FBI operative Eric O’Neill will keynote this April’s highly anticipated, invite-only Collaborate user conference.


Eric O’Neill is one of the nation’s most compelling voices on modern cyber risk. His career began in the FBI’s elite intelligence program, where he spent years conducting surveillance on terrorists and spies before going undercover in the historic operation to expose Robert Hanssen, the most damaging cyber spy in US history. His extraordinary story inspired the major motion picture “Breach” and his acclaimed book “Gray Day”, cementing his reputation as a leading authority on national security, cyber espionage, and the rapidly evolving threat landscape.


Today, Eric advises global organizations on protecting themselves from increasingly sophisticated cyberattacks, including AI‑driven threats that place both reputations and critical systems at risk. As an in‑demand keynote speaker, he brings unforgettable insights and first‑hand stories from the dark web to audiences ranging from Fortune 100 companies to major universities and international associations. Through his work at The Georgetown Group, media commentary, and his latest book, “Spies, Lies and Cybercrime”, Eric continues to shape how organizations think about cybersecurity, leadership, and resilience in a world where digital threats are accelerating faster than ever.


“We are thrilled to welcome Eric O’Neill as our keynote speaker at Collaborate 2026. Eric’s extraordinary experience at the intersection of national security, cyber intelligence, and AI-driven threat prevention brings a powerful perspective to the challenges our customers face today. His insights mirror our own commitment to trust, innovation, and staying ahead of the risks shaping the future of work. There is no better voice to help set the tone for this year’s event, and we couldn’t be more excited to have him with us.” -Joelle Smith, President.


First Advantage will celebrate 10 years of Collaborate with customers, partners, and guests April 28-30, 2026, at The Diplomat in Hollywood, Florida. Collaborate 2026 is sponsored by Experian, Fama, Ferretly, iCIMS, ID.me, Karini AI, Premier Biotech, SambaSafety, Seyfarth, and TransUnion.
