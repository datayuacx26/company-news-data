# First Advantage Recognized for Best Work-Life Balance by Comparably

First Advantage has been honored as a company for Best Work-Life Balance in the large companies’ category by Comparably.


Each quarter, Comparably, a platform dedicated to providing culture and compensation data for both public and private companies, conducts surveys to gather feedback from employees. The Best Places to Work Awards are based on these ratings, covering a variety of categories that reflect employee satisfaction and workplace culture.


First Advantage’s achievement in the work-life balance category highlights its efforts to prioritize employee well-being, offering flexible work arrangements and promoting a healthy balance between professional and personal life. As a largely remote organization, First Advantage has adapted to the evolving workplace landscape, allowing employees to work from home while maintaining productivity and collaboration.


First Advantage has received 19 Comparably Awards since 2021, including Best Company for HR Teams, Best Company for Career Growth and Best Company for Employee Happiness.
