Fall sports mean long days spent cheering on the sidelines, where the weather can pivot from a crisp chill to a blazing sun in the blink of an eye. “Lightweight layers are the ultimate styling essential for a busy mama. Think an easy tank or tee paired with a relaxed button-down, denim jacket or soft knit that can be added or peeled off, and won’t add bulk to everything else you’re carrying,” Stylist Jackie says.
