## **The Americana Aesthetic: Backyard BBQs + Coastal Getaways**


Whether you are headed to a park BBQ or a sandy beach, you can celebrate by leaning into the elevated side of the Modern Americana aesthetic. “Modern Americana is all about timeless pieces with a relaxed, current feel. Think: crisp whites, soft navy and subtle pops of red in breathable fabrics like cotton and linen,” Stylist Caro says. “The result feels polished without trying too hard—easy layers, clean lines and pieces that move seamlessly from day to evening.”


To modernize red, white and blue in a way that feels subtle and authentic, try color blocking with one or two shades or using a classic stripe to ground the look. Key pieces for a laid-back weekend include high-waisted denim shorts, relaxed linen sets, nautical stripes and breezy eyelet dresses.

## **Honoring the Military Community**


For **seasonal summer celebrations** that require more formal attire—such as visits to locations commemorating fallen heroes—opt for a look that is both polished and respectful. A structured midi dress paired with gold jewelry strikes the perfect balance of elegance and respect. “Keeping the overall look refined sets the right tone for tribute,” says Stylist Erin.


If your plans take you from a morning ceremony to an evening dinner, a light blazer or a soft knit sweater is an essential layer to keep your outfit intentional and weather-ready.
