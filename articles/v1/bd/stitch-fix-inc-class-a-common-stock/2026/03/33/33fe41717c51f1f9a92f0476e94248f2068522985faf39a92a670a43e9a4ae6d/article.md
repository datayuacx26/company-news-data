# The Event Edition: What to Wear to Every Dressy Spring 2026 Occasion

Spring is *party season* , and we’re so here for it. From Easter, Passover and Mother’s Day to showers, graduations and weddings, your calendar might already be looking jam-packed (phew!). We’re here to make[outfit planning for all of these occasions and more](https://www.stitchfix.com/women/blog/style-guide/spring-2026-fashion-trends) feel joyful and simple, not stressful.


Welcome to **The Event Edition** : your go-to guide for spring holiday outfit ideas, here to help you celebrate in pieces that feel modern, effortless and true to your style.


Whether you’re looking for what to wear on Easter Sunday,spring wedding outfit ideas or what to wear to a baby shower, we’ve got you covered with expert-approved advice that makes putting looks together easy.


To help, we’re putting common spring events into 3 simple style categories: garden parties, weddings and daytime socials. Most celebrations will fall under one of these—it might even be listed on the dress code. In this post, we’ll walk through these refined celebration moments and give you some expert guidance on dressing for each one.


## **The Modern Garden Party**


When the sun comes out and spring blooms begin to pop, so do opportunities for outdoor gatherings. This could be anything from Easter brunches to backyard birthdays and al fresco dinners, so the perfect garden party outfit combines the spring styles we *know* you’re excited to wear, with refined layers for fickle forecasts.


For casual garden party outfit ideas, it’s helpful to start by thinking about color and texture. Pastels are an obvious choice, and[this year, Stylist Caro advises leaning into softer versions of them](https://www.stitchfix.com/women/blog/inside-stitchfix/new-stitch-fix-blog-squad-and-stitch-fix-stylist/) : “For spring 2026, think muted pastels like sage green, peachy pink and dusty purple rather than sugary brights for a softer, modern feel.” Floral dresses are an ever-present spring staple, but a matching set can feel equally refined and perfectly on-trend.


When considering what to wear to a garden party, layers matter almost as much as your base. Style dresses or separates with light pieces like a cropped trench coat or soft, knit cardigan for spring layering outfits that transition beautifully throughout the day and will keep you comfortable if the breeze picks up. Ground your outfit with easy wedges or strappy sandals, and finish with a mini bag for polish: you’ll stay polished and on-point for every RSVP.


> “For spring 2026, think muted pastels like sage green, peachy pink and dusty purple rather than sugary brights for a softer, modern feel.”
> -Stylist Caro


## **The Elevated Wedding Guest**


Wedding season calls for a little something extra, and this year is no exception. Your style approach will vary depending on whether you’re attending a black-tie spring wedding or something[a little more laid back](https://www.stitchfix.com/women/blog/trends-and-occasions/wedding-attires-women/) , but regardless of dress code, you can definitely lean into these trends.


Cue the wedding march: romantic silhouettes and eye-catching prints that feel both elegant and current will reign supreme for formal wedding guest dresses this spring.


Polka dot dresses are classic, but that doesn’t mean they have to feel full-on vintage. “Smaller polka dots in airy fabrics like chiffon or silk lean more romantic than retro,” Stylist Jackie says. “Let the print be the star and keep everything else simple and refined.” Bias cut and midi-length wrap dresses are both flowy silhouettes that look great in photos and on the reception dance floor.


Updated floral prints remain a strong trend for spring wedding guest outfits too. Look for a soft, watercolor-esque floral dress, and pair it with delicate jewelry and strappy heels to let the print shine without distraction. It’s the perfect recipe for an outdoor ceremony or afternoon celebration.


> > “Smaller polka dots in airy fabrics like chiffon or silk lean more romantic than retro. Let the print be the star and keep everything else simple and refined.”
> > -Stylist Jackie


## **Daytime Socials**


March through June is the peak time for events that are not-quite-formal, not-quite-casual. These occasions call for daytime outfits that feel celebratory yet easy—you want to feel put together without being overdressed or spending hours on styling. We get it!


One easy solution for what to wear to a baby shower or[as a guest to a graduation ceremony](https://www.stitchfix.com/women/blog/trends-and-occasions/what-to-wear-to-graduation-as-a-guest/) ? “A matching set feels intentional and polished without overdoing it” says Stylist Caro. “It also makes styling super easy—just add shoes and accessories!”


A matching set also makes it easy to lean into seasonal motifs like pastels and springy prints. Elevated details like eyelet embroidery are another way to add texture and casual elegance—you’ll be perfectly on point as a guest at a wedding shower. Complete the look with kitten heels or modern wedges for a polished-yet-approachable feel.


If feminine silhouettes are more your vibe, tiered and poplin skirts or cream-colored bottoms styled with a blouse are another great option. Tops with romantic details like ruffles, lace trim or ruching are perfect for daytime celebrations that deserve a little dressiness.


> **“** A matching set feels intentional and polished without overdoing it. It also makes styling super easy—just add shoes and accessories!”
> -Stylist Caro
