# Your Guide to the Summer Romance Trend

The sun is shining and it’s finally time to give your closet that coveted main character energy. This summer, we’re swooning over the summer romance trend. With beautiful textures, effortless silhouettes and pieces that make you feel amazing, it’s the perfect wardrobe refresh. Our Stylists are here with the best pieces to help you lean into the trend and feel confident all season long. Let’s dive into the can’t-miss summer 2026 trend that your closet needs right now.


## **Summer Romance: Must-Have Pieces, Fabrics + Colors**


To help you nail this effortless vibe, we’ve rounded up the pieces that seamlessly blend comfort and polish. We are talking rich suede accessories, breezy smocked dresses, vintage-inspired blouses and the must-have clogs of the season—all tied together in a dreamy pastel color palette. Here is your quick cheat sheet to mastering the look with total confidence.


## **The Perfect Finish: Summer Suede**


If you thought suede was strictly for cooler weather, think again. This season, suede bags and clothing items, as well as mules in sun-bleached shades, are taking center stage as women’s accessory must-haves. Suede in any form brings a rich, touchable texture to lightweight outfits and offers a beautiful contrast to breezy silhouettes.


## **The Ultimate Silhouette: Smocked Dresses**


When temps rise, you deserve an outfit that delivers maximum style with zero effort. Enter smocked dresses: The ultimate summer style detail that transitions seamlessly from a sunny day out to a warm evening event. It’s the perfect piece to keep you feeling cool, comfortable and effortlessly polished.


## **Sweet Details: Pretty Feminine Blouses**


Nothing captures a carefree summer spirit quite like a beautiful, romantic blouse. Look for lovely details like eyelets, smocking and delicate lace trim—these details elevate even the simplest summer outfits. Feminine blouses are also much more versatile than you’d think, pairing well with anything from jean shorts to pencil skirts and more. They’re a key staple for adding a soft, vintage-inspired touch to your everyday look.


## **Grounded Elegance: Boho-Chic Clogs**


Move over wedges, because boho-chic clogs are officially the new heel of the season. They give you the perfect amount of lift without sacrificing comfort, making them ideal for everything from brunch to patio dinners.


“A smocked dress paired with wood clogs is the effortless combo of the summer,” Stylist Erin says, “Add a beaded bag to extend the artisanal feel.”


## **Sun-Kissed Hues: The Prettiest Color Palette**


To ground all of the above, this summer’s color story is all about soft, dreamlike tones that just feel romantic. We’re loving washed pastels like dusty rose, sunshine yellow and sky blue. Pair these shades back to crisp white, classic khaki and warm desert suede to keep your outfits looking fresh and feminine.
