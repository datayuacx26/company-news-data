# Celebrating Moms and Teachers with Favorite Daughter x Stitch Fix

From early morning alarms to afternoon dismissal, moms and teachers are the true heroes powering through the back-to-school season. You spend so much time caring for others, yet you rarely get the recognition you deserve. That’s about to change.


We worked with the co-founders Sara and Erin Foster of trending brand Favorite Daughter to create the exclusive, limited-edition Favorite Daughter x Stitch Fix collection. It celebrates your daily dedication with comfortable, stylish pieces that help you feel seen and valued.


## **A Collection Created for Moms and Teachers**


This collection is a true show of appreciation for women handling the heavy lifting of back to school.


> “I’ve always believed that the best clothes are the ones that make you feel like yourself,” says Sara Foster, co-founder of Favorite Daughter. “As a mom, there are so many moments where you’re focused entirely on everyone else—getting the kids out the door, keeping up with schedules and trying to balance it all. It’s incredibly easy to forget about yourself.”
>
>
> “This collaboration with Stitch Fix is about creating those chic, effortless, everyday pieces that make you feel confident without adding another decision to your day. Whether it’s school drop-off or whatever comes next, getting dressed should feel like a small moment of showing up for yourself,” says Sara.
