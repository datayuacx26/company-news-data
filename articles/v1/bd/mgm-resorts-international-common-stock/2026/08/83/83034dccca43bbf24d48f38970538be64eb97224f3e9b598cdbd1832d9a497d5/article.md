# The Cosmopolitan of Las Vegas to Host Second Annual Block Party at Block 16 Urban Food Hall

Exclusive bites, creative


cocktails and street festival energy return to the Las Vegas Strip on Sept. 5.


For photos and b-roll, click


[here](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1gu0XfvVl8c_sK58IcRcvDz7xmJS2x2mZ)


**L AS VEGAS, NV (August 17, 2026)** –


The party is back. Following a successful debut last year,[The Cosmopolitan of Las Vegas](https://cosmopolitanlasvegas.mgmresorts.com/en.html?kbid=2584521&ecid=PS_BB_BNGB_EVDOMES_TCOLV_010126&msclkid=b9aa313c565314b03c655b41bacf5ffb) will host the second annual Block Party on


Saturday, Sept. 5


, from


noon to 4 p.m


. at


[Block 16 Urban Food Hall](https://cosmopolitanlasvegas.mgmresorts.com/en/restaurants/block-16.html) , the venue that helped spark Las Vegas' food hall movement. The lively culinary celebration will feature exclusive dishes, inventive


cocktails, and an energetic street festival atmosphere.


Located on Level 2 of Boulevard Tower, Block 16 Urban Food Hall will transform into a dynamic celebration of food, flavor and entertainment as its acclaimed dining concepts, alongside neighboring venues, serve up one-day-only offerings,


tastings and specialty beverages. Free and open to guests of all ages, the afternoon will also feature live DJ sets, interactive experiences, surprise performances, photo-worthy moments and limited-edition merchandise available for purchase.


“Block 16 embodies the creativity, flavor and energy that define The Cosmopolitan’s food and beverage portfolio,” said Jessica Somple, Vice President of Food & Beverage at The Cosmopolitan of Las Vegas. “We’re excited to bring Block Party back for a second year with even more opportunities for guests to discover the diverse culinary concepts, innovative chefs and vibrant atmosphere that have made Block 16 a must-visit destination.”


**B LOCK PARTY BITES & SIPS**
Block 16


venues and offerings will include:


- **Naughty Patty’s:**


The Strip’s burger babe returns with crave-worthy, limited-time creations, including the


**Beer Cheese Burger and Blueberry Lemon Pie Concrete** . Playful character cutouts


will provide a colorful backdrop for photo opportunities.
- **Hattie B’s Hot Chicken:** The Nashville favorite turns up the heat with spicy tenders,


wings and Block Party specials.
- **District: Donuts. Sliders. Brew:** The New Orleans original


will showcase its signature combination of sweet and savory with over-the-top donuts, sliders and specialty menu items.
- **Tekka Bar: Handroll & Sake:** Fresh signature hand rolls take center stage alongside sake shots and sake bombs.


- **Bāng Bar by Momofuku:** Bold global flavors, chewy


bāng bread and boba tea will be complemented by a live vertical rotisserie display.
- **Lardo:** Born from a Portland food cart and built into a cult favorite, Lardo


is serving up a **Hot Italian Beef** sandwich layered with black truffle,


fontina and onion soup dip.
- **Ghost Donkey:** The hidden mezcal and tequila lounge brings its signature fiesta-inspired atmosphere to the celebration with colorful décor, samples of specialty cocktails featuring LALO Tequila and festive photo opportunities.


For a full list of participating venues and offerings, visit


[The Cosmopolitan website.](https://cosmopolitanlasvegas.mgmresorts.com/en/restaurants.html?filter=property,The_Cosmopolitan_Of_Las_Vegas)
