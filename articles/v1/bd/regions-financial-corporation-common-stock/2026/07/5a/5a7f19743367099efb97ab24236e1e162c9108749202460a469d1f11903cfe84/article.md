# Regions Bank Promotes Jamie Stogsdill to Lead Consumer Banking in Arkansas and Louisiana

[View All News](https://ir.regions.com/news-events/press-releases/default.aspx)


### Regions Bank Promotes Jamie Stogsdill to Lead Consumer Banking in Arkansas and Louisiana


Jul 13, 2026


*Twenty-plus year industry veteran will lead customer experience across key markets.*


LITTLE ROCK, Ark.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Regions Bank](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.regions.com%2F&esheet=54568574&newsitemid=20260713750056&lan=en-US&anchor=Regions+Bank&index=1&md5=f94f964638427dc35a77be5786427522) on Monday announced the promotion of Jamie Stogsdill as Arkansas and Louisiana Consumer Banking Executive, a senior leadership role overseeing retail-banking operations in Little Rock, Fayetteville, New Orleans, Baton Rouge, Shreveport and additional markets.


Regions Bank’s Arkansas and Louisiana Consumer Banking Executive, Jamie Stogsdill


Stogsdill’s elevation to this new leadership role is part of the company’s strategy for deepening banking relationships across Arkansas and Louisiana while consistently enhancing customer service.


**Who is Jamie Stogsdill?**


Jamie Stogsdill is a seasoned financial services veteran with more than 20 years of banking experience. Joining Regions in 2012 as a branch manager, Stogsdill served as a Consumer Banking Manager for Chattanooga and Knoxville, Tennessee, for nearly a decade.


In 2025, Stogsdill was appointed to serve as Retail Sales and Service Manager for Regions’ Middle and East Tennessee and Georgia/Carolinas districts.


In each role, Stogsdill has focused on:


- Driving growth
- Delivering world-class customer experience
- Strengthening team engagement


**What will Jamie Stogsdill lead at Regions Bank?**


As Arkansas and Louisiana Consumer Banking Executive, Stogsdill will lead branch-banking and retail operations across several metropolitan and community markets, including:


- Little Rock
- Northwest Arkansas
- Hot Springs
- Jonesboro
- New Orleans
- Baton Rouge
- Shreveport
- Monroe
- Lafayette
- Alexandria


Stogsdill, also supporting branches in Branson and Springfield, Missouri, and Texarkana, will focus on introducing the Regions Bank brand to more consumers and small-business owners seeking tailored, one-on-one financial guidance in reaching their own, unique goals. Further, she will work to deepen Regions’ existing customer relationships by collaborating with branch-banking teams in both states to consistently elevate the customer experience and deliver holistic, consultative service.


**Why this appointment matters for Arkansas, Louisiana and more:**


“Jamie brings Regions Bank’s core value of **putting people first** to life,” said[John Jordan](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fregions.doingmoretoday.com%2Fregions-financial-announces-john-jordan-to-serve-as-head-of-retail%2F&esheet=54568574&newsitemid=20260713750056&lan=en-US&anchor=John+Jordan&index=2&md5=fdff015edc4e33da329fb8593bfb1ade) , head of Retail for Regions. “She is committed to delivering an exceptional experience for customers while helping them build savings, manage expenses, and reach their goals. She brings that same care to leading her teams within the bank, developing them into future leaders who reflect Regions’ mission to make life better for the people and places we serve.”


**Jamie Stogsdill on her new Consumer Banking leadership role:**


“Regions Bank has a proud legacy of building trusted, lasting relationships across Arkansas and Louisiana,” said Stogsdill. “I’m honored to step into this role and build on that foundation empowering customers to succeed while fostering a strong growth culture among our teams.”


Stogsdill succeeds **Lisa Phillips** , who was[recently announced](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fregions.doingmoretoday.com%2Fregions-bank-announces-new-leaders-in-key-markets-to-drive-business-growth-and-customer-experience%2F&esheet=54568574&newsitemid=20260713750056&lan=en-US&anchor=recently+announced&index=3&md5=1dfd36719cf98cd399d44c9d9fbf2a4c) as Consumer Banking Executive for Regions in Georgia and the Carolinas. Stogsdill credited Phillips with championing a customer-first culture enabling the bank to build on its success in Arkansas and Louisiana.


**Stogsdill’s Honors and Credentials:**


- Four-time Regions Chairman’s Club Award honoree, the bank’s recognition for top performers
- Executive Certificate, Consumer Bankers Association Banking School


**Regions Bank in Arkansas and Louisiana:**


**Where does Regions Bank operate in Arkansas and Louisiana?**


Regions Bank maintains a strong branch presence in both states, with more than 130 locations serving customers from Fayetteville and Fort Smith down to New Orleans.


Regions also provides Commercial and Corporate Banking, Wealth Management and Mortgage services across Arkansas and Louisiana. In addition,[Regions Home Improvement Financing](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.regions.com%2Fpersonal-banking%2Fhome-loans%2Fhome-improvement-loans%2Fhome-improvement-financing&esheet=54568574&newsitemid=20260713750056&lan=en-US&anchor=Regions+Home+Improvement+Financing&index=4&md5=6db36ea4303ea3fea13e614c6d81fbc4) works with contractors to deliver point-of-sale lending solutions to homeowners seeking to make various upgrades.


**How is Regions Bank investing in Arkansas and Louisiana branches?**


Regions Bank[recently announced](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fs202.q4cdn.com%2F476346942%2Ffiles%2Fdoc_financials%2F2026%2Fq1%2F1Q26-Presentation_Website.pdf&esheet=54568574&newsitemid=20260713750056&lan=en-US&anchor=recently+announced&index=5&md5=9287359b0fce19f4393c86195a1acebd) a five-year branch enhancement plan that includes the Mid-South, Midwest and Southeast. The plan focuses on:


- Modernized branch designs
- More convenient locations
- Enhanced spaces for personalized, one-on-one financial guidance


By the end of the initiative, the vast majority of Regions Bank locations will undergo meaningful upgrades.


**What else sets Regions Bank apart?**


Regions offers the enhanced technology today’s consumers value – while also placing a premium on in-person, custom-tailored service.


While technology can handle basic transactions, Regions finds people primarily turn to convenient branches for one-on-one guidance on making a major purchase, creating a savings plan, or navigating other financial opportunities.[Regions Greenprint® plans](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.regions.com%2Fpersonal-banking%2Fgreenprint-financial-planning&esheet=54568574&newsitemid=20260713750056&lan=en-US&anchor=Regions+Greenprint%26%23174%3B+plans&index=6&md5=cd7dc8d24ae9a3e143c841c31245ad67) are a great example. At no cost, people and small-business owners[can work with a Regions banker to create a personalized plan to help them achieve their financial goals](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fregions.doingmoretoday.com%2Fa-retirement-dream-hits-the-road%2F&esheet=54568574&newsitemid=20260713750056&lan=en-US&anchor=can+work+with+a+Regions+banker+to+create+a+personalized+plan+to+help+them+achieve+their+financial+goals&index=7&md5=de6555ecccb0a96e347e3792fa429ebf) .

**About Regions Financial Corporation**


Regions Financial Corporation (NYSE:RF), with $161 billion in assets, is a member of the S&P 500 Index and is one of the nation’s largest full-service providers of consumer and commercial banking, wealth management, and mortgage products and services. Regions serves customers across the South, Midwest and Texas, and through its subsidiary, Regions Bank, operates more than 1,200 banking offices and more than 1,750 ATMs. Regions Bank is an Equal Housing Lender and Member FDIC.
