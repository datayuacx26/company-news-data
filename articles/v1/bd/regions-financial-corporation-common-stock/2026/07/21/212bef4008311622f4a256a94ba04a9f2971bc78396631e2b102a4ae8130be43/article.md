# Regions Bank Names Thom Smith as Private Wealth Regional Executive for Georgia and the Carolinas

[View All News](https://ir.regions.com/news-events/press-releases/default.aspx)


### Regions Bank Names Thom Smith as Private Wealth Regional Executive for Georgia and the Carolinas


Jul 14, 2026


*Veteran wealth management executive to lead growth and advisory services for high-net-worth clients across growing markets in the Southeast.*


ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Regions Bank](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.regions.com%2F&esheet=54569621&newsitemid=20260714615443&lan=en-US&anchor=Regions+Bank&index=1&md5=1a0f97de9150a0d835dacfe2948666ae) on Tuesday announced veteran wealth management executive **Thom Smith** has joined the bank to serve as[Regions Private Wealth Management](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.regions.com%2Fwealth-management%2Fprivate-wealth-management%2Fwealth-planning&esheet=54569621&newsitemid=20260714615443&lan=en-US&anchor=Regions+Private+Wealth+Management&index=2&md5=ae07924f428e4971ff89e824843d59a7) regional executive for Georgia, North Carolina and South Carolina.


Thom Smith is Regions Private Wealth Management regional executive for Georgia, North Carolina and South Carolina.


Smith’s move to Regions Bank is part of the company’s strategy for building more Private Wealth Management growth and consistently strengthening client service. Throughout his career, Smith has built and led high-performing teams, implemented growth initiatives, and supported the fiduciary and planning needs for high-net-worth and ultra-high-net-worth clients.


**What role will Thom Smith assume for Regions Private Wealth Management?**


As Private Wealth regional executive, Smith will oversee teams providing comprehensive wealth management, tailored investment services, financial planning services and insights, including:


- [Retirement and legacy planning](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.regions.com%2Fwealth-management%2Fprivate-wealth-management%2Fwealth-planning&esheet=54569621&newsitemid=20260714615443&lan=en-US&anchor=Retirement+and+legacy+planning&index=3&md5=4536bf55919e63cd86ab798e5d69001e)
- [Trust and estate planning](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.regions.com%2Fwealth-management%2Fprivate-wealth-management%2Ftrust-estate-planning&esheet=54569621&newsitemid=20260714615443&lan=en-US&anchor=Trust+and+estate+planning&index=4&md5=1c323a96b0f7c4a2ec3f6c493c842615)
- [Philanthropic and charitable giving solutions](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.regions.com%2Fwealth-management%2Fphilanthropic-solutions%2Findividuals-and-families&esheet=54569621&newsitemid=20260714615443&lan=en-US&anchor=Philanthropic+and+charitable+giving+solutions&index=5&md5=b9dc979ed344e70bc17c11aa02e1cfc4)
- [Natural resources and real estate properties management](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.regions.com%2Fwealth-management%2Fprivate-wealth-management%2Fnatural-resources-and-real-estate&esheet=54569621&newsitemid=20260714615443&lan=en-US&anchor=Natural+resources+and+real+estate+properties+management&index=6&md5=b06e32eea5cb62e29621a29bd775a332)
- Banking and lending solutions


These services are designed to help clients:


- Build and preserve long-term wealth
- Plan for retirement and generational wealth transitions
- Navigate complex financial decisions
- Align financial strategies with personal and business goals


**Where will Thom Smith lead growth?**


Smith will lead Regions Private Wealth Management’s expanding teams serving **high-net-worth and ultra-high-net-worth clients** across major Southeastern markets, including:


- Albany, Atlanta, Augusta and Savannah in Georgia
- Charlotte and Raleigh in North Carolina
- Charleston, Columbia and Greenville in South Carolina


His appointment supports Regions Bank’s continued focus on client growth in **Georgia and the Carolinas** , where population trends, business formation, and generational wealth transfers are creating increased demand for consultative wealth management services **.**


Smith’s leadership supports the bank’s[Regions360](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fregions.doingmoretoday.com%2Fbuilding-relationships-the-regions360-way%2F&esheet=54569621&newsitemid=20260714615443&lan=en-US&anchor=Regions360&index=7&md5=216b0f69ec6a706cece64a6e7f0f1d7f) approach, which integrates[Consumer Banking](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.regions.com%2Fpersonal-banking&esheet=54569621&newsitemid=20260714615443&lan=en-US&anchor=Consumer+Banking&index=8&md5=3207cbc21b22cb239838312adb4be9f5) ,[Commercial Banking](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.regions.com%2Fcommercial-banking&esheet=54569621&newsitemid=20260714615443&lan=en-US&anchor=Commercial+Banking&index=9&md5=567ed638d64c3d034a4dda7a895fe843) , and[Wealth Management](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.regions.com%2Fwealth-management&esheet=54569621&newsitemid=20260714615443&lan=en-US&anchor=Wealth+Management&index=10&md5=24172e6c3d15485da0e12902a252fce9) to deliver **holistic, client-focused financial solutions** . He will work alongside Regions market leaders including[Mary Beth Coke](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fdoingmoretoday.com%2Fregions-bank-announces-retirement-of-bill-linginfelter-appointment-of-mary-beth-coke-as-atlanta-market-executive%2F&esheet=54569621&newsitemid=20260714615443&lan=en-US&anchor=Mary+Beth+Coke&index=11&md5=29db5970045f5a9e320193e7819d1a7f) ,[Thad Walton](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fdoingmoretoday.com%2Flocal-experience-local-commitment-regions-bank-announces-thad-walton-as-charlotte-market-executive%2F&esheet=54569621&newsitemid=20260714615443&lan=en-US&anchor=Thad+Walton&index=12&md5=ad59d2cce1120b8987c15796417a1bcd) ,[Brian Jackson](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fdoingmoretoday.com%2Fbuilding-growth-deepening-roots-regions-bank-names-brian-jackson-as-savannah-market-executive%2F&esheet=54569621&newsitemid=20260714615443&lan=en-US&anchor=Brian+Jackson&index=13&md5=bcb4fc24c0f53a9a26a6a6701562b0ce) and others as part of a united focus on growth across Regions’ key lines of business.


**Leadership Perspective:**


“Thom is a seasoned, well-respected leader with a strong track record of building high-performing teams, executing growth strategies, and making life better for high-net-worth clients and their families,” said **Leslie Carter-Prall, Head of Regions Private Wealth Management** . “We are pleased to welcome Thom to Regions, and we look forward to his leadership with a service focus to current and new Private Wealth Management clients.”


Smith added:


“After many years in the industry, the opportunity to join a company like Regions, with its strong reputation, collaborative culture, and commitment to client service, was incredibly compelling,” Smith said. “I look forward to working with this talented team to deepen client relationships, invest in our associates, strengthen collaboration across the bank, and drive thoughtful, disciplined growth across Georgia and the Carolinas.”


**Who are Regions Private Wealth Management clients?**


Regions serves a broad range of clients with **complex financial needs** , including:


- Business owners and entrepreneurs
- Executives and professionals
- Multigenerational families planning wealth transfer


**Why Demand for Private Wealth Management is Growing:**


Regions’ advisors are seeing increased client engagement driven by:


- Retirement readiness and longevity planning
- Generational wealth transfer and inheritance planning
- Financial education for heirs and family members
- Business succession and liquidity events
- Philanthropic goals and community impact


**Thom Smith’s Experience and Background:**


Smith brings more than 30 years of leadership experience in private banking and wealth management. Most recently, he served as managing director at Fifth Third Private Bank, where he led an integrated private banking team across multiple markets, including Georgia, Alabama, Texas, and California.


Prior to that, Smith spent nearly two decades at Truist and its predecessor bank, BB&T, where he served as senior vice president and regional wealth managing director.
