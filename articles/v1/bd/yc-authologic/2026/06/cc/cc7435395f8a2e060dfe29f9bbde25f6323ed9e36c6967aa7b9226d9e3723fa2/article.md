# Denmark Launches AltID: What One of Europe's First Live EUDI Wallets Means for Banks

On June 3, 2026, Denmark launched AltID - one of the first official digital identity wallets in the EU.


Available on iOS and Android for anyone over 13 with a MitID account and a Danish CPR number, it stores a digital ID card and an age certificate locally on the device. No centralised database. No usage tracking. Zero-knowledge proofs for age verification - users confirm they meet an age threshold without revealing their date of birth or national ID number.


It is a quiet launch. No fanfare. Just an app in the store, backed by a government agency. And that is exactly why it matters.
