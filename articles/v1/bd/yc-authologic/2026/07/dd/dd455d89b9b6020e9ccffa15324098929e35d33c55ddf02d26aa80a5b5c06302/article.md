# EUDI Wallet readiness checklist

*Last updated: July 2026*


The EUDI Wallet Readiness Checklist is a free 20-question self-assessment for banks and other regulated businesses preparing for mandatory EUDI Wallet acceptance.


It covers 6 readiness areas - Strategic Readiness, Customer Experience, Technology & Integration, Orchestration & Fallback, Compliance & Risk, and Future Scalability - with a scoring guide that tells you whether your organisation is Operationally Ready, In Progress, or Not Ready.
