# Authologic vs Signicat | Identity verification comparison

*Last updated: July 2026*


Signicat and Authologic both verify identities for regulated businesses, but they belong to different categories. Signicat is a European digital identity platform with electronic signing at its core. Authologic is a global trust platform built wallet-first. It covers all three generations of digital identity: legacy documents and biometrics, today’s national eIDs and bank IDs, and next-generation wallet credentials. The platform routes each user to the best available method and provides automatic fallback. It gives businesses access to over 90 methods from more than 80 providers across over 236 countries and territories.


**The practical difference:** in an Authologic flow, a document scan is treated as a last resort. Based on Signicat's publicly listed eID coverage (as of July 2026), a user whose method isn't among its 35 supported schemes falls back to an alternative check, typically OCR-based document verification.


One in five onboarding applications is abandoned due to KYC and AML challenges,[costing banks an estimated $3.3 billion annually](https://resources.fenergo.com/blogs/customer-onboarding-expectations-vs-reality) in lost business. Document-based verification can contribute to that friction by requiring users to photograph physical documents and complete additional checks. Meanwhile, governments are issuing reusable credentials directly to mobile devices, and the new AMLR, applicable from July 2027, strengthens the role of secure electronic identification in customer due diligence - supporting a shift towards eID-first verification where suitable eIDAS-compliant methods are available.


Authologic and Signicat both address this transition, but with different product strengths and coverage. They are not the same response.
