# Hanmi Financial Corporation Announces First Quarter 2026 Earnings and Conference Call Date


[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_08767f6420a3d2c7983941e6a0cd01bf/hanmi/news/2026-04-07_Hanmi_Financial_Corporation_Announces_First_397.pdf) April 07, 2026 4:05pm EDT


LOS ANGELES, April 07, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- **Hanmi Financial Corporation (Nasdaq: HAFC) (“Hanmi”)** , the holding company for Hanmi Bank, today announced that it will report first quarter 2026 financial results after the market close on Tuesday, April 21, 2026. Management will host a conference call that same day, at 2:00 p.m. Pacific Time (5:00 p.m. Eastern Time) to discuss the results.


Investment professionals and all current and prospective shareholders are invited to access the live call on April 21 by dialing 1-877-407-9039 before 2:00 p.m. Pacific Time, using access code “Hanmi Bank”. To listen to the call online visit the investor relations page of Hanmi’s website at[www.hanmi.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=uGxtcKs_Q_AmKAtit8Zlm4ivY61VcFroJXQyO4MBVI1X1cefVuBYEAMj6eVUSZwHw9o2Hnzmi_akjpix8pWchA==) . The webcast will also be available for replay approximately one hour following the call.


**About Hanmi Financial Corporation**
Headquartered in Los Angeles, California, Hanmi Financial Corporation owns Hanmi Bank, which serves multi-ethnic communities through its network of 32 full-service branches, five loan production offices and three loan centers in California, Colorado, Georgia, Illinois, New Jersey, New York, Texas, Virginia and Washington. Hanmi Bank specializes in real estate, commercial, SBA and trade finance lending to small and middle market businesses. Additional information is available at[www.hanmi.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=uGxtcKs_Q_AmKAtit8Zlm187hvxT9pddQe8S1P2AWs2Fn0UTv48M04pfHUxMbnhCQcsabfGbvqVAktYYXWxq0A==) .
