# Hanmi Financial to Participate in the Janney 6th Annual Bank CEO Forum


[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_08767f6420a3d2c7983941e6a0cd01bf/hanmi/news/2026-02-03_Hanmi_Financial_to_Participate_in_the_Janney_6th_395.pdf) February 03, 2026 8:00am EST


LOS ANGELES, Feb. 03, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- **Hanmi Financial Corporation (NASDAQ: HAFC, or “Hanmi”)** , the parent company of Hanmi Bank (the “Bank”), today announced its participation in the Janney 6th Annual Bank CEO Forum on Wednesday, February 4, 2026 in Scottsdale, AZ.


Bonnie Lee, President and Chief Executive Officer, and Ron Santarosa, Chief Financial Officer, will host one-on-one and small group meetings throughout the day.


A copy of the presentation being used for meetings with institutional investors will be available in the Investor Relations section of the Company’s website at[www.hanmi.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=tzrg2spZi71QyTTX1UEAt1Z4jh-uaVV0XrINyvnOAglPfCPmBLtz4nEXZ9iC1XHDOr4dmPxENZdIO6e0sgoWEw==) .


**About Hanmi Financial Corporation**
Headquartered in Los Angeles, California, Hanmi Financial Corporation owns Hanmi Bank, which serves multi-ethnic communities through its network of 32 full-service branches, five loan production offices and three loan centers in California, Texas, Illinois, Virginia, New Jersey, New York, Colorado, Washington and Georgia. Hanmi Bank specializes in real estate, commercial, SBA and trade finance lending to small and middle market businesses.
