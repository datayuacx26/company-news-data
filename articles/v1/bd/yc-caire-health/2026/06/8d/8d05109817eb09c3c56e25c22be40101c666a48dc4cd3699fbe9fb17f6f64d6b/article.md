##### **~ Ball Ground, GA (JUNE 24, 2026) —**


##### **CAIRE Taps Sunset Healthcare Solutions as Canadian Distributor**


**[Sunset Healthcare Solutions](https://sunsethcs.com/)** will now carry CAIRE’s **[FreeStyle® Comfort®](https://www.caireinc.com/product/freestyle-comfort-provider/)** portable oxygen concentrator and the **[Eclipse 5®](https://www.caireinc.com/product/eclipse-5-provider/)** transportable oxygen concentrator, along with accessories and out-of-warranty parts.


“This marks a new chapter and expansion in how CAIRE serves its Canadian customers and provides access to critical oxygen therapy equipment. Since 2004, Sunset has made its mark in delivering excellence in highly-tailored customer service experiences and value, and we are delighted to expand our reach using their distributor network and well-respected team of HME professionals,” said Konstantin Grey, CAIRE Strategic Initiatives and Business Development Director, Respiratory Sales.


Other support services offered by Sunset will include:


- Order fulfillment across Canada.
- Logistics coordination and direct shipment support for providers and dealers.
- Distribution support for replacement parts and accessories across the CAIRE oxygen therapy portfolio.
- Streamlined Canadian operations with scalable support for future growth.


Sunset is a Chicago-based manufacturer and distributor of HME supplies that partners with over 1600 healthcare businesses to offer specialized support, innovative solutions, and a full range of quality HME products.


In addition, Sunset serves the United States along with key international accounts, extending its reach around the globe. Internal and external sales representation covers all US states and over 150 countries. Distribution facilities in the Chicagoland area and on the West Coast help maintain competitive shipping times.


“We work every day to provide our HME providers with the very best in service and supplies. Our work with CAIRE allows us to spotlight the benefits of a trusted and reliable portfolio of therapeutic oxygen solutions, which will serve our providers well as they care for a vulnerable, respiratory patient population,” said Bryan Vigna, International Sales Director.


Click the[link](https://sunsethcs.com/) to learn more about Sunset.

##### COMING IN AUGUST


##### **Careica Health Joins CAIRE-Authorized Service Network**


[Careica Health](https://careicahealth.com/) is now a part of the CAIRE Authorized Service Network, and will support Canadian customers with in-warranty repairs for CAIRE’s portfolio of oxygen therapy solutions, including the **[FreeStyle Comfort](https://www.caireinc.com/product/freestyle-comfort-provider/)** portable oxygen concentrator, the **[Eclipse 5](https://www.caireinc.com/product/eclipse-5-provider/)** transportable oxygen concentrator, and the **[NewLife® Intensity 10](https://www.caireinc.com/product/newlife-intensity-provider/)** stationary oxygen concentrator.


“Getting equipment repaired and returned quickly and at a low cost is essential for our HME providers. Through this expansion, we not only provide a Canadian resource for our customers, but we also put the spotlight on training for both the in-house Careica team and field representatives. We are committed to education, and we know this investment and time will win business,” said Konstantin Grey, CAIRE Strategic Initiatives and Business Development Director, Respiratory Sales.


Click the[link](https://careicahealth.com/) to learn more about Careica.
