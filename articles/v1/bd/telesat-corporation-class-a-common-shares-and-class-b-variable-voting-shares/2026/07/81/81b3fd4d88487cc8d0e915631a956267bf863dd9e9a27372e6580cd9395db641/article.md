# Telesat schedules second quarter 2026 earnings conference call for August 13, 2026 | Telesat

OTTAWA, CANADA – July 31, 2026 –[Telesat](https://www.telesat.com/about-us/) (Nasdaq and TSX: TSAT), one of the world’s largest and most innovative satellite operators, has scheduled a conference call for Thursday, August 13, 2026, at 10:30 a.m. EDT to discuss its financial results on the three- and six-month periods ended June 30, 2026. The call will be hosted by Daniel S. Goldberg, President and Chief Executive Officer, and Donald Tremblay, Chief Financial Officer, of Telesat.


Prior to the commencement of the call, Telesat will post a news release containing its financial results on its website ([www.telesat.com](https://www.telesat.com/) ) under the tab “Investors” and the heading “Investor News.”
