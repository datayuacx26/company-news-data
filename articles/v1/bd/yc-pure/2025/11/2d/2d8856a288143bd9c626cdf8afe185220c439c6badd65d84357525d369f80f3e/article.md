# 2025 US Mint Product Release Details: 250th Anniversary United States Marine Corps American Eagle One Ounce Silver Proof Coin (25MPM)

## What we know so far about the 250th Anniversary United States Marine Corps American Eagle One Ounce Silver Proof Coin (25MPM):


This year, the U.S. Mint is commemorating the 250th anniversary of the United States Armed Forces with the release of three limited-edition American Eagle One Ounce Silver Proof Coins, each featuring a their own special privy mark. These coins honor the Army, Marine Corps, and Navy, and are expected to be highly sought after by collectors. Earlier this year, the[Army American Eagle One Ounce Silver Proof Coin (25APM)](https://www.usmint.gov/250th-anniversary-united-states-army-american-eagle-one-ounce-silver-proof-coin-25APM.html) , released on June 13, 2025, **** coinciding with the anniversary of the U.S. Army being founded. The second coin, the ****[Navy American Eagle One Ounce Silver Proof Coin (25NPM)](https://www.usmint.gov/250th-anniversary-united-states-navy-american-eagle-one-ounce-silver-proof-coin-25NPM.html) was released on October 10, 2025. The third coin, the[Marine Corps American Eagle One Ounce Silver Proof Coin (25MPM)](https://www.usmint.gov/250th-anniversary-united-states-marine-corps-american-eagle-one-ounce-silver-proof-coin-25MPM.html) , will be releasing on **November 10, 2025** . **** Introduced in 1986, the American Eagle Silver Proof Coin series has become an enduring symbol of artistry, quality, and national pride. Struck at the **United States Mint in Philadelphia** and bearing the **“P” mint mark** , this limited-edition release continues that tradition—blending exquisite craftsmanship with a meaningful tribute to one of America’s most storied military institutions. The obverse (heads) of the coin showcases Adolph A. Weinman’s timeless **“Walking Liberty”** design, a masterpiece long celebrated by collectors and historians alike. Depicting Liberty in full stride, draped in the American flag and carrying branches of laurel and oak, the design represents strength, peace, and civic virtue. For this special edition, the Mint returned to its historical assets to achieve a design that more closely reflects Weinman’s original vision. This refined rendition includes enhanced details and Weinman’s **traditional artist mark** , alongside the inscriptions “LIBERTY,” “IN GOD WE TRUST,” and “2025.” A distinctive **privy mark honoring the 250th anniversary of the United States Marine Corps** further distinguishes this release. Each coin incorporates a **reeded edge variation** for enhanced security and bears the **“P” mint mark** , signifying its production at the Philadelphia Mint. The coin is presented in an **elegant blue velvet, satin-lined case** and accompanied by an official **certificate of authenticity** . The presentation box displays the **United States Mint seal** , while the outer sleeve features an image of the obverse design, creating a refined and cohesive presentation.As with most privy-marked releases, these coins are anticipated to sell out quickly and command a premium on the secondary market just as the V75 Privy did years ago.
