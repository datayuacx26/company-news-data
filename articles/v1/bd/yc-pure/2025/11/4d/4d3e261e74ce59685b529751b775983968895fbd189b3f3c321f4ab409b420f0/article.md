# 2025 US Mint Product Release Details: Limited Edition Silver Proof Set 2025 (25RC)

## What we know so far about the Limited Edition Silver Proof Set 2025 (25RC):


The 2025 Limited Edition Silver Proof Set is an extraordinary collection that highlights the artistry and heritage of American coinage. Featuring all five 2025 American Women Quarters™ Silver Proof Coins, this set pays tribute to five remarkable figures: Ida B. Wells, Juliette Gordon Low, Dr. Vera Rubin, Stacey Park Milbern, and Althea Gibson. Each coin showcases a beautifully detailed design with a brilliant mirror-like background and frosted foreground that creates a striking cameo effect. The common obverse features a portrait of George Washington, originally sculpted by Laura Gardin Fraser for the 1932 bicentennial. Containing 2.473 ounces of .999 fine silver and presented in an elegant hinged box, this set brings together artistry, craftsmanship, and historical significance in one remarkable presentation.


Alongside the quarters, the set also includes the American Eagle One Ounce Silver Proof Coin, the Kennedy half dollar, and the Roosevelt dime, all minted at the San Francisco Mint with exceptional precision. The American Eagle Silver Proof Coin features Adolph A. Weinman’s timeless image of Liberty on one side and a powerful eagle landing with an oak branch on the other, symbolizing peace and strength. Scheduled for release on[November 6, 2025](https://www.usmint.gov/limited-edition-silver-proof-set-2025-25RC.html) , this limited-edition collection will not be issued beyond the 2025 release year. It is an essential addition for collectors and a meaningful gift for any special occasion. Elegant, inspiring, and rich in detail, it captures the very best of American numismatic tradition.
