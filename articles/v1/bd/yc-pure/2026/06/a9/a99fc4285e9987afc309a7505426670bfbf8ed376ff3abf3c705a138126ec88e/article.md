# 2026 US Mint Product Release Details: Semiquincentennial Silver Proof Set 2026 (26RH)

## What we know so far about the **Semiquincentennial Silver Proof Set 2026 (26RH)** :


The 2026 United States Mint Silver Proof Set celebrates the 250th anniversary of the United States with a special one-year-only release featuring ten proof-quality coins struck at the San Francisco Mint. This historic set includes five Semiquincentennial Quarters™, a Native American $1 Coin, Jefferson Nickel, Lincoln Penny, and newly redesigned Enduring Liberty Half Dollar and Emerging Liberty Dime. The half dollar and dime, along with all five commemorative quarters, are struck in 99.9% fine silver, making this set one of the most significant annual proof offerings in recent years.


At the heart of the collection are the five Semiquincentennial Quarters honoring defining moments in American history: the Mayflower Compact, Revolutionary War, Declaration of Independence, U.S. Constitution, and Gettysburg Address. Each quarter features unique obverse and reverse designs that highlight influential figures and events that shaped the nation’s development. From the Pilgrims’ journey aboard the Mayflower to Abraham Lincoln’s enduring message of unity and liberty, these coins serve as miniature works of art that commemorate the ideals and milestones that continue to define America.


Adding to the set’s appeal are two special anniversary coin designs appearing for one year only. The Enduring Liberty Half Dollar replaces the traditional Kennedy design with a striking depiction of the Statue of Liberty, while the Emerging Liberty Dime marks the return of Liberty to the dime for the first time in more than 80 years. Combined with the 2026 Native American $1 Coin honoring Polly Cooper and the Oneida Nation’s contributions during the Revolutionary War, this Silver Proof Set offers collectors a remarkable tribute to America’s past while celebrating the nation’s Semiquincentennial milestone.
