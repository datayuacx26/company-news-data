# 2026 US Mint Product Release Details: Best of the Mint 1916 Mercury Dime Gold Coin and Silver Medal Set (26BM1)

## What we know so far about the Best of the Mint 1916 Mercury Dime Gold Coin and Silver Medal Set (26BM1):


The United States Mint has officially launched the first release in its highly anticipated Best of the Mint series with the 1916 Mercury Dime Gold Coin and Silver Companion Medal Set. Created as part of the Nation’s 250th anniversary celebration, this special release reimagines one of America’s most beloved coin designs in 24-karat gold while pairing it with a newly designed silver medal. The Best of the Mint program highlights five iconic coins selected through public input and expert recommendations, bringing historic American numismatic designs to a new generation of collectors. The 1916 Mercury Dime Gold Coin and Silver Companion Medal Set serves as the inaugural release in this semiquincentennial series, making it a significant addition for collectors interested in both United States Mint history and modern commemorative issues.


The gold coin faithfully recreates the classic 1916 Mercury Dime design, featuring Liberty wearing a winged cap symbolizing freedom of thought. To mark the 250th anniversary of American independence, the coin includes a special Liberty Bell privy mark bearing the numeral “250.” On the reverse, the iconic fasces, olive branch, and battle ax remain, symbolizing national unity, peace, and readiness. Struck at the United States Mint’s West Point facility, the 24-karat gold coin is paired with a silver companion medal struck in Philadelphia, creating a unique two-piece set that connects historic and modern Mint artistry.


The accompanying silver medal expands on the legacy of the Mercury Dime by depicting the transformative years from 1916 through 1945, a period marked by global conflict, economic hardship, and significant social change. Its continuous design spans both sides of the medal, portraying Liberty in defensive and offensive stances while incorporating imagery representing World War I, World War II, the Great Depression, Hooverville shacks, and the Dust Bowl. Rays extending from Liberty’s sword symbolize hope emerging from adversity, tying together one of the most consequential eras in American history. With limited quantities available and its status as the first release in the Best of the Mint series, the 1916 Mercury Dime Gold Coin and Silver Companion Medal Set is positioned to be one of the most notable numismatic releases of the year.
