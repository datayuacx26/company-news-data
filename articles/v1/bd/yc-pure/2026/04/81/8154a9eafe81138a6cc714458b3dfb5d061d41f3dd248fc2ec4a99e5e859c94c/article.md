# 2026 US Mint Product Release Details: American Buffalo 2026 One Ounce Gold Proof Coin (26EL)

## What we know so far about the American Buffalo 2026 One Ounce Gold Proof Coin (26EL):


The **2026 American Buffalo One Ounce Gold Proof Coin** **(26EL)** is a stunning collector piece struck in one ounce of .9999 fine 24-karat gold—making it one of the purest gold coins in the world. As the first 24-karat gold proof coin ever issued by the U.S. Mint, it represents the collector version of the official American Buffalo Gold Bullion Coin.


Designed by James Earle Fraser, the coin’s imagery is based on the original 1913 Type I Buffalo Nickel. The obverse (heads) features Fraser’s iconic Native American profile, along with the inscriptions “LIBERTY” and “2026,” and includes a special 250th anniversary bell privy mark. The reverse (tails) showcases a powerful American buffalo (bison), accompanied by the inscriptions “UNITED STATES OF AMERICA,” “E PLURIBUS UNUM,” “IN GOD WE TRUST,” “$50,” “1 OZ.,” and “.9999 FINE GOLD.”


Each coin is struck at the United States Mint facility in West Point Mint, ensuring exceptional craftsmanship and detail. To complement its design, the coin is encapsulated and presented in a classic black clamshell case featuring the United States Mint seal. The case is housed within an outer sleeve adorned with a gold foil American Buffalo image and includes a Certificate of Authenticity with matching artwork.
