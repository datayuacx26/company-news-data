# New Selling Feature: Advanced Payment

## Introducing the Advanced Payment Feature


We’ve introduced a new **Advanced Payment** beta program, now available for Silver, Gold and Plum tier members.


This feature allows eligible users to receive a full advance on their item’s value—before the check-in process is complete. Once your package is delivered, funds are released within 24 hours, giving you faster access to your proceeds.


Eligible items will be clearly marked during checkout for easy identification.

To apply it on an already placed order, head over to your dashboard, go to the purchase tab and click on view order to the order you would like to have it be applied on.
