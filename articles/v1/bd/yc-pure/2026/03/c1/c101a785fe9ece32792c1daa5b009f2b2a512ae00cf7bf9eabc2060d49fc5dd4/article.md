# 2026 US Mint Product Release Details: American Eagle 2026 One Ounce Silver Enhanced Uncirculated Coin (26EG)

## What we know so far about the American Eagle 2026 One Ounce Silver Enhanced Uncirculated Coin (26EG):


The **American Eagle 2026 One Ounce Silver Enhanced Uncirculated Coin (26EG)** from the United States Mint features an **American Eagle Silver Dollar** produced at the West Point Mint and bearing the “W” mint mark. Struck from **one troy ounce of .999 fine silver** , this coin includes enhanced security features such as an anti-counterfeit reeded edge variation, ensuring both authenticity and peace of mind.


The obverse (heads) design showcases Adolph A. Weinman’s iconic full-length figure of Liberty walking forward, wrapped in the folds of the American flag, with her right hand extended and branches of laurel and oak in her left. The United States Mint returned to original historical assets to more closely reflect Weinman’s original vision, including the addition of his traditional artist mark. Inscriptions include **“LIBERTY,” “IN GOD WE TRUST,” and “2026.”** The reverse (tails) design depicts an eagle approaching a landing while carrying an oak branch, symbolizing strength and continuity. Inscriptions read **“UNITED STATES OF AMERICA,” “E PLURIBUS UNUM,” “1 OZ. FINE SILVER,” and “ONE DOLLAR.”**


Each coin is encapsulated and housed in a stylish **black clamshell presentation case** adorned with the official United States Mint seal. The set includes a **certificate of authenticity** listing the coin’s specifications. An outer sleeve featuring a 250th Anniversary Bell and a graphic with the words **“2026 American Eagle”** .
