# 2026 US Mint Product Release Details: American Eagle 2026 One Ounce Gold Enhanced Uncirculated Coin (26EH)

## What we know so far about the American Eagle 2026 One Ounce Gold Enhanced Uncirculated Coin (26EH):


The 2026 American Eagle One Ounce Gold Enhanced Uncirculated Coin is a special one-year release honoring the 250th anniversary of the signing of the Declaration of Independence. Struck at the United States Mint at West Point, this limited-edition coin features dual dates “1776–2026” along with a unique Liberty Bell privy mark bearing the number “250” to commemorate the Nation’s semiquincentennial celebration.


Containing 1 oz of gold in the iconic American Eagle design, the obverse showcases Augustus Saint-Gaudens’ celebrated depiction of Lady Liberty carrying a torch and olive branch, while the reverse features a detailed portrayal of a majestic eagle alongside classic inscriptions including “UNITED STATES OF AMERICA” and “1 OZ. FINE GOLD.” The coin also includes anti-counterfeit variable reeding for added security.


What sets this release apart is its Enhanced Uncirculated finish, which combines laser-frosted and laser-finished surfaces to create a striking high-contrast appearance. Each coin arrives encapsulated in a premium black presentation case with the United States Mint seal, accompanied by a specially designed outer sleeve and certificate of authenticity matching the semiquincentennial theme.


With its historic significance, unique privy mark, and limited availability, the 2026 American Eagle One Ounce Gold Enhanced Uncirculated Coin is expected to be a standout release for collectors and investors alike.
