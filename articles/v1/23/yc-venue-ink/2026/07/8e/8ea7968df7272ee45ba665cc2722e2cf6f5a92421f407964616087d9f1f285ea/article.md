# How Artists Celebrate Spooky Season

From the numbers, it looks like many of you will be showing up to the shop like this. Here's what artists are doing to stay booked and busy.


## **🧛🏻‍♂️ Host a scary party**


Events are boo-ming. To date this month, artists already created over 200 events and bookings are up a whopping 64%! One of our favorites is @rootedinkstudio's Witchy Flash Event. Use our[Events feature](https://www.venue.ink/flash-tattoo-events) to host your own.


October's not even over yet!


## **👻 Draw creepy flash**


Artists have been busy drawing and added over 4,000 *more* flash compared to last month. Good news, it's working. 24 days into October, artists are getting 15% more flash requests compared to the same timeframe last month. Use our[Flash](https://www.venue.ink/flash-upload-management) feature to add your own.


Some spooky new collections we spotted recently.

Last week we released our newest skin *Spooky* to huge fanfare. You know what's also spooky? Comic Neue (Google's own version of Comic Sans) has been added to our font collection. You can finally dress up your booking link as a design faux pas.
