## **Beyond Bookings: Pro Tips to Make Conventions Worth It**


You’re already booked. Cool. But how you set up, how you pace your day, how you interact with the people who stop by—that’s what turns a solid weekend into something that actually[grows your business](https://www.venue.ink/blog/how-to-get-more-tattoo-clients-12-essential-strategies-for-artists) . The little stuff adds up fast: how your booth looks, how you handle flash, how you take care of yourself between sessions.


This part of the guide covers the moves that don’t show up on your schedule, but still impact how much money you make, how many clients you keep, and how burned out you feel by the time you pack up.


Here’s what experienced artists keep in mind:


- **Don’t overpack your booth.** Bring what you need to work comfortably, and leave the rest. A cluttered setup makes you look unprepared and makes it harder to stay focused when you’re in the zone.
- **Display your flash clearly.** Print it clean, mount it well, and make your pricing easy to spot. People are more likely to walk up if they know what they’re looking at and what it’ll cost.
- **Leave buffer time.** Stack your day too tight and you’ll end up rushing through work or running behind. A little breathing room keeps the quality high and the stress low.
- **Be open—but not pushy.** Chat with folks walking by. Talk to other artists. The connections you make on the floor can turn into future bookings, guest spots, or collaborations.
- **Turn one-time clients into regulars.** Someone grabs a flash piece? Cool. Follow up later. Shoot them a thank-you message, repost the healed piece, or offer a discount for their next session. One solid appointment can turn into five if you handle it right.


At the end of the day, you’re not just selling tattoos: you’re building your name. Treat every interaction like it could lead to something long-term, and conventions stop being just another hustle and start working *for* you.
