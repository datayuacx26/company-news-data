# Win a Bishop, Vlad Blad, Dan Kubin (or whatever you dream of)

## **The details**


**What machines can I choose? Do I get to choose the model and color too?**
Winners can choose any one machine (including model and color) valued up to $1,500 USD MSRP including shipping to your door. Winners are responsible for paying any taxes or duties to receive the machine.


**What's the timeline for this giveaway?**
You can register and start earning entries from Dec 1, 2025 to Dec 31, 2025 at 11:59pm ET. Any deposits or payments booked with us between Dec 1 and 31 are counted as long as you are registered.


**Can I win more than one machine?**
Artists can only win one machine per giveaway.


**When will the prize be delivered?**
We'll be announcing and contacting winners by DM and email by Jan 16, 2025. Winners will have 14 days to respond with their dream machine and we'll organize an order and shipment to their address of choice.


**I'm an artist outside of US or Canada, can I participate?**
Yes! Artists around the world can participate as long as you're registered for the giveaway and not a resident of excluded jurisdictions where this giveaway is prohibited by law.


**Where can I learn more?**
You can learn more in our giveaway[T&Cs](https://www.venue.ink/december-giveaway-terms) .
