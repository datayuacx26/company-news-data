# Knarly Gav Joins the Party!

Walking into Knarly Gav Studios, you’re hit with a tropical breeze and a wave of warmth. Not physical temperature warmth, especially on the snowy Sunday morning we went to visit, but the warmth of a man who makes art to create, spread, and feel love. Maybe it’s his childhood spent surfing in the Caribbean or his time living on islands in the American South, but the sun shines in his work and is ever present in his energy. Gav works out of a small Brooklyn studio that doubles as his wife’s jewelry workshop, a fairly unassuming space that is decorated sparsely yet filled to the brim with joy.


Gav started his career in his mid 30s in Savannah, GA. He attributes some of his near instant success to being a fully formed adult by the time he started, an advantage against his younger peers.. Now, 17 years later, his work has become serious Brooklyn iconography with his most recognizable design being the cat/watermelon. His work is always evolving, pulling inspiration from early American tattooers, further back than sailor Jerry, focusing on hand drawn images that look like tattoos, and look like modern art, and look like him.
