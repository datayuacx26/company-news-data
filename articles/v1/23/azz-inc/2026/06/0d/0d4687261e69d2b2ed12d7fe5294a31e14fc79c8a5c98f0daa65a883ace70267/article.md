# Social

## Building a culture focused on people, safety, and community impact


AZZ’s culture is built on safety, teamwork, accountability, integrity, and respect for employees, customers, and communities. Across operations throughout North America, AZZ invests in employee development, workplace safety, workforce engagement, and community involvement while fostering a collaborative environment designed to support long-term operational success.


Through its corporate TRAITS values, Trust, Respect, Accountability, Integrity, Teamwork, and Safety, AZZ works to create a workplace culture focused on collaboration, operational responsibility, employee support, and continuous improvement across its manufacturing and infrastructure operations.


## Ensuring employee development and workplace safety


AZZ continues to invest in employee training, workforce development, leadership growth, and workplace safety initiatives designed to support employees across its galvanizing, coil coating, and surface technologies operations. The company maintains a strong focus on operational safety, employee well-being, and continuous improvement through training programs, safety initiatives, operational safeguards, and ongoing workforce engagement efforts.


## Supporting communities where employees live and work


Community involvement remains an important part of AZZ’s culture. Through AZZ Cares and employee-led initiatives, employees support charitable organizations, fundraising efforts, volunteer programs, disaster relief initiatives, educational programs, and local community organizations throughout North America. AZZ remains committed to supporting employee growth, professional development, and opportunities that help individuals contribute, succeed, and reach their full potential throughout their careers.


AZZ also supports employees and families during times of hardship through the **[AZZ Cares Foundation](https://azzcaresfoundation.org/)** , which provides assistance during emergencies, natural disasters, and personal crises while reinforcing a culture focused on teamwork, compassion, and support.


## A people-focused approach to long-term success


AZZ believes strong operations are built on engaged employees, safe workplaces, responsible leadership, and a collaborative culture. Through continued investment in people, workplace safety, and community engagement, AZZ remains focused on supporting employees and strengthening the communities connected to its operations throughout North America.
