# Environmental

## Environmental responsibility through efficient operations and sustainable metal coating technologies


AZZ supports environmental responsibility through operational efficiency, resource management, emissions reduction initiatives, and metal coating technologies engineered to extend infrastructure and product service life. Across galvanizing, coil coating, and surface technologies, AZZ continues to invest in advanced manufacturing processes, recycling initiatives, energy efficiency improvements, and environmentally responsible operational practices.


Through durable corrosion protection systems, recyclable materials, advanced coating technologies, and manufacturing process improvements, AZZ helps customers support long-term infrastructure performance while reducing maintenance demands, material waste, and lifecycle costs across critical industrial and infrastructure applications.


## Supporting sustainable infrastructure through advanced metal coating solutions


AZZ’s galvanizing and coil coating operations support infrastructure and manufacturing applications requiring long-term durability, corrosion resistance, and operational reliability. Hot-dip galvanizing and coil coating technologies help extend the service life of steel and aluminum products while supporting reduced maintenance requirements, improved lifecycle value, and more efficient use of materials and resources.


The company’s operations support a broad range of applications across transportation, electrical infrastructure, renewable energy, construction, industrial manufacturing, HVAC, appliances, and engineered products throughout North America.


## Galvanizing solutions engineered for long-term lifecycle performance


For nearly seven decades, AZZ has helped protect critical infrastructure through[hot-dip galvanizing](https://www.azz.com/hot-dip-galvanizing/) and advanced[metal coating technologies](https://www.azz.com/surface-technologies/) engineered to resist corrosion and extend infrastructure service life. Hot-dip galvanizing helps reduce maintenance requirements while supporting long-term durability across transportation, utility, construction, industrial, and infrastructure applications. Hot-dip galvanizing also contains no Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs), supporting environmentally responsible corrosion protection across infrastructure applications.


The[long service life](https://www.azz.com/galvanizing-life-cycle-cost-calculator/) and recyclability of galvanized steel support infrastructure lifecycle performance while helping reduce replacement frequency, material waste, and long-term resource demands. Hot-dip galvanized steel may also contribute toward LEED® certification credits in applicable building and infrastructure projects.


## Coil coating technologies supporting environmental efficiency


[Coil coating](https://www.azz.com/coil-coating/) is recognized as one of the industry’s most efficient and environmentally responsible methods for applying coatings to metal substrates prior to fabrication. Centralized coil coating operations help reduce waste, improve production efficiency, lower emissions, optimize paint usage, and support more consistent coating performance compared to traditional field-applied finishing methods.


AZZ Precoat Metals continues investing in advanced manufacturing technologies and operational improvements that support resource efficiency, emissions reduction efforts, recyclable materials initiatives, and enhanced manufacturing performance across its operations.


## Environmental stewardship through continuous improvement


AZZ continues to focus on operational improvements designed to increase energy and resource efficiency, reduce greenhouse gas emissions intensity, optimize water usage, minimize waste streams, and support responsible manufacturing operations. Sustainability initiatives include emissions reduction projects, Industrial Assessment Center (IAC) audits, recycling and reuse programs, process optimization efforts, and ongoing investment in manufacturing technologies designed to improve operational performance and environmental efficiency.
