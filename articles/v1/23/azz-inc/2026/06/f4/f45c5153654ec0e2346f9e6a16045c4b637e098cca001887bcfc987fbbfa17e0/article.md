AZZ Metal Coatings supports industries critical to modern infrastructure, utilities, transportation, industrial manufacturing, and construction throughout North America. As North America’s largest hot-dip galvanizer of fabricated steel, AZZ combines industry-leading galvanizing expertise, spin galvanizing, and advanced surface technologies capabilities engineered to improve corrosion protection, durability, reliability, and asset performance in demanding environments.


## Metal coating expertise supporting essential markets across North America


From bridges, substations, utility structures, transportation infrastructure, datacenters, airports, and water systems to industrial facilities and engineered fabricated products, galvanized steel and specialized coating technologies continue to play a vital role in supporting stronger, more durable infrastructure and operations.


With one of the industry’s broadest metal coatings footprints, AZZ combines operational scale, responsive production support, advanced coating technologies, and decades of expertise to support evolving customer requirements across diverse metal coatings markets.


## Market trends driving demand for metal coating technologies


Infrastructure investment, utility modernization, industrial growth, reshoring manufacturing operations, and[sustainability](https://www.azz.com/esg/) and infrastructure lifecycle initiatives continue driving increased demand for corrosion protection and advanced metal coating technologies across North America.


As industries focus on improving asset longevity, reducing maintenance costs, strengthening supply chain reliability, and supporting dependable operational performance,[galvanizing](https://www.azz.com/galvanizing/) and[specialized coating systems](https://www.azz.com/surface-technologies/) provide durable, cost-effective solutions engineered for infrastructure exposed to moisture, corrosion, abrasion, and environmental wear.


**Key metal coating market drivers include:**


- Infrastructure and datacenter investment
- Utility and grid modernization
- Renewable energy growth
- Industrial manufacturing expansion
- Transportation infrastructure improvements
- Reshoring and domestic manufacturing growth
- Sustainability initiatives
