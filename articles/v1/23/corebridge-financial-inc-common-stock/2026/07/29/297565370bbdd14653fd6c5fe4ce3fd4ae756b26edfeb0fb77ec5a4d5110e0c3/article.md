# Meet the Corebridge Financial Team competing at the 2026 PGA Championship

As the Official Life and Retirement Partner of the PGA of America, Corebridge Financial is once again proud to sponsor a team of PGA of America Golf Professionals who have earned the right to compete at this year’s PGA Championship alongside the world’s best players.


The 2026 Corebridge Financial Team is made up of 20 PGA members– teaching instructors and professionals at golf courses across the country – who secured their place in the PGA Championship based on their performance at the 2026 PGA Professional Championship at Bandon Dunes in April. Led by PGA Professional champion Jesse Droemer, the Corebridge Team of pros will contend for the coveted Wanamaker Trophy at the PGA Championship, one of golf’s four “major” events, taking place May 14-17 at Aronimink Golf Club in Newtown Square, PA.


Corebridge is also proud to support PGA of America Golf Professionals off the course in helping them plan for retirement with confidence through the Golf Retirement Plus Program and PGA Deferred Compensation plan.
