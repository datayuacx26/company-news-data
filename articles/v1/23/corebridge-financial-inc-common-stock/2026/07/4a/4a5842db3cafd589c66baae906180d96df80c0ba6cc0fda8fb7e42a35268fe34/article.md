# Corebridge Financial announces third quarter 2024 results

Corebridge Financial, Inc. (NYSE: CRBG) announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2024.


Kevin Hogan, President and Chief Executive Officer of Corebridge, said, "Corebridge had a very strong quarter as we continued to create long-term value by leveraging our diversified business model, strong balance sheet and disciplined execution. I am grateful to our colleagues and for their outstanding work to deliver on our commitments quarter after quarter. Every day, we’re living our purpose to help people take action to plan, save for and achieve secure financial futures.”
