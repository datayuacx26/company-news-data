# Retirement plan in school

This month, Corebridge Financial and Miami-Dade County Public Schools are commemorating one of the earliest implementations of a 403(b) retirement plan in a public school. In October 1964, The Variable Annuity Life Insurance Company (VALIC), a Corebridge Financial company, issued a 403(b) plan to a teacher at Coral Gables Elementary who enrolled at $25 per pay period, helping to pioneer 403(b) employer contracts for educators in the United States.


To help us celebrate this milestone, Frank Buggs, a retired educator from Miami-Dade County Public Schools and one of our longest 403(b) workplace retirement plan participants, shared his thoughts about retirement and his long-lasting relationship with Corebridge. Frank, who opened his Corebridge account fifty-five years ago, has been enjoying retirement for the last twenty-six years as a father, grandfather, and great-grandfather.


**Q. At Corebridge, we believe "action is everything" when it comes to making goals and dreams become reality. What does "action is everything" mean to you?**


A. Without action, I wouldn’t have anything. With action, I’m having a good retirement.


**Q. How has Corebridge helped you prepare for retirement?**


A. I’ve been very happy with Corebridge, especially the service I received from their financial professionals. They’ve helped me keep up with my financial situation and met with me annually to review my plan.


**Q. How has your Corebridge financial professional helped you in your retirement confidence?**


A. When I first started teaching, I lived paycheck to paycheck. The best advice I received from a Corebridge financial professional was to start investing, and they provided guidance and advice.


**Q. What advice would you give a new teacher about saving for retirement?**


A. Start investing in your 403(b) retirement plan now, so you will have a better financial future.
