# Corebridge named to 2026 Fortune 500

**1 min read | Published: July 16, 2026**


Corebridge Financial has been named to the Fortune 500 for the second time since becoming an independent company in 2022. The recognition underscores the company’s growth as one of the largest U.S. companies by total revenue.


“Our inclusion on the Fortune 500 is a reflection of Corebridge's market leadership and ongoing financial strength,” said CEO Marc Costantini. “Most importantly, it reflects the trust our customers place in us and our commitment to being there when they need us.”


As one of the largest providers of retirement and life insurance solutions in the U.S., Corebridge proudly partners with financial professionals and institutions to help individuals plan, save for and achieve secure financial futures.[Explore our broad suite](https://www.corebridgefinancial.com/what-we-offer) of annuities, life insurance, employer retirement plans and institutional products, and wealth management services.


For more information about the Fortune 500, visit[Fortune](https://fortune.com/ranking/fortune500/) .
