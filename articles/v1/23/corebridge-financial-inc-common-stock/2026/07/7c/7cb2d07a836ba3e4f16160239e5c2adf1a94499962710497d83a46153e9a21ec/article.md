# Corebridge Financial named 2024 Best Place to Work for Disability Inclusion

We are proud to share that Corebridge Financial earned a top score on the[Disability Equality Index®](https://disabilityin.org/what-we-do/disability-equality-index/2024companies/) and has been recognized as a 2024 Best Place to Work for Disability Inclusion by[Disability:IN](https://disabilityin.org/) − the leading nonprofit resource for business disability inclusion worldwide. Corebridge is committed to creating an inclusive workplace focused on attracting, retaining, and developing top talent that fosters a culture of belonging for all employees. Receiving this meaningful recognition underscores our commitment to fostering a culture of inclusion across our organization. This would not be possible without our colleagues whose efforts each day support Corebridge’s Values.


We are honored to have received this award which recognizes our commitment to create and cultivate an inclusive workplace where everyone has an opportunity to thrive. To learn more about careers at Corebridge Financial, please visit our[Careers page](https://www.corebridgefinancial.com/who-we-are/careers) .


## About the Disability Equality Index


The Disability Equality Index is the leading independent, third-party resources for the annual benchmarking of corporate disability inclusion policies and programs by multinational corporations.


The Disability Equality Index is an objective and confidential disability rating tool designed to assist business in advancing inclusion practices across five scored categories: Culture & Leadership, Enterprise-Wide Access, Employment Practices, Community Engagement, and Supplier Diversity. Participating companies receive a score, on a scale of zero to 100, with those scoring 80 or higher earning the distinction of “Best Places to Work for Disability Inclusion” for the benchmark year.
