# Team Corebridge turns action into impact during Global Volunteer Month

3 min read | Published: May 29, 2026


During Global Volunteer Month, Team Corebridge turned action into impact. Throughout April, Corebridge colleagues from across the country collaborated with our Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) partners, community organizations and clients to host 23 volunteer events.


Together, we helped address food insecurity for children and families, provided comfort to kids impacted by the LA wildfires and children of deployed military parents, supported students from elementary school through college as they planned for strong financial futures, raised funds to help end homelessness, maintained a local park, and offered workshops, mentoring and guidance to help promote career readiness for underserved groups.


## Corebridge Global Volunteer Month by the numbers:


- **475+** Corebridge volunteers
- **23** volunteer events
- **21** nonprofit organizations and schools supported

## **Promoting Financial Literacy with Junior Achievement**


In addition to Global Volunteer Month, Corebridge also celebrated Financial Literacy Month with Junior Achievement USA, a CSR partner, helping deliver financial literacy and career exploration programming to students through hands-on learning and experiences.
