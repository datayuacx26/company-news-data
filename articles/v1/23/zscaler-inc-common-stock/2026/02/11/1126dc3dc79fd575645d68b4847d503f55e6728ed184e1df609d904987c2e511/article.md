# Inter Bank: 33PB Data Security with Zscaler DSPM, DLP & AI-SPM

Zscaler DSPM (Data Security Posture Management) provides complete visibility into where sensitive data resides, how it moves, and who has access — across clouds, SaaS, databases, VMs, and private GenAI apps. For Inter, DSPM replaced a near-impossible manual process of tracking sensitive data across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. It classifies sensitive data automatically, identifies misconfigurations and excessive privileges, and alerts in real time — reducing Inter's detection and containment time from days to minutes.


Zscaler AI-SPM provides visibility into private AI models and LLMs, scanning for sensitive data usage to prevent oversharing, accidental exposure, and compliance violations. For Inter — which operates 135 AI models including private LLMs deployed on Claude via Amazon Bedrock — AI-SPM works alongside DSPM to reduce risk of sensitive data being ingested, retained, or exposed through AI pipelines. This is critical for financial institutions where AI models regularly interact with customer PII and financial records.


Shadow data sprawl refers to sensitive data that accumulates in unauthorized, unmonitored, or forgotten locations — across IaaS storage, SaaS applications, endpoints, and GenAI tools — without IT or security teams' knowledge. For financial institutions like Inter, shadow data creates regulatory exposure (LGPD, GDPR, SEC), increases breach risk, and complicates M&A due diligence. Inter's Project Data Dome audit discovered shadow data sprawl across all four of these environments, which directly drove the adoption of Zscaler DSPM for comprehensive classification and remediation.


Zscaler's unified policy framework allows organizations to define and enforce data security policies consistently across multiple geographies from a single management console. For Inter, this means meeting regulatory requirements simultaneously across Brazil (LGPD), the United States, Argentina, and Europe (GDPR) — without manually configuring separate policies for each region. Auditable policy enforcement logs also simplify regulatory reporting and reduce the time and cost of compliance audits across different regulatory frameworks.


Zscaler DSPM provides an immediate, comprehensive view of the data security posture of acquired organizations — identifying sensitive data locations, misconfigurations, excessive access privileges, and compliance gaps from day one of integration. For Inter, this visibility transforms M&A security integration from a weeks-long manual assessment into a streamlined, automated process. Security teams can identify and remediate protection gaps faster, reducing the window of elevated risk during acquisition integration and ensuring acquired entities are brought into compliance quickly.
