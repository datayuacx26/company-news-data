# J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference

#


## ****


At Novo Nordisk, our priority is patients — especially those living with diabetes and obesity who still lack access to effective treatment. We look forward to presenting three priorities that are important to our business:


- Deliver sustainable, competitive growth and expand access to treatments, including the newly available GLP-1 pill for obesity in the U.S.
- Advance a strong pipeline built on decades of diabetes and obesity leadership, combining world-class R&D with external innovation.
- Accelerate business development to partner and invest in the most promising opportunities that fit our strategy.
