# A Breakdown of Lemonade’s 2024 Giveback

At Lemonade, our dedication to social impact is woven into everything we do. As a[Certified B Corp](https://www.lemonade.com/blog/the-b-corporation-explained/) and Public Benefit Corporation, we make doing good an integral part of our mission.


When new customers sign up for renters, homeowners, car, or pet insurance with Lemonade, they get to choose a charity that matters to them. The leftover money from any unused premiums is then donated to these selected causes, turning the Lemonade community’s choices into meaningful action.


Curious about how this works? Learn more[here](https://www.lemonade.com/giveback) .


> In 2024, Lemonade policyholders made it possible to donate $2,112,608 to 43 nonprofits around the world.


Dive in to discover how over $2.1 million in donations from our amazing Lemonade community made a real difference in 2024.
