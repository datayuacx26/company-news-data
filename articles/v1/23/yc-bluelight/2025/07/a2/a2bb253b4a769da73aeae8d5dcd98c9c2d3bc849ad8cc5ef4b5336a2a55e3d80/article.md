## **The Customer**


Wyattworks is a family-owned and operated home service company with deep roots. Started in 1980, the business is now managed by the next generation of the Wyatt family, who are focused on strategic expansion into new trades and markets. As dedicated Nexstar members, they are committed to using a proven playbook for growth.


## The Challenge: A Playbook They Couldn't Run


Wyattworks had invested heavily in the Nexstar Business Planning Workshop (BPW), a core strategy for their growth. But there was a major roadblock: data. The team was trapped in a state of "confusion, misalignment, and wasted time."


Pulling accurate numbers from ServiceTitan into manual spreadsheets was a "monstrosity" of a task. This created several critical business problems:


- **Inaccurate Data:** Different people interpreted data in different ways, leading to a lack of confidence in the numbers
- **Wasted Time:** Managers were bogged down in spreadsheets instead of leading their teams.
- **Failed Strategy:** The reporting process was so unworkable that their investment in the BPW was lost. As owner Andy Wyatt admitted, **"We actually missed out on a large investment in the business planning workshop because it was so hard to get our data... we couldn't even do it."**


The tipping point came when they realized their core growth strategy had stalled. They needed a solution that could provide accurate, automated data tailored specifically to the Nexstar model they were committed to.


## The Solution: A Dashboard Built for Their Business


Wyattworks had seen other digital scoreboards, but found "no one's really confident" in the numbers from generic, off-the-shelf tools. They needed a "specialty piece."


Bluelight stood out because it was purpose-built for Nexstar members. The implementation was highly collaborative, with the Bluelight team attending Wyattworks' daily huddles for nearly a year to ensure the dashboard was a perfect fit.


The result was a true "control panel for their business" that automated data collection and visualized it according to their specific operational model.
