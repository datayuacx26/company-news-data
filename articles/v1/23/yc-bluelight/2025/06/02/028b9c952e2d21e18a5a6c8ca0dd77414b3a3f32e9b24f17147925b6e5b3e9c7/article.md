# Bluelight + Nexstar: Official Partnership

**Nexstar coaches operators to plan. Bluelight keeps that plan alive once the year starts moving.**


We are proud to be a Nexstar Strategic Partner. If you run a Nexstar shop, you already do the hard part most operators never do: you build a real revenue plan. Driver-based, built bottoms-up, tied to how the business actually works. That discipline is rare, and it is why Nexstar shops grow.


Here is the problem every planned business runs into anyway. You build the plan once, and then the business moves. You add techs. You open a location. The mix shifts. By a few months in, the plan describes a shop that no longer exists, and the morning meeting drifts back to whatever numbers someone pulled by hand that day. The planning discipline is still there. The plan just went stale.


That is the gap Bluelight closes. We are not a reporting layer on top of your numbers. We are where the plan lives and stays current. You build it bottoms-up from the same drivers you already plan around, tech count, jobs per tech, conversion, average sale, by division. ServiceTitan actuals flow against it in real time. When the business changes, you change the driver and the target recomputes. The plan keeps describing the shop you are actually running, not the one you budgeted for in November.


So the work you put into planning the Nexstar way does not go stale by spring. It stays live, every division tracking the same current plan, every morning meeting running on numbers the team trusts.


We built Bluelight to work the way Nexstar shops already think. We are glad to be in your corner.


If you want the longer version of why annual plans go stale and what to do about it, we wrote it up here:[The Budget You Built Last November Describes a Shop You No Longer Run](https://usebluelight.com/blog/servicetitan-budget-goes-stale) .
