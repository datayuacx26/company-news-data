# Introducing the AssemblyAI piece for Activepieces

We have released new and improved models since this article was published. See[our docs](https://www.assemblyai.com/docs) for our most current model offerings.


We've been working with Activepieces to make[AssemblyAI's Voice AI](https://www.assemblyai.com/blog/announcing-universal-1-speech-recognition-model/) available to no-code and low-code builders. With the[AssemblyAI piece for Activepieces](https://www.assemblyai.com/docs/integrations/activepieces?ref=assemblyai.com) , you can use AssemblyAI's models to transcribe audio with speech recognition models, analyze audio with audio intelligence models, and build generative features on top of audio with LLMs using[LeMUR](https://www.assemblyai.com/blog/lemur/) .
You can supply audio to the AssemblyAI piece and connect the output of our models to other services in your Activepieces flows.


Adding an action from the AssemblyAI piece for Activepieces


Each action in the AssemblyAI piece has intuitive input parameters, so Activepieces users can quickly integrate AssemblyAI into their processes. For example, in the image below, you can see how to configure a list of[PII redaction policies](https://www.assemblyai.com/docs/audio-intelligence/pii-redaction?ref=assemblyai.com#pii-policies) . Users can add multiple policies to the list and select each from a dropdown menu.


Want to learn how to build with AssemblyAI and Activepieces? Visit[our documentation to learn how to get started and which actions are available in the AssemblyAI piece for Activepieces](https://www.assemblyai.com/docs/integrations/activepieces?ref=assemblyai.com) .
