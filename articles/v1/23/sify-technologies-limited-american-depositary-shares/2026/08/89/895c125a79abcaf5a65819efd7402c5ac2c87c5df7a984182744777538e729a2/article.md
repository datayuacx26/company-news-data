# Episode 1: Scaling AI: From Experimentation to Enterprise Transformation

#### Guest Speakers:


**Pushpendra Kumar,** , Product Head – Cloud, AI & IT Managed Services at Sify Technologies


#### Series:


Infinit Loop: Session 2 – Sify Podcast Series


#### Episode Summary:


In the first episode of Season 2, we explore one of the biggest questions facing enterprises today: How can organizations scale AI securely, efficiently, and at scale? Trisha sits down with Pushpendra Kumar, Product Head – Cloud, AI & IT Managed Services at Sify, to discuss why successful AI adoption requires much more than powerful models. From cloud and data centers to security, sovereignty, managed services, and cost optimization, this episode unpacks the complete AI ecosystem enterprises need to build for long-term success. Key topics covered:


- Why AI is fundamentally different from previous technology transformations
- The infrastructure, cloud, and networking foundations required to scale AI
- Balancing AI innovation with cost optimization through FinOps and AIOps
- Why data sovereignty, governance, and security are business-critical for AI
- Practical advice for CIOs and CTOs scaling AI across their organizations
