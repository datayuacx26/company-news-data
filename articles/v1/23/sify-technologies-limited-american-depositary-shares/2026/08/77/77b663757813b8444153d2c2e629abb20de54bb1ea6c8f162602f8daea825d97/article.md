#### Guest Speakers:


**Raj Upadhyay,** Head of Technology Infrastructure, Coforge


#### Series:


Infinit Loop: Session 2 – Sify Podcast Series


#### Episode Summary:


In Episode 2 of Season 2, we explore what it really takes to build technology foundations that are future-ready. Trisha sits down with Raj Upadhyay, Head of Technology Infrastructure, Coforge Limited, to discuss how organizations can move beyond AI experimentation and build resilient, outcome-driven technology ecosystems. From modern infrastructure and governance to culture transformation and measurable business value, this conversation explores what separates successful AI adoption from the rest.


- Why AI has evolved from a technology trend into a business enabler
- How modern infrastructure powers secure and scalable AI adoption
- Balancing innovation with stability while keeping pace with business demands
- Why tangible ROI should drive every technology decision
