# Autolus Therapeutics to Participate in Upcoming Investor Conferences

[PDF Version](https://autolus.gcs-web.com/node/10891/pdf)


LONDON and GAITHERSBURG, Md., May 27, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Autolus Therapeutics plc (Nasdaq: AUTL), a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company developing, manufacturing and delivering next-generation programmed T cell therapies, announces that the Company will present and host meetings at two upcoming investor conferences.


**William Blair’s 46th Annual Growth Stock Conference**
Date and time: June 2, 2026; 1:20pm CDT / 7:20pm BST
Location: Chicago, IL
Presenter: Chief Executive Officer Dr. Christian Itin


**Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference**
Date and time: June 3, 2026; 10:30am EDT / 3:30pm BST
Location: New York, NY
Presenter: Chief Executive Officer Dr. Christian Itin


Webcasts of the presentations will be available on the “Events” page in the “Investor Relations & Media” section of the Company’s website at[https://www.autolus.com/investor-relations-media/events/](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=ArdSAQyr5NsApIuQvYn9S7EGYzGFA8VmcKDjPpLM_kM3w7y6FXgw5Wu7Idq5flF8HmnCoMcfHSytT9qhTeocqY7xxh5eOlTHlSjySVVdgaeB4mF09FFRAjpRnGnrfOA0w027WIkDbdX1bEcG-Df6I_gGwqFwUftV8kgwYXs9i1Y_peW3ooQ9qxhCiJ2Jl54k) . A replay of the webcasts will be archived on the Company’s website for 90 days following the presentations.


**About Autolus Therapeutics plc**
Autolus Therapeutics plc (Nasdaq: AUTL) is a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company developing, manufacturing and delivering next-generation T cell therapies and candidates for the treatment of cancer and autoimmune disease. Using a broad suite of proprietary and modular T cell programming technologies, Autolus is engineering precisely targeted and controlled T cell therapies that are designed to better recognize target cells, break down their defense mechanisms and eliminate these cells. Autolus has a marketed therapy, AUCATZYL®, and a pipeline of product candidates in development for the treatment of hematological malignancies, solid tumors and autoimmune diseases.
