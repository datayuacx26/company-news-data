# Ichor Systems Ltd attends Semicon Europa

Ichor Systems Ltd is set to attend **Semicon Europa** , November 12th – 15th in Munich, Germany. We will be exhibiting at booth[B1-430.](https://expo.semi.org/europa2019/Public/eBooth.aspx?IndexInList=1&FromPage=Exhibitors.aspx&ParentBoothID=&ListByBooth=true&BoothID=460108)


In addition to our global license to sell **Lam Research** Single Chamber **Plasma Etch** equipment, we also sell selected Alliance models, namely the **4520XL** , **4420XL** and **4720XL,** along with other varieties of these models.


If you have any requirements for **Single Chamber** and/or **Multi Chamber** Plasma Etch equipment for Metal, Conductor and Dielectric applications please come and see us. Ichor’s technical expertise and rigorous processes provide the **Ichor Advantage** for the highest quality and the lowest risk to meet customer’s expectations when purchasing refurbished **Lam Research** etch equipment.


In addition to supplying equipment, Ichor helps clients proactively manage **obsolescence** on legacy platforms. Manufacturers are facing a growing problem of obsolescence and Ichor is working to help forecast and plan the lifetime of equipment to gain efficiencies and increased return on investment.


We are currently working on several upgrades and have recently **released a repair/repair exchange** **program for the 4520XL RF set** along with a new plug and play, fully supported RF set upgrade coming soon.


[Please contact Ichor Systems Ltd for further information.](https://www.ichorsystems.com/contact-us)
