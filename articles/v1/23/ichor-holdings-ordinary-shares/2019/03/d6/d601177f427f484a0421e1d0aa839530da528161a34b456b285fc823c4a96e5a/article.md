# Ichor Systems Ltd attends Semicon China

Ichor Systems Ltd is set to attend **Semicon China** , March 20th – 22nd in the Shanghai New International Expo Centre. We will be exhibiting at booth T2154–[see exhibitor list](https://expo.semi.org/china2019/public/eBooth.aspx?IndexInList=0&FromPage=Exhibitors.aspx&ParentBoothID=&ListByBooth=true&BoothID=431793) .


In addition to our Global License to sell **Lam Research** Single Chamber **Plasma Etch** Equipment, we now have the license to sell selected models on the production proven multi chamber **Alliance A6** platform.


If you have any requirements for **Single Chamber** and/or **Multi Chamber** Plasma Etch Equipment for Metal, Conductor and Dielectric applications please come see us. Ichor’s technical expertise and rigorous processes provide the **Ichor Advantage** for highest quality & lowest risk to meet customer’s expectations when purchasing refurbished **Lam Research** Etch Equipment.


In addition to supplying equipment, Ichor helps clients proactively manage **obsolescence** on **legacy** platforms. Manufacturers are facing a growing problem of obsolescence and Ichor is working to help forecast and plan the lifetime of equipment to gain efficiencies and increase ROI.


For more information please contact Ichor Systems LTD on our[Contact Us Page](https://www.ichorsystems.com/contact-us) .
