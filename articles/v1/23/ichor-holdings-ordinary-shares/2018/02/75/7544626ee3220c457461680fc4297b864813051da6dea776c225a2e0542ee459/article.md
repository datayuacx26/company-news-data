# Ichor Ships First Advanced Dielectric System to Finland

**Ichor Systems Ltd** has shipped its first SCE 4520XL Advanced Dielectric Etch System, to Finland.


Ichor is the most experienced supplier of Lam Research refurbished Plasma Etch semiconductor systems and is the Global license holder for selling refurbished Lam Research dielectric etch equipment. Along with its experience serving semiconductor fabs, Ichor is continuing to expand its presence in niche markets.


Ichor’s expertise in semiconductor manufacturing and engineering has enabled the exploration of new applications in the MEMS and IoT sectors, whilst partnering with some of the most innovative technology research organizations in these fields. Ichor is already established as a valued leader in tool refurbishment and[obsolescence management](http://www.ichorsystems.com/blog/the-ticking-time-bomb-of-obsolescence)


for many high technology semiconductor manufacturing organizations.
