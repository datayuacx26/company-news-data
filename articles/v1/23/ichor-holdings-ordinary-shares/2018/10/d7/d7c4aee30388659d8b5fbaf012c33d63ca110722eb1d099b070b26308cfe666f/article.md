# Ichor Systems Ltd Attends Semicon Europa

Ichor Systems Ltd is set to attend **Semicon Europa** , November 13th – 16th in Munich, Germany. We will be exhibiting at booth[A4329](https://expo.semi.org/europa2018/Public/eBooth.aspx?IndexInList=0&FromPage=Exhibitors.aspx&ParentBoothID=&ListByBooth=true&BoothID=420599) and will also be hosting our famous whisky tasting event as in years past – **[see exhibitor list](https://expo.semi.org/europa2018/Public/eBooth.aspx?IndexInList=0&FromPage=Exhibitors.aspx&ParentBoothID=&ListByBooth=true&BoothID=420599)** .


In addition to our Global License to sell **Lam Research** Single Chamber **Plasma Etch** Equipment, we now have the license to sell selected models on the production proven multi chamber **Alliance A6** platform.


If you have any requirements for **Single Chamber** and/or **Multi Chamber** Plasma Etch Equipment for Metal, Conductor and Dielectric applications please come and see us. Ichor’s technical expertise and rigorous processes provide the **Ichor Advantage** for highest quality & lowest risk to meet customer’s expectations when purchasing refurbished **Lam Research** Etch Equipment.


In addition to supplying equipment, Ichor helps clients proactively manage **obsolescence** on legacy platforms. Manufacturers are facing a growing problem of obsolescence and Ichor is working to help forecast and plan the lifetime of equipment to gain efficiencies and increase ROI.


Please **[contact](https://www.ichorsystems.com/contact-us) Ichor Systems Ltd** for further information.
