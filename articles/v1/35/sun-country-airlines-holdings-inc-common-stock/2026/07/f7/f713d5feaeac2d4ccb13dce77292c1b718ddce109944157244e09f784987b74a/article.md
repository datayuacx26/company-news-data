# Summertime is still on your side: Last-minute summer getaways

There's still plenty of summer left to explore. Whether you're craving mountain adventures, beach days, or a charming coastal escape, these five destinations offer unforgettable experiences—and they're all just a Sun Country flight away.


[Kalispell,](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-kalispell)[Montana](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-kalispell)


If your ideal vacation includes alpine lakes, mountain peaks, and unforgettable scenery, Kalispell belongs at the top of your list. As the gateway to Glacier National Park, it's the perfect place to unplug and soak up the beauty of summer.


**Don't miss:**


-


Driving the iconic Going-to-the-Sun Road


-


Hiking to breathtaking alpine lakes


-


Exploring downtown Kalispell's local shops and breweries


-


Whitewater rafting or kayaking on the Flathead River


[Asheville,](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-asheville)[North Carolina](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-asheville)


Nestled in the Blue Ridge Mountains, Asheville is a summer favorite thanks to its cooler mountain temperatures, vibrant arts scene, and incredible food and craft beer culture. With many of the region's iconic trails, waterfalls, and scenic drives welcoming visitors once again, it's the perfect destination to mix outdoor adventure with a laid-back mountain town atmosphere.


**Don't miss:**


-


Driving a section of the Blue Ridge Parkway


-


Touring the historic Biltmore Estate


-


Exploring Asheville's breweries and local restaurants


-


Hiking to waterfalls in nearby Pisgah National Forest


[Myrtle Beach, South Carolina](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-myrtle-beach)


Nothing says summer quite like Myrtle Beach. With 60 miles of sandy coastline, lively boardwalk attractions, and plenty of family-friendly fun, it's an easy choice for a spontaneous getaway.


**Don't miss:**


-


Walking the Myrtle Beach Boardwalk


-


Riding the SkyWheel


-


Fresh seafood overlooking the ocean


-


Championship golf courses and mini golf alike


[Portland, Maine](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-portland-maine)


Cool ocean breezes, historic streets, and some of New England's best seafood make Portland a perfect late-summer destination. Wander the cobblestone streets of the Old Port before setting out to explore nearby lighthouses and rocky coastline.


**Don't miss:**


-


Fresh lobster rolls and local oysters


-


Exploring the Old Port district


-


Visiting Portland Head Light


-


Harbor cruises and island ferries


-


Read our full Portland Stories feature


[here](https://stories.suncountry.com/post/editors-pick-fall-in-love-with-portland-maine) .


[San Diego, California](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-san-diego)


With near-perfect weather year-round, San Diego is always a good idea—but summer is especially magical. From laid-back beach towns to world-class attractions and incredible sunsets, there's something for every type of traveler.


**Don't miss:**


-


Relaxing at La Jolla Cove


-


Exploring Balboa Park


-


Visiting the San Diego Zoo or Safari Park


-


Strolling the historic Gaslamp Quarter


-


Watching the sunset from Coronado Beach


Summer isn't over yet, and your next adventure could be just a few clicks away. Whether you're chasing mountain views, coastal charm, or sunshine by the beach, now's the perfect time to book your last-minute getaway. Get to Going.
