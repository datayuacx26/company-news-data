# Don’t miss it: Our 2026 destination list

Good travel doesn’t need fanfare, just a smart plan and the right destination. From mountain towns to island escapes, these ten spots offer the kind of trip that stays with you. All reachable nonstop, all ready when you are.


[Asheville, NC](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-asheville)


Creative and calm with mountain views in every direction. Asheville’s downtown is packed with indie shops, craft breweries, and just enough weird to make it memorable. It’s the kind of place where time slows down in a good way.


[Missoula, MT](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-Missoula)


Missoula blends riverfront ease with a punch of unexpected culture. One part wild, one part artsy, all wrapped in big Montana sky. Come for the trails, stay for the bookstores and breweries.


[Aruba](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-aruba)


Steady sunshine, warm water, and no rush whatsoever. Aruba does easy living right: beachfront breakfasts, late afternoon swims, and a breeze that takes the edge off everything. Your only job here is to show up and enjoy.


[Traverse City, MI](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-traverse-city)


Small-town charm with Great Lakes energy. You’ll find wineries, farm stands, and sunsets that feel like a personal reward. Whether you opt for a kayak or a nap, it’s all time well spent.


[New Orleans, LA](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-new-orleans)


Rich with rhythm, flavor, and stories around every corner. From beignets to brass bands, New Orleans invites you to lose track of time and lean in. It’s not just a destination, it’s a mood.


[Kalispell / Glacier National Park, MT](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-kalispell)


A straight shot to one of the country’s most stunning national parks. Kalispell brings you close to Glacier’s peaks, trails, and that quiet awe you can’t get from a screen. Pack those hiking boots and hit the trail.


[Cozumel, MX](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-cozumel)


Island pace, reef colors, and nothing to prove. Cozumel is all about the in-between moments – hammock naps, street tacos, and quiet snorkeling spots. Easy to get to, hard to leave.


[Savannah, GA](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-Savannah)


Moss-draped oaks, old streets, and a hush that makes you want to whisper. Savannah’s beauty isn’t loud, it’s all in the details. Ghost stories, garden squares, and southern comfort with style.


[Charleston, SC](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-charleston)


History in the walls, salt in the air, and food worth talking about. Charleston walks that line between refined and relaxed like no place else. It’s a city that invites you to linger.


[Vancouver, BC](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-Vancouver) ****


Mountains, ocean, and global flavor all in one view. Vancouver blends natural drama with urban cool. It’s a place to hike, eat well, and breathe easier. Come curious, leave full.


The best trips don’t shout, they just feel right. Wherever 2026 takes you, these spots offer something worth showing up for. All that’s left to do is pick one.
