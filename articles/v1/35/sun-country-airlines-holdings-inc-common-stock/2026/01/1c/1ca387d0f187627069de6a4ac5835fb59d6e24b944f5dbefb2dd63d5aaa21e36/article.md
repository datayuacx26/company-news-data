# Your guide to our dry-weather destinations

After spending the holidays packed into a house that somehow felt like a sauna, with that central heating making everything sweatier than it should be, it’s time to take a deep breath somewhere that doesn’t make you want to hyperventilate.


We know Uncle Bob already cornered you by the eggnog with a lecture about “how humid hot is the worst kind of hot,” and it probably swayed you to start dreaming about dry, sunny escapes. So, we put together a list of our most sun‑soaked, zero‑sweat, can‑actually‑breathe escapes to get away this winter.


[Phoenix, AZ](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-phoenix)


Sunshine, cacti, and desert adventures await in Phoenix. Hike the Sonoran Desert trails, explore the Desert Botanical Garden, or tee off at a golf course that feels endless under crystal-clear skies. Downtown’s art galleries, vibrant restaurants, and quirky coffee shops make it perfect for both adventure and laid-back days.


*Multiple times per week | Days vary*


[Tucson, AZ](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-tucson)


Just south of Phoenix, Tucson is dry, sunny, and quietly magnetic. Explore Saguaro National Park or stroll historic streets lined with adobe architecture. Cool desert evenings are perfect for stargazing or enjoying a patio dinner with tacos and tequila. It’s the kind of place that makes winter feel like a gentle hug instead of a frostbite warning.


*Seasonal | 1 flight per week | Day varies*


[Palm Springs, CA](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-palm-springs)


Think sun, pools, and effortlessly cool vibes. Palm Springs is a desert playground for those who love chic boutique shopping, mid-century modern architecture tours, and hiking in the nearby Joshua Tree or Indian Canyons. Poolside lounges, craft coffee, and sunsets that make your phone look professional—Palm Springs has it all.


*Sep 11, 2025 – May 25, 2026 | Up to 7 flights per week | Daily*


[Las Vegas, NV](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-las-vegas)


Bright lights, dry heat, and entertainment on demand. Beyond the casinos, enjoy world-class shows, poolside cabanas, foodie adventures, and desert trails just minutes outside the Strip. Vegas in winter is surprisingly manageable: dry, sunny days make exploring the city and nearby Red Rock Canyon a breeze.


*Multiple days per week | Days vary*


[Los Cabos, Mexico](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-los-cabos)


Where desert meets the sea, Los Cabos is your ultimate winter escape. Lounge on pristine beaches, go whale watching, or explore the bustling marina with shops and restaurants. Evenings bring vibrant sunsets and ocean breezes that make you forget Minnesota frost exists.


*Dec 6, 2025 – Apr 4, 2026 | Up to 5 flights per week | Sun, Mon, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat*
