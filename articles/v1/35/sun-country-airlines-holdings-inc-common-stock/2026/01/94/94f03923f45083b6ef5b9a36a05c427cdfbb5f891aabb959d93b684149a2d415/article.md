# 7 Beaches we can’t stop talking about: Ranked from great to unforgettable

This list started like many of our favorite office conversations, with someone casually mentioning beaches and suddenly the whole team had an opinion. Beaches are something we’re genuinely passionate about here at Sun Country Airlines We spend our days talking about warm destinations, sharing favorite escapes, and debating which shorelines truly deserve the top spots. The best ones have the same effect every time—you exhale the moment your feet hit the sand.


These beaches aren’t just beautiful on paper. They’re the spots people return to, talk about long after their trip ends, and plan vacations around again and again. We’ve narrowed it down to seven favorites, each with its own vibe, personality, and reason to make it the focus of your next getaway.


**[#7](https://stories.suncountry.com/all-posts/hashtags/7) La Jolla Shores**


[San Diego, California](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-san-diego)


[La Jolla Shores](https://www.sandiego.gov/lifeguards/beaches/shores) is about a 25-minute drive from the airport and delivers classic California coastal calm. The beach is wide, walkable, and backed by an easygoing neighborhood with coffee shops and casual lunch spots close by. Waves stay manageable, the scenery feels effortlessly beautiful, and the overall vibe is relaxed without trying too hard. It’s a great pick if you want ocean air, sunshine, and a slower pace without leaving the mainland.


**[#6](https://stories.suncountry.com/all-posts/hashtags/6) Siesta Beach**


[Sarasota, Florida](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-sarasota-bradenton)


About 25 minutes from the airport,


[Siesta Beach](https://www.siestakeychamber.com/explore-the-key/our-beaches/siesta-beach/) is famous for its powdery white sand that somehow stays cool underfoot. The shoreline is expansive, the water is usually calm, and sunsets feel like a nightly ritual. It’s approachable, easy to navigate, and perfect for travelers who want a straightforward beach escape that still feels special.


**[#5](https://stories.suncountry.com/all-posts/hashtags/5) Bavaro Beach**


[Punta Cana, Dominican Republic](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-punta-cana)


[Bavaro Beach](https://www.godominicanrepublic.com/things-to-do/playa-bavaro) sits roughly 25 minutes from the airport and stretches on and on, making it easy to find your own space. The water stays warm, the sand is soft, and the setup is ideal if you want to unpack once and let the beach set the rhythm for your days. This is beach travel at its most effortless.


**[#4](https://stories.suncountry.com/all-posts/hashtags/4) Eagle Beach**


[Oranjestad, Aruba](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-aruba)


Aruba Tourism Authority


[Eagle Beach](https://www.aruba.com/us/explore/eagle-beach) is about a 15-minute drive from the airport and known for wide-open sand and famously reliable sunshine. Crowds thin out quickly, the water stays gentle, and the pace feels unhurried from morning to sunset. It’s a favorite for travelers who want consistency, comfort, and beautiful beach days without surprises.


**[#3](https://stories.suncountry.com/all-posts/hashtags/3) Seven Mile Beach**


[Grand Cayman](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-grand-cayman)


Visit Cayman Islands


Just 10 minutes from the airport,


[Seven Mile Beach](https://www.visitcaymanislands.com/en-us/things-to-do/beaches/seven-mile-beach) delivers clear water, soft sand, and a polished feel that still feels relaxed. Everything is close and easy, from swimming spots to sunset dinners, which makes planning simple and days feel longer. It’s an ideal balance of calm, convenience, and unmistakable beauty.


**[#2](https://stories.suncountry.com/all-posts/hashtags/2) Flamingos Beach**


[Puerto Vallarta, Mexico](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-puerto-vallarta)


Flamingos Beach is one of the longest and most inviting stretches along Banderas Bay, known for its soft sand, gentle waves, and wide shoreline perfect for swimming, paddleboarding, or long sunset walks. The


[Krystal Grand Nuevo Vallarta resort](https://suncountry.com/vacation/select?origin=MSP&destination=PVR&startDate=2026-02-25&travelers=%7B%22numberOfAdults%22:2,%22numberOfChildren%22:0,%22numberOfLapInfants%22:0,%22numberOfSeatInfants%22:0,%22childrenAgeList%22:%5B%5D%7D&trajectory=ROUND_TRIP&endDate=2026-03-01) , featured in our vacation packages, sits right on this beautiful beach, giving travelers easy access to everything Flamingos Beach has to offer.


**[#1](https://stories.suncountry.com/all-posts/hashtags/1) Grace Bay Beach**


[Turks and Caicos](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-turks-and-caicos)


*Visit Turks and Caicos Islands*


Consistently ranked among the best beaches in the world,


[Grace Bay](https://www.visittci.com/providenciales/grace-bay-beach) sits about 15 minutes from the airport and feels almost unreal in person. The sand is soft and bright, the water is shallow and crystal clear, and the pace is slow in the best possible way. It’s the kind of place where beach days don’t need an agenda and scenery does all the work. If you’re picking one beach to build a trip around, this is it.


Seven beaches, seven different moods, one simple goal: more time on the sand, less time getting there. Wherever you land on this list, the beach is ready. Get To Going.
