# Our flight schedule is now open through April 2027

Summer is here but 2027 winter and spring travel is calling.


If escaping winter is already on your mind, you're in luck. Our schedule is now open through spring of 2027, making it easier than ever to start planning your next warm-weather getaway.


We've extended our schedule through April 13, 2027, giving you plenty of time to lock in spring break plans, book your favorite winter escape, get your holiday travel squared away, or simply give yourself an adventure to look forward to.


Our winter/spring schedule includes even more of the destinations you love most. We're adding flights to favorites like


[Fort Myers](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-fort-myers) ,


[Orlando](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-orlando) ,


[Las Vegas](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-las-vegas) ,


[Phoenix](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-phoenix) ,


[Cancun](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-cancun) ,


[Mazatlán](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-mazatlan) , and


[Puerto Vallarta](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-puerto-vallarta) , giving Midwest travelers plenty of opportunities to trade snow boots for sandals.


We're also bringing back nonstop service between Minneapolis and Montego Bay, Minneapolis and Melbourne, Florida, and resuming seasonal service between Duluth and Fort Myers—making it even easier for more communities to get away.


"Our winter 2027 schedule reflects a return to our 2024 flying levels with additional flights to many of our most popular leisure destinations, the return of service to communities like Melbourne and Duluth, and increased international frequencies," said Sun Country President Eric Levenhagen.


Whether it’s a beach vacation, visiting family, or checking another destination off your bucket list, booking early gives you the best chance to secure the flights and dates you want. With service to 11 Florida destinations, six in Mexico, and 12 destinations across the Caribbean and Central America, there's no shortage of warm-weather inspiration.


Wherever you're headed next winter or spring, we've got a seat waiting for you. Start planning today and Get to Going.
