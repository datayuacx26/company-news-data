# Celebrating Pride with Our Twin Cities Community

As Pride Month comes to a close, Sun Country is proud to reflect on this past weekend spent celebrating with our hometown community at the Twin Cities Pride Parade.


Employees, family members, friends, and colleagues came together in downtown Minneapolis to walk the parade route, connect with the crowd, and show support for the LGBTQ+ community. It was a colorful, high-energy day filled with plenty of smiles, Sun Country spirit, and moments that reminded us why showing up matters.


For Sun Country, participating in Pride is one way we celebrate the people who make our company and community stronger. Pride Month is a time to recognize the LGBTQ+ community, honor the history behind Pride, and continue supporting a workplace where employees feel welcomed, valued, and able to be themselves.


Thank you to everyone who joined us, cheered us on, helped coordinate behind the scenes, or made space to celebrate with us this month.
