# Our flight schedule is now open through December 2026

Attention, future travelers. Your 2026 fall plans just got a head start.


We’ve


[extended our schedule through December 15, 2026](https://ir.suncountry.com/news-releases/news-release-details/sun-country-airlines-extends-schedule-through-fall-2026) , giving you more time to map out post-Labor Day escapes, lock in MEA weekend plans, secure Thanksgiving travel, and line up those peak leaf-peeping getaways. The calendar is open — now the only question is where you’ll go.


This fall extension puts the spotlight on some of the season’s most popular destinations, including


[Boston](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-boston) ,


****[Las Vegas](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-las-vegas) ,


[Phoenix](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-phoenix) **,**[Orlando](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-orlando) **,**[Fort Myers](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-fort-myers) , and


[Syracuse](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-syracuse) . Whether you’re chasing vibrant autumn color, warm sunshine, or city energy, you can now book early and travel nonstop.


For our friends in Wisconsin, we’re also bringing back Eau Claire to Las Vegas service for fall 2026. Last served in fall 2024, this relaunch builds on the strong momentum and success of our winter service to Fort Myers.


> “Planning your summer travel should be simple, rewarding and stress-free. With nonstop flights and extended dates through 2026, we’re making it easier than ever for travelers to get where they want to go,” said SVP and Chief Commercial Officer Colton Snow.


Early planning means fewer surprises and more flexibility. No last-minute scrambling. No unnecessary connections. Just choose your destination, pick your dates, and pack your bag.


Wherever fall 2026 takes you, we’ll be ready. Get To Going.
