# Nonstop to Mexico: Our full list of destinations to explore

We all know Mexico is the go-to for sun, surf, and guac that’ll ruin that store bought stuff for life—but did you know there’s more to explore beyond your resort pool in Cancun? Picture this: diving into the world’s second-largest coral reef, strolling cobblestone streets that date back centuries, or watching humpback whales breach against a fiery Pacific sunset.


If you’re a planner (and we know you are), this roundup is your golden ticket to narrowing down the


*where* so you can focus on the


*when.* With nonstop flights and schedules built around your busy life, we make it easy to turn daydreams into itineraries. Whether you’re craving adventure, relaxation, or a little of both, we’ve got the perfect destination south of the border waiting for you. Let’s dive in.


[Cancun/Riviera Maya](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-cancun)


[Cancun](https://stories.suncountry.com/post/travel-guide-cancun-and-riviera-maya-resort-areas) offers endless adventure, from the famous white-sand beaches to world-class resorts. Explore ancient Mayan ruins or snorkel in cenotes, natural sinkholes with crystal-clear waters. It’s a paradise for sun-seekers and history buffs alike.


*When to go: June 4, 2025 – August 9, 2026 (daily)*


[Cozumel](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-cozumel) ****


Cozumel’s underwater wonders make it a top spot for scuba divers and snorkelers. With its relaxed island vibe, it’s also perfect for those who want to unwind on quiet beaches. Don’t miss the opportunity to explore the island by bike or jeep.


*When to go: December 6, 2025 – April 11, 2026 (Mon, Wed, Fri, Sat)*


**


[Mazatlan](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-mazatlan) ****


[Mazatlan](https://stories.suncountry.com/post/escape-the-chill-mazatl%C3%A1n-is-waiting-for-you) blends Old World charm with modern coastal beauty. Wander the historic district, filled with colorful buildings and plazas, or relax on the city’s long stretches of golden beaches. It’s a favorite for families and culture lovers.


*When to go: November 15, 2025 – April 11, 2026 (Sun, Mon, Wed, Sat)*


[Ixtapa/Zihuatanejo](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-ixtapa-zihuatanejo) ****


Two destinations in one: Ixtapa’s upscale resorts and Zihuatanejo’s fishing village charm create a unique blend of luxury and authenticity. Relax on pristine beaches or indulge in fresh seafood while taking in breathtaking sunsets.


*When to go: December 17, 2025 – April 5, 2026 (Sun, Wed, Sat)*

[Los Cabos](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-los-cabos) ****


Los Cabos is all about dramatic landscapes where the desert meets the sea. Spend your days whale-watching, exploring Cabo San Lucas’s nightlife, or soaking in the relaxed vibe of San Jose del Cabo.


*When to go: December 6, 2025 – April 4, 2026 (Sun, Mon, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat)*


[Puerto Vallarta/Riviera Nayarit](https://suncountry.com/flights/en/flights-to-puerto-vallarta) ****


A mix of beach bliss and cobblestone charm,


[Puerto Vallarta](https://stories.suncountry.com/post/area-guide-where-to-go-and-what-to-do-in-puerto-vallarta) offers vibrant local markets, lush jungle hikes, and a lively nightlife scene. Riviera Nayarit’s tranquil beaches are perfect for quiet escapes or luxurious stays.


*When to go: October 25, 2025 – April 18, 2026 (daily)*
