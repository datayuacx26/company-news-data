# Who can administer Botox?

Botox is a common cosmetic procedure that can improve aesthetic concerns such as fine lines and wrinkles. Sometimes, it is also used to relieve symptoms of certain medical conditions. If you’re considering Botox treatments, you may be wondering where you should go.


Not just anyone can inject Botox. Each state’s laws outline the professionals who are legally allowed to safely administer Botox injections. ## What is Botox?


Botox, or botulinum toxin, is a neurotoxin that can safely freeze or temporarily relax the facial muscles in small, controlled doses. It is primarily used as a cosmetic treatment to address the common aesthetic concerns of aging, like wrinkles and fine lines.


Botox is FDA-approved as a cosmetic treatment, but it is also sometimes used to reduce symptoms of conditions like excessive sweating, chronic migraines and TMJ disorder.


Botox is considered a safe and effective cosmetic treatment1. The results are temporary and typically last between three to six months. Patients often need to return for sessions to maintain results.


## Different types of cosmetic providers


As mentioned, state laws determine which professionals are allowed to administer Botox in each state. Some types of providers may be allowed to administer Botox in some states and unable to in others.


Let’s take a look at some of the different types of cosmetic providers that can administer these injections.


### Doctors (MD, DO)


Plastic surgeons and dermatologists are the most common types of doctors who perform Botox injections. But it’s also become increasingly common for other doctors, including anesthesiologists, neurosurgeons, family medicine doctors, pain management specialists, etc., to gain expertise in Botox injections.


Regardless of the doctor’s specialty, most experience and training for nonsurgical aesthetic procedures like Botox is gained after formal medical school training. It’s most important to assess the provider’s experience, reviews, gallery of before/after photos, and your rapport during a consultation to decide whether the provider is the best fit for you.


### Physicians Assistants (PA), Registered Nurses (RN), Nurse Practitioners (NP)


Physicians Assistants (PAs), Registered Nurses (RNs) and Nurse Practitioners (NPs) can also administer Botox in many states if they receive the proper training and meet the regulatory requirements. Training often involves a specialized injector course that teaches technique, safety and patient care.


While many states allow physician assistants (PAs), Registered Nurses (RNs) and Nurse Practitioners (NPs) to perform Botox injections on their own, some states require supervision from a physician or medical director.


### Dentists


Many states now allow dentists to administer Botox injections, but the stipulations can get a bit trickier for dentists than other providers. As with many providers, dentists must complete specialized training and meet certain qualifications to offer Botox injections.


But, some state laws stipulate that dentists can only administer Botox injections as part of a patient’s dental treatment plan, rather than stand-alone treatments. Some states may only allow dentists to administer Botox in areas related to the mouth and jaw. With that being said, dentists may be a convenient and safe option for those looking for Botox treatment. But, depending on your state laws, you may need to seek treatment elsewhere if you want Botox for aesthetic purposes.


### Estheticians


Estheticians are skin care professionals who are trained in enhancing the skin’s health and appearance with noninvasive treatments. Common procedures performed by estheticians include facials, microdermabrasion, waxing, chemical peels, laser hair removal and more. For the most part, estheticians are only permitted to perform surface-level skin care and cosmetic treatments.


However, estheticians may assist with Botox treatments by prepping patients, assisting the physician during the session and providing post-care instructions. In very few states, estheticians who meet certain qualifications may be allowed to administer the injections under doctor supervision.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is physician supervision?


Some states require physician supervision for Botox treatments. This means that a licensed physician must oversee the session, even if they aren’t administering the injections directly. In short, supervising physicians ensure the procedure is conducted safely and correctly.


### What is a Botox injector course


A Botox injector course is a training program that teaches safe and proper techniques for administering Botox injections. Many states require professionals to complete one of these courses before they can administer Botox.
