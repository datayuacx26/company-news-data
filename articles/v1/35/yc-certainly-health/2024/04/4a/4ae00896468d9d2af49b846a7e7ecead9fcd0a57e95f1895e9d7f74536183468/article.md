## What is Masseter Botox?


Masseter Botox is a Botox treatment that targets the masseter muscles of the jaw. These muscles are primarily responsible for helping with jaw movements like chewing and talking. Masseter muscles can also give the jaw a more square shape if they’re strong or prominent enough.


Masseter Botox can relax the muscles in the jaw, which can have both cosmetic and medical benefits. Masseter Botox for cosmetic purposes is used to soften and slim the jawline, giving it a more countered and less square look. Masseter Botox for medical purposes can alleviate symptoms of TMJ disorder.


TMJ disorder encompasses a variety of disorders that affect the temporomandibular joints, which connect the jaw to the skull. Common symptoms include jaw pain, headaches, stiffness and teeth grinding. By relaxing the masseter muscles, patients may find relief from these symptoms.


## How it works


Masseter Botox works the same way as any Botox treatment but targets the masseter muscles. Most other Botox treatments seek to reduce the signs of aging by targeting areas of the face where wrinkles and fine lines are prominent.


Botox is a neurotoxin that is injected in a tiny, controlled amount to freeze muscles in the face. This causes the muscles to relax, which can reduce the appearance of wrinkles and fine lines. In the case of masseter Botox, this relaxing effect can slim the appearance of the jaw and relieve tension that contributes to TMJ disorder.


## What are the benefits?


As mentioned, masseter Botox offers both cosmetic and medical benefits1. Let’s take a closer look at the primary benefits that masseter Botox offers:


- **Cosmetic benefits** - Masseter muscles can make the jaw appear more square. Masseter Botox can relax and soften the appearance of these muscles, which slims the jawline and gives it a more V-shaped contour.
- **Pain relief** - Masseter Botox’s relaxing effects on the jaw muscles can relieve tension in the jaw. This can reduce the jaw pain and discomfort associated with TMJ disorders.
- **Headache relief** - Tension headaches are another common symptom of TMJ disorders and bruxism (teeth grinding). Relaxing the muscle may reduce the severity and frequency of headaches associated with these conditions.
- **Teeth grinding** - Relaxing the masseter muscles may also help reduce bruxism (teeth grinding), which can exacerbate TMJ disorder, wear on teeth and cause other uncomfortable symptoms.
- **Improved movement** - TMJ disorders can make jaw movements like chewing and talking difficult or uncomfortable. Masseter Botox may help make jaw movement easier.
- **Nonsurgical** - Masseter Botox is nonsurgical and minimally invasive. This makes it an appealing solution for those who want to contour their face easily or those who want to try an alternative treatment path for TMJ disorder, bruxism and other conditions.
- **Temporary, but long-lasting** - Botox effects are temporary, so if you don’t like the results they will fade gradually over time. On the other hand, if you like the results and if the treatment provides relief from TMJ disorder symptoms, it lasts much longer than daily medications. Masseter Botox treatments can last for months.
- **Convenient** - Masseter Botox is minimally invasive and sessions usually take less than 30 minutes. Patients can also resume their normal activities almost immediately.


It’s also worth noting that there are side effects and considerations to keep in mind with masseter Botox, as with any cosmetic treatment. The freezing effect of Botox can sometimes affect expressiveness.


Also, masseter Botox has a special consideration since the masseter muscles are near the risorius muscle. Misplaced injections in the risorius muscle can affect your smile and lead to jowls. That is why it’s essential to find an experienced and knowledgeable masseter Botox injector.


## How long do Masseter and Jawline Botox results last?


Masseter Botox and jawline Botox treatments are temporary. But, you can expect results to last about three to six months.


You can expect to start seeing results in about three to five days, with many people seeing full results within a week.2 It’s important to closely follow any post-care instructions from your Botox provider to ensure optimal healing and results.


## Masseter Botox Cost Breakdown


The cost of masseter Botox injections can vary widely across providers. Factors that affect cost include your location, the experience of your provider and the number of Botox units needed for treatment.


The average cost of Botox units in the U.S. is around $15 per unit. So, if masseter Botox treatments use an average of 40 to 60 units, treatment costs will typically range from $600 to $900 per session.


In[NYC](https://certainlyhealth.com/blog/botox-cost) , the average cost of Botox units is a little higher, typically falling in the $9 to $24 per unit range depending on the borough. This means that masseter Botox treatments can range from $360 to $1,440, depending on the provider and units needed. It may be even more if you have particularly strong masseter muscles.


Although Botox is an FDA-approved cosmetic treatment, it isn’t FDA-approved to treat conditions like TMJ disorder. So, your insurance likely won’t cover treatment.
