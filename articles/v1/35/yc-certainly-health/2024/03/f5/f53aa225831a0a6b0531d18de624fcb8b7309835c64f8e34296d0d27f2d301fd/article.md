# Does masseter Botox help with TMJ disorder?

Botox is popular thanks to its cosmetic benefits, but did you know that it is sometimes administered for medical conditions like temporomandibular joint disorder (TMD)? Masseter Botox, which is Botox injected into the jaw’s masseter muscles, can alleviate some of the symptoms of TMJ disorder. But is there any medical basis to this, and does masseter Botox actually help? Let’s take a closer look at the potential benefits of masseter Botox for TMJ issues.

## How it works


Botox injections use a tiny amount of a toxin to freeze muscles in the face. This freezing effect helps to relax the muscles while also reducing their activity. This helps to lessen the appearance of wrinkles, fine lines and other signs of aging.


Masseter Botox specifically targets the masseter muscles of the jaw. Masseter muscles are primarily responsible for helping with common jaw movements, like talking and chewing. By relaxing these muscles with Botox and releasing the tension, those suffering from TMJ disorder may find some relief from common symptoms like pain, teeth grinding and headaches.


But is there empirical evidence that masseter Botox actually works for TMD? Preliminary studies of masseter Botox for TMJ dysfunction have found that it is effective for relieving TMJ disorder pain and clicking noises1. However, more comprehensive clinical studies are necessary to fully understand the efficacy of masseter Botox for TMJ problems.


## Benefits and risks


The primary benefit of masseter Botox is relief from TMD symptoms. Let’s take a quick look at all the benefits2 that masseter Botox offers:


- **Pain relief** - Masseter Botox can help relax the jaw muscles, relieving tension and reducing jaw pain and discomfort.
- **Headache relief** - TMD tension can lead to tension headaches and migraines. Relaxing the masseter muscles may lead to less frequent or less severe headaches.
- **Ease of movement** - Relaxing the masseter muscles may lead to easier jaw movement, making common movements like talking and chewing more comfortable.
- **Nonsurgical** - Masseter Botox is minimally invasive and nonsurgical. It can be a worthwhile treatment to try before resorting to more extreme treatment measures like surgical interventions.
- **Cosmetic benefits** - Along with reducing TMJ disorder symptoms, masseter Botox offers cosmetic benefits as well. It can help slim the jawline and give it a more V-shaped contour.
- **Long-lasting results** - Daily medications can offer temporary relief of TMJ dysfunction symptoms, but the effects of masseter Botox treatments can last for months. This can make symptom relief more manageable.
- **Convenience** - Masseter Botox is also convenient. Sessions can take less than 30 minutes and patients can resume normal activities almost immediately.

## TMJ vs. TMD


TMJ is an abbreviation for the temporomandibular joint. We have two temporomandibular joints that connect our jawbone to the skull. TMD is short for temporomandibular joint disorder. It refers to a variety of medical conditions that impact the function of your temporomandibular joints. This dysfunction can also affect the muscles around the joint, leading to pain and other uncomfortable symptoms.


There typically isn’t a singular cause for TMJ disorder, and it is often a combination of factors like previous jaw injury, arthritis, teeth grinding, stress and general wear on the jaw.


## What are the symptoms?


The various symptoms of TMJ disorder can cause discomfort and negatively affect quality of life. The severity of symptoms can vary for individuals, but pain in the jaw, especially when chewing or speaking, is one of the most common signifiers.


Those with TMJ disorders typically experience a combination of the following symptoms:


- Pain or tenderness in the jaw
- Teeth grinding/bruxism
- Difficulty chewing
- Locking of your joint
- Facial pain
- Jaw stiffness
- Popping or clicking of the jaw
- Headaches
- Migraines
- Tooth pain

## How much does it cost?


The cost of masseter Botox injection will vary based on factors like location, the experience of your provider and the number of Botox units needed for treatment.


On average,[Botox units cost](https://certainlyhealth.com/blog/botox-cost) about $9 to $24 per unit in NYC on average, but it can vary by region. The masseter area is a fairly large area to treat, especially if you have large masseter muscles. While some areas of the face can cost as little as $200 to $300 for a treatment, the cost of masseter Botox will typically be more expensive.


As mentioned, the masseter areas are fairly large. Typically, you can expect for a masseter Botox treatment to include 20-30 units per side of the jaw, so around 40-60 units total. The range of units will depend on how strong the masseter muscles are in the individual. Some individuals with particularly strong masseter muscles may even need up to 50 units per side of the jaw. Going off our previously discussed price per unit in NYC, a masseter Botox treatment may cost anywhere from $360 to $960 for a 40 unit treatment.


It’s also worth noting that while Botox itself is FDA-approved for cosmetic treatment, it isn’t FDA-approved as a treatment for TMJ disorders. This means that insurance likely won’t cover treatment and that your costs will be completely out of pocket. Also, Botox treatments are temporary, so maintaining results may require treatment every three to six months.
