# Hansgrohe Presents Hire Heroes USA Military Spouse Award

2025 marks Masco’s eighth year of partnership with nonprofit Hire Heroes USA, which helps connect U.S. military members, veterans and their spouses to successful, rewarding career paths.


In May, Masco had the honor of sponsoring the Military Spouse Award at the 3rd Annual Hire Heroes USA Empowerment Gala in Atlanta. This award recognizes a military spouse who has served as a silent hero for the military community with their selfless sacrifices and unwavering commitment. Congratulations to this year’s recipient, Verda Smith!


Team members from our Georgia-based Hansgrohe Group had a great time celebrating at the event and presenting the award. We’re grateful to Hire Heroes USA for bringing together supporters, partners, and changemakers for an unforgettable evening celebrating service, community, and impact.
