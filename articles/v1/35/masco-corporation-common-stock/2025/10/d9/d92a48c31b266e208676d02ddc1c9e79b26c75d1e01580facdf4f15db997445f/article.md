# Endless Pools Makes a Difference during “SWIMTember”

When Masco President and CEO, Jon Nudi, declared September 2025 a Month of Service, the small but mighty team at Endless Pools—a division of Watkins Wellness based in Aston, PA—dreamed up the concept of “SWIMTember,” a month dedicated to leveraging their passion around water safety to make a positive impact in the community.


SWIMTember challenged employees to swim in an Endless Pool in order to earn donations for the local YMCA. With teams competing under the leadership of six captains, the grand total came to 235 swims, which raised enough funds to provide free swim lessons for more than 150 community members! In addition, Team EP came together to support the Y in other ways, volunteering at a fundraiser, teaching free swim lessons, and creating swim kits for underserved youth who will receive free swim lessons.


Whether employees gave their time in the pool or on land, they made a big difference in their community!
