# Liv Labs Chosen for Springboard Enterprises 2023 Digital Health Innovation Cohort

Liv Labs Press Release


Sep 15, 2023


Selective accelerator helps women-led startups drive meaningful industry change


Liv Labs Inc., a consumer healthtech company making solutions for urinary incontinence, is pleased to have been selected for a highly-coveted slot in the Springboard Enterprises 2023 Digital Health Innovation Cohort. Liv Labs joins a veritable “who’s who” of women’s health companies building exciting businesses. The Digital Health Innovation program brings together specialized advisors, investors and industry leaders to set companies up for the next stage of growth.


“Springboard Enterprises stands out among accelerators for their sustained, expert support of women-led women’s health companies like ours. If we are to be judged by the company we keep, this is the network we want to be in,” said Liv Labs cofounder and CEO, Melody Roberts. “Best of all, the caliber of Springboard advising is extremely high. So it’s time well spent.” During the cohort, Liv Labs aims to refine digital solution go-to-market and partnership plans, while preparing for the next round of financing.


Springboard Enterprises is a leading network of industry experts, investors, and innovators dedicated to building companies at scale led by women who are transforming industries in technology, life sciences and healthcare. Since 2000, over 890+ Springboard portfolio companies seeking investment and human capital for product development and expansion have created over $39.1B in value. With 225 exits to strategic acquirers, 27 IPOs, and 10 Unicorns, Springboard Entrepreneurs are transforming industries.
