# Pippa by Liv Labs Recognized in Fast Company's 2025 Innovation by Design Awards

Liv Labs Inc.


Sep 25, 2025


Revolutionary pelvic floor fitness product––designed for effortless, everyday use––is recognized by Fast Company alongside design innovation from some of America's best-known companies, including Adidas, Nike, The North Face, Target, Google, and L'Oréal,


Photo credit: Sandra Riaño/Fast Company


Liv Labs, Inc., a consumer health and wellness startup, today announced that it has been chosen as a Fast Company 2025 Innovation by Design Awards honoree in the Product Design category for Pippa, its innovative new pelvic floor fitness product for women.


The Pippa Resistance Spring is an elegantly simple, wearable product designed to boost pelvic floor fitness during exercise and sports. It is worn internally (like a tampon), where it gently conforms to the body and activates pelvic floor muscles with each step, stretch, or squat. It works a bit like a resistance band, but for the inner core. No batteries, no routines—just a natural, effortless, on-the-go boost.


Carly Price, an accomplished designer and avid runner, invented the Pippa Resistance Spring to integrate into the active lives of modern American women, be they athletes, moms, or busy retirees. She and the Liv Labs research and development team designed, engineered and tested the Pippa Resistance Spring to optimize it for ease-of-use, comfort and convenience. To learn more, or to pre-order, visit


[https://www.livlabsfitness.com](https://www.livlabsfitness.com/) .


The annual Innovation by Design Awards is one of the most prestigious design competitions in the industry, spotlighting work that exemplifies originality, beauty, sustainability, user insight, cultural significance, and measurable business impact.


> "We're honored to be recognized by Fast Company for the Pippa Resistance Spring, listed among the most innovative products this year," said Melody Roberts, CEO of Liv Labs. "We aim to put people in expert control of their own physical wellbeing with beautiful, practical products and services. This award spotlights the importance of good design to achieving that aim."


This year's honorees were selected by a panel of leading designers, business executives from leading organizations, and Fast Company editors.


"The Innovation by Design Awards celebrate bold ideas and work that proves design is a true driver of business and societal progress. This year's honorees show how creative thinking can unlock new markets, build stronger organizations, and deliver meaningful impact at scale," said Brendan Vaughan, editor-in-chief of Fast Company.
