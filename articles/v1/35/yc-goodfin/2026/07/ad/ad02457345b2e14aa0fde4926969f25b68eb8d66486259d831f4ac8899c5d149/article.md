# The Goodfin Guild Grows: Welcoming Our Newest Guild Partner

When we launched The Goodfin Guild, we set out to do something different. We wanted to bring together the connectors, the builders, the people who don't just invest in the future but actively shape it. After recently welcoming Charles Belle, we've seen firsthand what happens when the right people come together.


Today, we're thrilled to introduce our newest Guild Partner, someone who embodies what The Guild stands for: strategic vision, passion for innovation, and community-driven values.


## Introducing Our Newest Guild Partner: Subha Rajana


Subha Rajana is a Silicon Valley deep-tech investor and serial entrepreneur with three decades of experience building and scaling technology ventures. She's a Venture Partner at 3i Partners investing in early-stage Indian startups, and an angel investor backing frontier technologies including fission energy. She's also an LP in MFV Partners and How Women Invest, working at the intersection of innovation, entrepreneurship, and women's financial empowerment.


What sets Subha Rajana apart is her focus on education and building financial confidence. Throughout her journey—from founding technology companies to investing in frontier sectors like deep tech, climate innovation, and digital health—she’s learned that interest in investing is not the barrier; clarity is. When people understand the *why* and *how* behind private-market investing, confidence naturally follows.


“Private markets are no longer reserved for insiders. With faster innovation cycles and emerging sectors reshaping how we live and work, more people want to invest in the future they believe in. Yet the biggest barrier remains knowledge.”


Subha believes the greatest opportunity in private-market investing lies in widening access and building confidence among those traditionally excluded from these conversations—from women and first-time investors to values-driven professionals who are curious but unsure where to start.


The opportunity isn’t just financial—it’s about empowerment, access, and creating a more inclusive investing culture where everyone feels they belong.


## Subha Rajana's Vision for The Guild


Subha Rajana joined The Guild because she believes in expanding access to investment education and helping to build a more inclusive, financially confident generation of investors. She hopes to share insights from her investing experience—especially in innovation-driven sectors—so individuals across communities can better understand emerging opportunities and make values-aligned financial decisions.


This vision aligns perfectly with The Guild's core purpose. We're not building a network for the sake of networking. We're creating a concentrated community of people who understand that the best opportunities come through relationships built on trust, expertise, and shared values.

## Why The Guild Continues to Matter


The private markets are evolving faster than ever. Technology is reshaping how capital moves and how wealth is created. But technology alone won't increase access to premium opportunities. People, trusted relationships, and communities built on shared values and mutual success will.


The Guild creates the infrastructure for this new model. It's not a platform—it's a movement. Guild Partners don't just attend events; they create them. They don't just access opportunities; they source them and share them. They don't just benefit from the network; they actively build it.
