# Introducing Goodfin GO

Every serious investor knows the math.


The biggest returns happen before the IPO. But private markets have never been designed for individuals. They've been designed for institutions—firms with dedicated analysts, proprietary data pipelines, and the operational infrastructure to move when windows open.


For everyone else, it's fragmented information, stale data, and deal flow that arrives too late or not at all.


We initially built Goodfin to fix access. Today, we're fixing everything else.


##### **Goodfin GO is live.**


We call it G. And it's not an add-on or an upgrade. It's the foundation we built Goodfin around—an agentic AI designed specifically for pre-IPO investing.


This isn't a vibe-coded proof of concept. Thousands of investors have already engaged with our AI. Millions of dollars have been invested through it. We've watched how real allocators use intelligence tools in their workflows—and built G to meet them where they are.


G operates the way a senior investment team operates. When you ask a question, you're not querying a database. You're activating a coordinated system of 30+ specialist agents—research, analysis, compliance, execution—working in parallel to surface what matters.


It meets the standards of the CFA Level III exam. It's been vetted by human advisors. And it's built on the research we conducted with NYU Stern on AI competence in financial services.


This is what expert-level actually looks like.


#### **How G works with you:**


G learns how you invest. Your risk profile. Your thesis. Your preferences. Every recommendation reflects your approach—not a generic watchlist pulled from yesterday's headlines.


When you research a company, G doesn't hand you a summary. It maps competitive landscapes, synthesizes data from vetted sources and Goodfin's proprietary intelligence, models outcomes, and scores risk. The kind of analysis that used to take weeks now takes minutes.


When you want to see how other investors are thinking, G pulls from our community Investor Ticker—conviction scores, investment likelihood, real-time sentiment from Goodfin's member community. Great investors don't think in isolation. Neither does G.


When you're ready to move, G handles execution. KYC. Signatures. The entire investment process—end to end. No document chasing. No back-and-forth.


And after you invest, G keeps watching. News alerts. Fundraising signals. Hiring trends. Secondary activity. IPO indicators. You stay ahead of the curve without drowning in noise.


#### **Not a wrapper. A new architecture.**


G isn't a chatbot layered on top of existing infrastructure. It's a multi-agent system coordinated by a directed graph architecture—purpose-built for the complexity of private markets where information is scarce, documents are sensitive, and deal structures vary.


And an important last note: Your data stays encrypted. Guardrailed. Never used to train external models.


This is what we've been building toward. A platform where individual investors get the same intelligence and execution that institutions have always had.


The gap is closing.
