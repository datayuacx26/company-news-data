## **Before You Delete: Understand What “Catalog” Means in WhatsApp Business**


‍


A WhatsApp Business Catalog is the product or service list shown on your business profile. The[WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/405903568419894) says each catalog item needs a title, at least one image, and country of origin. Optional fields include price, description, website link, and item code.


For Shopify merchants, the word “catalog” can mean three different things:


WhatsApp Catalog Changes What you are changing What it means Best next step


One catalog item A single product or service in WhatsApp Delete or edit the item in the app.


Connected Meta catalog A Meta product list linked to WhatsApp Business Disconnect it from the account.


Shopify-fed product list Products selected from Shopify collections Update the Shopify product or collection.


This distinction prevents the wrong fix. If one product is discontinued, delete that item. If the wrong Meta list is linked, disconnect it. If your Shopify collection contains products you do not want to show, fix the collection first.

## **How to Delete Catalog in WhatsApp Business App**


‍


Use this path when you only want to remove one product or service from the WhatsApp Business app.


To remove one item from the Tools tab,[WhatsApp’s catalog maintenance guide](https://faq.whatsapp.com/833697274483076) gives this process:


1. Open WhatsApp Business.
2. Tap **Tools** .
3. Tap **Catalog** .
4. Long press the image of the product or service.
5. Tap the delete icon.
6. Tap **Delete** .


This removes the selected item from the WhatsApp product list. It does not remove the same product from Shopify. It also does not edit old customer chats where the item may have been shared.


If you need to delete a collection, that is a separate action. A collection is a group of products inside the WhatsApp product list. You can delete a collection, and collection changes can take no more than[24 hours](https://faq.whatsapp.com/2929318000711140) .


Do not use collection deletion as a shortcut for stock changes. If a few items are unavailable, edit those items or change the source group. If the whole group is old, remove the collection.

## **How to Disconnect a Catalog From Your WhatsApp Business Account**


Disconnect a catalog when the linked Meta product list is wrong. This is different from deleting one product inside WhatsApp Business.


The official Meta page explains how to[disconnect a catalog from a WhatsApp Business account](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/3006875002918752) . WhatsApp also gives an app path for connected catalogs. You can tap **Tools > Catalog > More Options > Catalog settings** to disconnect a connected Meta catalog.


Use this option when:


- You connected the wrong Meta catalog.
- You changed the way products are sent to WhatsApp.
- You want a different product source attached to your WhatsApp account.
- Your team rebuilt the product feed in Meta Business tools.


Disconnecting only removes the link between the connected Meta catalog and your WhatsApp Business account. It does not delete products from Shopify or rewrite old chat messages.


Before you disconnect, tell the people who handle campaigns and customer replies. They may need to pause messages that point to the old product list.

## **Common WhatsApp Catalog Problems After Deleting or Removing Products**


‍


If a product or collection does not disappear immediately after removal, the issue is often related to where the catalog data is coming from or how updates are syncing. Before repeating the same steps, use the checks below to identify the actual cause.


- Check whether the item is coming from a connected Meta catalog or a Shopify-fed setup. Repeating the delete step inside the app may not remove a product that is being pulled from another source.
- If your app is not showing catalog changes, WhatsApp recommends basic troubleshooting steps:


- Update to the latest version of the app.
- Close and reopen WhatsApp.
- Check that your internet connection is stable.
- Review WhatsApp's guidance on its[catalog issues page](https://faq.whatsapp.com/780175293074108) .


- Rejected items require a different approach:


- WhatsApp marks rejected catalog items with a red exclamation icon.
- Tap **Learn more** to understand why the item was rejected.
- From the item page, you can request another review after addressing the issue.


- If you are unable to delete a collection:


- Try removing all products from the collection first.
- Once the collection is empty, attempt to delete it again.


- If your WhatsApp products come from Shopify through Zoko:


- Check the sync window before repeating the same deletion steps.
- The product may still be waiting for the next catalog update.

## **How Shopify Merchants Should Manage WhatsApp Catalog Cleanup**


For a Shopify store, product cleanup should start in Shopify. That is where you manage the product page, stock, image, price, and product groups.


Shopify calls these groups collections. The[Shopify Help Center](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/collections) says collections help group products by category, item type, sale status, size, colour, or season. If your WhatsApp list is based on Shopify collections, this is where you should fix the source.


Use this cleanup flow before removing a product from WhatsApp:


1. Open the product in Shopify.
2. Check stock, price, images, and product status.
3. Check the Shopify collection used for WhatsApp selling.
4. Review broadcasts, saved replies, and product links.
5. Update the product or collection.
6. Check WhatsApp after the expected sync window.


For example, you sell apparel in India and a kurta variant sells out after a weekend offer. First, update the variant in Shopify. Then check whether it belongs to the collection shown in WhatsApp. Next, pause any broadcast that still points to that variant. After that, direct shoppers to another available style.


This keeps your store, chat team, campaigns, and order conversations working from the same product truth. Customers see fewer unavailable items, and your team spends less time correcting avoidable questions.

### **Q. How Do I Delete Catalog in WhatsApp Business?**


Open WhatsApp Business and go to Tools > Catalog. Long press the product or service you want to remove, tap the delete icon, then tap Delete to confirm. This removes the selected item from your WhatsApp Business Catalog, but it does not delete the product from Shopify or old chats where it was already shared.


‍


### **Q. Can I Delete My Entire WhatsApp Business Catalog?**


If your account uses a connected Meta catalog, you may need to disconnect it rather than delete products one by one. WhatsApp lists this path under Tools > Catalog > More Options > Catalog settings.


‍


### **Q. Why Is My WhatsApp Catalog Product Still Showing After Deletion?**


The item may be coming from a connected Meta catalog or Shopify-fed setup. Check the product source, app refresh, and sync timing before repeating the deletion step.


‍


### **Q. Should Shopify Merchants Delete WhatsApp Catalog Products Manually?**


Manual deletion is fine for one old item. For regular product changes, update Shopify products and collections first, then check the WhatsApp product list.
