## **WhatsApp Business Catalog Setup Guide: Step-by-Step**


‍


Once your product details are ready, you can start setting up the catalog. This section keeps the process simple, so Shopify merchants can move from setup to customer-facing product discovery.


‍


### **Step 1: Set Up or Review Your WhatsApp Business Account**


Start with a complete WhatsApp Business profile. Add your business name, category, description, website, email, store hours, and contact details.


This helps customers trust the profile before they browse products or start a buying conversation. Shopify merchants should also make sure the profile matches their store branding.


‍


### **Step 2: Add Products to the Catalog**


Add each product with a clear name, product image, price, description, and link where available. Keep the details consistent with your Shopify product page.


For example, a fashion product should include size, color, fabric, price, and care details. A beauty product should include quantity, usage, and skin type where relevant.


‍


### **Step 3: Review Product Details Before Publishing**


Before publishing, check every product for missing or outdated information. Review names, images, descriptions, prices, variants, availability, and product links.


Use this quick check:


- Is the product name clear?
- Is the price correct?
- Are images accurate?
- Are variants explained?
- Is the product still available?


‍


### **Step 4: Share Catalog Items in Customer Chats**


After publishing, use catalog items inside real WhatsApp conversations. Share them when customers ask about sizes, prices, availability, product comparisons, or recommendations.


Catalog items can also support cart recovery, campaign replies, support chats, and repeat purchase conversations. This keeps product discovery close to the sales conversation.

## **Common WhatsApp Catalog Setup Mistakes to Avoid**


‍


A WhatsApp Catalog should make product discovery easier, not create more confusion. Shopify merchants should avoid these mistakes before sending catalog items into customer chats.


‍


### **Uploading Products With Missing Details**


Incomplete listings create more questions instead of fewer. Missing prices, weak descriptions, unclear images, and absent product links can slow the buying conversation.


Before publishing, check that every product includes the details customers usually ask for. This may include size, material, quantity, delivery notes, and availability.


‍


### **Ignoring Product Variants**


Shopify stores often sell products with sizes, colors, bundles, flavors, or materials. If these variants are unclear, customers may still need manual help before buying.


Fashion, beauty, food, and accessories brands should pay extra attention here. Clear variant details make WhatsApp browsing more useful.

### **Letting Product Information Become Outdated**


Old prices, discontinued products, and unavailable variants can weaken customer trust. They can also increase support workload when shoppers ask about items they cannot buy.


Regular updates help keep WhatsApp chats accurate and sales-ready.
