### How to Create a Catalog on WhatsApp


Creating a catalog is easy. Just follow these steps:


- Open the WhatsApp Business App.
- Go to the Settings menu.
- Click on Business Tools, then select Catalog.
- Press Add Product or Service to upload images and details.


Your product photos should be clear and bright. Add descriptions that tell the whole story — what makes your products different, their prices, and any deals you're running. A little extra detail here goes a long way in helping customers make decisions.
