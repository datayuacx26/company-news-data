## Eligibility Criteria for Obtaining the WhatsApp Blue Tick


Getting that blue tick might seem tricky. But with the right preparation, you'll be well on your way. Here's what you need:


**The Basics for Your Business**


- Your business needs to be legally registered — that's a given
- Your WhatsApp profile should be complete with your business name, what you do, website, and how to reach you
- Keep your account active by regularly talking with customers


**Extra Steps for WhatsApp API Users**


- Make sure you're verified through Facebook Business Manager
- Follow WhatsApp's messaging rules (no spam — your customers will thank you)
- Keep your brand name the same across all platforms


**Getting Ready to Apply**


- Have your business papers ready
- Double-check your profile info
- Make sure you're at least 18 years old
- Use a clear profile picture that shows your brand


**Subscription Options**


- Monthly plans are available if you want flexibility
- Annual subscriptions might save you money in the long run


**Watch Out For These Common Problems**


- Missing profile information can get you rejected
- Your business needs to look professional and established
- If you get turned down, read WhatsApp's feedback carefully


**Pro Tips for Success**


- Take time with your application — rushing leads to mistakes
- Keep your business documentation up to date
- If something goes wrong, you can always try again


And remember, you can make changes to your profile anytime before you apply. It's better to spend a little more time getting everything right than to rush and face rejection.
