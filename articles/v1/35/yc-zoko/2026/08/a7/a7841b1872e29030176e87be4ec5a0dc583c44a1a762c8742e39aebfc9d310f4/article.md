# MSG91 WhatsApp API Pricing in India: How the Cost Structure Actually Works

Every business comparing MSG91 WhatsApp API pricing eventually runs into the same question: if every provider connects to the same WhatsApp Business Platform, why do the prices look so different?


Part of the confusion stems from how WhatsApp pricing actually works. Since July 2025,[Meta](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/platform-pricing) has shifted from conversation-based billing to per-message pricing, with charges varying by message category and recipient country. On top of Meta's rates, many Business Solution Providers (BSPs) apply their own platform fees, subscriptions, automation charges, or feature-based pricing—making it difficult to compare providers on price alone.


MSG91 is one of the more visible names in this comparison, largely because it promotes a "no markup" approach to Meta's messaging rates. But choosing a WhatsApp API provider isn't just about the cost of sending a message. It's equally about the platform you're getting around the API—automation, Shopify integration, shared inboxes, campaign management, compliance support, and the operational effort required to run WhatsApp at scale.


This guide breaks down how MSG91 WhatsApp API pricing works, what's charged by Meta versus MSG91, where additional costs may arise, and what Shopify merchants should evaluate before choosing a WhatsApp Business API provider.

- MSG91's WhatsApp pricing in India follows a two-part structure: a monthly per-number subscription fee, plus Meta's own per-conversation charges for marketing, utility, and authentication messages.

## **How WhatsApp Business API Pricing Works, Structurally**


Every WhatsApp Business API provider in India is reselling access to the same underlying WhatsApp Business Platform that Meta operates. This means the actual per-message cost — what Meta calls conversation-based pricing — is set by Meta, not by the BSP.


Meta groups conversations into categories (marketing, utility, authentication, and service), each priced differently, with marketing conversations typically carrying the highest per-conversation rate since they cover promotional and re-engagement messages.


What a BSP like MSG91 actually controls is everything layered on top of that base rate: whether they add a markup to Meta's per-conversation price, what they charge as a monthly platform or number subscription fee, and what tooling (automation, inbox, catalogue, reporting) that subscription includes.

### **Why Two "Meta Rate" Quotes Can Still Differ**


This is the part worth understanding before comparing quotes: two providers can both say "we charge Meta's rate" and still end up costing very differently, because one might charge a higher monthly subscription to make up the difference, or restrict certain features to higher tiers. The headline per-conversation number is rarely the full picture of what you'll actually pay each month.

## **What to Actually Compare Beyond the Per-Message Rate**


Price-per-conversation is the headline number, but for most Shopify and ecommerce businesses evaluating WhatsApp API providers in India, it's not the number that ends up determining total cost or, more importantly, return on the channel.


Whether the platform is built for commerce or just messaging matters more than it looks: a low per-conversation rate on a tool that only sends broadcasts and can't sync your product catalogue, track abandoned carts, or confirm COD orders means you're paying less per message but doing more manual work around each one.


What's included in the subscription versus billed separately matters too — some platforms bundle automation (visual flow builders), shared team inboxes, and broadcast segmentation into a base subscription, while others charge extra for each, and a provider with a slightly higher monthly fee but a genuinely useful automation layer can end up cheaper in total than a barebones platform charging for every add-on.


Support for compliance and verification matters more once you're running WhatsApp at real volume, where a rejected template or a compliance misstep costs more in lost time than any difference in per-message pricing.


And whether pricing scales predictably matters for businesses with seasonal volume — a wallet-based, pay-as-you-go model works differently from a tiered subscription with message caps, and Indian D2C brands in particular should model both against actual expected conversation volume around festive sales rather than comparing sticker prices.

## **FAQs**


‍


### **1. Is MSG91's WhatsApp API pricing the cheapest option in India?**


MSG91 markets itself around a no-markup pass-through of Meta's rates, which can make its per-conversation pricing competitive. However, "cheapest" depends on your message mix (marketing vs. utility vs. authentication), your volume, and what's included in the subscription — so it's worth comparing total cost against your actual expected usage rather than a single quoted rate.


‍


### **2. Does MSG91 charge extra on top of Meta's conversation rates?**


MSG91 states it does not add a markup to Meta's official conversation rates for marketing, utility, and authentication messages, charging a separate monthly subscription fee instead. Confirm this directly with MSG91's current pricing page, since terms and promotional waivers can change.


‍


### **3. What is conversation-based pricing on WhatsApp?**


It's Meta's billing model, where each 24-hour conversation window with a customer is billed once, categorised by type (marketing, utility, authentication, or service), rather than charging per individual message sent within that window.


‍


### **4. Do I need a separate WhatsApp Business API subscription if I already use MSG91 for SMS or OTP?**


Typically, yes — WhatsApp API access, SMS, and OTP are usually billed and subscribed to separately even within the same provider's account, though credit wallets are often shared across products.


‍


### **5. Is GST applicable on WhatsApp API charges in India?**


Yes, GST applies on top of message and subscription charges billed by any India-based BSP, MSG91 included.


‍


### **6. Can I switch WhatsApp API providers without losing my number or chat history?**


Number migration between BSPs is generally possible since the number is verified with Meta rather than owned by the provider, but chat history and template approvals typically don't transfer automatically — this is worth confirming with both the outgoing and incoming providers before switching.


‍


### **7. Does a lower per-conversation rate mean lower total WhatsApp marketing cost?**


Not necessarily. Total cost also depends on the subscription fee, what automation and integration features are included versus billed separately, and how efficiently the platform helps you target opted-in customers instead of over-messaging, which increases both cost and spam-report risk.


‍


### **8.How is WhatsApp API pricing different from WhatsApp Business App, which is free?**


The Business App is free but limited to a single device/user and lacks the automation, catalogue integration at scale, and multi-agent support that API-based platforms like MSG91 or Zoko provide — the API pricing reflects that added infrastructure and business-scale support.


‍


### **9. Does pricing differ for Shopify-specific features like catalogue sync or abandoned cart flows?**


This depends entirely on the provider. Some BSPs charge for these as premium add-ons; Shopify-native platforms often build them into the core offering, since that's the primary use case they're designed for. Always confirm what's bundled versus billed separately for Shopify-specific automation.


‍


### **10. Where can I find MSG91's current, exact pricing figures?**


Directly on MSG91's official pricing page, since Meta's conversation rates and MSG91's own subscription terms are revised periodically, any number published in an article — including this one — should be treated as a point-in-time reference, not a guarantee.
