# DisplayLink DL-4xxx Series


## Product Family Overview


##


The DisplayLink DL-4xxx family, trusted by Lenovo, HP, ASUS, Logitech, Wacom, Yealink, Poly, Elgato, AOC, Mimo, and many others, has been utilized in products worldwide across enterprise, conferencing, industrial, embedded, and retail segments. Designed specifically for integrated display applications, the DL-41xx family delivers a versatile solution for a wide range of panel-based products.


Supporting resolutions up to 1920 × 1200, with integrated LVDS and support for eDP, the DL-41xx family requires no external memory, helping reduce bill-of-materials costs. It is ideal for customer-facing point-of-sale displays, portable monitors, meeting room controllers, signature pads, embedded panels, and external robotic displays. HDCP, DDC/CI, and USB 3.0 support are included as standard.
