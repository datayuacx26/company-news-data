# DisplayLink DL-3xxx Series


## Product Family Overview


##


Nominated as a CES Best of Innovation Honoree, the DisplayLink DL-3xxx family, trusted by the likes of Dell, Lenovo, HP, ASUS, Google, Targus, Yamaha, Zebra, Honeywell, Plugable and many others, has been utilized in hundreds of products worldwide across enterprise, conferencing, industrial, embedded and retail segments.


Enabling either a single 2K60 display or dual 1080p displays, the DL-3xxx family is ideal for entry-level designs enabling integrated HDMI, DVI, VGA & support for DP 1.1 plus Gigabit Ethernet and 5.1-channel Audio all over USB 3.0. Additional support for WOL, MAPT, and PXE boot satisfies enterprise requirements.


For commercial applications, the DL-1900E variant features Ethernet ingress to support your signage, meeting room and network video needs. Integrated Digital RGB and support for eDP make it suitable for a broad range of embedded and portable panel use cases.
