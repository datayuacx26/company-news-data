# Cyber Resilience Act


## What is the Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)?


The Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) is a European Union regulation that establishes mandatory cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements, including hardware, software, and connected devices. The CRA aims to ensure products are secure by design, supported throughout their lifecycle, and better protected against evolving cyber threats.


## Why CRA Matters


The growing number of connected products has increased the need for stronger cybersecurity protections. The Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) helps address this challenge by establishing common cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements placed on the EU market. The regulation improves transparency, strengthens product security throughout the lifecycle, and helps organizations and consumers make more informed technology decisions.

#### CRA Scope – What Is Covered?


## CRA Scope – What Is Covered?


The Cyber Resilience Act applies to a broad range of products with digital elements, including hardware, software, components, and certain remote services. While many connected products fall within the scope of the regulation, some categories—such as standalone cloud or SaaS services, non-commercial open-source software, products covered by equivalent sector regulations, and other exclusions defined by EU legislation—are not subject to CRA requirements.

#### CRA Applicability – New vs. Existing Products


## CRA Applicability – New vs. Existing Products


Which Products does the CRA apply to?


Starting **December 11, 2027** , new products with digital elements placed on the EU market must comply with the Cyber Resilience Act. Products already on the market before that date are generally exempt unless they undergo a substantial modification that impacts cybersecurity requirements or changes the product's intended purpose. When significant changes are made, a compliance reassessment may be required.
