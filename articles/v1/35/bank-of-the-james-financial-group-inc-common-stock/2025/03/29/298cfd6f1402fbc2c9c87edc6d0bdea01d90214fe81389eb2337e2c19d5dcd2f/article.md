# How To Use Mobile Banking To Help Pay Your Bills On Time

Online banking and mobile banking may sound similar, and while they offer similar features, they’re not the same thing. Online banking, sometimes called Internet or web banking, is used through a web browser, typically on a computer. This type of banking lets you view account balances, transfer money, open new accounts, and more. It does not require additional software, such as an app download, to utilize. Typically, online banking provides more detailed information about your banking accounts than mobile banking.


Mobile banking is accessed through a smartphone or tablet app. This type of banking provides unmatched convenience, allowing users to perform simple banking tasks anytime, as long as they have Wi-Fi or a mobile network connection. Mobile banking can also provide users with location-specific features that online banking can’t, such as finding nearby ATMs.
