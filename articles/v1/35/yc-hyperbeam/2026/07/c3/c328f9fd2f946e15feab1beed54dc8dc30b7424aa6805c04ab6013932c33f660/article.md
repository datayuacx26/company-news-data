# 5 Long-Distance Date Ideas: Movies, Activities, and More

Long-distance dating can be tough on couples. Luckily, we are in the age of technology where we can find easy ways to connect with our loved ones. Through digital means, dating doesn’t follow the same rules as it has in the past. To help you and your significant other out, we've created a list of the top five long-distance date ideas that can keep your relationship fresh and exciting.


## 1. Virtual Dinner Date


Light your candles, cook up your favorite meal, and make a video call to your favorite person. With a virtual dinner date, you can make it as romantic or as casual as you want it to be. Distance can't take the intimacy away from a good old fashioned dinner date. It's always fun to watch an online movie through Hyperbeam after a tasty dinner to make the night complete.


## 2. Take a Masterclass Together


Find a creative way to grow with your partner by taking a digital class for your date night. Online education is becoming increasingly popular and many couples benefit from taking advantage of Masterclass. There are a variety of classes you can take to change up your routine. Try one of these creative classes with your significant other. Some of the topics include:


- Painting
- Cooking
- Songwriting
- Acting


And the list goes on.


## 3. Do the “Favorites” Challenge


Do you know what items will bring a smile to your significant other’s face? If the answer is yes, then the “Favorites” Challenge will be easy! Simply pop into a nearby store and buy your partner’s favorite non-perishables, pack them up, and take them to the post office. This can include games, snacks, trinkets, or accessories. When they get their gift, have a virtual date night to see their face as they open it.


## 4. Zoom Escape Room Challenges


This is one of the most unique long-distance relationship date ideas. If you’ve ever been to an escape room, you are familiar with the concept: solve riddles to find your way out of the room. Now, with modern technology, this game can be done virtually anywhere as long as you have an internet connection. It can be done with friends, too, so consider it for a group date activity.


## 5. Movie “Watch Togethers”


The classic movie night is a go-to for many couples, and it should be no different in long-distance relationships. After a long, stressful day, this relaxing date idea is sure to be a favorite. Simply log in to your Hyperbeam account to connect with your favorite person over a new release, a timeless film, or a series you’ve both been waiting to start.


## Get Quality Time Online


The goal in a long-distance relationship is to find ways to have quality time with your significant other despite the miles that separate you. Digital dating is made easy through technology. Take advantage of our favorite long-distance date ideas to help your relationship grow.
