Meet Alan and Jeremy — two Ting Internet field sales reps who are out in their communities every day helping people get connected to better, faster internet. We asked them to share a bit about what their days look like, what they love about the job, and the moments that make it all worth it. If you’ve ever considered a career in field sales — especially one with flexibility, purpose, and great people — read on!


## What does a typical day look like for you as a Field Sales Representative with Ting Internet?


### **Jeremy:**


*“I start my day with something healthy so I can feel clean, working out and smoothies. I then check my schedule and make any calls I need to. Head to the territory I’ve picked to start with, prep myself and my satchel so I know I’m prepared for anything. And we’re off to the races. Dogs barking, birds chirping, Ting heads Tinging.*


*Hitting the first door is the hardest part of the whole day. If you get an interaction it makes the next door feel more comfortable. To get an order placed at the first interaction creates a feeling of conquering and achieving a goal.”*


### Alan:


*“I usually prepare the night before, I go over my territory and navigate my day. I wake up excited to get my day going. My goal is to get at least 2 to 5 sales or more per day. If it takes knocking on 60 doors or 117 doors a day I believe if you make a goal for yourself, you’ll be very successful.”*


## What do you enjoy most about working in field sales for Ting?


### Jeremy:


*“I am very grateful for the opportunity to bring Ting to people. I truly believe that I have something with more than enough value to knock on my neighbors’ doors and present them with it. The company’s values align a lot with my own. Simple, transparent, fair pricing and service that is reliable and affordable.”*


### Alan:


*“The energy that my managers, James Gallagher and Jerome Hamlette give me is very contagious. Ting has a very positive culture and opportunity for growth and recognition which I really like. I also believe it’s a great product that people actually believe in and when you sell a product that people believe in and you believe in, you’ll be very successful.”*


## How does Ting support you in your role — both in terms of training and day-to-day resources?


### Jeremy:


*“I feel the onboarding process is very important to our success. With some extensive training on my own through sales trainers like Andy Elliot and Jeremy Minor I’ve gained a lot of knowledge in which I see myself using almost everyday at work and outside of work.*

### Alan:


*“Of course our customers always come first, integrity is very big in my book, being prior law-enforcement it’s all about integrity. People buy from people they trust, and the ongoing mentorship and coaching from my manager is great. We do have weekly meetings with my manager. I actually speak to my manager several times a day and he’s always supporting me and helping.”*


## What skills or personality traits do you think help someone thrive in this role?


### Jeremy:


*“Emotional endurance and the ability to not carry the weight of the world with you to every door.*


*Having a great work ethic will enable reps to start the day on time and exceed expectations that are set for them, and not falter from rejections.*


*Being able to recognize the challenges that you face between potential customers. Some examples include being knowledgeable about installations, knowledge of specifications in the home and placement of modem/routers.”*


### Alan:


*“I believe being self motivated and driven is a very big factor. A passion for helping people, positive attitude and team spirit is going to help you go very far in sales. Remember you must be outgoing and approachable; people judge you in the first 7 seconds when they see you. Resilience and adaptability is a key for being a great sales person.”*


## Do you have a favorite customer interaction or success story that stands out?


### Jeremy:


*“Just today I spent some time with a family who was transitioning to Ting and needed assistance on device access to the network, also connecting mobile and YoutubeTV. Very cool to have the knowledge to help them through the transition. And through this switch they calculated savings of $3600 just in the first year. They were very excited about the transition!”*


### Alan:


*“Yes, the most memorable moment was when I knocked on a door and the customer said oh no, not Ting again you guys have been here 12 times, they walked to the yard and pointed at the landscaping and said you guys ruined my grass. I’ll never get Ting again. I looked at their face and said I am so sorry. I will do anything to make this experience better. They said, “fix my grass”; so I actually went to Home Depot, bought several items, including seeds and soil, went back and fixed their yard and then signed them up several days later. They were very appreciative and told their neighbours about me. I showed them that Ting is all about customer service.”*
