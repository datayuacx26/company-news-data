*By Raj Parmar, Senior Manager, People Strategy and Business Partnerships at Ting.*


Press enter or click to view image in full size


When I joined Ting Internet in February 2023 as the first dedicated HR Business Partner, I knew I was stepping into something special. What I didn’t know is how much this role — and this team — would change me for the better.


Fast forward to 2025, I’ve grown into the role of Senior Manager, People Strategy & Business Partnerships. That means I get to wear a lot of hats — leading people strategy, employee engagement, talent development, performance programs, DEI, career growth initiatives… and everything in between. But more than the titles, it’s been about ***impact*** — building a people function that’s approachable, innovative, and truly aligned to business success.


## **What I love most about Ting**


### **💙 The people**


Hands down, the best part. Kind, passionate, driven humans who show up with heart — every single day. Through the highs and the hard times, our people are what ground me.


### **🧠 Leadership that grows you**


Ting leadership is the kind that pushes you to think bigger and do better — especially my own leader. I’ve been incredibly lucky to work alongside Kerris Hougardy for over eight years. We’ve built so much together, and it’s been nothing short of fun, fulfilling, and truly career-defining. I think I can confidently say on behalf of both of us that we’re having the most fun here at Ting!


### **🚀 Stretching beyond the status quo**


I’ve never been one to do “traditional HR.” I like challenging old narratives — being part of an HR team people feel comfortable reaching out to. One that’s strategic, human-centered, and fun. At Ting, that mindset is not only welcomed — it’s expected.


### 🤖 **Bringing AI into HR (and making it actually useful)**


One of the most exciting parts of my journey at Ting has been experimenting with and integrating AI into the way we work — not as a buzzword, but as a genuine tool to simplify, educate, and empower. From designing custom GPTs to streamline HR communications/programs, to using Google Vids to build engaging learning content, Scribe to create step-by-step guides, and Notebook LM to generate quick, digestible summaries of training content — it’s been a game changer.

We’ve tapped into AI to analyze engagement survey data faster, build better employee resource materials, and even to help with recognition tools that make the employee experience smoother. Instead of drowning in admin, my team and I can focus on what matters most — people. AI has become part of how we work smarter, support teams better, and drive people’s strategy forward.


### **🛠️ Projects that move the needle**


From launching our wildly successful performance development cycle, to introducing impactful safety programs, designing career growth pathways, and even embedding AI into how we work — I’ve had the chance to help shape how Ting runs and grows.


### **🌍 Remote work for the win**


Pre-COVID me would never have believed it, but remote work is where I thrive. Since joining Ting, I’ve worked from Europe, the U.S., and my own cozy backyard in BC. I’m even heading to Spain and Italy next month to keep that work-travel vibe alive. The flexibility has been a game-changer for my productivity and mental health.


### **🌄 Real work-life balance**


At Ting, when we say we respect boundaries, we mean it. When I’m off, I’m *off* — whether I’m at F45 getting my sweat on, hiking in BC’s incredible mountains with my doggo Zeus, playing beach volleyball, or just eating my way through the city.


Joining Ting has been one of the most rewarding chapters of my career. The best part? It still feels like the beginning. I’m so excited for what’s ahead — for me, for the people team, and for all of us building something meaningful together. 🚀


## About Tucows


We do a lot, but at our core, we’re in the business of keeping people connected and keeping the Internet open. We’re made up of three companies: Tucows Domains, Ting, and Wavelo.


As[Tucows Domains](http://tucowsdomain.com/) , we help people find their place online as the world’s largest domain name wholesaler and the third-largest domain registrar globally.


As[Ting Internet](http://tinginternet.com/) , we deliver high-speed fiber internet service to communities across the United States.


As[Wavelo](http://wavelo.com/) , we believe the future of telecom is simple. We build telecom billing and operations software for Mobile Virtual Network Operators and Fiber Internet Services.
