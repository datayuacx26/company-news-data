*By Theresa Wellwood, People Enablement and Development Specialist*


My journey with Tucows began as a customer support agent at Hover, where I dove headfirst into the world of domains. While joining the Herd felt a bit daunting at first, the incredible training and unwavering support I received helped me quickly find my footing. From day one, I was struck by the collaborative, people-first atmosphere and the dedication of my team. My love for helping customers — and supporting my fellow agents — sparked a desire to keep learning and growing. It wasn’t long before I found myself assisting with the Hover knowledge base, creating and updating documentation to ensure it was as helpful and user-friendly as possible.

As I gained experience, I took on training new Hover employees and revamping our onboarding materials to ensure new team members felt welcome and prepared. Mentoring my peers became a passion, and I aspired to create a stronger connection between agent job satisfaction and quality outcomes. My love for data, first discovered during my time as a Data Analysis Coordinator for the Ontario Early Years Center, deepened as I began finding ways to connect numbers to meaningful stories. This passion has been a driving force throughout my career, helping me uncover insights and deliver impactful solutions.


My dedication to growth opened the door to an incredible opportunity: I was selected for the Project Nova program, where I took on Junior Project Management duties for the Wavelo Monos team. My time at Wavelo, combined with my love of ongoing learning, inspired me to take advantage of the Tucows Learning Benefit, earning both my Scrum Master Certification and the Google Project Management Certification. These certifications not only expanded my skill set but also strengthened my ability to deliver meaningful projects and solutions across the organization.

Today, I’m proud to serve as a People Enablement & Development Specialist with Tucows, a role that allows me to focus on what I love most — empowering people to grow, thrive, and unlock their potential. I’m passionate about creating opportunities that foster connection, collaboration, and meaningful development, all while maintaining a people-first approach. Most recently, I completed a course on AI for Training and Development, which has equipped me with innovative strategies to integrate AI into learning programs and further enhance the way we empower and support our teams.

> “I’m passionate about empowering people to grow, thrive, and unlock their potential.”


What makes my journey even more special is how my personal life has shaped my professional path. I’m a proud mom to seven amazing kids (yes, seven!), married to my best friend, and a devoted lover of baking. Fun fact: my 100-year-old sourdough starter is a family heirloom that brings joy and deliciousness to our home. Alongside my lively crew, we share our space with two very opinionated cats and a mischievous bunny, ensuring life is always full of fun, chaos, and plenty of love.


Throughout my time at Tucows, I’ve been inspired by the mentorship and encouragement I’ve received from incredible leaders. Whether as a support advisor at Hover or now as a training and development specialist, I’ve always been supported in thinking big, innovating, and exploring my passions. This encouragement has helped me grow as a leader and contribute to initiatives that strengthen Tucows’ culture and commitment to excellence.


Every day, I’m grateful for the opportunity to work with such an amazing team and to be part of a company that truly values its people. I’m excited about what lies ahead for me and for Tucows as we continue to innovate, connect, and grow together. Thank you, Tucows, for always investing in the heart of this company: the people.
