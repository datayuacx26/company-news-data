# It Sure Does Hit Differently

# **It Sure Does Hit Differently**


[Team Tucows](https://tucows.medium.com/?source=post_page---byline--a082d4bdcafa---------------------------------------)


4 min read


·


Apr 30, 2026


--


Almost six years ago, I joined Tucows, not entirely sure what to expect. Within weeks, I remember thinking, “I’ve found my dream job.”


It wasn’t just one thing. It was the people, the culture, and a mission that truly mattered: making the internet better. That alone felt worth showing up for. I didn’t know then that the work would eventually take me somewhere I never anticipated: standing in communities, watching that mission become real in ways I could actually see and touch.


Since joining[Wavelo](https://www.wavelo.com/) , the work has taken on a different kind of weight. Wavelo builds software that powers telecommunications companies, and when you sit close enough to that work long enough, you start to understand how real its reach is.

## The moment it clicked


The moment that made it real for me wasn’t in a meeting room or a product demo. It was when I travelled to a location where one of our client implementations was live. I pulled out my phone, activated an eSIM our team built, and used it to stay connected across the US.


**That was it. A piece of software our team had a hand in building, running in my pocket, working exactly as it should. Something about that simplicity hit hard.**


But that’s only part of the story. There’s something else that hits differently: visiting communities where our software powers the fibre internet running into people’s homes. Having coffee in a small coffee shop and knowing that the connection keeping their point-of-sale running, their music streaming, and their Wi-Fi open to customers traces back to software we built. Driving through a neighbourhood and thinking about the families, the small businesses, the social services, the fire stations, all of them connected because of work that happened on our side of the screen.


It sure does hit differently.


## A company that shows up for you


The support doesn’t stop at the work itself either. When I went on parental leave, I was supported at every step and able to fully disconnect, knowing my team had me covered. That kind of trust is rare, and it means everything when your family needs you present. Tucows and Wavelo genuinely walk the walk when it comes to supporting people, not just professionally, but as whole human beings.


## The people who invested in me


A big part of why I’ve grown here comes down to the people who’ve invested in me directly. Over the years, Tom McGillivray, Monique Krind-Jackson, Keith Beegan, Daniel Capoferri, Karaka Leslie, and Justin Reilly, to name a few, have each mentored and supported me in ways that shaped how I think, how I lead, and how I show up.

Beyond the mentorship, Wavelo invested in my continuous education, supporting certifications across Project Management, Product Management, and Change Management. They didn’t just give me opportunities. They made sure I had the tools to thrive in them.


That kind of leadership culture doesn’t happen by accident.


## Six Years In, and the Best Is Still Ahead


It’s easy to talk about impact in the abstract. It’s another thing entirely to stand in it and to be surrounded by people who helped you get there.


That’s what keeps me at Wavelo. The work is technical, complex, and genuinely consequential. The people around me take it seriously and still manage to make it enjoyable. And every so often, you get one of those moments where the gravity of what you’ve built becomes undeniably real.


If you’re thinking about what it’s like to build software that matters, in a company that gives you room to grow and shows up for you when life happens: this is it.

## About Tucows


We do a lot, but at our core, we’re in the business of keeping people connected and keeping the Internet open. We’re made up of three companies: Tucows Domains, Ting, and Wavelo.


- As[Tucows Domains](http://tucowsdomain.com/) , we help people find their place online as the world’s largest domain name wholesaler and the third-largest domain registrar globally.
- As[Ting Internet](http://tinginternet.com/) , we deliver high-speed fiber internet service to communities across the United States.
- As[Wavelo](http://wavelo.com/) , we believe the future of telecom is event driven. We build telecom billing and operations software for Mobile Virtual Network Operators and Fiber Internet Services.
