# The Ecosystem Isn't a Buzzword. It's Where the Next Wave of Value Lives.

# The Ecosystem Isn't a Buzzword. It's Where the Next Wave of Value Lives.


How Manhattan Marketplace makes innovation native to the platform.


- May 19, 2026
- Sanjeev Siotia
- 3 minutes


[Sanjeev Siotia Executive Vice President & Chief Technology Officer](https://www.manh.com/about-us/who-we-are/leadership-team/sanjeev-siotia)


-


In supply chain and commerce, the next wave of value will not come from the core platform alone.


It will come from the ecosystem built around it. Specialized agents. Industry accelerators. Regional extensions. New operational capabilities designed to solve highly specific problems.


But historically, every new capability came with the same tax: another integration project. Another disconnected system. Another cycle of custom work before the business saw any value.


That model does not scale into an AI-native future.


That is why we built Manhattan Marketplace.


### An enterprise marketplace built natively into the platform


Marketplace is a centralized ecosystem where customers and partners can discover and deploy intelligent agents, extensions, and accelerators built specifically for Manhattan Active® solutions. But the important part is this: these solutions are not bolted onto the platform from the outside.


They run natively on[ActivePlatform™](https://www.manh.com/solutions/manhattan-active-platform) itself.


That means they inherit the same operational framework, the same interoperability standards, and the same security model as the core applications already running your business.


And that changes the equation.


Because once innovation becomes native to the platform, deploying new capabilities starts to feel fundamentally different.


Instead of treating every enhancement like a one-off integration exercise, organizations gain a faster path from idea to operational impact. Teams can discover targeted solutions already designed for real operational workflows across industries, regions, and use cases. And they can deploy them directly into the environment they already trust.


### Lowering the barrier to ecosystem innovation


For developers and partners, the shift is equally important.


The challenge with partner ecosystems has never been a lack of ideas. The challenge has been the friction required to operationalize them.


[Agent Foundry™](https://www.manh.com/solutions/manhattan-active-platform/build-ai-agents-agent-foundry) helps remove that friction.


It gives partners a way to build intelligent solutions that plug directly into the Manhattan ecosystem without requiring massive, resource-intensive integration work. The result is a much lower barrier to innovation while still ensuring those solutions behave like a natural extension of the platform itself.


For example, a partner could publish an industry-specific warehouse workflow agent or a regional compliance extension directly into Marketplace. Customers could then discover and deploy those capabilities into their existing Manhattan environment without starting from scratch or stitching together disconnected systems.


### A shared foundation for interoperable innovation


That is the real power of an ecosystem built on a shared foundation.


You can build an agent.


We can build an agent.


Our partners can build an agent.


But because those solutions share the same deterministic operational spine, they can operate cohesively inside the broader platform instead of becoming isolated islands of functionality.


Marketplace ultimately creates a more scalable model for innovation across the entire ecosystem. Partners gain a clearer path to develop and distribute solutions. Customers gain faster access to targeted capabilities that extend the value of their Manhattan investments. And organizations spend less time managing integration complexity — and more time operationalizing innovation.


### Building the future of the Manhattan ecosystem


Partners are already developing for Manhattan Marketplace today, with customer access expected in upcoming quarters as additional solutions and capabilities are introduced over time.


Because in the AI era, openness alone is no longer enough.


The real opportunity is creating an ecosystem where innovation is interoperable by design.


That is the direction Manhattan Marketplace points toward: a future where extending your platform becomes dramatically faster, far more composable, and far less dependent on custom integration work.


The platform is the foundation. The software ecosystem is how you make it compound.


*Sanjeev Siotia is EVP & Chief Technology Officer at Manhattan Associates. Manhattan Marketplace is part of ActivePlatform™.*
