# Manhattan Recognizes its Valued Partners at Momentum 2026

## Manhattan Recognizes its Valued Partners at Momentum 2026


June 4, 2026


[PDF Version](https://ir.manh.com/node/21556/pdf)


ATLANTA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jun. 4, 2026--[Manhattan Associates Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.manh.com%2F&esheet=54547366&newsitemid=20260604059808&lan=en-US&anchor=Manhattan+Associates+Inc.&index=1&md5=43cb78921c1677011dec587731b3ce69) (NASDAQ: MANH) has announced the winners of the 2026 Manhattan Value PartnerTM (MVP) Excellence Awards. Presented at Momentum 2026, the company’s annual conference, the awards recognize partners whose innovation, expertise and collaboration are helping customers build more connected, agile and high-performing supply chain and unified commerce operations.


With deep industry knowledge and a strong commitment to customer success, these partners are delivering technologies, solutions and services that create measurable value and help organizations solve complex business challenges.


“At Manhattan, we know innovation scales faster through strong partnerships,” said Greg Betz, chief operating officer at Manhattan Associates. “Our partner ecosystem plays a critical role in helping customers modernize operations, move faster and deliver better experiences across supply chain and unified commerce. We are proud to recognize this year’s award winners for the expertise, investment and execution they bring to our shared customers in a market that continues to demand greater agility, resilience and business impact.”


Winners in the three categories were:


**Services & Engagement**


- **Deloitte Consulting** for collaborating on successful global programs and helping drive rapid customer value. They have made significant investments in their people and the future through Manhattan Product Training & Certifications, while leading strategic industry expansion.
- **enVista** for meaningful contributions across numerous business development cycles and implementation projects. Their strength across interconnected supply chain elements and disciplines, combined with the expertise, culture and drive of their teams, continues to fuel strong growth across Manhattan’s solution portfolio.


**Technology & Solution**


- **Veridian** for bringing strong market competency across a broad range of supply chain solution areas and technologies. As AI, automation and agentic solutions continue to reshape the industry, Veridian has invested in practical, enterprise-ready innovation that helps customers modernize faster, reduce complexity and realize greater value from their Manhattan solutions. Through its deep technical expertise and forward-looking approach, Veridian continues to help lead the market toward the next generation of supply chain execution.


**Collaboration**


- **Accenture** for driving large, complex, multi-channel and multi-geo implementations across all project phases, including critical areas such as warehouse automation and change management at scale. Accenture’s collaborative approach has been instrumental in creating value for customers and the broader ecosystem.
