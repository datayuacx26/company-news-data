# Juxta at HIMSS 2026 | Healthcare Operations Beyond GPS

### Healthcare Operations Need Positioning Beyond GPS


Juxta was in Las Vegas for HIMSS 2026, meeting healthcare operators, supply chain leaders, and technology partners. While HIMSS is often associated with digital health and care transformation, one message came through clearly: operational visibility inside healthcare environments still breaks down exactly where reliable coordination matters most.


Hospitals, clinics, ambulatory settings, receiving areas, back-of-house corridors, distribution points, and care coordination environments all create the same core problem. GPS does not hold up indoors, and fixed infrastructure adds deployment friction and cost.


### A Clear Signal from Healthcare Operations


Discussions with organizations connected to healthcare supply chain performance, medical technology, and large-scale care operations all pointed toward the same strategic need: better location continuity across facilities where traditional satellite-based assumptions fail.


### Why Healthcare Is a Strong Fit for Infrastructure-Free Positioning


Healthcare operations run across dense, dynamic, mixed-environment settings. Assets move between receiving areas, storage rooms, hallways, treatment spaces, and external handoff points. Infrastructure-free positioning becomes strategically relevant as a lighter operational layer supporting movement awareness without a major infrastructure rollout.


### The Biggest Takeaway: Showing the Workflow Beats Explaining the Category


The strongest motion was direct outreach into one-to-one demos, especially when people could see a concrete workflow rather than just hear a category description.


### What HIMSS 2026 Confirmed


Healthcare is not just a theoretical adjacency for Juxta — it is a category where positioning continuity has direct operational relevance.
