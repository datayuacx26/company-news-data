5. A contractor asks for a login that is not a shared Google account


At that moment you do not need another chart. You need an app-shaped system of record with permissions, automations, and optional AI that can read the same rows your team trusts.

## 🛠️ Field Notes From Painful Sheet Migrations


**Split "comment columns" from "state columns" before import.** Free-text rants can live in a notes field. Status must be an enum your automations understand. If status is a poem, flows cannot help you.


**Normalize owner names.**` jSmith` ,` John` , and` JS` are three owners to an automation. Pick one convention in the pilot import.


**Freeze the Sheet schema during dual-run.** If someone adds three columns on Thursday while you map on Wednesday, you will import fiction. Announce a schema freeze in the team chat.


**Resist building twenty views on day one.** Recreate the five views people already filter toward. Extra views are how ops apps become museums.


**Teach the cutover sentence:** "If it is not in Taskade Genesis, it did not happen." Until that sentence is true, you still have a Sheet company with an app hobby.


**Portal privacy is not optional.** Shared Sheet links that once felt "easy" are how client phone numbers leak. App Users exist so outsiders see a slice, not the warehouse.

## 📦 Worked Mini-Example: Contractor Job Log


Imagine a tab with columns: Date, Customer, Address, Job Type, Tech, Status, Price, Notes. Pain points: two techs edit status, the owner texts for updates, invoices lag because "Done" is inconsistent.


Taskade Genesis-shaped outcome:


1. Customer records separate from job records
