## What utilization actually means on Monday


Utilization is not a vanity chart. It is four questions:


1. Who is overbooked this week?
2. Who has slack you can sell?
3. Which clients are consuming more hours than scoped?
4. What happens if that pitch closes next Tuesday?


Float answers these with a beautiful schedule. Taskade Genesis answers them by making capacity a first-class board in the same workspace as projects and client portals.

## The per-seat tax for creative and services teams


Float bills per scheduled person. Guests can be free, but every designer on the grid is a seat. Runn bills per resource seat and commonly floors paid plans at 20 seats, which punishes a 12-person studio.


The irony of resource tools: the people you most need on the grid (flex contractors) are the ones finance hesitates to seat. So utilization looks "healthy" while the real calendar is chaos in Slack.

## Decision tree: Float vs Taskade Genesis


### Keep Float or Runn if...


- Your producers live in the schedule eight hours a day.
- You need estimates-vs-actuals finance dashboards as a system of record.
- Compliance wants a dedicated resource tool with mature SSO/SLA packaging.
- You are above ~40 scheduled people and already standardized training on Float.


### Choose Taskade Genesis if...


- Utilization is a weekly ritual, not a full-time scheduling department.
- You want agents to nag overbooking in the same workspace as client work.
- Seat minimums or contractor seating make the ROI ugly.
- You already run delivery, portals, or PM boards in Taskade and hate context switching.


### Hybrid is allowed


Many agencies keep Float for the schedule canvas and run delivery in Taskade Genesis. Hybrid fails when two truths diverge. Pick one canonical "planned hours" owner and write it on a sticky note if you must.

## Cost narrative for a 15-person studio


Imagine a 15-person studio: 10 full-timers, 5 rotating contractors. On Float Pro you might schedule all 15 and pay roughly $180/month before anyone tracks time. On Runn, a 20-seat floor can push you near the same number even if only 12 people appear on the planner most weeks. Neither invoice is "wrong." Both are priced for teams that treat the planner as a daily cockpit.


Now price the alternative narrative. The studio already runs delivery tasks, client feedback, and status updates somewhere. If that somewhere becomes Taskade Genesis, utilization is another board in the same cockpit, not a second product with a second login tax. The savings are not only dollars. They are the minutes lost reconciling "Float says 32 hours" with "Asana says the task is still in review."

If your producers truly live in Float, keep Float. If your producers live in client Slack and a task tool, stop pretending the schedule product is the center of gravity.

## FAQ


Is Taskade a Float alternative in 2026?


As a living utilization and capacity board, yes for many boutiques. As a dedicated resource-management product with Float's schedule density, no. Start at the[utilization generator](https://www.taskade.com/generate/agency-workflows/float-utilization) .


How is this different from Runn?


Runn optimizes scenario planning and resource seats (watch the minimum). Taskade Genesis optimizes ownership inside delivery workspaces. Compare your real seat invoice to your real ritual frequency.


Can we track billable utilization accurately?


You can track planned vs light actuals. For payroll-grade time and invoicing, keep a time or accounting system. A capacity board is not an ERP, and it works best when it stays that way.


Will an agent replace our producer?


No. Agents flag conflicts and draft reshuffles. Producers still make the political calls about who gets pulled off which client.


What if we already pay for Float Pro?


Run a two-week Taskade Genesis pilot for one pod without cancelling Float. If the pilot board dies after day three, you learned Float is earning its seats. If the pilot becomes the meeting agenda, renegotiate seats.
