## 📜 Why Exam Prep Tracking Breaks in Spreadsheets


Spreadsheets did not fail because tutors are disorganized. They fail for four structural reasons that show up at almost exactly the same point in a coaching business.


**Scores arrive from outside the system.** Practice tests are taken on official test-bank platforms, third-party question banks, and paper. Nothing writes back to your sheet, so every score is a manual re-entry. Manual re-entry decays the moment the roster grows past a handful of students.


**A spreadsheet has no row-level identity.** You either share the whole file or you maintain one tab per student. One tab per student kills every cross-student view you actually wanted: who is trending down, which section is weakest across the cohort, who has not tested in two weeks.


**Section breakdowns differ per exam.** Bar prep wants MBE and MEE. An IT certification wants domain percentages. The SAT wants Math and Reading/Writing. One flat sheet forces you to either add 30 columns or lose the detail.


**Parents and program managers are a third audience.** They need a weekly picture and nothing else. Spreadsheets give you two options — full access or a screenshot — and coaches end up rebuilding a report by hand every Sunday.


The three failure branches all share one property: the score exists but the loop does not close. A tracker earns its keep by turning an attempt into a drill list without a human remembering to.

## 🛟 Who Should Stay on an LMS or a Spreadsheet


This is the section most vendor comparisons skip. Three honest cases where switching is the wrong call.


**Stay on a course platform (Kajabi, Teachable, Thinkific) when:**


- **Selling a pre-recorded library is the business.** Their video player, DRM, checkout, and affiliate mechanics are genuinely strong and expensive to replicate.
- **Launch mechanics drive your revenue.** Email sequences, order bumps, and funnel pages are first-class there and not the point of a tracker.
- **Course completion is your metric.** If nobody asks about section-level scores, an LMS progress bar is already the right instrument.


**Stay on a spreadsheet when:**


- You coach **fewer than five students** and know every score from memory.
- Nobody outside you ever needs to read it. A sheet with one reader has no permissions problem.
- Your exam has **one number and no sections** , so there is nothing to model.


**Stay on a tutoring management suite when:**


- **Scheduling, invoicing, and tutor payroll** are the daily pain, not score history.
- You need **billing-grade session records** for a franchise or district contract.


Course platforms and tutoring suites are not wrong. They are purpose-built for delivery and billing. Taskade Genesis is a living workspace platform. Pick the constraint you refuse to compromise.

## ❓ FAQ: No-Code Exam Prep Score Trackers


Can students log their own practice scores?


Yes, through[App Users](https://www.taskade.com/learn/genesis/app-users) . Each student sees only their scores, assignments, and study plan, and logs new attempts on mobile-friendly views. Coaches see cohort views across all students. Start with two pilot logins before a full cohort rollout.


Can parents see student progress?


Yes with a parent App User login or shared read-only views you configure. Each login sees only the records you assign, so one family never sees another student's history. Coach-only cohort views stay private.


Does Taskade Genesis proctor exams?


No. Log scores from official practice tests, third-party platforms, or paper drills. Taskade Genesis tracks score history, section breakdowns, and study plans. It does not replace secure proctoring software. Keep proctoring with your exam vendor.


Does Taskade Genesis host video lessons?


No. It does not replace full LMS video hosting. Embed links to Loom, Vimeo, or your existing course platform beside student records. Keep the PDF bank and drill sources you already pay for and link them next to each tracker.


Which exams fit a Taskade Genesis score tracker?


SAT, ACT, GRE, GMAT, LSAT, bar exam, medical boards, IT certifications, and any exam with repeatable practice tests and section scores. Customize fields per exam by chatting with Taskade EVE. Bar prep carries MBE and MEE columns; an IT certification tracks domain breakdowns on the same profile.


Can I track multiple exams per student?


Yes. Add exam type as a field and filter views per test. One student prepping SAT and AP exams holds both histories on one profile without a second app.


How do I keep my existing score spreadsheets and my data?


Day two of the week-one rollout is importing students with their last known scores, so nothing you tracked gets thrown away. Records live in your own Taskade workspace, so you keep ownership and can hold the old spreadsheet as a read-only archive.
