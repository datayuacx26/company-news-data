## What a client portal is actually for


Most teams say "portal" and mean five jobs:


1. Clients see status without emailing you.

## The UI-skin problem (why portals feel hollow)


A skin is a user interface that reads and writes another system's tables. Skins are useful. They are also fragile when the business logic lives elsewhere:


- Airtable automations fire while Softr workflows fire, and nobody remembers which one owns the SLA timer.
- Client-visible fields drift from internal fields because two UIs edit one base with different permissions.
- AI features stay inside the builder credit budget instead of sitting next to the job record as an agent with tools.
- When you outgrow the base, the portal does not migrate with you. You rebuild the skin.
- Comment threads sprawl in email because the portal never became the conversation home.


Taskade Genesis flips the stack. The portal is generated *with* the memory layer (Projects), the intelligence layer (Agents), and the execution layer (Automations). The UI is still important. It is not the product.


That is the difference between a brochure login and an operating system your clients can see.

## Migration playbook (Softr / Airtable → Taskade Genesis)


Resist the urge to "big bang" a production portal on a Friday.


1. **Inventory fields.** Export or screenshot the client-visible fields and the internal-only fields. Mark secrets.
2. **Generate a twin.** Use the[Softr alternative client portal builder](https://www.taskade.com/generate/client-portals/softr-client-portal) and map columns to Project fields.
3. **Dual-run one client cohort.** Pick five friendly clients. Keep Softr as source of truth for two weeks while Taskade Genesis mirrors status.
4. **Move write path.** New requests enter Taskade Genesis first. Old portal becomes read-only, then sunset.
5. **Attach agents last.** Only after the board is trusted. An agent on a wrong schema amplifies mistakes.
6. **Document the cutover.** One page: who owns status, who notifies clients, which automation is canonical.

## FAQ


Is Taskade Genesis a Softr alternative?


For living client portals tied to ops, yes as an alternative *category* . For "Airtable UI builder with Softr's exact block set," no. Start at[/create](https://www.taskade.com/create) or the[portal generator](https://www.taskade.com/generate/client-portals/softr-client-portal) .


Can I keep Airtable and only replace Softr?


You can dual-run during migration. Long-term, Taskade Genesis is happiest when Projects are the system of record. Treating Airtable as forever master recreates the skin problem.


What about free Softr alternatives?


See[/compare/free-softr-alternative](https://www.taskade.com/compare/free-softr-alternative) . Free tiers everywhere have user, record, or AI caps. Evaluate the free path by the job (status page vs living ops), not by the logo.


Do clients need Taskade accounts?


Plan for authenticated client access through your published Taskade Genesis app patterns. Confirm current auth options for your workspace before promising a login model to customers.


Will this replace our CRM?


Only if your CRM is basically a request + status board. Full CRM pipelines, marketing automation, and quote CPQ stay in CRM tools. Taskade Genesis can sit beside them.


Can agents talk to clients directly?


Treat agent-to-client messages as drafts until a human approves, especially in regulated or high-stakes work. Agents are strongest as staff copilots on shared records.
