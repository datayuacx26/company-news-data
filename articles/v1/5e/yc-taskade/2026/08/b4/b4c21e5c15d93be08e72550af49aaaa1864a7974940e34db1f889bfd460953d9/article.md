Treat those figures as planning anchors, not forever invoices. Confirm live vendor pricing before you switch.

## 🏠 Sphere, ISAs, and Dual Roles Without IDX Myths


Solo agents rarely run a pure "internet lead only" business. After a CRM exit, protect three motion types:


1. **Sphere and past clients** who expect a human cadence, not a generic drip dump
2. **Paid portal / ad leads** that die without a five-minute text-back
3. **Under-contract coordination** where dates matter more than cute templates


Taskade Genesis can hold all three as stages and linked boards. It still will not replace your brokerage listing search. If someone sells you "CRM plus IDX in one migration weekend," ask where the MLS agreement lives. For most solos, that agreement is not something you want to reinvent under panic.


ISA reality check: if you hire inside sales help later, an owned board with clear stages and notes beats a tool your ISA has never seen. Document the dial script beside the lead record. Keep dispositions boring and consistent (no answer, wrong number, appointment set, nurture).


Buyer-seller portals reduce "any update?" texts when contingencies are active. That is where the[under-contract ops board](https://www.taskade.com/generate/real-estate-crm/fub-under-contract-ops-board) earns its keep next to the pipeline kit.
