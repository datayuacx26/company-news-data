## 📋 Migration Checklist: Portal Layer Without Burning the Course Host


Treat this as a two-week parallel run, not a big-bang cancel.


### Week 0: Inventory


1. List every Kajabi (or peer) module you opened in the last 30 days.
2. Mark video player, checkout, email, and affiliates separately from roster and progress.
3. Export active students, cohort names, start dates, and outstanding homework.
4. Decide which video links remain canonical (do not re-upload into Taskade Genesis).


### Week 1: Generate and map


1. Generate the[cohort ops portal](https://www.taskade.com/generate/education/kajabi-cohort-ops) .

5. Import a pilot cohort only (10 to 25 seats).


### Week 2: Portal truth


1. Invite one coach and three friendly students as App Users.

3. Turn on one automation: homework reminder or missed-check-in flag.
4. Keep checkout and video where they are. Update portal links in welcome emails.
5. Only after the pilot cohort graduates status visibility, migrate the next cohort's ops.

## 🤖 Agents and Automations That Earn Their Keep


Agents should sit on the same Projects students already use. Useful first agents:


- Draft a Monday check-in from incomplete homework rows
- Summarize office-hours questions into a coach brief
- Flag seats with no activity for N days
- Draft a mid-cohort progress email the coach edits once


Automations to enable after the pilot:


- Reminder when homework is overdue
- Status change when a coach marks a module complete
- Internal alert when three students stall on the same week (content bug signal)


Do not automate tone-deaf "you are failing" messages on day one. Start with coach-facing flags, then student-facing nudges once the copy is kind and accurate.

## 🛠️ Implementation Notes That Save a Rewrite


**Name fields in student language.** "Module 2 complete" beats internal codes like` M2_OK` . Students forgive plain words. They do not forgive mystery statuses.


**Separate coach-private notes from student-visible updates.** One leaked internal flag destroys trust faster than a late homework reminder.


**Limit automations in week one.** A single overdue nudge that sounds human beats five overlapping pings that feel like spam.


**Do not re-upload your entire video library "just because."** Link out. Migration theater burns days and creates two CDNs to maintain.


**Pilot with friendly students.** Power users who already message you in DMs will tell you which portal fields feel empty. Fix those before the next public cohort launch.


**Document the cancel criteria for the old LMS.** If you still need Kajabi for checkout this quarter, say so in writing. Portal migration and billing migration are different projects. Shipping both on the same weekend is how operators create outages.
