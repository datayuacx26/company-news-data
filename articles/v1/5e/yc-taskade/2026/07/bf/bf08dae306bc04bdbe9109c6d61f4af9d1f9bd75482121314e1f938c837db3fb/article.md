## What Does It Mean to Run Your Business in One Living System?


Running your business in one living system means your customers, projects, processes, and knowledge sit inside a single[Taskade Genesis](https://www.taskade.com/ai/apps) app instead of four disconnected tools. Connected projects hold the data, agents read it, and automations act on it. You describe the business in plain language, and the app is built around the shape your business already has.


The word doing the work is *living* . Most tools generate an app and stop, which is the whole reason[AI-generated apps go stale](https://www.taskade.com/blog/anatomy-of-genesis-app) the week after you build them. A living system keeps its state. The invoice knows which deal it came from, the agent knows what happened last Tuesday, and the automation fires whether or not anyone is at a keyboard.


Four shapes cover almost every business we see, and each one is cloneable below.


The system What it holds What it stops being


**Sales and CRM** Pipeline, deals, and clients A contact list plus a deal tracker


**Field service and ops** Assets, schedules, and service logs A spreadsheet plus a whiteboard


**Client portals** Projects, invoices, and messages A shared folder plus an email chain


**Finance and admin** Billed, collected, and overdue A billing tool you paste into


All four run on the same three-layer loop, **Workspace DNA** , which is the through-line of everything we ship. Memory feeds intelligence, intelligence triggers execution, and execution writes back to memory.


The kernel coordinating all of it is[Taskade TSK-1](https://www.taskade.com/blog/introducing-tsk-1) , and the architecture underneath is laid out in the[Workspace DNA teardown](https://www.taskade.com/blog/workspace-dna-architecture) .
