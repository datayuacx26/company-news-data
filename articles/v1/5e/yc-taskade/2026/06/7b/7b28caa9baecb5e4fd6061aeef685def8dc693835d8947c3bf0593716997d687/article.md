**Weaknesses** : Output is a static estimate you export, not a live project or budget tracker; software-scope focus, thin on agency services or resourcing; no estimate-vs-actual tracking.
**Pricing** : Free core plan; premium one-time fee around $19 (limited-time, originally $49) for sitemaps, user stories, and milestones.
**The catch** : It is a calculator, a great first number, but there's no project plan, budget tracker, or client quote app to run afterward.

### 6. Float: Best Resource-Led Budgeting


Float is the resource-management benchmark. It schedules people across projects, sets project budgets in hours or fees, and, with built-in time tracking on the Pro plan, shows scheduled versus logged time so a budget stays grounded in real capacity. For studios and agencies whose estimates are really a question of *who has the hours* , the resourcing-first approach is a genuine strength, and the per-person pricing with free guest seats keeps costs predictable.


**Best for** : Teams whose project estimates hinge on people's capacity and scheduled hours.
**Strengths** : Clean resource scheduling; project budgets in hours or fees; built-in time tracking (Pro); transparent per-person pricing with free guests.
**Weaknesses** : Resourcing-led, not a full scope-and-cost estimator; no client-facing quote; budgets live in Float, not a portable app.
**Pricing** : Starter around $7/person/mo and Pro around $12/person/mo (annual); Enterprise custom.
**The catch** : It estimates from capacity, not from a generated scope, and the budget is a hosted plan, not a live app you clone, brand, and turn into a client quote.
**Verdict** : Best if your estimate is mostly a resourcing question and you want capacity and budget in one scheduling tool.

Mosaic is the AI-resourcing entrant. For agencies and studios that want resourcing and forecasting to feel automatic rather than hand-maintained, the AI-driven allocation is a real strength, and the portfolio-level view of utilization helps spot over- and under-loaded teams early.


**Best for** : Digital teams that want AI to forecast capacity and allocate resources across a portfolio.
**Strengths** : AI-driven resource allocation; portfolio forecasting and workload management; visual, modern interface; surfaces utilization risks early.
**Weaknesses** : Forecasting and resourcing focus, not a scope-and-cost estimator with a client quote; custom pricing with no public free tier; output is a hosted forecast, not an owned app.
**Pricing** : Custom, tailored to organization size and needs, contact required for a quote.
**The catch** : It forecasts resourcing brilliantly, but there's no one-prompt cost estimate or client-facing quote, and the forecast isn't a cloneable app you own.
**Verdict** : Best for teams that want AI-powered resource forecasting across a whole portfolio.
