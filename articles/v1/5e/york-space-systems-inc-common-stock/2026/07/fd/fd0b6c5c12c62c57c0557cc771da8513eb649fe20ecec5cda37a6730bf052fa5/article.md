# York Space Systems First to Deploy Second Layer of T1TL Transport System, Confirms Health of 21 Additional Satellites on Orbit Within Hours

### York Space Systems First to Deploy Second Layer of T1TL Transport System, Confirms Health of 21 Additional Satellites on Orbit Within Hours


July 20, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s21.q4cdn.com/622448243/files/doc_news/York-Space-Systems-First-to-Deploy-Second-Layer-of-T1TL-Transport-System-Confirms-Health-of-21-Additional-Satellites-on-Orbit-Within--NUWV5.pdf)


*York’s second production lot launch brings its total T1TL satellites on orbit to 42*


DENVER--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[York Space Systems](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.yorkspacesystems.com%2F&esheet=54573168&newsitemid=20260720297422&lan=en-US&anchor=York+Space+Systems&index=1&md5=8e59e5fb6a75d92f2b9c41d51f8d711b) (York) (NYSE: YSS), a leading, US-based national defense and commercial prime providing a comprehensive suite of mission-enabling solutions, has made contact and confirmed the healthy status of all 21 satellites in less than five hours following launch vehicle separation. The system was launched on July 16, from Space Launch Complex 4 East at Vandenberg Space Force Base, CA aboard a dedicated Falcon 9 in support of the Space Development Agency’s (SDA) Proliferated Warfighter Space Architecture (PWSA) Tranche 1 Transport Layer (T1TL) mission. With this deployment, York is the first T1TL performer to deploy their second plane for SDA, delivering 42 satellites on orbit.


21 York-built satellites launched July 16 from Space Launch Complex 4 East at Vandenberg Space Force Base aboard a dedicated Falcon 9 in support of the Space Development Agency’s (SDA) Proliferated Warfighter Space Architecture (PWSA) Tranche 1 Transport Layer (T1TL) mission.


Deploying and confirming the health of all satellites within hours of launch continues to demonstrate the scalability and reliability of York's space vehicle production and operations model, supporting U.S. Government efforts to ensure resilient, low-latency satellite communications are available to U.S. and allied warfighters. A global network of[Atlas](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fatlasspace.com%2F&esheet=54573168&newsitemid=20260720297422&lan=en-US&anchor=Atlas&index=2&md5=f82ac3b0bc5835f690bff1197df07b56) Space Operations ground stations and the company’s Freedom Ground Software are supporting the mission and early orbit checkout.


"Rapidly confirming health across 21 satellites in a second consecutive T1TL launch is proof of York's mature high-rate production and disciplined mission operations capabilities at scale,” said Melanie Preisser, EVP and GM of York. “This T1TL deployment further reinforces York's continued role in enabling the DoD's vision for a proliferated architecture that ensures tactical assured communications resilience for the warfighter."


York continues to deliver operational capability at scale for a growing range of customers and mission sets. That consistent execution, across national security, civil, and commercial programs, positions York to continue capitalizing on the expanding portfolio of opportunities centered on proliferated space architectures.


“York remains committed to delivering the space-based capabilities that underpin U.S. national security,” added Preisser. “As geopolitical risks grow, so does the warfighter’s need for proliferated space capabilities on orbit, and York is proud to help meet that need.”


York has now placed 55 spacecraft in orbit. This launch marks the company’s eighth launch overall. The T1TL system is York’s third constellation, and fifth mission being operated simultaneously from their dedicated Mission Operation Centers. The Transport Network will provide a resilient tactical communication system for the warfighter and adds to the portfolio of capabilities demonstrated by York, which include Space Situational Awareness (SSA), Air Moving Target Indicator (AMTI)/Ground Moving Target Indicator (GMTI), Data Network, Custody, Targeting, and numerous classified missions.
