### Submitting Visits: The Quick-Step


Timely submission is everything. Enter each visit in the **Loula portal** as soon as possible—no waiting for Mercury to leave retrograde.


- **Deadline Rule:** Submit visits **during the cycle they happen or the very next cycle** .
- **Example Jam:** If a visit takes place on **September 7, 2025** , you’ve got until **September 30, 2025** (the end of the 9/16–9/30 cycle) to hit “submit.”
- **Pro Move:** Double-check the visit date before sending. Made a typo? Message **Loula Support** through the portal ASAP.


‍


### Visit Clarification: The Loula Remix


Sometimes our team might pop in with a quick “clarification request” while processing your visits.


- Reply within **48 hours** to keep your payment on track.
- Miss that window and your payout dances over to the **next cycle** —aka a longer wait for your dollars.

### The Pay Cycle Beat


[Loula pays](https://docs.joinloula.com/en/articles/10487187-loula-handbook#h_3a10d9ab3b) on a **semi-monthly** rhythm:


- **Cycle 1:** 1st – 15th
- **Cycle 2:** 16th – last day of the month


Here’s the kicker: **there’s a two-cycle delay** between submitting and getting paid.


- Submit in Cycle 1 → Paid on the **15th of the following month**
- Submit in Cycle 2 → Paid on the **last day of the following month**

### Why Loula’s Cycle Really Matters


A steady pay rhythm isn’t just nice to have—it’s a lifeline for most doula practices. California’s[prompt-payment law](https://djholtlaw.com/what-california-healthcare-providers-need-to-know-about-claim-reimbursement-and-dmhc-record-requests/) requires health plans to reimburse “clean claims” within **30 calendar days** of receipt, but that’s an ideal scenario. In reality, payers often contest or deny claims, triggering extra paperwork and stretching the process to **30–90 days** or more. Every time a claim is questioned, your income stream can stall while you wait for approvals, re-submissions, and appeals.


Loula’s two-cycle payout schedule is designed to smooth out those bumps. By aligning its payroll with the natural lag in insurance reimbursements, Loula absorbs the unpredictable waiting game on your behalf. You still submit visits promptly, but instead of chasing down multiple payers or worrying about late penalties and interest calculations, you can count on a **predictable semi-monthly deposit** . That reliability protects your cash flow, helps with budgeting, and keeps your focus where it belongs—supporting families, not tracking down payments.


**Bottom Line:** Stay in step with the Loula Cycle and your payments will keep hitting your account like clockwork—no off-beat surprises, just steady doula dollars.

## **10 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)**


**1. What is the Loula Cycle?**


The Loula Cycle is Loula’s semi-monthly payment schedule designed for doulas. Payments follow two repeating periods: **Cycle 1** runs from the 1st–15th of each month, and **Cycle 2** covers the 16th–last day of the month. Doulas are paid on a two-cycle delay, creating a predictable rhythm so you always know when money is coming.


‍


**2. How soon after submitting visits will I be paid?**


Payments arrive about one full cycle later. If you submit during **Cycle 1** , you’re paid on the **15th of the following month** . If you submit during **Cycle 2** , expect payment on the **last day of the following month** .


‍


**3. When is the deadline to submit a visit?**


You must submit visits **during the cycle they occur or by the very next cycle** . For example, a visit on **September 7, 2025** must be entered no later than **September 30, 2025** . Submissions after that miss the payout train and will not be processed for that visit.


‍


**4. What happens if I submit a visit late?**


Late submissions aren’t eligible for the intended cycle. Loula requires on-time entries to maintain accurate billing and compliance, so missing the deadline may mean forfeiting that payment altogether.


‍


**5. What is a “clarification request,” and how should I respond?**


Sometimes Loula’s team needs additional details—such as confirming visit times or correcting typos. You’ll receive a portal message labeled “clarification request.” Reply within **48 hours** to keep your payment on track. A slower response pushes your payout to the next cycle.


‍


**6. How can I avoid missing a clarification request?**


Enable **portal notifications** or email alerts. These reminders act like your personal metronome so you never miss Loula’s follow-up and can maintain smooth payment timing.


‍


**7. What if I make a mistake entering visit information?**


Double-check each entry before hitting submit. If you spot an error afterward, message **Loula Support** through the portal immediately. Prompt corrections help keep your visit eligible for the current cycle.


‍


**8. Can I submit visits immediately after they happen?**


Absolutely—and it’s the recommended approach. Submitting visits in real time protects you from deadline stress and ensures the Loula team has plenty of time to process and verify details.


‍


**9. Are there any tips to keep payments flowing smoothly?**


Yes:


- Submit visits the day they occur.
- Keep portal notifications on.
- Respond to clarification requests within 48 hours.
- Treat the Loula Cycle like a train schedule—being early or on time guarantees a seat (and a check).


‍


**10. Why is there a two-cycle delay before payment?**


The delay allows Loula to verify visit details, handle insurance processing, and ensure accurate payouts. Think of it as a buffer that keeps the whole payroll rhythm consistent and dependable for all doulas.
