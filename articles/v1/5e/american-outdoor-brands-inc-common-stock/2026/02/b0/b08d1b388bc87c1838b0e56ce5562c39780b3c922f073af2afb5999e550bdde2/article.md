# AOB Featured on Water Tower Research Small-Cap Spotlight Podcast

Tags


SOURCE: PODCAST


[PDF Version](https://ir.aob.com/node/9141/pdf)


American Outdoor Brands, Inc. (NASDAQ: AOUT) President & CEO Brian Murphy and CFO Andy Fulmer recently joined Water Tower Research for its WTR Small-Cap Spotlight podcast series.


Hosted by Tim Gerdeman, Chairman, and Doug Lane, Head of Consumer Products, the 30-minute conversation provides an in-depth look at AOB’s business, innovation strategy, recent product launches, and long-term growth opportunities. Doug has closely followed the Company through his research coverage and participation in recent earnings calls, making for an insightful and engaging discussion.
