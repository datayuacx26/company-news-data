# Employer Guide to Working with TMAs

**What is a TMA?**


TMAs (or Transportation Management Agencies) are typically non-profit organizations set up as public/private partnerships to improve transportation access in a city, region, or state. TMAs may cover multiple counties or cities so they can provide more transportation options across administrative borders.


Typically TMAs are created to provide transportation options in areas where they are lacking or non-existent, reduce single occupancy vehicle trips and their emissions, and improve parking access and impact. A typical TMA is run by members that can include local government and transportation departments as well as employers and property managers in the local area.


**Working with a TMA**


Employers don’t necessarily have to join a TMA board to benefit from their programs. Many TMAs have support websites, employer phone numbers, and transportation management coordinators that employers can use to access TMA programs and vanpool fleets. TMAs can provide additional resources for your employees to get started with vanpooling and commuter transit benefits information (including local and state benefits in addition to federal commuter transit benefits).
