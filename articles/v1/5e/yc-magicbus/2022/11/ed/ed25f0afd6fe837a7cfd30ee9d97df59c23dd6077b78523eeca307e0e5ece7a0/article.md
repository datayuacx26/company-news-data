# Commuter Survey

## How Do You Commute to Work?


Figuring out how to get to work can be the biggest challenge when starting a new job. We’d like to learn more about how you commute to work and find out the benefits and challenges for your mode of transit.


Vanpooling can offer a customized transportation option that can save money and minimize the challenges of driving alone such as traffic, detours, and paying for gas and insurance when driving alone. The MagicBus team can give you and your company the resources to get started with vanpooling including subsidy information to help save even more when you share the commute with vanpooling.
