# Flexible Scheduling Survey

## How do Employers Manage Flexible and Hybrid Schedules?


Companies and employers have embraced hybrid schedules that allow workers to commute to the office or workplace several times a week and work remotely for the rest.


At MagicBus we’ve been working to make it easier to plan, launch, and grow your vanpool program or employee shuttle service but we’re interested in how your company handles flexible schedules, hybrid work environments, and keeping employees connected and in sync across multiple work places – in office and remote.


### How is your company handling flexible scheduling?


We’d love to hear more about how your company is handling flexible scheduling in our survey below.
