# Newmark Arranges Sale of a Note Secured by an Entitled Adaptive Reuse Multifamily Project in San Pedro

**Los Angeles, CA (August 10, 2026)** — Newmark announces it has arranged the sale of a note secured by Topaz Tower, a fully entitled adaptive reuse development project located at 222 West 6th Street in San Pedro, California. The 12-story, 309,732-square-foot office tower is entitled for conversion to 246 market-rate multifamily units.


Newmark Executive Managing Director[Ryan Plummer](https://www.nmrk.com/people/ryan-plummer) , Co-Head of U.S. Capital Markets[Kevin Shannon](https://www.nmrk.com/people/kevin-shannon) , Vice Chairman[Ken White](https://www.nmrk.com/people/ken-white) , Senior Managing Director[Chris Benton](https://www.nmrk.com/people/chris-benton) and Managing Director[Anthony Muhlstein](https://www.nmrk.com/people/anthony-muhlstein) represented the lender. The buyer was 222 PV Harbor View, LLC, a private Los Angeles-based residential developer.


“Topaz Tower represents an exceptionally rare opportunity to acquire a fully entitled coastal high-rise multifamily conversion with scale, ocean views and an immediate execution path in a rapidly evolving submarket,” said Plummer.


Shannon added, “The South Bay remains one of Southern California’s most sought-after submarkets, sitting squarely in the path of unprecedented aerospace and defense investment. Coupled with its immediate proximity to West Harbor, the region’s newest waterfront entertainment destination, San Pedro offers a compelling combination of economic momentum and lifestyle appeal.”


Topaz Tower Apartments will include a mix of studio, one-, two- and three-bedroom residences with an average unit size of 835 square feet. Planned amenities include a rooftop deck with a resort-style pool, ocean-view units, balconies and high ceilings. The existing property contains 309,732 square feet of total building area on a 71,986-square-foot site, along with 750 parking spaces, 15,397 square feet of parking-structure retail and 13,482 square feet of tower retail.


Located one block from Metro’s J Line and less than two blocks from the $1 billion West Harbor waterfront redevelopment, the property sits within a Qualified Opportunity Zone and offers walkable access to Downtown San Pedro’s retail, dining and entertainment amenities.
