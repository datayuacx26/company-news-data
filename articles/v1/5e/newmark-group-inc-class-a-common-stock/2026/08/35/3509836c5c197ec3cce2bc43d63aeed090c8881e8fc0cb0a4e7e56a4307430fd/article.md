# Newmark Arranges $33.5 Million Fannie Mae Financing for Multifamily Complex in Reno, Nevada

***Photo courtesy of Elan Multifamily Investments* August 5, 2026 9:00 AM**
Newmark announces the Company has arranged $33.5 million in Fannie Mae financing for Ascent on Steamboat, a 204-unit multifamily community in Reno, Nevada, on behalf of Elan Multifamily Investments. The five-year, fixed-rate loan was secured by Executive Managing Directors[Lowell Takahashi](https://www.nmrk.com/people/lowell-takahashi) and[Vince Punzi](https://www.nmrk.com/people/vince-punzi) .


The transaction resulted from a year-long process in which ownership deliberated strategic options amid shifting market conditions. Rather than pursuing a conventional financing approach, Takahashi and Punzi cast a wide net across the lending landscape to identify capital sources capable of providing favorable terms with near-term flexibility.


“We worked closely with the sponsor to understand the evolving business plan and evaluate all available options, from selling the property to refinancing existing debt,” said Takahashi. “Throughout that process, our focus was on identifying a financing solution that would align with the sponsor’s needs. We brought a broad range of lenders to the table and ultimately closed a Fannie Mae loan that delivered attractive terms and provided the kind of certainty the sponsor needed moving forward and were able to close just over 30 days from application thanks to the proactive borrower team.”


Located at 3300 Skyline Boulevard in Reno’s sought-after Old Southwest neighborhood, Ascent on Steamboat offers residents a blend of urban convenience and outdoor accessibility. The garden-style community features one- and two-bedroom apartment homes with upgraded interiors, panoramic mountain and city views. It likewise features extensive amenities, including indoor and outdoor swimming pools, a fitness center and clubhouse.


The property also benefits from convenient access to Skyline Boulevard, McCarran Boulevard, Interstate 80 and Interstate 580, connecting residents to major employment centers throughout the Reno-Tahoe region.


“Ascent on Steamboat has benefited from years of disciplined capital investment and hands-on asset management,” said Elan Chief Executive Officer Christie Johnson-Cerna. “For us, the right financing isn’t simply about achieving the lowest interest rate, it’s about preserving flexibility and protecting long-term value. Throughout the process, the Newmark team served as trusted advisors, combining deep experience with a proactive, solutions-oriented approach.”


Newmark Managing Director[Hunter Curtis](https://www.nmrk.com/people/hunter-curtis) also assisted in securing the loan, in collaboration with Takahashi and Punzi.
