# Newmark Arranges Full-Building, 150,316-SF Industrial Lease for Dynamic Manufacturing in Forest Park, Illinois

Newmark announces the Company has arranged a long-term, full-building industrial lease totaling 150,316 square feet on behalf of the tenant, Dynamic Manufacturing, at 7801 Industrial Drive in Forest Park, Illinois.
