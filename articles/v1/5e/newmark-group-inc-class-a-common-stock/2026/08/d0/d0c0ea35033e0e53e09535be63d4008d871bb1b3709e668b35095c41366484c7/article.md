# Newmark Arranges $58 Million Fannie Mae Financing for Everleigh San Clemente in Orange County

Newmark announces the Company has arranged a $58 million Fannie Mae loan for Everleigh San Clemente, a newly constructed 150-unit Class A active-adult (55+) apartment community in San Clemente, California.
