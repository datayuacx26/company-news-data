# Newmark Pacific Arranges $16.5 Million Sale of Link OC Retail Center in Anaheim, California

Newmark Pacific announces it has arranged the $16.5 million sale of Link OC, a 26,385-square-foot necessity-based retail center located at 1071, 1081 and 1095 North Tustin Avenue in Anaheim, California.
