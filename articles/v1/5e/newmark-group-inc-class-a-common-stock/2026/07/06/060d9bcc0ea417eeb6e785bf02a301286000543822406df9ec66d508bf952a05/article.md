# Newmark Real Estate Arranges Sale of 205,000-Square-Foot Industrial Facility to DCI International in Wilsonville, Oregon

Newmark Real Estate announces the Company has arranged the $31 million sale of 26440 SW Parkway Avenue, a 205,100-square-foot industrial and manufacturing facility in Wilsonville, Oregon.
