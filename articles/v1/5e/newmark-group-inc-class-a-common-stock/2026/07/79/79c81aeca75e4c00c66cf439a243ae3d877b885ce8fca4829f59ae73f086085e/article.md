# Newmark Arranges $163 Million Fannie Mae Financing for LaSalle Multifamily Portfolio

Newmark announces the Company has arranged a $163 million loan on behalf of LaSalle Investment Management (“LaSalle”) to refinance a portfolio of three multifamily properties secured through a Fannie Mae credit facility.
