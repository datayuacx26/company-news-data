# Newmark Facilitates Sale and Financing of 140-Unit Multifamily Community in Charlotte, North Carolina

Newmark announces the Company has arranged the $27.8 million sale of and $15.9 million in acquisition financing for Reserve at Waterford Lakes, a 140-unit garden-style multifamily community.
