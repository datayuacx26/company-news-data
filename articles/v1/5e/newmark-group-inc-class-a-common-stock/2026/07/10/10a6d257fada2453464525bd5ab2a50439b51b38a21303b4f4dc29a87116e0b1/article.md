# Newmark Arranges Whole Foods Market Lease at 1524 Second Avenue on Manhattan’s Upper East Side

Newmark Retail announces it has arranged a 8,121-square-foot lease with Whole Foods Market at the base of Continental Towers, a luxury residential building located at 1524 Second Avenue on Manhattan's Upper East Side.
