# Newmark Arranges $132 Million Sale of Featherstone Industrial Portfolio in Northern Virginia

Newmark announces the Company has arranged the $132 million sale of the Featherstone Industrial Portfolio, a 13-building, 734,606-square-foot industrial portfolio in Woodbridge, Virginia.
