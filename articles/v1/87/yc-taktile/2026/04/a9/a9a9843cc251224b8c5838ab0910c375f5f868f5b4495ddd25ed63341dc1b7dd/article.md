## **Why this partnership matters: Document fraud is no longer an edge case**


Integrating specialized document fraud detection into your risk strategy is quickly shifting from a nice-to-have to an operational necessity. Here's why:


According to Inscribe's[2026 State of Document Fraud Report](https://www.inscribe.ai/reports/2026-document-fraud-report) , nearly 1 in 16 documents processed across their network showed signs of fraud in 2025. Fraud risk is broadly distributed across document types, and bank statements, pay stubs, tax forms, business filings, and invoices all fall within a 4-7% fraud rate range. This means that document manipulation can touch any process where documents are used to establish trust.


At the same time, the tools fraudsters use are becoming more advanced. For example, AI-generated document fraud grew nearly 5x between April and December 2025.


For fraud and underwriting teams, the challenge is twofold. Forged documents are harder to spot with the naked eye, and the sheer volume of suspicious applications strains manual review processes. Teams that rely solely on human reviewers face longer queues, higher workloads, and increased risk of both missed fraud and unnecessary friction for legitimate applicants.


Catching this kind of fraud requires purpose-built detection technology. Through our reseller partnership with Inscribe, Taktile makes that intelligence instantly accessible.
