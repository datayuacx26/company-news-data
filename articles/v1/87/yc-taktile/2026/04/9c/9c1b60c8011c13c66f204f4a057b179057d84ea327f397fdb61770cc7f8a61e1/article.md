# Taktile's Financial Spreading Agent: How credit teams are automating their most time-consuming task

Financial spreading is one of the most time-consuming tasks in commercial lending. With high application volumes and limited resources, teams often struggle to balance speed and accuracy.


For every credit decision, an analyst turns complicated financial documents such as tax filings and management reports into a standardized format for analysis. The process requires meticulous work like extracting line items and aligning time periods from across multiple document types.


**Until recently, financial spreading has been impossible to automate end-to-end.**


Traditional tools might be able to extract certain metrics from a highly standardized document format, but they can't handle variations, spot inconsistencies, or learn and improve their approach over time. ## Why financial spreading has resisted automation – and what's changed


To understand why advances in agentic AI mark such a significant shift, it helps to dig deeper into what makes financial spreading so complex.


Until now, most automation approaches have treated financial spreading as a document extraction problem: basic Optical Character Recognition (OCR) tools can learn to pull numbers from clearly defined document templates. But if a customer submits a slightly different format, the system breaks down.


Additionally, **data extraction only covers a fraction of the actual work. The real skill lies in interpretation and judgment.**


An expert analyst can reconcile misaligned time periods across multiple statements or apply institution-specific logic to determine what counts as an interest expense. A misclassified line item can directly impact a credit decision – and automated solutions haven’t been able to replicate this level of nuance.


Now,[research from Taktile Labs](https://taktilelabs.ai/benchmarks/finspread) shows that recent **AI** **advances are putting reliable automation within reach** . Specialized AI agents can now achieve 96%+ accuracy on financial spreading tasks, surpassing the 89% human baseline. This performance threshold was crossed in late 2025, making it feasible to automate this complex task for the first time.
