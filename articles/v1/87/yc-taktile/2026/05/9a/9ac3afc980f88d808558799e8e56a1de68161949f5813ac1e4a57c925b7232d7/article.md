# Taktile is a G2 Summer Leader. Hear from the financial services teams behind our momentum.

Many financial services teams have moved past the question of whether to adopt AI. Now, leaders across credit, fraud, AML, and compliance are learning how to deploy agentic AI within their workflows.


This shift has changed what teams are looking for in a platform. The teams accountable for decision outcomes should adopt solutions where AI agents, business logic, and human expertise can work together in a controlled environment.


As Taktile celebrates its recognition as a G2 Leader, we’re spotlighting how our customers are addressing that challenge today: the platform capabilities they're using, the outcomes they're seeing, and what they say about the experience in their own words.


## Building for the teams shaping what's next in financial services


At Taktile, we’re dedicated to helping financial institutions stay at the forefront of AI transformation. That’s why one of our major platform investments over the past year has been to expand our AI agent library, with specialized pre-built agents for supporting some of the highest-value use cases in financial services.


It's reviews like these that helped Taktile earn G2 recognition as a Leader and Momentum Leader this summer:


> *"I appreciate Taktile's leading strategy to be at the forefront of the decisioning market. They not only focus on if-then logic-based decisioning, but also enhance their tools with AI, helping teams stay ahead of the market.”*


## Using third-party data to make faster, smarter decisions


Our customers understand deeply how decision quality often depends on the data behind it. The main challenge, however, has been getting the right data into the right hands at the right moment, especially when so much of it can be gated behind third-party providers or unstructured resources that must be read line by line.


Teams are now leveraging AI agents to transform how they gather and analyze data. With the right setup, agents can pull from third-party data sources, parse documents, and surface important context in plain language. Speaking to the impact of these capabilities, one customer described what that looks like in practice:


> *"What Taktile provides is the ability to analyze third-party data sources and transform that into human natural language, allowing bank processors to expeditiously review loans and accounts, resulting in lower cost of operations and more accurate decision criteria evaluation."*


## Leveraging an agentic, low-code platform to make critical decisions independently


Our customers also prioritize making quick decisions as markets shift, fraud patterns evolve, or regulations tighten. To unlock efficiency while maintaining a high bar for risk mitigation, analysts and operators are adopting platforms that they can adjust autonomously.


Across our customer base, technical and non-technical users are getting flows into production on their own terms:


> *"I appreciate Taktile for its simplicity of use and easy functionality, which is really great compared to other products on the market. It's incredibly intuitive, allowing newcomers to learn and start building flows quickly. The ability to swiftly change flows based on changing business circumstances is a big plus."*


> *"I use Taktile for its simplicity, allowing quick flow changes with changing business needs. It's intuitive for new users to learn, and its AI helps build flows faster, enhancing workflow efficiency. The user interface offers visibility across teams, supporting growth as a business."*


## Iterating on logic quickly without sacrificing responsibility


Our customers operate in markets that move week to week. Fraud patterns evolve, credit conditions shift, and regulations tighten, all while users expect smoother experiences than ever.


To support that pace, we've spent the last year deepening the capabilities that help teams move fast without losing oversight: an updated end-to-end case management system for AML and fraud teams, more configurable agent oversight, and stronger versioning and audit trails across the platform.


One customer summed up what that looks like in practice:


> *"Taktile enables us to very quickly iterate our credit policy for a market that is constantly evolving, such that we can ensure we're always lending responsibly and also preventing the business from losses due to bad actors."*
