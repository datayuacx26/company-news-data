# Scaling AI across Nubank’s 100M+ customers

In this episode of *Risk and Robots* , Maximilian Eber, Co-Founder and CPTO of Taktile, sits down with Henrique Lopes, Head of AI at Nubank, to talk about what it really takes to put AI into production in a highly regulated environment. Henrique shares how Nubank’s first use case—accelerating code migrations—evolved into customer-facing applications, why they decided to build their own models on transaction data, and the trade-offs between rules, ML, and LLMs in banking. They also cover practical lessons on measuring success, running experiments, managing hallucinations, and knowing when to fine-tune versus train from scratch.
