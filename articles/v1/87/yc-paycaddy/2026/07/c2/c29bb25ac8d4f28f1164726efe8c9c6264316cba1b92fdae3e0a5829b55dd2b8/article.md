# How to Incentivize Card Use with a Benefit Strategy

Every brand placing their logo on a card wants maximum usage. This article outlines building a card benefit strategy to incentivize card use and maximize profitability.


## Step 1: Define Your Goals


Before designing a benefit strategy, establish clear objectives. Do you want to increase user retention, attract new customers, or boost transaction volume?


### Why Define Goals?


Goals are essential because they:


- Help you focus resources on meaningful benefits rather than wasteful ones
- Clarify what success looks like and how to measure it
- Enable tailoring your strategy to customer needs and business objectives


**Goal Example:** To increase transaction volume, offer higher cashback percentages for customers making a specific number of monthly transactions, encouraging more frequent card use.


## Step 2: Know Your Customers


Understanding customer needs and preferences is critical. What benefits appeal to them — discounts, cashback, or exclusive events?


### Research Methods


- Conduct surveys about desired benefits and perceived value
- Analyze transaction history to identify purchase patterns
- Study competitor benefit offerings


**Customer Preference Example:** For frequent travelers, airport lounge access or travel-related discounts would be compelling incentives.


## Step 3: Determine Your Budget


Decide how much you can afford to spend on rewards without breaking the bank.


### Budget Considerations


- Revenue generated by your card program
- Competitor spending levels
- Expected return on investment


**Budget Allocation Example:** A program generating $10,000 monthly revenue allocating 10% toward benefits would have a $1,000 monthly benefit budget.


For new programs lacking sufficient revenue, focus on sign-up bonuses and referral rewards to attract customers. As your program grows, increase benefits gradually while maintaining alignment with business objectives.


## Step 4: Design Your Benefit Strategy


Create tiered benefits based on transaction volume or frequency.


### Design Tips


- Offer escalating benefits for higher transaction volumes
- Include sign-up bonuses for new cardholders
- Provide referral rewards for customer acquisition


### Tiered Strategy Example


Transactions Per Month Cashback Percentage


0-10 0%


11-20 0.25%


21-30 0.5%


31+ 0.75%


## Step 5: Calculate ROI


Continuously evaluate whether benefits drive desired results.


### ROI Calculation Method


- Calculate total benefit costs
- Measure revenue increase since implementation
- Divide revenue increase by benefit costs


**ROI Example:** Spending $1,000 on benefits yielding $2,000 revenue increase equals an ROI of 2.
