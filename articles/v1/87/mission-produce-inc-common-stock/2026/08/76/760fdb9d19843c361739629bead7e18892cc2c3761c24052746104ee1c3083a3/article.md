# Premium Mangos – the Unexpected Door to Category Growth

Walk the produce department right now and mangos are having a moment—but not the mango you might expect. It’s the premium tier—organics, super jumbo sizes and specialty varieties like Keitt and Calypso—that shoppers are increasingly reaching for.1 These premium mangos are quietly fueling category growth—and for retailers, that shift is opening up a real opportunity to grow the category.


### **Premium Mangos Are a Category Entry Point**


Everyday-priced fruit may be how many shoppers first discover mangos, but there’s another door into the category, and it’s one more retailers should consider opening– **premium mangos** .


That means nearly one in six new mango shoppers started with a premium variety rather than an everyday one.2 For this meaningful share of buyers, premium fruit is how they find their way into the category in the first place.


These new buyers are worth paying attention to. Demand for premium mangos has climbed 22% since 2023—that’s more than 700,000 additional households buying premium mangos compared to just two years ago.1


Even better for the long game: today’s premium mango shoppers skew toward Gen Z, Millennial, Hispanic, and Asian households3—some of the most strategically important consumer groups for retailers today.


In other words, this isn’t a trend riding on a shrinking base. It’s growth built on the shoppers retailers want to win.


### **Premium Shoppers Are More Valuable Shoppers**


Offering premium mangos doesn’t just check a specialty box—it attracts a fundamentally more valuable shopper.


That’s the value of stocking premium alongside your everyday fruit. When shoppers have both options in front of them, many add premium to their basket rather than trading out of the everyday tier—and that combination drives more frequent trips and higher annual spend across the category.4


Premium mango shoppers are worth paying attention to on their own, too. They spend about $43 annually on the category—roughly 3x the category average—and make 9 trips a year, double the purchase frequency of non-premium shoppers.5


The takeaway for retailers: premium mangos bring in shoppers who buy more often and spend more across the category, making them one of the clearest ways to grow both trips and basket size in the mango set.
