# Cinco de Mayo, the Biggest ‘Mango-ccasion’ of the Year!

Cinco de Mayo is a celebration of culture, cuisine, and—of course, at Mission Produce— fresh produce sales! While avocados have long been recognized as a Cinco staple, this month’s AvoIntel is here to showcase another menu must-have– our World’s Finest Mangos™.


**In fact, Cinco de Mayo is the biggest mango holiday of the year!1**


In 2024, a staggering 12.4 million pounds of mangos sold during the week of Cinco de Mayo, resulting in a 25% lift compared to the prior week.1 So, with 93% of Cinco celebrators planning to make a purchase for their festivities this year,2 retailers, wholesalers, and foodservice operators have a big opportunity to win with mangos.


**How Consumers are Celebrating Cinco de Mayo**


Cinco de Mayo celebrations often revolve around food and drink, making it a key opportunity to showcase our favorite produce items!


**Three Must-Try Mango Recipes for Cinco de Mango**


**1. Avocado Mango Salsa**


This fresh and vibrant salsa is perfect for scooping with chips or spooning over grilled meats. Combine diced mangos, avocados, tomatoes, red onion, jalapeño, lime juice, and cilantro for a sweet and spicy twist on a classic.


****


**2. Mango Guacamole**


Give traditional guacamole a tropical upgrade! Mash ripe avocados and mix in finely chopped mangos, tomatoes, lime juice, and a pinch of salt for a creamy, flavorful dip that pairs perfectly with tacos and nachos.


**3. Chicken Mango Nachos**


A favorite of the[National Mango Board](https://www.mango.org/recipes/chicken-mango-nachos/) , these nachos are a perfect blend of savory and sweet, combining shredded chicken, diced mango, melted cheese, and a hint of spice. Top crispy tortilla chips with the mixture and bake until golden. Finish with fresh cilantro, a squeeze of lime, and of course, some diced avocados, for an irresistible party favorite!


Cinco de Mayo—or as we like to call it, “Cinco de Mango”—is a prime time to drive mango sales and elevate consumer engagement. With the right strategies and flavorful inspiration, this mango-ccasion is sure to be a sweet one!
