# How Well Do You Know Your California Avocados?

As a California-based company, we hold the California avocado season close to our hearts. Mission was founded in Oxnard, California, in 1983, and we’ve been selling the World’s Finest Avocados™ ever since. With the 2025 California season off to an early start, we’re looking forward to a high-quality season!


Keep reading for some fun facts about California avocados!


**Avocados: California’s Favorite Fruit**


California is the largest producer of avocados in the U.S.,1 and they have become an iconic part of California’s agricultural landscape. In fact, the world-famous Hass avocado tree originated right here in Southern California.1 Rudolph Hass propagated the first one in the late 1920s, and the rest is history.1


**48,000+ Acres of Green Goodness**


California boasts more than 48,000 acres of avocado trees,2 producing some of the highest-quality avocados in the world. At Mission, we work with hundreds of growers along the Pacific Coast to cultivate high-quality, high-producing trees that keep the avocado love going strong.
