# 5 Fresh Ways to Give Thanksgiving Leftovers an Avocado Upgrade

It is that time of year when shoppers are craving Inspiration for delicious ways to re-invent their Thanksgiving leftovers. Read more to learn how fresh avocados are the perfect ingredient to solve this annual challenge!

avocados bring balance to the post-holiday table—turning traditional leftovers into something fresh, flavorful, and new.


Here are


**five ways** consumers can reinvent Thanksgiving leftovers with avocado leading the charge:


#### **1. Turkey & Avocado Harvest Sandwich**


Layer sliced turkey, mashed avocado, leftover stuffing, and a touch of cranberry sauce between toasted sourdough. The creamy avocado balances the sweet-tart cranberry and savory turkey beautifully.


#### **2. Thanksgiving Leftover Tacos**


Warm up turkey and stuffing, then spoon into tortillas with avocado slices, a drizzle of gravy or hot sauce, and a squeeze of lime. Add shredded lettuce or slaw for crunch.


#### **3. Turkey & Avocado Omelette**


Whisk up a fresh start after the feast. Fold leftover turkey and fix-ins into a fluffy omelette, then top with diced avocado and a spoonful of cranberry sauce for a balanced, feel-good breakfast.


#### **4. Autumn Grain Bowl with Avocado**


Combine leftover roasted veggies, turkey, and stuffing with a scoop of quinoa or farro. Add diced avocado and a drizzle of olive oil or vinaigrette for a hearty, balanced lunch.


#### **5. Cranberry-Avocado Toast**


Spread mashed avocado on toast, top with a spoonful of cranberry sauce, and sprinkle with flaky salt and cracked pepper. It’s sweet, tart, creamy, and totally addictive.


With stable supply, favorable pricing, and endless creativity, avocados are proving their place on the Thanksgiving table—and beyond.
