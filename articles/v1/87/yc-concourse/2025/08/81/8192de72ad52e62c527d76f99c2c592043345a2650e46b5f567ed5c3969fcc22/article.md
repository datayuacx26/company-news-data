# AI Agents for Controllers: Redefining the Finance Function

Month-end has long meant late nights of reconciliations, checklist chasing, and audit prep.


AI agents change that. They handle these workflows in real time, moving the close forward while controllers focus on the decisions that matter most.


Controllers have always been the quiet force keeping a company’s financial foundation solid: managing risk, enforcing compliance, and guiding leadership with accurate, timely data. But the reality inside many finance teams hasn’t changed much in a decade: manual reconciliations, checklists in spreadsheets, and supporting documents gathered after the fact. The bottleneck isn’t skill. It’s the tools. Most ERPs and close platforms track what needs to be done, but leave controllers orchestrating the process instead of shaping policy and strategy.


AI agents change that dynamic in minutes. Sitting on top of your ERP and close tools, they can pull the right data, draft variance narratives, compile supporting documentation, and prepare checklist updates so you can review, refine, and approve without starting from scratch. The result is faster analysis, fewer manual pivots between systems, and more time to focus on the decisions that matter.


## **Why Legacy Close Systems Slow Controllers and Month-End Automation Efforts**


Even with modern close management tools like FloQast or BlackLine and ERP add-ons from NetSuite to SAP, controllers still run into familiar roadblocks. Often, they find themselves stitching together data from multiple systems, chasing down exceptions, and managing checklists that still require manual follow-through. The result is a close process that feels more like project management than true automation, and pulls attention away from the higher-value work the business needs


### **Fragmented data sources**


Most controller workflows span multiple systems: the ERP for transactions, spreadsheets for reconciliations, shared drives for supporting documentation, and project trackers for close checklists. None of these sources talk to each other in real time. Building a complete financial picture means hours of manual extraction, consolidation, and validation, repeated multiple times when numbers shift late in the process.


### **Manual reconciliations and close checklists**


Close management tools excel at logging status, but the heavy lifting still lands on the controller’s desk. These steps often require jumping into spreadsheets, writing pivot tables, and following up with multiple teams to resolve discrepancies. Every manual handoff introduces delay, and every spreadsheet formula is a potential point of failure.


### **Compliance and audit prep**


Controllers are responsible not only for closing the books but also for keeping them defensible. Yet supporting documentation is still too often compiled after the fact, weeks or months after the transactions occurred. This reactive approach creates last-minute scrambles when auditors request samples, increases the risk of missing evidence, and forces senior staff to spend critical close-window hours on administrative work instead of oversight.


## **What Are AI Agents for Controllers?**


AI agents act as a multiplier for a controller’s impact. They work alongside you, taking on the heavy, repetitive workflows that consume most of the close window and handling them in real time across the systems you already use. Instead of simply tracking tasks, they move them forward, reconciling accounts, drafting variance explanations, compiling audit documentation, and updating close checklists without waiting for manual follow-up.


With an agent in place, the same number of hours in your day goes further. Reconciliations are completed while you are reviewing policy changes. Variance analysis is underway while you are meeting with leadership. Audit support is being assembled in the background as transactions are processed. The work is happening in parallel, giving you the leverage to manage more without adding headcount or extending deadlines.


Interacting with an agent is simple. You can ask in plain language for what you need, whether it is reconciling AP and AR subledgers, flagging variances over a set threshold, or identifying GL accounts with notable changes, and the agent understands the intent and produces a clean, review-ready output with the narratives, numbers, and supporting evidence you need.


AI agents do not replace your ERP, close management software, or accounting platforms. They sit on top of them as an execution layer, enhancing the capabilities of the tools you already have. Implementation takes minutes. Once your data sources are connected and permissions are set, the system is ready to run. The result is speed, accuracy, and consistency without the disruption of a major system change, and the ability to focus on the policy, risk, and strategic oversight that drives real value.

## **How Controllers Use AI Agents to Automate Reconciliations, Audit Prep, and Variance Analysis**


Here are five examples of how controllers can use AI agents to save hours each week and remove friction from the close process:


> **List all GL accounts with a +10% variance from last month and draft explanations**


→ The agent scans transaction-level data, identifies accounts with significant movement, and produces contextual variance narratives ready to paste into management reports, cutting variance analysis time by **up to 80%** compared to manual spreadsheet work.


> **Compile supporting documents for all journal entries over $50k in the last quarter**


→ Pulls each relevant entry from the ERP, gathers linked invoices, receipts, and memos, and assembles a review-ready evidence packet for auditors or management sign-off.


> **Reconcile intercompany balances and suggest elimination entries**


→ Matches intercompany transactions across entities, flags mismatches, and drafts the corresponding elimination entries. Eliminating **3–5 hours of spreadsheet matching per entity** and reducing post-close adjustments by **up to 40%** .


> **Identify all transactions missing a cost center and recommend the correct assignment**


→ Detects incomplete coding, predicts the correct cost center using historical patterns, and presents suggested updates for review and approval.


> **Update close checklist status across all entities and flag overdue tasks**


→ Syncs with your close management tool, reconciles checklist progress, and alerts you to bottlenecks in real time so that you can resolve blockers before deadlines slip.
