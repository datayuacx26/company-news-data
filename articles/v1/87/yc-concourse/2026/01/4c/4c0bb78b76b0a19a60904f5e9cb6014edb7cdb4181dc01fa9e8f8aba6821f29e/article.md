# Concourse Raises $12M Series A and Launches General Availability

A year ago, we[came out of stealth](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/15/concourse-is-building-ai-to-automate-financial-tasks/) with a bet that AI agents could fundamentally change how finance teams work.


Today, Concourse AI agents are deployed inside Fortune 500 companies, unicorns, and some of the fastest-growing startups in the world, including **Palo Alto Networks, Front, and Tecovas.**


In the past 12 months, we've **grown revenue 19x** and **expanded our customer base 13x** . Finance teams using Concourse are producing **6x more analysis** in the same amount of time and **cutting manual work by up to 75%** .


Our waitlist to use Concourse grew to thousands of companies. The demand was clear that finance teams are ready to move into the agentic-first world. We're now making that transition accessible to everyone. We’re excited to announce that:


**Concourse is now generally available.** Any finance team can start using Concourse for free, connecting data sources like QuickBooks, Ramp, or Stripe in minutes and immediately deploying AI agents to automate analysis, generate reports, and answer complex questions.


**We raised a $12M Series A led by Standard Capital.** The Series A included participation from Andreessen Horowitz, CRV, Y Combinator, SV Angel, FOG Ventures, and Ritual Capital; CFOs from companies like Cursor, Vercel, and Carta, 1Password; and executives at OpenAI, Brex, Mercury, Color, and more. This funding accelerates our ability to deliver the most capable AI agents to finance teams of any size.


### **Why finance teams are moving to agents**


Finance sits at the center of every major business decision: pricing, hiring, resource allocation, strategic investment. But most finance teams are stuck in a decades-old tradeoff between software automation or the flexibility of manual work.


Traditional finance software gives you rigid workflows. Spreadsheets give you flexibility but require endless manual work. The result? Critical insights live scattered across disconnected systems, basic reporting takes days instead of minutes, and finance teams spend more time on data manipulation than driving the business forward.


**Concourse AI agents eliminate that tradeoff.**


Modern agents can reason through ambiguous questions, pull data from multiple systems, follow multi-step logic, and execute sophisticated analyses, all through natural language. For the first time, finance teams can combine the flexibility they need with true automation.


The impact is immediate. Teams using Concourse report:


- Presentations that used to take 5 days now completed in under an hour
- Data retrieval that previously required logging into multiple systems and downloading data now done in minutes
- Month-end close review processes automated from start to finish in hours
- Ad-hoc analysis that would take a full day now returned in minutes


This isn't incremental improvement. It's a fundamental shift in how finance teams operate and the impact they can have.


### **How Concourse works**


Concourse AI agents connect directly to your financial stack (ERPs, data warehouses, billing systems, CRMs, expense tools) and work alongside your team through natural language.


Teams use Concourse to:


- **Ask complex questions** and get instant answers across multiple data sources
- **Generate presentation-ready outputs** in minutes (PowerPoint, Word, PDF, email)
- **Automate recurring workflows** with proactive reporting agents that run on your desired schedule
- **Delegate entire projects** to agents that reason through problems and execute multi-step logic


Concourse agents show their work, surfacing the underlying SQL, Python, and reasoning behind every output through a built-in transparency panel. You can trust the answer and understand the why driving your business.
