# 8 Best Trovata Alternatives in 2026

Trovata helped modernize treasury by connecting directly to banks over APIs and giving teams real-time, multibank cash visibility without the months-long implementation of a legacy TMS. It is a strong product, but it is visibility-first, and teams that need deeper forecasting, risk, and payments, or different pricing, often start weighing **Trovata alternatives** .


This guide compares the eight best Trovata alternatives in 2026. They range from enterprise suites like Kyriba and GTreasury to AI-native forecasting tools and the new wave of AI agents that *execute* treasury work rather than just display it. For each, we cover what it does best, who it fits, and where it falls short.


## **What Are the Best Trovata Alternatives?**


> The best Trovata alternatives in 2026 are Kyriba and GTreasury (Ripple Treasury) for enterprise depth, HighRadius for AI cash forecasting, Nilus for explainable forecasting, Embat for European mid-market treasury, Modern Treasury for payment operations, and Concourse for teams that want AI agents to run treasury workflows end to end.


> **Concourse is the AI-agent alternative on this list.** Instead of being another cash-visibility dashboard, Concourse deploys autonomous finance agents that connect to your ERP, data warehouse, and banking data to run cash forecasting, variance analysis, AR and collections, and liquidity reporting end to end, with every number traceable back to source. It is built for finance teams that want AI to own the work, not just visualize it.


## **Why Teams Look for Trovata Alternatives**


Trovata is well liked for real-time cash visibility and ease of use. The friction tends to show up in three places.


**It is visibility-first.** Trovata’s core strength is aggregating bank data into a live, multibank cash view. Teams that need deep financial-risk management, FX and interest-rate hedging, payment rails, or in-house banking often want a fuller treasury management system, or an execution layer, on top of that visibility.


**Pricing starts in the low five figures.** Third-party pricing trackers list Trovata’s base package at[around $25,000 per year](https://zoftwarehub.com/products/trovata/pricing) , including one bank connection, up to 100 bank accounts, and up to a million transactions. That is reasonable for its tier, but it sends budget-sensitive and SMB teams looking for lighter options, and larger teams comparing it against fuller suites.


**Forecasting and reporting depth.** Reviewers frequently praise Trovata’s visibility while noting they want more from its forecasting and reporting customization. Since cash forecasting is the hardest job on the treasury desk, that gap matters: an[EY-Parthenon analysis](https://www.ey.com/en_us/insights/strategy/cash-forecasting-difficult-and-more-urgent-than-ever) of roughly 2,400 of the largest global companies found only **28% of cash forecasts landed within 10%** of actual free cash flow, and **91% of treasury teams** still lean on Excel per Strategic Treasurer’s survey[reported by CTMfile](https://ctmfile.com/story/survey-says-treasurers-want-more-accurate-cash-forecasting) .


None of this makes Trovata a bad tool. It means "best cash-visibility platform" and "best fit for your team" can be different answers. ## **How We Evaluated Trovata Alternatives**


No single platform is best for everyone. A 3,000-entity multinational and a 40-person startup need very different things. We compared the alternatives across six criteria that actually change the buying decision.


- **Bank & ERP connectivity.** How many banks and systems it connects to, and how automatically balances flow in.
- **Cash forecasting.** Accuracy, AI/ML methods, and how easily you can explain a forecast to leadership.
- **Automation depth.** Does it just report, or does it execute payments, reconciliations, collections, and reporting?
- **AI capability.** From basic anomaly detection to autonomous, agentic workflows.
- **Auditability.** Can every figure be traced back to source data and defended to auditors and a board?
- **Fit & pricing.** Ideal company size, and how cost and time-to-value compare with Trovata.

### **2. Kyriba, Best for Large Global Enterprises**


Kyriba is one of the most widely deployed enterprise treasury management systems, and the natural step up for teams that have outgrown a visibility-first tool. It brings bank connectivity, payments, fraud detection, FX and risk, and working-capital tools into one governed platform, at global scale.

**Keep in mind.** Cost and implementation effort are significant, and it is likely overkill for mid-market teams.


### **3. GTreasury (Ripple Treasury), Best for Enterprise Treasury + Payments**


GTreasury is a long-established enterprise TMS, in operation since 1986, combining cash forecasting, bank connectivity, and risk workflows in one place. In late 2025,[Ripple acquired GTreasury for $1 billion](https://ripple.com/ripple-press/ripple-breaks-into-corporate-treasury-with-gtreasury-acquisition/) and launched **Ripple Treasury** in January 2026, pairing an enterprise TMS that has processed roughly $12.5 trillion in annual payment volume with Ripple’s payments network and digital-asset infrastructure.


For a team that wants Trovata-style modernity but with deeper enterprise treasury and payments, GTreasury is a direct fit.


**Best for.** Mid-market to enterprise treasuries that want centralized operations plus a modern payments future.


**Keep in mind.** The Ripple integration is still maturing, so evaluate the current roadmap rather than just the legacy product.


### **4. HighRadius, Best for AI Cash Forecasting at High Volume**


HighRadius is an order-to-cash and treasury platform positioned as the AI-first cash-forecasting specialist, with agentic features across forecasting, cash application, deductions, and collections. It advertises up to 95% forecasting accuracy and is strong for high-transaction-volume environments, per its own[treasury systems roundup](https://www.highradius.com/resources/Blog/best-treasury-management-system/) .


If your reason for leaving Trovata is forecasting depth on a firehose of receivables data, HighRadius is built for exactly that.


**Best for.** High-transaction mid-market and enterprise teams whose priority is AI-driven cash forecasting and O2C automation.


**Keep in mind.** Accuracy claims depend heavily on your data quality. No forecast is better than the bank and ERP inputs feeding it.


### **5. Nilus, Best for Explainable, Driver-Based Forecasting**


Nilus is an AI-native treasury platform that stands out when teams need forecasts they can *explain* , tied to the underlying drivers and to concrete treasury actions rather than a black-box number. Its[buyer’s guide](https://www.nilus.com/post/the-7-best-ai-native-treasury-platforms-in-2026-honest-buyers-guide) positions it for PE-backed and multi-entity teams that need policy-backed liquidity decisions.


For a CFO who has to defend a forecast to investors, explainability is often worth more than a marginally higher accuracy claim.


**Best for.** PE-backed and multi-entity organizations that need explainable, policy-driven liquidity decisions.


**Keep in mind.** It is a newer, narrower platform, so validate coverage for your specific bank and ERP mix.


### **6. Embat, Best for European Mid-Market Treasury**


Embat is a modern European challenger, founded in Madrid in 2021 by two ex-J.P. Morgan executives and now serving[400+ corporate clients across Europe](https://fintech.global/2026/05/12/embat-raises-e30m-series-b-to-drive-european-growth/) . It gives mid-market teams one platform for cash visibility, forecasting, reconciliation, intercompany workflows, and payments, connecting to 15,000+ banks. Its agentic assistant, TellMe, automates the last mile of treasury work under human review.


For teams that ran into Trovata’s regional connectivity gaps in Europe, Embat is a strong, locally-strong alternative.


**Best for.** European mid-market and PE-backed teams that want a modern, regionally-strong platform.


**Keep in mind.** It is strongest in Europe, and global-scale coverage is less proven than the incumbents.


### **7. Modern Treasury, Best for Payment Operations at Scale**


Modern Treasury is less a visibility-first tool and more the money-movement layer. It provides payment operations infrastructure and APIs to initiate, track, and reconcile payments at scale, with a complete payment ledger. Companies that move large volumes of money programmatically use it to control and reconcile those flows.


**Best for.** Companies whose core treasury need is reliable, auditable payment operations and money movement.


**Keep in mind.** It is payment-operations infrastructure, so pair it with a forecasting or liquidity tool if you need full treasury analytics.


### **8. Coupa Treasury, Best for Teams Already on Coupa**


Coupa Treasury brings cash management, forecasting, and payments into Coupa’s broader business-spend management suite. Its natural buyer is an organization already standardized on Coupa for procurement and spend that wants treasury on the same platform rather than a standalone tool.


**Best for.** Companies already invested in Coupa that want treasury and liquidity alongside spend management.


**Keep in mind.** The value case is strongest inside the Coupa ecosystem; as a standalone tool it is less specialized than Trovata on pure cash visibility.
