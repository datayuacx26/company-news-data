# Production, Sales and Export Results for April, 2026

Honda Motor Co., Ltd. today announced a summary of automobile production, Japan domestic sales, and export results for the month of April 2026.


#### World Production


April 2026 YOY April 2025


Japan 58,762 units 122.9% 47,806 units


Outside of Japan 218,133 units 94.3% 231,288 units


Total 276,895 units 99.2% 279,094 units


#### Production in Main Regions


North America 153,614 units 105.9% 144,993 units


USA 97,045 units 105.7% 91,852 units


Asia 53,145 units 67.9% 78,266 units


China 28,842 units 61.1% 47,195 units


##### April 2026


- Production in Japan: 5th consecutive month of YOY increase
- Production outside Japan: 4th consecutive month of YOY decrease
- Worldwide production: 3rd consecutive month of YOY decrease


#### Sales in the Japanese Market


April 2026 YOY April 2025


Total 45,744 units 101.5% 45,082 units


Registrations 27,936 units 113.5% 24,611 units


Mini-vehicles 17,808 units 87.0% 20,471 units


##### April 2026


- Total sales in Japan: 1st YOY increase in 13 months
- New vehicle registrations: 1st YOY increase in 18 months
- Sales of mini-vehicles: 3rd consecutive month of YOY decrease
