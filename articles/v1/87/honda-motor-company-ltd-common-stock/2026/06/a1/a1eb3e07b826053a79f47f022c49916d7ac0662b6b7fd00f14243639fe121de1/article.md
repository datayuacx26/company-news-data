# Production, Sales and Export Results for May, 2026


-
-


Honda Motor Co., Ltd. today announced a summary of automobile production, Japan domestic sales, and export results for the month of May 2026.


#### World Production


May 2026 YOY May 2025


Japan 52,026 units 105.4% 49,348 units


Outside of Japan 221,281 units 92.9% 238,150 units


Total 273,307 units 95.1% 287,498 units


#### Production in Main Regions


North America 142,863 units 99.7% 143,319 units


USA 88,437 units 99.1% 89,196 units


Asia 65,694 units 77.1% 85,229 units


China 32,885 units 66.5% 49,477 units


##### May 2026


- Production in Japan: 6th consecutive month of YOY increase
- Production outside Japan: 5th consecutive month of YOY decrease
- Worldwide production: 4th consecutive month of YOY decrease


#### Sales in the Japanese Market


May 2026 YOY May 2025


Total 43,087 units 104.3% 41,298 units


Registrations 23,985 units 107.7% 22,276 units


Mini-vehicles 19,102 units 100.4% 19,022 units


##### May 2026


- Total sales in Japan: 2nd consecutive month of YOY increase
- New vehicle registrations: 2nd consecutive month of YOY increase
- Sales of mini-vehicles: 1st YOY increase in 4 months


#### Exports from Japan

Total 8,227 units 75.7% 10,875 units

USA 671 units 22.4% 2,991 units


Asia 412 units 216.8% 190 units


Europe 4,916 units 136.7% 3,596 units


Others 2,155 units 56.3% 3,827 units


##### May 2026


- Total exports from Japan: 1st YOY decrease in 2 months
