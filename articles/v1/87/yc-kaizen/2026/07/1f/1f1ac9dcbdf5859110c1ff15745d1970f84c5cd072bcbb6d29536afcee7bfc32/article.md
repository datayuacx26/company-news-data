# Medallion Credentialing Reviews: Is It Worth It in 2026?

## What is Medallion?


[Medallion](https://medallion.co/) is an AI-assisted credentialing platform built around an NCQA-certified Credentials Verification Organization (CVO), combining **AI agents with credentialing experts to manage credentialing** , payer enrollment, licensing, and provider monitoring.


## Medallion features


Medallion bundles credentialing, enrollment, licensing, and monitoring into a single platform, **powered by AI agents** with expert oversight. For healthcare ops teams, what matters is where each capability fits credentialing, enrollment, or licensing workflows and where it falls short.


- **CVO credentialing:** Combines AI agents that automate primary source verification with credentialing specialists who review critical stages, generating NCQA-compliant files in real time. Strong for organizations wanting a fully managed CVO with SLA guarantees.
- **Payer enrollment automation:** Submits and tracks applications across commercial and government payers, with status updates flowing back into the platform. Useful for health companies enrolling providers across dozens of payers.
- **Cross-state licensing:** Automates license applications, renewals, and live tracking across states, including IMLC, DEA, and CSR registrations. Built for health firms expanding into new states, but document handling within the platform creates findability issues as licenses are continuously verified and re-verified.
- **Delegated credentialing:** Manages NCQA-compliant workflows with full audit visibility, automated PSV, and committee management tools. Strong for health systems and large provider groups running delegated agreements with payers, where reportable committee tools and audit readiness matter most.
- **Roster management:** Ingests, standardizes, and validates provider rosters across entities, syncing with health plans without manual effort. Useful for consolidating fragmented roster data from spreadsheets or legacy systems, though best paired with credentialing or enrollment rather than used as a standalone capability.
- **Real-time compliance monitoring:** Sends alerts on credential expirations, sanctions, and license issues across the provider network. Strong for risk management at health systems with regulatory exposure.
- **CredAlliance:** Medallion's national credentialing clearinghouse that verifies provider credentials once and reuses them across participating networks with three-day SLAs. Built for payers and large health systems eliminating redundant credentialing.


## The ROI math on Medallion


[Medallion's pricing](https://medallion.co/pricing) is custom-quoted based on provider volume, delegation structure, payer complexity, integration requirements, and the mix of services included (CVO, enrollment, licensing, monitoring, privileging).


**Medallion's own ROI claims.**[Medallion's e-book](https://medallion.co/resources/ebooks/comparing-the-outcomes-and-costs-of-medallion-vs-traditional-provider-operations-methods) benchmarks itself at **49.8% cost savings and 78% faster operations** versus traditional in-house methods. The Headspace case study reports a 60% reduction in time the team spends on credentialing.


In **Medallion's worked example** , an organization currently spending around $750,000 annually on credentialing tools and staff would move to a Medallion-based model at roughly $376,380 per year: **a $373,620 savings, or a 49.8% reduction** . Medallion frames the FTE side as either reduction or, more commonly, reallocation of staff to provider-facing or revenue-related work.


These are vendor-reported figures based on Medallion's own ROI model rather than independent validation. **The cost of delay.** The other side of the ROI math is the delay cost. A single day of credentialing delay costs a medical group an **average of $10,122 in lost revenue** , according to a[2019 Merritt Hawkins survey cited by MGMA](https://www.mgma.com/mgma-stat/more-than-half-of-practices-report-credentialing-related-denials-on-the-rise-in-2021) .


Medallion's pricing sits in the **upper range** of credentialing platform options. The investment is typically justified by the ROI math above, but buyers should expect a **custom quote process** before getting firm numbers.

### The platform is easy to use and navigate


[Brent B., a Director of Medical Staff Operations at a medical practice](https://www.capterra.com/p/250098/Medallion/#Capterra___6027387) , mentions that Medallion has an "easy to use UI, excellent support staff." Another[review](https://www.g2.com/products/medallion/reviews/medallion-review-10665006) echoes the point, mentioning that **dashboard transparency and request submission** flows are simple to use.


### Strongest fit for delegated credentialing at scale


**Medallion's strongest vertical** signal is in delegated credentialing.[Anna F., Director of Delegated Credentialing and Enrollment](https://www.capterra.com/p/250098/Medallion/#Capterra___6200262) , says for organizations pursuing delegated credentialing or planning on obtaining it, "Medallion is a must."


Medallion's published[case study with Tampa General Hospital](https://medallion.co/resources/case-studies/medallion-drives-2-4m-in-roi-for-tampa-general) reports **$2.4M in ROI within the first six months** of engagement (Medallion's footnote breaks this out as $2.3M in operational ROI plus $100K in software cost savings).


### The platform is stable and the API is reliable


For organizations integrating Medallion into existing systems, **platform stability and API reliability** are make-or-break.[Sean C., a VP of Marketing and Program Management](https://www.capterra.com/p/250098/Medallion/#Capterra___6629944) at a hospital and healthcare organization, reports that the platform is "stable and our API connection hasn't failed" in their usage.


### Onboarding is fast for new customers


[Kit K., a Revenue Operations Manager](https://www.capterra.com/p/250098/Medallion/#Capterra___4045914) , writes "Onboarding was quick and easy," with Medallion **exceeding expectations** as her second credentialing vendor. For organizations switching credentialing vendors, this matters more than feature comparisons. A smooth handoff in the first weeks predicts whether the rollout will succeed or stall.


### Where Medallion falls short


### CAQH data ingestion is incomplete


Medallion pulls from CAQH ProView to pre-populate provider profiles, but the integration **doesn't reliably bring over all fields** .[Jennifer F., a Managed Care Coordinator](https://www.capterra.com/p/250098/Medallion/#Capterra___6200410) at a hospital, mentions the "CAQH pull doesn't pull all information over." For practices that selected Medallion partly to skip manual CAQH work, this is a notable gap.


### Quality, consistency, and contracting delays are real concerns


For provider groups whose business depends on credentialed clinicians' billing, these timeline failures translate to **real revenue loss** .[Jana S., a Medical Operations Manager](https://www.g2.com/products/medallion/reviews/medallion-review-10665006) , says the "Quality of work is subpar," citing constant turnover on her account that creates errors and erodes provider trust.


[Lisa W., a Medical Staff Credentialing Coordinator](https://www.g2.com/products/medallion/reviews/medallion-review-10665075) , writes that some of her providers are still not contracted after around two years. For provider groups whose **revenue depends on credentialed clinicians** , that's not a minor inconvenience.


### Customization is limited across profiles, reporting, and credentialing files


The platform's "configure within our framework" approach works for organizations whose workflows fit the defaults. A[verified user in hospital and healthcare](https://www.g2.com/products/medallion/reviews/medallion-review-10703294) notes that "reporting is currently minimal," and the **report builder isn't template or roster-friendly** .


[Irina S.](https://www.capterra.com/p/250098/Medallion/#Capterra___6644332/) also mentions a "lack of ability to customize profiles, reporting, credentialing files, data fields." For organizations with specialty-specific data needs, unique compliance requirements, or custom reporting demands, this determines whether Medallion fits at all.
