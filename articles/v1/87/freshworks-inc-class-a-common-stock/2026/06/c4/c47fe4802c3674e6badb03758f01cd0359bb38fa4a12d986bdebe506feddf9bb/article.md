# Overheard at Refresh 2026

Freshworks held its annual[Refresh](https://www.freshworks.com/events/refresh-virtual-summit-2026/) conference May 14 New York City, and theCUBE was there for all of the action. During the event, theCUBE's Bob Laliberte sat down with Freshworks executives, customers, and partners for conversations that spanned topics such as AI readiness, platform consolidation, and the future of AI-powered service.


Watch the full video series at[theCUBE](https://www.thecube.net/events/freshworks/refresh-2026) , or keep reading for highlights from each interview. You can also hear more from the people featured here at the[Freshworks Virtual Summit](https://www.freshworks.com/events/refresh-virtual-summit-2026/) on June 4 (registration is free).


##### Dennis Woodside: Fix the foundation first


“Every customer that I talk to is looking to AI to help them transform their service operations. A lot of companies are challenged in that their data is fragmented, their tech stack is fragmented, so the first thing they have to do is consolidate—especially on the IT side—into a system of record that is robust, broad, enterprise-grade.”

##### Kady Srinivasan: CIO as the growth engine


"A lot of the CIOs that I talk to have this lens of, 'I'm not here to run IT software, I'm here to help become the engine of the company's growth. They really think of themselves as the backbone of how they can get their customers, their employees, back to doing the work that they're supposed to be doing faster."

##### New Balance’s John Boers: The adoption sprint


“Before we rolled out Freshservice, we had a very old tool. It was very agent-centric, hard to use, confusing. With Freshservice, we were able to get a tool built out in a couple of months, ready for go-live, that was user-friendly, it looked good, and got tickets where they needed to be.”
