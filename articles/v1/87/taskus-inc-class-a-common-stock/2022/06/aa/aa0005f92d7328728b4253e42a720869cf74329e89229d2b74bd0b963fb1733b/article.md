# TaskUs opens Cali site ‘The Arena’ with on-site events and wellness support for teammates

*After more than a year of operations, TaskUs opened the doors of its Cali office, “The Arena,” to employees through a ceremonial ribbon-cutting event attended by teammates, journalists, and members of TaskUs leadership. (From left to right: Alejandro Ossa, Invest Pacific Director; Liliana Sierra, Secretary of Economic Development; Diana Muñoz, Director of the International Cooperation Office; Alejandro Alvira, a TaskUs teammate; Guillermo Ulloa, Vice President of Operations; Isabella Mariño, Director of ProColombia; and Fabian Saavedra, Director of PacificShore)*


**CALI, Colombia (June 9, 2022)** — TaskUs (Nasdaq: TASK), a provider of outsourced digital services and next-generation customer experience to fast-growing technology companies, opened the doors of its Cali office, “The Arena” to employees yesterday, June 8. A ceremonial ribbon-cutting event was held where teammates and guests personally experienced the unrivaled workplace facilities that TaskUs teammates can enjoy as they begin working on-site.


“As we embrace the new normal, we now have hundreds of employees that are finally coming to The Arena,” said Guillermo Ulloa, TaskUs Colombia Vice President of Operations. “The feedback from our teammates has been very positive. They love the colors, the lighting, the sports-themed lobby, and the soccer field! They are also very much looking forward to physically working alongside their fellow teammates and experiencing everything our site facilities have to offer.”


TaskUs Colombia[started its operations](https://www.taskus.com/press-release/taskus-grows-further-with-latest-expansion-in-cali-colombia/) in March 2021 on a purely Work@Home setup. And with the opening of The Arena and easing of COVID-19 restrictions, TaskUs is currently working to ensure a successful transition from Work@Home to on-site operations by providing ridiculously fun activities. This is on top of wellness support for teammates to navigate this transition period.


The Arena features bright, collaborative, and modern workspaces, including recreational areas such as a gym, a cafe, meditation rooms, and game rooms for all employees to use. It is strategically located in the north of the city with easy access to public transportation. The site is also close to the Chipichape Mall, where teammates can also have their meals or just simply unwind after work.


“The Arena has a vibrant and colorful personality that represents the mutual culture of TaskUs and Cali,” said Hemant Sehgal, TaskUs Senior Vice President of Operations. “It serves to inspire, energize, and invigorate the minds and bodies of our teammates on and off work, all while having fun. We are excited to welcome our teammates on-site, while supporting them throughout the process, and for more Caleños to discover what makes the TaskUs employee experience truly Ridiculously Good.”

**About TaskUs**


TaskUs is a provider of outsourced digital services and next-generation customer experience to fast growing technology companies, helping its clients represent, protect and grow their brands. Leveraging a cloud-based infrastructure, TaskUs serves clients in the fastest-growing sectors, including social media, e-commerce, gaming, streaming media, food delivery and ridesharing, HiTech, FinTech, and HealthTech. As of March 31, 2022, TaskUs had approximately 45,800 employees across twenty-three locations in 12 countries, including the United States, the Philippines, and India.
