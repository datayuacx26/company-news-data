# TaskUs Holds Grand Career Fair in Bohol on May 24

*TaskUs will be hosting a grand career fair on May 24, 2022, Friday, at Metro Centre Hotel, C.P. Garcia North Ave., Tagbilaran City, from 8 am to 5 pm.*


**TAGBILARAN, Philippines** (May 12, 2022) — TaskUs (Nasdaq: TASK), a provider of outsourced digital services and next-generation customer experience, is on the lookout for more teammates to be part of its growing team in Bohol. TaskUs powers the world’s most innovative and disruptive companies, from startups to tech giants, helping its clients grow their brands.


TaskUs will be hosting a grand career fair on May 24, 2022, Friday, at Metro Centre Hotel, C.P. Garcia North Ave., Tagbilaran City, from 8 am to 5 pm. Interested applicants, including those without any prior BPO experience, can drop by and bring their vaccination cards and one valid ID to enjoy the company’s one-day hiring process. Successful applicants will also enjoy exclusive promos and raffles. Interested candidates may pre-register through this link:[https://tinyurl.com/BoholWithUsGCF](https://tinyurl.com/BoholWithUsGCF)


“We always go where the talent is. It is our mission to provide people with work opportunities and, at the same time, contribute to local economic development,” said Regina Aguila, TaskUs’ Vice President of People. “This way, they no longer need to go far from their homes and their families. ”


Applicants will be required to follow safety protocols in the venue. They will need to answer a health declaration and contact tracing form, wear face masks and undergo temperature checks.


Those who are unable to attend can join online via Lizzy’s Virtual Space, TaskUs’ Virtual Recruitment Hub:[https://tinyurl.com/TaskUsVRH](https://tinyurl.com/TaskUsVRH)


**A Ridiculously Good Workplace**


*Unlike most offices, TaskUs Bohol’s office has bold colors, open spaces, and recreational areas that aim to improve Teammates’ productivity and wellness.*


TaskUs has been operating in Tagbilaran City since December 2019. Its site, Lizzy’s Playground (LPG), is strategically located at JLU Center Bloc, right at the heart of the city’s IT hub.


True to its name, LPG features Ridiculously Awesome amenities like an indoor slide and basketball court, to name a few. Unlike most offices, the site brims with bold colors, open spaces, and recreational areas. It is ready to welcome employees back on-site after more than two years of remote work due to the pandemic.


“Our formula is simple: we heavily invest in our people’s well-being because we firmly believe that happy employees make happy customers,” said Aguila. “Apart from our creative workspaces, we offer industry-leading and inclusive benefits such as HMO coverage extended to dependents including same-sex partners, a retirement savings program, tuition fee reimbursement, and scholarship for our employees’ children. If you are looking for a company where you can excel and feel secure, the right choice is Us.”


TaskUs has been consistently recognized globally by different award-winning bodies because of its people-first culture and unrivaled employee experience. To name a few, the company has been named “[BPO of the Year](https://www.taskus.com/blog/taskus-won-bpo-of-the-year-at-2021-ccw-excellence-awards/) ” at the 2021 CCW Excellence Awards and “[2022 Best Place to Work](https://www.taskus.com/blog/taskus-named-2022-best-place-to-work-by-business-intelligence-group/) ” by the Business Intelligence Group. It has also been recognized by Comparably for[seven consecutive years](https://www.taskus.com/blog/comparably-awards-taskus-ridiculously-good-workplace-culture-for-the-seventh-consecutive-year/) .

**About TaskUs**


TaskUs is a provider of outsourced digital services and next-generation customer experience to innovative and disruptive technology companies, helping its clients represent, protect and grow their brands. Leveraging a cloud-based infrastructure, TaskUs serves clients in the fastest-growing sectors, including social media, e-commerce, gaming, streaming media, food delivery and ridesharing, HiTech, FinTech, and HealthTech. As of March 31, 2022, TaskUs had approximately 45,800 employees across twenty-three locations in 12 countries, including the United States, the Philippines, and India.
