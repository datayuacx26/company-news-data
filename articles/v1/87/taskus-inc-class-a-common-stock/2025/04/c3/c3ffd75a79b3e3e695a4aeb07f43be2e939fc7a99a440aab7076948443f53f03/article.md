# TaskGPT Earns ISO 31700 Certification, Reinforcing Responsible AI Commitment

TaskGPT, our proprietary GenAI platform, has officially been awarded the ISO 31700 – Privacy by Design certification. This globally recognized certification underscores TaskUs’ commitment to embedding privacy, security and trust into AI applications.


TaskGPT powers a range of tools — AssistAI, PromptAI, AuthorAI and CallAI — that help frontline teammates enhance customer service interactions across channels. In addition, we strengthen AI performance, governance and security through partnerships, with best-in-class technology providers like:[NVIDIA to embed NeMo Guardrails](https://www.taskus.com/insights/how-taskus-pioneers-responsible-ai-through-customizable-nvidia-nemo-guardrails/) and[Amazon Bedrock to leverage GenAI foundation models](https://www.taskus.com/press-release/taskus-collaborates-with-amazon-web-services-to-power-taskgpt/) .
