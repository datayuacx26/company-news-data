# TaskUs Recognized as a Major Contender in Everest Group’s Financial Crime and Compliance (FCC) Operations Services PEAK MatrixⓇ Assessment 2025

NEW BRAUNFELS, Texas – September 26, 2025 – TaskUs, Inc. (Nasdaq: TASK), a leading provider of outsourced digital services and next-generation customer experience to the world’s most innovative companies, was named a Major Contender in the Everest Group’s Financial Crime and Compliance (FCC) Operations Services Peak MatrixⓇ Assessment 2025.


The Everest Group report evaluates service providers based on their market impact and vision & capability, providing a comprehensive assessment of the FCC operations services market. TaskUs’s inclusion as a Major Contender underscores its commitment to delivering innovative and effective solutions that address the complex challenges faced by financial institutions globally and is a reflection of TaskUs’ robust capabilities and strong market position in the evolving FCC landscape.


“With financial crime risks becoming increasingly relevant across industries, enterprises beyond the traditional banking sector are seeking FCC partners that can deliver domain-aligned execution, digital fluency, and rapid adaptability,” said Dheeraj Maken, Practice Director, Everest Group. “TaskUs has continued to evolve from its CX-led foundation, expanding its FCC coverage across KYC, AML, fraud, sanctions, and chargebacks. Its investments in verticalized FCC delivery and a maturing tech stack, including platforms for identity verification and fraud containment, have contributed to its positioning as a Major Contender in our Financial Crime and Compliance (FCC) Operations Services PEAK Matrix Assessment 2025.”


“We are proud to be recognized as a Major Contender in Everest Group’s Financial Crime and Compliance (FCC) Operations Services PEAK Matrix,” said Pragya Agarwal, Vice President of Financial Crime and Risk at TaskUs. “This acknowledgement validates our investments in advanced technology, deep regulatory expertise and our dedicated team who are committed to help our clients and their customers fight financial crime and fraud. We remain focused on delivering scalable and innovative solutions that empower financial institutions to navigate regulatory complexities and achieve operational excellence”.


TaskUs was also recognized as a Leader in Everest Group’s Trust and Safety PEAK Matrix AssessmentⓇ 2025.
