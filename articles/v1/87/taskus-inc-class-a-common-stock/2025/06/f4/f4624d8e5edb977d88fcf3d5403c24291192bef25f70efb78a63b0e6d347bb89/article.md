# TaskUs India Ranks in Top 10 Inspiring Workplaces Asia

NEW DELHI, India — June 25, 2025 — TaskUs (Nasdaq: TASK), a leading provider of outsourced digital services and next-generation customer experience for the world’s most innovative companies, has been named No. 5 in the 2025 Top 10 Inspiring Workplaces in Asia by the Inspiring Workplaces Group (IWG).


IWG evaluates companies across six core criteria: culture and purpose, leadership, well-being, inclusion, employee voice, and employee experience. TaskUs India’s placement reflects its strong performance across each of these pillars.


“India is one of TaskUs’ largest regions,” said Sapna Bhambani, SVP of operations and country lead. “Being named an Inspiring Workplace is a testament to the culture we’ve built — one that keeps our more than 12,000 employees happy, engaged, and supported. Our teammates are at the heart of everything we do.”


TaskUs India stood out for its frontline-first benefits, including generous leave policies, private medical insurance with inclusive coverage, career development programs, and wellness support. Other unique benefits include the NextGen Scholarship (up to INR 25,000 for employees’ children) and Hope Beyond Life, which offers health coverage, scholarships, and job opportunities to families of deceased employees.


TaskUs offices in Indore, Gurugram, and Mohali feature on‑site gyms and day care centers, supporting work‑life balance. To foster inclusion, gender‑neutral restrooms are also available at some sites.


Other global regions were also recognized in the 2025 Inspiring Workplaces awards: Colombia ([No. 3 in Latin America](https://www.inspiring-workplaces.com/content/2025-latin-american-inspiring-workplaces-winners-announced) ), Philippines ([No. 4 in Asia](https://www.inspiring-workplaces.com/content/2025-asian-inspiring-workplaces-winners-announced) ), and Greece ([No. 19 in Europe](https://www.inspiring-workplaces.com/content/2025-inspiring-workplaces-winners-announced-in-europe) ).


TaskUs India was also certified as a Great Place To Work® in 2023 and 2025.


**About TaskUs**


TaskUs is a leading provider of outsourced digital services and next-generation customer experience to the world’s most innovative companies, helping its clients represent, protect and grow their brands. Leveraging a cloud-based infrastructure, TaskUs serves clients in the fast-growing sectors, including social media, e-commerce, gaming, streaming media, food delivery and ride-sharing, technology, financial services and healthcare. As of March 31, 2025, TaskUs had a worldwide headcount of approximately 61,400 people across 28 locations in 12 countries, including India, the United States, and the Philippines.
