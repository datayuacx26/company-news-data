Badger Technologies’ AI-powered robots scan millions of products daily to help retailers keep shelves stocked and stores safe. With such volume comes both challenge and opportunity — a chance to improve how models are trained, making them faster, smarter and more accurate.


To scale fast and accurately, Badger turned to TaskUs for real-time data annotation to further sharpen the system’s hazard detection and inventory management capabilities.


In this case study, learn how TaskUs helped Badger achieve:


- Greater efficiency with flexible staffing, tooling and round-⁠the-⁠clock operations
- Faster, higher-quality outputs through optimized workflows
- Improved safety and better sales decisions
