# From Days to Seconds: Scaling HR Operations with Agentic AI

Over the last 10 years, many organizations have centralized[departments like HR](https://www.deloitte.com/nl/en/Industries/consumer/perspectives/deloitte-global-business-services-survey-2025.html) into shared service hubs to unify core operations. Bringing payroll, benefits and leave management under one roof, for example, drove initial efficiency gains. But standardizing a process doesn’t automatically equate to speed. HR teams can still get bogged down by administrative tasks like: manually reading forms, cross-checking databases and copying information from one system to another.


## THE CHALLENGE


When global scale brings local complexities to HR processes


For a global digital services company operating in more than 10 countries, HR shared services successfully supported a growing international footprint. But with every market bringing its own compliance rules, local execution was a challenge — specifically, inconsistent medical leave processing.


In one region, a digital doctor’s note suffices; in another, physical stamps and multiple government portals are mandatory. HR specialists would spend a good part of their days managing a fragmented, manual patchwork of steps.


Colombia was the most difficult area to handle. There, medical leave involves a complex web of requirements between the employer and private insurers. The central HR team faced three operational strains:


- **Bloated costs:** The company had to rely on an external vendor just to read and validate incoming medical forms.
- **Drained resources:** It took an HR specialist an average of 36 minutes per case to verify doctor credentials, match employee details and ensure diagnosis codes fit insurer guidelines.
- **Manual bottlenecks:** Teams had to hand review sensitive medical documents and manually plot leaves into the scheduling system.


The long administrative process kept employees waiting five to nine business days for their leave approval. The delay exacerbated the stress of having to deal with an unexpected health issue.


## THE SOLUTION


Agentic AI reduces the administrative burden


To both clear the backlog and free HR teams to focus on complex cases that require higher empathy, the company deployed[agentic AI](https://www.taskus.com/services/agentic-ai/) for the repetitive data entry and validation work.


Unlike simple automation or GenAI tools that only summarize text or wait for step-by-step instructions, AI agents act independently. They understand the end goal, follow multi-step workflows and navigate disparate software systems to seamlessly complete tasks from start to finish.


Integrating agentic capabilities into its internal chatbot, achieved even more:


- **Visual recognition:** The moment an employee uploads a medical certificate, the AI uses optical character recognition (OCR) to read the document.
- **Multi-step validation:** Agentic AI pulls out the key dates and medical codes, and cross-references everything against internal company records. If something is missing, the chatbot asks the employee to resubmit.
- **Automated scheduling:** For standard leave types, the AI agent approves the request instantly and automatically plots the paid days into the scheduling platform.


The technology also knows when to[handover to people](https://www.taskus.com/services/customer-experience/) . If a document is blurry or if there’s an anomaly, the AI packages the files and routes them to an HR specialist to handle.


## Results


Freeing HR from data entry to deliver genuine employee care


**Agentic AI reduced a nine-day waiting period to near real-time approval.** Today, **54% of all medical leaves are fully approved and processed by the system** . AI agents handle the downstream scheduling process too, **automatically plotting 72% of approved leaves directly into the workforce management system.**


This integration elevated the role of HR professionals. **Specialists got hours in their days back to focus on rare errors and complex cases** where[human judgment](https://www.taskus.com/insights/why-the-future-of-work-is-ai-amplified/) and empathy are irreplaceable.


Financially, **the company cut down its reliance on outside processing vendors** , turning a recurring expense into a permanent internal capability.


Ultimately, agentic AI transformed the employee experience. By getting rid of long processing and wait times, the company strengthened its commitment to taking care of its people.
