# How People Really Feel About Autonomous Vehicles

No industry scales on innovation alone. Success ultimately depends on whether people will use the product. Autonomous vehicles (AVs) have reached this point.


Multiple robotaxi companies are already picking up passengers in several U.S. cities, but most people haven’t tried one yet. We surveyed over 1,000 people to find out what makes them curious, why they might hold back and when they plan to take their very first ride.
