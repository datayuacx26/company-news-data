# The Agentic Enterprise: How to Set AI Agents up for Success

AI can already write, summarize and answer questions. The next challenge is putting those capabilities to work across real business processes. That’s where agentic AI comes in.


Instead of just responding to prompts, these systems pursue objectives, coordinate multiple steps and interact with business applications to carry tasks through to completion.


Bringing agentic AI into the enterprise raises three fundamental questions: Which work belongs to people? Which belongs to software? How should the two operate together?


Our white paper serves as a guide for setting up AI agents that work and deliver better results.


Inside, you’ll find:


- Top use cases across industries like financial services, healthcare, retail and more
- What makes agentic AI different from earlier AI applications
- Common deployment barriers and how to fix them
