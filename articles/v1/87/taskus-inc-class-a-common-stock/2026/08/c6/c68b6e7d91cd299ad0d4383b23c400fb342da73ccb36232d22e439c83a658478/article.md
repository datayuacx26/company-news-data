# Strengthening the Hybrid Engine Powering Autonomous Systems

Autonomous technology is already out in public spaces, from self-driving cars to sidewalk delivery robots. What keeps these machines moving is a combination of artificial and human intelligence.


Perception systems power routine navigation. Teleoperators step in the moment conditions become unpredictable. This partnership, known as human-AI complementarity, makes safety and scale possible.


This white paper explores how to optimize this joint system:


- Why AI struggles with real-world context, and how human intuition bridges the gap
- Strategies to keep remote operators sharp and focused during critical safety incidents
- Why supporting teleoperators today generates clean, high-fidelity data to train smarter, more independent systems for tomorrow
