# TaskUs calls for ridiculously good talent in Mohali at September 25 hiring event

**MOHALI, India** (September 22, 2021) – TaskUs (NASDAQ: TASK), a leading tech-enabled business services company delivering outsourced digital services and next-generation customer experience to innovative and disruptive companies, announces its arrival in the city of Mohali, India.


To celebrate the launch of the new site, TaskUs will be hosting an in-person hiring event, ***Mohali, Finally!*** at 11:00 AM and at 4:00 PM on Saturday, September 25, 2021, at Radisson Chandigarh Zirakpur in Patiala Road, Village Nabha, Zirakpur.


Anchor Naina Chandigarh will host the recruitment event, with Prachand The Band providing the entertainment.


Initial interviews will be conducted during the event, which will be open to hundreds of applicants. To ensure health and safety measures are observed, interested candidates must[register](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeN5jFmJm-cCZSwvAQJ2HCqgWf0x8I6sc0aRh3-6SsdMZA1yA/viewform) to receive an invitation.


Health protocols, such as wearing face masks and social distancing, will be strictly enforced. Only a maximum of 400 invitees will be accommodated per session. No walk-ins will be allowed.


There will also be information sessions during the event, as well as online sessions in the following weeks, that will help candidates understand TaskUs’s culture, available roles, and hiring process.


**Why Mohali, India?**


Mohali is home to esteemed universities and research institutions, producing approximately 70,000 graduates annually.


“TaskUs believes that Mohali is the next go-to IT/ITeS destination in the state of Punjab to provide best-in-class tech-enabled services,” said Sapna Bhambani, TaskUs Vice President of Operations for India. “Apart from its strategic location, we are excited to hone Mohali’s rising talent coming from both new graduates and experienced professionals.”


Successful applicants will begin their journey at TaskUs in the safety and comfort of their homes while the company builds its state-of-the-art workplace.


*Similar to the TaskUs Rangreza office in Indore (above), the Mohali site, which will be located at the heart of Sahibzada Ajit Singh Nagar, will feature world-class workspaces and recreational amenities.*


Like other TaskUs sites globally, the upcoming sports-themed Mohali office will feature extraordinary recreation areas, eye-catching architectural features, places for mindfulness and relaxation, collaborative spaces, and fun zones.


TaskUs is the first outsourcing company in India operating out of non-metro cities to provide in-house gym, zen room, daycare, mother care room, and medical facilities.


“As a people-first company, we want to provide equal career opportunities and employee benefits that are beyond the standard. We take pride in our ridiculously good employee experience, allowing our team to be their best in and outside of work,” said Bhambani.


TaskUs India teammates enjoy unique employee benefits. Apart from its competitive salary packages, the company also provides health care benefits that include private medical and life insurance plans on top of government-mandated benefits. It also allows employees to encash accumulated leaves at the end of the year.


In light of the current health situation, TaskUs covers COVID-19 testing costs and provides a 14-day paid leave for employees who test positive for the virus. The company has also covered COVID-19 vaccination for all employees and their dependents.


*Hundreds of employees have been inoculated through the TaskUs vaccination drive.*


One of TaskUs’s differentiators is its Wellness and Resiliency team, dedicated to offering free programs to support employees’ mental health. This benefit also extends to family members through the company’s Employee Assistance Program (EAP).


The company also offers ample career and professional growth opportunities. For instance, TaskUs has a Tuition Fee Reimbursement Program that covers a portion of the cost for furthering education toward an Associate, Bachelor, Master, or Doctoral degree, or approved certification program.


*Employees’ professional and personal development is a key focus area for TaskUs. This is why the company invests in upskilling programs and career planning.*


Meanwhile, TaskUs is a staunch advocate for diversity and inclusion. In India, TaskUs has a 60:40 male-female ratio and offers Employee Resource Groups (ERGs) that provide a safe space for minority or underserved identity groups and ally supporters.


The company is also committed to ensuring employees balance work and personal life by implementing policies including meeting-free Fridays, no-chat weekends, and a requirement of a truly unplugged vacation.


TaskUs has over 31,500 teammates around the world with offices across India, the U.S., the Philippines, Taiwan, Mexico, Greece, Ireland, and Colombia. The company currently has operations in three locations in India: Indore, Gurugram, and Mohali.
