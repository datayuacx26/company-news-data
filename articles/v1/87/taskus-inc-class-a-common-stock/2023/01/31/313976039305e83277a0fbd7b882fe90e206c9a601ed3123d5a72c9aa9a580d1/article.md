# TaskUs’ AI Services Wins at 2023 BIG Innovation Awards

**NEW BRAUNFELS, Texas** (January 11, 2023) –TaskUs (Nasdaq: TASK), a global provider of digital outsourcing services and next-generation customer experience to fast-growing technology companies, has been named a winner in the[2023 BIG Innovation Awards](https://www.bintelligence.com/big-innovation-awards) by the Business Intelligence Group for its AI Services offering.


Committed to and globally known for delivering Ridiculously Good outsourcing results and innovation, TaskUs provides best-in-class AI services to the global companies it works with. Under its AI Services offering, TaskUs executes large-scale data collection, annotation, and evaluation services to train and improve the technology today and in the future, ensuring its efficiency, inclusivity, and overall success. The company’s global experts on human-driven technology processes and solutions provide clients with a deep understanding of the best practices to scale data-training programs, making way for effective and efficient AI and ML.


“Innovation is ‘business critical’ in today’s society,” said Maria Jimenez, chief operating officer of the Business Intelligence Group. “We are thrilled to be honoring TaskUs as they are leading by example and improving the lives of so many.”


“We are immensely grateful that BIG Innovation Awards sees our efforts to continuously help build and develop ethical AI,” said Matthew Skyrm, Vice President of AI Services at TaskUs. “The critical role of quality data to ensure the efficiency, accuracy, and inclusivity of AI models can no longer be denied. This is why TaskUs will always prioritize providing excellent AI Services as part of our contribution to society.”


Organizations from across the globe submitted their recent innovations for consideration in the BIG Innovation Awards. Nominations were then judged by a select group of business leaders and executives who volunteer their time and expertise to score submissions.

**About TaskUs**


TaskUs is a provider of outsourced digital services and next-generation customer experience to fast-growing technology companies, helping its clients represent, protect and grow their brands. Leveraging a cloud-based infrastructure, TaskUs serves clients in the fastest-growing sectors, including social media, e-commerce, gaming, streaming media, food delivery and ridesharing, HiTech, FinTech, and HealthTech. As of September 30, 2022, TaskUs had approximately 48,700 employees across twenty-eight locations in 14 countries, including the United States, the Philippines, and India.


**About Business Intelligence Group**


The[Business Intelligence Group](http://www.bintelligence.com/what-we-do/) was founded with the mission of recognizing true talent and superior performance in the business world. Unlike other[industry award programs](http://www.bintelligence.com/awards-deadlines/) , these programs are judged by business executives having experience and knowledge. The organization’s proprietary and unique scoring system selectively measures performance across multiple business domains and then rewards those companies whose achievements stand above those of their peers.
