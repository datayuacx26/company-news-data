## Why are manual contract processes slowing down your sales cycle?


The modern sales stack—including CRMs, CPQs, cloud storage, conferencing and productivity tools, and word processors—addresses discrete tasks. But it lacks the connective tissue at the core of every deal: the contract. According to a Docusign and Deloitte survey,[45% of business leaders](https://www.docusign.com/resources/whitepapers/deloitte-unlocking-the-value-of-agreement-management) input data manually into contracts when that data already exists in other systems. Here’s what it costs sellers every day:


-


**Time, effort, and frustration** :[Around 70% of a seller's time opens in a new tab](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/15-sales-statistics/) goes toward non-selling activities. A seller might waste hours digging through shared folders for an up-to-date template for their deal requirements, manually inputting data from their CRM, or fixing formatting issues before sending.


-


**Risk, errors, and revenue** : Manual processes introduce significant risk. Manually inputting data into contracts when that data already lives in other systems can lead to errors in custom quotes and contracts, misplaced agreements, and incomplete data in the system of record. What may seem like small contract inefficiencies can end up significantly costing companies.


-


**Visibility, control, and relationships** : Sales, legal, and finance teams often work in silos, making it harder to strengthen customer relationships post-sale. After an agreement is signed, it’s often manually uploaded to a CRM and other repositories, but without an efficient system in place, the final version can be difficult to locate later and gather insights to help with renegotiations or expansions.
