# Accelerate Talent Velocity with IAM for HR

The moment a candidate accepts a job offer should be one of celebration. But for most HR teams, it’s the starting gun for a manual paperwork marathon.


In fact, in a recent Docusign survey of HR leaders, 60% of respondents said at least 10 documents need to be signed during onboarding alone. Add in offer letters, promotion docs, policy updates, and separation agreements, and you see that HR teams aren't just managing people—they are managing a mountain of highly variable, high-stakes paperwork.


When these agreements are scattered across emails, shared drives, and disconnected systems, the business slows down. Seventy-four percent of respondents said technical teams have missed project milestones due to delays in onboarding. Not to mention the headaches your new hires experience dealing with disjointed files that they can’t easily access, and general fatigue from form fills.


That’s why we’re building Docusign Intelligent Agreement Management (IAM) for HR: to help you create, commit to, and manage the entire employee lifecycle. IAM for HR utilizes AI-powered automation to return time to HR teams, transforming static agreements into dynamic assets that facilitate faster hiring and a superior employee experience.


## The hidden friction in the employee lifecycle


To accelerate talent velocity, we first have to address the agreement debt holding HR teams back. We’ve heard first-hand from HR leaders and analysts that four specific challenges are stalling growth:


-


**Inconsistent templates:** Without a central playbook, teams end up with dozens of slightly different templates for different roles and regions. One legal update triggers a manual chase across hundreds of files.


-


**The paperwork scavenger hunt:** Candidates often receive documents in fragments—one from the applicant tracking system (ATS), one from a background check, and another from IT. This disjointed experience leads to "ghosting" and slower conversion.


-


**Inaccessible data:** Critical data, like non-competes or specialized compensation, is often locked in PDFs. Finding out "who has a clawback clause?" shouldn't require weeks of manual digging. When HR teams are unable to surface this vital information when they need it most, it leads to policy compliance gaps.


-


**Identity risks:** Traditional digital signatures can leave organizations vulnerable to candidate fraud. Checking an ID on Day 1 is too late if the person who interviewed isn't the one who signed the offer.


## Introducing Docusign IAM for HR


IAM for HR helps pull recruiters and HR professionals out from beneath a pile of paperwork by automating tasks and working with your existing tools, so that they can focus on their people. Here’s how IAM helps HR teams prioritize this:

No more hunting through folders.[Agreement Manager](https://www.docusign.com/products/platform/agreement-manager) for HR helps teams create a single, searchable repository for every agreement an employee signs.


-


**Search by content:** Use AI to instantly surface specific clauses or terms across your entire workforce.


-


**Create an Employee Digital File:** Merge personal and work email profiles into a single view so you can see an employee’s complete hire-to-retire agreement history.

**People Hub:** View all your employees, automate workflows, bundle forms like NDAs and tax forms, and store them in Agreement Manager via a secure link.


### 2. Bidirectional extensions for your core HR systems


Data shouldn’t live in a silo. Our extension apps create a seamless loop with the tools you already use. You can pull candidate data directly into agreements to eliminate manual entry errors. And once signed, documents flow automatically back into the worker profile in your HCM or ATS. No manual uploads, no missing files.


### 3. On-demand I-9 and identity verification


We don’t treat trust like a day 1 checkbox. IAM for HR integrates identity verification (IDV) and on-demand I-9 verification directly into the workflow with live, on-demand verifiers.


-


**Verify with confidence:** Ensure the person signing the offer is the person you interviewed.


-


**Trust-first innovation:** Securely collect and store ID images in one place, reducing the need for third-party PEO storage or insecure email attachments.


### 4. Bulk send agreements instantly


Sending agreements for review across channels is easier than ever. Create workflows that allow you to instantly send to multiple parties to streamline reviews and keep agreements moving forward.
