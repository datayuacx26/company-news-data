# Docusign and Anthropic: Bringing Intelligent Contract Workflows to Cowork

Agreement management is essential to how businesses operate, yet much of the process still relies on manual, repetitive work spread across systems and teams. Today, organizations are turning to agentic AI that not only answers questions but takes action—automating agreement workflows that once required hours of human effort.


We’re excited to announce Docusign’s partnership with Anthropic to bring[Intelligent Agreement Management (IAM)](https://www.docusign.com/intelligent-agreement-management) into Claude Cowork. By connecting to[Docusign in Cowork opens in a new tab](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-plugins-across-enterprise) , businesses can securely create, review, send, and manage agreements from start to finish — all through natural language prompts in Claude.


With this connector, teams can move directly from a prompt to a workflow. AI goes beyond summaries and suggestions to trigger real time actions across legal, sales, procurement, HR, and finance.


Here are just a few examples of what teams can accomplish:


-


Create a contract from a Docusign-provided Master Service Agreement template, populate business details, and route it to Legal


-


Send a customer intake packet and receive notification when identity verification is complete


-


Review AI-suggested redlines to a service contract and initiate a vendor review aligned to company policy


-


Search for customer contracts expiring in the next 90 days that include a price increase clause


-


Find all active contracts containing a “data protection” clause and export a summary report


Each action is backed by Docusign’s IAM platform, ensuring workflows follow defined rules, permissions, and compliance requirements.


## Manage agreements where work happens


Teams increasingly embrace AI tools to plan, analyze, and coordinate work. But agreement execution happens outside of AI tools that organizations are using — forcing people to switch tools or manually hand off next steps.


By integrating Docusign directly into Cowork, teams can run agreement workflows without leaving their AI workspace. The result is faster execution, fewer handoffs, and less operational friction.


## Built for enterprise trust


The Docusign connector for Cowork is built on the **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** and uses Docusign’s trusted security and access controls. Customers must be licensed and authenticated, access is permission-based, and agreement data remains private and under the user’s control.


## Availability


The Docusign MCP connector is available in **beta today** through Anthropic’s Connectors Directory for Docusign customers. To learn more about how your team can use the Docusign MCP Connector to build tailored integrations and automate complex agreement workflows, visit our[detailed developer guide](https://www.docusign.com/blog/developers/claude-docusign-mcp-connector-guide) ."
