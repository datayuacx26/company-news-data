# Guardian Pharmacies Host Regional Clinical Symposiums


Two Guardian pharmacies hosted continuing education symposiums in August, providing community staff with practical training and professional development opportunities focused on improving resident care and operational excellence.


**15th Annual Southeast Regional Pharmacy Healthcare Symposium** Hosted by Guardian Pharmacy of South Carolina, the 15th Annual Southeast Regional Pharmacy Healthcare Symposium welcomed more than 95 participants from across the region to Charleston, South Carolina. With the theme *Leadership: It Starts at the Top* , the full-day event featured continuing education sessions on leadership values, personal leadership experiences, continuous glucose monitoring, and age-friendly pharmacy practices.


**Saliba’s CEU Symposium**
Saliba’s Pharmacy hosted its Continuing Education Symposium in Phoenix, Arizona, drawing dozens of participants. The program focused on critical topics including geriatric medication safety, fall prevention, leadership and employee retention, and survey readiness.


John Saliba speaks to the audience during the clinical symposium.


**Commitment to Education and Quality Care**
Both symposiums underscore Guardian’s commitment to supporting senior care communities through education, innovation, and collaboration. By equipping community staff with knowledge and strategies to improve safety and strengthen leadership, Guardian continues to advance its mission of enhancing resident care and empowering care teams.
