# Guardian Pharmacy Joins National Leaders at The Arc’s Annual Convention


Members of Guardian Pharmacy Services recently attended The Arc’s National Convention in Charlotte, North Carolina, joining leaders and advocates from across the country who share a commitment to supporting individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities (I/DD).


Guardian has long supported The Arc of the United States and its mission to advance inclusion, independence, and community participation for people with I/DD. The annual convention brings together providers, families, and partners to discuss policy, share best practices, and strengthen resources for the I/DD community.


**Supporting the I/DD Community Nationwide**


Guardian partners with I/DD providers across the country and remains committed to supporting organizations that promote inclusion, independence, and person-centered care. Events like The Arc’s National Convention create opportunities for collaboration, shared learning, and continued progress in strengthening resources for individuals with I/DD.


“This convention brings together so many people who are deeply committed to the I/DD community,” said Robert Moser, Director of Sales and Marketing at Guardian Pharmacy of Michigan. “It’s a valuable opportunity to connect, learn from others, and better understand how we can continue supporting the providers we work with every day.”
