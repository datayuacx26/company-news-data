# Guardian Pharmacy Joins 2025 Walk to End Alzheimer’s in Atlanta


Last month, members of Guardian Pharmacy Services and Guardian Pharmacy of Atlanta gathered to volunteer at the[2025 Walk to End Alzheimer’s](https://act.alz.org/site/TR?pg=entry&fr_id=18521) in Atlanta’s Piedmont Park.


Guardian Pharmacy is a proud sponsor of the Walk to End Alzheimer’s, and this marks our second year participating in the event.


Our teams raised $5,000, contributing to more than $700,000 raised in total to support the fight against Alzheimer’s disease. This funding will help advance critical initiatives, including care and support programs for families and groundbreaking research aimed at finding a cure.


With November being Alzheimer’s Awareness Month, we remain committed to supporting the fight against Alzheimer’s every step of the way. Together, we can end Alzheimer’s.
