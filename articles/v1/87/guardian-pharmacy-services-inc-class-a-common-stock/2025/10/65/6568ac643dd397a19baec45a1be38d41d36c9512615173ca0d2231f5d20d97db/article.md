# Guardian Pharmacy of Utah Hosts Open House at New Salt Lake City Location


On September 18th, Guardian Pharmacy of Utah hosted an open house event to celebrate the opening of its new West Valley facility in Salt Lake City. The event brought together colleagues, clients, and local business leaders for a pharmacy tour and networking opportunities.


Distinguished guests included West Valley City Mayor Karen Lang, as well as representatives from ChamberWest and the Utah Assisted Living Association, who spoke during the program and participated in a ribbon-cutting ceremony.


The pharmacy is led by Presidents Reece Christensen and Corey Smith, who welcomed attendees and highlighted Guardian’s continued commitment to providing specialized long-term care pharmacy services to the greater Salt Lake area.
