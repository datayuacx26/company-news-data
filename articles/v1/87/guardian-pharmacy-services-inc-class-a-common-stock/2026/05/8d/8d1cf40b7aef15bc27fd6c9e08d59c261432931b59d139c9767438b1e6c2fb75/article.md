# Guardian Pharmacy Services Participates in NatCon 2026 Conference


Guardian Pharmacy Services participated in NatCon, the largest national conference focused on mental health and substance use, hosted by National Council for Mental Wellbeing.


The three-day event was held April 27-29, 2026, at the Colorado Convention Center in Denver, bringing together nearly 6,000 professionals from across the country.


**Industry Engagement and Education**


The conference featured more than 500 speakers and sessions covering topics including crisis response, rural healthcare access, and workforce challenges. Attendees had the opportunity to earn national and state-specific continuing education and continuing medical education credits.


Guardian representatives from multiple pharmacies attended and exhibited at the event, including Michelle Mincks of Guardian Pharmacy of Missouri, Matthew Stanley of Guardian Pharmacy of South Carolina, and Emily Fera of Guardian Pharmacy of Denver.


**Focus on Behavioral Health Trends**


Sessions throughout the conference highlighted emerging trends in behavioral health, including the growing role of artificial intelligence in mental health care delivery.


Guardian Pharmacy Services’ participation reflects its ongoing involvement in industry discussions focused on improving care delivery for residential and inpatient behavioral health organizations.
