# Guardian Pharmacy Services Announces Senior Leadership Promotions to Support Continued Growth and Operational Alignment


Guardian Pharmacy Services announced a series of senior leadership promotions to its corporate and regional leadership teams designed to strengthen operational alignment, support the company’s continued growth, and further enhance service consistency across its national network of pharmacies.


Guardian promoted several experienced corporate leaders to senior vice president (SVP) roles. Corporate appointments include:


-


- Will Mudd, CPA, SVP, Finance
- Doug Towns, SVP, General Counsel & Senior Compliance Officer
- David Martin, SVP, Technology, Data & Senior Security Officer
- Jeff Tuch, CPA, SVP, Purchasing & Pricing Analytics
- Chris Crisafulli, SVP, Payor & Industry Strategy
- Rich Eakins, SVP, Sales, Account Management & Marketing


In addition, Guardian expanded its regional leadership team through the promotion of several tenured pharmacy presidents to SVP roles. Those promoted include:


-


- David Brown, SVP, Central Region
- John Saliba, SVP, West Region
- Khristy McClelland, SVP, Southeast Region
- Michael Counts, SVP, Midwest Region
- Marybeth Terry, SVP, East Region
- Tanner Corsini, SVP, South Central Region
- Rocky Anderson, SVP, North Central Region
- Bob Weir, SVP, Operations & Regulatory Support


Guardian also promoted several pharmacy presidents to regional pharmacy president (RPP) positions. New RPPs include:


-


- Ron Belville, RPP, California
- Matt Hopp, RPP, Georgia
- Tom Mullaney, RPP, Ohio
- Nathan Stauffer, RPP, Michigan
- Mike Flint, RPP, Wisconsin


Guardian emphasized its ongoing commitment to local pharmacy leadership, with each pharmacy location continuing to operate under the leadership of the local president and team to best serve the unique needs of the customers in their market.


“The promotions reflect the talent, strength and experience of our leadership team, as well as our continued investment in leadership development and organizational scalability,” said Fred Burke, president & CEO. “Our locally operated pharmacy model, supported by expanded leadership at the corporate and regional levels, positions us well to continue delivering consistent, high-quality service to residents and our facility partners across our expanding national footprint.”
