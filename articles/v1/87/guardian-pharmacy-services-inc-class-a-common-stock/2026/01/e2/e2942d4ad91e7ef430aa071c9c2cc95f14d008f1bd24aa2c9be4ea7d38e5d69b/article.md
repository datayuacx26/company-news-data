# Right Dose Pharmacy Opens New Ankeny, Iowa Headquarters to Meet Growing Demand for Long-Term Care Pharmacy Services


Right Dose Pharmacy has opened the doors to its new 17,000-square-foot headquarters in Ankeny, Iowa. The expanded facility marks a major milestone in the pharmacy’s continued growth and strengthens its ability to serve residents in assisted living, skilled nursing and behavioral health communities, as well as individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities across Central Iowa.


“This new headquarters is a reflection of how far Right Dose has come and where we’re headed next,” said Rockford Anderson, president of Right Dose Pharmacy. “As we’ve grown, it became clear we needed a space that could keep pace with our team, our technology and the level of care our partners rely on. This facility allows us to work more efficiently today while positioning us to better serve our communities for years to come.”


Designed with the team in mind, the new facility features a modern, flexible layout with expanded square footage and dedicated quiet areas that support staff productivity and workflow efficiency. In addition to supporting staff, the headquarters strengthens Right Dose’s longstanding role as a preceptor site for sixth-year Pharm.D. candidates from Drake University’s College of Pharmacy & Health Sciences, providing pharmacy students with an exceptional environment for advanced clinical learning.


In addition to the Ankeny location, Right Dose operates pharmacies in Cedar Rapids and Storm Lake, serving residents in long-term care communities across the state.
