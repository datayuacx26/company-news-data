# Guardian to Host 2026 Annual Clinical Symposium


Registration is now open for Guardian’s Annual Clinical Symposium, a two-day virtual continuing education event taking place May 7–8, 2026.


The symposium offers up to six hours of continuing education (CEUs) for pharmacists, nurses, and licensed nursing home administrators. Session topics include age-friendly care, diabetes management, safe medication handling, psychotropic medication use, and other issues impacting older adults and the senior care industry.
