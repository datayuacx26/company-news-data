# Guardian Pharmacy of Jacksonville Promotes Laura Polinsky to President


[Guardian Pharmacy of Jacksonville](https://guardianpharmacy.com/florida-jacksonville/) has promoted Laura Polinsky, Pharm.D. to pharmacy president. Polinsky will oversee all aspects of daily operations, cultivate customer relationships, and drive the pharmacy’s overall growth. She succeeds Khristy McClelland, R.Ph., who was promoted to senior vice president of Guardian Pharmacy Services’ Southeast region.


Polinsky joined Guardian Pharmacy of Jacksonville in 2011 as director of pharmacy operations, bringing valuable leadership experience from a national retail pharmacy organization. Over the past 15 years, she has played a critical role in the expansion and evolution of the pharmacy. This includes leading major automation and workflow initiatives, managing a facility relocation, and strengthening partnerships with communities across the First Coast. With her deep industry expertise and proven leadership, Polinsky is well-positioned to lead Guardian Pharmacy of Jacksonville into its next chapter of growth.


Founded in 2005, Guardian Pharmacy of Jacksonville provides specialized LTC pharmacy services to senior living and skilled nursing communities, as well as providers who serve individuals with mental and behavioral health disorders and intellectual and developmental disabilities throughout North Florida.
