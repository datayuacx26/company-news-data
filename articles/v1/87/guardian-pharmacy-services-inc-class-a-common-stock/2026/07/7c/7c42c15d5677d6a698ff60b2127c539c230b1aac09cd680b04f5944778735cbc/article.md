# Why More Families Are Asking: “Do You Use Guardian Pharmacy?”

## Why More Families Are Asking: “Do You Use Guardian Pharmacy?”


### Why your senior living community's pharmacy partner matters more than you may realize


Choosing an assisted living community is one of the most important decisions a family will make. Most people focus on the things they can easily see during a tour—cleanliness, dining, activities, apartments, and the friendliness of the staff.
But increasingly, families are asking another important question:


**“Which pharmacy do you use?”**


It’s a question that reflects a growing awareness of the important role medication management plays in safety, quality of care, and the day-to-day experience of residents and caregivers in senior living settings.


#### **Why the Pharmacy Matters**


Unlike retail or mail-order pharmacies, long-term care (LTC) pharmacies specialize in serving assisted living communities, working directly with caregivers, nurses, prescribers, and families to coordinate medication management. Their role extends far beyond simply filling prescriptions.


An LTC pharmacy connects in real time with the community’s electronic medication administration record (eMAR). This connectivity improves communication, reduces manual processes, and gives the pharmacist a 360º view of each resident’s medication regimen. In addition, an experienced LTC pharmacy helps communities by:


- Coordinating medication changes quickly
- Delivering medications directly to the community, including 24/7 emergency deliveries and pharmacist access
- Providing easy-to-use compliance packaging that simplifies medication administration and reduces errors
- Offering consultant pharmacist clinical expertise and oversight


When these services work together, residents benefit from safer, more coordinated care while caregivers spend less time resolving pharmacy issues and more time caring for residents.

#### **Why Communities Choose a Preferred Pharmacy Partner**


Most assisted living communities contract with a single LTC pharmacy partner to promote safer, more consistent pharmacy services. Having one “in-house” pharmacy that works closely with the community helps standardize medication management processes, streamline communication, and reduce unnecessary complexity for caregivers.


In some communities, however, residents may choose to continue using an outside retail or mail-order pharmacy rather than transitioning to the community’s preferred LTC pharmacy partner. When medications are supplied by multiple pharmacies, the community’s medication management process becomes more complex. Different packaging and labeling, separate communication channels, and the need to coordinate medication orders across multiple pharmacies increase the risk of delays, missed orders, and medication administration errors while adding to staff workload.


Using the community’s preferred LTC pharmacy partner helps mitigate these risks and supports safer medication management throughout the community.


#### **Ask the Question**


Families are asking more informed questions than ever before, and the pharmacy serving an assisted living community is an important part of the answer. Whether you’re touring a community or your loved one already lives in assisted living, ask:


**“Which pharmacy do you use?”**


If the community partners with Guardian Pharmacy, ask how the pharmacy supports residents and the care team. If it doesn’t—or residents commonly use outside retail or mail-order pharmacies—ask how medication changes are communicated, how after-hours or urgent needs are handled, how medication orders are reviewed and entered into the community’s eMAR, and how staff manage medications that arrive in different packaging and labeling formats.


The answers can provide valuable insight into how medications are managed and help families become stronger advocates for their loved ones’ care.
