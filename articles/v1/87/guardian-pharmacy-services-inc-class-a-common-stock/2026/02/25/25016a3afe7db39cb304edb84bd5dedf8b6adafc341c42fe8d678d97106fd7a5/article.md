# Guardian Pharmacy Services Hosts Annual Sales & Account Management Meeting in Atlanta


Guardian Pharmacy Services brought together nearly 300 employees from across the country in Atlanta last week for its annual Sales & Account Management Meeting. The event aligned sales, account management, and pharmacy leadership teams around key priorities for the year ahead.


Attendees included pharmacy presidents, sales and account management teams, marketing leaders, and consultant pharmacists representing more than 50 Guardian pharmacies nationwide.


Under this year’s theme, *Racing Towards Success,* sessions focused on sharing best practices, coordination and consistency across teams, enhancing service delivery, and improving the overall customer experience.


The meeting reaffirmed Guardian’s ongoing commitment to serving communities and residents in senior living and other long-term care settings.
