# Guardian Pharmacy Services Names 2025 Pharmacies of the Year


Guardian Pharmacy Services recognized two pharmacies with its 2025 Pharmacy of the Year award, marking the first time in the company’s history that the honor has been awarded to multiple locations.


The award was presented during Guardian’s annual Pharmacy Management Meeting, held April 7–9 in Frisco, Texas. The meeting brings together pharmacy leadership from across the organization to align on priorities and recognize performance.


**Pharmacy of the Year Recognition**


Southern Pharmacy Service’s Pink Hill location and Guardian Pharmacy of Jacksonville were recognized for high performance across key business metrics, including growth. The award reflects operational excellence and consistent performance across core areas of the business.


Pharmacy president Olivia Miller and regional president Mary Beth Terry represented Pink Hill at the event. Jacksonville was represented by pharmacy president Laura Polinsky and regional president Khristy McClelland. Both teams accepted the award alongside members of Guardian Pharmacy Services’ executive leadership team.


**Continued Focus on Performance and Growth**


The Pharmacy of the Year award is presented annually to recognize top-performing pharmacies across the Guardian network.


[Oklahoma’s Boomer Solutions received the award in 2024 .](https://guardianpharmacy.com/blog/2025/03/24/oklahoma-named-guardian-pharmacy-of-the-year-at-annual-pharmacy-management-meeting/)


Guardian Pharmacy Services is one of the nation’s largest LTC pharmacy services companies with 61 locations nationwide, 54 of which are full-service LTC pharmacies, serving over 8,400 communities and organizations in 38 states.
