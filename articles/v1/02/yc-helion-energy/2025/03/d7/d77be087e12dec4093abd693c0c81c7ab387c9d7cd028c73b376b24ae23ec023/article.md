# The hardware and people enabling a better world

When candidates ask about our culture, I always start with our mission, values, and the impact we’re working toward. But what really defines Helion? Two things: the hardware and the people. The moment you walk into our facilities, you see what makes Helion different. Hardware is everywhere – capacitors, testing stations, sci-fi-like machines, the remains of Trenta and, of course, Polaris. And then there’s the team: builders, problem-solvers, and people who thrive on execution and speed. That’s why hiring the right people is everything - our culture is a direct result of who we are and how we work together to build great hardware.


## **The work**


Building great hardware isn’t easy. Building great hardware for commercial fusion power plants is even harder. At Helion, the work is intense, and the stakes are high. It’s stepping into a place where the future is being built in real-time. You don’t just talk about impact – you build it, touch it, and see it come to life. You’re surrounded by people who could work anywhere but chose Helion because they’re obsessed with solving problems no one has cracked before. And they’re on a mission to save the planet. Urgency isn’t just encouraged; it’s necessary – because the world can’t wait. Ownership isn’t just an idea here – it’s what pushes us forward. Our problems don’t come with roadmaps – you won’t find them in a textbook or another company’s playbook. These are challenges no one’s even thought about yet. By being the first to take on these challenges, we will be the ones to do what no one has done before – bring fusion energy to the world. And the surprising part? You don’t need a fusion background to join us. Many of our best engineers and scientists had no fusion experience before joining. But today, they’re building a fusion generator that will change how the world is powered. And it’s not just our technical teams. Building the future of energy takes an entire team: operations, supply chain, finance, and beyond. If you’re world-class in your field and want to work on something on the cutting edge, there’s a place for you here.


## **So why Helion?**


We’re building fusion machines at a pace and scale that’s never been done before. This isn’t a science experiment, and it’s not another endless R&D cycle. Our mission is clear – deliver fusion energy to the world in this decade, and we’re already on our way. We’ve achieved fusion in six machines – more than any other company in history – and Polaris takes this to a whole new level. But what really sets us apart? We’re not just proving fusion works – we’re making it real. A system that is scalable, economically viable, and designed for real-world impact. Every breakthrough isn’t just a milestone – it’s a step toward delivering low-cost, zero-carbon fusion energy to the grid – at scale and for everyone. At Helion, you won’t just contribute to our mission – you’ll redefine what’s possible for yourself, surrounded by people who demand your best. If you’re looking for another opportunity to add to your resume, there are plenty of places to go. But if you’re looking to build something that will change the world, Helion is where you do it.
