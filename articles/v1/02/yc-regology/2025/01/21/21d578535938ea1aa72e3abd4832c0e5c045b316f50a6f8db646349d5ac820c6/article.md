# Banking in 2025: Sustainable Cost Management and Revenue Growth

## **Leveraging Regulatory Compliance as a Catalyst for Efficiency and Innovation**


In 2025, banks face a complex challenge where cost management and revenue diversification are critical for maintaining profitability. With rising operational expenses, pressure on net interest income, and regulatory demands, the industry must find more sustainable strategies. Let’s take a look at the key challenges banks are grappling with and the approaches they can adopt to overcome them.


## Rising Costs and Compliance Pressures


Banks’[operating expenses have surged](https://www2.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/industry/financial-services/financial-services-industry-outlooks/banking-industry-outlook.html) in recent years. Among U.S. banks with over $10 billion in assets, compensation costs alone grew by 4.1% year-over-year, reaching $149.6 billion in the first half of 2024, according to Deloitte. This spike is driven by performance-linked rewards in revenue-generating divisions such as trading, wealth management, and investment management, alongside competitive pay to attract top talent in fields like machine learning and generative AI.


Compliance costs remain a significant challenge. With new regulations—such as open banking,[Basel III Endgame](https://crsreports.congress.gov/product/pdf/R/R47855) , and updated debit card swipe fees—taking effect in 2025, banks are under pressure to adapt. A recent study revealed that the cost of financial crime compliance for U.S. and Canadian banks reached tens of billions, encompassing investments in anti-money laundering software, know-your-customer systems, and infrastructure upgrades. To address these cost pressures, banks must rethink their expense management strategies.
