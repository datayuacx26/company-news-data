# Park Dental Partners’ Leaders Featured on Dental Industry Podcast

Park Dental Partners is proud to share that leaders Dr. Chris Steele, Dr. Alan Law, and CEO Pete Swenson were featured on “The Group Dentistry Now Show: The Voice of the DSO Industry,” one of the dental industry’s most respected and widely followed podcasts. Available in both video and audio formats, the episode can be viewed on the[Group Dentistry Now website](https://www.groupdentistrynow.com/dso-group-blog/park-dental-dso-podcast/) .


Our leaders reflect on Park Dental’s remarkable journey, from its founding in 1972 by Dr. Brian Murn and Dr. Greg Swenson to the company’s successful initial public offering last year. They discuss how Park Dental Partners has evolved over more than five decades while staying true to its patient first values. The episode also dives into the organization’s accreditation, the strategy behind pursuing it, and the thoughtful integration of specialty dental services across the practice. Listeners will gain valuable insight into Park Dental Partners’ continued growth, innovation, and long-term vision for the future.


The podcast episode was recorded last month at Park Dental Partners’ Resource Group offices in Roseville, providing a behind the scenes look at the leadership and culture that drive the organization forward.


[Group Dentistry Now](https://www.groupdentistrynow.com/) is a leading multimedia company dedicated to delivering timely news, insights, and education to the dental industry. Its broad audience includes DSOs, dental group practices, dentists, vendors, and other key stakeholders, making this feature an exciting opportunity to share Park Dental Partners’ story with the wider dental community.
