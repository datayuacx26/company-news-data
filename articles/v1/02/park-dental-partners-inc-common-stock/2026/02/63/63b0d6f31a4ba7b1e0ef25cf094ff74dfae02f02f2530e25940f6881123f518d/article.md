# Park Dental Partners To Participate in the AADGP 2026 Annual Meeting and Expo Closing Panel

Park Dental Partners CEO Pete Swenson and Chief Clinical Officer, General Practices, Dr. Chris Steele are participating in a panel discussion on Friday, February 6, to share insights about our group practice model at the American Academy of Dental Group Practice’s AADGP 2026 dental conference at the Hyatt Regency Lost Pines Resort near Austin, Texas.


Moderator Bill Neumann, from Group Dentistry Now, will facilitate the discussion, which will include Mortenson Dental Partners CEO Bill Becknell and Aligned Dental Partners Co-founder John McClure. This panel will be the closing session of the conference and promises to be an enlightening conversation about three very distinct group practice models. Hope to see you there. More information can be found at[https://www.aadgp.org/aadgp2026/](https://www.aadgp.org/aadgp2026/) .
