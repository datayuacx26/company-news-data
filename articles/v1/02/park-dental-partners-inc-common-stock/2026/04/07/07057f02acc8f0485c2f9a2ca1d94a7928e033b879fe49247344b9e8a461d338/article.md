# Park Dental Partners’ Affiliates Sunlight Dental and Weddell Dental Rebrand as Park Dental on April 1

Park Dental Partners announced today that its affiliate practices Sunlight Dental in Surprise, AZ, and Weddell Dental in Bloomington, MN, have rebranded as Park Dental Surprise and Park Dental Bloomington Central, respectively, effective April 1, 2026. Both practices will remain in their current locations.


The rebrands formally align the practices with Park Dental Partners’ flagship general dentistry brand Park Dental, which has more than 50 practices across Minnesota and Wisconsin. The city-based names of Surprise and Bloomington Central also reflect the broader communities the dental teams proudly serve.


Park Dental Partners’ affiliation with Sunlight Dental, which occurred late in 2025, marks the organization’s entry into the fast‑growing Phoenix metropolitan area. Under the leadership of Dr. Martin Romero, the practice has quickly established a reputation for compassionate, patient-centered general dentistry and a commitment to serving patients of all ages.


Weddell Dental, which also became an affiliate of Park Dental Partners at the end of December 2025, is led by Dr. Kenneth Weddell. Dr. Weddell has more than 30 years of experience in dentistry. He and his team have provided the Bloomington community with exceptional patient care since 2012.


“Park Dental Surprise and Park Dental Bloomington Central embody the type of high-quality, community-focused practices we are proud to have as a part of our organization,” said Dr. Chris Steele, Chief Clinical Officer – General Practices, Park Dental Partners. “Both doctors’ passion for personalized care aligns perfectly with our mission.”
