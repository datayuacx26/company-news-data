# Cosmic Rundown: Flipper One, Bitwarden Concerns, Google Search Ads

## Flipper One Needs Community Support


The team behind Flipper Zero has announced[Flipper One](https://blog.flipper.net/flipper-one-we-need-your-help/) , and they're asking for help. The new device is a significant step up from its predecessor, featuring an application processor running Linux alongside the microcontroller architecture that made the original popular.


The[tech specs](https://docs.flipper.net/one/general/tech-specs) reveal a device that bridges the gap between portable hacking tool and full Linux computer. The Hacker News community has been diving deep into the implications for security researchers and hardware enthusiasts.


[Discussion on Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48220647)


## Bitwarden Users Urged to Export Data


A post making the rounds warns users to[get passwords out of Bitwarden](https://www.osnews.com/story/145029/get-your-passwords-out-of-bitwarden-while-you-still-can/) while they still can. The concerns center around recent changes to the password manager's policies and architecture.


For teams managing credentials across applications and content systems, this is a reminder to maintain export capabilities and avoid single points of failure in your security infrastructure.


[Discussion on Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48223258)


## Google Expands Search Ads


Google announced they're[testing new ad formats in Search](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/google-marketing-live-search-ads/) and expanding their Direct Offers pilot. The changes mean more commercial content in search results.


For content teams focused on organic SEO, this shifts the landscape again. The discussion has been heated, with developers and marketers debating the long-term implications for discoverability.


[Discussion on Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48220105)
