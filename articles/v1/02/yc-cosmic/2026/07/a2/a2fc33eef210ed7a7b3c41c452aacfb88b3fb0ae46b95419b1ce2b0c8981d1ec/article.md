# Cosmic Rundown: Kimi-K3, Bun's Rust Rewrite, and GrapheneOS Legal Trouble

## Kimi-K3 Goes Open


Moonshot AI released[Kimi-K3](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K3) , their latest frontier model, as open weights on Hugging Face. The[technical report](https://github.com/MoonshotAI/Kimi-K3/blob/main/k3_tech_report.pdf) details the architecture and training approach.


Kimi-K3 is already[available in Cosmic](https://www.cosmicjs.com/blog/kimi-k3-available-in-cosmic) for teams who want to use it through our AI text generation API. Select it from the model dropdown or pass in your API calls.


[Discussion on Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49065752)


## Bun's Rust Rewrite


The Bun JavaScript runtime is[being rewritten in Rust](https://lockwood.dev/ai/2026/07/27/how-is-the-bun-rewrite-in-rust-going.html) . The post covers progress, challenges, and why the team decided to move away from Zig.


This matters for anyone building on Bun. A Rust foundation could mean better memory safety and potentially easier contributions from the broader systems programming community. Whether it affects performance remains to be seen.


[Discussion on Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49067854)


## GrapheneOS Phone Wipe Leads to Charges


A US citizen[faces federal charges](https://www.techspot.com/news/113236-us-prosecutors-charge-atlanta-man-after-grapheneos-phone.html) after his GrapheneOS phone wiped itself during an airport search. GrapheneOS includes a feature that auto-wipes after failed unlock attempts.


The case raises questions about the legal status of security features that protect user data. GrapheneOS published a[detailed explanation](https://discuss.grapheneos.org/d/40700-grapheneos-protections-against-data-extraction-from-locked-devices) of their locked-device protections.


[Discussion on Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49063022)


## Vercel's TypeScript-to-Native Compiler


Vercel Labs released[Scriptc](https://github.com/vercel-labs/scriptc) , a TypeScript-to-native compiler that produces binaries without bundling a JavaScript engine. The compiled output runs as a standalone executable.


For CLI tools and serverless functions, this could mean faster cold starts and smaller deployment artifacts. The tradeoff is compatibility. Not all Node.js APIs will work, and the project is still experimental.


[Discussion on Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49063175)


## Nvidia's $750B in Deals


Bloomberg reports that[Nvidia's deal-making](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-27/nvidia-s-750-billion-deals-revive-fear-of-ai-circular-financing) is raising concerns about circular AI financing. The pattern: Nvidia invests in AI companies, who then buy Nvidia hardware.


Whether this is sustainable growth or a bubble waiting to pop depends on who you ask. For teams evaluating infrastructure investments, it is worth watching how these financial relationships evolve.


[Discussion on Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49071512)
