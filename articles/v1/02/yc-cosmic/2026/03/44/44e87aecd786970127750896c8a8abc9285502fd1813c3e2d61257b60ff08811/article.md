> Your CMS just joined the conversation.
> And it starts helping the moment you sign up.


Today we're launching two things that change how you interact with your CMS.


**Team Agents** are AI-powered team members that join your Slack workspace, WhatsApp groups, and Telegram channels. They have names, personas, goals, and the ability to read and write your CMS content, build and deploy applications, call external APIs, and delegate tasks to other agents.

## Why We Built This


Every content team we talk to has the same problem: the gap between creating content and shipping it. You're in Slack discussing a blog post. Then you switch to your CMS to write it. Then you need a developer to build the page. Then back to Slack to coordinate. Then you check analytics a week later to see if it worked, in a completely different tool.


Team Agents collapse that entire loop. Ask your agent to write a blog post in Slack, and it researches the topic, creates the CMS object with proper metadata, generates a featured image, and publishes it. Need to know what's working? A weekly workflow pulls analytics from your real tools and tells you what to focus on next. Need a new page? A code agent reads your codebase and ships it.


Content, data, and code. All from the same platform.


## How We Use It: A Real Example


We've been using Team Agents internally for weeks and they've become part of our daily workflow. Here's how it works:


1. A weekly analytics workflow logs into Vercel, Google Search Console, and Ahrefs, pulls performance data, and generates an action items report.
2. Our research agent searches the web and discovers content ideas.
3. Using keyword research and analytics data, our content agent drafts content and messages us for review.
4. Our engineering agent takes action items from the analytics workflow optimizing page performance, fixing technical SEO issues, and shipping code updates in a pull request.


The result is a self-improving engine that creates, publishes, measures, and optimizes content automatically. Automated code updates deliver proper technical fixes and performance improvements, ensuring a strong foundation. The loop runs itself, getting better with every cycle.

## What Team Agents Can Do


**Talk to your team naturally.** Define a persona with a name, role, and personality. Your agent carries on real conversations, not just command-response interactions. It remembers context, asks clarifying questions, and adapts its tone to the situation.


**Create and manage CMS content.** Blog posts, landing pages, product descriptions, email templates. Your agent understands your content schema and creates properly structured objects that match your existing types.


**Build and deploy applications.** Connected to GitHub, your Team Agent can read your codebase, create feature branches, commit changes, open pull requests, and deploy to production. Need a new landing page? Ask in Slack.


**Pull analytics and generate reports.** Computer Use agents browse your real analytics dashboards (Vercel, Google Search Console, Ahrefs, or any web tool) and extract data. Content agents synthesize it into actionable reports. The insights feed back into your content strategy.


**Call external APIs.** Connect to Resend for email, Stripe for payments, Shopify for commerce, or any REST endpoint. Your agent can send branded emails, check order status, or pull data from any service you use. Store your secrets and API access keys securely.


**Delegate to other agents.** Team Agents orchestrate. They can hand off specialized tasks to Content Agents, Code Agents, or Computer Use Agents and report back with the results.


**Run on a schedule.** Set a heartbeat so your agent proactively checks in. Daily standup summaries. Weekly content audits. Monthly performance reports. All delivered in Slack without anyone asking.


**Track goals.** Set measurable objectives like "increase signups by 10%" or "publish 20 blog posts this month" and your agent works toward them, reporting progress along the way.

## Multi-Channel From Day One


Team Agents aren't limited to Slack. They work across:


- **Slack** - Full workspace integration with thread-aware replies, rich formatting, image sharing, and mention-only mode. One-click OAuth setup.
- **WhatsApp** - Use Cosmic's shared number for a quick start, or connect your own WhatsApp Business number. Great for customer-facing agents that handle support or sales inquiries.
- **Telegram** - Create a bot via BotFather in 30 seconds. Works in groups and DMs with photo support.
- **Dashboard** - The Cosmic Agent lives here by default. Any Team Agent can also use the Dashboard channel for internal use without connecting external services.


The same agent, the same persona, the same capabilities, available wherever your team (or your customers) already communicate.


## Customer-Facing Agents


Here's where it gets interesting for revenue teams. Toggle "customer-facing" mode and your Team Agent becomes a front-line representative:


- **Sales qualification** - An agent on WhatsApp that answers product questions, qualifies leads, and books demos via Calendly
- **Customer support** - An agent on Telegram that troubleshoots issues, checks account status via API, and escalates to humans when needed
- **Onboarding** - An agent in Slack Connect channels that guides new customers through setup


Customer-facing mode hides internal configuration details and system info, so your agent presents a clean, branded experience.


## How It Works
