# Additional Objects Add-on: Scale Past Your Plan Limits Without Upgrading

Cosmic now offers an **Additional Objects** add-on. When a Project or Workspace approaches its Object limit, you can add capacity in packs instead of jumping to the next plan tier. Each pack adds **10,000 Objects** and **25 Object Types** for **$10/month** , billed monthly and prorated like every other add-on.


This is the relief valve for the most common scaling question we hear: "I love my plan, I just need more room for content." Now you add exactly the capacity you need, when you need it, and nothing else changes.


## What's New


- **Capacity in packs, not tiers.** Add as many 10,000-Object packs as you need from your billing page. Pricing is linear and predictable: $10/month per pack.
- **Object Types included.** Every pack also raises your Object Type limit by 25, so your content model can grow alongside your content. No separate add-on, no extra charge.
- **Project and Workspace level.** Buy the add-on on a standalone Project, or on a Workspace where it applies across all attached Projects. Billing follows wherever your plan lives.
- **Limits update instantly.** The moment the subscription is active, your effective Object and Object Type limits go up. Your usage meters reflect the new ceiling right away.
- **Works across the platform.** The higher limits apply in the dashboard and through the public REST API, so content creation keeps flowing whether it comes from an editor, a script, or an AI agent.
- **Transparent pricing.** The pricing calculator on cosmicjs.com now includes Additional Objects, so you can model your monthly cost before you commit.


## Why This Matters


Object limits exist to keep plans fair, but a hard cap with no middle ground forces an awkward choice: either upgrade to a much larger plan you do not need yet, or stop creating content. Neither is a good experience when you are in the middle of building something.


The Additional Objects add-on removes that friction. If you are on a plan you like and simply need more headroom, you add a pack or two and keep going. You stay on the plan that fits your team, and your bill grows in small, understandable steps that match your actual usage.


## How It Works


1. Go to **Billing > Add-ons** for your Project or Workspace.
2. Find the **Objects** section and enter the number of **packs of 10,000 objects** you want.
3. Click **Save** and confirm the charge. Each pack is $10/month and also adds 25 Object Types.
4. Your effective limits update immediately. Check **Usage** to see the new ceiling reflected in your Object and Object Type meters.


For example, adding 8 packs raises your limits by 80,000 Objects and 200 Object Types for $80/month, on top of whatever your plan already includes.


## Try It


Open **Billing > Add-ons** , choose how many packs you need in the **Objects** section, and save. The capacity is live as soon as the subscription is active.
