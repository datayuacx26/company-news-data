# Cosmic Rundown: Claude Code Steganography, Postgres 19, and Data Center Power Struggles

## Claude Code's Hidden Watermarks


A developer discovered that[Claude Code is steganographically marking requests](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48734373) sent through the tool. The technique embeds invisible identifiers into prompts, allowing Anthropic to track which requests originate from Claude Code versus other interfaces.


The[technical breakdown](https://thereallo.dev/blog/claude-code-prompt-steganography) reveals how these markers work at the prompt level. For teams building AI-powered workflows, this raises questions about prompt transparency and what metadata travels with your requests. If you're piping content through AI tools, understanding what gets attached matters.


## Postgres 19 Beta Preview


Snowflake published a[preview of PostgreSQL 19 features](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48733031) entering beta. The release focuses on performance improvements and developer experience enhancements that have been in discussion for multiple release cycles.


For teams running Postgres in production, the beta period is the time to test compatibility. The[discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48733031) covers which features are worth evaluating now versus waiting for stable release.


## Data Centers vs. School Air Conditioning


Henrico County, Virginia hosts 37 data centers. The county[asked schools to conserve electricity](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48734699) while those data centers continue operating at full capacity.


The[404 Media report](https://www.404media.co/henrico-virginia-datacenter-energy-cost-email/) documents the email sent to school administrators requesting conservation measures. This is the infrastructure tension playing out in real time: compute demand growing faster than grid capacity, with the burden distributed unevenly.


## Labor's Shrinking Share


The New York Fed published data showing[labor's share of income at its lowest post-war level](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48734234) . The analysis traces the decline through COVID and into 2026.


The[discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48734234) debates what's driving the shift: automation, market concentration, policy changes. For anyone thinking about long-term workforce planning, the macro trends matter.


## Quick Hits


**Qwen 3.6 27B** is getting attention as a[sweet spot for local development](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48721903) . The model size balances capability against hardware requirements, making it practical for developers who want to run inference locally.


**ZLUDA 6 released** , enabling[unmodified CUDA applications on non-Nvidia GPUs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48730713) . For teams locked into CUDA codebases but wanting hardware flexibility, this expands options.


**European digital ID wallets** are[dependent on Google and Apple safety services](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48730729) , raising questions about sovereignty when critical government infrastructure relies on two American companies.


**Open Source Low Tech** launched at[opensourcelowtech.org](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48683098) , documenting sustainable technology solutions that prioritize longevity over complexity.
