# World Kinect Corporation to Host Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Earnings Conference Call on February 19, 2026

MIAMI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Feb. 4, 2026--[World Kinect Corporation](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.world-kinect.com%2F&esheet=54415472&newsitemid=20260204510666&lan=en-US&anchor=World+Kinect+Corporation&index=1&md5=28ce4ff2801a288eeae21a0ca67bff16) (NYSE: WKC) invites you to participate in a conference call with its management team on Thursday, February 19, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. ET to discuss the company's fourth quarter and full year 2025 results, as well as certain forward-looking information. The company plans to release its fourth quarter and full year 2025 results after the market closes on the same date.


Participants can access the live webcast by visiting the company’s website at[ir.world-kinect.com/events](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fir.world-kinect.com%2Fevents&esheet=54415472&newsitemid=20260204510666&lan=en-US&anchor=ir.world-kinect.com%2Fevents&index=2&md5=60857ffdde9432a3fdfc9bc2b76a0f90) . An on-demand replay of the webcast will be available shortly after the call.


**About World Kinect Corporation**


Headquartered in Miami, Florida, World Kinect Corporation (NYSE: WKC) is a global energy management company offering fulfillment and related services to customers across the aviation, marine, and land-based transportation sectors. The company also supplies natural gas and power in the United States and Europe along with a broad suite of sustainability-related products and services.
