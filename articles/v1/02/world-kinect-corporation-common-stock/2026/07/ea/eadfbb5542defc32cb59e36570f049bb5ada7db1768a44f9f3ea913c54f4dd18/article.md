# Revesco Aviation and Machjet International join Air Elite® by World Fuel

**RELEASE DATE: July 16, 2026**


**CONTACT**
**World Fuel Services**
Peter Stevens,[\[email protected\]](https://www.world-kinect.com/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#346467405142515a4774435b465850195f5d5a5157401a575b59)


**NEWS SUMMARY**


Revesco Aviation in Perth and Machjet International on Queensland's Sunshine Coast have joined the Air Elite® by World Fuel network, expanding its member network across Australia.


**July 16, 2026 – HOUSTON** – World Fuel announced that Revesco Aviation at Perth Airport (YPPH) and Machjet International at Sunshine Coast Airport (YBSU) have joined the Air Elite® by World Fuel network. With these additions, the Air Elite network now includes 78 member locations worldwide, including 4 locations in Australia.


Revesco Aviation joins Air Elite at Perth Airport (YPPH), Western Australia's primary international gateway. Located approximately 12 kilometers from Perth's central business district, the facility offers a dedicated FBO, passenger lounge, hangar access, aircraft ground support, and airside passenger pick-up and drop-off.


As a designated Point of Entry, Revesco Aviation coordinates customs, immigration, quarantine, and Off Terminal Clearance (OTC) services, supporting international arrivals into Western Australia.


"Western Australia continues to see demand from operators supporting business, resources, government, and leisure travel," said Peter Hillier, CEO of Revesco Aviation. "We look forward to working with Air Elite members and providing the local knowledge and on-the-ground support needed for successful operations into Perth."


Machjet International joins Air Elite at Sunshine Coast Airport (YBSU), providing operators with access to Queensland's Sunshine Coast and the broader Southeast Queensland region. The location offers the operational advantages of a regional airport environment, including reduced congestion, streamlined ground handling, and greater scheduling flexibility than larger metropolitan airports.


The facility features a dedicated VIP FBO, passenger and crew lounges, secure hangarage and parking for up to Code C Aircraft, direct tarmac access, and 24/7 operational support. Located on Australia's east coast, the airport provides convenient access to both business and leisure destinations throughout the region.


"The Sunshine Coast continues to attract business and leisure travel from across Australia and the Asia-Pacific region," said Simon McDermott, managing director of Machjet International. "Joining Air Elite connects our local operation with a global network while allowing us to continue doing what we do best: supporting operators flying into Queensland and beyond."


"Australia continues to be an important market for business aviation, serving as both a destination and a strategic connecting point for operators moving throughout the Asia-Pacific region," said Malcolm Hawkins, senior vice president, global business aviation, World Fuel. "By adding Revesco Aviation and Machjet International, we're strengthening the support available to operators across two key Australian gateways while expanding the Air Elite network's reach in the region."


Operators visiting both locations will have access to World Fuel products and services, including Contract Fuel, AVCARD® by World Fuel, World Fuel Trip Support, and World Fuel Rewards.
