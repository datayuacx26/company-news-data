# 2026 Belgian Grand Prix: Business aviation planning considerations

The 2026 Formula 1 Belgian Grand Prix takes place July 17 - 19 at Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps, where race-week demand can affect aircraft parking, PPR feasibility, crew logistics, hotel availability, and circuit-area ground transport.


Liège (EBLG) is typically the first airport to evaluate for proximity, while Brussels (EBBR) may be better for operators needing stronger hotel inventory or major airport infrastructure. However, Brussels also requires early review of slot, noise, and PPR considerations, especially for late-night race-weekend departures.


As Christine Vamvakas of Universal Weather and Aviation notes, “Race-day departure is where the plan gets tested.”
