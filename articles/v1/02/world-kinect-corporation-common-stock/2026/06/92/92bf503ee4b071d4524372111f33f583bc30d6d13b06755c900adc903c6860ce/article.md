# Matthew Hough

## Matthew Hough


Chief Information Officer


Matt Hough is an accomplished technology executive and enterprise Chief Information Officer with a proven track record of leading large-scale digital transformation across complex, global organizations. He brings deep expertise in modernizing IT infrastructures, aligning technology strategy with business objectives, and leveraging data, cloud, and AI-driven solutions to accelerate growth and innovation.


Prior to joining World Fuel, Mr. Hough served as Chief Information Officer at Cintas Corporation, where he played a pivotal role in transforming the company’s technology landscape. During his tenure, he led the modernization of legacy systems and the successful deployment of SAP S/4HANA and AI-enabled solutions, helping support the company’s growth from approximately $3 billion to over $10 billion in revenue. He also spearheaded the migration of more than 200 SAP systems to the Google Cloud Platform in under a year, significantly enhancing scalability, agility, and cost efficiency. Under his leadership, the IT organization evolved into a high-performing global team, supporting the integration of more than 30 acquisitions annually while improving operational effectiveness and customer experience.


Mr. Hough began his career serving in the United States Navy as a Search and Rescue Swimmer during Desert Shield, where he developed the leadership, discipline, and resilience that continue to define his approach today.


He holds a Bachelor of Science in Management Information Systems from American Sentinel University and is a veteran of the U.S. Navy.
