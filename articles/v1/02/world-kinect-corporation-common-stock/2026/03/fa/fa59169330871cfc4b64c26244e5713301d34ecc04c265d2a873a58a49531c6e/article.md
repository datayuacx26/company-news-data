# Ruth Giansante

## Ruth Giansante


Chief Administrative Officer


Ruth Giansante is Chief Administrative Officer of World Kinect Corporation, responsible for driving cross-enterprise alignment to ensure strategic priorities translate into coordinated, measurable, and timely execution across the company. She also oversees Enterprise and Insurance Risk Management and Real Estate.


Ruth brings more than 20 years of leadership experience across Legal, Audit, Risk, Finance, and Sustainability. Since joining World Kinect in 2005, she has served in multiple senior positions, including Senior Vice President of Global Energy & Sustainability Services, Chief Financial Officer for the Land segment, Chief Audit Executive, and Associate General Counsel.


She holds a Juris Doctor and a Bachelor of Science in Finance and Accounting from the University of Puerto Rico, undertook advanced studies in International Law from the University of Miami, and completed the Advanced Finance Program at the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania.
