# World Kinect Corporation Declares Regular Quarterly Cash Dividend

MIAMI--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Mar. 19, 2026--[World Kinect Corporation](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.world-kinect.com%2F&esheet=54456660&newsitemid=20260319846306&lan=en-US&anchor=World+Kinect+Corporation&index=1&md5=0b10fcd56a034907ef57c1e423bdfa9c) (NYSE: WKC) announced today that its board of directors has declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.20 per share, which is payable on April 16, 2026 to shareholders of record on March 30, 2026.


**About World Kinect Corporation**


Headquartered in Miami, Florida, World Kinect Corporation (NYSE: WKC) is a global energy management company offering fulfillment and related services to customers across the aviation, marine, and land transportation sectors. We also supply natural gas along with a complementary suite of sustainability-related products and services.
