# Best Umbrella Insurance Companies: Standalone Carrier Comparison and Cost \[2026\]


Umbrella liability insurance sits above your auto, homeowners, renters, or landlord liability coverage and pays when a covered claim exhausts those underlying limits. The questions most shoppers care about are straightforward: what it covers, what it costs, and which insurers still offer strong standalone options without forcing you to bundle every other policy first.


This page focuses on the standalone side of the market. Coverage Cat is a brokerage, not a carrier, so the goal here is to compare the insurers and market types that matter most to personal umbrella shoppers.


## What Umbrella Liability Insurance Covers


[Umbrella insurance](https://www.coveragecat.com/insurance-types/umbrella-insurance) is excess personal liability insurance. It can help pay covered judgments, settlements, and defense costs above the limits of your underlying policies. Personal umbrella policies can also cover some claims, such as libel or slander, that standard auto and homeowners forms may not cover[(Investopedia, "What Is Umbrella Insurance Policy? Definition and Who Needs It", 2026)](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/u/umbrella-insurance-policy.asp) . The NAIC similarly describes umbrella coverage as liability and defense-cost protection that sits above primary insurance[(NAIC, "What's an Umbrella Policy?", 2026)](https://content.naic.org/article/whats-umbrella-policy) .


That usually matters most after:


- A serious car accident with damages above your auto liability limit
- A guest injury at your home or rental property
- A major dog-bite or pool claim
- A personal-injury claim involving libel, slander, or defamation

### RLI


RLI is a long-running specialty insurer with a nationwide umbrella footprint. It is often relevant when a buyer needs standalone flexibility, broader underwriting appetite, or coverage in harder personal umbrella markets like Florida. RLI's personal umbrella product typically tops out at $5 million per policy[(RLI, "Personal Umbrella Insurance", 2026)](https://www.rlicorp.com/personal-insurance/personal-umbrella-insurance) .


### Markel


Markel is a specialty market sold through agents. It is often a strong comparison point for households with clean-to-moderately complex risk profiles that still want standalone placement instead of bundling the umbrella with the same company as home or auto.


### Monoline


Monoline is a newer digital-first umbrella market. Its public product materials emphasize support for entity-owned property, short-term rentals, and a more flexible mix of underlying policies than many bundled carriers allow[(Monoline, "Standalone Umbrella Insurance Products - Policy Features", 2026)](https://www.monoline.com/products/standalone-umbrella-policy-offerings) .


## Standalone vs. Bundled Umbrella Coverage


Bundled umbrella policies can be convenient when your current home or auto carrier also offers strong umbrella pricing. Standalone umbrella policies matter when that is not true.


A standalone policy is often worth comparing when:


- Your current insurer does not offer umbrella coverage
- The bundled umbrella quote is uncompetitive
- You want to keep home and auto with different insurers
- You need a carrier that is more comfortable with rentals, trusts, LLCs, young drivers, or other underwriting complications
- You need a broker to compare several personal umbrella markets side by side


For a dedicated explainer, read[can I buy an umbrella policy from a different company?](https://www.coveragecat.com/insurance-types/umbrella/standalone-umbrella-insurance-policies) .


## Who Should Focus Most on Carrier Comparison?


Carrier choice matters more when you have:


- Teen or inexperienced drivers
- Rental property exposure
- Pools, boats, motorcycles, or recreational vehicles
- Trusts, LLCs, or multiple titled properties
- High income or net worth that pushes you beyond standard limits


If the bigger question is not the carrier but the limit, start with[how much umbrella insurance is recommended](https://www.coveragecat.com/insurance-types/umbrella/how-much-umbrella-insurance-is-recommended) and[high-net-worth umbrella insurance](https://www.coveragecat.com/insurance-types/umbrella/high-net-worth-individuals-and-umbrella-insurance) .


## How to Shop the Market


1. Confirm your current home, auto, renters, landlord, and watercraft liability limits.
2. Decide which umbrella limit you want to test first.
3. Compare at least one bundled option against one or more standalone options.
4. Check each carrier's underwriting requirements, especially for drivers, rentals, dogs, pools, and entity-owned property.
5. Review exclusions and defense-cost treatment before choosing the cheapest quote.


Most carriers still require underlying auto and home liability minimums before the umbrella can attach. A common threshold is $250,000/$500,000/$100,000 on auto and $300,000 on home or renters, but requirements vary by insurer and state[(Kiplinger, "How Much Umbrella Insurance Do I Need?", 2026)](https://www.kiplinger.com/personal-finance/insurance/umbrella-insurance/603237/how-much-umbrella-insurance-do-i-need) .
