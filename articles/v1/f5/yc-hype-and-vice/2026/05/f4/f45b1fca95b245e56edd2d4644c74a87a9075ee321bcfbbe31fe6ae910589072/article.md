# Texas Longhorns Game Day Style: What to Wear to DKR Stadium

Burnt orange, big energy, and Austin heat: welcome to Texas game day.
There’s nothing subtle about a Texas Longhorns game day. From the moment you step into DKR–Texas Memorial Stadium, it’s a sea of burnt orange, country music in the background, and that unmistakable Austin energy. Whether you’re heading to your first UT game or you’ve been singing “Texas Fight” since freshman year, one thing’s for sure: your outfit matters.
A Texas Longhorns game day outfit isn’t just about repping your team; it’s about doing it well. Think: cute, effortless, heat-proof, and just the right amount of bold. Because let’s be real. Burnt orange isn’t the easiest color to style, and Austin weather does not play around.


We’ve got you covered with outfit ideas, styling tips, and the must-haves to make sure you show up looking like the main character from tailgate to touchdown, and straight into post-game plans.
