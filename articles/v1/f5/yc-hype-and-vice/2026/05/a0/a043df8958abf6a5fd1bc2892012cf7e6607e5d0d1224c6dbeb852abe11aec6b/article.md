##### Cute Florida Gators Game Day Outfit Ideas:

##### What to Wear to the Swamp


Ben Hill Griffin Stadium, also known as the Swamp, brings serious energy. It’s loud, packed, and usually warm especially during early-season games. That means your outfit needs to look cute but also hold up through heat, walking, tailgates, and stadium seating.


Breathable pieces are key. Tanks, baby tees, lightweight jerseys, and cropped tops are great options for Florida’s warmer game days. If you’re wearing a jacket or crewneck, choose something easy to tie around your waist or carry once the temperature rises.


Comfortable shoes are also non-negotiable. Sneakers, cowboy boots, or flat boots are usually the safest choices. You want something that can handle a full day on campus without ruining the outfit.
