# Fight on: The Best USC Game Day Outfits

USC game day has its own kind of energy. It’s cardinal, gold, sunny, a little chaotic, and very LA. You’re not just getting dressed for a football game; you’re getting dressed for tailgates, campus walks, photos, the Coliseum, and whatever plans happen after.


The best USC game day outfit looks effortless but still says exactly who you’re cheering for. You want something cute, comfortable, and confident. Not too much. Never boring. Very Trojans.
Whether you’re heading to a tailgate, walking through campus, or cheering at the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum, here’s what to wear to a USC Game Day.


---


### Building a Game Day Color Story


##### Start With Cardinal and Gold


USC’s official colors are cardinal and gold, which makes getting dressed pretty easy. Those colors already do the talking, so your outfit does not need to be overcomplicated.
A USC baby tee with denim shorts? Done. A fitted tank with gingham shorts? Very game day. An oversized crewneck with biker shorts? Cool without trying.


USC game day style should feel like something you would actually wear in LA just with a little USC school spirit.


##### What to Wear to a USC Football Game


For a USC football game, your outfit has to work from morning to night. You might start at a tailgate, walk around campus, head into the Coliseum, and end up somewhere after the game. The outfit needs range.
For warmer games, keep it light. Think crop tops, fitted tanks, baby tees, tube tops, shorts, and mini skirts. It should feel cute but still easy enough to wear all day.


For night games or cooler weather, add a layer that makes the outfit better. A USC crewneck, quarter zip, or varsity jacket gives the look more shape without making it feel like you overplanned it.
And yes to comfortable shoes.


##### What to Wear for a Cooler USC Game


LA weather is usually on your side, but later games can cool down. That’s when layers make the outfit.
An oversized USC crewneck is always a good call. A varsity jacket makes the whole look feel more finished. Start with a tank, baby tee, or tube top underneath so you can adjust throughout the day.

### What Shoes to Wear on USC Game Day


Shoes matter because USC game day is not just “show up and sit down.” You’ll be walking around campus, heading to tailgates, making your way to the stadium, and standing more than you think.
White sneakers are the easiest choice. They go with almost every USC game day outfit and won’t ruin your day. Cowboy boots are cute with skirts, shorts, or dresses. Flat boots work if you want the outfit to feel a little more styled.
Skip the shoes that only look good for ten minutes. This is a long-day outfit.

### USC Game Day Outfit Checklist


Before you head out, make sure your look can handle the whole day:


A USC top or cardinal-and-gold piece
Comfortable shorts, a skirt, or biker shorts
Shoes you can actually walk in
A clear stadium bag
Sunglasses
Gold jewelry or simple accessories
A light layer for night games
