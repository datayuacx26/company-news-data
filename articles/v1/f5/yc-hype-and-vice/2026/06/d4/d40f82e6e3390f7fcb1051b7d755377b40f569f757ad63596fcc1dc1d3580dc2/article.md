# What to wear for Gameday at Michigan State | MSU Outfit Ideas

There’s nothing like gameday in East Lansing. Whether you’re heading to Spartan Stadium, tailgating with friends, or watching the Spartans from your favorite college bar, your outfit should feel spirited, comfortable, and cute enough for every photo. Michigan State’s official colors are green and white, making it easy to build a gameday look that feels classic, bold, and totally on-theme.
From sporty tanks to cozy crewnecks, here’s what to wear for gameday at Michigan State so you can cheer on the Spartans in style.


---

### Crewnecks and Layers for Cold Michigan State Games


Michigan weather can change fast, so layers are key. As the season gets colder, your Michigan State gameday outfit should keep you warm without sacrificing style.


[Oversized crewnecks,](https://www.hypeandvice.com/products/michigan-state-offside-crewneck4) sweaters, and cute sweatpants are all must-have for fall gamedays. For extra warmth, layer a long sleeve under your crewneck or add a puffer jacket, bomber, or[varsity-style jacket.](https://www.hypeandvice.com/products/michigan-state-university-a-game-varsity-jacket)

### What Shoes to Wear to a Michigan State Game


Comfortable shoes are non-negotiable for gameday. Between tailgating, walking around campus, and heading into the stadium, you’ll want shoes that can last all day.
Sneakers are always the best option. White sneakers go with every Michigan State outfit, while green sneakers or sporty trainers can make your look feel more styled. For colder games, try boots with a crewneck, oversized tee, or jacket.
Avoid anything too uncomfortable, especially if you’ll be standing, walking, or tailgating before kickoff


##### Michigan State Gameday Accessories


Accessories are the easiest way to finish your MSU gameday outfit. Add a clear stadium bag, sunglasses, gold jewelry, bows, tall socks, or a green hair ribbon or claw clip to make your look feel complete.
If your outfit is simple, accessories can make it feel more dressed up. A white tank and denim shorts feel instantly more styled with green accessories, layered jewelry, and cute sneakers.


##### Final Tips for What to Wear to a Michigan State Game


The best Michigan State gameday outfit is comfortable, spirited, and easy to wear from tailgate to post-game plans. Stick to green and white, choose pieces that match the weather, and build your look around one statement MSU item.
Whether you’re wearing a cropped tank, jersey, crewneck, or matching set, the goal is simple: look cute, feel confident, and be ready to cheer on the Spartans.
