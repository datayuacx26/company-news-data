# Indiana Hoosiers Gameday Outfits: What to Wear to Cheer on IU in Style

Game day in Bloomington calls for one thing: crimson and cream. Whether you’re heading to Memorial Stadium, Assembly Hall, tailgating with friends, or watching the Hoosiers from your favorite campus spot, your outfit should be Hoosier-approved, comfortable, and cute for every photo at the tailgate
From sporty tanks and cropped tees to cozy crewnecks and cold-weather layers, here’s what to wear for Indiana Hoosiers gameday so you can cheer on IU in style.

The easiest way to build an Indiana University gameday outfit is to start with IU’s signature crimson and cream colors. Crimson instantly makes your outfit eye-catching and Hoosier themed, while cream and white keep the look classic, clean, and easy to style.

##### Jersey and Oversized Tee Looks


Jerseys and oversized tees are a Hoosiers gameday staple. They’re comfortable, easy to style, and perfect for both football and basketball games. ### IU Basketball Gameday Outfits


Indiana basketball season is its own kind of gameday. For IU basketball outfits, think cute, comfortable, and ready for a packed arena. Since basketball season falls during colder months, layers are key.
A cropped tee under a crewneck, a jersey over a long sleeve, or a quarter zip with denim makes the perfect Indiana basketball gameday look. Stick with crimson and cream, then add sneakers, a jacket, and simple accessories to finish the outfit.


Outfit Idea: Indiana jersey layered over a fitted long sleeve, relaxed denim, sneakers, and a warm jacket.

### What Shoes to Wear to an Indiana Game


Comfortable shoes are a must for Indiana gameday. Between tailgating, walking around campus, and standing during the game, you’ll want shoes that look cute but can last all day.
White sneakers are always the easiest choice because they go with every crimson and cream outfit. Sporty trainers, platform sneakers, or casual boots also work well depending on the weather. For colder games, boots with leggings, denim, or a crewneck can make your outfit feel more seasonal.
