## **Embrace Stress-Free Travel Planning**


Traveling can be stressful, but with a bit of planning in 2025, you can ensure a smoother journey. Here are six ways to make travel less stressful:


- **Check Passport Expiration Dates** : Renew passports timely to avoid last-minute hassles. Passport processing times are currently 4-6 weeks, but plan ahead to avoid delays.
- **Reassess Points and Miles Strategy** : Evaluate and optimize your travel reward points. Consider opening a new credit card or maximizing the benefits of existing ones.
- **Apply for or Renew Global Entry** : This program speeds up customs and immigration processes. It's worth signing up if you frequently travel internationally.
- **Start a Savings Routine for Big Trips** : Create a dedicated travel fund by saving small amounts consistently. Automate contributions to ensure you stick to your goal.
- **Set Price Alerts to Dream Destinations** : Use airfare tracking tools to monitor and receive notifications on flight prices to your desired locations.
- **Ensure You Have a Real ID** : By May 7, 2025, all U.S. air travelers need a Real ID-compliant ID for domestic flights, so make sure yours is updated.

## **Explore Affordable Destinations**


Budget-conscious travelers can explore several affordable short-haul destinations in 2025:


- **Marrakech, Morocco** : Known for its affordable hotels, food, and unique shopping experiences in the souks.
- **Trouville Sur Mer, France** : A charming beach town on the Normandy coast, accessible by ferry from Portsmouth to Caen. Known for its sandy beaches, antiques markets, and seafood.
- **Amsterdam, The Netherlands** : Although generally expensive, there are budget-friendly options, such as cheap flights, local brown bars, and self-hired canal boats.
- **Valencia, Spain** : Cheaper than Madrid and Barcelona, it offers budget flights, free attractions like the City Of Arts And Sciences, and inexpensive local food.
- **Ljubljana, Slovenia** : This affordable European capital features free attractions like the old town, River Ljubljanica Kanal, and Ljubljana Castle. Food and drink are also inexpensive, with options like orange wine and street food.


These destinations provide budget-conscious travelers with various experiences, from historical tours and beach relaxation to vibrant city life and culinary explorations.
