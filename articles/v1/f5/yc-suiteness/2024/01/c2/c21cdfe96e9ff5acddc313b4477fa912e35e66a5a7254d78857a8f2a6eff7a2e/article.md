## Hidden Travel Hacks:


- *Leverage Hotel Loyalty Programs:* Sign up for airline or credit card loyalty programs to unlock exclusive perks, such as upgrades, amenities, and even money back.
- *Pack a Power Strip:* Hotel rooms often have limited power outlets. A power strip ensures you can charge all your devices simultaneously.
- *Use the 'Do Not Disturb' Sign Wisely:* Placing the "Do Not Disturb" sign on your door can deter housekeeping from knocking early in the morning, allowing you to sleep in or enjoy a leisurely morning.
