## What Are Connected Rooms?


Connected rooms are adjoining hotel rooms with a door between them, allowing guests to move between spaces without using the hallway. This setup offers the perfect balance of togetherness and privacy, making them ideal for families, friends, or multi-generational groups.


With Suiteness, finding and booking connected rooms has never been easier. Let’s explore why connected rooms are a must for your next group getaway and how Suiteness makes the process seamless.
