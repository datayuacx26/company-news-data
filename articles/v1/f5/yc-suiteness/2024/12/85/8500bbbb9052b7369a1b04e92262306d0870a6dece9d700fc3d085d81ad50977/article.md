# **Suiteness Launches Traversing.ai: A Game-Changing Solution for Hotels**


For over a decade, **Suiteness** has been dedicated to making travel more seamless and enjoyable for our community. From helping travelers find the perfect multi-room suites to delivering award-winning customer service, we’ve built our reputation on understanding the needs of guests and redefining the hospitality experience. Now, we’re excited to share our next big milestone: **the launch of Traversing.ai** , a cutting-edge platform designed to help hotels streamline operations, modernize systems, and thrive in an increasingly competitive industry.


## **Why Traversing.ai?**


As part of the Suiteness mission to continuously innovate, we’ve spent years working closely with hotels, understanding their unique challenges and opportunities. This experience led us to identify a need for a powerful, intuitive platform that solves key pain points for hotels, enabling them to operate more efficiently while delivering exceptional guest experiences.


**Traversing.ai** was born from this vision, combining our decade of expertise with advanced technology to support hotels in new and impactful ways.


## **How Traversing.ai Helps Hotels**


Traversing.ai empowers hotels to optimize their operations, reduce costs, and compete more effectively with large chains and OTAs. Here’s how:


- **Boost Direct Bookings:** Increase direct reservations by 2.7x, reducing reliance on OTAs and maximizing revenue.
- **Simplify Operations:** Consolidate up to 10+ tools into one state-of-the-art platform, saving time and cutting costs by over 50%.
- **Modernize Guest Payments:** Offer guests seamless payment options, such as Apple Pay and Buy Now, Pay Later, to enhance convenience and satisfaction.
- **Save Time:** Eliminate over 40 hours per week of manual work with automation and smarter workflows.


With Traversing.ai, independent hotels now have access to the same advanced technology and tools as major chains, leveling the playing field and enabling them to thrive in today’s digital-first landscape.


## **A Legacy of Innovation**


The launch of Traversing.ai represents a natural evolution of Suiteness’ journey over the past 10+ years. We’ve always been passionate about bridging the gap between guests and hotels, and this new platform extends that mission to empower our hotel partners directly.


Suiteness is no stranger to driving innovation. In 2018, we were recognized as the **Best Place to Work in Hotel Tech** by **Hotel Tech Report** , thanks to our focus on employee satisfaction, growth, and a culture of open communication and work-life balance. This same dedication to excellence is at the heart of Traversing.ai’s development and launch.


## **A Message to the Suiteness Community**


To our loyal Suiteness community, we want to say thank you. Your trust and feedback have been integral to shaping who we are today. Traversing.ai wouldn’t be possible without the insights we’ve gained from collaborating with hotels and travelers alike over the years.


We’re thrilled to share this next chapter with you and look forward to continuing to innovate in ways that enhance your travel experiences and help hotels succeed.


## **What’s Next?**


Whether you’re a traveler searching for unforgettable stays or a hotelier seeking to modernize your operations, **Suiteness and Traversing.ai are here to serve you.**


For travelers, you’ll continue to enjoy the seamless booking experiences you’ve come to love with Suiteness. For our hotel partners, Traversing.ai offers an unparalleled opportunity to elevate your business and deliver unforgettable guest experiences.
