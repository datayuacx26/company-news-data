# Amgen Named Among World’s Most Impactful Companies 2026 by TIME

Amgen has been recognized by *TIME* on the *World's Most Impactful Companies 2026* list. The list recognizes organizations that generate measurable, positive impact across society, the environment, and the global economy.


Amgen's recognition reflects its mission to serve patients with serious illness through innovative medicines. Amgen harnesses the best of biology and technology to fight the world's toughest diseases, reaching millions of people globally, making their lives easier, fuller and longer.


Inclusion in the list of the *World's Most Impactful Companies 2026* represents independent, third-party evaluation of net-positive contributions of products and services among four key dimensions: Society, Knowledge, Health, and Environment. The ranking recognizes 500 companies that address global challenges through their core business, based on a methodology that evaluates alignment with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals and science-based impact data.
