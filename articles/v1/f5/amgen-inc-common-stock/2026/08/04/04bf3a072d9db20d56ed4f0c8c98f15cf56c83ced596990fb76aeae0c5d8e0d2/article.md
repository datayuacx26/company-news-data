# Highlights from Amgen's 2026 Second Quarter Earnings Report

Today, Amgen shared its 2026 second quarter[earnings report](https://www.amgen.com/newsroom/press-releases/2026/08/amgen-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results) . Amgen’s employees are united by our mission to serve patients. We harness the best of biology and technology to fight the world’s toughest diseases, and make people’s lives easier, fuller, and longer.


"Our results demonstrate strong performance across our business. Our six key growth drivers grew 26% year over year, generating nearly 70% of second-quarter product sales. As we expand the potential of our existing medicines through new indications and advance the next wave of pipeline molecules through Phase 3, we remain confident in our ability to deliver growth well into the next decade," said Robert A. Bradway, chairman and chief executive officer.
