# Amgen Included in U.S. Veterans Magazine’s 2026 Top Veteran Employers List

Amgen has been recognized by *U.S. Veterans Magazine* as one of its 2026 Top Veteran Employers, highlighting the company's efforts to recruit, retain, and support military veterans.


The skills developed through military service—including leadership, teamwork, adaptability, and problem solving—help veterans make meaningful contributions across Amgen. As they transition into civilian careers at Amgen, veterans bring leadership, technical expertise, and a mission-driven mindset to serving patients around the world.


“When companies recognize and invest in overlooked talent pools, they gain a powerful competitive edge while making a meaningful difference,” said Mona Lisa Faris, president and publisher of *U.S. Veterans Magazine* .
