# FAQ: What Are Inter Wearables? Features, Setup, Security and Payments · Inter&Co Blog

# **GETTING TO KNOW THE PRODUCT**


## **What are Wearables?**


Wearables are smart devices designed to make your everyday life easier through passive NFC technology. As high-performance wearable devices, they connect with features from our partner companies — letting you make contactless payments and manage your credentials quickly and securely.


Beyond financial transactions, Wearables open up a whole new world of possibilities for your daily routine, offering versatile solutions like access control and task automation. And all of this comes wrapped in a sleek, minimalist design that brings practicality, discretion, and a touch of style to your lifestyle.


## Key Features:


- **Tap-to-Pay Transactions:** Make payments instantly — no need to pull out your phone or carry extra cards. Just tap and go! Transaction limits without PIN entry follow the current security protocols for contactless technology.
- **Global Interoperability:** Fully compatible with any contactless-enabled reader terminal — wherever you are in the world.


## **What is NFC Technology?**


NFC stands for Near Field Communication — it's a short-range wireless technology that works within just a few inches. Because it requires physical closeness to work, it keeps your information extra safe.


## **What are the benefits of using Wearables?**


- **Convenience:** Just tap your accessory to pay — that's it!
- **Security:** Safer than traditional payment methods;
- **No phone needed:** Once it's set up, your Wearable works completely on its own;
- **No charging, no battery** — ever.

## **Are Wearables compatible with all phones?**


Not quite. Your smartphone needs to have NFC enabled to work with your Wearable.

# **SAFETY & CARE**


## **Can I wear it in the shower or the pool?**


**This device is water and dust resistant, meeting the following international standard:**


- **IP68 Certification:** tested and submerged in up to 1.5 m of fresh water for 30 minutes without damage.
- Can be worn during physical activities, when washing hands, or on rainy days.
- Can be worn while swimming in pools or in the sea, provided it is in shallow waters.


## **Can I wear it during exercise?**


• The Ring is sensitive to scratches, so avoid exposing it to abrasive surfaces or impacts in daily use.


• Remove the ring before lifting or holding heavy or rigid objects.


• Do not wear the ring during exercises with equipment, especially with free weights such as bars and dumbbells.


## **Do the devices affect my health?**


The device uses passive NFC technology, operating exclusively through transponders that react only when brought close to active terminals. This means the hardware does not emit radiation, vibrations, or its own signals, making it completely inert and safe for continuous use.


## **Is contactless payment safe?**


Yes. For your security, contactless payment uses tokenization + advanced encryption.
