## What Are Gift Cards, and Why Are They a Smart Savings Strategy?


In Brazil, gift cards are often seen as a last-minute gift option for special occasions. In the United States, however, gift card culture is quite different. They function almost like a digital currency and play an important role in the financial planning of savvy consumers looking to maximize their budgets.


### How Does It Work in Practice?


A Gift Card is simply a physical or digital card loaded with a specific dollar amount for a particular retailer.


The real advantage for someone furnishing a home isn’t just convenience—it’s the indirect savings. ## Your Retail Shopping Roadmap: Where to Focus Your Purchases


For anyone furnishing a new home, the American retail landscape offers major chains where you can find almost everything you need in one place. Here’s where to focus your Gift Card strategy:


### Target and Walmart: From Everyday Essentials to Home Décor


For kitchenware, bedding, bath essentials, small appliances (such as blenders and toasters), and organizational products, Target and Walmart should be at the top of your list.


Target, in particular, is known for its stylish exclusive home décor collections that combine great design with affordable prices. Using Gift Cards at these stores can easily cover nearly 70% of your initial household setup needs.


### Amazon and Home Depot: Furniture and Home Improvement Essentials


If your new home needs TV mounts, curtains, light fixtures, or minor upgrades, Home Depot is the ultimate destination for the American do-it-yourself lifestyle.


Amazon, meanwhile, needs no introduction. It’s likely where you’ll order your bed, mattress, and ready-to-assemble furniture with delivery straight to your doorstep. Having Gift Cards already loaded into your account on these platforms helps streamline the purchasing process while ensuring you receive the savings upfront.
