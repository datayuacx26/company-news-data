# What People Are Buying This 4th of July


The Fourth of July is one of the biggest shopping periods of the summer.


Travel plans, family gatherings, outdoor activities, and seasonal sales all come together during the holiday week, driving spending across a wide range of categories. While every household celebrates differently, a few purchasing trends show up year after year.


Looking at this year's Inter Day offers provides a good snapshot of what many people are shopping for as Independence Day approaches.


## Summer Clothing and Outdoor Gear


July marks the heart of summer, and apparel remains one of the most popular categories during the holiday period.


Many shoppers use seasonal promotions to update their wardrobe, prepare for vacations, or pick up outdoor gear for trips and weekend activities.


Brands featured during Inter Day include:


- Columbia
- Adidas
- H&M
- Marshalls
- TJ Maxx
- Burlington

- Bloomingdale's
- Carter's

Whether it's activewear, summer essentials, or family shopping, clothing is consistently one of the most purchased categories around the Fourth of July.


## Travel and Getaways


For millions of Americans, Independence Day means getting out of town.


Some are planning long weekend road trips. Others are booking beach vacations, city escapes, or family visits. Travel spending traditionally increases during this period, making it one of the busiest holiday weekends of the year.


Among the Inter Day offers, travel-related brands include:


- Airbnb
- Hotels.com
- Uber


Travelers also tend to spend more on fuel, dining, and convenience purchases while away from home, making holiday weekends a major driver of overall consumer spending.


## Food, Takeout, and Family Gatherings


No Fourth of July celebration is complete without food. Backyard barbecues, family dinners, neighborhood gatherings, and last-minute takeout orders all contribute to increased spending during the holiday week.


Some of the restaurant and food brands featured during Inter Day include:


- Chipotle
- Panda Express
- Cracker Barrel
- The Cheesecake Factory
- IHOP
- DoorDash


For many families, food is at the center of the celebration, making dining-related purchases one of the biggest spending categories every year.


## Home Improvement and Outdoor Living


The long holiday weekend is also a popular time for home projects. Some people tackle renovations. Others refresh their patio, upgrade outdoor spaces, or prepare their home for guests and gatherings.


Inter Day features offers from brands commonly associated with home improvement and home décor, including:


- Home Depot
- Crate & Barrel
- HomeGoods


With summer in full swing, these purchases often support projects that homeowners have been planning for months.


## Beauty and Self-Care


Summer events, vacations, weddings, and social gatherings often lead to increased spending on beauty and personal care products.


Many shoppers take advantage of seasonal promotions to restock essentials or try new products.


Featured brands include:


- Sephora
- Ulta Beauty

These categories tend to see strong demand before holiday weekends and throughout the summer season.


## Technology and Entertainment


Holiday sales are no longer limited to major shopping events like Black Friday.
