# Oblenio Bio

[Active Portfolio Companies](https://www.pfizer.com/taxonomy/term/11726)


Oblenio Bio is a clinical-stage company pursuing broad immune reset as a transformative therapy for refractory autoimmune diseases through its LBL-051 T cell engager. Specifically engineered for autoimmune patients, this tri-specific unites validated targets BCMA, CD19 and CD3 to safely and completely deplete B cells and plasma cells to achieve long-term drug-free remission. The company is singularly focused on LBL-051’s innovative clinical development program to rapidly establish it as a life-changing treatment for the vast number of patients who endure severe autoimmune disease without relief.
