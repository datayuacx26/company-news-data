# Essential Utilities Recognizes National Water Quality Month and Reaffirms Commitment to Protecting and Providing Earth's Most Precious Resource

[PDF Version](https://www.essential.co/node/29631/pdf)


*Company highlights 140 years of water stewardship and plans to invest approximately $955 million in water and wastewater infrastructure in 2026 to support safe, reliable service and address emerging water quality challenges.*


BRYN MAWR, Pa.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 3, 2026--[Essential Utilities Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.essential.co%2F&esheet=54580816&newsitemid=20260803497147&lan=en-US&anchor=Essential+Utilities+Inc.&index=1&md5=facab55647a3595deab4ac913a3dc304) (NYSE: WTRG), one of the nation’s largest publicly traded providers of water, wastewater and natural gas services, proudly recognizes Water Quality Month this August. Established by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency in 2005, the annual observance highlights the vital importance of clean, safe water to public health, thriving communities and a sustainable environment.


“Water is one of our planet’s most vital natural resources, and protecting it is a responsibility we take seriously,” said Christopher Crockett, Ph.D., vice president and chief environmental, safety and sustainability officer at Essential. “As environmental stewards, we are committed to safeguarding water at every stage, from source protection and responsible treatment to the safe return of water to the environment. Preserving water quality demands vigilance, innovation and collaboration, and we work every day to minimize our environmental impact while ensuring future generations have access to clean, reliable water. Protecting this precious resource is at the heart of our sustainability mission.”


For 140 years, Essential’s water utility, Aqua, has been a trusted steward of water quality, delivering safe, reliable drinking water through ongoing investments in critical infrastructure, cutting-edge treatment processes and the dedication of skilled professionals. This commitment continues to drive innovation and strengthen the communities Aqua serves.


In 2026 alone, Aqua intends to invest roughly $955 million in water and wastewater infrastructure. These critical projects will support aging water main replacements, compliance with evolving water quality regulations, and system upgrades to address emerging contaminants like PFAS and lead.


“Water connects every aspect of our lives, making its protection one of our most important responsibilities,” said Aqua President Colleen Arnold. “During Water Quality Month, we recognize the incredible efforts of our employees who help keep our communities healthy by maintaining strong, resilient water systems and delivering high-quality service every day.”

**About Essential Utilities**


Essential Utilities, Inc. (NYSE:WTRG) delivers safe, reliable services that improve quality of life for individuals, families and entire communities. With a focus on water, wastewater and natural gas, Essential is committed to sustainable growth, operational excellence, a superior customer experience and premier employer status. We are advocates for the communities we serve, donating more than $4 million from the Essential Foundation each year, and are dedicated stewards of natural lands, protecting thousands of acres of forests and other habitats throughout our footprint.


Operating as the Aqua and Peoples brands, Essential serves approximately 5.5 million people across 9 states.
