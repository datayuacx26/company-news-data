# How to Prepare for the 51Talk English Language Assessment


Prepare for the 51Talk English Language Assessment with confidence. Learn what to expect, equipment requirements, speaking tips, and how to succeed online.


Every completed step brings you closer to starting your teaching journey with **51Talk** . Now it's time for the **English Language Assessment** —an opportunity to showcase your English communication skills, confidence, and readiness to teach learners in an online classroom.


While many candidates spend most of their time thinking about what questions they might be asked, successful preparation starts much earlier.


Having the right teaching equipment, practicing your spoken English, and creating a professional learning environment can help you perform with confidence and make a positive impression.


Whether you're an experienced educator or preparing to become an **online English teacher** for the first time, this guide will help you understand what to expect and how to prepare for your **51Talk English Language Assessment** .


## **Why the English Language Assessment Matters**


The **English Language Assessment** is an important milestone in the 51Talk teacher journey. It gives you the opportunity to demonstrate how effectively you communicate in English—one of the most essential skills for teaching learners online.


More than evaluating your language proficiency, the assessment also reflects your readiness to teach in a virtual classroom. Preparing in advance allows you to stay focused, communicate confidently, and perform at your best.


## **What to Expect During the English Language Assessment**


Knowing what to expect before your scheduled assessment can help you feel more confident and perform at your best.


The **51Talk English Language Assessment** takes approximately **5–10 minutes** and is completed online. Throughout the assessment, you'll complete several guided steps designed to evaluate your English communication skills and ensure you're ready for the next stage of the teacher journey.


### **Step 1: Enter the Assessment Room**


Log in to the Teacher Candidacy Portal and click **Start Assessment** when you're ready. Before beginning, make sure you've set aside enough uninterrupted time to complete the assessment.


### **Step 2: Watch the Assessment Instructions**


Take a few minutes to watch the instruction video carefully. It explains what you'll be doing during the assessment and helps you understand the process before you begin.


Listening carefully to the instructions can help you avoid common mistakes and complete the assessment with confidence.


### **Step 3: Complete the Device Check**


Before the assessment starts, the system will guide you through a quick equipment check.


You'll be asked to test your:


Camera


Microphone


Speakers


Completing these checks ensures everything is working properly so you can focus on the assessment without technical interruptions.


### **Step 4: Complete the Assessment**


During the assessment, you'll complete several short activities, including:


-


**Information Verification** – Confirm your personal and educational details.


-


**English Reading Activity** – Read a short passage aloud to demonstrate your pronunciation, fluency, and spoken English.


-


**Professional Conduct Questions** – Answer a few simple questions by following the on-screen instructions.


Read each instruction carefully and answer every section in English.


## **Prepare Your Equipment Before the Assessment**


Your preparation begins long before the assessment starts. Reliable equipment helps you avoid unnecessary interruptions and allows you to focus on communicating with confidence.


Before your scheduled assessment, make sure you have:


-


A laptop or desktop computer


-


A stable and reliable internet connection


-


A working webcam


-


A clear headset or microphone


-


A quiet, well-lit room with minimal distractions


Take a few minutes to test your camera, microphone, speakers, and internet connection before your schedule. Being technically prepared creates a smooth experience and demonstrates professionalism from the very beginning.


**Tip:** Mobile phones and tablets are not supported for the assessment. Complete your assessment using a laptop or desktop computer.


## **Practice Speaking English with Confidence**


Many candidates believe they need perfect English to succeed. In reality, confidence and clear communication are far more important.


The **51Talk English Language Assessment** is designed to evaluate how well you communicate your ideas.


Focus on speaking naturally rather than trying to sound perfect.


To build confidence before your assessment:


-


Practice introducing yourself in English.


-


Read short English passages aloud to improve pronunciation and fluency.


-


Speak at a steady pace and avoid rushing.


-


Use complete sentences whenever possible.


-


Maintain eye contact with the camera and smile naturally.


Remember, the goal isn't perfection—it's demonstrating that you can communicate clearly and confidently with learners.


## **Create an Environment That Sets You Up for Success**


Your surroundings can influence both your confidence and your performance.


Choose a place where you can complete the assessment without interruptions.


A good assessment environment includes:


-


A quiet room


-


Good lighting


-


A clean, distraction-free background


-


Minimal background noise


These simple preparations help you stay focused and allow your communication skills to take center stage.


## **Stay Calm and Be Yourself**


Every teacher has a unique story and teaching journey.


Whether you're a licensed professional teacher, an experienced online educator, a career shifter, a stay-at-home parent, or someone taking the first step toward teaching online, authenticity matters.


The assessment is not about memorizing answers. It's an opportunity to demonstrate your communication skills, confidence, professionalism, and enthusiasm for teaching.


Relax, stay positive, and let your personality shine throughout the assessment.


## **Need Assistance Before Your Assessment?**

Approach your **51Talk English Language Assessment** prepared and ready to showcase your potential.


After completing the assessment, continue with the remaining steps in the **51Talk teacher journey** to move one step closer to successfully starting to teach learners from around the world.
