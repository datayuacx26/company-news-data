# 51Talk Tech Check Guide: Build a Teaching Setup That Creates Better Learning Experiences


Prepare for your 51Talk Tech Check with the right teaching setup. Learn the computer, internet, webcam, headset, and classroom requirements for online teaching.


Every great online classroom begins long before the first lesson starts.


It begins with a teacher who is prepared.


Your computer, internet connection, webcam, headset, and teaching environment are more than just equipment—they are the foundation of every lesson you'll teach. They help learners hear every instruction clearly, stay engaged throughout the class, and enjoy a smooth learning experience from beginning to end.


Think of your teaching setup as an investment in your future as an educator. The stronger your setup, the more confidently you can teach, the more meaningful each lesson becomes, and the greater impact you can make on every learner you meet.


Before you begin teaching with **51Talk** , take time to build a classroom that's ready to inspire learning.


## **Why Your Teaching Equipment Matters**


Imagine you're teaching a learner who is excited to practice English.


Just as they begin speaking confidently, your audio cuts out.


Or your video freezes while you're demonstrating pronunciation.


Even the most engaging lesson can lose its momentum because of technical interruptions.


Great teaching isn't only about knowledge and communication. It's also about creating an online classroom where learners can stay focused without distractions.


A reliable teaching setup allows you to spend less time worrying about technology and more time doing what matters most—helping learners build confidence in English.


Every improvement you make to your teaching environment is an investment in better learning experiences.


## **Start with a Reliable Computer**


Your computer is the heart of your online classroom.


A device with the right specifications keeps your lessons running smoothly, allowing you to open teaching materials, interact with learners, and communicate without delays.


Prepare a computer that meets these minimum requirements:


-


Desktop computer or laptop


-


Windows 10 or newer


-


Intel Core i5 (8th Generation or higher) or AMD Ryzen 5 processor or higher


-


At least 8 GB RAM


-


At least 100 GB available storage


A reliable computer allows you to focus on your learners—not your technology.


**Please note:** Mobile phones and tablets are not supported for teaching or completing the Tech Check.


## **Let Learners See You Clearly**


Teaching is more personal when learners can clearly see the teacher guiding them.


An HD webcam helps students recognize facial expressions, understand demonstrations, and stay connected throughout the lesson.


Recommended specification:


-


Built-in or external webcam


-


Minimum 720p HD resolution


Sometimes a smile or encouraging expression can motivate a learner just as much as your words.


## **Make Every Word Count**


Online learning depends on clear communication.


A quality headset with a noise-cancelling microphone helps ensure every instruction, correction, and encouragement reaches your learners without distractions.


Prepare:


-


A noise-cancelling headset


-


An extended microphone for clearer voice pickup


When learners hear every word clearly, they can focus on learning with confidence.


## **Give Every Lesson a Stable Connection**


Imagine delivering an exciting activity only to lose your internet connection halfway through.


A stable internet connection keeps every lesson flowing naturally and helps learners stay engaged.


Recommended setup:


-


Minimum 25 Mbps internet speed


-


Wired or stable broadband connection


Pocket Wi-Fi and mobile hotspots are not supported because they may not provide the stability needed for a consistent online classroom experience.


Reliable internet is one of the best investments you can make in your teaching journey.


## **Create a Classroom That Learners Enjoy**


Your teaching environment is part of every lesson.


A clean, organized, and professional space helps learners focus on learning instead of distractions.


Prepare:


-


A quiet teaching space


-


A plain white background


-


Bright front-facing lighting


-


A professional online teaching setup


These details help create a welcoming classroom where learners feel comfortable, engaged, and ready to participate.


## **Every Detail Shapes the Learning Experience**


Each piece of equipment plays an important role.


A clear camera builds connection.


A quality headset improves communication.


Reliable internet keeps lessons uninterrupted.


A professional teaching environment creates trust.


Together, they create an online classroom where meaningful learning can happen every day.


When learners enjoy a better learning experience, they become more confident in using English. And every confident learner is another step toward fulfilling the **51Talk mission of making quality English education more accessible through passionate and well-prepared teachers.**


## **Quick Tech Check Checklist**


Before completing your Tech Check, make sure everything is ready:


✔ Desktop computer or laptop


✔ Windows 10 or newer


✔ Intel Core i5 (8th Generation or higher) or AMD Ryzen 5 processor or higher


✔ At least 8 GB RAM


✔ At least 100 GB available storage


✔ HD webcam (720p or higher)


✔ Noise-cancelling headset with microphone


✔ Stable broadband internet connection (25 Mbps or higher)


✔ No Pocket Wi-Fi or Mobile Hotspots


✔ Plain white background


✔ Bright front-facing lighting


✔ Quiet and professional teaching environment
