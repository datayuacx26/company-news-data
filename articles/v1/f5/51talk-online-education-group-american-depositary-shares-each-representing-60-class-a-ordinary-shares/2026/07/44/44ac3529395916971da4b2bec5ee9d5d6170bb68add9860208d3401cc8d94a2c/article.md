# Celebrate 15 Years of Learning, Growth, and Teacher Impact with 51Talk


Celebrate 15 years of 51Talk's journey in online English education. Discover its mission, teacher impact, and how to begin teaching from home with a global platform.


Fifteen years ago, **51Talk** founder Jack Huang set out with a simple yet ambitious vision: to make quality English education more accessible while creating meaningful opportunities for teachers to share their knowledge with learners around the world.


That vision has grown into a global learning platform that continues to connect passionate educators with students across different countries. For 15 years, 51Talk has helped build confidence in millions of learners while enabling thousands of teachers to grow professionally through online English teaching.


This anniversary is more than a celebration of time. It is a celebration of every lesson taught, every student inspired, and every teacher whose passion, dedication, and commitment continue to shape the future of learning.


## A Legacy Built One Lesson at a Time


Great achievements are rarely created overnight.


For the past 15 years, **51Talk** has continued to evolve with one goal in mind: to enable every student to speak up, stand out, and succeed in life.


What began as an online English education platform has grown into a global learning community that connects students across multiple countries and regions. At the heart of this journey are thousands of Filipino teachers whose warmth, resilience, adaptability, and genuine passion for teaching have earned the trust of learners from diverse cultures and backgrounds.


Filipino educators are known not only for their strong English communication skills but also for their patience, empathy, and ability to make every student feel encouraged and valued. Every class becomes more than a language lesson. It builds confidence, creates opportunities, and fosters meaningful human connections that extend far beyond the virtual classroom.


That legacy continues to grow with every Filipino teacher who begins a new journey, proudly sharing the country's world renowned teaching excellence with learners around the globe.
