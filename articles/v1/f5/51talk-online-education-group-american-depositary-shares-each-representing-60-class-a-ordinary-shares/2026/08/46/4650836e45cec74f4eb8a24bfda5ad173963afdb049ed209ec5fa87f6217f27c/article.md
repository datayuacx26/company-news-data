# How to Practice English Pronunciation at Home in Just 10 Minutes a Day


Improve your English pronunciation in just 10 minutes a day with simple exercises for F, V, and TH sounds, helping you prepare for online English teaching.


Becoming a confident **online English teacher** starts with clear communication—and that begins with pronunciation.


Take a moment and say these words out loud:


-


**Five**


-


**Friend**


-


**Family**


-


**Very**


-


**Visit**


-


**Think**


-


**Three**


-


**Thank you**


Did you notice yourself slowing down or wondering if you pronounced some of them correctly?


These are everyday English words, but they also contain some of the most commonly practiced English sounds—the **F** , **V** , and **TH** sounds.


As an **online English teacher** , these words matter more than you might think. Your learners listen carefully to how you pronounce every word, and many will naturally imitate what they hear. Every correctly pronounced word helps build their confidence and strengthens their English communication skills.


**The journey to clearer pronunciation begins with just 10 focused minutes a day.** With a simple daily routine, you can strengthen common English sounds, develop a clearer and more neutral accent, and build the confidence you'll need for your **51Talk English Language Assessment** , **Demo Teaching** , and every lesson that follows.


Small daily practice creates lasting improvement—not only for you, but also for every learner whose English journey begins with your voice.


## **Why Just 10 Minutes Makes a Difference**


Improving your pronunciation isn't about practicing for hours.


It's about practicing consistently.


Think about athletes, musicians, or even experienced teachers. They improve because they practice regularly, not because they spend one long day preparing.


The same is true for spoken English.


By dedicating just **10 minutes every day** , you'll gradually develop better pronunciation, stronger fluency, and greater confidence. These small improvements become natural habits that you'll carry into every **25-minute lesson** .


## **A Simple 10-Minute Pronunciation Routine**


You don't need a classroom or expensive equipment.


All you need is your voice and a willingness to improve.


## **Minute 1–2: Warm Up Your Voice**


Before speaking, warm up your mouth and voice.


Practice saying simple vowel sounds and common English words slowly.


Smile while speaking to help your voice sound more natural and energetic.


## **Minute 3–5: Read Aloud**


Choose a short English article, children's story, or news article.


Read it slowly.


Focus on:


-


Pronouncing each word clearly.


-


Speaking at a natural pace.


-


Pausing at punctuation marks.


-


Avoiding rushing through sentences.


Reading aloud strengthens pronunciation, fluency, and confidence at the same time.


## **Minute 6–8: Listen and Repeat**


Choose a short English video, podcast, or conversation.


Listen to one sentence.


Pause.


Repeat it exactly as you heard it.


Pay attention to:


-


Pronunciation


-


Word stress


-


Intonation


-


Rhythm


This simple exercise trains your ears and helps you sound more natural over time.


## **Minute 9–10: Record Yourself**


Finish by recording yourself reading a paragraph or introducing yourself.


Then listen to your recording.


Ask yourself:


-


Was I easy to understand?


-


Did I pronounce unfamiliar words clearly?


-


Did I speak at a comfortable pace?


-


Did I sound confident?


Recording yourself allows you to notice small improvements and identify areas you can continue to practice.


## **Common Pronunciation Sounds to Practice**


Every language has sounds that can be challenging.


Being aware of these sounds is the first step toward becoming a clearer and more confident speaker.


### **F and P Sounds**


The **F** and **P** sounds are commonly confused because they are produced differently.


Practice saying these words slowly:


-


**Five**


-


**Fan**


-


**Find**


-


**Fine**


-


**Phone**


-


**Family**


To produce the **F** sound, gently place your **upper teeth against your lower lip** and let the air flow out.


For the **P** sound, press both lips together before releasing the air.


Try alternating between words that begin with **F** and **P** , such as:


-


Fan – Pan


-


Fine – Pine


-


Fast – Past


Notice how changing just one sound creates a completely different word.


### **V and B Sounds**


The **V** and **B** sounds are another pair that require different mouth movements.


Practice saying these words:


-


**Very**


-


**Visit**


-


**Voice**


-


**Vacation**


-


**Best**


-


**Believe**


For the **V** sound, lightly touch your upper teeth to your lower lip while allowing your voice to vibrate.


For the **B** sound, briefly press both lips together before releasing the sound.


Take your time and focus on producing each sound clearly.


### **TH Sounds**


The **TH** sound is one of the most recognizable sounds in English and is often unfamiliar to many learners.


Practice saying:


-


**Think**


-


**Three**


-


**Thank you**


-


**This**


-


**Those**


-


**Thursday**


To produce the **TH** sound, gently place the tip of your tongue between your teeth and let the air pass through before continuing with the rest of the word.


It may feel unusual at first, but with consistent practice, it becomes much more natural.


### **Use the English Around You**


One of the easiest ways to improve pronunciation is to become more intentional with the English you're already exposed to every day.


Whether you're watching your favorite series, listening to music, watching short videos, enjoying podcasts, or even talking to AI voice assistants, you're surrounded by opportunities to learn.


Instead of simply listening:


-


Repeat short phrases aloud.


-


Copy the speaker's pronunciation.


-


Notice how words are connected.


-


Practice unfamiliar vocabulary immediately.


The more you listen actively, the more naturally you'll begin to speak.


## **Practice Like You're Already Teaching**


One of the best ways to prepare for becoming an **online English teacher** is to practice speaking as though you're already in front of a learner.


Try saying common classroom expressions such as:


-


"Great job!"


-


"Let's practice together."


-


"Can you repeat after me?"


-


"Excellent work!"


-


"Let's read the sentence together."


This helps you become more comfortable using English naturally before your first class.


## **Remember: Your Learners Will Learn From You**


As an **online English teacher** , you're more than someone who explains grammar and vocabulary.


You're a language model.


Learners don't just listen to what you teach—they listen to **how** you say it. They naturally imitate your pronunciation, rhythm, and speaking style.


Imagine introducing a new word like **"five," "very,"** or **"think."** When learners hear these words pronounced clearly and accurately, they're more likely to develop correct pronunciation themselves. Every word you model becomes part of their learning journey.


That's why improving your pronunciation isn't simply about sounding professional.


It's about helping learners build confidence by giving them a strong example to follow in every lesson.
