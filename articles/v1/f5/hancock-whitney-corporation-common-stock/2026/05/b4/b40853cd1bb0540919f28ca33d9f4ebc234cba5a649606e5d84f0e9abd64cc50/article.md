# Hancock Whitney Celebrates Opening of New Financial Center in Little Elm

### Bank expands banking center footprint to seven locations in North Texas


For More Information


Arian Pierre, PR & Social Media Producer
arian.pierre@hancockwhitney.com or 504.380.6257


Expansion across North Texas.


*Representatives of Hancock Whitney celebrated the opening of its newest financial center in Little Elm*


Hancock Whitney has officially opened its third financial center in North Texas this year, with the latest location in Little Elm, as the bank continues to expand its regional presence. The new center at 11700 FM 423 joins existing locations in Dallas, Plano, Rockwall, Greenville, and Mesquite. Hancock Whitney is also constructing additional financial centers in Plano and McKinney.


The Little Elm financial center offers a full suite of banking services designed to meet the needs of individuals, families, and businesses throughout the Plano and Richardson areas. Clients will have access to personal and business banking solutions, wealth management, and lending services, all delivered with Hancock Whitney’s hallmark commitment to exceptional service.


“Our new location in Little Elm is in an area of North Texas that is growing rapidly and represents a significant opportunity to broaden our reach and serve more clients with Hancock Whitney’s relationship


‑


focused banking experience,” said Will Pyle, Dallas Market President. “Little Elm is only minutes away from Frisco, The Colony, Hackberry, Oak Point, Cross Roads, and Prosper. Our mission is to help people achieve their financial goals while supporting the growth and vitality of the communities we serve. This new location marks another step in enhancing our presence and impact across North Texas.”


The Little Elm team brings extensive experience and personalized service to clients in the Dallas area. Led by Pyle, the team includes Sherhonda Dotson, Retail Sales Leader; Mario Hernadez, Financial Center Leader; Lynda Boschetti, Financial Center Supervisor; Sharm Patel, Universal Banker; and Quynh Wallace, Universal Banker.


Together, they provide comprehensive financial solutions tailored to individual and business needs. The grand opening celebration included a ribbon-cutting ceremony with local business leaders and community partners, underscoring Hancock Whitney’s commitment to fostering strong connections in the region.


In addition to expanding its physical presence in North Texas, Hancock Whitney remains committed to supporting the communities it serves. Recently, the bank donated $20,000 at the Little Elm ISD Education Foundation’s annual Derby Day. The premier community


‑


focused event benefits Little Elm ISD educator grants and student scholarships.


**About Hancock Whitney**


Since the late 1800s, Hancock Whitney has embodied core values of Honor & Integrity, Strength & Stability, Commitment to Service, Teamwork, and Personal Responsibility. Hancock Whitney offices and financial centers in Mississippi, Alabama, Florida, Louisiana and Texas offer comprehensive financial products and services, including traditional and online banking; commercial and small business banking; private banking; trust and investment services; healthcare banking; and mortgage services.
