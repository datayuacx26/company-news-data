# Socratix AI Selected for Circuit Accelerator Cohort 3

Through the program, we'll work directly with Circuit's Accelerator Advisory Council—C-suite leaders representing 31 credit unions across the network.


They will help us examine how we build, price, and deploy Socratix AI for credit unions. That means candid conversations about operational needs, implementation constraints, purchasing decisions, governance, and what it takes for new technology to earn trust inside a financial institution.


We're especially looking forward to learning from the teams handling fraud and compliance every day. Their experience gives us a clearer view of where investigations slow down, where current tools fall short, and where automation can make the greatest difference.
