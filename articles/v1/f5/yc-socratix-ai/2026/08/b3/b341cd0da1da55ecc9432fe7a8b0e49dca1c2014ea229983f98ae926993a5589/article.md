# How AtoB Delivered Instant Credit Decisions with Socratix AI

**AtoB** is a financial services platform for trucking businesses, providing fuel cards and working capital to help fleets operate and grow. The company processes hundreds of credit limit increase requests each month from customers seeking more working capital.


Every request requires a detailed underwriting review that goes beyond simple scoring—blending bank transactions, utilization trends, payment behavior, policy guardrails, and qualitative business judgment.
