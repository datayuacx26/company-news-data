# First Community Bank and Trust Reviews 100% of Its Checks for Fraud

Before Socratix, employees pulled check images from cold storage, selected comparison ranges, reviewed signatures and alterations, and rebuilt their baseline for each investigation. Decisions could vary with each reviewer's individual judgment.


Now, the check image, historical comparison, and narrative explanation arrive together in one view. Every flagged item has a consistent, definitive reason, while analysts retain the choice to follow or override the recommendation.


“


> It's really surprising how well it reads the imagery of the entire check. The handwriting comparisons by themselves are magical, and it gives you a narrative. I'm struck by descriptions like ‘hard pen stroke looping to the right,’ versus just saying ‘it's different.’ The system gives you a definitive reason as to why the item was flagged for additional review.


Tod Eastman


Chief Information Officer, First Community Bank and Trust


By handling the initial investigation and auto-clearing low-risk checks, Socratix gives the team more time for the smaller number of high-risk alerts that require human judgment and expertise.


The impact extends to customer service. In one case, a check written for **$1,100** had cleared for **$2,200** . The analyst contacted the customer, who was able to address the discrepancy immediately with the vendor. First Community now reaches out whenever a flagged check appears suspect, even when the issue is not fraud.
