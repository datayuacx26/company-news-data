# Socratix AI Selected for Curql Accelerate Cohort 4

We're excited to share that Socratix AI has been selected for Cohort 4 of **Curql Accelerate** , a national fintech accelerator focused on solving real challenges for credit unions.


Curql represents a collective of more than 160 credit unions. For its fourth accelerator cohort, it selected five companies working across fraud prevention, compliance, lending infrastructure, AI governance, and international payments. You can read[Curql's original cohort announcement](https://curql.com/press/curql-accelerate-welcomes-five-fintech-to-cohort-4) for the complete program details.
