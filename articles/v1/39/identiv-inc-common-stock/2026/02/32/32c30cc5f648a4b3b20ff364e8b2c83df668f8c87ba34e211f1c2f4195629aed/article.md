# Identiv Sets Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year 2025 Earnings Call for Thursday, March 12, 2026, at 5:00 PM EDT

SANTA ANA, Calif.


, Feb. 19, 2026


/PRNewswire/ --[Identiv, Inc.](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4623068-1&h=3965959138&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.identiv.com%2F&a=Identiv%2C+Inc.)
