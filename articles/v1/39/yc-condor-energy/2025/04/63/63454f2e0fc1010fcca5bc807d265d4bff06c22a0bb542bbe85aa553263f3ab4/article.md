# Tiburon Prospect

# Tiburon Prospect


- Extensive, stacked, fault-related trapping structures mapped on 2D seismic data.
- Deep-water clastic turbidite reservoirs in the Upper Zorritos with secondary potential in Heath Formation sands.
- Zorritos structure >100 km2. The Tiburon prospect offers the opportunity to not only test a large Zorritos resource but also target the potential for Heath reservoir to prove up a new play type.
- Positioned at a key migration focal point, receiving hydrocarbon charge from a proximal, oil mature source kitchen within the Heath Formation.
