# Piedra Redonda Gas Field

## 1 Tcf Discovered Gas Resource


One of the largest undeveloped offshore gas discoveries on the west coast of South America, strategically positioned to supply growing energy markets in Northern Peru and Southern Ecuador.


- **1 Tcf (2C)**
- **2.65 Tcf (3C)**
- **99% Methane**
- **4-10km Offshore**


### A Strategic Gas Resource


-


- Independently assessed by RISC Advisory
- Proven gas discovery with successful flow testing
- High-quality Mancora reservoir sands
- Multiple commercialisation pathways
- Located close to existing infrastructure and end markets
- Low-Cost Path to Commercialisation


### Low-Cost Path to Commercialisation


Piedra Redonda is located in shallow water close to shore, allowing a simple development concept with gas supplied directly to customers through a range of potential commercialisation pathways.

### Commercialisation Options


- Gas-to-Power: Scalable, low-cost solution leveraging existing transmission infrastructure.
- Pipeline Gas: Long-term supply to industrial and commercial users.
- CNG / Mini LNG: Flexible supply options for domestic and regional markets.
- LNG Export: Potential future export pathway.
- Securing a long-term gas sales agreement represents a key milestone in progressing Piedra Redonda towards development and future cash flow generation.

Commercialization Option Description Highlights


Gas-to-Power (GTP) Utilizing gas for local power generation. - Scalable - Low-cost - Quick to market


Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) Compressing gas for industrial and domestic use. - Flexible for local use - Low transportation cost


Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) or Mini LNG Converting gas into liquid form for export or transport. - High energy density - Suitable for international markets


Pipeline Distribution Connecting the resource to nearby industrial or domestic users via pipelines. -Long-term reliable supply -Low operational cost


Floating Liquified Natural Gas (FLNG) Using floating units to process and store LNG for distribution. - Flexibility in location - No need for extensive land-based infrastructure
