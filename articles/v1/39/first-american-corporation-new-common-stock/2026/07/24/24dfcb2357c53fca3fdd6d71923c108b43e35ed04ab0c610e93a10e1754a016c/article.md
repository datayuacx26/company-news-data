# First American Data & Analytics® Brings One of the Nation’s Largest Property Datasets to ArcGIS® for Decision-Ready GIS Workflows

GIS-ready property intelligence datasets link parcels, addresses, structures, ownership and transaction activity for site selection, infrastructure planning, risk analysis and other property-centric workflows


---


July 13, 2026, Santa Ana, Calif.


**[First American Data & Analytics](https://dna.firstam.com/)** , a premier provider of property intelligence, risk, valuation and data solutions and a division of First American Financial Corporation (NYSE: FAF), today announced that its industry-leading property intelligence datasets, the largest and most comprehensive in the nation, are now available within the ArcGIS® ecosystem from Esri®.


ArcGIS users can now access GIS-ready property intelligence—including nationwide parcel boundaries, ownership information, precise address points, sub address and unit address support, building footprints, sales and listings transaction intelligence, tax, valuation, land-use and market-activity data—delivered through web feature layers and other flexible delivery options.


“ArcGIS users are being asked to answer increasingly complex questions about property, land, infrastructure and market activity,” said Matt Key, vice president of property data at First American Data & Analytics. “Those decisions require more than parcel boundaries. They require trusted, current and connected property intelligence that reflects the real-world relationships between parcels, owners, buildings, addresses and transactions. By bringing First American Data & Analytics’ property data directly into ArcGIS, we are helping organizations turn GIS into a more complete decision-making environment.”


Organizations using ArcGIS to evaluate sites, plan infrastructure, manage land, assess risk or support investment decisions can now access authoritative property intelligence that is already normalized, connected and structured for GIS analysis. The GIS-ready property intelligence within ArcGIS connects parcels, addresses, structures, ownership and transaction activity through a common property identifier. By making authoritative property intelligence available within ArcGIS workflows, First American Data & Analytics enables organizations to access richer property context without disrupting the way they already work, helping them spend less time assembling fragmented datasets and more time making confident, informed decisions.


ArcGIS Property Intelligence is available through a modular suite of data offerings that enables organizations to build a trusted property foundation and extend it with specialized intelligence for site selection, land development, risk and underwriting and property operations.


First American Data & Analytics’ property intelligence for ArcGIS workflows is supported by the company’s deep property data foundation and decades of title, property and public records expertise. Developed through rigorous quality standards, transparent data-update methodologies, broad geographic coverage and validation by human experts, the data helps customers start with core property information and extend it with specialized intelligence for risk and underwriting; acquisition and property operations; and land and development analysis.


For more information about ArcGIS Property Intelligence, visit[https://dna.firstam.com/solutions/esri](https://dna.firstam.com/solutions/esri) . ****
