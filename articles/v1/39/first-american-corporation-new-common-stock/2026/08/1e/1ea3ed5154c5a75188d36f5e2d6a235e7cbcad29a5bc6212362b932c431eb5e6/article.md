# Docutech Executive Todd Sleight Named a 2026 HousingWire Insider Award Winner

Recognition highlights Sleight’s trusted leadership and impact across critical operations


---


August 4, 2026, Idaho Falls, Idaho


[Docutech](https://docutech.com/) ®, a leading provider of loan lifecycle document, eSign, eClose, and print fulfillment technology, announced today that Todd Sleight, vice president, division operations, was named among the winners of the 2026 HousingWire Insiders awards. Chosen by HousingWire’s selection committee, Insiders award winners are real estate professionals who are crucial to the success of their organizations and the visionaries driving their organizations to new heights.


“Todd knows this business inside and out. Where I have really seen him shine this year is through his leadership,” said Pam Forrester, senior vice president, division operations. “He listens first, asks thoughtful questions, and gives people the support they need to do their best work, earning him tremendous trust and respect across the organization. His leadership has strengthened our organization in countless ways, making this HousingWire Insiders recognition especially well deserved."


Sleight oversees critical document and servicing operations, including document generation, document management, lien releases, assignments, post-closing, and servicing processes. As his responsibilities have expanded, he has partnered closely with teams to create more efficient, streamlined workflows that improve quality and accuracy, while reducing turnaround times. Beyond driving operational improvements, Sleight has fostered a culture of continuous improvement across the organization. His ability to unite teams and thoughtfully guide change has strengthened collaboration, while advancing innovation across the organization.


"The best strategies in mortgage and real estate don't execute themselves — Insiders make them real,” said Sarah Wheeler, editor-in-chief at HousingWire. “These are the professionals who build the systems, run the processes, and carry the weight of organizational performance every single day. This year's class is exceptional, and HousingWire is proud to recognize the people who quietly power this industry." ****
