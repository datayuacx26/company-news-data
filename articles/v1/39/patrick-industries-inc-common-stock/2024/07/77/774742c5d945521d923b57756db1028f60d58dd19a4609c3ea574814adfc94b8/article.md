# Supplier of the Year Award


07/26/2024


Since 1972, the National Marine Distributors Association (NMDA), the largest marine distributors association in the boating industry, has recognized companies for excellence in supply chain, management, marketing, packaging, shipping and customer service.


This year, the Top Diamond Level Supplier of the Year Award was presented to Sea-Dog Line. As a leading supplier of more than 5,500 dock, deck and rigging hardware items, and 12-volt electrical/lighting and ventilation accessories, Sea- Dog also claimed the following awards:


• Diamond Level Marketing Award


• Diamond Level Supply Chain Efficiency Award


• Diamond Level Distributor Channel Support Award
