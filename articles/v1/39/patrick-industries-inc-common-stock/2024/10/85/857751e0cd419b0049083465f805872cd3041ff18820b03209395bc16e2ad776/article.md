# Transhield Defense Contract


10/07/2024


Transhield, a leader in protective cover solutions, has been selected as one of three awardees of a five-year, $50 million contract by the Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) to provide storage cover solutions for various DLA distribution centers, warehouses, and operations, along with other Department of Defense(DOD) sites. The contract aims to protect equipment from deterioration, improve logistics support, and reduce maintenance costs through cutting-edge technology and custom storage solutions.
