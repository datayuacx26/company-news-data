# Patrick and Care Camps Foundation Design Spaces of Hope


09/15/2024


Patrick is excited to announce that we’ve teamed up with Care Camps Foundation to make possible an upcoming episode of the popular home renovation show, Designing Spaces, which airs on Lifetime. Designing Spaces is known for its heartfelt and impactful renovations, and its special series, “Designing Spaces of Hope” amplifies this mission by focusing on projects that transform environments for those in need.


In the episode set to air September 18, the show will take viewers down to Camp Boggy Creek in Florida, a specialized retreat for children with serious illnesses. During this very special week, Care Camps Foundation funded a medically supervised camp for children battling cancer.


Thanks to the work of Care Camps Foundation, the camp offers a safe environment where children can experience the joys of camp life while receiving the care they need.


Our partnership in this episode underscores Patrick’s commitment to supporting Care Camps Foundation, enhancing the lives of children facing health challenges and empowering them to experience the joy, hope and healing of the outdoors.


Tune in September 18 at 7:30 AM on Lifetime TV, and join us in celebrating this wonderful initiative. The episode will re-air on September 25th at 7:30 AM as well.
