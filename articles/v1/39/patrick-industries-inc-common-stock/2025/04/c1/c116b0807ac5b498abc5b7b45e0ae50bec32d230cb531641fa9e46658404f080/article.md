# Patrick Industries Acquires Medallion, Enhancing Digital & Electronics Solutions


04/08/2025


Patrick Industries is excited to announce the acquisition of Medallion Instrumentation Systems, a premier provider of customized instrumentation and vehicle electronics for the marine, RV, powersports, on-highway, and military markets. This strategic move strengthens our ability to deliver advanced, integrated technology solutions that enhance performance, connectivity, and user experience.


Medallion’s engineering expertise in digital controls, lighting systems, integrated audio, sensors, wire harnessing, and touchscreen displays complements Patrick’s existing product offerings, allowing us to provide a seamless, full-technology solution tailored to our customers’ evolving needs.


Learn more about Medallion[here](https://www.medallionis.com/) .
