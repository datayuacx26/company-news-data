# Patrick Industries Expands Composite Offerings with Acquisition of Elkhart Composites


04/22/2025


Patrick Industries is proud to announce the acquisition of Elkhart Composites, Inc., a leading manufacturer of composite panel products. Elkhart Composites is known for providing durable, lightweight, and sustainable alternatives to traditional wood materials for RV sidewalls and ceilings.


With this strategic acquisition, Patrick strengthens its position as an industry leader in high-performance composite solutions and enhances our ability to deliver innovative materials that offer superior durability and efficiency to RV and Marine OEM customers.
