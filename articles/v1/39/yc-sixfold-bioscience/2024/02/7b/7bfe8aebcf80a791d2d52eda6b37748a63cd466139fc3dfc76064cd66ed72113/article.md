# Press Release Sixfold Bioscience to Present at SLAS 2024 Feb 6, 2024 Read more

SLAS is a global, non-profit professional community made up of scientists from academia, government and industry who are collectively focused on leveraging the power of technology to achieve scientific objectives.


Sixfold Bioscience team lead of Structure & Characterization, Dr. Alice Ghidini, will be presenting her talk on “‘Revolutionizing RNA Delivery: Harnessing the Body's Evolved Transport Pathways” in the 'Innovations in Analytical Techniques and Biophysical Tools for Biomolecular Characterization' session at 9.30 am EST on Wednesday 7th of February.
