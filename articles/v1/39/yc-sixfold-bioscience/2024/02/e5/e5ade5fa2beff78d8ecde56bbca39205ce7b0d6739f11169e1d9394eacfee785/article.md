# Press Release Søren Ottosen to Chair 5th RNAi-Based Therapeutics Summit Feb 10, 2024 Read more

The Summit will cover topics ‘Exposing Novel Approaches in R&D of the Next-Generation of RNAi Drugs’ and will feature leading speakers from the RNAi field, including: Alnylam, OliX, Sirnaomics, Abbvie and Dicerna.


Dr Ottosen will[chair](https://info.hansonwade.com/5th_RNAi-based_therapeutics_summit_official_brochure) day one and two of the summit:


- Day One, 8:55am: Chair’s Opening Remarks
- Day One, 5:00pm: Chair’s Closing Remarks & End of Conference Day One 5:00 pm
- Day Two, 8:50am: Chair’s Opening Remarks 8:50 am
- Day Two, 4:30pm: Chairs Closing Remarks & End of 5th RNAi Based Therapeutics Summit
