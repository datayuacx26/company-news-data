# Press Release Sixfold To Present at RNA Leaders Europe Feb 22, 2024 Read more

[RNA Leaders Europe](https://informaconnect.com/rna-leaders-europe/) is a leading conference focused on RNA delivery and therapeutics, bringing together leading companies in the space to discuss recent developments, as well as explore partnering and investment opportunities.


Sixfold Bioscience VP of Research, Dr Søren Ottosen, will be presenting his talk on “‘ **Mergo®: a scalable and programmable conjugation-based delivery system for short oligonucleotides** ” in the 'Delivery Showcase' session at Wednesday, 13 March 2024 12:35–12:50 CET (Central European Time, GMT+01:00).
