# Ethanol Free

#### May 6, 2026


## Ethanol Free


***By RFA President and CEO Geoff Cooper***


With the House set to vote next week on legislation allowing year-round E15 and making targeted reforms to the RFS small refinery exemption (SRE) program, anti-ethanol rhetoric and misinformation have reached a fever pitch. In recent days, groups like the Freedom Caucus[Foundation](https://x.com/FreedomCaucusF/status/2050265235097825336) , Small Refineries of America (a[front group](https://smallrefineriesofamerica.com/) for major refining companies like PBF, Delta, and HF Sinclair) and certain[members](https://x.com/repbrecheen/status/2049575972500848961?s=43&t=b9Jnx45Q8ZqyhCPVcDPBjg) of Congress have ramped up their inflammatory—and completely erroneous—attacks on E15, ethanol, and America’s farmers.


Enough is enough. It’s time to set the record straight.


Without a shred of credible evidence or analysis, the anti-E15 crowd suggests that simply *allowing* retail gas stations to *voluntarily* offer E15 would somehow lead to “higher gas prices” and restricting SREs to truly small refining companies would somehow result in “refinery closures” and “lost jobs.”


They’ve apparently adopted their motto from Chico Marx, who famously asked, “Who you gonna believe…me or your own lyin’ eyes?”

**Geoff Cooper**
