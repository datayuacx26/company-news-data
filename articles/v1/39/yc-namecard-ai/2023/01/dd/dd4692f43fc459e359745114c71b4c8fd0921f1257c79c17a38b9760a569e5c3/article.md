# Customer Stories: Trend Micro

#### Trend Micro, a global cybersecurity leader, helps make the world safe for exchanging digital information.


#### Introduction of Our Customer: Meet Trend Micro


Trend Micro, a global cybersecurity leader, helps make the world safe for exchanging digital information. Fueled by decades of security expertise, global threat research, and continuous innovation, Trend Micro’s cybersecurity platform protects 500,000+ organizations and 250+ million individuals across clouds, networks, devices, and endpoints.


*“I highly recommend Chainsight as a strategic partner for their reliability in web3 & blockchain security.”* by Paul Liu, the Director of Global Product, Marketing & Sales Enablement, Trend Micro


#### Understand Trend Micro’s Business Need


Trend Micro was first alerted to the rise of cryptocurrency due to the fact that many ransomware payments were being made using it. As the use of cryptocurrency and blockchain technology grows, there is an increasing demand for web3 safety measures from end users of cybersecurity services. The popularity of cryptocurrencies and non-fungible tokens (NFTs) as investment and payment options has led to a rise in digital asset transactions. This, in turn, has increased the need for solutions that can protect against potential risks and vulnerabilities of the web3 entities. Trend Micro, a leading cybersecurity firm, has recognized this growing demand and is working to provide innovative solutions to address the safety concerns of its end users in the web3 ecosystem.


#### Overcome the Challenges with Trend Micro


As a global cybersecurity leader, providing advanced threat defense techniques with faster detection and response is critical for Trend Micro. However, it was finding it difficult to detect web3-related risks among all the scattered data on blockchain.


The realm where blockchain and cryptocurrency are most vulnerable to deception is the Web2 world. Trend Micro frequently hears from cryptocurrency exchanges that numerous users fall victim to fraud due to browsing fake websites and being tricked by similar domain names when making transactions. These users hope that Trend Micro can assist in preventing such incidents. Furthermore, many companies engaged in cross-border e-commerce or those in need of conducting cross-border transactions are frequently misled by fraudulent websites.


#### How Chainsight Helped to Achieve Trend Micro’s Goals


Over the past year, Trend Micro’s information security team has engaged in negotiations with various suppliers offering Web3 security services. Upon comparison, Chainsight stands out for its precise application of technology and the ease of swiftly importing and integrating API.


Trend Micro’s first question to Chainsight is its ability to effectively warn and prevent risks associated with cryptocurrencies. Upon evaluation, the information security team discovered that the Chainsight solution is also compatible with Trend Micro’s antivirus software and can address compliance, security monitoring, and end-user protection in one solution.


Chainsight can provide solutions for collecting and identifying malicious addresses and URLs of web3 entities. Because its database tracks and analyzes both on-chain and off-chain data in real time with a high level of accuracy. The AI model can detect fraudulent domains and websites related to web3 scams. Chainsight also issues warnings for cryptocurrency wallet addresses with no transaction records, alerting users to potential risks that require caution. It can also provide exchange liquidity risk warnings during cryptocurrency exchange crises. These capabilities make Chainsight well-suited to blocking and preventing common cryptocurrency frauds in the Web2 world, aligning with Trend Micro’s product development plan. Therefore, Trend Micro and Chainsight started to integrate our products in 2022.

Some companies struggle to make progress after entering into a contract and beginning collaboration, but Chainsight is open to customer feedback and suggestions and quickly makes adjustments as time allows. They approach their work with dedication and strive to create superior products, remaining adaptable to our evolving needs. Chainsight is a company that can grow alongside its customers.
