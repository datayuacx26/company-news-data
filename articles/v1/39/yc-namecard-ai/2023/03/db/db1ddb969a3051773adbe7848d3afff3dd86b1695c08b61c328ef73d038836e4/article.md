# LinusTechTips Gets Hacked: A Wake-Up Call for Cybersecurity Awareness

Leonardo.ai


### Introduction


On March 23, the popular YouTube channel LinusTechTips fell victim to a hacking attack. The hackers hijacked the channel, changed the profile photo to Tesla’s logo, and started promoting crypto scams through interview videos featuring Elon Musk. Fortunately, the channel was taken down by Google after a few hours, and Linus Sebastian, the channel owner, quickly issued a statement on Floatplane. The channel is back online now, but still recovering from the incident.

### How did the hack potentially happen?


AK’s Tech Studio, another YouTuber, shared a similar experience that may offer some insights. As content creators, they often receive thousands of emails from potential commercial partners. AK mentioned that he had received an invitation to try out video editing software, which turned out to be a Trojan app designed to steal session cookies.


Session cookies are a tool the server records your login status. This allowed the hacker to take control of his channel, bypassing the security measure set up by Google and the user, such as passwords and 2FA.


The hacker hijacked AK’s youtube channel while leaving other personal information, such as credit card details and photos, untouched. This suggests that the hackers are specifically targeting YouTubers.

### Conclusion


This incident serves as a stark reminder that even the most tech-savvy individuals can fall prey to hackers. To protect yourself and your online presence, it is crucial to maintain a focus on cybersecurity. Here are some tips to help keep your accounts secure:


1. Use HTTPS on your entire site: Implementing SSL/TLS across your entire site will help encrypt all traffic, including session keys, and prevent sniffing attacks. HTTPS-everywhere is a common practice adopted by major banks and e-commerce systems for this reason.
2. Log out when you’re done: Always log out of websites after you’ve finished using them to minimize the risk of session hijacking.
3. Use anti-malware: Employ anti-malware software on both server-side and client-side machines to prevent cookie-stealing software from infiltrating your system.


In conclusion, the LinusTechTips hacking incident should serve as a wake-up call for everyone to prioritize cybersecurity. By adopting strong security practices, you can help protect yourself from similar attacks and safeguard your online presence.
