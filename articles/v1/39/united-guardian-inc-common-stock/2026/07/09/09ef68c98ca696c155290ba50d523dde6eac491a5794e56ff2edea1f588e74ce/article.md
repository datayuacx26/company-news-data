# United-Guardian Declares Cash Dividend

**United-Guardian (NASDAQ:UG)** announced that its Board of Directors declared a **cash dividend of $0.30 per share** at its July 14, 2026 meeting. The dividend will be paid on **August 4, 2026** to shareholders of record as of **July 28, 2026** .


According to United-Guardian, this represents a **20% increase** over the dividend paid in February and marks the **31st consecutive year** the company has paid a dividend. Management stated that the decision reflects the Board’s review of financial performance, expected expenses, and business initiatives, and its confidence in the company’s strategic direction.
