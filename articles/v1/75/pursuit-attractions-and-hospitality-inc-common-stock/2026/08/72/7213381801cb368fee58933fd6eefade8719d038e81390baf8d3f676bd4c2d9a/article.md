### Pursuit Announces First Major Organic Growth Investment at Tabacón Thermal Resort & Spa


August 4, 2026


*Three premium rainforest villas represent the first expansion since Pursuit acquired Tabacón in 2025, advancing the company's Vision 2030 growth strategy*


DENVER--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Pursuit Attractions and Hospitality, Inc. (NYSE: PRSU) today announced plans to develop three premium guest accommodation villas at Tabacón Thermal Resort & Spa, representing the first major organic growth investment at Tabacón since acquiring the award-winning Costa Rican resort in July 2025. The expansion reflects Pursuit's Vision 2030 strategy of creating long-term shareholder value through disciplined organic growth investments that enhance market-leading guest experiences, alongside strategic acquisitions in iconic destinations.


Located within Tabacón’s existing 570-acre resort property, the villas will help guests experience the resort at a deeper level and connect to Costa Rica's largest network of naturally flowing thermal springs, adjacent to the Arenal Volcano National Park protected rainforest reserve. With an anticipated opening in Summer 2027, each of the two-bedroom villas will span 2,250 square feet and feature a private plunge pool and an expansive outdoor terrace nestled within the rainforest.


The expansion enhances Tabacón’s premium offering and long-term value proposition, reinforcing Pursuit’s position in Costa Rica as affluent travelers increasingly seek high-end, nature-based experiences. Leveraging the strong international demand driven by Tabacón’s renowned thermal river attraction, the new accommodation concept directly addresses evolving guest preferences while supporting future growth.


Designed by acclaimed Costa Rican architecture firm Taller KEN, the villas embrace the same philosophy that has guided Tabacón since its founding: working with the rainforest rather than transforming it. Elevated on piles above the forest floor, the structures are designed to immerse guests in the sights, sounds and atmosphere of the surrounding landscape while minimizing their environmental footprint.


“Tabacón is one of the world's great nature-based hospitality experiences, and this investment responds to growing guest demand by expanding premium accommodation capacity in a way that enhances an already exceptional guest experience," said David Barry, president and chief executive officer of Pursuit. "It's exactly how we intend to grow our experiences: making thoughtful investments in exceptional assets that strengthen the guest experience, increase the long-term value of our properties and support sustainable growth for our shareholders.”


Barry added, "Just as importantly, we're committed to growing alongside the La Fortuna community and respecting the rainforest and thermal river that make Tabacón such an extraordinary place."


"We're seeing growing demand from guests who want more than exceptional accommodations, they want to feel completely immersed in the destination," said Andrey Gómez, general manager of Tabacón Thermal Resort & Spa. "These villas were created in response to that demand. They expand our premium offering while giving guests a more private, more personal way to experience the rainforest and thermal river that have made Tabacón one of Costa Rica's most iconic hospitality experiences."


Guided by Tabacón's Legacy of Regeneration and Pursuit's Promise to Place, the project has been designed to respect the surrounding landscape while supporting the resort's long-standing commitment to environmental stewardship and community partnerships. Tabacón also holds Costa Rica's Certification for Sustainable Tourism at the Elite level, the country's highest designation, and is a founding member of the La Fortuna Sustainability Alliance.

**About Tabacón Thermal Resort & Spa**


Located within a 570-acre rainforest property at the base of Arenal Volcano, Tabacón Thermal Resort & Spa is home to the only naturally flowing thermal river originating from the volcano. A member of Small Luxury Hotels of the World, Tabacón offers immersive hospitality where volcanic thermal waters, lush rainforest and personalized **** service come together in one of Costa Rica's most iconic natural settings.
