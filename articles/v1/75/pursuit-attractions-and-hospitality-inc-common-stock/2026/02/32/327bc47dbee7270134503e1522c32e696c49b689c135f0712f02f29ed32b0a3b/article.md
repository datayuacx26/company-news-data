### Pursuit Announces Date for Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Earnings Release and Conference Call


February 4, 2026


Conference Call to be Held at 5 p.m. Eastern Time on February 25, 2026


DENVER--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Pursuit Attractions and Hospitality, Inc. (“Pursuit” or the “company”) (NYSE: PRSU), announced today that it plans to release financial results for the quarter and year ended December 31, 2025 after market close on Wednesday, February 25, 2026 and will host a conference call at 5 p.m. Eastern Time on the same day to review its financial results and provide business updates.


A live audio webcast of the call will be available in listen-only mode through the["Events & Presentations"](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.pursuit.com%2Fevents-and-presentations%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54415879&newsitemid=20260204224132&lan=en-US&anchor=%26quot%3BEvents+%26amp%3B+Presentations%26quot%3B&index=1&md5=409ebbe63bccd4ae14d010e9dd540350) section of Pursuit’s website, where the company will also post its earnings press release and an earnings presentation prior to the call.


The live call can also be accessed by dialing (646) 844-6383 or (833) 470-1428 and entering the access code 736154. To avoid wait time and bypass speaking with an operator to join the call, participants can pre-register using the following registration link:[https://www.netroadshow.com/events/login/LE9zwo3iZNVZ4dr0dOJDb5PQv5OmMWwGfzf](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.google.com%2Furl%3Fq%3Dhttps%253A%252F%252Fwww.netroadshow.com%252Fevents%252Flogin%252FLE9zwo3iZNVZ4dr0dOJDb5PQv5OmMWwGfzf%26sa%3DD%26source%3Dcalendar%26ust%3D1770573120000000%26usg%3DAOvVaw32qSMBn6j52lZJxCKSKPiR&esheet=54415879&newsitemid=20260204224132&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.netroadshow.com%2Fevents%2Flogin%2FLE9zwo3iZNVZ4dr0dOJDb5PQv5OmMWwGfzf&index=2&md5=415238ea783541843f4fc927e0e9eb12) . A replay of the call will be available on Pursuit’s website shortly after the conference call and, for a limited time, by dialing (929) 458-6194 or (866) 813-9403 and entering the access code 258561.


**ABOUT PURSUIT**


Pursuit Attractions and Hospitality, Inc. (NYSE: PRSU) is an attractions and hospitality company that owns and operates a collection of inspiring and unforgettable experiences in iconic destinations in the United States, Canada, Iceland, and Costa Rica. Pursuit’s elevated hospitality experiences include world-class point-of-interest attractions and distinctive lodges, along with integrated restaurants, retail and transportation that enable visitors to discover and connect with stunning national parks and renowned global travel locations.
