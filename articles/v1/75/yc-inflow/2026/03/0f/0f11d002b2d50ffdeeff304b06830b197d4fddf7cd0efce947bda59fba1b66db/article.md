# 6 Best Alternatives to Talkspace for ADHD

If you've tried Talkspace, chances are you found it because traditional therapy felt impossibly difficult to access — long waitlists, scheduling that doesn't work around ADHD time blindness, and costs that add up fast.


Talkspace removes some of those barriers. The flexible scheduling, messaging between sessions, and the fact that you can join from your couch without commuting anywhere — those things genuinely matter for ADHD adults who've struggled with in-person therapy logistics.


But access to therapy isn't the same as ADHD management. A session once a week can help you understand your patterns. It can't be there on Tuesday afternoon when you've been staring at the same task for two hours, or on Thursday night when rejection sensitivity has flooded your entire emotional system.


This guide looks at what Talkspace offers, where the gaps are for ADHD specifically, and six alternatives that address different parts of the daily picture.

## What Is Talkspace?


[Talkspace](https://www.talkspace.com/) is a large online therapy and psychiatry platform connecting users with licensed therapists and psychiatric providers via video, phone, and messaging. It accepts many major insurance plans, which can significantly reduce the cost of access.


**At a glance:**


- **Best known for:** Accessible online therapy with insurance integration; messaging between sessions
- **Main use case:** Weekly therapy sessions for anxiety, depression, relationships, ADHD, and more
- **Designed for:** General adults seeking professional mental health support
- **Pricing:** ~$99/week self-pay. Standard insurance copay rates with many major plans.
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web
- **Note:** Talkspace acquired Wisdo (a peer support platform) in October 2025, adding a community dimension to its offering.


## Is Talkspace Good for ADHD?


Professional support with a good ADHD-informed therapist is genuinely valuable. The structural limitation is time.


### Where it actually helps


**Flexible scheduling and remote access.** For ADHD adults who've missed in-person appointments because of time blindness or avoidance, the ability to join from home on a flexible schedule makes attendance more realistic.


**Messaging between sessions.** One of Talkspace's differentiators is the ability to message your therapist between sessions — capturing thoughts, asking questions, or sharing what happened before the memory fades. This asynchronous format suits ADHD communication patterns well.


**Insurance integration.** Many major US insurance plans cover Talkspace, reducing or eliminating out-of-pocket cost. This makes professional therapy financially accessible to more ADHD adults.


### Where it falls short for ADHD


**One session a week is a small window.** ADHD challenges don't cluster around weekly appointments. The emotional dysregulation, task paralysis, and time blindness happen every day. A session can help you process what happened last week — but doesn't help in the moment.


**Therapist ADHD expertise varies.** Talkspace's network is large, which means quality and specialisation vary significantly. ADHD experience needs to be requested explicitly — it's not guaranteed by the matching algorithm.


**Therapy addresses patterns but not daily management skills.** Understanding why ADHD creates certain patterns is valuable. Building the daily habits, structures, and skills to manage them requires ongoing practice and tools that a weekly session can't fully support.

### 2. BetterHelp — Comparable Online Therapy, Worth Comparing on Cost


BetterHelp is the other major online therapy platform. It doesn't integrate with insurance as strongly as Talkspace — but its financial aid program and large therapist network make it worth comparing, particularly if you're currently paying self-pay for Talkspace.


For ADHD adults, the comparison between the two comes down to insurance coverage and therapist fit. Both require you to request ADHD experience during matching.


**Best for:** ADHD adults who want to compare online therapy options — particularly on price and therapist fit.


**Pricing:** ~$65–$100/week. Financial aid available. Insurance expanding in 2026.


**Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web.


### 3. Wysa — Free CBT Support for Every Day Between Sessions


Wysa is a free AI-powered mental health chatbot grounded in CBT and DBT. Always available, no scheduling — short conversational check-ins that address thought patterns and emotional regulation.


For ADHD adults who are in Talkspace sessions and struggling in the gaps between appointments, Wysa provides free daily support without any additional commitment.


**Best for:** ADHD adults in therapy who want free, always-available emotional support in the moments between sessions.

### 4. ADHD Online (Mentavi Health) — If Assessment or Medication Management Is Needed


If you need ADHD assessment or medication management alongside therapy, ADHD Online / Mentavi Health provides structured online assessment reviewed by licensed psychologists — typically within one to two weeks.


Talkspace therapy doesn't include psychiatric evaluation or medication management. ADHD Online fills that clinical gap if needed.


**Best for:** ADHD adults using Talkspace for therapy who also need formal assessment or prescription management support.


**Pricing:** $199 assessment. Follow-up appointments $175. No insurance.


**Platforms:** Web. Available in 40+ US states.


### 5. Finch — Low-Stakes Daily Self-Care Alongside Therapy


Finch is a self-care app built around a virtual pet bird. No clinical framework — just small, self-defined daily wellness goals with no judgment.


For ADHD adults in therapy who want a gentle daily touchpoint between sessions without the pressure of a structured tool, Finch provides a low-barrier way to show up for yourself every day.


**Best for:** ADHD adults who want a simple, non-clinical daily self-care habit alongside formal therapy.


**Pricing:** Free core version. Finch Plus ~$69.99/year.


**Platforms:** iOS, Android.


### 6. Headspace — A Daily Mindfulness Practice to Complement Therapy


Headspace is a structured meditation app that builds a daily mindfulness habit through short, guided sessions. For ADHD adults in therapy who want a daily emotional regulation practice to complement what they're working on with their therapist, Headspace's short structured sessions provide a consistent entry point.


**Best for:** ADHD adults in therapy who want a daily mindfulness practice to reinforce emotional regulation skills between sessions.


**Pricing:** $12.99/month or $69.99/year.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### Is Talkspace good for ADHD?


Yes, with an ADHD-experienced therapist. Talkspace's insurance integration and flexible formats make professional therapy more accessible for ADHD adults. Its limitations are the same as any weekly therapy service — it can't cover the daily management challenges that ADHD produces.


### Does Talkspace cover ADHD treatment?


Talkspace offers therapy and psychiatry services. Licensed therapists can provide ADHD-focused therapy, and the psychiatry service can evaluate and manage ADHD medication. Coverage depends on your insurance plan.


### Is Talkspace or BetterHelp better for ADHD?


Both are online therapy platforms with similar offerings. The comparison comes down to insurance coverage (Talkspace generally integrates more strongly), therapist fit, and cost. Both require you to specifically request ADHD experience during matching.
