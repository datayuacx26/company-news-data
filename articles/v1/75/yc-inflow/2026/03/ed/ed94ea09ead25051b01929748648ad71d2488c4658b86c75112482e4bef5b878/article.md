## What Is ADHD Online (Mentavi Health)?


[Mentavi Health](https://www.mentavihealth.com/) (formerly ADHD Online) provides online ADHD assessment through a structured questionnaire reviewed by licensed psychologists. Assessment results are typically returned within 24–48 hours.


**At a glance:**


- **Best known for:** Fast online ADHD assessment without long traditional waitlists
- **Main use case:** ADHD assessment and written diagnostic report for adults
- **Designed for:** Adults seeking accessible ADHD evaluation outside traditional psychiatric referral
- **Pricing:** $199 assessment. $175 follow-up appointments.
- **Platforms:** Web. Available in 40+ US states.
- **Important limitations:** Questionnaire-based only — no live clinical interview. Does not accept insurance directly. Does not prescribe medication directly.


## Is ADHD Online (Mentavi Health) Good for ADHD Assessment?


It fills a specific gap — fast, accessible assessment without a traditional referral pathway. Its limitations are important to understand before choosing it as your assessment route.


### Where it actually helps


**Speed.** Traditional ADHD evaluation routes in the US often involve 3–6 month waitlists. Mentavi Health's questionnaire-based assessment returns within 24–48 hours. For adults who've been waiting years to pursue a diagnosis and need something to start a clinical conversation, this timeline is meaningfully faster.


**Accessibility.** Available in 40+ states, online only, no commute or clinic visit required. Removes geographic and logistics barriers.


**Written report for physicians.** The assessment report is accepted by many physicians and pharmacists as documentation to support ADHD treatment conversations.


### Where it falls short


**Questionnaire-based only.** Traditional ADHD evaluation typically includes a clinical interview alongside questionnaire data — to identify comorbidities, gather developmental history, and distinguish ADHD from conditions with overlapping presentations (anxiety, depression, bipolar disorder, sleep disorders). A questionnaire-only assessment captures self-reported symptoms without the clinical context.


**No insurance.** The $199 assessment fee and $175 follow-up costs are out-of-pocket. For ADHD adults who are already managing financial executive dysfunction, significant upfront costs without insurance coverage is a real barrier.


**Does not prescribe medication directly.** Some ADHD adults looking for assessment also need medication management. Mentavi Health's assessment provides documentation — the prescription pathway still requires a separate prescribing provider.

### 2. Talkiatry — Full Psychiatric Assessment with Insurance


Talkiatry connects patients with board-certified psychiatrists via telehealth, accepting most major US insurance plans. For ADHD adults who want full psychiatric evaluation — with a live clinical interview, insurance coverage, and ongoing medication management in one provider — Talkiatry is a more clinically rigorous route than questionnaire-based assessment.


**Best for:** ADHD adults who want comprehensive psychiatric assessment with insurance coverage and ongoing medication management capability.


**Considerations:** Appointment timeline typically one to two weeks (compared to 24–48 hours for Mentavi). Availability varies by state and insurance plan.


**Pricing:** Standard insurance copay rates.


**Platforms:** Web, iOS.


### 3. BetterHelp — Therapeutic Support Alongside or Instead of Assessment


BetterHelp provides online therapy — not assessment or medication, but professional therapeutic support for the emotional challenges that ADHD produces. For ADHD adults who are uncertain whether they have ADHD and want to explore their difficulties with a professional before pursuing formal assessment, a therapist is often the appropriate first step.


**Best for:** ADHD adults who want professional support for ADHD-related emotional challenges — and who may not yet need or be ready for formal assessment.


**Pricing:** ~$65–$100/week. Financial aid available. Insurance expanding in 2026.


**Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web.


### 4. Circle Medical — Primary Care-Based ADHD Evaluation


Circle Medical is a telehealth primary care provider that handles ADHD evaluation and medication management as part of general primary care. For ADHD adults who want their ADHD assessment and treatment managed within a primary care context — rather than a separate specialist — Circle Medical's integrated approach is worth knowing.


**Best for:** ADHD adults who prefer their ADHD care within a primary care relationship rather than a specialist assessment service.


**Considerations:** Availability varies by state. Insurance accepted.


**Pricing:** Varies by insurance plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### Is ADHD Online (Mentavi Health) legitimate?


Yes. Mentavi Health provides structured ADHD assessment reviewed by licensed psychologists. The written report is accepted by many physicians and pharmacists. The main limitations are the questionnaire-based format (no live clinical interview), out-of-pocket cost, and no direct medication prescribing.


### How much does ADHD Online (Mentavi Health) cost?


The initial assessment costs $199. Follow-up appointments cost $175. Insurance is not accepted directly. There is no free tier.


### Can you get an ADHD diagnosis online?


Yes, through platforms like Mentavi Health, Talkiatry, and Circle Medical. The level of clinical rigor varies significantly — from questionnaire-based assessment to full clinical interviews with board-certified psychiatrists. Insurance coverage and timeline also vary by provider.
