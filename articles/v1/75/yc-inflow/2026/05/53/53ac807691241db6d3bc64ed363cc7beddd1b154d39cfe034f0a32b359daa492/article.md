## What Is BetterHelp?


[BetterHelp](https://www.betterhelp.com/) is one of the largest online therapy platforms globally. It connects users with licensed therapists via video, phone, and messaging — removing the barriers of in-person care like geography, scheduling, and waiting lists.


**At a glance:**


- **Best known for:** Accessible online therapy with licensed therapists; large provider network
- **Main use case:** Weekly therapy sessions for anxiety, depression, relationships, ADHD, and more
- **Designed for:** General adults seeking professional mental health support
- **Pricing:** ~$65–$100/week ($280–$400/month). Financial aid available. Insurance coverage expanding as of 2026.
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web


## Is BetterHelp Good for ADHD?


Yes — with the right therapist and the right expectations. It's professional support. The challenge is structural, not qualitative.


### Where it actually helps


**Access to real, licensed therapists.** For ADHD adults who've struggled to find an ADHD-informed therapist locally, BetterHelp's large network increases the odds of finding someone who gets it. You can request ADHD experience during matching.


**Messaging between sessions.** Unlike in-person therapy, you can message your therapist between sessions — useful for ADHD adults who process things asynchronously, or who need to capture a thought before it disappears.


**Multiple communication formats.** Video, phone, or text — accommodating different ADHD communication preferences and energy levels.


### Where it falls short for ADHD


**One session a week doesn't cover ADHD management daily.** ADHD challenges — task paralysis, emotional dysregulation, time blindness — happen every day. A weekly session can help you understand patterns. It doesn't give you tools in the moment when those patterns are active.


**ADHD expertise isn't guaranteed.** You have to request it. Matching doesn't automatically prioritise ADHD-informed therapists, and quality varies significantly by provider.


**Cost is a real barrier.** At $280–$400/month without insurance, BetterHelp is a significant ongoing expense. For ADHD adults who also struggle with financial management, this needs careful consideration.

### 2. Talkspace — Online Therapy with Stronger Insurance Integration


Talkspace is BetterHelp's closest direct competitor — online therapy via video, phone, and messaging. Its key differentiator in 2026 is stronger insurance integration, which can significantly reduce out-of-pocket cost.


For ADHD adults who've found BetterHelp's cost prohibitive, Talkspace is worth comparing on insurance coverage specifically.


**Best for:** ADHD adults who want online therapy and have insurance that may cover more with Talkspace than BetterHelp.


**Considerations:** Therapist quality varies, same as BetterHelp. Request ADHD experience specifically during matching.


**Pricing:** ~$99/week self-pay. Standard copay rates with insurance.


**Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web.


### 3. Wysa — Free CBT Support Between Sessions


Wysa is a free AI-powered mental health chatbot grounded in CBT and DBT principles. Available any time without scheduling — short conversational check-ins that surface and work through emotional patterns.


If you're in therapy but struggling in the gaps between sessions, Wysa is what you reach for on a difficult Wednesday when Friday's appointment feels far away.


**Best for:** ADHD adults in therapy who want free, always-available emotional support between scheduled sessions.


**Considerations:** General mental health content — not ADHD-specific. Not a replacement for professional care.


**Pricing:** Free core tier.


**Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web.


### 4. ADHD Online (Mentavi Health) — If You Need Assessment First


ADHD Online (now Mentavi Health) provides accessible online ADHD assessment — typically within one to two weeks, significantly faster than traditional psychiatric evaluation. The assessment is questionnaire-based with psychologist review, and the written report is accepted by physicians and pharmacists.


For ADHD adults who haven't been diagnosed yet and aren't sure what kind of support they actually need, assessment may need to come before therapy.


**Best for:** ADHD adults who suspect they have ADHD but haven't been assessed — and need a diagnosis before pursuing treatment options.


**Considerations:** Does not accept insurance directly. Assessment fee is $199. Questionnaire-based — no live clinical interview included.


**Pricing:** $199 assessment. Follow-up appointments $175.


**Platforms:** Web. Available in 40+ US states.


### 5. Finch — Low-Stakes Daily Emotional Check-Ins


Finch is a self-care app built around a virtual pet bird. No therapy, no clinical framework — just gentle daily self-care goals with no judgment for missed days.


For ADHD adults who feel overwhelmed by the idea of weekly sessions, financial commitment, and performance expectations — Finch provides a low-barrier starting point for daily emotional self-awareness.


**Best for:** ADHD adults who need the gentlest possible daily self-care routine before they're ready for more structured support.


**Pricing:** Free core version. Finch Plus ~$69.99/year.


**Platforms:** iOS, Android.


### 6. Headspace — For Building Daily Calm Alongside Therapy


Headspace is a structured meditation app — not therapy, but a useful companion to it. A daily mindfulness practice can help build emotional regulation capacity over time, which makes therapy sessions more productive.


For ADHD adults in BetterHelp sessions who want a daily practice to complement what they're working on with their therapist, Headspace's structured courses provide a clear starting point.


**Best for:** ADHD adults already in therapy who want a structured daily practice to reinforce what they're working on — not as a replacement, but as a complement.


**Pricing:** $12.99/month or $69.99/year.


**Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web.
