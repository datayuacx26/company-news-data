# Introducing GladeCore: AI-Powered Game Development Platform

## Welcome to the Future of Game Development


Today marks an exciting milestone for our team and the entire game development community. We're thrilled to announce the launch of GladeCore, a revolutionary platform that brings AI-powered characters and voice control to game development.


## What is GladeCore?


GladeCore is a platform that provides an on-device AI model used to create intelligent AI characters, or dynamically generate story, quest and other content.


## Core Features


- •


**LLM-Powered Dialogue** : Local LLM inference to generate dynamic NPC responses


- •


**Zero per Interaction Cost and Low Latency** : Runs fully offline - infinitely scalable with no per-use fees


- •


**Speech-to-Text (STT)** : Players can talk naturally to NPCs via microphone


- •


**Text-to-Speech (TTS)** : NPCs can respond aloud using ElevenLabs API or local TTS


- •


**Data-Driven Personalities** : Define NPC backstories, traits, and voices with Data Assets - no code changes required


- •


**Multiplayer Ready** : Multiplayer support for Pro and Enterprise tiers

## Why We Built This


Game development has always been about pushing boundaries and creating immersive experiences. With the rise of AI technology, we saw an opportunity to make these advanced capabilities accessible to developers of all skill levels.


Our team has spent years researching and developing the core technologies that power GladeCore. We've focused on creating a platform that's not only powerful but also intuitive and easy to integrate into existing workflows.


## What's Next?


This is just the beginning. We have an ambitious roadmap that includes advanced AI training tools, more personality presets, and expanded platform support. We're committed to continuously improving GladeCore based on community feedback and emerging technologies.


We invite you to join us on this journey. Whether you're a seasoned developer or just starting out, GladeCore is designed to help you bring your creative vision to life.
