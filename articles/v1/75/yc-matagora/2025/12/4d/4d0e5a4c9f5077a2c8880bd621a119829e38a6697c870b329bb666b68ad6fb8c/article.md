# Thinking About Migrating to Shopify? Here’s What You Need to Know Before You Switch

If you’re thinking about moving your ecommerce and point-of-sale operations to Shopify, you’re already on the right path. A unified commerce platform can transform how you sell, operate, and grow—but migrating both your online store **and POS** comes with challenges.


At Matagora, we specialize in **fully managed, white-glove Shopify migrations** for SMBs, ensuring your store, inventory, customer data, and hardware all move seamlessly. Here’s what you need to know before making the switch.


## Why Migrating POS + Ecommerce Is More Complex Than You Think


Moving to Shopify isn’t just “export your data, import it into Shopify.” A successful migration must account for:


-


Maintaining **complete order history**


-


Preserving your **product catalog** without duplicates


-


Keeping **barcodes, inventory, and variants** accurate and functional


-


Migrating **customer profiles, loyalty points, and historical data**


-


Setting up **POS hardware and workflows** for in-store operations


Mistakes in any of these areas can create messy dashboards, broken barcodes, lost data, or duplicated products—issues that can affect **sales, operations, and customer trust** .
