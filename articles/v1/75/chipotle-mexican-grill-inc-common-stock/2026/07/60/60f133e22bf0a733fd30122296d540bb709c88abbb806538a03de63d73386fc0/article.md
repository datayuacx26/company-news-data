# CHIPOTLE CELEBRATES NATIONAL AVOCADO DAY WITH FREE CHIPS AND GUAC

- For the first time ever, Chipotle is pairing free chips with its National Avocado Day guac offer and extending the celebration to two days
- Digital guests can score free chips and guac with an entrée purchase on July 31 and August 1 using code AVO2026 on the Chipotle app and[Chipotle.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4739055-1&h=3583881866&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.chipotle.com%2F&a=Chipotle.com) 1


NEWPORT BEACH, Calif.


, July 27, 2026


/[PRNewswire](http://www.prnewswire.com/) / -- Chipotle Mexican Grill (NYSE: CMG) today announced it is celebrating National Avocado Day with one of its biggest National Avocado Day offers yet. For two days, July 31 and August 1, guests can receive free chips and guac with the purchase of an entrée, exclusively on the Chipotle app and[Chipotle.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4739055-1&h=3583881866&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.chipotle.com%2F&a=Chipotle.com) using code AVO2026 at checkout.1


"Our hand-mashed guac and housemade chips are an iconic Chipotle pairing, and this year we're going bigger than ever for National Avocado Day," said **Stephanie Perdue, Senior Vice President of Brand Marketing at Chipotle.** "For the first time, we're giving fans two full days to celebrate with free chips and guac with the purchase of an entrée, because one day simply isn't enough for something this extra."


Chipotle's guac is hand-mashed throughout the day in restaurants. Each batch is made with 48 whole, ripe Hass avocados, which are revered for their rich, creamy texture. Whether enjoyed on the side, added to an entrée, or scooped with chips, guac is one of Chipotle's most iconic menu items.


**Behind the Scenes at a California Avocado Farm**
To highlight how Chipotle sources avocados for its fresh guac, the company is teaming up with brand superfans[Leah Kateb](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4739055-1&h=3526555651&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fleahkateb%2F%3Fhl%3Den&a=Leah+Kateb) and[Miguel Harichi](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4739055-1&h=887073846&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fmiguel_harichi%2F%3Fhl%3Den&a=Miguel+Harichi) for new social content that takes fans behind the scenes at one of the California farms where Chipotle sources its avocados. Debuting on July 30, the day before National Avocado Day, the video follows Leah and Miguel as they tour the farm, harvest ripe avocados and visit a Chipotle restaurant to learn how the brand prepares its iconic guac. Known for spending time in their garden, Leah and Miguel share an appreciation for the brand's commitment to sustainable sourcing and get a firsthand look at the fruit's journey from orchard to restaurant.

**When is National Avocado Day 2026 and what is Chipotle offering?**
National Avocado Day is celebrated each year on July 31. In 2026, Chipotle is making the celebration bigger than ever by offering free chips and guac with the purchase of a full-priced entrée by using code AVO2026 at checkout on the Chipotle app or Chipotle.com over two days: July 31 and August 1.


**How is Chipotle celebrating National Avocado Day 2026?**
For two full days, July 31 and August 1, 2026, guests can receive free chips and guac with the purchase of a full-priced entrée by using code AVO2026 at checkout on the Chipotle app or Chipotle.com.


**How do I get free chips and guac from Chipotle on National Avocado Day?**
To redeem the offer, guests must place a digital order through the Chipotle app or Chipotle.com, add one full-priced entrée and one regular side of chips and guacamole to their order, and enter code AVO2026 at checkout.


**How is Chipotle’s National Avocado Day 2026 offer different from National Avocado Day 2025?**
For National Avocado Day 2026, Chipotle is offering free chips and guacamole with the purchase of a full-priced entrée on July 31 and August 1 when guests order through the Chipotle app or Chipotle.com using code AVO2026 at checkout.


**What are the ingredients in Chipotle’s guacamole?**
Chipotle’s guacamole is made fresh throughout the day in its restaurants using six real ingredients: Hass avocados, red onion, jalapeño, cilantro, citrus juice and salt. Chipotle does not use preservatives or artificial flavors in its guac.


**Where can I redeem Chipotle's National Avocado Day offer?**
The offer is available only on eligible digital orders placed through the Chipotle app or Chipotle.com. It is not valid for orders placed in restaurants, through third-party delivery marketplaces or through Chipotle Catering.


**Where does Chipotle get its avocados?**
Chipotle primarily sources avocados from Mexico and California. Chipotle only uses Hass avocados because they're the creamiest, most delicious of all avocado varieties. Chipotle hand-mashes its guac in restaurant kitchens rather than serving a pre-packaged product.


**Is Chipotle’s food safe to eat?**
Chipotle does not serve iceberg lettuce in its restaurants, and its romaine and Supergreens salad blend are sourced in the U.S., not Mexico. Chipotle is continuing to follow the Cyclospora investigation closely and will evaluate any new information and take appropriate action.
