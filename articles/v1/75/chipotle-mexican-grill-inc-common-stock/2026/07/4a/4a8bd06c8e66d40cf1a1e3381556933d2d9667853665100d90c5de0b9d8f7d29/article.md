# CHIPOTLE BRINGS REAL-WORLD FOOD REWARDS TO PGA TOUR 2K25 IN FIRST-OF-ITS-KIND QUEST INTEGRATION

- Chipotle’s new *PGA TOUR 2K25* Quests will give players the chance to earn free food and unlock exclusive in-game gear1
- The integration launches today with Season 7 of *PGA TOUR 2K25*
- Chipotle will also drop a limited-edition Chipotle fork-inspired pitch mark repair tool for use on the course2


NEWPORT BEACH, Calif., July 8, 2026 – Chipotle Mexican Grill today announced that it will become the first brand to offer real food rewards in *PGA TOUR 2K25* , allowing players to earn free entrees, double protein offers and exclusive in-game gear tied directly to their in-game performance. 1

**Real Rewards on the Virtual Course**
Launching today with Season 7 of *PGA TOUR 2K25* , Chipotle will introduce a series of challenges within MyCAREER mode that reward players based on their gameplay. Players can compete in the Chipotle Hot Streak Quest, featuring three Chipotle-themed chapters – “Fairway to Foil,” “Built Different,” and “Drive for the Double Protein” – to earn rewards ranging from exclusive in-game gear to free double protein offers and free entrees. 1


They can also unlock a collection of exclusive Chipotle-inspired gear available only in PGA TOUR 2K25, including a taco-head “Chi-Putt-Le” putter, foil-inspired golf pants, and a Chipotle pepper bucket hat and polo shirt.


"Whether they're watching tournaments, playing a weekend round or competing in PGA TOUR 2K25, golf fans are finding new ways to engage with the sport,” said **Stephanie Perdue, Senior Vice President of Brand Marketing at Chipotle** . “This partnership allows us to meet them where they're already spending time and reward their passion with experiences that extend from the game into the real world."


**From Fork Lore to Real Fairway Gear**
Chipotle is also introducing an IRL limited-edition collectible Chipotle fork-inspired pitch mark repair tool that doubles as both a golf accessory and dining utensil. Inspired by the cult-like following of Chipotle's iconic black fork, the limited-edition pitch mark repair tool transforms one of the brand's most beloved cultural icons into a functional golf accessory that fans can use on the course. Through the “Chipotle Golf Collectible Sweepstakes,” Chipotle Rewards members can exchange 10 points for a chance to win a pitch mark repair tool from today until July 22. 2

**Chipotle's Growing Role in Golf**
The integration builds on Chipotle's expanding presence in golf, including its designation as the Official Mexican Restaurant of the[PGA TOUR](https://newsroom.chipotle.com/2026-01-07-PGA-TOUR,-CHIPOTLE-ANNOUNCE-NEW-PARTNERSHIP) , sponsorship of Hot Streak presented by Chipotle, partnerships with PGA TOUR players Max Homa, Maverick McNealy and David Ford, and the sellout success of the[Chipotle x Cobra Foil-Wrapped Burrito Head Cover.](https://newsroom.chipotle.com/2026-05-05-MAX-HOMA-BRINGS-BACK-VIRAL-CHIPOTLE-X-COBRA-BURRITO-HEADCOVER-TO-THE-PGA-TOUR)
