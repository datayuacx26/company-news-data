# Case Study - Bolt Threads: Optimizing Biomaterials R&D

Bolt Threads is a synthetic biology company developing sustainable biomaterials. From silks to leather alternatives, Bolt is engineering novel fabrics for our daily lives. In order to achieve their goals, Bolt Threads uses Benchling as a "universal source of truth" for their R&D platform. Bolt's former software solutions were inflexible, disconnected from one another, and time intensive to use. This slowed their overall progress and left their data scattered. Today with Benchling, scientists at Bolt Threads centralize all of their data, collaborate on project management, and can innovate and flexibly iterate on their R&D process.


Read the case study to learn more about how Bolt uses Benchling to gain deeper process insights, drive efficiency, and streamline operations.
