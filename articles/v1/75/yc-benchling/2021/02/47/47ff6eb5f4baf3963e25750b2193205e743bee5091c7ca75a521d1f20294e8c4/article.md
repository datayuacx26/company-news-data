# Benchling for Education: How software is preparing the next generation of scientists

During a time of unprecedented changes to education across the globe, we’re proud to announce our newest initiative,[Benchling for Education](https://www.benchling.com/educators) . As schools and universities continue to adopt remote teaching methods, the tools educators use for their courses will need to evolve as well. Today's global crises require advanced scientific solutions, so preparing students for STEM careers has never been more important. Benchling offers a free virtual solution – which only requires an email address and a computer with an internet connection – for biology teachers and their students.


Benchling provides a safe, engaging platform for students to explore and learn. Students can emulate how real scientists operate by using an[electronic lab notebook](https://www.benchling.com/notebook) and designing biological experiments with Benchling. Best of all, teachers can feel confident that their students are learning practical skills that can be applied to careers in science, medicine, and more.


> Benchling is a powerful tool to support the bioinformatics work we do in our course. We use it for depositing student homework and to conveniently catalog and create portfolios of student work during the semester. - John Bean & Michael Edgar, Science Faculty at Milton Academy (Milton, MA)


Benchling is a platform designed for modern scientists, and we strive to make it accessible to a variety of audiences. For educators, we’ve created this[Training Kit](https://help.benchling.com/hc/en-us/sections/6504315216525) to show you the basics and how to set up and organize your class on Benchling. For students, we’ve developed[self-serve lessons](https://help.benchling.com/hc/en-us/sections/10625480971277-Students) that allow them to practice designing experiments about PCR, cloning, and even CRISPR with Benchling. These resources don’t require prior experience with Benchling and can be applied to any foundational biology course.

Benchling’s wide range of applications can help teachers and students maximize its usage in the classroom and minimize the use of multiple tools. We’ve seen Benchling used to supplement activities such as lectures, take-home assignments or lab work. As a cloud-based platform, Benchling is also flexible for in-person or distance learning to comply with local regulations that may quickly change. You can check out our guide to[adapting lab courses for virtual learning](https://www.benchling.com/adapting-lab-courses-for-virtual-learning/) .
