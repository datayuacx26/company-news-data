# How to Run an Informatics Platform Evaluation

Evaluating an informatics platform for your R&D organization is no small task. The needs of your organization are complex, and making the wrong decision costs you time, resources, and an advantage over your competitors. Especially if you haven’t run an informatics platform evaluation before, you might not know where to start.


This document should serve as a guide to help you structure your evaluation process. Going into an evaluation with deliberate timelines, explicit organizational needs, and involvement from all relevant stakeholders will help ensure that you make the right decision.
