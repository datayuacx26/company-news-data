# LIMS migration doesn't have to be challenging

Migrating to a new laboratory information management system (LIMS) is one of the most challenging tasks IT professionals and LIMS administrators undertake. But it doesn’t have to be.


Transitioning to a new LIMS and making “simple” system upgrades are typical events for biopharma companies as they grow. However, the task is commonly seen as a complex and time-consuming process that threatens productivity, project deadlines, and budgets.


We are here to let you know that you can achieve a smooth and painless transition when migrating to a new LIMS—and the best way to do this is by partnering with a seasoned LIMS migration expert. Here, we discuss the reasons why you should consider choosing a LIMS vendor with migration expertise, such as Benchling, and what to expect when you do.


## LIMS migration partners will come with a plan to make the transition efficient


LIMS migrations are challenging when teams are misaligned, risks are underestimated, and needs and success metrics are ill-defined. Without a solid plan, chaos can quickly ensue—pushing the LIMS launch date back and causing confusion for teams and stakeholders. Migration experts will come with a plan of action that is proven to produce a successful, efficient, and sustainable LIMS deployment where everyone is on the same page and eager to use the new system. And with their experience, they will help you foresee, overcome, and mitigate technical and logistical challenges.


A LIMS migration expert will develop a thoughtful plan of action that establishes a dedicated project team with clear roles and responsibilities, defines the different stages of the LIMS migration, and enables flexibility to quickly respond to the needs and feedback of your scientific teams. As you refine the plan of action with your migration expert, they’ll aim to secure business and IT executive sponsorship and bring everyone to an agreement on a governance structure and success metrics. This will help keep people aligned on a structure and common goal and also bring a high level of transparency and organization for project tracking and coordination.


What’s more, a migration expert will work with you to identify which teams and use cases have the highest-priority needs for your LIMS, and they will structure a detailed roll-out plan with clearly defined phases, deliverables, and checkpoints. Because every migration is different, this may require detailed discovery sessions with your R&D teams to fully understand their end-to-end processes, analytics needs, and any other specific requirements. It is equally important to involve IT teams early in the planning phase because it will allow the experts to scope out a thorough data migration plan with APIs for any systems that your LIMS is replacing or integrating with.


When evaluating your LIMS vendor, ask them about their adoption rates, look for success stories in organizations similar to yours, and review the vendor’s[implementation methodology](https://www.benchling.com/resources/benchling-enterprise-implementation-methodology/) if they have one.

## LIMS migration partners will fit the tools to your workflow and your needs


Modern life science research involves many teams using many instruments and software. This creates a complex ecosystem that a LIMS (ideally) can fully integrate with to help you manage samples, complex molecules, and diverse projects. However, this connectivity can end up requiring tricky configurations with legacy LIMS that take time to get right. And oftentimes, it is never *exactly right* or takes terribly long to get there.


Partnering with an expert that has successfully integrated LIMS into an environment similar to yours offers many resource-saving benefits. First and foremost, the migration expert will help you choose the right LIMS for your workflow and ecosystem, so you can have confidence that you won’t need to replace the system in a few years. Second, they'll support you through integrations, configurations, and migrations to get you the functionality you need quickly. Third, they'll help you assess user acceptance and adjust the functionality prior to launching it in the production environment. This last point is important because it bolsters the adoption of the new system (i.e. your teams will want to use it), and the configurations will be optimized in a testing environment to avoid productivity disruptions.


Because every migration is unique, you can expect your partner to thoroughly review your scientific processes, work closely with your team to understand your specific integration requirements, and deliver your R&D organization’s need-to-have and nice-to-have functions.

## LIMS migration partners will make sure all your data is migrated and secure


Realizing your most valuable resource—your data—has been lost or riddled with errors is a nightmare that can set your project back and make you wonder if anything has been lost. This is all too common of a concern for life science companies migrating to a new[LIMS system](https://www.benchling.com/lims-software) . Partnering with a migration expert will help you avoid this risk and ensure your data will be intact, safe, and efficiently migrated.


During a LIMS migration, data must be extracted, translated, and transferred into a new location. You can choose to archive the data, transfer it to a new repository where you can easily revisit it, or fully migrate it into the new system so your organization can actively mine, analyze, and run reports on it.


Typically, data migration involves a combination of manual processes and automated programmable migration tools. Ideally, your new LIMS platform will support a wide variety of integration and ETL use cases, such as automatically syncing with other informatics tools and databases and ingesting data from instruments like liquid handlers and freezers.


Your LIMS migration partner will help you worry less and transfer confidently. They'll work with you to determine where and how the data will be transferred, prepare the data for migration (e.g., convert data to JSON), and validate that the data is accurate and has been transferred properly, not to mention is secure within the new system.

## LIMS migration partners will help your IT team become well-versed in the new system


It’s one thing to get your LIMS properly set up, but what happens when you need to make a system update or when you adopt a new instrument and need to reconfigure your software? Ideally, your team will be able to quickly make the adjustments themselves—without needing to take the time to reach out to the vendor’s technical support team and waiting for a response. To empower your IT team to manage the new system, the LIMS needs to be inherently straightforward to change and update; and the IT team needs to have the confidence and skills to do it.


While integrating and optimizing your LIMS, your migration partner should also train designated IT stakeholders on how to adjust configurations in the future so that they can swiftly and independently make the changes that your evolving workflows require.
