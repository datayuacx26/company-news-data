# Drive data-powered decisions with Benchling analytics integrations

On any given day, scientists are hopping among multiple systems and point solutions to review, input, and analyze data, resulting in inefficient workflows and costly errors for R&D companies. The expanding collection of instruments used by R&D teams complicates matters, producing massive amounts of data in proprietary formats.


These silos and lack of instrument integration have significant consequences. Benchling data reveals that scientists spend an average of 18 hours per week on cumbersome logistical data tasks, such as data capture, search, sample traceability, collation, and tracking scientific requests. 50% of scientists report that they struggle to access the insights they need to do their work.*


Solving this problem demands a central place where R&D teams can reference, integrate, and analyze their data — and that’s why we’re excited to introduce new Benchling analytics integrations.

### Standardized data


Benchling is extending the scope and reach of FAIR data best practices by creating a new[open source framework and library of converters](https://www.benchling.com/news/benchling-open-source-library-lab-instrument-data-converters) that automatically formats instrument-generated data to the Allotrope Simple Model (ASM) standard. Standardization is the first step to eliminating the manual effort of processing, integrating, securing, and managing instrument data used for scientific R&D.


### Analytics integrations with codeless dataset creation


Instead of writing code to organize and analyze data, scientists can point-and-click to create datasets from Registry or Result schemas. Then, they can easily push those datasets into analytics tools (like JMP and Pluto) and pull back their analyzed data, results, and charts directly into Benchling. Plus, scientists can directly upload results via Benchling’s API (in JSON format) to Notebook entries. Using these analytics integrations, R&D teams can quickly review, share, and take action on the data and results they’re generating.


### Streamlined workflows


With more standardized data, and more powerful analytics integrations (with JMP and Pluto), scientists can drive faster decision-making and better insights, helping them to accelerate their R&D timelines.
