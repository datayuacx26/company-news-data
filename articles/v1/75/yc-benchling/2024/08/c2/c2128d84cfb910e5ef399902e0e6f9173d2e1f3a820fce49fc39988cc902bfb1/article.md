# Benchling Convenes Global Community of R&D Scientists and Technology Leaders at Benchtalk

**San Francisco, CA and Boston, MA. August 27, 2024.**[Benchling](https://www.benchling.com/) , the R&D Cloud powering the biotech industry, announced the full agenda and speaker lineup for[Benchtalk](https://benchtalk.benchling.com/boston24/) , its annual industry conference taking place on October 1-2 in Boston. Benchtalk brings together key players in the tech-driven life sciences ecosystem, including R&D scientists, IT leaders, data scientists, and developers. Attendees will explore strategies on the topics shaping the future of biotech, from AI/ML integration and data governance to lab automation and scaling digital infrastructure.


This year’s two-day event features sessions led by top biotech and biopharma companies, including the following highlights:


-


**Eli Lilly,** advancing AI/ML through a focus on data quality.


-


**Merck and Sanofi,** driving a modern IT strategy to enhance data governance and digital transformation.


-


**Bristol Myers Squibb,** customizing tools to fit your science with practical tips from a developer’s journey.


-


**MacroGenics,** unifying process development workflows to meet the new scale and demands on development.


-


**Tango Therapeutics,** leveraging advancements in data visualization and reporting to turn data into actionable insights.


-


**Leading early-stage biotech companies,** navigating through scientific, personnel, and technical uncertainty to achieve successful system adoption.


Benchling CEO Sajith Wickramasekara will kick off the event with a keynote, presenting an exclusive first-look demo of the new Benchling Notebook and showcasing the latest advancements in the Benchling R&D Cloud. The agenda features a fireside chat with[Matthai Mammen](https://fogpharma.com/team/mathai-mammen-md-phd/) , CEO, Chairman, and President of FogPharma and former EVP of Pharmaceuticals R&D at Johnson & Johnson. Attendees can also take advantage of technical hands-on training, industry-specific breakout sessions, and networking.
