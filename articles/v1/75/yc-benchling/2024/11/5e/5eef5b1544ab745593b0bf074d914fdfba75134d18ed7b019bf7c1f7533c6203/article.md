# Meet the winners of the Changemaker Awards from Benchtalk London 2024

Earlier this month, we announced the inaugural winners of the[Changemaker Awards at Benchtalk in Boston](https://www.benchling.com/blog/changemaker-awards-boston-2024) . With Benchtalk London comes a new crop of Changemaker winners, and another opportunity to recognize innovation and excellence. Here's a look at the 2024 London Changemaker Award winners!


## Award: Benchling Champion


### Winner: Stefani Giacopazzi, LIMS Administrator, Single Cell Discoveries


[Single Cell Discoveries](https://www.scdiscoveries.com/) is the leading single-cell sequencing CRO, providing high-impact insights to biopharmaceutical companies, health systems, and academic research centers globally. Located in the Netherlands, their team of scientists is dedicated to developing customized solutions at high quality and rapid speed.


### Why this award


While Benchling may pride itself on being customer obsessed, our customers pride themselves on being Benchling obsessed. “I’ve been obsessed with the platform and the company since we implemented it back in January 2023. I eat, breathe, and sleep all things Benchling,” shares Stefani Giacopazzi. As Single Cell Discoveries’ Benchling guru, Stefani has earned the nickname "the Benchling Machine".


The majority of Stefani’s focus as a LIMS Adminsrator at Single Cell Discoveries is on developing new workflows, templates, and insights, as well as expanding and optimizing the data model as the company rapidly grows. With zero SQL knowledge prior to using Benchling, Stefani has quickly become an expert, writing new insights queries with ease on the platform.


> "I love the way Benchling has transformed our data management and sample tracking. A shameless supporter of all things Benchling, and I am so excited to watch the platform continue to grow to meet our ever evolving needs.”


Stefani GiacopazziLIMS Administrator, Single Cell Discoveries

With a deep understanding of Sanofi’s goals and strategic priorities, Anne-Francoise has driven alignment on how Benchling can be used to advance these objectives to impact real change.

## Award: AI/ML Innovator


### Winner: Cambrium


[Cambrium](https://www.cambrium.bio/) is building a sustainable future, one molecule at a time. Using a proprietary molecular design technology, Cambrium creates novel material building blocks with previously inaccessible functionalities, enabling innovators to create products that are better for people, and the planet.


### Why this award


With Benchling, Cambrium has moved towards a new, more reliable approach to data capture. With newfound instrument connectivity, all of the data generated in the lab goes through Benchling, serving as the primary way for pipelines to ingest lab data.


This ensures that the team can keep visibility into processes and data models, while preventing parallel data journeys from emerging. Benchling’s database also allows Cambrium to seamlessly plug in AI models with ease, making AI more accessible and less time intensive. As pioneers of AI/ML, Cambrium’s contribution to improving data capture has facilitated the development and application of more effective AI models, opening up endless possibilities for transformative change.


## Award: Human Impact Award


### Winner: Orchard Therapeutics


[Orchard Therapeutics](https://www.orchard-tx.com/) is carrying forward a rich history to change the future of medicine. Founded in 2015, Orchard’s roots are planted in some of the first research and clinical development involving hematopoietic stem cell (HSC) gene therapy. Playing a central role in the evolution of this technology, the team is continuing to make an impact by imagining new possibilities for those affected by severe genetic diseases.


### Why this award


Living up to its mission, Orchard Therapeutics announced earlier this year that the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has approved Lenmeldy™ (atidarsagene autotemcel) for children with early-onset metachromatic leukodystrophy (MLD). This milestone opens up tremendous new possibilities for children in the U.S. who previously did not have treatment options beyond supportive care. Based on data generated from the clinical development program, one-treatment of the new gene therapy, the potential to restore enzyme function to stop or slow disease progression has been shown effective, with up to 12 years of follow-up in the earliest treated children.


“As a mother who lost a child to MLD, it is difficult to articulate how much of a watershed moment this is for patients, families and advocates,” said Maria Kefalas, Ph.D., co-founder of the Calliope Joy Foundation and a founding member of Cure MLD. Here at Benchling, we celebrate the entire Orchard Therapeutics team for its remarkable contribution to the MLD community and beyond.


> “I am incredibly proud of the entire team at Orchard for their tireless effort to make this moment possible, and we look forward to ensuring broad and sustainable access to this remarkable innovation for eligible patients in need.”


Bobby GasparCo-Founder and CEO, Orchard Therapeutics


## Award: Changemakers of the Year


### Winner: R&D Data Management, Novonesis


[Novonesis](https://www.novonesis.com/en) means a “new beginning”. Staying true to these roots, the biosolutions company is offering a new beginning based on the power of biology. At a time where the world more than ever needs change, Novonesis is stepping up to make it, bringing transformative biosolutions to market across every industry.


### Why this award


The R&D Data Management team at Novonesis coordinated and delivered a global rollout of Benchling, successfully onboarding 1,3000 scientists onto the R&D cloud. In addition to establishing a global data model that is currently in use across the entire organization, the team is responsible for implementing transformative practices that have long-lasting impact.


With a detail oriented approach, unwavering commitment, and continuous improvement mindset, their relationship with the Benchling engagement team has been one underpinned by collaboration. Contributing insight to Benching’s Nordic Digital Science & Innovation Day over the past two years, as well as inputs for new Bencling solutions, Novonesis is bringing inspiration and innovation to both the scientific community and the technology supporting it.
