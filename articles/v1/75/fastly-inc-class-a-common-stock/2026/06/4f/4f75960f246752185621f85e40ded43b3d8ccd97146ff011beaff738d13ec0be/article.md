# Fastly Appoints Rumi Oku as Area Vice President for Japan

This press release is also available in[日本語](https://www.fastly.com/jp/press/press-releases/fastly-appoints-rumi-oku-as-area-vice-president-for-japan)


**TOKYO – June 1, 2026 –**[Fastly, Inc.](https://www.fastly.com/) (NASDAQ: FSLY), a leading global edge cloud platform, today announced the appointment of Rumi Oku as Area Vice President for Fastly Japan. With over 20 years of experience in the technology sector, spanning enterprise sales to organizational management, Oku will be responsible for acquiring new large customers, building partner relationships, and driving business growth.


Prior to joining Fastly, Oku served as Head of Sales, Gaming, Media, and Entertainment at Google Cloud Japan, overseeing the GME business and driving digital transformation for some of the world’s most recognizable brands. In this role, she was instrumental in expanding the enterprise customer base and fostering strategic alliances. Previously, she held key leadership positions at HashiCorp Japan as Sales Director and Partner Sales Director, where she led the successful launch of the company’s Japanese subsidiary.


"I am deeply honored and excited to take on the role of Head of Japan for Fastly, a company whose evolution I have followed closely for years," said Oku. "In the age of AI, where the balance between security and speed defines business success, Fastly’s technology - which delivers world-class value at the edge - is essential for corporate transformation. Leveraging my experience in the enterprise sector, I am committed to helping our Japanese customers grow their businesses and provide the best possible experiences for their end-users through Fastly’s innovative platform."


Nicola Gerber, Vice President for Asia Pacific and Japan at Fastly, added, “Japan is a critical growth market for Fastly, and Rumi’s exceptional track record of driving enterprise success and building strategic alliances is exactly what we need to accelerate our momentum. As businesses pivot toward an AI-powered future, the demand for edge security and performance has never been higher. I am confident that Rumi’s deep industry expertise and customer-centric leadership will be instrumental in helping Japanese organizations deliver fast, secure, and unmatched digital experiences.”
