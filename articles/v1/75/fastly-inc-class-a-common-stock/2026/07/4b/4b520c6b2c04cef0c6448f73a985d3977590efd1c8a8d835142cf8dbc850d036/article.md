# Fastly Named a 2026 Gartner® Peer Insights™ Customers’ Choice for Edge Distribution Platforms

SAN FRANCISCO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 7, 2026--[Fastly, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.fastly.com%2F&esheet=54566069&newsitemid=20260707074516&lan=en-US&anchor=Fastly%2C+Inc.&index=52&md5=f0068b126dd358ec82d7b47193df28c7) (NASDAQ: FSLY), a leader in global edge cloud platforms, today announced it has been recognized as a 2026 Gartner® Peer Insights™ Customers’ Choice for Edge Distribution Platforms. This distinction is based entirely on verified peer reviews, where Fastly received overall high ratings across the board for its platform performance and reliability, dedication to customer experience, and ongoing customer support.


Fastly received the highest overall rating of 4.8 out of 5 stars (based on 92 reviews) with a 95% Willingness to Recommend score as of April 30, 2026.


This latest acknowledgement builds on Fastly’s recognition last year as a Gartner® Peer Insights™ Customers' Choicefor Cloud Web Application and API Protection (WAAP) for seven consecutive years. Fastly believes this consistent, customer-centric recognition underscores the trust customers place in Fastly and the value its platform delivers.


According to Fastly, this public customer feedback reinforces its track record of innovation and execution, delivering compelling, real-time experiences with flexible observability and comprehensive security. As enterprises accelerate their AI initiatives, the Fastly platform enables them to optimize and scale AI workloads with speed and confidence.


“This recognition is incredibly meaningful because it comes directly from the technology and business leaders we partner with every day to ensure we continue to deliver an innovative and resilient platform,” said Kim Ogletree, Chief Customer Officer at Fastly. “At Fastly, innovation translates directly into real outcomes for our customers. They trust us to support them through their most critical moments, allowing them to move fast, stay secure, and remain ready for what's next. This distinction reaffirms that our edge platform equips enterprises to accelerate innovation securely and at scale.”
