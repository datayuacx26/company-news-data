## Why is email overwhelming for startup founders?


Startup founders send and receive 200+ emails a day. That volume comes from investors asking for updates, partners negotiating terms, employees escalating decisions, customers reporting issues, and vendors chasing invoices. Each thread demands context switching. Each reply surfaces a judgment call.


The inbox becomes a second full-time job.


Most founders triage manually: scan subject lines, open anything that looks urgent, archive the rest. That works at 50 emails a day. It breaks at 150. You start missing threads. Important asks sit unread for three days. Follow-ups pile up because you forgot who you're waiting on.


Slashy's auto-labeling sorts inbound mail into Important / Calendar / Billing / Newsletter / Order / Other / Work before you see it. The AI drafts adapt to your voice as the memory system learns from emails you send and respond to. Follow-up reminders ping you when someone ghosts. The calendar sidebar lives next to your inbox so you're not switching tabs to check availability.


The system was built to handle founder-scale email volume without the manual triage overhead.
