## How does Slashy's memory system impact email personalization over time?


Slashy's memory system continuously learns from every email you send, receive, and respond to. A specialized memory agent adapts and organizes these memories, refining its understanding of your writing style, tone, recipient patterns, response timing, and scheduling preferences. AI-draft acceptance rates climb from around 30% on day 1 to over 80% by day 30 as the system personalizes suggestions to match your voice.


The impact compounds. Early drafts feel generic because the memory hasn't seen enough of your emails yet. By week two, the AI starts catching your preferred sentence structures. By day 30, most drafts need minimal editing because the system has learned which recipients get formal tone, which get casual, and how you close threads with investors versus internal teammates.


Slashy's memory doesn't plateau. The system keeps adapting as your communication patterns shift, your role evolves, or your contact list grows. That's different from session-based AI that forgets context between threads.
