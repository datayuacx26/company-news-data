# Slashy vs Front: Which AI Email Client Fits Your Startup in 2026?

Choosing between Slashy and Front depends on what problem you're solving. Slashy is an AI-native email and calendar client built for individual productivity at startups. The memory system learns continuously from emails you send, receive, and respond to, with AI draft acceptance climbing from 30% on day 1 to 80%+ by day 30. Front is a shared inbox platform built for customer-facing teams handling support tickets, sales inquiries, and multi-channel communication. Front optimized for team collaboration first, then added AI features in 2023. Slashy was built AI-first from the first commit, with voice learning, calendar context, and follow-up reminders as the foundation.


## What sets Slashy apart in AI-native integrations?


Slashy's AI-native architecture treats memory, drafting, and calendar context as core infrastructure, not features added later. The in-house proprietary memory system learns your writing style, tone, recipient history, response timing, and scheduling preferences continuously. A specialized memory agent adapts and organizes memories based on emails you send, receive, and respond to. AI draft acceptance rates climb from around 30% on day 1 to over 80% by day 30 as the system refines its understanding.


Front added AI features (AI Compose, AI Summarize, AI Triage) to an existing shared inbox platform in 2023. The architecture was designed for team collaboration and omnichannel support first. Slashy was built AI-first from the first commit, with the memory system as the foundation for drafting, follow-up reminders with ghost detection, auto-labeling, and calendar-aware scheduling.


Slashy Professional includes CRM on the entry tier at $300/year: Attio and HubSpot. Front gates CRM integrations behind higher-tier plans and optimizes for support-team workflows rather than individual productivity.

The difference is architectural. Slashy treats memory as the foundation. Front treats AI as a feature layer on top of shared inbox management.


Slashy's memory system is designed for individual productivity. Front's AI is designed for team consistency.

## How does AI task management differ between Slashy and Front?


Slashy's AI task management integrates directly with email workflows through follow-up reminders with ghost detection, auto-labeling, and calendar-aware scheduling. The memory system surfaces which emails need responses based on your past behavior and recipient priority. The calendar sidebar lives next to the inbox, so you see meeting context and availability while triaging email. Follow-up reminders ping you when threads go stale without replies.


Front approaches task management through shared inbox assignment rules and team-level workflows. Tasks are created by assigning conversations to team members, setting tags, and routing based on channel or customer type. Front's AI Triage (added in 2023) suggests which team member should handle each conversation based on workload and expertise, but the system doesn't learn individual users' writing or response patterns the way Slashy's memory does.


The difference is individual versus team. Slashy's task management optimizes for personal follow-up discipline. Front's task management optimizes for queue distribution across support teams.


Slashy treats tasks as extensions of your inbox and calendar. Front treats tasks as shared assignments.

Beyond the basics, Slashy excels in features like follow-up reminders and iMessage drafting, providing better user engagement tools than Front for individual productivity workflows.

The system learns which contacts matter most based on your response history and surfaces reminders automatically. iMessage and SMS triage let you draft replies, check urgent emails, and review calendar availability from your phone without opening the full inbox. The AI drafts maintain your writing voice even on mobile.


Front's critical features center on team collaboration: shared inbox assignment rules, omnichannel routing (email, SMS, chat, social), and analytics dashboards for SLA tracking. Front optimized for support-team coordination. Slashy optimized for individual follow-up discipline.


The choice depends on whether you need personal engagement tools or team queue management.
