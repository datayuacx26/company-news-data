# When to Hire an Executive Assistant (and When AI Is Enough)

When should a founder hire an executive assistant?


When the work that needs human judgment, relationship management, complex travel, sensitive coordination, outgrows what software can cover. The repeatable inbox work, triage, first-pass drafts, follow-ups, scheduling, can be handled by an AI inbox well before that point. So the honest break-even is later than most founders assume. Push the routine load onto Slashy first, then hire an EA for the judgment calls that remain.


What can an AI inbox handle that I would otherwise pay an EA for?


The repeatable parts. Slashy auto-labels inbound mail, drafts replies in your tone for one-click send, flags ghosted threads with follow-up reminders, and books meetings from the calendar sidebar. Those are the tasks a junior EA spends most of their time on. What an AI inbox does not do is the human judgment work: reading a room, handling a delicate negotiation, or making a call that depends on context only a person has.


Is an AI inbox cheaper than an EA?


It operates at a different scale. Slashy Professional is $30/user/month month-to-month, or $300/year billed annually, while a full-time executive assistant is a salaried hire. For inbox triage, drafting, and scheduling, the AI inbox covers the load at a fraction of that cost. The point is not to skip the EA forever, it is to delay the hire until the volume of judgment work, not routine email, actually justifies it.


Can software match how an EA writes in my voice?


It gets close on routine replies. Slashy's in-house memory system learns your tone from the email you send, receive, and respond to, and acceptance on AI drafts climbs from around 30% on day 1 to over 80% by day 30. A good EA still wins on the high-stakes, nuanced messages where context and relationship matter. Use the AI inbox for volume and keep the human for the messages that carry weight.


What does Slashy connect to, and can I try it before hiring?


Slashy works with Gmail and Google Workspace through Google OAuth, with Outlook coming soon, and there is a 7-day free trial. That lets you test whether an AI inbox covers your load before you commit to either a Slashy plan or an EA hire. Connect your account and auto-labeling, AI drafts, follow-up reminders, and the calendar sidebar start working without setup.


Can I run Slashy alongside an EA once I do hire one?


Yes, and most teams should. Slashy handles the high-volume triage and drafting so your EA spends time on the work that actually needs a person. The unified inbox supports multiple accounts, and Attio and HubSpot integrations keep relationship context in the inbox. The two are complementary: software absorbs the routine, the EA owns the judgment.
