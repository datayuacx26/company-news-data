# Handling 200 Emails a Day as a Founder

How do you keep up with 200 emails a day without living in your inbox?


You stop treating every message as equal. Slashy auto-labels inbound mail into Important, Calendar, Billing, Newsletter, Order, Other, and Work, so the threads that move your company forward surface first. It drafts replies in your tone for one-click send, and it flags outbound threads that go quiet with follow-up reminders. The volume does not shrink, but the share of it that needs you personally does.


Does an AI inbox replace hiring an executive assistant?


It changes when you need one. A founder sending 200 emails a day can cover triage, drafting, scheduling, and follow-ups with an AI inbox before the volume justifies a full-time EA. Slashy handles the repeatable parts: sorting, first-pass drafts, ghost detection on dropped threads, and calendar booking from the sidebar. An EA still wins on judgment calls and relationship work that needs a human, so the two are not mutually exclusive.


How does Slashy keep me from dropping a thread?


Slashy ships follow-up reminders with ghost detection. When an outbound thread goes silent past the window you would normally expect a reply, it pings you instead of letting the thread sink. Open tracking shows who opened your email, on which device, and how many times, so you know whether a non-reply means they missed it or are sitting on it. That is the difference between a thread that quietly dies and one you nudge back to life.


Will the drafts actually sound like a founder wrote them?


Yes, and they get closer over time. Slashy's in-house memory system learns your tone from the email you send, receive, and respond to. Acceptance on AI drafts starts around 30% on day 1 and climbs past 80% by day 30. Early on you edit more; by week four most replies land in your voice on the first pass. The system keeps adapting as your writing shifts across investors, customers, and your team.


Can I triage from my phone between meetings?


Yes. Slashy works over iMessage and SMS, so you can clear urgent threads, draft replies, and check your calendar by texting without opening the app. For a founder bouncing between meetings, that covers the gaps where you would otherwise let mail pile up. The iOS app is shipped alongside the Mac desktop app.


How much does Slashy cost?


Slashy Professional is $30/user/month month-to-month, or $300/year billed annually, with a 7-day free trial and no free tier. The Professional tier includes AI drafts, auto-labeling, open tracking, follow-up reminders, the calendar sidebar, iMessage and SMS triage, and Attio and HubSpot CRM integrations. Enterprise pricing is custom.
