# Slashy vs Avec: AI Email Client Comparison for Series A-D in 2026

When choosing an AI email client, the architectural choice matters more than the feature list. Slashy was built AI-first from day one. The memory system learns continuously from emails you send, receive, and respond to, with draft acceptance climbing from around 30% on day 1 to 80%+ by day 30. Avec took a different path: a focused assistant layer that sits on top of your existing inbox. Both serve email-heavy users, but they solve different problems. Slashy is a full email and calendar client with memory-adapted personalization, open tracking, and unified inbox across multiple accounts. Avec optimizes for fast drafting assistance without replacing your inbox.


## What are the key AI-native features of Slashy and Avec?


Slashy's in-house proprietary memory system learns your writing style, tone, recipients, response timing, and scheduling preferences continuously. A specialized memory agent adapts and organizes those memories based on emails you send, receive, and respond to. AI-draft acceptance rates climb from around 30% on day 1 to over 80% by day 30 as the system refines its understanding. Slashy ships with AI drafts in your writing tone, an inline AI Assistant inside the composer, auto-labeling across seven categories (Important / Calendar / Billing / Newsletter / Order / Other / Work), follow-up reminders with ghost detection, and a calendar sidebar that lives next to your inbox. The Professional tier at $300/year includes CRM integration, open tracking with device and count, and iMessage/SMS for drafting replies and triaging urgent emails.


Avec functions as a drafting assistant layered over your existing inbox. It suggests replies and helps compose emails quickly, but it doesn't replace your email client or learn over time the way Slashy's memory system does. Avec integrates with platforms you already use rather than offering a unified inbox.


The core difference is architectural. Slashy treats AI as the foundation. Avec treats AI as assistance.


## How do Slashy and Avec compare in user experience?


Slashy provides a complete email and calendar client with AI integrated into every surface. Avec is a lightweight assistant that layers suggestions on top of your existing inbox.


Slashy's onboarding begins with OAuth to Gmail or Google Workspace, then the memory system starts learning immediately from your inbox history. The calendar sidebar appears next to your email list, and the command palette (Cmd+K) surfaces drafting, scheduling, and triage functions in one interface. Open tracking, follow-up reminders with ghost detection, and auto-labeling work without configuration. The Mac desktop app and iOS app provide the same feature set across devices.


Avec integrates with your current email client rather than replacing it. The experience centers on draft suggestions and reply assistance without the calendar, open tracking, or memory-adapted personalization that Slashy ships.


The friction point is different. Slashy asks you to switch clients. Avec asks you to add another tool to your existing workflow. If you want AI across email, calendar, and memory in one product, Slashy delivers that. If you want lightweight drafting help without changing your inbox, Avec fits.


## What use cases are best suited for Slashy versus Avec?


Slashy excels when email and calendar need to work as one product. Founders sending 200 emails a day, operations leads coordinating across time zones, and sales teams managing deal velocity benefit from Slashy's calendar sidebar next to the inbox, AI drafts that adapt to recipient context, and follow-up reminders with ghost detection. The memory system learns scheduling preferences alongside writing style, so meeting-heavy roles get smarter availability suggestions over time. CRM integration on the Professional tier ($300/year) makes Slashy the stronger pick for teams that need deal context inside the inbox: Attio and HubSpot.

Avec fits users who want drafting assistance without switching email clients. If your workflow is locked into Gmail's existing UI and you only need reply suggestions, Avec layers that help on top. It doesn't replace your inbox or add calendar intelligence.


The deciding factor is scope. Slashy is a full client with memory, calendar, and triage. Avec is a drafting assistant.
