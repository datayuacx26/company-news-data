# Slashy vs Lindy: Email Client vs AI Agent Platform

Slashy and Lindy both put AI in front of your email, but they are different categories of product. Slashy is an email client that happens to be AI-native: you connect Gmail, and it starts drafting, triaging, and tracking follow-ups on day one. Lindy is an agent-builder: a platform where you assemble multi-step automations, triggered by events, running across email, calendars, CRMs, and hundreds of other tools. One ships you a finished email experience. The other hands you the building blocks to construct your own. This comparison walks through what each actually is, where the time-to-value line sits, and who should pick which.


## Is Lindy an email client?


No. Lindy is a no-code AI agent platform. You use it to build agents that automate workflows, and email is one of many surfaces those agents can touch.


Lindy's model is a trigger-and-action canvas. You define a trigger (a new email arrives, a meeting ends, a form is submitted), then chain actions across integrations: draft a reply, update a CRM record, post to Slack, schedule a call. It is genuinely powerful for cross-tool automation, and it scales well past email into operations, sales, and support workflows.


But Lindy does not replace your inbox. You still read and manage mail in Gmail or wherever it lives. Lindy runs alongside, executing the automations you configured. The unit of work is the workflow you build, not the email you read.


Slashy is the opposite. It is the inbox itself, with intelligence built into every surface from the first commit. You do not assemble a drafting workflow. Drafting is already there, learning your voice as you go.


## How is Slashy different from Lindy?


Slashy is a purpose-built email client with email intelligence shipped out of the box. Lindy is a general-purpose platform you configure. The difference is buy versus build.


With Slashy, the product is finished. You connect Gmail or Google Workspace via OAuth, and the inbox is immediately drafting replies in your tone, sorting mail into seven labels, tracking opens, and flagging threads that went quiet. There is nothing to wire up. The in-house memory system starts learning from the email you send, receive, and respond to, and the experience sharpens over the first month.


With Lindy, the product is a kit. The depth is real: you can model an automation that no off-the-shelf email client would ship, spanning tools well beyond email. But you own the design and the maintenance. When your process changes, you update the workflow. When an integration shifts, you adjust. That flexibility is the point, and it is also the cost.


This is the honest split. Lindy wins when you need custom multi-step automation across many tools, and you have the time to build and own it. Slashy wins when you want dramatically better email without building anything.


## Can Lindy draft emails in my tone?


Lindy can generate email drafts inside a workflow you build, but tone fidelity depends on the prompts and context you configure. Slashy drafts in your tone automatically, powered by a memory system that learns continuously.


In Lindy, a drafting agent is something you assemble: a trigger, a prompt, and whatever context you feed it. The output quality tracks the effort you put into the configuration. It can be good, but the personalization is your responsibility to design and tune.


Slashy's drafting runs on an in-house memory system built specifically for email. A specialized memory agent captures your writing style, tone, the recipients you talk to most, and your response timing, and it adapts as you keep sending mail. AI-draft acceptance climbs from around 30% on day 1 to over 80% by day 30 as the memory closes the gap between generic output and your actual voice. You do not prompt it or maintain it. You approve drafts that already sound like you.

## Which has a memory system?


Both reference memory, but they are different things. Slashy ships an in-house memory system purpose-built for email personalization. Lindy offers agent memory you configure as part of building workflows.


Slashy's memory is the core product loop. It learns your voice, your recipients, your response patterns, and your scheduling preferences from real inbox activity, with no setup. It is wired into every surface: drafting, the 7-label triage, follow-up reminders, and the calendar sidebar all draw on the same growing memory. The result is a tool that compounds, moving from roughly 30% to over 80% draft acceptance across the first month.


Lindy's memory is a capability inside the builder. You can give your agents memory and context, but you decide what they remember and how that feeds each workflow. It is flexible and broad, spanning whatever tools your automation touches. It is not a turnkey email-personalization engine the way Slashy's is.


The distinction matters for fit. If you want email memory that works the moment you connect your inbox, Slashy ships it. If you want general agent memory you can shape across many tools, Lindy gives you the controls.


## Time to value: Slashy vs Lindy


Slashy delivers value in minutes. Lindy delivers value once you have built and tested your workflows. This is the central wedge between the two.


Slashy's onboarding is a Google OAuth connection. Within minutes the inbox is drafting in your tone, triaging into seven labels, tracking opens, and surfacing the calendar sidebar. The memory system improves the experience over the following weeks, but you get real value on the first session. Nothing to design, nothing to maintain.


Lindy's value arrives after configuration. You decide which automations to build, wire the triggers and actions, connect integrations, and test the flows. For a team with clear, custom multi-step processes across many tools, that investment pays off in automation no email client could match. For someone who just wants better email today, it is upfront work before the payoff.


Neither model is wrong. They optimize for different buyers. The question is whether you want a finished product now or a platform you shape over time.

## Pricing


Slashy uses simple per-seat pricing. Lindy is positioned around usage-based pricing for its platform.


Slashy Professional is $30 per user per month month-to-month, or $300 per year billed annually ($25 per user per month). There is a 7-day free trial and no card required to start. That single tier includes AI drafts in your tone, the 7-label auto-triage, open tracking with ghost-detection follow-ups, the calendar sidebar, iMessage and SMS triage, the command palette, and the unified multi-inbox.


Lindy is positioned as a usage-based platform, where cost scales with how much your agents run rather than a flat per-seat email fee. The trade-off mirrors the product trade-off: a platform priced for variable automation volume versus a finished client priced per seat. We are not quoting Lindy figures here because platform usage pricing shifts; check Lindy's site for current numbers. The structural point is that Slashy's cost is predictable per user, while Lindy's tracks the automation you run.


## Is Slashy secure, and does it train on my data?


Slashy holds SOC 2 Type II and CASA Tier 2 certifications. It does not train AI on your data, and AI providers retain zero data.


Slashy encrypts data at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2+. No user data is used to train AI models, and the AI providers Slashy uses operate under zero data retention. For founders and investors handling sensitive deal flow and private conversations, that contractual clarity matters as much as the features.


Any platform you build automations on, including Lindy, has its own security posture and data-handling terms that depend on the integrations you connect and the data those workflows touch. Review the policies of every tool in a workflow chain. With Slashy, the security boundary is the single product you connected.


## What to choose if...


**Choose Slashy ($30/user/month month-to-month, $300/year annual) if:**


-


You want dramatically better email without building or maintaining anything.


-


You are a founder or investor who needs drafts in your tone, fast triage, and reliable follow-ups today.


-


You value instant time to value: connect Gmail and the inbox works in minutes.


-


You want one finished product with one security boundary, SOC 2 Type II and CASA Tier 2, no AI training on your data, zero retention.

-


You need custom, multi-step automation that spans many tools beyond email.


-


You have clear processes worth modeling and the time to build and maintain workflows.

You want a general-purpose agent canvas, not a finished email client.

-


You adopt Slashy as your day-to-day email client for drafting, triage, and follow-ups, and use Lindy for the cross-tool operational automations that live outside the inbox.
