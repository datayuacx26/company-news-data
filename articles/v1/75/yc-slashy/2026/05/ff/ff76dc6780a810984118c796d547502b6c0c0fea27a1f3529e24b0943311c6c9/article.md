# Meeting Prep, Generated From Your Inbox

How does Slashy build meeting prep from my inbox?


Slashy keeps email and calendar in one product, with the calendar living in a sidebar next to your inbox. Because both run on the same surface, the thread history behind a meeting is right where the calendar event is. You walk into the call with the relevant emails, decisions, and open questions already in view, instead of digging through your inbox five minutes before the meeting starts.


Why does my calendar miss context that my inbox has?


Because a calendar event is usually just a title, a time, and a list of attendees. The actual substance, what was agreed, what is still open, who pushed back, lives in the email thread that set the meeting up. Most stacks split those into two apps, so the prep work is manual. Slashy puts the calendar next to the inbox so the event and the thread that created it stay connected.


Does meeting prep get better the more I use Slashy?


Yes. The in-house memory system learns who you talk to most, your response timing, and your scheduling preferences from the email you send, receive, and respond to. Over weeks it gets sharper at surfacing the threads that matter for a given meeting rather than every message with the same person. That is the same ramp behind draft acceptance climbing from around 30% on day 1 to over 80% by day 30.


Can I check meeting context from my phone before a call?


Yes. Slashy works over iMessage and SMS, so you can check your calendar and pull up relevant threads by texting on the way to a meeting. For back-to-back days, that covers the moments between rooms when you do not want to open the full app. The iOS app is shipped alongside the Mac desktop app.


What does Slashy connect to for calendar and email?


Slashy works with Gmail, Google Workspace, and Google Calendar through a single Google login. Outlook is coming soon. Because email and calendar share one account, there is no separate setup for prep, and the calendar sidebar appears next to your inbox as soon as you connect.


How much does Slashy cost, and can I try it first?


Slashy Professional is $30/user/month month-to-month, or $300/year billed annually, with a 7-day free trial and no free tier. The Professional tier includes the calendar sidebar, AI drafts, auto-labeling, follow-up reminders, iMessage and SMS, and Attio and HubSpot CRM integrations. Enterprise pricing is custom.
