# AI Email Drafts That Actually Sound Like You

How does Slashy learn my writing voice?


Slashy runs an in-house memory system that learns continuously from the emails you send, receive, and respond to. It picks up your tone, the phrases you reach for, who you talk to most, and how formal you get with different people. This is not a one-time setup scan. A specialized memory agent keeps organizing and refining what it stores, so the drafts track your voice as it shifts across different threads and recipients.


How long before the drafts actually sound like me?


Acceptance on AI-drafted replies starts around 30% on day 1 and climbs to over 80% by day 30 as the memory system grows. The early drafts get you most of the way and you edit the rest. By the end of the first month, most replies land in your tone on the first pass. The ramp is the whole point: tools that peak on day one stop getting better, while Slashy keeps adapting.


Can I send a draft without editing it?


Yes. Slashy drafts replies in your tone with one-click send, so a draft you trust goes out with a single keystroke. For threads where you want to adjust the angle, the inline AI Assistant inside the composer lets you redirect the draft without leaving the message. You stay in control of what actually sends; the memory system just removes the blank-page step.


Does it work across all my accounts?


Slashy supports Gmail and Google Workspace through Google OAuth, and you can run multiple accounts in a unified inbox with one-click account switching. Outlook is coming soon. The memory system applies across your connected accounts, so a draft from your work address and a draft from your personal address both reflect how you actually write to that kind of recipient.


Can I draft replies from my phone?


Yes. Slashy works over iMessage and SMS, so you can ask it to draft a reply, triage urgent mail, or check your calendar by texting. The drafts use the same memory layer as the desktop client, so a reply you fire off from your phone reads the same as one you write at your laptop. The iOS app is shipped alongside the Mac desktop app.


Is my sent email used to train shared AI models?


No. Your messages are not retained to train shared models. The memory that learns your voice is your own. Slashy is SOC 2 Type II and CASA Tier 2 certified, with encryption at rest and in transit.
