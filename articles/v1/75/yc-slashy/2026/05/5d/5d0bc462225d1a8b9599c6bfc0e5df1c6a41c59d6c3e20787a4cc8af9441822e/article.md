# Slashy vs Reclaim: AI-Native Email and Scheduling Compared

Slashy and Reclaim solve different problems. Slashy was built AI-first from the first commit as a full email and calendar client. The memory system adapts continuously based on emails sent, received, and responded to, with AI-draft acceptance climbing from approximately 30% on day 1 to approximately 80% by day 30. Reclaim optimized for calendar intelligence: focus-block management, meeting placement, and time-slot defense. No inbox. No drafts. If you need email and calendar unified, Slashy fits. If you need calendar-only AI, Reclaim fits. This comparison breaks down architecture, pricing, integrations, and who wins where.


## How does Slashy integrate email and task automation?


Slashy's in-house proprietary memory system learns continuously from the emails you send, receive, and respond to. A specialized memory agent organizes and adapts those memories, tracking your writing style, tone, recipients, response timing, and scheduling preferences. That memory powers task automation across the inbox: AI drafts in your writing tone with one-click send, follow-up reminders with ghost detection, auto-labeling that sorts inbound mail into Important / Calendar / Billing / Newsletter / Order / Other / Work, and a calendar sidebar that lives next to the inbox.


The memory doesn't plateau. Acceptance rates on AI drafts climb from around 30% on day 1 to over 80% by day 30 as the system adapts. The auto-labels get sharper. The follow-up reminders learn which threads you actually want tracked.


Slashy treats email and calendar as one surface. The calendar sidebar sits next to your inbox, so you can draft a reply and propose meeting times without switching apps. The iMessage and SMS surface lets you triage urgent email and check your calendar from your phone without opening the full client.


## What scheduling capabilities does Reclaim offer?


Reclaim automates calendar optimization with intelligent scheduling algorithms that defend focus blocks and place meetings based on your preferences. The tool reads your existing calendar and adjusts available time slots to protect deep work. You set preferences for meeting types, focus-block duration, and preferred meeting hours. Reclaim's scheduling links (similar to Calendly) propose times that fit those constraints, and the AI reschedules tasks and habits when conflicts arise.


Reclaim prioritizes meeting placement by analyzing calendar patterns and user-defined rules. If you mark mornings as high-energy time, Reclaim defends those slots for focus work and pushes meetings to afternoons. The tool integrates with Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar, syncing across both to prevent double-booking.


Reclaim does not draft emails. It does not triage your inbox. It does not learn your writing voice. The scheduling intelligence is calendar-only. If your workflow needs email and calendar unified, Reclaim leaves a gap. If you need focus-block defense and meeting optimization without an inbox client, Reclaim delivers exactly that.


## How do Slashy's memory learning benefits differ from Reclaim's approach?


Slashy's in-house memory system learns continuously from user behavior and improves productivity over time. Reclaim optimizes scheduling efficiency with static rules that don't adapt based on how you actually work.


Slashy's memory tracks writing style, tone, recipients, response timing, and scheduling preferences. The system organizes and refines those memories as you send and respond to emails. AI-draft acceptance climbs from approximately 30% on day 1 to approximately 80% by day 30. Auto-labels get sharper. Follow-up reminders learn which threads matter. The longer you use Slashy, the better it gets at predicting what you need.


Reclaim defends focus blocks and places meetings based on preferences you set manually. You tell Reclaim your high-energy hours, preferred meeting windows, and focus-block duration. The tool applies those rules consistently. The scheduling logic doesn't adapt based on which meetings you accept, which focus blocks you actually use, or how your work patterns shift over time.

## Comparing task automation versus scheduling efficiency


Slashy automates tasks through continuous AI learning. Reclaim optimizes scheduling through user-defined rules.


Slashy's task automation spans the inbox: AI drafts in your writing tone, follow-up reminders with ghost detection, auto-labeling that sorts inbound mail into Important / Calendar / Billing / Newsletter / Order / Other / Work, and a calendar sidebar that lives next to the inbox. The memory system learns your writing style, tone, recipients, response timing, and scheduling preferences. That memory powers one-click send on AI drafts, iMessage and SMS triage for urgent email, and open tracking with device and count.


Reclaim automates scheduling placement. You set preferences for meeting types, focus-block duration, and high-energy hours. Reclaim defends those slots and reschedules tasks when conflicts arise. The tool does not draft emails, triage your inbox, or learn your writing voice. The automation is calendar-only.


If you need task automation across email and calendar, Slashy integrates both. If you need focus-block defense without an inbox client, Reclaim delivers scheduling efficiency.


## Which platform offers better integration for startups?


Slashy integrates email and calendar into one AI-native client with CRM on the Professional tier ($300/year): Attio and HubSpot. Reclaim integrates with Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar only, with no email client or CRM.


Slashy ships that CRM integration on the Professional tier, so sales and BD teams can log emails to CRM records without leaving the inbox. The calendar sidebar lives next to your email, so you can draft a reply and propose meeting times in one surface. Slashy also integrates iMessage and SMS for inbox triage, Google Calendar for scheduling, and Zoom for meeting links. The memory system learns continuously, so the AI drafts and auto-labels get sharper the longer your team uses it.


Reclaim integrates with Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar to defend focus blocks and place meetings. No inbox. No CRM. No email drafts. The scheduling logic applies user-defined rules consistently across both calendars.

## Cost comparison: Slashy vs Reclaim


Slashy Professional costs $30/user/month month-to-month, or $300/year billed annually, and includes email, calendar, AI drafts, CRM integration, and continuous memory learning. Reclaim offers a free tier and paid plans starting at $8/user/month for calendar-only AI scheduling.


Slashy's Professional tier ships a full intelligent inbox: AI drafts in your writing tone, open tracking, follow-up reminders with ghost detection, a calendar sidebar, iMessage and SMS triage, auto-labeling, and CRM integration. The memory system learns continuously, with AI-draft acceptance climbing from approximately 30% on day 1 to approximately 80% by day 30.


Reclaim focuses on calendar optimization. The paid tiers defend focus blocks, place meetings based on user-defined rules, and integrate with Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar. No email client. No CRM. No drafts.
