# NUS Application With IB: Requirements and Strategy

An **NUS application with IB** results is submitted directly through the National University of Singapore Applicant Portal. IB Diploma students may apply using actual results or, if taking the May examination session, school-attested predicted results followed by their final transcript. Admission is competitive and holistic, so earning the IB Diploma makes you eligible but does not guarantee a place.


This guide explains the current requirements, application process, programme prerequisites, choice strategy, supporting documents, and common mistakes. Dates below refer to **Academic Year 2026/2027** , the latest published cycle, which is now closed. Future applicants must check the official NUS pages because requirements and deadlines can change annually.


### NUS application with IB: essential requirements


NUS accepts the **IB Diploma Programme** from applicants of any nationality. English must be one of the subjects taken, and applicants must eventually be awarded the full IB Diploma. Students who are currently or were previously enrolled as undergraduates at another university generally need to apply as transfer applicants instead.


NUS assesses applicants through open competition using:


- Academic performance, including overall and subject-level IB results
- Fulfilment of programme-specific subject prerequisites
- Interest, aptitude, achievements, and prior preparation
- Interviews, tests, portfolios, or other assessments where required


NUS does not publish a universal minimum competitive IB score for each programme. Its **Indicative Grade Profile** primarily reports Singapore-Cambridge A-Level and polytechnic outcomes; NUS states that the IB sample is too small to produce an accurate programme-level profile. Consequently, claims that a particular IB total automatically guarantees admission should not be treated as official cut-offs.

For the 2026/2027 cycle, the IB application window ran from **17 December 2025 to 23 February 2026** . A May-session applicant could leave the Personal Code field blank initially if it was unavailable, but had to update it later through the Applicant Portal.


Predicted grades must be attested by the school rather than prepared or uploaded as an unofficial student estimate. ### Check programme prerequisites before applying


A strong total score cannot compensate for a missing compulsory subject. NUS publishes an IB-specific prerequisite table, and students should check the version for their admission year before finalising their choices.


Examples for Academic Year 2026/2027 include:


- **Medicine:** a pass in HL Chemistry and either HL Biology or HL Physics
- **Dentistry:** a pass in HL Chemistry and either HL Biology or HL Physics
- **Law:** normally at least grade 5 in an accepted SL or HL English subject, with an alternative SAT-based route specified by NUS
- Numerous computing, business, engineering, and quantitative programmes: specified mathematics preparation, often involving **HL Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches (MAA)**


These examples are not a complete substitute for the official table. Requirements differ across single, double, and concurrent degree programmes, and NUS warns that they may change annually.


Medicine and Dentistry applicants presenting predicted IB results also face additional testing requirements. For the 2026/2027 cycle, Medicine required predicted results plus either **UCAT** or the stated SAT/ACT with Writing and AP combination; Dentistry required predicted results plus the specified SAT/ACT with Writing and AP examinations. Medicine applicants also had to complete the separate NUS Medicine admissions process.


### How to complete the application


### 1. Research and rank programmes


Shortlist programmes for which you satisfy every prerequisite, then rank them honestly. NUS considers choices in order, so an unsuccessful first choice can lead to consideration for later choices.


For Academic Year 2026/2027, IB applicants could receive up to **one first-choice bonus point** for an eligible non-interview single-degree programme. Medicine, Dentistry, and Law follow special ranking rules, while Architecture, Landscape Architecture, Industrial Design, and Nursing must be placed within the first three choices for consideration. Read the current rules rather than trying to manipulate the order based on unofficial admission-score rumours.


### 2. Prepare the required information


Typical materials include:


- Passport or Singapore identification documents, as applicable
- Official IB electronic transcript
- School-attested predicted results for May-session candidates
- IB Personal Code, entered in lowercase as instructed
- Mother Tongue Language evidence or an MOE exemption, if applicable
- English-language test results where required
- Relevant National Service or medical documentation, if applicable
- Additional programme-specific tests, portfolios, or records


All IB applicants must ask their coordinator to authorise the IB to send the electronic transcript to **National University of Singapore, institute code 000690** . Uploading a student result screenshot does not replace this mandatory release.


### 3. Complete the holistic assessment section


NUS requires applicants to answer **five short-response questions** and list up to **four achievements** , although applicants without relevant achievements may select the stated alternative. Useful evidence can include sustained service, leadership, research, internships, competitive activities, entrepreneurship, creative work, or programme-related projects.


Quality matters more than collecting disconnected certificates. Describe what you did, the scale of your contribution, what changed, and how the experience demonstrates preparation for your chosen field. The[RevisionDojo personal-statement planning guide](https://www.revisiondojo.com/blog/how-to-write-a-personal-statement-that-stands-out-ib-ap-students) can help you reflect on experiences, but NUS explicitly instructs applicants to answer in their own words without AI or ghostwriting. Do not ask Jojo AI or any other tool to draft or rewrite these admissions responses.


### 4. Submit and confirm completion


For the 2026/2027 IB cycle, the application was complete only after the online form, supporting documents, and **S$10 non-refundable fee** were submitted by the deadline. Applicants should retain the acknowledgement email and monitor the Applicant Portal, including spam folders and the email address used to register.


Do not use a school email account likely to be deactivated after graduation. Check the portal regularly for requests, assessment invitations, Personal Code updates, and the final decision.


### English and Mother Tongue Language requirements


If **three or more IB subjects are taught in a non-English language** , NUS requires an approved English test score. This is different from merely taking a language subject and should be checked against the current application guide.


The Ministry of Education **Mother Tongue Language requirement** applies only to specified applicant groups. At IB level, it may be fulfilled by a pass at SL or HL in an applicable MTL A or Language B subject. **Language ab initio at SL does not fulfil this requirement** , although an official MOE exemption or another recognised qualification may apply.


### How to strengthen the academic side


Because NUS does not publish a reliable IB cut-off table, focus on controllable evidence:


- Raise the total score without neglecting prerequisite HL subjects
- Use timed examination practice rather than passive rereading
- Review errors by concept, command term, and time-management problem
- Protect TOK and Extended Essay performance because they can contribute up to three core points
- Maintain evidence of sustained, relevant activity outside lessons

### Common application mistakes


The most damaging errors are procedural rather than dramatic:


- Assuming that passing the IB is enough for a competitive programme
- Applying without checking the exact mathematics, science, or language prerequisite
- Failing to authorise the official IB transcript
- Missing the separate final-result or Personal Code deadline
- Ranking interview programmes outside the positions permitted by NUS
- Writing vague activity descriptions that do not identify personal contribution
- Treating an unofficial IB score mentioned online as a guaranteed threshold
- Submitting the form but not the documents or fee


Build a checklist with separate columns for general admission, transcript release, programme assessments, scholarships, and financial aid. These can have different portals and deadlines.


### Conclusion


A successful NUS application with IB results requires more than a high total. You must hold the full diploma, meet every programme prerequisite, submit the correct predicted or actual results, authorise the official transcript, rank programmes strategically, and complete the holistic questions honestly.
