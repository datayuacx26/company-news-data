# IB Biology Data Booklet Arrives for 2028 Exams

The International Baccalaureate has published a new **Biology data booklet for first assessment in 2028** , giving affected Standard Level and Higher Level students access to reference diagrams, equations, constants and other course data. The official document is marked Version 1.0 and states that it was published in May 2026.


Students following the 2028 version of the course should use the booklet throughout their studies. During the written examinations, schools must provide every candidate with a clean copy, meaning students cannot bring a personally annotated or highlighted version into the exam room.


### Key facts about the Biology booklet


- **Applies from:** First assessment in 2028
- **Levels affected:** Biology SL and Biology HL
- **Exam access:** A clean copy must be available for the written examination papers
- **Course access:** Students should have access throughout the course
- **Contents:** Biological diagrams, equations, constants, statistical references, symbols and other relevant data
- **Official publication date:** May 2026, with no specific day stated in the document
- **Official distribution:** Schools obtain copies through IBIS or the IB Programme Resource Centre


The booklet itself is an IB publication. Its introduction says the resource accompanies the DP Biology guide and teacher support material, while the[official IB Biology subject page](https://ibo.org/programmes/diploma-programme/curriculum/sciences/biology/) confirms that Biology is regularly reviewed and directs schools to current subject updates.
