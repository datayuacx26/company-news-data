# Do Universities Consider IB Component Scores?

For most IB applications, **universities consider final subject grades rather than the marks from individual assessment components** . The standard IB transcript reports each course and its final grade from **1 to 7** , alongside broader Diploma Programme results. It does not ordinarily provide a paper-by-paper or Internal Assessment mark breakdown.


Component scores still matter because they are weighted and combined to produce each subject grade. They can also become relevant after results if you narrowly miss an offer, investigate an **Enquiry Upon Results (EUR)** , or are specifically asked by a university for additional evidence. This article explains what universities normally receive, what admissions conditions actually measure, and how to respond when detailed marks could help your case.


### What counts as an IB component score?


An IB subject is usually assessed through several components. Depending on the course, these may include written examination papers, an **Internal Assessment (IA)** , an individual oral, an investigation, an exhibition, a performance, or other coursework.


Each component receives a mark and has a specified weighting. The weighted marks are aggregated into a **scaled total mark** , which is then compared with the subject's grade boundaries to produce a final grade from **1 to 7** .
