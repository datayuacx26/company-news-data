# Switching from HL English Lit to LangLit in DP1

Yes, it may be possible to switch from **HL English A: Literature** to **HL English A: Language and Literature** during DP1. However, the change is not automatic and cannot be approved by the IB directly at a student's request. Your school must offer the course, approve the transfer, accommodate it within the timetable, and decide that you can realistically complete the missed learning.


The earlier in DP1 you ask, the more manageable the switch is likely to be. Before deciding, you need to understand that Language and Literature is not simply a less literary version of Literature. It includes substantial literary study but adds systematic analysis of non-literary texts, media, audiences, contexts, and communication.


### The short answer: your school decides whether you can switch


There is no universal IB rule guaranteeing students the right to change subjects during DP1. Equally, the IB's published regulations do not establish a general prohibition on moving between these two English A courses. In practice, subject-change policies, deadlines, class capacity, staffing, and timetabling are determined by individual schools.


Official assessment registration is handled through the school. The IB's[Diploma Programme general regulations](https://www.ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/become-an-ib-school/pdfs/dp-2026-general-regulations.pdf) state that students cannot register themselves or amend an existing registration themselves. The **DP coordinator** manages this process, so that person must confirm what is possible in your circumstances.


A switch is most realistic when:


- You are still relatively early in DP1.
- HL Language and Literature is offered and has space.
- The two classes fit the same timetable block.
- You can catch up on the non-literary texts and concepts already taught.
- The receiving teacher supports the change.
- Your overall Diploma subject combination remains valid.


As explained in RevisionDojo's guide to[switching IB subjects during DP1 or DP2](https://www.revisiondojo.com/blog/can-i-change-subjects-in-the-middle-of-dp1-or-at-the-start-of-dp2-a-detailed-guide) , timing matters because missed content and assessment preparation accumulate quickly.


### How HL Literature and HL Language and Literature differ


Both subjects belong to the DP **studies in language and literature** group, and both develop close analysis, interpretation, argument, and academic writing. At HL, the IB recommends for each course. Their assessment structures and weightings are similar, but the materials studied and the application of analytical skills are different.
