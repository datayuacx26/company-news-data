# RevisionDojo vs Kognity: IB Platform Comparison

RevisionDojo and Kognity support IB learning in fundamentally different ways. **Kognity is primarily a school-licensed digital textbook, assignment, and classroom analytics platform** , while **RevisionDojo combines independent revision, exam practice, AI feedback, and coursework support for students and schools** .


The better choice therefore depends on the problem being solved. Kognity is often stronger when a school wants a shared, centrally managed teaching resource. RevisionDojo is often stronger when students need an integrated system for independent revision, timed practice, feedback, and IA, EE, or TOK development.


### RevisionDojo vs Kognity: the short answer


Choose **Kognity** if your priority is an official co-published textbook model, teacher-set assignments, class engagement data, and consistent implementation across departments. Kognity describes itself as the IB's only digital-first publishing partner and says it is used by one in three IB schools. These are[Kognity's published claims](https://kognity.com/products/ibdp/) , rather than findings from an independent head-to-head study.


Choose **RevisionDojo** if the priority is exam preparation, targeted question practice, timed mock examinations, Jojo AI feedback, flashcards, and coursework workflows. Students can access[RevisionDojo's IB resources](https://www.revisiondojo.com/ib) independently, while schools can use classroom, assessment, and bulk-feedback features through[RevisionDojo for Schools](https://www.revisiondojo.com/schools) .


Neither platform replaces teachers, current IB subject guides, or authenticated assessment procedures.
