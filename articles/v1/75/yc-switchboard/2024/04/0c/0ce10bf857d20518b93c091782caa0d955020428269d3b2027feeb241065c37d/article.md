# Access more Customers: Turn on your Switchboard Integration with Third-Party Visibility Providers

Many transportation companies are turning to technology solutions that offer seamless integration with third-party visibility providers.


[Switchboard](https://www.onswitchboard.com/go/fleet-management) , a comprehensive logistics management platform, has emerged as a game-changer in the industry by offering seamless integration with leading visibility providers like project44, FourKites, MacroPoint, and GoHighway. This integration opens up a plethora of opportunities for transportation companies to streamline their operations and enhance customer satisfaction.


By integrating with third-party visibility providers,[Switchboard enables transportation companies to access real-time tracking and monitoring capabilities for their shipments.](https://www.onswitchboard.com/solution/fleet-management) Whether it's monitoring the location of trucks, tracking delivery statuses, or receiving alerts about potential delays, businesses can stay informed at every stage of the transportation process.


With out-of-the-box integrations, you're able to provide improved customer experience for your clients right away. By providing customers with real-time visibility into the status and location of their shipments, you can provide a higher level of customer service that leads to increased customer satisfaction and loyalty, ultimately driving business growth.


Switchboard also[offers powerful REST APIs](https://www.onswitchboard.com/for/developers) for those looking to build custom integrations with Switchboard.


In today's fast-paced logistics industry, embracing technology solutions that prioritize visibility and transparency is key to staying ahead of the curve and driving success.
