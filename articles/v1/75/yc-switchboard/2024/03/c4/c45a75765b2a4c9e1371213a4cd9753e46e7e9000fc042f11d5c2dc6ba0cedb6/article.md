### What is Samsara?


Established in 2015, Samsara is a fleet management platform that offers businesses various tools for monitoring and analyzing their fleet operations.


Samsara provides insights into vehicle health and performance, with reporting capabilities and integration options with third-party applications that make it a viable choice for businesses looking to optimize their fleet management operations.


### What is Motive?


Motive is a fleet management solution that aims to provide businesses with tools for operational oversight and regulatory compliance. It offers features such as predictive maintenance, asset tracking, and fuel management, focusing on delivering data-driven insights to optimize fleet performance.


While it emphasizes functionalities like advanced diagnostics and customizable alerts, its effectiveness and reliability may vary based on specific business requirements and operational contexts.

### What is[Switchboard](https://www.onswitchboard.com/) ?


Switchboard's Electronic Logging Device (ELD) features play a crucial role in assisting commercial motor vehicle drivers and fleet managers in adhering to Hours of Service (HOS) regulations set forth by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA). With an[automated logbook system](https://www.onswitchboard.com/solution/hours-of-service-eld) , Switchboard ensures accurate recording of driving hours, duty status changes, and rest breaks, eliminating the need for manual entries and reducing the risk of errors. This automated process not only saves time but also enhances compliance by providing a reliable record of drivers' activities.


In addition to automated logging, Switchboard offers[real-time monitoring capabilities](https://www.onswitchboard.com/solution/fleet-management) that allow managers and dispatchers to keep track of drivers' HOS status instantly. By having access to up-to-date information on drivers' hours and status changes, Switchboard promptly notifies drivers and managers of impending HOS violations. These alerts serve as proactive reminders, helping drivers manage their driving hours more effectively and avoid penalties for non-compliance.


[Switchboard IFTA Reporting Automation](https://www.onswitchboard.com/solution/ifta-automation) allows for one-click management of vehicle mileages so that filing[IFTA can be done in minutes, not days](https://www.onswitchboard.com/blog/easiest-way-to-calculate-ifta) . Along with[Switchboard Asset Tracking,](https://www.onswitchboard.com/solution/asset-tracking) fleet managers can make informed decisions regarding scheduling and route planning, and offer improved customer service to transportation clients.


Switchboard is[compliant with FMCSA and NSC regulations](https://www.onswitchboard.com/blog/canadian-mandate-certified-eld) , which offers hours-of-service compliance for all types transport carriers across Noth America.
