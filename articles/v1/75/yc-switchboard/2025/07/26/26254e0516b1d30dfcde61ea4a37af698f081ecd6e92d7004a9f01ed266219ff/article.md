# Editing your Hours-of-Service Logs

If you’ve ever had an autogenerated driving entry in your log that didn’t match up with the actual driving time, you know how frustrating it can be. Whether it’s a short stop or a brief engine idle that gets incorrectly logged as driving time, these small inaccuracies can throw off your entire day’s record. With this new feature, you can **ignore** those auto-generated entries with just a few clicks, ensuring your log timers reflect the real time spent on the road.


## **Key Benefits**


- **Accurate HOS Logs:** No more over-recorded driving time.
- **Less Time Spent on Edits:** Fix errors quickly without needing to dive into manual adjustments.
- **Better Driver Workflow:** Drivers can focus on driving without worrying about small inaccuracies.


This feature is designed to give you more control over your logs without disrupting your current processes. It’s a small adjustment, but one that can save time and ensure your records stay compliant, day in and day out.
