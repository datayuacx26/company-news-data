# Corsair Gaming Acquires Trak Racer Assets

[PDF Version](https://ir.corsair.com/node/11356/pdf)


***Creates a Complete Global Platform for Racing and Flight Simulation***


***Expands Addressable Market; Accelerates Direct-to-Consumer Growth***


MILPITAS, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 5, 2026-- Corsair Gaming, Inc. (Nasdaq: CRSR) (“Corsair” or the “Company”), a leading global provider and innovator of high-performance products for gamers, streamers, content-creators, performance PC builders, and sim racing enthusiasts, today announced that it has acquired all the assets of Trak Racer, a global designer and manufacturer of premium racing and flight-simulation cockpits, frames, seats, monitor stands, motion systems and accessories.


This press release features multimedia. View the full release here:[https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260805890873/en/](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260805890873/en/)


"This acquisition is a continuation of our strategy to scale our Gamer and Creator Peripherals segment into a much larger addressable market, with attractive margin expansion opportunities," said Thi La, Chief Executive Officer of Corsair. "Trak Racer meaningfully expands Fanatec's sim racing ecosystem, bringing an extensive SIM cockpit and accessories product portfolio, and a strong direct-to-consumer revenue mix that we believe will scale well across our global distribution network."


**Strategic Rationale**


The acquisition reflects Corsair's continued strategy of scaling its Gamer and Creator Peripherals segment into adjacent, enthusiast categories within the broader esports market. Sim racing represents one of the fastest-growing segments in the category, and Trak Racer's addition builds meaningfully on Corsair's existing Fanatec platform.


Founded in 2008 by Matt Sten, Trak Racer has built a loyal global following for its simulator cockpits, racing seats, and licensed and co-branded racing peripherals, with a product range spanning entry-level to premium and professional segments. The brand's portfolio complements Fanatec's existing sim racing technology, enabling a more complete, turnkey sim racing platform for consumers and professional racers alike.


**Highly Complementary Simulation Portfolio**


Corsair’s Fanatec and Trak Racer approach the simulation market from complementary positions. Fanatec is centered on the electronic control system, including high-performance wheelbases, steering wheels, pedals, shifters and software for PC and consoles. Trak Racer is centered on the physical simulator, including aluminum-profile and tubular cockpits, seats, display mounts, peripheral mounts, motion and haptic systems, and turnkey racing and flight simulators. Both Fanatec and Trak Racer have long-standing relationships with leading F1 teams.


The combined portfolio is expected to further support integrated bundles, simplify configuration and purchasing, and increase the attachment of cockpits, controls, displays, gaming PCs and accessories, while preserving the product quality, innovation and customer choice that define both brands.


**Direct-to-Consumer and Channel Opportunity**


As most of Trak Racer’s sales are direct to consumer, Corsair’s global retail and distribution infrastructure provides an expansion opportunity to grow Trak Racer by leveraging Corsair’s existing channel sale network.


**Expanding into Flight Simulation**


The transaction expands Corsair’s serviceable available market beyond sim racing into flight simulation, a compelling adjacent category. Trak Racer’s TR550 Flight Simulator will provide Corsair with an established foundation in the category, while its motion and haptic capabilities create additional opportunities across racing, flight and other forms of simulation.


In addition to giving Corsair an immediate position in flight simulation, the company will be able to leverage the same technical competencies that underpin sim racing: precision controls, structural rigidity, ergonomics, displays, PCs, software, VR, motion and haptics.


**Expanding Brand, Licensing and Motorsport Partnerships**


Beyond its cockpit and chassis technology, Trak Racer has a pipeline of brand licensing agreements and motorsport partnerships that management believes will support the platform’s growth in the years ahead. These include a licensing relationship with Mattel, Inc. (NASDAQ: MAT), owner of the Hot Wheels and Matchbox brands, under which co-branded Hot Wheels and Matchbox sim racing products are planned to launch beginning in 2027, and an expanded, multi-product licensing arrangement with Porsche that extends the relationship from a single co-branded cockpit into a broader ecosystem of Porsche-branded simulation hardware.


Trak Racer also recently introduced the TRZ, believed to be the first mass-produced, mainstream-priced full-size motion motorcycle simulator, which debuted to strong reception at the 2026 Sim Racing Expo in Charlotte, North Carolina, and is expected to open a new product category for the combined company.


In motorsport, Trak Racer maintains an official partnership with the BWT Alpine Formula One Team dating back to 2020, and has separately introduced a co-branded simulator cockpit with the Aston Martin Aramco Formula One Team.


**Corsair expects the acquisition to support its growth strategy through:**


- **Category expansion in a fast-growing market:** Extending Corsair's Gamer and Creator Peripherals segment further into the sim racing category, a large and growing market with attractive margin profile.
- **A more complete sim racing ecosystem:** Pairing Fanatec's sim racing technology with Trak Racer's cockpit, chassis, and accessory portfolio.
- **Strong D2C foundation:** Trak Racer brings a well-established, high direct-to-consumer revenue mix that aligns with Corsair's broader channel strategy.
- **Global scale:** Opportunity to grow Trak Racer's reach through Corsair's global distribution, retail, and DTC infrastructure.
- **Expanded brand and motorsport partnerships:** Trak Racer's licensing relationships with Mattel and Porsche, together with its partnerships with the Alpine and Aston Martin Formula One teams, are expected to create new co-branded product opportunities across the combined portfolio.


"Joining Corsair and Fanatec gives Trak Racer the platform to reach far more SIM enthusiasts faster than we could on our own," said Matt Sten, founder of Trak Racer, who has joined Corsair as Chief Technology Officer for SIM Solutions. "Corsair's global distribution and retail infrastructure, paired with Fanatec's engineering strength and product portfolio, will let us bring Trak Racer products to a much larger audience while staying true to the community that helped built this brand."
