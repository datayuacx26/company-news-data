# Template Folders

Today, we're introducing **folders for templates** . You can now group templates by product, campaign, or team, and move them around as your library grows.


## Organize Templates


Create folders and subfolders to organize your emails. Then drag templates into it or move them from the ellipsis menu.


## Reorganize as needed


Move a template back out of a folder at any time. Deleting a folder removes the folder itself, not the templates inside it. They're returned to All templates so nothing gets lost.


If you have any questions, please reach out to us and we'll be happy to help.
