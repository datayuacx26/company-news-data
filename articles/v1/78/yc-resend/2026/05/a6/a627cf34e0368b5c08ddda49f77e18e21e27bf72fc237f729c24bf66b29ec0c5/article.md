# Welcoming Tatira Andrade, our new Executive Assistant

Today, we're thrilled to share the news that[Tatira Andrade](https://resend.com/humans/tatira-andrade) is joining Resend as an Executive Assistant!


Tatira brings nearly 16 years of experience, with a strong background in executive support, complex logistics, and process optimization. She has worked with companies like Nubank, helping leadership teams streamline their daily operations and scale their workflows efficiently.


We’re very excited to have someone with her level of expertise dedicated to bringing clarity and structure to our daily operations and supporting our team's continuous growth.


## More about Tatira


**Why did you decide to become an Executive Assistant?**


Becoming an Executive Assistant wasn't exactly a childhood dream — I always say the profession chose me, rather than the other way around. I've always been fascinated by travel, geography, languages, and organizing things. Back in school, I was constantly elected class representative, the person who loved bringing everyone together and bringing order to chaos. Ultimately, this career is perfectly aligned with my natural strengths.


**Where do you find inspiration?**


I love making things easier and more streamlined. If I spot an opportunity to improve a process, it's almost impossible for me to resist fixing it. I also love traveling and creating seamless experiences for my family and friends, which translates incredibly well into my day-to-day work.

I'd probably be managing events or coordinating travel logistics. It's hard to imagine myself doing anything completely unrelated to what I do now, because I truly love what I do.

**What is your advice for ambitious EAs?**


Never stop learning. Keep up with new technologies, master new languages, and always stay updated. Have a genuine curiosity about the company and the people around you. You can never have too much knowledge.
