# Launch Week 6: Wrap Up

Today marks the end of Launch Week 6.


Last week, we shipped five new features and made one big announcement.


- Day 1: Automations
- Day 2: AI Email Editor
- Day 3: Resend CLI 2.0
- Day 4: Custom Tracking Domains
- Day 5: React Email 6.0
- Day 6: Resend Forward


Prefer a video recap?


## Day 1: Automations


On Monday, we launched Automations, a new way to orchestrate lifecycle emails triggered by events in your app.


With Automations, you can:


- [Define a trigger](https://resend.com/blog/introducing-automations#1-define-a-trigger) from an event in your app
- [Personalize each step](https://resend.com/blog/introducing-automations#2-personalize-each-step) with delays, waits, and conditions
- [Go live with confidence](https://resend.com/blog/introducing-automations#3-go-live-with-confidence) by testing runs before publishing
- [Get full visibility](https://resend.com/blog/introducing-automations#5-full-visibility-into-each-run) into every run in real time


[Automations Build lifecycle sequences triggered by events in your app. resend.com/blog/introducing-automations](https://resend.com/blog/introducing-automations)


## Day 2: AI Email Editor


On Tuesday, we announced the new Resend email editor, extended by AI.


The editor helps you:


- [Start with your existing content](https://resend.com/blog/ai-email-editor#start-with-your-existing-content) by turning a URL into an on-brand draft
- [Edit inline](https://resend.com/blog/ai-email-editor#inline-ai-editing) to rewrite, rephrase, or improve any block
- [Catch mistakes](https://resend.com/blog/ai-email-editor#get-help-catching-mistakes) before you hit send
- [Bring your own agentic stack](https://resend.com/blog/ai-email-editor#bring-your-own-agentic-stack) via the Resend MCP server


[AI Email Editor Build, edit, and review emails faster with AI built directly into the editor. resend.com/blog/ai-email-editor](https://resend.com/blog/ai-email-editor)


## Day 3: Resend CLI 2.0


On Wednesday, we shipped version 2.0 of the Resend CLI with four powerful new features:


- [Send emails using local React files](https://resend.com/blog/resend-cli-2#send-emails-using-local-react-files)
- [Manage Automations and events](https://resend.com/blog/resend-cli-2#automations-and-events) from your terminal
- [Listen to webhooks](https://resend.com/blog/resend-cli-2#webhook-listening) with a single command
- [Skills for AI agents](https://resend.com/blog/resend-cli-2#skills-for-ai-agents) to interact with the Resend API


The CLI now gives you access to 50+ commands across the full Resend API, without leaving your terminal.


[Resend CLI 2.0 Skills for AI agents, React Email support, Automations, and Webhook listening. All from the terminal. resend.com/blog/resend-cli-2](https://resend.com/blog/resend-cli-2)


## Day 4: Custom Tracking Domains


On Thursday, we launched custom tracking domains, free for all Resend users.


Tracking through a domain you own:


- Keeps your sender reputation isolated from shared infrastructure
- Aligns with your sending domain for better deliverability
- Shows your recipients a familiar domain on every link


It works for both[click tracking](https://resend.com/blog/introducing-custom-tracking-domain#1-click-tracking) and[open tracking](https://resend.com/blog/introducing-custom-tracking-domain#2-open-tracking) , and can be configured through the Dashboard or the API.


[Custom Tracking Domains Use your own domain for tracking links to improve deliverability and brand consistency. resend.com/blog/introducing-custom-tracking-domain](https://resend.com/blog/introducing-custom-tracking-domain)


## Day 5: React Email 6.0


On Friday, we announced a new major version of[react.email](https://react.email/) , featuring a new open-source email editor you can embed directly in your own app.


React Email 6 includes:


- **[An embeddable editor](https://resend.com/blog/react-email-6#open-source-editor)** that you can drop into your app with one line of code
- **[A composable extensions API](https://resend.com/blog/react-email-6#build-your-own-extensions)** to build your own custom blocks
- **[New templates](https://resend.com/blog/react-email-6#new-templates)** for authentication and e-commerce flows
- **[Unified package](https://resend.com/blog/react-email-6#unified-package)** for easier integration


[React Email 6.0 An open-source email editor you can embed in your own app, plus a new collection of templates. resend.com/blog/react-email-6](https://resend.com/blog/react-email-6)
