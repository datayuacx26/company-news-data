# Resend Connector for Claude

Our[remote MCP server](https://resend.com/changelog/remote-mcp-server) brought Resend to every agent. And it also enabled custom integrations with AI agents.


Today, Resend is available as a **one-click connector** in Claude's[connector directory](https://claude.ai/directory/connectors/resend) . Connect your account with OAuth and manage your entire Resend workspace from any Claude conversation.


The connector enables full MCP access to Claude along with all our Resend skills built-in.


## What you can do with it


- **Send from a conversation** : send a transactional email without leaving Claude.
- **Debug deliverability** : pull up logs, inspect a bounce, or check why an email failed.
- **Manage Contacts and Segments** : add contacts, build segments, and clean up your audiences in plain language.
- **Run Broadcasts** : draft, schedule, and send a broadcast to an audience.
- **Check Domains and API Keys** : verify domain status, review DNS records, and rotate API keys.


## Set it up


To set up the Resend connector:


1. Go to **Settings → Connectors** and search for Resend.
2. Click **Connect** .
3. Sign in with your Resend account and grant the permissions Claude requests.


It's the same[MCP experience](https://resend.com/changelog/remote-mcp-server) but with more security, better management, and improved DX.


## Manage connector access


You can view or revoke its OAuth permissions anytime from your Resend account's Team settings.


## Full coverage


The connector gives Claude native access to the full Resend platform through a single integration: emails, templates, broadcasts, contacts, logs, webhooks, and more.


[View a full list](https://resend.com/docs/mcp-server) of everything the server supports.
