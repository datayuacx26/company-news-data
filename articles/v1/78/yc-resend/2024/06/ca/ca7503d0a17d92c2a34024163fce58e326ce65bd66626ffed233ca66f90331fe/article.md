# Resend named to Redpoint's InfraRed 100

Resend has been included in the[Redpoint InfraRed 100](https://www.redpoint.com/infrared/100) . This prestigious list highlights the top 100 private companies in cloud infrastructure.


[Redpoint](https://www.redpoint.com/) , a top-tier venture capital firm with a portfolio including companies such as Snowflake, Twilio, Ramp, Nubank, HashiCorp, Netflix, and more, launched this list to showcase the builders who are creating industry-transforming companies.


To celebrate this, we visited Nasdaq with other founders to discuss the evolving landscape of cloud infrastructure.


Many of the late stage companies on this list served as inspiration for Resend, such as Stripe, OpenAI, Vercel, and others.


But we are also proud to be among the early stage companies on this list, such as Langchain, Ollama, Modal, Railway, and Zed.


List of early stage companies included on InfraRed 100


Resend is now only 18 months old, and we are just getting started.


We are thrilled to be the only communications platform selected for this year's list and look forward to continuing to build the future of email infrastructure.
