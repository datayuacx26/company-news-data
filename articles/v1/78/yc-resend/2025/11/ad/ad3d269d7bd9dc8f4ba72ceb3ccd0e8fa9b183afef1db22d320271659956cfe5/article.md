# Resend Forward 5: Wrap Up

Today marks the end of[Resend Forward](https://resend.com/forward) , our fifth launch week.


In the last seven days, we shipped six new features.


Here's a quick recap of what we launched:


- Day 1: Inbound Emails
- Day 2: Templates
- Day 3: New Contacts Experience
- Day 4: Unsubscribe Topics
- Day 5: React Email 5.0
- Day 6: One more (AI) thing


If you prefer watching instead of reading, check out this video:


## Day 1: Inbound Emails


On Monday, we announced general availability of our most-requested feature: Inbound Emails.


Inbound unlocks[entirely new use cases](https://resend.com/blog/inbound-emails#how-does-it-work) like:


- Replying to in-app emails
- Processing forwarded attachments
- Receiving support emails from users


[Inbound Emails Receive emails using webhooks. Parse content and attachments. Reply to your users. resend.com/blog/inbound-emails](https://resend.com/blog/inbound-emails)


## Day 2: Templates


Tuesday's launch introduced Templates, a new way to collaborate on emails with your entire team.


You can now:


- [Create a new Template](https://resend.com/blog/introducing-templates#creating-a-new-template) through the dashboard, the API, or by importing existing code.
- [Create template variables](https://resend.com/blog/introducing-templates#template-variables) to personalize email content.
- [Collaborate with your team in real-time](https://resend.com/blog/introducing-templates#collaboration) to craft the perfect email template.
- [Version your Templates](https://resend.com/blog/introducing-templates#versioning) and publish them to make them available for sending.


[Templates Create, edit, and send emails with Templates. resend.com/blog/introducing-templates](https://resend.com/blog/introducing-templates)


## Day 3: New Contacts Experience


On Wednesday, we shared the new Contacts experience:


- [Create custom properties](https://resend.com/blog/new-contacts-experience#custom-contact-properties)
- [Assign values to custom properties](https://resend.com/blog/new-contacts-experience#assign-custom-values-to-contacts) on your contacts
- Use these properties to[personalize your Broadcasts](https://resend.com/blog/new-contacts-experience#use-contact-properties-in-broadcasts)
- [View a Contact's engagement](https://resend.com/blog/new-contacts-experience#contact-activity-history) with your emails over time
- [Segment your Contacts](https://resend.com/blog/new-contacts-experience#segmenting-contacts) as you need


This new Contact model offers several benefits:


- You get a more accurate view of your interaction with each Contact
- You can personalize your broadcasts to each Contact
- You can segment your Contacts as you need
- We count your Contacts once, even if they are in multiple Segments


[New Contacts Experience Store custom properties on your contacts, track their activity, and segment them as you need. resend.com/blog/new-contacts-experience](https://resend.com/blog/new-contacts-experience)


## Day 4: Unsubscribe Topics


On Thursday, we introduced[Topics](https://resend.com/blog/unsubscribe-topics) , a new way to let users manage their email preferences.


You can now:


- [Create Topics](https://resend.com/blog/unsubscribe-topics#creating-a-new-topic) , which can be opt-in or opt-out by default
- [Assign Topics to Contacts](https://resend.com/blog/unsubscribe-topics#assigning-topics-to-contacts)
- [Send emails with specific Topics](https://resend.com/blog/unsubscribe-topics#how-it-works)


When users manage their preferences, they’ll see which Topics they’re currently subscribed to and can adjust their settings to their liking.


[Unsubscribe Topics Give your users more control over their subscription preferences. resend.com/blog/unsubscribe-topics](https://resend.com/blog/unsubscribe-topics)


## Day 5: React Email 5.0


On Friday, we announced a new major version of[react.email](https://react.email/) featuring:


- **[Dark Mode Switcher](https://resend.com/blog/react-email-5#dark-mode-switcher)**
- **[Tailwind 4](https://resend.com/blog/react-email-5#tailwind-4)**
- **[Resend Integration](https://resend.com/blog/react-email-5#resend-integration)**
- **[8 New Components](https://resend.com/blog/react-email-5#8-new-components)**


[React Email 5.0 New updates to React Email for improved UX and DX. resend.com/blog/react-email-5](https://resend.com/blog/react-email-5)


## Day 6: One more (AI) thing


On Saturday, we shared three new AI-powered features built to enhance your emails:


- [AI Subject Lines](https://resend.com/blog/one-more-ai-thing#ai-subject-lines)
- [AI Preview Text](https://resend.com/blog/one-more-ai-thing#ai-preview-text)
- [AI Image Alt Text](https://resend.com/blog/one-more-ai-thing#ai-image-alt-text)


All of these new features work for emails in all languages. We detect the language from the body of the email and generate text in the same language.


When used tastefully, AI can help you craft better emails and reach humans instead of spam filters.


[One more (AI) thing Use AI to generate subjects, preview texts, and alt texts in multiple languages. resend.com/blog/one-more-ai-thing](https://resend.com/blog/one-more-ai-thing)
