# Introducing new.email

This project has been discontinued.[Learn more](https://resend.com/blog/sunsetting-new-email) .


Today, we're excited to announce[new.email](https://new.email/) .


It's a new way to build beautiful, responsive, and cross-platform emails using natural language.


[Join the waitlist now](https://new.email/) to get early access.


## Why are we doing this?


When we started[react.email](https://react.email/) , the goal was to help **developers** create **modern** email templates. Building emails used to **take days** , and now it **takes minutes** .


But we're not done.


- What if you could build it in **seconds** , instead of **minutes** ?
- What if it were available for **everyone** , not just **developers** ?


That's why we built[new.email](https://new.email/) .


## What problem are we solving?


Every day, product teams need to send emails to their users.


Some teams can afford a developer to build their email templates, but many others depend on marketing, product, or design teams.


The result? Many companies send a mix of transactional and marketing templates that **don't look or feel like the rest** of the product.


Emails should be **consistent** , **beautiful** , **responsive** , and **cross-platform** , regardless of *who* is building them.


## Why now?


As heavy users of ChatGPT, Raycast AI, and Cursor ourselves, we've been exploring for a while how we can use LLMs to **make it easier for everyone** to send modern emails.


Today, we have a library of[54 high-quality email components](https://react.email/components) , and we were able to train an LLM to generate these templates.


Not only that, but we can **incorporate the deliverability insights** we've been learning by sending millions of emails every single day through Resend.


We've seen firsthand how products like Lovable, Bolt, and v0 are changing the definition of a developer.


Now, we're excited to explore what the future looks like for **LLMs + email** .
