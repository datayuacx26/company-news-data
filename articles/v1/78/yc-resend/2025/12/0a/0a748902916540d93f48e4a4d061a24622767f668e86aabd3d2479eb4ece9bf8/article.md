# Welcoming Danilo Campos, our new Design Engineer

[Danilo Campos](https://resend.com/humans/danilo-campos) is an award-winning designer based in Barcelona with a passion for coding. He has more than 15 years of experience in art direction, product design, and interaction design.


Our favorite project from him is[Pingado](https://pingado.app/) , a mobile app that helps you find curated brewing recipes and provides guidance to improve your coffee brewing skills.


[Pingado - Danilo's side project](https://pingado.app/)


You can see a taste of his work at[danilocampos.cc](https://danilocampos.cc/) .


## More about Danilo


**How did you get into design?**


When I was 8 years old, my older brother bought our first computer.


I already enjoyed drawing, so I quickly began exploring Paint Brush. A few years later, I started creating compositions in Photoshop to share with my friends and family. Once we got an internet connection, I discovered FrontPage, Dreamweaver, and Flash. I realized I could use code to create things I loved and share them with the world.


Since then, I've never stopped exploring the intersection of design and code.

Danilo's desktop


**Why are you at Resend?**


I've been following Resend for a while and have always admired the craft and attention to detail in every aspect of the product and branding. I couldn't be more excited to join the team, collaborating with people I admire to ship amazing things together.


**Where do you find #inspiration?**


I'm always looking for inspiration from other designers and studios. I even built my own[repository](https://re-mind.danilocampos.cc/) to save favorites. I also find references in the real world: music, street culture, books, and architecture.


**Favorite tool?**


Figma is my favorite tool. It's incredible how easy it makes collaboration between designers, and I use it every day for different purposes.


**Favorite hotkey?**


` Command + N` : Sometimes the blank page is scary, but it is also an opportunity to create something new.


**Favorite place to visit?**


I love visiting cafés where I can sit and enjoy my coffee with my wife and our dog, or with friends.


Isa and our dog, Alice, at Three Marks Coffee in Barcelona


**Advice for ambitious designers?**


Master the fundamentals and collaborate with people you admire and can learn from.
