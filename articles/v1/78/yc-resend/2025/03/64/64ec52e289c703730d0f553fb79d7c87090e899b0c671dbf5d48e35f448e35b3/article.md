# Multiplayer Editor

Sending a single email from one person to another is typically a solo player game. But what if you're working on a broadcast to send to an entire list of contacts?


The truth is that email marketing is a team sport, and that's why we're excited to announce the multiplayer editor.


The new multiplayer editor makes email creation a truly collaborative experience. Teams can now work together seamlessly, whether they're:


- Drafting content in real-time
- Fine-tuning designs together
- Improving copy together


This approach helps teams create better emails faster, while maintaining consistency and quality across all communications.


## Presence Indicators


Now, you can see who's currently viewing and editing the email.


## Live Cursors


You can see your teammates' cursors in real-time as they move through the editor.


This visual feedback creates a more collaborative environment where team members can:


- Coordinate edits and avoid conflicts
- See who's actively reviewing content
- Get immediate feedback from stakeholders


## Real-Time Fields


Not only is the email content collaborative, but all the fields in the editor update in real-time.


This includes the subject, preview text, and recipient, and even setting a scheduled date using natural language.


## Collaborative Styling


You can see your teammates' styling changes updated in real-time as they tweak the design.


All the font sizes, background colors, and custom CSS changes are visible to everyone.


## Conclusion


We're excited to partner with[Liveblocks](https://liveblocks.io/) to bring a true multiplayer experience to the email world.


And we can't wait to see how teams use these new features to streamline their email workflows.
