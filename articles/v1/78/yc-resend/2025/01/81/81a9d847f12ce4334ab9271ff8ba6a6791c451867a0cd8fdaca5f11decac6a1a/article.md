# Welcoming Giovana Yahiro, our new Designer Engineer

[Giovana Yahiro](https://resend.com/humans/giovana-yahiro) is a designer with 10 years of experience creating remarkable digital experiences.


She is the designer responsible for the Apple Award finalist app[Creme](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/creme-create-memorable-meals/id1638361026) . Before Resend, she worked at well-known design studios like[PORTO ROCHA](https://portorocha.com/) and[Work.co](https://work.co/) .


Her work exemplifies another level of craft and a unique talent for translating ideas into exceptional interaction design. You can see a glimpse of her work at[yahiro.work](https://yahiro.work/) .


## More about Giovana


**How did you get into design?**


Growing up, I was always a curious kid who loved figuring out how things worked. When I got my first computer, my curiosity grew even more. I quickly became fascinated by the web and wanted to understand how it all came together. That curiosity led me into creating my own websites, and I picked up a bit of programming along the way. Over time, that fascination evolved into a true passion, leading me into the world of design.

Giovana's desktop


**Why are you at Resend?**


I've been following Resend for quite a while, and I've always admired their work – the attention to detail, the level of craft and the delightful little features that make everything feel so unique and special. Joining the team gives me the chance not only to contribute to something meaningful but also to collaborate with people who genuinely care.


**Where do you find #inspiration?**


I find inspiration by being around people I admire. Whether it's through their work, conversations, or simply their energy – I always pick up on something that sparks inspiration in me.


**If you weren't designing, what would you be doing?**


As a kid, I always dreamed of becoming a veterinarian. Growing up surrounded by all kinds of animals, I developed a deep love and respect for them, which made me want to help care for them. So if I weren't designing, I hope I would be fulfilling this childhood dream.


**Favorite tool?**


Figma has been my favorite tool for a while now. I use it every day – for work, for side projects, or whenever I need to prototype anything.


**Favorite hotkey?**


` Command + Shift + 4` : It captures a portion of the screen. I'm constantly screenshotting and sharing things.


**Favorite place to visit?**


Any forest where I can hike with my dogs and surround myself with nature. It helps me reconnect and reset.


**Advice for ambitious designers?**


Surround yourself with people you admire – those who inspire you and genuinely want to see you grow.
