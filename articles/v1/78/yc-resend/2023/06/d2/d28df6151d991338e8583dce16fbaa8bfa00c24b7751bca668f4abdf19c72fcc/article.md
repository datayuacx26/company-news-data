# New Free Tier

We have exciting news for small teams and solo developers.


You can now **send up to 3,000 emails a month (100 per day) on our free plan** .


If you are currently on the free plan, your account has already been upgraded.


## Why We Did It


As developers ourselves, we know how important it is to have generous free tiers when you're hacking a side project, mailing your initial batch of users, or exploring new features like magic links.


We felt like 100 emails a month was not enough, so we **increased it by 30x** .


## What Else Changes


Along with larger sending quota, we are also adjusting some other limits on the free tier:


**Domains** : Now you can have up to one domain per team. If that isn't enough, all Pro plans will unlock unlimited domains.


**Multi-Region** : We love to see how people are reducing time to inbox with regional sending. Moving forward, selecting a region will only be available on a Pro plan.


If this change affects you and you'd like to talk to us about it, please[reach out](https://resend.com/contact) .
