# Avery Dennison® Launches Neo™ Matte Black Paint Protection Film | Avery Dennison | Graphics

## Avery Dennison® Launches Neo™ Matte Black Paint Protection Film, Expanding Premium Wet-Apply Color PPF Line


MENTOR, Ohio – October 28, 2025 – Avery Dennison® Graphics Solutions (NYSE:AVY) today announced the release of Neo™ Matte Black Paint Protection Film (PPF), an exciting addition to its premium wet-apply color PPF line. This highly anticipated film joins the successful Neo™ Noir Gloss Black PPF to give installers and car enthusiasts the two most popular black aesthetics backed by superior protection.


New Avery Dennison Neo Matte Black PPF is crafted for drivers who demand a sleek, modern style and a non-reflective, stealth aesthetic.


Neo Matte Black is crafted for drivers who demand a sleek, modern style and noticeable color depth. Its non-reflective finish offers dramatic restyling, a stealth aesthetic, and the lasting protection of a paint protection film.


The science-driven innovation of this unique polyurethane technology provides unmatched defense against rock chips, scratches, road debris, and long-term weathering. At a robust total 8.5 mil thickness, the film ensures the style is matched by enduring protection. Backed by a 7-year warranty, the wet-apply film also offers excellent adhesion, self-healing performance, a hydrophobic topcoat, stain resistance, and outstanding conformability around curves and recesses.


Installers will find Neo Matte Black highly versatile and installer-friendly. It can be applied as a full wrap for a complete color change or used to create stunning accents. The exceptionally smooth finish enables installers to consistently deliver impressive results.


“We believe car owners shouldn't have to choose between a show-stopping finish and long-term durability. Neo Matte Black is the embodiment of that promise,” said Brittany Folino, senior product manager, Avery Dennison Graphics Solutions. “It’s the impressive blend of modern, aggressive styling and guaranteed protection that our installers can confidently stand behind.”


Neo Matte Black joins the successful Neo Noir Gloss Black PPF to give installers and car enthusiasts the two most popular black aesthetics backed by superior protection.
