# Avery Dennison® Premieres Two New Supreme Wrapping Film™ - Blue Shades Just in Time for Summer | Avery Dennison | Graphics

## Avery Dennison® Premieres Two New Supreme Wrapping Film™


### Blue Shades Just in Time for Summer


MENTOR, Ohio – June 18, 2025 – Just in time for summer, Avery Dennison® (NYSE:AVY) Graphics Solutions today debuted two captivating new colors for its Supreme Wrapping Film™ line: Gloss Park Avenue Blue and Gloss Turquoise Ocean. These vibrant additions offer a sophisticated spectrum of blue-green shades, evoking everything from iconic luxury to pristine ocean waves, bringing sleek elegance to the world of automotive color change wraps.


The Avery Dennison Gloss Park Avenue Blue (pictured here) offers bold energy for discriminating drivers.


The new additions to the Avery Dennison portfolio are:


- **Gloss Park Avenue Blue:** This sophisticated and elegant blue-green instantly elevates any vehicle with a clean, classic feel, exuding timeless luxury and iconic refinement.
- **Gloss Turquoise Ocean:** Capturing the electric pulse of a vibrant, high-energy coastline, this dynamic turquoise brings sophisticated energy and a bold, modern edge to any vehicle.


“These exquisite new blues have been carefully curated to provide dramatic options for car enthusiasts,” said Brittany Folino, senior product manager, Avery Dennison Graphics Solutions North America. “They offer dynamic options for the automotive customization and restyling community, perfectly capturing the spirit of summer.”


Avery Dennison Supreme Wrapping Film combines an extensive color change collection with outstanding durability, 3D conformability and performance, custom versatility and easy-to-apply convenience.


Supreme Wrapping Film color change vehicle wraps feature Avery Dennison Easy Apply™ RS technology. This innovation makes application faster and easier, regardless of the skill level of the applicator. The technology enables installers to reposition the film and easily press out any wrinkles and bubbles. Moreover, the film and technology combination ensures excellent conformability around challenging curves and recesses.


Folino added that the expansion of the portfolio reflects the growing popularity and demand for Avery Dennison Supreme Wrapping Films. "This material continues to be the product of choice for premium vehicle wraps. It empowers installers to consistently deliver optimum impact and quality, ensuring client satisfaction with every project."
