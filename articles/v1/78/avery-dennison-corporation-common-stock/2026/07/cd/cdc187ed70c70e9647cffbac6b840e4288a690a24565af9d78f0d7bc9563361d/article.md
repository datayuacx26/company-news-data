# Avery Dennison® Expands Supreme Wrapping Film™ Portfolio with Five New Trend-Setting Colors

# Avery Dennison® Expands Supreme Wrapping Film™ Portfolio with Five New Trend-Setting Colors


MENTOR, Ohio – May 28, 2026 – Today Avery Dennison® (NYSE:AVY) Graphics Solutions debuted five new colors for its flagship Supreme Wrapping Film™ color change vehicle wrapping film product line. These strategic color additions provide installers with high-demand finishes that bridge the gap between luxury aesthetics and the reliable, professional-grade performance the industry expects from the SW900 series.


Avery Dennison debuted five new colors of its Supreme Wrapping Film™ color change vehicle wrapping film product line. Pictured from left to right, the colors are Gloss Ivory, Gloss Metallic Gothic Rose, Gloss Sand Dune, Matte Metallic Oasis Blue, and Satin Metallic Cyber Green.


All five films deliver the easy conformability Supreme Wrapping Film is known for by shops and installers worldwide. The new additions to the Avery Dennison portfolio include:


- **Gloss Ivory -** A sophisticated light beige that redefines "Quiet Luxury"
- **Gloss Sand Dune -** A timeless, medium beige designed to mirror modern OEM paint finishes
- **Satin Metallic Cyber Green -** An electric finish that captures light across every curve with a smooth, metallic glow
- **Matte Metallic Oasis Blue -** A serene turquoise blue that evokes ultimate tranquility
- **Gloss Metallic Gothic Rose -** A deep, moody rose with a heavy metallic flake for a premium custom-paint appearance


Gloss Ivory Supreme Wrapping Film from Avery Dennison is a sophisticated light beige that redefines ‘Quiet Luxury.


SW900 Gloss Sand Dune offers a timeless, medium beige designed to mirror modern OEM paint finishes.


An electric finish that captures light across every curve with a smooth, metallic glow – this is the new SW900 film color Satin Metallic Cyber Green from Avery Dennison.


The Spring 2026 Supreme Wrapping Film collection includes Matte Metallic Oasis Blue, which is a serene turquoise blue that evokes ultimate tranquility.


The new Avery Dennison Supreme Wrapping Film color Gloss Metallic Gothic Rose is a deep, moody rose with a heavy metallic flake for a premium custom-paint appearance.


"The stunning new tones in the Avery Dennison spring collection have been carefully curated to reflect the latest shifts in automotive styling," said Brittany Folino, Senior Product Manager for Avery Dennison Graphics Solutions. "These finishes allow the professional restyling community to offer their clients unique, trend-forward looks while maintaining the highest standard of installation quality."


Avery Dennison Supreme Wrapping Film combines an extensive color change collection with more than 140 film colors. The films deliver outstanding durability, 3D conformability and performance, custom versatility and installation convenience.


Supreme Wrapping Film color change vehicle wraps feature Avery Dennison Easy Apply™ RS technology. This innovation makes application faster and easier, regardless of the applicator's skill level. The technology enables installers to reposition the film and easily press out any wrinkles and bubbles. Moreover, the film and technology combination ensures excellent conformability around challenging curves and recesses.


"This latest extension signifies our commitment to the rising demand for premium finishes that perform as well as they look," Folino said. "Supreme Wrapping Film continues to be the professional's first choice because it delivers the quality and client satisfaction required in high-end restyling."
