# Helium Health Deploys HeliumDoc AI to Improve Healthcare Delivery System in Delta and Bayelsa States.

Helium Health, in partnership with the GSMA and supported by UK Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office, today unveils the HeliumDoc disaster-response initiative across Delta and Bayelsa State. This project embeds a web and USSD-based patient engagement platform into routine and emergency workflows, ensuring that critical healthcare services—including maternal, child, and chronic-disease care—remain uninterrupted during flood events.


This project shows what’s possible when innovation meets local insight. HeliumDoc AI, co-designed with healthcare providers and supported by the GSMA Innovation Fund for Humanitarian Challenges, is now being deployed in partnership with the Delta and Bayelsa State governments to help communities access quality healthcare both for their everyday needs and in times of emergency, such as floods and other natural hazards.


> ***“This tool also enhances the ability of healthcare providers to deliver timely, effective care no matter the circumstances*** ” said Ralph Oluwole, Co-founder Helium Health.


HeliumDoc’s intuitive interface guides users to operational facilities via real-time maps, broadcasts early-warning messages through AI-powered voice and SMS alerts, and connects patients to remote clinicians for teleconsultations. Communities without internet can simply dial * *347** 478# on any feature phone to access these services. Secure, on-demand retrieval of clinical records preserves continuity of care when infrastructure is compromised.


“Whether it’s a routine check-up or a crisis situation, healthcare must be accessible.That’s why we built HeliumDoc AI with input from local providers. In partnership with the State Governments, we’re rolling out this solution across Delta and Bayelsa first – for all users, all mobile device types, and all situations.”” said Dr Peter Ameh, Medical Operations Lead of Helium Health. “


**“ *By enabling virtual consultations in flood-affected regions, this digital solution ensures continuity of care even when physical infrastructure is compromised. This initiative highlights the critical role mobile technology can play in enhancing disaster preparedness and response for vulnerable and displaced communities*** ” said Kimberly Brown, Head of GSMA Mobile for Humanitarian Innovation.


Over the next quarter, Helium Health will train 350 healthcare workers in digital health delivery and emergency protocols, integrate HeliumDoc into both public and private facilities, and conduct widespread community sensitization—including radio features, town-hall demonstrations, and printed guides—to reach at least 10,000 residents in both Delta & Bayelsa State’s most vulnerable areas.


HeliumDoc is now live in Delta and Bayelsa State. To explore the platform, visit[www.heliumdoc.com/ng/](https://heliumdoc.com/ng/) or dial *** *347** 478#.**
