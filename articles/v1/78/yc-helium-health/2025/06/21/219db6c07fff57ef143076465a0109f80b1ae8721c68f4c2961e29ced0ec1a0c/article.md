# Helium Health Is Reimagining Preventive Care for Nigerian Youths

**In Nigeria, youths are at the heart of our future. But for too long, they’ve been left behind when it comes to healthcare. At Helium Health, we set out to change that—with empathy, innovation, and AI.**


As one of the finalists in the 2024 HealthTech Hub Africa (HTHA) Innovation Challenge, Helium Health had the opportunity to deploy a bold new initiative: an AI-powered digital platform called[HeliumDoc](http://heliumdoc.com/ng) , designed to help young people check their risk of[HIV,](https://heliumdoc.com/ng/public-health/ai-voice-call/hiv-risk-assessment/)[Tuberculosis (TB)](https://heliumdoc.com/ng/public-health/ai-voice-call/tuberculosis-risk-assessment/) , and[Diabetes](https://heliumdoc.com/ng/public-health/ai-voice-call/diabetes-risk-assessment/) —privately, easily, and on their own terms.


#### **The Problem We Were Solving**


Youths aged 15–35 in Nigeria face a disproportionate burden of disease, yet they remain one of the least served segments of the population. Financial dependence, fear of stigma, and distrust in traditional systems often delay or prevent them from seeking timely care. For many, even a simple health check feels out of reach.


The results? Late diagnoses. Missed opportunities for prevention. Poorer health outcomes.


We knew that to change the story, we needed more than technology. We needed a platform that was **youth-responsive, behaviorally informed, and community-integrated** .


#### **What We Built: HeliumDoc, Reimagined**


HeliumDoc was upgraded to meet this challenge. The platform now features:


- **Location-based referrals** to nearby clinics and providers
- **Risk stratification** for **[HIV](https://heliumdoc.com/ng/public-health/hiv-risk-assessment/1/) ,[TB](https://heliumdoc.com/ng/public-health/tuberculosis-risk-assessment/1/) ,** and **[Diabetes](https://heliumdoc.com/ng/public-health/diabetes-risk-assessment/1/)**
- **Voice AI capabilities** for users who call in or have an audio stratification
- **User friendly interface** optimized for young, on-the-go users
- **Data** **feedback loops** to inform future public health interventions
- **Realtime** feedback on risk to drive awareness and inform lifestyle modification.


And we didn’t build it alone. We co-created the tool with young people, public health officials, and frontline healthcare workers through a series of workshops and user testing sessions in Delta State.


#### **The Impact: Proof That It Works**


In just a few months, we saw remarkable results:


Perhaps most powerfully, the vast majority of users reported feeling informed, empowered, and heard—many for the first time in their healthcare journey—fostering a measurable increase in health-seeking behaviors across communities.


#### **What We’re Learning**


Digital health isn’t just about apps or algorithms. It’s about **access** , **trust** , and **designing for the realities people live in** .


Young people in Delta State responded to HeliumDoc because it respected their privacy, met them in their communities, and offered guidance without judgment. The success of this pilot proves that when digital tools are grounded in empathy, they work.


It also reinforces what we’ve long believed: **public health systems must evolve beyond diagnosis and treatment. They must embrace prevention, personalization, and co-creation.**


#### **What’s Next?**


We’re now working to deepen partnerships with state governments, integrate HeliumDoc into broader health infrastructure, improve the capabilities of our AI with local languages and explore similar deployments across other regions in Nigeria and beyond.


The HTHA project was just the beginning.
We envision a future where every young person—no matter where they live—has the tools to understand their health risks early, make informed decisions, and access care without barriers.
