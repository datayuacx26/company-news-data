# Announcing Helium Health’s $30M Series B

**LAGOS** , 5 June 2023 — Helium Health (the Company), a Lagos-headquartered healthtech company providing Software-as-a-service (SaaS) tools, financing, and insights to healthcare providers and public health organizations, announced today it has raised $30M in Series-B funding. The round was led by “[AXA Investment Managers Alts](https://alts.axa-im.com/) ” with participation from[Capria Ventures](https://capria.vc/) ,[Angaza Capital](https://www.angazacapital.com/) ,[Anne Wojcicki](https://www.linkedin.com/in/annewojcicki/) (Founder of 23&Me) and[Flatworld Partners](https://flatworld-partners.com/) . Existing investors[Global Ventures](https://www.global.vc/) ,[Tencent](https://www.tencent.com/en-us/) ,[Ohara Pharmaceuticals](https://www.ohara-ch.co.jp/english/) ,[LCY Group](https://www.lcygroup.com/lcygroup/en/) , WTI and[AAIC](https://aa-ic.com/en/top-en/) also participated in the round.


Helium Health will use the capital to expand the reach of its fintech product,[HeliumCredit](https://heliumhealth.com/helium-credit/) , the leading digital finance product for Africa’s healthcare sector. Launched in 2020,[HeliumCredit](https://heliumhealth.com/helium-credit/) has extended more than $3.5M in credit to over 200 healthcare facilities in Nigeria, including hospitals, clinics, pharmacies, and diagnostics centers, which have used the loans to purchase medical equipment, medications in bulk, and expand their locations. With easier access to credit, healthcare providers are able to increase patient retention, increase revenues, and improve the quality of care. Helium Health will launch[HeliumCredit](https://heliumhealth.com/helium-credit/) in Kenya this year. The Company will increase its lending portfolio to 1,000 healthcare facilities by 2024 in partnership with the U.S. International Development Finance Corporation (DFC).


In this growth phase, Helium Health also looks to deepen its collaborations within the public health and global health communities, another core focus of its work. Global health funders contribute about 15% of total health expenditure in sub-Saharan Africa and play a critical role in tackling the continent’s leading causes of death, such as HIV/AIDS, malaria, tuberculosis, maternal and child mortality. Helium Health aims to be the leading technology partner for public health entities by supporting the implementation of their programs and strategies in Africa. In the past year, Helium Health has received funding from organizations such as the[Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation](https://www.gatesfoundation.org/about/committed-grants/2022/11/inv-046250) and[MSD for Mothers](http://saferchildbirthsystems.com/where-we-work) to execute projects under their maternal health program strategies.


The Company will also continue to scale its SaaS suite for healthcare providers, through[HeliumOS](https://heliumhealth.com/helium-os/) , its Electronic Medical Records and Hospital Management Information System (EMR/HMIS) solution.[HeliumOS](https://heliumhealth.com/helium-os/) helps health facilities of any size or specialty run their operations more efficiently, increase revenues, and improve healthcare delivery. Used by more than 10,000 health workers to care for over 1 million patients, it is the widest-reaching EMR platform in West Africa. Helium Health is able to evaluate the creditworthiness of loan applicants and disburse loans as fast as 48 hours by leveraging its insights into the finances and operations of healthcare providers obtained from[HeliumOS](https://heliumhealth.com/helium-os/) . The Company continues to record high loan repayment rates.


Africa’s healthcare sector is heavily undercapitalized, with a financing deficit of $66BN per year. This lack of capital results in poorly developed infrastructure and a scarcity of health workers. Africa has one of the lowest health worker-to-patient ratios in the world, with 1.55 health workers per 1000 people. A poor-resourced environment, coupled with the lack of digitization and automation, negatively impacts healthcare delivery, leading to medical errors and long patient wait times. Less than 10% of healthcare facilities in Africa are digitized. Helium Health seeks to tackle this challenge by expanding the reach of its digital solutions to more healthcare facilities across Africa and helping under-resourced facilities deliver high-quality care.


“ *We believe in a future where good healthcare is a reality for all Africans, not just the few. We are deeply committed to supporting both private healthcare providers and public health stakeholders with finance, technology, and data to achieve that vision. We are delighted to have such seasoned healthcare investors accompany us on our journey,” said Adegoke Olubusi, Helium Health CEO/co-founder.*


“ *We are delighted to invest, through “AXA Investment Managers Alts” impact investing strategies, in Helium Health’s mission of providing digital solutions to improve the quality and efficacy of health services in resource constrained environments, whilst also directly equipping health sector enterprises with affordable financial services. This investment directly contributes to “AXA Investment Managers Alts” broader impact goals of improving financial inclusion and reducing inequalities globally,” said Jonathan Dean, Head of Impact Investing at “AXA Investment Managers Alts* “.


“ *We have seen first-hand the evolution of Helium Health over the years. The leadership team has a deep understanding of Africa’s healthcare sector and knows how to build products that meet its nuanced needs,” said Noor Sweid, Managing Partner, Global Ventures* .


### ABOUT HELIUM HEALTH


Helium Health is a Lagos-headquartered technology company that is revolutionizing Africa’s healthcare sector through technology, finance, and data. Helium Health offers a suite of SaaS products – EMR, HMIS/ERP, Revenue Cycle Management, and appointment booking/telemedicine – that act as digital infrastructure for healthcare providers, helping them to run their operations more efficiently, increase revenues, and improve the delivery of healthcare. With[HeliumCredit](https://heliumhealth.com/helium-credit/) , healthcare providers can access financing to scale their operations. Public health organizations can use Helium Health data to support better decision-making in their public health programs. Since its launch in 2016, Helium Health has worked with more than 1,000 healthcare facilities and 10,000 health workers, in addition to serving over 1M patients across six African markets and two GCC markets. Helium Health has 150 employees across 10 countries. Find out more at[http://www.heliumhealth.com](https://heliumhealth.com/) .
