# Space


CACI is a leading U.S.-based provider of complete, multi-source, actionable intelligence to the warfighter across all domains. We leverage decades of expertise supporting space missions – ranging from national security to human spaceflight – to deliver positive outcomes and protect our nation’s interests in the highly complex domain of space.

Our Capabilities


#### Mission operations


We operate some of the largest and most complex national security space mission systems, delivering end to end operations, sustainment, and technical expertise for resilient, continuous execution. We integrate mission data through industry-leading EO/IR, hyperspectral sensors, and operationally proven Agentic AI enabled imaging across all domains.


[Learn More](https://caci-dam.widen.net/s/ssghf6fzgr/20260327a_caci_fs_mission-operations)


#### Space mission management


CACI is leading the development of next generation automated space mission management and command and control systems, leveraging cutting edge AI techniques to deliver adaptive, intelligent scheduling and C2 capabilities that provide decision advantage at mission speed.


[Learn More](https://www.caci.com/space-domain-awareness)


#### Space data processing: sensing and sense-making


Leveraging our multi-phenomenology expertise, CACI’s sensemaking converts data into actionable intelligence through advance digital signal processing and Agentic AI. We deliver the tools that provide operational intelligence views with improved feature extraction, change detection, and robust geospatial analysis.


[Learn More](https://www.caci.com/PNT)


#### Counterspace


CACI develops and operates fixed and deployable EW solutions that provide custom signal processing systems, from front-end collection to operator visualization, to rapidly sense, deter, and counter adversarial space threats.


#### Optical communications


From the battlefield to deep space, CACI’s U.S.-made, mature, proven, low-risk optical communications technology and high-precision optics support some of our nation’s most critical missions.


[Learn More](https://www.caci.com/optical-communications)


#### Artificial intelligence


CACI delivers mission advantage by operationalizing advanced AI technologies where they create decisive impact. Our AI capabilities accelerate critical workflows, elevate decision‑making, and help operators stay ahead of rapidly evolving threats.


[Learn More](https://www.caci.com/artificial-intelligence)
