# 10 Top Medicare Supplement (Medigap) Companies for 2025

## 10 Top Medicare Supplement (Medigap) Companies for 2025


By Daniel Petkevich


Jul 14, 2025


## A quick overview of 10 Medicare Supplement companies you should consider.


*(Listed alphabetically to avoid implying a formal ranking)*


### Speak with a Medicare Advocate


Talk to one of our Licensed Medicare Agents today to find the right plans for you.


### AARP® / UnitedHealthcare


The country’s biggest Medigap issuer, available in all 50 states, backed by an A+ (Superior) AM Best rating, and consistently high customer-satisfaction scores.


### Aetna (Accendo / CVS Health)


Part of CVS Health, offers aggressive pricing on standard and high-deductible Plan G, strong enrollment growth, and solid financial strength.


### Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield


Multiple state affiliates with some of the lowest average Plan G rates in recent quote studies, plus household discounts in many markets.


### Bankers Fidelity


A 70-year-old niche carrier with responsive claims service and an A- (Excellent) AM Best rating—often appealing to shoppers who value smaller-company attention.


### Blue Cross Blue Shield (other affiliates)


Deep provider networks and, in many states, a “Blue-to-Blue” rule that lets members switch Medigap letters without fresh underwriting.


### Cigna


A (Excellent) AM Best rating, nationwide availability, multi-line household discounts, and a 4-star quality score from third-party reviewers.


### Humana


Nationwide footprint with member perks such as vision, hearing, and fitness benefits. Premiums can run higher than average, so shop the rate.


### ManhattanLife


Founded in 1850, independent, and flexible with health underwriting in some states. AM Best B++ (Good) and trending upward.


### Mutual of Omaha


Second-largest Medigap book, up to 12 % household discount, and an industry-low NAIC complaint index—excellent for long-term rate stability.


### State Farm


Top-tier customer-service ratings and highly competitive Plan G pricing in recent carrier-comparison research.


---


### How We Chose These Carriers


-


**Market share & stability** – the ten listed companies represent roughly two-thirds of all Medigap enrollment.


-


**Financial strength** – A- or better AM Best ratings (or improving B++) signal the reserves to keep paying claims.


-


**Customer experience** – we analyzed NAIC complaint indexes and third-party reviews.


-


**Plan G availability & pricing** – every carrier above offers Plan G, today’s most comprehensive option for new Medicare beneficiaries.


Remember: Medigap benefits are federally standardized—Plan G at one company covers the same gaps as Plan G at another. Price, discounts, underwriting rules, and service reputation are what set carriers apart.
