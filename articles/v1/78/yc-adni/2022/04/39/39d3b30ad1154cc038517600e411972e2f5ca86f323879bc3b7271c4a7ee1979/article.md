# #TeamLumify Nurses' Week Tour


Hey #LumifyFam! Can you believe it's almost time for Nurses' Week?! At Lumify, we believe that you deserve a celebration much bigger than a pizza party... or a rock. If you know, you know.


That's why we're excited to be hosting pop-up events in 8 cities throughout Nurses' Week! At each event, we'll have free food, giveaways, merch, and more just for you!


🎉👩‍⚕️🩺


[RSVP links here!!](https://linktr.ee/LumifyCare)


Don't forget to download the[Lumify App](https://www.lumifycare.com/) (or sign-up for the[Lumify Hub](https://www.lumifyhub.com/) for Android users) - it is your ticket to join us and stay up to date with our tour. 💙


**May 6th - Philadelphia, PA:** We want to see, hear, and feel your Philly pride...#goEagles! Meet us @WokWorks Food truck near Einstein Hospital from 12:00 - 2:00 PM- download the Lumify App and eat a delicious, free lunch with us AND enter to win some awesome giveaways! 🦅


**May 6th - Fresno, CA:** Come kick off nurses week with us - download the Lumify App and join us at Papi's for a night of laughs, mingling, and tasty tacos! We have some pretty big Central Valley fans at #TeamLumify... #gobulldogs.🐾 RSVP


[here](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/celebrate-nurses-week-w-lumify-tickets-328310935847?utm-campaign=social&utm-content=attendeeshare&utm-medium=discovery&utm-term=listing&utm-source=cp&aff=escb) .


**May 7th - Durham, NC:** Who doesn't love donuts? Even better... we partnered with the #1 donut shop in the Research Triangle @Early Bird Donuts to give y'all the donuts you deserve! Download the Lumify App and get a free donut and coffee from 6:00-2:00 PM. 🔬🔺


**May 8th - Brooklyn, NY:** We like treats & we know nurses love treats too! We partnered with the New Jersey famous[Brownie Bar](https://browniebar.com/) !! #Lumify and the Brownie Bar will be at Mount Sinai Brooklyn 1342 Kings Highway from 1:30-3:30 PM and is giving free brownies trios to healthcare workers who download the Lumify App. 🥛🍫 🍪


**May 9th - Boston, MA:** Baseball season is pretty huge from what we hear from our friends in Boston. #TeamLumify partnered with #NurseWallet to bring a night filled with giveaways, food, drinks, and more at Cask'n'Flagon from 6:30 -8:30 PM directly next to the historic #Fenway Park! ⚾️ Please RSVP[here](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/celebrate-nurses-week-w-nursewallet-and-lumify-registration-325001948577?utm-campaign=social&utm-content=attendeeshare&utm-medium=discovery&utm-term=listing&utm-source=cp&aff=escb) .


**May 10th - IG Live:** For our #TeamLumify that can't make it to celebrate in person with us..follow IG[@lumifycare](https://www.instagram.com/lumifycare/) and join us for an unreal lineup of GIVEAWAYS on IG Live! 🎁


**May 12th - San Francisco, CA:** Rain or shine, Karl the fog or not! We are making our way to Mission Bay to celebrate our Bay Area nurses at @Spark Social... get ready for a night of epic giveaways, fantastic SF food, and mingling with some Bay Area healthcare creators! Don't forget to pack a light jacket in case Karl shows up 😋 RSVP


[here](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/nurses-week-with-lumify-sf-tickets-327604422647) .


**May 13th - Burbank, CA:** Woot woot - we are popping up at Providence St. Joesph's Hospital with some partner brands to bring you a day of giveaways, fun, and mingling. LA, here we come! 😎


**May 13th - Houston, TX:** For all our critical care friends out there we will be right there with you at the NTI AACN conference. Come stop by our pop up meet up with us and enjoy free giveaways! 🧑‍⚕️🩺💊


We cannot wait to celebrate with you soon!! Follow us @lumifycare, share your #LumifyFam pics, and let us know the best part about your #LumifyNursesWeek!
