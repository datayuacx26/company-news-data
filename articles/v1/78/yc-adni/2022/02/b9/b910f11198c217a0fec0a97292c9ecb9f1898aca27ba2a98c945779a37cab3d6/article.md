# Lumify x Taylor Made Scrub Hats


At Lumify, we want to support you from head to toe!


That's why we're excited to collab with our friends at Taylor Made Scrub Hats to bring you a comfortable, light-weight, and stylish scrub hat!


Each scrub hat comes embroidered with the Lumify heart icon, ensuring you can show off your #LumifyFam pride throughout your shift.


Taylor Made Scrub Hats is a nurse-led scrubwear brand built to empower medical professionals through self-expression. With two goals in mind, staying safe & staying stylish, TMSH was created to give healthcare workers another reason to proudly wear their uniform every day.


What you wear impacts how you feel, and therefore, how you perform. Exceptional scrub hats themselves might not save lives, but they can ensure you look and feel great while you do!
