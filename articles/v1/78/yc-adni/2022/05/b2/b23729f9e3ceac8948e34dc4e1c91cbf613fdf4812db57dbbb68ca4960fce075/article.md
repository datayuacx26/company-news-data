# Celebrating AAPI Month!


#### We're celebrating #APPIHeritageMonth by highlighting our AAPI-Founded #LumifyPartners!


#### **Asian Care Unit 👨‍⚕️🩺💯**


#### Clara Jones, MSN, FNP-C


#### The Asian Care Unit is a home base for nurses, Physical Therapists, Dieticians, Occupational Therapists, Doctors, students, and more to share their lived experiences that are unique to Asians working in healthcare. Shop ACU on the Lumify Marketplace!


#### **Friends of Franz Podcast 🎤🗣🩺**


#### Christian Franz, BSN, RN


#### Friends of Franz is an exclusive look on the healthcare industry. Christian Franz, a Registered Nurse in New York City, and student at NYU in postbac premedical studies, hosts interdisciplinary clinicians and they tackle field-specific topics. You can access Friends on Franz on the Lumify Hub!


#### **Flowly 💆‍♀️🧘‍♀️🧖‍♀️**


#### Celine Tien and Narae Kim


#### Flowly is designed to help you relax and help better manage pain through a virtual reality experience. You are able to access relevant data to your journey in the Flowly app and join the Flowly community and health coaches as you progress! You can access Flowly in the Lumify Hub!


#### **MedVenture ✈️👨‍⚕️**


#### Emily Cheng BSN, RN, CCRN-CSC


#### MedVenture is the perfect app for travel nurses who are looking to mingle, meet up, and get to know their new home! Travel Nurses even leave reviews of facilities so you can decide your next big travel adventure and ensure it is the perfect fit for you! You can access MedVenture in the Lumify Hub!


#### **TX Socks 🧦**


#### Fanny, BSN, RN


#### Compression socks by a nurse for nurses!! Nurse your feet at work and check out these incredible socks…the best part, they come at an unbeatable price! Check out TX Socks on the Lumify Hub!


#### **Reframe 🍻🧠**


#### Ziyi Gao


#### Using Neuroscience backed research, Reframe helps you reframe your relationship with alcohol and establish happy, healthy habits through community, personalized goals, habit formation, and neuroscience on in app experience! Check out Reframe on the Lumify Hub!


#### **Fifth Window 🏃‍♀️🧘‍♀️**


#### Charlene Platon, MS, RN, FNP-BC


#### Fifth Window revolutionizes nurse well-being. You can access the Fifth Window app community on iOS and Android. Check it out on the Lumify Hub!


#### **Up at Dawn 🌸🌺**


#### Tesla Teitge, BSN, RN


#### Compression socks for nurses by nurses! The best part the designs are influenced by the flora and fauna in beautiful Hawaii. You can shop and use your Lumify points towards a pair of these awesome socks on the Lumify Marketplace!


#### **Wealthcare Pros 💰📊📉**


#### Daniel Tran, BSN, RN


#### Check out Wealthcare Pros for all your financial tips!! Daniel offers insane insight on how to enhance your personal finances. You can access Wealthcare Pros on the Lumify Hub.
