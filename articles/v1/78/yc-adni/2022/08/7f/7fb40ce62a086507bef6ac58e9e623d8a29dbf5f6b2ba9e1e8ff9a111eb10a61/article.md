# Introducing the #LumifyFam Cohort IV


We are so excited to have 23 new healthcare pros and students join our Lumify family. This is our biggest cohort to date!


Cohort IV truly exemplifies Lumify's mission to #UnifyHealthcare! This incredible group of #LumifyFam members includes nursing students, medical students, RNs, LPNs, OTs, travelers, MDs, PTs, RRTs, NPs, and PAs!
