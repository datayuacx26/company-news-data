# LGBTQ+ Healthcare Creators to Follow!


June marks the month of abundant celebrations– Men’s Health Month, Father’s Day, Juneteenth, and (a widely known favorite) Pride Month! Pride is celebrated each year in honor of the 1969 Stonewall riots that occurred in New York City. It is a time to celebrate what it means to be of lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, and queer (LGBTQ) identity. By doing so, we raise awareness to achieve equality, acceptance, and love within the LGBTQ community. So, break out your glitter, rainbow flags, and brightly colored clothing!
