# Book of the Week: Without a Doubt: How to Go from Underrated to Unbeatable


Calling all nurseprenurs: Without a doubt, you need to read this book!


[Surbhi Sarna](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAABoDkoBRPS1pK6xmLs1LzBxnXNTsLiJ8Ms) launched her book this week, Without a Doubt: How to Go from Underrated to Unbeatable. It shares the powerful story of how a teenage cancer scare inspired Surbhi to create a medical device which she grew into a company that sold for $275M.


As a founder, you get told "no" more often than "yes". You get doubted more than validated. I've been told "but you're just a nurse" while pitching my company. Without an MBA or network in Silicon Valley, Surbhi shares how she leveraged the naysayers as fuel to achieve success.


Jennifferre and I are privileged to be advised by Surbhi at YC. She is one of the most inspiring leaders I know and helped to transform our company's direction.


Listening to Surbhi's story from her own voice is a priceless masterclass! One of my favorite quotes: "If you don't try, you can't make anything happen." Big congrats to Surbhi!!
