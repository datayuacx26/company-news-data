# Introducing Lead Health Rewards, Powered by the Lumify Marketplace!


We’re so excited to partner up with our friends at


[Lead Health](https://www.leadhealth.com/) to help Lead travelers access awesome rewards and one unified marketplace filled with products healthcare workers need and love.


As Registered Nurses ourselves, one thing that always frustrated us was how difficult and expensive it is to buy the gear and resources we needed for our jobs. The infinite list of nursing essentials includes scrubs, sneakers, stethoscopes, trauma shears, continuing education, therapy, conferences, certifications, study materials, water bottles, and more. As a traveler, different hospitals have different requirements, adding to the list of essentials needed (we


*know* you own a few different scrub colors for example!).


That’s why we decided to build Lumify, a holistic marketplace that helps healthcare workers find, share, and buy the gear and resources we need to excel. And to help healthcare workers save money, we launched a rewards mechanism allowing employers to purchase marketplace credits (which we call points) for their clinicians to help them offset out-of-pocket expenses. An amazing way to support us, right?!


It was just a matter of time before Lead Health reached out excited to provide this unique perk to its traveler community! We’re so excited to share that all Lead travelers will now receive access to a Lead Health Rewards Portal powered by Lumify to shop for their gear and resources in one easy-to-use marketplace. Lead has generously purchased 300 points for all of its active travelers too!


Something we’re super proud of at Lumify is that over 50% of our brand partners are businesses founded by healthcare workers. This happened naturally, as healthcare workers build the best products for healthcare workers. So know that when you shop on the Lead Rewards Portal, you’ll be supporting your fellow healthcare pros. In our marketplace, you can find everything you need for your life as a traveler, from scrubs to CEs and more.


We can’t wait for you to check out your Lead Rewards Portal! Ready to activate your account to redeem your credits?! Here are some simple instructions:


Video Demo:


[https://youtu.be/lyStsgN1SiA](https://youtu.be/lyStsgN1SiA)


1. Look out for an email from


contact@lumifycare.com to activate your Lead Rewards account and unlock $$$ to shop!


2. Shop by category, by brand, or by browsing all of the products in the marketplace!
3. Click “Use Points” at checkout to save money!
4. Track your orders by clicking “Account” and then “View Order History”


Have a support question? Reach out to our team atcontact@lumifycare.com and we’d be happy to help!
