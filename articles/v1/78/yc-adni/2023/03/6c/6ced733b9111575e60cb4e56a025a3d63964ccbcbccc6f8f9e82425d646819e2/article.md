# Partner of the Day: Happyteeth


Happy National Dentist’s Day! 🦷🪥 Today, we’re spotlighting Happyteeth, a #LumifyPartner founded by a Dental Hygienist! Happyteeth creates purpose-driven bags and accessories for healthcare professionals.


“Dental health directly impacts our overall health! The more people that know how important teeth really are — the better! I’m so passionate about spreading this message and working with other healthcare pros to spread this message so it inspired me to start @givehappyteeth! For every item purchased, we donate a dental health kit to someone in need! 🦷” -Whitney DiFoggio, BS, RDH @teethtalkgirl
