# Airgain Secures Multi‑Year, Multi‑Million Dollar Design Win for Tier‑1 MNO 5G Home Connectivity Platform

# Airgain Secures Multi‑Year, Multi‑Million Dollar Design Win for Tier‑1 MNO 5G Home Connectivity Platform


February 23, 2026 6:20am EST


SAN DIEGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Airgain, Inc. (NASDAQ: AIRG), a leading provider of wireless connectivity solutions, today announced that it has secured a multi‑year, multi‑million‑dollar embedded antenna design win for a next‑generation 5G home connectivity platform being developed by a major North American Mobile Network Operator (MNO).


The program includes Airgain’s antenna systems for both a 5G fixed wireless access (FWA) router and a companion in‑home Wi‑Fi extender, supporting broad deployment across the operator’s next‑generation home broadband offering. Pre‑production units are expected to ship this quarter, with mass production anticipated later this year.


This announcement builds on Airgain’s previously disclosed Wi‑Fi 7 design win and further expands the company’s incumbent position within Tier‑1 North American MNOs. Together, these wins reinforce Airgain’s momentum in high‑volume Consumer broadband platforms and highlight the continued strength of its embedded antenna business.


“As mobile network operators accelerate deployments of 5G and hybrid access solutions, antenna performance becomes a critical differentiator for both outdoor and in‑home connectivity,” said Brian Makarowski, Airgain VP of Product and Marketing. “This new design win demonstrates our ability to deliver advanced multi‑band, multi‑technology antenna systems that meet the evolving performance demands of next‑generation home broadband.”


Airgain brings decades of experience delivering antenna systems for high‑performance customer‑premises equipment (CPE). In addition to its RF technology leadership, Airgain partners with global OEMs and ODMs through a comprehensive suite of engineering and professional services — including concept design, industrial design support, RF simulation, system‑level coexistence analysis, mechanical integration, verification and validation testing, and post‑launch performance benchmarking.


These expanded service offerings are increasingly critical as next‑generation platforms integrate complex 5G and Wi‑Fi 7 radio architectures with numerous antennas operating across multiple bands. Airgain’s ability to provide both the hardware and the engineering services required to optimize these systems positions the company as a strategic partner throughout the entire development lifecycle.


“Our embedded antenna business continues to serve as a stable and strategic foundation for Airgain,” said Jacob Suen, President and CEO of Airgain. “Wins like this enable us to fund and accelerate the growth of our next‑generation platforms, including AirgainConnect® Fleet™ and Lighthouse™, where we are expanding into higher‑value mobility, public‑safety, and enterprise connectivity markets. These investments are strengthening Airgain’s position as a diversified connectivity solutions provider and building the engine for our long‑term growth.”
