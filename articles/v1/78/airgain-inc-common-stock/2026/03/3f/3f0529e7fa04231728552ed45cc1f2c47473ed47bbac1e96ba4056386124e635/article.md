# Airgain Secures Multi‑Million‑Dollar Purchase Order from Leading IoT Solutions Provider


March 04, 2026 9:00am EST


SAN DIEGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- *Airgain, Inc. (NASDAQ: AIRG)* , a leading provider of wireless connectivity solutions, today announced that it has received a $4 million purchase order from one of the industry’s leading IoT solution providers, with shipments expected to be completed within the next 12 months.


The order represents a continuation of a multi‑year relationship and reflects renewed deployment activity from a long‑standing customer relying on Airgain’s IoT solutions for large‑scale field installations. Airgain’s technology supports deployments across connected commerce, payments, smart retail, digital signage, industrial automation, and other distributed applications that require reliable, high‑performance, multi‑carrier connectivity. This follow‑on order underscores the ongoing engineering collaboration between the companies and highlights Airgain’s ability to meet the customer’s requirements for compact design, platform consistency, and dependable operation.


“This order reflects the ongoing trust our customers place in Airgain’s IoT solutions,” said Brian Makarowski, VP of Product Management and Marketing at Airgain. “We continue to see strong demand across a wide range of connected applications, and we are proud to support this customer as they ramp deployments and expand into new markets.”


Airgain’s IoT portfolio remains a core contributor to the company’s business model, benefiting from long product lifecycles, diversified deployments, and a stable demand environment. As the company advances its broader strategy, IoT continues to provide a dependable foundation while Airgain expands into new growth platforms such as AC‑Fleet and Lighthouse. This latest commitment demonstrates continued momentum and supports improving demand trends within the IoT segment entering 2026.
