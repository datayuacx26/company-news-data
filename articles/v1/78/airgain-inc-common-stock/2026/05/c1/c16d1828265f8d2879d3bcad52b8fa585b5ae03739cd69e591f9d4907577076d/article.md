# Airgain Adds HPUE Technology to Support Stronger Connectivity in Hard-to-Reach Places

Airgain has expanded its AirgainConnect connectivity portfolio with the acquisition of[the High Power User Equipment](https://airgain.com/solutions/hpue-high-power-user-equipment/) (HPUE)[MegaFi® 2](https://airgain.com/products/megafi-2-hpue/) and[MegaGo® 2](https://airgain.com/products/megago-2-hpue/) product family from Nextivity. These HPUE solutions are designed to solve a fundamental limitation in cellular networks: uplink performance at the edge of coverage.


This adds another important option for organizations that need reliable wireless connectivity in places where standard cellular solutions can struggle, including remote areas, rural routes, dense urban environments, and other signal-challenged locations.

## What is HPUE (High Power User Equipment)?


HPUE (High Power User Equipment) is a class of cellular device that improves uplink performance by transmitting at higher power levels than standard devices, enabling more reliable communication in weak or hard-to-reach coverage areas.


Cellular connectivity depends on both downlink (receiving data) and uplink (sending data). In many edge-of-coverage situations, a device can still download messages or data, but it does not have enough transmit power to respond, upload, or maintain stable two-way communication. This downlink-uplink imbalance is one of the main reasons connectivity can break down at the edge of coverage.


The new HPUE solutions operate as 3GPP Power Class 1 devices, allowing up to +31 dBm (1.25 watts) of output power, compared with +23 dBm (200 mW) for standard Power Class 3 devices. That added uplink power helps extend connectivity in harder-to-reach areas.


## Why it Matters


HPUE solutions can deliver up to 6x the uplink power of standard cellular equipment. In practical terms, that can help organizations maintain more reliable voice and data connectivity in places where conventional devices often fall short.


Where HPUE improves connectivity:


- **

- **

- **

- **

These are exactly the kinds of environments where dropped or inconsistent connectivity can create operational headaches and, in some cases, real safety risks.

## Who Should Consider HPUE?


HPUE supports enterprise critical communications across mobile, fixed, and portable deployments. Common use cases include remote monitoring, data transmission, and employee communication and safety.


That makes the technology relevant for a broad set of organizations, including:


### Public Safety and First Responders


HPUE was originally developed for mission-critical communications use cases. For first responders, stronger uplink performance can support communications, data sharing, and connectivity in rural areas or disrupted environments.


### Utilities and Energy Providers


Field crews, remote equipment, and distributed infrastructure often operate across large geographic footprints where connectivity can be inconsistent. HPUE can help support more reliable field communications and data transmission across these environments.


### Transportation, Logistics, and Field Services


Organizations that rely on connected fleets, mobile workers, and field-based operations need wireless systems that perform outside ideal coverage zones. HPUE helps support stronger connectivity for these distributed operations.


### Enterprise, Institutional, and Industrial Environments


Large venues, campuses, medical facilities, industrial sites, and other complex environments can all present connectivity challenges related to building penetration, layout, or distance. HPUE provides another option for maintaining reliable voice and data connections in these settings.
