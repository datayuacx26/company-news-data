**About Photronics**


[Photronics](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=4fJKxktowPrHQdys_bjnjU-99BfUAwEErvtkXvFuEdisvPFFOpGbYmg391cMmcmJXPONKMnALrFqicVujAZLyvqaQ5gqJV0UxwzpU3-f2Dc=) is a leading worldwide manufacturer of integrated circuit (IC) and flat panel display (FPD)[photomasks](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=qt2qVnX79XMF-oEt5fH9ISdPek1d6bt_O2v5YZ47roxxGnfAvnqId2xwRdyk2feNQgXxi_9jL72THZi0QMmVUOOZ6Toz0kV1dLtBf1I6mVzzQASxlajQQXznWMvjiQdZ7cYXPFRE2To3FQRa3-wdgM30zXSnT94HUJanstQrwQo=) .
