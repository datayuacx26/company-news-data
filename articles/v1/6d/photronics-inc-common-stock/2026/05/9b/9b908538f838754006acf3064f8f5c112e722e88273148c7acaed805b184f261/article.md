# Photronics to Report Fiscal Second Quarter 2026 Results on May 28, 2026

## Photronics to Report Fiscal Second Quarter 2026 Results on May 28, 2026


May 13, 2026 at 8:00 AM EDT


[PDF Version](https://photronicsinc.gcs-web.com/node/19596/pdf)


BROOKFIELD, Conn., May 13, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Photronics, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=Cnd6oZKIr61aIG_hVEVb3uJEBXua2mrkiLAtrvo_aIdIiVdZNI-8CDE1--9fFkXJxXPWtYqvJ1CqpdP_-2X2yKrlJqgEI2VCrPaxgpdI5WJoImoQJSt7qWnj8gswJk_3) (Nasdaq: PLAB), a worldwide leader in photomask technologies and solutions, is scheduled to announce financial results for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 on Thursday, May 28, 2026, before the market opens.


Photronics will host a public conference call the same day at 8:30 a.m. Eastern time. During the call, company management will respond to questions concerning, but not limited to, the company’s financial performance, business conditions, and industry outlook. Some responses may contain information not previously disclosed. The call will be broadcast live and on-demand on the[Events and Presentations](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=P3tjxiCRjwZYeeu2bX58blp22V9qGVqY_yOM3qdCmIOKHuafm5hAra-ay9TZHVuIY-JWbJrLD3omdqOnuX6PE5r6IljD3h8-eOSahtjWahMw7Mkx-tLBNSJLsPhw7gZrPOqrewqhagXXbcnyppHOKA==) link on the Photronics website. Analysts and investors who wish to participate in the Q&A portion of the call should click here:[Conference Registration](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=xNCdE8zPUPQuQ4CLfirRl4avcmIoLCX7BWFr-xEygfEUWGy-3rOs_W2-VZNynT0ta68SiVv7kADuqLvdo2vS23BF6sZx-OHA1VOtCdikC6qgU-sTdh3FhA4q-ly0Ncsw) . It is suggested that participants register fifteen minutes prior to the call’s scheduled start time.
