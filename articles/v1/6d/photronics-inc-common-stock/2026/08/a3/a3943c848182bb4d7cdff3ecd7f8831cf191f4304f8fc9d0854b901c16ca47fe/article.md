# Photronics to Report Fiscal Third Quarter 2026 Results on August 26, 2026

## Photronics to Report Fiscal Third Quarter 2026 Results on August 26, 2026


August 12, 2026 at 4:00 PM EDT


[PDF Version](https://photronicsinc.gcs-web.com/node/19661/pdf)


BROOKFIELD, Conn., Aug. 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Photronics, Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=KHhAexsfAkf6fL_o3Zpzs9QPfImPC7NJv8H3f15A-cymXA449wcFF6pCNOkmp8o-fJY3uhwNEBZIy56om85lXfb9HfHzxIZ9AGbBb7c3EcGBv0cKWeJItlB0fRn2NaxP) (Nasdaq: PLAB), a worldwide leader in photomask technologies and solutions, is scheduled to announce financial results for the third quarter of fiscal 2026 on Wednesday, August 26, 2026, before the market opens.


Photronics will host a public conference call the same day at 8:30 a.m. Eastern time. During the call, company management will respond to questions concerning, but not limited to, the company’s financial performance, business conditions, and industry outlook. Some responses may contain information not previously disclosed. The call will be broadcast live and on-demand on the[Events and Presentations](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=KMq2a3GVXxNYT_kwyit72JcoZjTtWQyiIqcID3yy68QiBrqHBPxp2bvQE64kpV8vAUmrQqIRqIrY29FQ8ibjwM96t7McevnP4FPX2XYtjyUYMoByS_K4t5mRnXViZ0wvX-8GQuhC8xfi_SRnHABJDw==) link on the Photronics website. Analysts who wish to participate in the Q&A portion of the call should click here:[Conference Registration](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=hPCTdaBkc79lbREjROtOO-iJ4whp8qyf6u9uZAmmrRmzTl9AJfj433T1JL0aay7Im0ktjaL_QubCVDAJIc11jPxnkPLV8A-yPDCnD9eZbVtsE8btrLM5N5bajLrnZwnOtArNoamGj0iw6ccatCcc8-l0SKbxm8S_nDWy2igL_9B1Yle1IgsfQVAZ0WPPulQH) . It is suggested that participants register fifteen minutes prior to the call’s scheduled start time.
