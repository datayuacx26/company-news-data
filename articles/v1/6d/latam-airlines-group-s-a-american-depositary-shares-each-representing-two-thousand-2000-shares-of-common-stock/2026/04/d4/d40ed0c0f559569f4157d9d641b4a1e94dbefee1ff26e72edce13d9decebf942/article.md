# LATAM Airlines Group Announces the Publication of its 2025 Annual Integrated Report

### LATAM Airlines Group Announces the Publication of its 2025 Annual Integrated Report


04/07/2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/986550758/files/doc_news/2026/Apr/07/Publication-Annual-Integrated-Report-2025.pdf)


- LATAM Airlines Group S.A. (“LATAM Airlines Group” or the "Company”) (NYSE: LTM / SSE: LTM) and its affiliates, the leading airline group in South America, announces the publication of its 2025 Annual Integrated Report in English and Spanish.


The 2025 Annual Integrated Report highlights LATAM group’s key milestones throughout the year, including solid financial performance, the continued execution of its profitable growth strategy and initiatives aimed at enhancing both the customer and employee experience, alongside progress in environmental, social, and governance (ESG) dimensions. It captures how the group is advancing with purpose, creating opportunities and value for its customers, employees, and the environment.


The document in Spanish is available on the website of the Financial Market Commission at www.cmfchile.cl or on the Company’s Investor Relations website at[ir.latam.com](https://ir.latam.com/English/overview/default.aspx) in both languages.


Additionally, the Portuguese version of its Annual Integrated Report will soon be available on the Investor Relations website.


The publication of the Annual Integrated Report comes after the recent release of the Form 20-F, filed with the SEC, and together they provide a comprehensive view of LATAM’s annual performance.


In addition, shareholders may receive a hard copy of the Company's complete audited financial statements free of charge by requesting a copy from LATAM Airlines Group Investor Relations Office.
