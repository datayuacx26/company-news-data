# CBIZ Named Top 10 VAR Provider by Accounting Today for Second Consecutive Year

August 2026 – *Accounting Today* has published its latest Value-Added Reseller (VAR) 100 list ranking CBIZ as the number 8 top technology practice in the US for the second year in a row.


In 2025, CBIZ advanced a staggering 48 places from its 2024 position on the VAR list to earn its position among the top 10 providers. This year, CBIZ cemented its top 10 ranking with a high performing practice supporting more than 100 partnerships, including an expanded[alliance with Microsoft to further enhance AI-driven solutions](https://www.cbiz.com/insights/press-release/cbiz-expands-ai-partnership-with-microsoft-to-unlock-new-levels-of-productivity-insight-and-talent-attraction-retention-and-development) for clients, announced in April.


CBIZ’s Senior Vice President, Chief Information & Technology Officer and President of CBIZ Technology, Peter Scavuzzo celebrated the achievement, saying: *“We’re proud to rank in the top 10 again this year, but we’re even more pleased with what that means for our clients. With a deep bench of technology professionals, extensive cutting-edge capabilities, and expanded relationships, CBIZ is uniquely equipped to bring innovative solutions to market faster, helping clients create lasting business value.”*


CBIZ provides VAR solutions that serve clients across a wide variety of industries and sectors with *Accounting Today* specifically highlighting the firm’s Sage, Sage Intacct, NetSuite, Acumatica, Campfire, and Rillet offerings.


[See the complete list of firms recognized by Accounting Today here.](https://www.accountingtoday.com/data/the-2026-var-100)
