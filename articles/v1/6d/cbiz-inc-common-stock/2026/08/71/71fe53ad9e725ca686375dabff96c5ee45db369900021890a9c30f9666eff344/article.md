# Why Organizations Are Choosing Martus for Budgeting, Forecasting, and Financial Planning

Today, effective[budgeting and forecasting](https://www.cbiz.com/services/advisory/private-equity-advisory/performance-enhancement/financial-operational-cash-forecasting) are no longer limited to an annual planning cycle. Instead, they are critical components of modern financial management and strategic decision-making practices that enable organizations to translate financial visibility into the agility necessary to keep pace with the markets, clients, and competition.


Finance teams face challenges like disconnected planning processes, limited collaboration, inconsistent reporting, and forecasting limitations that can hinder their ability to adapt as circumstances evolve. To overcome these challenges, organizations need budgeting and planning solutions that provide real-time insight into performance, help improve the efficiency of their teams, and provide leaders with insights that can enhance decision-making.


Martus, a cloud-based budgeting, forecasting, and reporting platform, is designed to help organizations modernize their financial planning functions while improving collaboration, accountability, and visibility.


## Why Organizations Are Choosing Martus


Martus helps organizations move away from disconnected planning methods by creating a more centralized, collaborative platform for budgeting, forecasting, and financial reporting. To avoid relying on static annual budgets, manual reporting processes, or fragmented planning tools, organizations use Martus to create a strategic and engaging planning environment.


### Collaborative Budget Development


Martus enables department leaders, managers, and stakeholders to contribute directly to the budgeting process while finance maintains appropriate oversight and control. By baking collaboration into the budgeting process, departments are aligned, knowledge and strategies are shared, and everyone across the organization is more engaged in the budgeting process from start to finish.


When implemented correctly, that results in shared accountability as well as budgets that better reflect operational needs and strategic priorities.


### Forecasting and Scenario Planning


Because financial and economic conditions can change quickly, organizations need the ability to model various outcomes, assess associated risks, and adjust their plans as necessary.


Martus supports forecasting and “what-if” scenario planning to allow organizations to evaluate alternative approaches. As a result, organizations can continuously assess performance and update their budgeting plans throughout the year.


### Budget vs. Actual Analysis


Budgeting is most valuable when organizations can measure tangible results against the initial plan. Martus provides a centralized environment in which it’s easy to compare budgets, forecasts, and actual financial performance.


With increased visibility, leadership can better understand performance trends, identify variances, and enact changes before small issues become larger challenges.


### Improved Visibility and Reporting


To help finance teams provide timely insights based on large amounts of financial data, Martus delivers reporting tools that go beyond compliance and historical data. Visual dashboards and charts populated by up-to-date data can help any audience better appreciate trends and identify concerns.


Ultimately, the ability to quickly reference benchmarks and generate reports allows finance teams to spend less time organizing data manually and more time analyzing results, guiding conversations, and supporting the organization’s strategic goals.


### Integration with Existing Financial Systems


Accounting and ERP systems are significant investments for any organization. Martus complements those technology investments by pulling the financial data they house directly into the planning and forecasting process.


With support for seamless integration with more than 20 accounting and ERP systems, Martus enables organizations to leverage existing financial information while creating a more robust budgeting, forecasting, and reporting environment.


Martus’ broad compatibility avoids disruption by allowing organizations to modernize their financial planning capabilities without replacing the systems they already rely on to manage financial operations.
