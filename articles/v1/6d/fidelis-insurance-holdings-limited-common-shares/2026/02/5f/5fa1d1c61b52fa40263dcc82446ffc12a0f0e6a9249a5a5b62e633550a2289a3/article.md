# Aoife Woulfe Wins Senior Leadership Award At The Women In Finance Awards

Aoife Woulfe, Ireland Chief Underwriting Officer at Fidelis Insurance Group, now Pelagos Insurance Capital, has been presented with a Senior Leadership Award at the prestigious Women in Finance Awards. Held at the Crowne Plaza Santry, the ceremony celebrated visionary leaders and change-makers redefining the financial landscape across banking, insurance, and fintech.


The event, which attracted entries from across the Irish financial sector, recognized Aoife for her excellence in strategy, innovation, and leadership. As Chief Underwriting Officer for the group’s Irish operations, Aoife is noted for inspiring teams and actively promoting diversity within the industry.


This recognition highlights the high caliber of leadership within Fidelis Insurance Group, now Pelagos Insurance Capital, as it continues to expand its presence in the Dublin market.
