# Fidelis appoints Shane O’Donovan Group Chief Actuary

Shane O’Donovan has been promoted to Group Chief Actuary at Fidelis Insurance Group, now Pelagos Insurance Capital, marking a key internal leadership appointment for the business.


The appointment recognizes Shane’s contributions over the past five years, during which, he has played an important role across both the actuarial function and wider business initiatives.


Jonny Strickle, Group Managing Director, said: “I am delighted to announce Shane’s well-deserved promotion to Group Chief Actuary, Fidelis Insurance Group. This promotion recognizes Shane’s significant contributions over the past five years, not only within the actuarial function, but also across the broader business. He has played a key role in advancing our strategic objectives and has been instrumental in building out our actuarial capabilities.”


Shane has been central to strengthening the group’s actuarial capabilities and supporting the delivery of its strategic objectives as the business continues to grow.
