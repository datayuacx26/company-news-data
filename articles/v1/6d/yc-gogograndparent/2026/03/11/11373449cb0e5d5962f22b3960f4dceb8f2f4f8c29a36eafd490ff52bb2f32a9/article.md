# How GoGoGrandparent Works (Simple Guide for Seniors Without Smartphones)

Ordering a ride or getting groceries delivered shouldn't require a computer science degree. Yet for many older adults and people with disabilities, navigating app-based services can feel exactly that complicated. Tiny buttons, confusing menus, and constantly changing apps can turn a simple task into a frustrating experience.


Many people searching for rides for seniors without a smartphone run into this exact problem.


GoGoGrandparent was created to remove those digital barriers. Instead of needing to download apps or manage accounts on a smartphone, customers can access rides, groceries, meals, medication delivery, and everyday services using a simple phone call.


The goal is straightforward: make modern services accessible to people who prefer not to use technology.


## **What Is GoGoGrandparent?**


GoGoGrandparent is a service designed for older adults and people with disabilities who want independence without the stress of navigating smartphone apps.


Rather than relying on apps, customers can call a live team that **helps coordinate services like transportation, grocery delivery, restaurant meals, medication pickup, and help around the home.**


Think of GoGoGrandparent as a concierge layer that connects customers to services already operating in their community — while adding human support and oversight along the way.


For example, someone might call in the morning to arrange a ride to a doctor’s appointment and schedule groceries to be delivered later that afternoon — **all without needing a smartphone.**


## **How GoGoGrandparent Works: Step-by-Step**


### **Step 1: Contact the Service**


Customers simply **call GoGoGrandparent at 855-464-6872** when they need help with a ride, delivery, or other service.


There’s no app to download and no complicated online setup required.


### **Step 2: Service Coordination**


Once a request is made, the GoGoGrandparent team helps coordinate the service.


This might include arranging:


- a ride to a medical appointment
- grocery delivery
- restaurant meal delivery
- medication pickup
- help around the house


The team handles the technology behind the scenes so customers don’t have to manage multiple apps or platforms.


### **Step 3: Service Support and Oversight**


GoGoGrandparent connects customers with service providers operating in their area and helps monitor the experience along the way.


For transportation, the system prioritizes drivers with strong customer ratings on the platforms they operate on.


If something unusual happens — such as delays or confusion about a pickup location — the team can step in and help resolve the situation.


### **Step 4: Completion and Billing**


Before placing a request, customers receive an estimated cost so they know what to expect.


After the service is completed, the charge is applied to the payment method linked to the customer’s account, such as a credit card, debit card, or account credits.


Customers also receive a receipt that shows the total cost and any applicable service charges.


## **How GoGoGrandparent Connects to Transportation and Delivery Services**


GoGoGrandparent helps customers access existing transportation and delivery networks without needing to use smartphone apps.


When someone requests a ride or delivery, the system places a request through partner service providers operating in that city. GoGoGrandparent then helps coordinate communication, monitor the service, and assist if any issues arise.


This approach allows customers to access services available in their community while avoiding the complexity of managing multiple apps.


The focus is not just placing requests — it’s helping ensure the experience is understandable and manageable for the person using the service.

## **Accessibility and Service Availability**


GoGoGrandparent works with transportation networks operating in each city.


In many areas this includes standard vehicles and, increasingly, wheelchair-accessible vehicles through specialized partners.


Availability of accessible vehicles may vary depending on the transportation fleets operating locally. As GoGoGrandparent continues expanding partnerships, more accessible transportation options are becoming available in additional communities.


Customers who require specific accommodations are encouraged to speak with a GoGoGrandparent representative so the team can help determine the best options available in their area.


## **Scheduling Rides in Advance**


Customers can schedule rides in advance — sometimes months ahead of time — or request rides the same day.


Many customers choose to call when they are ready to leave so a ride can be requested immediately.


Scheduling options can vary depending on service availability and the timing of the request, but advance scheduling is available for many trips.


## **What Happens if a Driver Cancels or a Fee Appears?**


Transportation services rely on independent drivers, so cancellations or delays can occasionally happen across the entire rideshare industry.


If a customer experiences a problem — such as a driver cancellation or an unexpected no-show fee — GoGoGrandparent’s support team can help investigate the situation.


Customers can contact the **24/7 GoGoGrandparent hotline** to request assistance, and the team will work to resolve the issue whenever possible.


For many customers, having someone available to help sort out these situations is one of the most valuable parts of the service.


## **How GoGoGrandparent Pricing Works**


GoGoGrandparent uses a membership model combined with pay-as-you-go services.


Membership provides access to the GoGoGrandparent support system, which includes:


- 24/7 phone assistance
- service coordination
- trip monitoring and support
- help resolving issues like driver cancellations or no-show fees
- communication updates and notifications


Once a customer is a member, rides and deliveries are charged based on the price set by the underlying service provider, plus a small usage fee.


Membership options may vary depending on location, partnerships with local governments, or programs that subsidize services for seniors.


Customers can cancel their membership at any time by contacting GoGoGrandparent.


The service also sends phone or email reminders before membership renewals so customers know when billing will occur.

## **Why GoGoGrandparent Was Created**


GoGoGrandparent began in 2016 after a simple request from one grandmother.


She wanted to use modern services like rides and deliveries, but she did not want to learn how to use smartphone apps.


Her request revealed a much larger problem: millions of older adults are effectively locked out of the “app economy” because they prefer not to use smartphones or struggle with the technology.


GoGoGrandparent was built to bridge that gap — helping people access modern services using the technology they are already comfortable with: the telephone.


Today the service supports hundreds of thousands of customers across the United States and continues working to expand access to transportation, deliveries, and everyday support.
