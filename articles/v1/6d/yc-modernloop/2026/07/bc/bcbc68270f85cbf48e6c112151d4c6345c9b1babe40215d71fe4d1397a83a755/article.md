# How Carta optimizes hiring for complex roles by leveraging automation tools

Hiring for complex roles, especially at the leadership level, requires precision, speed, and a flawless candidate experience. In this webinar, Joey Quaranti, Carta’s Manager of Talent Strategy and Operations, talked about how Carta has streamlined its most complex and high-stakes hiring processes by leveraging automation tools. Learn how Carta’s recruiting team is saving time, improving coordination, and delivering exceptional experiences at scale. Listen to the replay to hear how:


- How Carta accelerates executive and technical hiring by automating the most time-consuming parts of the interview scheduling process.
- The impact of automation, including ModernLoop’s Zero-Click Scheduling and self-scheduling features, on recruiter productivity and candidate experience.
- Best practices from Carta’s lean recruiting team for using tools to reduce manual work while keeping the human touch where it matters most.
