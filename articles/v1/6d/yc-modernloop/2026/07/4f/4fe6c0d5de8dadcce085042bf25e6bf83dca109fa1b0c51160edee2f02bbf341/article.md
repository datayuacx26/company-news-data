## **Calendly – Best for Simple, User-Friendly Scheduling**


**Overview:** **** Calendly is known for its simplicity and ease of use. It provides a clean, intuitive interface that integrates with major calendar systems to facilitate basic scheduling without the hassle. While it’s not designed exclusively for recruiting, its straightforward approach makes it a popular choice for small (under 20) teams looking for basic meeting scheduling functionality rather than a proper recruiting automation tool.


**Strengths:**


- **User-Friendly Interface:** Calendly’s simplicity ensures quick setup and minimal training.


- **Seamless Calendar Integration:** Works well with Google Calendar, Outlook, and others, making scheduling smooth.


- **Affordable for Small Teams:** Ideal for companies that need a way to book meetings that don’t have the infrastructure built out for recruiting automation.
