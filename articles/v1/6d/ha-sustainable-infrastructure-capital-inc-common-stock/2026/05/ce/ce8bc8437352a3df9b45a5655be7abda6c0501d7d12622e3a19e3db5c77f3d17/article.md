# Ørsted and HASI Close Land Finance Deal Supporting ~1 GW of Solar and Storage Projects Across Three States

# Ørsted and HASI Close Land Finance Deal Supporting ~1 GW of Solar and Storage Projects Across Three States


May 27, 2026 8:30am EDT


**ANNAPOLIS, Md.--** HA Sustainable Infrastructure Capital, Inc. (“HASI”) (NYSE: HASI), a leading investor in sustainable infrastructure assets, and Ørsted, a leading global clean energy company, today announced the closing of a series of land transactions with HASI, encompassing approximately 6,600 acres across Ørsted’s Eleven Mile Solar Center in Pinal County, Arizona; Sparta Solar project in Bee County, Texas; and Muscle Shoals Solar project in Colbert County, Alabama.


Ørsted and its partners will continue to own and operate the projects, which are operational and hold long-term, investment-grade power purchase agreements (PPAs). Collectively, the three solar and battery projects represent approximately 1 GW of operational renewable capacity.


HASI has provided land financings across 6 GW of solar and storage projects in the past year, bringing its total land investment to more than 24 GW.


"By partnering with HASI, a leading sustainable infrastructure investor, we're able to optimize our capital position for future investment, while continuing to deliver energy to our customers across Arizona, Texas, and Alabama," said James Giamarino, Chief Commercial Officer at Ørsted Americas.


“We are pleased to partner with Ørsted by providing long-term land financing under several of their operational solar projects,” said HASI Co-Chief Investment Officer Manny Haile-Mariam. “Our expertise in energy markets and financial structuring allows us to offer competitive land financing products that deliver optimal outcomes for our clients and their projects.”


For more information about HASI’s land financing solutions, please visit[www.hasi.com/land-financing](http://www.hasi.com/land-financing) .


**About HASI**


HASI is an investor in sustainable infrastructure assets advancing the energy transition. With more than $16 billion in managed assets, our investments are diversified across multiple asset classes, including utility-scale solar, storage, and onshore wind; distributed solar and storage; RNG; and energy efficiency. We combine deep expertise in energy markets and financial structuring with long-standing programmatic client partnerships to deliver superior risk-adjusted returns and measurable environmental benefits. HA Sustainable Infrastructure Capital, Inc. is listed on the New York Stock Exchange (Ticker: HASI). For more information, please visit[hasi.com](http://www.hasi.com/) .


**About Ørsted**


Ørsted's Americas Onshore business encompasses over 6 gigawatts of wind, solar, and battery storage capacity in operation across eight states. As one of the largest multi-technology independent power producers in the U.S., we partner with landowners and local communities to deliver affordable, reliable energy. Supported by a team of approximately 250 employees, the business develops, constructs, and operates projects serving utilities and corporate offtakers with long-term power agreements.
