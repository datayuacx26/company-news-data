## **How to Hire International Full time Employees**


Hiring an employee from another country can be a great way to diversify your workforce and tap into new talent pools.


### However, there are a **3 things you must consider** when hiring international full time employees:


• First, you'll need to make sure you follow the proper protocols for their countries and remember that **every country has different laws and regulations** that you need to follow in order to make a full time contract with a candidate


• Second, you need to open a **legal entity** if you want to hire someone as a full time employee. In Latin America it takes between **10 and 17 business days,** more or less an entire month and **it costs around 200 to 400 USD on average.**


• Finally, you'll need to be aware of local minimum wages, if you're hiring remote employees, you'll need to think about compensating them fairly. Also you need to know **your employees currencies and their preferred payment methods** , so you'll probably have to adjust your payrolls to make that possible.
