## How do you avoid losing money to fees and currency conversion?


Receive in USD and convert only once, when the rate suits you. The biggest hidden cost of getting paid from abroad is the FX spread: cross-border payments lose 3 to 7 percent on average to fees and conversion (McKinsey Global Payments Report, 2026). Compare what actually lands in your account, not the advertised fee, and check the exchange rate against the mid-market rate before you convert.


## What does a US client need to pay a foreign freelancer?


Usually three things: a signed contractor agreement, a clear invoice, and a W-8BEN form on file. The W-8BEN certifies that you are a non-US person, which generally means your foreign-earned service income is not subject to US withholding. You handle your own taxes in your country, and the client keeps the W-8BEN as their record.
