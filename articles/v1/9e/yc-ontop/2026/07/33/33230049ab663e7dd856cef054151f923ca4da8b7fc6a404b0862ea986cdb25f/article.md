### What hidden costs reduce worker payments?


The contract states one number. The bank account shows another, smaller one.


‍

Some payment providers hide costs in exchange rates instead of showing explicit fees. ### Clarify Payment Terms Upfront


Most payroll problems stem from assumptions rather than clear agreements. Before starting work, ask when payments will be processed, which currency you'll be paid in, who pays for transfer fees, and how long international transfers take. If payment terms aren't written down, request clarification before signing anything. ### Understand Your Contract


Contracts specify what work must be done, when payments are made, whether someone is an employee or a contractor, and how the contract ends. Classification affects taxes and legal protections. Review notice requirements and final payment procedures carefully to avoid disagreements if circumstances change.


### Track Your Actual Earnings


Many workers focus on the pay they agreed to but overlook what actually arrives in their accounts. Currency conversion rates, transfer fees, intermediary charges, and deductions can differ significantly from the stated amount. Two workers earning the same amount on paper may receive different amounts after conversion spreads and transaction fees are applied. Tracking these differences reveals the true value of your international compensation and helps you identify patterns that erode your earnings over time.

Pay shouldn't be the only consideration when evaluating jobs in other countries. Check whether payments are processed on time, track your payment status, and ensure the platform is easy to use.
