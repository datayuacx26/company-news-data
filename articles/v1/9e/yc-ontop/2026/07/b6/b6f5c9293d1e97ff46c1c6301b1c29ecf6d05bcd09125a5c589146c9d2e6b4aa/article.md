## What Are Deel and Oyster?


**Deel** and **Oyster** are **global employment platforms** that solve an *important* problem: hiring talent in countries where you have *no legal presence* . Both act as the **legal employer on your behalf** , handling[payroll, taxes, benefits, and compliance](https://www.getontop.com/blog-category/global-compliance) so you can **bring on workers in new markets** without setting up a **local business entity** .


"Global employment platforms like **Deel** and **Oyster** exist to remove the single biggest barrier to international hiring — the *legal requirement* to establish a local entity before you can pay anyone." — Core EOR Value Proposition


🎯 **Key Point:** Both **Deel** and **Oyster** operate as **Employer of Record (EOR)** platforms — meaning they become the *official* legal employer so your company doesn't have to.


💡 **What This Means in Practice:** Instead of spending **months** and **thousands of dollars** incorporating a foreign entity, you can hire a worker in a new country in a matter of **days** .


- **Legal Employer Status:** The provider acts as the Employer of Record (EOR), **absorbing all local employment liability** and protecting your company from jurisdictional risks.
- **Payroll Processing:** Ensures workers are paid in full compliance with local regulations and in the **correct local currency** , eliminating manual conversion errors.
- **Tax Compliance:** Automates the complex process of calculating, withholding, and remitting local taxes, **handling all obligations automatically** .
- **Benefits Administration:** Manages the entire lifecycle of both **statutory and supplemental benefits** , ensuring your talent remains compliant and competitive in the local market.
- **No Local Entity Needed:** Allows your business to **enter new international markets rapidly** without the high cost and time investment of establishing a foreign legal entity.
