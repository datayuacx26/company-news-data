## **What is an Employer Of Record (EOR)?**


‍


Hiring an international employee can be difficult and time-consuming. You'll need to set up contracts, deal with different legal systems, and provide benefits like health care or vacation days—the list goes on!


‍


But all this hassle is a thing of the past if you use EOR services, which enable companies in one country to hire someone else from another nation without having them become independent contractors by themselves first; it's easy as pie for your company because everything happens online.


‍


An Employer of Record (EOR) is a third-party organization that hires and pays an employee on behalf of another company and takes responsibility for all formal employment tasks, allowing you to have a global team in countries where you don't have a legal entity.


‍


## **What does an EOR do?**


‍


With an EOR (Employer Of Record), the client company hands over employment responsibilities to a third party that handles[payroll solution](https://www.getontop.com/payroll) and administrative tasks. An EOR also ensure compliance with local laws and may conduct talent searches or vet candidates on behalf of their clients.


‍


Ontop (Employer Of Record), agrees with the Client to employ a worker and act as its employer. Nevertheless, the work performed by the employee is done so in favor of the Client. The client is who benefits from the employee's work, but the EOR is legally the party responsible for complying with the obligations of an employer. In this model, Ontop acts as an EOR between the Company and the employee.
