# Top global hiring platforms in 2026: how to choose

If you are hiring across borders in 2026, the platform you choose decides how fast you onboard, what you spend, and whether your people actually get paid on time. The market is crowded, so the real question is not which tool has the most features, but which one fits how and where you hire.


Ontop is a global payroll and payments platform that lets companies hire and pay teams in 150+ countries.[See how Ontop hires and pays global teams](https://www.getontop.com/) .


## Key takeaways


- Global hiring platforms fall into three types: employer of record, contractor management, and full payroll and payments engines.
- The best fit depends on whether you hire employees, contractors, or both, and in which countries.
- Total cost includes the FX spread on payouts, not just the per-seat fee.
- How the platform treats the worker (payout speed, currency control) is a real differentiator, not a footnote.
- Ontop covers hiring, payroll, and payments, and pays workers into a Global Account they control.


## What makes a good global hiring platform?


A good platform covers the countries where your people actually are, prices transparently, keeps you compliant, and pays workers quickly in a currency they control. Coverage and payout quality matter more than the length of the feature list.

## Frequently asked questions


### What is the best platform to hire globally in 2026?


The best platform is the one that covers your countries, matches your mix of employees and contractors, and pays people on time in a currency they control.


### Do I need an employer of record or contractor management?


Use an employer of record for employees where you have no entity, and contractor management for freelancers. Many teams need both.


### What hidden costs should I watch for?


The FX spread on cross-border payouts, setup or deposit fees, and off-boarding charges.
