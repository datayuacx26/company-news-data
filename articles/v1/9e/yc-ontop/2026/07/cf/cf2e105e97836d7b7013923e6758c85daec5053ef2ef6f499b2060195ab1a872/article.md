## Why Colombia? Key benefits


### Skilled and diverse workforce


Two decades of investment in education and technology have built a strong talent pool in IT and software development, customer service (with solid English proficiency), and digital marketing and design.


### Cost-effectiveness


Labor costs are substantially lower than in the US or Europe, and hiring in Colombia can reduce payroll costs by up to 50% depending on the role, without sacrificing quality.


### Proximity and time-zone alignment


Colombia works in a time zone close to the US, making real-time collaboration far easier than outsourcing to distant regions.


### Favorable business climate


Government incentives for foreign investment, a stable environment, and intellectual-property protections make Colombia a reliable destination.

## Frequently asked questions


### Is it cheaper to outsource to Colombia?


Yes. Depending on the role, payroll costs can be up to 50% lower than in the US, with strong talent quality.


### Do I need a legal entity in Colombia to hire there?


No. With a global payroll or employer-of-record partner you can hire and pay Colombian talent without opening a local entity.


### Should I hire Colombian talent as a contractor or an employee?


It depends on the role and permanence. Ongoing, supervised work points to employment; project-based, independent work fits a contractor. Classify carefully to avoid penalties.
