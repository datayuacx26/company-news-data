## **The Challenge: Why Contract Creation Used to Take 3 Hours**


Before automation, HR managers spent hours manually creating employment contracts, copying templates, adjusting legal terms, checking compliance, and managing signatures. Each contract could take up to three hours, often involving multiple reviews, version control issues, and unnecessary administrative work.


That inefficiency led to:


- Delays in onboarding and hiring
- Higher operational costs
- Lost time that could have been spent developing people and culture


## **Ontop’s Mission: Simplicity Through Smart Automation**


Ontop’s philosophy is clear: automation should simplify, not complicate. Ontop created easy-to-use AI for HR tech solutions. This helps boost productivity without the need for extra training or complicated setup. Every feature, like making job descriptions and generating contracts, is made to reduce repetitive tasks. This allows HR teams to spend more time on their employees.


## **The Journey: From 3 Hours to 6 Minutes**


When Ontop launched in August 2020, creating an employment contract took about three hours.
By the end of that year, the first version of the platform reduced it to 30 minutes.
By 2024, it dropped to 10 minutes.
Today, thanks to AI contract creation and automated HR workflows, the process takes only six minutes.


This represents a 30x improvement in efficiency and a complete transformation from manual paperwork to intelligent automation.


## **The Impact: Real Savings for HR Teams**
