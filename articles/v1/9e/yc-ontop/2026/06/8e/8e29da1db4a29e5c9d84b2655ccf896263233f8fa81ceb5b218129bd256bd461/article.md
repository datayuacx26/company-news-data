- Based on real Ontop data, **LATAM offers the best cost–quality ratio** , with an average salary of **$1,739 USD** , making it the region with the highest ROI for 2025.

- In contrast, **the United States and Canada are recommended only for senior roles** , with average compensation of **$2,973 USD** and **$2,133 USD** , respectively.

## **Emerging Markets for Remote Hiring in 2026**


‍


### **1. LATAM – Maximum Cost Efficiency (Avg: $1,739 USD)**


LATAM leads the **cost–quality ratio** in 2025, offering strong talent with time-zone compatibility for North American companies.


- **Recommended Countries** : Uruguay, Colombia, Ecuador, Paraguay, Honduras
- **Strengths** : Software Development, Marketing, Customer Experience, Product, Data
- **Competitive Advantage** : Strong English proficiency in the Southern Cone and Colombia


### **2. Asia – Competitive Technical Specialization (Avg: $1,502 USD)**


Asia remains the go-to destination for highly technical roles with efficient costs.


- **Top Countries** : India, Pakistan, Indonesia
- **Ideal Profiles** : Software Engineering, QA, Technical Support, Data Science, DevOps


### **3. Africa – Operational Cost Optimization (Avg: $750 USD)**


Africa provides the **lowest cost** options for operational and junior-level functions.


- **Ideal Applications** : Customer Support, Data Entry, SDR Junior, Admin Support
- **Note** : Limited availability for specialized roles—exceptions include Nigeria, South Africa, Egypt


### **4. Premium Markets – Investment in Specialized Talent**


Reserved for high-expertise roles and executive leadership.


- **Regions** : United States (~$3000 USD), Canada (~$2100 USD), Western Europe ($1500–$2500 USD)
- **Use Cases** : Executive Leadership, Compliance, Finance Analysis, Advanced Engineering
