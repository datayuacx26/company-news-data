# Dr. Wang Ruxing


#### Wuxi People’s Hospital


In China, we regularly perform three or more ablations per day. RMN allows us to get out of the heavy lead, focusing more on patient care in an easy, relaxed way.


As Chief of Cardiology at Wuxi People’s Hospital & Deputy Director of EP and Pacing for China’s Jiangsu province, Dr. Ruxing Wang has become a leading advocate, clinical researcher, and educator of EP fellows with respect to robotic arrhythmia care in China. Dr. Wang has an impressive safety record and states that “In China, we regularly perform three or more ablations per day. RMN allows us to get out of the heavy lead, focusing more on patient care in an easy, relaxed way. Why would I use traditional manual techniques?”
