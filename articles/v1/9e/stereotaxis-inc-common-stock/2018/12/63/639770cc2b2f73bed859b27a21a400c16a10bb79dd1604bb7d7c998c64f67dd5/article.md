# Dr. Brad Bacik

# Dr. Brad Bacik


#### Jefferson Torresdale Hospital


The technology allows me to get anywhere in the heart and has kept fluoroscopy time to under five minutes for almost every case.


A Cardiac Electrophysiologist at Jefferson Torresdale Hospital in Philadelphia, Dr. Brad Bacik has handled some of the most complex AF and VT cases. He trained under Dr. Hiroshi Nakagawa from the University of Oklahoma, a leading expert in applying RMN to cardiac ablation. Thanks to Stereotaxis, Dr. Bacik feels confident tackling challenging cases. “The technology enables me to reach any area of the heart while keeping fluoroscopy time under five minutes for nearly every procedure,” he explains. A RoboticEP user since 2005, Dr. Bacik has treated hundreds of patients, mentored aspiring electrophysiologists, and remains an active Society for Cardiac Robotic Navigation member.
