# Dr. Subhashini Gowda


#### Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital


The Stereotaxis system has allowed me to successfully manage a busy complex ablation program, while providing the best possible patient care with advanced and cutting edge technologies.


Dr. Subhashini Gowda of Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital in New Jersey has relied on Stereotaxis technology to help manage a vibrant practice. “The Stereotaxis system has allowed me to successfully manage a busy complex ablation program, while providing the best possible patient care,” she says, “with advanced and cutting edge technologies.”


Making patients happy by curing a condition that may have plagued them for a lifetime is what drives Dr. Gowda, and RMN has played a critical role in a wide range of procedures she has done thus far, says the 20+ year veteran EP. “The Stereotaxis system reduces the risk of cardiac perforation, reduces radiation, and provides the ease of working outside of radiation.”


Thank you Dr. Gowda for your long history of using RMN to provide patient care!
