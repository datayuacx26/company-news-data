# Dr. David Burkhardt


#### Texas Cardiac Arrhythmia Institute


Stereotaxis’ advanced, computer-controlled technology has exceeded our expectations, especially in terms of contact force and lesion effectiveness.


“Any job is easy if you love what you do,” says Dr. David Burkhardt. Even so, he’s worked very hard to be one of the best in his field.


Dr. Burkhardt, Cardiac EP at the Texas Cardiac Arrhythmia Institute (TCAI) in Austin, TX, is sought out worldwide for his expertise in treating complicated scar-related and difficult-to reach VTs. In 2009, TCAI became the first institution in Central Texas to implement Stereotaxis technology and Dr. Burkhardt holds the distinction of being the leading Robotic EP physician in the U.S.


“Stereotaxis’ advanced, computer-controlled technology has exceeded our expectations, especially in terms of contact force and lesion effectiveness,” he says.
