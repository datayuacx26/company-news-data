# Dr. Dave Mehta


#### Mount Sinai St. Luke’s


We’ve had countless procedures where Stereotaxis technology has been beneficial, allowing us to do things that are very difficult or impossible with a manual catheter.


“It’s amazing how far EP has come,” says Dr. Dave Mehta, Director of Cardiac EP at Mount Sinai St. Luke’s Hospital in New York. “When he began practicing, it was all diagnostic; there weren’t even ablations.”


Technology is not just a young man’s game in the case of Dr. Mehta, who has embraced robotic ablation and encouraged every operator at St. Luke’s to utilize Stereotaxis RMN for complex ventricular arrhythmias. “We’ve had countless procedures where Stereotaxis technology has been beneficial, allowing us to do things that are very difficult or impossible with a manual catheter,” says Dr. Mehta.


Today, St. Luke’s ranks fifth nationally in PVC/VT treatment with robotics. Congratulations to Dr. Mehta and his team for staying on the cutting edge!
