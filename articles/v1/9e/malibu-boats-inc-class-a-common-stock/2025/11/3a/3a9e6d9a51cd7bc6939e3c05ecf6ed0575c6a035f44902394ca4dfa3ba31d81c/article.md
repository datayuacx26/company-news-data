# Premium Audio: Why Malibu Boats Have The Best Sound System on the Water

Imagine watching Baywatch with the Hoff and his famous crew running down the beach toward the camera. Now imagine the same scene but without the equally famous piano, drums and vocals Baywatch soundtrack. It just isn't the same, right?


Now imagine watching Back to the Future without its incredible soundtrack. It wouldn't make sense, or be as memorable, seeing Marty and the Doc on screen without it!


Soundtracks matter.


The right music in the right moment can create memories that will stay with you for life. That's why when it was time to choose an audio partner, the Malibu Boats team searched far and wide for the[perfect audio system partner](https://www.malibuboats.com/discover-malibu/only-malibu/wetsounds) for our boat.


[Wet Sounds](https://www.wetsounds.com/) is the industry's leading provider of premium marine audio equipment. Its products are known for delivering crystal-clear, powerful sound that consistently performs to the highest standards, in the difficult and demanding marine environment.


## The Wet Sounds Advantage


There's a reason Wet Sounds has become synonymous with premium marine audio. It isn't just because their speakers can handle getting wet. The reason is that their systems have a singular engineering purpose: Thriving in one of the harshest environments any audio equipment will face. Think about it: Frequent water spray, harsh UV exposure, temperature extremes, vibration, and the open-air acoustics of being on the water all create unique challenges that Wet Sounds has spent years perfecting solutions for.


Recently[recognized as one of the Top 3 Marine Vendors for 2025](https://shop.wetsounds.com/blog/wet-sounds-named-a-top-3-marine-vendor-for-2025-by-mobile-electronics-industry-awards/) by the Mobile Electronics Industry Awards, Wet Sounds continues to set the standard for what's possible in marine audio. This recognition reflects their relentless commitment to innovation, performance, and delivering sound experiences that exceed expectations every single time.


## Built for the Marine Environment


What makes Wet Sounds speakers exceptional isn't just how they sound – although we'll get to that. It's how they're constructed. Every component is designed specifically for marine applications, using advanced materials like nylon and fiberglass composites, that can withstand the punishment of daily use on the water.


Across the range, Wet Sounds speakers also feature clever design details such as concealed mounting hardware, for an extra-clean and sleek look.


It's not all about looks and durability, though. Wet Sounds speakers are specifically designed and positioned to play your music cleanly and powerfully inside your Malibu, effortlessly sending it throughout our boat and across the water to your rider, without breaking a sweat (if speakers could sweat!)
