# Malibu MONSOON Engines: Everything You Ever Wanted to Know

When you go shopping for a wake boat, it's perfectly normal to get distracted by the flashy paint jobs, onboard gadgets, and interior customization options offered on modern wake boats. But the reality is that the most important component of any wake boat is the engine.


Given the critical role an engine plays in your wake boating experience, Malibu Boats proudly mak use of our world-famous MONSOON Engines. The MONSOON series was purpose-designed from the start, to offer reliable, efficient, and quiet performance tailored specifically to the unique demands of wake boats. They are the ultimate representation of everything required of an engine in a next generation wake boat. And we build them right next door to the very same factory building your Malibu boat.


Read on to find out how MONSOON engines deliver spectacular wake performance, precise torque control and unforgettable ride experiences, all making it the industry leader in marine engine performance.


## Built for the Way You Actually Boat


##


Most people don't realize that wake boat engine performance isn't really about top-end horsepower. The thing that really matters is what happens in the first few seconds after you hit the throttle - also known as the 'hole shot'.


MONSOON engines are designed specifically for wake sports, prioritizing low-end torque for quick "hole shots" even when boats are fully loaded.


An engine's ability to get a fully loaded hull up to surf or wakeboard speed quickly is the true test of its performance, and so that's exactly the challenge the MONSOON M5, M6 and LT4 engines were designed to solve.


### Efficiency in Wakesurfing Performance


When you're out on the water doing watersports, you're living at 3,500 RPM most of the time. Therefore, Malibu engineered MONSOON to hit hard right in that range. MONSOON's torque-focused performance is optimized to peak around 3,500 RPM, making it easier for boats to handle heavy ballast loads.


The practical result of this is that the engine isn't working nearly as hard to get the boat moving, which saves fuel. At the same speed, most Malibu boats run at lower RPM than the majority of inboard engines on the water today. This is a meaningful efficiency advantage that translates into lower fuel bills over a full season.


### Full-spectrum Propeller Performance


That strong low-end torque also allows Malibu Boats to run more pitch on the props, which means the MONSOON engine gets you out of the hole (the 'hole shot' we referred to earlier) with authority, and without giving up anything on top-end speed. For most engines, excelling at both ends of the performance spectrum is a genuine engineering challenge. The MONSOON line was purpose-built to live in both worlds.


### In-House Manufacturing


To pull this off, Malibu brought engine production in-house at our state-of-the-art, 70,000 square-foot manufacturing facility in Tennessee. It's there that the team performs research and development on dynos, followed by on-water testing. In fact, we've hit over 5,000 hours of combined testing, and still counting!


### Custom Gear Ratios Tuned for Your Boat


Our engineers calculated custom gear ratios for every single model in the lineup, because a lighter crossover boat and a full-size performance model have genuinely different powertrain needs. Despite weight differences between some models, every boat in the Malibu lineup gets an individually tuned engine setup specifically optimized for its hull.


The same thinking applies to our propellers. Malibu tested each of our models from sea level to 8,300 feet of elevation to find optimal propeller pitches for the full lineup. Thanks to these efforts, when you're running at capacity on a high-altitude mountain lake, your MONSOON engine delivers the same performance as it would at sea level. That's not a small thing.


## Quieter Than You'd Expect. And That Matters More Than You Think


Engine noise is one of those things you might not think much about until you've spent a day on a loud boat. By the time you're back at the dock, everyone's a little worn out - not from the sun and the water, but from the constant hum and vibration underfoot. The reason is that engine NVH (noise, vibration, and harshness - a standard industry metric) takes a real personal toll on the overall experience of boating.


### Independently Tested Quietness


For that reason, Malibu invested heavily in making the MONSOON engine line genuinely quiet. Our approach combines strategically placed acoustic foam with the latest direct injection technology, and the results have been independently verified. Testing conducted by Boating Magazine at 3,500 RPM revealed that MONSOON engines registered well below the threshold that can cause hearing damage.


### Noise: Bad for your Health


That might sound like a technical footnote, but consider this: many inboard boats on the water today run at noise levels that, over time, genuinely threaten your hearing health. Malibu's MONSOON engines represent a significant step forward on that front, and that's important because we know that your family is on this boat with you.


Beyond the health angle, a quieter engine makes the day better - you can hold a conversation at normal volume, the music on your stereo sounds the way it should, and you arrive back at the dock feeling refreshed rather than wrung out.


## Inherent Reliability


##


Every MONSOON engine is specifically engineered for absolute reliability at all times, meaning that you never have to worry about whether your MONSOON engine can keep up with your demanding surf lifestyle.


That starts with building an engine that is designed from the start, to operate in a marine environment.


But taking an automotive engine block and turning it into a reliable marine engine (a process called marinizing) is where many manufacturers run into trouble.


Malibu's engineers and partners, however, spent thousands of hours designing, 3D-printing, and testing over 200 custom parts to get this right. The most important of those innovations is built right into the front of the engine -and it changes everything about how our powertrain is designed, serviced, and trusted on the water.


### Front End Accessory Drive (FeAD)


MONSOON's Front-End Accessory Drive (FEAD) is our secret sauce for making the world's greatest wake sports engines. FEAD is a custom-cast, patent-pending accessory system designed to enhance the reliability and serviceability of MONSOON engines.


FEAD integrates a cooling system that controls engine oil fluid temperatures, enhancing serviceability and maintenance.


The big story about the FeAD system is that it eliminates the automotive water pump and moves critical components - including the alternator, Glycol circulation pump, thermostat housing, and transmission cooler - higher up in the engine where they're easier to service. It also eliminates several failure points that are common in more conventionally marinized engines.


The result is that MONSOON engines offer unmatched reliability, easy serviceability, and superior power for surfing and wakeboarding.


### Closed Cooling System


Perhaps the most significant reliability innovation is what Malibu did with cooling. In most marine engines, raw water (water drawn directly from outside the boat) circulates through the engine to keep it cool - but that same raw water brings problems with it. Debris, seaweed, and corrosion can all find their way into cooling passages and cause serious damage. In MONSOON Engines, raw water only touches the exhaust. Everything else, including the engine block, transmission cooler, oil cooler, run in a closed Glycol circuit.


Glycol is the same antifreeze compound used in automotive cooling systems and is more efficient than raw water, plus it doesn't carry any of the corrosion or debris risks. It's a fundamentally cleaner, more reliable fully integrated cooling system that delivers stable engine oil fluid temperatures and the kind of reliable engine you would expect from our next generation MONSOON engines.


### High-precision manufacturing


One of the first things we did when building the MONSOON line was to outfit our Tennessee facility with 'smart tools' - systems that show technicians the exact torque specifications for every fastener on a screen, and that won't advance to the next step until the correct torque is achieved with the correct tool. It completely removes human error from the assembly process, granting our manufacturing process a level of precision that compounds across every engine that comes off the line.
