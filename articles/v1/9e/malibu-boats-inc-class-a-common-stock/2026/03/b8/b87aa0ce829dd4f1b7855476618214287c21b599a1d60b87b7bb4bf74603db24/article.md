## What is Malibu's Surf Gate?


## ****


Surf Gate is the wake boat industry's original, best and most-awarded wave shaping technology on the market, and it's exclusively available on Malibu and Axis boats.


Surf Gate is a pair of hydraulically-actuated gates mounted at the stern, on either side of the hull. When activated by the touch of a button at the Malibu Command Center, a hydraulic actuator 'pushes' the gate outwards, diverting the flow of water alongside the hull and creating your specified wave.


Surf Gate allows you to create a perfect wave on either side of the boat with the push of a button. No need to shift people or weight.

### **Why Other Brands Use Tabs Instead**


Most of our competition relies on trim tabs or similar surface-mounted devices to shape their waves. And yes, tabs can create a surfable wave. They divert water, they generate push. But here's where the functional differences really matter.


Tabs are essentially flat plates that angle into the water flow. They create drag to redirect water and generate wave energy, which works, but it's not as hydrodynamically efficient as Surf Gate's integrated design. Because tabs sit at the transom and work by creating resistance, they require more ballast and more power to achieve similar results. They're also typically smaller and less adjustable in terms of how much water they can displace.


Surf Gate, on the other hand, was designed to work *with* the water flow coming off our hull, not against it. The result is cleaner wave transitions, less drag, more efficient performance, and waves that form faster with less ballast needed.


It's the difference between a system that was engineered as part of the complete boat design versus a solution that was adapted to fit an existing platform. Both will get you surfing, but the experience and efficiency just aren't the same.
