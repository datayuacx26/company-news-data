# What's New in Cinapse — May 2026

May has been a focused month of refinements. Tightening up the details that make day-to-day work in Cinapse feel more polished and predictable.


## **Text case management**


**‍** Elements and categories now support configurable text casing. Whether your production uses all caps, title case, or sentence case, you can set it once and have it applied consistently throughout the app.


## **Strip deletion confirmation**


**‍** Deleting strips now prompts a new type of confirmation before anything is removed. The dialog is context-aware, so it knows whether you're deleting a single strip or a selection. You always know exactly what's about to happen before it does.


## **Breakdown element tooltips**


**‍** Collapsed metadata rows in the breakdown panel now show a tooltip on hover. You can read the full content without needing to expand the row, which keeps the view clean while still keeping information accessible.


## **Bug fixes**


**‍** A handful of reliability improvements shipped this month as well: drag-and-drop collision detection is more consistent, and element syncing in PowerSync no longer skips updates it shouldn't.


June will bring new ways to sign in, including Google and Apple SSO, schedule downloads, and the debut of Cinapse Professional, our new plan built for individual professionals that need their own dedicated Cinapse subscription plan in between productions.
