# What's New in Cinapse — August 2026

This month we’re introducing Cinapse Production Intelligence: import a script and get Draft 1 of your schedule done fast. It ships alongside a full templates and presets system you can organize into folders and pull into any new production, plus a stack of customer-requested improvements.


## Cinapse Production Intelligence (Beta)


You’ve already read the script and thought through how the film or series needs to be made. **Cinapse Production Intelligence helps with what comes next: getting to Draft 1 of your schedule faster.**


Opt-in when you import a script from PDF or Final Draft, and Production Intelligence analyzes the script, identifies elements, organizes your categories and breakdowns, and summarizes each scene. It shows its suggestions alongside the original, so you decide what to use. Review its work and confirm the elements it identified made it onto the appropriate breakdown sheets before building on the draft.


Draft 1 is only the starting point. Cast availability, permit windows, location restrictions, daylight hours, and countless other production realities will shape the schedule. And most of the time, you don’t know those constraints yet.


**Production Intelligence gets ADs and producers to that starting point faster, so you can begin shaping the schedule, testing assumptions, and building an early budget.**


Available now in beta, with more to come.


## New templates & presets options


Every AD builds a way of working, and now more of it is reusable. On top of layout templates and color palettes, you can save report layout templates, report presets, and element category presets. Everything is managed from one place in My Account under Templates & Presets.


## Organize your templates & presets


As that library grows, a flat list stops working. Your templates and presets now organize into folders. Create them, drag to reorder, and rename in place, so you can group by show, by department, or however you actually work.


## Add templates & presets from the start


Spinning up a new production used to mean importing your layouts, colors, and templates one at a time. Now, every time you create a new production, you can bring all of your preferences in at the same time: stripboard layouts, report layouts, report presets, and more. Set it up once, and start every show with your preferences loaded in.


## More Features. More Improvements.


**Breakdown sheet Merge Elements.** Combine duplicate elements across your breakdown into one.


**Category Templates.** Save and export category setups to reuse across productions.


**Configurable built-in categories.** Set text case and section color on built-in categories.


**Full-screen report preset editor.** Edit report presets with a live preview on a mock production.


**Weather and sunrise/sunset.** Preferences and sun times on all banner types, extended 12 to 18 months out.


**More report flexibility.** Reports on boards without day breaks, and reports from any category type.


**CSV export improvements.** Scene in column A, decimal page counts, combined cast number and name, and two fixes, all customer requests.


**Print Preview.** The reports Print button is now Print Preview.


**Updated privacy controls.** New cookie preferences and privacy settings.


**Bug fixes & stability:** Movie Magic import improvements, upgrade and pause limits, background actor quantities now persisting even after importing a new version of the script, Reports Manager clock display time, MFA re-enable, and linked element sync.
