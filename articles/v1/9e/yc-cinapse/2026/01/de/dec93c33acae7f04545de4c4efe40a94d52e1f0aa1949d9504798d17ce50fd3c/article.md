# What's New in Cinapse — January 2026

January’s release delivers a mix of **new capabilities** , **production-management improvements** , and **important fixes** across scheduling, importing, permissions, and performance.


This update focuses on making Cinapse even more flexible as productions scale — while smoothing out real-world edge cases reported by working ADs and production teams.


## 🔁 Subscription & Production Management


### Upgrade Path: Starter → Team → Team Pro


You can now **upgrade seamlessly between Starter, Team, and Team Pro** as your production grows — without needing to reach out to the Cinapse team.


This makes it easier to start small and scale up only when you need additional features, seats, or permissions.


### 👥 Buy Additional Seats for Your Production


Productions can now **add more seats mid-run** , giving teams flexibility as new collaborators join.


If you only need seats for part of the production, our team can still help tailor this — but the default self-serve flow is now faster and clearer.


**To upgrade or add seats:**


- Select the production name next to the Cinapse logo, **or**
- Select **My Account** , **or**
- Select **Members**


Then navigate to **Production Settings → Plan** .


### 🔑 Transfer Ownership Workflow


A new **ownership transfer workflow** is now available, making it easier to hand off productions when roles change or teams transition.


You can access this via the **My Productions** page — right-click or use the menu on the right side of a production to transfer ownership, upgrade plans, rename the production, and more.


## 🖼️ Breakdown Sheets: Images (New!)


You can now **upload images directly to breakdown sheets** .


This unlocks richer visual context for props, wardrobe, locations, VFX, and more — especially helpful for prep-heavy or visually complex productions.


**Pro tip:** Upload reference images from Shotdeck or other visual libraries to keep everything in one place.


New breakdown reports that include images are coming soon.


## 📥 Importing & Data Integrity Improvements


We shipped several important fixes to script and schedule importing to ensure schedules reflect **exactly what you intended** — no missing data, no silent changes.


### Fixed:


- **Repeated strips and breakdowns** (e.g. weather cover scenes used multiple times throughout a schedule) are no longer deduplicated when importing` .cinapse` files
- Importing **Scriptation into an existing file** now correctly places new strips into the Scrapyard for use across all stripboards
- Importing Scriptation files now correctly respects **Episodic vs Feature Film** file types
- Overall improvements to reliability and accuracy across Scriptation and` .cinapse` imports


These fixes directly address real-world episodic, revision-heavy, and multi-board workflows.


## 🧭 Scheduling & Calendar Fixes


- Fixed an issue where **Editor users could not duplicate calendars**
- Fixed **right-click copy / paste menu** behavior on the stripboard
- Improved **change function performance** across schedules


## 🔎 Navigation, Search & UI Improvements


- Improved **free-text searching** across productions
- Fixed an issue where **special characters were not displaying correctly** in the production navigation
- General UI polish and stability improvements


## 🔐 Authentication & Platform Improvements


- Improved **login, sign-up, and authentication flows**
- Backend and platform updates to support safer, faster releases going forward


## What’s Next

- Reporting UI upgrades and new layouts
- Expanded publishing and permissions workflows
- Continued performance and reliability improvements
