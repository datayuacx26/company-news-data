# What's New in Cinapse — March 2026

March was defined by a single priority: Rebuilding Cinapse's reports system from the ground up. We also began laying the groundwork for Cinapse Professional, a new subscription tier launching Summer 2026.


## Reports rewrite


We rebuilt every core report type from scratch: DOODs, category lists, category-by-day, calendars, and shooting schedules.


The new engine gives us full control over layout, typography, and pagination. Reports render faster, look better in print, and are significantly easier for us to extend going forward.


After the initial rebuild, we spent the rest of the month on polish: re-enabling layouts, adding more layout options, improving the report preview experience, and cleaning up edge cases across all report types.


## Cinapse Professional groundwork


Behind the scenes, we began preparing the infrastructure for Cinapse Professional, our upcoming personal subscription tier. More on this soon.


## Bug Fixes


- Resolved a login bug affecting some users
- Fixed an auth-redirect issue on session expiry
- Corrected a production-day calculation that could miscount in specific multi-unit configurations
