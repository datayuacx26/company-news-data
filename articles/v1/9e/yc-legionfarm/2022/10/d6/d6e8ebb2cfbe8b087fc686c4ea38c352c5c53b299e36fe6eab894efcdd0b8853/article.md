Greetings from Romania, guys! That’s where Edris, whom you know as Mr Xdris, is from and today we’re going to tell you all about him and his path as a Warzone professional gamer.


Edris, a 22-year-old self-taught Graphic Designer and Architecture University student from Bucharest, has several interests, one of which is sports cars, which he enjoys driving, building, and filming for his YouTube channel. These hobbies he combines with his other great passion that he eventually turned into a job — video games.


It all started in 2010, when Mr Xdris and his brother got themselves their first Call of Duty games. At first, both boys weren’t so keen on multiplayer, but once Edris tried it, he couldn’t get enough of it. From that moment, he began playing every single CoD title after that, including Warzone, where he reached the top.


After a while, Mr Xdris’s exceptional skills began to be acknowledged not only by his friends, but also by the community. Edris has begun to participate in competitions, winning them multiple times while also breaking records for most kills. Amazing!


And just look at these stats and achievements!


• K/D 3.14
• 62 341 kills
• 576 wins
• 7% winrate
• One of the top 3 Players for Kills in Romania


When Edris was seriously considering the idea of having a career in professional gaming, he heard of Legionfarm and applied for an aspirant program. One of the motivations for doing so was his desire to help his mother who has diabetes. Since her treatment is quite expensive, Mr Xdris tries to do what he can to help her, which deserves pure admiration!


After successful graduation, Edris became our full-time Pro, who can teach how to be great in Warzone.


‘Legionfarm helped me develop my coaching and social skills. Finally, I can say I’m following my dream of being a professional gamer and taking care of my mother’ says he.


Besides pursuing his career further, Mr Xdris’s future plans involve taking care of his mother and her health while also investing in his car business. And we wish him nothing but the best of luck with these!
