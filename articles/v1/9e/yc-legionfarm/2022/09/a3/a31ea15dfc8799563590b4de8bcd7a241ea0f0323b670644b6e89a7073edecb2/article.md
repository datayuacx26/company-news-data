How are you doing, friends? Today we would like to take you to Germany where our brilliant PRO HitX_Chaos, also known as just Chaos, is from, and to tell her story!


HitX Chaos or just N as she prefers, is a 34-year-old Apex Legends professional player, mother of a 14-year-old daughter, and a multi-talented individual.


And that is not an exaggeration given that she was a drummer and singer in a band, worked in hospitals, cared for (and still cares for) animals, particularly dogs, enjoys traveling and learning new languages. Once she even spent a year on a trip from Mexico to Panama with her daughter who was five at the time. Just wow!


Another of Chaos’s passions is gaming, which she began when she was about 6 and, because there were no online games at the time, she and her friends played offline titles on Nintendo, Gameboy, and PC. When multiplayer games became popular, she fell in love with first-person shooters like the Unreal, Doom, and Battlefield franchises, where she even made it to the top 15 with the most kills made by using one particular weapon. N didn’t stop playing even after the injury that caused her right arm to lose sensation.


‘Whenever I heard a new good shooter is out, I wanted to give it a try,’ shares she.


That’s how she discovered Apex Legends, and she hasn’t stopped playing it since Season 5. Here are her current stats:


▪️K/D 2.5
▪️35,000 kills
▪️12% winrate
▪️Multiple times Master
▪️125 Arenas win streak


Knowing about her passion for gaming and helping others, Chaos’s friend advised her to apply for the LF Aspirant program. After successful training, N became a Pro and has been working with us for over 6 months. During this time, she accomplished a lot, including joining Esports Team HitBox.


‘Legionfarm is the first job in over 14 years that allows me to be flexible with my responsibilities,’ says she about her experience. ‘I enjoy the sessions with our clients a lot, they are wonderful people and it makes me happy to care for them and to make them happy’.
