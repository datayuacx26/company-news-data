Our PROs community is quite big and unites gamers from all over the world, including exquisite France, where Jeremie Mac Carthy, whom you know as Eiwiin, is from. Today it’s his story we want to share with you!⁠
⁠
Jeremie, our Apex Legends PRO, is 26 years old and works as a film editor and does film commercials in addition to teaching people how to be better gamers. His own gaming journey began when he was four years old and received his first Game Boy from his father. He switched to PC at the age of ten and, like many others, played CS, PUBG, and other games before discovering Apex Legends in 2020, which won his heart. ⁠
⁠
After spending a reasonable amount of time and making some impressive progress in the game, Eiwiin started coaching people there. That’s when he heard about Legionfarm and decided to join. After being with us for over 1,5 years, Jeremie has 629 hours on the platform, and over 300 played sessions. Check out his stats:⁠
⁠
Over 45,000 kills⁠
3.2 K/D⁠
Winrate 20%
⁠
‘I love having the opportunity to play and coach in my favorite game. It’s a great experience overall and a good addition to my job’, says Eiwiin.⁠
⁠
Apart from the opportunities and support provided by the platform, Jeremie truly enjoys spending time with his clients, with whom he frequently becomes good friends. And we’re sure the feeling is mutual! ⁠
⁠
As for the future, Eiwiin is planning on pursuing his career further.⁠
⁠
‘I want to continue evolving in the gaming industry and see where it brings me, and will definitely continue coaching,’ he shares. ⁠
⁠
Well, we wish him luck and will definitely be rooting for him!⁠
