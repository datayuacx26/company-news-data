Each of our PROs is a brilliant individual with a vibrant personality. So, today we’d like you to learn more about Bek, whom you know as BEKONX, our great Apex Legends and Warzone professional player!⁠
⁠
Besides working in LF, a 29-years-old Bek sells graphics cards and is interested in mining, to which he dedicated his Youtube channel. Yet, his true passion is gaming. At the age of six, Bek fell in love with video games after playing Karateka with a friend. He then went on to play CS 1.6, Doom, and other games. ⁠
⁠
When PUBG and BR games first appeared, they immediately drew BEKONX’s attention and led him to Apex Legend, which quickly became his favorite title so far.⁠
⁠
BEKONX’s friend Preepwalker approached him in 2019 with an offer to play Apex ‘as a coach’ with an amateur gamer. Following the session, the other player (who later revealed himself to be a member of LF) invited him to join the team. ⁠
⁠
‘He introduced me to the team that impressed me with harmony, professionalism, and friendliness. I liked how they talked and interacted with each other,’ he says.⁠
⁠
Thus, Bek became one of the first Apex PROs on the platform and then a VIP with whom you can play both Apex and Warzone now. ⁠
