# Legionfarm Hardware Program. Part 2

Our Hardware program stories are back, and today let us tell you about zorteip’s!
⁠
Pietro Zapani is our 25-year-old Warzone PRO from Brazil. His awesome skills and impressive tournament achievements attracted the attention of Legionfarm, and almost 1,5 years ago, zorteip joined our team. In addition to his work as a professional player, zorteip is pursuing a degree in engineering mechatronics and practicing wakeboarding.⁠


⁠Not so long ago, Pitro came home from a New Year’s celebration and found out that his PC was broken. That’s when he remembered our Hardware program and took part in it. With the help of this loan, zorteip was able to replace some parts of his already decent PC and continue his work. ⁠
⁠
Later, Pietro participated in the program again to buy a gaming laptop.⁠


⁠‘This literally changed my life because I’m a wakeboarder and I can visit multiple cable parks around Brazil and work at the same time, which I’m doing now’, he shares.⁠
