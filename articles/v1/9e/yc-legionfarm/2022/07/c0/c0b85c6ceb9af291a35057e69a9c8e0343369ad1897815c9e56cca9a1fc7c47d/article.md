Diogo, aka Adzonn, is a university student and a life-long gamer. Growing up on video games, Diogo always thought about making a career out of it. Call of Duty Warzone’s release prompted him to try his hand at streaming and esports, with both undertakings ending up tremendously successful. ⁠
• 753 wins⁠

⁠
Diogo started streaming a year ago. A great player and natural coach, he fits in with the LegionFarm family so well it’s like he was born for it. Having followed his friends’ example and applying for a job at LF, he’s been with us for around two months so far; a 5-star rating, 37 sessions with a total of 106 hours played, and Adzonn is just warming up!⁠
⁠
“So far, being at Legionfarm has been great,” he says. “I met new people, I am working professionally on what I like to do most, and that is good for me. It makes me even happier to do what I like and help others at the same time. I’ve met several clients through Legionfarm and some of them I already treat as friends, but I always keep it professional. I hope to stay here for a long time!”⁠
