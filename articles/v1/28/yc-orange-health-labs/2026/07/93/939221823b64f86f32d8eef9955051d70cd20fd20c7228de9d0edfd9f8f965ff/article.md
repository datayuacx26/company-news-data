# Intermittent Fasting: Can It Really Help With Diabetes?

In this episode of **The Orange Club Podcast** , Dietician Nidhi Nigam shares her perspective on intermittent fasting and its impact on health. While fasting has become a popular trend, its real value lies not in quick fixes but in consistent, long-term practice.


***Watch the clip here:***[Intermittent fasting and diabetes](https://youtube.com/shorts/O87V7ws2XXg?feature=share)


## More Than Just a Trend


Intermittent fasting has been widely promoted as a shortcut for weight loss. While it can help you shed a few kilos, using it only as a weight-loss strategy often backfires. Your body needs stability, and treating fasting like a temporary diet can cause more confusion than benefits.


## Long-Term Commitment Brings Results


The real advantage of intermittent fasting comes when it is done consistently over time. Practiced correctly, it can help improve insulin sensitivity and support better blood sugar management. For people with diabetes, this can make a meaningful difference in overall health.


## A Tool, Not a Quick Fix


Fasting isn’t a magic bullet, it works best when combined with a balanced approach to nutrition and lifestyle. The key is sustainability. Whether it’s intermittent fasting or a balanced diet, consistency matters more than intensity.
