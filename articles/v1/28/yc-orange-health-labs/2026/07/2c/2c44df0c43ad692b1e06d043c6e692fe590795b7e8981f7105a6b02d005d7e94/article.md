# Why You Should Start Your Health Journey With a Blood Test?

As another year comes to a close, health goals and fitness resolutions are about to take center stage again. But before you sign up for a gym membership or start a new diet plan, Dietician **Nidhi Nigam** suggests one crucial first step: **get your basic blood work done.**


***Watch the clip here:***[Why blood tests matter for health journey](https://youtube.com/shorts/K8QVuLOp1wE?feature=share)


## The Smartest First Step to Any Health Goal


Before beginning any fitness or diet routine, it’s essential to know where you stand. A simple blood test gives insight into your body’s current state, helping you make informed choices about what kind of plan suits you best. Without this, even the most well-intentioned health efforts can backfire.


## Health Checks Aren’t Just for When You’re Sick


Many people think blood tests are only needed when something feels wrong, but that’s not true. Getting a routine checkup once a year, even when you feel fine, helps detect potential issues early. For those with existing conditions like diabetes or thyroid imbalances, getting tested every six months is even more important.


## Make Blood Tests a Regular Habit


Think of your blood test like a seasonal reset, just as you refresh your wardrobe during a sale, your body deserves a similar check-in. A small effort today ensures you can set realistic goals and track your progress safely through the year.
