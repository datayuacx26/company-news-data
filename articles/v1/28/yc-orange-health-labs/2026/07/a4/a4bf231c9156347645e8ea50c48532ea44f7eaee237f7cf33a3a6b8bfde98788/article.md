# How Often Should You Take Whey Protein?

In this episode of *The Orange Club Podcast* , Dietitian Nidhi Nigam shares her perspective on protein supplements like whey and plant-based powders. Are they a smart addition to your diet, or should food always come first?


***Watch the clip here:***[How often to take whey protein](https://youtube.com/shorts/BVXq22DkAgY?feature=share)


## How Many Scoops Are Too Many?


A single scoop of whey or plant-based protein powder won’t damage your health. In fact, for people who live in PGs, travel often, or lack access to freshly prepared meals, supplements can be a practical way to meet daily protein needs. They fill nutritional gaps when your diet falls short, without throwing your body off balance.


## What Are the Effects of Long-Term Dependence?


Protein powders have only been widely used for about two decades, which means we don’t yet know their long-term effects. While they’re safe in the short term, there’s no data to confirm that relying on them for years is risk-free. Nidhi suggests that this uncertainty is reason enough to treat them as a backup rather than a daily habit.


## Food Should Always Be the First Choice


Whole foods naturally provide more than just protein, they come with fiber, vitamins, minerals, and other nutrients that powders can’t replicate. Building your meals around natural protein sources like pulses, lentils, eggs, dairy, or lean meats ensures balanced nutrition. Supplements can support you when food access is limited, but they shouldn’t replace real meals.
