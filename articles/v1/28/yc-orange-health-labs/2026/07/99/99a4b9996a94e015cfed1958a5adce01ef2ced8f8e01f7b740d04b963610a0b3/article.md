# The Misleading World of “Healthy” Advice

In this episode of **The Orange Club Podcast** , Dietician **Nidhi Nigam** talks about a silent yet powerful force shaping the health choices of today’s generation, misinformation. From misleading food labels to unqualified influencers, half-baked advice is everywhere, and it’s affecting how people eat, think, and live.


***Watch the clip here:***[Debunking “Healthy” Advice](https://youtube.com/shorts/IYM-kOXcxBg?feature=share)


## The Power of Popularity Over Expertise


A large following doesn’t equal authority. Many social media influencers share “tips” that sound convincing but lack scientific grounding. As Nidhi explains, *“People who don’t have the knowledge or authority are still dictating terms because they have 100K or 200K followers and people are listening to them.”* This blind trust is creating confusion and unhealthy habits among those genuinely trying to make better choices.


## Food Labels That Tell Only Half the Story


Marketing language on food products often paints a misleading picture. Words like *“natural,” “sugar-free,”* or *“low-fat”* can be deceiving; they don’t always mean the product is healthy. For many consumers, this “health halo” effect leads to choices that look good on the shelf but aren’t truly good for the body.


## Why It’s Time to Question the Source


Before taking any advice about what to eat or buy, it’s important to ask a simple question: *Who is giving me this information?* As Nidhi points out, the credibility of the person matters as much as the content itself. Checking for professional qualifications, such as certified nutritionists or medical professionals can help filter out noise from genuine guidance.


## Informed Choices Begin With Awareness


Health isn’t about trends, it’s about trust. By staying aware and learning to question the “why” behind every claim, we can protect ourselves from misleading advice and make decisions rooted in knowledge, not hype.
