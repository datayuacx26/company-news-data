# Sails.js Launches Official Community Space on Discord

First the news: the[Sails.js](https://sailsjs.com/) core team and[Sailscasts](https://sailscasts.com/) have collaborated to bring forth an official Discord community for Sails.js 🎉


You can[join the Sails.js Discord](https://sailsjs.com/chat) now.


## The journey so far


When I began my journey with Sails.js years ago, my first goal was to connect with other amazing developers using the framework. At that time, I knew Discord as a hub where developer-focused communities gathered to help one another.


However, Sails.js had a Gitter community. So, in 2021, I created the Sailscasts Discord to fill that gap. Fast-forward four years, and that small community has now grown into the official Sails.js community.


## A revival of the Sails community


We strongly believe that for any open-source technology to thrive, it must have a healthy community. With the launch of the official Sails.js Discord community, we are taking a step toward revitalizing the Sails ecosystem, focusing on nurturing our community.


The Sails.js Discord space is perfect for:


- Connecting with Sails.js maintainers, influencers, contributors, and every member of the Sails community
- Facilitating discussions about Sails.js and its ecosystem, answering questions, and sharing projects
- Receiving the latest updates on Sails.js releases and events like the annual[Sailsconf conference](https://dub.sh/sailsconf-af)


We are eager to see the Sails.js community thrive and grow. If you have any ideas or feedback, please share them in the **#discord-feedback** channel on the server.


Long live Sails.js!
