# Introducing sailscasts.com 2.0

I wrote a couple of[words](https://blog.sailscasts.com/goodbye-nuxt-hello-inertia-in-sails/) on my experimenting to make building fullstack apps with Inertia and Sails.


I’m super pleased to announce that the[experiment](https://sailscasts.com/courses/getting-started-with-waterline/what-is-a-waterline-adapter) was a resounding success and[sailscasts.com](https://sailscasts.com/) curently run on this stack.


## The new stack


- Sails
- Vue 3
- Inertia.js
- Tailwind CSS


> This stack is powered by[inertia-sails](https://docs.sailscasts.com/inertia-sails/) - an Inertia server side adapter for Sails


## Hybrid payments


The stack isn’t the only thing that has changed with[sailscast.com](https://sailscasts.com/) .[Sailscasts.com](https://sailscasts.com/) now support what I like to call **hybrid payments** .


What this means is that you can now[buy](https://sailscasts.com/courses/getting-started-with-sails/lifting-sails) a course and own it forever along side having a[subscription](https://sailscasts.com/pricing) or not.


I really like this flexibility as I want anyone no matter the budget to have access to the courses and resources on[sailscasts.com](https://sailscasts.com/) .[Here](https://sailscasts.com/courses/getting-started-with-waterline/what-is-a-waterline-adapter) is a course you can buy today on getting started with Waterline.


## Course teasers


Another thing I’m excited about with the new[sailscasts.com](https://sailcasts.com/) is the ability to now watch some lessons in a course as teasers which sort of give you a feel of what to expect in a course before committing to either buying it forever or grabbing a Sailscasts Solo or Pro membership. Check out this[teaser](https://sailscasts.com/courses/getting-started-with-waterline/what-is-waterline)


## Here is to simplicity


I’m super pumped to deliver this new version of[sailscasts.com](https://sailscasts.com/) to you all and looking forward to creating more[courses](https://sailscasts.com/courses) as this stack now makes working on[sailscasts.com](https://sailscast.com/) much more simpler!


## Conclusion


If you are interested in building an SPA like[https://sailscasts.com](https://sailscasts.com/) powered by Inertia, you should check out[create-sails](https://docs.sailscasts.com/create-sails/) - the modern way to scaffold a fullstack Sails apps with your pages being powered by Vue, React or Svelte components right in your Sails app.
