# ZAPP’S® BRINGS BOLD FLAVOR TO NEW ORLEANS FOR CARNIVAL SEASON

HANOVER, Pa. –


[Zapp’s](https://www.utzsnacks.com/collections/zapps) ,


a bold, daringly different brand of chips and pretzels inspired by the flavors of New Orleans, is celebrating the Mardi Gras season with the launch of its new Big Cheezy Sinfully-Seasoned


® Pre


tzel Stix, adding extra flavor to the city’s already electric atmosphere.


Big Cheezy Pretzel Stix are coated with a blend of cheddar cheese flavor and Cajun seasoning for a flavorful, crunchy pretzel. The new flavor joins Zapp’s existing


[Sinfully-Seasoned Pretzel Stix lineup](https://www.utzsnacks.com/products/zapps-seasoned-pretzel-stixs-variety-pack?srsltid=AfmBOopAitdXj0DjJ52Zmw_x7Ps_8y3OyKdnP-U4gWsIDuYX7dNCdqku&variant=40670523457580) , which includes Voodoo


® , Jazzy Honey Mustard, Sweet Cinnamon & Sugar, and Spicy Cajun.


“


*Zapp's is rooted in the unique flavors and fun of New Orleans and* *is* *inspired by all the city has to offer* ,” said


Utz Quality Foods, LLC a subsidiary of Utz Brands, Inc.


Senior Vice President of Marketing Stacey Schultz. “ *Carnival and Mardi Gras is the city’s signature season and the perfect time to introduce the Big Cheezy[Pretzel](https://www.utzsnacks.com/collections/pretzels) Stix, a bold[snack](https://www.utzsnacks.com/collections/new-all) inspired by the spirit of New Orleans.*


*”*


Available in 1.5-ounce bags and 16-ounce bags, Big Cheezy Pretzel Stix are twisted to hold more of Zapp’s special, secret seasonings for a bigger, bolder flavor Zapp’s fans crave.


Leading up to Mardi Gras, Zapp’s gave fans who won a spot on its exclusive


Tastemaker Tours


the opportunity to join in on an unforgettable Mardi Gras experience in the heart of NOLA. The tours highlighted the city’s unique flavors and culture, including sampling experimental menu items at a local bakery, joining a Mardi Gras parade, enjoying a jazz tour at the New Orleans Jazz Museum, and visiting the historic St. Louis Cemetery. This hands-on experience allowed guests to immerse themselves in the true essence of New Orleans, and Chanen and Juwan Johnson and Kool-Aid McKinstry surprised and delighted a tour, creating an unforgettable experience.


Fans looking to experience some of the Tastemaker spots can follow


[@RealZappsChips](https://www.instagram.com/realzappschips/)


for details.
