# Utz Brands, Inc. to Showcase Variety of Innovative Products at 2025 NACS Show

**HANOVER, Pa. (BUSINESS WIRE)** —


[Utz Brands, Inc](https://www.utzsnacks.com/) ., a leading U.S. manufacturer of branded salty snacks for more than a century, will showcase its newest flavor launches and product innovations at the 2025 National Association of Convenience Stores (NACS) Show. The event will take place **October 15–17, 2025, at McCormick Place Convention Center in Chicago, IL** , bringing together the largest gathering of convenience and fuel retailing professionals for networking, education, and product discovery.


At Booth **#N657,**


**Utz®,** ****[Boulder](https://www.utzsnacks.com/collections/boulder-canyon) Canyon®, Zapp’s®, Golden Flake®, and TGI Fridays® **** will debut a range of crave-worthy new snacks designed to meet consumer demand for bold flavor and simple ingredients.


## **Taste the Excitement: Our new flavors are here!** ****


·


**Utz Cheese Balls:** Introducing **Cheddar Bacon Ranch Flavored Cheese Balls** , joining the powerhouse lineup that includes **Cheddar** and **Red Hot** flavors. Now expanding into convenience stores, these three bold varieties deliver craveable twists on a nostalgic favorite. Our **Cheddar Bacon Ranch Flavor** brings smoky, tangy richness; **Cheddar** delivers the classic cheesy flavored crunch consumers know and love; and **Red Hot** packs a fiery kick[snack](https://www.utzsnacks.com/collections/new-all) fans can’t resist.


·


**Utz Ripples – Sizzlin’ Summer Burger Flavored[Potato](https://www.utzsnacks.com/collections/potato-chips) Chips:** Inspired by backyard cookouts, this chip combines the flavors of savory grilled burger notes with a hint of ketchup, mustard, and smoky seasoning for the ultimate summer-inspired chip.


·


**Utz Kettle Chips: Salt & Malt Vinegar** and **Jalapeño Flavored Chips** are now available in convenience stores with a **2.5 oz pack** . Building on the success of its kettle platform, Utz expands distribution with two bold, on-trend flavors. **Salt & Malt Vinegar** pairs a tangy bite with a salty crunch, while **Jalapeño** delivers the perfect balance of heat and flavor, meeting growing demand for bold, premium kettle snacks in the convenience channel.


·


**Boulder Canyon:** The No. 1 potato chip brand in the natural channel\[1\] is now experiencing rapid growth across other retail channels, focusing on **Avocado Oil, Malt Vinegar & Sea Salt Flavored Kettle Chips & Avocado Oil Classic Sea Salt Kettle Chips.**


·


**Zapp’s Bayou Blackened Ranch Flavored Kettle Chips:** Bold, New Orleans-inspired flavor combines Cajun-style blackened seasoning with zesty ranch for a perfect fusion to deliver a taste inspiredby

·


**Golden Flake Hot & Spicy Cheddar Flavored Pork Rinds:** A craveable flavor combination of top category trends of Hot & Spicy and Cheese on a beloved southern favorite.


·


**[TGI Fridays](https://www.utzsnacks.com/collections/tgi-fridays) Bacon Ranch Flavored Potato Skins:** A craveable, on-trend flavor combination that brings restaurant-inspired snacking to convenience store shelves.


In addition to flavor innovation, Utz is spotlighting what has always set our chips apart —simple, quality ingredients. Beginning in Q1 2026, Utz flagship potato chip products - Original Potato Chips, Original Ripples, and Original Kettle Chips - will feature new front-of-pack claims: “No Artificial Colors,” “No Artificial Flavors,” and “Only 3 Simple Ingredients.” These credentials have always been true of our products, but we’re now bringing them forward to make it easier for consumers to see — and appreciate — the better-for-you qualities that define how we make our chips.


“ *This year at NACS, we’re showcasing the continued evolution of our portfolio to meet consumer trends and changing needs,* ” said Stacey Schultz, SVP, Marketing at Utz Quality Foods, LLC, a subsidiary of Utz Brands, Inc.


“ *We are excited to continue to bring new consumer desired products to the Convenience Channel in 2026 across our full portfolio of snacks.”*

**About Utz Brands, Inc.**


[Utz Brands, Inc.](https://www.utzsnacks.com/)


(NYSE: UTZ) manufactures a diverse portfolio of savory snacks through popular brands, including Utz®, On The Border® Chips & Dips, Zapp’s® and Boulder Canyon®, among others.


After a century with a strong family heritage, Utz continues to have a passion for exciting and delighting consumers with delicious snack foods made from top-quality ingredients. Utz’s products are distributed nationally through grocery, mass merchandisers, club, convenience, drug and other channels. Based in Hanover, Pennsylvania, Utz has multiple manufacturing facilities across the U.S. to serve its growing customer base.
