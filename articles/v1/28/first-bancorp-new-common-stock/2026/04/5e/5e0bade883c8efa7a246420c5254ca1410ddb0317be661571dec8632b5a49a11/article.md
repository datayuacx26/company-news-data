# First BanCorp Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend on Common Stock

### First BanCorp Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend on Common Stock


April 22, 2026


SAN JUAN, Puerto Rico--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


First BanCorp. (the “Corporation”) (NYSE: FBP), the bank holding company for FirstBank Puerto Rico, announced today that its Board of Directors has declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.20 per share on its outstanding common stock. The dividend is payable on June 12, 2026 to shareholders of record at the close of business on May 28, 2026.


**About First BanCorp.**


First BanCorp. is the parent corporation of FirstBank Puerto Rico, a state-chartered commercial bank with operations in Puerto Rico, the U.S. and British Virgin Islands and Florida, and of FirstBank Insurance Agency, LLC. First BanCorp’s shares of common stock trade on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol “FBP.”
