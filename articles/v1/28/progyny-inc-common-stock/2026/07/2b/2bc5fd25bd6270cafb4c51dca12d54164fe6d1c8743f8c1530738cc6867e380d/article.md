# Finding the right fertility treatment


No two fertility journeys look exactly alike. Factors like age, medical history, relationship status, and family-building goals all play a role in determining the right path forward—but with so many options available, knowing where to start can feel overwhelming.


In this episode, Dr. Janet Choi and Lissa Kline put real-life fertility scenarios on the table, exploring the stories of individuals and couples at different stages of their family-building journeys. From trying to conceive and egg freezing to IUI, IVF, genetic testing, fertility preservation, and more, they discuss how fertility specialists think through each situation and the treatment paths that may be recommended. Let’s get into it!
