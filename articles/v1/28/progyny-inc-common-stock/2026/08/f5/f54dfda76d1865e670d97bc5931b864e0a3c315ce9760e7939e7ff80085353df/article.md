# How Texas Capital responded to employee demand for comprehensive women’s health benefits

Listening to employees is central to how Texas Capital approaches its benefits strategy. When employees expressed a growing need for women’s health and family building support, the company partnered with Progyny to deliver a more personalized care experience. Today, employees have access to comprehensive support across the women’s health journey, including family building, pregnancy and postpartum, parent and child well-being, and menopause and midlife care.


> *“ **We’ve seen lower multiple rates, quicker time to pregnancy, and improved efficiencies across medications, driving significant cost avoidance.***
>
>
> ***The addition of these benefits across the full spectrum of women’s health was meaningful for us* .* ”*
