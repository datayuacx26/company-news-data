# Primech A & P’s enhanced suite of services now includes advanced air sensors systems as part of a new partnership with uHoo

uHoo’s innovative air monitoring systems are making their way to Primech A & P’s arsenal of facility services technology.


This collaboration will combine uHoo’s advanced air sensor systems with the company’s disinfection solutions, making it easier for customers to keep their facilities safe.


uHoo’s advanced systems include ways to keep track of up to 13 environmental factors, and keep pollutants like carbon monoxide and PM2.5 levels low.


The system will be a valuable asset in assessing the real-time risks of COVID-19, allowing users to take action and prevent the spread before it happens.
