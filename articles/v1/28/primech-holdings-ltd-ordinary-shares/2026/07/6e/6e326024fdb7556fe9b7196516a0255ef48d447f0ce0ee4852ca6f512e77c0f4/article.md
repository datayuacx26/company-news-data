# Vernon Kwek meeting with BATU, e2i and LHUB

With Vernon Kwek’s vision for all of our team members, Primech have signed an MOU with BATU, e2i and LHub to form a Company Training Committee (CTC). This programme allows workers to have better job prospects by being ready for new technology and business models.
