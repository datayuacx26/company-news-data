# Primech A & P partners with Charge+ to convert its diesel fleet to electric

Primech A & P and Charge+ are partnering to help Primech A & P reach its goal of 70 EVs by 2025.


Charge+ will support the move by implementing EV charging stations on the premises of Primech A & P, allowing the company’s drivers to enjoy the convenience of tapping on the nationwide public charging network of Charge+.


Primech A & P believes that tackling climate change is everybody’s responsibility. By collaborating with Charge+ and Oyika, the company hopes to bring forth positive outcomes, and serve as an inspiration to others to go green.
