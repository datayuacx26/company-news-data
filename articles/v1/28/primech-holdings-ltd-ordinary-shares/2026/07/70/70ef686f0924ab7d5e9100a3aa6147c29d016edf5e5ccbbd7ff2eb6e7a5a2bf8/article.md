# Primech A & P receives its TÜV SÜD certifications; ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and ISO 45001

Primech A & P is proud to have been certified by the internationally accredited certification body, TÜV SÜD.


Widely acknowledged as a mark of distinction, the IMS certifications show our dedication to excellence, sustainability and reliability.


Here are the certificates Primech A & P has been awarded with, and what they mean:


ISO 9001: Certifies that our services are consistent and efficient.


ISO 14001: Conveys our pro-environment stance. This indicates that Primech A & P is environmentally responsible, and uses eco-friendly products to perform our services.


ISO 45001: Shows that we’re serious about safety. By complying with international safety standards, we minimise risks and create safer working spaces for all.
