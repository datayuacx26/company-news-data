# The M.Y Big Sweep programme 2

With the success of last month’s engagement, our team decided to have the programme monthly to allow different team members to participate due to limited slots available per session. The previous session consists mainly of team members from HQ office whilst this month includes mostly team members from operations.


A total of 4 household benefited from this session and we hope that many more households can stand to benefit from this programme in the future.
