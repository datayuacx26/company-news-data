## Powell Industries Announces Date and Conference Call for Fiscal 2026 First Quarter Results


### Jan 20, 2026


HOUSTON, Jan. 20, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Powell Industries, Inc. (NASDAQ: POWL), a leading supplier of custom engineered solutions for the management, control and distribution of electrical energy, today announced that it will release results for the fiscal first quarter ended December 31, 2025 on Tuesday, February 3, 2026 after the market closes.
