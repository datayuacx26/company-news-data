Hello fellow Buffers,


Today is one of the releases I’m most excited about! We’ve overhauled the Max mode into a state of the art agent that does more thinking at every step.


Simply run codebuff --max, and prompt away:


We’ve spiced up this mode to use a really interesting combination of models:


-


Gemini 2.5 Pro for its planning abilities


-


Claude Sonnet 3.6 for its reliable tool calling


The resulting hybrid thus can craft brilliant plans


*and* execute on them. It seems to work really well in early tests.

Give it a shot, and see how you like it! It is a bit more expensive, however, it’s much cheaper than using o3 would be, for what appears to be much better results.
