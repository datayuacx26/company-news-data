# Shimmer Launches 2.0: AI Tools to Amplify Human-Centered ADHD Coaching

💡


**Tl;dr:** Shimmer is an ADHD coaching platform designed to help neurodivergent adults reach their full potential. We’re excited to introduce Shimmer 2.0, leveraging AI tools to make your coaching experience even more effective, simpler, and action-oriented than before. But rest assured—this isn’t about a chat or voice bot running your sessions. Instead, it amplifies the human connection between you and your coach, ensuring every breakthrough is captured and every insight drives real change.


We launched on Product Hunt today! Check out our launch, and support us by upvoting by 11:59pm PT today (Thursday Jan 23).


## Background: Shimmer’s ADHD Coaching System


Shimmer is the leading ADHD Coaching Platform,[born from](https://www.shimmer.care/blog/shimmer-adhd-coaching-launch) our co-founder & CEO, Christal’s ADHD diagnosis in 2022. Since then, we’ve refined our experience and methodology alongside experts at NYU and Mount Sinai and delivered over 45,000 sessions.


At Shimmer, we’ve created a coaching cycle that supports you every step of the way, blending personalized guidance, real-time feedback, and tangible progress tracking. Here’s how it works:


- **Weekly 30-Minute Coaching Sessions:** Meet one-on-one with an ADHD-specialized coach who helps you define a clear North Star - your personal vision statement - and break it down into weekly goals.
- **In-App Tracking & Reflection:** Log daily actions, note what went well (and what didn’t), and reflect on lessons learned—all in one place.
- **Real-Time Coach Support:** Stay connected with your coach between sessions through chat, getting instant accountability and motivation when you need it.
- **Grow & Evolve:** Each week builds on the last—together, you’ll refine strategies, celebrate wins, and form lasting habits that move you closer to your ultimate[goals](https://www.shimmer.care/blog/setting-goals-that-work-with-your-adhd-brain) .


## **Why Shimmer 2.0?**


### Lessons from 45,000+ Coaching Sessions


At Shimmer, we’ve run over **45,000 coaching sessions** with ADHDers. Along the way, we discovered three big challenges they face in the coaching journey:


1. **Remembering what was discussed in each session** —the “aha” moments often slip through the cracks.
2. **Recalling and spotting themes across multiple sessions** —it’s tough to see the bigger picture when everything feels fragmented.
3. **Making sense of day-to-day experiences between sessions** —a coach can’t be with you 24/7, so staying on track can be difficult.


### Our Solution: A “Second Brain” That Bridges Every Gap


That’s where **Shimmer 2.0** comes in. We’ve built an **AI-powered system** to *capture, organize, and make sense of* all of your coaching moments—both in-session and throughout the week. Think of it as your “second brain,” offering:


- **Instant recall** of session insights when you need them most.
- **Pattern detection** so you can see recurring themes and long-term progress.
- **Daily support** that helps turn your coach’s guidance into consistent action, even when they’re not physically by your side.


By integrating AI at every step of your coaching journey, **Shimmer 2.0** shortens the path from *insight* to *action* —making it easier to stay accountable, keep building on your wins, and finally connect the dots between each breakthrough.


## **Our Philosophy: Safe, Human-Centered AI**


### Safety First & Ethical by Design


At Shimmer, we’re laser-focused on making sure AI serves you safely, ethically, and securely. We understand the sensitive nature of ADHD coaching sessions. That’s why **all** session transcripts are de-identified and stored securely, and we prioritize user consent above all. The insights generated by AI remain yours to **export, revisit, or delete** at any time. To ensure fairness and inclusivity, we actively work to **identify and eliminate biases** in our AI systems, with ongoing evaluations and updates.


### AI **Enhances** , Not Replaces


There’s a reason our amazing coaches are at the heart of the Shimmer experience:[**ADHD coaching](https://www.shimmer.care/blog/ultimate-guide-to-adhd-coaching) is deeply personal**, rooted in empathy, understanding, and a trusting coach-client relationship. AI helps by **automating** note-taking, spotting trends, and offering reflection prompts. But it doesn’t replace the **human compassion** and **expertise** that is critical for effective ADHD support.


We designed Shimmer 2.0 to work alongside expert coaches because compassion can’t be automated. The AI is just there to capture the magic of each session and support you in-between, acting like a second brain.”— Chris Wang, Co-founder & CEO of Shimmer


### Built with User Feedback at its Core


Our AI isn’t static—it evolves with you. We regularly incorporate feedback from members and coaches to improve its accuracy, relevance, and helpfulness. Your experiences shape how we innovate, ensuring that our tools stay aligned with your needs.


## **What’s New in Shimmer 2.0**


### AI-Powered Session Summaries (powered by[Daily](https://www.daily.co/) and[Deepgram](https://deepgram.com/) )


Recalling key takeaways from a coaching session can be a struggle, especially when you’re juggling multiple tasks or jumping straight into your next meeting. Shimmer 2.0’s AI automatically recaps each session’s main points and action items, so you can easily revisit breakthroughs without piecing together scattered notes.


### Action Insights & Progress Tracking


Even the best intentions can fall off your radar when life gets busy, leaving you scrambling right before a session. Our new Action Insights & Progress Tracking visually map out your weekly actions, highlight patterns, and keep you accountable—ensuring you know exactly what’s working and where to focus next.


### AI History & Smart Search


Digging through old notes or relying on memory alone often leads to missed details and repeated mistakes. With AI History & Smart Search, you can instantly look up specific topics from past sessions and spot recurring themes at a glance, making every lesson and strategy easier to recall and apply.


## **The Value: How Shimmer 2.0 Improves Your Coaching Journey**


Shimmer 2.0 weaves **AI** into your everyday coaching experience, turning scattered notes and fleeting insights into a well-structured path toward your goals. By eliminating the[gaps in memory](https://www.shimmer.care/adhd-101/working-memory) and ensuring consistent follow-through, it helps you and your coach focus on what really matters: **breakthroughs** and **lasting change** . Shimmer 2.0 helps you:


1. **Stay in the Moment ‍** By automating note-taking, you’re free to fully engage in each coaching conversation. No more frantic scribbling when you should be absorbing insights.
2. **Recall Made Easy ‍** ADHD coaching isn’t a one-time fix; it’s an evolving process across multiple sessions. Shimmer 2.0 keeps an organized record throughout your coaching journey, ensuring no valuable “aha” moment gets lost in the shuffle.
3. **More Frequent, Deeper A-ha Moments ‍** With AI Smart Search, you’ll quickly see how and why you overcame past hurdles. This creates a shortcut to replicating what worked and troubleshooting what didn’t.
4. **Measure Progress & Celebrate Wins ‍** Real growth becomes undeniable when you have tangible evidence. Whether it’s noticing fewer “self-doubt” moments or celebrating consistent wins, these metrics fuel[motivation](https://www.shimmer.care/adhd-101/motivation) and self-confidence.
5. **Elevated Coach Support ‍** Coaches can easily spot themes, patterns, and recurring challenges, which means more targeted support for you. When you both arrive at sessions on the same page, you can tackle the most pressing issues head-on.
