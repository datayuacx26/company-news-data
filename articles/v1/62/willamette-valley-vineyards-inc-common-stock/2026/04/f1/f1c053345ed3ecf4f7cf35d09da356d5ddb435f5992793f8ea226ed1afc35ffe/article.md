# Walla Walla Wines and Music Pairings

Wines from the Oregon side of the Walla Walla Valley are unmistakable – defined by volcanic soils, dramatic texture and a rich sense of history. Our Maison Bleue, Pambrun and Métis wines from this region show depth and intensity, making them the perfect pairing for soulful storytelling music.


A mix of R&B and Folk genres captures this pairing beautifully. The steady rhythm and emotional warmth echo the richness and structure of the wines, while narrative-driven lyrics reflect the rugged landscape and authenticity of the Walla Walla Valley.


This pairing is grounded and timeless. It’s best enjoyed during slower evenings, around a shared table or in moments when you want the wine and the music to speak with intention. Enjoy our curated Walla Walla Wines playlist on Spotify with a glass of Syrah or Merlot at home.
