# David Weinstock

David Weinstock was named Chief Financial Officer in March of 2023 after serving as interim Chief Financial Officer since June 28, 2022, and as Vice President and Chief Accounting Officer since 2009.


Mr. Weinstock has over 25 years of experience in the areas of finance, accounting, and controls. Prior to joining Essent, he held senior management positions at Advanta Corp., including chief accounting officer and vice president of investor relations. Prior to Advanta, Mr. Weinstock was a senior manager at Arthur Andersen LLP.


Mr. Weinstock holds a BS in accounting from The Pennsylvania State University and is a certified public accountant.
