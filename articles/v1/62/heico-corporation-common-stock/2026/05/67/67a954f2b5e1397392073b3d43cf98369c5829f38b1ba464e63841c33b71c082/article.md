# HEICO CORPORATION REPORTS RECORD NET INCOME (UP 49%) ON RECORD OPERATING INCOME (UP 41%) AND RECORD NET SALES (Up 25%) FOR THE SECOND QUARTER OF FISCAL 2026

******Consolidated Quarterly Organic Net Sales Growth exceeds 18%******


HOLLYWOOD, FL AND MIAMI, FL – **** HEICO Corporation (NYSE:HEI.A)(NYSE:HEI) today reported an increase in net income of 49% to a record $233.8 million, or $1.66 per diluted share, in the second quarter of fiscal 2026, up from $156.8 million, or $1.12 per diluted share, in the second quarter of fiscal 2025.
