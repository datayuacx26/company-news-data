# Soon | Soon Sync, Early Access - Automate Anywhere

Today we are announcing[Soon Sync](https://www.soon.app/soon-sync) , an upcoming product that will allow you to get the best of Soon’s automated services in the trading platform of your choice. Soon Sync is not yet available, but you can sign up for early access now.


‍


This product is made possible due to our[partnership with Front Finance](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/revolutionizing-crypto-investing-introducing-soon-sync-powered/) . Front makes it easy to integrate with over 100 of the world's top trading exchanges.


‍


With Soon Sync you’ll get automated portfolios, automated buying, automated selling and automated taxes, in addition to configuration options. You’ll get all the benefits of your favorite trading platform, paired with Soon’s automation. You will even be able to use Soon in multiple trading platforms at the same time.


‍


Soon’s current offering, allowing you to custody directly with us, will still be available. You can use Soon Sync separately, or use it in tandem with a Soon custodial account.


‍


About Front Finance: Founded in 2020, Front's mission is to build an open, connected and secure financial ecosystem. Front is a modern financial operating system that provides enterprise clients with the ability to enable digital asset transfers, crypto payments, account aggregation and registered securities trading, all within their platform. With integrations to over 300+ platforms like Robinhood, Coinbase and MetaMask, Front is creating an embedded financial ecosystem that is more open, connected and secure for businesses and users alike.
