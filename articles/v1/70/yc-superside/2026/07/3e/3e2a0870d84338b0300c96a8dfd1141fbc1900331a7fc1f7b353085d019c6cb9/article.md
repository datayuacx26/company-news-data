### THE CUSTOMER


## **Fortune 500 software company *scaling internal AI***


A Fortune 500 software company recognized the transformative potential of AI tools but **struggled to scale adoption across its over 15,000 global employees** , as limited time for training and concerns about real day-to-day usefulness slowed momentum.


Partnering with Superside, this organization **launched a comprehensive AI enablement initiative** to achieve their AI adoption targets and build confidence in their teams to begin using AI in daily workflows.


### **THE CHALLENGE**


Leadership at a Fortune 500 company faced a familiar but formidable challenge: **accelerating AI adoption across a massive, globally distributed workforce** . With over 15,000 employees spanning countless roles and responsibilities, building a unified approach to AI was no small feat.


While the organization had started experimenting with AI, usage was inconsistent:


- A small group of early adopters was actively testing tools, but **their approaches were not standardized or widely understood** .
- **Roughly 40% of employees said they simply did not have the time to explore AI** , and many others were uncertain about its reliability or the real value it could deliver.
- **The lack of clear expectations and role-specific guidance** made it difficult for employees to feel confident at using AI in their day-to-day work.


Driving meaningful transformation across such a large workforce demanded more than just access to tools—it called for a deliberate enablement that addressed cultural barriers and built employee trust. The goal was clear: **achieve a 20% increase in AI adoption across the company** within a few months of the program’s launch.


To reach that target, the company partnered with[Superside’s AI Consulting team](https://www.superside.com/ai-consulting) to design a scalable enablement program — one to spark excitement for AI and one to meet the needs of every role and department.


### **THE SOLUTION**


**Superside designed a company-wide AI transformation initiative** that combined strategic planning, learning experience, and world-class creative production. Rather than relying on traditional training modules, the program was built to capture the attention and inspire curiosity of the over 15,000 employees, making AI adoption more actionable.


What set this initiative apart was **Superside’s ability to own both strategy and execution** . The team brought the engagement plan to life through short-form video campaigns, custom training materials, and the launch of a multi-platform Digital Academy. This end-to-end approach **ensured consistent messaging and high engagement at scale** .


The program was rolled out in three strategic phases, each designed to build momentum around AI usage:


### **Phase 1: *Immersive Storytelling***


To spark excitement for the training and get employees’ buy-in, Superside led with narrative. We developed a creative campaign designed to address common pain points, debunk myths and change mindsets towards AI adoption. This included:


- **Tailoring around key employee personas** , creating characters and narratives to make learning relatable while reducing anxiety.
- **Delivering storytelling materials across multiple platforms** to create a consistent and engaging narrative around AI, leveraging universal elements such as conflict, curiosity and resolution to resonate with all borders and cultures.
- **Producing 5+ live-action, short-form videos** showcasing real-world AI use cases and success stories.


This narrative-driven approach ensured that employees felt empowered to experiment and take ownership of their AI journey.


### **Phase 2: *Digital Academy Foundations***


Superside created the blueprint for a scalable learning ecosystem that could reach over 15,000 employees:


- **Designing five persona-based learning paths** tailored to specific roles, workflows, seniority, and AI readiness levels, ensuring practical value for every department.
- **Built the initial Digital Academy curriculum** , complete with engaging content, tutorials, and resources.


Once the foundation was established, Superside expanded the Academy to drive deeper engagement:


- **Introduced generalistic AI training** to standardize AI best practices company-wide.
- **Rolled out additional content and advanced tool-based use cases** to help employees discover more ways AI could supercharge their work.
- **Supported this rollout with a 20-week project management office** to ensure testing, validate outcomes, and manage alignment between relevant stakeholders for cross-functional teams.


## **Building a *Culture of AI* Into the DNA of the Fortune 500**


What started as a bold goal to increase AI adoption by 20% became a company-wide movement. Within months, **this Fortune 500 enterprise doubled its initial target, with nearly 80% of employees actively using AI to accelerate workflows** across functions—from development and support to HR and marketing.
