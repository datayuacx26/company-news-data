## **What is Design Pickle?**


[Design Pickle](https://designpickle.com/) is a subscription-based creative platform built around reserved daily creative hours. The model is simple: submit your unlimited design requests and revisions for a predictable monthly fee.


Work is managed through a queue, with each request assigned to the designer or creative specialist best suited to the task, **whether that's graphic design, illustration, presentation design, motion graphics or video editing.**


The company's pricing combines a platform subscription with reserved creative hours, starting at[two hours per business day](https://help.designpickle.com/en/articles/3809732-which-plan-should-i-subscribe-to) . You can increase those hours as your workload grows, and higher-capacity plans include additional support such as production coordinators and art directors. Because the hours are reserved for your team each day,[unused capacity doesn't roll over](https://designpickle.com/free-design-hours) .


The service is aimed primarily at small marketing teams, agencies and solo operators who need a consistent stream of creative production but don't want to hire multiple designers. While the queue-based workflow is efficient for ongoing production, it becomes less effective when you need multiple large campaigns, highly strategic creative work or several complex projects to run simultaneously.


You can purchase more hours to increase output, but Design Pickle is still designed to manage work through a prioritized queue.
