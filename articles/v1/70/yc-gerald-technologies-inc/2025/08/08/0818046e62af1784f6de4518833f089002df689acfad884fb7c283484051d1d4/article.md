## What Are Foreign Transaction Fees and Why Do They Add Up?


A foreign transaction fee is a surcharge that your card issuer adds to any transaction processed in a foreign currency or that passes through a foreign bank. This fee typically ranges from 1% to 3% of the total purchase amount. While that might not sound like much on a single purchase, it can accumulate significantly over an entire vacation. Imagine spending $2,000 on hotels, food, and souvenirs. A 3% fee would add an extra $60 to your bill for absolutely nothing in return. These fees are often charged by major card networks and the issuing banks themselves. For more detailed information, the[Consumer Financial Protection Bureau](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-foreign-transaction-fee-en-1017/) offers a clear explanation. The key takeaway is that without a plan, you could be paying a premium on every swipe, which is why understanding how to get a cash advance or use alternative payment methods is crucial.
