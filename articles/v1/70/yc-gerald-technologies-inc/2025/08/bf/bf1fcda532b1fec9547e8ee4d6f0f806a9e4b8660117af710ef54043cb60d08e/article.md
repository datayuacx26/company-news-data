## What is the Better Business Bureau?


The Better Business Bureau, or BBB, is a private, non-profit organization that has been operating for over a century. Its primary mission is to foster a trustworthy marketplace by setting standards for ethical business behavior and monitoring compliance. The BBB helps consumers identify trustworthy businesses and provides a platform for resolving disputes. When you look up a company on the[BBB website](https://www.bbb.org/) , you can find its rating (from A+ to F), customer reviews, and any complaints filed against it. This information can be invaluable, especially when you need a financial product like a payday advance and want to avoid predatory lenders.
