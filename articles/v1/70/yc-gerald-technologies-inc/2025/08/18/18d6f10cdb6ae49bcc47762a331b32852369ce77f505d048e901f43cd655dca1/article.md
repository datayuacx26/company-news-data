## What is the United States International Trade Commission?


The United States International Trade Commission (USITC) is an independent federal agency that plays a crucial role in the nation's trade policy. You can find detailed information on their official website,[https://www.usitc.gov/](https://www.usitc.gov/) . In simple terms, the USITC investigates the impact of imports on U.S. industries and determines whether unfair trade practices are harming domestic businesses. Their findings can lead to tariffs or other protective measures. These decisions, while designed to protect local industries, can have widespread consequences for consumers. For instance, if a tariff is imposed on imported goods, the cost of those items can rise, making it more challenging to manage your household budget.
