# Ferguson Joins the S&P 500®

[View All News](https://www.corporate.ferguson.com/pressroom/news-releases/default.aspx)


### Ferguson Joins the S&P 500®


August 3, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s201.q4cdn.com/465729859/files/doc_news/Ferguson-Joins-the-SP-500-2026.pdf)


NEWPORT NEWS, Va.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[Ferguson Enterprises Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fcorporate.ferguson.com%2Fhome%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54581967&newsitemid=20260803472982&lan=en-US&anchor=Ferguson+Enterprises+Inc.&index=1&md5=df0a73f7ec72166cb3c71186ba0cd405) (NYSE: FERG), North America’s largest value-added distributor of essential water and air solutions in our $340B residential and non-residential construction markets, has been selected for inclusion in the S&P 500® index effective at the open of trading on August 5, 2026.


“Joining the S&P 500 is a significant milestone made possible by our 35,000 expert associates and their commitment to the specialized professionals who build and maintain North America,” said Kevin Murphy, Ferguson CEO. “This recognition is a testament to the dedication of our associates, the strength of our business model and the leading positions we hold in the market.”


Over the last five years, the company has transitioned from a London listed, FTSE 100 company to a solely NYSE listed, fully North American headquartered company. The addition to the S&P 500 marks the final step in this journey.


The S&P 500 is widely recognized as one of the premier benchmarks of the U.S. equities market. The index includes 500 leading companies across various important industries of the United States. Companies included in the index are selected using criteria that include market capitalization, liquidity and financial viability.


**About Ferguson**


Ferguson (NYSE: FERG) is North America’s largest value-added distributor of essential water and air solutions, serving specialized professionals in our $340B residential and non-residential construction markets. We help make our customers’ complex projects simple, successful and sustainable by providing expertise and a wide range of products and services from plumbing, HVAC, appliances, and lighting to PVF, water and wastewater solutions, and more. Headquartered in Newport News, Va., Ferguson has sales of $31.3 billion (CY’25) and approximately 35,000 associates in over 1,700 locations.
