# Alinea Invest — What is Alinea Invest?

## **IN A NUTSHELL**


- Alinea Invest is a mobile investing app built around a simple idea: building wealth should be stupidly easy, not a second job.
- Instead of picking stocks yourself, you invest in expert-built portfolios and themed “Playlists,” while an AI assistant called Allie explains what's happening with your money in plain language.
- You decide how hands-on or hands-off to be — automate your investing, learn as you go, and get the right information exactly when you need it, without having to become a finance expert first.


Alinea believes everybody deserves the freedom that comes from having their money work for them, without needing a finance degree, hours of research, or the anxiety that usually comes with investing. So we built an app that does the hard parts in the background: recommending diversified, expert-built investments, automating your contributions, and teaching you what matters in the moments it matters, all wrapped in an experience that actually feels good to use.

## **Our Mission and Vision**


### **Mission**


To make building wealth stupidly easy.


### **Vision**


To give people the freedom to spend their time how they actually want.


Investing is one of the most reliable ways to build long-term wealth, but most people avoid it because it feels complicated, risky, or built for someone with more money and more time. Alinea exists to remove that friction, so that not investing doesn't even feel like a choice.


## **What Does Alinea Do?**


Alinea combines expert-built portfolios, AI guidance, and automation into one app, so you can start investing in minutes and keep building wealth without constantly managing it.


### **Expert Portfolios**


When you sign up, Alinea asks about your goals and risk tolerance, then recommends a portfolio built by investment professionals. You're never starting from a blank slate or guessing which stocks to buy.


### **Investing Playlists**


Playlists are curated collections of stocks and ETFs organized around themes, interests, or values — so you can put your money behind what you actually care about. You can follow existing Playlists or build your own, and share strategies with the Alinea community. With Playlists Pro, you can also invest in managed portfolios modeled on the moves of politicians, hedge funds, world events, and AI, and they rebalance automatically.


### **AI Allie**


Allie is Alinea's built-in AI assistant. She translates portfolio performance and market activity into plain-English insights, so you can understand what's happening with your money without decoding financial jargon on your own.


### **Automated, Flexible Investing**


You can set up recurring contributions on your schedule, make one-time deposits whenever you want, and adjust how involved you'd like to be at any time. Alinea keeps your money moving forward whether you check the app every day or once a month.


### **Daily Briefing**


Every day, Alinea delivers a short, personalized summary of the financial news that actually matters to your investments — so you stay informed without having to go looking for it.


### **Potential Tracker**


The Potential Tracker shows you how your investments could grow over time, helping you visualize the long-term payoff of the habits you're building today.

## **Safety and Security**


Alinea Invest, LLC is an SEC-registered investment adviser. Investing accounts are held through DriveWealth, a member of FINRA and SIPC, so securities in your account are protected up to $500,000 (including a $250,000 limit for cash) if the broker fails though, as with any brokerage, that protection doesn't cover normal market losses. Bank connections use Plaid, and the app is protected with bank-level, 256-bit encryption. You must be 18 or older to open an account.
