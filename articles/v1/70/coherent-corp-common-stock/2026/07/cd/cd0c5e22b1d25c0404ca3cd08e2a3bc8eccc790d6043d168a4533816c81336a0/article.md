# COHERENT CORP. ANNOUNCES TIMING OF FY2026 SECOND QUARTER EARNINGS RELEASE

**SAXONBURG, Pa., Jan. 21, 2026** – Coherent Corp. (NYSE: COHR) (“Coherent,” “We,” or the “Company”), a global leader in photonics, announced today that it will release its financial results for the quarter ended December 31, 2025, on Wednesday, February 4, after the New York Stock Exchange closes. The release will be followed by a live audio webcast at 4:30 p.m. ET to discuss the results
