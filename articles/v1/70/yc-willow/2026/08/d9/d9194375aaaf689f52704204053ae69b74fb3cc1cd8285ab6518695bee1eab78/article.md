## What Is Wispr Flow?


Wispr Flow is a voice dictation tool built primarily for Mac users who want a fast, AI-assisted way to get words on screen without switching apps. It works system-wide, activating via a hold-to-talk mechanism, and uses AI to clean up speech into polished prose before the text lands in whatever field you're typing in. For a deeper look at how these two tools compare, see our[Wispr Flow vs Willow](https://willowvoice.com/blog/wispr-flow-vs-willow-voice-dictation) breakdown.


### Where the Architecture Shows Its Limits


Wispr Flow's design reflects a general-purpose use case. Its ~700ms processing window handles capture, transcription, interpretation, and formatting in a single pass, so text arrives later than a real-time engine. CS teams move constantly across CRM fields, support tickets, handoff notes, and email threads, and a tool optimized for polished prose in single-context writing behaves differently under that kind of multi-app load.
