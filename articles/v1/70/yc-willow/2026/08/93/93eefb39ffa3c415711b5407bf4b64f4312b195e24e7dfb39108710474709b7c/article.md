## AI Email Clients, Add-Ons, and AI Agents: Three Different Categories


Before picking the right tool, it helps to understand what you're actually choosing between. The phrase "AI email assistant" gets applied to three meaningfully different categories of software, and conflating them leads to frustration when a tool doesn't do what you expected.


### AI Email Clients


These are full inbox replacements built around AI features from the ground up. Think[voice dictation in Superhuman](https://willowvoice.com/use-cases/superhuman) or Shortwave. They handle reading, triaging, and replying inside a purpose-built interface. The tradeoff: you leave Gmail or Outlook entirely.


### Add-Ons and Extensions


Browser extensions and sidebar integrations sit on top of your existing inbox. They add AI-powered drafting, tone adjustment, or reply suggestions without requiring you to change clients. Most "AI email assistant Gmail" and "AI email assistant Outlook" searches lead here.


### AI Agents


A newer category. These tools connect to your inbox via API and take autonomous actions: categorizing threads, drafting replies for your approval, or routing messages based on rules you set. They operate more like background workers than assistants you invoke manually.


Most people searching for an AI email assistant want the second category: something that works inside the inbox they already use.
