## What Are AI Writing Assistants?


AI writing assistants help users produce written content faster by generating, editing, or refining text based on voice or typed input. By mid-2026, knowledge workers, developers, marketers, and clinicians use them daily. Some[AI dictation tools](https://willowvoice.com/blog/best-ai-powered-dictation-tools) focus on raw transcription speed; others add generative capabilities or learn your vocabulary over time so the correction loop shrinks with each session. Professional and enterprise teams increasingly factor cross-platform coverage, team deployment, and compliance into the decision as well.

## Grammarly


Grammarly started as a grammar correction tool and has grown to cover tone suggestions, clarity rewrites, and generative features. Teams often compare it against the best[speech recognition software](https://willowvoice.com/blog/best-speech-recognition-software) tools available. Its[full feature set](https://www.grammarly.com/features) now spans over 1 million apps and websites. GrammarlyGO adds generative capabilities for drafting emails, summarizing text, and rewriting passages from a prompt.


### Where It Works Well


-


For teams that need a shared writing standard, Grammarly's tone and clarity suggestions create consistency across contributors without requiring a style guide review every time someone sends a client email.


-


The browser extension covers most major writing surfaces, including Gmail, Google Docs, LinkedIn, and Slack, so there is minimal friction getting it into an existing workflow.


-


The business tier adds style guides, snippets, and admin controls, which makes org-wide rollout straightforward for teams already working in the browser.


### Where It Falls Short


-


Built around text editing, not voice input. It does not close the gap between thinking speed and typing speed.


-


Improves text you have already written instead of helping you produce a first draft faster.


-


For high-volume writers, that speed gap is the more pressing constraint Grammarly does not handle.

## Notion AI


Notion AI sits inside the workspace many knowledge workers already use for docs, wikis, and project notes. Select a block and ask it to summarize, expand, or rewrite directly inline. The tradeoff is scope: it performs well for meeting notes and project briefs, but writing that spans Gmail, Slack, Google Docs, and a CMS at the same time largely falls outside what it can reach.


### Where It Works Well


-


Inline editing within Notion blocks is fast and frictionless, letting you clean up rough notes or generate action items without leaving the page you are already on.


-


The AI can autofill database properties, generate project summaries from linked pages, and create structured templates from a plain-language prompt.


-


Teams already on Notion's paid plans get AI access bundled in at an add-on rate, so there is no separate tool to source or onboard.


### Where It Falls Short


-


Voice input is not a native capability. Notion AI works through typed prompts, so users who want to capture notes by voice or capture ideas hands-free need a separate[voice dictation software for Mac](https://willowvoice.com/blog/best-voice-dictation-software-mac) or Windows running on top.


-


Its writing assistance is optimized for Notion's block structure, which can produce output that requires reformatting when moved into other tools.


-


There is no personalization layer that learns your writing style or domain vocabulary over time.


## ChatGPT


ChatGPT is primarily a conversational AI, not a dedicated writing assistant, but millions of writers use it daily to brainstorm, outline, draft, and revise across every format. Its strength is versatility: you can shift from a blog post to a cold email to a headline in the same window, without switching apps or losing context.


### Where It Works Well


-


Brainstorming and ideation sessions where you want to generate a lot of raw material quickly and then filter it down yourself.


-


Long-form drafts where rough structure matters more than polished prose on the first pass.


-


Rewriting and editing passes where you paste in existing text and ask for a specific kind of revision, sharper opening, tighter argument, different tone.


-


Format switching, moving the same core content into an email, a LinkedIn post, or a bullet summary without starting from scratch.


### Where It Falls Short


-


ChatGPT has no persistent memory of your writing style across sessions by default, so it does not adapt to how you write over time the way a personalization-first tool does. Writers who need real-time input capture are better served by dedicated[voice to text software](https://willowvoice.com/blog/best-voice-to-text-software) tools.


-


Output quality varies considerably by how well the prompt is constructed, which means less experienced users often get generic results.


-


Context window limits vary by model and plan; many current[ChatGPT models](https://platform.openai.com/docs/models) support up to 128K tokens, which handles most writing tasks but can become a constraint in long research-heavy projects.


-


It is a browser or app experience, not a system-wide writing layer, so it does not follow you into Gmail, Notion, or Google Docs without a separate integration or copy-paste step.

## FAQs


### Is Willow Voice better than Grammarly for professionals who need to write faster?


Grammarly improves text you have already typed. Willow Voice removes the typing step by letting you speak at 150 WPM versus typing at roughly 40 WPM. If your bottleneck is draft speed, Grammarly does not close that gap.


### When should I choose Jasper AI or Copy.ai over a general-purpose tool like ChatGPT?


Choose Jasper or Copy.ai for high-volume, template-driven content with defined brand standards: ad copy, email sequences, product descriptions. ChatGPT works better when your writing spans multiple formats and you need flexibility over a structured production system.


### Can Notion AI replace a dedicated voice dictation tool like Willow Voice for knowledge workers?


Notion AI has no voice input, so it does not close the gap between speaking and typing speed. It works well within Notion's block structure, but professionals working across Gmail, Slack, Cursor, and other apps need a system-wide dictation layer that Notion AI cannot provide.
