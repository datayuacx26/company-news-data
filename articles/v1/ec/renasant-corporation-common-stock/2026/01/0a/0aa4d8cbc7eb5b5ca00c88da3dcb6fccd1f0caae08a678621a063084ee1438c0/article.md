# Renasant Announces 2025 Fourth Quarter Webcast and Conference Call Information

TUPELO, Miss., Jan. 09, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Renasant Corporation (NYSE: RNST) (the “Company”) will announce 2025 fourth quarter results following the NYSE's closing on Tuesday, January 27, 2026. The Company will hold executive management's quarterly webcast and conference call with analysts on Wednesday, January 28, 2026, at 10:00 AM Eastern Time (9:00 AM Central Time).


The webcast is accessible through Renasant's investor relations website at[www.renasant.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=dLJwTAmgMmweX_7FWlx2mOPESBTbCfT6tC8gisSvzDZDqv2Poy6b_ZX9yclHHNeVUEaLt7wkT3CsLNxxv_eEJjURgF8SL0J99GroHmLUau4=) or[https://event.choruscall.com/mediaframe/webcast.html?webcastid=YsDRiXm1](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=BYSWMUYteB6CBVz8bHL9-65Zn2WXDdt7RpDubpkhD07p5icBV8VCqvEu1jrOwgFJMR4QmcJnMATYuSZ80v1Y_osoQz0J7WjIoWiZmcjuisFa7rJEGfdjDowDSdsija5PfJCNnK9tVl4I3KS7-f8g5iBVad_yfoJ_GdlUmaq1wYlBogaJYdkinJKx6bCv0Ecc-d7_jP9RSIRpbhUOJBH2lKh7yr4a6Ff5xXyzjziIC-U=) . The webcast will be archived on[www.renasant.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=dLJwTAmgMmweX_7FWlx2mNi0bbxem8mGcLWTwmKhyLimSWIQYYMAMPv5SBSs--yQFX2wyi4vONguSmbMP-W526QoFzEkTGUzJ8S8fdGnLDU=) and will remain accessible for one year. Telephone replay access is available until February 11, 2026.
