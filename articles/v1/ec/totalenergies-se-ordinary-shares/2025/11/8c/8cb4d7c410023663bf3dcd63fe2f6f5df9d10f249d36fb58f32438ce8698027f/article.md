# Nigeria: TotalEnergies Completes the Divestment of its Non-Operated Interest in the Bonga Field

**Paris, November 25, 2025** – TotalEnergies announces that its subsidiary TotalEnergies EP Nigeria (TEPNG) has completed the[divestment of its 12.5% non-operated interest in the OML118](https://totalenergies.com/news/press-releases/nigeria-totalenergies-divests-its-non-operated-interest-bonga-field) Production Sharing Contract (PSC) to Shell Nigeria Exploration and Production Company Ltd (10%) and Nigerian Agip Exploration (2.5%) for an aggregated amount of USD 510 million.
