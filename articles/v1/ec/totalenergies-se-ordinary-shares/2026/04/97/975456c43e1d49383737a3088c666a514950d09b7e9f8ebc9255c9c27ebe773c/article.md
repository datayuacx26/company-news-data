# Clarifications on TotalEnergies’ taxation

Although TotalEnergies is neither mentioned nor targeted in this[article](https://www.lemonde.fr/en/economy/article/2026/04/07/oil-giants-profits-flow-partly-into-tax-havens_6752181_19.html) published by *Le Monde* , we wish to bring to readers' attention a number of factual and quantitative elements regarding **TotalEnergies' taxation** :


1- TotalEnergies pays taxes in the countries where it generates profits, very largely in the countries where it produces energy.


2- In 2025, current income tax and production taxes amounted to just over **$19 billion worldwide** for TotalEnergies.


3- In 2025, TotalEnergies' contribution to public finances **in France** is expected to exceed **€2 billion** .


4- With an average **global effective tax rate of more than 40%** (the corporate income tax rate in France is 25%), TotalEnergies does not shift its profits to low-tax jurisdictions and does not manipulate the allocation of profits within the Group, whether during periods of high prices or otherwise.


5- More information is available in TotalEnergies'[2024 Tax Transparency Report.](https://totalenergies.com/sites/g/files/nytnzq121/files/documents/totalenergies_tax-transparency-report-2024_2026_en.pdf)
