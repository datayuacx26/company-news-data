# Response to FranceInfo on TotalEnergies’ taxation

**TotalEnergies pays its taxes in the countries where it generates profits** , primarily in producing countries where it produces energy.


As detailed in our[Tax Transparency Report](https://totalenergies.com/sites/g/files/nytnzq121/files/documents/totalenergies_tax-transparency-report-2024_2026_en.pdf) , TotalEnergies has paid nearly **$100 billion in current income tax and production taxes to governments** since 2022 (France & the rest of the world).


In addition, TotalEnergies' **average global effective tax rate stands at 43%** over the same period (compared with a **25% corporate income tax rate in France** ).


With such an average tax rate, it is therefore quite clear that **TotalEnergies does not shift its profits to low-tax jurisdictions** .
