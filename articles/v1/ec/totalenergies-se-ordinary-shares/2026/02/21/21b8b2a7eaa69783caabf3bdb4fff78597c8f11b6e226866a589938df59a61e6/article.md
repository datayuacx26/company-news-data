# Fourth quarter and full year 2025 results

TotalEnergies generates stable cash flow of $7.2 billion in the 4th quarter, despite a drop of more than $5/b in oil prices


In 2025, TotalEnergies reports adjusted net income of $15.6 bn, down 15% year-on-year reflecting oil price decrease while
cash flow of nearly $28 bn decreased by only 7% year-on-year, benefiting from the accretive growth of its production


Return on average capital employed of 12.6%, the best among the majors for the fourth consecutive year
15% gearing at year‑end 2025


*******


**TotalEnergies proposes a dividend of €3.40/share for fiscal year 2025, a 5.6% increase**
