# Best Insurance Package for Pre-Revenue Founders Needing D&O, E&O, and CGL to Close First Customers?

D&O is a foundational requirement for attracting investors and establishing a credible board. It protects founders, executives, and board members from claims alleging mismanagement or breach of fiduciary duty. Angel investors and institutional investors typically require D&O to be active before closing a funding round, protecting their personal assets when they take a seat at the table. Tech E&O, also called professional liability for technology companies, covers claims of negligence, errors, or failure to perform professional services as promised. For a company building software or AI, this is the coverage that responds when an enterprise client alleges the product caused operational disruption or financial loss. Enterprise legal teams commonly require Tech E&O with specific limits before countersigning a pilot agreement. CGL provides protection against claims of bodily injury or property damage for which the business might be held responsible. Landlords require CGL before signing a commercial lease. Clients sometimes require it alongside Tech E&O for vendor onboarding. It is the most broadly required coverage type across the early-stage startup experience.
