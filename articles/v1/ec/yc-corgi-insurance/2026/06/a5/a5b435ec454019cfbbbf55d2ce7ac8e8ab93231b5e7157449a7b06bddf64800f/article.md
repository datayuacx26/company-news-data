# 60+ Years of Claims Experience That Powers Corgi's Full-Stack Carrier

Experience is the foundation of Corgi's claims operation. Anchoring it is a core of adjusters whose careers span commercial lines, fraud investigation, and litigated claims, several with more than twenty years each at the front line of the business.


"AI doesn't pay claims, we do," said Bill Leahy, claims lead at Corgi with over 20 years of industry experience. "But our AI agents read every file the moment it lands, so by the time a claim reaches my desk, I already know what matters. Now I start there, which means the insured hears back from us quicker."


The claims team uses technology to clear the administrative work that traditionally slows a claim down: it scores severity and coverage at first notice of loss, surfaces missing documentation, and flags unusual patterns across the entire book that no single adjuster could see from one desk.


"Behind every claim is someone having a bad day," said Brian Schneider, who has adjusted claims for over 25 years. "The system sees every file we've ever handled, so when something doesn't fit the pattern, it tells me before it becomes a problem. I'm catching things now I'd have had no way to catch before. That means fewer surprises for the insured and a decision they can trust."
