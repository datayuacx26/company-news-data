# AI Agents for Specialist Clinics: Use Cases for Aesthetic, Eye, Dental, Ortho, Oncology, and Health Screening Clinics

## Summary


Specialist clinics can use AI agents to capture and qualify enquiries, explain approved service information, route patients to the right doctor or location, automate appointment reminders, follow up with non-responders, support recall, and escalate complex clinical questions to staff. AI is especially useful when enquiries are high-value, repetitive, and time-sensitive.


> For specialist clinics, AI agents should be designed around conversion, preparation, follow-up, and safe escalation. The goal is to help patients move from interest to the right appointment while keeping clinical decisions with qualified staff.

## Why This Matters for Singapore Clinics


- Specialist clinic enquiries often represent high-value appointments or treatment pathways.
- Patients need more information before booking, which creates front desk workload.
- Slow follow-up can cause patients to compare and choose another provider.
- AI agents can standardise enquiry handling while escalating clinical nuance.


## Specialist clinic AI workflows to consider


Service or package enquiry qualification.


Doctor, location, and service routing.


Approved explanation of procedures or preparation steps.


Appointment booking or callback requests.


Reminder and no-show recovery.


Post-visit or post-procedure follow-up.


Patient recall for reviews.


Caregiver communication and escalation.


## How to keep specialist AI safe


Use approved content for service explanations.


Escalate symptoms, urgent issues, medication questions, and clinical suitability.


Make it clear when staff will follow up.


Review conversation transcripts to improve routing and content.


Do not let AI make clinical recommendations beyond approved rules.


## What Clinics Should Automate First


1. FAQs about services, doctors, locations, opening hours, and preparation instructions
2. Appointment booking, rescheduling, and cancellation requests
3. Appointment reminders and confirmation flows
4. No-show recovery and rebooking prompts
5. Patient recall for screening, vaccination, chronic care, and follow-up visits
6. Screening or package enquiry qualification
7. Post-visit follow-up, forms, and care instructions
8. Human handoff for complex cases


## What to Watch Out For


- AI should not replace clinical judgment or diagnose patients.
- Emergency or urgent symptoms should be escalated clearly and quickly.
- Patient consent, privacy, and PDPA obligations should be considered before collecting or processing personal data.
- Human handoff is essential for complex, emotional, sensitive, or clinically ambiguous situations.
- Generic chatbots may be enough for simple FAQs, but healthcare workflows usually require more governance, testing, and auditability.
- Integration quality matters. A chatbot that cannot connect to clinic workflows may simply create another inbox for staff to manage.

## FAQ


### Which specialist clinics can use AI agents?


Aesthetic, eye, dental, orthopaedic, oncology, cardiology, fertility, physiotherapy, and screening clinics can all benefit.


### Can AI agents increase specialist appointment conversion?


Yes, by responding faster, qualifying intent, routing patients, and following up when they do not respond.


### Can AI explain procedures?


It can share approved general information, but clinical suitability and medical advice should be escalated.


### Can AI support caregivers?


Yes. It can help caregivers with operational questions, reminders, and appointment coordination where appropriate.


### What specialist workflows should be automated first?


Start with enquiry capture, FAQs, appointment routing, reminders, and human handoff.
