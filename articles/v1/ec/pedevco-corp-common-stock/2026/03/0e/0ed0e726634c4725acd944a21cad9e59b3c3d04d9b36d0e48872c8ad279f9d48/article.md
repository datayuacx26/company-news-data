# PEDEVCO Announces Participation in the 38ᵗʰ Annual ROTH Conference

## PEDEVCO Announces Participation in the 38ᵗʰ Annual ROTH Conference


03/20/26


[PDF Version](https://www.pedevco.com/node/12256/pdf)


HOUSTON, March 20, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- PEDEVCO Corp. (NYSE American: PED) (“PEDEVCO” or the “Company”), a domestic energy company engaged in the acquisition and development of strategic, high growth energy projects in the Rocky Mountain region, today announced that President & Chief Executive Officer J. Douglas Schick, Chief Operating Officer Reagan Tuck (R.T.) Dukes, and Executive Vice President & General Counsel Clark Moore, will participate in the 38th Annual Roth Conference taking place March 22–24, 2026.


Members of management will host investor meetings on-site during the conference. An updated investor presentation is available on the Company’s website at[https://www.pedevco.com/investors](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=kqu6mE6_IUXmctDHPBCoNR7eM-FAeiHrm7GtPUYtwE5QyA9FUzRjNm6xCci2Y9hnlF2jQ-uCrBpmJvYlnRxjICVtmZV7q24ySPkd2NfRFvLdaWE6vf1THZITGg-s_Gx7) .

**About PEDEVCO Corp.**
PEDEVCO Corp. (NYSE American: PED) is a publicly traded energy company engaged in the acquisition and development of strategic, high growth energy projects in the United States. The Company’s principal assets are its Rockies Assets located in the D-J Basin of Wyoming and Northern Colorado and the Powder River Basin in Wyoming. The Company also holds assets in the Permian Basin located in eastern New Mexico. PEDEVCO is headquartered in Houston, Texas. More information about PEDEVCO can be found at[www.pedevco.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=93xkffdy6mhh_JII87ZDKMjZSc83VH4J5KpfDu-QhQmhp7izyr8JoVog96sHwqN7IC7Z3_qbGiBXkt_dIvPBJA==) .
