## Why Do You Need a Tool to Mass Apply to Multiple Jobs Fast?


The math of job searching in 2026 is straightforward and painful. Recruiters typically review the first 100 applications submitted to any given role. Every hour of delay costs you position. Applying manually to Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, and the dozens of other portals where real jobs live can take 30-45 minutes per application, and[the average job seeker needs to submit well over 100 applications](https://blog.hiringthing.com/2025-job-application-statistics-updated-data-you-need-to-know) to generate meaningful interview activity. That is 50 to 75 hours of repetitive form-filling, most of it before a human ever reads a word you wrote.


Tsenta was built to solve exactly this problem. The founders applied to 3,000+ jobs manually as international students, tried every tool on the market, and found that none of them actually worked end-to-end. So they built their own.


### Why Slow Tools Are Disqualifying: The Problems Mass Applicants Face


- **Latency kills your position.** A tool that processes applications hours or days after a role posts means you arrive after the first 100 are already in.
- **Limited ATS coverage leaves most jobs out of reach.** Most competitors cover only 25-30% of Fortune 500 job listings. Tools that only work on job boards miss the company portals where the most competitive roles live.
- **Autofill is not the same as applying.** Chrome extensions that pre-fill your name and email still require you to sign in, click through every step, and hit submit. That saves a few minutes, not hours.
- **Opaque pricing and hidden caps create budget surprises.** Add-on fees for cover letters, résumé optimization, and extra credits make the real cost difficult to predict.
- **Generic applications get filtered out.** Submitting the same résumé to every role without tailoring it to each job description reduces the quality of every application in the batch.


Tools that address all five of these problems close the gap between applying at volume and applying well. Tsenta was designed with all five in mind.

- **Per-role résumé tailoring with a visible diff:** Every application should go out with a résumé rewritten for that specific job description, with keyword alignment and ATS-safe formatting. You should be able to see every change before it is sent.
- **Speed:** Applications need to complete in seconds, not hours or days. Recruiters typically review the first 100 applicants, so latency is a competitive disadvantage, not just an inconvenience.

- **Multi-surface access:** The best tools let you apply from a web dashboard, a Chrome extension, a desktop app, a mobile app, or even a text message. Eight surfaces mean your job search is never stuck waiting for one device.
- **A receipt for every application:** Every submission should produce a record of exactly what was sent: résumé version, cover letter, answers to open-ended questions, timestamp. No surprises.
