# Tsenta vs Massive, $99/mo for 200 apps and 23 onboarding steps

Massive+ is $99 a month for up to 200 applications.


that's 50 cents per application at their most popular tier. it's also 23 onboarding steps before the bot does anything, a 4-day trial that requires a credit card upfront, and an onboarding question that asks "how many interviews do you want" as if interview invitations are theirs to grant.


let's get specific.


## what Massive actually is


UseMassive bills itself as "your job search on autopilot." they have two paid tiers, billed monthly or quarterly:


**Monthly:**


- Passive: $49/mo, up to 50 jobs/mo, sourced from "500K+ standard job sites," autopilot daily, 30-day money-back, cancel anytime
- Massive+ (most popular): $99/mo, up to 200 jobs/mo, sourced from "2M+ jobs including Workday, iCIMS, etc," autopilot daily, custom resumes and cover letters, priority support


**Quarterly (save 33%):**


- Passive: $99 / 3 months ($33/mo equivalent), up to 150 jobs per quarter
- Massive+: $199 / 3 months ($67/mo equivalent), up to 600 jobs per quarter


both tiers offer a 4-day Massive+ free trial, but you have to enter a credit card to start it. there's no free-as-in-actually-free way to try the product.


they market hard on social proof. "1,000+ interviews landed last week," "trusted by 100,000+ jobseekers," logos for TikTok, OpenAI, Stripe, Netflix on the homepage. they're a real, polished product with a real team. that's not in question.


what's in question is what you actually get for $99/mo.


## where Massive falls short


a few things to be honest about:


-


the math at the most popular tier is wild. $99 a month, up to 200 applications. that's $0.50 per application. and 200/mo isn't a "starter" cap, it's their flagship tier. the cheaper Passive tier is even worse on a per-app basis: $49 for 50 jobs/mo is $0.98 per application. almost a dollar per submission, for software that's doing the form-filling.


-


23-step onboarding. i'm not exaggerating. before you see anything happen, you walk through 23 separate screens asking about your background, preferences, goals, timing, location, salary, you name it. some of it is reasonable setup. most of it feels like a survey designed to qualify you into a higher tier.


-


one of those questions is "how many interviews do you want?" which is presumptuous in a way worth flagging. Massive doesn't grant interviews. companies do. asking a user to pre-commit to an interview count makes for a clean onboarding flow and an honest pitch becomes a misleading one.


-


there's no actual free tier. the "4-day trial" requires a credit card upfront. if you forget to cancel, you're billed. that's the standard subscription-trap pattern. cloud appliers in this category should let you try without commitment, because the whole point of the product is something you have to experience to know if it works.


-


the tier structure is confusing on purpose. the cheaper Passive tier sources from "500K+ standard job sites" but the Massive+ tier specifically itemizes "Workday, iCIMS, etc." which implies Passive doesn't have those. you only find out which ATSes your bot reaches by reading carefully across both tier descriptions.


-


web-only. no desktop app, no Chrome extension, no MCP integration, no mobile, no SMS. you're tied to their web UI, on one device.


-


transparency varies. they do tell you the application can be previewed before send, which is more than most cloud appliers. but it's not a "watch the bot work" experience. you preview, you trust, applications go out.
