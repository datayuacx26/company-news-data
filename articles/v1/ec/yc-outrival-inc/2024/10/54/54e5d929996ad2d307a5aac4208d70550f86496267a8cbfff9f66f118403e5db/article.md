# How AI Agents Are Shaping the Future of Higher Education and Customer Experience


Introducing OutRival


Last week, over cafecito in Miami, I sat down with some customer experience leaders, and we kept circling around a critical question:


How can customer-facing teams leverage AI to move beyond outdated solutions like prerecorded phone menus ([Interactive Voice Response-IVR](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/operations-blog/why-ivr-still-matters-in-an-ai-world) systems) and third-party offshore solutions like BPO’s ([business process outsourcing](https://www.forbes.com/advisor/business/business-process-outsourcing/) ), to create personalized experiences that drive both satisfaction and business results?


The conversation hit home: while these teams know the customer journey better than anyone else, they often lack the tools to turn their insights into action.


That’s why we built[OutRival](https://outrival.com/) .


OutRival is an AI platform that empowers customer experience teams, both technical and non-technical, to turn their insights into action, creating solutions that enhance customer satisfaction and drive revenue.


We’re starting by empowering Admissions Teams in Higher Education. Universities are leading the charge in modernizing student engagement and OutRival helps Admissions Teams create their own Conversational AI Agents and Workflows, transforming the way prospective students interact with universities, guiding them through the admissions process, answering questions, and delivering personalized experiences at scale. By leveraging AI, universities can enhance recruitment efforts, cut costs, and free up staff to focus on deeper student relationships.


OutRival is designed with an intuitive interface that anyone can use right away, no manual required. It ensures that every solution is not only efficient but also reliable, scalable, and built to meet the standards enterprises demand.


With OutRival, teams can quickly innovate in a collaborative way, while maintaining the safety, compliance, and oversight necessary for enterprise-level deployments, turning insight into action with confidence.


## A Legacy of Empowerment


When we started[Career Karma](https://careerkarma.com/blog/career-karma-40-million-series-b/) in 2018, we didn’t just help people break into tech; we built and led customer experience teams ourselves. To this day, we continue to make thousands of calls, guide people through their career journeys, and continue to learn that personalized relationships are what truly make the difference. Every conversation matters, and we see firsthand how voice, tone, and attention to detail can transform a single interaction into a lasting connection.


Over the last 2 years, as founders of OutRival, we’ve worked closely with customer experience teams across industries. We understand the pressures they face to deliver personalized interactions at scale, all while balancing efficiency and business impact. We’ve seen the disconnect between teams that understand customer needs and the tools available to bring those insights to life.


With OutRival, companies can[leverage their deep domain expertise](https://x.com/overlap_tech/status/1835418318205055352?s=46&t=B1Qpm2Ikt5ZJNZ9cebyNfA) through an intuitive interface, without needing to reskill or worry about mastering the latest AI technology. Our platform empowers them to design solutions that increase satisfaction, cut costs, and grow revenue, while ensuring that human relationships stay central to every interaction.


OutRival puts voice and personalization at the core of its platform, making sure that every customer experience is as personal and meaningful as it is efficient.


## Scaling Personalization: Amplifying the Human Touch with AI Agents


Every brand has a voice. Today, that voice is primarily powered by humans.


In the future, that experience will continue to be powered by humans, but they will be augmented by a different kind of technology.


Our friends in Silicon Valley refer to[this type of technology as an AI agent](https://x.com/rowancheung/status/1811611980039373146) , but what they really are is an extension of your team, like a personal assistant that works 24/7.


These AI agents handle repetitive tasks, such as answering routine questions or guiding customers through workflows, allowing your team to focus on what they do best: building relationships. The best part? With OutRival, anyone on your customer experience team can design these agents, no technical background required.


Soon,[there will be more AI agents than humans on the planet](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rowancheung_mark-zuckerberg-believes-there-could-be-more-activity-7221939306012033026-Q3sI/) . By empowering customer experience teams to create Conversational AI Agents that align with their brand's voice, companies can deliver the kind of personalized, memorable experiences that customers expect.


Higher education is no exception. With an alarming rate of college closures (currently,[one U.S. college closes every week](https://hechingerreport.org/colleges-are-now-closing-at-a-pace-of-one-a-week-what-happens-to-the-students/) ), universities face pressure to serve more students with fewer resources. Even as the number of institutions declines, demand for education remains high as individuals seek to prepare for a technology-driven future, with around[19 million enrollments](https://educationdata.org/college-enrollment-statistics) projected in 2024.


Each year,[over 25 million students](https://edworkforce.house.gov/uploadedfiles/writtentestimony_cellini_final.pdf) navigate decisions about where to attend and how to finance their education. System inefficiencies often add to this challenge: this year alone,[4 million financial aid calls went unanswered](https://www.edweek.org/teaching-learning/as-students-sought-fafsa-help-4-million-calls-went-unanswered/2024/09) , leaving[many students without the support they need](https://www.wwno.org/npr-news/2024-08-27/fafsa-troubles-students-are-still-waiting-on-their-financial-aid-packages) .


Moreover, colleges are recognizing[the importance of being accountable](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/to-ensure-value-for-students-and-taxpayers-colleges-must-be-held-accountable-for-student-outcomes/) for demonstrating[clear student outcomes](https://www.asa.org/research/metrics-matter-how-higher-education-is-leveraging-data-to-influence-postsecondary-and-workforce-success/) , such as job placement rates and post-graduation earnings, to justify rising tuition and secure future enrollment.


In this evolving landscape, Conversational AI technology is essential to helping institutions answer calls and manage these student/customer interactions efficiently, providing colleges with the tools they need to deliver personalized experiences without adding to their headcount.
