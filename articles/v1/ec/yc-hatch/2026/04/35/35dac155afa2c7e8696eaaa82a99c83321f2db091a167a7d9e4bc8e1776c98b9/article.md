## How it works


To understand how lead rating works in Hatch, it helps to know how it works in Google LSA first.


### A quick refresher on LSA lead rating


[Google LSA](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/7419052?hl=en&ref_topic=11555266&sjid=18331002108747785074-NA)


has a built-in feature that lets you rate the leads you receive — thumbs up or thumbs down. When you rate a lead positively, you're telling Google "send me more like this." When you rate one negatively, you're telling Google "this wasn't a fit."


Over time, that feedback trains Google's algorithm to send you better-matched leads. The more consistently you rate, the better your lead quality gets. It can also help you get credits for leads that weren't legitimate.


It's a powerful feature, but it lives inside Google's LSA portal, which means someone on your team has to log in there separately to do it. For busy teams, that extra step tends to get skipped. And when it gets skipped, lead quality doesn't improve.


### Now you can do that in Hatch


If you have the Google LSA integration enabled, you can rate leads directly inside Hatch. Here's how:


#### Step 1: Open Quick actions


From any conversation card, go to the right-hand panel and click **Quick actions** . You'll see a **Google LSA** option with a **Rate this Lead** button.


``


#### Step 2: Rate your satisfaction


Click **Rate this lead** and you'll be asked "How satisfied are you?" Choose from seven options:


- Unspecified
- Unknown
- Very satisfied
- Satisfied
- Neutral
- Dissatisfied
- Very dissatisfied


``


#### Step 3: Add a reason (optional)


Depending on your rating, you can select a reason why.


If you chose **satisfied or very satisfied** , your reason options include:


- Unspecified
- Unknown
- Booked customer
- Likely booked customer
- Service related
- High value service
- Other satisfied reason


``


If you chose **dissatisfied or very dissatisfied** , your reason options include:


- Unspecified
- Unknown
- Geo mismatch
- Job type mismatch
- Not ready to book
- Spam
- Duplicate
- Solicitation
- Other dissatisfied reason


There's also an open text field for other reasons.


``


#### **Step 4: Submit**


Hit submit and you're done. That feedback is sent back to Google, helping refine how their system matches leads to your business over time.


Instead of managing lead intake in one system and lead quality in another, Hatch becomes the single place where:


- Leads are received
- Conversations are handled
- Outcomes are logged
- Feedback is submitted


## Why this matters


This feature has a twofold benefit:


- **The advantages of lead rating itself.** Your ratings train Google's algorithm to send you better-matched leads over time — and flag bad leads so you can get credits back for ones that weren't legitimate. The more consistently you rate, the better your lead quality gets.
- **The advantage of doing it in Hatch.** Your team is already in Hatch handling conversations, following up, and booking jobs alongside AI agents. Now they can rate leads as part of that same workflow instead of logging into a separate portal to do it later (or not doing it at all). For multi-location teams managing multiple LSA accounts, this is especially valuable — lead rating stays centralized instead of scattered across separate Google logins.
