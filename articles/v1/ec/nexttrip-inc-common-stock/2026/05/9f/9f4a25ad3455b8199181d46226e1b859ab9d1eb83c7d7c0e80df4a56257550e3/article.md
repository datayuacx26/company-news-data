# NextTrip Reports Preliminary Fiscal Year 2026 Results, Signaling Emergence of a New Category at the Intersection of Media and Travel

**NextTrip (NASDAQ:NTRP)** reported preliminary fiscal 2026 revenue of approximately **$3.7 million** , up from about $0.5 million, exceeding **640% year-over-year growth** . Fourth-quarter preliminary revenue was about **$1.6 million** , versus $1.2 million in the prior quarter, with an additional **$1.6 million** in deferred revenue.


The company highlights its transition to a media-driven, content-to-commerce travel platform, powered by JOURNY.tv and supported by acquisitions including **Five Star Alliance** , **TA Pipeline** , and **GoUSA** content assets.
