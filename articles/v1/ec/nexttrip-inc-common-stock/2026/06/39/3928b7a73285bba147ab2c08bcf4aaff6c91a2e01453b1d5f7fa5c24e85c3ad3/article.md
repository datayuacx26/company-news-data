# NextTrip to Present at Planet MicroCap Las Vegas 2026, Powered by MicroCapClub

**NextTrip (NASDAQ:NTRP)** will present at **Planet MicroCap Las Vegas 2026** , held June 17–18 at the Bellagio in Las Vegas. Director of Corporate Development Richard Marshall will speak June 17, 9:30–10:00 AM (Track 2 – Da Vinci 2) and hold one-on-one investor meetings.


According to NextTrip, topics include scaling its JOURNY streaming platform, integrating GoUSA TV assets, progress with the KC Global Media joint venture, growth at Five Star Alliance and TA Pipeline, development of TravelMagazine.com, deployment of Agentic AI tools, and its dual travel bookings and advertising revenue model.
