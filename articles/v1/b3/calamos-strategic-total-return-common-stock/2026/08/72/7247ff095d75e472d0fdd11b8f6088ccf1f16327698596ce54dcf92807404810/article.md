# Calamos Brings 50 Years of Options Expertise to the ETF Arena, Launches Active Hedged Equity ETF (CHDG), Offering Equity Upside, Less Volatility

*CHDG is designed to maintain equity market exposure with lower portfolio volatility*


*Discretionary, active risk management adjusts options positions as markets change, seeking a smoother return profile*


*Investors benefit from Calamos' nearly 50 years of options expertise and 12 years running a hedged equity mutual fund*


METRO CHICAGO, Ill.


, Aug. 4, 2026


/PRNewswire/ --[John Koudounis](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4744628-1&h=3430647148&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.calamos.com%2Fabout%2Fexecutive_officers%2Fjohn-koudounis%2F&a=John+Koudounis) , President and CEO of[Calamos](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4744628-1&h=2516968108&u=https%3A%2F%2Fedge.prnewswire.com%2Fc%2Flink%2F%3Ft%3D0%26l%3Den%26o%3D4665408-1%26h%3D1890920132%26u%3Dhttps%253A%252F%252Fwww.calamos.com%252F%26a%3DCalamos&a=Calamos) , a pioneer in liquid alternatives, today announced the launch of the[Calamos Active Hedged Equity ETF (CHDG](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4744628-1&h=1016310486&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.calamos.com%2Ffunds%2Fetf%2Fcalamos-active-hedged-equity-chdg%2F&a=Calamos+Active+Hedged+Equity+ETF+(CHDG) ). Taking an active approach, the ETF adjusts its options positions to seek to capture market opportunities as conditions change, rather than following a rigid systematic strategy. This flexible approach seeks to achieve total return with lower volatility than equity markets.


"Investors want to preserve the wealth they've built and manage volatility in their portfolios. With CHDG, we are proud to bring our sophisticated, institutional-grade risk management capabilities to investors who prefer the ETF wrapper," said Koudounis. "This launch builds on nearly five decades of options expertise, continuing our tradition of innovation in derivatives since John P. Calamos, Sr. founded this firm in 1977."


Since launching the[Calamos Hedged Equity Mutual Fund](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4744628-1&h=2280223311&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.calamos.com%2Ffunds%2Fmutual%2Fhedged-equity-cihex%2F&a=Calamos+Hedged+Equity+Mutual+Fund) in 2014, Calamos has been helping investors dynamically navigate market downturns while capturing equity growth. The same approach, tested through multiple market cycles and drawdowns, will now be offered in a tax-efficient ETF wrapper with CHDG. Over the recent 10-year period, CIHEX is top quartile among peers in the US Fund Equity Hedged Morningstar peer group, earning an overall four-star rating as of June 30, 2026.


"With CHDG, we're excited to bring our team's active risk management approach to the ETF market, seeking smoother beta that adjusts with market conditions. The rolling quarterly hedge structures of systematic strategies can be roiled by short-term market conditions, leading to inconsistent market exposure over time. Instead, CHDG applies continuous portfolio monitoring to manage downside risk while preserving upside potential across market regimes. This can result in a more consistent, smoother experience — beneficial for model portfolio implementation," said[Matt Kaufman](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4744628-1&h=79555793&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.calamos.com%2Fabout%2Fmatt-kaufman%2F&a=Matt+Kaufman) , Head of ETFs.


CHDG will be managed by[Eli Pars](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4744628-1&h=1434714768&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.calamos.com%2Fabout%2Finvestment-organization%2Feli-pars%2F&a=Eli+Pars) , Co-CIO, Co-Head of Alternative Strategies, Co-Head of Convertible Strategies, and Sr. Co-Portfolio Manager;[David O'Donohue](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4744628-1&h=1019168506&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.calamos.com%2Fabout%2Fportfolio-management%2Fdavid-odonohue%2F&a=David+O%27Donohue) , SVP, Co-Head of Alternative Strategies, and Sr. Co-Portfolio Manager;[Jason Hill](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4744628-1&h=2359288660&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.calamos.com%2Fabout%2Fportfolio-management%2Fjason-hill%2F&a=Jason+Hill) , SVP and Sr. Co-Portfolio Manager;[Jimmy Young](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4744628-1&h=4063636950&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.calamos.com%2Fabout%2Fportfolio-management%2Fjimmy-young%2F&a=Jimmy+Young) , SVP and Co-Portfolio Manager;[Anthony Vecchiolla](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4744628-1&h=4153605822&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.calamos.com%2Fabout%2Fportfolio-management%2Fanthony-vecchiolla%2F&a=Anthony+Vecchiolla) , SVP and Co-Portfolio Manager; and[Jordan Rosenfeld](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4744628-1&h=3779889867&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.calamos.com%2Fabout%2Fportfolio-management%2Fjordan-rosenfeld%2F&a=Jordan+Rosenfeld) , VP and Co-Portfolio Manager. **The Morningstar Equity Hedged Category** is comprised of funds that use a variety of means to protect the value of their equity exposure during times of market weakness. These funds may exchange equity risk for some other risk premium, such as volatility. They may also make opportunistic trades, like employing market-timing moves to exit the market altogether. These funds use a variety of options trades to hedge their equity risk, including put writing, options spreads, collar strategies, and others. Funds in the category will typically have beta values to relevant benchmarks of less than 0.6.
