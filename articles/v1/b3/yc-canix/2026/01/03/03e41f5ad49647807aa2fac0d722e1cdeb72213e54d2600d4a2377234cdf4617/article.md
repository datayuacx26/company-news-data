# Canix Is Acquiring Trym: Building the Future of Cannabis Operations Together

We’re excited to share an important milestone in Canix’s journey: **Canix is acquiring Trym** , a cultivation-focused platform known for its mobile-first experience and deep expertise in crop steering and task execution.


This acquisition represents a meaningful step forward in our mission to build **the most comprehensive, user-friendly operating system for cannabis operators** .


**Why Trym**


From the beginning, Canix has focused on creating a true system of record for cannabis operations—bringing together cultivation, costing, and operational data in one unified platform. Trym complements this vision exceptionally well.


Trym has built powerful tools around:


- Crop steering and cultivation workflows
- Task management designed for real-world execution
- A mobile-first experience that meets operators where work actually happens


By bringing Trym into Canix, we’re combining the strengths of both platforms to better serve the needs of modern cannabis operators.


**What This Means for Customers**


This acquisition allows us to move faster and go deeper in several key areas that matter most to operators:


**A Stronger Mobile Experience**


Canix’s mobile app is already released and actively being expanded. With Trym’s mobile-first DNA and experience, we’re accelerating our ability to deliver intuitive, in-field tools that help teams execute, record, and communicate seamlessly.


**Improved Task Management**


Day-to-day execution is the backbone of any successful operation. Trym’s expertise strengthens our ongoing work to improve task management—making it easier to plan work, ensure accountability, and connect execution directly to outcomes.


**Deeper Cultivation Intelligence**


Trym’s background in crop steering and sensor-driven workflows enhances Canix’s ability to integrate environmental and cultivation data into operational decision-making, helping operators move from data collection to actionable insight.


**Expanded Engineering Expertise**


With the addition of Trym’s engineering team, Canix gains additional technical depth and development velocity. This means faster iteration, more innovation, and a continued focus on usability and reliability.


**A More Complete All-in-One Platform**


Together, Canix and Trym strengthen what operators increasingly demand: a single, unified platform that reduces complexity, replaces fragmented tools, and provides clarity across cultivation, operations, and costing.


**What’s Not Changing**


For existing customers, it’s important to be clear: **there is no forced disruption** .


- Trym customers will continue to use the Trym platform for at least one year
- Canix customers will see continued investment in the Canix platform
- Support and service levels remain unchanged


This acquisition is about thoughtful integration, not rushed consolidation.


**Our Commitment Going Forward**


At its core, this acquisition reinforces our commitment to the cannabis industry.


Canix will continue to invest in:


- Building software designed specifically for cannabis operators
- Prioritizing usability and real-world workflows
- Delivering a platform that grows alongside the industry


We believe the future of cannabis operations depends on clarity, integration, and execution—and this combination positions us to deliver exactly that.


We’re excited about what’s ahead and look forward to sharing more as we continue building the future of cannabis operations, together.


—


**The Canix Team**
