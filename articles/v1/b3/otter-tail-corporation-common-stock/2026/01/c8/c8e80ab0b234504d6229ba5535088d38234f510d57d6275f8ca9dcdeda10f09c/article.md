# Otter Tail Corporation Will Host Conference Call on 2025 Financial Results

# Otter Tail Corporation Will Host Conference Call on 2025 Financial Results


Company Release - 1/27/2026 3:30 PM ET


Download the PDF version PDF Format (opens in new window)


FERGUS FALLS, Minn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Otter Tail Corporation (Nasdaq: OTTR) will issue a news release announcing 2025 financial results after the stock market closes on Monday, February 16, 2026 and will host a live conference call and webcast on Tuesday, February 17, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. CT to discuss the corporation’s financial and operating performance.


Accompanying slides will be posted on the corporation’s website before the webcast begins. To access the live webcast, go to[www.ottertail.com/events-and-presentations](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.ottertail.com%2Fevents-and-presentations&esheet=54394573&newsitemid=20260127081269&lan=en-US&anchor=www.ottertail.com%2Fevents-and-presentations&index=1&md5=378d91070b39e834863379438e16d634) . Please allow time prior to the call to visit the site and download any software required to listen. A copy of the webcast will be available on the corporation’s website shortly after the call.


Please[click here](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fregister-conf.media-server.com%2Fregister%2FBIa41ce8c7d79342e49c757c7dfa8980c2&esheet=54394573&newsitemid=20260127081269&lan=en-US&anchor=click+here&index=2&md5=a581a7268b1e81ec69202760de4a12ed) to pre-register for the conference call and obtain your dial in number and passcode. Contact Beth Eiken at 701-451-3571 orbeiken@ottertail.com with any questions on how to participate.


**About Otter Tail Corporation:** Otter Tail Corporation, a member of the S&P SmallCap 600 Index, has interests in diversified operations that include an electric utility and manufacturing businesses. Otter Tail Corporation stock trades on the NASDAQ Global Select Market under the symbol OTTR. The latest investor and corporate information is available at[www.ottertail.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ottertail.com%2Fhome%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54394573&newsitemid=20260127081269&lan=en-US&anchor=www.ottertail.com&index=3&md5=0f2457a1832c12f88a0ee990e3e0610a) . Corporate offices are located in Fergus Falls, Minnesota, and Fargo, North Dakota.
