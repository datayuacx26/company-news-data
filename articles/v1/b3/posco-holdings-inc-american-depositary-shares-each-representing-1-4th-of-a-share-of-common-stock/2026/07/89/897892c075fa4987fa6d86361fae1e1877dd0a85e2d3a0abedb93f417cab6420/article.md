# POSCO Group Newsroom Introduction


## Welcome to POSCO Group Newsroom


The POSCO Group Newsroom is the official media representing POSCO Group as it moves towards becoming a sustainable 100-year company. It delivers unique and professional information exclusive to the POSCO Group Newsroom, including various business achievements, CSR activities, and employee stories, as well as global industry trends related to core businesses.


## POSCO Group Newsroom Sections


**Business** is filled with the latest news about POSCO Group's business. You can check the competitiveness of POSCO Group's core businesses such as steel, energy materials, infrastructure, and new businesses.


**Industry Report** is filled with expert columns and professional information that deeply analyze global industry issues and economic trends related to POSCO Group's major businesses.


**People & Culture** delivers stories of POSCO Group's social contribution activities, stories of POSCO employees, and recruitment information for future talents to join POSCO.


Lastly, in the **Press Center** you can quickly check all press releases distributed by POSCO Group.


We will become the POSCO Group Newsroom that delivers POSCO Group's news quickly and accurately.
