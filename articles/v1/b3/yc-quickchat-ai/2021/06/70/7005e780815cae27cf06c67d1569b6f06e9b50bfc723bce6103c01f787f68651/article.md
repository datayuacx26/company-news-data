# TOP 10 GPT-Powered Apps You Need To Know In 2021

List of **TOP 10 apps powered by GPT-3** , curated by Quickchat.


## GPT…What? 🤔


In case you’re wondering—GPT-3, or *Generative Pre-Trained Transformer 3* , is a powerful AI language model developed by OpenAI. With 175 billion parameters, it’s capable of handling a variety of tasks, pushing us closer to Artificial General Intelligence (AGI). Since its launch, OpenAI’s API has enabled developers to create **over 300 applications** , delivering GPT-3-powered **search, conversation, text generation, and more** .


## The Next Generation of AI Apps 🚀


Nine months after OpenAI API’s release, thousands of developers have built **incredibly innovative applications** . Below is our **TOP 10 list** of GPT-3-powered apps making waves in 2021.


---


## **#10 CopyAI**


🚀 *AI-powered copywriting for marketing and creative professionals.*


CopyAI helps marketers and content creators generate **engaging marketing copy** —from social media posts to website content. It assists in **brainstorming, refining, and automating writing tasks** , making it easier for businesses to produce high-quality content.

## **#9 Magic Sales Bot**


🎯 *Personalized cold emails that convert.*


Cold outreach can be tricky, but Magic Sales Bot uses GPT-3 to **personalize emails** for each lead, helping sales teams **increase engagement** and boost conversions.


*Magic Sales Bot generating customized cold emails with GPT-3*

## **#8 SnazzyAI**


📝 *AI-powered copy generation for marketing teams.*


SnazzyAI is a writing assistant similar to CopyAI, but with a focus on **creative, high-converting marketing copy** . It includes tools for **ad copy, landing pages, emails, and more** .


*SnazzyAI creating high-converting marketing content*

## **#7 Shortly**


✍️ *An AI-powered writing assistant for overcoming writer’s block.*


Shortly is perfect for content creators, bloggers, and novelists looking for **inspiration** . It generates **new paragraphs, ideas, and creative writing suggestions** based on your input.


*Shortly helping writers overcome creative blocks with AI suggestions*

## **#6 Writesonic**


📢 *Automated content creation for businesses and marketers.*


Writesonic specializes in **high-performing ad copy, blog posts, landing pages, and product descriptions** . It provides **AI-powered writing tools** that help users refine their messaging.

## **#5 Pages by Headlime**


🖥️ *AI-powered landing page builder—no coding required.*


Pages by Headlime allows users to **generate fully-designed, high-converting landing pages** just by describing their product or service.


*Pages by Headlime creating a landing page from a simple product description*

## **#4 HelpHub**


❓ *Automated AI-powered knowledge bases and help centers.*


HelpHub streamlines customer support by **generating FAQs, help articles, and documentation** using AI, reducing the time needed for manual support content creation.


---


## **#3 Grok**


💬 *AI-powered Slack assistant for summarizing conversations.*


Grok helps professionals keep up with **Slack messages** by providing **daily digests and conversation summaries** , ensuring that important updates don’t get lost in chat overload.


---


## **#2 OthersideAI**


📧 *Automated email writing assistant.*


OthersideAI takes a **short summary** of what you want to say and generates **well-crafted, fully written emails** in your personal style—saving time and improving communication.

## **#1 Flowrite**


📝 *AI-powered writing assistant for productivity.*


Flowrite helps professionals write **emails, messages, and posts** in their own style, just by providing a few bullet points. It’s an **all-in-one AI writing tool** for streamlining everyday communication.
