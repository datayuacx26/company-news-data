# Feature announcement: Translator

## What is Translator?


Ever been in a customer support chat, staring at a message in French, German, or some language you don’t know — not even sure if it’s a complaint or… a compliment?


In the AI era, language barriers shouldn’t slow you down.


Now available for all users, **Translator** lets you instantly translate any conversation right inside your chat — no extra tools, no copy-pasting, no Google Translate tabs.


## How it works


It’s simple.


Just go to **Settings** in your Inbox, hover over **Translate** , and select your preferred language. Once you’ve done this, all conversations in your account will be translated into that language.


Whether you’re supporting customers in Paris, Berlin, or anywhere in the world, Translator helps you understand your customer chats without ever leaving your chat.


No more tab-switching, just a seamless workflow.
