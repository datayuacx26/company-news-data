## Why Teams Switch from HubSpot Breeze to Quickchat AI


## 1. Transparent, Scalable Pricing


HubSpot Breeze AI is only available on expensive Professional and Enterprise plans and charges extra per conversation once you pass a small limit. That means your bill can jump unexpectedly when chat volume spikes.


Quickchat AI offers you two choices:


- **flat, predictable pricing** - from a free plan for small teams to business. No hidden credits or per-chat fees. Just simple, startup-friendly pricing that grows with you. If you need to connect AI Agent to Hubspot Inbox, it requires at least Business plan.
- **resolution-based pricing** - pay only for the conversations that your AI successfully resolves. Just simple, startup-friendly pricing that grows with you. The pricing starts from **$0.6** per resolved conversation.


👉[See Quickchat pricing](https://quickchat.ai/pricing)


## 2. Smarter Knowledge Management


**Breeze AI** depends on tightly structured HubSpot Knowledge Base articles and FAQs. If your docs are not perfectly formatted or constantly updated, Breeze may struggle to deliver accurate answers.


**Quickchat AI** , on the other hand, can handle **any kind of content** — from PDFs, internal docs, and manuals to databases or web pages. Its advanced retrieval system finds the most relevant, up-to-date information every time, no matter how complex your knowledge base is. 👉[Learn how Quickchat handles knowledge](https://docs.quickchat.ai/ai-agent/knowledge-base/)


## 3. Deep Customization and Brand Voice


**HubSpot’s AI** lets you pick a “friendly” tone and general role, but customization stops there.


With **Quickchat AI** , you can **fully define your AI’s personality, tone, and behavior** . Give it a unique name, match your brand’s voice, or even teach it how to handle refunds, upsells, or technical queries. You can set business rules, create workflows, and integrate it with your tools - all that in the Quickchat AI dashboard, without writing any code.


Your AI becomes a real digital team member, not just an add-on feature.

## 4. Multi-Channel Freedom


**Breeze** only works inside HubSpot’s Conversations Inbox and connected channels like your website chat or Messenger.

And it still keeps your HubSpot data current from all of them: the agent can[create contacts, log deals, and open tickets directly in your HubSpot CRM](https://quickchat.ai/post/connect-ai-agent-to-hubspot) while it chats.


Your customers get instant, consistent answers across every platform and your team gets a single AI system that supports both internal and external conversations.
