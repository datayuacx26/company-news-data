# Feature announcement: Sentiment Analysis

We don’t need to convince anyone that your users’ feelings matter, but catching their emotions across countless chats isn’t easy.


Now live for all Quickchat AI users, **Sentiment Analysis** is here to help you do just that.


## What is Sentiment Analysis


Sentiment Analysis taps into the users’ conversations with your AI Agent, analyzing each one to reveal emotions behind words — positive, neutral, or negative. Our AI does the heavy lifting, assigning one of three sentiments after every conversation.


It’s your window into what’s working, what’s not, and where to focus next.


## Why it matters


It’s a simple yet powerful way to gauge how your users feel without reading every single message.


- **Spot the unhappy fast:** Address dissatisfied users before small issues turn big.
- **See what’s working:** See when your AI Agent leaves people smiling.
- **Pair it with Topics:** Combine it with our other analytics feature,[Topics](https://www.quickchat.ai/post/feature-announcement-topics) , to dig deeper into what kind of issues make the users feel the way they do.
