Since Klarna AI will function as an AI Agent for customer service, the second option looks perfect:


Select “Customer Support Representative” in the AI Profession section


This choice significantly influences the tone and content of Assistant’s responses.


We have many more options to modify, but for now let’s tweak:


- **AI Creativity** : which determines the extent to which the AI Agent can generate innovative responses for queries that are related but not explicitly answered in the Knowledge Base
- **Reply Length** : offering control over the conciseness or detail of the replies
- **Links (URLs) Priority** : dictates how often URLs (such as to Klarna’s specific products or services) are incorporated into responses and prioritized above other information
