# Feature update: Message Sources

## Feature Update


[Earlier this month](https://www.quickchat.ai/post/feature-announcement-message-sources) , we announced that **Message Sources** will soon be even more useful by pointing to the exact paragraph from your Knowledge Base that influenced the AI’s response.


Now we’re happy to say — it’s already **live** ! 🔥


To use it, click the lightbulb 💡 icon next to the message you want to investigate in the Inbox.


Refining your Knowledge Base is crucial for **improving AI Agent performance.** Understanding why it responded the way it did is the first step toward that goal.
