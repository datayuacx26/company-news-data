# Driving Success in Industrial Manufacturing through Obsessive Innovation

## Driving Success in Industrial Manufacturing through Obsessive Innovation


May 09, 2023


In today's rapidly evolving industrial manufacturing landscape, innovation is a key driver of success. We need to constantly push the boundaries, challenge the status quo, and strive for excellence to stay competitive in a dynamic and ever-changing market. One approach that Ascent Industries Co. has taken is the mindset of "obsessive innovation."


What is Obsessive Innovation?


Obsessive innovation is a mindset and approach that goes beyond incremental improvements and encourages a relentless pursuit of innovation at all levels of the organization. It involves continuously seeking out new ideas, technologies, and processes to optimize operations, enhance products, and drive overall performance to new heights. Whether its within our Ascent Chemicals division or Ascent Tubular Products, we strive to deliver world-class products on time and on budget. Obsessive innovation requires a culture of curiosity, openness to change, and a willingness to take calculated risks.


Obsessive Innovation at Ascent Industries is built upon three key principles: Leadership, Efficient Execution, and Research & Development. Each principle brings a unique set of values to our pursuit.


Leadership plays a crucial role in driving the success of any manufacturing organization. Effective leadership sets the tone, establishes the culture, and provides the vision and direction for the company. Strong leadership fosters a culture of accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement throughout the organization, inspiring employees to strive for excellence.


Efficient execution is the backbone of manufacturing operations. It involves optimizing processes, resources, and technologies to ensure smooth and seamless operations that deliver products on time, meet quality standards, and exceed customer expectations.


Research & Development (R&D) is a critical pillar for manufacturing success as it drives innovation and differentiation in the market. R&D involves continuous exploration, experimentation, and development of new technologies, products, and processes to stay ahead of the competition and meet evolving customer needs.


With this mindset, the Ascent Industries team is ready to tackle any challenge, any project, or any unique needs our customers may have. We’re ready to go to work to meet our customers’ needs, help solve their challenges, and become a long-lasting partner.
