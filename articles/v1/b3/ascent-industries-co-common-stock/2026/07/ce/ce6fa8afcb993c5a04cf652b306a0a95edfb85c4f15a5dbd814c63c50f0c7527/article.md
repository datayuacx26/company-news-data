SCHAUMBURG, Ill.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Ascent Industries Co. (Nasdaq: ACNT) (“Ascent” or the “Company”), a specialty chemicals platform focused on the development, production, and distribution of tailored, performance-driven chemical solutions, will hold a conference call on Tuesday, August 4, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. Eastern time to discuss its financial results for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026. The results will be reported in a press release prior to the conference call.


Ascent management will host the conference call, followed by a question and answer period.
