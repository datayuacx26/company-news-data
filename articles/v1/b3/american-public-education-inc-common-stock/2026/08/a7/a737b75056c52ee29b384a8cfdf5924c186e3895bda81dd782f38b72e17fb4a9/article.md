# APUS Celebrates Top Scholars for Outstanding Academic Achievement

## President’s List for Fourth Quarter 2025 Spotlights Students Demonstrating Superior Scholarly Performance


**Charles Town, WV— January 2, 2026** –[American Public University System (APUS)](https://www.militaryplus.apus.edu/) , which includes


[American Military University (AMU)](https://www.amu.apus.edu/) and


[American Public University (APU)](https://www.apu.apus.edu/) , is proud to honor students’ exceptional academic achievements with its President’s List for the fourth quarter of 2025. This honor represents the University’s highest level of academic recognition.


View all students named to the President's List[here](https://militaryplus.apus.edu/docs/site/-/presidents-list-achievers/january-2026-presidents-list.pdf) .


“We celebrate these remarkable students whose commitment to learning sets a powerful example across our University community,” said APUS Provost Dr. Elizabeth Joy Johnson. “Their success is a testament to their hard work and resilience, and we are dedicated to supporting them as they continue to advance their academic and professional goals.”


APUS congratulates all students on this quarter's list, which is available[here](https://www.apu.apus.edu/academic/academic-leadership/presidents-list/) .


For more info on the President’s List, visit:[https://www.apu.apus.edu/student-handbook/your-apus-community/student-affairs/presidents-and-deans-lists/](https://www.apu.apus.edu/student-handbook/your-apus-community/student-affairs/presidents-and-deans-lists/) or emailstudentaffairs@apus.edu .


**About American Public University System**
[American Public University System](https://www.militaryplus.apus.edu/) (APUS) delivers affordable, high-quality, workforce-relevant higher education. With a rich, 34-year history and vibrant community of over 162,000 alumni from more than 80 countries, APUS is recognized for its innovative approach to online learning1.


APUS, which includes[American Military University](http://www.amu.apus.edu/) (AMU) and[American Public University](http://www.apu.apus.edu/) (APU), is accredited by the[Higher Learning Commission](https://www.hlcommission.org/) (HLC), an institutional accreditation agency recognized by the U.S. Department of Education. APUS is a wholly owned subsidiary of[American Public Education, Inc.](https://www.apei.com/) (Nasdaq: APEI). For more information, visit[www.apus.edu](https://www.militaryplus.apus.edu/) .


** *1* *APUS has been honored with the online Learning Consortium’s Gomory Award for Quality Online Education and is a five-time recipient of the consortium’s Effective Practice Award[https://www.apus.edu/newsroom/awards/](https://www.apus.edu/newsroom/awards/) .* **
