##### What inspired your curiosity and creativity?


When I was young, I was obsessed with taking things apart with screwdrivers, something

I also loved drawing cut away pictures of jets, submarines, and anything mechanical, I was alone at my senior school in already understanding how a

##### Thinking about one idea, was there a problem were you trying to solve?


For this year’s

I’d come across the

try and c

The result, in simple terms, a drone that carries an INER Wi-Fi access point powered continuously from the ground.


Flying 30m above obstacles, it extends coverage, improves reliability and helps connect people when they need it most.


##### What was the biggest challenge you faced?


There were numerous challenges we had to think hard about during the design process. As we developed the idea, we realised that regulatory issues would be just as big of a hurdle as the hardware side.


The first hurdle was that the


drone needs to be powerful enough to stay airborne in strong winds


.


Increasing power isn’t straightforward - a thicker tether adds more weight,


creating a cycle w


h


ere solving one issue creates another.


###### Every

A completely different challenge was the

possible

to ensure the system can be deployed quickly when it is needed.
