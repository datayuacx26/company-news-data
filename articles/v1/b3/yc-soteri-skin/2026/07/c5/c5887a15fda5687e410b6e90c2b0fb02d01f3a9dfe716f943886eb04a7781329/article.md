### Understanding Skin Conditions


Your skin tells a story- sometimes it is glowing, and other times it ends up with acne breakouts and flare-ups that leave you feeling frustrated and depressed. Skin problems, including dryness,[eczema](https://soteriskin.com/blogs/eczema) , and[psoriasis](https://soteriskin.com/blogs/skin-conditions/psoriasis-vs-eczema) , affect millions. People who are aware of **skin conditions** can take precautionary steps and avoid harmful products that can cause skin problems. There are **different** **skin conditions** , such as:


**Acne:** A common **skin condition** resulting from the clogging of the pores that produce whiteheads and blackheads and small bumps, and pimples.


**Psoriasis:** An autoimmune **skin disease** that comes up with scaly patches and causes a rash with itchiness. It is usually found on the knees, elbows, and scalp.


**Contact Dermatitis:** It is a rash that causes an acute or chronic inflammation when it comes in contact with an allergen or something that irritates your skin.


**Hives:** It is a skin rash that often results from allergic reactions and appears as raised red itchy bumps.


**Seborrheic Dermatitis:** A chronic skin disorder that appears as a yellowish, scaly, and greasy rash.
