### Eczema in Kids: Signs and Symptoms


[Eczema](https://soteriskin.com/blogs/eczema) signs and symptoms in kids or children usually vary. The need is not to confuse it with a simple rash, but to identify the early warning signs. Eczema in children often begins as patches of dry, scaly skin.


- Red patches that are dry, itchy (in lighter skin tones)
- Brown, purple, or grey patches in darker skin tone
- It can begin to show up on the cheeks, scalp, or creases of joints, such as the elbow, knee, and wrist.
- May evolve into an intense eczema rash in kids.
- Your child may become restless, and the very first behaviour that you will notice is constant itching, leading to an itch-scratch cycle.
- Increased irritability due to constant itch and discomfort.


Identifying these symptoms in their infancy allows for immediate intervention, preventing the skin from breaking and reducing the risk of secondary infections.


The intense itching (pruritus) seen with an[eczema](https://soteriskin.com/blogs/eczema) rash in kids often leads to:


- Sleep Deprivation and exhaustion for the child as well as the whole family
- Risk of secondary inflammation


Parents must monitor these signs vigilantly to keep the symptoms manageable, short-lived, and, most importantly, keep them away for lasting relief.
