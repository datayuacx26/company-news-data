Cybersecurity teams face a seemingly impossible responsibility to ensure absolute threat prevention without impacting performance or triggering false positives. Many vendors promise out-of-the-box protection, but independent test organizations provide the rigorous, real-world evaluation required to see if any platform can truly achieve this without the benefit of custom tuning.


AV-Comparatives just published the first half-year report of its


[2026 Business Security Test](https://av-comparatives.org/tests/business-security-test-2026-march-june/)


, covering the March–June 2026 test period. Across 16 enterprise solutions tested on fully patched Windows 11, Elastic Security was the only vendor to achieve a 100% malware protection rate while landing in the top tier for real-world protection and earning AV-Comparatives Approved Business Product Award. At the heart of this result is Elastic Defend, the native endpoint protection solution inside Elastic Security. Here is what the numbers say and why they turned out the way they did.


## Elastic is the only vendor with 100% in the Malware Protection Test


The Malware Protection Test simulates the scenario every business eventually faces: malware that's already on the disk or arrives over the LAN or on a removable drive, rather than straight off a website. AV-Comparatives ran 1,000 recent malware samples against every product, scanning on access and then executing anything that slipped through, so behavioral defenses had a chance to react.


Elastic came out on top, being the only vendor with a perfect score as stated by AV-Comparatives:


Elastic Security posted a perfect 100% score in our Malware Protection Test, the top result across the entire field this cycle.


###### Andreas Clementi, CEO, AV-Comparatives


AV-Comparatives Malware Protection Test (March 2026) Results


The gap between 99% and 100% may look small on paper, but it isn't; at enterprise scale, that one-in-a-hundred is the sample that becomes an incident, a ticket queue, and, in the wrong case, a breach. And Elastic reached it with zero false alarms on common business software, which is exactly the balance the test is designed to reward.


## Elastic tied at the top for the Real-World Protection Test


The Real-World Protection Test is the harder one, simulating what a business user actually runs into while browsing like malicious URLs, drive-by exploits, and socially engineered downloads. It also lets every layer of the product step in at any stage: URL blocking, download inspection, behavioral analysis, and memory protection. The test was run continuously from March through June 2026 across 400 test cases and is the closest test simulating the real-world scenarios inside a lab.


Elastic Security achieved the highest score and blocked 399 of 400 threats — a 99.8% protection rate alongside two other vendors.


AV-Comparatives Real-World Protection Test (March–June 2026) Results


Taken together, the two protection tests tell a consistent story: Elastic Security caught everything thrown at it in the malware test and all but one case in the real-world test, placing it above every other enterprise vendor in the field, including the names most organizations already have on their shortlist.


## A note on false positives


Alert fatigue is real, and protection scores only mean something when they're paired with accuracy. AV-Comparatives is rigorous about testing both. Elastic recorded zero false alarms on common business software in both tests, which is the metric that matters most for day-to-day operations and a hard requirement for certification. On less common and non-business files, Elastic landed in the "High" false-positive band and was flagged alongside other vendors for above-average false positives in the Real-World Protection Test.


That aggressive prevention is exactly why our protection numbers are so high. While it might flag the occasional uncommon or non-business file, we review those cases quickly and adjust our rules. For teams that regularly run unusual internal tooling, it's a known dimension worth tuning. And


[Elastic's centralized policy management](https://www.elastic.co/docs/solutions/security/configure-elastic-defend/configure-an-integration-policy-for-elastic-defend) ,


[trusted applications](https://www.elastic.co/docs/solutions/security/manage-elastic-defend/trusted-applications) lists, and


[endpoint exceptions](https://www.elastic.co/docs/solutions/security/manage-elastic-defend/elastic-endpoint-exceptions) are the tools to help and support through the process.


## Elastic is within the lines for the Performance Test


The Performance Test measures how much a product slows down everyday work like copying files, launching apps, browsing, and installing software on deliberately low-end hardware (a Core i3 with 8 GB of RAM). Elastic Security posted a total impact score of 24.2, sitting comfortably under the 40-point threshold required for certification with a Procyon office-productivity score of 90.8 against a clean-system baseline of 100. That is a solid result for a comprehensive security solution running on a modest machine, proving that the best protection doesn’t need to sacrifice performance.
