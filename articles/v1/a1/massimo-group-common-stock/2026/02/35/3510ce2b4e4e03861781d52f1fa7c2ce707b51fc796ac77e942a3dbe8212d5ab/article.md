# All-Season Fleet at Midstates Rendezvous

**PHOENIX, AZ** — On January 28, 2026, **Massimo** showcased its most advanced lineup to date at the **Midstates Winter Rendezvous** . The event highlighted a shift toward premium, climate-controlled utility, featuring the new **T-Boss 560** **UTV** and the **MVR HVAC Pro Series** golf carts.


### Key Highlights:


-


**T-Boss 560 UTV:** Features a powerful 33HP engine and a fully enclosed, factory-installed HVAC system. Designed for extreme environments, it offers comfortable seating for two passengers.


-


**MVR HVAC Golf Carts:** These neighborhood electric vehicles (NEVs) come standard with integrated heating and air conditioning, breaking the seasonal barrier for golf courses and residential communities.


-


**Year-Round Utility:** Massimo’s in-house HVAC technology aims to eliminate "fair weather" limitations, providing a consistent environment for work or recreation regardless of outside temperatures.


A Massimo representative noted that these models represent a significant step in "engineering-driven product development," with plans to expand the Sentinel series throughout 2026.
