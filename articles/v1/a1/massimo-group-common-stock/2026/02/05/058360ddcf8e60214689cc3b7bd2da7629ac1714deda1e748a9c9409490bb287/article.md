# NextGen Carts at 2026 PGA Show

**ORLANDO, FL** — On January 22, 2026, **Massimo Group (NASDAQ: MAMO)** took center stage at the PGA Show to unveil its second-generation **MVR HVAC Pro Series** . The Texas-based manufacturer is redefining the "fair-weather" reputation of golf carts by introducing fully enclosed cabins with integrated heating and air conditioning.


### The New Lineup


Massimo presented two primary models designed for year-round utility:


-


**MVR HVAC Pro:** Aimed at golfers and neighborhood users, featuring an automotive-style interior, premium seating, and a quiet 48V electric drivetrain.


-


**MVR HVAC Cargo Max Pro:** A commercial workhorse equipped with a heavy-duty dump bed, designed for grounds maintenance and fleet use in extreme weather.


### Innovation & Performance


The series isn't just about comfort; it incorporates Massimo’s new **AI-driven software** for intelligent system optimization and fleet management.


-


**Range:** Up to 45 miles on a single charge.


-


**Power:** 48V 5kW motor with available Lithium-Ion battery upgrades.


-


**Versatility:** 12 inches of ground clearance and 14-inch wheels to handle diverse terrain beyond the fairway.


By eliminating seasonal limitations, Massimo aims to help golf courses and commercial operators maintain peak productivity through summer heat and winter chill alike.
