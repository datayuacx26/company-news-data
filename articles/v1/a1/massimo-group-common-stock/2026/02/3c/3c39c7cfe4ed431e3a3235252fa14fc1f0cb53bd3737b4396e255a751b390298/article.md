# All-Weather Comfort at AIMExpo 2026

**ANAHEIM, CA (Jan 27, 2026)** — At this year's AIMExpo, **Massimo Group** made waves by unveiling a new generation of climate-controlled vehicles designed to bridge the gap between rugged utility and automotive comfort.


The presentation focused on two flagship categories:


-


**Sentinel Series UTVs:** These fully enclosed, heavy-duty UTVs feature factory-installed **heating and air conditioning (HVAC)** . Powered by an up to 1500cc engine, they allow operators to tackle extreme environments—from desert heat to winter snow—within a sealed, comfortable cabin.


-


**MVR HVAC Pro Golf Carts:** Expanding their electric lineup, Massimo introduced fully enclosed golf and utility carts. These models cater to gated communities and commercial campuses, offering a premium, weather-proof experience previously unavailable in standard cart platforms.


By integrating HVAC systems directly into their 2026 models, Massimo is moving beyond "fair weather" recreation. The move signals a strategic shift toward high-value, feature-rich units that offer year-round utility for both work and play.
