# Scouting spirit at moore outdoor expo

**SPRINGFIELD, MO**


- The Moore Outdoor Expo recently concluded its successful run, culminating in a significant contribution to the Ozark Trails Council of Scouts. Among the event's highlights was the generous donation of a T-Boss 550 UTV by Massimo Motors, a move that helped the fundraiser exceed its goals, bringing in over $9,000 for the local scouting organization.


Attendees of the expo, held in Springfield, MO, had the opportunity to view the rugged and versatile T-Boss 550, showcasing its capabilities for outdoor adventures. The UTV served as a key prize, generating considerable excitement and participation in the fundraising efforts.


"Massimo Motors is proud to support the Ozark Trails Council and the valuable work they do in shaping young leaders," said a Massimo Representative, "We believe in fostering a love for the outdoors, and the Scouting program plays a vital role in that."


The funds raised will directly benefit the Ozark Trails Council, supporting their various programs and initiatives that serve young people across the region, teaching them essential life skills, promoting character development, and encouraging outdoor exploration.


Organizers expressed their sincere gratitude to Massimo Motors for their impactful donation, noting that the T-Boss 550 was a major draw for attendees and significantly contributed to the fundraising success. The over $9,000 raised will empower the Ozark Trails Council to continue providing enriching experiences for local Scouts.
