# Luel raises $31.2M seed round led by General Catalyst and Lightspeed to build the global ground truth infrastructure for human data

Luel, the global ground truth infrastructure for human data, today announced it has raised $31.2 million in seed funding — one of the largest seed rounds in Y Combinator's history — to scale the platform that pays people around the world to turn their daily lives into AI training data. The round was led by General Catalyst and Lightspeed Venture Partners. During the raise, the company turned down three acquisition offers. Additional backers include Paul Graham, Orange Collective, Human Capital, Mo Amdani, and executives from Microsoft, xAI, Apple, and the Meta board.


## The data wall


Frontier AI development is running into a data wall. As labs push beyond the limits of publicly available internet content, they increasingly depend on specialized, high-quality human data — the kind that traditional data vendors are too slow, too inflexible, or too narrow to deliver. The global market for AI training data is projected to reach $4.8 trillion by 2033, and the demand is for real-world human behavior captured at scale, across languages, environments, and modalities the open internet cannot provide. Luel was built to close that gap.


## How Luel works


Luel operates a worldwide contributor network — hundreds of thousands of people across almost 100 countries — who are paid to record, upload, and license slices of their everyday lives. AI labs and enterprises submit a dataset specification covering modality, scenario, device requirements, and quality rules. Luel matches vetted contributors, runs every submission through a proprietary quality-assurance pipeline, and delivers the finished dataset quickly. Every approved submission ships with full provenance, and new dataset campaigns can be spun up in under 24 hours.


Active and recent projects span egocentric robotics video (gemstone carving, cooking, household chores, warehouse operations), computer-use data, stereo-separated and under-served multilingual speech for voice AI, and large imagery datasets. Luel can collect any human action, verify it, clear the rights, and ship it.


Within months, Luel has processed over 1 million submissions through its quality-assurance pipeline, runs 40+ active dataset campaigns at any given time, and works with customers that include generative AI labs, robotics companies, major social platforms, universities, hospitals, banks, and ML research groups. The company's business model compounds across three revenue streams: exclusive, bespoke datasets built to spec with custom annotations; an off-the-shelf catalog that can be re-licensed to additional buyers at no new collection cost; and third-party listings, where external labs post their own datasets on Luel's marketplace for a revenue share. Each delivery grows the catalog, and the catalog grows the margin.


## Where the funds go


The new funding will be used to scale the contributor network from hundreds of thousands into the millions, subsidize the collection of strategically important datasets, and deepen Luel's investment in research. The company is pursuing data-supply partnerships with labs building the most advanced AI systems in the world, and growing a dedicated team focused on next-generation models.


## The founders


Luel was founded in 2025 by William Namgyal, CEO (18), and Inigo Lenderking, COO (19). The two met in 2019 on the professional gaming circuit — William in the Bay Area, Inigo in Europe — and built a friendship entirely over the internet while earning money online as young teenagers. That experience — ordinary people earning real income through digital tasks — became foundational to how Luel works today.
