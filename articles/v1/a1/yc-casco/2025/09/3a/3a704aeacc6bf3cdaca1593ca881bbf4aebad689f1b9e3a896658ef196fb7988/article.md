# Casco Becomes Gold Sponsor of OWASP AI Exchange to Advance AI Security


Written by


[Rene Brandel](https://linkedin.com/in/renebrandel)


on


Mon Sep 15 2025


**Casco is proud to announce that we have become a Gold Sponsor of the[OWASP AI Exchange](https://owaspai.org/) .** This sponsorship reflects our mission to make AI systems resilient against evolving security threats while supporting a global community dedicated to defining what secure AI should look like.


## Security at the Core


AI agents and applications don't exist in isolation. They interact with APIs, databases, and infrastructure that were never designed with large language models in mind. This mix of deterministic and non-deterministic behavior creates new attack surfaces. An agent might overstep permissions, expose sensitive data through prompt injection, or chain together actions in ways that bypass existing security controls. Our research includes findings from[real-world AI agent vulnerabilities](https://casco.com/blog/we-hacked-ycombinator-agents) and[MCP tool poisoning attacks](https://casco.com/blog/mcp-tool-poisoning) .


Casco addresses these challenges with **autonomous red-teaming for AI systems** . Our platform continuously tests applications, agents, and supporting infrastructure against realistic attack scenarios. Findings are validated by human experts and delivered with remediation steps mapped to frameworks such as SOC 2, NIST’s AI RMF, and the EU AI Act. This allows organizations to stay ahead of adversaries while moving quickly to deploy AI features.


## Why OWASP AI Exchange


Within OWASP, the[AI Exchange](https://owaspai.org/) is becoming a central hub for AI security knowledge and collaboration. The AI Exchange's workstreams and publications complement OWASP's broader projects—such as the **OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications** —so that practitioners have practical guidance to secure AI in production. By supporting the OWASP AI Exchange, Casco is joining a community of researchers, engineers, and policymakers working to close the gap between AI adoption and AI security.


## A Shared Mission


> At Casco, we believe security should be effortless. Supporting the OWASP AI Exchange is a natural extension of our mission. By contributing to this community, we can help advance the practices that keep AI systems secure and resilient.

Through this sponsorship, Casco will contribute insights from real-world security testing while aligning our platform with the latest community-driven standards. The result is a stronger, more unified approach to AI security — one that empowers every innovator to build with confidence.


## Looking Ahead


This marks the beginning of a deeper engagement with the OWASP community. Casco is committed to sharing research, participating in discussions, and helping advance global AI security practices. For us, it's about supporting the community in **building a future where AI is secure by design** .
