# CrewAI + Casco


Written by


[Rene Brandel](https://linkedin.com/in/renebrandel)


on


Wed May 28 2025


Today, I’m thrilled to announce[CrewAI](https://crewai.com/) , the leading multi-agent platform, as Casco’s customer. We're proud that CrewAI has chosen Casco to further enhance the security of its platform, a decision that underscores their deep commitment to providing a secure and trustworthy environment for their users and enterprise clients.


> Security is such a corner piece at CrewAI, and Casco is the obvious security solution for AI systems. The team impressed me from the get go with the high standard they hold for security. As we empower the creation of production multi-agent crews and automations, we needed a partner with deep expertise in the unique security challenges these systems present. Casco's specialized focus on agentic AI security gives us the confidence to innovate rapidly and securely.


[João (Joe) Moura](https://www.linkedin.com/in/joaomdmoura/)


CEO at CrewAI


CrewAI is at the forefront, empowering developers and businesses to create sophisticated autonomous AI agents, and they're widely recognized for their innovative, enterprise-ready solution. As AI becomes more integral to how businesses operate, the need for robust security isn't just important—it's paramount. The complex nature of AI agentic systems brings unique security challenges that demand expert-led strategies to protect data and ensure everything runs smoothly. CrewAI’s own security-focused design philosophy, which emphasizes building with enterprise security in mind, truly resonates with us at Casco. CrewAI’s decision to work with Casco speaks volumes about their dedication to the highest security standards.


At Casco, our mission is to empower builders like CrewAI to innovate confidently. That's why we help companies confidently deploy AI agents and applications by simulating attacks on their AI systems, identifying vulnerabilities, and providing guided remediation. Our approach of using sophisticated attack simulation supervised by human experts, helps companies like CrewAI build and scale their innovative platforms with enhanced protection for their AI infrastructure. This collaboration underscores our shared dedication to a secure technological future.

## About Casco


I founded Casco with Ian Saultz, both of us ex-AWS AI & Security, because we saw the critical need to secure the rapidly evolving landscape of AI agents and applications.


At Casco, we specialize in evaluating AI agents and applications for security, safety, and accuracy. We help companies by simulating attacks on their AI systems, identifying vulnerabilities, and providing guided remediation to detect and mitigate security threats for AI agents in real-time. Our mission is to empower builders to innovate confidently with secure AI systems.


## About CrewAI


CrewAI is the leading multi-agent platform that empowers developers to build sophisticated AI agentic teams. Its framework is designed for creating autonomous AI agents tailored to any scenario, rapidly becoming a standard for enterprise-ready AI automation and fostering the future of intelligent collaboration.
