# Better together: Vouch Insurance and Casco


Written by


[Rene Brandel](https://linkedin.com/in/renebrandel)


on


Sun Jun 08 2025


Today, I'm excited to annouce a partnership between[Vouch Insurance](https://vouch.us/) and Casco. Vouch Insurance customers can get a 20% discount for annual pentests with Casco. Casco customers are eligible to receive up to 5% off coverages through Vouch.


## How to activate this perk


1. Go to your[Vouch Insurance customer portal](https://app.vouch.us/loginportal/send)
2. Select **Partner Offers & Discounts** .
3. Search for "Casco".
4. Select "Redeem Offer Now".


> Having seen firsthand the cyberthreats companies face, we believe robust security shouldn't be a barrier to innovation but a springboard for confident growth. That's why we're excited about working with Casco, empowering companies with proactive defenses like comprehensive pentesting to help them build the future, securely.

## About Casco


I founded Casco with Ian Saultz, both of us ex-AWS AI & Security, because we saw the critical need to secure the rapidly evolving landscape of AI agents and applications.


At Casco, we specialize in evaluating AI agents and applications for security, safety, and accuracy. We help companies by simulating attacks on their AI systems, identifying vulnerabilities, and providing guided remediation to detect and mitigate security threats for AI agents in real-time. Our[supervised pentesting service](https://casco.com/supervised) delivers[high-quality penetration tests](https://casco.com/blog/what-is-a-high-quality-penetration-test) that meet enterprise and insurance requirements. Our mission is to empower builders to innovate confidently with secure AI systems.
