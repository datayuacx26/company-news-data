## What is CREST?


[CREST](https://www.crest-approved.org/) (Council of Registered Ethical Security Testers) is an international not-for-profit accreditation and certification body that represents the technical information security industry. CREST accreditation is widely recognized by regulators, enterprises, and governments as a mark of quality for security testing providers.


## Why CREST Matters


CREST accreditation ensures that Casco meets rigorous standards across several dimensions:


- **Technical Competence** : Our security engineers demonstrate proven expertise through CREST examinations and ongoing professional development
- **Ethical Standards** : We adhere to a strict code of conduct governing how we handle sensitive information and conduct security assessments
- **Quality Processes** : Our penetration testing methodologies follow industry best practices with documented procedures and quality controls
- **Insurance and Liability** : CREST-accredited companies maintain appropriate professional indemnity insurance


For our clients, this means confidence that their security assessments are conducted by qualified professionals using proven methodologies.
