# How We Hire


Written by


[Rene Brandel](https://linkedin.com/in/renebrandel)


on


Sun Jan 18 2026


Our mission is to make all software effortlessly secure. Today, software is not secure and security is not effortless.


This is a complex problem but our interview process is simple.


The process:


1. 30 min interview with[Rene](https://linkedin.com/in/renebrandel) (Founder & CEO)
2. 30 min interview with[Ian](https://linkedin.com/in/iansaultz) (Founder & CTO)
3. 5-day paid work trial


- Work alongside our team day-to-day
- Ship real features to customers
- This is your opportunity to see if we are a mutual cultural fit!


We will debrief as a team after your work trial.


You get a decision within 24h.


Qualities we look for:


- Customer obsession
- Ownership
- Bias for Action
- Craftsmanship
- Transparency
