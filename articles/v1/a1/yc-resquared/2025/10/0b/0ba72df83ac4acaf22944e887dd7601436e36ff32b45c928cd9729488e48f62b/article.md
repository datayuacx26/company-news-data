When you are the owner and the dealmaker, time is stretched thin.


You need a way to create conversations with local operators **without piling on extra work.**


That is where[Collin Goslin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/collin-goslin-14b98576) , a Michigan-based owner of three neighborhood strip centers, landed. He was unsure whether Resquared would make a difference.


A year later, his pipeline told a different story.


## **Before Resquared: depending on brokers meant uneven focus**


Brokers had competing priorities, and the smaller spaces were not getting consistent attention.


*“Relying on brokers to bring us, it’s just…you never know what their interest is. They can’t spend enough time on your center. They have other clients. **And Resquared really helped us out, and just added people to our database** .”*


## **After Resquared: 1,000+ leads**


Landlords often ask if local business owners will actually reply. Collin’s experience shows they do.


*“I’ve gotten like a **thousand or more leads** . And concrete people I’ve spoken to with serious interest, it’s been **around 250** . So it’s been good.”*


That’s enough interest to keep his calendar packed: around **20 tours a week** and a steady cadence of 1–2 LOIs.


Instead of hoping someone else pushes his listings, Collin now builds direct relationships with local operators.


### **Data credibility matters**


*“They reply. For the most part, these small businesses are not paying for a million domains or aliases. **So they got one \[email domain, that is in Resquared\]** .”*


That direct line means he can book tours quickly and keep momentum, especially with mom-and-pop tenants who prefer simple, human outreach.
