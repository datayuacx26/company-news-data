## **5 Reasons Cold Emails Fail**


### **1. Lack of Personal Touch**


Mass emailing without personalization is a surefire way to be ignored. It’s not enough to just drop in the business name, you need to show that you understand their specific challenges or goals.


**Fix it:** Do some quick research. Mention something recent about their business or highlight a pain point they’re likely facing. Make it feel like the email was written just for them.

### **2. Bad Subject Lines**


Your subject line is your first impression. If it doesn’t grab attention, your email won’t even get opened.


**Common mistakes:**


- **Too generic:** "Quick Question" (Lacks context or value)
- **Overly pushy:** "Revolutionary Solution Inside!" (Sounds like spam)
- **Too long:** "Want to learn how our platform can transform your business operations?" (Gets cut off on mobile)


**Fix it:** Keep it short, specific, and benefit-driven. Aim for under 60 characters.


What works well for me is creating lists based on both the company’s industry and location. This lets me craft subject lines that feel personalized and relevant, like:


- Space available for pizza shops in Downtown Tampa
- Looking for a bookkeeping service in South Philly?
- Best social media tools for beauty salons in Miami Beach


### **3. Missing Value Proposition**


Nobody cares about your features, they care about the outcome. Instead of saying, "Our platform uses AI," try "We help teams save 10+ hours a week with our AI."


**Fix it:** Know your[value proposition](https://www.re2.ai/post/how-to-write-compelling-emails) . Highlight how your solution helps them save time, increase revenue, or solve a pressing challenge.


### **4. Unclear Next Steps**


A vague or overwhelming call-to-action (CTA) can kill your email’s momentum.


**Common mistakes:**


- Giving too many options
- Asking for too much time
- Being too vague


**Fix it:** Use a low-friction CTA like:


- Can I send over a quick case study?
- Can I share a short video that shows how this works?
- Is this something you’d be interested in?
- Would it be the world’s worst idea to chat more about this?


### **5. You’re Getting Caught in[Spam Filters](https://www.re2.ai/post/how-to-avoid-spam-filters)**


Even if your email is perfectly written, it won’t matter if it never reaches the inbox.


**Common triggers:**


- Adding images or attachments to the first email
- Using overly promotional language (e.g., “guaranteed results,” “100% free”)
- Not warming up your domain or sending from a brand-new address


**Fix it:**


- Keep your first message plain-text and lean
- Avoid attachments or embedded images
- Warm up your sending domain before launching campaigns


Even if you’ve warmed up your domain, always use a separate domain for cold email campaigns. Never send from your primary business domain.

Keep it short, aim for 2-3 brief paragraphs.[Here are some templates](https://landing.re2.ai/cold-email-templates-prospect-local-businesses?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=backlink&utm_campaign=why-your-cold-emails-arent-getting-responses) you can use.

### **How many follow-ups should I send?**


Most industries agree that it takes an average of **8 touchpoints** to convert a prospect, but this number can vary significantly depending on your audience and niche.


I aim to follow up across **multiple channels** to increase visibility and engagement. Here's a simple multi-channel follow-up sequence I recommend:


- **Day 1:** Send the first email.
- **Day 2:** Make the first call. If they don’t answer, leave a voicemail.
- **Day 3:** Send a DM on their social media.
- **Day 4:** Send the second email.
- **Day 5:** Call again.
**Day 6:** Send the third email and follow up with an SMS (mention that you’ve also reached out by email).
- **After a few days, final step:** Make one last call (leave a voicemail if there's no answer) and send a “breakup” or “walk-away” email.


Finally, add the prospect to a nurture list so they receive future updates, new features, or relevant content about your product or service.


### **Should I include links or attachments?**


Avoid attachments to prevent getting caught in spam filters. Only send links or attachments after the lead has replied.
