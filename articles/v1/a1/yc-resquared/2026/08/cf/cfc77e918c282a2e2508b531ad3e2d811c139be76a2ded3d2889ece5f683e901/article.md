## What is commercial real estate software?


Commercial real estate software is any tool that helps CRE teams find, win, and manage business. It spans several categories: prospecting and lead generation, CRM, listing and marketing, virtual tours, lease management, and data and analytics.


Most teams do not run on a single product. They combine a few tools into a connected stack, so a lead found in one place flows into the systems that nurture and close it.


## What is a tech stack in Commercial Real Estate?


A tech stack is a combination of technologies such as systems, tools, frameworks, software, and programs used to build and operate a digital product or service.


For commercial real estate, a tech stack usually means **software as a service (SaaS)** that contains all the main features needed to prospect, nurture, and convert leads. For example, lead generation, email marketing, CRM, analytics, and more.


Your tech stack could include the software you use to **generate leads** , the tool for **email and social media marketing** to connect with them, and the software you use to generate **reports** about your results for your team.


Ideally, you should use fewer but more complete software solutions. In this article, we will explore popular software and tools in these main categories:


- **Lead generation**
- **CRM** (Customer Relationship Management)
- **Virtual Tour**
- **Lease Management**
- Commercial Real Estate **Listing**
- **Data & Analytics**
- **Marketing** : social media, email marketing


The value of a stack comes from connection, not volume. A prospecting tool that does not feed your CRM is just a list, and a CRM that never sees your outreach is just a database. Pick fewer tools that talk to each other, so a contact entered once updates everywhere and no lead slips through.

## CRM Software for Commercial Real Estate


CRM (Customer Relationship Management) software helps you in all the processes of managing leads and customers. It involves creating contacts for your leads, nurturing them, converting them into customers, and then nurturing your customers to retain them.


### Salesforce


[Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/) is a **cloud-based CRM** that helps leasing teams manage customer relationships and sales pipelines.


Key features:


- Highly configurable
- Offers **robust reporting, analytics, and integrations** with 3rd party tools
- Likely the **most expensive option**
- Often requires a strong learning curve


### Hubspot


[Hubspot](https://www.hubspot.com/) is another popular CRM platform that offers a range of marketing and sales tools, including email marketing, social media management, and landing page creation.

## Virtual Tour Software


Virtual tour software can help leasing teams **create 3D or 360-degree virtual tours of commercial properties** , allowing potential tenants to view the space remotely.


While most commercial real estate professionals realize that most prospects don't sign a lease on a virtual tour alone, virtual tours are still an essential marketing tool in today's market.


### Matterport


[Matterport](https://matterport.com/) is a leading virtual tour software that offers 3D scanning technology and virtual reality integration. It allows leasing teams to create **immersive, interactive virtual tours** that can be shared easily with potential tenants.


Key features:


- Most advanced camera technology
- Wide range of customization options (e.g., add annotations, floor plans, etc.)


### Cupix


[Cupix](https://www.cupix.com/) is a web-based virtual tour software that allows leasing teams to create 3D virtual tours using just a standard 360-degree camera. Its platform offers advanced features such as **tagging and measurement tools** , as well as integrations with other software tools.


Key features:


- Relatively inexpensive compared to Matterport (a good option for smaller firms)

## FAQ


### What is the best commercial real estate prospecting software?


It depends on who you sell to. Teams selling to local and brick and mortar businesses tend to favor tools with a large verified business database plus built in outreach, like[Resquared](https://www.re2.ai/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=backlink&utm_campaign=software-tools-commercial-real-estate-tech-stack) . Teams focused on property owner research often use Reonomy or listing platforms like LoopNet and Crexi for sourcing.


### What should a commercial real estate tech stack include?


At minimum a prospecting or lead generation tool, a CRM, and a way to run outreach and reporting. Depending on your role you may add listing, virtual tour, and lease management software. The goal is fewer tools that integrate cleanly, not more tools that do not talk to each other.


### How much does commercial real estate software cost?


Pricing ranges widely. General CRMs like HubSpot offer free and low cost tiers, while enterprise platforms like Salesforce and specialized CRE suites run into the thousands per year. Prospecting and data tools are usually priced per seat or per contact volume.
