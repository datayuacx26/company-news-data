# How Active8 bypassed gatekeepers and got 77 leads from one email campaign


[Yasmim Mayumi](https://re2.ai/authors/yasmim-mayumi) ·


February 4, 2026


### **The Situation**


Active8 is a fitness studio offering activities like MAT, personal training, and Pilates. Their goal was to reach trainers who wanted a space to teach private or group classes.


Victoria was responsible for demand generation and setting up Active8’s campaign in Resquared.


*“Without Resquared, Active8 would have been prospecting by Googling gyms or focusing on people in the industry that we knew.”*


The bigger issue was visibility into the right people.


*“ **There was no way for us to identify any sort of trainers** , especially because a lot of the trainers that we’re reaching out to or we want to work with are already working at a gym and have a schedule or a class there.”*


This made traditional, in-person outreach difficult and often unproductive.


*“Typically, if you’re going into a gym to try and not necessarily poach, but work with their trainers, going in can be met with a lot of resistance. **There is always a gatekeepe** r.”*


### **The Challenge**


Active8 needed a way to reach trainers directly, without relying on walk-ins, referrals, or manual research.


*“ **With Resquared, we didn’t have that same resistance to there being a gatekeeper for us to talk** to the trainers. We were able to reach out to them a lot more directly instead.*


*We’re a really nimble team. There also wouldn’t be a way for us to easily track the leads that were generated when we have such a high volume of responses.”*


### **The Strategy**


Victoria leaned into AI-assisted outreach to keep the process simple and scalable.


*“I leaned really heavily into the AI process. I was offering a space for rent to people who are trainers. I reached out to the owner’s name. I mentioned who I am in the studio. **I mentioned our boutique and referenced the location, but I didn’t give an exact location** .”*


She shared the tip of not giving all the details in the first email, so prospects are more likely to reply asking for more information.


*“ **I didn’t give an exact location so that I could get some response or inquiry** on it rather than them just searching it up.”*


To personalize outreach at scale, Active8 used AI Snippets to tailor each message to the recipient’s business.


*“I utilized the AI snippet, which worked really well here. **It was able to give me some good insight into the different studios** .”*


Follow-up also played a major role in performance. A simple 3-step email sequence allowed Active8 to stay top of mind without adding manual work.


*“Looking at my responses, the majority of my responses and leads that I generated came from the third step of the sequence.*


*We see a lot of traction in this continued follow-up, which before Resquared, wouldn't have been possible for Active8. We’re a really nimble team. So no one was able to maintain these conversations.”*


### **The Results**


The campaign delivered immediate results, driven by Victoria’s execution and the effectiveness of AI-powered outreach.💛


*“ **I’m at 77 leads, 189 responses.** We’ve had multiple people visit the space now. We’re in the midst of conversing with different contract opportunities for renting out the space.”*
