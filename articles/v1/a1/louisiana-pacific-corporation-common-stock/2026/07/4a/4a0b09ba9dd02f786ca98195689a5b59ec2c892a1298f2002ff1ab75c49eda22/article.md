# LP’s Commitment to Safety On and Off the Jobsite

At LP, safety is more than just workplace practice, it’s a responsibility we carry each and every day. As June is Safety Month, we want to acknowledge the importance of creating safer environments, strengthening safety awareness, and continuing to prioritize the well-being of our people and the communities.


LP takes pride in the safety of our employees, partners, and customers. Safety is central to everything LP does. Across our facilities and jobsites, we strive to promote a culture where safety is part of every decision and every action.


Watch a short video on top safety tips and what it means to a few of our employees at LP!


**A Shared Commitment to Safety**


Safety Month is an opportunity to recognize the ongoing efforts that help protect our people and communities. By staying aware, communicating, and prioritizing responsible practices, LP continues to build a culture centered around safety.


At LP, safety is not limited to the month of June, it’s a commitment each person upholds every day. This is how we build better products, stronger relationships, and safer communities all around.
