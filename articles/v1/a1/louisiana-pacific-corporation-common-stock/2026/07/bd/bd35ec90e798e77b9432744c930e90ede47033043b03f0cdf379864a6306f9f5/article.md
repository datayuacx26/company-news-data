# Preparing Homes for Summer Fire Risks with LP® FlameBlock® Fire-Rated Sheathing and LP BurnGuard™ FRT OSB

Summertime brings warmer temperatures, outdoor activities, and more time spent enjoying the spaces around our homes. However, the season can also bring increased fire risks due to dry conditions, outdoor cooking, fire pits and other heat sources. Building materials designed with fire resistance in mind can play an important role in helping improve a home’s resilience.


At LP, we develop products that help builders and homeowners create durable structures. Two product solutions - LP® FlameBlock® Fire-Rated Sheathing and LP BurnGuard™ FRT OSB – are designed to provide added protection where fire performance matters.


**Understanding Summer Fire Risks**


Summer fire hazards can come from many different sources, including:


-


Outdoor grills


-


Cooking equipment


-


Fire pits


-


Lightning strikes


-


Dry vegetation


-


Increase wildfire risk in certain regions


-


Construction or maintenance activities involving heat


Fire prevention practices are essential, but the materials used to build your structure or home can also impact how it may perform when exposed to fire.


**LP® FlameBlock® Fire-Rated Sheathing**


Combining flame-spread and burn-through resistance. LP® FlameBlock® Fire-Rated Sheathing is an APA-certified OSB with Pyrotite® coating, a noncombustible cementitious material. It’s evaluated to help meet code requirements for fire-resistant construction that elevates your build.


When it comes to defending from fire, LP® FlameBlock® Fire-Rated Sheathing combines proven flame-spread and burn-through resistance in one panel.


-


Meets Code Requirements


-


Exceed Expectations


-


Affordable Price Point


It is designed for exterior wall and roof applications and is proven to slow down the spread of flames with a 20-year limited warranty. LP® FlameBlock® Fire-Rated Sheathing is a component of many 1- and 2-hour fire-resistance-rated wall assemblies. Find your specific assembly using the assembly name, construction type, required rating, or stud type[here](https://www.lpcorp.com/products/panels-sheathing/fire-rated-osb-sheathing/assemblies)


**LP BurnGuard™ FRT OSB**


LP BurnGuard™ FRT (Fire-Retardant-Treated) OSB is an OSB solution with the fire-retardant blended within the strands for protection through the thickness of the panel for consistent performance. It installs just like regular OSB and meets the same testing and code requirements as FRT plywood, with a 20-year limited warranty.


-


Flame Spread Resistance


-


One Warranty


-


Easy to Install


The LP BurnGuard™ panel is the industry’s first FRT OSB panel, as well as LP’s newest addition to our fire solution portfolio that will work alongside LP® FlameBlock® Fire Rated Sheathing.
