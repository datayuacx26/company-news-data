# 8 Ways to Boost Your Home's Curb Appeal

When it comes to first impressions, your home's exterior sets the tone. Whether you're planning a full renovation or looking for weekend projects with big visual impact, a few thoughtful updates can dramatically increase curb appeal. From fresh color palettes to upgraded siding, these ideas can help create an exterior that's both welcoming and stylish.


**1. Refresh Your Siding**


If your siding is faded, worn, or outdated, replacing it can completely transform your home's appearance. Siding covers most of your home's exterior, making it one of the biggest opportunities to boost curb appeal.

-


**Sand Dunes** for a warm, inviting look


-


**Garden Sage** for a nature-inspired feel


-


**Quarry Gray** for timeless versatility


-


**Summit Blue** for classic coastal charm


-


**Cavern Steel** for a modern exterior


With 16 curated color choices, homeowners can easily create a cohesive exterior design while enjoying the lasting beauty, durability, and additional benefits of a factory-finished product.


**2. Incorporate Board & Batten Accents**


Board and batten siding continues to be one of the most popular exterior design trends. Even adding it to a front gable, porch accent, or entry feature can create architectural interest and a custom-built appearance.


Consider:


-


Highlighting a front-facing gable


-


Creating contrast with a different siding profile


-


Mixing horizontal lap siding with vertical board & batten


LP® SmartSide® ExpertFinish® Board & Batten Siding works especially well on farmhouse, Craftsman, and contemporary homes.

**4. Upgrade Your Front Door**


Your front door is one of the easiest places to add personality and create a focal point.


Popular front door colors include:


-


Deep navy


-


Sage green


-


Charcoal black


-


Warm stained wood tones


-


A bold accent color, such as sunny yellow or muted coral


Pairing a bold front door with complementary siding and trim colors can instantly make a home feel more welcoming.


**5. Add Layers of Landscaping**


Landscaping remains one of the most cost-effective ways to improve curb appeal.


Focus on:


-


Fresh mulch


-


Seasonal flowers


-


Decorative planters


-


Well-maintained shrubs


-


Defined garden borders


Even small landscaping updates can frame your home and make the entire property feel more polished.


**6. Refresh Trim and Exterior Details**


Sometimes the smallest details make the biggest difference.


Consider updating:


-


Trim


-


Shutters


-


Porch columns


-


Fascia


-


Soffits


LP® SmartSide® ExpertFinish® Trim can help create clean lines and crisp contrast that highlights architectural features and gives a home a finished appearance.


**7. Update Outdoor Lighting**


Exterior lighting can dramatically improve the look and feel of a home.


Look for opportunities to add:


-


Statement sconces


-


Pathway lighting


-


Pendant lights above porches


-


Garage lighting accents


Updated fixtures add character during the day and create warmth and visibility after dark.


**8. Clean and Maintain Key Exterior Surfaces**


Not every curb appeal upgrade involves a major project. Sometimes, routine maintenance can make the biggest difference.


Refresh your exterior with:


-


Washing siding


-


Cleaning walkways and driveways


-


Touching up paint


-


Washing windows


-


Trimming overgrown landscaping


Routine maintenance helps every exterior feature look its best and protects your investment over time.


**The Bottom Line**


Boosting curb appeal doesn't always require a major renovation. Simple improvements like updating landscaping, refreshing exterior colors, incorporating architectural details, and maintaining key surfaces can dramatically transform a home's appearance.


For homeowners looking to make the biggest visual impact, siding remains one of the most effective upgrades because it combines color, texture, and design flexibility in one project. Whether you prefer a costal-inspired look, a timeless and classic aesthetic, or the warmth of natural wood tones, the right exterior color palette can help create a home that feels fresh, inviting, and uniquely yours.
