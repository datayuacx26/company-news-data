# Introducing the LP® Everyday Series™ Products: A Solution for Shed Flooring and Roofing

As demand grows for flexible, backyard living spaces, builders are looking for efficient ways to deliver high-impact projects with repeated success.


That’s why LP is launching the Everyday Series™ Flooring and Roofing product line, designed specifically for shed construction. The product line includes three colored flooring options and a radiant barrier roof sheathing product. Each product features engineered wood strand technology and a durable overlay that is pressed into the board during manufacturing.


Overall, these products focus on supporting efficiency without compromising performance.


**What Does the LP® Everyday Series™ Portfolio Include?**


The LP®Everyday Series™ portfolio brings together key materials builders rely on in the shed space:


**1. LP® SilverTech® Everyday Series™ Roof Sheathing**


This roof sheathing product is APA rated, and features a silver radiant barrier overlay to enhance heat reflection and keep the shed interior bright and cool.


-


8ft length, 4ft width, and a 0.418-inch thickness


-


10-year prorated limited warranty


**2. LP® ProStruct® Everyday Series™ Flooring**


This product is available in three color options and features a tongue-and-groove edge profile for easy installation on a shed build. It is designed and rated for interior shed flooring only.


-


8ft length, 4ft width, and a 0.578-inch thickness


-


1/5-year prorated limited warranty


**Color options include:**


-


LP® ProStruct® Gray Flooring


-


LP® ProStruct® Dark Brown Flooring


-


LP® ProStruct® Two-Tone Flooring


These colors elevate the interior look of shed, helping builders offer the homeowners a polished, finished appearance.


Every product in the Everyday Series™ line is developed with real-world demands in mind. The LP® Everyday Series™ line offers high performing products at a reasonable price point, making it an easy choice for builders wanting to upgrade from standard OSB or plywood.
