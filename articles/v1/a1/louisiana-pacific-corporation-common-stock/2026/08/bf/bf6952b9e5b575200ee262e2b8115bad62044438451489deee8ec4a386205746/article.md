# Curb Appeal Month: The Perfect Time to Refresh Your Home's Exterior

August is officially curb appeal month, making it the ideal time for homeowners to take a fresh look at their home’s exterior. Curb appeal is really about creating a home that feels welcoming, reflects your personal style, and provides a sense of pride every time you pull into the driveway.


The exterior of your home works hard year-round. It protects your family from the elements, sets the tone for your property's overall appearance, and often determines the first impression guests have when they visit. Whether you've lived in your home for six months or twenty years, investing in your home's exterior can make it more enjoyable, more comfortable, and easier to maintain.


Whether you're planning a complete exterior transformation or looking for practical upgrades that make a noticeable impact, investing in your home's curb appeal can provide both immediate enjoyment and long-term benefits. From landscaping improvements to updated siding colors, August offers the perfect opportunity to create an exterior you'll love coming home to.


**Start With the Feature that Makes the Biggest Impact: Siding**


When most people think about curb appeal, they picture colorful landscaping, a new front door, or updated lighting. While those upgrades certainly help, siding covers the largest portion of your home’s exterior and often has the greatest influence on its overall appearance.


If your siding is faded, worn, cracked, or simply outdated, replacing it can dramatically transform your home's look. Modern siding options provide homeowners with more opportunities to personalize their home's exterior without compromising performance.

**Choosing Colors That Feel Timeless**


Exterior color trends come and go, but the most successful curb appeal projects often focus on colors that feel timeless rather than trendy. Warm neutrals, soft grays, earthy greens, and rich blues continue to be popular because they complement a variety of architectural styles and blend naturally with surrounding landscapes.


LP® SmartSide® ExpertFinish® Trim & Siding offers a collection of professionally curated colors designed to help homeowners achieve a polished look. When selecting exterior colors, consider the surrounding environment, roof color, stone or brick accents, and landscaping. The goal is to create a balanced look that feels intentional and inviting.

**Pair Exterior Improvements With Simple Curb Appeal Enhancements**


While siding often creates the most dramatic transformation, combining it with a few smaller projects can maximize results.


-


**Refresh Landscaping:** Fresh mulch, trimmed shrubs, flowering plants, and defined landscape beds can instantly create a more polished appearance. Landscaping frames the home and enhances exterior features.


-


**Upgrade Exterior Lighting:** Path lights, porch lights, and accent lighting can improve safety while creating a welcoming atmosphere after dark.


-


**Modernize the Front Entry:** Replacing outdated hardware, updating house numbers, or adding seasonal containers can provide a quick visual refresh without requiring a major renovation.


-


**Clean Outdoor Surfaces:** Power washing walkways, patios, porches, and driveways can make an immediate difference in the overall appearance of the property.


These smaller upgrades often work best when paired with larger exterior improvements, helping create a cohesive look across the entire property.


**August Is the Perfect Time to Evaluate Your Exterior**


As summer begins to wind down, August offers an ideal opportunity to evaluate your home's exterior before cooler weather arrives.


Walk across the street and view your home from a visitor's perspective. Do the colors still reflect your style? Are there areas that appear worn or outdated? Could the exterior feel more welcoming or cohesive?


Often, homeowners become so accustomed to their home's appearance that they stop noticing opportunities for improvement. Curb Appeal Month serves as a reminder to revisit those areas and explore upgrades that can make a meaningful difference.
