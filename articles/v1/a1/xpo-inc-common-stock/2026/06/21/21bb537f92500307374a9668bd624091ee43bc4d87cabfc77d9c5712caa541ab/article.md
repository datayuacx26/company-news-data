# XPO Provides North American LTL Operating Data for May 2026

## XPO Provides North American LTL Operating Data for May 2026


June 3, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.xpo.com/node/21816/pdf)


GREENWICH, Conn., June 03, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[XPO](http://www.xpo.com/) (NYSE: XPO), a leading provider of freight transportation in North America, today reported certain preliminary LTL segment operating metrics for May 2026. LTL tonnage per day increased 0.5%, as compared with May 2025, attributable to a year-over-year increase of 3.3% in shipments per day and a decrease of 2.7% in weight per shipment. Actual results for May 2026 may vary from the preliminary results reported above.


**About XPO**


XPO, Inc. (NYSE: XPO) is a leader in asset-based less-than-truckload (LTL) freight transportation in North America. The company’s customer-focused organization efficiently moves 16 billion pounds of freight per year, enabled by its proprietary technology. XPO serves 55,000 customers with 594 locations and 37,000 employees in North America and Europe, and is headquartered in Greenwich, Conn., USA.
