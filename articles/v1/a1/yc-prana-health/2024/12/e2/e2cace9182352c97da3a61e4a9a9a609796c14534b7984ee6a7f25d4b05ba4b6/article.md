# Telehealth vs Urgent Care: Which Should You Choose?

When you're sick but it's not an emergency, should you visit urgent care or try telehealth? This guide helps you make the right choice for your situation—and your wallet.


## Cost Comparison


- Telehealth visit: $39-75 (Prana Health: free AI consultation, $39 flat for a physician visit)
- Urgent care visit: $150-300 without insurance
- Emergency room: $500-3,000+


## When to Choose Telehealth


Telehealth is ideal for:


- Cold and flu symptoms
- Allergies and sinus infections
- Skin rashes (via video)
- UTI symptoms
- Mental health concerns
- Prescription refills
- Follow-up appointments
- Minor injuries you can show on camera
- Health questions and advice


## When to Choose Urgent Care


Visit urgent care when you need:


- X-rays or lab tests
- Stitches for cuts
- IV fluids for dehydration
- In-person examination
- Splints or casts
- Complex wound care


For emergencies like chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, or stroke symptoms, go to the ER or call 911 immediately.
