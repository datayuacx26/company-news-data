# From the Engine Room: What’s Under the Accelerant Hood

Here’s a look inside the engine room: how we’re building, scaling, and evolving the technology that powers the[Accelerant Risk Exchange](https://accelerant.ai/overview/) .


The Accelerant Risk Exchange sits at a critical point in the specialty insurance value chain, connecting underwriting expertise with capital in a more direct and structured way. Rather than operating through a series of disconnected handoffs,[Members](https://accelerant.ai/underwriters-and-mgas/) and[Risk Capital Partners](https://accelerant.ai/risk-capital-partners/) participate in a shared environment where information, performance, and activity are continuously visible.


This model changes how the market operates in practice. It enables closer coordination between underwriting and capital, creates a more consistent view of portfolio performance, and establishes a foundation for faster, more informed decision-making across both sides of the ecosystem.


## Replatforming the Specialty Insurance Value Chain


[Accelerant’s technology](https://accelerant.ai/the-technology/) vision is centered on building the infrastructure that supports better underwriting and capital allocation across the specialty market.


Our proprietary technology, unique data infrastructure, and expert insights combine to create differentiated underwriting results. This provides Members with the tools to grow profitably while giving Capital Partners access to curated, high-quality risk portfolios. At the center of this ecosystem are three key components: Data & AI, the Platform, and the Expert Service Model.


## Leveraging Reliable, Intelligent Data


[D](https://accelerant.ai/resources/the-data-driven-underwriter-thriving-in-an-ai-enhanced-risk-world/)[ata is the fuel](https://accelerant.ai/resources/the-data-driven-underwriter-thriving-in-an-ai-enhanced-risk-world/) that powers the Accelerant Risk Exchange. Accelerant has built a repeatable, reliable pipeline that[ingests massive amounts of data](https://accelerant.ai/resources/a-functional-data-structure-built-for-whats-next/) from Member bordereaux files, TPAs, and third-party sources. Our platform then standardizes, validates, and structures this data, surfacing trusted, consumable insights for Members, Capital Partners, and internal teams alike.


To date, we’ve processed over 140 million rows of data across more than 600 insurance products—representing a vast number of individual data points across policies, claims, and exposures.


This level of scale allows performance to be analyzed at a far more granular level, giving Members clearer visibility into trends, segments, and drivers of loss. Increased volume, higher quality, and greater transparency are creating a new foundation for smarter, faster decision-making in which no data is left behind.


## A Platform Built for Members—with Members


Accelerant has implemented significant features based directly on Member feedback, including:


- Premium, claims, and loss performance analytics with advanced segmentation
- Custom reporting and actionable portfolio insights
- Community tools for member collaboration and knowledge sharing
- Continued expansion of benchmarking tools
- Online bordereaux submission
- Rich exposure data and property TIV analysis
- Streamlined planning tools
- Underwriting referral automation
- AI-powered risk scoring and high-risk aggregation detection


We’re particularly excited about the last two items on the list. These enhancements were built API-first to integrate seamlessly into Members’ existing systems and further streamline workflows.


Traditionally, referrals are managed across emails, spreadsheets, and disconnected systems, making it difficult to track status or maintain consistency. But with fully automated underwriting referrals, Members can submit, track, and collaborate on referrals digitally, reducing email back-and-forth and improving transparency and turnaround times.


Additionally, AI-powered risk scoring was developed by our data science team, combining Member data and third-party data to assign risk grades to new submissions. This feature helps Members assess risks more quickly and with greater context.


With such a rich feature set, Members use the platform not just as a passive repository, but as a core part of their day-to-day operations. They are using it for everything from real-time loss performance analytics to open claims tracking and premium growth reporting, making it easier to manage their businesses.
