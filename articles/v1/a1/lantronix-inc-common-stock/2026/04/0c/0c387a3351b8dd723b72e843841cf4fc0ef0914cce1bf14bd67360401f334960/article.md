# Lantronix CFO Brent Stringham Nominated for Orange County Business Journal CFO of the Year Award

April 2, 2026


### Lantronix CFO Brent Stringham Nominated for Orange County Business Journal CFO of the Year Award


**IRVINE, Calif., April 2, 2026 —**[Lantronix Inc.](http://www.lantronix.com/?utm_source=newswire&utm_medium=press-release&utm_campaign=stringham-cfo-nomination) (Nasdaq: LTRX), a global provider of Edge AI and Industrial IoT solutions that power NDAA-compliant unmanned systems, critical infrastructure and resilient enterprise networks, today announced that its chief financial officer Brent Stringham has been nominated for the prestigious 2026 CFO of the Year Award sponsored by the Orange County Business Journal (OCBJ).


The OCBJ CFO of the Year awards program honors outstanding financial professionals at Orange County companies who drive innovation, operational success and sustainable growth. Winners will be announced on May 12, 2026, during the awards ceremony held at the Irvine Marriott.


“We are incredibly proud that Brent has been recognized with this nomination,” said Saleel Awsare, president & CEO of Lantronix. “For more than a decade with Lantronix, Brent has played a pivotal role in strengthening our financial foundation and positioning the company for long-term success. His leadership in strategic cost management, operational efficiency and disciplined financial planning has been instrumental to improving our profitability and financial discipline.”


A seasoned financial executive, Stringham has served Lantronix in senior financial roles since 2012. He currently leads the company’s global finance organization and brings deep institutional knowledge and a strong track record across both public and private companies. Prior to joining Lantronix, he held finance leadership positions at Iteris Inc., Netlist Inc. and Ernst & Young, where he obtained extensive experience in financial planning, reporting and corporate governance.


The OCBJ CFO of the Year nomination highlights Lantronix’s continued commitment to strong financial stewardship and leadership excellence as the company expands its global footprint in next-generation Edge AI, connectivity and intelligence solutions.


**About Lantronix**


Lantronix Inc. Its comprehensive portfolio of hardware, software and services powers applications from secure video surveillance and intelligent utility infrastructure to resilient out-of-band network management. By bringing intelligence to the network edge, Lantronix helps organizations achieve efficiency, security and a competitive edge in today’s AI-driven world.
