# Lantronix to Showcase AI-Powered Compressor Optimization Platform at AICD Show

April 8, 2026


### Lantronix to Showcase AI-Powered Compressor Optimization Platform at AICD Show


***Subscription-based Kompress.ai by Lantronix AI-powered platform delivers up to 30 percent energy savings and scalable recurring revenue***


**IRVINE, Calif., April 8, 2026 —**[Lantronix Inc.](http://www.lantronix.com/?utm_source=newswire&utm_medium=press-release&utm_campaign=kompress-ai) (Nasdaq: LTRX), a global provider of Edge AI and Industrial IoT solutions that power NDAA-compliant unmanned systems, critical infrastructure and resilient enterprise networks, today announced it will present its new[Kompress.ai](https://www.lantronix.com/kompress/?utm_source=newswire&utm_medium=press-release&utm_campaign=kompress-ai) by Lantronix, a turnkey, brand-agnostic AI-powered platform for industrial air compressor performance optimization, at booth Number 007 at the Association of Independent Compressor Distributors[AICD Show](http://www.aicd.org/) , which will be held April 19–21 at the Rosen Center in Orlando, Fla.


Developed in collaboration with[Vodafone IoT](https://iot.vodafone.com/) , a global leader in managed IoT connectivity services, Kompress.ai by Lantronix integrates Lantronix Intelligent-Edge hardware, AI-driven cloud analytics and Vodafone IoT’s secure global cellular connectivity, ensuring reliable data transmission from industrial sites located anywhere in the world. Designed to transform how companies manage and optimize industrial air compressors of any make, model or age, the solution assists operators to cut energy costs, reduce downtime and meet sustainability and ESG compliance goals, all without needing to rip-and-replace existing compressor infrastructure. Commercial availability is expected in the United States, Canada and Europe this fall.


**Unlocking Value in a $27B+ Market**


One of the most energy-intensive systems in industrial operations, compressed air systems account for more than 10 percent of global industrial electricity use, representing a significant opportunity for cost reduction and emissions reduction.


For service companies struggling with technician shortages and increasing customer demands, Kompress.ai by Lantronix:


- Cuts emergency service calls
- Reduces diagnostic time through remote insights
- Enables proactive maintenance scheduling
- Extends compressor life across entire mixed-brand fleets
- Provides a scalable path to recurring subscription revenue


For industrial operators, service companies and OEM partners, Kompress.ai by Lantronix delivers:


- **Energy Efficiency Gains:** Demonstrated reductions in energy consumption of up to 30 percent through AI-driven optimization
- **Reduced Downtime:** Predictive analytics that minimize unplanned outages and extend equipment life
- **Lower Total Cost of Ownership:** Provides notification for required maintenance when needed, optimizing service calls while managing service costs across compressor fleets.
- **Fleet-Agnostic Deployment:** Rapid integration across mixed-brand, legacy and modern systems
- **Compliance Readiness:** Automated support for ISO 50001 and ESG reporting requirements


**Purpose-Built for Industrial IoT and Sustainability**


Kompress.ai by Lantronix supports Modbus, Canbus analog & digital I/O and direct sensor integration, enabling rapid installation on any compressor within 30 minutes or less, resulting in modernization of fleets with hundreds of legacy units in weeks, not years. All hardware and connectivity are NDAA-compliant, with end-to-end encryption and secure cloud data storage.


“Kompress.ai by Lantronix represents a strategic expansion of our Edge AI portfolio, expanding Lantronix into one of the world’s largest yet most under-optimized industrial asset classes,” said Mathi Gurusamy, chief strategy officer at Lantronix. “For compressor distributors and service companies, Kompress.ai enables recurring‑revenue service models and improved efficiency through brand‑agnostic, data‑driven monitoring. With rapid cellular deployment and continuous remote insights, teams can address technician shortages while delivering immediate ROI and energy savings for customers.”


**Investor Takeaways: Scalable Growth and Recurring Revenue**


Kompress.ai by Lantronix represents a major expansion of Lantronix’s recurring revenue model and critical infrastructure strategy, building on its success in the generator segment.


- **Expands Addressable Market in Critical Infrastructure** sectors requiring secure, resilient connectivity, including utilities, energy, defense, transportation and emergency response.
- **Accelerates Edge Networking Platform Adoption** building on Lantronix’s portfolio of industrial gateways, PoE platforms and Edge AI networking solutions designed for distributed infrastructure.
- **Supports Recurring Revenue Opportunities** with remote management, secure networking and scalable deployments to create long-term lifecycle revenue opportunities through upgrades and expanded deployments.


- **Creates Large Installed Base Opportunity** based on significant retrofit potential across brownfield compressor fleets globally.
- **Reinforces Strong Market Tailwinds** driven by industrial automation, digitization and tightening energy regulations.


- **Positions Lantronix for Multi-Year Infrastructure Modernization Trends** as governments and infrastructure operators worldwide are investing in resilient networks to support automation, unmanned systems and digital transformation initiatives.


“Together, Lantronix and Vodafone are helping optimize performance and unlock recurring revenue in energy-intensive industries,” Gurusamy added. “This collaboration strengthens the strategic positioning of Lantronix in the rapidly expanding market for industrial connectivity, Edge AI infrastructure and resilient networking for critical systems.”
