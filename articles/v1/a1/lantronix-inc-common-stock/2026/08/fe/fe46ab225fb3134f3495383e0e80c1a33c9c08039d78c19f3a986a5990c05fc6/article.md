### Lantronix to Report Fiscal 2026 Fourth Quarter Results on Aug. 26, 2026


**IRVINE, Calif., Aug. 13, 2026** —


[Lantronix Inc.](https://www.lantronix.com/?utm_source=newswire&utm_medium=press%20release&utm_campaign=FY26Q4%20earnings%20announcement%20call) (NASDAQ: LTRX), a global provider of Edge AI and Industrial IoT solutions that power NDAA-compliant unmanned systems, critical infrastructure and resilient enterprise networks


, today announced it will release financial results from its fiscal 2026 fourth quarter, ended June 30, 2026, after the close of the market on Wednesday, Aug. 26, 2026


.


Management will host an investor conference call and audio webcast at 1:30 p.m. Pacific Time (4:30 p.m. Eastern Time) on Aug. 26, 2026. To access the live conference call, investors should dial 1-844-802-2442 (U.S./Canada) or 1-412-317-5135 (international) and indicate they are participating in the Lantronix fiscal 2026 fourth-quarter call. The webcast will be available simultaneously via the investor relations section of the


[Company’s website.](https://www.lantronix.com/investor-relations/?utm_source=newswire&utm_medium=press%20release&utm_campaign=FY26Q4%20earnings%20announcement%20call)


Investors can access a conference call replay starting at approximately 4:00 p.m. Pacific Time on Aug. 26, 2026, on the


[Lantronix website](https://www.lantronix.com/investor-relations/earnings-releases/?utm_source=newswire&utm_medium=press%20release&utm_campaign=FY26Q4%20earnings%20announcement%20call) . A telephonic replay will also be available through Sept. 2, 2026, by dialing 1-855-669-9658 (U.S./Canada) or 1-412-317-0088 (international) and entering passcode 3642439.
