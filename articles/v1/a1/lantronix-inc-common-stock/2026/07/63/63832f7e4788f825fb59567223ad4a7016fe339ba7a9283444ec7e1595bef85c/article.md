## **Why Wi-Fi 6?** ****


#### **1. Unmatched Performance in Noisy Environments**


Industrial settings are rife with RF interference from machinery, sensors, and legacy equipment. Wi-Fi 6’s


**OFDMA (Orthogonal Frequency-Division Multiple Access)**


and


**MU-MIMO**


technologies allow


**simultaneous data transmission to multiple devices** , reducing latency and improving throughput by


**up to 4x**


compared to Wi-Fi 5.
**For OEMs:**


Fewer dropped connections, smoother real-time data flows, and


**better performance in crowded RF spaces** .


#### **2.** **Future-Proof Scalability**


Industrial IoT deployments often start small but scale rapidly. Wi-Fi 6 supports


**up to 4x more connected devices** per access point, thanks to


**improved spectrum efficiency** and


**BSS Coloring** (which reduces co-channel interference).


**For OEMs:** Design once, deploy everywhere


**without re-engineering** for device density.


#### **3.** **Lower Latency for Time-Sensitive Applications**


From robotic arms to predictive maintenance, industrial systems require


**sub-10ms latency** . Wi-Fi 6’s


**Target Wake Time (TWT)** and


**enhanced QoS** ensure deterministic performance, even in high-traffic networks.


**For OEMs:**


**Real-time control** and


**predictive analytics** without jitter or lag.


#### **4.** **Enterprise-Grade Security (Without the Headache)**


Security is the #1 barrier to industrial IoT adoption. Wi-Fi 6


**mandates WPA3** , delivering


**stronger encryption (GCMP-256)** ,


**protection against brute-force attacks** , and


**enhanced open-network security** .


**For OEMs:**


**Compliance-ready** (CRA, NDAA) with


**Lantronix InfiniShield™** secure boot, FOTA updates, and role-based access control


**built in** .


#### **5.** **Power Efficiency for Battery-Powered Devices**


Wi-Fi 6’s


**TWT** lets devices


**schedule wake-up times** , drastically reducing power consumption for sensors, handheld scanners, and remote monitors.


**For OEMs:**


**Longer battery life** , fewer maintenance cycles, and


**lower total cost of ownership (TCO)** .
