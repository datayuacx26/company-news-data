# Lantronix NTC-550 Series Industrial IoT Gateways Accelerate Industrial Digital Transformation

May 26, 2026


### Lantronix NTC-550 Series Industrial IoT Gateways Accelerate Industrial Digital Transformation


**The rugged 5G NTC-550 gateway with Wi-Fi 6 and serial connectivity replaces complex wired builds, reducing installation time and costs**


**IRVINE, Calif., May 26, 2026 —**[Lantronix Inc.](http://www.lantronix.com/?utm_source=newswire&utm_medium=press-release&utm_campaign=ntc-550) (Nasdaq: LTRX), a global provider of Edge AI and Industrial IoT solutions that power NDAA-compliant unmanned systems, critical infrastructure and resilient enterprise networks, today announced its new[NTC-550 Series](https://www.lantronix.com/products/ntc-550-series/?utm_source=newswire&utm_medium=press-release&utm_campaign=ntc-550) ruggedized industrial IoT gateways, which combine ultra-fast 5G connectivity and networking with the robust performance of Wi-Fi®, serial and I/O interfaces for use in the rapidly growing market of remote, challenging and off-grid environments. This announcement underscores Lantronix’s position as a leading global provider of cutting-edge connectivity solutions for enterprise and industrial IoT markets, including fast-growing industries such as mobile network operators and utilities.


“Critical infrastructure operators are increasingly challenged to deploy secure, high-performance connectivity in remote and difficult environments,” said Mathi Gurusamy, chief strategy officer at Lantronix. “Lantronix’s ruggedized industrial gateways create a powerful, easy-to-deploy platform that delivers resilient connectivity in locations that traditional networks cannot reach.”


**Capitalizing on the Accelerating Global Shift Toward Wireless Industrial Infrastructure**


With carrier certification, global approvals and a disruptive price point, the NTC-550 Series empowers enterprises to eliminate costly Ethernet infrastructure — potentially thousands of dollars per drop — while retaining the high-speed, low-latency performance traditionally associated with wired networks.


In addition, by addressing key pain points such as high deployment costs, long installation timelines, limited mobility and the need to support a high density of connected end points, the NTC-550 solution opens new revenue streams across private 5G, edge computing and industrial automation markets. Its flexible, future-ready design supports a wide range of use cases, enabling customers to scale efficiently while reducing total cost of ownership.


**Lantronix NTC-550 Series Industrial IoT 5G Gateways for Connectivity Anywhere**


Lantronix’s new NTC-550 Series features 5G NR Release 16 SA/NSA with automatic 4G LTE fallback that keeps networks connected virtually anywhere. It features multiple versatile interfaces, including Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth, GNSS, serial, I/Os and ignition sensing for diverse applications. Its high-speed flexible networking is based on Gbps WAN plus multiple-gigabit Lan ports for fast, scalable deployments.


Built for harsh environments, its rugged metal housing and wide temperature tolerance range make it ideal for unmanned and/or mobile operations. Delivering cost-effective enterprise performance, Lantronix’s NTC-550 Series features advanced routing, a strong firewall and remote management at an affordable price point, making it an ideal solution for enterprises, domestic and global systems integrators, mobile network operators, OEMs and infrastructure vendors.


**Key Investor Takeaways**


Lantronix’s new NTC-550 Series industrial IoT gateways strengthen the strategic positioning of Lantronix in the rapidly expanding market for industrial connectivity, Edge AI infrastructure and resilient networking for critical systems.


Key functions:


- **Expands Addressable Market in Critical Infrastructure.** Targets growing demand across utilities, energy, defense, transportation and emergency response sectors requiring secure, resilient connectivity.
- **Accelerates Edge Networking Platform Adoption.** Builds on Lantronix’s portfolio of industrial gateways, PoE platforms and Edge AI networking solutions designed for distributed infrastructure.
- **Creates Additional Channel Opportunities.** Expands Lantronix’s reach into European and international critical infrastructure markets.
- **Supports Recurring Revenue Opportunities.** Creates long-term lifecycle opportunities with Percepxion™ remote management, secure networking and scalable deployments through upgrades and expanded deployments.
- **Positions Lantronix for Multi-Year Infrastructure Modernization Trends.** Supports the resilient-network investments of governments and infrastructure operators worldwide to support automation, unmanned systems and digital transformation initiatives.
