# Transition Networks PoE Switches with DMS Integrate with Siemens Siveillance™ Video Client

March 29, 2022


- [Former Transition Blog Archive](https://www.lantronix.com/blog/category/former-transition-blog-archive/)


### Transition Networks PoE Switches with DMS Integrate with Siemens Siveillance™ Video Client


The integration of our switches with Device Management System (DMS) into the[Siemens Siveillance™ Video Client](https://new.siemens.com/global/en/products/buildings/security/video-surveillance/video-management-siveillance-vms.html) provides time-saving features for security integrators and network administrators. DMS provides the tools necessary to establish and document a baseline deployment, automatically discover, remotely configure, diagnose and troubleshoot attached IP-powered devices (PDs). These tools ensure lower overall cost, less downtime, and easier management and maintenance of the entire PoE+ network.


[Watch a video](https://www.lantronix.com/resources/videos/sm8tat2sa-manage-transition-networks-switches-siemens-siveillance-vms-walk/) on how our DMS integrates into Siemens Siveillance.
