# Uplogix Now Lantronix

September 12, 2022


- [General](https://www.lantronix.com/blog/category/general/)


### Uplogix Now Lantronix


## Same People. More Products. More Solutions.


Together, we provide customers with innovative solutions and support to help them realize their network’s full potential. Uplogix’s complementary high-end product offering is a timely addition to the Lantronix product portfolio.


Uplogix’s[LM83X](https://www.lantronix.com/products/lm83x/) and[LM80](https://www.lantronix.com/products/lm80/) series of products like Lantronix portfolio of OOBM products are designed for the challenges of remotely managing enterprise network infrastructure while reducing cost, complexity and downtime.


Former Uplogix customers will also have access to the Lantronix[SLC](https://www.lantronix.com/products/lantronix-slc-8000/) and[EMG](https://www.lantronix.com/product-families/emg/) series of console managers and our centralized management software solution,[ConsoleFlow](https://www.lantronix.com/consoleflow/) . For end-to-end network connectivity solutions Lantronix also offers a variety of IoT devices, such as[switching](https://www.lantronix.com/products-class/network-switches/) and[media conversion](https://www.lantronix.com/products-class/media-converters-device-servers/) , and end points like[Gateways](https://web.archive.org/web/20220212160527/https:/www.lantronix.com/products-class/external-gateways/) ,[modems](https://web.archive.org/web/20220212160527/https:/www.lantronix.com/products-class/industrial-modems/) ,[trackers](https://web.archive.org/web/20220212160527/https:/www.lantronix.com/products-class/telematics-gateways/) , and more!


Now with Lantronix, we also offer extensive LTE solutions with our[Connectivity Services](https://web.archive.org/web/20220212160527/https:/www.lantronix.com/connectivity-services/) .
