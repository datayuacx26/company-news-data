# Transition Networks Now Lantronix

October 11, 2021


- [General](https://www.lantronix.com/blog/category/general/)


### Transition Networks Now Lantronix


## Same People. Same Products. More to Offer.


FOX3 Tracker


Together, we provide customers and partners with innovative solutions and products to help them realize their network’s full potential. Our broader IoT connectivity solution will take any network to the next level.


Our network connectivity solutions, such as[switching](https://www.lantronix.com/products-class/network-switches/) and[media conversion](https://www.lantronix.com/products-class/media-converters-device-servers/) , have now expanded to include a variety of IoT end points like[Gateways](https://www.lantronix.com/products-class/external-gateways/) ,[modems](https://www.lantronix.com/products-class/industrial-modems/) ,[trackers](https://www.lantronix.com/products-class/telematics-gateways/) , and more!


Our embedded solutions, like[NICs](https://www.lantronix.com/products-class/nics/) ,[CWDM](https://www.lantronix.com/products-class/cwdm-modules/) , and[SFPs](https://www.lantronix.com/products-class/sfps/) , have also expanded to include[SOM development kits](https://www.lantronix.com/products-class/embedded-solutions/) ,[Embedded RF Modules](https://www.lantronix.com/products-class/embedded-wireless-modules/) , and[Engineering Design Services](https://www.lantronix.com/professional-services/) .


SLC Series


Remote Environment Management has also expanded with our latest[Secure Remote Access (SRA Series) solution](https://www.lantronix.com/products/sra-series/) in addition to the[SLC Series](https://www.lantronix.com/products/lantronix-slc-8000/) for Data Centers, with the[EMG 7500 Series](https://www.lantronix.com/products/lantronix-emg-7500/) and[SLB Series](https://www.lantronix.com/products/lantronix-slb/) for Branch Offices and Remote Sites.


ConsoleFlow


Now with Lantronix, we also offer extensive network management solutions through[ConsoleFlow](https://www.lantronix.com/consoleflow/) as well as LTE solutions with our[Connectivity Services](https://www.lantronix.com/connectivity-services/) .
