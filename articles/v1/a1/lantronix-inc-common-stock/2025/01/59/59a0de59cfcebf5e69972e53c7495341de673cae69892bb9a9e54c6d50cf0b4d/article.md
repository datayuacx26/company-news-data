# NetComm Industrial IoT Now Lantronix

January 10, 2025


- [General](https://www.lantronix.com/blog/category/general/)


### NetComm Industrial IoT Now Lantronix


**Expanding Horizons with Advanced 4G and 5G Technology**


Lantronix accelerates innovation with the acquisition of NetComm’s Industrial IoT portfolio. This strategic move:


- Unlocks cutting-edge 4G and 5G technology.
- Enhances offerings with Gateways, Routers, and Modems.
- Strengthens global reach with a new foothold in Australia and New Zealand.


Adding blue-chip enterprise customers, Lantronix expands its network and unlocks opportunities for cross-selling and collaboration in high-potential markets.


By integrating these advanced solutions, Lantronix is setting a new standard in IoT connectivity, empowering applications in critical infrastructure, asset monitoring, and telecommunications.


New products to the Lantronix portfolio:


1.


Industrial IoT Serial Modem:[NTC-100](https://www.lantronix.com/products/ntc-100-series/)

Rugged 4G Industrial IoT Router:[NTC-140](https://www.lantronix.com/products/ntc-140-series/) ,[NTC-220](https://www.lantronix.com/products/ntc-220-series/)


3.


Rugged 5G Industrial IoT Router:[NTC-500](https://www.lantronix.com/products/ntc-500-series/)
