# Strengthening Financial Industry Network Resilience with Out-of-Band Management

### Strengthening Financial Industry Network Resilience with Out-of-Band Management


### **Enabling Secure Operations Across Data Centers and Branch Offices**


Financial institutions depend on reliable, secure network connectivity to process transactions, deliver digital services, and safeguard sensitive data. With increasingly distributed IT infrastructures and rising cyber threats, maintaining visibility and control across all sites — especially during outages or security incidents — is a top operational priority.


Out-of-Band (OOB) management provides a dedicated, independent pathway for network visibility and control when the production network is unavailable. For over 20 years, banks, credit unions, and financial service providers,[Lantronix OOB management solutions](https://www.lantronix.com/products-class/device-mgmt-oob/) deliver enhanced uptime, faster incident recovery, and stronger cybersecurity defenses across data centers, branches, and remote offices.


#### **Isolated Management for Stronger Cybersecurity**


Managing your network through the same data plane used for business traffic introduces unnecessary risk. If attackers compromise the production network, they may also gain access to critical infrastructure management interfaces.


Lantronix OOB solutions reduce this risk through an Isolated Management Infrastructure (IMI) that separates management traffic from operational traffic:


- **Physical Separation |** Following guidance from the NSA and CISA, Lantronix connects directly to console ports on routers, switches, and firewalls, ensuring true physical segmentation of management access.
- **Reduced Attack Surface |** Isolation prevents threats that affect the production network from reaching the management plane.
- **Hardened Management Path |** The Lantronix **[LM-Series console servers](https://www.lantronix.com/products-class/ai-driven-out-of-band-management/)** run a locked-down Linux OS, preventing unauthorized software installation and protecting stored credentials, keys, and secrets.


This architecture strengthens your cybersecurity posture by preventing lateral movement and protecting the management plane — the foundation of network control.


#### **Secure Access and Granular Control**


For financial networks, enforcing strict access control is essential for compliance and risk mitigation. Lantronix OOB solutions provide advanced security controls that protect against insider threats and credential abuse:


- **Encrypted Management Sessions |** All traffic is encrypted end-to-end, using Reverse SSH and IPsec tunnels.
- **FIPS 140-2 Validated Cryptography |** Lantronix solutions meet federal standards for cryptographic security.
- **Centralized AAA Integration |** Full compatibility with TACACS, RADIUS, and LDAP enables centralized Authentication, Authorization, and Auditing (AAA), with support for multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- **Command-Level Authorization |** Administrators can define granular permissions for each user, down to port- and command-level access — reducing operational risk and improving compliance visibility.


#### **Faster Network Recovery and Automated Operations**


Out-of-Band management is more than a backup plan — it’s a cornerstone of financial network resilience. When outages or incidents occur, Lantronix OOB solutions keep your IT teams connected to critical devices, enabling faster diagnosis and recovery.


- **Resilient Access During Outages |** Maintain secure management access to remote infrastructure even during ransomware events or major network disruptions and serving as an Isolated Recovery Environment (IRE) with config and OS backups stored locally on the console server and backed up in the Lantronix Control Center.
- **AI-Driven Automated Remediation |** The LM-Series integrates rules-based AI to work as an expert system that analyzes console data and can automatically perform pre-approved actions — such as clearing an interface, rebooting a device, or restoring configurations — to minimize downtime.
- **Automatic Rollback and Restore |** Features like Surgical Rollback and Bare Metal Restore allow administrators to revert misconfigurations rapidly or replace failed hardware automatically from the last known-good configuration.


These capabilities help financial IT teams meet stringent uptime and business continuity requirements with confidence.


#### **Continuous Logging and Forensic Visibility**


Accurate, tamper-proof records are essential for compliance, auditing, and incident response in the financial sector. Lantronix OOB solutions provide complete visibility, even during outages.


- **Comprehensive Session Logging |** Every users’ keystrokes, commands, and configuration changes are captured for full accountability.
- **Independent Data Capture |** Logs and diagnostic data are collected locally on the LM-Series console server and backed-up in the Control Center, ensuring continuity of evidence even when the primary network is unavailable.
- **Layer 1–3 Monitoring |** Continuous state-aware monitoring supports proactive diagnostics and deeper forensic insight into network performance and failures.
