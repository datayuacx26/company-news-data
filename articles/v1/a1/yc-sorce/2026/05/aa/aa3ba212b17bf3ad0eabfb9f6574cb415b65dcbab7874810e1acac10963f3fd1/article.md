# Cook Jobs: Compare Kitchens, Shifts, and Pay

When you search for cook jobs, you need more than a list of open roles. The job has to match your schedule, requirements, location, pay expectations, and the way you actually want to work.


Sorce is building industry-specific job search flows so line cooks, prep cooks, production cooks, and kitchen workers can find better-fit opportunities faster. ## Why this job search matters


Cook roles are durable search targets because experience level, station, shift, and kitchen pace vary widely.


That is why this search should be specific. A plain list of jobs is not enough; you need to know what to compare and what information improves the match.


## What to compare before applying


For cook roles, compare:


- line, prep, grill, or production station
- hourly pay and overtime
- shift and weekend schedule
- menu complexity
- benefits and advancement path


The best role is not always the one with the highest headline pay. A job with better schedule fit, clearer requirements, stronger benefits, or a shorter commute may be the better move.


## What to add to your Sorce profile


A stronger profile helps Sorce filter toward roles that make sense. For cook jobs, include:


- stations worked
- knife skills
- food safety certification
- availability
- preferred kitchen type


This is especially important in restaurant & food service because employers often use specific requirements that do not show up in the title alone.
