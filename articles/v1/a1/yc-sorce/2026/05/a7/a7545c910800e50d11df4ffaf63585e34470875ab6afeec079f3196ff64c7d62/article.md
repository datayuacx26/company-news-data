## What to compare before applying


For deli roles, compare:


- prep vs customer counter work
- opening or closing shift
- food safety requirements
- production volume
- benefits and training


The best role is not always the one with the highest headline pay. A job with better schedule fit, clearer requirements, stronger benefits, or a shorter commute may be the better move.


## What to add to your Sorce profile


A stronger profile helps Sorce filter toward roles that make sense. For deli jobs, include:


- deli or food prep experience
- knife and slicer comfort
- customer service skills
- availability
- food safety training


This is especially important in restaurant & food service because employers often use specific requirements that do not show up in the title alone.
