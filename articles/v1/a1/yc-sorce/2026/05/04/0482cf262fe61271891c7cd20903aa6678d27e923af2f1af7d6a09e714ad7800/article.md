## What to compare before applying


For automotive technician roles, compare:


- flat-rate vs hourly pay
- ASE or OEM certification
- tool requirements
- shop volume
- benefits and training path


The best role is not always the one with the highest headline pay. A job with better schedule fit, clearer requirements, stronger benefits, or a shorter commute may be the better move.


## What to add to your Sorce profile


A stronger profile helps Sorce filter toward roles that make sense. For automotive technician jobs, include:


- vehicle systems experience

- schedule availability
