## Why this job search matters


Engineering listings appeared across manufacturing, utilities, water/wastewater, automation, and design employers.

## What to compare before applying


For engineering roles, compare:


- discipline and license requirements
- field vs office work
- project type
- software tools
- benefits and travel


The best role is not always the one with the highest headline pay. A job with better schedule fit, clearer requirements, stronger benefits, or a shorter commute may be the better move.


## What to add to your Sorce profile


A stronger profile helps Sorce filter toward roles that make sense. For engineering jobs, include:


- engineering discipline
- licenses or EIT status
- CAD or analysis tools
- industry experience
- travel preference


This is especially important in technology / engineering because employers often use specific requirements that do not show up in the title alone.
