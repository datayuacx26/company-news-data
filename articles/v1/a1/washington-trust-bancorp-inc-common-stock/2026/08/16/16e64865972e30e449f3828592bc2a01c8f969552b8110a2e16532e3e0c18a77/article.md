# Washington Trust Supports 2026 Summer Pops at Roger Williams Park

**WESTERLY, R.I.** — The Washington Trust Company is proud to support the Rhode Island Philharmonic Orchestra's 2026 Summer Pops concert at Roger Williams Park, a free community event presented by the City of Providence and the Rhode Island Foundation.


The Summer Pops concert will bring thousands of Rhode Islanders and visitors together for an evening of music, community and celebration at the Temple to Music, one of the state's most iconic outdoor venues. Guests are encouraged to bring lawn chairs and blankets, and food trucks featuring local, regional and international cuisine will be on site throughout the event.


This year’s performance, “Outstanding Overtures,” will feature some of the most recognizable overtures from Broadway, film and classical music, performed by the Rhode Island Philharmonic Orchestra under the direction of audience favorite Troy Quinn. The 69-piece orchestra will present two 40-minute sets.


“Washington Trust is honored to support Summer Pops and help bring this special community tradition to Roger Williams Park,” said **Edward O. ‘Ned’ Handy III, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Washington Trust** . “For generations, music and the arts have brought people together, strengthened communities and enriched quality of life across Rhode Island. We are proud to partner with the City of Providence, the Rhode Island Foundation and the Rhode Island Philharmonic to make this free event accessible to everyone.”


“I’m thrilled that the Rhode Island Philharmonic Orchestra is returning to Roger Williams Park for another incredible summer concert at the Temple to Music,” said **Mayor Brett Smiley.** “This free, family-friendly event has become a cherished Providence tradition, bringing our community together to enjoy world-class music in one of the region’s most beautiful public spaces. I encourage everyone to join us for another memorable evening in the park.”


The event is presented by the City of Providence, the Rhode Island Foundation and Washington Trust, with additional support from the Providence Department of Art, Culture and Tourism, the Providence Parks Department, the Roger Williams Park Conservancy and KLR.


**Event Details**


**What:** Rhode Island Philharmonic Orchestra Summer Pops 2026


**When:** Wednesday, August 5, 2026, at 7 p.m.


**Rain Date:** Thursday, August 6, 2026


**Where:** Temple to Music, Roger Williams Park, Providence, R.I.


**Admission:** Free and open to the public


For more information, visit


www.riphil.org/summer-pops .
