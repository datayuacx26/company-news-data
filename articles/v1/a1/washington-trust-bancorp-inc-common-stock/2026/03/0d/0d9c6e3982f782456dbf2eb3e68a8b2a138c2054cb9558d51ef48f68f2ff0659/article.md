### Washington Trust Bancorp, Inc. Announces Quarterly Dividend


March 19, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s202.q4cdn.com/917433312/files/doc_news/Washington-Trust-Bancorp-Inc--Announces-Quarterly-Dividend-2026.pdf)


WESTERLY, R.I.


, March 19, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- The Board of Directors of[Washington Trust Bancorp, Inc.](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4645459-1&h=3652940628&u=https%3A%2F%2Fir.washtrust.com%2Foverview%2Fdefault.aspx&a=Washington+Trust+Bancorp%2C+Inc.) , (Nasdaq: WASH), today declared a quarterly dividend of 56 cents per share **for the quarter ending March 31, 2026** . The dividend will be paid April 10, 2026 to shareholders of record on April 1, 2026.

("the Corporation), Nasdaq: WASH, is the publicly-owned holding company of The Washington Trust Company ("Washington Trust", "the Bank"), with $6.6 billion in assets as of December 31, 2025. In 2025, Washington Trust reached a milestone of 225 years in operation, marking its commitment to helping the people, businesses, and organizations of New England improve their financial lives.
