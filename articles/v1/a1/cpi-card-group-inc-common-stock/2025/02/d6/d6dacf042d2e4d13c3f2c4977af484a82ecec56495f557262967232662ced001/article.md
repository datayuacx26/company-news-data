# Case Study: CPI worked with First State Bank of Gainesville to double their digital wallet usage in two months

Prior to adopting Push Provisioning, digital wallet usage was minimal. Manual provisioning was introduced in 2019, but over the course of five years, only two percent of the card base had enrolled. In just two months of using Push Provisioning, the number of cardholders using digital wallets has doubled.
