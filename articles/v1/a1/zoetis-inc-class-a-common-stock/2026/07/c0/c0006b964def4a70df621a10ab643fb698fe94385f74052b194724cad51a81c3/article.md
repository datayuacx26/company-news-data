## **Nadia Koziar: ‘Horses and dogs have always been my best friends**


Nadia Koziar always wanted to live on a farm, surrounded by as many animals as possible. In her first job as a sales representative for veterinary products, she began to collect strays. “I’ve always had trouble saying ‘no’ to an animal in need of care. Once I moved to a small farm, my family grew to include three horses, eight dogs, two cats, six chickens and a pygmy goat.”


She says she loves working at Zoetis “since the work that I do directly benefits my pets now and will benefit many more animals in the future. I currently only have three dogs and a horse, but all of them receive Zoetis medications and preventatives.”

## Alexandre Merlo: ‘Surrounded by animals’


“I was raised on a small farm, surrounded by animals. My grandparents, aunts and uncles all had dogs, cats, chickens, horses. Those early experiences with animals drove me to veterinary medicine and, ultimately, to Zoetis.”


Today, he lives in an apartment in Brazil with his family and Rosinha, a 5-year-old Shih Tzu, who can usually be found sleeping with his son or daughter. “Rosinha is my third child.”

## Tracey Williams: In need of more ‘lap space’


Growing up, Tracey Williams did not have any family pets, but all that changed as soon as she was on her own. “I now have nine cats and a dog. I don’t have enough lap space for all my cats, so they love it when I have company over. For me and so many others at Zoetis, this is more than just a job. This is personal.”
