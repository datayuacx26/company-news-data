# Innovators for Animals: Caitriona Fenton

Caitriona Fenton, One Health Operations Senior Manager, underscores the need for collaborative efforts to effectively combat infectious diseases, drawing on her veterinary health background and personal experiences. She expresses excitement about her future at Zoetis, citing the innovative and impactful work the company does in addressing transboundary and emerging infectious diseases, especially in the post-pandemic era.
