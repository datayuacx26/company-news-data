# David Caouette

David joined Immuneering as Vice President, Corporate Communications in July 2026. He is a senior corporate communications leader with expertise in corporate media relations, patient advocacy, executive, financial and crisis communications, as well as issues management. Prior to joining Immuneering, David held communication leadership roles at Pfizer, Seagen, Biogen, City of Hope, Amgen, Merck and Disney. He earned a BA in journalism from the University of Maine and holds a management certificate from the University of Washington.
