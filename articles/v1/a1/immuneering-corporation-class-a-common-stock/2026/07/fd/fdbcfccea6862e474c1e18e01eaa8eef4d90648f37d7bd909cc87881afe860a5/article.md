# Jyoti Joshi

Jyoti joined Immuneering as Director, Quality Systems and Compliance in 2026. She brings more than 14 years of quality assurance and quality systems experience in the biotech industry, including building quality management systems from the ground up. Prior to joining Immuneering , she held quality roles at SpringWorks Therapeutics, Resilience, bluebird bio, Organon Teknika, and Merck. Jyoti holds an M.S. in Environmental Science from the University of North Carolina and a B.S. in Chemistry from Carnegie Mellon University.
