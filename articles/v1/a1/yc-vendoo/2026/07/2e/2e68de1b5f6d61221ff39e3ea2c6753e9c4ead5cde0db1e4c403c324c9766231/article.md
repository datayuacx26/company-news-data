## Manual Crosslisting: The Free (But Slow) Route


If you're new to resale or only have a handful of active listings, manually crosslisting from Poshmark to eBay is completely possible. It costs nothing besides your time


. The basic workflow is straightforward.


### **Downloading Media (Don’t Screenshot)**


Start with the original photos and videos saved on your phone or computer whenever possible.


Avoid taking screenshots of your Poshmark images. Screenshots reduce image quality, crop important details, and often include interface elements that look unprofessional.


This matters even more on eBay because buyers frequently use the


**zoom feature** to inspect stitching, fabric texture, flaws, and tags.


If you no longer have the originals, downloading the uploaded images is still preferable to taking screenshots.


### **Copying Titles & Descriptions**


Next, open your Poshmark listing and copy the title and description. Don’t forget to also copy any hidden fields such as notes or SKU.


Create a new listing on eBay and paste this information into the appropriate fields.


eBay requires additional information that Poshmark does not


. You'll need to review your title, complete item specifics, choose shipping options, and verify category placement before your listing is ready.


💡


**Tip:** On Poshmark, your title and description are “searchable,” meaning the words contained therein help your items to yield in search results. On eBay, however, only the


title and item specific fields


are “searchable.”


Take the time to enter all relevant fields.


### **The Time Cost**


For most clothing listings, manually crosslisting takes approximately 5 to 10 minutes per item


. eBay requires a lot of information and item specifics, which can make each listing feel like it takes


*forever.*


When you really think about it, that means that 20 listings can take several hours. 100 listings might suck up an entire weekend, and 500+ listings could take months.


Time is money


. For anyone running a serious resale business, manual data entry eventually becomes the bottleneck that limits growth.

## Poshmark vs. eBay: Key Listing Differences to Fix


One of the biggest mistakes new sellers make is assuming they can simply


*copy and paste* every field. There’s a little more than that to selling on eBay


Before publishing on eBay, make these adjustments.


- **Strong Titles:** To maximize SEO (search engine optimization). Include important keywords such as brand, item type, size, color, condition, material, style name, or model number whenever space allows. Better titles improve visibility in eBay search results.


- **Item Specifics:** eBay relies heavily on Item Specifics for search placement and filtering. Depending on the category, you may need to complete fields like sleeve length, neckline, inseam, pattern, department, fabric type, vintage status, season, closure, and more. These fields are not optional if you want maximum exposure.


- **Shipping Models:** This is where many new sellers make mistakes. Poshmark uses one prepaid shipping label that the buyer purchases at checkout. eBay requires you to configure shipping yourself. You can choose calculated shipping based on package weight and dimensions, create flat-rate shipping policies, or offer free shipping by building shipping costs into your price. Incorrect shipping settings can reduce profits or discourage buyers.


- **Pricing:** There is a lot more competition on eBay, so it’s


*that much* more important to price your items in consideration of market value. Use the eBay Product Research tool for competitive pricing.


Taking a few extra minutes to optimize these areas usually produces better search visibility than simply duplicating your Poshmark listing.
