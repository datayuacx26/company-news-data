## What are eBay’s Sneaker Fees?


Sneakers are one of the[best selling items on eBay](https://blog.vendoo.co/best-selling-items-on-ebay-for-2024) , but it’s important to clearly understand the fees involved to ensure you’re making a good profit. eBay’s sneaker fees fall into two categories: insertion and final value. Let’s break them down.


### Insertion Fees


eBay’s insertion fee is what sellers pay to list an item on their platform, whether the item sells or not


. It might be easier to think of it as a listing fee. While the fee is usually $0.35 per listing, eBay does give sellers 250 free listings per month.


As a bonus, sneakers with a starting or Buy It Now price of $150 or more have waived insertion fees


, which don't count towards your 250 free listings. But one thing to keep in mind is that fees apply per category. So if you decide to list in two categories, you will pay the insertion fee twice.


### Final Value Fees


When it comes to eBay sneaker selling fees, the final value fee is the one you’ll want to pay the most attention to. That’s because final value fees are the biggest chunk of your eBay fees


. The good news? If you’re selling higher value sneakers ($150+), the fees are lower. The bad news is the fees can be a little tricky to calculate.


**eBay calculates the final value fee based on the total amount of the sale** , which includes:


- Item price


- Shipping charges collected from the buyer


- Handling charges


- Sales tax (if applicable)


The two main factors that impact how much you pay are the sale price of the sneakers and whether you have a store subscription. In addition, there is a per-order fee on top of the final value fee: $0.30 for orders $10 or less, and $0.40 for orders over $10


.


However, if you're selling sneakers priced $150 or more in the Men’s or Women’s Athletic Shoes categories, this per-order fee is waived.


**📝 Note on Authenticity Guarantee Sales:**


Suppose your sneakers qualify for eBay’s Authenticity Guarantee, and you use the free shipping label provided. In that case, eBay does not charge a final value fee on the shipping cost, just on the item price and other applicable fees. More details on this are in the next section.

## eBay’s Authenticity Guarantee: How It Impacts Fees


Image Source:[eBay](https://www.ebay.com/authenticity-guarantee/sneakers)


The Authenticity Guarantee is a free service for eligible sneakers priced at $100 or more


. When an item qualifies, it’s sent to a professional authenticator before being shipped to the buyer. This protects both the buyer and seller and helps prevent counterfeit issues.


The main benefits of this program include a boost in buyer confidence that they are buying authentic sneakers, extra protection for returns and claims, and the ability to stand out in search results with the Authenticity Guarantee badge.


In addition, with this program buyers pay a flat $14.95 shipping fee (U.S. orders), but you don’t pay anything extra


. In fact, the final value fee for the shipping cost is waived for Authenticity Guarantee orders, which ends up saving you money.


### How Does It Work?


If your sneakers are eligible, eBay will automatically add them to the Authenticity Guarantee program. You’ll know your listing qualifies if it has the blue Authenticity Guarantee checkmark in the search results and listing page.


Here’s how it works:


1. You make a sale of an eligible pair of


sneakers.


2. eBay provides a prepaid FedEx shipping label, which you use to send the item to their authentication center.


3. Once the item is received,


it’s typically inspected


within 2 days. During this inspection


the


authenticator:


- Verifies that the sneaker matches the listing title, description, and photos


- Performs a physical authentication check to confirm the item is genuine


4. After inspection, one of two things happens:


- If the sneakers pass, they


are shipped


to the buyer via


2 day


delivery


- If the sneakers don’t match the listing or can’t


be authenticated


, they are shipped back to you and the buyer


is


refunded


**This entire process is 100% free for sellers and is handled by eBay and its authentication partners.**


### Eligibility and Requirements


To qualify for the Authenticity Guarantee program, all of the following must apply:


- **Sneakers are priced at $100+** (Note: eBay has expanded this to include select, highly coveted sneaker models starting at **$75** ).
- **High-Value Protection:** If your sneakers sell for **$750 or more** , eBay automatically upgrades the final delivery to require a signature from the buyer to protect you against "item not received" claims.
- **Buyers pay a flat $14.95 shipping fee** , and eBay provides you with a prepaid FedEx shipping label to ship the shoes to the hub completely free of charge.
- Listing is in the Men’s, Women’s, or Kids' Athletic Shoes categories


- It cannot be marked as customized


- Sold by a


US based

- It cannot be marked as in-store pickup, local pickup, or having a military address.


- Sneakers must be on the list of eligible brands, including Nike, Adidas, Yeezy, Hoka, and more.

### Seller Protections and Benefits


One of the biggest benefits of the Authenticity Guarantee program is the added protection it gives sellers. Once your item is authenticated and shipped, you’re no longer responsible for authenticity-related issues.


You’re also protected from fraudulent returns. If a buyer wants to return their sneakers, the item must first undergo re-authentication to ensure it’s still in the same condition.


On top of that, listings with the Authenticity Guarantee badge tend to sell faster and for higher prices because buyers feel more confident buying from sellers backed by eBay’s program.

## Strategies to Minimize Fees and Maximize Profits Selling eBay Shoes


Want to keep more profit in your pockets? Here are some ways to reduce eBay sneaker fees and, as a result, make your sales more profitable.


### Price Strategically


Remember that a sale at $150 triggers lower fees and waives the per order charge. So if you’re close to the $150 threshold, try bumping your price up and improving your listing to get that higher price.


### Use eBay Store Subscriptions


A Basic Store subscription or higher reduces your final value fee and gives you more free listings each month. For sneakers under $150, you’ll reduce your final value fee from 13.6% to 12.7%, and for sneakers that sell over $150, you’ll reduce your final value fee from 8% to 7%.


### Leverage Free Insertions


All sellers get 250 free listings per month, and sneakers priced at $100 or more don’t count against that limit. Staying within that range can help you avoid extra listing fees.


### Authenticity Guarantee


Listings that qualify for Authenticity Guarantee show an official badge that can boost buyer trust, help your items sell faster, and potentially help them sell for a higher price. It also removes the final value fee on shipping when you use eBay's provided label.


### Offer Competitive Shipping


Avoid overcharging for shipping. When buyers are already spending a lot of money on a pair of sneakers, an inflated shipping price can cause hesitation. By charging a flat fee of $14.95 or similar, your buyer won’t feel like they’re overpaying for shipping.


### Crosslist with Tools


Selling across multiple marketplaces is one of the best ways to maximize your profit. Use a tool like[Vendoo](https://web.vendoo.co/register/) to help you list on additional marketplaces to help your items sell faster by getting them in front of a larger audience.


### Monitor Fee Updates


eBay occasionally updates its fee structure. Make sure to bookmark the Seller Center and check any emails eBay sends to make sure you’re up to date and can adjust your strategy if any fee changes are made.

## eBay Shoe Fees FAQs


### What percentage does eBay take on sneakers?


For sneakers priced at $150 or more, eBay takes 8% for non-store sellers and 7% for Basic Store and above. Sneakers priced under $150 have higher fees: 13.6% for non-store sellers and 12.7% for Basic Store and above.


### Are eBay sneaker fees higher than other platforms?


No, eBay sneaker selling fees are generally lower. This is especially true for sneakers that sell for over $150.


### Do I pay fees if my sneakers don’t sell?


In most cases, no. Final Value Fees are only charged on items that sell. For insertion fees, eBay gives you 250 free listings per month, and sneakers priced at $100 or more don’t count towards that total. However, if you go over these limits, you may pay a $0.35 insertion fee.


### Is an eBay Store worth it for sneaker sellers?


If you’re selling consistently, yes. A Basic store subscription costs $21.95/month and includes 1,000 free fixed priced listings. It also offers lower final value fees. These savings can really add up and more than cover the monthly fee if you sell enough.


### What sneakers are most profitable to sell on eBay?


While many sneaker categories perform well, success depends on sourcing in-demand models and trends. Reviewing the[top selling items on eBay](https://blog.vendoo.co/best-selling-items-on-ebay-for-2024) can give you insight into which sneakers and styles are generating consistent sales.
