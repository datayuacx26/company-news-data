# How to Delete a Depop Listing (2026): Step-by-Step

Here's how to delete a Depop listing, if you just need the fast version: open the item from your shop, tap Edit, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete Listing


. Confirm on the pop-up and it's done immediately. Depop doesn't build in an undo, so once you confirm, that's final.


One exception is worth flagging before you start. A listing that's already sold can only be deleted after it lands in your Sold tab, since Depop pulls sold items out of your active shop automatically. And if you're not fully certain you want a listing gone for good, deactivating it, marking it sold without an actual buyer, is the reversible alternative


. It keeps the listing's data intact instead of erasing it.


## Main Takeaways


- **Deleting a Depop listing is permanent.** It wipes the views, likes, and saves for good, and Depop support can't restore them.
- **Deleting a depop listing option lives inside Edit,** not the share or three-dot menu. Open the listing, tap Edit, then scroll to the bottom.
- **There's no native bulk delete.** As of 2026, Depop's app removes listings one at a time, with no multi-select tool.
- **Deactivating is different from deleting.** Marking an item sold without a buyer hides it from search but keeps it in your sold history and stats.
- **Cross-platform sales are urgent.** An item sold on Poshmark, eBay, or Mercari needs to come down from Depop fast to avoid a double sale, and knowing[how to delete an eBay listing](https://blog.vendoo.co/how-to-delete-ebay-listing) matters just as much when the sale happens there instead.
- **Ranking ignores deletions.** Depop's algorithm weighs account activity and freshness, not deletion history.


## Delete vs. Deactivate: Which One Do You Actually Need?

Deleting and deactivating look similar in Depop's menus, but they lead to very different outcomes for your shop. Whether you should deactivate a Depop listing vs. delete it really comes down to one question: do you want it gone for good, or just out of sight for now?


- **Visibility** : Delete removes a listing from your shop and search immediately and permanently. Deactivating, marking it sold without a buyer, hides it from search but keeps it in your account.
- **Reversibility** : Delete can't be undone, and Depop support won't restore it. Deactivating can be reversed by editing the item back to active.
- **Engagement history** : Delete wipes every view, like, and save attached to the listing. Deactivating preserves that history along with the listing's place in your sold stats.
- **Best use case** : Delete when an item sold elsewhere and needs to disappear immediately. Deactivate when you're pausing a listing, decluttering temporarily, or unsure whether you'll relist it.


## How to Delete a Depop Listing (Step-by-Step)


Depop's mobile app handles nearly all listing management, and here's how to delete a listing on Depop from start to finish. The flow is identical on iOS and Android.


1. Open your profile, tap the shop tab, and select the listing you want gone.
2. Tap Edit in the upper right corner. It's the same screen you'd use to change price or description, not a separate delete menu.
3. Scroll past the photos, title, and pricing fields to the very bottom, where a red Delete Listing button sits below everything else.
4. Tap Delete Listing. Depop shows a confirmation prompt warning that the action can't be reversed.
5. Confirm again. The listing disappears from your shop and search immediately, along with every view, like, and save attached to it.


One friction point sellers hit here: they expect the delete option to sit in the three-dot menu next to sharing and boosting tools


. It doesn't. Depop tucks it inside Edit specifically so you don't delete a listing by accident while browsing your own shop.


### Deleting an Active (Unsold) Listing


An active listing that hasn't sold is the simplest case


, and it's the one Depop designed the flow around: the steps above apply with no extra wrinkles, and the whole process takes about ten seconds per item. Sellers reach for this case most often after a pricing mistake, a listing built from the wrong photos, or an item that got damaged, lost, or sold in person before Depop ever saw a buyer.


Compare that to a listing that's already sold, which Depop pulls out of your active shop automatically and requires one extra step to reach. That's covered next.


### Deleting a Sold Listing


Once an item sells, Depop moves it out of active listings and into a dedicated Sold tab on your profile. You can't delete a sold item from the main shop view. Instead, open Sold, find the item, and follow the same Edit-then-Delete path described above.


Plenty of sellers skip this step on purpose


. Keeping sold listings visible gives buyers a track record, showing the shop has real transaction history and building trust the way a seller rating does on other marketplaces. If your goal is credibility rather than a tidy closet, leaving sold items in place usually serves you better.


### Deleting a Listing on Depop Web/Desktop


Depop's desktop dashboard handles basic upkeep, editing prices, updating descriptions, checking order status, but it isn't a full replacement for the app. Try to delete a listing from a browser and you'll often find the option missing or buried in a way that doesn't match the mobile flow above.


The safest approach is to treat the web dashboard as a viewing and light-editing tool


, and switch to your phone when you actually need to remove something. Depop is built app-first, and listing management, deletion especially, reflects that.

## Common Mistakes When Deleting Depop Listings


A few mistakes come up often enough to flag directly.


- **Deleting instead of deactivating** : once a listing is gone, you lose the option to quickly relist it.
- **Losing engagement history** : deletion wipes the data that helped a listing surface in search, so a relisted version starts from zero.
- **Skipping a duplicate check** : not confirming whether an item already sold elsewhere before deleting a Depop duplicate leaves gaps in your shop records and complicates reconciling sales later.
- **Assuming support can undo it** : deletion is treated as final, and Depop support doesn't restore listing data or history once it's gone.


## Does Deleting Listings Affect Your Depop Search Ranking?


Depop hasn't published a ranking penalty tied to deletion activity, and there's no evidence in seller guidance that the algorithm tracks how often you delete items


. What discovery actually rewards is activity and freshness: shops posting new listings regularly, responding to messages fast, and keeping items updated tend to surface more than dormant ones. Deleting a stale, zero-view listing costs you nothing there.


The real risk sits elsewhere. Deleting a listing that's actually performing well, one that's built up views and likes for weeks, throws away momentum you can't get back. That's a lost-visibility cost you're choosing to pay, not a penalty Depop imposes.

## Frequently asked questions


### How do I delete a sold listing on Depop?


Sold items move automatically to the Sold tab on your profile once a sale completes. Open the item there, tap Edit, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete Listing, then confirm on the pop-up. You can't delete a sold item from the main shop view, it only becomes deletable once it shows up in Sold.


### Can I recover a deleted Depop listing?


No. Deletion is permanent, and Depop support can't restore a listing once it's confirmed deleted, including its photos, description, views, likes, and saves. If you might want the item back later, deactivate it instead by marking it sold without a buyer, which keeps the listing's data intact.


### Why can't I find the delete button on Depop?


The delete option isn't in the share menu or the three-dot menu next to a listing, which is where most sellers look first. Open the listing, tap Edit in the top corner, then scroll past the photos, title, and price fields to the red Delete Listing button at the very bottom. Depop places it there deliberately so you don't delete a listing by accident while browsing your own shop.


### Can I delete multiple Depop listings at once?


Not natively. Depop's app requires deleting listings one at a time, with no multi-select option as of 2026. Sellers clearing out large batches of inventory typically turn to a crosslisting tool like Vendoo, which can manage delisting across a full shop instead of tapping through each item individually.


### Does deleting a listing hurt my Depop search ranking?


Not directly. Depop's algorithm weighs account activity and listing freshness, not deletion history, so removing a stale listing carries no ranking penalty. The real cost is losing the views and likes a well-performing listing had already built up, a loss of momentum, not an algorithmic punishment.
