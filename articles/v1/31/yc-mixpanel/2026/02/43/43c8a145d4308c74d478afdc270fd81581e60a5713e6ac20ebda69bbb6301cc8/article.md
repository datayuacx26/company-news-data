# Hands-On Workshop in Singapore on Advanced Product Analytics with Mixpanel – March 3rd

🇸🇬


Calling all users based in Singapore! We're running a hands-on workshops on 3 Mar as part of our Data & Donuts user event series! Seats are limited so


[sign up](https://events.mixpanel.com/data-and-donuts-singapore-mar26/) asap to secure your space. Detailed agenda below


👇


Hands-on Workshop: Building Ground Truth for PMs


3 Mar, 9am - 12pm, WeWork Mapletree Anson This workshop is designed for product and analytics teams who want to move beyond basic dashboards and learn how to analyze real user behavior using Mixpanel’s advanced capabilities. Using an AI-powered product as a sample dataset, we’ll explore how modern product teams answer critical questions around activation, engagement, retention, and growth—without relying on industry-specific assumptions. The AI use case provides a familiar, flexible context that makes it easy to translate insights to any digital product.


Participants will learn how to:


• Identify where users drop off in complex, multi-step funnels • Understand real user journeys with flows and path analysis • Measure meaningful retention based on user actions, not just logins • Discover which behaviours drive long-term value and monetisation • Segment users by behaviour, not just demographics or plans This session is ideal for teams looking to level up their product analytics practice and apply Mixpanel insights to real-world product decisions—whether they’re working on consumer apps, SaaS, or emerging AI products.
