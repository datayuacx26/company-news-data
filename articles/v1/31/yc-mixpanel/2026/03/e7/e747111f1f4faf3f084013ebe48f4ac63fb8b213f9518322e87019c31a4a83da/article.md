# Introducing AI Metric Trees: Automate Your Growth Strategy with AI-Powered Metric Mapping

🌳


[Introducing AI Metric Trees](https://docs.mixpanel.com/changelogs/2026-03-12-metric-trees-ai) !


🌳


Quick refresher: Metric Trees map *how your business actually grows*, connecting your north star to the bets, drivers, and inputs that move it. If you're not using one yet, it's one of the best ways to align your team around what actually matters. Now there's an even faster way to get started. Just enter your company URL and a little context and you'll get a full structured draft in seconds. North star, strategic bets, drivers, input metrics, all laid out. The part we're especially excited about: it surfaces metrics and drivers you might not have thought of, based on your growth model and industry best practices. It's like a second brain for your strategy doc. From there, make it yours. Edit the structure and connect nodes to your real Mixpanel data.

[Learn more](https://docs.mixpanel.com/docs/metric_tree#option-build-with-ai)


Metric Trees are available as an add-on for Enterprise plan customers. AI Metric Trees doesn't use your Mixpanel data and is powered by OpenAI and industry best practices.
