# Automating Revenue Capture for Dealerships on CDK Global

*CDK runs the dealership and ShortLoop makes sure every call into it counts.*


For dealer groups and multi-rooftop operations that run on CDK, the DMS is the business operating system where ROs, customer records, and vehicle history live. That's exactly why an AI that doesn't integrate with it falls flat fast.


ShortLoop is now fully integrated with CDK Global. The AI reads directly from CDK before a call begins and writes everything back when it ends. As a result, CDK stays current, and more ROs are booked, leads are converted, and more cars are sold.


## What's New


#### Informed calls that start with CDK data


ShortLoop AI pulls customer profiles, vehicle history, and open repair orders directly from CDK before the conversation starts. Whether it's a returning customer asking about their car or a new caller looking to schedule, the AI has the right context from the first word.


#### **CDK stays current, automatically**


When the call ends, ShortLoop writes it all back: call notes, op codes, appointment details, and updated customer records, directly into CDK. Advisors open their queue, and the context is already there. Without interruptions to the workflow or the need for handoff or manual re-entries.


#### **Multi-rooftop support for auto groups**


For dealer groups running dealerships on CDK, ShortLoop handles calls across rooftops, accommodating dealership-specific customization. Each location keeps its own workflows, op codes, and escalation paths, while the group gets consistent AI coverage and revenue capture across all stores.


#### **Outbound campaigns using real CDK data**


ShortLoop's outbound AI launches targeted campaigns directly from CDK - maintenance reminders, no-show reschedules, recall notifications, and seasonal campaigns. Every call and text is informed by the actual vehicle and history on file, and outcomes sync back into CDK after every interaction.


#### **Sales lead capture into CDK**


Every inbound sales call answered is captured and logged by ShortLoop’s AI. The AI handles inventory questions, schedules test drives, and logs the interaction directly into the CDK. Nothing needs to be manually entered and nothing falls through.

### **About CDK Global**


CDK Global is a leading provider of cloud-based software for dealerships and OEMs. Its Dealership Xperience platform connects sales, service, inventory, accounting, and customer engagement across more than 15,000 North American dealerships, making it one of the most widely used systems in automotive retail. Fortellis, CDK’s technology platform, enables seamless integration of solutions across the industry.
