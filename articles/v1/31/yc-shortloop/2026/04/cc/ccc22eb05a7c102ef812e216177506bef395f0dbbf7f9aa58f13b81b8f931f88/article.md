# Smarter Inbound and Outbound Phone AI Inside myKaarma

*myKaarma runs your service lane. ShortLoop makes sure it starts at the phone.*


myKaarma knows more about your customers than almost any platform in the service lane. But that intelligence stops the moment a call goes unanswered. Now, with improved inbound capabilities, smart routing and targeted outbound - ShortLoop's custom Phone AI aims to change that.


## What's New


**Every call answered. Every appointment in mKOS.**


ShortLoop answers every inbound call on the first ring during business hours and after. The AI captures intent, selects the correct vehicle, adds service notes, and books the appointment directly into mKOS so advisors see full context the moment they open the file.


**Deeper dealership-specific customization**


ShortLoop AI can identify how and with which opcodes appointments are booked based on dealership-specific data. It can be configured to offer specific convenience options to customers based on their service lane, for instance, shuttles and rentals for main lane services, and recommend waiting for quick express lane services. ShortLoop’s AI also accommodates advisor and technician preferences, whether it’s diesel techs, EV-certified specialists, or a customer's preferred advisor. Scheduling logic also accounts for time of day and day of the week, honoring availability patterns later in the day, around holidays, and on weekends.


**Outbound campaigns that capture revenue**


ShortLoop’s AI uses real customer and vehicle records to run targeted outbound messages like maintenance reminders, no-show reschedules, and recall notifications. All outreach is personalized, even at high volumes. Lastly, every interaction is automatically synced back into myKaarma, so nothing falls through the cracks between the phone and the platform.


## **Getting Started**


The ShortLoop + myKaarma integration is now available for dealerships operating on the mKOS (myKaarma Operating System) platform.


Our team works directly with dealership teams to configure AI workflows around existing mKOS processes, from appointment logic and accommodating preferences to outbound campaign setup, so nothing changes in how your advisors work.
