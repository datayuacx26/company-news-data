# ShortLoop Partners With Dealer-FX to Automate Fixed Ops

Every missed call in fixed ops is a lost RO, and every customer on hold is a retention risk. Dealer‑FX’s ONE Platform powers the entire service lifecycle, giving dealerships a unified, OEM-aligned workflow, but without instant phone response, customers drop before they ever reach the lane. ShortLoop solves this with custom Phone AI, because without real integration, AI is just a very expensive answering machine. Through deep platform integration with Dealer‑FX, ShortLoop uses actual customer data, service history, and live scheduling so every call becomes a contextual, revenue‑producing interaction that drives appointments and retention.


## What's New


**Appointments are scheduled directly inside Dealer‑FX**


The AI selects the correct vehicle, adds notes and service needs, and books the appointment into the Dealer‑FX scheduler, ensuring advisors see everything the moment they open the file.


**AI can assist with multi‑vehicle households using Dealer‑FX’s consolidated profiles**


When a customer owns multiple vehicles, ShortLoop can identify each one, reference its individual history, and guide the caller to the right service based on mileage, prior work, or OEM schedules.


**Outbound campaigns run on Dealer‑FX data**


ShortLoop’s outbound AI uses real customer and vehicle records to run targeted outreach including maintenance reminders, declined service follow‑ups, recall notifications and syncs outcomes back after every call.
