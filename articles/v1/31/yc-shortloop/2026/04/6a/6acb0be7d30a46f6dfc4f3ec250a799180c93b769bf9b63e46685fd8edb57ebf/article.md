# Closing the Gap Between the Phone and Your PBS v10 APEX

*PBS Systems runs the operation, and ShortLoop steps in when the operation can't answer the phone.*


PBS Systems dealers often say the same thing about why they chose v10 APEX: when something needs to change, someone actually picks up the phone. That relationship-first approach has made PBS one of the most trusted DMS providers in North America for over 35 years. But that same expectation - a real, responsive experience, is exactly what customers expect when they call the dealership. And without instant phone coverage, that expectation goes unmet.


ShortLoop is now integrated with PBS Systems. The AI reads directly from the PBS before a call begins and writes everything back when it ends. PBS stays current, and more ROs are booked, more leads are captured, and more revenue is retained.


## What's New


**Real-Time sync with PBS Systems**


The AI captures intent, selects the right vehicle, applies op codes and remarks, and books the appointment. Call notes, appointment details, op codes, and updated customer information are written back into PBS Systems. The DMS stays current and advisors stay informed without the need for manual re-entries.


**Multi-Location Consistency and Customization**


For multi-store PBS groups, each location gets consistent AI coverage while keeping its own configuration. ShortLoop can be configured to match how each store runs, with custom greetings, advisor routing, transportation options, department escalation paths while ensuring the standard is maintained across multiple rooftops.


**Personalized Outbound campaigns at scale**


ShortLoop's outbound AI uses actual customer and vehicle records from v10 APEX to run targeted campaigns for maintenance reminders, service follow-ups, recall notifications, and seasonal outreach. So every campaign is personalized, even at scale, and outcomes sync back into v10 APEX after every call and text.


**After-Hours Sales coverage**


ShortLoop doesn't just stop at service. ShortLoop’s AI handles after-hours inbound sales inquiries, capturing high intent leads. It also logs every interaction, so no lead goes unrecorded after the floor closes.
