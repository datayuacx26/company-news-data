# Allient Inc. Introduces Vyxalon™ Apex Drone Drive for Professional Drone Propulsion Systems - Allient, Inc.

**Allient Inc. Introduces Vyxalon™ Apex Drone Drive for Professional Drone Propulsion Systems**


[Download printer-friendly version](https://allient.com/d/?h=ab1b634)


**Buffalo, N.Y. – 5 August, 2026 –** Allient Inc. (Nasdaq: ALNT) (“Allient” or the “Company”), a global designer and manufacturer of advanced specialty Motion, Controls, and Power products and solutions for targeted industries and applications, has today announced the release of the Vyxalon™ Apex Drone Drive, a next-generation electronic speed controller (ESC) designed for professional drone propulsion systems.


Learn more about the Vyxalon™ Apex Drone Drive[here](https://allient.com/product/vyxalon-apex-drone-drive/) .


The Vyxalon™ Apex Drone Drive is the latest addition to the Allient Intelligent Controls platform and combines advanced Gallium Nitride (GaN) power technology with high-speed sensorless and encoder-based Field-Oriented Control (FOC) to deliver unmatched propulsion efficiency, flight performance, and reliability for mission-critical drone applications. Designed to operate in the most demanding environments, the drive offers cutting edge thermal and energy efficiency, low noise and low vibration operation, and field-programmable tuning to maximize flight range, uptime, and mission-specific configurability.


With compact electronics, advanced protection and diagnostic features, and support for multiple communication interfaces, the ESC is ideal for drone platforms across defense, industrial, robotics, and agriculture applications.


The Vyxalon Apex Drone Drive will meet National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) supply chain requirements and will be included in the Defense Contract Management Agency (DCMA) Blue UAS Framework / Catalog.


"The Vyxalon™ Apex Drone Drive is a significant step forward in our commitment to precision motion control for unmanned systems," said Jesse Dowd, General Manager of Allient Rochester. "As drone technology advances, our customers require higher efficiency, power density, and reliability in mission-critical environments. The Apex Drone Drive delivers exactly that, giving next-generation UAS platforms the performance edge they need."


For more information about Allient’s servo drives, visit[here](https://allient.com/product-category/servo-drives/) .
