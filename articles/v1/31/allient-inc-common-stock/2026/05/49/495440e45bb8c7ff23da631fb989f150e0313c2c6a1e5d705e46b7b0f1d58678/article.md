# Allient Inc. to Showcase Latest Motion Technologies at XPONENTIAL and MDEX 2026

**Allient Inc. to Demonstrate Advanced Motion Solutions at Robotics Summit & Expo 2026**


[Download printer-friendly version](https://allient.com/d/?h=ca16840)


**Buffalo, N.Y. – 26 May, 2026 –** **** Allient Inc. (Nasdaq: ALNT) (“Allient” or the “Company”), a global designer and manufacturer of specialty Motion, Controls, and Power products and solutions for targeted industries and applications, has today announced it will showcase its advanced motion solutions at Robotics Summit & Expo 2026, taking place May 27–28 at the Boston Convention and Exhibition Center in Boston, Massachusetts.


If you are attending, please visit us at Booth #222 or[get in touch](https://allient.com/contact-us/) to schedule a meeting during the event.


At Robotics Summit & Expo, Allient will feature live and static demonstrations showcasing its motion integration and system capabilities, including:


- **Pyxmos** **™** **Demo:** Pyxmos™ drive operating a frameless motor in a compact tabletop unit.
- **Integrated Servo:** Custom integrated HeiMotion® Dynamic motor **,** demonstrating Allient’s ability to integrate motor, electronics, and gearing into a compact, application‑specific solution.
- **Custom Frameless Motor Assembly:** Axial flux or Megaflux™ motor integrated into a custom housing **,** highlighting Allient’s capability to deliver fully engineered, application‑ready frameless motor assemblies.


Allient will also present its motion technologies and integrated solutions for robotics and automation applications, including:


- **Electroflux Series Frameless Torque Motors:** High-performance motor in a compact package to meet the rigorous demands of advanced robotic applications.
- **SA Series Frameless Axial Flux Motors:** Low-profile motors with slotless, zero-cogging design, ideal for camera systems, gimbals, and scanning or imaging applications.
- **HeiMotion** **®** **Stainless:** High‑performance stainless‑steel servo motor designed for demanding environments requiring durability, precision, and hygienic construction.
- **Megaflux™** **:** Frameless direct‑drive torque motor series offering high torque density, smooth motion, and multiple frame diameters for integration flexibility.
- **Quantum** **™** **:** Advanced motor technology designed for high‑efficiency, high‑precision applications across industrial and automation markets.
- **C-Series Linear Motors:** Ultra‑low‑profile, cog‑free linear motors engineered for high precision and smooth, easily integrated motion control.
- **P-Series Linear Motors:** High‑force ironless motors delivering exceptional force density, strong thermal efficiency, and zero cogging for optimized performance.
- **HT Series:** Robust motor solutions engineered for high‑torque, high‑reliability performance in heavy‑duty applications.
- **SR Series Frameless Torque Motors:** Specialized motor/actuator technology optimized for reliability and performance in mission‑critical systems.
- **Brushed Gearmotor:** Compact brushed gearmotor offering dependable performance for cost‑sensitive or legacy system requirements.


“Allient will showcase its motion technologies and integrated solutions for robotics and automation applications at Robotics Summit & Expo,” said Robert Mastromattei, Chief Commercial Officer & Group President at Allient. “The combination of precision components, integrated platforms, and live demonstrations highlights the performance and precision required across advanced robotics systems.”


For more information about Allient’s upcoming trade shows and events, visit[here](https://allient.com/trade-shows-events/) .
