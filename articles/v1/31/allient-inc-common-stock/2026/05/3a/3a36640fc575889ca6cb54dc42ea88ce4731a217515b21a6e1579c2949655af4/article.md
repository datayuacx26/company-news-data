# Allient Inc. Wins Rochester Top Workplaces Award 2026 - Allient, Inc.

**Allient Inc. Wins Rochester Top Workplaces Award 2026**


[Download printer-friendly version](https://allient.com/d/?h=c9b95f9)


**Rochester, N.Y. – 6 May, 2026 –** Allient Inc. (Nasdaq: ALNT) (“Allient” or the “Company”), a global designer and manufacturer of specialty Motion, Controls, and Power products and solutions for targeted industries and applications, today announced that its Rochester, NY location has been recognized as a 2026 Rochester Top Workplaces Award winner.


The Top Workplaces Award is based entirely on employee feedback, making it one of the most credible and meaningful forms of workplace recognition. Companies are evaluated on culture, leadership, engagement, and overall employee experience.


Allient’s facility in Rochester, NY, serves as the company’s center of excellence for servo drives and motion controller products and engineered OEM solutions. Allient Rochester specializes in designing and manufacturing servo drives, motion controllers, single & multi-axis integrated assemblies, and value-add sub-system and system level solutions for advanced manufacturing OEMs. Our work accelerates customer innovation by reducing complexity, shortening engineering cycles, and delivering high‑performance hardware and software solutions that integrate seamlessly with Allient’s broader portfolio of motors and precision motion systems.


“We are honored to be named a Rochester Top Workplace,” said Jesse Dowd, General Manager of Allient Rochester. “This recognition reflects the dedication and talent of our team. As our business continues to grow and the demand for advanced motion and control solutions increases, our people remain at the heart of everything we do.”


For more information about Allient’s Rochester facility, visit[here](https://allient.com/allient-rochester/products/) .


**ABOUT ALLIENT INC.**


Allient (Nasdaq: ALNT) is a global engineering and manufacturing enterprise that develops solutions to drive the future of market-moving industries, including medical, life sciences, aerospace and defense, industrial automation, robotics, semi-conductor, transportation, agriculture, construction, and facility infrastructure. A family of globally responsible companies, Allient takes a One-Team approach to “Connect What Matters” and provides the most robust, reliable, and high-value products and systems by utilizing its core Motion, Controls, and Power technologies and platforms.


Headquartered in Buffalo, N.Y., Allient employs more than 2,500 team members around the world.
