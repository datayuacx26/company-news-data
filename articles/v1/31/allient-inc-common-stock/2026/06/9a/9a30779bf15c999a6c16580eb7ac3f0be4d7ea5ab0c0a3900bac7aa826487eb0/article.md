# Allient Inc. to Showcase Motion Control and Automation Technologies at Automate 2026 - Allient, Inc.

**Allient Inc. to Showcase Motion Control and Automation Technologies at Automate 2026**


[Download printer-friendly version](https://allient.com/d/?h=b2334d8)


**Buffalo, N.Y. – 17 June, 2026 –** **** Allient Inc. (Nasdaq: ALNT) (“Allient” or the “Company”), a global designer and manufacturer of specialty Motion, Controls, and Power products and solutions for targeted industries and applications, has today announced it will present its motion control and automation technologies at Automate 2026, taking place June 22–25 at McCormick Place in Chicago, Illinois.


Visit us at Booth #1557, or[contact us](https://allient.com/contact-us/) to schedule a meeting during the event.


Allient will highlight a selection of technologies at Automate 2026, including:


**Frameless Torque Motors and Axial Flux Motors for Robotic and Humanoid Robotic Platforms.** As a pioneer and one the largest manufacturers of torque motors and motor assemblies for robotics and automation, Allient (Allied Motion) will be featuring the high torque ElectroFlux motors and other frameless motors from <25mm to > 300mm diameters with axial length down to 10mm.


- SA Series Axial Flux Motors
- PerformeX Slotless DC Motors & PerformeXG Slotless Gear Motors
- ElectroFlux Series
- MegaFlux Series


**Servo** **Drives and Motion Control Electronics Demo:** Our new IPC Controller will run multiple Pyxmos™ Servo Modules, driving our stages showcasing Allient’s fully integrated ecosystem of motors, motion stages, and controls.


- AiC IPC Controller
- Pyxmos™ Module Servo Drive & Pyxmos Rack Servo Drive Platform
- Integrated Drives (HTE and HTB)


**Precision Motion Stages Demo:** A wider range of precision motion stages, including linear, rotary, and multi‑axis platforms, each driven by Allient’s own rotary and linear motors, demonstrates the depth of our motion control capabilities.


- ThruSight-Focus XYZ Stage & ThruSight-Theta Stage
- 60x60 XY Stage
- Standard Hybrid Hexapod


Custom assembly capabilities from Allient highlight how frameless motors can be integrated into fully engineered, application-specific housings or actuators. Allient develops custom motors and assemblies when unique performance, packaging, or environmental requirements call for designs beyond standard offerings.


“We’re excited to be exhibiting at Automate 2026, where we will be showcasing our latest motion control and automation technologies,” said Robert Mastromattei, Chief Commercial Officer & Group President at Allient. “Automate provides a great opportunity to connect with customers and discuss how our technologies can support their applications.”


For more information and to schedule a meeting with Allient’s experts, visit[here](https://allient.com/automate/) .


**ABOUT ALLIENT INC.**


Allient (Nasdaq: ALNT) is a global engineering and manufacturing enterprise that develops solutions to drive the future of market-moving industries, including medical, life sciences, aerospace and defense, industrial automation, robotics, semi-conductor, transportation, agriculture, construction, and facility infrastructure. A family of globally responsible companies, Allient takes a One-Team approach to “Connect What Matters” and provides the most robust, reliable, and high-value products and systems by utilizing its core Motion, Controls, and Power technologies and platforms.


Headquartered in Buffalo, N.Y., Allient employs more than 2,500 team members around the world.
