# High Voltage Drive Systems - Allient, Inc.

High-voltage drive systems are key to managing power flow between the battery and electric motors in electric vehicles (EVs), offering higher efficiency, reduced energy losses, and support for more powerful motors. These systems, operating between 300-800 volts DC, enhance vehicle performance with benefits like longer driving ranges, faster acceleration, and reduced overall vehicle weight.


The shift toward higher voltage systems is driven by performance demands, particularly in larger vehicles, and the need for weight reduction. Higher voltages allow for smaller, lighter components and improved system integration, eliminating the need for additional converters. Applications such as electrohydraulic steering, thermal management systems, brake-by-wire, and active suspension benefit from these advancements in electrification.


However, higher voltage systems present challenges, including the need for components to be rated for these voltages, such as IGBTs, connectors, and insulation materials. Allient addresses these challenges through its expertise in developing high-voltage EDS motors and drive electronics, with a focus on safety, compliance, and system optimization.


At Allient, our cross-functional engineering teams and vertically integrated manufacturing processes allow us to deliver customized, ruggedized solutions that meet the highest performance and reliability standards. With a global presence and advanced testing facilities, Allient is at the forefront of vehicle electrification, offering tailored solutions for today’s electric vehicles and future innovations.
