### Omnicom Declares Dividend


May 5, 2026


NEW YORK


, May 5, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- The Board of Directors of Omnicom (NYSE: OMC) declared a quarterly dividend of 80 cents per outstanding share of the corporation's common stock. The dividend is payable on July 9, 2026 to Omnicom common shareholders of record at the close of business on June 10, 2026.


**About Omnicom** Omnicom (NYSE: OMC) is the world's leading marketing and sales company, built for intelligent growth in the next era.
