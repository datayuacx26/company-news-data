### Omnicom Schedules First Quarter 2026 Earnings Release and Conference Call


April 2, 2026


NEW YORK


, April 2, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Omnicom (NYSE: OMC) will publish its first quarter 2026 results on Tuesday, April 28, 2026 after the New York Stock Exchange close of trading. The company will also host a conference call to review such financial results on Tuesday, April 28, 2026, starting at 4:30 p.m. Eastern Time. A live webcast of the call will be available at Omnicom's investor relations website,[investor.omc.com](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4656714-1&h=483231487&u=http%3A%2F%2Finvestor.omnicomgroup.com%2Finvestor-relations%2Foverview%2Fdefault.aspx&a=investor.omc.com) , along with the related earnings press release and slide presentation. A webcast replay will be made available after the call concludes.
