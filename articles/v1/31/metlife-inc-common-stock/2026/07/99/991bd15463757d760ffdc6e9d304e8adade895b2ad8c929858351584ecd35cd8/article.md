# MetLife Pet Insurance teams up with The Animal Foundation to Support Las Vegas Pet Adopters

*Leading pet insurance provides 30-day coverage for new pet adoptions at one of the country’s largest single-site animal shelters*


**NEW YORK, November 20, 2025** — MetLife Pet Insurance (MetLife) is proud to announce its new relationship with The Animal Foundation, a leading animal welfare organization based in Las Vegas, Nevada. MetLife will offer 30 days of pet insurance coverage to every adopter, providing new pet parents with the tools and resources they need to help their furry family members thrive.1


“We’re proud to join forces with The Animal Foundation, an organization that shares our deep commitment to animal welfare,” said Brian Jorgensen, CEO of MetLife Pet Insurance. “This collaboration builds on our work with more than 100 shelters nationwide and reflects our mission to empower pet parents. Together, we’re helping more families protect the health and happiness of their beloved pets.”


The Animal Foundation is one of the largest single-site animal shelters in the U.S., serving the Las Vegas Valley and beyond. In 2024 alone, the Foundation saved more than 20,000 animals, facilitated over 10,000 adoptions, and provided critical care to animals in need. This collaboration with MetLife Pet Insurance ensures that every pet adopted through the Foundation may be protected against the unexpected, and their new owners can enjoy financial peace of mind.


As pets are adopted from shelters, caring for their health and well-being becomes a top priority. MetLife Pet Insurance offers flexible, customizable coverage designed to support pets at every stage of life—from routine preventive care like flea and tick control, vaccinations, and dental cleanings to unexpected visits for accidents or illnesses. Plans are highly customizable, allowing pet parents to select the deductibles, annual benefit limits, and reimbursement percentages that best suit each pet and pet parent’s unique needs. The MetLife Pet Insurance mobile app allows pet parents to easily manage policies, track claims, and access a live 24/7 vet chat.


“We’re thrilled to work with MetLife Pet Insurance to support new pet families at a time when shelters like ours are facing unprecedented capacity challenges,” said Daryl Sprague, Chief of Development and Marketing Director of the Animal Foundation. “This collaboration allows us to equip adopters with an essential resource—pet insurance—that will help them confidently welcome a new furry family member and provide the care their pet deserves. It’s a win for the pets, the families, and the broader community.”
