# When Is The Best Time To Post On Instagram?

On the surface, being an Instagram influencer doesn't seem to require much technical knowledge. That's because users only see what's in front of them - the content, the captions, and how you reach them.


Your average Instagram user may not realize how creators constantly monitor analytics, experiment with different posting schedules, and continuously test new ways to engage their audiences better.


Posting at the right time is an incredibly important part of being an[Instagram](https://beacons.ai/i/platform-instagram) creator. The right timing goes a long way in encouraging Instagram engagement, helping you grow your following, and ending up on Instagram Explore.


But is there a single, universal, 'best time' to post? Not exactly - but you can get close enough to see results.


## What is the best time of day to post on Instagram?


There is no single 'best' time of the day to post on Instagram because the ideal posting time depends on factors such as:


-


The day of the week (you might also find some select days of the week to be the best days to post)


-


Where your specific audience is located


-


If your audience is from multiple regions


-


Demographic information (their age, interests, etc.)


In general, it's a good practice to design your initial posting schedule according to the average best posting times for your region and experiment a bit. Then, you can use analytics to gradually zero in on posting times that bring in the most engagement and adjust your schedule accordingly.


## The scientifically-proven best times to post on social media


These optimal times for posting are scientifically proven because they were obtained by analyzing large bodies of data and insights from various sources including HubSpot, Later, and other influencer marketing software companies.


Note that the times below are listed in Eastern Standard Time (EST).


### Monday posting slot


The best time to post on Instagram on Monday is between 11 am and 2 pm.


### Tuesday posting slot


The best time to post on Instagram on Tuesday is between 10 am and 3 pm, or later at 7 pm. The 7 pm slot was found to offer some of the highest engagement levels!


### Wednesday posting slot


The best time to post on Instagram on Wednesday is between 7 am and 4 pm. That's a reasonably broad window, and the peak times for engagement are observed at 11 am and 2 pm.


### Thursday posting slot


The best time to post on Instagram on Thursday is between 10 am and 2 pm, or later at 6-7 pm.


### Friday posting slot


The best time to post on Instagram on Friday is between 9 am and 2 pm, although the 9 am slot offers higher engagement.


### Saturday posting slot


The best time to post on Instagram on Saturday is in the morning between 9 am, and 11 am.


### Sunday posting slot


Sunday is the worst day to post on Instagram, so the engagement is relatively low in general. However, the best time to post on Instagram on Sunday is between 8 am and 2 pm.

## How to determine the best time to post to your IG account


Every Instagram account is unique. Just because one influencer has success on Monday mornings doesn’t mean the same is true for your account.


Here are some suggestions on how you can find the best time to post:


### Schedule your posts at different times of the day to see what works


Figuring out what the best time to post on Instagram is for you requires some trial and error. Start by scheduling posts at different times of the day, and monitor their engagement metrics. After you zero in on the best posting times, you can adjust your post scheduling accordingly.


Generally, you can expect to find different peak times for posting on different days. You might find that early mornings on weekdays are better for engagement or that late-night weekend posts drive more engagement. Keep experimenting and schedule your posts for the following week to see what works and what doesn't.


### Try to hold off on sharing posts from other accounts


Every once in a while, curating content and sharing something important is great. However, it's best not to frequent this practice for two main reasons:


1.


It directs your traffic to other sources. As a creator/influencer, it's important to build and cultivate your own following. A big part of this involves making your audience engage with you, so don't lose focus.


2.


It can be bad for business. Look at it this way - if you're sharing lots of content for free, you're cheapening the value of sponsorship deals. Brands might not be happy about paying you for sponsored posts if you're driving traffic to other Instagram pages for free.


### Post reels and IGTV videos to see how they affect engagement


We've mainly discussed posts that appear on a user's feed. But, the same principles apply to reels and IGTV videos. Did you know that[74% of businesses](https://www.renderforest.com/blog/video-marketing-statistics#:~:text=74%25%20of%20businesses%20say%20that%20videos%20receive%20the,8x%20higher%20engagement%20than%20those%20shared%20from%20YouTube.) cited videos as the content which receives the most engagement on their social media platforms?


So it's worth experimenting with video content and figuring out peak times to post it, to optimize your posting schedule.


## Things to keep in mind as a creator


Finding the best times to post is important for your social media marketing strategy. Here are some things to keep in mind when you're diving into the technical stuff.


### Day of the week


Instagram user engagement varies depending on the day - users might be more active early Friday and favor a later start on Mondays, for example. So, observe different posting times for each day and don't limit yourself to a single 'best time' across the board.


### Breaks during traditional working hours


9-5s come with much-needed lunchtime breaks and similar short breaks from working hours. So, if you have a large working audience, experiment with posting during work breaks and see how many of your followers are checking Instagram.


### The time zones of yourself and your audience


If you're sitting in, say, Australia, but the bulk of your audience is from the US, then accounting for time zones is vital. Otherwise, if you post at a time that's 4 am on a Tuesday for your audience, then you might end up with low engagement. So make sure the timing of your posts isn't off and your schedule caters to different time zones.


### How frequently you post


Settling on a good frequency to post from your Instagram account is a delicate balance. You need to post enough to keep your audience engaged and keep Instagram's algorithm happy, but overposting might just annoy your followers, which is detrimental for engagement.
