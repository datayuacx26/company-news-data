# When Is The Best Time To Post On TikTok?

While TikTok used to be the new kid on the block, it's long since evolved into one of the most relevant and biggest social media platforms available today. We're currently in the era of influencers and content creators, and TikTok has gained attention for its enormous marketing potential. ## Why are specific times and days important?


The times and days you post on TikTok are important because they dramatically influence your engagement metrics including watch time, likes, and comments. If you post most of your[TikTok](https://beacons.ai/i/tiktok) content when your target audience is dormant, then the content receives fewer likes, shares, and comments.


Low post engagement also decreases the likelihood of your content appearing in TikTok's "For You" section. "For You" is a feature on TikTok where the TikTok algorithm selects content to recommend to users on their feed. Since this content is personalized for each user, it naturally receives the most engagement.


User engagement is a key deciding factor regarding whether your content gets featured in the "For You" section. So, by posting at the right time, your content receives more engagement. Reaching the "For You" section has a snowball effect that leads to more views and followers.

## Keep time zones in mind when posting


The times we listed above correspond to Eastern Standard Time (EST), the time zone for the eastern states in the US including New York. However, if you have a global audience or most of your following is from different regions, your posting times need to account for different time zones.


### Know where your audience is located


Figuring out where your audience is located helps adjust your posting times to their time zones. For example, if your audience is located in a part of Canada in the ADT timezone, there's a 2-hour difference (EST is 2 hours behind).


### Track when your audience is active


Tracking when your audience is active helps you identify the best times to post. After all, the chances of engagement are the highest when your followers are most active.

## Proven ways to grow your following


Posting your content at the right time is crucial to increase user engagement. However, if your following isn't big enough, then there's only so much engagement you can get.

When designing your TikTok content, figure out what makes your content stand out. What's so special about your content that users have to see it? Challenges pop up on social media platforms all the time, and getting in on the trend helps expand your outreach and grow your following. So even if a particular challenge isn't in your ballpark, it's worth participating for the sake of engagement.

Posting consistently is generally a good practice on social media platforms to increase visibility, and TikTok is no exception. Posting regularly guarantees that your audience sees your content frequently, increasing their opportunity to interact and engage with it.

## Frequently asked questions


There are lots of questions about when to post on TikTok to drive the most engagement. Here are some of the most common questions about posting on TikTok and our suggestions.


### Does it matter what time you post?


Yes. It's important to identify posting times that foster the most user engagement. Ideal posting times correspond to slots where your following is most active.


### How many times a day should you post?


For a given day, there are three 'best' average time slots for posting content. So you should post between 1-3 times a day, during the best posting slots.


### How many views does it take to go viral?


TikTok's algorithm uses the initial views to determine whether to make a TikTok video go viral or not. For example, the algorithm might initially broadcast the video to 300-500 viewers. If these viewers respond well to the video in a short time frame, and the post gains appreciable likes, then the video is likely to go viral.


### How long does it take for a video to go viral?


TikTok videos have a long lifespan, especially compared to platforms like Instagram. A TikTok video won't necessarily go viral within 48 hours - it can even go viral weeks after you post it. On average, TikTok videos go viral within 6 hours.


### Can your video go viral days later?


Yes. TikTok videos have a long life span and can go viral days or even weeks later.


### Is it bad to post too many TikToks?


Posting too many TikToks a day can be bad because it might overwhelm your followers.


### Is it bad to miss a day of posting?


It's generally best to maintain a consistent posting schedule on TikTok. Each video you post potentially brings in more followers, so missing out on posting can decrease your follower gain. However, if you're short on quality content, missing out on a day won't ruin your metrics.
