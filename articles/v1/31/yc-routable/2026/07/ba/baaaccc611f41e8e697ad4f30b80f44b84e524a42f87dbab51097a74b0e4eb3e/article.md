# SuiteWorld 2024: What to Expect + Predictions

SuiteWorld 2024 is set to be an exciting event for Oracle NetSuite users. This year’s conference will focus on the latest trends and technologies transforming businesses, including artificial intelligence (AI), automation, and omnichannel commerce. Attendees will be able to learn how NetSuite remains the premier platform for managing and growing their businesses.

Here are some of the highlights of what you can expect at SuiteWorld 2024:


-


Over 200 new AI features on the NetSuite platform:


This includes features for automating tasks, improving forecasting, and making better decisions.


-


A focus on accounts payable (AP) automation:


NetSuite will discuss how AI can streamline AP processes, from invoice matching and approval to payments.


-


New solutions for specific industries


: Examples include life sciences and food and beverage. These solutions are designed to meet the unique needs of these industries and help businesses improve their efficiency and compliance.


-


SuiteWorld On Air:


Offers live keynotes, breakout sessions, training workshops, and networking opportunities for those unable to attend in person.


SuiteWorld is a must-attend event for anyone who wants to learn about the latest trends and technologies in cloud ERP and digital business management. Attendees will gain valuable insights into how NetSuite can help them navigate the evolving business landscape and achieve their growth objectives.


Now, for a sneak peek into what you can expect from this event. Let’s explore how this conference will explore the great beyond with NetSuite.


Here are some predictions for SuiteWorld 2024:


-


Customer Experience (CX):


Enhancing the customer experience is a top priority for many businesses. The conference may explore how NetSuite can be leveraged to personalize customer interactions, improve service delivery, and gather valuable customer insights.


-


Data Analytics and Business Intelligence:


As businesses become more data-driven, SuiteWorld could showcase new capabilities in NetSuite for analyzing data, generating actionable insights, and making informed business decisions.


-


Omnichannel Commerce:


With the rise of e-commerce and the need to provide seamless customer experiences across multiple channels, the conference may address how NetSuite can support omnichannel strategies, from inventory management to order fulfillment.


-


Global Expansion:


For businesses expanding globally, SuiteWorld might delve into how NetSuite can help manage complex international operations, including multi-currency transactions, tax compliance, and regulatory requirements.


-


Partnerships and Integrations:


NetSuite’s ecosystem of partners and integrations is a significant strength. The conference could spotlight new partnerships and integrations that extend NetSuite’s functionality and reach.
