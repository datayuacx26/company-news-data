### Kohl’s Corporation Declares Quarterly Dividend


05/20/2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s204.q4cdn.com/271435555/files/doc_news/Kohls-Corporation-Declares-Quarterly-Dividend-97de4331b-2026.pdf)


MENOMONEE FALLS, Wis.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


The Board of Directors of Kohl’s Corporation (NYSE: KSS) (“Kohl’s” or the “Company”) today declared a regular quarterly dividend of $0.125 per share on the Company’s common stock. The dividend is payable June 24, 2026, to shareholders of record at the close of business on June 10, 2026.
