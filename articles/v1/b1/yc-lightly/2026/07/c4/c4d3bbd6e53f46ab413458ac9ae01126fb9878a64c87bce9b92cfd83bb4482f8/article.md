# Lightly at RSNA 2025 in Chicago

Lightly teamis going to RSNA 2025 in Chicago. RSNA is renowned for showcasing innovative imaging techniques, clinical insights, and practical applications of AI. It’s a setting where people openly exchange ideas, compare approaches, and explore how technology can support day-to-day work in medical imaging.


For us at Lightly, RSNA is a great opportunity to connect with the medical AI teams we collaborate with. Many of them use LightlyTrain in their daily work for medical image classification, detection and segmentation, using both labeled and unlabeled data to organizing their entire training workflows. We always learn a lot from hearing how they approach real clinical data.


**You can find us at Booth 5755** , where we’ll be happy to discuss your workflows, data bottlenecks, or how LightlyTrain can fit into your radiology AI pipeline.
