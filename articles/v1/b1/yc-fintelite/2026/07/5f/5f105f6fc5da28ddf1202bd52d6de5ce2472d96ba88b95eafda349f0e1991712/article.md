# How to Automate Invoice Matching for Accounts Payable (2-Way & 3-Way)

Every invoice that comes in, your team has to check it against a purchase order, and sometimes a receipt as well, before it can be paid. As they pile up, keeping up with these checks gets harder, small mistakes or duplicate payments start creating serious problems.


The good news? This repetitive check can now run on autopilot, thanks to AI. What used to take hours is now quickly done in minutes. This guide will show you how to easily automate invoice matching, step by step.


## **What Is Automated Invoice Matching?**


Automated invoice matching uses software to check invoices against other records, like purchase orders or receipts, instantly in one workflow. This means invoices move through approval faster with little to no manual effort.


In practice, here’s what happens every time an invoice arrives:


- **Capture** : Invoice, along with PO and receipt, is retrieved via email, upload, or scan
- **Extract** : All data is pulled out and centralized in one place without manual input
- **Match** : Data is cross-checked against your matching rules, whether that’s[2-way or 3-way matching](https://www.avidxchange.com/blog/2-way-vs-3-way-matching-faqs-pros-cons/)
- **Flag or approve** : Clean invoices move forward automatically, mismatches get flagged for review
- **Post** : Approved invoices are pushed to your accounting or ERP system for payment


## **Documents You Can Automate Matching With**


Besides the invoice, other documents required for accounts payable vary by business. One team may only need a purchase order, another adds receipts or pricing agreements. And it doesn’t matter what format they arrive in, scanned, PDF, email, e-invoice, or a photo taken on the go. Here’s what usually gets automated:


### **Purchase orders**


The original approved order, used to confirm price, quantity, and terms.


### **Vendor quotations**


The initial quoted price, used to confirm the PO and invoice reflect what was agreed.


### **Goods receipt notes**


Confirmation that the goods or services were actually received, used in 3-way matching.


### **Delivery notes**


Shipment confirmation from the vendor, used to verify what was delivered against the order.


### **Contracts or pricing agreements**


A document used to confirm negotiated rates match what’s billed.


## **Essential Technology for Automated Invoice Matching**


Several core technologies work together to make automated matching possible. Each one plays a different role in the process.


### **AI OCR Data Extraction**


Before any matching can happen, the system needs to read the document.[Intelligent OCR](https://fintelite.ai/data-extraction/) (optical character recognition) converts scanned or image-based documents into readable text, accurately extracting all the specific fields that matter regardless of layout.


### **Rules Engines**


Rules engines apply your matching logic, tolerance thresholds, approval hierarchies, and what counts as a match versus a mismatch. This is where 2-way, 3-way, or 4-way matching comes into play.


### **Routing Automation**


No more manually forwarding invoices and chasing approvals.[Routing automation](https://fintelite.ai/document-routing-automation/) skips that step, sending invoices to the right next process based on preset conditions, outcomes, and approval level.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**


Whether you’re evaluating invoice matching automation or planning an implementation, these FAQs cover the questions businesses ask most often.


****How are invoice matching exceptions handled?****


When an invoice doesn’t meet your validation rules, it is automatically flagged and routed to the right reviewer. Your team can focus only on exceptions while compliant invoices continue through the workflow.


****How long does invoice matching take from data extraction to validation?****


The processing time depends on document volume, workflow complexity, and system integrations. With platforms like Fintelite AI, the end-to-end processes are typically completed in <3 minutes, allowing invoices to move through the approval process much faster than manual workflows.


****Can I implement complex validation rules for invoice matching?****


Yes. Fintelite AI makes it easy to create custom validation rules without coding. For example, you can compare line items, set price or quantity tolerances, detect duplicate invoices, and define approval conditions that match your business process.


****Is invoice matching limited to 2-way or 3-way matching?****


No. While 2-way and 3-way matching are the most common, you can also compare invoices with other supporting documents, such as contracts, delivery notes, or any documents required by your workflows
