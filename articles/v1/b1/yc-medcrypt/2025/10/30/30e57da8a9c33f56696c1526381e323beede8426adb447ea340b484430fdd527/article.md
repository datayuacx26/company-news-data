# Medical Design & Outsourcing: Axel Wirth and Naomi Schwartz on Post-Quantum Cryptography and the Future of Medical Device Security

In their *Medical Design & Outsourcing* byline, Medcrypt’s **Chief Security Strategist Axel Wirth** and **VP of Regulatory Strategy Naomi Schwartz** explain why **medical device manufacturers (MDMs)** must begin preparing for a post-quantum world. As quantum computing advances, today’s encryption standards will eventually be rendered obsolete — posing serious risks to patient safety and regulatory compliance.


Axel and Naomi emphasize that waiting until NIST finalizes its post-quantum standards could leave organizations scrambling. Instead, MDMs should start assessing cryptographic assets, mapping dependencies, and building migration roadmaps now. The article offers practical, forward-looking guidance to help device makers adapt securely and efficiently.


Published in *Medical Design & Outsourcing* , a leading industry resource for medtech engineers and executives, this byline reinforces **Medcrypt’s leadership at the intersection of cybersecurity, regulation, and innovation** , showcasing the company’s proactive stance on preparing the industry for next-generation security challenges.
