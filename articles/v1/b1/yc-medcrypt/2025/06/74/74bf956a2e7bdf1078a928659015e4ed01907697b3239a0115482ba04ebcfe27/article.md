# MDDI Q&A: Mike Kijewski on Medcrypt’s Security Intelligence Platform and the Future of Medical Device Cybersecurity

In this **Q&A with MDDI’s Bob Kronemyer** , Medcrypt CEO **Mike Kijewski** explores the drivers behind the company’s newly launched **Security Intelligence Platform** and what it means for the future of connected medical devices. The discussion highlights how today’s medtech ecosystem faces unprecedented security challenges — from managing software dependencies to maintaining real-time visibility into vulnerabilities across device portfolios.


Mike outlines how Medcrypt’s platform helps manufacturers go beyond compliance, giving them the tools to proactively monitor, assess, and mitigate cyber risks. He also offers a forward-looking perspective on where the industry must head to ensure patient safety in an increasingly connected world.


The article reinforces Mike’s position as a trusted thought leader in medical device cybersecurity and showcases Medcrypt’s commitment to supporting the entire lifecycle of device security — from design through postmarket monitoring.
