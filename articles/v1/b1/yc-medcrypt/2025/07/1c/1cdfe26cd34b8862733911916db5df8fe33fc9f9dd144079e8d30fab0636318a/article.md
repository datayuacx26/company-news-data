# Medcrypt Recognized as a Representative Vendor in the 2025 Gartner® Market Guide for Medical Device Risk Management Platforms

The latest **Gartner® Market Guide for Medical Device Risk Management Platforms** (published **July 15, 2025** ) provides valuable insights into how healthcare delivery organizations (HDOs) and medical device manufacturers (MDMs) can strengthen their cybersecurity posture across connected device ecosystems.


Medcrypt was recognized as a **Representative Vendor in the Medical Device Manufacturer Risk Management** category for its role in helping device makers assess and mitigate security risk throughout the product lifecycle.


According to the report, platforms like Medcrypt’s are critical to addressing the increasing complexity of IoMT security — enabling organizations to leverage **risk scoring, exploitability analysis, and lifecycle visibility** to manage vulnerabilities more effectively and comply with evolving regulatory expectations.
