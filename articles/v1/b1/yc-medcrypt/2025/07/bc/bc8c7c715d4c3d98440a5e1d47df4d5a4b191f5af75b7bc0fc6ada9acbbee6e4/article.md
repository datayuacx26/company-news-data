# Medcrypt’s Jobe Recognized in the 2025 Security Business Innovator Awards

*SecurityInfoWatch* and *Security Business Magazine* have announced their **2025 Security Business Innovator Award** winners, and Medcrypt’s **Jobe** is among the select few recognized for their outstanding contributions to advancing cybersecurity in healthcare and connected device ecosystems.


Now featured in the **July 2025 issue of Security Business (page 56)** , Jobe’s recognition underscores Medcrypt’s growing influence in the broader security community. The **Security Business Innovator Awards** are the industry’s only 100% peer-nominated honors program — spotlighting integrators, consultants, practitioners, manufacturers, and service providers shaping the future of security through technology and leadership.


Each year, *SecurityInfoWatch* receives hundreds of nominations, with only a handful of professionals chosen as Innovators. This recognition reinforces **Medcrypt’s commitment to elevating cybersecurity practices and promoting cross-industry collaboration** that strengthens digital and physical safety alike.
