# 24x7 Magazine: Axel Wirth on Legacy Devices and Hospital Cybersecurity Risks

In a recent *24x7 Magazine* feature by **Alyx Arnett** , Medcrypt’s Chief Security Strategist **Axel Wirth** sheds light on one of healthcare’s most persistent security challenges: **legacy medical devices** . These systems, often running on unsupported or outdated operating systems, represent a significant vulnerability within hospital networks.


Axel explains that the most serious consequence of these vulnerabilities isn’t just data loss — it’s patient harm. He urges health systems to take immediate steps to **inventory connected assets, segment networks, and demand accountability from vendors** , emphasizing that waiting for a cyberattack or regulatory action is not a viable strategy.


This piece builds on Medcrypt’s ongoing advocacy for secure device design and lifecycle management, positioning the company as a **trusted authority in medical device cybersecurity** and a partner to healthcare leaders working to modernize clinical infrastructure.
