# Privacy by Design: How Ouster BlueCity Fuses Native Color Lidar with Edge Privacy

As urban city centers grow and transportation networks become increasingly complex, modern traffic management forces a challenging compromise on smart city planners. Today’s municipalities and departments of transportation (DOTs) require high-fidelity, real-time spatial intelligence to optimize traffic flow and protect vulnerable road users (VRUs). However, capturing this granular level of data frequently triggers significant, valid privacy concerns regarding public surveillance and identity tracking. For today’s transport planners, finding an advanced solution that strikes a balance between robust operational intelligence and community trust is paramount. The next generation of intelligent transportation systems (ITS) must be capable of delivering high-fidelity real-time data without compromising individual rights. Ouster BlueCity with Rev8 directly answers this call.


### **Ouster BlueCity delivers high-fidelity intelligence coupled with system-level privacy features at the edge**


Ouster BlueCity with Rev8 opens up a tremendous amount of new spatial intelligence to traffic engineers and planners. This breakthrough introduces the industry's first native color lidar detection system with up to 500 foot / 150 meter advance detection, simultaneously capturing both color and 3D depth information without the need for complex calibration. This capability provides unmatched situational awareness, delivering precise, high-fidelity digital traffic twins, simplified zone set-up, and more intuitive event analysis through improved color visualization. Adding native color to lidar detection while addressing concerns about privacy was paramount in Ouster’s product development process. Ouster BlueCity with Rev8 is designed with system-level privacy features at the edge, capturing the movement of multimodal road users in 3D and 2D native color while applying privacy-enhancing techniques to reduce the collection of identifying features such as facial features. As a result, municipalities and DOTs get full situational awareness for the traffic team, while ensuring privacy by design and default for everyone at the crosswalk.
