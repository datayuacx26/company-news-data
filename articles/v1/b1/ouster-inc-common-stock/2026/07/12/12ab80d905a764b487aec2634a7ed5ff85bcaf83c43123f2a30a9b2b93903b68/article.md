# Unlocking Federal Funding for Smart Infrastructure: Ouster BlueCity with Rev8 Achieves BABA Compliance

For state and local transportation agencies, the race to modernize roadways with intelligent transportation systems (ITS) can often hit a financial roadblock when procurement compliance does not align with technology needs. For Ouster ITS customers, a critical path forward has been officially cleared with the announcement of system-level Build America, Buy America (BABA) Act compliance for Ouster BlueCity—our complete lidar traffic management solution. When deploying Ouster BlueCity, traffic engineers gain peace of mind and accelerated timelines in their journey to transform mobility and safety across U.S. intersections, corridors, and highways.


## **What are BABA Act requirements?**


The Build America, Buy America (BABA) Act, enacted as part of the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act (aka Bipartisan Infrastructure Law, establishes strict domestic procurement preferences for all federally funded infrastructure projects in the United States. More specifically, the Act requires all iron, steel, manufactured products, and construction materials used in federally funded infrastructure projects to be produced in the U.S.To meet these stringent compliance standards, all iron and steel manufacturing processes must occur domestically, and construction materials must be entirely produced in the U.S. BABA compliance for manufactured products, including those used in projects overseen by the Federal Highway Administration (FHWA), requires final manufacturing (assembly) to occur in the U.S., a rule that took effect on October 1, 2025, to replace prior general waivers. Moving forward, as of October 1, 2026, the domestic content requirements take full effect, requiring at least 55% of the total cost of all components to be mined, produced, or manufactured domestically. This regulatory framework is designed to bolster domestic supply chains, stimulate U.S. manufacturing, and ensure that federal tax dollars directly support local industries and workers across transit, highway, water, and broadband infrastructure deployments.
