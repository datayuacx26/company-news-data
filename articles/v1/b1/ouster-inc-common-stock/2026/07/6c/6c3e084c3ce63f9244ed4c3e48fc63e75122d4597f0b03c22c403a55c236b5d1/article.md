# How the Ouster BlueCity solution is helping to modernize the ITS industry | Ouster

Imagine a traffic signal that doesn't just change based on a pre-set timer, but truly understands the real-time flow of vehicles, cyclists, and pedestrians in three-dimension. Picture a system that prioritizes safety, respects privacy, and works reliably in rain, snow, or total darkness. This is the reality Ouster BlueCity solution is creating, and it all began as an ambitious idea in a university lab.


The story of the Ouster BlueCity system is a testament to innovation and the power of perseverance. It’s the journey of a passionate team, starting with a small seed of an idea and growing into a global leader in Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS), transforming how we navigate our cities.


## **Roots at McGill University and an all-Canadian dream**


The Ouster BlueCity story starts with a clear vision and a drive to solve a fundamental urban problem: the inefficiencies and safety risks of outdated traffic management. Asad Lesani, and Ian Boyd, both with deep roots in engineering and technology, saw a glaring gap in the market during their time at McGill University. Existing camera-based traffic systems were prone to errors, struggled in adverse weather, and often raised privacy concerns. They believed there had to be a better, smarter, and more effective way. This led them to develop the BlueCity solution. Long hours and relentless testing resulted in a transformational solution: a combination of AI and lidar technology to capture real-time, multimodal data on road users, speed and trajectories. This provides unprecedented insights and automation to create a pathway to safer roads and enhanced traffic flow.
