# Setting a new standard in ITS: Ouster BlueCity with Rev8 steals the show at ITS America

## **The show floor**


On the show floor, Ouster BlueCity was displayed in the booths of customers and partners.


### **ADASTEC**


In collaboration with the Michigan Department of Transportation,[ADASTEC](https://www.adastec.com/) SAE Level-4 automated bus demonstration using Ouster digital lidar sensors, showcased ADASTEC’s SAE Level-4 automated driving software platform and the capabilities of their automated bus in a real-world environment.


### **Cisco**


At the[Cisco](https://www.cisco.com/) Industrial IoT booth, Ouster BlueCity solution was featured as an embedded technology in their smart infrastructure demonstration. Today, Ouster works alongside Cisco to bring the benefits of lidar technology to key customers.


### **Econolite**


[Econolite](https://www.econolite.com/) , an Ouster BlueCity partner, showcased Ouster BlueCity at their booth, which seamlessly integrates with Econolite’s[Cobalt® ATC controllers](https://www.econolite.com/products/traffic-signal-controller/cobalt-traffic-signal-controller/) and[EOS next-generation firmware](https://www.econolite.com/products/traffic-signal-controller/eos-traffic-signal-controller-software/) , providing a comprehensive solution that transforms any intersection or roadway into a dynamic, data driven environment. Today, Ouster and Econolite provide Ouster BlueCity to over 100 intersections in Utah alone.
