# Nashville 100+ Intersection and Roadway Deployment | Ouster

Ouster was[awarded](https://investors.ouster.com/news-releases/news-release-details/ouster-accelerates-software-adoption-2025-record-bookings-and) a multimillion-dollar contract by the City of Nashville, Tennessee, to deploy its complete traffic management solution, Ouster BlueCity, at more than 100 intersection and roadway sites following successful pilots and deployments in key locations. This initiative is a collaborative effort with the Nashville Department of Transportation and Multimodal Infrastructure (NDOT),[Vanderbilt University](https://www.linkedin.com/company/vanderbilt-vector-lab/people/) and Ouster BlueCity distributor[Southern Lighting &amp; Traffic Systems](https://southernltg.com/) (SLTS) to enhance mobility and safety in the city.


This expansion follows successful pilots at 8 locations in the north Nashville area and a 5-intersection corridor deployment on Broadway, Nashville’s famous Honky Tonk Highway, which attracts an estimated 17.3 million tourists and country music lovers annually. The City is leveraging 3D lidar to move towards corridor-level analytics across a multi-mile stretch in Nashville. By using the latest advancements in distributed perception, which stitches together 3D lidar data from multiple intersections into a single view, the City can access corridor-level detection of multimodal road user movements. Today, the Broadway deployments are helping to manage large crowds, congestion and road closures that often occur in the thriving tourist area.
