## App that shows Alternate Side Parking (ASP) hours and suspensions


If you read the signs, you will see that ASP happens several times per week for street cleaning. Since the beginning of the pandemic,[Alternate Side Parking reform](https://www1.nyc.gov/html/dot/html/motorist/alternate-side-parking.shtml#reform) reduced street cleaning to only 1 day per week, even if the signs still display multiple days.


Luckily there are parking holidays when ASP is suspended. Also, ASP is suspended when there are snow storms.


[SpotAngels](https://dl.spotangels.com/DoA1liP8jnb) will show you ASP hours in your neighborhood, help you save your car’s location, send you a reminder to move it and will send you notifications when ASP is suspended.
