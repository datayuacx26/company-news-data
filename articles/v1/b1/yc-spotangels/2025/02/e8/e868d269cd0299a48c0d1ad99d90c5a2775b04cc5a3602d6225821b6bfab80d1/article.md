# Parking near Queens ZOO Tips and Tricks

Parking near the Queens Zoo can be pretty laborious since this zoo is the most commonplace for families to visit. Spending an afternoon at the Zoo might be one of the most enjoyable ways to utilize your free time.


Queens Zoo is a 7.3-hectare (18-acre) zoo in Flushing Meadows-Corona Park in Queens, New York City. Enjoy the experience without stressing about finding your parking spot! Here is how to find cheap parking near the zoo


## How much does parking near Queens Zoo cost?


Although parking is not available at the Zoo itself, it is available in a nearby lot on 111th Street, between 54th and 55th Avenues which is free between 6 am and 10 pm.
