# Where to Find Free Parking in Daly City

Daly City is more famous for its Bart station than for its free parking. With these tips and tricks, you’ll be able to find a free parking spot or two in.


To find those free street parking spots, here is what you need:


1. An understanding of where those spots exist. ([Check out this free map](https://www.spotangels.com/daly-city-parking) !)
2. Familiarity with parking rules in Daly city so you’ll know that free spot is actually free.


## **Map of Free Parking**


Most of the spots in Daly City are for residents only. But some are free. To locate them you can use the SpotAngels map before navigating to your desired area.


The map updates in real-time, so simply type in the address you’re parking near, the time you expect to be there, and your duration. The spots where you can legally park will appear on the map along with their prices. The green spots are the ones that are free charge!


Pro tip: beware of Street Cleaning! Many free parking spots have street cleaning hours where you can’t park. But don’t worry, you will find the info on the Spotangels map.


## **Daly City Parking Rules:**


Daly City has some generous parking rules on weekends 🙂 You can park for free in most of the residents’ only spots for the entire weekend. Meters are free of charge on Sunday.
