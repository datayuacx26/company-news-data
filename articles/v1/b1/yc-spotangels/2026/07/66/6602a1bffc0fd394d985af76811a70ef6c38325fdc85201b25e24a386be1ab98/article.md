## **Prices to expect & online deals:**


Let’s set expectations correctly – a dedicated monthly spot in Bellevue is likely to cost more than $220/month. But don’t panic, you can also find spots below $60 a month.


## **Tips if you have a limited budget:**


If you have a limited budget, here are some tricks and tips:


### **Use parking only when you need it:**


- If you need parking for work, you can find spots to rent during working hours for a reduced price.
- In case you need a monthly spot when you come back home, you can rent a monthly spot for overnight and weekends only.
- If you can’t afford a monthly parking spot, you will need to play the Bellevue street parking game. Don’t worry SpotAngels is here to help! -> Check this[Bellevue street parking map](https://www.spotangels.com/bellevue) to see free parking options around you.


### **Take advantage of the Park + Ride at transit stations**


If you are commuting to Bellevue or the other way around, you can buy a monthly parking spot near transit stations.
