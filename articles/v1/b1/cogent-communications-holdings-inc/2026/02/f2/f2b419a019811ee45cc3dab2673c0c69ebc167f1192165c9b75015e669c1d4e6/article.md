# Cogent for Financial Services

*May 20, 2026 byHayley Levine*


[Financial Services is one of the fastest-moving industries in the world. Financial Services professionals constantly push forward, to execute trades at exactly the right moment and ensure that client money is being invested responsibly. With so much happening day-to-day, a strong internet infrastructure isn't just helpful, it's essential. Cogent has been supporting Financial Services companies for over 25 years, and as the industry looks toward the future, Cogent will be there to support it with reliable connectivity.](https://www.cogentco.com/about-cogent/cogent-blog/5077-cogent-for-financial-services)


[Read on for more](https://www.cogentco.com/about-cogent/cogent-blog/5077-cogent-for-financial-services)


Whether you are a trader, analyst, or managing customer accounts, having a dedicated internet connection is crucial to your success. Cogent offers[dedicated fiber connectivity](https://www.cogentco.com/products-and-services/internet/dedicated-internet-access) directly to your office suite in over 1,800 lit office buildings across North America, and can deliver high speed Internet service in millions of additional buildings.


Cogent's[VPN products](https://www.cogentco.com/products-and-services/vpn) give financial firms a private, alternative to the public internet. Whether you leverage MPLS IP-VPN, VPLS, or Ethernet Point-to-Point, our VPN services deliver private connectivity between your office locations, keeping sensitive client and trade data secure across multiple branch locations. These solutions ensure that a multi-office firm operates on one secure, reliable network.


In Financial Services, timing is everything. Getting your data from point A to Z as fast as possible is critical – especially for trading, and Cogent's[Optical Wavelengths](https://www.cogentco.com/products-and-services/transport/optical-wavelengths) are an ideal solution. On our wholly-owned North American wave network, we can deliver dedicated connectivity that’s lower latency and diverse from other carriers. Cogent can provide up to 400G capacity across more than 1,100 data centers in North America, and if you need more capacity, we can design a custom solution for you.


Cogent's full product catalog also gives you an[on-ramp to cloud providers](https://www.cogentco.com/solutions/cloud-connect-solutions) like AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud. Whether you're storing documentation or running complex algorithms, a strong and secure pathway ensures everything runs without interruption, and makes the cloud a seamless extension of your network.


The financial industry has always been at the forefront of progress. To keep moving forward, you need an internet partner you can count on, and Cogent is ready to help get you there.
