# Plan Ahead of The Holiday

## **💰 Plan Before You Spend**


Decide how much you can comfortably spend on gifts, food, and decorations. Setting a clear limit helps avoid impulse purchases that add up fast.


## **📝 Create a Holiday Budget**


List everyone you’re buying for and assign a spending limit for each. Don’t forget small costs like wrapping paper, postage, and party supplies. Tracking even small expenses keeps your plan on track.


## **🎁 Get Creative**


Thoughtful, homemade, or experience-based gifts can be more meaningful than expensive items. Secret Santa swaps or group gifts are fun and budget-friendly.


## **📅 Start Saving Early**


After the holidays, consider a holiday savings account. Small monthly deposits can make next year’s celebrations stress-free.
