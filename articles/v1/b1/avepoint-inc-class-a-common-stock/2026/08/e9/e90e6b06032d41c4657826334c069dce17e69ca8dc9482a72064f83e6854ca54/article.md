# Introducing AI Agent Management: Discover and Monitor AI Agents Across Every Microsoft 365 Tenant

Do you know how many AI agents are running across your customers’ Microsoft 365 environments, who created them, or what business data they can access?


For many partners, that answer is not yet clear.


As organizations adopt Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Studio, SharePoint agents, and other Microsoft solutions, AI agents are multiplying across the business faster than most IT teams can discover, govern, or optimize. This growth mirrors broader market trends:[93% of enterprises](https://www.avepoint.com/ebooks/ai-opportunity-governance-msp-research) are already using AI applications, while 91% have implemented AI-related security policies or controls, highlighting how governance is becoming an important part of daily operations.


Customers are increasingly relying on managed service providers (MSPs) to guide AI readiness, establish oversight practices, and support long-term adoption. But without a centralized view of users, workspaces, content, and AI agents across tenants, partners struggle to support adoption, optimize costs, and deliver effective AI governance services at scale.


## What is AI Agent Sprawl?


AI agent sprawl refers to the rapid growth of AI agents across an organization without effective management and oversight.


As AI agents expand across platforms such as Microsoft 365, Power Platform, Salesforce, and standalone AI tools, organizations can struggle to understand their AI landscape. This creates challenges around ownership, data access, usage, and risk.[According to The Road to AI Readiness study](https://www.avepoint.com/ebooks/ai-opportunity-governance-msp-research) , 51% of partners cite governance and compliance as the primary barrier to customer AI adoption, ahead of data security, value realization, and technical expertise concerns.


**Common customer challenges include:**


- Fragmented and siloed AI agents
- Increased security and compliance risks
- Rising costs from unused or unmanaged agents
- Limited visibility and governance


For MSPs managing multiple Microsoft 365 tenants, these challenges grow across every customer environment. Without centralized visibility, understanding AI activity across multiple customer tenants can become time-consuming and operationally inefficient.
