# AvePoint Innovates: Building Trust in an AI-Powered Organization

Trust in an AI-powered organization is earned through resilience, governance, and optimization. These are not separate challenges; a security incident can quickly become a resilience issue, a governance gap can drive unnecessary costs, and a single AI agent can expose all three at once.


That's why this quarter's AvePoint Innovates focused on helping organizations strengthen confidence across their digital ecosystem. Here's a look at the latest innovations across the[AvePoint Confidence Platform](https://www.avepoint.com/products/confidence-platform) .


## **Resilience: Recovering What Matters**


AI can only deliver value when the systems, data, and identities behind it remain available. This quarter's resilience innovations help organizations recover faster across identities, infrastructure, SaaS applications, and emerging AI workloads.

## **Governance and Security: Knowing What Matters Before Something Goes Wrong**


If resilience is about recovering what matters, governance is about understanding and protecting it before issues arise. AI didn’t create data sprawl or overexposure, but it has made both far more visible and far more urgent to address. As data continuously evolves, governance must evolve with it. That requires classification that adapts alongside the data it is designed to protect and manage.


### Introducing Kinetic Classification


Traditional classification has often been treated as a point-in-time exercise.[Kinetic Classification](https://www.avepoint.com/lp/intelligent-classification-model) , now in[private preview](https://www.avepoint.com/events/private-preview) , introduces a continuous approach built on the Unified Scan Engine customers already use:


1. **Configure.** Define what’s important, including sensitive data, risk indicators, and business priorities.
2. **Discover.** Understand what data exists, where it lives, and how it is growing.
3. **Classify.** Apply intelligence and context to identify risk, sensitivity, and business value.
4. **Secure.** Enforce policies, manage access, and monitor activity.
5. **Manage the lifecycle.** Archive, retain, or remove data based on business needs.


Data changes constantly, so classification must evolve with it. That’s the trusted data foundation organizations need to adopt AI with confidence.


### AgentPulse Expands to Salesforce Agentforce


Governance begins with visibility. As organizations manage AI agents across multiple platforms, maintaining a unified view becomes increasingly difficult.


[AgentPulse](https://www.avepoint.com/solutions/agentic-ai-governance) now extends beyond Microsoft environments to include Salesforce Agentforce, enabling customers to inventory agents, understand activity, and centralize visibility across their AI ecosystem. This expansion marks another step toward AvePoint's broader vision for agent management.


### Automated Owner Identification: Phase 2


Earlier this year, AvePoint introduced automated owner identification to help organizations pinpoint likely owners for workspaces and content without manual guesswork.


Phase 2 makes those ownership insights faster, more scalable, and more transparent. Ownership insights are now generated from more efficient aggregated views rather than large volumes of raw data, and administrators can see live progress as recommendations move through the pipeline.


### MyHub: Governance Moves Into the Data Owner’s Workflow


Identifying ownership is only the first step. Effective governance also requires making it easy for owners to take action.


Through[MyHub](https://www.avepoint.com/products/data-owner-engagement) , governance insights and remediation actions are embedded directly into SharePoint and Teams so data owners can understand issues, assess impact, and address concerns within their existing workflows. By empowering the people closest to the data, organizations can scale governance more effectively while reducing reliance on IT.


### Lifecycle Governance Expands Further


The same ownership and accountability model continues to expand across the digital estate:


- **Solution Renewal.** Extends lifecycle management to complete Power Platform business solutions, including ownership, promotion, compliance, and renewal processes.
- **Enterprise Applications Governance.** Approaching release, this capability automatically discovers applications, confirms ownership, and streamlines renewal workflows.
- **Azure Resource Group Governance.** Coming soon, this capability provides visibility into ownership, lifecycle management, and cost across Azure resources and subscriptions.
