GunBroker.com Announces Slingit Integration for AI-Powered Used Gun Workflow


GunBroker.com, the world's largest online marketplace for firearms, hunting, shooting sports and related products, today announced an integration with Slingit, the AI-powered platform helping firearm dealers buy and sell used guns smarter and faster.


The integration connects the used gun counter directly to GunBroker.com, giving dealers a streamlined path from evaluating and acquiring a firearm to creating and publishing a professional GunBroker.com listing.


With the Slingit AI Sidekick running on a mobile device, dealers follow a fast, consistent process to appraise and acquire a firearm from a gun owner at the counter. A series of in-app photos are used to read the serial number, identify the firearm and included accessories, apply a condition grade, and deliver a suggested fair-market valuation in as little as five minutes.


Once acquired, Slingit transforms the appraisal photos into studio-quality listing images using AI-powered background removal and replacement, lighting enhancements, borders, and dealer logo overlays. It then creates optimized titles and descriptions designed to reach buyers on GunBroker.com and through search engines (SEO) and answer engines (AEO).


The dealer reviews the completed listing and publishes it directly to GunBroker.com in just a few clicks.


The result is a more modern workflow for used guns. Dealers can move inventory from acquisition to a professional GunBroker.com listing in as little as five minutes - helping them bring more inventory online, reach GunBroker.com's nationwide audience, and keep their teams focused on customers at the counter.


"I built GunBroker.com to give buyers and sellers across the country an easy to use, trusted place to do business and supporting dealers to power the marketplace has always been central to our philosophy." Said Steve Urvan, founder of GunBroker.com and Chief Executive Officer of its parent, Outdoor Holding Company. "We continually work to streamline and improve the selling process. Slingit takes friction out of selling used inventory. By pairing its AI tools with the reach of GunBroker.com, we are giving dealers of every size a faster and easier way to put quality used firearms in front of millions of prospective buyers nationwide."


"Slingit was built to help dealers grow their used gun business," said Walt Geer, CEO of Slingit. "Our AI Sidekick helps every employee become an expert on virtually every firearm in circulation and streamlines the work required to create effective online listings. By connecting Slingit with GunBroker.com, we close the loop - from acquisition to a professional listing that reaches buyers nationwide."


The GunBroker.com and Slingit integration is available now to participating firearm dealers. To learn more, visit Slingit.com.
