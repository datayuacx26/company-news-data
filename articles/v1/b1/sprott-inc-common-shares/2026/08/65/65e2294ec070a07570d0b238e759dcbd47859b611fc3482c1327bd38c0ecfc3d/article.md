# Celebrating Five Years of Sprott Physical Uranium Trust (SPUT)

Five years ago, the[Sprott Physical Uranium Trust (TSX U.U)](https://sprott.com/investment-strategies/exchange-listed-products/physical-commodity-funds/uranium/) — fondly called "SPUT"— launched a new chapter in uranium investing. Since debuting in July 2021, SPUT has become the world's largest physical uranium fund,* providing investors with a transparent, exchange-listed vehicle that offers exposure to physical uranium without the operational risks associated with uranium mining companies. As the Trust marks its fifth anniversary, it stands as an important milestone for both Sprott and investors seeking access to the long-term nuclear energy theme.


## Five Things We've Learned in Five Years of SPUT


1. **Nuclear energy has re-entered the global conversation.**
2. **Investor interest in physical uranium has grown significantly.**
3. **Physical uranium remains a unique asset class.**
4. **The long-term investment case is driven by fundamentals, not short-term headlines.**
5. **SPUT continues to provide a simple, transparent way to access physical uranium.**


## 2021 to 2026


Over the past five years, the investment case for uranium has continued to evolve. Governments around the world have increasingly embraced nuclear power as a critical source of reliable, low-carbon electricity, driven by growing electricity demand, energy security concerns and global decarbonization goals. At the same time, investors have sought efficient ways to participate in these long-term trends. Throughout this period, SPUT has remained focused on a straightforward objective: holding physical uranium on behalf of investors through a simple, transparent investment structure.


As we celebrate SPUT's fifth anniversary, we want to acknowledge the investors, partners and industry participants who have supported the Trust since its inception. While the uranium market has experienced periods of both enthusiasm and volatility, the long-term fundamentals supporting nuclear energy continue to attract attention. We look forward to continuing to provide investors with access to one of the world's most important energy commodities for years to come.
