# Supabase Beta October 2021

We released three new Auth providers, multi-schema support, and we're gearing up for another Launch Week.


### Quick recap#


Watch a recap of this month's release.


## Slack, Spotify, and MessageBird logins#


Thanks to[@HarryET](https://github.com/supabase/gotrue/pull/245) and our friends at[MessageBird](https://github.com/supabase/gotrue/pull/210) we have 3 new Auth providers this month:[Slack](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/social-login/auth-slack) ,[Spotify](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/social-login/auth-spotify) , and[MessageBird](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/phone-login/messagebird) phone logins.


## Multi-schema support#


### Dashboard#


Browse data in any database schema using the Schema switcher in the Dashboard.


### API#


You can access any schema with your API, after enabling access in the Dashboard.[Docs](https://supabase.com/docs/reference/javascript/initializing#api-schemas) .
