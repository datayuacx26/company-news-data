# Kristian Kerr

# Kristian Kerr


Head of Macro Strategy


Kristian Kerr serves as LPL Financial’s Head of Macro Strategy. In this role, Kristian leads the firm’s Strategic and Tactical Asset Allocation Committee (STAAC). He is responsible for driving LPL’s broader house investment strategy and expanding the adoption of the STAAC’s voice and views across the LPL platform.


## Before joining LPL Financial


Kristian began his career in 1999 and has a strong and diverse background in global financial markets. Prior to joining LPL, he was the Western Region Head of Foreign Exchange & Macro at Citi Private Bank in San Francisco. Before Citi, Kristian spent seven years as a Multi-Asset Portfolio Manager in New York for a large global macro hedge fund. Kristian has also held various strategy and trading roles at other global financial institutions over the course of his career.


### Education & Achievements


BBA in International Business, Schiller International University in Madrid, Spain.
