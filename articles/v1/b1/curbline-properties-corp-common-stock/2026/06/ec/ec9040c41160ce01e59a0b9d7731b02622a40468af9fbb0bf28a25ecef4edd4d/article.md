### Curbline Properties Issues 2025 Corporate Sustainability Report


June 30, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/417291320/files/doc_news/2026/06/Sustainability-Press-Release-6-30-26_vF.pdf)


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Curbline Properties Corp. (NYSE: CURB), an owner of convenience centers in suburban, high household income communities, announced today the release of its 2025 Corporate Sustainability Report. The report is Curbline’s first overview of the Company’s sustainability efforts and was completed in alignment with the Task Force on Climate Related Financial Disclosures (TCFD).


“I am pleased to present Curbline Properties' 2025 Corporate Sustainability Report, marking both our first report as a standalone company and our first full year of sustainability reporting. 2025 was a year of significant growth for Curbline as we look to scale the fragmented but liquid convenience marketplace expanding our portfolio by 81 properties,” said David R. Lukes, President and Chief Executive Officer. “This report provides an overview of our efforts, establishes important baseline metrics that will help guide future decision-making, and provides transparency for our stakeholders.”


**2025 Report Highlights:**


- Established and executed on an underwriting framework for acquisitions providing a path to maximize operational efficiencies and incorporate appropriate energy sustainability initiatives into the onboarding process
- Invested almost $1 million into new and more efficient HVAC units which are estimated to have reduced annual energy usage by approximately 23%
- Implemented systems to measure and track utility usage across the portfolio to gauge like-for-like performance going forward
- Inventoried green and public transportation across the portfolio as part of the Company’s nascent efforts to expand options for shoppers, vendors and tenant employees
- A Nominating and Sustainability Committee composed entirely of independent directors strives to ensure that the Board of Directors has an appropriate balance of knowledge, experience, skills, expertise and diversity required for our Board as a whole and oversees the Company’s sustainability efforts


**About Curbline Properties**


Curbline Properties is an owner and manager of convenience shopping centers positioned on the curbline of well-trafficked intersections and major vehicular corridors in suburban, high household income communities. The Company is a self-managed real estate investment trust (REIT) that is publicly traded under the ticker symbol “CURB” on the NYSE.
