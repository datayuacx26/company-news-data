We are proud to share that Kayne Anderson Real Estate has been named Best Asset Manager of 2025 (Real Estate – Best of the Best, Mid Cap) by[The Korea Economic Daily](https://www.linkedin.com/company/the-korea-economic-daily/) Global Edition, in collaboration with leading institutional investors in Korea ([The Korean Investors](https://www.linkedin.com/company/the-korean-investors/) ).
