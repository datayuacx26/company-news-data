# Proven Robotics for Contested Maritime Environments

In today’s rapidly evolving maritime mission space, one question matters more than any other: when the environment is contested, which systems are ready to perform?


Today’s maritime operations demand more than advanced technology. They demand systems that can operate in hostile conditions, extend mission reach and reduce risk to personnel without sacrificing reliability. Robotics and autonomous systems are delivering that advantage now.


The difference isn’t autonomy alone. The difference is proven performance.


### **Built for Real-World Conditions**


Contested maritime environments introduce uncertainty: Limited communications, harsh conditions and fast-changing missions. Systems must perform without ideal conditions and without constant oversight.


The Aerosonde® Uncrewed Aircraft System (UAS) is built for that reality. It’s designed to support expeditionary, land- and sea-based operations while delivering the persistence and flexibility required for modern maritime missions. With more than 700,000 flight hours, Textron Systems is constantly improving the reliability and efficacy of autonomous craft in real operational environments.


Mission success on the water also requires capable surface assets. Textron Systems’ uncrewed surface vehicle (USV) portfolio, including the TSUNAMI® USV, CUSV® and MMUSV, bring the same proven, multi-domain performance to the maritime environment. Designed for modularity, endurance and autonomous operation, these USVs support missions ranging from reconnaissance to payload delivery to mine countermeasures – providing the coverage, reach and survivability needed in contested seas.


### **Proven in the Harshest Environments**


With more than 40 payload integrations available and a variety of size and power configurations, the Aerosonde family of UAS is built to support complex, evolving mission sets. From intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance (ISR) to communications relay and beyond, it gives operators the flexibility to configure capability based on mission need.


Textron Systems’ USVs are built for real world performance, combining rugged architectures, open systems design and scalable autonomy to operate independently or as part of a larger unmanned team. With proven payload integration, advanced command and control and modular mission kits, they deliver dependable surface capability when conditions challenge traditional platforms. A core strength of the portfolio is autonomous mine-countermeasure capability: integrating high-resolution sonar, autonomous route planning and precision payload deployment to detect, classify and neutralize naval mines while keeping personnel at a safe distance. In contested logistics environments, where undersea threats can halt mobility, USV based mine clearing restores access and protects critical maritime routes.


In surface‑warfare mission profiles, TSUNAMI’s ability to host coordinated target‑tracking sensors and demonstrate guided‑munition employment concepts provides commanders with an adaptable tool for maritime domain awareness and precision effects.


### **Persistence That Extends the Mission**


Aerosonde delivers long-endurance, ship-based capability that supports continuous awareness and informed decision-making. Its ability to integrate multiple payloads allows operators to adapt in real time without introducing new platforms or additional logistical burden.


USVs further extend mission persistence on the surface, offering multi-role utility from littoral sensing to route reconnaissance to minefield clearance. Together, they expand operational capacity, reduce complexity and increase confidence in contested waters.


### **A Portfolio That Works Together**


Maritime superiority depends on more than a single platform. It depends on how systems work together to deliver capability across domains.


The Ship-to-Shore Connector (SSC) plays a critical role in that equation. As the next generation of the Landing Craft, Air Cushion, SSC provides a modernized, reliable means of transporting personnel and payloads from ship to shore.


With its agile movement across land and sea, SSC can reach contested or dangerous shores giving warfighters the access they need. The Aerosonde UAS extends awareness, increases standoff and supports distributed operations from sea-based platforms. Textron Systems’ USVs add autonomous surface capability that enhances security, clears hazards and strengthens resilient logistics.


This isn’t about one platform doing everything. It’s about the right systems working together so operators can move faster, see more and operate smarter.


### **The Advantage Is Readiness**


In contested environments, the margin for error is small. Operators need systems that reduce risk, maintain tempo and adapt as missions change. They need technology that performs and delivers in real-world conditions.


Textron Systems continues to provide solutions that are proven, adaptable and ready to support the mission wherever it leads. When it matters most, readiness wins.
