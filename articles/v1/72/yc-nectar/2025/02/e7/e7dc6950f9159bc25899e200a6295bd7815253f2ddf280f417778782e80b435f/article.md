# Nettzero Partners with Nectar for ESG Reporting

NettZero, an Australian sustainability platform, has announced a strategic partnership with US-based utility intelligence developer Nectar. The collaboration brings together NettZero's expertise in sustainability consulting and reporting with Nectar's automated utility data collection technology, creating a more comprehensive offering for organizations navigating increasingly complex emissions reporting requirements.


## A shifting regulatory landscape


Australia's sustainability landscape is evolving rapidly. Stricter regulations around corporate emissions disclosure, combined with growing stakeholder expectations, have made accurate and timely environmental data a business imperative rather than a nice-to-have. Many organizations still rely on manual processes to gather utility data — a practice that introduces delays, errors, and gaps that undermine the credibility of sustainability reports. The partnership between NettZero and Nectar addresses this challenge head-on.


## Upgraded service offerings


By integrating Nectar's automated data collection technology, NettZero has significantly upgraded its service capabilities. Nectar's platform connects directly with utility providers to collect electricity, gas, water, and waste billing data automatically, eliminating the manual effort that has traditionally slowed down the reporting process. This enables NettZero to offer its clients more accurate tracking and reporting of Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions, backed by auditable data that flows continuously rather than in delayed batches.


## Tailored solutions for emission reporting


The partnership allows NettZero to deliver tailored emission reporting solutions that combine automated data ingestion with localized sustainability expertise. For Australian businesses facing new disclosure mandates, this means a faster path to compliance with less internal resource strain. The combined offering covers the full reporting lifecycle — from raw utility data collection through to calculated emissions and formatted reports ready for regulatory submission.


As regulatory frameworks continue to tighten globally, partnerships like this one between NettZero and Nectar demonstrate how specialized technology and domain expertise can come together to make sustainability reporting more reliable, efficient, and scalable.
