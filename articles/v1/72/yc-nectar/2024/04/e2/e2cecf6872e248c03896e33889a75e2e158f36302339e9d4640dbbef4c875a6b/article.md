# Best utility data management solutions

Choosing the right utility data management solution can make the difference between a sustainability program that runs on solid data and one that constantly struggles with gaps and inaccuracies. We evaluated the leading strategies and platforms for precise utility data gathering, scoring each on four key criteria: **Automation** , **Coverage & Accuracy** , **Cost & Scalability** , and **Additional Features** .


## 1. Nectar


Nectar offers fully automated utility data collection with global coverage spanning electricity, gas, water, and waste providers. The platform connects directly to utility portals, handles two-factor authentication workflows, and ingests billing data continuously without manual intervention. Nectar's parsing engine extracts line-item detail from bills — not just top-level totals — giving teams granular visibility into demand charges, rate structures, and consumption patterns. For organizations managing dozens or hundreds of sites, Nectar scales without requiring proportional increases in headcount.


## 2. Arcadia


Arcadia provides partial automation for utility data collection, primarily focused on the US market. While the platform covers a broad set of domestic utilities, its international coverage is limited. Arcadia works well for organizations with a predominantly US-based portfolio, but companies with global operations may find coverage gaps that require supplementary manual processes.


## 3. Carbon accounting platform built-in features


Many carbon accounting platforms — such as Persefoni, Watershed, and Sweep — include basic utility data ingestion capabilities. These built-in features are convenient for teams already using the platform for emissions calculations and reporting, but they typically offer limited automation and rely heavily on manual uploads or CSV imports. For organizations with complex utility portfolios, these features alone are rarely sufficient.
