#### Work Opportunity Tax Credit (WOTC) FAQ


##### What is the Work Opportunity Tax Credit (WOTC)?


WOTC is a federal tax credit available to employers who hire individuals from certain targeted groups facing barriers to employment.


##### How much can employers save with WOTC?


The credit amount varies based on employee eligibility and hours worked, but it can provide meaningful reductions in federal tax liability over time.


##### Who qualifies for WOTC?


Eligibility depends on whether a new hire belongs to a designated target group, as defined by federal guidelines.


##### Why do employers need help with WOTC?


The program includes strict deadlines, complex requirements, and ongoing tracking, making it difficult to manage without a dedicated solution.


##### How does BBSI support WOTC participation?


BBSI coordinates enrollment and integrates the program into your operations, while Equifax manages screening, compliance, and reporting.


##### Do clients pay upfront for the program?


No. Clients are only billed once tax credits are successfully realized.
