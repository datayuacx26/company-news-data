## How It Works


### Structural Analysis


Punchcard reads the full document structure — column headers, row labels, footnote references — to understand what each number represents.


### Mathematical Accuracy


Every footing and cross-footing is checked — line items against totals, subtotals against grand totals, across every table in the filing.


### Prior Year Consistency


Prior year comparatives are checked against the actual prior year filing to catch numbers that drifted between periods.


### Internal Consistency


Cross-references between the financial statements and footnotes are verified — same account, same number, everywhere it appears.


### Review Checklist


You get a clear set of exceptions with exact page references, variance amounts, and in-product tickmarks to demonstrate completeness.
