# What does CARE Certified mean?

In an era where consumers are increasingly concerned about the ethics and stories behind their food choices, the CARE Certified program stands out as a comprehensive certification.


CARE Certified is a sustainability standard that verifies:


-


Animal welfare


-


Environmental stewardship


-


Community impact


By having three specific pillars, it provides a


**holistic approach** to responsible food production.


###


What Does CARE Certified Mean?


CARE Certified is a sustainability standard that signifies a commitment to humane animal care practices, environmental responsibility, and community well-being. For farmers and ranchers raising your food, this certification indicates that their operations adhere to rigorous standards designed to ensure the health and welfare of animals, land, and the community.


###


CARE Certified for Beef


As of today, the program includes:


-


1,200+ ranches


-


33 states


-


Over 1.1 million certified animals


It ensures animals are raised with attention to welfare, handling, and overall care.


The certification aims to provide consumers with verified humane beef options, helping them make informed choices aligned with their values.


###


What Expectations Are Included in CARE Certified?


The CARE Certified program encompasses a set of robust requirements designed to ensure the humane treatment of animals.


Some key components of the


**Animal Care** pillar include:


-


Handler / Employee Training


-


Antibiotic Stewardship


-


Safe Transportation


-


Animal Identification


-


Secure Supply Chain Procedures/Processes


-


Animal Health and Nutrition Plan


-


Allow Cattle to Express Natural Behaviors


Some key components of the


**Environmental Stewardship** pillar include:


-


Land and Water Resources


-


Grazing Management Plan


-


Animal Productivity


-


Waste Management


-


Energy Reduction


-


Long-term Strategy


Some key components of the


**People & Community** pillar include:


-


Safe Working Environment


-


Employee Growth and Development Opportunities


-


Legacy and Succession Planning


-


Local Community Support


##


How CARE Certified Is Verified


Verification to the CARE Certified standard involves a thorough process designed to ensure compliance with the established requirements. This includes:


1.


**Initial Assessment**


Producers undergo an initial assessment to evaluate their animal care practices against the CARE Certification standards.


1.


**Annual on-Farm Audits**


Annual on-farm audits are conducted to verify adherence to standards. These audits assess various aspects of animal welfare, environmental stewardship, and people and community-focused practices.


1.


**Documentation Review**


Producers are required to maintain comprehensive records that demonstrate compliance with the animal care standards. These documents and records are reviewed by on-site auditors and reviewers to ensure they meet the standards.


1.


**Corrective Actions**


If any deficiencies are identified during audits, producers must implement corrective actions to address the issues and improve their practices.


1.


**Ongoing Annual Review**


Annual reviews ensure that standards are maintained, and necessary adjustments are made to uphold the integrity of the CARE Certified label and the standard itself.


##


Who Verifies CARE Certified?


CARE Certified is verified onsite by independent third-party auditors and internal reviewers who specialize in animal welfare and sustainability standards. These auditors and reviewers assess compliance with the CARE Certification requirements through on-site visits and comprehensive evaluations that are aligned in the CARE Certified Standard.


**Why It Stands Out**


While many animal welfare programs exist, CARE Certified distinguishes itself by integrating animal care with environmental stewardship and community impact. This holistic approach ensures that all aspects of sustainability are addressed. Compared to other certifications, CARE Certified emphasizes:


-


Comprehensive Standards: CARE Certified includes criteria for animal welfare, environmental stewardship practices, and community engagement, making it a well-rounded sustainability standard.


-


Transparency, Traceability and Verification: The rigorous verification process ensures that claims are substantiated via traceability and verification, providing consumers with confidence in the products they purchase.


-


Educational Focus: CARE Certified encourages ongoing education and training for producers, fostering a culture of continuous improvement in animal welfare practices, as well as environmental and community and people support.


**Why Brands and Producers Use CARE Certified**


Brands and producers choose to pursue CARE Certified for several reasons:


-


Commitment to Sustainability: For producers, obtaining CARE Certified status reflects a genuine commitment to animal welfare, environmental stewardship, and community well-being, enhancing their reputation and credibility. They understand that many consumers today are very disconnected from the food they eat. They want to be able to share their story and their commitment to sustainability and providing a safe, nutritious product and participating in a program like CARE Certified allows that to be communicated through the CARE Certified label on food products.


-


Consumer Demand: As consumers become more conscious of their food choices, brands that offer CARE Certified products can meet the growing demand for ethically and sustainably produced beef, pork, poultry and dairy products.


-


Market Differentiation: The CARE Certified label sets products apart in a competitive marketplace, allowing brands to appeal to socially responsible consumers.


###


Common Questions About CARE Certified


###


**Is it regulated?**


Yes, CARE Certified operates under strict standards that are regularly reviewed and updated. A committee of experts in the focus areas of CARE also review the standards on an annual basis. The certification process ensures that producers adhere to established guidelines for animal welfare, environmental stewardship, and people and community. There are also traceability measures that must be in place to be approved as well, ensure complete supply-chain traceability of animals and products.


**Is it audited annually?**


Yes, CARE Certified producers participate in annual audits to ensure ongoing compliance with the certification standards and to continuously improve their practices. This helps maintain the integrity of the certification.


**What does it help promote?**


CARE Certified promotes the farms, brands and retailers that prioritize humane treatment of animals, adhere to environmental stewardship practices, and engage positively with their communities. It assures consumers that they are supporting ethical and sustainable food production.
