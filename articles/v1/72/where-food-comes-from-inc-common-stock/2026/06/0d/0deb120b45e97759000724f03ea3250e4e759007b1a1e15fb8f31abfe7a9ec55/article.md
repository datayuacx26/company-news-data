# Verified Natural Beef Certification Explained: How Ranchers Access Premium Natural Beef Markets

In today’s competitive beef industry, standing out requires more than quality, it requires proof. As consumer demand for


**natural beef, transparency, and verified production practices** continues to grow, ranchers are turning to


**Verified Natural Beef (VNB) certification** to elevate their operations.


The Verified Natural Beef program is a powerful step forward in the value-added certification journey, helping producers access premium markets while meeting strict natural production standards.


In this guide, we’ll break down what Verified Natural Beef certification is, how it compares to other programs, and why it’s a smart investment for ranchers, retailers, and consumers alike.


"Certified seal showcasing 'Where Food Comes From' verification for natural beef, ensuring quality and transparency in sourcing."


##


[What Is Verified Natural Beef (VNB)?](https://www.imiglobal.com/verification-standards)


**Verified Natural Beef (VNB)** is a certification program that verifies cattle are raised using


**strict natural production practices** , including:


-


No added growth hormones


-


No antibiotics


-


No animal by-products in feed


The program is administered by Where Food Comes From and aligns with


[USDA Process Verified Program (PVP)](https://www.ams.usda.gov/services/auditing/process-verified-programs) **** standards, ensuring credibility and third-party verification.


###


**Where VNB Fits in the Certification Ladder**


VNB builds on earlier programs, including:


-


Source Verified (traceability foundation)


-


Non-Hormone Treated Cattle (NHTC)


Once a rancher achieves VNB certification, they inherently meet the requirements for both Source Verified and NHTC, making it a


**high-efficiency, high-impact certification step** .


A herd of cows standing attentively in an open grassland under a clear blue sky, capturing the tranquility and vastness of rural landscapes.


##


**Verified Natural vs. Organic: What’s the Difference?**


This is one of the most common questions across the supply chain.


While both focus on natural practices,


**Verified Natural Beef and USDA Organic are not the same** .


###


**Key Differences:**


**Verified Natural Beef (VNB):**


-


No hormones


-


No antibiotics


-


No animal by-products


-


Focus on animal inputs and feeding practices


**USDA Organic:**


-


Includes all-natural requirements


-


Adds strict regulations on land use, feed sourcing, and inputs


-


Requires certified organic feed and farming systems


In short:


**Organic = broader agricultural system requirements**


**VNB = focused, flexible natural beef standard**

##


**Core Requirements of Verified Natural Beef Certification**


To qualify for VNB certification, producers must meet strict program standards:


###


**1. Source Verification Foundation**


All cattle must be Source Verified, meaning:


-


EID tags are used for traceability


-


Birth and movement records are documented


-


Origin is verifiable throughout the supply chain


###


**2. No Antibiotics or Hormones**


Cattle must be raised:


-


Without antibiotics


-


Without hormone growth promotants


This aligns directly with consumer demand for


**hormone-free and antibiotic-free beef** .


###


**3. No Animal By-Products**


Feed must exclude all animal by-products, ensuring a natural diet consistent with VNB standards.


###


**4. Annual Onsite Audits**


Producers must complete a


**comprehensive annual audit** , reviewing:


-


Animal handling practices


-


Feed and nutrition protocols


-


Recordkeeping systems


These audits ensure ongoing compliance and program integrity.
