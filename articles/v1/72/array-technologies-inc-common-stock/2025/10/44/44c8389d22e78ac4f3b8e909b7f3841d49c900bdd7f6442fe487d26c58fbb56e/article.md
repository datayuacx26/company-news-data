## ARRAY’s DuraTrack® & OmniTrack™ Verified by Intertek as Compatible With 2,000-Volt (2kV) Solar Module-Wired Systems


Intertek has verified[ARRAY’s DuraTrack®](https://arraytechinc.com/products/duratrack/) and[OmniTrack™](https://arraytechinc.com/products/omnitrack/) solar trackers have been certified by Intertek for compatibility with 2,000-volt systems under UL 3703 and UL 2703 standards. This confirms their readiness for higher-voltage solar architectures without modifications, enabling reduced electrical losses, lower material costs, and improved performance.
