# Purpose-Driven, People-Powered: MicroHealth Recognized as 2026 Top Workplace Culture Excellence & Technology Industry Honoree

By **Morgan Dingle** | Apr 3, 2026


Vienna, Virginia – Every day at MicroHealth, people show up with a shared purpose: to solve complex challenges, support critical federal missions, and do work that truly matters. That commitment — felt in project rooms, virtual standups, and quiet moments of problem-solving — is what has once again earned MicroHealth national recognition as a **2026 Technology Top Workplace** .


This marks the fourth consecutive year MicroHealth has been honored — not because of a single initiative or moment, but because of the collective experience of the people who power the organization. The award is based entirely on employee feedback gathered through the Energage engagement survey, making it a reflection of how it truly feels to work here.


### Where Culture Meets Action


At MicroHealth, culture isn’t defined by posters on a wall or words on a slide deck. It’s shaped by leaders who listen, teams that collaborate, and an environment where individuals are trusted to bring their full selves to the work.


One team member shared, “I love my job because it offers numerous challenges that inspire me to grow and expand my knowledge.”


Another reflected, “The leaders are very supportive they make sure that you have all the resources needed to be successful.”


That lived experience is reflected not only in individual voices, but in the breadth of recognition MicroHealth received through the 2026 Top Workplaces Culture Excellence Awards. These honors highlight the specific ways our people-first values show up every day — across leadership, growth, flexibility, and care for the whole person. Together, they tell a deeper story about how culture at MicroHealth translates into meaningful action.


**In 2026, MicroHealth was recognized for excellence in:**


- Innovation
- Work‑Life Flexibility
- Compensation & Benefits
- Leadership
- Purpose & Values
- Employee Appreciation
- Professional Development
- Employee Well‑Being
- Technology Industry Top Workplace


These Culture Excellence awards reinforce what our people have been telling us all along: when employees feel supported, heard, and empowered, they do their best work. At MicroHealth, culture isn’t a single initiative — it’s a system of trust, care, and opportunity that continues to grow stronger because of the people who make it real every day.


### Supporting the Whole Person


That sense of empowerment is reinforced by a people-first approach that extends beyond the workplace. From comprehensive health benefits and tuition reimbursement to generous paid time off and spiritual wellness resources, MicroHealth is intentional about supporting employees at every stage of life and career.


This investment isn’t just about benefits — it’s about trust. It’s about creating space for growth, balance, and sustainability, so employees can thrive both professionally and personally. That commitment continues to make MicroHealth a sought-after place to work within the federal IT community.


The Culture Excellence & Technology Industry Top Workplaces awards stands apart because it reflects authentic employee experiences. Energage’s research-backed survey measures engagement, alignment, trust, and connection — the drivers of strong organizational health. MicroHealth’s results demonstrate a workplace grounded in collaboration, transparency, and shared purpose.


### About the Award


Energage champions organizations that put people first, partnering with employers to strengthen their workplace cultures and elevate their employer brands. Through the Top Workplaces program — now in its 18th year — Energage recognizes outstanding organizations across the country and in more than 60 regional markets.


The Technology Top Workplaces distinction specifically honors tech companies that prioritize trust, engagement, and employee well-being. Winners are determined solely by the voices of their workforce through a confidential, research-backed engagement survey.


The Culture Excellence Top Workplaces Awards go a step further by recognizing organizations that excel in specific areas of the employee experience. Each distinction is earned based on how employees rate their workplace across key cultural drivers — from leadership and growth to flexibility, well‑being, and purpose. Together, these awards reflect where culture isn’t just defined — it’s consistently lived, reinforced, and felt across the organization.


According to Energage CEO Eric Rubino, “A Top Workplaces award carries special meaning because it comes directly from employees. In today’s environment, organizations succeed when leaders listen, create space for honest feedback, and ensure people feel truly heard. Companies that do this well see lasting results.”


For MicroHealth, this recognition isn’t an endpoint. It’s a milestone — and a reminder that the most impactful achievements are built together.
