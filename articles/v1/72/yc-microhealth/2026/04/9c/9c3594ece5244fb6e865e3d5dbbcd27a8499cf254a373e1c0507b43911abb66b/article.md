# Winning Where It Works: MicroHealth USA TODAY 2026 Top Workplace Honoree

By **Morgan Dingle** | Apr 9, 2026


Estimated reading time:


1


minute


Vienna, Virginia – MicroHealth LLC, an Information Technology firm supporting federal missions nationwide, has once again been named a **2026 USA TODAY Top Workplaces Award recipient,** joining a select group of just **924 organizations nationwide** recognized for exceptional workplace culture among companies with fewer than 500 employees. The award reflects MicroHealth’s continued focus on building an environment where employees feel valued, supported, and empowered.


TheUSA TODAY Top Workplaces award is based entirely on **employee feedback** collected through a confidential survey administered by Energage. MicroHealth employees provided insights and securing our team **10 key Culture Badges** that highlight our strongest culture drivers, measure engagement, alignment, performance, and satisfaction. This feedback placed MicroHealth among the top‑rated organizations in its peer group.


Heidi Ashbaugh, Senior Manager of Communications and Marketing at MicroHealth, shared, “Being named a Top Workplace year after year is especially meaningful because it reflects the voices of our employees through the annual survey. It’s rewarding to know the energy our employees and leaders invest in creating a fun, supportive environment is truly making an impact.”


MicroHealth supports its employees with a well‑rounded benefits package that includes a 401(k) with an immediate 6% employer match, comprehensive insurance coverage, and meaningful workplace perks. Together, these offerings help create an environment where employees can focus on both professional excellence and personal well‑being.
