# MicroHealth CTO Andrew Jacobs Honored with Prestigious Accelerate 125 Health IT Leaders Award for AI Innovation

By **Morgan Dingle** | Dec 10, 2025


Vienna, Virginia – December 10, 2025 – MicroHealth LLC, a leading Information Technology company, proudly announces that Chief Technology Officer Andrew Jacobs has been recognized as an Accelerate 125 Health IT Award honoree for his pioneering work in generative artificial intelligence and secure enterprise AI solutions.


The ACCELERATE 125 Health IT Awards, presented on Dec. 9, 2025, in Reston, Virginia, celebrate visionaries who are redefining excellence across emerging technologies including AI and Data, Agentic AI, Cybersecurity, Quantum Computing, and UX/UI. Jacobs joins an elite group of innovators recognized for advancing innovation, driving impact, and championing digital transformation across the federal landscape.


“Generative AI is more than just technology,” said Jacobs, CTO of MicroHealth. “It is a tool to help people work smarter, make better decisions, and solve complex problems responsibly.[MicroHealth’s proprietary generative AI platform](https://www.microhealthllc.com/solutions/maiko/) shows what happens when innovation meets security and accessibility.”


### Transforming Federal Health IT Through Secure AI Innovation


Under Jacobs’ technical leadership, MicroHealth has developed innovative artificial intelligence solutions that prioritize flexibility, security, and usability for federal healthcare organizations. His team has created advanced AI architectures that support multiple large language models simultaneously, enabling organizations to select optimal AI tools for specific use cases rather than being constrained by single-vendor solutions.


Jacobs has championed the implementation of enterprise-grade security protocols that protect sensitive healthcare data and personally identifiable information (PII) while delivering powerful AI capabilities that meet federal compliance requirements, including HIPAA, FISMA, and FedRAMP standards. His vision for accessibility has resulted in AI solutions that function seamlessly across all platforms—desktop computers, mobile devices, and tablets—ensuring federal healthcare workers can leverage artificial intelligence regardless of their device or location.


### Advanced AI Capabilities Driving Federal Healthcare Innovation


Additionally, Jacobs directed the development of advanced retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) techniques and prompt engineering methodologies that significantly improve AI accuracy, reliability, and contextual awareness compared to standard large language model implementations. His team has created flexible integration frameworks that enable federal healthcare organizations to incorporate AI capabilities into existing workflows and systems without extensive custom development—reducing implementation timelines from months to days while maintaining security and compliance.


As technical ambassador for MicroHealth’s AI initiatives, Jacobs has played an instrumental role in hundreds of product demonstrations for federal healthcare agencies, personally showcasing MAIKO’s capabilities while pioneering advanced safeguarding technologies that deliver contextually-aware, accurate responses. His leadership has positioned MicroHealth as a trusted leader in secure enterprise AI solutions for federal health IT.
