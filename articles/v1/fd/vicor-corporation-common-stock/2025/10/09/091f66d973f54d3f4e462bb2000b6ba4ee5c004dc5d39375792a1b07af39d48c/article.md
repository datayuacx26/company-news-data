# Vicor and Macnica to co-host seminar on heat dissipation methods and thermal design using power modules

Vicor K.K Japan (Headquarters: Shinagawa ward, Tokyo) will hold an online power system design seminar co-hosted by Macnica and Vicor on Wednesday, November 26th at 2:00 pm.


Following popular requests during our power supply webinar held in May, Koji Nishikawa, Field Application Engineer at Vicor K.K., will address thermal design and methods of efficiently dissipating heat while ensuring reliability within limited space and at minimal cost.


Power delivery networks (PDNs) built with Vicor power modules are thermally adept and highly efficient. Power modules not only reduce losses and simplify thermal design, but also enable high power supply using techniques such as liquid cooling.


If you are having trouble with significant heat generated by high-power supply systems or need to conform to allowable temperatures of components and avoid operational shutdowns, register today.
