# Vicor presents the weight saving benefits of DC-DC modular converters for EV applications

Effective implementation of active roll control or suspension systems have demanding power requirements—fast transient speeds and high performance are essential to optimize these applications. The current best in class technologies require the addition of either batteries or super capacitors to comply with these system needs, and the resulting systems add weight and consume package space in the vehicle.


Vicor will present at the 5th China Automotive Intelligent Chassis Conference 2025 a compact power system that delivers the highest power density DC-DC conversion modules and best in class power transient capabilities to support active chassis systems. These solutions reduce the power system package space requirements by over 60%, and reduce weight by over 50%.
