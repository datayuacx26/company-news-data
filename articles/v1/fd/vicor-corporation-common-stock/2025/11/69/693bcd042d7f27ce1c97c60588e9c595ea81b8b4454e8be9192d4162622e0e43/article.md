# Vicor Factorized Power Architecture to be showcased at Marubun booth at Edge Tech+ 2025

Andover, MA, November 5, 2025 – Vicor Corporation (Andover, MA) announced that Vicor K.K Japan (Headquarters: Shinagawa ward, Tokyo) will showcase its portfolio of high-performance DC-DC converters at the booth of MARUBUN CORPORATION (Headquarters: Chuo-ku, Tokyo) at Edge Tech+ 2025 on Nov 19 – 21, 2025.


Featured products include the Vicor Vertical Power Delivery (VPD) board, which incorporates Vicor proprietary Factorized power architecture (FPA). FPA separates the functions of voltage regulation and transformation, enabling high-power density, improved efficiency, and superior thermal performance compared to conventional architectures.


In addition to the VPD board, the VITA 62 compliant DC-DC converter will also be on display, designed to meet the demanding requirements of rugged and mission-critical embedded systems.


Visitors are invited to stop by booth No.: BN-05(6) to learn more about the latest Vicor innovations in power delivery.


Date: November 19 – 21
Venue: Pacifico Yokohama
