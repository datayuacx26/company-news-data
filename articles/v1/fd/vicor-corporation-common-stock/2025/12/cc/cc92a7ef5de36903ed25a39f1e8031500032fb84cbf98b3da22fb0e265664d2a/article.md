# Vicor to explain how to maximize ATE throughput at Power Electronics Technology China Seminar in Shenzhen

Automated test equipment (ATE) OEMs are finding it more difficult to drive down the cost of testing while keeping pace with perpetual innovation. ATE test system design needs to meet the ever-increasing IC demands quickly and reliably while driving down costs. Vicor will explain how to maximize throughput in the smallest floor footprint in an effort to optimize overall productivity.


Vicor will present “Low profile DC-DC power solution maximizes ATE throughput” at the Power Electronics Technology China Seminar held in Shenzhen on Dec. 6th, 2025. Learn how to design the optimal power system solution for ATE which are effective in a wide variety of applications. Vicor’s patented technology and advanced power module packaging process enable designers to achieve a low profile, high efficiency and high power density that will boost throughput.


Subject: Low profile DC-DC power solution maximizes ATE throughput
Date: December 6th, 2025.
Time: 14:40 – 15:10


[Learn more about the seminar.](https://www.vicorpower.com/resource-library/events/16th-asia-power-technology-development-forum-2025)
