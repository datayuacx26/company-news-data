# Vicor to showcase innovative 48V high performance power solutions for automotive, HPC and industrial at electronica Shanghai 2026

Vicor, the leader in high performance power modules, will showcase cutting-edge 48V high-performance power solutions at electronica Shanghai 2026. During the exhibition, Vicor will present its best-in-class power modules and 48V solutions addressing the pressing needs in automotive, HPC and industrial.


With the rapid development of AI, electric mobility and intelligent manufacturing, the demand of high-density and high-efficiency power systems is increasing. The Vicor 48V power module ecosystem enables solutions for the entire power delivery network. Low noise power modules simplify filter design, and thermally-adept packaging significantly reduces power losses in the form of dissipated heat. The broad Vicor ecosystem of advanced power modules, architectures and topologies simplify the transition to 48V by facilitating quick prototyping and implementation of a complete 48V power delivery network. Providing extremely high power density and excellent conversion efficiency, Vicor solutions meet the thermal demands and space challenges of electric vehicle architectures, HPC and various industrial applications, delivering reliable and safe power for the next generation of power electronic systems.


At the show Vicor will present how to “Future-Proof Automotive High-Voltage-to-SELV Conversion Using Sine Amplitude Control (SAC™) Technology” on July 2nd at 14:45-15:15 at the Automotive Electronics Technology Innovation Seminar.
