# Vicor will exhibit innovations in power design TECHNO-FRONTIER 2026

Vicor Corporation (Andover, MA) announced that Vicor K.K Japan (Headquarters: Shinagawa ward, Tokyo) will exhibit at the TECHNO-FRONTIER 2026 Power System Exhibition, which is sponsored by the Japan Management Association (JMA), from July 15 – 17, 2026.


Vicor delivers highly efficient, high-power density solutions based on innovative 48V power architectures. The modular designs unlock new levels of innovation through compact form factors, design flexibility and reduced size and weight.


At the exhibition booth, Vicor will showcase its latest DC-DC converters and a broad range of power solutions for applications including high-performance computing, industrial equipment, robotics, transportation, aerospace & defense. In addition, Vicor plans to display three prototype power units developed in collaboration with a domestic automotive manufacturer: a 400V to 800V converter, a 400V to 12V converter and an 800V to 48V/12V converter.


During the exhibition Vicor will also host a series of exhibitor seminars addressing industrial equipment, next-generation aircraft, automotive and mobility applications. Advanced registration is required for all seminar sessions.
