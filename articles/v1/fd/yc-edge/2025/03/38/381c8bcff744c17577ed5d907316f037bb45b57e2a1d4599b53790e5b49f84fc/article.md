# Edge Now Supports AI Proofreading: For Free!


### Proofreading, previously a separate product, is now available for drafting customers for free


[Evan Zimmerman](https://substack.com/@evanzimmerman)


Mar 18, 2025


Today we are making an exciting announcement at Edge. We are announcing a state of the art patent proofreader. And we are not charging extra for it. If you are using drafting, you just get the proofreader for free.


[Try Edge now!](https://withedge.com/contact-us)


To start, Edge tracks three types of errors:


-


Antecedent basis errors. Edge not only tracks obvious errors and obvious passes in the claims but also ambiguous cases


-


Patent profanity throughout the application


-


Reference errors, like referring to a figure that doesn’t exist


In our internal testing, the Edge proofreader meets or exceeds the performance of other proofreaders. On top of that, Edge’s user interface is easy to use and understand.


To access the Edge proofreader, click the “Proofreader” button on the right, next to “references.” No downloads and no fiddling with various clouds. Just a proofreader that works directly in Edge.


From there, you can view every identified error, or click on a category to filter only errors in that category.


If you have multiple errors of the same type, like multiple antecedent basis errors for the same phrase, you can see that they are grouped together with a count. You can click on the error and use the arrows to filter between them.


[Get a free proofreader](https://withedge.com/contact-us)


Our proofreader is going to get smarter over time, adding in more error types, improving the sophistication of error checking, smarter error sorting, and correction suggestions. You can’t find a proofreader like this anywhere else and it’s just going to keep getting better.
