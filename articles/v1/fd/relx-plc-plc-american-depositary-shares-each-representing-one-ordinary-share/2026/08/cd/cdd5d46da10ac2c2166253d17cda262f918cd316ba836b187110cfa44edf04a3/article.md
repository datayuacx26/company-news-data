# The people behind the technology

## **One mindset, many technologies**


Although these five technologists work in different businesses, their stories reveal remarkable similarities.


Every one of them is embracing emerging technologies - not simply because they're new, but because they help remove repetitive work, improve quality and create more time to focus on what matters most: delivering better outcomes for customers.


## **The people behind every product**


Customers never see the code reviews, the performance testing, the database changes, the deployment pipelines or the countless conversations that shape every new feature. They don't see the experimentation with AI coding tools or the collaboration between engineers, product managers, designers and customer-facing teams that happens long before a product reaches their screen.


What they do experience is confidence, in applications that respond quickly when they matter most, AI that delivers more accurate answers, products that are intuitive to use, information that is easier to find. In short, technology they can trust.


Behind every one of those experiences are people like Enodi, Jaikishore, Shayla, Hiral and Graeme - along with thousands of other technologists across RELX who bring curiosity, collaboration and continuous learning to work every day.


Different RELX businesses. Different technologies. Different customers - But one shared purpose: To build products that solve real problems, create lasting value and help customers make better decisions every day.
