# Five reasons RELX is such a great place to worK in 2026

#### Over the course of the last few months we have told many fascinating stories about our peoples' experiences of working here. From these stories, five clear ideas have emerged from our colleagues as to why RELX is such a great place to work.


## **Purpose**


> Our solutions help solve complex business problems and find answers to so many questions. We help businesses grow and avoid risk, we support regulatory compliance, we help fight crime, we help find missing children, we help build brands, we advance science. We do this with the best content, both proprietary and ethically sourced, and we make it available on the best technology, using AI with innovation and responsibility. We evolve quickly, and there’s never been a more exciting time to be here than right now.


> What distinguishes RELX is its commitment to a purpose-driven employee experience. By treating community engagement via RELX Cares as a core responsibility, the company demonstrates true corporate maturity. This creates an environment where professional development and social impact coexist, allowing employees to translate their values into action, making RELX a truly inspiring place to work.


## **Community**


> RELX is a great place to work for people who value purpose and community, because at RELX, giving back is a part of who we are. Through initiatives like RELX Cares, employees are genuinely encouraged and empowered to make a difference, whether it's through volunteering, fundraising or championing causes that matter to them. What makes it special is the sense of belonging and shared purpose it creates.


> RELX creates space for people to bring their whole selves to work - not just their skills, but their values too. Through RELX Cares, employees are supported to give back in meaningful ways, even amid demanding schedules, allowing us to slow down and reconnect with empathy and presence. That culture of trust and shared responsibility shows that purpose isn’t separate from work - it’s woven into how we support one another and our communities.


## **Flexibility**


> There is a lot of flexibility with work-life balance, while at the same time allowing incredible space for any given individual to take on responsibility to implement ideas which other employers would consider 'too far out of their lane'. Where employees are looking to advance their skills across multiple segments and skillsets this is a great place to be.


> RELX is a great space to be because the business is constantly evolving products for our customers, so you can also achieve a good work-life balance if you plan ahead and manage your portfolio.


## **Learning**


> RELX operates across many sectors, providing opportunities to gain insights from diverse industries. This diversity allows you to expand your knowledge and expertise. The company supports career growth by offering the flexibility to move between different areas within the organization, helping you explore new roles and challenges. RELX excels at setting its people up for success by providing top-notch courses and training programs. These resources not only help you develop your craft but also enhance your overall career development.


> At RELX you are working in a dynamic and innovative company, with a strong foundational history. There is such a wide range of expertise in the business that there are so many opportunities for professional development and learning, and I always feel inspired by, engaged with and interested in the work that I am doing.


## **People**


> Without a doubt, it’s the people that make this a great place to work. RELX is filled with talented, kind and driven professionals who make it a truly special place to work. While I may be biased, I can say with full confidence that the calibre of talent, strength of character and dynamic nature of our colleagues create an unbeatable culture of both performance and satisfaction.


> RELX combines high standards with long-term thinking. We invest in people and performance, and we trust leaders with real accountability. That mix of ambition, development and integrity is distinctive.


> The people make it. We have awesome talent, who are skilled and great humans. What keeps great people here is we support each other and have an environment where we can be our true selves, supportive but also with the stretch we need to develop and grow. There is a lot of fulfilment that comes with that.


> I believe RELX is an exceptional place to work, thanks to its vibrant culture. Every day, I have the opportunity to collaborate with talented and innovative colleagues who inspire and support one another.
