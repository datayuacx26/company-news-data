# United in Pride: Stories of Belonging, Allyship and Community across RELX

### This Pride Month, RELX is **United in Pride** – in the everyday acts of allyship, inclusion and care that help colleagues feel they truly belong. Across teams, regions and businesses, unity is built in moments that may seem small at first: a manager’s steady support, a conversation where someone feels heard, an employee resource group creating space for connection, a policy that recognises and respects your life outside work.


These moments shape how we work together across our global organisation. When we stand together, we create a community where everyone can thrive. Here we explore stories of unity, belonging and pride from eight colleagues across the globe.


### **Arren Jay Nabos**


#### **Event Support Specialist II, RX/RELX Philippines**


As an Event Support Specialist at RX, Arren manages inbound calls, chats, and emails for both B2B and B2C events, while also helping improve customer service through quality assurance and subject matter expertise.


> “I was initially drawn to RELX because of its strong commitment to inclusivity, work-life balance, and its core value of “valuing what matters.” Since joining, this has been reinforced by a supportive culture that genuinely prioritises employee well-being, inclusion, and belonging.”


That sense of belonging has shown up in visible and practical ways, from employee resource groups (ERGs) to inclusive benefits. “Seeing the strong presence of ERGs reflects RELX’s commitment to inclusion across all communities. I truly felt this through inclusive policies, such as extending benefits to partners, and through Pride Circle Manila, where employees are encouraged to come together and celebrate in the Philippines’ largest Pride march.”


Arren’s own journey has grown alongside that community. “I felt particularly supported and valued when the ERG core team reached out to invite me into a leadership role, an opportunity that meant a lot to me. From that moment last year to becoming President this year, it has been a journey shaped by trust, inclusion, and support.”


For Arren, unity is visible in allyship. “It’s reflected in how colleagues uplift one another, celebrate inclusion, and create safe spaces where everyone feels seen and valued.”


### **Lex Coe**


#### **Commercial Knowledge Lawyer, LexisNexis – UK**


#### Lex Coe’s work as a Commercial Knowledge Lawyer is rooted in clarity, accuracy and insight. Sitting in the Commercial Practical Guidance team at LexisNexis UK, Lex maintains a substantial body of legal content on Lexis+, writes commentary on legal developments, and contributes to sprint, operational efficiency and AI projects.


#### But joining LexisNexis also marked something deeply personal. “Joining LexisNexis was the first opportunity for me to truly bring my whole self to work. Early on, I began leading the LexisNexis UK Pride ERG and found a community that enabled me to learn, grow and feel supported."


> "Not having to question whether it was safe to share my gender, sexuality or relationship status allowed me to shed a long-held mask. This significantly improved both my psychological safety and my performance at work.” **


#### For Lex, belonging is built in everyday moments. “While working at LexisNexis, I came to better understand my gender identity, and the support I received after sharing that I was non-binary with my team was incredible.” What mattered most were the practical actions that made respect real: “Small but meaningful actions - such as colleagues helping me update my name across our systems - demonstrated genuine care and allyship.”


#### That care has helped them connect authenticity with contribution. “RELX’s commitment to inclusion, wellbeing and continuous learning fosters a genuinely supportive and innovative culture.”


### **Dino Masovic**


#### **Regional Account Manager, ICIS – UK**


#### Dino Masovic’s work as a Regional Account Manager for Southern Europe at ICIS is about connection. “My role sits at the intersection of people, markets and strategy. I spend my days building relationships, understanding what matters to our customers and helping them use ICIS intelligence to make clearer, more confident business decisions in a constantly changing market.”


#### For Dino, belonging makes people better at their work because it makes room for the full person behind the role. “A sense of belonging matters to me because I do my best work when I feel I can bring my personality, background and perspective into the room, not just my job title. I really felt that through the ERG space at RELX where conversations around identity, culture and personal growth felt open and genuinely human.”


#### Unity, for Dino, is shared purpose with room for difference. “For me, unity is about different people coming together with the same purpose: to grow, support each other and become the best version of ourselves."


> "Through Pride and the UK ERG, I’ve felt that when people are encouraged to be authentic, they bring more energy, trust and creativity into everything they do. That is when unity feels real.”


### **Gabriela Marinho Righetto**


#### **Associate Scientific Editor, Elsevier – Brazil**


#### As an Associate Scientific Editor at Elsevier, Gabriela Marinho Righetto works with scientific manuscripts, evaluating the integrity and reliability of data, as well as each submission’s scope and fit for the journal.


#### “I was initially drawn to Elsevier because of its significant role in disseminating scientific knowledge worldwide. Since joining, I've come to appreciate the work that goes behind publishing and the high ethical standards the company upholds.”


#### Belonging, for Gabriela, means not having to wonder whether support includes her. “A sense of belonging is important to me because it creates an environment where people feel respected, supported, and comfortable being who they are."


> "Since joining Elsevier, I've never had to question whether policies, opportunities or support truly applied to me because of my sexual orientation, race, or gender - I knew it did.”


#### That certainty shapes everyday life at work. “As a woman married to another woman, I've never felt uncomfortable sharing aspects of my personal life at work. The support has been reflected in both larger institutional matters (like benefits and healthcare coverage), and in everyday interactions with colleagues. I've never felt the need to hide or think twice before mentioning my partner in conversations. It just felt natural to do so, as anyone would.”


#### For Gabriela, unity is not sameness. “Inclusion is promoted not by expecting everyone to be the same, but guaranteeing equality while encouraging people to be themselves authentically.”

### **Yvana Lemos Vaér**


#### **Senior Marketing Analyst, RX – Brazil**

#### For Yvana, belonging begins with voice. “Belonging is important because it reaffirms to me that my ideas are heard and opinions are valued.” She points to a Pride moment rooted in community and opportunity. “In June 2025, we held a fair for LGBTQ+ entrepreneurs inside our office, and that filled me with pride.”


#### That pride is also personal. “When I share my experiences as a Black lesbian woman with my colleagues, I am always heard and receive support and acceptance from them.”


> “Through the PRIDE Committee, I feel the unity within RX. In it, we not only get to talk about topics that are important to us as a community, but we can also generate more information and knowledge to gain more allies for our causes.”


#### What makes RELX a great place to work for Yvana is "the diversity of people, the dynamic nature of daily work, and the professionals who are extremely dedicated to their activities.”

#### **Solutions Consultant Manager, Corporate Legal, LexisNexis – US**

For Shelby, belonging is both personal and purposeful. “A sense of belonging is critical at work as it empowers me to be myself, promotes psychological safety, and drives innovation, growth, and new ways of thinking.”


> "I feel very privileged that I consistently feel a sense of belonging at LexisNexis, and it is near and dear to my heart to ensure others feel the same sense of belonging I do."


That commitment fuels her ERG and council work. “The company's support of the Pride ERG community and investment in partnering with Out & Equal demonstrate a commitment to inclusion.”


When asked what makes RELX a great place to work, Shelby’s answer is simple: “The people.” For her, that people-first culture is reinforced by the policies and practices that help employees feel valued and supported.
