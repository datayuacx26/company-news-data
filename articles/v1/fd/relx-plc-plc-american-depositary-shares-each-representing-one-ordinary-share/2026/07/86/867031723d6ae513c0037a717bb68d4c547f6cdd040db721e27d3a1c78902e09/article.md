# Our Stories, Our People

This year, we have turned the spotlight on the people who make RELX a unique place to work. We believe that what we do matters, but *how* we do it—and *who* does it—matters even more. By sharing these perspectives, we’ve opened a window into our culture, our purpose, and the incredible individuals driving progress for our customers and communities worldwide. These stories aren't just about celebrating success; they are about showing what's possible when talented people are empowered to grow, innovate, and lead with purpose. Over the course of the year we have profiled people from all four of our business areas.


## Innovation at Speed


Across our business, we are constantly challenging ourselves to innovate faster and more efficiently. As we heard in[Restless Innovation](https://stories.relx.com/restless-innovation/index.html) , this means leveraging our long-standing use of extractive AI—as well as our more recent adoption of generative AI—and embedding both into our internal processes to become better, faster, and more cost-effective.


This drive is powered by our people. We met product managers from across our business who are building groundbreaking tools to help customers evaluate risk, advance science, and improve legal outcomes. They are at the forefront of what’s next, turning complex data into trusted, actionable insights, as explored in[Numbers to Narratives](https://stories.relx.com/numbers-to-narratives/index.html) . By fostering a culture of ‘extreme reuse’ and collaboration, we ensure that great ideas and learnings are shared quickly, accelerating our ability to solve our customers’ most pressing problems.
