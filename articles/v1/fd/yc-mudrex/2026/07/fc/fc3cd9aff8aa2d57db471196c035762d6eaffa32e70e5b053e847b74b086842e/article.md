# Story Network (IP) Rebrands to Data Network (DATA): Migration Details for Mudrex Users

Mudrex will support the **Story Network (IP) rebranding to Data Network (DATA)** and the associated token migration.


As part of this transition, all eligible IP holdings on Mudrex will be automatically converted to DATA at a fixed ratio of **1 IP = 1 DATA** . Users do not need to take any action to participate in the migration.


## **Why Is Story Network Rebranding to Data Network?**


The Story Network ecosystem is evolving its identity from **Story Network (IP)** to **Data Network (DATA)** as part of a broader rebranding initiative. While the token name and ticker will change, the migration is designed to provide a seamless experience for existing holders.


For Mudrex users, this means that eligible IP balances will be automatically converted to DATA without requiring any manual steps. Your holdings will transition to the new token as part of the migration process.


## **Migration Timeline**


📅 **IP trading suspension on Mudrex:** July 5, 2026


📅 **DATA balances credited and trading resumes:** July 10, 2026


## **What Should IP Holders Expect During the Migration?**


All eligible IP holdings on Mudrex will be automatically converted to DATA at a ratio of **1 IP = 1 DATA** .


During the migration period between **July 5 and July 10, 2026** , IP balances may temporarily not appear in user portfolios, and trading will be unavailable. This is an expected part of the migration process.


Once the migration is complete, users will receive their corresponding DATA balances, and trading will resume on Mudrex.

### **Do I need to do anything to receive DATA?**


No. If you hold eligible IP tokens on Mudrex during the migration period, your holdings will be automatically converted to DATA.

The conversion ratio is **1 IP = 1 DATA** .

### **Can I trade IP during the migration?**


No. IP trading will be suspended beginning **July 5, 2026** , and will resume once DATA is listed on **July 10, 2026** .
