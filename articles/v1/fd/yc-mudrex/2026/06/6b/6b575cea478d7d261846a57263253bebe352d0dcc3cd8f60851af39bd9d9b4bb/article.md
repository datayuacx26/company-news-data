# Toncoin (TON) Is Rebranding to Gram (GRAM): Migration Details for Mudrex Users

Mudrex will support the **Toncoin (TON) rebranding to Gram (GRAM)** and the associated token migration.


As part of this transition, all eligible TON holdings on Mudrex will be automatically converted to GRAM at a fixed ratio of **1 TON = 1 GRAM** . Users do not need to take any action to participate in the migration.


## **Why Is TON Rebranding to GRAM?**


The TON ecosystem is evolving its identity from **Toncoin (TON)** to **Gram (GRAM)** as part of a broader rebranding initiative. While the token name and ticker will change, the migration is designed to provide a seamless experience for existing holders.


For Mudrex users, this means that eligible TON balances will be automatically converted to GRAM without requiring any manual steps. Your holdings will transition to the new token as part of the migration process.


## **Timeline**


📅 **TON trading suspension on Mudrex:** June 15, 2026


📅 **GRAM balances credited and trading resumes:** June 23, 2026


## **What Happens to Your TON Holdings?**


All eligible TON holdings on Mudrex will be automatically converted to GRAM at a ratio of **1 TON = 1 GRAM** . Coinsets containing TON will also be rebalanced to include GRAM.


During the migration period between **June 15 and June 23, 2026** , TON balances may temporarily not appear in user portfolios, and trading will be unavailable. This is an expected part of the migration process.


Once the migration is complete, users will receive their corresponding GRAM balances, and trading will resume on Mudrex.
