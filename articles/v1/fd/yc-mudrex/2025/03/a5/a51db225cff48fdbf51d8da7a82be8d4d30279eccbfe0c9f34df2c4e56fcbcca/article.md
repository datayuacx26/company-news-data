# Mudrex Crypto Market Recap | March 11, 2025 – Mar 17, 2025

India is at a pivotal moment in its Web3 journey. Funding for Indian Web3 startups has surged, yet regulatory uncertainty remains a major hurdle. At the same time, global crypto developments—such as potential ETF staking and traditional banks entering Bitcoin trading—are reshaping the landscape. Even Telegram is making moves in the crypto space. So, what does all this mean for investors? Let’s dive in.


## **India’s Web3 Boom: Growth vs. Regulation**


India’s Web3 ecosystem is experiencing a funding boom, with startups raising $564 million in 2024, more than double the $270 million raised in 2023. With 4.8 million open-source developers, India is a global blockchain development hub.


However, regulatory uncertainty is holding back progress. The government continues to tighten tax rules and closely monitor crypto transactions. While Web3 innovation is thriving, unclear policies create potential risks for investors.


### **Why This Matters for Investors:**


- More funding means more innovation, better crypto products, and new investment opportunities.
- Unclear regulations could lead to sudden policy shifts that impact businesses and investments.
- India’s role in crypto is too big to ignore—keeping track of regulations can help investors make informed decisions.


## **Could ETF Staking Boost Crypto’s Popularity?**


Cboe, a major U.S. stock exchange, has requested regulatory approval for staking in Fidelity’s Ether ETF.


### **What’s Staking?**


Staking involves locking up ETH to help secure the blockchain and earn rewards—similar to earning interest in a savings account. Currently, staking ETH offers around 3.3% annual returns, paid in ETH.


### **If Approved, This Could Mean:**


- Ether ETFs becoming more attractive with passive income potential.
- More institutional adoption, leading to greater stability for ETH.
- A precedent for staking in other crypto investment products.


The SEC hasn’t approved it yet, but they’re showing signs of warming up to crypto-related products. If staking is allowed, ETH ETFs could see a major boost in demand.


## **Can Telegram Take Over Crypto? The TON Factor**


Toncoin (TON), a crypto linked to Telegram, saw a 15% price surge after French authorities allowed Telegram’s founder, Pavel Durov, to leave the country.


Despite this price jump, activity on the TON blockchain has been at record lows, leading some to call it a “dead” project. Telegram-based crypto games once fueled its growth, but interest has declined.


### **Why Investors Should Watch TON:**


- The price surge highlights how much influence Durov still holds over the project.
- If Telegram revives its Web3 efforts, TON could rally further.
- Sustained growth depends on real user adoption, not just hype.


## **BBVA Joins the Crypto Game: Big Banks Bet on BTC & ETH**


Spain’s second-largest bank, BBVA, has received regulatory approval to offer Bitcoin and Ethereum trading.


This move aligns with the EU’s MiCA regulations, making it easier for banks to enter the crypto space. BBVA has been exploring crypto services since 2020 but waited for regulatory clarity before launching.


### **Why This Matters:**


- Traditional banks entering crypto increase legitimacy and adoption.
- Easier access for investors could drive BTC & ETH demand and boost prices.
- Competition among banks may lead to lower fees and better services for crypto investors.


BBVA joins other major banks like Deutsche Bank and Société Générale in expanding into crypto, marking another step toward mainstream adoption.


## **What’s Next for Crypto Investors?**


- India’s Web3 funding boom is promising, but regulatory clarity is crucial for sustained growth.
- If ETF staking is approved, ETH could see significant gains.
- Telegram’s TON remains a wild card—watch for real adoption beyond the hype.
- BBVA’s entry into crypto signals growing institutional interest, boosting long-term confidence in BTC and ETH.


Expect high volatility as global regulations and institutional moves shape the crypto market. Stay informed, stay ahead.
