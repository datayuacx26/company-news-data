## If You Tax Crypto, Regulate It Too


In India, the Supreme Court is raising tough questions about crypto’s future. On May 5, judges challenged the government: Why impose a hefty 30% tax on crypto transactions without clear regulations to govern the space? They warned that an unregulated crypto economy could pose risks to the nation if left unchecked.


The government’s response? A vague promise to “take a look” at current policies. This could be a turning point, pushing India toward proper crypto laws that provide clarity for users and businesses. However, it might also lead to stricter oversight. Watch this space, big changes could be coming!


## FIFA on The Block


FIFA is diving into Web3, partnering with Avalanche to build its own blockchain. The plan? Move its NFT collection to the new platform and potentially launch a “FIFA Coin” for fans. Aimed at enhancing digital experiences for the 2026 World Cup, expected to draw 5 billion viewers, this shift will leverage Avalanche’s tech to scale efficiently and cut costs, replacing older systems on Algorand and Polygon.


A global brand like FIFA embracing blockchain signals crypto’s growing mainstream appeal. This move could onboard millions of new users and boost the NFT and fan token markets. Get ready for a game-changer in sports and crypto!
