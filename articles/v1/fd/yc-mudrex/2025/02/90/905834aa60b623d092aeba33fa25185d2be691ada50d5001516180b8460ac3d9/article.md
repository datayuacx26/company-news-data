# Mudrex Crypto Market Recap | Feb 18, 2025 – Feb 24, 2025

This week proved that the crypto market is becoming increasingly resilient to shocks. Despite experiencing the largest crypto hack in history, the market held steady thanks to swift crisis management and clear communication. Meanwhile, major players like Google and the U.S. Federal Reserve made significant moves that could shape Bitcoin’s future and global liquidity trends.


Let’s dive into the biggest developments in crypto this week and what they mean for the market.


## **The Largest Crypto Hack Ever: Bybit Loses $1.5 Billion**


Bybit, the second-largest exchange by trading volume, suffered a massive security breach, losing nearly $1.5 billion in digital assets. The hacker gained unauthorized access to one of the exchange’s cold wallets and drained large amounts of mETH (Mantle Staked Ethereum) and stETH (Lido Staked Ethereum) before swapping them for ETH on decentralized exchanges.


To put this into perspective, crypto-related thefts in 2025 have already reached 70% of 2024’s total $2.2 billion losses, and it’s only February. This underscores the persistent security challenges facing the industry.


Bybit’s CEO, Ben Zhou, quickly addressed the situation, reassuring users that the exchange remains solvent and that all other cold wallets are secure. Additionally, Bybit coordinated with other major exchanges to blacklist the hacker’s wallet addresses, limiting the attacker’s ability to liquidate the stolen funds.


What’s remarkable is that the crypto market remained stable despite this massive hack. Normally, such an event could trigger panic selling, but Bybit’s quick response helped prevent FUD (Fear, Uncertainty, and Doubt) from spreading. This reflects the industry’s growing maturity and ability to handle crises effectively.


## **Google Exploring Bitcoin Wallet Logins: A Game Changer or a Privacy Risk?**


A Web3 specialist recently revealed that Google is working on integrating its authentication services with Bitcoin wallets, aiming to simplify user access to digital assets. The move is part of a broader effort by Google to improve blockchain usability and lower entry barriers for mainstream adoption.


While this initiative could make Bitcoin more accessible to everyday users, it has sparked debate within the crypto community:


### **Potential Benefits:**


- **Easier Access** – Users could log into Bitcoin wallets with Google credentials, making it more user-friendly.
- **Mainstream Adoption** – A major tech giant embracing Bitcoin could accelerate its global adoption.
- **Enhanced Security** – Google’s infrastructure could help reduce phishing attacks and unauthorized access.


### **Concerns and Risks:**


- **Privacy Issues** – Storing Bitcoin wallet access credentials with a centralized entity contradicts the decentralized ethos of crypto.
- **Security Risks** – If Google’s authentication system is compromised, linked Bitcoin wallets could be at risk.
- **Regulatory Scrutiny** – Governments may push for more oversight, reducing Bitcoin’s permissionless nature.


Adding to the security discussion, Google is also researching Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKP) to improve privacy and encryption between on-chain and off-chain systems. If implemented correctly, ZKPs could enhance security while maintaining the ease of access that Google aims to provide.


## **The Fed May Pause Quantitative Tightening (QT) Soon—A Boost for Crypto?**


The Federal Reserve is approaching its goal of keeping around $3 trillion in bank reserves, which means it may soon pause its balance sheet reduction process—known as Quantitative Tightening (QT).


### **Why Does This Matter for Crypto?**


- The U.S. Treasury needs to issue a large number of short-term Treasury bonds (T-bills) to rebuild cash reserves.
- However, there’s no extra liquidity cushion in the financial system, making it harder for banks to absorb these bonds.
- The Fed is closely monitoring the risk of a cash shortage, similar to the 2019 repo market crisis.
- If the Fed slows or stops QT, it would mean more liquidity in the financial system, which could benefit risk assets like Bitcoin and crypto.


Historically, more liquidity leads to higher asset prices, and Bitcoin has often thrived in loose monetary conditions. However, short-term volatility could still occur if Treasury bond issuance drains liquidity faster than expected.


## **European Central Bank (ECB) Adopting Blockchain for Payments**


The European Central Bank (ECB) has announced plans to integrate Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) into its payment infrastructure. This move could pave the way for a Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) and revolutionize cross-border transactions.


### **How Will It Work?**


- **Phase 1:** The ECB will integrate blockchain technology into the TARGET payment system, its existing settlement infrastructure.
- **Phase 2:** A more advanced blockchain-based system will be explored for long-term adoption.


### **Why Is This Important?**


- **Faster Transactions:** Blockchain can reduce settlement times from days to seconds.
- **Enhanced Security:** DLT can lower fraud and hacking risks by improving transaction transparency.
- **Less Dependence on U.S. Payment Systems:** The ECB wants to reduce reliance on non-European payment providers and strengthen the eurozone economy.


The ECB is expected to release more details soon, but this move signals that even traditional financial institutions see blockchain as the future of payments.


## **What’s Next?**


While the Bybit hack was a major blow, the quick response from the exchange and the broader industry highlights crypto’s increasing resilience. Looking ahead, investors should keep an eye on another major event:


### **Solana (SOL) Token Unlock – Will It Impact Prices?**


On March 1, around 11.2 million SOL tokens (worth over $2 billion) will be unlocked as part of FTX’s bankruptcy process. Since these tokens were previously locked, their sudden release into the market could have significant effects on SOL’s price and liquidity.


- If large amounts of SOL are sold immediately, it could lead to downward pressure on the price.
- If investors absorb the supply without panic selling, SOL’s price could remain stable or even recover.
- Some traders may view this as a buying opportunity if prices dip temporarily.


The coming days will reveal whether Solana holders will face turbulence or whether the market will absorb the unlock smoothly. Either way, traders should be prepared for potential volatility.


## **Final Thoughts**


This week showcased both the challenges and resilience of the crypto market. The Bybit hack was a major setback, but the market’s stability in its aftermath signals growing maturity. Meanwhile, Google’s Bitcoin wallet integration, the Fed’s monetary policy shifts, and the ECB’s blockchain adoption indicate that crypto is becoming more deeply embedded into global finance.


As always, staying informed and prepared is key in this fast-moving space.
