# Determ Alternatives for 2026: 10 Tools Compared on Pricing and Video Coverage

The best Determ alternatives for 2026 split into two groups depending on what a team actually needs. PR/comms-first teams that want to stay close to Determ's affordable monitoring lane but need deeper media-relations tooling should look at Muck Rack (a journalist database plus real, if limited, TikTok and Instagram social listening since 2024), Cision (the largest media-contact database and press-release distribution, with TikTok support still rolling out), Signal AI (AI-native media intelligence with strong, current, named case studies), or Onclusive (unified media intelligence, though with weaker public customer proof than its peers). Teams that have concluded they need genuine social-video coverage should look at Meltwater (real TikTok video transcription and the strongest named customer roster), Brandwatch (broad enterprise consumer intelligence), Talkwalker (non-gated speech-to-text and video-logo recognition), Mention or Brand24 (budget/mid-market, text-first), or Syncly Social for brands whose core problem is that spoken and on-screen mentions inside TikTok, Reels, and Shorts videos are invisible to every keyword- or caption-matching tool, Determ included. Determ itself remains a genuinely well-reviewed (4.4/5 on Capterra), affordable choice for small-to-mid PR teams whose monitoring needs are centered on news, blogs, and text-based social — its own users' most common complaint is that TikTok and Instagram Reels simply can't be tracked yet.


Search "Determ alternatives" and most of what comes back reads like a features checklist: price tiers, keyword limits, a logo wall. What it usually skips is the question that actually determines fit — is your team outgrowing Determ because you need deeper PR and journalist tooling, or because your monitoring gap is video, not press?


Those are different problems with different answers. A PR team drowning in manual outreach doesn't need better TikTok coverage; a brand team that keeps missing shade-match complaints and dupe comparisons inside short-form video doesn't need a bigger journalist database. Determ itself is a legitimate, well-liked tool for the first group — its 4.4/5 rating across 39 verified Capterra reviews isn't a fluke. Its real, confirmed limit is that it doesn't monitor TikTok or Instagram Reels at any tier, which is exactly the gap the second group runs into.


Here's what Determ actually does well, where its real gaps are, and how ten alternatives — including some genuinely surprising findings about who has and hasn't shipped real video capability — compare on pricing and coverage.


## What Determ actually does well (and where it falls short)


Determ is an AI-powered media monitoring platform built specifically for PR and communications teams — its own homepage describes it as helping users "monitor online media and social conversations in real time and use AI to turn complex coverage into clear insights, risks, and next steps." It isn't pitched as a social-listening or influencer tool, and it doesn't try to be one.


The product has real strengths. Independent reviewers on Capterra rate it 4.4/5 across 39 verified reviews, citing fast setup, responsive support, and clear affordability relative to Meltwater or BuzzSumo. Determ began life in 2014 as Mediatoolkit, spun off as an independent company in 2016, and rebranded to Determ in 2023 — it's a small, Zagreb-based team, not a recent entrant (Source:[Determ — About, 2026](https://determ.com/about/) ).


Its real, independently confirmed gaps are narrower and more specific than most "alternatives" roundups suggest. Determ's own pricing page lists its monitored sources as News (100M+ sites), Facebook, Instagram, X, blogs, YouTube, Reddit, Bluesky, Medium, and Traditional media — TikTok is not on that list at any tier (Source:[Determ — Pricing Page, 2026](https://determ.com/pricing/) ). Reviewers confirm this is a real pain point, not a theoretical gap: "TikTok and Instagram Reels cannot be tracked yet which can sometimes make it hard to analyze results," one wrote (Source:[Capterra — Determ Reviews, 2026](https://www.capterra.com/p/154479/Determ/reviews/) ). Other reviewers flag sentiment accuracy that's "still kinda off-beat," a 90-day mention-history limit on some plans, and occasional stability issues.


Determ's homepage also cites its own performance claims — "84x faster crisis reaction," "20h saved weekly," "5,000+ users" — which are vendor-reported marketing figures, not independently audited statistics, and should be read as such.


## Why teams look for Determ alternatives


Three patterns show up consistently in why teams outgrow Determ, and only one of them is about video.


**No short-form video coverage at any tier.** Teams whose brand conversation increasingly lives in TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts videos — spoken aloud or shown on-screen rather than typed — get zero coverage from Determ. This is confirmed by both Determ's own source list and direct reviewer complaints, not an assumption.


**A ceiling on scaling PR operations.** Determ's tier structure tops out at 10 topics and 10,000 mentions per month before requiring a custom enterprise conversation. Teams running many simultaneous campaigns, markets, or sub-brands may need broader enterprise scale.


**No journalist database or press-distribution layer.** Determ's feature set is monitoring, alerts, and reporting — not media outreach or pitching. PR teams that need to find and pitch journalists, not just track coverage they've already earned, outgrow Determ's scope by design; Cision's roughly 1.4 million-contact database and Muck Rack's 250,000+ curated journalist contacts serve that different job (Source:[Rephonic — Muck Rack Pricing, 2026](https://rephonic.com/blog/muck-rack-pricing/) ).

## The 10 best Determ alternatives in 2026


### 1. Syncly Social


**Best for:** brands and PR/marketing teams whose customers talk about them out loud or show them on-screen inside TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts videos — conversation Determ and most PR-first tools cannot see at all. Its Audio Intelligence (speech-to-text) and AI Vision (on-screen text and logo detection) — covered in more depth on its[video analysis](https://syncly.app/social/feature/video-analysis) page — enable genuine untagged-mention detection across more than 10 platforms as part of its[social listening solution](https://syncly.app/social/solution/social-listening) , capability Determ doesn't offer at any price.


**Pricing:** custom (see below for a free trial link).


### 2. Meltwater


**Best for:** PR/comms teams and agencies that have outgrown Determ's self-serve tiers and want media monitoring, social listening, and named-customer proof in one enterprise platform. Meltwater's beauty and consumer roster is the strongest researched in this piece: Shiseido, Yves Rocher, and Bon Parfumeur are all named, current customers (Source:[Meltwater — Shiseido](https://www.meltwater.com/en/customer-stories/shiseido) ). It's also the one enterprise peer here with confirmed TikTok video-transcription depth, via its TikTok Marketing Partners integration (Source:[GlobeNewswire, March 2025](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/03/03/3035544/0/en/TikTok-insights-now-available-within-the-Meltwater-platform.html) ).


**Pricing:** no public list price; analyst estimates put median spend around $25,000/year, ranging from $6,000 to $150,000+ depending on tier (Source:[Vendr Marketplace, 2026](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/meltwater) ).


### 3. Brandwatch


**Best for:** teams that have outgrown Determ's 10-topic ceiling and want deep historical archives — 1.7 trillion conversations back to 2010 — plus broad language coverage. Brandwatch's own 2026 roadmap lists "deeper analysis of video and image content" as a forward-looking goal, not a shipped feature (Source:[Cision/Brandwatch press release, 2025](https://www.cision.com/about/press-releases/2025-press-releases/brandwatch-strengthens-ai-leadership-with-deeper-insights-and-expanded-data-coverage-302621990/) ) — don't assume it already matches Meltwater or Syncly Social on video AI.


**Pricing:** no public list price; comparable estimates place entry tiers around $800-$2,000/month, scaling to enterprise custom quotes.


### 4. Cision (CisionOne)


**Best for:** PR/comms teams that need journalist outreach, press-release distribution through PR Newswire, media monitoring, and executive reporting in one workflow — the "one system for coverage, contacts, and pitching" job Determ doesn't attempt. CisionOne currently covers Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, Reddit, Bluesky, and blogs/forums; a TikTok API integration was announced as "coming soon" in a September 2025 product update but hadn't shipped as of this writing (Source:[PR Newswire, 2025](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cisionone-expands-ai-powered-platform-with-enhanced-instant-insights-and-social-intelligence-302557904.html) ).


**Pricing:** no public list price; Vendr's procurement data puts the median real contract at $12,677/year across 102 tracked purchases, with full enterprise suites reaching $50,000-$150,000+ (Source:[Vendr Marketplace, 2026](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/cision) ).


### 5. Muck Rack


**Best for:** PR/comms teams whose main frustration with Determ is the lack of a journalist-relationship layer — Muck Rack's database includes more than 250,000 curated journalist contacts. The most genuinely surprising finding here: Muck Rack acquired social-analytics vendor Keyhole in September 2024 and shipped integrated Social Listening — including real TikTok and Instagram tracking, subject to each platform's API limits — in February 2025, making it a more social/video-capable PR-first tool than Determ, Cision, or Onclusive (Source:[Muck Rack, 2024](https://muckrack.com/blog/2024/09/03/muckrack-acquires-keyhole/) ;[Muck Rack, 2025](https://muckrack.com/blog/2025/02/12/introducing-integrated-social-listening) ). That said, Muck Rack's own help documentation discloses real limits — TikTok tracking is restricted to profiles on watchlists plus qualifying public accounts, and Instagram is limited to hashtag tracking rather than general keyword search — so treat it as more coverage than most PR-first tools offer, not parity with a dedicated video-listening platform.


**Pricing:** no public list price; annual-contract-only. Estimates range from roughly $5,000+/year (Starter) to $20,000-$50,000+/year (Premier) (Source:[Media List, 2026](https://medialist.com/article/16/muck-rack-pricing) ).


### 6. Onclusive


**Best for:** teams that want media monitoring across print, broadcast, online, and social plus PR-attribution modeling in one platform, without needing Cision's press-distribution scale. The honest caveat here: despite multiple searches, no specific, verifiable named-customer case study or public client-logo list could be located for Onclusive — its public proof of results is materially weaker than Meltwater's, Muck Rack's, or Signal AI's, and no confirmed TikTok or short-form-video capability was found either.


**Pricing:** entry self-service tier around $4,999/year for 5 users; Onclusive Social starts around $15,000/year; full enterprise platform scales into the mid-five-figures annually.


### 7. Signal AI


**Best for:** PR/comms teams that want an AI-first media-intelligence platform with genuinely strong, current, metrics-backed case studies rather than a generic enterprise pitch. Signal AI processes roughly 5 million documents daily across 200+ markets in 75 languages, and its public case-study page shows specific, quantified results: Bird & Bird ("1 hour/day time savings, halves media monitoring costs"), SThree ("22.2M impressions via Topic Analysis"), and The MBS Group ("3-hour faster daily newsletter delivery") (Source:[CaseStudies.com — Signal AI, 2026](https://www.casestudies.com/company/signal-ai) ). No confirmed TikTok or short-form-video capability was found.


**Pricing:** no public list price; transaction-data estimates range $9,000-$38,000/year, averaging around $21,000/year (Source:[Vendr Buyer Guides — Signal AI](https://www.vendr.com/buyer-guides/signal-ai) ).


### 8. Mention


**Best for:** PR/comms-oriented teams whose monitoring centers on press, blogs, and basic social caption tracking, and who don't need Determ's lower entry price. Mention's TikTok "support" is caption- and hashtag-level tracking, not audio or video understanding, and its self-serve tiers were discontinued in July 2025.


**Pricing:** Company Plan $599/month (annual billing only), 50,000 mentions/month cap (Source:[CheckThat.ai — Mention Pricing, 2026](https://checkthat.ai/brands/mention/pricing) ).


### 9. Talkwalker


**Best for:** teams that want Blue Silk AI's speech-to-text and logo/video recognition — non-gated and included across tiers — at enterprise scale, without Determ's PR-report or journalist-facing features. Talkwalker's video and audio AI is described generically as covering "audio and video social media posts" rather than confirmed as purpose-built for TikTok, Reels, or Shorts specifically — a meaningfully different claim than "built for short-form video."


**Pricing:** no official public pricing; third-party estimates range from roughly $9,000-$13,000/year (Core) to $150,000+/year (Enterprise) — treat as directional, not exact (Source:[ITQlick, 2026](https://www.itqlick.com/talkwalker/pricing) ).


### 10. Brand24


**Best for:** budget-conscious teams whose monitoring needs are genuinely text- and keyword-first. Brand24's case-study roster (Uber, Piwik PRO, Hostinger) is general SaaS/tech, not PR-specific, and it has zero confirmed audio transcription or on-screen visual capability at any tier — an honest, entirely text-first profile.


**Pricing:** Individual $199/month (annual) up to Enterprise $1,499+/month (Source:[Brand24 — Pricing Page, 2026](https://brand24.com/prices/) ).

## Definitions


**PR/comms-first media monitoring** : a monitoring platform — like Determ, Cision, or Muck Rack — built around the needs of PR and communications teams: press coverage, journalist relationships, crisis alerts, and executive-ready reporting. This is distinct from a social/video-listening platform built around understanding what's said and shown inside short-form video content across TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.


## Key takeaways


-


Determ is a genuinely well-reviewed (4.4/5 on Capterra), affordable PR monitoring tool with one clear, confirmed gap: no TikTok or Instagram Reels coverage at any tier.


-


Vendor fit splits into two real buyer needs — deeper PR/journalist tooling (Muck Rack, Cision, Signal AI, Onclusive) versus genuine social-video coverage (Meltwater, Brandwatch, Talkwalker, Syncly Social) — and forcing every alternative into one framing misses the difference.


-


The most surprising finding: Muck Rack, a pure PR tool, has shipped more real TikTok and Instagram social listening than Cision, Onclusive, or Determ itself, since acquiring Keyhole in 2024.


-


Public customer proof varies widely even within the same category — Signal AI and Muck Rack both show strong, specific, named results; Onclusive and Cision's public case-study depth is materially weaker.


-


No PR or social monitoring tool replaces the other's core job — the honest question is which gap (media relations or video coverage) is actually costing your team the most right now.


There's no single universal "Determ replacement" here. Determ does its actual job — PR and text-based social monitoring for small-to-mid teams — honestly and affordably. The real decision is which gap matters more: deeper journalist and media-relations tooling, which points toward Muck Rack, Cision, or Signal AI, or genuine video coverage, which is where Determ (and most of its PR-first peers) simply doesn't compete.
