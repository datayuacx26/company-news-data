# Sprinklr vs Sprout Social vs Agorapulse: Which Fits Your Budget in 2026?

**TL;DR:** Sprinklr, Sprout Social, and Agorapulse sit at three very different price points in 2026 — Agorapulse starts at $79/user/month self-serve, Sprout Social runs $79-$399/seat/month self-serve, and Sprinklr eliminated its self-serve tier entirely on April 30, 2026, now requiring a $50,000+/year enterprise sales process. Four weeks later, Sprinklr acquired video-analysis technology (ViralMoment) that neither Sprout Social nor Agorapulse has matched — but it's brand-new, undisclosed in scope, and locked behind that same enterprise-only paywall.


Sprinklr, Sprout Social, and Agorapulse show up on nearly every "best enterprise social media platform" shortlist, usually treated as roughly interchangeable options that differ mainly by feature checklist.


They aren't interchangeable. As of this year, one of them requires a multi-month sales process and a six-figure budget just to get a price quote — and the other two don't.


If you're evaluating these three for your team, the pricing and access model matters more than any individual feature, because it determines whether you can even trial the product before committing. It also sets up a genuinely new wrinkle: the vendor with the least accessible pricing just became the first of the three to acquire real video-content-analysis technology.


This comparison breaks down what each platform actually costs, who it's built for, and which of the three — if any — analyzes what's actually said or shown in a video, not just what's typed in a caption.

## How They Compare Head-to-Head


Each platform was built for a different scale and budget. Here's what each genuinely offers — and where it falls short.


### Sprinklr: The Enterprise-Only Suite That Just Bought Video Intelligence


Sprinklr positions itself as a unified Customer Experience Management (CXM) platform spanning marketing, social, care, and insights — not a standalone social media tool (Source:[Sprinklr, 2026](https://www.sprinklr.com/) ). It has the broadest channel coverage in the category, including region-specific channels like WeChat, Telegram, and Viber, and the strongest published multilingual listening claim: 160+ languages for recognition, 100+ for sentiment, and 130+ for translation (Source:[Sprinklr Help Center, 2026](https://www.sprinklr.com/help/articles/ai-enrichments/languages-supported-in-social-listening/641dde9d55c4c33ae8b81944) ). It was named an Exemplary Provider in the 2026 ISG CXM Buyers Guide (Source:[Sprinklr/ISG, 2026](https://investors.sprinklr.com/news/press-releases/detail/258/sprinklr-named-exemplary-in-the-2026-customer-experience) ) and a Leader in Forrester's Q4 2024 Social Suites Wave (Source:[Sprinklr Newsroom, 2024](https://www.sprinklr.com/newsroom/sprinklr-named-a-leader-in-q4-2024-social-suites-report-by-independent/) ).


But on April 30, 2026, Sprinklr discontinued its self-serve tier entirely. Every buyer now enters through an enterprise sales process — real contracts run $50,000 to $500,000+ per year, plus $25,000-$150,000 in separate implementation costs, over a 3-6 month sales cycle, with no published price and no free trial (Source:[Chatarmin, 2026](https://chatarmin.com/en/blog/sprinklr-pricing) ).


Four weeks later, on May 28, 2026, Sprinklr acquired ViralMoment — technology that performs frame-by-frame analysis of a video's audio, visuals, and on-screen text, explicitly built to close what Sprinklr itself called the "video listening gap" (Source:[CMSWire, 2026](https://www.cmswire.com/customer-experience/sprinklr-acquires-viralmoment-to-close-the-video-listening-gap/) ;[Sprinklr Investor Relations, 2026](https://investors.sprinklr.com/news/press-releases/detail/255/sprinklr-acquires-viralmoment-to-define-the-next-era-of) ). That's a genuine capability shift, and the first of these three vendors to make it. But rollout scope, tier availability, and pricing remain undisclosed, and it doesn't yet appear in G2 reviews of the product (Source:[G2 — Sprinklr Sellers page](https://www.g2.com/sellers/sprinklr) ).


**Where Sprinklr wins:** Broadest channel coverage in the category, including WeChat, Telegram, and Viber. Strongest published multilingual listening claim. First of the three to acquire genuine video-content-analysis technology.


**Where it struggles:** No accessible entry point — the self-serve shutdown means no trial and no published price before a multi-month sales cycle. ViralMoment's actual availability, scope, and cost are unknown.


Best for large enterprises (500+ employees, often regulated industries) with the budget and procurement runway for a six-figure, multi-month contract — not a fit for a team that wants to self-serve or trial before buying.


### Sprout Social: The Mid-Market Pick With the Top Listening Reputation


Sprout Social calls itself an "AI-powered Social Intelligence Platform" spanning listening, publishing, engagement, and analytics, with published, self-serve pricing across four of its five tiers ($79-$399/seat/month) plus a custom Enterprise tier (Source:[Sprout Social Pricing](https://sproutsocial.com/pricing/) ). It carries the largest review pool of the three — roughly 6,957 on G2 at 4.4/5 — and was named G2's #1 Social Listening product in two consecutive 2026 reports (Source:[G2 — Sprout Social Reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/sprout-social/reviews) ;[Sprout Social Investor Relations, 2026](https://investors.sproutsocial.com/news/news-details/2025/Sprout-Social-Named-1-Social-Listening-Product-in-G2s-2026-Winter-Reports-Achieving-40-Top-Rankings-Overall/default.aspx) ).


Sprout's own support documentation confirms its listening is keyword, hashtag, caption, and mention-based — the company states plainly that it "cannot search and sync sound" on TikTok due to platform API limits (Source:[Sprout Social Support, 2026](https://support.sproutsocial.com/hc/en-us/articles/6136328798605-TikTok-and-Sprout) ), and third-party comparisons describe its image handling as tag-based rather than true visual analysis (Source:[YouScan, 2026](https://youscan.io/blog/sprout-social-vs-hootsuite/) ). In July 2025, Sprout acquired NewsWhip, a media-intelligence firm, to strengthen predictive text and news-signal capabilities — a move toward smarter text analysis, not video content analysis (Source:[GlobeNewswire, 2025](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/07/30/3124174/0/en/Sprout-Social-Acquires-NewsWhip-Enhancing-Predictive-Intelligence-Capabilities-and-Accelerating-AI-Roadmap.html) ).


**Where Sprout wins:** G2's #1-ranked Social Listening product across two consecutive 2026 reports. Native CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot) tying conversations directly to contact records. Published, self-serve pricing with a genuine 30-day free trial.


**Where it struggles:** Listening is confirmed text and keyword-only — no audio transcription or visual content analysis, and TikTok sound tracking is explicitly limited by API constraints. The full feature set (AI reply enhancement, sentiment analysis, API access) requires the $399/seat/month Advanced tier.


Best for mid-market to enterprise teams — Sprout reports 501-1,000+ employee accounts as its fastest-growing segment — that want a well-reviewed, provable platform without an enterprise sales process.


### Agorapulse: The Self-Serve Option With the Cheapest Entry and Best-Rated Support


Agorapulse is an all-in-one social media management platform — publishing, unified inbox, reporting — historically built for SMBs and agencies and now pushing upmarket. All three core tiers ($79-$199/user/month) are fully self-serve, with a true month-to-month billing option and a 30-day free trial requiring no credit card (Source:[Agorapulse Pricing](https://www.agorapulse.com/pricing) ).


Agorapulse acquired Mention, the social listening tool, in 2025 through Mention's French judicial receivership process (Source:[Netinfluencer, 2025](https://www.netinfluencer.com/listening-to-the-future-agorapulse-acquisition-of-mention-explained/) ). Its own "Advanced Listening" documentation describes scanning for keywords, phrases, and mentions across social platforms, blogs, forums, and news sites — no visual or audio analysis, before or after the acquisition (Source:[Agorapulse Support, 2026](https://support.agorapulse.com/en/articles/10365572-advanced-listening-explained) ). Nearly a year and a half later, Mention still runs as a separate product with a separate login (Source: same).


**Where Agorapulse wins:** The cheapest published entry point of the three, genuinely self-serve with no sales call required. The best-rated support in the category — over 800 G2 reviewers give it a 9.3/10 average score, with roughly a 30-minute response time (Source:[Agorapulse, 2026](https://www.agorapulse.com/best-social-media-management-tool/) ).


**Where it struggles:** Listening remains keyword and phrase-only even after the Mention acquisition, and Advanced Listening is a separate paid add-on at $100 per search query (Source:[Agorapulse Support, 2026](https://support.agorapulse.com/en/articles/10365572-advanced-listening-explained) ). No large, recognizable enterprise case study appears among its named customers, unlike Sprinklr or Sprout Social.


Best for SMBs and small-to-mid agencies that want fast self-serve setup and don't need enterprise-scale listening depth.


## The Video Listening Gap: Who Actually Analyzes Content, Not Captions


**Text-based social listening** : monitoring that matches keywords, hashtags, captions, or @mentions — the mechanism behind Sprout Social's and Agorapulse's listening products today. **Video-content-native listening** : analyzing a video's actual audio (via transcription) and visual content (via computer vision and on-screen-text recognition) to catch brand mentions that are spoken or shown but never typed into a caption at all.


Sprout Social and Agorapulse are both confirmed, through their own documentation, to run pure text-matching listening — neither transcribes audio nor visually analyzes video content, even after Agorapulse's Mention acquisition (Source:[Sprout Social Support, 2026](https://support.sproutsocial.com/hc/en-us/articles/6136328798605-TikTok-and-Sprout) ;[Agorapulse Support, 2026](https://support.agorapulse.com/en/articles/10365572-advanced-listening-explained) ).


Sprinklr is the exception, and the timing is worth noting directly: on April 30, 2026, it eliminated the only accessible way to buy in — no self-serve tier, no trial, just an enterprise sales process. Four weeks later, on May 28, it acquired ViralMoment, adding frame-by-frame audio, visual, and on-screen-text analysis explicitly meant to close the video-listening gap (Source:[CMSWire, 2026](https://www.cmswire.com/customer-experience/sprinklr-acquires-viralmoment-to-close-the-video-listening-gap/) ). That's a real, first-of-its-kind move among these three vendors. But it landed with no disclosed rollout timeline, no confirmed tier availability, and no G2 reviews yet reflecting it. The one vendor with genuine video-content analysis is also, for now, the one a mid-market or SMB brand can't actually buy without a $50,000+/year, multi-month contract.
