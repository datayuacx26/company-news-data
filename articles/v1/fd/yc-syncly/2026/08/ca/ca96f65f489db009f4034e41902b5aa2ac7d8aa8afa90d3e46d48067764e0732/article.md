# Best Digimind Alternatives Now That It's Onclusive Social

**TL;DR:** Digimind, the 27-year-old French social listening platform many enterprise teams still search for by name, no longer exists as a standalone product — Onclusive acquired it in 2022 and fully rebranded it "Onclusive Social" on October 29, 2024, with digimind.com now redirecting entirely to onclusive.com. If you're comparing Digimind alternatives for a budget-conscious enterprise team in 2026, the real fork in the road is buying process, not just price: Sprout Social and Hootsuite offer genuinely self-serve, published pricing you can start today, while Onclusive Social, Talkwalker, Meltwater, Brandwatch, and Syncly Social all compete at the enterprise-quote tier — where Onclusive Social's ~€15,000/year floor is the cheapest entry point, but the sales-gated buying experience is identical to its pricier peers.


If you searched for "Digimind pricing" this year and landed on a page that says "Onclusive Social," you're not lost. You're just catching up to a rebrand that quietly finished more than a year ago.


That's the uncomfortable starting point for anyone evaluating Digimind in 2026: the product is real, the reviews are good, and the company you're actually about to buy from doesn't go by that name anymore. Onclusive — backed by Symphony Technology Group — acquired Digimind in mid-2022, ran it under a "Digimind, an Onclusive company" co-brand for a couple of years, then folded it fully into "Onclusive Social" in October 2024. Trade press has since reported that the legacy digimind.com marketing site is set to close entirely around March 2026.


None of that makes Digimind a bad tool. It's a genuinely credible, well-reviewed platform with real customers and a real AI feature. But it does mean a "Digimind alternatives" search in 2026 is really two searches at once: teams who assumed the product still existed under that name, and teams who knew about the rebrand and are now asking whether Onclusive Social's lower enterprise price point is actually more budget-friendly — or just a smaller number attached to the same demo-gated sales process as everyone else. This guide covers both, plus the video and audio coverage question that decides whether any of these tools can actually see what your customers are saying about you on TikTok.


## Why Do Budget-Conscious Enterprise Teams Look for Digimind Alternatives?


The single biggest reason teams go looking for a Digimind alternative in 2026 isn't price — it's that "Digimind" has largely stopped existing as a distinct brand, and that identity churn creates real evaluation risk before budget even enters the conversation.


**The rebrand timeline, precisely:** Digimind was founded in 1998 in Grenoble, France, and built a genuine 27-year track record in social listening and consumer intelligence before Onclusive announced its acquisition on July 25, 2022 (Source:[Research Live, 2022](https://www.research-live.com/article/news/onclusive-acquires-digimind/id/5101432) ). A lighter "Digimind, an Onclusive company" co-branding followed — it's still the name on Digimind's current G2 seller page — before Onclusive announced the full integration and rename to "Onclusive Social" on October 29, 2024 (Source:[Media OutReach via Taiwan News, 2024](https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/news/5960436) ). Today, digimind.com redirects entirely to onclusive.com, and trade press has reported the legacy marketing domain is scheduled to close around March 19, 2026 — a specific date that recurred consistently across search results this year but couldn't be traced to a single, independently fetchable primary source, so treat it as reported rather than confirmed.


That two-stage timeline matters because most quick searches collapse it into one date or miss it entirely — which means a team that "evaluated Digimind six months ago" may already be evaluating a product that no longer carries that name.


**The second, more familiar reason is pricing opacity that persists despite a comparatively low entry point.** Onclusive Social's starting price is consistently estimated at around €15,000 per year across third-party sources — genuinely lower than Brandwatch, Sprinklr, or Talkwalker's higher enterprise tiers (Source:[Capterra, 2026](https://www.capterra.com/p/221928/Digimind/) ). But there's still no published rate card, no self-serve checkout, and every path to a number runs through a sales conversation (Source:[TrustRadius, 2026](https://www.trustradius.com/products/onclusive-social/pricing) ). Reviewer sentiment is genuinely mixed on top of that: aggregated G2 and Capterra reviews praise the platform's sentiment analysis and reporting depth, but also describe "extremely late deliverables" and, in one case, support that's "almost worthless" (Source:[Capterra, 2026](https://www.capterra.com/p/221928/Digimind/reviews/) ).

**Quote-only pricing** : A vendor pricing model where no rate card is published publicly and every quote requires a sales conversation, regardless of how large or small the deal is.


That's the tension worth sitting with before you get attached to Digimind's lower absolute number. ## Which Digimind Alternatives Cover Video and Audio Content, Not Just Text?


Only two vendors in this comparison — Talkwalker and Meltwater — ship confirmed, non-gated video and audio capability; Onclusive Social's 2018-era "AI Sense" image-recognition feature is real but no longer confirmed by current product marketing, and video or audio content analysis in any form is absent from its current pitch — putting it in the same ceiling as Sprout Social, Hootsuite, and Brandwatch.


This is the honest, easy-to-overstate-or-understate part of the story, so it's worth being precise. Digimind's own data scientists built a genuine AI layer called "AI Sense" back in 2018, working with IBM Research, using deep learning to detect brand logos inside images (Source:[PRWeb, 2018](https://www.prweb.com/releases/digimind_unveils_digimind_ai_sense_to_enhance_insights_relevance_and_user_experience/prweb15883986.htm) ). That's a real, independently developed capability — not a "dumb keyword tool" claim. But Onclusive's current product page has moved the "AI Sense" name onto different ground: it now describes a real-time sentiment engine that powers a summarization feature ("In Brief") and an alerting feature ("Sentinel"), with no mention of logo detection, image recognition, speech-to-text, audio transcription, or motion-video analysis anywhere on it (Source:[Onclusive, 2026](https://onclusive.com/products/social-listening/) ). Whether the original image-recognition layer is still live under the hood isn't something current marketing confirms either way — what is confirmed is that video and audio content analysis, in any form, isn't part of the current pitch. An independent third-party comparison backs that reading: "Neither platform offers complete TikTok analytics integration," referring to Digimind and Hootsuite alike (Source:[Mention, 2025](https://mention.com/en/blog/digimind-vs-hootsuite/) ).


**Text/mention-matching listening** : Monitoring that scans captions, hashtags, and platform metadata for keyword or mention matches — it can't hear what's said out loud in a video or see what's shown on screen.


**Video-content-native listening** : Monitoring that processes the actual audio and visual content of a video — transcribing spoken words and detecting on-screen text, logos, or visual cues — surfacing mentions even when the caption and hashtags say nothing about your brand.


By that definition, Onclusive Social, Sprout Social, and Hootsuite are all text/mention-matching tools at their core, with Onclusive Social's 2018-era static-image logo detection layered on top as a genuine but now-unconfirmed exception — it doesn't appear anywhere in current Onclusive product marketing. Brandwatch has publicly roadmapped deeper video and image analysis but hasn't shipped it.[Talkwalker](https://syncly.app/blog/8-best-talkwalker-alternatives-for-teams-without-an-enterprise-budget) and Meltwater are the two enterprise peers that have actually shipped video-content-native listening — non-gated speech-to-text and video-logo recognition in Talkwalker's case, and real TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram transcription in Meltwater's. Syncly Social was built video-native from the start specifically to close this gap, with[video analysis](https://syncly.app/social/feature/video-analysis) and audio transcription running by default rather than as an upsell.


That gap matters more than it used to. Health and beauty alone now accounts for 79.3% of TikTok Shop sales, generating more than $1.34 billion annually (Source:[BeautyMatter, 2025](https://beautymatter.com/articles/tiktok-live-and-the-rise-of-a-new-role-in-beauty) ) — a category where a growing share of brand-relevant conversation happens in spoken video or on-screen visuals a caption-matching tool simply never sees.


## Which Digimind Alternative Fits a Budget-Conscious Enterprise Team?


"Budget-conscious enterprise team" isn't one buyer profile — it splits into teams that want the lowest number within the enterprise-quote tier, and teams that want to skip the quote-gated sales process altogether, and the right alternative depends on which one you actually are.


### 1. Syncly Social


[Syncly Social](https://syncly.app/social) is built for teams whose customers talk about them out loud or show them on-screen inside TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts — conversation that Onclusive Social's image-only AI was never built to detect, and that neither Sprout Social nor Hootsuite catch either. Audio Intelligence (speech-to-text) and AI Vision (on-screen text and logo detection) run natively across video content, not scoped down to static images. Pricing is custom, but the path in is a genuine free trial rather than a demo-only gate — a meaningfully different buying experience from every enterprise-tier vendor on this list, including Onclusive Social itself. It's the strongest fit if your evaluation criteria include real[social listening](https://syncly.app/social/solution/social-listening) coverage of video content specifically, alongside[competitor analysis](https://syncly.app/social/solution/competitor-analysis) that doesn't stop at what competitors caption.


### 2. Sprout Social


Sprout Social is the most accessible buying process in this entire comparison: published, self-serve pricing from $79/seat/month up through $399/seat/month, with Enterprise the only custom tier (Source:[Sprout Social, 2026](https://sproutsocial.com/pricing/) ). No demo required to start. The honest trade-off is that it's confirmed keyword, hashtag, and caption-only listening — Sprout's own documentation states it "cannot search and sync sound" on TikTok (Source:[Sprout Social Support, 2026](https://support.sproutsocial.com/hc/en-us/articles/6136845197709-TikTok-FAQs) ) — meaning it gives up even Onclusive Social's narrow image-recognition layer in exchange for a fully self-serve buying process.


### 3. Hootsuite


Hootsuite is the other genuinely self-serve option, with published tiers generally cited between $99 and $399 per user per month, though a live Capterra comparison widget lists it closer to $249/user/month — likely reflecting different plan tiers being surfaced (Source:[Capterra, 2026](https://www.capterra.com/p/221928/Digimind/alternatives/) ). Its listening layer is now "powered by Talkwalker" following Hootsuite's 2024 acquisition of that company (Source:[Hootsuite Newsroom, 2024](https://www.hootsuite.com/newsroom/press-releases/hootsuite-agrees-to-acquire-talkwalker) ), but no source confirms whether Talkwalker's video and audio AI is actually exposed inside Hootsuite's own listening tier — an open question worth asking directly in a sales call rather than assuming either way.


### 4. Brandwatch


Brandwatch competes on depth rather than price: a historical archive stretching back to 2010 and enterprise-grade consumer-research tooling, at a custom price point generally comparable to or above Onclusive Social's floor. Its 2026 roadmap lists deeper video and image analysis as a stated goal, not a shipped feature — so paying more than Onclusive Social's floor for Brandwatch today doesn't automatically buy more video coverage.


### 5. Talkwalker


Talkwalker is the strongest "spend about the same, get real video AI" case in this comparison. Third-party estimates put its entry-level Core tier at roughly $9,000 to $13,000 per year — potentially below Onclusive Social's own floor — while shipping non-gated speech-to-text and video-logo recognition on every tier, not just the top one. The trade-off is a steeper learning curve than Onclusive Social's reviewer-praised ease of use, and marketing copy that describes its video capability generically rather than confirming platform-specific depth on TikTok or Reels.


### 6. Meltwater


Meltwater combines PR and social intelligence with genuine, already-shipped TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram video transcription through its TikTok Marketing Partners integration — a real step up from Onclusive Social's image-only AI. Pricing is quote-based, with analyst estimates ranging from roughly $6,000 at the Starter tier to $150,000-plus at Enterprise, median around $15,000–$25,000 per year (Source:[Vendr, 2026](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/meltwater) ). Named customers include[Shiseido](https://www.meltwater.com/en/customer-stories/shiseido) ,[Yves Rocher](https://www.meltwater.com/en/customer-stories/yves-rocher) , and[Bon Parfumeur](https://www.meltwater.com/en/customer-stories/digital-business-lab) (Source: Meltwater customer stories, 2026).
