# Emplifi vs Sprinklr vs Khoros: Which Enterprise Suite Fits Your Team

**TL;DR:** Emplifi, Sprinklr, and Khoros show up on the same enterprise shortlists, but they're built for different jobs and different buyers. Emplifi unifies marketing, commerce/UGC, and care at the lowest of the three price floors but only reads text, while Sprinklr is the broadest, most expensive suite and the only one with a real (if unshipped) bet on video intelligence. Khoros is a capable community platform whose 2026 question isn't a missing feature — it's whether the team behind it is still there next year.


Three names, one shortlist, three completely different products. That's the trap with Emplifi vs Sprinklr vs Khoros: all three get lumped into "enterprise social/CX suite" searches and vendor-comparison spreadsheets, so it's easy to assume they're competing head-to-head for the same deal. They aren't. One is a unified customer-experience platform, one is the biggest, priciest all-in-one suite in the category, and one just went through a change of ownership that matters more than any feature on its roadmap.


Get this wrong and the cost isn't a bad UI — it's a multi-year, five- or six-figure contract signed with the wrong assumptions baked in. Meanwhile, the channel where a growing share of your customers actually talk about your brand — TikTok, Reels, Shorts — is exactly where none of these three vendors has a fully confirmed answer.


This piece skips the feature-grid theater and goes straight at the three questions that actually decide a purchase: what each platform is built for, what it really costs, and whether any of them can hear or see what's happening inside a video rather than just what's typed in a caption.


## What Are Emplifi, Sprinklr, and Khoros Actually Built For?


**Direct answer:** Emplifi unifies social marketing, social commerce/UGC, and consumer care into one "Autonomous CX" platform. Sprinklr is the broadest all-in-one customer experience management (CXM) suite at the largest enterprise scale. Khoros is a community-hosting and social-engagement platform now mid-relaunch under new ownership. None of the three was built to solve the same problem as the other two.


**Emplifi** grew out of Socialbakers — founded in Prague in 2008, acquired by Astute (an Audax Private Equity company) in September 2020, and rebranded Emplifi in June 2021 (Source:[BusinessWire, 2020](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200909005665/en/Astute-Acquires-Socialbakers-to-Strengthen-Customer-Experience-Offering-with-Social-Media-Marketing) ). Today it spans three pillars — social marketing, social commerce/UGC (via its 2022 Pixlee TurnTo acquisition), and consumer care — powered by an internal AI layer it calls "Emplifi Fuel." Emplifi says 20,000 brands use the platform (Source:[Emplifi, 2026](https://emplifi.io/) ), and it holds a Leader position in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Social Media Management and Listening (Source:[PR Newswire, 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/emplifi-named-a-leader-in-the-2026-gartner-magic-quadrant-for-social-media-management-and-listening-302821225.html) ).


**Sprinklr** positions itself as a unified CXM platform spanning marketing, social, care, and insights, with the widest channel list in the category — including region-specific channels like WeChat, Telegram, and Viber that most competitors skip — plus the category's strongest published multilingual listening claim, at 160+ languages (Source:[Sprinklr Help Center, 2026](https://www.sprinklr.com/help/articles/ai-enrichments/languages-supported-in-social-listening/641dde9d55c4c33ae8b81944) ). Sprinklr also holds a 2026 Gartner MQ Leader position (Source:[CX Today, 2026](https://www.cxtoday.com/community-social-engagement/gartner-magic-quadrant-for-social-media-management-and-listening-2026-the-rundown/) ) and was named an Exemplary Provider in the 2026 ISG CXM Buyers Guide (Source:[Sprinklr/ISG, 2026](https://investors.sprinklr.com/news/press-releases/detail/258/sprinklr-named-exemplary-in-the-2026-customer-experience) ).


**Khoros** is different in kind, not just degree. Its historical differentiator has always been owned-community depth — forums, Q&A, gamified engagement — rather than listening breadth, and its current architecture makes that explicit: an April 2026 relaunch split the product into Aurora AI (community) and Iris AI (social/listening/engagement), two separately branded pillars (Source:[PR Newswire, 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/khoros-launches-aurora-ai-a-new-dawn-for-enterprise-community-302739636.html) ). Khoros doesn't appear in the same Gartner Magic Quadrant as the other two, consistent with its positioning as a community/engagement specialist rather than a listening-category competitor.

## How Does Pricing Compare Across Emplifi, Sprinklr, and Khoros?


**Direct answer:** All three are quote-only with no published enterprise list price, but they sit at meaningfully different floors. Emplifi is the cheapest by third-party estimate, Khoros sits in a wide and genuinely disputed middle band, and Sprinklr is confirmed the most expensive and least accessible of the three.

Emplifi's pricing is estimated at roughly $1,000–$3,000+ per month for enterprise scale, with no free trial and a one- to two-week sales cycle just to receive a quote (Source:[SocialRails, 2026](https://socialrails.com/blog/emplifi-pricing) ).

Khoros is where a clean number falls apart, and that's worth stating plainly rather than papering over. Two independent pricing trackers disagree by roughly 3–10x depending on segment: SocialRails puts Khoros at $10,000–$50,000+ per year (Source:[SocialRails, 2026](https://socialrails.com/blog/khoros-pricing) ), while Vendr — which sources its estimates from actual buyer contract data — puts the median at $164,649 per year, with a range of $50,000–$500,000+, sometimes exceeding $1M for multi-module enterprise deals (Source:[Vendr, 2026](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/khoros) ). We're presenting both rather than averaging them into one misleading Khoros figure, because the gap itself is informative: it suggests Khoros pricing varies enormously by deal structure and negotiating leverage, which is exactly the kind of uncertainty worth flagging before you sign an annual, hard-to-exit contract.


## Which of the Three Actually Understands Video and Audio Content?


**Direct answer:** This is the axis that actually separates B2C brands living on TikTok, Reels, and Shorts — and the honest answer is a spectrum of certainty, not a scoreboard. Emplifi is a confirmed no. Khoros is a confirmed unknown. Sprinklr has acquired the technology but hasn't shipped it to buyers yet.


**Text/mention-matching listening** : a social listening mechanism that identifies brand-relevant content by matching keywords, hashtags, captions, or @mentions in the text layer of a post.


**Video-content-native listening** : analyzing the actual audio (via speech-to-text) and visuals (via computer vision or on-screen-text recognition) inside a video — capturing mentions that are spoken or shown on-screen but never typed anywhere.


Here's where each vendor lands on that spectrum:


1.


**Emplifi — confirmed no.** Emplifi's own listening documentation is unambiguous: on TikTok it captures "@mentions... in other users' posts and comments," capped at the top 1,000 most-liked items; on YouTube it reads the title and description, not the video itself (Source:[Emplifi Documentation, 2026](https://docs.emplifi.io/platform/latest/home/listening-data-sources-available-coverage) ). Emplifi has been an official TikTok Marketing Partner since May 2022, but that partnership covers content publishing and performance analytics — not listening-side video understanding (Source:[Emplifi, 2022](https://emplifi.io/press/emplifi-becomes-tiktok-marketing-partner-help-brands-unleash-power-of-video/) ). No video-content analysis is documented anywhere on the platform, on any channel.


2.


**Khoros — a genuinely unresolved unknown.** Neither Khoros's Iris AI product page nor its April 2026 launch materials document a specific mechanism — speech-to-text, computer vision, on-screen-text detection — for analyzing video or audio content, despite marketing language that gestures at "conversations at scale across billions of sources in 187 languages" (Source:[Khoros, 2026](https://khoros.ai/iris/) ). That's an absence of evidence, not evidence of absence — but Khoros's historical 2022 partnership with listening specialist Talkwalker (since acquired outright by Hootsuite) suggests its native listening depth has needed outside help before, and the 2025-2026 workforce turnover makes any undocumented claim about Iris AI's current capabilities harder to trust either way.


3.


**Sprinklr — a real acquisition, not yet a real product.** Sprinklr acquired ViralMoment on May 28, 2026, adding genuine frame-by-frame audio, visual, and on-screen-text analysis — explicitly framed by Sprinklr as closing its own "video listening gap" (Source:[Sprinklr Investor Relations, 2026](https://investors.sprinklr.com/news/press-releases/detail/255/sprinklr-acquires-viralmoment-to-define-the-next-era-of) ). That framing is itself telling: Sprinklr admits it didn't have this capability before the acquisition. As of this writing, roughly two and a half months post-acquisition, rollout scope, tier availability, and pricing all remain undisclosed, and the capability doesn't yet appear in Sprinklr's G2 reviews.


The honest takeaway: if video-native listening is your deciding factor today, none of the three enterprise suites in this comparison currently offers a confirmed, accessible answer. Emplifi's answer is a documented no. Khoros's is an unresolved unknown. Sprinklr's is a real acquisition without yet a real, evaluable product.

## Which Enterprise Suite Fits Which Team?


**Direct answer:** Emplifi fits teams that want unified CX at the lowest price floor and treat listening as one feature among several. Sprinklr fits large enterprises that can clear a six-figure, multi-month sales process for the broadest platform at scale. Khoros fits teams whose priority is community hosting — but only if they're willing to weigh a real organizational-stability risk alongside the feature set.


-


**You want marketing, commerce/UGC, and customer care in one Gartner-recognized system, at the lowest of the three price floors →** Emplifi is the strongest fit. The tradeoff: quote-only pricing with no self-serve entry, and a listening mechanism that's confirmed text-only.


-


**You need the broadest channel and language coverage at the largest enterprise scale, and can clear a multi-month, six-figure sales process →** Sprinklr fits. It's also the only one of the three with a documented — if not yet shippable — bet on real video intelligence, so it's worth watching if ViralMoment reaches general availability during your evaluation window.


-


**Your primary need is owned-community hosting — forums, Q&A, gamified engagement — or you're an existing Khoros customer weighing renewal →** Khoros can still be the right tool on pure capability grounds; its Communities product scores reasonably well with reviewers on discussion depth and moderation. But this is the one of the three currently under real organizational disruption, and that deserves to be evaluated as a standing item, not folded quietly into a feature comparison.


-


**Your actual gap is video-native listening — catching what's said or shown on camera, not typed in a caption →** none of these three enterprise suites currently delivers that with certainty. ## Key Takeaways


-


Emplifi, Sprinklr, and Khoros aren't competing for the same buyer — they're built for unified CX, broadest-at-scale CXM, and community hosting, respectively.


-


Pricing floors are genuinely different: Emplifi (~$1K–$3K+/mo), Sprinklr ($50K–$500K+/yr, median ~$129,380), and Khoros (disputed — $10K–$50K+/yr or a $164,649/yr median, depending on the source).


-


Video/audio listening sits on a certainty spectrum, not a tie: Emplifi is a confirmed no, Khoros is a genuinely unresolved unknown, and Sprinklr has acquired the technology (ViralMoment, May 2026) but hasn't shipped it to buyers.


-


Khoros's most consequential 2026 fact isn't a feature gap — it's the May 2025 IgniteTech acquisition, 116+ confirmed layoffs, and a ground-up product relaunch, all of which raise a real stability question for any buyer or renewing customer.


-


If your gap is specifically video-native listening, none of these three enterprise suites currently delivers a confirmed, accessible answer.


## The Verdict


There's no single winner in Emplifi vs Sprinklr vs Khoros, because they were never actually competing for the same deal. Emplifi is the unified-CX pick for teams that want marketing, commerce, and care in one system at the lowest of the three price floors. Sprinklr is the broadest-at-any-cost pick for enterprises that can clear a six-figure sales process — and the only one of the three with a real, dated bet on video intelligence, even though that bet isn't a shippable product yet. Khoros is the genuine wild card: a capable community platform whose real 2026 question isn't a missing feature, it's whether the team and roadmap behind it will still be there next year.


Match the vendor to the job you actually need done, not the shortlist you inherited. And if the job is hearing and seeing what your customers say and show on camera — not just what they type — none of these three enterprise incumbents has fully closed that gap yet.
