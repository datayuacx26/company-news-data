# 7 Best Synthesio Alternatives for Enterprise Teams in 2026

**TL;DR:** Synthesio, an Ipsos company, remains a genuinely well-regarded enterprise social-and-search intelligence platform — 4.3/5 on G2 across 151 reviews — but its quote-only pricing (no published rate card) and complex query setup send teams looking for alternatives. There's also a real, dated wrinkle worth knowing: in January 2026, Synthesio announced video intelligence through a partnership with ViralMoment; four months later, Sprinklr — a direct Synthesio competitor — acquired ViralMoment's assets, and neither company has said what that means for Synthesio's clients.


Synthesio shows up on nearly every enterprise social-intelligence shortlist, usually praised for the Ipsos market-research pedigree behind it — a credibility angle pure-tech listening vendors like Brandwatch or Talkwalker don't have.


But there's a real, dated complication in Synthesio's story that most "Synthesio alternatives" roundups miss entirely, because it only became relevant this year.


If you're evaluating Synthesio or actively looking to replace it, you need more than a feature checklist. You need to know exactly what's confirmed about its capabilities today, what's genuinely uncertain, and which alternatives actually close the gaps that matter to your team.


This guide profiles Synthesio honestly first, walks through the one open question every enterprise buyer should ask before signing, then breaks down seven real alternatives — including where each one stands on video-content understanding.


## Synthesio: Where It's Strong, and Its Real Gaps


Synthesio has been an Ipsos company since Ipsos acquired it for $50 million in October 2018 (Source:[GlobeNewswire, 2018](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2018/10/30/1639419/0/en/Synthesio-Acquired-By-Ipsos-Reinforcing-Social-Media-Intelligence-Capabilities.html) ). Founded in Paris in 2006, it now positions itself as "the shortest path to insight," claiming to process 80 billion social signals annually across 10+ search platforms and 2,000+ ecommerce sources, with a GenAI layer called "Signals" (Source:[Synthesio, 2026](https://www.synthesio.com/) ).


The platform genuinely earns its reputation. It holds a solid 4.3/5 on G2 across 151 reviews, and reviewers consistently single out customer support as a standout — one Capterra reviewer called it "so much better than what I have ever gotten with any other social media tool" (Source:[Capterra, 2026](https://www.capterra.com/p/160240/Synthesio/) ). A third-party comparison (from a competing vendor, so read with that bias in mind) even credits Synthesio with "deeper conversation context in sentiment tuning and topic clustering compared to Talkwalker" (Source:[Merciv, 2026](https://www.merciv.com/blog/talkwalker-competitors-alternatives) ).


The real friction points are just as clear. Synthesio publishes no pricing anywhere — every source confirms a contact-sales, custom-quote model, with third-party estimates from ITQlick placing entry around $1,000/month for a single user, scaling to $20,000+/month at 100-user enterprise scale, plus $5,000-$50,000+ in separate implementation costs (Source:[ITQlick, 2026](https://www.itqlick.com/synthesio) ). Reviewers also flag a complex query-setup process and a dashboard some call "not user friendly," with sentiment-analysis accuracy needing improvement (Source: same).


**Where Synthesio wins:** Genuine Ipsos market-research credibility. Broad social, search, and ecommerce data coverage. Consistently praised customer support.


**Where it struggles:** No published pricing or self-serve path. Complex query setup. And, as of this research, a genuinely unresolved question about its most newsworthy 2026 capability — covered next.


## What Happened to Synthesio's Video Intelligence After Sprinklr Bought ViralMoment?


**Partnership-dependent video intelligence** : a listening platform's video-content-understanding capability that relies on a third-party technology vendor rather than in-house-built infrastructure — meaning the capability's continuity depends on that third party's independent survival, which can change without the listening platform's own customers being notified.


On January 19, 2026, Synthesio's own blog announced an "official platform partnership" with ViralMoment, describing real video-intelligence capabilities — analysis of on-screen text, spoken audio, visuals, and actions across TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube, and YouTube Shorts, supporting 76+ languages (Source:[Synthesio, 2026-01-19](https://www.synthesio.com/blog/ipsos-synthesio-viralmoment-partnership-brings-leading-video-intelligence-to-our-clients/) ).


Four months later, on May 28, 2026, Sprinklr — a direct Synthesio competitor in the enterprise social-intelligence space — announced it had acquired the assets of ViralMoment Inc. (Source:[Sprinklr Newsroom, 2026-05-28](https://www.sprinklr.com/newsroom/sprinklr-acquires-viralmoment-to-define-the-next-era-of-multimodal-customer-intelligence/) ). Multiple independent outlets confirmed this as an asset purchase, with ViralMoment's own CEO quoted saying the move would "bring that capability into an enterprise platform built to operationalize those insights at scale" — language suggesting the ViralMoment team and technology moved into Sprinklr (Source:[MarketScreener, 2026](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/sprinklr-inc-acquired-assets-of-viralmoment-inc-ce7f5ddadf8bfe20) ).


Here's the part no "Synthesio alternatives" content has connected: no source anywhere — not Sprinklr's press release, not Synthesio's own blog, not independent trade coverage — mentions the Synthesio partnership at all, in either direction. Nothing confirms it was terminated. Nothing confirms it continues unaffected. A direct check of Synthesio's current site in August 2026 found zero mention of video intelligence, ViralMoment, or Sprinklr anywhere, despite the site still actively promoting other newer features.


That silence isn't proof of anything on its own — it could reflect a quiet ongoing integration just as easily as a quietly lapsed one. But it's a real, timely due-diligence question: if you're evaluating Synthesio specifically for video coverage, ask directly whether the ViralMoment capability still works, under what terms, and for how long.
