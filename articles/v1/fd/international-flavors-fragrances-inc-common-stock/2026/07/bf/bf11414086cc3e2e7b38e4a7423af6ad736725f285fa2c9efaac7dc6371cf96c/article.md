# IFF Declares Dividend for Third Quarter 2026

NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 28, 2026-- IFF (NYSE: IFF) announced that its Board of Directors has declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of $0.40 per share of its common stock, payable on October 9, 2026 to shareholders of record as of September 18, 2026.
