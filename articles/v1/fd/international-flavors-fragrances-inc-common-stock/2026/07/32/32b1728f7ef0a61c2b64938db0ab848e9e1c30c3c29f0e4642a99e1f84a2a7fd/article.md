# IFF to Release Second Quarter 2026 Results on August 4, 2026

NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 9, 2026-- IFF (NYSE: IFF) today announced that it will release its second quarter 2026 earnings results following the market close on Tuesday, August 4, 2026. The management team will host a live webcast on Wednesday, August 5, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. ET to discuss results and outlook with the investor community.


Investors may access the live webcast and accompanying slide presentation on the company's website at[ir.iff.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fir.iff.com&esheet=54568024&newsitemid=20260709663454&lan=en-US&anchor=ir.iff.com&index=1&md5=3aac27b4942e112c541a4a798591c436) . For those unable to listen to the live webcast, a recorded version will be made available for replay.
