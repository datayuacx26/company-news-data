# IFF to Webcast Fireside Chat at Barclays Global Consumer Conference on Sept. 10

NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 18, 2026-- IFF (NYSE: IFF) today announced that Chief Executive Officer Erik Fyrwald will participate in a fireside chat at the Barclays Global Consumer Conference on Thursday, Sept. 10, 2026 at 945 a.m. ET.


Investors may access the live webcast on the Company's website at[ir.iff.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fir.iff.com%2F&esheet=54591555&newsitemid=20260818490253&lan=en-US&anchor=ir.iff.com&index=1&md5=e72c61ed83349a6e6e91ac2a128f7d77) . For those unable to listen to the live webcast, a recorded version will be made available for replay.
