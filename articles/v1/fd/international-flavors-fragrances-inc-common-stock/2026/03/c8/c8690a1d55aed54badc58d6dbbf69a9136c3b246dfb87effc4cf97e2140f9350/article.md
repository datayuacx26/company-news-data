# IFF Declares Dividend for First Quarter 2026

NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Mar. 4, 2026-- IFF (NYSE: IFF) announced that its Board of Directors has declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of $0.40 per share of its common stock, payable on April 10, 2026 to shareholders of record as of March 20, 2026.
