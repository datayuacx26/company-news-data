### State Street Corporation (NYSE: STT) Announces Date for Release of Second-Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Conference Call Webcast


June 02, 2026


BOSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


State Street Corporation (NYSE: STT) plans to announce its second-quarter 2026 financial results on **Thursday, July 16, 2026** at approximately 7:30 a.m. ET. A conference call to review the firm’s financial results will be held at 11:00 a.m. ET.


The conference call will be accessible via audio webcast on State Street’s Investor Relations website,[http://investors.statestreet.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Finvestors.statestreet.com&esheet=54546449&newsitemid=20260602840441&lan=en-US&anchor=http%3A%2F%2Finvestors.statestreet.com&index=1&md5=69b9b8fbd125547108b14ccb6a5ed7b2) , or by telephone at (+1) 805 309 0220 (Participant Passcode: 93090#). Materials will be available on the website prior to the call.


For those unable to listen to the live webcast, a replay will be available on the website for approximately one month.


**About State Street Corporation**


State Street Corporation (NYSE: STT) is one of the world's leading providers of financial services to institutional investors including investment servicing, investment management and investment research and trading. With $54.5 trillion in assets under custody and/or administration and $5.6 trillion* in assets under management as of March 31, 2026, State Street operates globally in more than 100 geographic markets and employs approximately 51,000 worldwide. For more information, visit State Street's website at[www.statestreet.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.statestreet.com%2F&esheet=54546449&newsitemid=20260602840441&lan=en-US&anchor=www.statestreet.com&index=2&md5=09b78e9dd69c2f18e3dab8e839392021) .
