State Street Corporation (NYSE: STT) reported its second-quarter 2026 financial results today. The news release, presentation and additional financial information can be accessed on State Street’s Investor Relations website,[http://investors.statestreet.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Finvestors.statestreet.com&esheet=54571790&newsitemid=20260715543773&lan=en-US&anchor=http%3A%2F%2Finvestors.statestreet.com&index=1&md5=e6b2e64d126cd571d3dfaeaa7c69bd5c) . **About State Street Corporation** State Street Corporation (NYSE: STT) is one of the world's leading providers of financial services to institutional investors including investment servicing, investment management and investment research and trading. With $57.9 trillion in assets under custody and/or administration and $6.3 trillion* in assets under management as of June 30, 2026, State Street operates globally in more than 100 geographic markets and employs approximately 51,000 worldwide. For more information, visit State Street's website at[www.statestreet.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.statestreet.com&esheet=54571790&newsitemid=20260715543773&lan=en-US&anchor=www.statestreet.com&index=2&md5=23a869cf25b310b6f1d7d72cdfb0b1fd) .
