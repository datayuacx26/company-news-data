# CERAWeek 2026: Powering What’s Next

At CERAWeek 2026, one theme stood out: rapidly growing energy demand.


As artificial intelligence, data centers, and industrial growth continue to accelerate, the conversation is shifting from whether demand will increase to how the grid will keep pace. While perspectives vary, Vistra


sees the outlook as real but manageable.


The focus is clear: deliver reliable, dispatchable power and continue advancing solutions that support long-term growth. As a competitive company, Vistra is built to think creatively and respond to evolving customer needs.


In this[video](https://www.ceraweek.com/en/news/executive-interviews?videoId=6391493358112) , Vistra President and CEO Jim Burke


shares his perspective on rising data center demand, the importance of reliability, and the growing role of nuclear energy in powering what comes next.
