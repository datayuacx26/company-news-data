# Powering Energy Efficiency and Opportunity in United ISD

When TXU Energy was selected as United ISD’s electricity supplier, the goal was simple: deliver reliable energy at a strong value.


But as the partnership gets underway in 2026, the impact is already going beyond the bottom line.


Under the agreement, TXU Energy provided the district with $60,000 in Greenback rebates to fund energy-efficiency improvements across school facilities. From upgraded HVAC systems to more efficient chillers, these investments are helping the district reduce energy use and lower costs.


For a district the size of United ISD – more than 40,000 students – even small gains in efficiency can add up quickly.


“These investments to become more energy efficient are directly contributing money back into the classrooms instead of on the electricity invoice,” said TXU Energy Sales Executive


Eric Holz.


As part of the partnership, TXU Energy is also contributing $20,000 to the district’s scholarship fund, further extending the impact from operational savings to student opportunity.


It’s a simple but powerful idea: when schools spend less on energy, they can invest more in students.


And that’s exactly the kind of outcome partnerships like this are designed to deliver.
