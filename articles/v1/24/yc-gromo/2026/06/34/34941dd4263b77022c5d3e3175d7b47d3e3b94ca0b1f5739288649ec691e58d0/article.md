## The Legal Side


GroMo partners are commission agents. You connect customers with RBI-licensed financial institutions. You don't handle money. You don't process applications. You don't make approval decisions. You share links and explain products.


**Don't do this** :


- Promise guaranteed approvals (banks decide, not you)
- Submit fake documents
- Charge customers for applications (everything's free to apply)
- Collect payments directly
- Create multiple partner IDs to game referrals


**Tax** : GroMo income counts as business income. If you earn above ₹2.5 lakh annually (FY 2026-27 basic exemption), you'll need to file ITR. Consult a CA. Most partners start below the threshold.


**Data security** : GroMo encrypts all information. Don't screenshot customer documents. Don't share data across products without consent.
