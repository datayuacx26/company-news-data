## Mistakes to avoid in your first year


I see people make the same few mistakes with 1-year planning. They lock all their money in illiquid instruments, ignore inflation, or treat investment as their only income source.

Diversifying across 2-3 instruments, like splitting funds between an RD and a liquid fund, reduces this risk.
