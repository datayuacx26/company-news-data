## Compliance: The Non-Negotiable Part of Affiliate Content


Compliance stops your commission from getting clawed back. Every piece of content must be accurate no misleading claims about guaranteed approvals or hidden fees.


This matters for products like personal loans, which carry a first-3-EMI clawback clause, or Aditya Birla Mutual Fund SIPs, which require 3 months of maintenance before payout. If your content promises "guaranteed loan approval" for a product that checks CIBIL scores, you risk complaints and losing your payout.


Before writing, check:


- Is the payout locked behind a training module?
- Are there clawback conditions?
- Are you targeting the right profile? (Don't pitch SBI Credit Card to someone who fits SBM Novio better).


Being upfront "approval depends on CIBIL and income eligibility" actually builds more trust than overselling. Partners who understand[personal loans without income proof](https://gromo.in/blog/personal-loans-without-income-proof-india) know how to phrase eligibility honestly without scaring off real leads.

## Avoiding Common Content Mistakes


Most affiliate content fails because of avoidable mistakes, not bad luck. Recognizing these early saves months of wasted effort.


Common pitfalls include:


- **Overpromising** : Claiming "100% guaranteed approval" for products that need eligibility checks.
- **Ignoring audience fit** : Pitching premium credit cards to audiences who need secured cards like IDFC WOW Black.
- **Posting once and forgetting** : Content needs repetition to sink in.
- **Skipping training** : Many products are compliance-gated; your content can't go live properly until you finish GroMo's certification.
