## Login Security: Protecting Your Account and Earnings


GroMo uses OTP authentication instead of a static password. It is harder for someone to steal a code that expires in thirty seconds than it is to crack a password you’ve reused on three other sites.


But your account still has real money and customer data inside it. If someone else gets access, they can mess with your earnings or your reputation.


A few rules of thumb:


- Never hand out your OTP. Real support won't ask for it.
- Log out if you’re using a phone that isn’t yours.
- Keep the app updated so you have the latest security patches.
- If something feels off, report it through the in-app chat immediately.
