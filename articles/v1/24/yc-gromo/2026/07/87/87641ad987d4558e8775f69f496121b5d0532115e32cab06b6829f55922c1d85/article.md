## How to vet an app before wasting your time


Don't just download and start. Check a few things first. Skipping this step is how people waste weeks on apps that never pay.


Ask these questions before downloading any "earning app":


**Is the payout instant?** GroMo credits earnings immediately after confirmation. Many apps hold funds for 30-45 days. That's a long time to wait for ₹200.


**Are commissions flat or percentage-based?** Flat fees on high-value products beat percentage cuts on cheap goods every time.


**Do I need to pay to join?** Legitimate affiliate models, including GroMo, don't charge partners. If they're asking for money, that's a red flag.


**What's the fine print?** Clawback clauses on personal loans or SIP cancellations can reverse payouts. Read the terms so you don't get surprised.


**Is there training?** GroMo's free certification programs actually help. Customers trust certified advisors more than random link-sharers.

New affiliates lose money by ignoring eligibility rules or chasing the wrong products. These mistakes add up.
