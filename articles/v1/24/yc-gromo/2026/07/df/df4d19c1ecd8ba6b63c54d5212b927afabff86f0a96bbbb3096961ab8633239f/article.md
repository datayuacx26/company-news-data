## Common Mistakes to Avoid in Affiliate Marketing Jobs


Most people don't fail because the opportunity isn't real. They fail because they stop after three "no's." Inconsistency kills affiliate income.


Other common errors:


- **Over-promising features** : Never claim a physical card when the product is virtual-only.
- **Ignoring eligibility criteria** : Pitching to someone who doesn't meet income or credit score requirements wastes everyone's time.
- **Skipping training** : GroMo's free certification exists to prevent mis-selling.
- **Chasing every product** : Focusing on 2-3 high-payout categories often beats spreading thin across all six.
- **Neglecting follow-ups** : Many leads drop off simply because no one reminded the customer to complete KYC.
