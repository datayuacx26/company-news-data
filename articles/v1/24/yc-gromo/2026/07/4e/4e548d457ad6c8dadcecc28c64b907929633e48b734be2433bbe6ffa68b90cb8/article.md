## What Is an Affiliate Program Exactly?


Companies pay independent partners commissions for generating leads or sales. You get a unique link or code. When someone buys through that link, you get paid.


Amazon Associates is the version most people know promote retail products, earn a percentage. GroMo applies the same logic to finance: credit cards, savings accounts, loans. The banks get customers, you get a commission, the middleman infrastructure disappears.


You don't hold stock or handle returns. The job is purely matching a customer with a product they might actually want. Traditional businesses need capital, staff, and physical infrastructure. Affiliate programs strip all that away.
