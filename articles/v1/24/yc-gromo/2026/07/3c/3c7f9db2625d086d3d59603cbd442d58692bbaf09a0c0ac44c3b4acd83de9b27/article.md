## Mistakes that kill your earnings


A lot of students leave money on the table by making simple errors.


- **Promising approvals:** Don't guarantee a card. The bank decides. Overpromising destroys trust.
- **Skipping training:** The GroMo Academy modules explain who qualifies. Skip them, and you waste time on bad leads.
- **Ignoring documents:** Loans and demat accounts need PAN and Aadhaar. Confirm the person has these before you pitch.
- **Being spammy:** Don't just blast links. Talk to people.
- **Forgetting follow-ups:** Use the app's reminders. If an application is half-done, nudge them.
