## Fixed Deposits: The Traditional Safe Bet


FDs are still the default for anyone who hates risk. You get guaranteed returns, a fixed tenure, and capital protection. Right now, banks and NBFCs are offering 6% to 8% annually, depending on tenure.


Platforms like Jiraaf have made this easier by offering marketplace FD bookings with rates that often beat your local branch, starting from just ₹1,000. You can compare issuers and lock-in periods online without the paperwork. It’s a smoother experience than waiting in a bank queue.


FDs are perfect for emergency funds or parking money for a specific goal like a wedding. But they won't make you rich. Returns rarely beat inflation over the long run, which is why pairing them with growth assets is usually the smarter play.


## Bonds: Fixed Income with Higher Returns


If you want better rates than an FD but can't stomach stock market swings, bonds are the middle ground. Digital platforms now offer curated bonds delivering 8% to 15% per annum from vetted issuers.


The whole process is paperless. You do your KYC online, browse options by tenure, and invest starting at ₹1,000. It works well for investors who want fixed income that outpaces standard bank deposits without rolling the dice on equities.


Just keep in mind that bonds carry securities market risk. Returns aren't guaranteed the way FDs are, so check the issuer's credit rating before you commit.


## Mutual Funds and Demat Accounts: Market-Linked Growth


Mutual funds pool money into diversified portfolios managed by professionals. For long-term wealth creation especially through SIPs this remains one of the most popular paths in India.


Opening a demat account is step one. Several platforms now offer free account opening with zero commission on mutual funds and IPO investments, which has pulled millions of first-time investors into the market.


The catch is discipline. SIPs require consistent contributions over years to see real compounding.
