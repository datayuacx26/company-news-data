## How to choose your product mix


The highest payout number on the app is not the best product for you. A product that pays ₹3,000 is worthless if nobody in your circle qualifies or needs it.


Before picking products, ask yourself:


- Who do I actually know? Salaried professionals, shopkeepers, students, homemakers?
- What financial problems do they complain about?
- Which products actually solve that problem without a hard sell?
- Can I explain the product in one sentence, in their language?


Partners who start with their audience and work backward tend to do better than those chasing whatever product is trending on social media that week.
