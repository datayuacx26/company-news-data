## What Is Credit Card Refer and Earn?


Credit card refer and earn is a distribution arrangement. You introduce someone to a card, the bank underwrites and approves as usual, and when the card is issued you receive a fixed commission.


**GroMo** is an Indian financial products distribution app, and the 30+ card brands below sit inside its catalogue alongside personal loans, savings accounts and demat accounts.


Two features separate this from ordinary app referral schemes. The payout is a real commission funded by the issuing bank rather than a signup bounty. And it is flat per card, which means the skill that pays is matching the right card to the right spender, not chasing bigger numbers.


*Flat per approval. The match matters more than the amount.*


Worth being precise about what these programmes are. They are not the banks' own customer referral offers. Each card below is available for distribution through the GroMo app, and the rate you see is what GroMo pays a partner on an approved and issued card. The same distinction applies to loan distribution, described in this guide to[what a loan agent does](https://gromo.in/blog/what-is-a-loan-agent/) .

## Secured Cards, the Category Most People Skip


Secured cards are issued against a fixed deposit, so approval depends on the deposit rather than on a credit score. GroMo carries several, including IDFC WOW! BLACK, SBM Novio, Axis Bank Indian Oil Visa, IDFC FIRST EARN, IDFC Hello Cashback and Axis Bank MY Zone Visa.


Secured cards matter for one reason. Every applicant declined for a regular card is a secured card customer, and a year of clean repayment usually makes them eligible for the regular card afterwards. That turns a dead conversation into two commissions over time, and it is the single most under-used move in card distribution. ## Who Should You Actually Refer?


Matching the card to the spending is what separates a paid case from a wasted application.


**Fuel every week.** Indian Oil Axis Bank, IndianOil RBL XTRA and IDFC FIRST HPCL are built for commuters and drivers.


**Online shopping monthly.** Axis Flipkart and Jupiter Rupay pay back on exactly the platforms these customers already use.


**Groceries and household spending.** RBL Shoprite is designed around the monthly grocery run.


**Travel a few times a year.** Scapia, IndusInd Tiger and AMEX Platinum Reserve suit this group, in ascending order of fee and spending.


**UPI for everything.** KIWI, Zagg, Jupiter and YES Bank POP-CLUB turn scan-and-pay into rewards.


**No credit history, or a past rejection.** Secured cards against a fixed deposit, every time.


A customer declined for one card is not a lost cause, since another issuer may accept the same profile. Sending the same person to five banks at once, though, creates a burst of hard enquiries and lowers the score everything depends on. ## Mistakes That Cost You the Commission


- **Referring on commission instead of fit.** The Rs 2,000 card pays nothing when the applicant doesn't qualify.
- **Promising approval.** Only the bank approves. Never you.
- **Hiding the annual fee.** It appears on the first statement, and the customer remembers who stayed quiet.
- **Collecting money from the applicant.** The bank pays you. A request for a file charge is a warning sign.
- **Handling someone's OTP, PIN or card details.** The applicant completes their own journey, always.
- **Stopping at approval.** Some issuers pay only after issue or first spend, so follow the card to the customer's hand.


*Every one of these is avoidable in the first week.*


## Frequently Asked Questions


**What is credit card refer and earn?**


Credit card refer and earn is a distribution arrangement where you introduce someone to a credit card and earn a fixed commission once the bank approves and issues it. The bank decides approval, the limit and the interest rate. You find the customer and support the application.


**How much commission does a credit card referral pay?**


On GroMo the rates run from a token amount up to Rs 2,000 per approved card depending on the brand. Rates are campaign-linked and change, so check the product card before quoting anything to anyone.


**When do I get paid for a credit card referral?**


After approval and issue, not on application. Some issuers confirm the commission only once the card is activated or first used.


**Do I need my own credit card to refer one?**


No. Holding a card is not a requirement for recommending one. What matters is understanding the fees, rewards and eligibility well enough to explain them honestly.


**What happens if my customer is rejected?**


Nothing is deducted and nothing is owed. Offer a card that fits better, or a secured card against a fixed deposit if the rejection was down to a thin credit file.


**Are these the banks' own referral programmes?**


No. Every card listed here is available for distribution through GroMo, and the rate quoted is what GroMo pays its partners on an approved and issued card, not what the bank offers directly to its own customers.


## Conclusion


Card referral pays a flat amount, and that single fact should shape how you work. Chasing the largest commission usually means chasing the strictest eligibility, and an application that gets declined pays exactly nothing.
