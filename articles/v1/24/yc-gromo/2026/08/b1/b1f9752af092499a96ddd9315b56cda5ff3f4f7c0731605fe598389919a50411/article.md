# Top 10 Apps to Complete Task and Earn Money Online in India

Every complete task and earn money app pays you for the same thing: a few minutes of attention. What separates them is what one task is actually worth. Watch a video and you might make 30 paise. Complete a survey and it's ₹10 to ₹75, usually as Play credit you can't spend outside the Play Store. Get one savings account opened for someone and it's ₹750, paid in cash to your bank.


> **Key Takeaways**


- Task and survey apps in India realistically pay ₹50 to ₹200 on a good day, or roughly ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 a month at best, and most of it arrives as coins, vouchers or Play credit rather than money (app listings and published reviews).
- Google Opinion Rewards pays ₹10 to ₹75 a survey but only in Google Play credit in India, and that credit expires after a year (Google Opinion Rewards Help).
- The highest-paying "task" on this list is completing one financial product application on GroMo: ₹100 to ₹2,000 depending on the product, ₹750 for a savings account, all paid as cash (GroMo product catalogue).
- Earnings everywhere depend on task availability and approvals. Nothing here is a salary.

### 2. Google Opinion Rewards


**The task.** Short surveys, often four or five questions, triggered by places you've been or things you've bought.


**Payout.** ₹10 to ₹75 a survey, which is genuinely the best per-minute rate among the survey apps here. The catch is the currency: in India it pays Google Play credit only, which can't go to UPI or a bank, and unused credit expires after a year.


Worth it if you spend on the Play Store anyway. It won't build savings.


### 3. Teek Task


**The task.** Small digital tasks, no technical skill required.


**Payout.** A few rupees per task, but the withdrawal threshold is the lowest here at ₹50, and users report payment within 24 to 48 hours. A low threshold matters more than it sounds: plenty of apps let you accumulate an amount you can never actually cash out.


Good first stop if you want proof the money is real before spending more time.


### 4. TaskBucks


**The task.** App installs, offers, games and surveys, converted into coins.

Suits students with genuinely free time and modest expectations.


### 5. Task Planet


**The task.** A mix of tasks, surveys and quizzes, with instant rewards.


**Payout.** Small amounts per task, in cash and rewards. It's one of the more established apps in this category, which mostly buys you reliability rather than better rates.

## Can You Earn Real Money by Completing Tasks Online?


Yes, with a ceiling. Task and survey apps reliably produce pocket money, and for a student covering a recharge that's a genuine result. What they don't do is grow. Ten hours a week for a year leaves you earning the same rate as your first day.

## FAQ: Complete Task and Earn Money Apps


**Which app pays the most per task in India?**


GroMo, by a wide margin, because one task is a completed financial product application rather than a survey. Payouts run from ₹100 to ₹2,000 per approved application against a few rupees for a typical micro-task. It also asks the most of you.


**Can I earn money by completing simple tasks online without investment?**


Yes. Every app on this list is free to join, and none require a deposit. Be careful with any app that asks you to pay a registration or unlocking fee, because legitimate task platforms make money from advertisers or product partners, never from you.


**How much can I realistically make from task apps in a month?**


Published reviews of apps like TaskBucks put an active user at ₹50 to ₹200 on a good day, which works out to roughly ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 a month. Most people earn well below that, because the good days need task inventory that isn't always there.


**Why do some apps pay in coins instead of money?**


Coins let an app set a withdrawal threshold and keep balances that never get cashed out. That's not automatically dishonest, but it's why the threshold matters more than the coin balance. Check what it takes to convert coins to rupees before you start earning them.


**Is commission income from GroMo legal for a salaried person?**


Yes. It's commission income, not employment, so no second employer record is created. Check your contract for a conflict clause, declare it in your ITR, and note the Section 194H TDS threshold of ₹20,000 in a financial year. Government employees need prior sanction.
