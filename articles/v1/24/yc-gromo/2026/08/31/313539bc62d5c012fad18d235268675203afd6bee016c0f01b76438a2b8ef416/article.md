Very different economics underneath, because a commission repeats and a bonus doesn't.

## Two Money Models Hiding Behind One Phrase


The phrase gets used for two things that behave nothing alike.


A referral bonus is a customer-acquisition offer. A broker wants signups, so it hands existing users a link and a flat reward when someone new joins. The reward is set by whatever campaign is live that month. It can be cut, paused or withdrawn without notice, because it's a promotion rather than a contract.


Distribution works the other way round. You're the channel, not the customer. The product partner pays a distribution fee for every account you bring that clears KYC and goes live, and that fee is defined per product rather than per campaign. Volume raises your income in a straight line. Nothing resets when a promotion ends.


Both start with you telling someone that they should open a demat account. Only one of them treats you as a business.


*One is a promotion. The other is a payout schedule.*


## What a Broker Referral Bonus Actually Pays


Look closely at the terms of any broker scheme and three limits show up.


The reward is flat. Whether your friend opens the account and never logs in again, or funds it with a large sum, you're paid the same amount. Effort and outcome are disconnected.


The trigger is narrow. Most schemes need the new user to complete the account and place a first trade inside a window. Miss the window, lose the payout.


The catalogue is one item wide. A broker pays you for demat accounts because that's the only thing the broker sells. Someone who tells you they don't invest is a dead end, even though that same person might badly need a credit card or a personal loan.


There's also a quieter problem. A referral link tied to a single brand makes you a promoter of that brand, so when the offer shrinks, your income shrinks with it and you have nowhere to move.


## What a Distributor Commission Actually Pays


A distributor is a registered channel partner with a payout table. Open a demat account for someone and the commission is credited once the depository participant confirms the account is live and activated, not merely applied for.

Three things change immediately.


Your catalogue widens. Demat becomes one row in a list that also holds credit cards, savings accounts, personal loans and credit lines, so almost every conversation has a product attached to it. The customer who says no to investing might still take a credit line.


Your income compounds through relationships rather than links. A person you helped open a demat account is now someone who trusts you with money decisions. Six months later, that's your warmest lead for a card.


Your payout is tied to a defined event. Approved and activated, then paid. On[the GroMo app](https://gromo.in/blog/gromo-app/) , each product carries its own rate card, and the commission lands in your wallet without a monthly settlement cycle.

## Why Demat Pays Less Than a Credit Card


Time to be blunt about it. Demat is not the product that will pay your rent.


Card issuers pay more because the economics behind a card are richer. An approved credit card generates interchange on every swipe, annual fees, interest on revolved balances and EMI conversion income. That's why the top slab on[credit card payouts reaches up to Rs 2,400](https://gromo.in/blog/real-money-earning-apps-2026-gro-mo-referral-income-2400-per-credit-card-2026/) for an approved SBI Card.


A demat account earns its provider far less at the moment it opens. Revenue arrives later, through brokerage on trades the customer may or may not place. Distribution fees follow that reality, so the demat rate on any platform sits well below the card rate. GroMo publishes the live rate inside the app because it moves with the partner and the campaign, which is exactly why you should check it there rather than trust a number in a blog post.


So why sell demat at all? Three reasons hold up.


Demand is broad and rising. India's demat base sat near 23.15 crore accounts at the end of June 2026, and net additions that month hit a four-month high (NSDL and CDSL monthly data, retrieved 2026-08-10). The people opening those accounts are ordinary salaried savers, not only active traders.


Rejection is rare. A demat account carries no credit underwriting, so approval mostly depends on clean KYC documents. Compare that with a card, where a thin credit file kills the application and your commission with it.


Demat opens the door. Someone who lets you handle their first market account will listen when you suggest a better savings account. Think of it as a low-friction first sale that earns the right to a bigger second one.


*Zero joining fee. Payouts land as soon as a sale is confirmed.*

## FAQ: Demat Account Refer and Earn, Answered


**Do I need a demat account myself to earn from demat referrals?**


No. Distribution and investing are separate activities, and you can be paid for sourcing accounts you don't hold. Opening one anyway is smart, because you'll answer questions better once you've been through the flow yourself.


**How much does one demat account pay on GroMo?**


Less than a credit card, where the top slab runs up to Rs 2,400 (gromo.in rate card, retrieved 2026-08-10). The exact demat figure moves with the partner and the running campaign, so quote the live rate in the app rather than a number you read elsewhere. Earnings always depend on approvals and activation.


*The questions that come up most often, in one view.*


**When does the money actually reach me?**


After the account is opened and activated, not when the form is submitted. GroMo processes payouts instantly once the sale is confirmed, so there's no monthly settlement wait. An application stuck in KYC pays nothing until it clears.


**Is any of this legal for a salaried person?**


Distributing financial products is commission income, not employment, so no second employer record is created. Check your employment contract for a conflict-of-interest clause first, and declare the income in your ITR. TDS under Section 194H applies once your commission crosses Rs 20,000 in a financial year. Government employees need prior sanction before taking on outside work.


**Should I only sell demat accounts?**


Selling one product would be a slow way to earn. Demat works best as an easy first sale that builds trust, with cards, loans, savings accounts and credit lines carrying the heavier payouts. Partners who earn well run the full catalogue.
