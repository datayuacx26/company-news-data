# How Affiliate Links Work: Earn with GroMo's Zero Investment Model

An affiliate link is just a URL with your ID baked into it. That’s it. It’s how platforms know a specific sale or sign-up came from you. When someone clicks your link and finishes an action applying for a credit card, opening a demat account the system logs it under your name. That is the entire mechanism GroMo uses to pay out commissions to its 60 lakh+ partners.


If you’ve ever wondered how influencers or that guy from college makes money just by sharing links, this is the answer. In fintech, where a single referral can pay you thousands, these links are the whole game.


## How Does an Affiliate Link Actually Work?


The link carries a unique identifier usually your partner ID inside a normal URL. That ID sticks to the user’s session, typically via cookies. When the required action happens, the system matches it back to you.


You don't need a spreadsheet. You don't need to argue with anyone about who sent whom. If you’re a GroMo Partner promoting a credit card, your link ensures that when a customer clicks and applies, the backend knows exactly who to credit. It’s automatic.


Here’s the actual flow:


1. You grab your unique link from the GroMo app for a product (like a credit card or loan).
2. You share it on WhatsApp, Instagram, a blog, or directly with a friend.
3. The customer clicks and lands on the brand’s application page.
4. Their session gets tagged with your ID.
5. They finish the process card dispatched, loan approved, account opened.
6. You get paid.


This beats the old "go apply and mention my name" method because it removes human error. The customer doesn't have to remember anything.


## Why Affiliate Links Beat Word-of-Mouth Referrals


The problem with verbal referrals is people forget. You tell someone "mention my name," they forget, and you lose the commission. Affiliate links eliminate that trust gap. The tracking is automatic.


In India’s fintech space, this matters because the journeys are long. Getting a credit card involves KYC, document uploads, video verification, and waiting for dispatch. A lot can go wrong between the first click and the final step. Without a tracking link, you’d constantly be fighting to prove you sent the lead.


That’s how GroMo can confidently pay out ₹100 crores to partners. Every rupee traces back to a specific link and a specific action. It’s an unbroken chain.


Compare this to the old days of insurance or PAN card agencies, where you had to manually track paperwork and wait weeks for payouts. Affiliate links compress all that verification into a background process that just works.


## Types of Affiliate Links on GroMo


GroMo splits its links into three main buckets. Each has different rules and payouts.


**Credit Card Links** These usually require the customer to finish the application, do a video KYC, and get the card dispatched. Payouts vary. The AmpliFi Fi-Federal Credit Card, for example, pays ₹1,700–₹2,650 per dispatch, but the customer has to open a linked Fi savings account first.


**Loan Links** This covers personal, business, and loans against mutual funds. Abhiloans pays ₹525–₹800 for loans against mutual funds, with extra percentages for higher disbursal amounts.


**Investment & Demat Links** These are for trading accounts like Aditya Birla Money or Motilal Oswal. You get paid when the account activates and the user makes a first trade within a set window (usually 15–30 days).


The fine print matters here. Terms like "account must stay active for 90 days" or "first trade must exceed ₹1,000" can kill a payout if you ignore them. Read the rules before you share the link.

## Affiliate Links vs Referral Codes: The Difference


They do the same job, but one is lazier (in a good way). A referral code forces the customer to manually type something in. An affiliate link tags them the second they click.


That friction matters more than you’d think. Codes rely on memory. Links don’t. The tracking starts the moment the link is hit.


For GroMo Partners, this usually means better conversions. You aren’t asking the customer to do extra work. You just send them into a tracked journey. It’s why GroMo’s link-based model tends to work better than the old referral-code systems used by cashback apps.

**Frequently Asked Questions**


**Q: What is an affiliate link in simple terms?** A: A URL assigned to you that tracks sales or sign-ups. When someone uses it, you get the credit.


**Q: How long does tracking last?** A: It varies. Some links track for 30 days (like Motilal Oswal). Others require action within days.


**Q: Can I share links on social media?** A: Yes WhatsApp, Instagram, blogs but don’t spam, and follow the product’s rules.


**Q: Do affiliate links expire?** A: Most have a tracking window. If the user waits too long to act, you might lose the credit.


**Q: Is a link different from a referral code?** A: Yes. Links track automatically. Codes require the customer to type them in, which they often forget.


**Q: What if people click but don’t buy?** A: Clicks don’t pay. You only earn when the tracked action (like card dispatch or loan approval) happens.
