## What Is a Zero Balance Savings Account?


A zero balance savings account is a regular savings account with the minimum balance requirement removed. Standard accounts ask you to maintain an average monthly balance and deduct charges when you slip below it. A zero balance account skips both the requirement and the penalty.


Everything else stays intact. You get an account number, a debit card, UPI, net banking and interest on whatever balance you hold. Banks offer these accounts as digital-first products because online onboarding costs them less, which is why almost every account below opens end to end from your phone.


Who should pick one? Anyone who can't promise a steady minimum balance. Students, people starting their first job, second accounts kept for UPI spending, and households where the balance swings month to month.

## FAQs


### Which is the best zero balance savings account in India?


Kotak 811 leads for the overall digital experience, SBI's BSBD account is the strongest pure zero balance product, and IDFC FIRST wins on app quality with monthly interest credits. The best one depends on whether you value network, app or rates.


### How does online bank account opening with zero balance work?


Every account here follows the same flow. Enter Aadhaar and PAN, verify with OTP, fill the digital form, then complete video KYC. The account activates the same day, usually within minutes.


### How can I do a Kotak 811 account open online?


Start the 811 journey through the GroMo app or Kotak's site, enter Aadhaar and PAN, verify with OTP and finish the video KYC. The Super and Classic variants both open fully online with zero balance.


### Does Axis Bank have a zero balance account?


Yes. Axis Bank's digital savings lineup includes zero balance variants that open online with Aadhaar, PAN and video KYC, backed by the bank's full branch and ATM network.


### Is a zero balance account really free forever?


The zero balance promise means no penalty for low balance. Individual services like physical card variants, cheque books or SMS alerts can still carry charges by the bank's schedule, so scan the tariff page of the variant you pick.


### Can I open a zero balance account online without going to a branch?


Yes, for every account on this list except the branch route of SBI's BSBD, which also offers a digital start through YONO. Aadhaar, PAN and a video KYC call replace the branch visit entirely.
