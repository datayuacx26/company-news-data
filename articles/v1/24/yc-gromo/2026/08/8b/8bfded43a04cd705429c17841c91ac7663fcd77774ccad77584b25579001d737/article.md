## What Is Personal Loan Refer and Earn?


Personal loan refer and earn is a distribution arrangement. You introduce a borrower to a lender, the lender underwrites and approves as usual, and when the money is disbursed you receive a percentage of that amount.


**GroMo** is an Indian financial products distribution app, and the 20+ lender brands below sit inside its catalogue alongside credit cards, savings accounts and demat accounts.


Two features separate this from ordinary app referral schemes. The payout is a share of a real transaction rather than a fixed bounty, so it scales with the size of the loan. And it pays only on disbursal, which means an application that stalls at KYC or gets declined earns nothing however much effort went into it.


*A share of a real transaction, not a bounty for an install.*


Worth being precise about what these programmes are, because the wording gets loose across the industry. They are not the lenders' own customer referral offers, and the same distinction applies to the[demat account referral programmes](https://gromo.in/blog/demat-account-refer-and-earn/) compared elsewhere on this blog. Each brand below is available for distribution through the GroMo app, and the rate you see is what GroMo pays a partner on a disbursed case.

## Who Should You Actually Refer?


Matching the borrower to the brand is what separates a paid case from a wasted one.


**Small amount before payday.** Short-tenure lenders such as Payme, Digi Credit and Ram Fincorp are built for this, with quick decisions and one-time repayment.


**Salaried with a modest income.** Tez Credit and KreditBee both work at the lower end of the salary range where larger lenders decline.


**Large ticket, long tenure.** InCred, Bajaj Finserv, IDFC First Bank and Moneyview suit borrowers who need lakhs rather than thousands and can repay over years.


**Self-employed.** Prefr, KreditBee and Unity SFB accept self-employed profiles, which many lenders will not.


**Existing card holder.** HDFC Insta Jumbo only fits people already holding an HDFC credit card.


A borrower declined by one lender isn't a lost cause, since another brand on the shelf may accept the same profile. Sending the same file to five lenders at once, though, creates a burst of hard credit enquiries and lowers the score everything depends on. The wider role is set out in this guide to[what a loan agent does](https://gromo.in/blog/what-is-a-loan-agent/) .


## Mistakes That Cost You the Commission


- **Referring on rate instead of fit.** The 4% brand pays nothing if the borrower doesn't qualify for it.
- **Promising approval.** Only the lender approves. Never you. Say the borrower looks eligible, never that it is confirmed.
- **Collecting money from the borrower.** The lender pays you. Any request for a file charge is a warning sign, not a business model.
- **Disappearing after the link is shared.** Most drop-offs happen at income proof and video KYC, which is exactly where a follow-up call converts.
- **Handling someone's OTP or documents.** The borrower completes their own application, always.
- **Ignoring ticket size.** A high percentage on a Rs 20,000 loan is worth less than a low percentage on a Rs 5,00,000 one.


**Best for large, long-tenure loans.**


*Every one of these is avoidable in the first week.*


## Frequently Asked Questions


**What is personal loan refer and earn?**


It is a distribution arrangement where you introduce a borrower to a lender and earn a share of the disbursed loan amount. The lender approves and lends, you find the borrower and support the application, and the commission is paid once the money reaches the borrower's account.


**How much commission does a personal loan referral pay?**


On GroMo the published rates run from around 0.5% to 4% of the disbursed amount depending on the brand, with most sitting between 2% and 3%. Rates are campaign-linked and change, so check the product card before quoting anything.


**When do I get paid for a personal loan referral?**


On disbursal, not on application or approval. If the borrower drops out or the lender declines the file, nothing is earned.


**Do I need a licence to refer personal loans?**


No national licence exists for loan referrals in India. Registering on a distribution platform that already holds the lender tie-ups is enough to start, and joining GroMo needs PAN and Aadhaar.


**Can I refer the same customer to more than one lender?**


You can, but not all at once. Each application is a hard credit enquiry, and several in a short window lower the score the approval depends on. Try one well-matched lender, then another if it is declined.


**Are these the lenders' own referral programmes?**


No. Every brand listed here is available for distribution through GroMo, and the rate quoted is what GroMo pays its partners on a disbursed case, not what the lender offers directly to its own customers.
