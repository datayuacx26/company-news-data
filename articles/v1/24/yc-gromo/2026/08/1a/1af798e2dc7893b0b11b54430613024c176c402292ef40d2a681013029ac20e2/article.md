Another pays in coins worth a fraction of a rupee. Both call it earning.

## What Does an Earn Money Online Mobile App Actually Pay?


Four payment models exist on this list, and the model matters more than the app.


**Paid for an outcome.** You are paid when something completes, such as a financial product application being approved. Highest value per action, and it pays nothing when the outcome does not happen.


**Paid for attention.** Surveys, videos, offer walls. Small, steady, and capped by how many offers exist for you today.


**Paid for work.** Micro-tasks and freelance gigs, priced per task or per project. Pays properly, and it is real work.


**Paid for a game result.** Cash prizes from skill games. The most volatile, and the one with active legal uncertainty in India.


*Same word, earning. Two very different scales.*


The gap between those models is wider than most lists admit. One approved credit card on GroMo pays up to Rs 2,000 as cash, while a single survey on Google Opinion Rewards pays Rs 10 to Rs 75 in Play Store credit that can't leave the Play Store (app listings, retrieved 2026-08-13).


Before installing anything, check three things: what currency it pays in, what the minimum withdrawal is, and whether it wants money from you first. Those three answers rank this list. Nothing else really matters.

## 2. Google Opinion Rewards


**What it is.** Google's own survey app, which sends short questionnaires triggered by places you have visited and things you have bought.


**What you do.** Answer four or five questions when a survey arrives. It takes under a minute.


**What it pays.** Rs 10 to Rs 75 a survey, the best per-minute rate among survey apps (Google Opinion Rewards Help, retrieved 2026-08-13). In India it pays Google Play credit only, so it can't reach a bank account or UPI, and unused credit expires after a year. That last detail catches people out.


**Best for** people who buy apps, games or subscriptions on the Play Store anyway. It will not build savings, which is the difference between a rewards app and a real[money earning app](https://gromo.in/blog/money-earning-apps/) .


## 3. Chillar


**What it is.** An offers and rewards app whose Play Store listing describes earning real money by completing simple daily tasks, with coins called chillars.


**What you do.** Complete offers, play integrated games, collect a daily bonus for opening the app, and refer friends. The listing advertises up to 600 chillars for completing offers, plus a share of what a referred friend earns on their first three offers (Play Store listing, retrieved 2026-08-13).


**What it pays.** Chillars convert to money and are withdrawn to UPI or a Paytm wallet. Individual offers are small, so this is pocket money rather than income.


Don't expect it to replace anything.


**Best for** filling dead time, with the daily bonus giving a reason to return.


## 4. Sikka


**What it is.** A tasks and rewards app built for Indian users, offering surveys, videos and a spin-and-win feature according to its Play Store listing.


**What you do.** Complete surveys, watch videos, finish small tasks and spin for bonus coins.


**What it pays.** Coins redeemable for cash, vouchers and discounts. Per-task value is among the lowest here, and how much you can earn depends entirely on how many surveys you qualify for.


Qualification rates decide everything here.


**Best for** students and anyone who enjoys the streak-and-reward loop more than the money.


## 5. Rupiyo


**What it is.** A rewards app that pays for offers, games and spins, with earnings paid as cash and wallet credit.


**What you do.** Complete offers, play, spin the wheel, and refer friends. The referral programme pays a share of what your referrals earn once they complete their first three offers.


**What it pays.** A few rupees per completed offer, withdrawn to Paytm or a bank account. The referral share is where regular users make most of their money.


Solo grinding pays badly. Referring pays better.


**Best for** people with a network they can genuinely refer, rather than solo grinders.


## 6. Bonus Buddy


**What it is.** An offers app that pays for completing tasks, with withdrawals to UPI.


**What you do.** Pick offers from the wall, complete them properly, and claim a daily bonus. There is a bonus for finishing ten offers and a referral programme on top.


**What it pays.** Cash to UPI, with payments processed within a few hours of the request according to its listing. The app also advertises much larger monthly figures for its top users, which are marketing claims rather than typical earnings, so treat them accordingly.


Read the offer conditions. Every time.


**Best for** people who want money in UPI quickly and will actually read the offer conditions.


*Four checks before you install anything.*


## 7. Toloka


**What it is.** A global micro-tasking platform where businesses post small data tasks and users complete them for payment.


**What you do.** Register with a phone number, then pick tasks such as checking business details, verifying search results, identifying image content or correcting typos. No special qualification is needed.


**What it pays.** Earnings are credited once the requester accepts your work, held in dollars and cashed out through Payoneer. Payment per task is small, so the hourly rate depends on how fast and accurately you work.


Toloka is task work, not an offer wall.


**Best for** methodical people who don't mind an international payout route.


## 8. Funngro


**What it is.** An Indian freelancing platform aimed at young people, connecting students and teenagers with brands that need small pieces of work done.


**What you do.** Sign up, browse gigs from partner companies, and complete real freelance projects such as content, design, research and marketing tasks.


**What it pays.** Rs 50 to Rs 1,000 or more per project, paid over UPI, with no joining fee (Play Store listing, retrieved 2026-08-13). It's real freelance work, so it's paid like freelance work.


**Best for** students aged 14 to 25 who want portfolio work rather than coins, and the only app here designed specifically for that age group. Anyone over 18 can also distribute financial products, a route explained in this guide to[becoming a GroMo Partner](https://gromo.in/blog/gromo-partner/) .


## 9. Ludo Cash


**What it is.** A skill-based Ludo game that runs cash-prize tournaments, positioned as free to play with no deposit required.


**What you do.** Play Ludo matches and tournaments against other players, and withdraw prize winnings over UPI.


**What it pays.** Entirely prize-dependent. You win some and lose some. Nothing here is predictable income.


**Best for** people who already play Ludo for fun. Read the next paragraph before you install it.


**The legal position.** India's Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 prohibits online money games, covering games of skill as well as chance, and challenges to it are still before the Supreme Court. Apps like this one operate on a no-deposit model, which is why they describe themselves as free to play. The safe rule for a reader is simple: never deposit money into a game in the expectation of winning money back.


## 10. KickCash


**What it is.** An Indian rewards app built around games and missions, with withdrawals to UPI and bank transfer.


**What you do.** Play the listed games, complete daily missions and milestones, join tournaments, and refer friends for bonuses.


**What it pays.** Rewards are tied to gameplay achievements and mission completion rather than a fixed rate per task. The app advertises instant UPI withdrawal. Treat the advertised totals as marketing.


**Best for** people who would be playing casual games anyway. The same caution about depositing money applies here.

## Which App Pays the Most Per Hour?


Ranked honestly, the order is not close. One approved application on GroMo is worth up to Rs 2,000, which is more than an offer wall returns in a month of daily use. Freelance gigs on Funngro come second at Rs 50 to Rs 1,000 a project, and surveys sit third at Rs 10 to Rs 75 each, in Play credit rather than cash (app listings, retrieved 2026-08-13).


The catch is that the order reverses on effort. Surveys need a minute and no conversation. A financial product needs you to know the product and talk to somebody who wants it. Pick the point on that trade-off you'll actually keep doing.


## How to Choose an Earn Money Online Mobile App


Four questions settle it faster than any review.


- **What does it pay in?** Cash to a bank is worth more than wallet credit, which is worth more than Play credit, which is worth far more than coins.
- **What is the withdrawal threshold?** An app paying a rupee a task with a Rs 500 minimum is asking for five hundred tasks before you see anything.
- **Does it want money from you?** A joining fee, a deposit or a paid starter pack means the answer is no.
- **What is the ceiling?** Ask what a really good month looks like. For offer apps that answer is small, and no amount of effort changes it.


If the honest answer is that you want the highest number per hour spent, the outcome-based option wins by a wide margin. If you want the least effort per rupee, surveys win. Most people should run one of each and drop whichever bores them first. ## Which Apps Should You Avoid?


- **Anything with a joining fee.** No legitimate earning app charges you to start.
- **Deposit-to-play games.** Money games are prohibited under India's 2025 online gaming law, and the case is still in court. Never fund a game wallet to chase winnings.
- **Apps asking for your OTP, PIN or documents** outside their own verified flow.
- **Apps demanding SMS or call log access** for a task that has nothing to do with either.
- **Screenshot income proofs** in referral groups. Those are marketing, not evidence.


## Frequently Asked Questions


**Which mobile app is best to earn money in India?**


The answer depends on what you want per hour, and it differs by person. GroMo pays the most per action because one approved financial product pays up to Rs 2,000 as cash. Funngro and Toloka pay for real work. Survey and offer apps pay least but ask least.


**Can I really make money from my phone without investment?**


Yes, and none of the ten apps here needs an investment to start. Anything that asks for a joining fee, a deposit or a paid kit before you can earn should be avoided.


**Which earning apps pay directly to a bank account?**


GroMo, Bonus Buddy, Rupiyo and KickCash pay cash to UPI or a bank account. Google Opinion Rewards pays only Google Play credit in India. Toloka pays in dollars through Payoneer.


**Are money earning apps safe?**


The ones that pay from advertising or commission are legitimate businesses. Risk sits with apps that demand a deposit, ask for permissions unrelated to the task, or want your OTP. Check what an app asks for before you install it.


**How much can a beginner earn from a mobile app?**


On offer and survey apps, realistically a few hundred rupees a month. On commission-based work, a single approved application can be worth more than that, but it depends entirely on approvals and nothing is guaranteed.


**Are cash game apps legal in India?**


India's Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 prohibits online money games, including games of skill, and legal challenges are pending before the Supreme Court. Apps offering free entry with prizes describe themselves as outside that definition. Do not deposit money into any game in the expectation of winning money back.
