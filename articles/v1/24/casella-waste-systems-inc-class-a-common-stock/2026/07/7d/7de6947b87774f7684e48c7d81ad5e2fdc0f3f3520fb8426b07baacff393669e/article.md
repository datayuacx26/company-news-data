# Safety, Service, and Shared Purpose: 2026 Maintenance & Operations Summit


The 2026 Maintenance & Operations Summit brought together teams from across Casella’s operating footprint for a high‑energy week centered on collaboration, safety leadership, and shared purpose. Momentum built quickly as teams and partners engaged directly, opening meaningful conversations about shared challenges and opportunities. Sessions focused on elevating training, improving operational consistency, and reinforcing safety as a company‑wide responsibility, underscored by a powerful keynote that highlighted the human impact behind safe work. Together, these moments shaped a gathering defined by learning, empathy, and a shared drive to continuously improve.

#### About Casella Waste Systems, Inc.


Casella Waste Systems, Inc., headquartered in Rutland, Vermont, is one of the largest recyclers and most experienced fully integrated resource management companies in the Eastern United States. Founded in 1975 as a single truck collection service, Casella has grown its operations to provide solid waste collection and disposal, transfer, recycling, and organics services to more than one million residential, commercial, municipal, institutional, and industrial customers and provides professional resource management services to over 10,000 customer locations in more than 40 states.
