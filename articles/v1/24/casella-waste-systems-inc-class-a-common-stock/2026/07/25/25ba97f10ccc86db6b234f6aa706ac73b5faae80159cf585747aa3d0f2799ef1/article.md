# Capturing Opportunity: Turning Landfill Gas into Clean Energy in Chemung, NY


A long-standing partnership between Casella and Chemung County is helping transform landfill gas into renewable natural gas (RNG), creating a valuable energy resource while reducing greenhouse gas emissions. Through collaboration with Waga Energy, the project captures gas generated through the natural decomposition of waste and converts it into lower-carbon energy that can be delivered to the local gas grid. The facility supports New York State’s energy and climate goals while demonstrating how strategic partnerships can advance environmental stewardship, operational excellence, and long-term community benefits.

#### About Casella Waste Systems, Inc.


Casella Waste Systems, Inc., headquartered in Rutland, Vermont, is one of the largest recyclers and most experienced fully integrated resource management companies in the Eastern United States. Founded in 1975 as a single truck collection service, Casella has grown its operations to provide solid waste collection and disposal, transfer, recycling, and organics services to more than one million residential, commercial, municipal, institutional, and industrial customers and provides professional resource management services to over 10,000 customer locations in more than 40 states.
