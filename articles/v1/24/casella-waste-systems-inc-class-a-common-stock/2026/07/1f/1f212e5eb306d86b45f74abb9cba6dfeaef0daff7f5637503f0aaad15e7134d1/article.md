# Mastering the Machine: Eric Patch on PFAS, Precision, and Purpose


Environmental Technician Eric Patch


has become the steady hand behind one of Casella’s first landfill‑based PFAS treatment systems, earning the nickname “the foam whisperer” for his deep understanding of the technology. His journey, from third‑shift truck mechanic to mastering a complex, first‑of‑its‑kind environmental system, reflects more than a decade of hands‑on learning and dedication. Today, Eric’s commitment to doing the job safely, precisely, and with pride plays a vital role in protecting the environment and strengthening Casella’s leadership in PFAS mitigation. For him, the work is challenging, meaningful, and exactly where he wants to be.

#### About Casella Waste Systems, Inc.


Casella Waste Systems, Inc., headquartered in Rutland, Vermont, is one of the largest recyclers and most experienced fully integrated resource management companies in the Eastern United States. Founded in 1975 as a single truck collection service, Casella has grown its operations to provide solid waste collection and disposal, transfer, recycling, and organics services to more than one million residential, commercial, municipal, institutional, and industrial customers and provides professional resource management services to over 10,000 customer locations in more than 40 states.
