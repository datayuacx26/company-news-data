# Turning Insight Into Impact: How UMass Chan and Casella Are Advancing Campus Sustainability Together


When it comes to achieving sustainability goals, education and collaboration can be just as important as infrastructure. Through its collaboration with Casella, UMass Chan Medical School is gaining valuable insights into the recycling process, using data, hands-on learning, and facility tours to help improve waste diversion efforts across campus. By seeing firsthand what happens to materials after they leave a recycling bin, students, faculty, and sustainability leaders are building confidence in the system and finding new opportunities to reduce waste, increase recycling, and create lasting environmental impact.

#### About Casella Waste Systems, Inc.


Casella Waste Systems, Inc., headquartered in Rutland, Vermont, is one of the largest recyclers and most experienced fully integrated resource management companies in the Eastern United States. Founded in 1975 as a single truck collection service, Casella has grown its operations to provide solid waste collection and disposal, transfer, recycling, and organics services to more than one million residential, commercial, municipal, institutional, and industrial customers and provides professional resource management services to over 10,000 customer locations in more than 40 states.
