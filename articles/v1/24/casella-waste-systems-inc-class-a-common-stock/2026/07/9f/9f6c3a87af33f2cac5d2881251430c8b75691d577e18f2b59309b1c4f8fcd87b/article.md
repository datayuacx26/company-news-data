# Stitching Together a More Sustainable Approach with Portland Mattress Makers


The partnership between Casella and Portland Mattress Makers is rooted in a shared commitment to durability, resource conservation, and responsible material recovery. By crafting long‑lasting mattresses and working with Casella to ensure those products are properly recycled at the end of their life, the PMM team is helping divert valuable materials from disposal and strengthen the regional circular economy.


"Sustainability starts with having something that lasts a long time, " said George Samaras, Owner of Portland Mattress Makers. "We knew we wanted to take meaningful action, but it was Casella that reached out and showed us exactly how mattress recycling could work. Their approach aligned perfectly with the direction we’d been hoping to go, and partnering with them made the transition both practical and impactful."


Together, Casella and Portland Mattress Makers are demonstrating how thoughtful manufacturing and innovative recycling solutions can reinforce one another, creating products that last longer, waste less, and support a more sustainable future.
