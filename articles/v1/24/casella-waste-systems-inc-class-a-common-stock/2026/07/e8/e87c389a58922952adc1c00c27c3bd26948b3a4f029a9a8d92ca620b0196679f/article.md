# Adam Stoutenburg Named Otsego County Chamber of Commerce Volunteer of the Year


A standout commitment to community service has earned Casella Oneonta, NY Territory Sales Representative Adam Stoutenburg the honor of being named the[Otsego County Chamber of Commerce’](https://otsegocc.com/) s 2026 Volunteer of the Year. The award, presented at the Chamber’s Annual Business Dinner on May 14, recognizes individuals whose leadership, dedication, and service strengthen the fabric of the county. The Otsego County Chamber, long known for supporting local businesses and celebrating regional excellence through its annual awards program, selected Stoutenburg for his meaningful contributions and unwavering commitment to the community. His recognition reflects both his personal dedication and the values that guide Casella’s community engagement across the region.

#### About Casella Waste Systems, Inc.


Casella Waste Systems, Inc., headquartered in Rutland, Vermont, is one of the largest recyclers and most experienced fully integrated resource management companies in the Eastern United States. Founded in 1975 as a single truck collection service, Casella has grown its operations to provide solid waste collection and disposal, transfer, recycling, and organics services to more than one million residential, commercial, municipal, institutional, and industrial customers and provides professional resource management services to over 10,000 customer locations in more than 40 states.
