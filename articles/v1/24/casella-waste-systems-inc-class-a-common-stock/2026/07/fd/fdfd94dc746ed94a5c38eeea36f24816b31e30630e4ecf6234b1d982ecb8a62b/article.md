# Dan Gagnon: Running for Life


Dan Gagnon running a marathon in support of St. Jude's Children's Hospitals.


In this episode of **[Beyond the Bin](https://casella.buzzsprout.com/)** , we have a special interview with Dan Gagnon, a driver for Casella. When Dan Gagnon decided to quit smoking in 2010 he replaced it with a new habit, running. He never dreamed that more than a decade later he would be using his 17-mile commute as a training opportunity for his seventh marathon, or that he would be helping to improve many other lives and not just his own. Like many Casella drivers, Dan's commitment to service extends far beyond the cab of his truck and in October, Dan will be running in the Boston Marathon, his fifth marathon in support of St. Jude's Children's Hospital.


To learn more about our efforts to support St. Jude's Children's Hospital through Dan's running please visit **[casella.com/RunDanRun](https://www.casella.com/RunDanRun)** .

#### About Casella Waste Systems, Inc.


Casella Waste Systems, Inc., headquartered in Rutland, Vermont, is the Northeast’s largest recycler and most experienced fully integrated resource management company. Founded in 1975 as a single truck collection service, Casella has grown its operations to provide solid waste collection and disposal, transfer, recycling, and organics services to more than 900,000 residential, commercial, municipal, institutional, and industrial customers throughout the Northeast, and professional resource management services to over 10,000 customer locations in more than 40 states.
