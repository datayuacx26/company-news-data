# Case Study: How Badho Streamlined Data Analytics with CamelAI

## Company Overview


**Company:**[Badho](https://www.badho.in/) **Industry:** Supply Chain & Trade Software **Head of Product:**[Manmohan Singh Charan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/maciitr/)


Badho focuses on making trade more efficient, transparent, and accessible by simplifying supply chain operations.


## The Challenge


Badho's teams struggled with retrieving and analyzing data efficiently. Business stakeholders needed specific queries that required analyst time, and deeper analysis required close collaboration -- creating bottlenecks based on analyst availability and slowing decision-making.


## Why CamelAI


Badho selected CamelAI for its "reasonable accuracy and almost zero training time." Previous local solutions required extensive training and exhibited accuracy and cost issues.


## Implementation


- **Data sources:** Postgres database and Facebook
- **Primary users:** Data analysts
- **Usage frequency:** Daily


## Results


The organization achieved significant efficiency gains:


- **Analysts save an estimated 3-4 hours daily** compared to previous methods
- Business stakeholders no longer wait for routine queries
- Non-technical users better understand SQL and query logic
- The chat-based interface proved most valuable, requiring no SQL knowledge while enabling faster data access


## The Impact


With CamelAI, everyone adopting the platform removes SQL as a barrier, making data-driven culture adoption significantly easier than previously possible. The Head of Product emphasized that when the entire team can access data independently, the organization moves faster and makes better decisions.

> "Go ahead. Try it out... It has never been this convenient to get relevant data!" -- Manmohan Singh Charan, Head of Product
