# How Oasis LMS Uses CamelAI to Help Users Uncover Hidden Buying Patterns

## About Oasis LMS


[Oasis LMS](https://oasis-lms.com/) is a learning management platform serving organizations like the[American Heart Association](https://www.heart.org/) and[American Cancer Society](https://www.cancer.org/) . The platform enables educators to deliver professional training, certifications, and webinars to working professionals.


## The Challenge: Endless Reporting Requests


Oasis LMS faced a common SaaS challenge: each customer needed customized reports. CEO[Thomas Wong](https://www.linkedin.com/in/thomas-wong-44819a65/) noted: "Our joke is you can never have enough reports." Non-technical educators using the platform couldn't easily explore data independently, creating bottlenecks for engineering teams.


## Why Oasis LMS Chose CamelAI


The company adopted[CamelAI](https://camelai.com/) to enable natural-language data exploration. Wong explained that users initially asked simple questions, but soon discovered deeper analytical possibilities. CamelAI's[API](https://camelai.com/api) enabled seamless integration into their existing platform.


## From Reports to Revenue Insights


A customer asked CamelAI about purchasing patterns affecting marketing strategy. The tool identified spikes in user registrations coinciding with product launches, enabling the customer to adjust release schedules and improve conversions.


Wong emphasized: "Most tools stop at Level 1 questions—simple filtering and counting. CamelAI goes to Levels 3 and 4—understanding patterns and recommending revenue-improving steps."


## Seamless Setup and Strong Trust


Implementation proved straightforward. Wong noted: "Setup was amazing...your team was super responsive." After verifying results against SQL queries, trust was established quickly.


## Results and Value


- **Self-serve reporting** for non-technical users
- **Discovery of hidden marketing insights** through purchasing pattern analysis
- **Reduced engineering workload** by eliminating custom report requests
- **Faster business decisions** powered by natural-language data access


Wong concluded: "Sometimes users don't even know how to articulate what they want. But CamelAI interprets it, finds connections, and surfaces insights that make you think differently."
