## Why Creators Are Moving Away from Link-in-Bio Tools


### 1. You Don't Own the Platform


Linktree, Beacons, Stan Store — they're all renting you space on their platform. If they change their pricing, shut down, or deprioritize your content type, you're starting over.


When you build your own site, you own it. Your domain, your design, your data.


### 2. Generic Design Kills Your Brand


Your TikTok feed has a vibe. Your merch has a vibe. Your Linktree... looks like every other Linktree. A custom website lets your online home match the brand you've built everywhere else.


### 3. You're Losing Sales


Link-in-bio tools add friction. Your follower clicks your bio link, lands on a list of links, clicks again to get to your shop, then has to navigate a third-party checkout. Every click loses people.


A custom site with an integrated shop means: bio link → your page → buy. Two steps instead of four.


### 4. No Analytics Depth


Linktree tells you how many people clicked each link. A real website with analytics tells you where visitors came from, what they looked at, how long they stayed, and where they dropped off. That data helps you create better content and sell more.

## Tips for a High-Converting Creator Page


**1. Mobile first, always.** Over 90% of your TikTok audience will visit on a phone. Design for thumb-scrolling, not mouse-clicking.


**2. Put the money-maker above the fold.** Your best-selling product, your most important link, or your email signup should be visible without scrolling.


**3. Use your actual content.** Embed your best TikToks, not stock photos. Your followers want to see *you* .


**4. Keep it fast.** A slow page loses visitors. Sites built on Cloudflare Workers load in under a second globally.


**5. One clear CTA per section.** Don't overwhelm visitors with choices. Each section should guide them toward one action.


**6. Update regularly.** Swap in your latest content, current promotions, and seasonal offerings. A stale page feels abandoned.


**7. Add an email signup.** Social platforms come and go. An email list is the one audience you truly own. Offer something free (a guide, template, discount code) in exchange for their email.
