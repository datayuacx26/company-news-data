# Understanding Firearms: A Practical Guide for Organization Leaders Evaluating Weapons Detection

Organizations evaluating weapons detection technologies are often asked to make important security decisions without a deep understanding of how modern firearms are designed or how their physical characteristics affect detection.


#### This paper provides a practical overview of:


- Common handgun types
- Their components
- How they’re constructed


This is intended to help organizational leaders make more informed decisions when assessing weapons detection solutions.
