# Weapons Detection – What Is It?

As schools explore technologies designed to help support safer learning environments, the term weapons detection is often used broadly, yet not all solutions work the same way. This guide is designed to help K-12 leaders better understand what weapons detection is, what it is not, and the important differences between the technologies available today.


Focused specifically on concealed weapons screening at school entrances, this whitepaper provides a practical overview of how modern screening systems work, what types of threats they are designed to detect, and what schools should consider when evaluating potential solutions.


#### Readers Will Learn:


- What “weapons detection” means and why the term can describe very different technologies


- The differences between metal detection, weapons detection, and other screening approaches


- What entrance screening systems can realistically detect and where their limitations exist


- Key factors that influence detection performance and operational effectiveness


- How to balance security objectives with throughput, staffing, and student experience considerations
