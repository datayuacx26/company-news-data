# American Chemistry Council Elects New Class to Board of Directors


##### INGEVITY IN THE NEWS


During today’s Annual Business Meeting, the American Chemistry Council (ACC) approved twenty-three individuals to serve on its board of directors for new or extended terms beginning January 1, 2025.


“I’m humbled by the continued commitment of our members to lead ACC forward as the premier U.S. advocacy organization for chemical manufacturers,” said Chris Jahn, American Chemistry Council president and CEO. “I look forward to continuing to recruit and grow the diverse representation on our Board so that we can serve our industry in new ways and as one collective voice.”


The ACC board of directors sets council policy, provides strategic direction, and approves advocacy priorities and the annual budget.
