# Plant Spotlight – North Charleston

The roots of Ingevity’s Performance Chemicals operations in North Charleston, South Carolina, trace back to the 1940s. A paper mill adjacent to Ingevity serves as a visible reminder of the sustainable nature of the renewable wood-based raw materials the plant processes and derivatizes into products that enable crops to grow fuller, make roads last longer and otherwise enhance our lives. If you visit us here, we welcome you to also arrange a stop at Ingevity’s global headquarters just a few blocks away or the Ingevity Technical Center across the street.


See more of the collaborative problem-solving and genuine attitude inside our North Charleston plant, with[this employee spotlight](https://www.ingevity.com/news/employee-spotlight-scott-cordell/) from kettle operator Scott Cordell and his teammates.
