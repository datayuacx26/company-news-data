# Plant Spotlight – Covington

Located in a wooded river valley in the mountains of Covington, Virginia, you’ll find Ingevity’s longest-running Performance Materials operations. In this video, team members share why they’re proud to be part of the sustainable impact Ingevity makes in the community – and far, far beyond. From safety to innovation to integrity, we work together to realize[The IngeviWay](https://www.ingevity.com/the-ingeviway/) vision of all Ingevity has to offer to our world.


The activated carbon our Covington team members make for our customers are used in automobiles all over the world to clean and protect the environment from harmful evaporative emissions. Activated carbon is also used to purify in food and beverage, municipal water treatment, specialty and industrial chemical processes.
