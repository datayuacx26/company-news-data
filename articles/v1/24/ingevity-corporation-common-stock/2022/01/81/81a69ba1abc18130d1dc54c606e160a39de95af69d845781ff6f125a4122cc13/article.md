# Plant Spotlight – Changshu

Located in Suzhou, China near the Yangtze River, you’ll find Ingevity’s Changshu plant specializing in the production of our Performance Material products. In this video, you’ll learn more about the rich culture and landscape Changshu offers, as well as the impact our team makes daily to purify, protect and enhance the world. The activated carbon our Changshu team members make for our customers are used in automobiles all over the world to clean and protect the environment from harmful evaporative emissions. Activated carbon is also used to purify in food and beverage, municipal water treatment, specialty and industrial chemical processes.
