# Plant Spotlight – Waynesboro

Our Performance Materials Waynesboro facility is located in Burke County, Georgia, 30 miles outside of Augusta. It is home to a tight-knit community famously known for hosting the world’s largest field trial competition for bird dogs.


The products produced in Waynesboro have a direct and lasting impact on the air quality of the entire planet. Fulfilling Ingevity’s mission to purify, protect and enhance the world around us, our employees are committed to making products that both reduce global emissions and purify the environment. The plant is the leading supplier of activated carbon honeycombs for the auto emissions and air purification industries.


In this video, our employees share why they are proud to be part of the sustainable impact Waynesboro is having on the world, the surrounding community and beyond. From safety to continuous improvement, to growth and development, Waynesboro employees work together to create a culture that is truly unique.
