# Lovelace Westside Hospital Completes Medical-Surgical Expansion to Meet Growing Community Needs

**ALBUQUERQUE, N.M. (July 10, 2026)** – Lovelace Westside Hospital has expanded its inpatient capacity with the addition of 15 new medical-surgical beds, increasing access to care for patients across Rio Rancho and Albuquerque's Westside as the region continues to grow.


The newly renovated unit features modern patient rooms with updated finishes, new flooring and warm, welcoming design elements that support comfort, healing and an exceptional patient experience.


"As our community grows, so does the need for greater access to high-quality hospital care," said Tracie Stratton, chief executive officer of Lovelace Westside Hospital. "Adding these new inpatient beds allows us to care for more patients close to home while enhancing the environment where they receive care. This investment reflects our ongoing commitment to expanding access and ensuring our hospital is well-positioned to meet the healthcare needs of our community for years to come."


The expansion is part of Lovelace Westside Hospital's continued investment in delivering exceptional, patient-centered care while strengthening healthcare access throughout New Mexico. By increasing inpatient capacity, the hospital is better equipped to meet rising demand, reduce wait times and ensure patients can receive timely, compassionate care close to where they live.


To learn more about Lovelace Westside Hospital and its commitment to providing quality, compassionate care, visit[lovelace.com](https://lovelace.com/) .


**About Lovelace Westside Hospital**
Lovelace Westside Hospital (LWSH) just celebrated its 40th birthday of serving Rio Rancho and Westside residents of Albuquerque high quality, convenient care. The 92-bed acute care facility includes a 24-hour emergency department where patients typically see a provider in 30 minutes or less. LWSH has been recognized by the American Heart Association (AHA) for its commitment to guideline adherence and quality improvement for stroke patients, earning the AHA’s *Get With The Guidelines® - Stroke GOLD PLUS* for four years in a row (2021 – 2024). The hospital offers services tailored to everyone in the family, including surgical services, sleep studies, a nationally-accredited bariatrics program and more. Money and the Leapfrog Group named LWSH to their list for *2022 Best Hospitals and Surgery Centers for Billing Ethics,* showing its dedication to delivering the highest quality of care for patients, while ensuring adherence to the maximum ethical standards for billing.
