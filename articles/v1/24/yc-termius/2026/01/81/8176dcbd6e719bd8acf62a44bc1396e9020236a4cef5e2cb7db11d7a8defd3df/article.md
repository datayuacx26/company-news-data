# Workspaces: Focus Without Losing Context - Termius Blog

Modern infrastructure work rarely happens in a straight line.


You might be working on one host, checking logs on another, and waiting on a long-running command. Every task is important. Each one needs your attention, but not all at the same time.


Traditional terminal tabs are not helpful here. If you keep switching, focus breaks. If you close tabs, you might lose visibility. If you keep everything open, it will create noise.


Good focus means you know where to look and when. That's why engineers want to have a clear structure. A way to group related work, reduce noise, and stay aware of what’s running in the background.


That’s the idea behind **Workspaces** .


#### Organize work by intent, not by tabs


Instead of treating each connection as an isolated tab, Workspaces let you organize terminals by **intent** : a deployment, an incident, a feature rollout, or an investigation.


Create a Workspace from a` New Tab` by selecting recent connections. Termius opens them together, ready to work.


#### Focus mode: one task at the center


When a Workspace opens, it starts in Focus mode.
One terminal takes center stage. The rest stay visible in a side list – present, but not distracting.
When a background command finishes, its tab notifies you. You don’t have to keep checking or guessing.
If autocomplete is enabled, each terminal also shows its working directory and active command. You can see what’s happening at a glance, without switching views.


You stay focused without losing awareness.


#### Split view: everything in parallel


When awareness matters more than isolation, **Split view** gives you the full picture.


All terminals in a Workspace are visible on screen at the same time. You can resize them, move between sessions quickly, and keep every environment in view.
Watch logs, compare outputs, and track progress across hosts in real time. It’s ideal for investigations, rollouts, or coordinated changes.
Switch between **Focus mode** and **Split view** anytime.


The Workspace stays the same. Only your perspective changes.


#### Broadcast commands


Some tasks need consistency.


With **Broadcast** , you can send a command to every terminal in a Workspace at once, whether you’re in Split view or Focus mode. Run checks, apply changes, or validate configs across multiple hosts at once.


One action. No misses.


#### Come back to the same environment


When your environment is set up just right, you shouldn’t have to rebuild it. Save a Workspace as a **template** and restore it later with one click.


Connections, working directories, and even running commands are all remembered. The next day, you pick up exactly where you left off.


#### Take control of your focus


Bring structure to your terminal work with Workspaces. Group related sessions, switch views as needed, and let Termius handle the complexity.
