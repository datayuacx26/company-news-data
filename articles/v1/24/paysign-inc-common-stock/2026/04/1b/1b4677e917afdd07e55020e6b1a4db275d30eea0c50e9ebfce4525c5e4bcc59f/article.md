# Paysign is a Sponsor at the 2026 Innovative Payments Conference, Washington, D.C.

Paysign will be a sponsor at the annual **[Innovative Payments Conference](https://www.ipa.org/ipc.html)** , “Making Sense of Payments in a Changing World”in Washington, D.C. on April 29 – May 01, 2026. This 3-day conference will consist of revolutionary content, discussions, and networking opportunities with those leading the way in payments compliance, legislation, regulation, and innovation.
