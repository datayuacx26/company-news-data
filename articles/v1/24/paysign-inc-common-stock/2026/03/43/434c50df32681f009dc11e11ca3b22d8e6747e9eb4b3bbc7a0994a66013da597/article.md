# Paysign is a Sponsor at the 2026 International Plasma Protein Therapeutics Conference, Milan, Italy

Paysign is excited to be a sponsor at the Plasma Protein Therapeutics Association **[2026 International Plasma Protein Congress](https://www.pptaglobal.org/events/international-plasma-protein-congress)** held on April 28-29 in Milan, Italy. This conference will explore the plasma industry, the need to increase plasma collection, patient health, safety, and the developments within regulatory and policy categories.
