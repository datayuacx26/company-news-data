DENVER--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Janus Living, Inc. (NYSE: JAN), a pure-play senior housing real estate investment trust (REIT), is scheduled to report second quarter 2026 financial results after the close of trading on the New York Stock Exchange on Tuesday, August 4, 2026.


Janus Living will host a conference call and webcast on Wednesday, August 5, 2026 at 12:00 p.m. Eastern Time to review its financial performance and operating results.
