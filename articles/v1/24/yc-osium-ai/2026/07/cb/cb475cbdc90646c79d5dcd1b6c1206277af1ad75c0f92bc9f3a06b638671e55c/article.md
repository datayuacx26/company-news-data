# Osium AI joins a consortium to lead innovation in materials and chemicals discovery with AI

We are excited to announce that Osium AI joined a leading consortium focused on advancing materials and chemicals development. Osium AI brings its unique AI expertise and platform to support its partners in speeding up their R&D processes.


Together with Sorbonne University, Expiris, Fluorotechnique, PCAS, Maestria, Atem Composites and CNRS, we are pooling expertise, resources, and cutting-edge technology to accelerate progress towards our shared vision.


Being recognized among such renowned research institutions and industry players is a tremendous honor and underscores Osium AI’s position at the forefront of innovation in materials and chemicals discovery.
