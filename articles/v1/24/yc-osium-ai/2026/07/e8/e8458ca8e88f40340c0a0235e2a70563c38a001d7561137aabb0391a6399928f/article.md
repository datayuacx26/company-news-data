# Sarah Najmark won the 2024 Prize for the AI Woman Engineer

Our CEO Sarah Najmark has been awarded the 2024 AI Women Engineer Prize by French Prime Minister Élisabeth Borne.


Since its creation in 2001 by[Tilder](https://www.tilder.com/) and[Challenges](https://www.challenges.fr/) , this prestigious award has celebrated outstanding female leaders advancing the field of artificial intelligence. Sarah’s recognition not only underscores her remarkable achievements and the impact of Osium AI (YC S23), but also serves as a powerful reminder of the importance of increasing women’s representation in tech.
