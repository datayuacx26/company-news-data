WILDLIGHT, Fla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Rayonier Inc. (NYSE:RYN) plans to release its second quarter 2026 earnings after the market closes on Wednesday, August 5, 2026.


Rayonier will host a conference call and live audio webcast at 10:00 a.m. (ET) on Thursday, August 6 to discuss these results. The conference call can be accessed by registering online at[Q2 2026 Rayonier Earnings Call Webcast](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fevents.q4inc.com%2Fanalyst%2F857399297%3Fpwd%3DFVW3yDQT&esheet=54571252&newsitemid=20260715436157&lan=en-US&anchor=Q2+2026+Rayonier+Earnings+Call+Webcast&index=2&md5=d8c299eb95a26b1e1e35e79ea1ecb5e4) , at which time registrants will receive dial-in information.


**About Rayonier**


Rayonier is a land resources real estate investment trust (REIT) with a portfolio comprising over four million acres in the U.S. South and U.S. Northwest. The company is focused on managing its timberlands on a sustainable basis while optimizing its overall portfolio value by delivering land to its highest and best use. Rayonier also operates six sawmills, an industrial-grade plywood mill, residential and commercial real estate developments, and a rural land sales program. Rayonier is committed to corporate responsibility, third-party forest certification, and supporting climate change mitigation through its land-based solutions business. More information is available at[www.rayonier.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.rayonier.com&esheet=54571252&newsitemid=20260715436157&lan=en-US&anchor=www.rayonier.com&index=3&md5=73c0298a5b08b5e33c46a60847967f19) .
