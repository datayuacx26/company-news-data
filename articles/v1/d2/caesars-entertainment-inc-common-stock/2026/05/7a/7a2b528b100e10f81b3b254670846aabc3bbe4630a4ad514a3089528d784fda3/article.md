# Say Hello to Gigolo: Lisa Vanderpump’s Bold New Cocktail Lounge is Now Open Inside The Vanderpump Hotel

*The intimate venue offers signature cocktails, a unique design and a personal tribute inspired by Vanderpump’s beloved Giggy*

Named in honor of Vanderpump’s beloved dog, Gigolo, also affectionately known as Giggy, the lounge carries deep personal meaning reflected throughout its design. Giggy holds a special place in Lisa Vanderpump’s heart, and at the center of Gigolo stands a playful sculptural tribute to him. Standing 62 inches tall, the custom resin statue pays homage to her late Pomeranian companion, who was a constant presence by her side on and off camera and inspired the creation of The Vanderpump Dog Foundation. The Vanderpump Dog Foundation has been instrumental in writing and passing animal cruelty laws, has saved more than 3,000 dogs in California alone and has aided thousands more worldwide.


Designed by Vanderpump Alain, Lisa Vanderpump and her business partner Nick Alain’s design company, Gigolo seats 50 guests and is sultry, sexy and refined. The space reflects Vanderpump Alain’s signature bespoke aesthetic, with deep red oversized chandelier shades, delicate crystal lighting, red roses and brass accents. Black velvet banquettes and a custom Vanderpump Alain black-and-red carpet are a bold, rich and lush design choice as a comfortable and welcoming entrance to the hotel. Stunning black metal lattice wraps the lounge's perimeter, while the central circular bar anchors the space and offers eight bar-top games. Along the back wall, a large liquor display cabinet adds a striking focal point.


“To commemorate my beloved Gigolo, our lovedog who inspired The Vanderpump Dog Foundation, creating a bar in his honor has always been a dream of mine,” said Lisa Vanderpump. “He has meant so much to us and to so many others, and to see a giant statue of him in Las Vegas is a meaningful moment for me. The other meaning of Gigolo is also something we had a lot of fun with – creating a dark, sultry space with a wink and plenty of sexy undertones. I think it will be a welcome respite to guests and visitors, nestled into the bosom of The Vanderpump Hotel.”


In true Vanderpump fashion, a lounge wouldn’t be complete without a playful yet polished cocktail list. At Gigolo, the menu delivers on that promise with clever, conversation-starting drinks featuring inventive names and refined flavor profiles. All with a hint of Gigolo’s sexy undertones, drinks include the **Talk Dirty To Me** (an intimate martini for two, featuring an oversized skewer stuffed with olives as a shared garnish) and the **Slow Burn** (a blackberry-flavored Old Fashioned smoked with hickory). The bar’s signature cocktail is the **Hush Hush** , a drink that requires the customer to spill a secret into a tiny envelope, which is locked into the Vanderpump Vault, to be kept or revealed… that is the question. Vanderpump aficionados can also enjoy Vanderpump classics such as the **Pumptini** , the **Pump & Bump** , the **Giggy Tonic** , and signature **Vanderpump Wines** .


“Lisa has the extraordinary ability to develop lounges that not only attract guests but build a genuine sense of connection,” said Sean McBurney, Chief Commercial Officer and Regional President of Caesars Entertainment. “Every concept she creates becomes the place to be and be seen. Gigolo delivers exactly that: a fun, intimate bar that reflects Lisa’s authenticity, brings her personality to life and reinforces the hotel’s identity as a must-experience destination.”


Occupying the space formerly known as Bound Bar, Gigolo sits next to the hotel’s check-in area and across from the Caesars Sportsbook at The Vanderpump Hotel. Gigolo is open Monday through Thursday from noon to 2 a.m., and Friday through Sunday from 10 a.m. to 2 a.m.

**About Lisa Vanderpump**


Businesswoman, TV personality, author, and philanthropist Lisa Vanderpump hails from London, England. Alongside her husband, Ken Todd, Vanderpump has been a powerhouse in the restaurant and nightclub industry for over 40 years. Their portfolio includes notable Los Angeles venues such as SUR and TomTom, as well as *Vanderpump Cocktail Garden* at Caesars Palace, *Vanderpump à Paris* at Paris Las Vegas, *Pinky’s by Vanderpump* at the iconic Flamingo Las Vegas, as well as *Wolf by Vanderpump* at Caesars Republic Lake Tahoe and *Wolf by Vanderpump Scottsdale* at Caesars Republic. In partnership with Caesars Entertainment. The Vanderpump Hotel is the Vanderpumps’ 40th concept and an iconic addition to Lisa’s portfolio.


Vanderpump rose to fame on Bravo’s *The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills* and is the star and Executive Producer of the successful spinoff *Vanderpump Rules* . Her brand blends glamour with approachability, a quality evident in her television ventures, including *Vanderpump Dogs* on Peacock and *Overserved with Lisa Vanderpump* on E!. Most recently, she stars and executive-produces *Vanderpump Villa* on Hulu, and recently starred in *Gordon Ramsay’s Food Stars* on FOX. She is also the creative force behind a line of lighting and furniture with designer Nick Alain, Vanderpump Alain, a luxury bedding and home accessories line, Vanderpump Home, a pet accessories line ( *[VanderpumpPets.com](http://vanderpumppets.com/)* ), and a collection of signature beverages, including *Vanderpump Wines* and *Vanderpump Vodka* . Lisa’s Floral line, *Vanderpump Blooms,* is currently available at Kroger stores nationally.


**About The Vanderpump Hotel**


The Vanderpump Hotel is a first-of-its-kind destination on the Las Vegas Strip, created by renowned restaurateur and television personality Lisa Vanderpump in partnership with Caesars Entertainment. Blending elevated design with a distinct sense of personality, The Vanderpump Hotel introduces a boutique, lifestyle-driven hospitality experience in the heart of one of the world’s most dynamic destinations. Located on the iconic corner of Las Vegas Boulevard and Flamingo Road, directly across from Caesars Palace, the hotel offers an intimate footprint with 188 guest rooms, including 21 suites. The property features a 40,000-square-foot casino; Gigolo, a bespoke lounge and The Bar at The Vanderpump Hotel, an inviting destination in the center of the casino – all curated to deliver a sophisticated yet playful atmosphere. Culinary and nightlife offerings are anchored by GIADA, the award-winning flagship restaurant from celebrity chef Giada De Laurentiis; Soleia, a 65,000-square-foot rooftop pool and event space with open-air, panoramic views from 11 stories above the Las Vegas skyline; and Drai’s After Hours, located in its original home beneath the hotel. Caesars Sportsbook at The Vanderpump Hotel provides a boutique, lounge-style setting for sports fans and gaming enthusiasts alike.
