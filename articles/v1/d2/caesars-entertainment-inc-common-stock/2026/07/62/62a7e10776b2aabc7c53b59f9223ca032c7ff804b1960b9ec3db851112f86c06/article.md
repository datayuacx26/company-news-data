### Take a Trip to The Strip: Summer Offerings at Caesars Entertainment’s Las Vegas Resorts


07/01/2026

**LAS VEGAS (July 1, 2026)** – From brunch to can’t-miss show deals, Caesars Entertainment’s Las Vegas resorts are giving guests an excuse to visit The Strip all summer long.


**Special hotel offers for America250**


In celebration of America’s 250th anniversary, guests can score special hotel offers for future stays.


**[Red, White and a Free Night](https://www.caesars.com/events/america-250/red-white-and-free-night)**


**[America’s 250th Military and Veteran Offer](https://www.caesars.com/deals/military-discounts)**


**[A Suite Escape](https://www.caesars.com/book/hotel-list?arrivalDate=12/14/2026&departureDate=12/15/2026&dateSearchFormat=exact-dates&propCode=lvm&tripCode=LVSTE)**


**More Beef Wellington, please**


Chef Gordon Ramsay’s iconic dish is now available in two new forms:


**The Wellington Burger at[Gordon Ramsay Burger at Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino](https://www.caesars.com/planet-hollywood/restaurants/gordon-ramsay-burger)**


**Beef Wellington Culinary Class at[Gordon Ramsay Steak at Paris Las Vegas](https://www.caesars.com/paris-las-vegas/restaurants/gordon-ramsay-steak)**


**New pricing on the Inclusive Summer Package**


Guests can now book the Inclusive Summer Package at[Harrah’s Las Vegas](https://www.caesars.com/harrahs-las-vegas) ,[The LINQ Hotel](https://www.caesars.com/linq) and[Flamingo Las Vegas](https://www.caesars.com/flamingo-las-vegas) for $150. The package includes resort fees, taxes, complimentary beverages, two meals per day at select restaurants and two tickets to the[High Roller Observation Wheel](https://www.caesars.com/linq/things-to-do/attractions/high-roller) .


- For more information or to book, guests can visit[caesars.com/las-vegas/hotels/deals/inclusive-summer-package](https://www.caesars.com/las-vegas/hotels/deals/inclusive-summer-package) .


**Caesars Show Package: Two for $99**


Those looking to enjoy an entertainment-filled summer can take advantage of the "Caesars Show Package: Two for $99."


With the limited-time offer, guests can purchase two tickets for $99 from a variety of participating shows across Caesars Entertainment's Las Vegas resorts, with the ability to mix and match.


Participating shows include:


**Horseshoe Las Vegas:** Laugh Factory (excluding the 6:30 p.m. show), Paranormal, Sin City Stones, X Rocks, BattleBots


**Flamingo Las Vegas:** RuPaul’s Drag Race Live!, Piff the Magic Dragon


**Harrah’s Las Vegas:** Donny Osmond, X Country, Colin Cloud Mastermind, MJ Live, Menopause the Musical, Wayne Newton


For more information on the package and to purchase, guests can visit[www.caesars.com/shows](http://www.caesars.com/shows) .


**A** ***berry*** **cold treat for a very hot day**


Visitors can cool off with a scoop of ice cream or milkshake from[Salt & Straw at Flamingo Las Vegas](https://www.caesars.com/flamingo-las-vegas/restaurants/salt-and-straw) or[Paris Las Vegas](https://www.caesars.com/paris-las-vegas/restaurants/salt-and-straw) .


**The Berries Series**


**The Tacolate Milkshake**


- The legendary ice cream taco will soon be available in drinkable form, starting Friday, July 31.


**Brunch at GIADA now served daily at The Vanderpump Hotel**


Guests can now enjoy brunch seven days a week at[GIADA at The Vanderpump Hotel](https://www.caesars.com/vanderpump-hotel/restaurants/giada) from 8 a.m. to 2:30 p.m.


The curated menu features brunch staples but with Giada De Laurentiis' signature Italian flair, including Caprese Frittatas, Filetto & Eggs, Polenta Waffle and a selection of pizzas and pastas.


**Golden Hour at Ramsay’s Kitchen at Harrah’s Las Vegas**


The new Golden Hour at[Ramsay’s Kitchen at Harrah’s Las Vegas](https://www.caesars.com/harrahs-las-vegas/restaurants/ramsays-kitchen-by-gordon-ramsay) allows guests to experience the three-course prix fixe menu for $59.95, from 4 to 5 p.m.


The menu includes choices for both the first and second courses, finishing off with the famous Sticky Toffee Pudding for dessert.
