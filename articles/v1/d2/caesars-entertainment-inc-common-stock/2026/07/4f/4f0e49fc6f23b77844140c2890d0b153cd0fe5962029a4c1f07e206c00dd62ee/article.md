### Caesars Sportsbook Powers New Era of Betting at Westgate SuperBook


07/28/2026


[Download this Press Release (opens in new window) DOCX](https://s202.q4cdn.com/508919455/files/doc_news/2026/Jul/28/Caesars-Entertainment_Westgate-SuperBook_Tech-Launch-Press-Release_Final.docx)


*Expanded wagering options, upgraded mobile capabilities and more than 20 self-service betting kiosks are now live at Las Vegas' iconic SuperBook ahead of football season*


**LAS VEGAS (July 28, 2026)** – Caesars Entertainment, Inc. (NASDAQ: CZR) ("Caesars") and Westgate Las Vegas Resort & Casino today announced that the Westgate SuperBook, powered by Caesars Sportsbook, is now live with new wagering capabilities ahead of football season, bringing together the legendary SuperBook experience and the modern sports betting features today's sports fans expect. Guests can now access expanded betting options, upgraded mobile wagering capabilities and more than 20 self-service wagering kiosks located throughout the SuperBook and Westgate Las Vegas Resort & Casino.


While the technology behind the SuperBook has evolved, it remains the iconic sports viewing and wagering destination that fans have known and loved for decades. Sports fans can now enjoy Same Game Parlays, innovative features like the new Flex Parlay, live in-play wagering, convenient on-property account registration and a broader range of betting opportunities across professional and collegiate sports.


“The SuperBook has been one of the defining sportsbook destinations in Las Vegas for decades,” said Eric Hession, President of Caesars Digital. “As sports fans prepare for football season, we're enhancing its offerings with more ways to wager, more convenience and more flexibility while preserving everything that has made it special. The SuperBook has a tremendous history, and our goal is to build on that legacy while delivering the modern betting experience expected by today's sports bettors.”


Long recognized as one of the preeminent sportsbook destinations in Las Vegas, the SuperBook and its renowned Westgate Las Vegas SuperContest® franchise continue to define one of the most iconic sports betting experiences in the world. Entries are now open for SuperContest, SuperContest Gold, SuperContest Survivor and SuperContest College for the 2026 season.


“Our guests visit the SuperBook for the atmosphere, the viewing experience and the energy that simply can't be replicated,” said Cami Christensen, President and General Manager of Westgate Las Vegas Resort & Casino. “This milestone with Caesars makes it easier than ever to engage with the action while enjoying everything that has made the SuperBook a destination for sports fans.”


In addition to the SuperBook's iconic contests, Caesars Sportsbook is introducing new ways for fans to engage throughout football season, including the Pro Pick’em Contest and Nevada-exclusive $1 Million Parlay Cash Giveaway. Sports fans can learn more and register for SuperContest, SuperContest Gold, SuperContest Survivor and SuperContest College in person or[here](https://www.westgateresorts.com/hotels/nevada/las-vegas/westgate-las-vegas-resort-casino/casino/supercontest/) . Fans can also register for the new Caesars Sportsbook football contests in person at the Westgate SuperBook, powered by Caesars Sportsbook.


These additions arrive ahead of one of the busiest stretches on the sports calendar, positioning the SuperBook with an expanded wagering menu and elevated experience for football season. As part of the upgraded experience, sports fans can now register for a Caesars Sportsbook mobile account on property, creating a seamless connection between in-person and mobile wagering, deposits and withdrawals.


Caesars Sportsbook mobile users will also benefit from Caesars' Universal Digital Wallet functionality, providing seamless access to account balances and funds while traveling across jurisdictions where Caesars Sportsbook is live, including Nevada. The addition of more than 20 Caesars Sportsbook self-service kiosks throughout the resort provides convenient around-the-clock access to sports betting, while in-person wagers placed at the SuperBook via kiosk or over the counter will continue to earn WoW Rewards points and tier credits.


To stay up to date on the Westgate Las Vegas SuperContest® and happenings at the SuperBook, follow @SuperBookNV on X and Instagram throughout the football season.
