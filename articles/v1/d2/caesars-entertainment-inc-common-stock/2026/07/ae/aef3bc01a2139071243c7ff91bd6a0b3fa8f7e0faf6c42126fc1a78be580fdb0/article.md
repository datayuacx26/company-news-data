**Pendulum Entertainment Group Announces Strategic Live Entertainment Partnership with Caesars Entertainment for 2027**


**LAS VEGAS, NV** **–** **(July 29, 2026) — Pendulum Entertainment Group** has officially confirmed a partnership with Caesars Entertainment for a new immersive live entertainment experience set to debut at PH Live, the award-winning venue inside Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino, in 2027.


The upcoming production, ***Arcade Orchestra*** , will bring a high-energy, fully immersive, multi-generational performance to the resort, expanding Planet Hollywood’s commitment to delivering dynamic, world-class entertainment options to the Las Vegas Strip.


"We are thrilled to collaborate with the team at Caesars Entertainment to bring this dynamic, captivating vision to life," said Jonathan DaSilva, Co-Founder and CMO of Pendulum Entertainment Group. "PH Live provides the ideal stage for our long-term residency and offers the perfect environment to deliver a truly cutting-edge, engaging spectacle for our audience."


Additional details regarding performance schedules and ticket availability will be announced at a later date.
