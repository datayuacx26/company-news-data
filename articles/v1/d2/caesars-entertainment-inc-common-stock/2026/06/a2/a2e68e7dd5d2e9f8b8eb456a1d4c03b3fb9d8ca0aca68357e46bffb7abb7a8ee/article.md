# A New Jewel on the Las Vegas Strip: Lisa Vanderpump Celebrates Grand Opening of The Vanderpump Hotel

LAS VEGAS--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jun. 12, 2026-- A dazzling new destination has arrived on the Las Vegas Strip. On Thursday, June 11, television personality, entrepreneur and restaurateur Lisa Vanderpump celebrated the official grand opening of The Vanderpump Hotel, expanding her acclaimed hospitality portfolio with the debut of her first hotel venture.

The evening began with a dramatic and unmistakably Vanderpump arrival. Vanderpump pulled up in a custom Rolls-Royce and made a theatrical entrance along the main casino floor flanked by bellmen. Wearing a shimmering silver dress with purple and green accents and long sheer gloves, Vanderpump was greeted by invited guests, VIPs and media attending the highly anticipated debut of the 188-room boutique hotel.


Clark County Commissioner Tick Segerblom, alongside Caesars Entertainment leadership, commemorated the milestone and presented Vanderpump with a Key to The Strip, recognizing her growing impact in Las Vegas and her evolution from restaurateur to hotelier.


Designed by Lisa Vanderpump and Nick Alain through their design company Vanderpump Alain, The Vanderpump Hotel brings Lisa Vanderpump’s signature “industrial romantic” aesthetic to life through a layered, highly personal approach that combines feminine elegance with a masculine edge. Bespoke Vanderpump Alain furnishings and lighting shape the space, while thoughtful details create a cohesive narrative throughout the hotel.


Playful nods to her British heritage are woven throughout the resort, from a striking mural of Vanderpump strolling through a rainy London street alongside an iconic red telephone booth, to a classic English taxicab installation welcoming guests from Las Vegas Boulevard. A gallery wall of black-and-white photographs showcases cherished moments from Vanderpump’s life, from family and close friends to a mix of famous and furry faces.


Across the casino floor, a dramatic walkway is lined with chandeliers from the original resort, reimagined with new crystals and custom shades. The Vanderpump Hotel recently opened Gigolo, a sexy, sultry lounge named after Lisa Vanderpump’s beloved late Pomeranian “Giggy,” serving as an endearing tribute to her canine companion and complemented by a menu of creative, inventive and delicious cocktails.


“The Vanderpump Hotel has been a true labor of love,” said Lisa Vanderpump. “Like all my passion projects, this one is deeply personal. I think of it as my very own jewel box on the 50-yard line of the Las Vegas Strip – intimate, inviting and filled with carefully curated details that each tell a story. To finally share it with the millions of Las Vegas visitors from around the world is a dream come true. Working with the Caesars Entertainment team has been such a wonderfully collaborative experience, and I feel very fortunate to have such an incredible team and to have opened this beautiful hotel.”


Vanderpump was joined by some of her famous friends for the event, including Sharon Osbourne, Lance Bass and Garcelle Beauvais. Additionally, cast members from "Vanderpump Rules" and “Vanderpump Villa” enjoyed the party. Guests enjoyed a curated selection of bites and cocktails inspired by Vanderpump destinations, complemented by offerings from GIADA, reflecting the hotel’s broader culinary experience. The evening concluded with a 600-drone show above the Las Vegas Strip.


“The grand opening of The Vanderpump Hotel marks an incredible milestone in our partnership with Lisa,” said Sean McBurney, Chief Commercial Officer and Regional President of Caesars Entertainment. “What began as a conversation about a single cocktail lounge has grown into a meaningful and highly successful collaboration, now spanning multiple destinations and culminating in a hotel. Being part of Lisa’s next chapter in hospitality is a very special moment, and we couldn’t be more excited to open the doors of The Vanderpump Hotel and welcome our guests.”


The resort is also home to Drai’s After Hours, Soleia, the elevated rooftop pool, Giada De Laurentiis’ namesake restaurant, GIADA, The Bar at The Vanderpump Hotel, Starbucks and a Caesars Sportsbook.


The Vanderpump Hotel marks the television star and entrepreneur’s first foray into hotels and builds on the success of her expanding Las Vegas footprint. **About Lisa Vanderpump**


Businesswoman, TV personality, author, and philanthropist Lisa Vanderpump hails from London, England. Alongside her husband, Ken Todd, Vanderpump has been a powerhouse in the restaurant and nightclub industry for over 40 years. Their portfolio includes notable Los Angeles venues such as SUR and TomTom, as well as *Vanderpump Cocktail Garden* at Caesars Palace, *Vanderpump à Paris* at Paris Las Vegas, *Pinky’s by Vanderpump* at the iconic Flamingo Las Vegas, as well as *Wolf by Vanderpump* at Caesars Republic Lake Tahoe and *Wolf by Vanderpump Scottsdale* at Caesars Republic. In partnership with Caesars Entertainment.


The Vanderpump Hotel is the Vanderpumps’ 40th concept and an iconic addition to Lisa’s portfolio. Vanderpump rose to fame on Bravo’s *The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills* and is the star and Executive Producer of the successful spinoff *Vanderpump Rules* . Her brand blends glamour with approachability, a quality evident in her television ventures, including *Vanderpump Dogs* on Peacock and *Overserved with Lisa Vanderpump* on E!. Most recently, she stars and executive-produces *Vanderpump Villa* on Hulu and recently starred in *Gordon Ramsay’s Food Stars* on FOX. She is also the creative force behind a line of lighting and furniture with designer Nick Alain, Vanderpump Alain, a luxury bedding and home accessories line, Vanderpump Home, a pet accessories line ( *[VanderpumpPets.com](http://vanderpumppets.com/)* ), and a collection of signature beverages, including *Vanderpump Wines* and *Vanderpump Vodka* . Lisa’s Floral line, *Vanderpump Blooms,* is currently available at Kroger stores nationally.


**About The Vanderpump Hotel**


The Vanderpump Hotel is a first-of-its-kind destination on the Las Vegas Strip, created by renowned restaurateur and television personality Lisa Vanderpump in partnership with Caesars Entertainment. Blending elevated design with a distinct sense of personality, The Vanderpump Hotel introduces a boutique, lifestyle-driven hospitality experience in the heart of one of the world’s most dynamic destinations. Located on the iconic corner of Las Vegas Boulevard and Flamingo Road, directly across from Caesars Palace, the hotel offers an intimate footprint with 188 guest rooms, including 21 suites. The property features a 40,000-square-foot casino; Gigolo, a sexy, sultry lounge and The Bar at The Vanderpump Hotel, an inviting destination in the center of the casino – all curated to deliver a sophisticated yet playful atmosphere. Culinary and nightlife offerings are anchored by GIADA, the award-winning flagship restaurant from celebrity chef Giada De Laurentiis; Soleia, a 65,000-square-foot rooftop pool and event space with open-air, panoramic views from 11 stories above the Las Vegas skyline; and Drai’s After Hours, located in its original home beneath the hotel. Caesars Sportsbook at The Vanderpump Hotel provides a boutique, lounge-style setting for sports fans and gaming enthusiasts alike.
