# Wingstop Inc. to Participate at the Barclays Eat, Sleep, Play — It’s All Discretionary Conference

DALLAS, Nov. 30, 2015 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Wingstop Inc. (“Wingstop” or the “Company”) today announced that it will participate at the Barclays Eat, Sleep, Play – It’s All Discretionary Conference. The conference is being held at Barclays headquarters in New York City.


The Company will hold investor meetings on Tuesday, December 8th and webcast its presentation beginning at 9:45 AM ET. The presentation will be webcast live and later archived on the investor relations section of Wingstop’s corporate website at ir.wingstop.com under the ‘News & Events’ section.


**About Wingstop**


Founded in 1994 and headquartered in Dallas, Texas, Wingstop Inc. (Nasdaq:WING) operates and franchises more than 800 restaurants across the United States, Mexico, Russia, Singapore, the Philippines, Indonesia, and the United Arab Emirates. The Wing Experts menu features classic and boneless wings with 11 bold, distinctive flavors including Original Hot, Cajun, Atomic, Mild, Teriyaki, Lemon Pepper, Hawaiian, Garlic Parmesan, Hickory Smoked BBQ, Louisiana Rub and Mango Habanero. Wingstop’s wings are always cooked to order, sauced and tossed and served with a variety of house-made sides including Wingstop’s world famous hand-cut seasoned fries. Wingstop has experienced 11 consecutive years of positive sales increases, was named a 2014 Best Franchise Deal by QSR magazine, was ranked the 2015 #5 Top Growth Chain by Nation’s Restaurant News and has been voted “Best Wings” in markets across the country.
