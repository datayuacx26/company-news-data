# Smith Micro Expands SafePath® Platform with New SDK and API Deployment Options

**PITTSBURGH, PA, July 27, 2026** – Smith Micro Software, Inc. (NASDAQ: SMSI), a leading provider of software solutions that simplify and enhance the mobile experience, announces the availability of[SafePath® SDKs and APIs](https://www.smithmicro.com/safepath/safepath-sdk-api/) , delivering expanded deployment options that will enable mobile and other service providers to integrate trusted SafePath family safety capabilities directly into their own mobile applications.


SafePath SDKs and APIs give service providers greater flexibility in how they deliver mobile family safety services, allowing them to embed key SafePath family safety features and functionality within their own branded applications, while maintaining a consistent user experience. By leveraging Smith Micro’s SafePath SDK and API options, service providers can deliver location insights, digital safety tools, parental controls, and other family-focused features without requiring their end users to download and manage a separate application.


The new deployment options are the latest expansion of the SafePath platform portfolio, which already includes fully branded applications and customizable deployment models designed to meet the unique needs of service providers worldwide.


“We recognize that every mobile provider has a different strategy for engaging subscribers,” said[Tim Huffmyer](https://www.smithmicro.com/company/management-team/) , Chief Executive Officer of Smith Micro. “The SafePath SDK and API solutions give our partners the flexibility to deliver family safety experiences in the way that best fits their business and customer experience goals. Whether providers want a standalone app or integrated capabilities within an existing application, SafePath now offers another path to bringing valuable family safety services to market.”


By integrating SafePath directly into an operator’s existing digital experience, the SafePath SDK and API deployment can help simplify onboarding, reduce app fragmentation, and increase visibility of family safety features within applications customers already use every day.


SafePath SDKs and APIs provide access to a range of capabilities, including:


- Family location awareness
- Place and arrival notifications
- Screen time and parental controls
- Content filtering and digital safety tools
- Emergency and safety features
- Driving insights and family activity monitoring


Built on the same proven SafePath platform used by leading mobile service providers around the world, the SDK and API configuration is designed to accelerate deployment while allowing operators to maintain control over branding, user experience, and feature implementation.


“With the availability of SafePath SDKs and APIs, we are making our family safety services more accessible and easier to deploy and expanding our total addressable market,” added Huffmyer.”As service providers look for new ways to strengthen customer engagement and deliver value-added services, we’re committed to providing flexible solutions that help them meet evolving market needs.”


SafePath SDKs and APIs are available now for mobile providers and other strategic partners.


**About SafePath®**


The SafePath platform provides comprehensive and easy-to-use tools to protect digital lifestyles and manage connected devices through a single app. As a carrier-grade solution, SafePath empowers wireless service providers and cable operators to bring to market full-featured, on-brand family safety solutions that provide in-demand services such as location, parental controls, screen time management, and driver monitoring to mobile subscribers. SafePath-based solutions activate new revenue opportunities for service providers while helping to increase brand affinity and reduce subscriber churn. Learn more at[smithmicro.com/safepath](https://www.smithmicro.com/safepath/) .
