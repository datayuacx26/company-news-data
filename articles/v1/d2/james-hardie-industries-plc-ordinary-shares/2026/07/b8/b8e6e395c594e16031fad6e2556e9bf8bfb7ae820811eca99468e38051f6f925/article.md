# Get Recognized for Your Work During James Hardie’s Exterior Design Week 2026

Great exterior design doesn’t just look good — it performs in the real world. This year’s theme, “Designed to Perform,” seeks to showcase some of the best examples of exterior design using[Hardie® products](https://www.jameshardie.com/product-catalog/) and the unmatched craftsmanship of our trade pros.


### **Nominations are open from Wednesday, April 15-Friday, May 1.**


Nominate yourself or someone else in one of five categories:


- **James Hardie Home:** A project where exteriors and outdoor living spaces work together to deliver long-lasting beauty and protection. Any project exemplifying exterior design and featuring products from the James Hardie full portfolio — including Hardie®,[TimberTech®](https://www.timbertech.com/) ,[AZEK Exteriors®](https://azekexteriors.com/) , and[StruXure®](https://struxure.com/) — are eligible.
- **Standout Expression:** A home that features[Hardie® Artisan® products](https://www.jameshardie.com/product-catalog/exterior-siding-products/hardie-artisan-siding/) or products from the[Hardie™ Architectural Collection](https://www.jameshardie.com/product-catalog/exterior-siding-products/hardie-architectural-panel-siding/) .
- **Total Transformation:** Exterior renovations where smart material and design choices dramatically improve both appearance and long-term performance. Any re-side project featuring Hardie® products is eligible! Must include before and after photos.
- **Color with Confidence:** A home that celebrates the confident use of our 2026 Color of the Year — Iron Gray from the[Statement Collection®](https://www.jameshardie.com/statement-collection-colors/) .
- **Exterior Design Excellence:** A home that exemplifies performance-led exterior design, balancing aesthetics, durability, climate considerations, and expert execution. Must feature a Complete Exterior™ by James Hardie — including Hardie® siding, trim, and soffit — to be eligible.


**All submissions must be COMPLETED projects to be considered. Landscaping will also factor in our decision-making process.*


**Multiple submissions are permitted!*


### **Enter by May 1! Here’s how:**


Email your submissions to[\[email protected\]](https://www.jameshardie.com/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#e58797848b81a58f848880968d8497818c80cb868a88) . Be sure to include your first/last name and the name of your company (if you are nominating someone else, please include this information as well as their email address and phone number).


In your email, tell us which category (or categories) you’re submitting a nomination for.


Include any relevant photos of the project and tell us which products/colors are featured.


Tell us if the homeowner(s) consent to photography, if your project is chosen to be recognized. We’ll provide a release form for you and them.


Leading up to and during Exterior Design Week 2026, James Hardie will feature various professionals and their projects on our channels — celebrating the great work you do and showcasing what’s possible when design and durability come together with Hardie® products.


We’re excited to see your submissions! Reach out to[\[email protected\]](https://www.jameshardie.com/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#9ffdedfef1fbdff5fef2faecf7feedfbf6fab1fcf0f2) with questions.
