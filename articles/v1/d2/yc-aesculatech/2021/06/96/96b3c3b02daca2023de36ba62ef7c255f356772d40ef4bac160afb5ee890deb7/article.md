# FDA CONFIRMS HUMIDIFEYE IS 510(K) DEVICE

This month, the AesculaTech team met with CDRH at FDA to discuss the Humidifeye device. The FDA confirmed that Humidifeye is a 510(k) device and a predicate was established. Despite pandemic delays, development is on track and we are excited to have a paved path the market in the U.S.
