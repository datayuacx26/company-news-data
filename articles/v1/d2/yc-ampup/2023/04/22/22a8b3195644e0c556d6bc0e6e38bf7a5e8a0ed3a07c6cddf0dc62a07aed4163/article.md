# Legrand’s Newest EV Charger Runs on AmpUp

‍[Legrand®](https://www.legrand.us/) a global specialist and leading single-source supplier of electrical wiring solutions, has expanded into the electrical vehicle charging space with the launch of the[Legrand Networked Level 2 Commercial Electrical Vehicle Charger](https://www.legrand.us/solutions/electric-vehicle-charging?utm_source=press-release&utm_medium=text&utm_campaign=evc-launch) . The commercial EV Charger comes factory-configured with software from leading EV charging provider,[AmpUp](https://hubs.ly/Q01Lxpsb0) , offering an effortless installation and charging management experience.


The commercial EV charger provides facility managers and owners with an all-in-one solution suitable for offices, parking garages, multi-unit dwellings, fleets, and more. Because it comes preconfigured with the AmpUp charging solution, customers can access advanced charging management software right out of the box. This integration offers a significant advantage, allowing for faster commissioning and setup, and providing greater insights and control for facility managers.


The launch of the EV charger showcases the latest initiative in Legrand’s focus on sustainability within the electrical space. The company is committed to investing in research and development to create new and innovative solutions that enable the transition to a more sustainable future.


The product is user-friendly and efficient for electric vehicle charging in commercial settings. Drivers can initiate charging quickly by tapping an RFID card or scanning a QR code on the unit using the AmpUp EV driver app.


“AmpUp is proud to partner with major electrical supplier Legrand to meet the EV charging needs of the future” said Tom Sun, CEO and Cofounder of AmpUp. “Legrand has a rich history in global electrical sales, so we’re excited to partner on EV charging as an addition to their sustainable energy solutions.”


“Legrand’s goal is to provide electrical and digital infrastructure that improves people’s lives where they work, meet and live. We do this with industry-leading solutions that are both simple and sustainable,” said Ravi Ramanathan, President of Electrical Wiring Systems – Legrand North and Central America. “The new Legrand EV charger is a perfect example of our commitment to delivering innovative solutions that not only meet the needs of our customers but also contribute to a cleaner and more sustainable future. Our partnership with AmpUp was an easy choice, as two leaders in our industries, we are excited to bring a differentiated and comprehensive charging solution.”


The integrated Legrand and AmpUp solution is now available to United States customers and is eligible for various state and utility incentive programs.
