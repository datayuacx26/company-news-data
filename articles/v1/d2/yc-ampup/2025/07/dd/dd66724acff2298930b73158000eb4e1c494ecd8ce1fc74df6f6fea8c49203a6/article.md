# AmpUp EV Cloud Load Management Now UL-Certified

At AmpUp, we're constantly pushing the boundaries to ensure your EV charging infrastructure is not just powerful, but also incredibly safe, reliable, and future-proof.


That's why we're thrilled to share a major achievement: AmpUp's Energy Management System has officially received UL certification for cloud-based load management!


This isn't just a technical badge; it's a benefit for every AmpUp customer.


## Why UL Certification is a Game-Changer for You


In the rapidly evolving EV charging landscape, safety and performance are paramount. UL certification indicates that our cloud-based load management technology has undergone rigorous testing and validation to meet the highest safety and operational standards for critical electrical infrastructure in both the United States and Canada.


AmpUp now stands among an elite group of fewer than 10 companies nationwide to achieve this critical certification. For you, this translates directly into confidence in your charging operations.


## Compliance is No Longer Optional – And We've Got You Covered


The industry is maturing, and so are its regulations. The National Electric Code (NEC) 2023 now mandates that EV chargers with load management capabilities *must* carry UL certification. This isn't a suggestion; it's a mandatory standard in[17 of the 50 US states](https://citel.us/en/where-is-the-national-electrical-code-in-effect-as-of-2025#:~:text=As%20of%20March%201%2C%202025,Council%20on%20August%2012%2C%202022) , with more states expected to follow soon, like California, Connecticut, and North Carolina.


What does this mean for your business?


- AmpUp is the Safest Solution: If you operate in one of the 17 (and growing) states where NEC 2023 is standard, AmpUp's Pro and Multi plans, featuring our UL-certified cloud-based load management, ensure your deployments are fully compliant. No headaches, no delays, just confidence.
- Smoother Installations: Electrical contractors and installers can now recommend and deploy AmpUp solutions with complete assurance. Our UL certification validates their professional judgment and protects them from liability concerns, ensuring a more efficient and worry-free installation process.
- Stronger Partnerships & Reduced Risk: For our channel partners and distributors, this certification provides a distinct competitive edge. They can offer you a solution that is not only robust but also guaranteed to meet regulatory requirements, translating to reduced project risk and a more reliable investment for you.
