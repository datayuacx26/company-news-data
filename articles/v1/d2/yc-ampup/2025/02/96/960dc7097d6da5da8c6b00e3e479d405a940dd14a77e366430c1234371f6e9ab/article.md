# AmpUp & Tritium Upgrade EV Fast Chargers

SANTA CLARA, CALIFORNIA — AmpUp, a leading provider of electric vehicle (EV) charging solutions, has announced a collaboration with Tritium, a global leader in DC fast chargers, to offer an efficient upgrade program for fast-charging stations across North America.


This partnership simplifies the process for Tritium RT50 owners to integrate their charging stations into the AmpUp network, offering enhanced features to improve EV charging infrastructure and customer experiences.


## Seamless Integration of Tritium RT50 Chargers into AmpUp Network


The program will be delivered in two phases to help Tritium RT50 owners transition smoothly to the AmpUp charging network:


- **Phase One** : This phase ensures a hassle-free integration of existing Tritium stations into the AmpUp network, enabling site hosts to provide their Tritium charger’s serial number and written approval for transfer. AmpUp will facilitate the entire integration process free of charge, offering site hosts the option to choose preferred cloud services.
- **Phase Two** : In this phase, Tritium RT50 owners have the opportunity to upgrade their chargers to a brand-new 50kW DC fast charger with several advanced features, such as dual charging capabilities, universal compatibility, and scalable power. These chargers can be easily installed on existing RT50 pads, minimizing downtime and providing a more reliable charging experience.


## **Enhancing EV Charging Capabilities for the Future**


By upgrading to a new 50kW DC fast charger, Tritium RT50 owners will benefit from enhanced charging capabilities, including:


- **Dual Charging** : Charge two vehicles simultaneously, doubling charging capacity.
- **Universal Compatibility** : Support for CCS and NACS cords, enabling compatibility with virtually any **electric vehicle** on the market.
- **Scalable Power** : Upgradable to 75kW, providing future-proofed performance as EV demand increases.
- **Sustainability** : AmpUp ensures the responsible recycling of the old RT50 chargers, minimizing environmental impact.


“This upgrade program underscores AmpUp’s commitment to advancing EV infrastructure and streamlining the transition to cleaner, more sustainable transportation,” said[Tom Sun](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ts-ampup/) , CEO and co-founder of AmpUp. “Our collaboration with Tritium allows site hosts to modernize their charging stations with minimal disruption while unlocking the full potential of their infrastructure.


### **About AmpUp**


AmpUp is a leading provider of EV charging software solutions that simplify the deployment and management of electric vehicle charging stations. Trusted by companies across industries, AmpUp’s platform helps businesses and property owners optimize the performance of their charging networks. With over 1,500 charging stations deployed in over 75 North American markets, AmpUp is at the forefront of EV charging technology.


### **About Tritium**


Tritium is a global leader in the development and manufacturing of DC fast chargers for electric vehicles. Founded in 2001, Tritium designs innovative, reliable, and user-friendly chargers that support the transition to clean energy. Tritium’s compact and robust chargers are designed to thrive in harsh conditions while ensuring maximum uptime and efficiency for charging networks.
