# AmpUp & Hubject Expand EV Charging Access

SAN FRANCISCO, CA — AmpUp, a leading provider of EV charging software solutions, today announced a new strategic partnership with Hubject, a global leader in EV charging interoperability. This collaboration aims to expand and streamline electric vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure across North America, making it more accessible for both site hosts and EV drivers.


Through this partnership, AmpUp will integrate Hubject’s Intercharge Network with its innovative software platform, enabling site hosts to offer EV drivers access to a broader, more seamless network of charging stations. The collaboration will ensure interoperability across different charging networks, providing an optimized and simplified experience for all parties involved.


“Charger availability and ease of use are vital for making the EV transition accessible to all,” said[Tom Sun](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ts-ampup/) , CEO and co-founder of AmpUp. “By partnering with Hubject, we are committed to enhancing the charging experience for our customers while expanding charging opportunities in their communities.”


## Key Benefits of the Partnership


- **Broader Charging Access:** Hubject’s extensive global network will enable AmpUp’s customers to integrate with millions of chargers, offering drivers more options for convenient charging.
- **Seamless Interoperability:** The integration of Hubject’s solutions with AmpUp’s platform will ensure that all EV drivers, regardless of their network affiliation, can access and use the charging stations with ease.
- **Advanced Station Management:** AmpUp’s software will provide real-time monitoring, smart load management, and detailed reporting to help site hosts maximize efficiency and revenue from their charging stations.


## AmpUp Advantage


AmpUp’s platform is designed to support the growth and management of EV charging infrastructure, offering key features such as:


- **Smart Load Management:** Optimize energy usage to reduce operational costs and increase charging station throughput.
- **Real-Time Monitoring:** Gain full visibility into station performance, track usage, and ensure chargers are always operational.
- **Revenue Optimization:** Drive increased revenue with tools like dynamic pricing, idle fees, and detailed performance analytics.


“We are excited about the potential of this partnership with Hubject to expand the reach of the Intercharge Network,” said a spokesperson from AmpUp. “This collaboration represents a major step forward in simplifying the EV charging ecosystem, making it easier for operators and drivers to connect and interact with EV infrastructure.”
