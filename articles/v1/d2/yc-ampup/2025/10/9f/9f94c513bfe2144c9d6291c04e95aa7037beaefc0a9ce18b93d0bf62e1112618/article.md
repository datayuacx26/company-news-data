## Why EPRI's Vetted Product List Matters


For those working to expand electric vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure, the[EPRI Vetted Product List](https://www.epri.com/vpl) is the gold standard.


Founded in 1972, EPRI is the world's preeminent independent, non-profit energy research and development organization. Their trusted experts collaborate with over 450 companies in 45 countries, driving innovation to ensure the public has access to clean, safe, reliable, and affordable electricity.


EPRI’s VPL is a comprehensive, consolidated resource for utilities, state agencies, and industry stakeholders to vet products and equipment for EV charging deployment. Inclusion on this list means a product has been rigorously evaluated against criteria developed through industry consensus, utility input, and government agency requirements.


Inclusion on the EPRI VPL is critical because it:


- Simplifies Utility and Rebate Approval: Many[utility and state incentive programs](https://www.ampup.io/resources/ev-charger-rebates-and-incentives) , which provide millions of dollars in funding for EV infrastructure, require products to be EPRI-listed for eligibility. AmpUp's presence on the VPL helps our customers more easily access these vital funds and accelerate their projects.
- Ensures Reliability and Quality: The rigorous vetting process verifies that the product meets the highest standards for performance, interoperability, and long-term sustainability.
- Streamlines Deployment: By using a pre-vetted solution, charging network owners, fleets, and municipalities can save significant time and resources that would otherwise be spent navigating complex and often slow individual utility approval processes.
