# Level 2 EV Chargers: Smart Choice for Business

As EV adoption continues to grow, businesses are looking for ways to attract EV drivers and prepare for the future. One smart investment? Level 2 EV chargers. These chargers strike the perfect balance between cost, functionality, and availability of incentives, making them a great choice for workplaces, retail locations, multifamily residences, and fleets. Here’s why they’re worth considering right now.


## What is a Level 2 EV Charger?


Level 2 chargers are the most popular choice for business properties because they’re faster than standard Level 1 chargers but more affordable than DC fast chargers. They use a 240-volt power source, similar to a home dryer or oven, and can fully charge most EVs within 4-8 hours. This makes them ideal for places where people park for extended periods, such as:


- **Workplaces:** Employees can conveniently charge during work hours.
- **Retail Locations:** Shoppers can top up while they shop, encouraging longer visits.
- **Multifamily Residences:** Tenants can charge overnight, making properties more attractive to EV owners.
- **Fleets:** Vehicles can be charged during off-hours, reducing downtime.


## NEVI Funding Confusion: Tax Credits Are Unaffected for Now


The[recent suspension](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/dot-suspends-ev-electric-vehicle-charging-network/739521/) of the National Electric Vehicle Infrastructure (NEVI) funding has caused confusion for states and businesses across the US. The US Department of Transportation halted the $5 billion NEVI program, which was building a nationwide network of DC fast chargers. However, this suspension *does not impact Level 2 chargers* , making them an excellent business investment.


While NEVI funding for DC fast chargers is on hold, federal tax credits remain intact. According to both the[Department of Energy](http://afdc.energy.gov/laws/ev-tax-credits#:~:text=Businesses%20%26%20Fleets&text=If%20you%20purchase%20EV%20charging,each%20single%20item%20of%20property) and the[IRS](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/alternative-fuel-vehicle-refueling-property-credit) websites, businesses can still claim a tax credit of[up to 30%](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p6028.pdf) , subject to meeting requirements.


This stability in incentives makes Level 2 chargers a reliable investment, especially during this period of uncertainty for DC fast chargers.


## State Incentives: A Boost for Level 2 Charger Installation


Many states offer additional incentives that make Level 2 chargers an even more attractive investment. These state-level incentives significantly reduce upfront costs and accelerate the return on investment. To explore what’s available in your state, the US Department of Energy offers a[searchable database of incentives](https://afdc.energy.gov/laws/search#/?technology=ELEC&law_type=INC&law_type=STATEINC&law_type=UPINC) . It’s an excellent resource for finding state-specific opportunities that fit your business needs. For example:


- **Maryland:** The[EVSE rebate program](https://energy.maryland.gov/transportation/pages/incentives_evserebate.aspx) covers up to 50% of the cost of equipment and installation.
- **New York:** The[Charge Ready NY](https://www.nyserda.ny.gov/All-Programs/Charge-Ready-NY) program offers up to $4,000 rebates per Level 2 charging port.


It’s important to check, as each state’s incentive program and enrollment period differ. Plus, some states don’t offer EV charger incentives – but there’s a good chance your utility might.


## Utility Incentives: Further Reducing Costs


Many utilities also offer rebates and incentives for businesses to install Level 2 chargers. These utility incentives can be combined with federal and state programs, making the investment even more affordable for companies.


Here are a few examples:


- **Austin Energy (Texas):** Its[Workplace Charging Program](https://austinenergy.com/green-power/plug-in-austin/workplace-charging) offers up to $3,000 rebates per charging station installed.
- **Eversource (Connecticut):** It provides[business EV charging rebates](https://www.eversource.com/content/business/save-money-energy/clean-energy-options/electric-vehicles/business-ev-charging-rebates) to encourage installing EV chargers and accepts Level 2 charger applications.


Check your utility’s website to see what incentives or rebates it offers.


## The Rise of EV-Ready Requirements in Multifamily Housing


As EV adoption rises, multifamily properties increasingly consider EV charging a must-have amenity. Five states (New York, California, Washington, Oregon, and Massachusetts) now require **EV-ready parking spots** and **EV charging ports** in new multifamily developments. Installing Level 2 chargers in multifamily residences offers several benefits:


- **Attracting tenants:** EV owners actively seek properties with charging options, which can reduce vacancy rates and increase property value.
- **Future-proofing:** Meeting EV-ready requirements now prepares properties for the increasing demand for charging infrastructure.
- **Competitive advantage:** Offering EV charging sets properties apart from competitors who don’t have this amenity.


As more states adopt similar requirements, installing Level 2 chargers becomes a strategic move to stay ahead of regulatory trends and meet tenant expectations.
