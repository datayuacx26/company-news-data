# California EV Charging Incentives for Multifamily Operators

If you own or operate apartments in California, now’s the time to plug into state funding for EV charging. The[Communities in Charge project](https://thecommunitiesincharge.org/eligibility/) , backed by the[California Energy Commission](https://www.energy.ca.gov/) ’s Clean Transportation Program, recently opened a new funding wave worth up to $56.5 million for multifamily housing and nearby spots where tenants can charge.


## How California’s Communities in Charge EV Charging Program Works


The Communities in Charge project is run by[CALSTART](https://calstart.org/) with support from[GRID Alternatives](https://gridalternatives.org/) and[Tetra Tech](https://www.tetratech.com/) . CALSTART already manages more than $1 billion in national clean-transportation incentives, so this program has serious weight behind it.


- **Who can apply?** California property owners or stakeholders ready to install Level 2 chargers at multifamily or adjacent tenant-accessible sites.
- **When?** Applications opened in early August and close January 9, 2026, at 5 p.m. PT.
- **What’s covered?** Up to $8,500 per Level 2 charging port for the base incentive, with an additional $3,500 for qualifying customers.
- **Equity first.** Projects that serve disadvantaged, low-income, and Tribal areas get priority through an equity-based scoring system.


## What Happens After You Submit Your EV Charger Application?


Applications flow through the Incentive Processing Center and are reviewed as they arrive:


- Readiness Tier 1 projects, or those that are basically ready to break ground tomorrow, receive an immediate *Notice of Final Award* .


- Readiness Tier 2 projects get a *Notice of Conditional Award* and 90 days to submit additional paperwork before securing final approval.
