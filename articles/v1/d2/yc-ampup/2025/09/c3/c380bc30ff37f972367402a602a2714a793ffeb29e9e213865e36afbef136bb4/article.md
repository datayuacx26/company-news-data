While EVs grab the headlines, charging infrastructure is the backbone of this transition. That’s where AmpUp comes in. Our network has powered over **110 million electric miles** , saving more than **50 million pounds of CO₂,** and we’re just getting started.

From take-home fleet reimbursement to public charging deployments, our mission is to make EV charging accessible and reliable for everyone.

## **More About World EV Day**


‍ **‍**[World EV Day](https://www.worldevday.org/) , which takes place on September 9 each year, is a global event that spotlights EVs. It's a day for everyone, from EV enthusiasts and drivers to businesses and policymakers, to celebrate the transition to sustainable electric transport. The initiative was launched in 2020 by[ABB](https://global.abb/group/en) and[Green.TV](http://green.tv/) to promote the adoption of EVs worldwide and raise awareness of the role EVs play in reducing carbon emissions.


### ‍ **Where is World EV Day happening?**


**‍** There’s no single location for World EV Day – it’s a global movement. People and organizations worldwide participate in local events, online campaigns, and educational initiatives. This year, you’ll find meet-ups in North America, Europe, South America, and Australia, as well as social media buzz and conversations about the future of mobility. Those in the EV space contribute by raising awareness, sharing their stories, and offering incentives to encourage EV adoption.

Showcasing your role in the clean transportation revolution through marketing, social media, or public commitments strengthens your brand’s connection to sustainability, and participating in events like World EV Day highlights those efforts.


World EV Day is about more than just recognizing electric vehicles – it's about inspiring action, educating communities, and accelerating the global shift toward sustainable transportation.
