# AmpUp Now Powers Shell Recharge Stations

Shell Recharge has announced that it will discontinue support for third-party charging stations after April 20, 2024. This means the ‘Shell Sky’ platform will be deactivated, leaving owners of commercial EV chargers searching for a solution.


Fortunately, AmpUp is already in the process of redirecting hundreds of Shell Recharge commercial EV stations and is committed to providing a seamless transition to keep your Level 2 and Level 3 chargers operational and efficient. As a trusted leader powering thousands of stations across North America, AmpUp is your go-to partner for reliable EV charging solutions.


Read on to learn more about this transition.
