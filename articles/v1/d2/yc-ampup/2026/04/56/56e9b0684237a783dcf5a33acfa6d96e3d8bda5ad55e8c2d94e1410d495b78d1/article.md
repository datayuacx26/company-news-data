# Earth Day 2026: How AmpUp is Powering a Greener Future

At AmpUp, sustainability isn't a seasonal message. It's the foundation of everything our EV charging platform is built to do. This Earth Day 2026, we're reflecting on what our network has accomplished and what every EV charging session adds up to, because a greener future is not built in a single day. It's built one electric mile at a time.


## Our Earth Day 2026 Impact at a Glance


To date, the AmpUp EV charging platform has:


- Processed more than **45,600 MWh** of electricity across our network
- Enabled over **159 million electric miles** driven on clean power instead of gasoline
- Avoided **73 million pounds** of CO2 emissions
- Deployed over **10,000 charging ports** nationwide across commercial, private, and public sites


Every kilowatt-hour we deliver is a small but real step toward the sustainability goals that Earth Day was created to champion.


## How EV Charging Drives Real Sustainability Gains


Numbers this large can be hard to picture. Seventy-three million pounds of CO2 stays abstract until you ask what it would take to put that much carbon into the atmosphere in the first place.


It would take burning 3.7 million gallons of gasoline. It would take burning 36.8 million pounds of coal. It's the yearly exhaust of 7,700 gas-powered cars, all of them running, all of them queuing at the pump, all of them idling in traffic for twelve straight months. Those are the emissions that didn't happen, because drivers plugged into AmpUp's EV charging network instead.


Now flip the frame. Think about what the Earth has to do to pull 73 million pounds of CO2 back out of the sky. It's the quiet, years-long work of 33,000 acres of U.S. forest, breathing for a full year. It's what 551,000 urban tree seedlings would draw down over a decade of growth. It's the carbon equivalent of diverting more than 11,700 tons of waste from landfills and back into circulation through recycling.


All equivalencies above are calculated using the[EPA's Greenhouse Gas Equivalencies Calculator](https://www.epa.gov/energy/greenhouse-gas-equivalencies-calculator) , the standard reference for translating emissions data into real-world comparisons.


Every one of those comparisons points to the same truth. Climate progress is cumulative. Sustainability isn't a single heroic act. It's the compound effect of millions of small, daily decisions to plug in instead of fill up, and an EV charging platform exists to make every one of those decisions easier.


## Why EV Charging Matters for the Planet


Transportation is one of the largest sources of greenhouse gas emissions in the United States. Replacing gasoline miles with electric miles is one of the most direct levers individuals, businesses, and communities have to reduce their carbon footprint. But the lever only works if EV charging is reliable, accessible, and easy to deploy at scale.


That's the role of a modern EV charging platform. It turns isolated chargers into a connected network. It gives property owners the tools to launch and manage charging without hassle. It gives drivers the confidence that a charger will be there when they need one. Every one of those operational details becomes a sustainability decision, because a charger that works is a charger that keeps gasoline in the ground.


## Scaling Sustainability Across America


With more than 10,000 ports deployed and growing, AmpUp's EV charging network now spans commercial properties, multifamily communities, workplaces, hospitality venues, fleet depots, and public sites across the country. Each new port extends the reach of clean transportation. Each new site host is someone choosing to be part of the solution.


The real measure of our impact isn't the port count alone. It's the cumulative kilowatt-hours flowing through our platform, the gasoline miles never driven, the pounds of CO2 never released into the air we share. Every mile, every megawatt-hour, every port compounds into a greener future for the communities we serve and the planet we share.
