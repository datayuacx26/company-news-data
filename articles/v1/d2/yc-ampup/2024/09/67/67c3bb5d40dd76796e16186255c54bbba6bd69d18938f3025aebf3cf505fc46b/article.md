# AmpUp & EVSE’s Urban EV Charging Vision

In a recent episode of *The EVs for Everyone Podcast* ,[Mike DiNucci](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mikedinucci/) , CRO of AmpUp, and[Dean Spacht](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dean-spacht-mba-pmp-cssgb-5016b150/) , Executive Director at[EVSE](https://evsellc.com/) , shared exciting insights into their collaborative effort to transform urban EV charging. The partnership has led to the installation of cutting-edge Level 2 EV chargers on streetlight poles in Los Angeles and beyond, addressing critical challenges like limited parking and curbside access. This episode explores how AmpUp’s intuitive software and EVSE’s retractable cable technology are setting a new standard for cities aiming to accelerate EV adoption. Listen to the the full episode below to learn how their innovative approach is paving the way for a more accessible, electrified future.
