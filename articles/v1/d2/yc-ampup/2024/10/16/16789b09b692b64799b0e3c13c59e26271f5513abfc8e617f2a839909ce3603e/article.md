# How to Start Your Own EV Charging Business

With nearly 14 million new electric cars hitting the road in 2023, the demand for electric vehicle (EV) charging stations is growing faster than ever. If you've considered installing EV chargers at your business, now is the time to act. This guide will help you understand everything from choosing the best business model to securing funding so you can tap into this booming market.


## **Why Start an EV Charging Business?**


Electric vehicle sales were up 35% in 2023 alone and continue to break records. With more than 40 million EVs now on the road worldwide, there's an increasing need for accessible charging infrastructure. This represents a fantastic business opportunity for property owners, as EV chargers are not yet as ubiquitous as gas stations, leaving plenty of room for growth. AmpUp’s guide will walk you through critical steps to get your charging station off the ground.


## ‍ **Choosing the Best Business Model**


**‍** There are several ways to structure your EV charging business, depending on your resources and goals:


- ‍ **Owner-Operator Model:** You own and operate the stations, giving you maximum control and a profits. This requires significant investment but is ideal if you want a long-term asset.
- ‍ **Franchise Model:** Partner with an established charging network for a lower-risk entry. You’ll benefit from a proven system and brand support.
- ‍ **Host Model:** Rent space on your property for EV chargers while a third-party operator handles installation and maintenance. This is an excellent option for extra income without the operational hassle. Collaborate with government agencies to secure funding, especially in underserved areas. This option is perfect if you’re looking to align your business with public sustainability goals.
- ‍ **Subscription or Membership-Based Model:** Offer drivers a subscription plan for unlimited charging or discounted rates. This model provides a steady stream of recurring revenue and builds customer loyalty. **‍**
- ‍ **Charging Network Model:** Ideal for those who own multiple properties, this model focuses on setting up charging stations at different locations maximizing revenue potential through flexible pricing and market density. Success depends on efficient station management, strategic location planning, and ensuring reliable charging services. AmpUp can help with these factors.


## **Understanding EV Charger Levels**


EV chargers come in three primary levels, each with different capabilities:


- **Level 1:** This device connects to a standard 120V outlet and charges about 3 miles per hour. While easy to install, it is too slow for most commercial uses.
- ‍ **Level 2:** This charger requires a 240V outlet and can charge up to 30 miles per hour. It requires professional installation and comes with dedicated software for faster charging and automated billing. It's perfect for workplaces, retail spots, or apartment buildings where people park for extended periods.
- ‍ **Level 3 (DC Fast Chargers):** These chargers use direct current and provide the fastest charge—up to 124 miles per hour. They require advanced hardware and electrical infrastructure. These chargers are ideal for high-traffic areas like amenities off highways and can fully charge a vehicle in as little as 20 minutes.


## ‍ **EV Connector Types**


‍ **‍** When selecting chargers, consider which connector types to support:


- **SAE J1772 (J Plug)** : Standard for Level 1 and Level 2 charging in North America.
- **CCS (Combined Charging System)** : Popular for DC fast charging in North America and Europe.
- **CHAdeMO** : Nearly obsolete in the US, mostly used in Japan and on select older models such as the Nissan Leaf.
- **NACS (North American Charging Standard)** : Originally for Tesla, it is now increasingly being adopted by other automakers like Ford and GM.

## Maximizing ROI for Your EV Chargers


‍ **Adding EV chargers can attract customers, increase property value, and even open new revenue streams. To maximize your return on investment (ROI), match your charger level to customer needs:**


- **High Turnover Locations:** Retail stores and restaurants benefit from Level 3 chargers for quick recharges.
- **Longer Stays:** Hotels, workplaces, and apartment complexes are best suited for Level 2 chargers to serve customers over more extended periods.

In the US, you’ll need to comply with the National Electric Code (NEC) Article 625, which covers safety standards for EV chargers. AmpUp can help ensure your setup meets all national and local requirements, including California’s CTEP standards. **‍**


## **Important Industry Terms to Know**


- ‍ **Open Automated Demand Response (OpenADR):** Helps balance energy demand during peak periods by facilitating communication between operators, utilities, and energy management systems. **‍**
- **Open Charge Point Protocol (OCPP):** Enables communication between chargers and management systems, improving interoperability. It's hardware agnostic, enabling EV drivers to move between networks without downloading multiple apps or creating new user accounts. AmpUp software is OCPP-compliant.
- ‍ **ISO 15118:** A global standard for vehicle-to-charger communication, enabling seamless billing and plug-and-charge capabilities.
- ‍ **NEMA 4 Enclosure:** Protects EV chargers from outdoor elements, ensuring they withstand dust, dirt, and water. Its NEMA 4X rating makes it particularly suitable for harsh environments **.**
