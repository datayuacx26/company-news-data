# AmpUp’s Level 2 Charger Rebates in Connecticut

## Eligibility Requirements


1. Must be located in CT, Eversource[electric territory](https://www.eversource.com/content/residential/services/communities-we-serve) .
2. Available for new installations and rip and replace of existing faulty chargers with AmpUp-enabled chargers. Including any inoperational Enel X Way, EVBox and Juicebar chargers!
3. Applies to Public, Fleet, Commercial & Multi-Unit Dwelling (MUD) properties.
4. Chargers must be operational by March 31, 2025.
5. Chargers are required to participate in[AmpUp Adapt’s grid services initiatives](https://ampup.io/en-us/adapt) .


## Benefits


1. New Installations:[Eversource Make-Ready Funding](https://www.eversource.com/content/business/save-money-energy/clean-energy-options/electric-vehicles/business-ev-charging-rebates) and 3 Years of AmpUp Pro Software.*
2. Rip and Replace: Free Hardware and 3 Years of AmpUp Pro Software at no cost.
3. Site hosts are only responsible for the installation/commissioning cost.
4. Maximum rebate amount of $25,000 per location.


## Incentive Timeline


1. Site hosts pay for hardware initially and a rebate check will be issued within 60 days of the site becoming operational.
2. The site host or installer simply needs to notify AmpUp by emailinggnu@ampup.io once the site is operational. *Please ensure the AmpUp software license is activated on all chargers.*


* This program works alongside Eversource’s make-ready funding for new installs by providing free AmpUp software to site hosts.
