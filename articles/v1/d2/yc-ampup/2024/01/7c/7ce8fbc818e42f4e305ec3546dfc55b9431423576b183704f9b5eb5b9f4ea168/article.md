# 4 Places That Need More EV Charging in 2024

Electric Vehicles (EVs) are not just vehicles; they're a choice - a symbol of a more thoughtful and greener way of moving through the world.


Across the nation, we're witnessing a real change - a shift that exceeds the limitations of traditional work environments and delves into the very essence of our daily journeys.


[The 2023 EV Driver Survey by Plug In America](https://pluginamerica.org/survey/2023-ev-driver-survey/) paints a vivid picture of this new landscape. Gathered feedback reveals a striking 90% satisfaction rate among EV owners, with the majority indicating they are "likely" or "very likely" to choose an EV for their next vehicle purchase.


Moreover, the survey brings to light the escalating need for EV charging infrastructure to cater to the requirements of present and future EV drivers. The survey highlights the increasing demand for electric vehicles (EVs) and the extensive infrastructure for charging them. In this blog, we explore four unexpected places where EV charging is not just a luxury, but a necessity to address the needs and preferences of an EV driver.


## 1. Workplace Charging: The Future of Employee Benefits


- Offices – The modern workplace is evolving, and so are its amenities. Offering EV charging at offices is not just an employee perk; it's a statement of a company's commitment to sustainable practices.[Workplace EV charging](https://www.ampup.io/use-cases/workplace-ev-charging-solutions) can be a decisive factor for environmentally conscious employees when choosing their employer.


- Hospitals and Medical Facilities – In the healthcare sector, the adoption of EV charging stations can significantly benefit both healthcare professionals and visitors. With long shifts, having access to EV charging at hospitals ensures that medical staff can rely on their vehicles being charged and ready for their commute home. Additionally, patients and visitors appreciate the convenience of charging their EVs during their hospital visits.
- Brick-and-Mortar Stores – Retail locations increasingly recognize the value of offering EV charging solutions. It's not just about providing a service but also about attracting a growing demographic of EV drivers. Retail EV charging solutions can increase dwell time, encouraging shoppers to spend more time and money in stores while their vehicles charge.


## 2. Municipalities: Schools and Public Spaces


- Educational Campuses – High schools and colleges are ideal locations for EV charging stations. They cater to staff, students, and visitors who are increasingly conscious of their carbon footprint. Implementing EV charging infrastructure in educational institutions is a step towards promoting sustainable transportation among the younger generation.
- Public Spaces – Municipalities, including public parks, libraries, and community centers, play a crucial role in the nationwide EV charging expansion strategies. By integrating EV charging facilities, these public spaces can support the growing number of EV users and encourage eco-friendly transportation within the community. These locations can also serve a town fleet transitioning to electric, such as maintenance and utility vehicles.


## 3. Hospitality Industry: Welcoming EV Drivers


- Airbnb and Hotels –[EV Charging for Hospitality](https://www.ampup.io/use-cases/ev-charging-management-for-hotels) , including Airbnb hosts and hotels, is uniquely positioned to offer EV charging amenities. Providing EV charging facilities can enhance guest satisfaction and position these establishments as preferred choices for the growing number of EV-driving travelers. EV drivers often prefer hotels with EV charging for a road trip since an overnight charge can top off the battery before they hit the road again.

By incorporating EV charging facilities, these buildings are not just accommodating a trend but are actively participating in shaping a sustainable future for their residents.
