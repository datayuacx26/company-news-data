## Welcome to the Pinnacle of Tech Innovation: CES in Review


CES, the Consumer Electronics Show, has been a cornerstone of technological advancement since its inception in 1967 in New York City. Starting with 250 exhibitors and 17,500 attendees, it has grown to over 115,00 visitors, becoming a global hub for innovators and industry leaders.


**Key Highlights:** ‍


- **Global Reach:** CES is not just a trade show; it's a global phenomenon, attracting tens of thousands worldwide.
- **Diverse Exhibitors:** From its humble beginnings, CES now hosts thousands of exhibitors, showcasing everything from 3D Printing to AI and EVs.
- **Annual Event:** CES continues to be an eagerly anticipated annual event, marking its value in the tech calendar. *Source:*[Featured Exhibitors CES 2024](https://www.ces.tech/exhibits/featured-exhibitors.aspx)
