# Hotel EV Charging: Attract More Guests

In the hospitality industry, offering EV charging can set your hotel apart, attracting eco-conscious travelers and enhancing guest satisfaction. As electric vehicles become more popular, convenient charging options are increasingly a deciding factor for guests. By investing in EV charging, you're meeting current needs and positioning your hotel as a forward-thinking choice for the future.


## **EV Charging as a Competitive Advantage**


**‍** Not all hotels offer EV charging yet, so installing chargers now can give you an edge over competitors. By positioning your hotel as an EV-friendly destination, you increase its appeal to a demographic that is only going to grow. Guests often view charging stations as essential amenities, and by offering this convenience, you make your hotel stand out as a modern, forward-thinking choice.


Let's dive into the benefits of offering EV charging at your hotels and how you can get started.


### **Benefits of Adding EV Charging Stations to Hotels**


1. **Increased Bookings from EV Owners** by offering EV charging, you open your doors to a large and[growing market of EV drivers](https://ampup.io/blog/us-ev-sales-are-reaching-record-highs-yes-really) . For some EV owners, the availability of charging is essential to choosing where they stay. With chargers on-site, your hotel becomes an easy choice for travelers who would otherwise worry about finding a place to charge.
2. **Enhanced Guest Experience Convenience** is key in hospitality, and EV chargers can play a significant role in a positive guest experience. When a guest checks in after a long drive, plugs in their car, and goes to bed knowing they’ll have a full charge in the morning, you’re not just offering them a room; you’re offering peace of mind
3. **Improved Brand Image** Installing EV charging stations reflects your commitment to emissions reduction. For many guests, this can improve your hotel’s appeal and reinforce a positive image. It shows that your brand is willing to invest in a sustainable future.
4. **Longer Stays and Additional Revenue** **Opportunities** EV charging can even encourage longer stays, as guests may decide to spend more time at your property while their vehicle charges. During this time, they might enjoy your amenities, enjoy meals in your restaurant, or book additional services, generating extra revenue for your hotel.

## ‍ **Tips for Promoting Your Onsite EV Chargers**


**‍** Once you have charging stations installed, make sure potential guests know about them!Here are a few ways to promote your new amenity:


- **List It on Booking Platforms** : Many booking sites now allow hotels to specify EV charging as an amenity. Make sure it’s listed on platforms like Google, TripAdvisor, and other booking engines.
- **Highlight It on Your Website** : Include a section on your website about EV charging, ideally on the main page or in your amenities section. Mention how it aligns with your hotel’s commitment to sustainability.
- **Use Social Media** : Share the news on social media to reach potential guests who might be EV owners. You can also post updates and stories about EV adoption in your area or industry trends to keep your audience engaged.
- **Incentivize Your Guests:** Consider offering incentives, like discounts or loyalty points, to guests who book with you and drive an EV. This gesture can encourage loyalty and positive reviews.
