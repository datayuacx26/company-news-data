# Marvell Technology, Inc. Declares Quarterly Dividend Payment


March 19, 2026


SANTA CLARA, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Marvell Technology, Inc.](https://www.marvell.com/) (NASDAQ: MRVL), today announced a quarterly dividend of $0.06 per share of common stock payable on April 30, 2026 to stockholders of record as of April 10, 2026.


**About Marvell**


To deliver the data infrastructure technology that connects the world, we’re building solutions on the most powerful foundation: our partnerships with our customers. Trusted by the world’s leading technology companies for over 30 years, we move, store, process and secure the world’s data with semiconductor solutions designed for our customers’ current needs and future ambitions. Through a process of deep collaboration and transparency, we’re ultimately changing the way tomorrow’s enterprise, cloud and carrier architectures transform—for the better.
