# Diebold Nixdorf’s Vynamic® Transaction Middleware Recognized by Global Payments Awards

[View All News](https://investors.dieboldnixdorf.com/news-and-events/press-releases/default.aspx)


### Diebold Nixdorf’s Vynamic® Transaction Middleware Recognized by Global Payments Awards


July 15, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s27.q4cdn.com/808990265/files/doc_news/2026/Jul/15/Diebold-Nixdorf-s-Vynamic-Transaction-Middleware-Recognized-by-Global-Payments-Awards.docx)


*Cloud-native, API-driven platform honored by World Business Outlook and PayTech Awards 2026 for helping financial institutions modernize payments infrastructure*


**NORTH CANTON, Ohio** – Diebold Nixdorf (NYSE: DBD), a world leader in transforming the way people bank and shop, today announced that its[Vynamic® Transaction Middleware](https://www.dieboldnixdorf.com/en-us/banking/portfolio/software/transaction-middleware/) solution recently received two international recognitions for innovation in payments. The company was named **Best Payment Solutions Provider Global 2026** by World Business Outlook and earned Highly Commended recognition in the **Best Payments System for Banks and FIs** category at the PayTech Awards 2026.


Vynamic Transaction Middleware is Diebold Nixdorf’s cloud-native, API-driven platform designed to help financial institutions modernize rapidly, eliminate siloed systems and deliver a smooth and consistent payment experience. It provides the core technology powering every payment interaction, including ATMs, POS terminals, mobile, web, branch, instant payments and card‑based transactions. Vynamic Transaction Middleware unifies issuing, acquiring, authorization and orchestration into a single platform.


Today, Vynamic Transaction Middleware is used by more than 80 financial institutions, supports more than 160,000 terminals worldwide and processes more than 20 billion transactions annually. In global deployments, the platform has helped institutions reduce operational complexity, streamline surrounding systems and accelerate time to market for new services while maintaining stability in high-volume payment environments.


**Andy Zosel, Diebold Nixdorf executive vice president and chief technology officer, said:** “These recognitions reflect the proven scale and flexibility of Vynamic Transaction Middleware, which helps banks and processors simplify complex environments, accelerate innovation and prepare for the continued evolution of global payments. They also reinforce Diebold Nixdorf’s success and strategy of expanding deeper into the branch by connecting payment capabilities with the broader banking experience.”


[World Business Outlook Awards](https://worldbusinessoutlook.com/awards/) recognize and celebrate exceptional accomplishments, significant contributions, dedication, hard work and unwavering commitment to grow in the Business and Finance sector. The[PayTech Awards](https://informaconnect.com/paytech-awards/) , presented by FinTech Futures, celebrate innovation and outstanding achievements in the payments industry.
