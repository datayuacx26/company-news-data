# Case Study: Modernizing Payments Infrastructure with a Platform-First Strategy

#### The Results: Modernizing Payments for Agility, Compliance and Future-Readiness


Bankart modernized its infrastructure with Diebold Nixdorf’s payment agnostic services, powered by Vynamic Transaction Middleware, a cloud-native, microservices-based platform designed to support innovation across payment rails, to unify payment processing on a single platform for all payment instruments. Additionally, the company reported about 30% faster time to market for onboarding new clients, while gaining a more scalable foundation for future growth.
