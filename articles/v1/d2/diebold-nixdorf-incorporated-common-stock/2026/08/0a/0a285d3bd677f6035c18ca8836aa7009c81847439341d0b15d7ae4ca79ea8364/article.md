# Infographic: The Top 7 Reasons to Consider Cash Recycling ATMs

As digital payments rise and self-service becomes more important, ATMs remain a critical touchpoint for consumers, businesses, and staff. Cash recycling ATMs plays a key role in supporting payment flexibility and operational efficiency.


The DN Series™ Cash Recycling ATMs are built to meet these demands head-on. With a globally proven fourth-generation recycling engine and more than 20 years of innovation behind it, the DN Series is engineered for what’s next.


**Reduce Cash Handling and Refocus Staff on Customers**
By automating cash processes, Diebold Nixdorf’s cash recycling ATMs can reduce teller cash-handling time by up to 90%. With fewer manual tasks, staff can focus more on meaningful customer interactions.


**Drive Efficiency with an Integrated, Intelligent Platform**
Modern cash recycling ATMs go beyond hardware – it’s a part of a smart, connected system.


**Ensure reliable cash access even when branch doors are closed**
When lobby access is limited, or even closed, ensuring around-the-clock cash access becomes critical. That’s why financial institutions that haven’t yet adopted deposit automation are prioritizing it now.
