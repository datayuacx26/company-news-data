## What are DTF transfers?


Direct to Film (DTF) is a heat transfer process where designs are printed onto a special film and coated with an adhesive powder, then transferred onto a fabric surface using a heat press. Unlike heat transfer vinyl (HTV), you don’t need to cut or weed DTF designs. Simply apply the transfer as one full-color print.


DTF transfers are simple to use, work on almost any fabric, and produce detailed, full-color prints with a seamless finish.


## How does DTF printing work?


Just like its name implies, Direct-to-Film (DTF) works by printing your design onto a special film. Adhesive powder is applied on top of the printed design, and the powder is then cured using heat to bond it to the ink, making the DTF transfer ready to be applied to fabric using a heat press.


The result is a vibrant, flexible print that works on a wide range of fabrics, both light and dark, like cotton, polyester, blends, canvas, fleece, and denim.


## How to apply DTF transfers at home


You don’t need to own a DTF printer to use Cricut DTF transfers; you can create and order your own personalized DTF transfers in Cricut Design Space®. Upload your original artwork or choose from Cricut’s library of content, customizable templates, AI-generated images, and more. Once you’ve finished your design, finalize your order by clicking “Add to Cart” in the upper right corner of the Design Space Canvas and get your DTF transfer shipped straight to you.


Once your transfer arrives, follow these steps to apply DTF transfers at home:


1. Preheat your Cricut heat press. Check the[Cricut Heat Guide](https://cricut.com/en-us/heatguide) for precise settings.
2. Prepare your surface. Use a lint roller and pre-press your blank for a few seconds to remove moisture.
3. Position the DTF transfer design-side down on your fabric. You should be able to see your design through the clear film the transfer was printed on.
4. Apply firm pressure for the amount of time recommended in the Cricut Heat Guide.
5. Once it has completely cooled, peel off the clear film layer to reveal your finished project.


## What materials, fabrics, and tools do you need?


Getting started with DTF transfers is simple. Create and purchase your DTF transfer in Cricut Design Space. Once your transfer arrives, you’ll need a Cricut heat press and a blank t-shirt or other fabric to apply your design to. DTF does not need to be cut or weeded, so you can apply your design without extra prep work!


## Best fabrics for DTF transfers


Unlike[sublimation prints and Infusible Ink designs](https://cricut.com/blog/sublimation-and-infusible-ink-with-cricut/) , DTF does not limit you to only using certain kinds of fabrics. DTF transfers work well with items like T-shirts, hoodies, and hats, transferring well onto cotton, polyester, cotton/poly blends, canvas, denim, and more in both light and dark fabrics.

### The verdict


For versatility and photo-quality, full-color designs, Cricut DTF transfers are a great option. Choose HTV for simple designs in a solid color or with few color layers. Working with 100% polyester or polyester blends in light colors? Sublimation is ideal and will never crack, peel, or fade. And for hard goods like plastic and metal — especially outdoor pieces — UV DTF hits the mark.


## Where to buy DTF transfers and what to look for


Cricut makes it easy to design, purchase, and ship custom DTF transfers right to your door. To get started, simply open the Design Space app and follow the prompts to create a DTF transfer.


### What to look for in a DTF transfer order


**Print quality:** A good DTF transfer will have sharp details and vibrant colors.


**Feel:** DTF transfers should be soft and flexible, not stiff or plasticky.


**Durability:** Quality DTF transfers are wash-resistant and crack-resistant.


**Print & ship time:** If you’re ordering a DTF transfer, it’s important to plan ahead for time-sensitive projects since you will have to wait for the transfer to be shipped to you before you can apply it to your shirt (or other material).


**Clear instructions:** Look for guidance on how to apply the transfer for best results. For Cricut DTF transfers, you can visit the[Cricut Heat Guide](https://cricut.com/en-us/heatguide) and select “Cricut DTF Transfers” under the Heat-Transfer Material drop-down for application instructions.


## Conclusion


DTF transfers are a flexible and beginner-friendly way to create custom apparel. From matching family T-shirts, custom party favor tote bags, or even starting a small business, DTF transfers help you create professional-looking projects for any occasion.
