Teacher Appreciation Week is the perfect time to celebrate the people who show up every day with patience, creativity, and care, and the best gifts are the ones that feel personal.


With Cricut Design Space® and a Cricut cutting machine, it’s easy to create teacher appreciation gifts that look professional and personalized while being more budget-friendly and thoughtful than a typical store-bought gift. Whether you’re crafting for one special teacher or putting together gifts for the whole team, these ideas are simple enough that even beginner crafters can make them with confidence.


## Why personalized teacher gifts stand out


A personalized gift instantly feels more meaningful. Adding a teacher’s name, their favorite colors, or a nod to their teaching subject makes your gift feel intentional. Plus, personalization doesn’t have to mean complicated! Cricut Design Space makes it easy to customize any project, so you can create one-of-a-kind teacher appreciation gifts in just a few steps.


## Teacher appreciation gift ideas you can make with a Cricut


Here are five creative, practical, and heartfelt ideas teachers will actually use and love.


### 1. T-shirt with student-drawn hearts


Turn their students’ artwork into a wearable keepsake! Have each child draw a small heart (or another simple shape or design of your choice), then upload a scanned image of the drawings into Design Space to create a custom T-shirt that teachers will treasure long after the school year ends. Check out how teacher Sydney Natysin did this to surprise her class for Valentine’s Day in this[feature from People](https://people.com/teacher-turns-her-students-heart-drawings-into-a-shirt-exclusive-11680554) .


### 2.[Personalized tote bag](https://design-beta.cricut.com/landing/project-detail/660742c2ff0f973907e0013d)


**Estimated time requirement: 30–45 minutes**


A good tote bag is one of the most practical teacher appreciation gifts, but with a little personalization, it can also be a fun way for teachers to get to show off a little personality while carrying around papers to grade, books, supplies, or everyday essentials. Customize the tote with the teacher’s name, grade level, or a fun design or phrase like “Teaching is my superpower.”


### 3.[Gift basket with custom tags](https://design-beta.cricut.com/landing/project-detail/5e7ece635221a2e04ce28cdf)


**Estimated time requirement: less than 30 minutes**


Gift baskets are a great way to bundle smaller items—think snacks, self-care treats, or classroom supplies.


Use your Cricut to create custom tags, labels, or themed packaging to tie everything together. It’s an easy way to elevate even simple items into something that feels special.


## Personalized teacher gifts teachers will actually use


### 4.[Teacher drink tumbler](https://design-beta.cricut.com/landing/project-detail/61804e8c0d535c5f2bf38757)


**Estimated time requirement: 30 **–** 45 minutes**


Custom drinkware is always a hit. Add a name, monogram, or fun teaching-related quote to create a tumbler they’ll use daily.


### 5.[Lined paper drink tumbler](https://design-beta.cricut.com/landing/project-detail/621fd7a73e7d7aa91192f395)


**Estimated time requirement: less than 30 minutes**


This fun design mimics notebook paper—perfect for educators! It’s a clever twist on a practical item and makes a great conversation starter.


### 6.[Solar system door sign](https://design-beta.cricut.com/landing/project-detail/621fefab772c2a7e91b411a3)


**Estimated time requirement: 1 hour**


Help teachers personalize their classroom with a custom door sign. Classroom decor is a thoughtful option for teacher appreciation week gifts because it adds personality to their space. This solar system design is perfect for science teachers, but you can easily adapt it for any subject.


### 7.[Name desk plaque](https://design-beta.cricut.com/landing/project-detail/66071d1f3f10ead9e2efc4d9)


**Estimated time requirement: 30 minutes – 1 hour**


A desk plaque is a simple but meaningful gift. Adding the teacher’s name is a given, but you can also personalize further by adding the name of the school, grade level, or their teaching subject. This kind of personalization is a small detail that makes their workspace feel more personal and polished.

Short on time? These DIY teacher appreciation gifts come together quickly but still feel thoughtful.

A heartfelt card goes a long way. Customize with a handwritten message from you or your child for an extra personal touch. Pair your card with a gift card or small treat for an easy, last-minute win.


## Budget-friendly DIY teacher gifts


You don’t need to spend a lot to give something meaningful. In fact, many of the best homemade teacher gifts are simple, and using a Cricut machine helps you create a polished look while keeping costs low, so your gift feels premium without the price tag. If you’re looking to gift on a budget, here are some great combos that don’t break the bank:


- A personalized tote bag + a favorite snack
- A custom tumbler + a gift card to their favorite drink place
- A handmade card + a small plant to decorate their desk
