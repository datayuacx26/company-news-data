# Cricut to Announce Fourth Quarter 2025 Financial Results on March 3, 2026

SOUTH JORDAN, Utah, Feb. 10, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Cricut, Inc. (“Cricut”) (NASDAQ: CRCT), the creative technology company that has brought a connected platform for making to millions of users worldwide, today announced it will report its financial results for the fourth quarter ended December 31, 2025 after the U.S. markets close on Tuesday, March 3, 2026. Cricut management will host a conference call and webcast to discuss the results that afternoon at 3:00 p.m. Mountain Time (5:00 p.m. Eastern Time).


A live webcast of the earnings call will be available on Cricut’s investor relations website at[https://investor.cricut.com/](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=x8PtoSfz6BC9i2ezBvKfNn9gp50Wg5HDrt6MCXmGSuXnJhrwng2HcnH406F82FuOmHEh7ispDeQGsun302PyGEpafC2tU47CNYtwCAc2Zygy-GvXePeTfGIXdH7_5Yqv) . **About Cricut, Inc.**


Cricut, Inc. is a creative platform company that makes it easy for users to create meaningful personal items. Cricut hardware and software work together as a connected platform for consumers to make beautiful, high-quality projects quickly and easily. These industry-leading products include a flagship line of smart cutting machines — the Cricut Maker® family, the Cricut Explore® family, and the Cricut Joy® family — accompanied by other unique tools like Cricut EasyPress®, the Infusible Ink™ system, and a diverse collection of materials. In addition to providing tools and materials, Cricut fosters a thriving community of millions of dedicated users worldwide.


Cricut has used, and intends to continue using, its investor relations website and the Cricut News Blog ([https://inspiration.cricut.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=x8PtoSfz6BC9i2ezBvKfNsHl5Yq8RExi9NPYwkYz_x4_bmMQg4qVH52hmo5SvyJCw3N8jJmmdVxt7jWHvUn2Yx7gJqVFF0TQGwpY1kVSNTo2IokNjkrN-8V1OOQQKgI2) ) to disclose material non-public information and to comply with its disclosure obligations under Regulation FD. Accordingly, you should monitor our investor relations website and the Cricut News Blog in addition to following our press releases, SEC filings and public conference calls and webcasts.
