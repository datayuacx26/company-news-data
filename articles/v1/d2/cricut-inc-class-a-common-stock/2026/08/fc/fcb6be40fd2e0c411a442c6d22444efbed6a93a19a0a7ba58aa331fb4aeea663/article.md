Cricut Patterns is a curated library of premium crochet and knitting patterns from independent designers. Browse hundreds of inspiring projects, save your favorites, and unlock patterns using Cricut credits. Best of all, you don’t need a Cricut machine to enjoy Cricut Patterns; it’s designed for anyone who loves making by hand.


Whether you’re picking up a crochet hook for the first time or searching for your next knitting project, Cricut Patterns makes it easy to discover high-quality designs from talented independent creators for beginners and experts alike.


# A new place to find your next knit or crochet project


Finding your next project should be just as enjoyable as making it.


Cricut Patterns brings together over 850 premium crochet and knitting patterns created by independent designers, giving you one destination to browse beautiful, thoughtfully designed projects across a wide range of styles and skill levels.


Explore patterns for:


- Sweaters and wearables
- Blankets and throws
- Hats, scarves, and accessories
- Home decor
- Plushies and amigurumi
- Baby gifts
- Seasonal and holiday projects
- Giftable handmade makes


Whether you’re looking for an easy weekend project or something more advanced, you’ll find inspiration for every stage of your creative journey.


# Made by independent designers


Every project in Cricut Patterns is created by an independent crochet or knitting designer.


Finding high-quality crochet and knitting patterns from trusted designers can be challenging. The good news is, we did the searching so you wouldn’t have to! Cricut conducted an extensive review of the crochet and knitting world, evaluating thousands of artists and patterns across the internet to identify some of the very best independent designers. We then partnered with artists whose patterns combine beautiful design, clear instructions, and finished projects we believe you’ll love.


Rather than offering generic patterns, Cricut Patterns showcases carefully curated premium designs from talented creators, giving members access to patterns that would typically be purchased individually on other platforms, and allowing those patterns to all be stored in one convenient place. At the same time, the experience gives independent designers another way to share their work with a growing creative community.


# How Cricut Patterns works


Getting started with Cricut Patterns is simple!


## 1. Browse the collection


Search or browse by:


- Artist
- Crochet vs. knitting
- Project type (wearables, decor, blankets, etc.)
- Difficulty level
- Moment (seasonal moments, holidays, life events, and more)


## 2. Explore the project details


Each pattern page includes helpful information before you unlock it, including a product description, the difficulty level, yarn requirements, other materials and tools needed, and finished dimensions. Plus, check out more photos of the completed design. These details help you choose projects with confidence before committing your credits.


## 3. Save your favorites


Not ready to start? Save patterns to revisit later by clicking the heart or the bookmark to build your project wishlist. These saved patterns can be easily found again in the future, along with any purchased patterns, on the “My Project Bag” page.


## 4. Unlock your pattern


When you’ve found the perfect project, unlock it by clicking the “Download pattern” button.


Screenshot


Currently, each pattern costs four Cricut credits. These are the same credits used across other eligible Cricut experiences, including Cricut Coloring Pages, Cricut Photo Art, and features in Design Space such as AI Project Designer and Create AI.
