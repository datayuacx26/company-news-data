Turn a favorite photo, a fun idea, or a simple text prompt into a printable coloring page with Cricut’s new Coloring Pages experience! Whether you’re making activities for kids, creating classroom printables, planning a birthday party, or designing relaxing adult coloring pages, Cricut Coloring Pages helps bring your ideas to life, with no design skills required.


With[Cricut Coloring Pages](https://creativelabs.cricut.com/coloring-pages?utm_source=cricut_blog&utm_medium=blogpost&utm_campaign=coloring-pages&utm_content=how-to-make) , you can turn a photo into a coloring page, generate custom artwork from a text prompt, and instantly create printable black-and-white outline designs ready to color and share.


## What is Cricut Coloring Pages?


Cricut Coloring Pages is a creative tool powered by Cricut AI that transforms photos, ideas, and memories into printable coloring pages. Simply upload an image or describe what you want to create, and Cricut AI will generate unique black-and-white line art images in seconds.


You can use Cricut Coloring Pages to:


- Turn family photos into coloring pages
- Create custom coloring pages for kids
- Design holiday or party-themed printables
- Make relaxing adult coloring art
- Create classroom and homeschool activities
- Transform pets, vacations, and memories into personalized artwork


Best of all, Cricut Coloring Pages works independently, so no Cricut machine is required.


## What you’ll need to get started


To use Cricut Coloring Pages, all you’ll need is a Cricut Design Space account and a home printer.


If you have a free Design Space account, you will receive a one-time allotment of AI Credits to try the Cricut Coloring Pages or other Cricut AI offerings within Design Space. After those credits have been used, you will need to[subscribe to Cricut Access](https://myaccount.cricut.com/subscription) for repeating monthly credits.


## How to turn a photo into a coloring page


One of the most exciting features of Cricut Coloring Pages is the ability to turn a photo into a coloring page instantly. Check these super simple step-by-step instructions for how to do just that:


### 1. Pick your favorite image


Choose your favorite photo, like a family portrait, pet photo, or vacation memory from your computer or phone camera roll.


### 2. Upload your image


Once you’ve picked your image, upload it to Cricut Coloring Pages by dropping it in the upload box or clicking into the upload box to select photos from your device’s camera roll.


### 3. Choose your art style


Cricut Coloring pages offers nine art styles to choose from for your coloring page: Classic, Comic Book, Cute, Adventure, Anime, Simple, Line Art, Caricature, and Magazine Cartoon. Select which style you’d like for your image based on your desired outcome and personal preference.


### 4. Add an optional prompt


You’re almost ready to generate! If you’d like to add more specific instructions or there are changes you’d like made to your image for your coloring page, you should write out those instructions in the prompt box where it says, “Add optional text.”


### 5. Generate your coloring page


Select “Generate for 1 credit” at the bottom right corner of the screen, and watch your image come to life as a coloring page!


Note: Before generating, you will be required to sign in or create a Cricut Design Space account.


### 6. Download and print


Now that your coloring page has been created, you can download and print it from your home printer, and it’s ready for coloring!


If you’d like to change anything about your coloring page, you can also select the “Try Again” button in the middle of the screen. Doing so will take you back to the prompt screen with your uploaded photo, where you can add or change your prompt to get whatever end result you have in mind.

### Tips for better photo-to-coloring-page results


For the best coloring page results:


- Use clear, well-lit photos
- Choose images with simple subjects
- Avoid cluttered backgrounds
- Use clear prompts that explain exactly what you’re expecting (including any specific details you want added or changed from your photo)
- Regenerate variations if needed


AI-generated coloring pages may occasionally include unusual details or inconsistencies, but adjusting your prompt usually improves your results.


## How to create a coloring page from a text prompt


You can also create custom coloring pages using only a text description. Simply type what you want to see on your coloring page, and Cricut Coloring Pages will generate a unique printable artwork from your imagination. Check out these step-by-step instructions for more help:


### 1. Navigate to the Text Prompt screen


Toggle to “Text Prompt” from the top middle of the screen. From here you will be able to write your prompt and generate your coloring page without an image reference.


### 2. Type your prompt and select your style


Type out your prompt under where it says “Describe your dream coloring page.” Once you’ve written your prompt, select which of the nine drawing styles you would like your coloring page to be in from the right side of the screen.


### 3. Generate and download your coloring page


Select “Generate for 1 Credit” from the bottom of the page and watch your idea come to life!


From here, you can download and print your coloring page from your home printer, and it’s ready for coloring! If you’d like to make any changes to your coloring page, select “Try Again” and rework your prompt before generating once again.

## Tips for Better AI Coloring Pages


To get the best results from an AI coloring page generator, try these simple tips:


### Be specific


Detailed prompts help AI understand what you want. Include subjects, settings, styles, and moods.


### Include age or complexity


Specify whether you want a simple kids coloring page or intricate adult coloring art.


### Experiment with variations


If the first result isn’t perfect, tweak your wording and generate again. The good news is, Cricut Coloring Pages will save all of your coloring pages results under “My Recent Pages,” so if you end up deciding you liked a previous generation more than a newer one, you can go back and download that one.


### Avoid copyrighted characters


Some prompts referencing copyrighted characters or brands may be blocked in order to protect intellectual property rights.
