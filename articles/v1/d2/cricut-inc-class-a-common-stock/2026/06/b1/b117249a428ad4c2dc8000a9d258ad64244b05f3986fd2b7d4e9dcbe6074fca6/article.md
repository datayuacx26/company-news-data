Ever wish you could transform a favorite photo into a beautiful watercolor portrait, a trendy sketch, or an heirloom portrait? With Cricut Photo Art, you can turn your personal photos straight from your camera roll into custom artwork that’s fully compatible with Cricut Design Space.


Whether you’re creating personalized gifts, custom wall décor, greeting cards, unique apparel, or just a fun new profile picture,[Cricut Photo Art](https://creativelabs.cricut.com/photo-art?utm_source=cricut_blog&utm_medium=blogpost&utm_campaign=photo-art&utm_content=how-to-make) helps you generate stylized artwork in just a few clicks.


## What is Cricut Photo Art?


Cricut Photo Art is a Cricut AI tool that transforms a photo into a new image, styled to look like custom artwork. You simply choose a size for your generated artwork, select an image style, generate your image using a Cricut AI credit, and download the finished artwork for your next creative project.


Unlike generic AI image generators, Cricut Photo Art is designed with makers in mind. It helps you create project-ready images that can be used as inspiration or incorporated into projects made in Cricut Design Space.


## How to Turn a Photo into Custom Artwork with Cricut Photo Art


Creating custom artwork is as simple as these five steps:


### Step 1: Upload a Photo or Describe Your Idea


Start by uploading a photo from your camera roll that you’d like to transform.


You can upload:


- Family photos
- Pet photos
- Vacation snapshots
- Portraits
- Product photos
- Inspiration images


Once you’ve uploaded your photo, you can also choose to add details to the prompt box below the image upload box. Doing so is not necessary but allows you to add instructions on any changes you’d like to make to your photo in the process of transforming it. The more specific your description, the more personalized your artwork can become.


Alternatively, skip the photo entirely and use a prompt to generate artwork from scratch.


### Step 2: Choose a Size


Select the image size ratio that works best for your project. Whether you’re creating a greeting card design, wall art, or a custom shirt graphic, choosing the right dimensions helps set you up for success.


Size options include:


- Auto (matches the size of the uploaded photo)
- Portrait (2:3)
- Landscape (3:2)
- Square (1:1)


### Step 3: Select a Style


Cricut Photo Art offers over 20 different artistic styles to help you transform your image exactly how you want it. Popular styles include Watercolor Portrait, Modern Pop Art, Urban Ink, Retro Print, Papercraft, and Art Nouveau.


### Step 4: Generate Your Artwork


Click “Generate for 1 Credit” to create your photo artwork. Each image generation uses one Cricut AI credit.


If you have not signed into your Design Space account yet, a pop-up screen will prompt you to log in or create an account before continuing. You must have a Design Space account to use Cricut Photo Art.


### Step 5: Review and Download


Once your artwork is generated, review the results carefully. AI-generated content can occasionally include mistakes or unexpected details, so it’s always a good idea to double-check before using your design. If you’d like to create a new version, simply select “Try Again” to regenerate the image, switching to a new style or adding new instructions in the prompt box.


When you’re happy with the result, download the image and save it as either a PDF or a PNG for use in whatever project you have planned.


## Best Photos to Use for Better AI Artwork


Not all photos produce the same results. For the best experience, choose photos that include:


- Clear subject(s)
- Good lighting
- Clear details
- Sharp focus
- A simple background


Portraits, pet photos, and close-up subjects often generate the strongest results.

## AI Photo Prompt Ideas for Cricut Projects


If you’re starting with a prompt instead of a photo, try this simple formula:


**Subject + Style + Details + Project Use**


Check out a few fun examples below:


### Pet Portrait Prompt


“A cheerful golden retriever in a watercolor portrait style for a framed Cricut gift project.”


### Holiday Gift Prompt


“A cozy winter family portrait in a hand-painted watercolor style for personalized holiday wall art.”


### Party Decor Prompt


“A colorful retro pop-art illustration of a birthday celebration for custom party decorations.”


### Greeting Card Prompt


“A delicate floral illustration in sketch style for a handmade greeting card.”


### Custom Apparel Prompt


“A vintage-inspired mountain landscape illustration for a nature-themed T-shirt design.”


When writing prompts, keep them short, clear, and specific. Include the subject, artistic style, mood, and intended project whenever possible.


## Cricut Photo Art vs. Cricut Coloring Pages: Which Tool Should You Use?


Both Cricut Photo Art and Cricut Coloring Pages use AI technology, but they serve different creative purposes.


Use **Cricut Photo Art** when you want stylized full-color artwork made from your personal photos.


Use **[Cricut Coloring Pages](https://creativelabs.cricut.com/coloring-pages?utm_source=cricut_blog&utm_medium=blogpost&utm_campaign=coloring-pages&utm_content=how-to-make&_gl=1*m6yppl*_gcl_aw*R0NMLjE3ODE3NDE3ODQuQ2owS0NRandpOG5SQmhEaEFSSXNBSFpmX3BZMGg3aEZ5aTFhRnEzUS1KV2NrN0FQc1lkUk9KOWNGRV9RaEI3VVVEU0F1cC15Vl9jaVNNQWFBdHpYRUFMd193Y0I.*_gcl_au*MTk4MDU0MjY2OS4xNzc2NzkxMjYz)** when you want ready-to-print, black-and-white coloring pages based on text prompts and photos.


If you’re interested in creating printable coloring sheets, check out our guide on how to[make custom coloring pages with Cricut](https://cricut.com/blog/make-custom-coloring-pages-with-cricut/) .


## How Cricut AI Credits Work


Photo Art uses Cricut AI credits to generate images.


Here’s what you need to know:


- A Cricut Design Space account is required.
- Each image generation uses 1 Cricut AI credit.
- Regenerating an image uses an additional credit.
- Cricut Access subscribers receive recurring AI credits based on their subscription plan.


To learn more, explore[Cricut AI tools](https://cricut.com/blog/cricut-launches-ai-project-designer/) and Cricut AI credits in Design Space.


## What Happens to Uploaded Images?


When you upload an image to Cricut Photo Art, the image is sent to the AI image-generation service to create your artwork. Uploaded images are not saved to Cricut servers, and the original uploaded image is discarded after processing.


## Download and Use Your Artwork in Design Space


After generating your artwork:


1. Download the finished image.
2. Open Design Space.
3. [Upload the image into Design Space](https://help.cricut.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009556313-How-to-upload-images-into-Design-Space) .
4. Add the image to your Canvas and incorporate it into your next Cricut project.


Your artwork can be used as inspiration for personalized gifts, home décor, apparel, cards, party decorations, and more.
