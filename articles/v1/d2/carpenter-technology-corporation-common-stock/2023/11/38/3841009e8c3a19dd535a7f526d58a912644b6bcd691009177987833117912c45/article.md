### Cobalt Alloys for Minimally Invasive Procedures


As medical procedures advance and robotics play a more significant role, the demand for smaller incisions and more intricate procedures is on the rise. Carpenter Technology offers a range of smaller diameter cobalt-base materials, specifically designed to meet this demand:


**[BIODUR® CCM](https://www.carpentertechnology.com/alloy-finder/biodur-ccm-bioblu-acube-100) :** Widely used in orthopedic applications, this non-magnetic cobalt-chromium-molybdenum alloy excels in high strength, corrosion resistance, and wear resistance. It meets several industry specifications and is available in small diameters with tight tolerances.


**[CONICHROME®](https://www.carpentertechnology.com/alloy-finder/conichrome) :** Ideal for vascular stents and suture wires, Conichrome exhibits remarkable strength, ductility, and corrosion resistance. Carpenter's cold work capabilities provide exceptionally high mechanical properties.


**[BIODUR® L-605](https://www.carpentertechnology.com/alloy-finder/biodur-l-605) :** Suitable for a variety of medical applications, L-605 boasts good oxidation and corrosion resistance, making it ideal for heart valves, vascular stents, orthopedic cables, and more.


**[MP35N®](https://www.carpentertechnology.com/alloy-finder/mp35n) :** This non-magnetic alloy offers ultra-high tensile strength, good ductility, toughness, and excellent corrosion resistance. Its drawability, coupled with Carpenter's capabilities, allows the production of small sizes for minimally invasive procedures.
