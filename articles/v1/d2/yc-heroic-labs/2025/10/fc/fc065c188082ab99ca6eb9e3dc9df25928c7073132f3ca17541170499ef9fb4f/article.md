# Announcing SOC 2 Type II

We’re pleased to announce that Heroic Labs has achieved SOC 2 Type II compliance certification, as validated by independent auditors at[Johanson Group LLP](https://www.johansonllp.com/) .


## What This Means for You


Game studios trust us with their player data, multiplayer infrastructure, and LiveOps systems. This certification provides independent validation that our security controls meet enterprise standards, tested over months of continuous operation, not just reviewed on paper.


SOC 2 Type II compliance is table stakes for enterprise game infrastructure. We undergo regular audits, annual penetration testing, and continuous security monitoring to maintain these standards. It’s part of our broader commitment to infrastructure that scales with your success.


## Built for Gaming at Scale


Game backends handle millions of concurrent players, real-time multiplayer sessions, and sensitive player data. Our security controls have been designed specifically for the unique demands of game infrastructure:


- **Multi-cloud resilience** across AWS and GCP
- **Zero-downtime deployments** to maintain 24/7 game operations
- **Encrypted data storage and transmission** for player data protection
- **Comprehensive access controls** with multi-factor authentication
