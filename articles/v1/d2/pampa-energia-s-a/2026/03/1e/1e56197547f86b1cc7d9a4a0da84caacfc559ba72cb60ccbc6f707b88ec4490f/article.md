# Upgrade in credit rating from Fitch Ratings

**Buenos Aires, March 3, 2026.** Pampa Energía S.A. (‘Pampa’ or the ‘Company’; NYSE: PAM; ByMA: PAMP) announces that today, Fitch Ratings upgraded the Company’s long-term foreign and local currency ratings from ‘B-’ to ‘B’, and the debt rating from ‘B’ to ‘B+’. The rating outlook was revised to ‘positive’ from ‘stable’.
