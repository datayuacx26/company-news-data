# Reopening of the 2037 international bond for US$500 million

**Buenos Aires, May 14, 2026.** Pampa Energía S.A. (‘Pampa’ or the ‘Company’; NYSE: PAM; ByMA: PAMP) announces the successful reopening of its international bond maturing November 2037 with an annual interest rate of 7.750% (the ‘2037 Notes’) for US$500 million and a yield of 7.6%.


The offering was more than twice oversubscribed, pricing at the lowest spread over U.S. Treasuries in Pampa’s debt issuance history, extending the maturity profile and strengthening Pampa’s debt profile. The 2037 Notes now have a total outstanding amount of US$950 million.
