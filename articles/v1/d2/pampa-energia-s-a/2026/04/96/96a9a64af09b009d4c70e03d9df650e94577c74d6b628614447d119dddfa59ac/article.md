# CB Series 27 issuance

**Buenos Aires, March 31, 2026.** Pampa Energía S.A. (‘Pampa’ or the ‘Company’; NYSE: PAM; ByMA: PAMP) announces that today Pampa successfully issued its CB Series 27 for a total amount of US$200 million, with a 36-month maturity and an annual interest rate of 5.49%.


The CB Series 27 were fully placed, with allocation prorated due to strong investor demand. The transaction received orders for more than US$425 million, over twice the amount offered, and a total of 8,591 bids.


This issuance reaffirms market confidence in Pampa and supports its investment and development plans in the energy sector.
