### 2. Wave


Wave is another popular choice among small business owners seeking a free accounting solution that doesn’t compromise on functionality. It offers core accounting features along with additional tools like payroll and invoicing.


#### Key Features


- **Free Accounting Software** : Wave’s basic accounting features are completely free, making it highly attractive for startups and freelancers.
- **Invoicing and Payments** : Create and send professional invoices, and accept payments online seamlessly.
- **Payroll Services** : Wave offers affordable payroll services, helping businesses manage employee payments and taxes efficiently.
- **Bank Reconciliation** : Easily reconcile bank statements with your accounting records to ensure accuracy.
- **Reports and Insights** : Gain valuable insights into your business’s financial health through Wave’s reporting tools.


### 3. Zoho Books


Zoho Books is a cloud-based accounting platform ideal for small and growing businesses, especially those that want an all-in-one solution integrated with other Zoho apps. It’s a tax-compliant system that covers end-to-end bookkeeping needs – from managing invoices and payables to automating workflows and monitoring financial reports.


#### Key Features


- Supports invoicing, expense tracking, sales orders, bills, and bank reconciliation, covering all basic accounting functions in one place.
- Includes built-in automation to reduce tedious data entry and lets you create custom workflows (e.g., recurring invoices, payment reminders), saving time on routine tasks.
- Track inventory levels, manage purchase and sales orders, and even handle advanced inventory controls (warehousing, serial number tracking) in higher-tier plans.
- Handle multi-currency transactions and automatic sales tax calculations, helping businesses stay compliant with local tax laws while operating globally.
- It integrates with Zoho’s suite (CRM, payroll, etc.) and other apps, making it a solid choice if you prefer a single platform for all business software needs.


### 4. FreshBooks


FreshBooks is renowned for its user-friendly interface and extensive features tailored for service-based businesses and freelancers. It simplifies invoicing, expense tracking, and time management.


#### Key Features


- FreshBooks is designed with simplicity in mind, making it easy for non-accountants to manage their finances.
- Create customized invoices and receive payments online, with automated reminders for overdue payments.
- Track expenses effortlessly and categorize them for better financial management.
- Monitor billable hours and automatically include them in client invoices.
- Manage projects and collaborate with team members directly within the platform.


### 5. Xero


Xero is a cloud-based accounting software known for its scalability and extensive integrations. It caters to businesses of all sizes, offering advanced accounting features along with customizable dashboards.


#### Key Features


- Xero grows with your business, offering features suitable for both small startups and larger enterprises.
- Create professional invoices and manage payments efficiently with automated reminders.
- Track inventory levels in real-time and manage purchases and stock adjustments.
- Connect bank accounts securely to reconcile transactions effortlessly.
- Generate detailed financial reports and utilize customizable dashboards for business insights.


### 6. Sage 50 Accounting


Sage 50 Accounting (formerly Peachtree) is a powerful, easy-to-use accounting solution tailored for small and mid-sized businesses. It’s a desktop-based software with cloud-connected features for added flexibility, meaning you install it on your PC for reliability, but still get online benefits like bank feeds and cloud backups.


#### Key Features


- Manage stock with support for assemblies (build products from components) and multiple costing methods (FIFO/LIFO). Premium and Quantum editions add serialized inventory tracking for detailed stock control.
- Track job expenses and revenues to assess project profitability. Higher tiers include advanced job costing by phase/cost level and more sophisticated budgeting tools, ideal for construction, manufacturing, and other project-driven industries.
- Offers modules tailored to specific industries (construction, manufacturing, distribution) in the Premium and Quantum versions, providing reports and features designed for those sectors.
- Generate comprehensive financial statements, cash flow reports, and departmental reports. An internal audit trail logs all transactions and edits (with user timestamps) for accountability – useful when multiple people manage the books.
- Supports several users (up to 5 in Premium, 40 in Quantum) with role-based permissions in the Quantum edition. This allows controlled access for each team member and facilitates collaboration in larger accounting teams.


### 7. Puzzle


Puzzle is a modern accounting software designed specifically for startups, positioning itself as a “replacement for QuickBooks” for young companies. It combines a real-time financial data platform with a general ledger, giving founders and finance teams up-to-the-minute insights into their finances.


#### Key Features


- Puzzle’s AI-native ledger automates repetitive bookkeeping tasks. It can draft financial statements, perform bank reconciliations, and even conduct an AI-driven month-end review, drastically reducing manual data entry and errors.
- Provides dashboards for key startup metrics – cash on hand, burn rate, runway, annual/monthly recurring revenue (ARR/MRR), etc. – so founders have instant visibility into their financial health and performance.
- Built-in support for revenue recognition policies and automatic ARR/MRR calculations, which is crucial for subscription-based startups and ensuring GAAP-compliant financials as you grow.
- Emphasizes collaboration between founders, accountants, and finance teams. Users can tag colleagues on transactions, add comments or attachments (like receipts), and maintain an audit log, making it easy to resolve queries and keep everyone on the same page.
- Puzzle merges a live data feed with traditional accounting, meaning your books update continuously as transactions occur. This “streaming” ledger approach enables continuous close and real-time reporting, rather than waiting for month-end batch processes.
