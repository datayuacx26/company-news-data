# The Top 5 Delaware Registered Agent Services

When establishing a business in Delaware, having a registered agent is a legal requirement.


A registered agent acts as your business's official contact for legal and tax correspondence with the state. They receive important documents such as tax notices, legal summons, and official state correspondence on behalf of your business.


Choosing the right Delaware-registered agent service is crucial for ensuring compliance and maintaining good standing with the state.


Beyond basic legal requirements, many registered agent services offer additional benefits such as mail forwarding, compliance reminders, and document management.


Here, we explore some of the top Delaware registered agent services available today, highlighting their features, strengths, and suitability for different business needs.


## **What Does a Registered Agent Do?**


A registered agent serves as the official point of contact between your business and the state of Delaware. Registered agents are required for all Delaware businesses (as well as businesses in other US states). Their primary responsibilities include:


- Receiving legal documents and official correspondence from the state on behalf of your business.
- Ensuring the timely delivery of important documents such as tax notices and legal summons.
- Providing a physical address within Delaware where legal documents can be served during business hours.


In addition to these core responsibilities, many registered agent services offer supplementary services such as compliance monitoring, document storage, and mail forwarding to support businesses in meeting their regulatory obligations effectively.
