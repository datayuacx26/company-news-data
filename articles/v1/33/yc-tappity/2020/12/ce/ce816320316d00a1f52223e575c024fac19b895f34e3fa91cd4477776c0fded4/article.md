# Tappity Live Classes is here!

After testing with lots of kids and parents, we’re so excited to announce the launch of Tappity Live Classes—classes your kids control! Our first class debuts on Monday, December 14th 2020 at 2pm PT!


Every live class will contain fresh and fun science content full of engaging interactions. And with a new class every other day, they’ll be learning in record time!


Read on to learn more —>


### What’s a Tappity Live Class?


Every Monday, our science host introduces a new topic to explore throughout the week. For our first week we’ll be covering the world of ancient Egypt technology! In true Tappity fashion, kids will be able to participate in each Live Class using a variety of new interactions while earning points when they participate!


Each class—four unique ones per week— is ~20 minutes long, and cover the weekly theme through a variety of learning methods:


-


**Mondays** : Science Story Time / Topic Introduction


-


**Wednesdays** : Drawing Activity


-


**Fridays:** Science Trivia


-


**Sundays:** Experiments / Crafts


### How can my kids attend?


All of our classes stream at 2:00 PM Pacific Time except for the Sunday class, which airs at 8:00 AM Pacific Time. If your family’s not able to attend, our Live Classes will be accessible in the app after they've streamed!


Want to see what’s coming up next? Look out for our weekly email newsletters or check out[our class schedule](https://www.tappityapp.com/content-schedule) .


### Missed a class? Watch it later!


All Tappity Live Classes will be accessible in the app after they've streamed! Better yet, even though they're pre-recorded, each session will remain interactive, so your child can participate as if they were watching it live.


**See you on Monday, December 14th at 2pm PT, and don’t forget to update the app!**


Warmly,


The Tappity Team

## **FAQs**


### **How do I join a Tappity Live Class?**


1. Install latest version of Tappity on your device (25.5.3+) -- Required


2. When it's time, open Tappity and tap on "Tappity Live" on the home screen (WiFi Required).


### **I don’t see Tappity Live Class in the app. What should I do?**


Tappity Live Classes is only supported on iOS devices that are at least iOS 13.2+. If you do not see Tappity Live button on the homescreen, you can also tune in on our website: https://app.tappityapp.com/live (Account Required).


### Will Tappity Live Classes be viewable later?


Yes! For families who can't attend the live event, we will be posting recordings of the live streams in Tappity shortly after.
