# Congratulations to the 2020 Junior Science Fair winners!

Earlier this summer, we announced the first and biggest[online science fair](http://www.juniorsciencefair.com/) for kids. Today, we're incredibly excited to share the winners.


Our panel of judges reviewed over 300 incredible submissions--and it was no doubt, one of the toughest decisions they've ever had to make. In the end, three submissions were selected for their science, creativity, presentation, and enthusiasm: Adam from Sheffield, UK, Elena from Bakersfield, California, and Vivian from British Columbia, Canada. Congratulations!


Of course, we also want to thank the 100’s of kids and families around the world who participated in the science fair. Your curiosity is inspiring!


To see the winning submissions and announcement by Kari Byron from Mythbusters, please visit:[www.juniorsciencefair.com/winners](http://www.juniorsciencefair.com/winners) .


Thank you everyone for making the inaugural Junior Science Fair such a fun and amazing success!


-The Tappity Team
