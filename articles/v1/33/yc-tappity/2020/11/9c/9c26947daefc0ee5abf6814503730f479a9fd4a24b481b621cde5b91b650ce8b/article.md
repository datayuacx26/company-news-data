# Tappity Stickers Are Coming!

To celebrate our cast of new characters and hours of new content, we've created Tappity stickers! They'll be available for purchase on our website or through achievements in the app at the end of December. Sticker designs will include Haley, Quentin, AngleBert, Chip, the President of Earth, and a host of science-themed objects. Best of all, you can combine stickers together to dress up the characters!


We have worked with[Sticker Mule](https://stickermule.com/) to produce our high-quality stickers. Beyond producing the best stickers possible they also provide tools like Trace that acts as a[background eraser](https://www.stickermule.com/trace) ​ and automatically cuts out a character from the background to turn it into a sticker. We'll send out an email update when the stickers are available.
