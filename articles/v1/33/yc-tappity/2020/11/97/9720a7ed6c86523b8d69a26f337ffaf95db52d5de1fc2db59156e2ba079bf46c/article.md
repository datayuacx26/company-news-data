# Our biggest content update is here!

This month we launched a suite of new content and feature updates to make Tappity better and more interactive than ever!


## Tappity Originals - Season 1 **✨**


Completely redesigned from the ground up, Tappity Originals are immersive, cinematic, and story-driven science adventures where your child saves the future of science with Tappity's new cast of characters. In each episode, we've added new interactions, like swiping and 3-D models, so there's never a dull moment! Check out Episode 1 today!


## Tappity Field Trips **✈️**


Can't go outside? No problem. Tappity Field Trips are short bite-sized adventures led by Chip, the world's smartest potato, on the Tappity Transporter. Your child will explore new destinations, beautiful wildlife, and even go back in time to hear from famous scientists! Best of all, they'll literally be in the driver seat of Tappity Transporter.


## Tappity Points **💯**


As your child progresses through lessons, completes Live activities, and participates in Trivia, they'll earn Tappity Points in addition to badges. The more points the earn, the higher the level they'll achieve!


Try it out and let us know what you think!
