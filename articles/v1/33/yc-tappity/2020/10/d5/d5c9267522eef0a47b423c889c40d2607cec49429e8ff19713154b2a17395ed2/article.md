# Tappity just got more personalized

We’re excited to announce that Tappity now supports profiles! One of our most requested feature, you can now individually track the progress of all your kids under a single account. This means as a child completes activities in Tappity, their progress will not interfere with the progress of their siblings or friend! Plus, you’ll have the ability to customize settings by profile, e.g. difficulty level & name and get a clearer picture of each child’s progress through our popular email updates. Check it out and let us know what you think!


-The Tappity Team


## FAQ’s


### **How do I set up a Profile?**


Update to version 26.1+ of Tappity on your device. Once you do, tap on the *Menu* button at the top left corner of the home screen, and tap on *Child Profiles* . Then, simply enter your child’s information and save it. Unfortunately, older versions of Tappity will not support this functionality.


### **How many profiles can I create?**


At this time, you can create as many profiles as you’d like under a single account.


### **What happens to my child’s existing progress when I create a new profile?**


Every time you create a new profile, your child gets a blank slate of Tappity. In other words, existing progress isn’t carried over to a new profile.


### **How do I know which profile is being used?**


You can check which profile you’re playing Tappity in by tapping on *Child Profiles* in the *Menu* option *.* The active profile will be highlighted.


### **How do we switch between profiles?**


Go to the *Menu* , and select on *Child Profiles* . From there, if you have more than one profile, you can tap on profile you want to play in. Once you’ve selected the profile, click on *Done* .


### **Do you have to pay more to add a profile?**


No, this feature is available to anyone even if you do not have a subscription. Currently, you can create as many profiles under a single Tappity account.
