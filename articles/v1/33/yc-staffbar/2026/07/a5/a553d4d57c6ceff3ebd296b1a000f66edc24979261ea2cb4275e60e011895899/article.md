# Android Revenue Tracking Available in Superwall

If your app is on Google Play, then I've got some good news: Superwall now supports Android revenue tracking 🥳! That means Superwall users have access to best-in-class charts, reporting and analytics for iOS *and* Android apps. And, yes, it's still absolutely free. We're launching this as a private beta, but we welcome anyone to join if you're on Android.


Before today, iOS devs had access to rich subscription analytics inside Superwall. They've been able to keep tabs on things like ARR, MRR, or active subs. Diving into cohort-based proceeds and viewing churn predictions is easy on iOS, too. Now? Android teams get those same exact insights.


👉[Get started following these steps](https://superwall.com/docs/dashboard/dashboard-settings/overview-settings-revenue-tracking-google-play) .


### In-depth analytics for Android apps


If you're running a cross-platform app, your Android revenue data used to be siloed or worse, invisible. But now, once you enable Google Play revenue tracking, you'll get full visibility into how your Android app is performing across critical metrics. Set it up once, and your overview metrics will be front and center:


Superwall's Overview metrics are now available on Android.


Plus, that also means Superwall's revenue charts unlock, too. And they're getting better all the time. Recently, we[released five powerful charts](https://superwall.com/blog/introducing-5-powerful-new-charts-arr-mrr-active-subscribers-auto-renew) , and all of them are now open to Android:


- 📈 Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
- 📊 Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- 🧾 Active Subscriptions
- 🔁 Auto Renew Status
- 💰 Cohorted Proceeds


And like all Superwall charts, you can segment by user properties, break down by paywall, campaign, country, and more. Superwall has always been the best way to test paywalls, and now it's also your full-service monetization engine complete with the revenue data you need to make the best decisions.


### Real-time Android insights, no-code integration


To set this up, just head to your Android app's Superwall dashboard. Then, head to **Settings → Revenue Tracking** , and plug in your package name and JSON key. To get that information, we've got a[step-by-step guide in our docs](https://superwall.com/docs/dashboard/dashboard-settings/overview-settings-revenue-tracking-google-play) . Once that's done, you can start enjoying Android revenue data without an app update or SDK integration required.


Revenue tracking setup is found within settings for your Android app.


Of course, if you want the best analytics experience and even more granular breakdowns (like which paywall drove revenue for which user), then be sure to pair it with our SDK, and you'll unlock the entire picture.


### A few tips and tricks


To see what's possible with Android revenue tracking, here are some ideas to get your data-driven juices flowing:


- See how many Android users are about to churn vs. auto-renew.
- Attribute Android subscription revenue to specific Superwall campaigns.
- Filter MRR by product ID, country, or even user-generated attributes.
- Track true ROI on Android ad spend using our Cohorted Proceeds chart.
