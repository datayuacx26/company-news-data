# Superwall's New Pricing: More Aligned, Generous & Transparent

This change means we'll likely take a ~20% haircut in MRR, and we're totally fine with that. Why? Because it makes us more aligned with you, the builders growing sustainable subscription apps.

- ✅ Free Unlimited Integrations

## Introducing: Monthly Attributed Revenue


Instead of billing for every dollar you generate (MTR), Superwall only bills for money we help you generate with an actual Superwall paywall. We're calling this **Monthly Attributed Revenue (MAR)** . This means:


- If you come over to Superwall with existing revenue, we will never charge you for any of it
- You can use Superwall for purchasing only (no Superwall paywalls) and get unlimited charts, webhooks, and integrations, no questions asked, no credit card needed.
- You can use Superwall with your own native paywalls, and only pay for what you use


## The "Indie" Plan — 100% Free up to $10K in MAR


If you want to use Superwall paywalls, campaigns, targeting, or experiments, these are 100% FREE up to $10K in Monthly Attributed Revenue (MAR) — revenue directly driven by Superwall paywalls.


Once you cross $10K MAR, we take a 1% fee on total MAR.


That's 4× more generous than leading competitors.


Our thinking here is simple. We don't want to charge you on total app revenue or vanity metrics: only on what we actually help generate.
