# Introducing: Surveys & Back-to-Back Paywalls

Your feedback keeps us evolving, and today we have some exciting Superwall updates for you. From capturing real-time feedback on paywalls to enhanced event chaining, there's a lot to explore. Let's get started!


## Feedback in real-time with Surveys


Unravel the mystery behind users bypassing your paywall. With Surveys, you can now capture direct feedback when users opt to close your paywall. It's not just about gathering insights to inform product decisions, but also a dynamic opportunity to present another paywall based on the` survey_response` trigger.


## Boost conversions with event-driven campaigns


Leverage the` transaction_abandon` and` paywall_decline` events to turn user actions into golden opportunities. These aren't just indicators of user choices — they're the building blocks for crafting dynamic campaign sequences. By chaining these events to other campaigns, you increase the odds of turning interactions into conversions.


## Mastering Superwall on iOS


A comprehensive tutorial on integrating Superwall into iOS apps covers everything from initial setup to advanced optimization techniques.


## Enhanced debugging capabilities


Debugging improvements now ensure the` paywallPresentationRequest` event includes all necessary on-device inputs used in rule evaluations, eliminating guesswork about paywall display decisions.
