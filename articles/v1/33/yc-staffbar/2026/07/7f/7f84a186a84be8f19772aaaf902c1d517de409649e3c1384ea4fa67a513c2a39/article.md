# New Pricing & Superwall's Manifesto

## Our manifesto 🧘


AWS gives you the power to scale. It reduces your barrier to build an app like Facebook from "can you operate data centers?" to "can you apply for a credit card?".


The App Store gives you the power of distribution. It reduces your barrier to build viral software from "can you build the new iPhone?" to "can you get past app review?".


Superwall gives you the power to monetize. It reduces your barrier to build a business from "can you make money?" to "can you design a good product?".


Why is this so important?


It matters because today's reward system is broken. It rewards apps with the best monetization – not the best product, and that is a shame.


> It's a shame everyone has Apple's distribution, but only the best monetized apps are in everyone's pockets.


It's Superwall's mission to change that.


We promise to give you super powers so you can focus on honing your craft and challenging the status quo. We build for you, so you can build for them.


- It's why we help[Citizen](https://citizen.com/) fight crime.
- It's why we help[PhotoRoom](https://www.photoroom.com/) sell online.
- It's why we help[Polycam](https://poly.cam/) build homes.
- It's why we help[Mojo](https://mojo-app.com/) tell stories.
- It's why we help[Yubo](https://www.yubo.live/) make friends.
- It's why we help[Hornet](https://hornet.com/) find dates.
- It's why we help[Rapchat](https://rapchat.com/) write music.
- It's why we help[Backbone](https://playbackbone.com/app/) have fun.


It's why we'll help you change the world, too.


## New pricing


Today concludes SuperWeek, our week-long event where we released a brand new feature every single day. Our last reveal is not so much a feature, but a gift.


Starting today, your first 250 paywall conversions/month are 100% free of charge. This small step makes good on our mission to empower developers – regardless of their budget – to build huge businesses. Because we believe in the power of great products, not just deep pockets.


Welcome to the new era of monetization, where your product and your vision lead the way.
