# Introducing the Superwall Client App for iOS

Today, we're happy to introduce the Superwall client app for iOS. You can download it[here](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/superwall/id6523434157) . Using our app, you can:


- View any of your app's overview data, including proceeds, paywall conversion rates, and more.
- Check out recent transactions like trial starts, direct subscription events, etc.
- Check out data from today, the past week, 90 days and more.
- Use our widgets to view metrics on your Home Screen, Lock Screen or Control Center.
- Check out all of your paywalls and campaigns.
- Or, make a Siri Shortcut using our App Intents to see your app's performance in any way you want.


The Superwall app is the best way to keep tabs on your app's performance on the go. Over time, we'll be adding more functionality to it as well (notifications are coming!). We've been testing this for some time now, and we're proud to bring a native, well-designed app to make your Superwall experience even better.


## Want free analytics and performance metrics on the go? Use Superwall.


This is also a fantastic time to remind everyone that at Superwall, we have 100% free revenue tracking. All developers and companies need a well-designed overview page, charts, and a client app to check on the go — that's not something you should have to pay for.


And with us, you don't.


Get started by signing up for a free Superwall account, and then head to the[revenue tracking](https://superwall.com/docs/overview-settings-revenue-tracking) page to get started with our no-code integration.


## Home Screen. Lock Screen. Control Center. App Intents.


Finally, as with anything we ship, it needs to be dripping with quality, produce a premium user experience, and feel fantastic. To that end, here are two features I personally love in the client app that I think others will enjoy:


- See your metrics on the go with widgets. You can edit them to choose an app and a metric you'd like to see over the previous 24 hours.
- Open the app and say, "Hey Siri, what can I do here?" to see our available App Intents. We'll be sure to add even more of these in the future. Pro tip: Setup an automation inside Shortcuts to get an overview of your app's performance in morning.


All sorts of widgets for Superwall.


Go check it out today! As always, we're open to feedback. Feel free to reach out to us (right within the app, too!) and let us know what you'd like to see in it.
