# Stably AI is Now Backed by Vercel Ventures! 🚀

We're thrilled to announce that Stably AI is now backed by Vercel Ventures!


Last month in San Francisco, we pitched at Vercel AI Demo Day and took the **#1 spot out of 900+ companies** , with judges from Cursor, OpenAI, and Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch. We were honored to hear Guillermo call testing "mission-critical" in the AI era — and see how naturally Stably fits into the Vercel ecosystem.


## Testing in the Age of AI-Assisted Development


In AI-assisted development, testing isn't a final QA checkbox. It's **real-time feedback** for engineers and AI coding agents like Claude Code and Cursor as they ship. And only fully automated testing can power fully automated coding.


That's exactly what we do: **the moment a Vercel preview deployment is created, Stably runs instant, autonomous end-to-end tests** — clicking through the app like a real user, reading the code diff, checking tickets, designs, and docs to catch bugs before they ship.


## What's Next


Most of our users already build on Next.js and rely on Vercel previews — this partnership lets us go even deeper into their workflow. We're excited to share what's coming next.
