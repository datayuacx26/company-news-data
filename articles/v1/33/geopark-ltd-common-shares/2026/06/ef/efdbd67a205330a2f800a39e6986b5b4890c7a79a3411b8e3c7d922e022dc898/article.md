# GeoPark strengthens its road safety culture with positive results in Colombia and Argentina

In observance of World Road Safety Day, GeoPark highlighted the progress achieved in road safety across Colombia and Argentina, reflecting a prevention-focused culture aimed at protecting the lives of employees, contractors, and neighboring communities.


During 2025, GeoPark traveled more than 14.8 million kilometers in Colombia, a distance equivalent to more than 40,000 road trips between Bogotá and Yopal. Thanks to the commitment of its teams and contractors, the Company reduced reported road incidents by 15% compared to the previous year, decreasing from 19 to 16 minor events. GeoPark also completed 349 consecutive days without recordable road incidents and maintained 100% certification of drivers involved in its operations.


In Argentina, where GeoPark assumed operatorship of its assets in Vaca Muerta on October 16, 2025, the Company traveled more than 323,000 kilometers during its first months of activity, a distance equivalent to driving between Neuquén and Buenos Aires more than 280 times. Since the start of operations, GeoPark has achieved 90 days without recordable road incidents, reflecting a strong safety culture that supports every stage of its growth.


These results reflect GeoPark’s commitment to risk identification, safe driving practices, and responsible decision-making before, during, and after every journey.


“At GeoPark, we understand that Creating Value and Giving Back also means protecting lives on the road. The results achieved in Colombia and Argentina reflect a safety culture built through responsible decisions made every day by our employees, contractors, and partners to protect themselves, their colleagues, and the communities with whom we share the roads,” said Carlos Gómez, GeoPark’s Health and Safety Manager.


The Company will continue promoting awareness, training, and prevention initiatives to strengthen safe mobility in the territories where it operates.
