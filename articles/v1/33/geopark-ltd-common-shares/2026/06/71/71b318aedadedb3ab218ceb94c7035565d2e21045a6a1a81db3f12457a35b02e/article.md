# GeoPark is one of the most attractive companies to work for in the energy sector, according to Merco Talento 2026

GeoPark was included in the Merco Talento 2026 ranking as one of the most attractive companies to work for in the ‘Oil & Gas and Hydrocarbon Distribution’ sector. In the overall ranking, the Company placed 134th, representing an advance of 40 positions compared to the 2025 results.


This result is part of the 17th edition of Merco Talento, one of the country’s leading reputation and employer attractiveness monitors, which evaluates organizations’ ability to attract and retain talent based on the perception of different stakeholder groups.


“Since GeoPark’s founding in 2002, people have been at the center of our business. This recognition from Merco Talento shows that we are moving in the right direction, building a differentiated value proposition that has allowed us to bring together the best team. Thank you to our employees who, from the Llanos in Colombia to Neuquén in Argentina, live our cultural principles, foster an environment of respect, and enthusiastically embrace the opportunity to be part of our company’s growth,” said Agustina Wisky, Chief People Officer at GeoPark.


For this edition, Merco gathered input from employees, university students, human resources experts, unions, academics, headhunters, job seekers, and the general public through more than 75,000 surveys, with the purpose of identifying the most highly regarded organizations in terms of workplace quality, employer brand, and internal reputation.


GeoPark promotes an employee value proposition based on skills development, profit participation, and access to benefits that contribute to their well-being and professional growth. 100% of its employees have the opportunity to become GeoPark shareholders.


Those interested in learning about job opportunities and joining GeoPark’s talent community can register at jobs.geo-park.com.


**ABOUT GEOPARK**


GeoPark (NYSE: GPRK) is an independent oil and gas exploration and production company with over 20 years of operations in Latin America and a proven track record of value creation. Its two-pillar strategy aims to maximize its core platform in Colombia, where it is one of the leading operators, and to build a new long-term growth engine in Vaca Muerta, one of the world’s most important unconventional reservoirs.
