# Pyka’s Autonomous DropShip Completes First Flight

### **Pyka’s Autonomous DropShip Completes First Flight**


*Group 3+ autonomous aircraft for contested logistics and multi-mission operations, built on a proven commercial UAS platform, achieves concept to flight in 6 months ‍*


**Alameda, California — April 27, 2026 —** Pyka today announced the successful first flight of DropShip, its next-generation heavy-lift autonomous aircraft built for contested logistics and multi-mission operations. From concept to flight in just six months, this program sets a new benchmark for rapid development of advanced autonomous systems, delivering the mission-critical capabilities required today. It is engineered to extend reach, resilience, and payload modularity in austere environments.


This rapid six-month timeline was fueled by Pyka’s deep commercial foundation. The company's 1,400 lb MTOW autonomous platform has already logged over 10,000 flights across agriculture and logistics, leveraging years of real-world operating experience to deliver mission-ready autonomy at speed.


“DropShip builds on a technology platform that is already executing high-cycle, industrial missions in extremely demanding conditions with commercial customers,” said Michael Norcia, CEO and Co-Founder of Pyka. “Our focus throughout its development has been rapid iteration, manufacturability, and real-world performance. This first flight shows how quickly we can extend our existing technology to new missions.” **Multi-Mission Performance in a Cost-Effective Package**
DropShip delivers exceptional carrying capacity, range, and endurance for the missions that matter in contested and austere environments. Its hybrid propulsion system, modular open architecture, and precision airdrop capability give operators the flexibility to adapt rapidly across logistics, sustainment, and evolving mission sets. Simplified maintenance, containerized transport, and streamlined training enable autonomous mass at scale on accelerated timelines at a much lower cost structure than comparable platforms. **Proven Technology, Rapid Deployment**
DropShip incorporates core technologies already proven in commercial deployments of Pyka’s commercial fleet, including proprietary autonomous flight software, electric propulsion, and flight control systems. The aircraft’s design is informed by real-world operational data from Pyka’s first-generation cargo platform, which brings established commercial cargo pedigree and multiple DoW operational exercises in contested logistics scenarios.


This industrial foundation enables faster fielding, lower program costs, and a more direct path to mission-ready deployment.


**Future Testing and Operational Readiness**
Following this successful first flight, testing will continue to expand the aircraft’s operating envelope. Future trials will validate performance across a range of mission conditions, with a primary focus on reliability and mission flexibility. As the program progresses, Pyka will continue to press to deliver DropShip’s capabilities on a timeline that stresses immediate operational readiness.


**About Pyka** Pyka is America’s leading manufacturer of heavy-lift autonomous aircraft. Pyka’s aircraft are in commercial operation globally with customers across agriculture, logistics and defense. The company’s proprietary technologies are designed for demanding, real-world environments and proven to enhance safety, efficiency, and reliability in critical missions worldwide.
