# Velos accepted to NVIDIA Inception Program

Excited to announce that Velos has been accepted to NVIDIA Inception, a startup accelerator program connecting breakthrough AI companies with[NVIDIA](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nvidia/) 's cutting-edge tech ecosystem.
The program is designed to help startups accelerate technical innovation and business growth. Inception supports members of its global community with valuable benefits from NVIDIA and partners. The program’s benefits are tailored for startups and include technical tools and training for AI developers, preferred pricing on NVIDIA hardware and software, and opportunities to connect with NVIDIA’s network of venture capital firms.


Velos plans to use NVIDIA NIM to serve document understanding models with low-latency, high-throughput inference, and NVIDIA GPU infrastructure to train and fine-tune proprietary insurance-specific models. We're excited to leverage NVIDIA's technology landscape to accelerate accuracy and expansion into new document types and lines of business.
