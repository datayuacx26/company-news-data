# Velos named to Fintech Global 2025 Insurtech 100

We’re excited to share that Velos has been named to[FinTech Global’s InsurTech100](https://fintech.global/insurtech100/) list for 2025, recognizing the most promising and innovative startups transforming insurance.


Selected from over 2,100 applicants, InsurTech 10, reflects the leading edge of innovation across underwriting, distribution, claims, and data infrastructure. For Velos, this recognition reinforces a simple belief: **insurance operations don’t have to be manual, brittle, or slow — and AI can finally change that.**


## Why it matters for insurance


P&C carriers, MGAs, and TPAs are under intense pressure to do more with less. Teams are buried in documents, emails, spreadsheets, and exception-heavy workflows that were never designed to scale. Highly skilled operators spend their days moving data between systems, reviewing forms by hand, and chasing down missing information — instead of focusing on decisions that actually move the business forward.


Velos was built to solve this problem. We created an AI-native automation platform that can understand unstructured insurance data, execute complex workflows end-to-end, and adapt when real-world variability shows up — without requiring brittle rules or armies of engineers.
