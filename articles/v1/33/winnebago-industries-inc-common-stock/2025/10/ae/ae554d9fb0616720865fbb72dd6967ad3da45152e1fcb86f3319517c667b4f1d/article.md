# Two iconic brands, one iconic game - Winnebago and McDonald's team up for the return of the MONOPOLY® Game


Winnebago is teaming up with McDonald’s as a prize partner to bring back the iconic MONOPOLY® Game at McDonald’s* after nearly a decade. From **October 6 through November 2** , McDonald's is offering chances to win1 two adventurous prizes that help you explore the great outdoors, including a ****[2026 Winnebago® View® 24D RV Motorhome](https://winnebago.com/models/product/motorhomes/class-c/view/2026-24d) 2 and a[2026 Winnebago® Thrive® 18FBS Travel Trailer](https://www.winnebago.com/models/thrive/18fbs) **3.**


“Winnebago has participated as a prize partner previously in our 67-year history, and we are thrilled to be back to provide prizes for the 2025 McDonald’s MONOPOLY® Game — a beloved tradition that brings excitement and nostalgia to millions,” said Amber Holm, Winnebago Industries’ Chief Marketing and Experience Officer.


**How to play**


For the first time, McDonald’s is inviting customers to collect properties and instantly win prizes with both physical and digital game pieces. Players can register to play the MONOPOLY® Game at McDonald’s, reveal and redeem prizes and digitally collect properties in the McDonald’s app. Get the McDonald’s app today to learn more and register to play!
**Your outdoor adventure starts here:**


Here’s how you can get a chance to win a new Winnebago RV:


- **Collect to win:** Collect **Park Place (#821)** and **Boardwalk (#822)** to win a prize of a ****[2026 Winnebago® View® 24D RV Motorhome](https://winnebago.com/models/product/motorhomes/class-c/view/2026-24d) 2 **.**

Good luck!
