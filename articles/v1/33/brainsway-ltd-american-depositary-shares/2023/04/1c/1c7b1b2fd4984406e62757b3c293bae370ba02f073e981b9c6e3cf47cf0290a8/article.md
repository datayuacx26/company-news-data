# Is There a Cure for OCD?

What is treatment-resistant depression?


## Medication for OCD


Only five medications have been FDA-approved over the last 35 years to treat OCD, yet 40% to 60% of patients fail to achieve a response from them. It also takes a significant period of time -- eight to 12 weeks on average -- to see if there will be a response.


In addition, many patients have difficulty tolerating the higher doses needed to treat OCD.
