# Xtant Medical to Participate in the H.C. Wainwright 27th Annual Global Investment Conference

BELGRADE, Mont., Sept. 4, 2025 /PRNewswire/ — **Xtant Medical Holdings, Inc.** (NYSE American: XTNT), a global medical technology company focused on surgical solutions for the treatment of spinal, orthopedic, and woundcare disorders, today announced that Sean Browne, CEO, and Scott Neils, CFO, will be participating in the H.C. Wainwright 27th Annual Global Investment Conference
, which is being held September 8-10, 2025
, in New York
and virtually.


A company presentation will be available on demand[here](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4501961-1&h=3486890544&u=https%3A%2F%2Fjourney.ct.events%2Fview%2F099ddf07-0cfd-43e7-b98f-ca41f0bc9d7f&a=here) as of Friday, September 5 at 7:00 A.M. (ET). In addition, Xtant’s management team will be available for virtual one-on-one meetings on September 8th. Interested investors should contact their representative at H.C. Wainwright. A replay of the presentation will be posted to the[Presentation & Events](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4501961-1&h=1901191657&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.xtantmedical.com%2Fevents&a=Presentation+%26+Events) page of Xtant’s Investors website for 90 days.
