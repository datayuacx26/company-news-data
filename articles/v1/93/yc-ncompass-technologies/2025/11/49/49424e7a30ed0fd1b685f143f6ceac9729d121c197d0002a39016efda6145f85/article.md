# About the Bug Reports category

Report bugs or unexpected behavior in nCompass. Include clear steps to reproduce and relevant logs or screenshots.


---


This category is for reporting bugs, crashes, or unexpected behavior in any nCompass product or open-source project.


**Why use this category?**
Use it to let the team and community know about issues you’ve found so they can be tracked, verified, and fixed.


**How is it different?**
Unlike *Support* , which is for usage questions or setup help, *Bug Reports* focuses only on confirmed or suspected defects.


**What should topics here contain?**


- A clear, concise title describing the issue
- Steps to reproduce (include code snippets or commands)
- Expected vs. actual behavior
- Environment details (OS, version, cluster setup, etc.)
- Logs, screenshots, or error traces if available


If your issue relates to our open-source SDK or tools, please also open an issue on[GitHub](https://github.com/nCompass-tech/ncompass) .
For private or account-related issues, emailsupport@ncompass.tech .


**Do we need this category?**
Yes. It separates verified bugs from general support questions and keeps a clean record of issues for both the community and maintainers.
