# About the Feature Requests category

Suggest and discuss new features or improvements for nCompass products and tools. Share ideas that would make your workflow better.


---


This category is for proposing new capabilities, enhancements, or integrations you’d like to see in nCompass. If you prefer, you can also reach out to us directly at` support@ncompass.tech` or raise it on our[open source repository](https://github.com/nCompass-tech/ncompass) .


**Why use this category?**
Use it to share feature ideas, vote on others’ suggestions, and help guide our product roadmap based on real developer needs.


**How is it different?**
Unlike *Bug Reports* , which deals with existing behavior, *Feature Requests* focuses on future improvements and new functionality.


**What should topics here contain?**


- A clear description of the requested feature or improvement
- The problem it solves or use case it supports
- Example scenarios or workflows that would benefit
- Any related links, screenshots, or prototypes


**Do we need this category?**
Yes. It provides a structured space for community feedback and helps us prioritize development based on user impact.
