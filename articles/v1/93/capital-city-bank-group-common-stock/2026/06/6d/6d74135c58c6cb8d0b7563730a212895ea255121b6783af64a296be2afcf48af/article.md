# Capital City Investments Adds Financial Advisor Matt Edwards To Serve Tallahassee Families


[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_2e304d963607ff5eb88a48ebb09e1002/ccbg/news/2026-06-18_Capital_City_Investments_Adds_Financial_Advisor_236.pdf) June 18, 2026 12:35pm EDT


**TALLAHASSEE, Fla., June 18, 2026 —** Capital City Investments, a division of Capital City Wealth, announces[Matt Edwards](https://financialadvisors.ccbg.com/fl/tallahassee/matt-edwards.html) has joined its team of financial advisors to help individuals and families across Tallahassee and the North Florida region navigate long-term financial planning and retirement strategies with confidence.


“Edwards works closely with clients to develop personalized financial strategies focused on retirement planning and wealth management. Known for his relationship-driven approach, he helps clients simplify complex financial decisions and create plans tailored to their personal goals and evolving needs.


“Financial Planning should be personal, local and rooted in trust,” said Don May, regional market executive for North Florida and South Georgia. “Matt’s experience, analytical mindset and commitment to helping clients make informed decisions strengthens his ability to serve families throughout the Tallahassee region.”


Before joining Capital City Investments, Edwards served as a[financial advisor](https://financialadvisors.ccbg.com/) in Thomasville, Georgia, where he provided personalized[wealth management](https://www.ccbg.com/wealth/wealth-management) services and[financial advice for retirement planning](https://www.ccbg.com/wealth/our-services) guidance to individuals and families pursuing long-term financial stability.


He is active in the Rotary Club of Thomasville, where he serves as chairman of the organization’s “Big Impact Project,” supporting local fresh produce distribution efforts in partnership with Second Harvest of Thomasville. Edwards has been married for 23 years and is the father of two daughters. Outside of work, he enjoys golf, hunting, fishing and music, including recording original music.


To learn more about Matt Edwards, visit[financialadvisors.ccbg.com](https://financialadvisors.ccbg.com/)
