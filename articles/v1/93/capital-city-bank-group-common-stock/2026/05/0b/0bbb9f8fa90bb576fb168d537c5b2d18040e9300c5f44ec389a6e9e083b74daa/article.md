# Capital City Bank Expands Tallahassee Presence with Business Banker Willie Correa

# Capital City Bank Expands Tallahassee Presence with Business Banker Willie Correa


[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_2e304d963607ff5eb88a48ebb09e1002/ccbg/news/2026-05-14_Capital_City_Bank_Expands_Tallahassee_Presence_234.pdf) May 14, 2026 8:00am EDT


**TALLAHASSEE, Fla., May 13, 2026** –[Capital City Bank](http://www.ccbg.com/) announced the addition of[Willie Correa](https://bankers.ccbg.com/fl/tallahassee/willie-correa.html) as a business banker, strengthening its commitment to serving business owners across Leon County and the greater Tallahassee region. With more than 20 years of Tallahassee-based experience in commercial lending, business banking and relationship management, Correa supports local business owners with customized lending solutions, deposit services and cash management strategies designed to help businesses grow and succeed.


“Business owners in the Tallahassee region need a banking partner who understands the local market and delivers responsive, practical financial solutions,” said Don May, regional market executive for North Florida and South Georgia. “Willie’s extensive experience in commercial banking, combined with his strong community ties and relationship-focused approach, enhances our ability to serve clients and support continued growth across the region.”


Correa is a relationship-driven business banker and commercial lender who helps businesses grow through personalized banking strategies, commercial lending support and business development opportunities. Fluent in Spanish, he works closely with clients seeking[lenders for business loans](https://www.ccbg.com/business/business-loans-lines-of-credit) as well as those looking to establish a


[business bank account](https://www.ccbg.com/business/business-services) and build long-term financial relationships.


Prior to joining Capital City Bank, Correa spent more than 20 years in the banking and financial services industry, holding roles of increasing responsibility in commercial lending, business banking and relationship management. His experience includes structuring lending solutions, managing deposit relationships and supporting ongoing credit needs, including renewals and portfolio growth. Throughout his career, he has contributed to the development of business banking teams and helped drive initiatives focused on expanding commercial client relationships and service offerings.


An active member of the Tallahassee community, Correa has served as president of Capital Action and Catholic Charities. He has also coached youth baseball in Tallahassee for more than 20 years. His long-standing community involvement reflects his commitment to building strong relationships and supporting local businesses.


Correa is based at the Capital City Bank Bradfordville Road Office at 6691 Thomasville Road. More information is available at[bankers.ccbg.com/fl/tallahassee/willie-correa.html](https://bankers.ccbg.com/fl/tallahassee/willie-correa.html) .


Founded in 1895 and headquartered in Tallahassee, Florida, Capital City Bank provides a full range of consumer, wealth management, business and commercial banking services. The Bank has repeatedly been selected best bank or financial institution in multiple communities across its footprint, including Tallahassee, Crawfordville, the Emerald Coast and Palatka, Florida, and Cairo, Georgia. Additionally, the Company has been chosen for Florida Trend ** magazine’s “Best Companies to Work For in Florida” 14 years in a row and the American Banker magazine’s “Best Banks to Work For” every year since the list’s inception in 2013.
