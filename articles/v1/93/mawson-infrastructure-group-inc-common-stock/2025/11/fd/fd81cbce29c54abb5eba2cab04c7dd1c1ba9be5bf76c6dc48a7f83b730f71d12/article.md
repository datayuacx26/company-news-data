# Mawson Infrastructure Group Inc. Announces Monthly Financial Update for October 2025

**MIDLAND, Pa., Nov. 25, 2025** -- Mawson Infrastructure Group Inc. (NASDAQ: MIGI) (“Mawson” or the “Company”), a U.S.-based technology company that designs, builds, and operates next-generation digital infrastructure platforms providing services to the artificial intelligence (AI), high-performance computing (HPC), digital assets (including Bitcoin mining), and other intensive compute applications market sectors, announced today its unaudited revenue results for the month of October 2025.


Kaliste Saloom, Interim CEO of Mawson, said, "At Mawson, transparency is central to our approach, and we remain committed to sharing both our progress and our challenges. While October’s financial results reflect a transitional period, we are energized by the strategic steps we’ve taken to position the Company for growth. The extension of our Bellefonte lease provides a strong foundation for future operations, and our previously announced GPU pilot program is advancing as planned. This initiative is a critical component of our strategy to diversify beyond Bitcoin mining and expand into HPC and AI workloads — markets that we believe represent significant long-term opportunities. These developments reinforce our confidence in Mawson’s ability to adapt and thrive. We look forward to building on this momentum and are working to deliver improved performance in the months ahead."


**October 2025 Unaudited Revenue**


- Total monthly revenue was $3.3 million, down 30% Y/Y from October 2024 and down 36% M/M from September 2025.
- Digital colocation revenue was $1.6 million, down 59% Y/Y from October 2024 and down 56% M/M from September 2025.
- Energy management revenue was $1.6 million, up 191% Y/Y from October 2024 and up 29% M/M from September 2025.
- Digital assets mining revenue from self-mining operations was $0.1 million, down 55% Y/Y from October 2024 and down 62% M/M from September 2025.


**About Mawson Infrastructure**


Mawson is a U.S.-based technology company that designs, builds, and operates next-generation digital infrastructure platforms. The company provides services spanning AI, HPC, digital assets (including Bitcoin mining), and other intensive compute applications. Mawson delivers both self-mining operations and colocation/hosting for enterprise customers, with a vertically integrated infrastructure model built for scalability and efficiency.


A core part of Mawson’s strategy is powering its operations with carbon-free energy resources—including nuclear power—ensuring that its compute platforms support the rapid growth of the digital economy in an environmentally sustainable way. With 129 megawatts of capacity already online and more under development, Mawson is positioning itself as a competitive provider of carbon-aware digital infrastructure solutions.
