# Mawson Infrastructure Group Inc. to Present at the Sidoti Winter Smallcap Virtual Investor Conference January 20th, 2022

**Sydney, Australia and New York City, USA — January 12th, 2021** — Mawson Infrastructure Group Inc. (NASDAQ:MIGI) (“Mawson”), a digital infrastructure provider, is pleased to announce it will present at the Sidoti Winter Smallcap Virtual Investor Conference being held January 19-20, 2022. **Event** : Sidoti Winter Smallcap Virtual Investor Conference **Location** : Virtual **Link** : https://sidoti.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_r8P2AwYjRaqQq4EAPP5cFQ **Date** : Thursday, January 20th, 2022 8:30am ET **Format** : Presentation and 1:1 investor meetings. Mawson management will also be available for 1:1 investor meetings, available through the Sidoti platform. For more information or to register for this event, please visit[https://www.sidoti.com/events/sidoti-winter-virtual-small-cap-investor-conference](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.sidoti.com%2Fevents%2Fsidoti-winter-virtual-small-cap-investor-conference&esheet=52561805&newsitemid=20220112005645&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.sidoti.com%2Fevents%2Fsidoti-winter-virtual-small-cap-investor-conference&index=2&md5=ed18a62e5484463f6e8422ee27d38948) . About Mawson Infrastructure Mawson Infrastructure Group (NASDAQ: MIGI) is a digital infrastructure provider, with multiple operations throughout the USA and Australia. Mawson’s vertically integrated model is based on a long-term strategy to promote the global transition to the new digital economy. Mawson matches sustainable energy infrastructure with next-generation mobile data centre (MDC) solutions, enabling low-cost Bitcoin production and on-demand deployment of infrastructure assets. With a strong focus on shareholder returns and an aligned board and management, Mawson Infrastructure Group is emerging as a global leader in ESG focused Bitcoin mining and digital infrastructure. For more information, visit:[www.mawsoninc.com](https://mawsoninc.com/) .
