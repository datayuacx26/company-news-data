# Mawson Infrastructure Group Inc. to Present at the 2024 Gateway Conference in San Francisco, CA

**MIDLAND, Pa., Aug. 06, 2024** (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Mawson Infrastructure Group Inc. (NASDAQ: MIGI) (“Mawson” or “the Company”), a publicly traded digital infrastructure company, announced that it will be presenting at the[2024 Annual Gateway Conference](https://www.gateway-grp.com/conference) , which is being held September 4-5 in San Francisco, CA.


***Mawson's CEO and President, Rahul Mewawalla** , is scheduled to present and speak on Thursday, September 5th at 10:00 a.m. Pacific Time (PT) or 1:00 p.m. Eastern Time (ET)* . For additional information, please emailIR@mawsoninc.com .


The 2024 Annual Gateway conference plans to bring together selected growth companies with investors, analysts, partners, and other capital markets constituents across sectors such as technology, cleantech, consumer, industrials, and financial services. Senior leadership and company executives from over 100 invite-only companies will be joining investors and analysts at this conference. For the list of invited CEOs and companies, please see[https://www.gateway-grp.com/conference](https://www.gateway-grp.com/conference) .


**About Mawson Infrastructure Group, Inc.**


Mawson Infrastructure Group (NASDAQ: MIGI) is building the next generation digital infrastructure platform. Our innovation, technology, and operational expertise enables us to operate and optimize digital infrastructure to accelerate the digital economy including the growth of digital assets, artificial intelligence, and other high-performance computing solutions using a carbon-free energy approach using a carbon-free energy approach.
