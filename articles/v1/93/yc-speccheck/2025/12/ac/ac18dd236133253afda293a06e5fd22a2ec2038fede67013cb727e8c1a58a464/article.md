# HOYA Vision Care Joins the SpecCheck Lab Network!

### **HOYA Vision Care Becomes the First Lab Network to Offer SpecCheck’s Modern Order Management Platform**


We’re thrilled to announce a major milestone for the optical industry: **HOYA Vision Care** is now the first major lab network in the country to offer SpecCheck’s modern online order management platform to their eye care professional customers. This partnership represents a significant step forward in expanding access to smarter, more connected ordering technology, giving practices across the country a unified way to manage their work with greater clarity and control.


[Click here to read our official press release.](https://www.speccheckrx.com/hoya-press-release-12.3.25)


### **A Shared Commitment to Independence**


HOYA has long championed the success of independent eye care professionals. By implementing SpecCheck’s unified order management solution, HOYA is delivering on that commitment and expanding access to modern, intuitive technology for practices nationwide.


This collaboration brings together **HOYA’s industry-leading lens manufacturing network** and **SpecCheck’s all-in-one ordering dashboard** , giving eye care professionals a streamlined, accurate, and future-ready workflow. From consolidated ordering to real-time status tracking, the partnership equips practices with tools that help them work more efficiently while maintaining independence in an increasingly complex landscape.


### **Getting Started Is Easy**


HOYA customers can begin using SpecCheck today! If you’re new to SpecCheck, you can easily[create a free account](https://dashboard.speccheckrx.com/?screen_hint=signup) . If you’re already using SpecCheck, you can connect your HOYA lab account using our[step-by-step guide](https://support.speccheckrx.com/en/articles/9151292-add-a-new-lab-partner) .


And as always, if you have any questions, our[support team](https://www.speccheckrx.com/contact) is here to help every step of the way.
