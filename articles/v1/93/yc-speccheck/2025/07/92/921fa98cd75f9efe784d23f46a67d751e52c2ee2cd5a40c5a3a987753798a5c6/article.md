# One-Click Redos & Multiple Pairs - Now For All Orders!

## **🆕 What’s New**


We just released one of our most requested features: **the ability to redo or duplicate *any order* , even if it wasn’t originally submitted through SpecCheck.** This update makes SpecCheck the most reliable, efficient, and easy way to submit redos and multiple pair orders to your lab partners!


To take advantage of this new feature, **please use the 'Redo Order' or 'Duplicate Order' buttons** found on the Order Details page of the original order. This will ensure that all available data is automatically imported. Please note: if the original order wasn’t submitted through SpecCheck, some data may be missing, but we’ll always import everything we can!


## **💡 Why We Did It**


While we understand that SpecCheck doesn't currently support submission for all order types (e.g. insurance orders), this update makes processing redo and multiple pair orders faster and easier, regardless of how the original order was submitted.[Log in now to try it out!](https://dashboard.speccheckrx.com/)
