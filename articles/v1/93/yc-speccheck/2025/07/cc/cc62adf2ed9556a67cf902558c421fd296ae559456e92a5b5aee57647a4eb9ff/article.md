# Introducing the Patient Order Tracker

## **Order Updates For Patients. On Your Terms.**


How many times a day does your front desk hear the same question: “Are my glasses ready yet?” Every call is an interruption, pulling your staff away from patients in front of them and adding more to-dos to your already full plate.


The **Patient Order Tracker** , exclusively included with[SpecCheck Pro](https://support.speccheckrx.com/en/articles/11029789-speccheck-plan-comparison) , lets your patients access simple order updates that you control from our HIPAA compliant online portal. Patients won’t see lab production statuses, and you always control when they're notified that their order is ready for pickup. This new feature:


✅ Reduces inbound “where's my order?” calls
✅ Works with all jobs (even insurance and non-SpecCheck orders)
✅ Requires only a patient's email
✅ Built to be simple, smart, and fully in your hands


Let patients stay informed and confident in your service, while your team stays focused on care. It’s the kind of upgrade patients notice, and tell their friends about.[Learn more here](https://support.speccheckrx.com/en/articles/11818553-patient-order-tracker) .
