# Introducing Your New Order Creation Experience: Faster, Smarter, and More Intuitive

## **🆕 What’s Changed**


The entire order creation experience has been redesigned and streamlined into a **single-page layout** , eliminating the need to click through multiple screens. Everything is now organized in one intuitive view, complete with a live progress bar that guides you step-by-step.


We’ve also **standardized the order submission process** to be the same across all order types. Whether you’re ordering a standard Single Vision lens or a specialty design, every order follows the same consistent process, making SpecCheck easier to navigate and faster to use than any other Rx ordering tool.


## **💡 Why We Did It**


This update was primarily **driven by your feedback** ! By introducing these changes, we’ve made our ordering tool faster and more intuitive. It also lays the foundation for future enhancements coming soon to SpecCheck.
