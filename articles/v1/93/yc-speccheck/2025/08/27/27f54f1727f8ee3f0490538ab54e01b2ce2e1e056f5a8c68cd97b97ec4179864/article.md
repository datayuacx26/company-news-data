# Introducing Advanced Practice Reporting

## **Your Data Has Never Been This Powerful**


Say goodbye to tedious spreadsheets and waiting on lab reports.[Advanced Practice Reporting](https://support.speccheckrx.com/en/articles/11029789-why-upgrade-to-speccheck-pro#h_45011dc944) — available exclusively with **SpecCheck Pro** — puts your complete order history at your fingertips for *all orders* with your lab partners, not just those placed through SpecCheck. This new feature includes:


📈 **Overview Dashboard** – See top products, order volume, redo %, and more
🔍 **Deep Dive** – Explore every detail of every order, apply custom filters, and date ranges
😎 **Product Utilization** – Track trends for lenses, coatings, materials, and add-ons over time
📦 **Invoice Checker** – Instantly find invoices and shipment details
⚡ **Performance Metrics** – Measure redo reasons, turnaround time, and AR/Photochromic sales


Harness the power of your data to make smarter business decisions, identify growth opportunities, and streamline operations. Learn more about Advanced Practice Reporting[here](https://support.speccheckrx.com/en/articles/11909671-advanced-practice-reporting) .
