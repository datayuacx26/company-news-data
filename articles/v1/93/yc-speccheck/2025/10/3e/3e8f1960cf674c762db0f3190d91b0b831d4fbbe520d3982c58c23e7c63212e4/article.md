# New: Delayed Orders

We've all experienced that moment of panic: you hit submit and instantly realize you missed a coat, typo'd the axis, or forgot special instructions. As the most requested new feature from our survey, we are proud to release **Delayed Orders** ! Eliminate stress by giving your office a built-in delay window to catch and fix errors, *before* the job is officially sent to the lab.


The **Order Delay Period** has been set to **5 minutes** by default for all users. While Free users are strictly limited to just this 5 minute delay (or can disable the feature entirely),[SpecCheck Pro](https://support.speccheckrx.com/en/articles/11029789-why-upgrade-to-speccheck-pro) users unlock custom delay periods ranging from 10 minutes up to a full 24 hours!


Configure Delayed Orders by going to **Settings -> Ordering Preferences -> Order Delay Period.**
