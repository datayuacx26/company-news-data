# Introducing My Frames

We’re excited to introduce **My Frames** , now available in your SpecCheck dashboard! My Frames is the fastest and most complete way to manage your frame inventory and add frame details to your Rx orders.


With My Frames, you can:
✅ Create and manage frame records for every frame you carry — including independent brands
✅ Edit or delete frame records anytime to keep your catalog up to date
✅ Quickly add frame details to orders by searching manufacturer, model, color, eye size, or UPC
✅ Build new Rx orders directly from any saved frame record
✅ Attach **.OMA trace files** for precise lens measurements and view A Box, B Box, ED, and Bridge details directly in SpecCheck


### **Access Levels**


- **Pro Users:** Create and manage an **unlimited** number of frame records, attach and view trace files, and use frame records directly when placing Rx orders.
- **Free Users:** Can view the My Frames tab and create **up to 3 frame records** to try it out, but cannot use them when placing orders.


**Upgrading to SpecCheck Pro?** We’ll import your existing frame inventory for you! Send us a .csv of your current list or ask us to preload all frames you’ve ever ordered on SpecCheck.


**Available exclusively on SpecCheck Pro for $39/month per location.**
