# LENS PACKAGES // Fewer Clicks, Faster Orders

**Lens Packages** on SpecCheck make it even easier and faster to place Rx orders. Save your favorite lens combinations, and add them to any Rx order with just one-click! Create your first package now by going to **Settings → Ordering Preferences** , or save a package on the order entry page when you submit your next order.


Free users can create up to two lens packages per lab.[SpecCheck Pro](https://support.speccheckrx.com/en/articles/11029789-why-upgrade-to-speccheck-pro) **users get unlimited lens packages** , making it easy to save all of your favorite combos![Log in now](https://dashboard.speccheckrx.com/) to build your first package today, or[click here](https://support.speccheckrx.com/en/articles/13521272-lens-packages) to learn more about how they work.
