# Platform Updates & Upgraded Rewards Program!

At SpecCheck, our mission has always been to build the most powerful platform in the industry to manage Rx orders. Guided by feedback from over 12,000 practices and lab partners, we are thrilled to roll out major platform enhancements designed to bring maximum value to your daily workflow!


Here is a look at what’s live in your dashboard:


### **🌟 Premium Tools Unlocked for All SpecCheck Orders**


We believe the features that make ordering fast, accurate, and effortless should be available to every practice. Starting today, the following tools are **100% free on every order submitted directly through SpecCheck** , regardless of whether you are on the Free or Pro plan:


- **My Frames:** Build, manage, and create orders using your own frame inventory directly in SpecCheck.
- **Flagged Orders:** Automatically get notified when an order is delayed or needs special attention.
- **Unlimited Lens Packages:** Save commonly ordered lens combinations and apply them with a single click.
- **Unlimited Starred Orders & Internal Notes:** Star priority jobs for automated status email updates and collaborate effortlessly with team notes.
- **Delayed Orders:** Set a custom grace period to hold, edit, or cancel orders before transmission to the lab.


**Please Note:** While external orders (phone, fax, Eyefinity, VisionWeb) will be listed in your dashboard, you’ll need to upgrade to[SpecCheck Pro](https://support.speccheckrx.com/en/articles/11029789-speccheck-plan-comparison) to access these!


### 🤑 **Supercharged Rewards: All Users Can Earn Up to $500/Year!**


Meet the brand-new **SpecCheck Rewards** ! We’ve introduced a point-based system that turns your everyday practice workflows into Visa or Amazon Gift Cards. Here’s all the good stuff now live in your rewards portal:


- **Earn More:** Earn up to **$500 per user annually** (that’s up to $2,500/year in rewards for a 5-person office)!
- **Earn Points on Orders & Payments:** Collect points every single time you place an order on SpecCheck (or via one of our[PMS integrations](https://support.speccheckrx.com/en/collections/19672374-ehr-pms-integrations) ), and when you pay your lab statement using SpecCheck.
- **2x Autopay Bonus:** Automatically **double your points** on every invoice paid using Autopay.
- **One Unified Point Balance:** Points earned across multiple lab partners automatically aggregate into a single balance.
- **Flexible Cash-Outs & New Options:** Redeem your points directly for **Amazon** or **Visa gift cards** whenever you want ($25 minimum per redemption).


Head to your dashboard’s Rewards tab to start earning today!
