## **Understanding the Evolution of Medical Record Retrieval for Legal Assistants**


### **The Challenge of Manual Retrieval**


Traditional medical record retrieval is one of the more time-consuming administrative functions in personal injury law firms. Manual retrieval can take weeks or longer, depending on provider responsiveness, authorization quality, request type, and applicable state and federal rules. A typical car accident case might involve 5 to 10 providers, while a catastrophic injury could require records from 30 or more sources, each needing individual follow-ups, payment processing, and completeness verification. For high-volume firms, tracking requests and following up with providers can consume substantial staff time.


### **The Rise of Digital Solutions**


Research from Clio's Legal Trends report finds that 79% of legal professionals use AI tools, with medical record retrieval emerging as a primary automation target. Modern platforms combine electronic submission, provider network access, AI-powered analysis, and case management integration to transform what was once a manual grind into a streamlined workflow.


This creates a clear market divide: AI-powered platforms working toward records in a couple of weeks versus traditional services that can take considerably longer, and choosing the right solution helps legal assistants reduce errors that delay case preparation.
