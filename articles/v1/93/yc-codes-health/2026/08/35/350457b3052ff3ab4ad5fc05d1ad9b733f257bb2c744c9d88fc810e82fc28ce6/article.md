## **Understanding Medical Records and HIPAA Compliance**


Medical records are central to personal injury litigation because they document diagnoses, treatment, medical history, and claimed damages.


The HIPAA Privacy Rule establishes federal protections for protected health information. For an individual's HIPAA right-of-access request, a covered entity generally must act within 30 calendar days. One extension of up to another 30 days is available when the requester receives timely written notice explaining the delay.


That federal access rule should not be treated as a universal deadline for every subpoena, discovery demand, authorization-based attorney request, or other legal records process.


**Important authorization elements generally include:**


-


Patient-identifying information


-


A description of information to be disclosed


-


Identification of the authorized recipient


-


An expiration date or event


-


Patient or authorized-representative signature and date


-


Applicable authorization and revocation language


Mayo Clinic’s own MC0072-01 incorporates the information necessary for its third-party release process.


For law firms, authorization accuracy matters because errors can cause provider rejection or additional back-and-forth.[Codes Health](https://www.getcodeshealth.com/) is a HIPAA-compliant medical-record retrieval platform that reviews record requests for preventable errors before submission.


## **General Steps to Request Medical Records from Mayo Clinic**


Mayo Clinic provides an enterprise third-party authorization form that applies to Mayo Clinic locations in Arizona, Florida, Rochester, and Mayo Clinic Health System. The form can authorize the release of information from all Mayo Clinic and Mayo Clinic Health System locations.


Learn more about[Mayo medical records](https://www.mayoclinic.org/patient-visitor-guide/medical-records?utm_source=chatgpt.com) .


**Standard request process:**


1.


**Obtain proper authorization:** Complete the applicable Mayo Clinic authorization form.


2.


**Verify patient information:** Make sure patient name, date of birth, Mayo Clinic number if known, and other identifying information are accurate.


3.


**Specify records needed:** List the record types and date ranges requested.


4.


**Identify the recipient:** Provide complete recipient information on the authorization.


5.


**Select delivery method:** Mayo’s form allows options including mail, Patient Portal, fax, email, pickup, CD/DVD, USB, and other specified methods.


6.


**Submit and follow up:** Send the authorization to Mayo Clinic Release of Information and monitor the request.


A government-issued photo ID is not listed by Mayo Clinic as a universal requirement for every standard third-party request. When a representative signs for a patient, however, documentation establishing legal authority may be required.


**Processing timeline expectations:**


-


**Standard request:** Mayo Clinic says requests are typically processed within about two weeks.


-


**HIPAA access request:** Up to 30 calendar days, with one permitted extension of up to 30 additional days when applicable.


-


**Amendment request:** Generally within[60 days](https://mcforms.mayo.edu/mc5200-mc5299/mc5256-03.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com) , with a possible extension under HIPAA.


-


**Patient Portal:** 24/7 access to a selected set of records.

### **Substance Use Disorder Records**


Mayo Clinic’s current MC0072-01 contains a dedicated section for “Substance Abuse and Addiction Treatment Records,” including assessment, history and physical examination, treatment plans, multidisciplinary notes, questionnaires, and treatment or discharge summaries.


These records may also fall under the federal confidentiality protections of 42 CFR Part 2. Updated Part 2 requirements became mandatory on February 16, 2026. HHS explains that Part 2 protects qualifying substance-use-disorder treatment records and imposes particular restrictions on disclosures and their use in legal proceedings.


Law firms requesting these records should make sure the authorization appropriately covers the information sought and should consider whether additional Part 2 consent or court-order requirements apply to the intended legal use.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**


### **How long does it take to get medical records from Mayo Clinic?**


Mayo Clinic says requests are[typically processed](https://www.mayoclinic.org/patient-visitor-guide/medical-records?utm_source=chatgpt.com) within about two weeks. Mayo also notes that federal HIPAA rules allow up to 30 days for applicable access requests. Under HIPAA, one additional 30-day extension may be available when the required written notice is provided.


Amendment requests are generally processed within[60 days](https://mcforms.mayo.edu/mc5200-mc5299/mc5256-03.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com) , subject to a possible extension.


### **Can I request my Mayo Clinic medical records for free?**


Yes. Mayo Clinic states that patients are not charged to receive their own records. Imaging CDs sent by U.S. mail are also free. FedEx delivery of an imaging CD is listed at $45. Mayo notes that reasonable cost-based charges may apply in some other circumstances as permitted by law.


### **What if I need medical records from multiple Mayo Clinic locations?**


Form MC0072-01 lets a requester select Mayo Clinic as the source, which includes all Mayo Clinic and Mayo Clinic Health System locations. For questions, contact[Mayo ROI](https://www.mayoclinic.org/patient-visitor-guide/medical-records?utm_source=chatgpt.com) at 507-284-4594, fax 507-284-0161, or MAYOHIMSROI@mayo.edu.


### **How do I get records for a deceased patient from Mayo Clinic?**


The person requesting records on behalf of a deceased patient must have appropriate legal authority. Mayo Clinic states that the necessary legal documentation may be required when someone signs on the patient's behalf. Contact the Release of Information to determine which documents are necessary for the specific situation.


### **What information is included in a Mayo Clinic record?**


The contents depend on the patient's care and the categories requested. Mayo’s third-party authorization allows requests for provider notes, emergency notes, therapy notes, laboratory results, pathology, imaging reports and images, medications, billing information, and other specified records.
