### **Why PI Firms Look Elsewhere**


The gap is straightforward: CaseFleet does not retrieve medical records from providers. For plaintiff firms where medical records drive case valuation, settlement preparation, and damages documentation, that upstream bottleneck often matters more than downstream organization tools.
