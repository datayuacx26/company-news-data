### **Why Plaintiff Firms Look Elsewhere**


With retrieval now on both sides of the comparison, the question shifts to how deep each platform goes. Plaintiff firms need insights tuned to their specific litigation needs. Breach-of-care detection, future medical expense extraction, pre-existing condition identification, and causation evidence matter more than general document summarization when you're valuing a case or preparing a demand.

Codes Health says that added client involvement is a meaningful driver of churn, and it takes the opposite approach, working toward complete, verified record sets in a couple of weeks.
