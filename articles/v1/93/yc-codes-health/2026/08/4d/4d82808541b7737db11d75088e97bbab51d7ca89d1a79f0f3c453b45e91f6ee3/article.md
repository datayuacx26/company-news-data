-

**Akron General** : Follow Akron General’s dedicated medical-records instructions.

Requested dates of service


-


Specific records requested


-


Recipient information


-

Required signature and date

**Step 4: Attach required documentation**

Cleveland Clinic Florida instructs requesters to include a copy of photo identification with a signature.

Akron General requires photo identification for in-person record pickup.

## **Understanding HIPAA: Your Rights and Data Security**


HIPAA authorization and access rules affect how attorneys and patients obtain protected health information.


### **Patient Rights Under HIPAA**


-


Individuals generally have a HIPAA right to inspect or obtain copies of protected health information in a designated record set, subject to limited exceptions.


-


HIPAA permits reasonable, cost-based fees for access requests. For certain electronic requests, a provider may choose an optional flat-fee method of up to $6.50. The $6.50 figure is not a universal HIPAA fee cap.


-


Cleveland Clinic currently states that electronic records requested through MyChart are free and that emailed copies cost a $6.50 flat fee plus tax.


-


HIPAA authorizations generally identify the information being disclosed, the recipient, and other required authorization elements.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**


### **How long does it typically take to receive medical records from the Cleveland Clinic?**


Cleveland Clinic states that typical medical-record requests take seven to 10 business days, depending on record availability. Formal MyChart record requests can take up to three business days. Cleveland Clinic’s general radiology guidance says to allow approximately 48 hours, while Indian River lists three business days for radiology CDs.


### **Can I request medical records for a family member from the Cleveland Clinic?**


Yes, when the requester has appropriate legal authority. Cleveland Clinic states that a personal representative who signs for a patient may need documentation establishing that authority, such as guardianship or power-of-attorney documents. For deceased patients, estate or probate documentation and a death certificate may be required depending on the circumstances.


### **What specific information is required on a Cleveland Clinic authorization?**


Requirements depend on the applicable facility form. Cleveland Clinic’s standard Ohio authorization requests patient identification information, recipient details, dates of service, requested record categories, purpose of disclosure, and a signature and date. If the medical record number is unknown, the Cleveland Clinic says it can be left blank. Psychotherapy notes require separate authorization.


Florida facilities use their own release forms and instruct requesters to include photo identification with their signature.


### **Are there any fees associated with requesting medical records from the Cleveland Clinic?**


Yes, depending on the request type and delivery method. Cleveland Clinic states that electronic records requested through MyChart are free and that emailed records cost a $6.50 flat fee plus tax. For Ohio third-party requests, the state’s 2026 maximum fee schedule allows a $24.58 search fee, $1.62 per page for the first 10 pages, $0.84 per page for pages 11 through 50, and $0.33 per page after page 50, plus applicable postage.


### **What should I do if my medical records request to the Cleveland Clinic is denied or delayed?**


Review the rejection or deficiency notice, correct any missing information, and resubmit through the appropriate facility channel. If a request has exceeded the facility’s stated processing range, contact the relevant HIM department using the verified contact information above and document the original submission date, confirmation, and follow-up communications.
