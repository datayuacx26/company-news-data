Personal injury intake teams operate under constant pressure to qualify cases quickly while ensuring complete medical documentation. Because most personal injury claims are resolved without trial, the quality and completeness of medical documentation can materially affect negotiation and settlement preparation.


### **The Cost of Delays: Impact on Case Value and Client Trust**


Every week, waiting for records delays case evaluation, demand preparation, and settlement negotiations. For intake teams managing dozens of simultaneous cases, traditional retrieval processes create compounding bottlenecks:


-

-

-


Client frustration that can erode trust


-


Manual follow-ups that consume substantial staff time for volume practices


### **Establishing a Strong Foundation: How Records Influence Early Case Strategy**


Medical records shape every aspect of PI case development from initial case acceptance decisions through expert retention and demand package preparation. Intake teams need speed for case evaluation, completeness verification, provider discovery to identify all treatment sources clients may have forgotten, and authorization error prevention, since a rejected request can force resubmission and restart the applicable provider or statutory response period. Under HIPAA, covered entities generally have up to 30 calendar days, though shorter state deadlines may apply.
