## **Understanding the Medical Record Retrieval Landscape**


Medical record retrieval has historically been one of the most time-consuming aspects of pre-litigation case development. Law firms have traditionally relied on staffing agencies, in-house paralegals, or third-party retrieval vendors to manage the labor-intensive process of requesting, following up on, and organizing medical records.


### **Challenges of Traditional Approaches**


Traditional approaches to medical record management present several operational challenges: extended timelines that typically span 30 to 90 days, incomplete deliveries that force costly re-requests, heavy paralegal burden (often 15 to 25 hours per case on manual review and follow-up), client involvement requirements when rapid-delivery services require patients to navigate portals, and limited analytical insight since raw documents arrive without breach identification or expense quantification.


Modern plaintiff firms handling personal injury, mass tort, and medical malpractice cases need more than document retrieval, they need actionable medical intelligence, complete record sets verified for accuracy, predictable timelines, integration with existing case management systems like Filevine, and human-verified AI analysis built to withstand litigation scrutiny.
