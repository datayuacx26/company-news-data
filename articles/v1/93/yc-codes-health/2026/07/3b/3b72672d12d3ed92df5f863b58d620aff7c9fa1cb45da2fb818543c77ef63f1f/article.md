## **Ensuring Complete and Compliant Medical Record Sets**


### **Why Authorization Errors Derail Timelines**


Incomplete or defective authorizations are a leading cause of denied requests. Missing patient signatures, unclear expiration dates, or unchecked boxes for sensitive records can force a resubmission and add real time to your retrieval. Codes Health's AI review catches these errors before submission, automatically flagging misspellings, missing dates of service, and signature issues that would otherwise cause provider rejections.


### **The Completeness Verification Difference**


Receiving records is only half the battle. Verifying completeness helps prevent trial surprises. Look for platforms that:


-


Compare delivered records against the original request


-


Identify missing date ranges, facilities, or record types


-


Flag gaps between billing records and treatment documentation


-


Provide confirmation of what was and wasn't received
