## **Why Solo Personal Injury Lawyers Need Streamlined Medical Record Retrieval**


### **The High Cost of Manual Retrieval for Solo Practitioners**


Every hour spent chasing medical records is an hour not spent on case strategy, client communication, or settlement negotiations. Traditional manual retrieval consumes resources solo attorneys can't afford to waste:


-


**Time drain** : hours spent tracking requests, making follow-up calls, and managing provider correspondence


-


**Extended timelines** : retrieval that can stretch from weeks into months, especially when providers are slow to respond or authorizations are rejected


-


**High error rates** : incomplete authorizations, missing information, and lost follow-ups that require a restart


-


**Disorganized delivery** : hours of manual sorting, indexing, and chronological organization once records finally arrive


For a solo practitioner managing 20 active cases with several providers each, that adds up to well over 100 record requests a year, a heavy load that affects case capacity.


### **Impact on Case Valuation and Settlement**


Medical records don't just document injuries, they help determine case value. Incomplete records can mean:


-


Missed diagnoses that reduce damages calculations


-


Gaps in treatment documentation that defense counsel can exploit


-


Overlooked future medical expenses that lower settlement demands


-


Weaker causation evidence that undermines negotiations


More complete, better-organized medical evidence can help attorneys evaluate damages, causation, and treatment gaps more thoroughly.


## **Core Features: What to Look for in Medical Records Software**


### **Ensuring Data Security and Compliance (HIPAA)**


Any platform handling protected health information should use safeguards appropriate to its role, have the right agreements in place, and clearly document its HIPAA and data security practices. Beyond baseline compliance, look for:


-


**SOC 2 Type II certification** as an added layer of assurance


-


**Encrypted transmission** for all record transfers


-


**Secure document storage** with access controls


-


**Business associate agreement** coverage where applicable


### **Efficient Authorization and Release Management**


Incomplete authorizations are a leading cause of denied medical record requests. Missing patient signatures, unclear expiration dates, or unchecked boxes for sensitive records can force a resubmission and add days or weeks to your timeline. The best platforms include:


-


**Pre-submission AI validation** that catches errors before they cause rejections


-


**E-signature workflows** for HIPAA-compliant authorization collection


-


**Provider-specific requirement checking** to meet individual facility standards


-


**Automatic flagging** of misspellings, missing dates, and signature issues


[Codes Health's AI review](https://www.getcodeshealth.com/) catches these errors before submission. The system automatically flags misspellings, missing dates of service, and signature issues that would otherwise cause provider rejections.


### **Comprehensive Provider Access and Follow-up**


Provider relationships shape retrieval speed. Evaluate platforms based on:


-


**Network reach** : connections across a broad range of healthcare facilities


-


**Multi-channel outreach** : calls, faxes, emails, and portals


-


**Persistent follow-up** : ongoing provider contact until records arrive

**Real-time tracking** : visibility into outreach attempts and responses
