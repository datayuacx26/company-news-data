## **1. Codes Health: Filevine's Partner for AI-Native Medical Record Retrieval**


Medical record retrieval represents the single largest bottleneck in personal injury case preparation. Traditional retrieval can take weeks or months, especially when firms must repeatedly follow up with providers or correct rejected requests.


**What It Does:** Codes Health combines a proprietary facility database, AI-native request validation, and an operations layer that follows up until records reach the law firm. The platform connects with claims clearinghouses, custodian integrations, proprietary provider networks, fax, and patient portals, extending beyond conventional retrieval workflows.


**Why It Matters for PI Firms:** The Filevine partnership goes beyond a standard[third-party integration](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/filevine-partners-with-codes-health-to-bring-ai-native-medical-record-retrieval-to-legal-teams-302823827.html) by embedding Codes Health's entire retrieval service within Filevine. This means:


-


Submit medical, billing, and imaging requests directly from the case file


-


AI validates authorizations, date ranges, and provider-specific requirements before submission


-


Track real-time status updates and communicate with the Codes Health team inside Filevine


-


Receive completed records and billing delivered back into the same project


**The Authorization Problem Solved:** Codes Health identifies incomplete authorizations as a leading cause of denials in its retrieval workflow. Missing signatures, unclear expiration dates, and incomplete permissions for sensitive records can require resubmission and restart the applicable provider or state processing period.[Codes Health's AI review](https://www.getcodeshealth.com/) catches these errors before submission, flagging misspellings, missing dates of service, and signature issues that would otherwise cause provider rejections.


**Complete Records vs. Speed:** Some competitors advertise same-day retrieval, but Codes Health cautions that these models often return only the records immediately available, requiring additional client involvement to fill remaining gaps and leaving firms chasing missing material. That added burden can contribute to dissatisfaction and churn. Codes Health prioritizes completeness over raw speed, verifying records against the[original request](https://www.filevine.com/webinars/one-case-file-zero-gaps-how-filevine-codes-health-end-records-chasing/) before delivery to ensure firms receive everything needed for case preparation.


**Continuous Platform Evolution:** Codes Health's MIT-educated engineering team continuously builds out additional workflows and products, ensuring the platform constantly evolves, improves, and becomes more comprehensive to meet the changing demands of modern legal practices.


**Turnaround:** Records delivered in a[couple of weeks](https://www.getcodeshealth.com/#features) , not months. The platform manages provider outreach across calls, faxes, emails, and portals, then verifies completeness before delivery.


**Pricing:** Flat fee structure. Contact Codes Health for volume arrangements. High-volume firms can request custom integrations with their existing CRM platforms and other medical software.

**What It Does:** LOIS answers case questions, drafts demands, and analyzes documents with source citations. The suite includes:


-

-


**AI Doc Review:** Generate document summaries and extract key facts


-


**DemandsAI:** Draft demand letters based on case records


-


**Ask LOIS:** Ask questions about your case and receive cited answers


-

**Why It Matters for PI Firms:** Combined with Codes Health's organized medical records, LOIS can analyze treatment timelines, identify damages documentation, and surface relevant facts for demand packages, cutting down hours of manual review time for complex medical records.


**AI Precision for Medical Records:** General-purpose AI platforms, including ChatGPT, cannot be relied on to match the precision, connected case context, and human-verified workflows of a purpose-built medical-record platform like Codes Health.


**Best For:** Firms wanting AI analysis without switching platforms or uploading records to external tools.


## **3. QuickBooks Online Integration: Financial Visibility**


Personal injury firms on contingency fees must track prepaid case costs, settlement disbursements, and revenue by case for profitability analysis. Most firms struggle with this.


**What It Does:** Through certified middleware partners, QuickBooks Online connects case activity in Filevine to financial reporting:


-


Track prepaid case costs at the matter level (medical records fees, expert fees, court costs)


-


Sync invoices, expenses, and trust accounting data


-


Reduce duplicate data entry between systems


-


Support additional revenue and cash flow reporting through further configuration


**Why It Matters for PI Firms:** Firms that don't properly track prepaid case costs risk losing significant revenue when those costs aren't reimbursed at settlement. The integration helps ensure proper reimbursement, protects working capital, and maintains liquidity for taking on new cases.


**Setup:** This isn't plug-and-play. Configuration requirements, implementation timing, and fees vary by integration provider and workflow scope. Request a[written quote](https://filevine.com/partners/directory) before budgeting.
