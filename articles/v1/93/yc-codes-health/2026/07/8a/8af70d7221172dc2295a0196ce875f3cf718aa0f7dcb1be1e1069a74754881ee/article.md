# LlamaLab vs EvenUp vs Codes Health

Personal injury law firms evaluating AI-powered medical record solutions face a critical choice: prioritize speed, downstream automation, or complete, verified documentation. This comparison breaks down how LlamaLab, EvenUp, and[Codes Health](https://www.getcodeshealth.com/) serve different stages of case development and why choosing the right tool for your workflow prevents expensive bottlenecks. Understanding these distinctions helps firms build a medical records strategy that supports faster settlements and stronger case outcomes.


## **Key Takeaways**


-


LlamaLab focuses on rapid retrieval, same-day to 48 hours for electronically connected providers, and is named as one of several defendants in a pending 2026 lawsuit involving alleged improper record access across a health information exchange network


-


EvenUp is not a medical record retrieval service. It generates demand letters and case analysis but requires firms to already have complete records. EvenUp reports serving over 2,000 firms, including 20% of the Top 100 US PI firms


-


Codes Health delivers complete records in a couple of weeks, not months, with human-verified AI analysis and flat-fee pricing, aiming to avoid the incomplete-record follow-up cycles that can contribute to client churn with faster services


-


Codes Health integrates directly with Filevine, allowing users to submit requests, track status, and receive completed records without leaving the case file


-


General AI platforms like ChatGPT are not designed for litigation-grade medical record review, while Codes Health's purpose-built AI is designed specifically for legal-grade analysis


-


For most PI firms, an effective workflow uses Codes Health for retrieval alongside downstream tools for demand generation, since incomplete records limit the value of even the best AI-generated demands


## **Understanding the Core Offerings: What Each Platform Actually Does**


Before comparing features, firms must understand a fundamental distinction: these three platforms solve different problems at different stages of case development.


### **LlamaLab: Speed-Focused Record Retrieval**


LlamaLab positions itself as an AI-native retrieval platform emphasizing same-day to 48-hour turnaround for electronically connected providers. The platform states it serves several hundred law firms and offers:


-


Provider discovery that LlamaLab states finds roughly 3x more treatment locations than client memory alone


-


Authorization validation with a self-reported rejection rate under 2%


-


Per-provider pricing, with plans stated to start around $50 per provider depending on volume commitment, or a quote-based unlimited option


-


A clinical team for case qualification


**Pending litigation** : LlamaLab is named as one of several defendants in a lawsuit that Epic Systems and a group of health systems filed in[January 2026](https://www.hipaajournal.com/epic-sues-health-information-exchange-network-improper-record-access/) , alleging that Health Gorilla and its clients, including LlamaLab, improperly accessed patient records through health information exchange networks for non-treatment purposes. The complaint references roughly 300,000 patient records across the named defendants. LlamaLab has publicly denied the allegations and stated it intends to contest them. The case remains in early stages, with motions to dismiss and sever still pending, so firms evaluating LlamaLab may want to track the litigation as part of standard vendor due diligence.


### **EvenUp: Downstream Demand Automation**


EvenUp operates in a completely different category. The platform does not retrieve medical records. It assumes you already have complete documentation and focuses on:


-


AI demand letter generation through its AI Drafts Suite


-


MedChrons automated medical chronologies


-


Settlement Repository with verdict and settlement data


-


Case management integrations with Litify, SmartAdvocate, and CASEpeer


EvenUp reports serving over 2,000 firms, including 20% of the Top 100 US PI firms, and reached a valuation above $2 billion following an October 2025 funding round. In December 2024, Business Insider reported that former employees described the platform relying more heavily on manual human review than its marketing suggested; EvenUp has said it uses a combination of AI and human review to maintain accuracy, and that its AI capabilities continue to improve.


### **Codes Health: Complete Records with Verified Analysis**


[Codes Health](https://www.getcodeshealth.com/) combines retrieval and analysis in a single platform, delivering complete medical records in a couple of weeks rather than the months typical of traditional processes. The platform differentiates through:


-


Multi-channel retrieval using claims clearinghouses, custodian integrations, proprietary provider networks, fax, and other traditional methods


-


Human-verified AI insights combining automation speed with expert validation


-


Completeness verification checking delivered records against original requests before delivery


-


Flat-fee pricing with no per-page or per-provider charges


-


[Strategic Filevine integration](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/filevine-partners-with-codes-health-to-bring-ai-native-medical-record-retrieval-to-legal-teams-302823827.html) embedding retrieval into case management workflows


Codes Health is[trusted by hundreds](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/filevine-partners-with-codes-health-to-bring-ai-native-medical-record-retrieval-to-legal-teams-302823827.html) of personal injury, mass tort, and medical malpractice firms and is backed by Y Combinator, Amplify Partners, and Haystack.


## **Speed vs. Completeness: The Critical Trade-Off**


The fundamental tension between LlamaLab and Codes Health reflects a strategic choice every PI firm must make.


### **LlamaLab's Speed-First Approach**


LlamaLab states that a majority of requests from electronically connected providers are completed same-day to within 48 hours, compared to the 30 to 60 days it cites for manual or portal-based retrieval.


Codes Health notes that same-day retrieval services can sometimes deliver incomplete records that require client involvement for follow-ups, which may lead to case delays and contribute to client churn. Fast partial records may extend total case timelines when multiple follow-up requests become necessary to obtain complete documentation.


### **Codes Health's Completeness-First Approach**


[Codes Health](https://www.getcodeshealth.com/) prioritizes delivering complete record sets in a couple of weeks, using multiple retrieval channels to ensure nothing is missed:


-


Clearinghouse connections for billing and claims data


-


Custodian and proprietary provider network integrations for clinical records


-


Traditional methods (fax, phone, portals) for providers without electronic access


-


Missing record detection identifying gaps before attorney review


**Why completeness matters for litigation** :


-


Incomplete records weaken settlement demands


-


Missing documentation creates trial surprises


-


Follow-up requests restart provider response timelines


-


Gaps give defense counsel ammunition for alternative causation theories


### **The Authorization Quality Advantage**


Incomplete authorizations are a leading cause of denied medical record requests. Missing patient signatures, unclear expiration dates, or unchecked boxes for sensitive records can restart the request clock, which follows the 30-day federal HIPAA standard, though state authorization rules can vary.[Codes Health's AI review](https://www.getcodeshealth.com/) catches these errors before submission. The system automatically flags misspellings, missing dates of service, and signature issues that would otherwise cause provider rejections, supporting first-time acceptance and faster total turnaround.
