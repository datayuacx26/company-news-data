## **Understanding the EvenUp Alternatives Landscape**


Before diving into specific alternatives, it helps to understand two categories of legal AI tools that increasingly overlap rather than sit in separate lanes:


-


**Retrieval and Analysis Tools:** get medical records from providers, analyze them for litigation insights, and build chronologies. Codes Health operates primarily in this category.


-


**Demand and Case-Preparation:** Tools focus on turning records into settlement packages, demand letters, and case valuations. EvenUp, Supio, and ProPlaintiff.ai are best known here, though some platforms, including EvenUp's broader PLAAS offering, now include retrieval-linked services as well.


The line between these categories isn't fixed, and firms should compare which services are native to a platform, bundled, delivered through a partner, or available only on certain plans, rather than assuming a strict retrieval-versus-demand divide. Regardless of category, downstream tools can only work with the records and data they're given, so retrieval completeness remains foundational to case outcomes.

-

**The Cost of Traditional Methods:**


Beyond direct retrieval fees, traditional methods can impose hidden costs: paralegal time spent chasing providers, delays extending case timelines, incomplete records discovered late in litigation, manual chronology building taking hours per case, and missed insights that affect case valuation.


**Authorization Errors Drive Delays:**


Authorization errors can force a request to be corrected and resubmitted, delaying the applicable provider or statutory response timeline. Deadlines vary by state and request pathway; HIPAA's individual-access framework generally allows up to 30 days. Codes Health's AI review is designed to catch authorization issues before submission, automatically flagging misspellings, missing dates of service, and signature issues that would otherwise cause provider rejections.
