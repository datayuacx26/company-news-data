## **The Legal AI Landscape: Why Your Retrieval Strategy Matters**


The plaintiff legal tech market has expanded rapidly, with platforms promising to reduce the manual burden of medical record review, demand letter generation, and case chronology creation. However, a critical assumption underlies most of these tools: that your firm already has complete, timely access to the medical records needed for analysis.


That assumption creates a real gap for many practices. Traditional retrieval can stretch across weeks or months, and incomplete authorizations can send requests back to the start. When that happens, the speed of your AI analysis stops mattering, because your cases stall at the retrieval stage, not the analysis stage.


### **Common Pre-Litigation Bottlenecks**


Law firms handling personal injury, mass tort, medical malpractice, and workers' compensation cases frequently run into these workflow problems:


-


**Incomplete authorizations** : Missing patient signatures, unclear expiration dates, or unchecked boxes for sensitive records cause provider rejections and add real delay to an already lengthy federal timeline


-


**Multiple provider coordination** : Complex injury cases often involve nine or ten different treatment facilities, each requiring independent tracking and follow-up


-


**Missing record gaps** : Records that appear complete may still lack critical documentation from specialists, imaging centers, or pharmacy records


-


**Manual review burden** : Attorneys and paralegals spend hours reviewing thousands of pages to identify relevant diagnoses, treatments, and breach-of-care indicators


-


**Vendor fragmentation** : Coordinating between retrieval services, record review vendors, and case management systems creates accountability gaps


Understanding which platform addresses these specific bottlenecks, rather than which offers the longest feature list, is what actually determines the value each provides to your practice.

## **Authorization Quality: The Hidden Cause of Retrieval Delays**


One often-overlooked factor in retrieval speed is authorization quality. Incomplete authorizations are a leading cause of denied requests. Common issues include:


-


Missing or illegible patient signatures


-


Unclear expiration dates


-


Unchecked boxes for sensitive record categories (mental health, substance abuse, HIV)


-


Misspellings of patient names or provider names


-


Missing dates of service


Each rejection adds delay to an already lengthy process, since medical record requests generally fall under a 30-day federal turnaround standard under HIPAA, with some state rules varying. A rejection just means starting that clock over.


Codes Health addresses this through proactive AI error checking that reviews authorization forms before submission. The system automatically flags common rejection causes, including misspellings, missing dates of service, and signature problems, before they reach providers.


This pre-submission verification isn't something Supio or Eve Legal offers, since both platforms assume records are obtained through separate channels.
