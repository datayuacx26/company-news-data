## **Understanding the Unique Medical Record Needs of Small Personal Injury Law Firms**


Small PI firms often function as their own pre-litigation department, handling case qualification, damages documentation, demand preparation, and negotiation without the staff resources of larger practices. This creates distinct medical record needs:


-


**Volume challenges** : A solo practitioner managing 20 active cases with several providers each can easily face well over 100 record requests a year. Without automation, this consumes substantial administrative time.


-


**Timeline pressure** : Settlement negotiations and statutes of limitations create hard deadlines. Extended retrieval windows compress case development timelines.


-


**Budget constraints** : Per-page pricing models can generate surprise invoices. Small firms need predictable costs to maintain profitability on contingency cases.


-


**Quality requirements** : Incomplete records can weaken negotiating positions and create trial surprises. Missing treatment notes, imaging studies, or billing records can undermine case value.


The ideal retrieval partner for small firms combines speed, completeness verification, transparent pricing, and minimal administrative burden. These criteria separate modern AI platforms from traditional manual services.

-


Misspellings in patient or provider names

Missing dates of service
