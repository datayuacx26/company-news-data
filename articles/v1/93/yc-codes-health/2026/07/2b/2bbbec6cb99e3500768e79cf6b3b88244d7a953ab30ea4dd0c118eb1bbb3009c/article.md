## **Understanding the Unique Challenges of MDL Cases for Medical Record Retrieval**


Mass tort litigation operates at a fundamentally different scale than individual personal injury cases. A single-plaintiff car accident might involve 5 to 10 medical providers; a mass tort involving pharmaceutical injuries could require records from 10,000+ plaintiffs, each with multiple treatment facilities.


**The MDL challenge multiplies exponentially.** The 25 largest MDLs account for approximately 95.7% of all pending actions, meaning a handful of dockets drive most retrieval demand. Plaintiffs often have extensive pre-existing medical histories that defense counsel will scrutinize for alternative causation, and multi-state plaintiffs are treated at facilities with different recordkeeping systems, authorization requirements, and fee structures. Bellwether trial selections add further pressure, requiring rapid identification of the strongest cases from large plaintiff pools.


Standard retrieval often struggles at this scale: costs for traditional per-request retrieval vary by vendor and often include a service fee plus separate provider, copy, certification, or delivery charges, and manual review of large record sets can require substantial staff time that's difficult to sustain across high-volume dockets.
