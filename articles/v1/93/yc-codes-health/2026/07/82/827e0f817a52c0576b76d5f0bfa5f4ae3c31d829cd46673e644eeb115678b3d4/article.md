## **Addressing the Scale Challenges of Large Personal Injury Firms**


The largest personal injury law firms face medical records challenges that smaller practices rarely encounter. When a firm manages hundreds of active cases simultaneously, with each case requiring records from several providers, that's thousands of active retrieval requests at any moment.


**The volume equation creates compounding problems:**


-


**Staff bandwidth** : Traditional manual processes can consume many hours weekly just tracking requests, making follow-up calls, and managing provider correspondence


-


**Error multiplication** : At scale, even a modest authorization rejection rate means a meaningful number of requests restarting from scratch each month


-


**Consistency gaps** : Different staff members following different procedures can lead to unpredictable outcomes


-


**Visibility challenges** : Knowing the real-time status of hundreds or thousands of active requests becomes difficult without centralized systems


Top personal injury law firms increasingly treat retrieval as infrastructure that affects case velocity, not just an administrative task. Codes Health reports that some customers have experienced meaningfully more case capacity using platforms designed for this operational reality, though individual results vary.
