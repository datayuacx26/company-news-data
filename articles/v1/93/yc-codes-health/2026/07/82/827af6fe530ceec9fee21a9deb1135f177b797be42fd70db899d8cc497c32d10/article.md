## **The Role of Health Information Exchanges in Modern Retrieval**


Modern medical record retrieval increasingly leverages Health Information Exchanges (HIEs) and TEFCA networks to access patient records across provider systems. These electronic networks can enable faster retrieval than traditional fax-based methods for participating facilities.


**HIE Advantages:**


-


Electronic access to records from participating providers


-


Reduced turnaround times for connected facilities


-


Standardized data formats improve accuracy


-


Reduced manual handling of paper records
