# Independence Realty Trust Publishes 2024 Sustainability Report

### Independence Realty Trust Publishes 2024 Sustainability Report


October 31, 2025


PHILADELPHIA--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Independence Realty Trust, Inc. (“IRT”) (NYSE: IRT), announces the publication of its 2024 Sustainability Report, which can be accessed by going to visit[https://investors.irtliving.com/sustainability/](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.irtliving.com%2Fsustainability%2F&esheet=54348443&newsitemid=20251031896356&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.irtliving.com%2Fsustainability%2F&index=1&md5=6c36c3cab8df588f74581a98f26bd15b) . The report summarizes IRT’s sustainability achievements through calendar year 2024 as part of the company’s broader mission to provide exceptional living experiences for residents and attractive returns for shareholders.


“We believe that operating multifamily real estate communities can be conducted with a conscious regard for the environment and wider society, while also benefiting our business success and long-term value creation for our residents, associates and investors,” said Scott Schaeffer, Chairman and CEO. He continued, “Our core values are integrated into each of the four pillars of our sustainability strategy, which are enhancing residential living, supporting our associates, managing our environmental impact, and promoting good governance.”


**About IRT** Independence Realty Trust, Inc. (NYSE: IRT), an S&P 400 MidCap Company, is a real estate investment trust (“REIT”) that owns and operates multifamily communities, across non-gateway U.S. markets. IRT’s investment strategy is focused on gaining scale near major employment centers within key amenity rich submarkets that offer good school districts and high-quality retail. IRT’s main objective is to provide attractive risk-adjusted returns to shareholders through diligent portfolio management, strong operational performance, and a consistent return on capital through distributions and capital appreciation. More information is available at[www.irtliving.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.irtliving.com&esheet=54348443&newsitemid=20251031896356&lan=en-US&anchor=www.irtliving.com&index=2&md5=371bd8d54fcb126a434bc2859cb64168) .
