## 1. Coldreach


**Best Salesforge alternative for research-first AI SDR outbound**


Coldreach is the best Salesforge alternative for teams that want outbound built around research quality, not just sending capacity.


Salesforge is strong when you want to scale email and LinkedIn execution. Coldreach is stronger when the limiting factor is relevance: who to contact, why now, what pain they likely have, what they are doing today, and whether they are actually in-market.


Coldreach is a research-first AI SDR. For every lead, it answers three qualifying questions before writing outreach:


1.


**Pain:** What likely business pain or priority makes this account worth contacting?


2.


**Status quo:** What are they doing today, and what does that imply about their current process?


3.


**In-market:** Is there evidence that this company is likely to care now?


Only after that research does Coldreach write the email.


That matters because most AI outbound tools still start from a list and then add personalization on top. They might reference a title, a company description, a hiring post, or a generic signal. Coldreach starts with the buying reason first.
