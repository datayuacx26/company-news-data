The itinerary is set. The playlist is locked. The group chat has officially reached "we should all wear this at brunch" levels of enthusiasm.

## The Mix-and-Match Outfit Strategy


Packing light doesn't mean packing boring. The trick is choosing pieces that work together in different ways.


Here's an easy way to plan your outfits:


- • Two casual daytime outfits
- • One brunch-ready look
- • One "just in case we go somewhere nice" outfit
- • A lightweight layer for cool evenings
- • Comfortable shoes you can actually walk in


When everything coordinates, you can pack fewer pieces while still having options. And yes, this strategy leaves room for an “I bought something cute, and now I need space to bring it home” situation.
