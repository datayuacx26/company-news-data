# NAPCO Security Technologies Honored with SSI Supplier Stellar Service Award for RMR Support Programs

**Amityville, NY** — NAPCO Security Technologies is proud to announce it has been named the recipient of this year's SSI Supplier Stellar Service Award in the category of RMR Support Programs. This prestigious recognition highlights NAPCO's ongoing commitment to helping dealers and integrators build sustainable, profitable businesses through innovative service-driven solutions.


Security industry professionals determine the SSI Supplier Stellar Service Awards and recognize manufacturers that deliver exceptional service, support, and partnership. NAPCO's win in the RMR category underscores the strength of its portfolio and its focus on empowering partners with tools that generate consistent, long-term revenue streams.


NAPCO's RMR support programs include a range of solutions designed to help dealers grow recurring revenue, including StarLink communicators, MVP Access & MVP EZ, cloud-based Access Control, NA-Series Panels, FireLink commercial fire alarm systems, etc. These offerings enable reliable, scalable services while simplifying deployment and ongoing account management.


"We're honored to receive this Stellar Service Award for our RMR Program from the security dealers we proudly serve. This recognition reflects our commitment to helping dealers grow through innovative recurring revenue solutions—from StarLink and FireLink to our MVP Cloud Services platform. We thank our dealers for their continued trust and partnership, and remain dedicated to supporting their success with smart, scalable security solutions." Said Dominic Macri, Sr. Director of Technical Services.


NAPCO continues to invest in products and programs that support long-term dealer success, combining advanced technology with hands-on service, training, and support.


***About NAPCO Security Technologies, Inc.** NAPCO Security Technologies, Inc. (NASDAQ: NSSC) is one of the world's leading manufacturers of technologically advanced electronic security equipment including intrusion and fire alarm systems, access control and door locking systems. The Company consists of NAPCO plus three wholly-owned subsidiaries: Alarm Lock, Continental Access, and Marks USA. The products are installed by security professionals worldwide in commercial, industrial, institutional, residential and government applications. NAPCO products have earned a reputation for technical excellence, reliability and innovation, poising the Company for growth in the rapidly expanding electronic security market, a multi-billion dollar market.*
