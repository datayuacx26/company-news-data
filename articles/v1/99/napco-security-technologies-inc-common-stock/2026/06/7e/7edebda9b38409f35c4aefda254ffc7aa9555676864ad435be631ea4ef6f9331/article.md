# NAPCO Expands MVP Access Ecosystem with New BadgePass Integration

**Amityville, NY & Ridgeland, MS** – NAPCO Security Technologies today announced a new integration between its MVP-EZ and MVP Access cloud-based access control platform and BadgeHub, the credential management and badging platform from BadgePass.


The integration enables organizations to seamlessly synchronize cardholder information between BadgeHub and NAPCO's MVP® platform, simplifying credential issuance, reducing duplicate data entry, and streamlining access management. In addition, customers can now easily print and issue access cards directly from the BadgeHub mobile app. With just a few clicks, the BadgeHub® app prints the credential and automatically configures the cardholder's door access permissions within MVP Cloud, eliminating manual programming and accelerating employee and visitor onboarding.


Designed to support organizations of all sizes, NAPCO's MVP cloud-based access ranges from MVP-EZ, a mobile-first solution ideal for smaller installations, to MVP Access, a feature-rich platform offering browser-based management, custom administrative permissions, advanced reporting, and multi-site control. Together, they provide a scalable path from a few doors and users to thousands, and an affordable monthly flat-rate per door.


"At NAPCO, we're focused on delivering access control solutions that are simple to deploy, easy to manage, and scalable for virtually any application," said Rob Etmans, Director of Access Control, NAPCO Security Technologies. "The addition of BadgeHub enhances the MVP ecosystem by streamlining credential issuance and cardholder management while helping dealers, locksmiths, integrators, and end users deliver a more complete security solution."


The integration enables HR, Security, Facilities, and Administrative departments to efficiently onboard employees and visitors, issue credentials, and manage access privileges through intuitive cloud-based platforms accessible from virtually anywhere.


Supporting NAPCO's own cloud-ready access control panels and award-winning wireless locking solutions, MVP provides a unified infrastructure for new and retrofit applications alike. While NAPCO prides itself on delivering an integrated hardware and software ecosystem, the company continues to expand the MVP platform through strategic technology partnerships. The BadgePass integration joins MVP's growing list of connected solutions, including video integrations with Hanwha Vision and Digital Watchdog, helping customers create more comprehensive security systems.


"BadgePass is committed to helping organizations improve security and streamline identity management," said Phillip King, President of BadgePass. "This integration with NAPCO's MVP platform combines modern credential issuance with flexible cloud-based access control, creating a more efficient experience for dealers, locksmiths, integrators, and end users alike."


The BadgeHub integration for MVP-EZ and MVP Access is available now through authorized NAPCO dealers, locksmiths, and security integrators, while its access hardware is sold through distributors nationwide.
