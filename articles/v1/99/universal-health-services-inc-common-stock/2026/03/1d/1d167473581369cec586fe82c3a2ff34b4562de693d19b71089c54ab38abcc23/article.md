# Universal Health Services, Inc. Named to the 2025 GHX Millennium Club


MilleniumClubAward_Social_Graphic


**King of Prussia, PA (March 27, 2026) —** Universal Health Services, Inc. (NYSE: UHS), one of the nation’s largest and most respected providers of hospital and healthcare services, has been named to the 2025 GHX Millennium Club.


The GHX Millennium Club recognizes an elite group of healthcare provider and supplier organizations that are helping lead the industry’s transition toward a more automated, connected and resilient supply chain.


The Millennium Club honors organizations that have demonstrated a sustained commitment to digital transformation – helping improve operational efficiency, data accuracy and supply chain visibility across the healthcare ecosystem.


This is the third consecutive year UHS has been named to the GHX Millennium Club.


“Healthcare is rapidly evolving, driven by automation, data and AI that are transforming supply chain operations, said Prashant Malhotra, Director of Information Systems – Supply Chain, UHS. “UHS’ recognition as a GHX Millennium Club honoree for the third year in a row underscores our dedication to enhance supply chain resilience, innovation and smarter decision-making in healthcare as we continue to deliver efficient and intelligent operations.”


For more information and the full list of honorees, visit[Millennium Club | GHX](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4647821-1&h=2974264313&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ghx.com%2Fawards%2Fmillennium-club%2F&a=Millennium+Club+%7C+GHX) .
