# Philadelphia Business Journal Largest Public Companies Annual Ranking 2026


ACBJ- PL-11737_TEXT_BADGE_FINAL


**King of Prussia, PA (August 10, 2026)** — UHS ranks at #5 on the annual list of largest public companies in the Philadelphia area as published by the *Philadelphia Business Journal* . Companies are ranked by fiscal year 2025 revenue.


This list includes publicly traded companies that are headquartered within the counties of Bucks, Chester, Delaware, Lancaster, Montgomery and Philadelphia in Pennsylvania; Burlington, Camden and Gloucester in New Jersey; or Kent and New Castle in Delaware.
