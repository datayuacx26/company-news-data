# Forbes: Heroes Of America – Two Legendary CEOs Who Helped Build Our Nation


Screenshot 2026-06-29 at 8.45.54 AM


*Robert Reiss with Alan B. Miller at the ribbon-cutting ceremony for the new Alan B. Miller Medical Center that recently opened in Palm Beach Gardens, Florida.*


**June 2026 —** Founder and Executive Chairman of Universal Health Services, Inc. (NYSE: UHS) Alan B. Miller is featured in a new *Forbes* article, *“Heroes of America: Two Legendary CEOs Who Helped Build Our Nation,”* alongside hospitality icon J.W. Marriott, Jr. In the piece, contributor Robert Reiss highlights the shared leadership principles that helped both executives build industry-leading organizations: putting people first, pursuing operational excellence and embracing innovation to better serve those who depend on them.


The article spotlights Mr. Miller’s unwavering commitment to integrity, trust and patient-centered care, including his belief that “character is destiny” and “the patient is number one, number two and number three.” It also recognizes his vision for leveraging technology to enhance patient outcomes while preserving the human connection at the heart of healthcare.


Learn more about Mr. Miller’s leadership philosophy that has shaped UHS and continues to inspire leaders across industries.
