# The CEO Forum: Alan B. Miller and Marc D. Miller on the Future of UHS


website thumbnails – Alan B. Miller (Chairman) & Marc D. Miller (CEO), Universal Health Services


**June 2026 –** In this interview with The CEO Forum’s Robert Reiss, Alan B. Miller, Founder and Executive Chairman of Universal Health Services, Inc. (NYSE: UHS) and Marc D. Miller, President and CEO of Universal Health Services, share their vision for the future of UHS on the occasion of the opening of the[Alan B. Miller Medical Center](https://uhs.com/news/alan-b-miller-medical-center-celebrates-ribbon-cutting/) in North Palm Beach Gardens, Florida.


From the conversation: “We are concerned about the patient. The patient is number one, number two and number three,” said Alan B. Miller. “And we make it clear that, from the outset, I want our hospitals to treat patients the way I would want my own family treated.”
