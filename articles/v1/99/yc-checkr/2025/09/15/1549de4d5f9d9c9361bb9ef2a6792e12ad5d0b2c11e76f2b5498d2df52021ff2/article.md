# G2 Grid and Compare Report

## What are G2 ratings?


Customers, investors, and analysts use G2 to compare and select the best software solutions based on peer reviews and synthesized social data. Keeping ratings unbiased is G2’s top priority. A LinkedIn account or verified business email address is required to validate a G2 reviewer’s identity and employer. Only the opinions of real users and data from public sources factor into the ratings.
