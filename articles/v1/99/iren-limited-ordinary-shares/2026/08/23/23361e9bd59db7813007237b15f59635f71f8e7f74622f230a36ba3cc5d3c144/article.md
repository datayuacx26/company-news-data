NEW YORK, Aug. 13, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- IREN Limited (NASDAQ:[IREN](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=RIbAknSTAOt46a1I3VGEmSH4Yzfh_k9lekqW_RDG7YQqeTU0U9jDbBLCyaY4N1ej) ) ("IREN") today announced that it will release its financial results for the fiscal year ended June 30, 2026, on Thursday, August 27, 2026 and host a conference call beginning at 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time.

**About IREN**


IREN is a vertically integrated AI Cloud platform, delivering data centers, compute and software for AI training and inference. IREN’s platform is underpinned by its expansive portfolio of land and grid-connected power in renewable-rich regions across North America, Europe and APAC.
