# Reducto Is Now Available on AWS Marketplace

## **Enterprises can now purchase Reducto through AWS Marketplace — simplifying procurement and enabling teams to use committed AWS spend on the most accurate document intelligence platform.**


---


Reducto, the leading AI-powered document intelligence platform, today announced its availability on[AWS Marketplace](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-55iompy2idj36) . Enterprise customers can now subscribe to Reducto directly through AWS Marketplace, streamlining procurement and enabling organizations to apply their existing AWS committed spend — including Enterprise Discount Programs (EDPs) — toward Reducto's document parsing and extraction APIs.


## Why AWS Marketplace Matters for Enterprise Document Intelligence


For enterprises operating at scale, procurement is often the bottleneck between evaluating a tool and actually deploying it. Many of the world's largest organizations have committed cloud spend with AWS, and purchasing software through AWS Marketplace allows them to consolidate billing, simplify vendor onboarding, and draw from budgets that have already been approved.


Reducto's availability on AWS Marketplace means that enterprise teams — from engineering and data science to procurement and finance — can adopt Reducto faster, with less friction, and without the overhead of a separate vendor contract. Pricing on AWS Marketplace is usage-based, consistent with Reducto's standard pay-as-you-go model, so teams only pay for what they process.


## A Modern Alternative to Legacy Document Processing


Many enterprises currently rely on legacy cloud OCR services such as AWS Textract for document processing. While these tools handle basic text extraction from simple, well-structured documents, they often fall short when confronted with the kinds of documents that matter most in production environments: complex multi-column layouts, nested tables, scanned forms with handwriting, financial charts, redlined legal PDFs, and documents with mixed visual elements like checkboxes and graphs.


Reducto was purpose-built to solve these problems. By combining traditional OCR with proprietary vision-language models (VLMs) and a multi-pass Agentic OCR system, Reducto reads documents the way a human would — capturing layout, structure, and meaning with exceptional accuracy. The result is clean, structured, LLM-ready output that enterprises can plug directly into RAG pipelines, process automation workflows, and AI applications.


Enterprise customers across industries have migrated from legacy cloud OCR services to Reducto to achieve the accuracy and reliability their production workloads demand. With Reducto now available on AWS Marketplace, that migration is easier than ever — teams can transition without disrupting their existing AWS billing relationship.


## Built for the Most Demanding Enterprise Use Cases


Reducto powers document workflows for leading AI-native startups, global financial institutions, and Fortune 10 enterprises. Common use cases include:


- **Financial services:** Parsing 10-K filings, bank statements, invoices, and transaction records. Extracting complex charts and tables for financial due diligence and analysis.
- **Healthcare:** Structuring lab reports, patient records, and medical forms with HIPAA-compliant processing. Enabling clinical AI workflows that depend on accurate data extraction.
- **Legal:** Converting redlined PDFs to text, extracting contract clauses, processing court filings, and automating due diligence document review.
- **Insurance:** Extracting data from ACORD forms, claims documents, and policy filings with schema-level precision.


Reducto supports 30+ document formats — including PDFs, images, Excel spreadsheets, and PowerPoint slides — and offers advanced capabilities such as intelligent document splitting, structured data extraction with custom schemas, and AI-powered document editing and form filling via its Edit API.
