# ibex to Participate in the 2026 Sidoti Small-Cap Virtual Conference

WASHINGTON, March 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[IBEX Limited](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=hQS0RFnEWFo40NYOtSnUGJiAMUndR4IvTMDXHO2sfSyq8CYcaIbiMAd3mKhQzMzQ--NHpPNY7ZkuRE0Qz_44hg==) (NASDAQ: IBEX), a leading global provider of business process outsourcing (BPO) and AI-powered customer engagement technology solutions, today announced that CEO Bob Dechant and CFO Taylor Greenwald will present at the Sidoti Small-Cap Virtual Conference on Wednesday, March 18, 2026 at 10:45 a.m. ET.


A live webcast of the presentation will be available[here](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=JeiHyjgNh5DJ2-DmsTg0Iq7-xUc0dgQzeR8aUn__VtvoSldE-rSbrWssUfEnK68M1vz4I2JAHpsY028xyR2gNCJT_6tutJma_OU8z2PYHAQm9eie9oPXuUxJvYmOP2Gx7nrBxedAFYsAXoBZgY0Cgg==) and will be posted on the Company’s[investor relations site](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=xga8S7aPe7prux4idPjgVrcAxjufVk4HpTwKPfhjaJgAXpavQAUu3r4k1qhNjlkbFC6_S1hYslKSrzgROBj5AJaPMcvcne8consJIdV9ctM=) following the event.


The management team will also participate in 1×1 investor meetings throughout the day on Wednesday, March 18, 2026.


For more information about the Sidoti Small-Cap Conference or to schedule a meeting with management, please contact[IBEX@gateway-grp.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=mUdSyp28w1RIhIqsXza-j1SCrflqNGCbFvi33tGsb_1sLqvhBhMoEd16izWy1XgBwd3MKzg3KfsXOS-n45_4ObdHsIXI8e4Q_XUqSIEpqYw=) .
