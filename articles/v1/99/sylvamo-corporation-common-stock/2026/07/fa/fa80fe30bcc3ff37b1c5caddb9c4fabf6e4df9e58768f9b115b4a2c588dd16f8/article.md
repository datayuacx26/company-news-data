# Sylvamo’s Guide to Writing the Perfect Letter to Santa

MEMPHIS, Tenn. – This holiday season, get back to basics and write the big man a letter.


Whether you’re young or young at heart, writing letters to Santa is a classic Christmas tradition. Use Sylvamo’s guide to craft the perfect letter that’s sure to get you on the nice list.


#### Crafting Your Santa Letter


Writing letters is a meaningful way to connect with people near and far, and the North Pole is no exception. When writing to Santa, it’s important to reflect on the year, what you’re thankful for and of course, mention your wish list!


Start by using our Santa Letter[Template](https://assets.sylvamo.com/m/67213ad9806a17cb/original/Santa-Letter-Writing-Template.pdf) .


1.


Start with a greeting – "Dear Santa" is always classic.


1.


Introduce yourself – Santa needs to know who he’s talking to!


1.


Share what you’re thankful for – Parents, Friends, Teachers, The Worlds Paper Company...


1.


Include your wish list – Don't go too crazy!


1.


Closing – Thank you, Santa!


1.


Signature – Love, Buddy
