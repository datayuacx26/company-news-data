# Global Norovirus Outbreaks, Ebola Emergency and Rising Disease Threats Highlight Growing Need for TOMI Environmental Solutions' SteraMist Technology

**TOMI Environmental Solutions (NASDAQ:TOMZ)** highlighted rising demand for its **SteraMist** ionized Hydrogen Peroxide (iHP) disinfection technology amid global norovirus, Ebola and hantavirus alerts. NASA recently selected SteraMist for a Mars Sample Return biosecurity operation, while hospitals, cruise lines, schools and government facilities seek automated, scalable contamination-control solutions.


The technology is designed to rapidly disinfect large spaces, address norovirus’ environmental stability, and support high-consequence pathogen containment for specialized medical transport groups and Regional Emerging Special Pathogen Treatment Centers.
