# TOMI Environmental Solutions Announces Definitive Agreement to Merge with Carbonium Core

**TOMI Environmental Solutions (NASDAQ:TOMZ)** signed a definitive Merger Agreement with **Carbonium Core** , a U.S. developer of graphite and rare earth metals for advanced nuclear, defense, and critical infrastructure applications.


Subject to approvals and conditions, Carbonium shareholders are expected to own about **90%** of the combined company. Closing is targeted for **Q3 2026** , contingent on at least **$10 million** in financing and NASDAQ listing approval. The combined business aims to be a pure-play on nuclear-grade graphite and critical materials, support advanced reactor supply chains, and help TOMI improve capital access, balance sheet strength, and NASDAQ compliance while exploring cross-platform technology integration.
