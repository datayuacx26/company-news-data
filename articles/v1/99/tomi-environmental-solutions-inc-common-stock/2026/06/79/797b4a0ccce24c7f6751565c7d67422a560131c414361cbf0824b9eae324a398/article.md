# TOMI Expands European Footprint with New Regulatory Approvals for Binary Ionization Technology

**TOMI (NASDAQ:TOMZ)** received new regulatory approvals in **Austria** and **Italy** for its Binary Ionization Technology (BIT), advancing its European expansion. BIT is now authorized in Austria, Italy, the Netherlands, Belgium, Denmark, Germany, Hungary, Great Britain, and Northern Ireland under the EU Biocidal Products Regulation (BPR).


Approved as a **PT2 disinfectant** in the Netherlands since March 2026, BIT can use BPR’s mutual recognition process to streamline authorization in additional EU states. Parallel mutual recognition is anticipated for **France, Ireland, Poland, Portugal, Romania, and Spain** , supporting faster regional rollout.
