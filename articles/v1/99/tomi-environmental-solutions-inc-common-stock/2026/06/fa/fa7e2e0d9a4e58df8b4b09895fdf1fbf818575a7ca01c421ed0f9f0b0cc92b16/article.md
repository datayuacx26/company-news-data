# TOMI Environmental Solutions Highlights Massive Biosecurity Opportunity Ahead of FIFA World Cup

**TOMI Environmental Solutions (NASDAQ:TOMZ)** outlines growing biosecurity demand ahead of the 2026 FIFA World Cup, expected to attract about 6.5 million fans across North America.


The company highlights increased inbound interest and investments in its **SteraMist iHP** disinfection technology from healthcare and other critical sectors worldwide.


TOMI describes the World Cup as a catalyst for long-term biosecurity adoption across transportation, hospitality, food systems, public venues, and defense, suggesting a potential multi-billion-dollar market opportunity.
