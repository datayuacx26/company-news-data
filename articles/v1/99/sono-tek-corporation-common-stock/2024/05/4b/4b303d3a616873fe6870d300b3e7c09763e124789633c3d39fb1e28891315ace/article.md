# Sono-Tek Announces Year-End FY 2024 Earnings Conference Call

**Conference Call on Thursday, May 23, 2024 at 9:30 am ET**


MILTON, N.Y., May 20, 2024 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Sono-Tek Corporation (NASDAQ: SOTK), the leading developer and manufacturer of ultrasonic coating systems, today announced that the Company will hold a conference call to discuss its year-end fiscal year 2024 financial results, ended February 29, 2024, on Thursday, May 23, 2024 at 9:30 am ET. The year-end FY 2024 financial results press release will be issued before the market opens on May 23, 2024.


**Conference Call Dial-in Information**


To participate, please call 1-844-481-2752 at least 10 minutes prior to the start of the call and ask to join the Sono-Tek call.


**Webcast Information**


A simultaneous webcast of the call may be accessed through the Company’s website, Events & Presentations | Sono-Tek, or at https://event.choruscall.com/mediaframe/webcast.html?webcastid=Msi7H3Uz
