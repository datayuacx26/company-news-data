# Lument Provides $27.3 Million Freddie Mac Refinance for Texas Multifamily Communities

**New York, NY — July 10, 2026 —** Lument today announces the closing of two Freddie Mac loans totaling $27.3 million to refinance two multifamily communities in Texas with a total unit count just under 500. Lument’s Phil Frasca led the transaction. The sponsor is a repeat Freddie Mac client with over 1,000 units under ownership throughout Texas.


The two communities were acquired by the borrower several years ago as part of a larger portfolio. One is located in Brownsville, and the other is in Wichita Falls.


“Getting to the finish line with attractive terms is the most important aspect of multifamily financing in this ever-changing market,” Frasca said. “With the help of the expert teams at Freddie Mac and Lument, we were able to navigate volatile treasury yields and pending loan maturities, ultimately delivering an optimal outcome that included cash-out proceeds and partial interest only.”


The Freddie Mac loans carry fixed interest rates, five-year terms, and 30-year amortizations. One loan features three years of interest only and the other features two years.


The transaction was brought to Lument by Steve Sieling of Dallas-based Azur Commercial Capital.
