# Lument Closes $21.2 Million HUD Express Lane Refinance for Nebraska Assisted Living Community

**New York, NY — April 24, 2026 —** Lument announces the recent closing of a $21.2 million U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD)/Federal Housing Administration (FHA) Section 232/223(f) Express Lane refinance for Pemberly Place Senior Living, a seniors housing community in Lincoln, Nebraska, with 120 apartment units and a licensed capacity for 132. Quintin Harris, managing director, and Patrick Roan, director, led the transaction for Lument.


“The Express Lane is the result of HUD listening to lenders and borrowers and making meaningful changes that accelerate transactions,” said Lument’s Harris. “FHA programs have long offered some of the most competitive terms in the market, and now they pair that strength with enhanced speed and execution certainty, providing a critical advantage in a volatile market.”


Lument obtained a firm commitment from FHA just seven days after application submission and advanced to closing consistent with customary market timelines.


“This is a strong example of how FHA’s 232 Express Lane is driving efficiency while maintaining disciplined underwriting,” HUD said in a statement promoting the transaction.


The FHA loan has a fixed interest rate and 35-year term. The refinance pays off existing bank debt from a construction loan originated by Lument several years ago. In addition, Lument helped structure a shareholder note to reimburse the borrower for previous capital expenditures.


Built in 2017, Pemberly Place Senior Living features 132 units consisting of assisted living, independent living, and memory care options. It has an on-site medical clinic and offers an extensive range of healthcare services. Locally owned and operated, the community has a partnership with MJ Senior Living, a Nebraska-based management company.
