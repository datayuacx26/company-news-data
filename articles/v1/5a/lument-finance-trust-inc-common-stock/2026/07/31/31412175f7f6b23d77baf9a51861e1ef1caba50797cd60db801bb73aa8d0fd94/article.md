# 2026 Argentum Largest Seniors Housing Providers: Report Takeaways

Argentum’s[2026 Largest Providers Report](https://info.argentum.org/2026-largest-providers?submissionGuid=a4f48dbe-f4b4-4256-b50e-884774c49339) presents insights and data on an industry in motion. Investor interest, growing providers, new operating models, and evolving resident needs are just some of the many dynamic elements shaping seniors housing as it moves into a new era. Using provider-reported information and data-fueled analysis, the newly expanded Largest Providers report presents a well-rounded snapshot of the country’s leading seniors housing organizations and the trends shaping their strategies.


Similar to previous iterations, the report features detailed information on the top independent living (IL), assisted living (AL), memory care (MC), and continuing care retirement communities (CCRCs). To help broaden the perspective, this year’s report adds new data points: operational resident capacity, geographic reach, international operations, ownership structures, leadership representation, and changes within the provider landscape. Supplementing the data are expert insights, including a look at[financing success](https://www.lument.com/financing-success-in-the-new-era-of-seniors-housing/) and a profile of women in leadership.


**Ownership Breakdown**


By breaking down ownership mix data, Argentum is creating a new baseline for understanding how the largest seniors housing companies are organized. Majority ownership was the most common model at 56%, representing approximately 197,000 units. Private equity-dominant ownership represented only a small portion of the landscape at 13%, approximately 7,200 units.


Source: 2026 Largest Providers survey data.


**Top Rankings**


Brookdale Senior Living tops the rankings for the seventh year in a row with 47,085 total units in 584 communities. A close second, Discovery remains at number two after reaching that status last year with 46,074 total units across 414 communities. Keeping its spot in third is LCS with 30,957 units in 119 communities.


The rest of the top five consisted of Erickson Senior Living (26,425 units in 23 communities) at number four and Atria Management Co. (21,215 units in 170 communities) at number five, both the same as last year.


In terms of dramatic improvements from year to year, Experience Senior Living moved up 55 places, from 107 to 52; Allegro Living LLC moved from 83 to 29 (up 54 places); and Sinceri Senior Living moved 29 places, from 37 to eight.


Discovery Senior Living added the most units year-over-year, going from 33,692 to 46,074. Sinceri’s growth was reflected in their adding 10,695 units, going from 4,638 to 15,333.


Notable newcomers to the list include Evergreen Management (ranking 51) and Navion Senior Solutions (ranking 68).
