[Laca Wong-Hammond](https://www.lument.com/mergers-acquisitions/laca-wong-hammond/) , Head of Mergers & Acquisitions at Lument, shares her perspective on what drives successful transactions. In this brief discussion, she outlines the three P’s of transaction marketing: **preparation** , **positioning** , and **process** , and explains why each plays a critical role in achieving a strong outcome.


From organizing financials and preparing for due diligence to crafting a compelling growth narrative and managing a competitive sale process, Laca highlights the key considerations that can help sellers maximize value and position their business for a successful transaction.
