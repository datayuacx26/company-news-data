# Webcast Recording: Will Strong Multifamily Fundamentals Offset Fallout From the War?

In our latest In Conversation webcast, Lument CEO[Jim Flynn](https://www.lument.com/leadership/james-flynn/) ,[Beacon Economics](https://beaconecon.com/) Founding Partner[Chris Thornberg](https://beaconecon.com/staff/christopher-thornberg/) , and[Granite Towers Equity Group](https://www.granitetowersequitygroup.com/) Co-Founder and Managing Partner[Mike Roeder](https://www.granitetowersequitygroup.com/mike-roeder-bio) discussed the latest market dynamics and financing opportunities. Watch the replay to gain insights into:


- The key metrics that economists watch as they assess the intersection of the economy and the multifamily market.
- The consequence of higher gasoline prices on operating expenses, including materials, insurance, and payroll — and steps investors can take to offset them.
- The resilience of the American consumer in the face of rising prices and what it means for rents, vacancies, and turnover.
- The availability of capital, both for acquisitions and refinancing, and the approaches investors can use to get a deal done in this environment.
- The changing leadership at the Fed and its implications for rates, market volatility, and financing.
- The metros that can be expected to outperform regardless of economic headwinds.
- The strategies that investors can employ to maximize revenue in this market.
