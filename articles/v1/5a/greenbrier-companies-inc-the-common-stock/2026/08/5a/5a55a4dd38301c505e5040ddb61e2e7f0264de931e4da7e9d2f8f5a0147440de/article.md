# AAR Circular Letter – July 2026

## About AAR Circular Letters


Circular letter summaries are produced monthly by[Greenbrier Management Services Group](https://www.gbrx.com/services/management-services/) .


[Download GMS brochure:](https://www.gbrx.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/gms-brochure_single-pages_hires.pdf)


AAR circular letters provide information of general applicability to the railroad industry, including proposed and final rules, revisions, standards and recommended practices, and calls for comments. These summaries provide the main points of circular letters issued in the prior month, as well as important implementation deadlines.


For access to the complete circular letter, please go to[my.aar.org/circulars](https://my.aar.org/circulars) . Circular letters are a fee-based service provided by the AAR’s Transportation Technology Center Incorporated (TTCI). You may subscribe online atpublications@aar.com or call 719.584.0538.

**Subject:** Implementation of Revisions to MSRP Section E, BRAKES AND BRAKE EQUIPMENT, Standard S-401 Brake Design Requirements

**C-14813**


**Subject:** Correction to the Office Manual Price Matrix Effective July 1, 2026, Replacement Price Master, Rule 73


**Summary:** This circular addresses the correction of the Office Manual Price Matrix effective July 1, 2026, specifically the addition of a newly approved Job Code 4368 OUTLET GATE CLOSED that was missing from the published Price Matrix and distributed DAT file. The new Job Code is intended to provide billing participants visibility for hatches, doors, or gates left open, with no labor or material cost, and a replacement Price Master will be distributed via a new DAT file in the Railinc CRB application.


**Date:** 07-08-2026


**Effective Date:** 07-08-2026

**CPC-1446**


**Subject:** Reinstate language of MSRP Section C Part III, M-1002, Specifications for Tank Cars, Chapter 2, Paragraph 2.2.13.3


**Summary:** Following discussions with industry and in response to feedback regarding the revised language in Chapter 2, Paragraph 2.2.13.3, the TCC has decided to reinstate the original language of the Gas Purging standards from the 2024 version of the M-1002. The reinstated language specifies that tank cars offered in a purged state under pressure must have all service fittings or fitting groups tagged with a yellow ‘CAUTION’ tag indicating pressure in the tank and the pressure at time of offering, with the tag made of material capable of withstanding a 30-day exposure to open weather without deterioration.


**Date:** 07-13-2026


**Effective Date:** 07-13-2026

### Call for Comment


**C-14817**


**Subject:** DRAFT S-9505.V1.0 Standardized List of ITC Default Railroad Data. in support of AAR’s MSRP – Section K-VI Railway Data Management and Communications


**Summary:** The Positive Train Control Interoperability Committee (PTCIC) of the Association of American Railroads (AAR) is publishing DRAFT S-9505.V1.0 Standardized List of ITC Default Railroad Data for review and comment. This standard defines the current list of default railroad data of certain Interoperable Train Control (ITC) components for all ITC railroads and is comprised of three parts including the standard format of the singular file containing all ITC railroad default data that can be used across the ITC Onboard PTC Applications (OPA), the list itself, and the default railroad data XML schema.


**Date:** 07-10-2026


**Comment Period:** 10 Days


**C-14826**


**Subject:** Solicitation of Comments, Car Repair Billing Data Exchange, Retiring the “Short” Price Master DAT File


**Summary:** The Car Repair Billing Committee Material Survey Rewrite TAG is considering retiring the “Short” AAR Price Master DAT file distributed by Railinc. Comments are solicited to determine the impact of retiring this file as redundant and only producing the “Long” Price Master DAT file.


**Date:** 07-22-2026


**Comment Period:** 30 Days


### Notice of Circulars


**C-14820**


**Subject:** Informational Circular: MSRP F – Sensors: Relocation of AEI standards and Section F reprint


**Summary:** The Association of American Railroads Equipment Health Monitoring Committee (EHMC) announces a new edition of the Manual of Standards and Recommended Practices (MSPR), Section F, SENSORS. This circular advises that AAR publications specific to automatic equipment identification (AEI) systems and data are being relocated from MSRP Section K-III to Section F – Sensors, with affected publications now residing in Section F.


**Date:** 07-14-2026


**Effective Date:** 07-14-2026


**C-14825**


**Subject:** Informational Circular, Canadian Car Repair Facility Labor Rate – Effective July 1, 2026


**Summary:** The Canadian Car Repair Billing Labor Rate calculation for April 1, 2026 is $268.34 CAD, which is an increase of one dollar and sixteen cents from the previous calculation. The currency conversion factor used to calculate the Health & Welfare portions of the labor rate has also changed, driving this quarter’s increase.


**Date:** 07-21-2026


**Effective Date:** 07-21-2026
