# RailPulse™ Brings Real-Time Visibility to Customers

*A telematics platform is helping railroads and shippers improve visibility, performance and network efficiency across North America.*


RailPulse™ is advancing how the rail industry captures, shares and uses data, delivering real-time visibility into railcar location, condition and performance.


Designed as a secure and standardized platform, RailPulse™ enables railroads, shippers and equipment owners to operate with greater transparency and coordination.


RailPulse™ devices can:


- Provide active GPS location tracking


- Monitor the open or closed status of doors, hatches or gates


- Measure when a railcar is loaded or unloaded


- Measure temperature and humidity


- Detect impacts that may lead to lading damage


“The device has accelerometers, so we can measure impacts. It gives us mechanical, position and speed data about the railcar. Additionally, the data shows exactly when the railcar is going to be delivered to a customer,” Senior Vice President of International Engineering, Product Development & Automation


Peter Jones said.


This secure data is encrypted and transmitted to the cloud, allowing fleet owners and shippers to access current information on their equipment. The added visibility helps shippers better plan inventory and operations, while railroads and fleet owners can improve asset utilization and monitor equipment performance.


As the freight rail industry continues to modernize, RailPulse™ is helping create a shared data foundation that improves efficiency, service and supply chain transparency across the North American rail network.


RailPulse™ is helping the industry move toward a single source of truth for railcar data. The platform helps locate lost or misplaced cars, identify misuse and support theft deterrence and faster claim resolution. Real‑time alerts on loading, unloading and dwell time, along with unit train and wheel‑health monitoring, give shippers, railroads and lessors a clearer picture of how their assets are performing. For Greenbrier teams and customers, the value is straightforward: better visibility, more accurate information and a more dependable rail network built on shared data.
