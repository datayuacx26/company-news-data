# Cipher Digital Announces Date of Second Quarter 2026 Business Update Conference Call

NEW YORK, July 21, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Cipher Digital Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=KpnjWmEga6UcAmNfVZX1yk2GX3Jo2mfiLTodjjIVhlGe4JKpSSG4HeC4h3fbJjfb_1i42f93TnF4wKpgvTZc6nQtPLuSNQle9w17VkRjBVE=) **(NASDAQ: CIFR)** (“Cipher” or the “Company”) today announced it will provide a business update and release its second quarter 2026 financial results before U.S. markets open on Tuesday, August 4th, 2026. Cipher will host a conference call and webcast that day at 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time.


The live webcast and a webcast replay of the conference call can be accessed from the investor relations section of Cipher’s website at[https://investors.cipherdigital.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=W4ghbXSrjGz3h2oVZ0UDxBAKW0Pemu-FqBcgs-3e0wnH9hCPkUgXGU42GUhJqj3aZlN3bRjACFhX375NsJoJHjC-pzx7lxHrVV6i9noRJCIZgtCN8SWuN5Pvqd7iScnBYnaf0D7NAcsTkuibI2ju3A==) .


**About Cipher**
Cipher develops and operates industrial-scale data centers engineered for next-generation computing at the highest standards of innovation, precision, and excellence. The Company brings together deep expertise across power sourcing, construction, engineering, operations, real estate, and technology to deliver high-quality data centers purpose built for HPC workloads. By partnering with premier tenants, Cipher seeks to meet the growing demand for industrial-scale data center capacity and become a leading HPC development platform that is built for hyperscale.
