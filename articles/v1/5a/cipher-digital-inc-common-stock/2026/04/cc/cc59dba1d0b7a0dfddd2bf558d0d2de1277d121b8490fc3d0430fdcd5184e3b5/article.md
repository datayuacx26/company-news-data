# Cipher Digital Announces Date of First Quarter 2026 Business Update Conference Call

NEW YORK, April 21, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Cipher Digital Inc.](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=XA0sW-UiN15nBopJwarFJHgPJaV7f6BYE8a9nZ_MTLtdRMMQKkl9oUNxPOIZcnbBv3xNbTBwO6Ci_mBn702eNUKRySO6f5hDw8XjpR9Yzac=) **(NASDAQ: CIFR)** (“Cipher” or the “Company”) today announced it will provide a business update and release its first quarter 2026 financial results before U.S. markets open on Tuesday, May 5th, 2026. Cipher will host a conference call and webcast that day at 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time.


The live webcast and a webcast replay of the conference call can be accessed from the investor relations section of Cipher’s website at[https://investors.cipherdigital.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=tY16x2efu4DaIRy53FV5y5LU3D8fYdbqEdYB0smi8TfLOww9CcTHusbAdgmDQITAOK4upRX4BL2TUFzBikqxEOocg1KLmZ7pZvz9U63G3MhFU06NiYMENowppg-mam2gWU0ytakG7L9NlXH03H5YPg==) .


**About Cipher**
Cipher develops and operates industrial-scale data centers engineered for next-generation computing at the highest standards of innovation, precision, and excellence. The Company brings together deep expertise across power sourcing, construction, engineering, operations, real estate, and technology to deliver high-quality data centers purpose built for HPC workloads. By partnering with premier tenants, Cipher seeks to meet the growing demand for industrial-scale data center capacity and become a leading HPC development platform that is built for hyperscale.
