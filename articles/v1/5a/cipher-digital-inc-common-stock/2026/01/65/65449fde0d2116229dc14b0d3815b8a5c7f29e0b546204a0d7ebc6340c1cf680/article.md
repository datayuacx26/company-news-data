# Cipher Mining appoints new executives to strengthen leadership team

NEW YORK - Cipher Mining Inc. (NASDAQ:CIFR) announced Tuesday the appointments of Lee Bratcher as Head of Policy and Government Affairs and Drew Armstrong as Head of Strategic Initiatives. The bitcoin mining company, which has seen its stock surge over 210% in the past year according to[InvestingPro](https://www.investing.com/pro/CIFR) data, continues to strengthen its leadership team amid rapid expansion.


Bratcher, who previously served as President and Founder of the Texas Blockchain Council, brings experience in energy regulatory matters and policy development. At the Texas Blockchain Council, he worked with policymakers and industry leaders, contributing to the development of legislation during both the 87th and 88th Texas Legislative Sessions. In his new role, Bratcher will lead Cipher’s energy policy strategy, represent the company in its ERCOT membership, and manage community and regulatory engagement efforts.


Armstrong joins Cipher from Cathedra Bitcoin (TSX-V:CBIT), where he served as President, Chief Operating Officer, and Chairman. His prior experience includes founding Galaxy Digital’s bitcoin mining business unit and working at Barclays on securitized products, including the first data center asset-backed security offering in the United States. Armstrong will lead strategic growth projects as the company expands into the high-performance computing (HPC) industry.


"As Cipher continues to grow into one of the world’s largest data center developers, adding experienced leaders of the caliber of Lee and Drew is critical," said Tyler Page, Chief Executive Officer, according to the company’s press release.


Bratcher will report to Cipher’s Co-President Will Iwaschuk, while Armstrong will report to Co-President Patrick Kelly.


Cipher Mining develops and operates industrial-scale data centers for bitcoin mining and HPC hosting. The company is listed on the Nasdaq under the ticker CIFR.


In other recent news, Cipher Mining Inc. has announced the acquisition of a 200-megawatt site in Ohio, named Ulysses, marking its first expansion outside Texas. This site includes 195 acres of land with secured capacity from AEP Ohio and is set to begin operations in the fourth quarter of 2027. In addition, Cipher Mining has completed a $333 million senior notes offering through its subsidiary, Cipher Compute LLC, to support its Texas facility. The notes, due in 2030, were issued as part of the same series as the previously issued $1.4 billion notes.


Cipher Mining also plans to redeem all outstanding warrants to purchase shares of its common stock that remain unexercised by December 26, 2025, for a redemption price of $0.01 per warrant. This follows the company meeting the threshold required under the Warrant Agreement for redemption. Analyst firms have shown confidence in Cipher Mining’s prospects, with Compass Point reiterating a Buy rating and a $28 price target after the Ohio expansion announcement. Similarly, H.C. Wainwright maintained its Buy rating with a $30 price target following a new AI colocation agreement with Fluidstack for its Barber Lake facility.
