## Place your Anthropologie in-store pickup order


1. Go to the Anthropologie website and start shopping for any item you want to purchase.
2. When viewing a product, select a size and color if needed.
3. Look under the product details for “Free In-Store Pickup” to see stores near you that have the item available.
4. If you don’t see any nearby stores listed, click on “Check other stores”. This will launch a modal where you can input your zip code and see which stores have the item in stock.
5. Add items to your cart by clicking “Add to Basket”.
6. When ready to checkout, proceed to the checkout page.
7. At checkout, be sure to select “Pick Up” instead of “Ship” as your delivery method.
8. Review your order and select your preferred store location. You can still change the store location by clicking “Edit Address” under “Order Review”.
9. Complete the checkout process.
10. You will receive a confirmation email that your order was placed, but wait for the second confirmation email that says your order is ready for pickup.
11. Once you receive the “ready for pickup” email, you’re ready to dispatch Getcho!


TIP: Keep your order confirmation number and take a screenshot of the pickup confirmation email. You’ll need to share these with your Getcho driver.
