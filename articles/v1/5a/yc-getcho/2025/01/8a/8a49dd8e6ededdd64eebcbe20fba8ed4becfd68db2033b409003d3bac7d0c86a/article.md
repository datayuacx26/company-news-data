1. Visit[aritzia.com](https://www.aritzia.com/us/en/home)
2. Start shopping and click on filters
3. Select “Ready to Ship” to remove sold out items
4. Find your desired product and select your size
5. Click “Pick up in Store” on the product page
6. Click “Check nearby stores” (allow location access for best results)
7. View store availability - click “Check all store inventory” for a full list
8. Select your preferred store and click “Add to bag”
9. Continue to cart and verify the correct store is selected under “Pick up at store”
10. Proceed to checkout (guest checkout available)
11. Confirm “Pick up store” selection with store image
12. Complete payment and wait for confirmation email
13. You’ll receive a second email when your order is ready for pickup
