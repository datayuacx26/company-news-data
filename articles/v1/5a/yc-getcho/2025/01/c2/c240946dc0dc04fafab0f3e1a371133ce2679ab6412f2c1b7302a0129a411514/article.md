Getcho makes it easy to get your Patagonia orders delivered right to your door. Here’s how to get started:


### **Place your Patagonia in-store pickup order**


1. Visit[patagonia.com](https://www.patagonia.com/)
2. While shopping, toggle “In-Store Pickup” on the left-hand side
3. The site will default to your closest store, but you can click “Change” to select a different location
4. Enter your zip code to see other store options and click “Set as Store” for your preferred location
5. Add available items to your cart (you can click “Check All Local Availability” to see stock at nearby stores)
6. At checkout, ensure in-store pickup is still selected
7. Complete your purchase and wait for the “Order Ready for Pickup” email
8. Once you receive confirmation that your order is ready, proceed with Getcho


**TIP:** Make sure to keep “In-Store Pickup” selected throughout your shopping experience to see accurate availability.

### **Pickup details**


1.


**Store address:** Make sure you enter the address for the right Patagonia location. Start typing Patagonia and you’ll be able to see the locations near you.


2.


**Name on order:** Enter the name you ordered with.


3.


**Phone number:** Enter your own phone number. That way, a driver can call or text you if they have any issues.

This form is pretty clear-cut. Enter your phone number and any notes that will help a driver find you to drop off your order (apartment number, special instructions, etc…).


Submit your order now, or schedule for later. Getcho will send you a tracking link to track your driver. Feel free to contact your driver and check in any time!
