Getcho makes it easy to get your Forever 21 orders delivered right to your door. Here’s how to get started:


### **Place your Forever 21 in-store pickup order**


1. Visit[forever21.com](https://www.forever21.com/) and start shopping
2. Toggle “Free Pickup At” to select your store


- Allow location access when prompted, or manually enter your zip code
- View “Stores Near You” and select your preferred location


3. Browse items and select your desired products
4. On the product page:


- Select your size to check store availability
- Click “Free Pickup” if the item is In Stock at your store
- If unavailable, click the store name to view other locations with stock


5. Add items to your cart
6. Proceed to checkout


- Verify “Free Pickup” is selected
- For “Pickup Contact” you can enter your information or “Getcho Courier”


7. Complete your purchase
8. Wait for the confirmation email stating your order is ready for pickup (typically within 2 hours)
9. Once you receive confirmation that your order is ready, proceed with Getcho


**TIP:** Make sure to wait for the final “ready for pickup” email before scheduling your Getcho delivery.
