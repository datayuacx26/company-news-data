# Get Your Foot Locker Order with Same-Day Delivery from Getcho


Need your sneakers or athletic wear from Foot Locker delivered to your home? Use Getcho for same-day delivery of your Foot Locker orders.


## **How to Getcho Foot Locker for Same Day Delivery**


Getcho makes it easy to get your Foot Locker orders delivered right to your door. Here’s how to get started:


### **Place your Foot Locker in-store pickup order**


1. Visit[footlocker.com](https://www.footlocker.com/) and start shopping
2. Click “Find a Store” to set your preferred location


- Allow location access or manually enter your zip code/address
- Select your store by clicking “Set My Store”


3. Browse items available for pickup at your selected store


- You can change stores anytime by clicking “Change Store”


4. On the product page, select your size to check store availability


- Look for “In-stock and available for pickup within 2 hours” under “Pick Up In Store”
- If unavailable, it will show “Ship to Store” instead


5. Add items to your cart
6. Proceed to checkout


- Verify “Pick Up In Store” is selected and shows “In-store and available for pickup within 2 hours”
- Confirm the store address is correct


7. Complete your purchase
8. Wait for the confirmation email stating your order is ready for pickup
9. Once you receive confirmation that your order is ready, proceed with Getcho


**TIP:** Make sure to wait for the final “ready for pickup” email before scheduling your Getcho delivery.


### **Download Getcho**


**Download the Getcho app** from the[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/getcho/id1460402210) or the[Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gtcho) and create a free account if you don’t have one yet.


### **Pickup details**


1.


**Store address:** Make sure you enter the address for the right Foot Locker location. Start typing Foot Locker and you’ll be able to see the locations near you.


2.


**Name on order:** Enter the name you ordered with.


3.


**Phone number:** Enter your own phone number. That way, a driver can call or text you if they have any issues.


4.


**Pickup notes:** Enter in all of the information your driver will need to find your order. Something like:


*“This is an in-store pickup order under Jane Smith. Order number is 123456. Call if you have any problems!”*


### **Input your drop-off details**


This form is pretty clear-cut. Enter your phone number and any notes that will help a driver find you to drop off your order (apartment number, special instructions, etc…).


Submit your order now, or schedule for later. Getcho will send you a tracking link to track your driver. Feel free to contact your driver and check in any time!


## **Help Your Driver Find Your Order**


Once your driver is on their way, you just have one more step: Send your driver a screenshot of the email confirmation you received.


That’s it! Your driver will grab your order and bring it straight to you. Enjoy the free time you saved by using Getcho.
