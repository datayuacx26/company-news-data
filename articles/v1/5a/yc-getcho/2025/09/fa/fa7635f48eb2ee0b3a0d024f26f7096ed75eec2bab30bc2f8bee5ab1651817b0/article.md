### Place your Mejuri curbside / in-store pickup order


1. Shop for your items on[mejuri.com](https://mejuri.com/)
2. On the detail view of each item, you’ll see where it’s in stock and available
3. If you click on the location (“Change Store”), you can select another store and check availability
4. A view will open showing all stores, where you can “Save Preferred Store”
5. Add your items to bag and proceed to checkout
6. At checkout, select “Pickup in Store” and choose your preferred location
7. Complete checkout and you will receive an order confirmation
8. Once your order is ready (typically within 4 hours), you’ll receive another email notification that you can now pick it up


TIP: Keep your order confirmation number and take a screenshot of the barcode. You’ll need to share these with your Getcho driver.
