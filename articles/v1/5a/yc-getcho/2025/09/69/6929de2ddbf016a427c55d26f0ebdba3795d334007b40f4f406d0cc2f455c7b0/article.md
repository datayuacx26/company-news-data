4. If the item is available, you’ll see your closest location appear alongside a few others
5. Select the location closest to you
6. Note: You will see processing times here, which can range from 1 to 5 days
7. Add to bag and proceed to checkout
8. Pickup should be automatically selected
9. Complete checkout and you will receive an order confirmation
10. Once your order is at the store and ready, you’ll receive another email notification


TIP: Keep your order confirmation number and take a screenshot of the barcode. You’ll need to share these with your Getcho driver.
