### Place your Container Store curbside / in-store pickup order


1. Go to[containerstore.com](https://www.containerstore.com/) and select your desired item
2. You’ll see “Store Pickup” and the amount left at the store nearest you
3. To switch stores, select the store name and type your zip code
4. You’ll see stores with the item in stock
5. You can toggle “Only In-Store Stores” and click on the store you want to change to
6. Or simply click “Pick Up Here” to keep the same store
7. Add to cart and proceed to checkout
8. In-store pickup should be automatically selected
9. Note: In certain zip codes, you’ll also see “Local Delivery” as an option where you can schedule the delivery
10. Review your order and complete checkout
11. You will receive a confirmation email
12. Once your order is ready, you’ll receive another email notification


TIP: Keep your order confirmation number and take a screenshot of the barcode. You’ll need to share these with your Getcho driver.
