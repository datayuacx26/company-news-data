# Get Your Rag & Bone Order with Same-Day Delivery from Getcho


Shopping for premium fashion at Rag & Bone just got even more convenient. With their seamless pick-up in store option and Getcho’s same-day delivery service, you can have your favorite Rag & Bone pieces delivered to your door within hours of placing your order.


## **How to Shop Rag & Bone for Same-Day Delivery**


Rag & Bone makes it incredibly easy to shop for items and pick them up at your nearest store location. Combined with Getcho’s delivery service, you can enjoy the convenience of same-day delivery without the wait.


### Browse and Select “Pick Up In Store”


1. Shop for any item on the Rag & Bone website
2. On the product detail page, if you haven’t set your location, click “Pick Up In Store”
3. Allow access to your location or enter your zip code
4. Browse the list of stores near you that have the item in stock
5. Select your preferred store location


### Complete Your Order


1. Once you select a store, you’ll see it confirmed in stock on the product page
2. Proceed to checkout when ready
3. You can checkout as a guest or as a returning customer
4. At checkout, you’ll see “Pick Up In Store” at the top under shipping with your selected store details and hours


### **Important Timing Information**


Keep in mind that items are typically “Available at our store in three business hours. We’ll email you when it’s ready.” Wait for that confirmation email before sending your Getcho driver to pick up your order.


## **Use Getcho for Pickup and Delivery**


Once you receive the email that your order is ready for pickup, use Getcho to have it delivered to you.


### **Download Getcho**


Download the Getcho app from the[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/getcho/id1460402210) or the[Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gtcho) and create a free account if you don’t have one yet.
