### Place your Fleet Feet curbside / in-store pickup order


1. Navigate to[fleetfeet.com](https://www.fleetfeet.com/) and browse for your items
2. On each item, you can click “Pick Up In Store”
3. Select a size, color, width, etc.
4. Click on “Pick Up In Store” to see a list of locations that have the item in stock
5. Select your preferred location
6. If you want to change locations, click “Change Location” and retype your zip code
7. Add items to your cart and proceed to checkout
8. On the checkout page, you’ll see store pickup already selected
9. Input your details (including “Delivery” information - this is just for your contact information)
10. Complete your payment
11. You will receive a confirmation email
12. Once your order is ready, you’ll receive another email notification


TIP: Keep your order confirmation number and take a screenshot of the barcode. You’ll need to share these with your Getcho driver.
