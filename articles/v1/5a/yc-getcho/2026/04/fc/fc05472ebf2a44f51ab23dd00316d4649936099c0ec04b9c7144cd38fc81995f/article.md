## What “Built for Shopify” actually means


Built for Shopify is Shopify’s way of vouching for an app. To earn the badge, an app has to clear Shopify’s bar across several dimensions:


- **Performance** — loads fast, doesn’t slow down the merchant admin, and uses modern best practices.
- **Security & privacy** — handles merchant and customer data correctly, with proper OAuth scopes, webhook configuration, and data protection.
- **Ease of use** — follows Shopify’s UX guidelines, uses Polaris components, and integrates cleanly into the native admin.
- **Merchant value** — real installs, real reviews, and measurable results for the merchants who use it.


Apps that earn the badge also get better visibility in[the Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/) , additional trust signals during discovery, and priority placement in search results.


## Why merchants should care


If you’re a Shopify merchant evaluating delivery apps, the Built for Shopify badge is a shortcut. Specifically, it tells you four things:


1. **The app won’t slow your store down.** Getcho has been benchmarked against Shopify’s performance standards. You can install it without worrying about checkout speed or admin load times.
2. **Your data is handled correctly.** Shopify has reviewed our data handling, webhook configuration, and OAuth scopes. We don’t ask for permissions we don’t need.
3. **The app feels native.** Getcho lives inside your Shopify admin the way a first-party feature would. No clunky iframes, no separate dashboards for simple tasks.
4. **Real merchants are getting value.** Built for Shopify requires verified merchant reviews and a track record of solving real operational problems — not just installs.


## What Getcho does for Shopify stores


For context — if you’re new to Getcho — we turn your Shopify store into a same-day delivery operation:


- **Automatic order sync.** Every eligible Shopify order flows into Getcho in real time. No CSVs, no copy-pasting addresses.
- **Multi-location routing.** Getcho reads your Shopify locations and inventory. Orders automatically route from the nearest store with stock.
- **Auto-dispatch on fulfillment.** When you mark an order fulfilled in Shopify, a driver is dispatched automatically — matched by vehicle size, timing, and distance.
- **Branded tracking pages.** Your customers see[your brand](https://getcho.app/services/branded-tracking/) on tracking pages, SMS, and email — not a generic courier logo.
- **Shopify POS support.** Dispatch deliveries from in-store purchases just as easily as online orders.
- **Returns, rentals, and[inventory transfers](https://getcho.app/services/inventory-transfers/) .** Same courier network handles pickups, round-trip rentals, and store-to-store transfers.


And because Getcho connects to a multi-fleet network — Roadie, Uber, DoorDash, Frayt, Bungii, and more — you’re not locked into a single courier. Orders route to the best available fleet in real time, so reliability doesn’t depend on one provider’s coverage that day.
