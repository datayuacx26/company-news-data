# Get Your H&M Order with Same-Day Delivery from Getcho


H&M is built for the trend you want *right now* —so waiting days for shipping kind of defeats the point. Good news: H&M offers **free in-store pickup** , and[Getcho](https://getcho.app/how-getcho-works/) turns that pickup into a same-day delivery. Place your order online, and we’ll send a driver to grab it and bring it straight to your door. Fast fashion that’s actually fast.


## **How to Getcho H&M for same-day delivery**


Getcho is an on-demand delivery service with low prices and great customer service. Alongside ordering from stores and businesses, you can use Getcho to pick up your H&M in-store pickup orders. Place your order on[hm.com](https://www2.hm.com/en_us/index.html) and use the Getcho app to send a driver to collect it for you the same day.


### Place your H&M free in-store pickup order


1. Shop for your items on[hm.com](https://www2.hm.com/en_us/index.html) or in the H&M app
2. (Handy shortcut) On any category page, set **“Pick up today at: \[store\]”** to filter the whole catalog down to items available for pickup at your store today
3. On a product page, choose your size and select **“Free Pick Up At \[your store\]“** —it confirms your size is available and “Ready within 4 hours”
4. To pick or change your store, search by city, address, or zip code; H&M shows per-size availability at nearby stores (Available / Few available / Out of stock)
5. Add your items to the bag, proceed to checkout, and confirm **Pick up in store** ($0.00). Note the cutoff: **order before 4pm for same-day pickup**
6. Complete checkout. H&M emails/texts you when your order is ready— **usually within 4 hours** . In many locations, pickup is via a **self-service locker** , and the email includes a **code to open the locker**


TIP: When your “ready for pickup” email arrives, screenshot it—especially the **locker code** (many H&M stores use self-service pickup lockers). Your Getcho driver uses that code to grab the order straight from the locker. At staffed pickup counters, they’ll show the pickup email and order number instead.

1.

2.


**Name on order** : Enter the name you ordered with.


3.

### **Input your drop-off details**


Enter your phone number and any notes that will help a driver find you to drop off your order (apartment number, special instructions, etc…)


Submit your order now or schedule for later. Getcho will send you a tracking link to follow your driver. Feel free to contact your driver and check in anytime!


## **Help your driver find your order**


Once your driver is en route, you have just one more step: send your driver a screenshot of the “ready for pickup” email/text and your order number so they can collect it at the H&M pickup counter or locker.


That’s it! Your driver picks up your order and delivers it to your door. Enjoy the time you saved by using Getcho.
