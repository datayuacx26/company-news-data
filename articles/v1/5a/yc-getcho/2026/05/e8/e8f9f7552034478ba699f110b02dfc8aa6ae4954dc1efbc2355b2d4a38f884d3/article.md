One skein short on yarn, out of a paint color, missing a frame size—and standard shipping wants three days. Michaels offers free **same-day Store Pickup** , and[Getcho](https://getcho.app/how-getcho-works/) turns that into a same-day delivery: place the order online, and we’ll send a driver to grab it and bring it to your door. ## **How to Getcho Michaels for same-day delivery**


Getcho is an on-demand delivery service with low prices and great customer service. Alongside ordering from stores and businesses, you can use Getcho to pick up your Michaels Store Pickup orders. Place your order on[michaels.com](https://www.michaels.com/) and use the Getcho app to send a driver to collect it for you the same day.


### Place your Michaels Store Pickup order


1. Shop for your items on[michaels.com](https://www.michaels.com/)
2. On each item, check the **Store Pickup** option—it shows whether it’s in stock at your store, how many are available, and the aisle (e.g. “11 In Stock — Main Aisle”). Note: some bulk and “Online Only” items aren’t available for pickup.
3. To choose or change your store, search by city, state, or zip and pick a location showing **“Store Pickup TODAY”**
4. Add your items to the cart and select **“Pick up Today — Free”**
5. Proceed to checkout (“Getting your Order”), confirm your Pickup Location, and fill in the **Pickup Person Information**
6. Complete checkout and you’ll receive an order confirmation. You’ll get a notification (and optional text updates) when your order is ready


TIP: Michaels notes that the **pickup person must bring the order number and their ID** to collect the order. Keep your order confirmation number handy and take a screenshot of it—you’ll share it with your Getcho driver.


### **Download Getcho**


Download the Getcho app from the[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/getcho/id1460402210) or the[Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gtcho) and create a free account if you don’t have one yet.


### **Input your pickup details**


1.


**Store address** : Make sure you enter the address for the right Michaels location. Start typing “Michaels” and you’ll see the locations near you.


2.


**Name on order** : Enter the name you ordered with.


3.


**Phone number** : Enter your own phone number so a driver can call or text you if there are any issues.


4.


**Pickup Notes** : Enter everything your driver needs to collect your order. Something like:


*“This is a Store Pickup order under John Smith. Order # is 10805800. Please bring the order number to the pickup counter. Call if you have any problems!”*


### **Input your drop-off details**


Enter your phone number and any notes that will help a driver find you to drop off your order (apartment number, special instructions, etc…)


Submit your order now or schedule for later. Getcho will send you a tracking link to follow your driver. Feel free to contact your driver and check in anytime!


## **Help your driver find your order**


Once your driver is en route, you have just one more step: send your driver your order number (and a screenshot of the confirmation) so they can collect it at the Michaels pickup counter.


That’s it! Your driver picks up your order and delivers it to your door. Enjoy the time you saved by using Getcho.
