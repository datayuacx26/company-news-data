# Get Your Ulta Beauty Order with Same-Day Delivery from Getcho


Out of your go-to foundation the morning of an event? Need a last-minute gift set or a skincare refill *today* ? Ulta Beauty offers free **in-store pickup** , with most orders ready in **2 hours or less** —and[Getcho](https://getcho.app/how-getcho-works/) turns that into a same-day delivery. Place your order online, and we’ll send a driver to grab it and bring it straight to your door. No drive to the store, no shipping wait.


## **How to Getcho Ulta for same-day delivery**


Getcho is an on-demand delivery service with low prices and great customer service. Alongside ordering from stores and businesses, you can use Getcho to pick up your Ulta in-store pickup orders. Place your order on[ulta.com](https://www.ulta.com/) and use the Getcho app to send a driver to collect it for you the same day.


### Place your Ulta in-store pickup order


1. Shop for your items on[ulta.com](https://www.ulta.com/)
2. (Handy shortcut) Use the **“In Store”** filter and check **“Available at \[your store\]”** to see only items you can pick up nearby today
3. On a product page, under **“Pickup and delivery options,”** choose **Pickup** ( **Ready in 2 hours or less** , free) and confirm your store, then **Add for pickup**
4. In your bag, make sure each item is set to **Pick up** at your chosen store, then proceed to checkout
5. On the **Pickup in store** screen, fill in the Pickup Information. If you want your Getcho driver to collect it, expand **“Alternate pickup person (optional)”** and add their details
6. Place your order. You’ll get an order notification by email, and a “ready for pickup” alert—usually within **2 hours or less**


TIP: Keep your order confirmation number and take a screenshot of the “ready for pickup” email. You’ll share these with your Getcho driver, and adding them as the alternate pickup person makes collection seamless.


### **Download Getcho**


Download the Getcho app from the[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/getcho/id1460402210) or the[Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gtcho) and create a free account if you don’t have one yet.


### **Input your pickup details**


1.


**Store address** : Make sure you enter the address for the right Ulta location. Start typing “Ulta” and you’ll see the locations near you.


2.


**Name on order** : Enter the name you ordered with (or the alternate pickup person, if you added one).


3.


**Phone number** : Enter your own phone number so a driver can call or text you if there are any issues.


4.


**Pickup Notes** : Enter everything your driver needs to collect your order. Something like:


*“This is an in-store pickup order under John Smith. Order # is 1612273900. I’ve added the driver as an alternate pickup person. Call if you have any problems!”*


### **Input your drop-off details**


Enter your phone number and any notes that will help a driver find you to drop off your order (apartment number, special instructions, etc…)


Submit your order now or schedule for later. Getcho will send you a tracking link to follow your driver. Feel free to contact your driver and check in anytime!


## **Help your driver find your order**


Once your driver is en route, you have just one more step: send your driver a screenshot of the “ready for pickup” email and your order number so they can collect it at the Ulta pickup counter.


That’s it! Your driver picks up your order and delivers it to your door. Enjoy the time you saved by using Getcho.
