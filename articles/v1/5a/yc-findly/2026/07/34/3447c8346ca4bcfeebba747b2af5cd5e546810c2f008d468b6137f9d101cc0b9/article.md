# Findly Selected for Google for Startups Accelerator: AI First

## A New Chapter in Our Journey


‍


This selection marks a significant milestone in[Findly's](http://dashboard.findly.ai/) journey. It's a testament to the hard work and innovation of our team, and the belief in our vision to make data accessible and understandable for everyone.


‍


### The Power of Partnership 🤝


Through this partnership with Google, we'll have the opportunity to work hand-in-hand with their experts to leverage their most advanced AI technologies.


‍


‍


## About the program


‍


Our journey with the[Google for Startups Accelerator: AI First](https://startup.google.com/programs/accelerator/ai-first/north-america/) begins at Google Cloud Next 2024, where we'll be showcasing[Findly](http://dashboard.findly.ai/) on stage at the AI First Accelerator Startup Showcase. The program will conclude with an exclusive Demo Day in June.


‍


### What This Means for Our Users 👥


Through this accelerator, we'll be supercharging[Findly's](http://dashboard.findly.ai/) ML capabilities thanks to Googles experience and resources, to help deliver better insights. Get ready for a more personalized and efficient data experience!


‍


## Stay Tuned!


‍


We'll be sharing updates throughout our journey in the accelerator on our[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/86297460) and[X](https://twitter.com/findlyai) profiles.


‍


In the meantime, keep those data questions coming! Findly is always ready to chat. 😄
