## Knowledge Base Support on AWS and Azure


Garrett VanLee


June 29, 2026


Connect and interact with all your data in one place. Rescale now connects engineering data stored in AWS S3 and Azure Blob Storage directly to the Rescale Assistant through vectorized search. Once connected, engineers can ask natural language questions and receive fast, contextually grounded answers drawn from their organization’s own data, rather than relying only on general AI knowledge or manual file searches.


[Learn more](https://rescale.com/documentation/rescale-data-connectors/) about Rescale’s data connectors to explore how to connect and harness your engineering data sources.
