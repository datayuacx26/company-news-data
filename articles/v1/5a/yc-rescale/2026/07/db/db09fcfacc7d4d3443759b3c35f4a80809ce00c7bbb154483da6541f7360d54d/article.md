## Simulation Monitoring Automation


Garrett VanLee


July 10, 2026


Detect diverging and stalled simulations in real time, before they waste hours of compute. The Simulation Monitor automation runs alongside iterative solver jobs on Rescale, providing a live dashboard with residual plots, tabular data switching, and automated CSV exports without requiring an interactive workstation. It supports Ansys Fluent, STAR-CCM+, and CFX today, with a plugin-based framework designed for fast extension to additional solvers.


[Learn more](https://rescale.com/platform/advanced-modeling-simulation/) about how Rescale automations streamline routine modeling and simulation tasks.
