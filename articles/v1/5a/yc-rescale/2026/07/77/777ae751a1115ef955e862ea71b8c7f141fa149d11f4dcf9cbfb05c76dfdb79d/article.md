## Rescale Interlink: Open-Source Data Transfer and Job Management


Garrett VanLee


July 17, 2026


Move simulation data between your desktop and the cloud faster with Rescale Interlink, an open-source hybrid CLI and GUI tool for managing files and jobs on Rescale. FIPS 140-3 compliant and available on Windows, Mac, and Linux, Interlink delivers multithreaded parallel transfers, a visual file browser, auto-download of completed job results, and full job submission, all in one lightweight, community-driven application.


[Learn more](https://github.com/rescale-labs/Rescale_Interlink) about Rescale Interlink in the public GitHub repo.
