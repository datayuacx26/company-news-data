## File Sharing Across Multi-Step Simulation Workflows


Garrett VanLee


July 31, 2026


Move files across multi-step workflows without manual hand-offs or separate storage setup. Rescale Workflows can now use cloud storage to pass files directly between job and workstation steps, helping engineering teams keep preprocessing, solve, and post-processing stages connected inside one reusable workflow.


Watch the demo below and[learn more](https://rescale.com/platform/workflows/) about Rescale Workflows for building and executing deterministic, multi-step simulation processes.
