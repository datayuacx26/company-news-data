## Parametric Optimization via Local AI Inference


Andy Nicolas


August 7, 2026


Explore more design options faster with local AI Physics inference. Rescale’s inference tooling lets engineers connect geometry, surrogate models, and outputs in a lightweight design workflow so they can run parametric studies and optimization loops directly on their own device (e.g. local workstation) without needing inference servers or HPC clusters. These capabilities are coming soon to customers and we’re excited to share this preview of what’s possible with Rescale AI Physics.
