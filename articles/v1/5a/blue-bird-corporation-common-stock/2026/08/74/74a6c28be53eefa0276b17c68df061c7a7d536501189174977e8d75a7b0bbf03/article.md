# Blue Bird’s Vision Electric Bus Named “The Coolest Thing Made in Georgia”

**Macon, Ga. (August 5, 2026) –** Blue Bird Corporation (Nasdaq: BLBD) walked away from the[Georgia Chamber](https://www.gachamber.com/) ’s annual Congressional Luncheon at The Classic Center with the title “[The Coolest Thing Made in Georgia](https://www.gachamber.com/news/blue-bird-vision-electric-school-bus-wins-coolest-thing-made-in-georgia-competition/) ,” beating out a field of standout products chosen by public vote.


“We’d like to thank the Georgia Chamber and all of the voters for being named ‘The Coolest Thing Made in Georgia’ for our zero-emissions electric school bus. This recognition reflects years of innovation, dedication and a shared belief that student transportation can be safer, cleaner and, yes, apparently cooler than we ever imagined,” said John Wyskiel, Blue Bird’s president and CEO.


The contest ran as a social media bracket. Georgia manufacturers, makers and creators entered their products, then went head-to-head across a series of rounds. The public decided who advanced.


“Public voting drove the entire contest, which makes the win land differently than an industry panel picking a favorite,” said Lauren Beaty, Blue Bird’s grants and community engagement manager. “Georgians looked at our innovative electric school bus and decided it belonged at the top.”


Blue Bird is transforming the student transportation industry through cleaner energy solutions. The company is the market leader in low- and zero-emission school buses with the highest percentage of non-diesel buses sold in student transportation. As part of Georgia’s manufacturing story for close to a century, the Vision Electric bus is one of its alternative-fuel models made in Fort Valley.


Blue Bird’s Vision Electric bus was named “The Coolest Thing Made in Georgia” in a statewide contest.
