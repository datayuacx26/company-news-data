## **Summer Maintenance Checklist**


To help transportation departments prepare for the upcoming school year, here are several essential areas technicians should review during summer school bus maintenance.


#### **Inspect First Aid and Body Fluid Kits**


Safety equipment should always be one of the first items reviewed during seasonal maintenance. Technicians should confirm all first aid kits and body fluid cleanup kits are fully stocked, properly secured and compliant with district and/or state requirements.


#### **Review Cross Arms and Stop Arms**


***School bus stop arm inspection during summer maintenance checks ensures high visibility and proper electrical deployment for student loading zone safety.***


Cross arms and stop arms are among the most visible and important safety components on a school bus. During summer inspections, technicians should examine these systems for:


- Cracks or physical damage
- Loose mounting hardware
- Electrical or deployment issues
- Visibility concerns or faded reflective material


Any malfunctioning equipment should be repaired or replaced promptly to help maintain safe loading and unloading procedures for students.


#### **Check Seat Covers and Foam**


Bus interiors experience heavy daily wear throughout the school year. Torn seat covers, damaged foam or loose seat components can create both safety and comfort concerns.


Summer is the ideal time to:


- Replace worn seat covers
- Repair damaged seat foam
- Inspect seat frames and hardware
- Clean and refresh bus interiors


Interior refreshes also help improve the overall experience for drivers and students alike.


#### **Inspect Interior and Exterior Lighting**


***Inspection of school bus brake lights and exterior safety lighting systems ensures optimal visibility, driver communication, and regulatory compliance on daily routes.***


Lighting systems are essential for visibility, communication and safety. Technicians should conduct a full inspection of both interior and exterior lights, including:


- Warning lights
- Marker lights
- Turn signals
- Brake lights
- Interior dome lights
- Stepwell lighting


Lighting issues that seem minor during summer inspections can quickly become major operational problems once routes resume.


#### **Consider LED Lighting Upgrades**


Many fleets are also using summer maintenance periods to modernize buses with LED lighting upgrades.


LED systems can provide several benefits, including:


- Improved brightness and visibility
- Longer service life
- Reduced maintenance frequency
- Better energy efficiency


For fleets managing aging buses, upgrades like LED lighting can help improve reliability while reducing future maintenance demands.


#### **Examine Windshields and Mirrors**


***Properly aligned school bus mirrors and a clear windshield assembly is vital for maximum driver visibility and safe fleet operations.***


Clear visibility is critical for safe school bus operations. Windshields should be checked for:


- Chips and cracks
- Worn seals
- Visibility impairments
- Wiper wear and performance


Mirrors should also be inspected to ensure proper alignment, stability and visibility coverage. Replacing worn or damaged components before the school year begins can help drivers maintain maximum visibility on routes.
