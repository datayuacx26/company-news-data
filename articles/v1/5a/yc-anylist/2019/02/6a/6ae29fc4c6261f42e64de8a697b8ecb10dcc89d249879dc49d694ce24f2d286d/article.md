# AnyList Complete Pricing Update

Effective March 1, 2019 the price of[AnyList Complete](https://www.anylist.com/complete) subscriptions will be changing as follows:


- The price of a one year individual subscription will increase from US$7.99 to US$9.99.
- The price of a one year family subscription will increase from US$11.99 to US$14.99.


We always strive to offer straightforward pricing that delivers an excellent value, and as part of that effort we’ve held AnyList Complete prices constant since 2014. However, we do need to raise prices occasionally as expenses grow due to inflation and other factors.


We’ve also dramatically expanded the functionality of AnyList Complete since 2014, adding features such as a[desktop web app](https://www.anylist.com/web/about) , a[Mac app](https://www.anylist.com/mac) , an[Apple Watch app](https://help.anylist.com/articles/apple-watch/) ,[import of recipes from the web](https://help.anylist.com/articles/feature-overview-recipe-import/) , a[meal planning calendar](https://help.anylist.com/articles/meal-planning-calendar-add-recipe-ingredients/) ,[stores & filters](https://help.anylist.com/articles/anylist-feature-overview-stores-and-filters/) , recipe scaling,[web image search](https://help.anylist.com/articles/add-item-photo/) ,[list folders](https://help.anylist.com/articles/move-lists/) , and[item prices](https://help.anylist.com/articles/anylist-feature-overview-item-prices/) .


While we know that no one likes to see prices increase, we hope you’ll be understanding of the need for a modest increase to the price of AnyList Complete.


We greatly appreciate your continued support, which is what allows us to properly maintain the existing app features, continually improve the app with new features, bring the app to new platforms such as[Amazon Alexa](https://help.anylist.com/articles/anylist-skill-for-amazon-alexa/) and[Android](https://www.anylist.com/android) , and provide great, personal customer support.


We’ve got lots planned for 2019 and beyond, and we always welcome your suggestions for what to work on next. If you have questions or suggestions,[contact us](https://www.anylist.com/contact) and we’ll get back to you. Thanks for your support!


– Jason & Jeff
