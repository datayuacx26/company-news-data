# Introducing Online Shopping

We’re excited to announce that you can now **shop online for groceries** , right from AnyList! We support many retailers and services, including Instacart, Walmart, Kroger, Safeway, Albertsons, Amazon Fresh, H-E-B, and Shipt.


Online shopping is currently available in the latest version of our iOS app, for customers in the United States. Support for other platforms and countries will be considered for the future.


For full details, including step-by-step screenshots and a complete list of supported retailers and services, please see our help site:


[Online Grocery Shopping Overview →](https://help.anylist.com/articles/feature-overview-online-shopping/)


This functionality has been highly requested over the past few years, so we’re excited that it’s now available! If you have any questions or feedback, please[let us know](https://www.anylist.com/contact) . Your feedback is greatly appreciated, and helps us to make AnyList more useful for everyone.
