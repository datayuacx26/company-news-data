# PowerBank Secures New Opportunity in Nova Scotia

*PowerBank to support local engineering firm in solar consulting services for Victoria County*


**Toronto, Ontario, July 30, 2026 — PowerBank Corporation (Nasdaq: PBK) (Cboe CA: PBK) (FSE: 103)** (“ **PowerBank** ” or the " **Company** "), a leader in independent energy development and asset ownership in North America, is pleased to announce it will be supporting TriMac Engineering of Sydney, Nova Scotia (“ **TriMac** ”) with solar feasibility studies and related technical and development assessments for solar consulting services for the Municipality of the County of Victoria (“ **Victoria County** ” or “ **the Municipality** ”) in Nova Scotia. The contract was executed between TriMac and the Municipality on July 8, 2026, and PowerBank will be supporting TriMac through a fee for service agreement.


Victoria County issued a request for proposals (“ **RFP** ”) on June 20, 2026, for consulting services to develop the Victoria County Solar Strategy: Solar Feasibility and Implementation Roadmap. In the tender document for the RFP, the Municipality said, “The Municipality is advancing this work in response to increasing energy reliability concerns, rising utility costs, and climate-related disruptions.”


The work will involve evaluating municipal buildings and properties across Victoria County to identify suitable solar opportunities and develop an implementation roadmap. The assessment may consider rooftop and ground-mounted solar, depending on the characteristics and feasibility of each property. Potential system sizes will be determined through the feasibility studies based on factors such as available roof or land area, electricity consumption, structural or site constraints, interconnection capacity and project economics. As part of the feasibility and implementation work, the team may evaluate available federal, provincial or utility funding and incentive programs that could support future solar installations identified through the studies.


PowerBank has an established partnership with TriMac, as they have been working together on the development of three Community Solar Projects in Nova Scotia under the Provincial Community Solar program that are expected to have a combined capacity of 12.4 MW. Read more about the three projects in the most recent update


[here](https://powerbankcorp.com/news/powerbank-provides-progress-update-on-three-community-solar-projects-in-nova-scotia) .


PowerBank’s proven expertise, with over 100 MW of completed projects and a development pipeline exceeding 1 GW, underpins the project’s execution. Strategic partnerships and institutional-grade development capabilities position PowerBank to deliver reliable, high-impact energy solutions. These capabilities are increasingly valuable as AI and data center growth place unprecedented demand on the North American grid.
