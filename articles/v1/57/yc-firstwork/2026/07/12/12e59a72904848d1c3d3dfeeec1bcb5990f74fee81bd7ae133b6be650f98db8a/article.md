# What Are the Top 10 AI Callers for SMBs and Enterprise Use

## **What is an AI caller?**


An AI caller is an automated voice or conversational AI tool that contacts candidates on behalf of a recruiting or staffing team. Using natural language processing and voice synthesis, AI callers can screen applicants, schedule interviews, send reminders, collect documents, and follow up without a human coordinator making each call manually.


In 2026, AI callers range from simple power dialers with AI-assisted scripts to fully autonomous voice agents capable of holding multi-turn conversations, handling objections, and escalating to a human when needed.


The best AI callers for frontline staffing agencies do three things well:


- Speed: candidate outreach within minutes of an offer as opposed to hours
- Persistence: automated multi-touch follow-up sequences that do not give up after one attempt
- Handoff logic: knowing when to escalate to a human coordinator and when to keep the automation running


## **Why AI callers matter**


Candidate drop-off between offer and first shift is one of the most expensive problems in frontline staffing. Industry-wide annual loss reported to be close to 2.3 billion USD, specific to staffing*. The gap is caused by a lack of timely, consistent follow-up.


AI callers close that gap by automating the outreach that coordinators do not have time to do manually, at scale, without adding headcount. Agencies using AI calling tools report improvements in fill rates, reductions in no-shows, and faster time-to-first-shift across their candidate pipelines.


Most AI callers are built for the top of the funnel: sourcing and screening. There is a clear dearth of AI callers that are built for what happens after the offer, which is where most candidate drop-off actually occurs.

## **Frequently asked questions about AI callers**


**What is an AI caller?**


An AI caller is an automated tool that uses voice AI and natural language processing to contact candidates on behalf of a team. It can handle screening, scheduling, follow-up, reminders, and document collection without a human making each call. In staffing, AI callers are used to increase outreach volume, reduce manual coordinator workload, and improve candidate engagement throughout the hiring process.


**What is the best AI caller for staffing agencies in 2026?**


The best AI caller depends on which part of the funnel you are automating. For post-offer automation and reducing candidate drop-off, Firstwork is the only platform built specifically for the offer-to-first-shift problem in frontline staffing. For high-volume outbound sourcing, Bland AI and Salesfinity are strong choices. For no-code deployment, Synthflow leads the market.


**How do AI callers reduce candidate drop-off?**


AI callers reduce candidate drop-off by automating the follow-up touchpoints that human coordinators cannot complete at scale. After a candidate accepts an offer, an AI caller can immediately send confirmation, collect required documents, send onboarding reminders, and follow up if a candidate goes quiet, all without coordinator involvement. Platforms like Firstwork are purpose-built for this post-offer automation workflow.


**What is the difference between an AI caller and a power dialler?**


A power dialer automates the dialing process but still requires a human to handle the conversation. An AI caller replaces the human in the conversation entirely, using voice AI to speak with candidates, respond to questions, and complete structured workflows autonomously. Some platforms, like Kixie and Salesfinity, sit in between, using AI to assist human callers rather than replace them.


**Can AI callers integrate with staffing ATS platforms?**


Yes. Most leading AI callers in 2026 offer integrations with major staffing ATS platforms, including Bullhorn, Greenhouse, Workday, and JobAdder. Synthflow and Thoughtly offer native integrations. Bland AI and Retell AI support integrations via API. Firstwork is designed to integrate with the ATS and onboarding infrastructure used by frontline staffing agencies.

AI caller pricing in 2026 varies significantly by platform and usage volume. Developer-focused tools like Retell AI and Bland AI typically charge per minute of call time, ranging from $0.05 to $0.15 per minute. No-code platforms like Synthflow and Thoughtly offer monthly subscription plans starting from around $500 per month. Enterprise platforms like NICE CXone require custom pricing.
