# Firstwork + Yardstik: Automated Screening & Verification

Frontline hiring operates on a simple equation: speed minus friction equals fill rate. Every hour a qualified applicant spends waiting for a background check to clear is an hour your operation runs short-staffed. Every manual handoff between your ATS, your screening vendor, and your onboarding system is a point where workers drop out, and your cost-per-hire climbs.


The gap is structural. Most screening workflows were designed for knowledge workers applying to one job at a time. Frontline hiring doesn't work that way. A warehouse operator, a home health aide, a delivery driver: they're applying to multiple roles simultaneously. The employer who clears them fastest wins the hire.


That's why we built a native integration with[Yardstik](https://yardstik.com/) , the Human Trust Platform purpose-built for high-volume workforce screening, identity verification, and credential checks.


The result: background screening, MVR checks, drug screening, license verification, employment verification, and identity verification, all triggered automatically inside Firstwork activation flows, with results returned directly into the same workflow.


No platform-switching. No recruiter follow-up loops. No applicant drop-off while compliance catches up.


## What the Integration Does


When an applicant reaches a defined stage in a Firstwork activation flow, the system automatically initiates the appropriate Yardstik screening package. The specific checks triggered depend on the role, the industry, and the employer's compliance requirements.


Yardstik screening report, automatically available on Firstwork


Here's what that looks like in practice, mapped to real frontline use cases.


### Use Case 1: Criminal Background Checks for Staffing Agencies


**The problem:** A staffing firm places 500+ light industrial workers per month across client sites. Each client has different screening requirements — some need county-level criminal checks, others require national database searches plus federal court records. Recruiters spend hours manually initiating checks, tracking statuses across platforms, and chasing down results before they can confirm placements.


**How it works:** When an applicant completes document upload in Firstwork, the system triggers the client-specific Yardstik screening package automatically. County criminal searches, national database checks, and federal records searches run in parallel. Results flow back into the Firstwork workflow. If the applicant clears, they advance to onboarding. If the report surfaces a record, it's flagged for review, all within the same interface.


**The outcome:** Recruiters stop toggling between systems. Applicants stop waiting in limbo. Time from application to placement compresses from days to hours.


### Use Case 2: MVR Checks and License Verification for Logistics and Delivery


**The problem:** A last-mile delivery company onboards drivers across multiple metros. Every driver needs a motor vehicle record check, a valid commercial or standard license verification, and in some cases, DOT compliance screening. Drivers who pass all checks but experience a multi-day delay between application and activation often accept a competing offer in the interim.


**How it works:** Firstwork's activation flow triggers Yardstik's MVR check and license verification as soon as the applicant submits their driver's license through Firstwork. The system validates that the license is current, checks the driving record for disqualifying violations, and returns the results to Firstwork. Cleared drivers move immediately into vehicle assignment and route training.


**The outcome:** Drivers reach their first delivery faster. The company reduces the dropout rate that costs them every time a qualified driver takes another offer during a screening delay.


### Use Case 3: Healthcare Screening and Credential Verification


**The problem:** A home health agency must verify that every caregiver holds valid certifications (CNA, HHA, CPR), passes a criminal background check, clears drug screening, and has verified employment history before they can be assigned to a patient. Manual coordination across screening vendors, state licensing databases, and internal compliance teams creates a bottleneck that delays staffing by a week or more.


**How it works:** When a caregiver applicant submits a form on Firstwork, the system initiates Yardstik's healthcare screening package: criminal background check, drug screen, employment verification, and professional license verification, all triggered simultaneously. Firstwork tracks credential expiration dates post-hire, flagging renewals before they lapse.


**The outcome:** Caregivers move from application to patient assignment in days instead of weeks. The agency maintains continuous compliance without manual tracking spreadsheets.


### Use Case 4: Sequential Screening for High-Volume Seasonal Hiring


**The problem:** A hospitality chain ramps up 2,000+ seasonal workers every summer. Running full screening packages on every applicant is expensive, especially when a significant percentage drop out before completing the process. The company pays for background checks on candidates who never show up for orientation.


**How it works:** Firstwork's activation flow leverages Yardstik's Sequential Screening methodology. The process starts with identity verification and a national database search, fast, low-cost checks that filter out unqualified or disengaged applicants early. Only applicants who pass and continue engaging move to the next stage: county criminal checks, drug screening, and employment verification. Each stage is triggered automatically within the Firstwork workflow based on applicant progress.


**The outcome:** Screening costs drop because expensive checks only run on applicants who are verified, engaged, and progressing. The company fills seasonal roles faster without overspending on candidates who were never going to complete the process.


Every step is logged. Every check is auditable. Every applicant has a single, continuous record from first click to first shift.


## What This Means for Hiring Teams


This integration eliminates the operational tax that frontline hiring teams pay every time they switch between a hiring platform and a screening vendor. The specific gains:


1. **Reduced time-to-hire.** Screening runs in parallel with other activation steps instead of blocking them. Applicants who would have dropped out during a multi-day screening delay stay in the pipeline.
2. **Lower screening costs.** Sequential Screening means expensive checks only run on applicants who've already passed faster, cheaper ones. No more paying for background checks on candidates who ghost after day one.
3. **Continuous compliance.** Firstwork monitors credential expirations, license renewals, and re-screening requirements post-hire, so compliance doesn't end at onboarding.
4. **Fewer manual touch points.** Recruiters and compliance teams manage the entire screening-to-activation pipeline from a single interface. No more copying applicant data between systems, chasing down screening reports, or manually updating statuses.
