# Introducing Rebolt

# Why does Rebolt exist?


“Again?” That’s what I thought as I watched my father copy the same data, again and again, from an email into a spreadsheet.
It wasn’t lack of skill. It was a lack of better options.


The sad truth is, most companies still run like this.
Smart people stuck doing repetitive tasks.
Reading emails, filling out documents, checking the same things over and over.
**Work that shouldn’t be work.**


And when a company wants to automate, they face three scenarios:


- They surrender and don't do it, because it is expensive or slow
- Buy bloated, overpriced software that doesn’t really fit
- Hire engineers, if you can afford it


**What if you could have a great engineer, without hiring anyone?**


One that understands your company. That never sleeps. That gets it done.
That’s Rebolt.


We built Rebolt to **eliminate the repetitive** and give people room **to do what matters.**


# Why now?


For the first time, thanks to Large Language Models (LLMs), the technology behind ChatGPT, computers can **read, write, and reason** like humans do.


That changes everything.
