# Pax AI Raises $4.5M Seed Funding to Revolutionize Duty Drawback with AI-Powered Tariff Refunds

## The problem: why importers were left behind


**Penny Chen** , Co-Founder and CEO, witnessed the problem firsthand at **Flexport** . We've spoken to countless importers who tried to file duty drawback refunds for years but were rejected by traditional providers, deemed "too small" because duty drawback has historically been a complex and resource-intensive process.


Pax AI was born from that frustration. By leveraging artificial intelligence and automation, we've made complex drawback work more efficient for importers with serious recovery potential. This same technology allows us to process high-volume claims for major importers, from emerging brands to Fortune 500 companies.
