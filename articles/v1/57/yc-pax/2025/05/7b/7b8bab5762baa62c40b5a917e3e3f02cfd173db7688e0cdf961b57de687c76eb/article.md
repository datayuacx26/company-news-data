# Tariffs vs. Taxes: What's the Difference and Why It Matters for Importers and Exporters

## How Pax helps you manage tariffs and taxes


At Pax, we know navigating tariffs and taxes can be overwhelming for companies that operate across borders. Between ever-changing duty rates, overlapping regulations, and shifting trade policies, even the most experienced teams can struggle to stay compliant and cost-efficient. Pax is designed to remove that friction by transforming how businesses manage tariff exposure, import taxes, and duty recovery.


### Automated tariff classification and landed cost visibility


With Pax, tariff classification becomes a proactive, data-driven process. Our platform uses AI-powered logic and the latest HS code databases to accurately classify your goods and identify the applicable tariff rates based on product details and country of origin. This gives your team a real-time view of landed cost, including all duties, taxes, and fees, before the goods even ship.


Rather than managing classifications manually or relying on outdated spreadsheets, you get automated accuracy and the ability to make faster sourcing or pricing decisions. For companies importing across multiple jurisdictions, this means dramatically fewer surprises at the border and much more control over unit economics.


### Real-time trade policy tracking


Tariff schedules and import tax regimes shift constantly, especially in politically sensitive sectors like electronics, energy, and agriculture. Pax keeps you ahead of these changes by automatically tracking and interpreting updates to trade agreements, retaliatory tariffs, and policy shifts—including debates over reciprocal tariffs or ongoing trade wars.


When something changes that could impact your supply chain, like a new Section 301 duty rate or the expiration of a free trade agreement, Pax flags it immediately. Your team gets plain-language summaries that explain what changed, why it matters, and what options are available to mitigate the impact. That way, you’re never caught off guard when geopolitical events translate to real business costs.


### Seamless duty drawback filing and refund recovery


Pax doesn’t just help you manage tariffs—it helps you recover them. If you import goods into the U.S. and then export them again (or use them in manufacturing exported products), you may be eligible for a refund through the duty drawback program. Pax simplifies this process from start to finish.


By connecting your import, export, and bill of material data, Pax automatically identifies eligible transactions, calculates potential refunds, and generates compliant submissions to Customs. Our workflow replaces months of manual claim preparation with a guided, Turbo Tax-like experience that ensures no opportunity is missed.


Whether you’re filing under unused merchandise, manufacturing, or substitution provisions, Pax helps you unlock refunds that would otherwise go unclaimed. Refunds often total hundreds of thousands of dollars.


### Built for modern trade teams


Pax was designed for importers, exporters, and manufacturers who need clarity in an increasingly complex trade environment. Whether your team sits in supply chain, finance, or compliance, Pax gives everyone a single, shared source of truth. By automating manual work and surfacing cost-saving opportunities, Pax transforms tariff and tax management from a rote administrative task into a strategic lever.


Instead of reacting to global changes, you can plan ahead. Instead of drowning in documents, you can focus on outcomes. And instead of leaving money on the table, you can recover what’s yours.
