# Jake Stolowski Featured in Modern Materials Handling’s Article

### Matthews’ Jake Stolowski highlights the advantages of light-directed batch picking carts


*Modern Materials Handling* provides comprehensive coverage of productivity solutions for materials handling professionals in distribution, warehousing and manufacturing.


Their article “[Warehouse automation: Lights-directed picking carts boost productivity](https://www.mmh.com/article/warehouse_automation_lights_directed_picking_carts_boost_productivity) ” discuses the current warehousing landscape, and the emphasis on using high-tech solutions like AI and robotics to improve productivity. However, simpler and more affordable solutions can also significantly improve operations, especially since many warehouses are smaller and predominantly manual.


For the article, writer Amanda Loudin interviewed Jake Stolowski, Senior Manager of Lighting Pick project management for Matthews Automation. Jake shared his perspective on why batch picking carts are an ideal entry point into automation. He also explains that these systems require very little infrastructure—just Wi-Fi and a host connection—and can be custom designed to fit SKU profiles and productivity goals.


He also highlights the evolution of the technology: modern carts run on lightweight electric tool batteries instead of heavy car batteries, making them easier to use. Also, smart carts are now robust enough to operate in freezer environments, a growing need for 3PL providers serving meal delivery companies. Wi-Fi connectivity and adaptable hardware ensure the carts keep pace with changing warehouse demands.


According to Stolowski, when implemented correctly, light-directed batch picking can increase productivity by about 40% and deliver nearly 100% picking accuracy compared to paper-based systems. The combination of scalability, fast ROI, and ease of adoption makes it one of the most accessible automation tools for facilities of all sizes.
