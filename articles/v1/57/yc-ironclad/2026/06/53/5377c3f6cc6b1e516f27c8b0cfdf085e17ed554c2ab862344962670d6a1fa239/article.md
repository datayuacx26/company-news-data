# Legora and Ironclad Partner to Integrate AI for In-House Legal Teams


*With Legora and Ironclad seamlessly connected, legal teams can easily move between AI for legal analysis, contracts, and workflows across both platforms.*


**NEW YORK & SAN FRANCISCO, June 17, 2026** —[Legora](https://legora.com/) , the agentic operating system for legal work, and[Ironclad](https://ironcladapp.com/) , the leading AI contracting platform, today announced a strategic partnership. This first-of-its-kind, AI-to-AI integration will pair Legora’s AI for legal analysis and research with Ironclad’s AI across the full contracting lifecycle.


“This is the first time two legal AI platforms will be connected this deeply, and in both directions,” said Max Junestrand, CEO and Co-Founder of Legora. “It reflects how we build: complementary to the systems legal teams already trust, and focused on helping teams do their best work.”


Legal teams are under more pressure than ever: higher workloads, faster turnaround expectations, and constant regulatory change, but the tools they rely on still operate in silos. The Legora and Ironclad partnership was built to close that gap. This integration will connect two leading legal AI platforms to better serve mutual customers.


Legora provides legal analysis grounded in files, legislation, case law, and other authoritative sources, and Ironclad offers contract intelligence, playbooks, and workflows to help teams understand, negotiate, and operationalize their contracts. This integration will give in-house legal teams a more connected way to bring legal analysis, context, and execution together, so they can move faster on complex legal work.


Through the integration, mutual customers will be able to:


- Bring Ironclad contract intelligence into Legora
- Bring Legora’s legal intelligence into Ironclad
- Perform deep analysis grounded in the Ironclad repository


“Legal teams don’t need more disconnected tools; they need AI systems that work together,” said Dan Springer, CEO of Ironclad. “By connecting Ironclad and Legora, we’re providing a faster way to understand regulatory changes, analyze litigation and disputes, determine how their contracts are impacted, and act on it inside the systems they already trust.”


Legora and Ironclad will roll out the integration in phases, with capabilities and availability communicated to mutual customers over time.
