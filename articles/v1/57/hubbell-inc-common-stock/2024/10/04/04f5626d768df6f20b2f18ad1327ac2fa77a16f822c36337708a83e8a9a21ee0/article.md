# Crafting Custom Oil and Gas Solutions for Worldwide Markets

Since its founding in 1888, the name Hubbell has been synonymous with innovation and excellence. Founded by the visionary Harvey Hubbell, our company revolutionised the future of power, transforming how it's harnessed and utilised.


Today, we proudly uphold the foundational principles of quality and innovation set by Harvey Hubbell over 135 years ago. We craft our high-quality, reliable products to meet the comprehensive needs of our customers.


This is especially true of our commitment to the oil and gas industry. Our decades of expertise in the requirements of energy infrastructure have positioned us to deliver top-tier products and solutions tailored to our oil and gas customers.


At Hubbell, we understand the rigorous demands of harsh and hazardous environments. That's why we engineer our products to withstand the toughest conditions, ensuring robustness and reliability where it matters most.


## Discover the brands who make up Hubbell Harsh and Heavy Industrial:


### [Hawke International](https://www.hubbell.com/hawke/en)


Manufacturing renowned cable glands in Manchester, England since 1957, Hawke also offer a range of connectors, enclosures, breathers and control stations.


### [GAI-Tronics](https://www.hubbell.com/gai-tronics/en)


Providing critical communication and alert devices that are essential for safety in industrial environments.


### [Chalmit](https://www.hubbell.com/chalmit/en)


Over a century of experience goes into each of Chalmit’s globally certified lighting products, each built to supply robust and reliable LED illumination.


### [Killark](https://www.hubbell.com/killark/en)


Since 1913, Killark has supplied a range of industrial and explosion proof products to harsh and hazardous locations globally. Products include lighting, cable glands, controls and wiring devices.


## Global Expertise for End-to-End Project Support


We are proud to have industry-leading experts based within each of our brands. They understand the intricacies and challenges of the market and work closely with customers to find the perfect solution tailored to their needs. From the inception of a project, through planning and design, to the supply of a comprehensive electrical package for any scale of infrastructure and application, our experience in the oil and gas industry is unparalleled.


Hubbell has offices globally. Our customer care teams based around the world are dedicated to supporting our clients through commissioning and installation, to when the products are in use.

“We provide a unique approach because we have experts available to support our customers at every stage of the process. We can advise on products, design a solution, then support throughout the life of the asset. All with a focus on minimising the total cost of ownership.”
