# Revolutionizing Industrial Lighting: The Eclipse X High Bay LED Light

Effective lighting is critical to the smooth running of the workplace. When this doesn’t exist, the safety of employees can be compromised.


The correct configuration of[industrial LED lighting](https://www.hubbell.com/chalmit/en/products/lighting-controls/industrial-lighting/cl/535419) can improve safety and productivity within the workplace. Benefits include reduced eyestrain and general fatigue felt by operators. Good lighting is critical to safety and is a contributing factor to the welfare and comfort of your workforce.


## Designed for easy installation


The[Eclipse X is a high bay LED light](https://www.hubbell.com/chalmit/en/products/eclipse-x-industrial-ecxi/p/15824042) designed to minimise installation and maintenance time whilst increasing photometric and electrical efficiency.


The innovative, detachable hinge system allows installation and wiring to the mounting and terminal arrangement without the need to support the body, control gear and lamp assembly. This enables the installation process to be completed easily by a single user. The overall weight of the fixture has been reduced, making it easier to handle when being installed.


The Eclipse X is available in a range of mounting options: Ceiling. Stanchion. Wall. Pendant. High Bay. Medium Bay. Low Bay


## Focusing on sustainability


Using a next generation energy efficient control gear and LED array, the Eclipse X has been designed with sustainability in mind. With a range of lumen outputs, mounting options and a copper-free (epoxy painted) aluminum housing, the Eclipse X will operate efficiently and effectively a range of environments.


The long lifespan of the product, and the retrofit options available, reduce the environmental impact of fully replacing fittings. The slimmer line enclosure minimises impact and the reflectors are designed to limit uplight option for complete cut off. These features help to lower light pollution and reduce environmental impact.


## Retrofit LED lighting made simple


The Eclipse X can be easily retro-fit into existing Eclipse II applications with minimal installation time. Reduced weight and ease of access mean work can be easily completed by a single installer.


Mark Poutney, Brand Manager at Chalmit outlines the benefit of choosing the updated fixture:


“The Eclipse X is a great choice where a high-quality, low maintenance and adaptable LED luminaire is required in a[Zone 2](https://www.hubbell.com/chalmit/en/products/eclipse-x-zone-2-ecxc/p/15824620) or[industrial environment](https://www.hubbell.com/chalmit/en/products/eclipse-x-industrial-ecxi/p/15824042) . The several design updates we made make it is easy to install or retrofit where previously an Eclipse II was in place.”
