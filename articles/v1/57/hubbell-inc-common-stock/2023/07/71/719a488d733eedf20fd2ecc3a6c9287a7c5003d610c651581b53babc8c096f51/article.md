# Selecting the Correct Universal Luminaire For Your Projects Just Got Easier

Choosing the correct luminaire for your project just got easier with the Chalmit Neo-X bulkhead. Do you need a large output, but space is a premium? We have the perfect alternative to traditionally sized fluorescent fittings.


## Introducing the NEO-X Hazardous Area Bulkhead Luminaire


Our[range of lighting](https://www.hubbell.com/chalmit/en/Products/Lighting-Controls/Harsh-Hazardous-Lighting/cl/535418) is extensive, but we aim to innovate while making things as simple as possible for our customers. You now have the opportunity to illuminate larger areas with a smaller fitting.


The[Neo-X bulkhead luminaire](https://www.hubbell.com/chalmit/en/neo-x-led-bulkhead-luminaire) boasts an impressive output for a compact fitting and is suitable for global use in any harsh or hazardous environment. It really is our most


versatile ever fitting, whatever the application and environment.


### Benefits & Certifications


A certified temperature range from -55°C up to +55°C, suitable for


Zone 1 and 21 areas to EN 60079-10-1 and EN 60079-10-2 with installation to EN 60079-14.


The optional benefit of a hot swap battery with excellent IP allows the maintainable components to be easily accessible, reducing time and cost spent on maintenance.


## A Universal Lighting Solution


The[Neo-X bulkhead luminaire](https://www.hubbell.com/chalmit/en/neo-x-led-bulkhead-luminaire) can offer a universal solution. It performs as a bulkhead, linear, low bay and as exit lighting. It’s suitable and safe for confined spaces like air locks and double door areas.


If only one type of fitting is utilised on projects, inventory decreases and can assist in speeding up installation and maintenance as only knowledge of one product is needed.
