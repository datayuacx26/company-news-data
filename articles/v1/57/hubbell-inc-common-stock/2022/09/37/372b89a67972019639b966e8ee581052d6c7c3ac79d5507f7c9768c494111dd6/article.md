## **The game changers in Wi-Fi performance**


New Wi-Fi wireless standards make networks faster, safer, and more stable. Faster wireless means laptops and mobile devices can better support cloud-based gaming and play higher quality video. The new standards also enable new augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR) applications.


Wi-Fi 6e is just coming to market now. Industry leaders call it a game changer for consumers and our industry. By expanding 802.11ax into the 6 GHz radio spectrum (an extra 1200 MHz), it delivers more bandwidth and lower latency. This supports more devices on a network without interruption.


Version 7, due in the next few years, extends these benefits with extremely high throughput. It also improves network congestion and interference. Expect a four-fold performance boost.


## **Moving all that data**


All that new tech requires new equipment. New 6 GHz clients will allow users to take advantage of the potential, while providing backward compatibility. What does that mean for your supporting equipment, such as antennas?


The technology refresh may mean a new infrastructure, depending on your current equipment. Have you planned for multi-gig capabilities? The version 6e maximum throughput is 9.6 Gbps. That requires Cat 6A cabling, which already handles 10 Gbps data rates.


Once you have new Wi-Fi capabilities in place, count on more IP devices being deployed. Users tend to fill available bandwidth. New devices and components will feature smaller form factors. Our equipment features enhanced cable management that adapts to future needs.
