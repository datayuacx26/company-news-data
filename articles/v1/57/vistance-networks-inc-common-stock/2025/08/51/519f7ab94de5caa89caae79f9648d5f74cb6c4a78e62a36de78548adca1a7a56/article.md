Are you making it easy for your customers to watch what they want? Flipping around a channel guide is no way to deliver immersive, flexible video experiences for your customers—especially when they’re each looking for different streams, like weather, news, market updates, sports scores or other content. This challenge could limit engagement and revenue. CommScope is leading the charge toward delivering a targeted customer content experience with our groundbreaking **Video Stitching Engine** technology and its powerful multiview capabilities.


## Introducing CommScope’s multiview solution powered by Video Stitching Engine technology


For service providers, there’s no such thing as too much content or too seamless a delivery—and there’s always interest in improved monetization. CommScope’s multiview solution enables viewers to watch multiple video streams simultaneously on a single screen—ideal for sports fans tracking multiple games, traders monitoring financial news or families juggling entertainment preferences. And forget scrolling through the guide to see what’s on—Video Stitching Engine technology supports advanced video mosaic for live/linear content discovery.


With Video Stitching Engine technology, CommScope offers both fixed multiview channels and fully personalized unicast experiences, giving service providers and enterprises unmatched flexibility with its dual-path rendering, and support for more than 32 simultaneous video tiles. Whether for sports, news or enterprise applications, the multiview solution is transforming how content is delivered, discovered and monetized. CommScope’s Professional Services can help deploy the solution by collaborating with your teams to provide oversight, support and expertise from start to finish, and beyond.


## A potential boon for revenue


CommScope’s multiview solution is designed to help service providers boost ad revenues. Video Stitching Engine technology supports L-Bar, banner and tile ads, as well as interactive click-throughs, creating new revenue streams for service providers. The multiview solution has the potential to improve ad revenue streams by:


- **Expanding ad inventory** by turning passive screen space into monetizable real estate
- **Personalized ad targeting** through integration with CommScope’s MDC and metadata platforms
