# CommScope’s 2025 Sustainability Report Is Now Available


CommScope proudly released our 2025 Sustainability Report today, detailing our ongoing work, recent successes and ongoing strategies to improve our corporate responsibility during 2024. This report covers every aspect of our efforts to improve energy efficiency of our products and be a responsible steward internally and externally.

We also offer this[infographic](https://webresources.vistancenetworks.com/download/assets/2025+Sustainability+Infographic/5b912a18f74111f09e46e213ce2458ce) , which presents its highlights more succinctly. Both show that CommScope made positive strides in 2024 toward more sustainable business practices and continued to execute strong ethical business practices.


- CommScope reduced our market-based Scope 1+2 greenhouse gas emissions (GHG) by 44.1% compared to our 2019 base year levels and reduced our Scope 3 emissions by 44.2%.
- Our annual Pulse Survey revealed increased job satisfaction across the board in categories including pride in CommScope, intent to stay, general job satisfaction and being heard by management.
- We continue to ramp up production of fiber-optic infrastructure to meet the demand for expanded broadband networks reaching rural and underserved communities, helping close the digital divide and helping every family participate in the digital economy.
- CommScope increased our vigilance against unethical business practices, internally and throughout our supply chain, with 100% employee compliance in our annual Ethics and Compliance training and 202 sustainability assessments and audits of our global suppliers.


Together, through these efforts—and others—CommScope continues to be publicly recognized and receive accolades. A few examples of this recognition include:


- [Our responsible business practices](https://www.newsweek.com/rankings/americas-most-responsible-companies-2024)
- [Our focus on the mental well-being of our employees](https://www.newsweek.com/rankings/americas-greatest-workplaces-mental-wellbeing-2024)
- [Our efforts to promote sustainability in our work](https://www.newsweek.com/rankings/americas-greenest-companies-2025)


There is much to celebrate in the accomplishments of 2024. While work still remains to be done, CommScope is proud to be a part of the solution for our employees, customers and the world we serve.
