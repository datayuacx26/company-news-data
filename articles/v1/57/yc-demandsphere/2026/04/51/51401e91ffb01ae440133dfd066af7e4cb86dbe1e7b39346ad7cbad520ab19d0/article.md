# FOUND Meetup NYC is back for SEO Week 2026

This is our second year running FOUND during SEO Week in New York, and we’re looking forward to it even more than the first.


[Register free on Luma →](https://luma.com/p7r3nbvk)


The format is simple: we take over a great space, the same amazing loft we had last year in the Flatiron District, bring in food and host an open bar. It’s a relaxing and cozy vibe after a busy day of sessions.


We have room for 80 people. As of today, we’re about a third full and filling up quickly.


## Lightning talks and a Mac Mini


One of the things that made last year’s event memorable was the great panel with some amazing speakers.


This year, we’re hosting lightning talks and anyone is free to apply to speak.


The rules are straightforward: you get 3-5 minutes, the audience votes, and the winner takes home a Mac Mini. Second and third place will get prizes as well.


We already have four strong speakers accepted for the talks and are still accepting submissions. If you have something worth sharing - a tactic, a finding, a tool, a take - this is a great format for it.


[Submit a lightning talk →](https://forms.gle/sd7XQvDBQDvQ2n88A)

- **When:** April 28-29, 2026, 7:00 PM - 11:00 PM ET
- **Where:** 22 W 23rd St, New York, NY 10010 (Flatiron District)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Capacity:** 80 people
- **Headcount deadline:** April 18, 2026 (we need a firm count for catering)
