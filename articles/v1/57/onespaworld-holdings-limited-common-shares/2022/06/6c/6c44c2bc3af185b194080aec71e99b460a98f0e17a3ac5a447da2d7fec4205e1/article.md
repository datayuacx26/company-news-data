# Ocean-Inspired Spa Therapies For Your Home


27 June 2022 | Giovanna Maselli


The gentle swaying of the ocean can provide a relaxing view and a much-needed moment of peace and contemplation for anyone. Pair this with the relaxing ambiance and ocean views from the spa and you are ready to reset, restore and revive your mind and body.


In the spirit of this earthly treasure, we have simplified some of our ocean-inspired therapies for you to extend your spa experience and bring a bit of wellness and the nourishing benefits of the sea home with you.


## **EXFOLIATING SEA SALT SCRUB**


Combine the riches of the ocean with the power of aromatherapy, to de-stress and stimulate your body’s renewal process with a soothing scrub. Using your nourishing body oil of choice, mix in a 1/2 cup of the body oil, 1 cup of sea salt and 3 to 4 drops of your preferred essential oil into a bowl. Once mixed, apply it during your daily shower, gently rubbing it in circular motions on your body. Once complete, rinse the mixture off thoroughly with warm water.


## **SEA-INSPIRED SELF MASSAGE**


Set aside 15 to 20 minutes to soothe your body with a moment of self-massage at home. Opt for a body oil that helps de-stress or detoxify your body. An ocean-inspired product, loved by our spa guests is[ELEMIS Cellutox Active Body Oil](https://www.timetospa.com/elemis-cellutox-active-body-oil.html) . A powerful synergy of Sea Buckthorn, Sea Fennel, Lemon and Juniper essential oils boosts your body’s detoxification process and prevents the buildup of toxins. As ocean sounds play in the background, lather the oil onto your body, massaging the areas that carry the most tension – your shoulders, lower back and legs.
