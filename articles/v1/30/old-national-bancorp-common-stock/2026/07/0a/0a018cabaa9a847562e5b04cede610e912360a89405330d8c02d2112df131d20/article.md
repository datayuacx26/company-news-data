Looking for practical financial tips, timely economic perspective or a deeper look at how Old National is helping expand access to financial opportunity? This quarter’s Real-Life Finance® podcast episodes offer something for everyone, with approachable conversations designed to make finance feel more relatable and easier to understand.


Hosted by Old National Financial Empowerment Director Ben Joergens, Real-Life Finance® brings financial literacy to life through real stories, expert insights and everyday solutions listeners can use to build confidence in their financial decisions.


### Start Small with Your Money


Christen Osowski, Community Banking Market Manager in Chicago, breaks down the money basics that can help reduce stress and build confidence. From budgeting and everyday spending habits to credit, saving and getting started with investing, this episode offers practical guidance for anyone looking to start small and build better financial habits over time.[Click here to listen.](https://rlf.buzzsprout.com/2393337/episodes/19027145-real-life-finance-podcast-17-start-small-with-your-money?t=0)


### Making 529 College Saving Plans Less Intimidating


Kyle Aiton, Small Business Banking Relationship Manager at Old National Bank, joins Ben to talk about saving for education through 529 plans. In recognition of 529 Day, Kyle shares a real-life perspective on starting small, automating savings and fitting financial planning into busy family life.[Click here to listen.](https://rlf.buzzsprout.com/2393337/episodes/19140968-real-life-finance-podcast-18-making-529-plans-less-intimidating?t=0)


### Economic Pulse


Matt Finn, Chief Economist Managing Director at Old National Bank, joins Ben for an economic check-in focused on what everyday consumers should pay attention to and what they can tune out. The conversation covers inflation, interest rates, tariffs, oil prices, housing affordability, labor trends and AI, with practical takeaways to help listeners make sense of the headlines.[Click here to listen.](https://rlf.buzzsprout.com/2393337/episodes/19287846-real-life-finance-podcast-19-economic-pulse?t=0)


### Expanding access: The story of Generations Community Bank


Rafael Sanchez, Chief Impact Officer at Old National Bank, and Al London, President and CEO of Generations Community Bank, discuss the creation of Indiana’s first Minority Depository Institution and its mission to expand access to financial opportunity. They explore why MDIs matter, how Generations Community Bank is working to serve underserved communities and the role relationship-based banking plays in building trust, financial capability and long-term wealth.[Click here to listen](https://rlf.buzzsprout.com/2393337/episodes/19401818-real-life-finance-podcast-20-expanding-access-the-story-of-generations-community-bank?t=0) **.**
