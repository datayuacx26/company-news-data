# Scott Morgan joins Okta as Chief Legal Officer

Okta has named veteran legal leader Scott Morgan as its new Chief Legal Officer to lead its global legal, regulatory, governance, and government affairs functions.


"We’re excited to welcome Scott to our leadership team. Scott is a proven builder and a high-integrity, deeply experienced, team- and growth-focused operator who is well aligned to our mission and culture,” said Okta COO Eric Kelleher. “Most importantly, Scott is fired up about Okta’s market opportunity and vision to help every organization become a secure agentic enterprise.”


Morgan’s recent experience includes more than 12 years at Splunk, where he played a pivotal role in guiding the company’s strategic growth from ~$100M in annual revenue to $5B and led its successful integration into Cisco. Earlier in his career, Morgan served as legal counsel at Autodesk and Tellabs, and he worked as an associate at law firms Morrison & Foerster LLP and Thoits, Love, Hershberger & McClean LLP. He is a recognized leader in the legal community and frequently advises senior executives and boards on artificial intelligence, governance, and leadership.


“The future is incredibly compelling for Okta,” Morgan said. “Moving swiftly into agentic identity opens up an entirely new and massive opportunity. I’m thrilled to join this world-class team defining the future of enterprise security, and to help build a lasting, secure future, both for Okta and our customers."
