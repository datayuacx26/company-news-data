# Unlocking enterprise security and agility via Okta Integration Network SaaS integrations

**TL;DR:** Modern enterprise security requires deep integration between identity providers and SaaS applications. Buyers today prioritize interoperable providers that go beyond basic access control integrations to unlock business agility and threat defense. The[Okta Integration Network (OIN)](https://www.okta.com/integrations/) provides a vehicle for SaaS services to support two critical identity security capabilities: **Express Configuration** and **Universal Logout** .


## Enterprise-ready identity integrations: Scaling market adoption for SaaS providers


[Enterprise-ready applications are now required to deliver more](https://auth0.com/blog/enterprise-ready-identity-2-0-how-saas-builders-stand-out-with-auth0/) than basic single sign-on (SSO) and multi-factor authentication (MFA) support. Buyers are looking for providers with standards-based identity integrations like these SaaS products, which unlock business agility and protect enterprise resources from identity-related attacks.


### Two capabilities critical for high-value identity integration


Two capabilities play a critical part in high-value identity integration. These capabilities enable both automated SaaS onboarding and the ability to respond to identity threats in real time:


- [Express Configuration](https://auth0.com/docs/authenticate/identity-providers/enterprise-identity-providers/okta/express-configuration?_gl=1*hixgl1*_gcl_aw*R0NMLjE3NzU2OTEwMDIuQ2p3S0NBanctZGZPQmhBakVpd0FxMFJ3STJWU3h1dnk0emJPb2xIcUt6Q3JsQWp4RUlTc254ZDk2M2Z5ZWFRSDhjU0htMTdRSFVKdXdob0NDcW9RQXZEX0J3RQ..*_gcl_au*MTQ1NzQyOTYxOC4xNzc1NTc0NzYxLjE2NzE1MDI1MDAuMTc3ODI2ODIyMi4xNzc4MjY4MjMy*_ga*MTk2NzM2MjE4Mi4xNzIyNTQ0Mjc2*_ga_QKMSDV5369*czE3Nzg1MjE1NzMkbzY5NSRnMSR0MTc3ODUyMjI1MyRqNjAkbDAkaDA.) : Automates the setup of OpenID Connect (OIDC), System for Cross-domain Identity Management (SCIM), and Universal Logout (ULO), collapsing several steps into an automated flow that configures identity integrations for SaaS in minutes.
- [Universal Logout](https://auth0.com/docs/authenticate/login/logout/universal-logout?_gl=1*hixgl1*_gcl_aw*R0NMLjE3NzU2OTEwMDIuQ2p3S0NBanctZGZPQmhBakVpd0FxMFJ3STJWU3h1dnk0emJPb2xIcUt6Q3JsQWp4RUlTc254ZDk2M2Z5ZWFRSDhjU0htMTdRSFVKdXdob0NDcW9RQXZEX0J3RQ..*_gcl_au*MTQ1NzQyOTYxOC4xNzc1NTc0NzYxLjE2NzE1MDI1MDAuMTc3ODI2ODIyMi4xNzc4MjY4MjMy*_ga*MTk2NzM2MjE4Mi4xNzIyNTQ0Mjc2*_ga_QKMSDV5369*czE3Nzg1MjE1NzMkbzY5NSRnMSR0MTc3ODUyMjI1MyRqNjAkbDAkaDA.) : Lets Okta customers revoke active user sessions across connected apps in real-time, so a compromised credential or hijacked session can be shut down the moment it’s detected.
