# Webinar: Inside Nourish Labs - How Dietitians use Data to Personalize Care

Historically, nutrition care has lacked access to the clinical data needed to fully personalize treatment, particularly for patients managing complex conditions. Without timely, relevant data, it can be difficult for providers to deliver truly tailored nutrition interventions.


To address this gap, and at the direct request of our dietitians, earlier this month we launched Nourish Labs, bringing clinically relevant lab testing directly into the Nourish platform to support more precise, data-informed nutrition care.


Earlier this week, we hosted a webinar and live Q&A with three Nourish registered dietitians to discuss how having access to integrated lab data has shaped their experience delivering patient care. With nearly 50,000 patients taking advantage of Nourish Labs in the month since we launched this feature, it’s clear that this is data patients want access to, if the traditional logistical and financial barriers are removed.
