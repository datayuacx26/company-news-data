# Nourish Publishes First Annual Impact Report (2025)

Nourish is excited to share its[2025 Impact Report](https://d3f4micfw8j9t2.cloudfront.net/blog/2025_Nourish_Impact_Report.pdf) . This report offers a snapshot of what was built and accomplished across the organization over the past year, highlighting how Nourish continued to advance its mission of tackling America’s healthcare crisis through personalized nutrition care.


Some standout milestones from 2025 include:


- Expanding to a clinical network of 6,000+ registered dietitians nationwide, covering more than 50 specialties
- Surpassing 500,000 patients served nationwide
- Strengthening care delivery through an AI-powered platform, designed to reduce friction for both patients and providers
- Introducing[lab services](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/providers/nourish-offer-free-lab-testing-members-inform-nutrition-care) directly into the Nourish platform to make care more actionable and accessible
- Deepening partnerships with health plans, employers, and provider organizations, across the healthcare ecosystem


As Nourish looks ahead to 2026, the team is energized by what’s possible. The momentum built in 2025 sets the foundation for another year focused on scaling and deepening impact, while continuing to shape the future of dietitian-led metabolic care. In the year ahead, Nourish will focus on making care simpler, more personalized, and more connected—delivering meaningful, lasting change for patients, all while staying anchored in its mission: to improve people’s health by making it easy to eat well.
