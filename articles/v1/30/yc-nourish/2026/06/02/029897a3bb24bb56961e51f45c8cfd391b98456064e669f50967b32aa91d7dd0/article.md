# Nourish Shows Patients Completing 6+ Dietitian Appointments Achieve 2.3x Greater Weight Loss

For high-acuity patients managing obesity, diabetes, and other complex metabolic conditions, sustained engagement with a clinician is what drives meaningful, lasting improvement in outcomes.


At Nourish, our care model is built around this principle. As a dietitian-led metabolic health clinic, we design care pathways that match intensity to clinical need: higher-acuity patients attend more dietitian sessions on average as a result of their conditions requiring active, ongoing management.


Today, we're publishing data that demonstrates the impact of this approach:


- 2.3x greater weight loss for high-acuity patients completing 6+ appointments compared to those completing 1–2
- 50% greater A1c reduction for high-acuity patients completing 4+ appointments compared to those completing 1–3
- 1.7x more sessions for high-acuity vs. low-acuity patients at 24 months


This data demonstrates the Nourish care model working as designed: matching care intensity to clinical need, driving sustained engagement, and delivering meaningful clinical results.
