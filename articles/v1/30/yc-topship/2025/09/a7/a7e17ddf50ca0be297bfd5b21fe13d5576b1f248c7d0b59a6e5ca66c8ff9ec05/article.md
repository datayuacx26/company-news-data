## **Why Tracking Matters in International Shipping**


Transparency is the backbone of global logistics. When you track a parcel from China to Nigeria, you get:


- **Peace of mind** – No more guessing where your package is.


- **Control** – Anticipate delays, update your customers, or adjust your inventory plans.


- **Trust** – Businesses that provide reliable tracking enjoy higher repeat customer rates.


As


**McKinsey & Company** notes, modern customers “expect visibility into their shipments as a baseline service, not a luxury.” This is especially true in high-volume routes like China-to-Nigeria trade.


## **How International Package Tracking Works**


Every shipment is assigned a


**unique tracking number** when it’s booked. This number allows you to monitor its journey at key checkpoints:


- **Origin scan** – Package accepted by the carrier in China.


- **Export customs clearance** – Checked by Chinese authorities before leaving.


- **Transit updates** – Flight or vessel departures/arrivals.


- **Import customs clearance** – Inspected by Nigerian Customs Service.


- **Final mile delivery** – Out for delivery to your address or pickup station.


Carriers and logistics platforms use


**barcodes, RFID tags, and GPS updates** to provide these statuses in real time.


## **Steps to Track Shipping from China to Nigeria**


Here’s how you can easily keep tabs on your shipment:


- **Get your tracking number** – Usually provided by your supplier, freight forwarder, or shipping company.


- **Enter the number on a carrier’s website** – Examples: DHL, FedEx, UPS, or EMS.


- **Use a third-party tracking platform** – Sites like 17track.net or Parcelsapp.com allow multi-carrier tracking.


- **Track directly via Topship** – When shipping with


[Topship](https://www.topship.africa/) , you’ll have an integrated dashboard showing your parcel’s exact location and expected delivery time.


- **Enable notifications** – SMS or email alerts help you stay updated automatically.


## **Common Tracking Issues (and How to Solve Them)**


Even with tracking, you may encounter challenges like:


- **Status not updating** – Sometimes carriers batch-update their systems. Wait 24–48 hours before panicking.


- **“In transit” for too long** – Sea freight can take weeks, unlike air freight. Consider your mode of transport.


- **Customs delays** – Missing documentation can hold up clearance. Work with experienced forwarders like Topship to avoid errors.


- **Invalid tracking number** – Double-check with your supplier; sometimes numbers are generated before packages are scanned.


Pro tip: Always keep your invoice and shipping documents handy, they’re often required to resolve tracking issues.
