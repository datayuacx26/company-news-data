**Topship Plugin on WooCommerce - From Clicks to Deliveries**


Growing your brand on WooCommerce or WordPress? Topship's plugin


can help you automate the shipping and delivery experience for your business and customers.


Here's a detailed guide to help you access the full functionality of the WordPress plugin on your online store.


**Step 1:**


Download Topship's WooCommcerce / WordPress plugin. **[Click here to download](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1YljJ6O0PdkZIn_6P3iFUleT1fQ2Byu4u?usp=drive_link)** .


**Step 2:**


**Add** , **Upload** , **Install** and **Activate** the plugin


**Step 3:**


Now your plugin is activated.


Click on Topship on the left menu bar, and **fill the registration form.**


**Critical Notes:*


*i. The information you input on this form must match the information on your existing Topship account (First Name, Last Name, Phone Number, Email, and Password). Dont have one? S[ign up to Topship](https://ship.topship.africa/signup)*


*ii. The address information you input on this form will be set as your brand's default pick-up address.*


*iii. If registration fails, please re-visit (i) or contact support*


**Step 4:**


Your plugin is activated!


Now, simply run a test order on your website to confirm it works.


**Understanding your plugin's settings**


**Dashboard:** A record of all delivery orders from your website. Track, manage, and cancel shipments easily.


**Pending Request:** A record of failed shipment orders. Quickly review, resolve and retry.
