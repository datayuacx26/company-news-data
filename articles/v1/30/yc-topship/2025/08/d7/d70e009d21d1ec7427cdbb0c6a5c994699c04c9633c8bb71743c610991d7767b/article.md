## Air Freight vs. Sea Freight: What’s the Difference?


Before we talk numbers, you need to understand the two main shipping methods:


**Method** **Estimated Delivery Time** **Best For**


**Air Freight** 5–10 working days


Urgent, lightweight, or high-value items


**Sea Freight** 30–45 days (on average)


Bulk shipments, lower-cost items, or non-urgent cargo


Air freight is unquestionably faster, but it can be 2x–4x more expensive. Sea freight, on the other hand, is cost-effective for large volumes, but requires patience, and better planning.


## Average Shipping Timeframes (Air and Sea)


Here’s a more detailed look at typical shipping times:


### **✈️ Air Freight**


- **China (Guangzhou or Yiwu) to Lagos or Abuja:** 5–10 business days.


- **China to Port Harcourt or Kano:** May take 1–2 extra days, depending on final delivery arrangements.


These estimates assume the use of a reliable logistics platform like Topship, and no additional customs hold-ups.


### **🚢 Sea Freight**


- **China to Lagos (Apapa or Tincan Port):** 35–45 days


- Add another


**3–7 days** for clearing and local delivery


Depending on port congestion, weather conditions, or customs clearance delays, sea shipping can stretch longer.


## Key Factors That Affect Delivery Time


Understanding these variables can help you avoid unpleasant surprises:


- **Port of Origin in China:** Shipping from inland cities like Chengdu or Xi’an takes longer than from coastal hubs like Guangzhou or Shenzhen.


- **Shipping Method:** Air cargo is faster but pricier. Sea cargo is cheaper but takes longer.


- **Carrier Type:** Consolidated cargo (where your shipment is grouped with others) often takes longer to process.


- **Customs Clearance:** Delays in Nigerian or Chinese customs can add 3–10 days.


- **Seasonality:** Shipping times are slower during Chinese holidays (e.g., Chinese New Year) and end-of-year peak periods.

## Tips to Avoid Delays


Even the most efficient freight companies can be slowed down by things beyond their control, but


**you can reduce risk** by doing the following:


- **Double-check all required documentation** (e.g., commercial invoice, product description, HS code).


- **Avoid shipping during Chinese holidays** like Golden Week and Chinese New Year.


- **Use a trusted shipping partner** with experience handling customs clearance in Nigeria.


- **Track your shipment** and stay in communication with your supplier and shipping partner.
