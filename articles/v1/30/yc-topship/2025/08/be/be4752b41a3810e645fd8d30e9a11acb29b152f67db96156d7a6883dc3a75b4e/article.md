## **Groupage Shipping (Consolidated Freight)**


Also known as Less-than-Container Load (LCL), this method allows multiple shipments to share a single container—splitting costs and maximizing efficiency. According to


[UNCTAD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_Trade_and_Development) , maritime transport accounts for over 80 percent of global trade, making sea-based methods like groupage highly economical for cost-sensitive shippers


This is one of the


**cheapest ways to ship to Nigeria** , especially for small businesses importing from China, the UK, or the US.


**When to use it:**


- You have small to medium volumes (1–10 cartons)


- You’re flexible with delivery time (7–30 days)

## **Standard Sea Freight**


Sea freight remains the most affordable option for bulk shipments, especially for non-urgent deliveries. According to the


[UNCTAD Review of Maritime Transport 2023](https://unctad.org/publication/review-maritime-transport-2023) , global shipping volumes have rebounded post-COVID, and sea freight continues to dominate cross-border logistics for its cost-effectiveness.


Sea shipping cost is typically


**$1–$4 per kg** , depending on the destination port, weight, and size. In contrast, air freight can cost


**$5–$12 per kg** .


**When to use it:**


- You’re shipping pallets or containers


- You can wait 4–8 weeks for delivery


- You want the cheapest shipping method available


✅

## **Economy Air Freight**


While air freight is significantly faster, it’s also more expensive. In fact, global air cargo rates averaged


**$1.70 per kilogram** in early 2024, according to


[Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1109407/global-air-freight-rates/) , making it less economical for large shipments. This service is often offered by global carriers (like DHL, FedEx, or Aramex) but with slower delivery tiers that are more affordable than express options.


Economy shipping typically takes


**5–10 working days** , depending on origin and customs processing.


**When to use it:**


- You’re sending documents or light packages under 30kg


- You need delivery in less than two weeks


- You’re not in a rush for express

**multiple air freight options** to help you pick the most affordable rate.

## **Postal Services (When It Makes Sense)**


For very light items (under 2kg) or documents, national postal services like


**USPS** ,


**Royal Mail** , or


**China Post** can be cost-effective.


However, be aware of:


- Longer delivery times (2–8 weeks)


- Unreliable tracking


- Higher risk of loss or damage


Postal services are


**only recommended for non-urgent, low-value shipments** .


## **Factors That Affect Shipping Costs**


Knowing what drives shipping prices helps you make better decisions:


- **Origin and destination** : Remote areas attract higher charges


- **Weight and volume** : Heavier and bulkier items cost more


- **Delivery speed** : Faster = pricier


- **Customs duties and taxes** : Varies by product and country


- **Insurance** : Optional but smart for high-value goods
