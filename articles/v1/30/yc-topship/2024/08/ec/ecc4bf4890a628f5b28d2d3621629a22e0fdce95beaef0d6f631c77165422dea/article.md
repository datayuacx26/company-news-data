## Understanding The Shipping Options on Topship


- **[Tobi From Topship](https://blog.topship.africa/author/hello/) /

minutes


If you try to book any type of shipment or delivery on the Topship app, you will be presented with multiple shipping options offering different benefits at varying prices.


Topship partners with tried and tested logistics and shipping stakeholders to design multiple options for Nigerians looking to ship within and out of Nigeria. Whether you’re sending a parcel to London or hauling large containers from China, we’ve got a service for you.


**Here’s a quick overview of the shipping options on the Topship app:**


**Shipping option or Service** **About**


**Key Features**


**Who Is This For**


[Budget (Import & export service)](https://blog.topship.africa/budget-topship/)
Ship into and out of Nigeria without worrying about customs clearance charges.


Use the


*Budget* shipping option and join thousands of businesses and individuals enjoying zero customs clearance charges on imports and exports.


Export:


i. Ships to the UK, USA, Canada and major EU countries.


ii. Timeline of 7-15 working days.


iii. Price includes import duties/customs clearance fees.


Import:


i. Door-to-door imports from the USA (more routes are coming soon!!)


ii. Timeline of 7-15 working days.


iii. Price includes import duties/customs clearance fees.


For the individual/business that ships high-value items and needs to save on import duties


Suitable for packages of all sizes.


Longer delivery timeline.


No import duties/customs clearance fees.


**Express (Import & export service)**


**** Express is the fastest way to ship from Nigeria worldwide.


With expedited delivery and processing times, you can rest assured that your items will reach their destination quickly and safely.


i. Ships to and from any city worldwide


ii. Timeline of 3-7 working days


For the individual/business that needs an urgent delivery


Suitable for packages of all sizes.


Fastest delivery timeline.


**Saver / Saver Priority**


Saver is the cheaper way to ship from Nigeria to any location worldwide.


With a slower delivery timeline than Express, it offers affordability instead of speed.


i. Ships to any city worldwide


ii. Timeline of 7-10 working days


For the individual/business that has ample time to ship and needs a more affordable option


Suitable for packages of all sizes.


[Shop and Ship](https://blog.topship.africa/topship-shop-ship/) Shop & Ship allows you to easily shop from your favourite international online stores and has zero customs clearance charges. It also delivers your items to your doorstep in Nigeria at a low cost.


Shop & Ship – Standard


i.Ships from the UK, US, Canada


ii. Timeline of 7-14 working days


iii. Price includes customs clearance charges


Shop & Ship – Express


i.Ships from the UK, US, Canada, and more


ii. Timeline of 5-7 working days


iii. Price does NOT include customs clearance charges


For online shoppers.


Faster doorstep delivery to your


home in Nigeria.


Shop without paying customs clearance fees (when you choose the Standard option)


**Sea Freight (Ship Via Sea)**


**** Import or export large and heavy items with our Sea Freight shipping option. Allows you to ship large packages of over 100kg to the US


Export


i. Ships to the USA and UK at N600/kg and £4/kg


ii. Timeline of 2-3 months


iii. Minimum weight requirement of 100kg


Import


i.Select routes are available. For a quote, go to your Topship app or contact support at hello@topship.africa


For large-scale importers and exports.


For individuals relocating to and from Nigeria


Suitable for packages that weigh 100kg or more


Slowest delivery timeline.


**China Imports**


Expand your inventory and grow your business with China Imports.


i.Ships from warehouse in China


ii. Timeline of 10-25 working days


iii. Price includes customs clearance charges


Businesses shipping goods from manufacturers and suppliers in China.


Individuals shopping from Chinese online stores.


Each service (or shipping option) is designed to solve specific shipping problems and provide a more flexible and predictable shipping experience for you or your business.
