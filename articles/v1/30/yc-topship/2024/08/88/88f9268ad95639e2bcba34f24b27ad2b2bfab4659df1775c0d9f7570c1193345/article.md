Since our inception, Topship has been a crucial link for businesses and individuals seeking reliable shipping solutions for both exports and imports.


Our mission is to simplify shipping and offer flexibility, which has led us to develop services and shipping options like[Budget, Express, Saver, and China Imports](https://topship.africa/exports) .


We’re taking a significant step forward by introducing our Sea Freight service, furthering our commitment to affordable, efficient, and reliable shipping.


In a market like ours, we recognise the diverse needs of our customers. Our service does not stop at providing options for business owners or individuals shipping small to mid-sized packages but also caters to those with large volumes and heavy packages who need cost-effective solutions for frequent shipments.


Topship has partnered with its first Sea Freight shipping provider service to address these needs, providing an ideal option for those looking to ship larger and heavier packages without the constraints of short delivery timelines.


### **What you need to know about the Sea Freight shipping option**


1.


#### **Lowest rates in the market**


Sea shipping is generally more affordable due to its longer delivery timelines. With Sea Freight, you can ship from Nigeria to cities in the USA for as low as ₦600/kg and


£4 per kg to the UK.


You can also import at very reasonable rates. This makes it the most affordable choice for shipping larger items, allowing you to save on shipping costs without compromising efficiency.


1.


#### **More flexibility**


Topship’s Sea Freight service offers flexibility beyond a single option. A step away from our standard air freight services, you can confidently import or export heavier and larger items, knowing your packages will be delivered to your doorstep at the final destination. Warehouse pick-up options are available.


1.


#### **Bulk shipping-friendly**


Sea Freight provides flexibility in shipping without the pressure of meeting a minimum weight requirement of 100kg. You can combine shipments with friends, family, or business associates and have your items shipped together.


###


### **So, how does Topship’s Sea Freight service work?**


#### **Sea Freight – Import**


Import heavy and large-volume items from countries across Europe, the Americas, Asia, and Africa.


Fill out and submit the Sea Freight import form, and we will share a quote within 24 hours or sooner. To access the import form, go to the Topship App, tap on Book shipments -> Imports -> Import Via Sea.


#### **Sea Freight Export**


Experience a straightforward and cost-effective exportation process with Sea Freight.


**For Sea Freight Exports to the USA;**


💸 Rates start at ₦600/kg


📦 Minimum weight requirement of 100kg


🚫 Excludes customs clearance and doorstep delivery


⌛Delivery timeline 8-12 weeks


**For Sea Freight Exports to the UK;**


💸 Rates start at £4


✅ Cost inclusive of customs clearance


📦 Doorstep delivery also available at an extra cost


⌛Delivery timeline 8-12 weeks


🚢 Only ships once a month
