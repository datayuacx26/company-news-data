# StandardAero Hosts Mobile Newton Room in Gosport to Inspire Future Aviation Talent

### StandardAero Hosts Mobile Newton Room in Gosport to Inspire Future Aviation Talent


StandardAero is proud to host the Mobile Newton Room in our Gosport, UK facility from June 15-26, bringing an innovative and immersive STEM learning experience to 116 local students and helping strengthen the future talent pipeline for the aviation industry.


Hosted by StandardAero in collaboration with Boeing and FIRST Scandinavia, the Mobile Newton Room delivers hands-on, curriculum-aligned education designed to engage students in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) through real-world aviation applications.


The featured learning module, “Up in the Air with Numbers,” is designed for students ages 13-16 and combines aviation-focused mathematics challenges with specialized flight simulators in an interactive learning environment.


Beyond providing an exciting educational opportunity, the initiative reflects a broader commitment to developing the next generation of aerospace professionals and creating stronger connections between education and industry.


### Building Awareness of Aviation Careers Early


One of the aviation industry’s ongoing challenges is ensuring a sustainable pipeline of skilled engineers, technicians, and aviation professionals for the future. Programs like the Mobile Newton Room help address this challenge by introducing students to aerospace concepts and career opportunities at an early stage.


By experiencing STEM subjects through practical scenarios, students gain a better understanding of how mathematics, engineering, and technology are applied in real-world aerospace environments. This type of experiential learning can play a critical role in increasing student confidence, engagement, and interest in technical career pathways.


For many students, initiatives like this also represent their first direct exposure to aviation maintenance, aerospace engineering, or simulator-based operational learning.


### Supporting Apprenticeship and Workforce Development Pathways


As aerospace and aviation maintenance organizations continue investing in workforce development, partnerships between industry and education become increasingly important.


StandardAero and partners Boeing and FIRST Scandanavia recognize the value of creating stronger awareness around apprenticeship opportunities and technical career routes available within aviation. Programs such as the Mobile Newton Room help bridge the gap between classroom learning and future employment possibilities by demonstrating how STEM education can lead directly into rewarding careers in aerospace.


By supporting initiatives that inspire curiosity and technical confidence, StandardAero continues contributing to long-term workforce sustainability within the aviation sector.
