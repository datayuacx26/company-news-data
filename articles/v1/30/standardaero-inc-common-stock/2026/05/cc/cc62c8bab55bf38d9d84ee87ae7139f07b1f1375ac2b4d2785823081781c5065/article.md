# StandardAero Expands Maintenance Insight™ Capabilities

### StandardAero Expands Maintenance Insight™ Capabilities


In the fast-evolving world of aerospace maintenance, predictive technologies are becoming essential for improving readiness, reducing costs, and extending engine life. StandardAero continues to lead that transformation with its Maintenance Insight tools and applications.


### More Than 20 Years of Predictive Maintenance Expertise


StandardAero has spent over two decades developing sophisticated reliability models and predictive maintenance technologies for military aircraft engines. These tools are currently supporting several major defense platforms including:


- C-130 Hercules with the T56 engine
- T-38 Talon with the J85 engine
- C-5 Galaxy with the TF39 engine
- B-52 Stratofortress with the TF33 engine
- F-15 Eagle and F-16 Fighting Falcon fleets using F100 and F110 engines


StandardAero plans to extend/make available these same predictive maintenance methodologies into commercial aerospace applications.


### How Maintenance Insight Tools Improve Fleet Readiness


The Maintenance Insight platform includes several advanced applications designed to support both operational and maintenance planning decisions:


**


#### Dispatch or Deployment Readiness Tool (DRT)


For installed engines, the DRT evaluates whether an engine is likely to require removal before the aircraft’s next scheduled maintenance window. This allows operators to proactively manage risk and minimize unexpected downtime.


**


#### Work Scope Cost Optimizer (WCO)


For engines already in repair, the WCO identifies the repair depth that delivers the best cost per engine flying hours after a shop visit. This helps maintenance planners balance performance, cost efficiency, and operational availability.


#### [Fleet Fly Forward Forecasting](https://standardaero.com/airframe/bombardier/)


The Fleet Fly Forward tools provide long-range forecasting of future engine shop visits, including: anticipated work scopes; material requirements; & future maintenance scheduling needs.These forecasting capabilities allow operators to improve logistics planning and optimize supply chain support.


### A Smarter Approach to Reliability Modeling


What makes StandardAero’s predictive maintenance tools different is the depth and adaptability of the underlying reliability models.


Unlike traditional engine trending systems that focus primarily on sensor-monitored failure models, Maintenance Insight models evaluate the entire system and account for all failure mechanisms. The models also adapt dynamically to the specific health trends of individual engines.


This adaptive approach is helping redefine predictive maintenance across the aerospace industry.


According to StandardAero, fleets using Maintenance Insight tools typically achieve approximately a 10% improvement in cost per engine flying hours compared to fleets without the technology.


### New Features Strengthen Planning Accuracy


StandardAero has recently introduced two major enhancements to its predictive maintenance suite:


**Next action indicator for WCO**


The new “Next Action Indicator” feature within the Work Scope Cost Optimizer provides planners with visibility into the time-on-wing benefits associated with incremental repair actions.


This capability becomes especially valuable when material shortages or operational constraints prevent a fully optimized repair strategy.


**Enhanced reliability calculations for DRT**


The Dispatch or Deployment Readiness Tool is also receiving a patented (patent pending) enhancement that improves reliability calculations related to:


- Engine margin degradation rates
- Cycles per flying hours


This advancement enables more accurate readiness forecasting and maintenance planning for deployed operations.
