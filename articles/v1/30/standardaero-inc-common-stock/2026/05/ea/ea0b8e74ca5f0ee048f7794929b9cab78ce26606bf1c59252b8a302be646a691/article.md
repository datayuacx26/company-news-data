# StandardAero Delivers Standout Challenger 605 Retrofit for Sky Quest

### StandardAero Delivers Standout Challenger 605 Retrofit for Sky Quest


As[Sky Quest](https://flyskyquest.com/) grew from a four-aircraft operator into a 21-aircraft management company, its needs evolved. Scaling a fleet demands partners who can support growth without introducing friction, uncertainty, or inconsistent outcomes.


For a full interior retrofit and high-performance connectivity upgrade on a 2014 Bombardier Challenger 605, Sky Quest turned to a longstanding partner at[StandardAero’s Springfield, IL service center](https://standardaero.com/locations/springfield-il/) . The project delivered a dramatically refreshed cabin, near home-level internet connectivity, and reinforced the role of a maintenance partner equipped to grow alongside an aircraft management company.


### A Project Designed to Support Growth


The Challenger 605 underwent a full interior transformation, with nearly every cabin component updated or replaced. The scope included:


- Complete interior retrofit, including sink, headliner, carpet, and veneer
- Veneer finish updated from high-gloss to matte for a modern aesthetic
- Custom two-tone seating with diamond stitching
- Installation of[Gogo Galileo](https://standardaero.com/avionics/connectivity/gogoconnectivity/) HDX STC for high-performance in-flight WiFi
- Completion of minor maintenance items in parallel, including a 24-month inspection and hourly checks


This delivery marked Sky Quest’s second Challenger 605 project with the StandardAero Springfield team, following a prior interior project and STC work for 5G antenna installations.


> I couldn’t be happier! The owner… doesn’t want me to charter it! He doesn’t want anybody in the airplane because he doesn’t want anybody messing it up!
>
>
> - Brian Parsons, Director of Operations, Sky Quest


### Why Sky Quest Chose StandardAero


According to Sky Quest Director of Operations Brian Parsons, the decision extended beyond price.


**


#### Trust & Communication


From initial walkthrough, to direct interaction with technicians, Brian and Sky Quest valued the transparency and continuity between sales, project management, and the Springfield shop floor.


**


#### Value Over Lowest Cost


While pricing was competitive, Sky Quest’s deciding factor was the quality of workmanship, particularly StandardAero’s veneer methods, which delivered greater long-term value for the aircraft owner.


#### [A Proven Relationship](https://standardaero.com/airframe/bombardier/)


A history of successful projects and demonstrated Challenger expertise provided confidence that the StandardAero Springfield team would execute a complete retrofit with minimal disruption.


### Overcoming Challenges


During the course of this large-scale refurbishment, the project presented a few unexpected hurdles. What stood out to Sky Quest was how StandardAero handled those issues . StandardAero kept Sky Quest informed of the hurdles and by working together, outlined solutions that were agreeable to the customer while keeping the project moving.


### Results That Exceeded Expectations


The finished aircraft delivered on both aesthetics and performance.


The owner was extremely pleased with the new interior, so much so that chartering the aircraft became a harder sell for Sky Quest to accept because the owner enjoyed the aircraft so much more after the upgrades. Connectivity performance exceeded expectations as well, with Gogo Galileo HDX delivering Wi-Fi comparable to home internet speeds. Sky Quest ultimately featured the aircraft in a promotional video highlighting the Gogo Galileo connectivity experience.


For Brian, the defining factor was ownership. “What mattered most was that the shop stood behind the work,” he noted. “Issues were addressed head-on, and the final product clearly adds value for the owner.”
