# StandardAero Signs Exclusive, Multi-Year Contract with Tanzania’s Precision Air For PW127M MRO Support

**SCOTTSDALE, Ariz. – August 13, 2026 –** StandardAero (NYSE: SARO), a leading independent pure-play provider of aerospace engine aftermarket services including engine maintenance, repair and overhaul (MRO) and engine component repair, has been selected by leading private Tanzanian airline operator[Precision Air](https://www.precisionairtz.com/) to provide MRO services for the Pratt & Whitney Canada PW127M turboprops powering its fleet of nine regional turboprop aircraft comprised of ATR 42-500/600 and ATR 72-500 platforms. This new contract extends Precision Air’s long-standing partnership with StandardAero.


Under this exclusive, multi-year contract, StandardAero – the world’s largest independent[Pratt & Whitney Canada](https://www.rtx.com/en/prattwhitney/services/pwc-engine-services) authorized PW100 Designated Overhaul Facility (DOF) – will provide Precision Air with a range of PW127M MRO services from its overhaul locations in Summerside, Canada and Gonesse, France, supported by its service center location in Nairobi, Kenya. StandardAero has been serving the PW100 operator community for more than 40 years, having been appointed as the first independent MRO provider for the engine in 1984.


Commenting on behalf of Precision Air, Christian Shirima, Head Of Maintenance stated: “We are delighted to renew our contract with StandardAero, which has been instrumental in attending to our engine needs, be it HSIs, overhauls, leased engines or unscheduled shop visits. Throughout our collaboration, StandardAero has demonstrated proven technical excellence and flexible support solutions. As Precision Air seeks to expand on its MRO capabilities on the ATR fleet, it will continue to integrate StandardAero’s engine programs into our maintenance planning and quality systems, enabling coordinated scheduling, shared technical data exchange and consistent compliance with regulatory requirements and the OEM’s directives. This renewed contract also complements the work of our Approved Training Organization. By aligning engine maintenance timelines and technical updates with our training curriculum, we ensure that technicians and flight crews receive the latest instruction on engine systems, troubleshooting and safety procedures. This coordinated approach reinforces best practices, improves maintenance proficiency, and enhances overall safety culture.”


“StandardAero is truly delighted to extend our long-running relationship with Precision Air through this new contract,” commented Nasser Skalli Housseini, Regional Sales Director – Europe, Middle East & Africa for StandardAero. “We know how important it is for Precision Air to operate reliably for its customers, and we are committed to providing dependable engine services that support this objective. We look forward to continuing to provide Precision Air with responsive, reliable engine services for many years to come.”


With more than 60 years of turboprop MRO experience, StandardAero is proven and trusted globally to provide reliable engine support tailored to an operator’s specific needs. StandardAero’s service offering on the PW100 family includes extensive component repair support, rental engine solutions, on-site field service, and engine condition trend monitoring (ECTM) services as a CAMP Systems Designated Analysis Center (DAC).


Precision Air is Tanzania’s premier airline, with a 30+ year legacy of dependable service and warm hospitality. From its hub in Dar es Salaam, Precision Air operates an extensive network that seamlessly links major domestic destinations like Kilimanjaro, Zanzibar, Arusha, Mtwara, Kahama and Mwanza with regional routes to Nairobi and Comoros. More than just an airline, Precision Air is a vital bridge for tourism and business, proudly connecting the world to East Africa’s most iconic national parks, cultural treasures and coastal gems.
