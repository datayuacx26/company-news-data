**ABOUT TH INTERNATIONAL LIMITED**
TH International Limited (Nasdaq: THCH) (“Tims China” or the “Company”) is the parent company of the exclusive master franchisees of Tim Hortons coffee shops in mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau.


The Company’s philosophy is rooted in world-class execution and data-driven decision making and centered around true local relevance, continuous innovation, genuine community, and absolute convenience.
