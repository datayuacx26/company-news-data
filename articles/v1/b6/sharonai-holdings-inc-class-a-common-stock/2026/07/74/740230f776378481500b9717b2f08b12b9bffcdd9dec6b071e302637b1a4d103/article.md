# NVIDIA Highlights Sharon AI as an Early Partner in New AI Infrastructure Initiative

We’re proud to share that NVIDIA has featured Sharon AI in its latest announcement introducing a new business model designed to expand access to AI infrastructure at scale.


In the announcement, NVIDIA outlines a new approach to supporting the next generation of AI factories, helping AI cloud providers deliver accelerated computing to startups, enterprises, model builders and research organizations through a revenue-sharing and credit-support model.


As part of the announcement, NVIDIA identified Sharon AI as one of the first companies working with NVIDIA under this new initiative.


The post also highlights Sharon AI’s planned deployment of up to 40,000 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GB300 GPUs, supporting our mission to build sovereign, large-scale AI compute infrastructure capable of serving the growing demands of AI training and inference.


As our CEO and Co-Founder, James Manning, said in the announcement:
*“This strategic collaboration with NVIDIA marks a pivotal moment in Sharon AI’s mission to deliver sovereign, large-scale AI compute infrastructure.”*


The rapid adoption of AI is driving unprecedented demand for scalable, high-performance compute. We’re excited to be working alongside NVIDIA as this next generation of AI infrastructure is deployed, helping provide customers with access to world-class accelerated computing that can support the most demanding AI workloads.


We’re honoured to be recognised by NVIDIA as an early participant in this initiative and look forward to continuing to build the infrastructure that will power the next wave of AI innovation.


We encourage customers, partners and the broader AI community to read NVIDIA’s announcement and follow our journey as we continue expanding Sharon AI’s AI factory capabilities.
