# QuinStreet to Participate at Upcoming Investor Conferences

FOSTER CITY, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 12, 2026-- QuinStreet, Inc. (Nasdaq: QNST), a leader in performance marketplaces and technologies for the financial services and home services industries, today announced that management will participate at the following investor conferences:


**8th Annual Needham Virtual FinTech & Digital Transformation Conference** Date: August 13
Location: Virtual


**9th Annual B. Riley Securities Consumer & TMT Conference** Date: September 10
Location: InterContinental New York Times Square


**Lake Street 10th Annual Best Ideas Growth Conference Big 10** Date: September 10
Location: Metropolitan Club New York City


**19th Annual Barrington Research Virtual Fall Investment Conference** Date: September 22
Location: Virtual


The Company looks forward to discussing the details of its performance momentum, market opportunity, and business model with investors.
