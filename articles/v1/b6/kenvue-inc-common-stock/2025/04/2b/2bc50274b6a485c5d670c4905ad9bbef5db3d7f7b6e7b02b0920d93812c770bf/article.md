When you think of skincare, your face might be the first thing that comes to mind. But a truly effective skincare routine should cover your entire body — from head to toe. ## **Exfoliate to remove dead skin cells**


Your body naturally sheds[dead skin cells about every 30 days](https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/327394)


, but when this process slows, buildup can produce dry, flaky skin — especially on the arms and legs, which[tend to get drier](https://www.health.harvard.edu/blog/have-you-exfoliated-lately-202405203042)


as you age. Exfoliation helps speed up surface cell turnover, unclog pores, and reveal smoother, healthier-looking skin.


[Aveeno® Tone + Texture Daily Renewing Lotion](https://www.aveeno.com/products/tone-texture-renewing-lotion-sensitive-skin)


exfoliates to visibly improve skin tone and texture. This daily exfoliating body lotion contains 4% natural PHA, pro-vitamin B3, and prebiotic oat to moisturize dry skin all day long and smooth rough skin.

is made of water? Your skin barrier — the outermost layer of your skin — plays a crucial role in keeping moisture in and irritants out. Without proper hydration and protection,[you may experience](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/skin-barrier)


dryness, sensitivity, rough patches or breakouts.
