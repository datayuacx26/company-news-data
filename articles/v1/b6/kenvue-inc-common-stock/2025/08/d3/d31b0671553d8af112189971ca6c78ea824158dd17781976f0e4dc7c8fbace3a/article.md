# From sensitive teeth to joint discomfort: How these latest innovations from Kenvue support everyday health

At Kenvue, scientific innovation isn’t just about discovering “what’s next”—it’s about providing the solutions that people need *right now.* That’s why our trusted brands have continued to expand and evolve over the years, offering research-driven solutions for a range of health concerns—from teeth sensitivity to digestive irregularity to joint discomfort.


Here’s a look at three Kenvue products designed to support everyday care from head to toe.


### **The issue: Sensitive teeth can disrupt daily life**


Many people deal with[tooth sensitivity](https://www.mouthhealthy.org/all-topics-a-z/sensitive-teeth)


that can be triggered by hot or cold drinks, acidic foods, sweets and more. The discomfort can interfere with everyday activities like eating a meal to even the simple act of brushing.


### **The innovation:**[LISTERINE® Clinical Solutions Sensitive Teeth Mouthwash](https://www.listerine.com/mouthwash/clinical-solutions-sensitive-teeth)


LISTERINE® now brings its expertise in oral care to a new solution for sensitive teeth. This innovative mouthwash provides 24-hour relief and protection from tooth sensitivity with continued use, while also killing 99.9% of bad breath germs. Formulated with powerful ingredients to block exposed dentin tubules in sensitive areas of the teeth, this rinse helps reduce pain from common sensitivity triggers like cold, heat or sweets—and it freshens breath with a mild mint flavor.

### **The issue: Occasional constipation in kids**


As many parents know, young[children can sometimes experience constipation](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/constipation-in-children/symptoms-causes/syc-20354242)


, which can hinder regular bowel movements and result in hard, dry stools.


### **The innovation:**[Zarbee’s® Children’s Regularity Support Prebiotic Fiber Powder](https://www.zarbees.com/product/childrens-regularity-support-powder)


This new prebiotic fiber powder is designed to support regular bowel function **** in children ages 1 and up. Each serving provides six grams of fiber—the same amount you’d get from a full cup of cooked broccoli—with no added sugar, sweeteners, dyes or flavors.


The tasteless, grit-free powder blends easily into food or drink, making it an easy, daily way to help keep little tummies happy.

As people age,[joint discomfort](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/symptoms/17752-joint-pain)


—commonly felt in hands, hips, feet and knees—can interfere with everything from climbing stairs to enjoying workouts.


### **The innovation:**[Proactive Support* Muscle & Joint Supplements from the Makers of Tylenol®](https://www.tylenol.com/products/supplements/proactive-support-muscle-joint)


Tylenol® has unveiled a drug-free daily supplement designed to support joint comfort and flexibility in adults 18 years and older.* Powered by a blend of turmeric and tamarind, the supplement works to improve joint mobility in just five days* and is available in two targeted formulas: Muscle & Joint and Muscle & Joint + Stress.


The acetaminophen-free caplets were developed with doctors and help promote your body’s healthy inflammatory response to active lifestyles.*
