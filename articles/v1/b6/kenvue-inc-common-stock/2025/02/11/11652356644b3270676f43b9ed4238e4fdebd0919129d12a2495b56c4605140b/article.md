# Suffering from allergies? Try these 3 Kenvue products for relief

At Kenvue, we’ve been committed to using science-backed knowledge to deliver real and impactful solutions to everyday problems for more than 135 years. Our scientists continuously work to evolve our brands, offering an even greater level of care.


We’ve also been pioneers in the allergy space, delivering breakthrough products that provide safe and effective relief from frustrating allergy symptoms. Below are three Kenvue products to stock your medicine cabinet.


**The issue:** Anyone with allergies knows how miserable they can make you feel. From a runny nose to watery eyes to an itchy throat, allergies make it hard to focus on work, enjoy time with family and friends or tackle everyday tasks. And when those allergy symptoms strike, swallowing a pill isn’t always convenient — especially if you’re on the go — and the relief may not last as long as you need.


**The product:**[Zyrtec® Dye-Free Chewables](https://www.zyrtec.com/products/zyrtec-chewables)


Get 24-hour relief from allergy symptoms caused by ragweed, tree pollen, dust mites, pet dander and hay fever with these dye-free chewables. Suitable for adults, with an offering available for children two years and older ([Children’s Zyrtec® Chewables](https://www.zyrtec.com/products/zyrtec-chewables-children-2-plus)


), these easy-to-take tablets require no water and start working within the first hour, providing effective relief from more than 200 different allergens.


---


**The issue:** The seasons changing can occasionally bring the dreaded double-hitter… allergies *and* a cold. Even if you’re lounging indoors in recovery mode, when allergy symptoms hit, they hit fast.


**The product:**[Benadryl® Allergy Dye-Free LIQUI-GELS®](https://www.benadryl.com/products/benadryl-allergy-dye-free-liquigels)


Two birds, one stone! Each of these liquid gels contains 25 mg of the antihistamine diphenhydramine HCl to temporarily relieve allergy symptoms caused by common allergens such as pollen, dust mites, mold and pet dander, as well as common cold symptoms. Whether day or night, these antihistamine liquid gels will ease your hay fever or other upper respiratory allergy symptoms.


---


**The issue:** Just like with adults, kids are not exempt to allergies, especially those caused by triggers such as pollen, pet dander or dust mites. Unfortunately, allergies can run in the family — if one parent has allergies, there’s a 30% to 50% chance their children will have allergies, too. And if both parents have allergies, the likelihood jumps to 60% to 80%.


**The product:**[Children’s Benadryl® Dye-Free Liquid](https://www.benadryl.com/products/childrens-benadryl-dye-free-allergy-liquid)


This kid-friendly bubble gum flavored liquid contains 12.5 milligrams of the antihistamine diphenhydramine HCI (5 mL) and is designed to relieve children’s indoor and outdoor allergy symptoms, including runny nose, sneezing, itchy nose, itchy throat and itchy, watery eyes. The syrup, intended for children ages 6 to 11, is both alcohol-and dye-free and formulated to be free of sugar, parabens and high fructose corn syrup.
