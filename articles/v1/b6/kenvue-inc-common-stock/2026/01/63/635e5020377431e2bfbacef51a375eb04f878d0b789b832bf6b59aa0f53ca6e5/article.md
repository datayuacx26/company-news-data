# Kenvue declares quarterly cash dividend

**SUMMIT, N.J. January 28, 2026 –** Kenvue Inc. (NYSE: KVUE) today announced that its Board of Directors declared a quarterly dividend of $0.2075 per share on its common stock. The quarterly dividend is payable on February 25, 2026, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on February 11, 2026.
