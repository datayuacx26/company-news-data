# 3 ways argan oil can improve your health and beauty routine

At Kenvue, our focus is on creating skin and beauty products that are formulated with high-quality ingredients, and one of those ingredients is argan oil of Morocco, which is found in our argan oil shampoos, conditioners, hair oils, body washes and lotions.


But first, what is argan oil? Native to Morocco, argan oil is derived from the kernels found within the argan nut. The sweet, nutty flavor is great to add to recipes — and the nutrition is great for your health. But using beauty products with argan oil can also act as a one-stop shop to help improve the health, look and feel of both your hair and skin.
