# 8 things you might not know about Neutrogena®

In the world of science-backed skin care, Neutrogena® sets the standard. As one of the world’s most popular skin health and beauty brands, their portfolio of cosmetics, face and body care, sun protection (and more!) bring out the best of skin — no matter your tone, type or issue. But do you know everything about the household name? Here’s everything you need to know about Neutrogena®.


### **1. Neutrogena® began as just one product.**


In 1954, beauty salon supplier Emanuel Stolaroff discovered a patented soap formula that easily rinsed off skin and arranged to import the product to department stores and drug stores. The soap’s literal transparency reflected the company’s guiding principle and, 70 years later, it remains on the market.


### **2. They have a sunscreen that looks invisible on every skin tone.**


Most mineral sunscreens have a major problem: They leave behind a white cast on many skin tones. Without wanting to change the level of protection already offered by the Purescreen+™ line, Kenvue scientists were determined to find a solution. The result is Neutrogena® Purescreen+™ Tinted Mineral Sunscreen, which blends into skin to help protect against UV damage, leaving skin glowing and radiant with no white cast.


### **3. We have committed to 100% recyclableᵃ or refillableᵇ packagingᶜ by 2025ᵈ.**


Across Kenvue brands, we are increasing the use of recyclable and renewable plastics, designing for recyclability, and introducing refillable product options across our portfolio. Today, Neutrogena® has plant-based makeup wipes, but by 2025, the brand will have[100% recyclable or refillable plastic packaging](https://www.kenvue.com/our-commitments/enrich-a-healthy-planet)


. Recently, the brand has made strides in sustainability, including[adding Hydro Boost refill pods to reduce plastic waste by 89% versus buying a new jar.](https://www.kenvue.com/our-commitments/enrich-a-healthy-planet)


### **4. Their technology,**[Skin360™](https://skin360.neutrogena.com/)


**, creates personalized skincare recommendations based on a photo of your face.**


Combining decades of expertise with a database of over 10,000 images of real people — of all ages, genders, ethnicities, and skin tones — this first-of-its-kind, revolutionary technology helps consumers navigate products customized to their skincare needs. The virtual skin analysis tool measures dark spots, wrinkles, fine lines and texture to determine your skin’s vitals. That virtual analysis, plus your answers to a few simple questions, help the tool give suggestions for your morning and evening routine.


### **5. The fan-favorite Hydro Boost product line provides advanced hydration with science.**


Hydrated skin is happy skin! That’s why the Neutrogena® team invented Hydro Boost, where all products have one key ingredient — hyaluronic acid. This naturally occurring substance is found in your skin, with one main job to bind to water and hold it within the skin’s surface. From cleanser to mascara to lip gloss, this extensive line has something for everyone.

### **7. The brand’s “Heroes of Skin Health Equity” initiative is aimed at improving skin health equity for those with Black and brown skin.**


The brand publicly recognizes the work of individuals who are likewise striving to make skin care more equitable and have partnered with individuals to effectively close the “skin health equity gap” faced by many in the Black, Indigenous and People of Color (BIPOC) community. This initiative celebrates individuals making a difference within each of their “For People With Skin” pillars: Advocacy, Education, Expertise, and Transparency.
