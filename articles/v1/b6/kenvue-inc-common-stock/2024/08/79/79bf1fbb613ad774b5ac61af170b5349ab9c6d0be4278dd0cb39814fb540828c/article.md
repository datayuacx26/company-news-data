# 4 tips to nurture your scalp for stronger, healthier hair

When picking out hair care products, the focus is often on what will make hair shiny, soft or even what smells the best. But healthy hair starts at the root.


Here’s why a healthy scalp is important and tips to improve your hair care.


### **So why is a healthy scalp so important?**


The average scalp has about 100,000 follicles, and each follicle contains a hair root that’s made of protein that eventually grows a hair. These follicles also produce[an oil that helps keep the scalp moisturized, protected, soft and shiny.](https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/326764#stages-of-hair-growth)


If you don’t take good care of your scalp, you could face everything from[hair breakage and lack of shine to dandruff, psoriasis and even premature hair loss.](https://www.healthline.com/health/beauty-skin-care/healthy-scalp#healthy-scalp-benefits)


In order to create an environment for healthy hair to grow, you have to keep your scalp and follicles clean and clear of excess oil and dead skin, and that starts with using the right kind of products.

When it comes to shampoos and conditioners, avoid those that contain sulfates, which have the potential to remove too much moisture from your hair and[leave your scalp dry and irritated](https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/327013#are-they-dangerous)
