# Peptides & skincare? Here's what they are — and why you should add them to your routine

If you’ve spent any time reading about different skincare products to add to your daily routine, you’ve likely heard about peptides. But with all the different information and ingredients out there, it can be hard to figure out just what peptides are and why they can be beneficial. Let’s break it down.


## **What are peptides?**


Peptides are chains of amino acids that your body produces in every cell that help form the building blocks of essential proteins like collagen and elastin, which provide structure, strength, firmness and smoothness to hair, skin and nails.


## **What are the benefits of using products with peptides?**


As you get older, the natural production of these proteins,[collagen and elastin slows down, and signs of aging like wrinkles and sagging skin can start to appear.](https://www.exuviance.com/learn-about-skin-care/all-about-ingredients/what-are-peptides-for-skin.html?q=peptides)


Although peptides naturally occur in our skin, it hasn’t always been easy to stabilize and deliver them in skin care products like makeup, creams and serums. But thanks to modern science, experts have been able to[create specific peptides and delivery systems that enable easy absorption within the skin surface](https://www.neutrogena.com/the-bar/what-are-peptides-and-how-do-they-benefit-your-skin.html?q=peptide)


, while also remaining intact and delivering targeted benefits that address a variety of skincare concerns.
