# 8 tips to combat hair loss and promote healthy hair growth

The products you use on your hair can also play a role in its health and growth. Things to remember include:


- **Carefully wash and shampoo:** If your hair is already fragile and thinning, it’s vulnerable to additional damage from harsh products and handling. Be gentle when washing, and use moisturizing shampoos and conditioners, like the[Maui Moisture Nourish & Moisture + Coconut Milk Shampoo](https://mauimoisture.com/products/nourish-moisture-coconut-milk-shampoo/)


and[Aveeno® Oat Milk Daily Moisture Conditioner](https://www.aveeno.com/products/oat-milk-blend-conditioner)


that will coat the strands instead of stripping the moisture.9 For an extra layer of protection consider switching to a leave-in conditioner to help reduce breakage and mend hair shafts.7


- **Limit the use of heat and chemicals:** When you use hair dryers, curling irons, and flat irons, the heat might give you the style you want, but it’s also causing the hair to heat up — which can cause damage. If you want to use the blow dryer, switch to the lowest heat setting. As for perms, coloring, and chemical straightening, it’s best to leave it to a professional salon, as they can make sure they’re using products that will minimize any damage.8


- **Incorporate essential oils:** People have been using essential oils for various purposes for centuries, and they’re known for their ability to not only moisturize hair but boost the health of the scalp. It’s important to read the instructions on how to dilute certain oils for use, and you only need a couple of drops in your shampoo and/or conditioner. Popular essential oils for hair health include:9 jojoba, chamomile, sandalwood, rosemary and clary sage.
