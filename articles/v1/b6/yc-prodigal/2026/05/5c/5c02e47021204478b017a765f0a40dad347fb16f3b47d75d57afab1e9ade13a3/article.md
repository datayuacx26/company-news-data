# The dialer strategy behind a 66% collections increase -

66% might sound too good to be true, right?


We thought the same thing at first, like, “These numbers must be off!” But after taking a deeper dive, the data spoke loud and clear:


By leveraging our AI-powered dialer strategy, trained on rich digital and voice interaction data – the client boosted collections performance, earning **66% more fees per hour** than with traditional rule-based methods.


## The problem with traditional dialer strategies


Most dialer strategies today heavily rely on static rules like account age, balance, credit scores, payment data and client details. Sure, these strategies get the job done, but think about it, how often do you actually update these rules or analyze your strategy's effectiveness?


Are you using all that rich data from your digital channels, emails, texts, or payment portals?


Even more, have you ever considered how valuable insights from customer call transcripts, including their tone and sentiment, could enhance your dialing strategy?


Without leveraging these insights, your agents are likely spending way too much time on low-yield accounts, that could've easily been handled through a simple text or email nudge.

One of our clients, managing 8-9 diverse portfolios with over 200 agents, faced precisely these issues. ## Introducing dynamic dialer strategy


We crafted an AI-driven dialer strategy that dynamically updates dialing priorities based on real-time signals.


By integrating CRM data, digital interactions, payment portal activities, call audio, and transcripts, every engagement updated the strategy instantly.


This ensured agents focused on accounts that offered the highest return on effort.
