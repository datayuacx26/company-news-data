# Slash collection costs while delighting borrowers -

Small and medium-sized lenders face mounting pressures from multiple fronts: skyrocketing operational costs, severe staffing shortages, and an increasingly competitive market dominated by agile fintechs.


Meanwhile, borrower expectations have fundamentally shifted. Today's consumers demand instant gratification and minimal wait times. They're frustrated by traditional collection approaches that feel outdated and impersonal.


Whereas, tech-savvy lenders are disrupting the market with digital-first approaches, putting traditional lenders at risk of losing market share.


Here are five battle-tested strategies that protect your profits while keeping borrowers happy:


## **Move collections to digital (Where your borrowers already are)**


Before: Endless phone calls, full voicemail boxes, and frustrated agents


Now: Borrowers get payment links by text, make payments online, and choose how they want to be contacted


Gone are the days of relying solely on call centers. Modern collection platforms automate digital communications and payment processing, meeting borrowers where they are - on their smartphones and computers. This reduces operational overhead while providing the instant, self-service experience customers prefer.


## **Let AI handle the simple things**


Before: Paying agents to answer "What's my balance?" for the thousandth time


Now: AI handles the grunt work 24/7 while your best people tackle what matters


AI-powered virtual agents now handle routine queries and payment processing with remarkable efficiency. This 24/7 availability dramatically reduces wait times while cutting staffing costs. The best part? Today's virtual agents are sophisticated enough to maintain a natural, empathetic conversation flow.

## **Stop the documentation headache**


Before: After every call, agents spend 2+ minutes typing notes.


Now: AI captures call notes automatically, letting agents move straight to their next borrower.


Post-call documentation traditionally consumes 30-40% of agent time. Automated note-taking solutions now capture and summarize interactions automatically, allowing agents to focus on what matters most: helping borrowers.


## **Make quality monitoring actually work**


Before: Reviewing 1-2% of calls means missing problems until it's too late


Now: AI reviews every call, spots risks early, and helps agents improve


Manual call monitoring is expensive and time-consuming. AI-powered QA tools can now analyze 100% of calls, providing agents with personalized feedback while ensuring compliance. This reduces monitoring costs while improving service consistency.


## **Talk to borrowers when they'll actually respond**


Before: Calling everyone during work hours, hoping they'll pick up.


Now: Reaching out through text, email, or calls based on what works for each borrower.


Strategic deployment of digital channels (email, SMS, chat) reduces expensive voice interactions while meeting borrowers' communication preferences. This creates a win-win: lower costs and higher customer satisfaction.


## **The future is here**


The most successful lenders in 2025 will be those who recognize that cost reduction and customer experience can - and should - go hand in hand. By embracing innovative solutions that automate routine tasks while providing better service, lenders can thrive in an increasingly competitive landscape.


The first step? Assess your current collection operations and identify areas where automation could reduce costs while enhancing the borrower experience. The technology exists - it's time to put it to work.
