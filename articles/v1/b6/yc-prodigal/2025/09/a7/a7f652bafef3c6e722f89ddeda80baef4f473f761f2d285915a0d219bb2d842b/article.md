# Which is the best AI for debt collections in 2025? -

Debt collection has undergone a major transformation. No longer driven just by manual outreach and persistence, it is now powered by artificial intelligence at every stage.


AI isn’t a future consideration, it’s already the backbone of modern collections and loan servicing. Rising delinquencies, tighter margins, and stricter compliance requirements mean teams can’t afford to rely on outdated processes.


The real question today isn’t whether you need AI. It’s which AI platform will actually deliver sustainable results.


## **What to look for in an AI for debt collections?**


When evaluating AI solutions for collections, teams often ask:


Will this platform **increase collections rates** ?


Can it **keep us compliant** as we scale?


Does it **integrate smoothly** with our existing CRMs, dialers, and payment systems?


Will it help us **reduce operational costs** while improving customer experience?


Not all AI platforms are built the same. Some focus narrowly on voice bots or email automation. Others deliver full lifecycle intelligence across compliance, agent performance, and customer self-service.

### **2. Skit.ai**


Skit.ai focuses on automating outbound calling with natural-sounding AI. Its Large Collection Model (LCM) allows businesses to deflect or replace agent-led calls.


While useful for voice campaigns, it mainly addresses just one part of the collections process, *i.e.* voice engagement **.** It does not extend into omnichannel strategies, compliance intelligence or customer self-service.


### **3. TrueAccord**


TrueAccord is built around email and SMS nudges, sending reminders and prompts to debtors. Its system optimizes outreach timing and tone, but it is limited to digital messaging.


It does not cover voice engagement, real-time agent assistance or broader compliance and analytics needs.


### **4. Floatbot (LEXI)**


Floatbot’s LEXI agent offers chat automation, voice bots and multilingual dialog flows. This makes it suitable for teams needing simple conversational automation, especially in multiple languages.


However, it functions mainly as a conversational layer, without deep collections intelligence or full lifecycle coverage.


## **5. Balto**


Balto listens to live calls and provides real-time prompts to help agents stay on script and compliant. While effective as a coaching and compliance assist tool, it is not a collections platform.


It supports agents but does not automate debt collections or cover multiple communication channels.
