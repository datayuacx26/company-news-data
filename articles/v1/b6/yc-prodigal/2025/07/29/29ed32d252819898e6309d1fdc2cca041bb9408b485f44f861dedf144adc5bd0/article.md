# More than 70% consumers abandon payment and never come back -

You made the right collections strategy. You invested in outreach, data, compliance, agent training and the latest tech.


The consumer even clicked through to your payment portal. Then… nothing. They dropped off. No payment. No follow-through.


Getting payment on the portal is the weakest link in most organizations.


We analyzed billions of payment interactions and identified eight critical failure points that sabotage even the best collections strategies. More importantly, we share how to fix them.


### **1. Treating every consumer like they're the same person**


**Problem:** Generic experiences don't connect personally when the same messaging goes for someone who's made six on-time payments and someone who's 90 days delinquent. Without personalized messaging, consumers see another faceless corporation demanding money. It results in lower engagement, fewer payments and more drop-offs.


**Fix:** Dynamic personalization adapting to each consumer's history and behavior. Show relevant payment options, use messaging that acknowledges their circumstances. Make them feel seen, not processed.


### **2. Losing money to digital drop-offs every day**


**Problem:** Multiple screens, repeated data entry, unclear instructions create friction when consumers are ready to pay you right now. Most organizations have no follow-up for abandoned payments. These consumers wanted to pay but hit a roadblock, and now they're gone.


**Fix:** Streamline flows to reduce steps. Deploy trigger-based follow-ups via SMS and email while payment intent remains high.


### **3. Your payment options are stuck in 2010**


**Problem:** Consumers carry smartphones with Apple Pay and Google Pay, yet many portals only accept card payments. Plus, inflexible payment plans that force unrealistic commitments. One unexpected expense, and their rigid plan becomes impossible.


**Fix:** Accept every payment method that consumers use and enable flexible payment plans. Allow partial payments to drive more completions.


### **4. Channel switching feels like starting over**


**Problem:** Every time a consumer switches channels to make a payment, they have to re-enter everything. This disjointed experience prevents payments as consumers simply give up when switching channels requires restarting.


**Fix:** True omnichannel intelligence to maintain context everywhere. Whether switching devices or moving between self-serve and live agents, the journey should continue smoothly.


### **5. User experience on your portal is working against you**


**Problem:** Slower loading, confusing navigation and broken mobile design. These are conversion killers. When consumers can't find their next step or account info, they leave. Abandoned payment attempts rarely get second chances.


**Fix:** Modern, mobile-first design which loads instantly with clear visual hierarchy, contextual help and responsiveness that works flawlessly everywhere.

### **6. Your agents are flying blind**


**Problem:** While consumers struggle with self-serve, agents struggle to help. Without real-time visibility into portal activity or payment history, agents waste time gathering basic information. Manual processes result in longer calls, frustrated consumers and missed payments.


**Fix:** Unified data platform that provides complete consumer journey. Real-time portal activity and payment history at agents' fingertips.


### **7. You're making decisions without data**


**Problem:** Why do consumers abandon payments? Which messages drive completions? What payment terms work? Without actionable analytics, you're guessing.


Most portals out there don't provide behavioral insights.


**Fix:** Behavioral analytics that reveal not just what happened, but why. Abandon point analysis, A/B testing, and predictive insights to optimize engagement strategies.


### **8. Consumers don't trust your portal**


**Problem:** Consumers hesitate entering payment info into portals due to insecurity. Unclear security badges or outdated design that triggers abandonment, making consumers fall back to "safer" channels like calling a human agent.


**Fix:** Visible security features, clear compliance messaging, modern design instilling confidence. Bank-level encryption and trust signals that are prominently displayed.


## **From portal to profit center**


Modern consumers want to pay on their terms, through their preferred channels, with options matching their reality.


These failure points are opportunities as each abandoned payment represents recoverable revenue.


The solution isn't another generic portal. It's an intelligent payment experience that adapts to each consumer by maintaining context across channels and reducing friction.
