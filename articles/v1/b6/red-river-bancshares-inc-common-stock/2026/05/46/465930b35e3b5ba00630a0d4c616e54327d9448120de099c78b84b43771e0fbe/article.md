# Red River Bank Announces Relocation of Banking Center and New Facility for Commercial and Private Banking Clients

[PDF Version](https://ir.redriverbank.net/node/9446/pdf)


**SHREVEPORT, La., May 8, 2026** – Red River Bank (the Bank) announces the relocation of its Market Street Banking Center located at 601 Market Street, to 401 Market St., Suite 100, in the American Tower. The Bank also built a new space dedicated to its Commercial and Private Banking teams, that will be located adjacent to the East Kings Banking Center at 1753 E. 70th Street.


For years, the former Market Street Banking Center served as the home for both Retail Banking and the Bank’s Commercial and Private Banking teams. As the Bank continued to grow in the Shreveport market, leadership recognized the need for expanded space, improved accessibility, and a more tailored environment for both customers and employees.


“When we began planning the relocation of our Market Street Banking Center, it became clear that our Commercial and Private Banking teams had outgrown the shared space,” said Blake Chatelain, President and CEO of Red River Bank. “These teams serve some of the region’s most complex financial needs, and they deserve a dedicated environment designed specifically for their clients and their work. Building a new facility at our East Kings location allows us to deliver exactly that.”


The new Commercial and Private Banking building at the East Kings banking center, which is a combined loan and deposit production office (LPO/DPO), was designed to support deeper client engagement, collaborative workspaces, and long‑term growth. The relocation also allows the relocated Market Street Banking Center to operate with improved efficiency and customer flow in its new home.


“Our investment in these new facilities reflects our long‑standing commitment to Shreveport,” added Shreveport Market President Katie Provenza. “We’re proud to strengthen our presence here and to provide spaces that elevate the experience for every customer who walks through our doors.”


Both the relocated branch and the new LPO/DPO are now open and fully operational.


**About Red River Bank**
Red River Bank is a Louisiana state-chartered bank established in Alexandria, Louisiana, in 1999 that provides a fully integrated suite of banking products and services tailored to the needs of its commercial and retail customers. Red River Bank operates from a network of 28 banking centers throughout Louisiana and three combined loan and deposit production offices, one each in New Orleans, Louisiana, Lafayette, Louisiana, and Shreveport, Louisiana. Banking centers are located in the following Louisiana markets: Central, which includes the Alexandria metropolitan statistical area ("MSA"); Northwest, which includes the Shreveport-Bossier City MSA; Capital, which includes the Baton Rouge MSA; Southwest, which includes the Lake Charles MSA; the Northshore, which includes the Slidell-Mandeville-Covington MSA; Acadiana, which includes the Lafayette MSA; and New Orleans, which includes the New Orleans-Metairie MSA. Red River Bank is a subsidiary of Red River Bancshares, Inc., which trades under the trading symbol RRBI on the Nasdaq Global Select Market. Learn more at www.redriverbank.net.
