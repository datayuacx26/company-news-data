# Meter Feeder at the FedEx Forward Service Provider Summit: Unlocking New Opportunities for Fleets

Meter Feeder is thrilled to be attending the FedEx Forward Service Provider Summit in Orlando for the first time. As a company dedicated to streamlining parking ticket management for fleets, we are looking forward to showcasing our innovative solution that reduces costs, eliminates administrative headaches, and provides unparalleled convenience. This exclusive event offers an exciting opportunity to connect with industry leaders and demonstrate how Meter Feeder can save fleets both time and money by simplifying the parking ticket process.


**What We Do**


Our solution is simple but powerful: we can find parking tickets across approximately 2,500 locations, saving fleets up to 40% on ticket fees and another 30% on administrative costs. We don’t just make it easier to manage parking tickets—we streamline the entire process, offering the convenience that busy fleet managers need to focus on what really matters: running their business.


**Why This Summit Matters**


Attending the FedEx Forward Service Provider Summit is a unique opportunity for us to expand our network and introduce our solution to fleets that may not realize just how much they’re losing to inefficient parking ticket management. We’re hopeful that through meaningful conversations with service providers and industry leaders, we can demonstrate the value we bring to the table—cutting costs and saving time for some of the largest fleets in the world.


If you’re at the summit, we’d love to connect and show you how we can streamline your operations and make parking tickets a thing of the past.
