# ScanSource and Intelisys Leaders Named 2026 CRN Channel Chiefs

February 18, 2026


**ScanSource and Intelisys Leaders Named 2026 CRN Channel Chiefs**


*Eddie Acosta, Melissa Andrews, Jolynn Antonacci, Casey Huffling and Mark Jones make prestigious annual list*


**GREENVILLE, SC —**[ScanSource, Inc.](https://www.scansource.com/en) (NASDAQ: SCSC), a leading technology distributor uniquely positioned to address complex, converging technologies, today announced that


[CRN](http://www.crn.com/) **®** , a brand of


[The Channel Company](http://www.thechannelco.com/) , has named five ScanSource and Intelisys leaders to its list of 2026 Channel Chiefs. The annual recognition celebrates IT vendor and distribution executives who are shaping channel strategy and driving innovation and partnership across the industry. The ScanSource 2026 Channel Chiefs include:


- Eddie Acosta, SVP, Territory Sales, Intelisys


- Melissa Andrews, VP, Marketing, ScanSource


- Jolynn Antonacci, VP, Marketing, Intelisys


- Casey Huffling, SVP, Specialty Technologies, ScanSource


- Mark Jones, VP, Sales, ScanSource


“It is an honor to see our leaders recognized for their continued impact across the channel,” said Mike Baur, Chairman and CEO, ScanSource, Inc. “Their strategic focus, leadership and dedication create meaningful impact across our Company and valuable opportunities for growth for our partners.”


“Leaders recognized as CRN Channel Chiefs this year have demonstrated unwavering commitment to their partners and the broader IT channel. Their vision, passion and drive for innovation continue to shape the future of our industry. We are proud to celebrate their achievements and the positive impact they make every day,” said Jennifer Follett, Vice President, U.S. Content, Executive Editor, CRN.


The annual CRN Channel Chiefs list spotlights the most influential leaders across the IT channel, celebrating those who champion collaboration, drive innovation and empower their partners and customers to achieve shared success.


CRN’s 2026 Channel Chief list is featured online at


[www.CRN.com/ChannelChiefs](http://www.crn.com/ChannelChiefs) .
