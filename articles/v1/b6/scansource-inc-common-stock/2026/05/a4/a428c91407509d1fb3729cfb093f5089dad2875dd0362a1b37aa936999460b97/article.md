# ScanSource’s Impact 360 Community Program Receives National Halo Award

May 8, 2026


**ScanSource’s Impact 360 Community Program Receives National Halo Award**


**GREENVILLE, SC —**[ScanSource, Inc.](https://www.scansource.com/en) (NASDAQ: SCSC), a leading technology distributor uniquely positioned to address complex, converging technologies, today announced the Company has been


recognized with a Silver Halo Award for Best Education Initiative from


[Engage for Good](https://engageforgood.com/) , one of the nation’s leading honors for excellence in corporate–nonprofit partnerships.


ScanSource earned the award because its Impact 360 program delivered measurable improvements in learner engagement and was built through deep, strategic collaboration with community partners. The program also introduced innovative approaches to addressing educational inequality, actively involved educators and participants in program design, and demonstrated a clear, long-term commitment to sustainable educational impact.


Impact 360 is a partnership between ScanSource, the ScanSource Charitable Foundation, Public Education Partners, and Greenville County Schools designed to support students through mentorship, engagement and enrichment. The program was launched in 2024 as a pilot at Thomas E. Kerns Elementary School, a Greenville County Title I school, where ScanSource serves as the school’s PTA partner. In this role, ScanSource employees actively support the school community through hands‑on involvement, relationship‑building and programming that responds directly to the needs of students, educators and families. The program has now expanded into other schools across ScanSource’s footprint.


“We are honored to receive this prestigious recognition,” said Wendy Thacker, Vice President of Philanthropy and Community Engagement, ScanSource. “Impact 360 strengthens our culture by inviting employees to do more than give, it empowers them to show up, lead with purpose, and build real relationships. This recognition belongs to our employees, partners, and school communities. Together, we’re investing in children, and that’s impact worth celebrating.”


Past winners of the Halo Award for Best Education Initiative include: Applied Materials & Last Mile Education Fund; Discovery Education & Boeing, Dupont, United Airlines, Chevron and other corporations; Subaru of America & AdoptAClassroom.org; Macy’s & Reading is Fundamental; and Samsung & DonorsChoose. For more information about the 2026 Halo Awards, visit


[here](https://engageforgood.com/resource/engage-for-good-announces-2026-halo-award-winners-honoring-corporate-nonprofit-partnerships-that-prove-purpose-and-business-go-hand-in-hand/) .


This recognition highlights ScanSource’s commitment to investing time, talent and resources in local schools while strengthening employee engagement and long‑term community impact in Greenville. For more information about ScanSource’s Impact 360 program, visit


[here](https://www.scansource.com/about/community) .
