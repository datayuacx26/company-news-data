# ScanSource and Intelisys Leaders Spotlighted on the 2025 Women of the Channel List

May 12, 2025


**ScanSource and Intelisys Leaders Spotlighted on the 2025 Women of the Channel List**


**GREENVILLE, SC —**[ScanSource, Inc.](https://www.scansource.com/en)


(NASDAQ: SCSC), a leading hybrid distributor connecting devices to the cloud, announced today that


[CRN](http://www.crn.com/)


**®** , a brand of


[The Channel Company](http://www.thechannelco.com/)


, has recognized five ScanSource and Intelisys employees as prestigious 2025 Women of the Channel honorees. The ScanSource and Intelisys honorees include:


- Alex Britt, Director, Sales, ScanSource


- Jamie Hunt, Business Development Manager, Intelisys


- Brodie Smith, Sales Director, ScanSource


- Ariana Wills, Business Development Manager, Intelisys


- Elisa Wipplinger, Senior Manager, Cisco Supplier Services, ScanSource


The annual CRN list celebrates women from vendors, distributors, solution providers and other channel-focused organizations who make a positive difference in the IT ecosystem. The CRN 2025 Women of the Channel honorees are innovative and strategic leaders committed to advancing channel excellence and supporting the success of their partners and customers.


“It is an honor for our leaders to be recognized by CRN for their innovation, expertise, and commitment to both the channel and ScanSource,” said Tony Sorrentino, President, Specialty Technologies. “I am proud of the level of leadership and dedication each person honored brings to our company. Each of the leaders exemplifies the qualities of our ScanSource core values and goes above and beyond to meet the needs of our partners, suppliers, and team members.”


“I’m thrilled to see our leaders honored by CRN as 2025 Women of the Channel,” said Ken Mills, President, Intelisys. “Their unwavering support and commitment to our partners, Intelisys, and the channel foster strong relationships and drive mutual growth. I am grateful for their contributions to Intelisys and proud to have them on our team.”


“It’s an honor to recognize the outstanding accomplishments of these women, who are leaders and change-makers in the IT channel,” said Jennifer Follett, VP, U.S. Content and Executive Editor, CRN at The Channel Company. “Each woman spotlighted on this list has shown exceptional dedication to building creative strategies that propel transformation, growth, and success for their organizations and the entire IT channel. We are pleased to spotlight their important contributions and look forward to their future success.”


The 2025 Women of the Channel will be featured in the June issue of CRN Magazine and online at


[www.CRN.com/WOTC](http://www.crn.com/WOTC)


.


**About ScanSource, Inc.**


ScanSource, Inc. (NASDAQ: SCSC) is a leading hybrid distributor connecting devices to the cloud and accelerating growth for channel partners across hardware, software as a service (SaaS), connectivity and cloud. ScanSource enables channel partners to deliver solutions for their end customers to address changing buying and consumption patterns. ScanSource uses multiple sales models to offer hybrid distribution solutions from leading suppliers of specialty technologies, connectivity and cloud. Founded in 1992 and headquartered in Greenville, South Carolina, ScanSource was named one of the 2024 Best Places to Work in South Carolina and on FORTUNE magazine’s 2025 List of World’s Most Admired Companies. ScanSource ranks #776 on the Fortune 1000. For more information, visit
