# ScanSource’s Mary Gentry Honored as One of 50 Women to Watch for Boards

August 19, 2025


**ScanSource’s Mary Gentry Honored as One of 50 Women to Watch for Boards**


*Gentry recognized by prestigious program as a qualified board-ready leader*


**GREENVILLE, SC —**[ScanSource, Inc.](https://www.scansource.com/en) (NASDAQ: SCSC), a leading hybrid distributor connecting devices to the cloud, today announced that Mary Gentry, Senior Vice President, Finance and Treasurer, has been recognized as one of the 2025 50 Women to Watch for Boards, a 50/50 Women on Boards program. The 50 Women to Watch for Boards program spotlights accomplished executives ready to serve on public company boards to drive meaningful impact in corporate governance.


The 50 Women to Watch for Boards program is overseen by an elite selection committee composed of public company board chairs, nominating and governance committee chairs and stock exchange leaders. The committee selects the 50 women who demonstrate the highest level of qualifications and potential to serve on public company boards, ensuring that the program represents top women talent across industries.


“We are incredibly proud to celebrate Mary’s recognition by the 50 Women to Watch for Boards program,” said Mike Baur, Chair and CEO, ScanSource, Inc. “This honor is a testament to the strategic value she brings as a trusted, highly collaborative partner to our leadership team and Board. Mary leads with integrity, purpose and a deep commitment to advancing our organizational goals. Her insight, dedication and ability to build alignment across our stakeholders make her an invaluable leader within our Company.”


Gentry is an accomplished Fortune 1000 finance executive with deep public company leadership experience in technology and banking industries. She brings a wealth of finance, strategy and investor relations experience to board service. Gentry currently serves as Senior Vice President, Finance and Treasurer at ScanSource. She also co-chairs the ScanSource ESG Steering Committee and served as co-executive sponsor for the Company’s Women in the Workforce Employee Network Group.


For more information on Gentry’s recognition and leadership, visit


[https://5050wob.com/honorees/mary-gentry/](https://5050wob.com/honorees/mary-gentry/) .
