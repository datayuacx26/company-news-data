# MCP Now Available to All, Workflow Improvements, Flexible Performance Cycles & more

What we launched in early access last month is now live for everyone: Humaans MCP is available to all Growth and Enterprise customers! Also, Workflows have gained new triggers and filters and Performance Cycles now support weekly cadences.


---


**Humaans MCP: Now Available to All**


When we launched Humaans MCP last month, it was in early access. Now it's available to every Growth and Enterprise customer by default.


With Humaans MCP connected, your AI assistant can query and act on your people data without tab-switching or data exports. Ask “Who’s on leave this week?” or “Show me headcount by department” and get a live answer straight from Humaans.


- **Works with any MCP-compatible client** : connect Claude, ChatGPT, or any other assistant your team already uses, via a single integration;
- **Covers the full platform** : employee data, compensation, time away, performance, documents, workflows, tasks- nearly 400 tools exposed;
- **Respects your permissions model** : the AI assistant only ever sees and acts on what the connected user is authorised to- every request is scoped to their role;
- **Admin controls built in** : decide which permission roles can access MCP, see every connected application at a glance, and revoke access whenever you need to;


Setup guides are available at[humaans.io/mcp](https://humaans.io/mcp) . If you have questions, your Customer Success Manager is happy to help.


---


**Workflows: New Triggers, Filters & Tags**


We've continued expanding Workflows with new options for when automations fire and what data they can reference - giving you more precision in every automation you build.


- **Trigger after N years** : date-based triggers now support years as an interval: for example, three years after an employment start date- not just days, weeks, or months;
- **Gender and leaving reason filters** : these are now available as filter conditions in both enrolment triggers and conditional workflow filters, so you can target automations more precisely;
- **Date of birth email tag** : you can now include an employee's birthday as a dynamic tag in workflow emails, useful for anniversary and milestone automations;


Together, these additions make it easier to build automations that fire for exactly the right people at exactly the right time.


---


**Performance: More Flexible Recurring Cycles**


Recurring performance cycles can now be set to repeat every X weeks (up to 12), giving you cadence options that match how your teams actually work.


- **Weekly-based cadences** : configure a recurring cycle to run every 1-12 weeks, rather than being limited to monthly or quarterly intervals;
- **Works with your existing templates** : set the cadence in your cycle configuration- no changes needed with your existing review templates or scoring setup;


Now you and your team can run performance reviews, on whatever schedule you choose.


---


**Other Improvements**


- **Authorised Applications management** : users can now view and revoke the applications they've connected to Humaans, including MCP clients, directly from their profile dropdown. Owners and admins can manage all Authorised Applications across the organisation in Settings → API Tokens → Authorised Applications;
- **New table columns** : Place of work country and Place of work city are now available in Tables, with remote workers' details picked up automatically.
