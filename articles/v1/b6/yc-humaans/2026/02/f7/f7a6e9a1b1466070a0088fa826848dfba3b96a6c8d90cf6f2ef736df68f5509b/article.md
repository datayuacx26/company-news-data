# Humaans X Deel

Humaans is excited to announce a new integration with ****[Deel](https://www.deel.com/) , the leading global payroll and employer of record (EOR) platform that helps you hire and pay employees & contractors in 130+ countries.


Hiring and managing international employees brings complexity fast. As organisations expand globally, HR teams often find themselves juggling disconnected systems for people data, payroll, and compliance, creating friction across day-to-day operations.


The Humaans + Deel integration simplifies this experience by connecting your core HR system with Deel’s global employment infrastructure. With employee data flowing seamlessly between platforms, teams can reduce manual work, improve data accuracy, and operate with greater confidence as they scale internationally.


### About Humaans


Humaans is a next-generation HRIS supporting globally distributed organisations. Powered by a flexible data model, deep integrations, fast APIs, and enriched by AI, Humaans removes manual work, reduces compliance risk, and enhances your employee experience.


### About Deel


Deel is the all-in-one Global People Platform that enables companies to hire, pay, manage, and support international teams—from onboarding and payroll to mobility and compliance. Trusted by over 35,000 companies worldwide, Deel helps organisations of all sizes scale globally with speed, flexibility, and confidence.
