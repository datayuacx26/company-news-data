# New in Humaans: Zinc Integration

**Humaans and Zinc Partner to Bring Background Check Reports into Your HRIS**


Background checks are a critical part of hiring, but for most People teams, the reports live somewhere separate from the rest of the employee record.


Today we're excited to announce our Humaans × Zinc integration, bringing completed background check reports directly into Humaans automatically.


Zinc is a global background checking platform that automates pre-employment screening across 190+ countries, covering criminal record checks, employment references, right-to-work verification, and more. Built to make the screening process fast, compliant, and candidate-friendly, Zinc is trusted by teams hiring both locally and at scale. **


Click[here](https://zincwork.com/) to learn more about Zinc.


This integration makes it easy to keep employee records complete, eliminate manual document handling, and ensure background check reports are always where they need to be.


By integrating Zinc and Humaans together, you will be able to:


- Automatically import completed background check reports into Humaans, with no manual downloading or uploading required
- View reports directly on the employee's profile, saved as a PDF under the Zinc Background Checks document type
- Keep records accurate and up to date, with reports syncing on an hourly basis
- Hire with confidence across 190+ countries, knowing your documentation is always in order


Learn more about this integration[here](https://intercom.help/humaans/en/articles/14112169-how-to-enable-zinc-integration) *.*
