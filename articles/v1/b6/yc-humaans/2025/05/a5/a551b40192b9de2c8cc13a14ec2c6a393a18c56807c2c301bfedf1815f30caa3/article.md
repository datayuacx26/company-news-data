# From Spreadsheets to Scale: How Humaans Tables Helps Sonio Run Their Global HR

## **Sonio at a glance**


- **Headquarters:** Paris, France
- **Sector:** Health Tech / MedTech (Prenatal and Women Health)
- **Additional offices:** United States (Boston and remote across 6 states)


When[Sonio](https://sonio.ai/en/) 's People team needed to manage multiple global payrolls and maintain compliance as a regulated medical company, they turned to Humaans. The growing ultrasound AI start-up, recently acquired by Samsung, needed to streamline operations while ensuring accurate data for regular audits they face across their offices in France and multiple US states. With Humaans as their HRIS, Sonio has successfully centralized their employee data, automated key HR processes, and maintained compliance.


Clément Moreau, Director of People & Culture at Sonio has continuously looked to optimize their people operations by leveraging Humaans'[powerful Tables feature](https://humaans.io/features/tables) to manage complex reporting needs. By centralizing data, providing easy-to-use reporting, and offering powerful[workflow automations](https://humaans.io/features/workflows) , Humaans has helped Sonio build a scalable, efficient People function as they expand their innovative AI-powered prenatal healthcare solution.


> *"Humaans has become the cornerstone for how we manage our global workforce. It's one source for everything. It's always the first thing I check and it allows us to work seamlessly as a team." — Clément Moreau, Director of People & Culture at Sonio*


### **Key results**


- **Streamlined regulatory compliance** with clear audit trails and documentation
- **Save 4 hours of time weekly** through centralized data management and automated workflows
- **Unified employee data across multiple countries and simplified complex reporting** with Tables


## **The Challenge: Complex Data Management Across Borders**


As Sonio expanded their innovative AI solution for prenatal healthcare across France and the United States, their People team faced mounting challenges. Managing employee data across multiple countries and states created significant complexity:


- **Multiple payroll systems:** Managing three different payroll systems (US, France, remote employees)
- **Regulatory compliance:** As a medical company, Sonio faces regular audits requiring detailed documentation of HR processes
- **Globally distributed workforce:** Team members spread across France, the UK, Spain and 6 different US states
- **Specialized talent needs:** Recruiting at the intersection of healthcare and AI technology required precise data tracking


## **The Solution: Centralized Data with Humaans Tables**


### **Single Source of Truth for Critical People Data**


Humaans Tables transformed how Sonio manages their employee information by creating a live, fully editable and constantly in sync place for all their People data that serves as their single source of truth.


This centralization eliminated the need to maintain separate spreadsheets or toggle between multiple systems to access critical employee information. For regulated health tech companies like Sonio, having accurate, accessible data is not just convenient, it's essential for their regular compliance checks.


### **Simplified Error Detection and Correction**


One of the most valuable aspects of Tables for Sonio is how it simplifies the process of identifying and correcting data errors:


> *"I think one of the key aspects of Tables to me is the ease of spotting mistakes and correcting them. What happens a lot is you're onboarding and the person in charge didn't put the right title, or we forgot to update the salary. With Tables, it's just so easy to correct directly where you see the mistake, and it’s applied across the HRIS."*


This streamlined approach to data management has been particularly valuable when preparing reports for shareholders. Tables extends beyond convenience to critical compliance and operational needs. Accurate data ensures regulatory compliance across multiple jurisdictions, prevents financial misstatements in consolidated reporting, and enables meaningful cross-subsidiary analysis. This comprehensive data integrity is essential when operating within the more complex and consequential reporting structures inherent in parent-subsidiary relationships, where errors can impact not just the local entity but the entire corporate structure.


### **Regulatory Compliance Made Simple**


For a medical tech company like Sonio, regulatory compliance is non-negotiable. Humaans has become an essential tool during their regular quality and security audits:


> *"In a highly regular industry, medical companies like us face regular audits. With Humaans, I can just screenshot the specific workflow and the auditors are satisfied. Humaans is our compliance safety net."*


The ability to quickly demonstrate compliance has saved the team countless hours of preparation and eliminated potential regulatory issues.


## **Seamless Integration with Key Tools**


Sonio leverages Humaans' integration capabilities to create a connected HR ecosystem:


- **Ashby integration:** Streamlines the recruitment process, automatically creating profiles in Humaans
- **Forms automation:** Sends forms automatically via email that feed directly into Humaans
- **Google Workspace & Slack:** Connects with the team's everyday collaboration tools


> *"Before, it was manual to create a lot of things. Now with Humaans, most of it is integrated. I hire in Ashby, it creates in Humaans, and then with the Forms feature, it sends the email automatically with the form that feeds directly into Humaans."*


## **A Foundation for Future Growth**


Humaans has become Sonio's cornerstone for managing their global workforce, enabling their growth in the complex healthcare technology sector:


> *"If you compare it to not having Humaans, it saves us hours and hours. The main thing with Humaans is how you've developed over the last few years. It's what you're going to build or keep building that's bringing value. For me, it's something that's really mandatory when you grow, you need to have an HRIS that grows with you."*


As Sonio continues to expand their innovative AI solution for prenatal healthcare, Humaans provides the solid foundation they need to scale their people operations efficiently.
