# Product Update: eSignature general release, Performance reporting, Policy updates

This month, we’re excited to roll out a powerful set of updates to enhance your experience with Humaans. From our general availability of eSignature to further performance and time away policy improvements, these new features will help you further streamline and control your people processes!


## **eSignature (General release)**


We’re thrilled to announce that eSignature is now live for all accounts! A huge thank you to everyone who shared feedback during early access, your input helped us shape this into a powerful and seamless experience.
​
You can now send, sign, and securely store contracts and key documents directly in Humaans - everything in one seamless flow. No more switching between tools or dealing with manual uploads.
​
​ **This enables you to:**


- Get contracts and important documents signed in just a few clicks;
- Automatically store signed documents in the right employee profiles;
- Send contracts in bulk to be signed by multiple users;
- Effortlessly track signing progress and stay in control at every stage.


We’d love for you to start using eSignature and unlock its full value. For questions, your dedicated CSM or our support team are here to help.


## **Performance Reporting**


We’re excited to share the latest addition to our Performance Management module, the ability to get granular reporting for all active and completed performance cycles. You’ll now see a dedicated tab (Reporting) giving you a powerful, table-based overview of each cycle’s Instances and Reviews with a range of insightful, cycle-specific data points. It’s designed to make performance tracking more transparent, data-driven, and shareable across your organisation.
​
​ **This enables you to:**


- Quickly assess review progress with the overview table, which provides rating visuals per reviewee;
- Compare manager, self, peer, and upward ratings side by side in the overview table;
- Switch to specific review type tables for more detailed information on specific review types;
- Spot confidence gaps with the new Confidence column: Underconfident, Aligned, or Overconfident;
- Sort, filter, and customise columns, similar to Tables functionality.


It’s designed to make performance tracking more transparent, data-driven, and shareable across your organisation.


##
**Performance permissions**


We’ve added two new permissions for performance reviews, giving Admins and Owners more control over what other team members can view or manage in a cycle.
​
​ **This enables you to:**


- Grant users access to all review content, AI summaries, peer nominations and exports;
- Let selected users create, edit and manage performance cycles.


## **Archiving and duplicating policies**


You can now **** archive and duplicate Time Away policies directly from Settings > Policies, making it easier than ever to manage and maintain your policy setup.
​
​ **This enables you to:**


- Archive policies you no longer want to assign, without losing historical data;
- Keep your policy list cleaner, with archived policies hidden by default;
- Prevent archived policies from being assigned to people or locations;
- Duplicate existing policies to quickly create new versions or variants;
- Ensure past allocations remain accessible and untouched, even when a policy is archived.


It’s a smoother, smarter way to keep your Time Away setup tidy and scalable.


### **Other improvements:**


- We now offer Romanian and Polish as options for in-app languages;
- You can now view Unrounded FTE in reports and tables for exact calculations as needed;
- Place of work city" and "Place of work country" filter available platform wide making it easier to see people in a specific geographic area, or enrol them into performance cycles or workflows;
- We’ve incorporated Employment fields into Workflow forms, including custom fields that live in the Employment section;
- You can now hide Birthdays and Anniversaries completely through role settings.


We hope you find these new updates useful! If you have any feedback or challenges to share, feel free to reach out – we're here to help.
