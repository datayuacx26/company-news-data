# Introducing Humaans eSignature

Managing employee contracts, onboarding paperwork and salary change agreements shouldn’t feel chaotic. At Humaans, we believe every HR workflow should be fast, reliable, and tightly connected to your people data. That’s why we’ve built Humaans eSignature, and now, we’re excited to announce it’s generally available to all customers.


With eSignature, you can send, sign, and manage documents directly inside your HRIS, no third-party tools, no messy exports, no disconnected workflows.


## **Why eSignature matters**


Electronic signatures aren’t just about speed. They’re about trust, compliance, and employee experience. By embedding eSignature into Humaans, HR teams can:


- **Populate documents instantly** with employee data, no copy-paste needed;
- **Connect contracts to processes** like onboarding, promotions, and salary changes;
- **Keep everything in one place** , signed contracts are automatically stored in employee profiles.


It’s contract management, built into the flow of work. We’ve designed the signing process to work for every use case. Need an employee to acknowledge a policy update? Done. Want to send a salary change agreement to a personal email? Easy. Recipients get a secure link that expires after a set time, with the option to request a new one if needed. Every action, from opening to signing, is tracked and auditable, and each completed document includes a certificate page with full details for compliance.


And because not every document requires a signature, you can decide whether a recipient signs, or simply receives the document as complete.


## **Built for HR**


The document builder gives you the tools to make contracts professional and clear, with images, tables, columns, different fonts, and more. Templates save you time by letting you define the structure once and then reuse it across the employee lifecycle. And profile fields are grouped by category, making it simple to drop in the right data without hunting around.


Taken together, these details mean People teams can finally treat contracts like the streamlined workflows they should be, not a set of admin tasks to be managed off-platform that can create issues.


## **Designed for trust**


Contracts are more than legal documents, they’re moments of trust between a company and its people. From offer letters to salary changes, every interaction matters.


With Humaans eSignature, HR teams deliver a seamless, secure, and connected experience that saves time and strengthens trust at scale.
