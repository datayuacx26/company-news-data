# How Humaans Requests Helps Wilson Browne Streamline Payroll and Expenses

### **Wilson Browne at a glance**


**Sector:** Legal Services


**Headquarters:** Northamptonshire, UK


**Team size:** 180 employees across six offices


Wilson Browne Solicitors is a full-service law firm with over 180 employees across the Midlands. As the firm continues to grow, so too does the administrative complexity behind managing payroll, overtime, and expenses across multiple offices and departments.


For Sally Mitchell, HR Director, and Ann Johnson, Payroll Administrator, managing overtime, pension percentage changes, and expenses used to involve a lot of manual coordination over email. With Humaans Requests, they’ve built a streamlined and transparent process that brings all payroll-related updates into one place. The team now saves several hours each month gathering the information needed for payroll, with a clear, real-time view of every request.


“Humaans has completely transformed how we manage overtime and expenses. Everything is tracked, authorised, and visible in one place.” - **Sally Mitchell, HR Director, Wilson Browne Solicitors**


### **Key Results**


- **Several hours saved every month** by removing manual tracking and email submissions
- **Overtime and expenses centralised** in one auditable workflow so they can review and authorise easily
- **Faster turnaround and lower risk of errors** between approvals and payments


## **The Challenge: Growing Firm, Fragmented Processes**


Before Humaans, managing overtime and expenses at Wilson Browne was a slow, manual process that relied heavily on email chains and spreadsheets. Employees submitted overtime hours or expense forms to their line manager via email. The messages would then make their way, to Ann in payroll for review.


“Someone would email their overtime, another would hand in a paper form, someone else would forget entirely,” Ann recalls. “Tracking it all down each month took time. With multiple offices and teams working different hours, it simply meant more coordination and follow-up to gather everything we needed.”


The lack of centralisation also made it difficult to maintain clear records for audit or compliance. “If we needed to check when something was paid, it could take half an hour to find the right form, it just wasn’t sustainable,” said Ann.


## **The Solution: Streamlined Submissions with Humaans Requests**


When Humaans introduced[Requests](https://humaans.io/features/requests-and-approvals) , Sally and Ann saw an immediate opportunity to simplify how the firm managed overtime and expenses.


### **Overtime submissions made simple**


Now, employees submit overtime directly through Humaans using a standardised Request form. They enter all required details in clearly defined fields, ensuring consistency and accuracy. Each submission automatically routes to the employee’s line manager for authorisation, and then to Ann for final payroll approval.


“We’ve built our own custom fields so people enter the right information the first time. Managers approve in one click, I review and note the payment month in the comment box, and that’s it. No chasing, no confusion, just a clean record.”


The result is full visibility for everyone involved: employees can see the status of their submissions, managers know exactly what’s pending, and payroll has a clear, auditable trail for every approved overtime claim.


### **Expenses with structure and traceability**


The team also uses Requests to manage expense claims. Employees upload their completed expense sheets and attach receipts directly in the form. Everything is logged and stored securely.


“It’s brilliant,” says Sally. “If anyone ever asks when something was paid or where a receipt is, we can find it in seconds.”


## **The Impact: Time Saved and Payroll Simplified**


Since adopting Humaans Requests, the firm has seen a dramatic improvement in both efficiency and accuracy. “It’s made life easier for everyone,” says Sally. Where the old system might have required hours of chasing and reconciling, now every submission follows the same clear path from employee to manager to payroll.


“Managers love it because it feels like a clear and efficient authorisation process,” Sally adds. “If they have a query, they can comment, decline, and it automatically goes back for resubmission. It’s professional, transparent, and simple.”


### **Smooth Adoption & Implementation**


The tool has now expanded beyond HR and payroll: operations and IT teams use it to manage assets, and senior partners can use Humaans to stay updated on new joiners, leavers, and upcoming probation reviews. For Sally and Ann, Humaans is more than just a platform, it’s a partnership.


“Lucy has been absolutely fantastic,” says Sally. “She doesn’t just tell us what the system can do, she sits down with us, shows us how to build it, and helps us make it fit our processes. That hands-on support has been brilliant.”


Their feedback has even shaped the product. “We’ve suggested features and actually seen them appear in updates,” Sally adds. “It’s great to know Humaans listens and evolves with us.”


### **The Foundation**


As Wilson Browne continues to expand, Humaans provides the structure and scalability they need to keep people operations running smoothly.


“Humaans just makes everything easier,” says Sally. “It’s intuitive, reliable, and constantly improving. For us, it’s become part of how we work, and I wouldn’t go back.”
