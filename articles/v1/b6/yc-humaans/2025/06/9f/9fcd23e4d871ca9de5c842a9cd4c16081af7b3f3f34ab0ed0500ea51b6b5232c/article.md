# Product Update: AI Performance Review Summaries, Multi-language, Spaces and more

This month, we’re excited to roll out a powerful set of updates to enhance your experience with Humaans. We’re introducing our AI-powered performance review summaries, making review analysis faster and more insightful. You can now manage multiple organisations under a single account with Spaces, enabling consistent reporting, visibility, and processes across complex setups. And with support for 10 languages, Humaans is now even better for global teams.


### **AI Performance Review Summaries**


We’ve received a lot of excitement for our newest AI development. AI Performance Review Summaries can save you days of manual data processing. You’ll find them directly on the employee profile card, giving managers an instant overview of performance. By pulling insights from all previous reviews, it automatically highlights strengths, growth areas and generates a full written summary to support development conversations.


This enables you to:


- Instantly surface strengths and growth areas;
- Remove guesswork from development discussions;
- Aggregate and analyse feedback instantly.


AI summaries help managers and employees quickly identify key themes, making it easier to run more focused and productive feedback sessions.


###
​ **Multi-language**


Humaans can now speak your team’s language! With multi-language support, users can switch to the language that fits them best, whether it’s Swedish, French or one of several other supported languages. In this initial release, we support British English, American English, Svenska, Français, Deutsch, Español, Norsk, Italiano, Dansk, Simplified Chinese (简体中文), and Traditional Chinese (繁體中文).


This enables you to:


- Let employees use Humaans in their preferred language;
- Simplify onboarding and daily usage across different regions;
- Support global teams with a better, more inclusive and accessible experience.


###
**Spaces (Multi-Account Support)**


Spaces bring additional flexibility to how you structure people and data inside Humaans as you scale Whether you're managing multiple legal entities, brands, or locations, or working with external collaborators and contractors, Spaces give you the control to separate and organise information while maintaining a unified overview. It’s especially valuable for companies navigating M&A activity or operating in complex, multi-entity environments.


This enables you to:


- Manage multiple entities in a single Humaans account (build for multi-entity businesses and contingent workers);
- Separate external collaborators and employees into distinct Spaces;
- Run unified processes across entities and locations.


### **Performance Reviews Improvements**


We're continuing to ship improvements based on your feedback to make performance reviews more powerful and adaptable. You can now control visibility and add reviewers manually in your performance cycles, giving you even more flexibility.


This enables you to:


- Change performance review visibility for managers;
- Add multiple manager reviews manually when needed;
- Request self, peer, manager and upwards reviews.


### **Platform alerts**


We’ve introduced a new safeguard to keep your integrations running smoothly. If an integration owner leaves, you’ll now get notified to take action.This enables you to:


- ​​See clearly who owns which integration;
- Get alerts when ownership needs updating;
- Avoid disruptions by disconnecting and reconnecting in time.


### **Slack integration Updates**


We’ve made several improvements to give you more flexibility and control when connecting Slack to Humaans, highly requested by IT Teams to further enhance data access security..


This enables you to:


- Connect Slack without needing public permission, making it easier for security teams to approve;
- Send weekly digest posts to private channels, DMs and group messages for more targeted visibility;
- Limit Slack updates to only include events from the company’s primary Space.


We hope you find these new updates useful! If you have any feedback or challenges to share, feel free to reach out. We're here to help.
