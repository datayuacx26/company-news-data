# How Humaans Tables Transforms People Data Management

People teams are under growing pressure to drive impactful initiatives while managing increasingly complex employee data. The days of static employee records and outdated spreadsheets should be behind us - yet many companies still rely on manual exports, version-controlled files, and time-consuming updates.


[Humaans' Tables](https://humaans.io/features/tables) changes that. It brings powerful spreadsheet functionality directly into your HRIS, creating a seamless, efficient way to manage people data collaboratively across departments, without ever leaving the platform.


Here’s what Tables helps you unlock:


- 10x faster reporting: no more exporting or reformatting data
- One secure source of truth: reduce compliance risk and manual errors
- Real-time collaboration: HR and Finance work from the same live view
- Smarter decision-making: instant access to accurate, up-to-date information
- Save hours every week: from performance reviews to compensation planning


### **Hidden Risk of Spreadsheet Chaos**


For companies growing their headcount at pace, the challenges multiply exponentially.[Threecolts](https://humaans.io/blog/how-threecolts-manages-data-and-generates-reports-10x-faster-using-humaans-data-tables) , an ecommerce software scale-up that acquired eighteen companies in just three years, found themselves managing complex people data across 500+ employees in 32 countries.


"Every single time I wanted to do some analysis on our teams, I had to download figures into a spreadsheet, then customize it. It took a lot of tweaking to get the data to show the view I want." Head of People, Gemma Bianchi, Threecolts


Most HRIS platforms are good at storing data, but bad at letting teams work with it. Want to run analysis? You export to Excel. Want to collaborate? You share static files and start a parallel offline process. Before long, different teams are working off different versions, leading to errors, duplication, and compliance risks. Processes like performance reviews, compensation adjustments, or headcount planning, working with outdated or fragmented data can lead to costly mistakes. This is especially risky when multiple people are collaborating across the data.


**That’s where Tables comes in.** No more exporting. No more reformatting. No more emailing spreadsheets. Just one secure, live source of truth that everyone can work from, fully permissioned, fully in sync.
