# How SignUp use Humaans to scale their People Ops

## **SignUp at a glance**


- **Office locations:** 8 offices worldwide, with employees across Europe, North America, and Asia-Pacific
- **Employees:** 400+ (and growing rapidly)
- **Sector:** SaaS within Business process automation.
- **HR tech stack:** Humaans, Microsoft, various ERP systems
- **Challenge:** How do you build scalable people processes while integrating newly acquired companies across multiple regions?


## **Scaling People ops at speed**


SignUp Software has undergone a dramatic transformation in recent years. Originally founded in 1999 as an education-focused business, the company pivoted into financial software in 2003 with the creation of ExFlow. For years, it remained a family-owned one-product business with around 40 employees.


That changed rapidly after listing on the Swedish stock market in 2021, and subsequent acquisition by Insight Partners in 2023. With new backing, the company entered a new phase of rapid scaling through both organic growth and acquisitions. Aligning with the company vision to be the leading Independent Software Vendor within the Microsoft Dynamics 365 ecosystem, SignUp actively pursued acquisitions and partnerships with other players in the Microsoft field to enhance their capabilities and market reach.


> *“Humaans gives us the foundation we need to keep pace with rapid growth” — Marie Norinder, HR Director, SignUp Software*


When Marie Norinder joined as HR Director in 2024, SignUp had 180 employees. Within just twelve months, the headcount has more than doubled to nearly 400, supported by acquisitions and partnerships in Denmark, the Netherlands, and the US. This growth required the company to quickly rethink how it managed people and systems.


### **Key results so far**


- **Consolidated employee data** across multiple acquisitions into one system
- **Reporting is faster and more reliable** , with automated recurring reports for payroll and headcount
- **Tables reduce admin time** by enabling bulk edits instead of manual updates


## **Implementing scalable HR processes with Humaans**


SignUp needed an HR system capable of scaling globally, matching their ambition. The previous platform was designed around Nordic labor law, which created issues in new markets.


After evaluating several platforms, SignUp selected Humaans for its flexibility, scalability, and user-friendly design. The ability to customise[workflows](https://humaans.io/features/workflows) for different regions,[centralise employee data](https://humaans.io/features/insights) , and[streamline reporting](https://humaans.io/features/reports) made it the best fit for the company’s international ambitions.


> *“The implementation support was fantastic. Ayrton understood our needs immediately, took the workload off our team, and made the transition seamless.” — Marie Norinder*


## **Looking ahead**


With Humaans powering their People infrastructure, SignUp is now preparing for its next phase of growth. “We’ve only scratched the surface of what Humaans can do. Having one global, flexible HR system helping us scale is critical,” said Marie.
