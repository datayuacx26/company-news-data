# Humaans x Remote

Humaans is excited to announce a new integration with[Remote](https://remote.com/en-gb/) , the leading employer of record (EOR) platform that makes it simple for businesses to hire, pay, and manage global teams compliantly.


As companies grow across borders, people data and employment systems become increasingly fragmented. HR teams are often caught in manual updates between HRIS and EOR systems, slowing down onboarding, payroll updates, and compliance reporting.


The new **Humaans + Remote integration** bridges this gap, enabling seamless, automated data syncing between your HRIS and Remote’s platform, ensuring accuracy, compliance, and efficiency across your global workforce.


## About Humaans


Humaans is a next-generation HRIS supporting globally distributed organizations. Powered by a flexible data model, deep integrations, fast APIs, and enriched by AI, Humaans removes manual work, reduces compliance risk, and enhances your employee experience.


## About Remote


Remote helps companies of all sizes employ global talent legally and easily, no matter where they’re based. Through its employer of record (EOR) model, Remote handles payroll, benefits, taxes, and compliance for international hires — empowering organizations to scale their teams globally with confidence.
