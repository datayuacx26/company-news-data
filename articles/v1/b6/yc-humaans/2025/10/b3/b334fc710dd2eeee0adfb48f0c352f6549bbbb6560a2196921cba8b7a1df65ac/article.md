# Product Update: Workflow permissions, eSign improvements, Document Uploads in Requests & more.

This month, we’re introducing a new set of updates to help you stay in control of your people processes even further. From more flexibility with Workflows and eSignature permissions to smarter performance management tools and request enhancements. Read on.


## **Workflow Permissions**


We’re thrilled to announce the launch of Workflow Permissions Control. A major update that unlocks far more flexibility and security across your workflows.


This enables you to:


- Assign, View and Manage workflow permissions to specific Roles, beyond just Admins, Finance, and Owners;
- Control permissions at the individual workflow level, so users can access some workflows but not others;
- Ensure visibility respects role scope. For example, Managers can only see their direct reports within workflows.


## **eSignature Permissions and Improvements**


We’ve rolled out major enhancements to eSignature, making it even more powerful and easier to manage at scale.


This enables you to:


- Assign eSignature permissions to any company wide permission role;
- See and create eSignature documents based on role-specific permissions;
- View documents directly in My Tasks, allowing signatures to be completed right in the platform;
- Enhanced document formatting tools: tables, columns, custom fonts, images and improved numbered list formatting (1.1, 1.2, 1.2.1, 1.2.2 etc.) to make drafting legal contracts easier;
- Include upcoming compensation details - great for upcoming salary increase confirmation letters;
- Allow end users to securely refresh expired signing links themselves;
- Signed documents are now securely sealed with digital tamper-evident seals.


Together, these updates make document signing smoother, smarter, and more secure for every team.


##
**Hide specific questions from reviewees**


You can now hide specific questions in performance review templates from reviewees, giving you more control and privacy over how feedback is shared.


This enables you to:


- Exclude questions or ratings from being visible to reviewees (“Hide from reviewee” option);
- Apply it to any review type (except Self Reviews) and question type, including ratings;
- Ensure hidden questions and answers stay invisible across reviews, reports, and exports.


A simple yet powerful way to tailor reviews to your organisation’s feedback style.


## **Document uploads in requests**


You can now include document uploads as part of creating a request. When configuring request templates, simply add document fields (required or optional), to capture the files you need. This allows teams to attach supporting documents directly in the request flow, making the process smoother and more complete from start to finish.


### **Other improvements:**


- Export performance review reports as CSV: analyse and share insights with ease;
- Duplicate performance cycles: create new cycles with a single click, carrying over all configuration details;
- “Last performance rating” field: now available in Reports and Tables;
- Drag-to-fill in Tables: quickly populate repeated data across cells.


We hope you find these new updates useful! If you have any feedback or challenges to share, feel free to reach out – we're here to help!
