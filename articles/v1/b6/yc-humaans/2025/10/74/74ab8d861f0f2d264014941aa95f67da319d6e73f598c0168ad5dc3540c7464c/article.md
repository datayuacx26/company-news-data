# From Fast Hiring to Effortless Onboarding: How Humaans Helps Wild Grow

## **Wild at a glance**


- **Sector:** Consumer goods / Sustainable personal care
- **Employees:** 140+
- **HQ:** London, UK


As Wild continues its rapid growth in sustainable personal care, the People team faces a familiar challenge: hiring fast while keeping every onboarding experience consistent, personal, and compliant. With up to 18 open roles at any given time, the admin load of managing recruitment, pre-boarding, and new-hire setup could easily overwhelm a small team.


That’s why Wild turned to Humaans. By automating pre-boarding, onboarding, and performance processes, Humaans has helped Wild create a streamlined, scalable foundation for people ops, one that saves time, ensures consistency, and helps every new joiner feel set up for success from day one.


> “Humaans is efficient, simple, and sleek, everything just works. It’s given us structure and time back while keeping things personal.” — **Alexis Conti, Senior People & Talent Executive, Wild**


### **Key results so far**


- **3 hours saved every week** by automating pre-boarding tasks
- **100% of new joiners complete documentation before day one** , no chasing, no errors
- **Consistent onboarding experience** across all teams through workflows
- **Faster manager readiness** with pre-scheduled 30/60/90-day probation plans sent automatically
- **Seamless performance reviews** that encourage open dialogue and development


## **The challenge: Fast hiring, limited time**


At any given moment, Wild’s People team manages a constant flow of recruitment, often 15 to 18 live roles, and welcomes multiple new starters each week. Without the right system in place, onboarding would mean endless emails, repeated document collection, and manual tracking to make sure every new hire has been set up right.


“Because we hire so frequently, the admin would be endless,” says Alexis. “If I had to manually check each person’s progress, chase missing forms, and upload every document myself, it would add at least three hours to my week, every week just to check we had the right personal information.”


Beyond the time pressure, Wild wanted a more consistent experience for new joiners and managers alike: one that felt personal and organized, not rushed or chaotic.


## **The solution: A single, automated flow for every stage**


With Humaans, Wild has been able to turn a previously fragmented process into a simple, connected experience, for both employees and the People team.


#### **Seamless pre-boarding**


Once a candidate accepts their offer, they’re automatically synced from Wild’s ATS into Humaans. Alexis simply adds their start date, title, and salary, and the system takes over. Automated emails invite new hires to upload their personal details, right-to-work documentation, and proof of address, no chasing, no spreadsheets, no missing information.


> “It’s honestly a lifesaver,” says Alexis. “Before, I was downloading attachments from emails, re-uploading them, and hoping I hadn’t missed something. Now it’s all in one place, and the new hire can’t move forward until they’ve completed everything. It saves me so so much time.”


#### **Structured onboarding that scales**


Wild runs two dedicated workflows — *Pre-Onboarding* and *Onboarding* — built directly in Humaans. Each includes detailed tasks for both new joiners and internal owners like IT and managers, complete with due dates, instructions, and links.


“When someone starts, they get their entire to-do list with everything they need to complete in their first weeks,” Alexis explains. “It’s detailed, visual, and automated, so I know they’re getting the same great experience every time.”


Managers also receive automated 30–60–90 day plans with guidance on how to prepare for their new hire’s arrival, from setting up one-to-ones and lunches to confirming goals for the first three months.


> “We even send managers reminders before someone joins, their checklist includes everything from scheduling meetings to onboarding essentials. It’s all pre-emptive, so no one’s caught off guard.”


#### **Performance management made simple**


Wild also uses Humaans to run structured performance reviews, from probation checks to quarterly cycles. New joiners complete a mid-probation check-in and an end-of-probation review with self, manager, and peer feedback, while ongoing employees take part in quarterly reflections.


“The rating system is great,” says Alexis. “It shows us where someone thinks they’re performing and how their manager sees it. If there’s a gap, that becomes a conversation point, it creates a much more open, productive review.”


## **The impact: clarity, consistency, and time back**


For a small team managing a big hiring volume, the shift has been transformative. Automations now handle repetitive admin, onboarding feels consistent across teams, and managers are better equipped to support their new hires.


“I don’t have to walk anyone through how to use Humaans,” says Alexis. “It’s so intuitive. People log in on day one, see their tasks, and just get started. It’s incredibly user-friendly.”


Across pre-boarding, onboarding, and performance, the People team saves several hours every week, time they can now spend on the parts of their role that matter most: building culture, improving experience, and supporting Wild’s continued growth.


### **The foundation for scale**


For Wild, Humaans has become the backbone of their people operations, the single, efficient platform that connects everything from hiring to performance.


> “If I think about what we’d be doing without Humaans,” Alexis reflects, “it would just be chaos. It’s really efficient, it’s easy, and it saves us hours every week. It just works. That’s the best part.”


As Wild continues to grow, Humaans ensures their people operations scale right alongside them. Organized, automated, and built to last.
