# Product Update: eSignature, Requests & Approvals updates, Multi-Space Admin roles, and more

This month, we’re excited to roll out a powerful set of updates to enhance your experience with Humaans. From smarter approval controls and role assignments to the announcement of our upcoming eSignature, these updates are designed to help you streamline operations and reduce manual work.


## **eSignature (Early Access)**


A highly requested addition to the Humaans capability is now here in Early Access - eSignature! You’ll soon be able to send, sign and store contracts and key documents directly in Humaans, all in one seamless flow. No switching tools, no manual uploads. We’re incredibly excited to bring this to you and would love your feedback as we get ready for general availability. Get early access and help us shape your experience.
​
This enables you to:


- Get contracts and key documents signed, with just a few clicks;
- Store signed documents automatically in the right employee profiles;
- Easily track signing progress and stay in control of every step.


Want early access or more information? Get in touch.


##
**Request and Approvals Improvements**


Advanced new capabilities for Request and Approvals are now available, including smarter assignee logic, custom rules and conditional flows. Manage complex approvals in one streamlined flow without creating multiple versions of the same request.
​
This enables you to:


- Choose dynamic assignees or approvers like manager’s manager or custom people fields (e.g. People Partner);
- Control who can create each type of request using filters like department, place of work, or contract type;
- Build conditional approval flows that route requests to the right approvers based on filters like location or team.


##
**Multi-Space Admin Roles**


Last month, we launched Spaces in Humaans, multi-account support that lets you manage multiple entities, brands, locations, or external collaborators while keeping a unified overview. You can now create custom permission roles that work across multiple Spaces giving admins greater flexibility to manage access across entities, teams or locations. You can also keep roles limited to a single Space using the new Space filter.
​
This enables you to:


- Limit custom roles to a specific Space using the new Space filter;
- Assign custom roles (e.g. US People Team) to manage multiple Spaces;
- Create custom admin roles that can manage all Spaces.


## **Organisation Level Reports Filter**


We’ve made it easier to zoom in on insights based on team structure. When building reports, you can now filter by employee type, giving you a clearer view of managers vs individual contributors.
​
This enables you to:


- Filter reports by “Manager” (anyone with direct reports) or “Individual contributor”;
- Understand patterns by role type across your organisation;
- Surface insights that help drive more targeted people strategies.


## **New Integrations**


Our list of available integrations is ever-expanding! We welcome these new partners to our ecosystem:

We hope you find these new updates useful! If you have any feedback or challenges to share, feel free to reach out – we're here to help.
