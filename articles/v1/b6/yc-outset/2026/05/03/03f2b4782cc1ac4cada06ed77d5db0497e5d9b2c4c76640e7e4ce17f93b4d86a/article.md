# Introducing: Custom Moderator Voices

## **What's New**


-


**Expanded voice library** , powered by our new partnership with Cartesia


-


**Regional accents and dialects** across dozens of languages, including British English, Brazilian Portuguese, and Indian English


-


**Per-study voice selection** , for teams running interviews across global markets


-


**Pairs with Custom Moderators** , so persona and voice can match the study


## **Why it Matters**


A participant in São Paulo hearing a Brazilian Portuguese moderator is in a different study than the same participant hearing an American voice.


The cost of a single global default shows up as shorter answers, more hesitation, and lower completion in markets where the moderator sounds out of place.


One customer running interviews across African markets surfaced this directly. Regional voice and accent support is the fix, and it's live now.


## **How to Use It**


-


In study settings, enable interviewer voice


-


Select your study's language, then choose voice and accent


-


Apply to your study before launch


Available now in all Outset plans. Powered by Cartesia (SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, GDPR).
