# Astronics Corporation Announces Third Quarter 2025 Financial Results Conference Call and Webcast

# Astronics Corporation Announces Third Quarter 2025 Financial Results Conference Call and Webcast

EAST AURORA, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Astronics Corporation](http://www.astronics.com/) (Nasdaq: ATRO), a leading supplier of advanced technologies and products to the global aerospace, defense, and other mission critical industries, announced that it will release its third quarter 2025 financial results after the close of financial markets on Tuesday, November 4, 2025.


The Company will host a conference call and webcast that same day to review its financial and operating results and discuss its corporate strategies and outlook. A question-and-answer session will follow.


***Third Quarter 2025 Conference Call***

Phone: (201) 493-6784
Webcast:[investors.astronics.com](https://investors.astronics.com/)


A telephonic replay will be available from 8:00 p.m. ET on the day of the call through Tuesday, November 18, 2025. To listen to the archived call, dial (412) 317-6671 and enter replay pin 13755702. The webcast replay can be accessed via[the investor relations section of the Company’s website](https://investors.astronics.com/news-events) where a transcript will also be posted once available.


**ABOUT ASTRONICS CORPORATION**


Astronics Corporation (Nasdaq: ATRO) serves the world’s aerospace, defense and other mission-critical industries with proven innovative technology solutions. Astronics works side-by-side with customers, integrating its array of power, connectivity, lighting, structures, interiors and test technologies to solve complex challenges. For over 50 years, Astronics has delivered creative, customer-focused solutions with exceptional responsiveness. Today, global airframe manufacturers, airlines, military branches, completion centers and Fortune 500 companies rely on the collaborative spirit and innovation of Astronics. The Company’s strategy is to increase its value by developing technologies and capabilities that provide innovative solutions to its targeted markets


For more information on Astronics and its solutions, visit[Astronics.com](http://www.astronics.com/) .
