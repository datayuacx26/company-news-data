# Helene Fischer’s 360° Open Air Tour Draws Nearly 750,000 Fans Across Europe

# Helene Fischer’s 360° Open Air Tour Draws Nearly 750,000 Fans Across Europe


July 23, 2026 |

### KEY TAKEAWAYS


-


Nearly 750,000 fans joined Helene Fischer across 15 open-air shows in Germany, Austria, Switzerland and the Netherlands


-


The tour ended with a sold-out show for 71,000 fans at Munich’s Allianz Arena


-


Produced and organised by Live Nation, the 360° show was one of the largest stadium productions worldwide


Helene Fischer brought her Open Air Tour 26 to a close with a sold-out show for 71,000 fans at Munich’s Allianz Arena.


The Munich finale capped a run of 15 open-air concerts across Germany, Austria, Switzerland and the Netherlands, attended by nearly 750,000 fans in total – making it one of the biggest chapters in Fischer’s live career to date.


Built around a 360° stage, the show was designed to bring Fischer as close as possible to fans throughout each stadium. Over two and a half hours, she celebrated 20 years of her career with a set featuring her biggest hits, joined by an international ensemble that included Thomas Seitel, 20 dancers and 12 band members.


Fans turned out in huge numbers throughout the tour. Berlin welcomed 90,000 people, while 120,000 attended two concerts in Hamburg and another 90,000 joined Fischer across two shows in Frankfurt. The Cologne show drew 85,000 fans, followed by 57,500 in Vienna and a sold-out crowd of 52,000 in Stuttgart.


“Every tour changes you – me included,” Fischer said. “I’m taking countless memories, emotions, and encounters with me, but above all, new energy and inspiration. I’m grateful for everything we’ve experienced together. And if this tour has shown one thing, it’s that the most beautiful moments happen when people forget everything around them for a few hours and simply enjoy the moment together.”


The 360° tour was produced and organised by Live Nation, marking the company’s second major tour collaboration with Fischer following her arena tour.


“We are immensely proud of this milestone in Helene Fischer’s success story, as she has once again demonstrated her unique popularity and outstanding quality,” said Marek Lieberberg, CEO of Live Nation GSA. “The 360° tour also showed how to deliver incomparable, magical moments to fans that will remain in their memories. That was our ambition from the very beginning.”
