# Local Noise Philadelphia Launches Search For The Region’s Next Breakout Artist

# Local Noise Philadelphia Launches Search For The Region’s Next Breakout Artist


July 9, 2026 |


[Venues](https://newsroom.livenation.com/news/?filter=Venues) |


Share on


### KEY TAKEAWAYS


-


Local Noise Philadelphia is a new battle of the bands-style competition celebrating the next generation of artists from across the region.


-


Bands and artists have until July 24 to submit for the chance to compete live at Brooklyn Bowl Philadelphia this August.


-


One winner will earn the opportunity to perform at Freedom Mortgage Pavilion, take the next step in their career and receive a cash prize.


Every artist starts somewhere.


**Local Noise Philadelphia** is designed to help the region’s next generation of bands and artists take the next step.


The new battle of the bands-style competition from


**Live Nation** gives artists and bands the opportunity to perform, build new audiences and connections, and compete for experiences that elevate their careers.


Bands and artists from across the region can submit to compete in


**Local Noise Philadelphia** through the


[online application portal](https://m.cmpgn.page/7VcQ86) now through


**July 24** .


Selected artists will compete in live semifinal performances on


**August 13 and 14 at**[Brooklyn Bowl Philadelphia](https://www.brooklynbowl.com/philadelphia) . The competition will culminate with the championship finals back at the venue on


**August 20** .


**The winning artist or band will receive:**


- The opportunity to perform at


**Freedom Mortgage Pavilion** during a major concert event later this year.


- A


**$5,000 cash prize.**
- Exposure to new fans and opportunities to build valuable industry connections.


**Local Noise Philadelphia** shines a spotlight on emerging artists from across the region while celebrating the depth and diversity of Philadelphia’s music scene. For artists, it’s an opportunity to gain valuable live performance experience, grow their fan base and take the next step in their careers. For fans, it’s a chance to discover new local artists before they reach the big stage.


**COMPETITION TIMELINE**


- **Submission Deadline:** July 24


- **Live Semifinal Performances:** August 13–14 at Brooklyn Bowl Philadelphia


- **Live Finals:** August 20 at Brooklyn Bowl Philadelphia


All bands interested in participating must submit an application through the


[online portal](https://m.cmpgn.page/7VcQ86) for a chance to perform. Official contest rules and eligibility requirements are available


[here](https://m.cmpgn.page/hqQVkC) .
