# For You Fest Turns The Social Feed Into A Live Festival Experience With Creator-Led Live Podcasts, Comedy, Music, Beauty, Fashion and Culinary Workshops, And Interactive Fan Experiences


July 8, 2026 |


[Festivals](https://newsroom.livenation.com/news/?filter=Festivals) |

### KEY TAKEAWAYS


- Lineup includes Quenlin Blackwell, Tana Mongeau, DJ Trixie Mattel, Trisha Paytas, Patrick Ta, Wisdom Kaye, Caleb Hearon & Drew Afualo, Meg Stalter, Owen Thiele, and more


- November 14-15 At Great Park in Irvine, CA


- Presale begins Friday, July 10 at 10am PT with guaranteed lowest price 2-Day GA Tickets available for one hour only


**For You Fest** turns the social feed into a live festival experience as the first large-scale festival built around creator culture. Fans can watch live podcast tapings, attend creator-led workshops, enjoy music and comedy performances, and meet the creators they follow online. The inaugural festival includes an all-star lineup including **Quenlin Blackwell, Tana Mongeau, DJ Trixie Mattel, Trisha Paytas, Patrick Ta, Wisdom Kaye, Caleb Hearon, Drew Afualo, Meg Stalter, Owen Thiele,** and more. **For You Fest** takes place November 14–15 at Great Park in Irvine, California. The presale for all ticket types begins **Friday, July 10 at 10am PT with guaranteed lowest price 2-Day GA Tickets available for one hour only at** ****[www.foryoufestival.com](http://www.foryoufestival.com/) .


**For You Fest** will feature programming on three stages (Podcast & Comedy, Music, Beauty & Fashion), including live podcast performances with **Quenlin Blackwell** (“ *Feeding Starving Celebrities”* ), **Tana Mongeau** *(“Brand Safe”* ), and **Owen Thiele** , comedy sets from **Caleb Hearon & Drew Afualo** and **Meg Stalter** , musical performances by **DJ Trixie Mattel** , **Trisha Paytas** and **Meg Stalter** , and beauty and fashion masterclasses with **Patrick Ta** and **Wisdom Kaye** .


The **Culinary** and **Beauty & Fashion Workshops** are add-on experiences that give fans exclusive, hands-on access to some of their favorite chefs, creators, and industry experts. Fans will enjoy interactive instruction, insider tips, and live Q&A sessions while learning new skills in fully equipped workshop stations. Whether creating delicious recipes from scratch or discovering the latest beauty and fashion techniques, attendees will leave with memorable experiences, exclusive products, recipes, specialty ingredients, signed items, or custom keepsakes, depending on their selected workshop. In addition, **Meet & Greets** offer attendees the opportunity to connect one-on-one with their favorite creators beyond the stage. Fans can share a personal moment, take photos, create content together, and make lasting memories in an intimate setting that brings them closer to the personalities they love.


In addition to live activities across the stages, fans can enjoy shopping in the Trend Market, where they can discover, sample and shop buzzworthy brands, and enjoy crave-worthy bites and sips at the Food & Bev Village.


The presale for 2-Day and 1-Day General Admission, Pit Viewing, VIP, Besties and Suite Tickets begins at 10am on July 10 at[www.foryoufestival.com](http://www.foryoufestival.com/) , with guaranteed lowest price 2-Day GA Tickets available for one hour only. A general onsale will follow if tickets remain. Layaway plans are available. Children 2 and under may enter for free with a ticketed adult.


Pit Viewing Ticket offers access to exclusive front-of-stage viewing, with complimentary water. Tickets options include Pit Viewing at the Podcast & Comedy Stage or Pit Viewing at the Music Stage.


VIP Guests will enjoy unlimited access to the VIP Lounge, featuring shade, relaxed seating, complimentary beauty amenities to touch up your festival flare, early access to shop at the Trend Market, complimentary tote pre-packed with festival samples, air-conditioned restrooms, private bar with drinks for purchase, complimentary water refill station, access to a dedicated Premium entrance lane into the festival, an exclusive Photo-Op, dedicated checkout lane at the festival store, and onsite VIP hospitality staff.


The 2-Person Besties Package includes preferred entry, unlimited access to the ultra-exclusive Besties Lounge which includes shade, relaxed seating and complimentary all-day drinks and grab & go snacks, access to the VIP Lounge, exclusive front-of-stage viewing at both the Podcast & Comedy Stage and the Music Stage, dedicated viewing at the Beauty Stage, one reserved parking spot, a dedicated concierge, private golf cart transportation, access to the Artist Village and the Guest Lounge, one meet and greet of your choice, early access to the Trend Market, and an exclusive Besties festival gift.


Get the gang together and grab a Suite! Suites offer unlimited access to a dedicated, private space for 15 guests per day, expansive views of the Podcast & Comedy Stage, access to Pit Viewing at the Music Stage, expedited entry via a dedicated Hospitality Entrance, a private bar, hosted daily dinners, complimentary grab and go snacks, access to the VIP Lounge and all GA areas, air-conditioned restrooms, two reserved parking spaces, and on-site Suite hospitality staff.


For You Fest is produced by C3 Presents, producers of Lollapalooza, and Live Nation.
