# Stripe Sessions is back on May 6, 2025

[Please join us May 6–8 at Moscone West in San Francisco](https://stripesessions.com/?utm_medium=owned-surfaces&utm_source=35f2&utm_campaign=US%2FCA_4ef8&utm_content=8e61&utm_term=a6efaed093c0) for Stripe Sessions, our annual user conference. This year, we’re doubling the number of breakout sessions, expanding our training program to give you more hands-on support, and adding a demo stage to showcase more of Stripe’s key product updates.


Our agenda is informed by everything we learned from you at Sessions 2024, where more than 5,700 attendees joined us as we discussed[the future of payments](https://stripe.com/sessions/2024/the-future-of-payments) , hosted fireside chats with industry leaders such as[Jensen Huang](https://stripe.com/sessions/2024/a-conversation-with-nvidias-jensen-huang) , and debuted our[biggest product updates](https://stripe.com/sessions/2024/opening-keynote) . Based on your feedback, our program this year will include:


- Keynotes by our cofounders, John and Patrick Collison, who will talk about the future of the internet economy and share a first look at new products designed for your revenue growth.
- Fireside chats with some of the most influential people in finance and technology.
- Breakout talks where you can learn about new tools and creative strategies for revenue growth, including sessions on how to unlock new monetization options, how to create payment experiences that increase customer satisfaction and loyalty, and more.
- A demo stage to highlight key product updates and offer hands-on solutions for your business.
- Our biggest-ever expo, which will provide a space to meet industry experts, business leaders, and Stripe’s most innovative partners, and see the latest applications of AI technology in our AI showcase.
- Time with Stripe experts to discuss products and use cases with our expanded training program, now covering more topics—with smaller class sizes—and half-day options that allow you to customize your curriculum depending on where you need more support.
- Networking lunches at Yerba Buena Gardens and evening receptions with food and drinks.


We’re excited to give you more of what you like most about Sessions: in-depth talks, hands-on product support, and useful demos.[Register today to secure your spot](https://stripesessions.com/?utm_medium=owned-surfaces&utm_source=35f2&utm_campaign=US%2FCA_4ef8&utm_content=8e61&utm_term=a6efaed093c0) .
