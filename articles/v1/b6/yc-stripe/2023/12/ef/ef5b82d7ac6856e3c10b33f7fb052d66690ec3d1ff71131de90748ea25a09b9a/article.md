# Stripe Sessions 2024—come join us

Earlier this year, thousands of you—representing more than 1,500 companies and 90 countries—joined us in San Francisco for Stripe Sessions. In 2024, we’re going even bigger.[Please join us April 23–25 at Moscone West in San Francisco](https://stripesessions.com/?utm_medium=organic-social&utm_source=c448&utm_campaign=US%2FCA_4b9d&utm_content=a7a1&utm_term=22eeedf10b84) for our largest event ever. The program includes:


- Keynotes by our cofounders, John and Patrick Collison, who will talk about payments as a profit driver and what we’re building next.
- Fireside chats with special guests you won’t want to miss.
- Breakout talks for businesses of all sizes on how to increase revenue with payment optimizations, turnkey monetization, and finance automation. A few of our speakers include:


- [Anu Ramanathan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/anu-ramanathan-982416/) , SVP, Head of Product and Design, Sotheby’s
- [Brendan Gualdoni](https://www.linkedin.com/in/brendangualdoni/) , Head of Digital Commerce Platforms, Wiley
- [Dan Chandre](https://www.linkedin.com/in/danchandre/) , Head of Acuity and Payments, Squarespace
- [Daniela Amodei](https://www.linkedin.com/in/daniela-amodei-790bb22a/) , President and Cofounder, Anthropic
- [George Whitaker](https://www.linkedin.com/in/georgewhitaker/) , Senior Director, Enterprise Technology, PGA of America
- [Leigh O’Neill](https://www.linkedin.com/in/leighoneill1/?originalSubdomain=au) , Executive General Manager of Money, Xero
- [Ryan Tabone](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ryant/) , Chief Product and Technology Officer, Lightspeed


- Opportunities for networking, interactive learning, and meeting Stripe partners in our expo hall.
- Time with Stripe experts to discuss products and use cases.
- Evening receptions with food, drinks, and networking.


That’s 2.5 days of in-depth talks, useful demos, inspiring speakers, new connections, and endless opportunities. It’s going to be both fun and informative, and we look forward to seeing you there.
