# Benchtop Thermal Testing Solutions Webinar

inTEST Thermal recently hosted a webinar detailing how you can quickly and easily test components, chips, electronics, materials, and other devices on your benchtop.


Barry Johnson, Global Product Director at inTEST Thermal Solutions, walked through a number of tips and tricks on how to quickly and accurately test for and then mitigate frost when going cold.


Barry also discussed how to:


- **Maximize Lab Space:** Discover how a small, powerful thermal cycling system can optimize your lab's footprint.


- **Mitigate Frost During Cold Testing:** Learn effective strategies to prevent frost buildup.


- **Improve Test and Cycle Times:** Get insights on enhancing performance with PID controls.


- **Solutions for Data Logging and Remote Communications:** Find out how to drive and capture all your test activities efficiently.


Barry Johnson is an electrical engineer and thermal testing expert with more than 20 years of experience in addressing temperature-related testing, cycling, and characterization challenges.
