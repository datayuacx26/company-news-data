# Webinar: Thermal Plates Uncovered

This webinar, hosted by Cody Dorris and Barry Johnson from InTest Thermal, focused on introducing thermal plates as an alternative to traditional thermal chambers for testing applications. Barry explained the key advantages of thermal plates, including their smaller size, higher heat dissipation capacity, faster temperature transition rates, and cost-effectiveness compared to chambers.


The presentation covered the technical specifications of thermal plates, including cooling options using liquid nitrogen or compressor-based systems, heating capabilities, and the importance of proper test subject placement for optimal results. Barry discussed application considerations, including the need for flat, thermally conductive surfaces and the use of covers to maintain a frost-free environment during testing.


The webinar also covered controller features, including overdrive capabilities and remote interface options via USB, Ethernet, and other protocols. Cody addressed attendee questions about custom plate designs, minimum plate sizes, cost comparisons with chambers, safety features, and remote-control options.
