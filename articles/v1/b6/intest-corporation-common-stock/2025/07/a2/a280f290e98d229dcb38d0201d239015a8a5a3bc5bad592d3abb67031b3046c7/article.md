# Thermal Testing 101 Webinar

##


###### Thermal testing is a vital process in modern manufacturing and engineering, ensuring that products can withstand extreme temperature conditions without compromising performance or safety. From electronics to aerospace, thermal testing helps validate product durability, temperature resistance, and compliance with industry standards.


###### An informative session where we'll delve into the essential techniques and cutting-edge technologies that ensure your products perform reliably under extreme temperature conditions. Whether you're new to thermal testing or looking to enhance your knowledge, this session will provide valuable insights to help you succeed.


## **Webinar Highlights**


- **Different Technologies of Thermal Testing:** Discover the various methods and tools used to ensure your products can withstand extreme temperatures.


- **Challenges in Thermal Testing:** Learn about the common obstacles faced during thermal testing and how to overcome them.


- **Rules for Successful Thermal Testing:** Understand the best practices and guidelines to achieve accurate and reliable results.


- **Hints & Tips for Successful Thermal Testing:** Gain practical advice and insider tips to enhance your thermal testing processes.


- **Applications:** Explore the wide range of industries and scenarios where thermal testing is crucial for product reliability and performance.


This informative session will be hosted by Ruchal Humbare, Marketing Communications Manager at InTest Thermal Solutions, and feature our special guest, Barry Johnson, Global Product Director and Stephen Wagner, Regional Sales Manager. With over three and a half decades of expertise in the test and measurement industry, Barry has extensive experience in PCB, module, and system testing. Recently, his focus has been on designing and specifying thermal solutions for sectors such as semiconductor, telecom, automotive, aerospace, and industrial.
