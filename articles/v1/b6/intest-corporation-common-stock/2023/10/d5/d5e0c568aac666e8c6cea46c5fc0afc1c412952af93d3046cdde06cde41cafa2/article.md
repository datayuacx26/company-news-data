# Easily Add Thermal Testing to Industrial PCB Manufacturing Processes

## The Problem:


Product reliability challenges over the specified thermal range of your printed circuit boards (PCBs).


## The Solution:


Thermal testing with a portable ThermoStream


The supply chain for electronic components has been in a state of uncertainty for several years. Obsolescence, long lead times, and high prices have lead R and D teams to have to continuously source alternative components.


Just because a sourced component has the same spec, does not mean that it will work the same when it is integrated into a PCB.


To ensure the reliability of your products over their operating temperature range, thermal testing is required.
