# Product Update: March 2025

Welcome to this month's update of newly released features. We recently launched a few pay features that make it easier to pay contractors on-demand and keep payments running on autopilot. We also incorporated AI into our I-9 workflow to improve fraud detection. And, we have fresh monetizing worker payments content for you.


## Pay


**Paycard disbursements on-demand** - For companies doing 1099 contractor payments who want more control or to pay after a job/shift is completed, we’ve made it possible to send funds to a paycard whenever you want. Now you can use the API to trigger funds from being sent to a paycard at any time.[Learn more.](https://docs.zeal.com/reference/trigger-disbursement-contractor-payment) **‍**


**‍ Top up flows** - To ensure you always pay workers on time, we’ve implemented automated transfer workflows with rules so that you always have money in your reserve account. You can either set funds to transfer based on an account minimum and maximum, or based on a schedule. This is especially helpful for companies using Instant Pay, Paycards, or Direct Pay to more easily manage your reserve.[Learn more.](https://docs.zeal.com/reference/get-provider-balance)


## Onboard


**I-9 AI evaluations** - We’re now incorporating AI models when evaluating IDs to more accurately surface potentially fraudulent ID submissions. Admins will be able to see a task in the Dashboard with details about what the evaluation determined to review before being sent to E-Verify. Details of the evaluation are also available in the API and the I-9 summary report.
