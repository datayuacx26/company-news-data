## Garnishment Management


Handling garnishments for 1099 contractor payments is a complex but essential responsibility. Businesses making payments to independent contractors may be legally required to process deductions for child support, tax levies, creditor garnishments, or student loans. Requirements vary state to state. Failing to properly manage these withholdings can result in financial penalties, legal issues, and operational inefficiencies.


Most payment processors are not built to handle garnishments for independent contractors, leaving businesses to track, calculate, and remit payments manually. Without automation, managing these deductions can become an administrative burden, increasing the risk of errors and non-compliance.


### 1099 Garnishment Compliance: Zeal Handles It


Payment processors do not support automatic garnishment deductions for 1099 payments. This means businesses must manually process and remit garnishments to the appropriate agencies, adding risk and inefficiency to their operations. Since garnishments often have strict deadlines and complex requirements, relying on a manual process can lead to missed payments and potential legal consequences.


Zeal, however, provides built-in garnishment management for 1099 payments. Our system automates the entire process, ensuring that:


- Deductions are calculated accurately based on garnishment orders.
- Payments are sent to the correct agencies on time.
- Compliance requirements are met without additional manual work.


### Why This Matters for High-Volume 1099 Payments


For businesses managing large contractor workforces, manually processing garnishments at scale is not sustainable. Zeal automates garnishment deductions for independent contractor payments, reducing administrative overhead and ensuring compliance with federal and state regulations. With Zeal, businesses can focus on scaling their contractor network without worrying about garnishment compliance challenges.
