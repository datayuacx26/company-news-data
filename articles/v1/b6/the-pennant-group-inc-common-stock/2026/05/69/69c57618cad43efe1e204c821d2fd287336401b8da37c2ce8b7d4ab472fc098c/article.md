# Pennant Group to Participate in the 2026 RBC Global Healthcare Conference

[PDF Version](https://investor.pennantgroup.com/node/9986/pdf)


EAGLE, Idaho, May 18, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- The Pennant Group, Inc. (NASDAQ: PNTG), the parent company of the Pennant group of affiliated home health, hospice and senior living companies, announced today that it will participate in the upcoming 2026 RBC Global Healthcare Conference on May 20, 2026. John Gochnour, President and Chief Operating Officer, Lynette Walbom, Chief Financial Officer, and Andy Rider, President of Senior Living, will participate in a fireside chat on May 20, 2026 at 9:30 a.m. Eastern Time. A live webcast of the event will be accessible at[https://kvgo.com/rbc/the-pennant-group-inc-may-2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=iEF5YP1ZfqSaB6swrEdpkNkCyNgaTmE_YGGJnPb10QIkKH4cFv8NtkMNb_9UJn7yGCIrJ1kT8wN0S_Pi3auC2ULl0cw7J0jX-7QQTlPenI2-YKn73p6KWqf-E-hzCKoCWsjF4NGpGu1_NOFo43fHVOlLg7eZtVuNN6aidNkiytSAB0Fgg2JneJbCOPu5Y58-) .
