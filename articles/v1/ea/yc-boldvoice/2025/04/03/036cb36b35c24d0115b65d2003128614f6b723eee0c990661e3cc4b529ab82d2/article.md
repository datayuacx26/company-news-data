To help you prep smarter (not harder), we’ve rounded up five of the best apps for IELTS speaking. Whether you're aiming to improve fluency, sharpen pronunciation, or reduce hesitation, these apps will help you move closer to your target band score.

## 2. EngVarta


For learners who want real-time practice with human conversation partners, EngVarta offers a unique approach. This IELTS speaking practice app connects you with live English experts for guided speaking sessions, which are especially helpful if you struggle with hesitation or overthinking.


‍


Each call helps simulate the pressure of a real IELTS exam, where you need to[listen](https://www.boldvoice.com/blog/listening-comprehension) , process, and respond on the spot. After each session, you get personalized feedback highlighting grammar[mistakes](https://www.boldvoice.com/blog/common-mistakes-in-english) , mispronunciations, and areas for improvement. This immediate correction loop is excellent for building speaking fluency and reducing[filler words](https://www.boldvoice.com/blog/filler-words-in-english) or awkward pauses.


‍


However, because EngVarta is fully conversation-based, it doesn’t include structured lessons, quizzes, or pronunciation drills like other apps do. It’s great for improving your spontaneous responses, but not ideal if you’re looking for self-paced exercises or a visual learning environment.


‍


It’s also worth noting that EngVarta is a paid service. While the quality of feedback is strong, using the app daily can be expensive. Still, for learners who benefit from human interaction and want to overcome speaking anxiety, it’s a valuable tool to consider alongside other apps for IELTS speaking.

## 3. SmallTalk2Me


SmallTalk2Me takes a more tech-forward approach, combining IELTS speaking simulations with smart feedback powered by AI. It’s a great IELTS speaking practice app for anyone who wants detailed evaluations without having to schedule live sessions.


‍


The app offers a wide range of speaking prompts that mimic real exam questions. You record your answers and receive feedback on fluency, grammar, vocabulary usage, and coherence. It helps you recognize not only what you said, but *how* you said it—something that’s key when you’re aiming for a high speaking band score.


‍


A standout feature is the[job interview](https://www.boldvoice.com/blog/top-25-english-job-interview-phrases) simulator, which tests how well you can respond to[common professional questions in English](https://www.boldvoice.com/blog/interview-questions-and-answers-in-english) . This doubles as excellent speaking prep for IELTS Part 2 and Part 3 questions that often require longer, more structured responses.


‍


The app also includes a CEFR-based English level test, which helps you track improvement over time. On the downside, the AI may not always understand accent variations or subtle pronunciation differences, and it lacks the full spontaneity of real conversation. Still, SmallTalk2Me offers a solid balance between convenience, depth, and structure.


## 4. IELTS Speaking Assistant


IELTS Speaking Assistant is less flashy than some other apps, but it’s an incredibly practical resource. Focused on content rather than interactivity, it provides a massive bank of real IELTS questions, model answers, and topic ideas to help you prepare more strategically.


‍


If you're just starting out or feel unsure about how to answer IELTS questions, this app can be a lifesaver. You’ll get a feel for how examiners expect you to structure your responses and what kinds of vocabulary score well. The included model answers also help you compare your own responses and pick up useful phrases.


‍


However, this IELTS speaking app is more of a study aid than a practice tool. It doesn’t include voice recording, feedback, or AI assessments. You’re not speaking into the app or getting corrections—you’re mostly reading and learning. That makes it best for learners who already have a foundation in spoken English and want to fine-tune their understanding of IELTS topics.


‍


To get the full benefit, consider pairing this app with one that lets you practice speaking aloud, record yourself, and receive feedback.


‍


## 5. FixoLang


The IELTS Speaking test includes a cue card section that trips up a lot of test-takers. FixoLang is one of the few apps for IELTS speaking that replicates this experience in full. You’re given a topic, one minute to prepare, and then you speak—just like the real test.


‍


After you submit your answer, the app evaluates it across four key IELTS categories: grammar, coherence, vocabulary, and fluency. You also get a predicted band score, which gives you a realistic sense of how you're doing.


‍


The AI feedback is fairly detailed and helpful for identifying weak points, especially for learners who need practice organizing their thoughts. However, FixoLang’s prompts can become repetitive over time, which may limit your exposure to a broader range of IELTS speaking topics. It works well as a self-assessment tool, but for more varied practice and skill-building, you may want to pair it with another IELTS speaking practice app.


‍


Still, if you're looking for a low-pressure way to practice cue card responses and get fast feedback, FixoLang is a smart choice to have in your prep toolkit.
