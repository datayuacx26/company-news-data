## **Processing Warranty Repairs and OEM Coordination**


Warranty repairs are one of the last maintenance workflows most gyms still manage manually. While platforms like[OpenWrench](https://www.useopenwrench.com/) automate work orders, inspections, and vendor coordination, warranty claims with equipment manufacturers typically remain disconnected — tracked through original equipment manufacturer ([OEM) portals](https://www.knack.com/blog/oem-partner-portals-no-code/) rather than integrated software.


Most major fitness brands use online warranty portals that let operators register equipment, submit service requests, and track repair progress. Claims must be authorized by the manufacturer and routed to approved service providers before parts are repaired or replaced. The process works, but it’s slow — turnaround times often stretch[two to eight weeks](https://www.reddit.com/r/PlanetFitnessMembers/comments/1hbacxx/what_is_the_turn_around_time_for_machines_that/) depending on parts and approvals. During that time, key machines sit idle, frustrating members and cutting into revenue.


Today, these OEM systems rarely connect directly with facility management tools.[Matrix](https://matrixhomefitness.com/) and[Core](https://corehandf.com/) , for example, maintain integrations with fitness-tracking ecosystems like[EGYM](https://egym.com/int) and[Myzone](https://www.myzone.org/) , not maintenance software. This gap leaves operators manually logging warranty work orders and following up on claim statuses across multiple systems.


This is where OpenWrench adds value. The platform centralizes warranty information, service history, and part replacements in one place. Even when OEMs require external claim submission, facilities teams still have a clear record of what’s covered, what’s pending, and what’s next. By combining warranty tracking with[preventive maintenance workflows](https://www.useopenwrench.com/blog/commercial-kitchen-maintenance-checklist) , OpenWrench helps gyms reduce downtime, avoid duplicate repairs, and recover more value under warranty coverage.


Across the manufacturing world, automated warranty systems have been shown to[cut processing costs](https://dev.to/robertwilson1/reducing-warranty-costs-key-strategies-for-oems-371n) and claim cycle times while improving customer satisfaction. For gyms, adopting CMMS platforms like OpenWrench extends these same benefits to equipment management — transforming warranty repair from a slow, manual task into a transparent, data-driven process.


## **How We Compare the Best CMMS for Gyms**


Choosing the right cloud-based CMMS software requires more than comparing feature lists. The goal is to find a platform that fits your operational complexity, budget, and growth plans. Below is how we evaluated each option and why these criteria matter for day-to-day gym operations.


### What matters (and why)


To evaluate each platform fairly, we focused on the core capabilities that drive results in a fitness environment. These areas directly impact uptime, costs, and member experience.


- **Pricing:** Transparent pricing helps you forecast return on investment (ROI) and avoid hidden per‑user or implementation charges. For multi‑site gyms, unclear fees can turn a good tool into an unexpected budget risk.
- **Integrations:** The CMMS should connect with enterprise resource planning (ERP)/accounting and vendor networks, along with other core management tools. Clean data flows make approvals, purchase orders, and cost allocation faster and more accurate.
- **Customer support:** When a critical asset is down, response time matters. Look for onboarding help, training resources, and fast access to technical support.
- **Ease of use:** If the mobile app is hard to use, adoption drops. We looked for clear workflows, user-friendly interfaces, and mobile-first experiences.
- **Key features:** Work order management, preventive maintenance (PM) scheduling, asset management, vendor management, and real-time dashboards form the baseline. Strong analytics and performance metrics turn maintenance data into decisions.


Together, these factors separate systems your teams *tolerate* from systems they *champion* . With this framework set, here’s how the top CMMS platforms for gyms compare — and what makes each stand out.
