A vault temperature spike at 2 a.m. shouldn’t threaten your entire operation, yet it happens. One failed HVAC unit can destroy high-value cannabis products before anyone arrives for Monday’s shift. A camera outage during an inspection can turn into a regulatory compliance risk. A malfunctioning access-control panel can stop a store from opening for the day.


For dispensary owners, these failures aren’t just maintenance issues. They risk your inventory, customer experience, reputation, and your license. Your point of sale (POS) system manages payment processing and debit transactions, your online menu drives sales activity, and your seed-to-sale software handles traceability. But none of them alerts you when a refrigeration unit drifts out of range or when your last security inspection wasn’t logged.


Cannabis retail needs an all-in-one approach to facilities oversight. A CMMS gives dispensaries and cannabis businesses management tools to schedule preventive maintenance (PM), track work orders in real time, manage contractors, protect inventory, and maintain documentation for state regulators.


We compared nine CMMS platforms through the lens of cannabis operations, facility complexity, and multi-state growth. Here’s what we learned.


## **How We Compare the Best CMMS for Cannabis Dispensaries**


Dispensaries operate under rules that shift by state, license type, and local ordinance. A CMMS that works for a grocery store won’t automatically fit the needs of cannabis operations. Our evaluation focuses on compliance tools and criteria that matter for cannabis retail, MSOs, and vertically integrated companies:


- **Compliance documentation:** Support for audit-ready logs across security systems, vault equipment, refrigeration units, HVAC, and environmental controls
- **Multi-location scalability:** Ability to manage 5 locations or 50 while accommodating different state regulations and inspection processes
- **Vendor management:** Tools that help track contractor access, record visit history, and streamline communication with HVAC, security, and facilities vendors
- **Mobile functionality:** User-friendly workflows for budtenders and store managers who need fast, in-store work request submission
- **Integration potential:** API support for enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems, cannabis POS systems, customer relationship management (CRM) tools, e-commerce platforms, and delivery management systems.
- **Pricing transparency:** Clear understanding of subscription costs, vendor fees, and add-on modules
