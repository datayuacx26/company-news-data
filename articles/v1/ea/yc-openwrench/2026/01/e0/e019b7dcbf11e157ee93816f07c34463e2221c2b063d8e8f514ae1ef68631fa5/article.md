Connecting facilities management software to gym-specific platforms like ClubReady, Jonas Fitness, or PerfectGym eliminates these inefficiencies. Unified APIs — such as those offered by OpenWrench — bring this same efficiency to maintenance workflows, bridging equipment records with member management systems to boost reliability and cut operational friction.


## **Processing Warranty Repairs and OEM Coordination**


Warranty claims remain among the few maintenance tasks most gyms still manage by hand. While platforms like OpenWrench handle work orders, inspections, and vendor management automatically, manufacturer warranty processes typically stay disconnected — tracked through original equipment manufacturer (OEM) portals rather than unified software.


Leading fitness equipment manufacturers maintain dedicated warranty portals where operators register purchases, initiate service tickets, and follow repair status. Each claim must receive manufacturer approval and route through certified technicians before any parts get serviced or swapped. The system works, but slowly — resolution times commonly stretch three to six weeks based on parts availability and authorization delays. Meanwhile, premium machines sit unused, members complain, and revenue leaks away.


These OEM platforms rarely sync with facility management tools today. Life Fitness and Precor, for example, integrate with member engagement apps like Fitbit and Apple Health rather than maintenance systems. This gap leaves operators copying warranty details between systems and chasing claim updates across multiple dashboards.


OpenWrench fills this void. The platform brings warranty details, maintenance logs, and component replacements into one unified view. Even when manufacturers require claims through their own portals, facility teams keep complete records of what's covered, what's in progress, and what needs attention next. By combining warranty tracking with preventive maintenance schedules, OpenWrench helps gyms cut equipment downtime, eliminate redundant service calls, and capture more value from warranty coverage.


Throughout industrial sectors, automated warranty management has proven to slash administrative costs and accelerate claim resolution while improving customer outcomes. For fitness operators, adopting facilities management software like OpenWrench extends these same benefits to equipment care — turning warranty repairs from a tedious manual chore into a streamlined, insight-driven workflow.
