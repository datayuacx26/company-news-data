## Marriott Vacations Worldwide Named a 2026-2027 Best Company to Work For by U.S. News & World Report


**ORLANDO, Fla.** — Marriott Vacations Worldwide Corporation (NYSE: VAC) has been named a 2026-2027 Best Company to Work For by U.S. News & World Report, with recognition in the Overall, Hospitality & Entertainment, South and Internships categories. The recognition reflects the Company’s continued focus on creating a workplace where associates feel supported, connected and empowered to grow, with rankings informed by key factors such as pay and benefits, work-life balance, stability, belonging and career opportunities.


“This recognition is a reflection of our associates and the culture they help shape every day through their care, commitment and service,” said Denise Haeggberg, chief human resources officer. “We strive to create an environment where every associate feels valued, supported and inspired to do their best work — for one another and for the owners, members and guests who trust us to deliver memorable vacation experiences.”


Marriott Vacations Worldwide brings its culture to life through programs and experiences that support associates professionally and personally, from leadership development and career growth opportunities to associate engagement, recognition and inclusion initiatives. Guided by its values of Caring Culture, Customer-Obsessed, Integrity First and Excellence Always, the Company is focused on creating a workplace where associates can feel connected to one another, proud of the experiences they help deliver and supported in building fulfilling careers with lifelong growth opportunities.


Earlier this year, Marriott Vacations Worldwide was recognized by Newsweek on its lists of America’s Greatest Workplaces and America’s Greatest Workplaces for Women.


**About Marriott Vacations Worldwide Corporation**


Marriott Vacations Worldwide Corporation is a leading global vacation Company that offers vacation ownership, exchange, rental and resort and property management, along with related businesses, products, and services. The Company has approximately 120 vacation ownership resorts and approximately 700,000 owner families in a diverse portfolio that includes some of the most iconic vacation ownership brands. The Company also operates exchange networks and membership programs comprised of more than 3,200 affiliated resorts in over 90 countries and territories and provides management services to other resorts and lodging properties. As a leader and innovator in the vacation industry, the Company upholds the highest standards of excellence in serving its customers, investors and associates while maintaining exclusive, long-term relationships with Marriott International, Inc. and an affiliate of Hyatt Hotels Corporation for the development, sales and marketing of vacation ownership products and services. For more information, visit www.marriottvacationsworldwide.com.
