# Amprius Recognized by TIME as One of America’s Top GreenTech Companies for 2026

# Amprius Recognized by TIME as One of America’s Top GreenTech Companies for 2026


March 26, 2026 8:30 am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_433739fff1c1ca150231c0ca841605ec/amprius/news/2026-03-26_Amprius_Recognized_by_TIME_as_One_of_America_s_162.pdf)


FREMONT, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Amprius Technologies](https://amprius.com/) **(NYSE: AMPX** ), a leader in silicon anode lithium-ion batteries, today announced it has been named as one of[America’s Top GreenTech Companies 2026 by TIME](https://time.com/article/2026/03/25/americas-top-greentech-companies-2026/) .


This press release features multimedia. View the full release here:[https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260326554015/en/](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260326554015/en/)


Amprius Recognized by TIME as One of America’s Top GreenTech Companies for 2026


Amprius was honored by TIME and Statista as one of America’s Top GreenTech Companies 2026, recognizing its overall innovation, environmental impact, and financial strength. The Company is among the top 250 companies selected from more than 3,500 evaluated worldwide, reflecting its continued growth and expansion across commercial applications as demand increases in its core markets, including aviation, defense, and advanced mobility.


“This recognition from TIME is meaningful because it brings together companies that are not just developing new technologies, but actually improving how energy is used and delivered,” said Tom Stepien, CEO of Amprius. “At Amprius, we are laser-focused on creating batteries that push performance while also reducing the energy required to operate them. That combination is what drives real sustainability-focused progress, and we are proud to be included in this group.”


The Company’s inclusion in TIME’s Top GreenTech Companies reflects its continued progress across innovation and commercial execution. Amprius’ strong[Q4 and full-year 2025 fiscal results](https://ir.amprius.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/160/amprius-technologies-reports-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2025-financial-results-and-recent-business-highlights) demonstrated robust revenue growth, improving margins, and rising demand across its core markets. The Company also recently announced[its first U.S.-based manufacturing](https://ir.amprius.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/157/amprius-secures-strategic-u-s--based-manufacturing-partner-to-scale-domestic-silicon-anode-battery-production) partner, marking a significant milestone in expanding capacity and strengthening its domestic supply chain, further positioning Amprius as a leader in sustainable energy solutions.


Amprius continues to advance high-performance energy solutions that improve efficiency and reduce energy intensity. This approach was also recognized at the[CES Innovation Awards](https://ir.amprius.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/154/ces-recognizes-amprius-with-an-innovation-award) in January of this year. Together, these developments reflect the combination of innovation, climate-focused impact, and growing market adoption that define Amprius’ selection in this year’s Top GreenTech Companies.


**About Amprius Technologies, Inc.**


Amprius Technologies, Inc. is a leader in advanced lithium-ion battery technology, delivering high-energy and high-power silicon-anode batteries with up to twice the energy density, range, and flight time of conventional graphite-based cells. Headquartered in Fremont, California, Amprius operates an R&D lab and pilot manufacturing facility for silicon anodes and cells. To support scalable production, the Company employs a contract manufacturing strategy, enabling rapid capacity expansion with minimal capital investment. Committed to driving innovation in energy storage, Amprius powers next-generation applications in aerospace, defense, and mobility. For additional information, please visit[amprius.com](https://amprius.com/) and the Company’s[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ampriustechnologies/) page.
