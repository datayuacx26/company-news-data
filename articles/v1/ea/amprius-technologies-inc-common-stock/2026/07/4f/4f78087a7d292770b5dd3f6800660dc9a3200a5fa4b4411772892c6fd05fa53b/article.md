# Air taxis can’t take off as a business until batteries catch up

Battery storage capacity has been improving at about 7% per year,[according to BloombergNEF data](https://edz.bib.uni-mannheim.de/edz/pdf/swd/2021/swd-2021-0307-4-en.pdf) cited in a European Commission report. At that pace, today's best cells would reach the viability threshold in roughly eight years. New materials could cut that timeline. A battery cell's performance on paper, though, is never what a finished pack delivers in practice. The cooling systems, connectors, and structural casing that turn individual cells into a usable unit consume space and add weight, reducing what the pack can actually store. A cell that clears the threshold on paper may still fall short inside an aircraft. Every route, business plan, and certification deadline in the industry turns on whether that gap closes fast enough to build a transportation network, not just a demonstration fleet.
