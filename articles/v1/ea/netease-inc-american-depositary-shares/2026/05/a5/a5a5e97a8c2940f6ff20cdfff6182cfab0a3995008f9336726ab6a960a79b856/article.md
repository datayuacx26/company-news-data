# Racing Master Available Now in Europe, North America, the Middle East, and North Africa


NetEase Games today announced that Racing Master, the mobile racing game developed in collaboration with Codemasters, is now officially available on iOS, Android, and PC emulator. Featuring over 100 officially licensed vehicles recreated through 1:1 real-world scanning, faithfully reproduced world-famous real racing circuits and iconic city street tracks from across the globe, as well as authentic driving sounds recorded from real vehicles, Racing Master delivers a truly immersive and realistic racing experience.


Europe, North America, the Middle East, and North Africa players can now download and play Racing Master via the App Store, Google Play or PC Emulator：[https://racingmasterna.onelink.me/QJxp/f8v2ateg](https://racingmasterna.onelink.me/QJxp/f8v2ateg)


Racing Master launch CG:[https://youtu.be/sTp4k6hLTeQ](https://youtu.be/sTp4k6hLTeQ)


Ahead of its launch in Europe, North America, the Middle East, and North Africa, Racing Master has also received featured pre-registration recommendations from both the App Store and Google Play. Racing Master will feature a diverse lineup of tracks that combine world-famous real-world racing circuits with city-inspired street courses. Players can race through iconic locations including Chicago, Amsterdam, Barcelona, San Francisco, the Alps, Sicily, Red Bull Ring, and Indianapolis, delivering a thrilling mix of urban street racing and professional circuit competition.


To celebrate its launch, Racing Master will feature a limited-time opening car pool headlined by the Lamborghini Aventador SVJ (LB834) ’19, Lamborghini Gallardo LP570-4 Superleggera ’11, and Toyota Sprinter Trueno GT-Apex (AE86) ’85. In addition, two American supercars — the Ford GT ’17 and Chevrolet Corvette C7 ’19 — will receive a limited-time drop-rate boost, giving players even more chances to expand their dream garage from day one.


Racing Master has rolled out a series of in-game events and rewards for players. All players can earn up to 300 pulls as launch rewards.By participating in the Launch Special: The Crew Up Project event and completing designated invitation missions, players can obtain the Ford GT ’17, and if the squad successfully completes the event challenge, all players will also receive the Corvette C3 as an additional reward. In addition, players who accumulate log in for 7 days and complete designated beginner missions can obtain at least three gold-tier vehicles, including the Ferrari F8 Tributo '20, Lamborghini Squadra Corse Huracán, and Audi RS7 Sportback '20.
