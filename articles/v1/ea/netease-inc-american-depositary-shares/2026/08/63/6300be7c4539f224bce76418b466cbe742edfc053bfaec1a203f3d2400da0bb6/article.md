# NetEase Games Unveils an Action-Packed Gamescom 2026 Line-Up


**Play Sea of Remnants, Marvel Rivals, Where Winds Meet and More at this year’s gamescom**


NetEase Games is heading to gamescom 2026 with an exciting line-up of playable titles, immersive booths, live performances and exclusive rewards.


Visitors can find NetEase Games at:


- Marvel Rivals and Where Winds Meet: Hall 7.1, Booth B-050
- Sea of Remnants: Hall 7.1, Booth A-051
- NetEase Games Business Area: Hall 4.2, Booth D-050


**Set Sail in Sea of Remnants**
Embark on a vibrant adventure in Sea of Remnants, where players can explore the bustling city of Orbtopia and a vast archipelago of mysterious islands. Meet unforgettable NPCs, engage in strategic turn-based combat, experience a long-form roguelike progression system, and uncover countless stories and discoveries along the way.


The adventure continues beyond the game. Visitors can customize their own puppet, take photos with characters from the game, create personalized beach postcards and complete on-site missions to unlock more than 10 types of exclusive merchandise. Around 10 cosplayers—and one giant hamster mascot(!)—will also bring the vibrant world of Sea of Remnants to Gamescom!


**Enter the Battle in Marvel Rivals**
Jump into the action of Season 9.5 at the Marvel Rivals booth! Jubilee, The Hood, Magik, Emma Frost and Psylocke cosplayers will be there to cheer players on, pose for photos and selfies, bringing iconic heroes into the real world.


The immersive booth inspired by the Egyptian setting will feature a big Jeff The Landshark statue for players to take selfies with - and there is a photo booth for Marvel Rivals-themed instant photos! Additionally, there will be plenty of free giveaways, including badges and Gamescom exclusive keychains.More surprises will await on August 28, when mysterious special guests arrive at the booth for an exclusive stage performance and fan interaction.


**Discover New Legends in Where Winds Meet**
Step into the mesmerizing Wuxia world of Where Winds Meet with the demo for the highly anticipated main chapter finale of the Hidden Mountain expansion! Players will have the opportunity to explore the latest mountain peak region, try out the brand new Drunken Master martial arts style, and challenge a formidable boss - someone from the past that marks a significant turning point in the main storyline.


Additionally, at its captivating booth, Where Winds Meet has dedicated time to bringing the enigmatic Ghostlight Market to life. Players can immerse themselves in the intriguing market tricks and earn exclusive rewards along the way. Seven talented cosplayers will transform your favorite heroes of the Wuxia realm into reality, while a special robot cosplayer will seamlessly blend technology with martial arts aesthetics, delivering a unique wuxia-punk performance.


**More to Come**
In addition to the titles mentioned above, 2 more playable games and media interview opportunities will be available at the NetEase Games Business Area in Hall 4.2, Booth D-050. Featured titles include Once Human, which will reveal details about its biggest expansion since launch, alongside an exclusive hands-on preview of a highly anticipated upcoming title.


Follow NetEase Games on social media for more Gamescom 2026 announcements and surprises.


**About NetEase Games**
NetEase Games, the online games division of NetEase, Inc. (NASDAQ: NTES and HKEX: 9999), is a leading global developer and publisher of video game IP across a variety of genres and platforms. NetEase Games’ development and publishing slate includes titles such as Marvel Rivals, Where Winds Meet, Knives Out and Naraka: Bladepoint, and partnerships with major entertainment brands such as Warner Bros and Mojang AB (a Microsoft subsidiary).
