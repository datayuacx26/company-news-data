# How an Ohio School Distict Transformed Budget Visibility with FiscalVue

## From Data Overload to Financial Clarity


#### How a K-12 School District Transformed Budget Visibility with FiscalVue


###### **K-12 School District | 6 Schools | 2,000+ Students**


**"FiscalVue gives our finance team and school leaders the ability to understand their budgets in real time. That visibility leads to greater confidence, better planning, and more informed decisions across the district."**
— District Treasurer


A growing K-12 school district transformed how it manages and shares financial information by implementing FiscalVue alongside its existing ERP system. As budget complexity increased and finance staff faced a steady stream of budget questions from administrators, the district needed a better way to provide timely, accurate financial information without creating additional work.


With FiscalVue, principals and department leaders gained secure, role-based access to real-time budget information, allowing them to answer their own questions and monitor spending independently. The finance team reduced manual reporting and one-off budget requests while improving forecasting, financial transparency, and collaboration across the district.


The result was greater operational efficiency, more proactive financial management, and better decision-making, all without replacing the district's existing financial systems.
