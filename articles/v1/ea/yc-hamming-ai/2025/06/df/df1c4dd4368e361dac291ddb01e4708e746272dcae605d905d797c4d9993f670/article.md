## Why This Partnership Exists


I'll be direct: enterprise voice AI is different from startup voice AI. The stakes are higher, the compliance requirements are stricter, and "it usually works" doesn't fly when you're handling customer calls for large organizations.


Cisco's Webex AI Agent is sophisticated - handles multiple channels, personalized conversations, the works. But sophisticated also means more ways for things to break. More edge cases. More scenarios that are hard to test manually.


That's where we come in. We've been building testing infrastructure for exactly this kind of complexity.


## What Cisco Customers Get


**Before you ship:** We simulate thousands of test calls with different accents, background noise levels, and edge-case scenarios. The kind of testing that would take your team weeks happens in hours. We've seen teams cut 100+ hours of manual QA time this way.


**After you ship:** 24/7 monitoring. When something breaks - latency spikes, audio issues, agents going unresponsive - you find out immediately, not when customers start calling support.


**Compliance:** Automated red-teaming for the things auditors care about. Prompt injection testing. High-volume stress tests to find the breaking points before your customers do.
