# VisionWave advances patent application, introduces AI engine for defense

WEST HOLLYWOOD – VisionWave Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ:VWAV), a micro-cap defense technology company with a market capitalization of $7.65 million, has filed a response to the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office regarding its Radio Wave Finder technology continuation patent and introduced a new AI engine called Evolved Intelligence, according to a company press[release](https://www.vwav.inc/) . InvestingPro data shows the company’s stock has declined 33.45% year-to-date, reflecting the challenges facing early-stage defense tech[firms](https://www.vwav.inc/visionwave-advances-patent-application-introduces-ai-engine-for-defense-investing-com/) .


The defense technology company submitted its response on August 18, just six days after receiving an Office Action from the USPTO on August 12. The Office Action raised a procedural double-patenting matter that VisionWave addressed with a Terminal Disclaimer. Trading at $7.44, the stock has shown relatively low market volatility with a beta of 0.15, according to InvestingPro analysis.
