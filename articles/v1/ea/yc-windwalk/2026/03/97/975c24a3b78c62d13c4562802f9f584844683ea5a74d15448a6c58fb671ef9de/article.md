# SWORN 1.0 Update #2 is LIVE!

Soulforged,


This update comprises Boss Rush mode, new skin rewards, enemy difficulty modifiers and additional quality of life features. See the summary of the 1.2 update below:


###


New Gameplay Mode: Boss Rush


More Achievements & Accolades:


-


Added four new achievements for beating the Boss Rush Mode with all four playable characters.


-


Additional accolades to earn in-game.


###


Boss Rush Mode:


Boss Rush takes place in a single-room, arena-style encounter set within Camelot.


Bosses have been reworked to fit the Camelot setting:


-


They now spawn Camelot enemies instead of creatures from their original biomes.


-


Visual effects have been updated to reflect their corruption.


###


New Character Cosmetics


Cosmetic rewards for defeating Dragon Arthur are now complete and available to unlock.


*❗ Dragon Arthur has been reworked and can now only be encountered and defeated in Boss Rush mode*


###


Enemy Difficulty Modifiers


The Enemy Difficulty Modifier system is now complete.


-


On higher difficulties, Corrupted enemies have a chance to spawn, dropping bonus rewards.


-


Corrupted enemy attacks are now fully implemented, featuring updated animations and VFX.


-


The Corruption system has been reworked to increase run variety and introduce more interesting choices while helping offset the added difficulty from Corrupted enemies.


-


Each door now has a chance to become corrupted, offering:


-


A range of possible corruption effects


-


Increased reward potential


-


Higher corruption chances also increase the likelihood of receiving enhanced rewards.


###


Codex Updates


The Codex now includes a Wishlist feature:


-


Players can mark blessings directly in the Codex.


-


Wishlisted blessings are highlighted in the Blessing Selection Menu, making it easier to track and acquire specific blessings for quests or unlocks.


###


The overall Codex UI/UX has also been improved:


-


Hovered blessings are now highlighted in white for better clarity.


-


“Any” required blessings are highlighted in orange.


-


“All” required blessings are highlighted in red.


-


Tooltips have been repositioned and no longer obscure blessings.
