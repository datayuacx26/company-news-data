# SWORN 1.0 Update #1 is LIVE!

Arthur has brought in some reinforcements! Three new alternate Bosses have been introduced. When playing on Knight difficulty or higher, these new foes can sometimes appear at the end of a Biome instead of the Boss you normally would encounter.


###


New Content


**Lady Kay, Wall of Camelot**


Though corrupted, Lady Kay remains a stalwart warrior in service to Arthur. She guards the entrance to Cornwall with her gigantic shield, pummeling down any who dare challenge Arthur’s rule. Her greatshield is not unbreakable, however, and if the Soulforged can find a way to power through her defenses, they have a shot at taking her down.


**Sir Dagonet, Grasp of The Mire**


This gluttonous former Knight revels in his newfound power, delighted at any new playthings entering his swamp. His devastating hook can render Soulforged immobile, as he drags them around his swamp filled with traps and poison. Clever warriors will need to dismantle his fixtures around the arena, before they drown in Sir Dagonet’s mire.


**Lady Agravain, Scourge of Ships**


The source of much of the shipwrecks and current state of Deep Harbor, Lady Agravain has been rendered completely mad from her transformation. In a flurry of feathers and steel, this terrifying adversary dashes around her nest with breakneck speed, ripping unprepared Soulforged to shreds. Only those able to read her movements have a chance at keeping up.
