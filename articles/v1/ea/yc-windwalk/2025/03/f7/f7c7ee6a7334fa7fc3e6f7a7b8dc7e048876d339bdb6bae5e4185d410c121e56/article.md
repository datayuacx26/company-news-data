# Early Access &#38; The Mutinous Fae Update!

The first major update for SWORN is now available in Early Access! We can't express enough how thankful we are to our community for joining us on this journey — helping us refine and shape SWORN into a game that we are immensely proud of. Find out more about our first update below!


###


The Mutinous Fae Update


There are still secrets to discover. Even after defeating Arthur, the corruption yet continues to grow. You will need help from a new ally if you are truly to free Camelot.


Embark on a new quest to discover the true nature of the corruption. Descend into even greater danger, overcome new challenges, and defeat powerful new foes. Recruit your friends, gather your strength, and steel yourself to defeat the sinister evil behind The Grail.


**A New Quest**


-


A new ally who appears in Carmarthen after defeating Arthur


-


A new challenge mechanic to unlock the final area


**A Secret World**


-


A new biome with 8 new combat chambers, a new shop, and a new Fountain Chamber


-


5 new minor foes and 1 new major foe


-


2 new traps


-


A new final boss


-


New cutscenes


-


New music

Even in its Early Access state, the game is highly polished and built to feel like a complete base version, without major missing features or unfinished content. While we’re still working toward the full release, what’s available now is a substantial, fully playable version of the game.

Fully translated into English, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Spanish, Chinese (Traditional and Simplified)
