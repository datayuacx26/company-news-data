# Exploring the SWORN Hub – What is the SWORN Hub and Why Should You Join?

The SWORN Hub is a dedicated platform for all things SWORN, designed to enrich your gaming experience and reward you for being part of our community. Let's delve into the SWORN Hub’s core features, functionality, and rewards!


###


How Do I Join the SWORN Hub?


Joining the SWORN Game Hub is a straightforward process. Simply head to


[hub.sworngame.com](https://hub.sworngame.com/onboarding/login) , complete the registration, verify your email, and you're in!


###


What Rewards and Benefits Can I Expect from the SWORN Hub?


Prepare for a treasure trove of rewards within the SWORN Hub. Unlock free in-game items, earned through the completion of


social quests , and gain access to our exclusive giveaways. You can also claim your access key to SWORN playtests when these are available!


###


Which In-Game Items Can I Unlock?


Unlock an array of tiered weapons, each offering a distinct advantage to aid you in your gameplay. Accumulate


Crowns (One of the Hub’s point types) to unlock various tiered gear such as the Sword, Orb, Greathammer, and Mace. Enough crowns will even give you the chance to unlock legendary tiered gear. Choose your arsenal wisely and dominate the battlefield!


###


How Does the Hub’s Points System Work? Crowns and XP?


The SWORN Hub operates on a dual points system: 'Crowns' and 'XP'. Engaging in social quests will reward you with both Crowns and XP, while referring friends to the SWORN Hub will earn you additional XP.


Crowns serve as your key to unlocking free in-game items, while XP will help you to ascend the SWORN Hub challenge leaderboard. Each XP earned translates into an entry in our current live giveaway. The more XP you accumulate, the greater your chances of winning the giveaway.


###


What Are Social Quests?


Social quests are tasks on the Hub you can complete to earn


Crowns and XP .


Taking part is easy, within the hub you’ll be presented with a list of social actions which include joining our Discord community, following us on social media, referring a friend and more. Simply complete these actions and be rewarded with Crowns and XP. Being part of our community also means you won’t miss out on any game news or events!
