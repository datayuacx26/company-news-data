# Games made with Unity: July 2026 in review

***July was a big month for Unity developers. Thousands joined us in Korea for Unite Seoul, where we[announced Unity 7](https://unity.com/blog/unite-seoul-keynote-2026-recap) – a direct continuation of Unity 6, no breaking changes. Highlights on the road to Unity 7 include a default Fast Enter Play Mode and Surface Cache GI, which brings real-time global illumination to the Universal Render Pipeline (URP) with no baking required. We also put together a reel of Korean MWU games for the keynote.***


***Check it out below and then explore the latest games launched in July 2026, all made with Unity. Explore the full list in this month’s recap.***
