# Made In 90s Festival Returns Bigger Than Ever for 2026

# Made In 90s Festival Returns Bigger Than Ever for 2026


Made In 90s Festival is back for its[5th anniversary](https://shoobs.com/events/116885/made-in-90s-festival-26-mjr-ft-r-b-icons-xscape-lil-mo) , and 2026 is shaping up to be its biggest edition yet. On **Saturday 8 August** , up to **7,500 people** are set to take over Addington Park in Croydon for a full day of 90s and 00s R&B, Hip Hop, Garage, Dancehall, Afrobeats and more.


Leading the main stage are multi-platinum R&B icons **Xscape** , joined by hitmaker **Lil’ Mo** , with live performances from **Angel** and **Teanna Bianca** . The lineup also features a strong selection of UK DJs including DJ Silk, DJ Ace, Big Business, Gal Flex and more.


There’s also a dedicated **Back In The Days** second stage, bringing together House, Garage and Jungle sounds from names including Supa D, Pioneer, ColdSteps, Ill Blu and Scotti Dee.


Running from **1pm–10pm** , this is set to be a[major summer date](https://shoobs.com/events/116885/made-in-90s-festival-26-mjr-ft-r-b-icons-xscape-lil-mo) for anyone who loves classic R&B, throwback anthems and feel-good festival energy. Tickets are available now[here](https://shoobs.com/events/116885/made-in-90s-festival-26-mjr-ft-r-b-icons-xscape-lil-mo) .
