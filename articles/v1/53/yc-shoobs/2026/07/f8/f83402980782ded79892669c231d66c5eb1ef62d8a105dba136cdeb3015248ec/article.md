At Shoobs, we’re proud to be a female-founded organisation that loves celebrating women who break the mould and drive the events scene forward.


This International Women’s Day, we’re shining a spotlight on a few incredible female organisers you won’t want to miss! These innovative and empowering women are changing the game in their respective industries, and we're excited to share their achievements and the phenomenal events they’re bringing to life.

**ECKOES** is on a mission to take you into another world with her unique blend of afro-electronica!


Known for her electrifying performances at Glastonbury, All Points East, and more, **ECKOES** is bringing her otherworldly sound straight to the stage. Her debut album, mixing Björk with Burna Boy, has earned critical acclaim from tastemakers like BBC Radio 1, NME, and The Independent.


Expect a memorable night as ECKOES takes you on an emotional journey with her soulful vocals and captivating beats.

With a deep love for sound system culture and jazz, **ANIMAI** is making waves with her raw, emotive style. Her collaborations with artists like Flowdan and Youngsta have made her a standout talent, and her tracks have been championed by BBC 1Xtra and Clash.


Catch **ECKOES** live at 9:15 pm and **ANIMAI** at 8:15 pm. Get ready for a night of incredible music from two women who are changing the game!
