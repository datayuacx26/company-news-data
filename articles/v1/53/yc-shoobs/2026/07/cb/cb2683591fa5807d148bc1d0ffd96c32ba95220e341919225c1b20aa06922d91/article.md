# How Sweet the Sound


A night of live music and heavenly vibes… yes please!


Don’t miss the European debut of[How Sweet the Sound](https://shoobs.com/events/95419/how-sweet-the-sound?utm_medium=blog%20&utm_source=shoobs%20&utm_campaign=95419) , live at London’s Royal Albert Hall on Sunday 20th October! Expect a musical competition of breathtaking skill, with gospel artists from across Europe.


A night at the[Royal Albert Hall](https://shoobs.com/events/95419/how-sweet-the-sound?utm_medium=blog%20&utm_source=shoobs%20&utm_campaign=95419) is already considered a luxurious treat – now imagine stepping out of the October chill into the warmth of plush seats, lavish décor, and gorgeous music!


Enjoy choirs big and small raising their voices in song, from the Leeds Conservatoire Contemporary Pop Choir to Italy’s Sunshine Gospel Choir!


Vocalists aren’t the only talents in play, with the likes of Salient Voice and Kems The Poet contributing to the[Spoken Word Artists](https://shoobs.com/events/95419/how-sweet-the-sound?utm_medium=blog%20&utm_source=shoobs%20&utm_campaign=95419) category.


American Grammy Award-winning star[Donald Lawrence](https://shoobs.com/events/95419/how-sweet-the-sound?utm_medium=blog%20&utm_source=shoobs%20&utm_campaign=95419) will be hosting the event, utilising his signature charisma and compelling voice.


With gospel heavyweights Karen Gibson, Bazil Meade, and Muyiwa Olarewaju judging the performances, you know the experts aren’t here to play around! Which artists do you think should[win?](https://shoobs.com/events/95419/how-sweet-the-sound?utm_medium=blog%20&utm_source=shoobs%20&utm_campaign=95419)


How Sweet the Sound is set to heat up October – don’t miss out! Book[here](https://shoobs.com/events/95419/how-sweet-the-sound?utm_medium=blog%20&utm_source=shoobs%20&utm_campaign=95419) ASAP.
