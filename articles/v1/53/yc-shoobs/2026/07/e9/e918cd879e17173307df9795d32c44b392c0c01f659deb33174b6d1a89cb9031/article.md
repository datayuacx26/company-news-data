# MRCY in Manchester and Leeds!


Prepare for an unforgettable night of soulful sounds and stirring performances as MRCY takes the stage in[Manchester](https://shoobs.com/events/101303/mrcy-in-manchester?utm_medium=blog&utm_source=shoobs%20&utm_campaign=101303) and[Leeds](https://shoobs.com/events/101325/mrcy-in-leeds?utm_medium=blog%20&utm_source=shoobs%20&utm_campaign=101325) this November.


MRCY is the collaborative project of producer Barney Lister and vocalist Kojo Degraft-Johnson, two[talented artists](https://shoobs.com/events/101325/mrcy-in-leeds?utm_medium=blog%20&utm_source=shoobs%20&utm_campaign=101325) from opposite corners of England who have joined forces to craft a sound that bridges cultures and generations.


Their music combines the timeless warmth of Marvin Gaye with the contemporary cool of Anderson .Paak, creating a soulful landscape that feels both nostalgic and refreshingly new.


[MRCY’s](https://shoobs.com/events/101303/mrcy-in-manchester?utm_medium=blog&utm_source=shoobs%20&utm_campaign=101303) debut project, VOLUME 1, is an eight-track collection that reimagines the essence of Soul, blending heartfelt lyrics with masterful production. *“It’s like hearing a distant memory,”* says Barney, *“but one that speaks to where you are now.”*


This project has quickly earned them praise for its unique ability to resonate with listeners’ own journeys, making their[live performances](https://shoobs.com/events/101325/mrcy-in-leeds?utm_medium=blog%20&utm_source=shoobs%20&utm_campaign=101325) a must-see for anyone seeking music that touches the soul.


Catch MRCY live in[Manchester](https://shoobs.com/events/101303/mrcy-in-manchester?utm_medium=blog&utm_source=shoobs%20&utm_campaign=101303) on Tuesday, November 12th, with special guest Nectar Woode opening the evening. The following night, they’ll be in[Leeds](https://shoobs.com/events/101325/mrcy-in-leeds?utm_medium=blog%20&utm_source=shoobs%20&utm_campaign=101325) on Wednesday, November 13th, joined by Slow Cooked and Nectar Woode as support.


Each[performance](https://shoobs.com/events/101303/mrcy-in-manchester?utm_medium=blog&utm_source=shoobs%20&utm_campaign=101303) promises to be an intimate and immersive experience, so don’t miss the chance to witness this rising duo live and in their element.


[Tickets](https://shoobs.com/events/101325/mrcy-in-leeds?utm_medium=blog%20&utm_source=shoobs%20&utm_campaign=101325) are available now, secure yours and let MRCY take you on a soulful journey you won’t soon forget!
