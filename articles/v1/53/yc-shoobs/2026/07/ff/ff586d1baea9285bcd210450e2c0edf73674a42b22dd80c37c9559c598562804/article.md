# Fally Ipupa Live in London

##


## [Fally Ipupa Live in London](https://shoobs.com/events/84468/fally-ipupa-live-in-london?utm_medium=blog&utm_source=shoobs&utm_campaign=84468)


Fally Ipupa announces he’ll be returning to British shores to perform the biggest Francophone show that the UK has ever seen, at London’s The O2 on Sunday 25th October. Fally Ipupa has announced a further show on Saturday 24th October, with[tickets available here](https://shoobs.com/events/151332/fally-ipupa) .


Ipupa has taken the globe by storm with his exceptional talent and dynamic performances, fusing Ndombolo, Soukous, Rumba, and R&B in his music.


From his early days with Quartier Latin International to his successful solo career, he has garnered numerous awards, including Kora Awards, MTV Africa Music Awards, and BET Awards nominations.


Ipupa's electrifying stage presence, infectious energy, and soulful voice have solidified his position as a trailblazer in African music, inspiring millions and leaving an indelible mark on the industry.
