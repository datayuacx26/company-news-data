# Ollie's Partners with Cal Ripken Sr. Foundation 2026!

Ollie's is proud to partner with the Cal Ripken Sr. Foundation, August 30th through September 19th, 2026, to help transform the lives of over 1.4 million at-risk youth in underserved communities across the country each year. Now in its 25th year, the Foundation's mentoring programs, Youth Development Park fields, and STEM education initiatives have helped kids learn critical life skills while building positive connections with local law enforcement and youth mentors in a safe, fun, and healthy way. Stop by your local Ollie's today & round up your change at checkout to help transform communities in need and change kids' lives for the better!


To learn more about the Cal Ripken, Sr. Foundation, visit


[https://www.ripkenfoundation.org/](https://www.ripkenfoundation.org/)
