# Why Choose Merida to Live? A Look at the Heart of Yucatan

Living in Merida offers a combination that is hard to find: safety, quality services, beach access, and a city with its own cultural identity. It is no coincidence that more and more foreigners are choosing it as their new home.


## Why Is Merida Gaining Popularity?


The capital of Yucatan combines strong academic and healthcare offerings with consistent commercial growth. It is one of the safest cities in Mexico and continues to attract investment in new residential developments, improving employment opportunities and infrastructure.


## 5 Advantages of Living in Merida


Top-tier healthcare.


Merida has some of the best medical institutions in the southeast: Clinica de Merida, Star Medica, Centro Medico de las Americas, and Centro de Especialidades Medicas.


High educational standards.


From the Instituto Tecnologico de Merida to the Universidad Autonoma de Yucatan and Universidad Anahuac Mayab, the academic offering is solid at all levels.


Safe city.


According to the ENSU survey, 73.7% of Merida residents feel safe, a significantly higher percentage than in other Mexican cities.


Quality of life.


The UN has highlighted Merida in its Prosperous Cities Index as Mexico's city with the best quality of life. Its proximity to Gulf beaches adds something hard to beat.


Tourist destination.


Archaeological sites, cenotes, Progreso beach 30 minutes away, and unique gastronomy. You can have a staycation in your own city.


## Cost of Living


According to Expatistan data, the cost of living in Merida is approximately $23,600 MXN per month for one person and $50,000 MXN for a family of four. Compared to other cities in the country, it offers a very favorable equation between service quality and cost.
