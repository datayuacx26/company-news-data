# The 5 Best Cities in Mexico for a Peaceful Retirement

Mexico offers diverse alternatives for a quality retirement: colonial cities with cobblestone streets, safe environments, solid healthcare, and a cost of living that is accessible compared to the United States, Canada, or Europe.


## 1. Queretaro


Privileged location in the center of the country with excellent road connectivity. Its Historic Center and growing wine industry make it appealing. Combines quality urban services with access to nature and nearby small towns.


## 2. San Miguel de Allende


Art dominates its streets. Festivals, galleries, excellent restaurants, and easy access to nature. Designated a UNESCO World Heritage site in 2008 and recognized by the WTTC as a safe destination. A well-established international community of foreign residents.


## 3. Merida


The White City is friendly for those looking to retire peacefully. Forbes has considered it one of Mexico's best cities to live and invest in for its safety, cultural offerings, and tourist attractions. The World Bank ranks it fourth for business openness in the region.


## 4. Puebla


Strategic location and financial stability. Surrounded by Pueblos Magicos: Cuetzalan, Huauchinango, Zacatlan, and more. Cultural and gastronomic festivals throughout the year.


## 5. Puerto Vallarta


For those who prefer warmth, beach, and Pacific sunsets. Excellent connectivity with direct flights from US and Canadian cities. High-quality medical services with international specialists and a diverse international dining scene.
