# The Best Zones to Live in Merida: A Buyer's Guide

Merida is considered one of the most beautiful cities in Yucatan and the Mexican southeast. If you are looking to invest or settle here, these are the zones with the best living conditions and appreciation.


## Downtown Zone


The downtown area appeals to those seeking engagement in an economically vibrant setting. It concentrates tourism employment, medical facilities, financial institutions, and commerce. Properties typically feature colorful colonial architecture.


Garcia Gineres.


One of the most visited neighborhoods due to its location. Has the Universidad del Sur, Clinica de Merida, Parque de las Americas, and a wide range of shops and restaurants.


Santa Ana.


Wide real estate offering with supermarkets, hospitals, Parque Santa Ana, and Paseo Montejo nearby.


## North Zone


The north zone represents the strongest investment potential, with modern residential developments and notable appreciation. Beaches are only about 20 minutes away.


Temozon Norte.


Accessible via the Merida-Progreso highway. Has Universidad Marista, Christus Muguerza hospital, City Center, and banks.


Montecristo.


A quiet northern neighborhood with active security and connectivity to major avenues. Access to Chedraui Selecto, Sam's Club, banks, and parks.
