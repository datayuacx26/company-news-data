# Puerto Vallarta's Most Popular Neighborhoods to Live and Invest

Puerto Vallarta is one of the most important tourist destinations in Jalisco and all of Mexico. With over 6 million visitors annually, it offers a unique blend of Mexican culture, world-class beaches, and a well-established international community.


## Why Choose Puerto Vallarta


Climate.


Temperatures around 30°C year-round, ideal for those seeking sun-filled living or vacation.


Beaches.


Some of the best beaches in the world just minutes away. Playa Los Muertos is the most popular, with quieter alternatives for those who prefer them.


Cost of living.


More affordable than other top-tier tourist destinations. Competitive prices for services, food, and transportation.


Multicultural community.


A diverse community of foreign and Mexican residents, with services available in English and Spanish.


Outdoor activities.


Hiking, cycling, water sports, and natural parks in the surrounding areas.


## The Best Neighborhoods


Fluvial Vallarta.


Designed with full tourist infrastructure: restaurants, cinemas, and supermarkets. Housing with mountain or sea views at accessible prices.


5 de Diciembre.


Between the Hotel Zone and downtown. One of the most affordable neighborhoods to buy in. Many properties with bay views and modern designs.


Zona Romantica.


The cultural heart of Puerto Vallarta. Regional cuisine, boutique hotels, shops, and nightlife. Ideal for those who want to live at the center of it all, steps from the beach.
