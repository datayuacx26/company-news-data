9. **Experimentation with AI Pipelines**


Experimenting with different pipelines and model variations is crucial for optimizing performance.

10. **Open-Source Evaluation Frameworks**


Evaluating your LLM application is vital to ensure it meets the required standards and performs optimally.


- **[RAGAS](https://ragas.io/)** **:** Provides a suite of metrics for evaluating various aspects of RAG systems.


- **[DeepEval](https://github.com/confident-ai/deepeval)** **:** Facilitates unit testing of LLM outputs in Python, including regression testing and hyperparameter discovery.


- **[Guardrails AI](https://www.guardrailsai.com/)** **:** Ensures your AI models operate within specified parameters through a comprehensive validation framework.


####


11. **Monitoring and Evaluation Platforms**


Monitoring and debugging your LLM application in production is critical for maintaining its performance and reliability.


- **[Traceloop](https://www.traceloop.com/)** **:** An open-source monitoring platform built on OpenTelemetry, ideal for debugging complex AI applications.


- **[Langsmith](https://www.langchain.com/langsmith)** **:** Offers comprehensive monitoring, tracing, and debugging capabilities specifically for LLM applications.


- **[Langfuse](https://langfuse.com/)** **:** Another open-source platform, providing detailed traces for monitoring complex LLM applications.
