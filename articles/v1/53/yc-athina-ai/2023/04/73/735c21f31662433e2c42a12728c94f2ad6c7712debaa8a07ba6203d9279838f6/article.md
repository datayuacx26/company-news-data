# viz2viz: Prompt-driven stylized visualization generation using a diffusion model

Do not index


Original Paper


[https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.01919](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.01919)


Blog URL


**Original Paper:**[https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.01919](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.01919)


**By:**[Jiaqi Wu](https://arxiv.org/search/cs?searchtype=author&query=Wu%2C%20J) ,[John Joon Young Chung](https://arxiv.org/search/cs?searchtype=author&query=Chung%2C%20J%20J%20Y) ,[Eytan Adar](https://arxiv.org/search/cs?searchtype=author&query=Adar%2C%20E)


**Abstract:**


> Creating stylized visualization requires going beyond the limited, abstract, geometric marks produced by most tools. Rather, the designer builds stylized idioms where the marks are both transformed (e.g., photographs of candles instead of bars) and also synthesized into a 'scene' that pushes the boundaries of traditional visualizations. To support this, we introduce viz2viz, a system for transforming visualizations with a textual prompt to a stylized form. The system follows a high-level recipe that leverages various generative methods to produce new visualizations that retain the properties of the original dataset. While the base recipe is consistent across many visualization types, we demonstrate how it can be specifically adapted to the creation of different visualization types (bar charts, area charts, pie charts, and network visualizations). Our approach introduces techniques for using different prompts for different marks (i.e., each bar can be something completely different) while still retaining image "coherence." We conclude with an evaluation of the approach and discussion on extensions and limitations.

Revolutionizing Data Visualization with viz2viz: A Breakthrough for AI Engineers


The landscape of data visualization is undergoing rapid change, driven by the need for tools that can merge artistic flair with technical accuracy.


A standout innovation in this space is viz2viz, a system developed by researchers Jiaqi Wu, John Joon Young Chung, and Eytan Adar.


It leverages a diffusion model to convert text prompts into stylized visual representations, setting a new standard for data visualization creation and interpretation.


####


Challenges with Traditional Visualization Tools


While traditional data visualization tools are adept at presenting data clearly, they often lack the capability to create visualizations that are both visually engaging and informative.


The skills and effort required to design such detailed and stylized visualizations are significant barriers for many.


####


Introducing viz2viz


viz2viz addresses these challenges by automating the creation of stylized data visualizations from basic data representations.


Using advanced generative techniques, it transforms simple charts into visually appealing artworks without compromising the data's accuracy.


###


How viz2viz Works


viz2viz combines art and science in a three-step process to produce eye-catching visualizations:


####


A. Sketch Step


- The process begins with a sketch based on textual prompts, ensuring the visualization components are accurately and coherently generated.


####


B. Synthesis Step


- Components are then synthesized into a complete visualization. This step fine-tunes the visual elements to strike a balance between artistic style and data cohesion.


####


C. Refine Step


- Lastly, the visualization is refined, enhancing its resolution and detailing to ensure high quality and visual appeal.


####


D. Adaptability across Styles and Visualizations


- viz2viz excels in its versatility, accommodating various visualization types and styles, offering customized workflows.
