# ProCap Financial Partners with Ornn to Bring AI Compute Cost Data to Independent Investors

## ProCap Financial Partners with Ornn to Bring AI Compute Cost Data to Independent Investors


June 10, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.procapfinancial.com/node/7861/pdf)


- ProCap Insights will include Ornn’s compute data in its agentic investment research reports
- Partnership gives independent investors insights into one of the most critical costs driving the AI buildout


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jun. 10, 2026--[ProCap Financial, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.procapfinancial.com&esheet=54551004&newsitemid=20260610650733&lan=en-US&anchor=ProCap+Financial%2C+Inc.&index=1&md5=1d7b2c10e0259506713ab60c39084db1) (Nasdaq: BRR) (the “Company”), the first publicly traded agentic finance firm, today announced a partnership with[Ornn AI, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ornn.com%2F&esheet=54551004&newsitemid=20260610650733&lan=en-US&anchor=Ornn+AI%2C+Inc.&index=2&md5=f102bfc33165e2265d60df422b29c6a1) (“Ornn”), a market leading compute company building financial markets for AI. The partnership will give[ProCap Insights](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.procapinsights.com&esheet=54551004&newsitemid=20260610650733&lan=en-US&anchor=ProCap+Insights&index=3&md5=b5ceff4b897fe3ca223f2f7b877dd247) subscribers access to Ornn’s proprietary compute pricing data.


Compute has now become a foundational resource underpinning the AI economy and one of the largest costs behind the AI buildout. As demand for compute continues to accelerate, understanding its price, availability, and market dynamics is becoming increasingly important for investors. Through ProCap Insights, independent investors will gain access to unique data and analysis that help translate compute market trends into actionable investment insights.


“AI has quickly become the trade of the decade, but it is incredibly challenging for investors to determine what AI actually costs to run,” said **Phil Rosen, Chief Market Strategist at ProCap Financial** . “With Ornn’s data, our AI agents will be able to track the price of compute and develop research independent investors can use to make more informed investment decisions.”


“Compute sits at the center of the AI economy, yet most investors have limited visibility into the market forces driving its value,” said **Kush Bavaria, Co-Founder and CEO of Ornn** . “By partnering with ProCap Insights, we're making it easier for investors to incorporate compute market intelligence into their research and investment decision-making.”


To access or subscribe to ProCap Insights reports, please visit[www.procapinsights.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.procapinsights.com&esheet=54551004&newsitemid=20260610650733&lan=en-US&anchor=www.procapinsights.com&index=4&md5=9859d810732f8ddf63f7c30973fbbca0) .


To access Ornn’s Data platform for compute pricing insights, please visit[data.ornn.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fdata.ornn.com&esheet=54551004&newsitemid=20260610650733&lan=en-US&anchor=data.ornn.com&index=5&md5=77adb720c3b9a8774f2c670c6314bcee) .

Ornn's mission is to make AI infrastructure development more financeable.
