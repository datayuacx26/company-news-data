# Silvia Surpasses $50 Billion in Assets in 14 Months Since Public Launch

## Silvia Surpasses $50 Billion in Assets in 14 Months Since Public Launch


July 13, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.procapfinancial.com/node/7901/pdf)


- **Silvia has become the leading personal finance AI product**
- **Within two months of launching monetization, more than 10% of Silvia's monthly active users are paying members**
- **Power users have seen typical net worth gains of 16-41% in less than a year on the platform**


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jul. 13, 2026--[ProCap Financial, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.procapfinancial.com&esheet=54569191&newsitemid=20260713188584&lan=en-US&anchor=ProCap+Financial%2C+Inc.&index=1&md5=274b443d518a3da5b46c8a343604f09c) (Nasdaq: BRR), the first publicly traded agentic finance firm, today announced that[Silvia](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fcfosilvia.com&esheet=54569191&newsitemid=20260713188584&lan=en-US&anchor=Silvia&index=2&md5=0543523c915e6601e9abb00836838169) , its AI agent lab focused exclusively on finance, has surpassed $50 billion in assets on the platform. The milestone comes 14 months after Silvia’s public launch and reflects the desire from self-directed investors to use AI to manage their finances.


With Silvia's consumer product, investors can connect their stocks, bonds, crypto, real estate, cars, collectibles, precious metals, and private investments to see their entire portfolio in one place. Investors can then use Silvia’s proprietary AI agents to track their portfolio, receive personalized insights, run scenario planning, analyze documents, and more in real time.


**Silvia’s milestones:**


- More than $50 billion in total assets connected to the platform
- Average user net worth is more than $2.5 million
- Paying members chat with Silvia more than 20 times per week on average
- Within two months of launching monetization, more than 10% of Silvia's monthly active users are paying members
- Power users have seen typical net worth gains of 16-41% in less than a year on the platform


“I believe Silvia is the most underrated AI product on the market,” said **Anthony Pompliano, Chairman and CEO of ProCap Financial** . “We are solving a painful problem for self-directed investors and Silvia has become a major threat to the legacy financial players. People want to control their assets, make their own decisions, and they realize AI now makes it possible to grow their net worth faster than ever.”
