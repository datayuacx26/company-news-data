# ProCap Financial Launches ProCap Insights, the First Agentic Research Offering in Finance

## ProCap Financial Launches ProCap Insights, the First Agentic Research Offering in Finance


April 7, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.procapfinancial.com/node/7731/pdf)


- ProCap Insights is produced by AI agents, automating away the need for hundreds of analysts.
- ProCap Insights delivers high-quality research faster and cheaper than traditional financial services firms.


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Apr. 7, 2026--[ProCap Financial, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.procapfinancial.com&esheet=54481361&newsitemid=20260407302780&lan=en-US&anchor=ProCap+Financial%2C+Inc.&index=1&md5=287772c226ddbd4c4b0003666993bd33) (Nasdaq: BRR) (the “Company”), the first publicly traded agentic finance firm, today launched[ProCap Insights](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.procapinsights.com&esheet=54481361&newsitemid=20260407302780&lan=en-US&anchor=ProCap+Insights&index=2&md5=82a208b0729e16717115979f2a6c3c01) , the first agentic research offering in finance.


Leveraging the latest artificial intelligence technology, ProCap Insights delivers institutional-grade research to help independent investors make more informed investment decisions. Reports cover single-name stocks, thematic trends, and macro analysis across sectors and asset classes.


Research may also draw on trends derived from the proprietary and anonymized data captured by[Silvia](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.cfosilvia.com&esheet=54481361&newsitemid=20260407302780&lan=en-US&anchor=Silvia&index=3&md5=e770ca7f955534abdaba987256567742) , ProCap Financial’s free consumer product that tracks more than $30 billion in assets across thousands of users with an average net worth exceeding $2.5 million.


“Artificial intelligence is very good at finding hidden insights across financial markets, while also creating research cheaper and faster than ever before,” said **Anthony Pompliano, Chairman and CEO of ProCap Financial** . “While others are debating the impact of AI inside their firms, ProCap Insights is embracing this new technology to pioneer a better way of educating investors. We fundamentally believe the machines are smarter than humans and aspire to be a trusted resource for serious investors who want to make money.”


To lead this effort, **Phil Rosen** will join the Company as Chief Market Strategist. An award-winning financial journalist, Mr. Rosen is the co-founder of[Opening Bell Daily](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.openingbelldailynews.com%2Fauthors%2F379cbf86-5cdf-4990-a00b-ba9ca7bf54b5&esheet=54481361&newsitemid=20260407302780&lan=en-US&anchor=Opening+Bell+Daily&index=4&md5=4b8977bd011dd7c31a7c8fa371c16f4a) and the host of the investing show[Full Signal](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fplaylist%3Flist%3DPLLdFE3JnRtYHobfOT3niDCByX_sAFVki4&esheet=54481361&newsitemid=20260407302780&lan=en-US&anchor=Full+Signal&index=5&md5=f215133d9cc255a35e599112a0d7dbfd) .
