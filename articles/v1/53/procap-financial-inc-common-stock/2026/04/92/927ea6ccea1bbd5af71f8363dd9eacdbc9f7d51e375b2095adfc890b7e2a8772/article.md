# ProCap Financial Launches Agentic AI Podcast, Extending Agentic Research Offering to Audio

## ProCap Financial Launches Agentic AI Podcast, Extending Agentic Research Offering to Audio


April 15, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.procapfinancial.com/node/7741/pdf)


- New podcast created entirely by agentic AI surfaces investment insights traditional human research often misses
- Powered by ProCap Insights, the first agentic research offering in finance
- Available now on Spotify and Apple Podcasts


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Apr. 15, 2026--[ProCap Financial, Inc.](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.procapfinancial.com%2F&esheet=54511557&newsitemid=20260415149890&lan=en-US&anchor=ProCap+Financial%2C+Inc.&index=1&md5=10b9bbdeb070cb633c9a3e8813b492e4) (Nasdaq: BRR), the first publicly traded agentic finance firm, today announced the launch of **Best Stocks** , an agentic AI investment podcast powered by[ProCap Insights](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.procapinsights.com%2F&esheet=54511557&newsitemid=20260415149890&lan=en-US&anchor=ProCap+Insights&index=2&md5=dac73fa088699e8fa0e7d177a3e47252) , the first agentic AI research offering in finance.


ProCap Insights was built on a simple premise: AI agents can quickly dig through mountains of data and discover key insights often overlooked by humans. The Best Stocks podcast translates that research into an engaging audio format, delivering actionable investment intelligence to listeners.


“To create ProCap Insights we trained AI agents to research, write and publish at scale, and members of Congress, leading entrepreneurs and corporate executives are already seeing the impact,” said **Anthony Pompliano, Chairman and CEO of ProCap Financial** . “Now we are taking it to the next level and for the first time are delivering Wall Street-caliber investment research in an agentic audio format.”


Best Stocks is available now on[Spotify](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fopen.spotify.com%2Fshow%2F1tkAxdzyxpjgCu7UFcilGv&esheet=54511557&newsitemid=20260415149890&lan=en-US&anchor=Spotify&index=3&md5=2ae21ce4dbed7aa6689a2d01071d494b) and[Apple Podcasts](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fpodcasts.apple.com%2Fus%2Fpodcast%2Fbest-stocks%2Fid1892893784&esheet=54511557&newsitemid=20260415149890&lan=en-US&anchor=Apple+Podcasts&index=4&md5=3664f5a2f4a0a0db95c6a3fb1b4c3e31) .
