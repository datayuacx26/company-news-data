# Presidio Production Company to Join S&P Total Market Index and S&P Completion Index

# Presidio Production Company to Join S&P Total Market Index and S&P Completion Index


June 18, 2026 4:01pm EDT


*Inclusion Expected to Expand Presidio's Shareholder Base and Enhance Trading Liquidity*


FORT WORTH, Texas--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Presidio Production Company (NYSE: FTW) (“Presidio” or the “Company”), a differentiated oil and gas operator focused on the acquisition and optimization of mature, producing oil and natural gas assets in the United States, today announced its addition to the S&P Total Market Index and the S&P Completion Index, effective on June 22, 2026.


Will Ulrich, Chairman and co-CEO of Presidio, commented:


“Being added to the S&P Total Market Index and S&P Completion Index less than four months after our NYSE listing is a meaningful milestone for Presidio and our shareholders. These indices are benchmarks for a broad range of passive investment vehicles, and our inclusion reflects the market's recognition of Presidio's financial strength and operating performance. We remain focused on what got us here: disciplined PDP operations, a compelling dividend, and the AI-driven efficiencies of our Asset Intelligence Platform. We look forward to engaging a broader investor base and remain focused on delivering for our shareholders.”


**About the S&P Total Market Index and S&P Completion Index** The S&P Total Market Index is designed to track the broad U.S. equity market, including large-, mid-, small-, and micro-cap stocks. The S&P Completion Index covers the U.S. equity market excluding S&P 500 constituents, and together the two indices represent comprehensive exposure to the U.S. public equity universe. Both indices are widely used by institutional investors, index funds, and ETF providers as benchmarks and investment vehicles.


**About Presidio Production Company** Headquartered in Fort Worth, TX, Presidio Production Company (NYSE: FTW) is a yield-focused, differentiated oil and gas operator in the United States focused on the acquisition and optimization of producing oil and natural gas wells, without drilling. Presidio is a leading operator of stable oil and gas wells across the Mid-Continent, applying engineering expertise and AI-driven analytics to enhance performance and extend asset life. The Company's Class A common stock is listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker symbol “FTW”. To learn more, visit[https://bypresidio.com/](https://bypresidio.com/) .
