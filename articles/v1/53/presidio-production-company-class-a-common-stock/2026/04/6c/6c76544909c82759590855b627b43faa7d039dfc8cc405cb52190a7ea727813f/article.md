# Presidio Announces First Quarter 2026 Earnings Results Conference Call


April 27, 2026 7:30am EDT


FORT WORTH, Texas--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Presidio Production Company (NYSE: FTW) (“Presidio” or the “Company”), a yield-focused, differentiated oil and gas operator in the United States focused on the acquisition and optimization of producing oil and natural gas wells, without drilling, today announced that it will report first quarter 2026 results on Friday, May 15th, 2026. The Company will host an earnings conference call that morning at 11:00 AM ET / 10:00 AM CT to discuss financial results and business highlights.
