# Super Hi Announces Annual General Meeting on June 12, 2026 and Filing of Its Annual Report on Form 20-F

## Super Hi Announces Annual General Meeting on June 12, 2026 and Filing of Its Annual Report on Form 20-F


April 27, 2026


[PDF Version](https://ir.superhiinternational.com/node/7581/pdf)


SINGAPORE, April 27, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Super Hi International Holding Ltd. (NASDAQ: HDL and HKEX: 9658) (“Super Hi” or the “Company”), a leading Chinese cuisine restaurant brand operating Haidilao hot pot restaurants in the international market, today announced that it will hold an annual general meeting of the Company's shareholders (the "AGM") virtually at 11 a.m. on June 12, 2026, Hong Kong Time (at 11 p.m. on June 11, 2026, U.S. Eastern Time), for the purposes of considering and, if thought fit, passing each of the proposed resolutions set forth in the AGM notice. The AGM notice, the AGM circular, and the form of proxy for the AGM are available on the Company's website at[https://ir.superhiinternational.com](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=Zg7VaT9RFPATSJo0ILOh58Zj1w1dp5NvFkXbwWCdNzAHVP1MDDy987gH2pn99uOBpPz1Vhi5GJOKK-hCOeJB19Emmyc6FMaCvq11wvqE4GYjzC1ZNlc_rQpETCe5jG2XaNHO1cY9L755sWJvMHySkDBWEqmDDTltwrCk-aCRdnGWmJntLR1NcIJrm5Z9wn4ENj56xXSMvgPgGg05rqwNHXCrd6ASsavdnyg4NYt-jHZx9mZ9YZb5g0ndfA0gBhn5y_XEp9yVeam0X06gVWtlzWT3HwpU2wj21Os_Uadq561lf8pcdT993P21ofP-ArwKJu2MYcKu-oT1KZBL-uhlQf0Yy5xzqaQbXazyijK9iWo=) .


Holders of record of the Company's ordinary shares on the Company's register of members as of the close of business on April 27, 2026 (Hong Kong Time) are entitled to attend and vote at the AGM. Holders of the Company's American depositary shares (the "ADSs") as of the close of business on April 27, 2026 (U.S. Eastern Time), who wish to exercise their voting rights for the underlying ordinary shares, must submit their voting instructions to Citibank, N.A., the depositary of the ADSs, and are not permitted to attend or vote in person at the AGM.

**About Super Hi**
Super Hi operates Haidilao hot pot restaurants in the international market. Haidilao is a leading Chinese cuisine restaurant brand. With roots in Sichuan from 1994, Haidilao has become one of the most popular and largest Chinese cuisine brands in the world. With over 32 years of brand history, Haidilao is well-loved by guests for its unique dining experience — warm and attentive service, great ambiance and delicious food, standing out among global restaurant chains, which has made Haidilao restaurants into a worldwide cultural phenomenon. Haidilao has been ranked as one of the “world’s most valuable restaurant brands” for seven consecutive years since 2019, earning the title of *“World’s Strongest Restaurant Brand”* for 2024 (Brand Finance). As of December 31, 2025, Super Hi had 126 self-operated Haidilao restaurants in 14 countries across four continents.
