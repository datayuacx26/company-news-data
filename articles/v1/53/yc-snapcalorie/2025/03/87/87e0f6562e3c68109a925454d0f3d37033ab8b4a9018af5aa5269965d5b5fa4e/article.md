## What Happened to MyFitnessPal’s Free Barcode Scanner?


The barcode scanner has long been one of MyFitnessPal's most popular features. It allowed users to simply scan the barcode of packaged foods to instantly input nutritional information, saving time and minimizing manual entry errors.


However, as of late 2022, MyFitnessPal announced that the barcode scanner would become a feature exclusive to its premium plans, priced at $19.99 per month or $79.99 annually. This change left free-tier users scrambling to either upgrade or manually search for food items in the database.


### The Reason Behind This Change


While MyFitnessPal hasn’t provided a detailed explanation, the move aligns with broader trends in the app industry, where companies increasingly push free users toward premium plans to bolster revenue. By restricting popular features like the barcode scanner, MyFitnessPal incentivizes users to pay for upgraded services.


While this decision makes sense from a business standpoint, it alienates users who relied heavily on free access for managing their health. For many, this cost barrier has sparked a search for alternatives that are both effective and budget-friendly.
