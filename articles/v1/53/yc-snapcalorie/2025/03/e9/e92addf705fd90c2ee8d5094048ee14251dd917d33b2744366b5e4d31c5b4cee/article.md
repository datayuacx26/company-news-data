## MyFitnessPal Free Version vs Premium: Key Features


Before we dive into comparisons, it’s important to understand what exactly you get with **MyFitnessPal Free** versus **Premium** .


### **Features of MyFitnessPal Free Version**


The free version of MyFitnessPal includes:


- **Basic Food Logging** : Input your meals manually or scan barcodes to track calorie intake.
- **Exercise Tracking** : Log workouts to estimate calories burned.
- **Weight Management Goals** : Set daily calorie and macronutrient targets.
- **Community Forums** : Connect with other users for tips and motivation.


While the free version provides good functionality for beginners, it has limitations when it comes to advanced tracking and data insights.


### **Features of MyFitnessPal Premium**


Upgrading to MyFitnessPal Premium unlocks:


- **Detailed Macronutrient Breakdown** : View a complete analysis of carbs, protein, and fat by meal and day.
- **Custom Metrics** : Track additional nutrients like fiber, sugar, and sodium.
- **Meal Plans** : Get personalized meal suggestions tailored to your nutritional goals.
- **Ad-Free Experience** : Say goodbye to banner ads.
- **Advanced Reporting** : Access historical trends and deeper insights into your progress.


The current cost of MyFitnessPal Premium is around **$19.99/month or $79.99/year** , which can be a significant investment for many users.
