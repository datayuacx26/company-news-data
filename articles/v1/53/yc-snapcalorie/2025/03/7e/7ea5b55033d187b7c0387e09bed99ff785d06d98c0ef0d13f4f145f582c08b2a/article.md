### 3. **Learn to Estimate Calorie Counts**


You don’t necessarily need tools or apps to get a ballpark idea of your calories. For example:


- **Protein (meat, fish, or tofu):** ~150-200 calories per 3 oz.
- **Vegetables:** ~25-50 calories per cup, lower for greens like spinach.
- **Grains (rice, bread, pasta):** ~100-120 calories per serving (1 slice, 1/2 cup).
- **Fats (oil, butter):** ~120 calories per tablespoon.


This approach requires learning portion sizes and practicing mindful eating. It’s less precise, but free and straightforward for people with basic calorie goals.
