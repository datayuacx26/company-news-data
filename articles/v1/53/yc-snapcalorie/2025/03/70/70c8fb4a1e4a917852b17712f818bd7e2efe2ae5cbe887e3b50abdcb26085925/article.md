## Why Barcode Scanners Matter in a Calorie Tracker


For years, people have relied on calorie tracking apps to monitor their food intake. Barcode scanners became a favorite feature in these apps because they eliminated the hassle of manually searching for food items in a database. By simply scanning a product's barcode, users can instantly add its calorie and nutritional information.


This feature has transformed food tracking apps like MyFitnessPal and Lose It, making them household names in the fitness world. However, even with barcode scanning, users still face a few challenges:


- **Inconsistent database entries:** Some barcodes lead to incomplete or incorrect nutrition information, requiring manual corrections.
- **Time-consuming process:** Scanning every individual item on a plate, especially homemade meals, can feel like a chore.
- **High subscription fees:** Many popular apps with barcode features require expensive paid subscriptions to unlock full functionality.
