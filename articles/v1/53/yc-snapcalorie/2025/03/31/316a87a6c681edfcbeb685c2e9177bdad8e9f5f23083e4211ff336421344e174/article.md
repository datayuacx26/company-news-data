## Why Track Calories on the Paleo Diet?


Many believe that because the Paleo diet eliminates processed foods, calorie counting isn’t necessary. However, even on Paleo, calorie tracking can be essential for achieving specific health and fitness goals like weight loss, muscle building, or maintaining energy levels. Here’s why:


- **Portion Control:** Calories count, even if you're eating nutrient-dense foods like avocados, nuts, or sweet potatoes. Overeating can still stall progress.
- **Macronutrient Balance:** The Paleo diet focuses heavily on proteins, healthy fats, and limited carbohydrates. Tracking helps you strike the right balance.
- **Awareness of Hidden Calories:** Foods like almond butter or grass-fed beef are Paleo-friendly but can still be calorie-heavy. Knowing your intake ensures you're staying within limits.
