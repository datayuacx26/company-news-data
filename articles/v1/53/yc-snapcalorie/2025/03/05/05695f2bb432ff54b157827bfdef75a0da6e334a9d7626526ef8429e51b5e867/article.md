### How Does AI Calculate Calories?


Artificial Intelligence (AI) leverages advanced algorithms to process vast amounts of data. When it comes to food, AI analyzes visual and textual inputs—like images of meals or ingredient lists—and matches them with extensive nutritional databases to generate calorie and macronutrient information.


Here’s how AI can calculate calories:


1. **Image Recognition:** AI systems use computer vision to identify foods and portion sizes from photos. For example, it can differentiate between a slice of pizza and a bowl of salad, and estimate their caloric content.
2. **Estimation Algorithms:** AI considers factors like portion size, density, and preparation method to provide calorie counts as accurately as possible.
3. **Database Integration:** The AI compares food items against a comprehensive nutritional database, connecting raw data with verified calorie and nutrient information.


While traditional calorie tracking methods rely on manual input of food items, AI-powered solutions—like **SnapCalorie** —are eliminating that effort through automation.
