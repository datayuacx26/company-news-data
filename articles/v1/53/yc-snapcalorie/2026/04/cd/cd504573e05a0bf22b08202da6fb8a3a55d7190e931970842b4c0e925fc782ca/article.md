# What Happened to Cal AI? Why It Was Removed from the App Store


If you recently tried to download Cal AI on iPhone and could not find it, you are not imagining things. Cal AI appears to have been removed from the iOS App Store, which has left a lot of users wondering what happened and whether the app is gone for good.


The short version is this: Cal AI was recently acquired by MyFitnessPal, and reports now suggest the app was removed from the App Store after a viral post drew attention to the way its in-app payment flow worked on iPhone.


That does not necessarily mean the app is gone forever. But it does mean iPhone users who were relying on it, or planning to try it, suddenly need another option.


## Why was Cal AI removed from the App Store?


Based on current reporting, the issue appears to be related to how Cal AI handled checkout on iPhone.


The reporting says a viral post highlighted a payment screen inside the app that looked native to iOS, but was actually tied to an external Stripe-based checkout flow. That kind of setup can become a problem if it conflicts with Apple’s rules around in-app purchases for digital goods and subscriptions.


Apple tends to be strict here. If an app’s payment flow feels too much like a built-in App Store purchase while routing around Apple’s billing system, it can trigger enforcement quickly.


At the time of writing, Cal AI appears unavailable for download on iPhone. The Android version still appears to be live.


## Wasn’t Cal AI acquired by MyFitnessPal?


Yes. MyFitnessPal announced earlier this year that it had acquired Cal AI.


That made the removal even more surprising, because Cal AI had become one of the most visible AI calorie apps in the market. It had built a large audience around a simple promise: take a picture of your food and get a fast calorie estimate.


The acquisition suggested that the app was not disappearing. But an acquisition does not protect an app from App Store policy issues.


## Can existing users still use Cal AI?


That depends on whether the app is already installed and how the service is being handled on the backend.


In general, when an app is removed from the App Store:


- new iPhone users usually cannot download it
- existing users may still be able to use the installed app for some period of time
- updates can become an issue if the app stays unavailable
- subscription management may still need to happen through Apple or the original billing flow, depending on how the purchase was made


If you already have Cal AI installed, the best move is to check whether it still opens normally, whether your account is intact, and whether your subscription appears in your Apple subscriptions settings if you bought through Apple.
