# Is Cal AI Still on the App Store? What iPhone Users Should Know


If you searched for Cal AI on your iPhone and could not find it, the short answer is: **it does not appear to be available on the iOS App Store right now** .


That has created obvious confusion, especially because Cal AI had become one of the most visible AI calorie-tracking apps and was recently acquired by MyFitnessPal.


So if you are wondering whether Cal AI is gone, whether it is just a temporary App Store issue, or what you should use instead, here is the practical answer.


## Is Cal AI still available for iPhone?


At the time of writing, Cal AI appears to be unavailable for download on iPhone through the App Store.


That means:


- new users likely cannot install it on iOS
- users searching the App Store may not see the app at all
- people looking for the direct listing may still run into an unavailable page


The Android version still appears to be available, but for iPhone users the current issue is straightforward: if you want to download Cal AI today, it does not appear to be there.


## Why is Cal AI not on the App Store?


Current reporting suggests the removal may be tied to scrutiny around the app’s payment flow.


A viral post drew attention to a checkout screen inside Cal AI that reportedly looked native to iOS while routing through an external payment flow. If that conflicted with Apple’s App Store rules around in-app purchases, that could explain why the app was taken down.


Apple has historically been strict about how digital subscriptions and purchases are handled inside iPhone apps, especially when a payment flow appears tightly integrated into the app experience.


## Is this permanent?


Not necessarily.


Apps sometimes disappear from the App Store temporarily and return later after policy issues, checkout flows, or review concerns are resolved. So the more accurate answer is not “Cal AI is permanently dead.” It is “Cal AI does not appear to be available for iPhone users right now.”


That distinction matters if you are deciding whether to wait or switch.


## What should existing Cal AI users do?


If you already have the app installed, you may still be able to use it for now. But there are a few obvious risks when an app is unavailable in the App Store:


- future updates may be delayed
- reinstalling could become a problem if you remove the app
- subscription and account management can get more confusing
- support and reliability can feel uncertain even if the app still opens


If you depend on an app daily, uncertainty itself becomes a product problem.
