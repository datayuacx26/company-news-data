## Notice of Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders


02/16/2026


Download (opens in new window)


[Access Shareholder Meeting](https://www.virtualshareholdermeeting.com/CURR2026)


Currenc Group Inc. will host its Extraordinary General Meeting of Shareholders on February 25, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Standard Time (EST) in a virtual format.


You may access the meeting on the day of the event by visiting:


**www.virtualshareholdermeeting.com/CURR2026**


The bank, broker, or other nominee that holds your Currenc Group Inc. ordinary shares will provide you with proxy materials, which will include a unique control number. You will need this unique control number to access the virtual meeting platform and to vote during the meeting.


Only shareholders of record as of January 15, 2026 (the “Record Date”) are entitled to attend and vote at the meeting.


Additional information regarding the meeting and the matters to be voted upon is available in the Company’s proxy statement and related meeting materials.
