# Senior Cost Accountant & Analyst – Valencia


#### **Position overview**


Looking for a highly organized and detailed-oriented Senior Cost Accountant & Analyst. The ideal candidate will have heavy experience in manufacturing.


#### **Duties/Responsibilities**


-


- Ensure accurate inventory valuation and reporting for all locations.
- Work closely with supply chain to reconcile inventory discrepancies.
- Calculate and maintain accurate product costs, considering both direct and indirect costs.
- Analyze, determine, and report gross margin fluctuations.
- Assist with monthly financial close process which includes preparing journal entries and balance sheet reconciliations related to inventory and COGS.
- Reconcile gross profits related to distribution sales.
- Prepare yearly Cost of Goods Sold budget and margins.
- Meet with Production Operation department heads monthly to review department’s P&L Actual vs. Budget.
- Assist with annual physical inventory count and inventory observation audit.
- Assist in managing, including coordinating with auditors and providing necessary documentation.
- Provide financial analysis and insights to support strategic decision making.
- Participate in special projects and initiatives to improve financial processes and controls.


#### **requirements and skills:**


-


- Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel.
- Knowledge of ERP systems.
- Excellent analytical skills with the ability to interpret complex financial data.
- Detail-oriented with a high degree of accuracy in work output
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills with the ability to collaborate across teams.
- Proactive mindset with a commitment to continuous improvement.


#### **exeperience and education:**


- A minimum of 5-7 years of experience in cost accounting within manufacturing is required.
- Public company and SOX experience.
- Standard cost accounting experience is a plus.
- Bachelor’s degree in accounting or finance


#### **travel expectations:**


- Travel from Valencia office to Ventura manufacturing plant 1-2x per week.


#### **Position Location**


Valencia or Ventura, California
