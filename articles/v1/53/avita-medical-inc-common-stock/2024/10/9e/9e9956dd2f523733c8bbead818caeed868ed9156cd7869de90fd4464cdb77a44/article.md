# Reimbursement, Economics and Value (REV) Manager – East (remote)


#### **Position summary:**


-


The role will be responsible for implementing and managing field-based reimbursement and health economics activities for AVITA Medical’s portfolio. This position will support AVITA’s sales teams and customers serving as an expert on all matters related to coverage, reimbursement, coding, and health economics for AVITA’s products in the U.S. market.


If you qualify and are interested in this position, please email your resume to HR@avitastaging.wpengine.com.


#### **duties and responsibilities:**


-


-


Act as the main point of contact for the sales team and customers regarding coverage, reimbursement, coding, and health economics related to AVITA’s product portfolio.


-


Deliver support and training on reimbursement and health economics to the AVITA sales team and key external partners (such as hospitals, clinicians, investigator sites, and professional societies) when needed.


-


Prepare and deliver presentations to internal and external stakeholders on health economics and reimbursement topics.


-


Foster strong customer relationships to improve reimbursement outcomes and build support for AVITA Medical among payer groups.


-


Establish connections with decision-makers in government, physician circles, hospitals, and third-party payer organizations.


-


Monitor trends in coding, coverage, and reimbursement, incorporating these insights and feedback into AVITA’s strategic business planning processes.


-


Follow all work/quality procedures to ensure quality system compliance and high-quality work.


-


Other duties/projects as assigned.
