# AI teammates for customer experience

## Introduction


Every top-notch customer experience team is racing to adopt AI to accelerate every step of how they operate. Yet rolling out AI solutions often feels slow, fragmented, and **hard** .


Today, we’re making generally available our solution for making it feel easy: **AI teammates for customer experience.**


## The Problem:


**Fragmented AI:** Today, companies adopt a chatbot for docs or help center, product teams build in-app onboarding copilots, support teams adopt a copilot for their ticketing platform, and ops teams integrate workflow automation tools.


This works, but often the solutions don’t talk to each other and don't connect to all your knowledge sources, customer data, or APIs. Every limitation becomes a feature request you’re waiting on.


The result: 95% of[organizations](https://www.mulesoft.com/lp/reports/connectivity-benchmark) cite integration as their #1 barrier to AI adoption.


**Manual work:** Support engineers spend 49% of their time on repetitive[questions](https://www.zendesk.com/cx-trends-report/) and 50% of their day switching between[tools](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/) to gather context. They dig through error logs, CRMs, and Slack threads before they can start solving the problem. Current AI solutions cover basic chat Q&A, but include no workflows or actions to drive resolution.


81% of CX[leaders](https://www.zendesk.com/cx-trends-report/) want to consolidate to a single system.


## Introducing AI teammates


Our solution to both problems: AI teammates that solve the day 0 needs out of the box, with a full platform that lets you customize them to your needs and integrate anywhere as you grow.


Inkeep AI teammates are AI agents that meet the bar for what you'd expect out of anybody on your customer experience team. They can query and update:


- customer-specific data from your CRM and billing systems
- your docs and help center knowledge
- your product and SaaS APIs.


All with standard protocols (MCPs, Skills) in a unified management surface with clear governance, testing, and auditability.


## What AI teammates do


AI teammates don’t work in isolation: they form a **closed loop** that gets smarter with every interaction.


### Help customers


End users get fast, accurate answers from your docs, tickets, and community, with **cited sources** they can trust. Our customers commonly see **strong support deflection** while maintaining the response quality users expect from a human.


> [Fingerprint](https://inkeep.com/case-studies/fingerprint) cut support tickets by **48%** while increasing user activation by **18%** .


### Assist internal teams


Support agents get a personal **Coworker** that gathers context across your CRM, billing, and any data source; then provides **ready-to-approve** suggestions. No more tab-switching between six different tools.


### Automate workflows


Turn closed support tickets into **knowledge base updates** . Route complex issues to the right specialist. Sync data across systems. Teammates execute real work, not just chat.


## A platform you can extend


Many agent platforms today are geared for developers via SDKs and APIs, or no-code builders with visual editors. This makes a hard separation between IT or engineering teams who can work quickly with code, and business teams who need to manage agents without touching code.


Inkeep uniquely solves this with a no-code visual canvas that's easy to use for anybody on the team, and a Developer SDK for developers. And it's not a one-way decision -- there's **full 2-way sync between code and no-code** .


### Platform primitives


The platform provides everything you need:


- A library of 10,000+ MCP connectors and the ability to bring your own to connect to internal or your SaaS APIs
- Support for Skills to standardize operating procedures for agents.
- OTEL telemetry, traces UX, and built-in **Edit with AI** functionality


## Built for technical teams


Consumer support often hits higher deflection; technical B2B sits lower because docs are denser, knowledge is scattered across GitHub, Confluence, and Slack, and every company has unique workflows generic AI can’t cover out of the box.


Inkeep is built for that reality:


- **50+ content connectors** : Docs, GitHub, Confluence, Notion, Slack, Discord, Zendesk, and custom sources.
- **Customer intelligence** : Query account-specific data, not only static documentation.
- **Agentic workflows** : Execute actions, not only answers.
- **Platform extensibility** : Build custom tools when defaults aren’t enough.


## A Closed-loop system


We engineered Inkeep to not just answer customer questions, but to also prevent the next one.


Here's the workflow:


1. **Customer assistant** handles the question.
2. **Coworker** helps agents resolve escalations faster.
3. **Content writer** turns the resolution into a KB article.
4. **Auto-reply agent** deflects similar questions next time.
