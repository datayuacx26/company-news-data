# Unlocking Documents at Scale: Pulse Opens Its Platform

### **From Enterprise Workhorse to Public Platform**


Over the last year Pulse has powered document pipelines for Fortune 100 enterprises, global banks, private-equity firms, and fast-growing AI startups. In that time the platform has processed more than 600 million pages, including complex tables, dense charts, handwritten notes, and everything in between.


Sign up, upload a file, and receive structured, LLM-ready data in seconds.


[Try the Pulse Platform NOW](https://platform.runpulse.com/)


### **How Pulse Reads the Tough Information**


- **Structural vision pipeline** Detects tables, charts, stamps, and handwriting before extraction begins, preserving layout fidelity.


- **Adaptive reasoning layer** Applies large-model reasoning only when layout cues are ambiguous, keeping latency predictable.


- **Horizontal orchestration** Streams thousands of pages through parallel workers so real-time and batch jobs finish on schedule.


- **Schema guardrails** Validates field types and units before output leaves the pipe, eliminating manual cleanup.


### **Impact in Production**


- A Fortune 100 enterprise reduced contract review time by 90 percent.


- A global bank feeds Pulse outputs directly into credit-risk models, removing re-keying steps.


- An international healthcare network extracts lab results from scanned forms, accelerating patient turnaround times.


Across sectors customers are seeing up to 40 percent higher retrieval accuracy on downstream search and analytics workloads, without adding headcount.
