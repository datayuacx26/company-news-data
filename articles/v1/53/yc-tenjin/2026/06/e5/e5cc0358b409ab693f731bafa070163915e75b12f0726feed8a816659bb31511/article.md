## What You Should Know About Tenjin


Tenjin is a[mobile measurement partner](https://tenjin.com/glossary/mobile-measurement-partner-mmp/) (MMP) built for gaming studios and app teams that want precise attribution, clean data, and pricing that doesn’t punish growth.


Most marketing analytics tools are built for enterprise first and then scaled down. Tenjin was built the other way around: start for free, scale up when it makes sense, and never pay for features you don’t use.


## Who Uses Tenjin (In Real Life)


Most teams use Tenjin in one of two ways:


- Solo, small, or early-stage studios: signing-up for the All-Inclusive Free plan to start accurate attribution from day one without committing too much budget.


- Scaling and mid-sized teams: all-inclusive plan, running full campaign measurement, raw data access, and LTV reporting across multiple ad networks and mediators.


- Successful global studios: enterprise plans, running full campaigns measurement across different geos, granular data access, server-to-server integration and data pipelines, custom features, predictive modeling like pLTV.


## How Tenjin Pricing is Structured


The model is simple: you pay based on conversions, not arbitrary seat sounds or feature tiers.


- **All-Inclusive Free:** 2,000 conversions/month complimentary. Additional conversions are $0.04 each.
- **All-Inclusive S-M-L:** Starts at $200/month for 10,000 conversions/month. Save $400 on annual contracts. Additional conversions at ≤$0.03 each.
- **Enterprise:** Custom pricing, lowest cost per conversion, fully tailored to your team’s scale and needs.

## Key Factors That Influence Which Plan You Need


Tenjin pricing looks simple on the surface, that’s because it is. But if you’re still not sure about which plan would work best for you and your team, it all comes down to a few honest questions.


### How Many Conversions Are You Tracking?


This is the primary driver. Run the numbers:


- Under 2,000/month? The free plan is an excellent option.
- Between 2,000–10,000/month? Free works, but the All-Inclusive plan starts to make financial sense once your additional conversion costs approach $200/month.
- Above 10,000/month and growing? Choose between the All-Inclusive or Enterprise.


### Do You Need Hands-On Support?


Email and IM support is included on every plan. But if your team needs a dedicated Slack channel, a Customer Success Manager, UA consulting, or help with data migration and BI integration, then that becomes Enterprise territory.


### Do You Have Complex Data Infrastructure?


Raw data access, API access, and the full prediction suite are available on every plan. But if you need ETL pipelines, custom data science support, or deep BI integration, Enterprise gives you a team that can actually help build that alongside you.


### Are You in a Regulated or Compliance-Heavy Environment?


Good news: GDPR, CCPA, SOC 2, ISO 27001, and iOS 14+/Android 12 compliance are included on every plan. You don’t need to upgrade to stay compliant.


## How to Choose the Right Tenjin Plan


### **For Indie Studios and Small Teams**


Start on the free plan. There’s no reason not to. You get real attribution, accurate data, and full product access from day one, and there’s no credit card required. Plus, you’re able to upgrade at any time.


Just before your additional conversion costs start approaching the All-Inclusive S price point is the time to upgrade. The math makes it obvious.


### For Scaling Studios and Mid-Size Teams


The All-Inclusive Plan is really built for you. You get a lower cost per conversion, full product access, and the flexibility to cancel or upgrade anytime. The annual commitment saves at least $400/year or up to $1,400/year and your cost per conversion decreases as your volume grows.


### **For Large Teams and Complex Setups**


Enterprise is worth the conversation. Custom pricing, dedicated support, consulting, and data infrastructure help are necessary for teams where attribution is a serious operational function, deep business intelligence, and more power than dashboard usage.
