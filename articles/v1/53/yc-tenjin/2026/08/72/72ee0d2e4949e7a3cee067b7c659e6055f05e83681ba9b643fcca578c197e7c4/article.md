# iOS Subscription Revenue Reporting Now Included in Every Tenjin All-Inclusive Plan

*Tenjin’s native iOS Subscription Revenue Report connects trials, subscriptions, renewals, cancellations, and revenue to the campaigns that acquired each user. RevenueCat and Adapty integrations are also supported, bringing their subscription events into the same acquisition reporting environment.*


We’ve just released the **open beta of**[Subscription Revenue Reporting](https://tenjin.com/blog/tenjin-subscription-reporting-campaign-level-visibility/) **for iOS.** It gives subscription app teams a dedicated Tenjin report for measuring the full value of their acquisition campaigns, from new trials and paid subscriptions to renewals, cancellations, and revenue.


[Subscription performance](https://tenjin.com/subscription-reporting/) sits alongside attribution, spend, and user acquisition data in Tenjin. RevenueCat and Adapty customers can bring subscription events into this view without relying on separate dashboards or manual exports.


This feature is part of all Tenjin plans, including our all-inclusive free plan, with no separate add-ons or premium tiers.


## **A Dedicated View of Subscription Performance**


We built this report for how subscription businesses actually operate. Instead of treating subscription payments as a single purchase event, it gives you visibility across the customer lifecycle from a user’s first trial through renewal or cancellation.


Because it lives inside your existing Tenjin reporting, subscription metrics are available throughout the same views you already use to evaluate spend. You can break them down by campaign, ad network, country, Product ID, and the other dimensions available in Tenjin reporting.


The report includes:


- New Trials
- New Subscriptions
- New Subscription Revenue
- Renewals
- Renewal Revenue
- Cancellations
- All Subscriptions Revenue, combining new subscription and renewal revenue


Product ID is also available as a reporting dimension, so you can compare performance across individual subscription products, plans, or offers.


## **Make Campaign Decisions Based on Subscriber Value**


With Subscription Revenue Reporting, you can:


- Identify campaigns that drive high-quality subscribers, not only installs
- Compare trial conversion and renewal performance across acquisition sources
- Understand which products and offers generate the most subscription revenue
- Spot cancellation patterns associated with specific campaigns or channels
- Evaluate subscription LTV alongside acquisition cost and attribution data


The result is a more complete view of how your acquisition investment translates into recurring revenue.

## **Works Alongside RevenueCat and Adapty**


Tenjin’s native iOS Subscription Revenue Report gives you a dedicated view of subscription performance inside the same platform you use for attribution, spend, and campaign reporting.


[RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/integrations/attribution/tenjin) and[Adapty](https://adapty.io/docs/tenjin) remain supported integrations for teams that use those platforms to manage their subscription infrastructure. They can send subscription events into Tenjin, helping you connect subscription activity with the campaigns that acquired each user across your existing workflows.


These integrations complement Tenjin’s native report. You can keep using the subscription platform that fits your workflow while bringing lifecycle events, attribution, and acquisition data together in Tenjin.


## **Available Now for iOS**


Subscription Revenue Reporting is available now for iOS apps. To get started, configure your App Store Connect credentials in Tenjin and use the latest Tenjin SDK. You can also forward subscription events to supported ad networks through Subscription Revenue callbacks.


For setup instructions, see our[Subscription Revenue documentation](https://tenjin.com/docs/subscription-revenue/) . If you use RevenueCat or Adapty, you can also follow each platform’s respective third-party integration guide.


**Android support is up next as we continue expanding subscription reporting across platforms.**
