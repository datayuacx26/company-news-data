Change is the only constant in today’s dynamic business landscape, and as leaders, it is our responsibility to embrace, proactively initiate, and guide transformative initiatives.


In the[previous article](https://medium.com/p/e001e688d18d) , I introduced the 1111 model: 1 Product Owner, 1 Team, 1 Backlog and 1 Key Performance Indicator (KPI), and how we in[Payoneer](https://www.linkedin.com/company/payoneer/) revolutionized the way we work and elevated our team’s performance.


Implementing new models of operation across departments is not an easy task. It poses unique challenges that require a structured change management process. This process becomes important due to the complexity of such changes and the involvement of multiple departments.
Its purpose is to guide the transition from current practices to desired future ones and ensure that the changes are embraced and maintained within the organization.


In our case, we were lucky to have Ilona Semach, our Product Excellence Director, who was leading this change management process and implementing the 1111 methodology in[Payoneer](https://www.linkedin.com/company/payoneer/) . This process includes facilitating a cultural shift, engaging stakeholders, providing training, managing resistance, enabling incremental implementation, and establishing measurement processes for continuous improvement.

The process ensured a smooth transition and maximized the benefits of agility on several levels:


🔷Company
Alignment between team focus and company strategy with higher customer centricity.


🔷Teamwork
Tight alignment and partnership between R&D and Product (one Product Owner working with an engineering team) with much less cross-team dependencies.


🔷People
Organizations with good implementation of 1111 would have the lowest churn in the industry (as people are happy and the value of their work is clear).


In an era of rapid technological advancements and evolving customer needs, it is important that we stay ahead of the curve and continuously adapt our strategies to deliver exceptional products and services.


The 1111 Model represents a paradigm shift, ensuring a seamless integration of our processes, teams, and goals, ultimately propelling us towards greater success.
