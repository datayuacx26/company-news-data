# The Barcode Gets a Digital Upgrade. What’s Next for Your Business?

**


The familiar barcode, a staple of commerce for over 50 years, triggers the checkout beep we all know. It reliably tells a scanner a product's identity, its Global Trade Item Number (GTIN). This simple function served us well, but today’s connected world demands more. It’s time for an upgrade. A new standard,[GS1 Digital Link](https://www.zebra.com/us/en/resource-library/faq/what-is-GS1-digital-link.html) , breathes digital life into the humble barcode, preparing your business for what comes next in[retail](https://www.zebra.com/us/en/industry/retail.html) ,[logistics](https://www.zebra.com/us/en/industry/transportation-logistics.html) , and[manufacturing](https://www.zebra.com/us/en/industry/manufacturing.html) .


Imagine a single, 2D barcode on a product acting as a secure gateway to almost limitless online information. This next-generation code moves beyond product identification. It connects everyone in the supply chain, from the brand owner to the consumer, to a rich and dynamic data source.


## One Code, Powerful Results


This evolution simplifies the crowded label of today. A single 2D code becomes the source for everything, unlocking tangible results. A leading French grocery[retailer](https://www.zebra.com/us/en/about-zebra/newsroom/press-releases/2022/auchan-adopts-zebra-solution-to-save-35-million-products-from-food-waste-annually.html) , for example, worked with Zebra and its software partner Smartway to save an incredible 17,000 tons of food waste annually. Thanks to the implementation of its AI solution using Zebra mobile devices, the retailer saved 35 million products from going to waste in the first 12 months. Similarly, Australian retailer[Woolworths](https://www.gs1uk.org/insights/news/WoolworthsAustralia-reducing-waste-and-boosting-productivity-with-2D-barcodes) began a pilot with 2D barcodes on fresh meat and poultry, seeing the potential for a 40% reduction in food waste.


The information linked to the code lives online, meaning you can update it anytime without ever changing the printed barcode on the package. This keeps product details current and accurate, a critical advantage for managing recalls. Precise, item-level data helps businesses avoid the massive financial impact of broad product withdrawals, which can average[$10 million](https://cybra.com/gs1-sunrise-2027/) per incident. With our deep engagement in shaping these new best practices, we help make this transition seamless. We relentlessly pursue innovation that makes the complex simple, enabling enhanced supply chain traceability and unlocking new use cases directly at the point-of-sale.
