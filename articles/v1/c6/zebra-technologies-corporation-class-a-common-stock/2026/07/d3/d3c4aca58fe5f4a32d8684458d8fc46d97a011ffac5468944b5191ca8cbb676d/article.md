# Connected Frontline Solutions Optimize Food Production Workflows

**


Inside a bustling food and beverage facility, the smooth movement of materials dictates the pace of production. Any delay in getting raw ingredients to the line or moving finished goods to the warehouse creates a bottleneck.


These intralogistics challenges are magnified in a labor-constrained environment, where an inexperienced workforce may struggle with manual, point-to-point material moves, leading to errors and production delays. The associated costs of these inefficiencies accumulate quickly.


## The High Cost of Intralogistics Inefficiency


[CBRE Supply Chain Advisory](https://www.cbre.com/services/transform-business-outcomes/consulting/supply-chain-advisory) estimates that transportation and labor account for between[50% and 70%](https://www.cbre.com/insights/viewpoints/supply-chain-disruptions-create-new-opportunities-for-industrial-and-logistics-real-estate) of total supply chain costs. Errors, such as loading incorrect materials, lead to expensive rework. Reducing product[overfill](https://ijeri.org/IJERI-Archives/issues/spring2012/papers/IJERI%20spring%202012%20v4%20n1%20(paper%202).pdf) by just 1% on a high-volume line can save approximately $500,000 per year. These financial pressures drive facility leaders to seek more efficient ways to manage their operations and staff.


To overcome these hurdles, leading manufacturers focus on a strategy called the[Connected Frontline](https://www.zebra.com/us/en/blog/posts/2025/connected-frontline-technology-unlocks-team-potential.html) (CFL). This approach integrates sensing technologies and advanced software to empower workers. It optimizes the flow of goods with real-time data. Zebra serves as the world’s foundation for intelligent operations. We design hardware, software, and automation solutions for the frontline and maintain decades of leadership in data capture solutions.


## Elevating Performance with a Connected Frontline


A CFL strategy provides a base for[intelligent operations](https://www.zebra.com/us/en/cpn/intelligent-operations.html) by connecting workers, assets, and processes. Our vision sees frontline operations everywhere digitized, automated, and intelligent. Instead of relying on paper checklists, workflows shift to digital platforms. Imagine a low-stock alert from the Manufacturing Execution System. In a connected factory, this alert automatically triggers a task in an enterprise collaboration solution like[Zebra Workcloud Task Management](https://www.zebra.com/us/en/software/workcloud-solutions/workcloud-enterprise-collaboration-suite/workcloud-task-management.html) .


The system assigns the task to the most appropriate forklift operator based on location and availability. It sends instructions directly to a[mobile device](https://www.zebra.com/us/en/products/mobile-computers.html) . This provides real-time guidance to the worker for the correct material and drop-off point. Such digital methods optimize manual transport.


## Turning Real-Time Data into Targeted Action


Connected Frontline strategies also depend on gaining real-time[asset visibility](https://www.zebra.com/us/en/blog/posts/2025/unlocking-the-power-of-asset-visibility-for-frontline-operations.html) . By automating data capture, managers get an accurate, up-to-the-minute view of all materials and work-in-process (WIP). A comprehensive RFID solution automates the tracking of goods as they move through the facility.


This level of visibility enhances further with powerful communication and collaboration tools. A solution like[Zebra Workcloud Sync](https://www.zebra.com/us/en/software/workcloud-solutions/workcloud-enterprise-collaboration-suite/workcloud-sync.html) unifies the frontline with Push-to-Talk (PTT) voice capabilities and secure messaging on a single device. A line worker can instantly contact a supervisor about a quality issue. A manager can broadcast instructions to the entire forklift team. This seamless flow of information eliminates silos, accelerates problem-solving, and ensures the entire operation moves in unison.
