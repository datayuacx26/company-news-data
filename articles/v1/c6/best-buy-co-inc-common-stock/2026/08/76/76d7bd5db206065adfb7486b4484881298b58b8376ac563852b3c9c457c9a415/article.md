# Introducing the Best Buy 60th Anniversary Sneaker

By[Best Buy](https://corporate.bestbuy.com/)


August 10, 2026


For 60 years, Best Buy has been the place you go to discover what’s next in technology. When we started thinking about how to celebrate our anniversary, we figured we should do it the only way we know how: with technology.


Created in collaboration with[Nexbie](https://shoes.nexbie.com/?srsltid=AfmBOop0ksu6p8yxlc9YJj7ZA9z64bHrabPyphsE_SPj9SvGe_sF29hZ) , the sneakers are 3D printed as one seamless piece. **** There’s no stitching or glue, just an innovative lattice structure engineered for comfort, cushioning and breathability. It’s proof that with the right technology, even a wild idea (like a custom sneaker) can become reality.


Available in Best Buy’s iconic blue and yellow, the sneaker is a wearable celebration of the innovation, curiosity and bold ideas that have defined Best Buy for six decades. It’s a nod to 60 years of helping customers discover the latest technology, and proof that even after all this time, we still love showing our customers the endless possibilities of tech. Innovation can show up anywhere, even on your feet.
