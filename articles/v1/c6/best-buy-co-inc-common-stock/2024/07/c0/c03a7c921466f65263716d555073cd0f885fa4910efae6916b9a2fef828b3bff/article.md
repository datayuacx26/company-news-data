## **Copilot+ features**


### **Processing power**


Copilot+ PCs are powered by[Snapdragon X Elite and X Plus processors](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/windows/learn-about-copilot-in-windows/pcmcat1708112732293.c?id=pcmcat1708112732293&ref=212&loc=21295253691&gad_source=1&gclid=EAIaIQobChMI4euUkNnWhgMVIExHAR2A9w2KEAAYASAAEgLX__D_BwE&gclsrc=aw.ds) , which unlock new levels of personalization and speed through a turbocharged neural processing unit (NPU). With the new processors, users can expect significant enhancements to on-device AI performance, all-day battery life, as well as power efficiency and responsiveness. With 40+ TOPS, that’s a trillion operations per second, these Snapdragon processors are built to perform and handle tasks like never before.


### **AI power**


With the upgraded processing power and on-device AI experience, Copilot+ PCs deliver new AI experience at the click of a button, with features like:


- **Cocreator:** Enter a text prompt and images, and Cocreator can bring your vision and ideas to life with the help of AI. This feature can help bring out your creative side, whether that’s brainstorming a project, solidifying ideas, or creating a new art style.


- **Windows Studio Effects:** Prepare for a new school year with Windows Studio Effects. Enhancing your video and audio calls with features like Background Blur, Voice Focus, Portrait Light, Filters, and more, these tools utilize AI to elevate your communication experience. Windows Studio Effects change and expand the experience, whether you are attending virtual classes, collaborating on group projects, or making a presentation.


- **Language Translation:** Live Captions now have live translation for over 40 languages that can turn incoming audio that comes through your PC, whether web call or video streaming, into English subtitles. These translations happen in real-time across all applications, even when you’re offline. This effect offers easy-to-understand professor-assigned videos or clips, in-class materials, and more.
