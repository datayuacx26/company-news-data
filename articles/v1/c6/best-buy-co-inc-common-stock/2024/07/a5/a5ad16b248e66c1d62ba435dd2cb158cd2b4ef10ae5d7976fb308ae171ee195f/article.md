# Best Buy launches new way to shop its app through Google Play’s Collections

By[Best Buy](https://corporate.bestbuy.com/)


July 24, 2024


In the coming weeks, Android users will have an exciting new way to shop the[Best Buy® app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bestbuy.android&pcampaignid=web_share) with the rollout of Google Play’s Collections. We’re one of the first retailers to partner with Google on this new experience, which spotlights the most relevant content from your Android apps and brings it all into one easy-to-navigate feed.


When exploring Collections, you’ll have access to Best Buy’s latest deals, upcoming shopping events, reminders about hot products on[Best Buy Drops](https://corporate.bestbuy.com/best-buy-introduces-best-buy-drops/) ™ and more, personalized entirely to you. Scrolling through your Collections feed and see something that sparks your interest? Tap into the tile and you’ll seamlessly be brought directly into the Best Buy app where you can find more details and unlock a whole new world of discovery with some[awesome new features](https://corporate.bestbuy.com/best-buy-unlocks-personalized-discovery-and-shopping-with-new-app/) we’ve recently launched.


“This new way to engage with the Best Buy app through Collections perfectly demonstrates how we’re continuing to create personalized experiences for our customers,” said Brian Tilzer, chief data, analytics and technology officer at Best Buy. “We’re thrilled to be partnering with Google Play on the launch of Collections because it aligns with our goal to inspire our customers and help them discover how tech can bring what they love to the next level.”


## Best Buy features in Google Play’s Collections


Here’s some of what Android users can expect to see from the Best Buy app within Collections:


- Product and deal recommendations
- Upcoming shopping events
- Details on the next[Best Buy Drops](https://corporate.bestbuy.com/best-buy-introduces-best-buy-drops/) ™
- Our “Deal of the Day”
- Recently viewed products
- Items still left in your shopping cart
