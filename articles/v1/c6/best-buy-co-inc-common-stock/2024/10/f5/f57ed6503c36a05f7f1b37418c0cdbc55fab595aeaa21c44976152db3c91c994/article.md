It’s time to start thinking big. Really, really big. Because this fall and holiday season, Best Buy is *the* destination for XXL TVs. That’s right, extra *extra* large screen TVs—97 inches and larger—are now on display and ready for shoppers to experience in over 700 stores across the country.

**Standout features**


XXL TVs include incredible features that make all the difference when you’re creating the ultimate home viewing setup. Here’s a look at just a few of the features we’re eyeing.


- Never miss a big moment with Multi View watching. Multi View is built in on Samsung XXL TVs and accessible via streaming platforms for other brands. It allows you to split your XXL TV into four different screens showing four different things, so you never miss the big touchdown or the game winning play. When you use Multi View, your TV can split up into four, 48-inch screens, so you don’t have to worry about needing to squint to catch the big play.


- The[Samsung 98″ Class DU9000 Smart TV](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/samsung-98-class-du9000-series-crystal-uhd-smart-tizen-tv-2024/6578071.p?skuId=6578071) featuring Supersize Picture Enhancer uses AI to enhance the content you’re watching while removing noise and increasing sharpness for optimal viewing on the big(ger) screen.


- The built in a8 AI processor in the[LG 98″ Class 89 Series QNED Smart TV](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/lg-98-class-89-series-qned-4k-uhd-smart-webos-tv-2024/6578182.p?skuId=6578182) selects the ideal picture settings based on what you’re watching.
