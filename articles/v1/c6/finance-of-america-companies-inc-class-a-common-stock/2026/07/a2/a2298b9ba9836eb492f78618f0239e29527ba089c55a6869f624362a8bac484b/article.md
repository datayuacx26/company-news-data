# Finance of America Named to TIME’s America’s Best Companies 2026 List

### Finance of America Named to TIME’s America’s Best Companies 2026 List


July 9, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s205.q4cdn.com/103752380/files/doc_news/Finance-of-America-Named-to-TIMEs-Americas-Best-Companies-2026-List-2026.pdf)


*Recognition reflects Finance of America’s commitment to building a company that serves its employees, customers and shareholders.*


PLANO, Texas--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Finance of America Reverse LLC ("Finance of America" or the “Company”) (NYSE: FOA), the nation's leading provider of home equity-based retirement solutions, today announced it has been named on TIME’s list of America’s Best Companies 2026, recognizing organizations that demonstrate strong employee satisfaction, business performance and responsible corporate practices.


Presented by TIME in collaboration with Statista, the recognition evaluates companies using a broad range of measures, including employee feedback, financial performance and publicly available business information. Finance of America's inclusion reflects the Company's continued commitment to building a strong organization for its employees while delivering long-term value for the customers and communities it serves.


"We're honored to be recognized by TIME because this reflects something we've been working toward for years. We’ve focused on building a company where talented people can do meaningful work while helping older homeowners achieve greater financial confidence," said Kristen Sieffert, President of Finance of America. “This recognition is a reflection of our employees and the company they are helping build every day. We have been focused on creating a workplace where talented people feel supported, engaged and connected to a larger purpose: helping older homeowners achieve greater financial confidence in retirement. I’m proud of the progress we’ve made and even more excited about where we’re headed.”


The recognition comes as Finance of America continues to expand its retirement solutions platform and invest in helping older homeowners unlock the value of their home equity to achieve greater financial flexibility in retirement. The Company's success is built on the dedication of its employees and a shared commitment to providing thoughtful financial solutions that improve customers' retirement outcomes.


TIME's America's Best Companies 2026 list was announced on July 9, 2026. Additional information about the recognition and methodology can be viewed on[TIME.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Ftime.com%2Farticle%2F2026%2F07%2F09%2Famericas-best-companies-2026%2F&esheet=54568052&newsitemid=20260709414819&lan=en-US&anchor=TIME.com&index=1&md5=9a26a140380b7f816e73a9aea1ad814a) .
