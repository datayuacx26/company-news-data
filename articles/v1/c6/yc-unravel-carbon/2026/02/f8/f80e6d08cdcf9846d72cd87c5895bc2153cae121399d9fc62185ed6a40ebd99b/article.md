# Unravel Carbon and ACS Announce Strategic Partnership to Accelerate Climate Action Across Asia-Pacific

*Australia / Manila / Singapore – 2 February 2026* — **Unravel Carbon** , one of the world’s most advanced carbon management platforms, and **Adventure Consultancy Solutions (ACS)** today announced a strategic partnership that brings together world-class emissions technology with deep implementation, enablement and operational expertise to help organisations across Australia and the Philippines measure, manage and reduce their carbon footprint at scale.


‍


This collaboration unites Unravel Carbon’s enterprise-grade emissions management and decarbonisation platform and AI Agents with ACS’s proven track record in sustainability delivery, enabling businesses to transition from compliance-focused reporting to **practical, data-driven decarbonisation that achieves real operational and environmental outcomes** .


‍


“We see sustainability becoming a core operational capability, not just a reporting exercise,” said **Michael Musgrove, CEO of ACS** . “Partnering with **Unravel Carbon** allows us to help organisations act on carbon data quickly and at scale, without adding unnecessary complexity for our clients within APAC.”


### **Supporting Regulatory & Market Context**


Organisations in both Australia and the Philippines are facing **new mandatory climate disclosure requirements** that make robust carbon measurement and management tools essential:


‍


**Australia:** Australia has enacted a mandatory climate-related financial disclosure regime requiring large entities to prepare **sustainability reports aligned with the Australian Sustainability Reporting Standards (including AASB S2 Climate-related Disclosures)** as part of annual reporting obligations, beginning with financial years starting on or after **1 January 2025** . These standards embed rigorous climate-related disclosures on governance, strategy, risk management, metrics and targets, and will be phased in across entities by size and reporting profile. Assurance requirements are also being phased in, with full audit obligations targeted for later years.


‍


**Philippines:** The **Philippine Securities and Exchange Commission** has adopted **Philippine Financial Reporting Standards (PFRS) on Sustainability Disclosures** , based on global ISSB S1 and S2 standards, marking a national pivot toward mandatory climate and sustainability reporting. Tiered implementation requires larger listed companies to adopt these disclosures beginning with fiscal years from **1 January 2026** , expanding to other corporations through **2028 and beyond** , with external assurance requirements on Scope 1 and 2 emissions following thereafter.


These regulatory developments reflect a global shift toward **standardised, high-quality climate reporting** , reinforcing the need for end-to-end solutions that can support data integrity, transparency, and strategic decision-making.


### **Delivering Impact Across the Region**


Together, **Unravel Carbon** and **ACS** will offer a suite of AI Agents for **Sustainability on top of an audited carbon management platform and expertise** across Australia and the Philippines — empowering organisations to:


- **Comply with emerging regulatory frameworks** with confidence and efficiency
- **Standardise emissions measurement and reporting workflows** across Scope 1, 2 and 3
- **Drive emissions reduction strategies grounded in operational data and outcomes**
- **Scale up sustainability capabilities without expanding internal headcount**


**Unravel Carbon** already supports a roster of **large market-cap enterprises in Australia** , including **Officeworks, The Flight Centre, IXOM, The Iconic and Monde Nissin** , among others. These organisations leverage Unravel’s platform to unlock actionable insights and credible emissions data that underpin their decarbonisation journeys.
