# A consortium of investors acquires the Rise portfolio of 265 units from BNP Paribas REIM

The portfolio consists of 265 multi-family homes spread across the Randstad region in the cities of Almere, Barendrecht, Hoofddorp, and Zoetermeer, with a total area of more than 25,000 m². The homes, built between 2000 and 2006, average 92 m² in size and have 2 or 3 bedrooms. Most of the homes have an energy label of A or A+.


Cushman & Wakefield[Capital Markets](https://www.cushmanwakefield.com/en/netherlands/services/capital-markets) and[Project Development Services](https://www.cushmanwakefield.com/en/netherlands/services/project-development-services) , Sectie5, Houthoff, and Deloitte acted as advisors to the buyers in this transaction. Rabobank is financing the buyers in this purchase.


BNP Paribas REIM was advised by Capital Value, Loyens & Loeff, and SGS Search in the sale of this portfolio.


## Ardea Vastgoed


Marijn Mast and Irene Jong, Ardea Vastgoed: “We are proud of the acquisition of the Rise portfolio. Together with the other buyers, this acquisition is a very nice addition to the current portfolio, which we will further optimize and expand.”


Ardea Vastgoed, as a family office, specializes in acquiring investment properties for and with investors on their own account and risk. For our investors, we guide the entire process from purchase to structuring and financing. We focus on sustainable long-term relationships.


## Cushman & Wakefield


David Vos and Virginia Holt, Cushman & Wakefield Capital Markets team: “With the acquisition of this high-quality real estate portfolio, Ardea Vastgoed is taking the next step in their strategy to build a larger portfolio in the Netherlands. We look forward to further supporting them in this strategy with our expertise.”


## Sectie5


Thijs Burger and Victoria Renzelmann, Sectie5: “Due to our extensive network, we initiated and successfully completed the transaction of this high-quality residential portfolio in collaboration with Ardea Vastgoed. The portfolio seamlessly aligns with the strategy to invest in future-proof residential portfolios.”


## BNP Paribas REIM


Kristina Meier, BNP Paribas REIM: “We are pleased to have successfully managed the four properties over the past decade, maximizing their potential and contributing to the portfolio’s solid performance. We are delighted with this transaction, which marks a highly successful conclusion to the investment horizon.”

Clemens van Vuurde, Capital Value: “This transaction clearly shows that there is interest from private investors to jointly acquire larger residential portfolios. We expect the increasing interest from private investors to continue this year, which offers great opportunities for transactions in 2025.”
