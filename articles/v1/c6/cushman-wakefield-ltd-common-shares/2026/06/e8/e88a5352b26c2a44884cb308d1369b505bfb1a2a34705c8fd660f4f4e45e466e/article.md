# Cushman & Wakefield Appoints Pierre Nolte As Head of Germany & Netherlands

Pierre Nolte, Head of Germany & Netherlands


- *Nolte, who currently leads the firm’s German business, expands his country leadership responsibilities to include the Netherlands from 1 July 2026*


- *Following a structured transition and handover period, Annelou de Groot will leave the business*


**LONDON, 29 June 2026** – Cushman & Wakefield has appointed[Pierre Nolte](https://www.cushmanwakefield.com/en/germany/people/pierre-nolte) as Head of[Germany](https://www.cushmanwakefield.com/en/germany) &[Netherlands](https://www.cushmanwakefield.com/en/netherlands) – an expansion of his responsibilities to include the Dutch business. Following a structured transition and handover period, Annelou de Groot will leave the business.


Nolte, an Executive Partner who joined Cushman & Wakefield in 2014, has held multiple leadership roles with the firm. He was appointed Head of Germany a year ago, having previously been Head of[Offices & Leasing](https://www.cushmanwakefield.com/en/germany/industries/office) in Germany and Head of Tenant Representation, EMEA.


[Matthew Bouw](https://www.cushmanwakefield.com/en/australia/people/matthew-bouw) , Chief Executive, APAC & EMEA, Cushman & Wakefield, said: “Cushman & Wakefield has a proud and successful heritage in the Netherlands which, like Germany, is a key market for us and our clients. Expanding Pierre’s relentless focus on performance and our clients across both markets will accelerate growth and drive improved outcomes for clients. I would like to thank Annelou for her hard work and dedication, not only as Head of Netherlands but also as a key member of our APAC & EMEA executive leadership team, and to wish her well in the next phase of her career.”


Nolte will relocate to the Netherlands and will continue to travel regularly to the firm’s offices across Germany, the Netherlands and Europe.


Pierre Nolte, Head of Germany & Netherlands, Cushman & Wakefield, said: “It is an honour to take on this expanded role. Working across borders is something I have enjoyed throughout my career, but our businesses remain proudly Dutch in the Netherlands and German in Germany. With talented teams in both countries, supported by our global platform, I’m excited by the growth opportunities ahead for us and our clients.”
