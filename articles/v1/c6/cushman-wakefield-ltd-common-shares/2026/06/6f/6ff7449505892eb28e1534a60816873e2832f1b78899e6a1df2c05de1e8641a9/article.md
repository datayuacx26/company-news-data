# Cushman & Wakefield Recognized by Newsweek as One of America's Most Patriotic Companies

One of America's Most Patriotic Companies


**[NEW YORK](https://www.cushmanwakefield.com/en/united-states/offices/midtown-manhattan) , June 29, 2026** – Cushman & Wakefield (NYSE: CWK), a leading global real estate services firm, has been recognized as one of America’s Most Patriotic Companies 2026. This award is presented by Newsweek in partnership with Plant-A Insights Group.


America’s Most Patriotic Companies were selected through a national survey, independent analysis and media review. From more than 10,000 companies evaluated, 2,500 advanced to a survey of over 22,000 Americans, who assessed organizations on ethics, community impact and alignment with U.S. values, with added weight for support of service members, veterans, first responders and disaster response.


This recognition underscores Cushman & Wakefield’s ongoing support for the communities in which it operates, including initiatives that honor and assist veterans, promote responsible business practices and contribute to disaster relief and recovery efforts. Cushman & Wakefield’s dedication to the military and veteran community spans decades of meaningful engagement, from attracting and developing top veteran talent to partnering with industry leaders to support transitioning service members. Led by[Matt Disher](https://www.cushmanwakefield.com/en/united-states/people/matt-disher) , Executive Director at Cushman & Wakefield, the firm’s[Military and Veterans Programs](https://www.cushmanwakefield.com/en/united-states/military-and-veterans-programs) have earned a Top Ten Military Friendly designation for four consecutive years.


“We are honored to be recognized as one of America’s Most Patriotic Companies,” said[Bruce Mosler](https://www.cushmanwakefield.com/en/united-states/people/bruce-mosler) , Chairman of Global Brokerage and Chairman of Military and Veterans Programs at Cushman & Wakefield. “This recognition reflects our commitment to supporting the communities we serve, including those who have dedicated their lives to protecting and strengthening our country. We are proud of the work our people do every day to make a meaningful impact and will continue to build on these efforts.”


“Building a workforce that includes experienced veterans brings leadership, adaptability and operational discipline to our teams—delivering stronger outcomes for our clients,” said Disher. “By sharing our approach, we’re able to extend that impact across our business, with our clients and throughout the industry.”
