# Edward J. Minskoff Equities Retains Cushman & Wakefield to Market Final Availability at 51 Astor Place

51 Astor Place


*Leasing team will market the building's remaining 50,000 square feet across the eighth and ninth floors as demand for premier Manhattan office space continues to outpace supply*


**[NEW YORK](https://www.cushmanwakefield.com/en/united-states/offices/new-york) , August 17, 2026** — Ownership of 51 Astor Place has retained Cushman & Wakefield as the exclusive leasing agent for the building's remaining office availability. The leasing assignment will be led by Ethan Silverstein, Michael Movshovich, Anthony LoPresti, Hayley Shoener and Eric Hazen on behalf of owner Edward J. Minskoff Equities, Inc.


The remaining availability comprises the full eighth and ninth floors, totaling approximately 50,000 square feet, one of the very few blocks of new construction in Midtown South. As part of the leasing strategy, ownership is evaluating plans to deliver one of the two floors as a prebuilt suite in partnership with TPG, providing tenants with a move-in-ready opportunity in one of Manhattan's most supply-constrained office markets.


"With only two full floors remaining, leasing activity at 51 Astor Place reflects the continued strength of Manhattan's office market," said Edward J. Minskoff, Chairman of Edward J. Minskoff Equities. "As companies continue to prioritize high-quality office space, we're excited to partner with Cushman & Wakefield as we lease the building's remaining availability."


The assignment comes as Manhattan's inventory of new construction and trophy office space continues to tighten, leaving limited opportunities for tenants seeking premier space. 51 Astor Place is one of the few properties in Midtown South able to offer a combination of modern construction, efficient floor plates and immediate availability.


Located in NoHo, 51 Astor Place sits in one of the most dynamic neighborhoods in Midtown South, offering a highly walkable amenity base and direct access to multiple subway lines. The building has attracted a diverse tenant roster spanning technology, financial services, media and professional services, reflecting NoHo's continued evolution as a premier office destination.


"Opportunities to secure space in a modern office building of this quality have become increasingly rare," said[Anthony LoPresti](https://www.cushmanwakefield.com/en/united-states/people/anthony-lopresti) , Executive Managing Director at Cushman & Wakefield. "With two full floors available and a location in one of Manhattan's most sought-after neighborhoods, 51 Astor Place is well positioned to meet demand from tenants seeking high-quality office space in today's market."


**About Edward J. Minskoff Equities, Inc.** EJME was founded by Edward J. Minskoff in May of 1987. The company owns, develops and manages institutional quality commercial office buildings in major urban centers primarily in New York City. Selectively, EJME also develops high-end residential properties. EJME oversees all phases of its properties, including design/development, management, leasing and financing.
