# Security Alarm Systems for Your Small Business: Complete Guide

Arlo makes it easier for small businesses to build a security setup around their actual space instead of forcing everything into a one-size-fits-all system.


The[Arlo Pro 6](https://us.arlo.com/products/arlo-pro-6) is a strong option for flexible indoor or outdoor coverage, with 2K HDR video, a 160° Field of View, Motion Detection and Alerts, Color nNight Vision, Dual-Band Wi-Fi, and a swappable battery.


The[Arlo Essential Pan Tilt Indoor Camera](https://us.arlo.com/products/arlo-essential-pan-tilt-indoor) is useful for wider indoor coverage. It offers 360° pan, 180° tilt, Automatic Motion Tracking, Motion Detection and Audio Alerts, Privacy Control, cClear Night Vision, and Dual-Band Wi-Fi.


[Arlo Video Doorbells](https://us.arlo.com/products/arlo-doorbell) can help monitor front entrances, deliveries, and after-hours visitors with head-to-toe coverage and a wide 180° Field of View.


For darker exterior areas, the[Arlo Wired Floodlight](https://us.arlo.com/products/arlo-wired-floodlight) combines 2K HDR video with bright lighting and a wide field of view, making it a practical option for rear doors, side entrances, storage areas, walkways, and parking-adjacent spaces.


For businesses that need broader coverage,[Arlo bundles and multi-camera kits](https://us.arlo.com/pages/arlo-secure-kits) can help secure multiple zones without building the system one device at a time.


Then, tie every camera and device in your security network together with the[Arlo Security System](https://us.arlo.com/products/arlo-security-system) . This central hub provides an enhanced, secure connection for all of your indoor, outdoor, and doorbell cameras, as well as the included All-in-One Sensors with 8 detection functions, pulling them together into one device. It also gives you easy, one-touch access to crucial emergency features, and keeps your security system running, even if the Wi-Fi or power goes out.
