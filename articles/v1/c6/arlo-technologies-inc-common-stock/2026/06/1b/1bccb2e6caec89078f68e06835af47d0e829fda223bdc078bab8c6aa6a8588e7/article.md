# How to Stop Porch Pirates in 8 Simple Steps

Imagine you get a delivery notification. Your package has arrived, but when you check the front door, it is gone. That is the classic porch pirate problem.


A porch pirate is someone who steals packages from another person’s porch, doorstep, lobby, or home entry area. Package theft can happen in a few seconds, especially when deliveries are left in plain view. According to the U.S. Postal Service Office of Inspector General, at least[58 million packages were stolen in the United States in 2024](https://www.uspsoig.gov/reports/white-papers/package-theft-united-states) .


The good news: a few smart habits and the right home security tools can help you protect packages from porch pirates. Here are eight simple ways to reduce package theft with Arlo.
