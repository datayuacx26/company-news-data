# Best Pet Cameras: How To Prepare Your Home for a New Pet

## Key takeaways


- A home security camera can double as a pet camera, helping you check in on dogs and cats while you are away.
- The best pet camera setup depends on where your pet spends time: indoors, in the yard, near the front door, or outside at night.
- Useful pet camera features include 2-Way Audio, Night Vision, motion alerts, wide Field of View, and pet detection with Arlo Secure.
- Arlo cameras, doorbells, and floodlights can help protect your pet and your home with one connected security setup.


Adopting a new pet is an exciting milestone, but it also comes with a lot of new routines. From training a puppy to checking on a curious cat, the right pet camera can help you monitor, guide, and protect your new companion throughout the day.


[Dedicated pet cameras](https://blog.arlo.com/arlo-innovations/why-every-pet-owner-needs-a-security-camera-system/) can be helpful, but a home security camera can often do the job with more flexibility. With Arlo, you can check in from your phone, use 2-Way Audio, receive motion alerts, and keep watch over the places your pet uses most.
