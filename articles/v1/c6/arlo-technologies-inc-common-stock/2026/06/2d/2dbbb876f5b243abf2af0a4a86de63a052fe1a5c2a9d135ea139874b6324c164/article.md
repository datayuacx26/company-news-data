# How to Choose a Doorbell Camera for Apartments

A video doorbell is one of the easiest ways to add more security to an apartment. But renters have different rules than homeowners. You may not be able to drill into the wall, hardwire equipment, change the doorframe, or install anything your lease does not allow.


That does not mean you are out of options.


The right apartment door camera should be easy to install, simple to remove, and smart enough to help you see who is at your door, monitor packages, talk to visitors, and get alerts when activity happens outside your apartment.


[Arlo Video Doorbells](https://us.arlo.com/products/arlo-doorbell) are built for that kind of everyday front-door visibility, with 2K resolution, a wide 180° Field of View, Motion Alerts, 2-Way Audio, direct Wi-Fi connection, easy DIY installation, and an Integrated Siren.
