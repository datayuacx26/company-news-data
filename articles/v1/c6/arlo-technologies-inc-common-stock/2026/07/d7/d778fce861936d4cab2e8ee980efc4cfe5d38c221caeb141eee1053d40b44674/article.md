# Best Wireless Security System Cameras for Small Businesses

- **Video quality:** If you can’t see what’s going on in your wireless security system camera feed, you won’t be able to use that data to make your business safer. A wireless camera that records at least 2K video will strike a great balance between visibility and file size, allowing you to capture finer details. If you need an even bigger boost in video quality, consider picking up a 4K wireless security camera.
- **Battery life:** Since you’re running your security network without wires, you need a way to power your cameras for day-in, day-out recording. Long-lasting rechargeable batteries will power your cameras for months, and ensuring that they’re swappable means you can keep them going with minimal downtime.
- **Motion detection:** Recording your business after-hours helps boost overall security, but constant 24/7 coverage can drain your batteries.[Built-in motion detection features](https://blog.arlo.com/best-security-setups/what-is-a-motion-detector-camera/) can help you record the most important moments without wearing down battery life or filling up your data storage.
- **Ease of use:** You’re already busy enough running your business. The last thing you need to worry about is how to view and operate your security feed. The best wireless security cameras for business include intuitive and user-friendly apps so you can manage your camera network from a desktop or mobile device.
