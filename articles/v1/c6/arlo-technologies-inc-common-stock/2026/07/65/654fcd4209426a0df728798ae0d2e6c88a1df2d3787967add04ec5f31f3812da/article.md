# Best Wireless Security Cameras in 2026

- **Long battery life:** Since your wireless security cameras won’t be hooked up to a local power source, you need a strong, long-lasting battery to keep it going, even when you’re not there. Many Arlo cameras can last months off a single charge, ensuring you won’t miss a moment.
- **2K Video:** High-resolution video allows you to capture fine details like logos or license plates, and allows you to digitally zoom in and out without degrading quality. Look for cameras that offer at least 2K video for a fantastic balance between clarity and performance.
- **Alerts and notifications:** Delivery person arrives with a package? A driver accidentally gets too close to your car in the driveway? AI-powered notifications can send alerts directly to your phone when certain conditions are met so you can respond, even if you’re not at home.
- **Customizable zones:** Take control over the areas your wireless security cameras cover to protect your neighbors’ privacy and reduce unnecessary recording to save on battery life.
- **Two-way audio:** Communicate with anyone who rings your doorbell, right from the app, even when you’re out of the house. You can also set pre-recorded messages to direct deliveries so you don’t have to fret if you’re too busy to answer the door.
