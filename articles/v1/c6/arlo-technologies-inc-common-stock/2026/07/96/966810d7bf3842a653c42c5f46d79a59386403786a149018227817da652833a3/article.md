# Battery Powered Security Cameras: What You Need to Know

- When you’re shopping for a battery powered security camera, don’t overlook the battery life. Longer-lasting batteries mean enhanced security, more convenient placement and usage, and reduced cost over the long term.
- Temperature, motion activity, video resolution, night vision, and Wi-Fi strength can all impact the battery life wireless security cameras can get. Taking steps to mitigate these effects will help your cameras work more efficiently and last longer.
- Arlo Pro 6 XL and Arlo Essential XL cameras feature up to 2.5 times the battery life of standard cameras and can last up to a year between charges. Pair these cameras with an Arlo Solar Panel Charger for virtually limitless coverage.
