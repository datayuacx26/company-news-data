# What Is the Arlo Home Security System?

The beauty of the Arlo Home Security System is its modularity. If you have other Arlo products like the[Arlo Pro 6 camera](https://us.arlo.com/products/arlo-pro-6) , integrating them into your security setup takes minutes. It all happens in the[Arlo Secure App](https://us.arlo.com/pages/arlo-secure) .


Arlo Secure manages your entire infrastructure, from checking video feeds, managing notifications, and adding or removing devices from your network. Simply open up the app, tap a few buttons, and you’re ready to go.


A free Arlo Secure plan gives you a ton of security features out of the box, like motion detection alerts, 2-Way Audio, and Siren Activation. But if you want to get the most out of your DIY security setup, Arlo Secure Premium with Professional Monitoring gives you all of that and much more, like:


- 24/7 Professional Monitoring and 24/7 Emergency Response for the ultimate in security.
- Customizable Smart Activity Zones for automated recording and alerts.
- An Early Warning System that can detect people, vehicles, noises, and anything else you want to capture.
- AI-powered Event Captions and Video Search functionality.
- 60-Day Video History archives.


Arlo Secure unlocks professional-grade security in a low-cost, DIY package that monitors your home so you don’t have to. Pick up an[Arlo Secure Home Security Kit bundle](https://us.arlo.com/pages/arlo-secure-kits) , and get everything you need to protect your house for a full year.
