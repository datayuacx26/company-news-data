# How AI Security Cameras Can Keep Your Home Safe While You’re on Vacation

AI technology pairs with other essential features that take your home security experience to the next level. These include:


- **Activity Zones.** Manually set specific areas that you want your security camera to cover, and your Arlo cameras will automatically capture anything that enters that zone while ignoring everything outside of it. They’ll cut down on unnecessary alerts and help you save on battery life, since your cameras will only start to record when something enters a specific area. These zones also help you respect your neighbor’s privacy by keeping them and their property out of the recorded area.
- **Notifications.** Something suspicious happening nearby? AI security cameras can detect that for you then immediately alert you via a notification on your mobile app. That way, you can respond however you need to and get back to your vacation, whether that’s using the camera’s built-in 2-Way Audio to let delivery drivers know where to set packages or asking a solicitor to leave.
- **Auto-generated captions.** Sometimes it can be difficult to make out sounds or speech in your recorded footage. AI technology can provide transcripts for you, making it easier to understand what’s going on while you’re away from the house.
- **Video Search.** AI security cameras can automatically tag footage based on the content they detect, allowing you to easily search for the most important parts of your security feed for further investigation.
