# Arlo and Aloe Health Care’s Medical Alert System: What You Need to Know

For Arlo, acquiring Aloe Care Health made perfect sense. Their vision for ensuring the health and safety of their customers through their AI-enabled medical alert system pairs perfectly with our smart cameras like the Arlo Pro 6 and Arlo Ultra.


The[Arlo Essential line](https://us.arlo.com/products/arlo-essential-indoor-3rd-gen) offers the perfect indoor and outdoor camera for older adults looking to manage their personal safety and monitor threats. You’ll get clear 2K video footage and full 360° Field of View with the[Arlo Essential Pan Tilt Indoor model](https://us.arlo.com/products/arlo-essential-pan-tilt-indoor) , giving you a complete view of your surroundings wherever you install it.


[Arlo Secure Early Warning System Premium](https://us.arlo.com/pages/arlo-secure) plan elevates the experience, offering deeper levels of protection. 60-Day video history lets you review footage to track incidents and discover changes in health over time. The included Early Warning system includes noise and fire detection, sending alerts for potential falls and other hazards. Plus, you’ll also get 24/7 Emergency Response and 24/7 Professional Monitoring for ‘round-the-clock coverage. We’ll help keep older family members safe, so you don’t have to worry.


Arlo aims to provide a complete package for home security, and Aloe Care Health will enable us to further protect the health of our customers alongside improving the security of their family members and their property. Together, we’ll work to keep you safe and give you the ultimate gift: peace of mind.
