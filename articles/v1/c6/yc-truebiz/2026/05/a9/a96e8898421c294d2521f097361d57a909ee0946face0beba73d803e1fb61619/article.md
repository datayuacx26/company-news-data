# Signicat partners with TrueBiz to bring behavioral risk intelligence into KYB onboarding

### Signicat customers can now verify what a business actually does, not just that it exists


### TrueBiz brings automated merchant risk intelligence directly into Signicat onboarding workflows, closing the gap between merchant identity and merchant behavior.


---


## **A new layer of merchant risk intelligence for Signicat customers**


Businesses can pass identity verification while still operating in prohibited categories, misrepresenting their activity, or acting as fronts for higher-risk operations.


To detect this, onboarding teams often rely on **manual web research** : analysts reviewing merchant websites, social profiles, and digital footprints to determine what the business actually does.


Through this new integration with TrueBiz,[Signicat](https://www.signicat.com/) customers can now automate that step directly within their onboarding workflow.


After verifying a business’s identity through Signicat, teams can trigger a TrueBiz assessment to analyze the company’s **digital footprint and behavioral signals across the open web** , producing a decision-ready risk assessment in under 30 seconds.


---


## **Closing the gap between identity and behavior**


KYB answers an important question:


**Does this business legally exist?**


But risk teams must also answer another:


**Does the business actually operate the way it claims?**


TrueBiz closes that gap by analyzing more than **250 digital footprint signals** across the open web to determine:


-


If the business is operational online


-


What products or services it offers


-


If its activity aligns with a financial institution’s risk policies


The system returns a configurable **pass / fail recommendation** , along with structured risk indicators and transparent reasoning, all in under 30 seconds.


---


## **Built directly into Signicat onboarding workflows**


Signicat customers can now add behavioral merchant analysis to their existing onboarding process without introducing new tools or manual research steps.


By combining **Signicat’s identity infrastructure** with **TrueBiz’s behavioral intelligence** , financial institutions and payment providers can:


-


Reduce manual merchant reviews


-


Detect prohibited or misrepresented businesses earlier


-


Speed up onboarding times while strengthening risk controls


“Signicat has built the identity infrastructure that financial institutions worldwide depend on,” said **Danny Hakimian** , **CEO and Founder** of **TrueBiz** . “TrueBiz adds the layer beneath it: what does this business actually do online, and does that match what it claimed on the application?”


---


**Try it today - no integration required**


Signicat customers can test a TrueBiz assessment on any business before connecting it to their onboarding workflow. Enter a URL or business name and see the full risk assessment in under 30 seconds.
