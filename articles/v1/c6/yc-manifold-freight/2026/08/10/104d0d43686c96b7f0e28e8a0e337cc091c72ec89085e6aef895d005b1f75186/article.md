# Too Many Tabs, Not Enough Freight: EOS Trucking Finds the Fix with Manifold

Manifold is proud to welcome **EOSTrucking** ([eostrucking.com](https://www.eostrucking.com/) ) to the Manifold Family of Customers.


EOS Trucking, a truckload carrier based in North Little Rock, Arkansas, is known for its commitment to safety, on-time delivery, and a team culture that puts drivers and customers first. Serving industries from grocery and retail to auto parts and beyond, EOS has built a reputation for reliable service across both custom dedicated logistics and unique freight.


But even the most dependable carriers run into the same wall: too many open tabs and not enough opportunity.For Blair and the team at EOS, the challenge wasn’t effort; it was efficiency.Customer portals stacking up, email threads multiplying, and freight opportunities scattered across too many places to keep track of. It’s the kind of noise that eats into a team’s focus and leaves real revenue sitting on the table.


That’s exactly the problemManifold was built to solve. By consolidating every customer portal, load board, and quoting opportunity into a single, unified platform, Manifold eliminates the tab-switching and the second-guessing. Blair and the EOS team now have one clear view of their freight opportunities; the right loads surface automatically, bids go out faster, and nothing slips through the cracks because it came in through a portal someone forgot to check.


This partnership reflects Manifold’s mission to help carriers reclaim time, uncover lost revenue, and bring real clarity to the way they manage freight opportunities. EOS Trucking represents exactly the kind of forward-thinking team we love working with: one that recognizes when smarter tools can unlock serious growth.
