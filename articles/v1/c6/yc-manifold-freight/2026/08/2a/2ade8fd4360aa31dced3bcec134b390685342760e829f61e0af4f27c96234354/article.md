# Owner Operators Coop Selects Manifold to Find Better Paying Freight and Leave the Customer Portal Chaos Behind

Manifold is proud to welcome Owner Operators Coop as our newest carrier customer.


Before working with Manifold, Ron and the team at Owner Operators Coop were facing a challenge that’s all too familiar in today’s freight environment: juggling multiple customer portals and load boards just to find the next load for their backhuals. Logging in, searching, refreshing, comparing, only to find that the best-paying opportunities had already been claimed, or worse, were buried somewhere in a portal they hadn’t checked yet. Finding better-paying loads wasn’t a matter of effort. It was a matter of visibility, and the tools they had weren’t giving them enough of it.


That’s exactly where Manifold stepped in.


By consolidating all of their customer portal load opportunities into a single unified platform, Manifold automatically discovers and highlights the opportunities that match Owner Operators Coop’s available capacity and preferred lanes, including the higher-paying loads that used to slip through the cracks. No more tab-switching. No more missed bids. Just the right freight, at the right time, ready to book


This partnership reflects Manifold’s mission to help carriers and freight brokers reclaim time, uncover lost revenue, and bring clarity to the quoting process. Owner Operators Coop represents exactly the kind of forward-thinking team we love working with, one that recognizes when smarter tools can unlock real growth.
