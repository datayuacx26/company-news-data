# Tanager Logistics Taps Manifold to Unify Spot Freight Across Two Divisions

We're excited to welcome **Tanager Logistics** to the Manifold family as our newest customer!


Tanager Logistics is a freight brokerage based in Laredo, Texas, one of the busiest and most competitive freight corridors in North America. Situated at the heart of U.S.-Mexico cross-border commerce, Tanager's team of experienced brokers delivers creative, responsive solutions for shippers who need speed, precision, and deep market knowledge. Their affiliate carrier, **Double Diamond Transport** , gives the Tanager family firsthand insight into what it takes to move freight efficiently on both sides of the business: asset and non-asset alike.


Manifold's relationship with the Tanager family actually started on the trucking side. Double Diamond Transport came to Manifold first, looking for a smarter way to find backhauls from its customers’ portals. The platform delivered, consolidating their load opportunities into one unified view and helping the team identify and win higher-paying backhauls with far less friction.


When **John Bejin** , Director of Operations at Tanager Logistics, saw what Manifold was doing for Double Diamond Transport, the decision to bring it to the brokerage side was easy. The brokerage team was facing the same challenge that trips up so many freight companies today: opportunities buried across a growing number of customer portals, load boards, and email threads, each one demanding its own login, its own attention, its own follow-up. Brokers were spending too much time navigating systems and not enough time winning freight.


That's exactly the problem Manifold was built to solve. Our platform brings every customer portal opportunity and email opportunity together in one unified view: no more tab-switching, no more missed bids because a load came in through the wrong channel. With Manifold in place across both Double Diamond Transport and Tanager Logistics, John and the team now have complete visibility into their spot freight landscape, the tools to act on it faster, and the data to make sure they're always chasing the loads worth winning.


We're thrilled to partner with a team that understands freight from every angle, from the dispatcher to the brokerage floor, and is always looking for smarter ways to operate. That mindset is exactly what we're building Manifold for.


A big welcome to John and the entire Tanager Logistics team! We can't wait to help you win more freight with a lot less hassle.
