# Heyl Truck Lines Closes the Tabs and Opens a Smarter Way to Win Freight

Manifold is proud to welcome Heyl Truck Lines (heyl.net) as our newest carrier customer.


For more than 75 years, HeylTruck Lines has been a fixture in North American freight: family owned, headquartered in Akron, Iowa, and operating a fleet of over 500 trucks hauling temperature-controlled and dry freight across the U.S. and Canada. That kind of track record doesn’t happen by accident; it takes a team that takes operations seriously and is always looking for a better way.


So when Braxton and the team atHeyl started feeling the friction of their current workflow — multiple customer portals open at once, email threads to monitor, opportunities scattered across channels — they didn’t just accept it. They went looking for a solution. The challenge wasn’t a lack of effort; it was a lack of unified visibility. Too many tabs, too many logins, and not enough confidence that every opportunity was being seen.


That’s exactly what Manifold was built to fix.


By consolidating every customer portal and freight opportunity into one unified platform, Manifold gives Heyl’s team a single place to see, manage, and act on available freight, without the tab-switching, the manual refreshing, or the nagging feeling that something slipped through the cracks. With visiblity to every opportunity in their network, Heyl is now able to find the best backhauls from customers, increasing revenue simplifying their dispatching operations. And for a team with ambitions to push further into modern freight technology, Manifold is a platform that grows with them.


Heyl has spent over seven decades building something special. We’re excited to be a small part of what they build next.
