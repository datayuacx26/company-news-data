# Introducing the Cosmic Agent Marketplace

AI agents are powerful. But setting them up from scratch can be daunting.


You have to write and refine prompts. Configure integrations. Figure out where the agent lives. Connect it to your tools. Give it context about your business. And then test, iterate, and maintain it. For most teams, that friction is enough to shelve the idea entirely.


Today, we're removing that barrier.


**The[Cosmic Agent Marketplace](https://www.cosmicjs.com/marketplace/agents) is live.** It launches with 20 pre-built agents across 7 categories, each one ready to install in a single click and start working inside Slack, WhatsApp, or Telegram and in your Cosmic dashboard as a chat widget.


No prompt engineering required. No setup headaches. Just capable AI teammates, ready to go.


---


## What Is the Cosmic Agent Marketplace?


The Cosmic Agent Marketplace is a curated library of AI agents built specifically for teams using Cosmic. Each agent is pre-configured with the right instructions, context, and integrations to handle a specific job, whether that's writing content, building features, qualifying leads, handling support tickets, or migrating data.


You browse the catalog, click install, and the agent is live in your preferred messaging channel. From there, your team interacts with it directly in Slack, WhatsApp, or Telegram, just like messaging a colleague.


No dashboards to configure. No developer time required.


---


## 7 Categories, 20 Agents, One Marketplace


At launch, the marketplace spans seven categories covering the key functions of a modern team:


- **Growth** - Drive traffic, enhance SEO, optimize conversions, and identify opportunities
- **Content** - Write, edit, publish, and manage content across your site
- **Engineering** - Application development, feature building, bug fixes, and repo management
- **Sales** - Pipeline management, outreach, and deal support
- **Support** - Answer customer questions, triage tickets, and reduce response times
- **Migration** - Move content from legacy CMSes, flat files, spreadsheets, other systems into Cosmic
- **Ecommerce & Operations** - Inventory, orders, and operational workflows


### Meet Nora, Your Growth Strategist


The featured agent at launch is **Nora, Growth Strategist** . Nora is built to help teams identify growth opportunities, develop go-to-market strategies, analyze content performance, and prioritize what moves the needle. She lives in your Slack workspace and can answer strategic questions, help plan campaigns, and synthesize data on demand.

## How It Works


Installing an agent takes about 30 seconds:


1. **Browse the marketplace** at[cosmicjs.com/marketplace/agents](https://www.cosmicjs.com/marketplace/agents)
2. **Click Install** on the agent you want
3. Go through the setup steps with them (they walk you through it!)
4. **Choose your channel** : Slack, WhatsApp, or Telegram. They are also available as a chat widget in your dashboard, just enable the Cosmic Agent and switch between them
5. **Start collaborating** - the agent is live and ready


Agents connect to your Cosmic content bucket automatically, so they have context about your site, your content structure, and your team's work from day one. Content agents can read, write, and publish objects directly in your CMS. Engineering agents can read and commit to your connected repositories.


Every agent runs on Cosmic's AI infrastructure, so there are no external API keys to manage and no separate subscriptions to maintain. And they are fully customizable after you install them.
