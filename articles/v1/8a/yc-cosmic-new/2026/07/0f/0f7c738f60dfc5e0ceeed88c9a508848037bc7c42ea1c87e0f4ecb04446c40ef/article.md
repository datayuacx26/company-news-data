# Best Free Headless CMS Platforms in 2026 (Ranked and Compared)

If you're evaluating headless CMS platforms, the free plan is usually where the decision starts. You want to build something real, validate your architecture, and make sure the developer experience is solid before committing to a paid tier.


The problem: free plans vary wildly. Some give you a genuinely useful starting point. Others are barely enough to run a demo. And a few popular platforms don't list Cosmic at all on their comparison pages, even though Cosmic's free plan stacks up well against the field.


This post fixes that. We ranked the best free headless CMS platforms in 2026 by what you actually get on the free tier: content objects, API access, team members, and a realistic upgrade path.


---


## What to look for in a free headless CMS


Before the rankings, here's what actually matters on a free plan:


- *Objects / content entries:* how much content can you store before hitting a wall?
- *API access:* can you actually build a production-ready app, or are you rate-limited out of the gate?
- *Team members:* can more than one person use the free plan?
- *Buckets / workspaces:* can you run more than one project?
- *Upgrade path:* when you outgrow free, does the next tier make sense for your team size and budget?
- *AI features:* does the platform give you any AI tooling on free, or lock it behind enterprise?

### 2. Contentful


*Free plan includes:*


- 10 Team members
- 2 roles, 2 locales
- 25 Content types
- 100,000 API calls/month
- 50GB CDN bandwidth/month
- 10,000 records
- 1 Starter Space


Contentful's free plan is generous on team seats (10 users), which makes it appealing for larger teams just starting out. The 100k API call limit is enough for prototyping but can become a constraint on a high-traffic production site. The bigger limitation: Contentful's paid plans start at $300/month (Lite), which is a significant jump once you need more locales, roles, or API capacity.


*Best for:* larger teams who want to prototype with headless CMS before committing to a budget.


---


### 3. Sanity


*Free plan includes:*


- Up to 20 users
- Unlimited content types
- 1M API CDN requests/month
- 250,000 API requests/month
- 100GB asset storage
- 100GB bandwidth
- 2 datasets


Sanity's free tier is genuinely generous on users and API calls. The GROQ query language gives developers fine-grained control over content fetching. The tradeoff: Sanity has a steeper learning curve than most CMS platforms, and the free plan limits you to 2 datasets (projects). Sanity Studio (the editing interface) requires configuration that's less approachable for non-technical editors.


*Best for:* developers comfortable with a code-first CMS who want flexibility on content modeling.


---


### 4. Strapi


*Free plan includes:*


- Self-hosted (open source)
- Unlimited users
- Unlimited content types
- REST and GraphQL API
- Community support only


Strapi is open source, so the software itself is free. But "free" here means you're managing your own hosting, security, backups, and updates. For a small team without dedicated DevOps resources, that's a real operational cost. Strapi Cloud (managed) starts at $29/month, which removes the self-hosting burden but adds a recurring cost.


*Best for:* developers who want full control and are comfortable self-hosting.


---


### 5. Storyblok


*Free plan includes:*


- 1 user
- 1 locale
- 200 components
- 100,000 API calls/month
- 1TB asset storage


Storyblok's free plan is limited to a single user, which makes it essentially a solo-developer or personal-project tier. The 1TB asset storage is generous, but the 1-user cap means even a two-person team needs a paid plan. Storyblok's visual editor is its standout feature, but it's most valuable for content editors, who you can't add on free.


*Best for:* solo developers evaluating Storyblok's visual editing experience.


---


### 6. Prismic


*Free plan includes:*


- 1 user
- 1 locale
- Unlimited documents
- 4M API calls/month
- Page builder


Prismic's free plan has the highest API call limit in the group (4M/month), which is more than enough for any production project at the free tier. Like Storyblok, the 1-user cap is the main constraint. The page builder is included on free, which is a genuine differentiator for marketing teams who want visual editing without code.


*Best for:* solo developers or marketers who want high API throughput and a visual page builder.


---


### 7. Hygraph


*Free plan includes:*


- 3 seats
- 2 roles
- 2 locales
- 10 components
- 500,000 API calls/month
- Unlimited asset storage
- Comments and live preview


Hygraph's Hobby plan is one of the more collaborative free tiers in the group, with 3 seats, commenting, and live preview included. It's GraphQL-native, which is a strength for developers who want a flexible query layer but a constraint if your stack doesn't lean that way. The 10-component limit can become a bottleneck on more complex content models.


*Best for:* small teams who want collaborative features and GraphQL on a free plan.
