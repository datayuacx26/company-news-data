# 10 Headless CMS Platforms Compared: Features, Pricing, and Fit

If you're evaluating headless CMS platforms in 2026, you're dealing with a genuinely crowded market. The options range from fully managed cloud platforms to self-hosted open-source tools, from AI-native content infrastructure to legacy enterprise systems wearing a headless coat.


This guide cuts through the noise. We cover the top headless CMS platforms, what they're best for, real pricing, and who should use them. Updated for 2026.


## What to look for in a headless CMS in 2026


Before the list, here are the criteria that actually matter for teams evaluating CMS platforms today:


- *API quality:* REST API reliability, SDK support, and developer experience
- *Content modeling flexibility:* how easily can you structure your content for your specific use case
- *AI capabilities:* built-in AI content generation, semantic search, AI agents, and automation
- *Team collaboration:* roles, permissions, editorial workflows, and non-technical editor experience
- *Pricing transparency:* are there hidden per-seat charges, API call limits, or unpredictable overages
- *Framework support:* Next.js, Astro, Nuxt, Remix, Svelte, Vue
- *Self-hosted vs. managed:* operational overhead vs. reliability and scalability

### 2. Contentful


*Best for: large enterprise teams with complex localization and compliance requirements*


Contentful is one of the original headless CMS platforms. It has a mature content modeling system, strong localization support, and a large ecosystem of integrations. The tradeoff: it's expensive at scale, per-seat pricing adds up fast for larger teams, and the developer experience has lagged behind newer platforms.


*Pricing:* Free tier available. Community plan ($0), Basic ($300/month), Premium (custom). Enterprise pricing can run $3,000+/month for large teams.


*Watch out for:* per-seat pricing, API call limits, and the cost of Contentful Compose for non-technical editors.


Looking for a Contentful alternative?[See how Cosmic compares](https://www.cosmicjs.com/compare/contentful)

### 3. Sanity


*Best for: developer teams that want maximum content modeling flexibility and a real-time editing experience*


Sanity's standout feature is its Studio: a fully customizable, open-source editing environment you deploy yourself. Content is stored in GROQ-queryable datasets, and the real-time collaboration is genuinely excellent. The tradeoff is complexity: Sanity requires more developer time to set up and maintain than most cloud-native options.


*Pricing:* Free tier (up to 3 users, 2 datasets). Growth from $15/user/month. Enterprise custom. Costs scale with users and dataset size.


Looking for a Sanity alternative?[See how Cosmic compares](https://www.cosmicjs.com/compare/sanity)

### 4. Storyblok


*Best for: marketing teams who need a visual page builder with headless delivery*


Storyblok's visual editor is its core differentiator: marketers can see their content in context while editing, without needing to preview in a separate environment. It works well for component-driven marketing sites and landing pages. Developer experience is solid but less flexible than pure API-first platforms for complex content models.


*Pricing:* Free (1 user). Entry ($99/month). Business (from $599/month). Enterprise custom.


Looking for a Storyblok alternative?[See how Cosmic compares](https://www.cosmicjs.com/compare/storyblok)

### 5. Strapi


*Best for: teams that want self-hosted, open-source control over their content backend*


Strapi is the leading open-source headless CMS. You host it yourself (or use Strapi Cloud), and you get full control over your data and infrastructure. REST API and plugin ecosystem are mature. The tradeoff is the same as any self-hosted option: operational overhead, upgrades, and scaling are your responsibility.


*Pricing:* Community (free, self-hosted). Strapi Cloud from $29/month. Growth and Enterprise tiers available.


Looking for a Strapi alternative?[See how Cosmic compares](https://www.cosmicjs.com/compare/strapi)


---


### 6. Prismic


*Best for: marketing and content teams building slice-based, component-driven websites*


Prismic's Page Builder and Slice Machine give marketers and developers a shared language for building pages from reusable components. Strong Next.js and Nuxt integration. Less suitable for complex, multi-type content models or heavy API workloads.


*Pricing:* Free (1 user, 1 repository). Starter ($15/month). Small Business ($100/month). Enterprise custom.


Looking for a Prismic alternative?[See how Cosmic compares](https://www.cosmicjs.com/compare/prismic)


---


### 7. Hygraph (formerly GraphCMS)


*Best for: teams building federated content graphs across multiple data sources*


Hygraph's content federation feature lets you combine content from multiple APIs into a single unified schema. Useful for large enterprises with fragmented data sources. The editor experience is less polished for non-technical users.


*Pricing:* Free (limited). Scale ($199/month). Enterprise custom.


---


### 8. Builder.io


*Best for: marketing teams that need a drag-and-drop visual editor integrated into an existing stack*


Builder.io is positioned as a visual development platform as much as a CMS. The AI-powered visual editor lets marketers build and edit pages without code. Strong A/B testing and personalization features. Best suited for teams where marketing page velocity is the primary need.


*Pricing:* Free tier available. Growth ($19/month). Business (custom).


---


### 9. Payload CMS


*Best for: developers who want a TypeScript-native, self-hosted CMS with maximum control*


Payload is a newer open-source CMS built in TypeScript with a code-first content modeling approach. If you want to define your schema in code, own your database, and avoid vendor lock-in, Payload is a strong contender. The tradeoff is the same as any self-hosted option: you manage the infrastructure.


*Pricing:* Open source (free, self-hosted). Payload Cloud from $20/month.


---


### 10. Directus


*Best for: teams that want an open-source data platform with CMS capabilities layered on top*


Directus wraps any SQL database and exposes it via REST API with a visual data studio on top. More of a general data platform than a content-first CMS, but flexible enough for many content use cases.


*Pricing:* Open source (free, self-hosted). Directus Cloud from $15/month.

## How to choose: a quick decision framework


*Choose Cosmic if:* you want AI-native content infrastructure, a managed cloud platform, and a team that can publish without needing developer help for every change. Especially strong for teams using Next.js, Astro, or Nuxt.


*Choose Contentful if:* you're a large enterprise with complex localization needs, deep Salesforce/Marketo integration requirements, and budget for premium pricing.


*Choose Sanity if:* your developers want maximum flexibility and you're willing to invest time in a custom Studio setup.


*Choose Strapi or Payload if:* you need full self-hosted control and have the engineering capacity to run your own infrastructure.


*Choose Storyblok or Prismic if:* your primary use case is marketing-team page building with a visual editor.


*Choose Builder.io if:* your core need is AI-assisted drag-and-drop page building for marketing teams.
