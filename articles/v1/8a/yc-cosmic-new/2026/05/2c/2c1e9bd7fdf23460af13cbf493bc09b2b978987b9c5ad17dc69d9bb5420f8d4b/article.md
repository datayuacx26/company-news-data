# The Best Contentful Alternative in 2026

Contentful built its reputation as the headless CMS for enterprise teams. But in 2026, the story has changed. Pricing has climbed sharply, the platform has grown complex, and Salesforce's acquisition of Contentful is now complete. Teams at every size are asking the same question: is there a better option?


The answer is yes. And for developers who want speed, simplicity, and real AI-native tooling, **Cosmic** is the clear choice.


---


## Why Teams Are Leaving Contentful


Contentful's free tier disappeared. Its cheapest paid plan now runs hundreds of dollars per month before you unlock basic features. Add content localization, more environments, or a larger team, and costs escalate fast. On top of that, Contentful's interface and API surface have grown cluttered over the years, making onboarding new developers slower than it should be.


The Salesforce acquisition compounds the uncertainty. When a focused developer tool gets absorbed into a large enterprise platform, pricing pressure typically increases and roadmap priorities shift toward the acquirer's ecosystem.


The pain is real:


- **Unpredictable pricing** that scales against you as your project grows
- **Complex setup** for content modeling and environment management
- **No native AI tooling** baked into the content workflow
- **Slow time-to-first-content** for new developers joining a project


Teams don't just want a cheaper Contentful. They want something better.
