# Scope Agent builds defensible scopes in half the time

Built to be verified


## Your team still knows the job.


And instead of giving you a text box of scope items that you pray are correct, Scope Agent has an intuitive user interface that allows you to verify items and ensure nothing falls through the cracks. For every scope item Scope Agent gives you, it provides an auditable, traceable link back to the drawing or specification from which it originated.


What is the point of automating a scope of work if, one, you can't verify the information and, two, as a result, your teammates don't intimately know the job? We think that the teams that pore over the drawings know the job best, and the ones that can do that while producing the most defensible scopes will protect their margin and deliver the best project outcomes.


At the end of the day, that is what matters: protecting relationships with your subcontractors and delivering a great outcome for the owner.
