# Coming This Fall to YouTube: The Official Angels Among Us Docuseries

[View PDF](https://www.victoriassecretandco.com/static-files/bcab971a-3a54-4e6b-ae0b-a85b99f2e939)


67.2 KB


The road to the runway starts here.


What began as a nationwide search for Victoria’s Secret's next Angel quickly became something much bigger. More than 100,000 aspiring Angels answered the call, sparking excitement across social media, fan communities and beyond.


This fall, we're bringing that journey to audiences everywhere with *Angels Among Us,* an all-new docuseries streaming exclusively on YouTube.


The series follows a group of women as they pursue the opportunity of a lifetime: becoming the newest Victoria's Secret Angel and walking the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show runway.


Along the way, fans will get an inside look at the ambition, resilience, friendships, and unforgettable moments that define the journey from casting to catwalk. But Angels Among Us is more than a behind-the-scenes look at the Fashion Show.


It's the next step in expanding the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show to create new ways for fans to engage with the stories, personalities, people and moments that make the runway so iconic.


Angels Among Us brings audiences closer than ever to the women behind the runway and the dreams driving them forward. The journey will culminate with the reveal of Victoria's Secret's newest Angel on the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show runway this fall.


The runway is the destination. This is the journey.
