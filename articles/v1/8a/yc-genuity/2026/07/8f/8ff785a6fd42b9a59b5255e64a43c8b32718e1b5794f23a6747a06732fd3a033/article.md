# Platform Update: Spring 2024

# Platform Update: Spring 2024


Spring has sprung on the Genuity platform.


Colum Donahue • Published Mar 28, 2024


2 minute read


Check out what's new and what's next below, and as always, feel free toreach out with any questions, comments, or ideas!


---


#### State of IT Report


You deliver projects and services daily to run and expand the business, but few appreciate the complexity you manage. Business leaders often view IT as a black box of unaccountable, ever-increasing costs, yet they constantly ask for more. **It doesn't have to be this way.**


Introducing the Genuity State of IT report, **one comprehensive summary for communicating IT value.** Demonstrate what IT delivers every month with a completely customizable report for management. Report on helpdesk tickets, assets, vendor spend & contracts, and customize how you protect the company against the latest cyber security threats.


#### Help Desk


- We updated the Knowledge Base to make adding articles, review dates, and audiences easier with a modern UI design. An essential first step to adding AI-powered automation to your support desks with the goal of automating + 50% of requests.
- A modern design for the open ticket portal (Help Center) with the ability to customize branding.
- We launched a Task Scheduler for reminders and maintenance tasks.
- The ability to set a quick ticket view in the list view for fast actions.
- Easy to navigate quick filters (Ques) with the ability to customize views coming soon.
- We launched V2 of our task checklists.


*Coming Soon*


- Set a custom domain in the open ticket portal.
- The ability to customize help desk queues by agent.
- SSO for the Genuity mobile help desk app + tons of enhanced functionality.
- The ability to customize notifications and surveys via reusable templates.


#### Asset Management


- Updated analytics under-reporting, which allows you to filter data sets quickly and export the associated information.
- Run exportable reports to see which computers/laptops have specific software and which ones may be missing the same product or service.


*Coming Soon*


- Check-in and check-out assets with an audit log of changes
- Updated functionality for setting the depreciation and end-of-life values for enhanced visibility and better lifecycle management.


#### Contract Management


- Simplified App tracking and Usage.
- Updated parsing of connector data for vendors, making it easier to track the spending you want while ignoring vendor data that doesn't apply.
- New contract views that show the most important details faster.


Thank you for your partnership and continued support. We always love hearing from you, so seriously, don't hesitate toreach out with any feedback or suggestions!
