# The Hidden Dangers of Weak Email Security—and How to Fight Back


Don’t let weak email security be the weak link in your organization’s defense.


David Donnelli • Published Apr 17, 2025


1 minute read


In today’s digital landscape, email remains the cornerstone of business communication. However, poor email security coupled with inadequate alerting can turn your inbox into a gateway for cybercriminals. Without robust defenses, it becomes all too easy for attackers to compromise an account and maintain unauthorized access, causing damage that can ripple throughout your organization.


#### Spotting the Red Flags


Imagine a scenario where odd email rules are suddenly created, or users begin logging in anonymous proxies or vpns. Perhaps you notice unexplained forwarding of messages or the emergence of newly registered applications that seem, well, a little too suspicious. These are clear indicators that your current email filtering solution may not be alerting you to potential threats in real time. Without proactive notifications and automated responses—such as disabling an account based on predetermined criteria—your organization is left vulnerable to constant, evolving attacks.


#### Raising the Bar on Email Security


If your current email security system isn’t equipped to monitor these anomalies and act swiftly, it’s time to rethink your strategy. In an era where cyber threats are more sophisticated than ever, relying on outdated or incomplete security measures is simply not an option. Upgrading to a more robust solution isn’t just about protecting data; it’s about preserving trust, ensuring business continuity, and safeguarding your organization’s reputation.
