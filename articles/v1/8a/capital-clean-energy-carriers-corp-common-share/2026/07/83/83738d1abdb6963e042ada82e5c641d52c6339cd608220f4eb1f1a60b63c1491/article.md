# CCEC Honored with Marine Money’s Deal of the Year Award

## CCEC Honored with Marine Money’s Deal of the Year Award


July 23, 2026


[PDF Version](https://www.capitalcleanenergycarriers.com/node/17296/pdf)


Capital Clean Energy Carriers Corp. (CCEC) was very proud to be honored with Marine Money's Deal of the Year Award at the 38th Annual Marine Money Week, held at The Pierre Hotel in New York City.


The award recognizes the financing behind the world's first large liquid CO₂ carrier project, a landmark deal in the energy transition space. It was proudly accepted on stage by Niovi Iasemidi, Deputy Chief Financial Officer of CCEC at the time of the project conclusion.


Thank you to Marine Money for this recognition and for highlighting a landmark project supporting the global energy transition.
