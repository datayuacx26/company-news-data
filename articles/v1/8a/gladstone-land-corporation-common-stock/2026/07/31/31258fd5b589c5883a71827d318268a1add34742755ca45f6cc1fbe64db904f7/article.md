**About Gladstone Land:**


Gladstone Land is a publicly-traded real estate investment trust that invests in farmland located in major agricultural markets in the U.S., which it leases to farmers. The Company currently owns 142 farms, comprised of approximately 98,000 acres in 14 different states and over 55,000 acre-feet of water assets in California. Additional information can be found at[www.GladstoneLand.com](https://pr.report/npl0) .
