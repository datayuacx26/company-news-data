# How Shiprocket Scaled Seller Onboarding with Decentro’s UPI PG

Table of Contents


##


The eCommerce Logistics Opportunity in India


A report released at LogiMAT India estimates that the Indian freight and[logistics market will grow](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/transportation/roadways/indian-logistics-market-to-grow-at-8-8-annually-to-484-43-bn-by-2029/articleshow/108140755.cms?from=mdr) at 8.8% annually to USD 484.43 billion by 2029 — up from USD 317.26 billion in 2024 — driven by rapid technological advancement across the sector.


The post-pandemic eCommerce boom accelerated demand for faster deliveries and lower shipping costs, while simultaneously pushing logistics players to adopt cutting-edge technologies across warehousing, intralogistics, automation, and transportation management.


In this shifting landscape, the most forward-thinking logistics platforms have evolved from being mere enablers of e-commerce to becoming the central operating system for it — managing shipping, warehousing, stock management, order fulfilment, and increasingly, the end-to-end payment experience.


One such platform standing at the intersection of commerce and technology is our partner **Shiprocket** — and here is how Decentro helped them take their payment infrastructure to the next level.

Built as an operating system for direct commerce, the platform powers shipping, fulfilment, checkout, customer engagement, and marketing solutions for SMEs, D2C brands, and social commerce sellers across India.


**[Shiprocket](https://www.shiprocket.in/) is India’s largest eCommerce enablement platform** , providing digital retailers with an end-to-end customer experience stack. With **250+ ecosystem partners** , Shiprocket enables pan-India deliveries across **19000 + pin codes** and international shipping to **220 countries worldwide** .

##


The Challenge: Scaling Payments Alongside Merchant Growth


As Shiprocket scaled its merchant base — onboarding thousands of new sellers month over month — a critical bottleneck emerged: **the payment infrastructure powering seller payouts and collections wasn’t keeping pace.**


Shiprocket needed a banking and payments infrastructure partner that could:


1. **Handle high-volume UPI transactions** without disruption as merchant numbers grew
2. **Eliminate settlement delays** and provide real-time visibility into cash flow
3. **Reduce operational overhead** tied to reconciliation across thousands of seller accounts
4. **Accelerate merchant onboarding** — enabling new sellers to go live with payment capabilities in days, not weeks
5. **Integrate seamlessly** with their existing technology stack without lengthy engineering timelines


Managing these requirements through traditional payment gateways was proving expensive, slow to settle, and operationally cumbersome at Shiprocket’s scale.


##


How Decentro Empowered Shiprocket


Decentro’s **UPI Payment Gateway (PG) suite** provided the infrastructure backbone Shiprocket needed to supercharge its payment operations. Here’s how each component made a difference:


###


Uninterrupted UPI Capabilities at Scale


By leveraging[Decentro’s UPI stack](https://decentro.tech/products/upi-payment-gateway) , Shiprocket’s merchants gained access to robust, always-on UPI capabilities — enabling instant collections and payouts regardless of transaction volume. The API-first approach meant that as Shiprocket’s merchant base scaled month over month, the payment layer scaled seamlessly alongside it.


###


80%+ Cost Reduction & Instant Settlements


Direct bank-to-bank transfers via Decentro’s infrastructure delivered an **80%+ reduction in cost** compared to traditional payment gateway models. More importantly, Shiprocket eliminated delayed settlements — giving sellers direct visibility into their money flow in real time, dramatically improving seller trust and retention.


###


Accelerated Merchant Onboarding — MoM Impact


Perhaps the most significant outcome has been on **[merchant onboarding](https://decentro.tech/resources/merchant-onboarding-solutions) velocity** . With Decentro’s streamlined API integration, new eCommerce sellers onboarding onto Shiprocket are now enabled with full UPI PG capabilities significantly faster. This has translated into:


- A **measurable MoM increase in the number of payment-enabled merchant accounts** going live on the platform
- A **year-on-year growth trajectory** in UPI-transacting sellers that mirrors Shiprocket’s overall merchant growth curve
- **Integration timelines reduced by 10X** — from weeks to hours — enabling Shiprocket’s engineering teams to onboard new commerce platform partners without bottlenecks


###


Reconciliation Without the Headache


Decentro’s infrastructure eliminated the reconciliation overhead that had previously burdened Shiprocket’s operations team. With automated ledger matching and real-time payout status updates baked into the API layer, the team shifted focus from fixing discrepancies to driving growth.
