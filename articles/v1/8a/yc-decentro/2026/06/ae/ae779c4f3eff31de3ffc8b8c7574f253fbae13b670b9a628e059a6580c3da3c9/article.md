# Top 10 Razorpay Alternatives in 2026 | Compared

Table of Contents


If you’re running a business in India — whether you’re a startup, a scaling SaaS platform, or an enterprise with global ambitions — Razorpay has probably crossed your radar. It’s one of India’s most recognised payment gateways, and for good reason. But it isn’t the right fit for every business.


In this guide, we break down the **top 10 Razorpay alternatives in 2026** — with pros, cons, features, and market position to help you make the right call.


Razorpay built the category. It gave Indian developers their first truly modern payment gateway — clean APIs, fast onboarding, and a product that actually kept pace with India’s UPI-first payments revolution.


But it isn’t the only answer anymore.


India’s payment infrastructure has matured significantly. The **Indian payment gateway market was valued at USD 2.07 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach USD 4.01 billion by 2031** , growing at a CAGR of 11.66%. There are now excellent alternatives purpose-built for UPI-first India, multi-currency international payments, developer-heavy platforms, and everything in between.


Businesses today have needs that a single gateway cannot satisfy. Some need modular banking infrastructure they can build on top of. Others need multi-currency international collections paired with domestic UPI flows. Many want lower transaction fees, faster settlements, or simply a provider whose product focus matches their use case more precisely.


This listicle covers the **top 10 Razorpay alternatives in India for 2026** — what each one is built for, where it wins, and where it doesn’t. All information is based on publicly verified, current sources.

Frequently Asked Questions


**Q: What is the best alternative to Razorpay in India for 2026?**


There is no single best alternative — it depends on your use case. For API-first fintech and UPI infrastructure, Decentro is a strong choice. For instant settlements, Cashfree stands out. For enterprise bill payments, BillDesk is the incumbent. For the widest international coverage, PayU leads.


**Q: Which payment gateway has the lowest transaction fees in India?**


Easebuzz is among the lowest at approximately 1.5% average per transaction. Cashfree also offers competitive domestic rates from around 1.95%. UPI transactions specifically are zero MDR across most gateways in India under RBI guidelines. Always verify current pricing directly on each gateway’s pricing page, as rates change.


**Q: Does Decentro support international payments?**


Yes. Decentro supports multi-currency international payments through its partnership with Unlimit (announced May 2026), enabling Indian businesses to collect payments from global customers in addition to its core domestic UPI and banking infrastructure.


**Q: Which payment gateways in India have the RBI’s PA-CB (cross-border) license?**


As of mid-2026, companies that have received the PA-CB license include Cashfree Payments (the first, July 2024), Amazon Pay India, BillDesk, Adyen, Worldline, PayGlocal, Pine Labs, Easebuzz, PayU, and Razorpay, among others. Always verify current license status on the[RBI’s official website](https://www.rbi.org.in/) .


**Q: Can I use multiple payment gateways at the same time?**


Yes. Many businesses run two or more gateways in parallel — either for redundancy (routing traffic away from a gateway during downtime) or to support different customer segments. Juspay’s orchestration layer is specifically designed to intelligently manage this kind of multi-gateway routing.


**Q: What should I look for when switching from Razorpay to another gateway?**


Key things to evaluate: supported payment methods (UPI, cards, wallets, BNPL), settlement speed, transaction fees, international payment support, API documentation quality, sandbox access, RBI compliance status (PA/PA-CB licenses), and customer support responsiveness. For platforms and marketplaces, also check split payment and payout capabilities.


**Q: Is Paytm Payment Gateway still reliable in 2026?**


The Paytm Payment Gateway (operated by One97 Communications) continues to function independently from Paytm Payments Bank, which faced regulatory action in early 2024. The gateway business is operational, but businesses evaluating Paytm should monitor ongoing regulatory and business developments before making long-term infrastructure commitments.


**Q: Which gateway is best for UPI collections specifically?**


For pure UPI collection infrastructure with API-level control, Decentro’s[UPI Payment Gateway](https://decentro.tech/products/upi-payment-gateway) is purpose-built for businesses that need programmatic control over UPI flows. For consumer-facing checkout with high UPI success rates, PhonePe for Business and Cashfree are strong options. Juspay’s orchestration layer is the choice for maximizing success rates across multiple acquirers at high volumes.
