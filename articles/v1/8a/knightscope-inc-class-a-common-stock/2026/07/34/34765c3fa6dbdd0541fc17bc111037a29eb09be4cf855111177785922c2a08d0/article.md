# Why Our Security Agents Are Owners

# Why Our Security Agents Are Owners


Knightscope is building the nation’s first Autonomous Security Force for you — the Chief Security Officers of the United States of America. We need to talk about the human beings standing post. This is not about “human-in-the-loop.” The human IS the loop. It is high time that society, communities, the media, analysts — and, in far too many cases, the industry itself — give the person standing guard or walking patrol the support they so desperately need and deserve.


The private security industry has a secret it doesn’t like to say out loud: it burns through people. Constantly. Annual turnover at traditional guarding firms routinely runs 100% to 400%. The person protecting your headquarters in January is statistically unlikely to be there in December — or even April. Minimum wage. Minimal training. No career path. No stake in the outcome. Then everyone acts surprised when quality is inconsistent, posts go unfilled, and incidents get missed.


**You cannot build an elite force on a disposable workforce.**


The military figured this out generations ago. Elite units aren’t elite because of their equipment — they’re elite because of how they select, prepare, support, and invest in their people. We studied that playbook and are running it. Here is how we intend to build a truly elite American security force, in seven deliberate steps — and why the last one is the title of this post.


## 1. Recruit Great People


Elite starts at the front door. We don’t run help-wanted ads and hope. We recruit professionals — many with military, law enforcement, and executive protection backgrounds — who want a mission, not a shift. Our mission is to make the United States of America the safest country in the world. That mission does more recruiting for us than any job board ever will.


## 2. Vet Them Thoroughly


Every Knightscope agent is licensed — armed or unarmed — and screened well beyond the industry’s checkbox minimums. Background, character, judgment, and temperament.


## 3. Train Them


Selection gets you raw material. Training builds the force. Our agents train on protocols, de-escalation, and incident response — and, critically, they are beginning to learn not only how to operate within a technology-driven security operation, but also how to be part of the design process of the technology itself. Who better to shape the tools than the professionals who will stake their safety on them? A Knightscope agent isn’t a body at a post. They will be a trained node in an orchestrated system.


## 4. Arm Them with Unprecedented Technology


Traditional guards receive a flashlight, a logbook, and a #2 pencil. Our Augmented Security Agents will operate alongside autonomous robots, AI-driven software, and purpose-built wearable technology — the H1 ASA, a custom-designed wearable that puts live machine intelligence directly on the agent, built exclusively for our security force. The machines handle the monotony; the humans handle judgment. No security company in America will equip its people like this. That’s not an accident. It is by design.


## 5. Support Them with Supervision and Quality Control


Elite forces never operate alone. Our agents are backed by field supervisors and a dedicated quality control team — and we intend to support them all with 24/7 remote monitoring professionals who watch the same picture they see on the ground, but from a virtual camera in the sky. When something happens at 3:00 a.m., our agent isn’t guessing. They have real-time intelligence, command support, a clear escalation path — and a voice in their ear telling them someone is on their six. Eyes in the back of their head, far better than a head on a swivel.


## 6. Give Them a Uniform They Are Proud to Wear


Details matter. Pride matters. An elite force should look the part. We plan to produce — exclusively for our agents — all-new, professionally designed uniforms for the Knightscope Security Force because how an agent looks shapes how they carry themselves, how the public responds to them, and how much confidence a client feels the moment they walk on site. Gorgeous is not a word this industry uses to describe uniforms. It’s about to be.


## 7. Make Them Owners


Here is the step the entire industry skipped — and the one we believe changes everything.


You can recruit, vet, train, equip, support, and outfit a professional — and the rest of the industry could, in theory, copy every one of those steps. But there is one thing a traditional guarding firm will never do: make the person standing post an owner of the company.


This year, Knightscope extended equity ownership to the frontline of the Autonomous Security Force, twice. In March, our Board’s Compensation Committee approved inducement equity grants to hundreds of employees. In July, we did it again.


Think about what that means. The agent standing post at your facility tonight may hold stock options in a NASDAQ-listed company. When they do their job with excellence, the client wins, the Company wins — and they win, personally, over years, not just paychecks.


Ownership changes behavior. Owners don’t watch the clock. Owners don’t say “not my job.” Owners protect the mission like it’s theirs — because it is. Our culture code says it in two words: Own It. Now our cap table says it, too.


Name another security provider in America whose frontline agents are shareholders. We’ll wait.


## Why This Is the Heart of the Autonomous Security Force


Some people assume “autonomous” means humans are being written out of security. They have it exactly backward.


The Autonomous Security Force works because machines and humans each do what they do best. Robots and AI provide tireless detection and filter out the noise. Humans provide judgment, presence, and response. But that only works if the humans are exceptional — recruited, vetted, trained, equipped, supported, and invested in as the professionals they are.


Fragmented security fails because no one owns the outcome. The ASF wins because everyone does — one provider, one platform, one accountable force. And now, agents who are literally owners of the company stand behind that accountability.


Recruit. Vet. Train. Equip. Support. Look the part. Own it.


That’s how you build an elite force. That’s how you build the nation’s first Autonomous Security Force. That’s why our security agents are owners.


**Security. Handled.**
