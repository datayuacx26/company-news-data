# Types of EV Chargers in India: AC, DC, and Connector Types Explained

If you’re planning to buy an EV, understanding the types of EV chargers in India is one of the first things to figure out.


EV chargers in India are mainly divided into three categories: Level 1 chargers, Level 2 chargers, and DC fast chargers.


The right electric car charger depends on three things:


- charging speed


- power type (AC or DC)


- connector compatibility


Level 1 chargers usually support basic home charging through a household socket.
Level 2 chargers offer faster AC charging for homes, apartments, and workplaces.
DC fast chargers are built for highways, public charging stations, and quick top-ups.


Connector types also matter. In India, common EV charging connectors include **Type 2, CCS2, CHAdeMO, and GB/T** .


This guide explains the different EV charger types in India, how AC and DC charging work, and how to choose the right home EV charger or DC fast charger based on your driving needs.


## **Types of EV Chargers in India Based on Charging Speed**


### **Level 1 EV Chargers (Portable Charging Using Household Sockets)**


Level 1 charging is the simplest form of home EV charging.


This usually means charging from a regular household socket using a portable EV charger.


For many new EV owners in India, this is the first charging setup they use.


Best for:


- daily low-to-moderate driving


- overnight charging


- homes without wallbox installation


In practical terms, this often means using a 15A socket.


Portable chargers make this easy because they do not require permanent installation.

### **Level 2 AC EV Chargers (Faster Home and Workplace Charging)**


Level 2 charging is what most people mean when they talk about installing a proper home EV charger.


This is still AC charging.


But it is much faster than a household socket.

For regular city driving, a Level 2 AC electric car charger is often the most practical choice, offering the right balance of charging speed, cost, and convenience for daily use.


## **Is It Safe and Cost-Effective to Charge an Electric Car at Home?**


For most EV owners, yes. Home charging is usually the most practical long-term option.


It is often cheaper than public charging because residential electricity tariffs are lower. And if charging happens overnight, the car is ready by morning. From a battery perspective, AC charging is also gentler. It generates less heat compared to repeated DC fast charging. That makes it suitable for everyday charging. But safety depends on setup.


Before installing a home EV charger, check:


- dedicated parking availability


- proper earthing


- wiring quality


- power load capacity


- weather protection for outdoor setups


For faster home charging, a wallbox installation is usually better than relying only on a portable charger.


## **DC Fast Chargers Explained**


DC charging is where speed changes everything.


Unlike AC charging, DC battery chargers convert power inside the charger itself and send direct current straight to the battery.


This removes the car’s onboard conversion limitation.


That is why DC fast chargers charge much faster.


These chargers are usually found at:


- highways


- fuel stations


- fleet hubs


- commercial charging stations


Best for:


- long-distance travel


- highway top-ups

- commercial fleets

## **EV Charging Connector Types in India**


Connector compatibility decides whether a charger can physically connect to your EV and whether the charging standard is supported.


**These are the most common EV charging connector types in India:**


### **Type 2**


Type 2 is the most widely used AC charging connector for passenger EVs in India and is the standard for most home, workplace, and public AC charging setups.


### **CCS2**


CCS2 is currently the dominant DC fast charging standard for passenger EVs in India.


Used by many passenger EV brands in India, including:


- Tata


- MG


- BYD


- BMW


- Mercedes-Benz


- Kia


If you use public DC fast chargers regularly, this is the connector you will see most.


### **CHAdeMO**


CHAdeMO is an older DC fast charging standard mainly associated with earlier Japanese EV models. Its presence in India is now limited.


### **GB/T**


GB/T is widely used in China and is still seen in some electric bus fleets, older charging infrastructure, and certain commercial EV applications in India.

## **FAQs**


### **What is the difference between AC and DC EV chargers?**


AC chargers send alternating current, and the vehicle converts it into DC.


DC chargers send direct current directly to the battery, making charging faster.


### **Is home EV charging cheaper than public charging?**


Usually yes.


Home electricity rates are often lower than public fast charging rates.


### **Can I install a DC fast charger at home?**


In most cases, no.


DC fast chargers require high-capacity electrical infrastructure and are better suited for commercial locations.


### **Does fast charging damage EV batteries?**


Occasional fast charging is normal. But relying heavily on DC fast charging every day may increase battery heat over time. AC charging remains better for regular use.


### **Which connector type is most common in India?**


For passenger EVs:


- Type 2 for AC charging


- CCS2 for DC fast charging


These are the most common standards.
