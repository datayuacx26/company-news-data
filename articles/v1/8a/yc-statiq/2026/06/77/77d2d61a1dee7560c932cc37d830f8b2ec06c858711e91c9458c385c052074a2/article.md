# How Can You Extend EV Battery Life? 7 Simple Habits EV Owners Should Follow

EV batteries don’t suddenly start losing range. It usually happens little by little.


A few charging habits. Too much heat. Skipping some of the basics.


Over time, they can affect EV battery health and overall performance.


The good news is that most of these habits are easy to fix.


Here are a few simple ways to keep your EV battery life and battery health in better shape for the long run.


## **Habit 1: Are You Keeping Your EV Charge Between 20% and 80%?**


A lot of EV owners feel better seeing 100% on the dashboard.


For everyday driving, keeping your charge somewhere between 20% and 80% puts less stress on the battery cells. Charging to 100% before a long road trip? Perfectly fine.


Doing it every night when you only drive 30 or 40 kilometres the next day? Not necessary.


Most electric cars let you set a charging limit. Once you do it, you barely think about it again.


Small habit. Big difference over time.


## **Habit 2: Are You Protecting Your EV Battery From Extreme Heat?**


Heat is one of the biggest enemies of EV battery health.


Not one hot afternoon.


Repeated exposure.


If you’ve parked after a long drive on a hot day, you’ve probably noticed the car working to cool itself down.


That’s normal.


What you want to avoid is adding more heat when the battery is already hot.


Park in the shade when you can. Use covered parking if it’s available.


Just finished a long drive in peak summer? Give the battery some time to cool down before plugging in.


**Warm is normal. Very hot is not.**


## **Habit 3: Are You Using AC Charging for Everyday Charging?**


Fast DC charging is convenient.


Road trips. Tight schedules. Unexpected travel plans. Use it when you need it.


For everyday charging, AC charging is usually the better option.


It puts less strain on the battery and generates less heat during charging.


If most of your charging happens at home, you’re already doing your battery a favour.


Fast charging once in a while isn’t a problem.


Using it for every top-up isn’t ideal.


Whether you’re charging at home or using public charging networks like **[Statiq](https://www.statiq.in/)** , the goal remains the same: use fast charging when you need it and AC charging for everyday use whenever possible.


If you’re wondering how much impact fast charging actually has on battery health, you can also read our detailed guide on ***[whether DC fast charging damages EV battery life](https://www.statiq.in/blog/2026/04/13/does-dc-fast-charging-damage-ev-battery-life/)** .*


## **Habit 4: Are You Driving in a Way That Reduces Battery Stress?**


Driving habits play a bigger role in EV battery life than many people realize.


Frequent hard acceleration, sudden braking, and constant high-speed driving increase the load on the battery.


The same goes for regularly carrying heavy loads or towing. The harder the vehicle has to work, the more energy the battery needs to deliver.


One or two occasions won’t make much difference. It’s the repeated habit that adds up over time.


A smoother driving style helps improve efficiency, maintain range, and reduce unnecessary strain on the battery.


## **Habit 5: Are You Keeping Your EV Software Updated?**


Most updates aren’t about adding new features.


Many of them improve how the battery charges, cools, and manages energy behind the scenes.


Manufacturers regularly release updates to improve charging performance, battery management, and overall efficiency.


If your EV supports automatic updates, it’s worth keeping them enabled.


## **Habit 6: How Should You Store an EV That Won’t Be Driven for Weeks?**


Not every EV is driven every day. Before leaving it unused for an extended period, check the storage recommendations for your specific EV model.


One thing most manufacturers agree on: avoid leaving the battery at 100% for long periods.


The opposite isn’t ideal either. Parking it with a nearly empty battery can create its own problems.


A charge level somewhere around the middle is usually a safer place to leave it if the vehicle won’t be used for a while.


If you’re planning to store the car for several weeks or longer, it’s worth spending a few minutes checking the manufacturer’s guidelines first.


A little preparation before parking can help avoid unnecessary battery stress later.


## **Habit 7: How Can Proper Tire Maintenance Help EV Battery Life?**


When tire pressure is too low, the vehicle needs more energy to move. That puts additional strain on the battery and can reduce driving range.


Checking tire pressure regularly is a simple habit that many EV owners overlook.


It’s especially worth checking when the weather changes, as temperature shifts can affect tire pressure.


Extremely high tire pressure isn’t ideal either. It can make the ride less comfortable and affect overall handling.


Keeping your tires properly inflated helps your EV run more efficiently and reduces unnecessary load on the battery.


It’s also worth checking the overall condition of your tires. Good tire maintenance supports both battery performance and driving efficiency.


## **What Is the Best Way to Improve EV Battery Life?**


EV battery health isn’t determined by a single charge or a single drive.


It’s the result of small decisions repeated over time.


None of these habits take much effort.


Over the years, they can make a noticeable difference to battery performance, driving range, and overall ownership experience.


EV batteries are built to last.


A few good habits simply help them stay that way.


## **Frequently Asked Questions About EV Battery Life**


#### **How Long Does EV Battery Life Typically Last?**


Most EV batteries are designed to last between 8 and 15 years. Many continue to perform well beyond that, depending on driving habits, charging patterns, climate, and overall battery maintenance.


#### **What Reduces EV Battery Life the Fastest?**


Frequent exposure to extreme heat, regularly charging to 100%, allowing the battery to remain at very low charge levels, and relying heavily on DC fast charging can contribute to faster battery degradation over time.


#### **Does Fast Charging Affect EV Battery Life?**


Occasional DC fast charging is part of normal EV ownership. However, using fast charging for most charging sessions can generate additional heat, which may affect EV battery life in the long run.


#### **What Is the Best Charging Percentage for EV Battery Life?**


For daily use, many manufacturers recommend keeping the battery between 20% and 80%. This helps reduce unnecessary stress on the battery and supports long-term EV battery health.


#### **Does Hot Weather Impact EV Battery Life?**


Yes. Repeated exposure to high temperatures can affect battery performance and accelerate battery degradation. Parking in shaded areas and avoiding charging immediately after a long drive in extreme heat can help.


#### **How Can You Tell If EV Battery Life Is Declining?**


A noticeable drop in driving range, slower charging performance, or changes in battery efficiency can be signs of battery degradation. Monitoring your EV’s battery health data can help you spot changes early.


#### **Does Driving Style Affect EV Battery Life?**


Yes. Frequent hard acceleration, aggressive braking, and constant high-speed driving can increase the load on the battery. A smoother driving style can help improve efficiency and support long-term EV battery health.
