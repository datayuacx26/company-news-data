# Statiq EV Charging Network: Complete Guide to India’s Growing EV Charging Ecosystem

***Have you ever thought about charging uncertainty before buying an EV?***
Where will you charge? Will the charger be available when you get there? What if it’s occupied, offline, or too far from your route?


For many EV buyers, these questions come up early because charging still feels less predictable than traditional refuelling.


With petrol cars, fuel stations are familiar. They’re everywhere, and you rarely think twice before starting a trip.


EV charging changes that habit. As a result, you start planning differently. Battery percentage starts influencing your route, and long drives need more thought than before. That’s where a strong EV charging network starts to matter, because accessibility directly affects how confidently people drive.


Because EV adoption doesn’t grow through vehicle sales alone. It grows through reliable EV charging infrastructure that people can trust.


That’s the problem Statiq set out to solve.


What started in 2020 as an effort to make EV charging simpler has now grown into one of the largest EV charging networks in India, supporting journeys across cities, highways, homes, fleets, and businesses.


Here’s a complete guide to the Statiq EV charging network — how it works, what it offers, and why it has become an important part of India’s growing EV ecosystem.


## **When Was Statiq Founded?**


Statiq was founded in **2020** with a clear mission: to build a reliable and accessible EV charging network for India.


The company was incubated by **NASSCOM** and secured **$2.5 million in seed funding from Y Combinator** in its early stage.


Since then, Statiq has expanded steadily across hardware, software, and public charging infrastructure, while strengthening its presence across India.


Some key milestones include:


- **2021** — Recognised with the WWF Climate Solver Award for climate innovation


- **2022** — Raised $25.7 million in Series A funding led by Shell


- **2023** — Expanded its EV charging network across 60+ cities in India


- **2024** — Expanded globally into UAE and Nepal and secured ARAI CAT certification for in-house hardware


- **2025** — Expanded to 8,000+ chargers across 100+ cities in India


Today, Statiq operates **10,000+ EV chargers across 1,014+ cities** , making it one of the largest EV charging networks in India.


## **Statiq’s Growing EV Charging Network Across India**

## **What Features Does the Statiq EV Charging App Offer?**


A charging network is only useful when drivers can actually access it easily.


That’s where the[Statiq EV charging app](https://www.statiq.in/ev-charging-app) becomes useful.


It helps EV users find, reserve, charge, and pay — all from one place.


Here’s what it offers:


### **Find Nearby EV Charging Stations**


The app helps you locate nearby chargers with live status updates.


You can filter stations based on:


- connector type


- charging speed


- location


- availability


This makes it easier to find the right charger for your EV without unnecessary trial and error.


### **Check Real-Time Charger Availability**


Before you drive to a station, you can see whether a charger is:


- available


- occupied


- inactive


This reduces charging uncertainty and saves time.


### **Reserve Chargers in Advance**


One of the more practical features is advance booking.


Users can reserve charging slots and connectors up to seven days in advance.


This adds more predictability, especially during busy hours or long-distance travel.


### **Plan EV Road Trips Better**


The built-in route planner helps map charging stops across highways and city routes.


For EV owners planning longer drives, this makes route planning simpler and reduces range anxiety.


### **Secure Payments Inside the App**


Payments can be made directly through the app using the Statiq wallet and other supported payment options.


Users can also track:


- charging costs


- transaction history


- session details


from one dashboard.


### **Statiq Flux for Stable Charging**


To improve charging consistency, Statiq Flux helps reduce power sharing during active charging sessions. This allows EV users to experience more stable charging speeds with fewer interruptions.


### **Statiq Miles Membership**


For frequent EV users,[Statiq Miles](https://www.statiq.in/miles) offers additional benefits such as:


- 10% off on charging sessions


- 5% coin cashback


- priority support


- exclusive offers and events


It’s designed for users who charge regularly and want better savings.


### **24×7 Customer Support**


Charging support, payment issues, reservations, and app assistance are available round the clock.


That matters when charging becomes part of daily travel.

Statiq is not only built for EV users. It also supports businesses looking to enter the EV charging space through different ownership models.
