### AI Insights FAQs


What is AI Insights?


AI Insights automatically analyzes your business conversations to surface key trends, customer
sentiment, and performance metrics—helping you make smarter decisions faster.


What types of insights can AI Insights provide?


AI Insights highlights things like appointment requests, missed calls, average wait times,
cancellations, new customers, and top-performing team members.


Can AI Insights show what customers are calling about?


Yes. It identifies common topics and categories such as billing, support, scheduling, account
management, and new business inquiries.


How does AI Insights track customer sentiment?


AI Insights evaluates conversations to give you a clear snapshot of customer sentiment—positive,
neutral, or negative—across your calls.


Can I track call volume and performance trends?


Absolutely. You can monitor call volumes, missed and urgent calls, and identify peak call times
to better manage staffing and operations.


Can I ask questions about my call data?


Yes. You can use natural language to ask questions like “What are customers calling about most
this week?” or “Show me frustrated callers.”


What are action highlights?


Action highlights surface key follow-ups, such as calls needing callbacks, unresolved issues, or
service-impacting conversations—so nothing slips through the cracks.


Do I need to review every call manually?


No. AI Insights automatically analyzes conversations and surfaces what matters most, saving you
significant time.


How can AI Insights improve my business outcomes?


By turning conversations into clear, actionable data, AI Insights helps you improve customer
experience, optimize operations, and make more informed decisions.


Is my data secure?


Your data is stored securely, and access is controlled within your private knowledge base and
integrations.


What does AI Insights cost?


AI Insights is included in Ooma Office Pro Plus plans at no additional cost.
