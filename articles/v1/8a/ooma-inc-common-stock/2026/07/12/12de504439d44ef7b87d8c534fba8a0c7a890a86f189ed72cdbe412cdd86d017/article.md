## Real-world ways teams use AI Answering Service and AI Receptionist.


Here are some examples of how our customers use AI Answering Service
and
AI Receptionist for their business.


- Healthcare
- Retail
- Property Management


### Private Healthcare Practice


(5-10 Employees)


#### Challenge:


A busy three-physician medical practice struggles to answer every patient call during
peak hours. Front-desk staff are overwhelmed, hold times increase, and appointment
requests are sometimes missed after hours.


#### Solution:


Ooma AI Receptionist is deployed to automatically greet callers, answer common
questions (hours, location, insurance accepted), route calls to the appropriate
staff
member, and capture appointment requests 24/7.


#### Result:


- Fewer missed calls and reduced hold times
- Front-desk staff freed up for in-office patients
- Improved patient satisfaction with always-on responsiveness


### Regional Multi-location Retail Chain


(50-75 Employees)


#### Challenge:


A 25-location retail chain receives high call volumes across stores for inventory
checks, store hours, and return policies. Store associates spend significant time
answering repetitive questions, pulling them away from customers on the sales
floor.


#### Solution:


The company implements Ooma AI Answering Service to provide instant answers to common
inquiries and intelligently route calls to the correct store or department when
needed.


#### Result:


- Consistent customer experience across all locations
- Reduced call burden on store associates
- Faster response times, improving in-store service and sales focus


### National Portfolio Property Management Firm


(200+ Employees)


#### Challenge:


A national property management firm overseeing hundreds of residential and commercial
properties struggles with inconsistent call handling. Leasing inquiries, maintenance
requests, and tenant questions overwhelm on-site teams and create delays.


#### Solution:


The enterprise rolls out Ooma AI Receptionist across all properties to standardize
call answering, route leasing calls to centralized teams, log maintenance requests
automatically, and provide after-hours support.


#### Result:


- Unified, professional call experience across all sites
- Faster routing of high-priority inquiries
- Improved operational efficiency at scale
- Higher tenant and prospect satisfaction
