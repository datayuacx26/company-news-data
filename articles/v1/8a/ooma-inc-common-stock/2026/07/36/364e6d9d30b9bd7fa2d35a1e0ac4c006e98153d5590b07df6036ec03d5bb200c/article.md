### AI Answering Service FAQ


What is the key difference between AI Answering Service & AI Receptionist?


AI Answering Service is best for businesses that need a simple, lightweight solution. It
answers a handful of common questions and takes detailed messages for quick follow up.


AI Receptionist is best for businesses that need a more capable, full-service
experience.
It can transfer calls, book appointments, handle complex routing, and answer a much
wider
range of questions.


What is AI Answering Service?


Ooma’s AI Answering Service is a human-like voice agent that answers missed calls during
or after business hours. It can collect caller details, answer basic questions about your
business, and identifies urgent calls. Every interaction is recorded, transcribed, and
summarized, with notifications sent via SMS or email. You can manage AI Answering Service
and review call logs anytime in the Ooma Office admin portal.


What can AI Answering Service do?


It answers calls, gathers caller information, understands the reason for the call,
answers common questions, flags urgency, and sends notifications. Each call includes a
recording, transcription, and summary.


How easy is it to get started?


Setup takes just a few minutes. Add your greeting, business details, and FAQs, and AI
Answering Service is ready to start answering calls.


How much does it cost?


Plans start at $14.99/month and include up to 40 minutes of call time. The service can be
added to any Ooma Office plan.


When does AI Answering Service answer calls?


You can choose when the AI answers calls during business hours, after hours, or only when
you miss a call. It works as a flexible backup so you never miss an opportunity.


Can I customize the greetings and responses that AI Answering Service uses?


Yes. You can customize your greeting, provide business details, and add FAQs so the AI
responds in a way that reflects your brand.


What kinds of questions can the AI answer?


The AI can answer common questions about your business, such as hours, services,
location, and policies, based on the information you provide.


Do I need technical expertise to set it up?


No. Setup is simple and guided. You just enter your business details, create a greeting,
and add FAQs with no technical skills required.


Are calls secure and private?


Yes. Call recordings, transcriptions, and customer information are securely stored and
only accessible through your account.


### AI Receptionist FAQs

What is AI Receptionist?


AI Receptionist is a virtual receptionist for your business that answers calls, routes
customers, answers questions, and schedules appointments 24/7.


What can AI Receptionist do?


AI Receptionist can:


- Answer calls during and after business hours
- Route calls intelligently
- Schedule appointments
- Answer questions about your business
- Capture caller information
- Send call summaries and notifications
- Support multiple languages
- Send links and URLs to customers


How does appointment scheduling work?


AI Receptionist can schedule appointments through Ooma Online Booking Pages.


The booking system integrates with:


- Google Calendar
- Microsoft Calendar


This allows callers to book available appointment times directly during the call.


Can AI Receptionist transfer calls?


Yes. AI Receptionist can intelligently route calls to the right person, department, or
destination based on your business rules and call flows.


Can I customize the greeting and experience?


Yes. Businesses can customize:


- Greetings
- Voice options
- Routing behavior
- Business information
- Call handling rules


What information can the AI collect from callers?


AI Receptionist can collect:


- Caller name
- Phone number
- Reason for calling
- Best callback time
- Appointment details
- Additional business-specific information


Does AI Receptionist work after business hours?


Yes. AI Receptionist is designed to provide 24/7 call coverage, including after-hours
support.


Can AI Receptionist handle multiple calls at the same time?


Yes. AI Receptionist can manage multiple inbound conversations simultaneously without
placing customers on hold waiting for staff availability.


Can AI Receptionist be used during business hours?


Yes. AI Receptionist can support businesses during business hours by answering calls,
routing customers, scheduling appointments, and handling overflow traffic when teams are
busy.


Is AI Receptionist included with my Ooma plan?


No. AI Receptionist is an add-on service that can be added to any eligible Ooma tier.
This gives businesses the flexibility to add AI-powered call handling, routing, and
scheduling capabilities without changing their existing phone system plan.
