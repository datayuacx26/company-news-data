# Ibotta to Participate in Upcoming Investor Conferences


May 08, 2026 9:00 am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_7a2d4abc4b8007452e7e541bd4c767c1/ibotta/news/2026-05-08_Ibotta_to_Participate_in_Upcoming_Investor_145.pdf)


DENVER--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Ibotta, Inc. (NYSE: IBTA), the performance marketing platform for promotions, announced today that company executives will participate in the following investor events:


- On May 13, 2026 at 10:30 a.m. PT/1:30 p.m. ET, Chief Financial Officer Matt Puckett will participate in a fireside chat at the 21st Annual Needham Technology, Media, & Consumer Conference in New York. A webcast of the event will be available[here](https://event.summitcast.com/view/aZxVFEntdXZooQDEsVxUoT/UACP9CyGB8YajgidZ6UPcR) .
- On June 2, 2026 at 3 p.m. PT/6 p.m. ET, Chief Revenue Officer Chris Riedy will participate in a fireside chat at the 2026 Evercore TMT Global Conference in San Francisco. A webcast of the event will be available[here](https://event.summitcast.com/view/27L5UE5yiEyoGqKLrv7Uxe/invD2dMkoi8a92jcsAt7Rv) .


Audio webcasts will also be available on the investor relations section of the Ibotta website at[investors.ibotta.com](http://investors.ibotta.com/) .


**About Ibotta (“I bought a…”)**


Ibotta (NYSE: IBTA) is the leading provider of digital promotions for CPG brands, reaching over 200 million consumers through a network of publishers called the Ibotta Performance Network (IPN). The IPN allows marketers to influence what people buy, and where and how often they shop – all while paying only when their campaigns directly result in a sale. American shoppers have earned over $2.9 billion through the IPN since 2012. Ibotta is headquartered in Denver and has been listed as a top place to work by The Denver Post and Inc. Magazine.
