# Ibotta CEO to Present at The Citizens Technology Conference


February 12, 2026 4:15 pm EST[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_7a2d4abc4b8007452e7e541bd4c767c1/ibotta/news/2026-02-12_Ibotta_CEO_to_Present_at_The_Citizens_Technology_137.pdf)


DENVER--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Ibotta (NYSE: IBTA), the performance marketing platform for promotions, announced today that CEO and founder, Bryan Leach, will participate in a fireside chat at The Citizens Technology Conference in San Francisco on Tues., March 3 at 1:30 pm PT.


Audio webcasts of the event will be available on the investor relations section of the Ibotta website at[investors.ibotta.com](http://investors.ibotta.com/) .


**About Ibotta (“I bought a…”)**


Ibotta (NYSE: IBTA) is the leading provider of digital promotions for CPG brands, reaching over 200 million consumers through a network of publishers called the Ibotta Performance Network. The Ibotta Performance Network allows marketers to influence what people buy, and where and how often they shop – all while paying only when their campaigns directly result in a sale. American shoppers have earned over $2.6 billion through the Ibotta Performance Network since 2012. Ibotta is headquartered in Denver and has been listed as a top place to work by The Denver Post and Inc. Magazine.
