# Burke & Herbert Bank & Trust Company Deepens Commitment to Maryland with New Bethesda Branch

Alexandria, VA – Burke & Herbert Bank & Trust Company (Burke & Herbert), a wholly owned subsidiary of Burke & Herbert Financial Services Corp., today announced the opening of a full-service branch at 7815 Woodmont Avenue in Bethesda, Maryland. The branch joins Burke & Herbert’s commercial lending office which began operating in Bethesda in 2021. This expansion in Montgomery County supports Burke & Herbert’s strategic vision of providing superior quality financial services to families, businesses, and organizations across the region.


Located in downtown Bethesda, the full-service branch brings Burke & Herbert’s signature personal service within easy reach for the vibrant, busy community, and surrounding area. By adding a modern retail space with the commercial office in a bustling area, Burke & Herbert can more effectively meet the needs of new and existing clients.


The branch will be led by Dee Senarathna, a banking veteran with over twenty-seven years of experience in financial services with more than ten of those in branch management. Senarathna holds an associate degree in business with a specialization in Sales and Customer Development from the Open University of Sri Lanka. “I am looking forward to meeting the financial needs of businesses and families throughout Montgomery County,” said Senarathna, “and providing service beyond expectations to each customer who comes through our doors.”


“I could not be more pleased,” commented Regional President Michael Solomon. “The commercial team and I are looking forward to building a presence in the Maryland market that surpasses all expectations. Having a great location in the heart of Bethesda and a first-class retail team supporting us allows us to deliver the finest financial service in the entire area.”


“Our expansion in Maryland is about growth and continuity — combining a retail branch with a strong commercial team led by Michael Solomon underscores our commitment to being a quintessential community bank,” said Burke & Herbert’s Chief Executive Officer David Boyle. “For more than 173 years, our mission has been to help our clients thrive. We’re excited to bring our legacy of client-focused banking to Montgomery County.”


Headquartered in Alexandria, Virginia, Burke & Herbert Bank & Trust Company expanded its retail operations to Maryland in 2024, following a merger that included nine branches on the state’s Eastern Shore. Today, Burke & Herbert’s branch network consists of almost 80 branches across Maryland, Virginia, Delaware, West Virginia, and Kentucky. Burke & Herbert is also a member of the Allpoint Network where customers may access ATMs across the nation surcharge-free.


**About Burke & Herbert**


Burke & Herbert Financial Services Corp. is the financial holding company for Burke & Herbert Bank & Trust Company. Burke & Herbert Bank & Trust Company is the oldest continuously operating bank under its original name headquartered in the greater Washington, D.C. metropolitan area. With over 75 branches across Delaware, Kentucky, Maryland, Virginia, and West Virginia, Burke & Herbert Bank & Trust Company offers a full range of business and personal financial solutions designed to meet customers’ banking, borrowing, and investment needs.
