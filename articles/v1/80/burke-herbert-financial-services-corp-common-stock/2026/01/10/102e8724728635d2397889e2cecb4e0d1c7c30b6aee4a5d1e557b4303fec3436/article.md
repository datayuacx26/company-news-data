# Burke & Herbert Financial Services Corp. Announces Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Results and Declares Common Stock Dividend

Alexandria, VA, January 22, 2026 – Burke & Herbert Financial Services Corp. (the “Company” or “Burke & Herbert”)
(Nasdaq: BHRB) reported financial results for the quarter and the year ended December 31, 2025. In
addition, at its meeting on January 22, 2026, the board of directors declared a $0.55 per share regular cash
dividend to be paid on March 2, 2026, to shareholders of record as of the close of business on
February 13, 2026.
