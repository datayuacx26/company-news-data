# More choice and familiar convenience: Kleinanzeigen integrates Klarna Pay in full | Klarna International

**Berlin - July 22, 2026** Kleinanzeigen is expanding its popular "Sicher bezahlen" (Secure Payment) feature to include Klarna Pay in full. With this integration, the familiar direct bank transfer returns to the platform, enabling users to handle online purchases even more flexibly, seamlessly and securely.


**More flexibility at checkout**


The introduction of Klarna Pay Now is a meaningful addition to the payment options on Kleinanzeigen, giving users considerably more freedom of choice at checkout. Fully embedded in the familiar checkout, it keeps the path from find to purchase quick and seamless.


**Trusted buyer protection remains the standard**


The proven framework stays unchanged: using Klarna as a payment method incurs no additional fees for users; the platform's regular buyer protection fee applies. And should something not go as planned in a transaction, the buyer protection process via Kleinanzeigen also remains exactly as before.


**Clarity through focus**


This launch focuses purely on instant payment (Pay in full). Services such as deferred payments (e.g. Klarna Pay in 30 days), instalments or financing are not part of this offering.
