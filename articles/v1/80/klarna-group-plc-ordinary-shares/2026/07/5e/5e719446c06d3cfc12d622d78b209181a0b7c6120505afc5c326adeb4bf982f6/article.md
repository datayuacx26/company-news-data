# Klarna sinks its teeth into dental care with Curve Dental®, bringing flexible payments to practices nationwide | Klarna International

**New York, July 14, 2026** — Klarna, the global digital bank and flexible payments provider, announced today that it is now live with leading dental practice management platform


[Curve Dental](http://www.curvedental.com/)


®, giving patients a more transparent and modern way to pay for care directly at the point of treatment.


Powered by Stripe and available through


[Curve Pay](https://go.curvedental.com/curvepay)


, Klarna gives dental practices using Curve a seamless way to offer eligible patients flexible payment plans including pay in full, pay in four interest-free installments, and longer-term financing. Klarna pays the practice the full outstanding amount upfront, and the patient repays Klarna over time. Unlike traditional revolving credit, Klarna plans show the total cost upfront with a clear end date, giving patients a smarter way to manage the cost of care.


"Dental practices deserve a payment experience that matches the quality of care they deliver," said David Sykes, Chief Commercial Officer at Klarna. “Curve Pay puts Klarna directly inside the tools dental practices already run on, modernizing how tens of thousands of providers get paid while giving patients a real choice in how they structure their payments.”


“Dental care is personal, and the payment experience should feel just as clear, modern and patient-friendly,” said Scotti Mazikowski, Head of Payments at Curve Dental. “By bringing Klarna into Curve Pay, we’re helping practices offer more flexible payment options directly within the workflows they already use every day. It’s another important step in our strategy to make Curve the connected operating system for every part of the practice — from clinical care to financial performance.”
