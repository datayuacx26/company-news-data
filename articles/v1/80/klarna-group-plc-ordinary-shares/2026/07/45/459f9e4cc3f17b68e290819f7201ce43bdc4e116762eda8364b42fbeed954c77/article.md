# Klarna now available on Fragrance.com, bringing flexible payments to one of America's largest online beauty destinations | Klarna International

**New York, July 15, 2026 -** Klarna, the global digital bank and flexible payments provider, is now available at checkout on Fragrance.com, a beauty destination with over 40,000 products across fragrances, skincare, makeup, haircare, and candles. The integration is powered by Adyen.


Fragrance and beauty purchases are considered, often personal: shoppers research, compare, and return to the same brands season after season. Through this new partnership, Klarna gives Fragrance.com customers the flexibility to split purchases into four interest-free installments, finance larger orders, and earn cashback on qualifying purchases.


"Fragrance.com has spent nearly three decades making premium beauty accessible, and that mission aligns with everything Klarna stands for," said David Sykes, Chief Commercial Officer at Klarna. "Beauty shoppers are loyal and intentional, they deserve payment options that match how they actually shop. We're proud to bring Klarna’s flexible payment options to one of the category's most trusted retailers."


"We're constantly looking at where we can remove friction, in discovery, in browsing, and at checkout,” said Michael Nadboy, Chief Marketing Officer at Fragrance.com. “Our customers deserve an end-to-end experience that feels effortless, and offering Klarna is strengthening that experience, helping us to ensure every touchpoint lives up to the products we carry."
