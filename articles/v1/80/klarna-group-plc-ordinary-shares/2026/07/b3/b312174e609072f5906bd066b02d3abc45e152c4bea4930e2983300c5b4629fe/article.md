# Vibrant Planet and WARSI win the Klarna Climate Heroes Award and $50,000 each for their proven impact in protecting forest carbon | Klarna International

**Klarna** , the global digital bank and flexible payments provider, today announced


**Vibrant Planet** and


**KKI WARSI** as the winners of the


**Klarna Climate Heroes Award** . The award recognises climate solutions that deliver measurable impact that punches far above its weight in terms of budget and operational size.


The winners were announced at Blue Earth's Innovation Hub during London Climate Action Week on June 24.


Wildfire has long been treated as something to survive, not solve. Vibrant Planet is changing that. The US-based company has built an AI platform that brings together wildfire risk modeling, land treatment planning, and carbon measurement, turning what used to take government agencies years of manual work into something that happens in months. Peer-reviewed research on these treatment methods has shown they can cut high-severity wildfire risk by as much as 88%*. That matters beyond the flames: wildfire accounts for several gigatonnes of CO2 released every year globally, more than aviation.


For decades, protecting tropical forests has depended on the commitment of the communities living within them, yet those same communities have rarely had the legal standing to act. KKI WARSI is changing that. The Indonesia-based organization has spent more than 30 years helping Indigenous and local communities secure the legal rights needed to manage and protect their forests from deforestation. The result is 324,000 hectares of community forest protected across more than 200 communities, and tens of millions of tonnes of carbon protected. That matters beyond the forest edge: tropical deforestation accounts for roughly 10% of global emissions every year, yet community-led governance remains one of the most underfunded levers available to address it.


The Klarna Climate Heroes Award is designed to spotlight organisations that have moved beyond pilots and into real-world delivery, turning climate ambition into exponential effect.


"


*Great ideas are a vital foundation for climate progress, but real impact ultimately comes from execution* ," said


**Alexander Farsan, Head of Climate and Environment at Klarna** . "


*With the Klarna Climate Heroes Award, we celebrate and support changemakers like Vibrant Planet and KKI WARSI that are already delivering impact at scale, empowering them to go further* ."


**From potential to proven impact**


Klarna's broader climate initiatives, the Climate Transformation Fund and the AI for Climate Resilience Program, aim to identify and scale innovations with strong future potential, often supporting organisations as they move from pilot to scalable implementation.


The Klarna Climate Heroes Award, by contrast, recognises what has already been achieved but perhaps not highlighted in its own right.


**Global reach and strong demand**


The award attracted applications from organisations worldwide. After a rigorous selection process, the jury chose two winners, each representing a distinct and proven approach to some of climate's most urgent challenges.


"


*Vibrant Planet and WARSI bring two completely different answers to the same pressing problem, forests becoming an increasing source of CO2 emissions. One awardee uses advanced software to help institutions target resources where they are most effective at preventing wildfire emissions. The other has spent three decades putting Indigenous communities at the centre of forest stewardship to reduce deforestation* ," said


**Robert Hogland, jury member and Head of Climate at Milkywire** .


Eligible organisations included any mission-driven entity working on climate solutions, across sectors and geographies.


**Rigorous, impact-first selection criteria**


Submissions were evaluated based on: demonstrated climate impact supported by data; progress beyond pilot stage; ability to challenge conventional approaches; credibility and third-party validation; under-recognised impact; design for scalability; and a clear plan to accelerate further impact. Impact was assessed relative to each organisation's size and resources to ensure a level playing field.


**Accelerating what already works**


As global emissions continue to rise, scaling proven solutions remains critical. The Klarna Climate Heroes Award is designed to do exactly that: identify what works, and help it grow.


The award is delivered by Klarna in partnership with Milkywire, with the winner announced at Blue Earth during London Climate Action Week.
