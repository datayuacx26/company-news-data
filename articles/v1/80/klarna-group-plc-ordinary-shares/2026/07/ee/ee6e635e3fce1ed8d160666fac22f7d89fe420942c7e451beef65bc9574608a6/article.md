# Klarna celebrates 5 years of growth, innovation and partnerships in France | Klarna International

**Klarna** , the global digital bank and flexible payments provider, today marks five years since its official launch in France. What began in June 2021 as the introduction of Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) has evolved into something far broader: a full digital banking experience that has made Klarna a household name in France.


**Five years in figures**


Since entering the French market, Klarna has achieved significant milestones.


**7 million consumers in France** have used Klarna in the past 12 months.


**57,500 merchant partners** , spanning categories from fashion and beauty, where Klarna first made its mark in France, to electronics, travel, sports, home goods, and beyond.


**EUR 6.2 billion GMV over the past 5 years** .


**2.9 million monthly active users on the Klarna app in France** , rated 4.7 on the App Store and 4.5 on Google Play, making it one of the most trusted shopping and finance apps in the market.


"


*France has always been a strategically important market for Klarna. Over the past five years, we have seen French consumers and merchants embrace a new way of thinking about payments, one that is flexible, transparent, and built around their needs* ," says


**Clémence Le Floch, Country Manager France at Klarna** .


**From a hero product to a full digital bank**


Klarna entered France with a clear proposition: give consumers the freedom to split purchases into three interest-free instalments, with no hidden fees. This resonated deeply in France, where consumers have long been wary of the hidden costs and revolving debt associated with credit cards and short-term loans. Klarna offered a third way: the flexibility of credit, with the transparency and peace of mind of debit.


Five years on, that single product has become a full digital banking offering. French consumers can open a Klarna balance account in minutes, grow their savings, earn cashback on every purchase, and pay anywhere, online or in-store, with a flexible debit card that works wherever Visa is accepted. The BNPL roots are still very much alive, with instalment payments continuing to drive millions of transactions, but they are now just one part of a much bigger picture.


**Powering growth for French retailers**


Klarna's success in France has been built hand-in-hand with its merchant partners. Today, some of France's best-known brand names across fashion, home, electronics, and travel offer Klarna at checkout, from Cdiscount, Center Parcs and Veepee to Vestiaire Collective, Yves Rocher and Courir. These partnerships reflect a shared belief that giving consumers more payment choice directly drives conversion and customer satisfaction.


"


*Five years in, France is no longer a new market for us, it is a home market. We have built deep relationships with retailers across every sector, and we are seeing French consumers use Klarna not just as a payment tool, but as part of how they discover and shop* ," adds


**Clémence Le Floch** .


**Innovation at the heart of what is next**


As Klarna looks ahead to its next five years in France, customer-centric innovation is at the centre of its roadmap. The Klarna app now uses AI to deliver personalised product recommendations, compare prices, and provide a conversational shopping assistant that helps consumers find what they are looking for.


Globally, Klarna now serves over 119 million active consumers and over one million merchant partners, generating USD 127 billion in gross merchandise volume in 2025. In France, the company sees ample room to deepen its presence, particularly as the BNPL market continues to grow year on year and French consumers increasingly turn to digital banking for greater transparency and lower costs.
