# 17 months since melding into Medline, Minnesota team is kin in caring for skin


Manufacturing center in North Mankato, once part of Coloplast, still makes most of its clinician-loved products, which are now being prepped for the Medline Remedy© portfolio


Medline means a lot of things to a lot of people in and around healthcare: High-quality products and solutions at the right value. Superior supply chain resiliency. Tireless problem-solving. Continuous improvements for customers that come from listening closely to their needs, concerns and product preferences.


But for years, **Matt Nuytten’s** team of more than 100 in North Mankato, Minn., saw Medline as something else — its biggest rival.


“You’re always trained to fight against your competitor,” said Nuytten (pictured, left), who came to work for Coloplast in 2003 at its 108,000-square-foot manufacturing plant in the heart of town and became the facility’s director of operations four years later — the role he still holds there today. “You’re very passionate about your brand.”


So are clinicians around the country who swear by legacy Coloplast skin care products — the Sween 24 line of creams and moisturizers, the moisture-barrier ointment Critic-Aid and an assortment of Atrac-tain creams, lotions and foams, to name a few. Denmark-based Coloplast acquired the North Mankato plant from local manufacturer Sween Corp. in 1995 and relied on it to manufacture the bulk of its skin health portfolio for nearly 30 years.


That changed in 2024, when Medline completed its acquisition of Coloplast’s skin care business. Before Nuytten and product development manager **Keenan Deutsch** knew it, they were talking strategy as the newest members of the Medline team.


Quite a shift, huh?


“It’s a very unique situation,” said Deutsch (pictured, right). “Medline’s Remedy products had been positioned against the products we make here. When you have two organizations that have been competing against each other directly for years, and in this case a couple of decades, getting integrated is a special experience.”


## NorthMankato-Bottles2


About 100 employees in North Mankato have brought their Coloplast experience to Medline.


#### Best of both worlds


Once the plant and its employees officially became part of Medline in February 2025, the challenge was on: How to best marry two beloved skin care portfolios in ways that wouldn’t leave Coloplast loyalists feeling abandoned?


Extensive product reviews were conducted. Strengths and differences were examined. Customers were consulted.


“In the end, our goal is to have the absolute best-in-class skin care portfolio for healthcare, and to have all of it under the Remedy brand to make it easy for customers who may have bought both Coloplast and Remedy products in the past,” said **Noah Garber** (pictured), general manager for Remedy and Medline’s Readycare division. “When we spoke with customers, many expressed a clear preference for us to maintain the core products from Coloplast’s range.”


## NorthMankato-OldNew


Coloplast skin care products (top) and their new look as part of Medline’s “Simplified” line.


Overall, the consensus is that those products — now fully owned by Medline but continuing to bear the Coloplast name until late 2026 — are largely complementary, not duplicative, because they have simpler formulations with fewer ingredients. That often makes them a more fitting choice when allergens are a concern or when a patient has a specific skin state condition.


“Ultimately,” Deutsch said, “the result of our portfolio reviews and conversations with Coloplast customers was deciding to include what I would call the ‘dream team’ of legacy Coloplast skin care products under Remedy to maintain continuity in the market.”


Clinicians who depend on these products need not worry about confusion or unexpected surprises. The existing Coloplast product names — Sween 24, Critic-Aid, Atrac-tain, etc. — are not changing, Garber said. Neither are their formulations.


This year, they’re even getting their own Remedy subcategory, “Simplified,” as high-performing, simpler alternatives to Remedy’s “Clinical” line.


This grouping of Coloplast favorites will expand Medline’s catalog of strong clinical options to meet more user preferences, while also attracting more new eyes (and hands) to popular products that have been made in North Mankato for ages.


“I’m amazed at the breadth of customers we can reach now through Medline’s distribution networks,” Nuytten said. “We’re doing exactly the same things here that we were doing before, but the acquisition has given us a lot more opportunities.”


## NorthMankato-Cleanse


Cleanser from the new “Simplified” subcategory of Remedy is labeled and prepared for shipping.


#### Right place with the right people


As the North Mankato team continues its second year under the Medline umbrella, not *everything* will be the same. Aside from officially dropping the Coloplast name on Dec. 3 of this year, the biggest integration change will be more volume — the result of all that additional Medline reach.


The plant is increasing its output of former Coloplast products to meet the increased Medline customer demand, as well as adding production for existing Remedy products in the near future. That will mean some reconfigurations in a manufacturing environment that offers not just flexible space and an experienced staff but the benefit of being on U.S. soil.


“I think the biggest advantages that North Mankato offers us are additional capacity and a domestic source where we have some insulation from some global supply chain challenges,” said **Lauren Kehoe** (pictured), director of strategic sourcing for Remedy and ReadyCare. “The fact that we have ownership control and the agility to do it within our own plant is really valuable for our skin care customers.”


It also could mean expanding skin care solutions in entirely new directions.


“As we look toward the future, we want to think about how we can better enable proper skin care through new technologies and new approaches,” Garber said. “The R&D team that Keenan leads is a critical resource to help us do that. That’s a ton of value we didn’t have before this acquisition.”


Deutsch is all for it.


“That’s something we’ve really been pushing and working with our new colleagues on: How can we improve the existing products, and how can we bring something completely new to this portfolio?” he said. “We’ve had a lot of rich conversation there.”


Not too bad for recent rivals.


“It’s hard to make that switch overnight,” Garber said. “But over this first year, things have developed quite positively. There’s a lot of mutual respect. We’ve become much more unified.”


Added Nuytten: “If you come to our site, you’ll see people dressed in Medline attire. You’ll see Medline branding everywhere. The passion is the same, just under a different steward. And in the end, we all believe in getting the right products to the customer.”
