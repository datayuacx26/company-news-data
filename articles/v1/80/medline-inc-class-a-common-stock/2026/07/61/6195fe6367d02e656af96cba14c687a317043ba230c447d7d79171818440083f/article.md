# Medline announces Prime Vendor agreement with University of Toledo Medical Center

**Northfield, Ill., July 15, 2026 –** Medline (Nasdaq: MDLN) today announced it signed a new Prime Vendor agreement in February 2026 with the University of Toledo Medical Center, a part of UToledo Health, the academic health system of the University of Toledo. The medical center provides care to patients across northwest Ohio.


Under this agreement, University of Toledo Medical Center will have access to Medline’s comprehensive portfolio of medical-surgical products, including exam gowns and customer packs, as well as powerful distribution capabilities to help streamline ordering and the timely, reliable delivery of medical supplies.


“Partnering with Medline will allow our supply chain group to significantly enhance how we coordinate and collaborate with our clinical teams,” said Jennifer Pastorek, UToledo Health’s senior director of supply chain.


Marc Phillips, senior vice president of supply chain solutions at Medline, added: “We’re excited to work with the University of Toledo Medical Center as its Prime Vendor and support its team with our portfolio of products and services. This agreement builds on our collaboration to drive supply chain resiliency, operations and day-to-day product needs.”


Learn how Medline works with health care systems to improve clinical, financial and operational outcomes at[medline.com/acute-care](https://www.medline.com/about-us/who-we-serve/acute-care/) .
