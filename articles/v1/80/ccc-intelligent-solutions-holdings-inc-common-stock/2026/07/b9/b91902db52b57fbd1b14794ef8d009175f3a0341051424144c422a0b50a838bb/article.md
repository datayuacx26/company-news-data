# How Collision Repair Shops Can Capture More Repairs

Many shops are seeing repair volumes that are … uneven at best. So capturing every available job is critical. Improving your capture rate may not require a lot of new processes, but a FOCUS on a few important steps and topics.


## 1. First Contact Sets the Tone


A lot of repairs are lost before a car ever hits the floor. Missed calls, rushed conversations, or confusion at the front desk can push customers away fast. When someone calls or walks in after an accident, they’re already stressed. How your team handles that moment matters.


Strong shops train their front office staff to **slow things down** and **walk customers through what happens next** . Explain the process. Answer questions clearly. Make people feel like they’re in the right place. When customers feel taken care of from the start, they’re far more likely to stick with your shop.


Just as important: do it the same way every time. Every call, every estimate, every customer. No shortcuts just because the shop is busy.


## 2. Move Fast on Estimates


Waiting is one of the biggest reasons customers don’t come back. If it takes too long to get an estimate, schedule an appointment, or hear back from the shop, people start looking elsewhere—or decide not to fix the car at all.


Shops with high capture rates focus on **speed and clarity** . Get cars looked at quickly. Explain what you’re seeing as you write the estimate. Show photos. Point out safety-related items and why they matter. Customers don’t expect perfection—they expect straight answers.


You have access to Mobile Jumpstart in the CCC ONE Mobile App; write a preliminary, exterior estimate in less than two minutes and spend time explaining it to the customer.


A fast, well explained estimate builds confidence. And confident customers say yes more often.


## 3. Don’t Be Afraid to Talk About Money


Today’s repairs cost more than they used to. High deductibles are common, and many customers are nervous about paying out of pocket. If you ignore that concern, you’re probably losing work.


Good shops bring it up early. Explain why modern repairs cost more—sensors, calibrations, hidden damage. Help customers understand the risk of not fixing the car. Most people aren’t trying to avoid repairs; they’re trying to figure out how to afford them.


When shops can also offer flexible ways to pay or financing options, it keeps smaller repairs from driving away.[You can access a no-cost integration with Sunbit](https://www.cccis.com/collision-repairers/consumer-engagement/consumer-financing) to offer financing to your customers to address this.


Removing that money stress can be the difference between “I’ll think about it” and “let’s move forward.”


## 4. Follow Up Until You Get a Yes or No


One of the biggest missed opportunities is the estimate that never turns into a decision. Customers get busy. Life happens. That doesn’t mean the job is dead.


The best shops track every estimate that hasn’t converted and follow up—every time. A quick text, an email, or a phone call reminding them you’re ready to help goes a long way. Keep it helpful, not pushy.


A simple message like “Just checking in—happy to answer any questions or get you on the schedule” brings cars back that might otherwise disappear. Many shops recover real work just by being consistent with follow up.


## 5. Trust Keeps Cars Coming Back


Capture rate isn’t just about this repair—it’s about the next one too. Shops that communicate well during repairs, send updates, and deliver what they promise build a reputation customers remember.


When people trust you, they don’t shop around after an estimate. They tell friends. They come back. Over time, that trust raises capture across the board without adding more leads or advertising.
