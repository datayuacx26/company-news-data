# December 2025 Product Update: Submission 2.0 & VPN Checks

## What's New


### Edit Your Company Profile (launched 12/17)


Admins can now edit your organization's agent-facing guidelines directly in Kodex. Go to Settings > Theme Settings > Company Profile, then click Edit to open the rich text editor. Supports simple and advanced formatting including links and Vimeo-hosted videos.


### Timezone settings (including UTC)


All date fields now display timezone in the UI. Starting in January, you can also choose a timezone for your organization that applies across all date fields so each user's view is consistent (options include UTC, ET, PT, and more).


### New VPN checks for agent signup and signin (agent-facing)


Both agent signup and signin flows now detect VPN usage from law enforcement agents. If an agent is using a VPN, they receive a warning and can disable it before proceeding.


### New agent suspension and appeals process (agent-facing)


Qualifying agents who are rejected or deactivated now receive an automated email explaining the action, with a direct link to submit an appeal by re-applying online, without contacting you or Kodex support. Re-applications are reviewed by the Kodex verification team, and agents are notified of the decision.


### Submission 2.0 live across English-speaking countries (agent-facing)


Agent Submission 2.0 is now live for agents in the US, Canada, the UK, Ireland, Australia, and New Zealand. Remaining jurisdictions planned for January.
