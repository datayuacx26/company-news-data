# May 2025 Product Update: A Faster Agent Sign-Up Experience

> Law Enforcement Agents have a new sign in flow. Take a scroll to see what it looks like and the benefits of this update on verification and ease of use.


The new Kodex Account Signup flow — Agency Verification step


## Introducing a Faster, Smarter Law Enforcement Agent Sign Up Experience


### Enhanced Data Collection for Stronger Verification


We've introduced a more structured data intake process to improve the accuracy and completeness of information collected during signup. Capturing key details like **agency address** , **fingerprint ID** , **language preference** , and **role expertise** strengthens our ability to verify the legitimacy of government officials — and supports ongoing monitoring to detect any anomalous behavior over time.


### Improved Efficiency and Clarity


Law Enforcement Agents will find a redesigned signup flow that is more intuitive than before. Errors are flagged in real time, steps are easier to follow, and the password setup and reset process is much clearer.


### Mobile-Friendly Entry Point


It is now easier to start the signup process from a mobile device by entering an email to receive clear instructions for completing registration on a desktop or laptop. *To note when on mobile devices, Safari works best.*
