# 100,000 Strong: A Law Enforcement Request Milestone

We’re proud to share that **over 100,000 verified law enforcement professionals** are now part of Kodex Global.


But this isn’t really about us.


It’s about impact.


Every single connection on our platform represents the possibility of a crime stopped, a family protected, or a community made safer. Stories like the ones below remind us why bridging the gap between public and private matters so much.


## 2.5 Million Lives Saved at a Lady Gaga Concert


Large-scale events bring joy to thousands, but they can also attract bad actors. When intelligence surfaced on a planned[attack at a Lady Gaga concert](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c209r5pqzneo) at Copacabana Beach in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, law enforcement launched ‘Operation Fake Monster’ and needed a fast, trusted way to collaborate with private sector partners.


Through Kodex, those law enforcement agents were able to quickly verify requests, connect with the right stakeholders, and share the information needed to neutralize the threat. What could have been a tragedy became a story of prevention, and thousands of fans enjoyed a night of music, safely.


> *“Kodex was essential in this investigation. The platform acted as a secure and efficient bridge between law enforcement and Discord, allowing us to obtain emergency data tied to profiles involved in a serious threat. The information shared via Kodex helped us identify connections between users and confirm that there was, in fact, a coordinated plan to cause harm. This support was critical to advancing the investigation and protecting public safety.” ‍ - Police Commander of Piauí State, Brazil, Alesandro Gonçalves Barreto said about the incident.*

## The $400,000 Comeback: a crypto crime undone


Toronto Police launched an investigation into[an investment fraud scam](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/toronto/bitcoin-scam-1.6801018) carried out over the phone, where a husband and wife were tricked into sending $400,000 to scammers. The lead investigator worked seamlessly with a Kodex Partner to help regain a “significant portion” of the couple's lost funds from intake, and judicial authorizations, all the way through to the successful recovery of funds.


This case is a great example of how Kodex bridges the gap between public investigators and private industry, creating a secure, efficient channel for collaboration. What began as a devastating phone scam became a story of recovery and resilience.
