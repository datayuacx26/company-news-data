# April 2025 Product Update: Combo Search & Analytics Dashboard

## What's New


### Combo Search, find what you need faster


A powerful new way to search right from the homepage: one enhanced free-text search bar to start quickly, smart filters and preset options to narrow results with a click, and all the flexibility of advanced search seamlessly integrated for complex searches.


### Analytics & Insights Dashboard, smarter decisions and trends


A new view of your operations. Track trends like volume and response times, monitor active requests and action items at a glance, and surface high-level KPIs for your team or execs. Flexible filters by timeframe, agency, case nature, jurisdiction, and request type let you slice the data across individual organizations or in a unified view.


### Self-Service Features: Key Organization Settings


The Self-Service Hub now gives you more control over how your organization operates: attachment storage locations and data retention period, minimum due date settings, and applicable requestor jurisdictions and NDO workflow. Set it once and manage at your pace, no tickets needed.


## Coming Soon


- **Kodex API Enhancements:** Improved technical documentation and new self-service API endpoints for account lookups and data production attachments.
- **Agent Case Sharing:** Enable multiple vetted law enforcement agents to securely access and collaborate on specific requests.
- **Reimbursements:** Enhanced reporting and notifications for tracking reimbursement collections and payment statuses.
- **Target Revamp:** A redesigned UI for target submissions and internal reviews, supporting structured data collection, automated lookups, and faster investigations.
