# Campbell’s Foodservice showcases customer solutions at the National Restaurant Association Show 2026 - The Campbell's Company

Our Foodservice team returned to the National Restaurant Association tradeshow this year, bringing new flavors, immersive experiences, and real-world solutions.


The show is a great opportunity for us to showcase our incredible portfolio of brands and share how our expertise can help address challenges and drive growth for restaurants.


### **What did attendees experience at the Campbell’s Foodservice booth?**


The Foodservice team invited attendees to sample snacks, sip drinks, and participate in chef-led tastings. Additionally, guests enjoyed an interactive digital experience, including:


- “Common Grounds,” a Connections-inspired game


- “Quiz Bites,” a snacking quiz


- “Aura Flavor Fit,” an interactive V8® Energy game


> “We’re committed to our partners. That means understanding their ambitious goals, recognizing the complex challenges they face, and delivering smart, tangible solutions to meet this dynamic moment.”
>
>
> Chris Graziano, Associate Vice President, US Marketing, Campbell’s Foodservice


### **Which new soups, snacks and beverages did Campbell’s Foodservice debut?**


In addition to some of our classic soups like Chicken and Dumpling and French Onion, **** our chefs debuted four new globally inspired creations from the Campbell’s® Culinary Reserve frozen soups, including:


- Caldo Verde


- Thai Red Curry with Lentils


- Caribbean-Style Jerk Chicken


- Zuppa Toscana


We also debuted new beverages and snack innovations including:


- *V8 Energy* Zero Sugar Cherry Lime


- Goldfish® ** Flavor Blasted Sour Cream & Onion crackers baked with whole grain cheddar


### **What topics did Campbell’s Culinary Corner cover?**


During the event, we recorded a podcast hosted by Senior Brand Manager, Jenna Hagerich. Taped live on the show floor, Jenna led conversations with key industry leaders and our partners on a variety of topics, such as:


- Building brand loyalty


- Blending analytics with human insight in supply chains


- Maximizing menu trends without losing margin


- Purchasing strategy in uncertain markets


Don’t miss the conversations! You can listen to all the recordings now on[Campbell’s Culinary Corner](https://www.campbellsfoodservice.com/national-restaurant-association-show-podcasts/) .


From bold flavors to meaningful conversations, Campbell’s Foodservice focused on equipping our customers with delicious, practical solutions at the National Restaurant Association Show 2026.
