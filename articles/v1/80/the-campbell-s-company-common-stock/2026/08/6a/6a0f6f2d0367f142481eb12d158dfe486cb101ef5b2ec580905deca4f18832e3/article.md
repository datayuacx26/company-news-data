# Cape Cod Chips invests in the future of local culinary community on the Cape - The Campbell's Company

Cape Cod® Chips was inspired by the entrepreneurial vision of a local business owner in Cape Cod, Massachusetts. Today, that same spirit continues through the Next Wave Culinary Career Initiative, a new partnership with Cape Cod Community College designed to support aspiring food entrepreneurs and strengthen the local food economy.


Kate Barrett, head of Community Affairs at The Campbell’s Company, presents the $250,000 check to Interim Community College President, Dr. Ellen Kennedy and Chief Workforce Development Officer for CCAPE, Tammi Jacobsen


#### **Why *Cape Cod* Chips is investing in local culinary talent**


This partnership reflects our commitment to the community where *Cape Cod* Chips began.


Backed by a $250,000 investment from *Cape Cod* Chips *,* the Next Wave Culinary Career Initiative will provide hands-on training, mentorship, leadership development and industry connections to help participants build the skills and confidence needed to grow in the food industry.


The initiative is designed to help strengthen Cape Cod’s food ecosystem and support future opportunities in the region.


#### **All about the Next Wave Culinary Career Initiative**


The program includes two complementary tracks:


- **Kitchen-to-Career Accelerator:** Supports emerging food entrepreneurs as they develop and advance their business ideas.
- **Kitchen Leadership Lab:** Supports culinary professionals looking to build leadership skills and grow their careers.


Over the next 18 months, the program is expected to support up to 30 entrepreneurs, help launch or advance 15 to 20 food businesses, and create up to 25 jobs across the region. The Kitchen Leadership Lab will start in November 2026 and the Kitchen-to-Career Accelerator will start in early 2027.


Jen Vondenbrink, lead culinary instructor at Cape Cod Community College, demonstrates a sample lesson from the new Kitchen Leadership Lab curriculum
