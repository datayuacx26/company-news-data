# Celebrating 30 years of Pokémon with limited-edition Goldfish® - The Campbell's Company

Goldfish® is partnering with Pokémon to celebrate the franchise’s 30th anniversary with a limited-time snack collaboration featuring collectible, character-inspired cheddar crackers.


This collaboration encourages families to collect all four fun snack packs inspired by some of the most iconic Pokémon characters.


Each pack includes crackers in the shape of one of these characters:


- Pikachu
- Bulbasaur
- Charmander
- Squirtle


These themed crackers include custom shapes and unique colors sourced from plants, making snack time more playful and collectible.
