# Snack brands celebrate America’s 250th with limited-edition releases - The Campbell's Company

To celebrate the 250th anniversary of the United States, our Snacks brands are introducing limited-edition offerings, nostalgic flavor innovations, and patriotic packaging.


These releases highlight our heritage and bring the celebration to summer gatherings across the country!


### **Pepperidge Farm® Maggie’s Apple Pie**


*Pepperidge Farm* , known for distinctively crafted baked goods, is introducing a limited-edition cookie inspired by a nostalgic American classic:[Maggie’s Apple Pie](https://www.pepperidgefarm.com/product/maggies-apple-pie/) .


These cookies are a celebration of Margaret Rudkin, founder of Pepperidge Farm and pioneer in American baking. Margaret started baking for her son’s health in the 1930’s and founded Pepperidge Farm in 1937. Named after her family’s farm in Fairfield, Connecticut, she grew *Pepperidge Farm* from a bakery in her kitchen into a national brand synonymous with quality and timeless taste.


This new cookie features:


- Sweet apple and cinnamon flavors
- A rich, buttery cookie base
- Special lattice design on top


The result is a modern take on traditional apple pie.


> “ *The Maggie’s Apple Pie cookie pays homage to our brand’s heritage and the woman who started it all nearly 90 years ago, while capturing the nostalgic taste of a classic American dessert.* “
>
>
> -Ama Auwarter, Vice President, Bakery and Cookies


### **Snack Factory® Fireworks Berry Crème flavored Pretzel Crisps**


*Snack Factory* is introducing[Fireworks Berry Crème Pretzel Crisps](https://www.snackfactory.com/where-to-buy/) , a limited-time flavor created for summer celebrations.


This sweet-and-salty snack combines:


- Thin, crunchy pretzels
- Berry-flavored white crème
- Red and blue popping candy for added texture and fun


> “ *Summer is when people gather, share and look for snacks that feel celebratory, especially in a year filled with patriotic moments. Our new Fireworks Berry Creme Pretzel Crisps were made for backyard BBQs, Fourth of July spreads, road trips and every celebration in between.* “
>
>
> -Matt Venturella, Senior Brand Manager, Snack Factory
