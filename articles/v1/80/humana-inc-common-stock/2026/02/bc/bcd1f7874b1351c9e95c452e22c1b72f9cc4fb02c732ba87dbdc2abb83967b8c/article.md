# Humana Inc. to Present at the 2026 Leerink Partners Global Healthcare Conference

[PDF Version](https://humana.gcs-web.com/node/40676/pdf)


LOUISVILLE, Ky.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Feb. 26, 2026-- Humana Inc. (NYSE: HUM) announced today that Jim Rechtin, President and Chief Executive Officer, and Celeste Mellet, Chief Financial Officer, will make a presentation to investors at the Leerink Partners Global Healthcare Conference on Tuesday, March 10, 2026, at 10:40 a.m. Eastern time.


A live audio webcast of the presentation will be available via Humana’s Investor Relations page at[https://humana.gcs-web.com/](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fhumana.gcs-web.com%2F&esheet=54434440&newsitemid=20260226803980&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fhumana.gcs-web.com%2F&index=1&md5=7306f792bc9e1133b1baaf5ae069f4c6) . The company suggests webcast participants sign on approximately 15 minutes in advance of the presentation to allow time to run a system test and download any free software needed for access purposes.


**About Humana**


Humana (NYSE:HUM) is a leading U.S. healthcare company. Through our Humana insurance services and our CenterWell healthcare services, we make it easier for the millions of people we serve to achieve their best health – delivering the care and service they need, when they need it. These efforts are leading to a better quality of life for people with Medicare and Medicaid, families, individuals, military service personnel, and communities at large.
