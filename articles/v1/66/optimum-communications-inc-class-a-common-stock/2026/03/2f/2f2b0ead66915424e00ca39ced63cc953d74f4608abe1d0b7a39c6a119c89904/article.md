# Optimum To Participate in the NSR and BCG Global Connectivity Leaders Conference


March 24, 2026 8:30am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_b2e9bf9e1382cd0d22b39f62b3cbc174/alticeusa/news/2026-03-24_Optimum_To_Participate_in_the_NSR_and_BCG_Global_230.pdf)


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Optimum Communications, Inc. (NYSE: OPTU) today announced that Dennis Mathew, Chairman and CEO, will participate in the NSR and BCG Global Connectivity Leaders Conference on Thursday, March 26, 2026. Mr. Mathew’s remarks are scheduled to begin at 10:25 AM EST. A live webcast will be available online on the Optimum[Investor Relations](https://investors.optimum.com/news-events) website or by following this[link](https://event.summitcast.com/view/TG595k8jEf6QSqigEiUFgk/DngQauSVAiRs78rtszzpsE) .


**About Optimum Communications, Inc.** Optimum Communications, Inc. (NYSE: OPTU) is one of the largest broadband communications providers in the United States, delivering high-speed internet, video, mobile, and voice services to approximately 4.3 million residential and business customers across 21 states. As a brand built for the future, Optimum is committed to reimagining connectivity and delivering exceptional experiences through next-generation technology and customer-first innovation. The Company also operates Optimum Media, an advanced advertising and data solutions business that enables local, regional, and national brands to reach audiences across screens with precision and scale. Additionally, News 12 – its award-winning hyperlocal news network – provides trusted, community-focused journalism across the tri-state area and beyond.
