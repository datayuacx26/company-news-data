# Optimum Surpasses 700,000 Mobile Lines

# Optimum Surpasses 700,000 Mobile Lines


June 09, 2026 10:30am EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_b2e9bf9e1382cd0d22b39f62b3cbc174/alticeusa/news/2026-06-09_Optimum_Surpasses_700_000_Mobile_235.pdf)


*Continued Mobile Growth Reflects Strong Customer Demand for Affordability as Company Expands Savings on Bundled Services*


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Optimum Communications, Inc. (NYSE: OPTU), a leading provider of fiber internet, mobile, and TV services, today announces it has surpassed 700,000 mobile lines, marking a significant milestone in the company’s continued growth and momentum in the mobile market. The achievement underscores Optimum’s focus on delivering simple, flexible, and affordable connectivity solutions at a time when consumers are facing rising costs across nearly every aspect of daily life.


According to a recent national survey conducted on behalf of Optimum1, 79% of consumers have reduced spending in the past two months, with 30% reducing spending on either TV, Mobile, or Internet services. As households continue to look for smarter ways to manage essential expenses, affordability has become an increasingly important driver of connectivity choices.


“Surpassing 700,000 mobile lines is a reflection of the strong progress we are making as we continue to transform our business and strengthen the products and services we deliver for our customers,” said Michael Parker, President of Consumer Services at Optimum. “As connectivity becomes more essential than ever, customers are increasingly looking for value and reliability across their mobile and broadband services. At Optimum, we remain focused on building on this momentum by delivering straightforward, cost-effective solutions that deepen customer relationships and support our long-term growth.”


**Optimum Offers the Best Deal on Internet and Mobile Anywhere**


Optimum’s mobile growth is a key part of the company’s broader strategy to enhance connectivity, increase customer value, and strengthen its overall business performance. Optimum Mobile delivers dependable nationwide 5G coverage on America’s largest network, with simple pricing and no surprises.


Optimum currently offers the best deal on internet and mobile, anywhere, starting at just $60 per month for both2. Recently, the company introduced expanded savings on bundled services, enabling qualifying customers to save up to $20 per month when bundling Optimum Internet and a Mobile Unlimited plan – making it easier than ever to get exceptional value.


Additionally, for a limited time, qualifying customers who bundle Optimum’s Entertainment TV tier with Internet or Internet and Mobile can receive added video savings as well. Available on Optimum Stream, Entertainment TV provides a high-quality internet-delivered TV viewing experience with access to over 70+ top rated entertainment and lifestyle channels with 20 network apps included, plus exclusive local news content from Optimum’s News 12 service. By bundling with Optimum, qualifying customers can unlock savings across their connectivity services while enjoying a robust, budget-friendly entertainment experience.


For more information on Optimum Mobile including plans, device pricing, and availability, visit[Optimum.com/Mobile20](https://www.optimum.com/LP/mobile-savings) .
