# 623rd Consecutive Dividend

**York, Pennsylvania, August 18, 2026:** The York Water Company’s (NASDAQ: YORW) President and CEO, JT Hand, announced today that the Board of Directors at their August 17th meeting declared a quarterly dividend of $0.2280 per share. The dividend is payable October 15, 2026, to shareholders as of record date September 30, 2026.


This is the 623rd consecutive dividend to be paid by The York Water Company. York Water, which is the oldest publicly traded company in the nation, has never missed a dividend in over 210 years. This is believed to be the longest record of consecutive dividends in America.
