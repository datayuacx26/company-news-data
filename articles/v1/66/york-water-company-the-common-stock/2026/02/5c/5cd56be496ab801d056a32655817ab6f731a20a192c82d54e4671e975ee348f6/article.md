# Supporting Girl Scouts

Each year, York Water opens its distribution center to support Girl Scout troops across the region as they pick up the cookies they worked so hard to sell and deliver to you. We’re proud to be a part of the Girl Scout experience and the community’s favorite cookie distribution center every year.


To make the day even sweeter this year, we presented a check in support of STEM initiatives through Girl Scouts in the Heart of Pennsylvania, helping empower the next generation of innovators and leaders.
