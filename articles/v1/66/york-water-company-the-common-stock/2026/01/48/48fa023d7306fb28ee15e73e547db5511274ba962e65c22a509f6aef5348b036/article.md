# 621st Consecutive Dividend

**York, Pennsylvania, January 27, 2026:** The York Water Company’s (NASDAQ: YORW) President and CEO, JT Hand, announced today that the Board of Directors at their January 26th meeting declared a quarterly dividend of $0.2280 per share. The dividend is payable April 15, 2026, to shareholders as of record date February 27, 2026.


This is the 621st consecutive dividend to be paid by The York Water Company. York Water, which is the oldest publicly traded company in the nation, has never missed a dividend in over 210 years. This is believed to be the longest record of consecutive dividends in America.
