# The York Water Company Acquires Wastewater System Serving Pine Run Retirement Community

**York, Pennsylvania, March 30, 2026** – The York Water Company (NASDAQ: YORW), a trusted provider of water and wastewater utility services across southcentral Pennsylvania, announced today that it has closed on the acquisition of the wastewater system assets within the Pine Run Retirement Community in Hamilton Township, Adams County.


“Working with The York Water Company throughout this process has given us great confidence in their ability to serve the residents of Pine Run,” said Pine Run Management, LLC. “Their experience operating both water and wastewater systems makes them a strong partner for delivering efficient, high-quality service to customers. We are confident that the residents will be well served moving forward.”


This important acquisition builds on York Water’s recent purchase of the water supply system serving the Pine Run Community. With both transactions now complete, The York Water Company is the single provider of water and wastewater utility services for the Pine Run residents, extending service farther into Adams County and adding another 141 customers to the customer base. The consolidated utility service team enables a more streamlined and efficient approach to operation, maintenance, and customer service.


“This acquisition is a natural extension of our commitment to delivering high-quality, integrated utility services,” said JT Hand, President & CEO at The York Water Company. “The acquisition of the Pine Run community water system in December 2024 allowed us to connect customers to a safe, reliable, and sustainable source of supply and today we are able to provide professional wastewater operations in this community. Combining these services under one provider offers greater efficiency and convenience for our customers.”


The York Water Company is honored to provide water and wastewater services to over 214,000 customers and visitors in 58 communities in Adams, Franklin, Lancaster, and York counties.
