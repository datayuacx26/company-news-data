# NAWC Celebrates 2025

Since its founding, the National Association of Water Companies (NAWC) has provided member companies with advocacy, education and programming, communications and marketing support, and industry research and reporting. In 2025, NAWC celebrated its accomplishments with a special[Year in Review](https://nawc.org/2025-nawc-year-in-review-video/) video. The York Water Company is honored to be a founding member of NAWC and looks forward to continuing our work together in the years ahead.
