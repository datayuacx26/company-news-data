## What is a mail forwarding service?


‍


A mail forwarding service does more than ship mail from one address to another — it manages it. **A good provider**[receives your mail,](https://www.usestable.com/blog/best-corporate-mailing-services-for-receiving-and-sending-mail) **scans the envelope so you can review it online, then lets you decide what happens next: open and scan the contents, forward the item, put it in storage, or shred it.**


‍


That decision happens from your dashboard, not a mailroom.


‍


## How a mail forwarding service works for business


‍


The USPS still delivers billions of pieces of mail to millions of addresses every year. In fiscal year 2025,[108.7 billion pieces to 170.4 million delivery points](https://prc.gov/state-of-the-postal-service) . A lot of that mail lands in business mailboxes.


‍


That volume is exactly why mail forwarding providers matter for business. **Most presort your mail and clear out junk automatically — a real time-saver when you're getting dozens of envelopes a day.**


‍


**Mail forwarding providers also eliminate the need for an on-premises mailroom.** Most offer business and enterprise accounts with multiple user accounts, tagging and search, and other tools that make mail easy to sort and find.


‍


No more trips to the post office or sorting mail by hand — you log in and check a digitized inbox instead. This can be a huge advantage not only for businesses looking to move away from traditional[mailrooms](https://www.usestable.com/blog/digital-mailroom) , but also for businesses who rely on a remote or distributed workforce without a centralized office.


‍


## Benefits of a mail forwarding service for business


‍


Beyond helping you manage large volumes of snail mail, forwarding service providers offer several other advantages worth knowing about.


‍


### Digitizes critical documents


‍


When your mail arrives yout mail forwarding services or[virtual mailbox services](https://www.usestable.com/blog/how-does-a-virtual-mailbox-work) scans the envelope to your online mailbox first. From there, you can choose to have documents opened and scanned, **creating a permanent digital record you can share, save, or archive, so a lost paper copy is never a problem again.**


‍


### Offers remote accessibility and convenience


‍


As of March 2026, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reports[22.6% of employed Americans telework at least part of the time](https://www.makerstations.io/work-from-home-productivity-statistics/) — roughly 35 million people. That share has been ticking up, not down, over the past year — despite the headlines about return-to-office mandates — which is only pushing more businesses toward virtual mail over on-premises mailrooms.


‍


> **Whether you're traveling or managing a distributed team, mail forwarding gives you access to your mail no matter where you are.**


‍


### Gives you a permanent business address


‍


Most businesses need a permanent address, and that's not always simple. A home-office startup may not want a residential address on public record. A distributed team may not have a central office at all. A mail forwarding service solves both: your[business address](https://www.usestable.com/blog/how-to-get-an-address-for-a-business) stays the same even if your team or headquarters moves.


‍


### Increases security and privacy


‍


Privacy and security are real concerns for business owners, and a mail forwarding service adds a layer of separation between your home address and your business. **This keeps your home address, and your life outside the business, off the public record.**


‍


Look for a provider that backs this up with real security credentials — SOC 2 and HIPAA compliance, controlled-access facilities, and in-house (not third-party) mail handling — since not every provider on the market has all three.


‍


### Accelerates mail management


‍


Tired of daily trips to the post office? Or sifting through large piles of mail, half of which turns out to be junk mail? Forwarding providers sort your mail and give you 24/7 online access. Tag it, organize it into folders, or forward digital copies to teammates who need to see it.


‍


Most also offer check deposit, saving you the trip to the bank along with the one to the post office.
