# 6 best Anytime Mailbox alternatives for a virtual mailbox

**If you're evaluating**[virtual mailbox](https://www.usestable.com/products/virtual-mailbox) **services, Anytime Mailbox is rarely the final answer for business users. The per-scan fees and inconsistent third-party location quality leave most growing companies looking for something more reliable before long.**


‍


If you’re looking for an alternative to Anytime Mailbox (whether due to price, flexibility, features, or any other reason), we’ve collected six of the best options in this article. These options offer you a better way to send and receive mail than a P.O. Box, and include many other capabilities — like giving you a virtual office with a real street address.


‍


**Key takeaways**


- [Anytime Mailbox](https://www.usestable.com/blog/anytime-mailbox-review) prices vary by location and add up quickly once you factor in per-scan charges; several alternatives offer more predictable all-in pricing.
- Stable is the only provider on this list that combines a[virtual mailbox](https://www.usestable.com/products/virtual-mailbox) ,[registered agent](https://www.usestable.com/products/registered-agent) ,[virtual address](https://www.usestable.com/products/virtual-address) , and[electronic check deposit](https://www.usestable.com/products/electronic-check-deposits) under one roof, at a business-grade price.
- LegalZoom Virtual Mail (formerly Earth Class Mail) has been fully rebranded; if you've seen older comparisons referencing Earth Class Mail, the product and pricing have changed significantly.
- iPostal1 has the largest location network of any provider here, over 4,250 addresses, but relies on third-party operators, which creates service inconsistency at scale.
- If integrations and automation matter to your team, that single variable narrows the field considerably.


## **Why businesses look for an Anytime Mailbox alternative**


‍


Virtual mailbox adoption has grown as more businesses operate across distributed teams, remote offices, and flexible formation models. The category is no longer just for travelers or solo operators. Businesses now use virtual mailboxes to manage compliance mail, checks, notices, and team workflows without maintaining a physical office. According to[LP Information](https://www.lpinformationdata.com/reports/1832776/virtual-mailbox-service) , the global virtual mailbox service market is projected to grow from $2.77 billion in 2025 to $6.24 billion in 2032, a 12.3% CAGR.


‍


Anytime Mailbox sits in the middle of that market: a large location network, low entry prices, and flexible plans. That flexibility can turn into unpredictable pricing once scan fees, location-specific plans, and monthly volume enter the picture. Pricing depends on location, service quality varies because most locations are run by third parties, and costs compound fast for businesses with real mail volume. That may work for individuals or low-volume personal accounts. For businesses managing compliance mail, team access, and growing monthly volume, the unpredictability becomes a problem.


‍


Below, we compare six Anytime Mailbox alternatives for business users in 2026, including pricing, features, limitations, and the use case each provider fits best.


> **The real buying decision in this category isn't which service has the most locations. It's which service matches how your business actually handles mail, and what happens when your volume grows.**

### **2.**[iPostal1](https://ipostal1.com/) **: best for businesses that need a specific city or address**


‍


**What it is:** iPostal1 is one of the largest virtual mailbox networks in the world, with over 4,250 addresses across the US and internationally. The platform lets users manage mail through a web dashboard or mobile app.


‍


**Best for:** **Individuals, freelancers, and small businesses that need geographic flexibility and low entry pricing, especially those who want a specific city address or multiple locations.**


**Standout features:**


- 4,250+ real street addresses globally (largest network on this list)
- Plans with phone and fax bundled (Virtual Office plans)
- Package consolidation and international forwarding
- Mobile app for iOS and Android
- Mail consolidation to reduce forwarding costs


**Pricing:** Starting at $9.99/mo at Standard locations; price varies by location tier (Standard, Select, Premium, Prestige). Content scanning is not included at base: $2.25 for up to 10 pages, then $0.25/page. No annual contract required.


‍


**Watch out for:** iPostal1 relies on a network of third-party Commercial Mail Receiving Agencies (CMRAs), and **service quality, processing speed, and scan quality vary significantly by location.** Businesses that need automation, integrations, or team workflows tend to outgrow the platform quickly.

**Best for:** **Early-stage businesses already using LegalZoom for formation, compliance, or legal services, who want mail management in the same platform.**

**Watch out for:** **The location count is limited at 53 addresses across 44 states, which may not work if you need a specific city or state coverage.** Automation and team collaboration tools lag behind software-native competitors, making it a better convenience add-on for existing LegalZoom customers than a standalone choice for teams building mail workflows.

**What it is:** PostScan Mail is a virtual mailbox service with roughly 400 US-based addresses (plus some international options), known for its mobile app.

**Best for:** **Remote workers, freelancers, and small businesses that prioritize mobile access and don't need advanced team features or integrations.**

**Standout features:**

**Watch out for:** **PostScan Mail relies on a mix of company-owned hub locations and third-party partner locations. Negative reviews cluster around third-party sites: scan quality issues, mail mix-ups, and slower processing.** No registered agent or integration capabilities, and significant ad-on costs for scanning, shredding, and forwarding.

**Watch out for:** **US Global Mail operates from a single Houston, Texas address. **Watch out for:** **Physical Address has roughly 30 US locations, the smallest footprint on this list, so if location choice matters beyond a handful of cities, options are limited.** Evernote is the only integration, and there's no path to registered agent flexibility or team automation through the same platform.
