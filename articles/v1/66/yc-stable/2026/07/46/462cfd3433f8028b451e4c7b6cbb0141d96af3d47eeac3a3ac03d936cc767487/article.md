## Corporate consolidations & the hidden costs of physical mail


‍


Businesses face many challenges when closing or consolidating offices, from disposing of furniture to migrating IT infrastructure. With all these responsibilities, mail is often an afterthought. The truth is that missed mail can create significant operational risks for businesses.


‍


When an office closes, you can no longer receive mail at that location. Forwarding gaps often occur due to change of address processing delays, which results in missed mail until your address has been changed. This missed mail can result in missed deadlines, compliance exposure, and delayed cash flow.


‍


Distributed mailrooms are an option for managing mail after office consolidation, but they come with significant costs. Aside from staffing and facility costs, distributed mailrooms often lead to inconsistent processes and a lack of visibility across locations. These problems grow as companies scale or downsize their real estate presence. Mail has to be routed through centralized hubs, and courier costs may increase as your mail has to travel a longer distance.

## FAQs


### What happens to a company’s mail when it consolidates or closes an office?


When a company consolidates or closes an office, mail sent to that address can be delayed, returned, or missed entirely until it's redirected. That creates risks ranging from missed compliance notices and legal documents to delayed payments and interrupted business operations.


### What is the difference between virtual mailboxes and P.O. boxes?


While both services let you receive mail, a virtual mailbox is designed for businesses that want to manage mail digitally. Mail is received at a real street address, scanned, and made available online, with options for forwarding, check deposit, and automation. A P.O. Box is simply a mailbox at a local post office where mail must be collected in person.


### How do virtual mailrooms ensure data privacy and compliance?


Virtual mailrooms follow strict security standards and use encryption to ensure privacy and compliance. Stable is SOC 2 compliant, HIPAA compliant, and uses encryption and role-based access to protect sensitive documents.


### How can I reduce physical mail volume during corporate office downsizing?


A virtual mailbox won't reduce the amount of business mail you receive, but it will reduce the amount of physical mail your team has to process. Incoming mail is scanned and managed digitally, while junk mail can be filtered and only the items you need in hand are forwarded.
