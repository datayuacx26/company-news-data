## What is a virtual business address?


‍


A virtual business address is a real, physical street address that a business uses to receive mail, satisfy regulatory requirements, and maintain a professional presence — without occupying or renting space at that location.


‍


**It is not a P.O. Box.**


‍


That distinction matters. A P.O. Box can't be used to register an LLC, open a business bank account, or apply for an EIN with the IRS. A virtual business address — operated through a licensed Commercial Mail Receiving Agency (CMRA) — can be used for all of these.

## Frequently asked questions about virtual business addresses


‍


### **Can I use a virtual address to register my LLC?**


Yes. In most states, a virtual business address can be used as your LLC's principal business address on formation documents, including the Articles of Organization — as long as it's a real street address, not a P.O. Box. Rules vary by state, so confirm your state's specific requirements before filing.


### **Can I use a virtual address to open a business bank account?**


Yes, in most cases. Banks require a real business address — not a P.O. Box — and a CMRA-operated virtual address typically satisfies this requirement. Some banks may have additional verification steps; confirm with your bank before applying.


### **Can I use a virtual address for my EIN application?**


Yes. The IRS accepts a CMRA-based virtual address as the business mailing address on Form SS-4 and on business tax returns, as long as it's a real street address and not a P.O. Box.


### **Do I need to notarize USPS Form 1583?**


Yes. Form 1583 must be notarized or signed in front of a CMRA employee. Online notarization is widely accepted and typically takes 10–15 minutes. Stable guides customers through this process as part of onboarding.


### **Is a virtual address the same as a P.O. Box?**


No. A P.O. Box is a postal box at a USPS or retail location. It cannot be used for LLC registration, EIN applications, or most business banking. A virtual business address is a real street address operated by a licensed CMRA — it can be used for all of these, plus mail scanning, forwarding, and more.


### **How long does it take to set up a virtual business address?**


Most accounts are active within 1–2 business days once Form 1583 is submitted and identity verification is complete.


### **Can I use a virtual address if I'm in a regulated industry?**


Yes, but verify your provider's compliance certifications. Stable is SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliant, making it an appropriate choice for healthcare, legal, and financial services businesses with strict data handling requirements.


### **Can I use my virtual address as my registered agent address?**


Generally, no. A registered agent address must be a physical location in your state of registration where someone is present during business hours to receive legal documents. A virtual address and a registered agent address serve different purposes, though some providers, including Stable, offer both services.
