## What is a virtual mail service?


‍


A virtual mail service provides a[virtual address](https://www.usestable.com/products/virtual-address) and manages business mail on your behalf. It’s not the same as a P.O. box — it's an actual street address where business stakeholders, suppliers, and customers can send mail and packages.


‍


Typically, your provider receives, processes, stores, and forwards all your mail to keep you updated on vital business correspondence regardless of your location — which is essential for distributed and[remote teams](https://www.usestable.com/blog/16-benefits-and-perks-your-remote-team-members-will-love) .


‍


Some virtual business address services, such as Stable, also provide services like mail scanning so you can view your mail online. Many also offer mail shredding for the secure destruction of junk mail or unwanted correspondence.


‍


More advanced providers also offer mail management automations that reduce manual work even further. For example, you can automatically route mail to the right person or team, deposit incoming checks, filter out junk mail, or trigger notifications and workflows, helping your startup stay organized without someone constantly managing the inbox.


‍


A virtual mail service is often a better option for startups than[coworking spaces](https://www.usestable.com/blog/coworking-space-vs-virtual-mailbox) , physical office spaces, and home addresses. It's more cost-effective, offers comprehensive mail management services, and allows you to maintain your privacy.


‍


As a startup, you need to save money and stay organized. The right virtual mail provider can help you do both.

A virtual mailbox gives your business a real street address where you can receive mail, manage it online, and access services like mail scanning, forwarding, secure shredding, and check deposits.


‍


A registered agent is different. Every LLC and corporation is required to maintain a registered agent in each state where it is registered to do business. A registered agent receives legal and government documents — such as service of process, compliance notices, and state correspondence — on your company's behalf during business hours.
