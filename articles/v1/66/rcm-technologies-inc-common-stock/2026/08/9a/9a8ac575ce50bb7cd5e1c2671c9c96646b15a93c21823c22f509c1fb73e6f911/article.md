# How to Choose the Right School-Based Staffing Partner for Your District

Finding qualified school-based professionals has never been easy. Today, many districts are balancing provider shortages, growing student needs, tighter budgets, and increasingly complex compliance requirements, all while trying to ensure students receive the services they need.


The right staffing partner helps districts plan ahead, respond quickly when needs change, and reduce the administrative burden of recruiting, credentialing, and ongoing staffing management.


If your district is evaluating staffing partners, here are seven areas worth considering.


## **1. Look Beyond Fill Rates**


Every staffing firm can promise candidates. The better question is how they find and retain qualified professionals throughout the school year.


**Ask prospective partners:**


- How do you recruit before the school year begins?
- How do you respond to same-day coverage requests?
- What happens when someone calls out?
- How do you maintain staffing throughout the year?


Reliable staffing depends on planning, strong recruiting pipelines, and dedicated scheduling support. A partner that prepares early is often better equipped to respond when unexpected needs arise. District feedback consistently highlights fill rates, same-day response, and dependable substitute coverage as key evaluation criteria.


## **2. Evaluate Their Recruiting and Screening Process**


Successful placements begin long before staff enters the classroom.


**Ask how candidates are:**


- Sourced
- Interviewed and screened
- Credentialed
- Reference checked
- Matched to assignments


The strongest staffing partners don’t simply place available candidates. They take the time to understand each school’s environment and match professionals based on experience, role requirements, and overall fit.


For specialized positions such as BCBAs, school psychologists, speech-language pathologists, or high-acuity nursing, expertise in the recruiting process becomes even more important. RCM’s internal process includes structured screening, credential verification, compliance review, and role matching, with behavioral candidates receiving additional reviews from experienced BCBAs when appropriate.


## **3. Make Compliance a Priority**


School staffing involves much more than recruiting. Every placement should meet state and district requirements before entering a classroom.


**Your staffing partner should have established processes for:**


- Background checks
- Credential verification
- State-specific licensing requirements
- Fingerprinting
- Onboarding documentation
- Ongoing compliance monitoring


Requirements vary significantly from state to state. An experienced education staffing partner understands those differences and helps reduce the administrative burden on district staff.


## **4. Ask About Communication and Daily Support**


Communication is often what separates a transactional vendor from a true staffing partner. Ask who you’ll work with throughout the partnership.


**Will you have:**


- A dedicated account contact?
- A scheduling team that knows your schools?
- Regular staffing updates?
- Someone available when urgent needs arise?


Districts value consistent communication, quick response times, and working with people who understand their schools and staffing priorities.


## **5. Choose a Partner That Understands School Environments**


Working in schools requires a different approach than staffing hospitals or outpatient settings.


School-based professionals need to understand classroom routines, collaborate with educators and administrators, and support students across a wide range of educational and behavioral needs. Recruiting and placing therapists, behavioral professionals, school nurses, paraprofessionals, substitute teachers, and special education staff requires a partner that understands K-12 environments, IEP timelines, compliance requirements, seasonal hiring cycles, and the importance of maintaining continuity for students.


## **6. Think Beyond Today’s Vacancies**


Many districts focus on filling immediate openings. Strong staffing partnerships also prepare for future needs.


**Ask potential partners:**


- How do you build candidate pipelines?
- Do you recruit proactively throughout the year?
- Can you support anticipated staffing increases?
- How do you improve your continuity over time?


Partners that recruit year-round are often better positioned when new positions open, or unexpected vacancies occur. As they become more familiar with your schools, staffing patterns, and preferences, they can often make stronger matches and respond more efficiently.


## **7. Look for a Long-Term Partnership**


The lowest hourly rate doesn’t always deliver the greatest value.


**Consider the full experience your district will receive:**


- Responsiveness
- Communication
- Training
- Recruiting expertise
- Compliance support
- Placement quality
- Staffing consistency
- Operational support throughout the school year


As a partnership grows, a staffing provider gains a deeper understanding of your district’s staffing patterns, priorities, and preferences. That familiarity can improve communication, strengthen placement quality, and reduce the administrative effort required to keep positions filled.


The strongest partnerships are built on trust, communication, and a shared commitment to supporting student success.


## **Questions to Ask a School-Based Staffing Partner**


**As you evaluate staffing providers, consider asking:**


- How do you recruit for hard-to-fill positions?
- What is your average response time for urgent requests?
- How do you screen and credential candidates?
- How do you support compliance across different states?
- Who will manage our account day to day?
- What type of training to you provide?
- How do you supervise and evaluate contract staff?
- How do you maintain staffing consistency throughout the school year?
- What reporting or workforce technology do you provide?
- Can you support multiple school-based specialties under one partnership?
