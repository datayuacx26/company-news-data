# JBS Australia places first 'Enhancing the Food Production Workforce with Neuro-Inclusion' Pilot team member

JBS Australia has officially placed their first team member as a part of the “Enhancing the Food Production Workforce with Neuro-Inclusion” pilot, welcoming Morgan to the Dinmore Beef Processing Facility as the new Training Assistant.


Neurodiversity is a term used to describe the different ways people think, learn, communicate and experience the world. It includes conditions such as autism, ADHD, dyslexia and dyscalculia and with around 15–20% of Australians thought to be neurodivergent it is increasingly important to understand and support different ways of working.


This industry-first program, delivered in partnership with Team Cohesion and supported by the Australian Meat Processor Corporation (AMPC), aims to connect autistic job seekers with meaningful career opportunities in the red meat processing sector.


The pilot was developed following a year of national consultation and industry engagement, which highlighted both opportunities and barriers to improving employment outcomes for autistic individuals in the red meat processing industry where educational resources, workforce capability tools and neuro-inclusion training were developed, with the JBS Dinmore beef processing facility participating as one of four pilot sites in south-east Queensland.


These foundations then support the rollout of Phase 2, which focuses on creating practical employment pathways for autistic job seekers.


The pilot is currently in the phase where autistic job seekers are connected with JBS teams, providing opportunities to build new skills, contribute their strengths and develop rewarding careers in a supportive and inclusive workplace.


JBS Dinmore Plant Manager Trent MacNeil has said how proud he is to be part of the pilot and his excitement about the roles still to come.


Supporting the development and education of students within the meat sector ensures we’re investing in the sustainable development of the industry as a whole.
