# JBS Recognized as Loblaws Supplier of the Year

JBS has been named Supermarket Supplier of the Year by Loblaws, one of Canada’s largest supermarket chains, at its Vendor Summit held in Toronto on June 11. This prestigious recognition places JBS among the top performing suppliers in the country and reflects the strength of its partnership with one of the food industry’s leading retailers.


The achievement is especially notable given the scale of competition. More than 2,200 suppliers attended the event, representing some of the largest global food companies across multiple sectors. From this highly competitive group, JBS was selected for its consistent delivery of high-quality products, strong performance, and deep commitment to customer collaboration.


The recognition reflects the dedication and commitment demonstrated by teams across the business. Leaders from Canada, United States, and Australia proudly accepted the award, representing the collective efforts of many people who contribute daily to delivering strong results and building trusted partnerships with our customers.


This recognition highlights:


- A focus on quality and consistency
- An ability to work together across regions
- The strength of customer relationships


Most importantly, this award reflects the dedication, passion, and teamwork demonstrated by our people every day. It belongs to all team members who contribute to our success and help build trusted relationships with our customers.


JBS extends its gratitude and congratulations to all team members involved in achieving this milestone, an accomplishment the entire organization can be proud of.
