# JBS Riverina investing in the future of the industry via Meat Tech Futures Event

JBS Riverina in New South Wales recently kicked off the Meat Tech Futures Riverina event in partnership with schools across the region in Narrandera, Leeton, and Yanco region.


Meat-Tech Futures; run by Integrated STEM, is an immersive, hands-on student conference that brings together industry leaders, educators and innovators to showcase the cutting-edge technologies transforming the red meat processing sector. Through interactive experiences, real-world case studies, and exposure to emerging STEM applications, students gain insight into how automation, robotics, data analytics, and sustainable practices are shaping the future of food production.


This year marked a significant milestone for the program, with the first-ever combined showcase of both feedlot and processing operations. The integrated experience gave students a unique end-to-end view of the red meat supply chain, highlighting the diverse career opportunities available across the industry.


Students had the opportunity to test the MEQ carcase grading cameras, gaining firsthand experience with the technology used to objectively assess marbling and other carcase traits. They also toured both the feedlot and processing facility, providing valuable insight into the innovative practices and technologies driving the future of the sector.
