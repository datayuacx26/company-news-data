# Record Entries and Exciting Milestones at Australian Paddock to Palate Field Day Hosted by JBS Beef City

As Presenting Partner of the RNA Paddock to Palate Competition and Wagyu Challenge, JBS Australia was proud to host the weight gain award presentation for the first time at the JBS Beef City Feedlot, located just outside Toowoomba, Queensland.


These prestigious competitions are run in conjunction with the annual Royal Queensland Show (Ekka), which is celebrating its historic 150th anniversary this year.


The three-phase Paddock to Palate competition evaluates the best aggregate weight gain, carcase, and MSA eating quality. Meanwhile, the Wagyu Challenge introduces a four-phase format, culminating in a beef taste-off judged by some of Queensland's top chefs. This year attracted an enormous 1,358 head of cattle entries which is 25 per cent above last year.


While the weight gain phase winners were officially awarded on June 16, the final carcase and MSA eating quality results will be announced in Brisbane on Aug. 6, after which the beef will be available for a limited time, proudly branded under the divisions exclusive Royal 100 brand.


Holding the formalities at the feedlot, rather than offsite as in previous years, offered a fantastic opportunity for our Northern COO, Brendan Tatt, and Northern General Manager of Livestock, Edwin Cooke, to address attendees and provide a comprehensive update on current global and domestic beef market dynamics.


While the weight gain awards day has traditionally included a tour of the feedlot, hosting the lunch and presentation onsite for the first time this year created a more streamlined event and gave attendees a greater opportunity to experience Beef City firsthand.


Competition such as Paddock to Palate demonstrate JBS Australia’s commitment to giving producers opportunities to gain direct feedback on where their cattle stand for the improvement of all in the cattle industry.
