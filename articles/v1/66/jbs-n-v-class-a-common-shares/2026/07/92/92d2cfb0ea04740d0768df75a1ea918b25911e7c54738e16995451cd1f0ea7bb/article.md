# JBS Australia diverse protein portfolio on full display at Metcash

For the second year running, the diverse range of JBS protein varieties were on full display at the Metcash Expo in Australia.


The JBS Northern, JBS Southern, JBS Australia Pork and Huon Aquaculture business units collaborated, showcasing beef, lamb, pork and salmon to attendees.


‌The Metcash Expo brings together suppliers and independent retailers from across Australia, offering a platform to connect, showcase innovation, and strengthen commercial relationships.


JBS Australia Executive Chef Dan Johnston put his signature culinary flair on display, treating onlookers to a range of product demonstrations and some delicious samples.


From paddock to plate and ocean to aisle, the combined presence of four different Australian divisions gave customers and retailers a complete view of the high-quality, sustainable protein options JBS Australia provides.
