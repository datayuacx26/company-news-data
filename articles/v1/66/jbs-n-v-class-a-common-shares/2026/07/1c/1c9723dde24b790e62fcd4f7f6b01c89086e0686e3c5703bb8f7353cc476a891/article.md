# JBS Publishes 2025 Sustainability Report

Amstelveen, Netherlands – July 8, 2026 – JBS N.V. (“JBS” or the “Company”) (NYSE: JBS, B3: JBSS32), one of the world’s leading food companies, today published its[2025 Sustainability Report.](https://jbs-foods-site-uploads.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/705d22d3-d429-4bca-84f5-ac97a7f4a321/JBS_2025NonFinancialDisclosureReport-FINAL_EN.pdf) JBS’s 2025 Sustainability Report details execution against the Company’s strategy to deliver profitable and sustainable food production for a growing planet.


The Report, structured around JBS’s three strategic pillars — Maintain Excellence, Drive Progress, and Build Resilience — includes disclosures intended to support decision-useful information for investors on key topics such as workforce health and safety, operational emissions and energy, water, responsible sourcing, and supply chain transparency.


*Key highlights include:*


- **Capital investment:** Since 2020, JBS has invested nearly US$200 million across projects inside its facilities intended to reduce Scope 1 and 2 emissions.
- **Sustainability-linked financing performance:** JBS reported achieving interim KPI targets referenced in the JBS and Pilgrim's Sustainability-Linked Bond Frameworks, including reported reductions in Scope 1 and 2 greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions intensity of 18.8% and 24.4%, respectively.
- **Water and waste reduction initiatives:** JBS reduced total water consumption by 9.7% from 2024 to 2025. JBS increased recycling of waste produced in our operations by 9.2% from 2024 to 2025 and reduced waste sent to landfill by 25.8% from 2024 to 2025.
- **Renewable energy adoption** : In 2025, renewables accounted for 31.3% of JBS global energy use.
- **People and product integrity:** The Company reported 18.2 million training hours delivered globally and 78.9% of global facilities certified under Global Food Safety Initiative (GFSI) schemes.


As outlined in the Report, JBS has also introduced a more focused sustainability reporting framework better aligned to the Company’s global supply chain and opportunities to deliver measurable, near- and long-term impact. JBS is evolving its broader, long-term ambitions by prioritizing specific, time-bound operational targets supported by clear baselines, rigorous internal controls, and annual third-party assurance.


“Our successful execution against our sustainability goals directly translates to the growth, risk mitigation, and long‑term value that JBS can deliver for our customers, partners, teams, and shareholders,” said Jason Weller, Global Chief Sustainability Officer of JBS. “The evolution of our reporting framework sharpens our focus on actions we can directly manage, measure, and report with confidence, while continuing to engage across the value chain on shared challenges that affect our industry.”


JBS’s near- and long-term emissions targets reflect progress the company can directly track and control – Scope 1 and 2 emissions – anchored by clear, time-bound goals, operational KPIs, and independent assurance.


*Key climate targets include:*


- **Near-Term:** A 30% reduction in Scope 1 and 2 emissions intensity (tCO₂e/t of finished product) by 2030 (2019 baseline).
- **Long-Term:** A 70% reduction in Scope 1 and 2 emissions intensity (tCO₂e/t of finished product) across JBS processing facilities by 2050 (2019 baseline).
- **Renewable Energy Transition:** Reach 60% renewable electricity use globally by 2030.


“We are focused on turning ambition into measurable outcomes,” said Weller. “Our climate targets emphasize what JBS can execute and verify within our operations, while we work collaboratively with producers, customers, and partners to address the broader challenges shaping our industry. As a major player in the global food value chain, we have a responsibility to lead, and this reporting framework sets a clear, accountable standard for our stakeholders.”


In tandem with these operational goals, JBS continues to take meaningful action through programs that support better land stewardship among producers, expand supply chain transparency to mitigate deforestation risks, and enhance animal welfare.


*Key initiatives include* :


- JBS Farm Assurance is an Australian on‑farm certification program that verifies producers meet defined standards in animal welfare, traceability, and environmental management. It supports the specific claims made by brands — such as Grass Fed, Free Range, GMO‑free, Antibiotic‑free, and HGP‑free for Great Southern, and Certified Grain Fed, Antibiotic‑free, and HGP‑free for Portoro — and provides a verified paddock‑to‑plate paper trail that strengthens label integrity and builds trust with retail and export customers.
- JBS’s Green Offices offer free technical assistance to help Brazilian cattle producers — including indirect suppliers — achieve socioenvironmental compliance and environmental regularization of their properties. Since its launch in 2021, the program has supported the regularization of over 20,000 rural properties and directed more than 8,000 hectares toward forest recovery.
- JBS offers its suppliers in Brazil tools to verify the social and environmental compliance of their own suppliers, including a blockchain platform and Cowbot — through which farmers can check their suppliers’ compliance in real time via a WhatsApp interface.
- The JBS Fund for the Amazon finances conservation and sustainable development initiatives. One of its flagship programs is RestaurAmazônia, which supports farming families in restoring degraded areas in the Amazon by implementing agroforestry systems that combine cocoa cultivation with sustainable cattle ranching. Since 2021, the project has involved nearly 1,200 families, with over 44,000 hectares under improved practices, more than 1,000 hectares of agroforestry systems implemented, and a 47% increase in family income recorded.
