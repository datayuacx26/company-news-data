# North Queensland Producers Shine at Annual JBS Australia Carcass Competition

The Townsville Livestock team recently hosted JBS’s annual Carcass Competition, bringing together North Queensland producers to benchmark their cattle and gain valuable performance insights.


Now in its fourth year, the event continues to grow in popularity, offering producers the opportunity to receive detailed feedback and compare results across a range of classes designed to assess both on-hoof and carcass performance. Judges noted the high standard of entries this year, with only marginal differences separating results.


Standout performers on the night highlighted the value of consistency across multiple measures, reinforcing the importance of data-driven decision-making on-farm. Producers also emphasised the benefits of participating, with feedback from the competition helping guide continuous improvement on farm.


Townsville Livestock Manager Aaron Adcock said the competition plays an important role in supporting producers and strengthening the broader industry.


“This competition highlights the strength and consistency of livestock coming out of North Queensland, while also giving producers meaningful data to support on-farm decision-making,” he said.


Alongside the competition, attendees also took part in a fan favourite blind tasting experience, with Angus X recognised as the top-performing breed for the first time in the competition, based on eating quality attributes.
