# Local fire unions offer esophageal screening that can detect pre-cancer

Virginia Beach and Portsmouth firefighters are using the EsoGuard test, which detects precancer in the esophagus, to prevent cancer. Firefighters face a higher risk of cancer due to exposure to harmful chemicals. The test provides early detection, crucial for effective treatment. Virginia Beach firefighter Matt Gallina is undergoing treatment for esophageal cancer, discovered through early testing. Both local fire unions emphasize the importance of preventative screenings to keep members healthy and safe. The initiative aims to enhance early detection and prevention efforts, allowing firefighters to continue their vital work safely.
