# BrightView Reports Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results with Second Consecutive Quarter of Land Maintenance Revenue Growth

BrightView Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: BV) (the “Company” or “BrightView”), the leading commercial landscaping services company in the United States, today reported unaudited results for the third quarter ended June 30, 2026.


"We delivered our second consecutive quarter of Land Maintenance revenue growth, underpinned by the strategic investments we’ve made in our business,” said Dale Asplund, BrightView President and Chief Executive Officer. “Our continued focus on prioritizing our front-line employees and customers, expanding our salesforce, and commitment to operational excellence continue to strengthen our business and positions us to deliver sustainable growth over the near and long term. While this quarter had non-routine headwinds related to our self-insurance adjustment and higher fuel prices, we believe these costs don’t impact the long-term trajectory of the business and we remain focused and optimistic on delivering against our long-term outlook.”
