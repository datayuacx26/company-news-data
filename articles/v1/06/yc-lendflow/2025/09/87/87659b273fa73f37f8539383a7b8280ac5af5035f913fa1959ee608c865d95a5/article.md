# Lendflow Shortlisted as Finalist for the 2025 Banking Tech Awards

We’re pleased to announce that **Lendflow** has been shortlisted as a finalist in the **2025 Banking Tech Awards** by *FinTech Futures* , in the highly competitive **Best Digital Solution Provider – LendTech** category. Being recognized among the most forward-thinking companies in financial technology is a tremendous honor and a strong validation of the work our team has been doing to push boundaries in digital lending solutions.

- QuickFi: QuickFi Platform
