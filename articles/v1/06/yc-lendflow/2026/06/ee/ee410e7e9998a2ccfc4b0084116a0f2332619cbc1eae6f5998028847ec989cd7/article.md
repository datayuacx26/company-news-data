# Lendflow Wins Best Embedded Lending Solution at the 2026 Banking Tech Awards USA

The awards celebrate the organizations and technology providers shaping the future of financial services through transformative solutions and exceptional customer experiences.


This recognition marks an exciting milestone in Lendflow’s journey. Last year, we were honored to be recognized as a finalist, and this year’s win is a testament to the incredible work of our team, customers, and partners who continue to push the boundaries of what embedded lending can achieve.


At Lendflow, our mission has always been to make access to capital more seamless, intelligent, and accessible. Through our embedded lending infrastructure, platforms, software providers, and financial institutions can integrate lending experiences directly into their products, enabling businesses to access financing when and where they need it most. This award validates our commitment to building the technology that powers the next generation of business lending.


We’re especially proud because of the caliber of companies recognized alongside us. The Banking Tech Awards USA brought together some of the most innovative organizations in financial services, and this year’s winners represent the very best of the industry. Among them were **SavvyMoney** , winner of Best Embedded Finance Solution; **TransferMate** , winner of Best Embedded Payments Solution; **Better.com** , winner of Best LendTech Solution; **Socure** , winner of Best Digital Trust Solution; and **FICO** , winner of Best AI Solution.


Being recognized among such an accomplished group reinforces the growing importance of embedded financial services and the role technology plays in creating better experiences for businesses and consumers alike. The awards ceremony brought together hundreds of banking and fintech leaders to celebrate innovation, collaboration, and the achievements driving the industry forward.


Most importantly, this award belongs to our customers and partners. Every day, they trust Lendflow to power lending experiences that help businesses secure the capital they need to grow. Their success is what inspires us to continue innovating and raising the bar for embedded lending.


Thank you to the Banking Tech Awards judges for this recognition, and congratulations to all of the 2026 winners and finalists. We’re honored to be part of such an exceptional community of innovators and look forward to continuing our mission of transforming access to capital through embedded lending.
