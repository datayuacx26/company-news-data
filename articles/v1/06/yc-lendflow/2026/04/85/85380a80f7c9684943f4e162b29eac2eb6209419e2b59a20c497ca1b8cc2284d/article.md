# Lendflow Shortlisted for 2026 Banking Tech Awards USA

Hosted by FinTech Futures and Informa Connect, the Banking Tech Awards USA celebrate the very best in banking and financial technology, recognizing organizations that are pushing the industry forward through cutting-edge solutions and measurable impact.


### About the Awards


The 2026 awards highlight top performers across a wide range of categories, including AI, digital banking, payments, and lending technology. Finalists represent the pinnacle of innovation across the U.S. fintech ecosystem, selected from a highly competitive pool of nominations.


Winners will be announced on May 28, 2026, at a gala event in New York City, bringing together more than 350 senior leaders from across banking and fintech.


### Lendflow Among Industry Leaders


Lendflow has been shortlisted in the **Best Embedded Lending Solution** category, alongside other standout innovators including Fundbox, FinMkt, SavvyMoney, and TruStage.


This category recognizes platforms that are redefining how lending is embedded into digital experiences, empowering businesses and financial institutions to deliver seamless, scalable credit solutions.


Across the broader shortlist, Lendflow is in strong company. Other finalists span categories such as Best AI Solution featuring companies like FICO and Ocrolus, Best PayTech Solution, and Best Digital Banking System, with major financial institutions like JPMorganChase, Morgan Stanley, and Bank of America also represented.


### A Reflection of Continued Momentum


Being named a finalist reinforces Lendflow’s commitment to modernizing credit infrastructure and enabling embedded finance at scale. It also places the company among a select group of fintech innovators shaping the future of financial services.


This recognition builds on Lendflow’s broader momentum and industry acknowledgment for its work in embedded lending and credit infrastructure. As the fintech landscape continues to evolve, awards like the Banking Tech Awards underscore the importance of delivering flexible, data-driven solutions that meet the needs of modern lenders and platforms.


### Looking Ahead


We’re honored to be recognized alongside so many pioneering companies and look forward to the awards ceremony in May. Regardless of the outcome, this milestone reflects the impact of our customers, partners, and team in driving innovation in lending.


Stay tuned as we continue to push the boundaries of what’s possible in embedded finance.
