# Turning Feedback into Fuel: How to Deliver Feedback That Drives Success at Work

This article is from SHRM:


[https://shrm.co/c2utg5](https://shrm.co/c2utg5)


According to SHRM’s 2025


*State of the Workplace Report* ,


**employee experience ranks as a top priority for HR professionals** , second only to business strategy. Yet, many employees feel disconnected:


-


**34% feel unrecognized** for their work


-


**25% lack team collaboration and support**


-


**15% say performance evaluations are unfair**


So, what’s missing? One critical tool:


**effective, thoughtful feedback.**


Done right, feedback fuels growth, enhances performance, and builds a culture of trust. Done wrong—or not at all—it can lead to disengagement and turnover. The numbers speak for themselves:


**80% of employees who receive meaningful feedback weekly report being fully engaged** , according to Gartner.


As SHRM’s CHRO


**Jim Link** puts it, “Effective feedback must go beyond a checkbox mentality.” When feedback is impersonal or vague, it becomes noise instead of guidance.


###


Why Feedback Matters More Than Ever


Effective feedback is a powerful lever for:


-


**Recognition** – making people feel seen and appreciated


-


**Fairness** – setting expectations and offering constructive support


-


**Engagement** – motivating employees to take ownership of their development


-


**Delegation** – empowering team members to grow into new responsibilities


Executive coach


**Lucinda Smith** notes that strong delegation


**relies on clear, constructive feedback** , helping team members feel confident and capable. Feedback isn’t just about correcting mistakes — it’s about


**building future leaders.**


###


6 Feedback Tips That Actually Work


Here are six research-backed and experience-driven strategies to deliver feedback that inspires action and growth:


####


1. 🎯


**Be Specific and Objective**


Generic praise like “Nice job!” is forgettable. Instead, point to the exact behavior and its impact.✅


*Try this:* “Your summary helped the team clarify next steps and meet our deadline.”


####


2. ⚖️


**Balance Strengths with Growth Opportunities**


Celebrate wins, but also highlight areas to improve. This balanced approach builds trust and shows you’re invested in their success.


####


3. 🛠️


**Offer Guidance, Not Just Critique**


If something went off track, pair your observation with a solution.✅


*Try this:* “Next time, loop in the stakeholder early — they typically need visibility before final decisions.”


####


4. 📈


**Tie Feedback to Development**


Show how feedback aligns with the employee’s goals. Share your own journey—this personal touch helps the feedback resonate.


####


5. 💬


**Create Space for Dialogue**


Encourage employees to share thoughts, ask questions, and reflect. Two-way feedback helps clarify expectations and fosters deeper engagement.


####


6. 🔁


**Follow Up on Progress**


Reinforce improvement by acknowledging it. Recognition of effort reinforces growth and builds a culture of accountability.


✅


*Try this:* “I noticed you cc’d the stakeholder on the last few emails—great job applying the feedback.”


###


Drive the Organization Forward


Feedback isn’t just about correcting behavior—it’s about


**elevating performance, fostering growth, and building a culture of trust** . When leaders approach feedback with empathy, specificity, and consistency, they create an environment where employees feel valued, empowered, and ready to rise.


By integrating these strategies into your daily leadership style, you can:


-


Strengthen team communication


-


Boost engagement


-


Build future-ready leaders


-


Enhance overall organizational performance


Because when feedback is done right, it becomes a


**powerful tool for transformation** —not just for individuals, but for the entire organization.
