# CodeCanary is a YC-funded company

Today we're excited to announce our seed round and share that[CodeCanary](https://codecanary.ai/) is backed by[Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/) .


CodeCanary is an AI team member that continuously ingests product analytics to provide a once-weekly conversion rate optimization you can ship or A/B test.


We're building CodeCanary because we believe AI can autonomously increase businesses' revenue by businesses, but existing tools don't close the loop between product analytics and product improvements. We're starting with conversion rate optimization.


As part of YC, we've been working with group partners Dalton Caldwell, Diana Hu, Nicolas Dessaigne, and Richard Aberman. Our[YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/atonomo) highlights our team's background in building SaaS products and iteratively improving PLG products and onboarding experiences.


YC companies are some of the best early adopters and we're lucky to work with some incredible consumer apps that are growing incredibly fast like[CreativeMode](https://www.creativemode.net/) .
