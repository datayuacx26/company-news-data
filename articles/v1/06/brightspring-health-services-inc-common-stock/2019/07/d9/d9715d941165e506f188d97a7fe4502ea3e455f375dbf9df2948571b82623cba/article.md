# BrightSpring Chief Accounting Officer Selected for 41st Class of Leadership Louisville


July 19, 2019


**BrightSpring Health Services is excited to announce that Chief Accounting Officer Jennifer Phipps was selected for the 41st class of Leadership Louisville!**


The Leadership Louisville Center is recognized as one of the most innovative community leadership organizations in the country. Their signature “Leadership Louisville” program was designed for established leaders who are ready to make greater contributions to the region’s growth and success. For the next ten months, Jennifer and the selected group of professional peers will gain a larger understanding of the people and issues shaping our community’s future – and more importantly, how they can make a positive impact with said issues.


For more information about the Leadership Louisville program and the class of 2020,[click here](https://www.leadershiplouisville.org/leadership-louisville/leadership-louisville-class-of-2020/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=July2019-newsletter) .
