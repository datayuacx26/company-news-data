# BrightSpring Celebrated at Awards Reception for ‘Best in Human Resources’


August 9, 2019


BrightSpring Health Services was honored to be named a[“Best in Human Resources”](https://www.brightspringhealth.com/news/brightspring-recognized-as-best-in-human-resources/) award recipient by Louisville Business First, as part of their 2019 Best Places to Work in Greater Louisville program.


BrightSpring was celebrated, among other award-winning companies, at the Best Places to Work in Greater Louisville awards reception yesterday, Thursday, August 8 at the KFC Yum! Center. The company received a “Best in Human Resources” trophy, which is now on display at the company headquarters, in Louisville, Kentucky.


As part of the coverage, Business First also highlighted BrightSpring in a recent article, discussing how the company’s HR department tackled the issue around “improving company culture” by investing in its employees and establishing new cultural values and behaviors called LEGACY (Leadership, Engagement, Get Going!, Attitude, Communication, and You!).
