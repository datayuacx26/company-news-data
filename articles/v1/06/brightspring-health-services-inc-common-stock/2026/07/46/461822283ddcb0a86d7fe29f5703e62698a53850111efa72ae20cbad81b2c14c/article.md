# All Ways Caring HomeCare Celebrates Grand Opening of North Carolina Location


July 22, 2026


[All Ways Caring HomeCare](https://www.allwayscaring.com/) hosted a grand opening and ribbon cutting ceremony celebrating their new location in Cornelius, North Carolina.


“People are living longer than ever before, creating a growing demand for senior care services,” said Lisa Chidester, All Ways Caring State Director for North Carolina. “As the local population ages, this grand opening marks a vital expansion of specialized healthcare resources that help seniors remain safe and independent at home.”


In addition to traditional home care, All Ways Caring offers specialized support for veterans, individuals living with Alzheimer’s disease and other forms of dementia, and those requiring more complex care.
