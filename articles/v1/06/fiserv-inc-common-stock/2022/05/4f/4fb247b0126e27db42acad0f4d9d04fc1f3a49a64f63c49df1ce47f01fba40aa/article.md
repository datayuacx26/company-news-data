[Developer Studio](https://developer.fiserv.com/) , our single portal unifying all Fiserv API products to accelerate innovation, was named a 2022 Honoree for **Best Mobile Visual Design — Function** within the Website and Mobile Sites category of[The Webby Awards](https://winners.webbyawards.com/2022/websites-and-mobile-sites/mobile-specific-features-design/best-mobile-visual-design-function/216838/fiserv-developer-studio) .


Dubbed as “the Oscars® of the internet” in the industry and “the internet’s highest honor” by The New York Times, The Webby Awards is one of the oldest internet-oriented awards and is presented by the International Academy of Digital Arts and Sciences.


In addition to building a scalable framework for our merchant and banking solutions, the team synthesized the best features across the industry so developers can quickly and easily find and explore our solutions in an unparalleled user experience.


This award-winning mobile developer experience was created by our own in-house Developer Studio design team that focused on a user-centered, iterative approach that puts user design thinking at the forefront of the process. Ironically, the intent was not to start with mobility, but the user research indicated a strong mobile experience would be essential.


In an upcoming story, our VP of Product Design for the Developer Studio, will talk more about the journey and the technologies and techniques used along the way.


Congratulations to the entire Developer Studio Team! We are #FISVProud that your digital innovation has been acknowledged by the Academy.
