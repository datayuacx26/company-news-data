# Moichor x NectarVet: A Step Forward in Veterinary Innovation

At Moichor, we’ve always believed that the future of veterinary care lies at the intersection of science, technology, and streamlined clinical workflows. Today, we’re thrilled to announce our latest integration with NectarVet, the first truly AI-driven, all-in-one veterinary practice management platform.


This partnership marks an important milestone in Moichor’s ongoing mission to redefine diagnostic efficiency and empower veterinarians with faster, smarter tools, such as the[Pathview](http://ref1523384vax/) . By integrating directly with NectarVet, we're making it even easier for practices to seamlessly send samples, access results, and incorporate advanced diagnostics into their daily workflows — all within a next-generation PMS designed from the ground up with automation and intelligence in mind.


> "What drew us to NectarVet was not just the AI — it was the heart behind the product. Their team truly understands the day-to-day realities of veterinary work and has designed a platform that genuinely cares for the people who care for animals."
>
>
> — Joe Faiella CEO, Moichor


**Why NectarVet?**


NectarVet is an all-in-one cloud solution built for high-performing, compassionate care teams. From AI-powered SOAP generation to client communication tools, automated inventory, and integrated payment solutions, NectarVet is caring for vets—so they can care for pets.


The Moichor x NectarVet integration allows clinics to:


-


Order and view Moichor results directly within NectarVet’s dashboard, with no duplicate entries or disconnected portals


-


Leverage AI-powered medical summaries and plan suggestions that incorporate diagnostic insights


-


Save time and reduce burnout by eliminating repetitive tasks and surfacing the right information at the right time


-


Deliver higher-quality care with a fully integrated diagnostic + PMS ecosystem


> "Moichor is reimagining diagnostics from the ground up — not just delivering results faster, but improving how data informs care. Their approach aligns with our vision for intelligent, connected veterinary tools."
>
>
> — Joanna Chung, CEO & Co-Founder, NectarVet


This collaboration is a natural next step in Moichor’s innovation roadmap, and a powerful leap forward for veterinary teams looking for modern tools that work as hard as they do.


We’re excited to continue pushing the boundaries of what’s possible in veterinary diagnostics and look forward to empowering more clinics with the tools they need to thrive.
