# Omada Health to Participate in Canaccord Genuity’s 46th Annual Growth Conference


SAN FRANCISCO – July 13, 2026 –


[Omada Health](https://www.omadahealth.com/) (Nasdaq: OMDA), the virtual between-visit healthcare provider, today announced that members of senior management will participate in Canaccord Genuity’s 46th annual Growth Conference, taking place in Boston, including a fireside chat on Tuesday, August 11, at 8:00 a.m. EDT. The webcast will be available online at


[https://investors.omadahealth.com/news-events/events](https://investors.omadahealth.com/news-events/events) . A replay will also be available at the same link following the event.
