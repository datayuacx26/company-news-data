# Omada Health to Participate in Goldman Sachs Global Healthcare Conference


SAN FRANCISCO – May 26, 2026 –


[Omada Health](https://www.omadahealth.com/) (Nasdaq: OMDA), the virtual between-visit healthcare provider, today announced that members of senior management will participate in the Goldman Sachs 47th Annual Global Healthcare Conference 2026, taking place in Miami, including a fireside chat on Wednesday, June 10, at 8:20 a.m. PST. The webcast will be available online at


[https://investors.omadahealth.com/news-events/events](https://investors.omadahealth.com/news-events/events) . A replay will also be available at the same link following the event.
