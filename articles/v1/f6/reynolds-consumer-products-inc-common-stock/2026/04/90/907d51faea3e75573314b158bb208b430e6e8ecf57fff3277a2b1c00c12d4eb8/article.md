# Reynolds Consumer Products to Report First Quarter Financial Results on May 6, 2026

## Reynolds Consumer Products to Report First Quarter Financial Results on May 6, 2026


April 15, 2026


[PDF Version](https://investors.reynoldsconsumerproducts.com/node/10361/pdf)


LAKE FOREST, Ill.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Apr. 15, 2026-- Reynolds Consumer Products Inc. (Nasdaq: REYN) (the “Company”) announced it will report first quarter financial results on Wednesday, May 6, 2026.


The Company’s President and Chief Executive Officer, Scott Huckins, and Chief Financial Officer, Nathan Lowe, will host a live webcast to discuss the results at 7:00 a.m. CT (8:00 a.m. ET) that same day. A link to the webcast and all related earnings materials will be available at[https://investors.reynoldsconsumerproducts.com/](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.reynoldsconsumerproducts.com%2F&esheet=54508090&newsitemid=20260415669672&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.reynoldsconsumerproducts.com%2F&index=1&md5=aeb88daba52d19f5b287ed3c57b573d9) .


**About Reynolds Consumer Products Inc.**


Reynolds Consumer Products is a leading provider of household essentials designed to simplify daily life so consumers can enjoy what matters most. Found in 95% of U.S. homes, the Company offers trusted solutions for cooking, serving, cleanup and storage. Its portfolio features iconic brands like Reynolds® and Hefty®, along with store brand products tailored to retail partners. Reynolds Consumer Products holds the No. 1 or No. 2 market share in most of the categories it serves.
