## Chemed Corporation Declares Quarterly Dividend of 70 Cents


August 6, 2026 at 11:57 AM EDT


[PDF Version](https://www.chemed.com/node/22986/pdf)


CINCINNATI, Aug. 06, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Chemed Corporation (NYSE:[CHE](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=mTiVpqY7z2kuj3ZbqfRV1RCHEWHHiRDpLLbt68MJv5jwP8ors7zNQnlkaNvANdwXFviEFUIydj9RsvFvjeS_KA==) ) announced today that the Board of Directors has declared a quarterly cash dividend of 70-cents per share on the Company’s capital stock, payable on September 3, 2026, to shareholders of record as of August 17, 2026. This is a 10-cent, or 17%, increase over the 60-cent dividend paid in June 2026. This represents the 221st consecutive quarterly dividend paid by Chemed in its 55 years as a public company.


Listed on the New York Stock Exchange and headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, Chemed Corporation ([www.chemed.com](http://www.chemed.com/) ) operates two wholly owned subsidiaries: VITAS Healthcare and Roto-Rooter. VITAS is the nation's largest provider of end-of-life hospice care and Roto-Rooter is the nation’s leading provider of plumbing and drain cleaning services.
