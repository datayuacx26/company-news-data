## Chemed To Report Second Quarter 2026 Earnings July 28, Related Conference Call To Be Held On July 29

CINCINNATI, July 01, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --[Chemed Corporation](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=tyPehEiBVCCm5zGat_FBfAWTWYCp8NYgI1ZaEqMzPj7yXDatcEN5QZVfG6jaKMHP3UfmuQn-L8NuK-TKOcxG2x88MXe-oEHkmb3Cpf8TjcQ=) (NYSE: CHE) today announced that it will release financial results for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026, on Tuesday, July 28, 2026, following the close of trading on the New York Stock Exchange.

Listed on the New York Stock Exchange and headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, Chemed Corporation ([www.chemed.com](http://www.chemed.com/) ) operates two wholly owned subsidiaries: VITAS Healthcare and Roto-Rooter.
