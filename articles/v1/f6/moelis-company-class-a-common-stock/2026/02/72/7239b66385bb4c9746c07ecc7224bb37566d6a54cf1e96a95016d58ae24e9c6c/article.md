# Moelis Appoints Kyle Reidy to Lead Private Credit Secondaries

Press Release


## Moelis Appoints Kyle Reidy to Lead Private Credit Secondaries


New York


, February 12, 2026


*Expansion of the Firm’s Private Capital Advisory Business reinforces its continued investment in secondaries and sponsor-led capital solutions*


Moelis & Company (NYSE: MC), a leading global independent investment bank, today announced the appointment of Kyle Reidy as a Managing Director. He will join the Firm’s GP Advisory team to lead Private Credit Secondaries.


Kyle, who will be based in New York, brings over 17 years of experience delivering bespoke solutions to direct lending platforms, credit opportunity funds, credit hedge funds and private equity firms. He has a track record of building and scaling structured credit and financing platforms across market cycles. Kyle previously served as Global Head of Structured Credit Origination at Barclays. Before that, he spent over a decade at Deutsche Bank within its Structured Solutions Group.


Kyle will be joined later this year by Richard Saltzman, who will run the Firm’s GP Advisory business within the broader Private Capital Advisory platform headed by Matt Wesley.


Matt Wesley, Global Head of Private Capital Advisory at Moelis, commented, “As the demand for flexible liquidity solutions and more complex structures accelerates, Kyle’s deep expertise will further enhance our capabilities in private credit secondaries as we continue to help clients navigate an increasingly sophisticated market.”


“We’ve made significant strides in building a premier platform in the private capital advisory space, and Kyle’s hire reflects our continued momentum. The strength of the leadership in place and our ability to attract top talent underscore our strategy and commitment to scaling the platform to deliver differentiated, high-impact solutions to clients,” said Navid Mahmoodzadegan, CEO and Co-Founder of Moelis.
