## Moelis Hires Lillian Tsinikas to Expand Healthcare Services Group


New York


, April 28, 2026


*Appointment reflects continued investment and momentum in the Firm’s HCIT franchise*


Moelis & Company (NYSE: MC), a leading global independent investment bank, today announced that Lillian Tsinikas has joined the Firm as a Managing Director. Based in New York, she will work closely with members of Moelis’s Healthcare Services team, focusing on advising clients across the healthcare technology (HCIT) sector.


Lillian brings more than 15 years of M&A and investment banking advisory experience to Moelis. She joins from Lazard, where she co-led healthcare technology investment banking coverage. Prior to that, she held investment banking roles at Guggenheim Securities and TM Capital. Lillian began her career as a civil engineer before transitioning to corporate development and M&A.


Rich Harding, Head of Healthcare Services and the US Private Equity Solutions Group commented, “We’re pleased to welcome Lillian as HCIT continues to be one of the fastest-growing areas of the healthcare market. Her deep expertise and client relationships, combined with our broader healthcare and technology franchises and the strength of our sponsor coverage, will further enhance our ability to advise clients across this important and rapidly evolving sector.”
