## Moelis Expands Capital Markets Capabilities with Hire of Megan Dwyer Agar, Establishing Securitization and Structured Products Capability


New York


, July 22, 2026


Moelis & Company (NYSE: MC), a leading global independent investment bank, today announced the appointment of Megan Dwyer Agar as a Managing Director in New York. Megan will focus on securitization, as the Firm broadens its Capital Markets capabilities into structured products. She will advise clients on asset-backed and structured financings across a broad range of consumer and commercial asset classes.


Megan brings extensive experience structuring and executing securitizations across asset classes including credit cards, consumer and student loans, digital infrastructure and towers, whole business securitization, agricultural loans, and venture debt. She most recently served as a Managing Director in Structured Products Origination at Guggenheim Securities, where she led origination and execution of structured finance transactions, with particular expertise in new securitization platform development, master trust vehicles, and bespoke structured private placements. She previously held roles in Citigroup’s Global Securitized Products group.


Angus Whelchel, Head of Private Capital Markets at Moelis commented, “Securitization has become an increasingly important tool for companies seeking to finance efficiently and access diverse pools of capital. Megan’s expertise enables us to offer clients a broader set of tailored, asset-backed solutions across the capital structure. We are delighted to welcome her to the Firm.”


“Megan’s appointment marks another important step in the continued expansion of our global Capital Markets franchise. Together with the recent addition of Justin Polselli, it underscores the momentum and growth of our platform. Our ongoing investment in talent and capabilities enables us to provide our clients with the highest quality strategic advice and execution,” said Augusto Sasso, Global Head of Capital Markets at Moelis.
