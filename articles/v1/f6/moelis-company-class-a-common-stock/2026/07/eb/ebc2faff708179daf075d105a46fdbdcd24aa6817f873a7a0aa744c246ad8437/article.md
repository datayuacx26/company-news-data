## Moelis Hires Justin Polselli as a Managing Director in Capital Markets


New York


, July 15, 2026


Moelis & Company (NYSE: MC), a leading global independent investment bank, today announced the appointment of Justin Polselli as a Managing Director in New York. Justin joins the Firm’s Capital Markets franchise, where he will focus on debt capital markets.


Justin brings over 16 years of experience advising clients on high-yield bonds, leveraged loans, and private credit across a broad range of sectors. He most recently led the Middle Market and Direct Lending initiative within the Leveraged Finance Capital Markets team at Jefferies, advising on structuring, pricing, and execution across LBOs, refinancings, and M&A transactions.


“With momentum building across both public and private capital markets, companies are increasingly seeking customized financing solutions. Justin’s expertise across high-yield, leveraged loans, and private credit will help us deliver the creative, tailored outcomes our clients are looking for. We are pleased to welcome Justin to the Firm,” said Angus Whelchel, Head of Private Capital Markets at Moelis.


Augusto Sasso, Global Head of Capital Markets at Moelis commented, “Justin’s addition further strengthens the caliber of our Capital Markets franchise. His deep relationships across the lender community and proven execution, leveraging Moelis’s global advisory platform, enhance our ability to support clients across their most complex financing needs.”


## About Moelis & Company


Moelis & Company (“Moelis”) is a leading global independent investment bank that provides innovative strategic advice and solutions to a diverse client base, including corporations, governments, and financial sponsors.
