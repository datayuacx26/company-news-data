# Sherwin-Williams Color of the Year 2026: Universal Khaki

Neutral colors have long been a cornerstone of home design, offering a versatile foundation that allows your personal style to shine through furnishings, textures, and accents. In modern luxury interiors, neutrals continue to evolve, bringing warmth and depth to every space.


[Universal Khaki SW 6150](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/en-us/color/color-of-the-year/2026) , the Sherwin-Williams Color of the Year 2026, reflects this shift toward grounded, timeless design. This warm, earthy neutral features a mid-tone tan base with subtle yellow undertones, striking a balance between comfort and sophistication. Described as “Tailored & Timeless,” Universal Khaki offers both livability and longevity in today’s homes.


Discover how to use Universal Khaki in your home, including the best colors to pair with it, and how[Toll Brothers](https://www.tollbrothers.com/) model homes across the country are incorporating this trending neutral.
