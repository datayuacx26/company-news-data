## How to Choose the Right Cabinet Color


Choosing the right kitchen cabinet color is all about balancing your personal style with the other elements in your kitchen design. Start by considering lighting; lighter hues can help make the space feel even more open and spacious. Coordinate your cabinet color with your countertops and[backsplash](https://www.tollbrothers.com/blog/trending-backsplash-options) to create contrast and allow each feature to stand out. Finally, ensure the color complements your home’s overall design style, creating a cohesive, polished look that feels elevated and intentional.
