While sometimes used interchangeably, modern interior design and contemporary interior design are two distinct styles. Modern interior design is a bold, sleek aesthetic that debuted in the late nineteenth century, while contemporary interior design evolves with the times, shifting to accommodate new trends and tastes. Contemporary design borrows elements from various other popular interior styles to create fresh, fashion-forward spaces.

## Enhance Your Home’s Cool Contemporary Style


Continue reading for helpful tips on using color, texture, furniture, and décor to add even more cool contemporary interior design characteristics to your new Toll Brothers home.


### Color


Cool whites and soft greige walls are classic choices that work well with bolder accents. To infuse on-trend hues into your home, explore colors like icy blues, frosty purples, and watery greens. Dark, rich, cool tones such as navy, charcoal, and emerald work as moody neutrals that add depth.


### Texture


Layering textures helps make the spaces in your cool contemporary home look complete. For textiles, think about creating a mix of tactile experiences, like lustrous silk and nubby tweed or woven linen and plush faux fur. The same principle applies to other décor elements: rustic pottery combined with polished glass or a sleek mirror against limewashed walls creates sophisticated contrast.


### Furniture and Décor


Contemporary interior design celebrates clean lines. As a result, mid-century modern and Scandinavian furniture are often found in luxury contemporary interiors. Today, we also see the use of curves, such as sofas and chairs with rounded edges and soft lines. Sleek and streamlined pieces in wood or other natural materials are timeless and look beautiful when combined with decorative elements like chrome light fixtures, abstract wall art, and marble accents.
