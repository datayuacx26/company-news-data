## Amplify Your Home’s Warm Contemporary Style


Keep reading for helpful tips on using colors, textures, and furniture to add Warm Contemporary style to your new Toll Brothers home.


### Color


Warm Contemporary is defined by a light, neutral foundation that allows materials and form to take the lead. Crisp white cabinetry, light neutral walls, and soft white tile establish a bright, open backdrop, while light-toned wood flooring introduces warmth without visual weight. Rather than relying on bold or saturated hues, depth is layered through subtle contrast. Brushed gold hardware and plumbing add warmth and refinement, while darker wood accents and select black or charcoal elements provide quiet definition.


Accent color is used sparingly and thoughtfully, favoring muted blues, soft greens, and restrained yellow tones that feel natural rather than decorative. These colors appear through furnishings, textiles, and art, enhancing the space without overpowering its calm, architectural clarity.


### Texture


A curated mix of textures brings comfort and depth to your warm contemporary home. Classic materials like linen, honey-toned woods, and travertine or sandstone are mainstays. To keep the aesthetic current, introduce tactile accents such as brushed brass, acrylic, or mohair. Consider textured wall finishes like plaster or paneled detailing for added visual depth. You can also elevate the space with wallpapered ceilings in soft metallics or botanical and geometric patterns, enhancing interest from every angle.


### Décor and Furniture


Contemporary interior design often incorporates mid-century modern and Scandinavian furniture with crisp lines. However, curved furniture is increasingly trending, adding a softer, inviting element. Consider pieces with rounded silhouettes and wood finishes in walnut, oak, or pecan to enhance warmth. Most importantly, select furniture that is functional and comfortable.
