# The Warm Transitional Collection: One of the New Designer Appointed Collections by Toll Brothers

Last updated on June 15th, 2026


Transitional interior design is a fusion of traditional and contemporary styles. Think clean lines softened by natural textures, neutral color palettes, and a blend of formal elements and casual accents. As the name suggests, warm transitional interior design emphasizes warm tones, offering an appealing alternative to the cooler hues popular in recent years. This approach to home interiors is ideal for homeowners who desire a grounded, classic, and eminently livable space. With its nod to current trends and foundation in classic design, transitional style remains perpetually in fashion.

## Refine Your Home’s Warm Transitional Style


The next step to creating the perfect Warm Transitional style in your new home is through color, texture, décor, and furniture. Read on to learn how to bring more Warm Transitional design into your new Toll Brothers home.


### Color


Anchor each room in warm neutrals, such as creamy whites, tans, soft taupes, and greiges, and add depth with richly toned accents. Pops of color are welcome, but often reserved for art, décor, or occasional furniture pieces.


### Texture


Dial up your home’s comfort and appeal by layering a variety of textures. Crisp linens, plush chenille, sleek velvet, smooth and hammered metallics, glass, and leather all fit this style. Choose materials with contrasting textures for textiles and décor to add depth and quiet sophistication to each space.


### Décor and Furniture


The décor and furniture selected for your home can significantly enhance its visual interest. For furniture, choose clean lines and soft curves. Classic materials such as leather or tweed highlight the traditional aspect of transitional style, while curved accent pieces with a splash of color embrace the contemporary. Aim for a blend of modern and vintage décor—pair sleek gold-framed mirrors with found artisanal pottery or offset contemporary art with lacquered wood pieces.
