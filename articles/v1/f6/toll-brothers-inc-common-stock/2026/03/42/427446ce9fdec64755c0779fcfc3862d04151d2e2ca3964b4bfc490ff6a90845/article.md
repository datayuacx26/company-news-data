## Enhance Your Home’s Cool Transitional Style


Our Cool Transitional Collection provides a perfect foundation that can be easily enhanced with the thoughtful addition of color, texture, décor, and furniture. Read on to discover how to incorporate more cool transitional interior design into your new Toll Brothers home.


### Color


Walls painted in soft whites with cool-neutral undertones such as blue, purple, or gray create a timeless foundation that’s easy to build upon. Draw inspiration from the rich navy kitchen island in this Collection and choose blue accents that add depth and serve as versatile neutrals. Add splashes of color through your art, décor, and furniture.


### Texture


Layering textures creates a calm and inviting atmosphere. Pair contrasting textures, such as crisp linen and plush faux fur, soft velvet and smooth leather, for a balanced look. Metals in brushed, polished, or hammered finishes introduce a refined sheen.


### Décor and Furniture


Transitional interior design encourages a mix of traditional and contemporary décor and furniture. For example, a transitional style living room may feature a plush, softly curved couch; a coffee table with clean lines in a neutral wood tone, such as white oak; and an abstract painting in an antique silver frame. Larger furniture pieces often lean more traditional, while décor elements like light fixtures, pillows, and artwork in cool tones introduce a fresh, contemporary touch.
