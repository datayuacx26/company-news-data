# 9 Design Styles Inspiring the Toll Brothers Designer Appointed Collections

Last updated on May 27th, 2026


Toll Brothers is known for the distinctive design and unrivaled choice it offers to luxury home shoppers across the country. Now, the Company is giving home shoppers the ultimate opportunity to leverage that expertise with its Toll Brothers Designer Appointed Collections offered in new home communities nationwide.


Showcasing[nine distinct interior design styles](https://www.tollbrothers.com/designer-appointed-collections) , Toll Brothers Designer Appointed Collections provide home shoppers with a curated selection of premier brands, materials, color palettes, and styles to serve as their new home’s conceptual or completed design aesthetic. From Classic Farmhouse to Cool Contemporary, the Designer Appointed Collections provide a comprehensive interior design solution for home shoppers that is innovative and on-trend.


Home shoppers who choose a Designer Appointed Collection will not only enjoy a comprehensive professional design plan for their home but also benefit from the streamlined decision-making process and expedited timeline for the construction of their new home. Each Designer Appointed Collection includes thoughtful selections for every fixture and finish of the home at a variety of investment levels, providing a cohesive design aesthetic for any budget.


Toll Brothers created its Designer Appointed Collections with the help of[Ryan Young Interiors](https://ryanyounginteriors.com/) , a nationally recognized, award-winning design firm with whom Toll Brothers has worked for many years in creating its stunning model homes across the country.


Join us as we dive into the nine design styles that inspired the Designer Appointed Collections and discover what makes each of them an ideal choice for your new Toll Brothers home. Whether you are building a new Build to Order home from the ground up or searching for a[Quick Move-In home](https://www.tollbrothers.com/qmi) already under construction, choosing one of the Toll Brothers Designer Appointed Collections is sure to be an exciting step in your homebuilding journey.


Which Designer Appointed Collection is the perfect fit for your new Toll Brothers home?


## Classic Farmhouse


[Classic farmhouse](https://www.tollbrothers.com/blog/the-classic-farmhouse-designer-appointed-collection) is a timeless and charming style that pays homage to the rustic simplicity and enduring charm of rural farmhouses. This style embraces the warm, cozy, inviting atmosphere, blending traditional and practical elements. Classic farmhouse design captures the essence of country living, making it an ideal choice for those seeking a comfortable and nostalgic home.


[Sterling Grove by Toll Brothers in Surprise, AZ](https://www.tollbrothers.com/luxury-homes-for-sale/Arizona/Sterling-Grove)


## Modern Farmhouse


[Modern farmhouse](https://www.tollbrothers.com/blog/the-modern-farmhouse-designer-appointed-collection) interior design combines elements of traditional farmhouses with modern furniture, earth-toned colors, and warm metals. This fusion results in a space that strikes the perfect balance between rustic warmth and sleek sophistication, making it a sought-after choice for homeowners who appreciate both comfort and style.


[Hearthfield by Toll Brothers in Royersford, PA](https://www.tollbrothers.com/luxury-homes-for-sale/Pennsylvania/Hearthfield)


## Modern Organic


[Modern organic](https://www.tollbrothers.com/blog/the-modern-organic-designer-appointed-collection) is a captivating interior design style that seamlessly merges the staples of modern design with natural elements. This aesthetic emphasizes a deep connection with nature, making it an ideal choice for those who seek a balance between modern aesthetics and a soothing, eco-friendly atmosphere within their living spaces.


[Edge at Downtown Superior by Toll Brothers in Superior, CO](https://www.tollbrothers.com/luxury-homes-for-sale/Colorado/Downtown-Superior/Edge)


## Modern Art Deco


[Modern Art Deco](https://www.tollbrothers.com/blog/the-modern-art-deco-designer-appointed-collection) is a classic, sophisticated interior design style that seamlessly blends the opulence and glamour of the Art Deco movement from the early 20th century with a contemporary sensibility. This design aesthetic marries the best of both worlds, creating a space that exudes timeless elegance and a touch of avant-garde. This style incorporates bold patterns, rich jewel-toned colors, and high-contrast elements.


[Northbrooke by Toll Brothers in Cumming, GA](https://www.tollbrothers.com/luxury-homes-for-sale/Georgia/Northbrooke)


## Modern Mid-Century


[Modern mid-century](https://www.tollbrothers.com/blog/the-modern-mid-century-designer-appointed-collection) is a captivating fusion of timeless charm and contemporary sophistication. The style offers a fresh take on the classic elements that defined the mid-20th century. This design aesthetic harmoniously combines organic shapes, clean lines, and natural materials to create spaces that are both visually striking and highly functional.


[Bella Strada by Toll Brothers in Henderson, NV](https://www.tollbrothers.com/luxury-homes-for-sale/Nevada/Bella-Strada)


## Warm Transitional


[Warm transitional](https://www.tollbrothers.com/blog/the-warm-transitional-designer-appointed-collection) interior design is an inviting and versatile style that strikes a perfect balance between traditional and contemporary aesthetics. It combines the timeless elegance of classic design elements with the comfort and warmth of more modern and relaxed features, creating spaces that are both sophisticated and memorable. Warm transitional is a style that combines warm woods, soft whites, and rich textures.


[Toll Brothers at George’s Ranch in Boerne, TX](https://www.tollbrothers.com/luxury-homes-for-sale/Texas/Toll-Brothers-at-George%27s-Ranch)


## Cool Transitional


[Cool transitional](https://www.tollbrothers.com/blog/the-cool-transitional-designer-appointed-collection) interior design is a timeless and sophisticated style that blends the enduring charm of traditional design with the sleek, clean lines of contemporary aesthetics. This style incorporates materials and finishes with a cool color palette, such as cool greys and soft blues. This design approach offers a harmonious fusion of classic and modern elements, resulting in spaces that feel both inviting and effortlessly elegant.


[Toll Brothers at Tesoro Club in Port St. Lucie, FL](https://www.tollbrothers.com/luxury-homes-for-sale/Florida/Toll-Brothers-at-Tesoro-Club)


## Warm Contemporary


[Warm contemporary](https://www.tollbrothers.com/blog/the-warm-contemporary-designer-appointed-collection) is a captivating interior design style that effortlessly marries the clean lines and minimal aesthetic of contemporary design with the inviting, organic warmth of natural wood elements. This style creates a harmonious blend of modern sophistication and cozy comfort, making spaces feel welcoming and timeless.


[Laurel Pointe Lake Nona by Toll Brothers in Orlando, FL](https://www.tollbrothers.com/luxury-homes-for-sale/Florida/Laurel-Pointe)


## Cool Contemporary


[Cool contemporary](https://www.tollbrothers.com/blog/the-cool-contemporary-designer-appointed-collection) interior design embraces the principles of minimalism. Rooted in clean lines and simplicity, this style exudes a refined, understated elegance. Cool contemporary design is perfect for those who appreciate a sleek living environment that blends the serenity of cool-toned, calming hues with the crisp lines of contemporary design.


[Griffith Lakes by Toll Brothers in Charlotte, NC](https://www.tollbrothers.com/)
