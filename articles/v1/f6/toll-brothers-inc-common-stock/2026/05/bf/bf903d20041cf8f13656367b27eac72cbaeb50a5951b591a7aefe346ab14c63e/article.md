## What is Ridgefield, Washington Known For?


Ridgefield, Washington, is a city[rich in history](https://ridgefieldwa.us/404/Brief-Timeline) , from its deep Native American roots to its ties to the Lewis and Clark Expedition. Ridgefield’s charming historic downtown serves as a vibrant hub for the community, hosting various events that celebrate the city’s past, including the beloved[Clark County Fair](https://www.clarkcoeventcenter.com/) .


Just outside the city, the[Ridgefield National Wildlife Refuge](https://www.fws.gov/refuge/ridgefield) offers a serene escape, preserving Oregon oak woodlands, open pastures, and vital wetlands that provide sanctuary for protected species such as the Columbian white-tailed deer and sandhill cranes.


## Things To Do in Ridgefield, Washington


### The Regional Wine Experience


Ridgefield offers an array of inviting wineries just minutes from home, including[Windy Hills Winery](http://windyhillswinery.com/) ,[Bethany Vineyard & Winery](https://www.bethanyvineyard.com/) , and[Columbia Ridge Winery](https://columbiaridgewinery.com/) . Open year-round, these venues host a variety of wine experiences, from relaxed tastings to curated events, all set against a picturesque backdrop.


### Ridgefield Outdoor Recreation


Spanning 53 acres, the[Ridgefield Outdoor Recreation Complex](https://ridgefieldwa.us/296/Ridgefield-Outdoor-Recreation-Complex) is communal center for sports, recreation, and more. Open to both the[Ridgefield School District](https://www.ridgefieldsd.org/) and local residents, the complex features multipurpose sports fields, scenic walking trails, playgrounds, and more, all thoughtfully designed to engage visitors of every age. Another scenic spot,[Paradise Point State Park](https://parks.wa.gov/find-parks/state-parks/paradise-point-state-park) , offers hiking, fishing, and access to the[Lewis River](https://www.americanrivers.org/river/lewis-river/) . With endless ways to stay active and enjoy the outdoors, Ridgefield is an ideal destination for family fun and everyday recreation.


### Historic Downtown Ridgefield


Ridgefield’s charming historic downtown invites community, connection, and discovery. From locally owned shops to diverse dining experiences, it’s the perfect place to spend a relaxed afternoon with friends or family. Favorites like[Ridgefield Craft Brewing Co. Taphouse](https://www.ridgefieldcraftbrewing.com/) and[The Neighborhood Refuge](https://www.theneighborhoodrefuge.com/) offer a distinct taste of the local flavor, all set within a warm, walkable setting.


### Ridgefield’s Natural Beauty


Surrounded by open green spaces, scenic trails, and wildlife preserves, Ridgefield offers opportunities for year-round exploration. Paired with a vibrant community and engaging local attractions, Ridgefield delivers a lifestyle that feels grounded, enriching, and inspired by nature.


## Ridgefield’s Convenient Location


Ideally positioned with direct access to I-5, Ridgefield offers a seamless commute to nearby hubs including Vancouver, Camas, and Portland, each less than 30 minutes away. ## Top-Rated School Districts


Families in Ridgefield benefit from access to the highly regarded[Ridgefield School District](https://www.ridgefieldsd.org/) , consistently ranked among the top in Washington State. Known for its close-knit community and dedication to academic excellence, the district fosters an environment where students are supported, challenged, and inspired.
