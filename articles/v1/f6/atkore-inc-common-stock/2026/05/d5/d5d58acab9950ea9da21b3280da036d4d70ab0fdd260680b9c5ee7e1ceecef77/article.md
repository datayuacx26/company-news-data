## Atkore Inc. to Attend the 2026 KeyBanc Industrials & Basic Materials Conference


05/14/2026


Download (opens in new window)


HARVEY, Ill.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Atkore Inc. (the “Company”) (NYSE: ATKR), a leading manufacturer of electrical products for commercial, industrial, data center, and solar applications, today announced that John Deitzer, Chief Financial Officer, and Matt Kline, Vice President of Treasury & Investor Relations, are scheduled to participate in investor meetings at the KeyBanc Industrials & Basic Materials Conference on May 27, 2026 in Boston, MA.


To learn more about Atkore Inc. please visit the company's website at[https://investors.atkore.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.atkore.com&esheet=54536424&newsitemid=20260514803322&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.atkore.com&index=1&md5=08292d2d7be52405bca55a068a376938)
