### How to Optimize AI Prompts for High-Quality Thank-You Notes


AI-generated thank-you notes are only as good as the prompts you provide. To save time while ensuring **clear, thoughtful responses** , your prompts should be **brief, structured, and specific** .


### Example Optimized AI Prompt for Kevin Sun's Thank-You Note


Instead of a long, detailed prompt, a well-engineered but concise version could be:


*"Write a warm, professional thank-you email to Kevin Sun (CEO, Dex) after a networking chat. Mention Dex’s approach to personal CRM, his insights on small, consistent follow-ups, and his SF Half Marathon recommendation."*


This **prompt** still provides:1. **The recipient's name & role** – Ensures context.2. **Key topics** – Guides AI to include relevant details.3. **Tone & intent** – Specifies warmth and professionalism.


### How to Structure Effective AI Prompts


To get **high-quality** AI-generated thank-you notes while saving time, follow these principles:


-


**Keep it under 3 sentences** – AI works best with **concise, directive prompts** .


-


**Include only essential details** – Focus on **who, what, and key takeaways.**


-


**Specify tone & format** – Words like *warm, professional, concise* help set the right style.


-


**Avoid over-explaining** – AI fills in gaps, so **keep it direct and to the point** .


By using **Dex Copilot** , this thank-you note was generated in seconds, **automatically incorporating relevant meeting details without manual input** . AI drafts high-quality, personalized thank-you notes in seconds—so you can focus on **building relationships** , not writing emails.
