# Dex vs Contacts+: Which Personal CRM Is Best for Managing Relationships?

If you're looking for a contact management app that actually helps you maintain professional and personal relationships, you've probably come across both Dex and Contacts+. On the surface, they might seem similar. Both are[personal CRMs with mobile apps](https://getdex.com/blog/6-top-personal-crm-software-with-mobile-apps/) , both connect to your address book, and both promise to help you stay organized.


But dig a little deeper and you'll find two very different tools built for very different goals. Contacts+ is designed around clean, synced contact data. Dex is designed around the harder problem: remembering to follow up, staying in touch, and building relationships that actually last.


This comparison breaks down the key differences so you can choose the right tool for how you work.


## **Dex and Contacts+ at a Glance**


### [Dex](https://getdex.com/) **- Free to start; $12/month, billed annually**


Dex is a personal CRM built for people who want to be intentional about how they[maintain relationships](https://getdex.com/blog/personal-connections/) . It pulls interaction history from your email, calendar, LinkedIn, and now Outlook, then surfaces reminders and context when it's time to reach out.


In 2026, Dex's[AI features](https://getdex.com/docs/ai/messaging-dex) have become noticeably smarter, offering pre-meeting briefings, message suggestions personalized to your history with a contact, and auto-grouping of contacts based on shared context. You can even add notes or get reminders via SMS and WhatsApp, making it easy to capture moments on the go.


### [Contacts+](https://www.contactsplus.com/) **- Free tier available; $13.99/month, billed monthly**


Contacts+ is a contact management app focused on keeping your address book clean, current, and unified. It syncs contacts across Google, iCloud, and your phone, merges duplicates, and enriches profiles with social data. If your contact list is a mess of duplicates, outdated numbers, and missing details, Contacts+ is built to fix that.


It's useful for people who want their contacts well-organized, but it stops short of helping you decide who to call, when to reach out, or what to say.

## **Contact Management App: What Each Tool Actually Does**


### **How Contacts+ approaches contact management**


Contacts+ excels at solving a specific, concrete problem: your contacts are scattered, duplicated, and out of date. The app pulls everything together from multiple sources, merges duplicate entries, and helps you fill in missing information.


For people who've accumulated contacts across years of job changes, phone upgrades, and platform switches, Contacts+ does the cleanup work that nobody wants to do manually. It's a reliable tool for staying in touch in the most basic sense, having accurate, up-to-date information for the people you already know.


What it doesn't do is tell you who you should be reaching out to, when you last spoke, or what you talked about.


### **How Dex approaches contact management**


Dex takes a different angle. Rather than organizing your contacts, it helps you act on your relationships. It logs interactions automatically from[multiple synced accounts](https://getdex.com/integrations/) like Gmail, Google Calendar, LinkedIn, and Outlook, building a timeline for each contact so you always have context before a call or meeting.


[Follow-up reminders](https://getdex.com/docs/workflows/reminders) are the core of the experience. Dex prompts you to check in when it's been too long since you connected, suggests who to reach out to, and even drafts personalized messages based on your history. It's the difference between a well-organized address book and a system that actively helps you maintain professional relationships.


## **Tools for Staying in Touch: Which One Actually Helps?**


Both apps can technically be described as tools for staying in touch, but the experience is completely different.


With Contacts+, staying in touch means having the right phone number or email address when you need it. The app makes sure your contact data is clean and accessible, but the follow-through is entirely up to you.


With Dex, staying in touch is the product. Reminders surface automatically. Interaction history is logged without manual effort. And when you do reach out, you have context, recent notes, past meeting details, and a message suggestion that reflects the actual relationship.


For casual personal use, Contacts+ might be enough. For anyone serious about how to maintain professional relationships, whether you're a founder, a sales leader, a recruiter, or someone who simply values their network, Dex is built for the job.

## **Frequently Asked Questions About Contact Management Apps**


### **What is a contact management app?**


A contact management app is a tool that helps you store, organize, and access information about the people in your network. The best apps go beyond a basic address book to include interaction history, follow-up reminders, and integrations with email and calendar tools — making it easier to maintain relationships over time rather than just storing contact details.


### **What is the difference between a contact management app and a personal CRM?**


A contact management app focuses on organizing and maintaining accurate contact data. A personal CRM goes further by tracking interactions, prompting follow-ups, and helping you manage relationships over time. Dex is a personal CRM; Contacts+ is primarily a contact management app.


### **What are the best tools for staying in touch professionally?**


The best tools for staying in touch depend on how proactive you want to be. For passive organization, Contacts+ works well. For active relationship management — with reminders, interaction history, and AI-assisted outreach — Dex is built specifically for that purpose.
