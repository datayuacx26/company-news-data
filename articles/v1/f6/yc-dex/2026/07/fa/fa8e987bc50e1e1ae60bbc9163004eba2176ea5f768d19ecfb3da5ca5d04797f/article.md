# Dex vs Nat: Best Personal CRM for Relationship Building?

Dex and Nat are both personal CRMs built to help you stay on top of your professional relationships, but they solve the problem differently.[Dex](https://getdex.com/) is designed for managing your entire network, bringing together reminders, follow-ups, and interaction history from LinkedIn, email, calendars, and messaging apps.


[Nat](https://www.nat.app/) , on the other hand, is built primarily around Gmail, using your email and calendar activity to identify who you're losing touch with and making it easy to take notes without leaving your inbox. If you're deciding between the two, here's how they compare in features, integrations, pricing, and overall value.


## **Dex And Nat As A Personal CRM**


### **Dex - $12 per month, paid annually**


Dex is designed for people who want to stay in touch consistently. Its core value lies in remembering when and how to follow up, with interaction history pulled from connected tools like email, calendars, and social media accounts.


In 2026, Dex's AI became smarter at understanding your relationships, automatically surfacing context from emails, calendars, and notes, summarizing past interactions, and suggesting the best ways to reach out.


### **Nat - $370/year, no free tier, 10-day free trial**


Nat is designed for consultants, founders, and CEOs whose relationships live in email. It automatically figures out who you're losing touch with based on your Gmail and calendar data, categorizes contacts by relationship strength, and emails you around meetings so you can jot notes without opening another app.

## **Which Offers More Value?**


Dex Premium is $12/month billed annually with unlimited contacts, LinkedIn sync, Gmail and calendar integrations, messaging sync, reminders, and AI features. Professional is $20/month with Outlook, mail merge, and API/Zapier.


Nat has no free plan, and its public pricing is less transparent. At comparable prices, the value question becomes coverage: Nat sees your inbox; Dex sees your inbox plus LinkedIn, messaging, and calendar.


## **Choosing Between Dex and Nat**


Choose Dex if your network is bigger than your inbox, if the people who matter also live on LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and at events. Choose Nat if effectively all of your relationship activity flows through Gmail and you want the lightest possible layer on top of it.


Both tools work, but they support different habits. The better choice is the one that matches how you already work.


## **How Dex and Nat Handle Personal CRM Workflows**


### **After setting up**


**Dex:** Connect LinkedIn, Gmail, calendar, and messaging; your network syncs in within minutes.


**Nat:** Connect Gmail and calendar; Nat analyzes email patterns and auto-categorizes contacts by relationship strength. Fast setup, but blind to anything outside email.


### **After meeting someone new**


**Dex:** Add the contact or scan their card, note where you met, set a reminder. Works even when there's no email trail yet.


**Nat:** If you emailed or met via calendar, Nat picks them up and emails you to take notes. If you only connected on LinkedIn or exchanged numbers, there's nothing to detect.


### **Tracking long-term relationships**


**Dex:** Keep-in-touch reminders on your cadence, job change alerts, and pre-meeting briefs keep hundreds of relationships warm over years.


**Nat:** Losing-touch detection nudges you when email frequency fades — effective for email-based relationships, invisible for everyone else.

## **Where Nat Stands Out**


Nat's email-based note-taking is genuinely clever: you get an email around each meeting, reply with your notes, and Nat resurfaces them before you meet that person again. For consultants who spend their entire day in Gmail, that frictionless loop — with no new app to open — is Nat's best argument.


## **Why Dex Stands Out as the Better Personal CRM for Most People**


While both Dex and Nat help you stay in touch, Dex stands out because real networks don't live only in email. LinkedIn sync, messaging integrations, mobile apps, job change alerts, and AI features mean Dex keeps working where Nat stops seeing.


At the end of the day, the best CRM is the one that fits your workflow. Try Dex and see how it fits into your routine.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**


### **Is Dex better than Nat?**


Dex covers LinkedIn, messaging, mobile, and email, while Nat covers email. Nat is a reasonable pick if Gmail is genuinely the only place your relationships happen.


### **Does Nat have a free plan?**


No. Nat offers a trial but no permanent free tier. Dex also has no free tier; it offers a 7-day trial, then $12/month billed annually.


### **Does Nat sync with LinkedIn?**


No. Nat works from your Gmail and calendar data. Dex syncs up to 9,000 LinkedIn connections and alerts you to job changes.


### **Can I import my contacts from Nat to Dex?**


Yes, export your contacts and[import the CSV into Dex](https://getdex.com/integrations/csv) . Dex deduplicates against your synced sources automatically.


### **Which is better for consultants?**


Both target consultants. If your client relationships run through email, Nat's lightweight loop works; if you also source work through LinkedIn and referrals across channels, Dex gives you the fuller picture.
