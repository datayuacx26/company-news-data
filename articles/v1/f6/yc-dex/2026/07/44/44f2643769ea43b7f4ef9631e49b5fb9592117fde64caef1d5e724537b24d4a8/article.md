# Dex vs Monica: Best Personal CRM in 2026?

Dex and Monica are both[personal CRM tools](https://getdex.com/blog/personal-crm-for-networking/) , but they solve different problems. Dex is built around reminders, follow-ups, and keeping track of past interactions automatically. Monica is a privacy-first tool for manually logging personal-life details like family relationships, gifts, and journals.


This comparison is for people managing their personal relationships, not sales teams running pipelines. If you're deciding between Dex and Monica, the right choice comes down to how you want to interact with your network day to day.


## Dex and Monica as a personal CRM


### Dex - $12 per month, paid annually


Dex is for people who want to stay in touch consistently. It remembers when and how to follow up, with interaction history pulled from connected tools like email, calendars, and social media accounts.


In 2026, Dex's AI got better at understanding your relationships. It surfaces context from emails, calendars, and notes automatically, summarizes past interactions, and suggests ways to reach out.


### Monica - free self-hosted, or Monica Cloud from $9 per month


Monica is for people who value privacy and data ownership above automation. It's open source and self-hostable, so your contact data can live on your own server for free. Where it shines is personal-life detail: family relationships, gift logs, journaling tied to contacts, and birthday reminders, all entered manually.


## Dex vs Monica: key differences and best use cases


Dex is a better fit if you:


-


Want automatic data capture from LinkedIn, email, calendar, and messaging apps


-


Want reminders, pre-meeting briefs, and job change alerts that run on real data


-


Need native iOS and Android apps and a business card scanner


-


Are a founder, MBA student, freelancer, or professional networker


Monica is a better fit if you:


-


Want an open-source, self-hostable tool where you own the data outright


-


Use it for your personal life (friends, family, gifts, journals) rather than professional networking


-


Are comfortable with fully manual data entry and no email or LinkedIn sync

## Which offers more value?


Pricing can be a deciding factor, especially if you're choosing a tool for personal use.


Dex is $12 per month billed annually (Premium), with a Professional plan at $20 per month that adds LinkedIn sync for up to 9,000 contacts, Outlook, mail merge, and API/Zapier access. Both come with a 7-day free trial.


Monica is free if you self-host it on your own server (it's open source), and Monica Cloud, the hosted version, starts around $9 per month.


On sticker price the two are close ($9 vs $12), and self-hosted Monica is free if you run your own server. The difference is what the money buys. Monica Cloud is hosting for a manual system. Dex Premium includes LinkedIn sync, email history, messaging sync, reminders, and briefs that keep themselves current.


## Choosing between Dex and Monica


Choose Dex if your main problem is forgetting to follow up or losing track of past conversations across a professional network. Choose Monica if your main problem is wanting a private, self-hosted place to log personal-life details like gifts, family relationships, and journals.


Both tools work, but they support different habits. The better choice is the one that matches how you already work, not the one with more features.

## Where Monica stands out


Monica was built to help you be a better friend and family member. It maps family relationships, tracks gifts given over the years, links journal entries to specific people, and reminds you of birthdays. Its answer to privacy is self-hosting: the code is open source and the data lives on your server.


Dex's answer to privacy is certification and business model. It's subscription-funded with no ads, never sells data, is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified, and lets you export or delete your data at any time. Both are legitimate approaches; they just suit different people.


## Why Dex is the better personal CRM for most people


Both Dex and Monica help you manage relationships, but Dex is built for follow-through. If you need a system that reminds you to stay on track with the people who matter, the automatic integrations and reminders do the remembering for you.


That's the practical test to apply: will you actually keep the tool up to date? Monica stays accurate only as long as you keep typing. Dex stays accurate because your email, calendar, and LinkedIn keep it that way. Try Dex and see how it fits into your routine.

## Frequently asked questions


### Is Dex better than Monica?


For professional networking, yes. Dex automates data capture and adds LinkedIn, email, and messaging sync that Monica doesn't have. For privacy-first personal-life logging on your own server, Monica is the better fit. They're built around different priorities.


### Can I import contacts from Monica to Dex?


Yes. Monica supports exporting your contacts, and you can[import a CSV into Dex](https://getdex.com/integrations/csv) . Dex deduplicates against contacts already synced from LinkedIn, Gmail, and other sources.


### How should I compare Dex and Monica pricing?


Self-hosted Monica is free but costs server time and manual upkeep; Monica Cloud starts around $9/mo. Dex Premium is $12/mo and includes automatic integrations Monica has no equivalent for. Compare on total effort, not just dollars.


### Does Monica sync with LinkedIn or email?


No. Monica has no LinkedIn integration, no email sync, and no calendar sync, and it has no mobile app. All interaction logging is manual. That's a deliberate design choice in favor of privacy and simplicity.


### Does Dex have a free plan?


No. Dex offers a seven-day free trial; after that, Premium is $12/month and Professional is $20/month, both billed annually.
