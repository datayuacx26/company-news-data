## What Cloze actually does


Image from cloze.com


[Cloze](https://ai.cloze.com/platform/real-estate-crm/) is an AI-powered relationship management platform. In practice, it does three things:


**1. Passive data capture.** Connect your Gmail or Outlook, phone, calendar, and social accounts. Cloze logs every email, call, text, meeting, and social interaction to the relevant contact record. You do not have to log anything manually. This is Cloze's core strength and the reason people sign up.


**2. AI-driven follow-up suggestions.** Each morning, Cloze generates a daily agenda of people it thinks you should contact. The timing is driven by a combination of each contact's stage and segment — so you can set it to remind you every 3 days about warm sellers or every quarter about closed buyers. Cloze also factors in your communication patterns, detecting when relationships are going cold, and surfaces birthdays, anniversaries, and work milestones. The agenda is the feature that keeps users loyal.


Image from cloze.com


**3. Deal and relationship pipeline.** On Business tiers, Cloze adds pipeline management, lead routing, and marketing automation. This is where the product has expanded most aggressively since 2024, with deep integrations into real estate workflows.

## What Cloze does well (the honest pros)


### Auto-logging is genuinely set-and-forget


This is the feature that earns Cloze its loyalists. Connect Gmail or Outlook, and every email is logged to the right contact. Connect your phone, and calls and texts get tracked too. Calendar events are attached automatically.


The result is a contact timeline that builds itself. When you open a contact card before a meeting, you see every touchpoint without having typed a single note. For people who refuse to do manual data entry (which is most people), this is the killer feature.


Image from cloze.com


### The daily agenda changes your morning routine


Cloze's AI-generated daily agenda is not a gimmick. It analyzes your communication frequency with each contact, detects when relationships are going cold, and surfaces 5-10 follow-up suggestions each morning. Several reviewers describe this as the feature they would miss most if they left.


The agenda also includes birthdays, anniversaries, and work milestones pulled from synced data. For real estate agents maintaining a sphere of hundreds of past clients, this is genuinely valuable.


### Setup is fast


You can be operational in under 10 minutes. Connect email, grant phone access, and Cloze starts building your contact database. There is no import process or manual configuration required to start seeing value. Compared to CRMs that require weeks of setup and data migration, this is a real advantage.


### Ghostwriter drafts decent emails


The AI Ghostwriter feature, available on all paid plans, drafts emails based on your relationship context. It is not revolutionary, but it saves time on routine follow-ups and outreach messages. For real estate contexts, Cloze includes pre-built prompts trained on common agent communications (listing updates, price reductions, anniversary notes). {{VERIFY: Ghostwriter availability on Pro plan -- check pricing page features}}


## Where Cloze falls short (the honest cons)


### The auto-merge problem is real and documented


This is the single most important thing to know about Cloze. The platform automatically identifies contacts it believes are duplicates and merges them without asking for confirmation.


When the AI gets it right, this saves time. When it gets it wrong, you lose data.


We reviewed complaints across G2, TrustRadius, Capterra, and SourceForge. The auto-merge issue appeared on all four platforms, spanning reviews from 2022 through 2025. This is not a bug that got fixed. It is a design decision.


The merge tends to go wrong with common names, contacts at the same company, or people who share email domains. For anyone managing high-value relationships (investors, recruiters, consultants), losing the history on a key contact is not an acceptable risk.


### The real estate pivot has left general users behind


Cloze launched as a personal CRM for anyone. In 2024-2025, the product pivoted hard toward real estate. The homepage now leads with "AI-powered real estate CRM." Feature development prioritizes property profiles, MLS integrations, and brokerage workflows. The AI assistant Maia was trained on real estate contexts.


If you are a real estate agent, this is great. If you are a consultant, founder, investor, or job seeker, you are now using a product that is optimizing for someone else. The help center articles, templates, and AI prompts increasingly assume a real estate workflow. General users report feeling like afterthoughts.


### No LinkedIn sync at any price


Cloze does not offer native LinkedIn integration. For a CRM that markets itself on automatic data capture, the absence of the world's largest professional network is a glaring gap. You cannot import LinkedIn connections, track job changes, or save contacts from LinkedIn profiles.


If your professional network primarily lives on LinkedIn (and for most knowledge workers, it does), this is a significant limitation.


### No social media integrations


Cloze used to connect with social networks to pull interactions into contact timelines, but all social media integrations were discontinued for new users in April 2023. Twitter support was killed entirely. If you sign up today, there is no way to connect any social account to Cloze.


### Custom fields cost $29/mo


Most CRMs include custom fields in their base tier. Cloze locks them behind Business Gold at $29/user/mo. If your workflow requires tracking anything beyond Cloze's default fields (industry, deal stage, custom tags), you are paying nearly double the entry price to get there.


### The interface overwhelms new users


Multiple G2 reviewers describe the interface as "too full of information" and "too much not to be distracted." The density of auto-logged data creates noise. When every email, call, and social interaction appears on every contact timeline, finding the signal in the noise takes practice.


The learning curve is steeper than most personal CRMs. If you want a tool that takes 15 minutes to master, Cloze is not it.

## Frequently asked questions


### Is Cloze CRM free?


No. Cloze does not offer a permanently free tier. The 14-day trial gives full access to evaluate the product, but you must subscribe to continue using it. The cheapest option is Cloze Pro at $17/mo billed annually.


### Does Cloze work with LinkedIn?


No. Cloze has no native LinkedIn integration, no LinkedIn browser extension, and no automatic LinkedIn connection import. This is one of its most significant gaps for professionals whose networks are LinkedIn-centric.


### Is Cloze good for real estate?


Yes. Cloze has invested heavily in real estate features since 2024, including property profiles, MLS integrations (Zillow, MoxiWorks, SkySlope, Lone Wolf), AI prompts trained on agent workflows, and the Maia AI assistant.


### Does Cloze auto-merge contacts?


Yes. Cloze automatically identifies and merges contacts it considers duplicates. This is a core design decision, not a bug. When the match is correct, it saves time. When incorrect, it causes data loss that requires manual reconstruction. This is the most frequently cited complaint across G2, TrustRadius, and Capterra reviews.


### Can Cloze replace a full sales CRM like Salesforce or HubSpot?


For small teams with simple workflows, Cloze Business Platinum ($42/user/mo) offers pipeline management, lead routing, and marketing automation. But it lacks the reporting depth, customization, and third-party app ecosystem of Salesforce or HubSpot. For teams larger than 10 people or with complex sales processes, Cloze is probably too limited.
