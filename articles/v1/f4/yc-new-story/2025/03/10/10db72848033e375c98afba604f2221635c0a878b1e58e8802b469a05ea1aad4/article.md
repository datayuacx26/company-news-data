# How Housing Empowers Women to Break the Cycle of Poverty

When Esmeralda received her land title, she didn't just get a piece of paper – she gained control over her future. As a single mother, land ownership meant stability, safety, and the ability to build generational wealth for her son. Esmeralda's story represents a powerful truth we've witnessed repeatedly in our work: when women own property, entire communities thrive.


### The Gender Gap in Property Ownership


The reality is stark: women are significantly less likely to own property than men across Latin America. This disparity perpetuates cycles of poverty and vulnerability that disproportionately affect women and children. Without property rights, women often lack the collateral needed to access loans, start businesses, or create financial security for their families.


But we want to break the cycle – starting with home.


### Breaking Traditional Patterns


New Story aims for at least 60% of our participating landowners to be women. This is double the current national average in Mexico (30%). We’ve already seen this come to life in our recent projects in partnership with UR+PA including in Ahualulco, where 66.5% of the participants are women. This is a profound shift in who has access to economic power and opportunity.


When women own property, we see positive impacts in:


- Physical Security: Land ownership provides women with stable housing and protection from displacement.
- Financial Independence: Property serves as collateral for loans and creates opportunities for economic advancement.
- Generational Impact: Children, especially daughters, grow up seeing women as property owners and financial decision-makers.
- Community Leadership: Women with secure housing are more likely to participate in community development and local governance.


### Creating Systemic Change


Traditional charity models often overlook the systemic barriers that keep women from accessing property rights. That's why our approach starts with making land ownership accessible and affordable through market-based solutions. By working with local municipalities, financial institutions, and developers, we're creating pathways for women to participate in the formal housing market.


With land serving as a base for income generation, collateral for credit, and a means of savings for the future, women landowners can lift themselves out of poverty. They become active participants in the market and can build a formal relationship with a bank and access a loan for their home and business. Simply owning land increases net worth, and it also increases financial opportunities. This improvement of livelihood ultimately transcends to their children.


### Beyond Four Walls


A home is more than shelter — it's a foundation for transformation. For women entrepreneurs like Esmeralda, home ownership provides the stability needed to start and grow businesses. As she designs her home with our team, she dreams of having a second floor for her growing family and a space to operate her stylist and makeup business.


Every land title granted to a woman represents a step toward greater gender equity. Every home owned by a mother creates new possibilities for her children. And every community where women hold property rights becomes stronger, more resilient, and more prosperous.


#### Empowering women through property rights isn't just about housing — it's about creating lasting social change.
