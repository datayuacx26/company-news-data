# Keystone Debuts Montana High Country 391TB: Two Beds, Two Full Baths

A longtime leader in luxury fifth wheels, Keystone RV’s all-new 2027 Montana High Country 391TB floor plan combines the residential comforts of their luxury line with a true two-bedroom, two-bathroom layout, ideal for large families or those traveling with company.


## Dual Sanctuaries


Images by Camping World


The standout feature of the[391TB](https://cwrv.link/b2026montanahighcountry) is its configuration: two dedicated bedrooms, each with a full bathroom nearby. While many competing models offer a half-bath, the 391TB provides two complete washrooms with walk-in showers, linen storage, a porcelain toilet, and a decorative seamless vanity in each.


This rear bathroom strategically includes a dedicated exterior entrance. This “mudroom” style entry allows guests or children to use the facilities directly from the campsite without tracking dirt through the main living area. With a king bed, reading lights, bedside outlets, TV prep, and excellent wardrobe storage in each bedroom, the only choice is which one becomes your primary!

## The Upper Loft


Images by Camping World


Perhaps the most visually striking innovation in the[Montana High Country 391TB](https://www.keystonerv.com/product/montana-high-country/luxury-fifth-wheels/floorplans/391TB) is the rolling “Beauty-and-the-Beast” ladder. Reminiscent of a classic library, this ladder glides to your ideal location to access the massive three-bed loft.


Unlike cramped lofts found in older fifth wheels, this space boasts 35 inches of floor-to-ceiling height, allowing children to sit up comfortably. The loft is equipped with TV hookups for gaming, dual opposing windows for cross-ventilation, sleeping space for 3-4 little ones, and a sliding door that provides a new level of privacy compared to most[5th wheels with loft floor plans](https://blog.campingworld.com/find-your-rv/fifth-wheel-with-loft/) .


## Versatile Living, Dining, and Cooking Area


Images by Camping World


The dining and living areas focus on multi-purpose utility. The transforming “deskette” offers the ideal space for remote work — until you’re ready to eat. Then, it extends and pairs with a set of four freestanding chairs to create a full dining setup.


The living room is rounded out by a 75” pull-out sofa, dual theater seats, and a decorative entertainment wall with a built-in fireplace.


The U-shaped kitchen is a chef’s dream, featuring:


- **Solid surface countertops** with a full-height backsplash.
- **Accent lighting** beneath the cabinetry.
- **A workstation sink** and a massive residential refrigerator.
- **Innovative trash management** combining a dedicated trash can cabinet beneath the sink with a drop-through “porthole” — allowing you to customize your trash/recycling setup and access whatever’s collected beneath the “porthole” directly from outside.


## Exterior Performance and Comfort


Image by Camping World


Despite clocking in at nearly 44 feet, the 391TB handles smoothly, thanks to the 8,000-pound Curt® slipper roller axle suspension, the Helux™ pin box, and H-rated tires. To maximize the outdoor experience, Keystone added the Lippert® Titan™ touchscreen leveling system, an innovative water management system with shut-offs for every fixture, and three power awnings, providing nearly total coverage across the entire camp-side.


The exterior kitchen serves as the hub for outdoor entertaining, complete with a beverage fridge, TV, and a propane quick connect for grilling. And the outside shower comes with a quick hookup hose for easy foot, paw, or gear rinses after your adventures.


## Climate Control


To ensure the 44-foot coach stays comfortable in any climate, Keystone has equipped the 391TB with two standard A/C units and pre-wiring to add a third. A 35K BTU furnace and 60K BTU tankless water heater handle ducted heat and hot water, while a decorative fireplace heater provides supplemental warmth in the living area.


For extended-season capability, the Four Season Living package on the Montana High Country is tested and approved for 0 usage. Here are some additional features that make this new 5th wheel adaptable to more extreme temperatures:


- Reflective foil insulation added to the front cap
- Astrofoil insulation added to the slide floors and underbelly
- An enclosed underbelly
- Forced air heat to the underbelly, convenience center, and dump valves
- 12-volt heating pads on the holding tanks


Dual-pane frameless windows are an optional upgrade for added climate protection. Plus, an optional 5,500-watt generator can be added for expanded off-grid capability.
