# Rogue “Store” | Forest River’s Hybrid RV Redefines Extended Living

As a unique hybrid camper, the Rogue Store series spans four distinct RV classifications: Destination Trailer, Toy Hauler, Travel Trailer, and Fifth Wheel. These 2026 Rogue Store floor plans are specifically engineered for full-time living or extended seasonal stays. And they boast expanded cargo capacities and multi-use versatility that traditional destination trailers simply cannot match.


## Built for Extended RV Living


Image by Forest River


Standard destination trailers offer massive windows, high ceilings, and residential layouts, but they typically lack serious cargo capacity and are notoriously cumbersome to tow due to their flat front caps. Toy haulers offer ample storage but often sacrifice the residential upgrades needed for comfortable, long-term stays.


The[Rogue Store](http://forestriverinc.com/rvs/rogue-store) offers solutions to both dilemmas. By dropping a high-capacity garage space into a luxury, extended-stay floor plan, Forest River provides a platform where owners don’t have to leave golf carts, motorcycles, e-bikes, or mobile workshops behind.


## Core Innovation: The Insulated, “Swing-Away” Rear Wall


Image by Forest River


Much of the Rogue Store’s flexibility is made possible by an inventive design feature: an insulated, hinged rear wall.


Traditional toy haulers offer drop-down bug screens or sliding patio doors to close off the space once the toys are unloaded. Both can allow more heat transfer, making climate control more difficult. The Rogue Store’s insulated rear wall reduces drafts and heat transfer when closed and sealed, making the garage much more private and comfortable.


Depending on your needs, the garage still transitions from a rugged cargo bay to a cozy second bedroom, an expanded living area, or secure storage while you’re away.

The front bedroom features a queen bed in a slide-out, and the main bathroom is just steps away.


There are dual lofts above the front bedroom and the rear garage, adding upper sleeping areas for up to five. The rear garage features a Murphy bed system and a utility bathroom, offering the added versatility of a bath-and-a-half floor plan.
