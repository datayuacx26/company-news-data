# Forest River Redefines Hot-Weather Living: Mesquite Fifth Wheels

**ELKHART, IN** — Forest River’s newest engineering feat — the Mesquite fifth wheel — is specifically built to handle hotter climates, like those found in the Southern US. Thanks to their heavy-duty construction and proprietary thermal defense system, these entry-level luxury fifth wheels maintain a true comfort zone when outside temperatures soar.


## Engineered for Thermal Efficiency


Images by Camping World


The Mesquite lineup delivers improved thermal efficiency through four primary construction pillars:


**Maximum Insulation:** Every fifth wheel features 3″ thick vacuum-bonded sidewalls to provide a superior thermal barrier — 50% thicker than the industry-standard 2″ sidewall.


**Massive Cooling Power:** Comfort is maintained by a high-capacity system with three standard 18,000-BTU Furrion® Chill Cube air conditioners. The Chill Cubes feature soft-start technology, drawing approximately 11,000 watts at startup, meaning two units can run simultaneously even on 30-amp service, and all three can run on 50-amp service.


**Elite Window Technology:** In partnership with Lippert®, the Mesquite features dual-pane, argon-filled windows with Low-E coatings that block up to 95% of harmful UV rays. The advanced glass construction delivers 30-50% energy savings while significantly dampening outside noise and preventing condensation and fogging during rapid temperature shifts. Compared to standard single-pane windows, they deliver roughly 3.5x the R-value.


**Optimized Airflow:** The Mesquite’s duct system features thermoplastic duct joiners to prevent collapse and maintain consistent airflow, combined with a plenum inside each vent register that delivers up to 60% more effective air distribution.


## Upgraded Amenities


Images by Camping World


The[Mesquite](https://forestriverinc.com/rvs/mesquite) isn’t just about beating the heat. It uniquely offers upscale, wide-open living — and at an affordable entry point. The lineup is built on a 101” wide-body chassis with 7’ slide-out heights, creating an expansive interior in which Forest River has designed three unique floorplans


Key features include:


**Premium Interiors:** The interiors feature residential-style finishes throughout, including solid surface countertops, undermounted sinks with pull-down sprayers, full-extension drawer guides with 50-pound weight ratings, and convection microwave ovens. Cabinetry extends to the ceiling for maximum storage.


**Heavy-Duty Foundation:** Engineered for reliability with a 12” Monster Beam frame with oversized outriggers, Lippert® 7K axles, a 6-point electric auto-leveling system, and premium G-rated 14-ply tires with tire pressure monitoring (TPMS) and anti-lock brakes (ABS).


**Safety & Visibility:** Every Mesquite is prepped for the Furrion® Side Vision camera system, plus backup camera prep, to give drivers maximum visibility.


**Total Shade:** Dual power awnings provide 33 total feet of exterior shade coverage, with LED lighting integrated into both.


**Outdoor Living Ready:** An exterior LP quick connect makes camp cooking simple, while a lockable external command center, black tank flush, and an external hot/cold shower keep campsite management convenient and clean.

The Mesquite launches with three distinct floor plans: a rear kitchen, 1.5-bath ([3850HBRK](https://forestriverinc.com/rvs/mesquite/3850HBRK/15142) ), a dual bunk room, 1.5-bath ([3835TRIP](https://forestriverinc.com/rvs/mesquite/3835TRIP/15141) ), and a mid-living, rear kitchen ([3130ML](https://forestriverinc.com/rvs/mesquite/3130ML/15140) ). From couples seeking luxury appointments to families who need bunk room versatility, the Mesquite’s unique layouts have something for everyone.


All floor plans share the same core construction standards: 3″ vacuum-bonded sidewalls, argon-filled dual-pane windows, three 18K BTU air conditioners, 101″ wide-body chassis, 6-point auto-leveling, and ABS/TPMS-equipped G-rated tires.


The Mesquite lineup has begun arriving on dealer lots, with the 3850HBRK soon to be followed by the other unique layouts.
