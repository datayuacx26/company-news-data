# Starfighters Space, Inc. Files Second Quarter 2026 Form 10-Q


August 19, 2026 4:43pm EDT


KENNEDY SPACE CENTER, Fla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Starfighters Space, Inc.](https://starfightersspace.com/) (“Starfighters Space” or the “Company”) (NYSE American: FJET), the space company operating the world’s only commercial fleet of flight-ready Mach 2+ F-104 supersonic aircraft, today announced that it has filed its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”).


The Form 10-Q contains the Company’s financial statements and additional information regarding its results of operations, financial condition, liquidity, and business developments. The Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q is available on the SEC’s website at[www.sec.gov](http://www.sec.gov/) and in the Investors section of the Company’s website at[www.starfightersspace.com](http://www.starfightersspace.com/) .


**About Starfighters Space, Inc.**


[Starfighters Space](https://starfightersspace.com/) (NYSE American: FJET), with a fleet of F-104 aircraft based at NASA Kennedy Space Center in Florida and the Midland Air and Space Port in Texas, is the only company in the world with the commercial capability to fly at sustained Mach 2+ speeds for a variety of aerospace applications. The iconic F-104 jets are configurable as a platform for air-launched payloads, training for pilots, and to support RDT&E (Research, Development, Test, and Evaluation) for hypersonic technologies, missile defense systems, microgravity science, spaceflight hardware, advanced materials, and defense electronic systems.
