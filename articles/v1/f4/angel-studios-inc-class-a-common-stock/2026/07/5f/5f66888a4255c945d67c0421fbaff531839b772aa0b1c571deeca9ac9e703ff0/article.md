### **What Is** ***Tulsa*** **About?**


Tommy Colston has spent years battling addiction and trying to outrun the mistakes of his past.


His life changes overnight when a spirited nine-year-old girl named Tulsa arrives at his door claiming to be his daughter.


Reluctantly, Tommy agrees to care for her, but he keeps his distance emotionally, convinced he’s incapable of being the father she deserves. Tulsa, however, refuses to give up on him. Through her unwavering faith, compassion, and determination, she begins breaking through the walls Tommy has spent years building around his heart.


As unexpected challenges test them both, Tommy must decide whether he’s willing to confront his past and become the father Tulsa believes he can be.


### **The Healing Power of Family**


While *Tulsa* explores difficult themes, it’s ultimately a story about the unexpected ways family can transform us.


Neither Tommy nor Tulsa enters the relationship expecting to change the other’s life. Yet through small moments of grace, forgiveness, and perseverance, they begin to heal wounds neither thought could ever mend.


Their relationship becomes a powerful reminder that love often arrives when we least expect it.


### **Inspired by True Events**


Inspired by true events, *Tulsa* tells a deeply personal story that resonates because of its authenticity.


Its themes of hope, forgiveness, and perseverance make it more than a story about addiction—it’s a story about choosing a different future and believing people can change.
