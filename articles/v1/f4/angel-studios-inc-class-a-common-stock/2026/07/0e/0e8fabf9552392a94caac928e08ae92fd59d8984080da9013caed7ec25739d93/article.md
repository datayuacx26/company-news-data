# Where to Watch Unlimited


by Angel | July 23, 2026


What if the discovery that could change the world also made you its biggest target? *Unlimited* is an inspiring thriller that blends science, suspense, and faith as one young man is drawn into a dangerous mission to protect a breakthrough with the potential to transform humanity.


[Watch Unlimited now on Angel.](https://www.angel.com/watch/unlimited)


### **A Mission Bigger Than He Ever Imagined**


Simon Orwell is a gifted student whose life hasn’t turned out the way he expected.


When a former professor reaches out about a revolutionary device capable of providing unlimited energy, Simon travels to Mexico expecting an extraordinary opportunity. Instead, he finds the professor dead and discovers that a dangerous drug cartel is determined to claim the technology for itself.


Forced into hiding at a local orphanage, Simon meets Harold Finch, a former NASA scientist who helps him understand the true importance of the device—and the responsibility that comes with protecting it.


What begins as a fight for survival soon becomes a journey of purpose, courage, and self-discovery.


### **Inspired by True Events**


Inspired by true events, *Unlimited* reminds viewers that extraordinary change often begins with ordinary people willing to do the right thing.


While the story features thrilling chases and dangerous adversaries, its greatest message is one of hope. Simon’s journey isn’t just about protecting an incredible invention—it’s about discovering the unlimited potential within himself.
