What if learning about animals could also help your little one learn kindness, patience, and compassion? *Awesome Animals and Mini Me* is an interactive preschool series that invites families to sing, play, create, and discover together while exploring the amazing creatures of God’s world.

### **A Show That Keeps Kids Engaged**


*Awesome Animals and Mini Me* encourages little ones to participate every step of the way. Rather than simply watching, children are invited to answer questions, move their bodies, make crafts, and sing along throughout each episode. One Guild member shared:


“It’s interactive and gets the kids thinking.”


Another viewer wrote:


“My daughter was immediately glued in!”


With a blend of animation, live animals, music, and hands-on activities, there’s always something new to capture young imaginations.


### **Learning Through Wonder**


At its heart, *Awesome Animals and Mini Me* celebrates curiosity and reminds children that the natural world is full of opportunities to learn.


Each episode pairs fascinating animal facts with meaningful life lessons, helping children connect what they see in nature with values they can practice every day. Whether your child loves lions, elephants, birds, or bears, every episode offers new discoveries, memorable songs, and opportunities to learn together.
