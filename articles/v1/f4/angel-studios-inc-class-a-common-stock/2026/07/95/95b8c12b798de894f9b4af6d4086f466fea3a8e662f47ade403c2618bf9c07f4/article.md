### **What Is** ***Call Sign Courage*** **About?**


*Call Sign Courage: The Matt Lohmeier Story* tells the story of former Air Force lieutenant colonel Matt Lohmeier and the events that followed after he publicly expressed concerns about issues he believed were affecting the U.S. military.


The documentary examines the consequences of that decision, including the loss of his command, the impact on his family, and the years that followed. It also explores his eventual return to public service after being appointed Undersecretary of the Air Force.


Rather than simply recounting public events, the film focuses on the personal journey behind them—examining questions of integrity, leadership, sacrifice, and remaining true to deeply held convictions.


### **A Story About Conviction**


At its heart, *Call Sign Courage: The Matt Lohmeier Story* is about the challenges that can come with acting according to one’s conscience.


The documentary follows Matt Lohmeier through moments of uncertainty, criticism, and personal hardship while highlighting the support of family, friends, and fellow service members who walked alongside him.


Regardless of viewers’ familiarity with the events themselves, the film invites reflection on what it means to lead with conviction when the outcome is uncertain.


### **Leadership Under Pressure**


Military leadership often requires difficult decisions, and *Call Sign Courage: The Matt Lohmeier Story* explores what those decisions can look like when personal beliefs and professional responsibilities intersect.


Through interviews and firsthand accounts, the documentary offers insight into the pressures of public service, the weight of leadership, and the personal cost that can accompany controversial moments.


Rather than focusing solely on headlines, it seeks to tell the story of the individual behind them.
