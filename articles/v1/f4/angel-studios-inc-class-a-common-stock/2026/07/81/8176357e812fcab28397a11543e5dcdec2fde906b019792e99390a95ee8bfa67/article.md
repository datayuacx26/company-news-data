by Angel | July 13, 2026


Michael is a newly assigned pastor with a difficult responsibility: close a small, struggling church and sell the property.


The congregation is dwindling, the bills are piling up, and the future seems uncertain. But before Michael can complete his assignment, a group of Burmese refugees arrives seeking a place to belong.


What begins as an unexpected challenge soon becomes an opportunity that changes the entire community.
