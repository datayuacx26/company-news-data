### **What Is** ***Our Crossroads*** **About?**


In 1969 rural Ohio, a husband and wife with two young children are preparing for the next chapter of their lives when tragedy strikes another family.


Six siblings suddenly lose both of their parents, leaving them uncertain about their future and facing the possibility of being separated.


Compelled by their faith, the couple makes an extraordinary decision: to seek guardianship of all six children and keep them together.


Welcoming eight children under one roof is anything but easy. Financial challenges, emotional struggles, and the realities of raising a large family test their resolve. Yet through every obstacle, they continue choosing love, sacrifice, and faith over fear.


Decades later, their story unexpectedly reaches a photographer wrestling with his own spiritual questions, reminding him—and viewers—that one family’s courage can leave a lasting legacy.


### **Inspired by a Remarkable True Story**


At the heart of *Our Crossroads* is a true story of ordinary people responding to an extraordinary need.


Rather than turning away from an overwhelming situation, one couple chooses to open both their home and their hearts, believing that keeping the siblings together is worth every sacrifice.


Their story is a powerful reminder that lives are often changed through quiet acts of compassion rather than grand gestures.
