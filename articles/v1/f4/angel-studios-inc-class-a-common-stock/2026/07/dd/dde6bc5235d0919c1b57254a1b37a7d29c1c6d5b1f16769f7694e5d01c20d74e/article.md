# TWO BROTHERS DIRECT A STORY OF BROTHERS AT WAR: ANGEL’S NEW REVOLUTIONARY WAR MUSICAL DRUMMER BOY RELEASES EXCLUSIVELY IN THEATERS NOVEMBER 6, 2026

### TWO BROTHERS DIRECT A STORY OF BROTHERS AT WAR: ANGEL’S NEW REVOLUTIONARY WAR MUSICAL DRUMMER BOY RELEASES EXCLUSIVELY IN THEATERS NOVEMBER 6, 2026


Joel David Smallbone (for KING + COUNTRY, *Unsung Hero* ) Stars and Co-directs Alongside Ben Smallbone ( *Priceless* , *Homestead* ) Acclaimed Ensemble Cast Includes India Brown ( *Invasion* ), Ioan Gruffudd ( *Fantastic Four* , *Amazing Grace* ), Melora Hardin ( *The Office* , *Monk* ), Iain Glen ( *Game of Thrones* ), Charles Esten ( *Nashville* , *Outer Banks* ), and Olivier Award winner Beverley Knight ( *Cinderella* )


Provo, UT—July 29, 2026


Angel (NYSE: ANGX), the media and technology company pioneering the first audience-driven entertainment studio, today revealed the ensemble cast for *DRUMMER BOY* , the musical event of the year set during the American Revolutionary War. It opens exclusively in theaters nationwide on November 6, 2026.


As America’s fight for independence begins, two brothers on opposite sides of the battlefield and an enslaved woman each search for freedom in a world built to deny it. Hopeful music is woven throughout this epic adventure of love and sacrifice set against the backdrop of the founding of a nation highlighting a deeply human story of faith, forgiveness and the belief that no person is born to live in chains, of body or soul.


Brothers Ben Smallbone and Joel David Smallbone co-direct *DRUMMER BOY* and the film carries the emotional resonance that results from a lifetime of collaboration. Joel also stars. He is best known as one half of for KING + COUNTRY, the four-time Grammy Award-winning duo, and starred in *Unsung Hero* and *Journey to Bethlehem* . Ben directs beside him following his work on *Homestead* , a hit for Angel. Together with their other brother, Luke, the Smallbones deepen the authenticity of the film, drawing on the experiences of brotherhood.


Lucas Leach ( *Crookhaven* ) portrays Joel’s on-screen brother and rival. Their story is the emotional engine of the film with a bond tested by opposing loyalties.


The acclaimed ensemble cast includes India Brown ( *Invasion* ), Ioan Gruffudd ( *Fantastic Four, Amazing Grace* ), Melora Hardin ( *The Office* , *Monk* ), Iain Glen ( *Game of Thrones* ), Charles Esten ( *Nashville* , *Outer Banks* ), Beth Easdown and Olivier Award winner Beverley Knight ( *Cinderella* ).


“Telling a story about a family divided, alongside my brothers, as both a director and an actor, is among the most rewarding work of my life,” said Joel David Smallbone, co-director and star of *DRUMMER BOY.* “The sweeping storytelling with striking music will prompt audiences to claim it as their new favorite holiday tradition and, I hope, favorite musical.”


“From the first page, we set out to create the epic scale of a war juxtaposed with the emotive story of brothers at odds,” said Ben Smallbone, co-director of *DRUMMER BOY* . “Along the way, they discover that the forgiveness born at Christmas is their only hope.”

“ *DRUMMER BOY* is Angel’s first live-action theatrical musical, and we are bringing it to the big screen with an exceptional team of filmmakers and creative partners,” said Brandon Purdie, Executive Vice President of Theatrical at Angel. “This is a milestone for Angel and a reflection of what the Angel Guild champions: bold, distinctive stories meant to be experienced in theaters.”

**About Angel**


Angel (NYSE: ANGX) is a media and technology company successfully pioneering a first-of-its-kind audience-driven studio model. Founded by brothers who struggled to find films they could watch with their children, Angel was built on the belief that there was a global audience hungry for values-driven storytelling that amplifies light, celebrates hope, and inspires the moral imagination of viewers. That audience became the Angel Guild, a rapidly growing community of 2.7 million paying members who watch, screen, and vote on which films and television series get produced and distributed in theaters and on the Angel app. Angel has achieved one of the highest audience satisfaction scores in the industry* and the highest average domestic box office per title among all independent studios since 2023.** It has done so while evolving a new economic model that shares profits more fully with filmmakers.
