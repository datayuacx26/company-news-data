# Where to Watch Heaven Is for Real


by Angel | July 17, 2026


What would you do if someone you loved experienced something that challenged everything you believed? *Heaven Is for Real* tells the remarkable true story of a young boy whose near-death experience leaves his family grappling with questions about faith, hope, and eternity.


[Watch Heaven Is for Real now on Angel.](https://www.angel.com/watch/heaven-is-for-real)


### **A Story That Changed One Family Forever**


Todd Burpo is a small-town pastor, husband, and father whose life is turned upside down when his young son, Colton, survives a medical emergency.


After recovering, Colton begins describing vivid details of what he says he experienced during his experience—including things he couldn’t have known. As Todd listens to his son’s story, he must wrestle with questions that challenge both his faith and his understanding of the impossible.


### **A Heartfelt Drama About Faith and Hope**


At its core, *Heaven Is for Real* is less about extraordinary events than ordinary people responding to them.


The film explores how faith is tested during life’s most difficult moments and how hope can grow even when answers aren’t clear. Through Todd’s journey, audiences see the tension between skepticism and trust, as well as the importance of listening with humility and love.


It’s a story that invites reflection, regardless of where you are in your own faith journey.


### **A Thought-Provoking Film for the Whole Family**


Whether you’ve read the bestselling book or are discovering the story for the first time, *Heaven Is for Real* offers an uplifting exploration of faith, family, and the questions that have fascinated people for generations.
