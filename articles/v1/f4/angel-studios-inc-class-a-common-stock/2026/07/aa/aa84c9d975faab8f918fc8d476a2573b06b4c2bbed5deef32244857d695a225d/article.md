With plenty of humor and small-town charm, it’s a feel-good story about discovering that sometimes the best plans are the ones you never saw coming.

### **A Fresh Start in an Unexpected Place**


After her divorce and the death of her father-in-law, New Yorker Jesse returns to the small town she thought she’d left behind.


Moving in with her widowed mother-in-law isn’t exactly part of her plan, but it gives Jesse a chance to slow down and figure out what comes next. As she reconnects with the community, she begins building new relationships—and seeing her future in a completely different light.


What was supposed to be a temporary stay soon becomes something much more meaningful.


### **When Love Gets Complicated**


Jesse’s new beginning becomes even more complicated when she develops feelings for her widowed brother-in-law.


Their growing connection raises eyebrows throughout the tight-knit community, especially when Jesse’s ex-husband unexpectedly returns, determined to win her back.


Caught between her past and the possibility of a new future, Jesse must decide where her heart truly belongs.


### **A Story About Family, Faith, and Forgiveness**


While romance is at the center of the story, *In-Lawfully Yours* is ultimately about healing after loss and embracing second chances. It’s a reminder that life doesn’t always unfold according to plan—but that doesn’t mean it can’t lead somewhere beautiful.
