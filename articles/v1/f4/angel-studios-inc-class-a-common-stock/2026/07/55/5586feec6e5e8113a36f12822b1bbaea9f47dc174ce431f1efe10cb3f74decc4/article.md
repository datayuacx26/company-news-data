# Where to Watch Making Room: Faith in Action


by Angel | July 30, 2026


[Making Room: Faith in Action is a documentary now streaming on Angel](https://www.angel.com/watch/making-room-faith-in-action) , and it tells the kind of story that stays with you. It's quiet and real and full of the sort of love that doesn't make the news. It's about the daily, unglamorous, deeply meaningful work of caring for children who need a home.


#### Faith, Family, and the Children Who Need Both


At the heart of the documentary is Safe Haven Ranch, a private community of homes built around a simple but profound conviction: every child deserves a stable place to grow up, surrounded by Biblical values and people who genuinely care.


The film doesn't shy away from how hard that work has been. Over the years, Safe Haven Ranch has faced real struggles, and the documentary captures them honestly. But it also traces how, through perseverance and prayer, the right people kept showing up at the right moments.


#### Why It's Worth Watching


What makes *Making Room* resonate is its honesty. Viewers who've seen it consistently note that the hard parts aren't glossed over—and that's what makes the tender moments land. This isn't a polished, everything-worked-out story. It's the raw and sincere truth of what a community built on love and shared belief actually looks like from the inside.


For anyone who has spent time in foster care, worked with vulnerable children, or simply wondered what it means to live out your faith in a tangible way, this film offers something genuine. Those who've watched it describe being moved to both tears and gratitude—not because the story is easy, but because it's true.
