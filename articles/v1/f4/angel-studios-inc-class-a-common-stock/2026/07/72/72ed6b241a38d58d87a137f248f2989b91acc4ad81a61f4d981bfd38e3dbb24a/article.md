# Where to Watch Red, White, and Brass


by Angel | July 23, 2026


*Red, White, and Brass* tells the true story of Maka, a passionate Tongan rugby fan determined to support his country’s team at the 2011 Rugby World Cup.


There’s just one problem.


To earn tickets, he promises to provide a brass band for the event—even though he doesn’t actually have a band, any instruments, or musicians who know how to play them. Packed with humor, heart, unforgettable music, and a celebration of Tongan culture, this uplifting comedy proves that sometimes the most impossible ideas create the greatest memories.
