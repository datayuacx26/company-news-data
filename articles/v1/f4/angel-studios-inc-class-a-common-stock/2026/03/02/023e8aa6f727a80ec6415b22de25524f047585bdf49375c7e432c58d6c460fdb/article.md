# Angel Announces Participation in Fireside Chat and Panel Discussion at the 38th Annual Roth Conference

### Angel Announces Participation in Fireside Chat and Panel Discussion at the 38th Annual Roth Conference


Provo, UT—March 18, 2026


Angel (NYSE: ANGX) (the “Company”), a media and technology company guided by more than two million grassroots Angel Guild members championing values-driven stories, today announced that Angel co-founder and CEO Neal Harmon and CFO Scott Klossner will participate in the 38th Annual Roth Conference being held at the Ritz-Carlton in Laguna Niguel, CA, March 23-24, 2026.


On Tuesday, March 24th Harmon will join a[fireside chat](https://event.summitcast.com/view/kKmFFrbEes9oUCyrRFmQ5N/guest_book?session_id=Qo6CDpkEPXX5xcGAkNJu9K) at 9:00 a.m. PT / 12:00 p.m. ET and a later[panel discussion](https://event.summitcast.com/view/kKmFFrbEes9oUCyrRFmQ5N/guest_book?session_id=QvAQUaUgCBSiXGGG44RRbz) titled ‘Finding New Ways to Market Movies to Consumers’ at 1:00 p.m. PT / 4:00 p.m. ET — both events will be webcast live on the Company’s[investor relations website](https://www.angx.com/) and at the links above. Harmon and Klossner will also be available for one-on-one investor meetings throughout the conference.


To schedule a meeting with Angel’s senior management, please contact your Roth conference representative or Investor Relations at InvestorRelations@angel.com.


**About Angel**


Angel (NYSE: ANGX) is a media and technology company guided by more than two million grassroots Angel Guild members championing values-driven stories. Clearly expressing the kind of programming they crave, members of the Angel Guild act as virtual co-producers, greenlighting what films and television series get produced and distributed in theaters and on the Angel app. Propelled by this audience-first momentum, Angel has released more than 40 films and 20 television series that amplify light, including the animated musical epic DAVID and Sound of Freedom, which earned more than 250 million dollars at the worldwide box office. The company also has more than six billion views of its Dry Bar Comedy streaming franchise, which has attracted some of the world's best-known comedians. For more information, visit www.angel.com.
