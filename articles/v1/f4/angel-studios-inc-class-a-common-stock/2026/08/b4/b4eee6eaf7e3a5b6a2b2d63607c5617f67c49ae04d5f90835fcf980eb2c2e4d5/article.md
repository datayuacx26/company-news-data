# Where to Watch Scars on the Mountain


by Angel | August 4, 2026


In just a matter of hours, Hurricane Helene transformed a quiet Appalachian community into the center of one of the most devastating natural disasters in recent memory. *Scars on the Mountain* is a moving documentary that captures the heartbreak, resilience, and hope that emerged when neighbors became each other’s lifeline.

#### What Is *Scars on the Mountain* About


When Hurricane Helene made landfall, most people assumed the worst of it would be felt along the coast. Instead, the storm pushed 300 miles inland and came down on Yancey County, North Carolina—a small Appalachian community tucked into the mountains—with a force no one had prepared for. Record-breaking flash floods and landslides tore through the area within hours. Roads washed out, power lines went down, and communication cut off entirely. With no way in and no way out, the people of Yancey County were left to face the aftermath alone.


*Scars on the Mountain* documents what happened in the days and weeks that followed: the chaos, the loss, and the quiet, remarkable ways neighbors showed up for each other when every outside system had failed them.


#### Why This Documentary Is Worth Your Time


*Scars on the Mountain* doesn’t focus on the storm itself as much as the people who lived through it. Through firsthand stories, it captures what happened after the floodwaters receded.


It’s also a reminder that some of the most meaningful acts of courage aren’t found in headlines. They’re found in ordinary people checking on neighbors, sharing what little they have, and refusing to let one another face hardship alone.


Whether you followed the aftermath of Hurricane Helene or are learning about it for the first time, *Scars on the Mountain* offers an honest look at a community that endured the unthinkable—and the hope that emerged in the middle of it.


#### Where To Watch *Scars on the Mountain*


Whether you experienced Hurricane Helene firsthand or are discovering this story for the first time, *Scars on the Mountain* offers a powerful reminder of what people are capable of when they come together to care for one another. It’s the kind of documentary that stays with you long after it ends.
