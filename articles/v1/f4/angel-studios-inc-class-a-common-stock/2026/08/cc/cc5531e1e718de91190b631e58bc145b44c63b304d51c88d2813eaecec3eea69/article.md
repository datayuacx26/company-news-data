# Where to Watch Unitards


by Angel | August 15, 2026


What do you get when three guys decide the best way to bring their high school together is to start a dance crew? A whole lot of questionable choreography, unexpected school spirit, and a surprisingly big dose of heart.


[Watch Unitards now on Angel.](https://www.angel.com/watch/unitards)


### **Three Guys. One Dance Crew. Zero Experience.**


Lewis Grady has just been elected president of the Men’s Association at Skyline High, but the title comes with an assignment. Vice Principal Spooner wants him to find a way to bring school spirit and unity back to campus.


Luckily, Lewis has an idea.


Along with his two oddball friends, he starts a men’s-only dance group to perform at school assemblies and pep rallies. It might sound like a terrible plan, but somehow, it starts working.


### **Not Everyone Is Dancing Along**


There’s just one problem.


The girls’ dance team has a reputation to protect, and its coach isn’t about to let a group of inexperienced guys threaten the team’s standing. When she decides the Unitards need to be stopped, Lewis and his friends aren’t exactly ready to give up.


Instead, they come up with another plan to bring Skyline together.


What starts as a simple attempt to boost school spirit turns into something much bigger, challenging students to look past cliques, expectations, and the pressure to fit in.


### **A Comedy With a Lot of Heart**


*Unitards* is built around plenty of laughs, but underneath the goofy dance moves is a story about friendship, confidence, and finding the courage to be yourself.


The film also has a particularly personal connection to the community it portrays, as it was inspired by a real-life experience—and the filmmakers invited students from Skyline and people from the surrounding neighborhood to take part in the movie.


That gives the movie an unmistakably authentic feel. The students aren’t just background characters—they help make Skyline feel like a real school filled with real personalities.

If you’re looking for a feel-good comedy about friendship, school spirit, and embracing the things that make you different, *Unitards* is ready to bring some laughs to your next movie night.
