# Where to Watch Maya the Bee


by Angel | August 12, 2026


What if the thing that makes you different is exactly what helps you make a difference?


Freshly hatched Maya has a hard time following the hive’s rules—but her curiosity and kindness lead her to friendships that could save the day when the Royal Jelly mysteriously disappears. What follows is a colorful adventure filled with mystery, friendship, and plenty of buzzing along the way.


[Watch Maya the Bee now on Angel.](https://www.angel.com/watch/maya-the-bee)


### **Meet Maya the Bee**


From the moment Maya hatches, it’s clear she’s not quite like the other bees.


While the hive teaches its young to follow the rules and keep their distance from other insects, Maya can’t help but wonder what’s beyond the meadow. She’s especially curious about the creatures she’s been told not to trust—including the hornets who live outside the hive.


Before long, Maya’s curiosity turns into an unlikely collection of friendships.


She befriends all kinds of bugs, including a violin-playing grasshopper, a dung beetle, and even a young hornet. To Maya, they’re friends—and her willingness to look beyond the boundaries of the hive gives her a very different perspective on the world around her.


But Maya’s world is turned upside down when the Royal Jelly is stolen from the hive. With the hornets quickly blamed for the crime, suspicion spreads throughout the meadow. But Maya isn’t convinced. She knows that judging someone based on where they come from isn’t the same as knowing the truth.


Now, Maya and her unlikely group of friends must work together to find the missing Royal Jelly and prove that the hornets aren’t responsible.


### **A Story About Looking Beyond Differences**


At its heart, *Maya the Bee* is a story about friendship, courage, and learning to see others for who they really are.


Maya’s willingness to make friends with creatures she’s been told to avoid teaches her—and everyone around her—that differences don’t have to divide us. The story also explores bigger ideas like acceptance, compassion, forgiveness, and standing up for what’s right.


And because those lessons come wrapped in a playful animated adventure, young viewers can enjoy the story while discovering something meaningful along the way.
