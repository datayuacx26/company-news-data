# Where to Watch DEEPWATER HORIZON


by Angel | August 3, 2026


*Deepwater Horizon* is a gripping disaster drama inspired by the real events of April 20, 2010, when one of the worst environmental disasters in U.S. history unfolded aboard an offshore oil rig. Starring Mark Wahlberg, Kurt Russell, John Malkovich, and an all-star cast, the film honors the courage of the men and women who risked everything to save others.


[Watch Deepwater Horizon on Angel](https://www.angel.com/watch/deepwater-horizon) before it leaves the platform on August 31, 2026.


### **The Day Everything Changed**


For the 126 people working aboard the Deepwater Horizon oil rig, April 20, 2010, began like any other shift.


Routine inspections, equipment checks, and general responsibilities filled the day. But beneath the surface, warning signs were mounting. Within hours, a catastrophic explosion would turn an ordinary workday into a desperate fight for survival where every decision becomes a matter of life and death.


### **Inspired by Real-Life Heroes**


Rather than focusing solely on the disaster itself, *Deepwater Horizon* shines a light on the people who faced it.


The film follows workers who put themselves in harm’s way to protect their coworkers, rescue the injured, and lead others to safety in the midst of unimaginable chaos. Their courage, quick thinking, and selflessness transformed a devastating tragedy into a story of extraordinary heroism.


It’s a powerful tribute to the men and women whose actions saved countless lives.


### **A Tense and Emotional Disaster Drama**


*Deepwater Horizon* combines breathtaking visual effects with deeply personal storytelling.


The film captures both the overwhelming scale of the explosion and the human moments that unfold in the middle of the crisis—coworkers refusing to leave one another behind, families waiting anxiously for news, and ordinary people finding extraordinary courage under impossible circumstances.


The result is a disaster film that’s as emotionally moving as it is suspenseful.


### **A Standout Cast Brings the Story to Life**


Mark Wahlberg leads an outstanding ensemble cast that includes Kurt Russell, John Malkovich, Gina Rodriguez, Dylan O’Brien, and Kate Hudson.


Their performances bring authenticity and heart to the story, portraying the determination, fear, and resilience of people caught in one of the most dangerous workplace disasters in modern history.


Together, they create a film that honors the real individuals behind the headlines.


### **Stream** ***Deepwater Horizon*** **Before It’s Gone**


If you’re looking for a gripping true-story drama filled with suspense, unforgettable performances, and inspiring acts of courage, *Deepwater Horizon* is a powerful tribute to the heroes of one of America’s most devastating industrial disasters.
