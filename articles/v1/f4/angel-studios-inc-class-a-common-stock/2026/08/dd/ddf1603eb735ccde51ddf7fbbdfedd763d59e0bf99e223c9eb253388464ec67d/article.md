### **A Storm Brings Them Together**


A violent winter storm leaves a pregnant woman and an injured snowboarder stranded in the mountains with nowhere to go. Their search for shelter leads them to Rosemont Lodge, a long-abandoned property that seems like an unlikely refuge.


But the lodge isn’t quite as abandoned as they expected.


Its bitter, grieving owner and her protective caretaker are still there, each carrying their own wounds and reasons for keeping others at a distance. With the storm cutting them off from the outside world, the four strangers have no choice but to spend the holiday together.


What begins as an uncomfortable situation slowly becomes something none of them expected: a chance to find connection in the middle of their loneliness.


### **A Story About Love, Loss, and Redemption**


At its heart, *Rosemont* is a story about the people who enter our lives when we least expect them—and the difference they can make when we’re willing to let them in.


With its snowy mountain setting, heartfelt story, and themes of forgiveness and second chances, the film offers a warm reminder that family isn’t always defined by where we come from. Sometimes, it’s found in the people who show up when we need them most.
