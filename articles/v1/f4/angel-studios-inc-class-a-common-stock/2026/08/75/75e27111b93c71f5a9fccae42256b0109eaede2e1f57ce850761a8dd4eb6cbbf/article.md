#### What *MAHA Films: Toxic Nation* Is About


*MAHA Films: Toxic Nation* is a health documentary that looks at the many ways Americans are exposed to harmful substances in their daily lives—through the food they eat, the water they drink, the air they breathe, and even the products in their homes.


The film brings together a wide range of voices: doctors, journalists, chefs, advocates, and policy figures, all weighing in on how ultra-processed foods, seed oils, pesticides like glyphosate, heavy metals, microplastics, and fluoride may be contributing to the chronic disease epidemic sweeping the country. It also examines the regulatory systems meant to protect public health and asks why so many warning signs have gone unaddressed for so long.


#### Why This Documentary Is Worth Your Time


What sets *MAHA Films: Toxic Nation* apart from other health documentaries is how much ground it covers while still feeling focused. The film draws on a striking body of evidence—including the sobering reality that chronic disease now accounts for 92% of deaths in the United States.


But the film doesn't stop at alarming statistics. Viewers who've seen it have come away with a clearer picture of what they can actually do—practical changes that feel within reach. One reviewer, a practicing nurse practitioner, noted that even with a professional background in health, she walked away having learned something new. Others described it as eye-opening, empowering, and something they felt every American should see.
