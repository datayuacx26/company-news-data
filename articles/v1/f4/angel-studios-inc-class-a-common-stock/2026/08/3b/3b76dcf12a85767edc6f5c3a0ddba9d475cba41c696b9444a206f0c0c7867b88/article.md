# Where to Watch Young Washington


by Angel | August 12, 2026


Before George Washington became a general, a Founding Father, and the first president of the United States, he was a young soldier trying to find his place in a dangerous and rapidly changing world.


*Young Washington* brings that lesser-known chapter of his life to the screen, following a young Washington as a single decision thrusts him into the center of a conflict that will shape his future.


[Watch Young Washington now on Angel.](https://www.angel.com/watch/young-washington)


### **Before the Legend, There Was the Man**


Long before he became a name synonymous with American history, George Washington was learning what it meant to lead.


Set on the American frontier, *Young Washington* follows Washington as he navigates a world filled with uncertainty, shifting alliances, and growing tensions. A seemingly small misstep puts him in the middle of a much larger conflict, forcing him to make difficult choices while fighting to survive.


As the frontier erupts into war, Washington faces challenges that test more than his military skill. His honor, loyalty, courage, and sense of duty are all put to the test as he begins to discover the kind of man he wants to become.


### **A Pivotal Chapter of History**


*Young Washington* offers a look at a chapter of American history that isn’t always explored on screen: the experiences that helped shape Washington before he became the leader history remembers.


The film brings the danger and uncertainty of the frontier to life while exploring the choices and circumstances that would influence Washington’s future. Rather than beginning with the familiar legend, the story goes back to the beginning to show a young man still being shaped by his experiences.


For history fans, it’s an opportunity to see a familiar figure from a different perspective. For families, it’s an engaging way to spark conversations about American history, leadership, courage, and character.


### **An All-Star Cast**


The story is brought to life by an impressive ensemble, with William Franklyn-Miller portraying the young George Washington alongside acclaimed actors including Ben Kingsley, Andy Serkis, and Kelsey Grammer.


Together, the cast helps ground this larger-than-life historical figure in a story about a young man facing very real fears, failures, and difficult decisions.
