# Where to Watch Until Forever


by Angel | August 12, 2026


What happens when the future you thought you had is suddenly rewritten? Based on a true story, *Until Forever* follows high school sweethearts Michael and Michelle as a rare cancer diagnosis puts their love, faith, and plans for the future to the ultimate test.


[Watch Until Forever now on Angel.](https://www.angel.com/watch/until-forever)


### **A Love Story Tested by the Unexpected**


Michael and Michelle have grown up together, fallen in love, and begun building a life side by side. But when Michael is diagnosed with a rare form of cancer, everything they imagined for their future suddenly changes.


As Michael’s condition keeps him in the hospital, he faces more than a difficult diagnosis. His Christian faith is challenged by a skeptical patient, forcing him to wrestle with what he believes when life doesn’t unfold the way he hoped.


*Until Forever* doesn’t shy away from the difficult questions that come with suffering. Instead, it explores what happens when faith is tested, relationships are stretched, and hope has to be chosen in the middle of circumstances that can’t simply be fixed.


### **A True Story of Love and Commitment**


What makes *Until Forever* especially moving is knowing that Michael and Michelle’s story really happened.


Their experience offers a deeply personal look at love in the face of illness and uncertainty, while reminding viewers that commitment isn’t only about sharing life’s happiest moments. Sometimes it’s about staying, believing, and loving someone through the hardest ones.


Audiences have described the film as heartfelt, emotional, and deeply moving, with one viewer calling it “an amazing, heart-wrenching, love-filled movie of true love in all its forms.”


### **Stream Until Forever Today**


*Until Forever* is a moving reminder that even when life takes an unexpected turn, love, faith, and hope can carry us through places we never expected to go.
