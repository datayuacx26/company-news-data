# Where to Watch Miss Moxy


by Angel | August 12, 2026


What happens when a cat who thinks she has everything figured out suddenly finds herself far from home? For Miss Moxy, getting back to the Netherlands means relying on two unlikely companions—and discovering that the best adventures are often the ones we never planned.


[Watch Miss Moxy now on Angel.](https://www.angel.com/watch/miss-moxy)


### **A Journey Far From Home**


Miss Moxy has always been confident in her own opinions, especially when it comes to one important question: Cats are better than every other animal. So when she finds herself stranded in France, far from the comfort of home, she’s not exactly thrilled about accepting help from anyone.


Her unexpected travel companions couldn’t be more different. Brute is a friendly, endlessly optimistic dog, while Ayo is a resourceful bird with plenty to say. Together, the unlikely trio sets out to help Miss Moxy find her way back to the Netherlands.


But getting home won’t be easy.


### **An Unlikely Friendship**


As Miss Moxy and her new friends face obstacles along the way, she begins to discover that there’s more to friendship than she ever expected.


The journey pushes her outside her comfort zone and challenges some of the assumptions she’s carried with her. What starts as a reluctant partnership slowly becomes a genuine friendship—and the three travelers learn that they can accomplish more together than they ever could alone.


### **A Family Adventure With Heart**


With a perfectionist cat, a cheerful dog, and a resourceful bird at the center of the story, *Miss Moxy* offers plenty of laughs and adventure for younger viewers while delivering a message the whole family can appreciate.


Whether you’re looking for an animated movie for family movie night or a sweet story about discovering the value of friendship, *Miss Moxy* is ready to take you on an unforgettable journey.
