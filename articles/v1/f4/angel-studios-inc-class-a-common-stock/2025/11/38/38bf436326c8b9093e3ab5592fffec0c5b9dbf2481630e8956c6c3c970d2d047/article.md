# Angel Debuts Official Trailer for New Original Series The Wayfinders

### Angel Debuts Official Trailer for New Original Series The Wayfinders


New Coming-of-Age Fantasy Series Launches this December Exclusively on Angel.com and the Angel App


Provo, UT—November 21, 2025


**PROVO, Utah — \[November 21, 2025\]** — **Angel** (NYSE: ANGX), a media and technology company guided by 1.6 million grassroots Angel Guild members championing values-driven stories, reveals the official trailer for Season 1 of their upcoming original series, ***THE WAYFINDERS*** . The series follows three teens who are pulled from high school into a realm of magic and monsters, where courage is their only way home.


“This trailer gives audiences their first immersive look at the world behind *The Wayfinders* ,” said **Brandon Purdie** , Executive Vice President of Theatrical and Brand Development at Angel. “The Angel Guild has been enthusiastic about this series from the start, and we’re looking forward to sharing the full series with audiences.”


Watch the teaser trailer[HERE](https://youtu.be/gIB3MvgI-Mo)


When three high schoolers are pulled out of their world and dropped into a realm of swords, sorcery, and monsters, survival is just the beginning. With a rogue, a priestess, and a teenage werebear at their side, the unlikely heroes embark on quests through dungeons, battle hunters sent by a tyrant prince, and uncover a prophecy that may hold the key to both worlds. What starts as a desperate search for home becomes a journey that will test their courage and bind them together forever.


Created by **Jason Faller** and **Kynan Griffin** (The Outpost), The Wayfinders stars **Evan Nikolas Fields** (Hacks), **Tamara Smart** (Percy Jackson), **Vincent Mattis** (Teen Wolf), **Issy Knopfler** (Before We Die), and **Sam Buchanan** (My Fault: London) with **Mackenzie Crook** (Pirates of the Caribbean, The Office). The series is directed by acclaimed filmmaker **Glen Winter** (Smallville, Arrow, The Flash), and is written by **Adam F. Goldberg** (The Goldbergs), **Hans Rodionoff** (The Muppets Mayhem), and **Justin Partridge** .


Official Website:[https://www.angel.com/shows/wayfinders](https://www.angel.com/shows/wayfinders)
