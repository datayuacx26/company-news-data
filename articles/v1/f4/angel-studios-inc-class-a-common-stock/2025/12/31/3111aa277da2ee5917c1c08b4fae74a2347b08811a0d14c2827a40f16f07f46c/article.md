# ANGEL to Release ANIMAL FARM, Coming to Theaters May 1, 2026

### ANGEL to Release ANIMAL FARM, Coming to Theaters May 1, 2026


Andy Serkis’ adaptation of the classic novel boasts an all star cast including Seth Rogen, Glenn Close, Kieran Culkin, and Woody Harrelson


Provo, UT—December 12, 2025


**Angel** (NYSE: ANGX), an unprecedented technology company driven by 1.6 million grassroots Angel Guild members championing values-driven stories, has announced the acquisition of theatrical distribution rights for **Andy Serkis’** s highly anticipated animated adaptation of George Orwell’s ***Animal Farm*** **.** Boasting a legendary voice cast, ***Animal Farm*** was produced by **Adam Nagle and Dave Rosenbaum** for **Aniventure,** and **Jonathan Cavendish and Andy Serkis** for **Imaginarium** **Productions** , with animation from **Cinesite** . The film is scheduled for a wide theatrical release on **May 1, 2026** .


View the trailer[HERE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g8wLmj9SiKM)


“This is a project of tremendous heart,” said **Brandon Purdie,** Executive Vice President of Theatrical and Brand Development at Angel. “Andy Serkis’ visionary direction, combined with this powerhouse cast, creates a film that feels timely, urgent, and deeply human — even though the protagonists might be pigs, donkeys, and horses. We’re confident that moviegoers will see not just a story of farm animals, but a mirror held up to our world today.”


Andy Serkis **,** acclaimed actor and director, has shepherded this project to life with a screenplay by **Nicholas Stoller.** The film premiered at the Annecy International Animation Film Festival on June 9, 2025, where Pete Hammond from Deadline stated that the film “looks to me to be one of the most important films of the year.”Serkis added, “Orwell’s *Animal Farm* has never felt more relevant. In an age where power, propaganda, and inequality shape our societies, it’s vital that we remember his cautionary tale. This adaptation isn’t just a story for entertainment — it’s a reminder that democracy, freedom, and integrity are fragile, and must be watched over. My hope is that audiences walk away moved, thoughtful, and inspired to stand up for the values that matter. I’m honored that Angel and the Angel Guild are willing to bring this film to big screens around the world.”


**All-Star Voice Cast Includes:**


-


**Seth Rogen** as Napoleon


-


**Gaten Matarazzo** as Lucky, a young piglet torn between competing ideologies.


-


**Steve Buscemi** as Mr. Whymper, a human employee from the bank.


-


**Glenn Close** as Freida Pilkington, a scheming neighbor.


-


**Laverne Cox** as Snowball, reimagined in this version as a sow and rival to Napoleon.


-


**Kieran Culkin** as Squealer, Napoleon’s persuasive propagandist.


-


**Woody Harrelson** as Boxer, the loyal horse.


-


**Jim Parsons** as Carl the sheep, and as the rest of his flock.


-


**Andy Serkis** himself voices **Mr. Jones** , the farm’s original owner, and **Randolph the Rooster** .


-


**Kathleen Turner** as Benjamin the donkey.


-


**Iman Vellani** as piglets Puff and Tammy, close friends of Lucky.


Using its model driven by members of the Angel Guild, Angel is dedicated to ensuring that *Animal Farm* resonates with both longtime readers of Orwell’s classic and new generations discovering its themes for the first time.
