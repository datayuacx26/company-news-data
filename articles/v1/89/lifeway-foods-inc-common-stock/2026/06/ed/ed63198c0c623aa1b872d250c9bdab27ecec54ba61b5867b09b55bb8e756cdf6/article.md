### The Lifeway Story: A Legacy of Loving Your Guts


Before the days[Lifeway Kefir](https://www.lifewaykefir.com/what-is-kefir) could be found in grocery stores all across the country, it all started in the home of the Smolyansky family. Michael and Ludmila Smolyansky immigrated to America from the former Soviet Union and began making Lifeway Kefir in their basement in Skokie, Illinois.


The family knew there was something special about the cultured dairy superfood known as kefir, and they believed that everyone should be able to experience[the benefits](https://lifewaykefir.com/benefits-of-lifeway-kefir/) it could offer. Kefir had been popular in Eastern Europe for centuries, but America needed to catch up, and the Smolyansky family knew they were the just the ones who could make that happen. What started as a small dream turned into a historic American dream story, with Lifeway becoming a publicly traded company (LWAY) two years after its humble founding in 1986.


**Julie Smolyansky** , the daughter of Michael and Ludmila, **became Lifeway’s official CEO in 2002** and has since taken Lifeway from being an everyday brand to a household name. Through Julie’s leadership, Lifeway is proud and able to be celebrating 40 years of continued success and growth with more momentum than ever before.


Last year in 2025, Lifeway had the honor of ringing the NASDAQ closing bell after celebrating yet another year of astounding growth. This year, we were honored by being one of TIME magazine’s top companies for growth of 2026 in the United States, including #2 in Food and Beverage.


Not only has[Lifeway Kefir](https://lifewaykefir.com/benefits-of-lifeway-kefir/) been fully embraced by consumers everywhere, but so has the concept of the importance of gut health for overall wellness. The value of high-quality, nutrient-dense foods that nourish from within is of utmost importance to us at Lifeway because we know it is for all of you. Foods with[bioavailable nutrients](https://lifewaykefir.com/protein-and-the-importance-of-bioavailability/) that help you thrive, with[live probiotics](https://lifewaykefir.com/probiotics/) that support your gut, and that taste incredible while feeling like a treat to enjoy everyday are three core principles behind[all the products](https://www.lifewaykefir.com/all-products) we make.
