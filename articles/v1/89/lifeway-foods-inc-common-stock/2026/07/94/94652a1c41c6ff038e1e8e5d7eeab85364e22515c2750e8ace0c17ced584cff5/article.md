### Benefits of Watermelon


Despite many people assuming that watermelon’s made of mostly water, this gorgeous fruit’s got more going for it than meets the eye. Watermelon is a terrific source of vitamin C, potassium, and magnesium — all nutrients your body needs on a daily basis. It’s also the[highest source of the antioxidant lycopene](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/benefits-of-watermelon) of all fruits and veggies. Additionally, watermelon is a[natural source of the amino acid L-citrulline](https://www.healthline.com/nutrition/foods/watermelon) , which converts to the amino acid arginine once ingested. Arginine assists with healthy blood flow and overall circulation, so watermelon is a terrific fruit to eat for heart health, blood pressure support, and can be useful for pre or post workout purposes.
