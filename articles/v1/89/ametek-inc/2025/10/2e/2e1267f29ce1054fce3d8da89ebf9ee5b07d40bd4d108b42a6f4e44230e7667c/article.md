# AMETEK SI Launches PARSTAT Multichannel – Advanced Technology Series of Potentiostats

# AMETEK SI Launches PARSTAT Multichannel – Advanced Technology Series of Potentiostats


- OUR BUSINESSES
- Tuesday, October 14, 2025


**Oak Ridge, TN — October 14, 2025**


AMETEK Scientific Instruments (SI), under the Princeton Applied Research brand, announces the launch of the PARSTAT Multichannel – Advanced Technology (PMC-AT) series of potentiostats, a next-generation platform engineered to transform electrochemical research workflows.


Built for scientists pushing the boundaries in energy storage, nanotechnology, and materials science, the PMC-AT series combines modular scalability, ultra-sensitive measurements, and multi-user collaboration—enabling faster, more reliable insights across complex experiments. Powered by AMETEK SI’s ASPIRE software, the platform supports advanced analysis, multi-user collaboration, and patented Direct-to-Disk* data management.


“PMC-AT is more than an instrument—it’s a platform for discovery,” said Poojaa Deepak, Director Business Manager, Scientific Instruments. “It’s built to empower researchers pushing boundaries in electrochemistry, and advanced materials research.”


Key Features of PMC-AT:
• **Ultra-High Sensitivity:** Detects minute changes in physical and chemical properties with sub-nanometer precision
• **Scalable Architecture:** Customizable for diverse applications—from fundamental electro chemistry to complex battery applications.
• **Collaborative Workflows:** ASPIRE software enables autonomous operation and seamless data sharing across global teams
• **Secure Data Management:** Patented Direct-to-Disk* technology ensures fast, secure storage and remote access


PMC-AT is now available for order. To learn more or request for a demo visit[www.ameteksi.com](https://www.ameteksi.com/) or[follow AMETEK SI on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ametek-solartron-analytical/posts/?feedView=all) .


### About Scientific Instruments


AMETEK Scientific Instruments is the global leader in the design, manufacture, and support of instrumentation for both the electrochemical and materials communities. Comprised of Princeton Applied Research, Solartron Analytical, and Signal Recovery, AMETEK Scientific Instruments delivers solutions to meet performance and price requirements.
