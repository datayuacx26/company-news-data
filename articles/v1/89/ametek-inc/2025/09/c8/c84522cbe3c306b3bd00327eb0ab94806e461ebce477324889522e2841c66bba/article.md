# AMETEK MOCON® Launches New Small Volume Modules for Dansensor® CheckMate 4

# AMETEK MOCON® Launches New Small Volume Modules for Dansensor® CheckMate 4


- OUR BUSINESSES
- Monday, September 15, 2025


**Ringsted, Denmark — September 15, 2025**


AMETEK MOCON®, the global leader in gas measurement solutions, has introduced two new Small Volume modules for its industry-leading[Dansensor® CheckMate 4](https://www.ametekmocon.com/products/headspacemapgasanalyzers/checkmate-4) headspace gas analyzer. These modules are engineered to deliver precise oxygen measurement in packaging with extremely limited headspace – an increasingly critical need across industries.


Dansensor® provides advanced testing solutions for Modified Atmosphere Packaging (MAP), where headspace gas analysis plays an important role in ensuring product quality and extending shelf life. The new Small Volume modules bring unmatched accuracy and reliability to applications such as coffee capsules, pharmaceutical blister packs, medical vials and ampoules.


Each module features a specially designed probe pen that measures oxygen content directly, without gas extraction. This ensures fast, repeatable and non-destructive testing. One module is optimized for blister pack testing, while the other is tailored for ultra-small headspace environments.


“These advanced modules reflect our commitment to providing innovative solutions that meet our customers’ evolving needs,” said Dorthe Oldrup, global product manager at AMETEK MOCON®. “They expand the CheckMate 4’s capabilities to even the smallest packaging formats, delivering precise, efficient and reliable quality control. Since they are interchangeable with standard Headspace Gas modules, existing users can easily and affordably expand their measurement applications.”
