Trained on more than 250 million bills, including hundreds of thousands of highly complex invoices processed every year, it learns your historical coding patterns and applies them consistently at scale.


Applied to an average of one million multi-line bills each month, the agent:


- Reduces coding steps by up to 89%
- Increases automated multi-line bill coding by nearly 50%
