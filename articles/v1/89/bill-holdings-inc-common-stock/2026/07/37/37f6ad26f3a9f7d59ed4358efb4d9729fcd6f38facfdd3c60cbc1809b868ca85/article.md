## Key questions about the best business cards for travel


### What makes a business card good for travel?


A good business travel card combines strong rewards on travel purchases with perks that reduce travel costs and improve the experience. The most valuable travel cards typically offer elevated earning rates on flights and hotels, airport lounge access for productive layovers, and travel protections like trip cancellation insurance and lost luggage coverage.


### How do business cards for travel work?


1. **Apply and get approved:** Submit a business application. Requirements vary by issuer—some check only business credit, while others may impact personal credit upon acceptance.
2. **Issue cards to employees:** Most business travel cards let you issue employee cards (some at no additional cost) with individual spending limits and category controls.
3. **Earn rewards on travel spend:** Use the card for flights, hotels, and other[travel expenses](https://www.bill.com/product/travel) to earn points or miles at elevated rates. Some cards offer higher rates when booking through the issuer's travel portal.
4. **Redeem for travel or other value:** Use accumulated points for flights, hotel stays, statement credits, or transfer to airline and hotel loyalty programs. Some cards also offer cash back redemption.


### What features should you look for in a business card for travel?


- **Rewards earning rates:** Compare points or miles per dollar on travel categories like flights, hotels, and car rentals—both through the issuer's portal and for direct bookings.
- **Lounge access:** Airport lounge memberships can provide productive workspaces and comfortable layovers. Compare the breadth of lounge networks.
- **Travel protections:** Trip cancellation insurance, baggage insurance, car rental coverage, and cell phone protection can prevent costly surprises.
- **Employee card options:** Consider the cost of additional cards and what spending controls are available (per-card limits, category restrictions, real-time alerts).
- **Expense management integrations:** Cards that sync with accounting software like QuickBooks, NetSuite, or Xero can save significant time on reconciliation.
- **Redemption flexibility:** Some cards restrict points to a single airline or hotel chain, while others offer transferable points or cash back options.


### How much do business cards for travel cost?


Business travel card costs generally fall into these categories:


- **Annual fees** range from $0 (BILL Spend & Expense) \[5\] to $895 (The Business Platinum Card from American Express) \[7\]. Premium cards typically offset higher fees with travel credits, lounge access, and elevated rewards rates.
- **Employee card fees** vary significantly: some cards offer free employee cards \[6\]\[9\], while others charge per employee (e.g., $175 per authorized user for the Citi AAdvantage Executive \[10\] or $400 per Employee Platinum Card for the Amex Business Platinum \[7\]).
- **Foreign transaction fees** are typically waived on dedicated travel cards, though this varies by issuer.


## Benefits of business cards for travel


Business travel cards deliver value beyond basic rewards in several key areas:


**Rewards and cash back.** Top business travel cards offer 3x–10x points on travel purchases, with some cards providing additional multipliers when booking through their travel portals. \[6\]\[9\] These rewards can be redeemed for flights, hotel stays, statement credits, or transferred to loyalty program partners.


**Streamlined expense tracking.** Some business travel cards integrate with accounting and expense management software, automatically categorizing transactions and syncing with your books. \[1\]\[6\] This can reduce the time spent on manual expense reporting and reconciliation.


**Spending controls.** Business cards may allow you to issue employee cards with individual spending limits, category restrictions, and real-time alerts. \[1\]\[6\] This provides employees with spending flexibility while maintaining budget oversight.


‍ **Travel-specific perks.** Premium travel cards often include airport lounge access, elite hotel status, free checked bags, priority boarding, and travel insurance protections. \[6\]\[7\]\[10\] These perks can reduce travel costs and improve the experience for frequent business travelers.


## How to choose the right travel business card for your business


Factor How it affects your decision


**Travel frequency** Frequent travelers can justify premium annual fees through regular use of lounge access and travel credits. Occasional travelers may prefer a no-fee or low-fee card.


**Airline/hotel loyalty** If your team flies one airline or stays at one hotel chain consistently, a co-branded card may offer better value through elite status and brand-specific perks.


**Number of employees** Compare per-card fees—they range from $0 to $400 per employee and add up quickly across a team.


**Expense management needs** If you need budget controls, real-time visibility, and accounting software integration, look for cards with built-in expense management or that integrate with your existing tools.


**Rewards flexibility** General travel cards offer points transferable to multiple partners. Cards that are co-branded with a specific airline or hotel chain may offer higher earning rates but limit redemptions to one brand.


**Annual fee vs. value** Calculate net value by subtracting the annual fee from credits and benefits you will actually use — not just those available.
