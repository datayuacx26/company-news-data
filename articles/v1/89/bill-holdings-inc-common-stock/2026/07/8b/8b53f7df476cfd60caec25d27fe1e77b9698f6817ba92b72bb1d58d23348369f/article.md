## Key questions about accounts payable software


### What is accounts payable software?


[Accounts payable software automates](https://www.bill.com/learning/accounts-payable-automation) the process of receiving, reviewing, approving, and paying vendor invoices. Instead of managing invoices through email, spreadsheets, and manual data entry, AP software brings the entire workflow into a single platform—from the moment an invoice arrives to the point where payment is sent and recorded in your accounting system.


Modern AP platforms go beyond basic digitization. They use AI and machine learning to extract invoice data, detect duplicates, route approvals automatically, and flag potential fraud—reducing the manual work that traditionally makes AP one of the most time-consuming functions in finance.


### How does accounts payable software work?


1. **Invoice capture:** Vendors email or upload invoices, and the system automatically extracts key data—vendor name, amount, line items, due date—using OCR and AI.
2. **Coding and matching:** The software assigns GL codes based on historical patterns and matches invoices against purchase orders and receipts (2-way or 3-way matching).
3. **Approval routing:** Invoices are routed to the right approvers based on configurable rules such as amount, department, vendor, or project.
4. **Payment execution:** Approved invoices are grouped into payment runs via ACH, check, card, wire, or international transfer.
5. **Sync and reconciliation:** Transactions sync back to the general ledger or ERP, giving finance teams real-time visibility into cash outflows and outstanding liabilities.


### What features should you look for in accounts payable software?


- **Invoice processing automation:** AI-powered data extraction, duplicate detection, and automatic GL coding to reduce manual entry.
- **Approval workflows:** Multi-level routing based on amount, department, or vendor, with mobile approvals and audit trails.
- **Payment options:** ACH, checks, cards, wires, and international/multi-currency support to match how your vendors want to be paid.
- **PO matching:** Automated 2-way and 3-way matching to catch discrepancies before payment.
- **Integration capabilities:** Native two-way sync with your accounting software or ERP—QuickBooks, NetSuite, Sage Intacct, Xero, or Microsoft Dynamics.
- **Fraud detection:** Real-time monitoring and predictive analysis to catch suspicious invoices and payment anomalies.

## Benefits of accounts payable software


AP automation delivers measurable improvements across several areas:


**Faster processing and fewer errors.** AI-powered invoice capture and coding eliminate manual data entry, reducing processing time by 20% or more and improving accuracy on key fields to 99%. \[1\]\[3\] Automated matching catches duplicate invoices and discrepancies before payment.


**Stronger fraud prevention.** Modern AP platforms use predictive AI to monitor transactions in real-time, flagging suspicious activity such as unexpected vendor banking changes or duplicate payment attempts. BILL, for example, processes 5M+ fraud predictions daily across its network. \[1\]


**Better cash flow visibility.** Real-time dashboards and automated sync with accounting software give finance teams a live view of outstanding liabilities, upcoming payments, and available cash—supporting more accurate forecasting and faster month-end close.


**Reduced cost per invoice.** By automating capture, coding, approval, and payment, AP software replaces manual steps that traditionally require printing, routing, data entry, and filing—significantly reducing the total cost to process each invoice.


## How to choose the right accounts payable software for your business


Factor How it affects your decision


Invoice volume High-volume operations benefit from AI-powered automation and per-document pricing. Lower volumes may be well-served by simpler tools with lower monthly costs.


International payments If you pay vendors in multiple countries and currencies, prioritize platforms with built-in global payment support and tax compliance.


ERP and accounting system Match the platform to your accounting software. Check whether integrations are native two-way sync or manual export—and whether they're included in your pricing tier.


Team size and roles Compare per-user vs. per-document vs. flat-rate pricing. Consider whether approver-only users, requestors, or admins are charged separately.


Approval complexity Multi-level, department-based, or amount-based routing requires configurable approval workflows. Simpler operations may only need basic authorization.


Growth plans If you expect to add entities, locations, or international vendors, choose a platform that supports multi-entity management and global payments without requiring a migration.
