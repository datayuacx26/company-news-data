# BILL Launches New AI Agents to Power Touchless Transactions for the Fortune 5 Million

**SAN JOSE, Calif. – October 28, 2025 –** BILL (NYSE: BILL), the intelligent finance platform trusted by half a million businesses to manage, move, and maximize their money, today announced the launch of BILL AI, BILL’s suite of intelligent AI agents and features for the Fortune 5 Million—the small and midsize businesses that power the U.S. economy. The first BILL agents and agentic-powered features autonomously collect and validate W-9s, reconcile receipts, accelerate user onboarding, and execute routine tasks, removing the friction that slows businesses down.


**BILL AI Cuts Through Back Office Friction**


The Fortune 500 are largely automated, but the Fortune 5 Million are stuck with manual workflows and friction. For example, more than 90% of businesses say collecting W-9s is the most painful part of doing taxes, and many spend too much time on expense management. These inefficiencies force leaders into trade-offs between running the back office and growing their business. BILL AI agents eliminate these workflows, freeing teams to focus on growth and serving their customers.


“The Fortune 5 Million deserve the same power and precision as the Fortune 500,” said René Lacerte, CEO and Founder of BILL. “BILL AI removes friction from the back office by deleting the workflows that bury businesses. With data from having made over a trillion dollars in payment transactions across a network of millions of businesses and accounting firms, we are uniquely positioned to deliver AI at a scale that is unmatched in our category. The future of fintech is touchless B2B transactions, and BILL is leading the way for SMBs.”


**Agents Trained by a Trillion Dollars and a Billion Documents**


BILL AI has helped accelerate billions in payments and processed more than 1.3 billion documents. Since the beginning of 2025, our AI solutions have increased fully automated bills by more than 80%, and we’ve stopped 8 million fraud attempts in FY25 alone. With data from over $1 trillion in transactions across a network of 8 million members, we currently serve nearly half a million businesses and 9,000 accounting firms—this scale makes BILL the trusted AI solution for SMBs, with agents that are smarter, faster, and more reliable from the start.


**Agents that Delete Workflows, Deliver Touchless Transactions**


BILL’s new AI agents and features automate critical financial workflows and handle routine tasks so leaders can focus on running and growing their businesses:


**BILL W-9 Agent:**


- Touchless tax compliance that eliminates over 80% of manual steps for collecting W-9s. First-of-its-kind agent autonomously requests and pre-validates W-9s from vendors. It can erase most of the work associated with collecting up to 3 million W-9s, saving BILL customers an estimated 650,000 hours.
- In the coming weeks, the agent is expected to be available to 170,000+ businesses, making tax compliance faster, easier, and more reliable.


**BILL Reconciliation Agent:**


- Touchless receipts agent that automatically codes transactions, so receipts reconcile themselves. Provides full visibility into expenses, helping finance teams stay organized and compliant.
- In early rollout, transactions processed entirely by AI increased 533%, at 92% accuracy.
- Within six months, expected to scale to 40,000+ businesses that are managing 70 million+ receipts annually—with the ultimate goal of making receipts touchless.


In addition, BILL’s new **agentic-powered onboarding** will deliver a more intuitive setup for new Spend & Expense users tailored to each business, so employees can start spending compliantly from day one. The new **agentic-powered BILL Assistant** will also provide personalized, instant answers, intelligent recommendations, and complete tasks on behalf of customers—so they can spend less time managing the back office and more time growing their business.
