# Air Products to Speak at Bernstein Strategic Decisions Conference on May 27

LEHIGH VALLEY, Pa.


, May 20, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Air Products' (NYSE:APD ) Chief Executive Officer[Eduardo Menezes](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4692286-1&h=1979346949&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.airproducts.com%2Fcompany%2Fleadership%2Feduardo-menezes-bio&a=Eduardo+Menezes) and Chief Financial Officer[Melissa Schaeffer](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4692286-1&h=3811040033&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.airproducts.com%2Fcompany%2Fleadership%2Fmelissa-schaeffer-bio&a=Melissa+Schaeffer) will participate in a fireside chat at the Bernstein 42nd Annual Strategic Decisions Conference on Wednesday, May 27, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. USET.


Access the audio webcast from via Air Products' Investor Relations[Event Details](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4692286-1&h=3368151192&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.airproducts.com%2Fevents%2Fevent-details%2Fair-products-bernsteins-strategic-decisions-conference-sdc&a=Event+Details) website.
