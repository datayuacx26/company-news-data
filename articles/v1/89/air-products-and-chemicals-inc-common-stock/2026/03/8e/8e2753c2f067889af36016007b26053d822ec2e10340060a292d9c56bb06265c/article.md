# Air Products' Executive Leadership to Speak at J.P. Morgan Industrials Conference on March 18

LEHIGH VALLEY, Pa.


, March 11, 2026


/PRNewswire/ -- Air Products' (NYSE:APD ) Chief Executive Officer[Eduardo Menezes](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4638159-1&h=1885294190&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.airproducts.com%2Fcompany%2Fleadership%2Feduardo-menezes-bio&a=Eduardo+Menezes) and Chief Financial Officer[Melissa Schaeffer](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4638159-1&h=3867338058&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.airproducts.com%2Fcompany%2Fleadership%2Fmelissa-schaeffer-bio&a=Melissa+Schaeffer) will participate in a fireside chat at the J.P. Morgan Industrials Conference on Wednesday, March 18, 2026 at 8:05 a.m. USET.


Access the audio webcast from Air Products' Investor Relations[Event Details](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4638159-1&h=3579863501&u=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.airproducts.com%2Fevents%2Fevent-details%2Fair-products-jp-morgan-industrials-conference-2&a=Event+Details) website.
