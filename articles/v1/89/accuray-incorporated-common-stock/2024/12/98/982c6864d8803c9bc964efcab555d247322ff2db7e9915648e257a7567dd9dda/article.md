# Record-Breaking Attendance at Accuray French-speaking Users Meeting

The Accuray French-speaking Users Meeting held on December 5th – 6th in Lille, France, included customers from France, Switzerland, Luxembourg, and Belgium. It marked a milestone with the highest turnout for any Accuray French-speaking Users Meeting to date. More than 130 participants from 37 centers and four countries joined—both in person and virtually—creating a vibrant environment of knowledge-sharing and innovation. This remarkable engagement reflects the community’s commitment to advancing patient-focused radiotherapy techniques.


At the heart of this achievement was the guidance of a dedicated scientific committee. Through the expertise and leadership of Esther Bouche, Sébastien Carreau, Frédérik Crop, Arnaud Felin, Thomas Lacornerie, Dr. Xavier Liem, and Dr. David Pasquier, the agenda successfully bridged established clinical evidence with forward-looking strategies. Their curation ensured that each session provided actionable insights for evolving clinical practices.


### ‘When Technology Enables a Change in Precision’ the Topics Covered Included:


- **Refining Breast Irradiation:** Presentations on TomoDirect™ and[VitalHold](https://www.accuray.com/vitalhold/) ™ on the[Radixact® System](https://www.accuray.com/radixact/) offered insights into enhancing treatment accuracy, workflow, and patient comfort.
- **New Frontiers in Treatment:** From neurosurgical applications of the[CyberKnife® System](https://www.accuray.com/cyberknife/) to managing multiple brain metastases, discussions highlighted the evolving scope of stereotactic radiosurgery.
- **Quality and Precision:** Sessions on QA protocols,[Synchrony® real-time adaptive](https://www.accuray.com/blog/why-motion-matters-the-advantage-of-real-time-tracking/) , and the integration of MLCs on the CyberKnife® System demonstrated how innovation can elevate clinical outcomes.


### Accuray French-speaking Users Meeting Stands as a Real Success


With dynamic presentations, stimulating roundtable discussions, and a strong scientific foundation the French-speaking Users Meeting stands as a success for our long-term users and new customers. We look forward to building on this momentum and continuing to push boundaries—together.
