# Bowman Recognized on Zweig Group’s 2026 Hot Firms List

Bowman has been recognized as the 13th fastest-growing AEC firm on[Zweig Group’s 2026 Hot Firms List](https://zweiglist.com/hot-firm/) .


The annual ranking recognizes the top-performing firms across the United States and Canada and is widely regarded as one of the industry’s premier benchmarks for growth and business performance.


Bowman’s inclusion on this year’s list reflects the company’s continued execution of its growth strategy and its ability to expand capabilities, strengthen market presence and deliver value for clients across the infrastructure lifecycle.


As the company continues to scale its platform and broaden its reach, this recognition underscores the strength of Bowman’s business model and the collective efforts of employees across the organization who continue to drive its success.
