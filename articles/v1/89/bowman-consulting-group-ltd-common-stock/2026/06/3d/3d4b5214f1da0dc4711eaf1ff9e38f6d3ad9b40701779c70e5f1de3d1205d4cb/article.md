# Bowman Ranks on ENR Top 500 Design Firms, Lands Among Top 100 for Fourth Consecutive Year

Bowman’s investments in talent, capabilities and geographic reach continue to earn industry recognition, with the company ranking #71 on Engineering News-Record’s (ENR) 2026 Top 500 Design Firms list. This year’s ranking, which reflects revenue from design services performed in 2025, marks Bowman’s fourth consecutive year in the top 100 and our twenty-third appearance on the coveted list.


This milestone reflects the entrepreneurial spirit and expertise our employees bring to their work every day. Their willingness to collaborate across disciplines has opened new opportunities for cross-selling while helping clients access more of Bowman’s capabilities through a single trusted team. That approach continues to strengthen relationships, support our growth and position Bowman for continued success.


The[ENR Top 500 Design Firms](https://www.enr.com/toplists/2026-top-500-design-firms-preview) list is widely regarded as a benchmark for industry performance and market presence. Bowman’s position among the nation’s leading firms, alongside strong regional and sector rankings, speaks to the quality of our work, the strength of our client relationships and the growing impact of our teams across the industry.


Bowman also ranked #61 on ENR’s Top 100 Pure Designers list.
