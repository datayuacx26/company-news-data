# Virginia Teams Get a Close-Up Look at Bowman’s Newest Aircraft

Employees from Bowman’s Leesburg and corporate offices in Virginia recently had the chance to tour the newest addition to the company’s manned aerial fleet, a Cessna 208 Caravan, during a stopover at Leesburg Executive Airport.


The pilots hosting the visit walked employees through the aircraft, giving them a firsthand look at the newest addition to Bowman’s growing fleet.


The event marks the launch of a new national initiative that invites local offices to visit Bowman aircraft whenever they are operating nearby. The goal is simple: give employees more opportunities to see firsthand how the company’s aerial operations support its work, and to connect with the teams behind them.


Bowman’s aerial fleet continues to grow, and so does the chance for employees across the country to engage with it. Future stops are already being planned as the program expands to additional locations.
