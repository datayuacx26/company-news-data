# Bowman By Land, Air & Sea: Rethinking Port Asset Lifecycle Management

*Bowman by Land, Air & Sea is a blog series that explores the full scope of our services and how they work together to support today’s built environment. Whether a project calls for work on land, in the air or at sea, Bowman brings an integrated approach to every phase of development.*


Ports are complex by nature. The infrastructure that keeps them running spans land and water, often with limited visibility of what’s happening below the surface and even less clarity on how to act on that information.


With our Port Asset Condition Kit (PACK), Bowman brings asset data into a unified platform. More importantly, PACK established the foundation for a data-centric digital twin of port infrastructure. Combined with our inspection, engineering and environmental services, this creates a connected workflow that helps ports move from understanding their infrastructure to improving it—with one partner supporting every step.


## ****A Technology-Driven Approach to an Old Problem****


Consider a legacy port with decades of infrastructure but limited historical records. Some assets have been inspected multiple times, others rarely. Condition reports exist but in PDFs, spreadsheets or paper archives. Subsurface conditions are even less understood, with hydrographic surveys and sonar data stored separately from above-water inspections.


This is a common reality for many ports.


PACK changes this paradigm by creating a centralized, GIS-based platform for all port asset data. The proprietary platform integrates information from across the marine environment—including LiDAR, hydrographic surveys and utility mapping—into one system, giving users an easy-to-navigate view of assets above and below the waterline in both 2D and 3D.


Instead of navigating disconnected datasets, users interact with a single map-based hub that connects:


- Inspection history
- Condition ratings
- Georeferenced imagery & video
- As-built drawings & reports


This unified view allows port operators to move from fragmented data to a living system of record that evolves with every inspection and project. This is a critical first step toward building a digital twin that reflects real-world conditions over time.


## **Advancing Proactive Asset Management**


PACK’s true value lies in how it transforms data into clear next steps.


Through interactive dashboards, users can quickly assess asset conditions, identify high-priority repairs and understand the implications of different maintenance strategies. Condition ratings—ranging from satisfactory to critical—are directly tied to specific assets.


For a port modernizing its operations, this means:


- Improved understanding of asset conditions
- Confident capital planning
- Proactive risk management
- Enhanced team coordination


As this data becomes more complete and continuously updated, it strengthens the underlying data needed to expand into more advanced digital twin capabilities, including scenario planning and lifecycle forecasting.


## **Turning Plans Into Projects**


As ports use this data to plan maintenance and capital improvements, Bowman continues to support the next phase. Our multidisciplinary teams help advance projects through design, permitting and construction with the following services:


- Maritime engineering & design
- Environmental services
- Planning & resiliency support
- Construction inspection & management


These services are integrated, allowing clients to move seamlessly from understanding their assets to improving them without transitioning between multiple vendors or losing continuity along the way.


## **A More Connected Future for Ports and Harbors**


As ports modernize and adapt to new demands, the ability to understand and act on infrastructure data will only become more critical. Solutions like PACK provide the visibility needed to make informed decisions and Bowman’s integrated services ensure those decisions can be carried forward.
