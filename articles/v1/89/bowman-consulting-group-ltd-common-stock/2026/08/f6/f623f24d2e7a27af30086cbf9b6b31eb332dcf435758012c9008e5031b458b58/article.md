# Bowman Advances Five Spots on ENR’s Top 150 Global Design Firms List

Bowman has climbed to #121 on Engineering News-Record’s (ENR) Top 150 Global Design Firms list, advancing five spots from #126 in 2025. The recognition marks Bowman’s third consecutive appearance on the list and reflects the company’s continued growth and expanding position within the engineering and design industry.


The recognition follows Bowman’s strong showing across[ENR’s 2026 rankings](https://bowman.com/news/bowman-ranks-on-enr-top-500-design-firms-lands-among-top-100-for-fourth-consecutive-year) . Earlier this year, the company ranked #71 on ENR’s Top 500 Design Firms list, marking its fourth consecutive year among the top 100, and #61 on the Top 100 Pure Designers list. Bowman also earned recognition across ENR’s regional and market-sector rankings, reflecting the breadth of the company’s capabilities and growing presence across the industry.


This achievement reflects the expertise and commitment of our teams across the organization. As Bowman continues to scale, we remain focused on delivering high-quality solutions, creating value for our clients and building on the momentum that continues to move our company forward.
