# How Owner’s Representative Services Close the Solar & BESS Talent Gap

As utility-scale solar and battery energy storage system (BESS) developments continue to increase, many developers are finding they lack the experienced staff needed to bring projects to the finish line.


In fact, the[International Energy Agency](https://www.iea.org/news/energy-employment-has-surged-but-growing-skills-shortages-threaten-future-momentum) reports that more than half of the 700 energy companies it surveyed are struggling to find seasoned project and construction managers. This shortage is severe enough to delay projects and raise costs. For developers, this is noticeable during pre-construction and construction where oversight is essential for maintaining budget and schedule. Without these professionals, even a well-planned project can experience delays and coordination challenges.


## **Rethinking Project Staffing Strategies**


Given this shortage, many developers are re-evaluating how they staff projects. Rather than competing for scarce talent, they’re turning to third-party owner’s representative services: a model that scales up or down with project needs, without the cost or lead time of a full-time hire.


An owner’s representative acts as an extension of the developer’s team, supporting functions like construction management, QA/QC, stakeholder coordination and risk management. This model gives developers the skill set their projects require, without permanently increasing headcount.
