# Introducing the Martin Dashboard

Since we released the Martin web app in beta a few weeks ago, we've received tons of amazing feedback from you. We've gone back to the drawing board and built a brand new dashboard for Martin in addition to his text chat.


Now complete with all your to-dos, reminders, inbox, and calendar, Martin now keeps **all your context in one place.**


### The Martin Dashboard


For the past year, we've stuck to a simple interface on mobile. Now, as his capabilities grow beyond simple, one step commands, Martin is moving to web as well.


This dashboard lets you manage your calendar, inbox, to-dos, reminders, dispatched phone calls, and more. As we add more integrations and capabilities to Martin, they will each get a designated card on the dashboard.


At any point, you can outsource tasks to Martin directly in the chat on the right. Before Martin executes something important, like sending an email or text message, he'll confirm all the details with you in a widget. If the content is not to your satisfaction, you can edit the widget directly.


This brand new version of Martin on web is live at[app.trymartin.com](https://app.trymartin.com/) . As always, we love hearing your feedback.
