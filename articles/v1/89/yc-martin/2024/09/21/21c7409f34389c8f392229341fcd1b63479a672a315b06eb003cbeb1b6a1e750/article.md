# Gmail, Slack, Google Drive Integrations

Over the past month, we have been hard at work adding new capabilities and integrations to the Martin ecosystem. As a direct result of your feedback, we have added Gmail, Slack, Google Drive, and Phone Calls to Martin's arsenal of integrations. We are very excited to see how you use them!


### 1. Gmail Integration


##### *Read, Draft, and Send emails with Martin*


**Martin is fully integrated with Gmail. This means he can:**


-


**Compose** an email draft for you


-


**Send** an email for you


-


**Search** your inbox for a contact, topic, or label


-


**Read** your recent emails **Reply** to an email thread


-


**Forward** an email


-


**Add/remove labels** to emails


-


**Mark** an email as read/unread


-


**Archive** an email


-


**Trash** an email


Connect your inbox by going to Integrations panel in the app (the hammer icon). Tap on Inbox and log in with your Gmail account to get started.Call Martin to check your email on the drive to work. Shoot him a WhatsApp message to start a draft. Or...ping him on **Slack** to search for a message in your inbox.


### 2. Slack Integration


##### Ping Martin in your Slack workspace


You can now add Martin to your Slack workspace. Just like you would contact Martin over SMS, WhatsApp or email, you can now:


-


**Type @Martin in any channel**


-


**Type /martin in any channel**


-


**DM Martin in your Slack workspace**


Connect your Slack workspace by going to Integrations panel in the app (the hammer icon). Tap on Slack > Add New Slack Workspace to get started.On command, Martin can look something up, set a reminder, schedule an event, retrieve an email...and now, even start a doc in **Google Drive!**


### 3. Google Drive \[BETA\]


##### *Create, read, and edit docs in Drive*


**You can now connect Martin with Google Drive** (beta). This means you can go over meeting notes with him over the phone, or forward him an email that you want converted into a doc.


Martin's Google Drive actions currently include:


-


**Search** your Google Drive files


-


**Open & Read** Google Docs


-


**Create** Google Docs


-


**Compose & Edit** Google Docs


-


**Comment** on Google Docs


Soon, Martin will be able to read, edit, and create other types of files, upload files, and more. This integration is released only to beta testers for now.


### 4. Phone Call Reminders


##### *Martin can now remind you with a call*


Need a wake up call, a debrief session with Martin, or any other urgent reminder?


**You can now set reminders to be sent to you via a phone call (in addition to via email, text, or an app notification).**


Just tell Martin your preferred format when scheduling the reminder and you'll get the corresponding ping right on time.
