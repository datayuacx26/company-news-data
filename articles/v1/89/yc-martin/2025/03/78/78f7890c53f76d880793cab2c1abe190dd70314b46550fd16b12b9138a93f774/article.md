**Stop playing ping pong with Calendly links. Cc Martin instead.**


Like a human assistant, he'll just take care of it.


1) A co-worker asks for a meeting next week.


2) You cc[\[email protected\]](https://www.trymartin.com/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection) and say "find a time for us".


3) Martin checks your calendar and proposes a few times on the email thread.


4) Co-worker responds to you "that works".


5) If someone can't make the proposed slot, they can email the thread and Martin will work out a new time.


6) Once everyone confirms, Martin adds a calendar event on your calendars with a meeting link. Violà!
