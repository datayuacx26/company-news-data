# Apps, Notes, V2 To-dos, More Languages

Today, we're launching **Martin Apps** , including a new version of **To-dos** and **Notes.** Martin can also speak 13 more languages, and send texts to 100 more countries.


Each card on the Martin Dashboard ([app.trymartin.com](https://e.customeriomail.com/e/c/eyJlIjoxNjYyOTEsImVtYWlsX2lkIjoiZXhhbXBsZSIsImhyZWYiOiJodHRwczovL2FwcC50cnltYXJ0aW4uY29tIiwidCI6MTc0NjYwMjg2OX0/91231d44d18a4803137a81bac6903d0284084131c96921065ba57510a0d7350a) ) is now an app!


You can add and remove apps from your dashboard on the[apps page](https://e.customeriomail.com/e/c/eyJlIjoxNjYyOTEsImVtYWlsX2lkIjoiZXhhbXBsZSIsImhyZWYiOiJodHRwczovL2FwcC50cnltYXJ0aW4uY29tL2FwcHMiLCJ0IjoxNzQ2NjAyODY5fQ/32c6297ed214e910799dfedfa65b000deae1e704434d591e691ccdcf529052ad) .


In the future, we'll be adding more apps, and you'll get to choose which ones to activate and show on your dashboard.


**Jot down notes that can be read and edited by Martin.**


Got an email you want to save? Forward it to Martin.


Have an idea you want to brainstorm? Call Martin and note it down.


When Martin saves a note for you, it'll show up in your web dashboard under Notes. You can also find it in the Notes section of the Martin iOS app.


**Lists are now To-dos!**


You can now keep track of your **to-dos in one place** , **view past to-dos, add labels, and filter by labels.**


Moving forward, we'll be adding more task management specific features to make Martin your ultimate accountability friend.


**Looking for a brighter way to work?**


A light mode for Martin is finally here. Just click the theme toggle in the bottom left corner of your dashboard to switch it on.


Martin can now speak Spanish, French, Chinese, and 10 other languages.


You can switch his voice settings in the iOS or web app, and he'll use this language during phone calls and voice sessions in the app.
