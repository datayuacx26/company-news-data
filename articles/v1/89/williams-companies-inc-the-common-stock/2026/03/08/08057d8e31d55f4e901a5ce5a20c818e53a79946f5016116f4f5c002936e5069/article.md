# Williams Leadership to Share Insights on Energy Infrastructure and Innovation at CERAWeek 2026

*Senior executives from Williams will join global energy leaders at the world’s preeminent energy conference, March 23-27 in Houston*


TULSA, Okla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Mar. 10, 2026-- Williams (NYSE: WMB) President and Chief Executive Officer Chad Zamarin and additional leaders from Williams will join global energy executives and policymakers at CERAWeek 2026, March 23–27 in Houston.


Williams executives will contribute insights on unlocking America's full potential by delivering energy infrastructure solutions and innovations as the world navigates rapidly shifting market and technology dynamics.


- Zamarin will participate in a plenary session with other energy industry leaders to discuss[U.S. Gas Strategies in Changing Markets](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ceraweek.com%2Fen%2Fprogram%2Fus-gas-strategies-in-changing-markets-1068-50052&esheet=54442886&newsitemid=20260310320171&lan=en-US&anchor=U.S.+Gas+Strategies+in+Changing+Markets&index=1&md5=43561e53641aca4b69600b658b24c6c5) (Wednesday, March 25 at 12:10 p.m. CDT). The session will focus on how the strategies of U.S. energy companies are evolving in response to shifting market and policy conditions as well as the critical role of the U.S. as a global energy supplier amidst dynamic regulatory pressures, investor demands and global market opportunities.


- Debbie Pickle, Senior Vice President and Chief Human Resources Officer, will speak in an executive session entitled[Creating AI-ready Organizations](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ceraweek.com%2Fen%2Fprogram%2Fcreating-aiready-organizations-1068-50565-839645&esheet=54442886&newsitemid=20260310320171&lan=en-US&anchor=Creating+AI-ready+Organizations&index=2&md5=ac7a4f045a615817450fb2e23f73526b) (Tuesday, March 24 at 4:20 p.m. CDT). During this session, energy leaders will discuss re-engineering culture, talent and data infrastructure as the sector races to unlock AI’s potential. Pickle will also participate in the session[Skills for Tomorrow, Today: Cultivating Workforce Agility](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ceraweek.com%2Fen%2Fprogram%2Fskills-for-tomorrow-today-cultivating-workforce-agility-1068-50490-839571&esheet=54442886&newsitemid=20260310320171&lan=en-US&anchor=Skills+for+Tomorrow%2C+Today%3A+Cultivating+Workforce+Agility&index=3&md5=edf11c2e381ae5c7387e3fc7d345d54d) (Wednesday, March 25 at 11:20 a.m. CDT). The panel will focus on strategies to enhance recruitment and retention of skilled personnel to ensure operational readiness in today’s dynamic energy industry.


- Jaclyn Presnal, Vice President of New Energy Ventures, will speak on the panel[Hybrid Power for Data Centers with Gas and Nuclear Solutions](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ceraweek.com%2Fen%2Fprogram%2Fhybrid-power-for-data-centers-with-gas-and-nuclear-solutions-1068-50992-840062&esheet=54442886&newsitemid=20260310320171&lan=en-US&anchor=Hybrid+Power+for+Data+Centers+with+Gas+and+Nuclear+Solutions&index=4&md5=b5e82b71f745f11cf1286e8a7ae38a0f) (Monday, March 23 at 3:30 p.m. CDT). The discussion will focus on integrating intermittent renewables with onsite gas turbines and advanced battery systems to guarantee 24/7 uptime.


- Naveen Pandrangi, Vice President & Chief Transformation and Information Officer, will speak on the panel[Code is the Current: How AI is Engineering the Energy Transformation](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ceraweek.com%2Fen%2Fprogram%2Fcode-is-the-new-current-how-ai-is-engineering-the-energy-transformation-1068-51512&esheet=54442886&newsitemid=20260310320171&lan=en-US&anchor=Code+is+the+Current%3A+How+AI+is+Engineering+the+Energy+Transformation&index=5&md5=22307cfa56a3bfac569ba500217e8989) (5:30 p.m. CDT Monday, March 23). The session will cover how physical infrastructure is being rebuilt by the digital infrastructure of the future as the industry races to address affordability, reliability, security and sustainability needs while meeting skyrocketing demand.


- Zach Keith, Director of Business Development and Sustainability, will participate in the panel[Carbon Differentiated Natural Gas: Does it Matter?](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ceraweek.com%2Fen%2Fprogram%2Fcarbon-differentiated-natural-gas-does-it-matter-1068-51198&esheet=54442886&newsitemid=20260310320171&lan=en-US&anchor=Carbon+Differentiated+Natural+Gas%3A+Does+it+Matter%3F&index=6&md5=d9320140f0d60d9c77800d0a4ac58085) (Wednesday, March 25 at 2:30 p.m. CDT). The discussion will explore the state of methane performance today, challenges and opportunities of advancing regulations and what it will take for the market to reward better methane performers.


In addition to the panel discussions, Williams executives will be made available for media interviews during CERAWeek. To request an interview, please complete the media interview request form[on the Williams CERAWeek webpage](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Faction.williams.com%2Fceraweek2026-media-request%2F&esheet=54442886&newsitemid=20260310320171&lan=en-US&anchor=on+the+Williams+CERAWeek+webpage&index=7&md5=7dc5827354fe0a6d36de3cdf37cdffad) .
