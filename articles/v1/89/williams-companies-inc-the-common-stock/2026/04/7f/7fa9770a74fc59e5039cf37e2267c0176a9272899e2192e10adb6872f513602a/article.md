# Williams to Report First-Quarter 2026 Financial Results on May 4; Earnings Conference Call and Webcast Scheduled for May 5

TULSA, Okla.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Apr. 14, 2026-- Williams (NYSE: WMB) plans to announce its first-quarter 2026 financial results after the market closes on Monday, May 4, 2026.


The company’s first-quarter 2026 conference call and webcast with analysts and investors is scheduled for Tuesday, May 5, 2026, at 9:30 a.m. Eastern Time (8:30 a.m. Central Time).
