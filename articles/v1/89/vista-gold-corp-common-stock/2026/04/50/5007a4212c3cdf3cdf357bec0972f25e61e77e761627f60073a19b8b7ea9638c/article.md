# Vista Gold to Present at the Mining Forum Europe 2026 Conference

DENVER--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Vista Gold Corp. (NYSE American and TSX: VGZ) announced today that Frederick H. Earnest, President and CEO of Vista, will be presenting at the Mining Forum Europe 2026 conference in Zurich, Switzerland. Mr. Earnest’s presentation is scheduled for Monday, April 13, at 2:00 p.m. CEST (6:00 a.m. MDT or 8:00 a.m. EDT). The session will be live-streamed and available for on-demand viewing by accessing the link below. It can also be viewed on the Company's website at[www.vistagold.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.vistagold.com&esheet=54491956&newsitemid=20260409075242&lan=en-US&anchor=www.vistagold.com&index=1&md5=af0579a71452406e6c64af34aba7877c) . Additionally, Vista's management team will host a series of meetings with institutional investors and others during the conference from April 13 to April 15, 2026.


Presentation Webcast Link:[https://europe.miningforum.com/member-webcast/4401/](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Feurope.miningforum.com%2Fmember-webcast%2F4401%2F&esheet=54491956&newsitemid=20260409075242&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Feurope.miningforum.com%2Fmember-webcast%2F4401%2F&index=2&md5=6f7e9374caa249f6ae2a2c8624b70747)


**About Vista Gold Corp.**


Vista holds the Mt Todd gold project, located in the Tier-1 mining jurisdiction of Northern Territory, Australia. Mt Todd is among the largest development-stage projects in Australia. The Company has defined a clear pathway to value realization, targeting the commencement of detailed engineering and design in 2027. This milestone is expected to initiate an approximately 27-month period of design, construction, and commissioning, culminating in first gold production.


Mt Todd offers strong project economics, significant initial production, and compelling expansion and exploration upside. Mt Todd benefits from advanced local infrastructure, options for future expansion, and broad community support, underpinning its potential to become a long-lived, globally significant gold operation.


For further information about Vista or Mt Todd, please contact Pamela Solly, Vice President of Investor Relations, at (720) 981-1185 or visit the Company’s website at[www.vistagold.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.vistagold.com&esheet=54491956&newsitemid=20260409075242&lan=en-US&anchor=www.vistagold.com&index=3&md5=659e88b6ea20a16051a3b1320f4313f9) .
