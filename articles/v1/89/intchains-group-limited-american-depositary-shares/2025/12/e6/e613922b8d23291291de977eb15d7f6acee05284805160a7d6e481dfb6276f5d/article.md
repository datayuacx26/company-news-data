# ICG × Goldshell | 2025 Annual Summary: A Brilliant Closing!

As the 2025 journey concluded✨, Intchains Group (NASDAQ: ICG) and its flagship brand Goldshell have forged ahead side by side, complementing each other with synergy and unwavering dedication. What remarkable achievements have we accomplished together this year? What pivotal strategic layouts have we completed? Today, let’s unveil the standout highlights of ICG × Goldshell’s 2025 journey!


**January**


Goldshell’s computing servers achieved a groundbreaking milestone!


Goldshell announced the upcoming launch of its first Aleo-dedicated computing server — AE BOX, kicking off 2025 with a stellar start.


(Aleo leverages zk-SNARK technology, its proprietary Leo programming language, and a product positioning focused on privacy computing, directly addressing the core demands of the zk ecosystem: enabling **data privacy, verifiability and compliance** on the blockchain. It has thus become one of the indispensable “privacy layer” projects in the zk ecosystem.)


AE BOX Pre-launch Campaign


**February**


Goldshell consumer-grade computing servers: AE BOX/AE BOX PRO officially launched!


Goldshell co-hosted an AMA with Aleo to delve deep into collaboration and development in the zero-knowledge proof sector. The low-entry threshold of its consumer-grade computing servers garnered an overwhelming market response.


**March**


Goldshell’s marketing team shone at MiningDisrupt 2025 and unveiled the groundbreaking BYTE Series products.


The BYTE Series is designed to expand usage scenarios for Goldshell end users, featuring upgradable host machines that allow customers to quickly switch sub-cards for different projects. This innovative series solves the pain point of frequent server replacements caused by industry cycles, and marks a key step in the company’s product line optimization.


Goldshell BYTE Official Launch


Goldshell Team at MiningDisrupt 2025


**April**


Goldshell successfully completed the launch of the ECHO Series E-AE1M server, a product tailored for customized deployment solutions, flexibly adaptable to the operating environments of professional data center users.


**May**


Intchains Group’s ETH holdings surged by 23.2% month-on-month, with the Group’s capital management strategy undergoing continuous optimization.


**June**


Strengthened marketing campaigns for the AE Series servers, propelling Goldshell’s AE computing servers to a **70% market share** in the sector.


**July**


Intchains Group continued to increase ETH holdings with operating cash flow, ranking among the **top 10 public companies by ETH holdings** globally.


**August**


ICG entered into a strategic partnership with FalconX, enhancing ETH treasury yields through professional capital management and optimizing proprietary fund allocation;


Goldshell launched its 6th Anniversary Celebration, extending sincere gratitude to global users for six years of unwavering support.


**September**


Intchains Group deepened its commitment to the privacy computing track, with Goldshell unveiling the all-new XTM-dedicated computing server, alongside the official launch of XT BOX.


The first batch of XT BOX sold out within **3 days of launch** .


**October**


BYTE XT Card officially launched, enabling seamless algorithm switching for BYTE host users with new sub-cards.


**November**


Intchains Group proposed the acquisition of new business assets to optimize end-to-end business planning and strengthen proprietary capital management capabilities.


The Group completed the acquisition of an on-chain business operation platform during the month, expanding its on-chain service ecosystem.


**December**


Goldshell officially launched its on-chain business operation services; meanwhile, the ICG team was invited to attend the Noble Summit, conducting in-depth engagement with active North American investors.


CFO shared business insights and engaged with investors at the Capital Summit


**Summary**


2025 marked a pivotal year for Intchains Group as we adhered to our dual-track strategic development. We deepened our expertise in the privacy computing track and accelerated commercialization, continuously optimized product line layout, upgraded service systems, and enhanced user experience across the board.


In the new year, ICG will continue to forge ahead steadily in line with our core values, reassuring clients, shareholders and investors with solid operational management, while consistently injecting corporate value and positive influence into the industry’s development.
