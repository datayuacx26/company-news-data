# Chang M. Liu Named to the LA500 for the Sixth Consecutive Year

Chang M. Liu Named to the LA500 for the Sixth Consecutive Year


angela.wan+a


Wed, 05/20/2026 - 10:54


Congratulations President and CEO, **Mr. Chang M. Liu** , for being named in the Los Angeles Business Journal's[LA500 List](https://labusinessjournal.com/la500-2026/banking-finance-2026/la500-2026-chang-liu/) for the sixth consecutive year—a testament to his ongoing leadership and vision in shaping the business community in Los Angeles.


Now in[its 11th year](https://labusinessjournal.com/issues/la500/la500-2026/) , the Los Angeles Business Journal’s 2026 LA500 edition spotlights the leaders driving the region’s progress. This edition recognizes individuals whose vision and influence are helping communities recover from last year’s devastating wildfires while also preparing Los Angeles to host a series of major sporting events. The honorees this year also showcase the depth and diversity of talent and industries that contribute to the Los Angeles County economy.
