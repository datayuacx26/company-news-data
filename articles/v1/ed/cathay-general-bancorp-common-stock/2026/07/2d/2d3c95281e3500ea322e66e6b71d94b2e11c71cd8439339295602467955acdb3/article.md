SBA FAQ


[SBA Loan Programs](https://www.cathaybank.com/business/lending/SBA-loan-programs)


###


Eligible borrowers are generally for-profit businesses operating in the United States that meet SBA size standards and lender requirements.


###


The main SBA loan types include SBA 7(a) loans, SBA 504 loans and SBA Microloans. Each is designed for different business financing needs.


###


SBA loans may be used for working capital, real estate, equipment, business acquisition and certain refinancing needs, depending on the program.


###


The SBA does not set a single minimum credit score for all loans. Lenders review credit as part of the overall application.


###


Loan amounts vary by loan program, financing purpose and borrower qualifications.


###


Requirements vary, but lenders often request tax returns, financial statements, business details and ownership information.


###


Some SBA loans may require collateral or a down payment, depending on the loan type and borrower profile.
