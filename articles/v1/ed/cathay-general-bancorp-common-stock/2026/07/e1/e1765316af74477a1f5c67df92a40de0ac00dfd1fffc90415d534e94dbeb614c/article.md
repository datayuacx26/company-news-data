# Cathay Bank Recognized on TIME’s America’s Best Companies 2026 List

Cathay Bank Recognized on TIME’s America’s Best Companies 2026 List


angela.wan+a


Thu, 07/16/2026 - 19:09


Cathay General Bancorp, the holding company for Cathay Bank, is proud to be one of[TIME’s America’s Best Companies 2026](https://time.com/article/2026/07/09/americas-best-companies-2026/) . This recognition highlights organizations that excel in employee satisfaction, financial performance, and environmental, social, and governance (ESG) practices.


This honor reflects our ongoing commitment to our clients, team members, communities, and stakeholders.


The America’s Best Companies ranking evaluates organizations based on three core dimensions:


- Employee satisfaction
- Financial performance
- Sustainability and ESG transparency


Companies recognized on this list are selected through a rigorous process that includes the analysis of financial data, ESG metrics, and large-scale independent employee surveys involving approximately 217,000 workers nationwide.


### **A Legacy of Purpose Since 1962**


For over 60 years, Cathay Bank has been committed to supporting our clients on their financial journeys. Our mission is centered on helping individuals, families, and businesses build strong financial foundations and unlock opportunities for growth.


Over the decades, we’ve evolved alongside our communities, guided by the belief that banking is a trusted collaboration rooted in understanding, care, and long-term success.


### **Putting People First**


At the core of this recognition is employee satisfaction, an area where Cathay Bank continues to invest and grow. The ranking evaluates not only overall employee sentiment but also factors such as workplace environment, career development, equality, and organizational culture.


We are committed to fostering a workplace where team members feel professionally and personally valued, supported, and empowered.


### **Driving Sustainable and Responsible Growth**


Cathay Bank’s inclusion on this list underscores our commitment to sustainability and responsible business practices. Our ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) performance is assessed through key indicators, including environmental impact, governance standards, and social responsibility initiatives.


We recognize that long-term success relies on our contributions toward a more sustainable and equitable future, and we remain dedicated to continuous improvement in these areas.


### **Looking Ahead**


As we look to the future, Cathay Bank remains focused on empowering those we serve to move forward with confidence. This recognition serves as both a milestone and a motivation to continue raising that bar.
