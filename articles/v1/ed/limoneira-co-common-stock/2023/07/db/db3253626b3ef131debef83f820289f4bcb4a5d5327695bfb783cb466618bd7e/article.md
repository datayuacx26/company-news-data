# Lemon Kissed Green Fish Curry

## Lemon Kissed Green Fish Curry


Here’s a way to liven up your any-day fish recipe roster. Our delectable concoction is inspired by Thai fish curry recipes, with the addition of tangy lemon to the mix. Serve it with rice or noodles for a quick meal that looks like it took much more work.

### Instructions


- Cut the fish fillets into chunks about 2-inches square. Wash and pat dry, placing them into a bowl.


- Sprinkle with salt, 2 minced cloves of garlic, and the zest of 1 lemon, and half of the juice.


- Stir to coat the fish on all sides, massaging gently.


- Set aside for now.


#### Curry Sauce


- Heat the coconut oil in a large skillet, and sauté the shallots for about 3 to minutes, until fragrant.


- Add the remaining 4 cloves minced garlic, and the chopped red chili. Continue to cook for another 3 to 5 minutes, so that the flavors begin to blend.


- Next, add the ginger and turmeric, and cook for another 2 minutes.


- Season with salt to taste.


- Add the curry paste to a skillet, along with the fish sauce and can of coconut milk, with the heat at a medium-low temperature.


- Heat slowly, and stir until it simmers. Stir in the fish stock, and the remaining lemon juice.


- Add the diced carrots, and simmer until tender, or about 5 minutes.


- Cook the fish last. Discard any remaining marinade, and push the pieces of fish down into the sauce.


- Allow it to simmer for about 3 to 5 minutes, until the fish is opaque and flakes easily. Spoon the sauce over the fish as it cooks.


- When it’s done, stir in the chopped cilantro for about minute, and remove from heat. Serve immediately.


### Notes


Note: Choose and firm-fleshed white fish for this recipe, including halibut, sea bass, cod, haddock, and similar fish.
