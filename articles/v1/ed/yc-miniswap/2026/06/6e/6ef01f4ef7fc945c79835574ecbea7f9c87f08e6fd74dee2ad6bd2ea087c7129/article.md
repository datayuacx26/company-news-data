### The fees behind eBay's headline rate


eBay's published fee schedule is only the start. On top of it sits a set of conditional fees — ad placement, performance penalties, international surcharges — that can push eBay's real take past 20% of the order. All of it is documented, but it's spread across so many policy pages that most sellers only discover a fee once it lands on an invoice.


### How eBay's penalty tiers work


The penalties hobby sellers are most likely to hit are the ones tied to disputes. eBay marks an account Below Standard when more than 0.3% of transactions close without seller resolution, or when the defect rate exceeds 2%, both measured across a trailing window. One dispute can put a casual seller below standard for months, adding 6% to every sale in that window.

## Miniswap's Catalog Improves Listing Liquidity


Lower fees matter. But listing liquidity matters too. That's what Miniswap's catalogue is built to solve.


Every listing on Miniswap can be matched to an entry in Miniswap's catalogue of more than 20,000 unique minis. This way, the faction, game system, unit, and kit contents fill themselves in. All that metadata helps buyers find what they want faster, and helps you sell more of your inventory.


On top of this, users can set alerts on specific products. The moment you list a Leviathan Dreadnought, every buyer with an alert on it is notified.


On eBay, you can set alerts on keywords, but not on products. Matched demand is what the catalogue gives you as a seller.

## Frequently asked questions


**How much does Miniswap charge?** Sellers pay a 7% commission on the item price plus standard Stripe processing on the total order (2.9% + $0.30 per transaction for US domestic sales; UK and Canadian rates differ slightly). No listing fees, no insertion fees, no paid placement. Browsing and buying are free. Full details in[Payouts and Fees](https://miniswap.gg/help-center/seller/payouts-and-fees) .


**Is Miniswap better than eBay for Warhammer?** For buying and selling official Warhammer miniatures within the US, UK, or Canada, generally yes. Fees are lower (7% on the item price vs. eBay's roughly 13.6%+ on the full order including shipping and tax), and the structured catalogue means listings are found by faction, unit, and condition rather than keyword luck. eBay remains stronger for sales to buyers outside Miniswap's supported countries, mixed lots, and non-miniature categories. Many sellers use both through Miniswap's eBay Sync.


**Why is Miniswap cheaper than eBay?** Because the model is simpler. Miniswap charges one flat commission on the item price and passes through payment processing. There's no ad auction, no performance penalties, and no fees on shipping or tax. We charge enough to keep the team going and the platform improving, not to pull the maximum out of each sale.


**Is Miniswap legit?** Yes. Every seller's identity is verified, you're only charged when the seller ships your order, and all payments run through Stripe. We're rated 5 stars on Trustpilot, the r/Warhammer40k community vouches for us, and Miniswap is backed by Y Combinator.


**Does Miniswap ship internationally?** Yes. Miniswap now supports buyers and sellers across 34 countries, including the US, UK, Canada, most of the European Union, Australia, and New Zealand. Sellers can ship both domestically and across borders to any supported country they choose, buyers see prices and pay in their own local currency, and cross-border orders are sent Delivered Duties Unpaid, so any import duties are billed by the buyer's carrier on delivery.


**Can I keep selling on eBay too?** Yes. Miniswap's eBay Sync mirrors your eBay listings here automatically, and a sale on either platform updates inventory on both, so you reach a dedicated hobby buyer base with no extra work.


**Can I sell 3D printed minis on Miniswap?** No. Miniswap has a no-3D-print and no-counterfeit policy, so all listings must be official, commercially produced miniatures. eBay or Etsy remain options for printed minis.


**What are eBay's hidden fees?** Beyond the 13.6% final value fee (as of June 2026): Promoted Listings (2–5%+), Below Standard penalty (+6%), INAD penalty (+5%), international fees (1.65%), currency conversion (3%), insertion fees ($0.35/listing after 250/mo), and dispute fees ($20/chargeback). All percentage fees apply to the total buyer payment including shipping and tax.
