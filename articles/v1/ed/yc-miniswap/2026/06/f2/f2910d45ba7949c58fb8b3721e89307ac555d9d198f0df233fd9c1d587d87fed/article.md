### The fee behind Etsy's headline rate


Etsy's base fees are only part of the story. The real cost on the platform is Offsite Ads: a 12–15% surcharge on any sale Etsy attributes to its external advertising. It can more than double what you pay on a given sale.


### How Offsite Ads work


Etsy runs ads for your products on Google, Facebook, Instagram, and Pinterest. If a buyer clicks one of those ads and purchases from you within 30 days, Etsy charges you 15% if your shop has earned under $10,000 in the past year (you can opt out), or 12% if you've earned over $10,000 (you cannot).


Attribution is invisible until after the fact: a buyer can find your listing organically, bookmark it, and come back to purchase, but if they also clicked an Etsy ad in the past 30 days, the sale is attributed and the fee applies.

## Miniswap's Catalog Improves Listing Liquidity


Lower, flatter fees matter. But listing liquidity matters too. That's what Miniswap's catalogue is built to solve.


Every listing on Miniswap can be matched to an entry in Miniswap's catalogue of more than 20,000 unique minis. This way, the faction, game system, unit, and kit contents fill themselves in. All that metadata helps buyers find what they want faster, and helps you sell more of your inventory.


On top of this, users can set alerts on specific products. The moment you list a Leviathan Dreadnought, every buyer with an alert on it is notified.


On Etsy, getting found means writing your own titles and tags and competing with promoted listings in a search engine built for crafts. Matched demand is what the catalogue gives you as a seller.

## Frequently asked questions


**How much does Miniswap charge?** Sellers pay a 7% commission on the item price plus standard Stripe processing on the total order (2.9% + $0.30 per transaction for US domestic sales; UK and Canadian rates differ slightly). No listing fees, no insertion fees, no paid placement. Browsing and buying are free. Full details in[Payouts and Fees](https://miniswap.gg/help-center/seller/payouts-and-fees) .


**Is Miniswap better than Etsy for Warhammer?** For buying and selling official Warhammer miniatures within the US, UK, or Canada, generally yes. Base fees are close, but Etsy's Offsite Ads can more than double the cost of an attributed sale, and Etsy has no faction filters, condition fields, or product-level alerts. Etsy remains the better home for 3D prints, STL files, alternative sculpts, and handmade hobby accessories.


**Why is Miniswap cheaper than Etsy?** On base fees the two platforms are nearly even. The difference is Offsite Ads — a 12–15% surcharge on attributed sales that's mandatory for sellers over $10,000/year — plus listing fees charged again every time an item sells. Miniswap has no ad programs and no listing fees, so the number never changes: 7% plus processing, every time.


**Is Miniswap legit?** Yes. Every seller's identity is verified, you're only charged when the seller ships your order, and all payments run through Stripe. We're rated 5 stars on Trustpilot, and the r/Warhammer40k community vouches for us.


**Does Miniswap ship internationally?** Yes. Miniswap now supports buyers and sellers across 34 countries, including the US, UK, Canada, most of the European Union, Australia, and New Zealand. Sellers can ship both domestically and across borders to any supported country they choose, buyers see prices and pay in their own local currency, and cross-border orders are sent Delivered Duties Unpaid, so any import duties are billed by the buyer's carrier on delivery.


**What about pro painted minis?** Miniswap supports all conditions, including painted models, and gives professional painters a dedicated audience of wargamers rather than a general craft audience. We're also building commission matchmaking to connect painters with buyers directly.


**Can I sell 3D printed minis on Miniswap?** No. Miniswap has a no-3D-print and no-counterfeit policy, so all listings must be official, commercially produced miniatures. Etsy remains the natural home for printed minis and STL files.


**What are Etsy's hidden fees?** Beyond the 6.5% transaction fee and 3% + $0.25 payment processing (as of June 2026): $0.20 listing fees charged on listing and again each time an item sells, and Offsite Ads — 12% for sellers over $10k/year (mandatory, with lifetime enrollment) or 15% for sellers under $10k (optional), capped at $100 per transaction. Etsy Ads, the on-platform pay-per-click program, is a separate cost on top. The transaction fee and Offsite Ads are calculated on item price plus shipping.


**How is Etsy's Offsite Ads program different from Etsy Ads?** Etsy Ads are on-platform ads you choose to buy, priced per click. Offsite Ads are external ads Etsy runs on Google, Facebook, Instagram, and Pinterest — and you pay 12–15% of the sale if a buyer clicks one within 30 days of purchasing. Sellers over $10k/year cannot opt out of Offsite Ads. They are separate programs, and their fees stack.
