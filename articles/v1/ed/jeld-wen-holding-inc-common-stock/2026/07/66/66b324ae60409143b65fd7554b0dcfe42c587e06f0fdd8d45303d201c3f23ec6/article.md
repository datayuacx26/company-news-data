# Elevating quality and beauty in iconic homes showcased on America ByDESIGN™

JELD-WEN was proud to be featured on a recent episode *[America ByDESIGN](https://www.bydesign.global/americabydesign/ambd)*[™](https://www.bydesign.global/americabydesign/ambd) , showcasing how our premium windows and doors help shape two architectural masterpieces: Cleveland’s Ghost House and Fox Residence. These homes exemplify how visionary architecture and premium building solutions come together to create spaces that inspire.


At JELD-WEN, we believe every home should reflect both beauty and performance. Our windows and doors are engineered to deliver lasting durability, energy efficiency and timeless design. In Ghost House, expansive glass panels and clerestory windows frame natural light, while Fox Residence uses elegant door systems to connect indoor and outdoor living seamlessly. These details aren’t just aesthetic; they’re a promise of quality that homeowners can trust.


Our commitment to craftsmanship ensures that architects and builders can bring bold ideas to life without compromise. By partnering with innovators, we help transform challenging spaces into award-winning homes that stand the test of time.
