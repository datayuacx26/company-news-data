# JELD-WEN strengthens sustainability transparency with new EPDs


JELD-WEN


strengthens


sustainability transparency with Environmental Product Declaration (EPD) data to help customers evaluate


the


environmental impact and performance of our


windows


,


doors


and building products.


As builders, architects and homeowners increasingly seek products backed by transparent environmental data, JELD


-


WEN is expanding its portfolio of


third


-


party


verified Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) across both North America and Europe. This global effort reinforces our commitment to delivering credible


sustainability


information


that supports more informed building decisions


for customers


and communities


.


### What an EPD means to our customers


An Environmental Product Declaration


(EPD)


is a standardized, independently verified report that


details a product’s environmental


impacts


across its entire life cycle, from raw material sourcing through


manufacturing


,


use


and end of life.


For builders and


design professionals focused


on LEED, BREEAM, DGNB, and other green building standards, EPDs


provide


a trusted, comparable data source to help evaluate products and select lower-impact solutions.


By expanding our EPD portfolio, JELD


-


WEN gives customers clearer visibility into the sustainability performance of our windows, doors, and related building products. Further, EPDs can help customers


assess


indirect greenhouse gas emissions (


S


cope 3)


.


###
A c


oordinated


global effort


JELD-WEN has released new


[EPDs in North America](https://www.jeld-wen.com/en-us/documents?Document%20Type=Sustainability&query=EPD) for several major product categories, including a range of architectural interior doors and wood window products.


These disclosures


encompass many of JELD-


WEN's most


common door styles and window types,


offering


customers clearer insight into the environmental impacts of the products they use every day


.


We are also continuing


work


to expand EPD coverage to


additional


product types.


The


EPDs


are


third


-


par


ty v


erified by


UL Environment


,


aligned with ISO 14025 standards


,


and provide


transparent data on carbon footprint, material composition


,


and resource use. This expanded set of


disclosures


strengthens JELD


-


WEN’s commitment to consistent, credible sustainability reporting across the region.


Across Europe, JELD


-


WEN has completed new


EPDs aligned with EN 15804


, covering major building envelope products such as timber windows, exterior doors, and related glazed components manufactured throughout our European facilities. As of January 2026, 76 EPDs are available on the


[EPD Hub](https://manage.epdhub.com/) , which are segregated by country of manufacture. In addition, we have contributed to


national


market EPDs and Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) for construction products. These EPDs provide transparent, third-party verified


data that support European green building requirements by detailing each product’s global warming potential, energy use, material impacts, and end-of-life recovery scenarios


.


### Behind the scenes: collaboration across regions


Developing EPDs requires


close


coordination


,


from


collecting


plant-level


data to working with life


cycle assessment experts and ensuring


third-party


verification. Our sustainability team


partners


closely with manufacturing, engineering


,


and quality operations across North America and Europe to ensure


accuracy,


consistency


and credibility in every disclosure.

to ex
