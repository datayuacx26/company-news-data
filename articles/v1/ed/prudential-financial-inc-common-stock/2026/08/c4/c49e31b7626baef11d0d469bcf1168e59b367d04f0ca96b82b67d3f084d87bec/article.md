08/11/2026


NEWARK, N.J.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Prudential Financial, Inc. ([NYSE: PRU](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.prudential.com%2Fhome%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54587302&newsitemid=20260811657182&lan=en-US&anchor=NYSE%3A+PRU&index=1&md5=babba145593deaea40fd08c829dc00c5) ) announced today the declaration of a quarterly dividend of $1.40 per share of Common Stock, payable on September 10, 2026, to shareholders of record at the close of business on August 25, 2026.
