# Meet the Women Behind Cratejoy's Most-Loved Brands

Kristen · Co-Founder


"Baking should feel like a deep breath, not a chore."


## [Bake Eat Love (BEL Box)](https://www.cratejoy.com/products/bakeeatlove)


Monthly Baking Kit


Kristen wanted a baking experience that would teach real technique without culinary school. After trying several and finding none that matched, she and her husband Nick built it themselves. BEL Box was created to share the love of baking from scratch by empowering baking enthusiasts to make beautiful treats and encouraging them to share their joy with others. Each month, everything arrives pre-measured and ready. No guessing, no grocery runs. Just a guided recipe, quality ingredients, and a reason to make your kitchen smell like the best version of home. They shipped the first BEL Box in June 2019, and their family has since grown to include preschoolers Chloe and Valerie, who now help test recipes in the kitchen.
