## Best affordable bras FAQs


Here are quick answers to the questions budget shoppers ask most.


### What makes a bra actually affordable?


Affordability comes down to cost per wear, not the price on the tag. A $20 bra you replace three times a year can cost more over 18 months than one durable bra at triple the price. Count the washes it survives, then divide.


### How do I know if a budget bra will hold up after washing?


Read reviews for durability signals, not first-impression fit alone. Look for repeated mentions of cups warping, fabric shrinking, or wires bending after a few washes. Fewer mechanical parts, like hooks and underwire, mean fewer things that can fail.


### Is a $69 bra worth it compared to a cheap 2-pack?


It depends on how long each one lasts. A $69 SoftForm worn for 12 to 18 months can land below a $20 two-pack replaced every few months on a per-wear basis. If a cheap pair holds its shape through a year of washing, it wins on the math. If it warps by month two, it does not.
