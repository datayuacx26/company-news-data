## Best shapewear for hip dips FAQs


### Does shapewear minimize hip dips?


Yes, but only if the garment applies compression laterally at the outer hip, not just across the front. Shapewear built around front-only tummy panels won't reach the dip zone. Look for garments with side-panel compression and structural boning that defines the waist above the dip.


### Should I wear a brief or a short for hip dips?


It depends on your hemline. Shorts cover the full outer hip-to-thigh curve and work best under midi and maxi dresses, fitted trousers, and bodycon skirts. Briefs deliver the same waist-and-hip engineering but end higher, making them the right choice for above-the-knee skirts and shorter hemlines where a short's leg band would show.


### Why does my shapewear make my hip dips look worse?


Two common reasons. The garment compresses the front without holding the sides, pushing volume laterally into the dip zone. Or the waistband rolls down and bunches at the outer hip, creating an extra ridge right where the dip is. Shapewear with flexible side boning and zoned lateral compression prevents both failure modes.
