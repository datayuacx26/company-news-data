# How Universities Are Using 3D Printing to Shape the Next Generation of Engineers

Preparing the next generation of engineers requires access to the right technologies, expertise, and industry partnerships. At 3D Systems, we work with universities to support hands-on learning, advance research, and equip students with the skills needed to drive innovation in additive manufacturing.


Across universities and schools, 3D printing and additive manufacturing have become essential tools for preparing students for careers in today's fastest-growing industries. From aerospace and consumer technology to automotive, healthcare, and robotics, 3D Systems is helping students gain hands-on experience designing, prototyping, and manufacturing real-world components long before they enter the workforce.
