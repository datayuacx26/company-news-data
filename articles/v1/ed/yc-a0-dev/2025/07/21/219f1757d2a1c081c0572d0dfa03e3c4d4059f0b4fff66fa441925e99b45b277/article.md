# a0.dev - Phase 1 (WAGMA)


7/3/2025


Today we’re rolling out **Phase 1** of a0.dev. Since we first launched a0 at the start of the year, alot has changed. We’ve added countless new features and improvements and now it’s time to formalize things. Phase 1 is the beginning of the journey for a0.dev and our new mantra is **WAGMA** . We Are All Gonna Make Apps.


# So What’s New?


## World’s fastest IOS Build


You can now turn apps made with a0.dev into real apps on your phone in seconds. We created the[world’s fastest build](https://x.com/a0_dev/status/1933949391678918780) process to help users install their apps. Just connect your App Store Developer Account using our[new wizard](https://x.com/a0_dev/status/1937460540642828686) and a0 will do the rest of the hard work for you, like: building the ipa, signing it, hosting it, and giving you an easy QR Code to install it.


## Submit your Apps to the App Store


We didn’t just stop at builds for your phone. Using the same deploy modal that let’s you build for your phone, you can upload builds directly to the app store in a **single click** . We’ll create all of the provisioning and distribution profiles and send your build to App Store Connect for you.


## New Website Design


We’ve been hard at work to improve the look of the a0 website. Since we first released we’ve updated our landing page, dashboard, chat screen, settings screens, and more. We’re pushing improvements to our website everyday to help make your app development experience better.


## Cloning Projects


You can now completely duplicate a project made with a0. Just click the clone icon in our header. This is super helpful for trying to make different variations of an app, saving templates, and more.


## Payments Support


We’re building a custom payments solution into a0 that will help you quickly create subscriptions in app store connect and configure them through the a0 website. We’re super excited about this feature as it will make it even faster to start earning money with apps made with a0. The Beta version of payments support is out now, expect more soon!


## Stripe Integration


We’ve added Stripe integration to help you easily add payments to your apps deployed to the web. Now you can accept credit cards, Apple Pay, and other payment methods without having to deal with complex payment APIs. Just connect your Stripe account and start accepting payments in minutes.


## Agent Mode, Thinking Mode, & Turbo Model


We released a new Agent Mode feature to a0 that greatly increases the capabilities of our system. a0 can now read the file-system, write files better, search for context, read logs and much more. We also released a thinking mode which helps our agent solve harder problems and a Turbo Model for faster edits. Try them out now!


## Convex Integration


Convex is now available to help you build real-time features faster. With Convex, you can easily add live updates, real-time chat, collaborative editing, and other interactive features to your apps without worrying about complex backend infrastructure. More on this soon.


## Github Integration


Connect your Github account to maintain a 2-way sync between an a0 project and a Github repo. This makes it easy to version control your apps, collaborate with team members, and maintain a proper development workflow while building with a0.


# So What’s Next?


This is just the start of our journey with a0.dev. We’re building the world’s best platform for app developers, whether you’re a seasoned engineer or a vibe-coder. Soon we’ll be releasing improvements to our mobile-app to help make the development process even easier and let users make apps on the go!
