# Company Update | March 2024

As we prepare for YC Demo Day this week, we're excited to showcase some of the significant product updates from last month:


‍


##### **Support for Double Precision Floats**


Enhanced accuracy and precision in simulations with support for double precision floats, crucial for high-fidelity aerospace modeling.


‍


##### **Monte Carlo Dashboard**


A dedicated dashboard for managing and analyzing Monte Carlo simulation runs, providing insights into variability and performance metrics.


‍


##### **Data Point Graphs**


**‍** Visualize simulation data with detailed graphs that track key metrics over time, facilitating in-depth analysis and decision-making.


##### **Tabs and Tiles: Organize and Customize Your Workspace**


**‍** Improved workflow efficiency with customizable tabs and tiles, allowing users to organize their workspace according to their preferences and project needs.


‍


##### **Multiple Viewports: See Your Simulation from Different Angles**


**‍** Enhanced perspective and visualization capabilities by enabling multiple viewports, offering simultaneous views of different aspects of the simulation.


‍


##### **Customizable FOV: Adjust the Field of View for Each Viewport**


**‍** Fine-tune visualization parameters with customizable field of view (FOV) settings for each viewport, optimizing the user experience.


‍


##### **Entity Tracking: Monitor Entities and Track Their Rotation**


**‍** Track and monitor entities within simulations, including real-time monitoring of rotation and other dynamic parameters.


‍


##### **Send Commands & Components to the Sim from Python**


**‍** Expanded integration capabilities with Python scripting, allowing users to send commands and dynamically interact with simulations programmatically.


‍


##### **Support for Inspecting Entities: View Live Component Data**


**‍** Real-time inspection of entities to view live component data such as position, velocity, acceleration, and more, facilitating detailed analysis and debugging.
