# Company Update | May 2024

Monthly update! This is a small glimpse of the things we pushed live this month! 😎


‍


##### ‍ **Monte Carlo Replay**


**‍** We added a CLI command for downloading the results of a Monte Carlo run for offline analysis and support for replaying the simulation data of a Monte Carlo sample locally in the editor. This is useful for debugging and analyzing failure modes, as shown in the video investigating a faulty rocket iteration.


‍


##### ‍ **Command Palette**


**‍** With the growing complexity of data and attributes in a given simulation, finding what you’re looking for is now much easier with the use of an IDE-style command palette, with lookup autocomplete and click-enter selection.


‍


##### Self-Service Onboarding


**‍** We’ve completed our self-service onboarding flow with payment integration, including both seat license & usage-based metering.


‍


##### ‍ **Miscellaneous QoL Improvements**


- **‍** 5x performance improvement (in terms of ticks/second).
- Dynamic streaming of graph data for long-running sims.
- Ability to reset the viewport to an entity.
- Better handling of tab switching.
