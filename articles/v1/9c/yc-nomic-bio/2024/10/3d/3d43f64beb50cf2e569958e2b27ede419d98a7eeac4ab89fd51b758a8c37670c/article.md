# True Multiplexing with the nELISA

### In Brief


This white paper examines the limitations of traditional multiplexed ELISA systems, particularly the challenges posed by reagent-driven cross-reactivity (rCR), which compromise assay fidelity. It introduces nELISA as a breakthrough technology that eliminates these limitations, allowing for significantly increased content and throughput in protein quantification, from research to clinical applications.
