# The nELISA: A Massively Multiplexed & Miniaturized Immunoassay Platform for High-Throughput Protein Profiling

The nELISA brings a quantum leap to protein profiling by enabling the scaling of content without the need to compromise on throughput or cost. By enabling scientists to measure more proteins in more samples for less, Nomic is supporting new discoveries and accelerating the development of new drugs and diagnostics.
