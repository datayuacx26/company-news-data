# How Long Does It Take to Send Money to Nigeria from the USA?

Blessing had already told her mother in Ibadan the money was coming. What she hadn’t figured out was how long it would take to send money to Nigeria from the USA when you’re on a lunch break in Houston and the light bill is due tomorrow. She opened her banking app, found the international wire option, and stared at a line that read “processing time: 2 to 5 business days.” Five days. Her mother needed the money by Friday, and it was already Wednesday.


If this sounds familiar, you’re not alone. Every week, thousands of Nigerians in the US ask the same question: **how long does it take to send money to Nigeria, and why can the timing feel so unpredictable?** The honest answer is that it depends entirely on how you send it. Some methods take days. Others take minutes. This article breaks down exactly how long does it take to send money to Nigeria from the USA depending on the method you choose, and how to avoid the wait altogether.


## **How long does it take to send money to Nigeria through a US Bank?**


If you’re wondering how long does it take to send money to Nigeria through a traditional bank wire, the answer is usually 2 to 5 business days. According to[NairaCompare’s 2026 guide to sending money from the USA to Nigeria via bank wire](https://nairacompare.ng/blogs/how-to-send-money-from-the-usa-to-nigeria-via-bank-wire) , a standard SWIFT transfer from a US bank to a Nigerian bank typically takes this long because the payment often passes through one or more correspondent banks before it reaches its final destination. Each step in that chain adds processing time, and if you initiate your transfer late in the week, it may not even begin moving until the following Monday.


This is one of the biggest reasons people search for the best way to send money to Nigeria from the USA without relying on a traditional bank. Bank wires also tend to be the most expensive option, with fees ranging from $25 to $45 per transfer on top of exchange rate markups, according to the same source.


## **How long does it take to send money to Nigeria through remittance apps?**


Dedicated remittance apps generally move much faster than banks. Based on data from[Monito’s comparison of USA-to-Nigeria transfer providers](https://www.monito.com/send-money/united-states/nigeria/usd/ngn) , some providers complete transfers within 24 hours, while others take 2 to 3 working days depending on the platform. Similarly,[Firstcard’s 2026 guide](https://www.firstcard.app/learn/how-to-send-money-to-nigeria) notes that most app-based transfers to Nigerian bank accounts land within minutes to one business day, a significant improvement over traditional banking timelines.


So, if you’re sending money through an app, you can generally expect a much shorter wait than with a traditional bank, often the same day, and sometimes within minutes. But speed is only part of the story. With so many apps now competing on the US-to-Nigeria money transfer corridor, it’s worth looking at more than just delivery time. Fees, exchange rates, convenience, and the other services available to you can all make a difference.


## **What can affect your money transfer time to Nigeria?**


A few factors consistently influence how quickly your money reaches Nigeria, no matter which platform you use:


- **The transfer method.** Bank wires route through correspondent banks, which adds delays. Digital-first platforms that specialize in the US-to-Nigeria corridor tend to have more direct relationships with Nigerian banks, which speeds things up.
- **The time and day you initiate the transfer.** Transfers started after a platform’s daily cutoff, or on a weekend, are often held until the next business day.
- **Verification requirements.** First-time transfers or larger amounts may take longer due to identity verification and compliance checks.
- **Currency conversion.** Converting USD to naira can add processing time depending on the platform’s exchange infrastructure.


So while speed matters, it shouldn’t be the only thing you compare when choosing a way to send money home. This is also why so many people search for zero-fee options and the cheapest ways to send money to Nigeria alongside transfer speed. Fees, exchange rates, and delivery times all affect the overall value of a transfer, and the right platform can help you save both time and money.
