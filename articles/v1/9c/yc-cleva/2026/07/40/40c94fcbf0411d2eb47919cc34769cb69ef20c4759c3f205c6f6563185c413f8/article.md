## What Is a Domiciliary Account?


A **domiciliary account** is a bank account that allows you to receive, hold, and send foreign currencies such as US dollars, British pounds, or euros.


For years, Nigerians relied on a **domiciliary account** to receive international payments from employers, clients, relatives, and business partners abroad.


Unlike a regular naira account, a **domiciliary account** lets you keep funds in foreign currency instead of converting them immediately.


While that can be useful, it isn’t always the most convenient option for people who receive USD frequently.
