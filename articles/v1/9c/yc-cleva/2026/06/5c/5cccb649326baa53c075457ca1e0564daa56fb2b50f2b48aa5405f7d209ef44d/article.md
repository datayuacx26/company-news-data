# How to Receive Payments from Etsy as an International Seller

Etsy has made it possible for millions of creators to turn handmade products, vintage finds, and digital downloads into thriving businesses. But while opening a shop is relatively straightforward, getting paid can feel like the real challenge, especially if you’re selling from a country where payment options are limited.


If you’ve been wondering how Etsy pays international sellers, what payment methods are available, or how to receive your earnings in USD, this guide has you covered.


We’ll explain how it works, what you need before opening a shop, and how[Cleva](http://www.getcleva.com/) can help eligible international sellers receive and manage their earnings more easily.


## **How does Etsy pay sellers?**


Before listing your first product, it’s important to understand how this platform handles payouts.


Every sale you make on Etsy is first processed through Etsy Payments, the platform’s integrated payment system. Buyers can pay using various methods, including credit cards, debit cards, gift cards, PayPal (where available), Apple Pay, and Google Pay.


After deducting applicable fees, taxes, refunds, or advertising charges, Etsy sends your available balance according to your selected payout schedule.


Instead of paying sellers after every sale, it groups eligible earnings and transfers them on scheduled payout dates.


## **Can international sellers receive payments from Etsy?**


Yes, but there’s an important condition.


To receive payments, your country must currently support Etsy payments. If Etsy payments isn’t available where you live, you may not be able to open a new shop or receive payouts through the platform.


That’s why checking your country’s eligibility should be one of the first things you do before investing time in building your store.


If you’re eligible, you’ll also need to provide valid banking information during the shop setup process.


## **What you need before opening an Etsy Shop**


Before you can start selling, prepare the following:


- A valid government-issued ID
- Personal information for identity verification
- A payment card for account verification
- Banking details for receiving payouts
- Tax information where required


Having everything ready speeds up the verification process and helps you launch your Etsy store without unnecessary delays.


## **How to receive payments from Etsy**


Receiving money from this platform becomes much easier once you’ve completed your account setup.


Here’s the typical process:


### 1. Create and verify your shop


Sign up, create your storefront, verify your identity, and complete all required onboarding steps.


### 2. Enable Etsy payments


If Etsy payments is available in your country, activate it during setup.


This allows customers to pay using multiple payment methods while Etsy manages payment processing on your behalf.


### 3. Add your payout details


During setup, Etsy will ask for banking information so it knows where to send your earnings.


Ensure every detail is accurate to avoid payout delays.


### 4. Start selling


Upload your products, optimize your listings, and begin accepting orders. As customers complete purchases, your available balance will appear in your Etsy Payment account.


### 5. Receive your scheduled payouts


Once your payout date arrives, Etsy transfers your eligible earnings to your linked account after deducting applicable fees.
