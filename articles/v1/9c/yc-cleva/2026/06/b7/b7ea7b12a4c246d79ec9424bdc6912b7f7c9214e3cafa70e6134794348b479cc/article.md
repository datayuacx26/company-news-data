## **How to cancel Spotify Premium**


If you ever decide to pause your subscription, here’s how to cancel your premium subscription:


1. Log into your Spotify account.
2. Open Account Settings.
3. Navigate to Your Plan.
4. Select Change Plan.
5. Choose Cancel Premium.
6. Confirm your cancellation.


Your Premium benefits will remain active until the end of your current billing cycle.


You can always subscribe again whenever you’re ready.
