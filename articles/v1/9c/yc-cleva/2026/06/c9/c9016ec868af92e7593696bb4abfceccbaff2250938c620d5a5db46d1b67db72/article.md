# How to Pay for Ads Easily with Your Cleva Classic Card

Sade runs a skincare brand out of Lagos. Last year, she finally saved up enough to run her first proper ads campaign, ₦200,000 set aside, strategy locked in, product photos done. She went to add her GTBank debit card to Meta Ads and got rejected. She tried her Access card. Rejected. She asked a friend to use their card; it worked for three days, then got flagged.


By the time she figured out a workaround, her window for the campaign, tied to a seasonal moment she’d planned months around, had closed. She spent months preparing to run ads and couldn’t actually pay for them.


That story isn’t unusual. For a lot of Nigerian entrepreneurs, small business owners, and marketers, running ads isn’t the hard part. Paying for them is.


Paying for ads in Nigeria has always been an exercise in patience and frustration. But it doesn’t have to be that way anymore.


## Why paying for ads Is harder than it should be


You can have the best creative, the sharpest targeting, and a well-tested funnel, but if your payment method fails, none of that matters. Ad platforms like Meta, Google, TikTok, and LinkedIn don’t pause politely. They cut your campaign immediately, and recovering that momentum costs time, money, and sometimes the entire audience you spent weeks building.


For Nigerians running ads, the payment problem usually shows up in one of four ways:


1. **Local cards get blocked.**


Most Nigerian bank cards aren’t set up for recurring international charges. Even if the first payment goes through, the second or third one might not. Ad platforms flag the inconsistency and pause your ads.


2. **Naira-to-dollar conversion is unpredictable.**


You budget ₦500,000 for a campaign. By the time the charge hits, the exchange rate has shifted, your bank applies its own fees, and suddenly you’re short. Your ads pause again.


3. **One card, too many campaigns.**


If you manage ads for multiple clients or run several products simultaneously, using a single card turns budget tracking into a guessing game. Everything blurs together.


4. **Payment failures kill your data.**


Ad platforms reward consistency. Every time your ads pause due to payment issues, you lose the learning phase data you’ve already paid for. You essentially restart from scratch, and that costs money every single time.


This is exactly why having the right card specifically built for international payments is non-negotiable when you’re running ads.

1. **Set spending alerts.**
