## **What are stablecoins?**


Stablecoins are digital tokens designed to hold a steady value, usually pegged one-to-one with the US dollar. Unlike other digital assets that can swing wildly in price within a single day, a stablecoin is built to stay stable. This explains the name.


That stability makes stablecoins practical for sending money to Nigeria. You don’t have to worry as much about the value changing before it arrives.


Because stablecoins move on digital rails, they avoid many layers involved in traditional bank transfers. This allows them to settle globally in minutes instead of days.


For families needing rent, school fees, or emergency funds, that speed can make all the difference. It’s often why people choose stablecoins for sending money to Nigeria.
