# Wisconsin SNAP online purchases: New EBT setting starts March 1, 2026 | Forage Blog

Starting March 1, 2026, Wisconsin is turning on a new EBT setting for out-of-state and online/app transactions. That could mean a declined purchase unless the cardholder opts in to out-of-state transactions first.


## **1. Here’s what to know**


-


Wisconsin is turning on a new **opt-in setting** for certain EBT transactions


-


This change starts **Sunday, March 1, 2026**


-


**Online** and some **out-of-state** SNAP purchases will be blocked unless you opt in


-


If a transaction is blocked, the merchant will see **“62 – Restricted Card”** error


## **2. Here’s what’s not changing**


-


Purchases made **in Wisconsin** are not the focus of this change


-


Purchases in Wisconsin’s bordering states are still enabled:


-


Minnesota


-


Illinois


-


Michigan


-


Iowa


-


A declined transaction does not automatically mean there’s an issue with your balance or card


## **3. If you use your EBT card online or out-of-state, here’s what you’ll need to do**


**If you plan to shop with EBT online or outside Wisconsin, you’ll want to opt in to out-of-state transactions using one of the following methods:**


### Option 1 (easiest): Un-block Online and Out of State purchases in the ConnectEBT mobile app or website


1.


**Download the ebtEDGE Mobile App** (for[iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ebtedge/id1398046228) , for[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fis.ebtedge&hl=en_US) ) or **visit**[ebtEDGE.com](http://ebtedge.com/)


2.


**Log in** using your User ID and Password


1.


Don’t have an account yet? Tap “Register Here” follow the steps to create an account


3.


Once logged in, **add your EBT/QUEST card** and accompanying information


4.


From the home screen, **tap your card**


5.


Under “ **Account Services** ”, tap “ **See More** ”


6.


Tap “ **Protect My Account** ”


7.


Under “ **Block Internet Transactions** ” and “ **Block Out of State Transactions** ”, ensure both are switched “ **OFF** ”


8.


Tap “ **SAVE** ”


*Note: If you’d like to increase security on your card, consider tapping “Freeze Card” between planned transactions (in-store or online).*


### Option 2: Unlock by phone


1.


**Call QUEST Card Services** at 877-415-5164


2.


Select your **language**


3.


**Enter your 16-digit QUEST/EBT card number**


4.


Press number for “ **Account Services** ”


5.


Select “ **Protect My Account** ”


6.


Check if “ **Block Internet Transactions** ” and “ **Block Out of State Transactions** ” is enabled


7.


If they are, switch to “ **OFF** ”


## **Bottom line**


If you use Wisconsin SNAP benefits for online grocery purchases, it’s a good idea to update your card settings before March 1. If you need to opt in, doing it ahead of time could help you avoid any issues at checkout.
