# Colorado SNAP Soda Ban Paused Indefinitely — What to Know

***Last updated: April 17, 2026***


**If you use SNAP in Colorado, nothing has changed about what you can buy.** The state's proposed ban on buying soda and sugary drinks with EBT has been indefinitely paused. There is no new start date.


## **What happened**


Colorado pulled the proposed waiver from the State Board of Human Services' April 3rd agenda after two public hearings and a pending lawsuit. **The original April 30, 2026 start date is off the table.**


## **What to do right now**


Nothing. You can keep using your EBT card exactly the way you have been. No items have been removed from SNAP eligibility in Colorado.


## **Could this come back?**


Possibly. We're watching this closely and will update this post if anything changes.


For background on what was originally proposed, see our[earlier post on Colorado's SNAP soda rules](https://www.joinforage.com/blog/new-snap-rules-for-soft-drinks-in-colorado-starting-march-1-2026) .
