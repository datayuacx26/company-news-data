# Increase checkout conversion with Integrated Balance Check

When low-income shoppers aren't sure how much money they have on their EBT cards, they hesitate. And that uncertainty is costing many retailers money.


Without knowing how much EBT they have, customers under-fill their carts. They may switch to another app or a competitor’s website to check their balances. They may even abandon checkout altogether. And, all too often, they don’t return.


That’s why we built **Integrated Balance Check** . It’s a new feature that helps cost-conscious shoppers fill their carts and check out with confidence by enabling them to securely check their EBT balance at any point in their shopping journey.


## Secure by design


When shoppers know exactly how much they can spend, they buy with confidence—often using their entire available balance.


Retailers who help them check their balance at the right moments see immediate increases in three of the metrics that matter most. Our partners have seen up to:


-


Higher AOV: ⬆️9%


-


Improved conversion: ⬆️9%


-


Increased order frequency: ⬆️37%


Unlike legacy solutions, Integrated Balance Check is secure by design. Only authenticated cardholders can check a balance. The shopper verifies with their own card number and PIN, and Forage works with you to keep every check gated behind that authentication.


## How it works


Because it offers a better customer experience, Integrated Balance Check is quickly becoming table stakes for enterprise retailers. Here's how it works.


1.


**Embed Integrated Balance Check** in your app or website (e.g., homepage, product page, cart page, checkout flow).


2.


**While shopping,** your customer enters their EBT card number and PIN.


3.


**Their current balance** is displayed in a secure, Forage-hosted flow.


4.


**Your customer keeps shopping.** No app-switching. No guesswork.


## Launch in as little as one week


Integrated Balance Check is available now, with two ways to launch:
