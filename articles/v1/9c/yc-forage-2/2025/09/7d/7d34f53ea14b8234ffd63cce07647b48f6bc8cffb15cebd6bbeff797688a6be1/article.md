# Forage Helps Retailers Navigate New SNAP Compliance Rules | Forage News

## ***With new state waivers restricting soda, candy, and energy drinks from SNAP purchases, Forage provides retailers with item-level eligibility support to stay compliant by January 2026.***


**SAN FRANCISCO – September 29, 2025 /**[PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/forage-steps-up-to-help-retailers-navigate-new-snap-item-eligibility-requirements-302568596.html) **/** –[Forage](https://www.joinforage.com/) , the mission-driven fintech powering EBT SNAP payments for leading grocers and delivery platforms, today announced a new commitment to help its partners successfully navigate upcoming SNAP program changes.


Beginning as soon as January 1, 2026, twelve states – Arkansas, Colorado, Florida, Idaho, Indiana, Iowa, Louisiana, Nebraska, Oklahoma, Texas, Utah, and West Virginia – will enforce new rules under USDA-approved waivers that restrict SNAP purchases of items such as soda, candy, energy drinks, and other sugar-sweetened products. While the specific definitions vary by state, the goal is consistent: to encourage healthier SNAP purchasing patterns as part of the U.S. Department of Health & Human Services[Make America Healthy Again](https://www.hhs.gov/maha/index.html) initiative.


Forage is working closely with grocery retailers and platforms to ensure compliance across states by providing item-level eligibility classifications tailored to each state’s waiver requirements. This support helps retailers avoid classification errors, audits, or potential penalties for allowing purchases of soon-ineligible SNAP items, while preserving a seamless checkout experience for shoppers.


> *“Our mission has always been to make government benefits easier to use,” s* aid Ofek Lavian, co-founder and CEO of Forage. *“With these new waivers, our partners are facing unprecedented complexity. Forage is uniquely positioned to simplify the path forward and give retailers the confidence to continue serving families with dignity and convenience.”*


> *“Every retailer we work with has questions about how these rules will be implemented,”* added Klay Fennell, Head of Government & Regulatory Affairs at Forage. *“By combining our technical expertise with deep policy insight, Forage is helping retailers turn compliance from a looming risk into a competitive advantage.”*


The announcement comes ahead of[GroceryShop 2025](https://groceryshop.com/) in Las Vegas, where Forage leaders will meet with grocers, delivery platforms, CPG brands, and retail technology providers to discuss how the company’s unique expertise is helping partners navigate SNAP’s evolving compliance landscape.


With less than four months until the first deadline, Forage is committed to supporting its partners through this transition by ensuring compliance, safeguarding program integrity, and helping the 42 million Americans who rely on the SNAP program maintain access to essential food benefits.
