# Connecting Chicago's future leaders to careers in rail

Future leaders recently got a firsthand look at careers in transportation when Norfolk Southern welcomed participants from the[Always Giving Back Foundation's](https://agbfd.org/) 2026 Summer Business Cohort to Calumet Yard.


Throughout the day, students from across Chicago connected with NS employees, explored career opportunities in the railroad industry, and learned how rail helps connect communities and strengthen the economy.


**A day of learning and leadership**


Founded to help break cycles of poverty and increase economic mobility, the Always Giving Back Foundation provides mentorship, educational opportunities, and professional experiences that empower young people to become future community leaders.


During the visit, NS employees shared their career journeys and perspectives, helping participants envision future careers in transportation and beyond. The visit also featured:


- Presentations from Summer Business Cohort participants


- Career development and networking opportunities


- A guided tour of Calumet Yard


**In our words**


“Supporting education, workforce development and economic opportunity is at the heart of our community impact efforts. Through our partnership with the Always Giving Back Foundation, students can build new skills, explore career possibilities, and connect directly with Norfolk Southern employees. Together, we're helping create pathways that empower the next generation to reach their full potential.” – Kristin Wong, Director Norfolk Southern Foundation & Community Impact


**Investing in stronger communities**


Through partnerships like the Always Giving Back Foundation, we help students explore career opportunities, build professional skills, and prepare for future success while strengthening the communities we serve.
