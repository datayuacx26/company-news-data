# N-able Honored with Three Stevie Awards at the 2026 American Business Awards®

*Company recognized for cybersecurity innovation, AI solutions, and commitment to people-first culture*


**Burlington, Massachusetts – June 10, 2026** –[N‑able](https://www.n-able.com/) , Inc. (NYSE: NABL), a global cybersecurity company delivering business resilience, announced it has been recognized with three Stevie® awards at the American Business Awards®.


N‑able earned the following honors:


- Gold Stevie Award for Cybersecurity Solution
- Bronze Stevie Award for Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning Solution
- Bronze Stevie Award for Achievement in Human Resources


The Gold award for Cybersecurity Solution recognizes N‑able’s continued innovation to deliver advanced, scalable security solutions that help IT service providers protect their customers against evolving threats while improving operational efficiency.


N‑able’s Bronze award for Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning Solution highlights its investment in AI-powered technologies across its platform, enabling smarter automation, faster threat detection, and improved decision-making for IT professionals.


The Bronze award for Achievement in Human Resources reflects how N‑able builds and scales a strong global culture that puts people at the center. Through global programs and initiatives, the company fuels employee engagement, supports career development, and a delivers a values-driven N-ablite experience.


“I’m incredibly proud of our teams for earning this recognition across cybersecurity, AI innovation, and our workplace culture,” said John Pagliuca, CEO at N‑able. “These awards reflect the strength of our strategy and the dedication of our employees to deliver meaningful outcomes. From advancing business resiliency with intelligent solutions to fostering an environment where our people can thrive, this recognition reinforces our commitment to driving long-term success.”


The Stevie American Business Awards are among the premier business awards programs in the United States. Each year, thousands of nominations from organizations across industries are evaluated by business professionals worldwide.


Details about The American Business Awards and the list of 2026 Stevie winners are available at[www.StevieAwards.com/ABA](http://www.stevieawards.com/ABA) .
