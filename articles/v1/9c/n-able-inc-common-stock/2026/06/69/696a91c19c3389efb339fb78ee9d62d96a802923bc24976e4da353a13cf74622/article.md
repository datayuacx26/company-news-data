# N-able Announces Empower 2027 and Launches Global “Empower on the Move” Series

*Flagship conference to take place in Scottsdale, Arizona, with new regional events bringing cybersecurity insights and peer collaboration directly to customers worldwide*


**


**BURLINGTON, Massachusetts – June 2, 2026** —[N‑able](https://www.n-able.com/) , Inc. (NYSE: NABL), a global cybersecurity company delivering business resilience, today announced plans for Empower 2027, its flagship customer conference, alongside the launch of its Empower on the Move global event series. ****


Empower 2027 will take place September 20-22, 2027, in Scottsdale, Arizona, bringing together managed service providers (MSPs), IT professionals, value-added resellers (VARs), distributors, and industry experts from around the world. The conference will feature keynote presentations, technical breakout sessions, certifications, networking opportunities, and strategic discussions centered on key industry priorities including cybersecurity, automation, AI, and business growth.


Building on the momentum of Empower 2026 in Fort Lauderdale, which welcomed more than 600 attendees, N‑able is launching its regional Empower on the Move events to help make the Empower experience more accessible to customers and partners globally. The first several events in the series will take place in the following cities, with additional locations expected to be announced later this year:


- Manchester, U.K. — June 22–23, 2026
- Frankfurt, Germany — June 24–25, 2026
- Johannesburg, South Africa — July 21, 2026
- Melbourne, Australia —September 1, 2026


The new Empower on the Move series extends the core Empower experience into key markets, enabling more customers to participate. Unlike traditional roadshow formats, the regional events will feature executive participation, including select appearances from Mike Adler, Chief Technology and Product Officer, hands-on technical training led by N‑able’s “Head Nerds,” localized programming, and opportunities for attendees to connect directly with peers and N‑able leadership.


“Empower has always been about creating meaningful opportunities for our customers to learn from industry experts, collaborate with peers, and gain practical strategies they can immediately apply to their businesses,” said Vikram Ramesh, Chief Marketing Officer at N‑able. “We consistently hear that learning directly from one another is the most valuable part of the experience. We’re continuing to evolve and expand that experience to meet customers where they are, while delivering the high-impact education, cybersecurity expertise, and business insights our community values most.”


“Empower consistently delivers real-world insights we can put into practice right away and that is why we choose to return year after year,” said Ted Clouser, CEO at PCA Technology Solutions. “The combination of technical depth, peer collaboration, and direct access to N‑able leadership makes it one of the most valuable events we attend each year.”


Registration for upcoming Empower events is now open. For more information and event updates, visit:[https://www.n-able.com/empower](https://www.n-able.com/empower) .
