# When exploits move in hours, patching must move faster

## Introducing AI-accelerated vulnerability and patch management


Today the time to exploit known vulnerabilities has shrunk from weeks to[within hours to days of disclosure for about 40% of exploits](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) . The workflows most teams rely on were built for a slower cycle and a smaller surface, and that gap is where exposure now lives.


- To help close that gap, the latest updates to N‑central and N‑sight bring three important capabilities to public preview.
- Vulnerability and Patch Management now operate as one workflow, to speed up patching operations.
- Third-party patch coverage extends to over 560 applications and growing, addressing a common entry point for bad actors.
- N‑zo Patch Expert answers patching questions in plain language, helping techs make decision faster, and more accurately.


Each one closes part of the gap on its own, and together they help protect endpoints and shrink both the attack surface and time window bad actors count on.


## Vulnerability and patch, in one motion


Identify, prioritize, remediate. Until now, the first two lived inside Vulnerability Management and the third lived inside Patch Management. The latest update brings all three into a single workflow, so the same technician who sees a critical CVE on a device can deploy the patch and verify the fix without leaving the screen.


The reason this matters goes beyond saved clicks. When detection and action share the workflow, the time between knowing a risk and closing it collapses.


## The coverage map, redrawn


The new third-party patch engine extends that coverage to over 560 applications across Windows, macOS, and Linux, with hundreds now newly available on Windows including Microsoft Office, Adobe Reader, Zoom, and the browsers and business apps your team works with most. The new engine runs alongside your current policies and automation In practice, that means business applications that once sat on the edge of your patching program now sit inside it.


To see the growing list of patchable applications visit the[N‑able supported third-party patches page](https://documentation.n-able.com/PME/supported-third-party-patch.htm) .


## N‑zo Patch Expert, on your team’s questions


N‑zo Vulnerability Expert is already in your environment, helping your team identify and prioritize risk. N‑zo Patch Expert builds on that foundation. It analyzes patching data and guides remediation in natural language, across Linux, macOS, and Windows where the new engine is enabled.


Ask which assets carry the most missing critical patches. See which environments have the highest share of non-compliant devices. Understand why a specific group of devices keeps failing installations, and what the pattern looks like. The answers arrive in plain language, grounded in live data: what to patch, why, and how to resolve failures.


## Taken together


Each of the three is a meaningful addition.


- The unified workflow allows techs to close critical gaps and address active threats faster.
- The application coverage map extends what can be protected.
- N‑zo Patch Expert turns the information in your live data into answers your team can act on.


With these updates, the path from identifying risk to reducing it becomes smoother and more efficient.
