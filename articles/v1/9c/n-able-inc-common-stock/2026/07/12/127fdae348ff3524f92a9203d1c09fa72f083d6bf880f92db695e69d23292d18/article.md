# Cove Data Protection has been named a Champion in Omdia Global BDR Matrix for the third consecutive year

We’re proud to share Cove Data Protection™ has been named a Champion in the 2026 Omdia Global Managed Backup and Disaster Recovery (BDR) Leadership Matrix for the third consecutive year. We feel this recognition reflects our continued focus on delivering resilient, security-first data protection, supported by ongoing innovation including expanded anomaly detection capabilities and Disaster Recovery as a Service (DRaaS).


For an independent analyst firm to rank you among the top performers in a competitive market once is noteworthy. Three years in a row is a different kind of statement. It means the bar kept rising, and Cove Data Protection kept clearing it.


Omdia, one of the world’s leading technology research and analyst firms, publishes its Global Managed BDR Leadership Matrix annually to evaluate vendors delivering backup and disaster recovery technology. In 2026, N‑able secured its Champion status for the third consecutive year, placing it alongside only four other vendors in the top quadrant of a field that included 15 evaluated companies.


This isn’t a participation award. It’s a rigorous, multi-dimensional assessment, and earning it repeatedly signals something durable about how Cove is built, how it performs for partners, and where it’s headed.


## What the Omdia BDR Champion award recognizes


The Omdia Global Managed BDR Leadership Matrix evaluates vendors across three primary inputs: an independent analyst assessment, ecosystem feedback from the partner community, and performance metrics including revenue growth and market share. Vendors are then scored on two axes, leadership and momentum, and placed into one of four quadrants: Champion, Contender, Scaler, or Foundation.


To earn Champion status, a vendor must demonstrate the highest levels of excellence in both channel and technology capability over the prior 12 months, as assessed by Omdia analysts and the partner community alike.


Placement is determined by:


- **Analyst assessment:** Partner vision and leadership, execution of partner strategy, portfolio competitiveness, and channel initiatives
- **Ecosystem feedback:** Partner program and enablement metrics, sales engagement, and the overall partner experience
- **Performance metrics:** Revenue growth and market share relative to peer vendors
