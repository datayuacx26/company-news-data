# N-able Earns Multiple Workplace Awards Highlighting Employee Experience

*Company recognized by Comparably and Great Place to Work*


**Burlington, Massachusetts – April 9, 2026** –[N‑able](https://www.n-able.com/?utm_source=Business+Wire&utm_medium=press+release+&utm_campaign=2025+Q3+Comparably+Awards) , Inc. (NYSE: NABL), a global cybersecurity company delivering business resilience, today announced a series of workplace and people‑focused awards.


Across the first quarter, N‑able was recognized by two organizations using employee sentiment and leadership benchmarks, reflecting the company’s focus on supporting its people as it scales globally. These honors include:


- [Best Company Outlook](https://www.comparably.com/awards/winners/best-company-outlook-2026-large) (4-time winner)
- [Best Company for Women](https://www.comparably.com/awards/winners/best-company-for-women-2026-large) (4 -time winner)
- [Best Company for Diversity](https://www.comparably.com/awards/winners/best-company-for-diversity-2026-large) (3-time winner)
- [Certified™ by Great Place To Work®](https://www.greatplacetowork.com/solutions/certification?utm_campaign=certification&utm_medium=paid&utm_source=bing&utm_content=search.ad&utm_term=great%20place%20to%20work&utm_audience=prospect&utm_campaign=cp.nbau.jsb&utm_medium=paid&utm_source=bing&utm_content=search.ad&utm_term=great%20place%20to%20work&utm_audience=prospect&msclkid=2580c1ca6c27161e9d77a80e61adb513)


“These awards reflect the culture we are building at N‑able, one where people feel supported, heard, and inspired to do their best work,” said Kathleen Pai, Chief People Officer at N‑able. “By investing in leaders who walk the talk, belonging programs and empowering career growth, we create a workplace where individuals can thrive and drive meaningful impact in our industry.”


Earlier this year, N‑able announced receiving several additional accolades from Comparably, NC Tech, Best in Biz, and Women in Tech. To explore career opportunities and learn more about the people-first culture at N‑able, visit the[N‑able Careers page](https://careers.n-able.com/?utm_source=Business+Wire&utm_medium=press+release+&utm_campaign=2025+Q3+Comparably+Awards) .
