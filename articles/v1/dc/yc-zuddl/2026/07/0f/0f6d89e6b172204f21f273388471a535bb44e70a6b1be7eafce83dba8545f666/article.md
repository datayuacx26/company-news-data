# Zuddl Bags 60+ Awards in G2’s Spring 2023 Report Across 8 Categories

**Earlier this year, Zuddl also earned a spot in G2’s 2023 Best Software Awards for Top 50 Marketing and Digital Advertising Products and Top 100 Fastest Growing Software Products.**


‍


Zuddl, the unified SaaS platform for managing events and webinars, has been awarded 60+ badges in the Spring 2023 Grid® Report released by[G2](https://www.g2.com/) , the world’s largest and most trusted software marketplace.


Key highlights from Zuddl’s wins include Leader (Virtual Event Platform and Webinar), Easiest to Use (Webinar, Event Management Platform, Event Registration & Ticketing) and Best Support (Event Management Platform, Event Networking & Matchmaking, Mobile Event Apps) to name a few.


“From 6 awards last summer to ten times as many in Spring 2023, we’re absolutely thrilled to see our growing recognition as a leader in the B2B events and webinar space. We truly look forward to keeping up the momentum!” said Bharath Varma, CEO & co-founder of Zuddl.


> From 6 awards last summer to ten times as many in Spring 2023, we’re absolutely thrilled to see our growing recognition as a leader in the B2B events and webinar space.


These rewards are a result of positive reviews from verified users compared to similar products in these categories. For inclusion in the report, a product must have received 10 or more reviews.


“Rankings on G2 reports are based on data provided to us by real software buyers,” said Sara Rossio, Chief Product Officer at G2.
