# Zuddl Bags 60+ Awards in G2’s Spring 2023 Report Across 8 Categories

Earlier this year, Zuddl also earned a spot in G2's 2023 Best Software Awards for Top 50 Marketing and Digital Advertising Products and Top 100 Fastest Growing Software Products.


SAN FRANCISCO, April 19, 2023 /PRNewswire-PRWeb/ -- Zuddl, the unified SaaS platform for managing events and webinars, has been awarded 60+ badges in the Spring 2023 Grid® Report released by[G2](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=3841304-1&h=713955306&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.g2.com%2F&a=G2) , the world's largest and most trusted software marketplace.


Key highlights from Zuddl's wins include Leader (Virtual Event Platform and Webinar), Easiest to Use (Webinar, Event Management Platform, Event Registration & Ticketing) and Best Support (Event Management Platform, Event Networking & Matchmaking, Mobile Event Apps) to name a few.
