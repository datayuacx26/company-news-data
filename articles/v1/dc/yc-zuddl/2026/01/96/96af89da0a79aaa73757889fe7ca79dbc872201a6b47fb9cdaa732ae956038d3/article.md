# The Zuddl W50 Award Winners [2025 Edition] | Zuddl Blog

On International Women's Day 2025, we kicked off a special campaign to celebrate the remarkable women shaping the future of B2B events.


We[proudly launched](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7303845007298375680) our **first-ever Zuddl W50 Award** to spotlight 50 standout women selected from nominations by event industry peers. Throughout March, we received hundreds of nominations, and based on stringent criteria, we narrowed down the top 50.


At our latest virtual event,[Huddl with Zuddl (watch on demand)](https://www.zuddl.com/huddl-with-zuddl) , we announced these inspiring winners.


This blog post brings you all the details—the winners and their professional backgrounds, LinkedIn profiles, and exactly why they’ve earned this recognition. Feel free to connect, get inspired, and learn from these phenomenal leaders.
