# Field Marketing, Flagship Events, Webinars—Run Them All on One Platform | Zuddl Blog

B2B event marketers wear many hats—and juggle even more tools. One day you're setting up a field dinner in Chicago. Next, you're running a multi-track virtual summit. Then it’s back to webinar mode for a product launch.


Three different formats. Three different goals. And if you're using three different tools? That's a recipe for chaos.


What if you could run **every event format** —field, flagship, webinars, roundtables, partner events, virtual meetups—on a single, unified platform?


Spoiler: you can. And once you do, you’ll never go back.


## Why Format Switching Slows Teams Down


Let’s say you’re using one tool for webinars, another for in-person check-ins, and a third for virtual conferences. Every time you switch formats, your team has to:


- Re-learn a new backend interface
- Create net-new templates and branding assets
- Configure (or rebuild) integrations from scratch
- Pull data from multiple platforms into one spreadsheet


The result? Slower launches, confused teammates, inconsistent attendee experiences, and murky ROI.


That’s not a scalable strategy.


## What B2B Marketers Really Need


In 2025, events don’t live in silos. Your flagship conference influences ABM plays. Your field events feed pipeline for sales. Your webinars warm top-of-funnel leads.


And guess what? Your tech should treat them that way.


Here’s what you actually need:


- A single place to **run, manage, and measure all event formats**
- Consistent branding across every touchpoint
- Shared CRM/MAP integrations to track engagement across the funnel
- Reusable workflows and templates to save time
- Unified dashboards so you can compare performance across formats


When you bring every format into one system, your entire GTM team wins.
