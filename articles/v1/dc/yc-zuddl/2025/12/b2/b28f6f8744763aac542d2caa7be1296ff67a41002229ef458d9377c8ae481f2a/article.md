# Event Personalization at Scale: Beyond First Name Tokens | Zuddl Blog

“Hi {First Name}, thanks for registering!”


Sure, that’s personalization… technically. But if that’s where your strategy ends, you’re missing a huge opportunity. In the B2B world, personalization isn’t just a nice-to-have—it’s what separates “just another webinar” from a memorable, revenue-generating experience.


The challenge? Doing it at scale, across multiple event formats, audiences, and workflows.


Let’s break down what **event personalization for B2B** really looks like in 2025—and how modern platforms like Zuddl are making it easier than ever.


## **Why Personalization Is a Growth Lever (Not Just a Gimmick)**


Here’s what the data says:


- 77% of B2B buyers say they’re more likely to engage with content that feels tailored to them
- Personalized experiences increase the chance of converting MQLs by 80% (according to Demand Gen Report)
- Attendees are 2x more likely to return to an event that remembers their preferences


In short: personalization drives performance. Period.


And in an event context, that means:


- Showing attendees content that matches their job role
- Dynamically curating agendas based on interest
- Customizing emails, sessions, and follow-ups based on behavior


## Where Most Personalization Falls Short


Here’s the reality. Most B2B event personalization looks like this:


- A personalized email header
- A “Welcome, Anshuman!” banner on the event portal
- Maybe a segmented post-event email


It’s a start—but it barely scratches the surface.


Why? Because it’s not contextual. It doesn’t reflect what the attendee did, what they care about, or what outcome they want.


## What Personalization *Actually* Looks Like at Scale


Let’s say you’re hosting a virtual summit with 500 registrants. You should be able to:


- **Offer dynamic session suggestions** based on registration responses (e.g., “Show me product marketing content”)
- **Customize email content** based on persona, region, or stage of the funnel
- **Auto-populate attendee portals** with content relevant to their industry or previous engagement
- **Personalize follow-up sequences** based on session attendance, poll responses, or booth visits
- **Route leads differently** based on intent signals during the event


That’s personalization that drives outcomes—not just feels nice.
