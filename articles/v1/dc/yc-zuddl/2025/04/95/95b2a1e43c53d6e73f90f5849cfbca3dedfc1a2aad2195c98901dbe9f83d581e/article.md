# Audience Engagement: How to Keep Attendees Engaged Before, During, and After the Event | Zuddl Blog

In the world of B2B events, attendee engagement is the golden ticket. It’s the difference between an event that’s quickly forgotten and one that leaves a lasting impact. For expert B2B event marketers, the challenge is not just about drawing a crowd but about keeping that crowd engaged throughout the entire event lifecycle. From the moment an attendee registers long after the event has ended, engagement should be at the forefront of your strategy.


So what are the best practices for maintaining high levels of event engagement before, during, and after your event? Get ready to dive into actionable tips, real-world examples, and some fun insights to make your next event an unforgettable experience.


## **Introduction**


Event engagement is more than just a buzzword; it’s the heartbeat of your event’s success. Engaged attendees are more likely to participate actively, network effectively, and walk away with a positive impression of your event. According to a study, 91% of event professionals believe that increasing engagement is a critical priority for their events.


So, how do you ensure your attendees are not just present but fully engaged? It’s about creating a continuous journey that captivates their interest from start to finish. Let’s break down the strategies into three phases: before, during, and after the event.


## **Before the Event: Building Anticipation and Excitement**


Engagement starts long before the doors open. Creating a buzz and building anticipation can significantly boost participation and set the tone for a dynamic event.


### **Crafting Compelling Pre-Event Content**


1. **Teasers and Announcements** : Use social media, email campaigns, and your event website to share teasers about what’s to come. Highlight keynote speakers, unique sessions, and any special activities planned. Share behind-the-scenes content or sneak peeks to create FOMO (fear of missing out).
2. **Interactive Pre-Event Surveys** : Engage your audience early by sending surveys to gather insights on what they hope to gain from the event. This not only shows that you value their input but also helps you tailor your content to meet their needs.
3. **Personalized Invitations** : Personalization goes a long way. Use your CRM data to send personalized invitations and reminders. Mention previous events they attended or specific sessions that align with their interests.


### **Utilizing Social Media and Online Communities**


1. **Create Event Hashtags** : Develop a unique hashtag for your event and encourage attendees to use it in their posts. This will help build a sense of community and make it easier to track conversations.
2. **Host Pre-Event Webinars** : Webinars are a great way to engage attendees and provide valuable content before the event. Consider hosting Q&A sessions with keynote speakers or panel discussions on trending industry topics.
3. **Online Community Engagement** : Set up a dedicated space on LinkedIn, Facebook, or your event platform where attendees can start networking and discussing event-related topics.


### **Implementing Gamification Elements**


1. **Pre-Event Challenges** : Introduce gamification by setting up challenges or competitions that attendees can participate in before the event. This could be anything from trivia quizzes related to the event’s theme to social media challenges.
2. **Leaderboards and Rewards** : Create leaderboards to display top participants and offer rewards for early engagement. This encourages friendly competition and keeps the excitement levels high.


## **During the Event: Keeping the Momentum Going**


Once the event kicks off, it’s crucial to keep the engagement levels high. This is where the magic happens, and your efforts to engage attendees pay off.


### **Creating Interactive Sessions**


1. **Live Polling and Q&A** : To keep attendees involved, use live polling and Q&A sessions during presentations. Tools like Slido or Mentimeter can facilitate real-time interaction and make sessions more dynamic.
2. **Interactive Workshops** : Move beyond traditional lectures and incorporate hands-on workshops. These sessions allow attendees to apply what they’ve learned and engage with the content on a deeper level.
3. **Panel Discussions with Audience Participation** : Invite attendees to participate in panel discussions by submitting questions or even joining the panel for certain segments.


### **Enhancing Networking Opportunities**


1. **Speed Networking Sessions** : Organize speed networking sessions where attendees can have brief, structured conversations with multiple people in a short period. This is a great way to break the ice and encourage connections.
2. **Networking Lounges** : Create dedicated physical and[virtual spaces for networking](https://www.zuddl.com/platform/attendee-mobile-app#attendee-app-networking) . Use icebreaker activities and conversation starters to help attendees mingle.
3. **Facilitated Group Discussions** : Host facilitated group discussions or roundtables on specific topics. This encourages meaningful interactions and deep dives into subjects of interest.


### **Leveraging Technology for Engagement**


1. **Event Apps** : Invest in a robust event app that offers features like personalized agendas, interactive maps, and real-time updates. According to a study, 88% of event planners use[event apps](https://www.zuddl.com/platform/attendee-mobile-app) to enhance attendee engagement.
2. **Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR)** : Incorporate VR and AR experiences to provide immersive and interactive content. This can be particularly engaging for virtual and hybrid events.
3. **Interactive Exhibitor Booths** :[Encourage exhibitors](https://www.zuddl.com/platform/event-speaker-and-exhibitor-portal) to create interactive booths with live demos, quizzes, and giveaways to attract and engage attendees.


## **After the Event: Sustaining Engagement and Building Loyalty**


The event might be over, but your engagement efforts shouldn’t be. Keeping the conversation going post-event helps reinforce connections and keeps your brand top-of-mind.


### **Post-Event Surveys and Feedback**


1. **Immediate Feedback** : Send out post-event surveys while the experience is still fresh in attendees’ minds. Ask for feedback on sessions, speakers, and overall experience to gather valuable insights.
2. **Incentivize Feedback** : Encourage more responses by offering incentives like discounts on future events or access to exclusive content.


### **Sharing Event Highlights**


1. **Recap Videos and Blogs** : Create engaging recap videos and blog posts highlighting key event moments. Share these on your website, social media, and email newsletters.
2. **On-Demand Content** : Make session recordings available on-demand for attendees to revisit. This is especially valuable for those who couldn’t attend live sessions.
3. **Social Media Highlights** : Use social media to share highlights, attendee testimonials, and memorable moments. Tag attendees and encourage them to share their experiences.


### **Maintaining the Community**


1. **Follow-Up Webinars** : Host follow-up webinars to delve deeper into popular topics discussed during the event. This provides additional value and keeps the conversation going.
2. **Exclusive Access** : Offer attendees exclusive access to future events, content, or networking opportunities. This helps build a sense of loyalty and community.
3. **Continued Engagement in Online Communities** : Keep the online communities active with regular posts, discussions, and updates. Encourage attendees to share their experiences and continue networking.

## **TL;DR Summary**


Before the event, engage attendees with compelling content, social media interaction, and gamification. During the event, maintain engagement through interactive sessions, enhanced networking opportunities, and technology. After the event, sustain engagement with feedback surveys, event highlights, and continued community activities. By implementing these best practices, you can ensure your attendees remain engaged and leave with a positive impression of your event.
