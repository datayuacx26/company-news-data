# Zuddl Sizzles This Summer With 65+ Awards in G2's Grid® Report Across 8 Categories

SAN FRANCISCO, June 21, 2023 /PRNewswire-PRWeb/ -- Zuddl, the unified events and webinar platform for B2B companies and conference organizers, has received 65+ badges in the Summer 2023 Grid® Report released by G2, the world's largest and most trusted software marketplace.


Key highlights from Zuddl's wins include:
Leader (Virtual Event Platform, Asia Pacific Event Management Platforms), Easiest Setup (Virtual Event Platform and Webinars), and Best Support (Event Management Platforms, Event Networking & Matchmaking, Mobile Event Apps, Event Registration and Ticketing) to name a few.
