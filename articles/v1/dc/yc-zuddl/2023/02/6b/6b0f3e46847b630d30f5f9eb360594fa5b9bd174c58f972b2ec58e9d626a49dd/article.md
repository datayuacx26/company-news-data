# Zuddl Ranks High in G2's 2023 Best Software Awards

[Zuddl](https://www.zuddl.com/?utm_campaign=global-g2_bestsoftware2023_pr-february-2023&utm_medium=ref&utm_source=&utm_term=prweb&utm_content=pressrelease) , the unified SaaS platform for managing events and webinars, today announced it has been named to[G2’s 2023 Best Software Awards](https://www.g2.com/best) , making it to the top 50 marketing and digital advertising products and the top 100 fastest growing software products. As the world’s largest and most trusted software marketplace,[G2](https://www.g2.com/) is visited by 80 million software buyers each year. Its annual Best Software Awards rank the world’s best software companies and products based on authentic, timely reviews from real users.


This is Zuddl’s first time receiving a G2’s Best Software Award recognition, earning its place this year due to its ability to simplify the event tech stack for B2B marketers and conference organizers by providing a unified solution that streamlines the process of organizing and hosting virtual, in-person, or hybrid events, and webinars.


Bharath Varma, co-founder & CEO of Zuddl, expressed his excitement about the recognition, stating, "We're thrilled to start the year on such a high note with these two awards from G2. This further fuels our passion to empower B2B marketers by enhancing sales and marketing collaboration, lowering the total cost of ownership, and simplifying the event program execution at scale."


“While G2 publishes the Best Software Awards each year, they’re really awards from customers,” said Sara Rossio, Chief Product Officer at G2. “Representing a vote of confidence from real software users, these awards also spotlight those that have risen to the top among thousands of companies. Congratulations to all those named, as buyers will continue to lean on these awards as a trusted source to inform their software decisions in the year ahead.”


> This further fuels our passion to empower B2B marketers by enhancing sales and marketing collaboration, lowering the total cost of ownership, and simplifying the event program execution at scale.


The top 100 companies are ranked based on a combination of[Satisfaction and Market Presence scores](https://research.g2.com/methodology/scoring#satisfaction%20and%20market%20presence%20scores) for each vendor, and the top 100 products are ranked based on reviews across all categories they are a part of. To be recognized as a Best Software Award winner, a software company or product must receive at least 50 approved and published reviews during the 2022 calendar year. Scores reflect only data from reviews submitted during this evaluation period.


To learn more, view[G2’s 2023 Best Software Awards](https://www.g2.com/best) and read more about[G2’s methodology](https://research.g2.com/methodology/scoring#product-lists) .
