## 1. One Page Website Example


A one page website funnels all of your visitor’s attention toward a single goal, like a signup or a purchase, without any extra navigation to cause distraction. This simplicity is its greatest strength. Because everything a user needs is in one place, they are less likely to get lost. In fact, a study found that single page sites can improve ease of navigation, making visitors 60% more likely to return.


These sites also tend to load faster, which is critical since 40% of users will abandon a website if it takes more than three seconds to load.


Visitors form an opinion about your site within just 50 milliseconds, so a clean one pager with a clear message makes a powerful first impression. Many startups use one page examples for pre launch campaigns because they are quick to build and easy to adjust, making them one of the most effective modern website ideas


**Best for:** Startup landing pages, event registrations, app announcements, and personal portfolios where a single, focused message is key.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### What are one page examples?


One page examples are websites that present all their information on a single, scrolling page. Instead of using a navigation bar to jump between pages like “About” or “Contact,” users simply scroll down through different sections on the homepage.


They are great for focused campaigns and clear storytelling.


### Are one page websites good for SEO?


They can be. While it can be more challenging to rank for a wide variety of keywords with a single page, they can be highly optimized for a specific niche or keyword.


Their fast load times and mobile friendly nature are also positive signals for search engines.


### When should you use a one page website?


A one page website is ideal when you have a single, clear objective.


They are perfect for event registrations, product launches, portfolios, or any scenario where you want to guide the user through a linear story without distractions.


### What should a one page website include?


A great one page site should have a logical flow.


This typically includes a compelling hero section, a clear explanation of the product or service, social proof like testimonials, and a strong call to action at the end (and often repeated throughout).

Yes. With[WeWeb AI](https://www.weweb.io/product/ai) , you can build sophisticated one page websites with features like animations, forms, and database connections, all without writing code.


### How are single page examples different from landing pages?


The terms are often used interchangeably, but there can be a subtle difference.


A landing page is almost always a single page designed for a specific marketing campaign.


A one page website can be the business’s entire online presence, just consolidated onto a single page.
