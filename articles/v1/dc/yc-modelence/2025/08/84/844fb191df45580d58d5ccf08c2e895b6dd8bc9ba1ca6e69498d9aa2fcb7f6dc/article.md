# Modelence July hackathon: Meet the winning apps

We recently wrapped our very first Modelence Hackathon, and what an event it was! Developers across the globe brought ideas to life - all built with Modelence, our all-in-one TypeScript-first framework for AI-native apps.


## Top 3 winners


Here are the standout developers, their creations, and what they won:


### 1. Smart Repositories


A smart platform to[discover trending GitHub repositories](https://modelence.com/blog/smartrepos) - with personalized, AI-powered insights and recommendations.


- GitHub:[https://github.com/samyakjain-1/SmartRepos](https://github.com/samyakjain-1/SmartRepos)
- Live:[https://smartrepos.modelence.app/](https://smartrepos.modelence.app/)
- Prize: $500


### 2. FinChat


Chat naturally with AI to manage your finances and track expenses effortlessly.


- GitHub:[https://github.com/yashshah1910/FinChat](https://github.com/yashshah1910/FinChat)
- Live:[https://finchat.modelence.app/](https://finchat.modelence.app/)
- Prize: $200


Read more about this project and how it was built from the FinChat author in[this blog post](https://modelence.com/blog/finchat-expense-tracker) .


### 3. Post Generator


An all-in-one AI solution for creating, managing, and saving social media posts.


- GitHub:[https://github.com/mdabdulmazidkhan/PostGen_modelence](https://github.com/mdabdulmazidkhan/PostGen_modelence)
- Live:[https://post-generator.modelence.app/](https://post-generator.modelence.app/)
- Prize: $100
