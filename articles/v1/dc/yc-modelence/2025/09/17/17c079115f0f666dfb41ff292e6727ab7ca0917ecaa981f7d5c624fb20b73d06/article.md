# FinChat: Track your expenses just by chatting (powered by Modelence)

I've always found expense tracking a chore - apps felt more confusing than helpful. That frustration sparked an idea: what if managing money was as easy as chatting with a friend?


That's how FinChat was born - an AI-powered tool that lets you log expenses and track spending through natural conversation. At the[Modelence Hackathon](https://modelence.com/hackathon) , this idea earned me 2nd place - and showed me how powerful the right platform can be.


## How FinChat works


Forget forms and rigid workflows. Just type:


- **"How much have I spent?"** → FinChat replies in the chat with your total, while the dashboard gives a clear breakdown by category
- **"₹500 pizza"** → instantly logged under Food and added to your dashboard


Behind the scenes, it auto-categorizes, timestamps, and gives you real-time breakdowns - all with a modern UI.


Most finance tools demand you fit into their rigid systems, inputting data manually or decoding cryptic reports. FinChat flips that. Tell it "₹500 pizza" or ask "How much have I spent?" and it handles the rest - recording expenses, categorizing them (like Food or Transport), and even offering a breakdown on a sleek dashboard. It's designed for real life, where budgets evolve with every coffee or cab ride.
