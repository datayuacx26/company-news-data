# Meridian Bank Opens Full-Service Branch in Bonita Springs

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
Contact: Christopher J. Annas


484.568.5001
CAnnas@meridianbanker.com


Bonita Springs, Fl. (November 13, 2025) – Meridian Bank is pleased to announce the opening of its new full-service branch in Bonita Springs, Florida. This expansion builds on the success of Meridian’s Loan Production Office, which opened in Bonita Springs in 2022 to serve the growing commercial lending needs of the region.


The new full-service branch, located at 12611 Bonita Beach Road, marks another milestone in Meridian Bank’s continued growth and commitment to Southwest Florida. Customers can now enjoy a complete range of personal and business banking services, from deposit accounts and residential mortgages to commercial lending and treasury management solutions, all backed by personalized service and robust technology.


Meridian’s Southwest Florida business team is led by Wayne Griest, a Naples resident and seasoned banking executive who was formerly the CEO of Continental Bank in Philadelphia. Wayne has assembled a team of veteran Southwest Florida bankers including Lorrie Kopko (SWFL Retail Market Leader), Joel Whittenhall (SVP Commercial Lending) and Dan Klimek (VP Commercial Lending). Together, this group brings broad local expertise and decades of combined banking experience to the community.


“Our expansion in Bonita Springs represents Meridian’s loyalty to customers and commitment to supporting the economic vitality of the communities we serve,” said Chris Annas, CEO of Meridian Bank. “We’ve seen tremendous growth and opportunity since opening our Loan Production Office, and this full-service branch allows us to better serve both individuals and businesses in the region.”


The Bonita Springs team is on track to reach $150 million in loans by year-end, reflecting the strength of Meridian’s relationships and its focus on helping local customers achieve their financial goals.


**About Meridian Bank**
Meridian Bank, the wholly owned subsidiary of Meridian Corporation (Nasdaq: MRBK), serves Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Delaware, Maryland and Florida through a network of bank branches, mortgage and wealth management offices.
