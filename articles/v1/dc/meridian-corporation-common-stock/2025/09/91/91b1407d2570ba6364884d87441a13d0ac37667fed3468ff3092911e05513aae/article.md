# Meridian Bank Named a 2025 Delaware Top Workplace by The News Journal

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
Contact: Christopher J. Annas


484.568.5001
CAnnas@meridianbanker.com


**Malvern, Pa. (September 9, 2025)** – Meridian Bank, the wholly owned subsidiary of Meridian Corporation (Nasdaq: MRBK) has been named a Delaware Top Workplace by The News Journal in its August 24 edition. Ranked first among midsize companies, Meridian is also the highest-ranking financial institution.


The Top Workplaces program recognized Meridian for scoring well above other commercial banks in three key categories: pay (17% higher than benchmark), interdepartmental cooperation (16% higher) and execution (16% higher). Meridian also earned special recognition for ranking in the top 5% of its benchmark in four key culture areas: supportive managers, innovation, cross-team cooperation, and work-life balance.


“Meridian is the kind of company where employees always rally around each other, both in good times and in bad. This culture of support comes from the top—I can pick up the phone and talk to the founder of the company, Chris Annas, and he will answer. That has not changed since the day I was hired 13 years ago, and I take pride in the fact that we all truly matter,” said Dan Premo, a Meridian Mortgage Loan Originator who has been the #1 USDA Lender in the state of Delaware since 2015.


Only 57 companies made this year’s list, and


[the full list of winners can be found on The News Journal’s website](https://www.delawareonline.com/picture-gallery/news/2025/08/22/delawares-top-workplaces-honorees-gather-in-wilmington/85773861007/) or in its August 24 issue


*.* The data was gathered through an anonymous survey of more than 215 Meridian Bank employees. Energage LLC partnered with The News Journal to administer the survey, which measured employee experiences, culture drivers and component themes within the organization including alignment, execution and connection.
