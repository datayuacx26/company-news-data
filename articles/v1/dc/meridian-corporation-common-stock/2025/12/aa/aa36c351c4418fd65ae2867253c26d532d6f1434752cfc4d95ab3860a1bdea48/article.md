# Meridian Bank Named a 2025 Baltimore Top Workplace by The Baltimore Sun

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
Contact: Christopher J. Annas


484.568.5001
CAnnas@meridianbanker.com


**Malvern, Pa. (December 12, 2025)** – Meridian Bank, the wholly owned subsidiary of Meridian Corporation (Nasdaq: MRBK) has been named a Baltimore Top Workplace by The Baltimore Sun in its December 7 edition. Ranked ninth among midsize companies, Meridian is also the highest-ranking financial institution.


The Top Workplaces program recognized Meridian for scoring well above other commercial banks in three key categories: pay (17% higher than benchmark), interdepartmental cooperation (16% higher) and execution (16% higher). Meridian also earned special recognition for ranking in the top 5% of its benchmark in four key culture areas: supportive managers, innovation, cross-team cooperation, and work-life balance.


“Coming from another bank, joining the Meridian team has been extremely rewarding. What really sets Meridian apart—and why being named a Top Workplace means so much—is the personal support and sincere appreciation I receive for my contributions. It’s empowering to work for a company that values its people,” said Sunmeet Sawhney, VP, Residential Branch Manager.


Only 38 companies made this year’s list, and[the full list of winners can be found on The Baltimore Sun’s website](https://www.baltimoresun.com/2025/12/03/top-workplaces-rankings-2/) or in its December 7 issue. The data was gathered through an anonymous survey of more than 215 Meridian Bank employees. Energage LLC partnered with The Baltimore Sun to administer the survey, which measured employee experiences, culture drivers and component themes within the organization including alignment, execution and connection.
