# Chris Godshall Heads Meridian C&I Lending

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
Contact: Christopher J. Annas


484.568.5001
CAnnas@meridianbanker.com


**Malvern, Pa.** **(August 28, 2025) –** Meridian Bank announced the promotion of Christopher J. Godshall to Head of Commercial & Industrial Lending. In this new role, Godshall will manage Meridian’s core of experienced business lenders in its three regions across the Delaware Valley, as well as in its new Florida branch.


A 2000 graduate of Penn State, Godshall joined Meridian’s C&I Lending Team in 2004 as one of its original members. Working closely with the veteran bankers who founded that team, he advanced quickly, helping to build the Bank’s presence in the important Montgomery and Bucks counties. In 2018, he was named Regional Vice President, with responsibility for that entire area’s operations.


Meridian Bank President and CEO Chris Annas said, “Chris joined us right at our beginnings and was a great student with some incredible mentors. I’m proud that Merdian has been able to provide such a successful career path for Chris, and even prouder that, through his tireless and diligent work, he is eminently qualified to step right into this important new role.”


A member of the Bucks County Economic Development Council board, Godshall is an avid auto enthusiast. He, with his wife and daughter, resides in Lansdale, PA.


**About Meridian**
Meridian Bank, the wholly owned subsidiary of Meridian Corporation, is an innovative community bank serving Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Delaware, Maryland and Florida. Through more than 20 offices, including bank branches and mortgage locations, Meridian offers a full suite of financial products and services. Meridian specializes in business and industrial lending, retail and commercial real estate lending, electronic payments and wealth management solutions through Meridian Wealth Partners. Meridian also offers a broad menu of high-yield depository products supported by robust online and mobile access.
