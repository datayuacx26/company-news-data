# Geoff Sheehan to Head Meridian Equipment Finance

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
Contact: Christopher J. Annas


484.568.5001
CAnnas@meridianbanker.com


**Malvern, Pa. (June 26, 2025)** – Meridian Bank announced the appointment of Geoff Sheehan to the position of President of its leasing subsidiary, Meridian Equipment Finance® LLC. Previously Meridian’s Senior VP Commercial & Industrial Lending, Sheehan has been tasked with expanding the company’s extensive portfolio of industry relationships.


A 2005 graduate of Penn State University, Sheehan joined Meridian Bank in 2006 shortly after its founding. Under the tutelage of the experienced core of business bankers that formed the bank’s early nucleus, he quickly advanced. As a senior lender, he managed the Bank’s Chester County commercial lending operation before being appointed in 2021 to head Meridian’s entire C&I Lending Team.


Meridian Equipment Finance® LLC, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Meridian Bank, is a general equipment financing company providing commercial equipment financing and proactive solutions to vendors (manufacturers, distributors and equipment resellers), brokers, Meridian Bank customers, portfolio partners and a wide variety of direct end-user customers.


Meridian Bank President and CEO Chris Annas noted, “Geoff will bring the same energy and enthusiasm he exhibited as a commercial lender to growing the leasing arm. Equipment financing is a critical need of most businesses, and his lending experience puts him in perfect position to connect these two important aspects of our customer relationships.”


Sitting on the boards of the Chester County Economic Development Council and the Church Farm School in Exton, Sheehan, with his wife and two children, resides in West Chester, PA.
