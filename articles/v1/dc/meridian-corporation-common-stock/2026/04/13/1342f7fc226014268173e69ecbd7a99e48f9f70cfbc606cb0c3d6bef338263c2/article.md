# Heather Tanigawa Joins Meridian Bank as Senior Vice President, Treasury Management Sales Officer

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
Contact: Christopher J. Annas


484.568.5001
CAnnas@meridianbanker.com


**Bonita Springs, Fl. (April 30, 2026)** – Meridian Bank is pleased to welcome Heather Tanigawa as Senior Vice President, Treasury Management Sales Officer. In her role, Tanigawa will focus on providing leadership within the Treasury Management team, driving revenue growth, and deepening customer relationships across the Southwest Florida region. She will partner closely with relationship managers to deliver tailored treasury solutions and act as a trusted advisor to help commercial customers operate more efficiently and support their growth objectives.


Tanigawa brings 21 years of industry experience to Meridian, having begun her career as a teller at Wachovia before transitioning to a Financial Specialist role at CNL Bank in 2011. She later moved into Treasury Management, where she advanced her expertise following CNL Bank’s acquisition by Valley Bank. Most recently, she served as Vice President, Treasury Sales Officer at Valley Bank, where she spent eight years supporting Commercial Real Estate, Commercial and Industrial, and Private Equity customers, as well as government programs focused on community infrastructure.


“Heather’s strong background in Treasury Management and proven ability to deliver meaningful solutions to customers and commercial partners make her a valuable addition to the team as we grow our presence in Florida,” said Wayne Griest, Executive Vice President, Commercial Lending, Meridian Bank. Griest, formerly the CEO of Continental Bank in the Philadelphia Suburbs, now runs Meridian’s Florida operations.


A graduate of Florida SouthWestern State College, Tanigawa holds an associate’s degree in business administration and management, as well as a Certified Treasury Professional (CTP) designation. She resides in Fort Myers with her two teenage sons and enjoys spending time traveling with her family.
