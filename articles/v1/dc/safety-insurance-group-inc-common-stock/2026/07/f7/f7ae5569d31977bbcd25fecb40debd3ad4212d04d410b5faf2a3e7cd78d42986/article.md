# COVID-19: A Message To Our Policyholders

We’re writing to let you know that paper mail may be delayed due to the impacts that COVID-19 has had on the mail delivery system. This means that paper mail communications you’re expecting from Safety Insurance by mail, including bills and other notifications from us, may take longer than usual to arrive. We’re sorry for any inconvenience this may cause.


## We’re here to help


To help you with this potential issue, here are some tools you can use to conveniently manage your[Safety Insurance account](https://www.safetyinsurance.com/myaccount/) . All of these are available once you enroll in Safety’s “My Account” online, which can be accessed via our website at[SafetyInsurance.com](https://www.safetyinsurance.com/) or through the[Safety Mobile App](https://www.safetyinsurance.com/custsupport/safety_mobile.html) .


- **E-Bill**
Go paperless and receive e-mail notifications when an electronic version of your bill is available, simply by signing into[My Account](https://www.safetyinsurance.com/myaccount/) .
- **E-Customer**
Receive policy information, account communications, and disclosures, electronically. Contact your Safety agent for more information about potential e-customer discounts and to enroll.
- **Sign up for Alerts**
Receive text or email alerts when a bill is ready to view or coming due.
- **Pay Your Bill Online**
It’s the easiest and fastest method of payment - just sign into[My Account](https://www.safetyinsurance.com/myaccount/) or[Safety Mobile](https://www.safetyinsurance.com/custsupport/safety_mobile.html) . If you do not have a My Account, you can also “[Pay as a Guest](https://www.safetyinsurance.com/myaccount/billpay.pl?guest=1) ”.


If you haven’t already,[set up an online account](https://www.safetyinsurance.com/myaccount/register.pl) today to take advantage of these My Account features.


## Have questions?


If you would like to speak with a member of our team, we are ready to assist you at 1-800-951-2100


or you can contact your local independent agent.


In these times of uncertainty, we want our policyholders to know that we are here to help. As we monitor the ongoing developments of the COVID-19 pandemic, please be assured that we are open for business and all departments remain fully functional. Please refer to our[COVID-19 Information Center](https://www.safetyinsurance.com/custsupport/covid19.html) for additional information.
