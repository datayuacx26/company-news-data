# COVID-19: A Message To Our Policyholders

As a result of the changing consumer patterns and needs during the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic, the Safety Personal Auto Relief Credit that was originally provided for April and May has been extended to include June. Any Safety Insurance personal auto policyholder that has a policy in effect as of June 1, 2020 will receive a **15% credit** off their premium for the month of June.


**Relief Credit Details**


- You must have made at least one payment on your policy and maintain continuous coverage to be eligible.
- Credits will be applied automatically in late June and you do not need to take any action.
- Credit will appear on your billing statement and on SafetyInsurance.com under the “My Account” feature.
- If you have paid in full you will receive a refund check 7 to 10 days after the credit has posted.


We recognize that many of our customers continue to face both personal and professional challenges. Our staff remains committed to providing flexibility and understanding regarding payment terms and coverage options.


For more information on the steps we are taking to Help you Manage Life’s Storms, please visit our[COVID-19 Information Center](https://www.safetyinsurance.com/custsupport/covid19.html) . If you have additional questions, please don’t hesitate to contact your Independent Agent or a member of our team via online chat or by calling 800-951-2100.


Our thoughts remain with everyone during these challenging times and we thank you for your business.


Be well,


George Murphy, CEO & President
