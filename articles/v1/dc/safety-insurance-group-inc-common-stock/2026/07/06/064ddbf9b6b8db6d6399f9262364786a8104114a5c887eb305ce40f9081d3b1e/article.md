# Safety Insurance Named DCC Elite® for 2024

**Des Moines, IA – September 5, 2023 –** Today[Deep Customer Connections](http://deepcc.com/) awards 2024 DCC Elite® certification to four property and casualty insurance carriers:[EMC Insurance Companies](https://www.emcins.com/) ,[Mutual of Enumclaw Insurance](https://www.mutualofenumclaw.com/) ,[Safety Insurance](https://www.safetyinsurance.com/) , and[WCF Insurance](https://www.wcf.com/) . The honor represents exceptional performance in, and commitment to, ease of business as experienced by independent agents.


“While 98% of agents continue to rate ease of business with carriers as a critical factor in their placement decisions, last year most property and casualty carriers lost ground in terms of meeting their agents’ expectations, especially in terms of underwriting responsiveness and claims handling,” explains Jason Bogart, CEO of Deep Customer Connections. “DCC Elite certification recognizes carriers that have a strategic commitment to mastering ease-of-business with independent agents.”


DCC Elite certification requires two levels of qualification. First, each carrier must demonstrate mastery in ease of business, verified by Deep Customer Connections using[standardized metrics and competitive benchmarks](https://deepcc.com/dcc-index/) drawn from each carrier’s own agent network. Second, eligible carriers must pledge to implement specific strategies to further improve their performance in ease of business.


“The DCC Elite carriers step up in a profound way to support their distribution network,” says Bogart. “Many carriers claim to make it easy for their agents to write business, but few actively invite anonymous feedback from each of their agents to make sure that they deliver on their promise.


“DCC Elite carriers have proven their dedication to ongoing innovation and improving ease of business when it comes to serving their agents.”
