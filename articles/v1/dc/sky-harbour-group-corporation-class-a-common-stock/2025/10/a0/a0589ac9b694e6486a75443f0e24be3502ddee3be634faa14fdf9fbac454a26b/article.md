# Sky Harbour Announces New Long Beach, California (LGB) Hangar Campus Development

[BACK](https://skyharbour.group/news/)


### 10.22.25


Sky Harbour Group Corporation (NYSE: SKYH, SKYH WS), an airport infrastructure company building the first nationwide network of Home Base Operator (HBO) campuses for business aircraft, announced the execution of a ground lease agreement with the City of Long Beach, California, for the development of a Sky Harbour HBO campus at Long Beach Airport (LGB), serving the Long Beach, Orange County and Greater Los Angeles, regions.
