# BusinessWire: Sky Harbour Announces Closing of $200 Million Tax-Exempt Warehouse Drawdown Committed Bank Facility with J.P. Morgan

[BACK](https://skyharbour.group/news/)


### 9.5.25


Sky Harbour Group Corporation (NYSE: SKYH, SKYH WS) (“SHG” or the “Company”), an aviation infrastructure company building the first nationwide network of Home Base Operator (HBO) campuses for business aircraft, announced the closing of a $200 million tax-exempt warehouse drawdown committed bank facility. The initial borrower is Sky Harbour Capital II, LLC (“SKYH Capital II”), a wholly owned subsidiary of SHG. The lender and administrative agent is JPMorgan Chase Bank (“J.P. Morgan”). The initial tax-exempt note underlying the committed facility (the “JPM Facility”) was issued through the Public Finance Authority (Wisconsin) (“PFA”).
