# Sky Harbour Group Announces an Expansion to its Future New York Stewart International Airport (SWF) Hangar Campus

[BACK](https://skyharbour.group/news/)


### 12.22.25


Sky Harbour Group Corporation (NYSE: SKYH, SKYH WS) (“SHG” or the “Company”) today announced that the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey (PANYNJ) has authorized the execution of a lease amendment to expand Sky Harbour’s development site at New York Stewart International Airport (SWF) from 16 acres to 26 acres. The expansion is expected to increase rentable hangar square footage by approximately 150,000 square feet and create a more efficient site layout.
