# Sky Harbour Announces New Dallas-Fort Worth, Texas (FTW) Hangar Campus Development

[BACK](https://skyharbour.group/news/)


### 12.10.25


Sky Harbour Group Corporation (NYSE: SKYH, SKYH WS) (“SHG” or the “Company”), an airport infrastructure company building the first nationwide network of Home Base Operator (“HBO”) campuses for business aircraft, today announced that the City of Fort Worth has authorized the execution of a ground lease agreement for the development of a Sky Harbour HBO campus at Fort Worth Meacham International Airport (FTW).
