# From 2 Days to 2 Minutes: How Williams Company Streamlined Job Costing With Agave’s Procore - Vista Integration

## Williams: A Cornerstone of Florida Construction


© 2024 Lincoln Barbour


For more than a century, Williams Company has been a cornerstone of Florida’s construction landscape. Founded in 1920, Williams specializes in building schools, multi-family housing, cold storage facilities, retail stores, senior living communities, and even theme parks across the Southeast. Their portfolio includes some of the region’s most landmark projects — from SeaWorld Orlando and Legoland to 35+ years of work with Target public schools across eight districts.


With annual construction volume projected to hit a record $350M in 2025, Williams is entering an unprecedented era of growth. But behind the scenes, their disconnected software was holding them back.


## The Problem: Project Financials Trapped in Silos


As Williams grew to a higher volume of projects, their back-office processes couldn’t keep pace. Job cost data between their Vista ERP and Procore project management software didn’t sync in a way that worked for the business, forcing the accounting team to rely on manual workarounds.


"Before Agave, everything was manual. Data entry would take me between 1 to 2 whole days each month."


Anna Canterini


Project Accountant at Williams Company


She did all of this for dozens of projects across four companies. The process was so painful that Anna only did it monthly, even though she and PMs wanted it done daily.The issues didn’t stop there. With project financials available only monthly, Project Managers were left without the visibility they needed — and the ripple effects spread across the company’s four divisions:


- **Vendors** were set up inconsistently across systems, causing project delays. For instance, PMs had issued Subcontracts from Procore to vendors not yet set up in Vista, causing confusion between Williams’ Operations and Accounting teams, and with external vendors.
- **Sub-jobs** are essential to how Williams buckets un-reimbursables, general conditions & general requirements, and audit-sensitive costs — couldn’t be tracked the way they needed.
- **Job-cost reports** were delayed and at times inaccurate, obscuring visibility of issues for leadership, accounting, and field teams.


## The Turning Point


CFO **Roger Whitty** spotted Agave in the Construction Financial Management Association (CFMA) forum and decided to test us right away.


“When I saw you guys on the CFMA posting board, I thought, ‘All right, let’s see if they can really handle this.’ On that first call, I tried to blow you out of the water with sub-jobs. You were confident you could get past that — and you delivered.”


Roger Whitty


Chief Financial Officer at Williams Company


For Williams, Agave’s potential to support Vista sub-jobs was the deciding factor. One integration provider had tried and failed, and others simply declined the opportunity because it was too complex. Williams was skeptical when we said we could help.


But with Agave, Williams was syncing data in days. In just a couple months, Agave had full support for Vista sub-jobs, which enabled Williams to expand to cover job costs, vendor records, and sub-jobs - all flowing between Procore and Vista automatically. As of publishing, Williams has synced over 35k records spanning dozens of projects with a sync success rate of over 99%.


## The Results: Saving Two Days per Month with Agave


With Agave, Williams’ accounting and project management teams saw an immediate transformation. Project Managers finally have the real-time visibility they need to stay on top of costs, with full support for sub-job cost tracking:


- **Job cost updates** that used to take 2 days of manual work per month now run automatically in minutes.
- **Project Managers** can check real-time cost data daily instead of waiting for month-end reports.
- **Vendors** stay perfectly aligned across Vista and Procore, ensuring billing happens on-time without mistakes.


And for Anna, resolving occasional data-validation issues is simpler and faster. Instead of repeatedly uploading spreadsheets to Procore and Vista to see what errors occur, she simply checks Agave’s automated alerts to see if any issues have happened more than once, and clicks into the Agave dashboard to see suggestions for how to resolve them.
