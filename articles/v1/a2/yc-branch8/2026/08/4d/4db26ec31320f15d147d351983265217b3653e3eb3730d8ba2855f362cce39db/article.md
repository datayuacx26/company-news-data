# Managed Squad vs In-House Team Total Cost 2026: A Full Breakdown

**Quick Answer:** A fully-loaded in-house engineer in Sydney costs AUD $226,000–$254,000 in year one (1.7–2x base salary), while an equivalent managed squad member costs AUD $82,000–$104,000 including management, tooling, and recruitment. Managed squads win on total cost below 15-person team size through year 2.5–3.


---

That comparison ignores roughly 40–60% of the true cost sitting in benefits, tooling, management overhead, ramp time, and attrition. When you model the managed squad vs in-house team total cost for 2026 with all variables included, the picture reverses for most mid-market companies scaling engineering capacity across borders.

## The Fully-Loaded In-House Engineer Costs 1.6–2.2x Stated Salary


Shipturtle's 2025 analysis pegged the real first-year cost of an in-house hire at 1.6 to 2.2 times the stated salary (Shipturtle, 2025). Our own data at Branch8 confirms this range. For a mid-level full-stack engineer in Sydney earning AUD $130,000 base, here's what the fully-loaded number looks like:


### Direct compensation and statutory costs


- Base salary: AUD $130,000
- Superannuation (11.5% in FY2025-26 per ATO): AUD $14,950
- Payroll tax (average 5.45% in NSW per Revenue NSW): AUD $7,085
- Workers' compensation insurance: AUD $1,300–$2,600


### Recruitment and onboarding spend


- Agency recruitment fee (typically 18–22% of base salary per Dover's January 2026 data): AUD $23,400–$28,600
- Internal HR time for sourcing, interviewing, onboarding (estimated 40–60 hours at $85/hr): AUD $3,400–$5,100
- Average time-to-fill for software engineers in Australia: 45–65 days (Seek Labour Market Insights, 2024)


### Tooling, infrastructure, and workspace


- IDE licenses, CI/CD tooling, cloud dev environments (GitHub Enterprise, Jira, Datadog): AUD $4,800–$7,200 per developer per year (Gartner IT Key Metrics, 2024)
- Hardware (MacBook Pro M3, monitors, peripherals): AUD $5,000–$6,500 amortised over 3 years = AUD $1,700–$2,200/year
- Office space per desk in Sydney CBD: AUD $12,000–$18,000/year (JLL Australia Office Market Report Q4 2024)


### Management overhead — the hidden multiplier


- Engineering manager time allocation per direct report: roughly 15–20% of a manager's capacity (Harvard Business Review, "The Optimal Span of Control," 2023)
- At a typical EM salary of AUD $185,000 fully loaded, that's AUD $27,750–$37,000 per engineer managed


### The total


Adding it up for year one: **AUD $226,000–$254,000** per mid-level engineer in Sydney. That's 1.74x–1.95x the stated base salary. Subsequent years drop the recruitment fee, landing at roughly AUD $200,000–$225,000 — still well above the sticker price.


Firestorm Cyber's 2026 analysis found US in-house IT staff averaging $93,324/year per person in direct salary alone, with total costs 30–40% higher once benefits and overhead are included (Firestorm Cyber, 2026). Scale that to Australian and UK markets where salary floors are higher, and the gap widens further.


## Staff Augmentation Looks Cheap Until You Count the Friction


Staff augmentation — hiring individual contractors through a vendor — is the most common first step for companies looking offshore. Typical rates for a mid-level developer in Vietnam run USD $3,000–$4,500/month, or in the Philippines USD $2,500–$4,000/month (Accelerance Global Software Outsourcing Rates, 2025).


But staff augmentation has structural costs that don't appear on the invoice:


- **Your team manages them.** Engineering managers in Sydney or London spend the same 15–20% per augmented contractor. The management overhead doesn't disappear; it often increases due to timezone gaps and communication friction.
- **Attrition is your problem.** Average developer attrition in Vietnam's outsourcing sector runs 18–24% annually (TopDev Vietnam IT Market Report, 2024). Each departure restarts the ramp cycle.
- **Ramp time compounds.** Stack Overflow's 2023 Developer Survey found new hires in unfamiliar codebases take 3–6 months to reach full productivity. Every replacement resets that clock.


For a 5-person augmented squad over 12 months, our modelling shows an effective cost uplift of 25–35% above the contract rate once you factor in management time, ramp losses, and replacement costs. That USD $4,000/month developer effectively costs USD $5,000–$5,400/month.

## Managed Squads Absorb Costs That In-House Teams Externalise to You


A managed squad — the model Branch8 operates — bundles engineering talent with a dedicated tech lead or delivery manager, QA, DevOps support, and operational accountability under a single contract. The provider owns recruitment, retention, tooling, ramp, and management overhead.


Here's the managed squad vs in-house team total cost 2026 comparison for a 5-person cross-functional engineering squad (2 senior devs, 2 mid-level, 1 QA engineer):


### In-house (Sydney-based, year one)


- 5 engineers fully loaded at AUD $226,000–$254,000 average: **AUD $1,130,000–$1,270,000**
- Plus 1 part-time engineering manager allocation: **AUD $92,500–$111,000**
- Recruitment fees (one-time): **AUD $117,000–$143,000**
- **Year-one total: AUD $1,339,500–$1,524,000**


### Staff augmentation (Vietnam or Philippines)


- 5 developers at USD $3,500/month average: USD $210,000/year (approximately AUD $325,000 at current FX)
- Management overhead from your Sydney EM: AUD $92,500–$111,000
- Attrition/replacement cost (modelled at 20% turnover): AUD $32,500
- Productivity loss from ramp cycles: estimated 15% output reduction = AUD $48,750 equivalent
- **Year-one total: approximately AUD $499,000–$517,000**


### Managed squad (Branch8 model, APAC-based)


- Monthly squad fee including tech lead, QA, and delivery management: USD $22,000–$28,000/month
- Annual: USD $264,000–$336,000 (approximately AUD $409,000–$520,000)
- Your management overhead: near zero — the squad's embedded tech lead handles daily coordination
- Recruitment, ramp, attrition, and tooling: included in the fee
- **Year-one total: approximately AUD $409,000–$520,000**


## The Revenue Impact of Ramp Time Gets Ignored in Most Comparisons


Time-to-productivity is the cost line item that never appears on a spreadsheet but frequently determines whether a product ships on schedule. Based on our Branch8 engagements across 2023–2025:


- Average ramp time for an in-house hire joining an existing codebase: **3.2 months** to full productivity
- Average ramp time for a staff-augmented contractor: **2.8 months** (slightly faster due to pre-screening, but offset by integration friction)
- Average ramp time for a managed squad taking over a defined product scope: **4–6 weeks**


The managed squad ramps faster because the provider handles team composition, internal knowledge transfer, and workflow setup before the engagement clock starts. In a 2024 engagement for an ASX-listed fintech client, we stood up a 6-person squad on a Next.js 14 + Node.js microservices stack within 3 weeks from contract signature. The squad shipped the first production feature in week 5. An equivalent in-house hiring process — factoring in the 45–65 day time-to-fill per role — wouldn't have had a complete team assembled by that point, let alone shipping code.

## Three-Year TCO Favours Managed Squads Below 15-Person Team Size


The cost advantage of in-house teams improves over time as recruitment fees amortise and institutional knowledge compounds. Our modelling suggests the break-even point — where a fully-loaded in-house team in a Tier 1 city (Sydney, London, Auckland) matches managed squad TCO — sits at approximately **year 2.5–3** for teams of 5, and **year 1.5–2** for teams of 15+.


Below 15 engineers, the management infrastructure required for an in-house team (engineering managers, HR, procurement of tooling) creates fixed costs that don't scale linearly. A managed squad distributes those fixed costs across the provider's portfolio.


Above 15 engineers, in-house economics improve because you're amortising that management infrastructure across more heads, and institutional knowledge becomes a genuine competitive asset that's harder to replicate externally.


According to Deloitte's 2024 Global Outsourcing Survey, 76% of companies outsourcing technology functions cited cost optimisation as the primary driver, but 57% reported that operational agility — the ability to scale up or down within weeks — delivered more business value than the cost savings alone (Deloitte, 2024).


## Market-Specific Salary Differentials Across APAC


Not all offshore markets are equivalent. From our hiring data across Second Talent and Branch8:


- **Vietnam:** Mid-level full-stack developers command USD $1,800–$3,200/month. Strong in JavaScript/TypeScript ecosystems. Ho Chi Minh City's developer pool has grown 22% year-over-year (TopDev, 2024).
- **Philippines:** USD $1,500–$2,800/month. Excellent English proficiency reduces communication overhead. Higher attrition rates in Manila metro (25%+ per year in some segments) compared to regional hubs like Cebu.
- **Taiwan:** USD $2,800–$4,500/month. Strong hardware-software integration skills. Lower attrition (~12% annually) but smaller available talent pool for web/mobile.
- **Malaysia:** USD $2,000–$3,500/month. Kuala Lumpur offers a mature tech ecosystem with multi-lingual talent (Malay, English, Mandarin).


In Vietnam vs the Philippines, the talent pool differs in specialisation density. Vietnam has deeper bench strength in backend and DevOps (reflecting its engineering university pipeline), while the Philippines excels in full-stack web development and customer-facing applications where English fluency matters.

## What Actually Drives Cost: A Decision Checklist


Before choosing your model, score your situation against these criteria:


### Choose in-house when:


- You're building a team of 15+ engineers in a single product domain
- Your IP sensitivity requires all code to be written by direct employees (regulatory requirement, not just preference)
- You have 3+ months of runway before the team needs to ship
- You already have engineering management capacity with open headcount
- Your retention rate exceeds 85% annually


### Choose staff augmentation when:


- You need 1–3 specialists for a defined engagement (under 6 months)
- Your existing team can absorb management overhead
- The work is well-scoped and doesn't require deep product context


### Choose a managed squad when:


- You need a cross-functional team of 4–12 delivering against product outcomes
- Speed-to-production matters more than long-term headcount building
- You lack local engineering management bandwidth
- You want to test an APAC talent strategy before committing to an entity setup
- Your managed squad vs in-house team total cost 2026 analysis shows you're below the 15-person break-even threshold


The right model isn't ideological — it's mathematical. Run the numbers with every cost line visible, not just the ones that show up on an invoice.
