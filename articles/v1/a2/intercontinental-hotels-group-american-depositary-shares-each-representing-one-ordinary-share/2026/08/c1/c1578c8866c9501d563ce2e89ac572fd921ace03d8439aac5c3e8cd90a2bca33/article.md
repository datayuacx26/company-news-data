# IHG Hotels & Resorts signs world’s first Hotel Indigo branded residences at Mountain View Ras El Hekma


*The Residences at Hotel Indigo Mountain View Ras El Hekma will bring 280 branded residential units to Mountain View’s beachfront masterplan, extending the brand’s neighborhood-inspired hospitality into residential living*


IHG Hotels & Resorts signs world’s first Hotel Indigo branded residences at Mountain View Ras El Hekma[download](https://www.ihgplc.com/~/media/Images/I/Ihg-Plc/images/news/2026/ihg-hotels-resorts-sign-world-first-hotel-indigo-branded-residences-at-mountain-view-ras-el-hekma.jpg)


**Cairo, Egypt – 18 August 2026:** IHG Hotels & Resorts, one of the world's leading hotel companies, has announced the signing of The Residences at Hotel Indigo Mountain View Ras El Hekma in collaboration with Mountain View for Development and Real Estate Investment. The agreement marks the debut of the world's first branded residences under the Hotel Indigo brand. It builds on the signing of the 100-key Hotel Indigo Mountain View Ras El Hekma hotel, further strengthening IHG's Luxury & Lifestyle portfolio and growth in Egypt.


Set within Mountain View’s master-planned beachfront destination on Egypt’s North Coast, The Residences at Hotel Indigo Mountain View Ras El Hekma will comprise 280 branded residential units, including studios and one, two and three-bedroom apartments. The residences will be located alongside Hotel Indigo Mountain View Ras El Hekma, creating an integrated lifestyle proposition with access to hospitality-led services and amenities.


**Haitham Mattar, Managing Director, India, Middle East & Africa, IHG Hotels & Resorts** , said: “The signing of The Residences at Hotel Indigo Mountain View Ras El Hekma is an important milestone for IHG and for Hotel Indigo globally. Launching the world’s first Hotel Indigo branded residences reflects how our brands continue to evolve beyond traditional hospitality to meet changing guest and owner expectations. Together with Mountain View, we are creating a residential experience that combines the individuality and neighborhood character Hotel Indigo is known for with the trusted service and quality of IHG in one of Egypt’s most exciting destinations.”


Egypt remains one of IHG’s priority growth markets in the region, and this signing reinforces the Group’s long-term commitment to expanding its Luxury & Lifestyle portfolio in the country.
Ras El Hekma is emerging as one of Egypt’s most attractive coastal destinations, supported by long-term investment in tourism, infrastructure and mixed-use development. The signing reflects IHG’s strategic approach to branded residences, with a focus on distinctive, brand-led projects in locations where trusted hospitality, considered design and long-term service standards enhance the strengths and appeal of the residential offering.


**Eng. Amr Soliman, Founder and Executive Chairman of Mountain View** , said: “This collaboration with IHG Hotels & Resorts represents a strategic milestone in Mountain View’s journey to develop integrated destinations that redefine coastal living in Egypt. We are not simply delivering residential units; we are creating lifestyle driven communities that offer residents a complete experience, bringing together quality design, an extensive range of services and a genuine connection to the surrounding destination. The launch of The Residences at Hotel Indigo Mountain View Ras El Hekma, the first Hotel Indigo-branded residential development worldwide, reflects our international partners’ confidence in Mountain View’s vision and our ability to deliver innovative real estate products that meet global standards. It also demonstrates our belief in Ras El Hekma’s significant potential to become a vibrant, integrated, year-round coastal destination that brings together residential living, hospitality, entertainment and distinctive lifestyle experiences.”


The development forms part of Mountain View’s Ras El Hekma masterplan, a beachfront community designed to bring together hospitality, residential, retail, leisure and shared amenities. The Residences at Hotel Indigo Mountain View Ras El Hekma will be positioned within this wider destination, offering residents a coastal lifestyle experience shaped by the surrounding neighborhood and supported by the adjacent hotel.


Hotel Indigo is inspired by the neighborhoods it calls home, celebrating the stories, culture, and character of local communities through distinctive design and experiences. The Residences at Hotel Indigo Mountain View Ras El Hekma will extend this philosophy into residential living, with homes and resident experiences designed to reflect the destination’s coastal setting and local character.


The signing reinforces IHG’s ambition to grow its branded residences business through a disciplined, brand-led approach that prioritises long-term quality over volume. As the first Hotel Indigo branded residences globally, The Residences at Hotel Indigo Mountain View Ras El Hekma represents a new expression of the brand, bringing its neighborhood-inspired spirit into a residential setting for the first time.


IHG Hotels & Resorts currently operates 10 hotels in Egypt across four brands: InterContinental, Crowne Plaza, Holiday Inn and Staybridge Suites with a pipeline of 26 hotels scheduled to open in the coming years, underscoring the Group’s long-term commitment to Egypt’s hospitality sector.

## About Mountain View for Development and Real Estate Investment:


Mountain View for Development and Real Estate Investment is a leading Egyptian real estate developer established in 2005 with the purpose of creating integrated urban communities. Guided by its vision of “Developing the Land and Bringing Happiness to Those Around Us” and its mission of “Working Together to Build Communities of Value and Meaning,” the company has, over more than 20 years, developed over 24 distinguished projects across four key destinations: East Cairo, West Cairo, North Coast, and the Red Sea Coast, led by a team of experienced professionals and industry experts.


Mountain View’s ecosystem includes a number of economic and social entities that reflect its holistic vision and support the achievement of its goals, including Mountain View Clubs, which delivers integrated sports, social, and recreational experiences; Mountain View Hospitality, the company’s hospitality arm dedicated to delivering elevated guest experiences; and Mountain View Foundation for Development, its social development arm focused on creating a positive and sustainable impact on individuals and communities. The company has also introduced innovative architectural designs and pioneering concepts within its communities, including The Lighthouse, a community hub designed to enhance quality of life and foster deeper human connections.


In 2024, Mountain View expanded its regional footprint through the launch of Mountain View Saudi Arabia in partnership with leading real estate developers in the Kingdom. This expansion marks a significant milestone in the company’s growth journey, with projects such as One Mountain View and Hayat Mountain View reflecting its commitment to developing innovative, integrated communities that meet evolving market needs and modern lifestyles.
