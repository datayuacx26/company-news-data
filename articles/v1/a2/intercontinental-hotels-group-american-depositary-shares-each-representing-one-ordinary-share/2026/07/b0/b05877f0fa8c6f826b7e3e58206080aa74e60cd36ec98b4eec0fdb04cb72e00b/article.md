# IHG Hotels & Resorts Unveils New Dual-Branded EVEN Hotel and Staybridge Suites Near Universal Orlando Resort


*Premium design meets spacious suites and lively social spaces - built for how modern travelers live, work and unwind*


Exterior mural at EVEN Hotel Orlando Universal Blvd by IHG and Staybridge Suites Orlando Universal Blvd by IHG[download](https://www.ihgplc.com/~/media/Images/I/Ihg-Plc/images/news/2026/ihg-unveils-new-dual-branded-even-hotel-and-staybridge-suites-exterior-mural-image.jpg)


Resort-style outdoor pool at EVEN Hotel Orlando Universal Blvd by IHG and Staybridge Suites Orlando Universal Blvd by IHG[download](https://www.ihgplc.com/~/media/Images/I/Ihg-Plc/images/news/2026/ihg-unveils-new-dual-branded-even-hotel-and-staybridge-suites-pool-mcoun-even-hotels-orlando.jpg)


**ATLANTA (July 1, 2026)** –[EVEN Hotel Orlando Universal Blvd by IHG](https://www.ihg.com/evenhotels/hotels/us/en/orlando/mcoun/hoteldetail) and[Staybridge Suites Orlando Universal Blvd by IHG](https://www.ihg.com/staybridge/hotels/us/en/orlando/mcoub/hoteldetail) announce the opening of their dual-branded property, bringing together two complementary stay experiences in one of the nation’s most sought-after destinations for leisure and business travel. Thoughtfully designed with contemporary finishes, elevated amenities and welcoming spaces to restore and connect, the hotel delivers a vibrant and stunning arrival experience that is as impressive as its prime Orlando location.


Developed in partnership with Daiwa House Group, the hotel marks a significant milestone for both brands and furthers IHG’s continued expansion in one of the most in-demand hubs for travel. This new property places guests within easy reach of Universal Orlando Resort, including the highly anticipated Epic Universe theme park, and the Orange County Convention Center, while serving as a sophisticated getaway just moments from the action.


With a combined 288 guest rooms, the hotel brings together the best of both brands to create a flexible and design-forward stay. Guests can seamlessly move between spaces that are curated to the needs of a modern traveler, without having to make any compromises across the properties – including an expansive 1,500 sq. ft. Athletic Studio, 24-hour marketplace, game room, resort-style outdoor pool, guest laundry facilities, storage units for long-stay travelers, and an inviting courtyard with BBQ grills and pet walking areas.


**Jen Gribble, SVP, Global Marketing, Premium, Essentials and Suites Brands at IHG Hotels & Resorts, said:** "Today’s travelers are looking for stays that feel more flexible, more personalized and more aligned with how they want to live, work and recharge. The opening of **EVEN Hotel and Staybridge Suites Orlando Universal Blvd** brings two powerful brands together in a way that delivers exactly that – giving guests the freedom to choose a stay that fits their needs."


Among the 120 EVEN Hotel guest rooms, restoration and comfort take center stage with premium bedding, cooling linens and spa-like showers designed to promote a truly refreshing stay experience. Guests have access to a flexible range of fresh food and beverage options throughout the day – from morning lattes and on-the-go breakfast, to evening cocktails and dinner at EVEN Kitchen & Bar. After dining, travelers can keep moving at their own pace with daily rituals such as an evening tea service, and natural spaces that relax, soothe, and calm.


At the same time, the Staybridge Suites experience offers all the comforts of home in each of the 168 guest rooms, with spacious studio and one-bedroom suites featuring fully equipped kitchens, separate living spaces and room to spread out in both king and two queen configurations. Mornings begin with a complimentary daily breakfast buffet, while on select evenings, guests are invited to unwind at the Staybridge Suites Social, a relaxed happy hour with light bites, beer and wine. Outdoor gathering spaces with fire pits and grilling areas create a natural place to connect, making it just as easy to feel at home as it is to explore. Staybridge Suites guests can also purchase from a variety of wholesome and indulgent menu items at EVEN Kitchen & Bar.


**Daiwa House Group Project Manager said:** "Orlando is a destination that invites both exploration and relaxation, and this property is designed to support both. With stays designed to support working, unwinding or hosting, guests can experience the city with a level of flexibility and comfort that feels tailored to how they travel."


The property serves a wide range of travelers – from families visiting Orlando’s world-famous theme parks to professionals attending nearby conventions. By bringing residential-style flexibility and restorative-driven comfort together under one roof, it offers a more intuitive way to travel, where guests can move seamlessly between moments of energy and ease.
