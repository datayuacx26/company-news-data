# IHG Hotels & Resorts Expands Its Upper Luxury Presence in Greater China with the signing of Regent Chengdu


**15 July 2026, Shanghai -** IHG Hotels & Resorts has signed an agreement with Newhope Real Estate to introduce the Regent brand to Chengdu, marking the brand’s debut in the city and further solidifying IHG’s luxury footprint across Greater China’s premier urban destinations.


Poised to open in the Q3 of 2029, Regent Chengdu will embody the brand’s signature belief that life is at its best when we rise beyond distractions. Regent hotels and resorts follow the philosophy to elevate guests above the noise, allowing them to present the very best versions of themselves.


Regent Chengdu Signing Ceremony[download](https://www.ihgplc.com/~/media/Images/I/Ihg-Plc/images/news/2026/ihg-expands-its-upper-luxury-presence-in-greater-china.jpg)


**Daniel Aylmer, Chief Executive Officer, Greater China, IHG Hotels & Resorts, said:** *“Chengdu has long been a key market for IHG in Greater China and is also home to our West China regional headquarters. As a gateway to Southwest China, the city brings together rich cultural heritage, a vibrant consumer market and strong potential for luxury hospitality growth. Regent’s debut in Chengdu marks an important milestone for the brand while further strengthening our luxury presence in the region. Together with Newhope Real Estate, we look forward to creating a landmark hotel that captures the distinctive character of Chengdu while delivering Regent’s signature experience. We remain committed to creating meaningful stays for our guests and long-term value for our hotel owners.”*


Situated in the heart of Jinjiang District, Regent Chengdu will rise between Levels 39 and 48 of a 233-metre landmark tower, just moments from Chunxi Road, Tianfu Square, and the city's historic commercial core. The hotel will feature 180 guestrooms and suites, each framing sweeping panoramic views of the city skyline and the meandering Jinjiang River.


**Jiang Mengjun, Chairman and President of Newhope Real Estate, said** : *“We are delighted to partner with IHG on the development of Regent Chengdu. As a company rooted in this city, we are committed to contributing to Chengdu’s continued evolution and creating destinations that connect the city with the world. Located within the D10 Wangjiang development, Regent Chengdu reflects our vision of bringing world-class hospitality to Chengdu while celebrating the city’s distinctive character. Together with IHG, we look forward to creating a destination where guests can experience the city through Regent’s refined approach to modern luxury.”*


For more than half a century, guests have made grand entrances through Regent doors, illustrating its auspicious history with cinematic memories, mythical lore, and Asian influences. Founded 1970 and now, part of IHG Hotels & Resorts’ luxury and lifestyle portfolio, Regent aims to create uplifting experiences to spark fresh perspectives and elevate guests above the noise.


Regent Chengdu (rendering)[download](https://www.ihgplc.com/~/media/Images/I/Ihg-Plc/images/news/2026/ihg-expands-its-upper-luxury-presence-in-greater-china-img-01.jpg)


Regent Chengdu will present meticulously designed space, signature dining destinations, and a dedicated spa sanctuary. Hallmarks of Regent design and hospitality will also feature, including *Personal Havens* offering intimate spaces throughout the hotel that inspire indulgent moments, as well as *Experience Agent* to serve as a single point of contact from pre-stay through to departure and *With Compliments* touches as a matter of course. These moments, will infuse every stay with thoughtful, personalised service whilst inviting guests to discover a rare sense of tranquility high above the city's vibrant pulse.


Since its acquisition in 2018, IHG has embarked on a carefully curated revitalisation of Regent to cement its position in the minds of the UHNWI and the epitome of modern upper luxury. IHG’s reimagining of Regent debuted with Regent Shanghai Pudong in 2020, followed by Regent Chongqing in 2021.


Regent Hotels & Resorts continues to expand its footprint across the world's most coveted urban and resort destinations. There are now 11 Regent hotels and resorts open around the world (3,212 rooms), and 12 properties in the pipeline (2,210 rooms). In Greater China, the brand currently operates six hotels across Shanghai, Hong Kong, Beijing, Chongqing, and Taipei, with several more projects in the pipeline. The signing of Regent Chengdu marks another milestone in this strategic growth, further anchoring the brand's presence across the region's key gateway cities.
