# IHG Hotels & Resorts Expands Thailand Portfolio with Holiday Inn Express Krabi Ao Nang


- *Holiday Inn Express debut in Krabi*
- *New signing deepens partnership with owner Chok Deesuk Co., Ltd.*
- *158-key conversion hotel underscores growing momentum for IHG's conversions in Thailand*


**


[download](https://www.ihgplc.com/~/media/Images/I/Ihg-Plc/images/news/2026/ihg-expands-thailand-portfolio-with-holiday-inn-express-krabi-ao-nang-landing-image.jpg)


**Krabi, Thailand, 23 July 2026** –[IHG Hotels & Resorts](https://www.ihgplc.com/en) , one of the world’s leading hotel companies, today announced the signing of Holiday Inn Express Krabi Ao Nang, a 158-key conversion property in partnership with Chok Deesuk Co., Ltd **.** , further expanding the company’s presence in one of Thailand’s most sought-after leisure destinations.


Following a rebrand, the hotel will join IHG’s globally recognised Holiday Inn Express brand and is set to open by the end of 2026.The signing builds on IHG’s longstanding relationship with the owner, who also owns the nearby Holiday Inn Resort Krabi Ao Nang Beach.


**Pathana Jitsaereetham, Director, Development, Thailand, IHG Hotels & Resorts said** , “We are delighted to strengthen our collaboration with Chok Deesuk Co., Ltd. through this signing. Building on our existing partnership, this latest agreement reflects the confidence owners continue to place in IHG’s brands, commercial expertise and ability to deliver long-term value.


The conversion of this property to Holiday Inn Express reflects the growing appeal of conversions in Thailand, where conversion signings accounted for nearly 40% of signed keys in 2025. This trend reflects owners’ increasing confidence in the value of joining IHG's global enterprise, powerful distribution channels and award-winning loyalty programme.


Ao Nang continues to attract strong demand from both domestic and international travellers seeking beach escapes, outdoor adventures and easy access to some of Thailand’s most iconic island destinations.


We look forward to expanding our presence in Krabi together and bringing the trusted Holiday Inn Express experience to one of Thailand’s leading beach destinations."


Holiday Inn Express Krabi Ao Nang will be the first Holiday Inn Express hotel in Thailand and the second in South East Asia to feature the brand’s latest Generation 5 (Gen 5) design. The Gen 5 design introduces flexible, experiential environments designed to attract and excite today’s travellers, encouraging guests to connect, work and unwind in intuitive social spaces. This next-generation design reinforces the brand’s commitment to delivering smart simplicity with enhanced comfort, connectivity, and functionality.


Located in the heart of Ao Nang, Krabi’s vibrant tourism hub, the 158-key hotel will undergo a comprehensive rebranding programme ahead of its opening later this year. Guests will enjoy convenient access to the destination’s attractions, alongside facilities including an all-day dining restaurant, pool bar, swimming pool and fitness centre.


Strategically located just 1 kilometre from Ao Nang Beach, the hotel enjoys a prime position along one of the destination’s main commercial streets, surrounded by shops, restaurants and entertainment options. The property also offers convenient access to key tourist attractions and is located approximately 40 minutes by car from Krabi International Airport.


Ao Nang serves as the gateway to many of Krabi’s most renowned attractions, including Poda Island, Chicken Island, Hong Island and Railay Beach. The destination has become increasingly popular among leisure travellers, families, couples and adventure seekers, supported by its lively dining scene, outdoor recreation offerings and vibrant nightlife.


**Peeranat Deechaisatetha, Chief Business Development Officer, Chok Deesuk Co., Ltd., said,** “We are pleased to expand our partnership with IHG through the signing of Holiday Inn Express Krabi Ao Nang. Our strong collaboration at Holiday Inn Resort Krabi Ao Nang Beach has reinforced our confidence in IHG’s brands, and its ability to drive results.


The addition of Holiday Inn Express Krabi Ao Nang complements our existing resort offering and allows us to cater to a broader range of travellers visiting the destination. Together, these two properties strengthen our presence in Krabi, while supporting its continued development as a leading tourist spot. We look forward to working closely with IHG to enhance the destination's appeal and deliver high-quality hospitality experiences to travellers from around the world.”


With the addition of Holiday Inn Express Krabi Ao Nang, IHG continues to expand its presence across Thailand, offering guests greater choice across a diverse portfolio of brands and destinations.


IHG currently operates 42 hotels across 11 brands in Thailand, with 39 properties in the pipeline, reinforcing its continued commitment to growth and innovation in the country.
