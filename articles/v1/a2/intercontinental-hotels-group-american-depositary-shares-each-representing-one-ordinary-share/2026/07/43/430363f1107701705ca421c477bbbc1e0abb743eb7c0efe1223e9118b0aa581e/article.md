# IHG Hotels & Resorts Highlights Key Hotel Openings in Greater China in Second Half of 2026


**2 July 2026, Shanghai –** IHG Hotels & Resorts will expand further across Greater China in the second half of 2026, with a series of new hotel openings across major cities, transport hubs, and leisure destinations.


The openings span luxury & lifestyle, premium, and essential brands, reflecting a diversified portfolio designed to meet a wide range of travel needs across the region.


**Daniel Aylmer, Chief Executive Officer, IHG Greater China,** said: “Greater China remains one of the most important and dynamic markets for IHG globally. Our continued development in the region reflects the long-term strength of our multi-brand portfolio and our close partnerships with hotel owners. Each opening contributes to the ongoing evolution of our presence across key markets, as we continue to focus on delivering relevant and consistent experiences for guests across different travel needs.”


The following highlights represent a selection of upcoming openings across key segments and destinations.


**Key Gateway and Urban Hub Openings**


IHG will further strengthen its presence in major gateway cities and transport-linked locations.

The second half of 2026 will also see continued development of IHG’s lifestyle and conversion-focused brands.
