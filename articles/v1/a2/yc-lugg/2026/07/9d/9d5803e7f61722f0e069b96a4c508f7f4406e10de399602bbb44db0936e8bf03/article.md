# Furniture assembly is now part of your store delivery

Getting[furniture delivered](https://lugg.com/furniture-delivery?ref=lugg.ghost.io) just got better, now your Lugg movers can build it too! Lugg, the on-demand delivery service available nationwide, now lets you add assembly to any[store delivery](https://lugg.com/store-delivery?ref=lugg.ghost.io) and the same Luggers who haul your new dresser or bookshelf up the stairs will put it together before they go. One booking, one trip, and nothing left in a box.


Assembly starts at $34 and scales with the truck size you book. You'll see the exact price the moment you toggle it on, same upfront pricing you know and love!


So far, the most common thing people add it to is a bed frame, which is no surprise to anyone who's ever opened one flat-packed across two boxes.


## What's new


For the first time, building your furniture is part of the delivery. Flat-pack pieces go from boxed up to finished, placed in the exact room you want them: the[dresser](https://lugg.com/dresser-delivery?ref=lugg.ghost.io) against the wall, the bookshelf standing, the table on its legs, all in the same visit.


## How to add assembly


You'll see the option while you're booking a store delivery:


1. Start your store delivery by entering your pickup and destination addresses.
2. Choose your truck and time.
3. Toggle on Assembly.
4. We deliver AND build it for you.


That's it. Same booking, same movers, and one less thing for you to figure out on delivery day.


Thought you'd handle it yourself, then life got in the way? You can still add assembly on mid-delivery, even after your Lugg movers have arrived to your destination!


**Before, we just delivered the box to your room. Now, we deliver and build it, all in one Lugg.**


## What Lugg can (and can't) build


If it came in a box or needs tools, your Lugg movers can likely build it. A few things stay off the list for safety reasons.


Lugg can assemble Lugg can't do


Sofas and sectionals TV mounting


Beds and bed frames Plumbing


Dressers and wardrobes Electrical hookups


Bookshelves and shelving Appliance hookups (gas, water, electrical)


Tables and desks —


One heads-up: your Lugg movers come with a standard toolkit. If a piece needs specialty tools or specific instructions, have those on hand so they can put it together right.


## Clearing out the old furniture?


If a new bed frame is replacing an old one, add a stop and your Lugg movers can drop the old one at a[donation center](https://lugg.com/furniture-donation-pickup?ref=lugg.ghost.io) or the[dump](https://lugg.com/junk-pick-up?ref=lugg.ghost.io) on the same trip — without booking anything separate. They won't haul away packaging on their own, but with a junk stop added, the empty boxes can go too.
