# Apollo.io Earns G2, Ramp, and Brex Recognition for GTM Leadership and Growth

**Apollo achieves top rankings across G2 Implementation, Results, Relationship indexes and International grids, as well as Ramp and Brex recognition for outstanding growth and adoption**


SAN FRANCISCO


, May 21, 2026


/PRNewswire/ --[Apollo.io](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4693793-1&h=2979079106&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.apollo.io%2F&a=Apollo.io) , a leading AI-native go-to-market platform, today announced industry recognition for its capabilities and exceptional growth from G2, Ramp and Brex. Collectively, the recent accolades underscore Apollo's business momentum, high customer satisfaction, and increasing user adoption of the company's unified AI-powered go-to-market platform.


The[G2 Spring 2026 Reports](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4693793-1&h=3423797411&u=https%3A%2F%2Fcompany.g2.com%2Fnews%2Fg2-spring-2026-reports&a=G2+Spring+2026+Reports) recognize Apollo for earning a total of 612 badges across 1,167 reports, with #1 rankings in more than 30 mid-market reports spanning implementation, results, relationship indexes, and international grids – more than doubling its mid-market reports from last quarter. Apollo also retained its overall #1 ranking in Lead-to-Account Matching and Routing, and earned five new SMB #1 standings, including first-place rankings in SMB Lead-to-Account Matching and Routing.


The AI-powered GTM leader was also recognized by Ramp as the[fastest growing sales data provider](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4693793-1&h=3346285097&u=https%3A%2F%2Framp.com%2Fvendors%2Fcategories%2Fsales-data-providers&a=fastest+growing+sales+data+provider) in May 2026, besting competitors such as LinkedIn Sales Navigator and Clay; and one of the[fastest growing software vendors overall](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4693793-1&h=286310496&u=https%3A%2F%2Framp.com%2Fleading-indicators%2Ftop-saas-vendors-on-ramp-may-2026&a=fastest+growing+software+vendors+overall) , alongside other industry leaders like Anthropic, Granola and Lovable. Additionally, Brex listed Apollo as one of its Spring 2026[top 25 fastest-growing software vendors](https://edge.prnewswire.com/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4693793-1&h=1530826471&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.brex.com%2Fjournal%2Fbrex-benchmark-may-2026%3Fref_code%3Dos_tw_spring_benchmark_list&a=top+25+fastest-growing+software+vendors) , based on metrics including momentum, scaling velocity, and dollar growth acceleration.


"GTM is being redefined and Apollo's industry recognition reflects our position at the forefront of our space," said Marcio Arnecke, CMO of Apollo.io. "Today's revenue teams need more than a data platform or point solution. Apollo delivers a truly end-to-end AI-powered GTM solution, serving companies of any size and reaching teams wherever they work."
