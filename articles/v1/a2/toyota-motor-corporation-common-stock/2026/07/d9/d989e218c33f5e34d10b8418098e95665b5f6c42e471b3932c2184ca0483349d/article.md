# Rally Finland: Preview TGR-WRT's home heroes ready to fly in Finnish forests

## Rally Finland: Preview


TGR-WRT’s home heroes ready to fly in Finnish forests


2026.07.24 (Fri)


-
-
-


TOYOTA GAZOO Racing World Rally Team and its five FIA World Rally Championship contenders are set to put on a spectacular show on home roads when Rally Finland stages round 10 on July 30-August 2.


2026 GR YARIS Rally1


The fastest round of the WRC takes place in the forests of central Finland around the city of Jyväskylä, which are considered the spiritual home of rallying. Their smooth gravel roads containing frequent jumps and crests delight drivers and fans alike.


It is home ground too for TGR-WRT, which has its headquarters in Jyväskylä just three kilometres from the Rally Finland service park. The team has won seven of the eight editions it has contested, including a historic sweep of the top five positions one year ago – a feat achieved only once before in the WRC – led by a long-awaited first victory for hometown hero Kalle Rovanperä.


This year, TGR-WRT arrives home with a fresh new Finnish hero in Sami Pajari, who last week became the 17th driver from his country to win a round of the WRC with an impressive victory on similarly fast roads at Rally Estonia.


It means that all five of the team’s drivers have now won at least one rally this season. The quintet occupies the top five places in the drivers’ standings, covered by 47 points with five rounds remaining, and all five relish competing on the roads of Rally Finland.


Elfyn Evans leads the championship by 25 points and won Rally Finland with TGR-WRT in 2021 and 2023, making the Welshman one of only four foreign drivers to have triumphed on the event multiple times. His closest challenger Takamoto Katsuta learned his trade as a rally driver with the TGR WRC Challenge Program in Jyväskylä after moving from Japan and has twice finished on the podium of Rally Finland, coming third in 2023 and second last year.


Pajari’s Estonia win promoted him to third in the standings, 33 points from the lead. He finished fourth on his GR YARIS Rally1 debut in Finland in 2024 and fifth last year. Five points further back, nine-time world champion Sébastien Ogier is another two-time Rally Finland winner, his 2024 win coming 11 years after his first. Oliver Solberg finished a strong second to Pajari in Estonia to sit just nine points behind Ogier and is a previous WRC2 winner in Finland.


Team Principal Jari-Matti Latvala will once again drive his home event in a GR Yaris Rally2 car, co-driven by fellow former TGR-WRT driver Juho Hänninen.


A total of 13 GR Yaris Rally2 cars feature on the entry list, including that of the WRC2 championship leader Roope Korhonen (who narrowly beat Latvala to win the class one year ago) and fellow Finn Teemu Suninen, who both finished on the podium in Estonia. Other customer drivers lining up in WRC2 include Briton Gus Greensmith, Finland-based Australian Taylor Gill, young Finn Tuukka Kauppinen, Estonia’s Joosep Ralf Nõgene, Frenchman Adrien Mosca and Austrian Johannes Keferböck.


The TGR WRC Challenge Program will again be well represented in Jyväskylä, where many of its drivers are based. Yuki Yamamoto finished a fine fifth in WRC2 last year, including taking a maiden stage win shared with Latvala. Rally2 rookies Takumi Matsushita and Shotaro Goto will look to build upon solid WRC2 debuts in Estonia, where Jaspar Vaher showed stage-winning speed on home roads. Rio Ogata and Kanta Yanaguida will again drive Rally3 machinery in WRC3 while Tomiya Maekawa co-drives Finnish driver Jarkko Nikara.


The 75th anniversary edition of Rally Finland begins in the heart of Jyväskylä on Thursday evening with the traditional mixed-surface Harju stage, which is run again to close out Friday after two loops of stages in the forests north-east: the classic Laukaa is reversed and paired with returning Saarikas plus new Sydänmaa and Hoho tests. Saturday features familiar roads to the south-east with Parkkola, Västilä, Päijälä and Leustu all returning from last year. A new-look Himos-Jämsa stage – the longest of the rally at 30.02 kilometres – is run twice to form Sunday’s finale.


**Quotes: Jari-Matti Latvala (Team Principal)**
“It was fantastic to see Sami score his first win in Estonia and it’s been wonderful to watch his growth this season. It’s also remarkable that all five of our drivers have now won rallies this season, and they all still have chances in the championship. They’re also all capable of winning Rally Finland, our team’s home event, and I think we can expect a tight fight. Elfyn and Seb have won the rally multiple times, while Taka has been on the podium twice. Oliver had a great rally in Estonia, building back his confidence, while Sami has now proven he can win and the excitement will be high for him in Finland. Meanwhile, I’m grateful to have the opportunity – with thanks to Akio Toyoda – to drive my home rally once more in what should be a big battle in WRC2, where the level is also really high.”


Elfyn Evans / Scott Martin


**Elfyn Evans (Driver car 33)**
“Rally Finland is one of my favourite events on the calendar. It’s an amazing sensation to drive these Rally1 cars on those roads, and in terms of pure driving thrills, nothing else beats it during the season. You need to be able to find a good rhythm and a good feeling with the car to be competitive, but when everything’s working well, it can give you great confidence and enjoyment behind the wheel. I’m looking forward to it as always and hoping for some conditions that are more in our favour and enable us to be in the fight from the start of the weekend.”


Sébastien Ogier / Vincent Landais


**Sébastien Ogier (Driver car 1)**
“Finland is one of the homes of rallying and one of the most enjoyable rallies to drive. It’s also one of the most challenging to win, especially against fast drivers from the north of Europe, and it’s a big honour to have been able to achieve that a couple of times in my career. Rally Estonia was not easy for us driving on those roads for the first time after five years away, but at Rally Finland we will have a higher target in mind as I’ve been there the last two years and been competitive, so I’m looking forward to it.”


Oliver Solberg / Elliott Edmondson


**Oliver Solberg (Driver car 99)**
“Rally Finland is something very special. It’s a rally that every driver wants to win at some point in their career because it’s such a unique challenge. The way that the stages flow, with the speed and the crests and the jumps, feels amazing. I’ve had some good rallies there in the past in Rally2 cars, but I’m sure that my first Rally Finland driving for TGR-WRT as the home team, in such a great car in such a fast rally, will be my most special experience there yet. After a good, consistent rally in Estonia, it would be great if we can finish on the podium again.”


Takamoto Katsuta / Aaron Johnston


**Takamoto Katsuta (Driver car 18)**
“Rally Finland is like a second home rally for me. I moved to Finland when I started rallying and learned pretty much everything I know there, and I still have many friends in Jyväskylä. I have many great memories from the rally – I made my WRC debut there 10 years ago – and to finish second to Kalle last year was very special for me. I was very happy that he could win, but I hope I can go one better. It’s a very demanding rally: you have to be fully committed straight away, and you need full trust in your pacenotes and the car. But I have a good feeling and will try my best.”


Sami Pajari / Marko Salminen


**Sami Pajari (Driver car 5)**
“To win Rally Estonia was amazing, and a feeling that is still sinking in. There was not much time to celebrate or relax, as two days after the rally we were already testing for Rally Finland. Like Estonia, this is one of my favourite rallies, but it’s even more special to be able to compete at home in front of all my family and friends and a lot of fans cheering for us. The stages are beautiful and I really enjoy driving them. Many will be expecting that we can win again; we can’t underestimate the competition, but we of course hope that we can make it happen. I’m really looking forward to it.”


**Yuki Yamamoto (Driver WRC Challenge Program GEN2)**
“I’m looking forward to this Rally Finland. It’s always a special rally for me as my second home rally. I’ve been living in Jyväskylä for five years, so the atmosphere feels very familiar, and the roads are the most beautiful in the calendar. I had a bit of a disappointing rally in Estonia, so I really want to bounce back in Finland. Last year, it was a really good rally and a real turning point for me, so I hope to have the same good feeling and keep that strong pace through the whole weekend to fight against top drivers.”
