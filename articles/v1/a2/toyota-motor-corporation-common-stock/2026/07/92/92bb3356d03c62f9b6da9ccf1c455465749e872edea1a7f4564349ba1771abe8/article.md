# Toyota Brings the Buzz with Honeycomb and Hot Honey Colors for 2027 TRD Pro Models

**PLANO, Texas (July 28, 2026) —** Toyota is expanding its TRD Pro color palette for 2027 with the introduction of a new Honeycomb exterior color for Tundra, Sequoia and Tacoma, along with a slightly different shade, Hot Honey, for the 4Runner.


Built to stand out on and off the road, the new Honeycomb color gives certain TRD Pro models a bold, high-impact appearance that underscores their adventurous character. For 2027, Tundra and Sequoia TRD Pro models receive purpose-built upgrades designed to enhance their off-road capability, including newly designed and vehicle specific tuned FOX® enhanced QS3 shocks engineered to help provide improved feel, stability and adjustability over rough terrain. Standard red tow hooks add a rugged, functional accent to the exterior, while available ISO-dynamic seats are designed to help support comfort and stability during off-road driving for Tundra and Tacoma.


The 2027 Tundra and 2027 Sequoia press releases have been updated with details about their 2027 TRD Pro models. Those releases are linked[here](https://pressroom.toyota.com/new-2027-toyota-tundra-brings-rugged-updated-styling-advanced-technology-and-new-trailhunter-package/) and[here](https://pressroom.toyota.com/toyota-updates-2027-sequoia-with-elevated-style-advanced-technology-and-newly-available-trailhunter-package/) .


Additional details, specifications and pricing for the 2027 Tundra and Sequoia will be announced closer to the start of production in fall 2026.


**Limited Warranty**
Toyota’s 36-month/36,000 mile basic new-vehicle warranty applies to all components other than normal wear and maintenance items. Additional 60-month warranties cover the powertrain for 60,000 miles and against perforation from corrosion for 60 months with no mileage limitation. Hybrid-related components that require repairs needed to correct defects in materials or workmanship are covered for 8 years/100,000 miles, whichever comes first from original date of first use when sold as new. The hybrid battery is covered for 10 years/150,000 miles, whichever comes first, and is transferable across ownership. The 2027 Toyota Tundra and Sequoia also come with ToyotaCare, a plan covering normal factory-scheduled maintenance, for one year or 10,000 miles, whichever comes first, and 2 years, unlimited mileage of Roadside Assistance.
