# [Toyota Times] Former Japan National Soccer Team Coach Okada Visits Toyota Technical Skills Academy: Lessons for Life

Takeshi Okada, former head coach of the Japanese national soccer team, held a special class at Toyota Technical Skills Academy. What did this legendary coach, someone who knows what it takes to compete on the world stage, share with these students?


Takeshi Okada is the former head coach of Japan's national soccer team and current principal of FC Imabari High School Satoyama in Ehime Prefecture.


His teaching philosophy resonated with Chairman Akio Toyoda, leading to a bond that has been given shape in “[Akio Juku](https://toyotatimes.jp/en/newscast/162.html)


,” a special lecture held at the school.


Okada hosted the “Okada Juku” in return.


Okada toured the school prior to the session, saying “I want to share something that might help them, even if just a little, as they make their way through life.”


How did he deal with his players as a coach under the pressure of competing on the world stage? What does it take to grow, how do you build a strong organization, what values does he hold dear...?


The students hung on his every word, all based in experience. Different though soccer and manufacturing may be, the students appeared to sense a common thread.


What did this legendary coach share with the young people who will shape the future of manufacturing? The “Okada Juku” kicks off!
