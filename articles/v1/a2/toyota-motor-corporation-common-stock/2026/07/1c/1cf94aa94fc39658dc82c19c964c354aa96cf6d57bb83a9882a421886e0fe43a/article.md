# Toyota Financial Services Expands Headquarters

**FRISCO, Texas (July 15, 2026) –** Toyota Financial Services (TFS) this week began occupying a newly constructed 242,000-square-foot, seven-story building at its headquarters campus, capable of seating over 1,000 team members. This extension of the Toyota North America campus called “TFS North”, adds much-needed office space, additional parking, and facilitates the ability to co-locate larger teams. TFS team members supporting customer experience, dealer experience and data & technology will work out of this location.


TFS North exemplifies thoughtful appreciation for team member well-being and productivity throughout the building. Recognized as one of the largest mass timber office spaces in the United States, the building’s commitment to health and sustainability is more than just a feature. The extensive use of Southern Yellow Pine timber creates a warm and inviting atmosphere.


Tim Conlon, real estate senior manager, shared that one of the most exciting priorities in the design of TFS North was creating a space that feels open, welcoming, and connected. “The natural wood structure was intentionally highlighted, and wide-open views were preserved throughout the office to bring warmth and character into the workplace.” The overall result increases visibility, communication, and collaboration, helping team members feel more connected to one another and to the environment around them.


Team members will enjoy a host of amenities, including a state-of-the-art gym and locker rooms, micro markets, furnished balconies, and a future restaurant. More subtle features include white noise systems that help reduce distractions and enhance concentration in open areas, window shades that adjust with the sun’s movement, and daylight harvesting that adjusts lighting to save energy.


An adjoining 800-space parking structure makes for easy parking and EV charging. Plus, soon-to-be-installed space counters will make it easy for team members to find a parking spot.


Alec Hagey, president and CEO of TFS, highlighted the advantages of the expanded office space, saying, “Our new building provides a modern environment where team members can come together to collaborate and provide the best possible service to our customers and dealers. As team members occupy this building, we will be able to reconfigure our existing headquarters space to reduce overcrowding, enhance collaboration, and foster innovation to position our company for future growth.”


Additional TFS teams will continue to move into the space in the coming weeks with full occupancy anticipated in the fall of 2026.

**About Toyota Financial Services** ****
Toyota Financial Services (TFS) is the brand for finance and related products for Toyota in the United States, offering retail auto financing and leasing through Toyota Motor Credit Corporation (TMCC) and Toyota Lease Trust. TFS also offers vehicle and payment protection products through Toyota Motor Insurance Services (TMIS). The company services Lexus dealers and customers using the Lexus Financial Services brand.


As of March 31, 2026, TFS employed approximately 3,900 team members nationwide and had over $150 billion in assets. The company is part of a worldwide network of comprehensive financial services offered by Toyota Financial Services Corporation, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Toyota Motor Corporation.
