Jul. 31, 2026


# Toyota Joins in Supporting 2026 Kumamoto Earthquake Relief Efforts


Announcement


Toyota would like to express our deepest sympathies and condolences to all those affected by the 2026 Kumamoto Earthquake.


In response to the situation, Toyota Motor Corporation has decided to provide the following emergency aid.


##


1. Provision of relief supplies and lending of vehicles to disaster-affected municipalities


In cooperation with vehicle dealers, Toyota Rental & Leasing stores, Toyota Motor Kyushu, Inc., and related group companies, we will provide relief supplies, lend vehicles, and dispatch disaster relief volunteers to the affected municipalities and other organizations, including the deployment of power supply vehicles, such as the Moving e, to supply emergency power at evacuation centers. We will work to provide appropriate support while carefully identifying what aid is most needed.


##


1. Donations and relief funds


A total of 30 million yen will be donated to the Japanese Red Cross Society, the Central Community Chest of Japan, and Japan Platform.


We will continue to provide the support needed by consulting with the government, local governments, and relief organizations, as well as carefully confirming the local situation.


We sincerely hope for the earliest possible recovery of the affected areas.


Toyota Motor Corporation works to develop and manufacture innovative, safe and high-quality products and services that create happiness by providing mobility for all. We believe that true achievement comes from supporting our customers, partners, employees, and the communities in which we operate. Since our founding over 80 years ago in 1937, we have applied our Guiding Principles in pursuit of a safer, greener and more inclusive society. Today, as we transform into a mobility company developing connected, automated, shared and electrified technologies, we also remain true to our Guiding Principles and many of the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goals to help realize an ever-better world, where everyone is free to move.
