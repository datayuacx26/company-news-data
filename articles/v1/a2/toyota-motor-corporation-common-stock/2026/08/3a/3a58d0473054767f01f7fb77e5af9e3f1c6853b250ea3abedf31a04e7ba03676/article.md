# Meet the Drivers Behind the Helmets in TOYOTA RACING’s PIT STOP TONIGHT

What happens when you combine the split-second intensity of a NASCAR® pit stop with the quick wit of one of YouTube’s most recognizable creators?


You get


*PIT STOP TONIGHT* – TOYOTA RACING’s new four-episode YouTube series that trades traditional driver interviews for fast-paced entertainment designed to show fans a different side of the people behind the wheel.


Hosted by Kareem Rahma, creator of


*Subway Takes* and


*Keep the Meter Running* , the series features Toyota drivers Bubba Wallace, Christopher Bell, Chase Briscoe and Jade Avedisian stepping into a late-night talk show unlike any other. Before each interview begins, a professional pit crew races against the clock to build the show’s themed set in real time, creating a high-energy opening inspired by the speed and precision of motorsports.


The result is a format that feels as unpredictable as racing itself.


Fans have seen these drivers compete on the track.


*PIT STOP TONIGHT* gives them the chance to see who they are when the helmet comes off.


Throughout each episode, Rahma leads drivers through unexpected games, comedy bits and conversations that move beyond race-day talking points. The format encourages genuine reactions and unscripted moments, revealing personalities that don’t often make it into post-race interviews. It’s a different way of telling TOYOTA RACING’s story – one built around people first.


Developed specifically for YouTube,


*PIT STOP TONIGHT* embraces creator-led storytelling, entertainment-first programming and the kind of shareable moments that thrive on the platform. Produced by Portal A in partnership with Saatchi & Saatchi and YouTube Creative Works, the four-episode series launched throughout the Summer.


In the premiere episode, Team Toyota driver Bubba Wallace, 2025 Brickyard 400 champion and NASCAR star, pulls into the hot seat to answer spicy fan questions, take on unpredictable challenges and navigate fast-paced segments full of surprises. From racing hot takes to unexpected pit stops and questionable sushi, the episode blends the humor and chaos of motorsports with pop culture for one unforgettable ride.

In episode two, Kareem Rahma sits down with racing phenom Jade Avedisian, a Team Toyota development driver and the first woman to win a CARS Tour race.


Hold on tight as Jade tackles high-energy challenges including Pit Crew Charades, What’s That Sound and a chaotic Driver’s Ed test through “Kareem Town” as she competes to earn her official


*PIT STOP TONIGHT* driver’s license. From childhood dreams becoming reality to mini burnout demonstrations, the episode showcases why Jade is one of motorsports’ most exciting rising stars.


**Watch Episode 2 below:**


In episode three, Kareem Rahma welcomes Toyota Joe Gibbs Racing driver Chase Briscoe following a standout season on the track.


Watch the action go from 0 to 100 with Pit Crew Charades, Fess Up or Fuel Up and a competitive round of Podium Pop Off. From the story of almost being named “Race” to fearlessly sharing his camera roll, Chase gives fans an entertaining look at what fuels him both on and off the track.


**Watch Episode 3 below:**


In the fourth and final episode, Kareem Rahma welcomes Toyota Joe Gibbs Racing driver Christopher Bell for an interview packed with quick wit, unexpected challenges and plenty of laughs. Bell opens up about his driving style, race-day mindset and the nerves that still fuel his competitive edge before jumping into a series of comedic segments, including a role reversal where he interviews Kareem, a brainstorm for a chaotic “NASCAR 2” and the dizzying “Spin Interview.” The episode offers another entertaining look at the personality behind one of NASCAR’s top drivers.
