# Toyota Recalls Certain Model Year 2024-2025 Tacoma Vehicles

**PLANO, Texas (Aug. 6, 2026)** — Toyota is conducting a safety recall involving certain MY2024-2025 Tacoma TRD Offroad vehicles. Approximately 48,000 vehicles are involved in the recall for the U.S.


Over time, corrosion can build up and cause the oil reservoir on the shock absorbers in the subject vehicles to detach. If the reservoir detaches while driving, it can create a road hazard, potentially increasing the risk of a crash or injury to another road user.


All known owners of the subject vehicles will be notified to return their vehicle to a Toyota dealer. For all involved vehicles, the dealer will inspect the front and rear shock absorber assemblies and will replace the affected shock absorber assemblies with improved ones, at no cost.


For all involved vehicles, Toyota will notify customers by early October 2026.


Information about automotive recalls, including but not limited to the list of involved vehicles, is current as of today’s filing date and is subject to change thereafter. To see if your vehicle is involved in a safety recall visit Toyota.com/recall or nhtsa.gov/recalls and enter your Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) or license plate information.


For any additional questions, Toyota customer support is also available by calling the Toyota Brand Engagement Center (1-800-331-4331).
