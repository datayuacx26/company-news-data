# Rugged, Powerful and Charged Up for Adventure: 2027 Toyota bZ Woodland

- Available In Base and Premium Grades with Standard All-Wheel Drive and X-MODE®
- 375 Total System Horsepower with Powerful Acceleration (0-60 MPH in 4.5-seconds)
- Ample Utility with 74.3-Cubic Foot Rear Cargo Area with Seats Folded Flat and 8.4-inch Ground Clearance
- Well-Equipped Interior with Standard SofTex®-Trimmed Heated Front and Rear Seating
- Tech Forward Cabin with 14-Inch Toyota Audio Multimedia Touchscreen and Customizable Interior Ambient Lighting
- 281 Mile Manufacturer-Estimated Driving Range Rating (260 Mile Manufacturer Est. Range Rating when Equipped with Optional All-Terrain Tire)*
- Equipped with North American Charging System (NACS) Port for Compatibility with Most DC Fast Charging Stations in the U.S.


**PLANO, Texas (Aug. 5, 2026)** – The bZ Woodland, Toyota’s rugged addition to its battery-electric (BEV) lineup, is back for its second year and is expected to arrive at dealerships nationwide later this year. Blending midsize SUV utility with exhilarating electric performance, it’s equipped with standard all-wheel drive (AWD) featuring X-MODE® and brings a highly capable, adventure-ready twist to the bZ family. To bolster its rugged utility, it has 8.4 inches of ground clearance, and up to 3,500 pounds of towing capacity.


The bZ Woodland delivers a whopping total system output of 375 horsepower and has an estimated 4.5-second 0-60 mph. It has a manufacturer-estimated driving range rating of 281 miles* when equipped with all-season tires, and a 260-mile range rating when equipped with available all-terrain tires.


Overall, features and specifications remain unchanged from the 2026 model year. The 2027 bZ Woodland has a starting Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail Price (MSRP) of $45,380 on the core grade, excluding Dealer Processing and Handling fee,[click here](https://pressroom.toyota.com/toyota-full-line-pricing/) for full line pricing.


*Driving ranges will vary depending on speed, outside temperature, accessory use, how/where you drive, charging habits, and other factors


**Amped Up for Adventure**
The bZ Woodland comes equipped with All-wheel drive with electric motors housed in the front and rear eAxles. It comes standard with X-MODE ®, an AWD function that helps to optimize brake control and power delivery at the wheels for enhanced control and traction.


Another feature of the AWD X-MODE ® system is Grip Control. When activated and used at low speeds, the system modulates motor power delivery to enhance capability in adverse conditions. This feature was developed as an added function to react to road surface characteristics and maintain a constant speed to improve the driver’s focus on what’s ahead.


The bZ Woodland’s AWD capability combines with the smooth, yet exhilarating, driving performance inherent in an all-electric vehicle. Its powerful drive is complemented by a low center of gravity, thanks to its battery pack placed flat under the floor. A battery cross-framing structure also adds to overall vehicle rigidity for stable handling.


The Toyota bZ Woodland is powered by a 74.7-kWh lithium-ion battery and includes a NACS charging port for access to a broad network of DC fast-charging stations in the U.S.


Charging features include:


- DC fast charging from 10% to 80% in around 30 minutes under ideal conditions
- Battery pre-conditioning to help optimize fast-charging performance in cold weather
- Plug & Charge capability with compatible charging networks (requires an active Remote Connect trial or subscription and is 4G network dependent)
- 11-kW onboard charger
- Standard dual-voltage 120-240V home charging cable
- Standard J1772 and CCS1 charging adapters


These features are designed to improve charging convenience at home and help drivers make the most of public charging infrastructure while on the road.


**Rugged Style Inside and Out**
The bZ Woodland adds a more rugged design direction to Toyota’s bZ family. It features matte black overfenders, a distinctive outdoor-oriented appearance, and increased dimensions compared with the standard bZ SUV.


Notable design and utility enhancements include:


- Nearly 6 inches of additional cargo length
- Nearly 1 inch of additional rear cargo height
- Standard 18-inch aluminum wheels with black aero covers
- Up to 74.3 cubic feet of rear cargo area with second-row seats folded flat


That added space gives the bZ Woodland more cargo versatility than the standard bZ, making it a good fit for active lifestyles and gear-heavy weekend use.


Inside, the 2027 bZ Woodland offers a spacious, well-equipped cabin built on Toyota’s dedicated e-TNGA battery-electric platform. The SUV is designed to deliver both comfort and ample power for daily driving.


The cabin emphasizes modern usability, with a clean layout, generous passenger space, and intuitive controls. All grades feature standard SofTex®-trimmed seating as well as roof rails. The base grade bZ Woodland comes with plenty of comfort and convenience features, including heated front and outboard rear seats, a heated steering wheel, dual Qi-capable wireless device chargers, dual-zone automatic climate control, and customizable ambient interior lighting.


The Premium grade adds JBL® Premium Audio, front seat radiant heating, a fixed glass panoramic sunroof, a digital rearview mirror, ventilated front seats, and driver’s seat memory setting. All-Terrain tires are also available on both bZ Woodland grades.


The 2027 bZ Woodland’s sleek design and tech-forward cabin has the following available exterior colors:


- Raven
- Stepping Stone
- Halo (Extra cost color)
- Pavement
- Steel
- Trueno Blue


Interior colors include:


- Stone Brown SofTex-trim
- Black SofTex-trim


**Two Grades Adds More Convenience and Luxury**
Choosing the right bZ Woodland is easy, with two grades available: bZ Woodland AWD and bZ Woodland Premium AWD.


**bZ Woodland AWD**


- Roof rails
- 18-in. wheels with black covers
- Dual Qi-capable wireless device chargers
- 14-in. Toyota Audio Multimedia touchscreen
- Heated, power-adjustable, and power-folding exterior mirrors
- Dual-zone automatic climate control
- 8-way power-adjustable front seats
- SofTex-trimmed seats
- Heated front seats and heated outboard rear seats
- Heated steering wheel
- Digital Key* capability (requires an active Remote Connect trial or subscription and is 4G network dependent)
- Smart Key System on all doors and rear liftgate
- Available all-terrain tires (260-mile manufacturer-estimated range rating with this option)


The **bZ Woodland Premium AWD** adds or upgrades features such as:


- JBL® premium audio system with 9-speakers (includes subwoofer)
- Ventilated front seats
- Driver memory seat
- Auto-tilt side mirrors with memory settings
- Digital rearview mirror with HomeLink®
- Front radiant foot and leg heaters
- Panoramic fixed-glass roof with power sunshade


**Toyota Audio Multimedia System & Connected Technology**
The 2027 Toyota bZ Woodland has a standard 14-inch touchscreen featuring the Toyota Audio Multimedia audio system, developed by the Texas-based Toyota Connected Technologies team. The base grade’s system has six-speakers and supports wireless Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™. A JBL® premium audio system with 9-speakers (including subwoofer) and amplifier is standard on the Premium grade model.


Interior tech features include standard dual Qi® wireless charging with enhanced power output, multiple USB-C ports, and a 120V/1500W AC outlet in the rear cargo area. It has a top-mount Multi-Information Display (MID) with indicators and a 7-inch customizable LCD display with speedometer, charge meter, power output meter, drive mode indicators, charging information and more.


Both 2027 bZ Woodland grades also include a 5-year Connected Services trials for Safety Connect***, Service Connect***, and Remote Connect*** and a 30 day/3 GB Wi-Fi Connect*** trial. Additional services available depending on grade, such as Drive Connect*** visit[Toyota Connected Services](https://www.toyota.com/connected-services/) to learn more.


Both 2027 bZ Woodland grades also include Toyota Digital Key capability, which enables a smartphone to be used instead of a physical key (an active Remote Connect*** trial or subscription is required). In addition, the Toyota app and an active Remote Connect trial or subscription allows customers to check charging status, start or stop charging, locate nearby charging stations and manage remote vehicle functions.


***4G network dependent, subscription required after trial.


**Safety & Convenience**
Both 2027 bZ Woodland grades come standard with Toyota Safety Sense 3.0, Toyota’s suite of active safety and convenience features. This suite includes:


- Pre-Collision System with Pedestrian Detection
- Full-Speed Range Dynamic Radar Cruise Control
- Lane Departure Alert with Steering Assist
- Lane Tracing Assist
- Road Sign Assist
- Automatic High Beams
- Proactive Driving Assist


Additional features include:


- Blind Spot Monitor with Rear Cross-Traffic Alert
- Front and Rear Parking Assist with Automatic Braking
- Safe Exit Alert
- Toyota Star Safety System


Both grades also have a Panoramic View Monitor with Multi-Terrain Monitor and 360-degre Overhead View.


**Limited Warranty**
Toyota’s 36-month/36,000-mile basic new-vehicle warranty applies to all components other than normal wear and maintenance items. Additional 60-month warranties cover the powertrain for 60,000 miles and corrosion with no mileage limitation. The Electric Vehicle Driving Components, including the traction battery, are covered for 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first. Toyota dealers have complete details on the limited warranty.


The Toyota bZ Woodland also comes with ToyotaCare, a plan covering normal factory-scheduled maintenance, for one year or 10,000 miles, whichever comes first, and three years of Roadside Assistance, regardless of mileage.
