# OPAL Fuels Announces First Quarter 2026 Earnings Release Date and Conference Call

[PDF Version](https://investors.opalfuels.com/node/9746/pdf)


WHITE PLAINS, N.Y.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Apr. 28, 2026--[OPAL Fuels](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.opalfuels.com&esheet=54523938&newsitemid=20260428798208&lan=en-US&anchor=OPAL+Fuels&index=1&md5=2fab013041b32731534ae019e1f6fd2a) (Nasdaq: OPAL) today announced that it will release its earnings results for the first quarter ended March 31, 2026, before market open on Monday, May 11, 2026. A conference call will take place on Monday, May 11, 2026 at 11:00 a.m. Eastern Time.


A listen-only connection to the investor presentation will be accessible at[https://edge.media-server.com/mmc/p/yubhgs6w](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fedge.media-server.com%2Fmmc%2Fp%2Fyubhgs6w&esheet=54523938&newsitemid=20260428798208&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Fedge.media-server.com%2Fmmc%2Fp%2Fyubhgs6w&index=2&md5=cb2b9bc8d5031254ba925ce41616542c) . Investors can also listen to a webcast of the presentation on the company's Investor Relations website at[https://investors.opalfuels.com/news-events/events-presentations](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.opalfuels.com%2Fnews-events%2Fevents-presentations&esheet=54523938&newsitemid=20260428798208&lan=en-US&anchor=https%3A%2F%2Finvestors.opalfuels.com%2Fnews-events%2Fevents-presentations&index=3&md5=35509234c8fa57b7eccb85ca5df940ef) .


**About OPAL Fuels**


[OPAL Fuels](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.opalfuels.com&esheet=54523938&newsitemid=20260428798208&lan=en-US&anchor=OPAL+Fuels&index=4&md5=863ec4cbd33125585516138da98cdf4c) (Nasdaq: OPAL) is a leader in the capture and conversion of biogas into low carbon intensity RNG and renewable electricity. OPAL Fuels is also a leader in the marketing and distribution of RNG to heavy duty trucking and other hard to decarbonize industrial sectors. For additional information, and to learn more about OPAL Fuels and how it is leading the effort to capture North America’s harmful methane emissions and decarbonize the economy, please visit[www.opalfuels.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.opalfuels.com&esheet=54523938&newsitemid=20260428798208&lan=en-US&anchor=www.opalfuels.com&index=5&md5=da8f6a2c1516646a034a6a6564c8ec10) .
