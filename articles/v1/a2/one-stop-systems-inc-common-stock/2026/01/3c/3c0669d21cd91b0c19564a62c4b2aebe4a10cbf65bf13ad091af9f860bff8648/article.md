War


Pete Hegseth, who has repeatedly emphasized that


“speed wins; speed dominates”


and that advanced compute must move


“from the data center to the battlefield.”


OSS specializes in taking the latest commercial GPU,


FPGA


,

NVMe

War

OSS directly enables this mission. AI models are only as effective as the compute infrastructure that supports them
