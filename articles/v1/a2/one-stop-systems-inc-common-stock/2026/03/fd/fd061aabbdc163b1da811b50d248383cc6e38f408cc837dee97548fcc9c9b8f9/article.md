## Big News from OSS


We’ve had a few press releases this quarter detailing a demonstration with Tauro Technologies, a new commercial robotics customer, and an award to support the US Navy’s P-8A Poseidon Aircraft Program.


OSS Announces $10.5 Million in New Awards to Support U.S. Navy P-8A Poseidon Aircraft Program


“Under the terms of the contract, OSS will deliver rugged data storage units to support the C5ISR mission capabilities onboard the P-8A Poseidon.


These units are designed and manufactured by OSS and incorporate hot-swappable canisters of high-capacity NVMe flash storage. The design enables rapid removal and replacement for secure data offload and analysis in demanding airborne environments.”[Read more on our website.](https://onestopsystems.com/blogs/press-releases/oss-announces-10-5-million-in-new-awards-to-support-u-s-navy-p-8a-poseidon-aircraft-program?utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-8hg6UlIotWjCmZ_GBte1VySA2Ajxlb6ka-1xCW65l-8X5Z0dCxvzvtl4rP3M8kLmTJ4i8o)


OSS Announces New Commercial Robotics Customer


OSS will provide a Gen5 ruggedized, 3U, liquid-cooled, short-depth server (SDS), engineered to support commercial autonomous operation of heavy equipment in the construction and mining sectors.


The order leverages OSS's core competencies in rugged enterprise-class system design, advanced liquid-cooling technologies, and low-latency AI compute, enabling real-time perception, decision-making, and autonomy for mission-critical industrial applications.[Read more on our website.](https://onestopsystems.com/pages/press-release-viewer?i=162288&utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-8hg6UlIotWjCmZ_GBte1VySA2Ajxlb6ka-1xCW65l-8X5Z0dCxvzvtl4rP3M8kLmTJ4i8o)


One Stop Systems and Tauro Technologies to Showcase Sensor Bridge at AFCEA West


Together with Tauro Technologies, we brought real-time sensor fusion to AFCEA West in the form of the Torrey Reef 2U SDS IGX.


This technology can be applied at the edge in air, land, and maritime platforms, demonstrating how complex data can be ingested, synchronized, and processed in real time. By merging OCP open compute scalability with IGX deterministic edge AI, the Torrey Reef IGX delivers mission-ready sensor fusion in contested environments.[Read more on our website.](https://onestopsystems.com/blogs/press-releases/one-stop-systems-and-tauro-technologies-to-showcase-sensor-bridge-at-afcea-west?utm_source=hs_email&utm_medium=email&_hsenc=p2ANqtz-8hg6UlIotWjCmZ_GBte1VySA2Ajxlb6ka-1xCW65l-8X5Z0dCxvzvtl4rP3M8kLmTJ4i8o)

## Uncle Sam Wants GP (You)!


By: Robert Kalebaugh, VP of Sales


The character of modern warfare is being reshaped by data. Sensors, autonomy, electronic warfare, and AI-driven decision systems are now decisive advantages, but only if compute power can be deployed fast enough and close enough to the fight. This reality sits at the center of recent guidance from the Trump administration and Secretary of War Pete Hegseth, who has repeatedly emphasized that ‘speed wins; speed dominates’ and that advanced compute must move ‘from the data center to the battlefield.’


OSS specializes in taking the latest commercial GPU, FPGA, NIC, and NVMe technologies, the same acceleration platforms driving hyperscale data centers, and delivering them in rugged, deployable systems purpose-built for U.S. military platforms. At a moment when the Department of War is prioritizing speed, adaptability, and commercial technology insertion, OSS sits at the intersection of performance, ruggedization, and rapid deployment.
