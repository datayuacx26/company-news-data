## The Oceanside 1U SDS Lite Immersion


First presented at Sea Air Space 2026 in April, the Oceanside 1U SDS Immersion Lite Server delivers rugged, high performance compute in an ultra-compact half-width 1U form factor, purpose-built for immersion-cooled deployment in demanding defense and edge environments.


Leveraging OSS’s proven rugged edge supercomputing expertise, it meets the stringent requirements of mission-critical applications where thermal efficiency, reliability, and compute density are paramount.


## Rugged Compute Meets AI Labeling at the Edge


Our partnership with Latent AI began in 2023, and since then, we have combined our respective strengths in rugged compute and low-latency, agnostic AI software to power efficient compute at the edge. At SOF Week 2026, the OSS 3U SDS and Latent AI’s Latent Assisted Label were hard at work demonstrating how, together, our products cut the latency and manual effort involved in labeling AI training data at the rugged edge.


This ruggedized 3U SDS-LC doesn’t


just survive harsh environments—it thrives in them, running up to 35 AI workloads simultaneously. Air-cooled and MIL-STD-810G rated, this Gen5 short depth server supports configurations of up to 5 of the worlds most powerful GPUs, 16 of the fastest NVMe solid state storage devices, the lowest latency, highest bandwidth PCIe scale-out expansion systems, the highest performance SAN & NAS storage software, extreme 400Gbps networking solutions and the most secure advanced system management, monitoring and control.


When combined with the 3U SDS, the Latent Assisted Label optimizes AI models to run effectively by minimizing latency and maximizing the AI’s inference speed. This optimization solves the performance bottleneck and demand issues that occur when AI models are not optimized. Together, the 3U SDS and Latent Assisted Label allow for real-time imaging, providing data to
operators at the speed of need to support mission success.
