# More Than a Resume: The People, Culture, and Mindset that Drive OSS

**By Eyren Danielson, VP of HR**


****


If you look at our job boards, you’ll see the high-performance technical skills we work with every day—things like PCIe architecture, ruggedized hardware engineering, and advanced AI workflows. That expertise is essential because we design systems that power AI, defense operations, autonomous trucking, and deep learning.


But a resume only tells a small part of the story.


At One Stop Systems (OSS), our mission is to bring datacenter-class performance out of traditional, climate-controlled rooms and onto the rugged, tactical "edge." To do that, we rely heavily on our unique culture and the amazing team of people who build it.


If you’re considering


[joining us](https://onestopsystems.com/pages/contact-hr) , here is a look at the core mindsets, supportive culture, and collaborative values that help our employees thrive.


# 1. Embracing Growth and Versatility


We don’t build one-size-fits-all hardware. We design custom, tailored solutions—from liquid-cooled edge supercomputers to specialized storage arrays—often adapting to the evolving needs of our customers.


- **The Employee Experience** :


We love supporting people who are comfortable exploring new ideas. Instead of being boxed into a rigid, static role, you’re encouraged to follow your strengths and try new things. If a project evolves or a better design approach comes along, we collaborate as a team to adapt and pivot smoothly.


- **Why it matters** :


Technology shifts fast, and so do our customers' needs. We want you to feel empowered to stretch your capabilities, tackle new variables, and grow your skillset in a dynamic environment.


# 2. Work Hard, Play Hard: The OSS Employee Experience


Our teams handle complex, high-stakes engineering projects, and we know that kind of focus requires a healthy balance. That’s why the employee experience is at the absolute heart of how we operate.


- **The Employee Experience** :


We look for people who want to be part of an actual community, not just a workplace. We value a great sense of humor and an openness to connecting with colleagues.


- **The Fun Side of OSS** :


We purposefully weave moments of connection and fun into the work week to celebrate the incredible effort our team puts in. Whether it’s company-wide events, casual perks, or just taking a breath to laugh together, we believe a happy, energized crew is what fuels breakthroughs. On any given week, you might find us playing a game of basketball, enjoying trivia with treats, hosting a friendly salsa and guacamole contest or local hike, heading out to a Padres game, bringing in an ice cream truck during the summer.


# 3. Collaborative Grit


While our hardware is rugged, our internal culture is collaborative and deeply supportive. We intentionally foster an approachable environment where everyone feels valued.


- **The Employee Experience** :


We place a high value on humility, empathy, and open communication. We look for team members who treat everyone across the organization—from the production team to sales engineers and executives—with the exact same level of warmth and respect.


- **Why it matters** :


We pride ourselves on an open-door environment where people freely share ideas, support one another through tight deadlines, and joyfully celebrate collective wins.


# 4. Shared Ownership and Resilience


Bringing high-performance technology to moving maritime vessels or military vehicles means creating systems our clients can truly rely on.


- **The Employee Experience** :


We focus on collaborative problem-solving. When challenges arise or a test doesn't go as planned, we don’t look for blame—we lean on each other to find solutions. We value professionals who take pride in the quality of their work and enjoy collaborative troubleshooting.


- **Why it matters** :


Our partners trust us with mission-critical applications. The people who enjoy working here are those who see a complex technical puzzle as an exciting challenge to solve alongside a supportive team.


# The OSS Bottom Line


We can teach you our specific workflows, and we can train you on our proprietary engineering pipelines. But we can’t teach the drive to innovate, the willingness to collaborate across teams, or the desire to cultivate an environment that balances high performance with high-spirited fun.


If you look at our


[open positions](https://onestopsystems.com/pages/open-positions) and see a list of requirements you can fit—but more importantly, you see a culture of freedom, growth, and vibrant community that excites you—we want to hear from you.
