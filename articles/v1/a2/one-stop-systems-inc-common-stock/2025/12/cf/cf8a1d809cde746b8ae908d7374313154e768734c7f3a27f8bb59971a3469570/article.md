# A Year of Breakthroughs: Powering Your Next-Generation Edge Applications

By: Mike Knowles, CEO, One Stop Systems


As we bring another successful year to a close, we at One Stop Systems (OSS) are incredibly proud of the progress we’ve made — progress that directly translates into greater capability and success for *you* , our valued customer.


We’ve expanded our product portfolio and deepened our technical expertise, driving measurable impact across both Commercial and Defense markets.


Our core strategy is simple: bringing enterprise and data center class technologies directly to the edge.


This approach positions OSS as the undisputed leader in enabling next-generation capabilities, including advanced AI/ML, sensor fusion, and autonomy on vehicle platforms.


Most notably, our robust, high-performance solutions have played a critical role in accelerating key US Department of Defense initiatives, helping to rapidly deploy advanced commercial technologies to the battlespace and meet the urgent call for "speed to capability."


This reinforces our position as the trusted partner for your most mission-critical applications.


**Maximum Performance, Minimum Footprint**


Over the past year, we have introduced several new products and solutions leveraging scalable GPU and PCIe architectures.


These solutions consistently deliver higher performance and lower latency compared to current high-performance compute or embedded systems.


We understand that SWAP-C (Size, Weight, Power, and Cost) is everything at the edge.


Our unique architectures offer two distinct pathways to advantage:


1. **Maximize Capability:** Achieve orders of magnitude greater compute and decreased latency within the *same* physical size and power constraints.


2. **Minimize Footprint:** Leverage superior performance to *reduce* your overall SWAP-C, leading to significant performance and cost savings.


Both approaches ensure you have the hardware processing spare capacity to easily integrate new software, AI, and ML capabilities down the road, effectively future-proofing your platforms and extending your tech refresh cycles.


From standard, quickly deployable products to bespoke designs that solve your most challenging demands, OSS delivers the greatest compute and lowest latency per rack space or compute volume.


Operational Excellence for Your Confidence


While driving this leadership in edge computing architectures, we have also focused on maturing our company operations to ensure you always receive a world-class experience.


●


**Expert Leadership:** We have built out a strong leadership and program management team with decades of experience overseeing large-scale commercial and defense programs.


●


**World-Class Quality:** Our operations and manufacturing groups have implemented world-class workflows and quality systems.


This includes achieving AS9100 and ISO14001 certifications, maintaining sustained on-time delivery above 95%, and securing multi-year, best-in-class customer satisfaction ratings.


●


**Defense-Ready:** We are proud to have been approved for a facility clearance to support DoD classified programs and are on track to obtain our CMMC certification, operating with the rigor expected of top defense and technology suppliers.


This year’s achievements underscore a clear promise: OSS is executing, scaling, and leading for your success **.**


We are designing and delivering products that set industry performance standards and expanding our role in the most strategically important technology initiatives of our time.


We enter the coming year with strong momentum and a deep commitment to advancing your capabilities and the vital missions you serve.
