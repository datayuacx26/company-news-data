# Why Faster Giving Matters: How The Blackbaud Giving Fund Helps Nonprofits Receive Donations at the Speed of Need

# What is The Blackbaud Giving Fund? How we Champion Philanthropic Giving


Print


#### [FAQs](https://blackbaudgivingfund.org/blog/category/faqs/) ,[Giving](https://blackbaudgivingfund.org/blog/category/giving/) ,[Resources](https://blackbaudgivingfund.org/blog/category/resources/)


November 3, 2025 | 6 min read By:[Matthew Nash](https://blackbaudgivingfund.org/blog/authors/matthew-nash/)


The Blackbaud Giving Fund delivers donations from generous donors directly to the nonprofits they care about. In 2024, donors played a key role in supporting good causes and ensuring the incredible nonprofits that matter most to them continue to thrive. During that time, The Blackbaud Giving Fund disbursed $558.7 million to over 123,000 nonprofits worldwide, bringing total lifetime giving to $2.75 billion. More than half of those funds came through corporate donation matches from


[workplace giving programs](https://blackbaudgivingfund.org/blog/what-is-workplace-giving-and-why-its-important-for-nonprofits/) , doubling the impact of individual contributions and expanding support for critical causes across the globe.


Despite ongoing economic pressures, donors gave consistently and with purpose, underscoring that philanthropy is driven not only by financial capacity but also by shared values and a commitment to a better world.


### **Best Practices for Fundraisers to Maximize Workplace Giving**


With corporate philanthropy programs on the rise, fundraisers can take practical steps to strategically strengthen their connection with workplace giving opportunities:


1. [Claim and optimize](https://blackbaudgivingfund.org/blog/step-by-step-guide-to-attract-donors-on-blackbaud-verified-network-with-examples/) **your Blackbaud Verified Network profile.** Make sure to state your nonprofit’s information, mission, and impact clearly to attract workplace donors.


2. **Highlight matching gift opportunities** . Many employees don’t know their donations can be doubled. Call this out in appeals, on your website, and in follow-up communications.


3. **Engage corporate partners** . Share impact stories with companies that support your mission to strengthen ongoing relationships.


4. **Showcase volunteer opportunities** . Employers are increasingly interested in connecting employees with causes. Pair fundraising asks with


[volunteer experiences](https://blackbaudgivingfund.org/blog/how-to-create-high-impact-low-lift-volunteer-activities-while-avoiding-team-burnout/) to boost engagement and connection with your cause to further your mission.


5. **Communicate impact clearly** . Donors giving through workplace programs want to see results. Share stories, data, and updates that prove their contributions make a difference.


### **How The Blackbaud Giving Fund Supports Nonprofits**

[Blackbaud Verified Network](https://nonprofit.yourcause.com/login/create-account) platform. Using this platform, nonprofits benefit from:


- **Fast and secure disbursements.** When an individual donor, employee with access to a workplace giving program, or company donates to a nonprofit using our partners,


[YourCause](https://www.yourcause.com/) or


[JustGiving](https://www.justgiving.com/) , The Blackbaud Giving Fund quickly and safely disburses the entire donation amount directly to the nonprofit the donor selected using industry-leading security. If the nonprofit is enrolled in


[ACH](https://blackbaudgivingfund.org/blog/how-to-register-for-ach-on-blackbaud-verified-network-a-step-by-step-guide/) , funds are deposited directly into their bank account, so they receive the money up to three days sooner.


- **Free Resources.** The Blackbaud Giving Fund supports nonprofits as they work to spark change and support their communities. With the free


[Blackbaud Verified Network](https://nonprofit.yourcause.com/login/create-account) platform, fundraisers can elevate their mission, expand their reach, and make progress toward their mission by:


- Accessing free


[resources](https://blackbaudgivingfund.org/blog/6-ways-to-transform-your-fundraising-with-blackbaud-verified-network/) that help them further their reach to millions of potential donors and support their fundraising and engagement efforts.


- Enrolling in


[ACH payments](https://blackbaudgivingfund.org/blog/how-to-register-for-ach-on-blackbaud-verified-network-a-step-by-step-guide/) to receive donations faster than waiting for a check in the mail.


- [Building out a profile](https://blackbaudgivingfund.org/blog/getting-started-with-blackbaud-verified-network-a-simple-guide-to-setting-up-and-optimizing-your-profile/) to appeal to current and potential donors.


- Evaluating donor and volunteer data, and more.


- **Access to over 8 million potential donors** who use the platform to discover and support nonprofits. These donors rely on the platform to discover legitimate nonprofits that are in good standing with the IRS or local government legislation. Nonprofits with active profiles on Blackbaud Verified Network show potential donors they are a legitimate organization, dedicated to their mission.


### **Why It Matters Now**


Employee engagement is slipping across industries, and companies are turning to corporate social responsibility (CSR) programs as part of the solution. CSR initiatives, including employee giving and volunteering, not only strengthen communities but also


[reduces turnover](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2022.01517) . That makes workplace giving a win-win: nonprofits receive critical resources, and companies build stronger, more connected workforces.
