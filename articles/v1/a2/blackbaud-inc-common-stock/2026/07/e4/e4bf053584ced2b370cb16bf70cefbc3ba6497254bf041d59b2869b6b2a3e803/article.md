# Import Mapping Assistant + Document Intelligence for Expense Management

*Less Manual Work, More Time for Mission: New Automation in Financial Edge NXT®*


Every hour your finance team spends re-keying an import file or transcribing a receipt is an hour not spent moving your mission forward. That trade-off used to be the cost of doing business. It isn’t anymore.


Blackbaud Financial Edge NX® is built around a simple premise: your accounting platform should tell you what needs your attention and quietly handle what doesn’t. Not a chatbot bolted onto old software, but intelligence built into the workflows your team already runs every day, from the first import to the last approval.


Two recent additions show where that’s headed.


##### Import Mapping Assistant: Smarter, Faster Data Imports


Every finance team has a file that never quite matches the system’s expectations, such as a payroll export or a spreadsheet from a program team that’s formatted its own way, making imports into Financial Edge tricky. Historically, that meant someone manually lining up every column, every time, and hoping nothing got missed.


Financial Edge NXT now does that work for you. The Import Mapping Assistant, powered by Blackbaud AI, reads your file, matches it to the right fields even if the labels don’t match exactly, and flags only what genuinely needs a second look. It also remembers how your organization maps recurring files, so the imports you run monthly get faster every time you run them.


The shift isn’t just speed — it’s attention. Your team stops auditing every field and starts trusting the system to point them to the ones that matter.


The Import Mapping Assistant uses intelligent pattern recognition to detect and suggest field mappings during import. Instead of starting from scratch, users receive guided suggestions based on their data:


- **Intelligent Auto-Detection** : Automatically matches fields using name similarity and data type, reducing manual setup
- **Learns Over Time** : Improves suggestions based on previous imports and usage patterns
- **User Control** : Users can review, adjust, or override mappings before finalizing
- **Confidence Scores** : Each suggestion includes a confidence level to help guide decisions
- **Reusable Templates** : Save and reuse mapping templates for recurring imports, helping maintain consistency across teams


Import Mapping Assistant is part of a broader effort to take routine, manual work off your plate, so your team has more time for the work that actually needs their judgment.


##### AI-Powered Document Intelligence for Expense Management: Now in Limited Availability


Expense management has stubbornly resisted automation. Someone still opens each receipt, reads it, and types what it says into a form, line by line, receipt by receipt.


AI-Powered Document Intelligence for Expense Management closes that gap, and it’s built on a serious technology stack: Microsoft Azure AI Document Intelligence paired with Blackbaud AI, purpose-built to read nonprofit financial documents rather than a generic OCR bolted on after the fact.


Upload a hotel receipt, a vendor invoice, or a credit card charge, and Document Intelligence extracts the details your team used to key by hand—amounts, dates, descriptions, and expense categories—and drops them into an open request, fully coded and ready for review. The source document attaches automatically for a one-click audit trail, and nothing about your approval workflow changes.


It covers both sides of expense management, not just one:


- **Invoice requests** : Snap a photo of a receipt from your phone or upload an invoice from your desk, and a populated request is waiting in the queue.
- **Credit card charges** : Upload a receipt against any transaction and AI maps it to the right vendor and category automatically, even mapping preferred merchants like “Hotels” or “Restaurants” to your organization’s specific chart of accounts. This is especially valuable for high-volume cardholders drowning in monthly reconciliation.


And it gets smarter with use. The model learns from every correction your team makes, so accuracy compounds the longer you run it.


That combination is what makes this a genuine value-add rather than a convenience feature. It removes data entry from two of the highest-volume, most error-prone workflows in the finance office, without asking your team to change how they review or approve a single thing.


##### A Consistent Focus: Practical AI That Solves Real Problems


These capabilities reflect a broader approach to how we’re building Financial Edge NXT, embedding intelligence directly into the workflows finance teams use every day. This is practical automation designed to eliminate repetitive tasks, reduce errors, and give your team meaningful time back.


Whether it’s mapping an import or processing an expense document, the goal is the same: less manual effort, greater accuracy, and more time to focus on advancing your mission.


##### Availability


- The Import Assistant is generally available to Financial Edge NXT customers in the U.S. and Canada.
- AI-Powered Document Intelligence for Expense Management is now in Limited Availability, expanding access following its Early Adopter Program, with continued rollout underway.
