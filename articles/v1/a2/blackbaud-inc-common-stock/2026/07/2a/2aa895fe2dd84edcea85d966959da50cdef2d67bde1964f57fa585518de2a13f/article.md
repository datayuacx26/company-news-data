# All the Biggest Announcements from Blackbaud’s 2026 K–12 User Conference

Blackbaud’s highly anticipated K–12 User Conference recently brought educators, administrators, and technology leaders to Boston for its first in-person gathering since 2019. Over three days, more than 600 attendees joined 80+ sessions focused on the future of school technology.


Here’s a look at the biggest announcements to come out of Blackbaud’s 2026 K–12 UC. ****


##### From System of Record to System of Action


Across the UC, one vision tied every announcement together: Blackbaud’s Total School Solution is evolving from a system of record—designed primarily to store data and surface insights—to a system of intelligent action designed to analyze information, make recommendations, and complete tasks, with[humans always at the center](https://blog.blackbaud.com/ai-future-human-centered-education/) of decision-making. That means smarter workflows, less administrative burden, and built-in AI to expand what school teams can do, giving them back time for the work that matters most.


##### Updates Across the Blackbaud K–12 Portfolio


###### Enrollment & Admissions


- **Dynamic Enrollment Contracts (Early Adopter Program opens this fall):** new dynamic contract logic adapts to each family and seamlessly connects enrollment, aid, billing, and financial setup—eliminating manual handoffs and creating a smooth transition from enrollment to financial management.
- [Admissions Agent](https://www.blackbaud.com/newsroom/article/blackbaud-unveils-new-ai-innovation-for-k12-independent-schools) **(Early Adopter Program opens this winter):** agentic AI purpose-built to provide a personalized admissions experience to every family and move candidates through the admissions funnel
- **Candidate Insights (in development):** AI-powered admissions intelligence designed to help teams evaluate applicants more consistently and with less bias


###### Student Information & Learning Management


- **A Common Records Engine (limited availability now):** that syncs data in real time between Blackbaud Student Information System™ and Blackbaud Raiser’s Edge NXT®, breaking down silos between departments and empowering your advancement teams
- **Grading Hub (live in product):** centralizes grading settings and reduces the clicks required to manage marking periods
- **New Assignment Experience (live in product):** a redesigned modernized workflow for teachers and students, including AI tools to help teachers create and manage assignments
- **Parent-Submitted Attendance (live in product):** parents can submit attendance directly through the parent portal, while schools retain full control
- **Student Success Insights (in development):** a configurable early-warning system that draws on grades, attendance, and conduct data to help identify students who need additional support


###### Business Office & Billing


- **Billing Management (available now):** a full rebuild of the billing experience, living inside the same platform as enrollment and student information
- **Direct Financial Edge NXT GL posting (live in product):** streamlined posting between billing and your general ledger, Blackbaud Financial Edge NXT
- **Collections Assistant (Early Adopter Program open now):** AI-powered support for collections workflows
- **Parent Support AI (pilot launching August 2026):** handles family billing questions outside of business hours


###### Analytics & Advancement


- **Core Data – Enhanced (formerly SchoolBI; Early Adopter Program live):** bring all education management data into a single warehouse, with one-click DASL/NAIS reporting and natural-language querying coming later this year
- **AI Premium Features for Raiser’s Edge NXT (available now):** surface high-potential donors and generate major gift action plans in one click
- [Development Agent](https://www.blackbaud.com/products/blackbaud-development-agent) **(available now):** an autonomous AI agent already helping advancement teams manage follow-ups and flag lapsed donors


###### Customer Success


- **87% reduction** in phone hold times
- **Virtual Assistant** that diagnoses issues and guides customers through resolution conversationally
- **A customer success team more committed to K–12 than ever,** under dedicated leadership and built for the rhythm of a school year


**Capabilities shown span a range of availability — some are already in customers’ hands, others are entering Early Adopter Programs, and some are early prototypes. Not everything shown will ship exactly as demonstrated.* **

Every direction announced at the K–12 UC reflects where Blackbaud is headed next, shaped directly by feedback from the educators and administrators who use these tools every day.
