# Blackbaud Announces the 2026 Blackbaud Impact Awards Finalists

*Celebrating the Organizations and Individuals Using Technology, Innovation, and Collaboration to Drive Greater Impact* **


We’re excited to announce the finalists for the[2026 Blackbaud Impact Awards](https://www.blackbaud.com/impact-awards/2026-finalists?ICID=bbwebsite-newsroom-impactawards-fin-26) , recognizing outstanding organizations and leaders using Blackbaud technology to advance their missions, strengthen communities, and achieve extraordinary results.


As the world’s leading provider of AI-powered solutions for social impact, Blackbaud partners with organizations across the social impact ecosystem, including nonprofits, healthcare organizations, educational institutions, foundations, and corporate social impact teams. The Blackbaud Impact Awards celebrate customers that are embracing innovation, breaking down barriers, and using technology to create measurable, meaningful change.


This year’s finalists represent a remarkable cross-section of the social impact sector across North America, the UK, Australia and New Zealand. Their stories show how organizations are using data, collaboration, digital transformation, and forward-thinking leadership to tackle complex challenges, deepen engagement, improve operations, and help people and communities thrive.


“The stories behind this year’s finalists highlight the incredible ingenuity, resilience and passion that define the social impact community,” said Todd Lant, chief customer officer, Blackbaud. “Across sectors and geographies, these organizations are finding innovative ways to solve challenges, strengthen connections, and create lasting change. We’re honored to celebrate their achievements and the impact they’re making every day.”


The Blackbaud Impact Awards continue to grow as a global celebration of customer excellence. Winners across the seven award categories will be announced on Tuesday, August 18, and will be celebrated live at[bbcon 2026](https://www.bbconference.com/2026) in Columbus, London, and Sydney. Learn more about each finalist’s specific achievements at[www.blackbaud.com/impact-awards](https://www.blackbaud.com/impact-awards/2026-finalists?ICID=bbwebsite-newsroom-impactawards-fin-26) .
