Waiting for donations is a price you’ve been paying. It’s not charitable. It’s not necessary. And it ends here.


For years, nonprofits have quietly absorbed a problem hidden in plain sight: the time between when an employee donor gives and when funds are actually delivered. That delay slows programs, forces cash workarounds, and delays impact. Not because nonprofits chose it, but because the system made it normal. But we’re breaking “normal.”


## **THE REAL COST OF WAITING**


When donations take weeks or months to arrive, the cost isn’t abstract. It shows up immediately:


- Programs get delayed or scaled back


- Finance teams rely on reserves or short-term fixes


- Fundraisers lose momentum tied to active campaigns


- Leadership makes decisions without full visibility into available funds


This causes a decrease in efficiency and, in many cases, impact. The Blackbaud Giving Fund’s role is to move generous donations to nonprofits at the speed of need. When the infrastructure between a corporation and a nonprofit works, nonprofits can stay focused on delivering their mission. Speed isn’t a luxury. It’s part of whether your nonprofit can execute when it matters most.


## **WHAT EXPEDITED GIVING CHANGES**


Expedited giving will be available on Blackbaud Verified Network in the summer of 2026 and will dramatically shorten the time it takes for donations to reach nonprofits. In its pilot testing, expedited giving has demonstrated 90% to 99.95% faster funding. That shift does more than accelerate transactions. It changes how nonprofits operate.


Instead of waiting, fundraisers can:


- Plan with greater confidence


- Deploy funds closer to the moment of need


- Reduce reliance on financial stopgaps


- Maintain campaign momentum from donation to impact


At its core, expedited giving removes friction. Corporate giving, employee donations, and payment infrastructure work together so that funds move when intent occurs, which means faster control over donations.


## **WHY DONOR EXPECTATIONS ARE FORCING THE SHIFT**


Donors already operate in real time. They can track packages, monitor investments, and receive instant confirmations in nearly every part of their lives. Corporate giving is no exception to the expectation of instant gratification. When there’s a disconnect between when a donor gives and when impact happens, it creates friction:


- Spend less time worrying about cash flow and more time on the mission


- Donors question where their money is


- Fundraisers field unnecessary follow-ups


- Trust becomes something that has to be explained, rather than assumed


Speed and transparency must go hand in hand. When funds move quickly, the system feels more reliable, even if the donor never sees the backend. In a landscape where trust is increasingly fragile, that matters.


## **THE ROLE OF INFRASTRUCTURE IN BUILDING TRUST**


Trust isn’t built through messaging alone. It’s built through systems that work the way people expect them to. The Blackbaud Giving Fund operates within a broader ecosystem of corporate partners,


[workplace giving programs](https://blackbaudgivingfund.org/blog/what-is-workplace-giving-and-why-its-important-for-nonprofits/) , and nonprofit networks to ensure donations move efficiently and securely.


That infrastructure matters because it:


- Connects nonprofits to corporate and employee giving at scale


- Ensures compliance and oversight throughout the process


- Reduces delays caused by disconnected systems


- Creates a more transparent path from employee donor to nonprofit


For fundraisers, this isn’t theoretical. It directly affects how quickly you can put donations to work.
