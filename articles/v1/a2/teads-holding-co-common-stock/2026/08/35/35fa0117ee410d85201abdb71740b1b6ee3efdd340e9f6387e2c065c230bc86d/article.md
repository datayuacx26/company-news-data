# Talks With Teads | Ford on Capability-First Storytelling, Unified Brand Building, and Human-Centered Technology

In this Talks with Teads episode,[Markus Hutchins](https://www.linkedin.com/in/markushutchins/) , Senior Director of Global Brand at Ford Motor Company, shares how the company’s Ready Set Ford platform unifies brand storytelling across markets, why capability-led narratives convert curiosity into loyalty, and how Ford balances heritage and innovation to keep customers confident through change.


## **Key Interview Insights for Marketers:**


### **Capability That Converts**


A key term you’ll hear throughout this interview is “capability,” which is what Ford leads with. From Raptors to Mustangs to Broncos, vehicles are engineered and tested on more surfaces and in more conditions—off-road and on-track—so that capability shows up in every drive. Ford’s message is, “You’re more capable than you think.” In a world defined by uncertainty, Ford invites drivers to rely on themselves, and their vehicle, to get more done with confidence.


### **One Platform, One Story: Ready Set Ford**


Ford launched Ready Set Ford, its first unified global brand platform since 2012, aligning marketing across regions. Scale ensures that the brand is seen; story ensures that it’s remembered. The platform keeps the narrative consistent from creator content to dealer interactions, so that the same capability-first point of view shows up at every touchpoint.


### **Storytelling That Starts with the Customer**


Ford is shifting from demographics to psychographics, focusing on what motivates people and *how* they want to live. Content is built around real needs and passions of drivers, then tailored across channels so it “stops the scroll” without losing the throughline: Ford enables you to do more.


### **Balancing Heritage and the EV Era**


Innovation is in Ford’s DNA, from the Model T to today’s software-defined vehicles. Ford views EVs as simply the next chapter, not a departure. The brand continues to expand its EV portfolio while listening to evolving customer needs, using over-the-air updates to deliver improvements that keep vehicles feeling fresh, familiar, and future-ready.


### **Technology With a Human Center of Gravity**


The biggest forward-looking challenge and opportunity is AI. Ford’s principle is simple: use AI and digitization to cultivate relationships, not just accelerate conversion. When tech feels like efficiency for the brand alone, it misses the mark; when it helps a customer feel understood, it builds trust.


### **Measuring What Matters: Brand Momentum**


Ford tracks brand momentum with a mix of leading indicators, including branded search volume and core brand attributes—capability, passion, community, and trust. The question behind the metrics is, are people seeking Ford out on their own? Are we delivering on the expectations they have of the brand?


### **Dealer- and Creator-Led Alignment**


Ford brings creators and dealers into the same narrative system so shoppers hear and feel the Ready Set Ford promise consistently—online, on the lot, and on the road.


That alignment ensures first impressions match the test-drive experience and what owners share afterward.


### **Looking Ahead**


Ford will continue to scale a unified global story while personalizing locally—leading with capability, expanding EV confidence through software and education, and deploying AI to deepen relationships. The brand’s promise remains clear: empower people to do more than they thought possible, and back it up with vehicles that prove it every day.
