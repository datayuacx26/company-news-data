# From intelligence to execution: Introducing Govly 2.0

For over five years, Govly has been the most complete source for federal opportunity data: spanning task orders, awards, GWACs, and IDIQs.


Today we’re excited to launch Govly 2.0. Govly 2.0 combines best-in-class federal data and expanded SLED and Canada coverage with new AI-powered Agents and Workflows that do real work for you. All secured by the most trusted compliance standards in the market, including the first CMMC Level 2 certification in our category.


In other words, **Govly won’t just deliver work to your inbox. It will actually do the work.** Helping you find, pursue, and win more public sector business while you focus your time on the highest-leverage activity.


In a world where we’re all being asked to do more with less, Govly is the platform that helps you actually do it.


## Workflows: Request partner quotes automatically, and more


Automate routine capture and sales steps with visual, rule-based logic that connects data and actions across Govly.


Workflows launches with a custom-built, customer-tested Distributor Quoting Flow—designed for reseller primes and VARs to automate the process of requesting quotes from distributors by routing each request to the correct manufacturer automatically. This eliminates manual quoting steps and enables inside sales reps to complete three times more bids than non–Workflow-enabled teams.


Out of the box, Govly includes prebuilt workflows like this one, with the flexibility for customers to customize and build their own over time.


## Agents: Research, analyze, and execute automatically with AI-powered Agents


Give Agents a goal and they’ll handle the steps, from finding opportunities to generating capture materials, all on their own.


Powered by Govly’s unmatched dataset—and enhanced by your own uploaded context—Agents outperform generic AI tools by working with deeper, more relevant information.


Use Agents to dig into opportunities, summarize forecasts, generate capture plans, or uncover insights that would take hours to find manually. For example, the Government Programs Agent helps you analyze where programmatic dollars are flowing and surface opportunities aligned with your focus areas.


Govly 2.0 includes a set of ready-to-use Agents for common capture workflows, with the flexibility for customers to customize and train their own over time to fit their teams, data, and goals.


## Signals: Stay ahead of the solicitation


Signals is an automated government news intelligence system that changes how you stay informed about procurement and policy developments.


It continuously collects updates from both traditional media and first-party government sources—including budget documents, congressional orders, and agency reports—then uses AI to validate relevance and surface what matters most to you.


Starting with personalized updates tied to your saved searches and interests, Signals replaces manual news tracking with AI-curated, actionable government intelligence.


No more waiting on analysts. You’ll see critical information even if it never makes it into the trade press—so you can react early to shifts in budgets, policies, and priorities that shape your pipeline.


## Find state and local opportunities with the most complete real-time SLED data on the market


Govly pulls opportunities automatically from over 10,000 state and local sources, including key procurement offices in Texas, California, Florida, and beyond.


While other platforms rely on manual records requests that can delay postings by days or weeks, Govly’s tech-forward system updates in real time—so when a solicitation drops, it’s on Govly that same day.
