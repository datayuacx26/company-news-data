### **1. Your Skin Will Be Happier**


It takes about two seconds to hop on Google and see just how many studies link dairy consumption to acne. More dairy seems to result in more acne, both more of it and worse severity.


It hasn’t been pinpointed *why* exactly dairy causes acne, but doctors, scientists, and dermatologists point to mild milk allergies, added hormones, or lactose as the probable culprits. Whatever it is, it’s real; ask any of the millions of people who have cut out dairy and seen a positive change in their skin.


Switching to dairy-free ice cream will make dessert time guilt-free, and not in the way most people would think. A face full of zits is no myth, and with vegan ice cream, forget the worry of waking up with bad skin the next day.


### **2. Avoid Sensitivity Reactions**


**Fun fact** : about[36% of American adults](https://milk.procon.org/lactose-intolerance-by-country/) have some degree of lactose intolerance.


Lactose, a type of sugar found in dairy products, requires a naturally occurring enzyme called “lactase” to be digested. Lactose intolerance occurs when the small intestine does not make enough of this enzyme, which leaves the body unable to digest the lactose. The result is bloating, gas, diarrhea, and cramps. Let’s remember that ice cream isn’t supposed to make us *literally* scream.


Do you know what doesn’t cause uncomfortable and unpleasant symptoms? Vegan ice cream. All the creaminess, none of the cramps. Vegan ice cream: 2, dairy ice cream: 0.


### **3. Better Digestion**


Even for those who don’t have lactose intolerance, dairy is still a known culprit for less than optimal digestion.


Dairy is a trigger of various digestive conditions such as IBS, EoE, and FPIES. Hopefully, you don’t know what all or any of those conditions are. If you don’t, don’t worry, it’s not something you need to Google.


Reducing or cutting out dairy commonly results in more consistent digestion. By consistent, we mean less bloating, less gas, and a more regular “schedule.” Here we are again, vegan ice cream offering all of the goodness without any nasty repercussions.


Take the scream out of ice cream and switch to vegan ice cream.

Two words: cow farts.


Cows, along with many other livestock, emit a few different greenhouse gases through their farts. The one to worry about is methane. Worsening the matter, methane diffuses into the air more quickly than other greenhouse gases. The result is a quicker, more intense warming effect on our atmosphere.
