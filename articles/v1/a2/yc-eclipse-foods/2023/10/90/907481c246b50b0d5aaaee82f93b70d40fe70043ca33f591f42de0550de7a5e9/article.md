### **Chocolate Covered Banana Pops**


Chocolate covered banana pops are a fun, easy, and tasty DIY treat you can make in a matter of minutes in the comfort of your kitchen.


If Reese’s Cups, fruit, and popsicles had a baby, this is what it would be. This dairy-free dessert is low-calorie, requires less than five ingredients, and is made exclusively from whole foods. This recipe is so simple you’ll be making it in your sleep in no time. All you’ll need is:


- Three bananas
- One cup of vegan chocolate
- Coconut oil
- Peanut butter (optional)


Step 1: Peel bananas and cut them in half. Insert a popsicle stick or chopstick lengthwise into the bananas pieces. Place the banana-popsicles on a baking sheet and freeze for 15 minutes.


Step 2: Combine chocolate and coconut oil in a small bowl and microwave, occasionally stirring, until completely melted.


Step 3: Remove the bananas from the freezer and dip them into the melted chocolate. Feel free to dip only one side, one half, or the entire thing.


Optional: Use a spoonful of peanut butter to make drizzles over the banana pops. If you’re a PB lover and plan on trying this, we recommend doing the PB step *after* the chocolate dip. (Dipping the banana into the chocolate after it’s been covered in PB is messy…take our word for it.)


Also optional: sprinkle crushed nuts, coconut flakes, or sprinkles over the banana pops.


Step 4: Place the pops back on the baking sheet and back into the freezer for another 10-15 minutes.


There you have it. Quick, easy, vegan, *and* delicious.
