# Belly Blends Gelatin Bites Recipe

These Belly Blends Gelatin Bites are a fun, tummy-friendly snack that doubles as an activity. Made with our Pine-Apple Guava Belly Blend and just a few simple ingredients, they set into perfectly wiggly, cut-into-any-shape treats kids love and parents feel good about. They’re gently flavored, naturally colorful, and surprisingly easy to whip up. Just heat, whisk, chill, and slice!


*Recipe developed by Ashley Walterhouse of[Fit Mitten Kitchen](https://www.fitmittenkitchen.com/) for Once Upon a Farm*


## Belly Blends Gelatin Bites Recipe


**Time:** 4 hours 10 minutes


### Ingredients


- 1 Once Upon a Farm[Pine-Apple Guava Belly Blend](https://onceuponafarmorganics.com/products/pine-apple-guava-belly-blend-1) pouch
- 2 packets unflavored gelatin
- 1.5 cups organic no sugar added juice*, divided


### Directions


1. In a small saucepan over medium heat, add Belly Blend pouch and ¾ cup juice, whisk until combined. Allow mixture to come to a simmer and continue to simmer for 3 minutes.
2. Bloom the gelatin: While the pouch and juice are heating, sprinkle gelatin in a glass bowl and pour the remaining ¾ cup juice over the gelatin.
3. Once the juice and pouch have simmered, remove from heat and pour into the bowl of bloomed gelatin. Whisk until mixture is fully combined.
4. Pour gelatin liquid mixture into a lightly greased 8x8 pan and transfer to fridge for 4 hours.
5. Using your favorite cookie cutters, cut gelatin into shapes (a great task for kiddos!). Serve immediately, or store in single layer in airtight container in the refrigerator.


****Note:*** *This recipe was tested with no added sugar concord grape juice, veggie fruit blend juice, fruit punch juice and apple juice. Pineapple juice is not recommended for this recipe, as it contains enzymes that will not allow the gelatin to set.*
