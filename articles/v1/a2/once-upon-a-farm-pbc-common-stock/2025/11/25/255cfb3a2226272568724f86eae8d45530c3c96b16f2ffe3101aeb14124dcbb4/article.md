# Kid-Friendly Recipe: Apple, Cherry & Elderberry Immunity Blend Mocktails

During the holiday season—or for any reason, really—it's fun to get a little fancy with your drinks. Even more fun when the kids get to partake, too! This easy, family-friendly mocktail recipe, using our most popular[Immunity Blend](https://onceuponafarmorganics.com/collections/fruit-veggie-blends#immunity-blends) , comes together in minutes and is great for the whole family. A little fizzy, a little tart, a little sweet—and a whole lot fun and yummy.


Thanks to Ashley Walterhouse of[FitMittenKitchen](https://www.fitmittenkitchen.com/) for the recipe!


## Cherry Immunity Blend Mocktail Recipe


Serves: 4


Time: 5 minutes


### Ingredients


- 8 ounces orange juice
- 4 ounces tart cherry juice (no sugar added)
- 2 ounces freshly squeezed lime juice
- 1 Once Upon a Farm[Apple, Cherry & Elderberry Immunity Blend](https://onceuponafarmorganics.com/products/apple-cherry-elderberry-2) pouch
- 12 ounces lime seltzer water
- 1 tablespoon pure maple syrup (optional, taste test before adding)


### Directions


In a large pitcher (32-ounce capacity), add orange juice, cherry juice, lime, Once Upon a Farm pouch, and seltzer water. Stir to combine. Taste test before adding maple syrup. Stir before serving. Add apple and orange slices for garnish. Enjoy!
