# Cran-Berry Smash Cosmo Mocktail Recipe

This Cran-Berry Smash Mocktail recipe will be a *smash* hit for kids and adults. Best served in fun glasses (plastic ones for kids!) for a fancy touch. Thank you to Ashley Walterhouse of[Fit Mitten Kitchen](https://www.fitmittenkitchen.com/) for this recipe!


## Cranberry Mocktail Recipe


**Time:** 5 minutes


**Yield:** 2 drinks


### **Ingredients**


- 1/2 cup unsweetened cranberry juice
- 1/4 cup orange juice
- 3 tablespoons lime juice
- 1 Once Upon a Farm[Cran-Berry Smash pouch](https://onceuponafarmorganics.com/products/cran-berry-smash)
- 1/2 cup sparkling water


### **Directions**


In a cocktail shaker or 16-ounce glass mason jar, add cranberry juice, orange juice, lime juice, Cran-Berry Smash pouch, and sparkling water. Secure lid tightly, and gently shake before pouring into 2 drink glasses. Garnish with orange or lime slice for a Cosmopolitan vibe.


*Note: Feel free to add a splash of pure maple syrup or simple syrup, to taste, if using unsweetened cranberry juice. If using a sweetened cranberry juice blend, taste test before adding additional sweeteners.*
