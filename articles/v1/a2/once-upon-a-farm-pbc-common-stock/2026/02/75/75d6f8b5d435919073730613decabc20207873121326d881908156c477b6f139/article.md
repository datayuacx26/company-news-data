Meet Taylor Morrison, a pediatric sports dietitian and mom of two who specializes in helping young athletes fuel their bodies for performance, recovery, and growth. With a practical, family-centered approach, Taylor supports athletes and parents in building confidence around nutrition.


As an O’Farm Expert, Taylor partners with Once Upon a Farm to share trusted guidance, practical tips, and approachable nutrition education for families. Through this collaboration, she helps bring awareness to the importance of fueling growing bodies with high-quality, thoughtfully crafted foods—making nutrition feel simpler, more accessible, and empowering for both kids and parents.


## About Taylor Morrison


Taylor Morrison, MS, RD, CSSD, LDN is a pediatric sports dietitian whose mission is to help young athletes fuel when fueling feels hard. She understands how nutrition can empower athletes to perform their best and has seen first-hand the role nutrition can play in injury prevention and recovery. She also knows that fueling for sport can be confusing for many young athletes and a frustrating obstacle to their best performance.


In her private practice, Taylor educates, coaches and counsels youth athletes on nutrition for injury prevention, recovery and performance. She is passionate about helping young athletes and their families understand nutrition, specifically how it relates to fueling a young body for sports. Her mission is to give these athletes the knowledge and skills to confidently and independently make their best fueling decisions as they advance in their sport.


Taylor lives in Dallas, TX with her husband and two very active children.
