Meet Marina Chaparro, MPH, RDN, CDCES: pediatric nutrition expert, diabetes educator, and mom who knows feeding kids is about more than just nutrients—it’s about connection. With a warm, practical approach rooted in science and real life, Marina helps families feel more confident at the table.

## About Marina Chaparro


Marina Chaparro, MPH, RDN, CDCES, is a nationally recognized pediatric dietitian, diabetes educator, media spokesperson, and mom of two dedicated to helping families nourish children with confidence. She is the Founder & CEO of Nutrichicos, a bilingual pediatric nutrition practice focused on celebrating culture, fostering connection, and bringing joy back to the table.


As the host of *Messy Bites* , Marina blends evidence-based nutrition with real-life parenting—helping families cut through the noise, reduce fear around food, and raise adventurous, confident eaters. Her philosophy is simple: *less fear, more joy and connection at the table* .


With over 500 media appearances and features in outlets such as *Parents* , *NPR* , and *The Washington Post* , Marina is known for translating science into practical, relatable guidance for modern families. She holds degrees from Boston University and Florida International University and lives in Miami, Florida, with her family.
