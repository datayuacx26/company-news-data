Soft, snackable Power Wheels sandwich a homemade blueberry cream cheese frosting for a fun twist on a classic dessert—no baking required. Developed by Ashley Walterhouse of[Fit Mitten Kitchen](https://www.fitmittenkitchen.com/) , these come together in minutes and make a perfectly sweet bite for snack time, playdates, or an easy after-dinner treat.


## Blueberry Crumble "Cookie" Sandwiches


- **Time:** 5 minutes
- **Yield:** 5 sandwiches


### **Ingredients & Tools**


- 10[Blueberry Crumble Power Wheels](https://onceuponafarmorganics.com/products/blueberry-crumble-power-wheels-8-boxes) (2 per sandwich)
- 4 ounces cream cheese, room temperature
- 2 tablespoons low sugar blueberry greek yogurt
- ¼ cup freeze dried blueberries, pulverized into powder
- 2–4 tablespoons confectioners sugar (or to taste)


### **Directions**


1. Make the frosting: In a medium bowl using an electric mixer on medium speed, beat together cream cheese, yogurt and freeze dried blueberries until combined. Mix in 2 tablespoons confectioners sugar, then taste test before adding additional sugar.
2. Assemble: Add 2 tablespoons frosting onto the base of one Power Wheels bar, then top with another, creating your sandwich. Repeat process with remaining Power Wheels. *Tip: Use a medium cookie scoop to easily dispense the frosting.*


### **Notes**


- Frosting will keep in the fridge up to 3 days if you are only making a couple of sandwiches at a time. Still have leftover frosting? Spread on a blueberry bagel for a yummy treat.
