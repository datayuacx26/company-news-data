# Strawberry Shortcake Recipe With Power Wheels

Familiar, indulgent flavors, with a feel-good twist. Once Upon a Farm's Strawberry Shortcake Power Wheels soft-baked bars make up the base for this strawberry shortcake treat. Developed by Ashley Walterhouse of[Fit Mitten Kitchen](https://www.fitmittenkitchen.com/) , this sweet recipe comes together in minutes and can be made with no added sugar.*


**Sugar is an optional ingredient to sweeten the homemade whipped cream.*


## Strawberry Shortcake Power Wheels Dessert


- **Time:** 10 minutes
- **Yield:** 4


### **Ingredients & Tools**


- 4[Strawberry Shortcake Power Wheels](https://onceuponafarmorganics.com/products/strawberry-shortcake-power-wheels-8-boxes?variant=42309718409298)
- 1 cup sliced strawberries
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- ⅔ cup heavy whipping cream
- 2 teaspoons granulated sugar (optional)
- ½ teaspoon vanilla extract


### **Directions**


1. Make strawberry sauce: In a small skillet over medium heat, add sliced strawberries and lemon juice. Allow the strawberries to cook down for just 1–2 minutes, until juices start to release. Transfer to a heat proof bowl and rest on the counter for 5 minutes before transferring to the fridge.
2. Make the whipped cream: In a cold metal bowl using an electric mixer, beat together heavy whipping cream, sugar (if using) and vanilla on medium-high speed until stiff peaks form. This should take 3–5 minutes.
3. Assemble: Place one Strawberry Shortcake Power Wheels bar in a small shallow dish. Cover with whipped cream and spoon on fresh strawberries.


### **Notes**


- Feel free to use freshly sliced strawberries to top the shortcake. Cooking them down gives them more flavor and juice without adding extra sugar, compared to traditional strawberry shortcake.
- Whipped cream will keep for 1 day in the fridge. Rewhip for a minute before serving. Or, add straight to your morning coffee.
