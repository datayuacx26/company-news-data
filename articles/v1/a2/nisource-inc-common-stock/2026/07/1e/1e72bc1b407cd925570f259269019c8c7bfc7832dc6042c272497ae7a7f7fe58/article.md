# Inspiring Curiosity, Energizing the Future: NiSource Charitable Foundation Hosts Annual ‘Introduce a Girl to STEAM’ Event Across Five States

COLUMBUS, OH — The NiSource Charitable Foundation, in partnership with NiSource operating companies Columbia Gas and NIPSCO, hosted its second annual ‘Introduce a Girl to STEAM Signature Event,’ inviting more than 300 school-aged girls to explore careers pathways in Science, Technology, Engineering, Art and Mathematics (STEAM).


Aligned with the Foundation’s mission to create strong and sustainable communities where NiSource employees and customers live and work, this program aims to expand access to hands-on learning, support local nonprofits and inspire young people to someday thrive in the future workforce.


“At NiSource, we are passionate about engaging our communities and helping shape a brighter future through the power of service and outreach,” said Melody Birmingham, Foundation board trustee and executive vice president and group utilities president at NiSource. “When we create spaces where curiosity is encouraged, and girls can see themselves reflected in STEAM careers, we’re not just teaching skills, we’re opening doors.”


The girls engaged in interactive demonstrations, took part in team challenges designed by engineers and heard from inspiring women leaders in STEAM fields at the events held simultaneously in five states across Columbia Gas and NIPSCO locations. NiSource volunteers, including the company’s women’s employee resource group DAWN (Developing and Advancing Women at NiSource), organized and supported the events in Indiana, Ohio, Kentucky, Pennsylvania and Virginia.


“Our goal is to inspire girls to dream of someday making a difference in the world, using science, technology and art to solve real-world challenges,” said Reggie Fields, executive director of the NiSource Charitable Foundation. “You never know what may pique someone’s wonder and imagination. We’re proud to support programs that nurture future innovators while investing in the communities we serve.”


Local partner organizations supporting events in each state were:


- Indiana: Boys and Girls Clubs of Northwest Indiana


- Kentucky: Junior Achievement
- Pennsylvania: Girl Scouts of Western Pennsylvania
- Ohio: Girl Scouts of Ohio’s Heartland
- Virginia: Pretty Purposed


**About the NiSource Charitable Foundation**
The NiSource Charitable Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization and serves as the charitable arm for NiSource Inc. and its companies operating under the NIPSCO and Columbia Gas brands. The Foundation’s mission is to create strong and sustainable communities where our employees and customers live and work. The Foundation lives up to its mission by providing charitable grants to nonprofit organizations and by encouraging employee volunteerism. Learn more at www.NiSource.com/Foundation.
