# DNOW Announces Q4 and Full-Year 2025 Earnings Conference Call

HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Feb. 17, 2026-- DNOW Inc. (NYSE:DNOW) has scheduled a conference call to discuss the results for the fourth quarter and full-year 2025 on **Friday, February 20, 2026 at 8:00 am** (US Central Time). Financial results for the fourth quarter and the year ending December 31, 2025 are expected to be released that morning before the market opens.


Investors, analysts, and stakeholders are invited to join the call, which will be available on a listen-only basis through the Investor Relations section of DNOW’s website:[ir.dnow.com](https://ir.dnow.com/) . Participants are encouraged to log in early to register for the webcast.


For those preferring to join by phone, dial **1-888-660-6431** (North America) or **1-929-203-2118** (international), using Access Code **7372055** , fifteen minutes before the scheduled start. The call replay will be available online for 30 days following the event.
