# How Communities Can Extend Public Safety Visibility During Summer

## **The Challenge**


Summer brings larger crowds, more events, and more activity to public spaces.


Parks fill with families. Recreation programs expand. Seasonal programming, like festivals, concerts, farmers' markets, and Fourth of July celebrations, brings thousands of people together. Construction projects accelerate.[School campuses, trailheads, beaches, community pools, and entertainment districts all see more activity](https://www.nhregister.com/news/article/west-haven-beaches-close-at-sunset-22207526.php?utm_source=chatgpt.com) .


Summer can also coincide with an[increase in violent crime](https://apnews.com/article/why-is-there-more-violence-summer-shootings-d5919ea324df5dbc732baf227123f849) in some communities. National reporting has found that some forms of violence, including shootings and aggravated assaults, can rise during summer months. Holidays such as the[Fourth of July](https://www.nbcphiladelphia.com/news/local/whitemarsh-4th-of-july-parade/4196127/) often require additional planning as agencies prepare for larger gatherings and increased public activity.


Recent incidents have led some communities, including[Highland Park, Illinois](https://www.cnn.com/2025/04/23/us/highland-park-shooting-robert-crimo-sentencing) ,[and Philadelphia, Pennsylvania](https://6abc.com/post/mass-shooting-philadelphia-9-people-including-4-juveniles/15031295/) , to revisit summer event safety planning and cross-department coordination.


During the summer months, city departments need to maintain safety across more locations and in places that often were not designed around permanent security infrastructure. Many have limited access to wired power or network connectivity. Others only require additional coverage during seasonal events or periods of heavy use.


**Summer creates a citywide coordination challenge:**


- Law enforcement agencies coordinate patrols across festivals, concerts, sporting events, and holiday celebrations while continuing to answer calls for service throughout the community.
- Parks and Recreation teams oversee athletic fields, community centers, trails, and public parks where hundreds of residents gather each week.
- Public Works departments support road closures, construction projects, special events, and critical infrastructure while monitoring equipment and public assets spread across the city.
- Public Housing Authorities manage multiple properties, parking areas, playgrounds, and common spaces with teams responsible for large geographic areas.
‍


Every department faces these seasonal demands, often with leaner teams as summer vacations reduce available staff. But the challenge extends beyond staffing.


When an incident occurs, departments often spend valuable time collecting information from separate systems, reviewing video from different sources, or relying on eyewitness accounts that provide only part of the picture.


Clear visibility allows agencies to understand what happened faster, coordinate resources with confidence, and move from response to resolution more efficiently.
