# Vitesse Energy Declares $0.4375 Quarterly Cash Dividend


July 29, 2026 4:06pm EDT[Download as PDF](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_d6fc5ea25aa5d7db7c77750dc79d3549/vitesseoil/news/2026-07-29_Vitesse_Energy_Declares_0_4375_Quarterly_Cash_55.pdf)


GREENWOOD VILLAGE, Colo.--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- Vitesse Energy, Inc. (NYSE: VTS) (“Vitesse”) today announced that its Board of Directors declared its third quarter cash dividend of $0.4375 per share on its common stock, payable September 30, 2026, to stockholders of record as of September 15, 2026.


**ABOUT VITESSE ENERGY, INC.**


Vitesse Energy, Inc. is focused on returning capital to stockholders through owning financial interests predominantly as a non-operator in oil and gas wells drilled by leading U.S. operators.
