# How CAN I/O Modules Fit into Modern Vehicle Networks

## What is a CAN I/O Module?


CAN I/O Module is a digital sourcing system that can be configured and customized to ensure a smooth communication between the line of network and transferring of data, while maintaining the power loads and the overall control of a system.


## How CAN Bus I/O Modules Works in Modern Vehicles


CAN I/O modules are important in supporting modern vehicle networks by enabling distributed control, smart actuation and real-time data collection across heavy-duty and off-highway platforms. We offer various types of heavy-duty vehicle electronics such as 12-output, 20-output and RTD input models. The Industrial CAN module uses a 12-pin sealed Deutsch connector (DT04-12PA equivalent). All 6 outputs on this module may be configured as a sourcing digital output or a PWM Output. This module uses J1939 Proprietary Peer-to-Peer Messaging.


## Key Advantages of CAN I/O Modules in Heavy Duty Vehicle Networks


The use of implementing CAN Bus I/O Modules into modern vehicles are beneficial:


- **Enhancement of function and performance** offers systems and subsystems in a vehicle


- **Reduced Wiring:** By enabling the ECU to ensure quick communication and simplify vehicle assembly and reduce on cost.


- **Efficient and Robust:** Prevents faulty messages and is designed to catch and correct or terminate messages with errors.


- **Flexible and Customizable:** All 6 inputs on this module may be configured as either a sourcing digital input, a 32V analog input, a 4-20mA current-reading input, a 0-5V ratio metric input, or a frequency-reading input.
