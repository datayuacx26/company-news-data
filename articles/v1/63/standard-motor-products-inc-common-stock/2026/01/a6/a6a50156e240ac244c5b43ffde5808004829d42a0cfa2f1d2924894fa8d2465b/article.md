# How Output Modules Simplify Distributed Control in Off-Highway Equipment

Output modules provide high quality distributed control systems that ensure smarter and more modular actuation across complex vehicle systems. Our vehicle output modules are digital systems that can be configured and customized to ensure accurate communication between the line of network and transferring of data, while maintaining the power loads and overall control of a system. This module provides outputs with short circuit and overload protection as well as open load detection.


Additionally, vehicle Output modules are important in supporting modern vehicle networks by enabling distributed control, smart actuation and faster signal transmission across heavy-duty and off-highway platforms. We offer various types of off-highway equipment electronics such as digital switching systems, automotive h-bridge modules, sealed bus bar and battery separator.


## Key Features


**Efficient Signal Transmission:** Provides a quick and accurate exchange of information to enhance the overall performance of systems.


**Increased System Reliability:** Corrosion-prone wire connections and mechanical switches are eliminated, providing much more dependability and greater system performance in challenging environments.


**Flexibility:** Customizable for your application needs.


**Reduced Wiring:** Simplified wiring for easier integration in off-highway vehicles
