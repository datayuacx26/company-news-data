# SMP’s Dale Burks Explains Impact of Right to Repair on Texans

*SMP Executive Vice President & Chief Commercial Officer Dale Burks recently collaborated with the Motor and Equipment Manufacturers Association (MEMA) in publishing an op-ed focusing on the Right to Repair Act, its impact on Texans, and a call to action for Texas elected officials to support the bipartisan legislation.*
**
*Right to Repair legislation is intended to protect vehicle owners’ and independent repair shops’ right to critical information, tools, and equipment needed to repair modern cars and trucks. SMP believes that this legislation is the most pressing issue facing our industry.*
**
*The article can be read on the Texas Politics website by clicking[here](https://texaspolitics.com/2026/04/24/how-texas-leaders-can-deliver-an-affordability-victory-for-families-and-small-businesses/) .*
