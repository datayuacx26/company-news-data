# How Durable Electronics Improve Performance in Extreme Operating Conditions

SMP Engineered Solutions offer an extensive range of products that are designed to perform in extreme environments. All ranges of electronic devices are designed, manufactured and tested for the most operational reliability. These devices are specifically engineered to endure severe operating conditions through many key designs that make them trustworthy.


## Product Design Features


When looking for rugged and durable vehicle electronics, it is important to focus on key designs that support uptime, product lifespan and are dependable in any working condition.


**Environmentally Sealed**


Rugged electronic modules are weather resistant and designed with barriers to prevent contaminants such as dust, moisture, and corrosive fluids from getting to the internal components.


**Thermal Management**


Built to maintain safe operating temperature that properly dissipates generated heat while preventing hardware degradation, system failure and throttling. This ensures optimal performance and extended lifespan of a durable electronic component.


**Vibration Resistant**


Engineered and tested for durable vehicle electronics to endure mechanical vibrations throughout all working conditions.


**Shock Protection**


Internal engineered to be shock resistant and withstand impacts and mechanical vibrations.


**Electrical Protection**


- Provides robust circuit provision in over voltage and ESD protection


- Overcurrent and Fault Protection: Prevents the permanent hardware damage from short circuits.
