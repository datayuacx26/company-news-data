# Standard Motor Products, Inc. Announces Increase in its Quarterly Dividend


[Download PDF](https://www.smpcorp.com/media/kqbknxpx/q1-dividends-release-2-2-26.pdf)


New York, NY, February 2, 2026


......Standard Motor Products, Inc. (NYSE: SMP), a leading automotive parts manufacturer and distributor, announced today that its Board of Directors has approved an increase in its quarterly common stock dividend from thirty-one cents per share to thirty-three cents per share, payable on March 2, 2026, to shareholders of record on February 16, 2026.


## About SMP


With over 100 years in business, Standard Motor Products, Inc. is a leading independent manufacturer and distributor of premium replacement parts in the automotive aftermarket and a custom-engineered solutions provider to vehicle and equipment manufacturers in diverse non-aftermarket end markets. SMP sells its products primarily to retailers, warehouse distributors, original equipment manufacturers and original equipment service part operations in the United States, Canada, Europe, Asia, Mexico and other Latin America countries. For more information, download the SMP® Parts App or visit[SMPcorp.com](https://www.smpcorp.com/) .
