# SMP Named Excellent Customer Service Award Winner

We are proud to announce that SMP has once again been recognized with a significant award from a key distribution partner. On January 14, O’Reilly Auto Parts held its annual conference during which SMP was recognized with the Excellent Customer Service award for 2025.


This award is presented to suppliers who deliver exceptional service and value to O’Reilly Auto Parts each and every day. One element that was key in SMP receiving the award was responding quickly to an important request from O’Reilly. Another vendor cancelled over 50 technician training sessions, and we responded by supporting all of these training events, in addition to the 600+ SMP-hosted Real-World Training sessions already scheduled. In 2025, we also worked to improve all aspects of communication with the O’Reilly Sales Force. These efforts led to sales increases in several key categories.


To win a prestigious award from O’Reilly is an honor and reflects our ability to execute on all eight pillars of our value proposition every single day. We have partnered with O’Reilly Auto Parts for over 50 years and continue to provide industry-leading customer service, sales support, and training which will enable our continued growth over the coming years. This award is a true reflection of everyone’s efforts throughout our organization. Thank you and congratulations to everyone.
