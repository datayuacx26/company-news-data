# SMP Releases 2025 Sustainability Report

We are pleased to announce the release of our 2025 Corporate Sustainability Report, highlighting our commitment to strong corporate citizenship with a focus on enhancing our sustainability profile. The report provides insight into SMP’s initiatives, future goals, and achievements in relation to its business, employees, communities, and other stakeholders.


Eric Sills, Chairman and CEO of SMP, stated, “It is my privilege to present SMP’s 2025 Sustainability Report, a reflection of our progress and the commitments that continue to guide us. At SMP, we believe that taking care of our employees and communities is of equal importance to our business objectives. That’s why we have worked hard to create meaningful connections between our employees, their families and our communities to foster a more fulfilling and enjoyable workplace.”


Highlights of this report include:


• Inclusion in the U.S. Department of Energy’s Better Plants Initiative with commitment to reduce energy intensity by at least 25% over the next decade across our U.S. facilities and beyond
• Meeting the aftermarket’s increasing demand for high-quality parts for hybrid and electric vehicles through our comprehensive range of proven and emerging technologies
• Continued expansion of our product portfolio to provide necessary parts to keep vehicles operating safely and efficiently
• Diverting waste from landfills through prevention, recycling, and reduced packaging size and materials
• Employee collaboration driving local initiatives through charitable donations, blood drives, scholarship programs, volunteering, and financial assistance for those impacted by natural disasters
• Development of initiatives to attract and retain world-class talent, and collaboration with strategic partners to help achieve our social and workforce goals
• Continued efforts to foster a safe, supportive, resilient workplace where employees can perform at their best
