# How Modular Electronics Simplify System Architecture for OEMs

With fast-evolving electronics in the OEM market, many companies face pressure to design and simplify products that are flexible, energy efficient, easily scalable and market ready. When faced with challenges in limiting flexibility in electrical designs, many companies work to maintain the same design while implementing new functionality.


## Understanding Modular Electronics


Modular electronics are devices that monitor and control automation functions by regulating the flow of electric current for proper operations within the off-highway equipment. Integrating a modular electronic system helps simplify system architecture for OEMs by reducing wiring, faster development cycles, improved diagnostics and easier system expansion across all product lines.


## Key Benefits


Modular electronics provide various benefits within your system application. The advantages include:


-


- **Reduced Wiring:** Improves flexibility and space optimization of the control unit


- **Faster Development Cycles:** Reduce time to market by utilizing pre-engineered tested components to enhance flexibility, improve reliability and quality


- **Improved Diagnostics:** Pre-engineered and tested to enhance real-time alerts and data aggregation


- **Easier System Expansion:** More expansive as product lines evolve over time making it more adaptable for specific applications


## Conclusion


To sum up how modular electronics is fitting in simplifying OEM electronic systems, is knowing that it optimizes the equipment. It is done by regulating electrical current, monitor and control the overall function of off-highway vehicles for faster development. While reducing wiring and simplified architecture for easier expansion and increased reliability.
