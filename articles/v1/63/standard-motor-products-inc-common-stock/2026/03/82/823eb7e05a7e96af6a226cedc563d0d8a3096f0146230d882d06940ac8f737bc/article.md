# Benefits of Using RTD Input Modules for Precision Thermal Monitoring

Resistance Temperature Detectors (RTD) Input Modules are sensors that measure temperature changes as resistance changes. It converts resistance changes from the sensor into temperature information, while providing accurate signal conversion and data transmission across off-highway and industrial equipment. They are built for the harsh environments across applications in the off-highway and industrial equipment.


## **Key Performance Benefits of Using CAN RTD Module**


RTD temperature sensor provides high resolution and stability across all performances, such as:


**Reliable And Accurate Data:**


RTD is superior in long term stability in applications that require more complex control.


**Linear Response:**


The sensor provides a highly linear response that makes signal processing straightforward.


**Enhanced Diagnostics:**


Designed to detect short circuits while improving system troubleshooting.


## **Common Application**


RTD Temperature Sensors are commonly used in a wide range of heavy-duty and industrial systems such as:


- Agriculture


- Marine systems


- Construction


- Heavy Trucks


- Lawn and Garden


- Mobile Hydraulics


## **Considerations for Using RTD Temperature Sensors**


RTD Temperature Sensors offer high stability and accuracy of temperature measurements, making it reliable for most off-highway and industrial equipment. Before implementing RTD temperature sensors, it is important to consider operating temperature range, sensor wiring configuration and overall required accuracy.
