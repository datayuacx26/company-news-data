# Huber Heights Launches 24/7 Multilingual Voice AI for City Services

Huber Heights, OH – January 29, 2026


The City of Huber Heights has launched multiple 24/7 multilingual voice AI lines, deploying AI agents across departments throughout the city: including the city's front desk, zoning, public works, parks and recreation, and more – to give residents always-on access to city services in English and Spanish.


Serving a population of more than 43,000 residents, Huber Heights receives a wide range of calls every day, from bulk trash pickup and zoning questions to permits and service requests. Like most local governments, the city operates with finite staff and finite budgets, while demand from residents is constant.


In its first few months of operation, EffiGov's voice AI has handled over 70% of routine inquiries, ensuring residents receive immediate, accurate responses while freeing city staff to focus on complex cases that require human expertise.


Before deployment, EffiGov worked closely with Huber Heights staff, shadowing day-to-day operations across departments to understand existing workflows and service needs. The result is a system that works directly with municipal systems, modernizing constituent services without new hardware or major IT overhauls.


"Every call matters," said Aubteen Pour-Biazar, Co-Founder and CEO of EffiGov. "The resident calling about bulk trash pickup at 5:30pm has a legitimate need. So does the one calling about a zoning variance or a pothole report. Huber Heights trusted us with the constituent interface, the frontline of city services, and we're honored to help ensure every resident gets heard, at any time, in the language they speak."
