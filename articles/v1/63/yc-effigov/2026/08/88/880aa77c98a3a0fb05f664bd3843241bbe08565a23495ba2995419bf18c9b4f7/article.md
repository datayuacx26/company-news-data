# San Joaquin County Mosquito and Vector Control District Launches 24/7 Voice AI for Resident Service Requests


Stockton, CA – August 10, 2026


The San Joaquin County Mosquito and Vector Control District has launched a 24/7 voice AI line with EffiGov, giving residents always-on access to report mosquito problems, request inspections, and reach district staff at any hour.


Native integration


Every request the agent takes is geo-verified in **Esri ArcGIS** and filed as a **Survey123** record, straight into the tools the district’s field crews already use, with no staff rekeying.


Esri ArcGIS


Geo-verify the address


Survey123


File the request as a record


Serving more than 790,000 residents across San Joaquin County, the district fields a heavy, seasonal call volume, from mosquito complaints and requests for mosquito fish to green-pool reports and dead-bird questions. Like most vector control agencies, it runs on finite field and office staff while demand climbs through the summer.


The district went fully live, around the clock, on July 8, 2026, and the numbers followed fast. Calls now resolve in a median of just over a minute.

Before launch, EffiGov worked closely with district staff to mirror existing workflows, reviewing live calls together each week and tuning the agent so residents get accurate, consistent information about what the district does and does not do.


"When mosquito season hits, every call is someone with a real problem in their yard," said Aubteen Pour-Biazar, Co-Founder and CEO of EffiGov. "The district trusted us with the first thing a resident hears, and we're proud it now answers every one of those calls, day or night, and gets the request to the field team without anyone waiting on hold."
