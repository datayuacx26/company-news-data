# MRAM in ARINC 664 & Similar deterministic Automotive and Industrial Systems

## Everspin Magnetoresistive RAM (MRAM) in ARINC 664 (AFDX) and similar deterministic Automotive and Industrial Systems


While ARINC 664 (AFDX) is not yet the dominant standard in Low Earth Orbit (LEO) like it is in commercial aviation, it is actively being transitioned into the space domain as part of a push toward Integrated Modular Avionics (IMA) for Space. Research projects like MISSION have already confirmed the feasibility of using AFDX for spacecraft onboard data networks, demonstrating its ability to handle high-traffic, real-time communications in space environments.
