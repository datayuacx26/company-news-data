# Everspin Signs Deal With Microchip Technology to Boost Manufacturing

Everspin Technologies has signed a long-term manufacturing agreement with Microchip Technology to expand onshore production capacity for MRAM and TMR sensor products. The partnership establishes a new MRAM manufacturing line at Microchip’s Oregon facility, strengthening U.S.-based semiconductor supply chain resilience and ensuring long-term production scalability through the 2030s.
