# Edge AI Solutions, The Memory Crisis, and Sustainable AI

This episode of the Embedded Insiders podcast explores the rising challenges and innovations shaping edge AI, sustainable computing, and semiconductor memory systems. The episode features Sean Dougherty, VP of Sales at Everspin Technologies, and his insights into the growing “memory crisis,” emphasizing how surging memory demand and rising costs driven by AI workloads are straining traditional memory technologies and reinforcing the need for more efficient, high-performance alternatives, including MRAM.
