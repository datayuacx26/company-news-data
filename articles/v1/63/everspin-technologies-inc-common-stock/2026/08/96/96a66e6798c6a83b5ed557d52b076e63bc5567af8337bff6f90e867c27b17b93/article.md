# Dynamic AI Demands Drive Memory Diversity

In an EE Times article by Gary Hilson, industry experts look at how AI is changing memory requirements across data centers, networks and edge systems, and why no single memory technology fits every workload.


Everspin Vice President of Worldwide Sales Sean Dougherty discusses where MRAM fits as AI moves closer to the edge. He talks about the value of persistent memory for local inference, including retaining data through a power interruption, and how Everspin’s UNISYST MRAM can address applications that have traditionally relied on NOR flash.
