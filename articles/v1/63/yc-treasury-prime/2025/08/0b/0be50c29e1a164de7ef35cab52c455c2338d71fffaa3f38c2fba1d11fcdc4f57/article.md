# Prime Connect Spotlight: Prime Banking & Prime Analytics — Built for Banks and Their Customers

In today’s fast-evolving financial landscape, banks and fintechs demand digital-first tools that are not only functional but user-friendly and compliant. Treasury Prime’s latest offerings— **Prime Banking** and **Prime Analytics** — meet these needs head-on. From front-end customization to real-time reporting capabilities, these products empower institutions to launch branded banking experiences quickly and confidently.


‍


## **Prime Banking 🎯**


### **What is Prime Banking?**


**‍** Prime Banking is Treasury Prime’s full-featured, white-label digital banking experience. It enables banks and fintechs to quickly offer user-friendly web and mobile interfaces for account opening, money movement, and transaction management—no need to build a front end from scratch. ([Original Blog](https://www.treasuryprime.com/blog/prime-banking---the-digital-banking-experience-built-for-banks-and-their-customers) )


### **Key Highlights:**


- **Customizable UX** : Reflects your brand identity across devices.


- **Customer Onboarding** : Secure flow for both individuals and businesses, including KYC and KYB.


- **Account Management** : View balances, transactions, and account details.


- **Payments & Transfers** : ACH, wire transfers, and remote check deposit (RDC).


- **Compliance & Control** : Built-in transaction monitoring and regulatory support.


### **How it Works:**


During the Prime Connect live stream, we walked through a full onboarding experience for a fictional user, including business setup, account creation, and approval. Once onboarded, users can:


- View checking account details


- Send a $1 wire transfer in just a few clicks


- Monitor transaction activity


- Deposit checks remotely


What’s more, every interface element can be styled and branded to match the partner’s design system—offering total flexibility with zero-code customization.


## **Prime Analytics 📊**


### **What is Prime Analytics?**


**‍** Prime Analytics is an integrated reporting and visualization tool designed for both banks and fintechs. Available directly through the Treasury Prime Console, it provides real-time insights into customer behavior, transaction volumes, and compliance-related metrics.


### **Key Features:**


- **Real-Time Dashboards** : See daily and monthly activity in clear, intuitive charts.


- **Prebuilt Views** : Ledger transactions, ACH volume, balance changes, net account openings, and more.


- **Source Data Access** : Drill into tabular data, export reports, and filter by date or program.


- **Compliance Insights** : Track and export data for regulatory reporting needs.


### **How it Works:**


During the Prime Connect live stream, we highlighted how simple and intuitive the interface is, even in the staging environment. In production, users will see:


- Enhanced visualizations (line graphs, bar charts, summaries)


- Filters by transaction type, date, and entity


- Editable fields and export options for custom reporting


- Real-time monitoring without custom analytics development
