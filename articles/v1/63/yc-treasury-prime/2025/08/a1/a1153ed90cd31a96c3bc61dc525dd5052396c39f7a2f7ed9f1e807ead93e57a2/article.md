# Say Hello to Our New Sandbox Console

We’ve officially upgraded our Sandbox developer dashboard and bank console to match our new Production Console, giving you access to an enhanced experience that will make building, testing, and managing bank integrations faster and more intuitive than ever.


This upgrade isn’t just a facelift—it’s a reimagining of how you interact with The Console. When we made big changes in production earlier this year, we knew we had to do the same thing to our sandbox developer console!


### **🧰 What’s New?**


We’ve been listening closely to feedback from our developer and fintech partners, and we’ve built a new Console that brings clarity, flexibility, and power to the surface. Here’s what’s waiting for you:


- 📊 Prime Analytics


- Get deeper insight into your business with our new **embedded reporting dashboard** . With Prime Analytics, you’ll have access to powerful BI tooling—right inside the Console. In the Sandbox developer console, Prime Analytics provides a lightweight version of our more robust tool in Production.


- 💸 Enhanced Payments Control


- **Return Incoming ACH** with just a few clicks
- **Create Book Transfers** between internal accounts
- **Mark Originated ACHs as Returned** for better payment lifecycle management
- **Void Wires** —with the option to **apply fees**


- 👀 New & Improved Views


- We’ve expanded how you can see and manage your transactions and accounts:
- Incoming ACH view
- Incoming Wire view
- Check Deposits (RDC)
- FBO Table view for cleaner account oversight
(...with more features to come).


### **📅 When can you use it?**


Today! The legacy sandbox developer dashboard and bank console now redirects to[https://app.sandbox.treasuryprime.com](https://app.sandbox.treasuryprime.com/) .


More good news, you can use the same email and password as before, so need to make any changes.
