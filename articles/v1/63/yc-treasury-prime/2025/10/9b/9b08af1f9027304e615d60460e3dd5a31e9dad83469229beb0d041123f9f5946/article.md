# Treasury Prime Joins the U.S. Bank Connected Partnership Network

We’re proud to announce that Treasury Prime has joined the award-winning[U.S. Bank Connected Partnership Network](https://partnerdirectory.usbank.com/) , expanding access to our cutting-edge embedded banking technology for corporate treasury clients nationwide.


Through this collaboration, U.S. Bank clients can now access Treasury Prime’s[Bank OS platform](https://www.treasuryprime.com/products/overview) to further modernize their financial operations. The partnership also welcomes U.S. Bank into the Treasury Prime Bank Network, which now includes more than 15 leading financial institutions across the country.


#### ‍ **A shared commitment to advancing treasury management**


U.S. Bank and Treasury Prime are partnering to accelerate treasury modernization, supporting corporate finance teams through embedded experiences that drive efficiency and support scalable growth.


Through the Connected Partnership Network, U.S. Bank clients can integrate with Treasury Prime’s Bank OS to access key features, including advanced subledgering and AI-powered reconciliation tools. These capabilities help strengthen internal controls and streamline operations for back-office teams, enabling treasury leaders to operate with speed and precision.


Adding U.S. Bank to Treasury Prime’s[bank network](https://www.treasuryprime.com/blog/4-benefits-of-joining-an-embedded-finance-bank-network) also reinforces the multi-bank model at the heart of our infrastructure. Through our[OneKey product,](https://www.treasuryprime.com/newsroom/multi-bank) enterprises can connect to U.S. Bank and other[leading institutions](https://www.treasuryprime.com/blog/why-leading-financial-institutions-trust-treasury-prime-for-embedded-banking) through a single API, eliminating the need for one-off integrations and accelerating time to market.


For banks, this model lowers the operational burden of supporting embedded finance programs independently while unlocking access to a broader pipeline of fintech partners. For fintechs, it offers flexibility to scale with intention, supporting diversified banking relationships with consistent compliance and oversight across partners.


“As a recognized leader in treasury and payments innovation, U.S. Bank shares our vision for empowering clients with the technology to modernize their operations,” says Jeff Nowicki, Chief Banking Officer at Treasury Prime. “This partnership also marks an important milestone in the growth of our bank network, as we continue to expand access to top-tier financial institutions for fintechs and enterprises nationwide.”


Together, U.S. Bank and Treasury Prime are working to build a more connected ecosystem where businesses and fintechs can harness technology to build new financial experiences. It reflects an important advancement of our mission — streamlining how banks and enterprises work together by delivering infrastructure that powers the next generation of embedded finance.
