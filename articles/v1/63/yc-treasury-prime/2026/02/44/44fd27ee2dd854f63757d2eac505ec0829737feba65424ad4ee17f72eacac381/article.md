# The infrastructure behind institutional-scale investment flows

Investment platforms managing capital across hundreds of LPs and SPVs face a compounding infrastructure problem: as deal volume grows, so does the operational burden of manually reconciling wires, onboarding new relationships, and managing deposit concentration risk across a single banking partner.
