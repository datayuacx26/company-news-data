Treasury Prime CEO Chris Dean appeared on *Advancements With Ted Danson* , a television series that explores innovative technologies and emerging trends shaping industries around the world. Airing on Bloomberg TV, the program highlights advancements that are transforming the way businesses operate and serve their customers.


In this episode, Dean discusses Treasury Prime's mission to help banks modernize through technology and shares his perspective on the future of banking. From digital-first customer expectations to embedded finance and bank-fintech partnerships, the conversation explores how financial institutions can adapt to a rapidly evolving landscape.
