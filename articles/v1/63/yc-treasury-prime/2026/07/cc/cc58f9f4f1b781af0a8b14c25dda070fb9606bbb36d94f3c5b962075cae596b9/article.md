# Treasury Prime Appoints Travis Champion as Vice President of Revenue

**SAN FRANCISCO, May 6, 2026** –[Treasury Prime](https://www.treasuryprime.com/) , today announced the appointment of Travis Champion as Vice President of Revenue. In this newly unified role overseeing sales, marketing and broader commercial functions, Champion will lead the company’s go-to-market strategy as Treasury Prime continues to scale its embedded banking platform for fintechs, banks and corporate clients.


Champion’s leadership will focus on aligning sales, marketing and strategic partnerships to drive more efficient conversion, accelerate customer acquisition and expand Treasury Prime’s reach across new markets. He will also build an AI-powered, scalable revenue engine to support the company’s next phase of growth.


“Bringing Treasury Prime’s sales and marketing teams together under a single leader is a critical step in how we scale,” said Chris Dean, CEO of Treasury Prime. “Travis brings the rare combination of strategic vision and hands-on execution that this role demands. As we accelerate and deepen our bank and fintech partnerships, his leadership will be critical in shaping the next chapter of Treasury Prime’s story.”


Champion joins Treasury Prime with over 25 years of experience leading high-performing sales and marketing organizations across the fintech and financial services landscape. Most recently, he served as Head of GTM, Partnerships & Business Development at Minna Technologies, where he led the company's North America launch and grew its cardholder reach to over 100 million US cardholders, culminating in Minna's acquisition by Mastercard in 2024. Throughout his career, he has led commercial strategy across multiple high-growth phases, contributing to successful exits and increased valuations.


“Embedded banking is entering a pivotal phase, and Treasury Prime is well-positioned to help shape what comes next,” said Travis Champion, Vice President of Revenue at Treasury Prime. “The platform, the partnerships and the team here are truly best-in-class. I'm excited to help scale our go-to-market motion and bring Treasury Prime's capabilities to more banks and fintechs building the financial products of the future.”


Champion’s appointment underscores Treasury Prime’s focus on scaling growth and expanding its ecosystem of banks and fintech partners as it continues to build the leading embedded banking platform.


**About Treasury Prime**


Treasury Prime is building the future of finance. Through its cutting-edge embedded banking software, Treasury Prime facilitates seamless connections between banks and enterprise partners, equipping them with everything they need to launch innovative financial products with a strong commitment to responsible practices. Beyond its core banking offerings, which encompass accounts and payment infrastructure, Treasury Prime also provides a robust partner marketplace. This marketplace offers a wide range of auxiliary services tailored to meet the diverse needs of bank-fintech partnerships. Treasury Prime was named Best Banking-as-a-Service Platform in the Tearsheet Embedded Awards 2021 and 2022, and was named to CB Insights' annual 2021 Fintech 250 list.
