# Preparing Your Bank for Embedded Finance Success

Community banks play a vital role in the financial system, but many struggle to keep pace with shifting consumer expectations. Embedded finance offers an opportunity to evolve, allowing institutions to extend their reach and drive growth through digital channels without abandoning their community roots.
