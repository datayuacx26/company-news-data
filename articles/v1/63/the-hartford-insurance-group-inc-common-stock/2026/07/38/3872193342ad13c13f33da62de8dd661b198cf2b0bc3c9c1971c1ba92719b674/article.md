## Ripley Main Street Receives $100,000 Small Business Accelerator Grant From The Hartford


July 28, 2026


Download PDF (opens in new window)


RIPLEY, Miss., July 28, 2026 – Ripley Main Street has been awarded a $100,000 Small Business Accelerator Grant from The Hartford, in partnership with Main Street America. This grant will help develop Brown & Covington, a new small business accelerator site that will support local artisans, emerging entrepreneurs and workforce development opportunities in downtown Ripley.


Brown & Covington will give artisans a place to create and sell products while receiving business support and mentorship. The site will also provide hands-on training in production and retail operations, helping participants build practical skills.


“As a leading insurer of more than one million small businesses, The Hartford understands the vital role they serve in our communities, and we are proud to go beyond the policy to support their success” said Chris Jones, head of Small Business at The Hartford. “Through our partnership with Main Street America, we are helping remove barriers to growth by expanding access to flexible, affordable accelerator spaces where entrepreneurs can build experience, reach new customers and contribute to downtown revitalization.”


“Access to affordable space and accessible education can make or break a new small business,” said Matt Wagner, Chief Innovation Officer at Main Street America. “The Hartford Small Business Accelerator Program is helping Main Streets remove those barriers in real, tangible ways – and in doing so, they’re building more resilient, opportunity-rich communities for everyone. Through this program, Main Street programs are identifying the kinds of support entrepreneurs need and creating spaces that further their district’s and community’s goals.”


The program will launch with a cohort of six makers and support artisans working in pottery, jewelry, candles, skincare, woodworking, sewing and other handcrafted goods. It will also bring together local partners, including AbilityWorks, area high schools and heritage tourism organizations, to expand workforce development opportunities and strengthen the local small business ecosystem.


“One of the things we love most at Ripley Main Street is looking at an overlooked building or space and imagining what it could become. Brown & Covington brings that vision to life. We're turning a long-vacant historic building into a place where artisans can launch businesses, build entrepreneurial skills, and connect with customers. Our community is full of talented makers, and we're excited to help them take that next step while creating opportunities for people of all abilities. Thanks to The Hartford and Main Street America, we're creating opportunities that will strengthen downtown Ripley for years to come”, said Elizabeth Behm, Executive Director, Ripley Main Street.


Ripley Main Street’s grant is part of The Hartford's multiyear, $2 million Small Business Accelerator Grant Program with Main Street America, which supports 15 communities and approximately 1,500 small businesses nationwide.


**About Ripley Main Street**


Ripley Main Street is a nonprofit organization dedicated to building a vibrant downtown where small businesses, entrepreneurs, artisans, and the community can thrive. By transforming overlooked and vacant buildings into meaningful destinations, the organization is creating new opportunities for businesses to grow, artists and makers to share their talents, and residents and visitors to experience downtown Ripley in new ways. Guided by a long-term vision to preserve the city's historic character while fostering entrepreneurship and creativity, Ripley Main Street is helping shape a downtown known for its unique destinations, growing arts community, and support of local businesses.


**About Main Street America**


Main Street America (MSA) leads an inclusive, impact-driven movement dedicated to reenergizing and strengthening older and historic downtowns and neighborhood commercial districts through place-based economic development and community preservation. We work in collaboration with thousands of local partners and grassroots leaders across the nation who share our commitment to advancing shared prosperity, creating resilient economies, and improving quality of life.


Main Street America was founded in 1980 as the National Main Street Center®, a program of the National Trust for Historic Preservation. Over the past 40 years the MSA network has helped to generate over $115.27 billion in local reinvestment, rehab 345,801 buildings, create 815,894 new jobs, and start 181,647 new businesses in over 2,000 communities.
