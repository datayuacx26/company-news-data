## The Hartford Declares Quarterly Dividends Of $0.60 Per Share Of Common Stock And $375 Per Share Of Series G Preferred Stock


July 15, 2026


Download PDF (opens in new window)


HARTFORD, Conn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


[The Hartford](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.thehartford.com%2F&esheet=54571085&newsitemid=20260715353383&lan=en-US&anchor=The+Hartford&index=1&md5=590b2ff011c17554438e12780ba0e948) ’s Board of Directors declared a dividend of $0.60 per share of common stock, payable Oct. 2 to common stock shareholders of record at the close of business on Sept. 1.


The board also declared a dividend of $375 on each of the shares of the Series G preferred stock (equivalent to $0.375 per depository share), payable Nov. 16 to Series G preferred stock shareholders of record at the close of business on Nov. 2.
