## Emporia Main Street Receives $100,000 Small Business Accelerator Grant From The Hartford


July 28, 2026


Download PDF (opens in new window)


EMPORIA, Kan., July 28, 2026 – Emporia Main Street has been awarded a $100,000 Small Business Accelerator Grant from The Hartford, in partnership with Main Street America. This grant will help expand its successful makerspace and strengthen the entrepreneurial ecosystem in downtown Emporia.


Emporia Main Street's Fabrication Lab, a makerspace that supports entrepreneurs and small manufacturers, will benefit from the grant as the organization expands its innovation hub with additional space, new production equipment, a multimedia studio and flexible classroom space. The expansion will provide greater access to the tools, training and workspace needed to help turn ideas into thriving businesses.


“As a leading insurer of more than one million small businesses, The Hartford understands the vital role they serve in our communities, and we are proud to go beyond the policy to support their success” said Chris Jones, head of Small Business at The Hartford. “Through our partnership with Main Street America, we are helping remove barriers to growth by expanding access to flexible, affordable accelerator spaces where entrepreneurs can build experience, reach new customers and contribute to downtown revitalization.”


“Access to affordable space and accessible education can make or break a new small business,” said Matt Wagner, Chief Innovation Officer at Main Street America. “The Hartford Small Business Accelerator Program is helping Main Streets remove those barriers in real, tangible ways – and in doing so, they’re building more resilient, opportunity-rich communities for everyone. Through this program, Main Street programs are identifying the kinds of support entrepreneurs need and creating spaces that further their district’s and community’s goals.”


“We are incredibly grateful to The Hartford and Main Street America for recognizing the work taking place in Emporia through this highly competitive Business Accelerator Grant. Their investment is much more than an investment in equipment or space – it is an investment in the entrepreneurs, makers, small businesses, and creative talent that drive our regional economy,” said Casey Woods, Emporia Main Street Executive Director.


Emporia Main Street's grant is part of The Hartford's multiyear, $2 million Small Business Accelerator Grant Program with Main Street America, which supports 15 communities and approximately 1,500 small businesses nationwide.


**About Emporia Main Street**


[Emporia Main Street](https://emporiamainstreet.com/) is a nationally recognized, accredited Main Street America program dedicated to strengthening the economy and quality of life in Emporia and the surrounding region through business development, historic preservation, community engagement, and entrepreneurship. Guided by the Main Street America Four Point Approach® – Design, Promotion, Business Enhancement, and Organization – the organization provides entrepreneurs and existing businesses with practical resources including business consulting, alternative financing, market data, building redevelopment assistance, business incubation, workforce and educational programming, and an innovative Fabrication Lab that helps transform ideas into market-ready products. By combining economic development with community collaboration, Emporia Main Street works to create a vibrant downtown, support locally owned businesses, and build long-term prosperity for the region. Emporia Main Street is also a Kansas Main Street program.[Learn more about Kansas Main Street here.](https://www.kansascommerce.gov/program/community-programs/main-street/)


**About Main Street America**


Main Street America (MSA) leads an inclusive, impact-driven movement dedicated to reenergizing and strengthening older and historic downtowns and neighborhood commercial districts through place-based economic development and community preservation. We work in collaboration with thousands of local partners and grassroots leaders across the nation who share our commitment to advancing shared prosperity, creating resilient economies, and improving quality of life.


Main Street America was founded in 1980 as the National Main Street Center®, a program of the National Trust for Historic Preservation. Over the past 40 years the MSA network has helped to generate over $115.27 billion in local reinvestment, rehab 345,801 buildings, create 815,894 new jobs, and start 181,647 new businesses in over 2,000 communities.
