## **How to not lose your kids at** ® **Disney World**


Fifty thousand people in one park. Crowds move fast, and so do kids. Have the plan before you walk in.


-


**Pick a meet-up landmark** in each park and a simple rule: *if we get separated, we go here.* Say it out loud to your kids on the way in.


-


**Set a Life360 Place Alert** at that spot. You'll get a notification the moment someone arrives — no shouting across a crowd required.


-


**Tell your kids to find a cast member** — the ones with name tags — if they can't find you. ®Disney cast members are trained for exactly this.


If separation happens, open Life360, check the map, and head to your meet-up point. Calm wins every time. A plan beats luck.
