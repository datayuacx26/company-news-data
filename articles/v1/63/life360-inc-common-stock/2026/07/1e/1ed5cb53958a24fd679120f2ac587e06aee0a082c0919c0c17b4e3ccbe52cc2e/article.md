# DISNEY AND LIFE360 LAUNCH A BRAND NEW COLLECTION OF NEW DESIGNED LIFE360 TILE TRACKERS

***Disney's Mickey & Friends Lend Their Timeless Charm to these Disney designed Tile trackers to Help Families Everywhere Keep Track of Their Treasures***


****


**JULY 15, 2026 - SAN FRANCISCO, Calif. & BURBANK, Calif.,** Oh boy! Disney’s Mickey Mouse and Minnie Mouse are now featured on a brand new collection of Tile trackers that help families keep track of the things they love most. Today, Life360, the family connection and safety app, announced a new collaboration with Disney on a collection of Tile trackers. Featuring Disney’s iconic characters, Mickey Mouse and Minnie Mouse, the new lineup of limited-edition Bluetooth trackers brings a touch of Disney wonder to the peace of mind fans get from protecting their belongings with advanced location-sharing features.


Launching during the busy family travel season and just in time for back-to-school preparations, the collection will help Disney fans keep track of the essentials powering every adventure. Whether attached to luggage, passports, strollers, or backpacks, Life360 Tiles ensures nothing gets left behind.


“Disney is iconic when it comes to magical family experiences. And, Life360 exists to make everyday family life better,” said Mike Zeman, Chief Marketing Officer at Life360. “We are beyond thrilled to bring this collaboration to life, with Mickey and Minnie themed Tile trackers that are sure to bring both delight and peace of mind to Disney fans around the world.”


The limited-edition collection features:

-

-


**Tile Starter Pack** - Minnie Mouse ($49.99)


-


Includes a Tile and Tile Slim

Life360 Tile trackers seamlessly blend style and functionality. With a chic design and advanced capabilities, the discreet devices feature an extended 350-foot Bluetooth range, a 3-year battery, a ring feature for faster locating, and an SOS button for an extra layer of peace of mind. Compatible with both Android™ and Apple® devices, all Tile products are fully integrated into the Life360 app, bringing your most important people, pets, and belongings together on one easy-to-use map.
