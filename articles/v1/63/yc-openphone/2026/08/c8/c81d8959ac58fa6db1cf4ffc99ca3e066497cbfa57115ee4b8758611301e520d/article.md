# RingCentral SMS: Texting features, pricing, & key limitations

Evaluating what’s possible as far as texting with RingCentral? Here’s what you should know. You can send SMS and MMS messages to US and Canadian numbers in every RingCentral plan. But your texting is capped at a monthly limit. These limits start at 25 texts per user per month in the base plan. Even if you upgrade, you’re limited to 200 texts per user per month in the most expensive plan. If you exceed your monthly limit, RingCentral charges you a per-message fee for every message you send.


You should also know that you may run into delays in being able to complete texting registration using RingCentral. You have to register your virtual number with The Campaign Registry, and customers report this process can take several weeks. International outbound texting is also available, but it isn’t included in any plan. Plus, you have to contact support to send international texts.


In this article, we’ll cover everything you need to know about texting with RingCentral’s SMS. And if it turns out to be too limited for your business, we provide a RingCentral alternative at the end of the guide.


##


How much does RingCentral charge for SMS?


RingCentral’s pricing starts at $20 per user per month and lets you send SMS and MMS messages to customers. The platform offers three pricing plans, but each puts a monthly limit on the number of texts each user can send without paying extra:


- **RingEX Core plan** : 25 texts for $20 per user per month
- **RingEX Advanced plan** : 100 texts for $25 per user per month
- **RingEX Ultra plan** : 200 texts for $35 per user per month


Rates vary for sending SMS, MMS, and toll-free messages beyond the monthly limit. What you pay depends on whether you’re registered for A2P 10DLC, so costs can get confusing if you’re waiting for one or more numbers to be approved.


Here’s a breakdown of[RingCentral pricing](https://www.quo.com/blog/ringcentral-pricing/) for SMS. Keep in mind that it has different rates for registered and unregistered numbers. Registered numbers are numbers that have already registered with The Campaign Registry. Unregistered refers to messages you might receive from numbers that haven’t registered with The Campaign Registry.

While RingCentral’s per-message overage charges might seem minor at a glance, they add up. Here’s an example: A 10-person team on the Core plan sends an average of 200 texts per user per month. That’s 4x the monthly limit allocated in this plan. That means overage charges of $48 per month, since RingCentral charges you for messages sent and received. Your monthly phone bill goes up by 20% on overage charges alone.


International rates are only available in the admin portal, which you need a RingCentral account to access. If you don’t have one, you’ll need to contact their sales team to see what you’d pay. If international texting is a requirement for your business, this can be frustrating. It’s another example where you can’t estimate your monthly phone bill.

### 1. Users cite RingCentral’s texting registration process as difficult


Many users report problems with[RingCentral’s TCR registration](https://www.quo.com/blog/ringcentral-tcr-registration/) process. The Campaign Registry (TCR) is a third-party organization mobile carriers like AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile use to review[10DLC](https://www.quo.com/blog/what-is-a2p-10dlc/) applications. If you use a virtual phone number to text customers, registration is required.


Here’s what the TCR registration process looks like:


1. **Register your business** , including your legal name and Employer Identification Number.
2. **Provide your SMS campaign details** , including examples of messages you plan to send.
3. **Link your numbers** in the RingCentral admin portal.
4. **Confirm and submit your SMS registration** .


But with RingCentral, users said they are[waiting months for approval](https://www.reddit.com/r/RingCentral/comments/1hbirwj/is_there_anyone_who_successfully_completed_tcr/) ,[getting rejected multiple times](https://www.reddit.com/r/RingCentral/comments/1es0ty7/sms_tcr_registration_rant/) , or[being declared ineligible](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.ringcentral.com?search=TCR&stars=1) based on unexpected policy changes.


Delays in the TCR registration process can lead to issues with messages getting to your customers. Service providers may filter or block your texts, or you could be fined as much as $1,500 per message for violating federal compliance regulations. Take a look at these[RingCentral reviews](https://www.quo.com/blog/ringcentral-review/) on the registration process:

### 2. Shared texting requires an add-on


The benefit of using VoIP phones is everyone on your team has access to the same number. While this is true of calling in RingCentral, it isn’t the case with texting. You don’t get instant access to a shared team messaging inbox in your RingCentral plan. Without the ability to text as a team, it can be incredibly difficult to stay on top of customer conversations.


To access shared texting, you need to purchase the Business SMS Booster add-on, which costs an additional $25 per month. Many VoIP providers offer shared texting by default in their platforms, so you don’t have to pay extra to access this elsewhere.


*“* *Once we finally got approved, we then found out that the functionality we actually needed — a shared* *SMS* *inbox for our team — requires an additional paid add-on per user, which was not clearly explained during the sales process.” –*[RingCentral user on TrustPilot](https://ie.trustpilot.com/reviews/69d529a59c60c2414d4dbe87)


### 3. RingCentral stores calls and texts separately


The RingCentral app separates calls and texts into different views, making it harder for you and your team to keep conversations in context. You have to click between the “Phone” and “Text” tabs to get a full picture of customer conversations, which means it takes longer to review customer histories — and you could miss important information in the process.


### 4. You can’t send or receive MMS from toll-free numbers


Having a toll-free number gives your business a more professional look and lets customers text your business for free. RingCentral offers toll-free numbers for calls and SMS, but you can’t use them for MMS messaging.


This limits what’s possible in a couple of ways:


- You can’t send PDFs, audio files, or documents, which can be helpful for things like product demos, order confirmations, or onboarding instructions
- Customers aren’t able to send videos, images, or other attachments to troubleshoot problems


If you want to send MMS, you’ll have to use another number, which can get confusing for you, your team, and your customers.


### 5. RingCentral caps how many messages and recordings you can keep


Calls you save with RingCentral’s automatic call recording can only be stored for 90 days. The platform caps total recordings at 100,000 calls per account. On-demand recordings are limited to 200 per user.


SMS message storage is also restricted to 5,000 total messages in each user’s SMS inbox. If your business sends high-volume SMS, you could hit this limit quickly and lose important customer conversations.


To increase your storage limits, you’ll have to use RingCentral Archiver, which is only available to users on the Ultra plan. It lets you back up your data to a third-party cloud storage service like Dropbox or Google Drive. That means additional setup, extra storage fees, and more time spent searching through archives to find what you need.


### 6. Vanity phone numbers require an expensive setup fee


Vanity numbers are brandable, memorable, and convenient. If you want to set one up through RingCentral, you’ll have to pay a one-time setup fee of $30, plus a recurring $4.99 monthly fee.

FAQs


**How do you set up SMS on RingCentral?**


You can start sending SMS messages on RingCentral after you register your number with The Campaign Registry. However, you can also[set up your number to receive SMS texts](https://support.ringcentral.com/article-v2/Setting-up-phone-numbers-to-receive-text-messages.html) before your registration is complete. Here’s how, as provided in RingCentral Support:
1. **Sign in to the**[Admin Portal](https://service.ringcentral.com/) **.**
2. Click the **Phone System** tab.
3. Go to **Business SMS** , then click **Inbound SMS** .
4. Click the button under Select phone numbers to choose the numbers you want to enable for inbound SMS.
5. Click **Add numbers** in the popup window. You can click **Remove** if a phone number should stop receiving text messages.
6. Select the phone numbers you want to set up to receive text messages.
7. Click **Add** .
8. Click Done.


**Does RingCentral support MMS?**


RingCentral supports MMS messaging for local phone numbers in the US and Canada. However, toll-free numbers on RingCentral don’t support MMS.


**Can you send bulk SMS with RingCentral?**


Yes, you can[send a bulk text](https://support.ringcentral.com/article-v2/Sending-a-bulk-text-message-in-the-RingCentral-app.html) to up to 10 recipients at once in RingCentral.


**Why is my RingCentral SMS not sending?**


The most common reason why your RingCentral SMS isn’t sending is incomplete A2P 10DLC registration. Until your brand and campaign are approved, carriers may filter or block your business texts. Other causes include exceeding your plan’s monthly message limit or texting an unsupported number type.


**Is RingCentral SMS HIPAA compliant for healthcare texting?**


RingCentral can support HIPAA-compliant texting, but only under a signed Business Associate Agreement. HIPAA compliance with SMS depends on team usage. Healthcare teams should train their staff on best practices before sending patient texts in RingCentral. Get a full breakdown on whether RingCentral is HIPAA compliant.


**Does RingCentral AI offer SMS features?**


Yes,[RingCentral AI](https://www.quo.com/blog/ringcentral-ai/) offers SMS drafting and editing features. However, to access this feature, you have to upgrade to the RingEX Advanced plan.
