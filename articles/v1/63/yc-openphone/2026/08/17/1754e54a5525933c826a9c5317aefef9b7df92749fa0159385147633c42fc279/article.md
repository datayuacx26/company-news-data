# RingCentral call recording: How it works, its limits, & what to know

Looking for a business phone that offers phone call recording? With RingCentral, you’ll get access to call recording in all of its plans. On-demand recording is available on every plan, and automatic recording is available on the Advanced plan and higher.


But there’s a catch you should know: recording storage retention. On the Core and Advanced plans, recordings are kept for only 90 days before they’re permanently deleted. Even in its most expensive tier, this data retention period applies. If you’re a business that needs permanent access to your call recordings, you should look for a different business phone provider.


In this article, we explain how RingCentral’s call recording features work and how they fall short for most businesses. We’ll also share a[RingCentral alternative](https://www.quo.com/blog/ringcentral-alternatives/) you should consider so you can build better relationships with your customers.

##


How to record calls in RingCentral


Setting up call recording in RingCentral takes a few steps in your phone number settings. You can set them up the same way whether you have on-demand or automatic call recording. Here’s how:


1. Sign in to the **Admin Portal** .
2. Click the **Phone System** tab.
3. In the left panel, click **Company Call Handling** .
4. Under **Call recording** , turn on the toggles for either **On-demand call recording** or **Automatic call recording** .


Keep in mind that in many US states, you’re legally required to inform the other party that a call is being recorded. RingCentral lets you configure an automatic announcement, but you’ll need to enable it manually in the Admin Portal.


Once recording is enabled, the real question becomes how long you can access those recordings and how much storage you actually get.


##


How to access, listen to, and download RingCentral call recordings


You can access call recordings in RingCentral’s desktop and web apps, the mobile app, and in the admin portal. Here’s how:


### RingCentral desktop & web app


**


1. In the navigation bar, click **Phone** .
2. Click the **Recordings** tab, then select a recording. The details will appear in the main panel.


For any recording, you have these options:


- **View** . View the caller’s name and phone number, with options to call, fax, text, or message the caller. You can also add the caller to your contacts list or block their number.
- **Play** . Click the **Play** icon to listen to the recording. Use the slider to fast-forward or rewind.
- **Download** . Click the **Download** icon to save the recording as an MP3 file.


### RingCentral mobile app


**


1. In the bottom toolbar, tap **Phone** .
2. Tap the **Recordings** tab.
3. Tap any recording to view the details and access the controls.


For any recording, you have these options:


- **View.** View the caller’s name and phone number, with options to call, text, send a message, or view their profile.
- **Play.** Play or stop the recording.
- **Speaker.** Switch the audio source to speaker.
- **Copy.** Tap and hold their name to copy the phone number.
- **Share.** Send the recording to another person.


### RingCentral admin portal


**


1. Sign in to the **Admin Portal** . If it shows **Admin Portal** at the top right, click the dropdown, then click **My Extension** .
2. Click the **Messages** tab.
3. In the left panel, click **Recordings.**


Choose what you want to do with a call recording:


- **Play.** Click the **Play** button to listen to any recorded call. Use the slider to fast-forward or rewind.
- **Download.** Click the three-dot **More** icon, then select *Download* .
- **Forward.** To send the recording through email, click the three-dot **More** icon, then select *Forward* .


🚨


**Note:** RingCentral doesn’t have a bulk export feature to download all your recordings. You’ll have to download each recording individually if you want to be able to access them longer than 90 days.


##


RingCentral call recording limitations


Here are the biggest limitations of RingCentral’s call recording features:


### **1. 90-day call recording retention**


If you can’t store call recordings in your business phone, should you record them at all? With RingCentral, you risk losing your recordings 90 days after you record them. Even if you upgrade to its Ultra plan, you can’t change this 90-day limit.


If you have long sales cycles or need to resolve a customer dispute six months down the road, you won’t have access to valuable context in your recordings.


RingCentral recommends you download your recordings so you have access to them, but there are two challenges with this approach.


1. **No conversation context.** You don’t have the context you need in your business phone workspace. Recordings are downloaded with some metadata, but you lose context about the texts and calls you had before and after a specific recording.
2. **No bulk export.** You have to download your recordings individually from your RingCentral account. You can’t export recordings in bulk, which adds another task for your team. It isn’t enough to export your recordings either. You have to store, organize, and label them so your team can actually use them.


### **2. Recording volume caps**


RingCentral doesn’t just delete recordings after 90 days; it limits how many recordings you can store in your account. Automatic call recordings are capped at 100,000 per account. On-demand recordings are capped at 200 per user.


For high-volume departments in sales and support, these limits can affect your team’s usage over time. Team members may skip recording calls to save workspace storage. Your CRM hygiene will be affected, which can affect your sales performance metrics too.


### **3. Recording announcement friction**


To[stay compliant with call recording](https://www.quo.com/blog/call-recording-disclosure/) laws in different US states, RingCentral offers call recording announcements in its platform. However, these announcements are intrusive when talking to your customers. You can play a voice message to notify callers that the call is being recorded. But the only other option is a periodic audio tone, which means that callers will constantly be reminded the call is being recorded.

FAQs


**Where are RingCentral call recordings stored?**


Call recordings are stored in RingCentral’s servers and are accessible in the admin portal and RingCentral apps. Recordings can be downloaded for local storage, but there’s no bulk export tool. Automatic recordings are capped at 100,000 per account, and on-demand recordings are limited to 200 per user.


**When should you consider RingCentral call recording alternatives?**


Here’s a quick checklist to help you answer whether you should go with a RingCentral alternative for your call recording needs:
• If you need to access recordings after three months for training or compliance purposes.
• If your call volume is growing rapidly and you don’t want to worry about recording storage in your business phone.
• If you want more flexible call recording announcements.


**Why is my RingCentral call recording not working?**


Here are a few things you can do to make sure your RingCentral workspace is recording calls:
• Verify your plan supports the recording type you want.
• Confirm call recording is enabled in the Admin Portal for the right site and numbers.
• Check that each user has permission to record calls.


If older recordings are missing, remember they’re deleted after 90 days. If problems persist, contact RingCentral support.


**Is RingCentral call recording legal?**


Whether you can legally record a call depends on your state and the other party’s location, not on RingCentral. Some US states require only one-party consent; others require two-party consent. RingCentral lets you record and configure a recording announcement, but compliance is your responsibility. Confirm the consent laws that apply to you before recording.
