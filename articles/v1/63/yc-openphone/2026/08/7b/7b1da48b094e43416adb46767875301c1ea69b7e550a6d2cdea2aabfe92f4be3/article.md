# 11 best call forwarding app options in 2026 for Android & iPhone

A call forwarding app can help you avoid missing client calls and prevent you from keeping them on hold for too long.


The best call forwarding app depends on your setup: for a business team, Quo is the top pick because it forwards calls to a shared number your whole team can answer, includes unlimited US and Canada calling and texting, and starts at $15 per user per month. For personal iPhone use, the free Divert Calls app handles[basic single-number forwarding](https://www.quo.com/blog/how-to-forward-calls/) . Native iPhone and Android settings also forward calls for free, but only to one number and without team features, recording, or integrations.


In this article, we’ll compare the best call forwarding apps that provide a better calling experience for your team and customers. Then we’ll share key scenarios where other business phone features can work better than a free call forwarding app.


##


What is call forwarding software?


**


Call forwarding software redirects incoming calls from one number to another. Call routing is the rules layer on top: it decides which people or numbers ring, in what order, and when (by business hours, by caller, or by which teammate is free). A[ring group](https://www.quo.com/features/ring-groups) is the set of people a routed call can reach. Basic apps do one-number forwarding; a business phone system does routing and can[forward calls to multiple numbers](https://www.quo.com/blog/call-forwarding-to-multiple-numbers/) .


If you’re unavailable or using a different device, a call redirecting app ensures the call still reaches you or someone on your team. That way, important calls don’t slip through the cracks, whether you’re in a meeting, on the go, or off the clock.

How to choose the best call forwarding app


We looked at six important factors to find the best call forwarding apps for small businesses:


1. **Cost** : As a small business, every dollar counts. Are you paying a flat rate, or are you charged per minute or feature?
2. **Flexibility** : Can you forward calls based on different scenarios, like when you’re busy or away? As you know, speed to lead matters, so the right app lets you control who gets the call and when, not just blast it everywhere. Call management features like call screening and voicemail-to-email give you even more control over how each call gets handled.
3. **Reliability** : You need calls to go through every time. Does the platform reliably forward calls without delays or dropped connections?
4. **Ease of use** : You shouldn’t need a manual to set up call forwarding. Is the app’s interface intuitive and easy to manage from your devices?
5. **Scalability** : Can your team use it as you grow? Look for tools that let you add users, assign different numbers, and build out call routing workflows. If half your team is on desk phones and half on forwarded cell numbers, you don’t have a system — you have a patchwork. Scalability and integrations are what pull it back together.

Call forwarding works by redirecting incoming calls from one number to another. Calls can be redirected by changing the recipient’s phone settings or notifying the recipient’s cellular network.


Call forwarding was more common in the pre-internet era, where calls had to be routed across physical telephone lines. Now, virtual phone numbers make it possible to call multiple recipients at once with[shared numbers](https://www.quo.com/blog/how-to-share-a-business-phone-number-with-your-team/) and[simultaneous ring](https://www.quo.com/product/call-routing/simultaneous-ring) features.
