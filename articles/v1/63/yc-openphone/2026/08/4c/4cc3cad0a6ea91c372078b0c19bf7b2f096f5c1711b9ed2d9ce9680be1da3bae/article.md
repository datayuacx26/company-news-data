# 7 Plumbing field service software tools worth using

A basic scheduling app can put those jobs on a calendar. But once you’re running a few trucks, you need more than that. You need to know:


- Who called and what happened on their last visit
- Which plumber is free
- Whether their last invoice‌ was paid


That’s where[plumbing business software](https://www.quo.com/blog/plumbing-business-software/) comes in. It makes it possible to handle scheduling, dispatch, customer records, and payments, and do it all from one place.

##


**How to pick the right plumbing management software for you**


Before you buy any software, here’s a quick checklist of questions you can research and ask providers to help you get started.


**Pricing and contracts**


- Is pricing per user or a flat monthly rate?
- How does the pricing change as your team grows?
- Will you need to upgrade your plan or pay extra as you add more office staff or field technicians?
- Are there features locked behind higher tiers that you’d realistically need to manage jobs efficiently?
- Is there a free demo or free trial to test the software first?
- Can you cancel month-to-month, or is there a minimum contract obligation?


**Setup and onboarding**


- How long does the setup take for a team your size?
- Can you import customer records, equipment history, job notes, and service history into the platform?
- Can you import existing parts lists and flat-rate pricing books?
- What training is available to help your office team learn the system?


**Day-to-day usability**


- Is the system easy to use for both office staff and plumbers?
- Can technicians use the mobile app while they’re on-site?
- Does the app work offline in basements or low-signal areas?


**Integrations**


- Does it connect directly to your accounting software and payment processor?
- Can it integrate with the other plumbing business software you already use?


**Support**


- What customer support channels can you use after you sign up?
- What are the support hours?
- Can you reach a live person if something goes wrong during evenings, weekends, or emergency calls?


##


What features should you look for in plumbing field service software?


Finding the right plumbing field service software can be difficult when so many tools offer similar features. Focus on the features below to narrow down your options and find the right fit for your team.


- **Real-time field-to-office sync.** Your office shouldn’t have to call a plumber mid-job just to get an update. Look for software that keeps job notes, photos, parts used, and job status synced between the field and office.
- **Integration with your business phone system.** A lot of plumbing work starts with a phone call. Once your field service software connects to your small business phone system, a customer’s calls, texts, and job history all show up together.
- **Mobile app with offline access.** Plumbers won’t always be in spots with good signal. Your app should keep working offline, then sync everything once you’re back in range.
- **Smart scheduling and dispatching.** When several jobs come in at once, dispatchers need to know who’s free, nearby, and qualified. Look for tools that make it easy to assign work and adjust the schedule when an emergency comes in.
- **CRM and service history.** A plumber shouldn’t show up to a repeat job without knowing what happened last time. A CRM that shows previous jobs, work completed, and customer notes in one place helps them know what they’re walking into.
- **Quoting and invoicing or accounting integrations.** Plumbing jobs may involve giving customers good-better-best options for a repair or replacement. Look for software that supports flat-rate estimates and lets customers approve them on-site. It should also turn approved estimates into invoices and sync them with accounting tools like QuickBooks.
- **Inventory management.** Parts can be spread across several trucks and your warehouse. Look for software that tracks where each item is and updates inventory as technicians use parts on jobs.


**Reporting and job costing.** Track labor and material costs against what you charged. This shows you which jobs are profitable and helps you price similar work in the future.

**FAQs**


**What software do plumbing contractors use?**


Depending on their needs and size, plumbing contractors typically use platforms like Jobber, Housecall Pro, and ServiceTitan. These tools handle scheduling, dispatching, and live technician tracking. Many contractors also use business phone systems like Quo for customer calls and texts. Plus, they use accounting software like QuickBooks to manage invoices, payments, and bookkeeping.


**How much does field service management software cost?**


Plumbing field service software costs vary depending on whether you’re a solo plumber or running a team. Individual plans from providers like Jobber and Housecall Pro start at around $29-$59 per month. Team plans cost more, typically ranging from about $150 to $300 per month or more, depending on the number of users and which features you need.


**How does plumbing service software help to grow your business?**


The benefits of plumbing business software include faster scheduling and better client relationships. It dispatches plumbers efficiently, sends automatic reminders, and lets you collect payment on-site. That saves time, improves cash flow, and frees up your team to take on more work.
