# Top 10 customer callback software + features & pricing

Missed calls are a lot like dishes in the sink: easy to ignore one and impossible to look past once they pile up. The difference is, a sink full of dishes won’t cost you money — unless you run a restaurant; missed calls will. Often, it comes down to long wait times. The longer callers sit on hold, the more likely they are to hang up, leading to[abandoned calls](https://www.quo.com/blog/abandoned-calls/) , frustrated customers, and deals you don’t close.


One solution is to use callback software that lets customers request a callback rather than wait on hold. But this doesn’t solve the core issue: callers still have to wait to reach a rep who can help. Platforms that offer voice agents, shared numbers, call routing, and texting automations tackle wait times head-on so fewer calls are missed in the first place *.*


This guide compares the best customer callback software platforms for modern businesses, including their pricing, features, and pros and cons. It explains how traditional callback features work, then highlights other tools you can use to reduce missed calls.

**FAQs**


****What is customer callback software?****


Callback software lets callers request a callback instead of waiting on hold in a call queue. This can help reduce wait times in call centers dealing with high call volumes. But many small businesses find it works more as a temporary fix than a long-term solution. Instead, they use shared numbers to split responsibility for incoming calls, as well as AI voice agents automatically answer the phone 24/7 to prevent callbacks.


****What are the benefits of using customer callback software?****


Here are some of the biggest benefits of customer callback software:


**– Reduce missed opportunities.** If a caller can’t wait, a callback gives them another way to connect instead of hanging up and moving onto a competitor.
**– Improve customer experiences.** Getting a callback instead of waiting on hold can make frustrating situations feel more convenient to callers.
**– Manage busy periods.** Callbacks let you spread conversations throughout the day rather than making every caller wait in the queue. This ensures your team isn’t handling a high volume of calls for their entire shift.


That said, callback software still asks customers to wait for someone to get back to them. Many businesses now use AI voice agents instead, so every call is answered right away.


****What are the disadvantages of using customer callback software?****


Customer callback software isn’t a perfect solution to high call volume. Here are some challenges to consider before deciding if it’s the right fit for your business:


**– Customers still have to wait.** Callbacks replace waiting on hold with waiting for your team to call back. If customers need help right away, waiting for someone to call back could be frustrating.
**– Potential customers may choose another business.** Callers requesting quotes or booking estimates are likely comparing multiple options at once. Leads may opt for the competitor who answered the phone first. So if you don’t call back quickly, you could be leaving money on the table.
**– Your team has very little context.** Callback requests typically include a name and phone number. But you’re unlikely to have additional context, like why they’re calling or what service they need. Without more information up front, your team might have to ask the customer to explain everything again. This could make conversations feel slower and less personal.
**– You can still miss the callback.** By the time your team can call a customer back, they may no longer be available. This can cause you to waste time on multiple callback attempts, create more follow-up work, and lead to longer resolution times or lost customers.


****What are the types of customer callback software?****


Not all customer callback software works the same way. Different platforms help with different parts of the process, from reducing the need for callbacks to making it easier for customers to request one. Business phone systems like Quo offer features so you don’t need to worry about callbacks in the first place. These include features like shared phone numbers,[call waiting](http://quo.com/blog/call-waiting) , AI receptionists, ring groups, and auto-replies. Other customer callback software, like CallHippo and CallPage, offers features that let your customers request callbacks, such as widgets you can place on your website.
