##


How to choose the right RingCentral alternative


Start with why you’re switching. The most common reasons businesses move away from RingCentral include:


- Clunky user experience and steep ramp-up
- Features bundled into expensive tiers you don’t need
- Poor support experiences
- Storage limits on calls and texts.


Once you know your top pain point, weigh providers on these six criteria:


1. **Reliability:** System issues like downtime can hurt your reputation. Over time, it leads to lost customers and directly impacts your bottom line. Opt for providers with consistent connections and call quality.
2. **Value for money:** How much does the[VoIP](https://www.quo.com/blog/what-is-a-voip-phone/) system return for each dollar invested? Does it save you enough money by reducing costs and offering flexibility?
3. **Budget:** Low pricing shouldn’t be the most important thing on your list, but you should avoid hidden fees and excessive costs.
4. **Third-party integrations:** Check whether a provider has integrations with the rest of your tech stack. At Quo, we’ve seen how the right[business phone integrations](https://www.quo.com/integrations) can save hours of manual work.
5. **Ease of use:** Complex systems can mean increased spending on training staff. The result could be a lower ROI.
6. **Customer support:** Is customer support easy to reach? They should be able to offer quick solutions when you need them most. Through testing different providers, we’ve seen firsthand how easy it is for teams to adopt systems with a simple setup and features.

How to cancel your RingCentral account


Once you’ve decided to move to a different VoIP provider, you can choose to cancel or port your RingCentral phone numbers. Here’s how to cancel your RingCentral account:


1. **Check your contract status.** Ask your RingCentral account manager for a copy of your contract. You may have to cover early termination or cancellation fees.
2. **Download your data.** Export call logs, recordings, contacts, and any SMS history you need to keep. RingCentral does not guarantee data access after cancellation.
3. **Port your numbers first.** If you’re keeping your phone numbers, initiate the porting process with your new provider BEFORE canceling RingCentral. You may lose your numbers permanently if you cancel before porting is complete.
4. **Contact RingCentral support.** Call RingCentral’s billing department to request cancellation. They’ll ask you for:


- The main phone number associated with the company account
- The answer to your company account’s security question
- The last 4 digits of the company account’s credit card number


**Note:** Your account will be disabled for 30 days before being canceled. If you want to reactivate your account during this period, please call RingCentral support.
