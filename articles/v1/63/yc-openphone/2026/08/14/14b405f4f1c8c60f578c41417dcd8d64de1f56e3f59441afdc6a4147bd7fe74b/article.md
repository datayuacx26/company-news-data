# RingCentral AI: Features, pricing, and limitations you should know

If you’re evaluating RingCentral as your business phone platform, you’re likely wondering what RingCentral AI features are available.


Yes, RingCentral offers AI features in its business phone platform. They’re available in three different packages. First, you can use RingCentral’s AI Assistant in RingEX plans. This assistant offers features like call and video meeting transcription, SMS thread summaries, and writing and translating texts. Second, you can use RingCentral’s AI Receptionist, or AIR, to handle incoming calls. This feature is available as a standalone offering or as an add-on to RingEx. Finally, you can get RingCentral’s AI Conversation Expert, or ACE. It offers AI-powered conversation insights, call coaching, and comprehensive call summarization.


In this article, we’ll look at[RingCentral’s pros and cons](https://www.quo.com/blog/ringcentral-pros-and-cons/) , including AI and standard features, where it shines, and where it falls short. We’ll also look at a top alternative that may work better for your business needs.


##


RingCentral AI features: A complete breakdown


RingCentral has three main artificial intelligence offerings: RingEX AI Assistant, AIR, and ACE. These sound great in theory, but you’ll want to take note of their costs and limitations before signing up.


### 1. RingEX AI Assistant


The RingEX AI Virtual Assistant is RingCentral’s built-in productivity helper that’s bundled with their business phone platform. It transcribes phone calls, helps write text messages, and recaps team chats. It can even translate for your international customers.


Here are the virtual assistant features you can find in each of RingEX’s plans:


- **Core: $20 per user per month**


- AI video meeting transcriptions, translations, and summaries
- Phone call transcription, real-time note-taking
- Unread message summaries


- **Advanced: $25 per user per month**


- SMS drafting and editing
- SMS translation
- Writing SMS messages to team threads
- Team message translation


- **Ultra: $35 per user per month**


- Real-time notes for calls that take place on hardphones


### 2. RingCentral AI Receptionist


RingCentral’s AIR offers 24/7[AI phone answering services](https://www.quo.com/blog/best-ai-phone-answering-services/) that can:


- Schedule appointments
- Answer common questions
- Route calls to your team


You can customize its personality to match your communication style and use its automated SMS feature to answer specific customer inquiries.


Here are some of the key features you can find in RingCentral’s AI Receptionist:


- 24/7 call handling with automatic call routing
- Appointment scheduling, lead capture, and SMS follow-ups
- Multilingual conversational support
- Call queue integration
- SOC 2 and HIPAA compliant


AIR is available in two RingCentral packages:


1. **Standalone:** $49 per month, includes 100 minutes of call time
2. **Bundled:** $39 per month for 100 minutes of call time, bundled with RingEX Core


Before you sign up for an AIR plan, keep in mind a few limitations:


1. **Per-minute pricing.** AIR charges businesses for every call minute. Once you exceed your included minutes, it charges $0.50 for every additional minute. Further, RingCentral rounds up your calls, even for under-30-second interactions. Here’s a real user review:


*“A one-second AI receptionist pickup is considered a full minute. They also got rid of my robocall blocking feature, and I started getting robocalls again. I knew it was deliberate because they knew it would eat up my AI receptionist minutes so they could charge more.”* —[RingCentral review on Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6a469892afd8a1b778250b37)
2. **Lack of call forwarding to AIR.** RingCentral call forwarding isn’t flexible enough to accommodate AIR. You can route incoming calls to AIR, but you can’t forward unanswered calls to it. That limits your ability to use AIR as a backup call routing option.


### 3. RingCentral AI Conversation Expert


ACE is RingCentral’s call analysis tool that records customer interactions and tells you whether your customers sound satisfied, frustrated, or another[sentiment](https://www.quo.com/blog/customer-sentiment/) . It also updates your CRM automatically and gives your sales team coaching tips based on what happened during calls.


Several of the key features you can find in RingCentral ACE include:


- Coaching and performance analysis
- Deal and call scoring
- Automated summary and highlights
- Multi-level insights and reporting
- CRM and productivity suite integrations


ACE is available as an add-on to your RingEX plans. Pricing starts at $60 per user per month.


Before you sign up for ACE, keep in mind these limitations:


- **Add-on exclusive.** RingCentral only offers ACE as an add-on to its existing plans. It doesn’t include ACE in any existing RingEX offering. If you want to access ACE features, you’ll have to budget an additional $60 per user per month.
- **Gated features.** ACE’s conversation intelligence and analysis features are gated behind an add-on. However, these features are often offered by other VoIP providers in their plans. You might end up paying extra for features that you should already be able to access.

##


RingCentral AI limitations


It might seem like RingCentral’s AI product offerings give you everything you need in your business phone. However, there are some major limitations you should keep in mind:


### 1. No MCP or Claude and ChatGPT integrations


RingCentral doesn’t offer a Model Context Protocol, or MCP, to businesses. An MCP is an open standard that allows AI models like Claude and ChatGPT to connect directly to business tools and data sources. For phone systems, MCP support means an AI model can read call histories, search contacts, pull transcripts, and take action inside the phone platform without custom API development.


Since it lacks an MCP, you can’t connect your RingCentral workspace to Claude or ChatGPT to help you analyze your calls. If RingCentral had an MCP, you wouldn’t need to sign up for ACE to conduct call analysis. You could simply share your call transcripts and message threads with Claude, and it could analyze them for you.


### 2. Unpredictable overage fees


Each RingCentral AI has a different pricing structure. AIR has a per-minute pricing model. ACE charges teams for every user with access to its features. With such complicated[RingCentral pricing](https://www.quo.com/blog/ringcentral-pricing/) , it’s hard to know how much you’ll pay each month.


Here’s an example to show you how the math works out. A five-person HVAC company adds the AI Receptionist to their RingEX plan. Then a July heat wave hits, and the phone doesn’t stop ringing. AIR handles 200 calls in July, averaging two minutes each, for a total of 400 minutes.


- **AIR** **Base cost:** $39 per month for 100 minutes
- **Overage fees:** 300 minutes at $0.50 per minute = $150
- **Total AIR cost for July:** $189


The AIR add-on now costs more than 4x its advertised price. It’s also more expensive than RingEX that month.

### 3. Limited AI call recording storage


AIR might seem like a reliable way to capture your incoming calls 24/7. However, RingCentral limits call recording storage to 90 days. That means your AIR call recordings won’t be stored more than three months after they take place.


Even after recordings disappear from your RingCentral account, you’ll still have access to AI call summaries and transcripts. But you won’t have the full recording context to understand what was discussed. Plus, AI call notes are removed from your RingCentral workspace after 12 months. If you need to store recordings and call notes for compliance and to resolve customer disputes, you should consider a different platform.

##


RingCentral AI security and privacy: What you should know


RingCentral doesn’t use your data to train its models and doesn’t let third parties train on it either. AI call recording audio is discarded after transcription, message summaries are deleted once read, and drafts aren’t stored. Also, admins can turn the assistant off for specific users.


But if you’re in healthcare or another regulated industry, the AI layer has gaps the phone system doesn’t:


1. **HIPAA coverage for the AI Assistant isn’t clear.** RingCentral’s HIPAA document names every service its Business Associate Agreement covers. ACE, AI Receptionist, AIR Pro, AI Quality Management, AI Supervisor Assist, and AI Agent Assist all appear. The AI Assistant doesn’t. If you’re a healthcare business, it’s worth double-checking with the RingCentral team whether this is the case.
2. **Certifications cover the phone platform, not the AI.** HITRUST applies to RingEX, RingCX, and the RingCentral app. The annual SOC 2 plus HIPAA Security Rule audit covers RingEX and RingCX. You should check with the RingCentral team whether these apply to its AI features, as well as subprocessors.
3. **Nothing flags sensitive data for you.** RingCentral’s AI doesn’t recognize or classify special categories of data. You effectively take on the obligation to handle them lawfully. It has no PHI detection or redaction. If a patient shares a diagnosis on a transcribed call, it’ll be included in a stored transcript that outlives the audio.

FAQs


******How do you enable RingCentral AI features?******


RingCentral AI features are enabled by subscribing to the right plan tier or add-on, then turning them on in the Admin Portal. AI call notes and transcription come with the Advanced plan or higher, and AI message writing requires the Ultra plan. ACE and AIR are separate paid add-ons you activate from the admin dashboard. There is no single toggle that unlocks all of RingCentral’s AI features at once.


******Does the RingCentral free trial include AI features?******


RingCentral’s free trial gives you access to AI features related to calls. SMS is not available in its free trial, so you can’t use any AI features related to messaging.
