# How to forward calls: iPhone, Android, & landline

**To forward calls on iPhone:** go to Settings > Apps > Phone > Call Forwarding, then enter the number you want calls sent to.


**On Android:** open the Phone app, tap Settings > Calls > Call Forwarding, and add your forwarding number. If you don’t see the setting in your phone’s menu or use a landline, dial


***72** followed by the forwarding number to turn forwarding on, and


***73** to turn it off on most US carriers. If you use a virtual phone system, call forwarding is usually managed from a web or mobile app.


No one can be available 100% of the time. But even if your business phone is out of reach or you’re too busy to answer calls, you still need to be accessible to keep customers happy. Learning how to forward calls is a simple way for smartphone and desk phone users to avoid missed calls. But if you’re running a business, call forwarding has limitations. There are more flexible options worth knowing about.


This guide explains how to forward calls on iPhones, Androids, office phone systems and basic landlines. You’ll also learn a better method of call forwarding for when the limitations just aren’t worth it.

##


What is call forwarding?


**


Call forwarding automatically redirects incoming calls from one number to another. It’s set in advance and sends all calls to a single destination. It’s different from call routing, which directs calls across multiple options, or[call transfer](https://www.quo.com/product/call-forwarding/call-transfer) , which you do manually during a live call. You can use call forwarding to send calls to your mobile when you’re away from your desk, or to a colleague when you’re in a meeting.

How to forward calls by device


Call forwarding is useful when you’re too busy to pick up. But keep in mind there’s no way to forward business calls exclusively if you use the same number for both business and personal use.


### How to forward calls on an iPhone


Open **Settings** , go to **Apps** > **Phone** > **Call Forwarding** , toggle it on, and enter the number you want calls sent to. To turn it off, toggle it back off. For screenshots, dual SIM setup, and conditional forwarding options, see the full guide to[call forwarding on an iPhone](https://www.quo.com/blog/call-forwarding-iphone/) .


**💡** Forwarding calls doesn’t automatically forward texts. Learn[how to forward a text on an iPhone](https://www.quo.com/blog/how-to-forward-a-text-on-iphone/) .


### How to forward calls on Android


Open the **Phone** app, tap the **three-dot menu** > **Settings** > **Calls** (or Supplementary Services on some models) > **Call Forwarding** , choose your forwarding condition (always, busy, unanswered, or unreachable), and enter the destination number. To turn it off, return to the same setting and select Turn off. For screenshots and Pixel-specific steps, see the[full](https://www.quo.com/blog/call-forwarding-android/) guide to[call forwarding on Android](https://www.quo.com/blog/call-forwarding-android/) .


**💡** Forwarding calls on Android mobile phones won’t forward your texts. Learn[how to forward a text on Android](https://www.quo.com/blog/how-to-forward-a-text-on-android/) .

Manual star-code forwarding only works if someone remembers to dial it. The morning that person is on an odd shift or out sick, calls quietly stop forwarding — and you never know what you missed. That’s a single point of failure most growing businesses can’t afford.


Beyond the human error risk, basic forwarding has hard limits:


- It only moves calls, not texts.

FAQs


******What are the different types of call forwarding?******


Some common call forwarding types include:
**– Unconditional forwarding** : Every incoming call forwards to another number or destination.
**– Conditional forwarding** : Calls forward only when a specific condition is met, like:
**– Busy forwarding** when you are already on a call
**– No answer forwarding** after a set number of rings
**– Unavailable forwarding** when the line has no signal or is offline
**– Selective forwarding** : Only calls from specific numbers, contacts, or groups are forwarded.
– **Time-based forwarding** : Calls forward based on schedules, like after-hours, weekends, or holidays.


******How does call forwarding work?******


Call forwarding reroutes an incoming call away from the original destination to a different destination you choose.
At a high level:
1. Someone calls your business number.
2. Your phone system checks whether forwarding rules apply, like forward when unanswered.
3. The system places an outbound call to the forwarding destination, like another mobile phone.
4. When the forwarded destination answers, the caller and the recipient are connected.


**How much does call forwarding cost?**


The cost to use call forwarding varies depending on your service provider and plan, with some plans including it for free and others charging a per-minute fee. There is no additional cost to forward calls on Quo.


******Which caller ID do you see on forwarded calls?******


From the caller’s side, the call rings normally. There’s no message or indication that their call is being forwarded. In most setups, the person receiving the forwarded call sees the original caller’s number, so they know who is calling and can call back if needed.
That said, caller ID behavior can vary based on:
– The carrier receiving the forwarded call
– Whether the forwarding setup supports passing the original caller ID through
– Any privacy settings or compliance rules


****Why isn’t call forwarding working?****


The most common reasons call forwarding fails include:
– You’re out of your carrier’s network range
– You entered the forwarding number incorrectly
– Your carrier doesn’t support forwarding to the type of number you’re trying to reach, like international numbers
– You’ve activated conflicting rules, like forwarding all calls while being on Do Not Disturb
If you set it up through your phone’s settings and don’t see the option, try using your carrier’s dial codes instead. When in doubt, contact your carrier directly.

**Can you forward calls to international numbers?**


It depends on your carrier or phone system. Some carriers support international call forwarding, but it typically comes with international calling rates applied to every forwarded call — so costs can add up quickly.

**Are there security risks with call forwarding?**


The main risk is SIM-swap fraud, where an attacker contacts your carrier and impersonates you to redirect your calls to a number they control. Once your calls are forwarded to them, they can intercept two-factor authentication codes and gain access to your accounts.
The simpler version is social engineering: someone posing as IT support or your phone provider asks you or a teammate to set up forwarding directly.
To protect yourself: set a PIN or passcode with your carrier so account changes require verification, periodically check your forwarding settings to confirm nothing has been changed without your knowledge, and never set up call forwarding based on an unsolicited request.
