# Call forwarding not working: Troubleshooting guide

If call forwarding isn’t working, the most common causes are an incorrect forwarding code or number, a carrier that doesn’t have forwarding enabled on your plan, conditional forwarding set by mistake so calls only divert when you’re busy or unanswered, or a weak signal that stopped the code from registering.


The quick fix: cancel unconditional call forwarding by dialing ***73** on Verizon, ***73#** on AT&T, or **##21#** for T-Mobile, restart your phone, re-enter the forwarding number, and place a test call. **On iPhone** , check Settings, Apps, Phone, and Call Forwarding. **On Android** , open Phone, Settings, Calls or Supplementary Services, and Call forwarding. If it still fails, your carrier may need to reset forwarding on their end.


Keep reading for common reasons call forwarding isn’t working, the fixes, and a more reliable alternative for your business.


##


Quick fixes if call forwarding is not working


Start here. These steps clear up the most common problems in a few minutes:


1. [Turn off call forwarding](https://www.quo.com/blog/turn-off-call-forwarding/) , then re-enter the star code and forwarding number, double-checking every digit.
2. Dial the star code for your carrier to cancel all call forwarding: ***73** on Verizon, ***73#** on AT&T, or **##21#** for T-Mobile, then re-add forwarding in your phone’s settings.
3. Restart your phone.
4. Toggle airplane mode for 10 seconds to refresh the network connection.
5. Check that Do Not Disturb, Focus, or Quiet Mode isn’t silencing incoming calls.
6. Place a test call from another phone to confirm forwarding is working.


##


Why call forwarding isn’t working


If the quick fixes didn’t do it, one of these may be the culprit:


- **Wrong code or number.** A mistyped digit can disrupt forwarding.
- **Your plan doesn’t support it.** Some carrier plans don’t have forwarding enabled by default.
- [Conditional call forwarding](https://www.quo.com/blog/conditional-call-forwarding/) **is set instead of unconditional.** Calls only divert under specific conditions — when you’re busy, don’t answer, or are unreachable — rather than every time.
- **Weak cellular signal when you entered the code.** The code has to register with the network, and a poor signal can stop that.
- **Invalid MMI code or an “unexpected response from network” error.** Reset network settings and try again.
- **Network congestion.** Heavy traffic on the carrier’s network can delay or drop the request.
- **Forwarding to an incompatible number** , such as some VoIP or international numbers.
- **A recent software update** changed or reset your settings.
- **The SIM is seated incorrectly or damaged.**


##


How to fix call forwarding on iPhone


Here’s how to fix[call forwarding on iPhone](https://www.quo.com/blog/call-forwarding-iphone/) on iOS 17 or later:


1. Go to **Settings, Apps,** **Phone,** and **Call Forwarding** , and confirm it’s on and the number is correct.
2. If the toggle is missing, your carrier manages forwarding. Use the carrier’s star code instead.
3. As a last resort, reset network settings. Note that this clears saved Wi-Fi, cellular and APN, and VPN settings.
4. On a dual-SIM iPhone, pick the right line first — forwarding is set per line.


##


How to fix call forwarding on Android


Here’s how to fix[call forwarding on Android](https://www.quo.com/blog/call-forwarding-android/) :


1. Open the **Phone app,** **Settings,** **Calls** or **Supplementary services,** and **Call forwarding** , and confirm it’s on and the number is correct. On Google Pixel phones, tap **Calling accounts** and select the correct SIM card.
2. If your settings won’t save, the carrier may manage forwarding server-side — set it through the carrier’s code.
3. On a dual-SIM Android, select the correct SIM or line for forwarding and make sure it’s in the active slot.


##


How to check if call forwarding is active


Not sure whether forwarding is even on? Confirm it three ways:


- Dial ***#21#** to see your unconditional forwarding status on most mobile carriers.
- Open the call forwarding settings menu on your phone.
- Call your landline or mobile number from another phone and see where it rings.

FAQs


****Why does call forwarding only work sometimes?****


You likely set conditional forwarding, which only diverts calls when you’re busy, don’t answer, or are unreachable. To forward every call, set unconditional forwarding: for Verizon, it’s *72 followed by the 10-digit forwarding phone number; for AT&T, it’s *72, followed by a forwarding number, and then #; for T-Mobile, it’s **21*1 followed by the forwarding phone number, then #.


****Why do my calls ring once and go to voicemail?****


The fix is to increase the number of rings before voicemail in your phone or voicemail settings. It can also happen when your phone is in Do Not Disturb or Focus mode, has no signal, or is turned off, since calls go straight to voicemail in those cases. Check those first, then adjust your ring time if the call still drops too fast.


****How do I check if call forwarding is on?****


Dial *#21# on most mobile phones to check your unconditional forwarding status or open your call forwarding settings. On a landline, call your number from another phone.


****Call forwarding stopped working after a software update. What should I do?****


Re-enable forwarding in your settings, restart the phone, and reset network settings. If the problem persists, your carrier or device manufacturer may need to address it.


****My dual-SIM phone won’t forward calls. Why?****


Forwarding is set per line, so select the correct SIM or line in settings and make sure it’s in the active slot. Only the line that supports forwarding will work.
