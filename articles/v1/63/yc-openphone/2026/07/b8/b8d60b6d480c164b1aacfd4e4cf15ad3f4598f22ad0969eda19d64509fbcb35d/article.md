# How to set up call forwarding on iPhone in 6 easy steps

To forward calls on iPhone, go to **Settings** > **Apps** > **Phone** > **Call Forwarding** , toggle it on, and enter the number to forward to. iPhone forwards every call by default. To turn on conditional forwarding for when the line is busy, unanswered, or unreachable, dial your carrier’s code. To check whether forwarding is on, look for the green toggle in Settings or try dialing *#21#. To turn it off, toggle Call Forwarding off in Settings.


In this article, we’ll show you how to set up call forwarding on an iPhone in seconds. Plus, you’ll learn about a better alternative so you can ditch call forwarding altogether. On an Android? See[call forwarding on Android](https://www.quo.com/blog/call-forwarding-android/) instead.


##


How to forward calls on iPhone


Learning[how to forward calls](https://www.quo.com/blog/how-to-forward-calls/) to another number or device is a must for maintaining a work-life balance.


Here’s how to set up iPhone call forwarding on iOS 17 or later in six steps:


1. Open the **Settings** app from your iPhone’s home screen.
2. Go to **Phone** — search for it under Apps.
3. Select **Call Forwarding** .
4. **Toggle on** **Call Forwarding** **.** It turns green when active.
5. Select **** **Forward To** and enter the destination phone number for your calls.
6. Tap **Back** and double-check the forwarding number.


After you’ve turned on[call forwarding](https://www.quo.com/product/call-forwarding) , all incoming calls will be redirected to your forwarding number until you turn it off.


### How to check if call forwarding is active


Go back to **Settings** > **Apps** > **Phone** > **Call Forwarding** . If the toggle is green, forwarding is on, and you’ll see the destination number listed below it. You can also try dialing *#21# to check call forwarding status, though this may not work on every carrier.

How to set up conditional call forwarding for iPhones


By default, iPhones forward all incoming calls when you turn on call forwarding. But what if you only want to forward calls when you don’t answer? Or if your phone number is busy or unreachable? That’s where conditional call forwarding comes in.


Conditional call forwarding means your iPhone only forwards calls based on specific conditions. For example, when your number goes unanswered or is busy. Here’s how you can set up call forwarding for your iPhone:


**For T-Mobile users:**


- **Forward calls if unanswered:** **61*1+PhoneNumber#


- Turn off unanswered call forwarding: ##61#


- **Forward calls if not reachable:** **62*1+PhoneNumber#


- Turn off not reachable call forwarding: ##62#


- **Forward calls if number is busy:** **67*1+PhoneNumber#


- Turn off busy call forwarding: ##67#


*Source:*[T-Mobile self-service short codes](https://www.t-mobile.com/support/plans-features/self-service-short-codes)


**For AT&T users:**


AT&T wireless uses standard GSM codes for conditional forwarding:


- **Forward calls if number is busy** : **67*+PhoneNumber#


- Turn off busy call forwarding: ##67#


- **Forward calls if no answer:** **61*+PhoneNumber#


- Turn off no-answer call forwarding: ##61#


- **Forward calls if not reachable:** **62*+PhoneNumber#


- Turn off unreachable forwarding: ##62#


*AT&T’s own support docs only publish*[forwarding codes for landline/U-verse service](https://www.att.com/support/article/u-verse-voice/KM1000459/) *. AT&T wireless doesn’t publish its own codes, but its network reportedly uses the standard GSM codes above.*


**For Verizon users:**


- **Forward calls when unanswered or busy:** Dial *71 followed by the 10-digit forwarding number
- **Forward all calls:** Dial *72 followed by the 10-digit number you want to forward calls to
- **Turn off conditional call forwarding:** Dial *73


*Source:*[Verizon star codes](https://www.verizon.com/support/pound-star-codes/)


**On an MVNO (Mint, Cricket, Visible, etc.)**


These budget carriers run on a major network, so use the codes for their parent carrier: Mint and Ultra Mobile on T-Mobile, Cricket on AT&T, Visible on Verizon. Confirm with your provider’s support docs, since a few restrict or remap these codes.

How to turn off call forwarding for iPhones


To[turn off call forwarding](https://www.quo.com/blog/turn-off-call-forwarding/) , toggle off **Call Forwarding** in the phone **Settings** . You can also change the forwarding number as needed.


If you’ve set up call forwarding by dialing a carrier’s code, you can turn it off with a similar code. Here are the different carrier codes to turn off iPhone call forwarding:


- **T-Mobile:** Dial ##21# to turn off all-call forwarding, or ##004# to reset every forwarding type at once
- **AT&T:** Dial *73 to cancel all-call forwarding
- **Verizon:** Dial *73 to stop forwarding calls

##


Limitations of the iPhone call forwarding feature


Apple’s call forwarding feature may get the job done. However, it has some drawbacks you should keep in mind:


1. **Manual control only:** You need to remember to turn forwarding on and off manually. It doesn’t automatically route calls based on whether you’re in a meeting or are available. You have to remember when to forward and when to not forward calls throughout the day.
2. **Network dependency:** iPhone call forwarding can only be set up when you’re in range of your carrier’s network.
3. **No call separation:** Both personal and business calls get forwarded. You can’t route calls to different people or route only certain types of calls.
4. **No conditional call forwarding:** You can’t set forwarding rules like “forward when busy” or “forward during specific hours” unless you use star codes.

FAQs


****What’s the difference between call forwarding and call waiting?****


Call forwarding redirects incoming calls to another number.[Call waiting notifies](https://www.quo.com/blog/call-waiting/) you of incoming calls while you’re already on a call.


****What happens when you turn on call forwarding on an iPhone?****


When you turn on default call forwarding on an iPhone, all incoming calls are redirected to your forwarding number.


With conditional call forwarding, you can determine when you want to forward calls to another number. For example, you can specify if you want to forward calls when:
• You don’t answer the phone
• Your number is busy
• Your number is unreachable


To confirm call forwarding is on, check that the toggle is green in Call Forwarding settings or dial *#21#.


****Can I set up call forwarding to an international number on my iPhone?****


You can set up[international call forwarding](https://www.quo.com/product/call-forwarding/international-call-forwarding) on iPhones, but you’ll pay international rates for forwarded calls. The person calling you pays normal rates, but you’re charged for the international leg of the forwarded call.


****How do I share access to a Quo number with my team?****


Share access to a Quo number with your team by[inviting team members through the Quo dashboard](https://support.quo.com/core-concepts/administration/members#adding-team-members) or mobile phone app.


****How much does call forwarding cost on iPhone?****


Domestic call forwarding is usually included in your phone plan, but some carriers may charge you per minute or per forwarded call. You may also be charged extra for long-distance or international call forwarding. Your best bet is to check with your provider to be sure. With Quo, call forwarding is included at no extra cost.


****Does call forwarding also forward text messages?****


No, call forwarding only works for phone calls. If you want to[forward text messages](https://www.quo.com/blog/how-to-forward-texts/) , you’ll need a separate app or service that supports SMS forwarding.


**Can you forward calls from only certain numbers on iPhone?**


No, the iPhone’s built-in call forwarding is all-or-nothing. When you turn it on, every incoming call is forwarded; when you turn it off, none are. There’s no native way to forward calls from specific numbers while letting the rest ring through, or to send different callers to different people. Doing that requires[custom call routing](https://www.quo.com/product/call-routing) rules, which iPhone’s native feature and carrier codes don’t offer.


**What does the person calling me see or hear?**


The caller hears normal ringing and usually can’t tell the call was forwarded; they reach the forwarding number or its voicemail.


**How do I forward iPhone calls directly to voicemail?**


You can’t route calls straight to voicemail through iPhone’s built-in forwarding settings. It only supports forwarding to another phone number, not to voicemail directly. To actually send calls straight to voicemail — no ring, no delay — you’d need a virtual phone number app like Quo with customizable call routing rules.


**Why is call forwarding greyed out or not working on my iPhone?**


This is usually one of three things: your carrier doesn’t support call forwarding on your plan, you’re on Wi-Fi Calling or a network type that blocks the feature, or there’s a typo in the forwarding code if you’re dialing it manually. Restarting your phone or toggling Wi-Fi Calling off can sometimes resolve it immediately.
