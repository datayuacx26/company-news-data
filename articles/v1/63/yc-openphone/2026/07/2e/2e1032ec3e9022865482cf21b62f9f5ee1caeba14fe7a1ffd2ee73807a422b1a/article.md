# How to set up a business phone number that forwards to your cell

Ever tried juggling two phones? Even if you haven’t, you can probably understand how annoying it would be. If you run a business or have a business phone,[carrying two phones](https://www.quo.com/blog/carrying-two-phones/) is quite common. But it doesn’t have to be.


To get a business phone number that forwards to your cell, you have two options. **With a traditional carrier (** AT&T, Verizon), dial ***72** from your business line, enter your cell number, and wait for the confirmation tone; dial ***73** to turn standard call forwarding off. **With a virtual number app** like Quo, you sign up, pick or port a number, and route calls to your cell in the app, no star codes. ##


What is business call forwarding?


**


[Business call forwarding](https://www.quo.com/product/call-forwarding) routes incoming calls from your[business phone number](https://www.quo.com/blog/get-business-phone-number/) to another number. The forwarding number can be a[local phone number](https://www.quo.com/local-numbers) , a virtual number, a mobile number, or a voicemail system.


You get two routing options with business call forwarding: unconditional call forwarding and[conditional call forwarding](https://www.quo.com/blog/conditional-call-forwarding/) .


- **Unconditional call forwarding** forwards all your calls as long as you have call forwarding enabled. This is useful when you’re on vacation and need to forward your calls to an external[answering service](https://www.quo.com/answering-service) or a backup number.
- **Conditional call forwarding** forwards calls based on the conditions you’ve set. For example, you can turn off[call forwarding during business](https://www.quo.com/product/call-routing/business-hours-call-routing) hours and/or forward calls after hours.


##


How to set up a business number that forwards to your cell phone


There are two ways to forward business calls to your cell:


### 1. Forward calls with a traditional cell carrier


If you already have a[second phone number](https://www.quo.com/blog/get-second-phone-number/) for your business, you probably got it from a traditional cell carrier like AT&T or Verizon.


Forwarding business calls to your cell is easy with a traditional carrier. Here’s how to[forward calls with AT&T](https://www.att.com/support/smallbusiness/article/smb-local-long-distance/KM1194925/) :


1. Dial ***72** and listen for the dial tone.
2. Enter the number you want to forward calls to.
3. Wait for the confirmation tone. After the confirmation tone, the system will automatically place a call to the number to which calls will be forwarded. If the forward-to party answers the call, the feature is activated.
4. To[turn off call forwarding](https://www.quo.com/blog/turn-off-call-forwarding/) , dial ***73#** or toggle call forwarding off in your phone’s settings.


With[Verizon](https://www.verizon.com/articles/verizon-unlimited-plans/call-forwarding-everything-you-need-to-know/) , the process is the same. You can activate call forwarding by dialing *72 with your Verizon device and entering the 10-digit number that you want to forward your calls to. On[T-Mobile](https://www.t-mobile.com/support/plans-features/self-service-short-codes) , you dial ****21*1** , enter the phone number, and press **#** to set up call forwarding **.**


Depending on the carrier you’re using, you could also set up conditional call forwarding. This allows you to route your call somewhere else if the number is busy or you can’t answer the phone. Just keep in mind most traditional carriers don’t offer business hour settings to their customers.


Another issue with this method: you don’t know when you’re receiving a direct or a forwarded call. This means you won’t be able to distinguish between incoming calls with caller ID (or know how to answer when you pick up the phone). Plus, texts don’t forward along with calls and your personal number will be exposed to clients.

FAQs


****Can you forward a business phone to a cell phone?****


Yes, you can. You can[add a business line to your cell phone](https://www.quo.com/blog/add-business-line-to-cell-phone/) using a traditional carrier or buy a virtual number from a VoIP service provider. Alternatively, you can purchase a shared phone number that everyone can access from their devices and internet connections.

When you[sign up for Quo](https://my.quo.com/signup) , you can get a business phone number in minutes. Then, download the Quo app on your cell phone to start using your number.


****Can a landline number be transferred to a cell phone?****


Yes, you can[port your landline number](https://www.quo.com/blog/port-landline/) to your mobile phone. Once you inform your existing carrier and select a virtual number provider, you can send the information to your new provider. Expect the number to port in five to 30 business days (depending on your service).


**Will callers know the call was forwarded?**


Callers won’t know either way. With both carrier forwarding and a virtual number, the person dials your business number and the call reaches you. They never see that it routed to your cell, and there’s no tone or message that gives it away.


**Will I see the original caller’s number when a call is forwarded?**


What *you* see depends on the method. With carrier forwarding, the original caller’s ID usually passes straight through, so a forwarded business call looks like any other call on your cell. You can’t tell it came in on your business line until you answer. With a virtual app like Quo, incoming calls show which business number was dialed, so you know it’s a work call before you pick up and can answer in context.


**Will my text messages forward too?**


No, carrier call forwarding is voice-only; texts stay on the business line. A virtual number keeps calls and texts together in one app.


**How do I stop forwarding my business number?**


With a traditional carrier, dial *73 or *73# to turn off call forwarding on Verizon or AT&T, respectively. With a virtual app like Quo, toggle the forwarding flow off in your settings.


**Can I make calls that show my business number instead of my personal one?**


Yes. Forwarding only handles incoming calls, but a virtual number lets you place outbound calls from the phone you already carry and choose your business line as the caller ID. Your customer sees your business number, and your personal line stays private.


**Is it free to forward a business number to your cell?**


It depends on how you forward. With a traditional carrier, call forwarding itself is usually included. But if you’re paying for a second line to have a business number in the first place, that costs around $20 to $50 a month, depending on your carrier. A virtual number includes forwarding into the plan, so there’s no separate line to buy; Quo starts at $15 per user per month.
