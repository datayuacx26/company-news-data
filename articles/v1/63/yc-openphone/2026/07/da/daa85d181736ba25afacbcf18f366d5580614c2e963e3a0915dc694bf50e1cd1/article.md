# How to set up call waiting: 4 steps + an alternative

You’re a few minutes away from closing a new client over the phone when you get a notification— someone else is trying to call. Now you have a decision to make: do you put the first caller on hold to answer, or let the second caller go to voicemail and hope they don’t call a competitor?


It’s a tough decision to make. And the more calls you get, the harder it is to keep everyone happy.


Call waiting is one way to ensure every telephone call gets answered. But it doesn’t solve the underlying problem for teams that want to scale: you can still only talk to one person at a time.


This guide walks through the steps of setting up call waiting so you can take incoming calls while you’re already on the phone. We also introduce a better[call management system](https://www.quo.com/product/call-management) that helps growing teams answer more calls without making customers wait.


##


**What is call waiting?**


Call waiting is a phone feature that lets you handle a second incoming call while you’re already speaking with someone. That way, incoming callers don’t get a busy signal or end up in voicemail, which helps reduce missed calls.


In practice, it looks something like this: Let’s say you’re on an active call and another person calls you. With call waiting, you’ll see a notification or hear a dial tone that indicates the incoming call. You can then choose to:


- Put the first call on hold and answer the second one without hanging up
- Toggle back and forth between the two calls at any time
- Reject the new call — useful for routing callers to the next available teammate or step in your[call flow](https://www.quo.com/blog/call-flow/)


While you’re deciding what to do, the caller hears the phone ringing until someone at your business picks up and asks to put them on hold. Some callers will wait, while others may decide to hang up at this point.


Keep in mind, call waiting works for landline, mobile, and internet calls. Even international calls can be put on hold, which is useful if you manage long-distance calls across multiple time zones.


##


**How to set up call waiting on an iPhone**


You can use the following steps to set up call waiting on the cell phone number for your iPhone:


1. Under the **Settings** app, tap **Apps** and **Phone** .
2. Tap **Call Waiting** .
3. Toggle the **On** switch.


##


**How to set up call waiting on an Android**


Here’s how to set up call waiting with a cell phone number on Samsung Galaxy devices and other Android devices:


1. Open the **Phone app** and tap the three vertical dots to the right.
2. Tap **Settings** and **Supplementary services** .
3. Tap to enable the slider for **Call Waiting** .


Now, you can answer a second call while placing an ongoing call on hold.


Keep in mind, enabling Android’s call waiting feature doesn’t solve the underlying problem. You can still only talk to one caller at a time. The next caller will have to wait or hang up, often leading to missed calls and money being left on the table.
