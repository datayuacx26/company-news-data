# 17 Best apps for lawyers to boost productivity in 2026

Having an efficient set of apps that work on your computer and smartphone can make managing your law practice a lot easier. You’ll have access to the information you need when you need it, save time, and be able to offer a better client experience.


In this article, we’ll give you an overview of the best apps for lawyers. We’ve categorized the apps so you can pick ones that best suit your law firm’s needs from relevant categories.


If you’re a solo practitioner or part of a small firm, you don’t need to break the bank to build a solid tech stack. Many of the apps in this guide are free or available at a small-business-friendly cost. For example, with apps like Quo, Coffee, or HubSpot, you can stay organized and professional without much overhead.

Cloud storage solutions


Your team likely needs a simple way to pull up important documents at all times. You can use cloud storage to access documents from any computer or mobile device quickly.


🚩 **Remember:** It’s your responsibility to ensure your cloud storage solution aligns with professional standards These include, but aren’t limited to, standards outlined in the ABA’s[Model Rules of Professional Conduct](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/model_rules_of_professional_conduct_table_of_contents/) . For example, you must understand the[benefits and risks of relevant legal technology](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_1_1_competence/comment_on_rule_1_1/) . You also have to make “[reasonable efforts to ensure](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_5_3_responsibilities_regarding_nonlawyer_assistant/comment_on_rule_5_3/) that the services \[of an internet-based service to store client information\] are provided in a manner that is compatible with \[your\] professional obligation.”


### 14. OneDrive: Best for Microsoft users


#### Pros


- Included in Microsoft 365 subscription
- Personal Vault folder requiring additional authentication methods


#### Cons


- Some users find file-sharing with external clients to be clunky
- Server-side encryption means Microsoft could theoretically access your data


OneDrive is the best app for lawyers who already use Windows and Microsoft Office.


It comes pre-installed in new Windows operating systems and seamlessly works in the background to sync files and folders to the cloud.


You can install the OneDrive mobile app on your iPhone, iPad, or Android device. If your team uses both PC and Mac devices, Mac users can use OneDrive by installing the OneDrive app available in the Mac App Store.


On the security side, OneDrive uses AES 256-bit encryption for files at rest and TLS encryption in transit. Microsoft 365 plans also have compliance features like HIPAA support, audit logs, and data loss prevention. For most standard client matters, that baseline is solid.


That said, there’s one limitation to be aware of. Microsoft uses server-side encryption for most plans. This means they hold the encryption keys and could, in theory, access your data. Does your firm handle particularly sensitive matters, like criminal defense or national security? You may want to consider a zero-knowledge encryption solution where only you hold the keys.


Make sure to review Microsoft’s documentation and speak with your IT provider before choosingOneDrive as your main storage.


#### OneDrive pricing

****How can you ensure an app for lawyers is secure and compliant?****


Start by checking for encryption. Look for AES 256-bit encryption at rest and SSL/TLS in transit as a baseline. From there, review whether the tool supports compliance frameworks relevant to your practice, such as HIPAA, SOC 2, or CCPA. You should also consult the ABA’s guidelines on technology and client data. Ensure any tool you adopt helps you comply with legal ethics and client confidentiality rules. When in doubt, check the vendor’s security documentation and involve your IT provider before committing.

****How can you choose the right legal practice management software?****


Here’s how to choose the right management app for legal services:
– **Define the features you need.** Start by listing the core functions your firm relies on. This could be intake, billing, document management, and calendaring. Decide whether you want one multi-function platform that covers everything or a set of single-purpose tools you can mix and match. The all-in-one approach simplifies your stack. But the modular approach gives you more flexibility to pick best-in-class tools for each function.
– **Check integrations.** Whatever you choose should work with the tools you already use — your email, calendar, phone system, and payment processor. Poor integration means manual data entry, which defeats the purpose.
– **Evaluate security.** Confirm the platform supports encryption and offers role-based access controls. It should also meet the compliance standards relevant to your practice area. For health-related matters, that means HIPAA. For most firms, SOC 2 compliance is a solid baseline.
