# Grasshopper pricing: Plans, add-on fees, & what you actually pay

If you’re looking for a cost-effective business phone system, you’ve likely come across Grasshopper. At a starting price of $14 per month, it seems like a deal that’s too good to be true.


That’s because it is.


Grasshopper’s pricing starts at $14 per month for its True Solo plan, which includes one user, one number, and one extension. If you want unlimited users, you have to upgrade to its Solo Plus plan at $25 per month. If you want unlimited extensions, you have to upgrade to its Small Business plan at $55 per month. All these prices are available with annual billing.


In this article, we’ll cover Grasshopper’s pricing and plans in detail. We’ll also show you why Grasshopper’s platform isn’t as cost friendly as you think. By the end of this article, you’ll understand why Grasshopper isn’t worth the price for small businesses.


##


**Grasshopper’s pricing and plans**


Grasshopper’s pricing is different from most VoIP services. It charges teams for numbers and extensions. Other VoIP providers charge teams for every user in their workspace. This can get confusing as your team grows and your reps want their own phone numbers. This pricing structure can also quickly add up for your business.

Now, let’s see how each of these Grasshopper pricing plans breaks down:


### **True Solo plan**


**Pricing** : $14 per month billed annually, $18 per month if billed monthly. You can save $48 a year if you opt for annual billing.


**Best for:** Solo entrepreneurs or freelancers who need a basic, professional business line.


The True Solo plan comes with one user, one[Grasshopper phone number](https://www.quo.com/blog/grasshopper-review/#how-to-get-a-grasshopper-phone-number) , and one extension per account.


You get access to key business features, including:


- Mobile app and desktop app
- VoIP and[WiFi calling](https://www.quo.com/blog/what-is-wifi-calling/)
- [VoIP texting](https://www.quo.com/blog/voip-texting/)
- [Voicemail transcription](https://www.quo.com/features/voicemail-to-text)
- Custom greetings
- Instant text message responses for first-time callers
- Virtual fax
- Incoming call control that includes call screening and business hours
- Call forwarding
- AI voicemail sentiment


### **Solo Plus plan**


**Pricing** : $25 per month billed annually, $32 per month if billed monthly. You can save $84 a year if you opt for annual billing.


**Best for:** Small teams that need basic call routing and want to share a business number — but don’t need advanced features.


Grasshopper’s Solo Plus gives you access to the same features as the True Solo plan, plus unlimited users and three extensions per account.


You also get access to:


- Call recording
- IVR
- Simultaneous call handling
- AI call summaries and call sentiment


Keep in mind, you only get three extensions on this plan. If you have more than three team members and want each of them to receive forwarded calls, you’ll need to purchase additional extensions. These start at $3 per extension per month.


Where this plan falls short for teams is if you need additional phone numbers. If you want the additional features but need only one additional number in the plan, you’ll end up paying $34 per month instead of $25 per month. And that’s before any other additional fees.


### **Small Business plan**


**Pricing** : $55 per month when billed annually, $70 per month if billed monthly. You can save $180 a year if you opt for annual billing.


**Best for** : Growing teams that need multiple phone extensions and flexible call routing — but don’t require advanced features.


Grasshopper’s Small Business plan gives you the same features as the previous plans. Plus, it includes unlimited extensions and four business numbers.

Grasshopper add-on costs you should know


Grasshopper’s per-month plans are just the tip of the pricing iceberg. The actual cost depends on how many numbers you need and which features you require.


Here’s a quick breakdown of all the add-on fees that come with using Grasshopper:


Add-On Cost Notes


Additional numbers $9 per number per month Purchase required for True Solo and Solo Plus plans


Additional extensions $3 per extension per month Available for Solo Plus and Small Business plans


A2P 10DLC fees (required for texting) $19.50 one-time, plus $1.50–$10 per month Required to text US phone numbers


International calls $500 one-time deposit, plus call rates Minimum account age of 60 days required


Voice Studio $75 per order Professional greeting for your IVR


Virtual receptionist $95–$2,100 per month AI and human receptionists available through Smith.ai


Here’s what to know about the additional costs:


- **Additional extensions** . You can only purchase additional extensions once you upgrade to the Solo Plus plan. Additional extensions cost $3 per extension per month, billed annually, or $5 per extension per month, billed monthly.
- **Additional numbers.** Both the True Solo and Solo Plus plans only offer one number. Multiple users can only use extensions with the same number. If you want to add numbers to your plan, you’ll need to pay an additional $9 per number per month. This quickly drives up your monthly costs.
- **SMS registration.** Virtual phone number providers are required to register their numbers for business texting. This registration process is known as A2P 10DLC. Businesses have to pay a one-time registration fee , plus monthly fees, in order to text US customers.
- **International calling** . First, you have to make a $500 deposit to use international calling. Then you can add calling credits to your account, with certain countries costing as much as 95¢ per minute. Additionally, you won’t be able to make international calls until your account is at least 60 days old. This could be a dealbreaker for many businesses.
- **Voice studio** . $75 per order for professionally recorded voicemail greetings by voice actors. You can choose from English, Spanish, or French.
- **Virtual receptionist.** Grasshopper offers a virtual receptionist to handle incoming calls 24/7 through a partnership with[Smith.ai](https://www.quo.com/blog/smith-ai-alternatives) . You can choose between AI and human receptionists. AI receptionists start at $95 per month. Human receptionists start at $300 per month and go up to $2,100 per month.


##


Grasshopper’s cancellation and refund policy


If you decide Grasshopper isn’t right for you, you can cancel your account. However, you should know a few things about Grasshopper’s cancellation and refund policy:


1. **Grasshopper doesn’t offer refunds.** Grasshopper has an official no-refund policy. That applies to annual plans too. You won’t be issued a refund for canceling your subscription early.
2. **Number transfers can take two weeks.** To transfer your phone number from Grasshopper to a different carrier, you have to keep your Grasshopper account active. The transfer process can take anywhere between 2 and 18 days.
3. **Contact support to cancel.** You’ll need to contact Grasshopper’s support team to cancel your account. Make sure you do this after you transfer your phone numbers out of your account.

Grasshopper doesn’t let team members tag each other in conversations, which means they can’t work together to come up with the right responses for customers.


Plus, there’s no built-in way to assign tasks to your colleagues. You’ll have to manually delegate work through a different channel, like email.


Whether you want to follow up on a customer request or assign a follow-up task, Grasshopper forces you to collaborate outside its platform.


### **6. Texting limitations may cause work for you**


Your[business phone system](https://www.quo.com/blog/best-small-business-phone-systems/) should make work easier, not harder. But if you use Grasshopper’s SMS features, the reverse might be true.
Here are[Grasshopper texting limitations](https://www.quo.com/blog/grasshopper-sms/) you should know:


- **Solo proprietor businesses can’t text on Grasshopper.** If your business is a sole proprietorship, you can’t use Grasshopper to text customers. They don’t provide A2P 10DLC registration to sole proprietorships.
- **You can’t text international customers.** If you port in a number, you might lose international customers who prefer texting over phone calls.
- **You can’t send MMS messages through toll-free numbers to Canadian numbers** . This means you can’t send pictures, videos, or files about your business to customers in Canada, potentially giving more work to your team.
- [Group texting](https://www.quo.com/product/messaging/group-texting) **is only available for local numbers.** If you need to message multiple customers from a toll-free or[vanity number](https://www.quo.com/vanity-number) , you’ll have to find a suitable Grasshopper alternative.
- **Limited SMS auto-reply functionality.** Grasshopper’s Instant Response feature has multiple limitations. For starters, you can’t send instant responses after business hours. You also can’t set an auto-reply if a customer leaves a voicemail or hangs up before selecting an extension.

**FAQs**


****How much does Grasshopper really cost?****


Grasshopper starts at $14 per month for one user, one phone number, and one extension. You have to upgrade to the Solo Plus plan to unlock unlimited members and three extensions, which costs $25 per user per month. If you want unlimited extensions, you’ll pay $55 per user per month for the Small Business plan. Additional phone numbers on Grasshopper cost $9 per month each.


****How much does Grasshopper texting cost?****


Texting is included with Grasshopper plans, but you have to pay $1.50 per number per month. Keep in mind, this only gives you access to fewer than 6,000 texts per day. You also have to pay for two one-time fees:


1. The Campaign Registry, which costs $4.50
2. Carrier review, which costs $15


These fees are standard across most virtual number providers due to regulations large US cell carriers pushed for.[A2P 10DLC registration](https://www.quo.com/blog/what-is-a2p-10dlc/) fees go to The Campaign Registry, which reviews your registration, and other third-party entities that keep your registration active.


****Does Grasshopper charge per minute for calls?****


Domestic calls within the US and Canada are unlimited with no per-minute fees. However, international calls are charged per minute, with rates up to $0.95, depending on the destination.


****How long is Grasshopper’s trial?****


You can try Grasshopper for free for seven days. However, the trial ends early if you exceed its calling limits, which only include 50 minutes of call time. The trial doesn’t offer Grasshopper’s premium features or the ability to text numbers.


****Does Grasshopper use VoIP?****


Grasshopper is not a true VoIP service. It uses your existing carrier to route calls. That means you still need an active mobile plan to use Grasshopper.


****Can I record a phone call with Grasshopper?****


You can automatically record calls with Grasshopper if you’re on their Solo Plus or Small Business plan. Or you can use a Grasshopper alternative like Quo to get manual and automatic call recording for your numbers. You’ll also get access to AI-generated call transcriptions and summaries so you can quickly review your conversations at a later time.


****How much does Grasshopper cost compared to other VoIP providers?****


Grasshopper plans start at $14 per month, similar to Quo’s $15 per user per month starting price. But Grasshopper’s base plan doesn’t include additional numbers and extensions. If you want more numbers, you’ll have to pay an additional $9 per month, which pushes up your monthly costs. You could also upgrade to its Small Business plan, which costs $55 per month.


****Is Grasshopper free to use?****


Grasshopper isn’t free. You can try it with a seven-day free trial. The trial lets you make calls and use its basic features. However, it limits calls to 50 minutes. After the trial, you need to choose a paid plan. Plans start at $14 per month for one user, one phone number, and one extension.


****Does Grasshopper offer an API?****


No, Grasshopper doesn’t offer an API. You won’t be able to build custom[integrations](https://www.quo.com/blog/grasshopper-integrations/) to connect your business phone system with the tools you already use, like Jobber or Gong. If you need API access, you’ll need to consider an alternate provider like Quo.


****Does Grasshopper offer discounts?****


Similar to other virtual phone systems, Grasshopper offers discounts if you purchase an annual plan vs a monthly plan. Here’s how much you can save with annual billing:
• True Solo: Save $48 per year.
• Solo Plus: Save $84 per year.
• Small Business: Save $180 per year.
