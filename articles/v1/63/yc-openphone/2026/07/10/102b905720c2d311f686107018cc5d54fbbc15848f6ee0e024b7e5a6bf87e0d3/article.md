# Handle conversations right inside HighLevel

Is HighLevel your team’s source of truth? Every call, text, and AI summary now can flow straight into HighLevel with this integration. There’s no more copy-paste or switching apps mid-conversation. Here’s how it works:


##


💬 Run every client conversation inside HighLevel


Calls, texts, voicemails, and call recordings land in the native HighLevel
conversation thread automatically, and AI call summaries and next steps
post to contact Notes. No manual logging and no app switching.


##


👤 One connection per client


Connect Quo at the HighLevel Sub-Account (Location) level. Each client’s
phone setup stays isolated, with no shared credentials or crossed wires
between accounts.


##


⚡Speed-to-lead without sacrificing quality


Real-time contact sync means new HighLevel contacts appear in Quo the
moment they’re created, closing the gap between lead capture and first
outreach.


##


📊 AI insights, right on the contact


AI call summaries and next steps post to HighLevel contact Notes
automatically, so your team sees what happened on every call without
leaving the CRM.


##


Connect Quo to HighLevel


Quo workspace owners or admins can[set up the Quo Clio Manage integration](https://support.quo.com/core-concepts/integrations/clio) now in minutes.
