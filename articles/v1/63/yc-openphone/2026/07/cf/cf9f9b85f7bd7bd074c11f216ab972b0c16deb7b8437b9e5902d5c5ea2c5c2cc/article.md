# Smith.ai pricing: Hidden fees + 4 other things to know

Smith.ai launched in 2015 to help small businesses handle more calls with human receptionists. Today, it also offers[AI answering services](https://www.quo.com/answering-service) . This lets an AI voice agent handle incoming calls first, then route more complex ones to human reps.


But this adjustment hasn’t made Smith.ai any easier to scale. Its prices have risen quite a bit over the years — not to mention its add-ons and setup fees make it difficult to track your total costs.


This article covers everything you should know about Smith.AI pricing, including its pricing, hidden fees, and paid add-ons. We also cover a Smith.ai alternative that can more easily scale as your business grows.


##


**Smith.ai pricing and plans at a glance: What you get and what you’re missing**


Smith.ai is an AI-powered receptionist and live answering service that handles calls 24/7. You can sign up for one of two services: AI-first, or Human-first. This determines who picks up the call when a customer calls your business — either an AI receptionist or a human receptionist.


It’s designed for industries such as law firms and transportation and logistics companies that need calls answered without a full front desk.


But you should know Smith.ai is more expensive than other virtual receptionist services, making it harder to scale as you grow.

### **Smith.ai’s AI receptionist plans**


Here’s a closer look at Smith.ai’s pricing plans for its AI-first service.


#### **Free**


**Starting price:** Free


**Best for:** Businesses that only receive a few calls each month


Smith.ai’s free plan includes an AI voice agent with 25 calls per month. You’ll also get access to standard features. But keep in mind Smith.ai has no phone system of its own, which means customer conversations end up split across multiple platforms — making it more time consuming to catch up.


Here’s what you can expect to get:


- 25 calls per month
- Call transfers
- [CRM integrations](https://www.quo.com/blog/crm-phone-integration/) that automatically sync your call data to the correct contact
- Zapier integration with 9,000+ third-party tools
- AI that can[qualify leads](https://www.quo.com/blog/lead-qualification/) based on your criteria
- New lead intake
- Appointment scheduling answering service via Calendly
- One local or toll-free business number, no built-in phone system
- Automatic call recordings
- Call transcriptions and summaries


As mentioned, the free plan only includes 25 calls per month. After that, you’ll pay $3 per call unless you upgrade to another plan. It also doesn’t include features like dedicated onboarding support or lower overage rates, which are available on higher plans.


#### **Pro**


**Starting price:** $150 per month, billed monthly


**Best for:** Growing businesses that need daily support with call answering


Smith.ai’s Pro plan unlocks all the features you get in Starter. However, its pricing is complicated. Instead of a single plan, it comes in three different tiers. Here’s what each one includes:

- Dedicated onboarding setup

But what you *won’t* have access to are custom-built AI receptionists and custom integrations. That means you’ll need to build the AI voice agent yourself and may not be able to connect key parts of your tech stack. There’s also no way to get a dedicated success manager unless you upgrade. For businesses expecting a high volume of calls, the Pro plan isn’t scalable.

##


**Smith.ai’s paid add-ons and other fees to know**


There’s a lot more to Smith.ai’s pricing plans than what’s listed.


You’ll find more than 20 paid add-ons available on both the AI-first and Human-first services.


### **AI-first service add-ons and fees**


At the time of writing, Smith.ai only offers one add-on for its AI-first plans. It lets your AI voice agent hand callers off to your team when needed. For example, if you want someone to verify information or schedule appointments, you’ll pay an additional per-call fee.


Smith.ai doesn’t publicly list the price for this add-on, so you’ll need to contact the sales team for a quote.


### **Human-first service add-ons and fees**


Smith.ai’s Human-first plans carry the bulk of its add-ons and fees. As you’ll see below, it’s difficult to estimate how much you’ll be paying per month if you use any of these add-ons.


This includes:


- **Additional CRM Integrations:** $0.50 per call per additional CRM to sync call details to the correct contact.
- **Book appointments:** $1.50 per call to have your receptionist schedule appointments directly on your calendar, such as Calendly or Acuity.
- **Microsoft Teams, SMS, and Slack notifications:** $0.50 per call to get real-time call alerts sent to your team in Slack or Microsoft Teams, or via SMS. If a team member is unavailable to receive the notification, it’ll cost an extra $0.25 for each additional “ping.”
- **Text and email follow-up:** $0.50 per call for your receptionist to send a follow-up message to the caller after the conversation ends.
- **Conflict checks:** $0.50 per call to screen new leads in your CRM for conflicts of interest.
- **Call recording and transcription:** $0.25 per call to get a recording and written transcript of a conversation.
- **Dedicated Spanish line:** $1 per call to have a Spanish-speaking live agent answer the phone.
- **Accept collect calls:** $0.50 per call for attorneys to accept collect calls from inmates
- **Accept payments:** $1 per call to process customer payments directly. Smith.ai supports Square, PayPal, LawPay, TrialPay, CPACharge, and more.
- **Third-party intake:** $1 per call for a live agent to fill out a form in another software while they’re talking to your caller.
- **Complex call routing:** $1 per call to set call routing rules for each number based on time of day, extension, schedules, and availability. You can either set up ring groups that notify team members sequentially or alert everyone at the same time via[simultaneous ring](https://www.quo.com/product/call-routing/simultaneous-ring) .
- **Custom calendaring:** $1 per call for more complex bookings, like checking multiple calendars.
- **Extended intake:** $1.50 per call for up to six additional questions to qualify and organize leads.
- **Custom extended intake:** $0.25 per question to add more questions for your live agent to ask on the phone.
- **Business caller ID for outbound:** $10 per month so all outbound calls display your main business phone number.
- **Additional transfer destinations:** $15 per month for each extra number that calls can be transferred to.
- **Inbound call tracking numbers:** $5 per number per month to track which campaigns drive calls to your business.

**4 Key things to keep in mind before choosing Smith.ai**


Smith.ai’s pricing leaves a lot to be desired, but it’s not the only drawback to keep in mind.


Here are four more disadvantages of Smith.ai:


### **1. No phone system of its own**


While Smith.ai *does* give you one phone number per plan and lets you port your existing number, it isn’t a phone system. Instead, it’s a standalone AI and live receptionist answering service.


The problem is, customer conversations are split across two platforms. Your team has to switch between Smith.ai and your business phone system to see a contact’s full conversation history.


This makes following up much slower than it has to be. Your team could also miss important pieces of the conversation. Plus, it’s less convenient than a business phone system with a built-in AI receptionist.


### **2. Pricing that’s hard to scale as you grow**


Smith.ai’s pricing is hard to scale for most small and growing businesses.


For example, the free plan includes 25 calls handled by the AI receptionist. Once you go over that, you’ll pay $3 per call. Or you’ll have to upgrade to the $150 per-month tier.


But even on the highest tier, Smith.ai’s Enterprise plan only provides 500 calls for $800 per month. This is limiting for businesses with high call volumes.


### **3. Support that’s slow to resolve issues**


Many past Smith.ai customers describe slow response times from support. They describe much of their service as “hit-or-miss,” with some saying that issues dragged on for weeks before getting resolved.


One Smith.ai review reads:


*“Dealing with administration was impossible to get anything \[done\]. You go in circles dealing with different individuals.”* —[Capterra](https://www.capterra.ca/software/181278/smith-ai-live-website-chat#reviews)


### **4. Call transfers cost extra**


If you want your AI voice agent to transfer calls to a rep so they can verify information or schedule appointments, you’ll need to pay a fee. Smith.ai doesn’t publicly list how much this costs, which means you’ll need to contact its sales team for a quote.


With a Smith.ai alternative like Quo, your AI voice agent can transfer calls to your team without charging extra.
