# Top 10 sales call coaching software to close more deals

You know you should be coaching your reps more. But between discovery calls, pipeline reviews, and everything else on your plate,[sales call coaching](https://www.quo.com/blog/sales-call-coaching/) often gets pushed aside.


But you also know it only takes one mishandled objection to drive a lead to your competitor.


Sales call coaching software removes the bottleneck between you and the calls, so you can finally work closely with your reps. And do it consistently. It starts by turning every call into reviewable data. Then, it can use AI to identify what top performers do differently, where deals stall, and which objections reps struggle with.


The results are clear. A[Korn Ferry study](https://www.kornferry.com/insights/featured-topics/sales-transformation/building-the-business-case-for-sales-coaching) found companies with consistent sales coaching see 32% higher win rates and 28% higher quota attainment.


Below, we’ll walk you through 10 of the top sales call coaching software tools available today. We compared their features, pricing, strengths, and limitations to help you find the right fit for your team.


##


How we selected the top sales call coaching software


We focused on these seven criteria to decide which tools to include in this guide:


- **AI analysis depth.** We prioritized tools that go beyond recording and transcription to identify trends, flag patterns, and give you something to act on.
- **Ease of setup and adoption.** A coaching platform only works if your team actually uses it. We favored tools that are easy to deploy and simple for sales reps and managers to adopt without weeks of training.
- **Pricing transparency.** Many software companies hide their rates behind a sales call. We noted where pricing is public and gave realistic estimates where it isn’t, so you can disqualify tools that won’t fit your current budget.
- **Security and data handling.** These tools capture sensitive details from customer conversations and transcripts. Because of that, SOC 2 compliance, data retention controls, and encryption mattered heavily in our evaluation.
- **CRM and phone system integrations.** Call coaching software should fit naturally into your existing workflow. We evaluated how well each platform connects with popular CRMs, business phone systems, and sales tools to reduce manual work.
- **User reviews.** We read verified customer reviews on G2 and Capterra, along with forums like Reddit. This helped us identify recurring strengths and issues that aren’t always obvious from vendor claims.
- **Onboarding and ramp speed.** Getting new reps up to speed shouldn’t take months. We picked tools that offer searchable call libraries and recording storage, giving new hires access to your best sales calls from day one.

##


Which sales call coaching software is right for your team size?


There is no single best sales call coaching software. The right choice depends on your team size and the type of coaching or sales training you need. Here’s how to think about it:


- **Under 25 reps:** Start with Quo. Coaching features are built into your phone plan, so you don’t have to buy and manage a separate platform. If your team does most of its selling over video rather than phone calls, Avoma is worth considering alongside it.
- **25–100 reps:** As your team requires more complicated features, consider Dialpad Sell, Aircall, or Jiminny. These tools save time with automated scorecards, coaching insights, and live guidance during calls.
- **100–200 reps:** Gong and Chorus make more sense at this stage. They help you spot patterns across your team’s conversations, understand why deals are won or lost, and track win rates. If your reps must follow strict scripts or specific processes, consider Balto.
- **200+ reps:** Use Mindtickle or Allego to get a platform for sales coaching, roleplay, and content management. Many enterprise teams also use Gong to see how conversations affect pipeline and revenue.


##


7 Features you should look for in sales call coaching software


Finding the right sales coaching software can be exhausting because there are so many tools out there. This list will help you cut through the noise and find the right fit for your team.


- **AI call transcription and summaries.** Listening to every call takes too much time for busy managers. AI transcripts and summaries let you review a whole call quickly. Depending on the platform, you can also search for key phrases to find what you need
- **A searchable call library.** Your best rep’s calls are your best training tools. Every recorded call should be easy to find by rep, date, or keyword. Some sales coaching tools let you save short clips and organize them into playlists. Others keep things simple with a searchable list of recordings. The right choice depends on your team size.
- **Sentiment and intent detection.** Some tools flag frustrated callers, buying intent, or other signals that are important to you. This is useful for managers who want to prioritize which calls to review first.
- [Detailed call analytics](https://www.quo.com/blog/call-analytics/) **.** Call volume only tells you how busy your team is. Look for tools that also track call duration distribution, peak activity times, and per-rep performance over time.
- **CRM integration.** Your coaching software must share data with your CRM automatically. Otherwise, you and your reps have to input call notes manually every time. This wastes time and creates more errors.


- **Security and data compliance.** Sales coaching software stores private customer call recordings and transcripts. Look for SOC II certification, data retention controls, and encryption. Depending on your location, you may also need your tool to be compliant with CCPA, GDPR, or other regulations.
- **Transparent pricing.** Many enterprise software companies require you to speak with sales before they reveal their pricing. For small teams, that can make it harder to compare tools and find one that fits their budget. Prioritize vendors that publish clear pricing tiers or at least a starting price on their website so you can make a faster decision.

**What is sales call coaching software?**


Sales call coaching software automatically records and transcribes your sales conversations. It uses AI to analyze your calls for instant coaching insights like poor objection handling or competitor mentions. This helps you coach your entire team at scale without listening to every single recording. It also helps your reps improve with feedback based on real conversations instead of roleplay scenarios.


**What’s the difference between conversation intelligence and sales coaching software?**


Conversation intelligence is the underlying capability. It’s the ability to capture, transcribe, and analyze sales conversations to surface patterns and insights. Sales coaching software is a tool that uses conversation intelligence to help managers coach reps, track performance, and improve close rates.
