# GLP-1s Are Driving Consumer Behavior Changes: How Food Brands Can Adapt

[Innovation](https://thefreshfactory.co/blog-categories/innovation/) ,


[Trends](https://thefreshfactory.co/blog-categories/trends/)


February 27, 2026


GLP-1 medications are no longer a niche medical trend. A[Gallup survey](https://news.gallup.com/poll/696599/obesity-rate-declining.aspx) conducted over Q2-Q3 in 2025 revealed 12.4% of adults report taking injectable GLP-1s for weight loss. This figure had more than doubled since Gallup first measured it in 2024.


And the semaglutide wave doesn’t seem to be slowing down anytime soon. Circana projects a[21% adoption rate by 2034](https://www.circana.com/post/the-ripple-effect-of-glp-1s-today-and-in-the-future) .


The widespread use of GLP-1 medications is fundamentally reshaping how millions of Americans eat, shop, and evaluate food brands. For food and beverage brands, this is not a temporary shift. It is a structural change in consumption behavior.


Consumers on GLP-1 medications are eating less, but expecting more protein and fiber from every calorie consumed. That shift carries meaningful implications for product development, packaging strategy, portfolio architecture, and manufacturing agility.


So how can CPG brands adapt to these consumer behavior changes? Read on to learn more.


## How Do GLP-1s Work?


Understanding what the GLP-1 user looks for in snacks and beverages starts with understanding the science behind these drugs.


The[Cleveland Clinic defines glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/13901-glp-1-agonists) (GLP-1s) as a class of medications that is used to help regulate blood sugar levels. They work by triggering your pancreas to release more insulin, which helps to lower blood sugar levels. The results?


**Reduced appetite and increased feelings of fullness** : GLP-1s slow gastric emptying, meaning food stays in your stomach longer, helping you feel satisfied with smaller portions.


**Better blood sugar control** : By stimulating insulin release when blood sugar is elevated and reducing glucagon secretion, GLP-1s help maintain more stable glucose levels throughout the day.


**Significant weight loss** : The combination of reduced hunger, delayed stomach emptying, and metabolic changes leads to decreased caloric intake.


**Changes in food preferences and cravings** : Many users report reduced cravings for high-fat, high-sugar foods and a decreased desire to eat, fundamentally shifting what they want from their snacks and beverages.


## What Are GLP-1-Friendly Food Products?


The shift isn’t just about eating less. It’s about demanding more from every calorie consumed. In a[PwC consumer study](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/consulting/business-model-reinvention/glp-1-trends-and-impact-on-business-models.html) , GLP-1 users reported the following:


- 56% made healthier food choices
- 47% ate smaller portions
- 29% reduced their food and beverage spend


While Circana data expects grocery spending to rebound, there will be a persisting mix shift toward better-for-you categories that incorporate:


- High protein for muscle maintenance and lasting fullness
- Low sugar for steady energy and blood sugar control
- High fiber for digestive balance and prolonged satiety
- Vitamins and targeted supplements for daily nutritional support
- Electrolytes to support hydration as GLP-1 usage expands


[Packaging](https://thefreshfactory.co/blog/2026-food-packaging-trends/) is also getting a makeover for the GLP-1 consumer. Brands are introducing single-serve portions, resealable multi-packs, and smaller package sizes that align with reduced appetite and portion control needs.

## How to Meet GLP-1 Food Industry Trends


Brands that want to capture market share from this growing consumer base should consider the following:


### Product Launches


Develop new products specifically designed for GLP-1 users’ needs. Think high-protein, low-sugar portion-controlled snacks. Consider ready-to-drink protein beverages, nutrient-dense meal replacement bars, Greek yogurt cups with added fiber, or savory protein snacks like meat sticks and cheese crisps. Innovation should focus on delivering maximum nutrition in minimal volume.


### Reformulation and Resizing


Audit your existing portfolio and identify opportunities to reduce package sizes, increase protein content, lower added sugars, and boost fiber. This might mean launching 80-calorie instead of 150-calorie versions, offering resealable multi-packs instead of family-size bags, or reformulating recipes to hit 10+ grams of protein per serving. The key is maintaining taste and satisfaction while aligning with the nutritional priorities and smaller appetites of GLP-1 users.


### Marketing and Positioning


Communicate benefits that resonate with this audience, such as satiety, blood sugar support, muscle maintenance, and portion control. Use clear front-of-pack callouts for protein, fiber, and calorie counts to help GLP-1 users quickly identify products that fit their needs.


Consider partnerships with healthcare providers, weight management programs, or digital health platforms where GLP-1 users are already seeking guidance. Avoid clinical claims you can’t substantiate, but don’t shy away from positioning your brand as an ally in the GLP-1 health journey.


### Mergers and Acquisitions


Accelerate your GLP-1 strategy and future-proof your portfolio by acquiring or partnering with brands that already have credibility in the high-protein, low-sugar, or[functional nutrition](https://thefreshfactory.co/blog/functional-foods-2026/) space. Look for companies with strong digital engagement, loyal consumer bases in the health and wellness category, or proprietary formulations that deliver on GLP-1-friendly attributes. M&A can provide faster access to innovation pipelines, manufacturing capabilities, and consumer trust that would take years to build organically.
