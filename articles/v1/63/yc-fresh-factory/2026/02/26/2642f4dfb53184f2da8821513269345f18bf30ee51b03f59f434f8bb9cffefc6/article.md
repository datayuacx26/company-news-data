# Functional Food and Beverage Trends for 2026

[Innovation](https://thefreshfactory.co/blog-categories/innovation/) ,


[Trends](https://thefreshfactory.co/blog-categories/trends/)


February 3, 2026


While the idea of functional food isn’t new to the health food industry, we tend to see shifts in functional food and beverage trends. For example, we experienced a probiotic boom in the early ‘aughts followed by a protein revolution and a rise in healthy and ketogenic fats (avocado toast, anyone?).


From there, a plant-based wave hit the market. More recently, adaptogenic beverages (infused with ashwagandha, CBD, etc.), immunity-boosting foods (using ingredients like Manuka honey, elderberry, and tumeric) and foods/drinks for gut health (prebiotic sodas, fermented foods, etc.) have flown off the shelves.


Wondering what consumers want in 2026 and beyond? Here are the trends we are noticing.


## Protein-Packed Foods


Protein continues to be a hot topic in 2026, largely to support the GLP-1 lifestyle, but also for general strength building, weight management, and energy boosting. A[recent survey](https://www.scrippsnews.com/us-news/protein-tops-americans-2026-nutrition-priorities-survey-finds) conducted by Talker Research on behalf of Undeniably Dairy finds 57% of respondents plan to intentionally prioritize protein this year.


From beverages to bars, brands are requesting to pack more protein than ever into their SKUs. One difference we’re seeing from past protein booms is a functional twist. Protein is rarely found alone, and is instead often paired with fiber, probiotics, or low sugar.


## Brain-Boosting Foods


Cognitive wellness is on the rise, with the[global market for Brain Health Functional Food and Beverage](https://www.marketresearch.com/Global-Industry-Analysts-v1039/Brain-Health-Functional-Food-Beverage-42702798/) estimated to grow at a CAGR of 9.6% between 2024 and 2030. The category used to focus on memory improvement, but is expanding, driven primarily by young consumers seeking products to improve focus, mood, and stress resilience.
SPINS[2026 trend predictions](https://www.spins.com/resources/report/2026-trend-predictions/#) list trending brain food ingredients like magnesium L-threonate, citicoline, L-theanine, ashwagandha, and lion’s mane. Expect these ingredients to make their way into health claims on convenience food and drinks this year.


## Gut-Healing Foods


The gut health conversation continues to evolve beyond basic probiotics and prebiotics.[Sea moss](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/health/article/sea-moss-health-benefits-effects) , packed with minerals and fiber, is emerging as a go-to ingredient for digestive support and overall wellness. In addition, colostrum is gaining traction among adults for its gut-lining repair properties and immune-supporting compounds.


These ingredients are showing up in everything from smoothie add-ins to ready-to-drink wellness shots, as consumers seek science-backed solutions for digestive issues and inflammation.


## Foods and Drinks to Boost the Immune System


Immune health remains a priority for consumers post-pandemic, but the approach is shifting toward preventive, daily support rather than reactive solutions. Colostrum is leading the charge here too, valued for its high concentration of antibodies and immunoglobulins.


We’re also seeing a resurgence of traditional immune-supporting ingredients like elderberry, echinacea, and vitamin C, but with modern delivery methods, like gummies, ready-to-drink tonics, and functional snacks that fit seamlessly into busy lifestyles.


## Herbs and Spices for Flavor and Function


From flavor-packed aiolis to golden milk lattes, consumers crave bold flavors. Global flavors and spices continue to be a popular customer request at The Fresh Factory. But those herbs and spices are pulling double duty in 2026. Consumers want their food and drinks to also deliver functional benefits.


Turmeric, ginger, and cinnamon continue their reign for anti-inflammatory properties, while newcomers like black seed oil and fenugreek are gaining recognition.
