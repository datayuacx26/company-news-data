# The Fresh Factory Wrapped: A Year in Review

[Company News](https://thefreshfactory.co/blog-categories/company-news/)


January 6, 2026


As 2025 draws to a close, our team at The Fresh Factory continues to be busier than ever packing juices, crafting protein bars, and fueling all things fresh. Despite tariffs and inflation, our company grew year over year. For that, we are incredibly thankful for our team, suppliers, and the food innovators who trust us to bring their boldest creations to life.


Before we turn our attention to 2026, we wanted to take a moment to look back at everything that made this year special. From expanding our production capabilities to watching emerging brands we support hit major retail shelves, 2025 was packed with moments worth celebrating. Here are the highlights:


## By the Numbers


Thanks to new customers and continued investment/product expansion from existing customers, The Fresh Factory reported our highest quarterly billed revenue on record in Q3 2025 — $11.7M (CAD $16.7M). This represents a year-over-year increase of 44% from Q3 2024.


We also achieved positive EBITDA of $0.4M (CAD $0.5M) in Q3 2025, remaining profitable on a year-to-date basis. We look forward to that momentum launching us into the new year, as we also secured a three-year $4M revolving asset-based lending facility.


“Q3 was a pivotal quarter for us, highlighted by record revenue, positive EBITDA, and meaningful progress in scaling our snacks platform,” says[Bill Besenhofer](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bbesenhofer/) , CEO and co-founder. “With new lines online, a significantly larger facility secured, and enhanced liquidity from our credit facility, we’re positioned to grow alongside our partners and accelerate innovation across categories.”

## Growing Production Capabilities


As of Q3 2025, we had produced 64.4M units year-to-date, a 229% increase from the same period in 2024. We also expanded production with increased snack-bites capacity and new sachet-pouch-filling capabilities.


To build upon that growth, we secured a 10-year lease for a 154,000-square-foot facility in the southwest suburbs of Chicago. Buildout is underway!


Our new facility will support future scale, consolidate operations, and allow for significant production capacity across condiments and dips, beverages, and hot-fill categories.


## Sustainability in Action


We’re committed to being a market leader in sustainability, and 2025 saw meaningful progress toward that goal. We continued composting 100% of our food waste and installed high-efficiency HVAC systems in our new facility to reduce energy consumption.


In 2025, we offset our 2024 Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions through the following actions:


- Purchased 991 metric tons of verified carbon offsets, certified under the Green-e® Climate program
- Procured 2,180 megawatt-hours of Green-e® Energy-certified Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs)


Together, these actions reflect The Fresh Factory’s commitment to responsibly managing our environmental footprint while continuing to build a scalable and sustainable food manufacturing platform.


## A Heartfelt Thank You


As we look toward 2026, we’re more energized than ever about what’s ahead. This year proved that despite economic headwinds, there’s an unstoppable appetite for fresh, clean-label foods. We’re honored to be the partner that brings these innovative products to market.


We are endlessly appreciative to our team members who show up every day with dedication and precision, our suppliers who keep our ingredients flowing, and the bold brands who trust us with their vision. Their partnership makes everything we do possible. Cheers to another year of fresh thinking, bold flavors, and building the future of food together.
