# How Food Production Sustainability Guides The Fresh Factory

[Innovation](https://thefreshfactory.co/blog-categories/innovation/) ,


[Sustainability](https://thefreshfactory.co/blog-categories/sustainability/)


April 13, 2026


Sustainability is more than just a buzzword at The Fresh Factory. It’s embedded into our mission, “to build the infrastructure for the future of food and beverage, providing honest food for all in a sustainable way.”


Sustainability is also one of our guiding values: *We believe in creating a sustainable business* *that ensures the long-term viability of our organization while protecting the environment* *and benefiting society.*


We bake sustainability into everything we do, from[food sourcing](https://thefreshfactory.co/food-logistics/) to[production](https://thefreshfactory.co/manufacturing/) and[distribution](https://thefreshfactory.co/fulfillment/) . The benefits are then passed on to our partner brands, who offer smarter, better-for-you products to discerning consumers.


Read on to learn more about our food sustainability initiatives.


## Partnership with Planet FWD


In 2025, we entered into a strategic partnership with[Planet FWD](https://www.planetfwd.com/) , the leading carbon management platform for the food and beverage industry. Through the collaboration, we gain access to Planet FWD’s advanced emissions tracking and Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) tools to more accurately measure and reduce our carbon footprint.


It’s already paying off. We offset our 2024 Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions by purchasing 991 metric tons of verified carbon offsets, certified under the Green-e® Climate program, and procuring 2,180 megawatt-hours of Green-e® Energy-certified Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs). We also installed high-efficiency HVAC systems in our new facility to reduce energy consumption.


The partnership enables us to support sustainable food companies with actionable climate intelligence across their supply chains.


“We believe the future of food is fresh, clean-label, and climate-smart,” says Bill Besenhofer, CEO and co-founder of The Fresh Factory. “Teaming up with Planet FWD helps us move faster and smarter toward building a more sustainable food system — for ourselves and for the brands we serve.”


## Sustainable Food Systems


Our dedicated supply chain team works directly with local growers and producers, ensuring your clean label products start with the highest quality, sustainably sourced ingredients. We procure about 50% of our ingredients through local sources. In 2024, that meant we purchased 5.8M lbs of produce from local farmers.


The results?


- Fresher ingredients
- Reduced carbon footprint through shorter supply chains
- Stronger local food ecosystem partnerships
- Greater ingredient traceability and quality control


For any ingredients, fresh produce, or packaging we can’t source locally, we work with a trusted network of farmers and distributors around the country.


We also use produce seconds when possible, donate 100% of our product extras, and compost 100% of our food waste.


## Central Location + Responsible Distribution


Our convenient location in the heart of the Chicagoland food hub allows us to reach most of the U.S. by semi within two to three days, ensuring food products stay fresh and delicious. We’ve implemented route optimization strategies to reduce emissions and improve logistics efficiency, which lowers costs, improves delivery reliability, and offers a more sustainable approach.


“We’re producing fresh, clean-label items that don’t always have the longest shelf life,” says Mike Weglarz, The Fresh Factory CFO. “So we truly understand the importance of quality and making sure that we get customers the product when they need it. ”
