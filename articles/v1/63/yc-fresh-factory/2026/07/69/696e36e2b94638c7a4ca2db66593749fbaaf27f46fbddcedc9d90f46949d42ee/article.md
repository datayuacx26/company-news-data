# How to Secure Club Channel Distribution for Your Food Brand

[Tips](https://thefreshfactory.co/blog-categories/tips/)


July 27, 2026


Due to tariffs, inflation, and ongoing market volatility, today’s shoppers want the best bang for their buck. As a result, consumers continue to shift shopping to club stores like BJ’s Wholesale Club, Costco, and Sam’s Club.[Costco 2026 Q3 earnings](https://investor.costco.com/news/news-details/2026/Costco-Wholesale-Corporation-Reports-Third-Quarter-and-Year-To-Date-Operating-Results-For-Fiscal-2026/default.aspx) , for example, show an 11.6% year-over-year sales increase. Sam’s Club and BJ’s report[similar growth](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/future-proofing-value-what-club-channels-growth-signals-wesley-davis-sq7ne/) .


As club and bulk stores become a mainstay, the channel proves all the more valuable for CPG brands to reach new households and grow their customer base. Learn what club stores look for in new products and how to get your brand on the shelves beyond a trial rotation.


## Club Store Distribution Benefits


Beyond reaching a growing customer segment, becoming a supplier for club stores offers the following benefits:


- **High-volume sales per door** : Because club stores carry a curated assortment, each item that earns a slot moves significant volume.
- **Built-in credibility and brand halo** : Earning shelf space at Costco, Sam’s Club, or BJ’s signals quality and scale to other retailers, distributors, and consumers.
- **Stronger margins at scale** : While unit prices are lower, bulk/multi-pack formats and high sell-through volumes can improve overall economics for brands.
- **Access to a loyal shopper base** : Club members pay annual fees for access, which correlates with higher basket sizes, more frequent large-format purchases, and strong repeat-visit behavior.
- **Faster path to national distribution:** A handful of club accounts can offer a distribution breadth that would take years to build store-by-store through traditional grocery or specialty retail.


## Wholesale Club Retail Compliance


When going into club stores, you need to understand lead times, promotional plans, display-ready pallet requirements, and minimum order quantities. Consult with a broker if you’re new to the process.


### How to Become a Costco Supplier


Costco is known for its treasure hunt mentality, meaning the store goes out on a limb for unique products, as long as the retailer meets the Costco vendor requirements:


- Pass all industry audits
- Provide ingredient traceability
- Meet pallet and packaging requirements (important for safety and marketability)
- Support EDI and EFT capabilities
- Maintain liability insurance based on Costco’s risk assessment
- Meet sustainability standards


Costco requires certifications beyond GFSI standards, but advises to wait until a buyer accepts your product before attaining them. If interested, the Costco buyer will guide you through the process.


When[pitching to a Costco buyer](https://thefreshfactory.co/blog/pitching-food-brand/) , expect to be asked questions about your manufacturing process, manufacturer, and what raw materials are used. Partnering with a[co-manufacturer](https://thefreshfactory.co/manufacturing/) with expertise in Costco requirements, like The Fresh Factory, can better prepare you for that meeting.


Ready to take the leap? Costco provides an[informational page](https://www.costco.com/f/-/supplier-diversity) for suppliers with a form to fill out to start the conversation.


### How to Become a Sam’s Club Supplier


While Costco looks for novel items to add to its limited SKU rotation, Sam’s Club sticks to tried and true products. Walmart offers a[supplier onboarding readiness checklist](https://corporate.walmart.com/content/dam/corporate/sams-club/documents/sams-club-suppliers/supplier-checklist.pdf) with information on Sam’s Club supplier requirements, which include:


- Register with a DUNS number and complete company, tax, and banking information
- Pass required facility audits
- Meet[insurance requirements](https://corporate.walmart.com/content/dam/corporate/documents/suppliers/requirements/insurance-requirements.pdf) , including listing Walmart as an additional insured party
- Set up EDI (or WebEDI) integration to support order, invoice, and shipment data
- Meet[Walmart supply chain standards](https://corporate.walmart.com/content/dam/corporate/documents/suppliers/requirements/supply-chain-packaging-guide.pdf) for packaging and delivery


In addition, suppliers must be aligned with Walmart’s[core values](https://corporate.walmart.com/suppliers/requirements/standards-for-suppliers) : respect, service, excellence, and integrity. Interested suppliers can[apply through Retail Link](https://corporate.walmart.com/suppliers/apply-to-be-a-supplier) .


### How to Become a BJ’s Wholesale Club Supplier


BJ’s Wholesale Club buyers use[RangeMe](https://www.rangeme.com/bjs) for product discovery, making it one of the more approachable channels for smaller suppliers looking to break into club retail. Vendor requirements include:


- Submit a product sample along with a product information sheet detailing pricing, availability, and product specs
- Meet BJ’s safety and compliance standards for materials, labeling, and packaging
- Demonstrate a reliable supply chain able to meet the company’s demand for products at volume


Once a sample and product sheet are submitted, a BJ’s buyer reviews the submission for category fit before extending a vendor invitation. BJ’s looks for products with a clear, provable demand signal.


## Achieving Buy-Through at Club Stores


Getting accepted by a club retailer is only half the battle. Consistent sell-through determines whether you stay. Club buyers track velocity closely and remove underperforming items fast. To protect your spot:


- **Forecast demand accurately** : Empty shelves cost you sales and credibility with the buyer, while excess inventory can trigger costly markdowns or returns.
- **Plan for promotional cadence** : Club stores rely on rotating promotions and roadshows to get shoppers to try new items. Build promotional pricing and timing into your supply chain planning well in advance, as most clubs require lead times of several weeks to months to secure a promotional slot.
- **Nail display-ready packaging** : **** Club channels expect pallet-ready, shelf-ready packaging that requires minimal labor to set. In addition, your packaging should act as a billboard that quickly demonstrates the product’s value and differentiators.
- **Maintain consistent quality and supply** : **** Club buyers have limited SKU slots and little patience for instability. A single quality issue or missed delivery window can jeopardize the relationship.
- **Track your metrics like a buyer would** : Coming to a buyer meeting with your own performance data builds trust and makes renewal conversations easier. Know your sell-through rate, inventory turns, and margin contribution for each retailer.
- **Stay innovative** : Club stores, especially Costco, reward brands that keep refreshing their offering. New sizes, flavors, or seasonal items can re-energize a listing and justify continued shelf space.
