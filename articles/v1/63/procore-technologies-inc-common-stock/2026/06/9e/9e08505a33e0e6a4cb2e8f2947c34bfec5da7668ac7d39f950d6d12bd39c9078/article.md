# Track your construction materials from purchase to installation

Every construction project relies on the right people, equipment, and materials being available at the right time. When they aren't, schedules slip, crews stand idle, and costs quickly add up.


The price of construction materials keeps rising. Copper wire and cable prices jumped more than[24% over the past year,](https://www.agc.org/news/2026/06/11/prices-construction-materials-climb-highest-rate-pandemic-driven-steep-cost-increases-fuel-aluminum#:~:text=The%20producer%20price%20index%20for,of%20government%20data%20released%20today.) and the broader basket of construction materials increased[9.6% year over year](https://www.agc.org/sites/default/files/Files/Communications/PPI_Tables_May_2026.pdf) —more than double the rate of consumer inflation. When materials are that valuable, losing track of a shipment isn't just frustrating—it's a direct hit to project margins. Yet tracking materials after purchasing remains a heavy administrative burden. Contractors are forced into a difficult balancing act: tying up cash in excess inventory that sits unused or delaying work because critical materials aren't available when crews are ready to install them.


For self-performing general contractors and specialty contractors, material control breaks down across three distinct phases:


-


**Supply chain blind spots** that disrupt scheduling before crews ever arrive on site


-


**Inaccurate inventory counts** that leave teams guessing about quantities, locations, and receiving statuses


-


**Inefficient field issuance** that stalls installation and tanks crew productivity during complex material moves


**Generally available today, Procore Materials** is part of Procore's broader Resource Management vision—bringing labor, equipment, and materials together on one integrated platform. By connecting post-procurement workflows into a single hub, Procore Materials gives office and field teams a shared view of material status to improve resource planning, reduce waste, help protect margins, and keep projects moving with confidence.


### **Reduce non-productive field time**


Equipment and crews cannot install what isn't on site. When materials are delayed, your project schedule slips while your overhead costs rise. Paid labor waits around, and rented equipment sits idle. In fact, field data shows that **a quarter of a worker’s day is spent on non-productive tasks** —with a significant portion wasted hunting down materials or waiting on deliveries.


While you cannot control every global supply chain disruption, you *can* control how your project adapts. Procore Materials gives your teams real-time tracking to help spot delivery bottlenecks early, allowing you to proactively adjust your workflows and shift your crews to other active project areas.


By pulling purchasing and tracking data into one clear view, Procore Materials gives you field-ready control over your resources:


-


**Mobile receiving:** Track and verify material shipments directly from a mobile device


-


**Automated 3-way match:** Instantly cross-reference POs, packing slips, and invoices to help prevent overpayment and billing errors


-


**Real-time location tracking:** Know exactly where your materials assets are to prevent time wasted searching for them


-


**Scope-readiness planning:** Align your inventory with your project schedule to plan material needs by scope start date, giving your crews exactly what they need before a specific phase of work begins

Relying on disconnected Excel spreadsheets, paper logs, and isolated email updates inevitably creates administrative lag. When a shipment status changes, that critical data is usually trapped in one person's inbox. The rest of the team remains in the dark until a scheduling conflict occurs.


Procore Materials helps remove these data silos by putting your material status, constraints, and locations in one central hub where every stakeholder has visibility.
