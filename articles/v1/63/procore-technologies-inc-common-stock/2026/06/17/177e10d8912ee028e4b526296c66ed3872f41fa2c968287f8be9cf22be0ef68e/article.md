When a construction project closes, the real operational lifecycle for an owner begins — and Procore is built to ensure a seamless transition.


### **Transition to standardized operations.**


At the close of a construction project, there are two elements required to protect an owner’s substantial financial investment:


#### **Construction plan of record**


This is the sum total of all designs, updates, changes, financial records, and communications associated with the asset. The Plan of Record establishes data ownership. Savvy owners use Procore to capture and maintain construction data in their own instance to help prevent scaling challenges and preserve cross-program standardization as their capital portfolio expands.


#### **Capital asset lifecycle**


An asset register interlocks construction execution history and future operations and maintenance of each piece of equipment designated for use across a site. From facility systems like HVAC to structural components, the asset register forms the foundation of how a capital investment will be maintained over its lifespan. Procore enables layers of execution and warranty data to link directly to the asset register over time.


To streamline the usefulness of this data, Procore’s 400+ integrations—including operating and maintenance (O&M) and facilities management software—helps make sure that cross-departmental teams have access to trusted and accurate data.


Procore’s Integrated Handover designates warranty and maintenance data associated with each critical asset in the register, packaged cleanly and presented to the owner for immediate use with facilities management and operations teams. This helps eliminate operational friction and protects asset value from day one.
