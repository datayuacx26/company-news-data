# Putting your iron to work: A smarter way to track, manage, and maintain your fleet

Whether it’s a kitted-out service van, a specialized concrete pump, or a fleet of excavators, your iron is either making you money or costing you money. When you don’t have eyes on how or if that gear is being used, you’re paying the Iron Tax. This is the equipment version of the[lag-time leak](https://www.procore.com/blog/lag-time-leak) — the gap between what’s happening on the ground and what’s showing up on your financial reports.


### **Stopping the portal hop: Ending the visibility crisis**


For years, the industry’s answer to tracking equipment was just more software. You had one or more logins to track your owned equipment fleet, another for your rental portal, and a shop whiteboard that was usually three days behind. But this isn't just an IT headache. It’s a visibility crisis that leads to the double-buy.


When your records are scattered, you can’t see your total capacity to solve a problem on the fly. If a crew hits a snag and needs a skid steer immediately, a foreman shouldn't have to check three different apps to see if one is free. Without a unified view, the path of least resistance — calling in a new rental — can start to look attractive.


**We’re ending that guesswork by bringing Telematics directly into Procore** . Having your owned and rented assets visible in a single window helps make sure you never pay for a rental when the solution is already sitting in your fleet.


### **Maximizing usage and precision costing**


While a fuel spill is easy to spot and fix, the cost of a machine sitting idle is a much harder leak to track — and a much more expensive one to ignore. Integrating Telematics replaces the guesswork of "I think we have one available" with the certainty of seeing exactly where your fleet is standing.


Leveraging Map View allows you to identify the nearest available equipment instantly, moving assets with precision to help ensure a machine never sits in a staging area while another jobsite is forced to eat the cost of a third-party rental. Plus, this data flows directly into your job costing. We’re replacing inaccurate paper logs with a system that allows every hour of machine time to be captured and mapped to the correct job costing code.


You stop guessing at your burdened equipment rates and start bidding with the confidence of someone who knows their numbers to the penny.


### **Plugging the rental leakage**


One of the most expensive leaks for a specialty contractor is rental leakage — a machine sits idle for a week because the field forgot to call it in, or you get hit with over-billing because a contract end-date slipped through the cracks.


Through our integration with United Rentals, you can gain instant visibility into equipment status, engine activity, and contract dates for your rented equipment. This empowers your team to off-rent idle or past-due assets the second the work is done. It turns decentralized field rental decisions into a coordinated strategy, helping you stop those costly overcharges before they hit your mailbox.


### **Reducing downtime with proactive care**


The final piece of the leak is the unexpected breakdown. Usually, the office finds out a machine needs service only after it starts smoking. Telematics changes that conversation, moving your maintenance from a break-fix mentality to a proactive strategy.


By integrating actual run-time data and inspection records into one platform, you can guide maintenance based on reality, not a calendar. Catching a $500 oil change on a slow Saturday is always better than being held hostage by a $5,000 emergency repair on a Tuesday morning while your crew stands around waiting to work.


### **Iron doesn’t lie.**


At the end of the day, your equipment is an investment meant to grow your business, not grow your risk. When you put the right technology behind your iron, you aren’t just tracking a GPS dot on a map — you’re able to protect your margin, and know every asset you own is pulling its weight.
