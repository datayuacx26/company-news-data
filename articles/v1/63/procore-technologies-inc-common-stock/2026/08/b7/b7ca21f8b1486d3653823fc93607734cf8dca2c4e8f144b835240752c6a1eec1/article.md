# Stop inheriting chaos at handover: Procore Asset Management is now generally available

The most valuable record on your project isn’t created at handover. It’s created during construction — or it’s never reliably created at all.


Today, we’re closing that gap. We’re introducing **Procore Asset Management** , a new solution that builds a verified, structured record of a project’s physical assets as they’re designed and built, ready to hand over the moment a facility opens. And the foundational tool of that solution, the **Asset Register** , is now generally available across all regions, at both the company and project level.


Asset Management is built for the design and build phases of an asset’s life. The Asset Register is where it starts: the foundation that captures what’s installed, links it to the work that verifies it, and carries that record cleanly into operations. More capabilities will join the solution over time.


Here’s what the Asset Register does today, why it matters for your teams, and what’s next.


### **Move from reactive handover to proactive lifecycle readiness**


Ask anyone who's commissioned a facility what handover looks like. The answer usually involves a mess someone else has to clean up.


A new facility changes hands as a chaotic mix of binders, spreadsheets, and disconnected files. A "data dump" someone has to untangle before operations can start. Facilities teams spend weeks re-entering data that already existed on site. Owners inherit a record that’s difficult to trust. And the documentation that should have been captured at installation gets reconstructed from memory, months after the site crews have left.


The problem isn’t a missing report; it’s a missing system of record. Every other part of a project generates data *about* something. A submittal approves it, an inspection verifies it, an RFI clarifies it. But there’s been nothing to tie that activity to the physical thing it describes. So the proof scatters, and the chain of evidence breaks.


### **Where Asset Management starts: The Asset Register**


The Asset Register is the foundation of Asset Management: a centralized record of every fixed, installed, maintainable asset on your project. Not a spreadsheet, not a binder, but a live, structured register that takes shape from the construction workflows your teams are already doing.


Instead of compiling a handover package at the end, your teams link installed fixed assets (HVAC systems, chillers, switchgear, fire protection, boilers) directly to the inspections, RFIs, submittals, schedule activities, and documents that give them context, as the work happens.


The result is a continuous digital thread for every asset: what was installed, who recorded it, when it was inspected, and the O&M documentation that sits behind it. When close-out arrives, there’s nothing to scramble for. The record is already built.


### **What the Asset Register delivers at general availability**


The Asset Register reaches general availability across all regions: a full-featured foundation, not a minimal first release. Four handover problems that have cost teams for decades, fixed from day one.


**Set your standards once, not on every project** . Most teams start each job with a blank spreadsheet, so the same pump gets named five ways, and nothing adds up across projects. Set your asset types, fields, statuses, and naming rules once at the company level, and every project automatically follows them. Assets roll up to a portfolio view or drill down to a single job, and when one moves, its history goes with it. You get consistency without policing it by hand.


**Capture verified data in the flow of work.** Handover data is untrustworthy because it's pieced together at the end, after the crews who did the work are gone. Instead, link every asset to the inspections, RFIs, submittals, and schedule activities that verify it, as the work happens. Every change is timestamped and stamped with a name. So when a warranty claim or dispute lands months later, you point to the passed inspection and the approved submittal instead of arguing from memory.


**Move fast at scale.** Keep the register current without a dedicated admin. On a big job, building an asset register by hand is a non-starter. Create, edit, and delete in bulk, and import or update straight from Excel, including the exact file you just exported. Save the views your team actually uses, and push asset data into 360 Reporting and Procore Analytics to see how everything's performing across your portfolio. The register keeps up with the work instead of falling a month behind it.


**Hand over a record they can use on day one, not a data dump they have to decode** . This is the whole point. Instead of a hard drive full of PDFs, you deliver a structured, verified record that the owner's facilities team can load straight into their systems. Export filtered assets to Excel or a structured ZIP, submittal and related-item attachments included, from the list or BIM view. In the field, scan a QR code to pull up an asset's full record on the spot, and find any asset through Global Project Search. Permissions carry through everywhere, so people only see what they're cleared to. Day one is actually day one.


The Asset Register doesn't replace your EAM or CMMS, and it isn't trying to. It works during design and construction, then hands your operational systems a clean, structured record to run with. And it tracks one thing well: fixed, installed, maintainable assets like HVAC, switchgear, and fire protection, not loose materials or movable equipment.


### **What it means for your teams**


It’s the same record doing the work, but the story looks different depending on which side of handover you’re standing on.


#### **For owners: Achieve operational readiness on day one**


Picture the alternative most owners live with today. The contractor hands over a hard drive and a stack of binders, and your facilities team spends the first few months of occupancy reverse-engineering their own building: chasing down which pump was actually installed, hunting for a warranty that may or may not exist, re-keying data into the CMMS by hand. The building is open, but the record isn’t ready.


Asset Register flips that sequence. Because every fixed asset has been verified and linked throughout construction, what you receive on day one isn’t a data dump — it’s a living register your team can trust. The chiller in the register is the one on the roof, with its submittal, inspection history, and documentation already attached. Your facilities team starts from a known baseline instead of a question mark, and full operational readiness arrives on day one instead of months in.


That record keeps earning its place long after commissioning. The same verified data enhances asset evaluations over time, informing maintenance, replacement, and capital strategy across a 30–50 year lifecycle. It's the bridge from building the asset to running it, and it's why Asset Register is built to feed the[enterprise systems Owners already run](https://www.procore.com/owners) , not to replace them.


#### **For general and specialty contractors: Hand over clean, get paid faster, win the next job**


The pain of handover has always landed at the worst possible moment: at the end, when the crews are demobilizing, and final payment is on the line. The old way means someone spends the last weeks of a job assembling O&M manuals from scattered emails, tracking down as-builts, and reconstructing what got installed from memory. Retainage waits on paperwork that doesn't exist yet.


The Asset Register removes that scramble by capturing the record as the work happens. The inspections, RFIs, and submittals your teams are already completing link directly to the assets they describe, so the handover package assembles itself in the background. A specialty crew can scan a QR code at an installed asset and pull up its full record on the spot, confirming they're working from the current, approved information before they install. By the time you reach close-out, the deliverable is already built, in the structured format owners require, with the smart IDs that plug straight into their systems.


The payoff shows up in two places that matter most. **Cash flow** : a complete, owner-ready handover means retainage is far less likely to stall because documentation doesn't yet exist. And **risk** : if a warranty claim or a rework dispute surfaces months later, you're not defending yourself from memory. You have a timestamped chain of evidence, the approved submittal version, and the passed inspection that prove the work was done right.


Clean handovers protect the margin on this job. They're also what wins the next one.


### **Built for compliance-driven markets**


The "why" behind Asset Management shifts by region. Everywhere, the core driver is the same: certainty and operational readiness, by capturing verified data as the work happens to protect builder margins and ensure Day 1 facility activation for owners.


In the UK and Europe, that same record carries additional regulatory weight. The change history, linked documents, and structured information captured in the Asset Register map to ISO 19650 information management duties and support the[UK Building Safety Act](https://www.procore.com/en-gb/library/golden-thread-building-safety-act) 's Golden Thread obligation, a verifiable thread of information Owners and Main Contractors are required to deliver. For Owner-operators managing assets over decades, that construction-phase record also aligns with ISO 55000 / 55001 lifecycle frameworks and becomes the foundation your facility management and sustainability teams build on. Compliance builds up as the work happens, instead of as a scramble at practical completion.


For UK and EU companies, the **Assets and BIM integration (Viewer 2.0) is also generally available** on new projects, so you can extract assets from 3D models and work with them visually against field reality. Teams in North America and APAC can access this BIM integration open beta by contacting their Procore representative.

The Asset Register is the first step toward it.
