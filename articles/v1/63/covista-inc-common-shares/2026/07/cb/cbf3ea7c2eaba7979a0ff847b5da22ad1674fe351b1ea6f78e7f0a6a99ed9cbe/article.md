# Covista Earns Five National Honors Recognizing its Workplace Culture, Career Growth Opportunities and Commitment to Responsible Business

[View All News](https://investors.covista.com/press-releases/default.aspx)


### Covista Earns Five National Honors Recognizing its Workplace Culture, Career Growth Opportunities and Commitment to Responsible Business


July 20, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s21.q4cdn.com/400572492/files/doc_news/Covista-Earns-Five-National-Honors-Recognizing-its-Workplace-Culture-Career-Growth-Opportunities-and-Commitment-to-Responsible-Business-2026.pdf)


*Awards highlight the company's investment in colleagues, students and the communities it serves*


CHICAGO--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Covista (formerly Adtalem Global Education), America's largest healthcare educator, today announced it has been recognized with five prestigious national awards honoring the company's workplace culture, employee experience and commitment to corporate responsibility. The recognitions confirm Covista as one of the best healthcare education companies to work for in the country, with honors from some of the most respected names in business media, including TIME, Forbes, Newsweek and U.S. News & World Report.


The awards represent feedback from thousands of employees, job seekers and members of the public, and underscore Covista's ongoing investment in building a workplace where employees can grow, thrive and do meaningful work, while building a career. Covista offers faculty, staff and colleagues the opportunity to shape the future of healthcare through education, training the next generation of healthcare professionals across five accredited institutions. The company's 10,000 faculty and colleagues come from a range of backgrounds—business, academia, healthcare, government, nonprofits—all united in the company's vision to be the destination where healthcare ambitions are made possible.


**Honors Include:**


**TIME World's Best Companies 2025**


Covista was named to the inaugural[TIME World's Best Companies 2025 list](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Ftime.com%2F7315547%2Fworlds-best-companies-of-2025%2F&esheet=54572561&newsitemid=20260720817412&lan=en-US&anchor=TIME+World%27s+Best+Companies+2025+list&index=1&md5=08602efddd6997b4645befdd89a048f6) , which highlights leading global organizations that exemplify excellence in today’s corporate landscape. Companies were evaluated using three primary data sources: Employee Satisfaction, Revenue Growth and Sustainability Transparency. Out of hundreds of thousands of data points assessed, the top 1,000 companies were recognized for outstanding performance across these key areas.


**Forbes America's Dream Employers 2026**


Covista was named one of[America's Dream Employers 2026 by Forbes](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.forbes.com%2Flists%2Famericas-dream-employers%2F&esheet=54572561&newsitemid=20260720817412&lan=en-US&anchor=America%27s+Dream+Employers+2026+by+Forbes&index=2&md5=5114bcab7318f5e5cc5621eb8796f23f) , based on an independent survey of college students and employees at companies with at least 1,000 U.S. workers over the past three years. More than 266,000 data points were gathered to determine how frequently Covista was named a “dream employer” and how likely employees are to recommend the company as a place to work.


**Newsweek's America's Most Responsible Companies 2026**


Covista was included on Newsweek's list of[America's Most Responsible Companies 2026](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Frankings.newsweek.com%2Famericas-most-responsible-companies-2026&esheet=54572561&newsitemid=20260720817412&lan=en-US&anchor=America%27s+Most+Responsible+Companies+2026&index=3&md5=ef6c13bf4a8c7cca0477aa5c85f8baed) , which focuses on a holistic view of corporate responsibility that considers all three pillars of ESG: Environment, Social and Corporate Governance. The analysis is based on the quantitative data from more than 30 KPI across the three areas of ESG, and CSR reputation, derived from an extensive survey conducted among U.S. residents, assessing the public’s perception of each company's social responsibility efforts.


**Forbes America's Best Employers for New Grads 2026**


Covista earned a place on the[Forbes list of America's Best Employers for New Grads 2026](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.forbes.com%2Flists%2Fbest-employers-for-new-grads%2F&esheet=54572561&newsitemid=20260720817412&lan=en-US&anchor=Forbes+list+of+America%27s+Best+Employers+for+New+Grads+2026&index=4&md5=ae4b97066fe1e2e9be47bf0c9cd3cc40) , recognizing companies where early-career professionals can build meaningful careers. The list was compiled from an independent survey of over 100,000 young professionals working for companies and institutions employing at least 1,000 employees in the U.S. from all industry sectors.


**U.S. News & World Report Best Company to Work For – Midwest**


Covista was named a[2026-2027 Best Company to Work For – Midwest by U.S. News & World Report](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fcareers.usnews.com%2Fcompanies%2Frankings%2Fbest-companies%2Fmidwest&esheet=54572561&newsitemid=20260720817412&lan=en-US&anchor=2026-2027+Best+Company+to+Work+For+%26%238211%3B+Midwest+by+U.S.+News+%26amp%3B+World+Report&index=5&md5=bd10117c99ec351499883860fcfad96d) . The rating evaluated nearly 3,900 public and private companies across factors including pay and benefits, work-life balance, job stability, workplace comfort and career development opportunities.


"Being recognized across so many respected national rankings is a powerful validation of the culture we’ve worked hard to build inside and out," said Sara Hill, Chief Human Resources Officer, Covista. "These honors belong to our colleagues. Their trust, feedback and everyday commitment to one another and to our students is what earned Covista this recognition, and we’re proud to keep investing in the kind of workplace where people can do their best work and build highly impactful careers."


The recognition also reflects Covista’s approach to creating a workplace where doing exceptional work and making a meaningful impact go together. A key part of this is ***Covista Open Doors*** , the company's multi-year impact commitment to build and sustain the healthcare workforce. Through complementary investments by Covista and the[Covista Foundation](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.covista.com%2Fcovista-foundation&esheet=54572561&newsitemid=20260720817412&lan=en-US&anchor=Covista+Foundation&index=6&md5=1952745bf9adc903f2d177c690be66d5) , an independent 501(c)(3) public charity, the organizations have committed[$10 million and 50,000 volunteer hours](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.covista.com%2Fnews-insights%2Fpress-releases%2Fcovista-and-the-covista-foundation-commit-10-million-and-50000-volunteer-hours-to-build-and-sustain-the-healthcare-workforce&esheet=54572561&newsitemid=20260720817412&lan=en-US&anchor=%2410+million+and+50%2C000+volunteer+hours&index=7&md5=0b72b6e9696e94262fdbe62ca271b25a) over the next five years. The investments advance a structured, three-part strategy focused on inspiring the next generation of healthcare professionals through career exploration programs and community partnerships; removing barriers for students through scholarships and emergency grants; and supporting the mental health and wellbeing of healthcare workers through evidence-based programs that address burnout and promote career longevity.


"When our colleagues have the support they need to do their best work, that strength ripples into the classroom and out into the communities we serve," said Megan Noel, Chief Corporate Affairs Officer, Covista. "By investing in our people and our communities, we are building a more responsible business and a lasting contributor to education, healthcare and broader society."


More information about Covista’s colleague experience and impact commitments can be found[here](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.covista.com&esheet=54572561&newsitemid=20260720817412&lan=en-US&anchor=here&index=8&md5=acfd48d859dcbb93809ddfa6fd1619d1) .


**About Covista**


Covista (NYSE: CVSA) is America's largest healthcare educator, serving more than 100,000 students and supported by a community of 400,000 alumni across five accredited institutions. Through personalized, tech-enabled education powered by 10,000 faculty and colleagues, Covista expands access to healthcare careers and addresses the U.S. healthcare workforce shortage at scale. Covista is the parent company of American University of the Caribbean School of Medicine, Chamberlain University, Ross University School of Medicine, Ross University School of Veterinary Medicine and Walden University.
