# Nike Signs Gamecocks Athletes to Blue Ribbon Elite NIL Roster as South Carolina Partnership Begins

Nike and the University of South Carolina are announcing the addition of five top Gamecocks athletes to Nike’s prestigious Blue Ribbon Elite NIL program, ushering in a new era of athlete partnership in Columbia.


Blue Ribbon Elite, a first-of-its-kind initiative, represents a broad portfolio of athletes across disciplines, reimagining the NIL space by providing leading athletes and universities an unmatched level of collaboration that prioritizes the future of sport and athlete identity.


The announcement comes as a 10-year partnership takes effect between Nike, a brand born from college sport and built to empower the next generation, and South Carolina, a longtime athletic juggernaut among the leading programs in college sport.[Unveiled last summer](https://gamecocksonline.com/news/2025/08/22/south-carolina-announces-10-year-partnership-with-nike/) , the partnership makes Nike the official supplier of uniforms, footwear, apparel and equipment for all 21 Gamecocks athletic programs.


“The opportunity for our student-athletes to leverage Nike’s Blue Ribbon Elite Program is an incredible benefit,” says Jeremiah Donati, Athletics Director, South Carolina. “NIL has created meaningful opportunities for our student-athletes to build and grow their personal brands, and we look forward to partnering with Nike to expand those opportunities and help position our student-athletes for success both during and beyond their time at South Carolina.”


Joining Nike’s growing roster of roughly 80 elite NIL athletes are:


- Joyce Edwards (basketball)
- Nyck Harbor (football)
- Chloe Kitts (basketball)
- LaNorris Sellers (football)
- Dylan Stewart (football)

The Blue Ribbon Elite program,[named after Nike’s humble beginnings](https://about.nike.com/en/magazine/nike-swoosh-logo-history) , reflects the brand‘s long-held leading position in sport, college and NIL partnership, as well as an enduring commitment to making athletes better — from pioneering the first truly comprehensive “all-sports” partnership in the 1980s to today’s creative, career-building collaborations with top NIL athletes.


Nike’s partnership with each Blue Ribbon Elite athlete goes beyond brand representation, inspiring the student body and community to collaborate through brand campaigns, product innovation and creative direction.


In short: When programs and athletes sign with Nike, they don’t just rep the Swoosh — they help shape it.


Nike’s commitment to listening to the voices of its NIL athletes takes shape in individual, personalized support, both in their performance and training and in their most important moments away from the game. From product and styling to support with media and content, Nike leverages the full weight of the brand to provide a best-in-class partnership to athletes across its NIL roster — giving them tools for long-term success across sport, business and culture.


Nike’s NIL partnerships also extend to athlete-centered design and innovation, as well as support for athletes in their pursuit to stand for something bigger. This industry-leading approach reflects Nike’s five-decade history as the brand of athletes and underscores its continued commitment to inspiring and uplifting the next generation.


“South Carolina has built one of the most incredible athletic cultures in the country, and that kind of greatness deserves a partner that knows how to fuel it,” says Ann Miller, EVP, Global Sports Marketing. “From Dawn Staley’s program to every Gamecock competing for a title, we’re proud to put the Swoosh on their uniforms and welcome them as the newest member of our Nike Blue Ribbon Elite family.”
