# Innovation Meets Style: The One-of-One Radical AirFlow Looks at This Year’s Pre Classic

**In a sport decided by hundredths of a second, every edge matters,** physical and mental alike. At this year’s[Prefontaine Classic](https://about.nike.com/en/magazine/incredible-scenes-from-the-50th-anniversary-of-the-pre-classic) , where the world’s top track and field athletes were competing to be the fastest and best in their sport, one edge came in the form of custom warm-up apparel.


Ask Masai Russell, who claimed the women’s 100-meter hurdles after warming up in Nike’s newest cooling innovation: a one-of-one[Radical AirFlow](https://about.nike.com/en/magazine/what-is-nike-radical-airflow) garment designed specifically for her. The breathable material, which launched in[trail running](https://about.nike.com/en/magazine/utmb-all-conditions-racing-department-athletes-trail-running) in 2025 and is now making its debut across the track,[court](https://www.instagram.com/p/DaQJ_fBjdoS/?hl=en&img_index=1) and[beyond](https://about.nike.com/en/magazine/eliud-kipchoge-radical-airflow-new-york-city-marathon) , is built to harness airflow and maximize the body’s natural cooling system.


“I was astonished, because I’d seen the Radical AirFlow material and felt the texture before,” says Masai, “but actually trying it on and seeing it here in real life, in the flesh, was insane. I was so impressed by the feel.”


She wasn’t the only one. Eleven Nike athletes wore bespoke Radical AirFlow tops at Pre, including Tara Davis-Woodhall, who took the W for the women's long jump, and Melissa Jefferson-Wooden, who won the women’s 100 meters.


Designed with each athlete’s event and sense of style in mind, the garments served multiple purposes: Keep them cool, keep them confident, and unleash their speed.
