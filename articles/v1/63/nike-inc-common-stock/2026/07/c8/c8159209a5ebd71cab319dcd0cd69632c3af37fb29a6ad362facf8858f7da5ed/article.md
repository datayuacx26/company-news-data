# Inside A’ja Wilson’s Custom All-Star Tunnel Walk Look

With one-of-one projects like these, Giorgi understands that every successful collaboration starts with listening.


“It’s really important that these collections look and feel authentic to the athlete: who they want to be, what kind of vision they want to bring to life for their fans,” explains Giorgi. “I’ve worked with A’ja since the beginning, and I always tell her to make sure that she’s giving us her honest truth.”


Billingsley agrees, noting that years of working with A’ja has helped him understand her look down to its core. Style-wise, she loves pieces that have presence. She appreciates great tailoring, interesting proportions, textures, and details that tell a story.


“She wants to feel powerful without looking like she’s trying too hard,” he says. “That’s a balance I really enjoy because it’s less about dressing someone and more about revealing what’s already there.”


[A’ja knows exactly who she is](https://about.nike.com/en/magazine/behind-the-design-a-ja-wilson-signature-shoe) and doesn’t chase trends, he explains. She gravitates toward pieces that feel authentic. “She’s confident, she’s playful, she’s competitive, and she’s got that Southern warmth where she can make anybody feel comfortable. But don’t mistake that kindness for softness. There’s a real strength there.”


Being from the South himself, Billingsley says he and A’ja share an understanding that getting dressed is about more than looking good. It’s about respect — for yourself, for where you came from, and for the moment you’re stepping into.


They also know how to have fun with it.


“A’ja has a great sense of humor, so we’ll laugh through a fitting,” he says. “But when it’s time to create, she’s incredibly thoughtful. She asks questions, she’s collaborative, and she trusts the process. If it looks good on her, she’s in.”
