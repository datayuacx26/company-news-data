# 5 Questions With Multi-Hyphenate Quenlin Blackwell

**Q: What role does movement play in your life? Do you have any particular routines?**


Movement is how I stay sane. My job is chaotic, and my brain is louder than most people’s, so training is the one place where I’m not performing for anyone. Also, I walk runways now, so my body literally is part of the job. The hardest thing I’ve ever trained for was the runway. People think you just walk. You do not just walk. That was a childhood dream, and I was not about to be mediocre at my own dream. I was a cheerleader growing up in Texas. Full uniform, full commitment. Cheer taught me performance before I even knew that’s what I was training for. You learn how to be watched and still hit your mark.


As for my routines, I do sessions with a trainer, lots of walking. And recovery is sacred to me. Baths over showers, always. I’ve been drinking less. I watched a microplastics documentary, and now I have a stainless steel water bottle like everyone else who’s seen it. Wellness for me is also spiritual. I’m deep into astrology, and I treat my body like it’s part of the practice.


**Q: Do you remember your first pair of Nikes?**


White Air Force 1s from the Nike Factory Store at Allen Premium Outlets in Texas. My mom had a one-shoe-a-year rule, so that decision was serious. I kept them immaculate. Today, my favorite pair is my MF Doom SB Dunks. They were a gift from someone who told me my sneaker game was wack, and honestly, he was right. Now I own grails. I also wore my Nike Air Rifts, the tabi ones, through all of Coachella. Split-toe supremacy.


My dream Nike collab would be a shoe that feels like me: vintage soul, modern body. I’d love to bring the Air Rift back into the conversation or do a Dunk with details nobody’s done. Vintage materials on a silhouette everybody knows. Something collectors respect but the girls actually wear.


I’m the person who rewears and reworks, so I’d want to design one perfect sneaker with a story in every detail. Something people keep for 10 years, not a hype drop they flip.


**Q: What are you most excited about with this partnership?**


I love that it’s a real partnership and not a one-off. I’ve done campaigns, but this is a full year of building something together. I’ve been buying Nikes since I was a kid in Texas, so getting to design with the brand is a full-circle moment my seventh grade self would not believe.


A great partnership is when a brand trusts me to be me. My audience has been with me since I was 12, and they can smell a script from a mile away. The best partnerships give me a real seat at the creative table and let the work feel like something I’d make anyway.


Nike is about giving athletes what they need to perform at the highest level, and I’ve been performing my whole life, on the internet, on runways, on camera. We’re both obsessed with not being mediocre. Culture moves through my world and Nike’s world at the same speed. We both live where the girls and the sneakerheads overlap.
