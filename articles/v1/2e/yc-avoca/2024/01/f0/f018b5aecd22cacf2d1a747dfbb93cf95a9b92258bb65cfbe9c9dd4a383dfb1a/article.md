# Top HVAC Podcasts for 2025

Tyson Chen


Co-Founder


January 19, 2024


hvac


In the ever-evolving world of HVAC services, staying ahead of the curve is crucial for business growth. One of the best ways to keep abreast of industry trends, marketing strategies, and technological advancements is through insightful podcasts. Here are some valuable podcasts that offer a wealth of knowledge and tips to help your HVAC business thrive.


## 1. HVAC Know It All Podcast


**Episode:** Evolution Of An HVAC Business Ep.19 : HVAC Marketing That Works


This episode features the owner of McCready HVAC and Refrigeration Services sharing successful strategies for building a robust customer base. It covers maintaining a positive online presence, the effectiveness of targeted Facebook ads, community involvement, and the advantages of using a professionally wrapped service van as a mobile advertisement.


## 2. Service Business Mastery for Skilled Trades


**Episode:** How AI Is Going To Impact The Customer Service Side Of Home Services


Hosts Josh and Tersh, along with AI expert Tyson from Avoca AI, delve into the integration of AI in customer service for HVAC and plumbing. The episode covers how Avoca’s AI digital assistant works with systems like Service Titan, challenges in voice AI, and the future of AI in customer service.


## 3. HVAC Success Secrets


**Episode:** EP: 178 Sarah Ghirardo w/ ServiceTitan - Revolutionizing Marketing Strategies


Sarah Gerardo from Service Titan discusses branding and marketing strategies for dominating the market. The episode emphasizes word-of-mouth, community involvement, personal branding, and the use of company vehicles for advertising.


## 4. HVAC R&D


**Episode:** One Thing at a Time: Brittany Murphy Breaks Down Marketing for the Trades in 2024


Hosts Rhydon, Dennis, and Brittany Murphy discuss digital marketing strategies, incentivizing customers for reviews, customer cultivation, and the importance of a strong online presence.


## 5. Can’t Stop the Growth


**Episode:** The Future of Digital Marketing with Katie Donovan


Chad and Katie Donovan of Camp Digital discuss company culture and digital marketing trends. The conversation includes shifts in HVAC install and repair searches and the importance of customer service and Google Business profiles.


Each of these podcasts offers a unique perspective and practical advice for HVAC business owners. Whether it’s exploring new marketing strategies, understanding the impact of AI on customer service, or staying updated with digital trends, these podcasts are valuable resources for anyone looking to grow their HVAC business in today’s competitive environment.
