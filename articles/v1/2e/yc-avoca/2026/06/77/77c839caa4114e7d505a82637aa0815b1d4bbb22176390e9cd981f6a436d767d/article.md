# AI CSR Features That Matter Most During Busy Season

Carey Landon


Product Marketing


June 9, 2026


Busy season is where AI front offices either prove their value or quietly become a liability. Avoca is built specifically for home services, and that means building features that hold up when call volume spikes and your team is stretched.


Here's what to have configured to handle peak:


## **Live Capacity**


A true source of availability is foundational to how an AI CSR performs. Avoca reads directly from your CRM so it's always booking against what's actually on the board as opposed to a static window. There are cases where you may want to overbook intentionally, and Avoca supports that, but either way a live view is critical.


The Live Capacity view exists so you can see exactly what Avoca sees and manage around real capacity.


## **Priority Booking**


When your board is full and a high-value replacement call comes in, your AI CSR needs to handle it the way a good dispatcher would: book the replacement, move the tune-up.


Avoca's booking tiers make that explicit:


- **P1:** emergencies and urgent calls, booked immediately regardless of what's on the schedule
- **P2:** replacements and installs, which can overbook lower-priority slots
- **P3:** maintenance and tune-ups, which fill available capacity but yield to better work


Priority can be defined by equipment age, membership status, warranty, call type, or a combination. Use buffer days to push lower-priority work farther out during peak weeks and pull it back in when things slow down.


## **Intelligent Task Routing**


When hundreds of calls are coming in, your team can't manually review every notification to find the ones that need follow-up. Intelligent task routing does that automatically.


Define the call types that matter and Avoca routes them to the right person with an AI-generated summary already attached. Your team can manage their day and managers have a view of everything that needs to get done.


Common task types to configure:


- Calls that mention installs route to inside sales
- Old-equipment callers get flagged for a replacement conversation
- Dropped calls get instant follow-up


## **Holiday Hours**


It sounds simple, but being able to quickly update booking windows and holiday hours matters more than most shops realize. If your AI CSR doesn't know you're closed, it will keep booking.


Avoca makes it easy to set custom availability for any holiday or schedule change before the season gets hectic.


### **Emergency Guidance**


Busy season brings scenarios your standard booking rules may not account for. Emergency guidance lets you give your AI CSR specific instructions for edge cases: a gas smell, a customer without A/C during a heat wave, equipment that's been out for days. You define what qualifies and how your AI should respond, and it applies consistently across every call.


## **Avoca’s Commitment During Peak**


Many shops are adopting AI CSRs to manage customer calls, but an AI CSR is only as strong as the features behind it.


We're building Avoca to handle customers during the busiest time of year with features that give your AI CSR the context and flexibility to manage complex requests when volume is high. We know the busy season is critical for your business, and we don't take that responsibility lightly.
