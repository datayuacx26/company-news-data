- Its credits are overpriced.

### Reason 1: Steep Learning Curve


Due to Clay’s extensive features and integrations, being a new user, you might often struggle to start a complete prospecting process immediately. Being relatively new to the market, it has occasional bugs and slow performance.


When you use Clay, you will need time to understand its functionalities before achieving its full potential.

### Reason 2: Overpriced Credit System


Many users on review websites observed that credits provide poor value compared to using API keys directly. Sometimes, they’re up to ten times more expensive than alternative options for data enrichment. This results in a quick depletion of credits, especially when seeking customization or personalization.


Additionally, the pricing structure is complicated as different actions consume varying amounts of credits.


[Source](https://www.g2.com/products/clay-com-clay/reviews/clay-review-9370474)


### Reason 3: Limited Spreadsheet Features


Clay’s lack of advanced spreadsheet features, such as formatting options, restricts its effectiveness as a standalone tool for data enrichment. Users often find themselves relying on Google Sheets for tasks that require better formatting.


This reliance on separate tools complicates the process, disrupts their workflow, and reduces overall efficiency.
