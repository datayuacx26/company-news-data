If you work in finance or[accounting](https://www.veryfi.com/solutions/accountants/) , you know the drill. Invoices pile up. Receipts get lost. Someone’s always chasing down that one missing expense report. And meanwhile, you’re manually typing data from paper documents into your accounting system like it’s 1995.


Here’s the thing though: it doesn’t have to be this way anymore. At Veryfi, we’ve built AI that actually handles this stuff for you. Not just scanning documents and hoping for the best, really understanding them and pulling out exactly what you need.


## **What is Financial Document Processing?**


It’s basically getting computers to do the boring part of accounting. Instead of you typing invoice numbers, amounts, and vendor names into QuickBooks or NetSuite, software does it for you. It reads the document, grabs the important info, and puts it where it needs to go.


Imagine having an assistant who never gets tired and can knock out a thousand invoices in the time it takes you to finish your morning coffee. That’s what we’re talking about.


## **Why Manual Processing is Slowly Killing Your Team**


Let’s talk about what’s actually happening in most finance departments right now.


Your AP team is drowning in invoices, manually keying in data for hours every week. Someone else has to spot-check everything because humans make typos. Documents vanish into email chains or get buried on someone’s desk. And then month-end rolls around and it’s absolute chaos trying to make sure everything’s accounted for.


But the real cost isn’t just the time. Manual processing means errors, and a decimal point in the wrong place can really mess up your financial reports. It creates bottlenecks that delay payments, which can strain vendor relationships or cause you to miss out on those 2% early payment discounts that actually add up.


This is exactly why companies came to us. They were tired of the manual grind and needed a way to actually scale their finance operations without hiring an army of data entry people.


## **How Veryfi Actually Works**


We’ve combined a bunch of different AI technologies to make document processing that, honestly, just works.


**OCR That Can Handle Real Life** ****[Our OCR](https://www.veryfi.com/) doesn’t fall apart when you hand it a crumpled receipt or a blurry phone photo. It can read scanned PDFs, images, faxes (yes, some companies still use fax), even that receipt you accidentally put through the washing machine.


The AI understands context. It knows that the number next to “Total” is probably what you need to pay, not the subtotal or the tax amount. It can tell the difference between an invoice number and a PO number. Basically, it reads documents the way a human would, but without getting bored or making mistakes.


**It Gets Smarter Over Time** **** Veryfi learns your vendors, your workflows, even your chart of accounts. The more you use it, the better it gets at handling your specific documents. We’re also continuously training our models on millions of real-world documents, so we’re always improving accuracy across every industry.


**We Pull Out Everything You Need** **** We don’t just grab the vendor name and total and call it a day. Veryfi extracts line-item details, tax breakdowns, payment terms, PO numbers, dates, shipping info, basically any field you need. And we return it all as clean, structured data that plugs right into your existing systems.


**Built-In Error Catching** The platform automatically validates data, flags potential duplicates, and catches errors before they become problems. We’ve even built in fraud detection to spot suspicious patterns or anomalies. It’s like having a super careful accountant double-checking everything, but without the paranoia.
