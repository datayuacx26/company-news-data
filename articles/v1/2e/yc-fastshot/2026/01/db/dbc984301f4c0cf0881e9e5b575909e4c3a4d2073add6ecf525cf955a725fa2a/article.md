# Fastshot just got a fresh new look


We've rolled out a new design built around one goal: help you go from idea to deployed mobile app with less friction.


---


## What's new 👇


### 1️⃣ A stylish main page focused on your prompt


The homepage now puts the prompt window front and center. No clutter. No distractions. Just a clean, modern interface designed around your idea.


---


### 2️⃣ ⚡ Tesla-fast mobile app development


Fastshot is now powered by Opus 4.5 and Sonnet 4.5, delivering faster reasoning and cleaner execution.


What this means for you: your mobile app goes from prompt to ready in under 7 minutes.


---


### 3️⃣ 🚀 One-click deployment


Planning is great. Shipping is better.


With Fastshot, you can now get your mobile app in store in a single click.


---


## Why we redesigned


The old interface was built for exploration. The new one is built for **execution** .


We removed everything that didn't directly help you:


- Build faster
- Ship sooner
- Iterate cleaner


---


## What stayed the same


The core experience you love:


- Natural language app creation
- AI-generated design and logic
- Real iOS and Android apps
- Full code ownership
