# Reliance Matrix and Pasito expand partnership to bring AI-powered benefits guidance to more employers and employees

*Building on proven outcomes with Pasito’s platform, Reliance Matrix is now rolling out Pasito’s AI Benefits Assistant to its clients to support employees in making smarter benefit decisions year-round.*


‍


NEW YORK, NY — May 4, 2026 —[Pasito](https://pasito.ai/) , the AI-native workspace for employee benefits, and Reliance Matrix, a top-rated employee benefits carrier and absence management company, today announced an expansion of their strategic partnership, giving Reliance Matrix clients access to Pasito's newly launched AI Benefits Assistant.


Selecting the right benefits and navigating the benefits ecosystem year-round is increasingly difficult for employees. According to LIMRA’s Benefit Employee Attitude Tracker (BEAT) study, more than half of employees are unsure whether supplemental health benefits are even offered by their employer, and fewer than half of those who do have access say they understand them well.1


“ *Employees need more than basic information on their benefits. They’re looking for an experience tailored to their financial, physical, and mental well-being* ,” said **Stephen Ring, Vice President of Supplemental Benefits Distribution at Reliance Matrix.** “ *Through Pasito, our clients have seen that firsthand. Employees come into open enrollment better informed, and they can see exactly how their Reliance Matrix coverage works alongside their medical plan, and that clarity translates directly into stronger participation and fewer questions to HR.* ”


This expanded partnership also brings Reliance Matrix clients access to Pasito’s AI Benefits Assistant, a conversational AI agent that answers employee questions, compares plans, and delivers personalized recommendations in plain language year-round. The AI Benefits Assistant reduces administrative work for HR teams while offering always-on 24/7 support for employees. Employers can control the agent’s source documents and information, input custom instructions, and set branded tone and voice. Reliance Matrix clients also have access to Pasito’s platform, which includes automated communications, decision support, benefits guides, and microsites.


“ *Benefits should be a source of support, not stress* ,” said **Pauline Roteta, CEO and Founder of Pasito** . “ *Our partnership with Reliance Matrix reflects a shared mission: to meet employees where they are and guide them through some of their most important financial decisions, so every employee has the coverage they deserve for themselves and their families.* ”


The expanded partnership between Pasito and Reliance Matrix builds upon the organizations’ shared growth. To date, Pasito supports Reliance Matrix clients across more than 30 brokerages and 100,000 employees, bringing AI-powered benefits experiences to employees at scale.
