# Arrow Family of Companies Exceeds $1 Million in Giving and 13,700 Volunteer Hours

GLENS FALLS, N.Y. (March 18, 2026) — The Arrow Family of Companies is proud to announce its 2025 community giving totaling $1,054,048, marking the first time the organization exceeded $1 million in annual giving. This total includes $931,360 in corporate contributions to 424 organizations, as well as $122,688 in employee contributions.


**2025 Impact by the Numbers**


- $1,054,048 in total community giving — the first time Arrow has exceeded $1 million in annual donations.
- $122,688 donated by Arrow employees, a 13% increase year over year.
- 330 employee volunteers, representing 57% of the workforce.
- 13,705 volunteer hours dedicated to community organizations, up 13% from 2024.
- 694 unique community organizations supported through donations and volunteer service.
- More than $4.3 million donated in the last seven years.


“Giving back is a fundamental part of who we are as an organization,” said David S. DeMarco, President and CEO of Arrow Financial Corporation. “Surpassing $1 million in annual giving for the first time is an incredible milestone and a testament to the generosity and dedication of our employees. Whether through financial contributions or volunteer service, our team remains deeply committed to strengthening all of the communities we serve.”


Nearly half of Arrow’s giving in 2025 was Community Reinvestment Act (CRA) eligible, reinforcing the company’s focus on initiatives that support low- to moderate-income individuals and families. Funding supported programs focused on financial empowerment, housing stability, workforce and regional economic development, as well as arts and culture, healthcare, and organizations addressing basic community needs.


Several key initiatives also contributed to the company’s impact throughout the year. Through Arrow’s Thankful Campaign, $100,000 was directed to programs supporting children through after-school and mentoring initiatives. In the fourth quarter, Arrow distributed an additional $38,000 through its branch network to local food pantries to help alleviate increased demand following disruptions in SNAP benefit payments.


“Our team consistently demonstrates a deep commitment to serving their communities,” said Kate Otis, Vice President, Community Engagement Manager. “The growth in employee contributions and volunteer hours show that we put our caring into action and that making a meaningful difference is woven into our company culture.”


To learn more about Arrow’s community impact, visit[arrowfinancial.com/caring](https://www.arrowfinancial.com/caring) .
