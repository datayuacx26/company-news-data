# UniMac Introduces Game-Changing Management System


\[Ripon, Wisconsin, August 27, 2025\] UniMac, a leader in providing industrial laundry solutions for on-premises laundries, announces the launch of CORE technology, which enables laundry professionals to better manage their laundries.


CORE bridges the information gap between management’s lack of visibility to laundry room activities that impact quality, efficiency, and labor productivity.


“We could not be more excited to be launching this technology,” said Joe Hainline, Chief Technology Officer, Alliance Laundry Systems. “Hotels, long- term care, and many other on-premises laundries are laser focused on quality, hygienic results, and performance. CORE provides data to make better decisions to drive their goals, both in our dashboard and via AI powered notifications of key insights.”


UniMac CORE delivers real-time data on machine operations, key performance metrics, efficiency reports, and much more. In short, CORE gives managers the information to stay ahead on all the elements that impact finished quality and overall laundry performance – saving time and money.


The new feature, unveiled Aug. 23 at Clean Show 2025 in Orlando, is now available.
