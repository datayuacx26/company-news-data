# Huebsch Names Atlas International as the 2024 Distributor of the Year


\[Ripon, Wisconsin, August 23, 2025\] Huebsch announces Atlas International as won its 2024 Distributor of the Year. The award was presented Aug. 23 during a celebration held during the 2025 Clean Show in Orlando, Fla.


Atlas saw unprecedented growth year over year, despite an ultra-competitive Texas market. The company also added Spring and Fall shows to its calendar, which also contributed significantly to its 2024 performance.


“Atlas continues to impress through a willingness to invest in their business and stay agile in a rapidly changing competitive market,” said Craig Madson, Vice President of North America Commercial, Alliance Laundry Systems. “I’m looking forward to even more great things in the years ahead.”


Based in Dallas, Texas, and founded in 1980, Atlas provides vended and on-premises laundry solutions, service, and parts to parts of Texas, Oklahoma, and Louisiana. To learn more, visit[atlaslaundry.com](http://atlaslaundry.com/) .
