# Alliance Introduces Industry’s Largest Stack Tumbler


\[Ripon, Wisconsin, August 27, 2025\] Alliance Laundry Systems, the global leader in commercial laundry equipment, announces the introduction of its largest stack tumble dryer.


The stack 55-pound tumbler, 10 percent larger than the competition*, brings two 55-pound capacity drying pockets, eclipsing the company’s popular 45- pound stack, while occupying the same height and width as the dryer.


“This product is another example of Alliance answering calls from the market for greater drying capacity,” said Craig Madson, Vice President of North America Commercial, Alliance Laundry Systems. “In addition, the stack 55 brings laundromat owners another tool to drive greater revenue.”


The stack 55 is the perfect match for the laundromat trend of larger capacity washer-extractors. Owners increasingly are adding more 60-, 80- and even 100-pound capacity washer-extractors to meet customer demand. That comes with the need to add drying capacity, especially during peak periods.


Unveiled Aug. 23 at Clean Show 2025 in Orlando, stack 55 tumblers are available for order from Huebsch, Speed Queen and UniMac distributors.


** Based upon the average size of the largest stack dryer offerings from top competitors.*
