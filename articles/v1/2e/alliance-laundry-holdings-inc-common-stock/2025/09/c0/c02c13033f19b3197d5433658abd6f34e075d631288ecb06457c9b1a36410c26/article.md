# UniMac Names Hynes and Waller as 2024 Distributor of the Year


\[Ripon, Wisconsin, August 23, 2025\] UniMac announces Hynes and Waller has won its 2024 Distributor of the Year. The award was presented Aug. 23 during a celebration held during the 2025 Clean Show in Orlando, Fla.


Hynes and Waller had an exemplary 2024, with UniMac volume unit growth, as well as significant bottom line growth.


“Hynes and Waller has a rich history with our UniMac brand,” said Craig Madson, Vice President of North America Commercial, Alliance Laundry Systems. “So, their 2024 numbers are especially striking and worthy of this award.”


Hynes and Waller Vice President Mark Hynes also was bestowed a special honor at the event. Mark was the first annual honoree of the Bill Brooks Humanitarian Award, named in honor of longtime Alliance employee, Bill Brooks, who lived a life of service to his community and many others before his recent passing. During the presentation, it was noted that Mark embodies many of the same qualities as Brooks, including putting others first, high moral values and character, and a commitment to community service.


Based in Upper Marlboro, Maryland, Hynes and Waller has served Maryland, Washington DC, Virginia, Delaware, and West Virginia for almost 100 Years.
