# Speed Queen Announces Daniels Equipment as 2024 Distributor of the Year


\[Ripon, Wisconsin, August 23, 2025\] Speed Queen announces Daniels Equipment as the winner of its 2024 Distributor of the Year. The award was presented Aug. 23 during a celebration held during the 2025 Clean Show in Orlando, Fla.


Daniels was able to significantly grow sales year over year in a highly competitive Northeast market.


“The team at Daniels just continues to deliver exceptional results, which is a reflection of the team’s long history of excellence in customer service – before and after the sale,” said Craig Madson, Vice President of North America Commercial, Alliance Laundry Systems. “Quite simply, Daniels customers remain Daniels customers for life.”


Based in Auburn, New Hampshire, Daniels Equipment has been a leader in commercial laundry sales in the Northeast since 1973. The company has built an impressive track record of performance evidenced by numerous awards for sales and service. To learn more, visit[danielsequipment.com](http://danielsequipment.com/) .
