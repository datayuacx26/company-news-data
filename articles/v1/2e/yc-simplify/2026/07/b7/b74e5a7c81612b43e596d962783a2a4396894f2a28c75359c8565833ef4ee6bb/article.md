Corporate Risk evaluates exposures across credit, compliance, markets, financial crimes, operations, reputation, and technology. ****Best proof:**** a control test, risk assessment, forecast, reconciliation, audit finding, or dashboard that explains the method, limitation, and decision it supported.

Global Operations supports consumer and wholesale processes, fraud and claims, payments, shared services, reporting, analytics, strategy, and transformation. ****Best proof:**** a process map, automation, reporting workflow, communications deliverable, or operating change with a defined constraint and observable outcome.

## How Do I Apply for Wells Fargo Summer 2027 Internships?


1. **Separate internships from analyst programs.** Look for “Summer Internship,” a 10-week structure, and the December 2027–June 2028 graduation window. A 2027 CIB Analyst Program can be full time.
2. **Choose by work product.** Decide whether your strongest proof is credit analysis, customer research, controls, software, or process improvement.

5. **Apply and track the rolling process.** Save the requisition, submission date, location, resume version, and next action. Do not wait for the displayed end date.

## How Should I Prepare for a Wells Fargo Internship Interview?


No official source supports one universal intern interview sequence. Prepare from the selected program rather than assuming one assessment or interview format.


- **Commercial banking:** explain a credit question and your recommendation's assumptions.
- **Consumer, wealth and payments:** connect a customer need to product, data, and risk considerations.
- **Risk, audit and finance:** walk through evidence, controls, escalation, and the resulting decision.
- **Technology and data:** explain architecture, testing, security, reliability, and trade-offs.
- **Operations and corporate functions:** diagnose a process and balance speed, stakeholders, and controls.


For behavioral preparation, use concrete stories about integrity, analytical judgment, collaboration, adaptation, and responsible execution.


💡


****Represent your own experience directly.**** Current Wells Fargo requisitions prohibit unauthorized third-party recordings and require candidates to represent their own experience. Use preparation tools to organize evidence, not to substitute for your judgment during the hiring process.
