#### Software Engineering


Citadel engineers build high-performance systems, research platforms, web frameworks, and tools that move ideas into production. Strong evidence includes architecture, data structures, testing, deployment, performance constraints, and failure handling. A polished project matters most when you can explain why the system was designed that way and what changed after users or production conditions challenged it.


#### Quantitative Research Analyst


Quantitative Research Analysts turn mathematical questions into models, backtests, signals, and code. The path suits BS/MS candidates with probability, statistics, research, and programming depth. Strong evidence is an independent research question with explicit assumptions, data treatment, validation, limitations, and a conclusion that could have been disproved.


#### Trading and Investing


Trading and investing roles connect market structure, statistics, risk, and decisions under uncertainty. Strong candidates do more than claim an interest in markets: they can frame a decision, update when evidence changes, explain downside, and communicate what would invalidate the thesis. Programming or statistical software can strengthen the case when the role names it.


#### PhD Research and Research Engineering


PhD research roles apply machine learning, statistics, and domain research to complex financial data. Research engineering connects those ideas to robust automated systems. Strong evidence combines a defensible contribution with reproducible code, careful validation, and judgment about what belongs in a production research environment.


#### Operations and International Investing


Operations supports the trade lifecycle, settlements, cash management, counterparties, and process improvement. International investing roles use financial models, external data, and incomplete information to build or test a thesis. Strong evidence shows detail and ownership: identify the constraint, evidence, decision, stakeholders, and observable result.

#### What should I put on my resume for Citadel?
