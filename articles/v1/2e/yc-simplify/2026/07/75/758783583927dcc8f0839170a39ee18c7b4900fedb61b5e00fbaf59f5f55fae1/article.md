## Which Optiver Internship Should I Apply For?


Start with the work product you can defend, not the prestige of the title. Optiver's teams collaborate closely, but each internship asks for a different technical base and a different kind of proof.

#### Quantitative Trading


The Quantitative Intern path is the broadest undergraduate route for students who enjoy probability, statistics, programming, and decisions under pressure. Optiver may assess a candidate for either quantitative trading or quantitative research fit. Strong evidence is a model, simulation, or data investigation where you explain the assumptions, update the decision when evidence changes, and communicate the result clearly.


#### Institutional Trading


Institutional Trading combines quantitative judgment with external relationships. Interns learn market mechanics, represent markets, execute trades with counterparties, and understand how trading relationships develop. Strong evidence connects numbers to people: a market, pricing, sales, or negotiation decision where you explained uncertainty, earned stakeholder trust, and chose an action rather than merely presenting analysis.


#### Software Engineering


Software interns build applications that support a global trading firm and can see code reach production. The role values clean architecture, performance, reliability, computer science fundamentals, and collaboration with traders and engineers. Strong evidence is a shipped system whose design, tests, failure modes, performance constraints, and measurable effect you can explain without hiding behind a framework name.


#### FPGA Engineering


FPGA interns work on nanosecond response times, parallel algorithms, networking, and hardware that interfaces with trading systems. Candidates need hands-on digital design experience and languages such as VHDL, Verilog, or SystemVerilog. Strong evidence is a design you implemented and verified, including timing constraints, test benches, resource or latency trade-offs, and what failed before it worked.


#### PhD Quantitative Research


The selected Quantitative Research internship targets PhD candidates who can build predictive models, analyze market and order-flow data, and apply statistics or machine learning to live research questions. Strong evidence is a reproducible research contribution: state the hypothesis, baseline, data treatment, validation, uncertainty, limitation, code contribution, and what result would have changed your conclusion.

#### When do Optiver internship applications open?

#### What is the easiest way to get an Optiver internship?
