#### Journalism, Editorial, and Newsgathering


These teams find, verify, shape, and publish stories across CNN programs and digital surfaces. Work can include research, sourcing, writing, fact-checking, field support, and assembling material for producers or editors. The function sits closest to the daily news judgment of the newsroom.


****Best proof:**** two or three clips that show original reporting, reliable sourcing, clean structure, and a correction or editorial decision you can explain.


#### Production, Video, and Audio


Production interns help turn reporting and ideas into material an audience can watch or hear. Depending on the team, that can mean field logistics, footage management, editing, sound, rundowns, documentary research, or production coordination. The work connects editorial judgment to deadlines and technical workflows.


****Best proof:**** a short reel or finished segment that labels your contribution, tools, constraints, and the edit or production choice that improved the result.


#### Sports, Social, and Audience Growth


These teams package stories for specific platforms and learn how audiences discover, watch, and share them. Sports work can mix content planning, social publishing, promotion, audience development, and performance analysis around live events. The function sits between editorial, marketing, and distribution.


****Best proof:**** a campaign, account, or content experiment with a defined audience, platform decision, metric, and lesson rather than a folder of posts without context.


#### Research, Analytics, and Content Strategy


Research and strategy teams convert audience, content, product, or market data into a decision. Interns may clean information, build recurring reporting, investigate performance, compare alternatives, and explain findings to partners who do not work in data. The function supports programming and business choices across media teams.


****Best proof:**** an analysis that starts with a decision, documents the method, handles an imperfect data limitation, and ends with a recommendation someone could act on.


#### Communications, Publicity, and Business Operations


These roles help a brand communicate launches, talent, programming, and company priorities while keeping cross-functional work moving. Projects may include media research, briefing materials, press lists, presentations, event support, scheduling, or operating reports. The function rewards accuracy and audience awareness as much as enthusiasm for entertainment.


****Best proof:**** a brief, pitch, campaign, or process improvement with a clear audience, stakeholder constraint, and measurable or observable result.
