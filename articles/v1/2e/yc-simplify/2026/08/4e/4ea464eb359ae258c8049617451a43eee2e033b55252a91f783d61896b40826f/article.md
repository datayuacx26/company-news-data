Power internships can touch gas, hydro, steam, or nuclear technology. Nuclear assignments are especially systems-heavy: interns may analyze, design, model, test, or integrate equipment and software under safety and export-control constraints.


****Best proof:**** a calculation, simulation, design review, or validation result where you checked assumptions and explained a technical risk.


#### Wind Engineering


Wind teams work across product design, controls, aerodynamics, components, testing, manufacturing, quality, services, and cost reduction. The work connects analysis with machines that must perform safely at scale.


****Best proof:**** an engineering project where you balanced performance, reliability, manufacturability, safety, or cost and validated the result.


#### Electrification, Grid and Digital


Electrification covers grid solutions, automation software, power conversion, storage, and integration. Intern work can involve control systems, electrical studies, digital tools, data, simulation, or technical documentation.


****Best proof:**** a system model, control, software tool, or hardware test tied to a clear reliability or operating requirement.


#### Manufacturing, Supply Chain and Lean


These teams improve how equipment is built, sourced, inspected, and delivered. Assignments can include process improvement, quality, sourcing, cost analysis, supplier work, production equipment, or Lean methods.


****Best proof:**** a root-cause investigation or operational decision with a baseline, constraint, action, and measured result.


#### Finance and Business


Finance internships support analysis, accounting, audit, projects, and manufacturing decisions. Business assignments can also sit close to commercial operations or customers.


****Best proof:**** a financial or market model where you checked the inputs, compared alternatives, explained the recommendation, and showed how the decision affected operations.

## How Should I Prepare for a GE Vernova Internship Interview?


GE Vernova describes a staged process: apply, talk with a recruiter, talk with a hiring manager, then interview with key stakeholders. Intern hiring can be shorter. The recruiter checks basic fit, the manager tests your evidence against the assignment, and other stakeholders examine collaboration and judgment.


- **For nuclear and power roles,** review core engineering fundamentals, safety boundaries, validation, and a time you caught a bad assumption before it reached the result.
- **For wind or electrification,** prepare a design, control, software, or test example that connects technical choices to reliability and physical performance.
- **For manufacturing and supply chain,** walk through a defect, delay, cost, supplier, or process problem using the baseline, root cause, action, and sustained result.
- **For finance,** explain a model, reconciliation, audit, or recommendation and show how you checked the inputs before presenting it.


GE Vernova's stated working principles are innovation, customer service, Lean, One Team, and accountability. Prepare separate examples for improving a method, serving a user or customer, simplifying work, resolving disagreement, and owning a mistake.
