## What Internships Does SpaceX Offer?


The four pathways divide candidates by the kind of evidence they can defend. Pick the application that matches both your enrollment and the work you have already completed.


#### Engineering


Engineering interns can support Dragon, Falcon, Starlink, Starshield, or Starship through analysis, avionics, manufacturing, materials, propulsion, structures, integration, test, supply chain engineering, and launch operations. These roles sit inside vehicle and production teams rather than one centralized intern group. The route joins design with hardware execution, so candidates should show calculations, CAD, fabrication, testing, or operating results.


****Best proof:**** one system you designed or improved, then tested against a real constraint.


#### Software Engineering


Software interns can work on application software, flight software, guidance and control, product security, silicon engineering, and the systems behind Starlink, Starshield, and Starship. Teams span embedded code, networks, simulations, infrastructure, firmware, and customer-facing applications. The assignment determines whether the work is vehicle-side, factory-side, network-side, or customer-facing. Team fit shapes the preparation.


****Best proof:**** deployed software with clear architecture, debugging evidence, tests, performance results, and an explanation of the candidate's own contribution.


#### Graduate Engineer


Graduate Engineers work across SpaceX's major hardware programs after completing a relevant bachelor's degree and entering graduate school. The work can cover design, manufacturing, operations, avionics, propulsion, materials, structures, integration, and test, with responsibility comparable to the engineering route. This is a term-based student program, not the full-time new-grad route. Program fit still controls the assignment.


****Best proof:**** a technically deep research, laboratory, design-team, or prior-internship project where the applicant owned decisions and verification.


#### Business Operations


Business Operations interns support finance, food services, human resources, legal, purchasing, Starlink business operations, and supply chain operations. These teams connect company-wide functions with manufacturing, suppliers, employees, and Starlink customers. The work can include process automation, spend analysis, customer operations, legal research, employee systems, and operating projects. Function fit shapes the application.


****Best proof:**** an analysis or process change that improved cost, speed, accuracy, compliance, service, or another measured business result.

## How Should I Prepare for a SpaceX Internship Interview?


SpaceX says selected candidates complete technical phone screens. A team may also use a quiz, project, or programming test, but there is no promised universal sequence. Prepare for the selected group and confirm the format with the recruiter.


- **Know two projects deeply.** Explain the problem, your contribution, a hard decision, a failure or constraint, the test, and the result.
- **Connect skills to the program.** A Starlink software answer should sound different from a Starship manufacturing answer or a supply chain analysis.
- **Review the fundamentals your evidence invokes.** If the resume names a language, engineering principle, or business method, expect follow-up on how you used it.
- **Make mission fit specific.** Explain why the selected team needs your skill and what work you want to own. General enthusiasm for space is not enough.

#### Do SpaceX interns get paid?


Yes. Current postings list $21-$26 per hour for Business Operations, $28-$30 for Engineering, and $30-$40 for Software. Graduate Engineer lists $83,200-$100,100 annualized.


#### When do SpaceX internship applications open?


SpaceX posts Spring, Summer, and Fall opportunities separately. Fall 2026 applications are open. Summer 2027 openings are not posted yet, so monitor the live careers page.


#### What is the easiest way to get a SpaceX internship?


Pick a pathway whose enrollment and experience rules you meet, then show one or two projects with clear ownership, technical decisions, testing, and results. Apply across suitable terms or locations.


#### Can international or F-1 students intern at SpaceX?


Current postings require U.S. Person eligibility and name citizens or nationals, lawful permanent residents, refugees, and asylees. SpaceX says it cannot sponsor F-1 students for these opportunities.


#### Does SpaceX have a minimum internship GPA?


SpaceX publishes no minimum GPA for the Intern Program, but says a cumulative 3.5 GPA is preferred. Current applications still ask candidates to report GPA.
