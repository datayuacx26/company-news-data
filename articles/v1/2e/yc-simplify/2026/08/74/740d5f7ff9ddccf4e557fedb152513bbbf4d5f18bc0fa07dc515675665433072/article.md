## What Internships Does Airbus Offer?


Airbus recruits across commercial aircraft, helicopters, defence, space, digital systems, manufacturing, supply chain, and corporate teams. The four families below keep the role map broad while the current evidence remains tied to exact postings.


#### Aircraft and Helicopter Engineering


Aircraft and helicopter teams work across aerodynamics, structures, propulsion integration, flight physics, systems, testing, certification, maintenance, and product support. The exact work depends on the Airbus division and site; Filton, Broughton, Toulouse, Hamburg, and Grand Prairie do different things.


****Best proof:**** an engineering build or analysis with requirements, calculations, test evidence, a failure or design change, and a clear account of your own contribution.


#### Space, Defence and Digital Systems


Airbus Defence and Space teams work on satellites, secure communications, mission systems, cybersecurity, software, data, AI, and systems integration. Some positions require local language, security, or citizenship conditions that do not apply companywide. Software applicants still need system depth, not aerospace enthusiasm alone.


****Best proof:**** a tested software, embedded, cyber, data, or systems project with interfaces, reliability evidence, and one technical tradeoff you can defend.


#### Manufacturing, Quality and Supply Chain


Manufacturing and supply-chain teams turn designs into aircraft, helicopters, and space hardware. Intern work can involve production engineering, composites, industrial performance, supplier quality, procurement, logistics, lean work, or data analysis. Much of it sits onsite near parts and production teams.


****Best proof:**** a process, sourcing, quality, or operations project that identifies the constraint, validates the data, changes the workflow, and measures the result.


#### Business Operations and Corporate Functions


Airbus also hires students in finance, HR, communications, marketing, safety, program management, legal, and other corporate functions. The work supports regulated products and large international programs, so documentation and stakeholder judgment matter. These teams connect company operations to technical delivery.


****Best proof:**** a policy, dashboard, campaign, audit, training, or operating improvement with a defined audience, controlled execution, and a measurable outcome.

## How Should I Prepare for an Airbus Internship Interview?


Airbus says selected candidates may complete an interview or assessment centre. It does not publish one universal internship sequence. Prepare from the role, product, site, and evidence you submitted. A manufacturing interview and a communications interview should not sound alike.


- **For engineering and digital roles, defend the project.** Explain requirements, interfaces, calculations or code, testing, the failure you found, and why your fix worked.
- **For manufacturing and supply chain, follow the process.** Discuss data quality, root cause, supplier or shop-floor constraints, controls, and the metric you improved.
- **For corporate functions, name the stakeholder.** Show how an analysis, policy, training, campaign, or report changed a decision or operating result.
- **For every route, know the site.** Connect your evidence to the product and work done at that location without claiming knowledge the official sources do not provide.

## Airbus Internship FAQ


#### Is it hard to get an Airbus internship?


Airbus publishes no verified acceptance rate. Applicants compete on country eligibility, student status, field fit, timing, and evidence matched to the exact site and role.


#### Do Airbus interns get paid?


Airbus says all US internships are paid, but the two current US postings do not list an amount. Pay and benefits elsewhere depend on the country, entity, route, and requisition.


#### When do Airbus internship applications open?


Timing varies by country. UK 2027-28 industry placements open in early September 2026. The current US long-term roles appeared in late July 2026, but they do not establish a companywide Summer 2027 date.


#### What is the easiest way to get an Airbus internship?


There is no verified easy track. Start with a country and pathway whose rules you meet, then choose the role where your strongest project matches the actual product or process.


#### Can I apply for more than one Airbus internship?


Airbus allows parallel applications but says applying to more roles does not necessarily improve your chances. It advises choosing positions that genuinely fit your skills and experience.


#### What is the difference between an Airbus internship and apprenticeship?


An internship is a student work placement tied to university enrollment. An apprenticeship or dual-study route combines employment with vocational or degree education and uses different eligibility and duration rules.
