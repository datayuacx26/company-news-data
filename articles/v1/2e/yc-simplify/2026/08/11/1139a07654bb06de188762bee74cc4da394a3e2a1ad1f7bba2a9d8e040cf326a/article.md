## What Internships Does Deutsche Bank Offer?


Deutsche Bank's official programme spans business divisions and infrastructure areas. Current US inventory is concentrated in one family, but the evergreen map is wider. Pick the division before preparing technical material or networking questions.


#### Investment Banking and Capital Markets


Investment Banking and Capital Markets teams advise companies and institutions on financing, mergers and acquisitions, restructuring, and capital raising. Coverage groups build sector knowledge; product groups specialize in tools such as debt or equity capital markets. The work joins accounting, research, modeling, and client materials under tight deadlines.


****Best proof:**** a model or company analysis with sourced assumptions, checks, a clear recommendation, and an explanation of what changed after review.


#### Corporate Bank and Transaction Banking


Corporate Bank teams support cash management, trade finance, lending, securities services, and the operating flows behind institutional clients. Student pathways can include internships and apprenticeships, which use separate eligibility and program rules. Candidates need process judgment as well as financial interest.


****Best proof:**** an analysis or workflow improvement that reduced an error, clarified a control, reconciled data, or helped a stakeholder act on time.


#### Technology, Data and Innovation


Technology, Data and Innovation supports trading, risk, client platforms, payments, security, data, and the systems used across Deutsche Bank. Roles differ between software delivery, analytics, infrastructure, cyber, and technology process work. A banking interest does not replace technical depth.


****Best proof:**** a working system or analysis with architecture, tests, failure handling, performance evidence, and a precise account of your contribution.


#### Private Bank and Infrastructure Functions


Private Bank serves individual and wealth clients, while infrastructure functions include risk, finance, human resources, communications, compliance, operations, and other shared teams. These roles use different academic signals, but all require clear judgment and controlled execution.


****Best proof:**** work tied to the selected function, such as a risk review, client analysis, communication plan, control test, or operating improvement with a measured result.


### Investment Banking and Capital Markets
