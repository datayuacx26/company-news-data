# How to Get an Internship at Autodesk 2026-2027

To get an Autodesk internship, clear the country and enrollment rules first, then show how your work improves a real design, engineering, construction, manufacturing, media, or content workflow. Autodesk recruits on regional schedules, so a role in Canada does not establish eligibility or timing for the United States.


#### What You Should Know About Autodesk Internships


- ****Eligibility comes before portfolio polish.**** Autodesk requires interns to be at least 18, actively enrolled, and living and studying in the country where the internship is offered.


- ****Recruiting happens in two broad waves.**** Autodesk says most internship hiring runs from September through November and February through April, although each region and requisition can differ.


- ****Product context makes evidence stronger.**** A good project uses the right tool and explains the user, workflow, constraint, decision, test, and measurable result.

## When Do Autodesk Internship Applications Open?


Autodesk says it typically recruits interns from September through November and again from February through April. Treat those as search windows, not guaranteed opening or closing dates. A team can post earlier, close after enough applications arrive, or follow a university calendar that differs from another country.


Program length also changes by region. The[official Autodesk student page](https://www.autodesk.com/careers/students?ref=blog.simplify.jobs) says internships last at least 12 weeks. US internships generally run for three months in summer. Canadian programs commonly run 16 weeks in summer, fall, or winter. European internships often last three to six months in summer, while Asia-Pacific opportunities can run three to six months across summer, fall, or winter.

## What Internships Does Autodesk Offer?


Autodesk builds software used across architecture, engineering, construction, manufacturing, product design, and media. Internships therefore extend beyond conventional software engineering. The most useful role map connects a team's technical or business function to the customer workflow it supports.


#### Software, Cloud and Computer Graphics


These teams build desktop, web, and cloud products, graphics systems, platform services, developer tools, APIs, and the infrastructure behind Autodesk workflows. The job may emphasize C++, JavaScript, Python, distributed systems, rendering, geometry, testing, or reliability.


****Best proof:**** a working system with clear architecture, tests, failure handling, performance evidence, and an explanation of the tradeoff you owned.


#### AI, ML, Design and Manufacturing Research


Research teams explore AI, machine learning, computer vision, generative methods, geometry, simulation, and automation for design and manufacturing. Strong candidates connect model quality to a useful workflow instead of presenting a notebook in isolation.


****Best proof:**** an experiment with a baseline, data choice, evaluation metric, limitation, reproducible result, and explanation of how a designer or engineer would use it.


#### Product, UX and Industry Workflows


Product, user experience, and industry teams study how architects, engineers, builders, manufacturers, and creators complete complex work. Roles can involve product management, UX research, interaction design, analytics, customer discovery, or industry strategy.


****Best proof:**** a project that identifies the user and constraint, compares options, tests the decision, and shows how evidence changed the final product or workflow.


#### Content, Marketing and Business Operations


Autodesk also hires students for content, communications, marketing, finance, people, sales operations, customer success, and program work. These teams translate complex products and processes for specific audiences.


****Best proof:**** a campaign, content system, dashboard, analysis, or operating improvement with a defined audience, clean execution, stakeholder feedback, and a measurable result.


### Software, Cloud and Computer Graphics


**What the work can involve.** Software interns may build product features, services, developer tools, graphics capabilities, automation, or tests. The Quebec City Software Developer opening points toward C++ and computer graphics, while the Cloud Development role emphasizes product engineering in a cloud environment. The exact stack remains team specific, and both require evidence beyond a language list.


**What to show.** Use one project you can trace from requirement to release. Explain the architecture, your contribution, how you tested it, what failed, and how you measured improvement. For graphics work, prepare geometry, rendering, performance, or debugging detail. For cloud work, prepare interfaces, observability, reliability, data flow, and a practical tradeoff. A small, finished system is usually easier to defend than a sprawling unfinished one.


### AI, ML, Design and Manufacturing Research


**What the work can involve.** Autodesk research sits close to the workflows used to design and make things. The current Toronto AI opening targets MS or PhD students and joins Python, CAD or CAM context, prototyping, and research communication. The important signal is not simply that a candidate trained a model. It is whether the experiment answers a useful question and holds up under inspection.


**What to show.** Prepare a research project with a defensible baseline, data source, evaluation method, and limitation. Explain why the metric matches the task and where the approach breaks. If the work touches CAD, CAM, geometry, simulation, or manufacturing, describe the underlying user step instead of relying on tool names. Be ready to discuss a negative result and the next experiment it suggested.


### Product, UX and Industry Workflows


**What the work can involve.** A product or UX intern may study a complicated professional workflow, identify friction, define requirements, prototype an interaction, analyze behavior, or help a team decide what to build. Autodesk users often work across long projects, regulated processes, large files, and multiple collaborators. That makes workflow accuracy more persuasive than a polished screen without research.


**What to show.** Build the case around the user. Name who they were, what they were trying to accomplish, where the process failed, and what evidence changed your decision. Include research notes, alternatives, usability findings, constraints, and the effect of the final change. Simplify's[UX internship guide](https://simplify.jobs/blog/uiux-design-internship-guide/?ref=simplify.jobs) can help turn a portfolio walkthrough into interview evidence.


### Content, Marketing and Business Operations


**What the work can involve.** The Toronto Content Management internship supports content operations, localization, measurement, and coordination. Other business teams may work across communications, finance, people programs, sales operations, or customer programs. The shared skill is turning messy inputs into something a specific audience or stakeholder can use.


**What to show.** Use a piece of work with a clear audience, objective, process, and result. A content applicant can show taxonomy, localization, editorial operations, or performance analysis. An operations applicant can show a dashboard, reconciliation, workflow change, or project plan. Explain how you handled review, conflicting feedback, and quality checks. Remove confidential data and keep the method concrete.


## What Is the Autodesk Internship Experience Like?


Autodesk describes paid, project-based internships with an intern buddy, coaching and mentoring, technical talks, and project showcases. Those are companywide program signals, not a promise that every team follows the same daily schedule. Location, remote flexibility, hours, and events depend on the country and requisition.


The four current Canadian postings describe a 16-week fall term from August 31 through December 18, 2026 and a flexible-location model. Before accepting, confirm the work site, weekly expectations, equipment, pay, immigration terms, and whether school enrollment must continue through the placement. ## How Should I Prepare for an Autodesk Internship Interview?


Autodesk says candidates usually complete two to three virtual interviews. The sequence can include a recruiter, hiring manager, and team members. A technical role may begin with a coding question or add a 30 to 60-minute coding exercise. Autodesk says candidates generally receive an outcome within two weeks after the interview process ends.


- **Know the product context.** Explain which Autodesk users or workflows interest you and connect that interest to the team's actual problem.
- **Defend one project deeply.** Cover the requirement, your contribution, alternatives, test, failure, revision, and measurable result without inflating ownership.
- **Prepare for the functional assessment.** Practice code only for a coding role; use a portfolio, case, research discussion, or work sample when the job points elsewhere.
- **Show how you learn with others.** Autodesk hiring advice emphasizes learning, communication, fundamentals, and a resume tailored to the position.


⚠️


****Do not use live AI assistance in an Autodesk interview.**** Autodesk's candidate FAQ says AI interview assistance is prohibited unless it is part of an approved accommodation or a relevant assessment. Prepare with tools beforehand, then do the interview yourself.


## Autodesk Internship FAQ


#### Who is eligible for an Autodesk internship?


Autodesk says interns must be at least 18, actively enrolled in a degree-granting institution or accredited boot camp, and living and studying in the country where the internship is offered. Individual roles can add degree, field, location, or work-authorization requirements.


#### When do Autodesk internship applications open?


Most recruiting happens September through November and February through April. A country, university term, or team can use a different calendar, so the exact requisition controls the deadline.


#### How long is an Autodesk internship?


Autodesk says internships last at least 12 weeks. US summer programs are commonly three months, Canadian programs are commonly 16 weeks, and European or Asia-Pacific programs often run three to six months.


#### Do Autodesk interns get paid?


Autodesk describes its internships as paid, but it does not publish one global rate. Pay, currency, and benefits depend on the role, location, and program. The verified Canadian postings do not state a numeric amount.


#### What is the Autodesk internship interview process?


Candidates usually complete two to three virtual interviews. Technical applicants may receive a coding question or a 30 to 60-minute coding exercise. The exact sequence depends on the role.


#### Can I apply if Autodesk does not recruit at my school?


Yes. Autodesk says students can apply even when it does not recruit at their school, provided they meet the internship's eligibility and location rules.
