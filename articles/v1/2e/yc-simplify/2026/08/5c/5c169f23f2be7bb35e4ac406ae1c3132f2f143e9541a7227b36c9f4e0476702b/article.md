## What Internships Does Anduril Offer?


Anduril's student work spans software-defined systems and the physical products they control. Pick the discipline where you can show a complete technical decision, not merely relevant coursework.


#### Software Engineering


Software interns can support deployed systems, build product functionality, create monitoring metrics, debug failures, and coordinate fixes across teams. Useful preparation includes algorithms, data structures, cloud or storage systems, front-end frameworks, and languages such as C++, Go, Rust, Java, or Python. The strongest evidence is code you shipped, measured, and maintained.


#### Electrical and Embedded Systems


Electrical work can move from component selection and PCB design through embedded firmware, system integration, and lab debugging. Useful evidence includes oscilloscopes or logic analyzers, hardware interfaces, embedded Linux, C/C++, test automation, and a board or subsystem you brought from schematic to verified behavior.


#### Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering


Mechanical interns design and build prototypes, present reviews, analyze failures, execute hardware tests, and transition successful work toward production. Strong evidence combines first-principles calculations, CAD drawings, fabrication, FEA or CFD, test results, and a clear explanation of what changed after a failure.


#### Manufacturing Engineering


Manufacturing interns can support composite aerospace structures through trade studies, solution design, procurement, implementation, test, and verification. A strong project shows how you balanced producibility, quality, safety, cost, and schedule, then proved the improvement with a built or tested result.


#### Naval Architecture


Naval architecture work can include AUV hull resistance, internal arrangement, structures, hydrostatics, stability, propulsion, launch and recovery, and producibility. Strong evidence is a self-contained engineering artifact: a trade space, weight and buoyancy budget, structural drawing, stability result, or tested design supported by analysis.

## How Should I Prepare for an Anduril Internship Interview?


Anduril's official process includes a recruiter call, technical and behavioral interviews, and role-specific assessments that may involve live coding, presentations, or deep technical discussions. Treat those as possible formats, not a guaranteed sequence.


- **Software:** practice coding and debugging while explaining data structures, system boundaries, tests, monitoring, and trade-offs.
- **Electrical:** review circuits, embedded interfaces, firmware, lab tools, and a failure you isolated from measurements.
- **Mechanical and manufacturing:** prepare design reviews, first-principles reasoning, fabrication or process choices, tests, and a corrected failure.
- **Naval:** be ready to defend hydrostatics, stability, resistance, structures, assumptions, margins, and the engineering artifact you produced.
- **Behavioral and mission fit:** explain why this work matters to you, how you take ownership under uncertainty, and how you handle safety, disagreement, urgency, and incomplete information without turning the answer into a slogan.


## Anduril Internship FAQ


#### Is it hard to get an Anduril internship?


Anduril does not publish a verified acceptance rate. The process is selective because applicants must clear degree, student-status, export-control, location, and role-specific technical requirements. A complete project you can defend is stronger than a generic list of technologies.


#### Do Anduril interns get paid?


Yes. The five selected opportunities verified July 31, 2026 list $30–$55 per hour. The exact band depends on the role: Software reaches $55, while current hardware and manufacturing ranges start at $30.


#### When do Anduril internship applications open?


The selected Summer 2027 applications opened in June 2026 and say reviews begin in August 2026. Applying now enters the matching pipeline; it does not guarantee that an exact team seat is already open.


#### What is the easiest way to get an Anduril internship?


There is no shortcut. Choose the discipline whose eligibility rules you meet and show one finished technical artifact that resembles its work. Make your decisions, tests, failures, and individual contribution easy to verify.


#### Does Anduril require a minimum GPA for internships?


Anduril's official FAQ says there is no hard minimum GPA, although it commonly sees strong candidates with a cumulative 3.0 or higher. The role's other eligibility and technical requirements still apply.


#### Can international or F-1 students apply for Anduril internships?


Current selected roles require U.S. Person status for export-controlled work, and Anduril's official FAQ says it currently cannot sponsor F-1 students. U.S. Person eligibility includes more than citizenship alone, so use the official categories and answer the application accurately.
