# How to Get an Internship at American Express 2026-2027

To get an American Express internship, choose the student program that matches your class year, then choose one career area and build the application around evidence for that team. The Undergraduate, Sophomore Finance, and MBA routes use different eligibility rules and recruiting windows.


#### What You Should Know About American Express Internships


- ****Class year controls the route.**** The general Undergraduate Internship targets juniors entering their final year, Sophomore Finance targets sophomores, and the MBA program targets first-year MBA or advanced-degree students.


- ****The application calendar is split.**** American Express lists August through September for Undergraduate and Sophomore Finance applications, while MBA recruiting runs September through November.


- ****Technology adds another screen.**** US candidates complete behavioral screening and interviews, while technology applicants also complete a technical assessment.

## What Internships Does American Express Offer?


American Express groups student hiring around business teams rather than one interchangeable internship pool. The four families below cover the recurring US career areas without pretending that every family is open in every cycle.


#### Technology, Engineering and Data


Technology teams build customer applications, payment and servicing systems, data platforms, security controls, and the infrastructure behind American Express products. Student opportunities can sit in software engineering, data, analytics, information security, or industrial engineering. Team placement changes the technical work and the interview preparation.


****Best proof:**** a working system with clear architecture, tests, debugging evidence, performance results, and an explanation of what you personally built.


#### Finance, Risk and Analytics


Finance and risk teams connect cardmember behavior, product economics, controls, planning, fraud, and regulatory obligations to operating decisions. The Sophomore Finance Internship is a distinct student route, while analytics and risk work also appears across other programs. Applicants should show more than classroom finance vocabulary.


****Best proof:**** a model or analysis that documents the question, method, checks, recommendation, and measurable effect on a decision.


#### Product, Marketing and Customer Strategy


Product and marketing teams work on card products, acquisition, member engagement, merchant relationships, digital journeys, travel services, and customer communications. The work blends customer evidence with economics, operations, and delivery. A campaign plan without a result is weaker than a tested decision.


****Best proof:**** research or an experiment that changed a feature, audience, message, journey, or product decision and shows what happened afterward.


#### Operations and Corporate Functions


Corporate-function internships can cover colleague experience, internal audit, legal, communications, service operations, travel and lifestyle, and control work. These teams keep products and customer promises operating within policy and service standards. The strongest preparation depends on the function rather than the American Express name.


****Best proof:**** a process, control, policy, service, or communication improvement with a clear stakeholder, constraint, action, and result.

### Product, Marketing and Customer Strategy


**The work and candidate fit.** American Express lists product, marketing, digital product, communications, and travel and lifestyle among its US student areas. Yes. American Express says its internships are paid. It does not publish one universal US rate, so use the compensation listed on the exact requisition or offer.


#### When do American Express internship applications open?


The US student page lists August through September for Undergraduate and Sophomore Finance applications and September through November for MBA applications. Exact dates and availability vary by role.


#### What is the easiest way to get an American Express internship?

#### Does American Express offer internships for sophomores?
