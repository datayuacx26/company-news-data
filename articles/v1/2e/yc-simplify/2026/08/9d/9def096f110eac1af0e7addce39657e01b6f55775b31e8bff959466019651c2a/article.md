#### Civil, Water and Environmental Engineering


These teams work on site design, remediation, water systems, drainage, geotechnical questions, environmental studies, and construction documents. Interns may calculate, draft, collect field data, review submittals, or prepare reports.


****Best proof:**** a design or field project that states the governing requirement, method, quality check, and result. Explain where professional review changed it.


#### Transportation, Rail and Structural Engineering


Transportation teams cover roads, bridges, transit, rail systems, inspection, and structural analysis. Work moves between office calculations and field conditions, so safety and documentation matter alongside accuracy.


****Best proof:**** a load, circuit, alignment, or inspection project with assumptions, standards, calculations, and a correction you made after checking the result.


#### Buildings, Architecture and Digital Construction


These teams connect architecture, engineering, construction management, BIM, VDC, reality capture, and model coordination. Interns may find clashes, document progress, compare design intent with field conditions, or create stakeholder visuals.


****Best proof:**** a coordinated model or construction workflow that identifies the source files, review method, issue, resolution, and effect on delivery.


#### Energy, Electrical and Asset Systems


Energy and asset teams work on electrical networks, traction power, renewables, equipment condition, reliability, and lifecycle decisions. Assignments can include calculations, schematics, inspections, commissioning, or asset analysis.


****Best proof:**** a power, controls, reliability, or maintenance project with operating constraints, a safety check, and a recommendation tied to performance.


#### Planning, Sustainability and Project Management


These roles frame infrastructure choices before and during delivery. Work may include transport planning, economics, sustainability analysis, cost, scheduling, stakeholder coordination, or project controls.


****Best proof:**** an option analysis or delivery decision that names the objective, evidence, affected groups, constraint, recommendation, and feasibility check.

## How Should I Prepare for an AECOM Internship Interview?


AECOM says candidates can expect an in-person or video interview with technical hiring teams. A civil interviewer may test design assumptions and documentation. Rail or power teams may ask about systems and safety. A digital-construction interviewer may examine BIM coordination, file control, or field verification.


- **Bring one technical walkthrough.** Explain the requirement, your individual work, the check you performed, what failed, and the revision you made.
- **Know the local project context.** Read the business line, office, clients, software, site expectations, and deliverables named in the posting.
- **Prepare evidence about judgment.** AECOM's Code of Conduct emphasizes ethics, teamwork, respect, open communication, client needs, and compliance. Use a real example involving safety, disagreement, feedback, or a technical risk.
- **Ask precise questions.** Ask what the intern owns, who reviews it, how office and field time split, and what makes an intern useful by the midpoint.


Use the[behavioral interview preparation guide](https://simplify.jobs/blog/prepare-behavioral-interview-without-sounding-rehearsed/?ref=simplify.jobs) to organize examples without memorizing a script. Keep technical and behavioral evidence connected: the best story explains both the engineering decision and how you communicated it.

****Show the artifact and the outcome.**** A drawing, model view, calculation excerpt, field note, or redacted decision table makes your contribution easier to inspect. Remove confidential material and explain every line you show.


## Frequently Asked Questions


#### Is it hard to get an AECOM internship?


AECOM does not publish an internship acceptance rate. Difficulty depends on the exact discipline, office, school-year gate, technical requirements, and work-authorization terms. A strong application makes one relevant project easy for the local technical team to assess.


#### Do AECOM interns get paid?


Yes, the four verified US requisitions in this guide post hourly pay. Current selected ranges run from $17.92-$31.34/hr, depending on the role. Use the exact posting and written offer for location-specific pay and benefits.


#### When do AECOM internship applications open?


AECOM says it looks for interns throughout the year and that many Americas roles open in the fall. Offices can post on different schedules, while Australia and New Zealand use a November-through-February summer cycle. Follow the active requisition rather than waiting for one global date.


#### What's the easiest way to get an AECOM internship?


There is no verified easy route. The clearest path is to choose a role whose discipline, location, and study requirements already fit, then lead with a calculation, drawing, model, field project, or analysis that resembles the team's work.


#### Does AECOM hire civil engineering interns?


Yes. Civil engineering is one of AECOM's largest internship families. Current work includes site-civil design, remediation, water, transportation, structures, inspection, and project documentation, with requirements set by each office and requisition.


#### Does AECOM offer internships outside the United States?


Yes. On August 3, 2026, most active AECOM internships in the Simplify index were outside the United States, especially in Australia. Regional programs use different academic calendars, work-rights rules, currencies, and application materials.
