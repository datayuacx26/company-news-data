# How to Get an Internship at PwC 2026-2027

To get a PwC internship, choose the student path, season, service line, and office before you apply. Start and Advance serve different college stages, Winter requires full-time busy-season availability, and the interview format changes between Audit, Tax, Digital Assurance and Transparency, and Advisory.


#### What You Should Know About PwC Internships


- ****Start and Advance are not interchangeable.**** Start serves earlier college students and can lead to an Advance offer; Advance typically takes place about one year before graduation.


- ****The office can close before the broader program.**** PwC recruits for many roles on a rolling basis by location, so a listed office may disappear as offers are accepted.


- ****The interview depends on the service line.**** Audit, Tax, and DAT use two behavioral interviews, while most Advisory roles require a case interview.

## What Internships Does PwC Offer?


PwC separates student work by service line. Choose the client problem and evidence type before choosing a familiar office or title. The four families below cover the main US internship paths without treating every global PwC member firm as one recruiting system.


#### Audit and Digital Assurance


Audit interns support financial statement work, testing, documentation, and evidence review. Digital Assurance and Transparency, often shortened to DAT, examines controls, transactions, business processes, and technology risks across cloud, AI, and other systems. Both paths require accuracy and professional skepticism, but DAT adds more technology-control depth.


****Best proof:**** an audit, reconciliation, control test, or data review where you found an issue and supported the conclusion.


#### Tax


Tax internships can involve compliance, research, planning, transfer pricing, deals, data, and process automation. Some teams expect a CPA-oriented accounting path; others combine tax knowledge with analytics or software. Read the education language carefully instead of assuming every Tax role has the same degree requirement.


****Best proof:**** research or analysis that connects a rule, source, calculation, review step, and defensible recommendation.


#### Advisory


Advisory teams work on strategy, transactions, operations, risk, cybersecurity, technology transformation, and other client problems. The work and recruiting process differ by practice, which is why PwC does not publish one universal Advisory interview. Most Advisory positions include a case interview.


****Best proof:**** a structured recommendation that defines the client problem, tests assumptions, weighs tradeoffs, and shows how the analysis changed an outcome.


#### Technology and Engineering


Technology work appears inside DAT, Tax technology, Advisory, cloud and data teams, internal products, and engineering programs. Candidates may build automation, analyze systems, test controls, work with data, or support technical transformation. The business setting matters as much as the tool list.


****Best proof:**** a deployed system or automation with architecture, testing, reliability, user impact, and a clear account of your own contribution.

## How Should I Prepare for a PwC Internship Interview?


PwC publishes a clear service-line split. Audit, Tax, and DAT candidates complete two live virtual behavioral interviews back to back. Most Advisory positions require a case interview, with details supplied in the invitation. PwC also says candidates may not use generative AI during the assessment or interview process.


- **For Audit, DAT, and Tax, prepare evidence stories.** Use examples about accuracy, deadlines, unclear information, client or team communication, and a time you corrected your own work.
- **For Advisory, practice a spoken case.** Clarify the objective, structure the problem, state assumptions, work through evidence, and make a concise recommendation.
- **Use the PwC Professional framework carefully.** Connect each answer to a real action and result instead of repeating leadership language from the careers page.
- **Know every line of the resume.** Review the calculation, source, control, code, or analysis behind each claim and keep confidential client or school data out of shared materials.
