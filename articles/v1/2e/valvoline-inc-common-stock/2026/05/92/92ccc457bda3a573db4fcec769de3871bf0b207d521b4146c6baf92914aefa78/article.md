### Valvoline Instant Oil Change℠ Ranked No. 1 In Automotive Franchises


May 27, 2026


Recognition in *Entrepreneur* magazine’s 2026 ranking reflects franchise network strength and consistent execution across the system.


LEXINGTON, Ky.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--


Valvoline Inc. (NYSE: VVV), the quick, easy, trusted leader in preventive automotive maintenance, has been recognized as No. 1 in the automotive franchise category in *Entrepreneur* magazine’s 2026 ranking. The new ranking recognizes the Top 10 Franchises in Every Industry across major industry categories.


Valvoline Instant Oil Change earned its top ranking by building a strong franchise system based on a proven operating model, broad brand awareness, and a commitment to delivering quick, easy and trusted service.


“This recognition demonstrates the strength of the Valvoline Instant Oil Change franchise system,” said Adam Worsham, Chief Franchising Officer at Valvoline Inc. “It highlights the performance of our franchise partners throughout the network and our shared dedication to providing quick, easy and trusted service in communities nationwide.”


The recognition highlights the strength and consistency of the Valvoline Instant Oil Change franchise network and the performance of partners across the system. The ranking appears in the May/June 2026 issue of *Entrepreneur* .


**About Valvoline Inc.**


Valvoline Inc. (NYSE: VVV) delivers quick, easy, trusted service at more than 2,400 franchised and company-operated service centers across the United States and Canada. The Company completes more than 30 million services annually system-wide, from about 15-minute stay-in-your-car oil changes to a variety of manufacturer-recommended maintenance services such as wiper replacements and tire rotations. At Valvoline Inc., it all starts with our people, including the 13,000 team members who are working to drive the full potential of our core business, deliver sustainable network growth, and innovate to meet the evolving needs of our customers and the car parc.
