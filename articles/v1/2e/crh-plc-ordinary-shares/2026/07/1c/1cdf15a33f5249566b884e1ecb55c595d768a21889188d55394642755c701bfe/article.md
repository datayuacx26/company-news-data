# CRH Confirms Date for Q2 2026 Results

NEW YORK – July 9, 2026 – CRH (NYSE: CRH), the leading provider of building materials, will publish its Q2 2026 financial results before market open on Thursday, July 30, 2026, in advance of a conference call and webcast presentation at 8:00 a.m. (EDT).


CRH’s results and the related presentation will be available at[www.crh.com/investors/results-presentations](http://www.crh.com/investors/results-presentations) .


Registrations for the event can be made at[www.crh.com/investors](http://www.crh.com/investors) . Upon registration a link to join the call and dial-in details will be made available.


A replay of the webcast will be available on[www.crh.com](http://www.crh.com/) .
