# Asia: A large and growing direct lending opportunity

As private credit continues its global expansion, Bain Capital Credit's Andrew Schantz makes the case for Asia-Pacific direct lending in the latest issue of *Private Debt Investor Magazine* . Schantz outlines why structural differences from Western markets — including lower leverage, stronger maintenance covenants, and lender-friendly terms — are creating a compelling opportunity set for disciplined investors.
