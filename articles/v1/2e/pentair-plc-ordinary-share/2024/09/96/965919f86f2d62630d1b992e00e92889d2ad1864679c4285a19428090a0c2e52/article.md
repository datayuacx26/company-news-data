# Dive into serenity with Pentair at Piscine Global 2024

**Pentair Pool is pleased to invite you to discover its new visual identity at the Piscine Global trade fair from 19 to 22 November 2024. Visit our immersive 150m² stand and dive into a universe of solutions combining efficiency and sustainability for the renovation and construction of new pools.**


###
Discover innovative solutions that make the swimming pools of yesterday and tomorrow more sustainable


At our stand, you will have the opportunity to explore a technical room fully equipped with our flagship innovations for new and renovated pools that are more economical, reliable and automatic, such as these:

Variable speed pumps


Reduce your energy consumption with our variable speed pumps, including the silent[Swimmey VSe](https://www.pentairpooleurope.com/products/pumps/swimmey-vse) , for a tranquil swimming experience. You can also find the[Sta-Rite VS2](https://www.pentairpooleurope.com/products/pumps/la-sta-rite-vs2) , the[SuperFlo VS2](https://www.pentairpooleurope.com/products/pumps/superflo-vs2) and the[IntelliFlo3 VSF](https://www.pentairpooleurope.com/products/pumps/intelliflo3) , connected for easy control.


[Learn more about IntelliFlo3 VSF](https://www.pentairpooleurope.com/products/pumps/intelliflo3)

Eco-friendly filters

[Discover Clean and Clear Plus](https://www.pentairpooleurope.com/products/filters/clean-clear-plus)

IntelliTemp heat pump


A technological and aesthetic jewel and the only heat pump with a flow rate that automatically adapts to the conditions, guaranteeing energy savings and easy installation for professionals.


[More information about IntelliTemp](https://www.pentairpooleurope.com/products/heaters-and-heat-pumps/intellitemp)
