# Immerse yourself in our new brand visual system!

Our mission at Pentair is unique. While distinguishing us from the competition, it recounts the activity of our brands and the stories experienced by each of our market segments.


Today, to better share this mission with our customers and communicate our solutions to them with greater proximity, our brand visual system is evolving.


It includes the new names of our core segments, which more clearly reflect their respective mission and values. In addition, each of these segments has a unique visual identity to make it immediately recognisable.


Finally, the Pentair diamond, always present, symbolises the excellence of our solutions and also provides a different colour for each segment to enhance their visual strength.


A new trend in Pentair’s history


**The result?** A brand system that brings us closer to our different segments, gives us the opportunity to communicate in a more engaging manner with our audiences and allows them to identify more closely with our solutions.


With this new visual system, Pentair remains more focused than ever on the needs of its customers and users, to whom we continue to offer excellent solutions in the highest respect for sustainability standards.
