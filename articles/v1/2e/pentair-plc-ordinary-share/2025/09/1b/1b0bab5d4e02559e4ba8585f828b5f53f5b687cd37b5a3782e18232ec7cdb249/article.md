# Pentair at Aquanale 2025: head for serenity with a connected, sustainable swimming pool

Pentair will be back at[Aquanale 2025](https://www.aquanale.com/) , a major European event for the swimming pool industry. This year, Belgium is the show's partner country: the perfect opportunity to highlight the Pentair plant in Herentals and to point out that many of our flagship products are manufactured there.


For our teams in the Benelux and DACH regions, it's a unique opportunity to talk directly to pool professionals and distributors, and to present a **complete range of equipment** for both new build and renovation.


###


### The 2025 version of the swimming pool: more eco-responsible and intelligent


Building and renovating, consuming less water and electricity, simplifying maintenance: these are the major trends that Pentair supports on a daily basis. "


*Swimming pools must become more eco-responsible and accessible* ," explains Philippe Soille, Pentair Pool's Sales Manager for the Benelux countries.


Variable speed pumps, cartridge filters, intelligent heat pumps: our solutions aim to reduce costs and environmental impact, while improving user comfort. They also help to provide greater peace of mind for pool owners, while enabling professionals to **manage their pools remotely and optimise their travel** .


### A stand designed with you in mind


With product demonstrations, technical experts on hand and direct contact with the sales teams, the Pentair stand is first and foremost a **meeting place** . "We want to show that Pentair is a reliable partner that supports its customers and shares their experience," says Philippe Soille.


Visitors will also be able to take the opportunity to join the **PIP loyalty programme,** designed to provide pool professionals with a unique set of tools and benefits to develop their business hand-in-hand with Pentair.

### Building the future together


For both Philippe Soille and Oliver Frühwald, Aquanale 2025 is a high point: a chance to show that **Pentair is innovating, moving forward and building with its partners** . Pentair is looking forward to seeing you in Cologne to discover its sustainable product ranges, suitable for all projects, and to work together to imagine the swimming pool of tomorrow: more eco-responsible, more efficient and ever closer to your customers' needs.
