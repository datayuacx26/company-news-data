# A New Chapter for Space: Genenta Completes First-Tranche Investment in Sòphia High Tech

*[Raffaele Sansone](https://www.genenta.com/team/raffaele-sansone/) ,[Gianfranco De Nigris](https://www.genenta.com/team/gianfranco-de-nigris/) ,[Antonio Caraviello](https://www.genenta.com/team/antonio-caraviello/) ,[Pierluigi Paracchi](https://www.genenta.com/team/pierluigi-paracchi/) ,[Domenico Borrelli](https://www.genenta.com/team/domenico-borrelli/)*


*,Genenta enters Italy's space and defense supply chain with an initial stake, and a performance-driven path to control.*


Genenta Science S.p.A. (Nasdaq: GNTA), evolving into Saentra Forge, has completed the first tranche of its investment in **Sòphia High Tech S.r.l.** ("Sòphia HT"), an Italian company that manufactures critical parts for Europe's space and defense programs.
Following clearance under the Italian **Golden Power** framework, obtained on June 12, 2026, the first tranche was completed on **June 25, 2026** . Genenta currently holds a **19.9% stake** in Sòphia HT. The investment is structured in two tranches, for a total of **€6.0 million** ; the **second tranche is performance-driven** and, upon completion, would bring Genenta to a **51% controlling interest** .
The proceeds are intended to help Sòphia HT scale production capacity, accelerate technological differentiation, and strengthen commercial penetration — a further step in Genenta's transformation into a next-generation industrial consolidator in Italy's national-security-regulated sectors. *The entire Sòphia High Tech team gathers to mark a milestone that strengthens Italy's role in the global space supply chain.*
