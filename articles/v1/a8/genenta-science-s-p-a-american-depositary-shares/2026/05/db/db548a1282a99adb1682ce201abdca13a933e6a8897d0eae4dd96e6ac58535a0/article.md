# JOIN US for the event “Industrial Policy and Finance for Economic Security Guarding Strategic Sectors”

Fondazione Praexidia cordially invites you to the second edition of its annual conference


Industrial Policy and Finance for Economic Security
Guarding Strategic Sectors


**Monday, 22 June 2026 — 4:00 PM Chamber of Deputies, Sala Refettorio Via del Seminario 76, Rome**


The conference will open with an update of the research data conducted with Banca Investis on over 5,200 Private Equity transactions in Italy between 2000 and 2026, exploring the relationship between finance, industrial control and national economic security.
