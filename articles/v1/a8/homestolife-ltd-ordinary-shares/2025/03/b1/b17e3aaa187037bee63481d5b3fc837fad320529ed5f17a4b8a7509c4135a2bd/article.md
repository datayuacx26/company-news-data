# Sofa-Buying Checklist for First-Time Homeowners

Moving into your first home is exciting, and one of the most significant purchases you’ll make is your sofa. It’s the heart of your living room, where you’ll relax, entertain, and make memories. But with so many options out there, how do you choose the right one? Don’t worry—this checklist has got you covered!


**1. Measure Your Space**


Before falling in love with a sofa, measure your living room. Take note of:


- **Wall lengths**
- **Doorways and hallways** to ensure easy delivery.
- **Space needed for other furniture** like coffee tables or side tables.
- **Lift / Elevator:** If you live in HDB like most of us, measuring the interior of the lift can help you decide on buying the right sofa size. ****


*Pro Tip:* Use painter’s tape to outline the sofa’s dimensions on your floor to visualize its size. At HomesToLife, we offer various coffee and side tables, so you’re sure to find a piece that fits your home perfectly.


**2. Determine Your Needs**


Think about how you’ll use the sofa:


- **Relaxation:** A plush, deep-seated sofa is perfect for cozy movie nights. Whether you prefer a recliner or a stationary sofa, we have both options for you.
- **Entertaining:** Consider a sectional to accommodate guests. The more the merrier!
- **Versatility:** A sofa bed or storage sofa is ideal for smaller homes or multi-functional spaces.


*Don’t Forget:* If you have pets or kids, choose durable,[stain-resistant fabrics](https://homestolife.com/blogs/news/safe-guard-fabric?_pos=1&_sid=094cae79e&_ss=r) to keep your sofa looking great.


**3. Select a Style**


Your sofa should complement your home’s decor. Are you drawn to:


- **Modern Minimalism:** Sleek lines and neutral tones
- **Classic Elegance:** Rolled arms and tufted designs
- **Scandinavian Vibes:** Light wood accents and soft fabrics


*Tip:* Our customizable sofas come in over[400 options,](https://homestolife.com/blogs/news/understanding-the-different-types-of-sofa-upholstery?_pos=2&_sid=0ebcc6e53&_ss=r) so you can match your style perfectly.


**4. Prioritize Comfort**


If possible, test the sofa by sitting on it. Consider:


- **Seat Depth:** Do your feet touch the floor when seated, or do you prefer a more loungy feel?
- **Cushion Firmness:** Do you like firm support or soft plushness?
- **Backrest Height:** Do you prefer a standard back or a high back that provides full body support?

Your lifestyle will dictate the best sofa material:


- [Fabric:](https://homestolife.com/collections/fabric-sofas) Soft and cozy, available in a wide range of colors. Pet-friendly options offer added durability.
- [Leather:](https://homestolife.com/collections/leather-sofas) Timeless and easy to clean, though it may show scratches. We also offer kid- and pet-friendly leather options.


*Fun Fact:* Our[pet-friendly fabrics](https://homestolife.com/blogs/news/homestolifes-ultimate-guide-to-pet-friendly-furniture?_pos=2&_sid=6f8748ed2&_ss=r) are scratch-resistant and come in stylish textures and colors.


**6. Decide on a Color**


Neutral tones like gray, beige, or cream are versatile and timeless. For a statement piece, go bold with rich jewel tones or vibrant hues.


*Tip:* Lighter colors can make a small room look bigger, while darker shades hide wear and tear.


**7. Test for Durability**


Look for these features to ensure[long-lasting quality:](https://homestolife.com/blogs/news/commitment-to-quality-inside-homestolife-s-rigorous-testing-and-certification-process?_pos=9&_sid=619e131af&_ss=r)


- **Strong frames** made from hardwood or metal
- **High-density foam cushions** for lasting comfort
- **Reinforced stitching** to withstand everyday use


**8. Don’t Forget About Delivery**


Make sure your sofa can fit through doorways, stairwells, and lifts. Ask for precise dimensions and delivery guidelines.
