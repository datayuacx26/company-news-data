# HomesToLife Pop-Up Store — Now Open at Isetan Scotts!

We’re excited to share that HomesToLife pop-up store is now officially open at Isetan Scotts, Shaw House!


From 5 August to 31 October 2025, explore our best-loved pieces and experience the comfort, craftsmanship, and contemporary style that HomesToLife is known for.


📍 **Where:** Isetan Scotts @ Shaw House, Level 2 (Beside TCC)
🗓️ **When:** 5 August – 31 October 2025


🛋️ **Here’s what you can expect:**


- A showcase of **ready-stock furniture** for instant inspiration
- Our best-selling **sofa collections** , tailored for every lifestyle
- **Modern, functional pieces** that blend comfort with design
- **One-on-one styling advice** to help you pick the perfect piece


Whether you’re furnishing a new space or just browsing, this is the perfect place to explore our collections in person and reimagine your living space.


We’re now open – come by and say hi! Visit us and let our team help you discover the perfect piece to complete your home.
