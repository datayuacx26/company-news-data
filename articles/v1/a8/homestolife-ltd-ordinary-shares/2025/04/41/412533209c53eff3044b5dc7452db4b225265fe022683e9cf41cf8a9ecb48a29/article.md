### **How the Wrong Sofa Makes Pain Worse**


****


According to


Dr. Jesse Timm


, many people


unknowingly worsen their chronic pain


by sitting in


poorly designed furniture


for long hours.


***"Your spine is designed to support you, but it needs the right kind of support in return. A poorly structured sofa that doesn’t align with the natural curves of your back can lead to chronic discomfort, postural issues, and increased muscle fatigue," he explains.***


Here’s how the wrong sofa could be making your pain worse:


❌


Poor Posture


→ A slouchy sofa promotes bad sitting habits, leading to misalignment and strain on the


neck, shoulders, and lower back


.


❌


Lack of Lumbar Support


→ Without proper lumbar support, the


natural curve of your spine flattens


, increasing the risk of


lower back pain and stiffness


.


❌


Too Soft or Too Firm Cushioning


→ Overly soft sofas cause


sinking


, which


misaligns the spine


, while overly firm ones create


pressure points


that


increase tension


in muscles and joints.


❌


No Neck & Head Support


→ A sofa without an


adjustable headrest


can cause


forward head posture


, which is a leading cause of


chronic neck and upper back pain


.


How the Right Sofa Can Relieve Pain:


Dr. Jesse emphasises that the


right furniture provides proper support throughout the body


which aids in


long-term pain relief


.


***"A well-designed sofa encourages better posture, distributes weight evenly, and helps your body relax without strain. Features like lumbar support, adjustable headrests, and recliners can significantly improve spinal health," he shares.***

#### Dr. Jesse’s Expert Tip: How to Sit Correctly on a Sofa


Even the best sofa won’t help if you


sit incorrectly


. Dr. Jesse recommends this


simple technique


to


protect your spine and reduce pain


:


✔


Keep feet flat on the floor


(or supported with a footrest if reclining).


✔


Use lumbar support


—adjust it to maintain a slight


arch in your lower back


.


✔


Avoid slouching


—sit back fully so your spine is aligned with the backrest.


✔


Support your neck and head


with an adjustable headrest or a pillow.


✔


Switch positions regularly


—don’t stay in one position for too long.


***"Your sofa should work for you—not against you. If you find yourself constantly shifting, slouching, or experiencing discomfort, it might be time for a better seating solution," says Dr. Jesse.***
