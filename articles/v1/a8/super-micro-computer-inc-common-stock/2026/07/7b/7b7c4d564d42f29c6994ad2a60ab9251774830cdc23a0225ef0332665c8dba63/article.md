# Unlocking Flexible, High-Availability Edge Infrastructure with Supermicro Servers

# Unlocking Flexible, High-Availability Edge Infrastructure with Supermicro Servers

In today’s fast-paced digital world, businesses need IT infrastructure that adapts precisely to their unique demands—especially at the edge. Organizations require the freedom to select the ideal setup without compromise: whether a compact, edge-optimized model for remote sites or a high-performance rack server for core operations. The goal is to fine-tune environments for peak performance and cost efficiency.


A powerful full-stack hyperconverged infrastructure (HCI) approach, validated in partnership with Supermicro, delivers exactly that. These integrated solutions support a wide range of deployment scenarios and are available through strategic integration partners. They simplify procurement, deployment, and ongoing operations while enabling right-sized infrastructure that eliminates overprovisioning.


## Validated Supermicro Servers for Every Edge Scenario


The lightweight HCI software has been rigorously validated on a curated selection of Supermicro servers. Each model brings distinct advantages tailored to edge and distributed environments.


[Supermicro AS-E300-14GR](https://www.supermicro.com/en/products/system/iot/fan-based%20embedded/as-e300-14gr) For strong compute performance in space-constrained edge workloads, this server supports AMD EPYC 4004/4005 processors with up to 16 cores and 32 threads, all within a modest 65W CPU power envelope. Its compact form factor makes it ideal for retail stores, branch offices, manufacturing sites, and other locations with limited rack space—while still offering impressive expandability.


[Supermicro SYS-112B-FWT](https://www.supermicro.com/en/products/system/iot/1u/sys-112b-fwt) This 1U edge platform combines flexibility with generous expansion capabilities. It features front I/O, power, and serviceability designed for space-constrained environments. Support for both AC and DC power with redundant supplies maximizes deployment flexibility and uptime. Paired with the HCI software, it delivers enterprise-grade virtualization and high availability for small remote offices or retail locations.


[Supermicro AS-1115SV-WTNRT](https://www.supermicro.com/en/products/system/wio/1u/as%20-1115sv-wtnrt) Powered by AMD processors, this platform excels in parallel processing for virtualization, database management, and edge computing. It offers 10 front hot-swap 2.5" drive bays that support all-SATA, NVMe, SAS, or hybrid configurations. This versatility allows organizations to deploy applications locally while maintaining the high availability modern businesses depend on.


[Supermicro SYS-112B-WN2T](https://www.supermicro.com/en/products/system/iot/1u/sys-112b-wn2t) When high-performance, data-intensive workloads arise, this server shines. With 6 hot-swap 2.5" NVMe bays and 3 PCIe 5.0 slots in a compact 1U form factor, it supports GPU-accelerated compute right at the edge—perfect for applications requiring significant capacity and flexibility.


These validated systems allow the HCI software to be deployed confidently—whether as a single-server installation or two-node high-availability cluster—without unnecessary hardware.


## Key Use Cases and Benefits


**Seamless IT for Industrial and Distributed Environments:** This combination delivers a powerful, cost-effective upgrade path with high availability and minimal complexity, ideal for industrial settings, retail, manufacturing, healthcare, education, hospitality, and remote operations.


**Zero Downtime Deployments:** Organizations can transition smoothly to hyperconverged infrastructure, repurposing existing storage for backups while keeping operations running continuously.


**Flexible Installation and Scalability:** Deploy as a single server, simple two-node cluster, or expand into multi-node setups. Add capacity to existing servers or grow the cluster without disrupting services. The software’s agnostic nature (server- and hypervisor-compatible) and flexible licensing make scaling effortless.


**Reduced Hardware Footprint:**


**R** equiring just two servers for high availability, the solution dramatically cuts hardware needs compared to traditional three-node setups.


**Maximized Performance and Reliability:** Synchronous data mirroring between servers eliminates single points of failure, ensuring uptime for mission-critical applications.


**Cost and Energy Efficiency:** Local data processing reduces cloud expenses. The software-defined approach consumes far less power and cooling than legacy physical SANs, lowering both capital and operational costs.


**Strong Data Protection:** Resilient mirroring and flexible capacity additions protect data without increasing expenses or causing downtime.


## Why This Matters Now


As organizations rethink edge investments amid rising hardware costs, a resilient two-node architecture offers compelling economics. It reduces infrastructure footprint, power consumption, and procurement complexity without sacrificing availability—making it a strong differentiator for distributed environments.


## Conclusion


Pairing lightweight, high-availability HCI software with Supermicro’s high-performance, compact edge servers creates a flexible, scalable, and cost-effective IT foundation. This approach removes complexity, cuts hardware costs, and delivers zero-downtime operations across remote locations, industrial sites, and enterprise settings.


The solutions are available immediately through global channel partners and distributors as pre-integrated, ready-to-deploy appliances—streamlining everything from procurement to support.


Businesses seeking simplified, resilient edge infrastructure now have a practical, high-performing option that adapts to real-world constraints while future-proofing operations. Whether you are modernizing retail locations, manufacturing floors, or distributed branch networks, this validated combination delivers the right infrastructure, right where it is needed.
