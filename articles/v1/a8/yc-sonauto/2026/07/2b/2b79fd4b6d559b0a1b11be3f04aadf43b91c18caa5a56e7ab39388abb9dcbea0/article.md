# Sonauto is now Treblo

Sonauto is now **Treblo** . We're the same four people working towards the same goal, and everything in your library is where you left it. The new address is[treblo.com](https://treblo.com/) .


If you came here from a` sonauto.ai` link, you're in the right place. This post covers what's new and why.


## Why we changed the name


We picked "Sonauto" early, back when we were a research project, and it fit less and less as we grew. People couldn't pronounce it ("Son-auto? Sono-to?") and kept mistyping it into the address bar, which makes for a hard name to recommend to a friend. It also sounded too technical. That suited a model fine, but we're building[a way to play with music](https://treblo.com/about) , and we wanted a name that fit that goal better.


**Treblo** comes from *treble* , the top end of a sound where the melody usually lives. It's short, and easy to say. And the little bird in the corner of the app must like it more, since he's now showing a friendlier face.


## What's changing


- The domain.` sonauto.ai` is now` treblo.com` . Old links redirect, so anything you've already shared keeps working, but update your bookmarks when you get a chance.
- The name everywhere else: the app, the logo, and our social accounts.
- The API host. Developers should point at` api.treblo.com` . The old` api.sonauto.ai` host still resolves for now, but please migrate when you can. The[developer docs](https://treblo.com/developers/docs) list the current endpoints.


## What's *not* changing


- Your account. Same login and password; you don't need to do anything.
- Your songs. Every track you've made is still in your projects, every post is on your account.
- Your shared links. Songs, playlists, and artist pages keep playing at their old URLs, which now redirect.
- The team and the mission. Still the four of us, still answering our own support email, still trying to make music possible for the people who love it most.


## Do I need to do anything?


For almost everyone, no. Open the app like always and keep making music. If you have bookmarks pointing at` sonauto.ai` , update them, and if you build on our API, plan a move to` api.treblo.com` .


Thanks for sticking with us through the rename.


If you truly love something,[create it](https://treblo.com/login) .


— The Treblo Team
