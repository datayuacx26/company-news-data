# StackAI Joins Asana to Build the Future of Agentic Work Management

## A Letter to Our Customers


Three years ago, we had the realization that AI was going to fundamentally change how the world's largest organizations operate. So we created StackAI to bring agentic AI into your enterprises—the world’s most regulated organizations—including banks, hospitals, defense contractors, and more.


We thank you for being StackAI customers, for pushing us to build the best platform in the world, and for inspiring us every day. It is our immense honor to partner with you to bring AI to your teams in a safe, scalable way.


Now, it’s time to take StackAI to the next level.


We’re incredibly excited to announce today that StackAI is joining Asana, where humans and agents run workflows together from a shared plan, with shared memory, goals and context.


**For you, this doesn’t change anything about our platform, your existing workflows, or the way we collaborate as your partners.** You’ll keep using StackAI just as you do now. StackAI will continue operating as the same product you know and trust, with the same white-glove service, enterprise-grade governance, and integration breadth.


With Asana’s support and resources behind us, we’ll be adding more features, more tools, and unlocking even more AI use cases for you to accelerate ROI. Over time, this new era should only make the experience better for you.


Thank you again for your support and trust. We feel privileged to work with each and every one of you, and to build the enterprise of the future together.


**Please don’t hesitate to rely on your AI Strategist and Forward Deployed Engineer team should you have any questions. You can read the full press release**[here](https://asana.com/press/releases/pr/asana-acquires-stackai-adding-cross-system-execution-for-human-agent-teams/e7c73b97-ae8c-4e51-b927-189ccb184146) **.**


With gratitude,


Tony Rosinol and Bernard Aceituno


## FAQ


1.


**Does this change the StackAI product?**


No. StackAI continues to operate as its own product and brand following its acquisition by Asana.


2.


**What is Asana?**


Asana is a leader in collaborative project management software for teams. Asana helps you, your teams, and your stakeholders collaborate faster and smoother to achieve business objectives. To learn more about Asana visit[asana.com](http://asana.com/) or reach out to your AI Strategist.


3.


**Do I need to take any action as a result of this announcement?**


No. There are no actions needed on your end.


4.


**Does this mean that my workflows have to integrate with Asana?**


No. All of your integrations and connections will remain the same. You will not be required to transfer anything to Asana.


5.


**Does this change my relationship with Forward Deployed Engineers and AI Strategists?**


No. Your customer success team will remain with you. This transition will not impact your collaboration and partnership with the StackAI team.


6.


**Does this mean I lose access to my workflows?**


No. All of your workflows will remain functional exactly as they are.


7.


**Can I still use StackAI to build agents that don’t connect to Asana?**


Yes. StackAI will continue to support your unique and specific use cases.


8.


**Does this impact my on-premise or VPC deployment?**


No. All deployments will remain functional as they are.


9.


**How will Asana protect my data?**


Asana upholds a continuous commitment to protecting customer data. To learn more about how Asana processes your information, please see[Asana’s Privacy Statement](https://asana.com/terms/privacy-statement) . For further details and answers to other frequently asked questions, please visit the[Asana AI FAQ](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-ai-faq?language=en_US) and[Asana Trust Center](https://trustcenter.asana.com/) .
