# The 6 AI Agent Architectures Every CIO Should Know: Bernard Aceituno for CIO.com

Bernard Aceituno, co-founder of StackAI, was featured in[CIO.com](https://www.cio.com/profile/bernard-aceituno/) , where he argued that "AI agent" has become so overloaded a term that it's lost meaning, and that a CIO's real edge lies in architectural fluency: recognizing on sight which of six agent architectures a given business problem actually calls for.


Drawing on three years inside hundreds of enterprise AI deployments, Aceituno lays out six archetypes, each illustrated with anonymized real-world examples. The conversational assistant is the chat-based partner employees and customers turn to, strongest when it combines updated information, persistent memory, action-taking tools, and citations, as with a law firm assistant that made institutional knowledge queryable on demand. The triggered workflow runs silently when an email, ticket, or file arrives, executing a process without requiring user adoption, as with a commercial insurer that auto-sorts and processes broker submissions. The autonomous agent, given a task, plans its own steps and calls sub-agents for open-ended work like multi-source research or incident investigation.


The multi-agent team coordinates several specialized agents through a shared protocol, and Aceituno highlights the proposer-critic loop, where one agent produces an output and another evaluates it, as one of the smartest techniques in agent design for compliance, contract analysis, and auditable work. The human-in-the-loop (HITL) agent handles the mechanical 80% of a task while preserving human judgment at critical moments, as with a health system's prior-authorization drafting routed to a nurse for review. Finally, the scheduled agent runs the same defined task on a set cadence, like a private equity firm's Monday-morning portfolio briefing that lands before the weekly meeting.


His closing argument urges CIOs to treat the architecture decision as the first design choice, since adoption, governance, and trust all get easier when the fit is right. Read the full article[here](https://www.cio.com/article/4198444/the-6-kinds-of-ai-agent-architectures.html) .
