# StackAI Partners with Egnyte to Bring AI to the Content Environment

Egnyte recently announced its partnership with StackAI, bringing AI-powered workflow automation directly into the content environment teams already use to store and govern their business-critical documents. The integration lets organizations put that content to work with AI agents without giving up the security and governance they rely on.


With the new native integration, you can connect Egnyte file storage directly to StackAI's no-code workflow builder, alongside 100+ enterprise tools and any leading LLM. From there, teams can build agentic workflows that take action in Egnyte or use Egnyte documents for downstream tasks, automating complex, multi-step processes that used to eat up hours of manual work.


The article walks through four practical use cases:


-


Document ingestion. When a file lands in email, a StackAI agent can automatically classify, rename, and file it into the right nested folder based on your conventions. No manual sorting, and immediate compliance across incoming documentation.


-


RFP response. Connect a library of past proposals to an agent that drafts new RFP responses, routes them through a human-in-the-loop review, and uploads the final document back to Egnyte or emails it to the customer. Faster turnaround means more deals won, with messaging that stays on-brand.


-


Contract analysis and extraction. StackAI can pull key metadata from contracts stored in Egnyte, including parties, effective dates, termination clauses, and renewal windows. Legal teams can also automate redlining by comparing new agreements against gold-standard documents, then re-uploading the annotated copies. The result is far shorter contract turnaround and clearer visibility into legal risk.


-


Research and reporting. Agents can search across your Egnyte environment using Hybrid Search, pull in live data from web search, Salesforce, Snowflake, and other connected systems, and synthesize it all into a structured report.


Underpinning all of it is a security-first design. The integration connects Egnyte to StackAI through API credentials, with user management, versioning, audit logs, eight layers of governance, and SDLC-style approval workflows so you stay in control of how and when AI touches your corporate IP.


Your Egnyte environment already holds your organization's institutional knowledge. Now, StackAI gives you the workflows to act on it. Read the full announcement[here](https://www.egnyte.com/blog/post/the-future-of-ai-powered-enterprise-workflow-automation-egnyte-stackai) .
