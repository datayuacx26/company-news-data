## What This Partnership Looks Like in Practice


Across dozens of live deployments, the CES + StackAI partnership is already delivering measurable impact across several major capability areas:


### Unlocking Data with Natural Language Analytics


BI dashboards are powerful, but most users can't write SQL or navigate complex data models. CES and StackAI have deployed natural language analytics agents that connect directly to leading BI platforms and data warehouses—letting anyone in the organization ask questions of their data in plain English and get instant, accurate answers. These agents also translate natural language into SQL queries against cloud data warehouses, democratizing access to operational and financial data across the enterprise. The means faster decision-making, reduced dependency on data teams, and broader data literacy organization-wide.


### Accelerating Proposals & RFP Responses


Responding to RFPs and generating proposals is time-intensive, repetitive, and often inconsistent. An AI-powered proposal agent pulls from an organization's business development knowledge base to auto-generate tailored, formatted responses. This use case demonstrates how AI solutions mature rapidly when paired with strong strategic guidance. The impact: dramatically reduced turnaround times, improved consistency, and freed-up bandwidth for strategic pursuits.


### Powering Marketing & Content Intelligence


Marketing teams struggle to connect campaign spend to revenue outcomes, while content creation bottlenecks slow go-to-market velocity. AI agents now perform marketing attribution analysis, generate marketing copy, and deliver analyst-grade insights on campaign performance, all powered by live data connections. These agents give marketing teams the analytical horsepower of a dedicated data science team and the creative throughput of an expanded content operation without adding headcount.


### Prioritizing Dealers & Sites with Intelligence


Organizations with distributed networks need to prioritize where to invest, but the analysis is complex and often manual. AI agents synthesize market data, performance metrics, and strategic criteria to recommend prioritized dealer or site opportunities, applying weighted scoring models to surface the highest-value targets. This means smarter capital allocation, faster expansion decisions, and reduced analytical overhead for field operations teams.


### Automating Document Processing & Compliance


High-volume document workflows (invoices, legal garnishments, regulatory filings) consume enormous staff time and introduce error risk. Intelligent document processing agents extract, classify, validate, and route documents automatically. An AI agent for deployment might process hundreds of invoices with minimal human intervention; another might handle complex legal/payroll garnishment documents with rules-based logic and compliance checks at scale. The impact is massive: often 80%+ reduction in manual processing, while keeping enterprises away from risk.


### Deploying Client-Facing AI Advisors


Customers and constituents expect instant, knowledgeable answers, but scaling human expertise is expensive and inconsistent. Domain-specific AI chatbots and advisors have been deployed across industries including education, healthcare, and consumer services. Trained on curated knowledge bases with advanced RAG systems, these agents deliver expert-level guidance in real time—whether that's college admissions advice, care coordination recommendations, or customer service interactions. Each is tailored to its domain, tone, and compliance requirements, ensuring responses are not just lightning-fast, but trustworthy.
