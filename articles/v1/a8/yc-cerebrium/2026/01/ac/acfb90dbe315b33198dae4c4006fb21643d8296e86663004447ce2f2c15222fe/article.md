# Cerebrium is now ISO 27001 Compliant

Cerebrium is now ISO 27001 Compliant


We’re excited to share that Cerebrium is now ISO 27001 compliant.


This is an important milestone for us as a company and for the teams building on Cerebrium. With ISO 27001 added, Cerebrium now supports SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA, and ISO 27001, making us the most compliant serverless cloud for running CPU and GPU workloads globally.


As AI workloads move from experimentation into real production systems - especially in regulated industries - security, governance, and operational discipline become just as important as performance and cost. ISO 27001 validates Cerebrium’s adherence to rigorous international data sovereignty standards for information security management, protecting how our customers design, operate, and continuously improve their systems through our platform.


For enterprise customers, this unlocks the ability to run real-time AI workloads - LLMs, voice, video, and custom ML pipelines - on a serverless platform without compromising on compliance requirements. It also simplifies vendor reviews and security assessments when deploying globally across regions.


For startups experiencing international growth, this matters just as much. Many early-stage teams want to sell into enterprise, healthcare, or regulated markets, but get blocked by infrastructure compliance long before product readiness. By building on Cerebrium, startups can inherit a compliance-ready foundation from day one enabling hyper focus on shipping product, rather than navigating the jurisdictional complexities of diverse security frameworks surrounding each regional deployment.


Importantly, compliance at Cerebrium doesn’t come at the expense of performance. Teams continue to deploy CPU and GPU workloads globally, route traffic close to users, and scale in seconds - while meeting strict security and data protection standards.


We see this milestone as part of a broader commitment: building infrastructure that’s production-ready by default, whether you’re a startup serving your first enterprise customer or a global organization running AI workloads at scale.
