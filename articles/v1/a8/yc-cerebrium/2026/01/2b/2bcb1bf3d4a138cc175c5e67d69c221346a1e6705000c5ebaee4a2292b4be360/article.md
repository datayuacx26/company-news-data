# Introduction New Regions: India & Stockholm

Introduction New Regions: India & Stockholm


We’re excited to announce the launch of two new Cerebrium regions: **India and Stockholm** . These regions were added directly in response to customer demand, as more teams deploy production AI systems that require low latency, regional proximity, and stronger data residency guarantees.


With these new regions, customers can deploy workloads closer to their end users, unlocking lower latency for real-time applications such as voice agents, video pipelines, and interactive LLM workloads. Running compute closer to where requests originate helps improve responsiveness and overall user experience, especially for latency-sensitive use cases.


The Stockholm region also enables customers to meet European data residency requirements. It supports both CPU and GPU workloads and is GDPR compliant, making it suitable for teams operating in regulated environments or serving customers within the EU.


Both regions offer the same Cerebrium experience customers are used to - no rewrites, no special configuration, and full support for real-time and batch workloads. You can choose the region that best fits your latency, compliance, or business needs and deploy as usual.


Customers can get started immediately and learn more about deploying across regions in our documentation: https://docs.cerebrium.ai/cerebrium/deployments/multi-region-deployment


We’re continuously adding new regions as demand grows. If there’s a location you’d like to see supported next, feel free to reach out - we’d love to hear from you.
