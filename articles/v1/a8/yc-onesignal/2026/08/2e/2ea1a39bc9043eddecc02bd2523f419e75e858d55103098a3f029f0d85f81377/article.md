# Best Klaviyo Alternatives for eCommerce Brands With a Mobile App

A shopper who abandons a cart on your website is one kind of lost sale. A shopper who ignores a push notification on their phone is a different one entirely… and increasingly the more expensive one to lose. When **mobile app marketing** is a real growth channel for your brand, an email-first platform comparison only tells you half the story.


This guide helps you choose the right engagement platform for your primary customer channels, not to argue that every brand must replace Klaviyo. We'll cover the decision criteria to use, an at-a-glance comparison of the leading Klaviyo alternatives, a detailed "Klaviyo vs. OneSignal" analysis, implementation considerations, and a clear selection framework you can apply to your own stack.


### Why eCommerce brands with apps need a different evaluation lens


Klaviyo built its reputation on eCommerce email and SMS lifecycle marketing, and it does that work well. When you add a mobile app to the picture, the questions change. You start caring about push delivery on iOS and Android, in-app messaging, deep links that open the right product screen, and real-time alerts triggered by events in your app.


An evaluation focused only on email deliverability and campaign templates will miss those needs. The right lens starts from the channels and journeys your customers actually use, then works backward to the platform that supports them. Some teams find their current tool covers everything. Others discover their app has become central to retention, and their engagement infrastructure has not kept up.


Throughout this article, we'll keep the focus on your problems and outcomes: engagement, retention, conversion, and long-term customer value.


### What a push notification provider adds to an eCommerce app stack


**What is a push notification provider?**
A **push notification provider** is a platform that enables messaging to your app or web audiences through permission-based channels. For eCommerce teams, that means reaching shoppers with timely, relevant messages on the devices where they browse and buy.


A useful way to size up any provider is against six practical criteria: reliability, scalability, rich-content support, platform support, customizability, and cost. Each one maps to a real question. Will messages arrive on time at volume? Can you send rich media and interactive formats? Does it cover iOS, Android, and web? Can you shape the experience, and can you predict what it costs as you grow?


Push connects directly to concrete eCommerce journeys:


- Cart and browse recovery
- Price-drop alerts
- Order-status updates
- Re-engagement of lapsed users
- App-install prompts
- Onboarding series


These aren't hypothetical gains, either. Inspire Uplift saw a 6% increase in sales conversions within its first month on OneSignal, with 10% of its entire web push revenue coming from automated abandoned-cart alerts alone, per OneSignal's case study. Evino, meanwhile, sees roughly 8% of total app revenue driven directly by push.


These are illustrative use cases. Not every platform supports every journey natively, which is exactly what your evaluation needs to confirm.


**Where push fits alongside email and SMS**
Push, email, and SMS each do a different job, and the strongest programs coordinate them. Two message types matter here. Marketing campaigns are scheduled or triggered sends aimed at engagement and conversion. **Transactional alerts** are real-time, event-driven messages such as an order shipping or a payment confirmation, usually fired through an event-triggered API call.


Making that coordination work depends on a few building blocks: audience data that stays current, **deep links** that route a shopper to the exact product or screen, and **cross-channel orchestration** so a customer isn't hit with the same message three ways.


It helps to understand how OneSignal models this. A **User** is an individual who can have one or more subscriptions to channels such as push, email, and SMS. A **Subscription** is the specific device or channel through which that User receives messages. That distinction, described in OneSignal's documentation, keeps a single shopper unified across every device and channel they use.


### How to evaluate Klaviyo alternatives for mobile app marketing


Turn the six criteria into a working scorecard. For each one, know exactly what to validate during a trial or technical review.


**Evaluate delivery and experience quality**


- **Delivery reliability:** Test opt-in prompts, permission flows, and actual delivery rates. A **push notification service** that quietly drops messages costs you the exact moments you're paying to reach.
- **Scalability:** Confirm the platform handles your peak send volumes, such as a flash sale or a product launch.
- **Rich-content support:** Check for images, buttons, carousels, and interactive formats that make a message worth tapping.
- **Platform coverage:** Verify native support for iOS, Android, and web push, not just one surface.


**Check data, developer, and operational fit**


- **Customization and personalization:** Test segmentation controls and dynamic content against your real customer data.
- **SDK availability** for every platform you ship on.
- **API-triggered notifications** so an app event can fire a message in real time.
- **Event and profile-data ingestion** , identity resolution, and how the platform unifies a shopper across devices.
- **Deep-link behavior** , integrations with your commerce stack, reporting depth, and how marketers and developers share work.


Label each capability as native, integration-enabled, or something to confirm during evaluation. That distinction prevents you from assuming a feature exists because a competitor's marketing page implies it.


**Model the cost of growth**
Skip guessing at a total. Instead, list the cost drivers and price them against your own numbers:


- Contact or profile volume
- Subscriber volume
- Message volume
- Channel-specific credits or fees
- Feature tiers
- Implementation effort
- Ongoing operational time


The platform that looks cheapest at 5,000 contacts can flip once you add SMS credits or cross a subscriber threshold. Model growth, not just launch.

### Who should choose which platform?


There's no blanket winner. Klaviyo is the stronger fit when the primary need is eCommerce email and SMS, while a push-first or cross-channel platform warrants priority when app push, in-app messaging, and mobile Journeys are central to retention.


**When eCommerce email and SMS are the center of the strategy**


If your revenue comes mostly through email flows and SMS, and your app is a secondary surface, Klaviyo's eCommerce depth and revenue attribution make it a strong fit to evaluate.


**When the app is central to retention**


For an app-led retailer that lives on price-drop alerts, order-status updates, and re-engagement, a platform such as OneSignal deserves priority. Match your scenario to a profile:


- **Shopify-centric email and SMS team:** Omnisend or Klaviyo.

## **Frequently asked questions about Klaviyo alternatives**


#### **Is Klaviyo good for mobile app marketing?**


Yes, for eCommerce email and SMS — that's where Klaviyo's revenue attribution and lifecycle automation genuinely shine. It also supports mobile push. Where it's thinner is web push and Live Activities, so if your app is central to retention and you lean on those surfaces plus in-app messaging and API-triggered alerts, evaluate whether Klaviyo covers enough of that or whether a push-first platform fits better. Test your specific app journeys, such as a deep-linked price-drop alert, during a trial.


#### **What is the best Klaviyo alternative for an app?**


There's no single best answer; it depends on your primary channel. For app-led brands prioritizing mobile and web push with cross-channel orchestration, OneSignal is a strong candidate. For complex event-driven logic, look at Customer.io; for Shopify-centric email and SMS, Omnisend. Match the platform to the journeys your app needs most.


#### **Can Klaviyo send push notifications?**


Yes — Klaviyo ships iOS, Android, and React Native SDKs and supports mobile push on every plan. What matters for your evaluation is depth beyond "can it send push": Klaviyo doesn't support web push or Live Activities today, so an app-led brand leaning on those surfaces will still need to look elsewhere. Confirm SDK coverage, deep-link routing, and real-time transactional push against your specific requirements before committing.


#### **How do OneSignal and Klaviyo differ?**


Klaviyo is an email and SMS lifecycle marketing platform built for eCommerce, and OneSignal is a push notification delivery platform for web and mobile messaging that also supports email, SMS, in-app messaging, RCS, and Live Activities. They're frequently used together rather than as direct substitutes. Your decision hinges on whether email and SMS or mobile push and in-app messaging sit at the center of your strategy.


#### **Which platform supports transactional push alerts?**


OneSignal supports API-triggered messages so an app event can fire a timely alert, and MoEngage cites real-time transactional alerts through a single API. Brevo offers transactional messaging through an SMTP relay and API. During evaluation, test latency, deep-link behavior, and how the event reaches the platform, because transactional performance depends on your integration as much as the vendor.
