# FirstService Residential Announces James Magid Appointed to Community Association Managers International Certification Board


Thursday March 01, 2018


**EATONTOWN, N.J. (March 1, 2018)** – FirstService Residential, New Jersey’s leading property management company, is proud to announce James Magid, CMCA®, LSM®, PCAM® has been appointed to serve on the Community Association Managers International Certification Board (CAMICB). The mission of the CAMICB is to ensure community association managers practice with professionalism, integrity and knowledge. The Board administers the Certified Manager of Community Associations (CMCA®) certification program, the only internationally accredited certification program in the field.


Magid has been a community association management professional for over 25 years including a FirstService Residential associate for over 20 years. Currently, Magid is Vice President for FirstService Residential where he provides leadership and operational oversight to NJ associates and the client communities they serve.


Magid holds the Certified Manager of Community Associations (CMCA®) designation, as well as the Professional Community Association Manager (PCAM®) and the Large Scale Manager (LSM®) designations through Community Associations Institute (CAI). He is an active member of both the New Jersey chapter and the national level of CAI, serving on multiple boards and committees over the years. .


**About FirstService Residential**
