Carré x Coca-Cola®'s Football-Inspired Collection


**What:** a.k.a. Brands Holding Corp. (NYSE: AKA), a portfolio of next-generation fashion brands, today announced that Culture Kings’ private label streetwear brand Carré has partnered with Coca-Cola® to debut an exclusive football-inspired collection. Merging Carré’s contemporary streetwear aesthetic with Coca-Cola®’s iconic global heritage, the collaboration is rooted in the intersection of street and sport. Featuring oversized mesh jerseys, faded souvenir graphic tees and caps, and post-game track sets, the collection drop offers a modern tribute to the nostalgic fusion of sport, fashion, and culture, reminiscent of that memorable final match at the legendary Rose Bowl in Pasadena, California – the last time the game was played on U.S. turf.


**When:** The Collection officially launched June 1 online in the U.S. and at Culture Kings’ Las Vegas flagship store. It will launch June 9 online and in-stores at all Culture Kings Australia and NZ locations.


**Quote:** “We’re incredibly excited to see the collaboration between Carré and Coca-Cola® come to life,” said **Brad Lancaster, Global Head of Brands, a.k.a. Brands.** “Our teams have created a collection that truly honors football’s heritage while delivering the bold streetwear aesthetic our customers love. We look forward to celebrating the collection with our customers through an in-store takeover at Culture Kings’ Las Vegas flagship.”


**Media Opportunities:** On Saturday, May 30, Carré and Coca-Cola® took over the Culture Kings Las Vegas flagship, transforming the ~14,000-square-foot store into a high-energy activation showcasing the Carré x Coca-Cola® football-inspired collection. DJ Drewbyrd performed in-store, complete with a VIP bar experience, football simulator and more.


On Saturday, June 13, the party is moving to Los Angeles for a Culture Kings Summer Kick-Off event at the Hollywood Roosevelt Hotel, celebrating the Carré x Coca-Cola® collection with music by Donavan’s Yard.
