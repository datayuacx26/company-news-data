# 2016 Energy, 2026 Mindset: Connecting with Friends Never Goes Out of Style

The internet has spoken, and it sounds a lot like 2016.


From dog ears and flower crowns to lip kits, low-fi selfies, and throwback soundtracks, people across platforms are rallying around the classic Snapchat experiences that felt raw, playful, and unapologetically real — and still define Snapchat today.


To help Snapchatters jump right back into the era, Snapchat is rolling out a special 2016 Flashback today, making it easier than ever to revisit the moments, Lenses, and vibes that defined a decade.


Major celebrities, creators, and everyday Snapchatters are already leading the charge, revisiting their favorite throwbacks with some of Snapchat’s most iconic Lenses, and clips declaring “Snapchat is in” for 2026 are racking up views, proving this isn’t just nostalgia — it’s a full-fledged cultural return.

As culture comes full circle, Snapchat remains the place where being in the moment never goes out of style.
