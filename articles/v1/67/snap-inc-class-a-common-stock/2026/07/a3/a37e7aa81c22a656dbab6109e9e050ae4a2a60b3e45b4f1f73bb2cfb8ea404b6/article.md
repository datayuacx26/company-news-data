# See for Yourself: Watch the SPECS Launch on September 16

We’re excited to host the launch of[SPECS](https://www.specs.com/) , our new augmented reality glasses that make computing more human, on Wednesday, September 16th in Los Angeles, California.


Snap co-founder and CEO Evan Spiegel will share the first in-depth look at SPECS, demonstrating how they bring AI assistance, work tools, entertainment, and shared experiences into the world around us, helping people create, connect, learn, and get things done while staying present.


The event will bring together industry leaders, partners, developers, and creators who will be among the first to experience SPECS hands-on, discovering a new way to experience the world.


Register to watch the livestream at[specs.com/launch](http://specs.com/launch) on Wednesday, September 16 at 4:00pm PT.
