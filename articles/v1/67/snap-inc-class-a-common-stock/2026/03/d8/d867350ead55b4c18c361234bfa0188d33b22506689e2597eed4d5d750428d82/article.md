# Introducing AI Clips in Lens Studio

Today, we are introducing AI Clips in Lens Studio, a new AI-powered Lens format that transforms a single photo into a five-second video using a closed prompt system. AI Clips are available to Snapchat Lens+ subscribers.


While much of the AI video landscape today feels distant or technical, AI Clips make AI video instant, personal, and shareable. For the first time, developers can build and publish photo-to-video AI directly to Snapchat from the GenAI Suite in Lens Studio.


At their core, AI Clips are Snapchat Lenses that transform a Snap or Camera Roll photo into a five-second AI video. The magic lies in the closed-prompt design:


-


**Developer Control:** Creators define the creative direction and prompt


-


**Effortless for Snapchatters:** You simply tap to step into the experience


-


**Cinematic Results:** A single photo can place someone on a red carpet or in a surreal moment, creating storytelling that is personal and share-ready


What makes AI Clips stand out in the broader AI video landscape is what they don’t try to be. They aren’t open-ended, text-to-video tools or experimental demos. They are closed-prompt AI experiences designed for scale, creativity, and repeat engagement. There’s currently nothing else on the market that combines closed-prompt AI video generation with direct photo input, real distribution, and monetization.


With Lens+ subscribers actively looking for premium, immersive experiences, AI Clips aren’t just fun experiments, they’re a chance for developers to reach a tuned in audience with a single, well-crafted idea. And for developers[enrolled in Lens+ Payouts](https://developers.snap.com/lens-studio/monetization/lens-creator-rewards/lens-plus-payouts?lang=en-US) , this opens the door to earning directly from the experiences they design.


The feature is native to the GenAI Suite in Lens Studio, allowing both experienced and new developers to turn a single prompt into a published Lens in minutes with no external tools or complex workflows required.


AI Clips marks a significant shift in how we connect with AI videos, and we can’t wait to see the creativity of developers bring your photos to life in entirely new ways.
