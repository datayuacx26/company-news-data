# Introducing the Winners of the 2026 Snappy Awards Show

Snapchat’s first-ever[Snappys Awards Show](https://creators.snapchat.com/the-snappys?lang=en-US) brought together creators, industry leaders, and special guests to celebrate the Snap Stars shaping culture and community on Snapchat. The inaugural event recognized 22 standout creators for their creative excellence, cultural impact, and ability to connect with audiences in authentic and meaningful ways.


As part of the night, Snapchat announced that[Creator Subscriptions](https://newsroom.snap.com/snapchat-launches-creator-subscriptions) are now available to 100% of eligible Snapchatters–marking a major step in helping creators further build sustainable businesses and deepen relationships with their fans.


The Snappys build on Snapchat’s continued investment in creators–from new monetization opportunities to expanded distribution through Spotlight–making it easier than ever for creators to grow their audiences and find success on Snapchat, and the momentum speaks for itself:


-


More and more creators are using Spotlight to grow their audience. Creators posting to Spotlight daily grew by **33%** YoY in Q4'25. 1


-


We're seeing even faster growth in posters among verified Snap Stars. In Q4'25, we had **60%** more Snap Stars posting to Spotlight each day than we had the year prior. 2


-


Creators who post daily to Spotlight grow their followers more than **10x faster** than those who don’t post to Spotlight. 3


The night brought together a mix of top Snap Stars and cultural figures, including host Matt Friend, Harry Jowsey, Candiace Dillard Bassett, Jeremiah Brown, Nic Vans, David Dobrik, Dixie D’Amelio, Francesca Farago, JoJo Siwa, Skai Jackson, and more. Creators like[Ashton Hall](https://www.snapchat.com/@ashtonhallofc?lang=en-US) ,[Ella Moncrief](https://www.snapchat.com/@itsellamoncrief?lang=en-US) , and[Adam W](https://www.snapchat.com/@adamw?lang=en-US) –each recognized as winners at the Snappys Awards Show– continue to see rapid growth on Snapchat, reflecting the platform’s expanding opportunity for emerging and established talent alike:


-


[Ashton Hall](https://www.snapchat.com/@ashtonhallofc?lang=en-US) has seen remarkable growth over the past year, expanding his following from around 500 to over **850K** . In the last three months alone, he has averaged **47M** views on Stories and **5M** on Spotlight.


-


[Ella Moncrief](https://www.snapchat.com/@itsellamoncrief?lang=en-US) experienced breakout growth, scaling her audience from 1K to nearly **1M** followers within a single year.


-


[Adam W](https://www.snapchat.com/@adamw?lang=en-US) reached a total of **64M** Spotlight views over the last 12 months.

Snap Star[Ella Moncrief](https://www.snapchat.com/@itsellamoncrief?lang=en-US) and One to Watch award winner
