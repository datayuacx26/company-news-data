# Step into London's Legendary Blitz Club with Snap AR

Snapchat's AR Studio Paris has partnered with the Design Museum for the launch of the museum's latest exhibition on the history of the London night club Blitz where the culture of the 1980s began.


London’s 1980s club scene was a revolution in style, and the Blitz Club stood at its center. As part of the museum’s exhibition, visitors can unlock an immersive AR experience that transports them to a Tuesday night at the club. The entrance of the Design Museum will recreate the famous door policy of the club - where only the most creative, stylish, and flamboyant looks were allowed entry.


Visitors will be able to use Snap's technology to virtually try on daring high-fashion looks once worn by the Blitz Kids — a generation that redefined fashion, music, art, and culture.


The spirit of the Blitz lives on, giving today’s audiences a chance to step into one of London’s most influential cultural moments.


The exhibition opens from 20 September 2025 - 29 March 2026.


“


We're kicking off September fashion month in style - working again with our friends at the Design Museum to bring to life one of the most iconic moments for fashion and culture in the capital.


We're excited to throw it back to a Tuesday night in the 80s through Snapchat's technology, right in the heart of central London at the legendary club, Blitz. From virtually trying on high fashion that inspired global superstars, to theatrical makeup and daring punk looks from this era - we can't wait to see visitors to the Blitz exhibition walk through the exclusive club's doors and immerse themselves in the era of the ultimate trendsetters.


Ben Wymer, Senior Director Global Brand Experience and AR Studio Paris
