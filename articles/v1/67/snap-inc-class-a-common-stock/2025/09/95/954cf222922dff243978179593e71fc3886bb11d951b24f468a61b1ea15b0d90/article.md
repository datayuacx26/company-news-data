# Say it with a Sticker, Anywhere

Snapchatters love using stickers to enhance communication with their friends, whether they’re simply saying hello to the group chat or cheering up their BFF. Today, we’re introducing new ways for our community to use their favorite Snapchat stickers, wherever they’re chatting.


**iMessage App + Snapchat Keyboard**
Snapchat stickers are now available outside the app. Our new iMessage App and Snapchat Keyboard for iOS & Android bring Snap's sticker library to your fingertips for more fun chats. To get started, simply head to your settings on Snapchat.


**Customoji**
Bitmoji stickers are beloved by our community and have been shared over 210 billion times. Customoji auto-creates stickers with any given phrase to personalize Snapchatters’ communication even more. Search for a phrase in the library, and pick your favorite from a selection of Bitmoji stickers.


Happy chatting!
