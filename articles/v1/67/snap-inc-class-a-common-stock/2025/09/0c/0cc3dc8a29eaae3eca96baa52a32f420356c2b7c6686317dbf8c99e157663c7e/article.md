# Snapchat Fuels Creator Momentum in India With 4X Growth in Spotlight Posting and Doubling of Content Engagement

Snapchat today announced strong momentum in India’s creator ecosystem, driven by surging content engagement, rapid growth in new creations, and a rising community of Snap Stars the platform’s premier creators. Key growth highlights in India include:


-


**Engagement Growth:** Total time spent watching content on Snapchat has **doubled in India** 1


over the past two years, reflecting the platform’s deep resonance with young audiences.


****


-


**Top Creator Growth:** The number of official Snap Stars in India has increased by **1.5X in the past two years** , 2


highlighting rising demand for authentic creator-led content.


-


**Content Posting:** Content posted to Spotlight from creators and publishers has seen more than **4X year-over-year growth** , 3


cementing Snapchat’s role as a hub for creativity and storytelling.


The announcement comes alongside *Snapchat Creator Connect* , a multi-city initiative empowering Gen Z storytellers. With North India creators being so critical to India’s thriving creator ecosystem, Delhi was the latest stop reflecting strong adoption in the North and Snapchat’s growing footprint across India.


“


India has been at the center of our global creator strategy not just as one of our fastest-growing markets, but also as one of the biggest contributors to global content creation on Snapchat. These milestones are the result of deliberate, sustained efforts to build an inclusive and thriving creator ecosystem. That’s why we’ve invested in initiatives like ‘Snapchat Creator Connect,’ reaching creators beyond metros and into the heart of India, ensuring that diverse talent has access to opportunities and tools to grow.


Programs such as 'Snap School' provide mentorship and education for emerging voices, while ‘Snap With Stars’ enables meaningful collaborations with our most influential Snap Stars. Together, these efforts have created a flywheel of creativity, authenticity, and growth. The fact that we’ve seen content engagement double, 1.5X growth in Snap Stars, and 4X growth in Spotlight content reflects how strongly Indian creators have embraced Snapchat as their home for storytelling and community building. We’re committed to building on this momentum and continuing to empower the next wave of Indian creators to thrive on our platform.


Saket Jha Saurabh, Director and Head - Content & AR Partnerships, Snap Inc.


**Creator Connect @Delhi**


As part of its ongoing commitment to India, Snapchat hosted **Creator Connect in Delhi** on September 26th, bringing together leading creators, Snap Stars, and industry stakeholders. The event featured **Snap School** , an education and mentorship program designed to help creators refine storytelling skills and maximize growth on the platform. Sessions also highlighted monetization opportunities through **Snap’s global revenue share and rewards programs** , as well as AR innovations that enable creators to build deeper connections with their communities.


The event also witnessed Bollywood and Punjabi cinema’s most celebrated actors Ammy Virk and Sonam Bajwa in a fireside chat where they spoke candidly about life beyond work, their upcoming film, and why Snapchat is their platform of choice to share everyday moments with fans.


The evening also saw a strong interest in the demo of the fifth-generation Spectacles, with an electric response from the audience. From real-time Lenses to Spectator Mode, the see-through AR glasses impressed the crowd with their seamless mobile integration.

**Young India on Snapchat: Athletes & Artists Leading a New Wave of Storytelling**


Snapchat is fast becoming the platform of choice for Young India, where culture, sports, and entertainment converge. Rising cricketers like Abhishek Sharma, Arshdeep Singh, Washington Sundar, and Rishabh Pant are sharing everyday moments that bring fans closer to their authentic selves. Olympic gold medalist Neeraj Chopra has also partnered with Snapchat, using the platform’s storytelling tools to inspire millions to embrace sports, fitness, and culture with pride.


Beyond sports, some of India’s most-loved entertainers including Diljit Dosanjh, Karan Aujla, Guru Randhawa, and Rashmika Mandanna are engaging fans with humor, behind-the-scenes glimpses, and personal stories.


India is already one of Snapchat’s fastest-growing and most dynamic creator markets worldwide. By continuing to fuel authentic storytelling, investing in emerging voices, and delivering cutting-edge tools like AR and Spectacles, Snapchat is building a future where creators from every corner of the country can thrive. The momentum seen today is only the beginning, and Snapchat is committed to scaling this success to make India a true global hub for creator-led storytelling.
