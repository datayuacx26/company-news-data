# The Snapchat Generation Report: Where Culture Gets Real

At Snapchat, we don’t just reach the next generation – we understand them. Our audience of over 900 million monthly active users is one of the world’s most engaged, expressive, and influential communities. Made up of Gen Z and Millennials, the Snapchat Generation is where real relationships, self-expression, and cultural influence converge. From how they communicate to how they shop, Snapchatters are shaping the future, and we’re here to decode the insights that matter most.


Today, we’re releasing our **Snapchat Generation Report** , an inside look at what’s driving this generation on Snapchat, and how brands can show up authentically in their lives. Here’s what we’re seeing:

The Snapchat Generation is narrating, confessing, and yapping their way into deeper bonds. With voice notes and private podcast-worthy conversations – it’s not for clout, it’s for closeness.
