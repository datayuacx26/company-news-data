# Snap Map Grows to over 400 Million Monthly Active Users!

We’re excited to share that Snap Map has over **400 million monthly active users** !


Snap Map is unique in that it enables Snapchatters to stay connected to their world, whether they’re using it to meet up with friends they see nearby or to discover new places and experiences around them. Unlike other digital maps, our Map is about more than getting directions from Point A to Point B, it enables Snapchatters to stay connected, in real time, to their physical world. Every month more than 400 million Snapchatters open the Map to make plans, explore local hotspots and stay informed about what’s happening around them.


And we’ve built new ways for brands to be part of the Snap Map journey too. With Snapchat’s **Promoted Places** , businesses can show up where Snapchatters are already exploring — making it easier than ever to drive IRL visits, discover new customers, or just show up in the moments that matter.


As our community continues to grow, we’re even more excited about Snap Map and the way it has helped Snapchatters gather with friends in person, find new places they love, or maybe just feel a little more connected.
