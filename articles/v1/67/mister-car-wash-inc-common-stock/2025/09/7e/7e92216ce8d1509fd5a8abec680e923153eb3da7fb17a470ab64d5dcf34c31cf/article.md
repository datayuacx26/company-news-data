# Finding Calm in the Commute

For many of us, the daily commute is more than just getting from point A to point B—it’s where our days begin and end. Studies show that the average commuter spends over 200 hours a year in their car, equivalent to more than a month behind the wheel. That makes your car not just a vehicle, but a living space that plays a significant role in shaping your mindset for the day ahead and how you wind down at day’s end.


While traffic, red lights, and delays are out of our control, one thing we can control is the environment inside our car. A clean, clutter-free space helps reduce stress and provides a sense of calm. Stepping into a tidy car each morning can boost your confidence and set the tone for a successful day. And after a long day, a clean space gives you the chance to reset, clear your head, and head home with less tension.

## **Tips for a Calmer Commute**


- **Declutter daily** : Remove trash, coffee cups, and extra items at the end of each day. A fresh start every morning makes a difference.
- **Keep essentials organized** : Use small compartments or organizers for items you need—sunglasses, chargers, or work badges, so they don’t create chaos.
- **Make it part of your routine** : Just like you might schedule time to exercise or prepare a meal, plan a[weekly car wash](https://mistercarwash.com/) to keep your space consistently clean. Washing your car weekly isn’t just good for your paint—as the[AAA notes](https://cluballiance.aaa.com/the-extra-mile/advice/car/tips-for-keeping-your-vehicle-spotless-inside-and-out) , regular washing is essential to keeping your car in top-notch shape—making a wash not just cosmetic, but a good investment.
- **Add small comforts** : A favorite playlist, a subtle air freshener, or a clutter-free dashboard can transform your drive into a calm, confidence-boosting experience.
