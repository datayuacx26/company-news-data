# SuperWize and ABHI Partner to Bring Sovereign AI to Pakistan’s Fintech Sector

*ABHI will adopt SuperWize’s sovereign AI models, hosted and processed in Pakistan, to power AI across its financial services for companies and employees nationwide.*


**Islamabad, Pakistan, 6th August 2026:** SuperWize, Pakistan’s sovereign AI company, and ABHI (Private) Limited, the country’s leading embedded finance platform, today announced a partnership which ABHI will use SuperWize’s sovereign AI models and the two companies will work together to implement AI across ABHI’s operations in Pakistan.


With this partnership, ABHI will build its AI initiatives on SuperWize’s sovereign models, hosted and processed entirely in-country, supporting compliance with Pakistani data protection and regulatory requirements. The companies will jointly identify and deploy AI use cases across ABHI’s business, spanning customer support and conversational interfaces in English and Urdu, credit and risk workflows, and payroll and back-office automation.


ABHI serves thousands of companies and hundreds of thousands of employees across Pakistan with earned wage access, payroll, business financing, and payment solutions. The partnership is designed to bring the benefits of advanced AI to that user base while keeping data within Pakistan’s borders.


“Financial services touch every working Pakistani, which makes them exactly where sovereign AI matters most,” said Muhammad Imran Sargaana, COO of SuperWize. “ABHI has built one of the most important fintech platforms in the country. By running our models in-country, ABHI gets frontier AI capability with full data sovereignty, and its customers get smarter, faster, more accessible financial services.”


“Our mission has always been to make financial services instant, simple, and accessible for businesses and employees across Pakistan,” said Omair Ansari, Co-Founder and CEO of ABHI. “Partnering with SuperWize lets us embed AI across everything we do, from customer conversations in Urdu and English to credit decisions and payroll, while keeping our customers’ data secure and inside Pakistan. It is a natural next step for us as we build the neobank of the future.”
