# 5 Things You Can Learn from the Push Notifications by ProductHunt

ProductHunt, a community-driven platform for discovering new products, has successfully integrated push notifications into its mobile app strategy to engage users and drive engagement. In this article, we will explore several examples of ProductHunt's push notifications and examine their impact on user engagement and revenue generation.


‍


### 1. 🧡 Your comment is getting some love. \[Username\] upvoted your comment.


This push notification template is a personalized message that notifies users when their comment has been upvoted. It is a simple and effective way to encourage users to engage with the platform and create a sense of community.


### 2. 🧑‍⚖️ You be the judge. Choose your favorite products from the past week.


This push notification template is a call-to-action that encourages users to engage with the platform by voting for their favorite products. It is a creative way to drive engagement and create a sense of ownership among users.


### 3. 🫵 You decide Product of the Day. It's that time. Choose the best products from the previous week.


This push notification template is a call-to-action that encourages users to engage with the platform by choosing the best products from the previous week. It is a creative way to drive engagement and create a sense of ownership among users.

In conclusion, ProductHunt has successfully utilized push notifications to engage users and drive engagement.
