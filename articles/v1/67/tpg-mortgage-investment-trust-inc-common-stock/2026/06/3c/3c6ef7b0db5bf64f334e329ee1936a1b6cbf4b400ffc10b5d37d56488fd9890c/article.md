# TPG Mortgage Investment Trust, Inc. Announces Second Quarter 2026 Common Dividend of $0.24 per Share

## TPG Mortgage Investment Trust, Inc. Announces Second Quarter 2026 Common Dividend of $0.24 per Share


June 16, 2026 at 4:05 PM EDT


[PDF Version](https://www.mitt.tpg.com/node/14216/pdf)


NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jun. 16, 2026-- TPG Mortgage Investment Trust, Inc. (NYSE: MITT) (the “Company”) announced today that its Board of Directors declared a dividend of $0.24 per common share for the second quarter 2026. The dividend is payable on July 31, 2026, to shareholders of record at the close of business on June 30, 2026.


**About TPG Mortgage Investment Trust, Inc.**


TPG Mortgage Investment Trust, Inc. is a residential mortgage REIT with a focus on investing in a diversified risk-adjusted portfolio of residential mortgage-related assets in the U.S. mortgage market. The Company is externally managed and advised by AG REIT Management, LLC, an affiliate of TPG Inc. (NASDAQ: TPG).
