# eGain to Announce Fiscal 2026 Third Quarter Financial Results on May 14, 2026

**Sunnyvale, California (May 06, 2026)** : eGain (NASDAQ:[EGAN](https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/stocks/egan) ), the leader in AI-powered knowledge management for enterprise customer service, will announce its fiscal 2026 third quarter financial results after the close of regular market trading on Thursday, May 14, 2026, followed by an investor conference call and webcast at 2:00 p.m. Pacific Time (5:00 p.m. ET). Chief Executive Officer Ashu Roy and Chief Financial Officer Eric Smit will host the call and webcast.


- **When** : Thursday, May 14th at 2:00 p.m. Pacific Time (5:00 p.m. ET).
- **Webcast** : A live and archived webcast of the conference call can be accessed from the investors section of eGain’s website at[www.egain.com](https://www.egain.com/) .
- **Dial In** : To access the live call, dial 844-481-2704 (U.S. toll free) or +1 412-317-0660 (International) and ask to join the eGain earnings call.
- **Replay** : A phone replay of the conference call will be available starting two hours after the call and remain in effect for one week. To access the phone replay, dial 855-669-9658 (U.S. toll free) or +1 412-317-0088 (International). The replay access code is 8344740.
