# eGain to Participate in the Oppenheimer Emerging Growth Conference on February 4, 2026

**Sunnyvale, California (January 28, 2026)** : eGain (NASDAQ:[EGAN](https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/stocks/egan) ), a leading provider of AI-powered knowledge management and customer experience solutions, today announced that CEO Ashu Roy and CFO Eric Smit will be hosting virtual one-on-one meetings with institutional investors at the Oppenheimer 8th Annual Emerging Growth Conference on Wednesday, February 4, 2026.


For additional information or to schedule a meeting with eGain management, please contact either your Oppenheimer representative or Pondel Wilkinson, eGain’s investor relations firm, at[\[email protected\]](https://www.egain.com/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#730718161b011f1a33031c1d17161f5d101c1e) .

eGain is a leading provider of AI-powered knowledge management and customer experience automation solutions.
