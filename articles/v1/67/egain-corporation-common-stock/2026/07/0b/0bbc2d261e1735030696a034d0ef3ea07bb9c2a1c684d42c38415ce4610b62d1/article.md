# eGain to Present at the Oppenheimer 29th Annual Technology, Internet and Communications Conference on August 11, 2026 - eGain

**Sunnyvale, CA (July 30, 2026)** : eGain (NASDAQ:[EGAN](https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/stocks/egan) ), a leader in AI-powered knowledge management for customer service, today announced that management is scheduled to present at the Oppenheimer Virtual 29th Annual Technology, Internet & Communications Conference on Tuesday, August 11, 2026 at 10:15 am PT, with virtual one-on-one meetings with investors held throughout the conference.


You may register to access a live or replay video webcast of eGain’s presentation via a link posted to the investor relations section of eGain’s website at[https://www.egain.com/company/investors/](https://www.egain.com/company/investors/) or by clicking[here](https://event.summitcast.com/view/2na8Ek6QZtsJoeqs2JckGv/UNnAaEhAqjsWPtBexWHNCL) .


For additional information or to schedule a meeting with eGain management, please contact either your Oppenheimer representative, or eGain’s investor relations firm, PondelWilkinson, Inc., at[\[email protected\]](https://www.egain.com/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#a3d7c8c6cbd1cfcae3d3cccdc7c6cf8dc0ccce) .
