# Pinnacle Food Group Limited Announces Investor Day 2026 and Non-Deal Roadshow

VANCOUVER, BC, March 2, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Pinnacle Food Group Limited (Nasdaq:[PFAI](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pinnacle-food-group-limited-announces-investor-day-2026-and-non-deal-roadshow-302700272.html#financial-modal) ), a technology-driven smart farming and bio-engineering company, providing integrated farming-as-a-service solutions and developing biology-enabled platforms to support efficient and sustainable food systems, today announced that it will host its virtual "Investor Day 2026 & Non-Deal Roadshow" on Monday, March 9, 2026 (U.S. Time) / Tuesday, March 10, 2026 (Asia Time).


**Event Schedule**
