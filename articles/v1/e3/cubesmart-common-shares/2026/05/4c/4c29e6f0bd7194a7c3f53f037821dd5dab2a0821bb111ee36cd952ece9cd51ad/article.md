As one of the world’s most famous entertainment capitals, Las Vegas boasts an iconic lineup of immersive attractions, performances, cultural institutions, and nightlife hotspots along its flashy neon Strip. But the food scene is where this city truly shines. Las Vegas restaurants run the gamut from all-you-can-eat buffets featuring diverse global cuisine to extravagant steakhouses owned by some of the most high-profile celebrity chefs. So if moving to Las Vegas is on your radar, let’s explore which culinary destinations live up to the hype.


## 12 Best Restaurants in Las Vegas


Some of the most[unique things to do in Las Vegas](https://www.cubesmart.com/blog/city-guides/unique-things-to-do-in-las-vegas/) center on food. Want a lavish fine dining experience to mark a special occasion? On the hunt for a memorable meal that won’t break the budget? How about a live cooking station with international flair? No matter what you’re craving, these are the best restaurants in Las Vegas to satisfy every palate.


### Best Fine Dining in Las Vegas


From the most romantic restaurants in Las Vegas to chic, swanky establishments helmed by Michelin-starred chefs, the Las Vegas fine dining scene exists in a class of its own. Here’s where to make reservations for a luxurious night out on the town.


#### 1. Stubborn Seed – Luxury Meets Creativity


- Price Range: $$$$
- Michelin-star chef
- Known For: A multi-course tasting menu that celebrates seasonal flavors
- Website:[stubbornseed.com](http://stubbornseed.com/)


There are no Michelin-starred restaurants in Las Vegas, but Stubborn Seed is about as close as it gets. Chef Jeremy Ford earned a Michelin star for his Miami restaurant, and now he’s bringing his recipe for success to the Strip.


While still fairly new, this fine dining concept stands out among many other famous restaurants in Las Vegas. The ambiance feels moody without coming off as stuffy. Plus, whether you order à la carte or opt for the curated tasting menu, Stubborn Seed pairs creative techniques, eclectic flavors, and seasonal ingredients. Try the barramundi with butter-poached fennel, then round it out with an effervescent Shiso Highball.


#### 2. Partage – Modern European Refinement


- Price Range: $$$$
- James Beard–nominated chef
- Known For: Regional French delicacies with expertly-selected wine pairings
- Website:[partage.vegas](https://partage.vegas/)


James Beard–nominated Chef Yuri Szarzewski turns up the volume on fine dining in Las Vegas with the distinct French sensibilities of Partage. This restaurant features a nine-course tasting menu and a surprise monthly rotation of dishes that ensures no two experiences at Partage are ever alike.


The elegant, intimate atmosphere also makes it one of the most romantic Las Vegas restaurants, perfect for date night. Book a seat at the exclusive Chef’s Table to see the kitchen action up close, or unwind at the high-top bar for a more relaxed (but equally refined) setting. Be sure to peruse the wine list too, which features bottles sourced from regions all across France.


### Best Restaurants in Las Vegas on a Budget


Free image via[Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/bowls-of-delicious-ramen-24838465/)


These Vegas eateries prove that an excellent meal doesn’t have to come at a steep cost. Whether it’s a trendy lunch spot or a cozy local haunt, here are the best places to eat in Las Vegas on a budget.


#### 3. Arts District Kitchen – Elevated Pub Classics


- Price Range: $
- Known For: Comforting bar food with an aromatic Southeast Asian flair
- Website:[artsdistrictkitchen.com](https://artsdistrictkitchen.com/)


Step inside Arts District Kitchen and feel instantly at home in this neighborhood joint that’s both unassuming and effortlessly charming. The industrial-chic decor creates a slick urban vibe, while the gastropub fare makes a subtle nod to Southeast Asia.


From the hoisin-glazed pork belly tacos to the sweet chili and cauliflower bánh mì sandwich, Arts District Kitchen serves up unique, unexpected bites that beg to be savored with a local craft beer. An affordable happy hour menu also cements Arts District Kitchen as one of the best restaurants in Las Vegas on a budget. Think: housemade onion rings, pretzels, or hot fries for only $6.00.


#### 4. Monta Ramen – Japanese Noodle House


- Price Range: $
- Known For: Authentic Kurume-style ramen in a slurpable homemade broth
- Website:[montaramen.com](https://www.montaramen.com/)


Nestled in a corner of Chinatown Plaza, you’ll stumble on Manta Ramen, one of the best Asian restaurants in Las Vegas. This no-frills noodle house is known for steaming bowls of authentic Kurume-style ramen, a regional Japanese specialty that features slow-simmered pork bone broth with deep layers of umami and thin, springy noodles. Plus, all the menu items are under $20.00, so you can fill up on generous portions at an unbeatable price.


Order the Deluxe Tonkatsu ramen with a jammy egg, bamboo shoots, crunchy nori, and black garlic oil on top. Then, wash it down with a refreshing yuzu soda or Japanese rice lager.


### Best Downtown Las Vegas Restaurants


Venture a few miles north of the Strip, and you’ll hit Downtown Las Vegas. Known for its historic casinos, artsy vintage shops, unique museums, and open-air entertainment, this buzzy urban core also boasts an impressive culinary scene. These Downtown Las Vegas restaurants are the perfect first stop before a live concert on Fremont Street.


#### 5. La Mona Rosa – Authentic Mexican Hideaway


- Price Range: $$
- Known For: The robust flavors and colorful design aesthetic of Mexico City
- Website:[lamonarosalv.com](http://lamonarosalv.com/)


The lively culture of Mexico mingles with the celebratory atmosphere of Downtown at La Mona Rosa. This hidden gem on East Fremont Street welcomes you with an artful, colorful ambiance that could have been plucked from a Mexican mercado. The cuisine is just as vibrant, thanks to entrées such as the chef’s signature Shrimp al Mojo, sautéed in a garlic-poblano sauce and offset by the floral notes of a mezcal hibiscus margarita.


La Mona Rosa also ranks among the best Downtown Las Vegas restaurants for weekend brunch, where you can nosh on tres leches pancakes or churro French toast alongside bottomless mimosas.


#### 6. Carson Kitchen – Reimagined Comfort Food


- Price Range: $$
- Known For: A communal dining experience set to dramatic rooftop views
- Website:[carsonkitchen.com](http://carsonkitchen.com/)


Another staple of the Downtown Las Vegas restaurant scene, Carson Kitchen transforms your favorite comfort foods into uniquely modern, experimental delicacies. Think: gyro tacos with feta cheese crema, wild mushroom flatbread with miso chili crunch, and cocoa-espresso New York strip with red wine demi-glace.


Tuck into your meal around a communal table, snag a seat by the open kitchen, or dine al fresco on the rooftop lounge. Whatever atmosphere you choose, don’t sleep on Carson Kitchen’s wine and cocktail program. The festive, rum-forward sangria with seasonal fruit and black cherry purée is calling your name.


### Most Fun Restaurants in Las Vegas for Families


Free image via[Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-boy-making-funny-face-while-sitting-at-the-table-8209988/)


Foodies of all ages can enjoy a spectacular meal in Las Vegas. From unfussy, casual bistros to quirky themed eateries with outrageous menu options, these kid-friendly restaurants in Las Vegas score points with adults, as well.


#### 7. Black Tap – Craft Burgers and Shakes


- Price Range: $$
- Known For: Playful, over-the-top (in the best way) diner-style favorites
- Website:[blacktap.com](https://blacktap.com/)


Located inside The Venetian Resort, Black Tap dishes up an imaginative yet approachable twist on diner classics, making it one of the best family restaurants in Las Vegas. Kids will love the classic all-American burger, while adults can sink their teeth into an elevated black truffle burger with Havarti cheese, wagyu beef, and truffle-yuzu vinaigrette.


The real showstoppers of this menu are the CrazyShakes, featuring whimsical flavors and texture combinations everyone will want to save room for. Try the Vegas Golden Knights Shake with a vanilla frosted rim, chocolate cake, caramel ice cream, and sprinkles in the NHL hockey team colors.


#### 8. Nacho Daddy – Gourmet Spin on Tex-Mex


- Price Range: $$
- Known For: Nacho platters featuring unique, eclectic flavor combinations
- Website:[nachodaddy.com](https://nachodaddy.com/)


Families on the lookout for the most fun restaurants in Las Vegas should make a beeline for Nacho Daddy. There’s nothing ordinary about this avant-garde interpretation of Tex-Mex street food, where the menu leans delightfully offbeat. The ambiance also reflects this exuberance through bold wall murals, upbeat music, and local sports games broadcast at the bar.


True to its name, however, nachos take center stage around here. The Surf and Turf nacho platter is an absolute must-order, with large shareable portions to feed your whole table. Family members 21 and over will appreciate the extensive handcrafted margarita selection as well.


### Best New Restaurants in Las Vegas


Las Vegas is a city of constant motion and evolution, with new attractions and events continually emerging alongside world-famous ones like Cirque du Soleil and the Formula 1 Grand Prix. You can also see that same sense of innovation reflected in these new Las Vegas restaurants.


#### 9. Carbone Riviera – Coastal Italian Influence


- Price Range: $$$
- Known For: Sumptuous surf and turf entrées inspired by the Italian Riviera
- Website:[carboneriviera.com](https://carboneriviera.com/)


After debuting on the Strip in November 2025, Carbone Riviera became an instant classic for its elegant Mediterranean aesthetic, luxurious seafood entrées, and glamorous location inside the iconic Bellagio Hotel.


The warm terracotta and serene teal decor complement a soft, candlelit glow that makes Carbone Riviera one of the most romantic restaurants in Las Vegas. It’s the kind of establishment where couples linger and conversations lengthen over an exceptionally curated meal. Opt for Chef Mario Carbone’s famous lobster fettuccine, paired with a glass of citrusy, Italian-imported Pinot Grigio or an herbaceous House Negroni.


#### 10. Gymkhana – Tandoori-Grilled Specialities


- Price Range: $$$
- Known For: An elegant, upscale take on traditional South Asian street food
- Website:[gymkhanarestaurants.com](https://gymkhanarestaurants.com/)


It’s only been open since December 2025, but Gymkhana has wasted no time creating a deeply immersive experience that whisks your taste buds off to South Asia. The menu focuses on authentic tandoori-style cooking in which layers of spices and aromatics draw inspiration from culinary traditions across India. Meanwhile, the interior feels like an old-school club with rich wood panels, ornate chandeliers, and plush leather accents.


Gymkhana is also one of the best vegetarian restaurants in Las Vegas, thanks to its plant-based tasting menu. The creamy Black Lentil Dal Maharani is the perfect match for a crisp lychee martini.


### Coolest Restaurants in Las Vegas


Free image via[Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/woman-drinking-wine-761854/)


Many top restaurants in Las Vegas are at the forefront of unique gastronomic trends, vibrant global fusion concepts, and effortlessly sleek design. For a hip ambiance with an irresistible menu to match, here are the coolest restaurants in Las Vegas.


#### 11. Peppermill – Groovy Throwback Vibes


- Price Range: $$
- Known For: Round-the-clock brunch offerings and nostalgic neon decor
- Website:[peppermilllasvegas.com](http://peppermilllasvegas.com/)


For over 50 years, The Peppermill and Fireside Lounge has been a funky time capsule of Old Vegas. This historic institution makes vintage feel cool with round velvet booths, mirrored walls, neon lights, and colorful Tiffany lampshades. Plus, the kitchen is humming at all hours with a menu that runs the gamut from 24-hour breakfasts to shareable appetizers to classic after-dark nightcaps. Whether you indulge in a luscious crab cake Benedict for brunch or sip on a retro White Russian after dinner, The Peppermill lives up to its legendary status.


#### 12. Sparrow + Wolf – Live-Fire Technique


- Price Range: $$$
- Known For: Local, sustainable ingredients cooked over an open flame
- Website:[sparrowandwolflv.com](https://sparrowandwolflv.com/)


There’s something rustic yet polished about Sparrow + Wolf, an intimate neighborhood cookery, where seasonal entrées are intentionally made with local, sustainable ingredients. But don’t let this humble concept fool you. Sparrow + Wolf dishes up works of art on each plate, blending creative presentations and eclectic flavor combinations.


Much of the menu features live-fire cooking in a hearth oven to imbue each meal with a smoky depth. Order the Lumina lamb over braised white beans with a mezcal-forward cocktail to enhance the flame-kissed cuisine.

## Las Vegas Restaurants FAQs


### What Are the Famous Restaurants in Vegas?


Listing out all the famous restaurants in Las Vegas feels like trying to narrow down the[best neighborhoods in Las Vegas](https://www.cubesmart.com/blog/city-guides/best-neighborhoods-in-las-vegas/) : there are just so many to choose from. But a couple stand out as time-honored institutions that put Las Vegas on the map as a true culinary destination.[Golden Steer](https://goldensteer.com/) , this city’s oldest steakhouse, was a favorite watering hole of Elvis Presley, Dean Martin, Frank Sinatra, and Sammy Davis Jr. Meanwhile, the[Peppermill](https://www.peppermilllasvegas.com/) is beloved for its hearty brunches, late-night cocktails, and fun retro diner vibes.


### What’s the Most Popular Food in Las Vegas?


The Las Vegas food scene has an affinity for upscale, indulgent cuisine, and nowhere is this better reflected than in a juicy cut of prime rib. You can order this classic meal 24 hours a day at hotels, casinos, and steakhouses throughout the city. It’s often served with a fluffy twice-baked potato or even a succulent lobster tail on the side.


### What Is the Hardest Restaurant to Get Into in Vegas?


Since opening its doors in late 2025,[Carbone Riviera](https://carboneriviera.com/) has quickly cemented itself as one of the most exclusive Las Vegas restaurants and one of the hardest reservations to secure. Located in the Bellagio Hotel, Carbone Riviera offers an artful interpretation of coastal Italian fare with an emphasis on fresh seafood. The ambiance takes inspiration from Lake Como, while treating diners to a view of the iconic Bellagio Fountains.
