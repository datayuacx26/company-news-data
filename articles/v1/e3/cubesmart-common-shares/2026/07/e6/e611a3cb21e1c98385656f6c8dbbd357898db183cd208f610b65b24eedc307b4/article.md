## How to Prepare Your Storage Unit


Treat every organizational task as if you’re starting from scratch. You may well have worked on organizing things in the past, but the best approach is to get everything out and start again, or you risk only doing half a job, and that won’t cut it when it comes to effective long-term storage.


Here are some of our top storage unit organization tips before you embark on your journey of organization.


### Check Your Storage Unit


- First, take out everything currently in the unit and stack it separately. This may feel like a mammoth task, but it can help make things easier down the line. It can also be a good idea at this point to look at everything you’ve got and decide on anything you want to throw away. Be honest. If you haven’t used something for years, ask yourself if you still need it.
- Once everything is removed, take a few minutes to refresh the space to ensure your storage unit stays well-maintained and ready for continued use. This also makes it easier to plan and organize the layout when you move items back in.


### Take Inventory


- Having inventory of everything in your storage unit is an important starting point. List out every item that’s in your storage space and whether it’s a regularly used item that comes out of storage every so often or something that’s going to be there long-term.
- Having this list and knowing what’s in your unit, as well as the purpose of these items, will make a world of difference down the line.


### How to Organize a Storage Unit


If you’ve ever just felt better walking into a clean room, you’ll understand the feeling of walking into an organized storage unit. Here are some benefits and other reasons to enjoy cleaning up your unit.


### Benefits of a Well-Organized Storage Unit


Why should you bother with storage unit organization in the first place? A well-organized space not only saves you time but also maximizes every square inch of your unit. Imagine locating your holiday decorations in minutes instead of digging in boxes for an hour. Sounds great, right?


An organized storage unit minimizes stress. Knowing your items are logically stowed away and easy to access can lift a weight off your shoulders. Plus, when things are orderly, you’re less likely to misplace items.


Having your storage organized also helps you maintain an updated inventory. You’ll know exactly what you own, avoiding the hassle of buying duplicates. This not only saves money but also keeps your living spaces clutter-free.


## How to Efficiently Pack Your Storage Unit


Image via[Adobe Stock](https://as1.ftcdn.net/v2/jpg/04/20/31/48/1000_F_420314895_Eu9FSsYr8rGdlKlsIo62WrewBfoSlBOp.jpg)


Packing your storage unit effectively is crucial for maximizing space and ensuring that your belongings remain intact. Here are some helpful packing tips for efficient storage organization:


### Sort and Categorize Your Items


Before you start packing, take the time to sort through your items. Group related items together: this could be by category (holiday decorations, seasonal clothing, etc.) or by usage frequency. This will make it easier to find things later.


### Use Sturdy Boxes to Avoid Breakage


Image via[Adobe Stock](https://t3.ftcdn.net/jpg/05/25/46/22/240_F_525462275_w5u872JSyqL51GVxl6qh8pXw2E3f2TJa.jpg)


Invest in the[best storage bins and containers](https://www.cnn.com/cnn-underscored/home/best-storage-bins-containers) that professional organizers swear by and that can withstand stacking. Use high-quality storage tubs or thicker cardboard boxes that will soundly hold small and larger items. It’s also a good idea to label each box clearly to indicate its contents, making retrieval a breeze.


### Pack Wisely to Maximize Space


When filling boxes, utilize the space wisely. Fill empty spaces with padding material like bubble wrap or packing paper to prevent items from shifting during transport and storage. Consider packing heavy items at the bottom and lighter ones on top to maintain stability. Purchase reusable boxes with dividers for fragile items like glasses. Most CubeSmart facilities carry[packing and moving supplies](https://www.cubesmart.com/deals/packing-moving-supplies/) for easy organization on the spot.


### Disassemble Larger Items to Save Space and Prevent Damage


If possible, take apart larger furniture items like tables and chairs. This will not only save space but also reduce the risk of damage during storage. Be sure to keep screws and smaller pieces together in a labelled bag to avoid losing them.


### Leave Aisles Clear for Easy Storage Access


When packing your storage unit, aim to leave clear pathways or aisles. This not only helps to retrieve items more easily but also allows for better air circulation, which can prevent moisture buildup.


By following these packing tips, you’ll create a well-organized storage unit that maximizes space and maintains your belongings, making future visits to your unit much more pleasant!

## Storage Organization Ideas


Here are six great ways to ensure an organized storage unit. Whether you’re in a[10×10 storage unit](https://www.cubesmart.com/storage-resources/size-guide/10x10-storage-unit/) or[10×20 storage unit](https://www.cubesmart.com/storage-resources/size-guide/10x20-storage-unit/) , these tips will help you stay clutter-free.


### Ensure Storage Organization Success By Mapping Out the Layout


Revisiting layout mapping might seem repetitive, but it’s fundamental to storage unit success. Begin with a clear plan before you start stacking, ensuring every inch is effectively used without causing chaos.


Identify your key storage zones and adjust your unit’s blueprint to fit evolving storage needs. A clear map minimizes the guesswork when you add new items, ensuring each has a designated spot.


This proactive strategy also aids in maintaining order. By sticking to your layout, you foster good habits and prevent the gradual slide into disarray that can plague even the best intentions.


### Place Frequently Used Items Near the Front of Your Storage Unit


Image via[Adobe Stock](https://as2.ftcdn.net/v2/jpg/05/84/67/93/1000_F_584679362_TWtfehMhtSVgJlSuGCteYCdIDzfwjaUo.jpg)


One of the best storage organization ideas that should always be implemented is to place frequently accessed items upfront. This is not just practical but it’s vital for a smoothly functioning storage unit. Make use of clear plastic bins to store these items, allowing for visibility without opening containers.


Utilizing transparent bins not only aids in quick identification but also allows you to maximize shelf usage without sacrificing accessibility. It’s a win-win for those seeking efficient storage solutions.


It’s important to continuously assess the items you access most and adjust accordingly to ensure your current needs are reflected in your storage strategy. This ongoing assessment helps maintain functionality and prevents clutter from accumulating over time. Remember, flexibility is the key to long-term organizational success.


### Implement a Storage Shelving System to Keep Your Items Organized


Image via[Adobe Stock](https://as1.ftcdn.net/v2/jpg/05/08/52/04/1000_F_508520427_UTZSSYfA1RZyKdmKF7A3DicE18idJrg0.jpg)


When thinking about how to organize a storage space, remember that free-standing shelving is the backbone of any well-ordered storage unit. Choose versatile systems that can accommodate a range of items, from small tools to bulky duvets. The goal is to create storage unit shelving ideas that cater to diverse needs.


Regularly review your shelving setup. As your storage needs evolve, your system should too. Adjusting free-standing shelves to better fit your current inventory can prevent clutter and maximize efficiency.

### **Utilize Vertical Space in Your Storage Unit**


One of the most effective storage organization ideas is using vertical space. Think of your unit as a three-dimensional canvas, where height is as valuable as floor space. Stacking vertically can double or triple your storage capacity.


### **Use Your Furniture as a Second Storage Unit**


Furniture can do double duty in a storage unit. Hollow furniture like dressers, wardrobes, or even emptied out appliances can be used to store smaller items. It’s a smart hack in the quest for more space.


Always clean and wrap furniture before storage to shield both the furniture and the items stored within. If you’re shifting heavy furniture, use caution and follow our[6 tips for moving heavy furniture](https://www.cubesmart.com/blog/your-life/6-tips-for-moving-heavy-furniture/) to avoid injury.


Think creatively. A wardrobe might hold clothing, but a dresser can house tools, books, or decor. Using furniture as storage helps maximize space and makes the most of every item you store.


### Fill Storage Unit Gaps and Voids


Fill every gap and void smartly to make the best use of a storage unit. Roll up rugs to act as buffers between larger furniture pieces or slide flat items into narrow spaces.


Packing odd-shaped items like skis or fishing rods vertically in corners can save space. This way, they’re out of the way yet easily accessible when needed.


Foam peanuts or bubble wrap can fill empty spaces in boxes, preventing items from shifting during transit.


## Tips to Maintain Storage Organization


If you don’t keep up with it, even the most organized storage unit can get cluttered again. Here are some ways you can stay on top of your storage organization.


### Schedule Regular Storage Unit Organization Visits


Image via[Adobe Stock](https://as2.ftcdn.net/v2/jpg/02/03/95/43/1000_F_203954368_DETmttQHXx5ZUFRLfUD2hy8JoM9uXt9b.jpg)


Once you’ve achieved an organized space, maintenance is key. Plan regular visits to your storage unit to declutter, reorganize, and reassess your current layout.


Check for any items that might need to shift due to changes in accessibility needs or seasonal rotations. This proactive approach prevents disorder from creeping back in.


### Update Your Storage Inventory List Periodically


Keeping an updated inventory list is crucial. It not only tracks what’s stored but also helps in managing space efficiently. An accurate list means you avoid unnecessary trips or purchasing duplicate items.


Periodically review and revise your inventory list, especially after adding or removing items. This keeps your records current, and your unit organized.


Consider digital tools or apps that allow easy inventory management. These tools can streamline the process and provide access from anywhere, ensuring you’re always informed about your stored belongings.


### Use Clear Plastic Storage Bins for Visibility


Clear plastic bins offer the dual benefit of durability and visibility. You can quickly identify contents, making retrieval a breeze.


Label each bin with its contents for added clarity. This small step can save time and effort, reducing your rummaging through boxes.


Invest in quality bins with tight lids. This helps stored items last longer and maintain the overall neatness of your storage space.


### Should I Put Tarp on the Floor of My Storage Unit?


Using a tarp on the floor of your storage unit can be a wise decision, especially if you want to add an extra layer under furniture and other belongings.


If you decide to use a tarp, make sure it is large enough to cover the entire floor space, or large enough to spread under items like furniture. Overall, a tarp can be a simple yet effective solution for safeguarding your possessions in a storage unit.
