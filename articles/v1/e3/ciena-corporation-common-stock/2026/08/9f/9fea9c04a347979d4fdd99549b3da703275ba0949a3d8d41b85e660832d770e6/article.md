# Ciena Announces Reporting Date and Web Broadcast for Fiscal Third Quarter 2026 Results

### Ciena Announces Reporting Date and Web Broadcast for Fiscal Third Quarter 2026 Results


August 6, 2026


- [Download (opens in new window)](https://s25.q4cdn.com/550667411/files/doc_news/Ciena-Announces-Reporting-Date-and-Web-Broadcast-for-Fiscal-Third-Quarter-2026-Results-2026.pdf)


HANOVER, Md.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--[Ciena](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ciena.com%2F%3Fsrc%3DPR&esheet=54584659&newsitemid=20260806363262&lan=en-US&anchor=Ciena&index=1&md5=82ec79bc8e4f6d3d3967f2c969afce66) ® Corporation (NYSE: CIEN), the global leader in high-speed connectivity, expects to announce its fiscal third quarter financial results on Thursday, September 3, 2026 before the open of the U.S. financial markets. The press release will be available on Ciena’s website at[www.ciena.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ciena.com%2F&esheet=54584659&newsitemid=20260806363262&lan=en-US&anchor=www.ciena.com&index=2&md5=2a8de80cfb5df82d9aec868ebc9fd223) .


In conjunction with the announcement, Ciena will post an additional set of supporting materials to the[Quarterly Results page](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.ciena.com%2Ffinancials%2Fdefault.aspx%23section%253Dquarterly&esheet=54584659&newsitemid=20260806363262&lan=en-US&anchor=Quarterly+Results+page&index=3&md5=92a41509dc863b3a347f0ba3f320104f) of the Investor Relations section of its website. Ciena’s management will then host a live audio web broadcast beginning at 8:30 a.m. Eastern accessible via[www.ciena.com](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ciena.com%2F&esheet=54584659&newsitemid=20260806363262&lan=en-US&anchor=www.ciena.com&index=4&md5=7966c96a6e148d70b5e8bf00216421a6) .


**Rebroadcast Information**


For those listeners unable to participate in the live web broadcast, an archived version of the conference call will be available shortly following the conclusion of the live call in the[Events & Presentations](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.ciena.com%2Fevents-and-presentations%2Fdefault.aspx&esheet=54584659&newsitemid=20260806363262&lan=en-US&anchor=Events+%26amp%3B+Presentations&index=5&md5=78579cc7dc72693363497d984b1d42ff) section of the Investor Relations section of Ciena’s website.
