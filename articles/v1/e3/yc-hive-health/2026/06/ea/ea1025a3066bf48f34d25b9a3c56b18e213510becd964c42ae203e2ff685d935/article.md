# Preventive Care in HMO Benefits for Employees

## **What Is Preventive Care?**


Preventive care refers to healthcare services designed to prevent illness, identify risks early, and support long-term health management. Rather than focusing only on treatment after symptoms appear, preventive healthcare encourages employees to take a more proactive approach to their health *before* symptoms may arise.


Common examples of preventive care include:


- Annual Physical Exams (APE)
- Vaccinations
- Routine doctor consultations
- Blood pressure and blood sugar monitoring
- Cancer screenings
- Mental health consultations
- Nutrition and wellness counseling


Preventive care is different from emergency or reactive care. For example:


- Preventive care: Scheduling an annual physical exam to monitor cholesterol levels
- Reactive care: Being hospitalized later due to unmanaged cardiovascular disease


The goal is not simply to diagnose illness, but to reduce the likelihood of more serious health complications over time.


## **Why Preventive Care Matters for Employees**


One of the biggest benefits of preventive healthcare is early detection.


Regular screenings and consultations can help identify common conditions such as hypertension, diabetes risk, or elevated cholesterol levels before they become more severe. In many cases, employees may not notice symptoms until a condition has already progressed. Preventive care also encourages employees to build healthier long-term habits. Regular interaction with healthcare professionals can improve awareness around:


- Sleep
- Stress management
- Exercise
- Nutrition
- Mental health


This is especially relevant in today’s work environment, where burnout, prolonged screen time, sedentary routines, and sometimes altered sleep schedules due to work shifts have become increasingly common.


Beyond physical health, preventive care can also support emotional and mental well-being. Access to counseling or mental health consultations allows employees to seek support earlier instead of waiting for stress or anxiety to escalate into larger concerns.


## **Why Preventive Care Matters for Comapnies**


For HR leaders, preventive care isn’t just a healthcare initiative, it’s now also a workforce strategy. Employees who receive regular preventive care are often better equipped to manage their health before conditions begin affecting attendance, productivity, or day-to-day performance. Some of the business benefits of preventive healthcare include:


- **Reduced absenteeism -** Routine check-ups and accessible consultations may help reduce unplanned sick leaves and emergency-related absences.
- **Better long-term healthcare cost management -** Preventive care is generally more cost-effective than hospitalization or emergency treatment. Detecting health concerns early can reduce the likelihood of more expensive claims later on.
- **Improved employee trust and retention -** Healthcare benefits remain one of the most valued employee benefits in the Philippines. Companies that actively support employee wellness often build stronger trust and engagement among their teams.
- **Stronger wellness planning -** Many HMOs provide companies with health insights after Annual Physical Exams or wellness initiatives. These trends can help HR teams design more targeted programs around nutrition, stress management, or chronic disease prevention tailored for their people.


For mid-sized companies experiencing growth, preventive care also helps HR teams shift from reactive healthcare administration toward more sustainable workforce wellness planning.

## **Common Examples of Preventive Care in the Philippines**


In the Philippine corporate setting, preventive healthcare is often accessed through company-provided HMO benefits.


Some of the most common preventive healthcare services include the following:


### **Annual Physical Exams (APE)**


Annual Physical Exams are among the most widely used preventive care benefits in corporate healthcare plans. APEs typically include:


- Physical assessments
- Laboratory tests
- Chest X-rays
- Basic health screenings


These exams help establish employee health baselines and identify potential concerns early.


### **Vaccinations**


Some HMOs or company healthcare programs may cover administration fees for preventive vaccines such as flu shots or other immunizations.


### **Routine Consultations**


Access to both primary care and specialty doctors allows employees to address symptoms or concerns before they worsen, often offered online for the patient’s convenience.


### **Mental Health Consultations**


More companies today are including mental health support as part of their preventive healthcare offerings, recognizing that emotional well-being directly affects workplace performance and quality of life. For employees with current diagnoses, some HMOs like Hive Health also offer medicine assistance to help with prescription medication.
