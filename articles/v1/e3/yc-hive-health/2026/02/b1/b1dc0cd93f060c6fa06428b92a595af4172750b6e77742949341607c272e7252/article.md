# Annual Physical Exams in the Philippines: What to Expect from your HMO

## **Benefits of Annual Physical Exams for Corporations**


While APEs clearly benefit employees and their dependents, they also deliver measurable advantages for businesses.


### **Early Detection of Health Risks**


Regular annual physical exams help detect potential health concerns (such as high blood pressure, elevated blood sugar, or abnormal lab results) before symptoms worsen. Early detection can prevent complications that may lead to extended absences or hospitalization.


### **Reduced Sick Leaves and Unplanned Absences**


By encouraging preventive healthcare, companies may see fewer health-related absences. Employees who are aware of how they’re doing are more likely to improve their lifestyle, manage conditions proactively, and seek timely treatment if needed.


### **Better Workforce Planning Through Aggregated Health Insights**


Most HMOs provide employers with **aggregated, non-identifiable health reports** after APE season. These reports can highlight trends such as:


- Common risk factors
- Prevalence of lifestyle-related conditions
- Age-related health patterns


This allows HR teams to plan targeted wellness initiatives without compromising employee privacy.


### **Compliance with Company Health Policies and Industry Norms**


Many industries in the Philippines treat annual physical exams as standard practice. Offering APEs aligns companies with common corporate health program norms and demonstrates commitment to responsible workforce management.


### **Improved Employee Trust and Employer Branding**


Providing annual physical exams signals that the company prioritizes employee well-being. This can strengthen retention, engagement, and overall perception of employer support.


### **Support for HMO Utilization and Preventive Care Goals**


APEs encourage employees to maximize their HMO benefits responsibly. Instead of relying only on emergency care, employees are guided toward preventive healthcare—reducing long-term claims volatility.

## **What’s Typically Included in an Annual Physical Exam in the Philippines**


One of the most searched questions around **annual physical exam Philippines** is: *What does an APE actually include?*


While inclusions vary depending on company policy, HMO plan, and provider, here’s what is commonly covered.


### **1. Medical History and Physical Assessment**


This typically includes:


- Review of past illnesses and current symptoms
- Measurement of vital signs such as blood pressure, heart rate, and BMI
- Basic physical examination conducted by a physician


The goal is to establish or update the employee’s overall health baseline.


### **2. Laboratory Tests (Standard Inclusions)**


Common APE lab tests in Philippine corporate packages include:


- Complete Blood Count (CBC)
- Urinalysis
- Fecalysis
- Chest X-ray


These tests help screen for infections, respiratory issues, and other underlying conditions.


### **3. Additional Screenings (Depending on Company or Age Group)**


Some companies or HMOs include expanded screenings such as:


- ECG (Electrocardiogram)


- Pap smear


- Fasting Blood Sugar


- Lipid profile (often for employees above a certain age)


**Important:** Inclusions vary depending on the HMO and company policy, which is why clear coordination between HR and the provider is essential.


Some HMOs may also offer:


- Post-APE health and wellness consultations (diet, exercise, family planning, healthy habits)
- Administration fees coverage for preventive vaccines


## **Why Regular Physical Exams Matter for Employees**


From an employee perspective, the **importance of annual physical exams** lies in prevention—not just diagnosis.


### **Early Identification of Potential Issues**


Routine exams can flag conditions like hypertension, diabetes risk, or abnormal lab values before symptoms become severe.


### **Encourages Proactive Health Management**


APEs create an opportunity for employees to reflect on lifestyle habits and receive professional guidance.


### **Supports Long-Term Wellness**


Preventive healthcare plays a critical role in reducing chronic diseases, which are among the leading causes of workplace absenteeism.


### **Establishes a Year-to-Year Health Baseline**


Having consistent annual records allows employees and physicians to compare results over time and track changes accurately.


## **Sample Annual Physical Exam Checklist for Employees**


Here’s a practical **annual physical exam checklist in the Philippines** that HR teams can share.


### **Before the Exam**


- Book APE schedule and confirm location
- Fast for 8–10 hours if required
- Bring valid ID or HMO card


### **During the Exam**


- Medical history review
- Physical examination
- Lab tests and imaging


### **After the Exam**


- Receive results within the provider’s turnaround time
- Review findings with a physician if needed
- Follow up on flagged results or recommended procedures


Clear communication around this checklist helps minimize confusion during APE season.

## **How APE Results Are Usually Managed by Companies**


This is an area many HR teams want clarity on.


- Individual results are shared **directly with employees**
- Employers receive only **aggregated and anonymized reports**
- Data is used for wellness planning—not performance evaluation
- Employees are encouraged, but not forced, to pursue follow-up care


Maintaining strict data privacy reinforces employee trust and ensures compliance with health and privacy standards.


## **Common Questions HR Teams Ask About Annual Physical Exams**


### **Is an annual physical exam mandatory?**


It depends on company policy. Some organizations strongly encourage participation, while others make it optional.


### **Can employees skip certain tests?**


In most cases, employees can decline specific tests, but this may vary by provider or corporate agreement.


### **What happens if an employee misses the schedule?**


Companies may arrange a reschedule period or coordinate directly with the HMO provider.


### **Are APEs covered by HMOs?**


Yes, annual physical exams are commonly included in corporate HMO plans, though coverage details may vary.


### **Can dependents be included?**


Some HMO packages allow dependents to avail of APEs, depending on plan type and company benefits structure.


## **Benefits of Pre-Employment Medical Exams (PEME)**


To better understand APEs, it helps to compare them with **Pre-Employment Medical Exams (PEME) in the Philippines** .


### **PEME vs APE: Key Differences**


- **PEME:** Conducted before hiring to establish a health baseline and determine fitness for work
- **APE:** Conducted annually for existing employees to monitor ongoing health


### **Why PEMEs Matter**


PEMEs help ensure workplace safety, confirm role suitability, and establish initial medical records.


### **How PEMEs and APEs Work Together**


A well-structured corporate health program includes:


- PEME at the start of employment
- APE annually for monitoring and preventive care


Together, they create a comprehensive approach to employee health management.
