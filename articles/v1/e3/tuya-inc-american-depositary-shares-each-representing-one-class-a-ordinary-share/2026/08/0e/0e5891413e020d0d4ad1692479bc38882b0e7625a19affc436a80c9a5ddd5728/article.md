# Tuya Smart Joins ioXt Alliance | news | Tuya Smart

**Move enables Tuya to further advance IoT device security through participation in global initiative**


SANTA CLARA, CA—Oct. 16, 2020—Tuya Smart, a leading global AI+IoT (AIoT) platform provider, today announced it has become a member of the ioXt Alliance, the Global Standard for IoT Security.


The membership means Tuya will continue to drive the direction of IoT device security by participating in ioXt’s collaborative, cross-industry initiative to establish and advocate for best practices and standards in IoT and cyber security.


“We’re extremely excited about joining the ioXt Alliance because it means we can work with other ioXt members to disseminate and solidify knowledge and best practices around IoT device security,” said Fritz Werder, General Manager of Tuya North America. “Device security has never been more important and there has never been a better time to participate in a cross-industry forum around securing smart devices.”


The ioXt Alliance is a group of more than 200 leading OEMs, wireless carriers, standards groups, compliance labs, and government organizations dedicated to harmonizing best security practices and establishing testable standards that give manufacturers, retailers and consumers product confidence in a highly connected world. Products that are certified through the ioXt Certification Program are evaluated against eight different ioXt pledge principles that outline required levels of security. Once devices pass lab or self-attestation testing, they are deemed safe to receive the ioXt SmartCert label.


“Any company entering or already in the smart device segments now has tremendous pressure by government entities and consumers alike to make their products as secure as possible,” said Brad Ree, CTO of the ioXt Alliance. “The stakes are incredibly high, and that’s why participation from companies like Tuya Smart that are firmly rooted in IoT and are proponents of security standards is so important for us. We’re excited to have Tuya on board, and are confident that their ongoing participation will continue to advance IoT device security.”
