# Tuya Smart Brings AI to Real Life at CES 2026 | news | Tuya Smart

Las Vegas — On January 6, 2026, CES 2026 officially kicked off in Las Vegas. Tuya Smart (NYSE: TUYA, HKEX: 2391), a global AI cloud platform service provider, showcased its newly launched AI Life Assistant, Hey Tuya, alongside the AI Agent Development Platform, the Tuya Open open-source framework, as well as various AI hardware including the pet companion robot Aura and the world's first cellular version of "Fuzozo" among others. Together, they form a "Software-Hardware-Platform" Physical AI ecosystem, showcasing the potential of AI life transitioning from "reactive intelligence" to "proactive AI services."


### Hey Tuya Makes Its Overseas Debut


As a super AI life assistant based on a Multi-Agent collaborative architecture, Hey Tuya has been hailed by the media as a real-world prototype of "Jarvis" since its launch. At its CES debut, it showcased immersive demonstrations across scenarios such as home security, energy-saving, healthy living, and efficient office work, to showcase the future landscape of "AI Life."


Hey Tuya is powered by the OmniMem individual long-term memory engine, which allows it to remember users' habits and preferences, enabling the transition from "passive response" to "active service." It can not only engage in conversations on various topics via voice and text, but also identify plants, animals, and objects through the AI Lens. Additionally, it can create personalized morning news, commuting stories, and bedtime tales based on user interests, providing customized podcasts for a more tailored experience.


What makes it even more engaging is that Hey Tuya can express emotions through smart devices—transforming smart lighting and audio systems into interactive characters that respond to users' moods with light and music, becoming a caring and attentive life companion that listens and understands.


Currently, Hey Tuya is available for global public testing on major app stores. Users can experience it immediately via the Tuya App or by visiting the Tuya.AI website.


### AI Hardware Innovations


At CES 2026, Tuya showcased a variety of AI hardware products that are currently in high demand in the market, offering global developers more innovative inspiration.


**Cellular Version of "Fuzozo"**


Tuya, announced the strategic partnership with Robopoet to co-develop the cellular version of "Fuzozo." This AI emotional companion can generate different facial expressions and responses based on the user's touch frequency and location, enabling real-time interaction. With Tuya's support, it reduces reaction delays and offers smooth conversations, while also being capable of operating outdoors, providing emotional companionship to users all day long.


**PetCare: AI Health Tracker**


The PetCare is a pet collar that utilizes high-sensitivity sound capture and proprietary AI emotional analysis to help owners clearly review their pets' emotional fluctuations throughout the day. Based on emotional and health data, the product offers specific interaction suggestions, helping owners respond appropriately to their pets' needs—whether it's providing more playtime or comforting—thus creating a genuine, two-way emotional bond.


**AI Box**


The AI Box jointly showcased by Tuya and Intel features high-performance computing capabilities, enabling the smooth operation of complex local large language models. The device allows data to be processed locally without uploading to the cloud, delivering fast response times and enhanced privacy.


Seamlessly integrated into the Tuya ecosystem, the AI Box can connect and coordinate with Tuya’s full range of smart devices. Powered by an Intel-based voice assistant AI agent, it enables natural language control of Tuya devices through local processing. In addition, the AI Box supports a wide array of practical features, including customizable local video AI alerts, AI-powered temporal search and retrieval, multi-channel local storage for home camera recordings, and video summarization—unlocking a smarter, more convenient home living experience.


**AI Pet: Walulu**


Walulu is equipped with up to 19 types of nuanced emotional feedback, paired with authentic cat sounds, ensuring that each moment of companionship is full of surprises and warmth. It is powered by the ChatGPT large model, supporting multi-language conversations in Russian, Japanese, Korean, English, Vietnamese, and more, enabling smooth communication with global users. Moreover, it integrates haptic reactions, such as shaking, offering users a more immersive companionship experience.


**AI Kids Camera**


To help developers seize the AI education market opportunity, the Tuya-powered AI Kids Camera is equipped with cutting-edge, practical AI features. For instance, the AI object recognition feature encourages children to explore new things anytime, anywhere, making nature their "mobile classroom." The advanced AI system can also accurately identify both Chinese and English text, serving as a powerful daily learning assistant for kids. Equipped with both Wi-Fi and cellular connectivity, it provides all-time company regardless of the location.


**AI Dashcam**


The AI Dashcam goes beyond safeguarding travel safety to create a connected, intelligent, and convenient new driving ecosystem. Its core breakthrough lies in upgrading safety from passive recording to proactive protection. Utilizing advanced AI vision algorithms, the device can intelligently detect potential collision risks, such as the vehicle following too closely or pedestrians approaching and provide timely audio-visual warnings to help drivers anticipate and avoid hazards.


Additionally, the AI Dashcam serves as a smart connectivity node, integrating with mainstream smart home platforms and allowing users to stay informed about their home status and control devices remotely while in their vehicle, creating a seamless connection between the car and home.


### AI Development Engines and Cutting-Edge AI Solutions


Tuya presented the TuyaOpen open-source framework, the Tuya AI Developer Platform, and AI industry solutions at CES 2026. Developers can easily leverage AI capabilities and, based on Tuya's open, neutral, and internationalized AIoT ecosystem, collectively accelerate the creative deployment of Physical AI in various life scenarios.


**TuyaOpen Open-Source Framework**


TuyaOpen,an open-source software-hardware integrated framework, empowers developers to build AI agent-enabled hardware efficiently. It lowers development barriers by streamlining the hardware-AI agent integration process, fueling innovation in the physical AI era.


**Tuya AI Developer Platform**


This one-stop platform accelerates development, allowing developers to integrate an AI "brain" into hardware in just 10 minutes and to create working prototypes in 15 minutes. It allows developers to create AI Agents simply by describing their desired functionalities, with AI automatically handling the agent orchestration.


**Spatial Intelligence**


Customers no longer need to build systems from scratch. They can select intelligent function modules as needed and quickly assemble capabilities. Relying on the platform's general capabilities and industry know-how, this creates a foundation for scenario-based smart abilities.


**Ofstar AI Fleet Software-Hardware Integrated Solution**


Its core hardware, "AI Multicam," features 360-degree in-vehicle video monitoring with ADAS, DMS, and BSD, OBD vehicle diagnostics, extensive Bluetooth accessory capabilities, and powerful edge-side computing. It serves as the "fleet brain" to comprehensively collect and analyze multi-dimensional vehicle data for prediction and analysis.
