# Tuya Smart to Showcase Outdoor IoT and Cellular Connectivity Technology at 2022 Mobile World Congress | news | Tuya Smart

Tuya Smart ("Tuya" or the "Company") (NYSE: TUYA), a global IoT development platform service provider, today announced it will attend the 2022 Mobile World Congress (MWC) in Barcelona, Spain. The Company plans to showcase its cellular connectivity products and demonstrate how Tuya Smart supports the advancement of outdoor IoT devices through cellular connectivity capabilities.


At this year’s MWC in Barcelona, running from February 28 until March 3, Tuya Smart will showcase its comprehensive suite of cellular connectivity capabilities to thousands of visitors. This year’s MWC theme, “One Step Closer to the Smart Life,” reflects the progress the Company has made in supporting the expansion of the outdoor IoT technology industry, including strengthening device the accuracy, features and distances covered.


While IoT devices were previously restricted to indoor and smart home scenarios, devices have gradually expanded to short-range and mid-range outdoor solutions, backed by Wi-Fi, Zigbee, and Bluetooth technology. And increasingly developers are in search of solutions beyond the normal medium and short-range communication protocols. Tuya Smart offers a comprehensive suite of hardware and software solutions for short, medium, and long distance outdoor IoT product development, such as LTE Cat.1, NB-IoT and other long-range cellular communication protocols.


Tuya’s thriving ecosystem currently provides hardware and software support for a large portfolio of outdoor products including smart scooters and smart mobility, smart trackers, smart energy, outdoor lighting, industry, and agriculture. Tuya’s powerful platform development tools include the development of OEM apps, globally deployed cloud services and various network modules. The Company’s cloud services also support important features for outdoor scenarios, such as device positioning, geofencing, route tracking, and other energy efficient solutions to support device mobility and a strong travel experience to users.


For electric scooters and other outdoor travel applications, Tuya has tailored the support of key features such as real-time tracking of device battery or remaining ride distance for electric vehicles or scooters, to help users accurately plan their route and device charging. For shared electric scooters or power banks, Tuya Smart’s tools also support business developers in accurately tracking fleet location, charging station status and in managing the device pool.


Tuya Smart continues to expand its outdoor communication tools to help developers provide cutting-edge products for business and customers uses. As of September 30, 2021, the Tuya IoT development platform served over 446,000 developers around the world, with its IoT PaaS empowering over 3,800 brands to develop smart devices including Calex, Philips, Schneider Electric and more. As new mobility and outdoor applications for IoT continue to grow, Tuya Smart will support its thriving ecosystem of developers, OEMs and brands to build comprehensive products.
