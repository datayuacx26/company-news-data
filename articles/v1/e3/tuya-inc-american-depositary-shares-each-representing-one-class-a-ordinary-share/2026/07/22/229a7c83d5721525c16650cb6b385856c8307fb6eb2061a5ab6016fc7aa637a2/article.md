# Tuya Smart Demonstrates How AI is Transforming Smart Lighting and Buildings at Light + Building 2026 | news | Tuya Smart

Frankfurt, Germany — March 8, 2026 —Tuya Smart (NYSE: TUYA, HKEX: 2391), a global AI cloud platform service provider, today exhibited at Light + Building 2026, the world's leading trade fair for lighting and building services technology, in Frankfurt.


This year, Tuya Smart presented under the theme “Hey Tuya, Hey Brighter Life,” showcasing its innovative capabilities across AI platforms, lighting, and building technologies. Tuya also joined forces with ecosystem partners including Whale Vision, MoreSense Technology, XiongYuan Lighting, Changyou Technology, Linklite, and Golden Bright to jointly stage the Tuya Global Ecosystem Exhibition, integrating foundational AI capabilities with vertical industry scenarios and presenting developers worldwide with a new AI-driven vision of a green, intelligent, and sustainable future.


## Tuya’s AI Innovation Engines Accelerate Physical AI Applications


AI is rapidly becoming a key force reshaping the hardware industry ecosystem. At Light + Building 2026, Tuya unveiled several AI innovation engines—including the AI Agent Development Platform, Hey Tuya, and Spatial Intelligence—designed to free developers from complex technical infrastructures and enable faster product innovation and industry upgrades.


#### AI Agent Development Platform: Empowering Developers to Turn Ideas into Reality


At the Tuya booth, visitors experienced AI toys, pet companion robots, and desktop robots naturally interacting with people. These devices are not isolated products—they all “grow” from the same platform. Tuya’s AI Agent Development Platform effectively equips devices with both a “brain” and a “nervous system.” Developers can transform ideas into intelligent hardware prototypes that can speak, see, and interact within just 15 minutes, while also supporting the full path from prototype validation to large-scale mass production.


#### Hey Tuya: Your AI Life Assistant


Hey Tuya, Tuya’s AI life assistant, is powered by the company’s self-developed Physical AI Engine (PAE). It spans key home scenarios including security guardian, energy saving, work productivity, health, companionship, and pet care. The assistant supports multi-device connectivity, short-term and long-term memory, and deep integration with the global ecosystem of “Powered by Tuya” smart devices, enabling proactive perception of the physical environment and intelligent service delivery.


#### Tuya Spatial AI: A Zero-Code Architecture for Personalized Smart Spaces


Tuya Spatial AI platform is a one-stop solution that integrates scenario-based hardware packages with software capabilities, enabling customers to rapidly plan and deploy intelligent space solutions. Its core value lies in three dimensions: • Strong scenario building capability: Developers can freely build cross-industry solutions based on core capabilities, creating a scenario SaaS application in as little as 10 minutes. • AI empowerment: The platform provides spatial agents and AI services such as full-scenario data perception and multimodal scene recognition, allowing spaces to actively sense, understand, and respond to user needs. • Open integration: Rich APIs and developer tools allow rapid development of customized applications.


## From Indoor to Outdoor: Using AI to Illuminate Every Inch of Space


Tuya’s innovations extend beyond technology into deep insights across vertical scenarios. Leveraging strong AI capabilities, Tuya has developed comprehensive indoor and outdoor lighting solutions, showcased alongside ecosystem partners at the exhibition to deliver smarter and more comfortable lighting experiences.


#### AI Whole-Home Lighting Solution: Let Light Move with Your Imagination


This solution integrates AI scenarios, AI dimming, AI gateways, AI voice, and AI companionship to create a complete intelligent experience—from environmental perception and device linkage to voice interaction and emotional engagement.


Powered by comprehensive data and AI algorithms, users can simply upload a scene photo or enter a short text description, and the system will automatically generate a matching lighting scenario. By combining proprietary hardware with open software—along with standardized APIs and complete SDKs, the solution ensures efficient and reliable project deployment.


#### Ambient Lighting Solution: Unlock Personalized Lighting Experiences


The ambient lighting solution jointly launched by Linklite and Tuya attracted significant attention at the exhibition. For indoor environments, the solution integrates an HDMI screen synchronizer with a Zigbee gateway, synchronizing wireless lighting devices with screen content to create immersive lighting effects that enhance movies, entertainment, and gaming experiences.


For outdoor scenarios, Linklite combines products such as outdoor spotlights, pathway lights, string lights, and eave lights with Tuya’s ambient lighting solution to create dynamic garden lighting displays. Users can freely adjust brightness and color to match different moods and occasions, bringing outdoor spaces to life at night.


#### Lighting Driver Solution: The Core Engine for High-Quality Lighting


Efficient, safe, and intelligent power drivers are critical to lighting system performance. Tuya’s new generation smart lighting driver solution enables precise control of brightness and color temperature while significantly improving energy efficiency. This solution can be widely applied in commercial and office lighting scenarios, helping customers build energy-efficient and reliable lighting systems. XiongYuan Lighting, a global provider of advanced power solutions, showcased its upgraded smart driver power series powered by Tuya. These products feature built-in Zigbee, Bluetooth, and Matter multi-mode gateways, enabling seamless cross-gateway and cross-protocol connectivity—even under weak network or offline conditions.


#### Smart Street Lighting Solution: Powering the Smart City


At the exhibition, Golden Bright partnered with Tuya to introduce a new smart street lighting solution. The solution supports a variety of lighting systems—including high-mast lights, solar streetlights, outdoor street lamps, and high-bay lighting—and integrates Tuya’s App + SaaS management platform for street lighting. It enables on-demand lighting, fault self-diagnosis, and remote operation and maintenance, significantly reducing energy consumption and operational costs while supporting smart city infrastructure development.


## Tuya and Ecosystem Partners: Reimagining the Life of Buildings with AI


AI technology is transforming the entire lifecycle of buildings. At Light + Building 2026, Tuya and its ecosystem partners demonstrated how AI can reconstruct every “cell” of a building—from environmental perception to intent understanding, from precise control to autonomous decision-making. Buildings are evolving from collections of smart devices into truly intelligent spaces that can breathe, think, and adapt.


#### IPC Solution: Advancing AI Security Applications


Tuya’s AI-powered IPC solution continues to evolve with new capabilities. On one hand, it supports customizable smart events and free-of-charge recognition of five common targets: people, vehicles, pets, packages, and flames. On the other hand, it enables intelligent analysis of event footage—moving from simply identifying objects to understanding entire scenarios. At the exhibition, Tuya integrated this solution with WhaleView’s wall-mounted camera lights to create a smart courtyard lighting camera solution. With a built-in high-definition wide-angle camera, the system captures critical motion even in low-light environments while utilizing human detection and motion tracking algorithms to seamlessly integrate professional security capabilities into outdoor landscape lighting.


#### Building Electrical Solution: EnerVision AI Makes Every Kilowatt Traceable


Tuya introduced a new building electrical solution powered by its self-developed EnerVision AI. The system employs the globally advanced Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring (NILM) technology and supports non-destructive upgrades. Without opening walls or modifying each appliance, AI analyzes subtle current waveform changes to map energy consumption patterns of household devices—from air conditioners and refrigerators to hidden standby power usage. Based on AI algorithms, the system automatically optimizes electricity consumption strategies, reduces peak loads, and provides scientifically grounded energy-saving recommendations, making green buildings more achievable.


At the exhibition, Changyou Technology integrated its circuit breakers, smart meters, multi-mode 4G gateways, and waterproof smart boxes with Tuya’s Building Environmental Management System (BEMS) to deliver refined power management and energy monitoring for lighting, sockets, air conditioning, and other building systems.


#### Smart Sensor Solution: The “Nerve Endings” of Intelligent Buildings


Tuya’s Smart Sensor Solution includes a full range of sensors—such as motion detection, light sensing, and door/window sensors—allowing flexible deployment based on scenario needs. MoreSense Technology, a leading radar sensing and IoT communication module brand, showcased its 24G human presence detection and smart commercial lighting solution together with Tuya. By collecting real-time environmental data, the system enables accurate decision-making for spatial intelligence—from energy-saving automation like lights turning on when people arrive to dynamic air-conditioning adjustments.


## Smart Mobility Solution: Connecting People, Vehicles, and Homes


Tuya’s vision is expanding beyond static spaces to more dynamic mobility scenarios. In the mobility and wearable device exhibition area, Tuya showcased AI-powered solutions for commercial fleets, personal mobility, and even pet travel scenarios. For industry fleets, Tuya has launched the Ofstar AI Fleet integrated software and hardware solution, which integrates advanced communication, positioning, video, sensing, and AI technologies to achieve one-stop management from secure dispatching to asset protection. For personal mobility, Tuya demonstrated 4G smart vehicle control units and e-bike dashboards, enabling battery monitoring, remote control, and riding data management through the Tuya platform. These systems also support digital operations for both offline stores and online marketplaces. Meanwhile, Tuya is building a cross-scenario ecosystem connecting people, vehicles, and homes, breaking down the barriers between car systems and smart home devices so users can control home devices directly from their vehicles for seamless service integration.


At Light + Building 2026, Tuya Smart presented a wide range of lighting and building solutions, delivering a comprehensive AI-driven technology showcase to developers around the world. Looking ahead, Tuya will continue to iterate on AI products and services, focusing on advancing AI capabilities and real-world applications while empowering global developers to build a greener, low-carbon, and sustainable future together.
