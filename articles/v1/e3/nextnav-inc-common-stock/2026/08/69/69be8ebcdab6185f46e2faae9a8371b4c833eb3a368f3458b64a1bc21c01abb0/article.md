# NextNav Statement on Nomination of Danielle Thumann Severs

On Friday, August 7, President-elect Donald Trump[nominated](https://nam04.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.whitehouse.gov%2Fpresidential-actions%2F2026%2F08%2Fnominations-sent-to-the-senate-e61c%2F&data=05%7C02%7Cejeffs%40nextnav.com%7C32154ba1826c4858eeb208def4b3523e%7C207ec3d33ddb4d3b8b9189f1306ec59e%7C0%7C0%7C639217248275207893%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=RSsRWBonLv%2BiR8tZ7LGiRc2OifDyBVlmha0ps4EDiI8%3D&reserved=0) Danielle Thumann Severs to serve as a Commissioner on the Federal Communications Commission (FCC). The following statement can be attributed to Mariam Sorond, CEO of NextNav:


“ *We congratulate Danielle Thumann Severs on her nomination to the FCC. As Senior Counsel to Chairman Carr, she brings a wealth of institutional, legal, and telecommunications expertise on a wide range of regulatory issues that will be invaluable to the Commission. We urge the Senate to move swiftly to confirm her.* “
