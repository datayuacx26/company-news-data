# NextNav Statement on SpaceX Filing in the FCC docket

Following a[filing](https://nam04.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.fcc.gov%2Fecfs%2Fdocument%2F26110070253%2F1&data=05%7C02%7Cejeffs%40nextnav.com%7Ca46cae3cf89e407a42c808def7be9ac1%7C207ec3d33ddb4d3b8b9189f1306ec59e%7C0%7C0%7C639220595211425771%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=Iye%2BGCUZWZW50qtAEixgxRKmTz%2FflfUDaQ7q9XfKJkY%3D&reserved=0) from SpaceX in the Federal Communications Commission’s NextNav docket, the company released the following statement from Renee Gregory, NextNav’s Vice President of Regulatory Affairs:


*“We appreciate SpaceX’s engagement in the FCC’s PNT proceeding, which reinforces the urgency of putting scarce low-band spectrum to productive use and the growing industry recognition of the value these spectrum resources can unlock. We look forward to working with all stakeholders and the FCC to advance NextNav’s ground-based 5G-powered 3D PNT solution to complement and back up GPS while delivering significant national security, public safety, and economic benefits at no cost to taxpayers.”*
