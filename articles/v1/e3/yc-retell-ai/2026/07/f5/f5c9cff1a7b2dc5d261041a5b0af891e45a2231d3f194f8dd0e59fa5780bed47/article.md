## What Is Apple’s iOS Call Screening?


Apple’s new Call screening feature is built on Live voicemail. When enabled, it automatically answers calls from unknown numbers, prompts the caller to state the purpose of their call and then shows a live transcription of what they’d said.


**Here’s how it works, step by step:**


- The user must be on iOS 26 and explicitly opt in to Call Screening.


- Once enabled, calls from new or unknown numbers are answered by iOS, which plays a pre-recorded prompt:


*“Please state your name and reason for calling.”*


- The caller (rep) speaks, while the iPhone owner remains muted and unseen


- If the caller says nothing, the call ends automatically.


- After ~30 seconds, the phone begins to ring.


- While it’s ringing, the iPhone displays a live transcript of what the caller said, allowing the user to decide whether to answer or decline.


It works more like a pre-call voicemail; there’s no back-and-forth, no automated filtering, as[AI IVR](https://www.retellai.com/ai-ivr) and no AI-driven conversation. The caller’s message is transcribed in real time, and the user can then decide whether to pick up iOS performance also benefits from a[Mac optimization app](https://macpaw.com/cleanmymac) .


## Is This Update a Problem For AI Calling?


It's not the end for AI calling. It's the start of a smarter calling–and those who get ahead with this win the race. The takeaway isn’t “stop AI calls,” it’s “make calling better and scam-free.”


Here’s why this isn’t as disruptive as it sounds:


### Call Screening opt-in is hard to find


From what we have seen so far, the call screening feature is usually disabled by default, and the setting to enable it is pretty buried. And, frankly, people who will turn it on are often those who already screen or silence unknown numbers anyway.


### The call still gets ~30 seconds of airtime


Secondly, the call still goes on for 30 seconds. So, while this feature adds a buffer, it doesn’t block the call entirely. This means reps using[outbound dialer vendors](https://www.retellai.com/blog/best-outbound-dialer-tested-and-ranked) can still use parallel dialling and expect similar connections.


### There’s a Hidden Advantage


If used wisely, Call Screening could actually increase connect rates. Since the caller’s message is transcribed and displayed, reps with better context, clearer intent, and stronger openers can stand out instantly.


If your opener sounds like a telemarketer or is vague fluff, even compared to structured[AI telemarketing](https://www.retellai.com/ai-telemarketing) messaging congrats — you’re blocked.


But if it’s specific, human, and immediately relevant, Screening actually works in your favour.

## Best Practices to Avoid Flagged or Blocked Calls on iOS 26


Apart from your script, passing iOS 26 call screening requires the right pacing, systems and behaviours behind every call. Follow these best practices to maintain trust, stay compliant, and connect with more real prospects.


### 1. Monitor Your Caller ID Reputation


With iOS 26 tightening call screening and spam detection, your caller ID reputation now directly impacts whether your calls get through.


Phone carriers may mark certain phone numbers as “Spam Likely".Check your outbound call log to see if the call was not connected due to SIP error code 608. If that’s the case, that means it’s rejected due to being marked as spam likely.


Retell monitors your caller ID reputation in real time, alerting you if your numbers are at risk of being flagged. This allows you to take proactive steps to maintain call deliverability and avoid unnecessary blocks.


You can also reference external APIs and services to gauge spam risk, though results may vary by carrier:


- [Nomorobo](https://www.nomorobo.com/) : Use their app or API to check whether a number appears in their spam database.


- [IPQualityScore](https://www.ipqualityscore.com/phone-number-validator) : Provides a phone number reputation score that can indicate potential spam risk.


### 2. Register Your Numbers


Under iOS 26, unverified numbers face more friction than ever. Unknown or unregistered numbers are more likely to be screened, ignored, or flagged before a human ever sees the call.


Retell helps you get ahead and improve your call pickup rate with:


- **Verified Phone:** Retell registers your phone number directly with carriers, strengthening its reputation at the network level.


This reduces the likelihood of your calls being flagged or filtered as spam, helps them bypass aggressive call screening, and significantly improves your chances of reaching a real person, especially under stricter systems like iOS 26.


‍ **Branded Call:** Instead of seeing an unfamiliar or generic number, the callee sees your verified business name when the phone rings. This immediate brand recognition builds trust before the call is answered, making recipients more confident that the call is legitimate and relevant, and far more likely to pick up.


### 3. Manage Call Cadence and Frequency


With iOS 26’s updated screening rules, aggressive or repetitive dialling now triggers warnings much faster. Calling the same number repeatedly in a short span can prompt the iPhone’s Manage Unwanted Calls system to automatically silence or filter future calls.


To maintain visibility without getting flagged, spread out your follow-ups, rotate call times, and balance calls with other touchpoints like SMS or email.
