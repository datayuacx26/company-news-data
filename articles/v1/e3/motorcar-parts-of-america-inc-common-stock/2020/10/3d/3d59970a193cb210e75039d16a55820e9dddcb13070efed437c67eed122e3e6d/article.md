# MPA Officially Announces Brake Caliper Operations in Tijuana

Please check out our latest press release announcing the launch of MPA’s brake caliper operations in Tijuana. MPA is excited to expand into this new product line. The new brake caliper facility in Tijuana will continue to carry on the MPA tradition of making automotive parts of the highest quality.


MPA calipers feature OE style finishes for maximum corrosion resistance, all new EPDM rubber boots and seals and 100% new phenolic pistons. Each caliper unit comes installation ready with new bleeder screws, banjo bolts and application-specific hardware where required. MPA’s 100% end of line pressure testing and full quality inspection ensures reliability and worry-free operation right out of the box.
