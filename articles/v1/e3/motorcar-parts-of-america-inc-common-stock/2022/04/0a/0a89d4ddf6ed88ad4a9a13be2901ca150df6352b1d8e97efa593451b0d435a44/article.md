# A Longstanding Commitment to Sustainability

As we end the month of April, MPA would like to remind everyone about our focused effort to promote sustainability.


When you have a chance please visit MPA’s Facebook page at[https://www.facebook.com/MotorcarPartsofAmerica](https://www.facebook.com/MotorcarPartsofAmerica) to see how MPA is committed to sustainability and how we are partners with MERA (the Association for Sustainable Manufacturing).


And check out our latest technical article which has more detail on MPA’s sustainability efforts. You can read the article at the following link:[MPA’s Statement of Sustainability](https://cdn.motorcarparts.com/mpa/production/content/uploads/2022/04/Sustainability_4_11_2022.pdf)


Finally, here are some important points to keep in mind regarding MPA’s remanufacturing process and sustainability efforts:


• Remanufacturing conserves the energy embodied in a product


• Compared to traditional manufacturing, remanufacturing requires minimal additional energy


• Remanufacturing a starter or alternator saves up to 95% of the energy needed to produce a new part


If you want to keep in touch with MPA’s ongoing sustainability efforts and learn more technical details about MPA’s parts and products please sign up for Part Smart at[Get Part Smart](https://getpartsmart.motorcarparts.com/partsmart)


Thank you!
