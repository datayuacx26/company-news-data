# MPA Catalog Part Number Update

To expand coverage to more than 40 million vehicles, MPA has recently added over 100 new part numbers for late-model vehicles.


Check out the latest additions to Motorcar Parts of America’s catalog at the following link:[New Part Number Coverage Now Available.](https://8070346.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/8070346/Part%20Number%20Update.pdf)


The new part numbers are offered through Quality-Built and Quality-Built Black Series brands powered by MPA, which serve the professional installer market.


With a commitment to providing customer solutions, Motorcar Parts of America develops and catalogs new part numbers regularly, ensuring customers can find the latest Quality-Built parts to meet their needs.
