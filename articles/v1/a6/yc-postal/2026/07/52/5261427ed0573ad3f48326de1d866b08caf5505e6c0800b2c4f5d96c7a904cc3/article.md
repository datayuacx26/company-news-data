## Key considerations for founders


Choosing a registered agent isn’t complicated, but a few factors matter more than most founders expect.


- **State coverage.** Make sure the provider supports every state you plan to operate in, not just your incorporation state.
- **Pricing structure.** Look beyond the intro rate and check renewal costs, per-state pricing, and bundled fees.
- **Compliance depth.** Some providers only forward documents, while others help track deadlines or handle filings.
- **Operational fit.** Decide whether you want a formation-first tool or an ongoing compliance partner.
- **Privacy and security.** Confirm how legal mail is stored, who can access it, and whether certifications like SOC 2 are in place.
- **Ease of switching later.** Changing registered agents is possible, but smoother with providers that support migrations cleanly.
