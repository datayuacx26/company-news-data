## Frequently asked questions about registered agent services


A few questions repeatedly pop up when picking a registered agent. Let’s clear up the most commonly asked ones.


### What does a registered agent do?


A registered agent receives legal documents and government notices on your behalf. They're your business’s official point of contact, so nothing important slips through the cracks or gets lost.


### Which is the best registered agent service?


It depends on what you need. Some want nationwide coverage or legal extras, while others prefer simplicity and automation. If you’re leaning digital-first, Postal is a smart, modern option.


### Can you be your own registered agent?


Yes, but it comes with trade-offs. You’ll need a physical address, be available during business hours, and be okay with your details on public record.


### Is it hard to be your own registered agent?


Not necessarily, but it’s easy to miss something. Staying compliant means being reliably available, organized, and okay with sacrificing some privacy and flexibility.


### What happens if I don't have a registered agent?


You risk missing legal notices, falling out of good standing, or even losing your business license. States require one, so it’s not just a nice-to-have.


### Do you need to be a lawyer to be a registered agent?


No, you don’t. You just need a physical address in the state and availability during business hours. Most registered agents aren’t lawyers, so a legal background is optional.
