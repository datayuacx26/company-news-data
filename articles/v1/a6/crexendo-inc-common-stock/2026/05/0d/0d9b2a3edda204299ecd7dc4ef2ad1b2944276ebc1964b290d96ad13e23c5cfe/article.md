# Crexendo to Present at Planet MicroCap Las Vegas Powered by MicroCapClub

**PHOENIX, AZ /[ACCESS Newswire](https://www.accessnewswire.com/) / May 26, 2026 /** Crexendo®, Inc. (NASDAQ:CXDO), an award-winning software technology company supporting over 7 million end-users, and a premier provider of cloud communication platform software and unified communications as a service (UCaaS) offerings, including voice, video, contact center, and managed IT services tailored to businesses of all sizes, today announced that it will be presenting at the Planet MicroCap Las Vegas 2026 Conference Powered by MicroCapClub. The investor presentation is on Wednesday, June 17, 2026, at 9:30 AM PT at the Bellagio Resort & Hotel. Doug Gaylor, President & COO will be hosting the presentation and answering questions at the conclusion. Additionally, Crexendo will be doing 1×1 meetings on June 17 and 18 at the conference.
