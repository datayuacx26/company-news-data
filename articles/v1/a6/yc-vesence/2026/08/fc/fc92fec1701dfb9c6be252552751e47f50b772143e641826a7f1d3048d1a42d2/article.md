# Introducing Vesence Editor

## What It Is


Vesence Editor is the manual editor in the Vesence web app: a full in-browser editor for every surface an agent touches. Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams, each with its own editor rather than a preview.


## How It Works


Everything an agent proposes lands as tracked changes. Step through them, accept, reject, or simply type over them yourself. Edit formatting and styles in Word, cells and formulas in Excel, review slide markup in PowerPoint, adjust a draft email before it sends, and approve every Teams message. Formatting holds throughout.


## Why It Matters


Agents now do more of the drafting, but the judgment stays yours. The last edit before something reaches a client should be a lawyer's, made in seconds, without downloading a file or leaving the browser.
