# Introducing Vesence Projects

## What It Is


A project is a shared workspace for a matter. Files, chats, and drafts live together in one place, and everyone you add works from the same context. Think of it as an addition to your organization's storage through SharePoint or your DMS, not a replacement for it.


## How It Works


Create a project, add the documents, and invite colleagues as owners, editors, or viewers. Files are always shared with members. Chats can be too, so a colleague can see and continue your work rather than starting over. Agents work directly in the project's files, reading, drafting, and organizing where the team can see it.


## Why It Matters


Matter context is usually scattered across inboxes, desktops, and folders nobody else can reach. A project gives the work one home, so both your colleagues and your agents start with the full picture.
