# Introducing Vesence Apps

## What It Is


Vesence Apps are small pieces of software your team builds for the work in front of it. Not a template or a saved prompt, but a real interface: a chronology tracker, a cap table reconciler, a conditions precedent monitor.


## How It Works


Describe the tool you need and the agent builds it, in minutes. It runs inside Vesence and reaches your documents, email, and SharePoint through your own permissions, so an app only ever sees what you can see. Publish it to your firm's Library and it appears in everyone's launchpad.


## Why It Matters


Every practice group has a workflow that off-the-shelf software never quite fits, and building it used to mean a budget and a roadmap. Now the people who know the work build the tool. Disputes teams, M&A groups, and banks run their own today.
