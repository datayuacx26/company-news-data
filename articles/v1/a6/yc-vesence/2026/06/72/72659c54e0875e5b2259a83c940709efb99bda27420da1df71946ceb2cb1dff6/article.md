# Karin Karsten Joins Vesence

## Welcome, Karin


We're delighted to welcome Karin Karsten to Vesence as Founding GTM. Karin joins us from the front lines of Swedish transactional practice, bringing a practitioner's understanding of exactly the work Vesence is built to support.


## A Background in Capital Markets and Public M&A


Most recently, Karin was an Advokat and Manager at Vinge, one of the Nordics' leading law firms, working across capital markets and public M&A after progressing from Associate to Senior Associate. Before Vinge, Karin spent several years at Advokatfirman Cederquist, also in capital markets and public M&A.


That combination, deep transactional experience across two premier firms, means Karin knows firsthand where lawyers lose time and where agents can genuinely help.


## Building the Future of Legal Work


At Vesence, Karin will help bring coding agents to the firms and teams that stand to benefit most, making sure the product reflects how lawyers actually work. We couldn't be more excited to have Karin on the team.
