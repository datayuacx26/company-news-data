# AI Should Make Client Work Better, Not Just Faster

## Better, Not Just Faster


Most conversations about AI in law start and end with efficiency: fewer hours, faster turnarounds, lower cost. Henrik Hansson argues that this framing misses the bigger opportunity.


The real prize is not only doing the same work faster: it is making the experience of being a client dramatically better.


## What Better Looks Like


When agents handle the mechanical parts of legal work, lawyers get more time for judgment, counsel, and communication: the parts clients actually value. Quality becomes more consistent, responsiveness improves, and the relationship deepens.


That is the standard we hold Vesence to: not just speed, but a better outcome for the people the work is for.
