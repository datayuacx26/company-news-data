# Vesence at Legal Innovators California

## A Proud Sponsor of Legal Innovators California


Vesence is a proud sponsor of Legal Innovators California in San Francisco. This year's event is focused on the shift from AI experimentation to real-world delivery: how law firms are industrializing AI across legal workstreams and agentic ecosystems, proving ROI, and how in-house teams are building internal capability to reduce reliance on outside counsel.


## What to Expect


The program brings together 700+ legal professionals for two days built around what actually works, with leaders from across the industry sharing how they are moving from pilots to production.


- 700+ legal professionals
- Leaders from Google, Intel, Chime, Cooley, and Ogletree Deakins
- Two days built around what actually works
- June 10–11, CJM, 736 Mission St, San Francisco


## Come Say Hello


This is exactly the conversation Vesence is built for: coding agents that do real legal work across Word, Outlook, Excel, PowerPoint, and PDFs, with reviewability built in so lawyers can trust and direct the output. If you're attending, we'd love to meet you in San Francisco.
