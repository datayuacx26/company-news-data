# Cederquist and Vesence

## How We Got Here


For three months, our team was embedded within Cederquist's offices. We worked directly alongside their lawyers, observed document review sessions, and experienced firsthand the challenges of transactional legal work. Instead of guessing at features, we could see exactly where lawyers lost time and where AI could meaningfully help.


The result is a tool that fits naturally into how lawyers already work, rather than asking them to adapt to a new system.
