# Why Professionals Use Vesence

## Why Vesence Exists


Law firms produce thousands of documents, emails, and deliverables every week. The quality bar is absolute. A single inconsistency, a missed cross-reference, or a formatting error can erode client trust and create real risk. Yet the tools lawyers rely on every day were never designed for this level of precision at scale.


Vesence brings purpose-built AI directly into Microsoft Office, where the work already happens. No new software to learn, no browser tabs to juggle. Just intelligence embedded in Word, Outlook, and the web, working alongside your team.


The value of Vesence comes down to three things: raising the quality of every deliverable, turning document overload into structured insight, and accelerating drafting without sacrificing control.


## Quality


Every document that leaves your firm is a reflection of its standards. Vesence runs comprehensive quality checks across every channel before anything reaches a client.


In **Word** , Vesence checks style guides, formatting, cross-references, defined terms, and runs agentic checks for miscalculations and logical inconsistencies. In **Outlook** , it verifies attachments match your email content, ensures firm style guide compliance, and aligns responses with the full thread context. In the **web app** , it checks documents against LOIs, playbooks, and precedents across multiple files at once.


Vesence also brings[Tracked Changes to Outlook](https://www.vesence.com/platform/outlook) , giving partners and associates a familiar way to review and mark up emails with precision.

## Knowledge


Deals, disputes, and advisory matters generate enormous volumes of documents. Reviewing them manually is slow. Organizing them is tedious. Finding the right precedent when you need it takes longer than it should.


Vesence's **web app** lets you run bulk AI review on hundreds of documents, surfacing issues, missing clauses, and key terms in minutes. You can move, rename, and restructure files and folders across your workspace, so your data room stays clean without the busywork.


In **Word** , Vesence reviews individual documents against your requirements, playbooks, checklists, and standards, delivering precise, inline recommendations right where you work.


[Learn More About Knowledge →](https://www.vesence.com/solutions/knowledge)


## Drafting


Drafting is where most billable hours are spent, and where AI can have the most immediate impact. But speed without control creates risk. Vesence accelerates drafting while keeping your firm's standards intact.


In the **web app** , you can create dozens of firm-formatted documents from scratch or from templates, with styles, numbering, and definitions applied automatically. Edit multiple files side by side, applying consistent changes across all of them.


In **Word** , fill in templates instantly, generating first drafts aligned with the matter context and your firm's approved templates, properly structured, formatted, and ready for review.


In **Outlook** , draft professional emails with full context. AI that understands your entire email thread, your firm's standards, and all attachments, so responses fit seamlessly into ongoing conversations.
