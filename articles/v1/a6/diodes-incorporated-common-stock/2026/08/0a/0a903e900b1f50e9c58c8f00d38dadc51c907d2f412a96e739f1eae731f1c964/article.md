# Diodes Incorporated to Participate at Upcoming Financial Conferences

PLANO, Texas--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Aug. 11, 2026-- Diodes Incorporated (Nasdaq: DIOD), today announced the Company’s participation in the following financial conferences:


- **Needham Semiconductor & SemiCap 1x1 Conference** Participation Date: **Wednesday, August 19, 2026** Location: Virtual


- **Jefferies Semiconductor, IT Hardware & Communications Technology Conference** Participation Date: **Tuesday, August 25, 2026** Location: Four Seasons Hotel – Chicago, IL


- **Deutsche Bank Technology Conference** Participation Date: **Thursday, August 27, 2026** Location: Waldorf Astoria Hotel – Dana Point, CA


Representatives of Diodes will be available to meet with registered attendees on the designated day for each event. Portfolio managers and analysts can request a meeting by contacting their sales representative at the respective firms.


For webcast details and other event information, please visit the[Event Calendar](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.diodes.com%2Fevents-and-presentations%2Fevents&esheet=54587464&newsitemid=20260810873272&lan=en-US&anchor=Event+Calendar&index=1&md5=db650e1f7f52a8d167beeeb781f5795b) **** section of the Diodes website.
