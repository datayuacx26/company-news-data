# Diodes Incorporated to Participate at Upcoming Financial Conferences

PLANO, Texas--(BUSINESS WIRE)--May 20, 2026-- Diodes Incorporated (Nasdaq: DIOD), today announced the Company’s participation in the following financial conferences:


- **TD Cowen Technology, Media and Telecom Conference** Participation Date: Thursday, May 28, 2026
Location: InterContinental Barclay Hotel – New York, NY


- **Baird Global Consumer, Technology and Services Conference** Participation Date: Tuesday, June 2, 2026
Fireside Chat: 10:15 AM ET
Location: InterContinental Barclay Hotel – New York, NY


- **Evercore TMT Global Conference** Participation Date: Wednesday, June 3, 2026
Fireside Chat: 10:00 AM PT
Location: Omni Hotel - San Francisco, CA


- **Mizuho Technology Conference** Participation date: Tuesday, June 9, 2026
Location: Conrad Downtown - New York, NY


Representatives of Diodes will be available to meet with registered attendees on the designated day for each event. Portfolio managers and analysts can request a meeting by contacting their sales representative at the respective firms.


For webcast details and other event information, please visit the[Event Calendar](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Finvestor.diodes.com%2Fevents-and-presentations%2Fevents&esheet=54538853&newsitemid=20260519979412&lan=en-US&anchor=Event+Calendar&index=1&md5=c22414d8959627bc3dff96e6a7c91a5d) **** section of the Diodes website.
