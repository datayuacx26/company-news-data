# Smart Load Switch from Diodes Incorporated Delivers Low RDS(ON) for Automotive ADAS, Infotainment, and Display Cluster Power Rail Control

PLANO, Texas--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Jun. 10, 2026-- Diodes Incorporated (Diodes) (Nasdaq: DIOD) today expands its innovative load switch portfolio with the introduction of[DML1012ALDSQ](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.diodes.com%2Fpart%2Fview%2FDML1012ALDSQ&esheet=54550540&newsitemid=20260610808035&lan=en-US&anchor=DML1012ALDSQ&index=1&md5=dc92323b141cfbec810153b127135395) , an automotive-compliant* low drain-source on-resistance (RDS(ON)) N-channel MOSFET smart load switch. The device is ideal for reliable power sequencing and power rail control in automotive applications that include advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS), infotainment platforms, and display clusters.


The smart load switch features an input voltage range of 0.8V to 4V, allowing use in numerous subsystem power rail domains. The ultra-low RDS(ON) of 8mΩ (max.) minimizes on-state losses and reduces heat generation in and around the device. A junction-to-case thermal resistance (RθJC) of 8°C/W enables up to 6A of continuous output current. The device also features a low quiescent current, improving efficiency during power gating and minimizing standby power consumption. In addition, the enable control pin provides precise, microcontroller-driven power sequencing.


The DML1012ALDSQ incorporates several key protection features, allowing engineers to reduce PCB area and time to market, as compared with a discrete solution. The device minimizes inrush current by controlling the output voltage slew rate during start-up, which benefits system stability. Integrated quick output discharge ensures downstream components are fully discharged during shutdown. In addition, undervoltage lockout (UVLO) disables system function when the supply voltage falls below a safe threshold, ensuring predictable behavior and stable supply conditions.


The[DML1012ALDSQ](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.diodes.com%2Fpart%2Fview%2FDML1012ALDSQ&esheet=54550540&newsitemid=20260610808035&lan=en-US&anchor=DML1012ALDSQ&index=2&md5=a1579e014d28078b2fda095e30735aa9) is available in the V-DFN3030-8 (Type R) (3mm x 3mm) package and priced from $0.17 in 1,000-piece quantities. A standard compliance version, the[DML1012ALDS](https://cts.businesswire.com/ct/CT?id=smartlink&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.diodes.com%2Fpart%2Fview%2FDML1012ALDS&esheet=54550540&newsitemid=20260610808035&lan=en-US&anchor=DML1012ALDS&index=3&md5=5abda8feaf9a94b02400432686f7b606) , is available for industrial and commercial applications.
