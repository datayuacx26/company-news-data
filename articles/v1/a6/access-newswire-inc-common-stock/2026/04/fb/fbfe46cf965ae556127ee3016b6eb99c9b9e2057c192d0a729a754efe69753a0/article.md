# ACCESS Newswire vs. Business Wire: Why Modern PR Teams Are Making the Switch

### **The Core Problem:**


Distribution Without the Rest


Business Wire is, at its heart, a wire service. It does one thing — distribution — and does it well. But that’s all it does. There’s no media database. No pitching tools. No media monitoring. No social listening. No AI writing assistance.


That means if you’re a PR team using Business Wire, you’re also paying for a separate media database, a separate monitoring tool, a separate pitching platform, and likely a separate analytics dashboard. That’s four or five vendors, four or five logins, and four or five invoices — none of which talk to each other.


ACCESS Newswire replaces that entire stack with one platform.


### Pricing That Makes Sense


Business Wire’s pricing starts at $830 for a 400-word release, with overage charges of $230 per additional 100 words. Need multimedia? That’s another $450. It adds up fast.


ACCESS Newswire offers flat pricing starting at $415 per release or $714/month for a subscription that includes unlimited word counts, multimedia, and access to the full platform — media database, monitoring, pitching, analytics, and AI writing tools. No per-word fees. No hidden charges. And flexible contract terms with monthly, quarterly, and annual options.

### AI-Powered Everything


Business Wire has been slow to embrace AI. There’s no AI writing assistant, no AI-powered analytics, and no intelligent distribution recommendations.


ACCESS Newswire’s AImee AI assistant helps teams draft and optimize press releases across 29 industries. Our distribution reporting goes beyond pickup counts to deliver AI-powered engagement scoring and optimization insights. And it all runs on a closed-source, secure network — your data never touches a third-party AI model.


### Full IR Suite Included


Both platforms serve public companies, but ACCESS Newswire includes a full investor relations suite — IR website design and management, earnings calls with domestic U.S.-based dedicated operators, webcasting, investor days, and ADA and SEC compliance. Business Wire offers IR capabilities through its Q4 integration, adding another vendor to the mix.


### The Bottom Line


Business Wire is a premium wire service. ACCESS Newswire is a complete PR and IR platform. If your team needs more than distribution — and most teams do — the value equation isn’t close.
