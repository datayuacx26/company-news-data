# Global Partners Named to Newsweek’s America’s Greatest Workplaces for Parents and Families List

***Independent recognition follows company’s second consecutive year on America’s Greatest Workplaces list***


**Waltham, Mass. (July 16, 2026) –** Global Partners, a leading independent liquid energy supplier and retail operator, has been named one of America’s Greatest Workplaces for Parents and Families for 2026 by Newsweek, marking the first time the company has earned this distinction. The recognition comes just weeks after Global was named one of[America’s Greatest Workplaces](https://rankings.newsweek.com/americas-greatest-workplaces-2026) overall for 2026, its second consecutive year on that list.


The Parents and Families honor recognizes companies that support employees balancing careers with family responsibilities. As with the broader America’s Greatest Workplaces distinction announced in June, the recognition is based entirely on independent, third-party data. No information came from Global Partners’ own employee surveys; all insights were gathered through independent online access panels.


“Being recognized for how we support parents and families means just as much to us as being named a Greatest Workplace overall,” said Heather Hampton, Vice President of Talent at Global Partners. “Our teammates are balancing careers with raising families, caring for loved ones and everything in between, and we are committed to building a workplace that supports them through all of it.”


Newsweek partners with independent research organizations to produce its Greatest Workplaces rankings each year, drawing on employee interviews and workplace data collected through third-party sources rather than employer-submitted surveys. Global Partners’ back-to-back recognitions in June and July 2026, first for America’s Greatest Workplaces and now for America’s Greatest Workplaces for Parents and Families, reflect the company’s broader commitment to building a workplace culture where employees can thrive both professionally and personally.


**About Global Partners LP:**


*Building on a legacy that began more than 90 years ago, Global Partners has evolved into a Fortune 500 company and industry-leading integrated owner, supplier, and operator of liquid energy terminals, fueling locations, and guest-focused retail experiences. Global operates or maintains dedicated storage at 54 liquid energy terminals—with connectivity to strategic rail, pipeline, and marine assets—spanning from Maine to Florida and into the U.S. Gulf States. Through this extensive network, the company distributes gasoline, distillates, residual oil, and renewable fuels to wholesalers, retailers, and commercial customers. In addition, Global has a large portfolio of owned, leased and/or supplied retail locations across the Northeast states, the Mid-Atlantic, and Texas, providing the fuels people need to keep them on the go at their unique guest-focused convenience destinations. Recognized as one of Fortune’s Most Admired Companies, Global Partners is embracing progress and diversifying to meet the needs of the energy transition.*
