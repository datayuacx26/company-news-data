# Global Partners Fuels New Partnership with Hackensack Meridian Health, Driving Care Across the Region

***New fleet card program supports nearly 800 vehicles, delivers significant cost savings, and reinvests in community health initiatives***


**** **Waltham, Mass. (March 25, 2026) –** From hospitals to community care, Global Partners, a leading independent liquid energy supplier and retail operator, is fueling the front lines of healthcare through a new partnership with Hackensack Meridian Health, one of the nation’s leading healthcare networks. Through the partnership, Global will provide fuel fleet cards to support Hackensack Meridian Health’s approximately 800-vehicle fleet, ensuring reliable and efficient fueling for vehicles essential to delivering care and services across hospitals, outpatient facilities, and community programs.


By leveraging Global Partners’ nonprofit fleet card offering, Hackensack Meridian Health is expected to realize nearly $500,000 in fuel cost savings. These efficiencies will help streamline operations while allowing the organization to focus more resources on patient care and community health services.


“Supporting organizations that serve on the front lines of our communities is central to our mission,” said Michael Lingman, Global Partners Senior Director of Commercial Fleet Fuel Programs. “We are proud to partner with Hackensack Meridian Health to provide reliable fuel solutions that help power their essential services, reduce operating costs, and ultimately strengthen the communities we both serve.”


“In healthcare, you have to always be ready; that includes fueled-up vehicles ready to transport a patient or deliver care at a moment’s notice,” said Jose Lozano, Executive Vice President and Chief Growth Officer, Hackensack Meridian *Health* . “We are excited to partner with Global Partners to deliver cost-efficient fuel for our vehicle fleet, so we can focus on what Hackensack Meridian *Health* does best: delivering timely, quality care when patients need it most.”


Global Partners maintains a strong operational presence in New Jersey, where it owns and operates two terminal locations that store and supply liquid energy products, including gasoline and diesel. In addition, Global supplies seven retail gasoline stations across the state, helping to support local transportation needs and critical services. This regional infrastructure enables Global to reliably serve organizations like Hackensack Meridian Health with dependable fuel access close to where care is delivered.


In addition, Global Partners is contributing $25,000 to the Hackensack Meridian Health Foundation in support of its mission to advance health care, fund innovative programs, and strengthen local communities.


Together, this partnership reflects a shared commitment to keeping communities growing, moving, and thriving, combining operational efficiency with meaningful reinvestment in patient care and local health initiatives.
