# Introducing LG Intent: Predictive Buyer Intent Data from LeadGenius

Every revenue team knows the feeling: your reps are working accounts that look perfect on paper, but nobody on the other end is actually in a buying cycle. Meanwhile, the companies that *are* about to buy never make it into your CRM until a competitor has already closed them.


That gap between who you're targeting and who's actually ready to buy is exactly why we built **LG Intent** , LeadGenius's predictive buyer intent solution. LG Intent combines traditional research-based intent signals with agentic workflows and a proprietary AI suppression system to separate true buyers from the noise.


## What is LG Intent?


LG Intent is a predictive intent data product that identifies companies showing verified, scored, and logical buying signals. Instead of handing you a raw feed of every account that touched a topic, LG Intent layers multiple signal types together:


- **Research behavior** across an expansive intent topic taxonomy
- **News and events** , analyzed in real time to validate interest spikes
- **Hiring trends** , because a new VP of Finance often signals intent for finance software
- **Funding rounds** , since capital injections trigger predictable purchasing behaviors
- **Firmographics** , so company size, industry, and location filter out illogical matches
- **Contextual relevance** , cross-walking related topics the way buying rechargeable batteries implies buying a charger


## Built for scale, filtered for signal


The numbers behind LG Intent tell the story of both breadth and discipline:


**60,000+** Intent topics tracked, up from 5,000 in legacy approaches


**700 million+** Intent pairs monitored


**800 million+** False positives suppressed before reaching your team


**60+** Confidence score required before an account is surfaced


That last point matters. Most intent providers show you everything and let you sort out the mess. LG Intent only surfaces accounts that clear a high confidence bar, so every signal your team sees is one worth acting on.


## Why "predictive" changes the game


Traditional intent data tells you who is researching right now, which is valuable, but often means you're arriving at the same time as every competitor watching the same signals. Predictive intent looks earlier in the journey. By combining research activity with growth signals, technology adoption, leadership changes, and news triggers, LG Intent can surface accounts that are *entering* a buying cycle, not just accounts already deep in one.


The result: your team builds pipeline earlier, prioritizes smarter, and reaches buyers before the raised hand goes up.
