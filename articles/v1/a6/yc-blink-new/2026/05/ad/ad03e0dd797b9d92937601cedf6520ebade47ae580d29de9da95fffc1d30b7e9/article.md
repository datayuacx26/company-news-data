# OpenClaw for Solopreneurs: Replace a VA With One AI Agent

A competent VA costs **$800-2,000/month** . You get 20-40 hours of time, human judgment, and someone who makes mistakes you have to catch.


An OpenClaw agent running 24/7 costs **$22/month** on Blink Claw. It handles lead research, follow-up emails, content drafts, client reports, and competitor monitoring — all from your phone, while you sleep.


The comparison isn't perfect (a human VA does things no AI can, like attending a physical meeting). But for the specific, repeatable tasks that consume 3-4 hours of a solopreneur's day? The math has shifted decisively.


## The 6 Business Operations an OpenClaw Agent Handles


### 1. Lead Research and Qualification


Instead of spending 45 minutes per lead manually researching LinkedIn, their company website, and recent news, your agent does it for you.


**Setup in SOUL.md:**


```text
Lead research task:
When I send you a person's name and company via Telegram, research them:
- Find their LinkedIn profile, role, and recent activity
- Find any recent news about their company (funding, product launches, leadership changes)
- Identify 2-3 points of relevance to my business: [describe what you sell and who you serve]
- Format as a 1-page brief: who they are, why they might be interested in [your offer], talking points
- Send the brief to me via Telegram


```


**Time saved:** 45 min/lead → 5 min (just messaging your agent the name)


### 2. Follow-Up Email Drafts


Consistent follow-up is the difference between a $30K/year business and a $100K/year business. Most solopreneurs follow up inconsistently because it's tedious.


**Setup:**


```text
Follow-up drafting task:
I will send you context: who I met, when, what we discussed, what the next step should be.
Draft a professional follow-up email:
- Subject line: relevant to the specific conversation (not "Following up")
- Opening: reference the specific thing we discussed
- Body: value reminder (how I can help with their specific situation)
- CTA: one clear ask (call, document, decision)
- Tone: [your voice — direct/warm/professional/friendly]
Send the draft to me via Telegram. I'll approve and send it myself.


```


**Time saved:** 15-20 min/email → 3 min (review + approve)


### 3. Client Report Generation


If you have recurring clients who need regular updates, your agent assembles reports from your data sources:


**Setup:**


```text
Weekly client report (run every Friday at 4pm):
For each active client in my client list (I'll provide a Google Sheet):
- Pull the week's work log from Toggl (or wherever you track time)
- Pull the week's deliverables from the project folder in Google Drive
- Generate a short report: what was done, hours logged, what's next week, any blockers
- Create the report as a Google Doc named "[Client]-Week-of-[Date]"
- Send me a Telegram notification with links to all generated reports


```


**Time saved:** 2-3 hours every Friday → 20 minutes of review


### 4. Content Drafting


Your agent drafts content (emails, LinkedIn posts, blog outlines) from your notes and recordings:


**Setup:**


```text
Content drafting task:
I'll send you voice notes or rough bullet points via Telegram.
Transform them into:
- LinkedIn posts: 150-300 words, [my posting style], with a hook
- Email newsletter sections: 200-400 words, my brand voice
- Blog post outlines: problem → insight → solution structure, with 5-7 sections
Return drafts via Telegram. I'll edit and publish manually.


```


**Time saved:** 2-3 hours/week of content creation → 30 minutes of editing


### 5. Competitor and Market Monitoring


Your agent watches your competitors and your market so you don't have to:


**Setup in SOUL.md:**


```text
Market monitoring (run every Monday at 8am):
Monitor these sources weekly:
- Competitor websites: [list 3-5 competitor URLs] — check for new blog posts, product updates, pricing changes
- Industry keywords on Reddit: r/[your industry] posts with 50+ upvotes
- News search: "[your industry] news" from the last 7 days
- Job postings: if [competitor] posts more than 3 jobs in a week (signal they're growing)


Send me a Monday morning digest via Telegram:
- What competitors published
- What's trending in the industry
- Any signals I should act on


```


**Time saved:** 1-2 hours of manual research → 10 minutes reading the digest


### 6. Proposal and Scope Document Drafts


For project-based solopreneurs (consultants, designers, developers, copywriters), scoping is the most time-intensive pre-sales task:


**Setup:**


```text
Proposal drafting task:
I'll send you notes from a discovery call via Telegram: client name, their problem, what they need, timeline, any constraints.
Draft a proposal document:
- 1-page executive summary: the problem, my proposed solution, why me
- Scope of work: 3-5 bullet deliverables, specific and clear
- Timeline: week-by-week breakdown
- Investment: [your rate structure — I'll fill in actual numbers]
- Next steps: what they need to do to move forward
Format as a Google Doc and send me the link via Telegram.


```


**Time saved:** 3-4 hours per proposal → 45 minutes of review and customization
