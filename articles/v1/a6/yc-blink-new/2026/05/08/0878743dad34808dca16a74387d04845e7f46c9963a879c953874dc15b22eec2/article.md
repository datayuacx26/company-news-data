# Vibe Coding for Sales Teams: Build Your Stack, Own Your Pipeline

## How It Works


Vibe coding for sales teams follows a 3-step workflow:


1


#### Describe your tool


Start with what you actually need. Be specific. "Build me a prospect tracker where I can log companies with their ICP fit score, deal size, last contact date, and notes. I want to filter by score and sort by deal size." The more specific, the better the output.


Blink generates the full-stack app from that description. The database comes automatically — no Supabase account needed, no schema to design. You describe the data you want to track, Blink handles the rest.


2


#### Review and iterate


The generated app runs immediately. Check whether the fields match what you need, test the filters, and describe any changes in plain English. "Add a status field with values: outreach, qualified, demo-scheduled, proposal, closed-won, closed-lost." Iterate in the same session.


Blink is full-stack from day 1, not just the frontend. The backend logic, API routes, and database queries update when you ask for changes.


3


#### Add your team and deploy


Auth is built in — team members can sign up and log in immediately. No Clerk configuration, no Firebase Auth setup. Share the link, invite the team, and start tracking.


Hosting is included — no Vercel config needed, no DNS setup, no deployment pipeline. Your tool is live the moment you click deploy. 200+ models are available if you want to add AI features later, like automatic lead scoring or follow-up drafting.
