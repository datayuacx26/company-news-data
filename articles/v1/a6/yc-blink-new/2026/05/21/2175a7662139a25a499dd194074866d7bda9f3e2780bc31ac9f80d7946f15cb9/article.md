# Bolt.new vs Base44: Which AI Builder Should You Use? (2026)

## What Is Base44?


Base44 homepage — AI internal tool builder for business teams


Blink


Base44 is a no-code AI app builder designed for non-technical users who want to create business tools, internal dashboards, customer portals, and workflows without ever seeing a line of code. You describe what you need in plain language, and Base44 generates a complete frontend with a managed backend and database — deployed and hosted automatically.


Where Bolt thinks like a developer IDE, Base44 thinks like a business operations tool. Every plan includes authentication with role management, a visual database editor, payment processing, email marketing, and analytics. Base44 was acquired by Wix in 2024.


**Key specs:**


- **Pricing:** Free (25 msg/mo) / Starter $16/mo / Builder $40/mo / Pro $80/mo / Elite $160/mo (annual, verified May 2026)
- **Best for:** Non-technical founders, product managers, ops teams, business tool builders


**Limitations worth knowing:**


Base44 is optimized for internal tools, not consumer-facing apps. The credit-based pricing model can get expensive when your app has active users that trigger integration credits at scale. Free-tier users get just 25 messages and 100 integration credits per month. Customizing beyond Base44's visual editor requires exporting code.
