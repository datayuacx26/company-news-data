**Prototypes and MVPs.** The most expensive mistake in software is building the wrong thing at full engineering cost. Vibe coding makes the first iteration cheap enough to throw away. That changes the economics of early-stage product decisions entirely.

They weren't cutting corners — they were finding out fast whether anyone cared before committing to a full engineering team.


**Internal tools with limited scope.** A dashboard for your ops team. A reporting tool for your sales manager. A simple approval workflow. These are low-risk environments where AI-generated output can be quickly reviewed and deployed without long-term maintenance burden.


**Solo builders and indie hackers.** When there's no team to confuse and no compliance audit to pass, the tradeoffs that make traditional coding essential simply don't apply. One person with a clear prompt can ship something real over a weekend.


The pattern is consistent: vibe coding works best when the cost of getting things wrong is low and the value of moving fast is high.


## When traditional coding wins


[45% of AI-generated code fails security benchmarks across OWASP Top-10 categories](https://www.veracode.com/resources/state-of-software-security) , according to Veracode testing across Java, Python, C#, and JavaScript. That number matters when you're deciding what to ship without a human review pass.


**Security-critical systems.** Fintech, healthcare, anything handling financial transactions or regulated personal data. A security flaw here isn't a performance issue — it's a legal and regulatory liability. AI-generated code that passes a basic test can be a serious problem in production.


**Complex systems built for scale.** A vibe-coded app handling 50 users behaves differently at 50,000. Traditional development is designed for scale from the architecture up. AI tools optimize for the happy path, not the edge cases that surface under real traffic.


**Code maintained by a large team over years.** AI doesn't remember your previous decisions. Every prompt starts fresh. Patterns that experienced developers establish in one session won't automatically carry forward. Vibe-coded codebases accumulate technical debt faster than traditionally developed software — debugging grows harder as the codebase grows without consistent architectural decisions.


**Compliance-heavy environments.** Enterprise procurement, HIPAA, SOC 2. These require audit trails and documentation that AI-generated code rarely produces automatically. Traditional development gives you something to show a compliance auditor — a full history of who wrote what, why, and what it was tested against.


Traditional coding is not slow because developers are inefficient. It's thorough because the review-test-stage process exists to catch exactly the failures that vibe coding quietly introduces.
