# How to Build a Portfolio Website With AI (No Code Required)

## How to build your portfolio — step by step


**Estimated time: 2–4 hours from blank page to live URL.**


### Step 1: Write your brief (10 min)


Before opening any tool, write down four things: your name and professional tagline, your role (frontend dev / UX designer / motion artist / consultant), three to five projects you want to showcase, and which sections you need (gallery, contact form, about, testimonials).


This step costs ten minutes. It saves ninety.

Open[blink.new](https://blink.new/) and describe what you want. Here's a working starter:


> "Build me a portfolio website for a freelance UX designer. Include a hero section with my name and tagline, a project gallery with individual case study pages, an about section, a skills section, client testimonials, and a contact form that saves submissions to a database. Use a clean minimal dark design."


Blink generates the full app — frontend, database schema, contact form backend, and an admin panel to manage your projects. In minutes, not months.

### Step 3: Customize content and design (60–90 min)


Replace placeholder text with your actual bio and project descriptions. Swap in real screenshots and case study copy. Adjust colors, fonts, and layout by typing what you want: "Make the hero full-width. Add a subtle gradient background. Increase font size on mobile."


The built-in CMS lets you add and edit projects without touching code. Log in to the admin panel, click, type, save. No pull requests. No deployment.


### Step 4: Connect your custom domain (15 min)


Connect` yourname.com` in the Blink settings. SSL provisions automatically — no Certbot, no DNS wrestling. Blink hosts your site. One bill, one dashboard, zero DevOps.


Your domain goes live in under fifteen minutes.


### Step 5: Test and launch (30 min)


Check every page on mobile.[58.67% of global web traffic comes from mobile devices](https://www.statista.com/statistics/277125/share-of-website-traffic-coming-from-mobile-devices/) — a portfolio that breaks on a phone loses more than half its audience. Submit a test contact form to confirm submissions land in the database.


Then share the link. Your portfolio is live.


### Step 6: Keep it updated (5 min per new project)


Log into the admin panel when you finish a project. Add the case study, upload a screenshot, hit save. No code changes, no deployment pipeline, no asking a developer to push an update.
