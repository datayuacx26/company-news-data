## When HubSpot Still Makes Sense


To be honest: HubSpot is genuinely good at what it does. There are situations where the price is worth it.


**You need mature marketing automation at scale.** Multi-step behavioral workflows — trigger email A if someone opens but doesn't click, wait 3 days, branch on purchase history — HubSpot's automation engine is battle-tested and mature. Building that level of conditional logic in a custom tool requires meaningful engineering investment.


**You have 50+ people who need a shared CRM with governance controls.** At that scale, the value of a unified platform — where marketing, sales, and service share one contact record with native handoffs, partitioning, and audit trails — starts to justify the cost. SSO, custom objects, and enterprise permissions become genuinely important.


**You're in regulated enterprise sales.** If you're selling into Fortune 500 companies and need SOC 2-compliant tooling, HubSpot's enterprise tier covers those requirements. The price becomes a line item on a much larger contract.


For everyone else — the startup, the bootstrapped SaaS, the agency, the 5-15 person team — the math doesn't hold. You're paying for features you'll never use, locked into annual contracts, watching the bill grow every time your contact list crosses a tier.
