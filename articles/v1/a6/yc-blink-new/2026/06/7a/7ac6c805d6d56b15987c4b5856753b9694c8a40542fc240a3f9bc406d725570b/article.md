## Migrating Your Existing HubSpot Data


HubSpot exports contacts, companies, deals, and activity logs as CSV files. The migration process is straightforward:


1


#### Export from HubSpot


In HubSpot, go to Contacts → Export. Select contacts, companies, and deals. HubSpot generates CSV files for each object type. Download them all — this takes about five minutes regardless of database size.


2


#### Import into your Blink CRM


Blink generates a CSV import interface automatically as part of your CRM build. Upload each file, map the columns to your new fields, and confirm the import. All historical records, including deal stages and contact notes, transfer cleanly.


3


#### Connect your email and integrations


Blink includes built-in email OAuth for Gmail and Outlook. Connect in one click. For Slack notifications, Zapier triggers, or any other integration, describe what you want in the Blink chat and it adds the connection to your app.


4


#### Set up user accounts and permissions


Blink's built-in auth handles inviting your team. Set each user's role — admin, manager, or rep — and permissions apply across the entire application automatically.


5


#### Run both systems in parallel for one week


Keep HubSpot active while your team tests the new CRM. After one week of parallel operation with no issues, cancel your HubSpot subscription. The break-even on your Blink build typically arrives before the next HubSpot renewal.


## What AI Adds That HubSpot Doesn't


A custom CRM built with Blink can include AI features that HubSpot doesn't offer at any tier:


**AI-generated email drafts.** Instead of static templates with merge fields, your CRM can generate a unique first draft for each contact based on their company, role, and recent activity. Teams that switched from HubSpot templates to AI-drafted emails reported[open rates of 34% versus 22%](https://chatgpt.ca/blog/hubspot-replacement-case-study) with template-based sequences.


**Natural language reporting.** Instead of building reports through a UI, your team types questions: "Which rep closed the most deals last quarter?" or "Show me stalled deals over $5K." The CRM generates the query and returns formatted results.


**Automatic lead enrichment.** When a new contact enters the system, AI pulls publicly available data about their company — funding, headcount, industry, recent news — and adds a summary to the contact record. Reps start every conversation with context.
