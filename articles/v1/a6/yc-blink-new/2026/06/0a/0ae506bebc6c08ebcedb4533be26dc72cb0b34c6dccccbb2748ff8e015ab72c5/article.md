# What Sales Teams Build with AI: ROI Calculators, CRMs, and Custom Demo Tools

## Custom Demo Environment


**The problem:** Showing a generic SaaS demo doesn't match the prospect's industry. They can't visualize their workflow inside your product. The call feels like a pitch instead of a preview.


**What teams build instead:** A pre-filled dashboard showing what your product looks like for that specific prospect's industry, team size, and use case. Built before the call. Unique URL per prospect. Their logo, their data, their use case.


**What it replaces:** Manual setup in the production app, or a static slideshow with screenshots that don't move.


One SaaS sales team increased demo-to-close rate by 22% after switching to personalized demo environments. Each prospect received a unique URL. The dashboard showed their industry's typical metrics pre-populated.


Blink includes hosting — each demo URL lives on a real domain, shareable with prospects in a proposal or cold email, no configuration required.


## Lead Research Dashboard


**The problem:** Reps pull LinkedIn, website, and news data separately for each outbound call. Twenty minutes of research per prospect, across four browser tabs, repeated dozens of times per week.


**What teams build instead:** Enter a company name and URL. The tool pulls recent news, headcount changes, job postings, and tech stack automatically — all on one page, formatted for fast skimming before a call.


**What it replaces:** Four separate browser tabs opened for every outbound call, plus a copy-paste habit into a notes doc that nobody else can see.


Research time drops from 20 minutes to 3. The rep's first email is more specific, which improves reply rates. The output is shareable — managers can review call prep quality before a big deal.

**The problem:** Salesforce is too complex. The team uses 4 fields and pays for 400. The fields they actually need — specific to their product and their sales motion — don't exist in the standard setup without a developer.


**What teams build instead:** Exactly those 4 fields, plus the 3 custom fields Salesforce charges to configure — deal stage, competitor being displaced, and "reason lost." A pipeline view that matches the way the team actually discusses deals in their weekly meeting.


**What it replaces:** A Salesforce instance at $100/user/month that requires a certified admin to change a stage dropdown.
